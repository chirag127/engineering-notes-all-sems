1. Fill a container with the liquid to be tested.
2. Place a clean, dry glass rod on the surface of the liquid.
3. Measure the maximum height (h) that the liquid rises up the glass rod.
4. Measure the diameter (d) of the rod.
5. Calculate the surface tension using the formula: Surface tension (γ) = (2 * h * d) / r, where r is the radius of the rod.
6. Repeat the measurement several times to obtain an average value.
