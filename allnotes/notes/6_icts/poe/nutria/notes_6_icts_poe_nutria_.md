3. Gatka is a traditional Indian martial art that originated in the Punjab region. It is a form of armed combat that utilizes swords, sticks, and shields.

4. Silambam is a traditional martial art from Tamil Nadu that utilizes a long bamboo staff. It is a form of unarmed combat that combines strikes, kicks, and grappling.

5. All three of these martial arts traditions have been practiced for centuries and are still practiced in India today. They are often used for self-defense, physical fitness, and competitive sports.
