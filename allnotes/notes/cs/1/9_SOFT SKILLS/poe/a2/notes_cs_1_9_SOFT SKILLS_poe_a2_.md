### Transformation of Sentences:

1. Simple Sentence: It contains only one independent clause. e.g. "I am reading a book."

2. Compound Sentence: It contains two independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction. e.g. "I am reading a book, but I am unable to concentrate."

**Skimming:**

- Roughly scanning the text to get an overview of the structure and content.
- Reading headings, introductions and conclusions.
- Glancing at diagrams/images and their captions.
- Not reading in detail.
- Used to quickly grasp the overall meaning.

**Scanning:**

- Searching for specific information.
- Looking for key terms or names.
- Reading selectively.
- Not reading everything - focusing on locating relevant details.

- Rereading sections of text for better understanding.
- Going back over confusing sections.
- Reflecting on what has been read to reinforce learning and memory.
