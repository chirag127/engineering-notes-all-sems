KCS is an approach to knowledge management that focuses on the use of knowledge to support customer service and support processes. It is based on the idea that the best way to improve customer service is to ensure that knowledge is captured, shared, and reused to improve the customer experience.

KCS is based on the following principles:

1. Knowledge is the primary resource used to resolve customer issues.
2. Knowledge is the result of the collective experience of the organization.
3. Knowledge should be captured, shared, and reused.
4. Knowledge should be continuously improved.
5. Knowledge should be used to inform and improve customer service processes.
