### Organizational Structures in SPM

1. Organizational structure refers to the way in which an organization is structured and managed. It is the framework of roles, responsibilities, and relationships that are used to facilitate the completion of tasks and the attainment of organizational goals.

2. In SPM, organizational structures are typically hierarchical in nature, with a top-down approach to decision-making and task delegation. At the top of the hierarchy is the board of directors, which is responsible for setting the overall direction of the organization.

3. Below the board of directors are the executive team and other senior management personnel. These individuals are responsible for setting strategic goals and objectives, as well as implementing the policies and procedures set by the board of directors.

4. Below the executive team are the middle managers and other operational personnel. These individuals are responsible for carrying out the day-to-day operations of the organization and are often responsible for the supervision of employees and the management of resources.

5. Finally, at the bottom of the organizational structure are the employees, who are responsible for completing the tasks assigned to them by their supervisors. They are the ones who actually do the work and are the backbone of the organization.
