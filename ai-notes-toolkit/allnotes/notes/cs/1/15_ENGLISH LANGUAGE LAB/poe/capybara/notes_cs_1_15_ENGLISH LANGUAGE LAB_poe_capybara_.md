### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

To become proficient in writing, it is important to have a strong understanding of the rules of English grammar. Here are some tips that can help students train themselves to use correct and error-free writing:

- **Study the basics:** Start by learning the basic rules of English grammar, such as subject-verb agreement, proper use of tenses, and correct punctuation. This will form the foundation of your writing skills.
- **Read extensively:** Reading extensively not only improves your vocabulary but also helps you understand how sentences are constructed and how grammar is used in different contexts. It is recommended to read a variety of materials, such as newspapers, magazines, novels, and academic articles.
- **Practice writing:** Writing is a skill that can be improved with practice. Start with simple exercises, such as writing short paragraphs or essays, and gradually move on to more complex writing tasks. It is important to review your writing and identify the errors that you make, so that you can work on improving them.
- **Get feedback:** Feedback from teachers, peers, or professional editors can be very helpful in improving your writing skills. They can point out the errors that you may have missed and suggest ways to improve your writing. It is important to be open to feedback and use it to improve your skills.
- **Use writing tools:** There are several writing tools available online that can help you improve your grammar and writing skills. These tools can check your writing for errors, suggest corrections, and offer writing tips. Some popular writing tools include Grammarly, Hemingway Editor, and ProWritingAid.
- **Read and follow style guides:** Style guides provide guidelines for writing in a specific style or format, such as MLA, APA, or Chicago. Following these guidelines can help you write consistently and avoid errors. It is important to read and follow the style guide that is relevant to your field of study or profession.

By following these tips, students can train themselves to use correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar. With practice and persistence, they can develop strong writing skills that will serve them well in their academic and professional careers.



### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

To be an effective communicator in a technical environment, it is important to develop a style that is both clear and professional. Here are some tips for cultivating a relevant technical style:

1. Use precise language: Technical communication requires accurate and specific language, so avoid using vague or overly general terms. Use technical jargon only when it is necessary and appropriate, and define any unfamiliar terms.

2. Be concise: Technical communication should be clear and to the point. Avoid lengthy descriptions and unnecessary details. Use bullet points or numbered lists to break up information and make it easier to read.

3. Use visual aids: Charts, graphs, and other visual aids can help to illustrate complex ideas and make them easier to understand. Make sure that all visual aids are clear and accurate, and use them sparingly.

4. Use proper grammar and punctuation: Technical communication should be free of errors in grammar and punctuation. Use proper capitalization and avoid using all caps, which can be difficult to read. Use active voice whenever possible, and avoid using passive voice.

5. Be professional: Technical communication should be professional in tone and style. Avoid using slang or informal language, and avoid using humor unless it is appropriate and relevant. Use a professional email signature and make sure that all communication is courteous and respectful.

6. Practice your presentation skills: Effective technical communication often involves presenting ideas and information to others. Practice your presentation skills to become a confident and effective presenter. Use visual aids and make sure that you are well prepared and organized.

By cultivating a relevant technical style of communication and presentation, you will be able to effectively communicate technical ideas and information to others in a clear and professional manner. This will help you to succeed in your academic and professional endeavors.

### 1. Group Discussion: Practical based on Accurate and Current Grammatical Patterns.

In order to have an effective group discussion, it is important to use accurate and current grammatical patterns. Here are some practical tips for using proper grammar in group discussions:

1. Use proper subject-verb agreement: Make sure that the subject and verb in your sentences agree in tense and number. For example, if the subject is singular, the verb should also be singular.

2. Use correct punctuation: Proper use of punctuation is essential when communicating effectively. Use commas, semicolons, and colons appropriately to clarify your meaning.

3. Avoid common grammatical errors: Common grammatical errors such as using incorrect verb tenses or confusing homophones can detract from your message. Take the time to review and correct any errors in your speech or writing.

4. Use clear and concise language: Avoid using overly complex language or technical jargon that may confuse your audience. Use simple, direct language to communicate your ideas clearly.

5. Listen actively: Pay attention to the language used by your fellow group members and respond appropriately. Use active listening skills to ensure that you are fully engaged in the discussion.

By using accurate and current grammatical patterns in group discussions, you can communicate more effectively and ensure that your ideas are clearly understood by others. Practice these tips regularly to improve your communication skills and become a more effective group member.

### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

In order to deliver an effective and convincing presentation or keynote, it is important to follow correct methodologies of argumentation. Below are some key points to consider when preparing and delivering a presentation:

1. Know your audience: Before beginning your presentation, it is important to understand your audience's background, interests, and knowledge level. This will help you tailor your argument to their needs and ensure that they are engaged throughout the presentation.

2. Develop a clear and concise argument: Your argument should be easy to understand and focused on the key points that you want to make. It should be supported by evidence and presented in a logical order.

6. Anticipate objections: Anticipating objections and addressing them in your presentation can help strengthen your argument and make it more convincing. This can be achieved by considering different perspectives and presenting counterarguments.

7. End with a call to action: Ending your presentation with a call to action can help motivate your audience to take action based on the points you have made. This can be achieved by highlighting the benefits of your argument and providing clear steps that your audience can take.

By following these correct methodologies of argumentation, you can deliver a compelling and effective presentation or keynote that engages your audience and achieves your goals.

Sinha provides a comprehensive guide to English grammar and usage. Here are some key points from the book:

1. Parts of speech - English language has eight parts of speech: nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections. Nouns are words that represent people, places, things, or ideas. Pronouns are words that take the place of a noun. Verbs are words that express an action or state of being. Adjectives are words that describe a noun or pronoun. Adverbs are words that modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Prepositions are words that show a relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. Conjunctions are words that join two or more words, phrases or clauses. Interjections are words used to express strong feelings or emotions.

2. Sentence structure - A sentence is a group of words that express a complete thought. A sentence has two essential parts: a subject and a predicate. The subject is the person, place, thing, or idea that the sentence is about. The predicate is the action or state of being of the subject.

3. Tenses - The English language has three tenses - past, present, and future. Each tense has four forms - simple, progressive, perfect, and perfect progressive. The simple form is used to describe a general fact or habitual action. The progressive form is used to describe an ongoing action. The perfect form is used to describe a completed action in the past. The perfect progressive form is used to describe an ongoing action that started in the past and is still continuing.

4. Active and passive voice - In the active voice, the subject of the sentence performs the action. In the passive voice, the subject of the sentence receives the action. The passive voice is used when the focus is on the action rather than the subject.

5. Punctuation - Punctuation marks are symbols used to clarify the meaning of a sentence. The most commonly used punctuation marks are the period, comma, semicolon, colon, question mark, exclamation mark, and quotation marks.
