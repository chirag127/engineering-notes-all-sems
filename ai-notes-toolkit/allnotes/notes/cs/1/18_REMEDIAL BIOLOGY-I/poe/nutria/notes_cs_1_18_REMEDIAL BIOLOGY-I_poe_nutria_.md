


# Knowledge Centered Support (KCS)

KCS is a methodology used in knowledge management and customer service that focuses on the reuse of knowledge and information. It is based on the idea that knowledge should be the primary resource used in problem-solving and customer service. KCS is designed to help organizations create a knowledge base that can be reused and shared with customers and employees.

KCS is based on the following principles:

- Knowledge should be the primary resource used in problem-solving and customer service.
- Knowledge should be created and reused in a collaborative environment.
- Knowledge should be organized and stored in a way that is easy to find and use.
- Knowledge should be continually updated and improved.
- Knowledge should be shared and accessible to everyone.

### Structure and Function of Cell Membrane

Cell membranes are thin, semi-permeable barriers that separate the inside of a cell from its external environment. They are composed of a phospholipid bilayer, which is a double layer of phospholipids, and embedded proteins.

**Function**

Cell membranes serve several important functions, including:

- Regulating the passage of molecules in and out of the cell
- Providing structural support
- Serving as a recognition site for other cells
- Acting as a receptor for hormones and other signaling molecules
- Controlling the movement of organelles within the cell

**Structure**

Cell membranes are composed of two layers of phospholipids, which are molecules that have a hydrophilic (water-loving) head and two hydrophobic (water-hating) tails. The hydrophobic tails of the phospholipids are arranged in a way that they form a barrier, preventing the passage of most molecules. Embedded within this barrier are proteins, which can act as channels or pumps to allow certain molecules to pass through.
