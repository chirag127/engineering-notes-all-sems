### Definition of Cloud

* Cloud computing is a type of computing that relies on shared computing resources delivered over the internet.

* Cloud computing eliminates the need for users to install and maintain software on their own computers.

* Cloud computing enables users to scale their computing needs up or down as needed, making it an efficient and cost-effective way to meet their computing needs.
* Cloud computing is used by businesses of all sizes, from small startups to large enterprises, to reduce costs and increase efficiency.
