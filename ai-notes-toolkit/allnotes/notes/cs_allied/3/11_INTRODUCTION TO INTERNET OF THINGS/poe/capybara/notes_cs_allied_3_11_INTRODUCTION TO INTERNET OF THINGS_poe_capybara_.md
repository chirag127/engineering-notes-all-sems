Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items that are embedded with sensors, software, and network connectivity, which enables them to collect and exchange data. Here are some important points to understand the concept of IoT:

- IoT is a revolutionary technology that connects physical devices to the internet, enabling them to communicate with each other and with humans. This technology has the potential to transform the way we live and work.

- The devices in IoT can range from simple sensors to complex machines. These devices collect data from their environment and send it to the cloud for processing and analysis. The data can be used to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance the user experience.

- The three main components of IoT are devices, networks, and platforms. Devices are the physical objects that have sensors and connectivity. Networks are the communication channels that connect the devices. Platforms are the software systems that manage the devices and the data they collect.

- IoT is being used in various industries, including healthcare, transportation, manufacturing, agriculture, and smart homes. In healthcare, IoT devices are used to monitor patients remotely and provide personalized care. In transportation, IoT devices are used to track vehicles and optimize routes.

- Security and privacy are major concerns in the IoT ecosystem. As more devices get connected to the internet, the risk of cyber attacks increases. It is important to implement security measures such as encryption and authentication to protect the IoT devices and the data they collect.

- The future of IoT is bright, with new technologies such as 5G and edge computing enabling faster and more efficient communication between devices. With the growth of IoT, we can expect to see more smart cities, autonomous vehicles, and connected homes in the coming years.

In conclusion, IoT is a game-changing technology that has the potential to transform the way we live and work. It has numerous applications in various industries and can bring significant benefits such as improved efficiency and reduced costs. However, it is important to address the security and privacy concerns associated with IoT to ensure its successful adoption.

## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

### Sensors
- Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment.
- They can be analog or digital.
- Analog sensors measure a continuous range of values, while digital sensors only measure discrete values.
- Examples of sensors include temperature sensors, humidity sensors, light sensors, and motion sensors.

### Digital Sensors
- Digital sensors are sensors that output data in a digital format.
- They are commonly used in IoT applications because they are easy to interface with microcontrollers.
- Examples of digital sensors include digital temperature sensors, digital humidity sensors, and digital motion sensors.

### Actuators
- Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical action.
- They can be used to control various devices in an IoT system.
- Examples of actuators include motors, solenoids, and relays.

### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology
- RFID technology is used to identify and track objects using radio waves.
- It consists of a reader and a tag.
- The reader sends out a radio signal, which is received by the tag.
- The tag responds with its unique identification number, which is then read by the reader.

### Wireless Sensor Networks
- Wireless sensor networks (WSNs) consist of multiple sensors that communicate with each other wirelessly.
- They are commonly used in IoT applications because they can cover a large area and provide real-time data.
- Examples of WSNs include Zigbee, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), and Wi-Fi.

### Participatory Sensing Technology

Hybrid protocols combine the best of both worlds by allowing devices to transmit data whenever they have data to send, but also reserving time slots to prevent collisions.

- **MAC protocols for high-throughput IoT devices:** Some IoT applications require high data rates, such as video streaming or real-time monitoring. - **MAC protocols for interference-prone environments:** In some IoT environments, such as industrial settings or urban areas, there may be a lot of interference from other wireless devices or sources. #### Advantages of Survey Routing Protocols

#### Disadvantages of Survey Routing Protocols
