### Elicitation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

- Elicitation is the first step of the Requirement Engineering process, which aims to collect, analyze, and document the requirements of a software system from various stakeholders.
- Elicitation helps the analyst to gain knowledge about the problem domain, the needs and expectations of the customers and users, and the constraints and assumptions that affect the system .
- Elicitation involves various techniques, such as interviews, questionnaires, surveys, observation, brainstorming, prototyping, use cases, scenarios, and workshops  .
- Elicitation can be challenging due to factors such as communication gaps, conflicting or incomplete requirements, changing requirements, and stakeholder availability .
- Elicitation results in the production and validation of a Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document, which is a formal description of the purpose and environment of the software system .
- The SRS document defines the functional and non-functional requirements, the scope and boundaries, the quality attributes, the user interface, the data model, and the system behavior of the software system .
- The SRS document serves as a contract between the customer and the developer, a guide for the design and implementation, a basis for testing and verification, and a reference for maintenance and evolution of the software system .
- The SRS document should be clear, consistent, complete, correct, verifiable, modifiable, and traceable .
