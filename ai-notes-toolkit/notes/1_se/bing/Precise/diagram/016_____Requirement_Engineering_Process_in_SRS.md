### Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

```
+----------------+             +----------------+
| Elicitation    |------------>| Analysis       |
+----------------+             +----------------+
                                  |
                                  |
                                  v
                            +----------------+
                            | Specification  |
                            +----------------+
                                  |
                                  |
                                  v
                            +----------------+
                            | Validation     |
                            +----------------+
```

The Requirement Engineering Process in SRS consists of four main steps: Elicitation, Analysis, Specification, and Validation. During the Elicitation phase, requirements are gathered from stakeholders. In the Analysis phase, the requirements are analyzed for consistency and completeness. In the Specification phase, the requirements are documented in a Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document. Finally, in the Validation phase, the requirements are validated to ensure that they meet the needs of the stakeholders.
