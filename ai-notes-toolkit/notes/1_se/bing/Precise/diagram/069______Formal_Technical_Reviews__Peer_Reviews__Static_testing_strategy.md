#### Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews) Static testing strategy

Here is an ASCII diagram that represents the process of Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews) Static testing strategy:

```
+---------------------+
|   Review Planning   |
+----------+----------+
           |
           v
+----------+----------+
|   Review Meeting    |
+----------+----------+
           |
           v
+----------+----------+
|   Rework & Follow-up|
+----------+----------+
           |
           v
+----------+----------+
|   Review Reporting  |
+----------+----------+
           |
           v
+----------+----------+
|   Review Completion |
+---------------------+
```

The process starts with **Review Planning**, where the objectives, scope, and participants of the review are determined. Next, the **Review Meeting** takes place, where the participants discuss the work product and identify any issues or defects. After the meeting, any necessary **Rework & Follow-up** is performed to address the identified issues. The results of the review are then documented in the **Review Reporting** phase. Finally, the review is considered complete in the **Review Completion** phase.
