### SEI-CMM Model in SRS

The SEI-CMM (Software Engineering Institute - Capability Maturity Model) is a model used to assess the maturity of software development processes. It consists of five levels, each representing a different stage of maturity.

Here is an ASCII diagram representing the SEI-CMM model:

```
+-----------------+
| Level 5:        |
| Optimizing      |
+-----------------+
         |
+-----------------+
| Level 4:        |
| Managed         |
+-----------------+
         |
+-----------------+
| Level 3:        |
| Defined         |
+-----------------+
         |
+-----------------+
| Level 2:        |
| Repeatable      |
+-----------------+
         |
+-----------------+
| Level 1:        |
| Initial         |
+-----------------+
```

At Level 1, the Initial level, processes are ad hoc and unstructured. At Level 2, the Repeatable level, basic project management processes are established. At Level 3, the Defined level, processes are standardized and documented. At Level 4, the Managed level, processes are measured and controlled. At Level 5, the Optimizing level, processes are continuously improved.
