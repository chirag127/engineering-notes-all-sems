### Iterative Enhancement Models in SDLC

```
+----------------+
|  Requirements  |
+-------+--------+
        |
        v
+-------+--------+
| Design & Plan  |
+-------+--------+
        |
        v
+-------+--------+
|   Development  |
+-------+--------+
        |
        v
+-------+--------+
|     Testing    |
+-------+--------+
        |
        v
+-------+--------+
| Implementation |
+-------+--------+
        |
        v
+-------+--------+
|  Maintenance   |
+-------+--------+
        |
        v
+----------------+
| Back to Design |
+----------------+
```
The Iterative Enhancement Model in SDLC is an approach to software development where the software is developed and delivered in small, incremental stages. The process starts with the requirements gathering and analysis phase, followed by the design and planning phase. The development phase is where the actual coding takes place, followed by the testing phase to ensure that the software meets the requirements and is free of defects. The software is then implemented and released to the users. The maintenance phase involves fixing any issues that arise and making any necessary updates. The process then goes back to the design phase to plan for the next iteration of development. This cycle continues until the software is complete and meets all the requirements. This model allows for flexibility and the ability to make changes and improvements throughout the development process.
