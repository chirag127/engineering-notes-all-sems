ISO 9000 is a series of standards that define, establish, and maintain an effective quality assurance system for manufacturing and service industries. The ISO 9000 standard is based on the idea of a process approach to quality management. The standard is divided into several parts, including ISO 9001, which specifies the requirements for a quality management system.
