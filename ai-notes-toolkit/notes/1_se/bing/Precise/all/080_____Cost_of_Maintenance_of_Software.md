### Cost of Maintenance of Software

1. The cost of maintenance of software refers to the expenses associated with keeping a software system operational and up-to-date after its initial development and deployment.
2. Maintenance activities can include fixing bugs, adding new features, improving performance, and ensuring compatibility with new hardware and software.
3. The cost of maintenance can vary depending on several factors, including the complexity of the software, the frequency of updates, and the availability of skilled personnel.
4. It is estimated that the cost of maintenance can account for up to 90% of the total cost of ownership of a software system over its lifetime.
5. To minimize the cost of maintenance, it is important to follow best practices in software development, such as writing modular and maintainable code, and thoroughly testing the software before deployment.
6. One way to remember the factors that affect the cost of maintenance is the mnemonic **CAF**: **C**omplexity, **A**vailability of skilled personnel, and **F**requency of updates.
