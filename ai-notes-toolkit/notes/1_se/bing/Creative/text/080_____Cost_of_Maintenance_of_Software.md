### Cost of Maintenance of Software

- Software maintenance is the process of modifying, updating, or correcting software after it has been delivered to the customer or deployed in the operational environment.
- Software maintenance can be classified into four types: corrective, adaptive, perfective, and preventive.
- Corrective maintenance is the process of fixing errors or defects in the software that affect its functionality, reliability, or performance.
- Adaptive maintenance is the process of modifying the software to cope with changes in the environment, such as new hardware, operating systems, or standards.
- Perfective maintenance is the process of enhancing the software to improve its quality, usability, or functionality, such as adding new features, improving user interface, or optimizing performance.
- Preventive maintenance is the process of modifying the software to prevent potential problems or errors, such as improving documentation, code readability, or testing coverage.
- The cost of software maintenance depends on various factors, such as the size, complexity, quality, and age of the software, the type and frequency of changes, the availability and skill of the maintenance staff, and the tools and methods used for maintenance.
- According to some studies, the cost of software maintenance can range from 40% to 90% of the total cost of software development, depending on the application domain, the software lifecycle model, and the maintenance strategy.
- Some of the benefits of software maintenance are: increasing customer satisfaction, extending software lifespan, reducing software risks, and improving software competitiveness.
