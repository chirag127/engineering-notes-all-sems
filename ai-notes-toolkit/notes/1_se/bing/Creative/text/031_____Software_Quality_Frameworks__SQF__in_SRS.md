### Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) in SRS

- Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) are sets of standards, guidelines, and best practices that aim to ensure the quality of software products and processes.
- SQF can be applied to different aspects of software development, such as requirements, design, coding, testing, maintenance, and management.
- SQF can help software developers and stakeholders to define, measure, improve, and assure the quality of software products and processes.
- SQF can also help software developers and stakeholders to communicate and collaborate effectively, and to comply with relevant regulations and customer expectations.
- Some examples of SQF are:

  - ISO/IEC 25000:2014, also known as SQuaRE (Software product Quality Requirements and Evaluation), which provides a common reference framework for software quality requirements and evaluation.
  - IEEE 730:2014, also known as Software Quality Assurance Processes, which provides a standard process for software quality assurance activities throughout the software life cycle.
  - CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration), which provides a maturity model for software process improvement and assessment.
  - ISO/IEC 12207:2017, also known as Software Life Cycle Processes, which provides a standard framework for software life cycle processes and activities.
  - ISO/IEC 15504:2003, also known as SPICE (Software Process Improvement and Capability Determination), which provides a standard method for software process assessment and improvement.
