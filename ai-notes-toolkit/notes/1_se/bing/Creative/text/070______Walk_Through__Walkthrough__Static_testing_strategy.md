#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

- A walkthrough is a type of static testing technique that involves a review of a document or a code by a group of peers or experts.
- The main purpose of a walkthrough is to find defects, improve quality, and gain feedback or suggestions from the reviewers.
- A walkthrough is usually informal and less structured than other types of reviews, such as inspections or audits.
- A walkthrough typically follows these steps:
  - The author of the document or code prepares a draft version and invites the reviewers for a walkthrough session.
  - The author presents the document or code to the reviewers and explains the main objectives, assumptions, and logic behind it.
  - The reviewers ask questions, raise issues, or provide comments to the author during or after the presentation.
  - The author records the feedback and takes notes of the defects or improvement areas identified by the reviewers.
  - The author revises the document or code based on the feedback and sends the updated version to the reviewers for confirmation or approval.
  - The author and the reviewers close the walkthrough session after all the defects are resolved or agreed upon.
- Some of the benefits of a walkthrough are:
  - It helps to detect defects early in the development process, which reduces the cost and effort of fixing them later.
  - It improves the communication and collaboration among the team members and stakeholders, which enhances the quality and clarity of the document or code.
  - It provides an opportunity for the author to learn from the reviewers' expertise and experience, which improves the skills and knowledge of the author.
  - It increases the confidence and satisfaction of the author and the reviewers, as they can verify that the document or code meets the requirements and standards.
