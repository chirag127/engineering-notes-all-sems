
#### Flow Charts in Software Design

* Flow charts are diagrams used to represent the flow of data and information within a system.
* They are used to plan, document, and communicate the logic of a program or process. 
* Flow charts are composed of symbols, arrows, and text that represent the flow of information and the decisions that need to be made.
* Flow charts are used to help identify errors and optimize the process.
* Flow charts can be used to visualize algorithms, to document business processes, and to plan projects.
* Flow charts can also be used to troubleshoot problems and to plan and analyze processes.
