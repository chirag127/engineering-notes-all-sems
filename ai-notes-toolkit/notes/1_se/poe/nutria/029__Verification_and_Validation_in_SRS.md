
### Verification and Validation in SRS

1. Verification is the process of determining whether the developed software meets the specified requirements. It is done through reviews, meetings, and walkthroughs.

2. Validation is the process of determining whether the developed software meets the user's needs. It is done through testing, inspections, and audits.

3. Reviews are the most common form of verification. They involve a structured examination of the work products by peers.

4. Walkthroughs are a form of review in which the author of the work product leads a group of peers through the document.

5. Testing is the most common form of validation. It involves executing the software with test data and validating the results.

6. Inspections are a form of validation in which the work products are examined by peers to identify defects.

7. Audits are a form of validation in which the work products are examined to ensure they meet the specified requirements.
