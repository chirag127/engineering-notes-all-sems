
### Analysis in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. Requirement engineering is the process of gathering, analyzing, and documenting the requirements of a system.

2. It is an important part of the software development process and involves the identification of all stakeholders and their needs.

3. The goal of requirement engineering is to ensure that the system meets the needs of the stakeholders and provides a solution to the problem.

4. The process of requirement engineering consists of several steps, including requirement elicitation, analysis, specification, validation, and management.

5. Requirement elicitation is the process of gathering information from stakeholders in order to identify the requirements of the system.

6. Requirement analysis is the process of analyzing the information gathered from the stakeholders and determining the requirements of the system.

7. Requirement specification is the process of documenting the requirements of the system.

8. Requirement validation is the process of ensuring that the requirements are correct and can be implemented.

9. Requirement management is the process of tracking and managing changes to the requirements of the system.
