
### Low Level Design in Software Design

1. Low level design is a step in the software design process that occurs after the high level design. It is concerned with the detailed design of individual software components and the connections between them.

2. Low level design involves the definition of data structures, algorithms, and interfaces. It also involves the selection of appropriate software development tools and technologies.

3. Low level design is a bottom-up approach, starting with the design of individual components and then progressing to the design of larger components and eventually to the design of the entire system.

4. Low level design should focus on ensuring that the individual components of a system are designed to meet the requirements of the system. This includes ensuring that the components are efficient, reliable, and secure.

5. Low level design should also ensure that the system is designed to be maintainable and extensible. This includes designing the system to be modular, so that individual components can be replaced or modified without affecting the entire system.
