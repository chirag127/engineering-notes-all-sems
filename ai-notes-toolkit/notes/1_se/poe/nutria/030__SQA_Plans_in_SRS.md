
### SQA Plans in SRS

1. SQA (Software Quality Assurance) plans are a set of processes designed to ensure that a software product meets the highest possible quality standards. 
2. SQA plans should include: 
    - Establishing quality objectives 
    - Defining quality metrics 
    - Developing test plans 
    - Performing testing 
    - Evaluating results 
3. SQA plans should also include processes for identifying and addressing defects, as well as processes for tracking and reporting on the progress of the project. 
4. SQA plans should be tailored to the specific needs of the project, as well as the resources available. 
5. SQA plans should be regularly reviewed and updated to ensure that they remain relevant and effective.
