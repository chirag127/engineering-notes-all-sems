
### Documentation in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

- Documentation in requirement engineering process is the process of creating and maintaining documents that describe the requirements of a system.
- The documents created during this process contain information such as the system's purpose, functional and non-functional requirements, constraints, and design considerations.
- The goal of documentation in requirement engineering process is to ensure that all stakeholders have a clear understanding of the system's purpose and requirements.
- The documentation should be created in a way that is easy to understand and maintain.
- The documentation should be kept up-to-date as changes to the system are made.
- The documents should be reviewed and approved by all stakeholders before the system is deployed.
- The documents should be stored in a secure location and backed up regularly.
