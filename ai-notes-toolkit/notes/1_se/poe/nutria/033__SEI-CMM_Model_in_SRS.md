
### SEI-CMM Model in SRS

* The SEI-CMM Model is a software engineering process model developed by the Software Engineering Institute (SEI) at Carnegie Mellon University. 
* It is used to measure the maturity of an organization's software engineering process. 
* The model is composed of five levels, each of which has specific goals and objectives that must be achieved before progressing to the next level. 
* The five levels are: Initial, Repeatable, Defined, Managed, and Optimizing. 
* At each level, organizations must demonstrate that they have achieved certain goals and objectives before progressing to the next level. 
* For example, at the Initial level, organizations must demonstrate that they have established a basic process for software development. 
* At the Repeatable level, organizations must demonstrate that they have established a process for managing and controlling software development activities. 
* At the Defined level, organizations must demonstrate that they have established a process for ensuring quality and consistency in software development. 
* At the Managed level, organizations must demonstrate that they have established a process for measuring and improving software development processes. 
* At the Optimizing level, organizations must demonstrate that they have established a process for continuously improving software development processes.
