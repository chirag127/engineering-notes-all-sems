
### Evolutionary Development Models in SDLC

1. Evolutionary development models are software development life cycle (SDLC) models that involve iterative development of a software product. This type of model allows for the development of a product to be broken down into smaller, manageable parts. 

2. Evolutionary development models are iterative in nature, which means that each cycle of development builds on the previous one. This allows for the development team to identify and address any issues that may arise during the development process.

3. These models are particularly useful for software projects that require frequent changes or updates. They allow for the development team to quickly and easily make changes to the product without having to start from scratch.

4. An advantage of evolutionary development models is that they allow for the development team to make changes without having to go back and re-do large portions of the code. This makes them more efficient and cost effective than other software development models.

5. Another advantage of evolutionary development models is that they allow for the development team to test and evaluate the product as it is being developed. This allows for the team to identify and address any issues that may arise during the development process.

6. A disadvantage of evolutionary development models is that they can be difficult to manage. The development team must be able to keep track of the changes that have been made and ensure that the product is functioning as expected. Additionally, the development team must be able to accurately estimate the amount of time and resources required for each iteration of development.
