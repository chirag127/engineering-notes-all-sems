
### Review and Management of User Needs in Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. Requirements Engineering (RE) is the process of identifying, documenting, and managing the requirements of a system. 
2. The goal of RE is to ensure that the system meets the user's needs and expectations. 
3. User needs are the desires and expectations of the users and stakeholders of a system. 
4. User needs should be identified, documented, and managed in the requirements engineering process. 
5. Requirements should be derived from user needs in order to ensure that the system meets the user's expectations.
6. User needs should be documented in a System Requirements Specification (SRS). 
7. The SRS should include a section on user needs that details the user's expectations and desired outcomes. 
8. The SRS should also include a section on requirements management that outlines how user needs will be tracked and managed throughout the project. 
9. Requirements should be reviewed and validated regularly to ensure that they are still relevant and meet the user's needs. 
10. The SRS should be reviewed and updated as needed to ensure that the system meets the user's needs.
