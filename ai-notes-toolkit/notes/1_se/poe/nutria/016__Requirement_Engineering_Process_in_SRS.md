
### Requirement Engineering Process in SRS

1. Requirement engineering is the process of understanding and analyzing user needs and defining the scope of a system, product, or service.

2. The goal of requirement engineering is to identify, document, and manage the requirements of a system, product, or service.

3. The process of requirement engineering typically involves identifying stakeholders, gathering requirements, analyzing requirements, modeling requirements, validating requirements, and managing requirements.

4. Stakeholders are individuals or groups of individuals who have an interest in the system, product, or service under development.

5. Requirements gathering involves identifying and documenting the requirements of the system, product, or service. This includes understanding the problem domain, the user needs, and the constraints of the system.

6. Requirements analysis involves analyzing the requirements to ensure that they are complete, consistent, and feasible.

7. Requirements modeling involves creating a model of the system, product, or service to represent the requirements. This can be done using a variety of techniques such as use cases, activity diagrams, class diagrams, and state diagrams.

8. Requirements validation involves validating the requirements to ensure that they meet the user needs and are consistent with the problem domain.

9. Requirements management involves managing the requirements throughout the development process. This includes tracking changes to the requirements, resolving conflicts between requirements, and managing the scope of the system, product, or service.
