### ISO 9000 Models in SRS

ISO 9000 is a series of international standards for quality management and assurance. The ISO 9000 family of standards provides a framework for businesses to achieve consistent and high-quality results, and it is widely used in software development to ensure that software products meet customer requirements.

In software requirements specification (SRS), ISO 9000 models are used to ensure that the software product meets the customer's quality requirements. Below are some of the ISO 9000 models that are commonly used in SRS:

1. ISO 9001: This model specifies the requirements for a quality management system (QMS) in an organization. It focuses on customer satisfaction and continuous improvement, and it is used to ensure that the organization's processes are consistent and meet customer requirements.

2. ISO 9002: This model is similar to ISO 9001, but it is specifically for organizations that do not design or develop their products. It is used to ensure that the organization's processes for production and delivery are consistent and meet customer requirements.

3. ISO 9003: This model is also similar to ISO 9001, but it is specifically for organizations that do not design, develop, or produce their products. It is used to ensure that the organization's processes for inspection and testing are consistent and meet customer requirements.

4. ISO 9004: This model provides guidelines for organizations to improve their overall performance and achieve sustained success. It focuses on the continual improvement of the organization's processes and the satisfaction of all stakeholders, including customers, employees, and shareholders.

In SRS, ISO 9000 models are used to ensure that the software product meets the customer's quality requirements. By following the guidelines provided by these models, software development teams can ensure that their processes are consistent and meet customer requirements. This, in turn, leads to higher customer satisfaction, improved product quality, and increased business success.
