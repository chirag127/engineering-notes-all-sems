#### Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews) Static testing strategy

Static testing is a critical component of the software development process that helps identify defects early in the development lifecycle. Formal Technical Reviews, also known as Peer Reviews, is a static testing strategy that involves reviewing project deliverables and source code to identify defects, improve quality, and ensure compliance with project requirements. Here are some key points to consider when implementing Formal Technical Reviews:

1. **Objectives:** The primary objective of Formal Technical Reviews is to identify defects early in the development lifecycle and ensure that software requirements are met. Peer reviews also help improve software quality, reduce development costs, and increase productivity.

2. **Participants:** Peer reviews typically involve a team of subject matter experts, including developers, quality assurance professionals, and technical writers. Participants should be selected based on their experience, expertise, and knowledge of the project requirements.

3. **Roles and Responsibilities:** Each participant in a peer review has a specific role and responsibility. The author of the deliverable or source code being reviewed is responsible for addressing any defects identified. The reviewer is responsible for identifying defects and providing feedback to the author. The moderator is responsible for managing the review process and ensuring that all defects are addressed.

4. **Review Process:** The review process should be well-defined and documented. The review should be conducted in a formal setting, with all participants prepared and focused on the review. The moderator should provide guidance and ensure that the review is conducted efficiently and effectively.

5. **Defect Management:** Defects identified during the review process should be documented and tracked until they are resolved. The author of the deliverable or source code is responsible for addressing the defects and providing a resolution. The moderator should ensure that all defects are tracked and resolved in a timely manner.

6. **Tools and Techniques:** Various tools and techniques can be used during the review process to improve efficiency and effectiveness. Examples include checklists, templates, automated review tools, and collaboration software.

7. **Metrics and Reporting:** Metrics should be collected and reported to evaluate the effectiveness of the review process. Examples of metrics include defect density, defect removal efficiency, and review effectiveness.

In conclusion, Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews) is a critical static testing strategy that helps identify defects early in the development lifecycle, improve software quality, and ensure compliance with project requirements. By following a well-defined and documented review process and leveraging tools and techniques, organizations can improve their software development processes and deliver high-quality software to their customers.
