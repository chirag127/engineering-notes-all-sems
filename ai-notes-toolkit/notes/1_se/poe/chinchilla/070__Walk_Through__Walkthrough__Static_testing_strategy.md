#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

Walk Through, also known as a walkthrough, is a static testing technique that involves reviewing a software product or system manually. It is an informal method of detecting defects, errors, and potential issues in the software development process. In this technique, a group of people, including developers, testers, and end-users, gather to analyze the software and provide feedback.

Here are some key points to keep in mind while performing a Walk Through:

1. The walkthrough process should be well-defined and structured so that all the participants understand their roles and responsibilities.

2. The software product or system should be reviewed thoroughly, and any potential issues should be identified and reported.

3. The walkthrough should be conducted in a formal and professional manner, and all the participants should maintain a constructive and positive attitude.

4. The participants should be encouraged to ask questions and clarify any doubts they may have about the software.

5. The feedback provided during the walkthrough should be documented carefully and shared with the relevant stakeholders.

6. The identified defects and issues should be addressed and corrected as soon as possible to avoid any potential problems in the future.

7. The walkthrough can be conducted at various stages of the software development process, such as design, coding, and testing.

8. The walkthrough can be done manually or using automated tools, depending on the complexity and size of the software.

9. The walkthrough is an effective technique for detecting defects and issues in the software, and it can help improve the overall quality of the product.

In conclusion, Walk Through is an essential static testing strategy that can help identify defects, errors, and potential issues in the software development process. It is an effective way to improve the overall quality of the product and ensure that it meets the requirements and expectations of the stakeholders. By following the key points mentioned above, the walkthrough process can be conducted efficiently and effectively.
