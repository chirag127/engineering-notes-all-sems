### Need for Maintenance and Maintenance Planning

1. Preventive maintenance: Regular maintenance of equipment helps to identify and rectify minor faults or wear and tear before they lead to major failures. This increases the operational life of the equipment and reduces downtime.

2. Corrective maintenance: Unforeseen breakdowns and failures can be rectified through corrective maintenance. This helps to bring back the equipment to working condition and minimize loss of production.

3. Maintenance scheduling: Maintenance work has to be properly scheduled such that it does not interfere with normal production activities. The equipment under maintenance should be brought back to operation as soon as the maintenance work is completed. Proper scheduling helps achieve this.

Maintenance planning involves the following steps:

1. Data collection: Operational data such as number of hours for which equipment functioned without failure, types of past failures, etc. are collected. This helps in assessing the maintenance requirements of the equipment.

2. Evaluation: The collected data is evaluated to analyze the maintenance needs of the equipment. The types of maintenance required are identified for each equipment.

3. Maintenance scheduling: Based on the evaluation, a maintenance schedule is prepared, specifying the types of maintenance, frequency of maintenance, and time required for maintenance for each equipment. The schedule is prepared such that it does not disrupt production.

4. Resource planning: Resources such as laborers, spare parts, and other supplies required for maintenance are planned and made available as per the maintenance schedule.

5. Implementation and monitoring: The maintenance work is carried out as per the schedule and resources planned. The work is monitored to ensure completion within the scheduled time and cost. The equipment is brought back to operation immediately after maintenance.

The maintenance plan may be revised based on the monitoring results. This helps to achieve an efficient maintenance system.
