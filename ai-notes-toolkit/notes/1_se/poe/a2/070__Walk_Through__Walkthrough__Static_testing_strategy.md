#### Walk Through (Walkthrough) Static testing strategy

1. Walkthroughs are informal reviews of a software product conducted manually.
2. The goal is to identify potential defects and gather feedback.
3. Conducted by the author of the product along with other team members.
4. The author "walks" the team through the product, explaining functionality and decisions.
5. The team then provides feedback, comments, and suggestions.
6. Can uncover logical flaws, inconsistencies, and user experience issues.
7. Low cost but can miss deeper code-level issues.
8. Useful for reviewing designs, requirements, and high-level documentation.
9. Walkthrough findings are discussed and often addressed before more rigorous testing begins.
