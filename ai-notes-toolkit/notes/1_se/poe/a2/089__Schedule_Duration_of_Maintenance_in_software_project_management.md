### Schedule/Duration of Maintenance in software project management

- Maintenance phase starts once the software is delivered to the customer. It includes:
- Corrective maintenance: Fixing defects/bugs found by users.
- Adaptive maintenance: Modifying the software to adapt to changes in the environment. For example, changing interfaces to new system software releases.
- Perfective maintenance: Refining the software to improve performance or other attributes.
- Maintenance is an ongoing process as long as the software is used.
- The maintenance schedule and duration depends on:
- Number and severity of bugs/defects found.
- Frequency of changes in the environment requiring adaptation.
- Need for continued improvements requested by customers.
- Available maintenance resources in terms of staff and budget.
- The maintenance phase can take from a few months to many years based on the software usage. Proper maintenance planning and cost estimation is important for long term software projects.
