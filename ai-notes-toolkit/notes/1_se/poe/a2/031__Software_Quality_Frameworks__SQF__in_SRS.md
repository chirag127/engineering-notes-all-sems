### Software Quality Frameworks (SQF) in SRS

1. McCall's Quality Model - It focuses on 3 quality factors: Correctness, Reliability, and Efficiency. Correctness ensures the software meets specs. Reliability ensures consistent intended performance. Efficiency ensures optimal resource utilization.
2. Dromey's Quality Model - It proposes 6 quality factors: Portability, Reusability, Understandability, Conciseness, Consistency, and Maintainability. Portability refers to adaptability to new environments. Reusability refers to leveraging existing components. Understandability, conciseness, consistency, and maintainability relate to readability and modifiability.
3. ISO 9126 Standard - It defines 6 quality characteristics: Functionality, Reliability, Usability, Efficiency, Maintainability, and Portability. Functionality refers to capabilities as per specs. Reliability refers to consistency in performance. Usability refers to ease of use. Efficiency refers to resources utilized. Maintainability refers to ease of modification. Portability refers to adaptability to new environments.

The SRS should specify what quality factors and/or characteristics are relevant to the software being developed. The quality factors/characteristics chosen should be measurable through testing procedures outlined in the SRS. Specifying relevant quality frameworks in the SRS ensures a systematic approach to measuring and evaluating software quality.
