### Software Re-Engineering (SR) of Software

1. Reverse Engineering - Analyzing an existing software system to identify its components and their interrelationships, and create representations of the system in another form or at a higher level of abstraction.
2. Re-documentation - Updating outdated or insufficient documentation. This is important as the original documentation may be lost, out of date, incomplete or inaccurate.
3. Restructuring - Reorganizing a software system to improve qualities such as modularity, readability and maintainability without changing its external behavior.
4. Forward Engineering - Using re-engineered components or their representations to build a new version of the system that is more maintainable and adaptable to new requirements.

The goal of software re-engineering is to reduce software maintenance costs, allow software systems to evolve and adapt to new environments or changed requirements, and preserve or increase software quality. It can be useful when the existing software has become difficult to maintain and enhance.
