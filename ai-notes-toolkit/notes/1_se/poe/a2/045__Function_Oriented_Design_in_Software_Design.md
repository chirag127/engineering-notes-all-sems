#### Function Oriented Design in Software Design

1. Focus on Functions: In function oriented design, the primary focus is on functions or operations of the system. The system is viewed as a collection of functions that transform inputs into outputs.
2. Identify Key Functions: The key steps are to first identify the major functions of the system and then to organize the system around performing these functions. The functions are identified based on the requirements and objectives of the system.
3. Divide into Sub-functions: Once the major functions are identified, each function is further divided into sub-functions. This decomposition continues until the functions are simple enough to be easily understood and implemented.
4. Bind with Control: In addition to functions, the control flow that coordinates the functions is also defined. The focus is on transforming the system into a collection of cooperating functions.
5. Reduce Side-effects: Function oriented design emphasizes making functions independent, standalone and with reduced side-effects. This makes the functions more reusable, testable and maintainable.
6. Benefits: Some of the key benefits of function oriented design are:
- It produces systems that are modular, flexible and extensible.
- It enables division of work into smaller manageable parts.
- It increases reuse of functions.
- It emphasizes loosely-coupled functions that can work with other functions.
- It results in systems that are easier to understand, test and maintain.
