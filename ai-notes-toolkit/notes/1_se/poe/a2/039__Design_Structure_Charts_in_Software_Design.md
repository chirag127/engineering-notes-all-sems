#### Design Structure Charts in Software Design

1. Design Structure Matrix (DSM) is a simple, compact representation of dependencies between components or tasks.
2. It is represented by a square matrix with components or tasks in rows and columns.
3. The matrix entries show the dependency of the row component on the column component.
4. DSM helps in visualizing, analyzing and managing dependencies between components or tasks.
5. It aids in scheduling, risk analysis, division of work and managing complexity.
6. The dependencies can be updated as the design progresses to track dependency changes.
7. DSM can be converted into a Graph for further analysis of dependencies.
8. dendograms can be generated from the dependency strength for hierarchical clustering of components or tasks.
9. DSM is a useful technique for complex software or systems design to analyze, understand and manage dependencies between different parts.
