### Software Risk Analysis and Management in software project management.

1. Identify potential risks:
- Technical challenges and complexities
- Tight deadlines and timelines
- Budget overruns
- lack of resources
- Requirements changing

2. Assess the impact of risks:
- Estimate the likelihood of the risk occurring (high/medium/low)
- Estimate the impact on project objectives if the risk occurs (high/medium/low)

3. Mitigate the risks:
- Avoid the risk if possible
- Reduce the likelihood or impact of the risk
- Transfer the risk to a third party (eg. insurance)
- Accept the risk after evaluating the cost of mitigation vs impact of occurrence

4. Continuous monitoring:
- Re-evaluate risks at regular intervals or when there are changes
- Track identified risks and mitigation effectiveness
- Identify and mitigate new risks

The key steps in software risk analysis and management are identification of potential risks, assessment of risks and their impacts, mitigating the risks and continuous monitoring and re-evaluation of risks. A systematic risk management approach helps minimize threats to software projects and achieve objectives.
