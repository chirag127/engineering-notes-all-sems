#### Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews) Static testing strategy

1. Formal Technical Reviews, also known as Peer Reviews, are a type of static testing technique.
2. They are a systematic examination of a software work product by a team of qualified individuals to identify and report defects and areas for improvement.
3. The key goals of a formal technical review are:
- To uncover defects early in the lifecycle.
- To ensure that standards and procedures have been followed.
- To verify correctness, completeness, clarity, consistency, portability and Maintainability of the software.
4. The steps involved in a formal technical review process are:
- Planning and scheduling the review meeting.
- Kick off - Present overview of the material to be reviewed.
- Individual preparation - Reviewers study the material and identifies potential issues.
- Meeting - Facilitator guides the group through the identified issues and discussions.
- Rework and Follow up - Author incorporates the changes and reviews again if needed.
5. The benefits of formal technical reviews are:
- Detection of defects early in the lifecycle which reduces cost of fixing them later.
- Opportunity for training and feedback.
- Improved software quality and reliability.
- Spreading knowledge about standards and lessons learned across teams.
