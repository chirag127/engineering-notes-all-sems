### Prototype Model in SDLC

1. Prototyping is a software development model in which prototypes are developed to provide users an idea of how the final product will look like and work.
2. It involves iterative development of prototypes where each prototype is refined based on user feedback.
3. Prototypes can be of two types:

- Throwaway Prototypes: Prototypes that are eventually discarded and not part of the final product. They are developed to help understand user requirements.
- Evolutionary Prototypes: Prototypes that evolve into the final system through iterative refinements. Part of the final system is developed from the prototype.

4. The key steps involved in Prototype Model are:

- Identify basic requirements and assumptions
- Develop initial prototype
- Review prototype and gather feedback
- Refine prototype
- Repeat above two steps until acceptable prototype is achieved
- Deploy the system

5. The advantages of Prototype Model are:

- Users get an early idea of the system and can provide valuable feedback.
- Risks can be identified early and eliminated.
- Customizations can be incorporated easily.

6. The disadvantages of Prototype Model are:

- Insufficient analysis may lead to rework as hidden requirements may surface later.
- The cost of making changes increases as the process progresses.
- The model does not work well for large and complex systems.
