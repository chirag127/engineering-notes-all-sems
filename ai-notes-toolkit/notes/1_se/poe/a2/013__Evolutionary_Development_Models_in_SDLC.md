### Evolutionary Development Models in SDLC

1. Incremental Model: In incremental model, requirements are broken down into increments or parts. Each part is delivered in iterations. The key advantage is that it provides strong customer interaction and allows for risk handling. The delivery is divided into increments and each increment adds functions to the software.

2. Spiral Model: The spiral model focuses on risk analysis and iterative development. Each iteration involves a series of steps including planning, risk analysis, engineering and evaluation. Software is developed in iterations to gradually build a system. It allows for incremental releases of the product, enabling revaluation of requirements and design.

3. Agile Model: The agile model involves iterative and incremental approach. Requirements and solutions evolve through collaboration between self-organizing teams. It encourages adaptive planning, evolutionary development and delivery, a time-boxed iterative approach, and encourages rapid and flexible response to change. Key methods include Scrum, XP, Kanban, etc.
