
### Risk Planning in SPM

1. Risk planning is an essential part of any successful project management strategy. It involves identifying, analyzing and responding to potential risks associated with a project.

2. Risk planning should be done early in the project planning process, as it can help ensure that potential risks are identified and addressed before they can cause problems.

3. Risk planning involves identifying potential risks, analyzing their impact, and developing strategies to mitigate them. This can include developing contingency plans, identifying alternative solutions, and assessing the cost of potential risks.

4. Risk planning should also consider the impact of external factors such as changes in the economy or the political environment.

5. Risk planning should also consider the impact of internal factors such as changes in personnel or the availability of resources.

6. Risk planning should also consider the impact of technology, as new technologies can introduce new risks.

7. Finally, risk planning should consider the impact of changes in customer needs and expectations, as these can lead to unexpected risks.
