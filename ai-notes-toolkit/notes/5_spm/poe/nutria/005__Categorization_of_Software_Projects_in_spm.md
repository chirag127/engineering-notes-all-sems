
### Categorization of Software Projects in SPM

1. Software projects can be categorized in a number of ways, such as by size, complexity, and risk.

2. Size-based categorization of software projects divides them into small, medium, and large projects. Small projects typically involve fewer resources and less complexity than larger projects.

3. Complexity-based categorization divides software projects into simple, medium, and complex projects. Simple projects involve fewer activities and less complexity than complex projects.

4. Risk-based categorization divides software projects into low, medium, and high risk. Low risk projects involve fewer risks and less complexity than high risk projects.

5. Software projects can also be categorized based on the type of software development process used. For example, a waterfall process is typically used for small, simple projects, while an agile process is usually used for larger, more complex projects.
