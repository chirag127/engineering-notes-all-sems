
### Risk Management in SPM

Risk management is the process of identifying, assessing, and controlling risks associated with project activities. The purpose of risk management is to reduce the likelihood and impact of risks on the project.

1. Identify Risks: Identify potential risks associated with project activities, such as cost overruns, delays, and scope changes.
2. Assess Risks: Assess the likelihood and impact of identified risks.
3. Develop Risk Responses: Develop strategies to respond to risks, such as risk avoidance, risk transfer, and risk mitigation.
4. Monitor Risks: Monitor identified risks and take action as needed to reduce their impact on the project.
