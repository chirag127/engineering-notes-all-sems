
### Dispersed and Virtual Teams in spm

* Dispersed and virtual teams are teams of people who are geographically dispersed, either across different countries, cities, or even within the same city.
* Virtual teams are composed of members who communicate and collaborate primarily via technology such as email, video conferencing, and other digital tools.
* Such teams are becoming increasingly popular in the business world, as they allow for greater flexibility and cost savings.
* Dispersed and virtual teams require clear communication and coordination between members in order to be successful.
* It is important for virtual teams to have a shared understanding of the team's goals, objectives, and expectations.
* In order to ensure successful collaboration, teams should use tools such as project management software, task management systems, and other collaboration tools.
* It is also important to establish ground rules for communication, such as the frequency of meetings, the use of email, and the use of other digital tools.
* Finally, dispersed and virtual teams should be given the opportunity to build relationships and trust among team members. This can be done through team-building activities or even virtual team retreats.
