
### Cost Monitoring in SPM

1. Cost monitoring is the process of tracking and evaluating the costs associated with a project or organization.

2. It is an important part of project and financial management, as it allows organizations to identify areas of potential cost savings.

3. Cost monitoring includes tracking costs associated with materials, labor, and other resources used in the project.

4. It also includes tracking the costs of administrative overhead, such as insurance, taxes, and other associated costs.

5. Cost monitoring should be done on a regular basis, as it allows organizations to identify potential cost savings and ensure that the project is on track.

6. Cost monitoring also allows organizations to identify areas of potential cost overruns, which can be addressed before they become a major problem.

7. Cost monitoring can be done manually or through the use of software programs that track and analyze costs.

8. Cost monitoring is an important part of project and financial management, and should be done regularly in order to ensure that the project is on track and that potential cost savings are identified.
