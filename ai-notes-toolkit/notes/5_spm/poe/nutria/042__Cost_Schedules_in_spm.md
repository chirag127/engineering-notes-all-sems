
### Cost Schedules in SPM

1. Cost Schedules are used to create a plan of activities and resources needed to complete a project.

2. A Cost Schedule is typically composed of two parts: the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and the Cost Estimate.

3. The WBS is a hierarchical structure of activities that defines the scope of the project and is used to estimate the cost of each activity.

4. The Cost Estimate is a detailed estimate of the resources and costs necessary to complete the project. This includes labor, materials, and overhead costs.

5. Cost Schedules are developed using a variety of techniques, such as bottom-up estimating, top-down estimating, and parametric estimating.

6. Cost Schedules are used to monitor the progress of a project and to track the actual costs versus the estimated costs.

7. Cost Schedules are also used to identify cost overruns and take corrective action.
