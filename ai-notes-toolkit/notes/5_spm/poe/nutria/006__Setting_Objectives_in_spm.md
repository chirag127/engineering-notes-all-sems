
### Setting Objectives in SPM

* Objectives in SPM are the goals that are set for a student to achieve in the exam.
* Objectives should be realistic and achievable.
* Objectives should be specific, measurable, attainable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
* Objectives should be written down and reviewed regularly to ensure they are being met.
* Objectives should be tailored to the individual student's needs and abilities.
* Objectives should be monitored and adjusted as needed.
