
### Stress in SPM

* Stress is a normal part of life, and SPM is no exception. It is important to recognize that stress is a normal part of the exam process and to learn how to manage it.

* It is important to have a plan in place to manage stress and to stick to it. This could include regular exercise, healthy eating, good sleep habits, and taking time to relax and unwind.

* It is also important to set realistic goals and expectations and to focus on the process of studying rather than the outcome.

* Taking regular breaks during study sessions can help to reduce stress levels, as can talking to friends and family about your worries and concerns.

* It is also important to remember that everyone experiences stress differently and to find ways to manage it that work for you.
