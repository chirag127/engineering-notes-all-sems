
### Organizational Behavior in SPM

1. Organizational behavior (OB) is the study of how people act in organizations, and how organizations affect people’s behavior. 
2. OB is concerned with the behavior of individuals and groups within organizations, and how organizations can manage and adapt to change. 
3. OB focuses on the study of the behavior of people in organizations, including topics such as motivation, communication, decision-making, leadership, and team dynamics. 
4. OB research has identified a number of factors that influence behavior in organizations, such as culture, structure, and technology. 
5. Organizations are dynamic and complex systems, and OB research has identified a number of ways to effectively manage and lead in such environments. 
6. OB is an interdisciplinary field of study, drawing on insights from psychology, sociology, economics, and other disciplines. 
7. OB is a critical component of successful organizational management, and is essential for understanding and improving organizational performance.
