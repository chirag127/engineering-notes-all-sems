
### Managing People in SPM
1. Establish clear expectations: It is important to communicate expectations clearly to all employees. This includes job responsibilities, deadlines, and performance standards.
2. Provide support: Managers should provide employees with the tools, resources, and guidance they need to succeed.
3. Foster a positive work environment: A positive work environment is essential for employee morale and productivity. Managers should provide recognition, feedback, and rewards for good performance.
4. Develop relationships: Managers should aim to develop relationships with their employees. This includes understanding their backgrounds, interests, and goals.
5. Encourage collaboration: Managers should create an environment that encourages collaboration between employees. This includes setting up team meetings, providing team-building activities, and promoting open communication.
6. Monitor performance: Managers should monitor employee performance and provide feedback to ensure that employees are on track to meet their goals.
7. Handle conflicts: Managers should be prepared to address conflicts that arise between employees. This includes identifying the root of the problem and resolving it in a timely manner.
