
### Extreme Programming in SPM

* Extreme Programming (XP) is an Agile software development methodology that focuses on rapid delivery of high-quality software. 
* XP emphasizes communication, collaboration, and feedback between developers, customers, and stakeholders. 
* XP promotes iterative development and emphasizes the use of simple design and coding practices that enable frequent releases of software. 
* XP also encourages teams to use tools and techniques that allow for continuous integration and testing of code.
