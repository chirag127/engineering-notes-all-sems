
### Management Principles in SPM

1. Planning: Planning is the process of setting goals and objectives, determining the necessary resources and actions to achieve those goals, and monitoring progress to ensure that goals are met.

2. Organizing: Organizing is the process of assigning tasks and responsibilities to individuals and groups, and allocating resources to ensure that tasks are completed.

3. Leading: Leading is the process of motivating and inspiring people to work together to achieve goals.

4. Controlling: Controlling is the process of monitoring progress towards goals to ensure that they are achieved, and making adjustments when necessary.
