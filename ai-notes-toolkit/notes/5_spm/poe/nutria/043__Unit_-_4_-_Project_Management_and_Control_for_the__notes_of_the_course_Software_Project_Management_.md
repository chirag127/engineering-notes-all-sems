

## Unit - 4 - Project Management and Control 

Software Project Management (SPM) is a complex process that requires careful planning and execution in order to successfully complete a software project. Project management and control are important aspects of SPM and involve the following activities: 

1. Establishing project objectives and goals: This involves setting the project’s scope and timeline, as well as establishing the budget and other resources needed to complete the project.

2. Planning the project: This includes creating a project plan that outlines the tasks to be completed, the resources needed, and the timeline for completion.

3. Executing the project: This involves carrying out the tasks outlined in the project plan and making sure the project is completed on time and within budget.

4. Monitoring and controlling the project: This involves tracking progress, making adjustments as needed, and ensuring that the project is meeting its objectives.

5. Closing the project: This involves evaluating the project’s success and documenting the results.
