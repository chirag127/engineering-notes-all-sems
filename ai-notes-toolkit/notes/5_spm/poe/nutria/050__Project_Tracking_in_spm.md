
### Project Tracking in SPM

- Project tracking is the process of monitoring the progress of a project, including the tasks and resources involved.
- It involves tracking the project's goals, objectives, timeline, resources, and budget.
- Project tracking can be done manually or with a software program.
- Project tracking software can help to ensure that all tasks are completed on time and within budget.
- It can also help to identify any areas that need improvement or additional resources.
- Project tracking software can provide real-time reports and analytics to help project managers make better decisions.
- It can also help to identify any risks or potential issues before they become major problems.
- Project tracking software can help to ensure that all stakeholders are kept informed of the project's progress.
