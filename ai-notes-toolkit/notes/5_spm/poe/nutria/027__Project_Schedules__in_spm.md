
### Project Schedules in SPM
* Project scheduling is the process of organizing and managing tasks, resources, and timelines for a project.
* The purpose of project scheduling is to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.
* Project scheduling involves the identification of tasks, the allocation of resources, and the determination of the order in which tasks must be completed.
* A project schedule is a document that outlines the timeline for the completion of a project. It includes the start and end dates for each task, the resources needed to complete the task, and any dependencies that must be taken into account.
* Project scheduling also involves the identification of potential risks and the development of strategies to mitigate them.
* Project scheduling is an essential part of project management and must be done in a systematic and organized manner.
