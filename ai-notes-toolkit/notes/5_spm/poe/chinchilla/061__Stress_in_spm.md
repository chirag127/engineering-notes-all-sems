### Stress in SPM

Stress is a common issue faced by students during the SPM examination period. Here are some points to help you understand stress in SPM:

- SPM is a crucial examination that determines a student's future academic path. As a result, students tend to feel anxious and stressed about their performance.
- Stress can negatively impact a student's mental and physical health. It can lead to sleeplessness, loss of appetite, and difficulty concentrating, which can adversely affect their performance in the examination.
- One way to manage stress is to plan and organize your study schedule. Setting achievable goals and breaking down the syllabus into manageable sections can help reduce the feeling of overwhelm.
- It is essential to take regular breaks while studying to avoid burnout. Taking a break can help you relax and rejuvenate before getting back to studying.
- Engaging in physical activity or exercise can help manage stress. Exercise releases endorphins, which can improve your mood and reduce stress levels.
- Seeking support from family, friends, or a counselor can also help manage stress. Talking to someone you trust about your concerns can help you feel less overwhelmed.
- It is crucial to maintain a positive mindset and believe in yourself. Remind yourself of your strengths and abilities and focus on your progress rather than dwelling on your mistakes.
- Finally, it is essential to practice self-care during the examination period. Getting enough sleep, eating a balanced diet, and engaging in activities that bring you joy can help reduce stress levels.

In conclusion, stress is a common issue faced by students during the SPM examination period. However, with proper planning, support, and self-care, students can manage their stress levels and perform to the best of their abilities.
