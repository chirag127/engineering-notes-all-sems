### Organizational Behavior in spm

1. Individual Behavior: This refers to the behavior of individuals in an organization. It studies how individuals perceive and react to situations in the workplace and how it affects their work performance.
Factors influencing individual behavior:
- Perception: How individuals perceive a situation affects their behavior. Perception is influenced by various factors like personality, mood, values, etc.
- Ability: The skill and competence of an individual determines behavior. Those with appropriate abilities can perform better.
- Learning: Individuals learn from their experiences and environmental cues, which shapes their behavior.
- Attitudes: Individual's attitudes towards work, coworkers, etc. drive behavior. Attitudes are formed based on various factors.

2. Group Behavior: This refers to the behavior of groups in an organization. It analyzes how groups are formed, their characteristics, and how they affect individuals and productivity.
Factors influencing group behavior:
- Group formation: How groups are formed within an organization impacts group behavior. The composition, structure, size, etc. of a group determine its behavior.
- Group dynamics: The interactions between group members and the resulting forces that shape the group's behavior, performance, and productivity. Leadership, communication, conflicts, cooperation, etc. are aspects of group dynamics.
- Group norms: The acceptable standards of behavior within a group that are shared by its members. Norms influence how groups take actions and make decisions.

3. Organizational Behavior: This studies the effect of individuals and groups on the organization and vice versa. It analyzes how organizations influence and shape individual and group behavior to achieve optimal performance.
Factors influencing organizational behavior:
- Organizational culture: The shared values and beliefs that govern behavior in an organization. Culture influences the way people think, feel, and act towards work.
- Leadership: The way managers lead and direct employees impacts organizational behavior and performance. Different leadership styles elicit varied responses from subordinates.
- Structure: The way an organization is structured affects communication, coordination, and decision making, which in turn influences behavior and performance.
- Systems & Processes: Formal and informal systems and processes in an organization shape behavior. For example, performance appraisal processes affect behavior.
