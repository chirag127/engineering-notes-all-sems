### Cost Monitoring in spm

1. Cost monitoring is crucial for any project to stay within budget and meet financial goals. In spm, cost monitoring involves tracking the actual costs incurred vs estimated costs for all project activities and deliverables.
2. Some key things to monitor in terms of costs are:
- Labor costs vs labor hours estimated
- Material costs vs material quantity estimates
- Fixed costs vs budgeted amounts
- Variable costs and any fluctuations
3. Cost monitoring should be done regularly, at least monthly or quarterly. Variances between actual and estimated costs must be analyzed to determine the reasons and take corrective actions if required.
4. Earned value management is a technique that uses cost and schedule performance to assess project progress in monetary terms. It integrates scope, time and cost objectives to provide indicators of project performance.
5. To enable efficient cost monitoring, an accounting system should be set up to capture all costs as per the work breakdown structure. Actual costs must be recorded as soon as transactions occur for accurate cost tracking.
6. Cost monitoring metrics, reports and dashboards should be prepared to monitor key parameters and keep stakeholders informed of the cost status. This will help identify potential issues early and make sure the project finishes within budget.
