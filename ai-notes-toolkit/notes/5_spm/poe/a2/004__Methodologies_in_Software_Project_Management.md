### Methodologies in Software Project Management

1. Waterfall Model
- Sequential design process
- Rigid phases: Requirements, Design, Implementation, Testing, Deployment
- Each phase must be completed before moving to the next phase
- Difficult to accommodate changes once a phase is complete

2. Agile Methodology
- Iterative approach: Software is built incrementally from requirements
- Requirements and solutions evolve through collaboration between teams
- Encourages rapid and flexible response to change
- Commonly used frameworks: Scrum, Kanban, XP

3. Scrum
- Lightweight framework for managing complex projects
- Team works in "Sprints" to achieve a concrete goal in 2-4 weeks
- Daily meetings, tracking task boards, retrospectives
- Roles: Product Owner, Scrum Master, Development Team

4. Kanban
- Focuses on visualizing and limiting work in progress
- Board columns represent steps in the process (To Do, In Progress, Done)
- Limits are placed on columns to minimize bottlenecks
- Aims to balance demand against throughput

5. Extreme Programming (XP)
- Programming best practices taken to extreme levels
- Very short development cycles, simple design, constant testing
- Pair programming, continuous integration, refactoring
- Strong focus on communication and feedback
