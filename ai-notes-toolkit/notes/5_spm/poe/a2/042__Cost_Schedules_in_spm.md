### Cost Schedules in spm

1. A cost schedule is a detailed cost estimate prepared for the different activities involved in a project. It provides an itemized cost of each major component of a project.

2. Cost schedules are prepared at various stages of project planning and execution:

- During project conception: To estimate initial costs and check viability of the project.
- During detailed project planning: To arrive at a fairly accurate estimate of costs and investigate alternative strategies.
- During project execution: To monitor expenditures and take corrective actions if required.

3. The components of a cost schedule usually include:

- Direct costs: Costs of materials, labor, equipment, etc. directly related to the project activities.
- Indirect costs: Costs not directly related to project activities but incurred to support the project. e.g. administrative costs.
- Contingency allowance: Additional provision for unforeseen circumstances.

4. A well-prepared cost schedule should be comprehensive, practical, and flexible. It should provide details of individual cost components and the assumptions and basis on which the estimates are made. It should be reviewed and updated as the project progresses.
