### Organizational Structures in spm

1. Functional structure:
- Grouped by functional areas (Marketing, Finance, Production, etc.)
- High specialization
- Clear chain of command
- Inefficiencies across departments

2. Divisional structure:
- Grouped by product, market, or geographic region
- Increased autonomy for division managers
- Improves focus but can duplicate resources/efforts

3. Matrix structure:
- Hybrid of functional and divisional structures
- Employees report to both functional and divisional managers
- Aims to leverage benefits of different structures but can lead to confusion/conflict

4. Project-based structure:
- Temporary teams formed for specific projects
- Teams disband once project is completed
- Best for organizations with unique or temporary work
- Challenging to coordinate resources and team members across projects
