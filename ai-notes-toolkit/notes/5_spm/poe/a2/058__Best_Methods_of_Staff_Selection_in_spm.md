### Best Methods of Staff Selection in spm

1. Written Examination
- Tests knowledge and conceptual understanding of candidates on various topics related to the job
- Wide coverage of topics allows assessment of breadth of knowledge
- Objective type questions can be used for efficient evaluation of large number of candidates
- May not test skills and attitude required for the job

2. Interview
- Allows evaluation of skills like communication, interpersonal skills and attitude
- Flexibility to ask job-related scenarios and assess problem-solving abilities
- Subjective and prone to bias
- Not useful for evaluating large volumes of candidates

3. Assessment Centres
- Multiple evaluation methods like group discussions, presentations, job simulations, etc. used
- Provides a comprehensive assessment of key skills and competencies required for the job
- Time-consuming and expensive to conduct for a large number of candidates
- May induce stress and anxiety in candidates
