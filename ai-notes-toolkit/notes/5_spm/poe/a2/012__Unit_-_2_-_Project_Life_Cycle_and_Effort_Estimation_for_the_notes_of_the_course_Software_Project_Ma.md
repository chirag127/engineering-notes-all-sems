## Unit - 2 - Project Life Cycle and Effort Estimation

1. Project Life Cycle
- The series of steps/phases that a project follows from start to completion is called Project Life Cycle.
- The main phases are:
-- Initiation: Starting the project, identifying the need and feasibility.
-- Planning: Defining the project goal, scope, schedule, budget, resources, etc.
-- Execution: Carrying out the project work as per plan.
-- Monitoring and Control: Tracking progress, scopes, risks, issues, costs, quality, etc. and taking corrective actions.
-- Closure: Formal acceptance and closure of the project.

2. Effort Estimation
- Estimating the effort required to complete a project is important for planning the schedule and budget.
- The main techniques for effort estimation are:
-- Expert Judgment: Relying on expert experience to provide estimates.
-- Algorithmic Estimation: Using mathematical formulas/algorithms based on project parameters. E.g. COCOMO model.
-- Analogous Estimation: Basing estimates on actual efforts of similar past projects.
-- Bottom-up Estimation: Breaking down project into tasks and estimating individual task efforts to get total effort.
