### Basics of Software Estimation in spm

1. Understand the requirements thoroughly. Ask questions to clarify any doubts or vague requirements. Missing requirements or improperly understood requirements lead to improper estimates.
2. Break down the requirements into smaller, manageable tasks. This makes estimation easier and more accurate.
3. Consider the interdependencies between tasks. Some tasks may be dependent on the completion of other tasks, so the estimate should account for this.
4. Consider reusable components. If certain parts of the requirement can be met using existing components, the effort required to build those parts can be reduced.
5. Account for unknowns and risks. It is difficult to estimate the unknown, so buffers and contingencies should be included to account for unpredictable risks and challenges.
6. Get input from the team and arrive at a consensus. The estimates may vary from person to person, so discussing as a team leads to more accurate results.
7. Review and refine the estimates periodically. As the project progresses, you may gain more clarity or encounter unforeseen issues. Regular review of estimates helps to keep them aligned with reality.
