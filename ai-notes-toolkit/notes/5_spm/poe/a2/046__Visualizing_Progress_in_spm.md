### Visualizing Progress in spm

1. Show overall progress: spm allows you to show the overall progress of a project, task or workflow. You can set a total number of steps or tasks and then increment the progress % as and when tasks get completed. This gives a quick visible overview of how much has been achieved and how much is left.

2. Break down into smaller milestones: For large projects or complex tasks, it is better to break down the overall goal into smaller milestones. This could be breaking down a project into phases or a task into subtasks. Showing progress for these smaller milestones keeps the team motivated as they achieve interim wins.

3. Focus on what's important: When there are too many tasks or parallel workstreams, it can be overwhelming to track everything. Use spm to focus on the critical tasks or priorities. Show progress for the high impact work which will make the most difference to the outcome. This avoids distractions and keeps the team focused on what's important.

4. Foster transparency: Sharing visible indicators of progress promotes transparency within a team. Everyone is aware of what is being worked on, what's completing and what's facing blockers. This transparency allows for earlier identification and resolution of issues and better coordination between team members. It leads to an environment of trust and open communication.

5. Celebrate wins: Highlighting and celebrating achieved milestones is a great way to build morale and motivation. Whether it's incrementing a progress bar to 100% or simply calling out major tasks that have been accomplished, recognizing progress made keeps the team energized and feeling good about what they have achieved. Small wins along the way make the end goal seem more achievable.
