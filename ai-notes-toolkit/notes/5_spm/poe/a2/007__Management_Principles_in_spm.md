### Management Principles in spm

1. Planning: Deciding in advance what to do, how to do it, when to do it and who is to do it. Planning bridges the gap between where we are and where we want to be.
2. Organizing: Arranging resources and activities in a logical manner to achieve objectives. It involves identification and classification of required work, grouping the similar types of work and assignment of duties to individuals.
3. Staffing: Recruiting and selecting the right people for the right job at the right time. It ensures that there are sufficient personnel to achieve the organizational goals.
4. Directing: Issuing instructions and guiding subordinates towards achievement of goals. It initiates action and guides activities towards desired objectives.
5. Controlling: Monitoring actual performance and comparing it with the set standards. It helps to identify deviations and take corrective actions. The key steps in control process are: establishing standards, measuring performance, comparing performance with standards and taking corrective actions.
