### Communications Genres in spm

1. Informative Writing
- Explain a topic or concept. Provide facts and details.
- Examples: Reports, explanations, instructions.

2. Narrative Writing
- Tell a story. Include characters, plot, setting.
- Examples: Short stories, biographies, memoirs, personal experience essays.

3. Argumentative Writing
- Make a claim and provide reasoning and evidence to support it.
- Examples: Persuasive essays, debates, reviews.

4. Descriptive Writing
- Use vivid language to show, not just tell, the reader about a person, place, thing, or experience.
- Examples: Descriptive passages in stories or essays, poetry.
