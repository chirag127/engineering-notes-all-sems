### Dispersed and Virtual Teams in spm

1. Dispersed teams: Teams where members are physically separated and work from different locations. They collaborate using communication technologies.
- Pros: Access to talent beyond geographic limits, reduced real estate costs.
- Cons: Lack of face-to-face interaction can lead to communication issues and reduced productivity.

2. Virtual teams: Teams where members work across space, time, and organizational boundaries with links strengthened by webs of communication technologies.
- Pros: Increased creativity and innovation, access to diverse perspectives, cost savings.
- Cons: Challenges in building trust and relationships, technical issues can disrupt work, greater risk of misunderstandings and conflicts.

To overcome the challenges of dispersed and virtual teams:

- Establish clear goals and expectations
- Provide strong leadership and governance
- Invest in recruiting right talent with skills to collaborate remotely
- Provide robust communication infrastructure and practices
- Build relationships and trust through occasional face-to-face interactions
- Measure outcomes, not just outputs
