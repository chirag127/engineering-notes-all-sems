### Sequencing and Scheduling in spm

1. Sequencing refers to ordering of jobs/processes according to a set criteria. It ensures that jobs/processes are executed in a correct order.
2. The most common sequencing techniques are:
- First Come First Serve (FCFS): Jobs are executed in the order they arrive. No priorities are assigned. May lead to starvation of some jobs.
- Shortest Job First (SJF): Job with the shortest execution time is executed first. Minimizes average waiting time but may lead to starvation of large jobs.
- Priority Based: Jobs are assigned priorities and higher priority jobs are executed first. May lead to starvation of lower priority jobs.
3. Scheduling refers to allocation of resources to jobs. It determines which job gets the resources at what time and for how long.
4. The most common scheduling algorithms are:
- First Come First Serve (FCFS)
- Shortest Job First (SJF)
- Priority Based Scheduling
- Round Robin (RR): Each job gets a fixed time quantum. After that, it goes to the end of the queue. Ensures every job gets CPU cycles but may lead to context switching overhead.
- Multilevel Queue: Separate queues are maintained for different priority jobs. Higher priority queues get preference. Effective but may be complex to implement.
