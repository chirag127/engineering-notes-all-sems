### Stress in spm

1. Exam pressure: The SPM exam puts a lot of pressure and stress on the students to perform well. The stakes are high in this exam and students have to study a vast syllabus. This can lead to anxiety, sleeplessness, and other stress-related issues in students.

2. Expectations: Students have a lot of expectations from themselves and also from their parents and teachers to score high marks. Not being able to meet these expectations can cause a lot of mental stress and tension.

3. Peer pressure: Seeing peers study for long hours and the competition to get into good colleges can also add to the stress levels of students. Not being able to keep up with their peers can make some students feel inadequate and tensed.

4. Career concerns: Students are at an age where they are worried about their careers and future prospects. The SPM exam plays an important role in deciding the career path. This uncertainty and worry about the future can lead to stress and worry in students.

To deal with such exam-related stress, students should adopt some methods like:

- Maintaining a balance between studying and leisure activities
- Practicing relaxation techniques like meditation or deep breathing
- Talking to parents or teachers about their worries and feelings
- Taking breaks in between long study sessions
- Getting enough sleep and eating healthy meals
- Staying positive and confident about their preparation
