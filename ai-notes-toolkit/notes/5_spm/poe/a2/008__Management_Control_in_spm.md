### Management Control in SPM

1. Planning: Planning is the primary function of management. It involves setting objectives and determining appropriate courses of action to achieve those objectives.
2. Organizing: Organizing involves identifying and grouping activities, allocating resources, and establishing relationships so that work can be performed efficiently.
3. Leading: Leading involves influencing and motivating employees to work together to achieve organizational goals. It includes directing and communicating with individuals and groups.
4. Controlling: Controlling involves monitoring performance, comparing it against plans and standards, and taking corrective action as needed. Management needs to determine if goals are being met and take any necessary corrective actions.
5. Staffing: Staffing involves filling job openings with qualified individuals. It includes conducting job analyses, writing job descriptions, recruiting candidates, and training employees.
