### Stress in SPM

Stress is a common issue experienced by students during the SPM (Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia) examination period. Here are some points to understand stress in SPM:

- SPM is an important examination that determines the future of a student's education. Hence, students tend to feel pressure to perform well and achieve good grades.
- The pressure to perform well in SPM can lead to stress, which can have a negative impact on a student's physical and mental health.
- Stress can cause various symptoms, such as headaches, difficulty sleeping, loss of appetite, and anxiety.
- To prevent stress, students should plan their study schedule well in advance and ensure they have enough time to revise all the subjects. 
- Students should also practice relaxation techniques such as meditation, deep breathing, and regular exercise to reduce stress levels.
- It is also important for students to take breaks and indulge in activities they enjoy to reduce stress levels.
- Parents and teachers should provide support to students during the SPM period and encourage them to take care of their well-being.
- Seeking professional help from counselors or therapists can also be beneficial for students who are experiencing high levels of stress.

In conclusion, stress is a common issue experienced by students during the SPM examination period. However, with proper planning, relaxation techniques, and support from parents and teachers, students can effectively manage stress and perform well in their exams.
