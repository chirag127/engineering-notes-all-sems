### Agile Methods in Software Project Management

- Agile methods are a set of project management frameworks that break projects down into several dynamic phases, commonly known as sprints.
- Agile methods are based on delivering requirements iteratively and incrementally throughout the life cycle.
- Agile methods are an iterative methodology, meaning that after every sprint, teams reflect and look back to see if there was anything that could be improved so they can adjust their strategy for the next sprint.
- Agile methods are suitable for projects that have changing or unclear requirements, need frequent feedback from users and stakeholders, and value working software over comprehensive documentation.
- Agile methods are an umbrella term for various specific approaches, such as scrum, kanban, extreme programming, lean, and DSDM .
- Agile methods have some common principles and values, such as customer satisfaction, collaboration, self-organization, adaptability, simplicity, and continuous improvement.
- Agile methods have some common practices and tools, such as user stories, backlog, burndown chart, daily stand-up, retrospective, and online Kanban board  .
