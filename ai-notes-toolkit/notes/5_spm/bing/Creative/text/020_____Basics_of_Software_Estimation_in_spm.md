### Basics of Software Estimation in SPM

- Software estimation is the process of predicting the most realistic amount of effort, time, and resources required to develop or maintain software based on incomplete, uncertain, and noisy input.
- Software estimation is an essential part of software project management (SPM), which is the discipline of planning, organizing, monitoring, and controlling software projects.
- Software estimation is important for both developers and customers, as it helps them to make informed decisions, allocate resources, negotiate contracts, monitor progress, and control quality.
- Software estimation is challenging, as software projects are often complex, dynamic, uncertain, and unique, and involve human factors, technical factors, and environmental factors.
- Software estimation can be performed at different levels of granularity, such as project level, module level, function level, or task level, depending on the available information and the purpose of the estimation.
- Software estimation can be performed using different techniques, such as expert judgment, analogy, algorithmic models, machine learning, or hybrid methods, depending on the accuracy, reliability, and validity of the estimation.
- Software estimation can be improved by using historical data, applying multiple techniques, involving multiple experts, adjusting for risks and uncertainties, and validating and updating the estimates regularly .
