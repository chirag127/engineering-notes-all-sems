## Unit - 1 - Project Evaluation and Project Planning

- Project evaluation is the process of assessing the feasibility, desirability, and viability of a software project before initiating it.
- Project planning is the process of defining the scope, objectives, tasks, resources, schedule, budget, and quality standards of a software project after it has been evaluated and approved.
- Project evaluation and planning are essential for software project management, as they help to align the project with the organizational goals, stakeholder expectations, and technical requirements, and to ensure the project is realistic, achievable, and beneficial.
- Project evaluation and planning involve the following steps:

  - **Identify the project idea and need**: This step involves identifying the problem or opportunity that the project aims to address, and the potential benefits and risks of pursuing it.
  - **Conduct a feasibility study**: This step involves analyzing the technical, economic, social, legal, and environmental aspects of the project, and determining whether the project is feasible and desirable in terms of costs, benefits, resources, time, and quality.
  - **Prepare a project proposal**: This step involves preparing a document that summarizes the project idea, need, feasibility, objectives, scope, deliverables, assumptions, constraints, risks, and stakeholders, and requesting approval from the decision-makers.
  - **Define the project scope and objectives**: This step involves defining the boundaries, requirements, and expected outcomes of the project, and ensuring they are clear, specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.
  - **Identify the project tasks and dependencies**: This step involves breaking down the project scope into manageable and interrelated tasks, and identifying the logical and temporal relationships among them.
  - **Estimate the project resources and duration**: This step involves estimating the amount and type of resources (such as human, material, financial, and information) and time needed to complete each task, and applying appropriate estimation techniques and tools.
  - **Develop the project schedule and budget**: This step involves allocating the resources and duration to each task, and creating a timeline and a cost plan for the project, and applying appropriate scheduling and budgeting techniques and tools.
  - **Define the project quality standards and metrics**: This step involves defining the criteria and measures for evaluating the quality of the project processes and products, and applying appropriate quality management techniques and tools.
  - **Identify and analyze the project risks**: This step involves identifying the potential threats and opportunities that may affect the project, and analyzing their probability and impact, and applying appropriate risk management techniques and tools.
  - **Communicate the project plan**: This step involves communicating the project plan to the project team and stakeholders, and obtaining their feedback and approval, and applying appropriate communication management techniques and tools.
