### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

Activity planning is one of the most important management activities in software project management. It involves identifying, estimating, scheduling, and monitoring the tasks and resources needed to complete the project. The objectives of activity planning are:

- To define the scope and deliverables of the project, and break them down into manageable activities.
- To estimate the effort, duration, cost, and quality of each activity, and the dependencies and constraints among them.
- To allocate the available resources (such as staff, equipment, materials, etc.) to the activities, and balance the workload and the risk.
- To produce a detailed schedule that shows the start and end dates, milestones, and critical path of each activity, and the overall project timeline.
- To produce a budget and a cash flow forecast that reflect the expected income and expenditure of the project.
- To produce a baseline plan that can be used to measure and control the progress and performance of the project, and to identify and manage any deviations or changes.
- To communicate the plan to the project stakeholders, and obtain their approval and commitment.
