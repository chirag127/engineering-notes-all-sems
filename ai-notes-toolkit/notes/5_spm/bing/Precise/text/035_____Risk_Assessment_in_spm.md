### Risk Assessment in SPM

Risk assessment is a crucial step in the management of single point mooring (SPM) systems. A qualitative risk-based approach to integrity management has been developed, which targets high expenditure items for inspection and maintenance activities . This approach utilizes compiled data on SPM design, characteristics, assessment, and inspection to inform decision-making.

In project management, the benefits of performing risk assessment include reducing project risk exposure, making precise and clear decisions on key issues within every project phase, and providing a clearer definition of risks related to particular projects . Risk analysis and management is a key project management practice that helps to minimize surprises during project execution .

Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM) is a platform that allows for the alignment of strategy and work, planning, delivery, and tracking of value across different methodologies, and the empowerment of teams to drive business outcomes . The goal of risk assessment in this context is to prioritize risks according to their impact and likelihood on the project, resulting in a prioritized list of risks . This information can be used to determine whether a project is worth undertaking and which risks must be monitored.

Risk assessment is a step in the risk management procedure, and the project risk management plan addresses the process behind risk management . The risk assessment meeting allows the project team to identify, categorize, prioritize, and mitigate or avoid risks ahead of time.
