### Effort and Cost Estimation Techniques in SPM

Effort and cost estimation is an important part of Software Project Management (SPM). During project planning, an accurate Software Cost Estimation (SCE) is critical for both developers and customers in making decisions.

The project estimation approach that is widely used is the Decomposition Technique. This technique takes a divide and conquer approach. Size, effort, and cost estimation are performed in a stepwise manner by breaking down a project into major functions or related software engineering activities.

Some of the steps involved in this technique are:
1. Understand the scope of the software to be built.
2. Generate an estimate of the software size by starting with the statement of scope.

There are also other techniques such as Function-based and Constructive Cost Modeling (COCOMO). Function-based estimates effort based on its functional characteristics, while COCOMO is size-based and estimates effort, duration, and team size.

It is important to note that the wrong estimate can not only delay the completion of a software project but also increase its cost. Therefore, it is important to carefully analyze the requirements and choose the appropriate estimation technique.
