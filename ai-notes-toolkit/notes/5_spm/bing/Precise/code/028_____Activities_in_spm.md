### Activities in SPM

SPM, or School-based Project Management, is a program that allows students to engage in various activities that promote learning and personal development. Some of the activities that can be included in an SPM program are:

1. **Group projects**: Students can work together in groups to complete a project on a specific topic. This promotes teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills.

2. **Individual research**: Students can conduct research on a topic of their choice and present their findings to the class. This promotes independent learning and critical thinking skills.

3. **Community service**: Students can participate in community service activities, such as volunteering at a local charity or organizing a fundraiser. This promotes social responsibility and empathy.

4. **Extracurricular activities**: Students can participate in extracurricular activities, such as sports, music, or art. This promotes physical and mental well-being, as well as creativity.

5. **Field trips**: Students can go on field trips to museums, historical sites, or other places of interest. This promotes experiential learning and cultural awareness.

These are just some examples of the activities that can be included in an SPM program. The specific activities will vary depending on the school and the interests of the students. The goal of SPM is to provide students with a well-rounded education and to promote lifelong learning.
