### Best Methods of Staff Selection in SPM

1. **Assess cognitive ability**: This method evaluates the candidate's ability to learn, think, and solve problems. It is an effective way to predict job performance .
2. **Evaluate learning agility**: This method assesses the candidate's ability to learn from experience and apply that learning to new situations. It is a good predictor of future job performance .
3. **Situational judgement test (SJT)**: This method presents the candidate with hypothetical scenarios and asks them to choose the best course of action. It is a good way to assess the candidate's judgement and decision-making skills .
4. **Aptitude tests**: These tests measure the candidate's potential to learn and perform a specific job. They can be used to assess a wide range of abilities, including verbal, numerical, and spatial reasoning .
5. **Personality tests**: These tests assess the candidate's personality traits and how they might fit with the job and the company culture .
6. **Examination of previous work**: This method involves reviewing samples of the candidate's previous work to assess their skills and abilities .
7. **Interviews**: This is the most commonly used method of staff selection. It involves asking the candidate questions to assess their skills, experience, and fit with the company .

It is important to note that all these methods must be related to specific qualities detailed in the job holder profile . Additionally, more than 80 percent of employers use testing as part of their selection process .
