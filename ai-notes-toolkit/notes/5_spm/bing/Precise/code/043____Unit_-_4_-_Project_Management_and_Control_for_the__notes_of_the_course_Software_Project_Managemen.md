## Unit - 4 - Project Management and Control

Project management and control is an essential part of software project management. It involves the planning, monitoring, and controlling of project activities to ensure that the project is completed on time, within budget, and to the required quality standards.

1. **Planning:** This involves defining the scope of the project, identifying the tasks that need to be completed, and estimating the time and resources required to complete them. A project plan is then developed, which outlines the schedule, budget, and resources required for the project.

2. **Monitoring:** This involves tracking the progress of the project against the project plan. This includes monitoring the completion of tasks, the expenditure of resources, and the achievement of project milestones. Any deviations from the project plan are identified and corrective action is taken to bring the project back on track.

3. **Controlling:** This involves taking corrective action to address any deviations from the project plan. This may involve re-allocating resources, adjusting the schedule, or changing the scope of the project. The aim is to ensure that the project is completed on time, within budget, and to the required quality standards.

Effective project management and control is essential for the success of a software project. It helps to ensure that the project is completed on time, within budget, and to the required quality standards. It also helps to identify and address any issues that may arise during the project, and to keep the project on track.
