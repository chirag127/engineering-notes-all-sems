## Unit - 3 - Activity Planning and Risk Management

Activity Planning and Risk Management are two important aspects of Software Project Management. In this unit, we will discuss the following topics:

1. **Activity Planning:** This involves identifying the tasks that need to be completed in order to deliver the project successfully. It includes defining the scope of the project, breaking down the work into manageable tasks, and creating a schedule for completing these tasks.

2. **Risk Management:** This involves identifying, assessing, and managing risks that may impact the project. It includes identifying potential risks, assessing their likelihood and impact, and developing strategies to mitigate or avoid these risks.

By understanding and applying the concepts of activity planning and risk management, project managers can increase the likelihood of delivering successful projects on time and within budget. These concepts are essential for effective software project management.
