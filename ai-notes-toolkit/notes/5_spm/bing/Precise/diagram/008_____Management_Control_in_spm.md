### Management Control in SPM

Management control in software project management (SPM) is the process of monitoring and controlling project activities to ensure the project meets its goals and stays on budget. It involves setting performance targets, measuring progress towards those targets, and taking corrective action to ensure the project is on track .

Some key points to consider when discussing management control in SPM are:

- SPM is a proper way of planning and leading software projects. It is a part of project management in which software projects are planned, implemented, monitored, and controlled .
- Management control is one of the core tenets of the philosophy of SPM .
- Software project managers, like conventional project managers, are responsible for generating a project budget and adhering to it as closely as feasible, regulating spending and reassigning funds as needed .
