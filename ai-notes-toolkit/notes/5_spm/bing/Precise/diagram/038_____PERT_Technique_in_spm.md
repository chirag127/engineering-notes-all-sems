### PERT Technique in SPM

1. PERT (Program Evaluation and Review Technique) is a project management tool used to schedule, organize, and coordinate tasks within a project.
2. It is a method to analyze the tasks involved in completing a given project, especially the time needed to complete each task, and to identify the minimum time needed to complete the total project.
3. PERT was developed in the late 1950s for the U.S. Navy's Polaris missile program.
4. It is commonly used in conjunction with the critical path method (CPM).
5. PERT is a statistical tool that uses a network diagram to represent a project.
6. The diagram shows the sequence of activities, their dependencies, and the estimated time required to complete each activity.
7. The critical path is the longest path through the network, representing the minimum time required to complete the project.
8. PERT can be used to identify potential delays and to develop alternative plans to keep the project on schedule.
9. It can also be used to allocate resources, monitor progress, and control costs.
10. PERT is a valuable tool for project managers, helping them to plan, schedule, and control complex projects.
