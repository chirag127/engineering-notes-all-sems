### Activities in SPM

SPM, or School-Based Assessment Management, is a system that involves various activities to assess students' performance in school. Here are some of the activities that are typically included in SPM:

1. **Coursework:** This includes assignments, projects, and practical tests that students complete as part of their coursework.

2. **Written Examinations:** These are formal tests that students take to assess their knowledge and understanding of the subject matter.

3. **Oral Assessments:** These assessments evaluate students' speaking and communication skills.

4. **Observation:** Teachers observe students' behavior, participation, and engagement in class to assess their performance.

5. **Portfolio:** Students compile a portfolio of their work, which can include assignments, projects, and other evidence of their learning.

6. **Self and Peer Assessment:** Students assess their own work and the work of their peers to develop critical thinking and evaluation skills.

These activities are designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of students' performance and to help teachers identify areas where students may need additional support. They also provide students with opportunities to demonstrate their learning in a variety of ways.
