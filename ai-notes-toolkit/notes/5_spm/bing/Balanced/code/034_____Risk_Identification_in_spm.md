### Risk Identification in SPM

- Risk identification is the process of finding and documenting the possible sources of uncertainty that could affect the objectives of a software project.
- Risk identification is a crucial step in risk management, as it allows the project team to anticipate and prepare for potential threats and opportunities that could impact the project's scope, schedule, budget, quality, or stakeholder satisfaction.
- Risk identification is an iterative and continuous process that should be performed throughout the project life cycle, as new risks may emerge or old risks may change over time.
- Risk identification involves various techniques, such as brainstorming, checklists, interviews, surveys, historical data analysis, expert judgment, assumptions analysis, SWOT analysis, cause-and-effect diagrams, and scenario analysis.
- Risk identification results in a risk register, which is a document that records the identified risks, their characteristics, their sources, their impacts, their probabilities, and their responses. The risk register is a living document that should be updated and reviewed regularly.
