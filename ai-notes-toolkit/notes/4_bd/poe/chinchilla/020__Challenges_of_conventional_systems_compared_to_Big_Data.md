### Challenges of Conventional Systems Compared to Big Data

In today's data-driven world, conventional systems have become inadequate in handling the sheer volume and velocity of data generated. Here are some challenges that conventional systems face when compared to big data:

1. Limited Scalability: Conventional systems have limitations in scaling up or down to handle large volumes of data. As data grows, these systems struggle to keep up, leading to performance degradation and eventual system failure.

2. Limited Processing Power: Conventional systems have limited processing power and cannot efficiently process large datasets. This leads to slower processing times and increases the time required to derive insights from the data.

3. Limited Storage Capacity: Conventional systems have limited storage capacity and cannot store vast amounts of data. This leads to the need for data archiving, which makes it difficult to access the data when needed.

4. Inability to Handle Variety: Conventional systems are designed to handle structured data, making it difficult to handle unstructured data such as text, images, videos, and social media data.

5. Limited Analytical Capabilities: Conventional systems have limited analytical capabilities and cannot process data in real-time. This makes it difficult to derive insights from data in real-time.

6. Inability to Handle Data Velocity: Conventional systems are not equipped to handle the speed at which data is generated, making it difficult to process data in real-time.

7. Costly Infrastructure: Conventional systems require expensive hardware and software to store and process data. This makes it difficult for organizations to scale up their systems as data grows.

8. Inefficient Data Processing: Conventional systems require significant manual intervention to process data, making it time-consuming and error-prone.

In contrast, big data systems are designed to handle the challenges posed by conventional systems. They are scalable, can handle large volumes of data, have powerful processing capabilities, and can efficiently handle structured and unstructured data. Big data systems also provide real-time analytical capabilities, making it easier to derive insights from data in real-time. Overall, big data systems offer significant advantages over conventional systems in handling large volumes of data.
