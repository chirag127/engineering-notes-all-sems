### 5 Vs of Big Data

1. Volume: Refers to the huge amount of data generated every day which is massive in size. The volume of data is expanding rapidly and is posing challenges for its storage and processing.

2. Velocity: Refers to the speed at which the data is generated and processed. The data is streaming in at an incredible speed and needs to be processed in real-time for insights.

3. Variety: Refers to the different types and formats of data like structured, unstructured, text, images, videos, audio, etc. The variety of data adds to the complexity of its storage and processing.

4. Veracity: Refers to the uncertainty or trustworthiness of the data. The data needs to be verified for accuracy, consistency and reliability before using it for analysis.

5. Value: Refers to extracting useful information and insights from the huge amount of data to get business value and make better decisions. The value of data is derived from turning it into meaningful information through data analytics techniques.

The 5 Vs of Big Data help in understanding the characteristics and challenges of Big Data to determine appropriate solutions for its capture, storage, analysis and visualization. Gaining useful insights from Big Data can provide organizations a competitive advantage.
