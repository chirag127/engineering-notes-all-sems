### Big Data privacy

1. Collection of large volumes of data: Big data involves collection of huge volumes of data from various sources like internet activity, credit/debit card transactions, mobile activity etc. This extensive data collection poses privacy risks as personal information is gathered and stored.

2. Analytics leading to personal identification: The large data sets are analyzed using machine learning and data mining techniques to find patterns and insights. These analytics can lead to identification of individuals and reveal private information about them which can then be misused.

3. Third party sharing: The data collected is often shared with third parties for various purposes like targeted advertising. This further exacerbates the privacy risks as data is passed on to multiple entities and can be vulnerable to breaches or unauthorized use.

4. Inadequate regulations: There are no adequate regulations in place to control how the data collected can be used and shared. Also, the pace at which data is collected and analyzed far outstrips the legal frameworks for privacy and data protection, leaving loopholes that can be exploited.

5. Data security issues: With huge amounts of data being collected and stored, ensuring its security is a challenge. Data breaches are common and can expose personal details of millions of individuals leading to privacy and security risks.

In summary, while big data has significant benefits, it also introduces major privacy concerns that must be addressed through appropriate regulations and security measures to maintain individuals' privacy in the digital age. Proper consent and control should be given to data subjects on how their data can be collected and used.
