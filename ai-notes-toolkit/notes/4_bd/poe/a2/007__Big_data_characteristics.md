### Big data characteristics

1. Volume: Big data refers to extremely large data sets that may be analyzed computationally to reveal patterns, trends, and associations, especially relating to human behavior and interactions.

2. Velocity: Big data streams in at an incredible rate and must be processed very quickly. New data may be generated by the second or even by the millisecond.

3. Variety: Big data comes from a huge variety of sources and in a huge variety of forms. Structured and unstructured data must both be accommodated.

4. Veracity: The quality of captured data can vary greatly, affecting accuracy of analysis. Big data may include erroneous data, and methods must be implemented to assure veracity.

5. Complexity: Big data can exhibit complex interconnections and patterns that are neither simple nor straightforward. Sophisticated tools and techniques are required to reveal relationships and derive meaning.

The above points highlight the key characteristics of big data which make it distinct from traditional data sources and analyses. The volume, velocity, variety, veracity, and complexity of big data introduce both challenges and opportunities for making new discoveries and informed decisions.
