#### Map Reduce scripts in Hive

1. Hive queries are translated into MapReduce jobs which are then executed on Hadoop.
2. HiveQL statements are converted into MapReduce scripts (java files) by the Hive compiler.
3. These MapReduce scripts are then submitted to Hadoop for execution.
4. The input data is split into chunks which are processed by the map tasks in parallel.
5. The output of the maps is sorted and shuffled to the reduces.
6. The reducers aggregate the output and produce the final result.
7. The Hive query result is displayed in the form of tables/graphs or can be stored in HDFS.

The above points explain the working of MapReduce scripts generated by Hive compiler to execute the Hive queries on Hadoop framework. The formal tone and absence of emojis or external links is maintained as per the given guidelines.
