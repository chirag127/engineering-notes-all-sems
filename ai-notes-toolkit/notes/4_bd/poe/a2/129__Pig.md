### Pig

1. A pig is a stout-bodied, even-toed ungulate mammal with short legs, a short neck, and a long snout.
2. Pigs are highly social and intelligent animals. They live in complex social groups and are among the most intelligent domestic animals.
3. The domestic pig typically has a large head, with a long snout which is excellent for finding food. The pig's snout is used to dig for food and to detect foods location.
4. Pigs are omnivores and forage for a wide variety of plants and animals. They use their snouts to seek out roots, seeds, and other food items. In the wild, they eat grasses, roots, fruits, and insects. Domestic pigs tend to eat mostly grains, vegetables, and tubers and can consume a wide range of human foods.
5. A pig's diet requires large amounts of thiamine, which they obtain from foods like wheat. Thiamine deficiency can cause neurological problems and lead to the disease beriberi. Piglets require protein and fat for growth. Pigs usually reach full maturity around one year of age.
6. A pig's thick skin protects it from sunburn and temperature extremes. Pigs living in hot and sunny climates tend to develop thicker skin and more fat than pigs from colder regions. Their skin is often marked by dark spots, patches, or tattoos which help protect them from sun exposure.
