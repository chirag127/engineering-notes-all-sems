### Nature of data in Big Data

1. Volume: The amount of data is huge. It is in terabytes and petabytes. The data is so large that traditional databases are insufficient to store and process the data.
2. Velocity: The speed at which the data is generated and processed is very high. The data comes in at a rapid pace and needs to be processed on time to derive business value from it.
3. Variety: The data comes in all forms. It could be structured, semi-structured or unstructured data. The data could be in the form of images, videos, text, audio, etc. The variety and complexity of data makes it difficult to store and process the data.
4. Veracity: The data may be noisy, inconsistent or incomplete. The inherent inaccuracies and inconsistencies in the data pose challenges in processing the data to obtain meaningful insights. The veracity or trustworthiness of the data needs to be verified to get accurate results.

The points highlight the key characteristics of data in Big Data. The volume, velocity, variety and veracity of the data pose challenges to store, process and analyze the data using traditional techniques. New technologies and tools are required to handle the nature of data in Big Data.
