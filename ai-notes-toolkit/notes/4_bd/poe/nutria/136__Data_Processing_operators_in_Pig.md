

#### Data Processing Operators in Pig

1. **LOAD**: Loads data from the specified location into a relation.
2. **STORE**: Stores the output of a relation into the specified location.
3. **FILTER**: Filters the records based on the specified condition.
4. **FOREACH**: Applies a transformation to each record in the relation.
5. **GROUP**: Groups the records in the relation based on the specified expression.
6. **JOIN**: Joins two or more relations based on the specified condition.
7. **ORDER BY**: Orders the records in the relation based on the specified expression.
8. **DISTINCT**: Removes duplicate records from the relation.
9. **LIMIT**: Limits the number of records in the relation.
10. **COGROUP**: Groups the records in two or more relations based on the specified expression.
