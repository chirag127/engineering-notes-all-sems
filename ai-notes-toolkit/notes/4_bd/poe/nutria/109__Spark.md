
### Spark

Spark is an open-source distributed computing framework for large-scale data processing. It is designed to provide high-level APIs for data manipulation and analysis. Spark can be used for a variety of tasks, such as machine learning, streaming analytics, interactive queries, and graph processing. It is also capable of running on a variety of hardware and cloud computing platforms. Spark is highly scalable, allowing it to process large datasets quickly and efficiently. It also supports a variety of programming languages, including Java, Scala, Python, and R.
