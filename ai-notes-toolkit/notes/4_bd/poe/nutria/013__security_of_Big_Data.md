

#### Security of Big Data

1. Big Data security is the practice of protecting and managing large amounts of data.
2. It involves protecting data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction.
3. Security measures can include data encryption, data masking, access control, and data auditing.
4. Data encryption is the process of transforming data into a form that is unreadable and unusable without a key.
5. Data masking is a process of replacing sensitive information with fictitious data.
6. Access control is the process of granting and revoking access to data based on user identity and authorization.
7. Data auditing is the process of tracking and recording changes to data.
8. Big Data security also involves assessing potential threats and vulnerabilities, and implementing measures to mitigate those risks.
9. Organizations must ensure that their data is secure, compliant with regulations, and protected from malicious actors.
