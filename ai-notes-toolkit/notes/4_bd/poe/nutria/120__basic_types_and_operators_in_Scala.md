
#### Basic Types and Operators in Scala

* Scala is a type-safe language, which means that all variables must be declared with a specific type.
* The basic types of values in Scala are:
  * Integer: whole numbers, such as `1`, `2`, `3`, etc.
  * Double: decimal numbers, such as `1.0`, `2.5`, `3.14`, etc.
  * Boolean: `true` or `false`
  * String: text, such as `"Hello World!"`
* Operators are symbols that are used to perform mathematical and logical operations on values. The most common operators in Scala are:
  * Arithmetic operators: `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`
  * Comparison operators: `==`, `!=`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`
  * Logical operators: `&&`, `||`, `!`
  * Assignment operators: `=`, `+=`, `-=`, `*=`, `/=`
