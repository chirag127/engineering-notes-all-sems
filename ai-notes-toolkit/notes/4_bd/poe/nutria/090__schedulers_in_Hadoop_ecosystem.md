
#### Schedulers in Hadoop Ecosystem

* Hadoop Schedulers are responsible for managing resources and scheduling jobs in a Hadoop cluster.
* The two most commonly used schedulers are the Fair Scheduler and the Capacity Scheduler. 
* The Fair Scheduler is designed to provide a fair share of resources to each user, while the Capacity Scheduler is designed to ensure that resources are used efficiently.
* The Fair Scheduler allows users to configure pools, which are collections of jobs that share resources.
* The Capacity Scheduler allows users to configure queues, which are collections of jobs that share resources.
* Both schedulers will attempt to maximize the utilization of resources while ensuring that jobs are completed in a timely manner.
* The Fair Scheduler and the Capacity Scheduler can be configured to use either a first-in-first-out (FIFO) or a least-recently-used (LRU) scheduling algorithm.
* The Fair Scheduler and the Capacity Scheduler can also be configured to use a priority-based scheduling algorithm, which allows jobs to be scheduled based on their priority.
* The Fair Scheduler and the Capacity Scheduler can also be configured to use a delay scheduling algorithm, which allows jobs to be delayed until resources become available.
