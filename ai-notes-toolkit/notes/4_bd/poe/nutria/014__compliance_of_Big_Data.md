

#### Compliance of Big Data

* Big Data compliance is the process of ensuring that data collected and stored is done in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. 
* It is important to consider the privacy and security of data when collecting and storing it to ensure that it is compliant with applicable laws and regulations.
* Data privacy laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) have been enacted to protect the privacy of individuals and their data.
* Organizations must ensure that they are compliant with applicable laws and regulations when collecting, storing, and using data.
* Companies should have policies and procedures in place to ensure that they are compliant with applicable laws and regulations.
* Organizations should also have a risk management plan in place to mitigate any potential risks associated with data collection and storage.
* It is important to have an audit process in place to ensure that data is being collected, stored, and used in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
* Additionally, organizations should have a process in place to address any data breaches or other incidents that may occur.
