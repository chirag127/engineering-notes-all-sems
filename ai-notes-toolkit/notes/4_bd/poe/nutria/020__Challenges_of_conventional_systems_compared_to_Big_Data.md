
### Challenges of conventional systems compared to Big Data

1. Conventional systems are limited in the amount of data they can store and process, while Big Data systems are capable of handling much larger datasets.
2. Conventional systems are not able to handle unstructured data, while Big Data systems can process both structured and unstructured data.
3. Conventional systems are not able to process data in real-time, while Big Data systems can process data quickly and efficiently.
4. Conventional systems require manual intervention to update and maintain, while Big Data systems are self-updating and self-maintaining.
5. Conventional systems are not able to identify patterns or trends in data, while Big Data systems can detect patterns and trends in data.
6. Conventional systems are not able to provide insights into customer behavior or preferences, while Big Data systems can provide valuable insights into customer behavior and preferences.
