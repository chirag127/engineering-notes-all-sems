2. It has a wide range of features and capabilities, including data visualization, calculations, formulas, and macros.
3. Big Sheets allows users to easily collaborate on projects with others, and can be used to store and organize large amounts of data.
4. Big Sheets also offers a variety of features for data analysis, including the ability to filter, sort, and group data.
5. It also provides a range of charts and graphs that can be used to visualize data, as well as a range of statistical functions.
6. Big Sheets is a great tool for businesses, as it allows them to quickly and easily analyze data and make informed decisions.
