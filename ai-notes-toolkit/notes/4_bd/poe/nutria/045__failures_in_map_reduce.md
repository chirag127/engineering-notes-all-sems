
#### Failures in Map Reduce

1. Job Failure: The most common type of failure in Map Reduce is a job failure. This occurs when a job fails to complete successfully. It can happen due to a variety of reasons, such as an error in the job configuration, a system crash, or a hardware failure.

2. Task Failure: Another type of failure in Map Reduce is a task failure. This happens when a task fails to complete successfully, such as when a task runs out of memory or a network connection is lost.

3. Data Loss: Data loss can occur in Map Reduce due to a variety of reasons, such as a job failure, a task failure, or a system crash.

4. Network Congestion: Network congestion can occur in Map Reduce due to a variety of reasons, such as a large number of tasks running concurrently or a large amount of data being transferred.

5. Deadlocks: Deadlocks can occur in Map Reduce due to a variety of reasons, such as a job waiting for another job to finish or a task waiting for data to be transferred.
