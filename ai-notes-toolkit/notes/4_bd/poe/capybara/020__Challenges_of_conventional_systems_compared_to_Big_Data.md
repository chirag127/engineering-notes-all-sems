### Challenges of Conventional Systems Compared to Big Data

When it comes to managing and analyzing large amounts of data, conventional systems often fall short compared to big data solutions. Here are some of the challenges of conventional systems compared to big data:

- **Limited storage capacity:** Conventional systems often have a limited storage capacity, which means that they can only store a certain amount of data. This can be a problem when dealing with large amounts of data, as it may not be possible to store all of the data in a conventional system.

- **Slow processing speed:** Conventional systems may not be able to process data as quickly as big data solutions. This can be a problem when dealing with real-time data or when trying to analyze large datasets.

- **Lack of scalability:** Conventional systems may not be able to scale up to handle large amounts of data. This can be a problem when dealing with rapidly growing datasets.

- **Inability to handle unstructured data:** Conventional systems may not be able to handle unstructured data, such as text, images, and videos. This can be a problem when dealing with data from social media or other sources that produce unstructured data.

- **Limited analytical capabilities:** Conventional systems may not have the analytical capabilities needed to analyze large amounts of data. This can be a problem when trying to identify patterns or trends in the data.

- **Difficulty in integrating data sources:** Conventional systems may have difficulty integrating data from different sources. This can be a problem when trying to combine data from multiple sources to create a unified view of the data.

In contrast, big data solutions are designed to handle these challenges. Big data solutions are scalable, can handle unstructured data, and have powerful analytical capabilities. They are designed to process large amounts of data quickly and can integrate data from multiple sources. As a result, big data solutions are becoming increasingly popular for organizations that need to manage and analyze large amounts of data.
