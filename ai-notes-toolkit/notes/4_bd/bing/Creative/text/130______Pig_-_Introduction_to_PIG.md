#### Pig - Introduction to PIG

- Pig is a high-level scripting language that allows users to write complex data analysis programs for large-scale data sets.
- Pig is built on top of Hadoop, an open-source framework for distributed storage and processing of big data.
- Pig programs are translated into a series of MapReduce jobs that run on the Hadoop cluster.
- Pig provides a simple and expressive syntax that is similar to SQL, but also supports user-defined functions, complex data types, and nested data structures.
- Pig can handle both structured and unstructured data, and can perform various operations such as filtering, grouping, joining, sorting, aggregating, and transforming data.
- Pig can also integrate with other languages and tools, such as Python, Java, R, and Hive, to extend its functionality and interoperability.
- Pig is designed to be scalable, flexible, and extensible, and to support parallel execution and optimization of data flows.
