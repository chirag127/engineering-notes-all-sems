#### Security in Hadoop in Hadoop Environment

- Hadoop is a distributed system that stores and processes large amounts of data across multiple nodes in a cluster.
- Hadoop security is the process of protecting the data and the cluster from unauthorized access, modification, or deletion by malicious users or applications.
- Hadoop security can be divided into four main aspects: authentication, authorization, encryption, and auditing.
- Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user or an application that tries to access the cluster or the data. Hadoop supports various authentication mechanisms, such as Kerberos, LDAP, or OAuth.
- Authorization is the process of granting or denying permissions to a user or an application to perform certain actions on the cluster or the data. Hadoop supports various authorization mechanisms, such as HDFS permissions, ACLs, Ranger, or Sentry.
- Encryption is the process of transforming the data into an unreadable form to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure. Hadoop supports various encryption mechanisms, such as HDFS encryption, KMS, or TDE.
- Auditing is the process of recording and monitoring the activities and events that occur on the cluster or the data. Hadoop supports various auditing mechanisms, such as HDFS audit logs, Ranger audit, or Sentry audit.
