### Spark

Spark is a word that has two distinct meanings. As a noun, it means a small fiery particle thrown off from a fire, alight in ashes, or produced by striking together two hard surfaces such as stone or metal. Or as a verb, it means emit sparks of fire or electricity.

Some points to remember about spark are:

- A spark can also be a small flash of light produced by a sudden disruptive electrical discharge through the air.
- A spark can also be an electrical discharge serving to ignite the explosive mixture in an internal combustion engine.
- A spark can also be a trace of a specified quality or intense feeling, such as anger, interest, or excitement.
- A spark can also be a sense of liveliness and excitement, or a lively young fellow.
- A spark can also be used to describe the act of providing the stimulus for something, such as a fire, a protest, or a romance.
- A spark can also be used to describe the act of engaging in courtship.

Some examples of using spark in sentences are:

- A log fire was sending sparks onto the rug.
- Angry sparks were flashing in her eyes.
- There was a spark of light.
- A tiny spark of anger flared within her.
- There was a spark between them at their first meeting.
- The ignition sparks as soon as the gas is turned on.
- The explosion sparked a fire.
- The severity of the plan sparked off street protests.
- He went a sparking among the rosy country girls.
