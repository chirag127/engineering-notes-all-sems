### History of Big Data Innovation

1. The term "Big Data" was first used in the 1990s to describe the large amounts of data being generated by businesses, governments, and other organizations.
2. In 2005, Roger Mougalas from O'Reilly Media coined the term "Big Data" to refer to the large datasets that were difficult to store, manage, and analyze using traditional database technologies.
3. In 2010, the Apache Hadoop project, an open-source software framework for distributed storage and processing of large datasets, became widely adopted by companies and organizations.
4. In 2011, McKinsey Global Institute published a report highlighting the potential of Big Data to drive innovation, productivity, and economic growth.
5. In 2012, the Obama administration announced the "Big Data Research and Development Initiative" to invest in research and development of Big Data technologies.
6. In 2014, the term "Data Science" became widely used to describe the interdisciplinary field of extracting knowledge and insights from data.
7. In 2015, the Apache Spark project, an open-source data processing engine, became widely adopted for its ability to process large datasets in real-time.
8. In 2016, the rise of the Internet of Things (IoT) led to an explosion of data generated by connected devices, further driving the need for Big Data technologies.
9. In 2017, the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) for data analysis became increasingly popular, allowing for more advanced insights and predictions from Big Data.
10. In 2018, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) came into effect in the European Union, highlighting the importance of data privacy and security in the age of Big Data.

Mnemonic: **1990s, 2005, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018** can be remembered as **90s, 5, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18**. These are the years when major events in the history of Big Data innovation occurred.
