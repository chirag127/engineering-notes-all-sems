# Pig

- Pigs are intelligent, social animals that belong to the family Suidae.
- They are found all over the world, with the exception of Antarctica.
- Pigs are omnivores and eat a wide variety of foods, including plants, insects, and small animals.
- They have a keen sense of smell and are often used to locate truffles, a type of edible fungus.
- Pigs are known for their love of mud, which helps them regulate their body temperature and protect their skin from the sun and insects.
- There are many different breeds of pigs, including the domestic pig, which is commonly raised for meat.
- Pigs have been domesticated for thousands of years and have played an important role in many cultures.
- In some cultures, pigs are considered a symbol of wealth and prosperity.
- Pigs are intelligent and can be trained to perform a variety of tasks, including herding, hunting, and even playing video games.
- Despite their intelligence and social nature, pigs are often subjected to inhumane conditions on factory farms.
