### IBM Big Data strategy

- IBM, a US-based computer hardware and software manufacturer, had implemented a Big Data strategy, where the company offered solutions to store, manage, and analyze the huge amounts of data generated daily and equipped large and small companies to make informed business decisions .
- Smarter Planet was a corporate initiative of IBM, which sought to highlight how government and business leaders were capturing the potential of smarter systems to achieve economic and sustainable growth and societal progress.
- IBM suggests a six-step process to design a data strategy: 1) Understand your business objectives, 2) Assess your current state, 3) Map out data strategy framework, 4) Define your data’s target, 5) Implement your data strategy, and 6) Monitor and adjust your data strategy.
- IBM also recommends giving data assets and accelerators top priority, developing a process and culture around data that enables true standardization, re-use, portability, speed to action, and risk reduction across the end-to-end data lifecycle.
