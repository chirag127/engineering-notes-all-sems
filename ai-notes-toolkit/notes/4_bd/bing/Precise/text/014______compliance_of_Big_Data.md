#### Compliance of Big Data

1. **Data Protection and Privacy:** Organizations must ensure that they comply with data protection and privacy regulations when handling big data. This includes obtaining consent from individuals before collecting and using their personal data, and ensuring that the data is stored securely and used only for the purposes for which it was collected.

2. **Data Retention:** Organizations must comply with data retention regulations, which specify how long data must be kept and when it must be deleted. This is particularly important for sensitive data, such as financial or medical records.

3. **Data Quality:** Organizations must ensure that the data they collect and use is accurate, complete, and up-to-date. This is important for making informed decisions and avoiding errors.

4. **Data Governance:** Organizations must have a data governance framework in place to ensure that data is managed in a responsible and ethical manner. This includes having clear policies and procedures for data collection, storage, use, and sharing.

5. **Transparency:** Organizations must be transparent about their data collection and use practices. This includes providing clear and concise information to individuals about how their data is being used, and giving them the ability to access, correct, or delete their personal data.

6. **Accountability:** Organizations must be accountable for their data practices and must be able to demonstrate compliance with data protection and privacy regulations. This includes having regular audits and assessments to ensure that data is being handled in a responsible and compliant manner.

7. **Ethical Use of Data:** Organizations must ensure that they use data in an ethical manner, avoiding practices that could harm individuals or society. This includes avoiding discriminatory or biased decision-making based on data, and ensuring that data is used for the benefit of society as a whole.
