#### Introduction to NoSQL databases

- NoSQL databases are non-relational databases that store and retrieve data in ways that do not involve the use of a fixed schema like traditional relational databases.
- NoSQL databases are designed to handle large volumes of structured, semi-structured, or unstructured data.
- They are often used in big data and real-time web applications.
- NoSQL databases can be classified into four main types: document-based, key-value, column-family, and graph databases.
- Some popular NoSQL databases include MongoDB, Cassandra, Redis, and Neo4j.
- NoSQL databases offer several advantages over traditional relational databases, including scalability, flexibility, and performance.
- However, they may not be suitable for all use cases and may require a different approach to data modeling and querying.
