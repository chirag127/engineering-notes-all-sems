### Spark

- Apache Spark is an open-source distributed computing system.
- It is designed to process large volumes of data in parallel across a cluster of computers.
- Spark can process data in memory, making it faster than traditional disk-based systems like Hadoop.
- It supports a wide range of data processing tasks, including SQL queries, machine learning, and graph processing.
- Spark has a rich set of APIs for different languages, including Scala, Java, Python, and R.
- It has a thriving community of developers and users, with regular releases and updates.
- Spark can be deployed on a variety of platforms, including standalone, on-premises clusters, and cloud-based services.
- It is widely used in industry and academia for big data processing and analysis.
