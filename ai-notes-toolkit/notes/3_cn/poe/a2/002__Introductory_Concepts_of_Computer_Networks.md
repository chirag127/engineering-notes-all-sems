### Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks

1. Network: A network is a set of devices connected by physical media links. A network is recursively is a connection of two or more nodes by a physical link or two or more networks connected by one or more nodes.

2. Node: A node refers to a connection point, either a redistribution point or an end point for data transmissions. Examples of network nodes are computers, printers, and routers.

3. Link: A link refers to the physical or logical connection between two nodes. Examples of links are cables, telephone lines, and wireless connections.

4. Protocol: A protocol is a set of rules that govern data communication. It specifies the format, timing, sequencing, and error checking of data transmissions in a network. Examples of protocols are Ethernet, TCP, and IP.

5. LAN: A local area network (LAN) connects network devices in a local geographic area, such as an office building or home. Examples of LANs are Ethernet and WiFi networks.

6. WAN: A wide area network (WAN) connects network devices in a large geographic area, such as a city, country, or the world. Examples of WANs are mobile networks and the Internet.

7. Internet: The Internet is a global system of interconnected computer networks that use the TCP/IP protocol suite to communicate between networks and devices. It is the world's largest WAN, connecting billions of users and networks.
