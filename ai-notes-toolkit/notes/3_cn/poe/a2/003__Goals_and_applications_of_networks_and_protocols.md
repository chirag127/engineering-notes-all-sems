#### Goals and applications of networks and protocols.

1. Connectivity: The primary goal of networks and protocols is to provide connectivity between two or more devices. This allows devices and users to share data and resources.
2. Resource sharing: Networks enable sharing of resources such as storage, software, printers, etc. This reduces cost and increases efficiency.
3. Communication: Networks facilitate communication through email, instant messaging, video calling, etc. This allows people to communicate and collaborate from anywhere.
4. Access to information: The Internet provides access to a vast repository of information. This enables people to stay up-to-date with developments around the world.
5. Business: Networks form the backbone of e-commerce and business. They enable services like online banking, shopping, payment gateways, etc. This boosts business and economic activity.
6. Entertainment: Networks enable streaming of multimedia content and online gaming. This allows people to access entertainment from anywhere on devices like computers, tablets, and smartphones.
