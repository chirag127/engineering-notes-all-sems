#### Protocols and Standards in Network Architecture

1. Protocols: Protocols are rules and procedures for communication between devices on a network. They govern the format and order of messages exchanged between two or more communicating devices. Some important protocols used in computer networks are:
- HTTP: Used for accessing web pages on the Internet.
- FTP: Used for file transfer between devices.
- SMTP: Used for sending and receiving e-mails.
- TCP/IP: The fundamental protocols underlying the Internet. They provide end-to-end connectivity between devices on the Internet.

2. Standards: Standards ensure interoperability between devices from different vendors. Some important standards used in computer networks are:
- Ethernet: A family of standards for local area networks.
- Wi-Fi: A set of standards for wireless local area networks.
- 3G/4G: Standards for cellular data communication.
- ASCII: A standard for encoding English characters.
- HTML: A standard for creating web pages.

Following standards and protocols enables seamless communication between various devices and systems in a computer network. They are essential for the functioning of the Internet and networking applications.
