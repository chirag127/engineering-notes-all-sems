### Point-to-point networks in network layer

1. Point-to-point networks provide a dedicated link between two devices.
2. Each device has its own independent link connecting it to the other device.
3. Data is transmitted between the devices directly over the link.
4. The entire capacity of the link is reserved for transmission between the two devices.
5. No data from other devices is multiplexed on the link.
6. The two devices have sole access to the link.
7. Point-to-point networks are typically used to connect two remote devices and provide high-speed, low latency connections such as a T1/E1 link between two routers.
8. The link can be either physical (such as a leased line) or logical (such as an ATM virtual circuit or Frame Relay permanent virtual circuit).
