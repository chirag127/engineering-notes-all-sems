## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks and Physical Layer

1. Computer Networks: A computer network is a group of computers that are interconnected and able to communicate with each other to share resources and data. Networks allow computers to connect and share resources with other computers and devices.
2. Elements of Computer Networks: The key elements of a computer network are:
- Network interface cards (NICs): Provide a physical connection to the network and handle sending and receiving data.
- Network cables: Connect devices on the network with cables that transmit data.
- Network devices: Devices such as routers, switches, and servers that transfer data and connect network segments.
- Protocols: Rules and standards that govern communication between network devices. Protocols such as TCP/IP are essential for transmitting data between networks and computers.
3. Types of Networks: The most common types of computer networks are:
- Local Area Networks (LANs): Connect devices in a small geographical area like an office or home. Use network cables or Wi-Fi.
- Wide Area Networks (WANs): Connect devices in a large geographical area. Use leased lines, fiber optic cables or satellites. The internet is the largest WAN.
4. The OSI Model: The Open Systems Interconnection (OSI) model defines a framework for implementing protocols in seven layers. Control is passed down from higher to lower layers. It allows different networks to communicate through standard interfaces.
