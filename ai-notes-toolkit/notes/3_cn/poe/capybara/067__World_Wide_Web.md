### World Wide Web

The World Wide Web (WWW) is an information system that allows users to access and share information through the internet. Here are some key points to know about the World Wide Web:

- The World Wide Web was invented in 1989 by British computer scientist Tim Berners-Lee while working at CERN, the European Organization for Nuclear Research.
- The World Wide Web is often confused with the internet, but they are not the same thing. The internet is the physical network of computers and servers that underpins the World Wide Web. The World Wide Web is one of the most popular ways to use the internet.
- The World Wide Web is based on a system of hypertext links that allow users to navigate between different web pages. Hypertext links are clickable links that take the user to a different page or website.
- The World Wide Web is made up of millions of individual web pages that are hosted on servers all over the world. These web pages can include text, images, videos, and other multimedia content.
- The World Wide Web is accessible through web browsers, such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge. Users can access the World Wide Web on a range of devices, including desktop computers, laptops, smartphones, and tablets.
- The World Wide Web has had a profound impact on the way we access and share information. It has revolutionized the way we communicate, do business, and access knowledge. Today, the World Wide Web is an essential part of modern life for billions of people around the world.
- The World Wide Web has also raised concerns about privacy, security, and the spread of misinformation. As the World Wide Web continues to evolve, it will be important for users to be aware of these issues and to take steps to protect themselves online.
