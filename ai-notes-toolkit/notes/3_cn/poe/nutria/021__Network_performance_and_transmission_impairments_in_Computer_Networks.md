
#### Network Performance and Transmission Impairments in Computer Networks

* Network performance is affected by a number of factors, including transmission impairments, such as noise and interference, as well as delays and errors. 
* Noise is any unwanted signal that can interfere with the transmission of data and reduce the quality of the signal. 
* Interference is any signal that can interfere with the transmission of data, such as radio frequency interference (RFI) and electromagnetic interference (EMI). 
* Delays can occur due to the speed of light and the distance between the sender and the receiver. 
* Errors occur due to faulty hardware or software, or due to incorrect transmission of data. 
* Transmission impairments can be minimized by using error detection and correction techniques, such as parity and checksum. 
* Transmission impairments can also be minimized by using better signal-to-noise ratio, better coding, and better channel equalization.
