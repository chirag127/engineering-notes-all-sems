
#### Network devices in Computer Networks 

1. Routers: Routers are used to forward data packets across networks. They use routing protocols to determine the best path for a data packet to take from one network to another. Routers can also be used to connect multiple networks together, such as LANs and WANs.

2. Switches: Switches are used to connect multiple devices in a single network. They are used to create a local area network (LAN) and are often used to connect computers, printers, and other devices together.

3. Hubs: Hubs are used to connect multiple devices in a single network. They act as a central connection point for all devices in the network and are used to send and receive data.

4. Bridges: Bridges are used to connect two or more networks together. They are used to create a wide area network (WAN) and are often used to connect LANs together.

5. Modems: Modems are used to convert digital signals into analog signals and vice versa. They are used to connect computers to the internet and are often used to connect computers to a cable or DSL connection.
