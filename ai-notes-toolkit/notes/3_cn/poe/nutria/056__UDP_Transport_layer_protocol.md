
#### UDP Transport Layer Protocol

* UDP is a connectionless protocol used at the transport layer of the Internet Protocol Suite. 
* It is a best-effort protocol, meaning that it does not guarantee delivery or correct sequencing of data. 
* UDP is used for applications that do not require reliable delivery of data, such as streaming media and real-time gaming. 
* UDP is a datagram-oriented protocol, meaning that it operates on a packet-by-packet basis and does not require a virtual circuit to be established before packets are sent. 
* UDP packets are typically sent over IP networks, but can also be used over other network types. 
* UDP does not provide any flow control, error control, or order control. 
* UDP is a lightweight protocol, meaning that it has a small header size and requires minimal processing by the host.
