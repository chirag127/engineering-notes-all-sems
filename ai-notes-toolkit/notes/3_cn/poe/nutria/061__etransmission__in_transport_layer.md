
### eTransmission in Transport Layer

1. eTransmission is the process of sending digital data from one device to another over a network.
2. The transport layer is responsible for providing reliable data transmission services between two endpoints.
3. It is responsible for providing reliable data transmission services, error control, flow control, segmentation, and reassembly of data.
4. The transport layer protocols provide end-to-end communication between two applications.
5. The most common transport layer protocols are TCP and UDP.
6. TCP is a connection-oriented protocol that provides reliable data transmission services. It guarantees that data sent from one end will be received at the other end in the same order as it was sent.
7. UDP is a connectionless protocol that provides unreliable data transmission services. It does not guarantee that data sent from one end will be received at the other end in the same order as it was sent.
8. The transport layer also provides applications with services such as port numbers, segmentation, and multiplexing.
9. Port numbers are used by the transport layer to identify the applications that are communicating.
10. Segmentation is the process of breaking up a large data stream into smaller chunks for transmission over a network.
11. Multiplexing is the process of combining multiple data streams into one for transmission over a network.
