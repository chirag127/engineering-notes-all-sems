
#### Framing in link layer in Computer Networks

* Framing is the process of dividing data into frames before transmission takes place. It is used to ensure that data is transmitted reliably over a network.
* In the Link Layer of the OSI Model, the physical layer is responsible for the physical transmission of data across the network. The data is divided into frames by the link layer before being sent to the physical layer.
* Each frame contains the data to be transmitted, as well as information about the frame, such as its length, source, and destination.
* The frames are then transmitted over the network and reassembled at the destination.
* Framing is important for reliable transmission of data, as it ensures that data is not corrupted or lost during transmission. It also ensures that data is received in the same order it was sent.
