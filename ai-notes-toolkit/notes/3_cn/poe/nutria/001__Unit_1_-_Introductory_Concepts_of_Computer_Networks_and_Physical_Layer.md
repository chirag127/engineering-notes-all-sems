
## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks and Physical Layer

1. Computer networks are a system of interconnected computers that exchange data and share resources.

2. The physical layer is the lowest layer of the OSI model and is responsible for transmitting raw bits over a physical medium.

3. The physical layer is made up of hardware components such as cables, connectors, and network interface cards.

4. Network topologies are the physical and logical arrangement of computers and other devices in a network. Common network topologies include bus, star, and ring.

5. Network protocols are the set of rules that govern communication between two or more devices on a network.

6. Network media are the physical transmission mediums used to connect computers and other devices in a network. Examples include twisted pair, coaxial cable, and fiber optic cable.

7. Network devices are the hardware components used to connect and manage the flow of data in a computer network. Examples include routers, switches, and bridges.
