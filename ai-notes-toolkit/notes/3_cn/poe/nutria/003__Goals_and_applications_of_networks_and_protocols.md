

#### Goals and Applications of Networks and Protocols

1. The primary goal of networks and protocols is to enable communication between two or more devices. This includes exchanging data, sharing resources, and providing access to the Internet.

2. Networks and protocols provide the necessary infrastructure for devices to communicate with each other. They allow devices to send and receive data, as well as manage the flow of data.

3. Networks and protocols are used to establish a secure connection between two or more devices. This is accomplished through the use of encryption and authentication protocols.

4. Networks and protocols are also used to provide access to the Internet. This includes providing access to websites, email, online services, and other applications.

5. Networks and protocols are used to provide quality of service (QoS) to applications. This includes ensuring that applications have the bandwidth and latency necessary to perform their tasks.

6. Networks and protocols are used to route data between two or more devices. This is accomplished through the use of routing protocols, which determine the best path for data to travel.

7. Networks and protocols are used to provide network security. This includes preventing unauthorized access to data, protecting against malicious attacks, and ensuring the integrity of data.
