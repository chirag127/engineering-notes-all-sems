
#### Categories of networks in computer networks

1. Local Area Networks (LANs): These are networks that are used within a small geographical area, such as a home, office, school, or other building. They typically use Ethernet or Wi-Fi technology and have a limited area of coverage.

2. Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs): These are larger than LANs and are typically used to connect multiple LANs in a city or region. They use high-speed connections such as fiber optic cables.

3. Wide Area Networks (WANs): These are networks that span multiple cities, states, countries, or even continents. They are typically used by large organizations or governments to connect multiple offices. They use dedicated leased lines or public networks such as the Internet.

4. Wireless Networks: These are networks that use radio waves to transmit data. They are typically used for mobile devices and are becoming increasingly popular in homes and offices.

5. Virtual Private Networks (VPNs): These are networks that use encryption to provide secure connections over public networks such as the Internet. They are used by businesses to provide secure access to their internal networks from remote locations.
