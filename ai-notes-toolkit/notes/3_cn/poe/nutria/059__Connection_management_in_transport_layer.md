
### Connection management in transport layer

1. Connection management is the process of setting up and maintaining communication between two or more computers in a network. 
2. In the Transport Layer of the OSI (Open Systems Interconnection) model, the connection management protocol is responsible for providing reliable end-to-end communication between two endpoints. 
3. The most common protocol used for connection management in the Transport Layer is the Transmission Control Protocol (TCP). 
4. TCP establishes a connection between two endpoints by exchanging a series of messages known as the three-way handshake. 
5. Once the connection is established, data can be exchanged between the endpoints. 
6. The connection is maintained by sending periodic messages to check the status of the connection. 
7. When one of the endpoints wants to terminate the connection, a four-way handshake is used to close the connection. 
8. Other protocols used for connection management in the Transport Layer include the User Datagram Protocol (UDP) and the Stream Control Transmission Protocol (SCTP).
