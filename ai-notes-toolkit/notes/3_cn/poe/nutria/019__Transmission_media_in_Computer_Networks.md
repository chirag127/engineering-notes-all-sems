
#### Transmission media in Computer Networks

* Transmission media are physical channels which are used to transmit data signals between two or more devices. 
* The most commonly used transmission media are twisted pair cables, coaxial cables, fiber-optic cables, and wireless media.
* Twisted pair cables are the most common type of transmission media used in computer networks. They are made of two insulated copper wires twisted together in pairs. 
* Coaxial cables are used in cable television and broadband internet connections. They are made of a single copper wire surrounded by insulation and a metal shield. 
* Fiber-optic cables are made of glass or plastic fibers and are used for high-speed data transmission over long distances. 
* Wireless media, such as radio waves, microwaves, and infrared waves, are used to transmit data without the need for physical cables.
