#### ISP

ISP stands for Internet Service Provider. An ISP is a company that provides internet access to customers. Here are some key points to remember about ISPs:

- ISPs provide internet access to customers through various means, such as cable, DSL, or fiber-optic connections.
- ISPs may offer different plans with varying speeds and data allowances.
- Some ISPs may also offer additional services, such as email, web hosting, or virtual private networks (VPNs).
- To connect to the internet, a customer typically needs a modem and a router. The modem connects to the ISP's network, while the router allows multiple devices to share the internet connection.
- ISPs may have peering agreements with other ISPs to exchange traffic and improve connectivity.
- Net neutrality is a principle that ISPs should treat all internet traffic equally, without blocking or slowing down certain content.

A helpful mnemonic to remember the key functions of an ISP is **I**nternet **S**ervice **P**rovider = **I**nternet **S**peed and **P**lans.
