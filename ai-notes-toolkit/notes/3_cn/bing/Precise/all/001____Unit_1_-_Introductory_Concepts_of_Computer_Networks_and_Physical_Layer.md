## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks and Physical Layer

1. **Computer Networks** are a group of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share resources.
2. The **Physical Layer** is the first layer of the OSI model and is responsible for transmitting raw data bits over a physical medium.
3. The Physical Layer defines the electrical, mechanical, procedural, and functional specifications for activating, maintaining, and deactivating the physical link between communicating network systems.
4. Some common physical layer transmission mediums include copper wire, fiber optic cable, and wireless transmission.
5. The Physical Layer is also responsible for converting digital data into signals that can be transmitted over the physical medium.
6. Some common Physical Layer protocols include Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.
7. A **Mnemonic** to remember the OSI model layers is "Please Do Not Throw Sausage Pizza Away" (Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, Application).
8. A **learning trick** for the Physical Layer is to remember that it is the first layer and is responsible for the physical transmission of data. Think of it as the foundation of a building, without which the upper layers cannot function.
