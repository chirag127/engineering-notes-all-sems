### Basic Concepts of Cryptography in Application Layer

1. Cryptography is the practice of securing communication in the presence of third parties.
2. Cryptography in the application layer refers to the use of cryptographic techniques to secure data at the application level.
3. Cryptography can be used to achieve various security goals, such as confidentiality, integrity, and authentication.
4. Confidentiality is the property that ensures that the data is only accessible to authorized parties.
5. Integrity is the property that ensures that the data has not been tampered with.
6. Authentication is the property that ensures that the data comes from a trusted source.
7. Cryptography can be achieved through various techniques, such as encryption, hashing, and digital signatures.
8. Encryption is the process of converting plaintext into ciphertext, which is unreadable without the key.
9. Hashing is the process of converting data into a fixed-size string of characters, which can be used to verify the integrity of the data.
10. Digital signatures are used to verify the authenticity of a message, by using a combination of hashing and encryption.
11. Cryptography in the application layer can be implemented through various protocols, such as SSL/TLS and PGP.
12. SSL/TLS is a protocol used to secure communication over the internet, by encrypting the data transmitted between the client and the server.
13. PGP is a protocol used to secure email communication, by encrypting the email messages and providing digital signatures.
