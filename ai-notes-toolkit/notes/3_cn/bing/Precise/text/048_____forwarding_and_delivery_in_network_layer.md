### Forwarding and Delivery in Network Layer

- The network layer is responsible for forwarding and delivering data packets from the source to the destination.
- Forwarding refers to the process of moving a packet from one network element to another, while delivery refers to the process of delivering the packet to the final destination.
- The network layer uses routing algorithms to determine the best path for forwarding the packets.
- The forwarding process involves examining the destination address of the packet and selecting the appropriate outgoing link to forward the packet.
- The delivery process involves delivering the packet to the final destination, which may involve forwarding the packet through multiple intermediate nodes.
- The network layer may use various techniques to ensure reliable delivery of packets, such as error detection and correction, retransmission of lost packets, and flow control.
- The network layer may also provide quality of service (QoS) guarantees, such as bandwidth, delay, and jitter, to ensure that the packets are delivered in a timely and reliable manner.
- The network layer may also provide security features, such as encryption and authentication, to ensure the confidentiality and integrity of the data being transmitted.
