### Introductory Concepts of Computer Networks

1. **Computer Network**: A computer network is a group of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share resources.
2. **Types of Networks**: There are several types of networks, including Local Area Networks (LANs), Wide Area Networks (WANs), Metropolitan Area Networks (MANs), and Personal Area Networks (PANs).
3. **Network Topologies**: The physical and logical arrangement of devices in a network is known as its topology. Common topologies include bus, star, ring, and mesh.
4. **Network Protocols**: Protocols are the rules and standards that govern communication between devices on a network. Some common network protocols include TCP/IP, HTTP, FTP, and SMTP.
5. **Network Security**: Network security involves protecting the network and its resources from unauthorized access, misuse, and attacks. Common security measures include firewalls, encryption, and user authentication.
6. **Network Services**: Networks provide a variety of services to their users, including file sharing, printing, email, and internet access.
7. **Network Performance**: The performance of a network is determined by factors such as its speed, reliability, and capacity. Network performance can be improved through techniques such as traffic management and load balancing.
