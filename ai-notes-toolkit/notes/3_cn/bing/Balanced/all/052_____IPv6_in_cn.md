### IPv6 in cn

- IPv6 is the latest version of the Internet Protocol (IP), which is the set of rules that defines how devices communicate over the Internet.
- IPv6 was designed to overcome the limitations of IPv4, such as the exhaustion of address space, the lack of end-to-end connectivity, and the complexity of network configuration and management.
- IPv6 uses 128-bit addresses, which can support 2^128 (approximately 3.4×10^38) possible addresses, compared to 2^32 (approximately 4.3 billion) addresses in IPv4.
- IPv6 also introduces new features, such as stateless address autoconfiguration, neighbor discovery, multicast, anycast, mobility, security, and transition mechanisms.
- China is one of the leading countries in IPv6 deployment and adoption, with the goal of running a single-stack IPv6 network by 2030, and having 700 million active IPv6 users and 200 million Internet of Things devices using IPv6 by 2023.
- China has allocated a total of 1,024 /32 IPv6 prefixes to various entities, such as Internet service providers, universities, enterprises, and government agencies, as of December 2018.
- China has also implemented several IPv6 projects and initiatives, such as the China Next Generation Internet (CNGI), the China Education and Research Network (CERNET), the China IPv6 Council, and the China IPv6 Forum, to promote the research, development, and application of IPv6 in the country.
- Some of the benefits of IPv6 for China include:
  - Enhancing the network performance, security, and scalability for the growing number of Internet users and devices in China.
  - Supporting the development of emerging technologies and applications, such as cloud computing, big data, artificial intelligence, and 5G.
  - Improving the global connectivity and interoperability of China's Internet infrastructure and services.
  - Contributing to the innovation and leadership of China in the Internet industry and governance.

- Some of the challenges of IPv6 for China include:
  - Coordinating the transition and coexistence of IPv4 and IPv6 among different stakeholders and sectors.
  - Addressing the technical, economic, and policy issues related to IPv6 deployment and adoption.
  - Enhancing the awareness, education, and training of IPv6 among the public and professionals.
  - Developing and testing the IPv6 compatibility and functionality of various devices, software, and applications.

- Some of the mnemonics and learning tricks for IPv6 in cn include:
  - Remembering the IPv6 address format: 8 groups of 4 hexadecimal digits, separated by colons, with leading zeros and consecutive zeros omitted. For example, 2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:ff00:0042:8329 can be written as 2001:db8::ff00:42:8329.
  - Remembering the IPv6 address types: unicast (one-to-one), multicast (one-to-many), anycast (one-to-nearest), and broadcast (one-to-all). Unicast addresses start with 2 or 3, multicast addresses start with ff, anycast addresses are a subset of unicast addresses, and broadcast addresses are replaced by multicast addresses in IPv6.
  - Remembering the IPv6 address scopes: global (worldwide), unique local (private), link-local (local network), and site-local (deprecated). Global addresses start with 2 or 3, unique local addresses start with fc or fd, link-local addresses start with fe80, and site-local addresses start with fec0.
  - Remembering the IPv6 special addresses: loopback (::1), unspecified (::), IPv4-mapped (::ffff:0:0/96), and IPv4-compatible (::/96, deprecated). Loopback address is used to test the local host, unspecified address is used when the source address is unknown, IPv4-mapped address is used to represent an IPv4 address in an IPv6 context, and IPv4-compatible address is used to tunnel IPv6 packets over IPv4 networks.
