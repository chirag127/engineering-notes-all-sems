#### ISP

- ISP stands for **Internet Service Provider**  , a company that provides access to the internet and other related services to customers.
- ISPs can offer different types of internet connections, such as dial-up, broadband, cable, DSL, fiber-optic, satellite, wireless, etc. Each type has its own advantages and disadvantages in terms of speed, reliability, availability, and cost.
- ISPs can also provide other services, such as email accounts, domain registration, web hosting, browser services, security software, cloud storage, etc. Some ISPs may bundle these services with the internet access or offer them separately.
- ISPs can charge customers based on different factors, such as the type and speed of the internet connection, the amount of data usage, the duration of the contract, the number and quality of the additional services, etc. Some ISPs may offer discounts or incentives for loyal customers or referrals.
- ISPs can host websites for businesses and individuals, and can also build the websites themselves. ISPs can also provide technical support, maintenance, and troubleshooting for the websites and the internet connections.
- ISPs can be classified into different categories, such as Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 ISPs, based on their network size, coverage, and peering agreements. Tier 1 ISPs are the largest and most interconnected ISPs, which can reach every other network on the internet without paying any fees. Tier 2 ISPs are smaller and less interconnected ISPs, which have to pay fees to Tier 1 ISPs or other Tier 2 ISPs to reach some parts of the internet. Tier 3 ISPs are the smallest and most local ISPs, which have to pay fees to Tier 1 or Tier 2 ISPs to access the internet.
- ISPs can also be classified into different categories, such as access ISPs, hosting ISPs, transit ISPs, virtual ISPs, free ISPs, etc., based on their business model, service offerings, and customer base. Access ISPs provide internet access to customers, hosting ISPs provide web hosting and related services, transit ISPs provide network access to other ISPs, virtual ISPs resell the services of other ISPs, free ISPs provide internet access for free or in exchange for advertising, etc.

- A mnemonic to remember the meaning of ISP is **I**nternet **S**ervice **P**rovider, or **I** **S**urf the web with **P**rofessional help.
