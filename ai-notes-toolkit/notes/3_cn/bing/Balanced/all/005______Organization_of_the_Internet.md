#### Organization of the Internet

The Internet is a global network of interconnected computers and devices that communicate using standardized protocols and procedures. The Internet has different levels of organization that affect how it operates and who controls it. These levels are:

- **Computer and network hardware**: This level consists of the physical devices and infrastructure that enable data transmission and routing across the Internet. Examples of hardware include routers, switches, cables, servers, and modems. Hardware can be owned and managed by different entities, such as governments, corporations, universities, or individuals.
- **Internet access and Internet service providers (ISPs)**: This level involves the provision of access to the Internet for end users and organizations. ISPs are companies that offer Internet connectivity and related services, such as email, web hosting, and domain name registration. ISPs can vary in size, scope, and quality of service. They can also impose different policies and restrictions on their customers, such as bandwidth limits, content filtering, or censorship.
- **Navigation within the Internet**: This level concerns the methods and tools that help users find and access information and resources on the Internet. Examples of navigation tools include web browsers, search engines, directories, and hyperlinks. Navigation tools can be influenced by various factors, such as algorithms, popularity, relevance, and personalization. They can also be subject to manipulation, bias, or fraud, such as spam, phishing, or fake news.
- **Individual sites and communities**: This level refers to the specific websites, platforms, and online groups that users visit and participate in on the Internet. Examples of sites and communities include social media, blogs, forums, wikis, and e-commerce. Sites and communities can have their own rules and terms of usage, such as privacy policies, codes of conduct, and moderation systems. They can also create and shape the culture, norms, and values of the Internet users.
