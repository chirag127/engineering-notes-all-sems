#### IP

IP can have different meanings depending on the context. Here are some possible definitions of IP:

- **Intellectual property**: This is a legal term that refers to creations of the mind, such as inventions, literary and artistic works, designs, symbols, names and images used in commerce. Intellectual property is protected by laws such as patents, copyrights and trademarks, which enable people to earn recognition or financial benefit from what they invent or create.
- **Internet protocol**: This is a technical term that refers to a set of rules that enable the modern internet. It specifies the format for addresses and units of transmitted data. Every computer, modem, server and smartphone that connects to the internet has a unique IP address, which helps users to find it. Internet protocol is often used with another protocol called transmission control protocol (TCP), which ensures the reliable delivery of data over the internet.
- **Innings pitched**: This is a sports term that refers to a statistic in baseball and softball. It measures the number of innings a pitcher has completed, from the start of a game until he or she is replaced by another pitcher. One inning is equal to three outs, so if a pitcher retires three batters in a row, he or she has pitched one inning. Innings pitched is used to calculate other statistics such as earned run average (ERA) and strikeouts per nine innings (K/9).
