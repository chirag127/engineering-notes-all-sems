#### ISP

- ISP stands for **Internet Service Provider** .
- An ISP is a company that provides **access to the internet** to both personal and business customers .
- ISPs charge a **fee** for their services, which may vary depending on the speed, bandwidth, and type of connection.
- ISPs may also provide other services such as **email, domain registration, web hosting, and browser services** .
- ISPs can **host websites** for businesses and can also **build the websites** themselves.
- ISPs connect their customers to the internet through different methods, such as **cable, DSL, fiber-optic, satellite, or wireless**.
- ISPs are regulated by different laws and agencies depending on the country and region they operate in. Some of the issues that ISPs may face are **privacy, censorship, net neutrality, and competition**.
