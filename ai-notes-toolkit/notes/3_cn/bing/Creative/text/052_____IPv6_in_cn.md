### IPv6 in China

- IPv6 is the latest version of the Internet Protocol, which provides virtually unlimited IP addresses for electronic devices to communicate online.
- China is one of the leading countries in IPv6 deployment and application, with the goal of running a single-stack IPv6 network by 2030.
- China has over 900 million mobile subscribers and over 670 million 4G/LTE subscribers, which require a large number of IP addresses.
- China has implemented a series of policies and measures to promote IPv6, such as issuing IPv6 licenses, allocating IPv6 address resources, building IPv6 backbone networks, upgrading IPv6 applications and services, and enhancing IPv6 security and testing .
- China has achieved remarkable progress in IPv6 adoption, with more than 700 million active IPv6 users and 200 million IoT devices using IPv6 as of 2023 .
- China also aims to shape the IPv6 standards and contribute to the global IPv6 development, by participating in international organizations, forums, and events, and by conducting research and innovation on IPv6 technologies.
