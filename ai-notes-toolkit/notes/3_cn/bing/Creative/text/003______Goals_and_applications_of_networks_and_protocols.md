#### Goals and applications of networks and protocols

- A network is a collection of devices that can communicate with each other through a medium, such as wires, cables, radio waves, or optical fibers.
- A protocol is a set of rules and conventions that govern how devices exchange information over a network.
- The main goals of networks and protocols are:
  - To enable data sharing and collaboration among different users and applications.
  - To provide reliable and efficient transmission of data across different types of networks and devices.
  - To ensure security and privacy of data and users on the network.
  - To support scalability and interoperability of networks and devices.
- Some examples of applications of networks and protocols are:
  - The Internet, which is a global network of networks that uses the Internet Protocol (IP) and other protocols to connect billions of devices and users.
  - The World Wide Web, which is an application that uses the Hypertext Transfer Protocol (HTTP) and other protocols to deliver web pages and multimedia content over the Internet.
  - Email, which is an application that uses the Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) and other protocols to send and receive messages over the Internet.
  - Voice over IP (VoIP), which is an application that uses the Real-time Transport Protocol (RTP) and other protocols to transmit voice and video calls over the Internet.
  - File Transfer Protocol (FTP), which is an application that uses the Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) and other protocols to transfer files between devices over the Internet.
