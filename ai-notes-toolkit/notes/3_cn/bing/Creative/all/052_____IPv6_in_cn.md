### IPv6 in cn

- IPv6 is the latest version of the Internet Protocol (IP), which is the set of rules that defines how devices communicate over the Internet.
- IPv6 was designed to overcome the limitations of IPv4, such as the exhaustion of address space, the lack of end-to-end connectivity, and the complexity of network configuration and management.
- IPv6 uses 128-bit addresses, which can support 2^128 (approximately 3.4×10^38) possible addresses, compared to 2^32 (approximately 4.3×10^9) addresses in IPv4.
- IPv6 also introduces new features, such as stateless address autoconfiguration, neighbor discovery, multicast, anycast, mobility, security, and transition mechanisms.
- China is one of the leading countries in IPv6 deployment and adoption, with a plan to run a single-stack IPv6 network by 2030 .
- According to the Notice on Accelerating the Large-scale Deployment and Application of IPv6 issued by the Chinese government in 2021, China aims to have 700 million active IPv6 users by 2023, plus 200 million Internet of Things devices using the protocol.
- China also plans to upgrade its core network infrastructure, backbone networks, metropolitan area networks, access networks, and content delivery networks to support IPv6.
- China's IPv6 deployment is driven by several factors, such as the demand for more IP addresses, the development of 5G and IoT technologies, the promotion of digital economy and innovation, and the alignment with global standards and trends.
- Some of the challenges and opportunities for IPv6 in China include the coordination and cooperation among different stakeholders, the availability and affordability of IPv6 devices and applications, the security and privacy issues, and the education and awareness of users and operators.
