## Unit 1 - Introduction to Web Technology

Web technology is an essential part of modern society. It encompasses the tools, technologies, and platforms used to create, develop, and maintain websites and web applications. This unit aims to introduce you to the basics of web technology and its significance in business, education, and society.

In this unit, you will learn:

- The history and evolution of the World Wide Web
- The basic components of a web page, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- The role of web browsers and web servers in the delivery of web content
- The importance of web standards and accessibility
- The difference between static and dynamic web pages
- The basics of web hosting and domain registration
- The significance of web security and privacy

By the end of this unit, you will have a fundamental understanding of web technology and its importance in today's world. You will be able to identify the components of a web page and understand how they work together to create a website. Additionally, you will be able to explain the importance of web standards and accessibility, as well as the significance of web security and privacy. This knowledge will help you in your future studies and career in web development.
