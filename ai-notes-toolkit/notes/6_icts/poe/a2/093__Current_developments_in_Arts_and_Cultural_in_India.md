### Current developments in Arts and Cultural in India

1. Revival of Indian folk arts: There is a growing recognition of the country's traditional folk arts and crafts. Many state governments have set up academies and councils to promote folk arts like Chhau dance, Bhand Pather theatre, Pattachitra paintings etc. Several festivals dedicated to folk arts are also being organized.

2. Growth of contemporary art: Indian contemporary art is making its presence felt on the global stage. There is a thriving art market for modern and contemporary Indian art. Many private art galleries have opened up in major cities and are showcasing contemporary artists. Indian artworks are being featured in international auctions and exhibitions.

3. Development of art infrastructure: New museums, art galleries and cultural spaces are being established in India. Many metro cities now have iconic art museums and galleries like the Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya in Mumbai and the National Gallery of Modern Art in Delhi and Bangalore.

4. Digital preservation of cultural heritage: The government and several private organizations are working to digitize cultural and art treasures including rare manuscripts, paintings and other artifacts. This will make India's rich cultural heritage accessible to a wider audience.

5. Creative industries growth: The creative industries like performing arts, broadcasting, publishing etc. are expanding rapidly. This sector is being recognized as an engine of economic growth, job creation and export earnings. The government is formulating supportive policies and several private players are investing in the creative industries.
