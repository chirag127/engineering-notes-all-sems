### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times and Ancient India

- Ancient India was one of the major civilizations of the world. It had a glorious history and contribution to the world.
- The Indus Valley Civilization flourished around 3300 BCE. It had well-planned cities, developed architecture, and trade links with other civilizations.
- The Vedic period saw the rise of major religions - Hinduism, Jainism and Buddhism. The Vedas and Upanishads were composed during this period containing spiritual and philosophical ideas.
- The Mauryan empire was established in 322 BCE by Chandragupta Maurya. Under Ashoka, it covered most of the Indian subcontinent. Ashoka promoted the spread of Buddhism across Asia.
- The Gupta empire ruled large parts of India from 320 to 550 CE. It is considered the Golden Age of India with advancements in science, technology, engineering, art, dialectic, literature, logic, mathematics, astronomy, religion and philosophy.
- The medieval period saw the rise of major dynasties like the Chola, Chera, and Pandya in the south and the Pala and Gurjara-Pratihara in the north. They built grand temples and made other achievements in art, literature, and science.
- By the late 15th century, the Delhi Sultanate controlled much of northern India. The sultanate introduced Persianate art & architectural styles and aspects of Islamic governance. The Mughal Empire later took control starting from 1526 gradually expanding across much of the subcontinent.
- The major kingdoms and dynasties promoted a flourishing ancient Indian civilization with significant influence on South and Southeast Asia based on its advancements in culture, science, and arts.
