### Geography in India and Ancient India

1. Ancient India consisted of present-day India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and parts of Afghanistan. The Indus Valley civilization and the Ganges civilization emerged during this time.
2. The Indus Valley civilization flourished around 3000 BCE in present-day Pakistan. The civilization had an organized urban plan with baked brick houses, underground drainage systems, and water supply systems. They traded with Middle Eastern civilizations.
3. The Ganges civilization emerged around 1500 BCE in the Ganges river valley. It consisted of small kingdoms that later merged into the Mauryan and Gupta empires. The Vedic period and rise of Hinduism occurred during this time. Agriculture and trade were key parts of the economy.
4. The Mauryan empire was founded by Chandragupta Maurya in 324 BCE, extending over much of South Asia from Afghanistan to Bengal. The Mauryan empire had a centralized bureaucracy and a large army. Buddhism spread during this time from the teachings of Gautama Buddha.
5. The Gupta empire existed from 320 to 550 CE, covering much of the Indian subcontinent. It is considered a golden age of Indian history marked by advances in science, technology, engineering, art, dialectic, literature, logic, mathematics, astronomy, religion, and philosophy. The decimal number system, algebra, and the concept of zero emerged during this period.
