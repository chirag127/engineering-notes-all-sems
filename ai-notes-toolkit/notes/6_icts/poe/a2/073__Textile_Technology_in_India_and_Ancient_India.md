### Textile Technology in India and Ancient India

1. Cotton cultivation and spinning were invented in India around 4000 BCE. Indians were pioneers in cotton cultivation and spinning technologies.

2. The Indus Valley civilization knewblr advanced techniques of weaving, dyeing and printing on cotton fabrics. They produced cotton textiles in various patterns using techniques like plaiting, splicing, knotting, twining and braiding.

3. The spinning wheel was invented in India around 500 CE. The Indian spinning wheel or Charkha later spread to other parts of the world.

4. The technique of producing vegetable and animal dyed patterns through resist dyeing and block printing was invented in various parts of India. The Ajrakh prints and the Bagru prints of Rajasthan are example of this.

5. The famous Kanchipuram and Dharmavaram silk and cotton weaving was done using manual foot looms which produced intricately patterned fabric.

6. India was a major exporter of textiles in ancient and medieval times and Indian textiles were exported to Greece, Rome, Indonesia and other parts of Asia and Africa.

7. The Mughals and regional kingdoms like the Deccan sultanates patronized the textile industry. They led to the development of more intricate weaving, dyeing and embroidery techniques in India.
