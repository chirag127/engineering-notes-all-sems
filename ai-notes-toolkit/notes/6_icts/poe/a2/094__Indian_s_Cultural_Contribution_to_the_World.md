### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

1. Yoga: Yoga originated in India and has become immensely popular all over the world. It is a physical, mental and spiritual practice which increases the flexibility and strength of the body. Many Bollywood celebrities practice yoga and have contributed to its global popularity.

2. Ayurveda: Ayurveda is the traditional Indian system of medicine. It uses a holistic approach to health that is widely popular around the world. Ayurvedic herbs, oils and remedies are being increasingly adopted in the West.

3. Meditation: Meditation has its origins in ancient Indian spiritual practices. It is a practice of focused attention that reduces stress and increases concentration, creativity, and emotional positivity. Meditation is being widely practiced across the world for its psychological and physiological benefits.

4. Martial Arts: Many Indian martial art forms like Kalaripayattu and Varma Kalai have influenced martial arts all over the world like Karate and Kung Fu. The rhythmic movements and use of weapons in Indian martial arts have been adopted by martial arts in other countries.

5. Board Games: Many popular board games were invented in India like Chess, Ludo, and Snakes and Ladders. These games were later introduced to and gained popularity in other parts of the world. Chess in particular is considered to be a game that develops strategic thinking skills.

6. Religion: Indian religions like Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism and Sikhism have spread cultural ideas and practices to other parts of the world. Concepts like karma, reincarnation, yoga and meditation have been introduced to the world through the spread of Indian religions. Indian spirituality has had a profound influence on the West.
