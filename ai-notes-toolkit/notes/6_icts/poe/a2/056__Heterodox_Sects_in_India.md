### Heterodox Sects in India

1. Charvakas: Materialist philosophers who rejected the notion of an after-world, karma and the authority of the Vedas. They believed that the only goal of life was happiness which could be attained by fulfilling one's desires.

2. Ajivikas: Founded by Makkhali Gosala, they believed in determinism and the existence of destiny. They emphasized severe austerity and asceticism as the path to liberation. The sect died out around the 14th century CE.

3. Jainism: Founded by Mahavira, Jainism believes in strict non-violence or ahimsa and non-attachment to possessions. Liberation is achieved by subduing desires and purifying the soul through right perception, knowledge and conduct. The world is viewed as transient and ever-changing.

4. Buddhism: Founded by Gautama Buddha, Buddhism believes that liberation from suffering is possible by following the Eightfold Path. Life is characterized by suffering due to desires and one must attain enlightenment through wisdom, ethical conduct and mental development to escape the cycle of rebirth. The teachings of Buddha emphasize compassion for all living beings.
