### North Indian Literature

1. Sanskrit Literature: Sanskrit literature flourished under the patronage of rulers and was written by scholars and poets. Key works include the Vedas, Upanishads, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Puranas, poems by Kalidasa ( Kumarasambhava, Raghuvamsha), and Panchatantra stories.

2. Hindi Literature: With the influence of Bhakti movement in medieval India, saints like Surdas, Tulsidas, Kabir composed devotional literature in vernacular languages accessible to common people. Key works include Surdas's Sur Sagar, Tulsidas's Ramcharitmanas. Modern Hindi literature includes works by Premchand, Jaishankar Prasad, Sumitranandan Pant, Mahadevi Verma.

3. Punjabi Literature: The earliest Punjabi literature was in Shahmukhi script (Persian characters) with Sufi saints composing devotional poetry. Guru Nanak and later Sikh Gurus composed hymns and verses in Gurmukhi script. Key works include Adi Granth or Guru Granth Sahib. Contemporary literature includes works by Shiv Kumar Batalvi, Amrita Pritam.

4. Urdu Literature: Urdu developed in northern India with the influence of Persian, Arabic, and local Indian languages. Key works include poetry by Ghalib, Iqbal; short stories by Premchand, Manto; and novels by Qurratulain Hyder. Urdu literature made significant contributions to North Indian literature.
