### Evolution of languages in India

1. Sanskrit: Sanskrit is one of the oldest languages of India. It was primarily spoken in ancient India and used in ancient Indian religious texts and poetry. It originated around 3500 years ago and flourished during the Vedic period.
2. Prakrit languages: These were a group of languages that evolved from Sanskrit and were spoken from around 500 BCE to 500 CE. Some examples were Maharashtri, Shauraseni, Magadhi, etc. Large portions of ancient Indian literature were composed in these languages.
3. Pali: Pali was a Prakrit language that was used to compose the Buddhist scriptures in Sri Lanka and Southeast Asia. It flourished around 500 BCE.
4. Classical languages: From around the 4th century CE, the Gupta dynasty promoted Sanskrit as the main court language. This led to the flourishing of classical Sanskrit literature. Around the same time, Tamil also became a classical language of southern India with a vast collection of literature composed in it.
5. Modern languages: Starting in the 8th century CE, Prakrit languages diversified into Apabhramsha languages which eventually evolved into many of the modern Indo-Aryan languages and Dravidian languages of India. These include languages like Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, etc. which are the primary languages spoken in India today.
