### Pre-Vedic Religion in India

1. Animism and Nature Worship: The pre-Vedic religion in India was primarily animistic and nature worshipping in character. People worshipped manifestations of nature and spirits of ancestors, trees, mountains, rivers etc. They believed that these spirits control their destiny and propitiated them through sacrifices and rituals.

2. Fetishism: Fetishism or worship of physical objects believed to have magical or supernatural powers was also prevalent. People worshipped certain animals, trees, stones etc. which they believed possess magical powers.

3. Magic and Witchcraft: There was a strong belief in magic and witchcraft. People resorted to magical spells, charms, amulets, divination etc. to ward off evils or to achieve something. Witch doctors and sorcerers played an important role.

4. Ancestor Worship: Ancestors were worshipped as they were believed to influence the lives of the living. Food and sacrifices were offered to the spirits of the departed ancestors to win their favour and gain their blessings.

5. Polytheism: Though the pre-Vedic religion was animistic, magical and fetishistic, it was gradually moving towards polytheism. Powerful spirits and deities were being conceived and worshipped, though their number was not as large as in the later Vedic religion.
