#### Mystical Theory of origin of State

- According to mystical theory, state originated from divine origin. State was regarded as a divine creation and sovereign was considered as a representative of God on earth.
- In ancient Eastern societies like India and China, kings were considered to have divine sanction. The subjects considered it their religious duty to obey the king.
- The theory emphasized divine right of kings and denies that state was the result of human efforts or a social contract between the people.
- The mystical theory is no longer accepted as it gives absolute and unlimited power to the rulers which can be misused. It does not view people as the source of political power.
- The modern theories of origin of state lay emphasis on human factors and social contract rather than divine sanction as the basis of the state.
