### Martial Arts Traditions of India

1. Kalaripayattu: Originates from Kerala. Focuses on agility, coordination and physical strength. Uses weapons like swords, spears, shields and sticks. Aims to achieve a balance of mind, body and spirit.

2. Silambam: From Tamil Nadu. Uses a long stick as the primary weapon. Focusses on quick movements and deft footwork. Aims to defend and attack crucial points on the body. Develops discipline and confidence.

3. Gatka: From Punjab. Uses wooden sticks, swords and daggers. Focusses on courage, honour and chivalry. Performers display feats of bravery, strength and skill through choreographed battle reenactments.

4. Thang-Ta: From Manipur. Uses swords, spears, bows and arrows. Aims to combine attack and defence techniques seamlessly. Considered a way of life that inculcates values and disciplines aspirants mentally and physically.

The martial art forms of India originated as methods of combat and self-defence. Over time, they have evolved into ways to achieve a harmonious balance between the body and mind. Their principles of discipline, courage, honour and skill continue to be influential to this day.
