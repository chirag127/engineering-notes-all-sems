### Indian Architecture in Ancient India and present times

1. Ancient Indian Architecture
- Indus Valley Civilization: Well-planned cities, baked brick houses, public baths
- Mauryan Empire: Pillars of Ashoka with Persian and Greek influence, stupas like Sanchi Stupa
- Gupta Empire: Hindu temples like Dashavatara Temple, cave monasteries like Ajanta Caves and Ellora Caves
- Vijayanagara and Pallava: Temples like Kailasanathar Temple cut out of single rock, Shore Temple with Dravidian style architecture

2. Mughal Architecture
- Developed from Persian styles with arches, domes and vaults
- Focus on symmetry, geometry and grandeur
- Use of red sandstone and white marble
- Examples: Taj Mahal, Humayun's Tomb, Red Fort, Fatehpur Sikri

3. Colonial Architecture
- Victorian Gothic Revival: Mumbai University, Kolkata Victoria Memorial
- Indo-Saracenic: Blend of Indian and Mughal styles with Gothic and Renaissance styles; Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji Terminus railway station
- Buddhist Architectural Revival: Theosophical Society headquarters in Chennai

4. Modern Architecture
- Metropolis like Chandigarh and Gandhinagar developed with modern architecture
- Le Corbusier's buildings: Assembly Hall and High Court in Chandigarh which adapt modern styles to local climate
- Other developments like skyscrapers, evolved regional styles, eco-friendly 'green buildings'
