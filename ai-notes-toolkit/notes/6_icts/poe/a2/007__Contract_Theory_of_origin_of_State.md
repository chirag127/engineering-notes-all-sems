#### Contract Theory of origin of State

1. According to social contract theory, state originated from an implicit agreement or contract between people to form an organised society.
2. In the primitive state, there was no civil society and people lived in a state of nature. In this state, life was “solitary, poor, nasty, brutish and short” as described by Thomas Hobbes.
3. To overcome the difficulties of the state of nature, people decided to enter into a contract and constitute a civil society. According to Hobbes, in this contract people surrendered their rights to a sovereign in return for security and protection of life and property.
4. For John Locke, the social contract was designed to protect people’s rights to life, liberty and property. According to him, sovereignty reverts back to the people if the ruler fails to protect rights and liberties.
5. For Rousseau, the social contract aimed at achieving common good through a collective body based on the 'general will'. He believed that the particular wills of individuals must be subordinated to the general will.
6. The theory has been criticised on the ground that there was never any historical evidence of such a contract. It is just a hypothetical model to explain the origin of the state. However, it emphasised that state legitimacy must be based on the consent of the governed and protection of people's rights.
