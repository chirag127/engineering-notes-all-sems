# INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. India is a diverse country with many cultural traditions and social customs. Family and religion play an important role in shaping Indian culture.
2. Joint families are common where the son gets married and brings his wife to live in his parents' house. Elders are respected and looked after by the younger generations.
3. Marriage is an important institution and arranged marriages are common where families are involved in finding a suitable match for their children. Weddings are elaborate functions with many rituals and celebrations.
4. Indian cuisine is diverse with many regional variations influenced by local cultures, crops and spices. Wheat, rice and lentils are staples across the country. Spices and herbs like turmeric, chili, cumin seeds, ginger and garlic are widely used in cooking.
5. Indians wear a variety of traditional attire such as sarees, salwar kameez, kurtas, lungis and dhotis. Regional clothing varies based on climate, culture and religion.
6. Religion plays an essential role in the life of many Indians. The major religions are Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism and Buddhism. Hindus worship many deities like Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva and have various festivals such as Diwali and Holi.
7. Arts and handicrafts have flourished for centuries in India and include forms such as paintings, sculpture, weaving, pottery, carving and embroidery work. Indian cultural expressions are also visible in music, dance and cinema.
