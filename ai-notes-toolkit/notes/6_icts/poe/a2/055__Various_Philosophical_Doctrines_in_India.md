### Various Philosophical Doctrines in India

1. Vedanta: It represents the philosophical ideas contained in the Upanishads. Its central theme is the identity of atman (individual self) and Brahman (the ultimate reality). The major proponents of Vedanta school are Badarayana, Adi Shankara, Ramanuja and Madhva.

2. Samkhya: It is one of the oldest systematic philosophies of India. It proposes a dualistic view of the world where the two fundamental realities are prakriti (matter) and purusha (consciousness). It considers prakriti as the primal cause of the world. The major proponents of this school are Kapila and Ishwara Krishna.

3. Yoga: It focuses on the achievement of liberation through physical and mental discipline. According to this school, controlling and stilling the modifications of the mind is the path to liberation. The major proponents of this school are Patanjali and Shankara.

4. Nyaya: It is a system of logic that sets forth a methodology to investigate the nature of reality. It proposes that liberation is attained by acquiring true knowledge. The major proponents of this school are Gautama, Vatsyayana, Udyotakara and Vasubandhu.

5. Vaisheshika: It is an atomic theory where the physical and metaphysical aspects of the universe are explained through the interactions of atoms. According to this philosophy, liberation is achieved through knowledge of the categories of reality. The major proponent of this school is Kanada.
