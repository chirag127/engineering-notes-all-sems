### Puranas

1. The Puranas are ancient Hindu religious texts comprising narratives of the history of the universe from creation to destruction, genealogies of kings, heroes, sages, and demigods, and descriptions of Hindu cosmology, philosophy, and geography.

2. Composed primarily in Sanskrit, the Puranas are classified into 18 canonical texts according to traditional enumeration and have a mixed corpus of other topics like illustrations about codes of conduct, rituals, hymns and eulogistic poems, and pilgrimage guides.

3. The Puranas are post-Vedic texts which typically contain five topics known as Pancha Lakshana: the creation and dissolution of the universe; genealogies of gods, sages, and kings; ancient dynasties and lineages; descriptions of celestial realms, regions, mountains and rivers; expositions on duties of men in different stages of life.

4. The Puranas aim to prove and establish the supremacy of the deity or deities they focus upon. While they contain some elements of the Vedas, they also elaborate more dramatic and Everyday themes in a simpler manner to appeal to the common masses.

5. The Puranas played an important role in the history of Hinduism by providing common people a medium to learn about their religion and worship their deities through poetic stories, dramas and dialogue. They are regarded as Smriti and broadly classified into the Mahapuranas and Upapuranas based on their importance.
