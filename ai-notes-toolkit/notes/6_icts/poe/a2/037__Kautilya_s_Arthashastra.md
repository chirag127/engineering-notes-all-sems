### Kautilya’s Arthashastra

- Kautilya’s Arthashastra is an ancient Indian treatise on statecraft, economic policy and military strategy by Chanakya, who is also known as Kautilya.
- It was written around 300 BCE and is considered as one of the most comprehensive treatises on economics, politics, governance and military strategy ever written in history.
- The Arthashastra focuses on the facets of governance, including maintenance of law and order, revenue administration, foreign policy and war.
- The text advocates an authoritative and unitary state, in contrast with the earlier texts of Hindu philosophy which emphasize the primacy of dharma.
- Key topics covered in Arthashastra include:
-- Forms of government, such as monarchy and democracy, structures and duties of state officials.
-- Legal system, court system, contract law, property and financial matters, and torts.
-- Economics, including taxation, price controls, and economic development.
-- Military organization, personnel management, intelligence operations, and weaponry.
-- Foreign policy and war, including alliances, diplomacy, and theories of just war.

- The Arthashastra has been influential on modern Indian policy and is regarded as a major work on realist political thought in Sanskrit literature and Indian history.
