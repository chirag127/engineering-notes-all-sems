#### Theories of origin of State in India

1. Divine theory: The divine origin theory states that the state originated from divine powers. The kings were regarded as the representatives of God on earth. Eg: The theory of divine origin of state was popular during the ancient period in India. The kings derived their authority from the God.

2. Force theory: The force theory states that the state originated due to the use of physical force. According to this theory, the chief of a tribe or a successful soldier establishes his rule over a territory through physical force. Eg: The emergence of Magadh state during the pre-Mauryan period in India can be explained on the basis of this theory.

3. Evolutionary theory: The evolutionary theory emphasizes on the gradual evolution of state from primitive tribal organizations. According to this theory, the state evolved from family to tribe to city state and finally a full-fledged state due to the increasing socio-economic complexity and the need for an authority. Eg: The evolution of states in ancient India like Vedic state can be explained on the basis of this theory.

4. Social contract theory: The social contract theory states that the state originated as a result of an agreement among the individuals of a society. The individuals in a society surrendered their rights in favour of an authority in order to maintain law and order and gain security. Eg: The theory was proposed by European thinkers like Hobbes, Locke and Rousseau. However, this theory is less applicable in the Indian context.
