### Modern religious practices in India.

1. Hinduism: Many Hindus today worship at temples dedicated to their chosen deity. Temple rituals may include offering food, flowers, and incense. The festival of Diwali commemorates the victory of light over darkness. Holi is a spring festival celebrated with colorful powder and water.

2. Islam: Muslims follow the Five Pillars of Islam, including daily prayer, fasting during Ramadan, and a pilgrimage (Hajj) to Mecca. Mosques are places of communal worship and religious instruction. Eid al-Fitr marks the end of Ramadan with gifts for children and alms for the poor.

3. Sikhism: Sikhs worship in gurdwaras. A daily prayer and the singing of devotional hymns from the Guru Granth Sahib scripture are important practices. The festival of Vaisakhi celebrates the formation of the Khalsa, a collective Sikh initiate, in 1699.

4. Christianity: Christians attend churches for prayer, Bible readings, hymn singing, and ceremonies. Christmas celebrates the birth of Jesus, and Easter commemorates his resurrection. Missions and social services are an important aspect of the practice of Christianity in India.

5. Buddhism: Buddhists meditate and follow the teachings of the Buddha to attain enlightenment and escape suffering. Paying respect at Buddhist shrines and monasteries and following moral precepts are core practices. Vesak is a major festival celebrating the Buddha's birth, enlightenment, and death.
