### Jain Literature in Pali,Prakrit And Sanskrit

1. Jain literature in Pali: The earliest Jain literature was composed in Ardhamagadhi Prakrit and is referred to as the Jain Agamas or canons. These include the following:
- The Avasyaka Sutra - The most sacred Jain text containing reverential thought and conduct.
- The Uttaradhyayana Sutra - Contains discussions related to renunciation and liberation through self-effort.
- The Sutrakritanga - Deal with Jain tenets related to faith, knowledge, conduct,auspiciousness.

2. Jain literature in Prakrit: Several Jain texts were composed in Prakrit languages including:
- The Siddhasena Divakara's Sanmatitarka - A work on philosophy, ontology and epistemology.
- The Samavayanga Suttam - Describes daily activities and precautions for ascetics.
- The Kundakunda texts such as Samayasara and Niyamasara - On the path to liberation.

3. Jain literature in Sanskrit: There was a later phase of Jain writing in Sanskrit including:
- The Tattvartha Sutra by Umaswati - A systematic exposition of Jain metaphysics, epistemology and ethics.
- The Kundakunda texts such as Panchastikayasara - Translated from Prakrit to Sanskrit.
- Jinasena's Adipurana - A text related to Jain cosmology, metaphysics, mythology and history.
