### Northern Indian languages

1. Hindi: Hindi is the most widely spoken language in Northern India. It is the official language of the federal government of India. Hindi is written in the Devanagari script and is part of the Indo-Aryan language family.

2. Punjabi: Punjabi is the primary language of the Punjab region of India and Pakistan. It is spoken by over 100 million people and is written in the Gurmukhi or Shahmukhi script. Punjabi is also part of the Indo-Aryan language family.

3. Urdu: Urdu is the official language of Pakistan and is also spoken in Northern India. It is mutually intelligible with Hindi and is written in the Perso-Arabic script. Urdu vocabulary has a strong Persian influence as it absorbed much of the Persian lexicon.

4. Bengali: Bengali, also known as Bangla, is the primary language of the Bengal region in Eastern India and Bangladesh. It is the second most spoken language in India after Hindi and is part of the Indo-Aryan language family. Bengali uses the Bengali script, which is an abugida.
