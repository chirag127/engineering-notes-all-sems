### Society in Ancient India

1. Caste system: The ancient Indian society was divided into rigid social groups based on their occupation called castes. The Brahmins were the priests and teachers, Kshatriyas were the rulers and warriors, Vaishyas were the traders and merchants, and Shudras were the laborers. People were born into their caste and did not change it.
2. Joint families: Families were large and extended. Many generations of a family lived together in a joint family system. The eldest male was the head of the family. Property was shared and decisions were taken collectively.
3. Patriarchal system: The society was predominantly patriarchal where men were the authority figures and decision makers. Women were expected to be homemakers and subordinate to men.
4. Importance of religion: Religion and religious rituals played an important role in all aspects of life. People were highly religious and spiritual. Following duties outlined by religion and scriptures was considered very important.
5. Occupations: Most people were engaged in agricultural occupations as the society was largely agrarian. Caste determined occupations where people followed their traditional family occupations. Trade and commerce also grew in cities and towns.
