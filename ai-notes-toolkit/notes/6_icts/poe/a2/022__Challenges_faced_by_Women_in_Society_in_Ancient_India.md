#### Challenges faced by Women in Society in Ancient India.

1. Limited freedom: Women in ancient India had limited freedom. They were expected to be homemakers and were not allowed to make independent decisions about their lives. They had to seek permission from the male members of the family for their needs and activities.

2. Inequality: There was a lot of inequality in the society where men were considered superior to women. Women had a lower social status and were not treated equally in matters of education, property and job opportunities. They had limited rights and had to be subservient to men.

3. Dowry: The practice of dowry was prevalent which put pressure on the girl's family to pay a large amount of money and gifts during her marriage. This led to a financial burden on the family and even cases of dowry deaths and domestic abuse if more dowry was demanded.

4. Widowhood: The state of widowhood was particularly challenging for women. Widows had to renounce all luxuries and were subjected to inhumane treatment. They had limited choices and were expected to live a life of austerity and penance.

5. Child marriage: The practice of child marriage was common which led to exploitation of young girls. They were forced to marry at a young age and faced abuse, health issues and lack of opportunity to pursue education as a result.

The above points highlight some of the key challenges faced by women in the ancient Indian society which curtailed their rights and imposed restrictions on them. More gender equality and reforms were needed to improve their condition and provide them equal opportunities as men.
