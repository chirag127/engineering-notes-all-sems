### Biology in India and Ancient India

1. Ancient Indian scholars made notable contributions to Biology.
- The Vedas compiled around 1500 BCE contain information on Ayurveda with details on human anatomy and physiology as well as herbs and treatments.
- Sushruta (600 BCE) discussed surgery and categorized human disease. He described over 300 surgical procedures and 120 surgical instruments.
- Charaka (400 BCE) studied internal medicine and described various physiological concepts including metabolism and immunity.
- Kautilya (300 BCE) studied animal husbandry, forestry and agriculture.

2. Aryabhata (476 CE) studied botany and zoology. He wrote on plant classification and distribution. He gave the first correct explanation of fortnightly lunar phases and eclipse.
- Varahamihira (505 CE) wrote on plants and animals including insects, reptiles, fish and birds. His works contain details on habitat, behavior, breeding and migration.
- The writer Thirumoolar (5th to 10th century CE) described 3000 varieties of plants in his work 'Thirumandiram'.
