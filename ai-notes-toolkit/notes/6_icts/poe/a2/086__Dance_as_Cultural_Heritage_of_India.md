### Dance as Cultural Heritage of India

1. Indian dance has a long history and tradition. It is an integral part of Indian culture and has been a medium to express various emotions and ideas since ancient times.
2. There are numerous classical dance forms in India like Bharatnatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Manipuri, etc. Each form represents the culture and ethos of its respective region. These dance forms are typically accompanied by traditional Indian music and require rigorous training to master.
3. Bharatnatyam originated in Tamil Nadu and is characterized by movements of eyes and limbs coordinated with the bhava or expression. Kathak has roots in northern India and involves emotive footwork and body movements. Kathakali is a dance-drama form from Kerala depicting stories through expressive gestures and makeup.
4. Indian folk dances are also varied and provide a glimpse into the local traditions of different regions. Some famous folk dances are Bhangra and Gidda from Punjab, Garba and Dandiya Raas from Gujarat and Bihu from Assam.
5. Dance has religious significance too in India. For example, Bharatnatyam's movements and expressions are often compared to yogic asanas and spiritual qualities. The dance of divine love Raslila is integral to the tradition of Vaishnavism particularly in eastern India and features the love of Radha and Krishna.
