#### Brahmi Script

1. Brahmi script is one of the earliest writing systems developed in India around the 4th century BCE.
2. It was mainly used to write Sanskrit and Middle Indo-Aryan languages.
3. The script consists of 34 consonants and is written from left to right.
4. Brahmi gave rise to many scripts used in South and Southeast Asia including Devanagari, Tamil, Bengali, and Burmese.
5. The earliest and best preserved Brahmi inscriptions are the Edicts of Ashoka found throughout the Indian subcontinent and present-day Pakistan and Afghanistan.
6. Brahmi script is ancestral to nearly all the scripts used in South and Southeast Asia today and is important in the study of the origin and development of writing systems.
7. The Kharosthi script was used in ancient times in northwestern Indian subcontinent and is less suitable for writing Sanskrit effectively compared to Brahmi.
