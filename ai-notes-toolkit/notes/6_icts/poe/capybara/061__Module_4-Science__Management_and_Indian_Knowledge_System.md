## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

In this module, we will learn about the intersection of science, management, and Indian knowledge system. The following points will be covered:

- Science and Management: We will understand the relationship between science and management. Science provides the knowledge and tools to solve problems and management helps in implementing those solutions effectively.

- Indian Knowledge System: We will learn about the Indian knowledge system and its contribution to science and management. The Indian knowledge system is based on the principles of Ayurveda, Yoga, and Vedas, which have been used for centuries for solving various problems.

- Integration of Indian Knowledge System in Management: We will explore the integration of Indian knowledge system in management. Indian knowledge system emphasizes the importance of holistic approach, sustainability, and ethical practices, which can be incorporated in the management practices for better outcomes.

- Case Studies: We will analyze case studies of companies that have successfully integrated Indian knowledge system in their management practices. We will understand the challenges faced by these companies and how they overcame them.

- Future Scope: We will discuss the future scope of integrating Indian knowledge system in science and management. With the increasing focus on sustainability and ethical practices, Indian knowledge system can play a significant role in shaping the future of science and management.

In conclusion, this module will provide a comprehensive understanding of the relationship between science, management, and Indian knowledge system. It will help in developing a holistic approach towards problem-solving and management practices.
