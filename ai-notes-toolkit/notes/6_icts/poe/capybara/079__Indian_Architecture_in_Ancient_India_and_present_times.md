### Indian Architecture in Ancient India and Present Times

Architecture is one of the oldest and most important forms of art in India. Indian architecture has a unique style that sets it apart from other architectural styles. Here are some key points about Indian architecture in ancient India and present times:

#### Ancient Indian Architecture:
- Ancient Indian architecture was mainly focused on religious buildings such as temples, stupas, and monasteries.
- The most prominent example of ancient Indian architecture is the Taj Mahal, which was built in the 17th century by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan.
- Another significant example of ancient Indian architecture is the Sun Temple, which was built in the 13th century in Konark, Odisha.
- Ancient Indian architecture was characterized by the use of stone, brick, and wood, as well as intricate carvings and sculptures.

#### Present-day Indian Architecture:
- Present-day Indian architecture is a blend of traditional and modern styles.
- Modern Indian architects are influenced by global trends while still maintaining traditional elements in their designs.
- Some of the most famous present-day Indian architects include Charles Correa, Balkrishna Doshi, and Raj Rewal.
- Modern Indian architecture is characterized by the use of sustainable and eco-friendly materials.

#### Common Elements in Indian Architecture:
- Indian architecture is known for its intricate carvings and sculptures, which are often inspired by mythology and religion.
- The use of symmetry and geometry is also a common element in Indian architecture.
- The use of natural light and ventilation is also a key feature in Indian architecture.

Overall, Indian architecture has a rich history and unique style that continues to influence architects around the world. From ancient buildings like the Taj Mahal to modern structures, Indian architecture remains an important part of the country's cultural heritage.
