
### Persian Literature in India

1. Persian literature is a body of works written in the Persian language, which is an Indo-Iranian language, and is the official language of Iran, Afghanistan, and Tajikistan.

2. Persian literature has a long and rich history, with roots stretching back to the Achaemenid Empire in the 6th century BCE. It includes works from many different genres, including poetry, prose, and drama.

3. Persian literature has had a significant influence on Indian literature, particularly in the form of Sufi poetry. Sufi poets such as Rumi, Hafez, and Jami wrote extensively in Persian and their works were widely read in India.

4. Persian literature also had a major impact on the development of Urdu literature in India. Urdu was heavily influenced by Persian, and many of the greatest Urdu poets, such as Mirza Ghalib and Faiz Ahmed Faiz, wrote extensively in Persian.

5. Persian literature has also had an influence on Indian culture and art, with Persian-style miniature paintings and architecture being popular in India.

6. Persian literature continues to be studied and appreciated in India today, with many universities offering courses in the language and its literature.
