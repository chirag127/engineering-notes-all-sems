
### Socio religious reform movement of 19th century in India

1. The 19th century in India saw a surge in socio-religious reform movements, with a particular focus on Hinduism. 
2. These movements aimed to reform existing practices, often to bring about social change in the country. 
3. The Brahmo Samaj, founded by Ram Mohan Roy in 1828, was one of the earliest and most influential of these movements. It was a social reform movement that sought to abolish the caste system, child marriage and other social evils. 
4. The Arya Samaj, founded by Dayanand Saraswati in 1875, was another influential movement. It was a religious reform movement that sought to revive the Vedic religion and to promote social change. 
5. The Theosophical Society, founded by Madam Blavatsky and Colonel Olcott in 1875, sought to promote religious tolerance and harmony among different faiths.
6. The Ramakrishna Mission, founded by Swami Vivekananda in 1897, was a religious and social reform movement that sought to promote spiritual awakening and social service. 
7. The Aligarh Movement, founded by Sir Syed Ahmad Khan in 1875, was a political, educational and social reform movement that sought to promote modern education and social reform among the Muslim population of India. 
8. The Self-Respect Movement, founded by E.V. Ramaswamy in 1925, was a social reform movement that sought to promote social equality and end caste discrimination. 
9. The Indian National Congress, founded by A.O. Hume in 1885, was a political reform movement that sought to promote Indian nationalism and independence. 
10. These reform movements had a significant impact on the social and political landscape of India in the 19th century, and continue to have an impact today.
