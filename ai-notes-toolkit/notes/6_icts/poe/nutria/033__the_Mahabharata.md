
### The Mahabharata

1. The Mahabharata is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India, the other being the Ramayana.
2. It is believed to have been written by the sage Vyasa around 400 BCE.
3. The Mahabharata is one of the longest epic poems in the world, with over 200,000 verses.
4. The story revolves around the Pandava brothers and their struggle against the Kaurava brothers for the throne of Hastinapur.
5. The Mahabharata is a complex narrative with several subplots and characters.
6. It includes stories of gods and goddesses, the great war between the Pandavas and the Kauravas, and the philosophical discourses of Bhishma and Yudhishthira.
7. The Mahabharata is an important source of Hindu mythology and philosophy, and has had a great influence on Indian culture and literature.
8. The Mahabharata is also known for its many notable characters, such as Krishna, Arjuna, Bhishma, Karna, and Draupadi.
