
### Kingship in states in Ancient India

* Kingship was an integral part of the social and political system of Ancient India.
* Kings were expected to uphold the four core values of Dharma (righteousness), Kama (pleasure), Artha (wealth), and Moksha (liberation).
* The king was the supreme commander of the army, and was responsible for maintaining law and order in the kingdom.
* The king had the power to appoint and dismiss ministers, judges, and other officials, and had the power to levy taxes and make laws.
* The king was expected to be generous and compassionate towards his subjects, and to protect them from external enemies.
* The king was also expected to be a patron of the arts and culture, and to promote the spread of knowledge.
* The king was seen as a divine figure, and was expected to be a benevolent ruler who would provide justice and prosperity to his subjects.
