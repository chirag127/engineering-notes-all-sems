
### The Seven Limbs of the State in Ancient India

1. The King: The king was the head of the state and was responsible for the protection of the citizens. He was expected to be a just ruler and was the source of all laws.

2. The Ministers: The ministers were appointed by the king to assist him in running the state. They were responsible for providing advice and guidance to the king and for carrying out his orders.

3. The Judiciary: The judiciary was responsible for settling disputes between citizens and for enforcing the laws of the state.

4. The Military: The military was responsible for protecting the state from external threats.

5. The Revenue System: The revenue system was responsible for collecting taxes from citizens and for managing the state's finances.

6. The Education System: The education system was responsible for providing education to citizens and for promoting knowledge and learning.

7. The Religion: Religion was an important part of the state and was responsible for providing moral guidance to citizens.
