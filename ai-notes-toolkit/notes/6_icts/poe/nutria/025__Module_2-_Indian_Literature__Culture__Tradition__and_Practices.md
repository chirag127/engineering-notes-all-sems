
## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

1. Indian literature is rich and varied, with some of the oldest works in the world. Early works include the ancient Sanskrit epics, the Mahabharata and the Ramayana, as well as the Upanishads and the Vedas.
2. Indian culture is heavily influenced by its religious traditions, especially Hinduism and Buddhism. Indian culture is also influenced by the country’s history, geography, language, and other factors.
3. Indian tradition is based on the values of respect, hospitality, and family. It is also based on the concepts of karma and dharma, which are central to the Hindu religion.
4. Indian practices include a wide range of religious and spiritual activities, such as meditation, yoga, and puja. Other practices include the celebration of festivals and the observance of certain customs and rituals.
