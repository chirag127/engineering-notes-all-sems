
### Telugu Literature

- Telugu literature is one of the oldest literatures in India, with a rich and varied history.
- It is believed that Telugu literature began in the 11th century, with the works of Nannayya, Tikkana and Yerrapragada, known as the Kavitrayam.
- Telugu literature has been divided into four periods - the Pre-Nannayya period (900-1150), the Nannayya period (1150-1350), the Tikkana period (1350-1510) and the Yerrapragada period (1510-present).
- The works of these poets are known for their use of lyrical language, and for their focus on emotions and feelings.
- During the Nannayya period, there was a shift from the use of Sanskrit to Telugu, and the language began to be used in everyday life.
- During the Tikkana period, the language was further developed with the use of poetic forms such as the champu, kavya and prabandha.
- During the Yerrapragada period, the language was further refined and developed, with the introduction of new literary forms such as the khandakavya and the kavya.
- Telugu literature has been influenced by many other literatures, including Sanskrit, Prakrit, Tamil, Kannada and Marathi.
- Telugu literature has also been influenced by the Bhakti movement, which brought about a new wave of religious devotion and poetry.
- Modern Telugu literature has been influenced by the works of writers such as Sri Sri, Gurazada Apparao, Viswanatha Satyanarayana and Gudipati Venkata Chalam.
