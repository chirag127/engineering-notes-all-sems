
### Seals as Cultural Heritage of India

1. Seals have been used in India since the Indus Valley Civilization (2600-1900 BCE).

2. Seals were used to indicate ownership, authority, or authenticity of documents.

3. Seals have been found in all parts of India, ranging from the ancient cities of Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro to modern cities such as Delhi.

4. Seals have been found in various shapes and sizes, including circular, rectangular, and square.

5. Seals were often made of materials such as terracotta, stone, metal, and wood.

6. Seals were often decorated with images of gods, animals, and humans.

7. Seals were also used as a form of identification, with the owner's name and title often inscribed on the seal.

8. Seals were used to mark ownership of property, and were also used to authenticate documents, such as royal decrees.

9. Seals were also used to mark the end of a document, and to indicate that the contents were true and accurate.

10. Seals are an important part of India's cultural heritage, and are still used today in various forms.
