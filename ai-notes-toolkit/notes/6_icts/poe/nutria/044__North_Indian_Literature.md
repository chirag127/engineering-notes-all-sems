
### North Indian Literature

* North Indian literature is primarily composed of works written in Sanskrit and Prakrit languages.
* The earliest known works of North Indian literature are the Vedic hymns and the epics of Mahabharata and Ramayana.
* Later works include the devotional poetry of the Bhakti movement, which flourished in the medieval period.
* The modern period saw a rise in the writing of novels, short stories and poetry in regional languages such as Hindi, Bengali and Urdu.
* North Indian literature is also known for its folk tales, which are often passed down orally from generation to generation.
* North Indian literature has been heavily influenced by religious and philosophical thought, with themes such as karma and dharma playing an important role in many works.
