
### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times and Ancient India

1. The Indian Subcontinent has been home to many great civilizations and empires, dating back to the Indus Valley Civilization (3300-1300 BCE).

2. The Mauryan Empire (321-184 BCE) was the first great empire of the Indian subcontinent, and was renowned for its efficient administration and its vast military power.

3. The Gupta Empire (320-550 CE) was a major power in the region and was known for its advances in science, mathematics, literature, and art.

4. The Mughal Empire (1526-1857 CE) was known for its strong military and its religious tolerance, and was the last great empire of the Indian subcontinent.

5. The British East India Company (1600-1858 CE) was the first European power to gain a foothold in the region, and eventually gained control over much of India.

6. India was a major power in the region until its independence in 1947. The country has since become a major economic and cultural force in the region.
