
## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

1. India is a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic with a parliamentary system of government.

2. The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the land, and it lays down the framework that defines the political principles, procedures, powers, and duties of the government.

3. The Constitution of India divides powers between the central government and the state governments, and it outlines the duties, powers, and responsibilities of the three branches of government: the legislature, the executive, and the judiciary.

4. India is a federal union of 28 states and 8 union territories. The states are further divided into districts and local government areas.

5. The Parliament of India is the supreme legislative body and is composed of the President and two houses: the Rajya Sabha (the upper house) and the Lok Sabha (the lower house).

6. The President of India is the head of state and is elected by an electoral college for a five-year term.

7. The Prime Minister of India is the head of government and is appointed by the President. The Prime Minister is usually the leader of the party with the majority in the Lok Sabha.

8. The Supreme Court of India is the highest judicial body in India and is the final court of appeal in the country.

9. India is a multi-cultural, multi-ethnic, and multi-religious society, and the Constitution of India guarantees the right to freedom of religion.
