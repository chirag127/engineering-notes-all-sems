
### Sangama Literature in India

* Sangama literature is a period of Indian literature that flourished from the 8th to the 16th centuries. 
* During this period, literature in Sanskrit, Prakrit, and regional languages flourished. 
* The Sangama period is divided into three epochs: the early Sangama period (8th to 10th centuries), the middle Sangama period (11th to 13th centuries), and the late Sangama period (14th to 16th centuries). 
* During the early Sangama period, the Mahabharata and Ramayana epics were composed, as well as works of literature such as the Natyashastra, the Panchatantra, and the Jataka tales. 
* During the middle Sangama period, the Bhakti movement began, and works such as the Bhagavata Purana, the Ramcharitmanas, and the Bhagavad Gita were composed. 
* During the late Sangama period, works such as the Prabodha Chandrodaya, the Kumara Sambhava, and the Abhijnanasakuntalam were composed. 
* The Sangama period also saw a great flourishing of Sanskrit drama, with works such as the Mudrarakshasa, the Ratnavali, and the Mricchakatika being composed. 
* The Sangama period is considered to be one of the most important periods in the history of Indian literature, and its works have had a lasting impact on world literature.
