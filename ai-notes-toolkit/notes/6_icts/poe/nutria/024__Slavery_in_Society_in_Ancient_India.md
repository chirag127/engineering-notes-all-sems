
#### Slavery in Society in Ancient India

1. Slavery has been an integral part of Indian society since ancient times. It is believed to have been introduced by the Aryans in the Indus Valley around 1500 BCE.

2. Slavery was a form of labor in which people were owned by another person and forced to work without pay or any rights. Slaves were not allowed to own property or marry without the permission of their master.

3. Slavery was practiced in India for centuries, with some estimates suggesting that up to 15 million people were enslaved in the 19th century alone.

4. Slaves in India were mainly from lower castes and were used for domestic and agricultural labor. They were also employed in the fields of construction, mining, and military service.

5. Slavery was abolished in India in 1843, but the practice continued in some areas until the 20th century.

6. In modern India, the legacy of slavery can still be seen in the form of caste-based discrimination, particularly in rural areas. Despite the abolition of slavery, the caste system still exists and continues to be a source of inequality in Indian society.
