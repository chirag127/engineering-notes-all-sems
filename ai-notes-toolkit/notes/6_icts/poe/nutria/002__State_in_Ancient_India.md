
### State in Ancient India
1. The earliest form of state in ancient India was the tribal state, which was based on kinship and was headed by a chief.
2. The Vedic period saw the emergence of monarchical states, where the king was the supreme authority.
3. During the Mauryan period, the state was centralized and the king was the absolute ruler.
4. During the Gupta period, the state was decentralized and the king had to take into consideration the views of the local chieftains.
5. During the Mughal period, the state was once again centralized and the emperor was the supreme authority.
6. During the British period, the state was centralized and the British Raj was the supreme authority.
