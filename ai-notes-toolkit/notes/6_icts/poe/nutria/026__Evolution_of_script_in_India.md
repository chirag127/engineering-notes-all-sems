
### Evolution of script in India

1. The earliest known script used in India is the Indus script, which was used in the ancient Indus Valley civilization between 2600 and 1900 BCE.

2. The Brahmi script is believed to have developed from the Indus script and is the oldest surviving script in India. It is the ancestor of all modern Indian scripts, including Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada.

3. The Kharosthi script was used in the northwest of the Indian subcontinent and is believed to have developed from the Aramaic script. It was used in the Gandharan region until the 3rd century CE.

4. The Gupta script is a more recent development, which emerged in the 4th century CE under the Gupta Empire. It is the ancestor of modern scripts such as Bengali, Odia, and Gurmukhi.

5. The Sharada script was used in the Kashmir region from the 8th century CE until the 19th century. It is believed to have developed from the Gupta script.

6. The Nāgarī script is the ancestor of modern scripts such as Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati and Nepali. It is believed to have developed from the Gupta script in the 11th century CE.
