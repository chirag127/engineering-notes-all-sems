
### Jain Literature in Pali, Prakrit and Sanskrit
1. Pali literature is the oldest and most important source of Jain literature. It includes the texts of the Jain Agamas, commentaries on the Agamas, and other works.
2. Prakrit literature is the second oldest source of Jain literature and includes works in the Prakrit language, such as the Bhaktamara Stotra, the Panchastikaya, and the Saptamangala.
3. Sanskrit literature is the third oldest source of Jain literature and includes works in the Sanskrit language, such as the Tattvartha Sutra and the Ratnakaranda Shravakachara.
4. Jain literature also includes works written in other languages, such as Hindi, Gujarati, and English.
