
### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World 

1. India has a long and vibrant history of cultural contributions to the world. This includes a wide range of art, literature, music, dance, and architecture. 
2. India is known for its intricate and beautiful art, which includes painting, sculpture, and pottery. The Ajanta and Ellora caves are renowned for their intricate sculptures and frescoes. 
3. Indian literature has made significant contributions to the world, with works such as the Ramayana and Mahabharata. These epics are still read and studied by people around the world. 
4. India is also known for its rich musical heritage, with classical music forms such as the North Indian raga and the South Indian Carnatic music. Indian musicians have contributed to the world of jazz and popular music as well. 
5. Indian dance forms such as Bharatanatyam, Kathak, and Odissi are popular around the world and are performed in many countries. 
6. Indian architecture has also made significant contributions to the world, with the Taj Mahal being one of the most famous examples. Other examples include the Sun Temple of Konark and the temple complexes of Khajuraho. 
7. India is also known for its contributions to mathematics, with the invention of the decimal system, the concept of zero, and the Pythagorean theorem. 
8. India has also contributed to the world of philosophy, with the works of ancient Indian thinkers such as Patanjali and the Upanishads. 
9. India is also known for its contributions to science, with the invention of Ayurveda and the discovery of the laws of gravity and relativity. 
10. India has also made significant contributions to the world of technology, with the invention of the game of chess and the development of various forms of surgery.
