
### Famous Sanskrit Authors in India 

1. Kalidasa is one of the most renowned authors in the Sanskrit language. He was a dramatist, poet, and author who flourished in the Gupta period (4th-6th centuries CE). His works include the plays Abhijñānaśākuntala, Vikramorvaśīya, and Mālavikāgnimitra, and the epic poem Raghuvaṃśa.

2. Bhavabhuti was an 8th-century Sanskrit poet and playwright. He is known for his plays Uttararamacharita, Mahaviracharita, and Malatimadhava. He was a great scholar of the Vedas, Puranas, and the Upanishads.

3. Bhoja was a king and scholar of the 11th century CE. He is known for his works in Sanskrit literature, including the Rajamartanda, Sarasvatikanthabharana, and Vrikshayurveda. He was also a great patron of the arts, and his court was a center of learning.

4. Appayya Dikshitar was a 16th-century scholar and philosopher who wrote extensively on the Advaita Vedanta philosophy. He is known for his works such as the Advaitasiddhi, Vivarana, and Panchapadika.

5. Dandin was a 7th-century Sanskrit poet and author. He is known for his works such as Dashakumaracharita and Kavyadarsha. He is also credited with the authorship of the Sanskrit grammar work, Kavyalankara.
