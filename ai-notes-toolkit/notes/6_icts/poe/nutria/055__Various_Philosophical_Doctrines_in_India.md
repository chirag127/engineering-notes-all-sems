
### Various Philosophical Doctrines in India

- Hinduism is a major religion in India, and its philosophical doctrines are based on the Vedas, Upanishads, and other texts.
- Buddhism is a major religion in India, and its philosophical doctrines are based on the Four Noble Truths, the Eightfold Path, and other teachings.
- Jainism is a major religion in India, and its philosophical doctrines are based on the teachings of the Tirthankaras, and the doctrine of Ahimsa (non-violence).
- Sikhism is a major religion in India, and its philosophical doctrines are based on the teachings of the Gurus, and the doctrine of Sarbat da Bhala (goodwill to all).
- Advaita Vedanta is a major philosophical system in India, and its doctrines are based on the Upanishads, and the teachings of Shankara.
- Dvaita Vedanta is a major philosophical system in India, and its doctrines are based on the Upanishads, and the teachings of Madhvacharya.
- Nyaya is a major philosophical system in India, and its doctrines are based on the Nyaya Sutras, and the teachings of Gautama.
- Vaisheshika is a major philosophical system in India, and its doctrines are based on the Vaisheshika Sutras, and the teachings of Kanada.
- Samkhya is a major philosophical system in India, and its doctrines are based on the Samkhya Karika, and the teachings of Kapila.
