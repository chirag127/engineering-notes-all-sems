
### Jainism in India

- Jainism is an ancient religion that originated in India and is still practiced today.
- Jains believe in non-violence, respect for all living things, and the importance of self-control.
- Jains are divided into two main sects: Digambara and Svetambara.
- Jainism has been an influential force in Indian society and culture for centuries.
- Jains have a strong presence in India, with approximately 4.5 million adherents.
- Jainism is closely related to Hinduism and Buddhism, sharing many of the same beliefs and practices.
- Jainism has a strong emphasis on ahimsa (non-violence) and vegetarianism.
- Jains believe in the concept of karma and strive to live a life of non-attachment and spiritual growth.
- Jainism is an ethical and spiritual path that focuses on the pursuit of liberation from the cycle of birth and death.
