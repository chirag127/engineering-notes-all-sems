#### Varnāshrama System in Society in Ancient India

- The Varnāshrama system, also known as Varnashramadharma, developed in ancient Hindu society and refers to the segmental division of the Hindu society into four classes or varnas  .
- The term ‘Varnashramadharma’ consists of three words - ‘varna’, ‘asrama’, and ‘dharma’ .
- The four varnas are Brahman, Kshatriya, Vaishya, and Sudra .
- Brahman is the class of the priests and teachers .
- Kshatriya is the class of the kings, warriors, and aristocrats .
- Vaishya is the class of traders, merchants, and people engaged in other professions .
- Sudra is the class of laborers and service providers .
- Ashrama is a system of stages of life discussed in Hindu texts of the ancient and medieval eras .
- The four ashramas are Brahmacharya (student), Grihastha (householder), Vanaprastha (forest walker/forest dweller), and Sannyasa (renunciate) .
