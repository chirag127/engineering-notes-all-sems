### State in Ancient India

- States in ancient India were loosely categorized into monarchy, republican, and oligarchy. Monarchy was the most prevalent type of state in ancient India.
- The state in ancient India was considered necessary, for it ensured peace, order, and happiness. It was a social organization with political power.
- Ancient scholars were not unanimous in their opinion with regard to the origin of the state. The three theories of the origin of the state in ancient India are as follows: 1. Social Contract Theory 2. Divine Origin Theory 3. Organic Theory.
- The core issues in the study of political science are the state and the government. The institution of the state is studied in relation to its origin, nature, aims, and functions of the state in ancient India.
