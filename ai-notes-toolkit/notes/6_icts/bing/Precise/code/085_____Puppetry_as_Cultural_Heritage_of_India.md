### Puppetry as Cultural Heritage of India

- Puppetry is one of India’s most diverse cultural heritages, with a variety of regional genres .
- Indian puppetry is a subject as varied as the many cultural traditions of the Indian subcontinent.
- Puppetry has been in India a means of entertainment and a form of cultural and religious dissemination.
- In the post-colonial period, the high-speed modernization wave that invested India put many of the puppetry traditions at risk of extinction; several government and private initiatives contributed to save endangered styles, but many are still at risk.
- Indian Heritage Puppetry is a fascinating art form that has always sparked curiosity from an audience of all ages.
- Emerging from the grassroots level, the identification of puppetry in India varies from one location to the other.
- Aside from traditional puppetry, India has a thriving contemporary puppetry culture.
