#### Force Theory of origin of State

The Force Theory of the origin of the state is one of the several theories that attempt to explain how states came into existence. According to this theory, the state emerged as a result of the use of force by one group to subjugate another group. Here are some key points to consider:

1. The theory suggests that the state was created when one group or tribe conquered another group or tribe and established its rule over the conquered people.
2. The conquerors used force to maintain their control over the conquered people and to establish a system of government.
3. The use of force was necessary to maintain order and to protect the interests of the ruling group.
4. The ruling group established laws and institutions to govern the conquered people and to maintain their control over them.
5. Over time, the conquered people accepted the rule of the conquerors and the state became an established institution.

This theory has been criticized for its simplicity and for its failure to account for the complex social, economic, and political factors that contributed to the emergence of the state. Nonetheless, it remains an important theory in the study of the origin of the state.
