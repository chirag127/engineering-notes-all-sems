#### Contract Theory of origin of State

Contract theory is a theory that explains the origin of the state as a result of a social contract between individuals in a society. According to this theory, individuals in a society voluntarily give up some of their rights and freedoms to a governing body in exchange for protection and the enforcement of social order.

1. The theory is based on the idea that individuals have natural rights and freedoms that they are entitled to, but that living in a society requires some limitations on these rights in order to maintain social order and protect the rights of others.
2. The social contract is an agreement between individuals to form a government and abide by its rules in exchange for the benefits of living in a society with laws and protections.
3. The theory suggests that the state is formed as a result of this agreement and that its power and authority are derived from the consent of the governed.
4. The theory has been influential in the development of modern political thought and has been used to justify the existence of government and the need for individuals to obey its laws.
5. Some of the most well-known proponents of contract theory include Thomas Hobbes, John Locke, and Jean-Jacques Rousseau, each of whom had their own unique interpretation of the theory.
