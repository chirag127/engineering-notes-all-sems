### Northern Indian languages

- Northern Indian languages are primarily Indo-Aryan languages.
- The Hindustani lingua franca, which includes both Hindi and Urdu, is native to Northern India.
- Other Indo-Aryan languages native to Northern India include a wide range of western and eastern Hindi dialects, Bihari languages (such as Bhojpuri, Magadhi, and Maithili), Punjabi, Pahari languages, and Kashmiri.
- The historical evolution of modern North Indian languages, such as western and eastern Hindi dialects, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pahari, and Kashmiri languages, and Hindustani, is not well defined.
- The official languages of the states and union territories located in North India include Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Kashmiri, Dogri, and English.
