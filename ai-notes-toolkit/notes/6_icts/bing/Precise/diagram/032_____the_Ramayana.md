### The Ramayana

- The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic poem, composed in Sanskrit by the sage Valmiki .
- It belongs to the genre of Itihasa, narratives of past events (purāvṛtta), which includes the Mahabharata, the Puranas, and the Ramayana. The genre also includes teachings on the goals of human life .
- The Ramayana was composed probably not before 300 BCE and in its present form consists of some 24,000 couplets divided into seven books .
- It tells the story of the exile and then return of Rama, prince of Ayodhya .
- The Ramayana was an important influence on later Sanskrit poetry and the Hindu life and culture, and its main characters were fundamental to the cultural consciousness of a number of nations, both Hindu and Buddhist .
