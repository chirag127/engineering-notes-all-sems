### Textile Technology in India and Ancient India

- Textiles was a great industry in ancient India. Some of the finest cloth in the world was from India. Textiles became one of the major commodities of trade between India and other countries.
- Cotton was also cultivated in India before anywhere else.
- The textile technology was highly developed in India. India was probably the first nation where the production of cotton fiber and manufacture of textiles had started.
- India was highly skilled in making cotton fabrics like China for silk. Indian cotton fabric was prized for its whiteness and fineness.
- The manufacturing of cotton into cloth by tools is an ancient art in India.
- The earliest surviving Indian cotton threads date to around 4000 BC and dyed fabrics from the region are documented as far back as 2500 BC.
- In ancient Greece and Babylon, the very name ‘India’ was shorthand for ‘cotton’.
- India’s textiles are embedded in every aspect of its identity.
- The story of textiles in India is one of the oldest in the world and goes back to prehistoric times.
- Concrete evidence of textile production and use starts appearing from the proto-historic times i.e. 3rd Millennium BCE.
