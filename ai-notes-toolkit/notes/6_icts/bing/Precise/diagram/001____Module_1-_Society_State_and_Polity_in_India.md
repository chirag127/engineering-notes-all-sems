## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

1. Society in India is characterized by its diversity, with a wide range of religions, languages, and cultures.
2. The Indian state is a federal parliamentary democratic republic, with a President as the head of state and a Prime Minister as the head of government.
3. The Indian polity is based on the Constitution of India, which came into effect on 26th January 1950.
4. The Constitution provides for a separation of powers among the legislature, executive, and judiciary.
5. India has a multi-party system, with numerous political parties representing different ideologies and interests.
6. Elections are held regularly to choose representatives for the national parliament and state assemblies.
7. The Indian legal system is based on a combination of common law and civil law, with the Supreme Court of India being the highest court of appeal.
8. India has a mixed economy, with elements of both capitalism and socialism.
9. The government plays an active role in the economy, with a focus on promoting development and reducing poverty.
10. India is a member of various international organizations, including the United Nations, World Bank, and World Trade Organization.
