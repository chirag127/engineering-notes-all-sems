#### Evolutionary Theory of origin of State

The Evolutionary Theory of the origin of the state is one of the major theories concerning the origin of the state. According to this theory, the state is a natural development from the family and social organization of early human societies.

1. The theory suggests that the state originated from the family, with the head of the family being the first ruler.
2. As families grew and formed clans, the head of the clan became the ruler.
3. As clans joined together to form tribes, the head of the tribe became the ruler.
4. Eventually, tribes joined together to form a state, with the head of the state being the ruler.
5. The state, according to this theory, is a natural progression from the family, clan, and tribe, with the ruler being the head of the state.

This theory is based on the idea that the state is a natural development from the social organization of early human societies. It suggests that the state is a necessary and inevitable institution that arose as human societies became more complex.
