#### Purusārtha in Society in Ancient India

Purusārtha is a fundamental principle of social ethics in ancient Indian society. It refers to the four proper goals or aims of a human life. These goals are not only meant to set an ideal objective but also to convey the right means to achieve them. The four Purusārthas are Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha.

- **Dharma** refers to righteousness and moral values.
- **Artha** refers to prosperity and economic values.
- **Kama** refers to pleasure, love, and emotional fulfillment.
- **Moksha** refers to liberation from the cycle of birth and death.

The concept of Purusārtha is closely related to the four classes in society and the four stages in the life of an individual. The eventual goal of the varying darsanas is not only to achieve these four aims but also to lead a balanced life through the pursuit of Purusārtha   .
