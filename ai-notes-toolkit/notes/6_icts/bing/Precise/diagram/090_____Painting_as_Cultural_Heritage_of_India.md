### Painting as Cultural Heritage of India

- Indian art forms are a part of the rich culture and heritage of the country. Beautiful bursts of color with vivid patterns and symbolic images capture the culture beautifully on the canvas.
- Indian art is among the oldest in the world, with archaeologists discovering paintings that were created over 70,000 years ago. Therefore, Indian paintings hold a special place in history and continue to influence artists to this day.
- Traditional art in India depicted cultural, religious, and political developments through clever metaphors.
- The earliest known Indian paintings can be dated to pre-historic times, such as those found in places like Bhimbetka rock shelters in Madhya Pradesh.
- Each painting style that arose in India symbolized traditions, practices, and ideas that had been passed down from past generations. Though early Indian paintings were created on walls, the art form was later adapted to more contemporary materials such as paper, fabric, canvas, and other materials.
- India has one of the world’s largest collections of songs, music, dance, theatre, folk traditions, performing arts, rites and rituals, paintings, and writings that are known as the ‘Intangible Cultural Heritage’ (ICH) of humanity.
