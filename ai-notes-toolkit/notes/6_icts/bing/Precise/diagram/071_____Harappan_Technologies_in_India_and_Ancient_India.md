### Harappan Technologies in India and Ancient India

The Harappan Civilization, also known as the Indus River Valley Civilization, existed from 3300-1300 BCE and extended from modern-day northeast Afghanistan to Pakistan and northwest India . Some of the important innovations of this civilization include:

1. **Standardized weights and measures** .
2. **Seal carving** .
3. **Metallurgy with copper, bronze, lead, and tin** .
4. **Efficient system for conservation, harvesting, and storage of water** .
5. **Tools and weapons** such as flat-axes, chisels, arrowheads, spearheads, knives, saws, razors, and fish-hooks .

The Harappan civilization is referred to as a Bronze Age civilization, where unalloyed copper was used for manufacturing artifacts and rarely tin was mixed with copper to make bronze .
