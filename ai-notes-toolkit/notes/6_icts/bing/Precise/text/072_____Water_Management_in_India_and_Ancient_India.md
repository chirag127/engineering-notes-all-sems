### Water Management in India and Ancient India

- Hydrologic knowledge in India has a historical footprint extending over several millenniums through the Harappan civilization (∼3000 –1500 BCE) and the Vedic Period (∼1500 –500 BCE).
- The need to manage water propelled the growth of hydrologic science in ancient India.
- Mohenjo-Daro and Dholavira, the two major cities of the Indus Valley, are the best examples of advanced water management and drainage systems.
- The Great Bath of Mohenjo-Daro of the Indus Valley is considered the “earliest public water tank of the ancient world”.
- Visvesvarayya, the architect of water management in modern India, took inspiration from the ancient civilizations.
- Ancient Indian technology adopted simple, effective and sustainable methods in the domain of water treatment too (eg. reducing fluoride levels by adding Tulasi, Vetiver, and Drumsticks).
