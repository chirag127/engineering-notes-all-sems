### Geography in India and Ancient India

- India is a diverse landscape, with snow-capped mountains, deserts, plains, hills, and plateaus .
- In ancient times, the geography of India was a little different than what it is today. In the northern part of India stand the Himalayan Mountains and the Hindu Kush stand in the North West. The southern region of India is surrounded by three bodies of water .
- India got its name from the River Indus .
- The Indian subcontinent is a diverse and fertile region with monsoons, droughts, plains, mountains, deserts, and especially rivers, along which early cities developed in the third millennium B.C. Along with Mesopotamia, Egypt, China, and Mesoamerica, the ancient Indian subcontinent was one of the few places in the world to develop its own civilization .
- Ancient India was bounded to the north by large mountains and to the south by the Indian Ocean. Major rivers flowed from the northern mountains to the sea .
