### Indian Cinema as a Performing Art in India

- Indian cinema is known for producing the largest number of films in the world and has one of Asia's largest film industries, employing thousands of people .
- The Indian film industry makes films in various languages, including Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and Bhojpuri .
- India is a powerhouse of performing arts, showcasing resplendent costumes, dazzling jewelry, quaint adornments, and traditional practices .
- Cinema in India has evolved as a potent art form capable of mirroring socio-political and economic issues .
- The year 1931 marked the end of the silent era in Indian cinema with the release of India’s first sound film, Alam Ara, made in Hindi/Urdu .
- The early commercial success of Indian films paved the way for more motion pictures and set the ball rolling for cinema as a commercial art form .
- Indian theater is another type of performing art that is still prevalent, with a rich history originating from 200 BC .
- Indian performing arts, including cinema, enjoy a high status in Indian culture .
