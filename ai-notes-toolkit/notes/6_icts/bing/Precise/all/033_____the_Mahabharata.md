# The Mahabharata

The Mahabharata is one of the two great Sanskrit epics of the Hindus, existing in its present form since c. It is one of the two Sanskrit epic poems of ancient India, the other being the Ramayana. The Mahabharata is an important source of information on the development of Hinduism between 400 BCE and 200 CE and is regarded by Hindus as both a text about dharma (Hindu moral law) and a history.

Above all, the Mahabharata is an exposition of dharma (codes of conduct), including the proper conduct of a king, of a warrior, of an individual living in times of calamity, and of a person seeking to attain moksha (freedom from samsara, or rebirth).
