### Jainism in India

- Jainism, also known as Jain Dharma, is an Indian religion .
- It is one of the three most ancient religions of India, with roots that go back to at least the mid-first century B.C.E .
- The spiritual goal of Jainism is to become liberated from the endless cycle of rebirth and to achieve an all-knowing state called moksha .
- Jainism teaches that the path to enlightenment is through nonviolence and reducing harm to living things (including plants and animals) as much as possible .
- Jains are the only religious group in India where a majority say they are members of a higher General Category caste .
- On January 20, 2014, the Government of India awarded the minority status to the Jain community in India .
- Along with Hinduism and Buddhism, Jainism is one of the three most ancient Indian religious traditions still in existence and an integral part of South Asian religious beliefs and practices .
