### Seals as Cultural Heritage of India

1. Seals have been an integral part of the traditions and cultural heritage of India. They have been used for thousands of years for various purposes, including trade, and have been found in abundance across the expanse of the Indus Valley Civilization .

2. The famous seals of the Indus Valley Civilization are small rectangular pieces that have been called seals as their imprint has been found on clay pieces with remnants of cloth fiber. They are also found in circular and cylindrical shapes.

3. One of the most commonly discovered artifacts in Indus Valley cities are seals decorated with animal figures, such as elephants, tigers, and water buffalos.

4. The Pashupati seal is one of the most famous seals from the Indus Valley Civilization. It is currently housed in the National Museum in New Delhi.

5. Seals of Ancient India provide us with useful information about the civilization of the Indus Valley and its trade practices.
