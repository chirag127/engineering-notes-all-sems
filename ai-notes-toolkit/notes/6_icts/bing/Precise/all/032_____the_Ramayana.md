### The Ramayana

- The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic, composed some time in the 5th century BCE.
- It was composed in Sanskrit by the sage Valmiki .
- The Ramayana was an important influence on later Sanskrit poetry and the Hindu life and culture.
- Its main characters were fundamental to the cultural consciousness of a number of nations, both Hindu and Buddhist.
- The Ramayana belongs to the genre of Itihasa, narratives of past events (purāvṛtta), which includes the Mahabharata, the Puranas, and the Ramayana.
- The genre also includes teachings on the goals of human life.
- The Ramayana is about the exile and then return of Rama, prince of Ayodhya.
- It consists of some 24,000 couplets divided into seven books.
- Valmiki taught the Ramayana to Rama's sons, the twins Lava and Kush.
- By tradition, it is known as the Adi Kavya (adi = original, first; kavya = poem).
