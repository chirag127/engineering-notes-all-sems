#### Evolutionary Theory of origin of State

- The Evolutionary Theory of origin of State is the theory that explains the state as the product of historical and social evolution over a long period of time  .
- According to this theory, the state is not a sudden or artificial creation, but a natural and gradual development of human society from simple to complex forms of organization  .
- The Evolutionary Theory traces the origin of the state from the primitive stages of human society, such as the family, the clan, the tribe, the village, the city, and the nation, and shows how these units gradually merged or integrated into larger and more centralized political entities  .
- The Evolutionary Theory also identifies the factors and forces that influenced the formation and evolution of the state, such as population growth, economic development, social differentiation, cultural diffusion, warfare, religion, law, and ideology   .
- The Evolutionary Theory is regarded as the most scientific and convincing theory of origin of the state, as it is based on empirical evidence, historical records, and comparative analysis of different types of states and societies   .
- The Evolutionary Theory also helps to understand the diversity and complexity of the modern state, as well as the challenges and opportunities for its further development and transformation   .
