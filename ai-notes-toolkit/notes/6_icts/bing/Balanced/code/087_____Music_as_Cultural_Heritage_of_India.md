### Music as Cultural Heritage of India

- Music is an integral part of the cultural diversity of India. It reflects the rich historical and religious influences that have shaped the country's civilization for millennia .
- Music of India can be broadly classified into two main traditions: Indian classical music and Indian folk music .
- Indian classical music is based on the concepts of raga (melodic framework) and tala (rhythmic cycle). It is further divided into two styles: Carnatic music, which is prevalent in South India, and Hindustani music, which is dominant in North India  .
- Indian folk music is diverse and varied, reflecting the regional and linguistic differences of the country. It includes genres such as bhangra, lavani, dandiya, baul, qawwali, bhajan, and ghazal. It is often associated with festivals, rituals, and social occasions .
- Indian music has also been influenced by and has influenced other musical traditions, such as Persian, Arabic, Turkish, Central Asian, European, and American music. It has given rise to popular genres such as filmi (Bollywood music), Indi-pop, rock, and fusion  .
- Indian music is a living and evolving art form that continues to express the creativity, diversity, and heritage of the Indian people  .
