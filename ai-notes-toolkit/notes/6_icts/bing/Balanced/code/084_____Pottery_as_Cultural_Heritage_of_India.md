### Pottery as Cultural Heritage of India

- Pottery is one of the most ancient and tangible forms of Indian art, dating back to the prehistoric times  .
- Pottery reflects the cultural diversity and richness of India, as different regions have their own styles, techniques, motifs and colours of pottery .
- Pottery is also a functional and utilitarian craft, as it is used for various purposes such as cooking, storing, serving, decorating and religious rituals .
- Pottery is a living tradition that is still practiced extensively in India, especially in rural areas, where it is a source of livelihood and identity for many potters .
- Pottery is a valuable source of information for studying the history, society, economy, religion and culture of India, as it reveals the influences, interactions and innovations of various periods and regions  .
