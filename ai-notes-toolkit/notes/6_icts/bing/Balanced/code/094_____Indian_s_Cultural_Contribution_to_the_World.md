### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

India is a country of diverse and rich culture, heritage and history. It has made many contributions to the world in various fields such as mathematics, architecture, medicine, language, art, religion, philosophy, literature, music, dance, cuisine, yoga, ayurveda, astronomy, chess, martial arts, and more. Some of the notable contributions of India to the world are:

- Mathematics: India invented the concept of zero, the decimal system, the binary system, algebra, trigonometry, geometry, calculus, and many other mathematical concepts and techniques. India also developed the place value system, the Fibonacci sequence, the Pythagorean theorem, the concept of infinity, and the concept of negative numbers  .
- Architecture: India has a rich and diverse architectural heritage, ranging from ancient temples, palaces, forts, mosques, tombs, monuments, and caves, to modern buildings, bridges, dams, and skyscrapers. India is known for its urban planning, engineering, and artistic skills, as well as its use of materials, colors, patterns, and motifs. Some of the famous examples of Indian architecture are the Taj Mahal, the Qutub Minar, the Khajuraho temples, the Ajanta and Ellora caves, the Hampi ruins, the Konark Sun Temple, and the Lotus Temple  .
- Medicine: India is the birthplace of Ayurveda, the ancient system of holistic medicine that uses natural herbs, minerals, and metals to treat various diseases and ailments. India also developed the concepts of surgery, anesthesia, plastic surgery, cataract surgery, dental surgery, and vaccination. India also invented the technique of inoculation, the use of cotton swabs, the use of iron to treat anemia, and the use of turmeric as an antiseptic  .
- Language: India is home to 22 official languages and hundreds of regional languages and dialects. India also has a rich and diverse literary tradition, with works in Sanskrit, Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Marathi, Gujarati, and many other languages. India also influenced the development of many other languages, such as English, Persian, Arabic, Greek, Latin, and Chinese, through its words, grammar, scripts, and literature. Some of the famous examples of Indian literature are the Vedas, the Upanishads, the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, the Bhagavad Gita, the Panchatantra, the Kamasutra, and the works of Kalidasa, Rabindranath Tagore, R.K. Narayan, and Salman Rushdie  .
- Art: India has a rich and diverse artistic heritage, with various forms of painting, sculpture, pottery, metalwork, textile, jewelry, and handicrafts. India is known for its use of colors, symbols, motifs, and themes, as well as its fusion of different styles and influences. Some of the famous examples of Indian art are the Madhubani paintings, the Tanjore paintings, the Mughal miniatures, the Rajasthani paintings, the Pattachitra paintings, the Gandhara sculptures, the Chola bronzes, the Bidriware, the Kalamkari, the Bandhani, and the Zari  .
- Religion: India is the birthplace of four major world religions: Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism. India also has a large and diverse population of followers of other religions, such as Islam, Christianity, Judaism, Zoroastrianism, and Baha'i. India also influenced the development of many other religions, such as Taoism, Confucianism, and Shintoism, through its philosophy, ethics, and practices. India also introduced the concepts of karma, reincarnation, dharma, ahimsa, nirvana, moksha, yoga, meditation, and vegetarianism to the world  .
