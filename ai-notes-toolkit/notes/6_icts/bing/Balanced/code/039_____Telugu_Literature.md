### Telugu Literature

Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, which is a Dravidian language spoken by about 80 million people in India. Telugu literature has a rich and diverse history, spanning from the 10th or 11th century to the present day. Some of the main features and genres of Telugu literature are:

- **Poetry**: Telugu poetry is known for its lyrical beauty, musicality, and imagery. Telugu poets have used various forms and meters, such as the śataka (a collection of 100 verses), the padya (a verse with a fixed number of syllables), the kanda (a long narrative poem), and the geya (a song-like poem). Some of the famous Telugu poets are Nannaya, Tikkana, Srinatha, Potana, Annamacharya, Kshetrayya, Tyagaraja, and Sri Sri.
- **Epics**: Telugu literature has produced several epics, both secular and religious, that narrate the stories of heroes, gods, and sages. The most notable epics are the Telugu versions of the Mahabharata and the Ramayana, which were composed by Nannaya, Tikkana, and Errana in the 11th to 14th centuries. Other epics include the Harivamsa, the Bhagavata, the Markandeya Purana, and the Basava Purana.
- **Prose**: Telugu prose emerged in the 16th century, influenced by the Persian and Arabic languages and literature. Telugu prose works include historical accounts, biographies, travelogues, dictionaries, and commentaries. Some of the prominent Telugu prose writers are Pingali Surana, Allasani Peddana, Ketana, and Appakavi.
- **Drama**: Telugu drama developed in the 17th century, inspired by the Sanskrit theater and the folk arts. Telugu dramas are usually based on mythological or historical themes, and are performed with music, dance, and costumes. Some of the renowned Telugu dramatists are Siddhendra Yogi, Vedantadesika, Dhurjati, and Chilakamarthi Lakshminarasimham.
- **Novels**: Telugu novels emerged in the 19th century, influenced by the English and Bengali novels. Telugu novels deal with various themes, such as social reform, nationalism, romance, realism, and fantasy. Some of the notable Telugu novelists are Gurajada Apparao, Kandukuri Veeresalingam, Viswanatha Satyanarayana, Ranganayakamma, and Yandamuri Veerendranath.
- **Short stories**: Telugu short stories are a modern genre, which began in the early 20th century. Telugu short stories reflect the contemporary issues, such as urbanization, industrialization, feminism, and globalization. Some of the influential Telugu short story writers are Chalam, Gopichand, Ravi Sastry, and Madhurantakam Rajaram.
