### Trade in Ancient India

- Trade in ancient India was an important source of commerce and cultural exchange since the Harappan civilization (c. 2600-1900 BCE) .
- Trade in ancient India involved various goods such as gold, textiles, spices, precious metals, ivory, pearls, and gems  .
- Trade in ancient India was facilitated by various trade routes, both land and sea, that connected India with other regions such as Mesopotamia, Egypt, China, Southeast Asia, and Rome  .
- Trade in ancient India was regulated by various political and economic institutions, such as guilds, taxes, customs, weights and measures, and laws  .
- Trade in ancient India had a significant impact on the development of urbanization, art, literature, religion, and science in India and beyond  .
