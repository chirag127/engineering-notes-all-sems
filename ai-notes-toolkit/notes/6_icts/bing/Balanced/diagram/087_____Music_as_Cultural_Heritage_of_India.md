### Music as Cultural Heritage of India

Music is an integral part of the cultural heritage of India. It reflects the diversity and richness of the Indian civilization, which has been influenced by various historical, religious, and regional factors. Music of India can be broadly classified into two main categories: classical music and folk music. 

- Classical music is the art music that follows the rules and principles of ancient texts and traditions. It is based on the concept of raga, which is a melodic framework that evokes a certain mood or emotion, and tala, which is a rhythmic cycle that organizes the beats and accents. Classical music is further divided into two main styles: Hindustani music, which is prevalent in northern and central India, and Carnatic music, which is dominant in southern India. Both styles have their own distinct features, such as instruments, forms, compositions, and genres. Classical music is performed by highly trained and skilled musicians, who improvise and elaborate on the raga and tala according to their creativity and mastery. Classical music is considered to be a sacred and refined art form that connects the human soul with the divine. Some of the famous classical musicians of India are Ravi Shankar, Bismillah Khan, M.S. Subbulakshmi, and Lata Mangeshkar.  

- Folk music is the popular music that reflects the local culture and traditions of different regions and communities of India. It is based on the oral transmission and collective participation of the people, who sing and play music for various occasions, such as festivals, weddings, rituals, and celebrations. Folk music is more flexible and diverse than classical music, as it incorporates various elements, such as languages, dialects, melodies, rhythms, instruments, and genres. Folk music is also influenced by the social, political, and environmental changes that affect the lives of the people. Folk music is considered to be a lively and expressive art form that connects the people with their roots and identity. Some of the famous folk musicians of India are Bhupen Hazarika, Gurdas Maan, Ila Arun, and Shubha Mudgal.  

Music of India is a cultural heritage that showcases the unity and diversity of the Indian nation. It is a source of pride and inspiration for the people, who cherish and preserve their musical traditions and also innovate and experiment with new forms and genres. Music of India is a universal language that transcends the boundaries of time, space, and culture, and communicates the emotions and values of the human spirit.
