### Trade in Ancient India

- Trade in ancient India was an important source of commerce and cultural exchange since the Harappan civilization (c. 2600-1900 BCE) .
- Trade in ancient India involved various goods such as gold, textiles, spices, precious metals, ivory, pearls, and gems  .
- Trade in ancient India was conducted through both land and sea routes, connecting India with regions such as Mesopotamia, Egypt, Persia, China, Southeast Asia, and Rome  .
- Trade in ancient India was facilitated by the development of towns, ports, markets, roads, and coins  .
- Trade in ancient India was regulated by various laws, customs, and institutions, such as the Arthashastra of Kautilya, the guilds of merchants and artisans, and the royal patronage and protection  .
