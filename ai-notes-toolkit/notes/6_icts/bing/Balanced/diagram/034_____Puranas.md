### Puranas

- Puranas are a genre of ancient Indian literature that contain stories, legends, myths, cosmology, genealogy, and other topics related to Hinduism  .
- Puranas are considered as the fifth Veda, after the four Vedas (Rig, Sama, Yajur, and Atharva), which are the primary scriptures of Hinduism.
- Puranas are written in Sanskrit verse, mostly in the form of narrative couplets, similar to the style of the epics Mahabharata and Ramayana.
- Puranas are classified into two categories: Mahapuranas (major Puranas) and Upapuranas (minor Puranas). There are 18 Mahapuranas and 18 Upapuranas, although some sources mention more or less .
- Puranas are also categorized according to the three aspects of the divine: Sattva (purity), Rajas (passion), and Tamas (ignorance). Each Purana is devoted to a specific deity or theme, such as Vishnu, Shiva, Devi, or the Sun .
- Puranas are not considered as authoritative or binding as the Vedas, but rather as supplementary and popular texts that explain and illustrate the Vedic teachings in an accessible way .
- Puranas are also sources of historical, cultural, and artistic information, as they reflect the beliefs, values, and traditions of various periods and regions of India .
