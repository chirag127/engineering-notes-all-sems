### Astronomy in India and Ancient India

- Astronomy is one of the oldest sciences in India. It has a long and rich tradition of observation, calculation, and theory. 
- Astronomy in ancient India can be divided into two major classes: Vedic astronomy and Siddhantic astronomy. 
- Vedic astronomy is based on the sacred texts called the Vedas, which contain hymns, rituals, and cosmology. Vedic astronomy mainly deals with the motions of the Sun, the Moon, and the planets in relation to the Earth and the stars. Vedic astronomy also includes the concepts of time, calendars, eclipses, and astrology.  
- Siddhantic astronomy is based on the treatises called the Siddhantas, which are systematic and mathematical works on astronomy. Siddhantic astronomy developed from the third century BCE onwards, influenced by Greek, Babylonian, and Persian astronomy. Siddhantic astronomy mainly deals with the computation of planetary positions, orbits, and cycles. Siddhantic astronomy also includes the concepts of trigonometry, algebra, geometry, and spherical astronomy.  
- Some of the most prominent astronomers in ancient India were Aryabhata, Varahamihira, Brahmagupta, Bhaskara, and Lalla. They made significant contributions to the fields of planetary theory, celestial mechanics, mathematical astronomy, and cosmology.   
- Astronomy in ancient India was not only a scientific pursuit, but also a cultural and religious one. Astronomy was closely linked to the fields of philosophy, literature, art, and architecture. Astronomy was also used for practical purposes such as navigation, agriculture, and astrology.
