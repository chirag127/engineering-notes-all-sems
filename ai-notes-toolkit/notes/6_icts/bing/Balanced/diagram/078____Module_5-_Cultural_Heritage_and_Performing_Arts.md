## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

- Cultural heritage is the legacy of physical and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations, maintained in the present and bestowed for the benefit of future generations.
- Cultural heritage can be tangible, such as buildings, monuments, landscapes, books, works of art and artefacts, or intangible, such as oral traditions, languages, rituals, values, customs and skills.
- Performing arts are forms of creative expression that are performed in front of an audience, such as music, dance, theatre, opera, circus and puppetry.
- Performing arts are part of the intangible cultural heritage of many cultures and play a crucial role in preserving and transmitting history, identity, values and beliefs.
- Performing arts can also be a source of social cohesion, cultural diversity, artistic innovation and intercultural dialogue.
- Some examples of performing arts that are recognized by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage are:

  - Urtiin Duu, traditional folk long song from Mongolia, which is sung in a wide vocal range and accompanied by a horse-head fiddle.
  - Wayang puppet theatre from Indonesia, which uses intricately carved and painted leather puppets to enact stories from Hindu epics and local folklore.
  - Flamenco, an artistic expression fusing song, dance and music from Andalusia, Spain, which reflects the diverse influences of Romani, Arabic, Jewish and Christian cultures.
  - Kabuki, a classical Japanese theatre form that combines drama, music, dance and elaborate costumes and make-up to portray historical and contemporary stories.
  - Tango, a social dance and musical genre from the Rio de la Plata region of Argentina and Uruguay, which expresses the identity and emotions of the urban working class.

- To study and appreciate the cultural heritage and performing arts of different cultures, one can:

  - Visit museums, galleries, theatres, festivals and heritage sites that showcase and celebrate various forms of cultural heritage and performing arts.
  - Read books, articles, journals and online resources that provide historical, social and artistic contexts and analyses of different cultural heritage and performing arts.
  - Watch documentaries, films, videos and online performances that illustrate and demonstrate the diversity and richness of cultural heritage and performing arts.
  - Listen to recordings, podcasts, radio programs and online lectures that feature and discuss different aspects and genres of cultural heritage and performing arts.
  - Participate in workshops, classes, courses and online platforms that teach and promote the skills and knowledge of different cultural heritage and performing arts.
  - Engage in dialogue, exchange and collaboration with people from different cultural backgrounds and artistic disciplines who share an interest and passion for cultural heritage and performing arts.
