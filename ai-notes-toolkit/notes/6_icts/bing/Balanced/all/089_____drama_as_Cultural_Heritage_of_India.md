# Drama as Cultural Heritage of India

- Drama is a form of performing arts, where stories are enacted by the utilisation of dialogue, music, gesture, dance, and sound.
- Drama is one of the oldest and most diverse forms of cultural expression in India, dating back to the ancient Vedic period .
- Drama in India has been influenced by various religious, social, and linguistic traditions, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Islam, Sikhism, and regional languages .
- Drama in India has also been shaped by various classical theatre traditions, such as Sanskrit theatre, folk theatre, and Parsi theatre, which have their own distinctive features, styles, and conventions .
- Drama in India has contributed to the development of modern theatre, particularly in Hindi, Marathi, and Bengali languages, which have produced many renowned playwrights, actors, and directors .
- Drama in India is a rich and vibrant cultural heritage that reflects the diversity, creativity, and history of the Indian people.
