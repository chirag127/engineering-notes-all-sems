#### Force Theory of Origin of State

- This theory proposes that the origin of state is developed through the use of force.
- One person or a small group of people claim control over the population in a specific area by force .
- Once the rule is well established, the state is established.
- This theory is generally a result of war .
- The theory of force emphasizes the origin of the state in the subordination of the weak to the strong .
- Force has played an important part in the origin and development of some of the greatest empires of history.
- Force is an essential element of the state, but not the only one.
- The force theory of government has been advanced by a number of thinkers for a variety of purposes, such as Plato, Hobbes, and Machiavelli .
- The force theory of government has been criticized for being too simplistic, unrealistic, and unethical .
- The force theory of government does not explain the origin of state in terms of consent, contract, or natural law .
