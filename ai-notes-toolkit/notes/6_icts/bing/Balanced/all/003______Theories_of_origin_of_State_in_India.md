#### Theories of origin of state in India

- The state is a political institution that emerged in ancient India as a result of various factors, such as social, economic, religious and historical.
- Different theories have been proposed to explain the origin of state in India, based on different sources of evidence, such as texts, inscriptions, coins, art and archaeology.
- Some of the prominent theories are:

  - **Social contract theory**: This theory suggests that the state was formed by a voluntary agreement among the people, who gave up some of their rights and freedoms to a ruler or a government, in exchange for protection and order. This theory is based on the rational and legalistic view of the state, and is found in some of the ancient Indian texts, such as the Mahabharata, the Arthashastra and the Manusmriti .
  - **Divine origin theory**: This theory asserts that the state was established by the will of God or the gods, who chose a king or a dynasty to rule over the people. This theory is based on the religious and mythical view of the state, and is found in some of the ancient Indian texts, such as the Vedas, the Ramayana and the Puranas .
  - **Organic theory**: This theory proposes that the state was a natural and inevitable outcome of the social evolution and differentiation of the human society. This theory is based on the biological and historical view of the state, and is found in some of the ancient Indian texts, such as the Shantiparva of the Mahabharata, the Digha Nikaya of the Buddhist scriptures and the Rajatarangini of Kalhana .
