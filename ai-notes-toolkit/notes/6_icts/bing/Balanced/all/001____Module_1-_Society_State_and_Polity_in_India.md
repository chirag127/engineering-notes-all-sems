# Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

- This module aims to provide an overview of the historical and contemporary aspects of the society, state and polity in India.
- Society refers to the network of social relationships, institutions, norms and values that shape the collective life of a group of people.
- State refers to the political organization that exercises sovereign authority over a defined territory and population.
- Polity refers to the system of governance, laws, policies and institutions that regulate the state and society.
- The module will cover the following topics:

  - The origins and evolution of the Indian state and polity from ancient to modern times, highlighting the diversity and continuity of political ideas and practices.
  - The constitutional framework and the institutional structure of the Indian state and polity, including the executive, legislature, judiciary, bureaucracy, federalism, electoral system and political parties.
  - The major challenges and issues facing the Indian state and polity, such as poverty, inequality, corruption, communalism, regionalism, separatism, terrorism, human rights, social movements and civil society.
  - The foreign policy and the international relations of India, focusing on the principles, objectives, strategies and outcomes of India's engagement with the world.
  - The comparative perspective and the theoretical analysis of the Indian state and polity, drawing on the concepts and debates from political science, sociology, history and other disciplines.
