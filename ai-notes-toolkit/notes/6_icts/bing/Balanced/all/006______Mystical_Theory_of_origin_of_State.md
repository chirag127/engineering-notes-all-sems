#### Mystical Theory of Origin of State

- The mystical theory of origin of state is also known as the **divine origin theory** or the **theory of divine right of kings** .
- The exponents of this theory believe that the state did not come into being by any effort of man, but by the **will of God** .
- According to this theory, God created the state and chose some people to rule over others as his representatives .
- The rulers were therefore **sacred** and **infallible**, and their authority was **absolute** and **unquestionable** .
- The subjects had to obey the rulers as a **religious duty**, and any resistance or rebellion was considered a **sin** and a **crime** .
- Some examples of this theory are the **ancient Egyptian pharaohs**, the **Chinese emperors**, the **Roman caesars**, the **medieval European kings**, and the **Islamic caliphs** .
- This theory was challenged by the **rise of democracy**, the **growth of rationalism**, the **spread of humanism**, and the **emergence of nationalism** .
- This theory is now **rejected** by most political scientists as **unscientific**, **irrational**, **undemocratic**, and **oppressive** .
