### Puranas

- Puranas are a genre of ancient Indian literature that contain stories, legends, myths, cosmology, genealogy, and other topics related to Hinduism    .
- The word Purana means "ancient" or "old" in Sanskrit .
- Puranas are considered as the fifth Veda, after the four Vedas (Rig, Sama, Yajur, and Atharva), which are the primary scriptures of Hinduism .
- Puranas are written in Sanskrit verse, mostly in the form of narrative couplets, similar to the style of the two epics, the Mahabharata and the Ramayana .
- Puranas are divided into six categories, based on the main deity they glorify: Vishnu Puranas, Shiva Puranas, Brahma Puranas, Vaishnava Puranas, Shaiva Puranas, and Shakti Puranas    .
- Puranas are also classified into two groups, based on the age of their composition: Mahapuranas (major Puranas) and Upapuranas (minor Puranas)    .
- There are 18 Mahapuranas and 18 Upapuranas, although some sources mention more or less    .
- Puranas are not considered as authoritative or binding as the Vedas, but they are widely read and revered by Hindus for their spiritual and cultural value    .
- Puranas contain various themes, such as creation, destruction, rebirth, karma, dharma, ethics, devotion, pilgrimage, festivals, temples, sects, and social and historical events    .
- Puranas are also a source of information for art, architecture, music, dance, literature, astronomy, astrology, medicine, and other fields of Indian culture    .
