### Medicine in India and Ancient India

- Medicine in India is a diverse and ancient field of knowledge and practice that encompasses various systems of health care, such as Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, Yoga, Naturopathy, and Homeopathy.
- Ayurveda is the most ancient system of Indian medicine, which is based on the Vedas, the sacred texts of Hinduism. Ayurveda means "the science of life" and it aims to balance the three doshas (vata, pitta, and kapha) that govern the body, mind, and spirit .
- Ayurveda has eight branches: internal medicine, surgery, ear, nose, and throat, pediatrics, toxicology, rejuvenation, aphrodisiacs, and psychiatry. Ayurveda uses various methods of diagnosis, such as pulse, urine, stool, tongue, and eye examination, and prescribes various treatments, such as herbal medicines, diet, lifestyle, massage, yoga, meditation, and purification .
- Surgery was a well-developed branch of Ayurveda in ancient India. The most famous surgeon was Sushruta, who lived around the 6th century BCE. He wrote the Sushruta Samhita, a comprehensive treatise on surgery, anatomy, physiology, pathology, and pharmacology. He described various surgical procedures, such as cataract extraction, rhinoplasty, lithotomy, caesarean section, and amputation. He also invented various surgical instruments, such as scalpels, forceps, needles, and catheters .
- Another prominent physician of ancient India was Charaka, who lived around the 2nd century BCE. He wrote the Charaka Samhita, a comprehensive treatise on internal medicine, diagnosis, prognosis, therapeutics, and ethics. He classified diseases into three categories: those caused by imbalance of the doshas, those caused by external factors, and those caused by karma. He also described various methods of prevention, such as hygiene, diet, exercise, and mental health .
- Apart from Ayurveda, there were other systems of medicine in ancient India, such as Siddha, Unani, and Yoga. Siddha is a system of medicine that originated in Tamil Nadu and is based on the concept of siddhis, or supernatural powers. Siddha uses various methods of diagnosis, such as pulse, urine, and palm leaf reading, and prescribes various treatments, such as herbal medicines, metals, minerals, and animal products. Unani is a system of medicine that originated in Greece and was brought to India by the Muslim invaders. Unani is based on the concept of the four humors (blood, phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile) that govern the body and the temperament. Unani uses various methods of diagnosis, such as pulse, urine, and stool examination, and prescribes various treatments, such as herbal medicines, diet, lifestyle, massage, cupping, and bloodletting. Yoga is a system of physical, mental, and spiritual practices that originated in India and is based on the concept of the eight limbs (yama, niyama, asana, pranayama, pratyahara, dharana, dhyana, and samadhi) that lead to the union of the individual self with the universal self. Yoga uses various methods of diagnosis, such as observation, introspection, and intuition, and prescribes various treatments, such as postures, breathing, meditation, and ethical principles.
- Medicine in India has a long and rich history that reflects the diversity and complexity of the Indian culture and civilization. Medicine in India has contributed to the advancement of human health and well-being, as well as to the preservation and transmission of ancient wisdom and knowledge. Medicine in India continues to evolve and adapt to the changing needs and challenges of the modern world, while retaining its unique identity and heritage.
