### Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of music, dance, theatre, folk traditions, performing arts, rites and rituals, paintings and writings. These are known as the 'Intangible Cultural Heritage' (ICH) of humanity.
- India has 37 world heritage sites that showcase its architectural and historical legacy. Some of these sites are the Taj Mahal, the Khajuraho temples, the Ajanta and Ellora caves, the Hampi ruins, and the Mahabodhi temple.
- India has also nominated the Hoysala temples of Karnataka for inscription in the World Heritage List. These temples are known for their intricate carvings and sculptures that depict Hindu and Jain themes.
- India has recently got back a 10th-century goat-headed Yogini statue that was stolen and smuggled out of the country in 1993. The statue is a rare example of the Tantric cult of female deities that flourished in medieval India.
- India has a vibrant and diverse film industry that produces films in various languages and genres. Bollywood and Tollywood are the two most prominent film industries in India, but there are also regional film industries such as Kollywood, Mollywood, Sandalwood, and Ollywood.
- India has also witnessed a surge in contemporary art forms that explore new trends and themes in Indian paintings. Some of the prominent contemporary artists in India are M.F. Husain, S.H. Raza, Tyeb Mehta, Anish Kapoor, Subodh Gupta, and Bharti Kher.
- India has also joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, with three cities - Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana - being part of the network. These cities aim to promote lifelong learning and sustainable development through various cultural and educational initiatives .
- India has also celebrated various cultural festivals and events that showcase its diversity and unity. Some of these are the Republic Day Parade, the Durga Puja, the Kumbh Mela, the Hornbill Festival, the Jaipur Literature Festival, and the Khajuraho Dance Festival .
