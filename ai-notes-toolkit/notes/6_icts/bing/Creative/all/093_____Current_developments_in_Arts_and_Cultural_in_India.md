# Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India is a country with a rich and diverse cultural heritage, which is reflected in its various forms of art, music, dance, literature, architecture, and festivals.
- In recent years, India has witnessed some significant developments in the field of arts and culture, both at the national and international level. Some of these are:

  - **Statue of Prosperity**: In October 2021, India unveiled the world's tallest statue of Lord Ram, also known as the Statue of Prosperity, in Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh. The statue, which stands at 221 meters, is part of a larger Ram temple complex that is being constructed at the site of the demolished Babri Masjid. The statue is expected to boost tourism and cultural development in the region.

  - **UNESCO Network of Learning Cities**: In December 2021, three Indian cities - Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala, and Warangal in Telangana - joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities. The network is a platform for policy dialogue and peer learning among cities that promote lifelong learning for all. The cities were selected for their initiatives in promoting education, culture, social inclusion, and sustainable development .

  - **Dharohar - National Museum of Customs and GST**: In January 2022, India inaugurated the Dharohar - National Museum of Customs and GST in New Delhi. The museum showcases the history and evolution of customs and taxation in India, from ancient times to the present day. The museum also displays various artifacts, documents, uniforms, stamps, and medals related to the customs and GST departments.

  - **New Finds at Rakhigarhi - A Harappan Site**: In February 2022, Indian archaeologists announced the discovery of new findings at Rakhigarhi, a Harappan site in Haryana. The findings include a burial site with 11 skeletons, a copper vessel, a gold bead, and a terracotta figurine. The findings are expected to shed more light on the culture and lifestyle of the Harappan civilization, which flourished in the Indus Valley from 2500 to 1900 BCE.

  - **Devayatanam - An Odyssey of Indian Temple Architecture**: In March 2022, India launched the Devayatanam - An Odyssey of Indian Temple Architecture, a digital exhibition that showcases the diversity and beauty of Indian temple architecture. The exhibition, which is available on the Google Arts and Culture platform, features 3D models, videos, images, and stories of 100 temples from across India, spanning different periods, regions, and styles.

  - **Republic Day Parade Awards 2022**: In January 2022, India celebrated its 73rd Republic Day with a grand parade at the Rajpath in New Delhi. The parade, which showcases the cultural and military prowess of the country, also features various awards for the best performances and displays. The awards for 2022 included:

    - Best marching contingent: Indian Air Force
    - Best tableau: Uttar Pradesh
    - Best cultural performance: Ladakh
    - Best school children performance: Delhi Public School, Dwarka

  - **India nominates Hoysala Temples for inscription in the World Heritage List**: In January 2022, India nominated the Hoysala Temples for inscription in the UNESCO World Heritage List. The Hoysala Temples are a group of 50 temples built by the Hoysala dynasty between the 11th and 14th centuries in Karnataka. The temples are known for their intricate and elaborate carvings, depicting various themes from Hindu mythology, literature, and art.

  - **India gets back Goat-Headed Yogini Statue**: In February 2022, India received back a 10th-century stone statue of a goat-headed yogini from the United States. The statue, which belongs to the Pratihara style of art, was stolen from a temple in Madhya Pradesh in 1994 and smuggled to the US. The statue was recovered by the US authorities in 2015 and handed over to India as part of a bilateral agreement on cultural property protection.

- These are some of the current developments in arts and culture in India, which demonstrate the country's rich and vibrant heritage, as well as its efforts to preserve and promote it.
