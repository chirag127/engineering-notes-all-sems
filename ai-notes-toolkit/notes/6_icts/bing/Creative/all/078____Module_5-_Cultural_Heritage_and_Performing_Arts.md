## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

- Cultural heritage is the legacy of physical and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations, maintained in the present and bestowed for the benefit of future generations.
- Cultural heritage includes tangible culture (such as buildings, monuments, landscapes, books, works of art, and artifacts), intangible culture (such as folklore, traditions, language, and knowledge), and natural heritage (such as culturally significant landscapes, and biodiversity).
- Performing arts are forms of art that use the body, voice, or objects to convey artistic expression, such as music, dance, theatre, opera, and circus arts.
- Performing arts are often performed in front of a live audience, but may also be recorded, broadcast, or streamed online.
- Performing arts can serve various functions, such as entertainment, education, social commentary, cultural preservation, and artistic expression.
- Some examples of performing arts are:

  - Music: the art of creating and performing sounds, usually with instruments or vocals, that express emotions, ideas, or stories. Music can be classified into genres, such as classical, rock, pop, jazz, folk, and rap.
  - Dance: the art of moving the body in a rhythmic and expressive way, usually to music, that communicates emotions, ideas, or stories. Dance can be classified into styles, such as ballet, modern, hip hop, salsa, and tap.
  - Theatre: the art of staging and performing a scripted or improvised drama, comedy, or musical, that involves actors, costumes, props, lighting, and sound. Theatre can be classified into genres, such as tragedy, comedy, melodrama, and musical.
  - Opera: the art of combining music, drama, and spectacle, that involves singers, orchestra, chorus, costumes, sets, and lighting. Opera can be classified into periods, such as baroque, classical, romantic, and modern.
  - Circus arts: the art of performing feats of skill, agility, and daring, that involve acrobats, clowns, jugglers, magicians, and animals. Circus arts can be classified into types, such as traditional, contemporary, and new circus.
