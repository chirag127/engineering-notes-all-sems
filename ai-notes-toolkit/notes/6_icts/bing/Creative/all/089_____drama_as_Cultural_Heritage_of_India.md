# Drama as Cultural Heritage of India

- Drama is a form of performing arts, where stories are enacted by the utilisation of dialogue, music, gesture, dance, and sound.
- Drama is one of the oldest and richest forms of cultural heritage of India, dating back to the ancient Vedic period.
- Drama in India has been influenced by various religious, social, and literary traditions, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Islam, and Sanskrit .
- Drama in India has also been shaped by various regional and linguistic styles, such as Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Urdu, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, etc.
- Drama in India has been a medium of propagation for religion and social reforms, as well as a source of entertainment and education.
- Drama in India has produced many eminent playwrights, actors, directors, and genres, such as Bharata, Kalidasa, Bhasa, Bhavabhuti, Harsha, Sudraka, Vishakhadatta, Jayadeva, Tulsidas, Rabindranath Tagore, Mohan Rakesh, Vijay Tendulkar, Girish Karnad, Badal Sircar, Habib Tanvir, Naseeruddin Shah, Shyam Benegal, Mahesh Bhatt, etc.
- Drama in India has also contributed to the global theatre scene, by influencing and collaborating with other cultures and forms, such as Greek, Roman, Persian, Chinese, Japanese, British, American, etc.
