## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

- Indian literature is the literature produced in the Indian subcontinent and its diaspora. It includes works in many languages, such as Sanskrit, Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, and English. Indian literature reflects the rich and diverse cultural heritage of India, as well as its history, philosophy, religion, and social issues.
- Indian culture is the collective way of life, values, beliefs, customs, and traditions of the people of India. It is influenced by various factors, such as geography, climate, history, religion, ethnicity, and languages. Indian culture is known for its diversity and unity, as well as its artistic, scientific, and spiritual achievements. Some of the aspects of Indian culture are greetings, religious customs, festivals, family structure, marriage, symbols, cuisine, clothing, dances, epics, mythology, martial arts, and languages.
- Indian tradition is the set of practices and rituals that are followed by the people of India, either as part of their religion or culture. Some of the common Indian traditions are yoga, meditation, ayurveda, astrology, vegetarianism, respect for elders, hospitality, joint family system, arranged marriages, dowry, caste system, and social hierarchy. Indian tradition also includes various forms of folk art, music, and literature, such as Sama Veda, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Bhagavad Gita, Panchatantra, Kathakali, Bharatanatyam, and Bhangra.
- Indian practices are the actions and behaviors that are performed by the people of India, either as part of their daily life or on special occasions. Some of the common Indian practices are namaste, pranam, aarti, puja, havan, fasting, pilgrimage, charity, karma, reincarnation, samsara, moksha, dharma, ahimsa, satyagraha, swadeshi, and swaraj. Indian practices also include various forms of social service, education, science, technology, and innovation, such as Nalanda, Takshashila, zero, decimal system, surgery, metallurgy, and chess.
