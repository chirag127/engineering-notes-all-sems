### Medicine in India and Ancient India

- Medicine in India is a diverse and ancient field of knowledge and practice that encompasses various systems of health care, such as Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, Yoga, Naturopathy, and Homeopathy.
- Ayurveda is the most ancient system of Indian medicine, which is based on the concept of balance among the five elements (earth, water, fire, air, and ether) and the three doshas (vata, pitta, and kapha) that govern the body and mind  .
- Ayurveda originated in the Vedic period, when Indians attained a high degree of knowledge and wisdom in medicine, surgery, materia medica, and chemistry . Ayurveda is considered a holistic and natural approach to physical and mental health, and it uses various methods such as diet, herbs, massage, meditation, and purification to prevent and treat diseases.
- Some of the famous ancient Indian physicians and surgeons were Charaka, Sushruta, Vagbhata, Nagarjuna, and Dhanvantari  . They wrote authoritative texts on various aspects of medicine and surgery, such as the Charaka Samhita, the Sushruta Samhita, the Ashtanga Hridaya, the Sushruta Tantra, and the Sushruta Nidana  .
- These texts describe various diseases, symptoms, diagnoses, treatments, surgical procedures, instruments, and anatomical knowledge in detail. Some of the remarkable achievements of ancient Indian medicine and surgery include plastic surgery, cataract surgery, rhinoplasty, lithotomy, caesarean section, anesthesia, and vaccination  .
- Medicine in India has evolved over time and has been influenced by various factors, such as religion, culture, geography, and foreign invasions. India has also contributed to the development of medicine in other parts of the world, such as China, Tibet, Persia, Arabia, and Europe.
- Today, medicine in India is a blend of modern and traditional systems, and it offers a wide range of options and opportunities for health care seekers and providers. India is also a hub of medical tourism, research, and innovation in the field of medicine.
