### Color image enhancement

- Color image enhancement is the process of improving the visual quality of a color image by adjusting its brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, or other attributes.
- Color image enhancement can be used for various purposes, such as reducing noise, enhancing edges, highlighting features, correcting colors, or creating artistic effects.
- Color image enhancement techniques can be classified into two categories: global and local.
  - Global techniques apply the same transformation to all pixels in the image, such as histogram equalization, contrast stretching, or gamma correction.
  - Local techniques apply different transformations to different regions in the image, such as adaptive histogram equalization, unsharp masking, or retinex.
- Color image enhancement techniques can also be classified into two categories: spatial domain and frequency domain.
  - Spatial domain techniques operate directly on the pixel values of the image, such as point operations, filtering, or masking.
  - Frequency domain techniques operate on the Fourier transform of the image, such as filtering, enhancement, or compression.
- Color image enhancement techniques can be evaluated based on subjective and objective criteria, such as visual perception, image quality metrics, or application performance.
