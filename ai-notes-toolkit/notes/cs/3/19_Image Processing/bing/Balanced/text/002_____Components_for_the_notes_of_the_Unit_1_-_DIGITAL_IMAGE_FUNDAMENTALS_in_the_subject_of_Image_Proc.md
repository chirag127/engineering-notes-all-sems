### Components for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

- **Digital image processing** is the process of manipulating and transforming digital images using mathematical and computational techniques.
- **Digital image fundamentals** are the basic concepts and principles that underlie the representation, acquisition, enhancement, and analysis of digital images.
- The main components for the notes of the unit 1 are:

  - **Elements of visual perception**: This component covers the human visual system, the properties of light and color, and the psychophysical aspects of vision.
  - **Light and the electromagnetic spectrum**: This component covers the physical nature of light, the electromagnetic spectrum, and the interaction of light with matter.
  - **Image sensing and acquisition**: This component covers the devices and methods for capturing digital images, such as cameras, scanners, and sensors .
  - **Image sampling and quantization**: This component covers the process of converting a continuous image into a discrete image, by selecting a finite number of pixels and assigning them a finite number of intensity levels .
  - **Image representation and formats**: This component covers the ways of storing and encoding digital images, such as bitmap, vector, and compressed formats .
  - **Basic relationships between pixels**: This component covers the concepts of neighborhood, connectivity, distance, and adjacency of pixels, and the operations that can be performed on them, such as arithmetic, logical, and geometric operations .
  - **Image enhancement**: This component covers the techniques for improving the visual quality of an image, such as contrast stretching, histogram equalization, filtering, and sharpening .
  - **Image analysis**: This component covers the techniques for extracting information from an image, such as segmentation, edge detection, feature extraction, and pattern recognition .
  - **Image compression**: This component covers the techniques for reducing the size of an image, such as lossless and lossy compression, and the standards for image compression, such as JPEG, PNG, and GIF .
  - **Image synthesis**: This component covers the techniques for creating new images from existing images, such as image blending, morphing, and warping .
