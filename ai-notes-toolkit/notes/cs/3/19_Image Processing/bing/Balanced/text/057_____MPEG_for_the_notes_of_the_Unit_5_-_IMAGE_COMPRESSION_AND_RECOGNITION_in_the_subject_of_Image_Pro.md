### MPEG

MPEG stands for Moving Picture Experts Group, which is a group of experts that develops standards for digital video and audio compression. MPEG standards aim to achieve high compression ratios by exploiting the redundancy and irrelevancy in the data  .

Some of the main features of MPEG standards are:

- They use a hybrid of spatial and temporal compression techniques, which means they reduce the data both within and between the frames of a video sequence.
- They use a technique called Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT), which transforms the pixel values of a block of data into a set of frequency coefficients, which can be quantized and encoded more efficiently .
- They use a technique called chroma subsampling, which reduces the amount of color information in the data, since the human eye is more sensitive to brightness than to color.
- They use a technique called motion estimation and compensation, which predicts the motion of the objects in the video and encodes only the difference between the predicted and the actual frames.
- They use a technique called variable length coding, which assigns shorter codes to more frequent symbols and longer codes to less frequent symbols, which reduces the average code length.

Some of the main MPEG standards and their applications are:

- MPEG-1 (1993): Coding of moving pictures and associated audio for digital storage media at up to about 1.5 Mbit/s (ISO/IEC 11172). This standard is used for video CDs, MP3 audio, and some digital satellite TV services.
- MPEG-2 (1995): Generic coding of moving pictures and associated audio information (ISO/IEC 13818). This standard is used for digital TV, DVD, and some digital satellite and cable TV services.
- MPEG-4 (1999): Coding of audio-visual objects (ISO/IEC 14496). This standard is used for interactive multimedia, web streaming, and mobile devices.
- MPEG-7 (2002): Multimedia content description interface (ISO/IEC 15938). This standard is used for describing the content and features of multimedia data, such as color, shape, texture, motion, etc.
- MPEG-21 (2003): Multimedia framework (ISO/IEC 21000). This standard is used for defining the elements and processes for the creation, delivery, and consumption of multimedia content, such as rights, identifiers, formats, etc.
