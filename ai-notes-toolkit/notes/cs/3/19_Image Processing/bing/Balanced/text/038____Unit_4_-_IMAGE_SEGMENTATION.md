## Unit 4 - IMAGE SEGMENTATION

- Image segmentation is the process of partitioning an image into multiple segments, each of which consists of pixels that share certain characteristics .
- Image segmentation is typically used to locate objects and boundaries in images, such as faces, cars, roads, etc.  .
- Image segmentation can also be used for image analysis, such as measuring the size, shape, color, or texture of objects, or for image editing, such as cropping, masking, or blending images .
- Image segmentation can be performed using various techniques, such as thresholding, clustering, edge detection, region growing, watershed, or deep learning  .
- Image segmentation can be classified into two types: semantic segmentation and instance segmentation .
  - Semantic segmentation assigns a class label to each pixel, such as person, dog, or sky, without distinguishing between different instances of the same class .
  - Instance segmentation assigns a unique label to each pixel, such as person 1, person 2, or dog 1, allowing to identify and count individual objects in the image .
- Image segmentation can be evaluated using various metrics, such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, IoU, or Dice coefficient, depending on the task and the ground truth labels .
