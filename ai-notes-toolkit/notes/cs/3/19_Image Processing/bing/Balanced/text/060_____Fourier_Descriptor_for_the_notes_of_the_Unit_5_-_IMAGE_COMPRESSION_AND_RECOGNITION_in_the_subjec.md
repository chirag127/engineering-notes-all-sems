### Fourier Descriptor

- A Fourier descriptor is a method used in object recognition and image processing to represent the boundary shape of a segment in an image.
- A Fourier descriptor is based on the Fourier series of the boundary points of the segment, which can be expressed as complex numbers.
- A Fourier descriptor can be made invariant to translation, scale, rotation and starting point by using only the magnitude of the Fourier coefficients, normalizing them by the first coefficient, multiplying them by a constant factor and discarding the phase information .
- A Fourier descriptor can capture the essential information about the contour of the segment and can be used for shape-based image retrieval.
- A Fourier descriptor can also be used to characterize shape complexity and other geometric attributes of the segment.
- A Fourier descriptor is one of the most widely used shape representation schemes, along with moment invariants.
