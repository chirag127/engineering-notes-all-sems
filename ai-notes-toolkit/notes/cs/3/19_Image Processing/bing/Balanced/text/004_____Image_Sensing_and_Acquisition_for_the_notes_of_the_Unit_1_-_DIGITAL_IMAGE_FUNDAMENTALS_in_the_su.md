### Image Sensing and Acquisition

- Image sensing and acquisition is the process of capturing and converting an analog image of a physical scene or object into a digital form that can be processed by a computer.
- Image sensing involves detecting or sensing the information that constitutes an image, such as the intensity, color, or depth of the scene elements.
- Image acquisition involves processing, compressing, and storing the image data into a digital format, such as a file or a memory.
- Image sensing and acquisition can be performed by various devices, such as cameras, scanners, sensors, or medical imaging equipment.
- Image sensing and acquisition can be influenced by various factors, such as the illumination source, the reflection or absorption of energy by the scene elements, the resolution, the noise, the distortion, or the sampling rate of the device.
- Image sensing and acquisition is the first step in image processing, which is the manipulation and analysis of digital images for various applications, such as enhancement, restoration, segmentation, compression, recognition, or visualization.
