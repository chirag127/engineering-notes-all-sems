### Two-dimensional mathematical preliminaries for the notes of the Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS in the subject of Image Processing

- A digital image is a two-dimensional function, F(x,y), where x and y are spatial coordinates, and the amplitude of F at any pair of coordinates (x,y) is called the intensity or gray level of that image at that point.
- A digital image is composed of a finite number of elements, each of which has a particular location and value. These elements are called picture elements, image elements, pels, and pixels.
- A digital image can be represented as a matrix of pixel values, where each element of the matrix corresponds to the intensity of the image at a specific spatial location.
- A digital image can be manipulated by applying mathematical operations to its pixel values, such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, convolution, filtering, transformation, etc .
- Some of the mathematical tools that are useful for analyzing and processing digital images are :
  - Laplace transform: a method of converting a function of a continuous variable (such as time or space) into a function of a complex variable (such as frequency or wavenumber), which simplifies the solution of differential equations and the analysis of systems.
  - Fourier transform: a method of decomposing a function of a continuous variable into a sum of sinusoidal functions of different frequencies, which reveals the frequency spectrum and the periodicity of the function.
  - Z-transform: a method of converting a function of a discrete variable (such as discrete time or space) into a function of a complex variable, which simplifies the solution of difference equations and the analysis of discrete systems.
  - Cosine transform: a method of decomposing a function of a continuous or discrete variable into a sum of cosine functions of different frequencies, which is useful for data compression and image enhancement.
  - Wavelet transform: a method of decomposing a function of a continuous or discrete variable into a sum of wavelet functions of different scales and positions, which is useful for image denoising, deblurring, edge detection and inpainting.
