### Mean Filters for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

- Mean filters are a type of spatial filters that are used to smooth images by reducing the amount of intensity variation between neighboring pixels .
- Mean filters work by moving through the image pixel by pixel, replacing each value with the average value of neighboring pixels, including itself .
- Mean filters can be implemented using a convolution mask or kernel, which is a small matrix that defines the weights of the neighboring pixels.
- Mean filters can be classified into different types, such as arithmetic mean, geometric mean, harmonic mean, and contraharmonic mean, depending on the formula used to calculate the average value.
- Mean filters can effectively reduce noise in images, especially uniform or Gaussian noise, by averaging out the random fluctuations .
- Mean filters can also blur the edges and fine details of images, as they do not preserve the high-frequency components of the image .
- Mean filters can be modified to reduce the blurring effect, such as using a weighted mean, a median, or a bilateral mean, which take into account the spatial or intensity similarity of the neighboring pixels .
- Mean filters are one of the simplest and most widely used methods of image restoration, but they may not be sufficient for complex or non-uniform noise models.
