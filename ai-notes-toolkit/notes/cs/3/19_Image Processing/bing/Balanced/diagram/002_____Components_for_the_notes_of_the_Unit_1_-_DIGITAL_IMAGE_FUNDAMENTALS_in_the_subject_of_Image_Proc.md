### Components for the notes of Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS

- **Definition and examples of digital images**: A digital image is a representation of a two-dimensional scene using a finite set of discrete values, called pixels or picture elements. Examples of digital images are photographs, maps, charts, diagrams, etc.
- **Elements of digital image processing systems**: A digital image processing system consists of the following elements: an image source, such as a camera or a scanner; an image processor, such as a computer or a specialized hardware; an image display, such as a monitor or a printer; and an image storage, such as a disk or a memory.
- **Image sampling and quantization**: Image sampling is the process of converting a continuous image into a discrete image by dividing it into a grid of pixels. Image quantization is the process of assigning a finite number of values to each pixel, usually based on its brightness or color. Sampling and quantization are necessary for storing and processing digital images, but they also introduce errors and limitations, such as aliasing and quantization noise.
- **Image resolution and size**: Image resolution is the measure of the amount of detail that can be captured by a digital image. It is usually expressed in terms of pixels per unit length, such as pixels per inch (ppi) or dots per inch (dpi). Image size is the measure of the amount of memory or disk space required to store a digital image. It is usually expressed in terms of the number of pixels, such as width x height, or the number of bits per pixel, such as 8-bit or 24-bit.
- **Image enhancement and restoration**: Image enhancement is the process of improving the appearance or quality of a digital image by modifying its pixel values, such as contrast, brightness, sharpness, etc. Image restoration is the process of recovering a digital image from degradation or distortion, such as noise, blur, missing pixels, etc. Image enhancement and restoration are important for making digital images more suitable for human perception or further analysis.
- **Image compression and coding**: Image compression is the process of reducing the size of a digital image by removing redundant or irrelevant information, such as repeated patterns, background details, etc. Image coding is the process of representing a digital image using a set of symbols or codes, such as binary digits, characters, etc. Image compression and coding are useful for saving storage space and transmission bandwidth, but they also involve trade-offs between quality and efficiency.
