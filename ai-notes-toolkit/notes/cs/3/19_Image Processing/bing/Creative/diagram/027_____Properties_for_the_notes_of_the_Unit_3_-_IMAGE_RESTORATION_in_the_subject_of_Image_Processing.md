- Image restoration is the process of recovering an image from a degraded version, usually a blurred and noisy image .
- Image restoration is a fundamental problem in image processing, and it also provides a testbed for more general inverse problems.
- Image restoration can be formulated as an inverse problem, where the goal is to estimate the true image i(x,y) from the measured image iM(x,y) and the point-spread function b(x,y) that describes the degradation .
- Image restoration can be classified into two categories: non-blind and blind. Non-blind restoration assumes that the point-spread function is known or can be estimated, while blind restoration does not have any prior knowledge of the point-spread function .
- Image restoration can be performed using various methods, such as spatial filtering, frequency domain filtering, regularization, maximum likelihood, maximum entropy, Bayesian inference, and deep learning  .
- Image restoration techniques should satisfy some desirable properties, such as consistency, positivity, stability, and artifact-free .
- Image restoration techniques should also consider some important properties of natural images, such as cross-scale similarity and anisotropic image features .
