# Texture

- Texture is a property of an image that describes the spatial arrangement of color or intensity values in a local neighborhood.
- Texture can be used to characterize the surface quality of an object, such as roughness, smoothness, coarseness, etc.
- Texture can also be used to segment images into regions of interest, such as foreground and background, or to classify images based on their content, such as natural or artificial, organic or geometric, etc.
- Texture analysis is the process of extracting meaningful information from texture images, such as features, descriptors, models, patterns, etc .
- Texture synthesis is the process of generating new texture images from existing ones, such as by sampling, blending, stitching, etc.
- Texture compression is the process of reducing the size of texture images without losing too much quality, such as by quantization, encoding, decoding, etc.
- Texture recognition is the process of identifying the texture category of an image or a region of an image, such as by matching, learning, classifying, etc.
