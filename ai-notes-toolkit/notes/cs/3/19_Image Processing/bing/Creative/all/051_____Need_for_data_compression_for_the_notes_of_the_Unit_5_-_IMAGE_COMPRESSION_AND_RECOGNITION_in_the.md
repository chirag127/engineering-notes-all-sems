# Need for data compression

- Data compression is the process of reducing the amount of data needed for the storage or transmission of a given piece of information, typically by the use of encoding techniques   .
- Data compression has several benefits, such as:
  - Saving storage capacity and optimizing physical storage resources .
  - Speeding up file transfer and decreasing costs for storage hardware and network bandwidth .
  - Enhancing data security and privacy by reducing the exposure of sensitive information.
  - Improving data quality and integrity by minimizing the risk of data corruption or loss.
- Data compression is especially important for image processing, because:
  - Images are typically large in size and require a lot of storage space and bandwidth to store and transmit.
  - Images often contain redundant or irrelevant information that can be removed or reduced without affecting the visual quality or meaning of the image .
  - Images can be compressed by using different techniques, such as lossless or lossy compression, depending on the desired trade-off between compression ratio and image quality.
  - Image compression can enable faster and more efficient image recognition and analysis by reducing the complexity and dimensionality of the image data.
