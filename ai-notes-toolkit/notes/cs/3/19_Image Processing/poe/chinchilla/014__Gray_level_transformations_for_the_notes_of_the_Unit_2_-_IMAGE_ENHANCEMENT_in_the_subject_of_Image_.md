### Gray Level Transformations

Gray level transformations are used to enhance the quality of an image by changing the intensity levels of the pixels in the image. In this section, we will discuss the different types of gray level transformations that can be applied to an image.

#### Types of Gray Level Transformations

1. **Negative Transformation:** This transformation is used to obtain the negative of an image. In this transformation, the intensity levels of the pixels are inverted, i.e., the black pixels become white, and the white pixels become black.

2. **Logarithmic Transformation:** This transformation is used to enhance the low-intensity regions of an image. In this transformation, the intensity levels of the pixels are compressed using a logarithmic function.

3. **Power-Law Transformation:** This transformation is used to enhance the high-intensity regions of an image. In this transformation, the intensity levels of the pixels are raised to a power.

4. **Piecewise-Linear Transformation:** This transformation is used to adjust the contrast of an image. In this transformation, the intensity levels of the pixels are mapped to a new range using a piecewise-linear function.

5. **Histogram Equalization:** This transformation is used to enhance the contrast of an image. In this transformation, the intensity levels of the pixels are spread over the entire range of intensities.

#### Conclusion

Gray level transformations are an important tool in image processing, as they can be used to enhance the quality of an image. By applying the appropriate transformation, we can adjust the contrast and brightness of an image to make it more visually appealing. It is essential to choose the right transformation for a given image to achieve the desired result.
