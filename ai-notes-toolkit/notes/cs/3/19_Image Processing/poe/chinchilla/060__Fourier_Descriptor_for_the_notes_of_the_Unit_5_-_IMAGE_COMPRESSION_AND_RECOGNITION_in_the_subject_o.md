### Fourier Descriptor for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing

Fourier Descriptor is a technique used for image compression and recognition in the field of Image Processing. It involves the transformation of an image from the spatial domain to the frequency domain, where the image is represented as a sum of sinusoidal functions.

Here are some key points to understand Fourier Descriptor in the context of Image Compression and Recognition:

1. Fourier Transform: The first step in Fourier Descriptor is to apply Fourier Transform to the image. Fourier Transform is a mathematical technique that converts a signal from the time domain to the frequency domain. In the context of images, Fourier Transform is applied to convert an image from the spatial domain to the frequency domain.

2. Frequency Domain Representation: The output of the Fourier Transform is a frequency domain representation of the image. In this representation, the image is represented as a sum of sinusoidal functions, where each function corresponds to a certain frequency.

3. Fourier Descriptor: Fourier Descriptor is a set of coefficients that describe the frequency domain representation of the image. These coefficients can be used for image compression and recognition. In image compression, the coefficients can be truncated to reduce the size of the image. In image recognition, the coefficients can be used to compare two images and determine their similarity.

4. Image Compression: Fourier Descriptor can be used for image compression by truncating the coefficients. The higher frequency coefficients can be discarded as they represent smaller details in the image. This results in a smaller representation of the image, which can be stored and transmitted more efficiently.

5. Image Recognition: Fourier Descriptor can be used for image recognition by comparing the coefficients of two images. The similarity between the two images can be determined by comparing their Fourier Descriptors. This technique is commonly used in applications such as face recognition and object recognition.

6. Limitations: Fourier Descriptor has some limitations. It is sensitive to noise and small changes in the image can result in significant changes in the Fourier Descriptor. Additionally, Fourier Descriptor is not suitable for images with complex shapes and textures.

In conclusion, Fourier Descriptor is a powerful technique for image compression and recognition in the field of Image Processing. By converting an image from the spatial domain to the frequency domain, Fourier Descriptor allows for efficient storage and transmission of images and enables comparison of images for recognition purposes.
