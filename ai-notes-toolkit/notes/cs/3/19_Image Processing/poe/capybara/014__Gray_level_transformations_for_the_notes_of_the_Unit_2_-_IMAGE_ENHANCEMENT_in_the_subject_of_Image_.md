### Gray level transformations for the notes of the Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT in the subject of Image Processing

Gray level transformations are the techniques used for image enhancement. It is used to manipulate the intensity levels of an image without affecting its color values. This technique is used to increase the contrast of an image, brightness, or the darkness of the image.

The following are the different types of Gray level transformations:

- **Negative transformation:** In this transformation, the intensity values of the image are inverted. The pixels with high intensity values will be converted to low intensity values and vice versa. This transformation is used to obtain a negative image of the original image.

- **Log transformation:** The log transformation is applied to images with low dynamic ranges. This transformation is used to expand the range of pixel values in the dark areas of the image. The log transformation can be used to increase the contrast of the image.

- **Power-law transformation:** The power-law transformation is used to adjust the gamma value of the image. Gamma value determines the brightness of the image. By adjusting the gamma value, the image can be made brighter or darker.

- **Histogram equalization:** Histogram equalization is a technique used to enhance the contrast of an image. In this technique, the histogram of the image is equalized. This technique is used to increase the contrast of an image with a low contrast.

- **Contrast stretching:** Contrast stretching is a technique used to enhance the contrast of an image by expanding the range of pixel values. This technique is used to increase the contrast of an image with a low contrast.

In conclusion, Gray level transformations are an important technique used in image enhancement. It is used to manipulate the intensity levels of an image without affecting its color values. The different types of Gray level transformations are negative transformation, log transformation, power-law transformation, histogram equalization, and contrast stretching. These techniques are used to increase the contrast of an image, brightness, or the darkness of the image.
