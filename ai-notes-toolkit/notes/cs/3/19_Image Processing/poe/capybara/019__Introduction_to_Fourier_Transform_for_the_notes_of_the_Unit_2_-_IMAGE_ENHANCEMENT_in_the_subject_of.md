### Introduction to Fourier Transform for the notes of the Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT in the subject of Image Processing

Fourier Transform is a mathematical tool that is used to analyze signals and images in the frequency domain. It is an important concept in Image Processing and is widely used in various applications. The following points explain the basics of Fourier Transform:

- Fourier Transform converts a signal or image from the spatial domain to the frequency domain. This means that a signal or image is represented as a sum of sine and cosine waves of different frequencies.
- The frequency domain representation of a signal or image provides information about the different frequencies present in it. It helps in analyzing and understanding the characteristics of the signal or image.
- In Image Processing, Fourier Transform is used for image enhancement, filtering, compression, and restoration.
- Fourier Transform can be represented in two forms- Continuous Fourier Transform (CFT) and Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT).
- CFT is used for continuous signals, while DFT is used for discrete signals such as digital images.
- The Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) is an algorithm used for efficiently calculating the DFT of a signal or image.
- The frequency spectrum obtained from Fourier Transform has two components- magnitude and phase. The magnitude spectrum represents the amplitude of different frequencies present in the signal or image, while the phase spectrum represents the phase shift of each frequency component.
- In Image Processing, Fourier Transform is used for frequency domain filtering, where undesirable frequencies are removed from the image. This helps in enhancing the image and removing noise.
- Fourier Transform is also used for image compression by removing high-frequency components that are not significant for the image.
- The inverse Fourier Transform is used to convert a signal or image from the frequency domain back to the spatial domain.

In conclusion, Fourier Transform is a powerful tool in Image Processing that helps in analyzing and enhancing images in the frequency domain. Understanding the basics of Fourier Transform is essential for anyone working in the field of Image Processing.
