### Smoothing and Sharpening frequency domain filters for the notes of the Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT in the subject of Image Processing

Image Enhancement is a technique to improve the quality of an image by removing the unwanted noise and sharpening the edges. Smoothing and sharpening frequency domain filters are two important techniques used for image enhancement. Here are some key points to understand these techniques for the notes of Unit 2:

#### Smoothing Frequency Domain Filters
- Smoothing frequency domain filters are used to reduce the noise in an image and make it smoother.
- These filters work by removing the high-frequency components from the image, which are responsible for the noise.
- The most commonly used smoothing frequency domain filter is the Gaussian filter.
- The Gaussian filter works by convolving the image with a Gaussian kernel, which is a bell-shaped curve.
- The size of the kernel determines the amount of smoothing applied to the image.
- Smoothing frequency domain filters are also used to blur the image intentionally for creative purposes.

#### Sharpening Frequency Domain Filters
- Sharpening frequency domain filters are used to enhance the edges in an image and make it sharper.
- These filters work by increasing the high-frequency components in the image, which are responsible for the edges.
- The most commonly used sharpening frequency domain filter is the Laplacian filter.
- The Laplacian filter works by convolving the image with a kernel that highlights the edges in the image.
- The size of the kernel determines the amount of sharpening applied to the image.
- Sharpening frequency domain filters can also enhance the texture of the image by increasing the contrast between adjacent pixels.

In conclusion, smoothing and sharpening frequency domain filters are two important techniques used for image enhancement. Smoothing filters are used to reduce noise and make the image smoother, while sharpening filters are used to enhance the edges and make the image sharper. These techniques are widely used in various applications like medical imaging, satellite imaging, and digital photography.
