### Properties for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

When studying the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing, it is important to understand the following properties:

- **Spatial Domain Methods** - These methods operate directly on the pixel values of the image. They are simple and easy to understand, but they may not produce the desired results in all cases.

- **Frequency Domain Methods** - These methods operate on the frequency components of the image. They are more complex than spatial domain methods, but they can produce better results in certain cases.

- **Noise Reduction Techniques** - Image Restoration involves removing noise from an image. There are several techniques available for noise reduction such as Median Filtering, Gaussian Filtering, Wiener Filtering, etc.

- **Image Enhancement Techniques** - Image Enhancement techniques are used to improve the visual quality of an image. These techniques include Histogram Equalization, Contrast Stretching, Sharpening, and more.

- **Inverse Filtering** - Inverse filtering is a technique used to recover the original image from a degraded image. It is based on the assumption that the degradation function is known.

- **Deconvolution** - Deconvolution is a technique used to remove blur from an image. It is based on the assumption that the blur function is known.

- **Restoration Evaluation Metrics** - To evaluate the effectiveness of image restoration techniques, several metrics are used such as Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR), Structural Similarity Index (SSIM), Mean Square Error (MSE), etc.

- **Applications of Image Restoration** - Image Restoration has several applications such as Medical Imaging, Forensics, Astronomy, and more. It is used to restore degraded images and improve their visual quality.

By understanding these properties, one can gain a better understanding of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing.
