

### Image Sensing and Acquisition

- The process of capturing digital images from the real world is called image sensing and acquisition.
- The captured images are usually in the form of electronic signals that can be processed and manipulated using digital image processing techniques.
- The quality of the captured images depends on the characteristics of the image sensor and the acquisition system used.
- Image sensors are electronic devices that convert light into electrical signals. Common types of image sensors include CCD (charge-coupled device) and CMOS (complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor) sensors.
- The acquisition system refers to the hardware and software used to capture and store digital images. This includes cameras, scanners, and other imaging devices, as well as computer systems and software applications used to process and analyze the images.
- The resolution of the captured images is an important factor in determining their quality. Higher resolution images have more detail and are generally of higher quality than lower resolution images.
- Other factors that can affect the quality of captured images include lighting conditions, exposure settings, and image processing techniques used during acquisition.
- In summary, image sensing and acquisition is the process of capturing digital images from the real world using specialized hardware and software. The quality of the captured images depends on the characteristics of the image sensor and the acquisition system used, as well as other factors such as resolution and image processing techniques.
