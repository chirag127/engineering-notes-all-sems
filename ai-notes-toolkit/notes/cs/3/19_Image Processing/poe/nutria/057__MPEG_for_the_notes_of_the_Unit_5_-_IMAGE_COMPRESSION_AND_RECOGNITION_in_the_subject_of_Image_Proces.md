
### MPEG for the Notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the Subject of Image Processing

1. MPEG (Moving Picture Experts Group) is a standard for digital video and audio compression and decompression. It is used for digital television, streaming media, and digital storage.

2. MPEG compression is based on the Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) algorithm, which reduces the amount of data needed to represent a digital image or video.

3. MPEG-1 is used in Video CD and is the basis of the MP3 audio format. MPEG-2 is used in digital television, DVD, and Blu-ray Disc. MPEG-4 is used for streaming media and video conferencing.

4. Image compression is the process of reducing the size of an image file without affecting the quality of the image. Image recognition is the process of recognizing patterns in an image.

5. Image compression algorithms reduce the size of an image by removing redundant information or by transforming the image into a more efficient format. Image recognition algorithms use machine learning and pattern recognition to identify objects in an image.

6. JPEG (Joint Photographic Experts Group) is a commonly used image compression algorithm. It is used for compressing digital photographs and other continuous-tone images. JPEG is lossy, meaning that some of the original image information is lost during compression.

7. MPEG-4 is a newer image compression algorithm that is used for compressing digital video and audio. It is a lossy algorithm, but it is more efficient than JPEG.

8. Image recognition algorithms use machine learning and pattern recognition to identify objects in an image. They can be used for facial recognition, object recognition, and motion detection.
