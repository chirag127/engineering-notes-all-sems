
## Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT

* Image enhancement is the process of improving the visual appearance of an image.
* It is used to emphasize certain features of the image, such as contrast, brightness, sharpness, and color.
* Image enhancement techniques can be divided into two categories: spatial and frequency domain techniques.
* Spatial domain techniques process the pixels of an image directly, while frequency domain techniques process the frequency components of an image.
* Common image enhancement techniques include contrast stretching, histogram equalization, linear filtering, non-linear filtering, and sharpening.
* Image enhancement is used in many fields such as medical imaging, satellite imaging, and digital photography.
