
### Regional Descriptors for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMAGE COMPRESSION AND RECOGNITION in the subject of Image Processing

1. Regional descriptors are used to describe the characteristics of an image in a particular region.
2. They are used to identify and classify objects in an image.
3. Examples of regional descriptors include SIFT, SURF, and HOG.
4. SIFT (Scale-Invariant Feature Transform) is a method of extracting features from an image that are invariant to scale and rotation.
5. SURF (Speeded-Up Robust Features) is a method of extracting features from an image that are invariant to scale and rotation, but are more robust than SIFT.
6. HOG (Histogram of Oriented Gradients) is a method of extracting features from an image that are invariant to illumination and rotation, but are more robust than SIFT and SURF.
7. Regional descriptors are used in image compression and recognition applications such as facial recognition, object recognition, and image matching.
