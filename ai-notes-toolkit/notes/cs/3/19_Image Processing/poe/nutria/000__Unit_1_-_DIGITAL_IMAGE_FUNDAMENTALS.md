
## Unit 1 - DIGITAL IMAGE FUNDAMENTALS

1. Digital images are digital representations of physical objects, scenes, or concepts. 
2. Digital images can be created, stored, and manipulated using a variety of digital image formats. 
3. Digital images can be edited and manipulated using image editing software.
4. Digital images can be compressed to reduce their file size.
5. Digital images can be printed or displayed on digital devices such as computers, tablets, and smartphones.
6. Digital images can be used to create artwork, advertisements, websites, and other digital products.
7. Digital images can be used to represent data in a visual form.
8. Digital images can be used to analyze and recognize patterns in data.
9. Digital images can be used to enhance the quality of a product or service.
10. Digital images can be used to communicate information to a broad audience.
