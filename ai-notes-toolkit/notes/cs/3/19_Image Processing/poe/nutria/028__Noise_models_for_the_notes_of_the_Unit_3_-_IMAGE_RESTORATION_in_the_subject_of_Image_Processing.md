
### Noise models for the notes of the Unit 3 - IMAGE RESTORATION in the subject of Image Processing

* Noise models are used to describe and analyze the effects of noise on images.
* Additive noise models are used to describe the effects of noise that is added to an image.
* Multiplicative noise models are used to describe the effects of noise that is multiplied with an image.
* Gaussian noise is a type of additive noise that follows a normal distribution.
* Impulse noise is a type of noise that occurs in the form of isolated pixels with a random intensity.
* Salt-and-pepper noise is a type of impulse noise that is characterized by a large number of white and black pixels.
* Speckle noise is a type of multiplicative noise that is caused by coherent light sources.
* Image restoration is the process of removing noise from an image.
* Image restoration techniques can be divided into two categories: spatial domain techniques and frequency domain techniques.
* Spatial domain techniques include median filtering, averaging, and morphological operations.
* Frequency domain techniques include Fourier transform, wavelet transform, and Wiener filtering.
