
### Shift Codes for Unit 5 - Image Compression and Recognition
* Shift codes are a type of image compression technique used to reduce the size of an image while preserving its quality.
* The technique works by shifting the pixels of an image in a certain pattern. The pattern is determined by a set of parameters that are used to define the amount of shifting to be done.
* By shifting the pixels in a certain pattern, the image can be compressed to a smaller size without losing any of its original quality.
* Shift codes can be used for both lossless and lossy compression. Lossless compression preserves all the original data, while lossy compression removes some of the data to reduce the size of the image.
* Shift codes are used in a variety of image processing applications, including facial recognition, medical imaging, and satellite imagery.
* Shift codes can also be used to improve the performance of image recognition algorithms, as they reduce the amount of data that needs to be processed.
