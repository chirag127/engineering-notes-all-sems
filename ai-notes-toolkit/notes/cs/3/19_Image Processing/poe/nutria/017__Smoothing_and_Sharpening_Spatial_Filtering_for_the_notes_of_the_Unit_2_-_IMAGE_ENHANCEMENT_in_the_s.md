
### Smoothing and Sharpening Spatial Filtering

1. Smoothing is the process of reducing the level of detail in an image by reducing the contrast between adjacent pixels. This can be achieved by applying a low-pass filter to the image.

2. Sharpening is the process of increasing the level of detail in an image by increasing the contrast between adjacent pixels. This can be achieved by applying a high-pass filter to the image.

3. Spatial filtering is the process of applying a filter to an image in order to modify its characteristics. Commonly used filters include Gaussian, Laplacian and Sobel filters.

4. Image enhancement is the process of improving the quality of an image by modifying its characteristics. Commonly used techniques include contrast stretching, histogram equalization and gamma correction.
