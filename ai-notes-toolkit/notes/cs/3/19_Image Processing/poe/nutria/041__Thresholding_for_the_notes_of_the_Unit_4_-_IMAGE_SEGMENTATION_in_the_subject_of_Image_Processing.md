
### Thresholding for the Notes of Unit 4 - Image Segmentation in Image Processing 

1. Image segmentation is the process of dividing an image into multiple segments, or regions. 
2. Thresholding is a type of image segmentation that assigns pixel values to a range of values.
3. It is used to separate objects from the background of an image and can be used for a variety of applications such as object recognition, feature extraction, and image analysis.
4. There are two types of thresholding: global and local.
5. Global thresholding is the process of applying a single threshold to the entire image.
6. Local thresholding is the process of applying different thresholds to different regions of the image.
7. Thresholding can be used to simplify an image by removing noise or unwanted details.
8. It can also be used to identify objects in an image by segmenting them into distinct regions.
9. Thresholding is often used in combination with other image processing techniques such as edge detection and morphological operations.
10. Thresholding is an important step in image segmentation and can be used to improve the accuracy of the segmentation results.
