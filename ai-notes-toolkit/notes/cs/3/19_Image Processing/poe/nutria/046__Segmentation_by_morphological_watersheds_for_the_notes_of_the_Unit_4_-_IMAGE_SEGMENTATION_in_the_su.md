
### Segmentation by Morphological Watersheds

1. Morphological watersheds are a type of image segmentation method that uses the morphological properties of an image to partition it into distinct regions. 
2. This method is based on the concept of watersheds in geography, where a watershed is a region bounded by a line of high points. 
3. In image segmentation, the watersheds are determined by the image's morphological properties, such as intensity, color, and texture. 
4. Morphological watersheds use a set of morphological operators such as erosion, dilation, and opening to partition the image into distinct regions. 
5. The morphological operators are applied to the image in order to create a gradient image, which is then used to identify the watersheds. 
6. The watersheds are then used to separate the image into distinct regions. 
7. Morphological watersheds are used in a variety of applications, including medical image segmentation, object recognition, and image classification.
