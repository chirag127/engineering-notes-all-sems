
### Properties for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Image Restoration in the Subject of Image Processing

1. Image restoration is the process of recovering an image from its degraded form.
2. Image restoration techniques can be used to reduce the effects of noise, blur, and distortion in an image.
3. Restoration techniques can also be used to enhance the features of an image, such as sharpening, contrast enhancement, and color correction.
4. Image restoration techniques can be divided into two categories: deterministic and statistical.
5. Deterministic methods rely on the assumption that the image is composed of a set of known components, and the restoration process is based on the known characteristics of these components.
6. Statistical methods use statistical models to describe the image and its components, and the restoration process is based on the statistical properties of these models.
7. Inverse filtering is a deterministic method of image restoration that is used to remove blur from an image.
8. Wiener filtering is a statistical method of image restoration that is used to reduce noise in an image.
9. Wavelet filtering is a hybrid method of image restoration that combines deterministic and statistical techniques to reduce both blur and noise in an image.
10. Image restoration techniques can be used to improve the quality of digital images and make them more suitable for use in applications such as medical imaging, surveillance, and remote sensing.
