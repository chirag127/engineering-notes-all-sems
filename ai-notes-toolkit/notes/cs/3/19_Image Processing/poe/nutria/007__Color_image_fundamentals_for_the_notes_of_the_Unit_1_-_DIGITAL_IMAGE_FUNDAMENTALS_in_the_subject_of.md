
### Color Image Fundamentals

1. Color images are composed of three color channels: red, green, and blue.
2. Each color channel is represented by 8 bits, resulting in a total of 24 bits per pixel. This allows for up to 16.7 million different colors.
3. Color images are stored in the RGB color space, which is an additive color model.
4. The RGB color space is a device-dependent color space, meaning that the colors displayed on a monitor will be different from the colors printed on paper.
5. In order to ensure consistent color reproduction across different devices, color images are often converted to a device-independent color space such as CIE Lab or CIE XYZ.
6. Color images can also be represented in the HSV color space, which is a cylindrical representation of the RGB color space.
7. Color images can be manipulated using various color operations such as hue, saturation, and brightness.
8. Color images can also be manipulated using various color models such as CMYK and HSL.
9. Color images can be manipulated using various color management systems such as Adobe RGB and sRGB.
