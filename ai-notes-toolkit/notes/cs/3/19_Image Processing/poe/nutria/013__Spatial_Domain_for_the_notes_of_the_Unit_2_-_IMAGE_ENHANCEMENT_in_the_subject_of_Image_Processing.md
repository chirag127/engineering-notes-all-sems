
### Spatial Domain for the notes of the Unit 2 - IMAGE ENHANCEMENT in the subject of Image Processing

* Image enhancement is a process of improving the quality of an image.
* It involves adjusting the brightness, contrast, sharpness, color balance, etc. of an image.
* Image enhancement can be done in the spatial domain, which is the domain of an image's individual pixels.
* In the spatial domain, image enhancement can be done by adjusting the intensity of each pixel in the image.
* This can be accomplished by changing the intensity of a pixel to a new value, or by applying a mathematical transformation to the intensity of each pixel.
* Common transformations used in image enhancement include logarithmic and gamma transformations, histogram equalization, and spatial filtering.
* Logarithmic and gamma transformations are used to adjust the brightness and contrast of an image.
* Histogram equalization is used to improve the contrast of an image by redistributing the pixel intensities.
* Spatial filtering is used to reduce noise or enhance edges in an image.
