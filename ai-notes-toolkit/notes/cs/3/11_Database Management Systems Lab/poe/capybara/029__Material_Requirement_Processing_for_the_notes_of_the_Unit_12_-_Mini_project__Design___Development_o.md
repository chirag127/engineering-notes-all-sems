### Material Requirement Processing for the notes of the Unit 12 - Mini project (Design & Development of Data and Application ) for following in the subject of Database Management Systems Lab

In the field of manufacturing, Material Requirement Planning (MRP) is a crucial task that helps in ensuring the availability of necessary materials for production. MRP is a computer-based inventory management system that helps in managing the flow of materials within a manufacturing organization. 

The process of Material Requirement Processing involves the following steps:

1. Bill of Materials (BOM) Creation: A Bill of Materials is a comprehensive list of all the raw materials, components, and sub-assemblies required to manufacture a product. The BOM is created by analyzing the product design and identifying the required components and raw materials.

2. Inventory Check: Once the BOM is created, the inventory levels of the required materials are checked. The MRP system checks the inventory levels of all the components required for the production process.

3. Material Requirement Calculation: The MRP system calculates the material requirements based on the production schedule and the inventory levels of the raw materials and components. The system calculates the exact quantity of each component required to manufacture the product.

4. Purchase Order Creation: Once the material requirements are calculated, purchase orders are created for the required raw materials and components. The purchase orders are sent to the suppliers to ensure the timely delivery of the materials.

5. Material Receipt: After the materials are delivered, the MRP system updates the inventory levels and the production schedule.

Material Requirement Processing is a crucial task in the manufacturing process as it helps in ensuring the availability of necessary materials for production. The MRP system helps in optimizing inventory levels, reducing material wastage, and improving production efficiency. With the use of MRP, manufacturing organizations can ensure that they have the required materials at the right time and in the right quantity to meet the production demands.
