### Market Based Modelling

- Market based modelling is a technique of data analytics that aims to understand the behavior and preferences of customers based on their transactions in a market.
- Market based modelling can help marketers to identify the patterns of customer purchases, such as which items are frequently bought together, which items are substitutes or complements, and which items are more or less popular.
- Market based modelling can also help marketers to segment customers into different groups based on their purchase history, and to design personalized marketing strategies for each group, such as product recommendations, cross-selling, up-selling, and loyalty programs.
- Market based modelling can be performed using various methods, such as association rules, market basket analysis, clustering, and collaborative filtering. Each method has its own advantages and limitations, and can be applied to different types of data and business problems.
- Market based modelling requires careful data preparation, such as data cleaning, data transformation, data reduction, and data discretization, to ensure the quality and validity of the analysis results.
- Market based modelling also requires appropriate evaluation and interpretation of the results, such as using metrics, visualization, and domain knowledge, to derive meaningful and actionable insights from the data.
