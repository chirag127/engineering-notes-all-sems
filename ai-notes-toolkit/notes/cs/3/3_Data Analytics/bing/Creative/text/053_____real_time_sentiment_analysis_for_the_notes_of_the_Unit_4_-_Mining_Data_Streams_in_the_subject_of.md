### Real Time Sentiment Analysis

- Real time sentiment analysis is a machine learning technique that automatically recognizes and extracts the sentiment in a text whenever it occurs .
- Sentiment is the subjective opinion or attitude expressed by a person towards a topic, such as a brand, product, service, event, or person.
- Sentiment analysis can be performed at different levels of granularity, such as document, sentence, phrase, or aspect level.
- Sentiment analysis can also be classified into different types, such as polarity (positive, negative, or neutral), emotion (anger, joy, sadness, etc.), or intensity (strong, weak, etc.).
- Real time sentiment analysis is useful for analyzing live social and online feeds, such as tweets, comments, reviews, posts, etc., to gain insights into the opinions and emotions of the audience  .
- Real time sentiment analysis can help businesses and organizations to monitor their brand reputation, customer satisfaction, market trends, competitor analysis, crisis management, and product feedback  .
- Real time sentiment analysis can also help individuals to understand their own or others' moods, preferences, interests, and motivations.
- Real time sentiment analysis can be performed using various methods, such as rule-based, lexicon-based, machine learning-based, or hybrid approaches.
- Rule-based methods use predefined rules and patterns to identify and score sentiment expressions.
- Lexicon-based methods use predefined dictionaries or lists of words and phrases with associated sentiment scores or polarities.
- Machine learning-based methods use supervised or unsupervised learning algorithms to train models on labeled or unlabeled data and then apply them to new data.
- Hybrid methods combine two or more of the above methods to improve the accuracy and robustness of sentiment analysis.
- Real time sentiment analysis faces several challenges, such as dealing with noisy, informal, and unstructured data, handling sarcasm, irony, and figurative language, detecting implicit and mixed sentiments, and adapting to different domains, languages, and cultures .
- Real time sentiment analysis requires efficient and scalable techniques to process large volumes of streaming data in a timely manner .
- Real time sentiment analysis can be implemented using various tools and platforms, such as Repustate, MonkeyLearn, Microsoft Dynamics 365, etc.  .
