### Discovery

Discovery is the first phase of the data analytics lifecycle, which defines the roadmap of how data is generated, collected, processed, used, and analyzed to achieve business goals. In this phase, the following tasks are performed:

- Understanding the business issue: This involves identifying the problem or opportunity that data analytics can address, defining the objectives and scope of the project, and establishing the success criteria and metrics.
- Understanding the data set: This involves collecting the relevant data sources, assessing the quality and availability of the data, and exploring the data characteristics and relationships.
- Preparing the data: This involves cleaning, transforming, integrating, and formatting the data to make it suitable for analysis, as well as creating subsets or samples of the data if needed.

The discovery phase is crucial for setting the foundation and direction of the data analytics project, as it helps to clarify the business needs, data requirements, and expected outcomes. It also helps to avoid potential pitfalls and challenges that may arise later in the project. The discovery phase may require multiple iterations and feedback from stakeholders to ensure alignment and agreement on the project scope and plan.
