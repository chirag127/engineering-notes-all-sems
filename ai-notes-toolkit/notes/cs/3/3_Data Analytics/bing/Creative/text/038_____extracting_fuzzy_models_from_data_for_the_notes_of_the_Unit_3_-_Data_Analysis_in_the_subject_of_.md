### Extracting fuzzy models from data

- Fuzzy models are mathematical means of representing vagueness and imprecise information (hence the term fuzzy) .
- Fuzzy models have the capability of recognising, representing, manipulating, interpreting, and using data and information that are vague and lack certainty .
- Fuzzy models are useful for dealing with data and information that are typical of Big Data, such as uncertainty, incompleteness, inconsistency, and scalability .
- Fuzzy models can be classified into two main types: fuzzy rule-based systems and fuzzy clustering algorithms .
- Fuzzy rule-based systems are composed of a set of fuzzy rules that describe the input-output relationship of a system using linguistic terms .
- Fuzzy clustering algorithms are used to partition a data set into a number of fuzzy subsets, where each data point has a degree of membership to each subset .
- To extract fuzzy models from data, different methods and techniques can be applied, such as:
  - Fuzzy c-means (FCM) algorithm: a popular fuzzy clustering algorithm that minimizes an objective function based on the distance between data points and cluster centers .
  - Subtractive clustering algorithm: a fast and robust method for estimating the number and the initial locations of cluster centers for FCM .
  - Genetic algorithms: a population-based optimization technique that mimics the natural evolution process to find optimal or near-optimal solutions .
  - Neuro-fuzzy systems: a hybrid approach that combines neural networks and fuzzy logic to learn fuzzy rules from data .
  - Fuzzy association rule mining: a method for discovering interesting and useful patterns or associations from large data sets .
