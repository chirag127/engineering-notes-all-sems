### Analysis of Time Series

- A time series is a series of data points indexed or listed in time order.
- Time series analysis is the process of analyzing a time series to extract meaningful statistics and other characteristics of the data .
- Time series analysis can be useful to see how a given variable changes over time, and what factors influence it.
- Time series analysis can also be used to forecast future values based on past observations.
- Time series analysis can involve various methods and techniques, such as:
  - Descriptive analysis: summarizing and visualizing the main features of the data, such as trends, seasonality, cycles, outliers, etc.
  - Exploratory analysis: testing hypotheses and identifying patterns or relationships in the data, such as autocorrelation, stationarity, cointegration, etc.
  - Inferential analysis: estimating parameters and confidence intervals, testing significance, and comparing models or groups of data.
  - Predictive analysis: building and evaluating models that can forecast future values, such as regression, ARIMA, exponential smoothing, neural networks, etc.
  - Prescriptive analysis: optimizing decisions or actions based on the forecasts, such as optimal inventory management, pricing, scheduling, etc.
