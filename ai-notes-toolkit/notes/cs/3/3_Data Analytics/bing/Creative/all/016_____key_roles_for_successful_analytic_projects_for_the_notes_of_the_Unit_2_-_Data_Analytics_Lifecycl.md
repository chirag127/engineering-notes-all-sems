### Key roles for successful analytic projects

- **Business analyst**: This role is responsible for understanding the business problem, defining the objectives and scope of the project, identifying the data sources and requirements, and communicating with the stakeholders and the project team.
- **Data engineer**: This role is responsible for collecting, cleaning, transforming, and storing the data from various sources, ensuring the data quality and integrity, and creating data pipelines and workflows for the project.
- **Data scientist**: This role is responsible for exploring, analyzing, and modeling the data, applying appropriate statistical and machine learning techniques, generating insights and recommendations, and presenting the results and findings to the stakeholders and the project team.
- **Data visualization expert**: This role is responsible for designing and creating effective and engaging data visualizations, dashboards, and reports, using appropriate tools and platforms, and ensuring the data story is clear and compelling.
- **Project manager**: This role is responsible for planning, coordinating, and managing the project activities, resources, budget, and timeline, ensuring the project deliverables are met, and resolving any issues or risks that may arise during the project.
