# Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

## What is Data Analytics?

- Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to generate insights, make decisions, and solve problems.
- Data analytics can be applied to various domains, such as business, science, engineering, health, education, and social sciences.
- Data analytics can be performed using different methods, tools, and techniques, such as statistics, machine learning, data mining, data visualization, and data storytelling.

## Why is Data Analytics important?

- Data analytics is important because it can help individuals and organizations to:
  - Understand the past, present, and future trends and patterns in data
  - Identify the causes and effects of phenomena and events
  - Discover hidden and unknown information and knowledge from data
  - Optimize the performance and efficiency of processes and systems
  - Enhance the quality and reliability of products and services
  - Reduce the risks and uncertainties of decision making
  - Create value and competitive advantage in the market

## What are the types of Data Analytics?

- Data analytics can be classified into four types, based on the purpose and complexity of the analysis:
  - Descriptive analytics: It describes what has happened or is happening in the data, using summary statistics, charts, and dashboards.
  - Diagnostic analytics: It explains why something has happened or is happening in the data, using correlation, regression, and hypothesis testing.
  - Predictive analytics: It predicts what will happen or is likely to happen in the data, using forecasting, classification, and clustering.
  - Prescriptive analytics: It prescribes what should be done or can be done in the data, using optimization, simulation, and recommendation.

## What are the steps of Data Analytics?

- Data analytics can be performed following a systematic and iterative process, which consists of six steps:
  - Define the problem or question: It involves identifying and clarifying the objective and scope of the analysis, and the expected outcomes and deliverables.
  - Collect the data: It involves acquiring and accessing the relevant and reliable data sources, and ensuring the data quality and security.
  - Explore the data: It involves inspecting and understanding the data characteristics, structure, and distribution, and performing data cleaning and transformation.
  - Analyze the data: It involves applying the appropriate data analytics methods, tools, and techniques, and generating the data outputs and results.
  - Interpret the data: It involves evaluating and validating the data outputs and results, and deriving the data insights and conclusions.
  - Communicate the data: It involves presenting and reporting the data outputs, results, insights, and conclusions, using data visualization and storytelling.
