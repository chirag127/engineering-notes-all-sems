# Regression modeling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of Data Analytics

- Regression modeling is an **investment analysis tool** used by investors to compare two or more stock variables.
- Regression modeling is also a **statistical technique** that relates a dependent variable to one or more independent variables.
- Regression modeling provides a **function** that describes the relationship between the variables .
- Regression modeling helps to **predict** the effects of the independent variables on the dependent variable .
- Regression modeling also helps to **estimate** the relationships between the variables and test the **significance** of the associations .
- Regression modeling can be **linear** or **nonlinear**, depending on the shape of the function .
- Regression modeling can also be **simple** or **multiple**, depending on the number of independent variables .
- Regression modeling requires some **assumptions** to be met, such as normality, homoscedasticity, independence, and linearity .
- Regression modeling can be performed using various **software** tools, such as Excel, R, Python, SPSS, etc .
- Regression modeling can be applied to various **fields** and **problems**, such as economics, finance, marketing, engineering, biology, etc  .
