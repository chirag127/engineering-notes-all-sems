### Analysis vs Reporting

- Reporting is the process of presenting data in a structured and summarized way, such as tables, charts, dashboards, etc.
- Analysis is the process of exploring, interpreting, and explaining data, such as finding patterns, trends, outliers, correlations, etc.
- Reporting answers the question of **what** happened, while analysis answers the question of **why** and **how** it happened.
- Reporting is usually based on predefined metrics and dimensions, while analysis is more flexible and creative.
- Reporting is often used for monitoring and tracking performance, while analysis is used for generating insights and recommendations.
- Reporting and analysis are both important for data-driven decision making, but they have different purposes and audiences.
