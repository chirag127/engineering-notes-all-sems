### Data Preparation for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the Subject of Data Analytics

Data preparation is the process of transforming raw data into a suitable format for analysis. It involves various tasks such as accessing, loading, structuring, cleansing, unifying, and adjusting data to ensure its quality, consistency, and relevance. Data preparation is an essential stage in the data analytics lifecycle, which is a systematic approach to conducting a data analytics project. The data analytics lifecycle consists of six phases, based on the CRISP-DM methodology  :

- **Business understanding**: This phase involves defining the business problem, objectives, and requirements of the project, as well as identifying the stakeholders and their expectations.
- **Data understanding**: This phase involves collecting, exploring, and describing the available data sources, as well as assessing their quality, quantity, and suitability for the analysis.
- **Data preparation**: This phase involves performing the tasks mentioned above to transform the raw data into an analytical sandbox, which is a scalable platform that data analysts and data scientists use to process data  .
- **Modeling**: This phase involves applying various analytical techniques and algorithms to the prepared data to discover patterns, insights, and relationships that can address the business problem or objective.
- **Evaluation**: This phase involves assessing the validity, reliability, and usefulness of the analytical results, as well as comparing them with the business requirements and expectations.
- **Deployment**: This phase involves communicating and presenting the analytical results to the stakeholders, as well as implementing and monitoring the solutions or actions derived from the analysis.

Data preparation is a critical and time-consuming phase in the data analytics lifecycle, as it can affect the quality and accuracy of the subsequent analysis and results. Therefore, data analysts and data scientists should follow some best practices for data preparation, such as   :

- **Understanding the business context and the data sources**: Data preparation should be aligned with the business problem and objectives, as well as the data understanding phase. Data analysts and data scientists should know the purpose, origin, and characteristics of the data sources, as well as their limitations and constraints.
- **Using appropriate tools and techniques**: Data preparation can involve various tools and techniques, such as data extraction, transformation, and loading (ETL) tools, data integration tools, data quality tools, data wrangling tools, data visualization tools, and scripting languages. Data analysts and data scientists should choose the tools and techniques that best suit their data preparation needs and objectives, as well as their skills and preferences.
- **Documenting and validating the data preparation process**: Data preparation should be documented and validated to ensure its transparency, reproducibility, and reliability. Data analysts and data scientists should keep track of the data preparation steps, decisions, and assumptions, as well as the data sources, transformations, and outputs. They should also verify and test the data preparation process and results, as well as handle any errors, anomalies, or inconsistencies in the data.
- **Iterating and refining the data preparation process**: Data preparation is not a linear or one-time process, but rather an iterative and dynamic process that can change and evolve according to the data analytics lifecycle. Data analysts and data scientists should revisit and refine the data preparation process and results as they gain more insights and feedback from the data understanding, modeling, and evaluation phases. They should also adapt the data preparation process and results to the changing business needs and expectations.
