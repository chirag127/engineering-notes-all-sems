### Key roles for successful analytic projects

- A successful analytic project requires a team of people with different skills and responsibilities to work together towards a common goal.
- Some of the key roles for an analytic project are:

  - **Business sponsor**: The person who initiates and funds the project, defines the business problem and the expected outcomes, and provides the domain knowledge and data sources.
  - **Project manager**: The person who plans, coordinates, and monitors the project activities, manages the resources and risks, and communicates with the stakeholders.
  - **Data analyst**: The person who performs the data analysis, applies the appropriate methods and tools, and generates the insights and recommendations.
  - **Data engineer**: The person who collects, cleans, transforms, and integrates the data from various sources, and ensures the data quality and availability.
  - **Data scientist**: The person who applies advanced techniques such as machine learning, statistics, and optimization to the data, and develops predictive or prescriptive models and solutions.
  - **Data steward**: The person who defines and enforces the data policies and standards, and ensures the data governance and compliance.
  - **Data consumer**: The person who uses the data and the analytic results for decision making, action taking, or value creation.

- Depending on the size and complexity of the project, some of these roles may be performed by the same person or by multiple people. The roles may also evolve or change over the course of the project.
- The key to a successful analytic project is to have a clear and shared understanding of the roles and responsibilities, and to have a regular and effective communication and collaboration among the team members.
