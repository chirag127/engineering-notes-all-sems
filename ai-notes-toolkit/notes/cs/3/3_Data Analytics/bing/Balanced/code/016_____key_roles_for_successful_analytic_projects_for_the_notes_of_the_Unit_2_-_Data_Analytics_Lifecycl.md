### Key roles for successful analytic projects

- **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also secures the funding and resources for the project and approves the final deliverables. The project sponsor is usually a senior executive or a business leader who has a stake in the outcome of the project .
- **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication and collaboration among the project team and other stakeholders. The project manager is usually a skilled professional who has experience in managing projects and leading teams .
- **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. The business user is usually a business analyst, a line manager, or a deep subject matter expert .
- **Business Intelligence Analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can analyze and visualize the data and provide insights and recommendations to the business users and the project team. This person can also design and develop dashboards and reports that can help monitor and evaluate the performance of the project. The business intelligence analyst is usually a proficient user of data analysis and visualization tools and has a good understanding of the business domain .
- **Database Administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can manage and maintain the data sources and databases that are used for the project. This person can also ensure the security, integrity, and availability of the data and provide technical support and troubleshooting to the project team. The database administrator is usually a certified professional who has expertise in database systems and technologies .
- **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can design and build the data pipelines and platforms that can collect, store, process, and deliver the data for the project. This person can also implement the data quality and governance standards and optimize the performance and scalability of the data systems. The data engineer is usually a skilled programmer who has knowledge of data engineering frameworks and tools .
- **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced analytical techniques and methods to the data and generate predictive and prescriptive models and solutions for the project. This person can also evaluate and validate the accuracy and effectiveness of the models and solutions and communicate the findings and implications to the project team and the business users. The data scientist is usually a highly qualified professional who has a strong background in mathematics, statistics, and computer science .

These are some of the key roles for successful analytic projects. However, depending on the size, scope, and complexity of the project, there may be other roles involved as well, such as data stewards, data analysts, data quality analysts, data governance officers, etc. The roles and responsibilities of each person may also vary and overlap depending on the skills and expertise of the project team members  .
