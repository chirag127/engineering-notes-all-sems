### Discovery

Discovery is the first phase of the data analytics lifecycle, which defines the roadmap of how data is generated, collected, processed, used, and analyzed to achieve business goals. In this phase, the following tasks are performed:

- Understanding the business issue: This involves identifying the problem or opportunity that data analytics can address, defining the objectives and scope of the project, and establishing the success criteria and metrics.
- Understanding the data set: This involves collecting the relevant data from various sources, such as databases, files, web, sensors, etc., and assessing the quality, quantity, and availability of the data.
- Preparing the data: This involves cleaning, transforming, integrating, and formatting the data to make it suitable for analysis, using tools such as SQL, Python, R, etc.
- Exploratory analysis: This involves performing descriptive and visual analysis of the data to understand its characteristics, patterns, trends, outliers, and relationships, using tools such as Excel, Tableau, Power BI, etc.

The discovery phase is crucial for setting the foundation for the subsequent phases of the data analytics lifecycle, such as modeling, validation, and deployment. It helps to gain insights into the data and the business problem, and to plan the appropriate analytical approach and techniques.
