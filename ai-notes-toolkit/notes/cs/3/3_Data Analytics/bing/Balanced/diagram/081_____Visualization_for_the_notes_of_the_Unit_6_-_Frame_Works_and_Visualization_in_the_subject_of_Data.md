### Visualization for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

- Visualization is the process of transforming data into graphical or pictorial representations that can communicate information effectively and efficiently to the audience.
- Visualization can help data analysts and decision-makers to explore, understand, and communicate data insights, patterns, trends, outliers, and relationships.
- Visualization can also enhance data storytelling, which is the art of using data, narrative, and visuals to convey a message or persuade an audience.
- To create effective and engaging visualizations, data analysts need to follow a systematic framework that involves the following steps:
  - Define the context, objectives, and audience of the visualization. What is the purpose, message, and tone of the visualization? Who are the intended viewers and what are their needs and expectations?
  - Choose the appropriate data sources and methods of data analysis. What data is relevant and reliable for the visualization? What types of data analysis are needed to answer the questions or support the arguments of the visualization?
  - Select the suitable visualization techniques and tools. What visualization techniques can best represent the data and the analysis results? What visualization tools can help create the desired visuals with ease and efficiency?
  - Design the layout and aesthetics of the visualization. How can the visualization elements be arranged and formatted to optimize clarity, readability, and attractiveness? How can the visualization style and color scheme match the message and the audience?
  - Evaluate and refine the visualization. How can the visualization be tested and validated for accuracy, relevance, and effectiveness? How can the visualization be improved based on feedback and iteration?
- Some of the common and powerful tools and frameworks for data analytics and visualization are:
  - Apache Kafka, a distributed streaming platform that enables high throughput, scalability, reliability, and fault-tolerance for real-time data processing and analysis.
  - Apache Spark, a unified analytics engine for batch, streaming, SQL, machine learning, and graph processing that can handle large-scale and complex data sets.
  - Apache Flink, a stream processing framework for unbounded and bounded data streams that can provide high performance, accuracy, and state management.
  - Tableau, a data visualization tool with drag-and-drop functionality that can connect to various data sources and create interactive and dynamic dashboards and reports.
  - Power BI, a business intelligence tool that can connect to data sources and create interactive reports and dashboards that can be shared and accessed on various devices.
