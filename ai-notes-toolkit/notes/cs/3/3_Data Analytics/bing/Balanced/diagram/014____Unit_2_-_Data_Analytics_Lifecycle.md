## Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

The data analytics lifecycle is a framework for conducting data analysis projects. It consists of six phases:

- **Discovery**: This phase involves identifying the business problem, defining the objectives and scope of the project, and formulating the initial hypotheses or questions to be answered by the data.
- **Data preparation**: This phase involves acquiring, cleaning, transforming, and integrating the data from various sources, as well as exploring and visualizing the data to gain insights and identify patterns or anomalies.
- **Model planning**: This phase involves selecting and applying appropriate analytical techniques and methods, such as statistics, machine learning, or optimization, to the data, and evaluating the performance and accuracy of the models.
- **Model building**: This phase involves implementing and deploying the models, as well as testing and validating them on new or unseen data, and refining or tuning them as needed.
- **Communicating results**: This phase involves presenting and communicating the findings and recommendations from the data analysis to the stakeholders, using clear and concise language, charts, graphs, or dashboards, and addressing any feedback or questions.
- **Operationalizing**: This phase involves integrating the data analysis into the business processes or systems, and monitoring and maintaining the models and results over time, as well as updating or revising them as the data or the business needs change.
