### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

According to the web sources     , some of the key roles for successful analytic projects are:

- **Project sponsor**: This is the person who initiates the project and provides the requirements and the core business problem. The project sponsor also secures the funding and resources for the project and approves the final deliverables. The project sponsor is usually a senior executive or a business leader who has a stake in the outcome of the project.
- **Project manager**: This is the person who oversees the execution of the project and ensures that the key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication and collaboration among the project team and the stakeholders. The project manager is usually a skilled professional who has experience in managing complex projects and dealing with risks and issues.
- **Business user**: This is the person who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results of the project. The business user can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. The business user is usually a business analyst, a line manager, or a deep subject matter expert who has a direct interest in the project.
- **Business intelligence analyst**: This is the person who collects, cleans, and transforms the data from various sources and prepares it for analysis. The business intelligence analyst also creates and maintains the data models, reports, dashboards, and visualizations that support the project. The business intelligence analyst is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of the data sources, the data quality, and the data tools.
- **Database administrator (DBA)**: This is the person who manages the data storage and access for the project. The DBA ensures that the data is secure, reliable, and scalable. The DBA also performs the data backup, recovery, and maintenance tasks. The DBA is usually a technical specialist who has knowledge of the database systems, the data architecture, and the data governance.
- **Data engineer**: This is the person who designs, builds, and tests the data pipelines and workflows that enable the data ingestion, processing, and delivery for the project. The data engineer also implements the data integration, transformation, and optimization techniques that improve the performance and quality of the data. The data engineer is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of the data engineering tools, the data formats, and the data standards.
- **Data scientist**: This is the person who applies the advanced analytical methods and techniques to the data and generates the insights and solutions for the project. The data scientist also validates, interprets, and communicates the results and recommendations to the project team and the stakeholders. The data scientist is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of the data science tools, the data analysis methods, and the data storytelling skills.
