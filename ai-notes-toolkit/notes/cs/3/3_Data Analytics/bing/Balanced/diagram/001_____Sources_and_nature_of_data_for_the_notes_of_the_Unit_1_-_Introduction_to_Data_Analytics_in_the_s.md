### Sources and nature of data

- Data is any type of information that can be measured, accessed or used in any way in a given study.
- Data sources are the physical or digital locations where data is stored in a data table, data object, or some other storage format.
- Data sources can be classified into two types: statistical and non-statistical.
  - Statistical sources refer to data that is gathered for some official purposes, such as censuses, surveys, and administrative records.
  - Non-statistical sources refer to data that is collected for other administrative purposes or for the private sector, such as business transactions, social media, web analytics, and sensors.
- Data sources can also be classified into two types based on the origin of data: primary and secondary .
  - Primary sources refer to data that is collected directly by the researcher or analyst for the specific purpose of the study .
  - Secondary sources refer to data that is collected by someone else for a different purpose and reused by the researcher or analyst for the study .
- The nature of data refers to the characteristics or attributes of data, such as its type, format, quality, and validity.
  - Data types can be quantitative or qualitative, depending on whether they are numerical or non-numerical.
  - Data formats can be structured or unstructured, depending on whether they are organized in a predefined way or not.
  - Data quality can be measured by its accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, and relevance.
  - Data validity can be assessed by its reliability, objectivity, and generalizability.
- The choice of data sources and the nature of data depend on the research question, the availability of data, the cost and time of data collection, and the ethical and legal issues involved in data access and use .
