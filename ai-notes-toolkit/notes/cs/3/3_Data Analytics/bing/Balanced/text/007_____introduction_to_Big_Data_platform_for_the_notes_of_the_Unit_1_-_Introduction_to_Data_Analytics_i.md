### Introduction to Big Data Platform

- A big data platform is a structured data storage system for massive volumes of information .
- A big data platform combines software and hardware solutions for data management .
- A big data platform typically stores aggregated data sets in the cloud .
- A big data platform enables organizations to develop, deploy, operate and manage a big data infrastructure or environment.
- A big data platform helps with the volume, speed, and complexity or variety of the data collected.
- A big data platform works to wrangle the amount of information, storing it in a manner that is organized and understandable enough to extract useful insights.
