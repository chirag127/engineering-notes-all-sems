### Filtering Streams

- Filtering streams is the process of selecting a subset of data from a continuous stream of events based on some criteria or rules  .
- Filtering streams can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Reducing the volume or complexity of data for analysis or storage .
  - Focusing on specific segments or groups of interest .
  - Removing erroneous or unwanted data .
  - Enhancing the quality or accuracy of data.
- Filtering streams can be done in different ways, such as:
  - Applying logical or mathematical expressions to filter data based on values, ranges, or patterns  .
  - Using geospatial functions to filter data based on location, distance, or region.
  - Using temporal functions to filter data based on time, window, or interval.
  - Using reference data to filter data based on external or static information.
- Filtering streams can have some challenges or limitations, such as:
  - Filtering streams may affect the completeness or representativeness of data .
  - Filtering streams may introduce bias or error in the analysis or results .
  - Filtering streams may require careful design and testing of the filtering criteria or rules .
  - Filtering streams may depend on the availability and quality of the data source or stream.
