### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

According to the Data Analytics Lifecycle, there are several key roles that are essential for a successful analytic project. These roles are:

- **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also approves the budget, resources, and timeline for the project. The project sponsor is usually a senior executive or a business leader who has the authority and influence to drive the project forward.  

- **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication, collaboration, and integration among the project team and other stakeholders. The project manager is usually a skilled professional who has experience in managing complex and cross-functional projects.  

- **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. A business user is usually a business analyst, a line manager, or a deep subject matter expert.  

- **Business Intelligence Analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can collect, analyze, and report on the data and metrics related to the project. This person can also design and develop dashboards, visualizations, and reports that can communicate the insights and recommendations from the project. A business intelligence analyst is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of data sources, tools, and methods.  

- **Database Administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can manage, maintain, and optimize the data infrastructure and systems that support the project. This person can also ensure the security, availability, and quality of the data. A database administrator is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of database design, administration, and performance tuning.  

- **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can design, build, and test the data pipelines and workflows that enable the ingestion, transformation, and integration of data from various sources and formats. This person can also implement the data governance and quality standards and policies for the project. A data engineer is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of data engineering tools, frameworks, and languages.  

- **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced analytical techniques and methods to discover, interpret, and communicate the patterns, trends, and insights from the data. This person can also develop and deploy predictive models, algorithms, and solutions that can address the business problem and objectives. A data scientist is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of data science tools, methods, and languages.  

These roles may vary depending on the size, scope, and complexity of the project. However, they are all important for ensuring the success of the project and delivering value to the organization.
