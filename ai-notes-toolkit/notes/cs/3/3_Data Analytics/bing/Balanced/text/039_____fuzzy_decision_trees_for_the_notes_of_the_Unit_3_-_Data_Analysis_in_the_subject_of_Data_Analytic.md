### Fuzzy Decision Trees

- Fuzzy decision trees are a type of decision trees that use fuzzy sets and fuzzy logic to handle uncertainty and ambiguity in data  .
- Fuzzy decision trees can deal with both numerical and categorical data, and can tolerate imprecise, conflicting, and missing information .
- Fuzzy decision trees can represent classification knowledge more naturally to the way of human thinking, and can provide more flexible and interpretable results  .
- Fuzzy decision trees can be induced from data using various algorithms, such as Fuzzy ID-3, Fuzzy C4.5, Fuzzy CART, etc.  .
- Fuzzy decision trees can also be used for three-way classification, where some instances are classified as non-commitment cases if their belongingness is ambiguous .
