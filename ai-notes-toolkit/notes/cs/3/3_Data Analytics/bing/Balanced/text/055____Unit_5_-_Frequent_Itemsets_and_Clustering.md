## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

- This unit covers two important topics in data mining: finding frequent itemsets and clustering data points.
- Frequent itemsets are sets of items that occur together frequently in a transactional database, such as a supermarket or an online store. They can reveal interesting patterns and associations among the items, such as market basket analysis or recommendation systems.
- Clustering is the process of grouping data points into clusters based on their similarity or distance. Clustering can help discover the underlying structure and patterns of the data, such as customer segmentation or image compression.
- The main objectives of this unit are:

  - To understand the concepts and applications of frequent itemsets and association rules.
  - To learn the algorithms for finding frequent itemsets, such as Apriori and FP-Growth.
  - To evaluate the quality and usefulness of association rules using measures such as support, confidence, and lift.
  - To understand the concepts and applications of clustering and cluster analysis.
  - To learn the algorithms for clustering data points, such as K-Means and Hierarchical Clustering.
  - To evaluate the quality and validity of clusters using measures such as SSE, silhouette coefficient, and dendrogram.

- The main topics covered in this unit are:

  - Frequent Itemsets and Association Rules
    - Definition and examples of frequent itemsets and association rules.
    - Algorithms for finding frequent itemsets, such as Apriori and FP-Growth.
    - Measures for evaluating association rules, such as support, confidence, and lift.
    - Applications and challenges of frequent itemset mining and association rule mining.
  - Clustering and Cluster Analysis
    - Definition and examples of clustering and cluster analysis.
    - Algorithms for clustering data points, such as K-Means and Hierarchical Clustering.
    - Measures for evaluating clusters, such as SSE, silhouette coefficient, and dendrogram.
