### Operationalization

- Operationalization is the final phase of the data analytics lifecycle, where the insights and models derived from the previous phases are deployed and integrated into the business processes and systems.
- Operationalization aims to deliver the expected business value and outcomes from the analytics projects, such as improved decision making, enhanced customer experience, increased revenue, reduced costs, or optimized operations.
- Operationalization involves the following steps:
  - **Model deployment**: This is the process of moving the analytics and data science models from the development environment to the production environment, where they can be accessed and used by the end users or applications. Model deployment can be done in various ways, such as embedding the models into existing systems, creating standalone applications or dashboards, or exposing the models as APIs or web services.
  - **Model management**: This is the process of monitoring and maintaining the performance and quality of the deployed models, as well as updating and retraining them as needed. Model management can involve tasks such as validating the model outputs, tracking the model usage and feedback, assessing the model impact and value, and applying the model governance and security policies.
  - **Model refinement**: This is the process of improving and enhancing the deployed models based on the feedback and results from the model management. Model refinement can involve tasks such as collecting new or additional data, applying new or advanced techniques, tuning the model parameters, or testing new or alternative models.
- Operationalization requires a holistic and iterative approach that involves collaboration and coordination among various stakeholders, such as data scientists, business analysts, IT professionals, and domain experts. Operationalization also requires a supportive and agile culture and infrastructure that enables fast and flexible deployment and adaptation of the analytics and data science models.
