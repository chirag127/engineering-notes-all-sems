# Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

- A successful analytic project requires a team of people with different skills and responsibilities to work together towards a common goal.
- The key roles for a successful analytic project are  :

  - **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also secures the budget, resources, and stakeholder support for the project. The project sponsor is usually a senior executive or a business leader who has the authority and influence to drive the project forward.
  - **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager coordinates the project team, communicates with the project sponsor and other stakeholders, manages the project risks and issues, and monitors the project progress and performance. The project manager is usually a skilled professional with experience in project management methodologies and tools.
  - **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. The business user is usually a business analyst, a line manager, or a deep subject matter expert who can provide feedback and validation to the project team.
  - **Business Intelligence Analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can collect, analyze, and visualize data to provide insights and recommendations to the project team and the business user. This person can also design and develop dashboards, reports, and other data products to communicate the results and enable data-driven decision making. The business intelligence analyst is usually a skilled professional with knowledge of data sources, data quality, data analysis techniques, and data visualization tools.
  - **Database Administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can manage, maintain, and optimize the data storage and access for the project. This person can also ensure the data security, integrity, and availability for the project. The database administrator is usually a technical expert with knowledge of database systems, data modeling, data warehousing, and data governance.
  - **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can design, build, and test the data pipelines and infrastructure for the project. This person can also integrate, transform, and prepare the data for analysis and modeling. The data engineer is usually a technical expert with knowledge of data engineering tools, frameworks, and languages.
  - **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced analytical methods and techniques to discover patterns, trends, and insights from the data. This person can also develop, validate, and deploy predictive models and algorithms to solve the business problem and provide actionable solutions. The data scientist is usually a technical expert with knowledge of data science tools, frameworks, and languages, as well as statistics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.

- These roles are not mutually exclusive and may overlap or vary depending on the size, scope, and complexity of the project. Some roles may be performed by the same person or by a group of people. The project team should have a clear understanding of the roles and responsibilities of each member and collaborate effectively to achieve the project goals.
