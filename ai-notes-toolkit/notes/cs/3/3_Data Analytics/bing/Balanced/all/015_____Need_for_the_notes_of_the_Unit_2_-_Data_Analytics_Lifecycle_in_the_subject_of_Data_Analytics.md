# Need for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics

- Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to generate insights and support decision making.
- Data analytics lifecycle is a framework that describes the stages and activities involved in a data analytics project, from defining the business problem to deploying the solution and evaluating the results.
- The notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics are important for the following reasons:

  - They provide a comprehensive overview of the data analytics lifecycle and its components, such as discovery, data preparation, model planning, model building, communication, and operationalization.
  - They explain the key concepts, methods, tools, and best practices for each stage of the data analytics lifecycle, such as data sources, data quality, data exploration, data transformation, data modeling, data visualization, data storytelling, and data governance.
  - They illustrate the data analytics lifecycle with real-world examples and case studies from various domains and industries, such as retail, healthcare, finance, education, and social media.
  - They help the students to understand the objectives, challenges, and outcomes of each stage of the data analytics lifecycle, and how they relate to the business problem and the stakeholders.
  - They enable the students to apply the data analytics lifecycle to their own projects and assignments, and to evaluate the effectiveness and impact of their solutions.
