### CLIQUE and ProCLUS

#### CLIQUE
- CLIQUE (CLustering In QUEst) is an algorithm for subspace clustering in high dimensional data.
- It was proposed by Agrawal et al. in 1998.
- CLIQUE partitions the data space into non-overlapping rectangular units and identifies dense units.
- The dense units are then merged to form clusters.
- CLIQUE can automatically determine the number of clusters and can handle noise in the data.

#### ProCLUS
- ProCLUS (PROjected CLUStering) is an algorithm for subspace clustering in high dimensional data.
- It was proposed by Aggarwal et al. in 1999.
- ProCLUS is based on the idea of projected clustering, where clusters exist in subspaces of the data space.
- ProCLUS first identifies a set of cluster representatives and then assigns points to the nearest representative.
- The algorithm then refines the clusters by iteratively reassigning points and updating the representatives.
