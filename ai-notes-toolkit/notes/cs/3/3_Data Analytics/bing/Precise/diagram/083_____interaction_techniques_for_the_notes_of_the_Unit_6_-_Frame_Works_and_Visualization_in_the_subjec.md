### Interaction Techniques for Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualization in Data Analytics

Interaction techniques are methods that allow users to interact with data visualizations in order to gain insights and make decisions. Some common interaction techniques include:

1. **Brushing and Linking**: This technique allows users to highlight a subset of data in one visualization, and see the corresponding data highlighted in other related visualizations.

2. **Filtering**: This technique allows users to focus on a specific subset of data by filtering out data points that do not meet certain criteria.

3. **Zooming and Panning**: This technique allows users to zoom in on a specific area of a visualization to see more detail, or to pan around the visualization to see different parts of the data.

4. **Coordinated Views**: This technique involves displaying multiple visualizations of the same data set, with interactions in one visualization affecting the others.

5. **Details on Demand**: This technique allows users to see more detailed information about a specific data point by hovering over it or clicking on it.

These are just a few examples of the many interaction techniques that can be used in data visualization. The choice of technique will depend on the specific needs and goals of the user and the data being visualized.
