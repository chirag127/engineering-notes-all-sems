### Market Based Modelling

- Market based model assumes that there are two kinds of entities: items and baskets. There is a many-many relationship between items and baskets.
- The native application of the market-basket model was the analysis of true market baskets. That is supermarkets and chain stores documents the contents of every market basket brought to the register for checking.
- Frequent item sets and association rules can be used for a variety of tasks such as market basket analysis, cross-selling and recommendation systems.
- Finding frequent patterns plays a crucial role in mining associations, correlations, and many other innovative relationships among data. Furthermore, it helps in data classification, clustering and other data mining jobs.
- However, it should be noted that association rule mining can generate a large number of rules, many of which may be irrelevant or uninteresting.
