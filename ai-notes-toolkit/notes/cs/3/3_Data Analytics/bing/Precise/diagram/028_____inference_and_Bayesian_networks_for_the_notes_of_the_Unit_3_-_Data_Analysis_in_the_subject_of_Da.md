### Unit 3 - Data Analysis: Inference and Bayesian Networks

Inference is the process of drawing conclusions from data and evidence. In the context of data analysis, it refers to the use of statistical methods to make predictions or decisions based on data.

Bayesian networks are a type of graphical model that represents the probabilistic relationships between variables. They are used to model complex systems and make predictions based on observed data.

Some key points to remember about inference and Bayesian networks include:

- Inference can be used to make predictions or decisions based on data.
- Bayesian networks represent the probabilistic relationships between variables.
- Bayesian networks can be used to model complex systems and make predictions based on observed data.
- Bayesian methods can be used to update probabilities based on new evidence.

These are some of the key concepts to understand when studying inference and Bayesian networks in the context of data analysis. It is important to have a strong understanding of these concepts in order to effectively analyze and interpret data.
