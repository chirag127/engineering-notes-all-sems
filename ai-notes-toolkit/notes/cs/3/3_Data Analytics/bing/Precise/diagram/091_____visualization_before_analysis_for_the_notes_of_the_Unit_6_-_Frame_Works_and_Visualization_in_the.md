### Visualization before Analysis

Visualization is an important step in the data analysis process. It involves creating graphical representations of data to help identify patterns, trends, and relationships that might not be apparent from the raw data alone. Here are some key points to consider when using visualization before analysis:

1. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Visualization can be used as a tool for exploratory data analysis, allowing analysts to quickly identify patterns and relationships in the data. This can help guide the selection of appropriate statistical methods for further analysis.

2. **Data Cleaning:** Visualization can also be used to identify errors or inconsistencies in the data, which can then be addressed before proceeding with further analysis.

3. **Communicating Results:** Visualizations can be an effective way to communicate the results of data analysis to a non-technical audience. They can help make complex data more accessible and understandable.

4. **Choosing the Right Visualization:** It is important to choose the right type of visualization for the data and the question being addressed. Different types of visualizations are suited to different types of data and analysis.

In summary, visualization is a powerful tool that can help analysts better understand their data and communicate their findings. It is an important step in the data analysis process and should be used before proceeding with further analysis.
