### Market-Based Modelling

Market-based modelling is a technique used in data analytics to analyze customer transaction data and identify patterns and relationships between items. This technique is commonly used in the field of market basket analysis, which is the study of customer purchasing behavior.

Here are some key points to remember about market-based modelling:

1. Market-based modelling is used to identify associations between items in customer transaction data.
2. This technique is commonly used in market basket analysis, which is the study of customer purchasing behavior.
3. Market-based modelling can help businesses understand the relationships between items and make better decisions about product placement, promotions, and pricing.
4. Frequent itemset mining is a common technique used in market-based modelling to identify sets of items that frequently appear together in transactions.
5. Clustering can also be used in market-based modelling to group similar items or customers together.

These are some of the key concepts to remember when studying market-based modelling in the context of frequent itemsets and clustering in data analytics. It is important to understand these concepts in order to effectively analyze customer transaction data and make informed business decisions.
