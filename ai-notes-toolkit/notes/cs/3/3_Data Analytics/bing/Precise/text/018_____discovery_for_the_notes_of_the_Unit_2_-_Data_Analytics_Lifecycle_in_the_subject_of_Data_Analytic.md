### Discovery for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics

1. The discovery phase is the first stage of the data analytics lifecycle.
2. During this phase, the analyst works with the business stakeholders to identify the business requirements and objectives.
3. The analyst also assesses the current state of the data and determines whether additional data is needed.
4. The discovery phase is crucial for defining the scope of the project and setting realistic expectations for the outcome.
5. The analyst may use techniques such as interviews, surveys, and data profiling to gather information during the discovery phase.
6. The outcome of the discovery phase is a clear understanding of the business problem and the data that is available to address it.
7. The discovery phase sets the foundation for the rest of the data analytics lifecycle, and its success is critical for the overall success of the project.
