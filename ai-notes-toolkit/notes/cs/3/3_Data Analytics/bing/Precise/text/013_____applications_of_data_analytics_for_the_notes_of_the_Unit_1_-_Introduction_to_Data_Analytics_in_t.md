### Applications of Data Analytics

Data analytics has a wide range of applications across various industries. Here are some of the key applications of data analytics:

1. **Fraud Detection**: By carefully analyzing customer spending trends, unusual patterns in their spending habits can be detected .
2. **Energy Management**: Power distributing companies can use data analytics to predict the amount of energy to be consumed around a specific time of the day .
3. **Digital Advertisement**: Targeted advertisement is a vital part of digital marketing. Data analytics can be used to improve the effectiveness of digital advertisements .
4. **Security**: Predictive analysis can help in reducing crime .
5. **Transportation**: Data analytics can be used to revolutionize transportation, especially in areas where transportation is a major issue .
6. **Risk Detection**: Data analytics can be used to detect risks in various industries .

These are just a few examples of the many applications of data analytics. Data analytics is used in every field, from online shopping and high-tech industries to government decision making, budgeting, and planning .
