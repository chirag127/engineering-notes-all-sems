### Introduction to R for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

- R is a programming language and software environment for statistical computing and graphics.
- It is widely used among statisticians and data scientists for developing statistical software and data analysis.
- R provides a wide variety of statistical and graphical techniques, including linear and nonlinear modeling, classical statistical tests, time-series analysis, classification, and clustering.
- R is freely available under the GNU General Public License, and its source code can be modified and distributed by anyone.
- R has an active and growing community of users and developers, with numerous packages available for download from the Comprehensive R Archive Network (CRAN).
- R is known for its powerful data visualization capabilities, with packages such as ggplot2 and lattice providing advanced plotting options.
- R integrates well with other data analysis tools, such as databases, spreadsheets, and web scraping tools, making it a versatile tool for data analysis.
- R has a steep learning curve, but its capabilities and community support make it a valuable tool for anyone working with data.
