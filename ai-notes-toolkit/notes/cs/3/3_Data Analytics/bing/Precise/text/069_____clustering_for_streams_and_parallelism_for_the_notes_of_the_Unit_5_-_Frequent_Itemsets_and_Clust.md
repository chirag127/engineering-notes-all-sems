### Clustering for Streams and Parallelism

- Clustering for streams and parallelism is a topic covered in Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in the subject of Data Analytics.
- Data stream clustering is defined as the clustering of data that arrive continuously such as telephone records, multimedia data, financial transactions etc .
- The objective of data stream clustering is, given a sequence of points, to construct a good clustering of the stream, using a small amount of memory and time .
- Clustering for streams and parallelism is studied as a streaming algorithm .
- Some of the methods used for clustering high dimensional data include CLIQUE and PROCLUS .
- Frequent pattern based clustering methods are also used .
- Clustering can also be done in non-euclidean space .
