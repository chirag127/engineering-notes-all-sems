### Estimating Moments for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the Subject of Data Analytics

1. Moments are statistical measures that describe the shape of a data distribution.
2. The first moment is the mean, which measures the central tendency of the data.
3. The second moment is the variance, which measures the spread of the data.
4. Higher-order moments, such as skewness and kurtosis, measure the asymmetry and peakedness of the data distribution, respectively.
5. In the context of data streams, it is often necessary to estimate moments in an online fashion, as the data is continuously arriving.
6. One common approach for estimating moments in data streams is the use of sketching algorithms, which provide approximate answers with provable guarantees on the accuracy of the estimates.
7. Examples of sketching algorithms for estimating moments include the AMS sketch, the Count-Min sketch, and the Count sketch.
8. These algorithms work by maintaining a compact summary of the data stream, which can be used to estimate the moments of the data distribution.
9. The accuracy of the estimates can be improved by increasing the size of the sketch, at the cost of increased memory usage and computational complexity.
10. Estimating moments in data streams is an important task in many applications, including anomaly detection, data compression, and query optimization.
