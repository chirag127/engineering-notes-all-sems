### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

#### Unstructured Data

- Unstructured data refers to data that does not have a predefined data model or is not organized in a pre-defined manner.
- It is often text-heavy, but may contain data such as dates, numbers, and facts as well.
- Examples of unstructured data include emails, social media posts, videos, audio files, and images.
- Unstructured data can be difficult to analyze and process due to its lack of structure.
- Techniques such as natural language processing, text analytics, and image recognition can be used to extract valuable information from unstructured data.
- The growth of unstructured data has led to the development of big data technologies and tools to store, manage, and analyze large volumes of unstructured data.
