### Analysis of Time Series

Time series analysis is a statistical technique that deals with time series data, or trend analysis. Time series data means that data is in a series of particular time periods or intervals. The data is considered in three types: Time series data, cross-sectional data, and pooled data. Here, we will be focusing on time series data.

#### Unit 3 - Data Analysis

1. **Trend Analysis:** Trend analysis is the practice of collecting information and attempting to spot a pattern, or trend, in the information. In some fields of study, the term "trend analysis" has more formally defined meanings.

2. **Seasonal Analysis:** Seasonal analysis is a statistical technique that is used to estimate the seasonal component of a time series. The seasonal component is the regular pattern of variation in a time series that is related to the calendar, such as the time of year or the day of the week.

3. **Cyclical Analysis:** Cyclical analysis is the study of business cycles, which are fluctuations in economic activity that repeat over time. Cyclical analysis is used to identify the timing and magnitude of business cycles, as well as to forecast future economic activity.

4. **Irregular Analysis:** Irregular analysis is the study of the irregular component of a time series. The irregular component is the part of the time series that cannot be explained by the trend, seasonal, or cyclical components. It is often considered to be random or unpredictable.

5. **Forecasting:** Forecasting is the process of making predictions of the future based on past and present data and most commonly by analysis of trends. A common example might be estimation of some variable of interest at some specified future date.

These are some of the key points to remember while studying the analysis of time series for Unit 3 - Data Analysis in the subject of Data Analytics. It is important to understand these concepts in depth to be able to apply them effectively in data analysis.
