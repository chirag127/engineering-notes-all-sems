### Stock Market Predictions

1. **Big data** is used in the forecasting process, particularly in the financial market, as forecasting is used in the stock market to create an automatic prediction of volatility in share prices.
2. The main purpose of forecasting by data mining in the stock market is to **discover knowledge** that can assist decision-makers.
3. The stock market contains rich, valuable and considerable data, and these data need careful analysis for good decisions to be made that can lead to increases in the efficiency of a business.
4. Data mining techniques offer data processing tools and applications used to enhance decision-maker decisions.
5. It is important that companies use data mining with utmost care to improve their business by increasing revenue and reducing costs.
6. Stock market data analysis needs the help of artificial intelligence and data mining techniques.
7. The volatility of stock prices depends on gains or losses of certain companies.
8. Precisely predicting stocks is essential for investors to gain enormous profits. However, the volatility of the market makes this kind of prediction highly difficult.
9. Data Mining and Machine Learning could be used to guide an investor’s decisions.
