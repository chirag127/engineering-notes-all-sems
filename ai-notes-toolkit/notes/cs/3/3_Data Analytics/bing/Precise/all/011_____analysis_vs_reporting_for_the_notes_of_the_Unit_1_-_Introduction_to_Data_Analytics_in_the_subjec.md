# Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

### Analysis vs Reporting

- **Reporting** refers to the process of organizing data into informative summaries to monitor how different areas of a business are performing.
- **Analysis**, on the other hand, is the process of exploring data and reports in order to extract meaningful insights, which can be used to better understand and improve business performance.
- Reporting is focused on what has happened, while analysis is focused on why it happened and what can be done to improve performance.
- Reporting is often done on a regular basis, such as daily, weekly, or monthly, while analysis is done on an as-needed basis to answer specific questions or solve problems.
- Reporting is often done using pre-defined templates and formats, while analysis requires more flexibility and creativity in how data is presented and explored.
- Reporting is often done by business analysts or data analysts, while analysis is often done by data scientists or business intelligence analysts.
- Reporting is often focused on providing information to decision-makers, while analysis is focused on providing insights and recommendations to decision-makers.
- Reporting is often done using business intelligence tools, while analysis may require more advanced tools such as statistical analysis software or data visualization tools.
