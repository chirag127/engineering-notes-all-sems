### Discovery for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of Data Analytics

1. The discovery phase is the first phase of the Data Analytics Lifecycle.
2. During this phase, the analyst works with the business stakeholders to identify the business requirements and the underlying data analytics problem.
3. The analyst also assesses the resources available, including data sources, tools, and personnel.
4. The discovery phase is crucial for defining the scope of the project and setting realistic expectations for the outcome.
5. The analyst may conduct exploratory data analysis to gain a better understanding of the data and identify potential opportunities for analysis.
6. The discovery phase sets the foundation for the rest of the Data Analytics Lifecycle and is essential for the success of the project.
