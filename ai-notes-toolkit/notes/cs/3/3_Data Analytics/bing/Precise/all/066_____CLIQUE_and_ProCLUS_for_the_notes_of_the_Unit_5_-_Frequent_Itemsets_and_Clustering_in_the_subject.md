# CLIQUE and ProCLUS

## CLIQUE

- CLIQUE (CLustering In QUEst) is an algorithm for subspace clustering in high dimensional data.
- It partitions the data space into non-overlapping rectangular units and identifies dense units.
- The dense units are then merged to form clusters.
- CLIQUE can automatically determine the number of clusters and can handle noise in the data.

## ProCLUS

- ProCLUS (PROjected CLUStering) is an algorithm for subspace clustering in high dimensional data.
- It is based on the idea of projected clustering, where clusters exist in subspaces of the data space.
- ProCLUS first identifies a set of cluster representatives and then finds the relevant subspaces for each cluster.
- It then assigns points to clusters based on their proximity to the cluster representatives in the relevant subspaces.
