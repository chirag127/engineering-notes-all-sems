# Fuzzy Decision Trees

Fuzzy decision trees are a type of decision tree that is used to represent classification knowledge in a way that is more natural to human thinking. They are more robust in tolerating imprecise, conflicting, and missing information  .

- Fuzzy decision trees are more efficient for treating learning data of mixed type, including both numerical and categorical data .
- The Fuzzy ID-3 algorithm is used to build the decision trees .
- Fuzzy decision trees are built on the basis of pre-constructed decision trees using conventional methods with the aim of maximizing the classification accuracy .
- Subsequently, the instance whose belongingness is ambiguous is identified and determined as a non-commitment case .
