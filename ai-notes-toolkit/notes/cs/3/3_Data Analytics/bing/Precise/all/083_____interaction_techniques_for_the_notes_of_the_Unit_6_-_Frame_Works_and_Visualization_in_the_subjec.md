### Interaction Techniques for Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualization in Data Analytics

Interaction techniques are essential for data entry and manipulation in data analytics. They place a greater emphasis on input than output, with output being used to convey affordances and provide user feedback .

Interaction with visualizations is necessary for exploration, personalization, and wider engagement with data visualizations. However, the specific means and their effectiveness for visualizations still cause considerable controversies and open research questions .

Interaction design is a fundamental characteristic of visualization that refers to the "interplay between a person and a data interface involving a data-related intent" .

There are four key steps in creating a world-class interactive data visualization:
1. Data integration: Collect raw data and turn it into clean, analytics-ready information by performing data replication, ingestion, and transformation. Then store it in a data lake or data warehouse.
2. Goal definition: Define the goals of the visualization and the questions it should answer.
3. Visualization design: Choose the appropriate visualization techniques and design the visualization.
4. Interaction design: Design the interaction techniques to allow the user to explore and engage with the data .

Interaction techniques can be categorized to help discuss and evaluate their effectiveness. These categories can act as a framework to help lay an initial foundation toward a deeper understanding and a science of interaction .
