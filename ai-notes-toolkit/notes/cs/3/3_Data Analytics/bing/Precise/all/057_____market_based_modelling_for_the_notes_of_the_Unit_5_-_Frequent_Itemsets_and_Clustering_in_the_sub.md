# Market Based Modelling

Market based modelling is a technique used in data analytics to identify patterns and relationships between items in large datasets. This technique is commonly used in market basket analysis, where the goal is to identify items that are frequently purchased together.

Here are some key points to remember about market based modelling:

1. Market based modelling is used to identify associations between items in large datasets.
2. This technique is commonly used in market basket analysis to identify items that are frequently purchased together.
3. Market based modelling can help businesses to better understand their customers' purchasing behavior and make informed decisions about product placement, promotions, and pricing.
4. Algorithms such as the Apriori algorithm and the FP-growth algorithm are commonly used in market based modelling to identify frequent itemsets and generate association rules.
5. Market based modelling can also be used in other domains, such as healthcare and finance, to identify patterns and relationships in data.
