
### Descriptive Statistics for the Notes of Unit 6 - Frameworks and Visualization in the Subject of Data Analytics

1. Descriptive statistics is the branch of mathematics used to summarize and analyze data sets. 
2. It is used to describe the main features of a data set, such as its mean, median, mode, range, interquartile range, variance, and standard deviation. 
3. Descriptive statistics can also be used to generate graphical representations of data, such as histograms, box-and-whisker plots, and scatter plots. 
4. Descriptive statistics can help to identify patterns in a data set and to make predictions about future data. 
5. In the context of frameworks and visualization, descriptive statistics can be used to create visualizations of data, such as bar charts, line graphs, and pie charts. 
6. These visualizations can help to identify trends in data and to make comparisons between different data sets. 
7. Descriptive statistics can also be used to create summary tables and to calculate correlations between different variables. 
8. In the context of data analytics, descriptive statistics can be used to analyze large amounts of data and to identify meaningful patterns and relationships between different variables.
