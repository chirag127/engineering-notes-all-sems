
### Bayesian Modeling for Unit 3 - Data Analysis in Data Analytics

- Bayesian modeling is an approach to data analysis that uses probability theory to make inferences about data. 
- It is based on the Bayes theorem, which states that the probability of an event occurring given some evidence is equal to the probability of the evidence given the event. 
- Bayesian modeling uses this theorem to make inferences about data by calculating the probability of an event occurring given the data. 
- The Bayesian approach is useful for making decisions based on uncertain or incomplete data, as it allows us to incorporate prior beliefs and assumptions into our analysis. 
- Bayesian modeling can be used to make predictions about future events, estimate the probability of a certain outcome, or identify patterns in data. 
- It can also be used to compare different models and find the most likely one. 
- Bayesian modeling is typically used in combination with other data analysis techniques, such as regression, clustering, and machine learning.
