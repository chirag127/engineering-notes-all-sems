
### Systems and Applications for Data Analytics

1. Frame Works: 
    * Frame works are collections of software libraries and programs that provide a structure for developing software applications.
    * They are designed to simplify the process of developing an application by providing reusable components and tools.
    * Common frame works for data analytics include Apache Spark, Hadoop, and TensorFlow.

2. Visualization: 
    * Visualization is the process of creating visual representations of data.
    * Visualization can help to identify patterns, trends, and correlations in data.
    * Common visualization techniques include charts, graphs, maps, and heat maps.
