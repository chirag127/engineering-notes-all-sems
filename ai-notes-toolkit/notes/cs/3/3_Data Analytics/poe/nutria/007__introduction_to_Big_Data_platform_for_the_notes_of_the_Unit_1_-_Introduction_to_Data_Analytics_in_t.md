
### Introduction to Big Data Platform 

1. Big Data is a term used to describe large and complex data sets that are difficult to process using traditional data processing applications. 
2. Big Data is characterized by its volume, variety, velocity, and veracity. 
3. Big Data is often used to solve complex problems by providing insights from data analysis. 
4. The primary goal of Big Data is to provide meaningful insights from large sets of data. 
5. Big Data platforms are designed to store, process, and analyze large amounts of data. 
6. Big Data platforms can be used for predictive analytics, machine learning, and other data-driven applications. 
7. Big Data platforms are becoming increasingly important for businesses and organizations as they strive to gain insights from their data. 
8. Big Data platforms can be used to identify trends, detect anomalies, and make better decisions.
