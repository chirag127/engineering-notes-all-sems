
### Model Planning for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. Modeling is an essential part of the data analytics lifecycle and involves the process of creating, testing, and refining models to achieve specific goals.
2. The modeling process can be divided into four main steps: data pre-processing, model selection, model training, and model evaluation.
3. Data pre-processing is the process of cleaning, transforming, and normalizing the data to make it suitable for model training.
4. Model selection is the process of choosing the most appropriate model for the task at hand. This step requires knowledge of the data and the goals of the model.
5. Model training is the process of training the model with the data and adjusting the model's parameters to achieve the desired performance.
6. Model evaluation is the process of assessing the model's performance on unseen data. This step is essential to ensure that the model is accurate and reliable.
7. The modeling process is iterative and requires careful planning and execution to ensure the best results.
