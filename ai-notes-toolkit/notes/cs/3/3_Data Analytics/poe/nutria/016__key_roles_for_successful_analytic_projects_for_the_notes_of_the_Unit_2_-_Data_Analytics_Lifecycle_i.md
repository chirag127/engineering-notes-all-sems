
### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects
1. Business Analyst: Responsible for understanding the business needs and developing a plan to meet those needs.
2. Data Analyst: Responsible for collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data.
3. Technical Analyst: Responsible for developing and deploying the technical infrastructure needed to support the analytics project.
4. Project Manager: Responsible for managing the project timeline, budget, and resources.
5. Quality Assurance: Responsible for ensuring the quality of the data and the analytics project.
6. Communication: Responsible for communicating the results of the analytics project to stakeholders.
