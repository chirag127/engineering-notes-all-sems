### Market Based Modelling

Market based modelling is a technique used in data analytics to identify patterns and relationships between various items in a market. This technique is particularly useful in identifying frequent itemsets and clustering.

Here are some key points to remember about market based modelling:

- Market based modelling involves analyzing market data to identify patterns and relationships between items.

- This technique is particularly useful in identifying frequent itemsets, which are sets of items that frequently occur together in the market.

- By identifying frequent itemsets, analysts can gain insights into customer behavior and preferences, which can be used to inform marketing strategies and product development.

- Market based modelling can also be used to identify clusters of items that are frequently purchased together. This can be useful in developing product bundles and promotions.

- To perform market based modelling, analysts typically use algorithms such as the Apriori algorithm and the FP-Growth algorithm.

- These algorithms work by analyzing the frequency of itemsets in the market data and identifying those that occur above a certain threshold.

- Once frequent itemsets have been identified, analysts can use them to create association rules, which describe the relationships between items.

- For example, an association rule might state that customers who purchase bread and milk are also likely to purchase eggs.

- Market based modelling is a powerful tool for identifying patterns and relationships in market data, and can be used to inform a wide range of business decisions.
