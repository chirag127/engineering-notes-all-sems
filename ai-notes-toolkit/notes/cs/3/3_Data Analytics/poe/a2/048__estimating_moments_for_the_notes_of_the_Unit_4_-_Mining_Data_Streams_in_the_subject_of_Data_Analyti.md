### Estimating Moments for the Notes of Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams

1. Mean - Calculate the average of the data values. It is the sum of all values divided by the number of values.
2. Variance - Measure of the spread of the data values around the mean. It is the sum of squared deviations from the mean divided by the number of values.
3. Standard Deviation - Square root of the variance. It is the spread of the data values around the mean.
4. Skewness - Measure of the asymmetry of the data around the mean. Positive skewness indicates data concentrated on the left of the mean. Negative skewness indicates data concentrated on the right of the mean.
5. Kurtosis - Measure of the peakedness of the data distribution. Positive kurtosis indicates a sharper peak and heavier tails. Negative kurtosis indicates a flatter peak and lighter tails.

The moments are estimated using approximate algorithms like reservoir sampling and sketching on the high-speed data streams due to the huge volume of data and single pass nature of the streams. The approximate results give insightful statistics on the data.
