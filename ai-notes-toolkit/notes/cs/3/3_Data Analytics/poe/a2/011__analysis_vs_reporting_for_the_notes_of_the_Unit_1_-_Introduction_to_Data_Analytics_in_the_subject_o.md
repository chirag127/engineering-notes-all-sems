### Analysis vs Reporting

- **Analysis:** The process of inspecting and interpreting data to discover insights and patterns. Analysis involves exploring the data, identifying trends and relationships, finding noteworthy observations or anomalies, and forming conclusions. The goal of analysis is to gain a deeper understanding of the data and what it means.
- **Reporting:** The process of organizing and communicating key insights and findings from data analysis to the appropriate stakeholders. Reporting allows analytical results to be shared with others. It involves summarizing the major takeaways, often highlighting trends, insights, and recommendations. The goal of reporting is to inform and enable data-driven decision making.

Analysis and reporting are related but distinct steps in the data analytics process. Analysis comes first and is focused on exploration and interpretation. Reporting comes second and is focused on communication and conveyance. Both analysis and reporting require technical skills to work with data as well as soft skills to understand business context and communicate results. For a data analytics project to create value, both thoughtful analysis and clear reporting are essential.
