### Analysis of Time Series for the Notes of Unit 3 - Data Analysis

1. Time Series - A time series is a set of data points arranged in chronological order. It is a sequence of observations of a variable at regular time intervals.
2. Components of Time Series - A time series can be decomposed into three components - Trend, Seasonal Variation and Random Variation.
- Trend - Long term increase or decrease in the time series. It can be linear, exponential or seasonal.
- Seasonal Variation - Fluctuations that occur regularly based on seasonal events or patterns. For example, sales of air conditioners increasing in summers.
- Random Variation - Unpredictable fluctuations in the time series due to random events.
3. Time Series Analysis - The analysis of time series data to understand the data, patterns and trends is known as time series analysis. It is used to forecast future values and find periodic patterns. Some techniques used are -
- Moving Averages - Calculating averages of subsets of the time series data
- Exponential Smoothing - Assigning weights to recent observations and averaging the series
- ARIMA Model - AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average model which uses past values to predict future values
- Regression Models - Fitting curves/lines to the time series data to understand trends and forecast
