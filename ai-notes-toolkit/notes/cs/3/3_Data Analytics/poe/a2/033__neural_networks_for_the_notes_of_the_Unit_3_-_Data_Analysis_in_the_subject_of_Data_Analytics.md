### Neural Networks

Neural networks are a type of machine learning algorithm that is inspired by the human brain. They are made up of interconnected nodes that must be trained on large amounts of data to learn how to perform a task.

- Neural networks are made up of input and output layers with one or more hidden layers in between.
- Each layer contains a number of nodes that are connected to nodes in the adjacent layers.
- As the network is trained on data, the connections between nodes are strengthened or weakened to produce the desired output.
- Neural networks are good at learning complex patterns in large data sets and are often used for tasks like image recognition, natural language processing, and making predictions.
- The power of neural networks comes from their ability to automatically learn features and patterns in the data that would be nearly impossible to manually code. However, they are "black box" models that are difficult for humans to interpret.
- Common types of neural networks include feedforward neural networks, recurrent neural networks, and convolutional neural networks.
