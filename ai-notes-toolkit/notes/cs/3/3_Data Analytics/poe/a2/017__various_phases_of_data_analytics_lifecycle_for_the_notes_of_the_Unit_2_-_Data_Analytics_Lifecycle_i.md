### Various Phases of Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. Business Understanding: Understanding the business problem and requirements. Identifying the key business objectives and metrics that need to be achieved.
2. Data Understanding: Understanding the available data - source, volume, variety, velocity, veracity and its relevance to the business problem. Identifying the gaps in data and requirements for additional data.
3. Data Preparation: Selecting, cleaning and transforming the data as per the analysis requirements. Removing noise and inconsistent data.
4. Modelling: Applying appropriate modelling techniques (descriptive, diagnostic, predictive or prescriptive) based on the business objective. Evaluating and optimizing the models.
5. Evaluation: Evaluating the analysis to check if it achieves the initial business objectives. Performing accuracy tests and other metrics based evaluations.
6. Deployment: Deploying the analysis and insights into the business environment. Enabling key decisions makers to take actions and gain business value from the analysis.
7. Monitoring: Continuously monitoring the deployed solutions and analysis to track the business impact and make improvements.

The above points cover the key phases in a data analytics lifecycle. The lifecycle is iterative and feedback from each phase is used to improve the analysis and insights. A robust governance framework is essential to ensure consistency, accuracy, compliance and business value from data analytics.
