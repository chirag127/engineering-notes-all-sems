## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

1. Frequent Itemsets:
- Itemset: A set of items.
- Frequent itemset: An itemset whose occurrence in the dataset is greater than or equal to a user-specified minimum support threshold.
- Support of an itemset: The percentage of transactions in the dataset that contain the itemset.
- Apriori algorithm: An algorithm to discover frequent itemsets in a dataset. It uses a "bottom-up" approach to count the support of itemsets and prunes the candidates that have infrequent subsets.

2. Clustering:
- Cluster: A group of data points that are similar to each other.
- Clustering: The task of grouping similar data points into clusters.
- Types of clustering: Hierarchical clustering, K-means clustering, DBSCAN, Mean shift, etc.
- Evaluation metrics: Homogeneity, Completeness, V-measure, Silhouette score, etc.
- Applications: Market segmentation, Anomaly detection, Recommender systems, etc.
