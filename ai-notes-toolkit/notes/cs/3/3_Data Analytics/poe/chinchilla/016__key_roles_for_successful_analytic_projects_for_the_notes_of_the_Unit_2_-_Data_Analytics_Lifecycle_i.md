### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

In order to ensure the success of an analytic project, there are several key roles that need to be filled. These roles are critical to the success of the project and each plays a unique part in the project cycle. Below are some of the key roles that must be filled to ensure a successful analytic project:

1. Project Manager: The project manager is responsible for overseeing the entire project and ensuring that it is completed on time and within budget. They are responsible for managing the team, tracking progress, and communicating with stakeholders.

2. Data Analyst: The data analyst is responsible for analyzing and interpreting data. They use statistical methods to identify patterns, trends, and relationships in the data. They also create visualizations and reports to communicate their findings to stakeholders.

3. Data Scientist: The data scientist is responsible for developing predictive models and algorithms. They use machine learning techniques to build models that can make predictions based on historical data. They also use data visualization techniques to communicate their findings to stakeholders.

4. Data Engineer: The data engineer is responsible for building and maintaining the infrastructure that supports the project. This includes setting up databases, data pipelines, and other tools that are needed to collect and process data.

5. Business Analyst: The business analyst is responsible for understanding the business context of the project. They work with stakeholders to identify business problems that can be addressed with analytics. They also help to define the requirements for the project and ensure that the project is aligned with the overall business strategy.

6. Domain Expert: The domain expert is responsible for providing subject matter expertise. They have knowledge of the specific industry or field that the project is focused on. They help to ensure that the project is focused on the right problems and that the results are relevant to the industry.

7. Executive Sponsor: The executive sponsor is responsible for providing support for the project. They help to secure funding and resources for the project. They also help to communicate the importance of the project to other stakeholders and ensure that the project is aligned with the overall business strategy.

In conclusion, having the right mix of roles is critical to the success of any analytic project. Each role plays a unique part in the project cycle and must work together to ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget. By understanding the key roles and responsibilities, organizations can ensure that their analytic projects are successful and deliver value to the business.
