
## Modeling Data Flow Diagrams for the Notes of the Software Engineering Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab in the Subject of Software Engineering

- Data flow diagrams (DFDs) are graphical representations of the flow of data through a system. 
- They are used to illustrate the flow of data between processes and the storage of data within a system. 
- DFDs are divided into three levels: context diagrams, level 0 diagrams, and level 1 diagrams. 
- Context diagrams are the highest level of abstraction and are used to illustrate the system boundaries. 
- Level 0 diagrams are used to illustrate the major processes within a system and the data that is exchanged between them. 
- Level 1 diagrams are used to illustrate the details of the processes and data flows within the system. 
- DFDs can be used to identify potential bottlenecks, redundant processes, and other inefficiencies in a system. 
- They can also be used to illustrate how data is transformed and stored within a system. 
- DFDs are an essential tool for software engineers as they can be used to map out a system before development begins.
