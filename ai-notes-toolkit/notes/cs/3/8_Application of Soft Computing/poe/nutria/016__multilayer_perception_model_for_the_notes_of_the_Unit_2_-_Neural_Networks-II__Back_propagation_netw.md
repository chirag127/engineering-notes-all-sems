
### Multilayer Perception Model for Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks)

1. A multilayer perception (MLP) is a type of artificial neural network (ANN) composed of multiple layers of neurons connected together.
2. MLPs are used for supervised learning tasks such as classification and regression.
3. MLPs consist of an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer.
4. Each layer is composed of neurons, which take inputs from the previous layer and produce an output.
5. The output of the input layer is fed into the first hidden layer, which then feeds into the second hidden layer, and so on until the output layer is reached.
6. The output layer produces the final output of the network.
7. The weights and biases of each layer are adjusted in order to minimize the error between the actual and predicted output.
8. This process is known as backpropagation, and is used to train the network.
9. MLPs can be used for a variety of tasks, including pattern recognition, classification, and regression.
10. They are also useful for solving complex problems such as image recognition and natural language processing.
