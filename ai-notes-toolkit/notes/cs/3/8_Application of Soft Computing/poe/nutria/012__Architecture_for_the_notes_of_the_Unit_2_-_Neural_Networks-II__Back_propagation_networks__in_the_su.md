
### Architecture for the notes of the Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks) in the subject of Application of Soft Computing

1. Neural networks are a type of artificial intelligence that are modeled after the human brain and nervous system.
2. They are composed of interconnected nodes, which act as neurons, and are used to process and store information.
3. Neural networks can be used to classify data, recognize patterns, and make predictions.
4. Back propagation networks are a type of neural network that uses supervised learning.
5. In back propagation networks, an input is provided and the network adjusts its weights and biases in order to minimize the error between the output and the desired result.
6. The back propagation algorithm is used to adjust the weights and biases of the network.
7. The algorithm is based on the idea of gradient descent, where the weights and biases are adjusted in the direction of the steepest decrease in the error.
8. Back propagation networks are used in a variety of applications, including image recognition, natural language processing, and robotics.
