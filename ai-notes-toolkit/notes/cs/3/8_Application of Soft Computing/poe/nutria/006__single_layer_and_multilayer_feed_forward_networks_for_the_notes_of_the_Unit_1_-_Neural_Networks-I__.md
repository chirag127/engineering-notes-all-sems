
### Single Layer and Multilayer Feed Forward Networks

1. A feed forward neural network is a type of artificial neural network where the connections between the nodes do not form a cycle.
2. A single layer feed forward neural network consists of only one layer of neurons, while a multilayer feed forward neural network consists of more than one layer of neurons.
3. The input layer of a feed forward neural network is the layer that receives data from the external environment.
4. The output layer of a feed forward neural network is the layer that produces the final output of the network.
5. The hidden layers of a feed forward neural network are the layers between the input and output layers that process the data and pass it on to the output layer.
6. The weights of the connections between the neurons in the feed forward neural network determine how the data is processed.
7. The bias of the neurons in the feed forward neural network determines how the data is interpreted.
8. The activation function of the neurons in the feed forward neural network determines how the output of the neurons is calculated.
9. The learning algorithm of the feed forward neural network determines how the weights and biases of the neurons are adjusted to produce the desired output.
10. Feed forward neural networks are used for a variety of tasks, including pattern recognition, image recognition, natural language processing, and decision making.
