
### Genetic Operators for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm (GA) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing

1. Selection: This is the process of selecting individuals from a population for reproduction. It is based on the fitness of the individual, where the higher the fitness, the higher the probability of being chosen for reproduction. 

2. Crossover: Crossover is the process of combining two parent individuals to produce a new offspring. It is the most important genetic operator and is used to introduce variation into the population.

3. Mutation: Mutation is the process of randomly changing a gene in the offspring. It is used to introduce new genetic material into the population and to prevent the population from becoming stagnant.

4. Migration: Migration is the process of transferring individuals from one population to another. It is used to introduce new genetic material into the population and to prevent the population from becoming isolated.
