
## Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back propagation networks)

1. Back propagation networks are a type of neural network that use a supervised learning algorithm to adjust the weights of the neurons in the network.

2. The algorithm works by propagating the errors from the output layer backwards through the network to the input layer, adjusting the weights of the neurons as it goes.

3. The algorithm is based on the concept of gradient descent, where the weights of the neurons are adjusted in the direction of the steepest descent of the error surface.

4. The algorithm is iterative, meaning that it runs multiple times over the same data set, gradually improving the weights of the neurons until the error is minimized.

5. Back propagation networks are used for a wide variety of tasks, including pattern recognition, image recognition, and natural language processing.
