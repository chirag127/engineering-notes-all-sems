
### Applications for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing

1. Neural networks are a type of artificial intelligence that is used to recognize patterns and make decisions.
2. They are composed of interconnected “neurons” that process and transmit information.
3. Back propagation networks are a type of neural network that uses supervised learning to adjust the weights of the neurons to optimize the network’s performance.
4. Back propagation networks are used for a variety of tasks, including image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and robotics.
5. They are also used in applications such as medical diagnosis, financial forecasting, and autonomous driving.
6. Back propagation networks are composed of an input layer, hidden layers, and an output layer.
7. The input layer receives input data and passes it to the hidden layers.
8. The hidden layers process the data and pass it to the output layer, which produces the output.
9. The weights of the neurons are adjusted using the back propagation algorithm, which uses the error between the desired output and the actual output to adjust the weights.
10. The back propagation algorithm is an iterative process that continues until the desired output is achieved.
