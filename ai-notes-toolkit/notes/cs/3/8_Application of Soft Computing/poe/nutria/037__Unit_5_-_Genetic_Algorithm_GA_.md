
## Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA)

* Genetic algorithms are a type of optimization algorithm that use principles of natural selection and genetics to find the optimal solution to a problem. 
* They are based on the idea of survival of the fittest and are used to solve problems in many different fields, such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, robotics, and operations research.
* Genetic algorithms work by creating a population of potential solutions to a problem and then using selection, crossover, and mutation operators to evolve the population over time. 
* The goal of the algorithm is to find the best solution to the problem, which is usually measured by its fitness. 
* The selection operator is used to select the fittest individuals from the population and allow them to reproduce. 
* The crossover operator combines the genetic material from two or more individuals to create new individuals. 
* The mutation operator randomly changes the genetic material of individuals. 
* The algorithm terminates when a solution with an acceptable fitness is found or when it reaches a predetermined number of generations.
