
### Performance Measurements for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: Algorithms, Analyzing Algorithms, Complexity of Algorithms, Growth of Functions, Performance Measurements, Sorting and Order Statistics 

- Algorithms are a set of instructions used to solve a problem. 
- Analyzing algorithms is the process of understanding the behavior of algorithms and determining the resources they require to execute.
- The complexity of algorithms is a measure of the resources required to execute them, such as time and memory.
- Growth of functions is a measure of the rate at which a function grows as its input size increases.
- Performance measurements are used to evaluate the efficiency of algorithms.
- Sorting and order statistics are algorithms used to rearrange data in order of importance.
- Shell sort, quick sort, merge sort, and heap sort are examples of sorting algorithms.
- Comparison of sorting algorithms is the process of determining which algorithm is the most efficient for a given problem.
- Sorting in linear time is an algorithm that sorts data in a time complexity of O(n).
