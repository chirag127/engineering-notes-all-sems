
### Greedy Methods with Examples

Greedy algorithms are a type of algorithmic approach used to solve optimization problems. Greedy algorithms are characterized by making the locally optimal choice at each stage with the hope of finding a global optimum. 

Examples of greedy algorithms include:

* Knapsack problem: A classic problem of selecting a set of items with maximum value from a given set of items with certain weights and values.
* Optimal reliability allocation: An optimization problem of allocating resources to maximize reliability of a system.
* Minimum spanning trees: An algorithm to find a tree with minimum total weight in a weighted graph. Prim's and Kruskal's algorithms are two popular algorithms used to solve this problem.
* Single source shortest paths: Algorithms to find the shortest paths from a given source to all other vertices in a weighted graph. Dijkstra's and Bellman-Ford algorithms are two popular algorithms used to solve this problem.

These algorithms are commonly used in the field of design and analysis of algorithms.
