
### Divide and Conquer

Divide and Conquer is an algorithmic strategy that involves breaking a problem down into smaller, subproblems in order to solve the larger problem. The strategy is often used for sorting, matrix multiplication, convex hull, and searching.

#### Examples

* Sorting - QuickSort, MergeSort, HeapSort
* Matrix Multiplication - Strassen's Algorithm
* Convex Hull - Graham Scan
* Searching - Binary Search

### Greedy Methods

Greedy methods are algorithmic strategies that involve making a series of decisions that are locally optimal, with the hope of finding a global optimum. Examples of problems that can be solved using greedy methods include optimal reliability allocation, knapsack, minimum spanning trees, single source shortest paths, and more.

#### Examples

* Optimal Reliability Allocation - Greedy Algorithm
* Knapsack - Greedy Algorithm
* Minimum Spanning Trees - Prim's and Kruskal's Algorithms
* Single Source Shortest Paths - Dijkstra's and Bellman Ford Algorithms
