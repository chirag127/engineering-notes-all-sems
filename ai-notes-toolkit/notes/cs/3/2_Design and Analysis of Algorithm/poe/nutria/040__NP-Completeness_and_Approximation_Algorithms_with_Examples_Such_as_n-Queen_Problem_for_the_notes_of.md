
### NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms with Examples

NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms are a type of algorithms used to solve complex problems in polynomial time. These algorithms are used to find approximate solutions to problems that are otherwise too difficult to solve. Examples of NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms include Travelling Salesman Problem, Graph Coloring, n-Queen Problem, Hamiltonian Cycles and Sum of Subsets. 

These algorithms are used in the field of Design and Analysis of Algorithm. They are used to find solutions to a wide range of problems such as scheduling, routing, optimization, and resource allocation. 

Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP) is a classic example of an NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithm. In this problem, a salesman must visit all of the cities in a given graph, in the least amount of time. The goal is to find the shortest path that visits each city exactly once. 

Graph Coloring is another example of an NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithm. In this problem, the goal is to color each vertex of a graph in such a way that no two adjacent vertices have the same color. This problem can be used to solve a variety of problems such as scheduling and resource allocation. 

The n-Queen Problem is a classic example of an NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithm. In this problem, the goal is to place n queens on an nxn chessboard such that no two queens attack each other. This problem can be used to solve a variety of problems such as scheduling and resource allocation. 

Hamiltonian Cycles are another example of an NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithm. In this problem, the goal is to find a cycle that visits each vertex of a given graph exactly once. This problem can be used to solve a variety of problems such as scheduling and resource allocation. 

Finally, the Sum of Subsets problem is an example of an NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithm. In this problem, the goal is to find a subset of a given set of numbers such that the sum of the numbers in the subset is equal to a given target number. This problem can be used to solve a variety of problems such as scheduling and resource allocation.

These algorithms are used to find approximate solutions to problems that are otherwise too difficult to solve. Examples of NP-Completeness and Approximation Algorithms include Travelling Salesman Problem, Graph Coloring, n-Queen Problem, Hamiltonian Cycles and Sum of Subsets.
