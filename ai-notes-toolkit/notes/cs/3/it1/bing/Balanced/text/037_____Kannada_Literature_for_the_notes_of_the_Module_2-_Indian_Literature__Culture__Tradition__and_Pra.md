### Kannada Literature

- Kannada literature is the corpus of written forms of the Kannada language, a member of the Dravidian family spoken mainly in the Indian state of Karnataka and written in the Kannada script .
- The earliest records in Kannada are inscriptions dating from the 6th century AD onward. The oldest existing literary work in Kannada is the Kavirajamarga, a treatise on poetics, written by King Nripatunga Amoghavarsha I in the 9th century.
- Kannada literature has been influenced by various literary traditions, such as Jainism, Shaivism, Vaishnavism, Bhakti movement, Veerashaivism, Haridasa movement, Vachana movement, and Dalit literature.
- Kannada literature can be broadly divided into five periods: Old Kannada (9th to 14th century), Middle Kannada (14th to 18th century), Modern Kannada (19th to mid-20th century), Navya (mid-20th to late 20th century), and Post-Navya (late 20th to 21st century) .
- Some of the notable writers and works of Kannada literature are:

  - Pampa, Ranna, and Ponna, the three gems of Old Kannada literature, who wrote epics and courtly poetry in the champu style .
  - Basava, Allama Prabhu, Akka Mahadevi, and other Vachana poets, who composed devotional and mystical verses in the Middle Kannada period .
  - Kumara Vyasa, who wrote the Kannada version of the Mahabharata, called the Gadugina Bharata, in the 15th century .
  - Lakshmisa, who wrote the Jaimini Bharata, a popular retelling of the Mahabharata, in the 16th century .
  - Sarvajna, who wrote witty and wise three-line poems called tripadi in the 17th century .
  - Panchakshari Hiremath, who wrote the first Kannada novel, Indira Bai, in 1899 .
  - Kuvempu, who wrote the epic Ramayana Darshanam, which won him the Jnanpith Award, the highest literary honor in India, in 1967 .
  - Bendre, who wrote lyrical and innovative poetry, and also won the Jnanpith Award in 1973 .
  - Shivaram Karanth, who wrote novels, short stories, plays, and essays, and also won the Jnanpith Award in 1977 .
  - U.R. Ananthamurthy, who wrote novels, short stories, essays, and criticism, and also won the Jnanpith Award in 1994 .
  - Girish Karnad, who wrote plays, screenplays, and essays, and also won the Jnanpith Award in 1998 .
  - S.L. Bhyrappa, who wrote novels, essays, and translations, and is one of the most widely read Kannada writers .
  - Chandrashekhara Kambara, who wrote novels, short stories, plays, and folk literature, and also won the Jnanpith Award in 2010 .
  - Vaidehi, who wrote novels, short stories, and essays, and is one of the leading female writers of Kannada literature .
  - Devanur Mahadeva, who wrote novels, short stories, and essays, and is one of the prominent Dalit writers of Kannada literature .
