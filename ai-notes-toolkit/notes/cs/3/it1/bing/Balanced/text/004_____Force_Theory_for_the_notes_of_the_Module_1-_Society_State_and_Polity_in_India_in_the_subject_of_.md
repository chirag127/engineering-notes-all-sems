### Force Theory

- Force theory is one of the theories that explain the origin of the state.
- According to this theory, the state emerged as a result of conquest, violence, and coercion by a powerful individual or group over a weaker one.
- The conquerors established their authority and domination over the conquered people and imposed their will and laws on them.
- The state, therefore, is based on force and not on consent or contract.
- Some examples of force theory are the ancient empires of Assyria, Babylon, Persia, Rome, and China, as well as the modern colonial empires of Britain, France, Spain, and Portugal.
- The main criticisms of force theory are that it ignores the role of social contract, consent, and cooperation in the formation of the state, and that it does not explain the diversity and complexity of political systems and cultures in the world.
