### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times

- India has a long and rich history that dates back to 2500 BC, when the Indus Valley civilization emerged as one of the earliest urban cultures in the world.
- India witnessed the rise and fall of many empires and dynasties, such as the Mauryans, Guptas, Kushans, Palas, Cholas, Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, Mughals, Marathas, Sikhs and others, that contributed to its political, cultural, religious and economic diversity and development  .
- India was a major center of learning, art, literature, science, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, architecture and technology in the pre-colonial period. Some of the notable achievements include the decimal system, zero, algebra, trigonometry, calculus, Ayurveda, yoga, metallurgy, textiles, shipbuilding, chess, and the concept of zero .
- India was also a hub of trade and commerce, connecting with various regions of Asia, Africa and Europe through land and sea routes. India exported spices, cotton, silk, indigo, iron, steel, ivory, pearls, diamonds and other goods to foreign markets, and imported gold, silver, horses, porcelain, and other commodities. India had a flourishing maritime trade with Southeast Asia, China, Arabia, Persia and East Africa  .
- India's dominance in the pre-colonial times was challenged by the advent of European colonial powers, especially the British, who exploited India's resources, imposed oppressive policies, disrupted its social and cultural fabric, and suppressed its nationalist movements. India's independence from British rule in 1947 marked the end of the colonial era and the beginning of a new phase in its history.
