### Buddhism

- Buddhism is an Indian religion or philosophy that developed from the teachings of the Buddha (Sanskrit: "Awakened One"), a teacher who lived in northern India between the mid-6th and mid-4th centuries BCE .
- Buddhism is also known as Buddha Dharma and Dharmavinaya, which means "doctrines and disciplines" in Sanskrit.
- The followers of Buddhism are called Buddhists, who referred to themselves as Sakyan-s or Sakyabhiksu in ancient India.
- The main goal of Buddhism is to attain nirvana, which is the cessation of suffering and the cycle of rebirth .
- Buddhism is based on the Four Noble Truths, which are:
  - The truth of suffering (dukkha): All conditioned existence is unsatisfactory and subject to pain, sorrow, and impermanence.
  - The truth of the origin of suffering (samudaya): The cause of suffering is craving (tanha) and ignorance (avidya) of the true nature of reality.
  - The truth of the cessation of suffering (nirodha): The end of suffering is possible by eliminating craving and ignorance.
  - The truth of the path to the cessation of suffering (magga): The way to end suffering is to follow the Noble Eightfold Path, which consists of right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right concentration.
- Buddhism is divided into three main branches: Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana .
  - Theravada (the "Way of the Elders") is the oldest and most conservative form of Buddhism, which follows the Pali Canon, the earliest collection of Buddhist scriptures. Theravada emphasizes the individual attainment of nirvana through meditation and ethical conduct.
  - Mahayana (the "Great Vehicle") is the more expansive and diverse form of Buddhism, which accepts other scriptures and teachings besides the Pali Canon. Mahayana emphasizes the universal compassion and the bodhisattva ideal, which is the aspiration to attain buddhahood for the benefit of all sentient beings.
  - Vajrayana (the "Diamond Vehicle") is the esoteric and tantric form of Buddhism, which incorporates rituals, mantras, and visualizations as means of transforming the mind and body. Vajrayana claims to offer a faster and more direct path to enlightenment than the other branches.
- Buddhism has spread to various regions and cultures, such as Tibet, China, Japan, Southeast Asia, and the West, and has influenced art, literature, philosophy, and social movements .
