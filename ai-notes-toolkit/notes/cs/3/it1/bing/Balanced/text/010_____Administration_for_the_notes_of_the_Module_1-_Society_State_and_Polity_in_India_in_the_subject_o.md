### Administration

- Administration is the process of managing the affairs of an organization or a state by implementing policies, rules and regulations.
- Administration can be classified into two types: public administration and private administration.
- Public administration is the administration of public affairs by the government or its agencies. It involves the delivery of public services, the maintenance of law and order, the collection of taxes, the formulation and execution of public policies, etc.
- Private administration is the administration of private affairs by individuals, groups or organizations. It involves the management of business, finance, education, health, etc.
- Administration can also be classified into three levels: central, state and local.
- Central administration is the administration of the whole country by the central government. It includes the executive, legislative and judicial branches of the government, as well as various ministries, departments and commissions.
- State administration is the administration of a state or a province by the state government. It includes the governor, the chief minister, the state legislature, the state judiciary, as well as various departments and boards.
- Local administration is the administration of a district, a city, a town or a village by the local government. It includes the district magistrate, the municipal commissioner, the panchayat, the gram sabha, etc.
