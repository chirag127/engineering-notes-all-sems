### Mathematics in India

- Mathematics in India is the discipline of mathematics as it developed in the Indian subcontinent from ancient to modern times.
- Indian mathematics is characterized by a rich diversity of topics, methods, and styles, influenced by various cultural, religious, and philosophical traditions.
- Some of the major achievements of Indian mathematics include:
  - The development of the decimal place-value system and the use of zero as a number, which are the basis of the modern number system .
  - The discovery of several algebraic, trigonometric, and geometric concepts and techniques, such as the quadratic formula, the sine and cosine functions, the Pythagorean theorem, and the area of a triangle .
  - The invention of various methods of calculation, such as the use of fractions, decimals, negative numbers, surds, and algorithms .
  - The formulation of various mathematical theories and proofs, such as the theory of numbers, the theory of equations, the theory of series, and the theory of infinity .
  - The creation of various mathematical texts and treatises, such as the Sulba Sutras, the Aryabhatiya, the Brahmasphutasiddhanta, the Lilavati, and the Siddhanta Siromani  .
- Indian mathematics had a significant impact on the development of mathematics in other regions, such as China, the Middle East, and Europe, through trade, cultural exchange, and transmission of texts .
- Indian mathematics also had a close connection with other fields of knowledge, such as astronomy, astrology, medicine, architecture, art, and music .
