### The Ramayana

The Ramayana is a Hindu epic that narrates the life and adventures of Rama, the prince of Kosala, and his wife Sita. It is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India, along with the Mahabharata. The Ramayana consists of seven books and 24,000 verses. It is attributed to the sage Valmiki, who is also a character in the story.

The main plot of the Ramayana is as follows:

- Rama is the eldest son of King Dasaratha of Kosala and his first wife Kausalya. He is the ideal man, who is virtuous, brave, loyal, and compassionate. He is also an avatar of Vishnu, the supreme god of Hinduism.
- Rama wins the hand of Sita, the daughter of King Janaka of Mithila, by breaking the bow of Shiva, a divine weapon that no one else can lift or string. Sita is the ideal woman, who is beautiful, faithful, devoted, and pure. She is also an incarnation of Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth and prosperity.
- Rama is about to be crowned as the heir to the throne, but his stepmother Kaikeyi, who is influenced by her maid Manthara, demands that her son Bharata be made the king instead, and that Rama be exiled for 14 years. Dasaratha reluctantly agrees, as he had promised Kaikeyi two boons in the past. Rama accepts his fate and leaves for the forest, accompanied by Sita and his younger brother Lakshmana, who is loyal and courageous.
- In the forest, Rama meets many sages, allies, and enemies. He also encounters the monkey king Sugriva, who is in conflict with his brother Vali. Rama helps Sugriva kill Vali and become the king of the monkeys. In return, Sugriva promises to help Rama find Sita, who is abducted by Ravana, the king of Lanka and the leader of the demons. Ravana is a powerful and arrogant ruler, who has a boon that he cannot be killed by any god or demon. He is also a devotee of Shiva, and has ten heads and twenty arms.
- Rama and Lakshmana, along with Sugriva and his army of monkeys and bears, search for Sita across India and beyond. They are aided by Hanuman, the son of the wind god and the chief minister of Sugriva. Hanuman is a loyal, brave, and intelligent monkey, who can fly and change his size and shape. He is also a devotee of Rama, and performs many heroic deeds in his service.
- Hanuman finds Sita in Lanka, where she is held captive in a garden called Ashoka Vatika. He delivers Rama's message and ring to her, and assures her that Rama will come to rescue her. He also burns down the city of Lanka with his flaming tail, after being captured and insulted by Ravana's son Indrajit. Sita is tormented by Ravana and his demonesses, who try to persuade her to marry Ravana. She remains steadfast in her love and loyalty to Rama, and rejects Ravana's advances.
- Rama and his allies build a bridge across the ocean to reach Lanka, with the help of Nala, a monkey engineer. They wage a fierce war against Ravana and his army of demons. Many heroes and villains are killed in the battle, including Ravana's brothers Kumbhakarna and Vibhishana. Vibhishana, however, defects to Rama's side, as he is disgusted by Ravana's tyranny and immorality. He also reveals Ravana's weakness, which is his navel.
- Rama finally confronts and kills Ravana with a special arrow given by the sage Agastya. He frees Sita and crowns Vibhishana as the new king of Lanka. He then returns to Ayodhya with Sita, Lakshmana, Hanuman, and the other allies, after completing his 14 years of exile. He is welcomed by Bharata, who had refused to take the throne and had ruled as Rama's regent. Rama is then crowned as the king of Kosala, and he rules with justice and righteousness. He and Sita are revered as the ideal couple, and their reign is known as Rama Rajya, the golden age of peace and prosperity.
