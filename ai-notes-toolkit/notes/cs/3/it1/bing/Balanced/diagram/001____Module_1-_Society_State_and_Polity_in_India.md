## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

- Society is a group of people who share a common culture, language, religion, ethnicity, or history.
- State is a political entity that has a defined territory, a government, and sovereignty over its internal and external affairs.
- Polity is a form or system of government or political organization.
- India is a diverse and pluralistic society with multiple identities based on caste, class, religion, region, language, gender, etc.
- India is also a democratic and secular state with a parliamentary system of government and a federal structure of power-sharing between the center and the states.
- India's polity is influenced by various factors such as colonial legacy, nationalist movement, constitutional framework, party system, social movements, civil society, media, judiciary, etc.
- Some of the key issues and challenges facing India's society, state and polity are:
  - Poverty, inequality, and social exclusion
  - Communalism, secularism, and minority rights
  - Caste, reservation, and affirmative action
  - Regionalism, federalism, and center-state relations
  - Corruption, accountability, and transparency
  - Development, environment, and human rights
  - Globalization, foreign policy, and national security
- The module aims to provide a comprehensive and critical understanding of the historical, social, cultural, and political aspects of India's society, state and polity. It also seeks to develop analytical and comparative skills among the students to appreciate the diversity and complexity of India's reality.
