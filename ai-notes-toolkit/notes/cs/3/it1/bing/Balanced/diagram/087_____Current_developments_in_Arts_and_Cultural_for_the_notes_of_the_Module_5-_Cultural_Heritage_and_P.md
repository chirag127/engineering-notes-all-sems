### Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of art, music, dance, theatre, literature, architecture, and festivals.
- India is also home to 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and many smaller ones, and a vibrant folk and tribal culture.
- Some of the current developments in arts and culture in India are:

  - Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, which aims to promote lifelong learning and social inclusion through culture and education .
  - India has nominated the Hoysala Temples of Karnataka, which are examples of the unique Hoysala style of architecture and sculpture, for inscription in the World Heritage List.
  - India has got back a 10th-century sandstone sculpture of a goat-headed Yogini, which was stolen and smuggled out of the country, from the UK. The sculpture is a rare representation of the Tantric cult of Yoginis, who are female deities associated with mystical powers.
  - India has witnessed a surge in contemporary art, which explores new trends and themes in Indian paintings, such as urbanization, globalization, identity, gender, politics, and spirituality. Contemporary Indian artists use various mediums and techniques, such as digital art, installation art, performance art, and mixed media, to express their creative vision.
  - India has celebrated various cultural festivals and events, such as the Republic Day Parade, which showcases the diversity and unity of the country, the Dharohar exhibition, which displays the history and heritage of the customs and GST department, the Devayatanam project, which documents the odyssey of Indian temple architecture, and the Sydney WorldPride, which brings the LGBT community together  .

- These developments reflect the dynamism and diversity of Indian arts and culture, and their contribution to the social, economic, and environmental development of the country.
