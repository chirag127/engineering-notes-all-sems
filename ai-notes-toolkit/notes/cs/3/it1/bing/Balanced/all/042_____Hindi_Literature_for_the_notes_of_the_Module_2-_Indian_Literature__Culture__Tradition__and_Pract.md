# Hindi Literature

Hindi literature is the literature written in the Hindi language, which includes various dialects and scripts. Hindi literature has a long and rich history, dating back to the 11th century. Hindi literature can be divided into four main periods: 

- **Adikal (the Early Period)**: This period covers the literature from the 11th to the 14th century. The main genres of this period are religious poetry, heroic poetry, and lyric poetry. The most prominent writers of this period are Chand Bardai, Jayasi, Kabir, and Tulsidas. Some of the famous works of this period are Prithviraj Raso, Padmavat, Ramcharitmanas, and Bijak.
- **Bhaktikal (the Devotional Period)**: This period covers the literature from the 15th to the 17th century. The main genres of this period are bhakti poetry, sagun poetry, and nirgun poetry. The most prominent writers of this period are Surdas, Mirabai, Tukaram, and Nanak. Some of the famous works of this period are Sur Sagar, Mira Bai Padavali, Abhang, and Japji Sahib.
- **Ritikal (the Scholastic Period)**: This period covers the literature from the 18th to the 19th century. The main genres of this period are ritikavya, riti poetry, and rekhta. The most prominent writers of this period are Keshavdas, Bihari, Ghananand, and Mirza Ghalib. Some of the famous works of this period are Rasik Priya, Satsai, Prem Sagar, and Diwan-e-Ghalib.
- **Adhunik Kal (the Modern Period)**: This period covers the literature from the 20th century onwards. The main genres of this period are novel, short story, drama, essay, and criticism. The most prominent writers of this period are Premchand, Jaishankar Prasad, Mahadevi Varma, and Harivansh Rai Bachchan. Some of the famous works of this period are Godan, Kamayani, Chhayavad, and Madhushala.

Hindi literature reflects the culture, tradition, and practices of the Indian society. It also deals with various themes such as nationalism, social reform, feminism, and humanism. Hindi literature has influenced and been influenced by other Indian languages and literatures, such as Sanskrit, Urdu, Bengali, and English. Hindi literature is a valuable source of knowledge and inspiration for the students of Indian traditions, cultural and society.
