# Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module aims to introduce the students to the rich and diverse Indian Knowledge System (IKS) and its relevance and applications in various domains of science and management. The module will cover the following topics:

- What is IKS and why is it important to study it?
- The historical and cultural context of IKS and its sources and methods of knowledge generation and transmission.
- The contributions of IKS to various fields of science such as mathematics, astronomy, medicine, metallurgy, chemistry, ecology, etc.
- The principles and practices of IKS in management, governance, ethics, leadership, innovation, etc.
- The challenges and opportunities of integrating IKS with modern science and management.

Some of the learning outcomes of this module are:

- To develop an appreciation and respect for the indigenous knowledge systems of India and their relevance for contemporary society.
- To gain an understanding of the scientific and philosophical foundations of IKS and their similarities and differences with modern science.
- To explore the applications of IKS in various domains of science and management and their potential for solving contemporary problems.
- To develop a critical and reflective attitude towards the strengths and limitations of IKS and modern science and management.
- To foster a spirit of curiosity and inquiry towards the diverse and dynamic aspects of IKS and their scope for further research and innovation.
