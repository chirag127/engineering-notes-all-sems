# Conditions of the Welfare of Societies

- Welfare of societies refers to the well-being of the people in terms of their health, education, income, security, culture, and environment.
- Welfare of societies depends on various factors, such as the political, economic, social, and cultural systems of the country, the availability and distribution of resources, the quality and accessibility of public services, and the participation and empowerment of the citizens.
- In India, the welfare of societies is influenced by the diversity and complexity of the Indian society, which has various ethnic, linguistic, religious, caste, class, and regional groups, as well as urban-rural and gender differences.
- The Indian Constitution aims to establish a welfare state that provides political, social, and economic justice to all its citizens. The Preamble and the Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) reflect the vision and values of the welfare state in India.
- The social welfare services of the government of India are intended to cater to the special needs of persons and groups who, by reason of some handicap, social, economic, physical, or mental, are unable to avail themselves of the amenities and services provided by the community.
- Some of the social welfare schemes and programs of the government of India are:

  - National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP), which provides financial assistance to the elderly, widows, disabled, and poor households.
  - Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS), which provides supplementary nutrition, health care, and pre-school education to children below six years and pregnant and lactating women.
  - Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA), which guarantees 100 days of wage employment in a year to rural households willing to do unskilled manual work.
  - Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), which aims to provide universal access to banking services, financial inclusion, and social security.
  - Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY), which provides free LPG connections to poor women households.
  - Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY), which aims to provide affordable housing to the urban and rural poor.
  - Ayushman Bharat, which provides health insurance coverage of up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
  - Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP), which aims to improve the sex ratio, empower girls, and promote their education.
  - Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM), which aims to achieve universal sanitation and hygiene, and eliminate open defecation by 2019.
  - National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), which aims to enhance the livelihoods and incomes of the rural poor through self-help groups, skill development, and access to credit and markets.

- The welfare of societies in India also depends on the social issues and challenges that affect the people, such as caste system, child labour, illiteracy, gender inequality, superstitions, religious conflicts, and many more.
- The welfare of societies in India requires the collective efforts of the government, civil society, private sector, and the citizens to address the gaps and inequalities, and to promote the rights and dignity of all.
