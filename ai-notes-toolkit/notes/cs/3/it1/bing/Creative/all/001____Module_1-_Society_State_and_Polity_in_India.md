# Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

This module aims to introduce the students to the basic concepts and theories of society, state and polity in India. It also seeks to provide a historical and comparative perspective on the evolution and functioning of the Indian political system.

## Learning Objectives

- To understand the meaning and significance of society, state and polity in the context of India.
- To trace the historical development of the Indian state and its interaction with society and culture.
- To analyse the features and challenges of the Indian democracy and its institutions.
- To examine the role and impact of identity politics, social movements and civil society on the Indian polity.

## Topics Covered

- Society, State and Polity: Definitions and Dimensions
- Historical Origins and Evolution of the Indian State
- Constitutional Framework and Fundamental Rights
- Federalism and Centre-State Relations
- Democracy and Electoral System
- Political Parties and Party System
- Parliament and Executive
- Judiciary and Rule of Law
- Identity Politics and Social Diversity
- Social Movements and Civil Society
- Foreign Policy and International Relations
