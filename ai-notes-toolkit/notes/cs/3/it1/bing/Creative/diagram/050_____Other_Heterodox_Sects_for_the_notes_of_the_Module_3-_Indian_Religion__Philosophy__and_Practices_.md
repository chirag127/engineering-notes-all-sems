### Other Heterodox Sects

- Heterodox sects are those that do not accept the authority of the Vedas and the Brahmanical rituals and practices.
- They emerged in the 6th century BCE as a reaction to the rigid and complex Vedic religion, the social inequalities, and the need for new ethical and spiritual teachings.
- Some of the heterodox sects are:

  - **Ajivika**: Founded by Makkhali Gosala, this sect believed in fatalism, that is, everything is predetermined by fate and human actions have no effect on it . They also rejected the concepts of karma, rebirth, and liberation.
  - **Lokayata**: Also known as Charvaka, this sect advocated materialism, that is, only matter is real and there is no soul, god, or life after death. They also rejected the concepts of morality, ethics, and duty, and promoted hedonism, that is, the pursuit of sensual pleasures.
  - **Nityavadi**: This sect believed in eternalism, that is, everything is eternal and unchanging. They also rejected the concepts of causation, creation, and destruction.
  - **Sandehavadi**: This sect believed in agnosticism, that is, nothing can be known for certain and doubt is the only valid position. They also rejected the concepts of faith, revelation, and authority.
  - **Akriyavadi**: This sect believed in amoralism, that is, there is no moral value or obligation in human actions. They also rejected the concepts of good, evil, virtue, and sin.
