### Hindi Literature

- Hindi literature is the literature written in the various Hindi languages, which have writing systems.
- Hindi literature can be divided into four main periods: Adikal (the Early Period), Bhaktikal (the Devotional Period), Ritikal (the Scholastic Period), and Adhunik (the Modern Period).
- Hindi literature is composed in three broad styles: Gadya (prose), Padya (poetry), and Campū (prosimetrum).
- Some of the earliest forms of Hindi literature are the poetry of Apabhraṃśa, which are dialects of Prakrit, such as Awadhi, Magadhi, Ardhamagadhi, and Marwari.
- Hindi literature was influenced by various religious and philosophical traditions, such as Hinduism, Islam, Jainism, Buddhism, and Sikhism .
- Hindi literature also reflects the social and political issues of the Indian subcontinent, such as caste, gender, nationalism, colonialism, and modernization .
