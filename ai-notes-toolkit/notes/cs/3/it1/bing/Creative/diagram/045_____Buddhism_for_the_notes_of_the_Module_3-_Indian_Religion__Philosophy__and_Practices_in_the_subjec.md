### Buddhism

- Buddhism is an Indian religion or philosophy that developed from the teachings of the Buddha (Sanskrit: "Awakened One"), a teacher who lived in northern India between the mid-6th and mid-4th centuries BCE .
- Buddhism is also known as Buddha Dharma and Dharmavinaya, which means "doctrines and disciplines".
- The followers of Buddhism are called Buddhists, who referred to themselves as Sakyan-s or Sakyabhiksu in ancient India.
- The main goal of Buddhism is to attain nirvana, which is the cessation of suffering and the cycle of rebirth .
- Buddhism is based on the Four Noble Truths, which are:
  - The truth of suffering (dukkha): All conditioned existence is unsatisfactory and subject to pain, sorrow, and impermanence .
  - The truth of the origin of suffering (samudaya): Suffering is caused by craving (tanha) for sensual pleasures, existence, and non-existence .
  - The truth of the cessation of suffering (nirodha): Suffering can be ended by eliminating craving .
  - The truth of the path to the cessation of suffering (magga): The way to end suffering is to follow the Noble Eightfold Path, which consists of right view, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right concentration .
- Buddhism is divided into three main branches: Theravada, Mahayana, and Vajrayana .
  - Theravada (the "Way of the Elders") is the oldest and most conservative branch of Buddhism, which follows the Pali Canon, the earliest collection of Buddhist scriptures . Theravada emphasizes the individual attainment of nirvana through meditation and ethical conduct . Theravada is mainly practiced in Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Cambodia, and Laos .
  - Mahayana (the "Great Vehicle") is the largest and most diverse branch of Buddhism, which accepts the Pali Canon as well as other scriptures and teachings . Mahayana emphasizes the universal compassion and the bodhisattva ideal, which is the aspiration to attain buddhahood for the benefit of all sentient beings . Mahayana is mainly practiced in China, Japan, Korea, Vietnam, and Tibet .
  - Vajrayana (the "Diamond Vehicle") is the tantric branch of Buddhism, which incorporates esoteric practices and rituals, such as mantras, mudras, and mandalas, to achieve enlightenment in this lifetime . Vajrayana is mainly practiced in Tibet, Nepal, Bhutan, Mongolia, and parts of India .
- Buddhism has influenced many aspects of Indian culture, such as art, literature, philosophy, ethics, and social movements . Buddhism has also spread to other parts of the world, such as Southeast Asia, Central Asia, East Asia, and the West .
