### Geography
- Geography is the study of the physical features of the earth and its atmosphere, and of human activity as it affects and is affected by these.
- Geography is a key subject for various competitive examinations, as it covers the aspects of natural resources, climate, population, agriculture, industry, transport, trade, etc.
- Geography also helps to understand the cultural, historical, and political dimensions of different regions and countries.
- Geography can be divided into two main branches: physical geography and human geography.
- Physical geography deals with the natural phenomena and processes that shape the earth's surface, such as landforms, climate, vegetation, soils, water, etc.
- Human geography deals with the spatial patterns and interactions of human activities, such as population, settlement, culture, economy, development, etc.
- Geography also has many sub-disciplines, such as geomorphology, climatology, biogeography, hydrology, cartography, urban geography, regional geography, etc.
