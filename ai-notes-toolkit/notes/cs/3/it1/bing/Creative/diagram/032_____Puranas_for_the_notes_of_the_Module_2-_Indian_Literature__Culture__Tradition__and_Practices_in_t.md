### Puranas

- Puranas are a vast genre of Indian literature that deal with various topics, especially legends and traditional lore .
- Puranas are considered as one of the most ancient and important sources of Hindu culture, religion, history, and cosmology .
- Puranas are classified as Smriti (remembered) texts, as opposed to Shruti (heard) texts like the Vedas. They are also regarded as the fifth Veda.
- Puranas are composed in Sanskrit verse, mostly in the form of narrative couplets . They date from the 4th century BCE to the 10th century CE.
- Puranas are divided into six categories, based on the main deity they glorify: Vishnu, Shiva, Brahma, Shakti, Agni, and Surya .
- Puranas typically contain five topics: sarga (creation), pratisarga (recreation), vamśa (genealogy of gods and sages), manvantara (cosmic cycles), and vamśānucarita (history of royal dynasties) .
- Puranas also include various other topics, such as geography, astronomy, astrology, rituals, festivals, temples, ethics, law, philosophy, yoga, and art .
- Puranas are not considered as authoritative or infallible by Hindus, but rather as a means of popular education and entertainment .
- Puranas are widely read and revered by Hindus of different sects and traditions, as they provide a rich and diverse picture of Hindu worldview and values  .
