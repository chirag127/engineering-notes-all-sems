### Mathematics in India

- Mathematics in India emerged in the Indian subcontinent from 1200 BCE until the end of the 18th century.
- The mathematics of classical Indian civilization (400 CE to 1200 CE) is an intriguing blend of the familiar and the strange .
- Indian mathematics made important contributions to various fields, such as arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, astronomy, and calculus .
- Some of the notable achievements of Indian mathematics are:
  - The development of the decimal place-value system and the use of zero as a number .
  - The discovery of negative numbers, fractions, irrational numbers, and quadratic equations .
  - The invention of the sine, cosine, and tangent functions and their tables .
  - The formulation of the Pythagorean theorem, the area of a triangle, and the value of pi .
  - The derivation of the formula for the sum of a geometric series and the solution of indeterminate equations .
  - The development of the concept of infinity and infinitesimals and their applications to calculus .
- Some of the prominent mathematicians of India are:
  - Aryabhata (476-550 CE), who wrote the Aryabhatiya, a treatise on astronomy and mathematics that contains the earliest known use of the decimal system and the approximation of pi .
  - Brahmagupta (598-668 CE), who wrote the Brahmasphutasiddhanta, a work on arithmetic and algebra that introduces negative numbers and zero and gives rules for their operations .
  - Bhaskara II (1114-1185 CE), who wrote the Siddhanta Siromani, a comprehensive text on mathematics and astronomy that covers topics such as fractions, irrational numbers, quadratic equations, trigonometry, and calculus .
  - Varahamihira (505-587 CE), who wrote the Panchasiddhantika, a summary of five earlier astronomical works that contains trigonometric formulas and tables .
