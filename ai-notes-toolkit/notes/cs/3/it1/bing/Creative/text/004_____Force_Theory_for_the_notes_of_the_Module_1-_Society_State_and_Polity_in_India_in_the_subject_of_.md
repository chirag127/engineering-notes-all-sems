### Force Theory of Origin of State

- Force theory of origin of state is a theory that explains the emergence of the state as a result of the conquest and domination of the weak by the strong  .
- According to this theory, the state originated due to force exerted by the strong over the weak. The idea contained in the statement is that 'war begat the king'.
- Force theory of origin of state assumes that the state is a product of violence, coercion and subjugation. The state is seen as an instrument of oppression and exploitation of the masses by the ruling class or elite. 
- Force theory of origin of state is based on the historical evidence of the formation of some of the greatest empires and states through wars, invasions and annexations. For example, the Roman Empire, the Mongol Empire, the British Empire, etc. 
- Force theory of origin of state is criticized for being too simplistic, unrealistic and ethnocentric. It ignores the role of other factors such as social contract, divine right, natural law, consent, etc. in the origin and development of the state. It also overlooks the diversity and complexity of the state forms and functions across different cultures and regions.
