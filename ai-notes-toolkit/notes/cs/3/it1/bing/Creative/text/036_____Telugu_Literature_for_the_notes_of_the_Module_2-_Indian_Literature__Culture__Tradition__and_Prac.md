### Telugu Literature

- Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, a Dravidian language spoken mainly in the states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana in India.
- Telugu literature consists of various genres, such as poems, short stories, novels, plays, and song lyrics, among others.
- Telugu literature dates back to at least the middle of the first millennium CE, but the first extant works are from the 11th century CE, when the Mahabharata was first translated into Telugu by Nannaya.
- Telugu literature can be broadly divided into four periods: Ancient (11th to 14th century), Medieval (15th to 18th century), Modern (19th to 20th century), and Contemporary (21st century).
- Some of the prominent features and themes of Telugu literature are:

  - The influence of Sanskrit and Prakrit languages, especially in the ancient and medieval periods.
  - The use of various poetic forms and meters, such as śataka, padya, kanda, gadya, and campu .
  - The development of various literary schools and movements, such as Vīraśaivism, Bhakti, Navya, and Dalit.
  - The expression of various religious, social, cultural, and political aspects of Telugu society and history.
  - The contribution of various eminent writers, such as Nannaya, Tikkana, Srinatha, Potana, Vemana, Annamacharya, Kandukuri Veeresalingam, Gurajada Apparao, Viswanatha Satyanarayana, Sri Sri, and C. Narayana Reddy.
