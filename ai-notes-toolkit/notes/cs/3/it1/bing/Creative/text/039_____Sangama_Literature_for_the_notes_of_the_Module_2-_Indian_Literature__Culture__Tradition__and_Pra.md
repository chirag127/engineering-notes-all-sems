### Sangama Literature

- Sangama literature is the oldest literature to have been composed in the Tamilakam, the ancient region of South India that corresponds to modern Tamil Nadu and parts of Kerala, Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka .
- The word Sangama refers to college, and the commentary of Iraiyanar Agapporul (c. AD 750) alludes to three Sangamas that lasted at long intervals over 9900 years. These were at Then Madurai, Kapalapuram and Madurai respectively.
- The Sangama literature had fallen into oblivion for much of the second millennium of the common era, but were preserved by and rediscovered in the monasteries of Hinduism, particularly those related to Shaivism near Kumbakonam, by colonial-era scholars in the late nineteenth century.
- Broadly, Sangama literature consists of poems, grammar, epics and Tirrukural- all broadly being composed between 200 BCE and 1200 AD.
- The poems are concerned with two main topics: those of the first five collections are on love (akam), and those of the next two are on heroism (puram), including the praise of kings and their deeds.
- The poems are also classified into five landscapes (tinai) based on the mood, season, flora and fauna of the region. These are kurinji (mountainous), mullai (forest), marutam (agricultural), neital (coastal) and palai (desert) .
- The poems are attributed to 473 poets (including some women), besides 102 anonymous pieces. Some of the famous poets are Kapilar, Paranar, Avvaiyar, Nakkirar and Thiruvalluvar.
- The grammar of Sangama literature is based on the Tolkappiyam, the oldest surviving Tamil grammar text, which lays down the rules of phonology, morphology, syntax and semantics .
- The epics of Sangama literature are Silappatikaram, Manimekalai, Civaka Cintamani, Valayapati and Kundalakesi, which narrate the stories of legendary heroes and heroines, their virtues, vices, adventures and fates .
- The Tirrukural is a collection of 1330 couplets on ethics, politics, economics and love, written by Thiruvalluvar, who is considered to be the greatest of the Sangama poets. The Tirrukural is revered as a universal scripture and a masterpiece of Tamil literature .
