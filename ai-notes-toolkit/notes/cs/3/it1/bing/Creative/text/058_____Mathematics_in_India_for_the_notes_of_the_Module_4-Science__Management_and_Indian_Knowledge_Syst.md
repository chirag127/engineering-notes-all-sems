### Mathematics in India

- Mathematics in India is the discipline of mathematics as it developed in the Indian subcontinent from 1200 BCE until the end of the 18th century.
- Mathematics in India is characterized by a rich diversity of topics, methods, and styles, influenced by various cultural, religious, and philosophical traditions.
- Mathematics in India can be divided into four broad periods: the Vedic period (1200-500 BCE), the classical period (400-1200 CE), the medieval period (1200-1600 CE), and the modern period (1600-1800 CE).
- Some of the main achievements of mathematics in India are:
  - The development of the decimal place-value system and the use of zero as a number .
  - The discovery of various algebraic identities, such as the Pythagorean theorem, the quadratic formula, and the binomial theorem.
  - The invention of trigonometry, including the sine, cosine, and tangent functions, and the calculation of accurate values of pi and other constants.
  - The study of geometry, including the construction of geometric figures, the measurement of areas and volumes, and the solution of geometric problems.
  - The exploration of combinatorics, number theory, calculus, and infinite series, including the concepts of permutation, combination, congruence, divisibility, differentiation, integration, and convergence.
  - The creation of mathematical texts, such as the Sulba Sutras, the Aryabhatiya, the Brahmasphutasiddhanta, the Lilavati, and the Siddhanta Siromani, which contain various theorems, proofs, algorithms, and examples .
- Mathematics in India was influenced by and influenced other fields of knowledge, such as astronomy, astrology, medicine, music, art, and architecture .
- Mathematics in India was transmitted to and received from other regions of the world, such as China, Persia, Arabia, and Europe, through trade, travel, and cultural exchange .
