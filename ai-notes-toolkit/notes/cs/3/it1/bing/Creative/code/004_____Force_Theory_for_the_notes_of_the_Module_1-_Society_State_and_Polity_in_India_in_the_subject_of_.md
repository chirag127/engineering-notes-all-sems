### Force Theory

- Force theory is a view on the origin of the state that argues that nations were created through violence and coercion.
- According to this theory, the state originated due to force exerted by the strong over the weak, who established the political relation of command and obedience  .
- The theory implies that war begets the king, and that the state's sovereignty rests ultimately on the force .
- The theory is supported by some historical examples of state formation, such as the Roman Empire, the Mongol Empire, and the British Empire, which expanded through conquest and subjugation of other peoples .
- The theory is criticized for being too simplistic, deterministic, and ethnocentric, as it ignores the role of other factors, such as consent, contract, culture, and cooperation, in the origin and development of the state .
