# Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

This module aims to introduce the students to the rich and diverse Indian Knowledge System (IKS) and its relevance and applications in various domains of science and management. The module will cover the following topics:

- What is IKS and why is it important to study it?
- The historical and cultural context of IKS and its sources and methods of transmission.
- The contributions of IKS to mathematics, astronomy, medicine, metallurgy, architecture, linguistics, logic, aesthetics, and ethics.
- The principles and practices of IKS in management, leadership, governance, and innovation.
- The challenges and opportunities of integrating IKS with modern science and technology.

Some of the learning outcomes of this module are:

- To develop an appreciation and respect for the indigenous knowledge and wisdom of India.
- To understand the scientific and rational basis of IKS and its compatibility with modern science.
- To explore the potential of IKS for solving contemporary problems and creating sustainable solutions.
- To enhance the creativity and critical thinking skills of the students by exposing them to alternative perspectives and paradigms.
- To foster a sense of pride and identity among the students as inheritors and custodians of a rich and diverse cultural heritage.
