### Telugu Literature

Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, a Dravidian language spoken mainly in the states of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana in India. Telugu literature has a rich and diverse history, spanning over a thousand years and covering various genres and forms. Some of the main features and topics of Telugu literature are:

- The earliest extant works of Telugu literature date back to the 11th century CE, when the poet Nannaya translated the Sanskrit epic Mahabharata into Telugu. This marked the beginning of the classical period of Telugu literature, which lasted until the 16th century CE. Some of the prominent poets and writers of this period include Tikkana, Errana, Srinatha, Potana, Allasani Peddana, and Krishnadevaraya.
- The classical period of Telugu literature was characterized by the use of the champu style, which mixed prose and poetry, and the prabandha style, which was a long narrative poem. The themes of the classical works were mainly derived from Hindu mythology, history, and ethics. The śataka (century of verses) was a popular form of poetry that expressed the poet's personal views on various topics. The kavya (poetic composition) was another form of poetry that followed strict rules of meter, rhyme, and figures of speech.
- The modern period of Telugu literature began in the 19th century CE, with the influence of Western education, culture, and literature. The modern Telugu writers experimented with new forms and genres, such as novels, short stories, essays, dramas, and songs. They also addressed contemporary social and political issues, such as nationalism, freedom struggle, social reform, feminism, and Dalit literature. Some of the notable modern Telugu writers are Gurajada Apparao, Kandukuri Veeresalingam, Viswanatha Satyanarayana, Sri Sri, C. Narayana Reddy, and Gunturu Seshendra Sarma.
- Telugu literature has also been influenced by other regional and foreign languages and literatures, such as Tamil, Kannada, Urdu, Persian, Arabic, English, and French. Telugu literature has also influenced and been influenced by other forms of art and culture, such as music, dance, painting, sculpture, and cinema. Telugu literature has been recognized and honored with various awards and honors, such as the Jnanpith Award, the Sahitya Akademi Award, and the Padma Shri Award.
