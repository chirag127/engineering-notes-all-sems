### Pre-Vedic and Vedic Religion

- **Pre-Vedic Religion**: The Pre-Vedic religion of ancient India is found in the Indus Valley or Harappan civilization. Archaeological findings made in prehistoric and protohistoric sites suggest that these people revered both the male and female aspects.

- **Vedic Religion**: Vedic religion, also called Vedism, is the religion of the ancient Indo-European-speaking peoples who entered India about 1500 BCE from the region of present-day Iran. It takes its name from the collections of sacred texts known as the Vedas. Vedism is the oldest stratum of religious activity in India for which there exist written materials.

- **Nature of Religious Beliefs and Practices**: The nature of the religious beliefs and practices of the Aryans is known from the Rig Veda. They believed in many gods like Indra, Varuna, Agni, Surya, and Rudra.

- **Historical Vedic Religion**: The historical Vedic religion (also known as Vedicism, Vedism, or ancient Hinduism and subsequently Brahmanism or Brahminism) constituted the religious ideas and practices among some of the Indo-Aryan peoples of the northwest Indian subcontinent (Punjab and the western Ganges plain) during the Vedic period (1500–500 BCE).
