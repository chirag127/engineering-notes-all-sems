### Six System Indian Philosophy

The Six Systems of Indian Philosophy are:

1. **Nyaya**: Founded by Akshapada Gautama, it is a school that deals with logic and epistemology. It is known for its analysis of logic and its major contribution is its working out in profound detail the means of knowledge known as inference .
2. **Vaisheshika**: Popularly understood as the atomistic school of Indian Philosophy.
3. **Samkhya**: An extremely complex school of philosophy that goes deep into the understanding of the creator and the creation.
4. **Yoga**: Also known as Ashtanga Yoga.
5. **Purva Mimamsa**: One of the six systems of Indian Philosophy.
6. **Uttara Mimamsa or Vedanta**: The term Vedanta means the “conclusion” of the Vedas, the earliest sacred literature of India. It applies to the Upanishads, which were elaborations of the Vedas, and to the school that arose out of the study of the Upanishads.

These systems differ in one way or the other in terms of their concepts, phenomena, laws, and dogmas.
