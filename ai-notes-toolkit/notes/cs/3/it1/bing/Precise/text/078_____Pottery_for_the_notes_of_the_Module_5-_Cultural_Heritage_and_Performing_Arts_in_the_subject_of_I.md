### Pottery in Indian Traditions, Cultural and Society

1. Pottery has been a part of Indian culture since the early settlements of Mehargarh from the Indus Valley Civilization .
2. It is still practiced extensively in India today and plays an important role in studying culture and reconstructing the past .
3. Being an agricultural country, pots for storage of water and grains were in demand, and the real beginning of Indian pottery began with the Indus Valley Civilization .
4. The art of shaping and baking clay articles as pottery, earthenware, and porcelain has continued through the ages .
5. Pottery is among the most timeless living craft traditions of India and is practiced across the length and breadth of the country .
6. A visit to the countryside and “potter’ villages” speaks volumes about the economic importance of this craft .
7. Pottery is one of the most recognizable and tangible elements of Indian art and is still a cultural practice today .
8. It garners a better understanding of present society and makes it possible to visualize the past .
9. It can be used by prehistorians to date excavation sites and identify several different cultures .
