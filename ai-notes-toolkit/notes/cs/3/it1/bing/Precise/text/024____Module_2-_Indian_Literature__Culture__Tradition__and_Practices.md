## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

1. Indian literature has a rich and diverse history, with works in many languages including Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, and Tamil.
2. Indian culture is characterized by its diversity, with many different customs, traditions, and practices found throughout the country.
3. Indian traditions include a wide range of practices, from religious rituals to social customs such as arranged marriages and joint family systems.
4. Indian practices include a variety of art forms, including music, dance, and visual arts, as well as traditional medicine and cuisine.
