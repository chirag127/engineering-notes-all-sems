### Geography for the notes of the Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. India is located in the northern hemisphere and is the seventh largest country in the world.
2. The country is divided into 28 states and 8 union territories.
3. India is surrounded by the Bay of Bengal in the east, the Arabian Sea in the west, and the Indian Ocean to the south.
4. The Himalayan mountain range runs along the northern border of India, separating it from the rest of Asia.
5. The Deccan Plateau is a large plateau in southern India, bounded by the Eastern Ghats to the east and the Western Ghats to the west.
6. The Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Indus are the three major river systems in India.
7. India has a diverse climate, ranging from tropical in the south to temperate in the north.
8. The country is home to a wide variety of flora and fauna, including several endangered species.
9. India is rich in natural resources, including coal, iron ore, manganese, mica, bauxite, and titanium ore.
10. Agriculture is the primary occupation of the majority of the population, with rice, wheat, and pulses being the main crops.
