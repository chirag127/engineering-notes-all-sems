### Purusārtha

- Purusārtha is a key concept in Hinduism, which refers to the four proper goals or aims of a human life .
- The term Purusārtha is a composite Sanskrit word from Purusha (पुरुष) and Artha (अर्थ). Purusha means "spirit", "immaterial essence", or "primaeval human being as the soul and original source of the universe", depending on the darsana, the school of thought .
- The four Purusārthas are the inherent values of the Universe: Artha (economic values), Kama (pleasure), Dharma (righteousness), and Moksha (liberation) .
- The Purusārthas are the blueprint for human fulfillment. Working with them helps you create a satisfyingly balanced, meaningful life at the deepest and most holistic level .
- They offer a way for evaluating your life and making good decisions. Knowing your goals brings meaning to your spiritual practice .
