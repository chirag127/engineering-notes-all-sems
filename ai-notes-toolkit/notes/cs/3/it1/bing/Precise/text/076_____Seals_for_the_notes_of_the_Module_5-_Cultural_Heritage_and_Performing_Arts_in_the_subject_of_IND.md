### Seals for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Seals have been an integral part of Indian traditions and cultural heritage.
- In the Indus Valley civilization, seals were used for the identification of property and to stamp clay on trade goods.
- Seals were decorated with animal figures, such as elephants, tigers, and water buffalos, and have been one of the most commonly discovered artifacts in Indus Valley cities.
- Seals and amulets were sure to come as voyaging objects from India in their initial phase. The ideology behind the offering and making of seals was then deeply ingrained in the Southeast Asian societies.
