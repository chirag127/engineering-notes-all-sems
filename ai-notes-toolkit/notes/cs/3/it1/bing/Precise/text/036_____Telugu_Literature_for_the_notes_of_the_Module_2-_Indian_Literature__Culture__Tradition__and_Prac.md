### Telugu Literature for the notes of the Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, one of the classical languages of India.
- It has a rich history, with the earliest known work being the Andhra Mahabharatam, an 11th-century adaptation of the Mahabharata.
- Telugu literature flourished under the rule of the Vijayanagara Empire in the 15th and 16th centuries, with works such as the Annamacharya's devotional songs and the poetry of Allasani Peddana, known as the "Andhra Kavita Pitamaha" (Grandfather of Telugu Poetry).
- The 19th and 20th centuries saw the emergence of modern Telugu literature, with notable writers such as Gurajada Apparao, Kandukuri Veeresalingam, and Viswanatha Satyanarayana.
- Telugu literature is known for its rich tradition of poetry, with various forms such as padyalu (metrical poetry), vachanaalu (prose poetry), and prabandhamu (narrative poetry).
- It also has a strong tradition of drama, with the Kuchipudi dance-drama being a notable example.
- Telugu literature continues to thrive in the present day, with contemporary writers exploring various themes and styles.
