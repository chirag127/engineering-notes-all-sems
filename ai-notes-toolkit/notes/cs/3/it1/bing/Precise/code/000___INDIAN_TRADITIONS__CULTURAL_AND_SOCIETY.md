# Indian Traditions, Culture, and Society

India is a land of rich culture and tradition which teaches people kindness, generosity, and tolerance. Indian culture differs from place to place as it is a multilingual, multicultural, and multi-ethnic society. Indian culture is a unique blend of modern western culture and historical traditions.

Some of the unique customs and traditions of Indian culture include:

1. **Greeting - The Namaste**: Namaste is a popular way of greeting in India, which translates to ‘I bow to the divine in you’. This respectful way of saying hello, goodbye, and thank you has seen a huge uptake around the world during the Coronavirus outbreak as an alternative to handshakes and hugs .
2. **Festivals & Religion**: India is known for its festive season, with numerous festivals celebrated throughout the year. Religion has historically influenced Indian society on a political, cultural, and economic level. There is a sense of pride associated with the country’s rich religious history as the traditions of Hinduism, Buddhism, Sikhism, and Jainism all emerged out of India .
3. **Family Structure - Joint Families**: Joint families, where multiple generations live together, are common in India. This family structure is based on the principle of mutual support and cooperation.
4. **Symbols - Fasting**: Fasting is a common practice in India, with many people observing fasts for religious or spiritual reasons.
5. **Religious Customs - Holy Cow**: Cows are considered sacred in Hinduism and are revered in Indian society.
6. **Architecture - The Science Behind Temples**: Temples are an important part of Indian culture and architecture, with many ancient temples still standing today.
7. **Marriage - Arranged Marriage System**: Arranged marriages, where families play a role in selecting a spouse for their children, are still common in India.
