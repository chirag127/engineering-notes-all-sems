### Indian Architect

- Indian architecture is rooted in the history, culture, and religion of India.
- Among several architectural styles and traditions, the best-known include the many varieties of Hindu temple architecture and Indo-Islamic architecture, especially Rajput architecture, Mughal architecture, South Indian architecture and Indo-Saracenic architecture.
- Indian architecture, which has evolved through centuries, is the result of socio-economic and geographical conditions.
- Different types of Indian architectural styles include a mass of expressions over space and time, transformed by the forces of history considered unique to India.
- Balkrishna Doshi, an architect who helped bring modernism to his native India, at first collaborating with Le Corbusier and Louis Kahn and then developing his own approach to building in his country. He passed away at the age of 95 in Ahmedabad, India on Tuesday the 24th of January 2023.
