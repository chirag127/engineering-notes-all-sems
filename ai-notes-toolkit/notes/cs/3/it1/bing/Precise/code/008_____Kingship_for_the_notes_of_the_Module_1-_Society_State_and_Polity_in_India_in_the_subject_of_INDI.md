### Kingship for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Kingship in early Medieval India has been studied in detail, with a focus on the Cholas and the Eastern Gangas in the Mahanadi and Kaveri delta regions .
- The king enjoyed all powers of the state, but the main objective was the welfare and protection of society. The polity in Ancient India was the main instrument for social reform and economic progress .
- Hindu kingship has been interpreted in various ways, with some seeing the king as a constitutional monarch presiding over a modern administrative and fiscal structure .
- Manu, considered the father of Indian polity, depicted the systematized growth of society, religion, and polity, and dealt with various aspects of kingship based on Vedic ideals .
- India's foreign policy, as defined by Nehru, was based on Five Principles (Panch Shila): mutual respect for other nations' territorial integrity and sovereignty, nonaggression, noninterference in internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit, and peaceful coexistence .
- The legitimacy of the authority of the king was based on the fundamental idea of Rajdharma, as described in the Mahabharata .
