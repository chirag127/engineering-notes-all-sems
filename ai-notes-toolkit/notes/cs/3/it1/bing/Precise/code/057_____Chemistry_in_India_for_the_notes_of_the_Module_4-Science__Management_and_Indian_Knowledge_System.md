### Chemistry in India

- India has a very ancient tradition of chemistry. In ancient texts, amazing information is available on metals, ores, their quarries, compounds, and alloys. There are also details of hundreds of devices used in chemical experiments in ‘Rasayan Shastra’ which we call ‘chemistry’.
- Chemical techniques in India can be traced back all the way to the Indus valley or Harappan civilization (3rd millennium BCE).
- Alchemy (the older form of chemistry) had made great strides in India. Ayurveda, which used a variety of minerals, also played an important role in the development of chemistry. It developed as iatrochemistry.
- The scope of chemistry as a career option is immense in India. Candidates can pursue degree, diploma, and certificate courses in chemistry at undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral levels.
- Some of the best global universities for chemistry in India are Indian Institute of Science (IISC) - Bangalore; Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) - Bombay; Indian Institute of Technology (IIT).
