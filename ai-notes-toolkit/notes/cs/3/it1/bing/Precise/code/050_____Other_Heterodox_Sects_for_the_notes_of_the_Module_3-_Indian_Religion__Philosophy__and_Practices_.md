### Other Heterodox Sects

1. Heterodox schools of Indian philosophy are those that do not accept the authority of the Vedas.
2. These schools include Buddhism, Jainism, Ajivika, and Charvaka.
3. Almost every school of Indian philosophy is associated with a religious sect.
4. The majority of Asian Indians practice Hinduism, while other major religions include Sikhism, Buddhism, Jainism, Christianity, and Islam.
5. The Ajivikas philosophy held that all things are preordained, and therefore religious or ethical practice has no effect on one's future.
6. The Carvaka school is characterized as a materialistic and aesthetic school of thought.
