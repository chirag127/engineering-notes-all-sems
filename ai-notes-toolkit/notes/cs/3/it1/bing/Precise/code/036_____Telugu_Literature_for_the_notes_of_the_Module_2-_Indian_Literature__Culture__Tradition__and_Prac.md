### Telugu Literature for the notes of the Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, one of the classical languages of India.
- It has a rich history, with the earliest known work being the Andhra Mahabharatam, an 11th-century adaptation of the Mahabharata.
- Telugu literature flourished under the rule of the Vijayanagara Empire in the 15th and 16th centuries, with works such as the Annamacharya's devotional songs and Allasani Peddana's Manu Charitra.
- The 17th century saw the emergence of the Telugu novel, with works such as Kshetrayya's Padams and Tyagaraja's Kirtanas.
- The 19th and 20th centuries saw the rise of social reform movements, which led to the development of modern Telugu literature, with works such as Gurajada Apparao's Kanyasulkam and Sri Sri's Mahaprasthanam.
- Telugu literature continues to thrive today, with contemporary writers exploring a wide range of themes and styles.
