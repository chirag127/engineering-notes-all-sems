### Administration for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Administration refers to the management of the affairs of an organization or a state.
2. In the context of the state, administration involves the implementation of government policies and the management of public affairs.
3. The administration of a state is carried out by a bureaucracy, which is a body of officials appointed to carry out the functions of the government.
4. In India, the administration is divided into central, state, and local levels.
5. The central government is responsible for matters of national importance, such as defense, foreign affairs, and currency.
6. The state governments are responsible for matters within their respective states, such as law and order, education, and health.
7. Local governments, such as municipalities and panchayats, are responsible for local affairs, such as sanitation, water supply, and street lighting.
8. The Indian administrative system is based on the principles of democracy, federalism, and separation of powers.
9. The administration is accountable to the people through the elected representatives in the parliament and the state assemblies.
10. The administration is also subject to judicial review, which ensures that the actions of the administration are in accordance with the law and the constitution.
