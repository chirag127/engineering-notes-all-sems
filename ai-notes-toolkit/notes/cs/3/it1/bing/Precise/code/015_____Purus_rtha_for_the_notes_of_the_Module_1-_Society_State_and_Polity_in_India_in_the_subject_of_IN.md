### Purusārtha

Purusārtha is a fundamental principle of social ethics in Hinduism. It refers to the four proper goals or aims of a human life. These are not only meant to set an ideal objective but also to convey the right means to achieve them. The four Purusārthas are:

1. **Dharma** (righteousness, moral values)
2. **Artha** (prosperity, economic values)
3. **Kama** (pleasure, love, psychological values)
4. **Moksha** (liberation, spiritual values, self-actualization)

Equal emphasis on the four Purusārthas is highly essential for a balanced and healthy social life. History has many instances in its record when balance between individual and society was lost owing to undue emphasis on one Purusārtha or the other.
