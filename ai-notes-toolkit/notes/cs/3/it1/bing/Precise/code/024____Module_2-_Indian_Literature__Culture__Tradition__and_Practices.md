## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

1. Indian literature has a rich and diverse history, with works in many languages including Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, and others.
2. Indian culture is characterized by its diversity, with many different customs, traditions, and practices found throughout the country.
3. Indian traditions include a wide range of practices, such as religious festivals, traditional dance and music, and the use of traditional clothing and jewelry.
4. Indian practices include a focus on family and community, with an emphasis on hospitality and respect for elders.
5. Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices are deeply intertwined, with each influencing and shaping the others.
