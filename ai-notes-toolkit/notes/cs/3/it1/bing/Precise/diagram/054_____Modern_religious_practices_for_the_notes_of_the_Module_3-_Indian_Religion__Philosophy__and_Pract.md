### Modern Religious Practices

India is a country with a diverse range of religious practices and beliefs. According to the 2011 census, the majority of the population practices Hinduism (79.8%), followed by Islam (14.2%), Christianity (2.3%), Sikhism (1.7%), Buddhism (0.7%), and Jainism (0.4%). Zoroastrianism, Sanamahism, and Judaism also have an ancient history in India and each has several thousands of Indian adherents .

- **Hinduism** is the dominant religion in India, with around 80% of the population identifying as Hindu .
- **Islam** is the second most prevalent religion in India, with about 14% of the population adhering to it .
- **Christianity** is practiced by about 2% of the population .
- **Buddhism** is practiced by about 1% of the population .
- **Sikhism**, **Jainism**, and **Zoroastrianism** are also practiced in India, with smaller numbers of adherents .

Religion still plays a significant role in the lives of most Indians, even though India is a secular country . Indians regularly visit their houses of worship and most give to charitable causes . India's religious groups share several religious practices and beliefs, and after living side by side for generations, India's minority groups often engage in practices or hold beliefs that are more closely associated with Hindu traditions than with their own .
