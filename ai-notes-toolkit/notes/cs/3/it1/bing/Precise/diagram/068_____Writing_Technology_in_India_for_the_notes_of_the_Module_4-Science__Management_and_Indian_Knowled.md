### Writing Technology in India

1. Technical writing in India is a lesser known profession which is fast gaining recognition. Majority of the technical writing job vacancies in India are with the software companies and web development companies.
2. There are several top technical writing institutes in India, such as TechnoKraft in Bangalore, which has trained thousands of students in technical writing, instructional design, and software training.
3. Online platforms such as Coursera also offer technical writing courses in India, provided by institutions such as the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology.
4. Digitalization and the widespread proliferation of Roman-alphabet keyboards in India have led to the use of ad hoc Romanizations for transcribing Indian languages on the internet.
5. Technical writing courses are mostly offered as certificate or diploma courses and are mostly pursued by students of science background. The average fee to pursue a Technical writing course ranges from INR 5,000 to INR 40,000.
6. India also has a nostalgic passion for old typewriters, with institutes such as the Umapathi Typewriting Institute teaching typing in three languages – English, Hindi and the local language of Tamil.
