### Marriage

Marriage is a social institution that is recognized by the state and society. In India, marriage is considered a sacred bond between two individuals and their families. It is a union that is not only physical but also emotional, social, and spiritual.

- Marriage is a social institution that is recognized by the state and society.
- In India, marriage is considered a sacred bond between two individuals and their families.
- It is a union that is not only physical but also emotional, social, and spiritual.
- Marriage is an important part of Indian culture and tradition.
- It is a means of establishing a family and continuing the family lineage.
- Marriage is also seen as a means of providing social and economic security.
- In India, arranged marriages are still common, where the families of the bride and groom play a major role in selecting the partner.
- The legal age for marriage in India is 18 for women and 21 for men.
- The Indian constitution prohibits discrimination on the basis of caste, religion, or race in matters of marriage.
- However, caste and religion still play a major role in the selection of marriage partners in many parts of India.
