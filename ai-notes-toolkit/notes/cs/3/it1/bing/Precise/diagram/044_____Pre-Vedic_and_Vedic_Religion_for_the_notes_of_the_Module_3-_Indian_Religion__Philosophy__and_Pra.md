### Pre-Vedic and Vedic Religion

#### Pre-Vedic Religion
- The Pre-Vedic religion of ancient India is found in the Indus Valley or Harappan civilization.
- Archaeological findings made in the prehistoric and protohistoric sites suggest that these people revered both the male and female aspects.

#### Vedic Religion
- Vedic religion, also called Vedism, is the religion of the ancient Indo-European-speaking peoples who entered India about 1500 BCE from the region of present-day Iran.
- It takes its name from the collections of sacred texts known as the Vedas.
- Vedism is the oldest stratum of religious activity in India for which there exist written materials.
- The nature of the religious beliefs and practices of the Aryans is also known from the Rig Veda.
- They believed in many gods like Indra, Varuna, Agni, Surya, and Rudra.
