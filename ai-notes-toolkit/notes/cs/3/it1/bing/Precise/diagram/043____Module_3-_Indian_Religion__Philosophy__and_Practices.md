## Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

1. India is a land of diverse religions and philosophies.
2. Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism are the major religions that originated in India.
3. Hinduism is the oldest and the most widely practiced religion in India.
4. The Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, and Puranas are the sacred texts of Hinduism.
5. Hinduism believes in the concept of reincarnation and the law of Karma.
6. Buddhism was founded by Gautama Buddha in the 6th century BC.
7. The Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path are the central teachings of Buddhism.
8. Jainism was founded by Mahavira in the 6th century BC.
9. The principle of non-violence is the core of Jainism.
10. Sikhism was founded by Guru Nanak in the 15th century AD.
11. The teachings of Sikhism are based on the principles of equality, service, and devotion to God.
12. Yoga and meditation are ancient Indian practices that have gained worldwide popularity.
13. Ayurveda is a traditional system of medicine that originated in India.
14. Indian philosophy includes six schools of thought: Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa, and Vedanta.
15. These schools of thought have contributed to the development of Indian logic, metaphysics, ethics, and epistemology.
