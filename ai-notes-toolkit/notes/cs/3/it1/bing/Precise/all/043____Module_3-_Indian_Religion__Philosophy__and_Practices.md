# Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

1. India is a land of diverse religions, with Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism originating in the country.
2. Hinduism is the oldest and the most widely practiced religion in India, with over 80% of the population identifying as Hindus.
3. Hinduism is a complex religion with a vast array of gods and goddesses, and a multitude of beliefs and practices.
4. The core beliefs of Hinduism include the concepts of Dharma (righteousness), Karma (action and its consequences), and Moksha (liberation from the cycle of birth and death).
5. Buddhism, founded by Siddhartha Gautama, also known as the Buddha, is based on the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path.
6. Jainism, founded by Mahavira, emphasizes non-violence and the attainment of liberation through right knowledge, right faith, and right conduct.
7. Sikhism, founded by Guru Nanak, is based on the teachings of the ten Sikh Gurus and emphasizes the importance of devotion to God and service to humanity.
8. Indian philosophy includes six schools of thought: Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa, and Vedanta.
9. Indian practices include yoga, meditation, Ayurveda, and various forms of art and music.
10. These religions, philosophies, and practices have had a profound impact on Indian culture and have also influenced the world at large.
