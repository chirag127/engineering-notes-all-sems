# The representation of Women in Historical traditions for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Women in ancient India enjoyed high status in society and their condition was good .
- The Ancient and medieval status of women in modern Indian society regarding Equality, Education, Marriage and Family life, Race and Gender, Religion and Culture is maintained or deteriorated .
- There are thousands of communities living in India including all major religions of the world .
- India was one of the first countries in the world to elect a woman as prime minister, and the country currently has several highly influential women politicians .
- Women continue to be significantly underrepresented in the highest political positions .
