### Understanding Gender as a social category for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Gender is a social category that refers to the social and cultural differences between men and women.
- Gender is not the same as sex, which refers to the biological differences between males and females.
- Gender roles and expectations are learned through socialization and vary across cultures and historical periods.
- Gender is a hierarchical system that privileges men over women and reinforces unequal power relations between the two.
- Gender intersects with other social categories such as race, class, and sexuality to produce unique experiences and identities.
- Gender is a dynamic and fluid concept that is constantly being negotiated and redefined.
- Understanding gender as a social category is important for analyzing and addressing issues of inequality and discrimination in society.
