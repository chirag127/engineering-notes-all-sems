# Telugu Literature

Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, one of the classical languages of India. It has a rich history and tradition, with its roots dating back to the 11th century.

## Key Points

- Telugu literature has a rich history and tradition, with its roots dating back to the 11th century.
- Early Telugu literature was heavily influenced by Sanskrit and Prakrit, with many works being translations or adaptations of Sanskrit texts.
- The first known Telugu author was Nannaya Bhattaraka, who translated the Mahabharata into Telugu in the 11th century.
- Telugu literature flourished during the Vijayanagara Empire, with the production of many original works in various genres, including poetry, drama, and prose.
- The 16th century saw the emergence of the Telugu Bhakti movement, with the composition of devotional poetry by poets such as Annamacharya, Ramadasu, and Tyagaraja.
- Modern Telugu literature has been influenced by Western literary forms and ideas, with the emergence of new genres such as the novel and short story.
- Prominent modern Telugu writers include Sri Sri, Viswanatha Satyanarayana, and C. Narayana Reddy.
