# Famous Sanskrit Authors

Sanskrit is one of the oldest languages in the world and has a rich literary tradition. Here is a list of some famous Sanskrit authors and their works:

1. **Valmiki**: He is the author of the epic poem **Ramayana**.
2. **Maharshi Krishndvaipayan Vedvyasa**: He is the author of the epic poem **Mahabharata**.
3. **Bharat Muni**: He is the author of **Natyashastra**, a treatise on the performing arts.
4. **Bhasa**: He is the author of the play **Svapnavasavdatta**.
5. **Kalidasa**: He is one of the greatest Sanskrit poets and dramatists. Some of his famous works include **Abhijnanasakuntalam**, **Meghaduta**, and **Raghuvamsa**.
6. **Ashvaghosha**: He is a famous Sanskrit dramatist.
7. **Shudraka**: He is the author of the play **Mricchakatika (The Clay Cart)**.

These are just a few of the many famous Sanskrit authors. Their works have contributed significantly to the rich literary tradition of India.
