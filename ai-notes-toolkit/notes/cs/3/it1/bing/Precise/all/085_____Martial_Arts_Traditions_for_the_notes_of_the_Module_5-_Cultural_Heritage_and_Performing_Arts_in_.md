# Martial Arts Traditions

Martial arts are codified systems and traditions of combat practices, which are practiced for a variety of reasons – self-defense, competition, physical health and fitness, entertainment, as well as mental, physical, and spiritual development. India has an ancient tradition in diverse martial arts.

1. **Kalaripayattu**: Often known as the ‘Mother Of All Martial Arts’, this 3000-year old art form originating from Kerala, draws inspiration from the raw power, swift movements and sinuous strength of majestic animals – the lion, tiger, elephant, wild boar, snake and crocodile.
2. **Khusti**: The Indian Wrestling is also a part of Indian Martial arts found throughout India.
3. **Thang-Ta**: The ancient Manipuri Martial Art, also known as Huyen Lallong. Swords and spears are used in Manipuri Martial arts, a powerful yet gracefully complex technique.
4. **Lathi**: Lathi is India’s oldest armed martial art. It is also regarded as one of the world’s earliest martial arts weapons.

These are just a few examples of the rich tradition of martial arts in India. There are many more forms and styles, each with its own unique history and techniques.
