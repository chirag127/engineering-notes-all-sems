# Puranas

The Puranas are ancient Indian texts that contain a wide range of topics, particularly about legends and other traditional lore. They are considered to be an important part of Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices.

- The Puranas most eminently represent the deep mythic structuring of Indian civilization .
- They expand upon, modify, and transform the orthodox Brahminism of the Vedas, principally by the introduction of the idea of bhakti or devotion .
- The Puranas also portray interactions between individuals from many cultural traditions .
- They provide information about the foundations, planning, naming, antiquity, growth, and decay of towns and cities, which are very important for the study of the history of urbanization in ancient India .
- Traditionally, a Purana is said to treat five subjects, or “five signs”: the primary creation of the universe, secondary creation after periodic annihilation, the genealogy of gods and patriarchs, the reigns of the Manus (the first humans), and the history of the solar and lunar dynasties .
