# Geography for the notes of the Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Geography is the study of the Earth's physical features, including its landforms, water bodies, climate, and natural resources.
2. India is a country located in South Asia, with a diverse geography that includes the Himalayan mountain range, the Indo-Gangetic Plain, the Thar Desert, and a long coastline.
3. The Himalayas, which form the northern border of India, are the highest mountain range in the world and are home to many peaks over 8,000 meters, including Mount Everest.
4. The Indo-Gangetic Plain is a large, fertile region in northern India that is formed by the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Indus rivers.
5. The Thar Desert, also known as the Great Indian Desert, is located in western India and southeastern Pakistan and is one of the largest deserts in the world.
6. India's coastline stretches for over 7,500 kilometers and is home to many important ports and coastal cities.
7. India's climate varies from tropical in the south to temperate in the north, with a monsoon season that brings heavy rains to many parts of the country.
8. India is rich in natural resources, including coal, iron ore, manganese, mica, bauxite, and titanium ore.
9. The management of these resources is important for the sustainable development of the country.
10. Indian knowledge systems, including traditional practices and beliefs, have played a role in the management of natural resources and the environment.
