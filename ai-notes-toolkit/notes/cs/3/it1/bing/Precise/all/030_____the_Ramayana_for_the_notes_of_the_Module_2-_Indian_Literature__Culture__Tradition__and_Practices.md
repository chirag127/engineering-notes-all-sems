# The Ramayana

The Ramayana is an ancient Indian epic poem that narrates the struggle of the divine prince Rama to rescue his wife Sita from the demon king Ravana. It is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India, the other being the Mahabharata.

1. The Ramayana is attributed to the sage Valmiki, who is believed to have composed the work in Sanskrit.
2. The story of the Ramayana is divided into seven books, or kandas, and tells the story of Rama, the prince of Ayodhya, who is exiled to the forest for 14 years.
3. During his exile, Rama's wife Sita is abducted by the demon king Ravana, and Rama, along with his brother Lakshmana and the monkey god Hanuman, sets out to rescue her.
4. The epic culminates in a battle between Rama and Ravana, in which Rama emerges victorious and rescues Sita.
5. The Ramayana is not just a story, but also an educational medium used for the teaching of ethics and morality.
6. The characters of the Ramayana, particularly Rama, Sita, and Hanuman, are revered and worshipped by millions of Hindus worldwide.
7. The Ramayana has had a profound impact on Indian art, culture, and literature, and continues to be a popular and influential work to this day.
