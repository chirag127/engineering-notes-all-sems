### Theatre

Theatre is a form of performing arts that includes music, dance, and drama. It has been a part of various cultures and traditions globally since ancient times. India, in particular, is a country with a rich cultural and historical heritage, where music, dance, and drama have been an integral part of its culture since the dawn of time .

- **Traditional theatre** is the earliest form of theatre in India. It is closely related to the Natya Shastra, which is the fundamental bible for all classical dances and arts in India. The storytelling was woven with dance and song, for example, Kathak or through paintings and scrolls, such as scroll paintings .

- **Traditional theatre forms** in India are a combination of acting, singing, dance, music, dialogue, and narration. They also play crucial roles in culture and society, such as songs sung during agricultural work or music that is part of a ritual .

- **Theatre genres** in India today include street theatre, modern theater, folk theater, puppet theater, dance-dramas, and traditional theater. Just like the film industry, Indian theater has a long history .

- India has one of the world’s largest collections of songs, music, dance, theatre, folk traditions, performing arts, rites and rituals, paintings and writings that are known as the ‘Intangible Cultural Heritage’ (ICH) of humanity .
