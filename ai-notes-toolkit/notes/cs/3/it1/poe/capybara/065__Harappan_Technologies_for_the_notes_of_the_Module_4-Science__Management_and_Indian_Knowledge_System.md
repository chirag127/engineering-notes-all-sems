### Harappan Technologies

The Harappan civilization was one of the most advanced civilizations of ancient times. They had a deep understanding of science, technology, and management. Here are some of the Harappan Technologies that were used during the Harappan period:

- **Urban Planning:** The Harappans were excellent urban planners. They built their cities on a grid system, with wide streets and well-planned drainage systems. They also had a central marketplace and public buildings.

- **Brick-Making:** The Harappans were skilled at making bricks. They used a mixture of clay and sand to make their bricks, which were then dried in the sun. They also used kilns to make fired bricks, which were stronger and more durable.

- **Metallurgy:** The Harappans were skilled in metallurgy. They used copper and bronze to make tools, weapons, and ornaments. They also used gold and silver for jewelry.

- **Weaving:** The Harappans were skilled weavers. They used cotton and wool to make textiles, which were then dyed in various colors. They also used a variety of weaving techniques, including warp and weft, twill, and tapestry.

- **Pottery:** The Harappans were skilled potters. They used a variety of techniques to make pottery, including wheel-thrown pottery, coil-built pottery, and stamped pottery. They also used a variety of shapes and sizes for their pottery.

- **Agriculture:** The Harappans were skilled farmers. They used irrigation systems to water their crops and they grew a variety of crops, including wheat, barley, millet, and sesame. They also used domesticated animals, such as cows, sheep, and goats.

- **Trade:** The Harappans were skilled traders. They traded with other civilizations, including Mesopotamia, Egypt, and China. They traded goods such as textiles, pottery, metals, and spices.

These are just a few examples of the Harappan Technologies that were used during the Harappan period. The Harappans were an advanced civilization that made significant contributions to science, technology, and management.
