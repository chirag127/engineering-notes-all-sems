### Āshrama or the Stages of Life

Āshrama is a term used in Hinduism to describe the four stages of life. These stages are considered important for the spiritual and moral development of an individual. Here are the four stages of life:

1. Brahmacharya: This is the stage of life where a person focuses on education and learning. It is also known as the stage of celibacy. During this stage, a person is expected to live with a guru and learn about the Vedas and other sacred texts. 

2. Grihastha: This is the stage of life when a person gets married and starts a family. During this stage, a person is expected to fulfill their duties towards their family and society. This stage is considered to be the most important stage of life as it involves the responsibilities of raising a family and contributing to society.

3. Vanaprastha: This is the stage of life when a person starts to withdraw from the world and prepares for spiritual pursuits. During this stage, a person is expected to leave their family and live in a forest or an ashram. This stage is considered to be a preparation for the next stage of life.

4. Sannyasa: This is the stage of life when a person renounces all material possessions and dedicates their life to spiritual pursuits. During this stage, a person is expected to live a life of simplicity and devotion to God. This stage is considered to be the final stage of life, where a person prepares for their journey towards Moksha or liberation from the cycle of birth and death.

These four stages of life are considered to be important for the spiritual and moral development of an individual. They provide a framework for leading a life that is focused on personal growth and spiritual fulfillment.
