### Northern Indian Languages & Literature

Northern India is home to many languages and dialects which have contributed to the rich literary tradition of the region. Here are some of the prominent languages and their literary contributions:

- **Hindi**: Hindi is the most widely spoken language in Northern India and has a rich literary history. It is the official language of India and is written in the Devanagari script. Some of the most famous works in Hindi literature include the Ramcharitmanas by Tulsidas, the works of Premchand, and the poetry of Kabir and Surdas.

- **Urdu**: Urdu is another major language of Northern India and is widely spoken in states like Uttar Pradesh and Bihar. It is written in the Persian script and has a strong influence from Persian and Arabic. Urdu literature is known for its ghazals, nazms, and other forms of poetry. Some of the famous Urdu poets include Mirza Ghalib, Faiz Ahmed Faiz, and Allama Iqbal.

- **Punjabi**: Punjabi is the language of the Punjabi people and is spoken in states like Punjab, Haryana, and Delhi. It is written in the Gurmukhi script and has a rich literary tradition. Punjabi literature is known for its poetry, folktales, and religious texts. Some of the famous Punjabi poets include Waris Shah, Bulleh Shah, and Shiv Kumar Batalvi.

- **Bhojpuri**: Bhojpuri is a dialect spoken in parts of Uttar Pradesh and Bihar and has a unique literary tradition. Bhojpuri literature is known for its folk songs, poetry, and plays. Some of the famous Bhojpuri poets include Bhikhari Thakur, Ramdhari Singh Dinkar, and Manoj Tiwari.

- **Rajasthani**: Rajasthani is the language of the people of Rajasthan and has a rich literary tradition. It is written in the Devanagari script and is known for its folk tales, ballads, and poetry. Some of the famous Rajasthani poets include Kavi Kallol, Kesari Singh Barahath, and Kavi Bhagwandas.

In conclusion, Northern India has a rich literary tradition that is reflected in its many languages and dialects. These languages have contributed to the diverse literary landscape of the region and continue to inspire new works of literature to this day.
