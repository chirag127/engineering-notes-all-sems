### Dance for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

Dance is an integral part of Indian cultural heritage and has been practiced for thousands of years. It is a form of expression that connects the performer with the audience and carries a rich history and symbolism.

Here are some important points to understand about Indian dance:

- Indian dance forms can be broadly classified into two categories: classical and folk. Classical dances have a structured format and follow certain rules and regulations, while folk dances are more free-flowing and are usually performed by the common people.

- Some of the popular classical dance forms in India include Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kuchipudi, Manipuri, Odissi, and Mohiniyattam. Each of these forms has its own unique characteristics, costumes, and music.

- Classical dances are usually performed in temples, courts, and other important cultural events. They are often accompanied by live music and singing.

- Folk dances, on the other hand, are performed in villages and other rural areas. They are usually accompanied by traditional instruments like drums and flutes and are a way for people to celebrate their culture and traditions.

- Indian dance forms are known for their intricate footwork, hand gestures, facial expressions, and body movements. They are also known for their elaborate costumes and makeup, which add to the overall beauty of the performance.

- Many Indian dance forms are based on ancient texts and mythology. For example, Bharatanatyam is based on the Natya Shastra, which is an ancient Indian text on performing arts.

- Indian dance has undergone many changes over the years and has been influenced by various cultures and traditions. Today, it is a vibrant and dynamic art form that continues to evolve and grow.

In conclusion, Indian dance is a rich and diverse art form that reflects the country's cultural heritage and traditions. Understanding the history and significance of Indian dance can help us appreciate and preserve this important aspect of our cultural identity.
