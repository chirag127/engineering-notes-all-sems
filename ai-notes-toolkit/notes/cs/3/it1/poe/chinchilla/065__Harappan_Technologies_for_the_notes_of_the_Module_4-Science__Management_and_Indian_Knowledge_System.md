### Harappan Technologies

The Harappan Civilization, also known as the Indus Valley Civilization, was one of the earliest and most advanced civilizations in the world. They developed and used a variety of technologies that helped them to thrive and establish a complex society. Here are some of the key technologies used by the Harappans:

- **Urban Planning**: The Harappans were experts in urban planning and their cities were built on grid patterns with streets and buildings aligned to the cardinal directions. They had a sophisticated drainage system and public baths which were connected to underground sewer systems.

- **Agricultural Technology**: The Harappans were skilled in agriculture and used advanced techniques such as crop rotation, irrigation, and terracing to increase crop yields. They also used domesticated animals such as cattle, sheep, and goats for ploughing and transportation.

- **Metallurgy**: The Harappans were skilled in metallurgy and produced a wide range of copper and bronze objects such as tools, weapons, and ornaments. They also used gold and silver for jewelry and other decorative objects.

- **Crafts and Textiles**: The Harappans were known for their craftsmanship and produced a variety of objects such as pottery, beads, and seals. They also produced high-quality cotton and woolen textiles, which were traded throughout the region.

- **Transportation**: The Harappans were skilled in transportation and had a well-developed system of roads and waterways. They used boats and ships for trade and transportation of goods.

- **Writing System**: The Harappans had a unique writing system known as the Indus Script, which has not yet been fully deciphered. This writing system was used on seals, pottery, and other objects.

Overall, the Harappans were a highly advanced civilization that made significant contributions to the fields of technology and science. Their innovations and achievements continue to inspire and fascinate researchers and scholars to this day.
