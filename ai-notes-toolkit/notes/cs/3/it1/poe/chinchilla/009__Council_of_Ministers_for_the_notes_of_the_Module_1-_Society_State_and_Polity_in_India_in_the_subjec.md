### Council of Ministers

The Council of Ministers is an important part of the Indian Government. It is responsible for the administration of the country and assists the Prime Minister in decision-making. In this section, we will discuss the composition, functions, and powers of the Council of Ministers.

#### Composition of the Council of Ministers

- The Council of Ministers is headed by the Prime Minister, who is appointed by the President of India.
- The Council of Ministers includes Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State, and Deputy Ministers.
- The Cabinet Ministers are the senior-most members of the Council of Ministers and are responsible for major policy decisions.
- The Ministers of State and Deputy Ministers assist the Cabinet Ministers in their work.

#### Functions of the Council of Ministers

- The Council of Ministers is responsible for the administration of the country.
- It advises the President in the exercise of his/her functions.
- It formulates and implements policies related to various sectors such as agriculture, education, healthcare, defense, etc.
- It prepares bills and presents them in the Parliament for approval.
- It also oversees the implementation of various schemes and programs launched by the government.

#### Powers of the Council of Ministers

- The Council of Ministers has the power to make major policy decisions.
- It can recommend the dissolution of the Parliament to the President.
- It can advise the President on the appointment of judges to the Supreme Court and High Courts.
- It can recommend the imposition of emergency in the country.
- It can also recommend the dismissal of a State Government to the President.

In conclusion, the Council of Ministers plays a crucial role in the governance of India. It is responsible for the administration of the country and assists the Prime Minister in decision-making. The Council of Ministers has the power to make major policy decisions and recommend various measures to the President.
