### Pyrotechnics in India

- Ancient India had advanced knowledge of pyrotechnics and explosive materials.
- The formulae for various pyrotechnics and combustibles were described in several treatises like Agni Purana, Vrikshayurveda, etc.
- Saltpetre or potassium nitrate was produced by leaching the earth under old buildings and treating the leachate. Charcoal and sulfur were also commonly available materials.
- Incendiary arrows were used in warfare. They contained a highly inflammable mixture of lac, oil, saltpeter, etc.
- Smoke screens were created using green bamboo that produced thick smoke when burnt.
- Rockets were used for signalling and diversionary purposes as well as warfare. The Mongol invaders and the British forces used Indian technologies of rocketry.
- The festival of Diwali even today exhibits the Indian expertise in pyrotechnics by the bursting of crackers and rockets.
