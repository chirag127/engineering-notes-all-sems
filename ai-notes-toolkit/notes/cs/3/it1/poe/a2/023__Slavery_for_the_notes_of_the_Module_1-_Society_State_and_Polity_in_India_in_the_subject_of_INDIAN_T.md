### Slavery

- Slavery was prevalent in ancient India and slaves were treated as properties of their masters.
- Slaves had no rights and had to work under the command of their masters.
- People often became slaves as prisoners of war, debt defaulters, or those born to slave parents.
- The Manusmriti lays down rules regarding the treatment, rights, and liberation of slaves. It classifies slaves into different categories based on the ways they became slaves.
- The practice of slavery declined after the Muslim conquests and the British banned slavery in India in 1843. However, bonded labour continues in India in disguised forms till today.
- The caste system in ancient India also relegated certain communities like Dalits and Adivasis to a position similar to slaves as they had limited rights and had to serve the upper castes.
