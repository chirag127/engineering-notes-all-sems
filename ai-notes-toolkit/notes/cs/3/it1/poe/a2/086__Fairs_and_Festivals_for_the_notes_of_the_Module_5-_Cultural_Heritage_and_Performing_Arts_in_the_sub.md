### Fairs and Festivals

- Indian fairs and festivals are rooted in tradition and are an integral part of Indian culture. They are celebrated with great pomp and show.
- Some popular fairs and festivals in India are:
- Kumbh Mela: It is held every 12 years at one of the four holy sites namely Prayagraj, Haridwar, Ujjain and Nashik. It is the largest gathering of pilgrims in the world. Devotees gather to take a holy dip in the Ganges.
- Pushkar Mela: It is held every year in Pushkar, Rajasthan. It is a livestock fair where camels, horses, and cattle are traded. It also has various cultural events and activities.
- Hornbill Festival: It is held every year in the first week of December in Kohima, Nagaland. It showcases the rich cultural heritage of Nagaland through folk dances, music, handicrafts, and food.
- Onam: It is a harvest festival celebrated in Kerala. It marks the homecoming of the legendary King Mahabali. People decorate their homes with flowers and rangoli, wear new clothes and enjoy Onam Sadhya - a vegetarian feast.
- Bihu: It is a set of three festivals celebrated in Assam. Bhogali Bihu or Magh Bihu is a harvest festival, Rongali Bihu or Bohag Bihu marks the Assamese New Year and Kongali Bihu or Kati Bihu is a sombre festival. They are celebrated with feasting, dancing and traditional music.
