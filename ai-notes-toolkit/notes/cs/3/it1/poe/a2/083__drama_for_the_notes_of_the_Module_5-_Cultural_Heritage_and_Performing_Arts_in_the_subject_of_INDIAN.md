### Drama
- Indian theatre has a long and rich tradition spanning over 2000 years.
- Sanskrit drama originated in the 1st century CE. Three famous Sanskrit playwrights were Bhasa, Kalidasa, and Bhavabhuti. Their plays dealt with religious and moral themes from Hindu epics and Puranas.
- Tamil Nadu has a strong legacy of folk and traditional drama forms, such as Terukkuttu (street theatre), which uses music, songs, dance, and dialogue. Shadow puppetry is also popular in Tamil Nadu.
- Koodiyattam from Kerala is one of the oldest surviving theatrical forms in India. It originated in temples and courts and incorporates dance, music, and dialogue. The performances are lengthy and elaborate.
- Yakshagana from Karnataka combines dance, music, dialogue, costume, make-up, and stage techniques. The stories are drawn from Hindu epics and Puranas.
- Among modern Indian theatre forms are realistic theatre, as pioneered by IPTA, and experimental and avant-garde theatre. Theatre continues to be a popular art form in India, highlighting social issues and providing entertainment.
