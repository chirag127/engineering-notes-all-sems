### Music for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Indian classical music has its roots in Vedic literature and Hinduism. The Samaveda is a compilation of mantras and hymns that are typically chanted or sung.
- The two main traditions of Indian classical music are Carnatic music, which is prevalent in South India, and Hindustani music, which is prevalent in North India.
- Raga and tala are two main concepts in Indian classical music. A raga is a melodic structure, while a tala refers to the rhythmic structure. Performances are typically improvised and based on variations in melody and rhythm.
- Some of the major musical instruments used in Indian classical music include the sitar, sarod, veena, flute, and tabla. The sitar and veena are popular melodic instruments, while the tabla is a popular percussion instrument.
- Prominent composers of Indian classical music include Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri, and Hariprasad Chaurasia. Prominent performers include Ravi Shankar, Ali Akbar Khan, MS Subbulakshmi, and Zakir Hussain.
- Indian classical music has a very long tradition and history. However, its patronage has declined due to the popularity of other genres like Bollywood music. Efforts are being made to preserve and continue this tradition through education and performances.
