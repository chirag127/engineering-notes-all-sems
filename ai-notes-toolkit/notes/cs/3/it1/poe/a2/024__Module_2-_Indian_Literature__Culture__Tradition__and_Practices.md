## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

1. Indian Literature
- Vedas and Upanishads - The earliest literary compositions in India dating back to 1500 BCE. They contain hymns, prayers, mythological accounts, and philosophical discussions.
- Ramayana and Mahabharata - Two major Sanskrit epics dating back to around 500 BCE narrating the historical legends of Hindu deities and kings.
- Bhagavad Gita - A 700-verse Hindu scripture that is part of the Mahabharata, containing a conversation between Krishna and Arjuna on the topics of dharma and the purpose of life.

2. Indian Culture
- Family and marriage - Family and extended family play an important role. Marriages are often arranged by families based on matching social status and cultural compatibility.
- Cuisine - Diverse variety influenced by various regions and religions. Common ingredients include spices, lentils, rice, vegetables, and dairy. Flatbreads such as naan or roti are staple foods.
- Clothing - Includes saree for women and dhoti or lungi for men. Other styles include salwar kameez, sherwani, and kurta-pyjama. Clothes are often made of cotton and silk and adorned with tribal patterns or religious symbols.
- Festivals - Diverse range of festivals celebrating harvest, religion, and culture. Popular Hindu festivals include Diwali, Holi, and Onam. Other significant festivals include Eid al-Fitr, Christmas, and Buddha Jayanti.

3. Indian Traditions and Practices
- Religion - Predominantly Hindu, with large Muslim, Christian, Sikh, and other religious minorities. Hinduism emphasizes dharma, karma, and rebirth, with many deities worshipped in temples.
- Art - Includes styles such as Mughal miniature painting, Tanjore painting, Madhubani art, and Rajasthani art. Art is often religious or decorative in nature depicting deities, nature, and royal courts.
- Music and dance - Classical Indian music is melodic, with instruments such as the sitar, tabla, and sarod. Popular dance styles include Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, and Manipuri. Dance is often a spiritual expression or performance art.
- Yoga and Ayurveda - Yoga incorporates physical and mental disciplines to achieve a state of peace. Ayurveda is a system of traditional medicine using herbs, massage, and yoga to achieve balance and wellness. Both are popular aspects of health and wellness.
