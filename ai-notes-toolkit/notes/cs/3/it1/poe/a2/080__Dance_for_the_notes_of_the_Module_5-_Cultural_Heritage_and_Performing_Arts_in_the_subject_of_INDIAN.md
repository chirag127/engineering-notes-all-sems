### Dance

- Dance is a performing art form consisting of purposefully selected sequences of human movement. This movement has aesthetic and symbolic value, and is acknowledged as dance by performers and observers within a particular culture.
- Dance can be categorized and described by its choreography, by its repertoire of movements, or by its historical period or place of origin.
- Indian dance incorporates intricate footwork performed with complete limb synchronization and subtle facial expressions. The gestures of hand and body movements are very refined, intricate and artistically appealing.
- Classical Indian dance forms are traditionally performed by female dancers supported by musicians who sit on the stage. The verbal accompaniment in the form of religious verses or lyrics from ancient Indian texts enhance the visual appeal of dance performances.
- There are numerous classical Indian dance forms, including:
› Bharatanatyam from Tamil Nadu
› Kathak from North India
› Kathakali from Kerala
› Kuchipudi from Andhra Pradesh
› Manipuri from Manipur
› Mohiniyattam from Kerala
› Odissi from Odisha
- These dance forms have been preserved through tradition and are learned and performed in ceremonial or artistic contexts. They are epitomes of grace, elegance, harmony and rhythmic beauty. The costumes and elaborate jewellery add a visual appeal while the facial expressions and hand gestures bring out a deep sense of bhava or emotions.
