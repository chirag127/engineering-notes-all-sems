### Socio religious reform movement of 19th century

- The 19th century saw several socio-religious reform movements in India as a response to the rigid social customs and conventions that had become outdated.
- The movements called for removal of social evils like sati, child marriage and the caste system and promoted widow remarriage and women's education.
- Some important reformers and their contributions:
- Raja Ram Mohan Roy: Founded Brahmo Samaj in 1828 which denounced idolatry and aimed to purify Hinduism. Campaigned against sati and child marriage.
- Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar: Worked for widows' rights and remarriage. Proposed the Widow Remarriage Act of 1856. Contributed to women's education.
- Swami Dayananda Saraswati: Founded Arya Samaj in 1875. Reinterpreted the Vedas to oppose idol worship and ritualism. Condemned the caste system and untouchability. Promoted equal rights for women in religion and education.
- Jyotirao Phule: Campaigned against the caste system and untouchability. Started schools for girls and backward castes. Worked for widow remarriage and agriculture development.
- Maharshi Karve: Worked for the upliftment of widows and their remarriage. Started a widow home in Pune. Also worked for removing untouchability and promoting women's education.
