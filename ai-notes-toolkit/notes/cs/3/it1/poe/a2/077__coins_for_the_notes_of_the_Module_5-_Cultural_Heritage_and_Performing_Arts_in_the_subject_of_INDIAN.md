### Coins

- Coins are one of the important sources to understand economic, social, cultural history of a region.
- Punch marked coins were the earliest coins in India made of silver and copper. They were unearthed from many parts of the country and are mostly dated around 6th century BCE to 1st century BCE.
- Issues of the Indo-Greeks, Kushans and Guptas have different motifs and inscriptions which reflect the cultural elements of these dynasties and influence of foreign tribes and cultures.
- Gold coins were issued by Guptas which indicate flourishing trade and commerce during this period.
- Coinage of the medieval period reflects the patronage of different religions like Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism. Many rulers highlighted their religious affiliation through the motifs and inscriptions on the coins.
- Study of coins provides information about the political, dynastic, economic, trade, artistic development and cultural contacts established by various rulers and states. They are valuable sources to reconstruct the history of a region.
