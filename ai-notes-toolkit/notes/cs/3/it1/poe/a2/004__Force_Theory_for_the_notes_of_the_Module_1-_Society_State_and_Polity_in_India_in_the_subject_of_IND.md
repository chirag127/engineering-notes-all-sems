### Force Theory for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- According to the force theory, the state came into existence due to the use of force. The powerful people in the society used military force to establish their authority over others and created the state.
- The proponents of this theory argue that the earliest states were the results of conquest, and the first kings were conquerors and military leaders.
- The force theory gives importance to violence and warfare in the origin of the state. It ignores the cooperation and social contract between the people and the rulers.
- The theory was criticized as it could not explain the continuity of the state and the willingness of people to obey the state. The use of force alone cannot ensure a continuous political order.
- In conclusion, while military power was important in the formation of early states, it was not the sole factor. Other social, economic and religious factors were also responsible for the origin and continuation of the state. The force theory provides a partial explanation of the origin of the state.
