### Evolutionary Theory for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. According to Evolutionary theory, society and state evolved from simple to complex forms.
2. There are theories of Social Evolution - Unilinear Theory and Multilinear Theory.
3. Unilinear Theory - There is only one line of development. Societies go through stages from primitive to modern. e.g. Theory of Auguste Comte.
4. Multilinear Theory - There can be various lines of development and evolution can take different routes in different societies depending on circumstances. e.g. Theory of Leslie White, Julian Steward.
5. The theories explain evolution of family, marriage, etc. from primitive to modern forms.
6. They explain evolution of the state from primitive tribe to modern nation state.
7. They explain change in terms of increasing division of labour, social differentiation and growth of technology.
8. However, these theories are criticized as they treat societies as homogeneous, ignore cultural differences and view change as inevitable and unidirectional.
