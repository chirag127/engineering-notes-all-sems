### Challenges faced by Women

- Lack of equal rights: Women face inequality in legal rights as citizens. They have lesser property and voting rights and underrepresentation in policy-making.
- Limited access to resources and opportunities: Women have limited access to education, jobs and control over financial resources which makes them dependent on men and vulnerable to abuse and exploitation.
- Domestic and public violence: Women face high rates of domestic violence and assault which is exacerbated by their limited rights and opportunities to seek justice and protection.
- Patriarchal cultural norms: Deeply entrenched patriarchal cultural practices place women in subordinate roles and restrict their freedom and mobility leading to their mistreatment and abuse.
- Burden of unpaid care work: Women bear a disproportionate burden of unpaid care work like household chores and childcare which limits their ability to pursue economic opportunities and leisure.
