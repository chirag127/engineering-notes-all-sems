## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

- Ancient Indian sciences like astronomy, mathematics, medicine, metallurgy, etc. were quite advanced for their time.
- Indian astronomers proposed that the earth was round and revolved around the sun, long before western science discovered these concepts.
- The concept of zero and the decimal number system were invented in India. The decimal place value system and the invention of zero enabled ancient Indians to make astronomical calculations and develop advanced geometry.
- Ancient Indian medicine or Ayurveda is one of the oldest medical systems. It emphasized on maintaining health through exercise, yoga, meditation and herbal medicines.
- Ancient Indian metallurgists had developed the process of steel making and production of high quality steel. The famous Iron Pillar of Delhi is a testimony to the high level of metallurgical skill achieved in ancient India.
- The Arthashastra by Kautilya is one of the most comprehensive treatises on statecraft, economic policy, and military strategy ever written. It deals with principles of management, public finance, and international relations.
