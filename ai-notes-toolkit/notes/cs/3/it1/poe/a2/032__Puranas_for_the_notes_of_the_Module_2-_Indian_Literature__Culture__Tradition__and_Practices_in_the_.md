### Puranas for the notes of the Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Puranas are ancient Hindu religious texts which contain narratives about the origin of the universe, genealogies of kings, heroes, sages, and deities.
- There are 18 Mahapuranas which are divided into three groups of six - each emphasizing a particular deity: Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva.
- Puranas contain stories about the creation of the world, the origin of evil, and the destruction and recreation of the world. They also contain poetical legends meant to convey spiritual truths.
- Puranas contain accounts of legendary dynasties of kings and their deeds and also preserve some historical records. However, these records are often intermingled with mythical and supernatural elements.
- Puranas also contain descriptions of cosmology, philosophy, genealogy, migration of races, ages of humankind, and duties of the four stages of life - thus providing a glimpse into the Hindu traditions, cultural and societal practices.
- The Puranas are important sources of information about Hindu beliefs and practices and also reflect the diversity of cultural elements in ancient and medieval India. They have influenced dance, music, sculpture, and drama in India.
