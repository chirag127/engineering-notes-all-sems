### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

1. Yoga and Meditation: Yoga is one of the most famous Indian cultural exports to the world. It is a mental and physical discipline which originated in India. Meditation is also widely practiced all over the world which originates from the ancient Indian traditions.

2. Ayurveda: Ayurveda is the traditional Indian system of medicine which utilizes herbs and natural remedies to prevent and treat illnesses. It is one of the oldest systems of medicine and has gained a lot of popularity worldwide in recent years due to its holistic approach and lesser side effects.

3. Indian Cuisine: Indian food is popular all over the world due to its use of spices and herbs and vegetarian as well as non-vegetarian options. Dishes like curry, biryani, naan, samosa, etc have gained a lot of popularity globally. The aroma of Indian spices is recognizable anywhere in the world.

4. Music and Dance: India has a rich tradition of classical music and dance forms like Hindustani and Carnatic music, Bharatnatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Manipuri, etc. These art forms have intricate mudras and techniques and are recognized worldwide for their cultural significance. Many popular western musicians have been influenced by Indian classical music.

5. Philosophy and Spirituality: Ancient Indian scriptures and teachings of philosophers like Vedanta, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, etc have propagated many philosophical and spiritual ideas which emphasize on moral values, purpose of life, and unity of existence. These teachings have influenced thinkers and philosophers all over the world.

6. Festivals: There are many Indian festivals which are celebrated widely across the world, such as Holi, Diwali, Onam, Pongal, etc. These festivals showcase Indian culture and have been adopted in other countries also where there are Indian communities. The colorful celebrations and traditions associated with these festivals attract many non-Indians as well.
