### Harappan Script

- The Harappan civilization flourished around 2600 BCE to 1900 BCE in the Indus Valley region.
- The Harappans had a writing system which used pictographic symbols and had around 400-600 symbols. However, the script has not yet been deciphered.
- The script was written from right to left and included symbols for numerals as well. The numerals were written vertically.
- The script was mainly used for economic documentation to record lists, inventory, transactions, etc. No stone or clay tablets with long continuous texts have been found.
- Scholars believe the script may consist of logographic, syllabic, or alphabetic symbols. Efforts are ongoing to decipher the script to gain insights into the Harappan language and society.
- The lack of bilingual inscriptions or lengthy texts has made decipherment challenging. Decipherment may open doors to understanding the roots of Indian languages and civilization.
