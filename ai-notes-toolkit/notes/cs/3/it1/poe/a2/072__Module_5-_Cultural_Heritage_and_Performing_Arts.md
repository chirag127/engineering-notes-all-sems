## Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts

1. Cultural Heritage refers to the legacy of physical artifacts and intangible attributes of a group or society that are inherited from past generations, maintained in the present and bestowed for the benefit of future generations. This includes monuments, structures, places, significant objects, practices and living expressions linked to historical events or traditions. Some examples are -

    - Monuments like Taj Mahal, Red Fort, etc.
    - Temples, Churches, Mosques, etc.
    - Festivals like Diwali, Christmas, Eid, etc.
    - Art forms like Painting, Sculpting, Dance, Music, etc.
    - Handicrafts, textiles, etc. showcasing traditional skills and techniques.

2. Performing Arts refer to art forms like music, dance, opera, drama, magic shows, etc. which are performed live in front of an audience. Some examples are -

    - Classical Dance forms like Bharatnatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Kuchipudi, etc.
    - Theatre and Drama like Nautanki, Jatra, Ramleela, etc.
    - Folk Music and Dance forms from different regions of the country.
    - Instruments like Sitar, Tabla, Flute, etc.

3. The cultural heritage and performing arts of a country reflect its history, cultural practices and artistic traditions. They bring people together, foster creativity and also contribute to the economy through tourism and employment. However, many art forms are declining due to lack of patronage and changing values. Thus, it is important to preserve and promote our cultural heritage and performing arts.
