### Textile Technology in India

- India has a long history of textile production and manufacturing. Indian textiles were exported to other parts of the world for thousands of years.
- The Indus Valley civilization of ancient India from 3300 BCE grew cotton and transformed it into dyed fabrics. Evidence of woven cotton fabrics have been found at excavation sites like Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro.
- India was already exporting textiles by 500 BCE to Greece and Rome. Fine quality cotton muslins were produced in ancient India from places like Dhaka and Arni.
- The spinning wheel was invented in ancient India and reached Europe from India by the Middle Ages. The earliest archaeological evidence of the spinning wheel comes from excavations in ancient Gandhara (present-day Pakistan and Afghanistan) dated to roughly the 4th century BCE.
- The handlooms of India produced very high-quality fabrics popular all over the world. Indian artisans perfected complex techniques of weaving, dyeing, printing and embroidery. Each region developed their own unique style of fabrics, motifs and patterns.
- The modern Indian textile industry faces challenges from mechanization and powerlooms but handlooms still survive today due to the specialized skills and artistic quality of handwoven fabrics. The Indian handloom industry is the second largest employment sector in India and meets the demands of fashion and textile artisans all over the world.
