### Buddhist And Jain Literature in Pali,Prakrit And Sanskrit

- Buddhist literature was composed in Pali, the language of Theravada Buddhism. Important Buddhist texts in Pali include:
-- The Vinaya Pitaka: Rules for monks.
-- The Sutta Pitaka: Discourses of the Buddha. Includes key texts like the Dhammapada.
-- The Abhidhamma Pitaka: Philosophical and psychological teachings.

- Jain literature was composed in Prakrit and Sanskrit. Key texts include:
-- The Agamas: Collections of discourses in Prakrit.
-- The Tattvartha Sutra by Umaswati: A major Jain philosophical text in Sanskrit. It systematizes Jain thought.
-- The Purvas and the Angas: Other important Jain texts focused on philosophy, ethics, and biographies of saints.

- Both Buddhist and Jain texts focused on topics like the principles of karma and liberation, the importance of moral behavior and ascetic practices, critiques of Vedic religion, and biographies of key religious figures. They provide a window into the worldviews, practices, and societies of these ancient Indian religious and philosophical movements.
