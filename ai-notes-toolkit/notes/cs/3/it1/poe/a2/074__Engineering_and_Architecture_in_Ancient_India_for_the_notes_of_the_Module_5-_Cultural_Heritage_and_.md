### Engineering and Architecture in Ancient India

- Ancient India was renowned for its architectural brilliance and engineering marvels. Many structures have withstood the test of time and continue to inspire awe.
- Indus Valley Civilization (3300 BCE - 1300 BCE): The people of the Indus Valley Civilization had remarkable engineering skills. They built planned cities with fire-bricks, a sewage system and irrigation canals.
- Mauryan Empire (322 BCE - 185 BCE): The Mauryan empire saw remarkable feats of engineering such as the Grand Trunk Road stretching 1500 miles and the polished stone pillars at Kumhrar. The Ashoka Pillar at Delhi is a testament to the metallurgical skills of ancient India as it is made of seamless wrought iron.
- Gupta Empire (320 CE - 550 CE): The Gupta period marked a golden age for Indian architecture. Distinctive monuments with ornate pillars and sculptures were built such as the Dashavatara Temple and the cave temples of Ellora and Elephanta. The Iron Pillar of Delhi dating back to 400 CE is another example of India's sophisticated metallurgy.
- Vijayanagara Empire (1336 CE - 1646 CE): The Vijayanagara empire left behind magnificent stone temples and monuments such as the Virupaksha Temple and Vittala Temple. These structures had unique architectural styles and water management systems. The world's largest stone monolithic statue (Gomateshwara) was also built during this period.
