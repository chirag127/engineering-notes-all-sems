### Seals for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY.

- Temple architecture and sculpture: Indian temple architecture and sculpture evolved over two millennia with distinctive regional variations and styles. Prominent examples include the Sun temple at Konark, the Khajuraho temples, etc.
- Painting traditions: Indian painting traditions include fresco paintings, miniature paintings like Mughal miniatures, etc. The Ajanta and Ellora caves contain fresco paintings from ancient India.
- Musical traditions: India has a long tradition of Indian classical music including the two main traditions of Carnatic and Hindustani music. Folk music traditions are also popular and diverse in various regions of India.
- Dance traditions: Classical Indian dance forms like Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Manipuri, etc. have a long historical tradition. Folk dance forms are also numerous and diverse, varying with regions.
- Traditional crafts: India has a rich tradition of handicrafts and craft forms like weaving, pottery, jewelry making, etc. Many craft forms have historical significance and reflect cultural traditions of the region.
- Traditional theatre: Traditional Indian theatre includes forms like Sanskrit theatre, folk theatre, etc. Notable theatre forms are Nautanki, Bhavai, Yakshagana, Theyyam, etc. which incorporate music, dance, and dialogue.
- Traditional cuisine: Indian cuisine is diverse, reflecting the country's diverse regions and cultures. It uses a variety of spices and cooking techniques like steaming, boiling, etc. Cuisine varies based on religious and regional differences.
