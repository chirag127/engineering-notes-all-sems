
### Conditions’ of the Welfare of Societies 

1. The Indian Constitution provides for the welfare of the people by guaranteeing justice, liberty, equality and fraternity. 
2. It also ensures the dignity of the individuals and the unity and integrity of the nation. 
3. It ensures the right to life, liberty, equality and freedom of expression. 
4. It also provides for the protection of minorities and vulnerable sections of the society. 
5. It also provides for the promotion of international peace and security. 
6. The Indian Constitution also provides for the protection of the environment. 
7. It also provides for the promotion of scientific temper and humanism. 
8. The Indian Constitution also provides for the promotion of social, economic and political justice. 
9. It also provides for the protection of the right to freedom of religion. 
10. It also provides for the promotion of gender equality and the empowerment of women.
