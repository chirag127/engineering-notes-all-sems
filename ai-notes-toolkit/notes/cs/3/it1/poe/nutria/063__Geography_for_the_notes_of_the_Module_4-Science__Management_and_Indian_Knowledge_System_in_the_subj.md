
### Geography

* India is a large country with an area of 3,287,263 square kilometers. It is the seventh largest country in the world.
* India is surrounded by the Arabian Sea, the Bay of Bengal and the Indian Ocean.
* India is divided into 29 states and 7 union territories.
* India has a diverse geography, ranging from the Himalayas in the north to the tropical rainforests of the south.
* India has a wide variety of climates, ranging from tropical in the south to temperate in the north.
* India has a wide variety of natural resources, including coal, iron ore, petroleum, natural gas, bauxite, manganese, mica, limestone, and arable land.
* India is home to a wide variety of flora and fauna, including tigers, leopards, elephants, rhinoceroses, and many species of birds and reptiles.
* India has a long and varied history, stretching back to the Indus Valley civilization of the third millennium BCE.
* India has a rich cultural heritage, with many religions, languages, and customs.
