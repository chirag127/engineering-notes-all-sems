
### Four-class Classification for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Political System: This class covers the structure and functioning of the political system in India. It includes the Constitution of India, the various organs of the government, the role of the judiciary, the electoral system and the functioning of the various political parties. 

2. Social System: This class covers the various social institutions and organizations in India. It includes the family, the caste system, the various religious communities, the tribal communities and their rights, the various social movements and the various social organizations. 

3. Economic System: This class covers the economic system of India. It includes the various economic policies, the agricultural sector, the industrial sector, the banking system and the various economic reforms. 

4. Cultural System: This class covers the culture of India. It includes the various cultural aspects such as literature, art, music, dance, theatre, architecture and the various religious and spiritual practices.
