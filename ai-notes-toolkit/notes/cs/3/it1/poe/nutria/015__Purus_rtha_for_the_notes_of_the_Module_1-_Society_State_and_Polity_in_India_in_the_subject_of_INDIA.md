
### Purusārtha for the Notes of the Module 1 - Society State and Polity in India in the Subject of Indian Traditions, Cultural and Society

1. Purusārtha is a Sanskrit term meaning “aims of life”. It is an important concept in Hinduism and is closely related to the four goals of life.

2. The four Purusārthas are Dharma (righteousness), Artha (material prosperity), Kama (pleasure) and Moksha (liberation).

3. Dharma is the moral and ethical foundation of society. It is the foundation of Hinduism and is closely related to the caste system.

4. Artha is the pursuit of material wealth and prosperity. It is closely related to the concept of economic development.

5. Kama is the pursuit of pleasure and sensual gratification. It is closely related to the concept of leisure and recreation.

6. Moksha is the pursuit of liberation from the cycle of birth and death. It is closely related to the concept of spiritual enlightenment.

7. The four Purusārthas are closely intertwined and are interdependent. Dharma is the foundation of Artha, Artha is the foundation of Kama, and Kama is the foundation of Moksha.
