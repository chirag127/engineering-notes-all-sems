
### Painting in Indian Traditions, Cultural and Society

* Indian painting has a long history, stretching back to the ancient Indus Valley civilization.
* Indian painting has been traditionally done in miniature form, with intricate details and vibrant colors.
* The Mughal and Rajput schools of painting have had a lasting influence on Indian art.
* Indian painting has been influenced by the folk art of various regions, such as the Warli and Madhubani paintings of Maharashtra and Bihar, respectively.
* Indian painting has also been influenced by the various religions of India, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism.
* In modern times, Indian painting has been influenced by the Western art movements of Cubism, Expressionism, and Surrealism.
* Indian painting has also been influenced by the traditional art forms of various regions, such as Kalamkari, Phad, and Pattachitra.
* Indian painting has become increasingly popular in the West in recent years, with artists such as M. F. Husain, Bhupen Khakhar, and S.H. Raza gaining international recognition.
