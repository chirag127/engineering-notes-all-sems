
### The Seven Limbs of the State
1. Legislature: The legislature is the law-making body of the state. It is composed of representatives elected by the people. The legislature has the power to enact laws, amend existing laws, and repeal laws.

2. Executive: The executive is the body responsible for the implementation of the laws. It is composed of the President, Vice President, and Prime Minister. The executive has the power to enforce laws, appoint government officials, and make decisions on behalf of the state.

3. Judiciary: The judiciary is the body responsible for interpreting the laws and settling disputes. It is composed of judges appointed by the executive. The judiciary has the power to interpret laws, hear cases, and make rulings.

4. Police: The police are responsible for maintaining law and order. They are composed of officers appointed by the executive. The police have the power to investigate crimes, arrest suspects, and enforce the law.

5. Military: The military is responsible for defending the state from external threats. It is composed of soldiers appointed by the executive. The military has the power to protect the state, engage in warfare, and protect the nation's interests.

6. Revenue: The revenue is the body responsible for collecting taxes and other forms of income. It is composed of officials appointed by the executive. The revenue has the power to collect taxes, issue licenses, and enforce laws related to taxation.

7. Administration: The administration is the body responsible for the day-to-day running of the state. It is composed of officials appointed by the executive. The administration has the power to manage the state's resources, oversee the implementation of laws, and ensure the efficient functioning of the state.
