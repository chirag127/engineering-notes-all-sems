
### Theatre for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Indian theatre has a long and rich history, with evidence of theatrical performance dating back to the ancient Vedic period.
2. The traditional forms of theatre in India include Kathakali, Yakshagana, Koodiyattam, Ramlila, Nautanki, Bhavai, and many others.
3. These forms of theatre are based on Hindu mythology, epics, and folk tales, and are usually performed in open air venues.
4. In modern times, Indian theatre has been influenced by western theatrical forms, such as the proscenium stage, and the use of modern technology.
5. Contemporary Indian theatre is a mix of traditional and modern theatrical forms, and includes plays, musicals, and other forms of performance.
6. Indian theatre has a strong influence on Indian culture, and is used as a means to explore social issues, such as gender equality, caste discrimination, and poverty.
7. Indian theatre has also been used as a tool for social change and empowerment, with many theatre groups and organizations dedicated to raising awareness about social issues.
