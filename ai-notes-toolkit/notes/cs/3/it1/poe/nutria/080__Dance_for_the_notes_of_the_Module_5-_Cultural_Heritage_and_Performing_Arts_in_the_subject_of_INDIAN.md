
### Dance for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Dance has been an integral part of Indian culture for centuries. It is an expression of joy, celebration, and devotion.
2. Indian classical dances are divided into eight distinct styles, each of which has its own unique characteristics and expression. These are Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Mohiniyattam, Kathakali, Sattriya, and Manipuri.
3. Each dance style has its own unique costumes, makeup, and music. The music is usually composed of classical Indian instruments such as the sitar, tabla, and harmonium.
4. Each dance style also has its own set of steps and movements that express various emotions and stories.
5. Indian dance is not only a form of entertainment, but also a way to connect with the divine. It is believed that by performing these dances, one can attain spiritual bliss.
6. Indian dance is also a form of communication. Through the use of gestures, facial expressions, and body movements, dancers can convey a variety of messages.
7. Indian dance is also a form of physical exercise. The movements used in the dances help to improve flexibility, strength, and coordination.
8. Indian dance is also a form of art. The intricate steps and movements create a beautiful visual display that can be appreciated by all.
