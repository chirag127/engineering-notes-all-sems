

### Mathematics in India

Mathematics has been an integral part of the Indian culture since ancient times. The Vedic period saw the development of the earliest mathematical concepts, including the use of zero, the decimal system, and the concept of infinity.

In the classical period, India developed a number of important mathematical concepts, including the use of negative numbers, the study of arithmetic and geometry, and the use of algebra. Indian mathematicians such as Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, and Bhaskara II made great strides in mathematics during this period.

The modern period saw the development of Indian mathematics, with the development of calculus by mathematicians such as Srinivasa Ramanujan and the development of computers by pioneers such as Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar.

In recent times, India has made great strides in the field of mathematics, with the development of advanced mathematical theories, such as the theory of relativity, and the development of new technologies, such as artificial intelligence.

Today, India is home to some of the world’s leading mathematicians, and the country continues to make great strides in the field of mathematics.
