
### Chemistry in India 

* India has a long history of contributions to the field of chemistry, dating back to ancient times. 
* In the early centuries CE, Indian scholars developed theories of chemical reactions and were among the first to experiment with distillation and sublimation. 
* During the medieval period, Indian chemists developed a variety of processes for the manufacture of dyes and inks, as well as for the production of perfumes and medicines. 
* In the 19th century, Indian chemists made important contributions to the development of modern chemistry, particularly in the field of organic chemistry. 
* In the 20th century, Indian chemists made a number of important discoveries, including the development of new synthetic drugs and the synthesis of new materials. 
* Today, Indian chemists are continuing to make important contributions to the field, particularly in the areas of catalysis, nanochemistry, and green chemistry.
