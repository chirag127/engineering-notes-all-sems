
### Puranas 

1. Puranas are a genre of Hindu religious texts that are widely believed to be written between 500 BCE and 500 CE. 
2. The Puranas contain stories, legends, and mythology related to Hindu gods and goddesses, as well as cosmology and the history of India. 
3. The Puranas are composed in poetic form and often feature lengthy genealogies of kings and heroes. 
4. The Puranas are divided into five categories, each of which is associated with a particular deity: Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, Devi, and Surya. 
5. The Puranas are believed to be the source of much of the Hindu mythology and culture. 
6. They are also an important source of information about the history of India. 
7. The Puranas are also believed to be a source of spiritual knowledge and guidance. 
8. The Puranas are often used as a source of inspiration and guidance in Hindu religious ceremonies and rituals.
