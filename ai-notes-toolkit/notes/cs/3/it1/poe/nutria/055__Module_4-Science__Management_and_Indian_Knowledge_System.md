

## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

1. Science: Science is the systematic study of the physical and natural world through observation and experimentation. It is the foundation for modern technology, medicine, and other fields of study. 
2. Management: Management is the process of organizing and controlling resources in order to achieve organizational objectives. It involves planning, organizing, leading, and controlling resources to achieve desired goals.
3. Indian Knowledge System: The Indian Knowledge System is a collection of knowledge systems that have been developed over centuries in India. It includes various disciplines such as philosophy, mathematics, medicine, astronomy, and many other fields of study. It is based on ancient texts, such as the Vedas and Upanishads, as well as modern scientific research. The Indian Knowledge System is an important part of India's cultural heritage.
