
### Sangama Literature

Sangama literature is a term used to refer to the literature of India from the 3rd century BCE to the 8th century CE. This period was marked by the rise of a number of Indian religions, including Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism. Sangama literature encompasses a wide range of literary works, including poetry, drama, and religious texts.

- **Poetry**: Sangama literature includes a number of important poetic works, such as the Ramayana and Mahabharata epics, as well as the Panchatantra and Hitopadesha collections of fables.

- **Drama**: Sangama literature includes a number of important dramatic works, such as the plays of Kalidasa, Bhasa, and Sudraka.

- **Religious Texts**: Sangama literature includes a number of important religious texts, such as the Vedas, Upanishads, and Puranas. These texts are the foundation of Hinduism and are still studied and revered today.
