
### Marriage in India

* Marriage is an important social institution in India. It is based on the Hindu tradition of ritualistic union between two individuals who are considered to be of the opposite sex.
* In India, marriage is considered to be a sacred union between two individuals and is seen as a way to strengthen the bond between two families.
* Marriage is usually arranged by the family and the couple is expected to follow the traditions and customs set by their families.
* The traditional Hindu marriage consists of several rituals and ceremonies that are performed over several days. These include the engagement ceremony, the wedding ceremony, and the post-wedding rituals.
* In India, marriage is seen as a way of uniting two families, and the bride and groom are expected to be loyal and devoted to each other for life.
* Divorce is not common in India, but it is becoming more accepted in recent years.
* The Indian government has taken steps to protect the rights of women in marriage, such as the introduction of the Hindu Marriage Act of 1955. This act provides for the registration of marriages and for the protection of the rights of women in marriage.
