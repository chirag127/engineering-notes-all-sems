
### Coins for the Notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the Subject of Indian Traditions, Cultural and Society

1. Coins have been used as a form of currency in India since ancient times. Over the centuries, coins have been minted in various shapes and sizes. 
2. Coins also served as a medium for the expression of political and cultural values. It was a way for rulers to show their power, wealth and influence.
3. Ancient coins often depicted gods, animals, symbols and royal insignia on them. These coins have been used to study the history of the region and its rulers.
4. Coins also served as a medium for the expression of artistic values. Artistic styles such as calligraphy, painting, sculpture, and engraving were used to decorate coins.
5. Coins were also used to spread religious values. Many coins had religious symbols and images of gods and goddesses, which were used to spread religious messages.
6. Coins were also used to promote cultural heritage. Coins often featured images of famous monuments, buildings, and artworks. These images were used to promote the culture and heritage of the region.
7. Coins were also used to promote performing arts. Coins often featured images of musicians, dancers, and other performers. These images were used to promote the performing arts and to encourage people to participate in them.
