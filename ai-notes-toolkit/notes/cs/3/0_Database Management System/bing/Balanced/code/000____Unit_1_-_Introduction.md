## Unit 1 - Introduction

- This unit provides an overview of the course objectives, topics, and assessment methods.
- The course aims to teach the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence (AI), such as agents, search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, learning, and natural language processing.
- The course also introduces some of the applications and ethical issues of AI, such as expert systems, robotics, computer vision, and artificial neural networks.
- The course requires some background in mathematics, logic, and programming, as well as an interest in exploring the possibilities and limitations of AI.
- The course assessment consists of quizzes, assignments, and a final exam, which will test the students' understanding and ability to apply the concepts and techniques learned in the course.
- The course is divided into 10 units, each covering a different topic of AI. The units are:

  - Unit 1: Introduction
  - Unit 2: Intelligent Agents
  - Unit 3: Search
  - Unit 4: Knowledge Representation and Reasoning
  - Unit 5: Planning
  - Unit 6: Learning
  - Unit 7: Natural Language Processing
  - Unit 8: Expert Systems
  - Unit 9: Robotics
  - Unit 10: Computer Vision and Artificial Neural Networks

- The course follows the textbook "Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig, which is widely used and respected in the field of AI. The textbook provides additional examples, exercises, and references for further reading. The textbook is not mandatory, but highly recommended.
