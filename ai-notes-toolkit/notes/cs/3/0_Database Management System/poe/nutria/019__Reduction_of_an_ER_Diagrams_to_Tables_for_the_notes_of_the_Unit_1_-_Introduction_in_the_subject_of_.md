
### Reduction of an ER Diagrams to Tables for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Database Management System

1. Entity-Relationship (ER) diagrams are used to represent the relationships between entities in a database.
2. ER diagrams are composed of entities, attributes, and relationships. Entities are objects or concepts in the database, attributes are properties of the entities, and relationships are the connections between entities.
3. ER diagrams can be reduced to tables, which are more efficient to store and process data.
4. In order to reduce an ER diagram to tables, each entity must be converted into a table.
5. Each attribute of the entity must be converted into a column in the table.
6. Each relationship between entities must be converted into a foreign key in one of the tables.
7. After the ER diagram is reduced to tables, the tables can be used to store and process data in the database.
