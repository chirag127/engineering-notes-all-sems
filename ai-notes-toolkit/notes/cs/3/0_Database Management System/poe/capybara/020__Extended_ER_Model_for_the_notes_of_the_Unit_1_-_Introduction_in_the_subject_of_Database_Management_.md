### Extended ER Model for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Database Management System

The Extended Entity Relationship (EER) Model is an enhancement of the original Entity Relationship (ER) model. It is used to represent complex relationships between entities in a database. Here are some key points to remember about the Extended ER Model:

- The EER model is an enhancement of the ER model, which allows for the representation of more complex relationships between entities.
- In the EER model, an entity can have multiple subtypes that inherit attributes and relationships from the parent entity. This is known as specialization.
- The EER model also allows for the representation of overlapping subtypes, where an entity can belong to more than one subtype at the same time.
- Another feature of the EER model is the ability to represent relationships between relationships. This is known as a recursive relationship.
- The EER model also allows for the representation of constraints, such as minimum and maximum cardinality, and participation constraints.
- The EER model uses the same notation as the ER model, with the addition of symbols to represent the new features.

In summary, the Extended ER Model is an extension of the original ER model, which allows for the representation of more complex relationships between entities. It introduces new features such as specialization, overlapping subtypes, recursive relationships, and constraints. Understanding the EER model is essential for designing and implementing complex databases.
