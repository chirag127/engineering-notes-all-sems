### Introduction to Internet services and tools

1. The Internet is a global network of interconnected computer networks that use the standard Internet protocol suite (TCP/IP) to link devices worldwide.
2. The Internet provides a wide range of services and tools that facilitate communication, information sharing, and collaboration.
3. Some of the most popular Internet services include email, instant messaging, social networking, online shopping, and online banking.
4. Email is a service that allows users to send and receive electronic messages over the Internet.
5. Instant messaging is a service that allows users to exchange real-time text messages with one another over the Internet.
6. Social networking is a service that allows users to connect with friends, family, and colleagues, and share information, photos, and videos.
7. Online shopping is a service that allows users to purchase goods and services over the Internet.
8. Online banking is a service that allows users to manage their bank accounts and perform financial transactions over the Internet.
9. In addition to these services, the Internet also provides a wide range of tools that can be used for various purposes, such as web browsers, search engines, and cloud storage.
10. A web browser is a software application that allows users to access and view web pages on the Internet.
11. A search engine is a tool that allows users to search for information on the Internet by entering keywords or phrases.
12. Cloud storage is a service that allows users to store and access data on remote servers over the Internet.
