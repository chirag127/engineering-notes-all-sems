### Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on the client's computer by the server. They are used to store information about the user's interactions with the website. Here are some key points about cookies:

- Cookies are sent from the server to the client's browser as part of the HTTP response.
- The browser stores the cookies on the client's computer.
- When the client makes subsequent requests to the server, the browser sends the cookies back to the server as part of the HTTP request.
- Cookies can be used to store information such as user preferences, login information, and shopping cart contents.
- Cookies have an expiration date, after which they are automatically deleted by the browser.
- Cookies can be set to expire after a certain amount of time, or when the browser is closed.
- Cookies are domain-specific, meaning that they can only be accessed by the server that set them.
- Cookies can be used to track user behavior across multiple websites, which has raised privacy concerns.
- There are two types of cookies: session cookies and persistent cookies.
- Session cookies are temporary and are deleted when the browser is closed.
- Persistent cookies remain on the client's computer until they expire or are manually deleted.

Cookies are an important part of the functionality of many websites, and are commonly used to store user information and preferences. However, their use has also raised privacy concerns, and it is important for web developers to use cookies responsibly and transparently.
