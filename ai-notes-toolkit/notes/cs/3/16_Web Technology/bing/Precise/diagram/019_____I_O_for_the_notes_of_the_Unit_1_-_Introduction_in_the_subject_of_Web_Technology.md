### I/O

I/O stands for Input/Output. It is a fundamental concept in computing and refers to the communication between an information processing system (such as a computer) and the outside world, possibly a human or another information processing system.

- **Input** refers to the data or signals that are received by the system from the outside world. This can include data entered by a user through a keyboard or mouse, data received from a network, or data read from a storage device.

- **Output** refers to the data or signals that are sent from the system to the outside world. This can include data displayed on a screen, data sent over a network, or data written to a storage device.

I/O operations are essential for a computer system to interact with its environment and perform useful tasks. In the context of web technology, I/O operations are used to send and receive data over the internet, allowing web applications to communicate with servers and exchange information with other systems.
