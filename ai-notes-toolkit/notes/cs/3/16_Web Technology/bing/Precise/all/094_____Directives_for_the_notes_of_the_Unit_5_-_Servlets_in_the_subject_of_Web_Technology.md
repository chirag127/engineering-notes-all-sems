### Directives for the notes of the Unit 5 - Servlets in the subject of Web Technology

1. The notes should cover the basics of servlets, including their definition, purpose, and how they work.
2. The notes should explain the servlet lifecycle, including initialization, service, and destruction.
3. The notes should cover the different types of servlets, including generic servlets, HTTP servlets, and JSPs.
4. The notes should explain how to create and configure servlets, including the use of annotations and deployment descriptors.
5. The notes should cover the different methods for handling HTTP requests and responses, including GET, POST, PUT, and DELETE.
6. The notes should explain how to use servlets to handle form data, including the use of request parameters and multipart/form-data.
7. The notes should cover the use of cookies and sessions to maintain state between requests.
8. The notes should explain how to use filters and listeners to intercept and modify requests and responses.
9. The notes should cover the use of servlet contexts and attributes to share data between servlets.
10. The notes should include examples and code snippets to illustrate the concepts covered.
