### Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on the client's computer by the server. They are used to store information about the user's interactions with the website. Here are some key points about cookies:

1. Cookies are sent from the server to the client's browser as part of the HTTP response.
2. The browser stores the cookies on the client's computer.
3. The browser sends the cookies back to the server with each subsequent request.
4. Cookies can be used to store information such as user preferences, login information, and shopping cart contents.
5. Cookies have an expiration date, after which they are automatically deleted by the browser.
6. Cookies can be set to be accessible only by certain domains or paths, providing some level of security.
7. Cookies can be either session cookies or persistent cookies. Session cookies are deleted when the browser is closed, while persistent cookies remain on the client's computer until they expire or are deleted by the user.
8. Cookies have some limitations, such as a maximum size of 4KB and a limit on the number of cookies that can be stored per domain.

Cookies are an important part of the web technology landscape and are widely used to provide a personalized user experience on the web. They are used by servlets to store and retrieve information about the user's interactions with the website.
