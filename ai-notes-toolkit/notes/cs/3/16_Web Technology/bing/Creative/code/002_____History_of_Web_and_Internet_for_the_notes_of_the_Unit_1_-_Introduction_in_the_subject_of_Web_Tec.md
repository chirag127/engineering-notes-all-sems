### History of Web and Internet

- The **Internet** is a system of interconnected computer networks that can communicate and exchange data using a common set of protocols, such as TCP/IP.
- The **Web** is an application that runs on the Internet and allows users to access and share information using a standard format, such as HTML, and a protocol, such as HTTP.
- The Internet originated in the United States in the 1970s as a project of the Department of Defense to create a resilient and decentralized network that could survive a nuclear attack .
- The Web was invented in 1989 by Tim Berners-Lee, a British scientist working at CERN, a European research organization based in Geneva, Switzerland .
- Berners-Lee proposed the Web as a way to facilitate information-sharing among scientists around the world, using hypertext links to connect documents stored on different servers .
- Berners-Lee also created the first web browser, web server, and web page, and made his invention available to the public domain in 1993 .
- The Web soon became popular among the general public, as it enabled users to access a vast amount of information and services, such as news, entertainment, education, commerce, and social media .
- The Web has evolved over time, with the introduction of new technologies and standards, such as JavaScript, CSS, XML, HTTPS, AJAX, HTML5, and Web 2.0 .
- The Web has also faced challenges and issues, such as security, privacy, censorship, digital divide, and net neutrality .
- The Web continues to grow and change, as new applications and innovations emerge, such as cloud computing, big data, artificial intelligence, and the Internet of Things .
