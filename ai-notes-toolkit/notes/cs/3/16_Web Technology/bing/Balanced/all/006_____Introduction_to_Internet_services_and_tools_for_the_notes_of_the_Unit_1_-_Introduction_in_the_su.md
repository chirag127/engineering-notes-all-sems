# Introduction to Internet Services and Tools

- Internet services are the applications or functions that allow users to access, exchange, or create information over the internet.
- Internet tools are the software or hardware devices that enable users to connect, browse, or manage internet services.
- Some examples of internet services are:
  - **Communication services**: These services allow users to communicate with individuals or groups using text, voice, or video. Examples are email, chat, social media, and video conferencing.
  - **Information services**: These services provide users with access to a large amount of data, such as text, graphics, sound, and software. Examples are web, FTP, Gopher, and RSS.
  - **Resource sharing services**: These services allow users to share or access resources, such as files, printers, or storage, over the internet. Examples are cloud computing, peer-to-peer, and VPN.
  - **E-commerce services**: These services enable users to buy or sell goods or services over the internet. Examples are online shopping, online banking, and online auctions.
- Some examples of internet tools are:
  - **Web browsers**: These tools allow users to view and interact with web pages and other web-based applications. Examples are Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, and Mozilla Firefox.
  - **Search engines**: These tools allow users to find information on the internet by entering keywords or queries. Examples are Google, Bing, and Yahoo.
  - **Web development tools**: These tools allow users to create, edit, or manage web pages and web applications. Examples are HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP.
  - **Internet security tools**: These tools help users to protect their data and devices from malicious attacks or unauthorized access over the internet. Examples are antivirus, firewall, and encryption.
