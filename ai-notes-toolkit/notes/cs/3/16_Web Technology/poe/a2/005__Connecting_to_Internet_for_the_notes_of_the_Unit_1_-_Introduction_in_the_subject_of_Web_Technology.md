### Connecting to Internet for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Technology.

1. Internet: Internet is a global network of computers and other devices interconnected through various ways using a common set of rules and protocols known as TCP/IP protocol suite.

2. ISP: To access the Internet, you need to connect to an Internet Service Provider (ISP). An ISP is a company that provides access to the Internet in exchange for a monthly or annual fee. Common ISPs include cable companies, phone companies, and independent companies.

3. Communication Channel: The connection between your device and your ISP is called a communication channel. The most common types of communication channels for home Internet access are:

- DSL or ADSL: Uses your existing phone line
- Cable: Uses your existing cable TV connection
- Fiber: Uses fiber optic cables
- Mobile: Uses your cellular data or a mobile hotspot
- Satellite: Uses a satellite dish to connect to a satellite in orbit

4. Bandwidth: The amount of data that can be sent through a communication channel is known as bandwidth. More bandwidth means faster Internet speeds and the ability to do more activities simultaneously. Bandwidth is typically measured in megabits per second (Mbps).
