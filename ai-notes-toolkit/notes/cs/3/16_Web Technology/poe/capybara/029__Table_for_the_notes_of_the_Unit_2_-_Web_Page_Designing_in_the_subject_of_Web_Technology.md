### Table for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Web Page Designing in the Subject of Web Technology

In this unit, we will be discussing the principles of web page designing. Here are some important notes to take:

1. Design Principles
- Web page design should be visually appealing and easy to use.
- The design should be consistent throughout the website.
- The design should be responsive, meaning it should adapt to different screen sizes.
- The design should be accessible to all users, including those with disabilities.

2. Color Theory
- Color can convey emotions and impact the overall mood of the website.
- The color scheme should be consistent and complementary.
- The contrast between text and background should be high for readability.
- The use of too many colors can be overwhelming and distracting.

3. Layout
- The layout should be organized and easy to navigate.
- The use of whitespace can improve readability and highlight important elements.
- The use of grids can help maintain consistency and balance.
- The layout should be responsive to different screen sizes.

4. Typography
- The font should be easy to read and consistent throughout the website.
- The use of different font sizes and weights can help create hierarchy.
- The use of too many different fonts can be distracting.
- The font should be responsive to different screen sizes.

5. Images and Media
- Images should be high quality and relevant to the content.
- The use of multimedia, such as videos and audio, can enhance the user experience.
- The use of too many images or media can slow down the website and detract from the content.
- The media should be responsive to different screen sizes.

In conclusion, web page designing requires careful consideration of design principles, color theory, layout, typography, and images and media. By following these guidelines, you can create a visually appealing and user-friendly website.
