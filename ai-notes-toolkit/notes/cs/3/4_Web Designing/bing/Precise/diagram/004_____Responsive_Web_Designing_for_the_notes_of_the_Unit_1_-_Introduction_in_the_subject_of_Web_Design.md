### Unit 1 - Introduction: Responsive Web Designing

Responsive web design is an approach to web design that makes web pages render well on a variety of devices and window or screen sizes. It aims to provide an optimal viewing and interaction experience, with easy reading and navigation, and a minimum of resizing, panning, and scrolling.

Some key points to consider when designing a responsive website are:

1. Use flexible layouts: This means using relative units, such as percentages, rather than fixed units, such as pixels, to define the size and position of elements on the page.

2. Use media queries: Media queries allow you to apply different styles to your page depending on the characteristics of the device it is being viewed on, such as its screen size or orientation.

3. Use flexible images and media: Images and other media should be able to resize and adjust their position on the page to fit the available space.

4. Consider the user's context: The design of a responsive website should take into account the user's context, such as their location, the time of day, and the device they are using.

By following these principles, you can create a website that is accessible and usable on a wide range of devices, providing a better user experience for your visitors.
