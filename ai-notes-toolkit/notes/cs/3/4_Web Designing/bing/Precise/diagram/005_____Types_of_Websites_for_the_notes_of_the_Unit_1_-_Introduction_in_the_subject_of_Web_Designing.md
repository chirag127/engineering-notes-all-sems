### Unit 1 - Introduction: Types of Websites

There are several types of websites, each serving a different purpose. Some common types of websites include:

1. **E-commerce websites**: These websites allow users to buy and sell products or services online. Examples include Amazon, eBay, and Etsy.

2. **Blogs**: These websites are regularly updated with new content, often in the form of articles or posts. Examples include Huffington Post, TechCrunch, and Mashable.

3. **Social media websites**: These websites allow users to connect with others and share content. Examples include Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram.

4. **News websites**: These websites provide up-to-date information on current events. Examples include CNN, BBC, and Reuters.

5. **Educational websites**: These websites provide information and resources for learning. Examples include Khan Academy, Coursera, and edX.

6. **Entertainment websites**: These websites provide content for entertainment, such as videos, music, and games. Examples include YouTube, Netflix, and Spotify.

7. **Business websites**: These websites provide information about a specific business, such as its products, services, and contact information. Examples include Apple, Microsoft, and IBM.

8. **Personal websites**: These websites are created by individuals to showcase their personal interests, hobbies, or portfolio. Examples include personal blogs, photography websites, and artist portfolios.

These are just a few examples of the many types of websites that exist. Each type of website serves a specific purpose and can be designed and developed to meet the needs of its target audience.
