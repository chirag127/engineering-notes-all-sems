### Planning process for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Designing

1. **Define the scope of the unit**: Determine the key concepts and topics that will be covered in Unit 1 - Introduction of Web Designing.
2. **Gather resources**: Collect relevant study materials such as textbooks, articles, and online resources that provide information on the key concepts and topics of the unit.
3. **Organize the content**: Arrange the gathered information in a logical and coherent manner, ensuring that the content flows smoothly and is easy to understand.
4. **Create an outline**: Develop an outline of the notes, breaking down the content into sections and subsections to provide a clear structure for the notes.
5. **Write the notes**: Using the outline as a guide, write the notes in a clear and concise manner, ensuring that all key concepts and topics are covered.
6. **Review and revise**: Review the notes to ensure that the information is accurate and complete. Make any necessary revisions to improve the clarity and coherence of the notes.
7. **Format the notes**: Format the notes using appropriate headings, subheadings, and bullet points to improve readability and make it easier for students to study from the notes.
