### Registering domains for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. A domain name is the address of your website that people type in the browser URL bar to visit your website.
2. In simple terms, if your website was a house, then your domain name will be its address.
3. The process of buying a domain name is called Domain Name Registration.
4. You can register a domain name from a domain registrar.
5. Domain registrars are companies that manage the reservation of internet domain names.
6. You will need to provide some personal information and payment to register a domain name.
7. Most domain registrars also offer hosting services.
8. You can also transfer your domain name from one registrar to another.
9. It is important to renew your domain name registration before it expires, or you may lose it.
10. The cost of registering a domain name varies depending on the domain extension and the registrar you choose.
