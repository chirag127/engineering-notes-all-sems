### Registering domains for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. A domain name is the address of your website that people type in the browser URL bar to visit your website.
2. In simple terms, if your website was a house, then your domain name will be its address.
3. The process of registering a domain name is called Domain Name Registration.
4. To register a domain name, you need to visit a domain registrar.
5. Domain registrars are companies that manage the reservation of internet domain names.
6. Some popular domain registrars are GoDaddy, Namecheap, and Google Domains.
7. You will need to provide some personal information and payment information to register a domain name.
8. Domain names are registered on a first-come, first-served basis.
9. It is important to choose a domain name that is easy to remember, easy to spell, and represents your brand or business.
10. Once you have registered a domain name, you will need to set up web hosting to create a website and make it accessible to the public.
