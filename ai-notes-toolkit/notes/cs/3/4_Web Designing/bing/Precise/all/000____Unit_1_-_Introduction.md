## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. The introduction is the first section of any written work.
2. It is used to provide background information and context to the reader.
3. The introduction should be clear, concise, and engaging.
4. It should provide a brief overview of the topic and the purpose of the work.
5. The introduction should also include a thesis statement, which is the main argument or point of the work.
6. The introduction sets the tone for the rest of the work and is an important part of any written piece.
7. A well-written introduction can capture the reader's attention and make them want to continue reading.
8. It is important to revise and edit the introduction to ensure that it is clear, concise, and effective.
