# Unit 1 - Introduction to Web Designing

### Heading-Paragraphs

- A heading is a short phrase that describes the topic of the content that follows it.
- Headings are important in web design because they help to organize the content and make it easier for users to read and understand.
- Headings are typically larger and more prominent than the text that follows them.
- In HTML, headings are defined using the `<h1>` to `<h6>` tags, with `<h1>` being the largest and `<h6>` being the smallest.
- Headings should be used in a hierarchical manner, with the most important information being placed under the `<h1>` tag and less important information being placed under lower-level heading tags.
- Paragraphs are used to group related sentences together.
- In HTML, paragraphs are defined using the `<p>` tag.
- Paragraphs help to break up large blocks of text and make the content easier to read and understand.
- It is important to use proper spacing and indentation when writing paragraphs to improve readability.
- When designing a web page, it is important to consider the use of headings and paragraphs to organize the content and make it easy for users to read and understand.
