### Static and Dynamic Websites

#### Unit 1 - Introduction

- A **static website** is a website that is fixed and displays the same content for every user, usually written exclusively in HTML.
- A **dynamic website**, on the other hand, is one that can display different content and provide user interaction, by making use of advanced programming and databases in addition to HTML.
- Static websites are easier to create and host, while dynamic websites require more advanced knowledge and resources.
- Static websites are suitable for small websites with a limited number of pages and simple content, while dynamic websites are better for larger websites with more complex content and functionality.
- Examples of static websites include personal or small business websites, while examples of dynamic websites include social media platforms, e-commerce websites, and blogs.
