### Creating Emails in Cpanel

Cpanel is a popular web hosting control panel that allows users to manage various aspects of their website, including creating and managing email accounts. Here are the steps to create an email account in Cpanel:

1. Log in to your Cpanel account.
2. Navigate to the **Email** section and click on **Email Accounts**.
3. Click on the **Create** button.
4. Enter the desired email address and password.
5. Choose the mailbox quota or select the **Unlimited** option.
6. Click on the **Create** button to create the email account.

After creating the email account, you can access it using webmail or by configuring an email client with the appropriate settings. You can also manage the email account, such as changing the password or deleting the account, from the **Email Accounts** section in Cpanel.
