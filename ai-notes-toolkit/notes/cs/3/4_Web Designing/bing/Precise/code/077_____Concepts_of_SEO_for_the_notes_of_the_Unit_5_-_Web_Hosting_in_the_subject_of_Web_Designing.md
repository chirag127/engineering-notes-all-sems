### Concepts of SEO for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. **SEO** stands for **Search Engine Optimization**. It is the practice of optimizing a website to improve its ranking on search engine results pages (SERPs).
2. The goal of SEO is to increase the quantity and quality of organic traffic to a website from search engines.
3. SEO involves making changes to the website's design, content, and technical aspects to make it more attractive to search engines.
4. Some common SEO techniques include:
    - Keyword research and optimization
    - On-page optimization (e.g. title tags, meta descriptions, header tags)
    - Content creation and optimization
    - Link building
    - Technical SEO (e.g. site speed, mobile-friendliness, structured data)
5. SEO is an ongoing process and requires regular monitoring and adjustment to maintain and improve rankings.
6. SEO can be divided into two main categories: **on-page SEO** and **off-page SEO**.
    - On-page SEO refers to the optimization of elements on the website itself, such as content, structure, and technical aspects.
    - Off-page SEO refers to the optimization of external factors that can impact the website's ranking, such as backlinks and social media presence.
7. SEO is important for businesses as it can help increase visibility, drive traffic, and improve conversions.
8. There are many tools and resources available to help with SEO, including Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and various SEO software and platforms.
