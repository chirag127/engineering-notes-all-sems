### Web Hosting Basics

- Web hosting is an online service that makes your website’s content accessible on the internet.
- When you purchase a web hosting plan, you are renting space on a physical server to store all the website’s files and data.
- Web hosts provide the hosting technology and resources required for the effective and secure operation of your website.
- Web hosting also involves getting a domain name for your website, which is the unique address that visitors can use to access your site.
- There are different types of web hosting services, depending on your website’s needs and budget. Some of the common types are:
  - Shared hosting: This is where you share the server resources with other websites. It is the most affordable and suitable option for beginners and small websites .
  - VPS hosting: This is where you have a virtual partition of a server that is dedicated to your website. It offers more control, flexibility and scalability than shared hosting, but it is also more expensive and complex .
  - Cloud hosting: This is where your website is hosted on a network of servers that work together to provide high availability, performance and security. It is ideal for websites that have unpredictable traffic and need to scale up or down quickly .
- To host a website, you need to follow these four steps:
  1. Decide on your website type: You can choose between a static website, which has fixed content and layout, or a dynamic website, which has interactive and changing content and functionality.
  2. Research hosting providers: You need to compare different web hosts based on their features, prices, support and reviews, and choose the one that suits your website’s goals and requirements.
  3. Select a hosting plan: You need to pick a hosting plan that matches your website’s type, size and budget. You can also look for additional services, such as SSL certificates, backups, email accounts and more.
  4. Get a domain name for your site: You need to register a domain name that is unique, memorable and relevant to your website’s niche. You can either buy a domain name from your web host or from a separate domain registrar.
