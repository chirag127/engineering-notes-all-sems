# Static and Dynamic Websites

## Definition

- Static websites are ones that are fixed and display the same content for every user, usually written exclusively in HTML .
- Dynamic websites are ones that can display different content and provide user interaction, by making use of advanced programming and databases in addition to HTML .

## Advantages and Disadvantages

- Static websites are easier to create and host, faster to load, and more secure than dynamic websites  .
- Static websites are less interactive, less flexible, and harder to update than dynamic websites  .
- Dynamic websites are more interactive, more flexible, and easier to update than static websites  .
- Dynamic websites are harder to create and host, slower to load, and less secure than static websites  .

## Examples

- Static websites are suitable for simple and static content, such as portfolios, blogs, landing pages, etc .
- Dynamic websites are suitable for complex and dynamic content, such as e-commerce, social media, online games, etc .
