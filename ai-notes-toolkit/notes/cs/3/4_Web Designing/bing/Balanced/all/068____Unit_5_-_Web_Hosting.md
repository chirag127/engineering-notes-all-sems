## Unit 5 - Web Hosting

- Web hosting is an online service that enables you to publish your website or web application on the internet  .
- When you sign up for a web hosting service, you basically rent some space on a physical server where you can store all the files and data necessary for your website to work properly .
- A server is a special computer that is connected to the internet and can respond to requests from other devices.
- Web hosts are companies that provide the hosting technology and resources required for the effective and secure operation of your website .
- Web hosting can be classified into different types based on the features, performance, and pricing of the hosting service.
- Some of the common types of web hosting are:
  - Shared hosting: This is the most affordable and popular type of web hosting, where you share the same server and its resources with many other websites. This means that your website performance can be affected by the traffic and activities of other websites on the same server.
  - VPS hosting: This stands for Virtual Private Server hosting, where you get a dedicated portion of a server with its own resources and operating system. This gives you more control and flexibility over your website, as well as better performance and security than shared hosting. However, it is more expensive and requires more technical skills to manage.
  - Dedicated hosting: This is the most expensive and powerful type of web hosting, where you get an entire server for your website only. This means that you have full control and responsibility over your server, as well as the best performance and security. However, it is not suitable for beginners or small websites, as it requires a lot of technical knowledge and maintenance.
  - Cloud hosting: This is a modern and scalable type of web hosting, where your website is hosted on a network of servers that work together as one system. This means that your website can handle high traffic and load without any downtime or performance issues. However, it is more difficult to customize and monitor than other types of hosting, and it can be more expensive depending on your usage.
- To choose the best web hosting service for your website, you need to consider factors such as your website size, traffic, budget, security, and technical skills.
- You also need to compare the features and benefits of different web hosting providers, such as their uptime, speed, support, and reliability.
