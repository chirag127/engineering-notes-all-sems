# Basics of SEO

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization, which is the process of improving the ranking and visibility of a website in search engines. The higher or more frequently a website is displayed in a search engine list (like Google), the more visitors it is expected to receive.

SEO involves five main steps:

- **Keyword research.** Find what people search for and what keywords are relevant to your website's content and goals.
- **Content creation.** Craft content that matches the search intent and provides value to the users.
- **On-page SEO.** Make your content as clear and relevant as possible for both search engines and users. This includes using keywords, titles, headings, meta tags, images, links, and other elements.
- **Link building.** Build trust and authority from other websites by getting them to link to your website. This signals to search engines that your website is trustworthy and relevant.
- **Technical SEO.** Help search engines find, crawl, and index your content efficiently. This includes optimizing your website's speed, security, structure, navigation, mobile-friendliness, and other aspects.

SEO is an ongoing process that requires constant monitoring, analysis, and improvement. By following the SEO basics, you can increase your website's traffic, conversions, and revenue.
