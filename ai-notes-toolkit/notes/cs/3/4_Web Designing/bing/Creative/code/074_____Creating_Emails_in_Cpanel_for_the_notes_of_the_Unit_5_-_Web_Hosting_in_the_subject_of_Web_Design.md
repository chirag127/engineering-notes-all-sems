### Creating Emails in Cpanel

- Cpanel is a web-based control panel that allows you to manage various aspects of your web hosting account, such as domains, files, databases, email accounts, etc.
- To create an email account in Cpanel, you need to follow these steps:
  - Log in to your Cpanel account using your username and password.
  - Click on the **Email Accounts** icon under the **Email** section.
  - Click on the **Create** button to create a new email account.
  - Enter the desired email address, password, and storage quota for your email account. You can also choose to automatically create a mailbox and send a welcome email to the new email account.
  - Click on the **Create** button to confirm your settings and create your email account.
- To access your email account, you can use either webmail or an email client.
  - Webmail is a web-based interface that allows you to access your email account from any browser. To use webmail, you need to follow these steps:
    - Log in to your Cpanel account using your username and password.
    - Click on the **Email Accounts** icon under the **Email** section.
    - Click on the **Check Email** button next to the email account you want to access.
    - Choose the webmail application you want to use, such as Horde, Roundcube, or SquirrelMail.
    - Log in to your webmail account using your email address and password.
    - You can now read, compose, and manage your emails using the webmail interface.
  - Email client is a software application that allows you to access your email account from your computer or mobile device. To use an email client, you need to follow these steps:
    - Log in to your Cpanel account using your username and password.
    - Click on the **Email Accounts** icon under the **Email** section.
    - Click on the **Connect Devices** button next to the email account you want to access.
    - Choose the email client you want to use, such as Outlook, Thunderbird, or Apple Mail.
    - Follow the instructions to configure your email client with the correct settings, such as incoming and outgoing server, port, encryption, username, and password.
    - You can now read, compose, and manage your emails using the email client.
