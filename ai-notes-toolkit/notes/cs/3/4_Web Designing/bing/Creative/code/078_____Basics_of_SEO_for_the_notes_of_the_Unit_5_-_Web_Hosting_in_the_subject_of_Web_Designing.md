### Basics of SEO

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization, which is the process of improving the ranking and visibility of a website in search engines. The higher or more frequently a website is displayed in a search engine list (like Google), the more visitors it is expected to receive.

SEO involves five main steps:

1. Keyword research. Find what people search for and what keywords are relevant to your website's content and goals.
2. Content creation. Craft content that is informative, engaging, and answers the searcher's query. Use keywords strategically and naturally throughout your content.
3. On-page SEO. Make your content as clear and accessible as possible for both search engines and users. Use meta tags, headings, images, links, and other elements to optimize your web pages.
4. Link building. Build trust and authority from other websites by getting links from relevant and reputable sources. Links signal to search engines that your website is valuable and trustworthy.
5. Technical SEO. Help search engines find, crawl, and index your content efficiently by ensuring your website is fast, secure, mobile-friendly, and error-free.

SEO is an ongoing process that requires constant monitoring, analysis, and improvement. By following the SEO basics, you can increase your website's traffic, conversions, and reputation.
