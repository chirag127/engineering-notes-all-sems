## Unit 1 - Introduction

- This unit provides an overview of the course, its objectives, and its scope.
- The course is about artificial intelligence (AI), which is the study of how to create machines and software that can perform tasks that require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, and natural language processing.
- The course covers the following topics:
  - The history and evolution of AI, from its origins to its current state and future challenges.
  - The main concepts and techniques of AI, such as search, knowledge representation, logic, inference, machine learning, neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and ethics.
  - The applications and implications of AI in various domains, such as health, education, entertainment, business, and society.
- The course aims to:
  - Provide a comprehensive and balanced introduction to the field of AI, its methods, and its achievements.
  - Develop the students' ability to analyze, design, and implement AI systems and solutions for real-world problems.
  - Foster the students' critical thinking and creativity in using AI tools and techniques.
  - Enhance the students' awareness and appreciation of the ethical and social issues related to AI.
