# Static and Dynamic Websites

## Definition

- Static websites are ones that are fixed and display the same content for every user, usually written exclusively in HTML    .
- Dynamic websites are ones that can display different content and provide user interaction, by making use of advanced programming and databases in addition to HTML    .

## Advantages and Disadvantages

- Static websites are easier to create and host, faster to load, more secure, and cheaper to maintain   .
- Static websites are less interactive, less flexible, less scalable, and harder to update   .
- Dynamic websites are more interactive, more flexible, more scalable, and easier to update   .
- Dynamic websites are harder to create and host, slower to load, less secure, and more expensive to maintain   .

## Examples

- Static websites are typically used for informational or portfolio purposes, such as blogs, landing pages, resumes, etc  .
- Dynamic websites are typically used for functional or transactional purposes, such as e-commerce, social media, online banking, etc  .
