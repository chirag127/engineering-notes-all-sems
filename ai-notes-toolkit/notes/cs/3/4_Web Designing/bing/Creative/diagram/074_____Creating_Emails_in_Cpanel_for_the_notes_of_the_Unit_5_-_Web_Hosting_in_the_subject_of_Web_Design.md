### Creating Emails in Cpanel

- Cpanel is a web hosting control panel that allows users to manage various aspects of their websites, such as domains, files, databases, email accounts, etc.
- One of the features of Cpanel is the ability to create and manage email accounts for your domain name.
- To create an email account in Cpanel, follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Cpanel account and navigate to the **Email** section.
  2. Click on the **Email Accounts** icon.
  3. Click on the **Create** button to create a new email account.
  4. Enter the desired email address, password, and storage quota for the new account.
  5. Click on the **Create** button to confirm the creation of the new email account.
  6. You can now access your email account using webmail or an email client of your choice.

- To manage your email accounts in Cpanel, follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Cpanel account and navigate to the **Email** section.
  2. Click on the **Email Accounts** icon.
  3. You will see a list of your existing email accounts and their details, such as usage, quota, and status.
  4. You can perform various actions on your email accounts, such as:

    - Change the password or quota of an email account by clicking on the **Manage** button and editing the settings.
    - Delete an email account by clicking on the **Delete** button and confirming the deletion.
    - Access webmail for an email account by clicking on the **Check Email** button and choosing a webmail application.
    - Configure an email client for an email account by clicking on the **Connect Devices** button and following the instructions.
    - Enable or disable autoresponders, forwarders, or filters for an email account by clicking on the corresponding icons and adjusting the settings.

- Creating and managing email accounts in Cpanel is a simple and convenient way to communicate with your website visitors, customers, or colleagues using your own domain name.
