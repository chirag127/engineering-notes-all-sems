### Registering domains for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

- A domain name is a unique identifier for a website on the internet, such as google.com or wikipedia.org.
- A domain name consists of two parts: a name and a suffix, such as .com, .net, .org, etc. The name can be anything that is not already taken by another website, and the suffix indicates the type or category of the website.
- To register a domain name, one needs to use a domain name registrar, which is a company that connects domain names to IP addresses, which are numerical addresses that identify computers on the internet.
- The steps to register a domain name are as follows   :
  - Choose a domain name registrar from the many available options, such as Google Domains, GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.
  - Search for your desired domain name using the search function on the registrar's website, and check if it is available for registration. If not, try a different name or suffix until you find one that is available.
  - Add the domain name to your cart and proceed to checkout. You may also need to choose a hosting plan, which is a service that provides space and resources for your website on the internet.
  - Fill in all the required information, such as your name, address, email, phone number, etc. This information will be stored in a WHOIS record, which is a public database that shows the owner and contact details of a domain name.
  - Make your payment using the available methods, such as credit card, PayPal, etc. The cost of a domain name varies depending on the registrar, the suffix, and the duration of the registration. Typically, a domain name costs around $10 to $12 per year, but some suffixes may be more expensive or cheaper.
  - Verify your ownership of the domain name by following the instructions sent to your email by the registrar. This may involve clicking on a link, entering a code, or confirming your phone number.
  - Once you have verified your ownership, your domain name registration is complete, and you can start using your domain name for your website. You may need to wait for some time for the domain name to propagate, which means to become visible and accessible on the internet. This can take from a few minutes to a few hours, depending on the registrar and the suffix.
