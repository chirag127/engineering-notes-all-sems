### Planning process for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Designing

- The notes should cover the following topics:
  - What is web design and why is it important?
  - What are the basic principles and elements of web design?
  - What are the common tools and technologies used for web design?
  - What are the steps involved in the web design process?
  - What are the best practices and standards for web design?
- The notes should be organized in a logical and coherent manner, using headings, subheadings, bullet points, tables, diagrams, and examples to illustrate the concepts and techniques.
- The notes should be concise and clear, avoiding unnecessary details, jargon, and repetition.
- The notes should be accurate and up-to-date, citing reliable sources and references for the information and data provided.
- The notes should be engaging and interactive, using questions, quizzes, exercises, and feedback to test the understanding and application of the learners.
- The notes should be accessible and inclusive, using appropriate language, fonts, colors, images, and layouts to suit the needs and preferences of the learners.
