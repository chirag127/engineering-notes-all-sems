# Creating Emails in Cpanel

- Cpanel is a web hosting control panel that allows users to manage their websites, domains, email accounts, databases, and other aspects of their web hosting account.
- One of the features of Cpanel is the ability to create and manage email accounts for your domain name, such as info@example.com or sales@example.com.
- To create an email account in Cpanel, you need to follow these steps    :

  1. Log in to your Cpanel account using your username and password.
  2. In the Email section, click on the Email Accounts icon or link.
  3. Click on the Create or + Create button on the right-hand side of the screen.
  4. Enter a new email address in the Username text box, such as info or sales. You can also choose a different domain name from the drop-down menu if you have more than one domain in your account.
  5. In the Password section, enter a secure password in the Password text box, or use the Generate button to create a random password. You can also see the password strength indicator to check how strong your password is.
  6. In the Storage Space section, you can specify how much disk space you want to allocate for your email account, or leave it as Unlimited if you have enough disk space in your account.
  7. In the Additional Options section, you can choose to send a welcome email with the email account details to the new email address, or to stay logged in to the Cpanel interface after creating the email account.
  8. Click on the Create button at the bottom of the page to create your email account.

- After creating your email account, you can access it using the webmail interface or an email client such as Outlook or Thunderbird. You can also manage your email account settings, such as changing the password, setting up forwarders, filters, autoresponders, and more, from the Email Accounts interface in Cpanel.
