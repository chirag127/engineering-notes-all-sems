
## Unit 5 - Web Hosting

* Web hosting is the process of providing space on a server for websites, so that they can be accessed by users on the internet. 
* Web hosting services range from free hosting options to paid services with more features and better support. 
* When selecting a web hosting service, it is important to consider the type of website, the amount of traffic it may receive, the amount of storage space needed, and the level of technical support required. 
* There are several types of web hosting services, including shared hosting, virtual private servers (VPS), dedicated servers, cloud hosting, and managed hosting. 
* Shared hosting is the most affordable option, and is suitable for small websites with low traffic. 
* VPS hosting is more expensive than shared hosting, but provides more control and flexibility. 
* Dedicated servers are the most expensive option, but offer the highest level of performance and security. 
* Cloud hosting is a relatively new type of hosting, and is suitable for websites that experience sudden spikes in traffic. 
* Managed hosting is a type of hosting that includes additional services, such as security and backup services. 
* When selecting a web hosting service, it is important to read the terms and conditions carefully, and understand the costs associated with the service.
