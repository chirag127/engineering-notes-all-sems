
### Onpage Optimization Basics for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. Onpage optimization is the process of optimizing web pages to improve a website's search engine rankings and increase its visibility.

2. It involves optimizing the content, HTML, and other coding of a website to ensure that search engine crawlers can easily identify and index the content.

3. Keywords are an important part of onpage optimization, as they are the terms that search engines use to match a web page to a search query.

4. Meta tags, such as title tags and meta descriptions, are also important for onpage optimization. They provide search engines with a brief summary of the page and help them determine the relevance of the page to a search query.

5. It is also important to optimize the URLs of a website for onpage optimization. URLs should be short and descriptive and should include keywords that are relevant to the page.

6. Images should also be optimized for onpage optimization. This includes optimizing the file name, size, and alt text of images.

7. Internal linking is also important for onpage optimization. It helps search engines understand the structure of a website and how its content is related to each other.

8. Finally, optimizing the website's loading speed is important for onpage optimization. A slow website can negatively affect its ranking in search engine results.
