### Basics of SEO

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the practice of optimizing a website to rank higher on search engines like Google, Bing, and Yahoo. It is an important aspect of web designing and hosting. Here are some basics of SEO that you should be aware of:

- **Keywords:** Keywords are the words or phrases that people type into search engines to find what they are looking for. It is important to include relevant keywords in your website's content, meta descriptions, and title tags.

- **Content:** Content is king when it comes to SEO. Your website's content should be relevant, informative, and engaging. It should also be optimized for the keywords you are targeting.

- **Title tags and meta descriptions:** Title tags and meta descriptions are HTML elements that provide information about your website to search engines. They should be clear, concise, and include your targeted keywords.

- **URL structure:** Your website's URL structure should be clean and easy to understand. It should also include relevant keywords.

- **Internal linking:** Internal linking is the practice of linking to other pages on your website. It helps to improve navigation and makes it easier for search engines to understand the structure of your website.

- **Backlinks:** Backlinks are links from other websites that point to your website. They are an important factor in SEO and can help to improve your website's ranking on search engines.

- **Mobile optimization:** With the increasing use of mobile devices, it is important to ensure that your website is optimized for mobile users. This includes having a responsive design and fast loading times.

These are some of the basics of SEO that you should be familiar with when designing and hosting a website. By implementing these practices, you can help to improve your website's visibility and ranking on search engines.
