## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. Topic 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines like computers.
- AI makes it possible for machines to learn from experience, adjust to new inputs and perform human-like tasks.
- The ultimate goal of AI is to create general artificial intelligence - a machine that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. However, we are not there yet. Current AI technologies are narrow in scope and are designed to perform specific, limited tasks.
- Some examples of AI applications include:
   - Speech recognition systems
   - Image classification algorithms
   - Virtual assistants
   - Expert systems
   - Robotics

2. Brief History of AI
- The concept of AI was first proposed in the 1950s. The initial goals of AI were very optimistic and researchers expected that human-level intelligence in a machine would be attained within a generation.
- However, early successes in the field were followed by a period of stagnation and frustration known as the "AI winter". Researchers faced challenges and limitations that slowed progress.
- In the late 1990s and 2000s, the field experienced a revival with the growth of the Internet and the introduction of powerful new algorithms and massive amounts of data and computing resources. This led to technologies such as IBM's Deep Blue system beating the human world champion in chess in 1997.
- Recent advances in deep learning, neural networks and massive amounts of data have resulted in systems that can solve complex real-world problems in areas such as computer vision, speech recognition, natural language processing, etc. with superhuman performance. This has led to a lot of excitement around AI and its potential.
