### Onpage Optimization Basics

1. Choose relevant page titles and descriptions: The page title and description are important for SEO. Choose titles and descriptions that accurately reflect the content of the page and include relevant keywords.

2. Optimize content for keywords: Include keywords naturally throughout the content of the page. Place important keywords in headers, opening sentences of paragraphs, and first few words of webpage. Aim for a word count of at least 300-500 words with keyword density of 2-3%.

3. Use relevant headings and subheadings: Include an H1 heading with your target keyword. Use subheadings to break up content and include keywords. Headings and subheadings help search engines understand the structure and key topics of your content.

4. Include internal links: Include links to other relevant content on your site. This helps search engines crawl and index your site, and also provides value to users. Use relevant anchor text for internal links with keywords.

5. Ensure fast load times: Keep page load times as fast as possible. Slow load times can hurt user experience and rankings in search engines. Optimize images, minimize redirects, and clean up code to improve speed.

6. Ensure mobile-friendliness: Content must be optimized for mobile devices. This includes a mobile-friendly design, minimal use of flash or other plugins that don't work on mobile, and avoiding a solely app-based presence. Mobile optimization is increasingly important for search engine rankings.

7. Include relevant images: Include images that are relevant to the content and page topic. Name image files descriptively using keywords. Include alt text and title attributes for images that include keywords. This helps search engines understand the relevance of images to content.

8. Build internal links: Link to other relevant pages and content on your site. This helps search engines discover and crawl all your content, and provides value to users. Be strategic in how you structure internal links to reflect the key topics and categories of your content.

9. Optimize for user experience: Ultimately, onpage optimization should focus on providing value to users. Ensure content is useful, easy to read, and helps users accomplish goals. Focus on quality over manipulating rankings, as user experience is a key factor in sustainable organic search performance.
