### Concepts of SEO for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. Keywords Research: Finding the relevant keywords and key phrases that users may search for related to your website content is important for SEO. You can use free keyword research tools to find keywords with high search volume and low competition.

2. On-Page SEO: This includes optimizing your website content, page titles, headings and meta descriptions with relevant keywords. Having a fast website load time, mobile-friendliness and easy navigation also contributes to on-page SEO.

3. Backlinks: Getting high-quality backlinks from other authoritative websites linking to yours contributes to ranking in search engines like Google. Directory submissions, broken link building and guest posts on related blogs are some ways to earn backlinks.

4. Technical SEO: This includes optimizing website speed, mobile-friendliness, secure HTTPS implementation, easy crawlability, and site architecture. Following technical SEO best practices helps search engines crawl and index your website easily.

5. Content Creation: Publishing well-researched, unique content on your website regularly helps to keep your website updated and gives search engines more fresh content to rank. Content should be informative and relevant to your target audience.

6. Local SEO: Optimizing your website for local searches is important if you have a local physical business. This includes adding location-specific pages, Google Business listing set-up, and earning location-based backlinks and citations.

7. Reporting and Monitoring: Regularly tracking the performance of your SEO efforts through Google Analytics, search engine rankings and other SEO monitoring tools is important to see what is working and what needs improvement. Reporting SEO metrics to site owners keeps strategies data-driven.
