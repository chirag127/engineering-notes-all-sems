## Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

- Database Management Systems (DBMS) are software applications that interact with the user, other applications, and the database itself to capture and analyze data.
- A DBMS allows users to define, create, manipulate, and manage databases.
- Virtual Lab is a platform that provides remote access to labs in various disciplines of Science and Engineering.
- Virtual Lab allows students to perform experiments and learn concepts in a virtual environment.
- Mapping DBMS with Virtual Lab Lab refers to the process of integrating the two systems to provide a seamless learning experience for students.
- This integration allows students to access and manipulate databases in a virtual environment, providing a hands-on learning experience.
- The mapping of DBMS with Virtual Lab Lab can enhance the learning experience for students by providing a practical approach to understanding database concepts.
- This integration can also provide a cost-effective solution for institutions, as it eliminates the need for physical labs and equipment.
