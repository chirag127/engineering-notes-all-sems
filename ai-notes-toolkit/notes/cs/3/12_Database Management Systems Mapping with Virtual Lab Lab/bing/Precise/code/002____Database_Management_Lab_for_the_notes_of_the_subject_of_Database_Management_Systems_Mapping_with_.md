## Database Management Lab for the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

- Database Management Systems (DBMS) is a software system that enables users to define, create, maintain, and control access to the database.
- A database is an organized collection of data, stored and accessed electronically.
- The DBMS provides various functions that allow entry, storage, and retrieval of large quantities of information and provides ways to manage how that information is organized.
- The Virtual Lab for Database Management Systems provides a hands-on experience for students to learn and practice various concepts and techniques related to DBMS.
- The Virtual Lab includes various modules that cover topics such as database design, SQL, normalization, transaction management, and indexing.
- The Virtual Lab provides a simulated environment where students can practice creating and managing databases, writing and executing SQL queries, and performing other database-related tasks.
- The Virtual Lab is an effective tool for students to learn and practice the concepts and techniques of DBMS in a practical and interactive manner.
- The Virtual Lab can be used in conjunction with classroom lectures and textbook readings to provide a comprehensive learning experience for students studying DBMS.
