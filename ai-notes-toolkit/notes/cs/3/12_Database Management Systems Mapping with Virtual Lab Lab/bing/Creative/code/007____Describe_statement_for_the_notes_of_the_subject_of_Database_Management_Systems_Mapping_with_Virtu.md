## Statement for the notes of the subject of Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab

- Database Management Systems (DBMS) are software packages that store and manage data in a structured and organized way.
- DBMS provide various functions and features to support the development, administration, and use of databases, such as data modeling, schema creation, data manipulation, query processing, indexing, security, integrity, concurrency, and recovery.
- DBMS can be classified into different types based on the data model, the logical structure that defines how data is stored and related, such as relational, hierarchical, network, object-oriented, document, etc.
- Relational Database Management Systems (RDBMS) are the most widely used type of DBMS, which store data in tables consisting of rows and columns, and use Structured Query Language (SQL) as the standard language for data definition and manipulation.
- A virtual lab is a software-based environment that simulates a real-world laboratory, where users can perform experiments, learn concepts, and practice skills without the need for physical equipment, materials, or space.
- A virtual lab can be used to enhance the learning experience of students in various subjects, such as science, engineering, mathematics, etc., by providing interactive, engaging, and flexible learning opportunities.
- Database Management Systems Mapping with Virtual Lab Lab is a subject that aims to teach students the fundamentals of DBMS and RDBMS, and provide them with hands-on experience of using a virtual lab to create, manipulate, and query databases using SQL.
- The subject covers topics such as:

  - Introduction to DBMS and RDBMS, and their advantages and disadvantages
  - Data models, schemas, and instances, and their levels of abstraction
  - Entity-Relationship (ER) model, and its components and notation
  - Relational model, and its concepts and constraints
  - Relational algebra and calculus, and their operators and expressions
  - SQL, and its syntax and commands for data definition, manipulation, and querying
  - Normalization, and its rules and forms for reducing data redundancy and anomalies
  - Indexing, and its types and methods for improving query performance
  - Transactions, and their properties and states
  - Concurrency control, and its techniques and protocols for ensuring data consistency and isolation
  - Recovery, and its strategies and algorithms for restoring data after failures

- The subject also provides students with access to a virtual lab, where they can:

  - Create and populate tables using SQL commands
  - Perform various queries and operations on tables using SQL commands
  - Apply normalization rules and forms to tables
  - Create and use indexes on tables
  - Execute transactions and observe their effects on tables
  - Implement concurrency control and recovery techniques on tables
  - Evaluate and compare the results and performance of different queries and operations on tables

- The subject aims to help students to:

  - Understand the concepts and principles of DBMS and RDBMS, and their applications and limitations
  - Develop the skills and knowledge of using SQL as a tool for data definition, manipulation, and querying
  - Apply the techniques and methods of data modeling, normalization, indexing, transactions, concurrency control, and recovery to design and manage databases
  - Use the virtual lab as a platform for learning and practicing DBMS and RDBMS concepts and skills in a realistic and interactive way
  - Appreciate the importance and challenges of database management in various domains and scenarios
