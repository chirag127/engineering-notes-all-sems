
# Knowledge Centered Service (KCS)

KCS is a methodology for knowledge management that focuses on creating, maintaining, and delivering knowledge in order to improve service and customer experience. KCS is based on the idea that knowledge should be shared and used in order to maximize its value. KCS is often used in customer service and technical support departments, as well as other departments that require knowledge sharing and collaboration.

KCS is based on the following principles:

1. Knowledge is the primary resource for service and support.
2. Knowledge should be captured, organized, and shared.
3. Knowledge should be continually improved and updated.
4. Knowledge should be accessible to all who need it.
5. Knowledge should be used to improve customer experience.
6. Knowledge should be used to improve service and support processes.
