
### Stochastic Games for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Stochastic games are two-player games in which the players take turns in making moves.
2. Each move has an associated reward that is determined by the strategies chosen by each player.
3. Stochastic games can be used to model a variety of real-world problems, such as economic decision making, resource allocation, and negotiation.
4. The Nash equilibrium is a key concept in stochastic games, as it is the point at which no player can increase their reward by changing their strategy.
5. Stochastic games can be solved using dynamic programming, which involves working backwards from the end of the game to determine the optimal strategies for each player.
6. Reinforcement learning is another approach to solving stochastic games that uses trial and error to find the optimal strategy.
7. Monte Carlo Tree Search is a more recent approach to solving stochastic games, which uses a search tree to explore the game state space.
