
### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating intelligent machines that can think and act like humans. AI systems are designed to solve problems, recognize patterns, and learn from experience.

2. Knowledge Representation is a field of AI that deals with representing and reasoning about knowledge. It is concerned with how to represent facts and rules about the world in a form that can be used by a computer.

3. Knowledge-Based Systems (KBS) are computer systems that use AI techniques to solve problems. They are designed to use knowledge to solve problems and make decisions.

4. Expert Systems are KBS that are designed to mimic the behavior of human experts. They are used to solve problems in domains where experts are scarce or unavailable.

5. Machine Learning is a field of AI that focuses on developing algorithms that can learn from data. It allows machines to learn from experience and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed.
