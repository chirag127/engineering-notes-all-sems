
### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI Problems

1. Problem Solving: Problem solving is the process of finding solutions to difficult or complex problems. In AI, problem solving is the process of creating algorithms and programs to solve difficult problems.

2. Search Algorithms: Search algorithms are a type of problem solving algorithm used to find solutions to problems. Popular search algorithms include depth-first search, breadth-first search, and A*.

3. Heuristic Search: Heuristic search is a type of search algorithm that uses heuristics to guide the search. Heuristics are rules of thumb that help guide the search to a solution.

4. Knowledge Representation: Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a form that can be used by AI systems. This includes representation of facts, rules, and relationships.

5. Planning: Planning is the process of finding a sequence of actions that can be used to achieve a goal. In AI, planning is used to find a sequence of steps that can be used to solve a problem.

6. Learning: Learning is the process of acquiring knowledge through experience. In AI, learning is the process of creating algorithms that can learn from data and improve their performance over time.

7. Natural Language Processing: Natural language processing (NLP) is the process of understanding and generating natural language. NLP is used to enable AI systems to understand and generate human language.
