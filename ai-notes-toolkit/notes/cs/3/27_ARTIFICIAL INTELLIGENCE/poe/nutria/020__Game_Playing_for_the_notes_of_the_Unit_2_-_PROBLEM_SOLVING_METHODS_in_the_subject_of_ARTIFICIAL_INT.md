
### Game Playing 

Game playing is a problem solving method used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) that involves simulating the decision-making process of a human or an AI player. It is used to create AI agents that can play games with human players, or to create AI agents that can compete against each other.

Game playing is used in a variety of applications, including computer games, robotics, and autonomous vehicle navigation. It is also used in the development of AI systems such as game-playing agents and decision-making algorithms.

In game playing, the goal is to find the optimal solution to a given problem. The AI agent must evaluate all possible moves and select the one that leads to the best outcome. This process is known as "minimax" or "alpha-beta" search.

To evaluate a given move, the AI agent must consider the current state of the game and the available information. This information includes the rules of the game, the current board position, and the possible moves that can be made. The AI agent must then use this information to determine the best move to make.
