
### Problem Solving Methods in Artificial Intelligence KCS

* **Problem solving** is a process of finding solutions to complex problems. It involves breaking down complex problems into smaller, more manageable pieces and then finding solutions to each piece.

* **Search algorithms** are used to find solutions to problems. These algorithms are designed to explore a problem space and find a solution. Examples of search algorithms include depth-first search, breadth-first search, hill-climbing, and best-first search.

* **Heuristics** are used to guide search algorithms. Heuristics are rules of thumb that can be used to make decisions about how to explore a problem space. Examples of heuristics include the minimum cost heuristic, the maximum cost heuristic, the minimum distance heuristic, and the maximum distance heuristic.

* **Constraint satisfaction** is a technique used to find solutions to problems. It involves specifying constraints on the problem space and then finding solutions that satisfy the constraints. Examples of constraint satisfaction techniques include backtracking and local search.

* **Game theory** is used to find solutions to problems that involve multiple players. It involves analyzing the strategies of each player and finding a solution that is optimal for all players. Examples of game theory techniques include minimax, alpha-beta pruning, and Monte Carlo tree search.
