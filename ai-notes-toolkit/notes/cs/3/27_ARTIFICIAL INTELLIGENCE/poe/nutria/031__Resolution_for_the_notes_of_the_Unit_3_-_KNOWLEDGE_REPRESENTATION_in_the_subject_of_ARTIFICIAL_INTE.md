
### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation (KR) is the process of representing knowledge in a structured form. 
2. It is used to represent facts and rules about the world in a way that can be interpreted by machines. 
3. KR is used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) to enable machines to reason about the world. 
4. KR systems use symbols, rules, and logic to represent knowledge. 
5. Symbols are used to represent facts, objects, and relationships. 
6. Rules are used to represent logical relationships between facts. 
7. Logic is used to reason about the facts and rules. 
8. KR is used in AI to enable machines to learn and make decisions. 
9. KR is used to represent a wide range of knowledge, including facts, rules, beliefs, and plans. 
10. KR is used to represent knowledge in a variety of domains, including robotics, natural language processing, computer vision, and bioinformatics.
