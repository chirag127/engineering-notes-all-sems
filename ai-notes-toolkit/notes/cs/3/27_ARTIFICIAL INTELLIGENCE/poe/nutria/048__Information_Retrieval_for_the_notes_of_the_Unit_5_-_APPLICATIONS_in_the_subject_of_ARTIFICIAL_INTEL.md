
### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information Retrieval (IR) is the process of finding relevant information from a large collection of data. It is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that deals with the automatic retrieval of information from a given source.

2. IR systems use algorithms to search through large datasets and return relevant information to the user. These algorithms can be used to search for text, images, audio, or video.

3. IR systems can be used in a variety of applications, such as web search engines, document retrieval systems, and multimedia retrieval systems.

4. IR systems can also be used in natural language processing (NLP) applications, such as question answering systems, text classification, and sentiment analysis.

5. IR systems can be used to improve the accuracy and speed of decision making in business applications, such as customer relationship management (CRM) and supply chain management (SCM).

6. IR systems can also be used to identify patterns in data, such as customer segmentation, fraud detection, and text mining.
