
### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Negotiation and bargaining are two common methods of achieving agreement between two or more parties. Negotiation is a process of dialogue between two or more parties to reach a common understanding and agreement. Bargaining is the process of exchanging offers and counter-offers in order to reach a mutually acceptable agreement. 

Negotiation and bargaining are important skills for software agents, as they are often responsible for making decisions in complex environments. Negotiation and bargaining require the agent to understand the goals of the other parties involved, and to be able to make decisions that are in line with their own goals. 

Negotiation and bargaining can involve a variety of strategies, including the use of incentives, the exchange of information, and the use of power. The agent must also be able to identify and address any potential conflicts between the parties involved.

Negotiation and bargaining can also involve the use of negotiation protocols. These protocols are designed to ensure that the negotiation process is conducted in a fair and transparent manner. Protocols can also be used to ensure that the outcome of the negotiation is beneficial for all parties involved. 

Negotiation and bargaining can also involve the use of automated negotiation systems. These systems are designed to make the negotiation process more efficient and to provide better outcomes for all parties involved. Automated negotiation systems can also help to reduce the time and cost associated with negotiations.
