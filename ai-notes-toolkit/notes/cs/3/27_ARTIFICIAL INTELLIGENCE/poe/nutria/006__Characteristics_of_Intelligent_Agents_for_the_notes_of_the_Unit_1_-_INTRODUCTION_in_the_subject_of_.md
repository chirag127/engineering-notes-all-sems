
### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents 

1. Autonomy: Intelligent agents are autonomous, meaning they can act independently and make decisions without human input. 
2. Reactivity: Intelligent agents are reactive, meaning they can sense their environment and respond accordingly. 
3. Proactivity: Intelligent agents are proactive, meaning they can take initiative to achieve a goal.
4. Learning: Intelligent agents can learn from their environment, allowing them to adapt and improve their behavior over time. 
5. Social Ability: Intelligent agents can interact with other agents and humans, allowing them to communicate and collaborate.
