
### Language Models for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Language models are used to analyze and generate natural language text.
2. They are used in applications such as machine translation, speech recognition, and text summarization.
3. Language models can be trained using various techniques such as neural networks and Markov models.
4. Neural network-based language models are trained using large amounts of data and are capable of capturing complex relationships between words.
5. Markov models are based on the assumption that the probability of the next word in a sentence can be determined by the previous words in the sentence.
6. Language models can be used to assess the quality of machine translation and to determine the probability of a given sentence being correct.
7. They can also be used to generate text that is similar to that of a given corpus.
