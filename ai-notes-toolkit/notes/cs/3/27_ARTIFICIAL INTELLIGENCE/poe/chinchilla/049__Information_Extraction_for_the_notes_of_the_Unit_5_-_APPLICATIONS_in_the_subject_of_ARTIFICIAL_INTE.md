### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Information extraction is a vital task in the field of artificial intelligence. It involves the automatic extraction of structured information from unstructured or semi-structured data sources. In this unit, we will discuss the techniques and tools used for information extraction.

Here are some key points to consider when learning about information extraction:

- Information extraction is the process of automatically extracting structured information from unstructured or semi-structured data sources such as text documents, web pages, and social media.
- There are several techniques used for information extraction, including rule-based methods, statistical methods, and machine learning methods.
- Rule-based methods involve the use of handcrafted rules to identify and extract specific types of information from text.
- Statistical methods use probabilistic models to identify patterns and extract information from text.
- Machine learning methods involve the use of algorithms to learn patterns and extract information from text.
- Named entity recognition is a common task in information extraction that involves identifying and classifying named entities such as people, organizations, and locations in text.
- Relation extraction is another important task in information extraction that involves identifying and extracting the relationships between entities in text.
- Information extraction has many practical applications, including text classification, sentiment analysis, and question answering systems.

In summary, information extraction is a critical task in the field of artificial intelligence that involves the automatic extraction of structured information from unstructured or semi-structured data sources. By understanding the techniques and tools used for information extraction, we can develop more effective AI systems that can extract valuable insights from large volumes of data.
