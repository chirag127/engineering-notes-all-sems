### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Here are some important points about hardware used in artificial intelligence:

- **Central Processing Unit (CPU)**: This is the brain of the computer and is responsible for processing data. It is important to choose a high-performance CPU for AI applications.
- **Graphics Processing Unit (GPU)**: A GPU is used for parallel processing and can handle multiple tasks simultaneously. It is commonly used for deep learning and neural networks.
- **Field Programmable Gate Array (FPGA)**: An FPGA is a programmable chip that can be configured to perform specific tasks. It is commonly used for real-time processing and can be programmed to perform complex calculations quickly.
- **Memory**: AI applications require large amounts of memory to store and process data. It is important to have enough RAM and storage for optimal performance.
- **Networking**: AI applications often require communication between multiple devices. It is important to have a high-speed network to enable fast data transfer.
- **Sensors**: Sensors are used to collect data from the environment. They are commonly used in robotics and autonomous vehicles.
- **Actuators**: Actuators are used to control movement in robotics and autonomous vehicles. They can be used to control motors, servos, and other mechanical components.

It is important to carefully consider the hardware requirements for AI applications to ensure optimal performance and efficiency. Choosing the right hardware can make a significant difference in the accuracy and speed of AI algorithms.
