### Backward Chaining for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS.

1. Backward chaining is a problem-solving technique in which the steps required to achieve the goal are traced in reverse order.
2. It starts with the goal and works backwards to find the conditions that would lead to it.
3. The conditions are repeatedly broken down until base cases are reached.
4. It is useful when the paths to the goal are not clear or there are many paths.
5. Backward chaining is commonly used in expert systems and logic programming.
6. The steps are:

1. Take the consequent of the last rule and check if it matches the goal.
2. If yes, then the antecedent of that rule becomes the subgoal.
3. Repeat step#1 for the subgoal.
4. Continue until a rule's antecedent is found in the database.

7. The advantages of backward chaining are:

1. It leads to fast discovery of the solution.
2. It is useful when there are many paths to the goal.
3. It mimics the diagnostic approach of an expert.

8. The disadvantages are:

1. It may take a long time if the wrong rules are selected.
2. The order of the rules is important. The wrong order can lead to inefficiencies.

9. Backward chaining is commonly used in expert systems and logic programming. It is a handy way to solve problems in a systematic manner when the solution path is not straightforward.
