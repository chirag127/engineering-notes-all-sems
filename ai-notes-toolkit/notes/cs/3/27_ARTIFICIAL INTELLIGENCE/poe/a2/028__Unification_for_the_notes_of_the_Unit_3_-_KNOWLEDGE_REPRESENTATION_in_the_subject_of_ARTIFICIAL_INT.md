### Unification for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS.

1. Knowledge Representation - The task of representing knowledge in a computer so that it can be used to solve complex problems.
2. Why is KR required? - To enable intelligent systems and AI applications to use the knowledge. It makes knowledge accessible to automated reasoning.
3. Types of KR - Logic-based, Frame-based, Rule-based, Conceptual Graphs, Semantic Networks, Hybrid KR.
4. Logic-based KR - Uses logical statements to represent knowledge. E.g. Predicate Logic, Description Logic. Pros: formal semantics, reasoning capabilities. Cons: Difficult to understand, input knowledge must be fully explicit.
5. Frame-based KR - Knowledge is represented in frame-like structures. E.g. Frames, Scripts. Pros: Easy to understand, resembles human memory. Cons: Limited reasoning capabilities.
6. Rule-based KR - Knowledge is represented as condition-action rules. E.g. Production rules. Pros: Handling of uncertainty, resemblence to human thinking. Cons: Difficulty in maintaining consistency, scalability.
