# ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, and self-correction.

2. The ultimate goal of AI is to develop computers and computer software that are capable of intelligent behavior. AI research is highly active and emerging areas of research include neural networks, deep learning, and reinforcement learning.

3. Applications of AI include scheduling, planning, decision making, pattern recognition, learning, natural language processing, and perception. Modern AI systems rely on neural networks and deep learning to solve complex real-world problems.

4. The fundamental problem of AI is to develop computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. Such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision making, translation between languages, etc. The ultimate goal is to develop general artificial intelligence - a machine that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. It is a primary goal of artificial intelligence research.

5. The foundations of artificial intelligence were laid by logicians and mathematicians who modeled intelligence by formalizing logical and mathematical reasoning. The core problems of AI include programming computers for certain traits such as:
- Knowledge: Representing knowledge
- Reasoning: Drawing inferences and deriving conclusions
- Planning: Formulating complex and ordered sequences of actions to achieve a goal
- Natural Language Processing: Communicating with humans through natural language
- Perception: System to use input from sensors to deduce aspects of the world.
- Creativity: The ability to think originally and produce unique and valuable ideas.
