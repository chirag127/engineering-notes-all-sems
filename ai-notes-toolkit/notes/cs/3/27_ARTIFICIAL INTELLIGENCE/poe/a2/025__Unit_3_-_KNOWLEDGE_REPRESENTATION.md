## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

1. Knowledge representation refers to the way we encode concepts and ideas symbolically.
2. It is focused on understanding the meaning of things rather than just memorizing facts.
3. There are 3 main ways to represent knowledge:

- Propositional: Facts and statements, e.g. "birds can fly".
- Conceptual: Mental categories for grouping objects/ideas, e.g. the concept of "transportation".
- Schematic: Organized knowledge with complex relationships, e.g. a map of a city's transit system.

4. Knowledge can be represented in our minds in various forms: images, sounds, abstract symbols, analogies, etc. The form of representation affects how easily knowledge can be recalled, processed and understood.
5. Knowledge representation is important for artificial intelligence systems to reason about knowledge - they must have a way to encode and structure information so that it can be used to solve problems or make inferences. Various models and methods have been developed to represent knowledge in AI systems.
