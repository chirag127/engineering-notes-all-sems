## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

1. Brainstorming
- Generating multiple solutions without criticizing them
- Helps generate unique and creative solutions
- Facilitates teamwork and participation

2. Trial and Error
- Trying out various solutions and seeing which one works
- Useful when there are no obvious solutions or for complex problems
- Can be time-consuming and frustrating if solutions don't work

3. Algorithmic Approach
- Systematic step-by-step approach to solve the problem
- Breaks down the problem into smaller sub-problems and solves them
- Useful for structured and repetitive problems
- May not work for unstructured problems or those requiring creativity

4. Root Cause Analysis
- Identifying the underlying cause of the problem
- Solving the root cause instead of just the symptoms
- Useful to get to the source of chronic or recurring problems
- Requires careful investigation and analysis of the problem

5. Analogy
- Drawing parallels between the problem and a familiar solution
- Leveraging solutions from other contexts to solve the current problem
- Useful for gaining a new perspective on the problem
- The analogy can sometimes be inaccurate or incomplete
