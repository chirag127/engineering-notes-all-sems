### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

1. Perception: The ability to use input from sensors (such as cameras, microphones, wireless signals, etc.) to deduce aspects of the world.
2. Reasoning: The ability to use knowledge and logic to solve problems, draw inferences, make decisions, and achieve goals.
3. Learning: The ability to use experience to improve performance. Machine learning is a method of learning from data.
4. Problem solving: The ability to formulate problems, think creatively to solve them, and understand explanations for solutions.
5. Knowledge: The body of facts, principles, and methods that are used in processing perception, reasoning, learning, and problem solving.
6. Creativity: The ability to think originally and produce ideas and artifacts that are both novel and valuable. AI systems today have limited creativity.
7. Natural language: The ability to understand human language and to produce human language as output. This enables communication with humans.
8. Dexterity: The ability to physically manipulate objects. Robotics is focused on achieving dexterity. Current systems have limited dexterity.
9. Social skills: The ability to recognize human emotions and social dynamics. This allows for collaboration, relationship building, and negotiation with humans. AI systems today have limited social skills.
