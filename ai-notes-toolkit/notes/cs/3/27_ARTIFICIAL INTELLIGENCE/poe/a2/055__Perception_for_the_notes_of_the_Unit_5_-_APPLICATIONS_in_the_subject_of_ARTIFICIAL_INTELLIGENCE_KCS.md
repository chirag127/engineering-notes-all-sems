### Perception for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Perception refers to the ability to use input from sensors (such as cameras, microphones, wireless signals, and lidar) to deduce aspects of the world.
2. Perception is a core part of many AI applications including self-driving cars, facial recognition, speech recognition, robotics, and more.
3. The perceptual process consists of the following main steps:
 - Sensing: Gather input from sensors
 - Preprocessing: Filter, clean, and transform input
 - Feature extraction: Derive informative features/representations from the input
 - Recognition: Use features/representations to classify or predict aspects of the input
4. Perception systems must handle variability/noise in inputs and real-world conditions like occlusion, varying lighting, and more. Robust and accurate perception requires careful design and testing of all processing steps.
5. Deep learning has driven significant improvements in perception through learned feature representations and end-to-end trainable systems. However, deep learning also has limitations and does not solve all perception challenges. Other ML and hand-engineering approaches are also used.
