### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Understand the problem:
- clearly define the problem. What exactly is to be achieved?
- identify inputs and outputs. What is given and what is to be found?
- determine constraints and assumptions. Are there any restrictions? What is assumed?

2. Devise a plan:
- identify subproblems into which the main problem can be divided
- determine solution approaches to subproblems
- figure out the order in which the subproblems must be solved

3. Carry out the plan:
- implement the solutions to the subproblems
- test and debug the solutions
- integrate the solutions to subproblems into a solution to the overall problem

4. Check the solution:
- does the solution meet specifications?
- is the solution robust/ efficient?
- can the solution be improved?

The approach is iterative and may require going back to a previous step. The approach applies to AI problems involving search, reasoning, machine learning, etc. The effectiveness of the approach depends on the problem complexity and the AI techniques being used.
