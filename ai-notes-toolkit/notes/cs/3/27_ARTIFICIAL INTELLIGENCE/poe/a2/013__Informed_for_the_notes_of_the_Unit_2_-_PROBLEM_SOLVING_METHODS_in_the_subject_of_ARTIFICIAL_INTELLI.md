### Informed Search
- Search methods that have information about the search space.
- Use problem-specific heuristic functions to guide the search towards the goal.
- Faster than uninformed search but cannot guarantee optimal solutions.
- Examples: Greedy search, Hill climbing search, Beam search, A* search.

### Greedy Search
- Makes locally optimal choice at each stage with the hope of finding global optimum.
- Does not look ahead. Gets stuck in local optima.
- Needs admissible heuristics to avoid missing optimal solution.
- Complex problems may have many local optima, so greedy search may not work.

### Hill Climbing Search
- Starts with an arbitrary solution and then makes incremental changes to move to a better solution.
- Repeats until no better solutions are found.
- May get stuck in local optima. Needs random restarts to avoid local optima.
