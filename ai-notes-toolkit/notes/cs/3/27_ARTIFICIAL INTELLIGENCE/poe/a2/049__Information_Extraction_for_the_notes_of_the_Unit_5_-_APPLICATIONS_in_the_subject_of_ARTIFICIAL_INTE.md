### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information Extraction (IE) is the task of automatically extracting structured information from unstructured and/or semi-structured text data.
2. The key steps in IE are:
- Text Segmentation: Splitting text into meaningful chunks (sentences, phrases)
- Named Entity Recognition (NER): Identifying and classifying named entities (people, organizations, locations, etc.)
- Coreference Resolution: Resolving referential expressions (pronouns, definite noun phrases) to their appropriate entities
- Relation Extraction: Identifying relationships between entities or events
- Template Filling: Filling a pre-defined template with extracted information
3. Rule-based and Machine Learning methods are used for IE.
4. IE applications:
- Knowledge Base Population: Extracting information to populate knowledge bases
- Semantic Search: Enabling searches based on semantics and relationships, not just keywords
- Text Summarization: Generating summaries by extracting key information
- Question Answering: Answering questions by extracting relevant information from text
- Sentiment Analysis: Identifying opinion expressions and determining sentiment polarity
