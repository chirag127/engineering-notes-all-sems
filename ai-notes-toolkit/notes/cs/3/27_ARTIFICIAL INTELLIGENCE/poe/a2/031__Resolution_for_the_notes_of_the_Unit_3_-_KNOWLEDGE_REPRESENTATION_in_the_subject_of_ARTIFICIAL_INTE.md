### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1.  Knowledge Representation - Representing knowledge in a computer-understandable format is known as Knowledge Representation (KR). Some of the key tasks of KR are:
    -   Modeling knowledge about the world
    -   Facilitating automated reasoning and inference
    -   Aiding natural language processing
2.  Types of Knowledge - There are 2 types of knowledge that AI systems represent:
    -   Declarative Knowledge - Facts about the world. Ex: Delhi is the capital of India.
    -   Procedural Knowledge - Know-how or ways of doing things. Ex: The steps to bake a cake.
3.  Requirements of a good KR system - Some key requirements of a good KR system are:
    -   Expressiveness - Should be able to represent a broad range of knowledge.
    -   Clarity - Should have an unambiguous semantics.
    -   Extendibility - Should be able to represent new types of knowledge easily.
    -   Computability - Should be possible to write algorithms to process the knowledge.
4.  Knowledge Representation Methods - Some common KR methods are:
    -   Logic - Using logical statements and rules. Ex: First-order logic.
    -   Frames - Representing knowledge in frames or templates.
    -   Semantic Networks - Representing knowledge in a graph structure.
    -   Concept Maps - Diagram-based technique to represent relationships between concepts.
    -   Ontologies - Formal representation of a set of concepts and relationships.
