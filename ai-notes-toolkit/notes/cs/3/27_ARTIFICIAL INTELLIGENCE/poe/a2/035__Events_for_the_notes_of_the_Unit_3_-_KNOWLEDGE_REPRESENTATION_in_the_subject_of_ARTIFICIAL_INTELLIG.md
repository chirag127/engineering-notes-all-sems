### Events for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Propositional Logic
- Atoms and Literals: Atoms are simplest statements that can either be true or false. Literals are variables that represent atoms.
- Connectives: Connectives are logical operators that combine literals to form complex propositions. E.g. AND, OR, NOT, IMPLIES, EQUIVALENT.
- Well-formed formulas: Formulas that are constructed using connectives and literals according to the rules of propositional logic.
- Truth tables: Tables that show the outcome of a proposition for every possible combination of truth values of the literals it contains.

2. First-order Logic
- Terms: Refer to objects being discussed. Can be variables, constants, functions of variables.
- Predicates: Express properties of terms. Have a fixed arity (number of terms).
- Quantifiers: Express the quantity of terms that satisfy a predicate. E.g. "For all x, P(x)" or "There exists x such that P(x)".
- Knowledge bases: Collection of first-order formulae that represents knowledge about a domain.

3. Resolution
- Conversion to conjunctive normal form: Convert formula to AND of ORs of literals.
- Unification: Finding substitutions for variables to make two literals match.
- Resolution rule: A new literal is inferred from two literals whose variables unify but have opposite signs.
- Proof: A sequence of resolutions that leads to the empty clause, showing the formula is unsatisfiable.
