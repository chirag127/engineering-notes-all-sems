### Stochastic Games

- Stochastic games are a generalization of Markov decision processes(MDPs) to include multiple agents.
- In stochastic games, there are multiple players, each with their own set of actions and rewards.
- The state transitions and rewards depend on the joint actions of all players.
- Each player is trying to maximize their own reward.
- Stochastic games are useful for modeling multiagent systems where the agents have competing or cooperating goals.
- Solution concepts for stochastic games include Nash equilibria(stable state where no player can benefit from changing only their own action) and correlated equilibria(players coordinate by using shared randomness to determine actions).
- Finding optimal solutions for stochastic games is PSPACE-hard in general. Approximate and restricted solution methods must often be used.
