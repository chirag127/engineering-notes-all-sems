## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

1. Software Agents: Software agents are computer programs that perform tasks or make decisions for a user. They are autonomous and handle repetitive tasks such as filtering information, personalized searches, etc.

2. Types of Software Agents:

- Simple Reflex Agents: These agents take actions based on the current state of the environment. They have limited capabilities to handle complex problems.
- Goal-Based Agents: These agents have explicit goals and take actions that lead them closer to the goals. They plan a sequence of actions to achieve the goals.
- Utility-Based Agents: These agents associate values or utilities with different states and take actions that maximize the expected utility. They are capable of handling uncertainty in goals and actions.
- Learning Agents: These agents improve their performance based on experience. They detect patterns and insights from data to make better decisions in the future.

3. Applications of Software Agents: Software agents are used in applications such as:

- Personal Assistants: Manage tasks, schedules, and information for individuals.
- E-commerce: Provide recommendations and handle repetitive browsing and purchasing tasks.
- Network Management: Monitor network conditions and detect and resolve issues.
- Information Filters: Filter and categorize information based on user interests and priorities.
- Robotics: Navigate, manipulate objects, and learn in dynamic environments.
