### Alpha for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Problem Definition
- Clearly define the problem. Identify the input, output and constraints.
- Break down the complex problem into simpler sub-problems.
- Represent the problem using diagrams or mathematical formulations for better understanding.

2. Search Methods
- Breadth First Search: Explore neighbouring nodes first before going to deeper levels.
- Depth First Search: Explore deeper levels of a node before exploring neighbouring nodes.
- Best First Search: Choose the most promising path first based on a evaluation function.
- Hill Climbing: Move uphill towards increasing optimality. Gets stuck at local maxima.
- Simulated Annealing: Accepts worse solutions occasionally to escape local maxima.

3. Optimization Methods
- Brute Force: Test all possible solutions and select the best. computational complexity increases exponentially with problem size.
- Greedy Approach: Make the choice that looks best at the moment in hope of finding a global optimum. Does not guarantee optimal solution.
- Divide and Conquer: Break the problem into smaller instances of the same type, solve them independently and combine to get the final solution.
- Dynamic Programming: Break the problem into overlapping sub-problems, store the solutions and reuse them to avoid solving the same sub-problem multiple times.
- Gradient Descent: Move in the direction of decreasing error to reach a local minimum.

4. Heuristic Search Methods
- Use heuristic functions to guide the search towards goal and prune the search space.
- A* Search: Best First Search algorithm that uses a heuristic function to determine the promising path.
