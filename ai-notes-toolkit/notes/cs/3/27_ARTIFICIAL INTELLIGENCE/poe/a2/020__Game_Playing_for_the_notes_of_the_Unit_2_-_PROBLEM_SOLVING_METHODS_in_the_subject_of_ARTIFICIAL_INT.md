### Game Playing

1. Game playing is a type of problems that AI systems are focused on. The goal is to develop AI systems that can play strategic games like chess, checkers, poker, etc. at a high level.
2. The most crucial part is to develop algorithms that can evaluate game positions and identify optimal moves. Minimax algorithm is a common technique used to solve two-player turn-taking zero-sum games.
3. In Minimax algorithm, the AI system considers all the possible ways the game can play out by evaluating the maximum and minimum scores from all future moves. It selects the moves that lead to the best possible outcome.
4. Alpha-beta pruning is an optimization technique applied to Minimax algorithm to reduce the search space and increase the speed of the algorithm. It prunes away moves that cannot lead to optimal solutions.
5. Computer programs that play chess and other strategy games have surpassed human level performance using a combination of Minimax algorithm and alpha-beta pruning along with huge databases of opening moves and endgame positions. Deep Blue is an example of a chess-playing program that defeated the world chess champion in 1997.
6. Game playing has applications in planning, decision making, and other real-world problems. The strategies and algorithms developed can be adapted to solve problems in domains like business, military, and medicine. They help in analyzing complex scenarios and choosing the most optimal sequence of actions.
