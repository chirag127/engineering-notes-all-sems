## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

This unit covers some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains, such as:

- Natural language processing: the ability to understand and generate natural language, such as speech and text.
- Computer vision: the ability to perceive and interpret visual information, such as images and videos.
- Robotics: the ability to design and control machines that can perform physical tasks, such as navigation and manipulation.
- Expert systems: the ability to encode and apply human knowledge and reasoning in specific domains, such as medicine and law.
- Machine learning: the ability to learn from data and improve performance over time, such as classification and regression.

Some of the topics that will be discussed in this unit are:

- The main components and challenges of natural language processing, such as tokenization, parsing, semantics, and dialogue.
- The main components and challenges of computer vision, such as feature extraction, segmentation, recognition, and tracking.
- The main components and challenges of robotics, such as sensors, actuators, planning, and coordination.
- The main components and challenges of expert systems, such as knowledge representation, inference, and explanation.
- The main components and challenges of machine learning, such as data preprocessing, model selection, evaluation, and optimization.

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Describe the main applications and techniques of artificial intelligence in various domains.
- Compare and contrast the strengths and limitations of different approaches and methods in artificial intelligence.
- Apply some of the basic tools and frameworks of artificial intelligence to solve simple problems and tasks.
