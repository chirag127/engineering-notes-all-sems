# Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents can be used for various applications, such as information retrieval, decision support, automation, and entertainment. 

Some of the common characteristics of intelligent agents are:

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct human intervention. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.  
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing situations and cope with uncertainty and incomplete information.  
- **Interaction**: An intelligent agent can communicate and cooperate with other agents, humans, and systems. It can use various modes of interaction, such as natural language, gestures, or graphical user interfaces.  
- **Incrementality**: An intelligent agent can accommodate new rules and knowledge incrementally, without requiring a complete overhaul of its existing knowledge base. It can also revise and update its beliefs and actions based on new evidence and feedback.  
- **Goal-orientation**: An intelligent agent can pursue its own goals and objectives, and act rationally to achieve them. It can also balance multiple and conflicting goals, and prioritize them according to their importance and urgency.  
- **Knowledge-based**: An intelligent agent can use its own knowledge and reasoning to make decisions and take actions. It can also acquire new knowledge from various sources, such as observation, inference, or communication.
