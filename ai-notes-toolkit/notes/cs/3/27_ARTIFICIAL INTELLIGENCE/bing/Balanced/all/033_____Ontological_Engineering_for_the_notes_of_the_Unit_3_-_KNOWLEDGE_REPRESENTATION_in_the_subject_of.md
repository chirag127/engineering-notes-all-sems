# Ontological Engineering

- Ontological Engineering is a field of study that deals with the creation and management of ontologies, which are formal and explicit representations of the concepts, properties and relations of a domain of interest .
- Ontologies are useful for enabling semantic interoperability, knowledge sharing, reasoning, and natural language processing among different systems and agents .
- Ontological Engineering involves the following activities:
  - Ontology development process: the steps and tasks for creating an ontology, such as specification, conceptualization, formalization, implementation, evaluation, documentation, and maintenance.
  - Ontology life cycle: the stages and phases of an ontology from its inception to its retirement, such as feasibility study, design, construction, testing, deployment, use, evolution, and integration.
  - Methods and methodologies for building ontologies: the principles, guidelines, best practices, and standards for designing and developing high-quality ontologies, such as ontology engineering frameworks, ontology design patterns, ontology evaluation methods, and ontology alignment methods.
  - Tool suites and languages that support ontologies: the software and hardware resources that facilitate the ontology engineering tasks, such as ontology editors, ontology repositories, ontology reasoners, ontology languages, and ontology APIs.
- Ontological Engineering is applied in various areas of knowledge management, such as e-commerce, e-government, e-learning, e-health, e-science, and e-tourism.
