# Negotiation and Bargaining

- Negotiation is a process of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences.
- Bargaining is a subset of negotiation that focuses on the exchange of offers and counteroffers to determine the terms of the agreement.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, law, diplomacy, and personal relationships.
- Negotiation and bargaining can involve human-human, human-agent, or agent-agent interactions, where agents are software programs that can represent human interests or goals.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be classified into different types, such as distributive, integrative, cooperative, competitive, single-issue, multi-issue, one-shot, repeated, bilateral, or multilateral.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be influenced by various factors, such as information, communication, strategy, tactics, emotions, ethics, culture, and trust.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be supported or enhanced by artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as machine learning, natural language processing, game theory, decision theory, and multi-agent systems .
- AI can help human negotiators by providing information, analysis, advice, feedback, or automation.
- AI can also negotiate on behalf of humans or with other agents, by learning from data, generating offers, evaluating outcomes, and adapting to the environment.
- AI can improve the efficiency, effectiveness, fairness, and transparency of negotiation and bargaining processes, but also pose challenges, such as ethical, legal, social, and technical issues .
