### Constraint Propagation

- Constraint propagation is a technique for reducing the search space of a constraint satisfaction problem (CSP) by applying the constraints to eliminate inconsistent values from the domains of the variables  .
- A CSP is a problem where a set of variables must be assigned values from a finite domain such that a set of constraints are satisfied.
- Constraint propagation can be seen as a form of inference that deduces new information from the given problem specification.
- Constraint propagation can be performed by various algorithms, such as arc consistency, path consistency, or generalized arc consistency .
- Constraint propagation can be applied before, during, or after a search process, depending on the problem and the algorithm .
- Constraint propagation can be used for various applications, such as computer vision, scheduling, planning, or natural language processing  .
