### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are rules of thumb or strategies that guide the search process towards a goal state in a problem-solving task.
- Heuristics can be used to reduce the search space, speed up the search process, or improve the quality of the solution.
- Heuristics can be classified into two types: general and domain-specific.
- General heuristics are applicable to a wide range of problems, such as hill climbing, best-first search, simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, etc.
- Domain-specific heuristics are tailored to a specific problem domain, such as chess, sudoku, traveling salesman problem, etc.
- Heuristics can be evaluated based on their completeness, optimality, time complexity, and space complexity.
- Completeness is the property of a heuristic that guarantees that a solution will be found if it exists.
- Optimality is the property of a heuristic that guarantees that the solution found is the best one among all possible solutions.
- Time complexity is the measure of how long it takes for a heuristic to find a solution.
- Space complexity is the measure of how much memory is required for a heuristic to find a solution.
- Heuristics can be combined or modified to create new heuristics that suit the problem better.
- Heuristics can also be learned from data or experience using machine learning techniques, such as neural networks, decision trees, reinforcement learning, etc.
