## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- In this unit, we will learn about some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and industries.
- We will explore how AI can be used to solve problems, enhance productivity, improve customer experience, and create new opportunities.
- We will also discuss some of the challenges and ethical issues that arise from the use of AI in real-world scenarios.
- The topics covered in this unit are:

  - AI for healthcare: how AI can assist in diagnosis, treatment, prevention, and research of diseases and medical conditions.
  - AI for education: how AI can support learning, teaching, assessment, and personalization of education.
  - AI for entertainment: how AI can create and enhance content, games, music, and art.
  - AI for business: how AI can optimize processes, decision making, marketing, and customer service in various sectors and industries.
  - AI for social good: how AI can contribute to social and environmental causes, such as disaster relief, accessibility, human rights, and sustainability.
