### Events

- Events are the occurrence of things in the real world .
- Events are actions that involve changes in the state of the world or the relationships between entities.
- Events are an essential kind of knowledge in the world, which trigger the spring up of event-centric knowledge representation form like Event KG (EKG).
- Events can be represented by using predicates, arguments, and modifiers.
- Events can be classified into different types, such as atomic, complex, generic, specific, etc.
- Events can be related to each other by using temporal, causal, or thematic relations.
- Events can be extracted from natural language texts, images, videos, or other sources of data.
- Events can be used to support various applications, such as question answering, summarization, reasoning, etc.
