### Problem Solving Methods

Problem solving methods are techniques or algorithms that can be used to find solutions to problems in artificial intelligence (AI). Problem solving methods often involve investigating potential solutions to problems through reasoning techniques, making use of polynomial and differential equations, and carrying them out and use modelling frameworks.

Some of the common problem solving methods in AI are:

- **Search algorithms**: Search algorithms are methods that explore the state space of a problem to find a path from the initial state to the goal state. Search algorithms can be classified into uninformed search and informed search. Uninformed search algorithms do not use any domain-specific knowledge or heuristics to guide the search, while informed search algorithms use heuristics or estimates of the cost or distance to the goal to prioritize the search. Examples of search algorithms are breadth-first search, depth-first search, uniform-cost search, greedy search, A* search, etc .
- **Evolutionary computation**: Evolutionary computation is a family of problem solving methods that are inspired by the principles of biological evolution, such as natural selection, mutation, crossover, and reproduction. Evolutionary computation methods use a population of candidate solutions that are randomly initialized and then iteratively improved by applying evolutionary operators. The fitness of each candidate solution is evaluated by a predefined objective function, and the best solutions are selected to produce the next generation. Examples of evolutionary computation methods are genetic algorithms, genetic programming, evolutionary strategies, etc.
- **Knowledge representation and reasoning**: Knowledge representation and reasoning is a problem solving method that involves encoding the domain knowledge and facts of a problem in a formal language, such as logic, and then applying inference rules or algorithms to derive new facts or conclusions from the given knowledge. Knowledge representation and reasoning can be used to solve problems that require symbolic manipulation, common sense reasoning, planning, natural language understanding, etc. Examples of knowledge representation and reasoning methods are propositional logic, first-order logic, semantic networks, frames, ontologies, etc.
- **Machine learning**: Machine learning is a problem solving method that involves learning from data or experience to improve the performance of a task. Machine learning methods can be classified into supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning. Supervised learning methods learn a function that maps inputs to outputs from labeled data, such as classification or regression. Unsupervised learning methods learn patterns or structures from unlabeled data, such as clustering or dimensionality reduction. Reinforcement learning methods learn a policy that maximizes a reward signal from interacting with an environment, such as control or game playing. Examples of machine learning methods are linear regression, logistic regression, k-means clustering, principal component analysis, neural networks, decision trees, support vector machines, etc.
