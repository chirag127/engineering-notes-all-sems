## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- This unit covers some of the practical applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and industries.
- The topics include:
  - Natural language processing: the use of AI to understand and generate natural language texts and speech, such as machine translation, sentiment analysis, chatbots, etc.
  - Computer vision: the use of AI to process and analyze visual information, such as face recognition, object detection, scene understanding, etc.
  - Robotics: the use of AI to control and coordinate the actions of physical machines, such as autonomous vehicles, industrial robots, drones, etc.
  - Data science: the use of AI to extract insights and patterns from large and complex data sets, such as recommender systems, anomaly detection, fraud detection, etc.
  - Gaming: the use of AI to create and enhance interactive and immersive experiences, such as procedural content generation, game AI, virtual reality, etc.
- The objectives of this unit are:
  - To understand the basic concepts and techniques of AI in each of the above domains.
  - To appreciate the challenges and opportunities of AI in each of the above domains.
  - To explore some of the current and future applications of AI in each of the above domains.
- The assessment of this unit will consist of:
  - A written assignment on one of the topics, where you will research and analyze a specific AI application in detail.
  - A group project on one of the topics, where you will design and implement a prototype of an AI application using appropriate tools and frameworks.
  - A final exam covering all the topics, where you will demonstrate your knowledge and understanding of the concepts and techniques of AI in each of the domains.
