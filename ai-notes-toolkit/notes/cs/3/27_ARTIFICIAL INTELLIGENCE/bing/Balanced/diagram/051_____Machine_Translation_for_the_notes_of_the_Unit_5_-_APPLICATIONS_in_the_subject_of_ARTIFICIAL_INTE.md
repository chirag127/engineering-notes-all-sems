### Machine Translation

- Machine translation (MT) is a sub-field of computational linguistics that investigates the use of software to translate text or speech from one natural language to another  .
- Machine translation provides text translations based on computer algorithms without human involvement.
- Machine translation can be classified into different types based on the approach, such as rule-based, statistical, neural, example-based, and hybrid.
- Machine translation can be evaluated using different metrics, such as BLEU, METEOR, TER, and human judgments.
- Machine translation can be applied to various domains, such as e-commerce, education, entertainment, health, and communication.
- Machine translation faces various challenges, such as ambiguity, idioms, cultural differences, and quality assurance .
