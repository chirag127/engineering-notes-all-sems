# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology for service organizations to work more effectively with knowledge. KCS aims to improve service delivery, productivity, cost reduction, and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the main features of KCS are:

- KCS is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge as a by-product of solving problems.
- KCS involves the whole organization, not just the service desk, in creating and using knowledge. Everyone who interacts with customers, systems, or employees can contribute to the knowledge base.
- KCS uses a demand-driven approach, meaning that knowledge is created and updated based on the actual needs and feedback of the users, not on assumptions or predictions.
- KCS follows a four-phase adoption model: assess, design, adopt, and improve. Each phase has specific goals, activities, and measures to ensure a successful implementation of KCS.
- KCS has a set of practices, techniques, and standards that guide the creation and maintenance of knowledge articles. These include the use of templates, keywords, metadata, linking, flagging, and rating.

KCS is not to be confused with Kansas City Southern, a railroad company that operates in the US and Mexico.
