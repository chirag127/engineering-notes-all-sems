### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc.
- IR involves activities related to human cognition and knowledge management, such as understanding the user's information need, representing the documents and queries, ranking and filtering the results, and providing feedback and interaction.
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that aims to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, natural language processing, etc.
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value-added services, such as personalization, summarization, question answering, recommendation, etc.
- AI techniques can help to account for the intrinsic uncertainty and imprecision that characterize the subjectivity of IR, such as dealing with natural language, user preferences, relevance judgments, etc.
- Some examples of AI concepts and their applicability in IR are:
  - Pattern recognition: the process of identifying and classifying patterns in data, such as images, text, speech, etc. Pattern recognition can be used for tasks such as document classification, named entity recognition, sentiment analysis, etc.
  - Representation: the process of encoding and organizing information in a suitable form for processing and storage. Representation can be used for tasks such as document indexing, query expansion, semantic analysis, etc.
  - Problem solving and planning: the process of finding and executing a sequence of actions that achieve a goal or satisfy some constraints. Problem solving and planning can be used for tasks such as query optimization, result ranking, query reformulation, etc.
  - Heuristics: the process of using rules of thumb, shortcuts, or approximations to guide the search for solutions or improve the performance of algorithms. Heuristics can be used for tasks such as term weighting, relevance feedback, query suggestion, etc.
  - Learning: the process of acquiring and modifying knowledge from data and experience. Learning can be used for tasks such as document clustering, query classification, user modeling, etc.
