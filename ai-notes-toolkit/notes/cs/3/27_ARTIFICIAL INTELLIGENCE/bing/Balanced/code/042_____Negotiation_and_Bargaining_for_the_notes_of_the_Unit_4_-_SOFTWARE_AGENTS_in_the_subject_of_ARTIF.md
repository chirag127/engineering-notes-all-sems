### Negotiation and Bargaining

- Negotiation is a process of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences .
- Bargaining is a subset of negotiation that involves making offers and counteroffers until a deal is reached or rejected.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, diplomacy, law, and personal relationships .
- Negotiation and bargaining can be performed by humans, software agents, or a combination of both  .
- Software agents are autonomous programs that can interact with other agents or humans to achieve their goals  .
- Software agents can use artificial intelligence (AI) techniques to enhance their negotiation and bargaining skills, such as learning, planning, reasoning, and communication  .
- AI-based negotiation agents can offer benefits such as better winning deal ratio, reduction in negotiation time, cost, stress, and cognitive and intellectual effort required by the negotiator.
- AI-based negotiation agents can also face challenges such as ethical, legal, and social implications, human trust and acceptance, and coordination and cooperation with other agents .
- AI-based negotiation agents can be classified into different types based on their characteristics, such as cooperative or competitive, single-issue or multi-issue, one-shot or repeated, and bilateral or multilateral  .
- AI-based negotiation agents can use different strategies and tactics to achieve their objectives, such as concession, persuasion, deception, threat, and compromise  .
- AI-based negotiation agents can also use different models and frameworks to represent and analyze the negotiation problem, such as game theory, decision theory, argumentation theory, and social choice theory  .
- AI-based negotiation agents can be evaluated and compared based on different criteria, such as efficiency, effectiveness, robustness, fairness, and satisfaction  .
