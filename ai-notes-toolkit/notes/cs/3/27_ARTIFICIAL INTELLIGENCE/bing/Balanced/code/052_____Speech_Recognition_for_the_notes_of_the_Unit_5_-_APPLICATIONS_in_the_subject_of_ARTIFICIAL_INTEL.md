### Speech Recognition for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken words into machine-readable input.
- Speech recognition relies on artificial intelligence (AI) to analyze and interpret speech signals and transform them into meaningful information.
- Speech recognition can be used for various applications, such as voice control, speech transcription, speech translation, speech synthesis, speech authentication, and speech analytics.
- Speech recognition involves several components, such as:
  - Speech input: the source of speech signals, such as a microphone, a phone, or a speaker.
  - Feature extraction: the process of extracting relevant features from the speech signals, such as pitch, energy, duration, and spectral characteristics.
  - Feature vectors: the representation of the speech features in a numerical form, such as a matrix or a vector.
  - Decoder: the component that uses acoustic models, pronunciation dictionaries, and language models to map the feature vectors to the most likely words or phrases.
  - Word output: the final output of the speech recognition system, such as a text, a command, or a response.
- Speech recognition faces several challenges, such as:
  - Background noise: the presence of unwanted sounds that interfere with the speech signals, such as music, traffic, or other speakers.
  - Speaker variability: the differences in speech characteristics among different speakers, such as accent, dialect, gender, age, and emotion.
  - Speech variability: the variations in speech patterns within the same speaker, such as speed, volume, tone, and pronunciation.
  - Vocabulary size: the number of words or phrases that the speech recognition system can recognize and handle, which affects the accuracy and complexity of the system.
  - Domain specificity: the degree to which the speech recognition system is tailored to a specific domain or context, such as medical, legal, or conversational, which affects the relevance and accuracy of the system.
