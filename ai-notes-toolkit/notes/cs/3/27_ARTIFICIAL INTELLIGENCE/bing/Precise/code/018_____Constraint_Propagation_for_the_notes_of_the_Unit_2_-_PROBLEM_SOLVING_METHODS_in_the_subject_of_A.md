### Constraint Propagation

Constraint propagation is a technique used in artificial intelligence and constraint programming to reduce the search space of a problem by applying constraints to the possible values of the variables. This technique is used to solve problems such as constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs) and optimization problems.

Here are some key points to remember about constraint propagation:

1. Constraint propagation is the process of using constraints to reduce the search space of a problem.
2. It is used to solve problems such as constraint satisfaction problems and optimization problems.
3. The goal of constraint propagation is to reduce the number of possible solutions to a problem by eliminating values that are inconsistent with the constraints.
4. Constraint propagation can be applied to both discrete and continuous variables.
5. There are several algorithms for constraint propagation, including arc consistency, path consistency, and i-consistency.
6. Constraint propagation can be used in combination with other techniques, such as backtracking and local search, to solve problems more efficiently.
