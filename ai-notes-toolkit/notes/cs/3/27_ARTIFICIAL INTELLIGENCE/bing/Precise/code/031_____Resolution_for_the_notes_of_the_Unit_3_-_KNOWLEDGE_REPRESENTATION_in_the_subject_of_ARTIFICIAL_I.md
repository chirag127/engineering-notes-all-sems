### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge representation is the study of how to represent information about the world in a form that a computer system can use to solve complex tasks.
2. It is a fundamental concept in artificial intelligence, as it is the way that the system stores and manipulates information.
3. There are several techniques for knowledge representation, including semantic networks, frames, and production rules.
4. Semantic networks represent knowledge as a graph, where nodes represent concepts and edges represent relationships between concepts.
5. Frames are data structures that represent objects or events, and contain attributes and values that describe the object or event.
6. Production rules are a type of if-then statement that can be used to represent knowledge and make inferences.
7. Choosing the appropriate knowledge representation technique depends on the specific problem and the type of knowledge that needs to be represented.
8. Knowledge representation is an active area of research, with ongoing work on developing new techniques and improving existing ones.
