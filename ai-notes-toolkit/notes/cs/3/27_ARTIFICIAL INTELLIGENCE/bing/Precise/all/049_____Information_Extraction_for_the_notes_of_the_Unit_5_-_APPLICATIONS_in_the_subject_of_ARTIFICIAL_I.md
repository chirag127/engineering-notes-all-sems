# Information Extraction

Information Extraction (IE) is one of the applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that is covered in Unit 5 of the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS. 

Some of the key points to note about Information Extraction are:

- Information Extraction is the process of automatically extracting useful information from unstructured data sources.
- It is one of the AI applications covered in Unit 5, along with Language Models, Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing, Machine Translation, Speech Recognition, Robotics, Hardware, Perception, Planning, and Moving .
- Information Extraction can be used to process documents faster and simplify operational procedures, with fewer errors .
