# Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS: Information Retrieval

Information retrieval is the process of finding relevant information from a large collection of data. It is a key application of artificial intelligence, particularly in the field of natural language processing.

Some key points to consider when studying information retrieval in the context of artificial intelligence are:

1. Information retrieval systems are designed to help users find the information they need from a large collection of data.
2. These systems use various techniques to analyze and understand the user's query, and to retrieve the most relevant information.
3. Some common techniques used in information retrieval include keyword-based search, semantic search, and natural language processing.
4. Information retrieval systems can be used in a wide range of applications, including web search engines, digital libraries, and e-commerce websites.
5. The effectiveness of an information retrieval system is often measured by its precision and recall, which are measures of how well the system retrieves relevant information.
