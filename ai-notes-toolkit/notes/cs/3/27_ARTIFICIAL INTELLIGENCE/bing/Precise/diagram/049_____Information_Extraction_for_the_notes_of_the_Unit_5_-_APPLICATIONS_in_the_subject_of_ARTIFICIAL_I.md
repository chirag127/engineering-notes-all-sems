# Information Extraction

Information Extraction (IE) is one of the applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that is covered in Unit 5 of the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS. It is the process of automatically extracting useful information from unstructured data sources.

Some of the other applications of AI that are covered in Unit 5 include:
- Language Models
- Information Retrieval
- Natural Language Processing
- Machine Translation
- Speech Recognition
- Robotics

These applications involve the use of AI techniques to perform complex tasks more efficiently, with fewer errors and in a scalable manner. Information Extraction, in particular, involves the use of AI to process documents and extract relevant information in a faster and more accurate manner. This can simplify operational procedures and improve the overall efficiency of an organization.
