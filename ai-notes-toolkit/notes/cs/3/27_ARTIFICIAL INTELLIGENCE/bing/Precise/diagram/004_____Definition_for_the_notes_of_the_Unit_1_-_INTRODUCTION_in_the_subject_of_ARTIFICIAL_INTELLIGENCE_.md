### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Definition of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that deals with the development of intelligent machines that work and react like humans.
2. AI involves the study of how human intelligence can be represented and implemented in computer systems.
3. AI systems are designed to perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.
4. AI technologies include machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and cognitive computing.
5. The goal of AI is to create machines that can function autonomously and independently, without the need for human intervention.
6. AI has the potential to revolutionize many industries, including healthcare, finance, transportation, and manufacturing.
