## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

Software agents are computer programs that act autonomously on behalf of their users, to perform tasks that have been delegated to them. They are designed to operate in complex and dynamic environments, and can interact with other agents and with humans.

Some key characteristics of software agents include:

1. Autonomy: Agents operate without the direct intervention of humans or other agents, and have control over their actions and internal state.
2. Social ability: Agents interact with other agents and humans via some kind of agent communication language.
3. Reactivity: Agents perceive their environment and respond in a timely fashion to changes that occur in it.
4. Proactivity: Agents do not simply act in response to their environment, but are able to exhibit goal-directed behavior by taking the initiative.

Software agents can be used in a variety of applications, including e-commerce, information retrieval, and personal assistants. They can also be used in more complex systems, such as multi-agent systems, where multiple agents work together to achieve a common goal.
