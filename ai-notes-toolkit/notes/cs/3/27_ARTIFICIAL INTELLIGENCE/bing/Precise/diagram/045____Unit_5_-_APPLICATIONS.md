## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

1. Applications of mathematics in various fields such as science, engineering, economics, and finance.
2. Use of mathematical models to solve real-world problems.
3. Applications of calculus in optimization, motion, and growth and decay problems.
4. Use of probability and statistics in data analysis and decision making.
5. Applications of linear algebra in computer graphics and machine learning.
6. Use of discrete mathematics in computer science and cryptography.
7. Applications of geometry in architecture and design.
8. Use of trigonometry in navigation and surveying.
9. Applications of number theory in cryptography and coding theory.
10. Use of mathematical logic in computer science and artificial intelligence.
