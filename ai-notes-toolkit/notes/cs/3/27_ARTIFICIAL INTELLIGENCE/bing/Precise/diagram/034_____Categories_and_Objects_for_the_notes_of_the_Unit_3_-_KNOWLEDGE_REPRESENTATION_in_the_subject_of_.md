# Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION
## Categories and Objects

- **Categories** are used to group similar objects together. They are also known as classes or concepts.
- **Objects** are instances of categories. They are also known as individuals or members.
- Categories can be organized into a hierarchy, with more general categories at the top and more specific categories at the bottom.
- Objects can belong to multiple categories, and categories can have multiple objects.
- Categories can have properties, which describe the characteristics of the objects in the category.
- Objects can have values for the properties of their categories.
- Categories and objects are used in knowledge representation to organize and structure information.
