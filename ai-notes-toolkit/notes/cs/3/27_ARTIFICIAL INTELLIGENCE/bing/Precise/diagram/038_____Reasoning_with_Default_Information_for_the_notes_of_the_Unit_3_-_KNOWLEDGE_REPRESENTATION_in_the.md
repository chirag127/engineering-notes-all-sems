### Reasoning with Default Information

Reasoning with default information is a topic in the field of Artificial Intelligence, specifically in the unit of Knowledge Representation. It is concerned with how AI agents can use existing knowledge to draw conclusions, make predictions, or construct approaches towards a particular thought. This process is known as reasoning.

Some key points to consider when studying reasoning with default information include:

- It is a part of the larger field of Knowledge Representation and Reasoning (KR, KRR) .
- Reasoning is the logical process of drawing conclusions, making predictions, or constructing approaches towards a particular thought with the help of existing knowledge .
- It involves representing knowledge using logic and understanding the trade-off between representation power and computational properties .
- There are various fragments of first-order logic that are suited for Knowledge Representation, and reasoning algorithms and implementations can be used to support this representation .
