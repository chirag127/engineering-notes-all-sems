### Prolog Programming

Prolog is a high-level programming language associated with artificial intelligence and computational linguistics. It is a declarative language, meaning that the programmer specifies the desired relationship between objects, and the computer determines how to achieve that relationship.

Here are some key points to remember about Prolog programming:

1. Prolog is based on first-order predicate logic, which allows for the representation of complex relationships between objects.
2. Prolog programs consist of facts and rules, which define relationships between objects.
3. Prolog uses a technique called unification to match facts and rules with queries, allowing the programmer to ask questions about the relationships between objects.
4. Prolog is particularly well-suited for tasks involving symbolic reasoning, such as natural language processing and expert systems.
5. Prolog has built-in support for backtracking, which allows the programmer to explore multiple solutions to a problem.
