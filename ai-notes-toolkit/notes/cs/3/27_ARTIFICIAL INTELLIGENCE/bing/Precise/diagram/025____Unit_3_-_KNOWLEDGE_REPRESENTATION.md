## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

Knowledge representation is the field of artificial intelligence (AI) that focuses on designing computer representations that capture information about the world that can be used to solve complex problems.

1. **Introduction to Knowledge Representation:** Knowledge representation is a key aspect of AI systems, as it allows them to reason about the world and make decisions based on the information available to them.

2. **Types of Knowledge Representation:** There are several types of knowledge representation, including:
    - **Semantic Networks:** A semantic network is a graphical representation of concepts and their relationships.
    - **Frames:** A frame is a data structure that represents a stereotypical situation.
    - **Rule-Based Systems:** A rule-based system is a set of "if-then" rules that can be used to represent knowledge and make decisions.
    - **Ontologies:** An ontology is a formal representation of a set of concepts and their relationships within a domain.

3. **Reasoning with Knowledge Representation:** Knowledge representation allows AI systems to reason about the world and make decisions based on the information available to them. This can be done through various methods, such as forward chaining, backward chaining, and resolution.

4. **Applications of Knowledge Representation:** Knowledge representation has many applications, including expert systems, natural language processing, and machine learning.

5. **Challenges in Knowledge Representation:** There are several challenges in knowledge representation, including the representation of uncertainty, the representation of incomplete or inconsistent information, and the representation of change over time.
