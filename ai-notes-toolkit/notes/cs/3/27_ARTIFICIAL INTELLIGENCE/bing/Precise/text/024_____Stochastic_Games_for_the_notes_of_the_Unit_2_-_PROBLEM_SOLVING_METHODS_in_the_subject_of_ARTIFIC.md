### Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game in which the outcome is determined by both the actions of the players and chance elements. These games are used to model situations in which the outcome is uncertain and depends on the actions of multiple agents.

Some key points to remember about stochastic games are:

1. Stochastic games are used to model situations where the outcome is uncertain and depends on the actions of multiple agents.
2. The outcome of a stochastic game is determined by both the actions of the players and chance elements.
3. Stochastic games can be used to model real-world situations such as financial markets, political negotiations, and sports competitions.
4. In a stochastic game, players must make decisions based on incomplete information and must account for the uncertainty in the outcome.
5. Stochastic games can be solved using various methods, including game theory and reinforcement learning.
