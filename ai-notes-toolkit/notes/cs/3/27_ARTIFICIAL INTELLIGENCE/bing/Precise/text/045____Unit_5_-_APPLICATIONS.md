## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

1. Applications of mathematics: Mathematics has numerous applications in various fields such as science, engineering, economics, finance, and many more. It is used to solve complex problems, make predictions, and analyze data.

2. Applications of physics: Physics has a wide range of applications, from the study of subatomic particles to the behavior of the universe on a large scale. It is used in fields such as engineering, medicine, and telecommunications.

3. Applications of chemistry: Chemistry has many practical applications, including the development of new materials, the production of medicines, and the study of environmental issues.

4. Applications of biology: Biology has numerous applications in fields such as medicine, agriculture, and environmental science. It is used to study living organisms, their interactions with each other and their environment, and to develop new technologies and treatments.

5. Applications of computer science: Computer science has revolutionized the way we live, work, and communicate. It has applications in almost every field, from business and finance to healthcare and entertainment.

6. Applications of engineering: Engineering has a wide range of applications, from the design and construction of buildings and infrastructure to the development of new technologies and products. It is used to solve practical problems and improve our quality of life.

7. Applications of social sciences: Social sciences, such as psychology, sociology, and economics, have many practical applications. They are used to study human behavior, social interactions, and economic systems, and to develop policies and programs to improve society.

8. Applications of humanities: Humanities, such as history, literature, and philosophy, have many practical applications. They are used to study human culture, values, and beliefs, and to develop critical thinking and communication skills.
