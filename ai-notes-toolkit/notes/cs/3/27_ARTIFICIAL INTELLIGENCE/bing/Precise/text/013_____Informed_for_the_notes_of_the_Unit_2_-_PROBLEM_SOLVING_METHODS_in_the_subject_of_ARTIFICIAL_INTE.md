### Informed for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Informed search methods are a type of search algorithm that uses problem-specific knowledge to find solutions more efficiently than uninformed search methods.
2. Informed search methods use heuristics, which are problem-specific rules of thumb, to guide the search process.
3. Common informed search methods include greedy best-first search, A* search, and iterative deepening A* search.
4. Greedy best-first search expands the most promising node according to a heuristic function.
5. A* search expands the most promising node according to a heuristic function that takes into account the cost of reaching the current node and the estimated cost of reaching the goal from the current node.
6. Iterative deepening A* search is a variation of A* search that repeatedly applies a depth-limited version of A* search with increasing depth limits until a solution is found.
7. Informed search methods can be more efficient than uninformed search methods, but the quality of the solution found depends on the quality of the heuristic used.
8. Heuristics must be carefully designed to balance the trade-off between search efficiency and solution quality.
