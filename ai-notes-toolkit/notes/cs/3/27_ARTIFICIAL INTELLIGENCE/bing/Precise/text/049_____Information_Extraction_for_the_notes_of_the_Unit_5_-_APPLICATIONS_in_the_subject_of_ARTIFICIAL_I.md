### Information Extraction

Information Extraction (IE) is one of the AI applications covered in Unit 5 of the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS. It is the process of automatically extracting useful information from unstructured data sources. Some of the other AI applications covered in this unit include Language Models, Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing, Machine Translation, Speech Recognition, and Robotics .

IE techniques can be used to process documents faster and simplify operational procedures, resulting in fewer errors and increased efficiency . It is a powerful tool that can help organizations to automate data extraction and make better use of their data.
