## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION
- Introduction is the first chapter or unit of any subject or topic.
- It provides an overview of the subject or topic being discussed.
- It sets the foundation for the rest of the material to be covered.
- It is important to read and understand the introduction before proceeding to the rest of the material.
- The introduction may include definitions, background information, and a summary of the main points to be covered.
- It is a crucial part of any study material and should be read carefully.
