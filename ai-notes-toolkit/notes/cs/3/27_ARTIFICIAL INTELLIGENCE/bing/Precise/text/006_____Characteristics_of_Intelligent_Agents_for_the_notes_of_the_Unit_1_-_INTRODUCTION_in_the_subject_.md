### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a system that can perceive its environment, reason about it, and take actions to achieve its goals. Here are some characteristics of intelligent agents:

1. **Autonomy**: Intelligent agents can operate without human intervention and can make decisions on their own.

2. **Adaptability**: Intelligent agents can learn from their experiences and adapt their behavior accordingly.

3. **Goal-oriented**: Intelligent agents have specific goals and can take actions to achieve them.

4. **Reactivity**: Intelligent agents can perceive their environment and respond to changes in it.

5. **Proactivity**: Intelligent agents can take the initiative and act on their own, rather than just reacting to their environment.

6. **Social ability**: Intelligent agents can interact with other agents and humans in a cooperative manner.

These are some of the key characteristics of intelligent agents. They enable agents to operate in complex environments and achieve their goals effectively.
