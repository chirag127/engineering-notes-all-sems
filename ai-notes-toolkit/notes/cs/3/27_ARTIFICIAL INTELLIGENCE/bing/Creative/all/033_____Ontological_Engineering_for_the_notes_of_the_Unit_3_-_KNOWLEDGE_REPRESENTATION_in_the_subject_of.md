# Ontological Engineering

- Ontological Engineering is a field of study that deals with the creation and management of ontologies, which are formal and explicit representations of the concepts, properties and relations of a domain of interest .
- Ontologies are used to provide a common vocabulary and a shared understanding of a domain, to enable interoperability and integration of heterogeneous data and systems, and to support reasoning and inference over the domain knowledge .
- Ontological Engineering involves the following activities:
  - Ontology development: the process of designing, implementing, testing and documenting an ontology for a specific domain, using a suitable ontology language and tool.
  - Ontology evaluation: the process of assessing the quality, usability, validity and relevance of an ontology, using various criteria and methods, such as logical consistency, completeness, coverage, accuracy, clarity, etc.
  - Ontology evolution: the process of updating and maintaining an ontology over time, to reflect the changes in the domain or the requirements of the users, using techniques such as versioning, merging, alignment, etc.
  - Ontology reuse: the process of reusing existing ontologies or parts of them, to avoid duplication of effort and to leverage the existing knowledge, using techniques such as import, modularization, mapping, etc.
  - Ontology learning: the process of extracting and discovering ontological knowledge from various sources, such as text, databases, schemas, etc., using techniques such as natural language processing, machine learning, data mining, etc.
  - Ontology management: the process of storing, organizing, accessing and sharing ontologies and their metadata, using repositories, registries, catalogs, etc.
- Ontological Engineering requires the use of methods and methodologies that guide the ontology development process, such as the Ontology Development 101, the Methontology, the NeOn Methodology, etc.
- Ontological Engineering also requires the use of languages and tools that support the ontology representation, manipulation and reasoning, such as the Resource Description Framework (RDF), the Web Ontology Language (OWL), the Protégé, the OntoStudio, etc.
