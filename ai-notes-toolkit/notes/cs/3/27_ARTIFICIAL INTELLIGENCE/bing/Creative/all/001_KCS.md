# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology for service organizations to work more effectively with knowledge. KCS aims to improve service delivery, productivity, cost reduction, and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the main features of KCS are:

- KCS is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge as a by-product of solving issues.
- KCS involves the whole organization, not just the service desk, in creating and using knowledge. KCS encourages collaboration, feedback, and continuous improvement of knowledge.
- KCS uses a demand-driven approach, meaning that knowledge is created and updated based on the needs and requests of the customers and users.
- KCS follows a four-phase adoption model: assess, design, adopt, and improve. Each phase has specific goals, activities, and measures to ensure a successful implementation of KCS.
- KCS has a set of practices, processes, roles, and measurements that guide the service organization in applying KCS effectively and consistently .

KCS is not to be confused with Kansas City Southern, a US and cross-border railroad company that operates in North America .
