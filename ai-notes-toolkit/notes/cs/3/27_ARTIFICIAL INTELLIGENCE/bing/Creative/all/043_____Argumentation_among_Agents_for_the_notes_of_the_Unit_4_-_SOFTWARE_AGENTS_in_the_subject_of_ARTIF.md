# Argumentation among Agents

- Argumentation is a process of reasoning and communication that involves the exchange and evaluation of arguments, which are claims supported by reasons and evidence.
- Argumentation can be used to model the internal reasoning of an agent, as well as the interaction among agents who have different beliefs, goals, preferences, or values.
- Argumentation can help agents to resolve conflicts, persuade others, justify their actions, explain their opinions, and learn from each other.
- Argumentation can also provide a basis for designing and implementing multi-agent systems that can perform complex tasks, such as negotiation, deliberation, planning, and decision making.

## Basic Concepts of Argumentation

- An argument is a pair of a claim and a set of premises that support the claim. A claim is a statement that the arguer wants to establish as true or acceptable. A premise is a statement that provides evidence or reasons for the claim.
- An argument can be attacked by another argument that challenges either the claim or one of the premises of the original argument. An attack can be a rebuttal, which denies the truth of the claim or premise, or an undercut, which questions the relevance or sufficiency of the premise for the claim.
- An argument can also be supported by another argument that defends or strengthens the claim or premise of the original argument. A support can be a reinforcement, which provides additional evidence or reasons for the claim or premise, or a backing, which provides warrants or assumptions that justify the use of the premise for the claim.
- An argumentation framework is a structure that represents the arguments and the relations among them, such as attacks and supports. An argumentation framework can be used to analyse the acceptability of arguments, based on different criteria or semantics.
- An argumentation system is a set of rules or procedures that define how arguments can be constructed, exchanged, and evaluated by agents. An argumentation system can be based on different logics, languages, or protocols.

## Applications of Argumentation in Artificial Intelligence

- Argumentation can be used to specify the semantics of logic programs, by defining how the truth or falsity of a statement can be derived from a set of rules and facts, and how conflicts or inconsistencies can be handled .
- Argumentation can be used to generate natural language texts, by selecting and organizing the arguments that best suit the purpose, audience, and context of the communication .
- Argumentation can be used to support legal reasoning, by modelling the arguments and evidence that are used in legal cases, and by evaluating the strength and validity of the arguments .
- Argumentation can be used to facilitate multi-party human decision making and conflict resolution, by providing a platform for the participants to express and compare their arguments, and by suggesting possible outcomes or solutions .
- Argumentation can be used to implement multi-agent systems that can perform complex tasks, such as negotiation, deliberation, planning, and decision making, by enabling the agents to exchange and evaluate arguments, and by coordinating their actions and beliefs  .
