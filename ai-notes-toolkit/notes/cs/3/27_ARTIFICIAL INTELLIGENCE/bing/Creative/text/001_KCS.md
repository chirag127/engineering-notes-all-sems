# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology for service organizations to work more effectively with knowledge. KCS aims to improve service delivery, productivity, cost reduction, and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the main features of KCS are:

- KCS is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge as a by-product of solving problems.
- KCS involves the whole organization, not just the service desk, in creating and using knowledge. KCS encourages collaboration, feedback, and continuous improvement of knowledge.
- KCS uses a demand-driven approach, meaning that knowledge is created and updated based on the needs and requests of the customers and users, not on the assumptions or predictions of the service providers.
- KCS uses a structured format for knowledge articles, with a clear distinction between the problem, the cause, and the resolution. KCS also uses metadata, such as keywords, categories, and status, to make knowledge articles easy to find and use.
- KCS measures the value and quality of knowledge based on its usage and outcomes, not on its quantity or completeness. KCS uses metrics such as reuse rate, resolution time, customer satisfaction, and cost per incident to evaluate the impact of knowledge on service delivery.

KCS is not to be confused with KCS, the abbreviation for Kansas City Southern, a US and cross-border railroad company .
