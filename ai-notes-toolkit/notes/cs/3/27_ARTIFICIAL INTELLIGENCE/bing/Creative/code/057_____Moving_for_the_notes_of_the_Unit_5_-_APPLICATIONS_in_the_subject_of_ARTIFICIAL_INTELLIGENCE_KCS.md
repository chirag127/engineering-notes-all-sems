# Moving for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of a machine, program or system to learn and adapt by experience.
- AI can be classified into two types: artificial narrow intelligence (ANI) and artificial general intelligence (AGI).
  - ANI, or weak AI, is a type of AI that can only focus on one specific task or problem at a time. Examples of ANI are speech recognition, face recognition, chess playing, etc.
  - AGI, or strong AI, is the type of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. Examples of AGI are hypothetical and not yet achieved, such as HAL from 2001: A Space Odyssey, or Skynet from The Terminator.
- AI has been used in various applications to solve problems and improve efficiency in different domains. Some of the applications of AI are:
  - Big data: AI can help analyze large amounts of data and extract useful insights from them. Examples of big data applications are recommender systems, fraud detection, sentiment analysis, etc.
  - Computer vision: AI can help process and understand visual information, such as images, videos, or scenes. Examples of computer vision applications are face recognition, object detection, medical imaging, self-driving cars, etc.
  - Data mining: AI can help discover patterns and trends in data, such as text, web, or social media. Examples of data mining applications are text summarization, sentiment analysis, topic modeling, etc.
  - Machine learning: AI can help learn from data and make predictions or decisions based on them. Examples of machine learning applications are spam filtering, speech recognition, natural language generation, etc.
  - Natural language processing (NLP): AI can help process and understand natural language, such as speech or text. Examples of NLP applications are machine translation, chatbots, question answering, etc.
  - Health care: AI can help diagnose diseases, recommend treatments, monitor patients, or assist doctors. Examples of health care applications are IBM Watson, Google DeepMind, or Apple HealthKit.
  - National security: AI can help detect threats, enhance cybersecurity, or support military operations. Examples of national security applications are facial recognition, drone surveillance, or autonomous weapons.
  - Finance: AI can help optimize portfolios, predict market trends, or automate trading. Examples of finance applications are robo-advisors, algorithmic trading, or credit scoring.
  - Criminal justice: AI can help prevent crime, identify suspects, or assist lawyers. Examples of criminal justice applications are predictive policing, facial recognition, or legal analytics.
  - Transportation: AI can help improve traffic management, reduce accidents, or enable autonomous vehicles. Examples of transportation applications are self-driving cars, smart traffic lights, or ride-hailing services.
  - Smart cities: AI can help enhance urban planning, reduce pollution, or improve public services. Examples of smart city applications are smart grids, smart buildings, or smart waste management.
