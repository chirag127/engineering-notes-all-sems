# Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Negotiation and bargaining are processes of reaching an agreement between two or more parties that have conflicting interests or preferences.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be applied to various domains, such as business, politics, diplomacy, law, and personal relationships.
- Negotiation and bargaining can involve human-human, human-agent, or agent-agent interactions, where agents are software entities that can act autonomously and intelligently on behalf of their users or goals.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be classified into different types, such as distributive, integrative, cooperative, competitive, single-issue, multi-issue, bilateral, multilateral, etc., depending on the nature of the problem, the objectives of the parties, and the strategies they adopt.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be modeled and analyzed using various frameworks, such as game theory, decision theory, argumentation theory, social choice theory, etc., to understand the rationality, efficiency, and fairness of the outcomes.
- Negotiation and bargaining can be supported or automated by artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as machine learning, natural language processing, optimization, etc., to enhance the performance, accuracy, and convenience of the human or agent negotiators  .
- Negotiation and bargaining can benefit from AI in various ways, such as reducing the cognitive and emotional load of the negotiators, providing relevant information and suggestions, generating optimal and creative solutions, handling complex and uncertain scenarios, etc.  .
- Negotiation and bargaining can also pose challenges and risks for AI, such as ethical, legal, and social implications, trust and transparency issues, human-agent collaboration and coordination problems, adversarial and malicious behaviors, etc. .
