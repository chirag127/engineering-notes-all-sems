# Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

## Introduction

- Problem solving is the process of finding a solution to a given situation or task.
- Problem solving methods are systematic and logical approaches to solve problems.
- Problem solving methods can be classified into two types: algorithmic and heuristic.

## Algorithmic Methods

- Algorithmic methods are precise and deterministic procedures that guarantee a correct solution in a finite number of steps.
- Algorithmic methods are based on well-defined rules and formulas that can be followed mechanically.
- Algorithmic methods are suitable for problems that have a clear and unique solution, such as arithmetic calculations, sorting, searching, etc.
- Examples of algorithmic methods are: divide and conquer, recursion, dynamic programming, greedy method, etc.

## Heuristic Methods

- Heuristic methods are approximate and probabilistic procedures that do not guarantee a correct solution, but may find a good or optimal solution in a reasonable amount of time.
- Heuristic methods are based on experience, intuition, trial and error, and common sense that can guide the search for a solution.
- Heuristic methods are suitable for problems that have a large or infinite solution space, such as optimization, scheduling, game playing, etc.
- Examples of heuristic methods are: hill climbing, simulated annealing, genetic algorithm, neural network, etc.
