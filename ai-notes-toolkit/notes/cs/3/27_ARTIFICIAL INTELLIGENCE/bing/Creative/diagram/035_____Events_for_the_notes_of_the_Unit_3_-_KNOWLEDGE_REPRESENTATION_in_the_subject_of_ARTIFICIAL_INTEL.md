### Events

- Events are the occurrence of things in the real world   .
- Events are the actions which occur in our world  .
- Events are an important element as they are the initial thing to be considered in knowledge representation.
- Events can be represented by using predicates, arguments, and temporal information.
- Events can be linked to entities, attributes, and other events to form an event knowledge graph.
- Events can be used to describe behavior, causality, and scenarios in knowledge representation and reasoning.
