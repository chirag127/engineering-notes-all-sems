### Perception
- Perception is the process of acquiring, analyzing, and interpreting sensory information in order to gain an understanding of the environment .
- Perception involves both sensing and understanding the information that is gathered .
- Perception is essential for artificial intelligence, as it enables the AI system to interact with the world, make decisions, take action, and ultimately achieve a goal .
- Perception can be divided into two main subtasks: sensing and interpretation.
  - Sensing is the process of capturing sensory data from the real world using sensors, such as cameras, microphones, touch sensors, etc.
  - Interpretation is the process of processing the sensory data using algorithms, models, and techniques, such as computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition, etc.
- Perception can be applied to various domains and applications of artificial intelligence, such as robotics, self-driving cars, face recognition, speech synthesis, etc .
- Perception can also be influenced by factors such as noise, ambiguity, uncertainty, context, and prior knowledge.
- Perception can be evaluated using metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, speed, robustness, etc.
