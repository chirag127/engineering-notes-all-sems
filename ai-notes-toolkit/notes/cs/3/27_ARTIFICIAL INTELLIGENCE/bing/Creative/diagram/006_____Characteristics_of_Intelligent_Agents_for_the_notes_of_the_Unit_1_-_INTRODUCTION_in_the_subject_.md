### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents have the following distinguishing characteristics    :

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct human intervention. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing situations and cope with uncertainty and incomplete information.
- **Situativeness**: An intelligent agent is situated in a specific environment and can sense and affect it. It can also respond to relevant events and stimuli in a timely manner.
- **Sociability**: An intelligent agent can interact and cooperate with other agents, humans, and systems. It can communicate using a common language or protocol and follow social norms and conventions.
- **Goal-orientation**: An intelligent agent has one or more goals or objectives that guide its behavior. It can plan and execute actions that are expected to achieve its goals or satisfy its preferences.
- **Knowledge-based**: An intelligent agent has a representation of its environment, itself, and other agents. It can use this knowledge to reason, infer, and make decisions.
