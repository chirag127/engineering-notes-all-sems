# Definition

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that deals with creating machines or software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, natural language processing, computer vision, speech recognition, etc.
- AI can be classified into two main categories: weak AI and strong AI. Weak AI, also known as narrow AI, is the type of AI that is designed to perform a specific task or domain, such as playing chess, driving a car, or diagnosing a disease. Strong AI, also known as general AI or artificial general intelligence (AGI), is the type of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can do, such as understanding natural language, solving complex problems, or creating original art. Strong AI is still a hypothetical concept and has not been achieved yet.
- AI can also be classified into four main types based on the degree of human-like intelligence and rationality: systems that think like humans, systems that act like humans, systems that think rationally, and systems that act rationally. Systems that think like humans are the ones that try to model the cognitive processes of human beings, such as perception, memory, reasoning, learning, etc. Systems that act like humans are the ones that try to behave in a way that is indistinguishable from human behavior, such as passing the Turing test, playing games, or conversing. Systems that think rationally are the ones that try to follow the principles of logic, mathematics, or optimization, such as solving puzzles, proving theorems, or planning. Systems that act rationally are the ones that try to achieve the best possible outcome given the available information, resources, and goals, such as controlling a robot, making decisions, or negotiating.
- AI can also be classified into three main types based on the degree of human involvement and control: artificial narrow intelligence (ANI), artificial superintelligence (ASI), and artificial co-intelligence (ACI). ANI is the type of AI that is inferior to human intelligence and can only perform a specific task or domain, such as face recognition, spam filtering, or machine translation. ASI is the type of AI that is superior to human intelligence and can perform any task or domain better than humans, such as inventing new technologies, creating new knowledge, or dominating the world. ACI is the type of AI that is complementary to human intelligence and can collaborate with humans to achieve common goals, such as enhancing creativity, improving productivity, or solving social problems.
