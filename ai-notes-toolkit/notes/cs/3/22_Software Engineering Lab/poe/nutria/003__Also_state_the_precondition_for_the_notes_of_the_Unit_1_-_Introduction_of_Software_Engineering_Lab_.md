
### Preconditions for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab

1. Understanding of basic programming concepts such as variables, functions, loops, conditionals, and data structures.
2. Knowledge of object-oriented programming concepts such as classes, objects, methods, and inheritance.
3. Familiarity with software development life cycle models such as Waterfall, Agile, and Spiral.
4. Understanding of software engineering principles such as modularity, abstraction, coupling, cohesion, and reusability.
5. Knowledge of software engineering tools such as version control systems, bug tracking systems, and requirements management tools.
6. Familiarity with software development processes such as coding, testing, debugging, and documentation.
7. Understanding of software quality assurance techniques such as code reviews, inspections, and static analysis.
8. Knowledge of software testing techniques such as unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing.
