
### It is also suggested that open source tools should be preferred to conduct the lab for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab

* Open source tools are typically free to use, making them more accessible for students and researchers.

* Open source tools are often more up-to-date than proprietary software, making them better suited for modern development.
* Open source tools provide access to the source code, allowing users to modify and extend the tools for their own purposes.
* Open source tools are typically supported by a community of developers, making it easier to find help and resources.
