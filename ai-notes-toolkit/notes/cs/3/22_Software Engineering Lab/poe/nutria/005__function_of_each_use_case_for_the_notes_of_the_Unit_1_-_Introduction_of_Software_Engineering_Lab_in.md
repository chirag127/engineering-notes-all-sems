
### Function of Each Use Case 

1. **Requirements Gathering**: Requirements gathering is the process of gathering information from stakeholders and users to understand their needs and develop a solution to meet those needs. 

2. **Analysis**: Analysis is the process of breaking down a problem into its components to better understand the problem and develop a solution. 

3. **Design**: Design is the process of creating a blueprint for the system that meets the requirements gathered in the requirements gathering phase. 

4. **Implementation**: Implementation is the process of coding the design and building the system. 

5. **Testing**: Testing is the process of validating that the system meets the requirements and works as expected. 

6. **Maintenance**: Maintenance is the process of keeping the system up-to-date and running smoothly.
