### Draw the collaboration diagram for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab.

1. The lab coordinator will introduce the course and discuss the syllabus and evaluation scheme.
2. The teaching assistant will demonstrate the lab equipment and tools to be used in the course of the lab.
3. The students will be divided into groups of two or three.
4. Each group will be assigned a lab kit that will have all the required hardware and software tools.
5. The teaching assistant will explain the objectives and requirements of the first experiment or project.
6. The student groups will start working on the experiment or project under the guidance of the teaching assistant.
7. The teaching assistant will evaluate the work done by each group and provide necessary feedback.
8. The process will be repeated for each experiment and project in the course of the lab.

The above points cover the key steps in the collaboration between the lab coordinator, teaching assistant, and students to complete the lab activities. The collaboration diagram would depict these steps and the interactions between the various participants.
