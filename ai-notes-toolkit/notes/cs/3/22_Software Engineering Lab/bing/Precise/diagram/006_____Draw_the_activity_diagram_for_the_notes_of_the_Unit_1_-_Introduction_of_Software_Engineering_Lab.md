### Activity Diagram for Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

An activity diagram is a graphical representation of a workflow or process. It shows the flow of control from one activity to another, and can be used to model the behavior of a system or a business process.

```
|

Understand Key Concepts
|

|

End
```

1. The process begins at the `Start` node.
2. The first activity is to `Read Unit 1 Notes`.
3. After reading the notes, the next activity is to `Understand Key Concepts`.
4. Once the key concepts are understood, the next activity is to `Complete Exercises`.
5. After completing the exercises, the next activity is to `Review and Revise`.
6. The process ends at the `End` node.

This activity diagram provides a visual representation of the steps involved in studying and understanding the notes for Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab. It can be used as a guide to help students organize their study process and ensure that they cover all the necessary material.
