### Post Condition for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the Subject of Software Engineering Lab

1. The student will have a basic understanding of the concepts and principles of software engineering.
2. The student will be able to explain the importance of software engineering in the development of software systems.
3. The student will be able to identify the different phases of the software development life cycle.
4. The student will be able to describe the various software development models and their advantages and disadvantages.
5. The student will be able to explain the role of software requirements and design in the development of software systems.
6. The student will be able to discuss the importance of software testing and maintenance in ensuring the quality of software systems.
7. The student will be able to identify the various tools and techniques used in software engineering to support the development of software systems.
