### Use Case Diagram and Actors in Software Engineering Lab

A use case diagram is a visual representation of the interactions between the users (actors) and the system. It shows the relationships between the actors and the use cases in which they are involved.

In the context of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab, the use case diagram could include the following actors and their roles:

1. **Student**: A student is an actor who interacts with the system to learn about software engineering concepts and practices. They may access course materials, complete assignments, and participate in lab activities.

2. **Instructor**: An instructor is an actor who is responsible for teaching the course and guiding the students. They may create and upload course materials, assign and grade assignments, and facilitate lab activities.

3. **Lab Assistant**: A lab assistant is an actor who assists the instructor in the lab. They may help set up lab equipment, provide technical support to students, and assist with grading assignments.

4. **System Administrator**: A system administrator is an actor who is responsible for maintaining the system and ensuring its smooth operation. They may perform tasks such as managing user accounts, performing backups, and troubleshooting technical issues.

Each of these actors plays a specific role in the use case diagram, interacting with the system to achieve their goals. The use case diagram provides a high-level overview of the system and its functionality, helping to clarify the requirements and guide the development process.
