### Component Diagram for Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

A component diagram is a diagram used in the Unified Modeling Language (UML) to represent the structural relationship between the components of a system. In the context of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab, the component diagram would show the various components involved in the unit and their relationships.

Here are the steps to draw the component diagram for the notes of Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab:

1. Identify the components: The first step in creating a component diagram is to identify the components that are involved in the unit. These could include the lecture notes, lab exercises, assignments, and any other materials that are part of the unit.

2. Determine the relationships: The next step is to determine the relationships between the components. For example, the lecture notes may be related to the lab exercises, and the assignments may be related to both the lecture notes and the lab exercises.

3. Draw the diagram: Once the components and their relationships have been identified, the diagram can be drawn. Each component is represented by a rectangle with the component's name inside. The relationships between the components are represented by lines connecting the rectangles.
