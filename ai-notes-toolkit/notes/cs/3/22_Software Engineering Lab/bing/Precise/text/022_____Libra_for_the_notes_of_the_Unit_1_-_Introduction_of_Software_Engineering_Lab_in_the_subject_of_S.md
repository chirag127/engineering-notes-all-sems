### Libra

- Libra was a cryptocurrency created by Facebook, renamed into Diem at the end of December 2020 and wound down in January 2022.
- The Libra cryptocurrency was intended to be used as a simple, low-fee global blockchain-based digital currency.
- Facebook said that Libra would make sending money online cheaper and faster, and it would improve access to financial services, especially for people without bank accounts or with little access to banking.
