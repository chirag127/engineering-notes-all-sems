### Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

#### Precondition for the notes:
- The notes are intended for students studying the subject of Software Engineering Lab.
- The notes cover the introductory concepts of Software Engineering Lab.
- The notes are written in a formal style and are intended to be used as study material for exams.

#### Key Points:
1. Software Engineering is the application of engineering principles to the design, development, and maintenance of software.
2. The goal of Software Engineering is to produce high-quality software that meets the needs of its users, within budget and on time.
3. The Software Engineering Lab provides a practical, hands-on approach to learning the principles and practices of Software Engineering.
4. In the lab, students will work on projects that simulate real-world software development scenarios.
5. The lab will cover topics such as requirements analysis, design, coding, testing, and maintenance.
6. Students will learn to use tools and techniques commonly used in the software industry.
7. The lab will also cover best practices for software development, such as agile methodologies and version control.
