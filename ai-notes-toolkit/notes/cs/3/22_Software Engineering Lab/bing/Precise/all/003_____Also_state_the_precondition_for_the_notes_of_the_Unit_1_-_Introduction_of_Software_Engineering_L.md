# Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

## Precondition for the notes:
- The notes are intended for students studying the subject of Software Engineering Lab.
- The notes are intended to be used as study material for exams.
- The notes are written in a formal style and do not include external links or emojis.
- The notes are written in markdown format and the content is organized in points.

## Introduction to Software Engineering Lab:
- Software Engineering Lab is a subject that focuses on the practical application of software engineering principles and techniques.
- The subject covers various topics such as software development life cycle, software design, software testing, and software maintenance.
- The lab component of the subject provides hands-on experience in applying software engineering techniques to real-world problems.
- The goal of the subject is to equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary to develop high-quality software systems.
