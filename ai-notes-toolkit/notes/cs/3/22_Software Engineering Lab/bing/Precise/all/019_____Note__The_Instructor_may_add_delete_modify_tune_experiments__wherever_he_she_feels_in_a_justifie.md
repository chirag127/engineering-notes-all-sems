# Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

- Software Engineering Lab is a subject that focuses on the practical application of software engineering principles and techniques.
- The lab provides hands-on experience in designing, implementing, and testing software systems.
- The instructor may add, delete, modify, or tune experiments in a justified manner to enhance the learning experience.
- The experiments are designed to provide a deeper understanding of the concepts covered in the subject of Software Engineering.
- The lab provides an opportunity for students to apply their theoretical knowledge in a practical setting.
- The experiments are designed to be challenging and engaging, encouraging students to think critically and creatively.
- The lab provides a collaborative learning environment where students can work together to solve problems and learn from each other.
- The lab is an essential component of the Software Engineering curriculum, providing students with the skills and knowledge necessary to succeed in the field.
