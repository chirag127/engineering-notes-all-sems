### AgroUML

AgroUML is a tool used in the Software Engineering Lab for the creation of UML diagrams. It is part of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab in the subject of Software Engineering Lab.

- AgroUML is a free, open-source tool for creating UML diagrams.
- It supports the creation of various types of UML diagrams, including class diagrams, use case diagrams, sequence diagrams, and more.
- AgroUML is user-friendly and easy to use, making it a popular choice for students and professionals alike.
