### Why open source tools should be preferred to conduct the lab for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Software Engineering Lab

- Open source tools are software applications that are developed and maintained by a community of developers and users who share the source code and the license of the software.
- Open source tools have many advantages over proprietary or closed source tools, such as:
  - They are usually free or low-cost, which reduces the financial burden on students and educators.
  - They are more transparent and reliable, as the source code can be inspected and modified by anyone who has the skills and interest.
  - They are more innovative and diverse, as they benefit from the collective wisdom and creativity of the community.
  - They are more adaptable and flexible, as they can be customized and extended to suit different needs and preferences.
  - They are more compatible and interoperable, as they follow open standards and protocols that facilitate data exchange and integration.
  - They are more secure and ethical, as they respect the privacy and rights of the users and do not impose any restrictions or limitations on the use of the software.
