### System Failure

- A system failure is an event that causes the database to stop functioning normally and may result in data loss or corruption .
- A system failure can be caused by various factors, such as hardware malfunction, power outage, network disruption, software bugs, human errors, malicious attacks, natural disasters, etc   .
- A system failure can affect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the data stored in the database, which are the three main goals of database security .
- Confidentiality means that only authorized users can access the data and prevent unauthorized disclosure .
- Integrity means that the data is accurate, consistent, and complete and prevent unauthorized modification or deletion .
- Availability means that the data is accessible and usable by authorized users and prevent denial of service or disruption .
- To prevent or minimize the impact of system failures, database security best practices include   :
  - Implementing backup and recovery mechanisms to restore the database to a consistent state after a failure .
  - Applying encryption and authentication techniques to protect the data from unauthorized access or tampering .
  - Monitoring and auditing the database activities and performance to detect and respond to any anomalies or threats .
  - Updating and patching the database software and hardware to fix any vulnerabilities or bugs .
  - Educating and training the database users and administrators to follow the security policies and procedures and avoid human errors.
