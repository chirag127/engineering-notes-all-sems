- ER diagram stands for Entity Relationship Diagram, also known as ERD is a diagram that displays the relationship of entity sets stored in a database .
- ER diagrams help to explain the logical structure of databases .
- ER diagrams are created based on three basic concepts: entities, attributes and relationships .
- Entities are the objects or things that are important for the system. They are represented by rectangles in ER diagrams .
- Attributes are the properties or characteristics of the entities. They are represented by ovals in ER diagrams .
- Relationships are the associations or interactions between the entities. They are represented by diamonds or lines in ER diagrams .
- There are different notations or symbols for ER diagrams, such as crow's foot notation, arrow notation, Barker's notation, UML notation, etc  .
- Crow's foot notation is often recognized as the most intuitive style, as it uses simple and clear symbols to indicate the cardinality and optionality of the relationships .
- Arrow notation is simple and easily recognizable, as it uses single-headed or double-headed arrows to indicate the number of relationships entities can have.
- Barker's notation is based on the original notation developed by Peter Chen, and it uses boxes and lines to represent entities and relationships, with different symbols for different types of relationships.
- UML notation is based on the Unified Modeling Language, and it uses classes and associations to represent entities and relationships, with different symbols for different types of associations.
