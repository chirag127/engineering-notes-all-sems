
### BCNF for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Data Base Design & Normalization

1. Boyce-Codd Normal Form (BCNF) is a type of normal form that is used in database design to ensure that the database is free of certain types of anomalies.

2. BCNF is based on the concept of functional dependencies, which states that a given attribute is functionally dependent on another attribute if the value of the first attribute is determined by the value of the second attribute.

3. BCNF states that every determinant in a functional dependency must be a candidate key, meaning that it must be a minimal set of attributes that uniquely identify a row in the database.

4. BCNF eliminates certain types of anomalies, such as insertion, update, and deletion anomalies.

5. BCNF is stronger than other normal forms, such as third normal form (3NF) and fourth normal form (4NF).

6. BCNF is used to ensure that the data in the database is consistent and accurate.
