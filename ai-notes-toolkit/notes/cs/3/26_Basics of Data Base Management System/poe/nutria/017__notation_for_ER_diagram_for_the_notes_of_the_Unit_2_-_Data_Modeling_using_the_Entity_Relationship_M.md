
### Notation for ER Diagrams

ER diagrams are used to model the structure of a database. They are composed of entities, relationships, and attributes.

* **Entities** represent objects in the system, such as customers, orders, or products. Entities are represented by rectangles in an ER diagram.
* **Relationships** show the connection between entities. Relationships are represented by diamonds in an ER diagram.
* **Attributes** are the properties of an entity. Attributes are represented by ovals in an ER diagram.

ER diagrams also use lines to show the relationships between entities. A single line indicates a one-to-one relationship, while a double line indicates a one-to-many relationship.
