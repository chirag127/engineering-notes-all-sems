
### Attributes 
* An attribute is a property or characteristic of an entity that describes the entity. 
* In the context of relational databases, an attribute is a column in a table that describes a particular entity. 
* Attributes are also known as fields or columns. 
* Each attribute has a name and a data type. 
* Examples of attribute data types include text, number, date, and Boolean. 
* Attributes can also have constraints, such as being required, unique, or limited to a range of values. 
* Attributes can be used to uniquely identify an entity, and are often used to form relationships between entities.
