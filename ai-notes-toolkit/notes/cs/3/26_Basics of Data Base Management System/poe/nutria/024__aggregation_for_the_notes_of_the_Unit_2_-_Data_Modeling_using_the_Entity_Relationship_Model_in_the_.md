
### Aggregation 

1. Aggregation is a concept in data modeling used to represent a relationship between two entities. 
2. An entity relationship model (ERM) is a diagram that shows the relationships between entities in a database. 
3. An aggregation relationship is represented by a diamond shape connecting two entities. 
4. It is used to represent a one-to-many relationship between two entities. 
5. For example, in an ERM, a student entity may have a one-to-many relationship with a course entity. 
6. The student entity is the “one” side of the relationship, and the course entity is the “many” side of the relationship. 
7. This means that a student may take multiple courses, but a course can only be taken by one student. 
8. Aggregation relationships can also be used to represent a many-to-many relationship between two entities. 
9. For example, a student entity may have a many-to-many relationship with a teacher entity. 
10. This means that a student may have multiple teachers, and a teacher may have multiple students.
