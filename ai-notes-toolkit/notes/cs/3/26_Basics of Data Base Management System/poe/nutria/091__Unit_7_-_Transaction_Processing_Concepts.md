
## Unit 7 - Transaction Processing Concepts

1. Transaction Processing is a type of computing that is focused on the recording of business transactions. It is the process of capturing, storing, and processing data related to transactions.

2. Transaction Processing Systems (TPS) are computer systems used to record business transactions. They are designed to capture data quickly and accurately, and to provide information to decision makers in a timely manner.

3. Transaction Processing involves the capture, storage, and processing of data related to a business transaction. This data includes information about the customer, product, payment, and delivery.

4. Transaction Processing Systems are used to process a variety of transactions, including sales, purchases, payments, and returns.

5. Transaction Processing Systems are designed to ensure accuracy, reliability, and security of data. The data is stored in a secure database, and is subject to rigorous security standards.

6. Transaction Processing Systems are designed to provide quick and accurate feedback to decision makers. The system is designed to provide information in a timely manner, and to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
