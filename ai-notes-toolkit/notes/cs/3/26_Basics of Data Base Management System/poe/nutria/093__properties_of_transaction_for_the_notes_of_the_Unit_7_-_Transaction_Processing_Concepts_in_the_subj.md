
### Properties of Transactions

1. **Atomicity**: A transaction must be an atomic unit of work, meaning it must either be completed in its entirety or not at all.
2. **Consistency**: A transaction must ensure that the database remains in a consistent state.
3. **Isolation**: Transactions must be isolated from one another, meaning that the operations of one transaction must not affect the operations of another transaction.
4. **Durability**: The results of a transaction must be durable, meaning that once a transaction has been committed, its effects must persist even in the event of a system failure.
