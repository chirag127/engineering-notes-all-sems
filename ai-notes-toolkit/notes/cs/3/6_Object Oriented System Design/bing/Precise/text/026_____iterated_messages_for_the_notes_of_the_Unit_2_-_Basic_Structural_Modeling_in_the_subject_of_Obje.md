### Iterated Messages

- Iterated messages are used in sequence diagrams to represent a repetitive action.
- They are depicted using a frame with a guard condition in square brackets, which specifies the condition under which the iteration occurs.
- The frame is labeled with the keyword `loop` and contains the message that is being repeated.
- The iteration can occur a specific number of times or until a certain condition is met.
- Iterated messages are useful for modeling repetitive behavior such as looping through a collection of objects or retrying an operation until it succeeds.
