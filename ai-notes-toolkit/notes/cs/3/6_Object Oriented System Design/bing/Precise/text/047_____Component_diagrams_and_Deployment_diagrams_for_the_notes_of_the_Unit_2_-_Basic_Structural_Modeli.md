### Component diagrams and Deployment diagrams for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

- **Component diagrams** are used to represent the physical components of a system and their relationships. These diagrams show the components of a system, their interfaces, and the dependencies between them.

- **Deployment diagrams** are used to represent the physical architecture of a system and the distribution of its components. These diagrams show the nodes of a system, the components that are deployed on them, and the communication links between them.

- Both component and deployment diagrams are used to model the static aspects of a system, and are part of the Unified Modeling Language (UML).

- Component diagrams are useful for modeling the structure of a system, while deployment diagrams are useful for modeling its deployment and distribution.

- In component diagrams, components are represented as rectangles with optional component icons, and interfaces are represented as small circles or "lollipops" attached to the components.

- In deployment diagrams, nodes are represented as cubes, and components are shown as rectangles within the nodes. Communication links between nodes are represented as lines with optional arrows to indicate the direction of communication.

- Both component and deployment diagrams can be used to model complex systems, and can be used in conjunction with other UML diagrams to provide a complete view of the system being modeled.

- These diagrams are useful for system architects, developers, and other stakeholders to understand the physical structure and distribution of a system. They can also be used to identify potential bottlenecks or areas for optimization in the system's architecture.

- In summary, component and deployment diagrams are important tools for modeling the physical aspects of a system, and can provide valuable insights into its structure and distribution. They are part of the UML, and can be used in conjunction with other UML diagrams to provide a complete view of the system being modeled.
