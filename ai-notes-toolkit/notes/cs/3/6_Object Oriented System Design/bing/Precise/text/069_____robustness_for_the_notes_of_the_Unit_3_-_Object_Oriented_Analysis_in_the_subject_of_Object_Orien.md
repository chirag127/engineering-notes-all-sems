### Robustness
- Robustness refers to the ability of a system to continue functioning correctly even in the presence of invalid or unexpected inputs.
- In the context of object-oriented analysis, robustness is an important consideration when designing classes and their interactions.
- A robust system is able to handle errors and exceptions gracefully, without crashing or producing incorrect results.
- Robustness can be achieved through techniques such as input validation, error handling, and defensive programming.
- In object-oriented design, the use of encapsulation and information hiding can also contribute to the robustness of a system by limiting the potential for unintended interactions between objects.
- Testing and debugging are also important for ensuring the robustness of a system.
- Overall, the goal of robustness is to create a system that is reliable and resilient, able to withstand a wide range of usage scenarios and unexpected events.
