### Collaboration Diagrams

Collaboration diagrams are used in Object Oriented System Design to represent the structural organization of a system and the messages that are sent between objects within it. They are a part of Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling.

Some key points to remember about collaboration diagrams are:

1. Collaboration diagrams show the interactions between objects in a system.
2. They depict the relationships between objects and the messages that are sent between them.
3. Collaboration diagrams can be used to model both static and dynamic aspects of a system.
4. They are useful for understanding the flow of control and data within a system.
5. Collaboration diagrams can be used to identify potential design issues and to improve the overall structure of a system.

In summary, collaboration diagrams are a valuable tool for modeling the structural organization of a system and the interactions between its objects. They can help to improve the design of a system by identifying potential issues and suggesting solutions.
