### Architectural Modeling

Architectural modeling is a technique used in object-oriented system design to represent the overall structure and organization of a system. It is a part of the basic structural modeling process in Unit 2 of the subject of Object Oriented System Design. Some key points to consider when studying architectural modeling are:

1. Architectural modeling provides a high-level view of the system and its components.
2. It helps to identify the major components of the system and their relationships.
3. It is used to represent the static structure of the system, including its classes, objects, and their relationships.
4. Architectural modeling is an important step in the design process as it helps to ensure that the system is well-structured and organized.
5. It can be represented using various notations and diagrams, such as class diagrams and component diagrams.
6. Architectural modeling is an iterative process and may be refined and updated as the design process progresses.
