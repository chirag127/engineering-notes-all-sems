### Interaction Diagram
Interaction diagrams are used in Object Oriented System Design to model the dynamic behavior of a system. They are used to visualize how objects interact with each other to perform a specific task. There are two types of interaction diagrams: sequence diagrams and collaboration diagrams.

1. **Sequence Diagrams:** A sequence diagram shows the sequence of messages that are sent between objects in a system. It is used to model the flow of control and data within a system. The diagram consists of vertical lines representing the lifelines of objects, and horizontal arrows representing the messages sent between them.

2. **Collaboration Diagrams:** A collaboration diagram, also known as a communication diagram, shows the relationships between objects in a system. It is used to model the interactions between objects, including the messages that are sent between them. The diagram consists of boxes representing the objects, and arrows representing the messages sent between them.

Interaction diagrams are useful for understanding the behavior of a system and for identifying potential problems or areas for improvement. They are commonly used in the design phase of software development to model the interactions between objects in a system.
