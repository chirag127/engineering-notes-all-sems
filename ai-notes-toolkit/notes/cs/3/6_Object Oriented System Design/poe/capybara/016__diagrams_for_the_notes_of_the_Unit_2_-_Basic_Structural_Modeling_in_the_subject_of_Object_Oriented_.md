### Diagrams for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

In the field of Object Oriented System Design, diagrams play a crucial role in communicating the structure of a system in a clear and concise manner. The following are some of the most commonly used diagrams for representing structural models:

- **Class Diagrams:** Class diagrams are used to represent the static structure of a system by showing the classes, their attributes, and their relationships with other classes. They are the backbone of Object Oriented Design and serve as a blueprint for the system.

- **Object Diagrams:** Object diagrams are used to represent instances of classes and their relationships with other objects at a specific point in time. They are useful for testing and debugging software systems.

- **Package Diagrams:** Package diagrams are used to represent the organization of software components into logical groupings called packages. They help to manage complexity by breaking down large systems into smaller, more manageable pieces.

- **Component Diagrams:** Component diagrams are used to represent the physical components of a system and their dependencies. They are useful for understanding the deployment of software systems.

- **Composite Structure Diagrams:** Composite structure diagrams are used to represent the internal structure of a complex object or a class. They show the parts of a system and how they are connected.

- **Deployment Diagrams:** Deployment diagrams are used to represent the physical deployment of software components on hardware nodes. They are useful for understanding the distribution of a system across different machines.

In conclusion, diagrams are an essential tool for representing the structural models of Object Oriented Systems. Class diagrams, object diagrams, package diagrams, component diagrams, composite structure diagrams, and deployment diagrams are just a few examples of the most commonly used diagrams in the field. By mastering the use of these diagrams, designers can create clear and concise representations of complex systems that are easy to understand and maintain.
