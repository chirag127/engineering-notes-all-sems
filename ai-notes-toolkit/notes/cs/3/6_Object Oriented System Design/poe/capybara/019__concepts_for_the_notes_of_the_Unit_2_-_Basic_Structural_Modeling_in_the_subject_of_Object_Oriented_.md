### Concepts for the Notes of Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the Subject of Object Oriented System Design

In this unit, we will be learning about the basic structural modeling concepts in Object Oriented System Design. Let's take a look at some of the important concepts that will be covered:

- **Class Diagrams**: Class diagrams are used to describe the static structure of a system. They show the classes in a system, their attributes, operations and the relationships among them. Class diagrams are an important tool for communicating design decisions to stakeholders.

- **Object Diagrams**: Object diagrams are similar to class diagrams, but they show a snapshot of the system at a particular point in time. They show the objects in the system, their attributes and the relationships among them. Object diagrams are useful for visualizing the current state of a system.

- **Composite Structure Diagrams**: Composite structure diagrams show the internal structure of a class, including its parts and their relationships. They are useful for modeling complex systems that have many parts.

- **Package Diagrams**: Package diagrams are used to organize classes into packages, which are units of functionality. They show the dependencies among packages and can be used to manage the complexity of large systems.

- **Component Diagrams**: Component diagrams show the physical components of a system and how they interact. They are useful for modeling systems that are distributed across multiple machines.

- **Deployment Diagrams**: Deployment diagrams show the physical deployment of a system, including the hardware and software components, and the connections among them. They are useful for modeling systems that are deployed in a networked environment.

These are some of the important concepts that you will be learning in this unit. Make sure to study them well, as they are fundamental to understanding Object Oriented System Design.
