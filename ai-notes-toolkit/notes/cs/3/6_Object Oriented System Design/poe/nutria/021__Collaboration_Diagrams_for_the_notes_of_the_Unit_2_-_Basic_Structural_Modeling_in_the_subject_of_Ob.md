
### Collaboration Diagrams for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in the subject of Object Oriented System Design

1. A collaboration diagram is a type of UML diagram that is used to show the interactions between objects or components in a system. 
2. It is used to illustrate the relationships between objects in a system and how they interact with one another. 
3. Collaboration diagrams are a useful tool for visualizing the flow of information between objects and for understanding how the objects interact with each other.
4. The diagram consists of a set of objects (or components) connected by lines representing communication between them. 
5. The arrows indicate the direction of communication, while the boxes represent the objects or components. 
6. A collaboration diagram can be used to model the behavior of an object-oriented system in terms of the messages exchanged between objects. 
7. It is also useful for understanding the sequence of events that occur during the system's execution.
8. Collaboration diagrams can be used to identify potential problems in a system's design, such as deadlocks, race conditions, and resource starvation. 
9. They can also be used to identify opportunities for optimization and refactoring.
