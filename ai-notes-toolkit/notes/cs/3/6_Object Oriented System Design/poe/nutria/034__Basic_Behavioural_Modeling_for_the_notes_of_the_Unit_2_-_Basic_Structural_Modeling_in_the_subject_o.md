
### Basic Behavioural Modeling

1. Behavioural modeling is a way to capture the behavior of a system by using a set of models.

2. It is a form of abstraction which allows the system to be viewed from different perspectives.

3. Behavioural modeling is used to represent the behavior of a system through a set of models that are related to each other. 

4. It is used to design the system architecture and to understand how the system will behave in different scenarios.

5. Behavioural modeling can be used to capture the behavior of a system at different levels of abstraction.

6. It is used to define the system requirements, to design the system architecture, to develop the system and to validate the system.

7. Behavioural modeling can be used to capture the behavior of a system in terms of its inputs, outputs, states and events.

8. It is used to represent the behavior of a system in terms of its components and their interactions.

9. It can be used to capture the behavior of a system in terms of its processes, activities and functions.

10. Behavioural modeling can be used to capture the behavior of a system in terms of its rules and constraints.
