
### Introduction to UML

1. UML (Unified Modeling Language) is a graphical language for representing and documenting object-oriented systems. 
2. It is used to describe the structure and behavior of a system, and to visualize the relationships between different elements within the system.
3. UML diagrams are composed of three main components: classes, objects and relationships. 
4. Classes represent the structure of a system and are composed of attributes and methods. 
5. Objects are instances of classes and are used to represent the data that is stored in a system. 
6. Relationships describe how objects interact with one another and define the behavior of a system. 
7. UML diagrams can be used to model a wide range of systems, including software applications, databases, business processes, and more. 
8. UML is an important tool for understanding and designing object-oriented systems, as it provides a visual representation of the system’s structure and behavior.
