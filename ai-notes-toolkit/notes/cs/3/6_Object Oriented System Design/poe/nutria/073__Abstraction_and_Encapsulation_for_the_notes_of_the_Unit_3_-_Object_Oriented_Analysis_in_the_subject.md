
### Abstraction and Encapsulation

- Abstraction is the process of reducing a complex problem into simpler components that can be better understood and managed. It involves the identification and elimination of unnecessary details to focus on the essential features of a problem.

- Encapsulation is the process of bundling data and related operations into a single unit. It can be used to hide the inner workings of an object from the outside world, thus protecting the integrity of the object.

- Abstraction and encapsulation are two important concepts in object-oriented programming. Abstraction is used to simplify the complexity of a system, while encapsulation is used to hide the implementation details of an object.

- Abstraction and encapsulation can be used together to create highly efficient and secure systems. By abstracting the components of a system, it is easier to develop and maintain the system. Encapsulation ensures that implementation details are hidden from the outside world, thus providing a secure system.
