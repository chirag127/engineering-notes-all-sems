
### Unit 2 - Basic Structural Modeling in Object Oriented System Design

1. Structural modeling is a process of designing and analyzing the structure of a system. It involves the identification of components, their relationships, and their interactions.

2. Structural modeling is used to identify the system's components and their relationships, as well as to analyze the system's architecture.

3. Object-oriented modeling is a type of structural modeling that focuses on the objects, their attributes, and the relationships between them.

4. Object-oriented modeling is used to create a blueprint for the system's architecture and to identify the objects and their relationships.

5. Object-oriented modeling is used to analyze the system's architecture and to identify areas for improvement.

6. Object-oriented modeling is used to create an efficient and effective system design.

7. Object-oriented modeling is used to create a system that is easy to maintain and modify.
