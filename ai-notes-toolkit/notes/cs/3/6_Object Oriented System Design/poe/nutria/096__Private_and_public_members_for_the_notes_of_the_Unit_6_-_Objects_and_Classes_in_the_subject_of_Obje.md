
### Private and Public Members

* Private members are the members of a class that are not accessible from outside the class. They can only be accessed by other members of the same class.
* Public members are the members of a class that are accessible from outside the class. They can be accessed by other classes as well.
* Private members are typically used to store data that should not be exposed to the outside world.
* Public members are typically used to provide access to the data stored in the private members.
* Private members can also be used to implement certain behaviors that should not be exposed to the outside world.
* Public members can also be used to provide access to the behaviors implemented in the private members.
