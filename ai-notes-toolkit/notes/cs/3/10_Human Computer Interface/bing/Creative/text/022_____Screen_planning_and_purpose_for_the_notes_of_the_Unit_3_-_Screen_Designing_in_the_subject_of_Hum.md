### Screen planning and purpose

- Screen planning is the process of designing and organizing the layout, content, and functionality of a user interface for a computer system or application.
- Screen planning aims to create a screen that is easy to use, efficient, effective, and satisfying for the user, as well as consistent with the overall design goals and requirements of the system or application.
- Screen planning involves the following steps:
  - Define the purpose and objectives of the screen: what is the screen supposed to do, what information or actions does it provide or support, who are the intended users, and what are their needs and expectations?
  - Identify the screen elements and components: what are the main components of the screen, such as menus, buttons, text fields, images, icons, etc., and how are they related to each other and to the screen purpose and objectives?
  - Arrange the screen elements and components: how are the screen elements and components positioned, aligned, grouped, and spaced on the screen, and how do they follow the principles of visual hierarchy, balance, contrast, and harmony?
  - Apply the screen design guidelines and standards: what are the general and specific rules and conventions for screen design, such as color, font, size, shape, style, etc., and how do they ensure the usability, accessibility, and aesthetics of the screen?
