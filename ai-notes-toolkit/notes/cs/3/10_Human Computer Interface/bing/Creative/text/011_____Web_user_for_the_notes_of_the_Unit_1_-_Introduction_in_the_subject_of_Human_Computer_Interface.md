### Web user for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of interaction between people (users) and computers  .
- HCI deals with the design, execution and assessment of computer systems and related phenomenon that are for human use .
- HCI can be used in all disciplines wherever there is a possibility of computer installation.
- The basic goal of HCI is to improve the interactions between users and computers by making computers more usable and receptive to the user's needs .
- The long term goal of HCI is to design systems that minimize the barrier between the human's cognitive model of what they want to accomplish and the computer's understanding of the user's task.
- The foundations of HCI include the following aspects:
  - The Human: I/O channels, Memory, Reasoning and problem solving
  - The Computer: Devices, Memory, Processing and networks
  - The Interaction: Models, Frameworks, Ergonomics, Styles, Elements, Interactivity, Paradigms
- The scope and challenges of HCI and Interaction Design include the following topics:
  - Visual representation: Segmentation and variables of the display plane, Modes of correspondence
  - Text and gesture interaction: Text input and output, Pointing and dragging, Menus and forms, Direct manipulation and virtual environments
  - Cognitive models: Goals and tasks, Plans and methods, Operators and actions, Knowledge and skills, Errors and feedback
  - Social and organizational aspects: Communication and collaboration, Privacy and trust, Culture and diversity, Accessibility and inclusion
  - Evaluation techniques: Usability testing, Heuristic evaluation, User studies, Experimental design, Statistics and analysis
