### Icons and increases for the notes of the Unit 4 - Windows in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Icons are graphical symbols that represent objects, actions, or concepts in a user interface.
- Icons can help users to identify, locate, and select items on the screen, as well as to perform tasks or access functions.
- Icons can also convey information, such as status, feedback, or notifications, to the users.
- Icons should be designed to be clear, consistent, recognizable, and appropriate for the context and the target audience.
- Icons should follow the principles of good visual design, such as simplicity, balance, contrast, alignment, and harmony.
- Icons should also follow the conventions and guidelines of the platform or system they are used in, such as size, shape, color, style, and placement.
- Increases are techniques that enhance the visibility, legibility, or accessibility of icons or other graphical elements on the screen.
- Increases can help users to see, read, or interact with icons more easily, especially for users with low vision, color blindness, or other impairments.
- Increases can include magnification, enlargement, highlighting, color adjustment, contrast adjustment, or text labels.
- Increases should be applied with care and moderation, as they can also introduce clutter, confusion, or distraction to the user interface.
- Increases should also be adjustable, customizable, or optional for the users, as different users may have different preferences or needs.
