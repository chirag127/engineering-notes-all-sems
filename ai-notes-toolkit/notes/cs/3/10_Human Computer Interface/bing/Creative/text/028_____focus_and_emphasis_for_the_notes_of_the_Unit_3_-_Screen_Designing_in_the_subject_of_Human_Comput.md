### Focus and Emphasis for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

- Focus and emphasis are important principles of screen design that help to guide the user's attention and convey the meaning and importance of the information presented on the screen.
- Focus and emphasis can be achieved by using various techniques, such as contrast, alignment, proximity, repetition, hierarchy, color, typography, whitespace, and animation.
- Contrast is the difference between two or more elements on the screen, such as size, shape, color, brightness, or texture. Contrast can be used to create focal points, highlight important information, or separate different sections of the screen.
- Alignment is the arrangement of elements on the screen according to a common edge, axis, or center. Alignment can be used to create order, balance, harmony, and consistency on the screen. Alignment can also help to establish visual relationships and connections between elements.
- Proximity is the closeness or distance of elements on the screen. Proximity can be used to group or separate elements, indicate similarity or difference, or create associations or distinctions. Proximity can also affect the perception of size and space on the screen.
- Repetition is the use of the same or similar elements on the screen, such as shapes, colors, fonts, or images. Repetition can be used to create unity, coherence, rhythm, and pattern on the screen. Repetition can also help to reinforce the identity and style of the screen.
- Hierarchy is the arrangement of elements on the screen according to their relative importance, significance, or function. Hierarchy can be used to create structure, organization, logic, and clarity on the screen. Hierarchy can also help to guide the user's eye movement and navigation on the screen.
- Color is the hue, saturation, and brightness of an element on the screen. Color can be used to create mood, emotion, atmosphere, and personality on the screen. Color can also help to convey meaning, symbolism, and cultural values on the screen.
- Typography is the style, size, and arrangement of text on the screen. Typography can be used to create contrast, hierarchy, alignment, and repetition on the screen. Typography can also help to communicate the tone, voice, and message of the text on the screen.
- Whitespace is the empty or negative space on the screen. Whitespace can be used to create contrast, balance, focus, and emphasis on the screen. Whitespace can also help to reduce clutter, increase readability, and enhance aesthetics on the screen.
- Animation is the movement or change of elements on the screen over time. Animation can be used to create interest, engagement, interactivity, and feedback on the screen. Animation can also help to illustrate processes, transitions, and transformations on the screen.
