### Selection of device based and screen based controls for windows

- Device based controls are the mechanisms through which people communicate their desires to the system, such as keyboard, mouse, touch screen, speech recognition, etc. 
- Screen based controls are the graphical components that are available on the screen, such as buttons, text boxes, menus, lists, icons, etc. 
- The selection of device based and screen based controls depends on several factors, such as:
  - The nature of the task and the user's goals
  - The characteristics and capabilities of the devices and controls
  - The advantages and disadvantages of each device and control
  - The consistency and compatibility of the devices and controls with the system and the user's expectations
  - The usability and accessibility of the devices and controls for different types of users and situations
- Some general guidelines for selecting device based and screen based controls are:
  - Use device based controls that are familiar, intuitive, and easy to operate for the user 
  - Use screen based controls that are clear, concise, and meaningful for the user 
  - Use device based controls that are appropriate for the type and amount of input required by the system 
  - Use screen based controls that are appropriate for the type and amount of output displayed by the system 
  - Use device based controls that are compatible with the screen based controls and vice versa 
  - Use screen based controls that are consistent with the system's functionality and appearance 
  - Use device based controls that are adaptable and flexible for different user preferences and situations 
  - Use screen based controls that are responsive and provide feedback to the user
