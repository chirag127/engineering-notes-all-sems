### Selection of Device-Based and Screen-Based Controls for Windows

Device-based controls, often called input devices, are the mechanisms through which people communicate their desires to the system . Screen-based controls, often called widgets, are the graphical elements that display information or allow interaction on the screen.

Some of the factors that influence the selection of device-based and screen-based controls for windows are:

- The characteristics and capabilities of the device-based controls, such as pointing, selecting, entering, and manipulating .
- The characteristics and capabilities of the screen-based controls, such as appearance, behavior, feedback, and compatibility.
- The nature and purpose of the window, such as primary, secondary, dialog, or pop-up.
- The type and amount of information to be displayed or entered in the window, such as text, graphics, numbers, or commands.
- The user's preferences, expectations, and skills, such as familiarity, comfort, and efficiency .

Some of the guidelines for selecting device-based and screen-based controls for windows are:

- Use device-based controls that are appropriate for the task and the user, such as mouse, keyboard, touch screen, or voice input .
- Use screen-based controls that are consistent, clear, concise, and responsive, such as buttons, menus, icons, or sliders.
- Use multiple displays to extend the workspace, enhance the visibility, or compare the information, if needed .
- Use the Windows + P shortcut to quickly change how Windows 10 handles multiple displays, such as duplicate, extend, or switch.
- Use the Settings > System > Display menu to adjust the display settings, such as resolution, orientation, scale, or layout .
