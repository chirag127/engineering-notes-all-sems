### Human interaction with computers

- Human interaction with computers is the field of study that focuses on optimizing how users and computers interact by designing interactive computer interfaces that satisfy users’ needs.
- It is a multidisciplinary subject covering computer science, behavioral sciences, cognitive science, ergonomics, psychology, and design principles .
- Human interaction with computers is also sometimes termed human–machine interaction (HMI), man-machine interaction (MMI) or computer-human interaction (CHI).
- Human interaction with computers is research in the design and the use of computer technology, which focuses on the interfaces between people (users) and computers.
- Human interaction with computers researchers observe the ways humans interact with computers and design technologies that allow humans to interact with computers in novel ways.
- Human interaction with computers is a subfield within computer science concerned with the study of the interaction between people (users) and computers and the design, evaluation and implementation of user interfaces for computer systems that are receptive to the user's needs and habits.
- Human interaction with computers explores the interfaces between people and technologies, such as desktops, laptops, phones, tablets, virtual reality, wearable devices, the Internet of Things, and robotics.
- Human interaction with computers incorporates multiple disciplines, such as computer science, psychology, human factors, and ergonomics, into one field.
- Human interaction with computers helps to create intuitive and usable interfaces that enhance the user experience and satisfaction.
