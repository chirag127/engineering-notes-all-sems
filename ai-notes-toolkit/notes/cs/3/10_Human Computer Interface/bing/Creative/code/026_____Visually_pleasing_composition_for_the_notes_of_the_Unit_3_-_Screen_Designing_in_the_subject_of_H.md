# Visually pleasing composition for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Screen designing is the process of creating the layout, appearance, and functionality of the user interface (UI) of a software application, website, or mobile app.
- Screen designing involves applying the principles of visual design, such as color, typography, alignment, balance, contrast, and hierarchy, to create a UI that is aesthetically pleasing, easy to use, and consistent with the brand identity and user expectations.
- Visually pleasing composition is the arrangement of the visual elements on the screen, such as text, images, icons, buttons, menus, and navigation, in a way that creates harmony, unity, and clarity for the user.
- Visually pleasing composition can enhance the user experience (UX) by improving the readability, usability, and accessibility of the UI, as well as conveying the mood, tone, and message of the content.
- Some of the factors that affect the visually pleasing composition are:

  - **Grid system**: A grid system is a set of horizontal and vertical lines that divide the screen into columns, rows, and margins, and provide a framework for organizing the visual elements. A grid system can help create a consistent, structured, and aligned layout that follows the natural eye movement of the user.
  - **Whitespace**: Whitespace, also known as negative space, is the empty space between and around the visual elements. Whitespace can help create contrast, balance, and hierarchy, as well as reduce visual clutter and cognitive load for the user. Whitespace can also guide the user's attention and focus to the most important information on the screen.
  - **Color scheme**: A color scheme is a combination of colors that are used for the UI elements, such as background, text, images, icons, buttons, and links. A color scheme can help create contrast, harmony, and mood, as well as reinforce the brand identity and user expectations. A color scheme should be chosen based on the color theory, such as complementary, analogous, triadic, or monochromatic, and the color psychology, such as warm, cool, or neutral.
  - **Typography**: Typography is the art and technique of arranging and styling the text on the screen, such as font, size, weight, style, spacing, and alignment. Typography can help create readability, hierarchy, and personality, as well as convey the tone, voice, and message of the content. Typography should be chosen based on the legibility, readability, and suitability for the target audience and the medium.
  - **Visual hierarchy**: Visual hierarchy is the order of importance and prominence of the visual elements on the screen, based on their size, color, contrast, position, and shape. Visual hierarchy can help create clarity, emphasis, and direction, as well as guide the user's eye movement and scanning behavior. Visual hierarchy should be established based on the goals, tasks, and needs of the user and the content.
