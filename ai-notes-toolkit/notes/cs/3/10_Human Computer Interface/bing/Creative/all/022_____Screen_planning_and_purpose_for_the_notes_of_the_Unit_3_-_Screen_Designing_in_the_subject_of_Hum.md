# Screen planning and purpose

- Screen planning is the process of designing and organizing the layout, content, and functionality of a user interface for a computer system or application.
- Screen planning aims to create a screen that is easy to use, effective, and aesthetically pleasing for the intended users and tasks.
- Screen planning involves the following steps:
  - Define the screen purpose: what is the goal or objective of the screen? what information or actions does it provide or support?
  - Define the screen audience: who are the users of the screen? what are their characteristics, needs, preferences, and expectations?
  - Define the screen content: what are the elements that need to be displayed on the screen? what are their types, formats, and sources?
  - Define the screen functionality: what are the interactions that need to be enabled on the screen? what are their types, modes, and feedback?
  - Define the screen layout: how are the elements arranged on the screen? what are the principles, guidelines, and conventions that govern the screen layout?
  - Define the screen style: what are the visual and auditory aspects of the screen? what are the colors, fonts, icons, sounds, and animations that enhance the screen appearance and usability?
- Screen planning requires the use of various tools and techniques, such as:
  - Screen sketches: rough drawings or diagrams that show the basic structure and components of a screen
  - Screen prototypes: mock-ups or simulations that show the appearance and behavior of a screen
  - Screen specifications: documents that describe the details and requirements of a screen
  - Screen evaluation: methods that test and measure the usability and effectiveness of a screen
- Screen planning is an iterative and collaborative process that involves the input and feedback of various stakeholders, such as:
  - Screen designers: the people who create and implement the screen design
  - Screen developers: the people who code and program the screen functionality
  - Screen users: the people who use the screen for their tasks and goals
  - Screen evaluators: the people who assess and improve the screen quality and performance
