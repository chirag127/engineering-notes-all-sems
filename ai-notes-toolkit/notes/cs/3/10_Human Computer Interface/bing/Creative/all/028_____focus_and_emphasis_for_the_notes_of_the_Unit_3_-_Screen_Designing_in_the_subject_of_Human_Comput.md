# Focus and Emphasis for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

- Focus and emphasis are important principles of screen design that help to guide the user's attention and convey the meaning and importance of the information on the screen.
- Focus and emphasis can be achieved by using various techniques, such as contrast, alignment, proximity, repetition, hierarchy, color, typography, whitespace, and animation.
- Contrast is the difference between two or more elements on the screen, such as size, shape, color, brightness, or texture. Contrast can be used to create focal points, highlight important information, or separate different sections of the screen.
- Alignment is the arrangement of elements on the screen in relation to each other or to a common reference point, such as the edge, center, or baseline. Alignment can be used to create order, balance, harmony, or unity on the screen.
- Proximity is the closeness or distance of elements on the screen. Proximity can be used to group related elements, separate unrelated elements, or create visual hierarchy on the screen.
- Repetition is the use of the same or similar elements on the screen, such as color, shape, font, or style. Repetition can be used to create consistency, coherence, or rhythm on the screen.
- Hierarchy is the arrangement of elements on the screen according to their relative importance, such as size, position, color, or style. Hierarchy can be used to show the structure, organization, or priority of the information on the screen.
- Color is the hue, saturation, and brightness of an element on the screen. Color can be used to create contrast, mood, emotion, or symbolism on the screen.
- Typography is the choice and arrangement of fonts, typefaces, and text on the screen. Typography can be used to create contrast, hierarchy, readability, or personality on the screen.
- Whitespace is the empty or negative space on the screen. Whitespace can be used to create contrast, balance, clarity, or elegance on the screen.
- Animation is the movement or change of elements on the screen over time. Animation can be used to create interest, attention, feedback, or interaction on the screen.
