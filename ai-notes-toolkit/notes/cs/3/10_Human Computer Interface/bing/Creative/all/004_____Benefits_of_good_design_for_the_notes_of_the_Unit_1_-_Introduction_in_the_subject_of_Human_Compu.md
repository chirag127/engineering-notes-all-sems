# Benefits of good design for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Good design can enhance the usability, functionality, and aesthetics of a product or system.
- Good design can improve the user experience, satisfaction, and loyalty of the customers or users.
- Good design can reduce the errors, frustrations, and costs associated with the use of a product or system.
- Good design can increase the productivity, efficiency, and effectiveness of the tasks or goals that the product or system supports.
- Good design can foster the creativity, innovation, and collaboration of the designers and developers of the product or system.
- Good design can comply with the standards, guidelines, and principles of human-computer interaction, such as usability, accessibility, and user-centered design.
