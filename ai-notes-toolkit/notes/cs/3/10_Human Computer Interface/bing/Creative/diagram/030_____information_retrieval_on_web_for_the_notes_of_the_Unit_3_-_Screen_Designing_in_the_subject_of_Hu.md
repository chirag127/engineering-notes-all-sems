### Information retrieval on web

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding and accessing relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as the web.
- IR involves matching a user's query, which is a formal statement of information need, to the best information about that query.
- IR models are ways of integrating various sources of evidence about documents, such as the links, the structure, the content, the quality, etc., to rank them according to their relevance to the query.
- IR models can be classified into three main categories: Boolean, vector space, and probabilistic.
  - Boolean models use logical operators (AND, OR, NOT) to specify the exact terms that must or must not appear in the documents.
  - Vector space models represent documents and queries as vectors of term weights, and use cosine similarity or other measures to compute their similarity.
  - Probabilistic models use statistical methods to estimate the probability of relevance of documents given a query, based on the occurrence of terms in the documents and the collection.
- IR on the web poses some unique challenges and opportunities, such as:
  - The web is very large, dynamic, heterogeneous, and unstructured, which makes indexing, crawling, and ranking more difficult.
  - The web contains rich information, such as images, videos, links, metadata, etc., which can be used to enhance the retrieval process and the user experience.
  - The web users are diverse, with different backgrounds, preferences, intents, and devices, which requires personalization, adaptation, and optimization of the retrieval system.
  - The web users are often looking for direct information, such as answers to questions, or seeking to complete tasks, such as booking appointments, which requires natural language understanding and generation, as well as dialogue and interaction capabilities.
- IR on the web can be applied to various domains and tasks, such as web search engines, recommender systems, question answering systems, information extraction systems, etc.
