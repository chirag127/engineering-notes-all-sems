### Web user for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of interaction between people (users) and computers  .
- HCI deals with the design, execution and assessment of computer systems and related phenomenon that are for human use .
- HCI can be used in all disciplines wherever there is a possibility of computer installation.
- The basic goal of HCI is to improve the interactions between users and computers by making computers more usable and receptive to the user's needs .
- The long term goal of HCI is to design systems that minimize the barrier between the human's cognitive model of what they want to accomplish and the computer's understanding of the user's task.
- HCI involves three main components: the human, the computer and the interaction  .
- The human component includes the input/output channels, memory, reasoning and problem solving abilities of the user  .
- The computer component includes the devices, memory, processing and networks that enable the system to function  .
- The interaction component includes the models, frameworks, ergonomics, styles, elements and paradigms that define how the user and the system communicate  .
- HCI is a multidisciplinary field that draws from computer science, psychology, design, engineering, sociology, anthropology and other domains  .
- HCI is also a practical field that aims to create effective and usable interfaces and screens that meet the user's needs and expectations  .
- HCI is an evolving field that responds to the changes in technology, society and culture  .
