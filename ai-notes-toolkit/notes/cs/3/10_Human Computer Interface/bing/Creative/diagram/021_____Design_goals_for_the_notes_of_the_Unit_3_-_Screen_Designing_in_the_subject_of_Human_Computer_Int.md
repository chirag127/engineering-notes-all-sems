### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Screen designing is the process of creating the visual layout and appearance of the user interface for an interactive system.
- Screen designing involves applying the principles of human-computer interaction, graphic design, usability, and aesthetics to create effective and engaging screens.
- The design goals for the screen designing are:

  - **Clarity**: The screen should convey the information and functionality clearly and accurately, without ambiguity or confusion. The screen should use consistent and appropriate terminology, symbols, icons, colors, fonts, and layout.
  - **Consistency**: The screen should follow the established conventions and standards of the system, platform, and domain. The screen should use the same design elements and interactions across different screens and contexts, unless there is a good reason to deviate.
  - **Efficiency**: The screen should enable the user to perform the tasks quickly and easily, without unnecessary steps or distractions. The screen should provide shortcuts, feedback, and guidance to help the user achieve the goals.
  - **Aesthetics**: The screen should appeal to the user's senses and emotions, and create a positive impression and experience. The screen should use harmonious and attractive colors, shapes, textures, and animations, and avoid visual clutter and noise.
  - **Accessibility**: The screen should be accessible and usable by a diverse range of users, regardless of their abilities, preferences, or devices. The screen should follow the accessibility guidelines and standards, and provide alternative modes and options for different user needs.
