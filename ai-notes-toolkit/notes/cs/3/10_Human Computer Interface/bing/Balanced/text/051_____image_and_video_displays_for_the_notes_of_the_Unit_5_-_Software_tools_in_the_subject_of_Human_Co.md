### Image and Video Displays for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Software Tools in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

- Image and video displays are types of screenless displays that project images or videos directly onto the user's retina, the environment, or the air.
- Image and video displays can be used for various human-computer interaction (HCI) applications, such as augmentative and alternative communication, virtual and augmented reality, smart rooms, and games. 
- Image and video processing tools are software tools that enable the analysis, manipulation, and synthesis of image and video data for HCI purposes.
- Some examples of image and video processing tools are:
  - Face detection and recognition: These tools can identify and verify the identity of human faces in images or videos.
  - Gesture recognition: These tools can recognize and interpret human gestures, such as hand or body movements, as input commands for HCI systems.
  - Eye tracking: These tools can measure and record the direction and movement of the user's eyes, which can be used for attention, gaze, or emotion analysis.
  - Computer vision: These tools can extract high-level information from images or videos, such as objects, scenes, actions, or events.
  - Computer graphics: These tools can generate realistic or stylized images or videos from geometric models, textures, lighting, and shading.
  - Visualization: These tools can create graphical representations of abstract or complex data, such as charts, graphs, maps, or diagrams.
  - Animation: These tools can create dynamic or interactive images or videos that simulate motion, change, or transformation.
- Image and video displays and processing tools can enhance the user experience, engagement, and satisfaction of HCI systems by providing natural, intuitive, and immersive interfaces.
