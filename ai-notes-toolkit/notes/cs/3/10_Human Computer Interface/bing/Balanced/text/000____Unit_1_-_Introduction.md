## Unit 1 - Introduction

- This unit provides an overview of the course objectives, structure, and expectations.
- The course aims to teach the basic concepts and skills of artificial intelligence (AI), such as problem-solving, search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, learning, and natural language processing.
- The course also introduces some of the applications and ethical issues of AI, such as robotics, computer vision, speech recognition, and social impact.
- The course is divided into four modules, each consisting of several units and assignments.
- The course requires a background in discrete mathematics, logic, and programming.
- The course uses Python as the main programming language and some libraries and tools for AI, such as NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, and PyTorch.
- The course is assessed by quizzes, assignments, and a final project.
- The course follows the textbook "Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig, 4th edition, 2020.
