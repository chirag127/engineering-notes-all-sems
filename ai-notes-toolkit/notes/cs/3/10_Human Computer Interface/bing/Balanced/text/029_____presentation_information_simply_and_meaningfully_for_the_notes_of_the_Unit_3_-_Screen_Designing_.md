### Presentation information simply and meaningfully

- The goal of screen design is to present information in a way that is clear, concise, and easy to understand for the user.
- Screen design should follow the principles of visual communication, such as contrast, alignment, repetition, and proximity.
- Screen design should also consider the characteristics of the user, such as their goals, tasks, preferences, and abilities.
- Screen design should use appropriate visual elements, such as text, images, icons, colors, and layout, to convey the meaning and purpose of the information.
- Screen design should avoid unnecessary or distracting elements, such as clutter, noise, inconsistency, and ambiguity, that may confuse or frustrate the user.
- Screen design should provide feedback, guidance, and affordance to the user, such as labels, tooltips, buttons, and menus, that indicate the functionality and interactivity of the information.
- Screen design should adhere to the standards and conventions of the platform, domain, and genre of the information, such as web, mobile, desktop, or game, that influence the user's expectations and behavior.
- Screen design should evaluate and test the effectiveness and usability of the information, such as through user research, prototyping, and heuristic evaluation, that measure the user's satisfaction and performance.
