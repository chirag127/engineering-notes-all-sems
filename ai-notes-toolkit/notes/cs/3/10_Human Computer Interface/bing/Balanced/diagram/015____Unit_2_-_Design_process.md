## Unit 2 - Design process

- The design process is a series of steps that engineers, designers, and problem-solvers use to create solutions for various challenges or needs.
- The design process can be divided into five main stages: define, ideate, prototype, test, and implement.
- Define: This stage involves identifying the problem or need, defining the criteria and constraints, and researching the background and context of the situation.
- Ideate: This stage involves generating possible solutions, brainstorming, and selecting the most promising ideas based on the criteria and constraints.
- Prototype: This stage involves creating a physical or digital model of the selected solution, using materials, tools, and techniques that are appropriate for the problem and the context.
- Test: This stage involves evaluating the prototype, collecting feedback, and measuring the performance and outcomes of the solution against the criteria and constraints.
- Implement: This stage involves refining the solution, making improvements, and communicating the results and the process to the stakeholders and the users.
- The design process is not linear or fixed, but rather iterative and flexible. It can be repeated, modified, or adapted depending on the problem, the context, and the feedback.
