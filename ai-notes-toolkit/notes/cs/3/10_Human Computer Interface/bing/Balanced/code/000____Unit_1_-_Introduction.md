## Unit 1 - Introduction

- This unit provides an overview of the course, its objectives, and its main topics.
- The course is about artificial intelligence (AI), which is the study of how to create machines and software that can perform tasks that require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, and decision making.
- The course will cover the following topics:
  - The history and foundations of AI, including its goals, challenges, and ethical issues.
  - The methods and techniques of AI, such as search, knowledge representation, logic, inference, and machine learning.
  - The applications and domains of AI, such as natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and games.
- The course will also introduce some of the tools and languages used for AI programming, such as Python, Prolog, and TensorFlow.
- The course will require the students to complete assignments, quizzes, and a final project, which will involve implementing and testing an AI system of their choice.
- The course will also encourage the students to participate in discussions, debates, and presentations on various AI topics and issues.
- The course will help the students to develop the following skills and competencies:
  - The ability to understand and analyze the concepts and principles of AI.
  - The ability to apply and evaluate the methods and techniques of AI to solve real-world problems.
  - The ability to design and implement AI systems using appropriate tools and languages.
  - The ability to communicate and collaborate effectively with others on AI-related topics and issues.
- The course will also expose the students to the current trends and developments in AI, as well as its future prospects and implications.
