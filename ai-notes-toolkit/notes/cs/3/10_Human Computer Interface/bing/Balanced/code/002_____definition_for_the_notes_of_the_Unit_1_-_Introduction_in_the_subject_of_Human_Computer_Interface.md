### Definition

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and other technologies.
- HCI aims to design, evaluate, and improve the usability, accessibility, and user experience of computer systems and applications.
- HCI involves multiple disciplines, such as computer science, psychology, design, engineering, and social sciences.
- HCI covers various topics, such as user interface design, user research, usability testing, user modeling, human factors, ergonomics, accessibility, and user satisfaction.
- HCI is important for developing user-friendly, efficient, and effective computer systems and applications that meet the needs and preferences of the users.
