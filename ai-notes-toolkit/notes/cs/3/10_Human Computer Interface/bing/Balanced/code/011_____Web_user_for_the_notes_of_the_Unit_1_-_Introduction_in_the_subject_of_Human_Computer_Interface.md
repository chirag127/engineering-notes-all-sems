### Web user for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of the interaction between humans and computers, and the design, implementation and evaluation of computer systems that support human use  .
- HCI is an interdisciplinary field that draws from computer science, psychology, design, engineering, sociology and other domains to understand the human and the computer as a whole system.
- The main goals of HCI are to improve the usability, efficiency, effectiveness, satisfaction and accessibility of computer systems for human users, and to create new and innovative forms of human-computer interaction  .
- The main components of HCI are the human, the computer and the interaction .
  - The human refers to the user of the computer system, who has various characteristics, such as input/output channels, memory, reasoning, problem solving, emotions, preferences, etc.
  - The computer refers to the hardware and software of the computer system, which has various capabilities, such as devices, memory, processing, networks, graphics, sound, etc.
  - The interaction refers to the communication and exchange of information between the human and the computer, which involves various aspects, such as models, frameworks, ergonomics, styles, elements, interactivity, paradigms, etc.
- The main challenges of HCI are to understand the needs, goals, tasks and contexts of the human users, to design and implement computer systems that match the users' expectations and abilities, and to evaluate and improve the usability and user experience of the computer systems .
- The main methods of HCI are user research, user-centered design, prototyping, usability testing, user feedback, iteration and evaluation .
- The main applications of HCI are in various domains, such as education, entertainment, health, business, social media, gaming, art, etc  .
