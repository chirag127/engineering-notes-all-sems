### Screen planning and purpose

- Screen planning is the process of designing and organizing the layout, content, and functionality of a user interface for a digital product, such as a website, app, or software.
- Screen planning helps to achieve the following purposes:
  - To define the goals and scope of the product and its features.
  - To identify the target users and their needs, preferences, and expectations.
  - To create a consistent and intuitive user experience across different screens and devices.
  - To optimize the usability, accessibility, and performance of the product.
  - To communicate the brand identity and value proposition of the product.
- Screen planning involves the following steps:
  - Conducting user research and analysis to understand the user's context, behavior, and pain points.
  - Creating user personas and scenarios to represent the user's characteristics, goals, and tasks.
  - Developing user flows and sitemaps to map out the user's journey and navigation through the product.
  - Sketching wireframes and mockups to illustrate the basic structure, layout, and elements of each screen.
  - Adding visual design and interactivity to enhance the aesthetics, functionality, and feedback of each screen.
  - Testing and evaluating the screen design with users and stakeholders to identify and resolve any issues or gaps.
  - Iterating and refining the screen design based on the feedback and data collected from the testing and evaluation.
