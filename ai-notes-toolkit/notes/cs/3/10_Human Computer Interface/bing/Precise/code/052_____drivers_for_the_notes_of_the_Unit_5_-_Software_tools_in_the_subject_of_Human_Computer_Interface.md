### Unit 5 - Software Tools: Drivers

1. A driver is a software component that enables the operating system to communicate with hardware devices.
2. Drivers act as a translator between the operating system and the hardware device.
3. Without drivers, the operating system would not be able to send or receive data correctly to hardware devices.
4. Drivers are specific to the hardware device and the operating system.
5. Drivers are typically provided by the hardware manufacturer and can be updated to improve performance or fix issues.
6. Common types of drivers include display drivers, audio drivers, printer drivers, and network drivers.
7. Drivers can be installed manually or automatically through the operating system's update feature.
8. It is important to keep drivers up to date to ensure optimal performance and stability of the system.
