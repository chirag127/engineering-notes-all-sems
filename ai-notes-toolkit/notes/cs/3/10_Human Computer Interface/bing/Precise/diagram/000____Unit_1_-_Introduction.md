## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. The first unit of this course is an introduction to the subject matter.
2. This unit will provide an overview of the key concepts and ideas that will be covered in the course.
3. The goal of this unit is to provide a foundation for the rest of the course and to help students understand the context and importance of the material.
4. Topics that may be covered in this unit include the history and development of the subject, key theories and ideas, and the relevance of the subject to contemporary issues.
5. By the end of this unit, students should have a basic understanding of the subject and be prepared to delve deeper into the material in subsequent units.
