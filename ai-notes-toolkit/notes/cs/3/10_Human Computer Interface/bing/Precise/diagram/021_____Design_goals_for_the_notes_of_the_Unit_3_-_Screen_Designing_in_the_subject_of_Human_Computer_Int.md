### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface

1. The notes should provide a clear and concise overview of the key concepts and principles of screen designing in the context of Human-Computer Interface.
2. The notes should be well-structured and organized, with clear headings and subheadings to facilitate easy navigation and understanding.
3. The notes should include relevant examples and illustrations to help explain and reinforce the concepts being discussed.
4. The notes should be written in a clear and concise language, avoiding jargon and technical terms as much as possible.
5. The notes should be designed to be visually appealing and engaging, with the use of appropriate formatting, colors, and images to enhance the learning experience.
6. The notes should be regularly updated to reflect the latest developments and trends in the field of screen designing and Human-Computer Interface.
7. The notes should include references to additional resources and readings for students who wish to explore the topic in greater depth.
8. The notes should be designed with the needs and preferences of the target audience in mind, taking into account factors such as their prior knowledge, learning style, and level of interest in the subject.
