### Statistical Graphics

Statistical graphics are visual representations of data used in statistics. They are used to present quantitative information in a graphical form, making it easier to understand and interpret. Here are some key points to consider when designing statistical graphics for screen design in the context of Human-Computer Interface:

1. **Clarity:** The graphic should be easy to read and understand. Avoid clutter and unnecessary details that may distract from the main message.

2. **Simplicity:** Keep the design simple and straightforward. Use clear and concise labels and avoid using too many colors or patterns.

3. **Accuracy:** Ensure that the data is accurately represented in the graphic. Avoid distorting or manipulating the data to fit a particular narrative.

4. **Consistency:** Maintain consistency in the design of the graphic, such as using the same color scheme and font throughout.

5. **Accessibility:** Consider the needs of users with disabilities, such as those with color blindness or low vision. Use high contrast colors and clear, legible text.

6. **Interactivity:** Consider adding interactive elements to the graphic, such as the ability to zoom in or filter data, to enhance the user experience.

In summary, effective statistical graphics for screen design in the context of Human-Computer Interface should be clear, simple, accurate, consistent, accessible, and interactive. These design principles can help to effectively communicate quantitative information to the user.
