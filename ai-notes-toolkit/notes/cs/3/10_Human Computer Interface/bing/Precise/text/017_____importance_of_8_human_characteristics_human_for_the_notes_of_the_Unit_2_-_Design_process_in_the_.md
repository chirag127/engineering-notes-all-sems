### Importance of 8 Human Characteristics

1. **Perception**: Perception is the process by which humans interpret and organize sensory information. It is important in the design process as it helps designers understand how users will perceive and interact with the interface.
2. **Memory**: Memory is the ability to store, retain, and retrieve information. It is important in the design process as it helps designers create interfaces that are easy to remember and use.
3. **Learning**: Learning is the process of acquiring new knowledge, skills, or behaviors. It is important in the design process as it helps designers create interfaces that are easy to learn and use.
4. **Problem Solving**: Problem solving is the process of finding solutions to complex issues. It is important in the design process as it helps designers create interfaces that help users solve problems.
5. **Language**: Language is the ability to communicate using symbols, words, or gestures. It is important in the design process as it helps designers create interfaces that are easy to understand and use.
6. **Decision Making**: Decision making is the process of choosing between alternatives. It is important in the design process as it helps designers create interfaces that help users make decisions.
7. **Attention**: Attention is the ability to focus on specific stimuli. It is important in the design process as it helps designers create interfaces that capture and maintain the user's attention.
8. **Motor Skills**: Motor skills are the ability to perform physical actions. It is important in the design process as it helps designers create interfaces that are easy to use and interact with.

These 8 human characteristics are important in the design process of Human Computer Interface as they help designers create interfaces that are user-friendly and effective. By understanding these characteristics, designers can create interfaces that are easy to learn, remember, and use, and that help users solve problems, make decisions, and communicate effectively.
