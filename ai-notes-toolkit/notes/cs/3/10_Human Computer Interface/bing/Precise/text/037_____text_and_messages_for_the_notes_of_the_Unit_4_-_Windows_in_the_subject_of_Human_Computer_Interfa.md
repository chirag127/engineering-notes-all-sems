### Text and Messages for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Windows in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

1. Text and messages are important components of the user interface in Windows operating system.
2. They provide information, instructions, and feedback to the user.
3. Text can be displayed in various forms such as labels, tooltips, and status bars.
4. Messages can be displayed in the form of dialog boxes, notifications, and alerts.
5. Text and messages should be clear, concise, and easy to understand.
6. They should be written in a language that is familiar to the user.
7. The font, size, and color of the text should be chosen carefully to ensure readability.
8. Messages should be timely and relevant to the user's current task.
9. They should provide the user with the necessary information to make informed decisions.
10. Text and messages play a crucial role in enhancing the user experience and improving the usability of the Windows operating system.
