### Selection of Devices and Screen-Based Controls

When designing a user interface for a computer system, it is important to consider the selection of input devices and screen-based controls. These elements play a crucial role in the overall usability and effectiveness of the system.

1. **Input Devices:** The selection of input devices should be based on the tasks that the user will be performing and the environment in which the system will be used. Common input devices include keyboards, mice, touchscreens, and voice recognition systems. Each device has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the most appropriate device will depend on the specific needs of the user and the system.

2. **Screen-Based Controls:** Screen-based controls are the elements that the user interacts with on the screen, such as buttons, menus, and text fields. The selection of these controls should be based on the tasks that the user will be performing and the overall design of the user interface. It is important to choose controls that are intuitive and easy to use, and that provide clear feedback to the user.

In summary, the selection of input devices and screen-based controls is an important consideration when designing a user interface. The most effective systems are those that are tailored to the specific needs of the user and the tasks that they will be performing.
