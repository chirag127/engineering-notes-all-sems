### Characteristics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface

1. Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and to what extent computers are or are not developed for successful interaction with human beings.
2. A significant number of major corporations and academic institutions now study HCI.
3. HCI is a broad field which overlaps with areas such as user-centered design (UCD), user interface (UI) design, and user experience (UX) design.
4. The goal of HCI is to improve the interactions between users and computers by making computers more user-friendly and receptive to the user's needs.
5. HCI is important because it determines how people interact with technology and how easy or difficult it is for them to use it.
6. HCI is interdisciplinary, incorporating fields such as computer science, psychology, human factors, and ergonomics.
7. HCI practitioners aim to create user interfaces that are easy to learn, efficient to use, and enjoyable for the user.
8. HCI research includes developing new interface technologies, studying how people use technology, and developing guidelines for designing user interfaces.
9. HCI design principles include consistency, visibility, feedback, flexibility, and error prevention.
10. HCI has applications in many areas, including education, entertainment, healthcare, and business.
