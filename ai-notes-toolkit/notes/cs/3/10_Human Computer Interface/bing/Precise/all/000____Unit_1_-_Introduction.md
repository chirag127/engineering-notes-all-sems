## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. The introduction is the first part of any written work, speech, or presentation.
2. It serves to introduce the topic, provide context, and engage the reader or audience.
3. A good introduction should be clear, concise, and attention-grabbing.
4. It should provide a brief overview of the main points that will be covered in the rest of the work.
5. The introduction should also establish the purpose and relevance of the topic, and provide any necessary background information.
6. In academic writing, the introduction often includes a thesis statement, which presents the main argument or point of view of the author.
7. The introduction sets the tone for the rest of the work and is an important part of effective communication.
