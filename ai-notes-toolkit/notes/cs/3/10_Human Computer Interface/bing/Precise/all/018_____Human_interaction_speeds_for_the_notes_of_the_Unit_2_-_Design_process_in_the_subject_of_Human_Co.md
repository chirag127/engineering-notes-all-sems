# Human Interaction Speeds

Human interaction speeds refer to the speed at which people can perform using various communication methods. These speeds have been studied by a number of researchers .

- **Reading**: The average reading speed for prose text is 250-300 words per minute. For proofreading text on paper, the average speed is 200 words per minute .

These speeds can vary depending on the individual and the specific task being performed. Understanding human interaction speeds is important in the design process of human-computer interfaces, as it can help designers create interfaces that are efficient and intuitive for users to interact with.
