# Unit 3 - Screen Designing in Human Computer Interface

### Amount of Information

1. The amount of information displayed on a screen should be appropriate for the user's needs and the task at hand.
2. Too much information can overwhelm the user and make it difficult to find the relevant information.
3. Too little information can leave the user feeling uncertain and unsure of how to proceed.
4. The amount of information should be balanced and organized in a clear and concise manner.
5. The use of white space, headings, and visual hierarchy can help to organize the information and make it easier to understand.
6. The amount of information should also be appropriate for the device and screen size, with smaller screens requiring more concise and focused information.
7. The use of progressive disclosure, where additional information is revealed as needed, can help to manage the amount of information displayed on the screen.
8. User testing and feedback can help to determine the appropriate amount of information for a specific task or user group.
