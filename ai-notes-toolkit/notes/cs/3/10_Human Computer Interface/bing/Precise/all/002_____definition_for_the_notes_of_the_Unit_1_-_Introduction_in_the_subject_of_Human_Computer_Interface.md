# Unit 1 - Introduction: Human Computer Interface

### Definition

- Human Computer Interface (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and to what extent computers are or are not developed for successful interaction with human beings.
- HCI is an interdisciplinary field that integrates theories and methodologies from computer science, cognitive psychology, design, and many other areas.
- The goal of HCI is to improve the interaction between users and computers by making computers more user-friendly and receptive to the user's needs.
- HCI is important because it helps to ensure that computer systems are designed in a way that takes into account the capabilities and limitations of the user, resulting in a more effective and efficient use of technology.
- HCI is a constantly evolving field, as technology advances and new ways of interacting with computers are developed.
