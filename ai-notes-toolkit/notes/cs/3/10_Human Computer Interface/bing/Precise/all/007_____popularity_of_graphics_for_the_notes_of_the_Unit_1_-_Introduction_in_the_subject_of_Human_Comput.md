# Unit 1 - Introduction: Popularity of Graphics in Human Computer Interface

- Graphics have become an increasingly popular tool in the field of Human Computer Interface (HCI).
- The use of graphics in HCI allows for more intuitive and user-friendly interfaces.
- Graphics can convey information more effectively than text alone, making it easier for users to understand and interact with the interface.
- The use of graphics in HCI has been shown to improve user satisfaction and increase the efficiency of task completion.
- The popularity of graphics in HCI can be attributed to the rise of visual culture and the increasing importance of visual communication in society.
- The use of graphics in HCI is also supported by research in cognitive psychology, which has shown that humans process visual information more quickly and effectively than textual information.
- The use of graphics in HCI is expected to continue to grow in popularity as technology advances and the demand for more intuitive and user-friendly interfaces increases.
