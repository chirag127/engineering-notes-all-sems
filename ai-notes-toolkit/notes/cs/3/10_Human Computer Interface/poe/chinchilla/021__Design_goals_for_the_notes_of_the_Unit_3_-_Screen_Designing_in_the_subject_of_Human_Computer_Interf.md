### Design Goals for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

When designing screens for computer interfaces, it is important to keep in mind the goals of the design. The following are some design goals to keep in mind when taking notes on the topic of screen designing in the subject of human computer interface:

1. Clarity: The notes should be clear and concise, making it easy for the reader to understand the concepts being discussed. Use simple language and avoid technical jargon as much as possible.

2. Organization: The notes should be well-organized, with a clear hierarchy of information. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to make the notes easier to read and navigate.

3. Visuals: Screens are a visual medium, and the notes should reflect that. Use diagrams, sketches, and other visual aids to illustrate concepts and make the notes more engaging.

4. Consistency: Consistency is key in screen design, and the same goes for note-taking. Use a consistent format and style throughout the notes to make them more readable and easy to follow.

5. Completeness: The notes should cover all the important concepts and ideas discussed in the unit on screen designing. Make sure to include all the relevant information, examples, and case studies.

6. Relevance: The notes should be relevant to the subject of human computer interface and the specific topic of screen design. Avoid including tangential or irrelevant information.

7. Accuracy: The notes should be accurate and reflect the latest research and best practices in the field of screen design. Check your sources and make sure the information you include is up-to-date and reliable.

By keeping these design goals in mind, you can create effective and useful notes on the topic of screen designing in the subject of human computer interface.
