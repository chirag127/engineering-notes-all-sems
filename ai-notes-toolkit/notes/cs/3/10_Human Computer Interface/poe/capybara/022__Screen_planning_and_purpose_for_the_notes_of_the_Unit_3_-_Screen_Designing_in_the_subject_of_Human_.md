### Screen Planning and Purpose

Screen planning is an essential part of screen designing in Human Computer Interface. It involves the process of designing and organizing a visual layout for the screen of a computer or mobile device. The purpose of screen planning is to enhance the user experience by creating a user-friendly interface that is easy to navigate and understand.

Here are some important points to keep in mind when planning screens for your interface:

- **Identify the purpose of the screen:** The first step in screen planning is to identify the purpose of the screen. What is the user trying to accomplish on this screen? Is it to enter data, make a selection, or view information? Understanding the purpose of the screen will help you design it more effectively.

- **Create a visual hierarchy:** Once you know the purpose of the screen, you can start creating a visual hierarchy. This involves organizing the elements on the screen in order of importance. The most important elements should be the most prominent, while less important elements should be smaller or less noticeable.

- **Consider the user's workflow:** Another important factor to consider when planning screens is the user's workflow. How will the user navigate through the interface? What steps will they need to take to accomplish their goal? By considering the user's workflow, you can design screens that are intuitive and easy to use.

- **Use consistent design elements:** Consistency is key in screen design. Use consistent design elements such as fonts, colors, and buttons throughout the interface. This will help users recognize and understand the interface more easily.

- **Design for different screen sizes:** With the increasing use of mobile devices, it's important to design screens that can adapt to different screen sizes. Consider how the interface will look on a small mobile screen versus a large desktop monitor.

- **Test and iterate:** Once you have designed your screens, it's important to test them with users and iterate based on their feedback. This will help you identify any usability issues and improve the overall user experience.

In summary, screen planning is an important part of screen designing in Human Computer Interface. By identifying the purpose of the screen, creating a visual hierarchy, considering the user's workflow, using consistent design elements, designing for different screen sizes, and testing and iterating, you can create a user-friendly interface that enhances the user experience.
