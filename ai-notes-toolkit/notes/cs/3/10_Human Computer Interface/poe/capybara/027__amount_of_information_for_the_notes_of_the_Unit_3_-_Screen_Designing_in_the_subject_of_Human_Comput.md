### Amount of Information for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the Subject of Human Computer Interface

Screen designing is an essential aspect of Human Computer Interface (HCI) that is concerned with designing effective and efficient user interfaces for various computing devices. Here are the key points to note for this unit:

- **Introduction to Screen Designing:** This section covers the basic concepts of screen designing and its importance in HCI. It also discusses the principles of screen designing, such as simplicity, consistency, and feedback.

- **User Interface Design Process:** This section covers the user interface design process, including the steps involved in designing a user interface. It also discusses the importance of usability testing and user feedback in the design process.

- **Screen Layout and Navigation:** This section covers the principles of screen layout and navigation, including the use of visual hierarchy, color, and typography. It also discusses the importance of designing screens that are easy to navigate and understand.

- **Input and Output Controls:** This section covers the different types of input and output controls, such as buttons, text fields, and sliders. It also discusses the importance of designing controls that are easy to use and understand.

- **Designing for Different Devices:** This section covers the challenges of designing user interfaces for different devices, such as desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones. It also discusses the importance of designing user interfaces that are responsive and adaptable to different screen sizes and orientations.

- **Accessibility and Internationalization:** This section covers the principles of accessibility and internationalization in screen designing. It discusses the importance of designing interfaces that are accessible to users with disabilities and that can be used in different languages and cultures.

In conclusion, screen designing is a critical aspect of HCI, and understanding its principles and processes is essential for designing effective and efficient user interfaces for various computing devices.
