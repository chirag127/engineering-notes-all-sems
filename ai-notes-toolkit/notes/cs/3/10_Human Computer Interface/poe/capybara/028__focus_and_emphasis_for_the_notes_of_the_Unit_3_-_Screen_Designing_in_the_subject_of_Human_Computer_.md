### Focus and Emphasis for the Notes of Unit 3 - Screen Designing in Human Computer Interface

Screen designing is an essential element of designing a user interface. The design of the screen can determine the user's experience and satisfaction with the application. Here are some points on focus and emphasis to keep in mind while designing screens:

- **Hierarchy of information**: When designing screens, it is important to prioritize the information and arrange it in a clear hierarchy. The most important information should be placed at the top of the screen, followed by secondary information and so on. This hierarchy of information ensures that users can quickly and easily find what they are looking for.

- **Contrast**: Contrast is a vital aspect of screen designing. It helps to differentiate between different elements on the screen and creates a visual hierarchy. Designers can use contrast in color, size, and shape to draw attention to different parts of the screen.

- **Whitespace**: Whitespace, also known as negative space, is the area between different elements on the screen. It helps to create a clean and uncluttered design, making it easier for users to focus on the important information. Using whitespace effectively can also help to draw attention to specific elements on the screen.

- **Color**: Color is an important aspect of screen designing as it can evoke emotions and set the tone for the application. Designers should choose a color scheme that aligns with the application's purpose and brand. It is important to use colors consistently throughout the application to create a cohesive design.

- **Typography**: Typography is the art of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed. It is important to choose a font that is easy to read and appropriate for the application. Designers can use typography to create a visual hierarchy and draw attention to specific parts of the screen.

- **Visual cues**: Visual cues are elements that guide the user's attention and help them navigate through the application. Designers can use visual cues such as arrows, icons, and buttons to guide the user's actions and highlight important information.

In conclusion, focus and emphasis are essential elements of screen designing. By using hierarchy, contrast, whitespace, color, typography, and visual cues effectively, designers can create a user-friendly and visually appealing interface that meets the user's needs.
