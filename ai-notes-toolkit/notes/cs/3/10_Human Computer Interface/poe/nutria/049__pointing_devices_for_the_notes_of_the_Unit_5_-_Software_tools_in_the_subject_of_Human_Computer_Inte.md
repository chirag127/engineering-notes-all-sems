
### Pointing Devices
Pointing devices are input devices used to control the movement of the cursor or pointer on a display screen. They are commonly used in graphical user interfaces (GUIs) as a way of navigating and interacting with the computer. 

The most common pointing devices are:

* Mouse - a handheld device that is moved across a flat surface to control the movement of the cursor.
* Trackball - a stationary device that is moved by rolling a ball on its surface.
* Touchpad - a flat surface that is sensitive to pressure and movement, allowing the user to move the cursor by tapping or dragging their finger.
* Joystick - a handheld device that is moved in different directions to control the movement of the cursor.
* Stylus - a pen-like device used to write or draw on a touch screen.
