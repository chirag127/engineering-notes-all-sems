
### Presentation Information in Unit 3 - Screen Designing

- Presentation information should be clear and concise, and should be designed to be easily understood by the user.
- It should be designed to help the user to complete tasks quickly and efficiently.
- Presentation information should be relevant to the task the user is performing.
- It should be designed to provide feedback to the user on the progress of the task.
- Presentation information should be designed to be visually appealing and easy to read.
- It should be designed to be accessible to all users, regardless of their physical or cognitive abilities.
- Presentation information should be designed to be consistent across multiple platforms and devices.
- It should be designed to be easily updated and maintained.
