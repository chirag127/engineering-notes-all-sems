

## Unit 3 - Screen Designing

1. Screen design is the process of creating layouts for user interfaces on devices such as computers, tablets, and smartphones. 
2. It involves creating a visual hierarchy of elements on the screen and taking into account the user's needs and preferences. 
3. Screen design is a combination of art and engineering, and requires knowledge of the user interface design principles, typography, color theory, and layout. 
4. Screen designers must consider the user's experience and how the design will be used, as well as the aesthetics of the design. 
5. Good screen design takes into account the user's context and the device being used, as well as the user's goals and preferences. 
6. Screen design also involves creating wireframes and prototypes to test the design before it is implemented. 
7. Screen design should be responsive, meaning it should be able to adjust to different screen sizes and orientations. 
8. Screen design should also be accessible, meaning it should be usable by people with disabilities. 
9. Screen design should also be optimized for performance, meaning it should be able to load quickly and use minimal resources.
