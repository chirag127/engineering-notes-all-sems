### Popularity of Graphics for Notes - Unit 1: Introduction to Human Computer Interface

1. Graphics are visually appealing and help in easy understanding of concepts. Using diagrams, images and other graphics makes the notes more interesting and comprehensible for students.
2. Complex ideas or connections between different concepts can be explained well using graphics like flowcharts, graphs, diagrams etc. These help students grasp the topic faster and retain the information for longer.
3. Many students are visual learners. For such learners, graphics play a crucial role in understanding the topic. Even for auditory and kinesthetic learners, graphics are supplementing and add value to the learning process.
4. Images and graphics reduce the burden on students of reading lengthy paragraphs and make the topic more engaging. The visual elements keep students motivated to study and pay attention.
5. With the advancement in technology, it has become very convenient to create and incorporate various graphics into study notes. This makes the notes more useful as a study material for students.
6. Overall, graphics enhance the student experience of learning a topic and make the study material or notes more appealing, relatable and useful. Appropriate and relevant graphics should be included in notes to aid the learning process.
