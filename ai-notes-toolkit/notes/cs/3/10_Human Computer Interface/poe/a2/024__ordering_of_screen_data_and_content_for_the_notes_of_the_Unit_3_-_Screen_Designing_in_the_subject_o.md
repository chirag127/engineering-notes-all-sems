### Ordering of screen data and content for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface:

1. Define the purpose and goal of the screen: Determine the key tasks and actions the user would perform on the screen. Align the content and organization of the screen to facilitate the user goals.

2. Prioritize important information: The most important information for the user to complete their goal should be prominently visible on the screen. Place critical data and options in the prime screen locations - top left and centre.

3. Group related information: Logically group related content together to make the information easy to understand and navigate. Use consistent formatting like alignment, spacing and headings to indicate relationships between groups of information.

4. Ensure a logical flow: Arrange the content in an order that aligns with the typical user flow or process. Position subsequent steps or related information in a left to right, top to bottom logical flow.

5. Minimize clutter: Remove unnecessary text, images, widgets or formatting that could clutter the screen and confuse the user. The layout should appear clean and uncluttered to allow the user to easily focus on relevant information.

6. Stay consistent: Maintain consistency in the screen layout, formatting, labelling and interactions across the app or system. Predictable and repeated patterns improve learnability and usability.

7. Provide guidance: Include additional cues and guidance on the screen to aid the user if required. For example, indications of mandatory fields or the number of steps in a process. Help text can clarify labels or functions if needed.
