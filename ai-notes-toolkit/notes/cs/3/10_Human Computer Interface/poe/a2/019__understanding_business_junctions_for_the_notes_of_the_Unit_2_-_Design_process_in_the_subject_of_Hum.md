### Understanding Business Junctions for Unit 2 - Design Process notes

1. Identify the problem: The first step is to identify the actual problem that needs to be solved. This could be an internal issue, need for improvement or a customer problem. Clearly defining the problem helps in finding the right solution.
2. User research: Conduct research to understand the impact and scope of the problem. This includes user interviews, surveys, market research, etc. to get insights into user needs, behaviors and motivations.
3. Ideation: Generate multiple solutions or ideas to solve the problem. This could be done individually or in a group through techniques like brainstorming. Evaluate the solutions and narrow down to the most promising ones.
4. Prototyping: Create prototypes of the selected solutions to visualize and test the concepts. This could be sketches, wireframes or highly interactive prototypes. Testing the prototypes gives early feedback to refine and improvise the solutions.
5. Testing and feedback: Share the prototypes with users and stakeholders and collect their feedback. Analyze the feedback and comments to identify areas of improvement. Refine the solutions and iterate until a viable product is achieved.
6. Implementation: Develop the complete product or service by including the necessary features, functions and aesthetics. Deploy and implement the solutions to solve the problem.
7. Evaluate: Monitor the performance of the solutions and evaluate their impact. See if the user needs are met and business goals are achieved. Track metrics and user feedback to make improvements or changes if required.
