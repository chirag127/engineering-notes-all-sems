## Unit 3 - Screen Designing

1. Familiarize yourself with design software like Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, etc. These tools help you create low-fidelity and high-fidelity wireframes and prototypes for screening designing.
2. Learn about layout structures like navigation bars, sidebar, header, footer, grids, spacing, etc. Apply consistent spacing and alignment to give a neat structure to your screens.
3. Choose suitable typography for headings and body. Select fonts that are easy to read and go well with your design. Apply proper font sizes and weights for headings and paragraphs.
4. Learn about the principles of design like balance, contrast, repetition, proportion, etc. Use contrast to highlight important elements, apply the golden ratio for proportion, use repetition to maintain consistency, and balance the placement of elements.
5. Design considering user experience and usability. Place related elements close to each other, use clear labels and instructions, minimize clutter, make interactive elements easily tappable, and flow screens in a logical sequence.
6. Add suitable imagery and icons to convey meaning, enhance aesthetics, and avoid excessive text. Choose high-quality and consistent images and icons that load fast and go well with your color scheme and theme.
7. Ensure accessibility by using proper color combinations, adding alternative text to images, using built-in accessibility features of design tools, etc. so that your screens are usable by people with visual impairments.
8. Keep learning about the trends in screen designing and update your skills and portfolio continuously.
