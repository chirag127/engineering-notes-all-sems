### Organizing Screen Elements for Unit 3 - Screen Designing Notes

1. Grouping related elements
- Elements related to a specific task or concept should be grouped together.
- Proximity and alignment are used to indicate relationships between elements.
- Borders and backgrounds can be used to segregate groups.

2. Consistency
- Similar types of elements should have a consistent appearance (size, color, font, etc.).
- Consistency aids in learnability and usability.
- Users expect consistency. Violating expectations can lead to errors or frustration.

3. Simplicity
- The screen should not be cluttered and only necessary elements should be present.
- Unnecessary details, decorations, and visual clutter should be avoided.
- The design should be simple, intuitive, and easy to understand.

4. Flow
- The elements should be arranged in a logical flow or sequence.
- The flow could be based on the sequence of tasks or information hierarchy.
- The flow should be obvious and aid the user in navigating the interface.

5. Balance
- The elements should be balanced on the screen.
- No one area should be too heavy or crowded.
- Balance leads to a more appealing and organized design.
- Sufficient white space is important for balance.
