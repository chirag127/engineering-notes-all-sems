### Speech Recognition Digitization and Generation

For the notes of Unit 5 - Software tools in Human Computer Interface:

1. Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken words into text. It allows computers and devices to detect speech and convert it to machine-readable data.
2. The speech recognition system uses machine learning algorithms to identify patterns in speech waves and match them to corresponding text. It learns to associate sound waves with words and phrases.
3. The key steps in speech recognition are:
- Speech sampling: converting sound waves into digital audio samples
- Feature extraction: identifying unique characteristics of speech sounds
- Acoustic modeling: comparing characteristics to acoustic models of speech sounds
- Language modeling: using context to determine the most likely corresponding text
4. Speech synthesis is the process of converting text into speech. It synthesizes artificial speech that is generated from input text. It allows interfaces to produce synthesized speech output.
5. Speech synthesis systems use machine learning models that have been trained on massive amounts of data to generate artificial speech. They break down the text into phonetic units and recombine them to create speech sounds.
6. The key steps in speech synthesis are:
- Text analysis: converting input text into phonetic units
- Prosody generation: determining rhythm, stress, and intonation
- Speech waveform generation: synthesizing speech sounds and combining them
