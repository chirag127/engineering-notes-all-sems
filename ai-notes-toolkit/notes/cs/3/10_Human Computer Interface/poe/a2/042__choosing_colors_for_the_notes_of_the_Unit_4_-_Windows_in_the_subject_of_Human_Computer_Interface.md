### Choosing colors for the notes of Unit 4 - Windows

1. Choose complementary colors: For the notes, choose complementary colors like blue and orange, red and green, etc. These colors look contrasting yet appealing together. Using complementary colors helps in easy retention of the content.
2. Choose analogous colors: Choose colors that are adjacent to each other on the color wheel like yellow, yellow-green and green. These colors give a soothing effect and are easy on the eyes.
3. Use neutral and accent colors: Use neutral colors like white, gray, black as the background and use accent colors like red, blue, yellow, etc. for headings and important points. This helps in highlighting the key terms and sections easily.
4. Ensure sufficient color contrast: The colors chosen should have sufficient contrast to be easily readable. Using light shades of the same color can make the text difficult to read.
5. Limit the number of colors: Limit the colors used to 2-3 colors to avoid a cluttered look and make the notes more systematized and easy to follow.
6. Choose colors based on your preferences: If the notes are for personal use, you can choose colors that you like and find comfortable to read and understand. However, for formal notes, stick to standard and complementary color combinations.
