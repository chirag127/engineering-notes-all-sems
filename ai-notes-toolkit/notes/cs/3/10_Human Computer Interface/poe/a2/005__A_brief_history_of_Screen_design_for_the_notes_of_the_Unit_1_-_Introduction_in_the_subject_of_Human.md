### A brief history of Screen design for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Human Computer Interface.

1. Command Line Interface: In the early days of computing, users interacted with computers through a command line interface. Users typed commands to perform tasks and the computer responded with text.

2. Graphical User Interface: The introduction of the graphical user interface (GUI) was a major milestone in computing. The first GUIs used windows, icons, and menus to represent files and actions. This made computers more accessible to casual users.

3. WIMP Interface: The windows, icons, menus, and pointing device (WIMP) interface built on the GUI and is the basis for most modern computer interfaces. It uses windows, icons, menus, and a pointing device like a mouse to enable interaction.

4. Touchscreen and Multi-touch: Touchscreen interfaces have become prevalent in smartphones, tablets, and other devices. Multi-touch interfaces allow more complex gestures and interactions. Touch interfaces have enabled new ways of interacting with and manipulating on-screen objects.

5. Voice interfaces: Voice interfaces are emerging as a new way to interact with computers and devices. While still limited, voice interactions are becoming more advanced, supporting more complex questions and commands. Voice interfaces are ideal in situations where other input methods are unavailable, such as while driving.

6. Augmented and Virtual Realities: Augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) interfaces are enabling new immersive experiences. AR overlays digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses. VR interfaces are display-based and immerse users in an entirely digital experience. These interfaces are set to transform interfaces and interactive experiences.
