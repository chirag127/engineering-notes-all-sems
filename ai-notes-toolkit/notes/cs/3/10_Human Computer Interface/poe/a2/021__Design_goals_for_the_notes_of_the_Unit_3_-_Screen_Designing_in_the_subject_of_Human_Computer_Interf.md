### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Screen Designing in the subject of Human Computer Interface:

1. Learn the basic elements of screen design like layout, typography, color theory, imagery, etc.
2. Understand the importance of screen space and how to utilize it efficiently.
3. Gain knowledge about the gestalt principles of design and how to apply them in screen design.
4. Learn about different patterns and conventions followed in screen design.
5. Get familiar with the standard templates and wireframing tools used for screen design.
6. Understand how to design for different screen sizes and devices.
7. Learn how to create simple interactive prototypes to test the screen design.
8. Understand the significance of user experience and how screen design contributes to the overall UX.
9. Learn through examples and hands-on practice.
