### Importance of Good Design for Unit 1 Notes - Introduction to Human Computer Interface

1. Easy to Read
- Use clear and concise language
- Appropriate headings and formatting to segregate topics and ideas
- Large, easy to read fonts
- Good contrast between background and text color
- Minimal clutter and unnecessary elements

2. Structured Logically
- Content flows in a logical manner from basic to complex ideas
- Concepts build upon each other
- Ideas are grouped under appropriate headings and sections

3. Focus on Key Concepts
- Only essential terms, concepts and information are included
- Avoid repetition and verbosity
- Examples are used to reinforce key points

4. Visual Aids
- Images, diagrams and graphs are included to enhance understanding
- Visuals are relevant, captioned and referred to in the text
- Images are optimized for size and clarity

5. Free of Errors
- Information is factually correct
- Grammar, spelling and punctuation are accurate
- Appropriate style guidelines are followed

6. References Included
- References and citations are included for any data or ideas not devised by the author
- Appropriate and consistent referencing style is used

7. Consistency in Formatting
- Headings, formatting styles, referencing style, etc. are consistent throughout the notes
- Uniform layout makes notes easier to follow

8. Accessible and Mobile-Friendly
- Notes are accessible to all users including those with disabilities
- Mobile-friendly design allows for easy reading on other devices
