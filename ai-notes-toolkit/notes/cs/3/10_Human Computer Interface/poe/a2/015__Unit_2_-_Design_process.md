## Unit 2 - Design process

1. Understand the problem
- Clearly define the problem and the goals/objectives. What exactly is being designed and why?
- Do research to understand the context and constraints. Talk to stakeholders and users to gather requirements.

2. Ideate
- Brainstorm solutions without judging or evaluating ideas at this stage. Come up with as many diverse ideas as possible.
- Sketch solutions to communicate concepts and get feedback. This helps refine and iterate on ideas.

3. Prototype and test
- Create low-fidelity prototypes to test with users and get feedback. This could be sketches or wireframes.
- Iterate on the prototypes based on feedback. Repeat until a viable high-fidelity prototype is ready to be built.

4. Development and delivery
- Develop the designed solution. This could be a product, service, or system.
- Test and refine the end solution. Get user feedback and make improvements.
- Release and monitor the performance of the solution and make tweaks as needed.

The above points outline the key stages in a design process to solve a problem or achieve goals by designing a suitable solution. The process is iterative and feedback-driven to build the right solution.
