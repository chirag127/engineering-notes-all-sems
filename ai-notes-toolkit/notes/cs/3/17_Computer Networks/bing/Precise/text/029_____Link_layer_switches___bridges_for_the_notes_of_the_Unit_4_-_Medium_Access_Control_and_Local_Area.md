### Link layer switches & bridges for the notes of the Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks in the subject of Computer Networks

- Link layer switches and bridges are devices that operate at the link layer (layer 2) of the OSI model.
- They are used to connect multiple network segments and forward data packets between them.
- Switches and bridges use MAC addresses to determine the destination of a data packet and forward it to the appropriate port.
- Switches and bridges can improve network performance by reducing the number of collisions and increasing the available bandwidth.
- Switches are more advanced than bridges and can support more ports and faster data transfer rates.
- Switches can also support advanced features such as VLANs, Quality of Service (QoS), and link aggregation.
- Bridges are simpler devices that are typically used to connect two network segments.
- Bridges can also be used to divide a large network into smaller segments to reduce the number of collisions and improve performance.
- Both switches and bridges can be used to create larger and more complex network topologies.
