### Forwarding and Delivery

Forwarding and delivery are two important concepts in the network layer of the OSI model in computer networks.

1. **Forwarding** refers to the process of moving a packet from an incoming link to the appropriate outgoing link at a router. This is done by using a forwarding table, which maps destination addresses to outgoing links.

2. **Delivery** refers to the process of delivering a packet from the source host to the destination host. This involves forwarding the packet through a series of routers until it reaches the destination host.

In summary, forwarding is the process of moving a packet within a router, while delivery is the process of moving a packet from the source host to the destination host. Both processes are essential for the proper functioning of the network layer.
