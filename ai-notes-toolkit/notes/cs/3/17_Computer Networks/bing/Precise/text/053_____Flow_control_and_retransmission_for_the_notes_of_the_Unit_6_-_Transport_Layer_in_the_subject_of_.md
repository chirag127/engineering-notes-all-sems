### Flow control and retransmission for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

Flow control and retransmission are two important mechanisms used in the transport layer of computer networks to ensure reliable data transmission.

1. **Flow control** is the process of managing the rate of data transmission between two nodes to prevent a fast sender from overwhelming a slow receiver. This is achieved by using techniques such as buffering, windowing, and congestion avoidance.

2. **Retransmission** is the process of re-sending data that has been lost or corrupted during transmission. This is achieved by using techniques such as error detection, error correction, and retransmission protocols.

Together, flow control and retransmission help to ensure that data is transmitted reliably and efficiently over a computer network. These mechanisms are implemented in transport layer protocols such as TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) and UDP (User Datagram Protocol).
