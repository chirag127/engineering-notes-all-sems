### Services for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

1. **Communication Services**: These services enable the exchange of data between two or more devices. Examples include email, file transfer, and remote login.

2. **Network Services**: These services provide functionality to the network itself, such as routing, addressing, and name resolution.

3. **Application Services**: These services provide specific functionality to end-users, such as web browsing, online gaming, and streaming media.

4. **Management Services**: These services are used to manage and maintain the network, including monitoring, configuration, and troubleshooting.

5. **Security Services**: These services provide protection for the network and its data, including authentication, encryption, and firewalls.

6. **Storage Services**: These services provide storage and backup solutions for data on the network.

7. **Cloud Services**: These services provide access to shared computing resources, such as processing power, storage, and applications, over the internet.

8. **Virtualization Services**: These services allow multiple virtual machines to run on a single physical server, providing more efficient use of resources and improved flexibility.

9. **Internet of Things (IoT) Services**: These services enable the connection and communication between various devices and sensors, allowing for the collection and analysis of data from the physical world.

10. **Mobile Services**: These services provide connectivity and functionality for mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, including voice and data communication, location-based services, and mobile applications.
