### ISP

ISP stands for Internet Service Provider. It is a company that provides internet access to customers by providing a connection to the internet. Here are some key points to remember about ISPs:

1. ISPs can provide internet access through various technologies such as DSL, cable modem, fiber, wireless, and satellite.
2. ISPs can offer different internet plans with varying speeds and data allowances.
3. ISPs can also offer additional services such as email, web hosting, and virtual private networks (VPNs).
4. ISPs are regulated by government agencies to ensure fair competition and consumer protection.
5. ISPs can vary in size, from large national companies to small local providers.
