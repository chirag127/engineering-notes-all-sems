### Electronic Mail

Electronic mail, commonly known as email, is a method of exchanging digital messages between people using electronic devices. It is a widely used application of the Application Layer in the subject of Computer Networks. Here are some key points to note about email:

1. Email operates across computer networks, primarily the Internet, to send messages from one user to another.
2. Email messages can contain text, images, and other attachments.
3. Email uses the Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) to send messages from the sender's email server to the recipient's email server.
4. The recipient can then access the email message using an email client, such as Microsoft Outlook or Gmail, which uses protocols such as the Post Office Protocol (POP) or the Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP) to retrieve the message from the server.
5. Email is a store-and-forward system, meaning that the email message is first stored on the sender's email server before being forwarded to the recipient's email server.
6. Email is widely used for both personal and business communication, and is an essential tool for communication in today's digital age.
