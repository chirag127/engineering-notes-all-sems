### Connection Management

Connection management is a key function of the transport layer in computer networks. It involves establishing, maintaining, and terminating connections between two or more devices. Here are some key points to remember about connection management:

1. **Connection establishment:** Before any data can be transmitted between two devices, a connection must be established. This involves a three-way handshake process, where the two devices exchange messages to synchronize their communication.

2. **Data transfer:** Once a connection is established, data can be transmitted between the two devices. The transport layer is responsible for ensuring that the data is transmitted reliably and in the correct order.

3. **Connection termination:** When the data transfer is complete, the connection must be terminated. This involves another handshake process, where the two devices exchange messages to confirm that the connection is being closed.

4. **Connection state:** The transport layer maintains the state of the connection, including information about the sequence numbers of the data being transmitted and the current status of the connection.

5. **Error handling:** If errors occur during the data transfer, the transport layer is responsible for detecting and handling them. This may involve retransmitting lost or corrupted data, or adjusting the flow of data to avoid congestion.

Connection management is an important part of the transport layer's role in ensuring reliable communication between devices in a computer network.
