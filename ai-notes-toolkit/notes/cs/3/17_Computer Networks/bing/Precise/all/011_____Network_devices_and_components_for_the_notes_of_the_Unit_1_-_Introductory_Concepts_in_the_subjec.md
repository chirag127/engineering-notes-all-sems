### Network devices and components

#### Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts

In the subject of Computer Networks, it is important to understand the various devices and components that make up a network. Here are some key network devices and components:

1. **Router:** A router is a device that connects multiple networks and routes data between them. It uses routing tables and protocols to determine the best path for data to travel between networks.

2. **Switch:** A switch is a device that connects multiple devices on a network and directs data between them. It uses MAC addresses to determine the destination of data on the network.

3. **Hub:** A hub is a device that connects multiple devices on a network and broadcasts data to all connected devices. It is less efficient than a switch as it sends data to all devices, even if it is only intended for one.

4. **Network Interface Card (NIC):** A NIC is a hardware component that connects a device to a network. It provides a physical connection to the network and allows the device to communicate with other devices on the network.

5. **Modem:** A modem is a device that modulates and demodulates signals to allow data to be transmitted over a telephone or cable line. It is commonly used to provide internet access to a home or business.

6. **Firewall:** A firewall is a device or software that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic. It uses rules to determine whether to allow or block traffic based on its source, destination, and content.

These are just a few of the many devices and components that make up a network. Understanding their functions and how they work together is essential for anyone studying Computer Networks.
