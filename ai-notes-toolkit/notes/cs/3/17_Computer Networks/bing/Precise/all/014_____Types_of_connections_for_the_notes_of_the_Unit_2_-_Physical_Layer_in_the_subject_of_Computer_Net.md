# Types of Connections

There are two types of connections in the physical layer of computer networks:

1. **Point-to-Point Connection:** This type of connection is established between two devices directly. The entire capacity of the link is reserved for transmission between those two devices. An example of a point-to-point connection is a leased line between two buildings.

2. **Multipoint Connection:** This type of connection is also known as a shared connection. In this type of connection, more than two devices share a single link. The capacity of the channel is shared between the devices either spatially or temporally. An example of a multipoint connection is a local area network (LAN).
