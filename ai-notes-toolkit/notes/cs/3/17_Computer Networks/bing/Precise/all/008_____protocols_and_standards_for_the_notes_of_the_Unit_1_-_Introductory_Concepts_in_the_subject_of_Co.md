# Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts: Protocols and Standards in Computer Networks

- **Protocols** are a set of rules and standards that define how data is transmitted and received over a network.
- These rules govern the format, timing, sequencing, and error control of data transmission.
- Protocols are essential for ensuring reliable and efficient communication between devices on a network.
- Some common protocols used in computer networks include TCP/IP, HTTP, FTP, and SMTP.
- **Standards** are agreed-upon specifications that ensure interoperability between different devices and systems.
- Standards are developed by organizations such as the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).
- Standards ensure that devices from different manufacturers can communicate with each other and that data can be exchanged between different systems.
- Some common standards used in computer networks include Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth.
