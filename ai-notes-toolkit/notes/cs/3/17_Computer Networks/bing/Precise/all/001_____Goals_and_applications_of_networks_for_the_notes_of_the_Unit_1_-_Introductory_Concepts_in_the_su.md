# Goals and Applications of Networks

## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts

### Goals of Networks

1. **Resource Sharing**: Networks allow multiple devices to share resources such as printers, storage devices, and internet connections.
2. **Communication**: Networks enable communication between devices, allowing users to exchange information and collaborate.
3. **Reliability**: Networks can provide increased reliability through redundancy, allowing for continued operation even if one component fails.
4. **Scalability**: Networks can be designed to accommodate growth, allowing for the addition of new devices and users.
5. **Cost-effectiveness**: Networks can reduce costs by allowing multiple devices to share resources and by reducing the need for redundant hardware.

### Applications of Networks

1. **Email**: Networks enable the sending and receiving of email messages.
2. **File Sharing**: Networks allow users to share files and collaborate on documents.
3. **Online Gaming**: Networks enable online gaming, allowing users to play games with others over the internet.
4. **Social Media**: Networks enable the use of social media, allowing users to connect and share information with others.
5. **Video Conferencing**: Networks enable video conferencing, allowing users to communicate face-to-face over the internet.
6. **Streaming Media**: Networks enable the streaming of media, allowing users to access music, movies, and other content over the internet.
7. **E-commerce**: Networks enable e-commerce, allowing users to buy and sell goods and services over the internet.
