# Electronic Mail

Electronic mail, commonly known as email, is a method of exchanging digital messages between people using electronic devices. It is a widely used application of the Application Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

- Email operates across computer networks, primarily the Internet.
- Email messages are sent and received using an email client, which is a software program that manages email accounts.
- Email messages are sent from one email address to one or more recipient email addresses.
- Email messages can contain text, images, and other attachments.
- Email messages are stored on a mail server until they are retrieved by the recipient's email client.
- Email uses various protocols for sending and receiving messages, including Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP), Post Office Protocol (POP), and Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP).
- Email has become an essential tool for communication, both for personal and business purposes.
