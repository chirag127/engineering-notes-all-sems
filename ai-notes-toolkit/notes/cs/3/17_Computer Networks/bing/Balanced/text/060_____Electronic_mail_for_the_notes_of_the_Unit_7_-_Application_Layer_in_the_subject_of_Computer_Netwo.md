### Electronic mail

- Electronic mail (e-mail) is a computer-based application for the exchange of messages between users over the internet  .
- An e-mail address is a unique identifier for each user, typically in the format of name@domain.com.
- An e-mail message consists of two parts: a header and a body. The header contains information such as the sender, the recipient, the subject, and the date. The body contains the actual content of the message.
- E-mail messages are sent and received by e-mail clients, which are software applications that allow users to compose, send, receive, and manage e-mail messages.
- E-mail clients communicate with e-mail servers, which are computers that store and forward e-mail messages. E-mail servers use protocols such as SMTP, POP3, and IMAP to exchange e-mail messages with other e-mail servers and clients.
- E-mail is one of the most widely used applications of the internet, and has many advantages such as speed, convenience, cost-effectiveness, and flexibility  .
- E-mail also has some disadvantages such as spam, phishing, viruses, privacy issues, and etiquette problems  .
