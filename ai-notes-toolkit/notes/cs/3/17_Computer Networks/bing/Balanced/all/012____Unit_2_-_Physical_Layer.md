## Unit 2 - Physical Layer

- The physical layer is the first and lowest layer of the Open Systems Interconnection (OSI) model of computer networking.
- The physical layer is responsible for sending computer bits from one device to another along the network.
- The physical layer provides an electrical, mechanical, and procedural interface to the transmission medium, which can be electrical, optical, or radio waves .
- The physical layer performs the following functions :
  - Data encoding and decoding: It converts the digital bits into signals that can be transmitted over the medium, and vice versa.
  - Bit synchronization: It ensures that the sender and receiver are synchronized in terms of the timing and rate of the bit transmission.
  - Transmission medium selection: It determines the type and characteristics of the medium that can support the data transmission, such as bandwidth, latency, noise, etc.
  - Physical topology selection: It determines the shape and layout of the network, such as mesh, star, bus, ring, etc.
  - Multiplexing and demultiplexing: It allows multiple signals to share the same medium, and separates them at the receiver end.
  - Error detection and correction: It detects and corrects any errors or distortions that may occur during the transmission, such as bit flips, interference, etc.
- The physical layer can be implemented through a great number of different hardware technologies with widely varying characteristics, such as Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, fiber optics, etc.
