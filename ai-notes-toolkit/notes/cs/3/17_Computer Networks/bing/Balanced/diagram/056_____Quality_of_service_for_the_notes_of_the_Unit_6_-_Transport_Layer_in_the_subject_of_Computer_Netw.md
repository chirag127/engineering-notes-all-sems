### Quality of service for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

- Quality of service (QoS) is the ability of a network to provide reliable service to the traffic over various technologies and applications  .
- The transport layer is responsible for providing end-to-end communication between applications and ensuring the delivery of data in the order in which they were sent.
- Some techniques that can be used to improve the QoS at the transport layer are:
  - Scheduling: This is the process of arranging the packets in a queue according to their priority, delay, or bandwidth requirements. Scheduling can help to avoid congestion and ensure fair allocation of resources to different flows.
  - Traffic shaping: This is the process of controlling the rate and burstiness of the traffic sent to the network. Traffic shaping can help to smooth out the traffic and avoid sudden bursts that can cause congestion and packet loss.
  - Admission control: This is the process of deciding whether to accept or reject a new flow request based on the availability of resources and the QoS requirements of the flow. Admission control can help to prevent overloading the network and ensure that the accepted flows can meet their QoS guarantees.
  - Resource reservation: This is the process of allocating and reserving resources such as bandwidth, buffer, or CPU for a specific flow or application. Resource reservation can help to ensure that the reserved resources are not used by other flows or applications and that the QoS guarantees are met.
