### Flow control and retransmission for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

- The transport layer is the fourth layer of the OSI model that provides reliable and efficient communication between end systems.
- The transport layer performs the following functions    :
  - **Segmentation and reassembly**: The transport layer divides the application data into smaller segments and adds a header to each segment with a sequence number and other information. The header also contains a checksum to detect errors in the segment. The transport layer at the destination reassembles the segments into the original data.
  - **Connection-oriented and connectionless services**: The transport layer can provide either connection-oriented or connectionless services to the application layer. Connection-oriented services require the establishment, maintenance and termination of a logical connection between the source and destination. Connectionless services do not require any connection setup or teardown and send segments independently. TCP is an example of a connection-oriented protocol and UDP is an example of a connectionless protocol.
  - **Reliable and unreliable delivery**: The transport layer can provide either reliable or unreliable delivery of segments to the destination. Reliable delivery means that the transport layer ensures that all segments are delivered without errors, duplicates or losses. Unreliable delivery means that the transport layer does not guarantee the delivery of segments and may drop, reorder or corrupt them. TCP provides reliable delivery and UDP provides unreliable delivery.
  - **Flow control**: The transport layer also controls the rate of data transmission between the source and destination to prevent data loss or buffer overflow. Flow control can be implemented by using feedback mechanisms such as acknowledgments, windowing or sliding window protocols. Flow control can also improve the efficiency of data transmission by reducing buffer underflow or idle periods. Flow control is performed end-to-end, not across a single link  .
  - **Error control**: The transport layer also detects and corrects errors in the segments by using checksums, sequence numbers and acknowledgments. The transport layer can also request retransmission of corrupted or lost segments by using timers, retransmission buffers and negative acknowledgments. Error control is also performed end-to-end, not across a single link .
  - **Multiplexing and demultiplexing**: The transport layer also enables multiple applications to share the same network resources by using port numbers to identify the source and destination applications. The transport layer at the source multiplexes the segments from different applications and adds the port numbers to the headers. The transport layer at the destination demultiplexes the segments and delivers them to the corresponding applications based on the port numbers .
  - **Quality of service**: The transport layer can also provide different levels of quality of service (QoS) to the applications based on their requirements. QoS parameters include bandwidth, delay, jitter, reliability, security and priority. The transport layer can negotiate the QoS with the network layer and use different techniques such as congestion control, buffering, scheduling, reservation or differentiation to achieve the desired QoS .
