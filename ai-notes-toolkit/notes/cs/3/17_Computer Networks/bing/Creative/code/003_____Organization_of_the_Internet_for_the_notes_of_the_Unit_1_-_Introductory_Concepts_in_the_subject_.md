### Organization of the Internet

- The internet is a global network of interconnected computing devices that can send and receive data using common protocols and standards.
- The internet consists of different layers of organization, such as:
  - Computer and network hardware: The physical devices and components that enable data transmission and processing, such as routers, switches, servers, cables, etc .
  - Internet access and internet service providers (ISPs): The companies and organizations that provide users with the service of connecting to the internet, either through wired or wireless means, and that can control and regulate how users use the internet.
  - Navigation within the internet: The methods and tools that help users find and access the information and resources they need on the internet, such as domain names, IP addresses, URLs, search engines, browsers, etc.
  - Content and applications on the internet: The data and programs that users can create, share, and consume on the internet, such as websites, social media, email, online games, etc.
- The internet is also governed by different organizations and entities that control and regulate the internet, network protocols, and standards, such as:
  - Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE): An organization that develops and publishes standards for various aspects of networking, such as Ethernet, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, etc.
  - Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF): An organization that develops and maintains the core protocols and standards of the internet, such as TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS, etc.
  - Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers (ICANN): An organization that manages the domain name system (DNS) and the allocation of IP addresses and other internet resources.
  - World Wide Web Consortium (W3C): An organization that develops and maintains the standards and guidelines for the web, such as HTML, CSS, XML, etc.
  - Internet Society (ISOC): An organization that promotes the development and use of the internet for the benefit of society, and that advocates for the principles of openness, accessibility, and security of the internet.
