### Goals and Applications of Networks

- A network is a collection of devices that can communicate with each other using a common set of rules or protocols.
- The main goals of networks are to enable data sharing, resource sharing, and distributed computing among the devices.
- Data sharing refers to the ability of devices to exchange information, such as files, messages, or multimedia content.
- Resource sharing refers to the ability of devices to access and use common resources, such as printers, scanners, or storage devices.
- Distributed computing refers to the ability of devices to cooperate and perform tasks that require high processing power, such as scientific simulations, data analysis, or artificial intelligence.
- Some of the applications of networks are:
  - The Internet: The global network of networks that connects billions of devices and provides access to various services, such as web, email, social media, online gaming, etc.
  - The World Wide Web: The system of interlinked documents and resources that can be accessed through the Internet using web browsers.
  - E-commerce: The online buying and selling of goods and services using the Internet and web technologies.
  - E-learning: The online delivery of education and training using the Internet and web technologies.
  - Telecommunication: The transmission of voice, data, and video signals over long distances using networks of wires, cables, or wireless technologies.
  - Telemedicine: The delivery of health care services and information using networks of computers, sensors, and medical devices.
  - Smart homes: The integration of networks of sensors, actuators, and appliances that can monitor and control various aspects of a home, such as lighting, temperature, security, etc.
  - Smart cities: The integration of networks of sensors, actuators, and devices that can monitor and manage various aspects of a city, such as traffic, pollution, energy, etc.
