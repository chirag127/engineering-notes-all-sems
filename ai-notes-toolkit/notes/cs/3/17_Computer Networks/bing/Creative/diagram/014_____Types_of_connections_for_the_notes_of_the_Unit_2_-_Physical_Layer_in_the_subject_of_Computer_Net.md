### Types of connections for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

- The physical layer is the lowest layer of the OSI model that deals with the transmission and reception of raw bits over a physical medium.
- The physical layer defines the characteristics of the physical medium, such as the type of cable, connectors, voltage levels, modulation schemes, etc.
- The physical layer also defines the physical topology of the network, which is the arrangement of devices and cables in a logical or geometric shape.
- There are different types of physical topologies, such as bus, mesh, star, ring, tree, and hybrid.
- In a bus topology, all devices are connected to a single cable, called the bus, which acts as a shared medium. The advantage of this topology is that it is simple and cheap to install, but the disadvantage is that it has low reliability and scalability.
- In a mesh topology, every device has a point-to-point connection with every other device in the network. The advantage of this topology is that it has high reliability and fault tolerance, but the disadvantage is that it requires a lot of cables and ports.
- In a star topology, each device has a dedicated point-to-point connection with a central controller or a hub. The advantage of this topology is that it is easy to add or remove devices, but the disadvantage is that it depends on the central hub for communication.
- In a ring topology, each device is connected to two other devices, forming a closed loop. The advantage of this topology is that it has no collisions, but the disadvantage is that it has low performance and scalability.
- In a tree topology, devices are arranged in a hierarchical structure, with a root node at the top and leaf nodes at the bottom. The advantage of this topology is that it can support large networks, but the disadvantage is that it has high latency and complexity.
- In a hybrid topology, two or more different topologies are combined to form a complex network. The advantage of this topology is that it can meet specific requirements, but the disadvantage is that it has high cost and maintenance.
- Some examples of physical layer protocols include fiber cables, integrated services digital networks, ethernet, universal serial bus, bluetooth, and controller area network.
- A physical layer device typically includes both physical coding sublayer and physical medium dependent layer functionality. The physical coding sublayer is responsible for encoding and decoding the data into bits, while the physical medium dependent layer is responsible for transmitting and receiving the bits over the physical medium.
- Some examples of physical layer devices include cabling, connectors, receivers, transceivers, and repeaters.
