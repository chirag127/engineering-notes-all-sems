### Process-to-process delivery

- The transport layer is the fourth layer of the OSI reference model that provides communication services between the computers connected in the network  .
- The transport layer is mainly responsible for the process-to-process delivery of the entire message. A process is an application program that is running on the host .
- The transport layer uses port numbers to identify different processes on the same host and to multiplex and demultiplex data from different processes .
- The transport layer can provide reliable or unreliable delivery of data depending on the protocol used .
- The transport layer can also provide other functions such as flow control, congestion control, error control, and segmentation .
- The Internet model has three protocols at the transport layer: UDP, TCP, and SCTP.
- UDP (User Datagram Protocol) is an unreliable and connectionless protocol that provides minimal services such as checksum and port numbers .
- TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is a reliable and connection-oriented protocol that provides services such as error detection and correction, flow control, congestion control, and ordered delivery of data .
- SCTP (Stream Control Transmission Protocol) is a reliable and connection-oriented protocol that provides services such as multiple streams, multihoming, and partial reliability .
