# Link layer switches & bridges

- Link layer switches and bridges are network devices that operate at the data link layer (layer 2) of the OSI model and forward data frames based on MAC addresses .
- They are used to connect multiple LANs (local area networks) together to form a larger LAN, which is called network bridging .
- They can improve network performance by dividing a large network into smaller segments and reducing the collision domain and broadcast domain.
- They can also provide security and filtering functions by using MAC address tables and VLANs (virtual LANs).
- Switches and bridges are similar in functionality, but switches are more advanced and have more ports than bridges.
- Some switches can also operate at the network layer (layer 3) of the OSI model and forward data packets based on IP addresses, which are called layer 3 switches or multilayer switches.
