

### LAN Standards

LAN (Local Area Network) standards are a set of guidelines that define the technical specifications for local area networks. These standards ensure that different devices and network components can communicate with each other effectively. Here are some of the LAN standards that you should know:

- Ethernet: Ethernet is one of the most popular LAN standards. It uses a bus topology and supports data transfer rates of up to 10Gbps. Ethernet is widely used in both wired and wireless networks.

- Token Ring: Token Ring is another LAN standard that uses a ring topology. It uses a token-passing protocol to control access to the network. Token Ring was popular in the 1980s and 1990s, but it has since been replaced by Ethernet.

- FDDI: FDDI (Fiber Distributed Data Interface) is a LAN standard that uses fiber optic cables. It supports data transfer rates of up to 100Mbps and is often used in high-speed networks.

- Wireless LAN: Wireless LAN (WLAN) is a LAN standard that uses radio waves to transmit data. It is commonly used in homes, offices, and public places like airports and cafes. WLAN supports data transfer rates of up to 1Gbps.

- Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless LAN standard that is used for short-range communication between devices. It is commonly used for connecting devices like smartphones, laptops, and headphones. Bluetooth supports data transfer rates of up to 3Mbps.

- Zigbee: Zigbee is a LAN standard that is used for low-power, low-speed wireless communication. It is commonly used in smart homes and industrial automation systems. Zigbee supports data transfer rates of up to 250kbps.

These LAN standards are important to understand if you are studying computer networks. Knowing the technical specifications and capabilities of each standard will help you design and implement effective local area networks.
