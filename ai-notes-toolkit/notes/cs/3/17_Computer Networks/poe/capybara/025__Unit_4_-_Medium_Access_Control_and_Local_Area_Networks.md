

## Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks

In this unit, we will learn about Medium Access Control (MAC) and Local Area Networks (LANs). We will explore the different techniques used for MAC and the various types of LANs.

### Medium Access Control

1. MAC is a sublayer of the Data Link layer.
2. Its purpose is to control access to the shared medium.
3. There are three primary techniques used for MAC:
   - Contention-based
   - Token-based
   - Controlled access
4. Contention-based techniques are used in Ethernet LANs.
5. Token-based techniques are used in Token Ring LANs.
6. Controlled access techniques are used in wireless LANs.

### Local Area Networks

1. A LAN is a network that connects devices within a small geographical area.
2. They are used to share resources such as printers and files.
3. There are two primary types of LANs:
   - Wired LANs
   - Wireless LANs
4. Wired LANs use physical cables to connect devices.
5. Examples of wired LAN technologies include Ethernet and Fast Ethernet.
6. Wireless LANs use radio waves to connect devices.
7. Examples of wireless LAN technologies include Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

In conclusion, Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks are essential topics for understanding how networks function. By studying these topics, you will gain a deeper understanding of network architecture and be better equipped to design and troubleshoot networks.
