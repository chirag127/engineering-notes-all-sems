### Goals and applications of networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Connecting devices: Networks help connect devices like computers, laptops, mobile phones, etc to share resources and services. Devices can access the internet and other network resources through networks.

2. Sharing resources: Resources like files, data, storage, printing devices, etc can be shared over the network. This makes accessing resources more efficient and cost-effective as resources can be shared.

3. Remote access: Networks allow remote access to devices and resources. This enables users to access resources from anywhere and at any time. Applications like remote desktop allow accessing other devices remotely.

4. Communication: Networks enable communication channels between devices using services like email, instant messaging, video calling, etc. People can communicate and collaborate over the network.

5. Access to internet: Networks provide access to the internet to devices and resources. The internet connects various smaller networks across the world and allows access to information, services, and resources on the internet.

The goals and applications of computer networks are to connect devices, share resources, allow remote access, enable communication, and provide internet access. Networks make computing and accessing resources more efficient, convenient, and global.
