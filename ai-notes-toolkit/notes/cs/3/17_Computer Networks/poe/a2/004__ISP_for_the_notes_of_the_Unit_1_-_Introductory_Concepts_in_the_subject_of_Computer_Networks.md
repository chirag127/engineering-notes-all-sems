### ISP for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in the subject of Computer Networks

1. Internet Service Providers (ISPs): ISPs are companies that provide Internet access to individuals and other companies. ISPs connect customers to the Internet through telephone lines, cables, or wireless signals.

2. Bandwidth: The amount of data that can be carried from one point to another in a given time period. Higher bandwidth means faster data transmission. Bandwidth is often referred to as the size of the 'pipe' carrying information.

3. Download vs Upload: The rate at which data is transferred from the Internet to a computer is called download speed. The rate at which data is transferred from a computer to the Internet is called upload speed. Download speed is usually faster than upload speed for most Internet connections.

4. Latency: The time it takes for data to get from one point to another. Low latency means shorter delays, resulting in fast data transmission and less lag. High latency leads to slower data transmission and lag. Latency is affected by the physical distance between two points and traffic on the network.

5. Packet: Data sent over computer networks is broken down into packets. Packets are formatted units of data that include the sender's and recipient's address, the packet sequence number, and the data being sent. Packets are reassembled in the correct order by the recipient's computer.
