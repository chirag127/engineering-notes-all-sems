## Unit 2 - Physical Layer

1. The physical layer is the first layer of the OSI model. It is responsible for converting data into physical signals that are transmitted over a transmission medium.
2. The main functions of the physical layer are:
- Converting data into physical signals: The physical layer converts the binary data stream received from the upper layers into electrical or optical signals that are transmitted over the transmission medium.
- Transmission and reception of signals: The physical layer is responsible for transmitting and receiving the signals over the transmission medium.
- Synchronization: The physical layer also establishes and maintains synchronization between the devices that are communicating. This is crucial for the devices to interpret the transmitted signals correctly.
3. The transmission medium refers to the physical path between the transmitting and receiving devices. The various transmission media used are:
- Twisted pair cables: Copper wires that are twisted together to reduce interference. Used for telephone lines and local area networks.
- Coaxial cables: A single copper cable with a layer of insulation and a woven copper mesh shield. Used for cable TV networks.
- Fiber optic cables: Plastic or glass tubes that transmit light signals over long distances with low loss. Used for high speed communications.
- Wireless: Radio frequency waves are used to transmit and receive signals. Used for cell phones, Wi-Fi, etc.
