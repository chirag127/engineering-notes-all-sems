### Channel allocation for the notes of the Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Fixed Channel Allocation:
- In this, channels are allocated to different wireless networks beforehand and fixed.
- No dynamic changes are made to the channels.
- Issue: If the traffic in a network increases, it can lead to congestion as the allocated channel has fixed bandwidth.

2. Dynamic Channel Allocation:
- Channels are allocated dynamically to wireless networks based on the traffic and congestion.
- The channels are reassigned to balance the load and optimise the bandwidth utilisation.
- Issues in static allocation are addressed.
- Centralised and distributed algorithms exist for this.

3. Channel Bonding:
- Two or more channels are combined to increase the available bandwidth for a wireless network.
- Only suitable if multiple adjacent channels are available and not being used by other networks.
- Difficult to implement but can significantly improve throughput.
