### Transmission media for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Guided Transmission Media
- Twisted Pair Cable: Copper wires twisted together to reduce crosstalk. UTP and STP are the types. Used for telephone lines and networking.
- Coaxial Cable: Single copper wire surrounded by an insulator and braided copper wire shield. Used for cable TV and networking.
- Fiber Optic Cable: Made of glass or plastic fibers that transmit light pulses. Highest bandwidth with low attenuation. Used for high-speed networking and telecommunications.

2. Unguided Transmission Media
- Wireless: Does not require physical channels to transmit signals. Signals are transmitted as radio waves or microwaves. Used for Wi-Fi, cellular data, Bluetooth, etc.
