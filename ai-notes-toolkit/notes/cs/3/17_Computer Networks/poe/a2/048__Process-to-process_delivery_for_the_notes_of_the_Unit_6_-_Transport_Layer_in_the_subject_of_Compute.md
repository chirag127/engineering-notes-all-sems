### Process-to-process delivery for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. The Transport Layer provides process-to-process delivery of the entire message.
2. It is responsible for the reliable data transmission between the processes running on different hosts via the network.
3. It provides multiplexing and demultiplexing of the data streams. Multiplexing is combining several streams into one at the source and demultiplexing is separating the multiplexed stream into original streams at the destination.
4. It provides congestion control to avoid network congestion. It monitors the network load and accordingly adjusts the transmission rate.
5. It provides flow control to adjust the data transmission rate between the sender and receiver and avoid overflowing the receiver.
6. It provides error control through error detecting codes and automatic retransmission of damaged or lost packets.
7. The popular transport layer protocols are TCP and UDP.

- TCP provides reliable, ordered, and error-checked delivery of transmitted data.
- UDP provides fast but unreliable data transmission without ordering or error-checking.

The key transport layer services are multiplexing, demultiplexing, reliable data transfer, flow control, congestion control, and error control. The transport layer protocols TCP and UDP provide different combinations of these services.
