### Basic internetworking for the notes of the Unit 5 - Network Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Internetworking is connecting two or more networks to share resources and communicate.
2. The network layer handles the internetworking between networks. It determines the best path for data to reach the destination network.
3. Packet switching is used to transfer data between networks. Data is broken into packets that include the destination address. Each packet can take a different route to the destination and reassembled at the destination.
4. Routers operate at the network layer and transfer packets between networks using packet switching. They maintain routing tables that list available networks and paths to determine the best route for packets.
5. Protocols used at the network layer include Internet Protocol (IP), Address Resolution Protocol (ARP), Reverse Address Resolution Protocol (RARP), and Internet Control Message Protocol (ICMP).
6. IP addresses are assigned to networks and hosts to identify their locations and enable packet delivery. Special IP addresses are used for private networks and multicast groups.
