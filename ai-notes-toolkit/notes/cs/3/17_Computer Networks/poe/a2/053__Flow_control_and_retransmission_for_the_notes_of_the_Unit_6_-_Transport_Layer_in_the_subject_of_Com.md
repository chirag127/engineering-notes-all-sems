### Flow control and retransmission for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks:

1. Flow control:
- Ensures that sender does not overwhelm receiver with data
- Receiver controls transmission rate
- Prevents loss of data due to buffer overflow
- Examples: Stop and Wait, Sliding Window

2. Retransmission:
- Lost or corrupted segments are retransmitted
- Sender waits for acknowledgement (ACK) from receiver
- If ACK is not received within a timeout period, segment is retransmitted
- Go-Back-N and Selective Repeat are two retransmission strategies

3. Connection-oriented and connectionless transport:
- Connection-oriented: Virtual circuit is established, segments are delivered in order. Eg: TCP
- Connectionless: No virtual circuit, segments are delivered independently. Eg: UDP

The above points cover the key concepts related to flow control, retransmission and connection-oriented and connectionless transport which are important topics under the Transport Layer in Computer Networks.
