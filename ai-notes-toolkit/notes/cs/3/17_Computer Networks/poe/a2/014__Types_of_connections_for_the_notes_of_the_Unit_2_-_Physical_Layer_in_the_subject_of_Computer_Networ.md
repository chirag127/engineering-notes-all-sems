### Types of connections for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks:

1. Point-to-Point Connection:
- A dedicated link is established between two devices.
- Full bandwidth of the link is dedicated to transmission between those two devices.
- Examples: Leased lines, Dial-up connections

2. Broadcast Connection:
- A single device transmits data which is received by all other devices on the network.
- Examples: Wireless networks, Ethernet

3. Multipoint Connection:
- Three or more devices are connected to a link.
- The link bandwidth is shared between the connected devices.
- Examples: Bus topology, Ring topology
