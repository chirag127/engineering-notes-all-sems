### Electronic mail for the notes of the Unit 7 - Application Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Electronic mail (email) is a system for exchanging messages between people using electronic devices.
2. The email system transfers messages over a network using a protocol called Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP).
3. The messages are stored temporarily in mail servers until recipients fetch them using protocols such as Post Office Protocol (POP) or Internet Message Access Protocol (IMAP).
4. An email address is used to identify the sender and recipient of a message. It contains an identifier (username) and a domain name.
5. Email has largely replaced traditional letters and memos as the primary method of written communication over the Internet due to its convenience, low cost, and reasonable privacy.
6. However, email does have some security and privacy issues as the contents can be read by intermediaries if it is not encrypted on insecure networks or vulnerable devices.
