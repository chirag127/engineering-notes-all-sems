### Connection management for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Connection establishment:
- Client requests for connection establishment by sending SYN packet to server.
- Server accepts and sends SYN-ACK packet.
- Client sends ACK packet and connection is established.

2. Data transfer:
- After connection is established, client and server can send data packets.
- Sequence and acknowledgement numbers are used to track the packets.
- If packets are lost or corrupted, retransmission is done.

3. Connection release:
- Either client or server sends FIN packet to release the connection.
- The other end sends ACK and sends FIN if it has no data to send.
- This results in full duplex closure of connection.
- Half duplex closure also possible where one end sends FIN and other just sends ACK.
