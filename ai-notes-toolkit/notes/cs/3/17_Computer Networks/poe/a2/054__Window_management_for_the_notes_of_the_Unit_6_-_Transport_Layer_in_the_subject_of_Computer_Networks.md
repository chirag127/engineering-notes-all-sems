### Window management for the notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the subject of Computer Networks.

1. Transport layer provides end-to-end communication between applications running on different hosts.
2. The transport layer protocols provide logical communication between applications through transport services.
3. The transport services are characterized by transport protocols that use port numbers to multiplex various applications.
4. The two most popular transport protocols are TCP and UDP.
5. TCP provides reliable, ordered, and error-checked delivery of transmitted data.
6. UDP provides fast but unordered unstructed datagram delivery service (no error checking).
7. The transport layer also implements mechanisms such as sliding window and congestion control to provide flow control and congestion avoidance.
8. Flow control prevents a fast sender from overwhelming a slow receiver by limiting the number of packets a sender can transmit before receiving acknowledgments.
9. Congestion control prevents overload in the network and in the transport entities, i.e., the hosts and routers.
