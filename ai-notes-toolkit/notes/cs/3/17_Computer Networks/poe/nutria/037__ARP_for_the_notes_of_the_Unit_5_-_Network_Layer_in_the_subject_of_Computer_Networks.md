
### ARP for the Notes of Unit 5 - Network Layer in Computer Networks

1. Address Resolution Protocol (ARP) is a protocol used to map IP addresses to physical addresses such as a MAC address.
2. ARP is used to determine the MAC address of a device on the same network.
3. ARP requests are sent to the broadcast address to all devices on the network.
4. The device that has the IP address in the ARP request will reply with its MAC address.
5. ARP is used to maintain an up-to-date table of IP-to-MAC address mappings.
6. ARP is a layer-2 protocol, meaning it operates at the data link layer.
7. ARP is an essential part of the TCP/IP protocol suite and is used by all IP-enabled devices.
8. ARP is a stateless protocol, meaning it does not maintain any information about previous requests or responses.
