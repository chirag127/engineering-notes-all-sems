
### Protocols and Standards for Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts in Computer Networks

- **TCP/IP (Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol):** This is the standard protocol used for communication between devices in a network. It is the most widely used protocol and is the foundation of the modern internet.

- **Ethernet:** This is the most commonly used method of physical connection between computers in a network. It is based on the IEEE 802.3 standard and uses a twisted-pair or fiber-optic cable to connect two or more computers.

- **IP Address:** This is a unique identifier for a device in a network. It is used to identify and locate a device on the network.

- **Routing:** This is the process of forwarding data from one network to another. Routers are used to determine the best path for the data to take.

- **NAT (Network Address Translation):** This is a method of translating one IP address to another. It is used to connect multiple devices to the internet using a single public IP address.

- **Firewall:** This is a security measure that controls the flow of data between a network and the outside world. It is used to protect a network from malicious traffic and to protect the privacy of the users.
