
### Goals and Applications of Networks

1. The primary goal of a computer network is to enable communication between two or more computers.
2. Networks can also be used to share resources, such as printers or hard drives.
3. Networks can also be used to access the Internet and other online services.
4. Networks can be used to create virtual private networks (VPNs) to ensure secure communication.
5. Networks can be used to facilitate distributed computing, where multiple computers work together to solve a problem.
6. Networks can be used for distributed storage, where data is stored on multiple computers.
7. Networks can also be used for streaming media, such as audio, video, and other multimedia content.
8. Networks can be used to create distributed databases, where data is stored in multiple locations.
9. Networks can be used to create distributed applications, where multiple computers work together to provide a service.
10. Networks can be used to create distributed gaming environments, where multiple computers work together to provide a gaming experience.
