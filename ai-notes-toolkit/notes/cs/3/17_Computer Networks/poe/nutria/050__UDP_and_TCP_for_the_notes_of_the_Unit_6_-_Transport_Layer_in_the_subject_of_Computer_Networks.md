
### UDP and TCP

**UDP**
- UDP is an unreliable protocol, meaning that it does not guarantee delivery of data packets. 
- It is a connectionless protocol, meaning that it does not require a connection to be established between two hosts before sending data. 
- It is a best-effort delivery protocol, meaning that it does not guarantee that data packets will be delivered in the same order they were sent. 
- It is a lightweight protocol, meaning that it does not have the overhead of the connection establishment and maintenance that TCP has.

**TCP**
- TCP is a reliable protocol, meaning that it guarantees delivery of data packets. 
- It is a connection-oriented protocol, meaning that it requires a connection to be established between two hosts before sending data. 
- It is a sequenced protocol, meaning that it guarantees that data packets will be delivered in the same order they were sent. 
- It is a heavyweight protocol, meaning that it has the overhead of the connection establishment and maintenance that UDP does not have.
