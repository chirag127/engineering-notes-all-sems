
## Unit 1 - Introductory Concepts

* Introduction to Computer Science: an overview of the field, its history, and its impact on the world.
* Algorithms: the basic building blocks of computer programs, and the process of designing and analyzing them.
* Programming Languages: the tools used by programmers to create programs and communicate with computers.
* Data Structures: the ways in which data is organized and manipulated.
* Databases: systems for storing and retrieving data.
* Networking: the communication systems used to connect computers and other devices.
* Operating Systems: the software that manages computer hardware and software.
* Software Engineering: the design and implementation of software systems.
* Artificial Intelligence: the study of how computers can be made to behave intelligently.
