
## Unit 5 - Network Layer

1. The Network Layer is responsible for the delivery of packets from source to destination hosts across multiple networks. 
2. It is responsible for the logical addressing of hosts, and for routing packets from source to destination across multiple networks. 
3. The Network Layer provides services such as logical addressing, routing, packet fragmentation and reassembly, and error control. 
4. Logical addressing is the process of assigning unique identifiers to each host, which are used to identify the source and destination of each packet. 
5. Routing is the process of determining the best path for a packet to take from source to destination. 
6. Packet fragmentation is the process of breaking a large packet into smaller packets for transmission over a network. 
7. Reassembly is the process of reconstructing the original packet from the smaller packets that have been transmitted over the network. 
8. Error control is the process of detecting and correcting errors in the packet transmission process.
