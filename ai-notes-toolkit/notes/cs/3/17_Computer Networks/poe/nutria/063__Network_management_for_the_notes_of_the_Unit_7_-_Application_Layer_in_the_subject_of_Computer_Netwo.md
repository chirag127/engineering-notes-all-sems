
### Network Management for the Notes of the Unit 7 - Application Layer in the Subject of Computer Networks

1. Network Management is an important concept in Computer Networks that involves the planning, design, implementation, and maintenance of network systems.

2. Network Management encompasses both hardware and software components, as well as the protocols and standards used to manage them.

3. Network Management can be divided into two main categories: Local Area Network (LAN) Management and Wide Area Network (WAN) Management.

4. LAN Management involves the management of local networks, such as those within an organization or home. This includes tasks such as configuring, monitoring, and troubleshooting the network.

5. WAN Management involves the management of networks that span multiple locations, such as those used by businesses or educational institutions. This includes tasks such as configuring, monitoring, and troubleshooting the network, as well as managing its security.

6. Network Management protocols are used to manage network devices. These protocols include Simple Network Management Protocol (SNMP), Common Management Information Protocol (CMIP), and Transaction-Oriented Application Protocol (TOAP).

7. Network Management tools are used to monitor and manage networks. These tools include network monitoring software, network management systems, and network management consoles.

8. Network Management standards are used to ensure interoperability between different types of network devices. These standards include IEEE 802.1X, IEEE 802.11, and IEEE 802.15.
