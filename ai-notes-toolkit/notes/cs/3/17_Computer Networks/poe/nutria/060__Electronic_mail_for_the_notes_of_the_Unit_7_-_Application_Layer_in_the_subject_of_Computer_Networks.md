
### Electronic mail for the notes of the Unit 7 - Application Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

* Electronic mail (e-mail) is a system of sending messages from one computer to another over the Internet. 
* An email message consists of two parts: the message header and the message body. 
* The message header contains information such as the sender's and recipient's email addresses, the date and time the message was sent, and other information. 
* The message body contains the actual content of the message, such as text, images, and attachments. 
* Email messages can be sent to multiple recipients at once using a technique called mailing lists. 
* Email messages can also be sent to an entire group of people using a technique called broadcast email. 
* Email messages can be encrypted for added security. 
* Email messages can be stored in an email client, such as Microsoft Outlook or Mozilla Thunderbird, or they can be stored on a server. 
* Email clients and servers use protocols such as POP3, IMAP, and SMTP to communicate with each other.
