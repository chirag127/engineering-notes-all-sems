

## Unit 2 - Physical Layer

1. The physical layer is the first layer of the Open Systems Interconnection (OSI) model. It is responsible for the physical transmission of data between two devices.
2. It is responsible for the transmission of bits over a physical medium, such as a copper cable, fiber optic cable, or wireless connection.
3. It defines the electrical, mechanical, and procedural characteristics of the physical connection between the two devices.
4. It also defines the type of cable, connectors, and pin assignments used in the connection.
5. The physical layer is responsible for the transmission of bits from one device to another. It does not provide any error correction or data compression.
6. The physical layer is also responsible for the synchronization of data between two devices. It defines the timing of data transmission and the electrical signaling used to indicate the start and end of each bit.
7. The physical layer also defines the physical topology of the network, such as bus, star, or ring.
