
### Switching Techniques and Multiplexing

1. **Switching** is the process of routing data from one node to another in a network. It can be done using either circuit switching or packet switching. 
   - **Circuit switching** is a method of communication that establishes a dedicated physical connection between two nodes for the duration of the communication session.
   - **Packet switching** is a method of communication that breaks a data transmission into packets and sends them independently over a network.
2. **Multiplexing** is the process of combining multiple signals into one signal. It is used to increase the capacity of a network by allowing multiple users to share a single communication channel.
   - **Time-division multiplexing (TDM)** is a type of multiplexing that divides a communication channel into separate time slots and assigns each user a specific time slot.
   - **Frequency-division multiplexing (FDM)** is a type of multiplexing that divides a communication channel into separate frequency bands and assigns each user a specific frequency band.
   - **Wavelength-division multiplexing (WDM)** is a type of multiplexing that uses different wavelengths of light to transmit data over a single fiber.
