
### Transmission media for the notes of the Unit 2 - Physical Layer in the subject of Computer Networks

* Twisted-pair cable: It is a type of cabling that consists of two insulated wires twisted together. It is a common form of cabling used in computer networks, telephone systems, and other communication systems. It is relatively inexpensive and provides good electrical performance.

* Coaxial cable: It is a type of cable that consists of an inner conductor surrounded by an insulating layer, which is then surrounded by a layer of metal shielding. It is commonly used for television and internet connections.

* Fiber optic cable: It is a type of cable that uses light to transmit data. It is much faster than traditional copper cables and is used for long-distance communication.

* Wireless transmission: It is a type of transmission that does not require a physical connection between the sender and receiver. It is commonly used for Wi-Fi, cellular networks, and other wireless communication systems.
