
### Connection Management for the Notes of the Unit 6 - Transport Layer in the Subject of Computer Networks

* The connection management is the process of creating and managing connections between two devices in a computer network. 
* It includes the processes of establishing, maintaining, and releasing connections between the two devices. 
* The transport layer is responsible for connection management in a computer network. It provides services to the application layer, such as reliable data transfer, flow control, and connection establishment and termination.
* The transport layer is responsible for creating and managing connections between two devices. It uses a three-way handshake protocol to establish a connection between the two devices. 
* After the connection is established, the transport layer is responsible for maintaining and releasing the connection. It uses a four-way handshake protocol to release the connection. 
* The transport layer also provides services such as reliable data transfer, flow control, and error control. It uses protocols such as TCP and UDP to provide these services. 
* The transport layer also provides congestion control and network address translation (NAT). 
* The transport layer is responsible for providing end-to-end communication between two devices in a computer network.
