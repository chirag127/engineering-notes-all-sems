
### LAN Standards for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Medium Access Control and Local Area Networks in the Subject of Computer Networks

1. Ethernet is the most widely used LAN standard, and is based on the IEEE 802.3 standard. It is a bus-based system, meaning that all devices connect to a single cable.
2. Token Ring is another LAN standard, based on the IEEE 802.5 standard. It is a ring-based system, meaning that all devices connect to a single cable in a ring formation.
3. FDDI is a fiber-optic LAN standard, based on the IEEE 802.6 standard. It is a dual-ring system, meaning that two rings of fiber-optic cable are used.
4. Wi-Fi is a wireless LAN standard, based on the IEEE 802.11 standard. It is a mesh-based system, meaning that all devices connect to multiple access points.
5. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless LAN standard, based on the IEEE 802.15 standard. It is a point-to-point system, meaning that two devices can connect directly to each other.
