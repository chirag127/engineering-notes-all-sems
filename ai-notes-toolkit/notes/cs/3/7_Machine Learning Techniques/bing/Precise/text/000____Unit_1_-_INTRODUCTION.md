## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter or unit in any subject or topic.
2. It provides an overview of the subject and its importance.
3. It sets the stage for the rest of the material by providing context and background information.
4. It may include definitions of key terms and concepts.
5. It may also include a brief history of the subject and its development.
6. The introduction is an important part of any study material as it helps the reader to understand the subject and its relevance.
7. It is important to read the introduction carefully to gain a clear understanding of the subject before proceeding to the subsequent chapters or units.
