### Case-based learning for the notes of the Unit 3 - DECISION TREE LEARNING in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

- Case-based learning is a teaching method that uses real-life scenarios to help students apply theoretical knowledge to practical situations.
- In the context of decision tree learning, case-based learning can be used to help students understand how decision trees can be used to solve real-world problems.
- By working through examples and analyzing the decision-making process, students can gain a deeper understanding of the concepts and techniques involved in decision tree learning.
- Case-based learning can also help students develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills, as they must analyze and evaluate the information presented in each case.
- Overall, case-based learning is an effective way to engage students and enhance their understanding of decision tree learning in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques.
