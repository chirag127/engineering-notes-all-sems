### Learning Task for the notes of the Unit 5 - REINFORCEMENT LEARNING in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. Reinforcement Learning is a type of Machine Learning that focuses on training models through reward-based systems.
2. The goal of Reinforcement Learning is to develop a system that can learn to make decisions based on the feedback it receives from its environment.
3. Reinforcement Learning is used in various applications such as gaming, robotics, and self-driving cars.
4. The two main components of Reinforcement Learning are the agent and the environment.
5. The agent is the decision-maker and interacts with the environment by taking actions and receiving feedback in the form of rewards or punishments.
6. The environment is the world in which the agent operates and provides the agent with feedback based on its actions.
7. The agent's goal is to learn a policy that maximizes the cumulative reward over time.
8. There are various algorithms used in Reinforcement Learning such as Q-Learning, SARSA, and Deep Q-Networks.
9. Reinforcement Learning is an active area of research and new techniques and algorithms are being developed to improve its performance.
