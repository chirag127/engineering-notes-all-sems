### Data Science Vs Machine Learning

- **Data Science** is a field that encompasses a wide range of techniques and methods to extract insights and knowledge from data. It involves the use of statistical and computational methods to analyze, visualize, and model data to make informed decisions.

- **Machine Learning**, on the other hand, is a subset of artificial intelligence that focuses on the development of algorithms that can learn from and make predictions or decisions based on data. It involves the use of statistical and computational methods to build models that can improve their performance over time as more data is fed into them.

- While there is some overlap between the two fields, there are also some key differences. Data Science is more focused on the entire data analysis process, from data collection and cleaning to analysis and visualization, while Machine Learning is more focused on the development of predictive models.

- Data Science often involves the use of Machine Learning techniques, but it also encompasses a wider range of methods and techniques. Machine Learning, on the other hand, is a specific approach to data analysis that focuses on the development of algorithms that can learn from data.

- In summary, Data Science is a broader field that encompasses a wide range of techniques and methods for extracting insights and knowledge from data, while Machine Learning is a specific approach to data analysis that focuses on the development of algorithms that can learn from data. Both fields are important and complementary, and they are often used together in the analysis of complex data sets.
