### Entropy and Information Theory

- Entropy is a measure of the uncertainty or randomness of a system.
- In the context of decision tree learning, entropy is used to measure the impurity of a set of examples.
- The higher the entropy, the more uncertain or impure the set of examples is.
- Information theory is a branch of mathematics that deals with the representation, storage, and transmission of information.
- In decision tree learning, information theory is used to determine the best attribute to split the data on at each node of the tree.
- The attribute that results in the greatest reduction in entropy is chosen as the splitting attribute.
- This process is repeated recursively until all the data is classified or the tree reaches a predefined maximum depth.
- The goal of decision tree learning is to construct a tree that accurately classifies the data while minimizing the depth of the tree.
- Entropy and information theory play a crucial role in achieving this goal by providing a principled way to measure the quality of a split and to choose the best attribute to split on.
