### Case study of CNN for Diabetic Retinopathy

- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) have been applied effectively for predictions in various fields, including healthcare .
- CNNs have great potential in diagnosing diabetic retinopathy from eye images and classifying them accurately based on the severity .
- The diagnosis of diabetic retinopathy (DR) through color fundus images requires experienced clinicians to identify the presence and significance of many small features, which, along with a complex grading system, makes this a difficult and time-consuming task.
- CNNs can significantly improve the early onset of diabetic retinopathy in seemingly healthy patients.
- Most of these strategies are a two-tier classification system for Diabetic Retinopathy or non-Diabetic Retinopathy.
- A convolutional Neural Network (CNN) is a category of synthetic neural network which can be composed of one or greater convolutional layers, maximum normally carried out to analyze visual imagery.
