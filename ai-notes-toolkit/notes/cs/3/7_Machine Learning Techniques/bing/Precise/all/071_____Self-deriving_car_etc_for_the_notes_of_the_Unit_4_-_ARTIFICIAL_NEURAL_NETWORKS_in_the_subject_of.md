# Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

### Self-driving cars

- Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, use a combination of sensors, cameras, radar, and artificial intelligence (AI) to navigate roads and traffic without human input.
- The AI system in a self-driving car uses machine learning techniques, including artificial neural networks, to process the data from the sensors and make decisions about how to control the vehicle.
- Artificial neural networks are modeled after the structure and function of the human brain, with layers of interconnected nodes or neurons that process and transmit information.
- The neural network is trained using large amounts of data to recognize patterns and make predictions about the environment and the behavior of other vehicles on the road.
- The AI system in a self-driving car must be able to make complex decisions in real-time, such as when to change lanes, how to avoid obstacles, and how to respond to unexpected events.
- Self-driving cars have the potential to reduce accidents caused by human error, improve traffic flow, and increase accessibility for people who are unable to drive.
- However, there are also challenges and concerns associated with the development and deployment of self-driving cars, including safety, liability, and the impact on jobs in the transportation industry.
