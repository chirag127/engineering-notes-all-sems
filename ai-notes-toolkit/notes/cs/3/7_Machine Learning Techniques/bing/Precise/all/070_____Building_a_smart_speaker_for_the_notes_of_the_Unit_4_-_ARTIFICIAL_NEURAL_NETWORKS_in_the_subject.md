### Building a Smart Speaker for the Notes of the Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS in the Subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. A smart speaker is a type of wireless speaker and voice command device with an integrated virtual assistant that offers interactive actions and hands-free activation.
2. To build a smart speaker, one must have knowledge of Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) which is a unit in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques.
3. ANNs are computing systems inspired by the biological neural networks that constitute animal brains.
4. ANNs are used to model complex patterns and relationships between inputs and outputs, to find patterns in data, and to capture the statistical structure in an unknown joint probability distribution between observed variables.
5. A smart speaker can be built using ANNs by training the model to recognize voice commands and respond accordingly.
6. The model can be trained using supervised learning, where the input data is labeled with the desired output.
7. Once the model is trained, it can be integrated into the smart speaker to enable voice command recognition and response.
8. The smart speaker can then be used to perform tasks such as playing music, setting reminders, and providing information, all through voice commands.
