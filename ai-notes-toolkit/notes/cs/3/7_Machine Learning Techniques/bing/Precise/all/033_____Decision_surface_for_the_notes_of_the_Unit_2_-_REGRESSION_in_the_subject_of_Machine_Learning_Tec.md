### Decision Surface

- In the context of machine learning, a decision surface is a boundary that separates different classes or values in a dataset.
- It is used in classification and regression problems to make predictions based on the input data.
- In regression, the decision surface is a function that maps the input data to a continuous output value.
- The goal of regression is to find the best decision surface that minimizes the error between the predicted and actual output values.
- The decision surface can be linear or non-linear, depending on the complexity of the data and the model used.
- In linear regression, the decision surface is a straight line or a hyperplane that separates the data.
- In non-linear regression, the decision surface can take on more complex shapes to better fit the data.
- The decision surface is determined by the model parameters, which are learned from the training data.
- The quality of the decision surface can be evaluated using metrics such as mean squared error or R-squared.
- The decision surface can be visualized to gain insights into the relationship between the input and output data.
