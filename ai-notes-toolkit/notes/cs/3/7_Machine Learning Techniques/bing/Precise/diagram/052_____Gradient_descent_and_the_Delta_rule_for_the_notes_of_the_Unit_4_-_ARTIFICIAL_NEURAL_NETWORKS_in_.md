### Gradient Descent and the Delta Rule

Gradient descent is an optimization algorithm used to minimize a cost function by iteratively adjusting the parameters of a model. It is commonly used in machine learning to train models such as artificial neural networks.

The Delta rule, also known as the Widrow-Hoff rule, is a learning rule used to update the weights of a neural network. It is based on the idea of minimizing the mean squared error between the predicted output and the desired output.

Here are the key points to remember about gradient descent and the Delta rule:

1. Gradient descent is an iterative optimization algorithm used to minimize a cost function.
2. The Delta rule is a learning rule used to update the weights of a neural network.
3. The Delta rule is based on the idea of minimizing the mean squared error between the predicted output and the desired output.
4. The Delta rule can be used in conjunction with gradient descent to train a neural network.
5. Both gradient descent and the Delta rule are commonly used in machine learning to train models such as artificial neural networks.
