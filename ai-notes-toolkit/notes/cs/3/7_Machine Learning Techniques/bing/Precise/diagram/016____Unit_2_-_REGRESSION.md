## Unit 2 - REGRESSION

Regression is a statistical method used to study the relationship between two or more variables. It is commonly used to predict the value of one variable based on the values of other variables.

There are two main types of regression: linear regression and multiple regression.

1. **Linear Regression** is used to model the relationship between two variables by fitting a linear equation to the observed data. The equation takes the form `y = a + bx`, where `y` is the dependent variable, `x` is the independent variable, `a` is the y-intercept, and `b` is the slope of the line.

2. **Multiple Regression** is an extension of linear regression, used when there are two or more independent variables. The equation takes the form `y = a + b1x1 + b2x2 + ... + bkxk`, where `y` is the dependent variable, `x1, x2, ..., xk` are the independent variables, `a` is the y-intercept, and `b1, b2, ..., bk` are the slopes of the line.

Regression analysis can be used for various purposes, including:

- Predicting future values of the dependent variable
- Identifying the strength and direction of the relationship between variables
- Determining the relative importance of the independent variables in predicting the dependent variable
- Testing hypotheses about the relationships between variables.
