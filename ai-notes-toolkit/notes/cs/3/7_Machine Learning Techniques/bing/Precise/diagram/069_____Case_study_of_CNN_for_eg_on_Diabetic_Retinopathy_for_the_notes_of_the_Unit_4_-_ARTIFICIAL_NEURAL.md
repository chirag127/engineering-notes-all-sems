### Case study of CNN for Diabetic Retinopathy

- Diabetic retinopathy, also known as diabetic eye disease, is a medical condition in which damage occurs to the retina due to diabetes mellitus. It is a leading cause of blindness in advanced countries.
- Diabetic retinopathy affects up to 80 percent of those who have had diabetes for 20 years or more.
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) is one of the efficient algorithms of deep learning that is highly used to detect Diabetic Retinopathy features from retinal images.
- CNNs approach to Diabetic Retinopathy detection saves time and expense, and is more efficient and accurate than manual diagnostics.
- The diagnosis of diabetic retinopathy through color fundus images requires experienced clinicians to identify the presence and significance of many small features, which, along with a complex grading system, makes this a difficult and time-consuming task.
