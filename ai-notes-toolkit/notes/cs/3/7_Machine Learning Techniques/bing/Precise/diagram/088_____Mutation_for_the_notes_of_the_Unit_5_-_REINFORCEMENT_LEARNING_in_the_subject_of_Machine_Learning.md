### Mutation in Reinforcement Learning

- Mutation is a concept in reinforcement learning that leverages on the parallel evaluations of individuals in the population and natural mutation.
- Deep neuroevolution enables faster convergence and natural exploration, which are key to successful reinforcement learning .
- Mutation can be used in malware mutation using deep reinforcement learning and GANs to mutate a malware sample to bypass AI-powered classifiers while keeping its functionality intact .
- A context-aware adaptive route mutation scheme can be used as a reinforcement learning approach in moving target defense to reduce the risk of vulnerabilities exploited by attackers .
- Mutation testing can be used to test reinforcement learning systems, including reward rules, exploration and exploitation, and experience replay .
- Maximum mutation reinforcement learning can be used for scalable control, providing scalability while reducing hyperparameter sensitivity towards evolutionary operations .
