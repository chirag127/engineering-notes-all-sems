## Unit 4 - ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS

1. **Introduction:** Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are computational models inspired by the structure and function of the human brain. They are designed to recognize patterns and make predictions based on data.

2. **Structure:** ANNs are composed of interconnected nodes or neurons, organized into layers. The input layer receives data, and the output layer produces the network's prediction. Hidden layers between the input and output layers process the data.

3. **Training:** ANNs are trained using a dataset, where the network adjusts its weights and biases to minimize the error between its predictions and the true values. Common training algorithms include backpropagation and gradient descent.

4. **Applications:** ANNs have a wide range of applications, including image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and predictive analytics.

5. **Limitations:** ANNs can require large amounts of data and computational resources to train, and their predictions can be difficult to interpret. They can also suffer from overfitting, where the network becomes too specialized to the training data and performs poorly on new data.

6. **Future Developments:** Research in ANNs continues to advance, with new architectures and training methods being developed to improve their performance and capabilities. Deep learning, a subset of ANNs, has shown particular promise in recent years.
