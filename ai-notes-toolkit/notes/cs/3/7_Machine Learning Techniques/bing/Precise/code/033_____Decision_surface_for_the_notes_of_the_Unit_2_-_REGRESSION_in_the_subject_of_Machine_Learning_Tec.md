### Decision Surface

- A decision surface is a boundary that separates the data points in a classification problem.
- It is used to determine the class of a new data point based on its position relative to the decision surface.
- In regression, the decision surface is a hyperplane that separates the data points into different classes.
- The goal of regression is to find the best decision surface that minimizes the error between the predicted and actual class labels.
- There are different methods to find the decision surface, such as linear regression, logistic regression, and support vector machines.
- The choice of method depends on the nature of the data and the problem at hand.
- The decision surface can be visualized in two or three dimensions for low-dimensional data, but it becomes more difficult to visualize for higher-dimensional data.
- Understanding the decision surface can provide insights into the relationship between the input features and the target variable, and can help improve the performance of the model.
