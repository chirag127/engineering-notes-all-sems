### Self-driving car artificial neural networks

- Self-driving cars are vehicles that can operate autonomously without human intervention, using sensors, cameras, maps, and artificial intelligence (AI) to perceive the environment and navigate safely  .
- AI is the key technology that enables self-driving cars to learn from data, make decisions, and adapt to changing situations. AI can be divided into two main branches: machine learning and deep learning.
- Machine learning is a subset of AI that uses algorithms and statistical models to learn from data and perform tasks without explicit programming. Machine learning can be further classified into supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.
- Supervised learning is a type of machine learning that uses labeled data to train a model that can make predictions or classifications. For example, a supervised learning model can learn to recognize traffic signs or pedestrians from images that are labeled with the correct answers.
- Unsupervised learning is a type of machine learning that uses unlabeled data to find patterns or structures in the data without any guidance. For example, an unsupervised learning model can learn to cluster images of different types of vehicles based on their features.
- Reinforcement learning is a type of machine learning that uses trial and error to learn from its own actions and rewards. For example, a reinforcement learning model can learn to drive a car by interacting with a simulated environment and receiving feedback on its performance.
- Deep learning is a subset of machine learning that uses artificial neural networks to learn from data and perform complex tasks. Artificial neural networks are composed of layers of interconnected nodes that mimic the structure and function of biological neurons.
- Artificial neural networks can learn from data by adjusting the weights and biases of the connections between the nodes based on a learning algorithm, such as gradient descent or backpropagation. Artificial neural networks can have different architectures, such as feedforward, recurrent, or convolutional.
- Feedforward neural networks are the simplest type of artificial neural networks, where the information flows from the input layer to the output layer without any feedback loops. Feedforward neural networks can be used for tasks such as regression or classification.
- Recurrent neural networks are a type of artificial neural networks that have feedback loops that allow the information to persist over time. Recurrent neural networks can be used for tasks such as sequence modeling or natural language processing.
- Convolutional neural networks are a type of artificial neural networks that use convolutional layers that apply filters to the input data to extract features. Convolutional neural networks can be used for tasks such as image recognition or computer vision .
- Self-driving cars use a combination of different types of artificial neural networks to perform various tasks, such as perception, planning, control, and localization  .
- Perception is the task of detecting and recognizing objects, such as vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signs, and lanes, from sensor data, such as images, lidar, or radar  . Perception can be achieved by using convolutional neural networks to process the sensor data and output the bounding boxes, labels, and distances of the objects  .
- Planning is the task of generating a safe and optimal trajectory for the self-driving car to follow, based on the perception data, the map data, and the desired destination  . Planning can be achieved by using reinforcement learning to learn from the simulated or real-world driving scenarios and output the best actions for the self-driving car to take  .
- Control is the task of executing the planned trajectory by adjusting the steering, throttle, and brake of the self-driving car  . Control can be achieved by using feedforward or recurrent neural networks to map the planned trajectory to the control commands  .
