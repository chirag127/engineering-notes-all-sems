### Clustering

- Clustering is the process of dividing objects into similar groups based on their features or properties  .
- Clustering is a type of unsupervised learning, which means that the algorithm does not use any labels or predefined categories to group the objects  .
- Clustering is useful for data analysis, exploratory data mining, pattern recognition, image processing, recommender systems, anomaly detection, and many other applications  .
- Clustering algorithms use different criteria and methods to measure the similarity or dissimilarity between objects and to assign them to clusters  .
- Some of the common clustering algorithms are K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering, DBSCAN, Mean Shift, Spectral Clustering, etc  .
- Some of the challenges and limitations of clustering are choosing the appropriate number of clusters, dealing with noise and outliers, handling high-dimensional data, and evaluating the quality of clusters .
- Some of the libraries used for clustering in Python are Scikit-learn, SciPy, PyClustering, etc .
