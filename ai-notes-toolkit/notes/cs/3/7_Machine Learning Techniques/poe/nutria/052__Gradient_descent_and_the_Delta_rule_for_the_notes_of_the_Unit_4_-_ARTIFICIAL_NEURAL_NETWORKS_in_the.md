
### Gradient Descent and the Delta Rule

Gradient descent and the delta rule are two important concepts in the field of machine learning techniques, specifically artificial neural networks (ANNs).

**Gradient Descent** is an algorithm used to find the minimum of a function. It works by taking small steps in the direction of the negative gradient of the function, which is the direction of steepest descent. Gradient descent can be used to optimize the weights of an ANN, by minimizing the error of the network.

**The Delta Rule** is an algorithm used to update the weights of an ANN. It works by calculating the error between the desired output and the actual output of the network, and then updating the weights of the network according to the error. The delta rule is a type of supervised learning algorithm, as it requires a known set of inputs and outputs to work.

These two concepts are essential for understanding and working with ANNs, and are important to understand when using machine learning techniques.
