
### Clustering for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

* Clustering is an unsupervised machine learning technique that is used to group data points into clusters. 
* It is a form of data segmentation that is used to identify and categorize data points that have similar characteristics. 
* Clustering algorithms are used to find patterns in data and to group data points into meaningful clusters. 
* Clustering algorithms can be used to identify customer segments, to analyze customer behavior, to detect outliers, and to identify clusters of data points that have similar characteristics. 
* Clustering algorithms can be used to identify clusters of data points that are close together in terms of certain features, such as location, age, gender, or other demographic information. 
* Clustering algorithms can also be used to identify clusters of data points that are close together in terms of certain characteristics, such as customer preferences or product features. 
* Clustering algorithms can also be used to identify clusters of data points that are close together in terms of certain behaviors, such as customer purchase patterns or user engagement. 
* Clustering algorithms can be used to identify clusters of data points that are similar in terms of certain characteristics, such as customer preferences or product features.
