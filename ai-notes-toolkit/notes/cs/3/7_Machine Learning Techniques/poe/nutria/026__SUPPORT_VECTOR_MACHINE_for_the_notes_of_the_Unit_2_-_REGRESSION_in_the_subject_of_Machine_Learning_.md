
### SUPPORT VECTOR MACHINE

Support Vector Machines (SVMs) are a type of supervised machine learning algorithm used for both classification and regression tasks. SVMs are based on the concept of decision planes that define decision boundaries. A decision plane is a hyperplane that separates between a set of objects having different class memberships.

SVMs are used for both linear and non-linear regression. In linear regression, the decision plane is a hyperplane that separates the data into two classes. In non-linear regression, the decision plane is a non-linear surface that separates the data into multiple classes.

SVMs are powerful and effective in many applications, such as text classification, image classification, and predicting stock prices. They are also popular for their ability to handle high-dimensional data and their robustness to noise.

SVMs are also used in the field of bioinformatics, where they are used to classify proteins and other biological objects. SVMs are also used in the field of medicine, where they are used to detect diseases and predict drug responses.

SVMs are effective in many machine learning tasks because they are able to capture complex patterns in data and make accurate predictions. They are also able to work with small datasets and are relatively less prone to overfitting.
