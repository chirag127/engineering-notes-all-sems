
### Perceptrons

* A perceptron is a type of artificial neural network (ANN) used in supervised learning and consists of a single layer of neurons. 
* Each neuron is connected to the inputs and outputs of the system, and the weights of the connections determine the behaviour of the perceptron. 
* The perceptron is a linear classifier, meaning it can classify data into two classes based on a linear combination of input features. 
* The perceptron is trained using a learning algorithm which adjusts the weights of the connections between the neurons in order to minimize the error between the predicted and actual output. 
* Perceptrons are used for classification tasks such as image recognition, speech recognition and natural language processing. 
* Perceptrons are also used in reinforcement learning, where they can be used to approximate the value function of a given state. 
* Perceptrons are limited in their ability to learn complex non-linear relationships, and so are usually used in combination with other types of neural networks such as convolutional neural networks or recurrent neural networks.
