### History of ML

1. 1950 - Alan Turing publishes his paper on "Turing Test" to test a machine's ability to exhibit intelligent behavior equivalent to a human.
2. 1959 - Arthur Samuel writes a self-learning program for playing checkers.
3. 1965 - Herbert Simon predicts that machines will be capable of doing any work a human can do within 20 years.
4. 1997 - Deep Blue defeats Garry Kasparov in chess which was a major milestone.
5. 2006 - Geoffrey Hinton proposes a more efficient training algorithm for neural networks called "deep learning" which leads to major breakthroughs in ML.
6. 2011 - IBM's Watson defeats human champions at Jeopardy! which demonstrates the power of ML in tackling complex problems.
7. 2016 - AlphaGo defeats Lee Sedol in the game of Go, which is a more complex game than chess, showing the continued progress of ML and deep learning.
8. Recent years - Rapid progress in the areas of deep learning, neural networks, and large amounts of data and computing power lead to ML being applied to various tasks like computer vision, NLP, speech recognition, etc. with significant success.
