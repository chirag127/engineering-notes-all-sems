### Bayesian belief networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - REGRESSION in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. Bayesian belief networks are probabilistic graphical models that represent a set of random variables and their conditional dependencies via a directed acyclic graph (DAG).
2. The nodes in the graph represent random variables, and the directed edges represent conditional dependencies.
3. Each node is associated with a conditional probability table that encapsulates the probability distribution of the variable given its parents in the DAG.
4. Bayesian belief networks are well suited to modeling uncertain knowledge and updating beliefs in the light of new evidence. They provide a coherent probabilistic framework for explanation, prediction, and decision making under uncertainty.
5. Some advantages of Bayesian belief networks are:
- They provide an intuitive way to encode and visualize conditional independence relationships between variables.
- The topology of the belief network can be validated against conditional independence tests to check for modeling accuracy.
- Efficient algorithms exist for belief updating (computation of posterior probabilities) in the light of new evidence.
- They enable the fusion of information from multiple sources with different reliability and relevance.
