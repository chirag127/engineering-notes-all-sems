### Self-Driving Cars

- Artificial Neural Networks are used to train self-driving cars to detect traffic signs and lights, recognize lane lines, localize the car on a map, detect and avoid obstacles, etc.
- The input data is in the form of images, lidar data, etc. which is fed into the ANN.
- The ANN detects patterns in the data and learns the features to make predictions or decisions.
- Deep Learning techniques like Convolutional Neural Networks are used for image data to learn features like shapes, textures, etc. Recurrent Neural Networks can be used for time-series data like from lidars.
- The training data needs to be diverse and include all possible scenarios for the car to learn how to handle each situation properly.
- Regularization techniques need to be used to avoid overfitting due to limited data.
- The decisions or predictions made by the ANN need to be optimized to generate proper actions to control the car.
- Testing the trained model extensively in simulations and real-world trials is required before self-driving cars can be deployed commercially on roads.
- Self-driving cars have the potential to reduce traffic accidents and provide mobility for all. However, there are still many challenges to overcome around handling all possible driving scenarios and adverse conditions.
