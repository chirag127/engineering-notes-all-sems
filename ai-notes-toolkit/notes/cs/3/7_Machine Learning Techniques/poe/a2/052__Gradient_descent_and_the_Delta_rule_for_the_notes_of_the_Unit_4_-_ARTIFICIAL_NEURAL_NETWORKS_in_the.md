### Gradient descent and the Delta rule

- Gradient descent is an optimization algorithm used to minimize an objective function by iteratively moving in the direction of steepest descent as defined by the negative of the gradient.
- The goal is to reach a minimum of the function.
- For training neural networks, the objective function to be minimized is the loss function, computing the difference between the target outputs and the actual outputs.
- Gradient descent is used to update the weights of the network in order to minimize the loss function.
- The delta rule is a specific application of gradient descent for adjusting the weights in a neural network.
- It uses the error between the predicted output and the target output to determine how to adjust the weights to produce the desired output.
- The sizes of the adjustments are proportional to the slopes of the activation functions.
- The process is repeated over multiple epochs until the network approximates the desired outputs.
- Gradient descent and the delta rule are fundamental for training neural networks and enabling machine learning.
