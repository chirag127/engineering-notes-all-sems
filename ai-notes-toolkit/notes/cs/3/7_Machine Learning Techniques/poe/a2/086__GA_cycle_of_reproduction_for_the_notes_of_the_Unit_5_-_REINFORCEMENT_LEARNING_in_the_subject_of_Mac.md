### GA cycle of reproduction for the notes of the Unit 5 - REINFORCEMENT LEARNING in the subject of Machine Learning Techniques

1. Selection: Firstly, the individuals are selected from the population for reproducing offspring. The individuals with higher fitness have more chances of getting selected.
2. Crossover: Then the genes of the selected individuals are crossed over to produce offspring. The genes are split and recombined to generate offspring with combined genes of parents.
3. Mutation: Further, some random changes are made to the offspring gene sets. This leads to the variation in the genes of the offspring from parents. The mutation introduces diversity in the population.
4. Replacement: Finally, the offspring are evaluated and the worst individuals of the previous population are replaced by the offspring. This makes the next generation more fit than the previous one.

The above steps keep on iterating until the stopping criteria is met. The ultimate goal is to produceindividuals with maximum fitness. The genetic algorithms prove useful in optimization and search problems by exploring a wide range of solutions and converging to the optimal one.
