### GENETIC ALGORITHMS

* Genetic algorithms are inspired by biological evolution and natural selection.
* They are a type of evolutionary algorithm used to solve optimization problems.
* A population of candidate solutions to an optimization problem is evolved towards better solutions.
* Each candidate solution has a set of properties which can be mutated and altered.
* The candidates are evaluated using a fitness function to determine how good or bad they are.
* The highly fit candidates are selected to reproduce a new generation.
* Mutation and crossover are applied to the new generation.
* This process is repeated over many generations until a satisfactory solution is found.
* The key advantage is that genetic algorithms can search large, complex spaces efficiently and find global optimal solutions.
* However, they may be slow to converge on the solution and require tuning of hyperparameters like mutation rate.
