### Spaces, Tabs and New Lines

- **Spaces** are characters used to separate words or other elements in text. In most computer systems, a space is represented by the ASCII code 32.
- **Tabs** are characters used to align text or create indentation. In most computer systems, a tab is represented by the ASCII code 9. The width of a tab character is usually equivalent to a certain number of spaces, often 8.
- **New lines** are characters used to indicate the end of a line of text and the beginning of a new one. In most computer systems, a new line is represented by the ASCII code 10 or 13, or a combination of both (10 and 13). The representation of a new line varies between operating systems.
