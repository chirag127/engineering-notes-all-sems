# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments, wherever he/she feels in a justified manner

- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments in the course.
- These changes may include adding new experiments, deleting existing experiments, modifying the details of the experiments, or tuning the experiments to better fit the course objectives.
- The instructor will make these changes in a justified manner, meaning that the changes will be made for a valid reason and will be beneficial to the course and the students.
- It is important for students to be aware of this possibility and to be prepared for changes to the experiments in the course.
