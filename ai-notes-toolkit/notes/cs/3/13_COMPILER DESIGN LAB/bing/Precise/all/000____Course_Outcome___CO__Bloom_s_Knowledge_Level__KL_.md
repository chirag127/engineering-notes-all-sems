## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the specific knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to achieve after completing a course.
- Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is a framework for categorizing educational goals according to the level of cognitive complexity required to achieve them.
- The framework consists of six levels: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating.
- COs can be mapped to Bloom’s KL to ensure that the course content and assessments are aligned with the desired level of cognitive complexity.
- For example, a CO that requires students to “apply the principles of thermodynamics to solve problems” would be mapped to the “Applying” level of Bloom’s KL.
- Mapping COs to Bloom’s KL can help instructors design effective assessments and instructional strategies to support student learning.
- It can also help students understand the expectations for their learning and the level of cognitive complexity required to achieve the COs.
