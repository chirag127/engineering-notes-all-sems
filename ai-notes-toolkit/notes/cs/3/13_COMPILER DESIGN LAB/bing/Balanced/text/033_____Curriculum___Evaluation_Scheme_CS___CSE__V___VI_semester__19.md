### Curriculum & Evaluation Scheme CS & CSE (V & VI semester) 19

- The curriculum and evaluation scheme for computer science and engineering (CS & CSE) students in the fifth and sixth semesters of the 2019 scheme are based on the guidelines and regulations of the respective universities and colleges.
- The curriculum typically consists of core courses, elective courses, laboratory courses, project work, and seminars that cover various topics and skills related to computer science and engineering.
- The evaluation scheme usually involves internal and external assessments, such as internal sessional exams (ISE), mid-semester exams (MSE), end-semester exams (ESE), quizzes, assignments, practicals, viva-voce, etc.
- The weightage and distribution of marks for each course and assessment may vary depending on the university and college, but generally follow a standard pattern of credits and grades.
