### Processors and Resources

- Processors and resources are two major types of system components that are involved in the execution of real-time tasks.
- Processors are also called servers or active resources. They are responsible for processing the data and executing the instructions of the tasks.
- Examples of processors are computers, transmission links, disks, database servers, etc.
- Processors can be configured and optimized to meet the timing requirements of real-time applications. For example, Intel® Time Coordinated Computing (Intel® TCC) is a technology that enables workload-aware processor tuning and optimizations to help bound data access timings.
- Resources are also called passive resources. They are shared by the tasks and may be required during the execution of the tasks.
- Examples of resources are memory, files, printers, sensors, actuators, etc.
- Resources can be accessed by the tasks using different protocols and mechanisms, such as mutual exclusion, priority inheritance, priority ceiling, etc.
- Resources can also be synchronized with the processors using time-sensitive networking (TSN) protocols, such as IEEE 802.1AS and IEEE 802.1Qbv.
