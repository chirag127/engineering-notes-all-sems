### Model of Real Time Communication

- Real time communication is any online communication that happens in real time, with negligible latency and without intermediate storage  .
- Real time communication can be classified into two types: real time traffic and real time control.
- Real time traffic consists of streams of messages that are generated by their sources and delivered to their respective destinations on a continuous basis. The traffic includes periodic, aperiodic and sporadic messages.
- Real time control consists of commands and feedbacks that are exchanged between controllers and controlled devices to achieve a desired behavior.
- A model of real time communication consists of the following components:
  - End users: the sources and destinations of the messages, residing in different hosts.
  - Network interfaces: the devices that connect the hosts to the network, containing input and output queues and buffers.
  - Network: the medium that carries the messages from the sources to the destinations, consisting of links, routers and switches.
  - Protocols: the rules and algorithms that govern the transmission and reception of the messages, such as error control, flow control, congestion control and routing.
- A model of real time communication can be characterized by the following metrics :
  - Throughput: the amount of data that can be transferred per unit time, measured in bits per second (bps).
  - Delay: the time elapsed from the moment a message is generated by the source to the moment it is received by the destination, measured in seconds.
  - Jitter: the variation in the delay of the messages, measured in seconds.
  - Reliability: the probability that a message is delivered to the destination without errors, measured in percentage.
  - Quality of service (QoS): the degree to which the communication meets the requirements of the end users, such as bandwidth, delay, jitter and reliability, measured in various units.
