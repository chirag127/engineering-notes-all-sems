### Typical Real Time Applications

In the field of Real Time Systems, there are several applications that demand real-time performance. Some of the typical real-time applications are:

1. Aerospace and defense systems:
   - Real-time monitoring, control, and navigation systems for aircraft, spacecraft, and missiles.
   - Flight simulator systems for training pilots and astronauts.
   - Command and control systems for military operations.

2. Automotive Systems:
   - Anti-lock brake systems (ABS), traction control systems, and electronic stability control (ESC) systems.
   - Engine control units (ECU) and transmission control units (TCU).
   - Advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) like lane departure warning, adaptive cruise control, and collision avoidance systems.

3. Industrial Automation:
   - Process control systems for manufacturing and chemical plants.
   - Robotics and machine control systems.
   - Quality control systems for inspection, testing, and measurement.

4. Medical Systems:
   - Patient monitoring systems for hospitals and clinics.
   - Diagnostic imaging systems like MRI, CT, and PET scanners.
   - Life support systems like ventilators, dialysis machines, and anesthesia systems.

5. Multimedia Systems:
   - Video and audio streaming applications.
   - Real-time video surveillance systems.
   - Virtual reality and augmented reality systems.

6. Telecommunication Systems:
   - Real-time voice and video conferencing systems.
   - Real-time data transmission and processing systems.
   - Network monitoring and management systems.

These real-time applications require high reliability, availability, and accuracy. They also need to operate within strict timing constraints and respond quickly to external events. Therefore, Real Time Systems play a crucial role in ensuring the proper functioning of these applications.
