## Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System

Real-time systems are computer systems that must satisfy strict timing constraints. The goal of a real-time system is to carry out a task within a specific time frame. This unit introduces the concept of real-time systems and explains their significance in various industries.

Here are some points to remember:

- Real-time systems are used in a variety of fields, including aerospace, defense, healthcare, and industrial automation.
- Real-time systems can be classified into hard real-time systems and soft real-time systems. Hard real-time systems have strict deadlines that must be met, while soft real-time systems have more relaxed timing requirements.
- Real-time systems require specialized hardware and software to ensure timely execution of tasks. These systems are typically designed with redundancy and fault tolerance to ensure reliable operation.
- Real-time systems use a variety of scheduling algorithms to manage tasks and ensure timely execution. Some commonly used scheduling algorithms include rate monotonic scheduling, earliest deadline first scheduling, and deadline monotonic scheduling.
- Real-time systems must be tested and validated to ensure they meet their timing requirements. This involves verifying that the system can meet its deadlines under various conditions.
- Real-time systems have become increasingly important in modern society due to the rise of automation and the need for real-time processing of data.

In conclusion, real-time systems play a critical role in various industries, and understanding their fundamentals is essential for anyone working in these fields. By studying this unit, you will gain a solid understanding of the basics of real-time systems and their significance in modern society.
