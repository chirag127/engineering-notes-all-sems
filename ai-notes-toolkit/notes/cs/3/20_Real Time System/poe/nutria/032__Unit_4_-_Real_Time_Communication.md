
## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication

1. Real-time communication (RTC) is a type of communication that happens in real time, allowing for real-time interactions between two or more users.

2. RTC technologies enable users to communicate with each other in a variety of ways, such as text messaging, audio/video conferencing, instant messaging, and more.

3. RTC technologies are used in a variety of applications, including online gaming, online education, telemedicine, and customer support.

4. RTC technologies are designed to be secure, reliable, and low-latency.

5. The most popular RTC technologies include WebRTC, SIP, and XMPP.

6. To ensure the best user experience, RTC technologies should be optimized for the particular application and user device.
