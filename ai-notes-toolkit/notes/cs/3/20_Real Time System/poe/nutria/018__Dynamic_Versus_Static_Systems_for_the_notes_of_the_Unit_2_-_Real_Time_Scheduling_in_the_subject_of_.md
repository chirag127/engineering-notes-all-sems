
### Dynamic Versus Static Systems

* Dynamic systems are those that require frequent changes in order to meet the changing needs of the system. These changes can include hardware, software, or both.
* Static systems are those that remain the same over time, and do not require any changes.
* Dynamic systems are more complex and require more resources to maintain.
* Static systems are simpler and require fewer resources to maintain.
* Dynamic systems are better suited for real-time systems, as they can respond quickly to changing conditions.
* Static systems are better suited for non-real-time systems, as they can provide a more consistent and reliable output.
* Dynamic scheduling is used in real-time systems to ensure that tasks are completed in a timely manner.
* Static scheduling is used in non-real-time systems to ensure that tasks are completed in an efficient manner.
