
### Temporal Parameters of Real Time Workload

1. Real time systems are computer systems where the correctness of the system depends on the time at which the results are produced.
2. Real time systems must meet deadlines; otherwise, the system will produce incorrect results.
3. Real time systems are classified based on the type of deadline they must meet:
    - Hard real time systems must meet their deadlines with certainty.
    - Soft real time systems must meet their deadlines with a certain probability.
4. The temporal parameters of real time workloads include the following:
    - Period: The time interval between two successive occurrences of the same task.
    - Deadline: The maximum amount of time that a task can take to complete.
    - Execution time: The amount of time that a task takes to complete.
    - Utilization: The ratio of the total execution time of all tasks to the total period of all tasks.
5. Real time systems must be designed to meet the temporal parameters of the workload. This includes selecting appropriate hardware and software components and scheduling tasks to meet the deadlines.
