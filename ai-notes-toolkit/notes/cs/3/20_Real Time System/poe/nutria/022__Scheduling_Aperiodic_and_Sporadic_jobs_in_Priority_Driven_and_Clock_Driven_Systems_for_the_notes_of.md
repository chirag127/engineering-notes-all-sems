
### Scheduling Aperiodic and Sporadic Jobs in Priority Driven and Clock Driven Systems

1. Aperiodic jobs are those that are not scheduled with a fixed periodicity and can be triggered at any time.
2. Sporadic jobs are those that are triggered at random times, but have a maximum inter-arrival time.
3. Priority driven systems are those in which jobs are assigned a priority, and the highest priority job is always executed first.
4. Clock driven systems use a clock to determine when jobs should be executed.
5. Aperiodic and sporadic jobs can be scheduled in both priority driven and clock driven systems.
6. In a priority driven system, aperiodic and sporadic jobs are scheduled in priority order, with the highest priority job being executed first.
7. In a clock driven system, aperiodic and sporadic jobs are scheduled according to the clock, with the job closest to the clock being executed first.
8. In both priority driven and clock driven systems, the scheduling of aperiodic and sporadic jobs can be optimized by using pre-emptive scheduling algorithms.
9. Pre-emptive scheduling algorithms allow aperiodic and sporadic jobs to be executed before lower priority jobs, thus optimizing the overall system performance.
