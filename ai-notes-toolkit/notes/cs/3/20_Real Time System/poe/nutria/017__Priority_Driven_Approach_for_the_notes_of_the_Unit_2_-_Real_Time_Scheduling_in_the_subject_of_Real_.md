
### Priority Driven Approach for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System

1. Priority Driven Scheduling is a scheduling algorithm used in real time systems to ensure that tasks are completed in a timely manner.
2. It assigns priorities to tasks based on the importance of their completion.
3. The tasks with higher priority are executed first and the tasks with lower priority are executed later.
4. Priority Driven Scheduling helps to ensure that the most important tasks are completed first and the less important tasks are completed later.
5. Priority Driven Scheduling also helps to ensure that tasks are completed within the specified deadlines.
6. It helps to improve the efficiency of the system by allowing the tasks to be executed in the most efficient manner.
7. Priority Driven Scheduling also helps to ensure that the system is responsive and can handle multiple tasks simultaneously.
8. The priority of the tasks can be determined based on the time constraints, resource constraints and other factors.
9. The priority of the tasks can also be changed dynamically based on the changing needs of the system.
10. Priority Driven Scheduling is an important tool for ensuring that tasks are completed in a timely and efficient manner.
