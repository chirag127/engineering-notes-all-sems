### Weiler and Atherton Polygon Clipping

Polygon clipping is an important concept in computer graphics that involves determining the intersection of two polygons. Weiler and Atherton polygon clipping algorithm is commonly used to find the intersection of two polygons.

The algorithm involves the following steps:

1. Identify the vertices of the polygons: The first step is to identify the vertices of the two polygons. The vertices of a polygon are the points at which the sides of the polygon intersect.

2. Identify the edges of the polygons: The next step is to identify the edges of the two polygons. The edges of a polygon are the lines that connect the vertices.

3. Classify the edges: The edges of the polygons are classified as either entering or leaving edges. An entering edge is an edge that enters the other polygon, while a leaving edge is an edge that leaves the other polygon.

4. Identify the intersection points: The next step is to identify the intersection points between the edges of the polygons. The intersection points are the points at which the edges of the polygons intersect.

5. Create the output polygon: The final step is to create the output polygon by connecting the intersection points and the vertices of the polygons. The output polygon is the intersection of the two input polygons.

The Weiler and Atherton polygon clipping algorithm is widely used in computer graphics for various applications such as computer-aided design and 3D modeling.
