### Projections for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Three Dimensional in the Subject of Computer Graphics

In computer graphics, projections play a crucial role in creating 3D images on a 2D surface like a computer screen. There are various types of projections used in computer graphics, and understanding them is essential for creating realistic 3D images. In this unit, we will study different types of projections used in computer graphics. 

Here are some essential points to consider while studying projections for Unit 3 - Three Dimensional in the subject of Computer Graphics:

1. Projection is a process of representing a 3D object onto a 2D surface. There are two types of projections:
   * Perspective projection
   * Orthographic projection

2. Perspective projection is used to create a realistic 3D view. It creates the illusion of depth and distance by simulating the way the human eye sees objects. Perspective projection is further classified into two types:
   * One-point perspective
   * Two-point perspective

3. Orthographic projection is a technique used to represent a 3D object in a 2D plane. In orthographic projection, the object's shape and size are represented accurately, but there is no depth perception. Orthographic projection is further classified into three types:
   * Front view
   * Top view
   * Side view

4. Another important concept is the projection matrix, which is used to map the 3D world onto a 2D plane. The projection matrix depends on the type of projection used.

5. In computer graphics, we use homogeneous coordinates to perform projections. Homogeneous coordinates are a set of four values that represent the 3D position of an object and its depth.

6. We use the following steps to perform a projection:
   * Multiply the object's coordinates with the projection matrix.
   * Divide the resulting coordinates by the fourth homogeneous coordinate.
   * Normalize the resulting coordinates to get the final projected coordinates.

7. In addition to perspective and orthographic projections, we also use other types of projections in computer graphics, like Isometric projection, Oblique projection, and Axonometric projection.

8. Understanding the different types of projections is essential in computer graphics as it helps in creating realistic 3D images. It also helps in mapping 3D objects onto a 2D surface while preserving their shape and size.

In conclusion, understanding projections is crucial in creating realistic 3D images in computer graphics. It is essential to learn the different types of projections and their applications to create accurate and visually appealing 3D images.
