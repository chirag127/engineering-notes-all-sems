## Unit 2 - Transformations

Transformations are an essential topic when it comes to mathematics. They help us understand how to manipulate geometric figures and understand how they relate to each other. In this unit, we will learn about various transformations and their properties. Here are some key points about transformations that you need to know:

### Translation

- A translation is a transformation that moves an object from one position to another without changing its shape or size.
- It is also known as a slide.
- A translation is described by the distance and direction of the movement.
- The direction and distance can be represented by a vector.
- The result of a translation is congruent to the original figure.

### Reflection

- A reflection is a transformation that creates a mirror image of an object across a line called the line of reflection.
- The line of reflection is the perpendicular bisector of the segment joining each point and its image.
- The result of a reflection is congruent to the original figure.
- A reflection can be thought of as a rotation of 180 degrees.

### Rotation

- A rotation is a transformation that moves an object around a fixed point called the center of rotation.
- The angle of rotation is the measure of the angle between the original and final positions of a point.
- A rotation can be clockwise or counterclockwise.
- The result of a rotation is congruent to the original figure.

### Dilation

- A dilation is a transformation that changes the size of an object.
- The size of the object is changed by a scale factor.
- A dilation can make an object larger or smaller.
- The center of dilation is the fixed point about which the object is expanded or contracted.
- The result of a dilation is similar to the original figure.

### Combinations of Transformations

- Transformations can be combined to create more complex transformations.
- The order of transformations matters.
- The resulting figure depends on the order in which the transformations are performed.
- The composition of two or more transformations is also a transformation.

In conclusion, transformations are a crucial concept in mathematics. They allow us to manipulate geometric figures and understand their properties. By understanding the properties of different transformations, we can solve complex problems involving geometric figures.
