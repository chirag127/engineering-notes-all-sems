### Phong Model

The Phong model is a standard reflection model in computer graphics that compromises between acceptable results and processing cost. It describes the interaction of light with a surface, in terms of the properties of the surface and the nature of the incident light .

- Phong shading, also known as Phong interpolation or normal-vector interpolation shading, is an interpolation technique for surface shading invented by computer graphics pioneer Bui Tuong Phong .
- The Phong reflection model, also called Phong illumination or Phong lighting, is an empirical model of the local illumination of points on a surface .
- This model is used to simulate the appearance of shiny surfaces, such as polished metal or apple skin, where a bright spot appears at certain viewing point locations. This phenomenon is called specular reflection .
- The Phong illumination model can be broken down into three components: ambient, diffuse, and specular. Each type of light component consists of red, green, and blue values .
