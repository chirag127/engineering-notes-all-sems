### Color consideration for the notes of the Unit 5 - Hidden Lines and Surfaces in the subject of Computer Graphics

- Hidden lines and surfaces are the lines and surfaces that are not visible from a particular viewpoint or projection.
- Hidden surface removal or visible surface determination is the process of identifying and eliminating the hidden surfaces from the rendered image  .
- Color consideration is the choice of colors for the visible and hidden surfaces to enhance the realism and clarity of the image.
- Some factors that affect the color consideration are:
  - The lighting and shading model used to simulate the illumination and reflection of light on the surfaces.
  - The depth buffer or z-buffer that stores the depth or distance information for each pixel in the image.
  - The intensity field that stores the color and coverage information for each pixel in the image.
  - The transparency or opacity of the surfaces that determines how much light can pass through them.
- Some guidelines for the color consideration are:
  - Use contrasting colors for the visible and hidden surfaces to distinguish them easily.
  - Use darker colors for the hidden surfaces to indicate that they are farther away or occluded by the visible surfaces.
  - Use lighter colors for the visible surfaces to indicate that they are closer or illuminated by the light sources.
  - Use gradient colors for the surfaces to show the variation of light and shade on them.
  - Use complementary colors for the surfaces that belong to different objects or groups to create a harmonious and balanced image.
