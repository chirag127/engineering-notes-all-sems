### Comparison of Pig with Databases

- Pig is a scripting platform that runs on Hadoop clusters, designed to process and analyze large datasets.
- Pig uses a language called Pig Latin, which is similar to SQL.
- Pig Latin does not require as much code in order to analyze data.
- Although Pig Latin is similar to SQL, it does have significant differences.
- Pig relies on scripts and it requires special knowledge.
- Pig works faster than Apache Hive.
- Pig makes our life a lot easier, otherwise writing MapReduce is always not easy.
- Traditional databases can't manage the volume of data in Big Data.
