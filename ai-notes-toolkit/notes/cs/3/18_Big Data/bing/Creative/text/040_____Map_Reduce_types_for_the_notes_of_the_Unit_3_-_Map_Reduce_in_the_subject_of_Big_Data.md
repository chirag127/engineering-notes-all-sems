### Map Reduce types for the notes of the Unit 3 - Map Reduce in the subject of Big Data

- MapReduce is a **programming model** and a **software framework** for processing large data sets in parallel using multiple nodes in a cluster or a grid .
- MapReduce consists of two main phases: **map** and **reduce**. The map phase takes input data and transforms it into intermediate key-value pairs. The reduce phase aggregates the intermediate values associated with the same key and produces the final output .
- MapReduce can be used for various types of big data analysis, such as:
  - **Entertainment**: MapReduce can help users find the most popular movies based on their preferences and previous viewing history.
  - **E-commerce**: MapReduce can help online retailers analyze customer buying patterns based on their interests or historical purchases.
  - **Social media**: MapReduce can help social media platforms process large amounts of user-generated content, such as posts, comments, likes, shares, etc.
  - **Data warehouse**: MapReduce can help data warehouse systems store, query, and analyze massive amounts of structured and unstructured data.
  - **Fraud detection**: MapReduce can help detect fraudulent transactions or activities by analyzing large volumes of data from various sources, such as credit cards, bank accounts, online accounts, etc.
