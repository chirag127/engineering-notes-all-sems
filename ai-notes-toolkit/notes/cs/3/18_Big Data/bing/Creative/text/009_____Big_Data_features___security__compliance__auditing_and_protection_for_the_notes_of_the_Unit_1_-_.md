### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

- Security: The process of protecting big data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. Security involves implementing various measures such as encryption, authentication, authorization, firewall, etc. to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of big data. Security also involves mitigating the risks of data breaches, cyberattacks, or insider threats that may compromise the big data assets .
- Compliance: The process of adhering to the legal, regulatory, or ethical standards that apply to big data. Compliance involves ensuring that the big data collection, processing, storage, and usage are in accordance with the relevant laws, policies, or guidelines, such as data privacy, data protection, data governance, etc. Compliance also involves demonstrating the accountability and transparency of the big data activities to the stakeholders, such as customers, regulators, or auditors .
- Auditing: The process of verifying and validating the big data activities and outcomes. Auditing involves conducting periodic or ad hoc reviews, assessments, or audits of the big data systems, processes, or results to ensure their accuracy, quality, reliability, and performance. Auditing also involves collecting and analyzing the audit logs, records, or reports that document the big data operations, transactions, or events .
- Protection: The process of safeguarding the big data from loss, corruption, or damage. Protection involves implementing various measures such as backup, recovery, replication, or redundancy to ensure the availability and durability of big data. Protection also involves preventing or recovering from the potential disasters, failures, or errors that may affect the big data systems, processes, or results .
