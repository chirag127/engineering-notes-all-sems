# Drivers for Big Data

Big data is the term used to describe the large and complex datasets that are generated from various sources and require advanced techniques and technologies to store, process, and analyze. Big data has become a key asset for many organizations and industries that want to gain insights, improve decision making, and create value from their data. 

There are several factors that have contributed to the emergence and growth of big data in the last decade. These factors are known as drivers for big data, as they create the demand and the opportunity for big data analytics. Some of the main drivers for big data are:

- **The digitization of society**: The widespread use of digital devices, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, and sensors, has enabled the creation and collection of massive amounts of data from various sources, such as online transactions, social media, web browsing, mobile applications, and digital media. According to a report by IDC, the global data sphere will grow from 33 zettabytes in 2018 to 175 zettabytes by 2025 .
- **The drop in technology costs**: The advances in hardware and software technologies have made it possible and affordable to store, process, and analyze big data. The cost of storage, for example, has decreased from $569 per gigabyte in 2000 to $0.02 per gigabyte in 2020. The emergence of cloud computing has also enabled the scalability, elasticity, and accessibility of big data solutions, as well as reduced the capital and operational expenses for data management.
- **Connectivity through cloud computing**: Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services, such as servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics, and intelligence, over the internet. Cloud computing provides several benefits for big data, such as scalability, elasticity, accessibility, security, and cost-effectiveness. Cloud computing also enables the integration and interoperability of data from different sources and platforms, as well as the collaboration and sharing of data among users and organizations.
- **Increased knowledge about data science**: Data science is the interdisciplinary field that combines mathematics, statistics, computer science, and domain knowledge to extract meaningful insights from data. Data science has become a popular and valuable skill in the modern economy, as it can help solve complex problems, optimize processes, and generate value from data. The availability of data science education, training, and tools has increased the awareness and adoption of big data analytics among various sectors and domains.
- **Social media applications**: Social media is the collective term for the online platforms and applications that enable users to create and share content, as well as interact and communicate with others. Social media has become a major source and consumer of big data, as it generates and collects large volumes of data from user-generated content, such as posts, comments, likes, shares, reviews, ratings, and feedback. Social media also provides a rich and diverse data source for analyzing user behavior, preferences, opinions, sentiments, trends, and patterns .
- **The rise of Internet-of-Things (IoT)**: IoT is the network of physical objects, such as devices, vehicles, appliances, and machines, that are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity, and can communicate and exchange data with each other and the internet. IoT has enabled the collection and analysis of real-time data from various sources and environments, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart industries, smart healthcare, and smart agriculture. IoT has also created new opportunities and challenges for big data, such as data quality, security, privacy, and governance .

These drivers for big data have created a high demand and a competitive advantage for big data analytics, as well as new challenges and opportunities for data management and innovation. Big data analytics can help organizations and industries to gain insights, improve decision making, and create value from their data.
