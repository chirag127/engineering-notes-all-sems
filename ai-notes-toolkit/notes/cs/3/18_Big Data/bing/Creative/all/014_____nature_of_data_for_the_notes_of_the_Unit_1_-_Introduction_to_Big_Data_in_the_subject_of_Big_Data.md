# Nature of Data

- Data is any information that can be stored, processed, analyzed, or communicated.
- Data can be categorized as **structured** or **unstructured** .
  - Structured data is data that has a predefined format and can be easily organized in tables, databases, or spreadsheets. It is usually numeric or categorical in nature. Examples of structured data are customer records, sales transactions, sensor readings, etc.
  - Unstructured data is data that has no fixed format and cannot be easily organized in tables, databases, or spreadsheets. It is usually textual, image, audio, or video in nature. Examples of unstructured data are social media posts, emails, web pages, videos, etc.
- Data can also be characterized by the **3Vs** model: **volume**, **velocity**, and **variety** .
  - Volume refers to the amount of data that is generated and stored. Big data typically involves large volumes of data that exceed the capacity of traditional data storage and processing systems.
  - Velocity refers to the speed at which data is generated and processed. Big data typically involves high velocity of data that requires real-time or near-real-time analysis and decision making.
  - Variety refers to the diversity of data types and sources. Big data typically involves high variety of data that comes from different sources and formats, such as structured, unstructured, or semi-structured data.
- Data can also be classified by its **purpose** or **usefulness**.
  - Data can be used for **descriptive** purposes, which means to summarize or visualize what has happened or what is happening. Descriptive data can help to understand the current situation or the past trends.
  - Data can be used for **predictive** purposes, which means to forecast or estimate what will happen or what is likely to happen. Predictive data can help to anticipate the future outcomes or the potential risks.
  - Data can be used for **prescriptive** purposes, which means to recommend or optimize what should be done or what is the best course of action. Prescriptive data can help to improve the decision making or the performance.
