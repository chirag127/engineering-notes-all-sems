### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

- Security: The process of protecting big data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. Security involves applying various techniques such as encryption, authentication, authorization, firewall, etc. to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of big data. Security also involves addressing the challenges of data privacy, data provenance, data quality, and data governance .
- Compliance: The process of adhering to the rules, regulations, standards, and policies that govern the collection, storage, processing, and use of big data. Compliance involves ensuring that big data is collected and used in a lawful, ethical, and responsible manner, respecting the rights and interests of the data subjects, data owners, and data users. Compliance also involves ensuring that big data is aligned with the business objectives, strategies, and values of the organization .
- Auditing: The process of verifying, monitoring, and evaluating the security and compliance of big data. Auditing involves collecting, analyzing, and reporting on the activities, events, and incidents that occur in the big data environment, such as data access, data modification, data breach, data quality, data performance, etc. Auditing also involves identifying and resolving any issues, risks, or gaps in the security and compliance of big data .
- Protection: The process of safeguarding big data from any potential threats, damages, or losses. Protection involves implementing various measures such as backup, recovery, replication, redundancy, etc. to ensure the resilience, durability, and availability of big data. Protection also involves preparing for and responding to any emergencies, disasters, or crises that may affect the big data environment, such as natural disasters, cyberattacks, power outages, etc .
