### Nature of Data

- Data is any information that can be stored, processed, analyzed, or communicated.
- Data can be structured, semi-structured, or unstructured depending on the format, organization, and schema of the information.
- Structured data is data that follows a predefined and consistent format, such as relational databases, spreadsheets, or XML files.
- Semi-structured data is data that has some structure but not a fixed schema, such as JSON files, HTML documents, or log files.
- Unstructured data is data that has no predefined format or structure, such as text, images, audio, or video.
- Data can be categorized into different types based on the nature, source, or purpose of the information, such as numeric, textual, spatial, temporal, or multimedia data.
- Data can also be classified into different levels of quality, such as accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, or relevance.
- Data quality can affect the reliability, validity, and usefulness of the data for analysis and decision making.
