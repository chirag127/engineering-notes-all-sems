### Nature of Data

- Data is any information that can be stored, processed, analyzed, or communicated.
- Data can be categorized as structured or unstructured.
  - Structured data consists of information that is organized in a predefined format, such as databases, spreadsheets, or tables. It is usually numeric or categorical in nature.
  - Unstructured data consists of information that does not have a fixed structure or format, such as text, images, audio, video, or social media posts. It is usually textual or multimedia in nature.
- Big data is a term that refers to data sets that are so large, complex, diverse, or fast-changing that traditional software for data processing cannot handle them efficiently or effectively .
- Big data can be characterized by the following dimensions:
  - Volume: The amount or size of data that is generated or collected.
  - Velocity: The speed or rate at which data is generated, collected, or processed.
  - Variety: The diversity or heterogeneity of data sources, types, or formats.
  - Veracity: The quality, accuracy, or reliability of data.
  - Value: The usefulness, relevance, or significance of data for decision making or problem solving.
- Big data can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Enhancing insight and understanding of complex phenomena or patterns.
  - Improving efficiency and effectiveness of business processes or operations.
  - Creating new products, services, or business models.
  - Innovating or discovering new knowledge or solutions.
  - Supporting or influencing decision making or policy making.
