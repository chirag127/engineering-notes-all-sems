### Intelligent Data Analysis

- Intelligent Data Analysis (IDA) is an interdisciplinary study that is concerned with the extraction of useful knowledge from data, drawing techniques from a variety of fields, such as artificial intelligence, high-performance computing, pattern recognition, and statistics .
- IDA aims to discover hidden patterns, trends, associations, and anomalies in data, and to use them for decision making, prediction, classification, and explanation .
- IDA generally consists of three stages: data preparation, data mining, and result validation and explanation .
  - Data preparation involves data collection, integration, cleaning, transformation, and reduction.
  - Data mining involves applying various algorithms and methods to extract knowledge from data, such as clustering, classification, regression, association rule mining, anomaly detection, and text mining.
  - Result validation and explanation involves evaluating the quality and usefulness of the extracted knowledge, and presenting it in an understandable and actionable way, such as using visualization, summarization, or natural language generation.
- IDA can be applied to various types of data, such as structured, unstructured, semi-structured, temporal, spatial, multimedia, and streaming data .
- IDA can also be categorized into different paradigms, such as statistical, machine learning, neural network, fuzzy logic, evolutionary, and hybrid approaches, depending on the underlying assumptions, models, and techniques used .
- IDA is a rapidly evolving and expanding field, with many challenges and opportunities, such as dealing with big data, high-dimensional data, complex data, noisy data, uncertain data, and dynamic data  .
- IDA has many applications and benefits in various domains, such as business, science, engineering, medicine, education, social media, and security   .
