### Pig - Introduction to PIG for the notes of the Unit 11 - Hadoop Eco System Frameworks in the subject of Big Data

- Pig is a tool or platform for processing and analyzing large data sets, also known as big data, as data flows  .
- Pig provides a high-level of abstraction for processing over the MapReduce framework, which is a core component of Hadoop  .
- Pig provides a high-level scripting language, known as Pig Latin, which is used to develop the data analysis programs  .
- Pig Latin is a declarative language that allows users to specify the desired results without describing the details of how to achieve them  .
- Pig Latin scripts are internally translated into a series of MapReduce jobs that are executed on the Hadoop cluster  .
- Pig can handle structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data, and can perform various operations such as filtering, grouping, joining, sorting, and aggregating on the data  .
- Pig can also support user-defined functions (UDFs) written in Java, Python, Ruby, or other languages, to extend the functionality of Pig Latin  .
- Pig can be run in two modes: local mode and MapReduce mode  .
  - In local mode, Pig runs on a single machine without requiring Hadoop or HDFS  .
  - In MapReduce mode, Pig runs on a Hadoop cluster and accesses the data stored in HDFS  .
- Pig is useful for data analysts, data scientists, and developers who want to perform complex data analysis tasks on large data sets without writing low-level MapReduce programs  .
- Pig is also useful for integrating with other Hadoop ecosystem components, such as Hive, HBase, Spark, and Flume  .
