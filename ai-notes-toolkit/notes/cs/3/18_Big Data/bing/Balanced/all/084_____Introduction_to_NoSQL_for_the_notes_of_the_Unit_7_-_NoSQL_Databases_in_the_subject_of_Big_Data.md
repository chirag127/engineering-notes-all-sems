# Introduction to NoSQL

- NoSQL stands for **Not only SQL** or **non-relational** .
- NoSQL is a type of **database management system (DBMS)** that is designed to handle and store large volumes of **unstructured and semi-structured data** .
- NoSQL databases use flexible data models that can adapt to changes in data structures and are capable of handling **high performance, scalability, and availability** requirements .
- NoSQL databases come in a variety of types including **document databases, key-values databases, wide-column stores, and graph databases**.
- NoSQL databases have become more popular due to the demands of **Big Data**, which involves processing and analyzing large and complex data sets that are beyond the capabilities of traditional relational databases.
