### Challenges of conventional systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data.

1. Inability to handle large volumes of data: Conventional systems are unable to store and process large volumes of data. Big data can be in terabytes or petabytes which conventional systems cannot handle.
2. Inability to handle high velocity of data: The speed at which data is generated and processed is very high in big data. Conventional systems are not efficient to handle the high velocity of big data in real-time.
3. Variety of data: Big data consists of varied types of data like structured, unstructured and semi-structured data. Conventional systems are designed to handle a specific type of data and not versatile enough to handle the variety in big data.
4. Integrating diverse data: The integration of the diverse data in big data to derive insights is a challenge for conventional systems which are not designed to handle different types of data together.
5. Scalability: It is difficult to scale up conventional systems to handle the huge volumes and variety of big data which makes it a challenge to use such systems for big data.

The above points highlight the key challenges of using conventional systems for big data and the need for alternative systems to handle big data efficiently.
