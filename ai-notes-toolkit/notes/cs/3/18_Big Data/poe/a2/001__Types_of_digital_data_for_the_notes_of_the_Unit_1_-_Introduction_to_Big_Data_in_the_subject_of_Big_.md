### Types of digital data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data.

1. Structured data - Data that resides in fixed fields within a record or file. Examples: Databases, spreadsheets.
2. Semi-structured data - Data that does not reside in fixed fields but does have tags or other markers to separate elements. Examples: XML, CSV, JSON.
3. Unstructured data - Data that does not have a predefined data model. Examples: Email, documents, social media posts, images, video, audio.

The characteristics of big data are commonly defined by the three V's:

1. Volume - Extremely large amount of data.
2. Velocity - Data is generated and processed at a very high speed.
3. Variety - Data comes from a wide variety of sources in many formats.

To gain business insights and make impactful decisions from big data, we need to collect, store, and analyze the different types of data effectively and efficiently. The tools and technologies for big data help in achieving this.
