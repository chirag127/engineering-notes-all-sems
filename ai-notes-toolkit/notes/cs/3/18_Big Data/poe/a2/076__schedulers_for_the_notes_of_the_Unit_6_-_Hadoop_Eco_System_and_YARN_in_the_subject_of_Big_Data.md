### Schedulers

1. FIFO Scheduler - Default scheduler. Submits jobs in FIFO order.
2. Capacity Scheduler - Assigns maximum capacity to queues. Excess capacity is distributed to queues as per weights. Jobs are submitted to queues as per queue mapping.
3. Fair Scheduler - Assigns resources to jobs to achieve fairness and limits domination of cluster by few jobs. Each job gets an equal share of resources to progress at similar rates.
4. Delay Scheduler - Adds delays to job submissions to prevent congestion. Submits jobs after configured delays. Increases fairness and throughput.

The above content covers the basics of the different schedulers available in YARN for distributing resources to the applications on a Hadoop cluster in a fair and efficient manner.
