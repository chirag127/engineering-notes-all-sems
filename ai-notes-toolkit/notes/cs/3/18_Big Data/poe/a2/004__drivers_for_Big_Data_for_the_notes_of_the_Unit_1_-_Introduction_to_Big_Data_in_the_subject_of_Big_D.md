### Drivers for Big Data

1. Increase in data volume: There is a huge increase in the data volume due to various factors like social media, sensors, digitalization, etc. This large volume of data helps in analyzing patterns and insights which can lead to better decisions and strategic business moves.

2. Variety of data: The data available now is of different varieties like structured, unstructured and semi-structured data. This varied data can be analyzed collectively to fetch deep insights. For example, combining text data with location data can reveal patterns in customer sentiments across regions.

3. Velocity of data: The speed at which data is generated and processed is very high now. This velocity allows companies to capture opportunities and act quickly based on the insights derived from the data. High-speed processing and real-time analytics have made this possible.

4. Low cost of storage: The costs of data storage have reduced significantly with the advancement of technology. This has made it possible to store large amounts of data at lower costs, in turn enabling the storing and processing of big data.

5. Computing power: The computing power and capabilities have increased tremendously making it possible to process the huge volume and variety of data at high velocity. Technologies like Hadoop and cloud computing have enabled processing of data on a large scale.

The above points cover the major drivers that have led to the growth of big data and enable big data analytics. Big data when harnessed properly can provide a competitive advantage and business growth opportunities for companies.
