

### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

In the world of Big Data, security, compliance, auditing and protection are crucial features. Here are some points to keep in mind:

- Security is important to keep sensitive data safe from unauthorized access. This includes both physical and virtual security measures. 
- Compliance refers to adhering to legal and regulatory requirements, such as data privacy laws. Failure to comply can result in legal penalties and damage to a company’s reputation. 
- Auditing involves tracking and monitoring data usage to ensure that it is being used appropriately and legally. This can help identify any potential security breaches or compliance issues. 
- Protection refers to measures taken to prevent data loss, such as backup and disaster recovery plans.

Some specific features related to these areas include:

- Encryption: This is the process of encoding data so that it can only be read by authorized parties. This helps to protect data in transit and at rest. 
- Access controls: These are mechanisms used to limit access to data based on the user’s identity and permissions. This can include authentication and authorization measures. 
- Data masking: This involves obscuring certain data elements to protect sensitive information. For example, masking credit card numbers to prevent unauthorized use. 
- Activity monitoring: This involves tracking user activity to identify any potential security breaches or anomalies. This can help detect and respond to threats in a timely manner. 

Overall, security, compliance, auditing and protection are essential features for Big Data systems. By implementing these features, organizations can ensure that their data is safe and legally compliant, while also minimizing the risk of data loss or theft.
