
### Jobs for the Notes of the Unit 9 - Spark in the Subject of Big Data

1. Data Engineer: A data engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining data processing systems. They are also responsible for ensuring the quality and accuracy of the data.

2. Data Scientist: A data scientist is responsible for analyzing and interpreting large amounts of data. They use their knowledge of mathematics, statistics, and computer science to develop models and algorithms that can be used to make predictions or uncover insights.

3. Database Administrator: A database administrator is responsible for managing and maintaining databases. They are also responsible for ensuring the security, availability, and integrity of the data stored in the database.

4. Business Intelligence Analyst: A business intelligence analyst is responsible for understanding the data and using it to make decisions. They use their knowledge of data analysis tools and techniques to uncover insights that can help drive business decisions.

5. Data Visualization Specialist: A data visualization specialist is responsible for creating visual representations of data. They use their knowledge of data visualization tools and techniques to create charts, graphs, and other visuals that can help make data easier to understand.
