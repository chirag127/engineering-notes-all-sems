
### Nature of Data

1. Data is the raw material used in decision-making. It can be collected from a variety of sources such as surveys, experiments, and observations.
2. Data can be qualitative or quantitative. Qualitative data is descriptive in nature and includes characteristics such as colors, shapes, and sizes. Quantitative data is numerical and includes measurements such as height, weight, and time.
3. Data can be structured or unstructured. Structured data is organized into fields and records, while unstructured data is not organized in any particular way.
4. Data can be static or dynamic. Static data is unchanging, while dynamic data changes over time.
5. Data can be discrete or continuous. Discrete data is composed of distinct values, while continuous data is composed of a range of values.
6. Data can be used to make predictions and inform decisions. Analyzing data can help organizations identify trends, make predictions, and take action.
