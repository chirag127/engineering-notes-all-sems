

### Comparison with Traditional Databases for the Notes of Unit 11 - Hadoop Eco System Frameworks in the Subject of Big Data

1. Traditional databases are designed to store and process structured data, while Hadoop is designed to store and process both structured and unstructured data.
2. Traditional databases are limited to a single server, while Hadoop is a distributed system that can span multiple servers.
3. Traditional databases are optimized for transactions, while Hadoop is optimized for analytics.
4. Traditional databases support ACID (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability) properties, while Hadoop does not support ACID properties.
5. Traditional databases are expensive, while Hadoop is relatively inexpensive.
6. Traditional databases are typically used for operational applications, while Hadoop is typically used for analytics applications.
7. Traditional databases are typically used for OLTP (Online Transaction Processing), while Hadoop is typically used for OLAP (Online Analytical Processing).
