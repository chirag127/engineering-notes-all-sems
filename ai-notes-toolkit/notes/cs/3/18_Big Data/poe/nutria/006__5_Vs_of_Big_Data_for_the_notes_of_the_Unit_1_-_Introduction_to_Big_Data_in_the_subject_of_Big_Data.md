
### 5 Vs of Big Data

1. **Volume**: Big Data refers to datasets that are too large to be stored and processed using traditional methods. This includes datasets of petabytes and exabytes in size.

2. **Variety**: Big Data includes data of all types, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data. This includes data from social media, web logs, images, audio, video, and other sources.

3. **Velocity**: Big Data is characterized by its high speed of data generation and ingestion. This includes data streams generated by IoT devices, sensors, and other sources.

4. **Veracity**: Big Data is characterized by its uncertain and incomplete nature. This means that the data is often noisy, inconsistent, and incomplete.

5. **Value**: Big Data has the potential to provide valuable insights for businesses, governments, and individuals. This includes insights into customer behavior, market trends, and other areas.
