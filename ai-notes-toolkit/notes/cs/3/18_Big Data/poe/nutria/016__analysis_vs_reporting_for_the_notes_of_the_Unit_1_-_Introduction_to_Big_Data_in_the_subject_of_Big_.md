
### Analysis vs Reporting

Analysis and reporting are two distinct processes that are used to gain insight from big data. 

**Analysis** is the process of examining data to identify patterns, trends, and relationships. It is used to identify opportunities, draw conclusions, and make predictions.

**Reporting** is the process of summarizing and presenting the results of analysis. It is used to communicate the findings to stakeholders and decision makers.

Analysis and reporting are both necessary for successful big data projects. Analysis helps to identify meaningful insights, while reporting helps to communicate those insights in a useful way.
