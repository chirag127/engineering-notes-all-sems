
### Drivers for Big Data

1. **Data Explosion**: The amount of data available is growing exponentially and will continue to do so. This is due to the proliferation of devices, such as smartphones, and the many services that rely on data collection, such as social media and IoT.

2. **Cost of Storage**: The cost of storage has decreased dramatically in recent years, making it more feasible to store large amounts of data.

3. **Data Analysis**: Advances in data analysis have made it possible to extract meaningful insights from large datasets.

4. **Real-Time Analytics**: Real-time analytics has become increasingly important in many industries, as businesses need to be able to respond quickly to changing conditions.

5. **Cloud Computing**: Cloud computing has made it easier to store and process large amounts of data, as well as to access data from any location.

6. **Data Visualization**: Data visualization has become increasingly important for understanding complex datasets. Visualizations can make it easier to identify patterns and trends in data.
