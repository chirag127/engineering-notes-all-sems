### Cost of Maintenance

- The cost of software maintenance can vary depending on several factors such as the software type, the number of users, and the required activities.
- Generally, software maintenance costs around $5000 per month or $60000 per year .
- The cost of maintenance can be as high as 60% of the total cost of the software development process.
- The cost of software maintenance can include module independence, programming language, programming style, program validation and testing, documentation, and configuration management techniques.
- Some costs are reasonably constant, such as the cost of operations, support and corrective maintenance, while others will vary, such as license variations, upgrades and enhancements.
