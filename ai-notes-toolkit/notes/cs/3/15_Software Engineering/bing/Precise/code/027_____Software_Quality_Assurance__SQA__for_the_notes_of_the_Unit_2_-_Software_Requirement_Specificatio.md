### Software Quality Assurance (SQA)

Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is a process that ensures that developed software meets and complies with defined or standardized quality specifications. SQA is an ongoing process within the software development life cycle (SDLC) that routinely checks the developed software to ensure it meets desired quality measures.

SQA includes the following activities:
- Defining and implementing standards and procedures for software development
- Reviewing and auditing the software products and activities to verify compliance with the defined standards and procedures
- Ensuring that non-compliances are addressed and resolved
- Reporting the status of quality assurance activities

SQA is important for the following reasons:
- It helps to ensure that the software is of high quality and meets the needs of the users
- It helps to reduce the cost of development by identifying and fixing issues early in the development process
- It helps to improve the overall development process by identifying areas for improvement

SQA is an important part of the software development process and is essential for ensuring the delivery of high-quality software that meets the needs of the users. It is important to have a well-defined and implemented SQA process in place to ensure the success of software development projects.
