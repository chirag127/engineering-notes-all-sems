### Estimation of Various Parameters such as Cost for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

1. **Cost estimation** is the process of predicting the resources and time required to complete a software project.
2. **Cost estimation techniques** include expert judgment, analogy, parametric models, and bottom-up estimation.
3. **Expert judgment** involves consulting with experienced software developers to estimate the cost of a project.
4. **Analogy** involves comparing the current project to similar past projects to estimate the cost.
5. **Parametric models** use mathematical equations to estimate the cost based on various project parameters such as size, complexity, and team experience.
6. **Bottom-up estimation** involves breaking down the project into smaller components and estimating the cost of each component individually.
7. **Software project management** involves planning, organizing, and controlling the development of software projects.
8. **Software maintenance** involves modifying and updating software after it has been delivered to the customer.
9. **Software maintenance activities** include corrective maintenance, adaptive maintenance, perfective maintenance, and preventive maintenance.
10. **Corrective maintenance** involves fixing errors and defects in the software.
11. **Adaptive maintenance** involves modifying the software to adapt to changes in the environment.
12. **Perfective maintenance** involves improving the software by adding new features or enhancing existing ones.
13. **Preventive maintenance** involves modifying the software to prevent future problems.
