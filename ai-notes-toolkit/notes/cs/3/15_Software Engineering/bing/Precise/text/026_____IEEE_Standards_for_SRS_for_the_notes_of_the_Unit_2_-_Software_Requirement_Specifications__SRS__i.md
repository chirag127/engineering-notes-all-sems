### IEEE Standards for SRS

- The IEEE standard for Software Requirements Specification (SRS) is **ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2011**, which was last updated in 2018. It replaced the previous standard, **IEEE 830**, which was last revised in 1998 .
- The standard covers the processes and information recommended for an SRS document, as well as its format .
- The SRS should include descriptions of both functional and nonfunctional requirements.
- The IEEE standard provides several suggestions for organizing functional requirements, including by mode, user class, object, feature, stimulus, functional hierarchy, or combinations of these criteria.
- There is no single organizational approach that is considered the best; the approach used should make sense for the specific project.
