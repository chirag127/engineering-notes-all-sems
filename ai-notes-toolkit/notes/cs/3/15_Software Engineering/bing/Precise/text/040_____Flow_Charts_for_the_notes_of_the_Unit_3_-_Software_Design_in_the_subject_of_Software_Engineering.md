### Flow Charts for the notes of the Unit 3 - Software Design in the subject of Software Engineering

- A flowchart is a graphical representation of an algorithm or a process.
- It is used to design, document, analyze, or manage a process or program in various fields.
- Flowcharts use standard symbols to represent different types of actions or steps in a process.
- The most common symbols used in flowcharts are:
    - Oval: used to represent the start and end of a process.
    - Rectangle: used to represent a process or an operation.
    - Diamond: used to represent a decision point.
    - Parallelogram: used to represent input or output.
    - Arrow: used to show the flow of control.
- Flowcharts are useful in software design as they help to visualize the flow of control in a program or system.
- They can also help to identify potential problems or areas for improvement in a process or system.
- Flowcharts are commonly used in software engineering to design and document the logic of a program or system.
- They can also be used to communicate the design to others, such as stakeholders or team members.
- Flowcharts can be created using specialized software or by hand using pen and paper.
- It is important to follow standard conventions when creating flowcharts to ensure that they are easily understood by others.
