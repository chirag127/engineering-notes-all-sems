### Verification and Validation

Verification and validation are two important processes in software engineering. They are used to ensure that the software meets the specified requirements and performs as intended.

1. **Verification** is the process of checking that the software meets the specified requirements. It involves reviewing the design, code, and documentation to ensure that they comply with the standards and guidelines set by the organization. Verification is often done through inspections, walkthroughs, and peer reviews.

2. **Validation** is the process of checking that the software performs as intended. It involves testing the software to ensure that it meets the user's needs and expectations. Validation is often done through functional testing, system testing, and acceptance testing.

Both verification and validation are important for ensuring the quality of the software. They help to identify and fix any issues or defects before the software is released to the users. By performing verification and validation, the development team can ensure that the software meets the specified requirements and performs as intended.
