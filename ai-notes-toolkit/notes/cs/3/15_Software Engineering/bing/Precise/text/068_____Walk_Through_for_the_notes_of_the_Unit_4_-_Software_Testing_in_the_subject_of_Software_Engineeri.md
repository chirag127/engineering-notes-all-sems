### Walk Through for the notes of the Unit 4 - Software Testing in the subject of Software Engineering

1. **Software Testing** is the process of evaluating a software system or its components with the intent to find whether it satisfies the specified requirements or not.
2. The main objective of software testing is to identify defects, errors, or bugs in the software.
3. There are two main approaches to software testing: **Static Testing** and **Dynamic Testing**.
4. **Static Testing** involves the examination of the software without executing it, while **Dynamic Testing** involves executing the software and observing its behavior.
5. Some common techniques used in software testing include **Unit Testing**, **Integration Testing**, **System Testing**, and **Acceptance Testing**.
6. **Unit Testing** involves testing individual components or modules of the software.
7. **Integration Testing** involves testing the interactions between different components or modules of the software.
8. **System Testing** involves testing the entire software system as a whole.
9. **Acceptance Testing** involves testing the software to ensure that it meets the requirements of the end-users or customers.
10. Software testing is an important part of the software development process and helps to ensure the quality and reliability of the software.
