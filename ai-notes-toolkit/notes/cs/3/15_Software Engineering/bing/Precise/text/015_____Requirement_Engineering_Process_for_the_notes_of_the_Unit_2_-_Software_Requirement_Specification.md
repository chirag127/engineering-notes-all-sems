### Requirement Engineering Process

Requirement Engineering is the process of defining, documenting, and maintaining requirements in the engineering design process. It is a critical part of the software development process and involves eliciting, analyzing, specifying, validating, and managing the needs of stakeholders.

The Requirement Engineering process can be broken down into the following steps:

1. **Elicitation**: This involves gathering requirements from stakeholders through various techniques such as interviews, questionnaires, and workshops.

2. **Analysis**: This involves analyzing the requirements to ensure that they are complete, consistent, and unambiguous.

3. **Specification**: This involves documenting the requirements in a Software Requirement Specification (SRS) document.

4. **Validation**: This involves verifying that the requirements meet the needs of the stakeholders and are feasible to implement.

5. **Management**: This involves managing changes to the requirements throughout the development process.

It is important to note that the Requirement Engineering process is iterative and may involve revisiting previous steps as new information becomes available or as the needs of the stakeholders change.
