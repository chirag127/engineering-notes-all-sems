### Testing Objectives

The main objectives of software testing are:

1. **Verification**: To ensure that the software meets the specified requirements and design.
2. **Validation**: To ensure that the software meets the needs and expectations of the user.
3. **Defect Detection**: To identify and report defects in the software.
4. **Quality Assurance**: To ensure that the software meets the desired level of quality.
5. **Reliability**: To ensure that the software performs consistently and accurately under normal and abnormal conditions.
6. **Performance**: To ensure that the software performs efficiently and effectively.
7. **Usability**: To ensure that the software is easy to use and understand.
8. **Maintainability**: To ensure that the software is easy to maintain and modify.

These objectives help to ensure that the software is of high quality and meets the needs of the user. They also help to identify and address any issues or defects in the software before it is released to the public.
