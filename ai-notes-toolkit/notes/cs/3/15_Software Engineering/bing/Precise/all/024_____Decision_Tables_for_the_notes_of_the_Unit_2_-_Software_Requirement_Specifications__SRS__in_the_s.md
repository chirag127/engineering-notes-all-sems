### Decision Tables

Decision tables are a precise yet compact way to model complicated logic. Decision tables, like flowcharts and if-then-else and switch-case statements, associate conditions with actions to perform, but in many cases do so in a more elegant way.

Here are some key points to remember about decision tables:

1. A decision table is a table that represents the logic of a decision by specifying all possible conditions and their resulting actions.
2. Decision tables are used to model complex logic in a compact and easy-to-understand format.
3. Decision tables can be used to represent business rules, system behavior, and other types of decision-making logic.
4. Decision tables are often used in software development, particularly in the requirements and design phases, to specify system behavior.
5. Decision tables can be used to generate test cases for software testing.
6. Decision tables can be used to simplify and clarify complex decision-making processes.
