# Testing Objectives

The main objectives of software testing are:

1. **Verification**: To ensure that the software meets the specified requirements and design specifications.
2. **Validation**: To ensure that the software meets the needs and expectations of the users and stakeholders.
3. **Defect Detection**: To identify and report any defects or issues in the software.
4. **Quality Assurance**: To ensure that the software meets the desired level of quality and reliability.
5. **Regression Testing**: To ensure that changes to the software do not introduce new defects or issues.
6. **Performance Testing**: To ensure that the software performs well under expected and peak load conditions.
7. **Usability Testing**: To ensure that the software is easy to use and understand by the intended users.
8. **Security Testing**: To ensure that the software is secure and protects against unauthorized access or attacks.
9. **Compliance Testing**: To ensure that the software complies with relevant laws, regulations, and standards.

These objectives help to ensure that the software is of high quality, reliable, and meets the needs and expectations of the users and stakeholders. They also help to identify and address any issues or defects in the software before it is released to the public.
