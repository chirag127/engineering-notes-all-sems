## Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering

- Software engineering is the discipline of designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software systems that meet the needs and expectations of users and stakeholders.
- Software engineering is not only about writing code, but also about managing the software project, ensuring the quality of the software product, and following the software process.
- Software engineering is influenced by and interacts with other disciplines, such as computer science, mathematics, engineering, management, and psychology.
- Software engineering is a dynamic and evolving field that adapts to the changing needs and technologies of the software industry and society.
- Software engineering is guided by some fundamental principles, such as abstraction, modularity, reuse, verification, validation, and documentation.

Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

- The definition and scope of software engineering
- The history and evolution of software engineering
- The software life cycle and software process models
- The software engineering ethics and professional responsibility
- The software engineering standards and best practices
- The software engineering challenges and trends
