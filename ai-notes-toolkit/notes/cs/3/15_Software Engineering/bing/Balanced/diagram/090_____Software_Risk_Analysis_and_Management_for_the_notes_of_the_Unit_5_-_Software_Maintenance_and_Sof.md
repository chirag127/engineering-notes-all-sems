### Software Risk Analysis and Management

- Software risk is the probability of occurrence for uncertain events and their potential for loss within an organization.
- Software risk analysis is the process of identifying, categorizing, and measuring software risks .
- Software risk management is the sequence of steps that help a software team to understand, analyze, and manage uncertainty.
- Software risk management consists of the following activities:
  - Risk identification: finding out the sources of potential problems and documenting them.
  - Risk analysis: estimating the likelihood and impact of each risk, and prioritizing them based on their severity.
  - Risk mitigation: planning and implementing strategies to reduce or eliminate the risks, such as avoidance, transfer, reduction, or acceptance.
  - Risk monitoring: tracking and reviewing the status of the risks and their mitigation actions, and updating the risk information as needed.
- Software risk management can help to improve software quality, reliability, security, and performance, as well as to reduce costs, delays, and failures .
- Software risk management can be applied throughout the software development life cycle (SDLC), from planning to deployment and maintenance.
- Software risk management can benefit from the use of tools and techniques, such as risk matrices, risk registers, risk breakdown structures, risk assessment frameworks, and risk management software .
