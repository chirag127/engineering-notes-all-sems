### Efforts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

- Software maintenance is the process of modifying and updating software after it has been delivered to the customer. It involves correcting errors, improving performance, adapting to changing environments, and adding new features .
- Software project management is the discipline of planning, organizing, leading, and controlling software projects. It involves defining the scope, schedule, budget, quality, and risks of the project, as well as managing the stakeholders, resources, communication, and deliverables .
- Software maintenance and software project management are related because both require continuous monitoring, evaluation, and improvement of the software product and process. Software maintenance is one of the phases of the software development life cycle (SDLC), which is a framework for software project management.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:
  - Software evolution: the process of creating software and then upgrading it on a regular basis for different purposes, such as adding new features or removing obsolete functionality.
  - Software configuration management: the practice of identifying, organizing, and controlling the changes made to the software artifacts, such as code, documentation, and data .
  - Software quality management: the process of ensuring that the software meets the specified requirements and standards, as well as the expectations of the customers and users .
  - Software risk management: the process of identifying, analyzing, and mitigating the potential threats and uncertainties that may affect the software project outcomes, such as cost, schedule, quality, and scope .
  - Software process improvement: the activity of assessing the current software process and implementing changes to improve its effectiveness, efficiency, and quality .
