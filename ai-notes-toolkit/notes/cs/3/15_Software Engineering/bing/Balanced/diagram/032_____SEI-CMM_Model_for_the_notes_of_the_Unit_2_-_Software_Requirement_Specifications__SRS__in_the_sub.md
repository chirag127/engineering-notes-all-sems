### SEI-CMM Model

- SEI stands for Software Engineering Institute, a research and development center at Carnegie Mellon University that provides leadership in software engineering and cybersecurity.
- CMM stands for Capability Maturity Model, a framework that describes the key elements of an effective software process and the levels of organizational maturity that determine the quality and productivity of software development .
- The SEI-CMM model was developed in 1986 based on a study of data collected from organizations that contracted with the U.S. Department of Defense, who funded the research .
- The SEI-CMM model has five levels of maturity, each with a set of process areas that define the goals and practices for that level   :
  - Level 1: Initial. The software process is unpredictable, poorly controlled, and reactive. There is no stable software development environment and the success depends on individual efforts and heroics.
  - Level 2: Repeatable. The software process is disciplined and project management processes are established to track cost, schedule, and functionality. Basic project planning, configuration management, quality assurance, and subcontract management are in place.
  - Level 3: Defined. The software process is well-defined, documented, standardized, and integrated into a standard software process for the organization. Detailed software engineering and management processes are tailored from the organizational process according to each project's needs. Process training, peer reviews, intergroup coordination, and software product engineering are performed.
  - Level 4: Managed. The software process is quantitatively measured and controlled using statistical and other quantitative techniques. Quality and process performance are predictable and deviations are detected and corrected. Process metrics, quality management, and process change management are implemented.
  - Level 5: Optimizing. The software process is continuously improved based on a quantitative understanding of the common causes of variation and their impact on process performance. Innovative ideas and technologies are proactively identified and deployed to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of the software process. Defect prevention, technology change management, and process improvement management are conducted.
- The SEI-CMM model helps organizations to assess their current software process maturity, identify the key areas for improvement, and implement the best practices to achieve higher levels of quality and productivity in software development   .
