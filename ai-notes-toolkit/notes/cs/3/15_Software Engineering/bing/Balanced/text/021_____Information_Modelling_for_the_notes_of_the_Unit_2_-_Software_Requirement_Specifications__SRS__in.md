### Information Modelling

- Information modelling is a process of creating a representation of the data and information requirements of a system or domain.
- Information modelling can help to identify and document the data entities, attributes, relationships, constraints, and operations that are relevant for the system or domain.
- Information modelling can also help to define the data flow, data structure, data quality, data security, and data integrity of the system or domain.
- Information modelling can be performed at different levels of abstraction and detail, depending on the purpose and scope of the model.
- Information modelling can use different techniques and notations, such as entity-relationship diagrams, class diagrams, data dictionaries, data flow diagrams, etc.
- Information modelling can be part of the software requirement specification (SRS) process, which aims to capture and communicate the functional and non-functional requirements of a software system.
- Information modelling can help to reduce ambiguity, inconsistency, incompleteness, and redundancy in the SRS, and to facilitate the design, development, testing, and maintenance of the software system.
