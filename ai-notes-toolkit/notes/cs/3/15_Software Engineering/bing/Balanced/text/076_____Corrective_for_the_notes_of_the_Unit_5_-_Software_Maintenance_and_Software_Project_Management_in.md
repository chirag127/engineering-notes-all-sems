### Corrective for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

- Software maintenance is the process of modifying and updating software after delivery to correct faults, improve performance, or adapt to changing environments .
- Software maintenance can be classified into four types: corrective, adaptive, perfective, and preventive.
  - Corrective maintenance is the process of fixing errors or bugs that are reported by users or customers.
  - Adaptive maintenance is the process of modifying software to cope with changes in the software environment, such as new hardware, operating systems, or standards.
  - Perfective maintenance is the process of enhancing software to improve its functionality, performance, usability, or reliability.
  - Preventive maintenance is the process of modifying software to prevent potential problems or reduce complexity.
- Software maintenance requires a lot of resources and effort, as it can account for up to 90% of the total software lifecycle cost .
- Software maintenance can be challenging due to factors such as lack of documentation, staff turnover, code decay, and legacy systems .
- Software maintenance can be improved by applying good software engineering practices, such as modular design, coding standards, documentation, testing, configuration management, and change control .

- Software project management is the discipline of planning, organizing, leading, and controlling software projects .
- Software project management involves many activities, such as project initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, control, and closure .
- Software project management aims to deliver software products that meet the customer's requirements, within the budget and schedule constraints, and with the desired quality .
- Software project management faces many challenges, such as uncertainty, complexity, change, and risk .
- Software project management can be improved by applying various methods, tools, and techniques, such as project management frameworks, software development models, estimation methods, scheduling tools, quality assurance methods, risk management techniques, and communication skills .
