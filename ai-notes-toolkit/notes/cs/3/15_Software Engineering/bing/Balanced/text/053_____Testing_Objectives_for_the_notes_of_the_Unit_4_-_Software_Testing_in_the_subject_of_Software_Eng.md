### Testing Objectives

- The main objective of testing is to **ensure the quality** of the software product by detecting and correcting defects before the software is delivered to the end-users.
- Testing also aims to **verify** that the software meets the specified requirements and **validate** that it fulfills the intended purpose and user needs.
- Testing can also help to **evaluate** the software's reliability, performance, security, usability, maintainability, and other quality attributes.
- Testing can also provide **feedback** to the developers and stakeholders about the software's status, progress, risks, and issues.
- Testing can also support **decision making** regarding the software's release, deployment, maintenance, and improvement.
