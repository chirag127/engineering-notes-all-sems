# Software Quality Assurance (SQA) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

- Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is a process that assures that all software engineering processes, methods, activities, and work items are monitored and comply with the defined standards.
- Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) is a comprehensive description of the intended purpose and environment for software under development. The SRS fully describes what the software will do and how it will be expected to perform.
- The relationship between SQA and SRS is that SQA ensures that the software meets the requirements defined in the SRS, and that the SRS is consistent, complete, correct, and verifiable.
- The SQA activities related to SRS include:
  - Reviewing the SRS for clarity, completeness, consistency, correctness, and testability.
  - Verifying that the SRS conforms to the standards and guidelines for SRS documentation.
  - Validating that the SRS reflects the needs and expectations of the stakeholders.
  - Tracking and resolving any issues or defects in the SRS.
  - Auditing the SRS for compliance with the SQA plan and procedures.
  - Reporting the status and results of the SQA activities to the project management and the customer.
