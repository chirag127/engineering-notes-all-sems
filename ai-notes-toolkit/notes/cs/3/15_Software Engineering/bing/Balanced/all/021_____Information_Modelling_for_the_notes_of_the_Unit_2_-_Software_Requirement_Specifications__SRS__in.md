# Information Modelling

- Information modelling is a process of creating a representation of the data and information requirements of a system or domain.
- Information modelling can help to identify and define the data entities, attributes, relationships, constraints, and operations that are relevant for the system or domain.
- Information modelling can also help to document the data and information flows, interactions, and dependencies among the system components or actors.
- Information modelling can be done at different levels of abstraction and detail, depending on the purpose and scope of the model.
- Information modelling can use different notations and techniques, such as entity-relationship diagrams, class diagrams, data flow diagrams, data dictionaries, etc.
- Information modelling can support the analysis, design, implementation, and maintenance of a system or domain, by providing a clear and consistent view of the data and information aspects.
- Information modelling can also facilitate the communication and collaboration among the stakeholders, by providing a common vocabulary and understanding of the data and information requirements.
