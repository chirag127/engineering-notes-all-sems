# ISO 9000 Models

- ISO 9000 is a family of standards that provide guidelines and tools for quality management and quality assurance in various domains, including software engineering .
- ISO 9000 standards are based on the principle of continuous improvement and customer satisfaction.
- ISO 9000 standards are not specific to any technology, methodology, or product.
- ISO 9000 standards can be applied to any organization, regardless of its size, type, or field of activity.
- ISO 9000 standards are voluntary, but they can be used as a basis for certification by third-party auditors.

## ISO 9000 Models for Software Engineering

- ISO 9000 models for software engineering are derived from the general ISO 9000 standards and provide guidance for the application of quality management and quality assurance to computer software and related services .
- ISO 9000 models for software engineering include the following standards :

  - ISO 9000-1: 1994 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 1: Guidelines for selection and use. This standard provides an overview of the ISO 9000 family and helps the user to select the appropriate standard for their needs.
  - ISO 9000-2: 1997 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 2: Generic guidelines for the application of ISO 9001, ISO 9002 and ISO 9003. This standard provides generic guidance for the implementation of quality management and quality assurance standards in any organization.
  - ISO 9000-3: 1997 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 3: Guidelines for the application of ISO 9001:1994 to the development, supply, installation and maintenance of computer software. This standard provides specific guidance for the application of ISO 9001:1994 to computer software and related services.
  - ISO 9000-4: 1993 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 4: Guide to dependability programme management. This standard provides guidance for the management of dependability aspects of quality, such as reliability, availability, maintainability, and safety.
  - ISO 9000-5: 1996 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 5: Guidelines for quality plans. This standard provides guidance for the preparation, review, acceptance, and revision of quality plans.
  - ISO 9000-6: 1999 - Quality management - Guidelines to quality in project management. This standard provides guidance for the application of quality management principles and practices to project management.
  - ISO 9000-7: 1994 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 7: Guidelines for configuration management. This standard provides guidance for the establishment and maintenance of configuration management systems.
  - ISO 9000-8: 1994 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 8: Guidelines for quality improvement. This standard provides guidance for the identification, analysis, and implementation of quality improvement opportunities.
  - ISO 9000-9: 1995 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 9: Guidelines for the selection and use of quality management system consultants and their services. This standard provides guidance for the selection, evaluation, and use of quality management system consultants and their services.
  - ISO 9000-10: 1998 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 10: Guidelines for the selection and use of quality management system auditors and their services. This standard provides guidance for the selection, evaluation, and use of quality management system auditors and their services.
  - ISO 9000-11: 1994 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 11: Guidelines for auditing quality systems. This standard provides guidance for the conduct of audits of quality systems, either by internal or external auditors.
  - ISO 9000-12: 1994 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 12: Guidelines for quality control. This standard provides guidance for the establishment and operation of quality control activities.
  - ISO 9000-13: 1995 - Quality management and quality assurance standards - Part 13: Guidelines for quality assurance in design/development. This standard provides guidance for the application of quality assurance principles and practices to design and development activities.
