# Software Quality Frameworks

- Software quality frameworks are models that connect and integrate the different views of software quality, such as the customer view, the developer view, and the product view .
- Software quality frameworks aim to ensure that software products satisfy the stated and implied needs of the end-users, as well as meet the quality standards and requirements of the software development process  .
- Software quality frameworks typically consist of four components:
  - **Plan**: Define the quality objectives, scope, criteria, and metrics for the software product and process.
  - **Implement**: Execute the quality activities, such as testing, inspection, review, verification, and validation, to ensure the software product conforms to the quality plan.
  - **Monitor**: Measure and analyze the quality results, such as defects, errors, failures, and non-conformities, to identify the root causes and areas for improvement.
  - **Improve**: Implement the corrective and preventive actions, such as rework, redesign, refactoring, and optimization, to enhance the software product and process quality.
- Software quality frameworks may also incorporate quality models, such as ISO 25010, ISO 9126, or ISO 5055, that define the quality characteristics and sub-characteristics of software products, such as functionality, reliability, usability, efficiency, maintainability, and portability .
- Software quality frameworks help software engineers to plan, implement, monitor, and improve the software quality throughout the software development life cycle, and to deliver software products that meet the expectations and needs of the customers and stakeholders  .
