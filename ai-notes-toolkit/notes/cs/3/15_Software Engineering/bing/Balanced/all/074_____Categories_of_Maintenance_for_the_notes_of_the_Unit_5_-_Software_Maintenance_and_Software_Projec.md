# Categories of Maintenance

Software maintenance is the process of modifying and updating software after it has been delivered to the customer. Software maintenance can be classified into four categories, namely, corrective, adaptive, perfective, and preventive   .

- **Corrective maintenance** is concerned with fixing errors that are observed when the software is in use. These errors can be logical, coding, or design errors. Corrective maintenance is the most common and costly type of maintenance  .
- **Adaptive maintenance** is concerned with modifying the software to cope with changes in the environment, such as new hardware, operating systems, or business requirements. Adaptive maintenance is necessary to keep the software usable and relevant  .
- **Perfective maintenance** is concerned with enhancing the functionality, performance, usability, or quality of the software. Perfective maintenance is driven by the user's feedback and suggestions. Perfective maintenance is the most frequent type of maintenance  .
- **Preventive maintenance** is concerned with anticipating and preventing potential problems and errors in the software. Preventive maintenance is based on the analysis of the software's structure, design, and code. Preventive maintenance is the least common and costly type of maintenance   .
