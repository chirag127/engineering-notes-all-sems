### Software Quality Attributes

- Software quality attributes are the characteristics or properties that affect the software's ability to meet the requirements and expectations of the stakeholders.
- Software quality attributes can be classified into two categories: functional and non-functional.
- Functional quality attributes are those that relate to the specific functions or features that the software provides, such as correctness, completeness, security, usability, etc.
- Non-functional quality attributes are those that relate to the overall behavior or performance of the software, such as reliability, efficiency, maintainability, portability, scalability, etc.
- Software quality attributes can be measured using various methods, such as metrics, standards, models, testing, reviews, etc.
- Software quality attributes can be prioritized and traded off based on the needs and constraints of the project, such as cost, time, resources, etc.
- Software quality attributes can be improved by applying various techniques, such as design principles, patterns, best practices, refactoring, etc.
