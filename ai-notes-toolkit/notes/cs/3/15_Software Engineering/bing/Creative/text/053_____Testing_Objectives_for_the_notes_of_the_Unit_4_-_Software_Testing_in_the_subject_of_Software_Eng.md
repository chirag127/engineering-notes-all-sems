### Testing Objectives

- The main objective of testing is to **ensure the quality** of the software product by **detecting and correcting** the defects or errors in the software.
- Testing also aims to **verify and validate** the software product by **checking** whether it meets the specified requirements and expectations of the users and stakeholders.
- Testing also helps to **improve** the software product by **identifying and eliminating** the weaknesses or shortcomings in the software design, development, and maintenance processes.
- Testing also provides **confidence** to the developers, testers, users, and customers that the software product is **reliable, usable, secure, and efficient**.
- Testing also supports **decision making** by providing **objective and quantitative** information about the software product's quality, performance, and risks.
- Testing also facilitates **communication and collaboration** among the developers, testers, users, and customers by **establishing a common understanding** of the software product's features, functions, and quality attributes.
