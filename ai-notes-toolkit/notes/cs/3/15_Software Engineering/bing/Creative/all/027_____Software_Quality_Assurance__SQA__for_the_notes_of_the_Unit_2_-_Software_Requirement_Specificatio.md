# Software Quality Assurance (SQA) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

- Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is the process of ensuring that the software products or services meet the quality standards and expectations of the customers and stakeholders .
- Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) is the document that describes the functional and non-functional requirements of the software system to be developed .
- SQA and SRS are related in the following ways:
  - SQA helps to verify and validate that the software system conforms to the requirements specified in the SRS .
  - SRS provides the basis for software testing, which is a key activity of SQA .
  - SQA helps to ensure that the SRS is complete, consistent, clear, and correct .
- Some of the benefits of SQA and SRS are:
  - SQA and SRS help to improve customer satisfaction and trust by delivering software products or services that meet their needs and expectations .
  - SQA and SRS help to reduce the cost and time of software development by preventing and detecting defects early in the software life cycle .
  - SQA and SRS help to enhance the quality and reliability of the software system by ensuring that it meets the quality standards and criteria .
  - SQA and SRS help to facilitate communication and collaboration among the software development team, the customers, and the stakeholders by providing a common understanding and agreement on the software requirements .
