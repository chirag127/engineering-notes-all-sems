### Software Configuration Management Activities

Software configuration management (SCM) is a process that tracks and monitors changes to a software system's configuration metadata, such as documents, codes, and other entities, during the software development life cycle. The primary goal of SCM is to increase productivity with minimal mistakes. 

SCM includes the following activities: 

- **Configuration identification** – This is the process of identifying all of the components of a project and ensuring that these components can be found quickly throughout the project life cycle. Configuration identification also involves defining the configuration items (CIs) and their attributes, such as name, version, status, and dependencies. Configuration identification also establishes the baselines, which are the approved versions of the CIs that serve as the reference points for further development. 

- **Configuration control** – This is the process of implementing a controlled change process that ensures that only authorized changes are made to the CIs and that the changes are recorded and tracked. Configuration control also involves reviewing and approving the change requests, evaluating the impact of the changes, and coordinating the implementation of the changes. Configuration control also ensures that the CIs are consistent and compatible with each other. 

- **Configuration status accounting** – This is the process of recording and reporting all the necessary information on the status of the development process. Configuration status accounting involves maintaining a configuration database that stores the CIs and their attributes, the baselines, the change requests, and the change history. Configuration status accounting also involves generating configuration reports that provide visibility and traceability of the CIs and their changes. 

- **Configuration auditing** – This is the process of verifying that the CIs and their changes comply with the requirements, standards, and regulations. Configuration auditing involves conducting formal and informal audits that check the completeness, correctness, and quality of the CIs and their changes. Configuration auditing also involves resolving any discrepancies or defects that are found during the audits. 

- **Software release management and delivery** – This is the process of preparing and distributing the software products to the customers or end-users. Software release management and delivery involves planning, scheduling, packaging, testing, and deploying the software products. Software release management and delivery also involves providing support and maintenance for the software products. 

These activities are interrelated and iterative, and they require coordination and communication among the stakeholders, such as developers, testers, managers, customers, and users. SCM helps to ensure that the software products are delivered on time, within budget, and with the desired quality and functionality.
