### Similarity and Differences from Conventional Engineering Processes for Software Engineering

- Conventional engineering processes are the methods and practices used to design, build, and maintain physical systems or products, such as bridges, buildings, machines, etc.
- Software engineering processes are the methods and practices used to design, build, and maintain software systems or products, such as applications, websites, databases, etc.
- Both conventional engineering and software engineering are based on some common principles, such as:
  - Applying scientific and mathematical knowledge to solve problems
  - Following a systematic and disciplined approach to engineering tasks
  - Using appropriate tools and techniques to support engineering activities
  - Adhering to quality standards and ethical codes of conduct
  - Communicating effectively with stakeholders and team members
- However, there are also some significant differences between conventional engineering and software engineering, such as:
  - Conventional engineering deals with tangible and physical entities, whereas software engineering deals with intangible and logical entities
  - Conventional engineering products are subject to physical laws and constraints, whereas software engineering products are subject to logical rules and constraints
  - Conventional engineering products are usually more stable and durable, whereas software engineering products are more dynamic and evolving
  - Conventional engineering products are often more expensive and difficult to change, whereas software engineering products are often cheaper and easier to modify
  - Conventional engineering processes are more mature and standardized, whereas software engineering processes are more diverse and evolving
  - Conventional engineering processes are more focused on the construction phase, whereas software engineering processes are more focused on the design phase
  - Conventional engineering processes are more dependent on the skills and experience of the engineers, whereas software engineering processes are more dependent on the tools and techniques used by the engineers
  - Conventional engineering processes are more regulated and certified, whereas software engineering processes are more flexible and adaptable
