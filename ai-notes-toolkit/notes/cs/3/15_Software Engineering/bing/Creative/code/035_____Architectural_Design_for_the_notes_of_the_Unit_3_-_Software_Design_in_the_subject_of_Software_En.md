### Architectural Design

- Architectural design is the process of defining the high-level structure and behavior of a software system.
- Architectural design involves identifying the major components of the system, their interfaces, their interactions, and their distribution.
- Architectural design also considers the non-functional requirements of the system, such as performance, reliability, security, scalability, etc.
- Architectural design is influenced by the system requirements, the domain knowledge, the available technologies, the design principles, and the design patterns.
- Architectural design is an iterative and creative process that requires trade-offs and compromises among various quality attributes and constraints.
- Architectural design is documented using various views and models, such as architectural styles, architectural patterns, architectural frameworks, and architectural description languages.
- Architectural design is evaluated using various methods, such as reviews, analysis, simulation, testing, and measurement.
- Architectural design is an important and challenging activity in software engineering, as it affects the quality, cost, and maintainability of the software system.
