### SEI-CMM Model

- SEI stands for Software Engineering Institute, a research and development center at Carnegie Mellon University that provides leadership in software engineering and cybersecurity.
- CMM stands for Capability Maturity Model, a framework that describes the key elements of an effective software process and the levels of maturity of an organization's software development capability .
- The CMM was developed by the SEI in 1986 based on a study of data collected from organizations that contracted with the U.S. Department of Defense.
- The CMM defines five levels of process maturity, from level 1 (initial) to level 5 (optimizing), based on the extent to which the organization's software processes are defined, managed, measured, controlled, and improved  .
- The CMM also provides a set of key process areas (KPAs) for each maturity level, which describe the goals, activities, practices, and standards that are required to achieve that level of process capability  .
- The CMM is intended to help organizations assess their current software process, identify the strengths and weaknesses, and plan and implement process improvement actions  .
- The CMM is widely used in the software industry as a benchmark for quality and best practices, and as a basis for software process improvement initiatives  .
