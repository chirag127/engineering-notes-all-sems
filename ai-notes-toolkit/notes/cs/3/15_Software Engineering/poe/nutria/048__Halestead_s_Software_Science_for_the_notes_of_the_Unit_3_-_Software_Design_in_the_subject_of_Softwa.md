
### Halestead's Software Science

* Software Science is the study of the principles and processes of software development. It is a branch of computer science that focuses on the design and implementation of software systems.
* The goal of Software Science is to create software that is reliable, efficient, and maintainable.
* Software Design is the process of designing a software system to meet the needs of the user. It involves the selection of appropriate algorithms, data structures, and other components to create a system that meets the user's requirements.
* Software Design involves the identification of the problem to be solved, the development of an algorithm to solve the problem, the implementation of the algorithm in a programming language, and the testing and debugging of the program.
* Software Engineering is the application of engineering principles and techniques to the design and development of software systems. It involves the use of tools and techniques to ensure the quality and reliability of the software. It includes the use of software engineering methodologies such as Model-View-Controller (MVC), Object-Oriented Design (OOD), and Agile Development.
* Software Maintenance is the process of ensuring that software systems are up to date and functioning correctly. It involves the identification and resolution of software bugs, the implementation of new features, and the optimization of existing code.
