
### Spiral Model

The Spiral Model is a software development process used in software engineering. It is a combination of the iterative and incremental processes, and is designed to help manage the risk associated with large, complex projects.

* The model follows a spiral pattern, with each iteration representing a cycle of planning, risk assessment, development, and evaluation.
* At the beginning of each cycle, the team identifies and evaluates risks, and then develops a plan to address those risks.
* After the plan is developed, the team implements the plan, evaluates the results, and then decides whether to continue with the project or to start a new cycle.
* The model is used to manage the complexity of large projects by breaking them down into smaller, more manageable pieces.
* The model is also used to manage the risk associated with large projects by allowing for the development of a plan to address those risks.
* The model is also used to manage the cost of large projects by allowing for the development of a plan to address those costs.
