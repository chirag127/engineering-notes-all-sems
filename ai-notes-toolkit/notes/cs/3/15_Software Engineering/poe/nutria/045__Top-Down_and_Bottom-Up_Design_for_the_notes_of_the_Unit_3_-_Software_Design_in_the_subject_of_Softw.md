
### Top-Down and Bottom-Up Design

Top-Down design is a method of software design that starts with a high-level view of the system and works its way down to the details. It is based on the idea that it is easier to break down a complex system into smaller, more manageable parts.

Bottom-Up design is a method of software design that starts with the details and works its way up to the higher-level views of the system. It is based on the idea that it is easier to build a complex system from the ground up.

Both approaches have advantages and disadvantages. Top-Down design is good for quickly designing a system, but it can be difficult to make changes later on. Bottom-Up design is more flexible, but it can be difficult to get the big picture. It is important to consider both approaches when designing a system.
