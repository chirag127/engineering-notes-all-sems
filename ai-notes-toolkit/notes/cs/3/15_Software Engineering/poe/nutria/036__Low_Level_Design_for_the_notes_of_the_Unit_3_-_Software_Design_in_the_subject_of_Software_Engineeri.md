

### Low Level Design for Unit 3 - Software Design in Software Engineering

1. Low Level Design (LLD) is a detailed design approach used to describe the software components and their interactions.
2. It is the process of transforming the high-level design into a detailed design of the software components.
3. It is concerned with the internal structure of the software components and the interfaces between them.
4. It involves the design of data structures and algorithms, the selection of appropriate software components, the design of the user interface, and the design of the communication protocol.
5. It is important to consider performance, scalability, security, and other non-functional requirements when designing a software system.
6. The goal of LLD is to produce a detailed design that meets the requirements of the high-level design.
7. It is important to validate the design against the requirements to ensure that the design is correct and complete.
8. The design should be tested to ensure that it meets the requirements and is robust against errors and exceptions.
9. The design should also be reviewed by experts to ensure that it is efficient, maintainable, and extensible.
