
### Compliance with Design and Coding Standards for the notes of the Unit 4 - Software Testing in the subject of Software Engineering

- Design and coding standards are a set of guidelines and rules that must be followed when developing software.
- These standards ensure that the software is consistent, reliable, and maintainable.
- Design standards include the use of proper naming conventions, the use of consistent coding styles, and the use of appropriate data structures and algorithms.
- Coding standards include the use of proper indentation, the use of consistent formatting, and the use of appropriate comments and documentation.
- Adhering to these standards is important as it makes the software easier to understand and maintain.
- It also helps to ensure that the software is of high quality and meets the requirements of the user.
