

### Verification and Validation for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Verification is the process of evaluating a system or component to determine whether the products of a given development phase satisfy the conditions imposed at the start of that phase. 
2. Validation is the process of evaluating a system or component during or at the end of the development process to determine whether it satisfies the specified requirements.
3. Verification is typically done by developers and is concerned with assessing the quality of the product. 
4. Validation is typically done by users and is concerned with assessing the quality of the process used to produce the product.
5. Verification involves reviews, inspections, and walkthroughs of the software design, code, or other work products to ensure they meet the requirements.
6. Validation involves testing the software to ensure it meets the user requirements.
7. Verification is a static process and does not involve executing the software.
8. Validation is a dynamic process and involves executing the software.
