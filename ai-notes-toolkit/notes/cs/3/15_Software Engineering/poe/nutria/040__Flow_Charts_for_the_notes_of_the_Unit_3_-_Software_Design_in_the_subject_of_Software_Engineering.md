
### Flow Charts for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Software Design in the Subject of Software Engineering

1. Flowcharts are diagrams that illustrate the sequence of steps in a process. 
2. They are used to document and communicate the logic of a program or process, and can be used to analyze and improve the efficiency of a program or process. 
3. Flowcharts are composed of symbols that represent operations, decisions, and input/output. 
4. Each symbol is connected with arrows to illustrate the flow of the program or process. 
5. Flowcharts can be used to visualize algorithms, processes, and data structures. 
6. Flowcharts can also be used to document the design of a program or process. 
7. Flowcharts are often used in the design phase of software development, but can also be used in the debugging and testing phases. 
8. Flowcharts can help identify potential problems in the program or process and suggest ways to improve its efficiency.
