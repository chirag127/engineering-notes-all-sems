
## Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering

1. Software engineering is the application of engineering principles to the development of software.
2. It involves the use of scientific and mathematical methods to create and maintain software systems.
3. Software engineering is a process-oriented approach to software development that focuses on the design, development, and maintenance of software systems.
4. The goal of software engineering is to create reliable, efficient, and cost-effective software systems that meet the needs of their users.
5. Software engineers use a variety of techniques and tools to develop software, including modeling, coding, debugging, testing, and documentation.
6. Software engineering also involves the use of software development methodologies, such as agile and waterfall, to ensure the successful completion of software projects.
7. Software engineering is a highly interdisciplinary field that requires knowledge of computer science, mathematics, and other disciplines.
