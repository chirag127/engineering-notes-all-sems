
### Coupling and Cohesion Measures for the notes of the Unit 3 - Software Design in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Coupling is a measure of the degree of interdependence between software modules. It is a measure of how closely related two or more modules are. High coupling indicates that the modules are highly dependent on each other and changes to one module will likely affect the other modules. Low coupling indicates that the modules are loosely coupled and changes to one module will not likely affect the other modules.

2. Cohesion is a measure of the degree to which the elements of a module belong together. High cohesion indicates that the elements of the module are closely related to each other and serve a single purpose. Low cohesion indicates that the elements of the module are loosely related and serve multiple purposes.

3. Coupling and cohesion are important concepts in software engineering because they help to determine the structure and organization of software projects. By reducing coupling and increasing cohesion, software projects can be made easier to maintain and modify.
