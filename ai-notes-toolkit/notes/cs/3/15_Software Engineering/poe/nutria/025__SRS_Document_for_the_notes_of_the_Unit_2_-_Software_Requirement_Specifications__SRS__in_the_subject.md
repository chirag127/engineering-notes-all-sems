
### SRS Document for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Software Requirement Specification (SRS) is a document that specifies the requirements of a software system. It is used to define the functional and non-functional requirements of the system, as well as its interfaces with other systems.

2. SRS documents are typically created by software engineers, but can also be created by product managers, business analysts, and other stakeholders.

3. The purpose of an SRS document is to clearly define the scope of a software system, so that all stakeholders have a clear understanding of the system's capabilities.

4. SRS documents provide a detailed description of the software system's functions, features, and behaviors. They also provide information about the system's performance, reliability, and usability.

5. The SRS document should include a list of all the requirements for the system, including functional, non-functional, and interface requirements.

6. The SRS document should also include a list of all the assumptions and constraints that are relevant to the system.

7. The SRS document should be reviewed by all stakeholders to ensure that it meets their needs and expectations.

8. Once the SRS document is complete, it should be used as the basis for the design and development of the software system.
