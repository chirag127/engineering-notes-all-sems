
### Iterative Enhancement Models

1. Iterative enhancement models are a type of software engineering process which involves the continual improvement of a software product over time.

2. This process begins with the initial development of a software product, followed by a series of iterations or cycles of refinement and improvement.

3. Each iteration is designed to improve the quality and usability of the product, while also incorporating feedback from stakeholders and end users.

4. Iterative enhancement models are beneficial because they allow for the rapid development of a software product while still maintaining a high level of quality.

5. These models are often used in agile software development, where teams work together to quickly deliver a product with high customer satisfaction.

6. Iterative enhancement models are also used in traditional software development processes, such as the Waterfall model, where each iteration is used to refine and improve the product.
