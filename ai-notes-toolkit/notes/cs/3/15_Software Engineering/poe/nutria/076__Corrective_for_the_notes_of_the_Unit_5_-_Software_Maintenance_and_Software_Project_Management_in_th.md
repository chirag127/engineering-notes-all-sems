
### Corrective for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Software Maintenance: 
    - Software maintenance is the process of making changes to existing software to improve its performance, fix bugs, and keep it up-to-date with the latest technology.
    - It involves the development and implementation of corrective, adaptive, and perfective actions to ensure that the software continues to meet the requirements of the user.
    - The maintenance process includes identifying and resolving problems, making changes to the code, and testing the software to ensure that it is working properly.
    - The maintenance process also involves the documentation of changes, the updating of user manuals, and the training of users.

2. Software Project Management:
    - Software project management is the process of managing the development of software projects.
    - It involves the planning, scheduling, monitoring, and controlling of software development activities.
    - The goal of software project management is to ensure that the software is delivered on time, within budget, and with the desired quality.
    - It involves the management of resources, budget, and timeline, as well as the coordination of the development team.
