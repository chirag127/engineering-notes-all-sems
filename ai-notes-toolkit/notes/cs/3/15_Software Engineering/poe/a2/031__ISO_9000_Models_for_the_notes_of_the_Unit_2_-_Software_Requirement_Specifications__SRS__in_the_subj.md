### ISO 9000 Models for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

1. ISO 9000 is a set of international standards on quality management and quality assurance developed to help organizations meet the needs of customers and other stakeholders.

2. ISO 9001:2015 is the current version of ISO 9000 and is the most popular standard in the family. It deals with the requirements that organizations need to meet to demonstrate their ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements.

3. ISO 9000 promotes the adoption of a process approach to management, where the focus is on activities and actions that are required and how they interact. This encourages organizations to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of their processes and overall performance.

4. The ISO 9000 family of standards take a process-based approach that emphasizes:

- Understanding customer needs and meeting customer requirements.
- Leadership and people involvement.
- A systemic approach to management.
- Continual improvement of the organization's overall performance.

5. The standards are generic and can be applied to any organization, large or small, in any industry or sector. They are not prescriptive and do not specify absolute requirements. The standards have to be interpreted for each organization based on its particular circumstances.

6. The standards can help an organization meet its own goals, as well as satisfy the demands and expectations of its customers and other interested parties. When implemented effectively, the standards can create a more efficient, effective and competitive organization that enhances customer satisfaction.
