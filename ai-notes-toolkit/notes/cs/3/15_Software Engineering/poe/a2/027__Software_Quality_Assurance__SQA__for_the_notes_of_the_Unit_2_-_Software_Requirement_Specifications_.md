### Software Quality Assurance (SQA) for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Software Quality Assurance (SQA) is a set of activities for ensuring that the software being developed is according to the specifications and standards.
2. SQA involves reviewing and auditing the software artifacts and processes to identify and remove defects and problems.
3. SQA activities are performed at each stage of the software development lifecycle to help build quality into the software.
4. Some of the SQA activities are:
- Reviews: Informal reviews, technical reviews, inspection, walkthroughs, etc.
- Software testing: Unit testing, integration testing, system testing, acceptance testing, etc.
- Verification and validation: Ensuring that the software meets the specified requirements and is fit for use.
- Audits: Auditing the software development process and artifacts for compliance to standards and procedures.
- Collecting metrics: Collecting metrics on quality, productivity, processes, resources, etc. to track and monitor the software development.
5. The benefits of SQA are:
- Higher quality software delivered
- Lower costs due to early detection and removal of defects
- Increased user satisfaction
- Improved productivity and resource utilization
- Better processes and standards compliance
