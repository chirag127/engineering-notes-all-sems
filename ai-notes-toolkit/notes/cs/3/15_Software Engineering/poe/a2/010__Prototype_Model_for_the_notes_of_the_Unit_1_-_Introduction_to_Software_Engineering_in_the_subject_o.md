### Prototype Model for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Software Engineering in the subject of Software Engineering.

1. The Prototype Model is a software development model.
2. In this model, instead of freezing the requirements before a design or coding can proceed, a throwaway prototype is built to understand the requirements.
3. The prototype is developed based on the currently known requirements.
4. The client evaluates the prototype and provides feedback.
5. The requirements are modified if necessary.
6. The prototype is thrown away and a new prototype is evolved.
7. This process continues until the client is satisfied with the prototype.
8. The system is then developed based on the final prototype.
9. The main advantage of the prototype model is that the users get a working model of the system early and can provide valuable feedback.
10. The disadvantages are that it may be expensive to build multiple prototypes and the final system may be quite different from the prototype.
