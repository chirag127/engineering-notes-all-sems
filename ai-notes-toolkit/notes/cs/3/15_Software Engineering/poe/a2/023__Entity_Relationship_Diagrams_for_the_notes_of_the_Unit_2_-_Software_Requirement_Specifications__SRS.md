### Entity Relationship Diagrams for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering:

1. An Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) is a data modeling technique that creates a graphical representation of the entities and relationships of an information system.
2. ERDs are often used to model and design relational databases in software engineering and systems analysis.
3. The main components of an ERD are:
- Entities: Represented by rectangles. They are objects or concepts in the real world that are distinguishable from other objects.
- Attributes: Represented by ovals. They are properties of an entity that provide more details about the entity.
- Relationships: Represented by diamonds. They are associations between entities that describe how the entities are related to each other.
4. The three main types of relationships in an ERD are:
- One-to-one: One entity is linked to only one other entity.
- One-to-many: One entity is linked to many other entities.
- Many-to-many: Many entities are linked to many other entities via an intermediate table.
5. ERDs provide a structural view of data and are commonly used by database analysts and designers to model database schemas. They promote the efficient organization and utilization of data.
