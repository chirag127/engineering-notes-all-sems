### Formal Technical Reviews (Peer Reviews) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Software Testing in the subject of Software Engineering

1. Formal technical reviews, also known as peer reviews, are a systematic technique for detecting and removing defects from a software product before it is released.
2. The key goals of a formal technical review are:
- Finding and fixing defects early.
- Ensuring high quality.
- Promoting learning and better understanding of the product.
3. The steps involved in a formal technical review process are:
- Planning the review.
- Overview of the product.
- Individual preparation.
- Group meeting (review meeting).
- Re-work by author (if required).
4. The roles involved in a formal technical review are:
- Moderator: Manages the review process.
- Author: Wrote the product (document/code) being reviewed.
- Reviewers: Review the product and provide feedback.
5. The advantages of formal technical reviews are:
- Higher defect detection rate.
- Better product quality.
- Learning opportunity for team members.
- Early and rapid feedback to the author.
- Cost effective.
