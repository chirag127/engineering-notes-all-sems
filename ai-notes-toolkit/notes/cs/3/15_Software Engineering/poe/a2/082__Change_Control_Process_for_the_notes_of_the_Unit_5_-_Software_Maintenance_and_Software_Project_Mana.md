### Change Control Process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Maintenance and Software Project Management in the subject of Software Engineering.

1. Change Request: Any requested modification to the software or system is documented in a change request. This includes requests to fix defects/bugs or to make improvements/enhancements.
2. Analysis: The impact of the change is analyzed in terms of cost, timescales and risk. The implications of the change are fully assessed before proceeding to the next step.
3. Authorization: The change request is reviewed and authorization for the change is given by the appropriate authority (e.g. Project Manager, Change Control Board).
4. Implementation: The change is implemented by the developers/maintenance team.
5. Testing: The modified system is tested to ensure that it meets the required functionality and quality standards.
6. Documentation: Any necessary modifications to documentation are made to reflect the changes made to the software.
7. Release: The changed software is released and distributed to users.

The change control process ensures that only tested and authorized changes are made to the software. It helps to minimize disruption and risk. A formal change control process should be in place for the entire lifecycle of the software to track and control changes.
