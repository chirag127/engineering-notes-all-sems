- Expert opinion: Relying on experts' judgments to estimate effort.
- Algorithmic estimation: Using mathematical formulas/models to estimate effort based on size and other factors. e.g. COCOMO model.
- Analogy-based estimation: Basing estimates on historical data from similar projects.

2. Estimation of Duration:
- depend on effort estimation and the team structure & availability.
- PERT technique: Optimistic, most likely and pessimistic estimates are combined.

3. Estimation of Cost:
- Salaries & wages: Based on team structure and experience.
- Equipment: One-time costs and recurring costs for HW and SW tools.
- Facilities: Rent, utilities, etc.
- Other costs: Travel, supplies, etc.
