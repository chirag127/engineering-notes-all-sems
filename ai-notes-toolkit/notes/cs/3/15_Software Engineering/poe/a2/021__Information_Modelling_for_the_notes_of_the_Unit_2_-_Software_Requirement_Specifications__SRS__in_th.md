### Information Modelling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) in the subject of Software Engineering.

1. Information Modelling: It is the process of developing an abstract, simplified view of the information that is to be stored and managed in a system. It helps in identifying the types of data/information to be stored and the relationships between different data elements.
2. Purpose: The main purposes of information modelling are:
- To understand the information needs of the system.
- To identify the entities and relations.
- To achieve a consensus between the users and analysts on the basic information structure.
3. Types: The three main types of information models are:
- Conceptual data model: Represents the semantics of the information. Focuses on the major entities and relationships.
- Logical data model: Represents the data structurally. Focuses on the tables, columns, relationships, and constraints.
- Physical data model: Represents the physical implementation. Focuses on tables, columns, relationships, constraints, indexes, etc.
4. Benefits: The key benefits of information modeling are:
- It leads to a shared and common understanding of the information/data requirements.
- It provides a basis for designing the database.
- It makes the process of eliciting and documenting user requirements more effective and efficient.
- It leads to a system that better matches the users' needs.
