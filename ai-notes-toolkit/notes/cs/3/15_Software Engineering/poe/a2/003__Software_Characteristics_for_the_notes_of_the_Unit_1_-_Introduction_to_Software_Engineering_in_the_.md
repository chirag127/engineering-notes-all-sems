### Software Characteristics

1. Reliability - The ability of a software product to perform its required functions under stated conditions for a specified period of time.
- Fault Tolerance - The ability of a system to continue operating properly in the event of the failure of (or one or more faults within) some of its components.
- Robustness - The ability of a system or component to function correctly in the presence of invalid inputs or stressful environmental conditions.

2. Usability - The ease with which a user can learn to operate, prepare inputs for, and interpret outputs of a system or component.
- Learnability - How easy it is for users to accomplish basic tasks the first time they encounter the design.
- Understandability - How easy it is for users to understand whether the system is appropriate for their needs, how it works, and the consequences of actions.
- Operability - How easy it is for users to operate the system or component to accomplish tasks.

3. Efficiency - The amount of system resources a system or component uses to provide the required functionality.
- Time behavior - The timing requirements that the system must meet, including response time, throughput, and latency.
- Resource utilization - The amounts and types of memory and other system resources used by a software system or component.

4. Adaptability - The ability of a system or component to be adapted for different specified environments or scenarios.
- Modifiability - The effort required to make changes to a system or component.
- Reusability - The degree to which an asset can be used in more than one system, or in building other assets.
