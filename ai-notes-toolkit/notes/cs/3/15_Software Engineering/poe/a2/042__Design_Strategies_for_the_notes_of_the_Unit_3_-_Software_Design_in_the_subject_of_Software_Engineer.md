### Design Strategies for the notes of the Unit 3 - Software Design in the subject of Software Engineering.

1. Decomposition: Breaking down a complex problem into simpler subproblems.
- Divide and conquer.
- Reduces complexity.
- Makes problems tractable.

2. Abstraction: Ignoring unnecessary details and focusing on essential characteristics.
- Hiding complexity.
- Managing complexity.
- Encapsulation.

3. Modularization: Breaking the system into multiple modules that have distinct functions.
- Increase organization.
- Divide work.
- Well-defined interfaces.
- Limited interactions/dependencies.

4. Information Hiding: Hiding the internal workings/data of a module and only revealing the interfaces to other modules.
- Reduced coupling.
- Increased flexibility/maintainability.
- Localized impact of changes.

5. Layering: Arranging modules in hierarchical layers where each layer provides services to the layer above and receives services from the layer below.
- Separation of concerns.
- Interface definition.
- System architecture.
