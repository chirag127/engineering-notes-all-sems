# World Coordinate

- World coordinate is a system of coordinates that defines the position and orientation of objects in a virtual or augmented reality scene.
- World coordinate is usually defined by three axes: X, Y, and Z, which are perpendicular to each other and form a right-handed coordinate system.
- World coordinate is also known as global coordinate or scene coordinate, as it represents the reference frame of the entire scene.
- World coordinate is independent of the camera or the user's viewpoint, and it does not change with the movement or rotation of the camera or the user.
- World coordinate is useful for defining the spatial relationships between objects in the scene, such as their distance, direction, and alignment.
- World coordinate can be transformed into other coordinate systems, such as camera coordinate, screen coordinate, or device coordinate, by applying appropriate transformation matrices.
