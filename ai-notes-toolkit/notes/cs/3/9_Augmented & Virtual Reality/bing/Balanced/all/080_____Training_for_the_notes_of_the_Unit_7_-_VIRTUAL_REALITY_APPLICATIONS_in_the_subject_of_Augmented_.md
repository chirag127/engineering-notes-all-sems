# Training for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- Virtual reality uses software and hardware to immerse a user, and potentially other viewers, in a digital environment. They get to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.
- Virtual reality applications require interactive devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits, that send and receive information and create a sense of presence and agency for the user.
- Virtual reality applications can be classified into different categories based on their purpose, content, and level of immersion. Some of the common categories are:
  - Education and training: VR applications can be used to teach and train students and professionals in various fields, such as medical, military, engineering, and science. VR applications can provide realistic and safe simulations of complex scenarios, such as surgery, combat, or space exploration, that enhance learning outcomes and skills.
  - Entertainment and gaming: VR applications can be used to create immersive and engaging experiences for entertainment and gaming purposes. VR applications can offer different genres, such as adventure, horror, sports, or puzzle, that appeal to different preferences and tastes. VR applications can also create social and collaborative environments, where users can interact with other users or virtual characters.
  - Business and commerce: VR applications can be used to facilitate business and commerce activities, such as virtual meetings, product design, marketing, and shopping. VR applications can enable remote collaboration, communication, and visualization of data and products, that can improve efficiency and productivity. VR applications can also provide personalized and customized services and recommendations, that can enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  - Art and creativity: VR applications can be used to create and showcase artistic and creative works, such as paintings, sculptures, music, or stories. VR applications can provide tools and platforms for artists and creators to express themselves and share their visions with others. VR applications can also inspire and stimulate the imagination and creativity of users, who can explore and interact with different forms of art.
  - Health and wellness: VR applications can be used to improve the health and wellness of users, such as physical, mental, and emotional well-being. VR applications can provide exercises, therapies, and treatments, that can help users cope with various conditions, such as pain, stress, anxiety, depression, or phobias. VR applications can also provide relaxation, meditation, and mindfulness, that can enhance the mood and happiness of users.
