## Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS

- Virtual reality (VR) is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way by a person using special equipment, such as a headset, gloves, or controllers.
- Virtual environments (VEs) are specific types of VR that aim to create a realistic and immersive representation of a real or imagined world, where the user can navigate, explore, and manipulate objects.
- VR and VEs have many applications in various domains, such as entertainment, education, training, health care, engineering, and art.
- VR and VEs are based on several technologies and concepts, such as computer graphics, human-computer interaction, perception, presence, and immersion.
- Computer graphics are the methods and techniques for creating and displaying images, animations, and effects on a computer screen or other devices.
- Human-computer interaction (HCI) is the study and design of how humans and computers communicate and interact with each other, using various input and output devices, software, and interfaces.
- Perception is the process of acquiring, interpreting, and organizing sensory information from the external world, such as vision, hearing, touch, smell, and taste.
- Presence is the subjective feeling of being in a virtual environment, as if it were the actual physical environment, and of being there with other virtual or real entities.
- Immersion is the degree to which a virtual environment can create a sense of reality and involvement for the user, by providing realistic sensory stimuli, interactivity, and feedback.
