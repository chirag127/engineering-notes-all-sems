### Database - World Space

- Database is a collection of organized data that can be accessed, managed, and updated.
- World space is the physical environment where augmented reality (AR) applications are deployed and interacted with.
- Database - world space is the concept of using database to store, retrieve, and manipulate data that is relevant to the world space of AR applications.
- Some examples of database - world space are:
  - Data in space: a feature of Power BI mobile apps that allows users to pin reports to real-world locations using AR .
  - PointCloudsVR: a software that enables scientists to visualize and explore large 3D point clouds of planetary surfaces in VR.
  - T2AR: a project that demonstrates how astronauts can inspect and maintain equipment on the International Space Station using AR.
- Some benefits of database - world space are:
  - It can enhance the user experience and engagement of AR applications by providing contextual and relevant data.
  - It can improve the efficiency and accuracy of data analysis and decision making by allowing users to access data in situ.
  - It can reduce the dependency on network connectivity and bandwidth by storing data locally on the device or nearby servers.
- Some challenges of database - world space are:
  - It requires a reliable and robust system for spatial mapping and tracking to align the virtual data with the real world.
  - It involves complex data management and synchronization issues to ensure data consistency and integrity across multiple devices and users.
  - It poses privacy and security risks to the data and the users, especially when sensitive or personal data is involved.
