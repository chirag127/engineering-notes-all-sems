### Engineering

Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS

- Virtual reality (VR) is the use of computer modeling and simulation that enables a person to interact with an artificial three-dimensional (3D) visual or other sensory environment.
- VR applications immerse the user in a computer-generated environment that simulates reality through the use of interactive devices, such as headsets, gloves, controllers, etc.
- VR technology is used with special software and hardware that create and display the virtual environment and allow the user to manipulate and explore it.
- VR engineering includes the use of 3D modelling tools and visualisation techniques as part of the design process. This technology enables engineers to view their project in 3D and gain a greater understanding of how it works. Plus they can spot any flaws or potential risks before implementation .
- VR engineering has many benefits and use cases, such as:
  - Reducing costs and time by eliminating the need for physical prototypes and testing.
  - Enhancing collaboration and communication among engineers and stakeholders by allowing them to share and review the same virtual model.
  - Improving training and education by providing immersive and realistic scenarios for learning and practicing skills.
  - Increasing creativity and innovation by enabling engineers to experiment with different designs and solutions in a safe and flexible environment.
- Some examples of VR applications in engineering are:
  - In mechanical engineering, VR is used to model and design new hardware technologies, such as engines, turbines, robots, etc.
  - In civil engineering, VR is used to plan and construct buildings, bridges, roads, etc, and to simulate the effects of natural disasters, such as earthquakes, floods, etc.
  - In electrical engineering, VR is used to design and test circuits, systems, and devices, such as sensors, microchips, etc.
  - In biomedical engineering, VR is used to create and study virtual organs, tissues, and cells, and to develop and test new medical devices and treatments.
  - In aerospace engineering, VR is used to design and simulate aircraft, rockets, satellites, etc, and to train pilots and astronauts.
