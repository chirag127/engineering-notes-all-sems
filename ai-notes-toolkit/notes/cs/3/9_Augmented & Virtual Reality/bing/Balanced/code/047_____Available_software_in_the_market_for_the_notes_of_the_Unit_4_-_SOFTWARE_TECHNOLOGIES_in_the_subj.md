### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) are two technologies that enable users to experience immersive and interactive digital environments.
- AR overlays digital content on the real world, while VR creates a fully simulated environment that blocks out the real world.
- There are many software tools available in the market for designing and developing AR and VR applications. Some of the most popular ones are:

  - **Adobe Aero**: This is a software that allows users to create and share AR experiences without coding. Users can import 3D models, animations, and images, and use intuitive tools to place them in the real world. Users can also preview and test their AR scenes on their devices, and publish them to the web or social media. Adobe Aero is compatible with iOS and macOS devices, and integrates with other Adobe products such as Photoshop and Illustrator. 
  - **Tvori**: This is a software that enables users to create VR animations and stories using a VR headset and controllers. Users can use a library of assets, or import their own 3D models, sounds, and images, and manipulate them in a VR environment. Users can also record their movements and voice, and edit them in a timeline. Tvori supports Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, and Windows Mixed Reality headsets, and can export to various formats such as MP4, GIF, and FBX. 
  - **Masterpiece Studio Pro**: This is a software that helps users to create 3D models and animations for AR and VR applications. Users can use a VR headset and controllers to sculpt, paint, and rig their models, and use a desktop app to refine and optimize them. Users can also use a library of templates, presets, and assets, or import their own. Masterpiece Studio Pro supports Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, and Windows Mixed Reality headsets, and can export to various formats such as OBJ, FBX, and GLTF. 
  - **Vuforia Engine**: This is a software that enables users to develop AR applications for various platforms and devices. Users can use a suite of tools and features to create AR experiences that can recognize and track images, objects, text, and environments. Users can also use a cloud service to store and manage their AR content. Vuforia Engine supports iOS, Android, Windows, and Unity platforms, and integrates with various frameworks and libraries such as ARKit, ARCore, and OpenCV. 
  - **Unity 3D**: This is a software that allows users to create and run AR and VR applications for various platforms and devices. Users can use a powerful editor and a scripting language to design and code their applications, and use a library of assets, tools, and services to enhance them. Users can also use a platform-specific SDK to access native features and optimize performance. Unity 3D supports iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, and web platforms, and integrates with various frameworks and libraries such as ARKit, ARCore, Vuforia, and Oculus. 
  - **Oculus Medium**: This is a software that enables users to create 3D models and sculptures for VR applications using an Oculus headset and controllers. Users can use a variety of tools and brushes to shape and color their models, and use a library of stamps, layers, and presets to customize them. Users can also import and export their models to various formats such as OBJ, FBX, and GLTF, and share them with other users. Oculus Medium is compatible with Oculus Rift and Oculus Quest headsets. 

- These are some of the best software for AR and VR design in the market. However, there are many other software tools available, and users should choose the one that best suits their needs and preferences.
