### Scientific landmarks for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imaginary environments, using computer graphics, sound, haptics, and other sensory inputs.
- VR has been used for various applications, such as entertainment, education, training, therapy, art, and scientific visualization.
- Scientific visualization is the field of using VR to express complex ideas and scientific concepts, such as molecular models, statistical results, astronomical data, or medical images.
- Some of the scientific landmarks of VR are:

  - The first VR system was developed by Ivan Sutherland in 1968, called the Sword of Damocles. It was a head-mounted display (HMD) that projected stereoscopic images of simple wireframe models.
  - The first VR glove was invented by Thomas Zimmerman in 1982, which could sense the position and orientation of the hand and fingers, and provide tactile feedback.
  - The first VR cave was built by Carolina Cruz-Neira and colleagues in 1992, called the CAVE Automatic Virtual Environment. It was a room-sized VR system that projected images on the walls, floor, and ceiling, creating a 360-degree immersive experience.
  - The first VR web browser was created by Mark Pesce and Tony Parisi in 1994, called VRML (Virtual Reality Modeling Language). It was a standard for describing 3D scenes and objects on the internet, allowing users to navigate and interact with virtual worlds.
  - The first VR genome browser was developed by Erez Aiden and colleagues in 2016, called Juicebox VR. It visualized the connectivity of the human genome as a 3D landscape, allowing users to explore the structure and function of DNA.
  - The first VR simulation of the Milky Way was created by Marc Kuchner and Susan Higashio in 2020, using data from the Gaia mission. It animated the speed and direction of 4 million stars in the local galaxy, revealing new patterns and insights about the galactic dynamics.
