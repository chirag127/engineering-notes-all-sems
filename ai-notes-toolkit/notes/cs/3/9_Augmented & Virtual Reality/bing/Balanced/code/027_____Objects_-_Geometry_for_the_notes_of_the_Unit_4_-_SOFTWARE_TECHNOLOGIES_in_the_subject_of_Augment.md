### Objects - Geometry

- Objects are the basic building blocks of any 3D scene in augmented and virtual reality. They can be static or dynamic, visible or invisible, simple or complex.
- Geometry is the shape and structure of an object. It defines the vertices, edges, faces, and polygons that make up the object's surface.
- There are two main types of geometry: primitive and mesh.
  - Primitive geometry is composed of predefined shapes, such as cubes, spheres, cylinders, cones, etc. They are easy to create and manipulate, but have limited flexibility and detail.
  - Mesh geometry is composed of custom shapes, defined by a set of vertices and how they are connected. They can have any shape and level of detail, but are more complex and difficult to create and manipulate.
- Geometry can be modified by applying transformations, such as translation, rotation, scaling, and shearing. These operations change the position, orientation, size, and shape of the object.
- Geometry can also be modified by applying modifiers, such as subdivision, extrusion, bevel, boolean, etc. These operations change the topology and complexity of the object, adding or removing vertices, edges, faces, and polygons.
- Geometry can be imported from external sources, such as 3D modeling software, 3D scanning devices, or online repositories. These sources provide a variety of shapes and details, but may require optimization and conversion to be compatible with the target platform.
