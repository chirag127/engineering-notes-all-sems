### Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

- Taxonomy is a systematic way of classifying and organizing concepts or objects based on their similarities and differences.
- Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the perception of the real world by overlaying virtual elements on it.
- Mixed reality (MR) is a technology that combines the real and virtual worlds in a seamless way, allowing interaction and manipulation of both.
- A taxonomy of AR and MR applications can help to understand their characteristics, purposes, challenges and opportunities.
- Based on the literature review, a possible taxonomy of AR and MR applications can include the following facets and categories:

  - **Educational purpose**: the intended learning outcome or objective of the application, such as knowledge, comprehension, application, analysis, synthesis or evaluation .
  - **Domain**: the field or discipline that the application belongs to, such as science, engineering, arts, humanities, etc.
  - **Content**: the type of information or data that the application presents or manipulates, such as text, image, video, audio, 3D model, etc.
  - **Output technology**: the device or system that displays the AR or MR content, such as head-mounted display, handheld device, projection, etc.
  - **Input technology**: the device or system that captures the user's input or interaction, such as camera, sensor, gesture, voice, etc.
  - **Visual guidance**: the technique or method that helps the user to perceive and understand the AR or MR content, such as annotation, highlight, animation, etc .
  - **Environment**: the physical or virtual setting where the application takes place, such as indoor, outdoor, hybrid, etc.
  - **User**: the target audience or group of users that the application is designed for, such as children, adults, students, professionals, etc.
  - **Interaction**: the mode or style of interaction between the user and the AR or MR content, such as passive, active, collaborative, etc.

- An example of applying the taxonomy to an AR application for learning anatomy is:

  - **Educational purpose**: comprehension and application of anatomical knowledge.
  - **Domain**: science (biology, medicine).
  - **Content**: 3D models of human organs and systems.
  - **Output technology**: handheld device (smartphone, tablet).
  - **Input technology**: camera (marker-based tracking).
  - **Visual guidance**: annotation (labels, arrows, colors).
  - **Environment**: indoor (classroom, laboratory).
  - **User**: students (high school, college).
  - **Interaction**: active (exploration, manipulation).

- The taxonomy can be used to compare, evaluate and design AR and MR applications for different educational purposes and domains.
