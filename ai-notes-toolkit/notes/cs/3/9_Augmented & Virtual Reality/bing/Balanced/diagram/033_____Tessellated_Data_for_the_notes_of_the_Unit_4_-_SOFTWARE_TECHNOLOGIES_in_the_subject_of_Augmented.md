### Tessellated Data

- Tessellation is the dividing of datasets of polygons (sometimes called vertex sets) presenting objects in a scene into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation techniques are often used to manage datasets of polygons and divide them into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation can be of triangles, squares, diamonds, hexagons, H3 hexagons, or transverse hexagons.
- Tessellation can be used to create a dataset of appropriately sized bins for the full study area.
- Tessellation can improve the visual quality and performance of graphics rendering by adding more detail to the geometry of the objects.
- Tessellation can also be used to design interlocking motifs of patch shapes in quilts, textiles, and origami.
