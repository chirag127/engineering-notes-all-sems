### Education

- Virtual reality (VR) is the use of computer-generated environments and devices that simulate the physical presence and sensory experiences of a user in a different or imaginary world.
- VR can be used for various educational purposes, such as:
  - Virtual field trips: VR can allow students to visit places that are otherwise inaccessible, expensive, or dangerous, such as museums, historical sites, natural wonders, or outer space. VR can also provide immersive and interactive experiences that enhance learning outcomes and engagement. For example, Google Expeditions is a free app that offers over 900 VR tours for K-12 students.
  - Special education: VR can create new opportunities for students with special needs, such as autism, dyslexia, or physical disabilities, to safely explore the world and practice real-world skills in a no-risk environment. VR can also provide personalized and adaptive learning experiences that cater to the individual needs and preferences of each student. For example, Floreo is a VR app that helps children with autism develop social and communication skills.
  - Higher education: VR can enable students to learn complex concepts and skills that are difficult to teach or demonstrate in a traditional classroom setting, such as anatomy, engineering, or chemistry. VR can also provide realistic and immersive simulations that allow students to apply their knowledge and skills in various scenarios and contexts. For example, Microsoft HoloLens is a mixed reality device that allows medical students and clinicians to learn about the human body using 3D holograms.
  - Vocational training: VR can provide effective and efficient training for various professions and industries, such as healthcare, aviation, military, or construction. VR can also reduce the costs and risks associated with conventional training methods, such as equipment, materials, or safety hazards. For example, FundamentalVR is a VR platform that offers surgical training and assessment using haptic feedback and realistic graphics.

- VR can offer several benefits for education, such as:
  - Increased motivation and engagement: VR can capture the attention and interest of students by providing novel and exciting learning experiences that appeal to their senses and emotions. VR can also increase the sense of presence and immersion, which can enhance the retention and recall of information.
  - Enhanced collaboration and communication: VR can facilitate social interaction and cooperation among students and teachers, regardless of their physical location or time zone. VR can also foster a sense of community and belonging, which can improve the learning outcomes and satisfaction of students.
  - Improved accessibility and equity: VR can provide equal access and opportunity for education to students who face barriers or challenges, such as distance, disability, or poverty. VR can also promote diversity and inclusion, by exposing students to different cultures, perspectives, and experiences.

- VR can also pose some challenges and limitations for education, such as:
  - Technical issues and costs: VR requires specialized hardware and software, such as head-mounted displays, controllers, sensors, and applications, which can be expensive, complex, or unreliable. VR also depends on the availability and quality of internet connection, which can affect the performance and functionality of VR systems.
  - Ethical and social issues: VR can raise ethical and social concerns, such as privacy, security, safety, or morality. VR can also affect the psychological and physical well-being of users, such as causing cybersickness, addiction, or isolation. VR can also create ethical dilemmas, such as how to deal with virtual violence, harassment, or deception.
  - Pedagogical issues and challenges: VR requires careful design and implementation, such as aligning the VR content and activities with the learning objectives and outcomes, providing appropriate guidance and feedback, and assessing the learning progress and performance of students. VR also requires professional development and support for teachers, who need to acquire the skills and knowledge to use VR effectively and efficiently.
