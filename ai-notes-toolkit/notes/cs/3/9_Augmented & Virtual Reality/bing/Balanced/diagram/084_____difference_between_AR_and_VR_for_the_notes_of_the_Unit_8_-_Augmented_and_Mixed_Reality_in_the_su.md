### Difference between AR and VR

- AR stands for **augmented reality**, while VR stands for **virtual reality**.
- AR **enhances** the real-world environment by adding virtual elements to it, while VR **replaces** the current setting with a digital simulation .
- AR is only **25 percent virtual**, while VR is **75 percent virtual**.
- AR does not require a headset device, but VR does . AR can be experienced through smartphones, tablets, glasses, or projectors, while VR requires a head-mounted display (HMD) or a VR headset.
- AR users can **control** their presence in the real world, while VR users are **controlled** by the system. AR users are in contact with the real world, while VR users move in a completely fictional world.
- AR can be used for various purposes, such as education, entertainment, gaming, navigation, or marketing, while VR is mainly used for immersive gaming, simulation, and training.
