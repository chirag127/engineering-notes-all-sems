### Design Guidelines - 3D Travel Tasks

- 3D travel tasks are the tasks that involve moving the user's viewpoint or body in a 3D virtual environment.
- 3D travel tasks can be classified into different types based on the following characteristics :
  - The goal of the task: whether the user has a specific destination or direction to reach, or is exploring or searching the environment without a predefined goal.
  - The distance to be traveled: whether the user needs to move a short or long distance, or switch between different scales of the environment.
  - The amount of curvature or number of turns in the path: whether the user needs to follow a straight or curved path, or make frequent or infrequent turns.
  - The visibility of the target from the starting location: whether the user can see the destination or direction of travel from the beginning, or needs to discover it along the way.
  - The number of degrees of freedom (DOF) required for the movement: whether the user needs to move in one, two, or three dimensions, or rotate or tilt the viewpoint as well.
  - The required accuracy of the movement: whether the user needs to reach a precise location or orientation, or can tolerate some error or deviation.
  - The other primary tasks that take place during travel: whether the user needs to perform other tasks such as navigation, selection, manipulation, or communication while traveling, or can focus on the travel task only.
- The design of 3D travel techniques should consider the following guidelines :
  - The most common travel tasks should require a minimum amount of effort from the user.
  - Use high-speed transitional motions, not instant teleportation, if overall environment context is important.
  - Provide multiple travel techniques to support different travel tasks in the same application.
  - Make simple travel tasks easier by using target-based techniques for goal-oriented travel and steering techniques for exploration and search.
  - Use a physical locomotion technique if user exertion or naturalism is required.
