### User Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the process of navigating and orienting oneself in an unfamiliar environment.
- User centered wayfinding support is the design of wayfinding systems that consider the needs, preferences, and abilities of different users.
- User centered wayfinding support can improve the usability, accessibility, and satisfaction of users in complex environments such as hospitals, campuses, or virtual reality .
- User centered wayfinding support can involve various techniques, such as:
  - Providing clear and consistent signage and landmarks .
  - Using appropriate colors, symbols, and fonts .
  - Providing multiple modalities of information, such as visual, auditory, or haptic .
  - Providing feedback and confirmation of the user's location and direction .
  - Providing maps and overview displays .
  - Providing adaptive and personalized guidance .
- User centered wayfinding support can be evaluated using various methods, such as:
  - Observing and interviewing users in the environment .
  - Conducting usability tests and surveys .
  - Measuring objective and subjective indicators of wayfinding performance, such as time, errors, distance, confidence, and satisfaction .
  - Using eye tracking, physiological sensors, or virtual reality to measure attention, cognitive load, and emotional state .
