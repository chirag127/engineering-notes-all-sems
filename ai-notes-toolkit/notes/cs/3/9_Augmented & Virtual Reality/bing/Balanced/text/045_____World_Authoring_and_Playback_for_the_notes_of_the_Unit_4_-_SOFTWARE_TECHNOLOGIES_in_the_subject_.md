### World Authoring and Playback for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- World authoring and playback are the processes of creating and experiencing virtual and augmented reality content.
- Virtual reality (VR) is a simulated experience, where a scenario is replicated in a digital environment and where users are able to interact with that virtual world using computer-based objects.
- Augmented reality (AR) is a view of the real world in which computer-generated content is laid on top of the real world to augment the world with additional information or metadata such as sound, graphics, GPS data, and textual displays.
- World authoring and playback can be used for various purposes, such as education, entertainment, training, simulation, and visualization.
- World authoring and playback require different software technologies, such as authoring tools, rendering engines, interaction modules, and tracking systems.
- Authoring tools are software applications that help developers create and edit VR and AR content. They provide an intuitive user interface, hotspots, markers, interactives, and 360° media assets that help develop AR and VR.
- Rendering engines are software components that generate realistic graphics and sounds for VR and AR content. They use techniques such as ray tracing, shading, lighting, and animation to create immersive and interactive environments.
- Interaction modules are software components that enable users to interact with VR and AR content. They use events, gestures, voice commands, and haptic feedback to provide natural and intuitive user interfaces.
- Tracking systems are hardware and software components that track the position and orientation of users and objects in VR and AR content. They use sensors, cameras, GPS, and inertial measurement units to provide accurate and robust tracking.
