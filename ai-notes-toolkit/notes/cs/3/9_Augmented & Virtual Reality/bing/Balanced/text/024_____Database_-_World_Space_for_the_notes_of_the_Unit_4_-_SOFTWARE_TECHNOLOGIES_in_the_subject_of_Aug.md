### Database - World Space

- Database - World Space is a concept that refers to the use of databases to store and retrieve data that is relevant to a specific location or context in the real world, and to display it in an augmented reality (AR) environment.
- AR is a technology that overlays digital content and information onto the physical world, as if they are actually there with the user, in their own space.
- Database - World Space can enable various applications and benefits, such as:
  - Connecting data to the physical environment it is related to, such as pinning Power BI reports to real-world locations where the people who need to access those reports on location can find them .
  - Visualizing data in a more immersive and interactive way, such as viewing 3D models of buildings, landscapes, or planets in AR .
  - Assisting astronauts and engineers in inspecting and maintaining equipment in space, such as using AR to display instructions, diagrams, or videos on how to repair or operate scientific and exercise equipment.
- Database - World Space requires the following components and steps:
  - A database that stores the data that is relevant to a specific location or context in the real world, such as a SQL database, a cloud database, or a local database on the device.
  - A query that retrieves the data from the database, such as a SQL query, a REST API call, or a local file access.
  - A spatial anchor that attaches the data to a specific point in the real world, such as a QR code, a GPS coordinate, or a feature point.
  - An AR device that displays the data in the user's field of view, such as a smartphone, a tablet, or a headset.
  - An AR app that renders the data in a suitable format and style, such as a chart, a table, a 3D model, or a video.
