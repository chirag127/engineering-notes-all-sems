### Flight simulation

- Flight simulation is the use of virtual reality technology to create a realistic and immersive experience of flying an aircraft.
- Flight simulation can be used for various purposes, such as training, education, entertainment, research, and testing.
- Flight simulation can be classified into different levels of fidelity, depending on the degree of realism and complexity of the simulation.
- Flight simulation can involve different components, such as hardware, software, visual systems, sound systems, motion systems, and haptic systems.
- Flight simulation can provide various benefits, such as safety, cost-effectiveness, flexibility, repeatability, and feedback.
