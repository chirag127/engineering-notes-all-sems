### Tracking Devices for 3D User Interface Input Hardware

- Tracking devices are hardware that measure the position, orientation, and/or motion of a sensor or a user in 3D space.
- Tracking devices enable 3D interaction by providing input to the system based on the user's spatial actions.
- Tracking devices can be classified into different types based on the technology they use, such as magnetic, mechanical, acoustic, inertial, optical, or hybrid .
- Tracking devices can also be categorized based on the body part they track, such as head, eye, hand, or full body .
- Tracking devices can be integrated into different forms of input devices, such as 3D mice, data gloves, handheld devices, or wearable devices .
- Tracking devices can also use direct human input, such as gestures, facial expressions, or brain signals, to obtain data from the user's body.
- Tracking devices have various applications in 3D user interfaces, such as navigation, manipulation, selection, or system control .
- Tracking devices have various challenges and limitations, such as accuracy, latency, occlusion, interference, calibration, or cost .
