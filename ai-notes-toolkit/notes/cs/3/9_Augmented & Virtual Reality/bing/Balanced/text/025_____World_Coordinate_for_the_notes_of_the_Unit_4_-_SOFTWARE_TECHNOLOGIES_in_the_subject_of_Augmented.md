### World Coordinate

- World coordinate is a system of coordinates that defines the position and orientation of objects in a virtual or augmented reality scene.
- World coordinate is usually defined by three axes: x, y, and z, where x is the horizontal axis, y is the vertical axis, and z is the depth axis.
- World coordinate is also defined by a unit of measurement, such as meters, centimeters, or pixels, that determines the scale of the scene and the objects in it.
- World coordinate can be either right-handed or left-handed, depending on the direction of the z-axis. In a right-handed coordinate system, the z-axis points out of the screen, while in a left-handed coordinate system, the z-axis points into the screen.
- World coordinate can be transformed by applying translation, rotation, and scaling operations to the objects or the camera in the scene. These operations can change the position, orientation, and size of the objects or the view of the scene.
- World coordinate can be aligned with the real world coordinate system by using sensors, such as GPS, accelerometer, gyroscope, or camera, that provide information about the location, orientation, and movement of the device or the user. This can enable a more realistic and immersive experience of virtual or augmented reality.
