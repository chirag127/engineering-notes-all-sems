### Education

Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments. VR can be used for various educational purposes, such as:

- **Virtual field trips**: VR can enable students to visit places that are otherwise inaccessible, expensive, or dangerous, such as museums, historical sites, natural wonders, or even outer space. VR can also provide students with different perspectives and experiences, such as seeing the world from the eyes of an animal or a person from another culture. Virtual field trips can enhance students' motivation, engagement, and curiosity, as well as their spatial and cultural awareness .
- **Special education**: VR can create safe and controlled environments for students with special needs, such as autism, dyslexia, or physical disabilities. VR can help them practice real-world skills, such as social interactions, communication, or navigation, in a no-risk and personalized way. VR can also provide them with sensory stimulation, feedback, and reinforcement, as well as opportunities to explore and learn at their own pace .
- **Higher education**: VR can offer students and educators in higher education more realistic and immersive learning experiences, such as simulations, experiments, or scenarios, that are otherwise difficult, costly, or impossible to replicate in the real world. VR can also facilitate collaboration, communication, and creativity among students and educators, as well as provide them with feedback and assessment. VR can be used for various disciplines and domains, such as medicine, engineering, art, or psychology .
- **Vocational training**: VR can provide students and workers with hands-on and practical training for various skills and professions, such as welding, plumbing, firefighting, or surgery. VR can simulate real-life situations and challenges, such as emergencies, hazards, or complications, that require quick and accurate responses. VR can also reduce the risks, costs, and time involved in traditional training methods, as well as provide feedback and evaluation .
