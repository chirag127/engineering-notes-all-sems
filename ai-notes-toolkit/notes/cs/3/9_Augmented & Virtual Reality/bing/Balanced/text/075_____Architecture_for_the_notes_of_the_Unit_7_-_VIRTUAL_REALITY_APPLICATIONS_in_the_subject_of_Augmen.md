### Architecture for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments, using computer-generated graphics, sounds, and other sensory inputs.
- VR applications for architecture and construction can be used for various purposes, such as design, visualization, collaboration, education, and entertainment.
- VR applications for architecture and construction require a suitable hardware and software architecture, which consists of the following components:
  - VR devices: These are the devices that enable the user to experience VR, such as head-mounted displays (HMDs), controllers, trackers, gloves, haptic devices, etc. VR devices can be classified into three types: tethered, standalone, and mobile.
  - VR platforms: These are the software frameworks that provide the basic functionality and tools for developing and running VR applications, such as rendering, tracking, input, audio, networking, etc. VR platforms can be native, web-based, or hybrid.
  - VR content: This is the digital representation of the virtual environment and the objects within it, such as 3D models, textures, animations, sounds, etc. VR content can be created using various methods, such as modeling, scanning, photogrammetry, etc.
  - VR engines: These are the software applications that enable the creation and manipulation of VR content, such as Unity, Unreal Engine, SketchUp, etc. VR engines can be integrated with VR platforms and devices to provide a seamless VR experience.
  - VR services: These are the online platforms that provide various features and resources for VR applications, such as hosting, streaming, sharing, collaboration, analytics, etc. VR services can be cloud-based, peer-to-peer, or hybrid.
- VR applications for architecture and construction can benefit from the following advantages of VR technology:
  - Immersion: VR can create a sense of presence and realism for the user, allowing them to explore and interact with the virtual environment as if they were there.
  - Visualization: VR can enable the user to see the design and construction of a project in 3D, from different perspectives and scales, and with various effects and scenarios.
  - Collaboration: VR can facilitate the communication and cooperation among different stakeholders of a project, such as architects, engineers, clients, contractors, etc., by allowing them to share and review the VR content in real time or asynchronously.
  - Education: VR can provide a rich and engaging learning environment for the user, by allowing them to experience and experiment with various concepts and principles of architecture and construction.
  - Entertainment: VR can offer a fun and enjoyable experience for the user, by allowing them to play and create with the VR content, or to experience different stories and emotions.
