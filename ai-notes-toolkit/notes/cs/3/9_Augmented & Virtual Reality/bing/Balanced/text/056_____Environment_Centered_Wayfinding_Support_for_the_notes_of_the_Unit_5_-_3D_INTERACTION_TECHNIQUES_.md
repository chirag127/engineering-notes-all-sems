### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the cognitive process of defining a path through an environment, using and acquiring spatial knowledge, and being aided by natural and artificial cues .
- Wayfinding is important because it helps us better understand our environment, instead of being overwhelmed by a lot of small details.
- Environment centered wayfinding support is the design of a virtual environment that supports wayfinding by providing cues that are consistent with the real world, such as landmarks, signs, maps, etc.
- Environment centered wayfinding support can also be used to enhance wayfinding in the real world, by using a virtual environment to train or familiarize users with a real environment before they visit it.
- Some examples of environment centered wayfinding support are:
  - Using a virtual reality headset to explore a museum before going there in person.
  - Using a mobile app that shows a 3D map of a campus or a city, with directions and information about the places of interest.
  - Using a desktop application that simulates a flight simulator, with realistic terrain and landmarks, to practice navigation skills.
- Some challenges of environment centered wayfinding support are:
  - Maintaining a balance between realism and abstraction, to avoid confusion or distraction.
  - Providing enough cues without cluttering the virtual environment or obscuring the view.
  - Adapting the cues to the user's preferences, needs, and abilities.
  - Evaluating the effectiveness and usability of the wayfinding support.
