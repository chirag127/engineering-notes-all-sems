### Room / Stage / Area Descriptions

- A room, stage, or area is a virtual environment that simulates a physical space for the user to interact with in augmented or virtual reality.
- A room, stage, or area can have different properties, such as size, shape, layout, lighting, sound, objects, and events, that affect the user's experience and immersion.
- A room, stage, or area can be designed for different purposes, such as entertainment, education, training, socialization, or therapy, depending on the goals and needs of the user and the developer.
- A room, stage, or area can be created using different software tools, such as game engines, 3D modeling software, or specialized platforms, that allow the developer to design, edit, and test the virtual environment.
- A room, stage, or area can be accessed by the user using different hardware devices, such as head-mounted displays, controllers, trackers, cameras, or sensors, that enable the user to see, hear, and interact with the virtual environment.
- A room, stage, or area can be evaluated using different metrics, such as usability, functionality, aesthetics, engagement, presence, and satisfaction, that measure the quality and effectiveness of the virtual environment.
