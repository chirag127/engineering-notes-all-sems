### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) are two types of immersive technologies that create simulated or enhanced experiences for users.
- AR overlays digital content on the real world, such as images, videos, or information, while VR creates a fully immersive environment that blocks out the real world, such as a 3D simulation or a game.
- There are various software tools available in the market for designing and developing AR and VR applications, depending on the level of complexity, functionality, and platform compatibility required.
- Some of the most popular and widely used software tools for AR and VR design are:

  - **Adobe Aero**: This is a free and easy-to-use AR design software that allows users to create interactive and immersive AR experiences without coding. Users can import 3D models, images, and videos, and add animations, behaviors, and triggers to make them responsive to the environment and user input. Users can also preview and share their AR creations on iOS and Android devices. Adobe Aero is the best AR design software overall and the editor's choice.
  - **Tvori**: This is a low-cost and user-friendly VR design software that enables users to create and animate 3D scenes and characters in VR using Oculus or HTC Vive headsets. Users can use intuitive tools and gestures to manipulate objects, add sounds and effects, and record animations. Users can also export their VR creations to other platforms or software, such as Unity, Unreal Engine, or Sketchfab. Tvori is the best budget AR design software.
  - **Masterpiece Studio Pro**: This is a comprehensive and powerful VR design software that allows users to create and edit high-quality 3D models and animations in VR using Oculus or HTC Vive headsets. Users can use advanced tools and features, such as sculpting, painting, rigging, posing, and retopology, to create realistic and detailed 3D assets. Users can also export their VR creations to other platforms or software, such as Unity, Unreal Engine, or Blender. Masterpiece Studio Pro is the best value VR design software.
  - **Vuforia Engine**: This is a robust and versatile AR design software that enables users to create and deploy cross-platform AR applications for various devices, such as smartphones, tablets, headsets, or smart glasses. Users can use a variety of features and functionalities, such as image recognition, object tracking, spatial mapping, occlusion, and cloud services, to create engaging and interactive AR experiences. Users can also integrate Vuforia Engine with other platforms or software, such as Unity, Unreal Engine, or Android Studio. Vuforia Engine is the best AR design software for advanced users.
  - **Unity 3D**: This is a free and popular software for AR and VR design that allows users to create and develop 2D and 3D games and applications for various platforms, such as Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, or web. Users can use a range of tools and features, such as scripting, physics, animation, audio, and graphics, to create immersive and realistic experiences. Users can also use various plugins and extensions, such as AR Foundation, ARCore, or ARKit, to add AR and VR capabilities to their projects. Unity 3D is the best free software for AR and VR design.
  - **Oculus Medium**: This is a free and fun 3D design platform for Oculus headsets that allows users to sculpt and paint 3D models in VR using intuitive tools and gestures. Users can use various brushes, shapes, colors, and layers to create artistic and expressive 3D assets. Users can also export their VR creations to other platforms or software, such as Unity, Unreal Engine, or Sketchfab. Oculus Medium is the best 3D design platform for Oculus headsets.
