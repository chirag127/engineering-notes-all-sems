### Tessellated Data

- Tessellated data is the result of dividing datasets of polygons (sometimes called vertex sets) presenting objects in a scene into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation techniques are often used to manage datasets of polygons and divide them into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation can improve the visual quality and performance of graphics rendering by creating smoother and more detailed surfaces from coarse models.
- Tessellation can also be used to create spatial bins or cells for analyzing point-based data or raster data .
- Tessellation can be of different shapes, such as triangles, squares, diamonds, hexagons, or transverse hexagons.
- Tessellation has applications in various fields, such as computer graphics, cartography, textiles, quilting, origami, and art  .
