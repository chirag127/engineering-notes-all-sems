## Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- Virtual reality uses software and hardware to immerse a user, and potentially other viewers, in a digital environment. They get to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.
- Virtual reality applications can be classified into different categories, such as entertainment, education, business, health care, military, and social  .
- Some examples of virtual reality applications are:
  - Google Expeditions: a free app that allows users to go on virtual field trips to various places, such as museums, landmarks, and natural wonders.
  - Colosse: a free app that showcases a stunning animated story in VR, where the user can influence the outcome by looking at different characters.
  - Titans of Space Plus: a paid app that takes users on a guided tour of the solar system and beyond, with realistic 3D models and facts.
  - Google Earth VR: a free app that lets users fly over and explore any place on Earth in VR, with high-resolution imagery and 3D terrain.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR: a paid app that allows users to create graffiti art on realistic walls, with various spray cans, colors, and effects.
  - Littlstar: a free app that streams 360-degree videos and VR content from various sources, such as National Geographic, Discovery, and Red Bull.
  - Ocean Rift: a paid app that simulates an underwater world, where users can swim with various marine animals, such as sharks, dolphins, and turtles.
  - The Foo Show: a free app that features interviews with game developers and celebrities in VR, where users can join the conversation and explore the virtual sets.
  - Tilt Brush: a paid app that lets users paint in 3D space, with various brushes, colors, and effects, and share their creations online.
  - Virtual Desktop: a paid app that allows users to use their PC in VR, with multiple monitors, custom backgrounds, and wireless streaming.
  - Within: a free app that offers a variety of VR experiences, such as documentaries, animations, music videos, and interactive stories.
  - Fulldive VR: a free app that integrates various VR features, such as a browser, a camera, a gallery, a social network, and a market.
- Virtual reality applications have various benefits, such as enhancing learning outcomes, providing immersive entertainment, improving communication and collaboration, and offering new possibilities for creativity and innovation .
- Virtual reality applications also have some challenges, such as technical limitations, ethical issues, health risks, and social implications  .
