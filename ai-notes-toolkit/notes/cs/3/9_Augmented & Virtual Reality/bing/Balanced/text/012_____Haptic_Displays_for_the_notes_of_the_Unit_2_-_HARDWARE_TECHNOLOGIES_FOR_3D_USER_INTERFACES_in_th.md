### Haptic Displays

- Haptic displays are interfaces that provide haptic feedback, which is the sensation of touch, to the user .
- Haptic feedback can be divided into two types: cutaneous feedback, which is the stimulation of the skin receptors, and kinesthetic or proprioceptive feedback, which is the stimulation of the muscles and joints .
- Haptic displays can be used to create virtual objects, control virtual objects, enhance remote control of machines and devices, and provide tactile and force feedback in various applications.
- Haptic displays can be categorized by the type of stimuli or output they generate, such as force, vibration, motion, temperature, or electric current  .
- Haptic displays can also be categorized by the body part they target, such as the hand, the arm, the foot, the face, or the whole body.
- Haptic displays can range from small units that sit on the desktop or are worn on the hand to large robotic devices that can lift a person off the ground.
- Haptic displays can be based on different technologies, such as pneumatic, hydraulic, electric, magnetic, or piezoelectric actuators.
- Haptic displays can be designed to provide different levels of fidelity, realism, and immersion, depending on the application and the user's needs.
