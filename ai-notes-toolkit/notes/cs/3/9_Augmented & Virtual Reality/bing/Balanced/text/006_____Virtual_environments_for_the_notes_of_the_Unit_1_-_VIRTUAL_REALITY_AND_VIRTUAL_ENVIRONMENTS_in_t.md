### Virtual environments

- A virtual environment is a networked application that allows a user to interact with both the computing environment and the work of other users .
- Virtual environments can be used for various purposes, such as communication, collaboration, education, entertainment, simulation, and training.
- Virtual environments can be classified into different types based on the degree of immersion, realism, interactivity, and social presence they provide.
- Some examples of virtual environments are email, chat, web-based document sharing, virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality, and 3D virtual worlds  .
- Virtual environments can have various benefits and challenges for users, such as enhancing engagement, motivation, creativity, and learning outcomes, as well as causing cyber-sickness, privacy issues, ethical dilemmas, and technical difficulties.
