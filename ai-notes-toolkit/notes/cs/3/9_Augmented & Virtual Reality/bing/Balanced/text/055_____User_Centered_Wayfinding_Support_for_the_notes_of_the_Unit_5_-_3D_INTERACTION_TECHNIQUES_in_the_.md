### User Centered Wayfinding Support

- Wayfinding is the process of navigating from one location to another in an unfamiliar environment.
- User centered wayfinding support is the design of wayfinding systems that consider the needs, preferences, and abilities of different users.
- User centered wayfinding support can improve the usability, accessibility, and satisfaction of users in complex environments such as hospitals, campuses, or virtual reality .
- User centered wayfinding support can involve various techniques, such as:
  - Providing clear and consistent signage, landmarks, and maps .
  - Adjusting the field of view and presence in virtual reality to enhance spatial knowledge and orientation.
  - Using multimodal feedback, such as auditory, haptic, or visual cues, to guide users.
  - Incorporating user feedback and testing to evaluate and improve the wayfinding system .
- User centered wayfinding support can benefit from a multidisciplinary approach that involves user research, design, evaluation, and implementation .
