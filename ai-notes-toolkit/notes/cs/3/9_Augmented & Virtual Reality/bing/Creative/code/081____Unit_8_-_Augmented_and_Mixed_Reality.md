## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) and mixed reality (MR) are both immersive technologies that enhance the perception of reality by adding or manipulating digital elements.
- AR overlays virtual objects on the real-world environment, such as displaying information, graphics, or animations on a smartphone screen or a head-mounted display (HMD)  .
- MR not only overlays but also anchors virtual objects to the real world, allowing them to interact with the physical environment and the user, such as placing a hologram on a table or a wall and manipulating it with gestures or voice commands  .
- AR and MR differ from virtual reality (VR), which immerses users in a fully artificial digital environment, such as a 3D simulation or a video game, that blocks out the real world .
- AR and MR have various applications in different domains, such as education, entertainment, health care, tourism, manufacturing, and retail   .
- AR and MR devices include smartphones, tablets, HMDs, smart glasses, and projectors. Some examples of AR and MR devices are Google Glass, Microsoft HoloLens, Magic Leap One, and Meta 2   .
- AR and MR technologies rely on several components and techniques, such as sensors, cameras, displays, processors, computer vision, spatial mapping, tracking, rendering, and user interfaces   .
- AR and MR are still evolving and face some challenges, such as technical limitations, user acceptance, privacy, security, and ethical issues   .
