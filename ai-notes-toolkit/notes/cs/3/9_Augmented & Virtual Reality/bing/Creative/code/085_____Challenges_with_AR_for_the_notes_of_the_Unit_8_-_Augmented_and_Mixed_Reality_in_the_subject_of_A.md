### Challenges with AR

Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the real world with digital information and media, such as 3D models, images, videos, sounds, and texts. AR has many potential applications in various domains, such as education, entertainment, health, tourism, and industry. However, AR also faces some challenges that hinder its widespread adoption and use. Some of these challenges are:

- **Hardware quality**: Successful adoption of AR depends on the experience that you provide to the end user. Hardware quality matters, especially when you think about mobile AR solutions. The devices that support AR, such as smartphones, tablets, glasses, and headsets, need to have high performance, battery life, display resolution, camera quality, and sensors to enable accurate and immersive AR experiences . Hardware issues, such as devices malfunctioning, overheating, or having poor connectivity, may affect the functionality and usability of AR applications.
- **Content availability**: Content is definitely one of the biggest challenges in AR adoption. Without quality content, no hardware can make AR a success. Organizations that are trying to include AR in their internal work functions or external products and services need to think about 3D content availability for that technology. Creating, managing, and updating 3D content for AR is not easy, as it requires specialized skills, tools, and resources . Moreover, content needs to be relevant, engaging, and interactive to meet the needs and expectations of the users.
- **Awareness**: One of the most difficult challenges about AR is educating the broader market. Consumers are not exposed to AR regularly and do not see its wide-reaching applications in their daily lives. However, there are plenty of AR experiences available today that just lack exposure in the consumer market. For example, AR can be used to enhance shopping, gaming, learning, traveling, and socializing experiences, among others. Therefore, there is a need to raise awareness and educate the public about the benefits and possibilities of AR, as well as how to access and use it.
- **User experience**: User experience is another key factor that influences the adoption and use of AR. User experience refers to how users perceive and interact with AR applications, and how satisfied and engaged they are with them. User experience depends on many aspects, such as the design, functionality, usability, accessibility, and feedback of AR applications, as well as the context, environment, and preferences of the users. User experience can be affected by technical issues, such as latency, glitches, or errors, as well as human factors, such as comfort, privacy, or safety . Therefore, there is a need to design and test AR applications with the user in mind, and to ensure that they provide value, convenience, and enjoyment to the users.
- **Ethical and social issues**: AR also poses some ethical and social issues that need to be addressed and resolved. Ethical and social issues refer to the moral and societal implications of AR, such as how it affects the rights, responsibilities, and behaviors of the users and the stakeholders involved. Some of these issues include:

  - Privacy and security: AR applications may collect, store, and share personal and sensitive data of the users, such as their location, identity, preferences, and activities. This may raise concerns about the privacy and security of the users, as well as the potential misuse or abuse of their data by third parties, such as hackers, advertisers, or governments .
  - Trust and credibility: AR applications may alter or manipulate the perception and understanding of the users about the reality, such as by adding, removing, or changing information and media. This may affect the trust and credibility of the users, as well as the sources and providers of the information and media. For example, AR may be used to create fake or misleading news, propaganda, or advertisements, or to deceive or influence the users in various ways .
  - Social and cultural norms: AR applications may affect the social and cultural norms of the users, such as how they communicate, interact, and behave with others, as well as how they respect and value the diversity and differences of others. For example, AR may be used to create or enhance social and cultural experiences, such as by enabling virtual presence, collaboration, or empathy, or to harm or offend others, such as by creating or displaying inappropriate, offensive, or discriminatory content .
