### Tessellated Data

- Tessellation is the dividing of datasets of polygons (sometimes called vertex sets) presenting objects in a scene into suitable structures for rendering.
- Tessellation is especially important for real-time rendering, where data is tessellated into triangles, for example in OpenGL 4.0 and Direct3D 11.
- Tessellation can improve the visual quality and performance of 3D graphics by reducing the polygon count and avoiding cracks or gaps between adjacent polygons.
- Tessellation can be applied to different types of data, such as CAD models, point clouds, images, or geographic features  .
- Tessellation can be of different shapes, such as triangles, squares, diamonds, hexagons, or transverse hexagons.
- Tessellation can be controlled by various parameters, such as the size, angle, deviation, or quality of the tessellated polygons .
- Tessellation can be done on a global or a per-part level, depending on the needs and complexity of the data.
- Tessellation can also be used for artistic purposes, such as creating patterns, motifs, or origami designs.
