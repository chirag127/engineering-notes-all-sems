### Architecture for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments, using computer-generated graphics, sounds, and other sensory inputs.
- VR applications for architecture are software tools that allow architects to design, visualize, and communicate their projects in 3D, using VR headsets, controllers, and other devices.
- VR applications for architecture can have various benefits, such as:
  - Enhancing creativity and innovation by enabling architects to explore different design options and scenarios in a realistic and immersive way.
  - Improving collaboration and communication by allowing architects to share their designs and feedback with clients, colleagues, and stakeholders in VR, regardless of their physical location.
  - Increasing efficiency and accuracy by reducing the need for physical models, drawings, and mock-ups, and by allowing architects to detect and resolve design issues and errors in VR.
  - Improving customer satisfaction and engagement by providing clients with a better understanding and appreciation of the design intent, functionality, and aesthetics of the project in VR.
- Some examples of VR applications for architecture are:
  - ARki: A mixed reality application that allows architects to superimpose their 3D models on the real world, using a smartphone or tablet camera. ARki can be used to visualize and present architectural projects in their context, and to interact with them using gestures and voice commands.
  - Storyboard VR: A free prototyping and visualization tool that allows architects to create and edit VR scenes using a drag-and-drop interface. Storyboard VR can be used to test and refine design concepts, layouts, and interactions in VR, and to export them to other VR platforms.
  - Pair: A VR application that allows architects to create and customize 3D models of furniture, fixtures, and appliances, and to place them in a virtual room. Pair can be used to design and furnish interior spaces, and to collaborate with clients and suppliers in VR.
  - SmartReality: A VR application that allows architects to view and interact with their 3D models in VR, using a smartphone and a VR headset. SmartReality can be used to explore and evaluate architectural projects in different scales, perspectives, and lighting conditions.
  - Fuzor: A VR application that allows architects to import and synchronize their 3D models from various CAD and BIM software, and to visualize and simulate them in VR. Fuzor can be used to perform various analyses, such as structural, thermal, acoustic, and lighting, and to collaborate with other users in VR.
