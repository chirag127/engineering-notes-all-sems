# World Environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) is an enhanced, interactive version of a real-world environment achieved through digital visual elements, sounds, and other sensory stimuli via holographic technology.
- Virtual reality (VR) is an immersive experience that isolates users from the real world, usually via a headset device and headphones designed for such activities.
- Mixed reality (MR) is a combination of AR and VR that allows users to interact with both real and virtual objects in a shared environment.
- The world environment refers to the physical, social, cultural, and economic factors that affect the development and use of AR and VR technologies.
- Some of the factors that influence the world environment for AR and VR are:
  - The availability and accessibility of internet connectivity and devices that support AR and VR applications.
  - The demand and supply of AR and VR content and services for various domains and purposes, such as education, entertainment, healthcare, tourism, gaming, etc .
  - The ethical, legal, and social implications of AR and VR technologies, such as privacy, security, intellectual property, user safety, etc.
  - The environmental impact of AR and VR technologies, such as energy consumption, e-waste, carbon footprint, etc.
  - The innovation and competition in the AR and VR industry, such as new hardware, software, platforms, standards, etc .
- The world environment for AR and VR is constantly changing and evolving, creating new opportunities and challenges for the developers and users of these technologies.
