### AR systems and functionality

- AR systems are systems that combine real and virtual worlds, real-time interaction, and accurate 3D registration of virtual and real objects .
- AR systems can be classified into three types based on the display technology: head-mounted displays (HMDs), handheld displays, and spatial displays.
- AR systems can also be classified into three types based on the tracking technology: marker-based, markerless, and hybrid.
- AR systems can provide various functionalities for different applications, such as:
  - Education and training: AR systems can enhance learning outcomes by providing immersive and interactive experiences, such as simulations, games, and guides.
  - Entertainment and gaming: AR systems can create engaging and fun experiences, such as augmented reality games, movies, and art.
  - Design and planning: AR systems can facilitate collaborative and creative work, such as augmented reality maps, buildings, and data feeds.
  - Healthcare and medicine: AR systems can improve diagnosis and treatment, such as augmented reality surgery, rehabilitation, and telemedicine.
  - Marketing and commerce: AR systems can increase customer satisfaction and loyalty, such as augmented reality catalogs, try-on, and reviews.
