### Education

Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments, using computer-generated graphics, sounds, and other sensory inputs. VR can be experienced through head-mounted displays (HMDs) that cover the user's eyes and ears, or through projection systems that create a 3D image on a screen or a wall.

VR has many applications in education, such as:

- **Virtual field trips**: VR can transport students to different places and times, such as ancient Rome, the Amazon rainforest, or the surface of Mars, without leaving the classroom. This can enhance their learning experience and engagement, as well as expose them to diverse cultures and perspectives .
- **Special education**: VR can provide a safe and controlled environment for students with special needs, such as autism, dyslexia, or physical disabilities, to practice real-world skills and explore their interests. VR can also help them overcome their fears and anxieties, such as public speaking, social interactions, or phobias .
- **Higher education**: VR can offer students and educators more opportunities to access and create immersive content, such as simulations, experiments, games, and art. VR can also facilitate collaboration and communication among students and teachers across different locations and disciplines .
- **Vocational training**: VR can provide realistic and hands-on training for various professions and skills, such as medicine, engineering, aviation, military, and sports. VR can also reduce the costs and risks associated with traditional training methods, such as equipment, materials, and safety .

Some examples of free VR apps for education are:

- **Google Expeditions**: A VR app that allows teachers and students to explore over 900 virtual field trips, such as museums, landmarks, natural wonders, and historical sites.
- **Titans of Space**: A VR app that takes students on a tour of the solar system and beyond, with detailed information and visuals of planets, stars, and other celestial objects.
- **Discovery VR**: A VR app that features immersive videos and stories from Discovery Channel, such as wildlife, nature, adventure, and science.
- **InMind VR**: A VR app that lets students enter the human brain and learn about its functions and disorders, such as depression, anxiety, and schizophrenia.
- **The Body VR**: A VR app that allows students to travel inside the human body and learn about its anatomy and physiology, such as the circulatory, respiratory, and digestive systems.
