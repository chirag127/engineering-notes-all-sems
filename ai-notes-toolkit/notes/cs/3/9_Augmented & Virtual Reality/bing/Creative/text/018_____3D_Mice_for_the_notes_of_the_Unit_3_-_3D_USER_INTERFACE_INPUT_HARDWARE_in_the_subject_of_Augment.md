### 3D Mice

- A 3D mouse is a pointing and control device specialized for movement in virtual 3D (three-dimensional) environments.
- A 3D mouse provides this functionality through a variety of methods including accelerometers, multi-axis sensors, and infrared sensors.
- A 3D mouse is used in tandem with a traditional mouse, allowing individuals to employ a two-handed approach to their work.
- A 3D mouse is preferred by designers in many industries because it greatly improves user-comfort and productivity.
- A 3D mouse offers better ways of navigating around objects, letting users jump seamlessly between views or rotate around to make precise alterations.
- A 3D mouse can have different shapes and sizes, depending on the intended use and ergonomic design.
- A 3D mouse can be wireless or wired, depending on the connectivity and battery life.
- A 3D mouse can have different buttons and features, depending on the software compatibility and customization options.
