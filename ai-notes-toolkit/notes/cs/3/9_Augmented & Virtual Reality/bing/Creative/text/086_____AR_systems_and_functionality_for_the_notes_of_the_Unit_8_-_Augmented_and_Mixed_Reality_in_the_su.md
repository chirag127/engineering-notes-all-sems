### AR systems and functionality

- AR systems are systems that combine real and virtual worlds, real-time interaction, and accurate 3D registration of virtual and real objects.
- AR systems can be classified into three types based on the display technology: head-mounted displays (HMDs), handheld displays, and spatial displays.
- AR systems can also be classified into four types based on the tracking technology: marker-based, markerless, image-based, and sensor-based.
- AR systems can provide various functionalities for different applications, such as:
  - Education and training: AR systems can enhance learning outcomes by providing immersive and interactive experiences, such as augmented reality books, simulations, and games.
  - Entertainment and gaming: AR systems can create engaging and fun experiences, such as augmented reality filters, animations, and characters.
  - Healthcare and medicine: AR systems can improve diagnosis and treatment, such as augmented reality glasses, surgery, and rehabilitation.
  - Industry and engineering: AR systems can improve productivity and quality, such as augmented reality manuals, guides, and inspections.
  - Architecture and design: AR systems can facilitate collaboration and visualization, such as augmented reality maps, models, and data feeds.
