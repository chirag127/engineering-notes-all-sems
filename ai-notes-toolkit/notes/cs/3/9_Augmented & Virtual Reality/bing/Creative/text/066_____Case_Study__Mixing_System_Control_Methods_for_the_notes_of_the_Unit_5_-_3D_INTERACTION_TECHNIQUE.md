### Case Study: Mixing System Control Methods for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- The case study is based on the multiscale simulation of mixing processes using 3D-parallel, fluid-structure interaction techniques.
- The simulation involves the representation of rigid moving boundaries, such as screws and rotors, as well as free surfaces, using a simplified fictitious domain method coupled with a level-set approach.
- The simulation also requires the use of 3D interaction techniques for common tasks such as selection, manipulation, navigation, and system control .
- The 3D interaction techniques are designed and evaluated according to the domain-specific criteria of usefulness, usability, and user satisfaction.
- The 3D interaction techniques include:
  - A 2D mouse-based technique for selecting and manipulating 3D objects in the simulation, using a virtual trackball metaphor.
  - A 3D gesture-based technique for navigating and controlling the simulation, using a handheld device that tracks the user's position and orientation in physical space.
  - A multimodal technique for providing feedback and guidance to the user, using visual, auditory, and haptic cues .
- The case study demonstrates the advantages and challenges of applying 3D interaction techniques to a complex and realistic domain, such as mixing processes of complex fluids.
