### Voice Commands for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Voice commands are a type of system control technique that allow users to interact with 3D environments using speech input  .
- Voice commands are useful for actions that require a single activation, such as selecting an object, triggering an event, or switching modes.
- Voice commands are also suitable for hands-free and natural interaction, especially when the user's hands are occupied or the input device is not convenient .
- Voice commands can be combined with other modalities, such as gestures, to create multimodal interfaces that enhance the user experience and reduce ambiguity .
- Voice commands have some challenges, such as:
  - Users may need to learn the specific phrases that the system recognizes .
  - Speech recognition may be affected by noise, accents, or dialects .
  - Speech input may not be appropriate for some contexts, such as public or quiet environments .
  - Speech input may not provide enough feedback or confirmation for the user .
- Some examples of voice commands for 3D interaction techniques are :
  - "Select" or "Deselect" to manipulate objects in the scene.
  - "Zoom in" or "Zoom out" to change the view of the scene.
  - "Rotate" or "Translate" to move the camera or the objects in the scene.
  - "Menu" or "Options" to access the system settings or functions.
  - "Help" or "Tutorial" to get guidance or instructions from the system.
  - "Quit" or "Exit" to end the application or the session.
