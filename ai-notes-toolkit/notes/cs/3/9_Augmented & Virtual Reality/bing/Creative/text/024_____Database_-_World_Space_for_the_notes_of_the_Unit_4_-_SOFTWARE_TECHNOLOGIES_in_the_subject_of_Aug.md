### Database - World Space

- Database is a collection of organized and structured data that can be accessed, manipulated, and updated by software applications.
- World space is the coordinate system that defines the position and orientation of objects in a three-dimensional environment, such as augmented reality (AR) or virtual reality (VR).
- Database - world space is the concept of using database to store, retrieve, and display data in relation to the real-world locations and objects that are relevant to the data.
- Database - world space can enable various applications and benefits, such as:
  - Enhancing the user experience and interaction with data by providing contextual and immersive visualization.
  - Improving the efficiency and accuracy of data analysis and decision making by connecting data to the physical environment it is related to.
  - Supporting remote collaboration and communication by sharing data and annotations in a common virtual space.
  - Providing new opportunities for education, entertainment, and exploration by creating engaging and interactive data stories.
- Database - world space can be implemented by using various technologies and tools, such as:
  - AR and VR devices, such as smartphones, tablets, headsets, glasses, or projectors, that can capture the real-world environment and overlay digital content and information on it.
  - Spatial anchors, which are virtual markers that can be placed and recognized in the real world, and can be used to attach data to specific locations or objects.
  - Database management systems, such as SQL, NoSQL, or cloud-based databases, that can store and query data efficiently and securely.
  - Data visualization frameworks, such as Power BI, Tableau, or D3.js, that can create and display interactive charts, graphs, maps, or other visual representations of data.
  - Data integration and transformation tools, such as FME, that can connect and convert data from various sources and formats, and prepare it for visualization and analysis.
