### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) are two technologies that create immersive and interactive experiences for users.
- AR overlays digital information or objects on the real world, while VR creates a simulated environment that replaces the real world.
- Both AR and VR require software tools to design, develop, and deploy applications and content for different platforms and devices.
- Some of the available software in the market for AR and VR are:

It supports importing 3D models, animations, and assets from other Adobe tools, such as Photoshop and Illustrator. It also offers a library of ready-made assets and templates. Users can preview and test their AR scenes on their devices and publish them to the web or social media.
  - **Tvori**: The best budget AR design software. It is a VR-based tool that lets users create and animate 3D scenes and characters in VR. Users can export their VR scenes as videos, images, or AR experiences.
