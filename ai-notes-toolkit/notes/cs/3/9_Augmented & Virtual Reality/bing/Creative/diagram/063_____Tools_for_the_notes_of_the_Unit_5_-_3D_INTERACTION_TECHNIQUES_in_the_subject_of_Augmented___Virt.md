### Tools for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- 3D interaction techniques are methods of interacting with 3D virtual objects and environments using various input devices and modalities.
- 3D interaction techniques can be classified into four categories: selection, manipulation, navigation, and system control.
- Selection techniques are methods of specifying one or more objects of interest in the 3D scene, such as ray-casting, cone-casting, or image-based selection .
- Manipulation techniques are methods of changing the position, orientation, size, or shape of the selected objects, such as direct manipulation, handle-based manipulation, or gesture-based manipulation .
- Navigation techniques are methods of changing the viewpoint or location of the user in the 3D scene, such as walking, flying, steering, or teleporting .
- System control techniques are methods of accessing menus, commands, or options in the 3D interface, such as voice commands, gaze-based menus, or virtual buttons .
- 3D interaction techniques can be designed and evaluated based on various criteria, such as task performance, usability, user satisfaction, presence, immersion, and naturalness .
- 3D interaction techniques can be applied to various domains and applications of augmented and virtual reality, such as education, training, gaming, visualization, and design .
