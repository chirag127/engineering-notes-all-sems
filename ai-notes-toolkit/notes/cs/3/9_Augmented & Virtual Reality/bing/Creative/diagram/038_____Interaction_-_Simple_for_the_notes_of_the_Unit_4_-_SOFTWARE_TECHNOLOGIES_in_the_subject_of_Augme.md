### Interaction - Simple

- Interaction is the process of communicating and exchanging information between a user and a system, such as augmented reality (AR) or virtual reality (VR).
- Interaction can involve different modalities, such as visual, auditory, haptic, somatosensory and olfactory, depending on the system and the user's preferences.
- Interaction can also involve different levels of immersion, which is the degree to which the user feels present and engaged in the system's environment.
- In AR, interaction is typically achieved by overlaying computer-generated content on the user's view of the real world, using devices such as smartphones, tablets, glasses or headsets  .
- In VR, interaction is typically achieved by placing the user in a fully computer-generated world, using devices such as headsets, controllers, gloves or treadmills .
- Interaction in AR and VR can be classified into three types: input, output and feedback .
  - Input is the way the user provides information or commands to the system, such as gestures, voice, touch or eye gaze.
  - Output is the way the system provides information or responses to the user, such as graphics, sound, text or animations.
  - Feedback is the way the system confirms or acknowledges the user's input, such as visual cues, sounds, vibrations or haptic sensations.
- Interaction in AR and VR can be designed to achieve different goals, such as enhancing the user's perception, understanding, learning, enjoyment, collaboration or productivity .
- Interaction in AR and VR can also face different challenges, such as technical limitations, usability issues, user preferences, ethical concerns or social implications .
