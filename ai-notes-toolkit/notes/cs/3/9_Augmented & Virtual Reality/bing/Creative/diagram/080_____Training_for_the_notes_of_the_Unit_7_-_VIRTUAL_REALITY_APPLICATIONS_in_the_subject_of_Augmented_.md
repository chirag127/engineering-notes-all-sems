### Training for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- Virtual reality uses software and hardware to immerse a user, and potentially other viewers, in a digital environment. They get to explore and interact with virtual surroundings, and in some cases, also their physical environment.
- Virtual reality applications can be classified into different categories based on their purpose, such as:
  - Entertainment: VR applications that provide fun and engaging experiences for users, such as video games, movies, concerts, etc. Examples: Allumette, Colosse, Kingspray Graffiti VR, etc.
  - Education: VR applications that facilitate learning and training in various domains, such as medical, military, engineering, etc. Examples: Google Expeditions, Titans of Space Plus, Ocean Rift, etc.
  - Business: VR applications that enhance productivity and collaboration in various sectors, such as manufacturing, retail, tourism, etc. Examples: Virtual Desktop, Littlstar, The Foo Show, etc.
  - Social: VR applications that enable social interaction and communication among users in virtual spaces, such as chat rooms, avatars, etc. Examples: Within, Fulldive VR, Tilt Brush, etc.
- Virtual reality applications require interactive devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits, that send and receive information and enable the user to perceive and manipulate the virtual environment.
- Virtual reality applications can create various types of sensory feedback, such as visual, auditory, haptic, olfactory, etc, depending on the level of immersion and realism of the simulation.
- Virtual reality applications can have various benefits and challenges, such as:
  - Benefits: VR applications can provide immersive and realistic experiences that can enhance learning, entertainment, creativity, empathy, etc. They can also enable users to access and explore environments that are otherwise inaccessible, dangerous, or expensive .
  - Challenges: VR applications can also pose technical, ethical, and social issues, such as high cost, low quality, motion sickness, privacy, addiction, isolation, etc. They can also create ethical dilemmas and moral conflicts for users who may act differently in virtual reality than in real life .
