## Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- VR applications require software and hardware to create and display the simulated environment, and to enable the user to interact with it through various devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits.
- VR applications can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, business, health care, tourism, art, and social interaction  .
- Some examples of VR applications are:
  - Google Expeditions: A VR app that allows users to explore different places and cultures around the world, such as the Great Barrier Reef, the Taj Mahal, or the Amazon rainforest.
  - Allumette: A VR app that tells a story of love, sacrifice, and hope in a fantastical city in the clouds, using animation and interactive elements.
  - Colosse: A VR app that showcases a real-time story of a stone giant and his encounter with a human tribe, using stunning visuals and sound effects.
  - Titans of Space Plus: A VR app that takes users on a guided tour of the solar system and beyond, using realistic models and data of planets, moons, stars, and other celestial objects.
  - Google Earth VR: A VR app that lets users fly over and explore any place on Earth, using high-resolution satellite imagery and 3D terrain.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR: A VR app that allows users to create and share their own graffiti art on realistic walls and surfaces, using a variety of spray cans, colors, and effects.
  - Littlstar: A VR app that provides access to a wide range of 360-degree videos and VR content, such as documentaries, music, sports, and entertainment.
  - Ocean Rift: A VR app that simulates an underwater world, where users can swim with various marine animals, such as sharks, dolphins, turtles, and whales.
  - The Foo Show: A VR app that features interviews and discussions with creators and artists, such as game developers, filmmakers, and musicians, in their own virtual environments.
  - Tilt Brush: A VR app that lets users paint and sculpt in 3D space, using a variety of brushes, colors, and effects.
  - Virtual Desktop: A VR app that allows users to use their computer in VR, such as browsing the web, watching videos, playing games, or working on documents.
  - Within: A VR app that offers a curated collection of immersive stories and experiences, such as documentaries, animations, music, and art.
  - Fulldive VR: A VR app that integrates various VR features and functions, such as browsing, social media, video player, camera, and gallery.
