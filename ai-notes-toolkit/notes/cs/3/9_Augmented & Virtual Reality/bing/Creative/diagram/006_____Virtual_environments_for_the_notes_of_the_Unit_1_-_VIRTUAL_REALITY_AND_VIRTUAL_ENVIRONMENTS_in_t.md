### Virtual environments

- A virtual environment is a networked application that allows a user to interact with both the computing environment and the work of other users .
- Examples of virtual environments are email, chat, web-based document sharing, online gaming, social media, etc.
- Virtual environments can be classified into two types: immersive and non-immersive.
  - Immersive virtual environments are those that create a sense of presence and immersion for the user, such as virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR).
  - Non-immersive virtual environments are those that do not require special devices or equipment to access, such as desktop applications and web browsers.
- Virtual environments can have various benefits and challenges for different domains and applications, such as education, entertainment, health, business, etc.
  - Benefits can include enhanced learning outcomes, increased motivation, improved collaboration, reduced costs, etc.
  - Challenges can include technical issues, ethical concerns, social isolation, cyber-sickness, etc.
