### Database - World Space

- Database - World Space is a concept that refers to the use of databases to store and retrieve data that is relevant to the physical environment or location of the user or device.
- Database - World Space can be used to create applications that use augmented reality (AR) technology to overlay data onto the real world, such as Power BI Data in space , which allows users to pin reports to locations in the real world and access them on their mobile devices.
- Database - World Space can also be used to visualize and analyze data in three-dimensional (3D) space, such as PointCloudsVR, which enables scientists to explore and interact with large-scale point cloud data sets in virtual reality (VR).
- Database - World Space can provide benefits such as:
  - Enhancing the user experience and engagement by providing context-aware and immersive data visualization and interaction.
  - Improving the data quality and accuracy by enabling data collection and validation in the field or on location.
  - Supporting decision making and problem solving by providing data that is relevant and timely to the situation or task at hand.
  - Enabling new forms of data exploration and discovery by allowing users to manipulate and compare data in 3D space.
- Database - World Space can also pose challenges such as:
  - Ensuring the data security and privacy by protecting the data from unauthorized access or modification.
  - Managing the data complexity and volume by optimizing the data storage, processing, and transmission.
  - Designing the data interface and interaction by considering the user needs, preferences, and capabilities.
  - Evaluating the data quality and validity by verifying the data sources, methods, and results.
- Database - World Space is an emerging and evolving field that requires interdisciplinary collaboration and innovation among database developers, AR/VR developers, data scientists, and domain experts.
