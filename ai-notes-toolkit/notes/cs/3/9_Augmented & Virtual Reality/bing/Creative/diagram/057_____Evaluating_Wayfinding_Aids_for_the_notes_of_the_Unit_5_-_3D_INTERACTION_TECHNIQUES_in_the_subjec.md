### Evaluating Wayfinding Aids for 3D Interaction Techniques

- Wayfinding aids (WATs) are tools or techniques that help users navigate in 3D virtual environments (VEs) by providing spatial information or guidance.
- WATs can be classified into four categories: maps, compasses, landmarks and signs .
- Maps show the layout of the VE from a top-down or a perspective view, and can be embedded in the 3D scene (view-in-view map) or displayed on a separate screen (overview map).
- Compasses indicate the direction of the user's orientation or the destination, and can be integrated with the map or shown as a separate widget.
- Landmarks are distinctive objects or features in the VE that help users recognize their location or direction, and can be natural (e.g., a building) or artificial (e.g., a statue).
- Signs are textual or graphical cues that provide instructions or information about the VE, and can be placed on the walls, floors or ceilings of the VE.
- Evaluating WATs is important to understand their impact on user performance, experience and satisfaction in 3D VEs.
- Evaluation methods can be divided into two types: empirical and analytical.
- Empirical methods involve conducting experiments with real users and measuring various metrics, such as task completion time, accuracy, errors, workload, presence, preference, etc.
- Analytical methods involve applying mathematical models or formulas to estimate the performance or usability of WATs, such as Fitts' law, steering law, Hick-Hyman law, etc.
- Evaluation criteria can be based on three aspects: effectiveness, efficiency and satisfaction .
- Effectiveness refers to how well the WATs help users achieve their navigation goals, such as finding a destination, exploring the VE, or learning the spatial layout.
- Efficiency refers to how much time, effort or resources the WATs require from the users, such as cognitive load, mental rotation, eye movement, etc.
- Satisfaction refers to how much the users like or enjoy using the WATs, such as aesthetic appeal, comfort, engagement, etc.
- Evaluation results can provide insights and guidelines for designing and selecting the most appropriate WATs for different 3D interaction tasks and scenarios.
