### Education

Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments. VR can be used for various educational purposes, such as:

- Enhancing students' engagement and motivation
- Providing immersive and experiential learning opportunities
- Supporting students with special needs and diverse learning styles
- Developing students' skills and competencies in various domains
- Enabling collaboration and communication among students and educators
- Offering access to remote and inaccessible locations and cultures
- Facilitating assessment and feedback

Some examples and applications of VR in education are:

- Virtual field trips: VR can enable students to visit and explore places that are otherwise difficult or impossible to access, such as historical sites, natural wonders, museums, and outer space. For example, Google Expeditions is a VR app that offers over 900 virtual field trips for K-12 students .
- Special education: VR can create safe and controlled environments for students with special needs, such as autism, dyslexia, or physical disabilities, to practice real-world skills and scenarios. For example, TeachLivE is a VR platform that allows students with autism to interact with virtual avatars and learn social and communication skills.
- Higher education: VR can enhance the learning outcomes and experiences of students in various disciplines, such as engineering, medicine, art, and design. For example, Microsoft HoloLens is a mixed reality device that allows medical students and clinicians to learn about the human anatomy and physiology using 3D holograms.
- Vocational training: VR can provide realistic and hands-on training for students and professionals in various fields, such as aviation, military, construction, and hospitality. For example, VR Flight Simulator is a VR app that allows users to learn how to fly a plane using a virtual cockpit and realistic scenarios.
- Collaborative learning: VR can enable students and educators to communicate and collaborate with each other in virtual spaces, regardless of their physical location. For example, AltspaceVR is a VR app that allows users to create and join virtual events, such as lectures, workshops, and social gatherings.
- Assessment and feedback: VR can offer new ways of assessing and providing feedback to students, such as using gamification, analytics, and adaptive learning. For example, ClassVR is a VR platform that allows educators to create and deliver VR lessons, monitor students' progress, and provide feedback using a web-based portal.
