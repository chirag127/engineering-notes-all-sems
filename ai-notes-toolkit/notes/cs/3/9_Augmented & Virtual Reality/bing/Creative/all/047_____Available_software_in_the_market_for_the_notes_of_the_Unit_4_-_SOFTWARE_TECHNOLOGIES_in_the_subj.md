# Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) are two technologies that create immersive and interactive experiences for users. AR overlays digital information on the real world, while VR creates a simulated environment that replaces the real world.
- There are many software tools available in the market for designing and developing AR and VR applications. Some of the most popular and widely used ones are:

  - **Adobe Aero**: This is a software that allows users to create and share AR experiences without coding. Users can import 3D models, animations, images, and sounds, and use intuitive tools to place them in the real world. Users can also preview and test their AR scenes on their devices, and publish them to the web or social media. Adobe Aero is compatible with iOS and Android devices, and supports various file formats such as USDZ, GLB, OBJ, FBX, and more.

  - **Tvori**: This is a software that enables users to create VR animations and stories using a VR headset and controllers. Users can use a library of assets, or import their own 3D models, sounds, and images, and manipulate them in a VR environment. Users can also record their movements and voice, and edit them in a timeline. Tvori supports Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, Valve Index, and Windows Mixed Reality headsets, and can export to various formats such as MP4, FBX, OBJ, and more.

  - **Masterpiece Studio Pro**: This is a software that combines two powerful tools for creating 3D models and animations in VR: Masterpiece Creator and Masterpiece Motion. Masterpiece Creator allows users to sculpt, model, and paint 3D objects in VR, using a variety of brushes, tools, and shapes. Masterpiece Motion allows users to rig, skin, and animate 3D characters in VR, using a simple and intuitive interface. Masterpiece Studio Pro supports Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, and Windows Mixed Reality headsets, and can export to various formats such as FBX, GLTF, OBJ, and more.

  - **Vuforia Engine**: This is a software that enables users to develop cross-platform AR applications for mobile devices, smart glasses, and headsets. Users can use a variety of features such as image recognition, object recognition, model targets, area targets, and spatial anchors, to create engaging and realistic AR experiences. Users can also use Vuforia Studio, a cloud-based tool, to create AR content without coding, using drag-and-drop widgets and templates. Vuforia Engine supports iOS, Android, UWP, and Unity platforms, and can integrate with various frameworks and libraries such as ARKit, ARCore, OpenCV, and more.

  - **Unity 3D**: This is a software that is widely used for creating both AR and VR applications, as well as games, simulations, and interactive media. Users can use a powerful and flexible editor, a rich asset store, and a scripting language (C#) to create and customize their projects. Users can also use various plugins and extensions, such as AR Foundation, XR Interaction Toolkit, MRTK, and more, to add AR and VR features and functionalities. Unity 3D supports various platforms such as iOS, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux, and more, and can export to various formats such as APK, EXE, HTML, and more.

  - **Oculus Medium**: This is a software that is designed for creating 3D models and sculptures in VR, using an Oculus headset and controllers. Users can use a variety of tools, such as brushes, stamps, layers, and colors, to shape and paint their creations. Users can also import and export 3D models, and share their work with other users. Oculus Medium supports Oculus Rift and Oculus Quest headsets, and can export to various formats such as FBX, OBJ, GLB, and more.

- These are some of the best software tools for AR and VR design in 2023, according to JUST™ Creative. However, there are many other software tools available in the market, such as Unreal Engine, Blender, Sketchfab, Spark AR, Lens Studio, and more, that can also be used for creating AR and VR applications. Users should choose the software tool that best suits their needs, preferences, and budget.
