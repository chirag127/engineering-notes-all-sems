# Architecture for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates immersive and interactive simulations of real or imagined environments, using computer-generated graphics, sounds, and other sensory inputs.
- VR applications for architecture are software tools that allow architects, designers, engineers, and clients to visualize, explore, and modify architectural projects in VR, using devices such as headsets, controllers, and trackers.
- VR applications for architecture can have various benefits, such as:
  - Enhancing the design process by enabling rapid prototyping, testing, and iteration of ideas in a realistic and immersive way.
  - Improving the communication and collaboration among stakeholders by allowing them to share and review the same virtual model, regardless of their physical location.
  - Increasing the engagement and satisfaction of clients by allowing them to experience and interact with the design before it is built, and to provide feedback and suggestions in real time.
  - Reducing the cost and time of construction by minimizing errors, rework, and waste, and by optimizing the use of materials, space, and energy.
- Some examples of VR applications for architecture are:
  - ARki: A mixed reality application that allows users to superimpose 3D models of architectural projects on the real world, using a smartphone or a tablet. ARki can be used to showcase designs, perform site analysis, and evaluate the impact of the project on the environment.
  - Storyboard VR: A free prototyping and visualization tool that allows users to create and edit VR scenes using 2D sketches, images, and 3D models. Storyboard VR can be used to test and refine concepts, layouts, and interactions in VR, and to export the scenes to other VR platforms.
  - Pair: A VR application that allows users to create and customize virtual spaces using a library of furniture, appliances, and accessories. Pair can be used to design and furnish interiors, and to share and collaborate with others in VR.
  - SmartReality: A VR application that allows users to view and interact with 3D models of construction projects, using a smartphone or a headset. SmartReality can be used to monitor the progress and quality of the construction, and to compare the virtual model with the actual site.
  - Fuzor: A VR application that allows users to import and synchronize 3D models from various design software, such as Revit, SketchUp, and Rhino. Fuzor can be used to perform design review, analysis, and simulation in VR, and to collaborate with others in real time.
