# Gestrual Commands for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Gestural commands are a type of system control technique that use the hand or body movements to issue commands to a 3D user interface.
- Gestural commands have significant appeal for system control in 3D UIs because of their important role in our day-to-day lives.
- Gestural commands can be classified as either postures or gestures :
  - Posture: a static configuration of the hand, such as a fist, a point, or an open palm .
  - Gesture: a movement of the hand, perhaps while it is in a certain posture, such as a swipe, a circle, or a pinch .
- Gestural commands can be used to interact with virtual content by directly grasping visible objects, or through invisible gestural commands that are associated with corresponding features in the immersive 3D space .
- The design of gestural commands for 3D UIs requires considering several factors, such as:
  - The naturalness and intuitiveness of the gestures
  - The recognition accuracy and feedback of the system
  - The user fatigue and comfort
  - The social acceptability and privacy
  - The consistency and compatibility with other interaction techniques
- Some examples of gestural commands for 3D UIs are:
  - The pinch gesture for selection or manipulation of objects
  - The swipe gesture for navigation or scrolling
  - The circle gesture for undo or redo
  - The point gesture for ray-casting or pointing
  - The fist gesture for grabbing or releasing objects
