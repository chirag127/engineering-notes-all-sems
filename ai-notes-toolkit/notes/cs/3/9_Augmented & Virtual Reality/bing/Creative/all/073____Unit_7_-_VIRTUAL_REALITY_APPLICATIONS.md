# Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

- Virtual reality applications are applications that make use of virtual reality (VR), an immersive sensory experience that digitally simulates a virtual environment.
- VR applications require software and hardware to create and display the virtual environment, and to enable the user to interact with it through various devices, such as goggles, headsets, gloves, or body suits .
- VR applications can have various purposes and benefits, such as entertainment, education, training, business, health, social, and art .
- Some examples of VR applications are:
  - Google Expeditions: a VR app that allows users to explore different places and cultures around the world, such as museums, landmarks, and natural wonders.
  - Allumette: a VR app that tells a story of love and sacrifice through a 3D animated film that the user can watch from different perspectives.
  - Colosse: a VR app that showcases a story of a stone giant and his relationship with nature and humans, using hand-drawn animation and spatial audio.
  - Titans of Space Plus: a VR app that takes the user on a tour of the solar system and beyond, with detailed information and realistic visuals of the planets and stars.
  - Google Earth VR: a VR app that lets the user fly over and explore any location on Earth, using satellite imagery and 3D models.
  - Kingspray Graffiti VR: a VR app that allows the user to create and share graffiti art on realistic walls and surfaces, using a variety of spray cans and colors.
  - Littlstar: a VR app that provides a platform for watching and streaming 360-degree videos and VR content, such as documentaries, music, sports, and games.
  - Ocean Rift: a VR app that simulates an underwater world, where the user can swim with and learn about different marine animals and habitats.
  - The Foo Show: a VR app that features interviews and conversations with creators and artists, such as game developers, filmmakers, and writers, in their own virtual environments.
  - Tilt Brush: a VR app that enables the user to paint and draw in 3D space, using various brushes, colors, and effects.
  - Virtual Desktop: a VR app that allows the user to use their computer in VR, with multiple monitors, custom backgrounds, and wireless streaming.
  - Within: a VR app that offers a collection of immersive stories and experiences, such as documentaries, animations, music, and comedy, from various creators and genres.
  - Fulldive VR: a VR app that integrates social media, video streaming, and web browsing in VR, with features such as voice search, bookmarks, and recommendations.
