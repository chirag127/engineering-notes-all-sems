# Choosing Output Devices for 3D User Interfaces

Output devices, also called display devices, allow the machine to provide information or feedback to one or more users through the human perceptual system. Most of them are focused on stimulating the visual, auditory, or haptic senses.

Some of the factors to consider when choosing output devices for 3D user interfaces are:

- The type and quality of the output: Different output devices have different capabilities and limitations in terms of the type and quality of the output they can provide. For example, some output devices can produce stereoscopic images, while others can only produce monoscopic images. Some output devices can provide high-resolution, high-fidelity, and high-dynamic-range output, while others can only provide low-resolution, low-fidelity, and low-dynamic-range output. The type and quality of the output affect the user's perception, immersion, and performance in the 3D user interface  .
- The cost and availability of the output devices: Different output devices have different costs and availability in the market. Some output devices are expensive, rare, or require special hardware or software to operate, while others are cheap, common, or easy to use. The cost and availability of the output devices affect the feasibility, accessibility, and scalability of the 3D user interface  .
- The compatibility and interoperability of the output devices: Different output devices have different compatibility and interoperability with other devices, platforms, and standards. Some output devices are compatible and interoperable with a wide range of devices, platforms, and standards, while others are compatible and interoperable with only a few or none. The compatibility and interoperability of the output devices affect the flexibility, portability, and usability of the 3D user interface  .
- The user preferences and expectations of the output devices: Different users have different preferences and expectations of the output devices they use in the 3D user interface. Some users prefer output devices that are comfortable, natural, and intuitive, while others prefer output devices that are novel, exciting, and challenging. Some users expect output devices that are consistent, reliable, and accurate, while others expect output devices that are adaptive, responsive, and expressive. The user preferences and expectations of the output devices affect the satisfaction, engagement, and enjoyment of the 3D user interface  .
