### Training for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Virtual Reality (VR) is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real way by a person using special electronic equipment.
2. VR has a wide range of applications, including gaming, education, training, and therapy.
3. In gaming, VR can provide an immersive experience that allows players to feel as if they are inside the game world.
4. In education, VR can be used to create interactive simulations that allow students to explore and learn about complex concepts in a more engaging way.
5. In training, VR can be used to simulate real-world scenarios, allowing trainees to practice and develop skills in a safe and controlled environment.
6. In therapy, VR can be used to treat conditions such as anxiety and post-traumatic stress disorder by exposing patients to virtual environments that help them confront and overcome their fears.
7. VR technology is constantly evolving, with new advancements in hardware and software allowing for more realistic and immersive experiences.
