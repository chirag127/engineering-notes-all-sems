### Architecture for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Virtual Reality (VR) architecture refers to the use of VR technology in the field of architecture.
- VR architecture allows architects to create and visualize 3D models of their designs in a virtual environment.
- This technology enables architects to better communicate their ideas to clients and stakeholders, as well as to test and refine their designs before construction.
- VR architecture can also be used for training and education purposes, allowing students to explore and interact with virtual buildings and environments.
- There are several software and hardware tools available for VR architecture, including VR headsets, controllers, and specialized software for creating and viewing 3D models.
- VR architecture is a rapidly evolving field, with new developments and advancements being made on a regular basis.
