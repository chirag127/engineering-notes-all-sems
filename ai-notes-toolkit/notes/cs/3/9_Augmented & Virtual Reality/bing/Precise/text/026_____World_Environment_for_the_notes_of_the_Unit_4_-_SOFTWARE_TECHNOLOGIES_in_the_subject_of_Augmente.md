### World Environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) are shaping up to be future technological battlegrounds.
2. AR enables users to experience a perfect blend of the digital world and physical elements to create an artificial environment.
3. AR focuses on the interaction between the user and the virtual information registered in a 3-D space.
4. Connecting more people to AR and VR technologies could transform everything from education and healthcare to mining and tourism.
5. AR overlays digital content onto representations of the real environment, using smartphones, tablets, or AR glasses.
6. With Mixed Reality (MR), you can play a virtual video game, grab your real world water bottle, and smack an imaginary character from the game with the bottle.
