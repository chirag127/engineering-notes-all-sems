### The historical development of VR

- The development of virtual reality (VR) technology can be traced back to the 1950s .
- Morton Heilig invented the first multimedia device called the Sensorama in 1957, which is considered one of the earliest VR systems .
- In 1968, computer scientist Ivan Sutherland and his student Bob Sproull invented the first VR head-mounted display (HMD) system, The Sword of Damocles .
- The term “virtual reality” was popularized by Jaron Lanier in the 1980s .
- Lanier's research and engineering contributed a number of products to the nascent VR industry .
- The federal government, particularly the Department of Defense and the National Science Foundation, played a significant role in early VR research and technology development in the United States .
- Between 1986-1989, Furness developed a flight simulator known as the Super Cockpit, which featured computer-generated 3D maps, advanced infrared and radar imagery, and real-time audio and visual feedback for the pilot .
