### Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in Engineering

Virtual Reality (VR) is a technology that allows users to interact with a computer-generated environment in a seemingly real way. VR has many applications in engineering, including design, modeling, and visualization.

1. **Design and Engineering**: VR technology can be used to model and design new hardware technologies, such as engines. An environment can be created with different engineering parts being accessible.

2. **3D Modeling and Visualization**: VR engineering includes the use of 3D modeling tools and visualization techniques as part of the design process. This technology enables engineers to view their project in 3D and gain a greater understanding of how it works.

3. **Education**: VR and Augmented Reality (AR) can help students understand and interact more effectively with engineering concepts. These emerging technologies are characterized by their novelty, rapid development, and effects on human existence.

4. **Future Trends**: VR and AR are booming technologies that promise to transform many aspects of our lives, including engineering. The field of Extended Reality (XR), which includes VR and AR, is rapidly evolving and has many exciting future trends and predictions.
