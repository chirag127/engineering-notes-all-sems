### Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **Introduction**: Begin by introducing the topic of 3D interaction techniques in the context of Augmented and Virtual Reality. Provide a brief overview of the importance and relevance of the topic.

2. **Types of 3D Interaction Techniques**: Discuss the various types of 3D interaction techniques used in Augmented and Virtual Reality, such as hand gestures, voice commands, and gaze-based interactions. Provide examples and use cases for each technique.

3. **Design Considerations**: Discuss the design considerations that must be taken into account when developing 3D interaction techniques for Augmented and Virtual Reality. These may include factors such as user comfort, ease of use, and accessibility.

4. **Challenges and Limitations**: Discuss the challenges and limitations associated with the development and implementation of 3D interaction techniques in Augmented and Virtual Reality. These may include technical challenges, such as tracking accuracy and latency, as well as user-related challenges, such as motion sickness and cognitive load.

5. **Future Directions**: Conclude by discussing the future directions of 3D interaction techniques in Augmented and Virtual Reality. This may include emerging technologies and research areas, as well as potential applications and use cases.
