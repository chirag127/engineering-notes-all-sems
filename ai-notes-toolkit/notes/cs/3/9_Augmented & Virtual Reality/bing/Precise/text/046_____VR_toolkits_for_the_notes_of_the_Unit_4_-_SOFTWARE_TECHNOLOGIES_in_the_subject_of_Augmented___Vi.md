### VR Toolkits for Software Technologies in Augmented and Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) are two spectrums of immersive technology that have the potential to replace mobile computing. A range of major products came to market in 2016 from companies including Oculus VR, Sony, and Google.

Virtual Reality Software Development Kits (VR SDKs) provide the fundamental tools to design, build, and test VR experiences. These SDKs are the building blocks to create VR experiences such as mobile apps, marketing experiences, training simulations, and more.

Some of the most popular SDKs for VR software development include:
- **HTC Vive OpenVR SDK**: An open VR software development kit (SDK) and API that supports HTC Vive and other VR headset related devices.
- **SteamVR SDK** and **VRTK (Virtual Reality Tool Kit)**: Also used for HTC Vive.
- **Unity**: One of the top virtual reality tools for VR development globally, especially for VR headsets. Game developers develop apps, games, and even industrial applications compatible with Oculus, HTC Vive, and PlayStation VR.
- **Unreal Engine**: Has a variety of tried-and-true virtual reality and augmented reality developer tools designed to handle any task. As a result, the VR game engine provides game developers with an advanced real-time 3D creation tool for immersive experiences.
- **Blender 3D**: Computer Graphics Software Toolset.

There are also several tools for AR development, including:
- **Vuforia**
- **Wikitude**
- **ARKit**
- **ARCore**
- **ARToolKit**.
