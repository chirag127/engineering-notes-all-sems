### Interaction - Simple

Interaction in the context of software technologies, particularly in Augmented and Virtual Reality, refers to the ways in which users can interact with the virtual environment and its elements.

Some simple forms of interaction in AR and VR include:

1. **Gaze-based interaction**: This involves the user directing their gaze towards a specific point or object in the virtual environment to trigger an action or event.

2. **Hand gestures**: Hand gestures, such as pinching, swiping, or pointing, can be used to interact with virtual objects or navigate the virtual environment.

3. **Voice commands**: Voice commands can be used to trigger actions or events in the virtual environment, such as opening a menu or selecting an object.

4. **Controller-based interaction**: Physical controllers, such as gamepads or handheld devices, can be used to interact with the virtual environment and its elements.

These are some of the simple forms of interaction that can be used in AR and VR applications. As technology advances, more complex and immersive forms of interaction are being developed to enhance the user experience.
