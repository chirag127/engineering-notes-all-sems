### Manipulation Techniques and Input Devices for 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Manipulation techniques define how input data modify the properties of virtual objects in augmented environments where virtual content overlaps reality  .
- There are three main categories of interaction techniques applicable in handheld mobile AR: touch-based interaction, mid-air gestures-based interaction, and device-based interaction techniques .
- Touch-based interaction techniques often use the multi-touch capabilities of the device's display for object selection and manipulation. Complex multi-finger and hand gestures can be used to provide full 3D manipulation by touch .
- Mid-air gestures-based interaction techniques allow users to interact with virtual objects using hand and arm movements in the air.
- Device-based interaction techniques use the movement and orientation of the handheld device itself to manipulate virtual objects.
- A high precision when selecting and manipulating distant objects can be achieved using a zooming-based interaction technique that brings both real and virtual objects closer while maintaining the spatio-temporal registration between the virtual and real scenes .
- 3D interaction techniques designed in the 3D User Interfaces research community, such as navigation, selection, and manipulation techniques, open numerous opportunities for musical control .
