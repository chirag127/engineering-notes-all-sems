### Scientific landmarks for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **1968**: Ivan Sutherland created the first head-mounted display (HMD) system, which is considered to be the first virtual reality (VR) system.
2. **1980s**: The term "virtual reality" was popularized by Jaron Lanier, who founded the company VPL Research, which developed and sold VR gear, including HMDs and gloves.
3. **1991**: The first commercial VR entertainment system, Virtuality, was released by Virtuality Group.
4. **1993**: SEGA announced the SEGA VR headset for arcade games and the Mega Drive console.
5. **1995**: Nintendo released the Virtual Boy, a portable VR gaming system.
6. **2010**: Palmer Luckey created the first prototype of the Oculus Rift, which was later acquired by Facebook in 2014.
7. **2016**: Several major VR headsets were released, including the Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, and PlayStation VR.
8. **2018**: Standalone VR headsets, such as the Oculus Go and Lenovo Mirage Solo, were released, allowing for untethered VR experiences.
9. **2020**: The release of the Oculus Quest 2, a standalone VR headset with improved performance and features compared to its predecessor.
