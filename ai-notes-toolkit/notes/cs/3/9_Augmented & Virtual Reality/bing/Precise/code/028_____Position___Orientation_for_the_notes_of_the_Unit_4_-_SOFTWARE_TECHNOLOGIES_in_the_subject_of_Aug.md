### Position / Orientation

1. Position and orientation are important concepts in Augmented and Virtual Reality (AR/VR) as they determine the location and direction of virtual objects in the real or virtual world.
2. Position refers to the location of an object in a 3D space, usually represented by a set of coordinates (x, y, z).
3. Orientation refers to the direction an object is facing, usually represented by a set of angles (pitch, yaw, roll) or a quaternion.
4. In AR/VR, tracking the position and orientation of the user's head and/or hands is crucial for providing a realistic and immersive experience.
5. Various technologies can be used for tracking position and orientation, including sensors, cameras, and external tracking systems.
6. Accurate tracking of position and orientation is essential for ensuring that virtual objects are correctly aligned with the real world and that the user's movements are accurately reflected in the virtual environment.
7. In addition to tracking the user's position and orientation, AR/VR systems may also track the position and orientation of other objects, such as controllers or physical props, to enable interaction with the virtual world.
