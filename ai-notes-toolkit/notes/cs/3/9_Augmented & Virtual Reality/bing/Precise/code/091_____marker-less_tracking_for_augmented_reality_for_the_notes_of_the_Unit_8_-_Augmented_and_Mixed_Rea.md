### Marker-less Tracking for Augmented Reality

- Marker-less tracking for augmented reality is a classification task that allows developers to easily map environments and display augmented reality content without the need for target images or objects (markers).
- The ORB algorithm is used for feature detection and the FREAK algorithm is used for feature description.
- The classifiers used for the tracking task are KNN, Random Forest, Extremely Randomized Trees, SVM and Bayes classifier.
- Wikitude’s SLAM markerless augmented reality tracking is one of the most versatile cross-platform 3D-tracking systems available for mobile.
- Visual markerless pose trackers mainly rely on natural feature points (often also called interest points or key points) visible in the user’s environment.
