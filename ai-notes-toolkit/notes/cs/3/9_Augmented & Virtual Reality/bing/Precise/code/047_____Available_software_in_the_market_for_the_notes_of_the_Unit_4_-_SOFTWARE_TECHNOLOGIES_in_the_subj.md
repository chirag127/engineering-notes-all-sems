### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **Unity**: Unity is a popular software for creating AR and VR experiences. It offers AR Foundation to create applications for handheld and wearable AR devices.
2. **AR Headsets**: AR headsets are devices used to view augmented reality experiences. They can include sound as well.
3. **AR Platform**: AR platforms are used to create and manage the whole augmented reality experience.
