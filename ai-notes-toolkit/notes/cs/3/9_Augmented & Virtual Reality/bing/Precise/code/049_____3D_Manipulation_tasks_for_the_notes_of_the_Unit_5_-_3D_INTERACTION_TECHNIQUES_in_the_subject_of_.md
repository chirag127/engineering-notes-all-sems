### 3D Manipulation tasks for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- 3D manipulation is a fundamental task for many different scientific datasets, such as particle data in physics and astronomy, fluid data in aerography, and structured data in medical science.
- 3D manipulation tasks can be broadly defined as any act of physically handling objects with one or two hands. A narrower definition is spatial rigid object manipulation (shape preserving).
- Canonical 3D manipulation tasks include select, position, and rotate.
- Variables that affect manipulation include distance to object, object size, required translation distance, amount of rotation, object density, and others.
- The design of manipulation techniques depends on interaction tasks and variables.
- Application-specific manipulation tasks can fail to capture important task properties for real applications. For example, positioning a medical probe relative to virtual models of internal organs in a VR medical training application. Techniques must capture and replicate minute details of such manipulation tasks.
