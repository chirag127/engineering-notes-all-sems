### Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality
#### AR systems and functionality

- **Augmented Reality (AR)** is a technology that superimposes a computer-generated image on a user's view of the real world, thus providing a composite view.
- AR systems use a range of sensors, cameras, and projection systems to overlay virtual information onto the real world.
- AR systems can be categorized into two types: **marker-based** and **markerless**.
- **Marker-based AR** systems use physical markers, such as QR codes or images, to trigger the display of virtual information.
- **Markerless AR** systems, on the other hand, use sensors and algorithms to detect and track features in the real world, such as planes or objects, and overlay virtual information onto them.
- AR systems can be used in a variety of applications, including gaming, education, advertising, and navigation.
- AR systems can also be used in industries such as healthcare, manufacturing, and construction, to provide workers with real-time information and guidance.
- AR systems can be implemented on various devices, including smartphones, tablets, and head-mounted displays.
- The functionality of AR systems is constantly evolving, with advancements in areas such as object recognition, tracking, and rendering.
