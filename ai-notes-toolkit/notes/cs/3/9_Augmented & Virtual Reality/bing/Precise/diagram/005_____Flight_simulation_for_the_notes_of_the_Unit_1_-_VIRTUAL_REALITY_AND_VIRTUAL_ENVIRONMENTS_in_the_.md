### Flight Simulation

Flight simulation is the use of a device or software to replicate the experience of flying an aircraft. It is commonly used for pilot training, design and development of aircraft, and research into aircraft systems and behaviors.

One of the ways flight simulation can be experienced is through virtual reality (VR). A VR flight simulator is a computer program that allows users to experience the sensation of flying a plane without ever leaving the ground. VR flight simulators are often used by pilots in training, as they provide a safe and realistic environment in which to practice.

Some popular VR flight simulators include Aerofly, which emulates the classic Microsoft Flight Simulator X with high-quality graphics and highly detailed planes, airports, and flight controls. Microsoft Flight Simulator also supports virtual reality headsets, allowing users to immerse themselves in a realistic world of flight.

In addition to pilot training, VR flight simulators can also be used for research. For example, the Extended Reality Flight Simulation and Control Lab at Auburn University uses motion-base VR simulators to study human-machine interaction, advanced flight control laws, and innovative pilot cueing methods.

Overall, VR flight simulation provides a realistic and immersive experience for users, making it a valuable tool for pilot training and research.
