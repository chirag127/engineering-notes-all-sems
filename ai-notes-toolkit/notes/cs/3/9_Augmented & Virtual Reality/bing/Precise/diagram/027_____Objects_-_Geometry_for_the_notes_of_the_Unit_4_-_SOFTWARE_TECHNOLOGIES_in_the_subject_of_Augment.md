### Objects - Geometry

1. In the context of Augmented and Virtual Reality, objects refer to the digital representations of real-world or imaginary items that can be placed and interacted with in a virtual environment.
2. Geometry refers to the mathematical study of the properties, measurements, and relationships of points, lines, angles, surfaces, and solids.
3. In the context of software technologies, geometry is used to create and manipulate the shapes and forms of objects in a virtual environment.
4. Common geometric representations of objects include polygon meshes, NURBS surfaces, and point clouds.
5. These representations are used to define the shape, size, and position of objects in a virtual environment.
6. The use of geometry in software technologies allows for the creation of realistic and interactive virtual environments, enabling users to experience and interact with objects in a simulated world.
