### Tools for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **3D Modeling Software**: Tools such as Blender, Maya, and 3ds Max can be used to create 3D models for use in virtual and augmented reality environments.
2. **Game Engines**: Game engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine provide a platform for developing interactive 3D content for virtual and augmented reality.
3. **Input Devices**: Devices such as motion controllers, haptic gloves, and eye-tracking systems can be used to interact with virtual and augmented reality environments.
4. **Tracking Systems**: Tracking systems such as optical tracking, inertial tracking, and magnetic tracking can be used to track the position and orientation of the user and objects in virtual and augmented reality environments.
5. **Display Technologies**: Display technologies such as head-mounted displays, projection-based displays, and handheld displays can be used to present virtual and augmented reality content to the user.
6. **Audio Technologies**: Audio technologies such as spatial audio and binaural audio can be used to enhance the immersive experience in virtual and augmented reality environments.
