### Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES
#### VR Toolkits

1. **Unity 3D**: A popular game engine that can be used to develop VR applications. It has a large community and many resources available for learning and development.
2. **Unreal Engine**: Another popular game engine that can be used for VR development. It has a powerful graphics engine and is known for its high-quality visuals.
3. **A-Frame**: An open-source web framework for building VR experiences. It is based on HTML and is easy to learn for web developers.
4. **Vuforia**: A software platform for creating augmented reality applications. It supports a wide range of devices and has a large developer community.
5. **Google VR**: A platform for building VR experiences for Android and iOS devices. It includes tools for creating immersive content and supports a wide range of hardware.
6. **SteamVR**: A platform for building VR experiences for PC and console. It is developed by Valve and supports a wide range of hardware, including the HTC Vive and Oculus Rift.
7. **Oculus SDK**: A software development kit for building VR experiences for the Oculus Rift and Oculus Quest. It includes tools for creating immersive content and supports a wide range of hardware.

These are some of the popular VR toolkits available for developing VR applications. Each toolkit has its own strengths and capabilities, and the choice of toolkit will depend on the specific needs and goals of the project. It is important to research and compare the different options before making a decision.
