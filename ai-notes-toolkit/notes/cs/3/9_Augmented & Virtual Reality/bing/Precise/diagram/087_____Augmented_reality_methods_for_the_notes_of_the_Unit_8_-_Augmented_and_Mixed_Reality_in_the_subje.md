### Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality
#### Augmented Reality Methods

1. **Marker-based AR:** This method uses a camera and a visual marker to determine the center, orientation, and range of its spherical coordinate system. The marker is a simple pattern that the AR software can recognize, and the camera reads the marker and overlays a virtual image on top of it.

2. **Markerless AR:** This method uses a camera, accelerometer, and gyroscope to determine the position and orientation of the device. The AR software uses this information to overlay virtual images on the real world. This method is also known as location-based or position-based AR.

3. **Projection-based AR:** This method projects artificial light onto real-world surfaces. The projections can interact with the real world, for example, by changing the appearance of the surface or by creating the illusion of three-dimensional objects.

4. **Superimposition-based AR:** This method replaces the original view of an object with an augmented view of the same object. The AR software uses object recognition to determine the object and then overlays the augmented view on top of it.

5. **Mixed Reality:** This method combines real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time.
