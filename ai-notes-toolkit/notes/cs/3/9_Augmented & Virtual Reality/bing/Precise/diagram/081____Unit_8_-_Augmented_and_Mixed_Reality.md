## Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

Augmented Reality (AR) and Mixed Reality (MR) are technologies that allow users to experience a blend of the real and virtual worlds. Here are some key points to consider:

1. **Augmented Reality** involves overlaying digital information onto the real world, typically through the use of a smartphone or tablet camera. This can include images, videos, and 3D models.

2. **Mixed Reality** is similar to AR, but it also allows for interaction between the real and virtual worlds. This can be achieved through the use of specialized headsets or other devices.

3. Both AR and MR have a wide range of applications, including gaming, education, and marketing.

4. These technologies are still in their early stages, and there is much room for growth and development.

5. As with any technology, there are potential concerns regarding privacy and security that must be addressed.
