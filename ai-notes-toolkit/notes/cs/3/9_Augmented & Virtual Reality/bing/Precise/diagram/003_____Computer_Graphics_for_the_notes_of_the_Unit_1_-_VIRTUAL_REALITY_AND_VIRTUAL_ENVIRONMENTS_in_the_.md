### Computer Graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Computer graphics is the field of visual computing, where one utilizes computers both to generate visual images synthetically and to integrate or alter visual and spatial information sampled from the real world.
2. Computer graphics is responsible for displaying art and image data effectively and meaningfully to the user.
3. It is used to create and manipulate visual content, such as images, videos, and animations.
4. Computer graphics plays a crucial role in many areas, including science, engineering, medicine, business, industry, and entertainment.
5. In the context of virtual reality and virtual environments, computer graphics is used to create realistic and immersive simulations of real or imagined environments.
6. These simulations can be used for a variety of purposes, such as training, education, entertainment, and research.
7. Computer graphics techniques, such as 3D modeling, rendering, and animation, are used to create the virtual environments and objects within them.
8. Advanced computer graphics techniques, such as real-time rendering and physically-based simulation, are used to create realistic and interactive virtual experiences.
9. The use of computer graphics in virtual reality and virtual environments has the potential to revolutionize many fields, by providing new and powerful tools for visualization, interaction, and collaboration.
