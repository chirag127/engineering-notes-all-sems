### Gestrual Commands for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Gesture commands are a type of 3D interaction technique used in augmented and virtual reality environments.
2. These commands allow users to interact with virtual objects and environments using hand and body movements.
3. Gesture recognition technology is used to interpret the user's movements and translate them into commands.
4. Common gesture commands include pointing, grasping, and swiping.
5. These commands can be used for tasks such as selecting and manipulating virtual objects, navigating through virtual environments, and triggering actions or events.
6. Gesture commands can provide a more intuitive and natural way of interacting with virtual environments, compared to traditional input methods such as keyboards and mice.
7. However, the accuracy and reliability of gesture recognition can vary depending on factors such as the quality of the tracking technology and the complexity of the gestures.
8. Research is ongoing to improve the accuracy and usability of gesture commands in augmented and virtual reality environments.
