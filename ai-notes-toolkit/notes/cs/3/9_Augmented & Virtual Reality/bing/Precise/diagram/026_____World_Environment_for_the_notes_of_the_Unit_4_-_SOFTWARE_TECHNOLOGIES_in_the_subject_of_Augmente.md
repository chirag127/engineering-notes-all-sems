### World Environment for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) are rapidly expanding across industry, academia, and government and intertwining the physical and digital worlds.
2. There are four different elements that characterize virtual reality and separate it from other forms of technology such as mixed reality and augmented reality. One of these elements is a 3D-simulated environment, where an artificial environment is rendered through a medium like a VR display or a headset.
3. AR enables users to experience a perfect blend of the digital world and physical elements to create an artificial environment. AR focuses on the interaction between the user and the virtual information registered in a 3-D space.
4. Connecting more people to AR and VR technologies could transform everything from education and healthcare to mining and tourism.
5. These technologies provide different degrees of sensory immersion and interaction between the real world and digital content. AR overlays digital content onto representations of the real environment, using smartphones, tablets, or AR glasses.
6. With Mixed Reality (MR), you can play a virtual video game, grab your real world water bottle, and smack an imaginary character from the game with the bottle.
