### Design Guidelines - System Control

1. **Consistency**: System controls should be consistent in their appearance and behavior across the application. This helps users to easily understand and predict the behavior of the controls.

2. **Visibility**: System controls should be visible and easily accessible to the user. This helps users to quickly find and use the controls they need.

3. **Feedback**: System controls should provide clear and immediate feedback to the user. This helps users to understand the effect of their actions and to avoid mistakes.

4. **Simplicity**: System controls should be simple and easy to use. This helps users to quickly learn and use the controls without confusion.

5. **Flexibility**: System controls should be flexible and customizable to meet the needs of different users. This helps users to personalize the controls to their preferences.

6. **Error prevention and recovery**: System controls should be designed to prevent errors and to help users recover from errors. This helps users to avoid frustration and to quickly recover from mistakes.
