### Mobile Projection Interfaces

Mobile projection interfaces are a type of interface that allows users to interact with digital content in the physical world. These interfaces use small-scale projectors that can be integrated into mobile devices, such as smartphones, to project digital imagery into physical space .

One of the major limitations of Augmented Reality (AR) is that it always needs some type of interface device, such as a smartphone or head-mounted AR glass, to interact with AR content. This can limit its usage. Projection-based Augmented Reality offers a solution to this limitation by allowing users to interact with AR content without the need for an additional interface device .

The integration of projectors into mobile devices opens up a rich design space for mobile augmented reality applications. With a built-in projector, not only can the graphical scale of the applications be increased, but the range of possibilities for developing mobile augmented reality applications will also widen .
