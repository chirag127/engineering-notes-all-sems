### Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. The notes should be concise and to the point, covering all the important concepts and techniques related to 3D interaction in Augmented and Virtual Reality.
2. The notes should be well-organized and structured, with clear headings and subheadings to make it easy for the reader to navigate and find the information they need.
3. The notes should include diagrams and illustrations to help explain complex concepts and techniques.
4. The notes should include examples and case studies to demonstrate the practical application of the concepts and techniques discussed.
5. The notes should be written in a clear and concise language, avoiding jargon and technical terms that may be unfamiliar to the reader.
6. The notes should be regularly updated to reflect the latest developments and advancements in the field of 3D interaction in Augmented and Virtual Reality.
7. The notes should include references to additional resources and further reading for those who wish to explore the topic in more depth.
