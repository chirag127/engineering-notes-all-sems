# Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality
## Marker-less Tracking for Augmented Reality

- Marker-less tracking for augmented reality is a method that allows developers to easily map environments and display augmented reality content without the need for target images or objects (markers).
- One of the most versatile cross-platform 3D-tracking systems available for mobile is Wikitude’s SLAM markerless augmented reality tracking.
- In one study, an augmented reality application was adopted with markerless tracking as a classification task. The ORB algorithm was used for feature detection and the FREAK algorithm was used for feature description. The classifiers used for the tracking task were KNN, Random Forest, Extremely Randomized Trees, SVM and Bayes classifier .
- Markerless augmented reality works by scanning the surrounding environment and there is no trigger photo necessary to retrieve the augmented reality content. Apps that offer such features usually will ask the user to find a flat surface such as a table or floor for placing the AR elements.
- Estimating the pose of a camera (virtual or real) in which some augmentation takes place is one of the most important parts of an augmented reality (AR) system.
- Tracking is a very important research subject in a real-time augmented reality context. The main requirements for trackers are high accuracy and little latency at a reasonable cost.
