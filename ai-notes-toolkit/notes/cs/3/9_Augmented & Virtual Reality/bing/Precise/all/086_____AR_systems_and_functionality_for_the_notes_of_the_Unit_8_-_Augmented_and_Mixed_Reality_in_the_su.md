# Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

### AR systems and functionality

Augmented Reality (AR) is a technology that enhances the user's perception of the real world by overlaying digital information onto the physical environment. AR systems typically consist of the following components:

1. **Display:** The display is the primary interface between the user and the AR system. It can be a head-mounted display, a handheld device, or a projection system.

2. **Sensors:** AR systems use various sensors to track the user's movements and the environment. These sensors can include cameras, accelerometers, gyroscopes, and GPS.

3. **Processing:** The AR system processes the data from the sensors to determine the user's position and orientation, and to generate the digital content that is overlaid onto the real world.

4. **Interaction:** AR systems provide various means for the user to interact with the digital content, such as touch, gesture, or voice commands.

AR systems can be used for a wide range of applications, including gaming, education, advertising, and navigation. They can enhance the user's experience by providing additional information, visual cues, and interactive elements. AR systems can also be used to create mixed reality environments, where the digital and physical worlds are seamlessly integrated.
