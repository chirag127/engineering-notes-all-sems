# Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

### Visualization Techniques for Augmented Reality

1. **Introduction:** In recent years, the development of Augmented Reality (AR) frameworks has made AR application development widely accessible to developers without an AR expert background. With this development, new application fields for AR are on the rise.

2. **Data Integration:** A simple overlay of hidden structure on top of the system’s video feed can cause a number of issues. Therefore, data integration is an important aspect of AR visualization techniques.

3. **Scene Understanding:** Scene understanding is crucial for AR visualization techniques to work effectively. This involves understanding the real-world environment and how virtual objects can be integrated into it.

4. **Big Data Visualization:** One of the more promising methods for improving upon current Big Data visualization techniques is in its correlation with Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) that are suitable for the limited perception capabilities of humans.

5. **Visual Collaboration:** Visualizations in real-world environments benefit from the visual collaboration between real and virtual imagery. This is one of the key advantages of using AR for visualization.
