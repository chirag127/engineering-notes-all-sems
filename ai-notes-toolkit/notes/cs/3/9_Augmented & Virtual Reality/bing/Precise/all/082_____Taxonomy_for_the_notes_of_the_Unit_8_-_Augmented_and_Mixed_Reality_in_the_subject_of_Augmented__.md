# Taxonomy for Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **Definition:** Taxonomy is the practice and science of classification. In the context of Augmented and Mixed Reality, taxonomy refers to the classification of different types of AR and MR experiences and technologies.

2. **Purpose:** The purpose of taxonomy in AR and MR is to provide a framework for understanding, comparing, and organizing the various types of AR and MR experiences and technologies.

3. **Types of AR and MR experiences:** There are several ways to classify AR and MR experiences, including by the level of immersion, the type of interaction, and the type of content.

4. **Level of immersion:** AR and MR experiences can be classified by the level of immersion they provide, ranging from non-immersive to fully immersive. Non-immersive experiences provide a limited sense of presence in the virtual environment, while fully immersive experiences provide a strong sense of presence.

5. **Type of interaction:** AR and MR experiences can also be classified by the type of interaction they support, including gesture-based, voice-based, and touch-based interaction.

6. **Type of content:** AR and MR experiences can be classified by the type of content they display, including 2D, 3D, and video content.

7. **Conclusion:** Taxonomy is an important tool for understanding and organizing the diverse range of AR and MR experiences and technologies. By classifying AR and MR experiences by their level of immersion, type of interaction, and type of content, we can better understand and compare different types of AR and MR experiences.
