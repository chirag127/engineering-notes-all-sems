### Design Guidelines - System Control

1. **Consistency**: System controls should be consistent in their appearance and behavior across the application. This helps users to learn and predict the behavior of the controls, reducing confusion and errors.

2. **Visibility**: System controls should be visible and easily accessible to the user. This helps users to quickly find and use the controls they need.

3. **Feedback**: System controls should provide clear and immediate feedback to the user when they are interacted with. This helps users to understand the consequences of their actions and to confirm that their input has been received.

4. **Flexibility**: System controls should be flexible and customizable to accommodate the needs and preferences of different users. This helps users to tailor the system to their specific needs and to work more efficiently.

5. **Simplicity**: System controls should be simple and easy to use. This helps users to quickly learn and use the controls, reducing frustration and errors.

6. **Error prevention and recovery**: System controls should be designed to prevent errors and to help users recover from errors when they occur. This helps users to avoid frustration and to quickly recover from mistakes.

7. **Contextual relevance**: System controls should be relevant to the current context and task. This helps users to focus on the task at hand and to avoid being overwhelmed by irrelevant controls.

These are some of the key design guidelines for system control in the context of 3D interaction techniques in Augmented & Virtual Reality. By following these guidelines, designers can create effective and user-friendly system controls that enhance the user experience.
