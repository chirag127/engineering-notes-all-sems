# Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality

### Difference between AR and VR

- **AR (Augmented Reality)** is a technology that overlays digital information onto the real world, enhancing the user's perception of their surroundings. This can be achieved through the use of devices such as smartphones, tablets, or AR glasses.

- **VR (Virtual Reality)**, on the other hand, is a technology that immerses the user in a completely artificial environment, typically through the use of a VR headset. In VR, the user's perception of the real world is completely replaced by the virtual environment.

- The main difference between AR and VR is the level of immersion. While AR enhances the user's perception of the real world, VR completely replaces it.

- Another key difference is the type of content that can be experienced. AR content is typically anchored to the real world and can interact with it, while VR content is completely detached from the real world.

- AR and VR also differ in terms of the devices used to experience them. AR can be experienced through a variety of devices, including smartphones and tablets, while VR typically requires a dedicated VR headset.

- In terms of applications, AR has found widespread use in fields such as education, marketing, and gaming, while VR is commonly used for gaming, training, and simulation.

- Both AR and VR have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with digital content, but they do so in very different ways. While AR enhances our perception of the real world, VR transports us to entirely new ones.
