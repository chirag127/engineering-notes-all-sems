
### Tessellated Data for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

* Tessellated data is a type of data used to create a more realistic and immersive virtual reality experience. 
* It is used to create a 3D representation of a scene, object, or environment by using a series of small, uniform shapes, or “tiles”. 
* These tiles are typically made up of polygons, which are then combined to form a larger, more complex 3D image or scene. 
* Tessellated data can be used to create realistic textures, lighting, and shadows, as well as to create detailed models of objects and environments. 
* It is used in many different types of applications, such as video games, 3D modeling, and virtual reality. 
* Tessellated data is also used to create realistic simulations of physical phenomena, such as fluid dynamics, particle systems, and terrain.
