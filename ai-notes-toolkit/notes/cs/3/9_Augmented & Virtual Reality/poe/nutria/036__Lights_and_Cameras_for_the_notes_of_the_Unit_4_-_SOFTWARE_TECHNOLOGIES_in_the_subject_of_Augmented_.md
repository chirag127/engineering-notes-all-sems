
### Lights and Cameras for the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality 

- Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) technologies rely on the use of lights and cameras to generate the virtual environment. 
- Lights are used to provide illumination and to create shadows, while cameras are used to capture the user’s movements and gestures. 
- Lights and cameras are used to create a realistic and immersive experience for the user.
- Lights are used to create shadows, highlights, and reflections that help to create a more realistic virtual environment.
- Cameras are used to capture the user’s movements and gestures, which are then used to control the virtual environment.
- The use of lights and cameras can also be used to create a sense of depth in the virtual environment.
- The use of lights and cameras can also be used to create a sense of presence in the virtual environment.
