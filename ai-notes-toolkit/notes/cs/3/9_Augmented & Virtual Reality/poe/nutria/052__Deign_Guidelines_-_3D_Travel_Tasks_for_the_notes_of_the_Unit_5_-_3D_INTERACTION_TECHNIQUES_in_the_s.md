
### Design Guidelines - 3D Travel Tasks

1. When designing 3D travel tasks, it is important to consider the user's experience. This includes the user's comfort level with the task, the task's difficulty, the amount of time it will take to complete the task, and the user's ability to interact with the 3D environment.

2. It is important to provide clear instructions for the user to follow. This includes providing detailed information about the task, as well as providing visual cues to help guide the user through the task.

3. It is important to provide feedback to the user when they complete a task. This feedback should be informative and provide the user with an indication of their progress.

4. It is important to consider the user's physical abilities when designing 3D travel tasks. This includes providing the user with the ability to move freely in the 3D environment, as well as providing options for users with limited mobility.

5. It is important to consider the user's cognitive abilities when designing 3D travel tasks. This includes providing the user with the ability to understand the task, remember the instructions, and complete the task in a timely manner.

6. It is important to consider the user's emotional state when designing 3D travel tasks. This includes providing the user with a sense of accomplishment when they complete the task, as well as providing options for users who feel overwhelmed or frustrated.
