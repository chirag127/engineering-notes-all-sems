
### Medicine for the Notes of Unit 7 - Virtual Reality Applications

1. Virtual reality (VR) has been used in medical applications for many years.

2. VR can be used to simulate medical procedures, allowing medical students to gain experience without the need for live patients.

3. VR can also be used to help diagnose and treat patients with mental health issues, such as post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and phobias.

4. VR can also be used to help patients with physical disabilities, such as paralysis, to regain control of their limbs.

5. VR can also be used to help surgeons practice complex operations before they are performed on actual patients.

6. VR is being used in the field of rehabilitation, allowing patients to practice physical activities in a virtual environment.

7. VR can also be used to help medical professionals diagnose and treat patients with chronic pain.

8. VR is being used to help medical professionals better understand and treat various diseases, such as cancer and Alzheimer's.
