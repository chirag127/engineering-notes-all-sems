
### The Historical Development of VR

1. The concept of virtual reality (VR) was first proposed by Ivan Sutherland in 1965. He described it as a “window into a virtual world”.

2. In the 1980s, Jaron Lanier popularized the term “virtual reality” and founded the first company to produce VR products.

3. By the 1990s, the technology had become more widely available, with the release of the first commercial VR headsets.

4. In the 2000s, the development of VR technology accelerated, with the introduction of motion tracking, 3D audio, and head-mounted displays.

5. In the 2010s, VR technology became more accessible to consumers, as companies such as Oculus, HTC, and Sony released consumer-grade VR headsets.

6. In the 2020s, the use of VR technology has become more widespread, with applications in fields such as healthcare, education, and entertainment.
