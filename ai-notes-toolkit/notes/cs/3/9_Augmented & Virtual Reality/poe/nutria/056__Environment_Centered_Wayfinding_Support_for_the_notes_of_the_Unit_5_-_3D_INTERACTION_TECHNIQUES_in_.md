
### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support

1. Environment Centered Wayfinding Support (ECWS) is a technique for providing navigation assistance in augmented and virtual reality (AR/VR) environments.
2. ECWS uses 3D interaction techniques such as hand-gestures, voice commands, and gaze tracking to provide users with an intuitive way to interact with the environment.
3. ECWS can be used to provide users with information about their current location, as well as directions to their desired destination.
4. ECWS can also be used to provide users with information about the environment, such as points of interest, landmarks, and other relevant data.
5. ECWS can be used to create more immersive and engaging experiences in AR/VR environments, as well as to improve navigation efficiency.
