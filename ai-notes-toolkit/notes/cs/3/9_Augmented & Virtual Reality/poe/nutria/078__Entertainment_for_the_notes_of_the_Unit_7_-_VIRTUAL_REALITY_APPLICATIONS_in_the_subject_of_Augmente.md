
### Entertainment for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

* Entertainment applications of virtual reality can be used to create immersive experiences for users.
* Video games are one of the most popular and successful applications of virtual reality. Games such as Beat Saber, Skyrim VR, and Half-Life: Alyx, have been released as virtual reality experiences.
* Virtual reality can also be used to create interactive movies and other types of media. These experiences can be used to tell stories in a new and unique way.
* Virtual reality can be used to create virtual concerts and other live events. These events can be experienced from the comfort of one's own home.
* Virtual reality can also be used to create educational experiences. These experiences can be used to teach students about history, science, and other topics in an immersive way.
