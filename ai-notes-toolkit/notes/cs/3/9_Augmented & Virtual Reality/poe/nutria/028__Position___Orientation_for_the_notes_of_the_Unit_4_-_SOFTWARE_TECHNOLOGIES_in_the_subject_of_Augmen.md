
### Position / Orientation in Augmented & Virtual Reality

* Position and orientation are two of the most important components of augmented and virtual reality.
* Position is the location of an object in three-dimensional space. It is usually expressed as x, y, and z coordinates.
* Orientation is the direction an object is facing. It is usually expressed as three angles: yaw, pitch, and roll.
* Position and orientation are important for creating realistic and immersive experiences in augmented and virtual reality.
* To accurately track and update position and orientation, augmented and virtual reality systems use a variety of sensors, such as GPS, accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers.
* Software technologies such as SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) are used to track and update the position and orientation of objects in augmented and virtual reality.
* Position and orientation can be used to create a variety of experiences in augmented and virtual reality, such as navigation, object tracking, and collision detection.
