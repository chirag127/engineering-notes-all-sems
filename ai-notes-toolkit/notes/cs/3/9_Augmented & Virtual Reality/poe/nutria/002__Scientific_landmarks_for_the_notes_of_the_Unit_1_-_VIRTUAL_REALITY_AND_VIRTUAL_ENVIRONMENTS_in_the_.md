
### Scientific Landmarks for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments
1. The first scientific landmark in the field of virtual reality was the work of Ivan Sutherland in 1965, who developed the first head-mounted display (HMD) system. 
2. In the 1980s, Jaron Lanier coined the term "virtual reality" and developed the first commercial virtual reality system. 
3. In the 1990s, the CAVE (Cave Automatic Virtual Environment) was developed, which allowed multiple users to experience a virtual environment in a shared space. 
4. In the 2000s, the development of the Nintendo Wii and Microsoft Kinect enabled motion tracking in virtual environments. 
5. In the 2010s, the development of augmented reality technologies such as Google Glass and Microsoft HoloLens enabled the blending of virtual and real environments.
