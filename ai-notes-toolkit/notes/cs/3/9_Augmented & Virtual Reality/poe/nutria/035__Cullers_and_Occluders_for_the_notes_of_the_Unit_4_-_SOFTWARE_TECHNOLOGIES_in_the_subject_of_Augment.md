
### Cullers and Occluders

Cullers and Occluders are two important concepts in Augmented and Virtual Reality. 

* Cullers are used to determine which objects are visible on the screen. This is done by comparing the objects' position in the virtual world with the camera's position and orientation. The culler will discard any objects that are outside the camera's view.

* Occluders are used to determine which objects are hidden by other objects in the scene. This is done by comparing the objects' positions and sizes in the virtual world. Any objects that are behind other objects will be considered occluded and not visible to the user. 

Both cullers and occluders are important for creating realistic and immersive environments in Augmented and Virtual Reality. They allow for efficient rendering, as only the visible objects need to be drawn. They also help to create more believable scenes, as objects that are hidden from view are not visible to the user.
