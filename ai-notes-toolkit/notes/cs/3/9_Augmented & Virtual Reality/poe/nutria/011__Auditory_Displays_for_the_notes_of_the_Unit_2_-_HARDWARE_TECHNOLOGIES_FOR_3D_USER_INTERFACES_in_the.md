
### Auditory Displays for the notes of the Unit 2 - HARDWARE TECHNOLOGIES FOR 3D USER INTERFACES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

* Auditory displays are an important component of 3D user interfaces (3DUIs). They can provide users with audio cues that help them navigate their virtual environment, as well as provide feedback on their actions.
* 3DUIs can also use auditory displays to provide a more immersive experience. Audio cues can be used to indicate the direction of objects in the virtual environment, or to create a sense of depth and space.
* Auditory displays can also provide feedback on user actions. For example, they can be used to provide feedback on the success or failure of a user’s interaction with an object or environment.
* In addition, auditory displays can be used to provide environmental cues, such as the sound of wind or birds, to create a more realistic and immersive experience.
* Finally, auditory displays can also be used to provide information about the user’s current location or status in the virtual environment. This can help the user orient themselves and understand their current situation.
