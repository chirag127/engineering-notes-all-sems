
### Classification for the Notes of Unit 5 - 3D Interaction Techniques in the Subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **3D Interaction Techniques** refer to the ways in which users interact with 3D objects in a virtual or augmented reality environment.

2. **Haptic Interaction** is a type of 3D interaction technique that relies on physical feedback, such as vibrations or force, to provide users with a sense of touch when interacting with virtual objects.

3. **Gesture Recognition** is a type of 3D interaction technique that uses hand and body gestures to control objects in a virtual or augmented reality environment.

4. **Voice Recognition** is a type of 3D interaction technique that uses voice commands to control objects in a virtual or augmented reality environment.

5. **Eye Tracking** is a type of 3D interaction technique that uses eye movements to control objects in a virtual or augmented reality environment.

6. **Object Tracking** is a type of 3D interaction technique that uses a device's camera to track the position and orientation of objects in a virtual or augmented reality environment.
