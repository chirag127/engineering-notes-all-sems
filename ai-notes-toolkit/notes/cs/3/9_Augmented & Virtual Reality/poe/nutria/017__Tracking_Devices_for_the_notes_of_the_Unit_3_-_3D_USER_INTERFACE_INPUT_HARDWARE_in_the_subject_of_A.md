
### Tracking Devices for the Notes of Unit 3 - 3D USER INTERFACE INPUT HARDWARE

* Tracking devices are used to track a user's movements and actions in a virtual or augmented reality environment.
* Optical tracking systems use cameras to track the user's movements and actions. They are often used in virtual reality systems such as the Oculus Rift.
* Inertial tracking systems use accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers to track the user's movements and actions. They are often used in augmented reality systems such as the Microsoft HoloLens.
* Magnetic tracking systems use magnetic fields to track the user's movements and actions. They are often used in medical applications such as surgical robotics.
* Pressure-sensitive tracking systems use pressure sensors to track the user's movements and actions. They are often used in gaming applications such as the Nintendo Wii.
