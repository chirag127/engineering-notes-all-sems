
### 3D Mice

3D mice are a type of input hardware used in Augmented and Virtual Reality applications. They are used to interact with 3D objects and environments, providing users with a more intuitive and immersive experience. 

3D mice are typically composed of two or more buttons, a scroll wheel, and a 3D sensor. The 3D sensor allows the user to move the mouse in three dimensions, allowing for more precise control over objects in the 3D environment. 

3D mice are most commonly used in 3D modeling and animation applications, where they can be used to manipulate 3D objects and environments. They can also be used in Virtual Reality applications, allowing users to interact with virtual objects and environments in a more natural way. 

3D mice can provide a more intuitive and immersive experience than traditional 2D mice, and can be used to create more realistic 3D models and animations. They are also more ergonomic and can reduce the risk of repetitive strain injuries.
