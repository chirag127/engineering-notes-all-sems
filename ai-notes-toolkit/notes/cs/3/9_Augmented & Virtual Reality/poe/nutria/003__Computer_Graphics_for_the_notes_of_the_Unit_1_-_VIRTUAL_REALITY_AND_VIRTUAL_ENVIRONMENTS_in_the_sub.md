
### Computer Graphics for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

* Computer graphics are the creation of digital images, animations, and simulations using computer hardware and software.
* Computer graphics are used in a variety of applications, including video games, movies, scientific visualization, and even engineering and architecture.
* Virtual reality (VR) is a computer-generated simulation of a three-dimensional image or environment that can be interacted with in a seemingly real or physical way.
* Virtual environments (VEs) are computer-generated simulations of real-world environments. They can be used to explore, learn, and experience different places, events, and activities.
* Augmented reality (AR) is the integration of digital information with the user's environment in real time. AR can be used to enhance the user's experience of the real world through the addition of virtual elements.
* Computer graphics are used to create virtual environments and augmented reality experiences. They can be used to create realistic visuals, interactive experiences, and immersive simulations.
