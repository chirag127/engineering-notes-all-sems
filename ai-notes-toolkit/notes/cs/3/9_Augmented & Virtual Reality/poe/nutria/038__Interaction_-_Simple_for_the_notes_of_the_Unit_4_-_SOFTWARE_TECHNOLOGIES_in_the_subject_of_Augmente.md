
### Interaction - Simple

* Interaction is an essential part of augmented and virtual reality (AR/VR) experiences. 
* Interaction can be divided into two categories: simple and complex. 
* Simple interaction refers to basic user interaction with the environment, such as selecting objects, moving objects, and manipulating objects. 
* Simple interaction is often handled through a combination of hardware and software, such as motion controllers, gaze tracking, and voice recognition. 
* Simple interaction can also be handled through physical controllers, such as joysticks or gamepads. 
* Simple interaction is used to create a more immersive experience, as well as to provide feedback and control for the user.
