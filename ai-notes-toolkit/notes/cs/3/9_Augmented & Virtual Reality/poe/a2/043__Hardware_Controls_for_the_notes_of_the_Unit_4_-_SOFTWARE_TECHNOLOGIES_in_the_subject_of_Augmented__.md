### Hardware Controls

1. Head-Mounted Display (HMD): It is a wearable device that presents AR/VR visuals to the user. It tracks the user's movements and adjusts the view accordingly.
2. Sensors: Sensors like gyroscopes, accelerometers and magnetometers are used to track the user's motion and orientation. They provide inputs to the system to adjust the view based on the user's movements.
3. Hand Controllers: Controllers allow the user to interact with the AR/VR environment. They are tracked in the 3D space and their movements are mapped to actions like selection, manipulation, etc. They provide inputs to perform actions in the AR/VR system.
4. Tracking Systems: Tracking systems like optical, inertial, etc. are used to track the position and orientation of the user, hand controllers and other objects. They provide inputs to the system to determine the correct view and responses based on the movements and interactions.
5. Displays: AR/VR systems use displays like LCD, OLED, Micro-displays, etc. to present the augmented or virtual visuals to the user. The displays have wide field of view and high pixel density for an immersive experience.
