 Here are the Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality:

### 1. Be concise and to the point
- Notes should be brief and highlight key concepts and topics
- Avoid unnecessary elaboration and keep sentences short and simple

### 2. Use clear language
- Avoid complex words and minimize jargon
- Explain technical terms when first used
- Use active voice as much as possible for clarity

### 3. Organize content logically
- Group related concepts and topics together
- Use consistent headers to separate sections
- Sequence information in a logical flow or chronological order

### 4. Focus on understanding, not memorization
- Notes should aid comprehension, not just memorization
- Explain ideas and concepts thoroughly, not just state facts
- Use diagrams, examples, and visuals to reinforce understanding

### 5. Leave some flexibility
- Do not try to capture every detail
- Leave some room for following your own thoughts or including additional info
- Notes do not have to be exhaustive to be useful
