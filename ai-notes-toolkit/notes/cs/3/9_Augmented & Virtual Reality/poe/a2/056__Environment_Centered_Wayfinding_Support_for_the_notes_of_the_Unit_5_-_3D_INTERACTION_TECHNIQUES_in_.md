### Environment Centered Wayfinding Support for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Environment Centered Wayfinding: This technique focuses on using the environment and its spatial properties to aid navigation instead of relying on a map or compass. The key idea is to make the environment more navigable by emphasizing important landmarks and paths.
2. Techniques: Some techniques for environment centered wayfinding include:
- Landmark Enhancement: Important landmarks in the environment can be enhanced by highlighting, tagging or augmenting them to make them more noticeable. This makes it easier for users to remember the landmark and navigate to/from it.
- Path Enhancement: Frequently used paths or routes can be emphasized through techniques like highlighting, lighting or widening the path. This guides users along the correct route.
- Simplification: The environment can be simplified by removing unnecessary clutter and details so that important landmarks and paths are more focussed on. This makes spatial understanding and navigation easier.
3. Benefits: The benefits of environment centered wayfinding are that it is more intuitive and natural than map or compass based navigation. It does not require additional interactions to check a map and can work well for navigation in large open spaces. However, it may not be suitable for very complex environments or when a map provides a better overview of the overall space.
