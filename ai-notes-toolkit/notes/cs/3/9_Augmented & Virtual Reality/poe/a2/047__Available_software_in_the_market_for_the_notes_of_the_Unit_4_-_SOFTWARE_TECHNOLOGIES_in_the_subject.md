### Available software in the market for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality:

1. Unity: Unity is a cross-platform game engine developed by Unity Technologies. It is primarily used to develop video games and simulations for mobile devices, consoles, PCs, and websites. The engine is also capable of robotic simulations and architectural visualizations.

2. Unreal Engine: Unreal Engine is a game engine developed by Epic Games. It has been used to develop many blockbuster games and real-time 3D applications. The engine is free to use, with paid licensing options for developers and companies.

3. ARToolKit: ARToolKit is an open-source C/C++ library for building augmented reality applications. It uses computer vision techniques to calculate the real-world position and orientation of physical markers in real-time. ARToolKit supports a number of markers and tracking algorithms and works on Android, iOS, Linux, macOS, and Windows.

4. Vuforia: Vuforia is a software development kit for augmented reality. It uses computer vision technology to recognize and track planar images (such as images, QR codes, and text) and simple 3D objects in real-time. Vuforia supports development for Android, iOS, and UWP and can be used with Unity, Unreal Engine, and native development.

5. Wikitude: Wikitude is an augmented reality SDK and platform. It allows developers to build AR apps for mobile devices that can recognize and track images, 3D objects, and locations. Apps can integrate information from image recognition, sensors, and databases. Wikitude supports Android and iOS and can be used with Unity, Cordova, and Xamarin.
