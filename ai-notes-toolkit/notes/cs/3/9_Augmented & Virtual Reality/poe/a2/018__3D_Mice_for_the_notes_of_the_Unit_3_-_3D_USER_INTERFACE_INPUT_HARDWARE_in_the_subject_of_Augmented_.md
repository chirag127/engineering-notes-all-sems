### 3D Mice

- 3D Mice are the input devices used to interact with 3D environments in Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality.
- They allow the users to navigate and manipulate 3D objects with 6 degrees of freedom - x, y, z, pitch, yaw, roll.
- 3D Mice typically have additional buttons and scroll wheels for various functions like selection, zooming, etc.
- The 3 degrees of motion are tracked using various sensors like accelerometers, gyroscopes, magnetometers, etc.
- Some 3D Mice provide haptic feedback to the user for improved experience.
- Popular 3D Mice include 3Dconnexion SpaceNavigator, Logitech SpaceMouse, Immersion CyberGrasp, etc.
- 3D Mice are ideal for applications requiring precise 3D interactions such as 3D modeling software, architectural visualization, surgery simulations, etc.
- However, 3D Mice can have a steep learning curve and their adoption in consumer VR/AR is limited due to the additional cost.
