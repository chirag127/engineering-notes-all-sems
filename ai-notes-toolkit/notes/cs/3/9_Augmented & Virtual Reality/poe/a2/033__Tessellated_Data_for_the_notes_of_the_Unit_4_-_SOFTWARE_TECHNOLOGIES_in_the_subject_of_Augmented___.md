### Tessellated Data for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Tessellated data refers to data that is split up into small, regular, and repeated units or portions.
2. In the context of augmented and virtual reality, 3D models and environments are typically represented as tessellated data.
3. The 3D models are split into many small polygons, usually triangles, that fit together in a regular pattern to represent the shape of the object.
4. This tessellation allows the 3D shapes to be rendered efficiently on graphics hardware and enables techniques like level of detail to optimize performance.
5. The density of the tessellation, or in other words the number and size of the individual polygons used, impacts the quality and realism of the final rendered images or frames.
6. Higher tessellation densities, using more and smaller polygons, can represent finer details and curves more accurately but require more processing to render.
7. Optimizing the tessellation density is an important aspect of developing performant and high quality augmented and virtual reality applications.
