### Voice Commands for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Voice commands allow users to interact with and control AR/VR applications through speech.
2. Users can speak commands to perform actions like navigation, object manipulation, menu selection, etc.
3. Advantages: Hands-free interaction, natural and easy to use.
4. Challenges: Background noise can affect recognition, constraints of limited vocabulary and grammar, context-dependent interpretations, etc.
5. Examples: 'Select', ' Rotate', 'Zoom in/out', 'Pan left/right', 'Increase/decrease size', etc. can be used to control objects in a AR/VR scene.
6. Voice command systems use Speech Recognition to convert speech to text and Natural Language Processing to interpret the meaning.
7. Constraining the vocabulary and grammar can increase accuracy but limits the natural language capabilities.
8. Microphones can be used to capture the user's speech and headset-mounted or spatially-located microphones can capture speech even in noisy environments.
