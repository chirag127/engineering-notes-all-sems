### Database - World Space

1. Database is a structured collection of data. It organizes data into one or more tables (or other structures) of columns and rows, with a unique key identifying each row.
2. Database management system (DBMS) is the software that interacts with end users, applications, and the database itself to capture and analyze the data.
3. The world space is a 3D space defined within a scene that contains GameObjects The world space never changes and is the space the GameObjects exist in, independent of any Viewports.
4. Transformation - The process of changing the position, orientation, or scale of a GameObject. This is done by setting Transformation component values (like Transform.position or Transform.rotation).
5. Parenting - Establishing a hierarchical relationship between two GameObjects, where one GameObject (the child) follows the transform of another (the parent).
6. Prefab - APrefab is a GameObject that has been flagged as a Prefab Asset. This allows you to store a GameObject complete with components and properties, and reuse it in your scenes.
7. Prototyping - The process of quickly building functionality to test concepts and try out ideas. This is often done without regard to code quality or performance, focusing instead on feasibility and usability.
