### Graphical Menus for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Menus: Menus are graphical user interfaces that contain options or commands displayed on the screen. They are used to interact with the system or application.
2. Types: There are broadly two types of menus - pull-down menus and pop-up menus.
3. Pull-down menus: They are menus that are hidden and can be accessed by clicking on the menu bar to pull it down and display the list of options. Only one menu is displayed at a time.
4. Pop-up menus: They are menus that pop-up on the screen when the user performs a specific action like right-clicking on the screen. They provide context-sensitive options to the user. Multiple pop-up menus can be displayed and accessed simultaneously.
5. Uses in 3D interaction: In 3D applications like virtual environments or games, menus can be used to provide a way to manipulate objects, customize settings, accessing options, etc. They can be 2D or 3D and can use 3D interaction techniques like ray casting to access. They make the interactions easy and intuitive for users.
