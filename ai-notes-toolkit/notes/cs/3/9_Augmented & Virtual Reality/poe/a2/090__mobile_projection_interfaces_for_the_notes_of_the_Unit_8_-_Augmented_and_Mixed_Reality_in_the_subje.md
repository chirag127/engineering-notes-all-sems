### Mobile Projection Interfaces

- Mobile projection interfaces project digital information onto physical surfaces using a mobile device.
- They enable augmented reality experiences without the need for head-mounted displays.
- The mobile device uses its camera to track fiducial markers or feature points in the environment and then augments the live video feed with digital information projected onto the physical surfaces.
- Example applications include projecting icons onto a desk and interactions with the projected icons to access data and control devices.
- Challenges include correct projection of the digital information onto 3D physical surfaces and interactions with the projected information.
- Additional sensors and depth cameras can help to better integrate the digital information into the physical environment.
- Mobile projection interfaces make augmented reality experiences more accessible but the projection area is limited by the mobile device's projection capabilities.
