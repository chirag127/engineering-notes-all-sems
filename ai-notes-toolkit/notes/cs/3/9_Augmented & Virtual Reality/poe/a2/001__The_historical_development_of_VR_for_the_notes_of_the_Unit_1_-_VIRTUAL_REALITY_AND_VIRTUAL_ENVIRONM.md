### The historical development of VR

- 1950s: The idea of VR was first conceived. The first VR head-mounted display was created by amateur inventor Morton Heilig.
- 1960s-70s: Early VR and AR research conducted at Harvard and MIT. The first head-mounted display and audio systems were developed.
- 1980s: Jaron Lanier coined the term "virtual reality" and founded VPL Research, the first VR company. DataGlove and the Polhemus were also developed in this decade to track hand movements and head positioning.
- 1990s: The first commercial VR headsets released. Growth in the video game industry led to advances in real-time 3D graphics. VR started being used for medical, rehabilitation, and training purposes.
- 2000s: Increased research in VR at universities and companies. The first smartphones released and the iOS and Android mobile operating systems enabled portable AR/VR experiences on phones.
- 2010s: The Oculus Rift development kit released, popularizing VR for gaming. The HTC Vive, PlayStation VR, and other headsets released. Mobile AR started taking off. Continued research and development in VR/AR technologies and applications.
