### Training for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. Virtual Reality in Education
- VR can create immersive learning experiences for students.
- Students can explore historical sites, museums, distant locations, etc. via VR.
- Complex concepts in sciences and engineering can be taught via VR simulations.
- VR helps students with diverse learning styles to learn more effectively.

2. Virtual Reality in Healthcare
- VR is used for training healthcare professionals in surgical procedures via simulations.
- VR exposure therapy is used to treat phobias and PTSD. Patients can face their fears in a controlled virtual environment.
- VR helps reduce pain during painful medical procedures via distraction and relaxation techniques.
- VR can help diagnose and assess conditions like concussions, Parkinson's disease, etc.

3. Virtual Reality in Engineering
- VR enables engineers to visualize and interact with their designs in 3D before building prototypes.
- It allows collaboration and feedback from stakeholders via shared virtual environments.
- Complex engineering systems and processes can be simulated in VR to identify and fix issues.
- VR trains engineers on the use and maintenance of advanced equipment in a low-cost manner.
