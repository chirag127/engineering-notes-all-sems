### Architecture for the notes of the Unit 7 - VIRTUAL REALITY APPLICATIONS

1. Introduction
- Explain what Virtual Reality is.
- Discuss the potential and scope of Virtual Reality applications.

2. Hardware components
- Head-Mounted Display: Provides stereoscopic view, tracks head movements
- Sensors: Gyroscopes, accelerometers to track orientation and motion
- Controllers: Allow user input
- Computer: Powerful machine to render graphics in real-time

3. Software components
- Platforms: Windows, Linux, Android, etc. to develop and run applications
- APIs: Low-level and high-level APIs to access hardware capabilities
- Engines: Game engines to render graphics, physics and interactions
- SDKs: Software Development Kits to develop custom VR applications

4. Types of VR applications
- Gaming: Immersive 3D gaming environments
- Entertainment: Cinema, sports, music, etc.
- Social: Virtual meetings, hangouts, collaboration
- Educational: Learning simulations, tutorials, experiments
- Industrial: Product design, training, remote operation of machines
- Medical: Diagnosis, rehabilitation, phobia treatment, etc.

5. Challenges and future scope
- Some challenges are affordability, size, and resolution of devices; latency; and 3D content creation.
- The future of VR is promising with possibilities in many fields and continued technological advancements.
- The XR (Extended Reality) spectrum including AR and MR will play a significant role in the future.
