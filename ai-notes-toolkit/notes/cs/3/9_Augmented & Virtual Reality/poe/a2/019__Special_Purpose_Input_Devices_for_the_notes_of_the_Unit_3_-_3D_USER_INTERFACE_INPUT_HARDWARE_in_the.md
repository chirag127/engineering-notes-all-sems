### Special Purpose Input Devices

- Data Gloves: Fabric gloves with sensors that track finger movements and bending. Used for inputting hand gestures and manipulating objects in virtual environments.
- Trackers: External devices that track the position and orientation of objects or body parts. Examples include head trackers, hand trackers, and tracker tags. Used for tracking movements and motion for inputs.
- Motion Controllers: Handheld controllers with buttons and tracking capabilities. Allow users to input commands or manipulate objects in virtual/augmented environments through motion and gestures.
- Gesture Recognition Systems: Vision-based or sensor-based systems that detect and interpret gestures, motions, and poses as inputs. For example, cameras can track the movements of a user's hands, head, or body and translate these into inputs.
- Haptic Devices: Devices that provide haptic feedback to the user through force, touch, and/or vibrations. Allow users to "feel" and interact with virtual objects/environments. Examples include exoskeleton suits and haptic joysticks/controllers.
- Brain-Computer Interfaces: Systems that detect and translate brain signals into computer inputs. Allow for hands-free control of devices/systems through brain waves, cerebral activity, and other neurological signals. Still an emerging field of research.
