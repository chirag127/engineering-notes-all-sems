### Flight simulation for the notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

- Flight simulation is a virtual recreation of the experience of flying an aircraft. It is used for training pilots.
- It provides a realistic virtual environment and simulated aircraft controls for the trainee pilot to learn how to handle different flying conditions and situations.
- The key components of a flight simulator are:
-- A motion system to provide the feeling of motion and forces as experienced in a real aircraft.
-- A visual system to provide an out-of-the-cockpit view for the trainee pilot. This could include multiple projection screens or VR headsets to simulate the view.
-- A control loading system to simulate the forces required to move the aircraft control surfaces like ailerons, elevators, rudder, etc. The controls are designed to "feel" like real aircraft controls.
-- A computing system to simulate the dynamics of flight and the responses to the trainee pilot's control inputs. It keeps track of the simulated aircraft state and generates responses to control inputs.
- The high fidelity of modern flight simulators allows pilots to train for complex real-world situations in a safe virtual environment. This enables them to gain experience without risk to passengers or equipment. Simulators save costs and time compared to real aircraft training.
- The technology and components used in flight simulators serve as a key example of virtual environments and their applications. The realistic simulations would not be possible without technological developments in computing, visuals, motion control, and control loading systems.
