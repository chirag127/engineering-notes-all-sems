### Enhancing Interactivity in AR Environments

1. 360° Video - Allow users to view and interact with a 360° video. This provides an immersive experience and a sense of presence in the virtual environment. Users can look around the scene and control the camera view.
2. Spatial Anchors - Pin digital objects to a specific location in the real world so that they remain in a fixed position. This enables shared AR experiences and persistent content that can be experienced by multiple users.
3. Machine Learning - Use machine learning and computer vision to recognize objects, scenes and people in the real world. This enables contextual interactions, reactions and experiences. For example, an AR app could recognize furniture in a room and suggest virtual furnishings to complement the space.
4. Voice Input - Accept voice input to control the AR experience hands-free. Users can speak commands, queries and instructions to navigate, manipulate objects or get information. This is ideal when a hands-free experience is necessary.
5. Gesture Input - Use gestures and movements to interact with AR content. The front-facing camera on a mobile device or wearable technology like smart glasses can capture a user's gestures to control the AR experience. This provides an intuitive way to interact with augmented content.
6. Tangible Objects - Utilize physical objects as input controllers. For example, a toy gun could be used to shoot virtual targets or a physical pen could be used to draw and create 3D artwork in an AR environment. This bridges the gap between the physical and digital world.
