1. Surgical Training - VR is used to train surgeons in a risk-free virtual environment. They can practice complex surgeries and get used to the feel of conducting operations. This leads to better skills and fewer errors during actual surgeries.
2. Phobia Treatment - VR is used in exposure therapy to treat phobias and anxiety disorders. Patients are exposed to the thing they fear in a controlled virtual environment which helps desensitize the fear response. This is safer and more effective than traditional exposure therapy.
3. Pain Distraction - VR can be used as a distraction technique during painful medical procedures or physical therapy. The immersive virtual experience diverts the patient's attention from the pain and makes the experience more tolerable.
4. Diagnosis - Conditions like PTSD, ADHD, balance disorders, eyesight problems, etc. can be diagnosed using VR through simulations and interactive experiences which elicit responses and symptoms. This leads to more accurate and earlier diagnoses.
5. Accessibility - VR can provide experiences to those who are unable to leave the hospital or suffer from physical disabilities. They can attend events, go to places and engage in activities through VR which enhances their well-being.
