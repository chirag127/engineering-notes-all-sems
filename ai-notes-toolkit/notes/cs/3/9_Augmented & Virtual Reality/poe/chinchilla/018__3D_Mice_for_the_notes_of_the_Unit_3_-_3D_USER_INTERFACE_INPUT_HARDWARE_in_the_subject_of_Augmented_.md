### 3D Mice

3D mice are input devices designed to provide a more intuitive and natural way of interacting with 3D environments in augmented and virtual reality. They allow users to navigate and manipulate 3D objects with six degrees of freedom, providing a more immersive and efficient user experience.

Here are some key points to remember about 3D mice:

- 3D mice are typically handheld devices that allow users to move and rotate objects in three-dimensional space using six degrees of freedom. This means that users can move an object up/down, left/right, and forward/backward, as well as rotate it around its x, y, and z axes.
- 3D mice often feature a variety of buttons and controls that can be programmed to perform different functions, such as selecting, dragging, and scaling objects. These buttons can be customized to suit the user's preferences and workflow.
- 3D mice are often used in conjunction with other input devices, such as traditional mice and keyboards. This allows users to switch between different modes of interaction depending on the task at hand.
- 3D mice are particularly useful for tasks that require precise and detailed manipulation of 3D objects, such as architectural design, product design, and medical imaging. They can also be used for gaming and entertainment applications.
- Some popular manufacturers of 3D mice include 3Dconnexion, SpaceNavigator, and CadMouse.

In summary, 3D mice are an important input device for interacting with 3D environments in augmented and virtual reality. They provide a more intuitive and natural way of controlling objects, and can improve efficiency and productivity for a variety of tasks.
