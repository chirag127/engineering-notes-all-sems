### Tools for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

1. **Pen and Paper:** The traditional method of taking notes is still an effective way to capture important information. Use a pen and paper to write down the key concepts, terms, and techniques discussed in the lectures.

2. **Microsoft OneNote:** OneNote is a digital note-taking application that allows you to organize your notes by creating notebooks, sections, and pages. You can type, draw, and insert images and videos into your notes. OneNote also offers a feature to search for specific keywords within your notes.

3. **Evernote:** Evernote is another popular digital note-taking application that allows you to take notes, organize them, and share them with others. You can create notebooks, tags, and notes, and add images, audio, and video files to your notes. Evernote also offers a feature to search for specific keywords within your notes.

4. **Google Keep:** Google Keep is a simple note-taking application that allows you to create notes and reminders. You can add images, checklists, and voice memos to your notes. Google Keep also allows you to synchronize your notes across multiple devices.

5. **Notability:** Notability is a note-taking application designed for the iPad and iPhone. It allows you to take handwritten notes, type notes, and annotate PDFs. You can also record audio and add images to your notes. Notability also offers a feature to search for specific keywords within your notes.

6. **Trello:** Trello is a project management tool that can be used for note-taking as well. You can create boards, lists, and cards to organize your notes. You can also attach files, add labels, and set due dates to your notes. Trello also offers a feature to search for specific keywords within your notes.

7. **MindMeister:** MindMeister is a mind-mapping tool that allows you to create visual diagrams of your notes. You can create nodes, branches, and subtopics to organize your ideas. MindMeister also allows you to share your mind maps with others and collaborate on them in real-time.
