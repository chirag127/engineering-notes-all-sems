### Design Guidelines for the notes of the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

When designing notes for the Unit 5 - 3D INTERACTION TECHNIQUES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality, it is important to keep in mind the following design guidelines:

1. Use clear and concise language: The notes should be easy to read and understand. Use simple and straightforward language to explain the concepts and techniques.

2. Use headings and subheadings: Divide the notes into sections using headings and subheadings. This will make it easier for students to navigate through the notes and find the information they need.

3. Use bullet points and lists: Break down complex concepts into smaller, more manageable pieces using bullet points and lists. This will help students to remember the information more easily.

4. Use diagrams and illustrations: Visual aids such as diagrams and illustrations can be very helpful in explaining 3D interaction techniques. Use them to supplement the text and make the notes more engaging.

5. Provide examples: Provide examples of how the techniques are used in real-world applications. This will help students to understand the practical applications of what they are learning.

6. Include references: Include references to external sources such as books, articles, and websites. This will provide students with additional resources to explore if they want to learn more about a particular topic.

7. Format the notes consistently: Use a consistent format throughout the notes. This will make them easier to read and understand.

By following these design guidelines, you can create notes that are clear, concise, and easy to understand. This will help students to grasp the concepts and techniques of 3D interaction in Augmented & Virtual Reality more effectively.
