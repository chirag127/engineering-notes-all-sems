### Objects - Geometry

Geometry plays a crucial role in the creation of objects in the field of Augmented and Virtual Reality. It is the foundation on which 3D models are built, and it is essential to understand the different types of geometry used in this field. In this section, we will discuss the various types of geometry used in AR/VR.

1. **Points:** A point is the most basic element of geometry. It is a location in space and has no size or shape. In AR/VR, points are used to define the position of objects in the virtual world.

2. **Lines:** A line is formed by connecting two or more points. It has length but no width or depth. In AR/VR, lines are used to create wireframes, which are used as the basis for 3D models.

3. **Polygons:** A polygon is a flat, two-dimensional shape with straight sides. It is formed by connecting three or more points with lines. In AR/VR, polygons are used to create the surfaces of 3D models.

4. **Meshes:** A mesh is a collection of polygons that form a 3D shape. It is the most common type of geometry used in AR/VR, as it allows for complex shapes to be created.

5. **Curves:** A curve is a smooth, continuous line with no sharp corners. In AR/VR, curves are used to create organic shapes like the human body or other natural objects.

6. **Splines:** A spline is a curve that is defined by a set of control points. In AR/VR, splines are used to create smooth, curved surfaces.

7. **NURBS:** Non-Uniform Rational B-Splines (NURBS) are a type of curve that is commonly used in CAD software. In AR/VR, NURBS are used to create complex, organic shapes.

In conclusion, understanding the different types of geometry used in AR/VR is essential for creating 3D models and virtual environments. By mastering these concepts, you can create realistic and immersive experiences for your users.
