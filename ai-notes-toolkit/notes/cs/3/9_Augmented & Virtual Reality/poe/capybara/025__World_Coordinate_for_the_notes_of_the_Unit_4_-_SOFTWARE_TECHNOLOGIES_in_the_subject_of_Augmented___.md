### World Coordinate for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGIES in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

Here are some key points to understand about world coordinates in augmented and virtual reality:

- World coordinate system is used to represent the position and orientation of objects in a virtual or augmented reality scene.
- It is a 3D Cartesian coordinate system, where the origin is usually set at the center of the virtual world.
- The x, y and z axes represent the right, up and forward directions respectively.
- The units used in the world coordinate system can vary depending on the application, but meters are commonly used.
- World coordinates are used to position and orient objects in the scene, such as virtual cameras, lights and 3D models.
- When an object is positioned using world coordinates, its position and orientation is relative to the origin of the world coordinate system.
- Transformations such as translation, rotation and scaling can be applied to objects using world coordinates to achieve desired effects.
- World coordinates are often used in conjunction with other coordinate systems, such as camera coordinates or object coordinates, to create complex scenes.
- Understanding world coordinates is essential for developing augmented and virtual reality applications, as it forms the foundation for object placement and movement within the scene.

In summary, world coordinates are a fundamental concept in augmented and virtual reality, used to represent the position and orientation of objects in a 3D Cartesian coordinate system. They are essential for developing complex scenes and creating immersive experiences for users.
