### Taxonomy for the notes of the Unit 8 - Augmented and Mixed Reality in the subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

In order to understand the concepts related to Augmented and Mixed Reality, it is important to have a clear understanding of the taxonomy that is used. Here are the key points related to the taxonomy for the notes of Unit 8:

- Augmented Reality (AR) refers to the overlay of digital content onto the real world. This can be done through various technologies such as smartphones, tablets, and AR glasses.
- Mixed Reality (MR) is similar to AR but it overlays digital content in a more interactive way. This can involve the use of sensors and other technologies to create a more immersive experience.
- Virtual Reality (VR) is a completely digital experience where the user is immersed in a virtual environment. This is typically done through the use of VR headsets.
- Spatial Computing is a term used to describe the way that AR, MR, and VR technologies are bringing digital content into the physical world in a more natural way.
- Wearable technology refers to any technology that can be worn on the body. This includes devices like smartwatches, fitness trackers, and AR glasses.
- Holographic technology is a type of MR technology that uses light to create the illusion of a 3D object in space. This is often used in medical and engineering applications.
- Computer Vision is a technology that enables computers to interpret and understand visual information from the world around them. This is a crucial technology for AR and MR applications.
- 3D modeling is the process of creating a digital model of a real-world object or environment. This is often used in AR and MR applications to create realistic digital content.

By understanding these key concepts related to the taxonomy of Augmented and Mixed Reality, you will be better equipped to understand the concepts covered in Unit 8.
