### Scientific Landmarks for the Notes of the Unit 1 - VIRTUAL REALITY AND VIRTUAL ENVIRONMENTS in the Subject of Augmented & Virtual Reality

In the field of Augmented & Virtual Reality, various scientific landmarks have been achieved over the years. These landmarks have paved the way for further development of Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments. Here are some of the most significant scientific landmarks that you should know:

- **Sensorama (1962)**: Sensorama was the first virtual reality machine, which was invented by Morton Heilig in 1962. It was a simulator that used a combination of visuals, sounds, vibrations, and smells to create an immersive experience for the user.

- **The Sword of Damocles (1968)**: The Sword of Damocles was the first virtual reality head-mounted display (HMD), which was developed by Ivan Sutherland in 1968. It was a bulky and heavy device that used a computer to generate 3D graphics.

- **The Aspen Movie Map (1978)**: The Aspen Movie Map was the first interactive virtual environment, which was developed by a team at MIT in 1978. It was a computer program that allowed users to navigate through a virtual representation of Aspen, Colorado.

- **The Virtual Boy (1995)**: The Virtual Boy was a portable gaming console that used a stereoscopic 3D display to create a virtual reality experience. It was developed and released by Nintendo in 1995, but it was not successful due to its high price and limited game library.

- **The Oculus Rift (2012)**: The Oculus Rift was a virtual reality headset that was developed by Palmer Luckey and his team at Oculus VR in 2012. It was designed for gaming and entertainment purposes and became one of the most popular virtual reality headsets.

- **The HoloLens (2016)**: The HoloLens was a mixed reality headset that was developed by Microsoft in 2016. It allowed users to see and interact with virtual objects in the real world, which made it ideal for applications in fields like medicine and engineering.

These scientific landmarks have played a significant role in the development of Virtual Reality and Virtual Environments. By knowing about these landmarks, you can gain a deeper understanding of the history and evolution of this field.
