### 3D Mice

- 3D mice are input devices that allow users to manipulate 3D objects in a virtual environment with greater precision and control.
- They are designed to work with 3D modeling and CAD software, making it easier for designers to create complex shapes and models.
- These mice typically have a range of buttons, dials, and other controls that allow users to manipulate objects in 3D space.
- Some 3D mice also feature haptic feedback, which can provide tactile feedback as users interact with virtual objects.
- 3D mice can be used in a variety of industries, including architecture, engineering, and product design.
- They are also commonly used in virtual reality applications, where they allow users to interact with virtual objects in a more natural and intuitive way.
- Some examples of popular 3D mice include the SpaceMouse from 3Dconnexion and the CadMouse from the same company.
- When selecting a 3D mouse, it is important to consider factors such as compatibility with your software, the number and type of controls, and the overall ergonomics of the device.
