## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication

Real time communication is the exchange of information between two or more parties without significant delay. It can be synchronous or asynchronous, depending on the mode and timing of the communication.

Some examples of real time communication are:

- Voice calls
- Video calls
- Instant messaging
- Live streaming
- Online gaming
- Collaborative editing

Some benefits of real time communication are:

- It allows for immediate feedback and interaction
- It enhances social presence and engagement
- It reduces misunderstandings and errors
- It improves productivity and efficiency
- It supports creativity and innovation

Some challenges of real time communication are:

- It requires reliable and fast network connectivity
- It consumes more bandwidth and resources
- It may cause privacy and security issues
- It may cause distraction and interruption
- It may cause technical difficulties and compatibility issues

Some strategies to overcome the challenges of real time communication are:

- Use appropriate tools and platforms that suit the purpose and context of the communication
- Establish clear and respectful communication norms and etiquette
- Manage the communication frequency and duration
- Protect the communication data and devices
- Test and troubleshoot the communication system before and during the communication
