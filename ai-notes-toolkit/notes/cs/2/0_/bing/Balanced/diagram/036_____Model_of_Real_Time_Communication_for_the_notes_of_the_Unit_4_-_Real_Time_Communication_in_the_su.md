### Model of Real Time Communication

- Real time communication (RTC) is any live telecommunications method in which all users can interact in a live capacity, with negligible latency  .
- RTC can be classified into two types: real time traffic and real time control.
- Real time traffic means isochronous or synchronous traffic, consisting of a stream of messages that are generated by their sources and delivered to their respective destinations on a continuous basis.
- Real time traffic includes periodic, aperiodic and sporadic messages.
- Periodic messages are generated at regular intervals, such as audio or video data.
- Aperiodic messages are generated at irregular intervals, such as alarms or events.
- Sporadic messages are generated at random times, such as user inputs or commands.
- Real time control means the coordination and synchronization of the activities of different entities involved in the communication process, such as hosts, routers, switches, etc.
- Real time control includes functions such as admission control, scheduling, routing, congestion control, error control, etc.
- In the model of real time communication, end users of the message application systems are sources and destinations residing in different hosts .
- The network interface of each host contains input queue and output queue .
- Two buffer areas called input/output buffer are allocated to input and output queue to store queuing information .
- The messages are transmitted from the source host to the destination host through a network of routers and links .
- The network can be wired or wireless, and can have different topologies and protocols .
- The performance of real time communication can be measured by parameters such as throughput, delay and jitter .
- Throughput is the amount of data that can be transmitted or received per unit time .
- Delay is the time taken for a message to travel from the source to the destination .
- Jitter is the variation in delay among different messages .
- The goal of real time communication is to achieve high throughput, low delay and low jitter for the messages .
- The challenges of real time communication are the unpredictability and variability of the network conditions, such as traffic load, link failures, congestion, etc .
- The solutions for real time communication are the use of appropriate algorithms and protocols for real time traffic and real time control, such as reservation, prioritization, segmentation, retransmission, etc .
