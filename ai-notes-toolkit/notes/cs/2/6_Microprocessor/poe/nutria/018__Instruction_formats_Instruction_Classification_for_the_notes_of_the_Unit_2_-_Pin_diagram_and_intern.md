
### Instruction formats Instruction Classification for the notes of the Unit 2 - Pin diagram and internal architecture of 8085 microprocessor, registers, ALU, Control & Status, Interrupt and Machine Cycle

* Instruction Sets: These are the instructions which are used to perform various operations on the microprocessor. These instructions are written in assembly language and are stored in the memory.

* Addressing Modes: Addressing modes are the ways in which the microprocessor can access memory. The 8085 microprocessor supports five addressing modes - direct, indirect, immediate, register, and implied.

* Instruction Formats: Instruction formats are the layout of the instruction which specifies the type of operation to be performed by the microprocessor. The 8085 microprocessor supports three instruction formats - one-byte, two-byte, and three-byte.

* Instruction Classification: Instruction classification is the categorization of instructions into different categories depending on the type of operation they perform. The 8085 microprocessor supports six instruction classes - data transfer, arithmetic operations, logical operations, branching operations, machine control and assembler directives.
