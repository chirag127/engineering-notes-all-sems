### Logical Operations

The 8085 microprocessor is capable of performing logical operations. Logical operations involve manipulation of binary data, which is essential in digital circuitry. The 8085 microprocessor has several instructions that enable performing logical operations.

Some of the logical operations that can be performed by the 8085 microprocessor are:

- **AND operation:** The AND operation performs a logical AND between two binary numbers. The result is 1 if both the bits are 1, else it is 0. The instruction for performing the AND operation is `ANA`.

- **OR operation:** The OR operation performs a logical OR between two binary numbers. The result is 1 if either of the bits is 1, else it is 0. The instruction for performing the OR operation is `ORA`.

- **XOR operation:** The XOR operation performs a logical XOR between two binary numbers. The result is 1 if only one of the bits is 1, else it is 0. The instruction for performing the XOR operation is `XRA`.

- **NOT operation:** The NOT operation performs a logical NOT on a binary number. The result is the complement of the binary number. The instruction for performing the NOT operation is `CMA`.

- **Rotate operations:** The rotate operations perform a circular shift of the bits in a binary number. The 8085 microprocessor has four different rotate instructions: `RLC`, `RRC`, `RAL`, and `RAR`.

- **Compare operation:** The compare operation compares two binary numbers and sets the appropriate flags. The instruction for performing the compare operation is `CMP`.

Logical operations are essential for performing various tasks in digital circuitry. The 8085 microprocessor's logical instructions enable the manipulation of binary data, which is essential for data processing.
