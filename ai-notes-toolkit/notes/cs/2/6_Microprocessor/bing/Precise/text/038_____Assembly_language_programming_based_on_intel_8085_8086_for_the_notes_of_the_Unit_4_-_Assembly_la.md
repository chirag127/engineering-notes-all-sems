### Assembly language programming based on intel 8085/8086

Unit 4 of the subject Microprocessor KCS covers Assembly language programming based on intel 8085/8086. The following topics are included in this unit:

1. **Instructions**: Assembly language instructions are used to perform operations on data. These instructions are specific to the microprocessor and are executed by the microprocessor's control unit.

2. **Data transfer**: Data transfer instructions are used to move data between registers, memory, and input/output devices.

3. **Arithmetic**: Arithmetic instructions are used to perform mathematical operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

4. **Logic**: Logic instructions are used to perform logical operations such as AND, OR, XOR, and NOT.

5. **Branch operations**: Branch operations are used to change the sequence of instructions being executed by the microprocessor.

6. **Looping, counting, indexing**: Looping, counting, and indexing are techniques used to repeat a sequence of instructions a specific number of times or to access data stored in memory.

7. **Programming techniques**: Programming techniques are methods used to write efficient and effective assembly language programs.

8. **Counters and time delays**: Counters and time delays are used to control the timing of events in a microprocessor-based system.

9. **Stacks and subroutines**: Stacks and subroutines are used to manage the flow of control in a program and to store data temporarily.

10. **Conditional call and return instructions**: Conditional call and return instructions are used to execute a subroutine conditionally based on the result of a previous operation.

These topics provide a comprehensive understanding of Assembly language programming based on intel 8085/8086. It is important to study and understand these concepts in order to write efficient and effective assembly language programs.
