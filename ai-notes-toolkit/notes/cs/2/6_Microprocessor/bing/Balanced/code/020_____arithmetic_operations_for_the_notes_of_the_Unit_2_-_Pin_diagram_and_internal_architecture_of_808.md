### Arithmetic Operations

Arithmetic operations are the instructions that perform basic mathematical operations on the data stored in the registers or memory locations of the 8085 microprocessor. The 8085 microprocessor supports four types of arithmetic operations: addition, subtraction, increment, and decrement. The following points summarize the main features of these operations:

- Addition: The 8085 microprocessor can perform addition of two 8-bit numbers, one 8-bit number and a 16-bit number, or two 16-bit numbers. The result of the addition is stored in the accumulator or the destination register pair. The 8085 microprocessor also sets or resets the flags according to the result of the addition. The mnemonics for addition are ADD, ADC, ADI, ACI, DAD, and DAA.
- Subtraction: The 8085 microprocessor can perform subtraction of two 8-bit numbers, one 8-bit number and a 16-bit number, or two 16-bit numbers. The result of the subtraction is stored in the accumulator or the destination register pair. The 8085 microprocessor also sets or resets the flags according to the result of the subtraction. The mnemonics for subtraction are SUB, SBB, SUI, SBI, and DCR.
- Increment: The 8085 microprocessor can perform increment of an 8-bit number or a 16-bit number by one. The result of the increment is stored in the same register or memory location. The 8085 microprocessor also sets or resets the flags according to the result of the increment. The mnemonics for increment are INR and INX.
- Decrement: The 8085 microprocessor can perform decrement of an 8-bit number or a 16-bit number by one. The result of the decrement is stored in the same register or memory location. The 8085 microprocessor also sets or resets the flags according to the result of the decrement. The mnemonics for decrement are DCR and DCX.
