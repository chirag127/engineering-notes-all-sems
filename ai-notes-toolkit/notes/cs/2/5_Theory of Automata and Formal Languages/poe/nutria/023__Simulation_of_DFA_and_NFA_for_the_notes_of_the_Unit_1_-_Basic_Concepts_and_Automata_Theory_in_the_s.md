
### Simulation of DFA and NFA

* Deterministic Finite Automata (DFA) is a type of finite automaton that has a finite set of states and transitions between those states that are uniquely determined by the current state and input symbol. 
* Non-deterministic Finite Automata (NFA) is a type of finite automaton that has a finite set of states and transitions between those states that are not uniquely determined by the current state and input symbol. 
* A DFA can be simulated by an NFA by introducing ε-transitions.
* A NFA can be simulated by a DFA by introducing multiple states in the DFA to represent a single state in the NFA.
* The simulation of DFA and NFA is important for the understanding of the basic concepts of automata theory.
