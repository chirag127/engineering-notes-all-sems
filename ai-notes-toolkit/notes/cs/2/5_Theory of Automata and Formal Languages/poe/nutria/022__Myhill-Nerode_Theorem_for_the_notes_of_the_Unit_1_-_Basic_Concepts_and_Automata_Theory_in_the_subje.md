
### Myhill-Nerode Theorem

Myhill-Nerode Theorem is an important result in the field of Automata Theory and Formal Languages. It states that two languages are equivalent if and only if their corresponding minimal deterministic finite automata (DFA) have the same number of states.

The theorem is useful in proving that two languages are not equivalent, since it can be used to show that the minimal DFA for each language have different numbers of states.

The theorem can also be used to prove that a language is regular, since it can be used to show that the minimal DFA for the language has a finite number of states.
