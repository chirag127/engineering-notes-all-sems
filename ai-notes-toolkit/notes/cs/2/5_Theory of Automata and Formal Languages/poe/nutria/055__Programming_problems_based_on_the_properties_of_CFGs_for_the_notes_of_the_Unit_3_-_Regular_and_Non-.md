
### Programming problems based on the properties of CFGs 

1. Explain the difference between a regular grammar and a non-regular grammar. 
2. Describe the structure of a context-free grammar (CFG).
3. How can a CFG be used to define a language?
4. What is the pumping lemma for context-free languages?
5. How can the pumping lemma be used to prove that a language is not context-free?
6. What is a parse tree?
7. What is the difference between a regular expression and a CFG?
8. Give an example of a CFG that defines a language that is not regular.
9. Describe the Chomsky hierarchy of languages.
10. How can a CFG be used to parse a string?
