
### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

* Automata Theory is a branch of computer science that deals with the study of abstract machines (automata) and their properties.
* Formal Languages are languages that are defined by a set of rules and symbols.
* Finite Automata are machines that can be in only one of a finite number of states at any given time.
* Regular Expressions are used to describe regular languages, which are languages that can be expressed in terms of a set of finite automata.
* Pushdown Automata are machines that use a stack to store information and can recognize a wider range of languages than finite automata.
* Turing Machines are machines that can be used to simulate any other machine, and can recognize any language that can be expressed as a set of rules.
