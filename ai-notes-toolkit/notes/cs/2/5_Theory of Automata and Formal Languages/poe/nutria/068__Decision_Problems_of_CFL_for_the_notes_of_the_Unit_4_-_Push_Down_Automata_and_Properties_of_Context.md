
### Decision Problems of CFL 

1. The **Emptiness Problem** of CFL is to determine if a given CFL is empty or not.
2. The **Finiteness Problem** of CFL is to determine if a given CFL is finite or not.
3. The **Membership Problem** of CFL is to determine if a given string is a member of a given CFL or not.
4. The **Equivalence Problem** of CFL is to determine if two given CFLs are equivalent or not.
5. The **Coverability Problem** of CFL is to determine if a given CFL is coverable or not.
6. The **Inclusion Problem** of CFL is to determine if one CFL is a subset of another CFL or not.
7. The **Intersection Problem** of CFL is to determine the intersection of two given CFLs.
8. The **Minimization Problem** of CFL is to determine the minimum equivalent CFL of a given CFL.
