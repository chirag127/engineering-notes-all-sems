
### Definition for the notes of the Unit 4 - Push Down Automata and Properties of Context Free Languages in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

* Push Down Automata (PDA): A PDA is a finite state machine that can manipulate a stack of symbols. It is used to recognize strings in a context-free language.

* Properties of Context Free Languages: 
    * Closure under union: The union of two context-free languages is also a context-free language. 
    * Closure under concatenation: The concatenation of two context-free languages is also a context-free language. 
    * Closure under Kleene star: The Kleene star of a context-free language is also a context-free language. 
    * Pumping Lemma for Context Free Languages: The pumping lemma for context-free languages states that if a string is in a context-free language, then it can be broken down into three parts, and the middle part can be repeated an arbitrary number of times.
