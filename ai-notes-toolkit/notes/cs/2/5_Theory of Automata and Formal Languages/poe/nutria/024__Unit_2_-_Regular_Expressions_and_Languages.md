
## Unit 2 - Regular Expressions and Languages

1. Regular Expressions (regex) are a powerful tool used for pattern matching and text processing.
2. A regex is a sequence of characters that define a search pattern.
3. Regex can be used to search for patterns in strings, words, sentences, and entire documents.
4. Regex can also be used to validate input, such as email addresses and phone numbers.
5. Languages are sets of symbols, words, and rules for constructing meaningful sentences.
6. Languages can be formal or informal, natural or artificial.
7. Formal languages are used in programming and mathematical logic, while natural languages are spoken and written.
8. Regular expressions are used to describe patterns in languages, such as the syntax of a programming language.
9. Regular expressions can be used to match strings, words, and phrases in natural language.
10. Regular expressions can also be used to search for patterns in text, such as searching for specific words or phrases.
