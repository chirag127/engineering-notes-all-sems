### Simulation of Transition Graph and Regular language

- A finite state machine can be represented using a transition graph. In a transition graph,
- States are represented by nodes or circles.
- Transitions are represented by directed edges.
- The start state is represented by an arrow pointing to it or an edge from a blank node.
- Accepting or final states are represented by double circles.
- A regular expression can be converted into an equivalent transition graph and vice-versa.
- The language accepted by a transition graph is a regular language.
- A regular language can be represented using regular expressions.
- The regular expressions can be converted into transition graphs and vice-versa.
- This forms an equivalence between regular expressions and transition graphs.
- Regular languages are the set of strings that can be described by a regular expression.
- They are accepted by finite state machines or recognized by transition graphs.
