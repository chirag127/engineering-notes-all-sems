### Introduction to Theory of Computation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

1. Theory of Computation: Theory of Computation is a branch of computer science and mathematics that studies what problems can be solved using computational devices and what resources are required to solve them. It tries to understand the power and limitations of computational devices.
2. Models of Computation: In order to study computation, we need to define abstract machines or models of computation that give a framework for the types of problems we want to solve. Some of the basic models of computation are:
- Finite State Machines
- Pushdown Automata
- Turing Machines
3. Formal Languages: In theory of computation, we study languages that can be recognized or generated by computational models. These languages are called formal languages and are defined in a precise mathematical way. We study various classes of formal languages and the types of automata that can recognize them.
4. Applications: Theory of computation has a wide range of applications in various fields like software engineering, artificial intelligence, cryptography, etc. It helps us understand what can and cannot be computed, leading to developments in algorithm design and computational complexity.
