### Recursive and Recursively Enumerable language for the notes of the Unit 5 - Turing Machines and Recursive Function Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

1. A language L is said to be Recursive if there exists a Turing Machine M such that L(M) = L. In other words, the language L can be accepted by some Turing Machine.
2. A language L is said to be Recursively Enumerable if there exists a Turing Machine M such that L(M) ??? L. In other words, the language L can be generated by some Turing Machine.
3. Every Recursive language is also Recursively Enumerable but the converse is not true.
4. The languages generated by Type-0 grammar and the languages accepted by Type-0 grammar are same and they are the collection of Recursively Enumerable languages.
5. The languages generated by Type-1 grammar and the languages accepted by Type-1 grammar are same and they are the collection of Recursive languages.
6. Whether a language is Recursive or just Recursively Enumerable is undecidable. There is no Turing Machine which takes as input, another Turing Machine M and decides whether the language accepted by M is Recursive or just Recursively Enumerable. This is known as Rice's Theorem.
