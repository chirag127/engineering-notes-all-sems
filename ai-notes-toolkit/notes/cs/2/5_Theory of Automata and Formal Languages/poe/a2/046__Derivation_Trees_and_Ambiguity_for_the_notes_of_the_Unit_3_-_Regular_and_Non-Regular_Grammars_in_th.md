### Derivation Trees and Ambiguity for the notes of the Unit 3 - Regular and Non-Regular Grammars

1. Derivation Trees: Derivation trees are a visual representation of the sequence of derivations of a string from the start symbol of a grammar. They show how a string is generated from the start symbol by applying production rules one after the other.

2. Ambiguity: A grammar is ambiguous if there is more than one possible derivation tree for some string. Ambiguity must be avoided in grammars as it leads to confusion in interpretation.

3. Properties:
    - Every grammar has at least one derivation tree for the start symbol.
    - A grammar is ambiguous if there are two or more distinct derivation trees for the same string.
    - A grammar is unambiguous if there is at most one derivation tree for every string.

4. Eliminating Ambiguity: Some ways to eliminate ambiguity from a grammar are:
    - Omitting useless symbols and productions
    - Grouping symbols in productions
    - Adding more productions
    - Reordering components in productions
