### Languages for the notes of the Unit 3 - Regular and Non-Regular Grammars

1. Regular Languages:
- Languages accepted by finite state machines (FSMs) or automata.
- Have a regular expression that can denote the language.
- Examples: {a^n b^n | n >= 0}, {0^n 1^n | n >= 0}.

2. Context Free Languages:
- Generated by context free grammars (CFGs).
- Have production rules to derive strings.
- Examples: {a^n b^n c^n | n >= 0}, {w#w|w is a string with an even number of a's}.

3. Recursive Languages:
- Languages that can contain self-references.
- Not accepted by FSMs or generated by CFGs.
- Examples: {ww|w is a string with at least two a's}, {a^n b^n c^n | n is a prime number}.

4. Turing-Decidable Languages:
- Languages whose membership can be decided by a Turing machine.
- Class of recursively enumerable languages.
- Examples: All regular and context free languages.
