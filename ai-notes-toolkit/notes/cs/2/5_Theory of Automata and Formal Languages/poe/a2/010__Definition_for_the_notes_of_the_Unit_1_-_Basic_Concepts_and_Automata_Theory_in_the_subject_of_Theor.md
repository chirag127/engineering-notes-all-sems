### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory in the subject of Theory of Automata and Formal Languages

1. Automata: Automata refers to abstract self-propelled computing devices that follow a predetermined sequence of actions to process input strings. They are used to recognize patterns or languages.
2. Finite Automata: Finite Automata consists of a finite number of states and transitions between those states. They can only process finite length inputs. They are used to recognize regular languages.
3. Regular Languages: Regular languages are those languages that can be recognized by Finite Automata. They have a definite pattern in the form of sequences of characters. Examples include languages like (a|b)*abb(a|b)*, etc.
4. Transition Function: The transition function determines the next state based on the current state and input. It is used in automata to transition between states.
5. Accepting States: Accepting states are those states of an automata wherein if the automata enters those states after processing the complete input, then the input is accepted. Else, the input is rejected.
