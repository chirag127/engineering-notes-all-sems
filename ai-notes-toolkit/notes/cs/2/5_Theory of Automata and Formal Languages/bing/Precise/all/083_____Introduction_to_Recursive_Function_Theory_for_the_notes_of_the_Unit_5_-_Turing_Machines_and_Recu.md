# Introduction to Recursive Function Theory

Recursive function theory is a branch of mathematical logic and computer science that studies the properties of computable functions. It is closely related to the study of Turing machines and the concept of algorithmic computability.

Here are some key points to understand about recursive function theory:

1. A function is said to be computable if there exists an algorithm that can compute its values for all possible inputs.
2. The class of computable functions is also known as the class of recursive functions.
3. The study of recursive functions is important for understanding the limits of computation and the nature of computability.
4. Recursive function theory is closely related to the study of formal languages and automata theory.
5. One of the central results of recursive function theory is the existence of non-computable functions, which are functions that cannot be computed by any algorithm.
6. Recursive function theory also studies the properties of various classes of computable functions, such as primitive recursive functions and partial recursive functions.

This is just a brief introduction to recursive function theory. It is a fascinating and important subject with many deep and interesting results. I hope this overview has piqued your interest and encouraged you to learn more about this fascinating subject.
