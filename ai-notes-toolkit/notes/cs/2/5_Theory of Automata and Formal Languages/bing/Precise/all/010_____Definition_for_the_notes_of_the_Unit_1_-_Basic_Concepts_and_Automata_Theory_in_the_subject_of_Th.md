# Unit 1 - Basic Concepts and Automata Theory

### Definition

- **Automata Theory** is a branch of computer science that deals with the study of abstract machines and the computational problems that can be solved using them.
- **Formal Languages** are sets of strings of symbols that are constructed according to specific rules.
- **Automata** are abstract machines that can be used to recognize formal languages.
- **Finite Automata** are a type of automaton that has a finite number of states and can recognize regular languages.
- **Regular Languages** are a subset of formal languages that can be recognized by finite automata.
- **Context-Free Languages** are a subset of formal languages that can be recognized by pushdown automata.
- **Pushdown Automata** are a type of automaton that has a stack and can recognize context-free languages.
- **Turing Machines** are a type of automaton that can recognize recursively enumerable languages.
- **Recursively Enumerable Languages** are a subset of formal languages that can be recognized by Turing machines.
