### Formal Languages

- A formal language is a set of strings over a finite alphabet.
- A formal language can be defined by a grammar, a regular expression, or a finite automaton.
- A formal language can be classified into different classes based on its expressive power and computational complexity.
- Some common classes of formal languages are regular languages, context-free languages, context-sensitive languages, and recursively enumerable languages.
- Each class of formal languages has its own properties, closure properties, and decision problems.
- Each class of formal languages is associated with a class of abstract machines that can recognize or generate the language.
- Some common abstract machines are finite automata, pushdown automata, linear bounded automata, and Turing machines.
- The study of formal languages and automata theory helps to understand the limitations and capabilities of different models of computation.
