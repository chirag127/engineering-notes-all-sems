### Simplification of CFG

- Simplification of CFG is the process of removing certain productions and symbols that are not needed for the derivation of strings in the language generated by the CFG .
- Simplification of CFG is necessary to later convert them into normal forms, such as Chomsky normal form or Greibach normal form.
- Simplification of CFG consists of the following steps :
  - Removal of useless productions: These are the productions that can never take part in the derivation of any string, such as unreachable productions and non-terminating productions.
  - Removal of null productions: These are the productions of the form A -> ε, where A is a non-terminal and ε is the empty string.
  - Removal of unit productions: These are the productions of the form A -> B, where A and B are non-terminals.
  - Removal of equivalent productions: These are the productions that have the same right-hand side, such as A -> α and B -> α.
- Simplification of CFG does not change the language generated by the CFG, i.e., the simplified CFG is equivalent to the original CFG .
