
### Time Services for the Notes of Unit 5 - Real Time Operating Systems and Databases 

1. Time services are used to provide a consistent measure of time for applications running on an operating system. 
2. Time services are especially important for real-time systems, which require precise timing and synchronization of events. 
3. Time services typically provide functions for setting, retrieving, and manipulating the system clock. 
4. Time services also provide the ability to set alarms, schedule tasks to occur at a certain time, and measure the amount of time that has elapsed between two events. 
5. Database systems also use time services to ensure that data is consistent across multiple machines. 
6. Time services are typically implemented as a kernel module or library, and can be configured to use a variety of time sources, such as an external time server or a GPS receiver.
