
### Soft and Hard RT Communication Systems

* Soft Real-Time (SRT) communication systems are those which have a certain amount of flexibility in the timing of their operations. The system can tolerate some delays in the execution of operations, and still be able to complete the task successfully.
* Hard Real-Time (HRT) communication systems are those which require that the operations be executed within a strict time limit. If the operations are not completed within the specified time limit, then the task may not be completed successfully.
* In order to ensure that the operations are completed within the specified time limit, HRT systems must be designed to be fault-tolerant and able to handle any possible errors or delays that may occur.
* In order to ensure that the operations are completed within the specified time limit, HRT systems must use reliable communication protocols and techniques, such as message passing and time synchronization.
* HRT systems must also be able to handle large amounts of data and process it quickly in order to ensure that the operations are completed within the specified time limit.
* HRT systems must also be able to handle high levels of traffic, as they must be able to handle large numbers of requests and responses in a timely manner.
