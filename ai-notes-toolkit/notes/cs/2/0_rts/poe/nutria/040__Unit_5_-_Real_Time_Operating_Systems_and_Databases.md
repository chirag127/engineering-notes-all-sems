
## Unit 5 - Real Time Operating Systems and Databases

1. Real-time operating systems (RTOS) are specialized operating systems designed to serve real-time applications that require a predictable response time. RTOSs typically have small footprints, low overhead, and are designed to provide a deterministic response time. 

2. RTOSs are designed to handle multiple tasks simultaneously and provide a predictable response time for each task. They are commonly used for embedded systems, such as those found in medical and industrial devices, as well as for critical applications in aerospace, automotive and military systems.

3. Databases are a collection of data organized in a structured format. They are used to store, organize and retrieve data. Databases are used in a variety of applications, such as banking, finance, retail, healthcare, and many more. 

4. Database management systems (DBMS) are software programs that are used to manage databases. DBMSs allow users to create, edit, delete, and query data in a database. They also provide security, backup, and other features to ensure data integrity. 

5. Relational databases are the most common type of database. They are organized into tables, which are composed of rows and columns. Each row represents a single record, and each column represents a particular field or attribute of the record. 

6. NoSQL databases are an alternative to relational databases. They are designed to store data in a flexible and dynamic manner, and they are often used in applications that require fast access to large amounts of data. 

7. Object-oriented databases are a type of database that stores data in the form of objects. They are used in applications that require complex data relationships, such as document management systems.
