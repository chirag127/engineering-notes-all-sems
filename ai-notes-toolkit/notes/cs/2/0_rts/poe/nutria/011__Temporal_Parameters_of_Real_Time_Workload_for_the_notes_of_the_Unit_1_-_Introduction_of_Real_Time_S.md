
### Temporal Parameters of Real Time Workload

1. Real-time systems are systems that must respond to external events within a certain time frame in order to meet their performance requirements.
2. Temporal parameters are the characteristics of a real-time system that define its response time requirements.
3. The temporal parameters of a real-time system include the following:
    * **Deadline**: The maximum amount of time a system has to respond to an external event.
    * **Latency**: The amount of time it takes for a system to respond to an external event.
    * **Periodicity**: The amount of time between successive occurrences of an external event.
    * **Jitter**: The amount of variation in the time between successive occurrences of an external event.
4. The workload of a real-time system is the amount of processing required to respond to external events.
5. The temporal parameters of a real-time system must be taken into account when designing the system in order to ensure that the system is capable of meeting its performance requirements.
