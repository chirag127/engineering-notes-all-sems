
### Model of Real Time Communication

1. Real time communication is a type of communication where time is of the essence. 
2. It is characterized by quick response times and immediate feedback from all parties involved.
3. This type of communication requires a reliable network that can handle high-speed data transfer.
4. Real time communication is used in many applications, such as video conferencing, online gaming, and voice over IP (VoIP).
5. Real time communication is also used in industrial automation, healthcare, and military applications.
6. To ensure reliability, real time communication systems must have low latency, high throughput, and be fault tolerant.
7. Real time communication systems must also be secure, as they are vulnerable to malicious attacks.
8. To ensure security, real time communication systems must use encryption and authentication protocols.
9. Real time communication systems must also be able to handle large numbers of concurrent users.
10. To ensure scalability, real time communication systems must use distributed architectures such as client-server, peer-to-peer, or hybrid models.
