
### Preemption Ceiling Protocol

1. Preemption Ceiling Protocol is a technique used in Real Time Systems to ensure that resources are shared among all tasks in the system.

2. It works by assigning a preemption ceiling to each task, which is an upper limit on the amount of resources that can be allocated to that task.

3. The preemption ceiling is set based on the priority of the task. Tasks with higher priority will have higher preemption ceilings, meaning they can access more resources than tasks with lower priority.

4. This ensures that all tasks in the system have a fair share of resources, and that tasks with higher priority can get access to resources when they need them.

5. Preemption Ceiling Protocol also helps to ensure that tasks with lower priority do not starve for resources, as they will always have access to some resources, even if they are not the highest priority tasks.
