
### Periodic Task Model

* A periodic task is a task that is executed periodically at regular intervals of time.
* It is characterized by a fixed execution time period and an initial release time.
* It is composed of a set of activities that are executed periodically.
* The periodicity of the task is determined by its period and its execution time is determined by its deadline.
* The period of a task is the time interval between successive releases of the task.
* The deadline of a task is the maximum time limit for the completion of the task.
* The execution of a periodic task must take place within its period and before its deadline.
* The execution of a periodic task is triggered by the release of the task.
* The release of the task is the event that initiates the execution of the task.
* The release of the task is usually associated with the arrival of an external event or signal.
* The release of the task may also be triggered by the completion of the execution of a previous instance of the task.
* The scheduling of periodic tasks is a process of assigning the task to a processor for its execution.
* The scheduling of periodic tasks must take into consideration the periodicity of the task and the processor availability.
* The scheduling of periodic tasks can be done using different scheduling algorithms such as Earliest Deadline First (EDF), Rate Monotonic (RM), and Least Laxity First (LLF).
* The scheduling of periodic tasks must also consider the precedence constraints between tasks.
* The scheduling of periodic tasks must also consider the resource requirements of the tasks.
