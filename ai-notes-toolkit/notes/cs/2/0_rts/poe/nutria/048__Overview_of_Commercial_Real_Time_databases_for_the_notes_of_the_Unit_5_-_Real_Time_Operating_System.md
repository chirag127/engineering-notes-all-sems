
### Overview of Commercial Real Time Databases

* Real-time databases are databases designed to provide real-time access to data. They are typically used in applications where data must be retrieved and updated quickly and reliably, such as in financial applications, industrial control systems, and embedded systems.
* Commercial real-time databases are designed to provide high performance and reliable access to data. They are typically used in applications where data must be retrieved and updated quickly and reliably, such as in financial applications, industrial control systems, and embedded systems.
* Real-time databases are designed to provide fast and reliable access to data. They are typically characterized by a high degree of parallelism, low latency, and high throughput.
* Real-time databases are typically implemented using a distributed architecture, allowing data to be spread across multiple nodes. This allows for scalability and fault tolerance.
* Commercial real-time databases typically provide features such as replication, sharding, and high availability. These features allow for increased reliability and scalability.
* The most popular commercial real-time databases include Oracle Real-Time, IBM Informix, and Microsoft SQL Server. Each of these databases has its own strengths and weaknesses, and should be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
