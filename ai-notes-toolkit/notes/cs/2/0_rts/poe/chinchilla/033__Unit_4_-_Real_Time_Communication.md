## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication

Real-time communication is a vital aspect of modern communication technology, enabling people to connect and interact with each other instantly. In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to real-time communication:

- Introduction to Real-Time Communication: This section will provide an overview of real-time communication and its importance in modern communication technology.

- Types of Real-Time Communication: This section will discuss the different types of real-time communication, such as voice, video, and chat.

- Real-Time Communication Protocols: This section will cover the various protocols used in real-time communication, such as WebRTC, SIP, and RTP.

- Real-Time Communication Applications: This section will provide an overview of the various applications that use real-time communication technology, such as video conferencing, instant messaging, and live streaming.

- Real-Time Communication Challenges: This section will discuss the challenges faced in real-time communication, such as network congestion, latency, and security concerns.

- Future of Real-Time Communication: This section will provide insights into the future of real-time communication, including emerging technologies, such as augmented reality and virtual reality.

In conclusion, real-time communication has revolutionized the way people interact and communicate in the modern world. Understanding the different aspects of real-time communication is essential for anyone involved in communication technology, and this unit will provide an in-depth understanding of real-time communication and its applications.
