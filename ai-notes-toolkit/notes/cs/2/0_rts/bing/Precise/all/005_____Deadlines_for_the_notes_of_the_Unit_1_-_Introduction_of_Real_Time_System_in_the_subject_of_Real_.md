### Deadlines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

- A deadline is a specific time or date by which a task must be completed.
- In the context of Real Time Systems, deadlines are critical as they determine the time by which a task must be completed to ensure the correct functioning of the system.
- Deadlines can be hard or soft. A hard deadline is one that must be met, while a soft deadline can be missed without causing significant harm to the system.
- Deadlines are an important aspect of Real Time Systems as they help to ensure that tasks are completed in a timely manner and that the system operates as intended.
- It is important to set realistic deadlines to ensure that tasks can be completed within the given time frame.
- Deadlines can be set by the system designer or determined by the requirements of the system.
- Meeting deadlines is crucial in Real Time Systems as failure to do so can result in system failure or incorrect operation.
- It is important to monitor deadlines and take appropriate action if a deadline is missed, such as rescheduling the task or adjusting the system to compensate for the missed deadline.
- Deadlines are an essential part of the design and operation of Real Time Systems and must be carefully considered and managed to ensure the correct functioning of the system.
