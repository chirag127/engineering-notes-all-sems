### Deadlock

- A deadlock is a situation in which one or more processes are unable to proceed because they are waiting for some resources that are held by other waiting processes  .
- A deadlock can occur in a system that has multiple processes competing for a finite number of resources, such as CPU, memory, disk, printer, etc .
- A deadlock can also occur in a system that has multiple processes communicating with each other through messages, such as in a distributed system.
- A deadlock can cause the system to waste resources and time, and may require manual intervention to resolve.
- A deadlock can be prevented, avoided, detected, or recovered from, depending on the system design and the deadlock handling strategy .
