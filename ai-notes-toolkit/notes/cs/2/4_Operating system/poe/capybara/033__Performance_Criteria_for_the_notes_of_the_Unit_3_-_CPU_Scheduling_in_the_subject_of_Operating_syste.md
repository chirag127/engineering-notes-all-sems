### Performance Criteria for the notes of the Unit 3 - CPU Scheduling in the subject of Operating system

The performance criteria for CPU scheduling notes in the Operating System subject are essential for students to understand the concepts and principles of CPU scheduling. The following are the performance criteria for CPU scheduling notes:

- Understand the concept of CPU scheduling and its importance in the operating system.
- Identify the different types of CPU scheduling algorithms, including First Come First Serve (FCFS), Shortest Job First (SJF), Priority Scheduling, Round Robin Scheduling, and Multilevel Queue Scheduling.
- Explain how each CPU scheduling algorithm works, including their advantages and disadvantages.
- Compare and contrast the different CPU scheduling algorithms to determine which one is best suited for a particular system.
- Analyze the performance metrics of CPU scheduling algorithms, including CPU utilization, throughput, waiting time, and turnaround time.
- Evaluate the performance of CPU scheduling algorithms using simulation tools such as Gantt Charts and Critical Path Method (CPM).
- Evaluate the impact of CPU scheduling on the overall system performance, including the impact on the response time, throughput, and resource utilization.
- Understand the challenges in designing an efficient CPU scheduling algorithm, including the trade-off between fairness and efficiency.
- Understand the impact of multiprogramming, multiprocessing, and multithreading on CPU scheduling.
- Understand the role of real-time systems in CPU scheduling and the importance of meeting deadlines.

By understanding these performance criteria for CPU scheduling notes, students can gain a deep understanding of the concepts and principles of CPU scheduling, which can help them excel in their exams and future careers.
