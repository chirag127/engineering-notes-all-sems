### Performance Criteria for the notes of the Unit 3 - CPU Scheduling in the subject of Operating system

CPU scheduling is a fundamental concept in operating systems that aims to allocate CPU resources among various processes. In this unit, we will study the different scheduling algorithms used in modern operating systems. It is essential to understand the performance criteria for these algorithms to evaluate their suitability for different scenarios. The following are the performance criteria for CPU scheduling:

1. Turnaround time: The turnaround time is the time taken from the arrival of a process to its completion. This includes the time taken for the process to wait in the ready queue and the time taken to execute on the CPU. A good scheduling algorithm should have a low turnaround time as it indicates that processes are completing quickly.

2. Waiting time: The waiting time is the time taken by a process to wait in the ready queue before it gets a chance to execute on the CPU. A good scheduling algorithm should aim to minimize the waiting time as it indicates that the CPU is being utilized efficiently.

3. Response time: The response time is the time taken by a process to start responding after it has been submitted. This includes the waiting time and the time taken to execute on the CPU. A good scheduling algorithm should have a low response time as it indicates that the system is responsive and can handle interactive applications efficiently.

4. Throughput: The throughput is the number of processes that are completed in a given time period. A good scheduling algorithm should aim to maximize the throughput as it indicates that the CPU is being utilized efficiently.

5. Fairness: The fairness is the degree to which the scheduling algorithm allocates CPU resources among different processes fairly. A good scheduling algorithm should aim to be fair as it ensures that all processes get a fair share of the CPU resources.

In conclusion, the performance criteria for CPU scheduling are essential to evaluate the suitability of different scheduling algorithms for different scenarios. Understanding these criteria will help you design efficient and effective scheduling algorithms for operating systems.
