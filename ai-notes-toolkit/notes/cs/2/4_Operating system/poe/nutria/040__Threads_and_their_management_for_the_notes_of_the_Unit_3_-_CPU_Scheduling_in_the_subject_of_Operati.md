
### Threads and their Management

1. Threads are lightweight processes that are used to execute multiple tasks concurrently within a process.
2. Threads are created and managed by the operating system, and each thread has its own program counter, stack, and local variables.
3. CPU scheduling is the process of allocating CPU resources to threads in order to maximize throughput and minimize latency.
4. Threads can be managed in a number of ways, including preemptive and non-preemptive scheduling, priority scheduling, and round-robin scheduling.
5. Threads can also be managed using synchronization techniques such as semaphores, locks, and monitors.
6. Threads can be used to improve the performance of a system by allowing multiple tasks to be executed in parallel.
