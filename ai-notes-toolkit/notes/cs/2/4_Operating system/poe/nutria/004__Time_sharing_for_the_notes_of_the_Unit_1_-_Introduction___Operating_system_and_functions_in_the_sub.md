
### Time Sharing for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction: Operating System and Functions

1. Time-sharing is a method of multitasking in which a computer system can process multiple requests at the same time by sharing its processing power between multiple users.

2. An operating system is a computer program that manages the hardware and software resources of a computer. It is responsible for the allocation of resources and services such as memory, processor time, and disk space.

3. Operating systems can be divided into two categories: single-user and multi-user. Single-user operating systems are designed to be used by one user at a time, while multi-user operating systems allow multiple users to access the system simultaneously.

4. Operating systems provide a number of functions, such as memory management, process scheduling, device management, security, and networking.

5. Memory management is the process of allocating and deallocating memory to processes and applications.

6. Process scheduling is the process of allocating processor time to processes and applications.

7. Device management is the process of managing the hardware devices connected to the computer.

8. Security is the process of protecting the system and its resources from unauthorized access.

9. Networking is the process of connecting the computer to other computers or networks.
