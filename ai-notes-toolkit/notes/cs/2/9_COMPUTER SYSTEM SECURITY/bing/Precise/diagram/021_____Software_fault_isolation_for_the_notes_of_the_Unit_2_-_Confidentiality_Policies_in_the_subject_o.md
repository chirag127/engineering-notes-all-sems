### Software Fault Isolation

Software Fault Isolation (SFI) is a technique used to establish a logical protection domain by inserting dynamic checks before memory and control-transfer instructions. It is part of determining the cause of a problem, also known as “fault diagnosis”. The term may refer to hardware or software, but always deals with methods that can isolate the component, device or software module causing the error.
