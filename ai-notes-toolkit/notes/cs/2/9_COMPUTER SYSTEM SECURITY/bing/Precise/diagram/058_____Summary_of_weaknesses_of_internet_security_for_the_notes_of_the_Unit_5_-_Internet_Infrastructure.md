### Summary of weaknesses of internet security

1. **Lack of a high-level strategy**: Many businesses, especially new and small ones, simply lack a high-level strategy for their cybersecurity needs.
2. **Unsecured networks**: If the network isn’t secured, it’s trivially easy for nefarious parties to gain access to your system.
3. **Unsecured communication channels**: If the business is using unsecured communication channels, it’s easy for cybercriminals to intercept and steal sensitive information.
4. **Unknown bugs**: Unknown bugs in software can be exploited by cybercriminals to compromise an IT resource and advance the attack path.
5. **Outdated systems**: Outdated systems are more vulnerable to cyber attacks as they may not have the latest security patches.
6. **Lack of monitoring**: Lack of monitoring can make it difficult to detect and respond to cyber attacks.
7. **IoT and multiple connection points**: The increasing number of connected devices (IoT) and multiple connection points can increase the attack surface for cybercriminals.
8. **Untrained employees**: Untrained employees can inadvertently compromise the security of the system by falling for phishing scams or not following security protocols.
9. **Misconfigurations**: Misconfigurations are the single largest threat to both cloud and app security.
10. **Unsecured APIs**: Another common security vulnerability is unsecured application programming interfaces (APIs).
