### Basic Terminologies for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Basic Cryptography in the Subject of Computer System Security

1. **Cryptography**: The practice of secure communication in the presence of third parties.
2. **Encryption**: The process of converting plaintext into ciphertext to prevent unauthorized access.
3. **Decryption**: The process of converting ciphertext back into plaintext.
4. **Plaintext**: The original message or data that is fed into the encryption algorithm.
5. **Ciphertext**: The encrypted message or data that is produced by the encryption algorithm.
6. **Key**: A piece of information used by the encryption algorithm to encrypt and decrypt the message.
7. **Symmetric Encryption**: A type of encryption where the same key is used for both encryption and decryption.
8. **Asymmetric Encryption**: A type of encryption where different keys are used for encryption and decryption.
9. **Public Key**: A key that is publicly available and used for encryption in asymmetric encryption.
10. **Private Key**: A key that is kept secret and used for decryption in asymmetric encryption.
11. **Hash Function**: A function that takes an input and produces a fixed-size output, used for verifying the integrity of data.
12. **Digital Signature**: A mathematical scheme for verifying the authenticity of digital messages or documents.
13. **Certificate Authority**: An entity that issues digital certificates to verify the identity of individuals or organizations.
14. **Digital Certificate**: A digital document that verifies the identity of an individual or organization and associates them with a public key.
