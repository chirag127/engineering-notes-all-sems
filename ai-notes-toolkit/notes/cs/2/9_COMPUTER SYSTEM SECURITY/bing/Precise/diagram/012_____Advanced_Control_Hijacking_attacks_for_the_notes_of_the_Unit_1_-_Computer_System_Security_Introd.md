### Advanced Control Hijacking attacks

Advanced Control Hijacking attacks are a type of network security attack in which the attacker takes control of a communication between two entities and masquerades as one of them. These attacks exploit a program error, particularly a memory corruption vulnerability, at application runtime to subvert the intended control flow of a program. A control hijack attack is carried out by overwriting part of the data structures of a victim program, causing it to lose control of its control flow and, as a result, the program's and perhaps the underlying system's control .

#### Defense against Control Hijacking

A variety of defensive mechanisms have been proposed to mitigate control-flow hijacking attacks.

#### Conclusion

In conclusion, Advanced Control Hijacking attacks are a serious threat to network security and various defensive mechanisms have been proposed to mitigate their impact. It is important to stay vigilant and take necessary precautions to prevent such attacks.
