### The Marketplace for vulnerabilities

- Vulnerability is a cyber-security term that refers to a flaw in a system that can leave it open to attack.
- Vulnerability Disclosure Program provides a passive channel for customers and security researchers to report security vulnerabilities on any app in the Marketplace to Atlassian.
- The security and vulnerability management market has been growing rapidly in recent years, driven by the increasing frequency and sophistication of cyber attacks.
- The global Security and Vulnerability Management Market size is projected to grow from USD 13.8 billion in 2021 to USD 18.7 billion by 2026.
- Some of the major companies in the security and vulnerability management market are Strobes Security, IBM, McAfee, Broadcom Inc, Qualys Inc.
