### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

- Computer System Security is the protection of computer systems and networks from theft, damage, and unauthorized access.
- It involves implementing measures to prevent unauthorized access to computer systems, protect against malicious software, and ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data.
- Some common threats to computer system security include viruses, worms, Trojan horses, spyware, adware, and phishing attacks.
- To protect against these threats, it is important to have a strong security policy in place, keep software up to date, use strong passwords, and be cautious when opening emails or clicking on links from unknown sources.
- In addition to technical measures, user education and awareness are also important components of computer system security.
