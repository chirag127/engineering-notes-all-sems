### Computer System Security Introduction

Computer security, also known as cybersecurity or IT security, refers to the protection of computer systems and networks from theft, damage, and unauthorized access. It involves implementing measures to prevent, detect, and respond to security threats.

Here are some key points to learn for Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction:

1. **Threats to computer security:** These can include viruses, worms, Trojan horses, spyware, adware, and other types of malware. Hackers and cybercriminals can also pose a threat by attempting to gain unauthorized access to systems and data.

2. **Types of security measures:** There are various measures that can be implemented to protect computer systems, including firewalls, antivirus software, intrusion detection and prevention systems, and encryption.

3. **Importance of security policies:** Organizations should have security policies in place to define acceptable use of computer systems and to outline procedures for responding to security incidents.

4. **Security awareness:** It is important for users to be aware of security risks and to follow best practices for protecting their systems and data.

5. **Security standards and regulations:** There are various standards and regulations that organizations must comply with to ensure the security of their systems and data. These can include industry-specific regulations, such as HIPAA for healthcare organizations, and general data protection regulations, such as the GDPR.

These are some of the key points to learn for Unit 1 of the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY. It is important to have a thorough understanding of these concepts to ensure the security of computer systems.
