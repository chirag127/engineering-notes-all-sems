### Email security

Email security refers to the measures taken to secure the access and content of an email account or service. It allows an individual or organization to protect the overall access to one or more email addresses/accounts.

Here are some points to consider for email security in the context of basic cryptography:

1. **Encryption**: Encryption is the process of encoding messages or information in such a way that only authorized parties can read it. Emails can be encrypted in transit and at rest to protect the confidentiality of the message.

2. **Digital signatures**: A digital signature is a mathematical scheme for verifying the authenticity of digital messages or documents. It provides a way to ensure that an email message is from the sender it claims to be from and has not been altered in transit.

3. **Spam filtering**: Spam filtering is the process of identifying and preventing unsolicited and unwanted email messages from reaching the inbox. This can help protect against phishing attacks and other email-based threats.

4. **Secure email gateways**: A secure email gateway is a type of email security solution that is designed to prevent the transmission of emails that break company policy, contain threats or are considered spam.

5. **Two-factor authentication**: Two-factor authentication is a security process in which the user provides two different authentication factors to verify their identity. This can be used to protect access to an email account.

6. **Email archiving**: Email archiving is the process of capturing, preserving, and making searchable all email traffic to and from an individual or organization. This can help ensure the integrity and availability of email data.

7. **Email security policies**: An email security policy is a set of rules and guidelines that an organization implements to ensure the security of its email communications. This can include rules for the use of encryption, digital signatures, spam filtering, and other security measures.

These are some of the key points to consider when studying email security in the context of basic cryptography. It is important to understand and implement these measures to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of email communications.
