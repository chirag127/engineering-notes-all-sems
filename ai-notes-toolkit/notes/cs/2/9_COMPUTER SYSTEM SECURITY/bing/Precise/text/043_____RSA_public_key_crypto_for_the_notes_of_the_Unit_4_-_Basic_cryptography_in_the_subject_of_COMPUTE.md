### RSA Public Key Crypto

RSA is a public key cryptographic algorithm that is widely used for secure data transmission. It is named after its inventors, Ron Rivest, Adi Shamir, and Leonard Adleman.

1. RSA is based on the mathematical properties of large prime numbers and their use in the factorization of large composite numbers.
2. The security of RSA relies on the difficulty of factoring the product of two large prime numbers.
3. In RSA, each user has a public key and a private key.
4. The public key is used to encrypt messages, while the private key is used to decrypt them.
5. The public key is made available to anyone, while the private key is kept secret by the owner.
6. RSA is used in a variety of applications, including digital signatures, secure communication, and secure key exchange.
7. RSA is considered to be a relatively slow algorithm compared to other cryptographic algorithms, due to the large key sizes and the computational complexity of the algorithm.
