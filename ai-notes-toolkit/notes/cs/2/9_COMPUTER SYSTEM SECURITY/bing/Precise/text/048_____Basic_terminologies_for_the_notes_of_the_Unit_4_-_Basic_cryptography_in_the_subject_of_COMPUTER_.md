### Basic Terminologies for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Basic Cryptography in the Subject of Computer System Security

1. **Cryptography**: The practice and study of techniques for secure communication in the presence of third parties.
2. **Encryption**: The process of converting plaintext into ciphertext to prevent unauthorized access to the information.
3. **Decryption**: The process of converting ciphertext back into plaintext to make it readable again.
4. **Plaintext**: The original message or data that is fed into the encryption algorithm.
5. **Ciphertext**: The encrypted message or data that is produced by the encryption algorithm.
6. **Key**: A piece of information used by the encryption algorithm to encrypt and decrypt the data.
7. **Symmetric Key Cryptography**: A type of cryptography where the same key is used for both encryption and decryption.
8. **Asymmetric Key Cryptography**: A type of cryptography where different keys are used for encryption and decryption.
9. **Public Key**: A key that is publicly available and used for encryption in asymmetric key cryptography.
10. **Private Key**: A key that is kept secret and used for decryption in asymmetric key cryptography.
11. **Digital Signature**: A mathematical scheme for verifying the authenticity of digital messages or documents.
12. **Hash Function**: A function that takes an input and produces a fixed-size output, commonly used for verifying the integrity of data.
13. **Cipher**: An algorithm for performing encryption or decryption.
14. **Cryptanalysis**: The study of methods for obtaining the meaning of encrypted information without access to the key.
15. **Cryptosystem**: A system that defines the procedures for encryption and decryption, including the algorithms and keys used.
