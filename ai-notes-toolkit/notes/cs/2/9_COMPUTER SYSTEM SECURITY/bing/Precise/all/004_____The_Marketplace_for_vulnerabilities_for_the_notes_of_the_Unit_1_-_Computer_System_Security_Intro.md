### The Marketplace for vulnerabilities

- The marketplace for vulnerabilities refers to the buying and selling of information about security vulnerabilities in software and systems.
- Vulnerability management is the process of identifying, evaluating, and mitigating vulnerabilities in a system or network.
- Companies such as Strobes Security, IBM, McAfee, Broadcom Inc, and Qualys Inc. are some of the major players in the security and vulnerability management market.
- The global Security and Vulnerability Management Market size is projected to grow from USD 13.8 billion in 2021 to USD 18.7 billion by 2026.
- The security and vulnerability management market has been growing rapidly in recent years, driven by the increasing frequency and sophistication of cyber attacks.
- As organizations become more reliant on technology, they also become more vulnerable to cyber threats.
- There are various tools and platforms available for vulnerability management, such as those offered by AWS Marketplace.
- CISA (Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency) maintains a catalog of known exploited vulnerabilities to help organizations stay informed and protected.
