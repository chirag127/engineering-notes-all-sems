### Rootkits

Rootkits are a type of malware that allows cybercriminals to gain control of a computer system without the owner's knowledge. They are primarily used for malicious activities such as stealing confidential information like passwords and credit card information . Rootkits can enter a system through various tactics, including email phishing, corrupted files, or downloaded software from unsafe websites .

To protect against rootkits, it is important to follow best practices such as:
1. Only downloading computer drivers from authorized sources .
2. Checking emails thoroughly before engaging with them or any attachments .
3. Maintaining an up-to-date operating system, browser, and security software .
4. Being careful about what you download and whom you give access to do so .
5. Applying the latest updates to operating systems and apps .
6. Educating employees to be wary of suspicious websites and emails .
7. Backing up important files regularly and using the 3-2-1 rule .

Rootkits can also be used to enhance the security of a computer system by monitoring system activity or providing a backdoor for the owner to access, locate, and wipe information in case the device is stolen . However, the primary use of rootkits is for malicious purposes, so it is important to take precautions to prevent them from being installed on your system.
