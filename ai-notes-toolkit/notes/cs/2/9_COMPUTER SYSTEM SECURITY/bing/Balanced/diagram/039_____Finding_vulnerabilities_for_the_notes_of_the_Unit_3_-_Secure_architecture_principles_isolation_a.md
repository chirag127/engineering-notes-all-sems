### Finding vulnerabilities for the notes of the Unit 3 - Secure architecture principles isolation and least privilege in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

- Secure architecture principles are guidelines for designing systems that are resilient to attacks and can protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and resources.
- Two important secure architecture principles are isolation and least privilege.
- Isolation means separating different components or layers of a system, so that a compromise of one does not affect the others. For example, isolating the network, the application, the data, and the user interface can prevent an attacker from gaining access to the entire system by exploiting a vulnerability in one component.
- Least privilege means granting the minimum amount of permissions or access rights to each component or user of a system, so that they can only perform the tasks that are necessary for their function. For example, least privilege can limit the damage that an attacker can do by impersonating a legitimate user or process, or by exploiting a vulnerability that allows privilege escalation.
- Finding vulnerabilities for these principles involves identifying the potential threats and risks that can exploit the lack of isolation or least privilege in a system, and evaluating the impact and likelihood of such attacks. Some common methods for finding vulnerabilities are:

  - Threat modeling: A systematic process of identifying and analyzing the threats and vulnerabilities that can affect a system, and prioritizing the countermeasures to mitigate them.
  - Penetration testing: A simulated attack on a system to discover and exploit its weaknesses, and to evaluate its security posture and resilience.
  - Code review: A manual or automated inspection of the source code or binaries of a system to detect flaws, bugs, or vulnerabilities that can compromise its security or functionality.
  - Vulnerability scanning: An automated process of scanning a system or network for known vulnerabilities, and reporting the results and recommendations for remediation.
