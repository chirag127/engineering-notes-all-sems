# What is computer security and what to learn for the notes of the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

Computer security, also called cybersecurity or information technology security, is the protection of computer systems and networks from attack by malicious actors that may result in unauthorized information disclosure, theft of, or damage to hardware, software, or data, as well as from the disruption or misdirection of the services they provide.

Some of the topics to learn for the notes of the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction are:

- The basic concepts and terminology of computer security, such as threats, vulnerabilities, risks, attacks, assets, confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, authorization, accountability, etc.
- The types and categories of computer security, such as physical security, network security, application security, data security, web security, cloud security, mobile security, etc.
- The principles and goals of computer security, such as defense in depth, least privilege, separation of duties, need to know, security by design, security by default, etc.
- The challenges and trends of computer security, such as the increasing complexity and diversity of systems and networks, the evolving and sophisticated nature of attacks, the trade-offs between security and usability, performance, cost, etc.
- The methods and techniques of computer security, such as encryption, hashing, digital signatures, certificates, firewalls, antivirus, intrusion detection and prevention, access control, auditing, etc.
- The standards and best practices of computer security, such as the ISO/IEC 27000 series, the NIST Cybersecurity Framework, the OWASP Top 10, the CIS Controls, etc.
- The roles and responsibilities of computer security professionals, such as security analysts, engineers, architects, managers, auditors, consultants, etc.
