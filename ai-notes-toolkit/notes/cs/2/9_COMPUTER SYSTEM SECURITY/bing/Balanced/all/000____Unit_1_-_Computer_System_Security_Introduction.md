## Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction

- Computer system security is the protection of information and assets from unauthorized access, use, modification, or destruction.
- Computer system security aims to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information and assets, as well as the accountability and non-repudiation of actions.
- Computer system security involves the application of policies, procedures, techniques, and tools to prevent, detect, and respond to security threats and incidents.
- Computer system security covers various aspects, such as:

  - Physical security: the protection of hardware, software, and data from physical damage, theft, or tampering.
  - Network security: the protection of data and devices from unauthorized access, interception, modification, or disruption over a network.
  - Application security: the protection of software and services from malicious or erroneous inputs, outputs, or actions.
  - Data security: the protection of data from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or deletion.
  - Cryptography: the use of mathematical techniques to secure data and communications from unauthorized parties.
  - Authentication: the verification of the identity or attributes of a user, device, or service.
  - Authorization: the granting or denying of access rights or privileges to a user, device, or service.
  - Auditing: the recording and analysis of security-related events and activities.
  - Incident response: the process of identifying, containing, analyzing, and resolving security incidents.
  - Risk management: the process of identifying, assessing, and mitigating security risks.
