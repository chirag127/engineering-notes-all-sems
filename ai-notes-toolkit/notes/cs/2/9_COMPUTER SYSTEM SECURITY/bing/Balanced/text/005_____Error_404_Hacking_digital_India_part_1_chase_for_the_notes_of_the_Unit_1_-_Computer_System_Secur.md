- The story begins with the introduction of the main characters and their motivations for hacking.

  - Karan, a hacker who is skilled in breaking into networks and systems. He is Ravi's best friend and the technical expert of Error 404.
  - Neha, a hacker who is good at social engineering and phishing. She is Karan's girlfriend and the infiltration specialist of Error 404.
- The story also introduces the antagonists and their goals.
  - The digital India project is a government initiative that aims to provide digital infrastructure and services to the citizens of India. However, the project is riddled with corruption, mismanagement, and security flaws that endanger the privacy and security of the users.
  - The main antagonist is Mr. Sharma, the head of the digital India project and a corrupt politician who is involved in various scams and frauds. He is also the target of Error 404's hacking attempts.
  - The other antagonists are Mr. Singh, the chief security officer of the digital India project and a former hacker who is hired by Mr. Sharma to protect the project from cyber attacks. He is also the nemesis of Ravi and Error 404.
  - The story also involves various other characters such as government officials, media personnel, hackers, and citizens who are affected by the digital India project and Error 404's actions.
- The story follows the adventures and challenges of Error 404 as they try to hack into the digital India project and expose its flaws and secrets to the public. They also face various obstacles and threats from Mr. Sharma, Mr. Singh, and other enemies who try to stop them and capture them.
- The story also explores the themes of cyber security, ethical hacking, digital citizenship, privacy, and social justice.
