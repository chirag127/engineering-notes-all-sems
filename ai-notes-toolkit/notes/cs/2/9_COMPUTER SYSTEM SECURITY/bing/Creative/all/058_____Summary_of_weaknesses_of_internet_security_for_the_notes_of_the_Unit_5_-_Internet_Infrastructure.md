# Summary of weaknesses of internet security

- Internet security is the protection of online systems and data from unauthorized access, theft, damage, or misuse.
- Weaknesses of internet security are the flaws or gaps in the security measures that can be exploited by cybercriminals or hackers to compromise the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of the online systems and data.
- Some of the common weaknesses of internet security are:

  - **Lack of a high-level strategy**: Many businesses, especially new and small ones, simply lack a high-level strategy for their cybersecurity needs. They do not have a clear vision, goals, policies, or standards for securing their online assets and operations. This makes them vulnerable to cyberattacks and breaches.
  - **Unsecured networks**: If the network is not secured, it is trivially easy for nefarious parties to gain access to the system. Unsecured networks may not have encryption, authentication, firewall, or antivirus software to protect the data in transit or at rest. This exposes the data to interception, modification, or deletion by hackers .
  - **Unsecured communication channels**: If the communication channels are not secured, they can be intercepted, eavesdropped, or tampered with by cybercriminals. Unsecured communication channels may include email, instant messaging, social media, or voice calls. These channels may not have encryption, authentication, or verification mechanisms to ensure the identity and integrity of the sender and receiver of the messages.
  - **Unknown bugs**: Unknown bugs are the errors or defects in the software or hardware that can cause unexpected or undesired behavior or results. Unknown bugs may not be detected or fixed by the developers or vendors, and may be exploited by hackers to bypass the security controls or to execute malicious code on the system .
  - **Outdated systems**: Outdated systems are the software or hardware that are no longer supported or updated by the developers or vendors. Outdated systems may have known or unknown vulnerabilities that can be exploited by hackers to compromise the system. Outdated systems may also not be compatible with the latest security technologies or standards, and may pose a risk to the system and the data .
  - **Lack of monitoring**: Lack of monitoring is the absence or insufficiency of the tools or processes to detect, analyze, or respond to the security incidents or events on the system. Lack of monitoring may prevent the system administrators or security professionals from identifying or mitigating the cyberattacks or breaches in a timely manner. Lack of monitoring may also hinder the investigation or recovery of the system and the data after a security incident .
  - **IoT and multiple connection points**: IoT (Internet of Things) and multiple connection points are the devices or systems that are connected to the internet or to each other, such as smart phones, smart watches, smart appliances, smart cars, etc. IoT and multiple connection points increase the complexity and diversity of the online environment, and also increase the attack surface and the entry points for the hackers. IoT and multiple connection points may not have adequate security features or standards, and may be easily compromised or manipulated by cybercriminals .
  - **Untrained employees**: Untrained employees are the staff or workers who do not have the necessary knowledge, skills, or awareness to protect the system and the data from cyberthreats. Untrained employees may not follow the security policies or best practices, and may inadvertently or intentionally expose the system or the data to cyberattacks or breaches. Untrained employees may also fall victim to phishing, social engineering, or insider threats by cybercriminals .

- These weaknesses of internet security can have serious consequences for the system and the data, such as:

  - Loss of confidentiality: The data may be accessed or disclosed by unauthorized parties, such as hackers, competitors, or adversaries, who may use the data for malicious or unethical purposes.
  - Loss of integrity: The data may be altered or corrupted by unauthorized parties, such as hackers, who may change the data to mislead, deceive, or harm the system or the users.
  - Loss of availability: The system or the data may be disrupted or destroyed by unauthorized parties, such as hackers, who may deny or prevent the system or the users from accessing or using the data or the services.
