## Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies

- Confidentiality policies are rules or guidelines that regulate how sensitive or personal information is handled, stored, and shared by an organization or a system.
- Confidentiality policies aim to protect the privacy and security of the information owners, such as customers, employees, or patients, and to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse of the information.
- Confidentiality policies may be based on legal, ethical, or contractual obligations, or on the principles of trust and respect.
- Confidentiality policies may vary depending on the type, context, and purpose of the information, and the roles and responsibilities of the information users, providers, and custodians.
- Confidentiality policies may cover aspects such as:
  - The definition and classification of confidential information.
  - The scope and limitations of information access and use.
  - The methods and standards of information protection and encryption.
  - The procedures and protocols of information sharing and disclosure.
  - The rights and obligations of information owners and users.
  - The consequences and remedies of information breaches or violations.
- Confidentiality policies should be clear, consistent, and comprehensive, and should be communicated and enforced effectively within the organization or the system.
