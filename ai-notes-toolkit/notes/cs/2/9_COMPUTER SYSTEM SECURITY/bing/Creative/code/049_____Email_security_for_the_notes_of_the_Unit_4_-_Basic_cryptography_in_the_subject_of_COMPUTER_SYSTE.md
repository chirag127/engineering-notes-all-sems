### Email security

Email security is the practice of protecting email accounts and communications from unauthorized access, loss, or compromise. Email is often used to spread malware, spam, and phishing attacks, which can compromise the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of email data and systems. Email security also involves ensuring the authenticity and non-repudiation of email messages, which means verifying the identity of the sender and the integrity of the message.

Some of the topics related to email security are:

- **Encryption**: Encryption is the process of transforming plain text into ciphertext, which is unreadable by anyone who does not have the key to decrypt it. Encryption can be applied to the content of email messages, as well as to the connection between the email client and the email server. Encryption can prevent eavesdropping, tampering, and spoofing of email messages.
- **Digital signatures**: Digital signatures are a way of attaching a code to an email message that proves the identity of the sender and the integrity of the message. Digital signatures are based on public key cryptography, which involves a pair of keys: a public key and a private key. The sender signs the message with their private key, and the recipient verifies the signature with the sender's public key. Digital signatures can prevent impersonation, alteration, and repudiation of email messages.
- **Spam filtering**: Spam filtering is the process of identifying and blocking unwanted or unsolicited email messages, which are also known as spam. Spam filtering can be done at the email server level, the email client level, or both. Spam filtering can use various techniques, such as blacklists, whitelists, content analysis, and machine learning. Spam filtering can reduce the volume of email traffic, the risk of malware infection, and the exposure to phishing attacks.
- **Phishing detection**: Phishing detection is the process of identifying and preventing email messages that attempt to trick the recipient into revealing sensitive information, such as passwords, credit card numbers, or bank account details. Phishing detection can use various techniques, such as domain spoofing, sender spoofing, link spoofing, and content spoofing. Phishing detection can protect the recipient from identity theft, fraud, and data breaches.
- **User awareness training**: User awareness training is the process of educating and informing email users about the best practices and the potential threats of email security. User awareness training can include periodic sessions, simulations, quizzes, and feedback. User awareness training can help to minimize the human error and to increase the user's ability to recognize and report suspicious email messages.
