
### Summary of weaknesses of internet security

1. Lack of authentication: Since the Internet is a decentralized network, it lacks authentication and verification of users. This can lead to malicious actors being able to access confidential information without authorization. 

2. Lack of encryption: Without encryption, data can be easily intercepted and read by malicious actors.

3. Poorly configured firewalls: Firewalls are designed to protect networks from external attacks, but if they are not configured properly, they can be bypassed by attackers.

4. Vulnerabilities in software: Software applications can have vulnerabilities that can be exploited by attackers.

5. Denial of service attacks: Denial of service attacks are a type of attack that can be used to overwhelm a system with requests, causing it to become unresponsive.

6. Phishing attacks: Phishing attacks involve sending emails or other messages that appear to be from a legitimate source, but are actually malicious.

7. Malware: Malware is malicious software that can be used to gain access to a system or steal data.

8. Weak passwords: Weak passwords can be easily guessed by attackers, allowing them to gain access to a system.
