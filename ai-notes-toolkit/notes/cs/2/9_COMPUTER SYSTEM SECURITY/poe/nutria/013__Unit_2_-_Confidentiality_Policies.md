
## Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies

1. Confidentiality policies are an important part of any organization, as they help protect sensitive information from unauthorized access. 
2. These policies are typically written to protect confidential information, such as customer data, financial records, and other sensitive information. 
3. Confidentiality policies should include the following components:
    * A clear definition of what constitutes confidential information.
    * A description of the procedures and policies for protecting confidential information.
    * A description of the consequences for violating the policy.
4. It is important to ensure that all employees are aware of the confidentiality policy and understand their obligations.
5. The policy should be regularly reviewed and updated to ensure that it remains up to date with the latest technology and security measures.
