
### Sample Attacks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction

1. **Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks**: These attacks exploit network vulnerabilities to cause disruption of services or resources. Commonly, a DoS attack will flood the target with requests, preventing legitimate users from accessing the system.

2. **Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks**: These attacks involve an attacker intercepting and manipulating communication between two or more parties. The attacker can gain access to data, or even modify it, without either of the parties knowing.

3. **Social Engineering Attacks**: These attacks involve deceiving people into providing confidential information or performing certain actions. Attackers can use various techniques, such as phishing emails or phone calls, to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

4. **Malware Attacks**: These attacks involve malicious software which is designed to damage or gain unauthorized access to a computer system. Common types of malware include viruses, worms, and Trojan horses.

5. **Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) Attacks**: These attacks are similar to DoS attacks, but involve multiple computers or devices. This makes it more difficult to identify the source of the attack and can cause more severe disruption of services.

6. **Password Attacks**: These attacks involve attackers attempting to guess or crack passwords in order to gain access to a system. Attackers can use brute force methods, or use dictionary and rainbow table attacks to guess passwords.
