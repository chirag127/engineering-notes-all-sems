
### RSA Public Key Crypto

* RSA is an asymmetric encryption algorithm, meaning it uses two different keys for encryption and decryption. 
* It stands for Rivest-Shamir-Adleman, named after the three cryptographers who developed it. 
* RSA is the most widely used public key cryptography algorithm. 
* It is based on the difficulty of factoring large numbers. 
* The encryption key is made up of two large prime numbers, and the decryption key is the product of those two prime numbers. 
* RSA provides confidentiality and digital signatures. 
* It is used in a wide range of applications, such as secure communication, secure file storage, digital signatures, and authentication.
