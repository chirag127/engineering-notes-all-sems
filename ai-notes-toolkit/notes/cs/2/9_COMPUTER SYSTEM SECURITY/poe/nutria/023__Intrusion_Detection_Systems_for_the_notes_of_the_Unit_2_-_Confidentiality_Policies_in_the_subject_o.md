
### Intrusion Detection Systems

Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS) are designed to detect malicious or unauthorized activities on a computer system. They are used to identify and alert system administrators of potential security breaches on their networks.

* IDS can be used to detect suspicious network traffic, such as network scans and port scans, as well as malicious activities, such as buffer overflows, denial of service attacks, and worms.
* IDS can also be used to detect unauthorized access attempts, such as brute force attacks, dictionary attacks, and password guessing.
* IDS can be used to detect malicious software, such as viruses, Trojans, and spyware.
* IDS can also be used to detect unauthorized access to confidential data, such as customer records, credit card numbers, and financial information.
* IDS can be used to detect changes to system configurations, such as changes to firewall rules, user accounts, and system services.
* IDS can be used to detect malicious code, such as rootkits, backdoors, and keyloggers.
