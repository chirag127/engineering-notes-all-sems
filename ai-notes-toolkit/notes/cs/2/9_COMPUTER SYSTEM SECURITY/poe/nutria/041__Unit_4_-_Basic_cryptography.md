
## Unit 4 - Basic cryptography

- Cryptography is the practice of using codes and ciphers to protect data and communications.
- Cryptographic algorithms are used to encrypt and decrypt data.
- Cryptographic keys are used to authenticate users and to ensure data integrity.
- Cryptographic hashes are used to verify the integrity of data and to detect tampering.
- Cryptography can be used to protect data in transit, such as in email and web traffic.
- Cryptography can also be used to protect data at rest, such as in databases and file systems.
- Cryptography can be used to authenticate users, such as with passwords and biometrics.
- Cryptography can also be used to authenticate devices, such as with digital certificates.
- Cryptography can be used to protect digital rights management systems, such as for digital music and video.
