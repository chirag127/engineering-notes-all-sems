
### System call interposition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Confidentiality Policies in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY

1. System call interposition is a technique used to modify the behavior of system calls. It involves intercepting and modifying the system calls made by a process before they are passed to the kernel.

2. System call interposition can be used to enforce access control policies, such as confidentiality policies. It can also be used to enforce security policies, such as preventing processes from accessing certain files or directories.

3. System call interposition can be implemented in user-space or kernel-space. User-space implementations are simpler and easier to implement, but are limited in their ability to enforce policies. Kernel-space implementations are more complex and difficult to implement, but can enforce more robust policies.

4. System call interposition can be used in conjunction with other techniques, such as address space randomization, to further enhance system security.
