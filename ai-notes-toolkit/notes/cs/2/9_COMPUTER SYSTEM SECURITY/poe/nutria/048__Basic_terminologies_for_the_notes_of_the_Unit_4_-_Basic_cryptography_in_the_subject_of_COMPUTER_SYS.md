
### Basic Terminologies for Unit 4 - Basic Cryptography in Computer System Security

1. Cryptography: The practice of using mathematics and algorithms to protect data and communications.
2. Encryption: The process of transforming data into an unreadable form to protect its confidentiality.
3. Decryption: The process of transforming encrypted data back into its original form.
4. Symmetric Cryptography: A type of cryptography that uses the same key for both encryption and decryption.
5. Asymmetric Cryptography: A type of cryptography that uses different keys for encryption and decryption.
6. Hashing: The process of applying a cryptographic algorithm to data to produce a fixed-length output known as a hash.
7. Digital Signatures: A type of cryptographic signature used to verify the authenticity of a document or message.
8. Key Exchange: The process of securely exchanging cryptographic keys between two or more parties.
