
## Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure

1. The internet is a global network of interconnected computers, servers, and other devices. It is used to send and receive data, and is composed of many different types of networks and protocols. 

2. The physical infrastructure of the internet consists of cables, routers, and switches. Cables are used to connect networks and devices, while routers and switches are used to direct and control the flow of data. 

3. The internet protocol (IP) is the primary protocol used to send and receive data over the internet. It is responsible for addressing, routing, and delivering data packets between networks. 

4. Domain Name System (DNS) is a protocol used to map domain names to IP addresses. This allows users to access web content using a simple name, rather than having to remember an IP address. 

5. Network Address Translation (NAT) is a protocol used to hide the internal IP addresses of devices from the public internet. This provides a layer of security and privacy, as well as allowing multiple devices to share a single public IP address. 

6. Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) is a protocol used to ensure reliable delivery of data packets over the internet. It is responsible for re-sending lost or corrupted packets, as well as ensuring that data arrives in the correct order. 

7. Internet Service Providers (ISPs) are companies that provide access to the internet. They are responsible for connecting customers to the internet, as well as providing additional services such as web hosting and email. 

8. Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) are networks of servers used to distribute web content to users. They are used to reduce latency and increase availability of web content, by caching content on servers located around the world.
