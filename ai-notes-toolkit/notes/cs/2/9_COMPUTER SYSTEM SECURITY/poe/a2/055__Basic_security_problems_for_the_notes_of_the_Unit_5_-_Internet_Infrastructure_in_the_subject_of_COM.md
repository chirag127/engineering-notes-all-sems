### Basic security problems for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY:

1. Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks: An attacker floods a computer, server, or network with a large amount of traffic, effectively disrupting services. This prevents legitimate users from accessing the service.
2. Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) Attacks: Similar to DoS attacks but the attack originates from many attacking systems, making it harder to block. The attacker is able to amplify the attack and create more disruption with less effort.
3. Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attacks: The attacker positions himself in the network to intercept and relay messages between two parties while making them believe they are directly communicating with each other. This allows the attacker to steal data or credentials.
4. IP Spoofing: The attacker forges the IP address of the originating system/packet to disguise identity or impersonate another system. This is often used to bypass access controls or carry out other attacks while hiding the true origin.
5. Email Spam/Phishing: The attacker sends unsolicited bulk messages or malicious emails (phishing) to trick users into giving up personal information, downloading malware, or paying money. These continue to be a highly lucrative attack for criminals.
