### The Marketplace for vulnerabilities

- There exists an underground market for vulnerabilities and exploits. Hackers and security researchers find vulnerabilities in software and systems and sell them to others instead of reporting them to the vendors.
- These vulnerabilities and exploits are sold for high prices depending on their severity and how widely the target software is used. Prices can range from tens of thousands to millions of dollars.
- Governments also buy vulnerabilities and exploits for cyber espionage and surveillance. This makes the marketplace very lucrative for skilled hackers and researchers.
- However, this marketplace is illegal and unethical as it puts users at risk by not reporting the vulnerabilities to vendors who can then patch them and keep users safe. It also increases the proliferation of cyber weapons which can fall into the wrong hands.
- To counter this, some companies and organizations like Bugcrowd and HackerOne run bug bounty programs where vulnerabilities are reported responsibly and rewards are given as per the severity. This encourages secure disclosure of vulnerabilities. More companies and governments should adopt such approaches.
