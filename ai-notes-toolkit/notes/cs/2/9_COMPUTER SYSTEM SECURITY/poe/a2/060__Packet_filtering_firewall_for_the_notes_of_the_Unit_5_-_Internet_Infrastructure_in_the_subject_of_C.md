### Packet filtering firewall for the notes of the Unit 5 - Internet Infrastructure in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY:

- A packet filtering firewall examines the header of each packet and decides to forward or block it based on a set of predefined rules.
- The rules specify which source and destination IP addresses and port numbers are allowed to pass through the firewall.
- Only packets that match the rules are allowed to pass, others are discarded.
- Advantages:
--Simple to implement and efficient.
--Inexpensive to deploy.
--Provide good protection against basic attacks.
- Disadvantages:
--Vulnerable to IP spoofing attacks.
--Rules can become complex to manage as the number increases.
--Does not protect against vulnerabilities in applications or services.
- Rules can be configured to allow/block traffic to:
--Specific internal machines/servers.
--Whole internal network.
--Specific external machines/servers.
--Particular ports.
--Particular protocols.
