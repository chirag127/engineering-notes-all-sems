### Basic terminologies for the notes of the Unit 4 - Basic cryptography in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY.

1. Plaintext: The original message or information in readable format is called plaintext.
2. Ciphertext: The encrypted message which is unreadable is called ciphertext.
3. Encryption: The process of converting plaintext into ciphertext is called encryption.
4. Decryption: The process of converting ciphertext back to plaintext is called decryption.
5. Key: A key is a parameter used by a cryptographic algorithm to customize the transformation of plaintext into ciphertext and vice versa.
6. Cryptosystem: The algorithm along with all possible keys and procedures required to implement encryption and decryption is called a cryptosystem.
7. Cryptanalysis: The study of methods of obtaining the meaning of encrypted information without access to the secret information which is normally required to do so is called cryptanalysis.
