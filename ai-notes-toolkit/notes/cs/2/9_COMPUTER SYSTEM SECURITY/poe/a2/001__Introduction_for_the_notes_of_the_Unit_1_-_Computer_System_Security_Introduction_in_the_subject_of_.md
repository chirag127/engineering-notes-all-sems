### Introduction
- Computer System Security refers to the protection of computer systems and networks from the theft of or damage to their electronic data, software, or hardware components.
- As computer systems and networks have become increasingly connected and crucial to business, government, and personal activities, the need for computer security has grown dramatically.
- Threats to computer security include malicious software, such as viruses, worms, Trojan horses, ransomware, and spyware; malicious insiders and external hackers; and accidental human errors.
- To mitigate threats, computer security employs a variety of methods, including access controls, cryptography, network security appliances, and security software.
