### Sample Attacks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Computer System Security Introduction in the subject of COMPUTER SYSTEM SECURITY.

1. Denial of Service (DoS) Attack: An attacker makes a computer resource unavailable to its intended users. The attacker floods the target with a large amount of traffic that overwhelms its servers, causing it to crash or slow down.
2. Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) Attack: Similar to DoS attack but the traffic comes from multiple compromised systems. It is harder to block as the traffic comes from multiple sources. The attacker gains control of multiple computers to simultaneously send traffic to the target.
3. Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) Attack: The attacker secretly relays and possibly alters the communication between two parties who think they are directly communicating with each other. The attacker can steal data or credentials being sent across.
4. Phishing Attack: The attacker sends an email pretending to be from a legitimate company or website to trick the victim into providing personal information like passwords, account numbers, etc. or to click on malicious links or download attachments containing malware.
5. Malware: Malicious software like viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware, etc. are programs designed to infiltrate or damage a computer system without the owner's informed consent. Once installed, malware can steal data, use the computer to attack other systems, or take control of the computer for other nefarious purposes.
6. Password Cracking: The attacker tries to guess or extract passwords to gain unauthorized access to a system. Methods include brute-force attack (trying all possible combinations), dictionary attack (trying common words and phrases), and network traffic sniffing (looking for passwords sent over networks).
7. SQL Injection: The attacker inserts malicious SQL statements into input data to manipulate or leak data from the database. It exploits insecure coding practices where user input is not properly validated or sanitized before being used in SQL queries.
