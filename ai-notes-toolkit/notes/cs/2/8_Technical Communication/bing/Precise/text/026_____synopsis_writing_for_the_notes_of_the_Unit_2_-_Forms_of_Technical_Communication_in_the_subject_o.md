### Synopsis Writing for Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. A synopsis is a brief summary of the main points of a larger work.
2. It is used to provide an overview of the content and to help the reader quickly understand the key ideas.
3. In the context of technical communication, a synopsis can be used to summarize a technical document, such as a report or a manual.
4. The synopsis should be concise and to the point, focusing on the most important information.
5. It should be written in clear and simple language, avoiding technical jargon as much as possible.
6. The structure of the synopsis should follow the structure of the original document, highlighting the main sections and their key points.
7. It is important to ensure that the synopsis accurately reflects the content of the original document and does not omit any important information.
8. A well-written synopsis can help the reader quickly understand the content of a technical document and decide whether it is relevant to their needs.
