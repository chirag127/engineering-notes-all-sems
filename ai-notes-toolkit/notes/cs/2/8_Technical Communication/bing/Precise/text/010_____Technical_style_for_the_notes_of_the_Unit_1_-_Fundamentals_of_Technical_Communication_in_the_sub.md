### Technical style for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Formality:** Technical writing should be formal and professional in tone. Avoid using colloquial language or slang.
2. **Clarity:** The language used should be clear and concise, avoiding ambiguity and confusion.
3. **Accuracy:** Technical writing should be accurate and precise, with all information presented being factually correct.
4. **Objectivity:** Technical writing should be objective and unbiased, presenting information in a neutral manner.
5. **Organization:** Technical writing should be well-organized, with information presented in a logical and coherent manner.
6. **Conciseness:** Technical writing should be concise, with all information presented in a clear and succinct manner.
7. **Use of visuals:** Technical writing often makes use of visuals such as diagrams, charts, and graphs to help convey information more effectively.
8. **Use of headings and subheadings:** Technical writing often makes use of headings and subheadings to help organize information and make it easier to navigate.
9. **Use of lists:** Technical writing often makes use of lists, both bulleted and numbered, to help present information in a clear and organized manner.
10. **Consistency:** Technical writing should be consistent in its use of language, formatting, and style.
