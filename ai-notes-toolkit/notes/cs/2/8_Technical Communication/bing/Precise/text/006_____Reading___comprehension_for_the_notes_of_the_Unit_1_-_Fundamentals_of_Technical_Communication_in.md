### Reading & Comprehension

Reading and comprehension are fundamental skills in technical communication. Here are some key points to consider when studying this topic:

1. **Active reading:** Active reading involves engaging with the text, asking questions, and making connections to better understand the material.

2. **Vocabulary:** Technical communication often involves specialized vocabulary. It is important to understand the meaning of technical terms to effectively comprehend the text.

3. **Text structure:** Understanding the structure of a text, such as headings, subheadings, and paragraphs, can help readers navigate and comprehend the material.

4. **Summarizing:** Summarizing involves identifying the main ideas and key points of a text and expressing them in a concise manner. This skill can help readers retain information and communicate it to others.

5. **Critical thinking:** Critical thinking involves analyzing and evaluating information to form a judgment. This skill is important in technical communication, as it allows readers to assess the credibility and relevance of information.

6. **Visual aids:** Visual aids, such as diagrams, charts, and graphs, can help readers understand complex information. It is important to be able to interpret and analyze visual information in technical communication.

These are some of the key points to consider when studying reading and comprehension in the context of technical communication. It is important to practice these skills to effectively communicate technical information.
