### Forms for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Outline Form:** This form of note-taking is useful for organizing information in a hierarchical manner. It involves writing the main topic at the top of the page and then listing subtopics and supporting details underneath.

2. **Cornell Method:** This method involves dividing the page into two columns. The right column is used for taking notes during the presentation, while the left column is used for writing questions or keywords that can be used to review the material later.

3. **Mind Mapping:** This form of note-taking involves visually organizing information by drawing a central idea and then connecting related ideas and details around it.

4. **Charting Method:** This method involves creating a table or chart to organize information. It is useful for comparing and contrasting information or for organizing information into categories.

5. **Sentence Method:** This method involves writing notes in complete sentences. It is useful for capturing detailed information and for summarizing key points.

Each of these forms has its own advantages and can be used depending on the type of information being presented and the individual's personal note-taking style. It is important to experiment with different forms to find the one that works best for you.
