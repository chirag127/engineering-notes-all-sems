### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. **Reports:** Reports are written documents that present information in an organized format for a specific audience and purpose. They can be used to convey technical information, research findings, or recommendations.

2. **Proposals:** Proposals are written documents that outline a plan or idea and request approval or funding from an audience. They are often used in business and technical settings to present new ideas or projects.

3. **Manuals:** Manuals are written documents that provide instructions or guidance on how to use a product or perform a task. They are often used in technical fields to provide detailed information on how to operate or maintain equipment.

4. **Emails:** Emails are electronic messages that are used to communicate information quickly and efficiently. They are often used in business and technical settings to convey information, ask questions, or provide updates.

5. **Presentations:** Presentations are visual and oral forms of communication that are used to convey information to an audience. They can be used to present technical information, research findings, or recommendations in a clear and engaging manner.

6. **Memos:** Memos are short, written documents that are used to communicate information within an organization. They are often used to convey updates, announcements, or decisions to a specific group of people.

7. **Technical Descriptions:** Technical descriptions are written documents that provide detailed information about a product, process, or system. They are often used in technical fields to provide a clear and concise explanation of how something works.

8. **User Guides:** User guides are written documents that provide instructions or guidance on how to use a product or perform a task. They are similar to manuals, but are often more user-friendly and focused on helping the user achieve a specific goal.

9. **White Papers:** White papers are written documents that present information or arguments on a specific topic. They are often used in business and technical settings to provide in-depth analysis or recommendations on a particular issue.

10. **Specifications:** Specifications are written documents that provide detailed information about the requirements or characteristics of a product, process, or system. They are often used in technical fields to ensure that all parties involved in a project have a clear understanding of what is expected.
