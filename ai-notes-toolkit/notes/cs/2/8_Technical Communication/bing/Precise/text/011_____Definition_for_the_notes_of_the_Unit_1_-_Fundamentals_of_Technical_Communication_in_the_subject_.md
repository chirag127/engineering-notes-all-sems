### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Technical communication is the process of conveying information through writing, speech, and other media to a specific audience. The information is often complex, and the audience may have varying levels of expertise in the subject matter. Technical communication is used in a variety of fields, including science, engineering, medicine, and business.

Some key points to remember about technical communication are:

- It is audience-centered, meaning that the information is tailored to the needs and understanding of the intended audience.
- It is clear and concise, using language and visuals that are easy to understand.
- It is accurate and reliable, presenting information that is based on facts and evidence.
- It is ethical, avoiding plagiarism and giving credit to sources of information.
- It is well-organized, presenting information in a logical and easy-to-follow manner.

Technical communication can take many forms, including user manuals, reports, proposals, and presentations. Effective technical communication is essential for the success of any project or organization. It helps to ensure that all stakeholders have a clear understanding of the information being conveyed, and can make informed decisions based on that information.
