### Combination of Expression

Combination of expression refers to the use of multiple modes of communication to convey a message or information. In technical communication, it is important to use a combination of expression to effectively communicate technical information to the intended audience. Some common modes of expression used in technical communication include:

1. **Written communication:** This includes the use of written words to convey technical information. Examples include technical reports, user manuals, and instructional materials.

2. **Visual communication:** This includes the use of visual aids such as diagrams, charts, and graphs to convey technical information. Visual communication is particularly useful for presenting complex data or information in an easy-to-understand manner.

3. **Oral communication:** This includes the use of spoken words to convey technical information. Examples include presentations, lectures, and meetings.

4. **Non-verbal communication:** This includes the use of body language, gestures, and facial expressions to convey technical information. Non-verbal communication is particularly important in face-to-face interactions.

Effective technical communication often involves the use of a combination of these modes of expression to convey technical information in a clear and concise manner. The choice of modes of expression should be based on the intended audience, the complexity of the information being conveyed, and the context in which the communication is taking place.
