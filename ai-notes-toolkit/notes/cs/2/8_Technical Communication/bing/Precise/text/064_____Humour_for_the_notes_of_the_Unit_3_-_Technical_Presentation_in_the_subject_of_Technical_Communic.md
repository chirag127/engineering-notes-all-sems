### Humour for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Humour is the quality of being amusing or comic, especially as expressed in literature or speech.
2. Humour can be used as a tool to engage the audience and make the presentation more interesting.
3. Humour can be used to lighten the mood and make the audience more receptive to the information being presented.
4. Humour can be used to illustrate a point or to make a complex concept more understandable.
5. Humour should be used carefully and appropriately, as it can be offensive or inappropriate in certain situations.
6. Humour should be relevant to the topic and the audience.
7. Humour can be used to break the ice and establish a connection with the audience.
8. Humour can be used to create a memorable moment in the presentation.
9. Humour can be used to add a personal touch to the presentation.
10. Humour can be used to show the presenter's personality and make them more relatable to the audience.
