### Strategic competence

Strategic competence refers to the ability to use language effectively in order to achieve a specific purpose or goal. It is an important aspect of technical communication skills, as it enables individuals to communicate their ideas and information in a clear and concise manner.

Some key points to consider when developing strategic competence include:

1. Understanding the audience: It is important to understand the needs and expectations of the audience in order to tailor the message accordingly.

2. Clarity and concision: Technical communication should be clear and concise, avoiding unnecessary jargon or technical terms that may confuse the audience.

3. Organization: Information should be presented in a logical and organized manner, making it easy for the audience to follow and understand.

4. Use of visual aids: Visual aids, such as diagrams or charts, can help to convey complex information in a more accessible manner.

5. Revision and editing: It is important to revise and edit technical communication to ensure that it is accurate, clear, and free of errors.

Developing strategic competence is an ongoing process that involves practice and reflection. By focusing on these key points, individuals can improve their ability to communicate effectively in technical contexts.
