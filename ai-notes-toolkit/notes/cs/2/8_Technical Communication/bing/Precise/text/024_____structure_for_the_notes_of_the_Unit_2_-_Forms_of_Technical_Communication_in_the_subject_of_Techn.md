### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. **Written Communication**: This includes various forms of written communication such as emails, memos, reports, proposals, and technical manuals.
2. **Visual Communication**: This includes the use of visual aids such as diagrams, charts, graphs, and illustrations to convey technical information.
3. **Oral Communication**: This includes presentations, speeches, and meetings where technical information is conveyed verbally.
4. **Electronic Communication**: This includes the use of electronic media such as websites, social media, and multimedia presentations to convey technical information.
5. **Nonverbal Communication**: This includes the use of body language, gestures, and facial expressions to convey technical information.

These are the main forms of technical communication that are commonly used in the workplace. Each form has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the most effective technical communicators are able to use a combination of these forms to convey information in a clear and concise manner.
