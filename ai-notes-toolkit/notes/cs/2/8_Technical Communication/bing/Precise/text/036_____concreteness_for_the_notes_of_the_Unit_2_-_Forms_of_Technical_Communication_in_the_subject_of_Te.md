### Concreteness

Concreteness refers to the use of specific, definite, and vivid language in communication. It is an important aspect of effective technical communication. Here are some key points to consider when striving for concreteness in technical communication:

1. Use specific and precise language: Avoid vague or general terms and instead use specific and precise language to accurately convey information.

2. Provide examples and illustrations: Examples and illustrations can help to clarify abstract concepts and make them more concrete for the reader.

3. Use sensory language: Sensory language, which appeals to the senses, can help to create vivid and concrete images in the reader's mind.

4. Avoid jargon and technical terms: Unless the audience is familiar with the technical terms and jargon, it is best to avoid them or provide clear definitions to ensure that the information is concrete and understandable.

5. Use active voice: Active voice sentences, where the subject performs the action, are often more concrete and direct than passive voice sentences.

By following these guidelines, technical communicators can ensure that their writing is concrete and effective in conveying information to their audience.
