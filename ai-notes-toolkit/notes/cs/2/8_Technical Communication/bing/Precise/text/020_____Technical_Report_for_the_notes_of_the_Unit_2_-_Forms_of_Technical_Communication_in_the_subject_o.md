### Technical Report for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information through writing, speech, and other media to a specific audience.
2. The main forms of technical communication include technical reports, user manuals, white papers, design specifications, and presentations.
3. A technical report is a document that describes the process, progress, or results of technical research or the state of a technical or scientific research problem.
4. Technical reports are often used to communicate the results of research and development projects to management, funding agencies, and other interested parties.
5. User manuals provide instructions and guidance on how to use a product or service.
6. White papers are authoritative reports that provide information on a specific topic, often used to educate readers and help them make decisions.
7. Design specifications describe the requirements and constraints of a product or system, including its functionality, performance, and physical characteristics.
8. Presentations are used to communicate technical information to a group of people, often using visual aids such as slides or videos.
