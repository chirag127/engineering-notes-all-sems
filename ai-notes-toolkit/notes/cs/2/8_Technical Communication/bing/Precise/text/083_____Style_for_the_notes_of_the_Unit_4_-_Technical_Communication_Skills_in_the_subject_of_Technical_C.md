### Style for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Style refers to the way in which something is written, as opposed to the meaning of what is written.
2. In technical communication, style is important because it can affect the clarity, accuracy, and effectiveness of the communication.
3. Some key elements of style in technical communication include:
    - Conciseness: using only as many words as necessary to convey the intended meaning.
    - Clarity: writing in a way that is easy to understand and avoids ambiguity.
    - Consistency: using the same formatting, terminology, and tone throughout a document.
    - Objectivity: presenting information in a neutral and unbiased manner.
4. Technical communicators should strive to use a style that is appropriate for their audience and the purpose of the communication.
5. Style can be improved through practice and by seeking feedback from others.
