# Distinction between General and Technical Communication

General communication and technical communication are two different types of communication that serve different purposes and audiences. Here are some key distinctions between the two:

1. **Purpose:** General communication is used to convey information, ideas, and opinions to a broad audience, while technical communication is used to convey technical information to a specific audience.

2. **Audience:** General communication is intended for a general audience, while technical communication is intended for a specific audience, such as engineers, scientists, or technicians.

3. **Content:** General communication covers a wide range of topics, while technical communication focuses on technical subjects, such as science, engineering, or technology.

4. **Style:** General communication is often informal and conversational, while technical communication is formal and precise.

5. **Format:** General communication can take many forms, such as spoken, written, or visual, while technical communication often takes the form of written documents, such as manuals, reports, or instructions.

In summary, general communication is used to convey information to a broad audience, while technical communication is used to convey technical information to a specific audience. Technical communication is more formal and precise than general communication and often takes the form of written documents. These are some of the key distinctions between general and technical communication.
