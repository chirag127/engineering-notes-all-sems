# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

### Definition

Technical communication is a type of communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose. It is a form of written or oral communication that is used to convey technical information to a specific audience, usually in a workplace setting. Technical communication can take many forms, including but not limited to:

1. User manuals
2. Technical reports
3. White papers
4. Specifications
5. Proposals
6. Presentations
7. Online help systems
8. Technical articles
9. Technical blogs
10. Technical books

The goal of technical communication is to provide clear, concise, and accurate information to the intended audience in a way that is easily understood and actionable. Technical communicators must have a strong understanding of the subject matter, as well as the ability to communicate complex information in a clear and concise manner. They must also be able to tailor their communication to the needs and level of understanding of their audience. Technical communication is an essential part of many industries, including engineering, science, technology, and business. It plays a critical role in ensuring that products and services are used effectively and safely.
