# Speaking with a Purpose

Speaking with a purpose is an important aspect of oral communication, especially in technical communication. Here are some key points to consider when speaking with a purpose:

1. **Identify the purpose of your speech:** Before you begin speaking, it is important to identify the purpose of your speech. This will help you to focus your message and ensure that you are communicating effectively.

2. **Organize your thoughts:** Once you have identified the purpose of your speech, it is important to organize your thoughts in a logical and coherent manner. This will help you to present your ideas clearly and effectively.

3. **Use appropriate language:** When speaking with a purpose, it is important to use language that is appropriate for your audience. This includes using technical terms when necessary, but also avoiding jargon or overly complex language that may confuse your listeners.

4. **Engage your audience:** To effectively communicate your message, it is important to engage your audience. This can be achieved through the use of visual aids, body language, and vocal variety.

5. **Be concise:** When speaking with a purpose, it is important to be concise and to the point. Avoid rambling or going off on tangents, as this can distract from your message and make it difficult for your audience to follow.

6. **Practice:** Finally, it is important to practice your speech before delivering it. This will help you to become more comfortable with the material and to deliver it more effectively.

These are some key points to consider when speaking with a purpose in technical communication. By following these guidelines, you can ensure that your message is communicated effectively and that your audience is engaged and informed.
