### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. **Reports:** Reports are written documents that present information in an organized format for a specific audience and purpose. They can be used to convey progress, results, or recommendations.

2. **Proposals:** Proposals are written documents that outline a plan or idea and request approval or funding. They are often used to pitch new projects or initiatives.

3. **Manuals:** Manuals are written documents that provide instructions or guidelines on how to use a product or perform a task. They are often used to help users understand and operate complex equipment or systems.

4. **Emails:** Emails are electronic messages used to communicate information quickly and efficiently. They are often used for brief, informal communication within an organization.

5. **Memos:** Memos are brief, written documents used to communicate information within an organization. They are often used to convey updates, announcements, or decisions.

6. **Presentations:** Presentations are visual and oral forms of communication used to convey information to an audience. They are often used to present research findings, proposals, or progress reports.

7. **Websites:** Websites are collections of web pages that provide information on a particular topic or organization. They are often used to provide information to a wide audience.

8. **Social Media:** Social media platforms are used to communicate and share information with a wide audience. They are often used for marketing, customer service, and public relations.

9. **Video and Audio:** Video and audio recordings are used to communicate information in a visual and/or auditory format. They are often used for training, marketing, or public relations.

10. **Graphics and Visuals:** Graphics and visuals are used to communicate information in a visual format. They are often used to supplement written or oral communication and can include charts, graphs, diagrams, and images.

These are some of the common forms of technical communication. Each form has its own unique characteristics and is used for specific purposes. It is important to choose the appropriate form of communication for the intended audience and purpose.
