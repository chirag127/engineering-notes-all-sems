# Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

### Definition

Technical communication is a type of communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose. This can include, but is not limited to, user manuals, technical reports, and instructional materials. Technical communication is characterized by its clarity, concision, and accuracy, and is designed to enable the audience to understand and use the information effectively.

Some key points to remember about technical communication are:

- It is intended for a specific audience, often with a technical background or knowledge.
- It is designed to convey technical information for a specific purpose, such as to instruct, inform, or persuade.
- Clarity, concision, and accuracy are essential in technical communication.
- Technical communication can take many forms, including written, visual, and oral communication.
