### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

#### Downward Communication

- Downward communication refers to the flow of information from higher levels of an organization to lower levels.
- This type of communication is typically used by managers to convey information, instructions, and expectations to employees.
- Examples of downward communication include company policies, performance evaluations, and job assignments.
- Downward communication can be delivered through various channels, such as meetings, emails, and memos.
- Effective downward communication is essential for ensuring that employees understand their roles and responsibilities, and for maintaining a clear and consistent flow of information within an organization.
