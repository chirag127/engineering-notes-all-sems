# Solution of Communication Problems with Verbal and Non-Verbal Means

Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Verbal Communication**: Verbal communication refers to the use of spoken or written language to convey a message. Some ways to solve communication problems using verbal means include:
    - **Active Listening**: Paying attention to what the speaker is saying and providing feedback to show understanding.
    - **Clarification**: Asking questions to clarify any misunderstandings or confusion.
    - **Paraphrasing**: Restating what the speaker has said in your own words to ensure understanding.
    - **Summarizing**: Summarizing the main points of the conversation to ensure all parties are on the same page.

2. **Non-Verbal Communication**: Non-verbal communication refers to the use of body language, gestures, facial expressions, and other non-verbal cues to convey a message. Some ways to solve communication problems using non-verbal means include:
    - **Eye Contact**: Maintaining eye contact to show interest and engagement in the conversation.
    - **Body Language**: Using open and relaxed body language to convey a positive and receptive attitude.
    - **Gestures**: Using gestures to emphasize points and convey meaning.
    - **Facial Expressions**: Using facial expressions to convey emotions and reactions.

Effective communication involves a combination of both verbal and non-verbal means. By using these techniques, communication problems can be solved and effective communication can be achieved.
