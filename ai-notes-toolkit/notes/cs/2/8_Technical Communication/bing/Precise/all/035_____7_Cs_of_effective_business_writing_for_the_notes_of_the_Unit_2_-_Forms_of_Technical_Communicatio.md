### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication: 7 Cs of Effective Business Writing

1. **Clarity**: The message should be clear and easy to understand. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the reader may not be familiar with.

2. **Conciseness**: Keep the message brief and to the point. Avoid unnecessary repetition or information that is not relevant to the main message.

3. **Correctness**: Ensure that the information presented is accurate and free of errors. This includes checking for spelling, grammar, and punctuation errors.

4. **Coherence**: The message should be well-organized and logically structured. Ideas should flow smoothly from one to the next.

5. **Completeness**: The message should include all the necessary information for the reader to understand and take action. Avoid leaving out important details.

6. **Courtesy**: The tone of the message should be polite and respectful. Avoid using language that could be perceived as rude or confrontational.

7. **Consideration**: Consider the needs and perspective of the reader. Try to anticipate any questions or concerns they may have and address them in the message.

These 7 Cs can help ensure that your business writing is effective and achieves its intended purpose. By following these principles, you can communicate more clearly and effectively with your audience.
