### Discourse competence

Discourse competence refers to the ability to understand and produce written and spoken language in context. It involves the ability to use language effectively to achieve a specific purpose and to understand the relationship between different parts of a text or conversation. Here are some key points to consider when discussing discourse competence in the context of technical communication skills:

1. Discourse competence is an important aspect of technical communication, as it enables individuals to effectively convey technical information to a variety of audiences.
2. It involves the ability to organize information in a logical and coherent manner, using appropriate language and tone to convey the intended message.
3. Discourse competence also involves the ability to understand and use various text structures and genres, such as reports, proposals, and instructions.
4. Effective discourse competence requires an understanding of the audience and their needs, as well as the ability to adapt the message to suit the audience's level of technical knowledge and understanding.
5. Developing discourse competence involves practice and feedback, and can be improved through training and experience.
