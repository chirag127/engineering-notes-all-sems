# Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication: Reading & Comprehension

Reading and comprehension are fundamental skills in technical communication. Here are some key points to consider when reading and comprehending technical texts:

1. **Identify the purpose and audience of the text.** Understanding the purpose and intended audience of a text can help you better comprehend its content and structure.

2. **Pay attention to headings and subheadings.** Headings and subheadings can provide a useful overview of the text's content and organization.

3. **Look for keywords and definitions.** Technical texts often introduce new terms and concepts. Pay attention to keywords and their definitions to better understand the text.

4. **Take notes and summarize.** Taking notes and summarizing key points can help you retain information and improve your comprehension.

5. **Ask questions and seek clarification.** If you encounter something you don't understand, don't hesitate to ask questions or seek clarification.

6. **Practice active reading.** Active reading involves engaging with the text, making connections, and thinking critically about the content. This can help improve your comprehension and retention of information.

7. **Re-read and review.** Re-reading and reviewing the text can help reinforce your understanding and improve your comprehension.

By following these tips, you can improve your reading and comprehension skills in technical communication. Remember to practice and apply these skills regularly to continue to improve.
