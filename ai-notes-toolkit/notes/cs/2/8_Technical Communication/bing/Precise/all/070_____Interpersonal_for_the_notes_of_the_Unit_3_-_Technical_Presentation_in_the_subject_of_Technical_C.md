### Interpersonal

Interpersonal refers to the interactions and relationships between individuals. It is an important aspect of technical communication, as it involves the ability to communicate effectively with others in a professional setting.

Here are some key points to consider when discussing interpersonal communication in the context of technical presentations:

1. **Active Listening**: It is important to actively listen to others during a technical presentation. This involves paying attention to what the speaker is saying, and providing feedback to show that you are engaged in the conversation.

2. **Nonverbal Communication**: Nonverbal cues, such as body language and facial expressions, can convey a lot of information during a technical presentation. It is important to be aware of your own nonverbal communication, as well as the nonverbal cues of others.

3. **Effective Speaking**: Effective speaking involves being clear and concise in your communication. This is particularly important during a technical presentation, where complex information may need to be conveyed in a clear and understandable manner.

4. **Conflict Resolution**: Conflicts may arise during a technical presentation, and it is important to have the skills to resolve these conflicts in a professional and effective manner.

5. **Collaboration**: Technical presentations often involve collaboration between multiple individuals. It is important to have the skills to work effectively with others, and to be able to communicate and collaborate in a productive manner.

These are just a few of the key points to consider when discussing interpersonal communication in the context of technical presentations. By developing strong interpersonal skills, you can improve your ability to communicate effectively with others in a professional setting.
