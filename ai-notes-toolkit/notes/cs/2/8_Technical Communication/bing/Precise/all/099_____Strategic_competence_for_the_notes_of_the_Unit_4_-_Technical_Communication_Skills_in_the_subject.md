# Strategic competence

Strategic competence is the ability to use language effectively in order to achieve a specific goal or purpose. It is an important aspect of technical communication skills, as it enables individuals to communicate their ideas and information in a clear, concise, and persuasive manner.

Some key points to consider when developing strategic competence include:

1. Understanding the audience: It is important to understand the needs, interests, and background of the audience in order to tailor the message accordingly.

2. Clarity and concision: Technical communication should be clear and concise, avoiding unnecessary jargon or technical terms that may confuse the audience.

3. Organization and structure: The information should be presented in a logical and organized manner, with a clear structure that is easy to follow.

4. Use of visual aids: Visual aids, such as diagrams, charts, and graphs, can be effective in conveying complex information in a clear and concise manner.

5. Persuasion: The ability to persuade the audience is an important aspect of strategic competence, as it enables individuals to effectively communicate their ideas and convince others of their point of view.

In summary, strategic competence is an essential skill for effective technical communication, as it enables individuals to communicate their ideas and information in a clear, concise, and persuasive manner. By understanding the audience, using clear and concise language, organizing the information effectively, using visual aids, and employing persuasive techniques, individuals can develop their strategic competence and improve their technical communication skills.
