### Response for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication refers to the spoken interaction between two or more individuals.
2. The dimensions of oral communication include the speaker, the message, the channel, the listener, and the feedback.
3. Voice dynamics refer to the variations in pitch, volume, rate, and quality of the voice that can affect the delivery of the message.
4. Effective oral communication requires the speaker to have a clear understanding of the message, the audience, and the purpose of the communication.
5. The speaker should also be able to use appropriate voice dynamics to convey the message effectively.
6. The listener plays an important role in oral communication by providing feedback to the speaker, which can help the speaker to adjust the message or delivery.
7. Effective oral communication can enhance relationships, facilitate problem-solving, and promote understanding and cooperation.
