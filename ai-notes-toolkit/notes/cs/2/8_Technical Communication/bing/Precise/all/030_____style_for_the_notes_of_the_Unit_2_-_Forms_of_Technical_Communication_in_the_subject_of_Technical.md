### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. **Reports:** Reports are written documents that present information in an organized format for a specific audience and purpose. They can be used to convey technical information, research findings, or recommendations for action.

2. **Proposals:** Proposals are written documents that outline a plan or idea for a project or business venture. They are often used to persuade others to support or invest in the proposed idea.

3. **Manuals:** Manuals are written documents that provide instructions or guidelines for using a product or performing a task. They are often used to help users understand how to operate or maintain a piece of equipment or software.

4. **Emails:** Emails are electronic messages that are used to communicate information quickly and efficiently. They are often used in business and technical communication to share information, ask questions, or provide updates.

5. **Presentations:** Presentations are visual and oral forms of communication that are used to convey information to an audience. They often include slides, graphics, and other visual aids to help convey the information.

6. **Memos:** Memos are short, written documents that are used to communicate information within an organization. They are often used to provide updates, share news, or convey instructions.

7. **Technical Descriptions:** Technical descriptions are written documents that provide detailed information about a product, process, or system. They are often used to help users understand the technical aspects of a product or process.

8. **User Guides:** User guides are written documents that provide step-by-step instructions for using a product or service. They are often included with products to help users get started and troubleshoot any issues they may encounter.
