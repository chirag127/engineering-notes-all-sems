### Technical style for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical writing is a style of writing that is used to convey technical information to a specific audience.
- The main goal of technical writing is to communicate complex information in a clear and concise manner.
- Technical writing should be accurate, precise, and objective.
- Technical writing should be well-organized and easy to follow.
- Technical writing should use clear and concise language, avoiding jargon and technical terms when possible.
- Technical writing should use visuals, such as diagrams, charts, and graphs, to help convey information.
- Technical writing should be formatted in a way that is easy to read and understand.
- Technical writing should be reviewed and edited for clarity, accuracy, and completeness.
