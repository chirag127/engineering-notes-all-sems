# C.V./Resume Writing

C.V. (Curriculum Vitae) or Resume is a document that summarizes an individual's education, work experience, and other qualifications. It is used to apply for jobs and is often the first point of contact between a job seeker and an employer. Here are some points to consider when writing a C.V. or Resume:

1. **Format**: Choose a clear and easy-to-read format. Use headings, bullet points, and white space to make the document visually appealing and easy to scan.
2. **Length**: A C.V. can be longer than a Resume, but both should be concise and to the point. Aim for one to two pages for a Resume and two to three pages for a C.V.
3. **Content**: Include relevant information such as contact details, education, work experience, skills, and achievements. Tailor the content to the job you are applying for.
4. **Language**: Use clear, concise, and professional language. Avoid using jargon or slang.
5. **Accuracy**: Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date. Double-check for spelling and grammar errors.
6. **References**: Include references or a statement that references are available upon request.

Remember, a C.V. or Resume is a marketing tool to showcase your skills and qualifications to potential employers. Make sure it is well-written, visually appealing, and tailored to the job you are applying for.
