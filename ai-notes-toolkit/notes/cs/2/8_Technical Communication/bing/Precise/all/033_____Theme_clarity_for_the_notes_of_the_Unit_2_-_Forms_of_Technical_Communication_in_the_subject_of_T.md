### Theme Clarity for the Notes of Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Theme clarity refers to the clear and concise presentation of the main idea or message of a piece of technical communication.
2. It is important for the theme to be easily identifiable and understood by the intended audience.
3. The theme should be consistent throughout the document and should be supported by the content.
4. To achieve theme clarity, the writer should use clear and concise language, avoid jargon and technical terms, and use examples and illustrations to support the main idea.
5. The use of headings, subheadings, and bullet points can also help to organize the content and make the theme more easily identifiable.
6. In summary, theme clarity is essential for effective technical communication and can be achieved through careful planning, clear language, and effective organization of content.
