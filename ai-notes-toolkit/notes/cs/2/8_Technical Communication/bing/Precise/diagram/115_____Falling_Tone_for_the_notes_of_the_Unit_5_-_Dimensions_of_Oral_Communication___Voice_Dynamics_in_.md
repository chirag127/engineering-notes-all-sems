### Falling Tone

- Falling tone is a type of intonation pattern used in speech.
- It is characterized by a downward pitch movement at the end of a phrase or sentence.
- Falling tone is commonly used to indicate the end of a statement or to convey finality.
- It can also be used to express certainty, assertiveness, or to make a command.
- In English, falling tone is often used at the end of a declarative sentence or a command.
- It is important to use appropriate intonation patterns, including falling tone, to effectively communicate and convey meaning in oral communication.
