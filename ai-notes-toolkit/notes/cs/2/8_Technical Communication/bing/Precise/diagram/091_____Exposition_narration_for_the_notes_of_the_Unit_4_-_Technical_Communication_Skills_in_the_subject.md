### Exposition Narration for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the Subject of Technical Communication

Exposition narration is a type of writing that explains, informs, or describes. It is commonly used in technical communication to convey information in a clear and concise manner. Here are some key points to remember when using exposition narration in technical communication:

1. **Clarity:** The language used in exposition narration should be clear and easy to understand. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the reader may not be familiar with.

2. **Conciseness:** Exposition narration should be concise and to the point. Avoid including unnecessary information or details that do not add value to the message.

3. **Organization:** The information presented in exposition narration should be well-organized and structured in a logical manner. This helps the reader to easily follow and understand the message.

4. **Accuracy:** The information presented in exposition narration should be accurate and reliable. Ensure that all facts and figures are correct and up-to-date.

5. **Objectivity:** Exposition narration should be objective and unbiased. Avoid expressing personal opinions or subjective views.

By following these guidelines, exposition narration can be an effective tool for conveying information in technical communication. It is important to remember that the goal of exposition narration is to inform and educate the reader, so the language and tone used should be appropriate for this purpose.
