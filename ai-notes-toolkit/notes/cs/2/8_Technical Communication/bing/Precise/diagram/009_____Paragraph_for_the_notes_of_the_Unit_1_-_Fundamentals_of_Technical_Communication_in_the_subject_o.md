### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
2. The goal of technical communication is to inform, instruct, or persuade the audience.
3. Technical communication can take many forms, including written documents, visual aids, and oral presentations.
4. Effective technical communication requires clear and concise language, appropriate organization, and attention to the needs and expectations of the audience.
5. Technical communicators must be able to work collaboratively with subject matter experts, understand complex technical information, and translate it into language that is accessible to the intended audience.
6. Technical communication is an essential skill in many fields, including engineering, science, medicine, and business.
