### Strategic Competence

Strategic competence refers to the ability to use language effectively in order to achieve a specific purpose. It involves the ability to use various communication strategies to overcome difficulties in communication, such as repairing misunderstandings or using gestures to convey meaning.

In the context of technical communication, strategic competence is important for several reasons:

1. It allows individuals to communicate complex technical information in a clear and concise manner.
2. It enables individuals to adapt their communication style to different audiences, such as technical and non-technical stakeholders.
3. It helps individuals to overcome communication barriers, such as language or cultural differences.
4. It allows individuals to effectively manage communication in challenging situations, such as negotiations or conflict resolution.

Developing strategic competence involves practicing effective communication techniques, such as active listening, asking clarifying questions, and providing clear and concise explanations. It also involves being aware of non-verbal communication cues and adapting one's communication style to different situations and audiences.

In summary, strategic competence is a key skill for effective technical communication, as it enables individuals to communicate complex technical information in a clear and concise manner, adapt their communication style to different audiences, and overcome communication barriers. Developing strategic competence involves practicing effective communication techniques and being aware of non-verbal communication cues.
