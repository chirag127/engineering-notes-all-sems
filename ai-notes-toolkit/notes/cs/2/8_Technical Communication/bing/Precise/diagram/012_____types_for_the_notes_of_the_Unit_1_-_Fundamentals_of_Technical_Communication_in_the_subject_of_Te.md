### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

#### Types of Technical Communication

1. **Instructional Materials:** These include user manuals, tutorials, and other materials that provide instructions on how to use a product or perform a task.
2. **Technical Reports:** These are documents that present technical information in a clear and concise manner, often used to communicate the results of research or experiments.
3. **Proposals:** These are documents that present a plan or idea for consideration, often used to request funding or support for a project.
4. **Specifications:** These are detailed descriptions of the requirements for a product or system, often used in the design and development process.
5. **Emails and Memos:** These are short, informal documents used to communicate information within an organization.
6. **Presentations:** These are visual and oral presentations of technical information, often used to communicate ideas or results to a group.
7. **Technical Articles:** These are articles written for a technical audience, often published in trade journals or magazines.
8. **White Papers:** These are authoritative reports that provide information on a specific topic, often used to educate or persuade an audience.
