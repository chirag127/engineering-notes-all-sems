### Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Be prepared**: Know your material and be ready to present it in a clear and concise manner.
2. **Speak clearly**: Enunciate your words and use proper pronunciation to ensure that your message is understood.
3. **Maintain eye contact**: Look at your audience while speaking to engage them and show confidence.
4. **Use appropriate body language**: Use gestures and posture to reinforce your message and convey confidence.
5. **Be aware of your tone**: Use a tone that is appropriate for the situation and the message you are conveying.
6. **Listen actively**: Pay attention to what others are saying and respond appropriately.
7. **Be respectful**: Show respect for others by using polite language and avoiding interrupting or talking over them.
8. **Stay on topic**: Keep the conversation focused on the subject at hand to avoid confusion and ensure that the message is conveyed effectively.
9. **Be concise**: Keep your message brief and to the point to avoid losing the attention of your audience.
10. **Use visual aids**: Use visual aids such as slides or diagrams to help convey your message and keep your audience engaged.
