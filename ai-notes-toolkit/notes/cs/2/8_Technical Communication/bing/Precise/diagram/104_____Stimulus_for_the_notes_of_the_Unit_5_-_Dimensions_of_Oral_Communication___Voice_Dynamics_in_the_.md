### Stimulus

Stimulus is an important concept in the study of oral communication and voice dynamics. Here are some key points to consider when studying this topic:

1. A stimulus is any event or situation that evokes a response from an individual.
2. In the context of oral communication, a stimulus can be a verbal or non-verbal cue that triggers a response from the listener.
3. Stimuli can be external, such as a loud noise or a bright light, or internal, such as a thought or emotion.
4. The response to a stimulus can vary depending on the individual and the context in which the stimulus is presented.
5. Understanding the role of stimuli in communication can help individuals improve their communication skills and better understand the dynamics of interpersonal interactions.

These are some of the key points to consider when studying the concept of stimulus in the context of oral communication and voice dynamics. It is important to understand the role of stimuli in communication in order to effectively communicate and interact with others.
