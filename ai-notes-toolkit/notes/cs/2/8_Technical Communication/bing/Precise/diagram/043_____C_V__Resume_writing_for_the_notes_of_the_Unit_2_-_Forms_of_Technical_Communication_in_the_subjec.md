### C.V./Resume writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

A C.V. (Curriculum Vitae) or resume is a document that summarizes an individual's education, work experience, and other qualifications. It is often used as a tool for job applications and is an important part of the job search process. Here are some key points to consider when writing a C.V. or resume:

1. **Format**: Choose a clear and easy-to-read format for your C.V. or resume. Use headings, bullet points, and white space to make the document easy to scan.
2. **Length**: Keep your C.V. or resume concise. A good rule of thumb is to limit the document to one or two pages.
3. **Content**: Include relevant information about your education, work experience, and skills. Be sure to highlight any achievements or accomplishments.
4. **Customization**: Tailor your C.V. or resume to the specific job you are applying for. Use keywords from the job description and highlight relevant experience and skills.
5. **Accuracy**: Make sure all the information on your C.V. or resume is accurate and up-to-date. Double-check dates, job titles, and other details.
6. **Proofreading**: Carefully proofread your C.V. or resume to ensure there are no errors or typos.

Remember, a well-written C.V. or resume can help you stand out from other job applicants and increase your chances of landing an interview. Take the time to create a strong and effective document.
