### Methods of Presentation

Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Oral Presentation:** This method involves delivering information verbally, usually in front of an audience. It can be accompanied by visual aids such as slides or handouts to help convey the information.

2. **Written Presentation:** This method involves presenting information in a written format, such as a report or a paper. It allows the audience to read and review the information at their own pace.

3. **Multimedia Presentation:** This method involves using a combination of different media, such as text, images, audio, and video, to present information. It can be an effective way to engage the audience and convey complex information.

4. **Poster Presentation:** This method involves presenting information on a large poster, usually at a conference or other event. It allows the audience to view the information at their own pace and can be an effective way to convey visual information.

5. **Demonstration:** This method involves showing the audience how to do something, such as how to use a piece of equipment or how to perform a task. It can be an effective way to convey practical information.

6. **Interactive Presentation:** This method involves engaging the audience in the presentation, such as by asking them questions or having them participate in activities. It can be an effective way to keep the audience engaged and to facilitate learning.
