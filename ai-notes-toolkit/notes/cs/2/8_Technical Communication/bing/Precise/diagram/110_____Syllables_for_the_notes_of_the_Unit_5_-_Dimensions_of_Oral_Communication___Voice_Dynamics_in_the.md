### Syllables for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- A syllable is a unit of sound that forms a word or part of a word.
- Syllables are important in oral communication because they help to determine the rhythm and stress patterns of spoken language.
- In English, syllables can be divided into two main types: stressed and unstressed.
- Stressed syllables are pronounced with more emphasis and are usually longer in duration than unstressed syllables.
- The number of syllables in a word can affect its pronunciation and the way it is perceived by the listener.
- Syllable division is important for correct pronunciation and for understanding the meaning of words.
- In oral communication, it is important to use the correct syllable stress to convey the intended meaning and to avoid misunderstandings.
- Syllable stress can also be used to convey emphasis or to distinguish between words that are spelled the same but have different meanings.
- In summary, syllables play a crucial role in oral communication by helping to determine the rhythm, stress, and meaning of spoken language. Understanding and using syllables correctly can improve the clarity and effectiveness of oral communication.
