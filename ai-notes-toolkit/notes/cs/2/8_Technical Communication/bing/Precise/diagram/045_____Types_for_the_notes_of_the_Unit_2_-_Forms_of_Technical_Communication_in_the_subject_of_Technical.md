### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

#### Types of Technical Communication

1. **Instructional Materials:** These include user manuals, tutorials, and quick reference guides that provide step-by-step instructions on how to use a product or perform a task.

2. **Technical Reports:** These are documents that present technical information in a clear and concise manner. They can include research reports, feasibility studies, and progress reports.

3. **Proposals:** These are documents that present a plan or idea to a client or stakeholder. They can include project proposals, business proposals, and grant proposals.

4. **Technical Specifications:** These are documents that provide detailed information about the design and functionality of a product. They can include product specifications, software requirements, and engineering drawings.

5. **White Papers:** These are documents that present a company's position on a particular topic. They are often used to educate customers or stakeholders about a particular issue or to promote a particular product or service.

6. **Emails and Memos:** These are short, informal documents used to communicate information within an organization. They can include project updates, meeting agendas, and announcements.

7. **Presentations:** These are visual and oral presentations used to communicate technical information to an audience. They can include slide shows, webinars, and lectures.

8. **Technical Websites and Blogs:** These are online platforms used to communicate technical information to a wider audience. They can include product documentation, tutorials, and industry news.
