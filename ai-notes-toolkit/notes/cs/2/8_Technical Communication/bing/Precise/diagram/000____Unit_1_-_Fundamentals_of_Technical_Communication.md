## Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. **Definition**: Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information through writing, graphics, or other means to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
2. **Purpose**: The purpose of technical communication is to inform, instruct, or persuade the audience.
3. **Audience**: The audience for technical communication can vary widely, from experts in the field to laypeople with little or no technical knowledge.
4. **Characteristics**: Technical communication is characterized by its clarity, accuracy, conciseness, and accessibility.
5. **Types**: There are many types of technical communication, including user manuals, technical reports, white papers, and instructional materials.
6. **Skills**: Technical communicators need strong writing and editing skills, as well as the ability to understand and convey complex technical information.
7. **Tools**: Technical communicators use a variety of tools to create and deliver their content, including word processors, graphics software, and content management systems.
8. **Ethics**: Technical communicators have a responsibility to present information truthfully and accurately, and to consider the potential consequences of their communication.
