### Conciseness for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Conciseness refers to the use of the fewest and most effective words possible to convey a message.
- Concise writing is clear, direct, and to the point.
- Concise writing avoids unnecessary repetition, filler words, and overly complex language.
- Conciseness is important in technical communication because it helps the reader to quickly understand the information being presented.
- To achieve conciseness, one can use active voice, avoid redundancy, and use precise language.
- Concise writing is not only more efficient for the reader, but it also helps the writer to organize their thoughts and present their ideas more effectively.
