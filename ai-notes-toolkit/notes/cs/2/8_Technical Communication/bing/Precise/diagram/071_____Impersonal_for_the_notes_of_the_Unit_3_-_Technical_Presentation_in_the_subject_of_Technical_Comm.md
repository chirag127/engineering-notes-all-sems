### Impersonal

- Impersonal communication refers to the style of communication where the focus is on the information being conveyed, rather than the individuals involved in the communication.
- In technical communication, impersonal style is often preferred as it presents the information in a clear, concise, and objective manner.
- Impersonal communication is characterized by the use of passive voice, third person pronouns, and avoidance of personal opinions or emotions.
- This style of communication is commonly used in technical reports, scientific papers, and other forms of technical documentation.
- Using an impersonal style can help to establish the credibility of the information being presented, as it suggests that the information is based on facts and evidence, rather than personal opinions or biases.
- However, it is important to note that an impersonal style may not always be the most effective way to communicate, particularly in situations where building a personal connection with the audience is important.
