### Encoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Encoding is the process of converting information into a form that can be transmitted and understood by the receiver.
2. In oral communication, the speaker encodes the message using verbal and non-verbal cues such as tone of voice, body language, and facial expressions.
3. The encoding process is influenced by the speaker's communication skills, language proficiency, and cultural background.
4. Effective encoding requires the speaker to consider the audience and tailor the message accordingly.
5. In the context of voice dynamics, the speaker can use variations in pitch, volume, and pace to convey emotions and emphasize key points.
6. The use of pauses and silence can also be an effective tool in encoding a message.
7. In summary, the encoding process is a crucial step in oral communication, and the speaker's ability to effectively encode a message can greatly impact the success of the communication.
