### Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills: Focus

1. **Focus** refers to the ability to concentrate on a specific task or goal without being distracted.
2. It is an essential skill in technical communication as it allows the writer to effectively convey information to the reader.
3. To improve focus, one can try techniques such as setting specific goals, breaking tasks into smaller parts, and minimizing distractions.
4. In technical writing, it is important to maintain focus on the main topic and avoid including irrelevant information.
5. A focused technical document is more likely to be understood by the reader and achieve its intended purpose.
