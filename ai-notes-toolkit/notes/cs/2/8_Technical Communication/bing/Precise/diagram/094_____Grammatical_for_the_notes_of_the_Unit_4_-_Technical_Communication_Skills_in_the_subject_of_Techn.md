### Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills: Grammatical

1. Grammar is the set of rules that govern the structure of sentences and the use of words in a language.
2. Proper grammar is essential for effective technical communication, as it ensures clarity and precision in the transmission of information.
3. Common grammatical errors in technical communication include subject-verb agreement, verb tense, and pronoun usage.
4. Technical communicators should strive to use active voice, concise language, and clear sentence structure to enhance the readability of their writing.
5. Proofreading and editing are important steps in the writing process to ensure that the final product is grammatically correct and free of errors.
6. There are many resources available, such as grammar guides and online tools, to assist technical communicators in improving their grammar skills.
