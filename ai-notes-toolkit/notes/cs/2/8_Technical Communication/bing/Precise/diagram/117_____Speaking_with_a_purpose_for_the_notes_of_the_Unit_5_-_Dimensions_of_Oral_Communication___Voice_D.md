### Speaking with a Purpose

Speaking with a purpose is an important aspect of oral communication. It involves delivering a message with a specific goal in mind. Here are some key points to consider when speaking with a purpose:

1. **Identify the purpose:** Before delivering a message, it is important to identify the purpose of the communication. This could be to inform, persuade, entertain, or motivate the audience.

2. **Know your audience:** Understanding the audience is crucial when speaking with a purpose. Consider their background, interests, and needs when crafting the message.

3. **Organize the message:** A well-organized message is easier for the audience to follow and understand. Use an outline to organize the main points and supporting details.

4. **Use appropriate language:** The language used should be appropriate for the audience and the purpose of the communication. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the audience may not understand.

5. **Use nonverbal communication:** Nonverbal communication, such as body language and tone of voice, can enhance the message and help convey the purpose.

6. **Practice:** Practice delivering the message to improve confidence and effectiveness.

By following these points, speakers can effectively communicate their message with a clear purpose in mind. This can help to engage the audience and achieve the desired outcome.
