### Courtesy

Courtesy is an important aspect of technical communication. It refers to the use of polite language and behavior when interacting with others. Here are some points to consider when practicing courtesy in technical communication:

1. Use polite language: Avoid using language that may be considered rude or offensive. Use words such as "please" and "thank you" to show respect and appreciation.

2. Show respect: Treat others with respect, regardless of their position or status. This includes using appropriate titles and addressing people by their preferred names.

3. Listen actively: Pay attention to what others are saying and show that you are listening by making eye contact and nodding. Avoid interrupting or talking over others.

4. Be empathetic: Try to understand the perspective of others and show empathy towards their feelings and concerns.

5. Respond promptly: Respond to messages and inquiries in a timely manner to show that you value the time of others.

6. Be clear and concise: Communicate your message clearly and concisely to avoid confusion and misunderstandings.

7. Avoid negative language: Avoid using negative language or making negative comments about others. Instead, focus on the positive and offer constructive feedback.

By practicing courtesy in technical communication, you can build positive relationships with others and create a more effective and collaborative work environment.
