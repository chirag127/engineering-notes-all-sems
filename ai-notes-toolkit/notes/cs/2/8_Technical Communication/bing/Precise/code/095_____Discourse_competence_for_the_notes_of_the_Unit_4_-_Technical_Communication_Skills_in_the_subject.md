### Discourse competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

Discourse competence refers to the ability to understand and produce connected text, both spoken and written. It involves the ability to use language to achieve coherence and cohesion in communication. Some key points to consider when discussing discourse competence include:

1. Discourse competence is an essential component of effective communication, as it allows individuals to convey their thoughts and ideas in a clear and organized manner.
2. It involves the use of various linguistic devices, such as transitional words and phrases, to connect ideas and create a logical flow of information.
3. Discourse competence also involves the use of appropriate tone, style, and register to effectively communicate with different audiences.
4. Developing discourse competence requires practice and exposure to a variety of texts and communication situations.
5. Effective discourse competence can enhance an individual's ability to communicate in both personal and professional settings.
