## Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. **Definition:** Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information through writing, speaking, and other forms of communication to a specific audience.
2. **Purpose:** The purpose of technical communication is to inform, instruct, or persuade the audience about technical subjects.
3. **Audience:** The audience for technical communication can vary widely, from experts in the field to laypeople with little or no technical background.
4. **Characteristics:** Technical communication is characterized by its clarity, concision, accuracy, and objectivity. It is also often highly structured and uses specialized vocabulary and visual aids to convey information.
5. **Types:** There are many types of technical communication, including user manuals, technical reports, white papers, and instructional materials.
6. **Skills:** Effective technical communication requires a combination of writing, editing, design, and interpersonal skills. Technical communicators must also have a strong understanding of the subject matter they are communicating.
