### Strategies for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Understand the purpose of the presentation**: Before taking notes, it is important to understand the purpose of the presentation. This will help you focus on the most important information and organize your notes effectively.

2. **Identify the main points**: During the presentation, pay attention to the main points that the speaker is making. These points should be the focus of your notes.

3. **Use abbreviations and symbols**: To take notes quickly, use abbreviations and symbols to represent common words and phrases.

4. **Organize your notes**: Organize your notes in a way that makes sense to you. This could mean using headings, bullet points, or numbering to group related information together.

5. **Summarize**: After the presentation, take some time to summarize the main points in your own words. This will help you remember the information and make it easier to review later.

6. **Review and revise**: Review your notes regularly to reinforce your understanding of the material. Revise your notes as needed to make them more clear and concise.

7. **Use visual aids**: If the presentation includes visual aids such as slides or diagrams, incorporate these into your notes. This will help you remember the information and make it easier to understand.

These are some strategies that can be helpful when taking notes for a technical presentation. Remember to tailor your note-taking approach to your own needs and preferences.
