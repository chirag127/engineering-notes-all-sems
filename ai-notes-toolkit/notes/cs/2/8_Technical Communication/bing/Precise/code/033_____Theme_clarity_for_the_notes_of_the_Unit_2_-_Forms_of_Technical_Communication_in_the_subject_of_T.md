### Theme Clarity for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Theme clarity refers to the clear and concise presentation of the main idea or message of a piece of technical communication.
2. It is important for the theme to be easily identifiable and understood by the intended audience.
3. The theme should be consistent throughout the document and should be supported by the content.
4. To achieve theme clarity, the writer should:
    - Clearly state the main idea or message at the beginning of the document.
    - Use headings and subheadings to organize the content and make it easier for the reader to follow.
    - Use language and terminology that is appropriate for the intended audience.
    - Avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be unfamiliar to the reader.
    - Use visual aids, such as diagrams or charts, to help convey complex information.
5. Theme clarity is essential for effective technical communication as it helps the reader to quickly understand the purpose and main message of the document.
