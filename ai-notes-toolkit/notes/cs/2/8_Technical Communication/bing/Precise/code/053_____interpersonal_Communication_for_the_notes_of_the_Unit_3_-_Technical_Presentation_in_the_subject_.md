### Interpersonal Communication

Interpersonal communication is the process of exchanging information, ideas, and feelings between two or more people. This can be done through various channels, including verbal, nonverbal, and written communication. Interpersonal communication is an essential part of our daily lives and is important in building and maintaining relationships.

Here are some key points to consider when studying interpersonal communication for Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication:

1. Interpersonal communication is a two-way process that involves both sending and receiving messages.
2. Effective interpersonal communication requires active listening, empathy, and the ability to express oneself clearly and assertively.
3. Nonverbal communication, such as body language, tone of voice, and facial expressions, plays a significant role in interpersonal communication.
4. Interpersonal communication can be influenced by various factors, including cultural differences, power dynamics, and personal biases.
5. Conflict resolution and negotiation skills are important aspects of interpersonal communication.
6. Interpersonal communication skills can be improved through practice and self-reflection.
