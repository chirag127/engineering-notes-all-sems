### Tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Tone refers to the attitude or emotion conveyed through the voice.
2. Tone can be used to convey a wide range of emotions, including happiness, sadness, anger, and sarcasm.
3. Tone is an important aspect of oral communication, as it can greatly affect the way a message is received and interpreted.
4. Tone can be controlled through the use of pitch, volume, and inflection.
5. It is important to match the tone of your voice to the content of your message and the context in which it is being delivered.
6. In professional settings, it is generally best to use a neutral or formal tone.
7. Tone can also be used to convey emphasis or to highlight important points.
8. It is important to be aware of cultural differences in the use of tone, as what is considered appropriate in one culture may not be in another.
9. Practice and self-awareness can help improve the use of tone in oral communication.
