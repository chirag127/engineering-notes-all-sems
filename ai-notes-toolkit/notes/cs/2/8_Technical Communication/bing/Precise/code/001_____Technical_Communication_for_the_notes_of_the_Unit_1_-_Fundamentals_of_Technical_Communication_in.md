### Technical Communication

Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of conveying technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
2. The goal of technical communication is to inform, instruct, or persuade the audience.
3. Technical communication can take many forms, including written documents, oral presentations, and visual aids.
4. Effective technical communication requires clear and concise language, appropriate organization, and attention to the needs and expectations of the audience.
5. Technical communicators must be able to work collaboratively with subject matter experts, designers, and other stakeholders to produce effective communication materials.
6. Technical communication is an essential skill for professionals in many fields, including engineering, science, and business.
