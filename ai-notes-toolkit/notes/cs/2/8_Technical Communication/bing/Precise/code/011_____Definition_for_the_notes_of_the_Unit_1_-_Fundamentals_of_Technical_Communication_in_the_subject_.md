### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of conveying information through writing, speech, and other media to a specific audience.
2. The information conveyed is often complex and requires a clear and concise presentation to be understood by the intended audience.
3. Technical communication is used in a variety of fields, including science, engineering, medicine, law, and business.
4. The goal of technical communication is to inform, instruct, or persuade the audience.
5. Effective technical communication requires an understanding of the audience's needs, the subject matter, and the medium used to convey the information.
6. Technical communicators must be skilled in writing, editing, design, and the use of technology to create effective communication materials.
7. Technical communication is an essential part of any organization's success, as it helps to ensure that information is conveyed accurately and efficiently.
