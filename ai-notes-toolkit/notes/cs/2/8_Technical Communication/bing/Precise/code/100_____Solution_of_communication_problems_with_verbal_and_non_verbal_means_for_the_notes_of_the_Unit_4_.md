### Solution of communication problems with verbal and non verbal means

Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Verbal communication** involves the use of spoken or written words to convey a message. Some solutions to communication problems using verbal means include:
    - **Active listening**: Paying attention to the speaker, providing feedback, and avoiding interruptions can help improve communication.
    - **Clarity**: Being clear and concise in your message can help avoid misunderstandings.
    - **Tone**: The tone of your voice can convey emotions and attitudes, so it is important to be aware of how you sound when speaking.
2. **Non-verbal communication** involves the use of body language, gestures, and facial expressions to convey a message. Some solutions to communication problems using non-verbal means include:
    - **Eye contact**: Maintaining eye contact can show that you are paying attention and interested in the conversation.
    - **Posture**: Your body posture can convey confidence, openness, or defensiveness, so it is important to be aware of how you are sitting or standing.
    - **Gestures**: Using hand gestures can help emphasize points and convey emotions.

By using a combination of verbal and non-verbal communication techniques, it is possible to effectively communicate and solve communication problems. It is important to be aware of both the words you use and the non-verbal cues you give to ensure that your message is understood.
