### Essentials for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Purpose of Technical Presentation**: The main purpose of a technical presentation is to communicate technical information to a specific audience, usually in a clear and concise manner.

2. **Audience Analysis**: Before preparing a technical presentation, it is important to analyze the audience to understand their background, knowledge level, and expectations.

3. **Structure of Technical Presentation**: A technical presentation should have a clear and logical structure, with an introduction, main body, and conclusion.

4. **Visual Aids**: Visual aids, such as diagrams, charts, and images, can be used to enhance the understanding of the technical information being presented.

5. **Delivery**: The delivery of a technical presentation should be clear, concise, and engaging, with appropriate use of body language, eye contact, and vocal variety.

6. **Handling Questions**: It is important to be prepared to handle questions from the audience during or after the presentation.

7. **Evaluation**: After the presentation, it is important to evaluate its effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.
