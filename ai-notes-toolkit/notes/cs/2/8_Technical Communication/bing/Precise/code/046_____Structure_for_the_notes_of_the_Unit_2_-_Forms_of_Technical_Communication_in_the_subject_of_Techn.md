### Structure for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Introduction to Technical Communication:** Definition, purpose, and importance of technical communication in the workplace.
2. **Forms of Technical Communication:** Overview of the different forms of technical communication, including written, oral, visual, and electronic.
3. **Written Technical Communication:** Characteristics and examples of written technical communication, including reports, proposals, and manuals.
4. **Oral Technical Communication:** Characteristics and examples of oral technical communication, including presentations, meetings, and conferences.
5. **Visual Technical Communication:** Characteristics and examples of visual technical communication, including diagrams, charts, and graphs.
6. **Electronic Technical Communication:** Characteristics and examples of electronic technical communication, including email, websites, and social media.
7. **Conclusion:** Summary of the key points covered in the unit and the importance of understanding the different forms of technical communication.
