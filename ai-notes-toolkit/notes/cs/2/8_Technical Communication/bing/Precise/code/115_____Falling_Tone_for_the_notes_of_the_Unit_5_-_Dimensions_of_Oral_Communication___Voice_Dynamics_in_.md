### Falling Tone
- Falling tone is a type of intonation pattern used in speech.
- It is characterized by a downward pitch movement at the end of an utterance.
- Falling tone is commonly used to indicate the end of a statement or to convey finality.
- It can also be used to express certainty, assertiveness, or to make a command.
- In English, falling tone is often used at the end of a declarative sentence or a command.
- It is important to use appropriate intonation patterns, including falling tone, to effectively communicate in oral communication.
- In the context of voice dynamics, falling tone can be used to convey a range of emotions and attitudes, depending on the context and the speaker's intention.
- Proper use of falling tone can enhance the clarity and effectiveness of oral communication.
