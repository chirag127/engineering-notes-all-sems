### Downward Communication

Downward communication is the flow of information from higher levels of an organization to lower levels. It is typically used by managers to communicate with their subordinates. Some key points to remember about downward communication are:

1. Downward communication is used to convey information such as goals, objectives, policies, and procedures.
2. It is important for managers to communicate clearly and effectively to ensure that their subordinates understand the information being conveyed.
3. Downward communication can take many forms, including meetings, memos, emails, and reports.
4. Effective downward communication can improve employee morale and productivity by ensuring that employees understand their roles and responsibilities within the organization.
5. It is important for managers to solicit feedback from their subordinates to ensure that the information being conveyed is understood and to address any concerns or misunderstandings.
