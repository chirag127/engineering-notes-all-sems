### Technical style for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. **Clarity:** Technical writing should be clear and concise. Avoid using jargon or complex language that may confuse the reader.
2. **Accuracy:** Technical writing should be accurate and precise. Ensure that all facts, figures, and data are correct and up-to-date.
3. **Objectivity:** Technical writing should be objective and unbiased. Avoid expressing personal opinions or using emotive language.
4. **Organization:** Technical writing should be well-organized and structured. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to break up the text and make it easier to read.
5. **Format:** Technical writing should follow a consistent format and style. Use a clear and legible font, and ensure that all text is properly aligned and spaced.
6. **Visuals:** Technical writing should make use of visuals such as diagrams, charts, and graphs to help convey information more effectively.
7. **Tone:** Technical writing should adopt a formal and professional tone. Avoid using colloquial language or slang.
8. **Audience:** Technical writing should be tailored to the intended audience. Consider the reader's level of technical knowledge and use language and terminology that is appropriate for them.

These are some of the key points to keep in mind when writing technical notes for Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication. By following these guidelines, you can ensure that your notes are clear, accurate, and effective in conveying technical information.
