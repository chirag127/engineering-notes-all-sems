### Encoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Encoding is the process of converting information into a code or format that can be transmitted and understood by a receiver.
2. In oral communication, encoding involves the speaker converting their thoughts and ideas into spoken words and nonverbal cues.
3. The encoding process is influenced by various factors, including the speaker's language skills, cultural background, and emotional state.
4. Effective encoding is essential for successful oral communication, as it ensures that the intended message is accurately conveyed to the receiver.
5. In the context of voice dynamics, encoding involves the use of vocal techniques such as pitch, volume, and tone to convey meaning and emotion.
6. The study of voice dynamics can help speakers improve their encoding skills and enhance the effectiveness of their oral communication.
