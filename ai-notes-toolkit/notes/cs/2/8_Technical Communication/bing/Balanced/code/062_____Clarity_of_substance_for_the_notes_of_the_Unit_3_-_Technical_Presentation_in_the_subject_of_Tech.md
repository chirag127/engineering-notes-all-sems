### Clarity of substance

- Clarity of substance refers to the quality of being clear, coherent, and understandable in the content of a technical presentation.
- Clarity of substance is important for effective communication, as it helps the audience to follow the main points, arguments, and evidence of the presenter, and to avoid confusion, ambiguity, or misunderstanding.
- Clarity of substance can be achieved by using the following strategies:

  - Define the purpose and scope of the presentation, and state the main idea or thesis statement in the introduction.
  - Organize the content logically and coherently, using appropriate transitions, headings, and subheadings to guide the audience through the presentation.
  - Use clear and precise language, avoiding jargon, slang, or unnecessary words. Use consistent terminology and definitions throughout the presentation.
  - Provide relevant and sufficient evidence, examples, and data to support the main points and arguments. Cite the sources of information and acknowledge the limitations or uncertainties of the evidence.
  - Summarize the main points and restate the main idea or thesis statement in the conclusion. Provide recommendations or implications for further action or research if applicable.
