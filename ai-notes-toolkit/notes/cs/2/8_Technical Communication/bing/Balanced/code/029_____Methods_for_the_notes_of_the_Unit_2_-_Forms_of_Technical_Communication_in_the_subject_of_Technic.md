### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations, especially about technical or specialized topics, using various modes and media  .
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading the audience.
- Technical communication can be divided into two main categories: internal and external.
  - Internal technical communication is directed to the members of the same organization, such as colleagues, managers, or employees. It can include status reports, project proposals, memos, emails, manuals, specifications, etc.
  - External technical communication is directed to the customers, clients, stakeholders, or the general public. It can include websites, blogs, help files, user guides, white papers, articles, patents, videos, etc.
- Technical communication requires certain skills and knowledge, such as:
  - Understanding the needs, expectations, and preferences of the audience .
  - Researching and organizing the relevant information from credible sources .
  - Using clear, concise, accurate, and appropriate language and style  .
  - Designing and formatting the document or product for readability, usability, and accessibility  .
  - Using visual elements, such as images, graphs, tables, etc., to enhance the communication  .
  - Collaborating with other technical communicators, subject matter experts, editors, etc., to produce and revise the deliverables .
  - Adhering to the ethical and legal standards of the profession  .
