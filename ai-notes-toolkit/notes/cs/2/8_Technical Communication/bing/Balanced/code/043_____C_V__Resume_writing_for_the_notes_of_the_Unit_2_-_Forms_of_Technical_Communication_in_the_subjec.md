### C.V./Resume writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A C.V. (curriculum vitae) or a resume is a document that summarizes one's education, skills, work experience, and other relevant information for a job application.
- A C.V. or a resume is usually the first impression that a potential employer gets of a job seeker, so it is important to make it clear, concise, and professional.
- A C.V. or a resume should be tailored to the specific job and employer, highlighting the most relevant qualifications and achievements that match the job requirements.
- A C.V. or a resume should follow a consistent format and style, using appropriate headings, fonts, bullets, and spacing to make it easy to read and scan.
- A C.V. or a resume should be proofread and edited for accuracy, grammar, spelling, and punctuation, as well as for avoiding any false or exaggerated information.

Some general guidelines for writing a C.V. or a resume are:

- Use a reverse chronological order to list your education and work experience, starting with the most recent and working backwards.
- Include your contact information, such as name, address, phone number, and email, at the top of the document.
- Use a clear and descriptive title or objective statement that summarizes your main goal and qualifications for the job.
- Highlight your skills and achievements that are relevant to the job, using keywords and phrases from the job description and the employer's website.
- Use action verbs and quantifiable results to describe your accomplishments and responsibilities in your work experience, such as "managed a team of 10 people", "increased sales by 15%", or "improved customer satisfaction by 20%".
- Use bullet points to organize your information and make it easier to read and scan.
- Limit your C.V. or resume to one or two pages, depending on your level of experience and the industry norms.
- Avoid using personal pronouns, such as "I", "me", or "my", as well as any irrelevant or personal information, such as age, marital status, hobbies, or political views.
- Use a professional and appropriate tone and language, avoiding any slang, jargon, or abbreviations that may not be understood by the employer.
- Use a simple and legible font, such as Arial, Times New Roman, or Calibri, and a font size of 10 to 12 points.
- Use consistent formatting and alignment, such as bold, italic, or underline, to emphasize important information and headings.
- Use white or light-colored paper and a high-quality printer or scanner to produce a clean and crisp copy of your C.V. or resume.
- Save your C.V. or resume as a PDF file to preserve the formatting and avoid any compatibility issues with different software or devices.
- Review your C.V. or resume for any errors or typos, and ask someone else to proofread it as well.
- Update your C.V. or resume regularly to reflect your current skills and achievements, and to keep it relevant for the job market.
