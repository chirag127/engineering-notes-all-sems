### Synopsis writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A synopsis is a brief summary of the main points and ideas of a longer text, such as a book, an article, or a report.
- A synopsis can help the reader to quickly understand the main purpose, scope, and content of the text, without having to read it in full.
- A synopsis can also help the writer to organize and structure their text, by highlighting the key points and arguments, and showing how they are connected and supported by evidence.
- A synopsis should be concise, clear, and accurate, and should avoid unnecessary details, opinions, or interpretations.
- A synopsis should follow the same order and structure as the original text, and should use the same terminology and style.
- A synopsis should include the following elements:

  - The title and author of the text
  - The main topic or theme of the text
  - The main thesis or argument of the text
  - The main supporting points and evidence for the thesis or argument
  - The main conclusion or implication of the text

- A synopsis should be written in the present tense, and should use direct quotations sparingly, if at all.
- A synopsis should be formatted according to the guidelines of the specific assignment or publication, and should include proper citations and references for the original text.
- A synopsis should be proofread and revised for clarity, coherence, and correctness, before submitting or publishing it.
