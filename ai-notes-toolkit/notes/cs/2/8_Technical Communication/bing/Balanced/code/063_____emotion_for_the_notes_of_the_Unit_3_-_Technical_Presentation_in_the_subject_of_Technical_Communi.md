# Emotion

Emotion is a topic that relates to the technical presentation in the subject of technical communication because it affects how the presenter communicates with the audience, how the audience perceives the message, and how the message influences the audience's behavior and decision-making.

Some of the points that can be covered in the notes are:

- Emotion is a natural instinctive state of mind deriving from one's circumstances, mood, or relationships with others  .
- Emotion is often accompanied by a physical reaction, such as facial expression, body language, tone of voice, or physiological changes, such as heart rate, blood pressure, or sweating .
- Emotion can be classified into different types, such as basic emotions (e.g., anger, fear, joy, sadness, disgust, surprise), complex emotions (e.g., guilt, shame, pride, envy, jealousy, love, hate), or mixed emotions (e.g., ambivalence, bittersweet, nostalgia).
- Emotion can be influenced by various factors, such as personality, culture, context, mood, memory, cognition, motivation, or social interaction.
- Emotion can have positive or negative effects on the technical presentation, depending on how the presenter and the audience manage and express their emotions.
- Some of the positive effects of emotion on the technical presentation are: enhancing attention, interest, engagement, motivation, persuasion, recall, and learning.
- Some of the negative effects of emotion on the technical presentation are: impairing attention, comprehension, judgment, critical thinking, and decision-making.
- Some of the strategies to use emotion effectively in the technical presentation are: analyzing the audience's needs, expectations, and emotional state; choosing appropriate emotional appeals and tone; using verbal and nonverbal cues to convey emotion; balancing emotion and logic; and adapting to the feedback and reactions of the audience.
