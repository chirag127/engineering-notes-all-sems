### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations, especially about technical or specialized topics, using various modes and media, and for various purposes and audiences  .

Some of the common forms of technical communication are:

- **Letters and emails**: These are written messages that convey information, requests, feedback, or instructions to a specific recipient or group of recipients. They are usually brief, clear, and courteous, and follow a standard format and style. Letters and emails are used for both internal and external communication in organizations  .
- **Instructions**: These are step-by-step directions that guide the reader on how to perform a specific task or procedure. They usually include an introduction, a list of materials or equipment, a sequence of actions, and a conclusion. Instructions may also contain visuals, such as diagrams, screenshots, or photographs, to illustrate the steps. Instructions are used for both internal and external communication in organizations, and they may be delivered in print, online, or video formats  .
- **Status reports**: These are written documents that provide updates on the progress, achievements, challenges, and plans of a project or a team. They usually include an introduction, a summary of the current situation, a description of the work done, a discussion of the problems encountered and the solutions proposed, and a conclusion. Status reports are used for internal communication in organizations, and they may be delivered in print, online, or oral formats  .
- **Project proposals**: These are written documents that present a plan for a new project or a change in an existing project. They usually include an introduction, a statement of the problem or opportunity, a description of the proposed solution or approach, a discussion of the benefits and feasibility of the proposal, and a conclusion. Project proposals are used for both internal and external communication in organizations, and they may be delivered in print, online, or oral formats  .
- **Websites and blogs**: These are online platforms that provide information, opinions, or entertainment on a specific topic or theme. They usually consist of a homepage, a navigation menu, and a series of web pages or posts that contain text, images, videos, links, or other multimedia elements. Websites and blogs are used for both internal and external communication in organizations, and they may be interactive, allowing the users to comment, share, or rate the content  .
- **Online help**: These are online resources that provide assistance or guidance to the users of a product, service, or system. They usually consist of a help menu, a search function, and a series of topics or articles that contain text, images, videos, links, or other multimedia elements. Online help is used for external communication in organizations, and it may be embedded in the product, service, or system, or accessed through a web browser  .
- **User manuals**: These are printed or online documents that provide information on how to use, maintain, troubleshoot, or repair a product, service, or system. They usually consist of a cover page, a table of contents, an introduction, a series of sections or chapters that contain text, images, videos, links, or other multimedia elements, and an index. User manuals are used for external communication in organizations, and they may be delivered in print, online, or video formats  .
- **Technical manuals**: These are printed or online documents that provide information on how a product, service, or system works, its specifications, its components, its functions, or its features. They usually consist of a cover page, a table of contents, an introduction, a series of sections or chapters that contain text, images, videos, links, or other multimedia elements, and an index. Technical manuals are used for both internal and external communication in organizations, and they may be delivered in print, online, or video formats  .
