### Technical Proposal for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical proposal is a document that proposes a solution to a problem or a course of action for a specific situation.
- A technical proposal can be either solicited or unsolicited, depending on whether the client or the audience has requested it or not.
- A technical proposal should have the following elements:
  - A title page that includes the name of the project, the name of the proposer, the name of the client or the audience, and the date of submission.
  - An abstract or an executive summary that summarizes the main points of the proposal, such as the problem, the solution, the benefits, the costs, and the timeline.
  - A table of contents that lists the major sections and sub-sections of the proposal, along with their page numbers.
  - An introduction that introduces the problem, the background, the purpose, the scope, and the objectives of the proposal.
  - A literature review that surveys the existing research and literature on the topic, and identifies the gaps or the needs that the proposal addresses.
  - A methodology that describes the methods, procedures, tools, and techniques that will be used to carry out the project, and explains the rationale and the feasibility of the chosen approach.
  - A results or a discussion section that presents the expected or the actual outcomes of the project, and analyzes the implications and the significance of the findings.
  - A conclusion that summarizes the main points of the proposal, and reiterates the benefits, the costs, and the timeline of the project.
  - A list of references that cites the sources that have been used or consulted in the proposal, following a consistent citation style.
  - An appendix that includes any additional or supplementary information that supports the proposal, such as tables, graphs, charts, diagrams, figures, or samples.
