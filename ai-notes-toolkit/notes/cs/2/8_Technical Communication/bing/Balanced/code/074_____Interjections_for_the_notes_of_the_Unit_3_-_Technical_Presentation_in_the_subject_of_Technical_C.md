### Interjections

- An interjection is a word or phrase that expresses a strong feeling or sudden emotion, such as surprise, excitement, happiness, or anger  .
- An interjection is usually followed by an exclamation mark (!) or a comma (,) depending on the intensity of the emotion .
- An interjection can stand alone as a sentence or be inserted into a sentence .
- An interjection has no grammatical connection to the rest of the sentence  .
- An interjection can be a single word, a phrase, or a sound  .

#### Examples of Interjections

- Single-word interjections: Wow! Ouch! Yay! Ugh! Oops! Hooray!  
- Phrase interjections: Oh no! Well done! Good grief! Holy cow!  
- Sound interjections: Ah! Eh! Mm! Shh!  

#### Types of Interjections

- Interjections can be classified into different types based on the emotion or function they convey .
- Some common types of interjections are:
  - Interjections of joy or happiness: These interjections express a positive feeling of delight or satisfaction, such as Yippee! Hooray! Cheers! 
  - Interjections of surprise or wonder: These interjections express a feeling of astonishment or curiosity, such as Wow! Really? Amazing! 
  - Interjections of pain or sorrow: These interjections express a feeling of physical or emotional hurt or sadness, such as Ouch! Alas! Boo-hoo! 
  - Interjections of anger or annoyance: These interjections express a feeling of displeasure or irritation, such as Grr! Darn! Ugh! 
  - Interjections of agreement or approval: These interjections express a feeling of consent or appreciation, such as Yes! Sure! Bravo! 
  - Interjections of disagreement or disapproval: These interjections express a feeling of dissent or criticism, such as No! Never! Boo! 
  - Interjections of greeting or farewell: These interjections express a feeling of welcome or goodbye, such as Hello! Hi! Bye! 
  - Interjections of hesitation or doubt: These interjections express a feeling of uncertainty or indecision, such as Um! Er! Well! 
  - Interjections of attention or calling: These interjections express a feeling of alertness or summoning, such as Hey! Psst! Look! 
  - Interjections of apology or gratitude: These interjections express a feeling of regret or thankfulness, such as Sorry! Oops! Thanks!
