### Expert Technical Lecture for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations, especially about technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations .
- Technical communication also involves using technology to create and distribute communication, such as web pages, help files, or social media sites.
- Technical communication also provides instructions about how to do something, regardless of how technical the task is or even if technology is used to create or distribute that communication.
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication can take different forms, such as business letters, memos, reports, notices, proposals, websites, blogs, and more .
- Technical communication requires certain skills, such as understanding the audience, the context, and the goal of the communication; researching and organizing the information; using clear and concise language; designing and formatting the document; and revising and editing the document .
- Technical communication also follows certain principles, such as accuracy, clarity, conciseness, coherence, completeness, correctness, courtesy, and ethics .
- Technical communication is important for various reasons, such as enhancing the productivity and efficiency of the organization, improving the quality and safety of the products and services, facilitating the collaboration and communication among the stakeholders, and building the credibility and reputation of the organization .
