### Interview skills for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

- Interview skills are the abilities and techniques that help candidates perform well in job interviews and communicate their value to potential employers.
- Technical communication skills are the abilities and knowledge that help technical communicators create, organize, and present information in a clear, concise, and user-friendly way.
- Interview skills and technical communication skills are closely related, as both involve conveying complex information to different audiences and adapting to different situations and feedback.
- Some of the interview skills that are relevant for technical communication roles are:

  - **Nonverbal communication skills**: These are the skills that help candidates express their emotions, attitudes, and confidence through their body language, eye contact, gestures, and facial expressions. Nonverbal communication skills can help candidates create a positive impression, show interest and enthusiasm, and build rapport with the interviewer.
  - **Verbal communication skills**: These are the skills that help candidates articulate their thoughts, ideas, and opinions in a clear, concise, and coherent way. Verbal communication skills can help candidates demonstrate their knowledge, skills, and experience, answer questions effectively, and ask relevant and insightful questions.
  - **Friendliness**: This is the skill that helps candidates show their personality, warmth, and professionalism in the interview. Friendliness can help candidates establish a good relationship with the interviewer, show their fit for the company culture, and make the interview more enjoyable and comfortable.
  - **Brevity**: This is the skill that helps candidates deliver their messages in a concise and precise way, without unnecessary details, repetitions, or filler words. Brevity can help candidates avoid rambling, stay focused, and respect the interviewer's time and attention.
  - **Respect**: This is the skill that helps candidates show their appreciation, courtesy, and consideration for the interviewer and the company. Respect can help candidates avoid interrupting, arguing, or criticizing the interviewer, follow the interview etiquette, and express their gratitude and interest.
  - **Empathy**: This is the skill that helps candidates understand the interviewer's perspective, needs, and expectations, and tailor their responses accordingly. Empathy can help candidates address the interviewer's concerns, show their value and relevance, and build trust and rapport.
  - **Listening skills**: These are the skills that help candidates pay attention, comprehend, and remember what the interviewer says, and respond appropriately. Listening skills can help candidates avoid misunderstandings, show their interest and engagement, and provide relevant and accurate answers.
  - **Confidence**: This is the skill that helps candidates show their self-assurance, competence, and readiness for the role. Confidence can help candidates overcome nervousness, speak with authority and clarity, and highlight their strengths and achievements.

- Some of the technical communication skills that are relevant for interview skills are:

  - **Writing skills**: These are the skills that help candidates create effective and professional resumes, cover letters, portfolios, and thank-you notes that showcase their qualifications, achievements, and fit for the role. Writing skills can help candidates catch the interviewer's attention, highlight their value proposition, and follow up after the interview.
  - **Research skills**: These are the skills that help candidates gather, analyze, and synthesize relevant and reliable information about the company, the role, the industry, and the interviewer. Research skills can help candidates prepare for the interview, show their interest and knowledge, and ask informed and meaningful questions.
  - **Presentation skills**: These are the skills that help candidates deliver engaging and persuasive oral presentations that demonstrate their technical expertise, problem-solving skills, and creativity. Presentation skills can help candidates showcase their projects, products, or solutions, explain their process and rationale, and handle questions and feedback.
  - **Design skills**: These are the skills that help candidates create visually appealing and user-friendly documents, slides, graphics, or prototypes that support their written or oral communication. Design skills can help candidates enhance their message, attract and retain the interviewer's attention, and improve the readability and usability of their communication.
  - **Collaboration skills**: These are the skills that help candidates work effectively and efficiently with others, such as team members, subject matter experts, or stakeholders. Collaboration skills can help candidates show their teamwork, communication, and interpersonal skills, share their ideas and opinions, and receive and provide constructive feedback.
