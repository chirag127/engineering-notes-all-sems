### Socio-linguistic competence

- Socio-linguistic competence is the ability to understand and use language that is appropriate to the social and cultural context of communication  .
- Socio-linguistic competence involves knowing the norms, values, beliefs, and behavioral patterns of a culture, and how they affect language use.
- Socio-linguistic competence also involves knowing how to vary one's language according to the purpose, topic, setting, interlocutor, and register of communication.
- Socio-linguistic competence is different from linguistic competence, which is the system of unconscious knowledge that one has when they know a language.
- Socio-linguistic competence is important for effective technical communication, as it helps to avoid misunderstandings, misinterpretations, and conflicts that may arise from cultural differences or inappropriate language use.
