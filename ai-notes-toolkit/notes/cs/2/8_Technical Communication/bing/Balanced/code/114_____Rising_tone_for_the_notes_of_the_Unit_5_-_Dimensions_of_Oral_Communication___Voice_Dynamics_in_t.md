### Rising tone

- A rising tone is a type of intonation pattern in which the pitch of the voice rises at the end of a sentence or a phrase.
- A rising tone can be used for different purposes in oral communication, such as:
  - Asking yes/no questions, which expect an answer from the listener. For example: "Do you like pizza?"  
  - Requesting clarification, which indicates uncertainty or confusion. For example: "What did you say?" 
  - Expressing high energy emotions, such as happiness, anger, or shock. For example: "Wow, that's amazing!" 
- A rising tone can also be combined with a falling tone to create a fall-rise intonation, which shows contrast, reservation, or surprise. For example: "She's very smart, but she's lazy." 
- A rising tone can affect the meaning and tone of a message, so it is important to use it appropriately and effectively in oral communication.
