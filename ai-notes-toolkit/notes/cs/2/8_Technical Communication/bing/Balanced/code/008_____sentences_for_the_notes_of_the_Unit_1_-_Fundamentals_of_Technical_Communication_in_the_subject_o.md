### Sentences for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations.
- Technical communication is a broad field and includes any form of communication that exhibits one or more of the following characteristics:
  - Communicating about technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations.
  - Communicating by using technology, such as web pages, help files, or social media sites.
  - Providing instructions about how to do something, regardless of how technical the task is or even if technology is used to create or distribute that communication.
- The goal of technical communication is to create easily comprehensible information for a target specific audience.
- Technical communication requires the following skills:
  - Understanding the target audience and their needs, expectations, and preferences.
  - Gathering technical knowledge and information from reliable sources and experts.
  - Adopting communication skills such as clarity, conciseness, coherence, accuracy, and ethics.
  - Using appropriate writing tools and formats to create and deliver technical documents, such as product descriptions, user manuals, white papers, journal articles, and websites.
  - Applying design principles and visual aids to enhance the readability and usability of technical documents.
  - Revising and editing technical documents to ensure quality and consistency.
  - Evaluating the effectiveness and impact of technical communication on the audience and the organization.
