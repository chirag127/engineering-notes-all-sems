# Clarity of substance for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Clarity of substance refers to the quality of the content and the information that is presented in a technical presentation.
- Clarity of substance is important for achieving the purpose and the objectives of the presentation, as well as for engaging and persuading the audience.
- Clarity of substance can be achieved by following some guidelines, such as:

  - Define the topic and the scope of the presentation clearly and concisely.
  - Identify the main points and the sub-points that support the topic and the scope.
  - Organize the points and the sub-points in a logical and coherent manner, using appropriate transitions and signposts.
  - Use relevant and reliable sources of information, and cite them properly.
  - Use clear and precise language, avoiding jargon, ambiguity, and redundancy.
  - Use appropriate visual aids, such as graphs, charts, tables, diagrams, images, etc., to illustrate and enhance the information.
  - Use examples, anecdotes, statistics, facts, etc., to support and explain the information.
  - Use rhetorical devices, such as repetition, contrast, comparison, analogy, etc., to emphasize and highlight the information.
  - Use questions, feedback, and interaction to engage and involve the audience.
  - Use humor, stories, quotations, etc., to add interest and appeal to the presentation.
