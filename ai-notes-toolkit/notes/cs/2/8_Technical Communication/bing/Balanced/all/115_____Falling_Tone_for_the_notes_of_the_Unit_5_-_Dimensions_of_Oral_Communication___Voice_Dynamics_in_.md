# Falling Tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Falling tone is a type of intonation that involves a downward pitch movement at the end of a sentence or a phrase.
- Falling tone is used to communicate different meanings and functions in oral communication, such as:
  - Completing a statement or an idea . For example: _I like your shirt._ / _She is very smart._
  - Asking an information question that begins with who, what, where, when, why, how, etc  . For example: _Where are you from?_ / _How long have you been working here?_
  - Expressing certainty, confidence, or finality . For example: _That's the correct answer._ / _I'm sure about it._
  - Indicating contrast, disagreement, or correction . For example: _No, I don't think so._ / _Actually, it's the other way around._
- Falling tone is essential for clear communication in English, as it helps the listener to understand the speaker's intention, attitude, and mood  .
