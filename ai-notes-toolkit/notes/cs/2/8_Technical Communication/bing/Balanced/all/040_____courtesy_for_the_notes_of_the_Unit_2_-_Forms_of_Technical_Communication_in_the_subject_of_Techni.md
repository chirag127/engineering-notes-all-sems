# Courtesy

- Courtesy is one of the six qualities of effective communication, along with clarity, conciseness, completeness, correctness, and concreteness.
- Courtesy means taking into consideration the viewpoints and feelings of the receiver of the message, and showing respect, politeness, and goodwill .
- Courtesy can be achieved by using positive words, avoiding biased or offensive language, and addressing the audience appropriately  .
- Courtesy can also be enhanced by using a conversational and engaging tone, making eye contact, and using humor or stories when relevant and suitable.
- Courtesy can help to build rapport and trust with the audience, and to avoid misunderstandings or conflicts  .
- Courtesy is especially important in technical communication, which tends to be neutral and objective, and requires precision and accuracy .
- Courtesy can help to make technical communication more reader-friendly, easy-to-read, and constructive .
