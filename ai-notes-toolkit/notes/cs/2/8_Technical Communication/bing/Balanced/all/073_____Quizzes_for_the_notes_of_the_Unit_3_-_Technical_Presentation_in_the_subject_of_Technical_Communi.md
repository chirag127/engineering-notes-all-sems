# Quizzes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. What are the three main purposes of a technical presentation?
    - A) To inform, to persuade, and to entertain
    - B) To inform, to instruct, and to persuade
    - C) To instruct, to persuade, and to entertain
    - D) To inform, to instruct, and to entertain
    - Answer: B

2. What are the four steps of the presentation process?
    - A) Planning, preparing, practicing, and presenting
    - B) Planning, preparing, performing, and presenting
    - C) Planning, producing, practicing, and presenting
    - D) Planning, producing, performing, and presenting
    - Answer: A

3. What are the three types of audience analysis?
    - A) Demographic, psychographic, and situational
    - B) Demographic, psychological, and situational
    - C) Demographic, psychographic, and environmental
    - D) Demographic, psychological, and environmental
    - Answer: A

4. What are the three components of a presentation outline?
    - A) Introduction, body, and conclusion
    - B) Introduction, main points, and conclusion
    - C) Introduction, main points, and summary
    - D) Introduction, body, and summary
    - Answer: A

5. What are the three types of visual aids that can be used in a technical presentation?
    - A) Graphs, charts, and diagrams
    - B) Graphs, tables, and diagrams
    - C) Graphs, charts, and images
    - D) Graphs, tables, and images
    - Answer: D
