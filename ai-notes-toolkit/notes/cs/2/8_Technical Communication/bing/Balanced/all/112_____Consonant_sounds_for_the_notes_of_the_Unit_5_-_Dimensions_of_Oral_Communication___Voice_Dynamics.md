# Consonant sounds for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Consonants are letters that stand for a type of sound we use in speech.
- Consonants involve a partial or complete closure of the vocal tract, which is the passage of air from the lungs to the mouth and nose .
- Consonants can be classified according to three main features: the place of articulation, the manner of articulation, and the voicing.
- The place of articulation refers to the location of the closure or narrowing of the vocal tract, such as the lips, the teeth, the tongue, the palate, or the throat.
- The manner of articulation refers to the type of closure or narrowing of the vocal tract, such as a complete stop, a fricative, an affricate, a nasal, a liquid, or a glide.
- The voicing refers to the presence or absence of vocal cord vibration during the production of the consonant sound, such as voiced or voiceless.
