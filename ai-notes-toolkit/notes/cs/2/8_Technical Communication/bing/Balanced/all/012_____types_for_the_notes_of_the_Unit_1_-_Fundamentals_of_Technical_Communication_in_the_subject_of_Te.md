# Types for the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can be classified into four main types: instructional, informational, persuasive, and transactional.

## Instructional

- Instructional technical communication provides step-by-step guidance on how to perform a task or use a product.
- Examples of instructional technical communication are manuals, tutorials, recipes, procedures, and directions.

## Informational

- Informational technical communication provides factual and objective information about a topic or a phenomenon.
- Examples of informational technical communication are reports, articles, summaries, charts, graphs, and tables.

## Persuasive

- Persuasive technical communication aims to influence the attitudes, beliefs, or actions of the audience.
- Examples of persuasive technical communication are proposals, recommendations, advertisements, and reviews.

## Transactional

- Transactional technical communication facilitates the exchange of information or services between two or more parties.
- Examples of transactional technical communication are emails, letters, memos, invoices, and contracts.
