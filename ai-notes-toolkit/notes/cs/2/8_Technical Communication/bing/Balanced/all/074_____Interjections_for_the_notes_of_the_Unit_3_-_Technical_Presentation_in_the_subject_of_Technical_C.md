# Interjections

- An interjection is a word or phrase that expresses a strong feeling or sudden emotion, such as surprise, excitement, happiness, or anger   .
- An interjection is usually followed by an exclamation mark (!) or a comma (,) depending on the intensity of the emotion .
- An interjection can stand alone as a sentence or be inserted into a sentence .
- An interjection has no grammatical connection to the rest of the sentence   .
- An interjection can be a single word, such as "Wow!" or "Ouch!" or a phrase, such as "Oh no!" or "Good grief!"   .
- An interjection can have different meanings and tones depending on the context and the speaker's intention .

## Examples of Interjections

- Wow! That was an amazing performance! (expresses admiration)
- Ouch! That really hurt! (expresses pain)
- Yay! We won the game! (expresses joy)
- Ugh! I hate this homework! (expresses disgust)
- Oops! I spilled the milk! (expresses mistake)
- Shh! Be quiet! (expresses request)
- Hey! Stop that! (expresses demand)
- Well, I don't know what to say. (expresses hesitation)
- Oh, I see what you mean. (expresses understanding)
- Hooray! It's your birthday! (expresses celebration)
