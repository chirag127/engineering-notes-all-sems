### Rising tone

A rising tone is an intonation pattern in which the pitch of the voice rises over time. It is mainly used for:

- Questions with a yes/no answer 
- Requests for clarification (indicates uncertainty)
- High energy emotions such as happiness, anger, or shock

Examples of rising tone:

- Do you like chocolate?
- What did you say?
- Wow, that's amazing!

Rising tone can also indicate:

- Challenging or impatient attitude
- Suspicion or annoyance
- Incompleteness or continuation

Examples of rising tone with these meanings:

- You did what?
- Are you sure?
- Well, I think

Rising tone is one of the dimensions of oral communication and voice dynamics that can affect the meaning and tone of a message. It can convey different attitudes and emotions depending on the context and the speaker's intention.
