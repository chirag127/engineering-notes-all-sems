### C.V./Resume writing

A C.V. (Curriculum Vitae) or a resume is a document that summarizes your educational and professional background, skills, achievements, and other relevant information for a job or academic application. A C.V. is usually longer than a resume and includes more details, such as publications, research, awards, etc. A resume is typically one or two pages long and tailored to the specific position you are applying for.

Here are some general tips for writing a C.V. or a resume:

- Use a clear, professional, and consistent format and font throughout the document.
- Start with a header that includes your name and contact information, such as phone number, email address, and address.
- Write a brief and concise professional summary that highlights your main qualifications and objectives for the application.
- List your education in reverse chronological order, starting with the most recent or highest degree. Include the name and location of the institution, the degree and major, and the date of completion or expected completion. You can also mention any honors, awards, scholarships, or relevant coursework.
- List your work experience in reverse chronological order, starting with the most recent or current position. Include the name and location of the employer, the title and duration of the position, and the main duties and achievements. Use bullet points and action verbs to describe your accomplishments and skills. Quantify your results whenever possible, such as using numbers, percentages, or metrics.
- List any other relevant sections, such as skills, languages, certifications, licenses, publications, presentations, projects, volunteer work, hobbies, or interests. Choose the sections that best showcase your qualifications and relevance for the application. Use bullet points and keywords to highlight your abilities and achievements.
- Proofread and edit your document for spelling, grammar, punctuation, and formatting errors. Use a spell checker and a grammar checker tool, such as Grammarly, to catch any mistakes. Ask someone else to review your document and give you feedback.
- Save your document as a PDF file to preserve the format and layout. Name your file with your name and the type of document, such as John_Smith_CV.pdf or Jane_Doe_Resume.pdf.
