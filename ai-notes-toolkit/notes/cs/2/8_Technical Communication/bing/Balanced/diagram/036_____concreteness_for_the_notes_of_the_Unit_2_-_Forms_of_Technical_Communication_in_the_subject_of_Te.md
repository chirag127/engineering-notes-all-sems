### Concreteness

- Concreteness is an aspect of communication that means being specific, definite, and vivid rather than vague and general .
- A concrete communication uses specific facts and figures  and supports them with credible sources .
- Concreteness is one of the six C's of effective communication, along with clarity, correctness, completeness, conciseness, and courtesy .
- Concreteness helps the audience better understand and interpret the message as accurately as possible, in the way the sender intended .
- Concreteness also enhances the credibility and persuasiveness of the message, as it shows that the sender has done their research and has evidence to back up their claims .
- Concreteness can be achieved by using specific words, examples, explanations, and details that are relevant and meaningful to the audience  .
- Concreteness can be improved by avoiding abstract language, which refers to intangible ideas or qualities, such as love, hate, or honor.
- Concreteness can also be improved by replacing adjectives or adverbs with more precise and descriptive words.
- Concreteness is especially important in technical communication, as it helps to convey complex and technical information in a clear and accurate way.
- Concreteness also helps to avoid misunderstanding, confusion, or ambiguity in technical communication, which can have serious consequences in some fields or situations .
