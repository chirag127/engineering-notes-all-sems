### Style for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Style is the way a writer expresses ideas in a document.
- Style affects the clarity, accuracy, and effectiveness of technical communication.
- Style can be influenced by the purpose, audience, and context of the communication.
- Some general principles of style for technical communication are:

  - Use plain language: avoid jargon, slang, and unnecessary words.
  - Use active voice: emphasize the doer of the action rather than the receiver.
  - Use parallel structure: make sure that items in a list or series have the same grammatical form.
  - Use headings and subheadings: organize the document into sections and subsections with clear and descriptive titles.
  - Use lists and tables: present information in a visual and concise way.
  - Use consistent terminology: use the same word or phrase for the same concept throughout the document.
  - Use appropriate tone: match the level of formality and politeness to the audience and situation.
  - Use positive and respectful language: avoid negative, biased, or offensive words and expressions.
