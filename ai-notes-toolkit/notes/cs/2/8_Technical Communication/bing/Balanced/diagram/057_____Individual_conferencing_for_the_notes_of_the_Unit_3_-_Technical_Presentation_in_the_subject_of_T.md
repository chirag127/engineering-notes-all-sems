### Individual conferencing

- Individual conferencing is a process of meeting one-on-one with a student to provide feedback, guidance, and support for their technical presentation.
- Individual conferencing can help the student to improve their presentation skills, identify their strengths and weaknesses, and address any questions or concerns they might have.
- Individual conferencing can also help the instructor to assess the student's progress, provide personalized instruction, and build rapport and trust with the student.
- Individual conferencing can be done in person, online, or by phone, depending on the availability and preference of the student and the instructor.
- Individual conferencing can be scheduled at any stage of the presentation process, such as before, during, or after the research, planning, drafting, revising, or delivering of the presentation.

### Benefits of individual conferencing

- Individual conferencing can offer several benefits for both the student and the instructor, such as:
  - Enhancing the student's motivation, confidence, and self-efficacy for their presentation.
  - Providing the student with specific, constructive, and timely feedback that can help them improve their presentation content, structure, style, and delivery.
  - Addressing the student's individual needs, goals, challenges, and interests for their presentation.
  - Encouraging the student to reflect on their presentation process and outcomes, and to set realistic and achievable expectations for themselves.
  - Fostering the student's critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication skills for their presentation.
  - Developing the student's autonomy, responsibility, and ownership for their presentation.
  - Creating a positive and supportive learning environment for the student, where they can express their ideas, opinions, and feelings freely and respectfully.
  - Establishing a collaborative and trusting relationship between the student and the instructor, where they can exchange feedback, suggestions, and resources for the presentation.
  - Monitoring the student's progress, performance, and satisfaction for their presentation.
  - Evaluating the student's presentation skills, knowledge, and competencies, and providing them with appropriate grades, comments, and recommendations.
