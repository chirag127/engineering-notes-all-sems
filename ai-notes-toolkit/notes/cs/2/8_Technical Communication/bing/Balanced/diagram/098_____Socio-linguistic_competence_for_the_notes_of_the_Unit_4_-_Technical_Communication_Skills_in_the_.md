### Socio-linguistic competence

- Socio-linguistic competence is the ability to use language that is appropriate to social and cultural contexts .
- Socio-linguistic competence involves knowing how to vary one's language according to the setting, the topic, and the relationships among the people communicating .
- Socio-linguistic competence also includes understanding the pragmatic meaning of language, such as the implied or indirect meaning, the tone, the politeness, and the humor .
- Socio-linguistic competence is different from linguistic competence, which is the system of unconscious knowledge that one knows when they know a language.
- Socio-linguistic competence is important for effective communication across cultures and situations, as it helps to avoid misunderstandings, misinterpretations, and offense .
- Socio-linguistic competence can be developed by exposing oneself to different varieties of language, observing how people use language in different contexts, and reflecting on one's own language use and choices .
