### Types of Technical Communication

Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose. Technical communication can be classified into four main types:

- **Instructional communication**: This type of communication provides step-by-step guidance on how to perform a task, use a product, or follow a procedure. Examples of instructional communication are user manuals, tutorials, recipes, and online help.
- **Informative communication**: This type of communication provides factual information about a topic, such as its features, benefits, limitations, or applications. Examples of informative communication are reports, brochures, newsletters, and white papers.
- **Persuasive communication**: This type of communication aims to influence the audience's attitude, behavior, or decision regarding a topic. Examples of persuasive communication are proposals, advertisements, sales letters, and presentations.
- **Expressive communication**: This type of communication expresses the personal feelings, opinions, or experiences of the author or the organization. Examples of expressive communication are blogs, podcasts, reviews, and testimonials.
