### Conciseness
- Conciseness is the quality of using only the words that are necessary to convey the intended meaning.
- Conciseness helps the reader to understand the message quickly and easily, and avoids wasting their time and attention.
- Conciseness is especially important in technical communication, where the audience may have limited time, interest, or background knowledge to read the document.
- To achieve conciseness, the writer should follow these guidelines:
  - Use specific and precise words, rather than vague or general ones.
  - Avoid unnecessary repetition of words, phrases, or ideas.
  - Eliminate wordiness, such as redundant modifiers, filler words, or passive voice.
  - Use short and simple sentences, rather than long and complex ones.
  - Use lists, tables, or diagrams, rather than paragraphs, to present information clearly and efficiently.
  - Organize the document logically, and use headings, subheadings, and transitions to guide the reader.
  - Revise and edit the document carefully, and remove any irrelevant or outdated information.
