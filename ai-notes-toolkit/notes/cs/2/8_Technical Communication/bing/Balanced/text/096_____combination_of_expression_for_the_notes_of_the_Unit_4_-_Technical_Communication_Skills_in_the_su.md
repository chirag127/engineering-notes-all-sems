### Combination of expression for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying expert, technical, or highly complex information to a non-expert or more general audience.
- Technical communication often works to convince an audience to take some specific action, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication requires a concise, precise, and professional style that focuses on the goal of the communication rather than on personal opinions or feelings.
- Technical communication often uses a combination of various modes, such as text, image, video, sound, etc., to communicate effectively and efficiently .
- These types of communication are called multimodal, which means that a text is using more than one communication mode (such as words plus images) .
- Multimodal communication can enhance the clarity, accessibility, and appeal of technical communication, as well as cater to different learning styles and preferences of the audience .
- Some examples of multimodal technical communication are websites, infographics, podcasts, videos, presentations, etc.
