### Modes of Presentation

- Modes of presentation are the different methods or types of speech delivery used in technical communication.
- They are chosen based on the purpose, audience, and content of the presentation.
- The four main modes of presentation are:

  - **Impromptu**: This mode involves speaking without any prior preparation or notes. It is usually used for short, informal, or spontaneous presentations, such as answering questions, giving feedback, or introducing oneself. Impromptu presentations require quick thinking, confidence, and clarity of expression.
  - **Manuscript**: This mode involves reading a written text word for word. It is usually used for long, formal, or complex presentations, such as delivering a report, a lecture, or a speech. Manuscript presentations require careful writing, editing, and rehearsing. They also require attention to vocal variety, eye contact, and body language to avoid sounding monotonous or boring.
  - **Memorized**: This mode involves reciting a text from memory. It is usually used for short, formal, or persuasive presentations, such as giving a toast, a tribute, or a sales pitch. Memorized presentations require a lot of practice, memorization, and recall. They also require naturalness, enthusiasm, and flexibility to adapt to the audience and the situation.
  - **Extemporaneous**: This mode involves speaking from an outline or a set of notes. It is usually used for medium-length, semi-formal, or interactive presentations, such as giving a demonstration, a tutorial, or a workshop. Extemporaneous presentations require research, organization, and preparation. They also require fluency, coherence, and engagement to maintain the interest and attention of the audience.
