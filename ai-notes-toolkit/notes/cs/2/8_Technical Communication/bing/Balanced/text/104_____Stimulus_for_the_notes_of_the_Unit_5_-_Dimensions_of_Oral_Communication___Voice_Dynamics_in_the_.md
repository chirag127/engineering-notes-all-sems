### Stimulus for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- A stimulus is an idea or a concept that is activated by the sender in the communication process .
- A stimulus can be internal or external, depending on the source of the idea or the concept.
- A stimulus can be attention-getting in a productive or distracting way, depending on the context and the purpose of the communication.
- A stimulus can be verbal or non-verbal, depending on the mode of communication.
- A stimulus can be based on personal experiences, observations, opinions, facts, or emotions .
- A stimulus can be influenced by the oral sensory system, which includes the senses of taste, touch, smell, and proprioception.
- A stimulus can be enhanced by using gestures, sounds, colors, shapes, numbers, and other elements that help the receiver connect and understand the message.
- A stimulus can be evaluated by the receiver based on their perception, interpretation, and feedback.
