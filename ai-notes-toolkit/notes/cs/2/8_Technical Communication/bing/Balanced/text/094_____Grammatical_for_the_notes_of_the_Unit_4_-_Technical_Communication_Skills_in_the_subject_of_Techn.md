### Grammatical

- Grammatical is an adjective that means relating to grammar or conforming to the rules of grammar   .
- Grammar is the system and structure of a language, including the sounds, words, sentences, and punctuation .
- Grammatical meaning is the part of meaning that varies from one inflectional form to another (as from plays to played to playing) .
- Grammatical meaning is contrasted with lexical meaning, which is the part of meaning that is constant and does not depend on the inflectional form of a word .
- Grammatical meaning can be expressed by various grammatical categories, such as tense, mood, aspect, number, gender, case, person, voice, etc.
- Grammatical meaning can also be conveyed by word order, punctuation, intonation, and other syntactic and prosodic features.
- Grammatical meaning is important for understanding and producing correct and acceptable sentences in a language.
- Grammatical meaning can vary across different languages, dialects, registers, and styles.
- Grammatical meaning can be studied by grammatical analysis, which is the process of identifying and describing the grammatical categories, structures, and functions of words and sentences in a language .
