### Synopsis writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A synopsis is a brief summary of the main points and purpose of a longer document or text.
- A synopsis can help the reader to quickly grasp the main idea and scope of the document or text, and to decide whether to read it further or not.
- A synopsis should be concise, clear, accurate, and objective. It should not include any personal opinions, interpretations, or evaluations of the document or text.
- A synopsis should follow the same structure and order as the original document or text, but use shorter sentences and simpler words. It should also avoid unnecessary details, examples, quotations, and references.
- A synopsis should include the following elements:
  - The title and author of the document or text.
  - The main topic and purpose of the document or text.
  - The main arguments or points that support the purpose of the document or text.
  - The main conclusions or implications of the document or text.
- A synopsis should be written in the present tense, unless the document or text refers to past or future events. It should also use the same point of view and tone as the original document or text, unless otherwise specified by the instructor or the assignment guidelines.
- A synopsis should be formatted according to the style and citation rules of the discipline or field of study. It should also include a word count and a reference to the original document or text at the end.
