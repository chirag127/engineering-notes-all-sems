### Description for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- This unit covers the basic concepts and principles of technical communication skills, which are essential for engineers and professionals in the technical fields.
- The unit introduces the following topics:
  - The definition and characteristics of technical communication, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, and appropriateness for the audience and purpose.
  - The process and strategies of technical communication, such as planning, researching, drafting, revising, and editing.
  - The types and formats of technical documents, such as reports, proposals, manuals, instructions, and presentations.
  - The ethical and legal issues in technical communication, such as plagiarism, copyright, and confidentiality.
  - The use of visual aids and graphics in technical communication, such as tables, charts, diagrams, and photographs.
  - The oral and written communication skills for technical presentations, such as organization, delivery, and feedback.
- The unit aims to help the students to develop their technical communication skills and apply them to various technical situations and contexts.
- The unit also provides examples and exercises to practice and improve the technical communication skills.
