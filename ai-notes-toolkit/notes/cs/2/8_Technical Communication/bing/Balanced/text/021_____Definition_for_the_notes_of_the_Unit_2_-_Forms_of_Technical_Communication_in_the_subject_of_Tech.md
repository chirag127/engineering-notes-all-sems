### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Forms of technical communication are the different ways of presenting technical information and instructions to the audience, such as reports, manuals, proposals, memos, presentations, etc.
- The forms of technical communication vary depending on the purpose, audience, context, and medium of the communication.
- Some common characteristics of the forms of technical communication are:

  - They are clear, concise, accurate, and objective.
  - They use appropriate language, tone, style, and format for the audience and the situation.
  - They follow the principles of organization, coherence, cohesion, and emphasis.
  - They use visual aids, such as graphs, tables, diagrams, images, etc., to support and enhance the verbal information.
  - They adhere to the ethical and legal standards of the profession and the organization.
