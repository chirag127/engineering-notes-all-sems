### Essentials for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical presentation is a form of oral communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience, such as engineers, scientists, managers, or customers.
- The purpose of a technical presentation is to inform, persuade, or instruct the audience about a technical topic, such as a new product, a research project, a problem solution, or a proposal.
- The essentials for the notes of a technical presentation are:

  - **Audience analysis**: Identify the needs, expectations, background, and interests of the audience, and tailor the presentation accordingly.
  - **Objective statement**: Define the main goal and the specific outcomes of the presentation, and state them clearly and concisely at the beginning and the end of the presentation.
  - **Content organization**: Arrange the content of the presentation in a logical and coherent structure, using an introduction, a body, and a conclusion. Use transitions, headings, and signposts to guide the audience through the presentation.
  - **Visual aids**: Use appropriate and effective visual aids, such as graphs, charts, diagrams, images, or videos, to support and enhance the verbal message. Avoid clutter, distraction, and overload of information on the visual aids.
  - **Delivery**: Use clear and confident verbal and nonverbal communication skills, such as voice, eye contact, gestures, and posture, to engage and persuade the audience. Avoid filler words, jargon, slang, and technical terms that the audience may not understand. Practice and rehearse the presentation before delivering it.
  - **Feedback**: Solicit and respond to feedback from the audience, such as questions, comments, or suggestions, and use it to improve the presentation and the communication skills. Thank the audience for their attention and participation.
