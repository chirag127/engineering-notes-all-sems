### Considerations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 2 covers the various forms of technical communication, such as reports, proposals, manuals, instructions, memos, letters, emails, and presentations.
- The notes should include the following considerations for each form of technical communication:
  - The purpose and audience of the communication
  - The format and structure of the document
  - The style and tone of the language
  - The use of visuals, graphics, and tables
  - The ethical and legal issues involved
- The notes should also provide examples of each form of technical communication, and explain how they meet the criteria of effective technical communication, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, and correctness.
- The notes should highlight the differences and similarities between the forms of technical communication, and how to choose the appropriate form for a given situation or purpose.
- The notes should summarize the main points and key takeaways of the unit, and provide some practice questions or exercises to test the understanding of the concepts.
