### Distinction between General and Technical Communication

General communication and technical communication are two types of communication that differ in their purpose, style, audience, and content. Here are some points to highlight the distinction between them:

- General communication is informal in style and approach. It does not follow a set pattern or format. It is mostly oral and uses everyday language. It is not always for a specific audience or goal. It may contain a general message that is not technical or specialized. For example, a casual conversation with a friend, a news report, or a personal letter are forms of general communication.
- Technical communication is mostly formal in style and approach. It follows a set pattern or format that is appropriate for the context and purpose. It is both oral and written and uses technical terms and graphics. It is for a specific audience and goal. It contains a technical message that is specialized or complex. For example, a user manual, a research paper, or a presentation are forms of technical communication.

The distinction between general and technical communication is important because it helps us to choose the right communication style, method, and tools for different situations and audiences. It also helps us to improve our communication skills and effectiveness.
