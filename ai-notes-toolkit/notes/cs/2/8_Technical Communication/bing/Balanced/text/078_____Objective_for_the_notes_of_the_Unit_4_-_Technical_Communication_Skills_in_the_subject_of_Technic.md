### Objective for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- To understand the concept and importance of technical communication skills in the engineering profession.
- To learn the principles and techniques of effective oral and written communication in technical contexts.
- To practice the skills of preparing, delivering and evaluating technical presentations, reports and proposals.
- To develop the ability to communicate with different audiences, such as peers, supervisors, clients and the general public.
- To enhance the awareness of ethical, social and cultural issues in technical communication.
