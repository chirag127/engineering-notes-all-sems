### Response for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Oral communication is the process of expressing information or ideas by word of mouth.
- Oral communication has several dimensions, such as:
  - Purpose: the goal or objective of the communication, such as to inform, persuade, entertain, or instruct.
  - Audience: the intended recipients of the communication, such as individuals, groups, or organizations.
  - Context: the situation or environment in which the communication takes place, such as formal, informal, academic, or professional.
  - Channel: the medium or mode of delivery of the communication, such as face-to-face, telephone, video conference, or podcast.
  - Content: the information or ideas that are communicated, such as facts, opinions, arguments, or stories.
  - Structure: the organization or arrangement of the content, such as introduction, body, and conclusion.
  - Style: the tone, language, and expression of the communication, such as formal, informal, polite, or humorous.
  - Feedback: the response or reaction of the audience to the communication, such as verbal, nonverbal, or written.
- Voice dynamics are the qualities or characteristics of the speaker's voice, such as:
  - Volume: the loudness or softness of the voice, which can affect the clarity and impact of the communication.
  - Pitch: the highness or lowness of the voice, which can convey the emotion, attitude, or emphasis of the speaker.
  - Rate: the speed or pace of the voice, which can influence the comprehension and interest of the audience.
  - Pause: the silence or break in the voice, which can create emphasis, suspense, or transition in the communication.
  - Articulation: the clarity or accuracy of the pronunciation of the words, which can affect the understanding and impression of the speaker.
  - Modulation: the variation or change in the volume, pitch, rate, and pause of the voice, which can enhance the expression and delivery of the communication.
