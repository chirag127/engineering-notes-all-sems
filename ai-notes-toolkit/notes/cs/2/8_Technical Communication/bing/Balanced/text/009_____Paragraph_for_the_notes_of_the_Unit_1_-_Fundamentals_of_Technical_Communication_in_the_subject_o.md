### Paragraph for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A paragraph is a group of sentences that develops one main idea.
- A paragraph has three parts: a topic sentence, supporting sentences, and a concluding sentence.
- A topic sentence introduces the main idea of the paragraph and usually comes at the beginning.
- Supporting sentences provide details, examples, facts, or evidence that support the topic sentence.
- A concluding sentence summarizes the main idea of the paragraph and often restates the topic sentence in different words.
- A paragraph should be coherent, which means that the sentences are logically connected and flow smoothly.
- A paragraph should also be cohesive, which means that the sentences use transitional words and phrases to show the relationship between ideas.
- A paragraph should be concise, which means that it avoids unnecessary words and repetition.
- A paragraph should be clear, which means that it uses precise and accurate language and avoids ambiguity and confusion.
