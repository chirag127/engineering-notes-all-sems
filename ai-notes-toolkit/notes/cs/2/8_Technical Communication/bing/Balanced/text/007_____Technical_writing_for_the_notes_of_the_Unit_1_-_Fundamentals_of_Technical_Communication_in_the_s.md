### Technical writing for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical writing is the process of communicating technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose, such as instruction, explanation, or persuasion.
- Technical writing differs from other types of writing in terms of its purpose, audience, tone, style, format, and ethics.
- The purpose of technical writing is to inform, instruct, explain, or persuade the audience about a technical topic, such as a product, a process, a system, or a concept.
- The audience of technical writing is usually specialized, such as engineers, technicians, managers, customers, or users, who need to understand, use, or apply the technical information.
- The tone of technical writing is usually objective, clear, concise, accurate, and precise, avoiding personal opinions, emotions, or biases.
- The style of technical writing is usually formal, using standard grammar, punctuation, and spelling, avoiding slang, jargon, or colloquialisms, unless they are appropriate for the audience or the purpose.
- The format of technical writing is usually structured, using headings, subheadings, lists, tables, figures, and other visual aids, to organize and present the technical information in a logical and accessible way.
- The ethics of technical writing is based on honesty, integrity, respect, and responsibility, avoiding plagiarism, falsification, misrepresentation, or omission of relevant information, and acknowledging the sources of information, ideas, or quotations.
