### Features for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- The notes should cover the following topics:
  - Definition and scope of technical communication
  - Purpose and objectives of technical communication
  - Principles and characteristics of effective technical communication
  - Types and modes of technical communication
  - Audience analysis and adaptation
  - Ethics and professionalism in technical communication
- The notes should be concise, clear, accurate, coherent, and complete.
- The notes should use appropriate language, tone, style, and format for the intended audience and purpose.
- The notes should include examples, illustrations, diagrams, tables, graphs, or other visual aids to support the main points and enhance understanding.
- The notes should cite relevant sources and references to acknowledge the original authors and avoid plagiarism.
- The notes should follow a logical structure and organization, such as introduction, body, and conclusion, or problem, solution, and evaluation.
- The notes should use headings, subheadings, bullet points, numbers, or other devices to highlight the key information and facilitate scanning and skimming.
- The notes should be proofread and edited for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and consistency.
