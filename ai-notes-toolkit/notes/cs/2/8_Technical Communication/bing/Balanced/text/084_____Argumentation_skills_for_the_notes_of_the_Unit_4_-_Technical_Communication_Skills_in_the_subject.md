### Argumentation skills for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Argumentation skills are the ability to present and support a claim or a position using logical reasoning and evidence.
- Argumentation skills are important for technical communication because they help to persuade the audience, justify the decisions, and resolve the conflicts or problems in the technical field.
- Argumentation skills involve the following steps:
  - Identify the purpose and the audience of the argument.
  - Formulate a clear and concise thesis statement that expresses the main claim or position.
  - Provide supporting arguments that are relevant, valid, and reliable.
  - Anticipate and address the possible counterarguments or objections from the opposing side.
  - Use appropriate language, tone, and style that suit the context and the audience.
  - Organize the argument in a logical and coherent structure that follows the conventions of the genre and the format.
  - Cite the sources of information and evidence using the appropriate citation style and format.
  - Revise and edit the argument for clarity, accuracy, and effectiveness.
