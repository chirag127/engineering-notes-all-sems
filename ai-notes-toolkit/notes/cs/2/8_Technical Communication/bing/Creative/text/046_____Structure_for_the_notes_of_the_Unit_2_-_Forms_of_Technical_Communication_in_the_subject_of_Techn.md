### Structure for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 2 covers the following topics:
  - Definition and characteristics of technical communication
  - Types and purposes of technical communication
  - Principles and strategies of technical communication
  - Common forms of technical communication
- The notes should be organized as follows:
  - Introduction: Provide an overview of the unit and its objectives, and explain the importance and scope of technical communication in various fields and contexts.
  - Definition and characteristics of technical communication: Define technical communication and identify its main features, such as accuracy, clarity, conciseness, coherence, completeness, correctness, and appropriateness. Provide examples of technical communication from different domains and genres.
  - Types and purposes of technical communication: Classify technical communication into two broad categories: informative and persuasive. Explain the goals and functions of each type, and give examples of common documents and media that belong to each category. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of different modes and channels of technical communication, such as oral, written, visual, and digital.
  - Principles and strategies of technical communication: Outline the basic principles of effective technical communication, such as audience analysis, purpose identification, document design, and ethical considerations. Describe the steps and strategies involved in planning, drafting, revising, and editing technical documents and presentations. Provide tips and techniques for improving the quality and impact of technical communication.
  - Common forms of technical communication: Introduce some of the most widely used forms of technical communication, such as reports, proposals, manuals, instructions, memos, letters, emails, resumes, and presentations. Explain the purpose, structure, format, style, and tone of each form, and provide guidelines and examples for creating and delivering them. Compare and contrast the similarities and differences among various forms of technical communication.
  - Conclusion: Summarize the main points and learning outcomes of the unit, and provide some suggestions for further reading and practice.
