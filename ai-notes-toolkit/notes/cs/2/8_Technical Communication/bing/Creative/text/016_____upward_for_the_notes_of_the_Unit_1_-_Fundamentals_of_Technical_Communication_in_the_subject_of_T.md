### Upward Communication

Upward communication is the process by which lower-level company employees can directly communicate with upper management to provide feedback, complaints or suggestions regarding the day-to-day operations of the company. Upward communication is increasing in popularity among organizations to encourage a participative work culture.

Some of the main points to note about upward communication are:

- It is participative because it encourages lower-level employees to voice their opinions. In contrast, downward communication is directive in nature as it allows upper management to instruct employees on particular company matters.
- It is beneficial for both the employees and the management as it can improve employee morale, motivation, productivity, and loyalty . It can also help the management to identify and solve problems, make better decisions, and foster trust and respect among the employees .
- It can be challenging to implement effectively as it may face barriers such as fear of criticism, lack of feedback, information overload, distortion of messages, and organizational culture . To overcome these barriers, the management should create a supportive and open environment, use multiple channels of communication, provide timely and constructive feedback, and reward and recognize the employees who communicate upward .
- It can take various forms such as suggestion boxes, surveys, meetings, reports, performance appraisals, and informal conversations  . The choice of the form depends on the purpose, audience, and context of the communication.
