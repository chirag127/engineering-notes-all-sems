### Expert Technical Lecture for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the method of producing and sharing ideas and information in organizations.
- Technical communication can have different purposes, such as defining, instructing, informing, or persuading.
- Technical communication can deal with technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations .
- Technical communication can also use technology to create or distribute the communication, such as web pages, help files, or social media sites .
- Technical communication can provide instructions about how to do something, regardless of how technical the task is or even if technology is used to create or distribute that communication .
- Technical communication can take different forms, such as business letters, memos, reports, notices, proposals, websites, blogs, etc .
- Technical communication can target different audiences, such as internal or external, expert or novice, individual or group, etc .
- Technical communication requires certain skills, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, organization, design, ethics, etc  .
- Technical communication follows certain standards, such as style guides, formats, templates, conventions, etc  .
