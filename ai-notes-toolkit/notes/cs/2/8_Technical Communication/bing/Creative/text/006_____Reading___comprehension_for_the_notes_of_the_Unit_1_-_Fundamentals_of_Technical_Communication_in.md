### Reading & Comprehension

- Reading is the process of decoding written symbols and constructing meaning from them.
- Comprehension is the ability to understand and interpret what is read.
- Reading and comprehension are essential skills for technical communication, as they enable you to access, analyze, and evaluate information from various sources and formats.
- To improve your reading and comprehension skills, you should:
  - Identify your purpose and goal for reading. This will help you select the appropriate text, strategy, and level of attention.
  - Preview the text before reading. This will help you activate your prior knowledge, predict the content and structure, and formulate questions.
  - Use active reading strategies. These include skimming, scanning, summarizing, paraphrasing, annotating, questioning, and evaluating. They will help you focus on the main ideas, details, and arguments of the text, and relate them to your own knowledge and experience.
  - Monitor your comprehension and adjust your reading accordingly. This will help you identify and overcome any difficulties or gaps in your understanding, and check your progress and achievement of your reading goal.
  - Review and reflect on what you have read. This will help you consolidate your learning, synthesize the information, and apply it to your own context and purpose.
