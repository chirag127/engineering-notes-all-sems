### Stimulus for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- A stimulus is an idea or a concept that is activated by the sender in the communication process .
- A stimulus can be based on personal experiences, observations, opinions, feelings, or facts.
- A stimulus can be expressed in various forms, such as words, gestures, sounds, images, or symbols.
- A stimulus can be influenced by the sender's background, culture, mood, motivation, and purpose.
- A stimulus can be attention-getting in a productive or distracting way, depending on the context and the receiver's perception.
- A stimulus can initiate, maintain, or end a conversation, depending on the feedback and the response from the receiver.
- A stimulus can be enhanced by using appropriate oral communication skills, such as clarity, fluency, tone, pitch, volume, and pronunciation.
- A stimulus can be modified or adapted according to the situation, the audience, the channel, and the feedback.
- A stimulus can be evaluated by the sender and the receiver based on the effectiveness, appropriateness, and ethicality of the communication.
