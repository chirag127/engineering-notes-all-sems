### Features for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- The notes should cover the following topics:
  - Definition and scope of technical communication
  - Purpose and characteristics of technical communication
  - Process and principles of technical communication
  - Types and modes of technical communication
  - Ethics and professionalism in technical communication
- The notes should be concise, clear, accurate, and coherent
- The notes should use appropriate language, tone, and style for the intended audience and context
- The notes should follow a logical structure and organization, with headings, subheadings, lists, tables, diagrams, etc. as needed
- The notes should cite relevant sources and provide references for further reading
- The notes should include examples, illustrations, and exercises to enhance understanding and application of the concepts
- The notes should be formatted and presented in a consistent and attractive manner, using fonts, colors, margins, spacing, etc. as per the guidelines
- The notes should be proofread and edited for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and clarity
- The notes should be updated and revised as per the feedback and suggestions from the instructor and peers
