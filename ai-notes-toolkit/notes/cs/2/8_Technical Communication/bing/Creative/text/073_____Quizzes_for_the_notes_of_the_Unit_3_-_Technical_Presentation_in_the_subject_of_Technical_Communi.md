### Quizzes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. What are the three main components of a technical presentation?
    - a) Introduction, body, and conclusion
    - b) Problem, solution, and recommendation
    - c) Audience, purpose, and context
    - d) Content, delivery, and visuals
    - Answer: d) Content, delivery, and visuals
2. What are some of the characteristics of effective content in a technical presentation?
    - a) Clear, concise, and accurate
    - b) Relevant, organized, and coherent
    - c) Logical, persuasive, and ethical
    - d) All of the above
    - Answer: d) All of the above
3. What are some of the elements of effective delivery in a technical presentation?
    - a) Voice, posture, and eye contact
    - b) Gestures, facial expressions, and movement
    - c) Pace, volume, and tone
    - d) All of the above
    - Answer: d) All of the above
4. What are some of the principles of effective visuals in a technical presentation?
    - a) Simplicity, consistency, and contrast
    - b) Alignment, proximity, and balance
    - c) Clarity, relevance, and accuracy
    - d) All of the above
    - Answer: d) All of the above
5. What are some of the types of visuals that can be used in a technical presentation?
    - a) Tables, charts, and graphs
    - b) Diagrams, maps, and photographs
    - c) Videos, animations, and simulations
    - d) All of the above
    - Answer: d) All of the above
