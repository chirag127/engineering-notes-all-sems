### Synopsis writing for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A synopsis is a brief summary of the main points and ideas of a longer text, such as a book, an article, or a report.
- A synopsis can help the reader to quickly grasp the main purpose, scope, and content of the text, without having to read it in full.
- A synopsis can also help the writer to organize and structure their text, by highlighting the key points and arguments, and identifying the gaps or weaknesses in the text.
- A synopsis should be concise, clear, and accurate, and should avoid unnecessary details, opinions, or interpretations.
- A synopsis should follow the same order and structure as the original text, and should use the same terminology and style as the text.
- A synopsis should include the following elements:
  - The title and author of the text
  - The main topic or theme of the text
  - The main objective or aim of the text
  - The main thesis or argument of the text
  - The main supporting points or evidence for the thesis or argument
  - The main conclusion or implication of the text
- A synopsis should be written in the present tense, and should use the third person point of view, unless the original text uses a different tense or point of view.
- A synopsis should use direct quotations sparingly, and should cite the source of the quotation using the appropriate citation style.
- A synopsis should be proofread and edited for clarity, coherence, and correctness, and should be checked for plagiarism.
