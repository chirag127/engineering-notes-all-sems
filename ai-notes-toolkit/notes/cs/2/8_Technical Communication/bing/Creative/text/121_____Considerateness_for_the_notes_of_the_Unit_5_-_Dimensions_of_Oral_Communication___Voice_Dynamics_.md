### Considerateness for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Considerateness is the quality of being thoughtful and respectful of others in oral communication.
- Considerateness involves being aware of the needs, feelings, preferences, and expectations of the audience, and adjusting one's speech accordingly.
- Considerateness can enhance the effectiveness and credibility of oral communication, as well as the rapport and trust between the speaker and the listener.
- Some aspects of considerateness in oral communication are:

  - Using appropriate language and tone that suit the context, purpose, and audience of the communication. Avoiding jargon, slang, offensive, or biased words that may confuse or alienate the listener.
  - Adapting the content and structure of the message to the level of knowledge, interest, and attention span of the listener. Providing relevant, clear, and concise information that meets the listener's needs and expectations.
  - Showing respect and empathy for the listener's perspective, opinions, and feelings. Acknowledging and addressing any questions, concerns, or objections that the listener may have. Avoiding interrupting, dominating, or criticizing the listener.
  - Using nonverbal cues and feedback that complement and reinforce the verbal message. Maintaining eye contact, facial expressions, gestures, and posture that convey confidence, sincerity, and enthusiasm. Listening actively and attentively to the listener's response and feedback.
  - Being polite and courteous in oral communication. Using words and phrases that express gratitude, appreciation, apology, or request. Following the norms and etiquette of the communication situation and culture.
