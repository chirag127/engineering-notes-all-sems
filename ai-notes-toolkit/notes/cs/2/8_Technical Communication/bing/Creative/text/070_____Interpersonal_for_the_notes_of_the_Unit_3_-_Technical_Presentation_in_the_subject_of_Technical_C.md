### Interpersonal Skills

Interpersonal skills are the skills required to effectively communicate, interact, and work with individuals and groups. They are essential for technical professionals who need to present their ideas, collaborate with others, and persuade their audience. Some of the key interpersonal skills for technical communication are:

- **Communication skills**: The ability to convey information clearly, concisely, and accurately, using appropriate verbal and non-verbal cues. Communication skills include listening, speaking, writing, and reading. Technical professionals need to communicate with different audiences, such as peers, managers, clients, and the public, using different modes, such as reports, presentations, emails, and meetings. Communication skills also involve adapting to the context, purpose, and expectations of the communication situation.    

- **Empathy skills**: The ability to understand and share the feelings, thoughts, and perspectives of others. Empathy skills include showing respect, compassion, and sensitivity to the needs and emotions of others. Technical professionals need to empathize with their audience, such as their problems, goals, and preferences, and tailor their communication accordingly. Empathy skills also involve giving and receiving constructive feedback, and resolving conflicts and misunderstandings.   

- **Active listening skills**: The ability to pay attention, comprehend, and respond to what others are saying. Active listening skills include asking questions, paraphrasing, summarizing, and reflecting on what is being said. Technical professionals need to listen actively to their audience, such as their feedback, questions, and concerns, and address them effectively. Active listening skills also involve showing interest, engagement, and appreciation for what others are saying.    

- **Teamwork skills**: The ability to work cooperatively and productively with others towards a common goal. Teamwork skills include sharing information, ideas, and resources, coordinating tasks and responsibilities, and supporting and motivating others. Technical professionals need to work in teams, such as with other technical experts, project managers, and stakeholders, and contribute to the team's success. Teamwork skills also involve respecting diversity, managing conflicts, and building trust and rapport.    

- **Presentation skills**: The ability to deliver information effectively and persuasively to an audience. Presentation skills include planning, preparing, and practicing the content, structure, and delivery of the presentation. Technical professionals need to present their work, such as their findings, solutions, and recommendations, to various audiences, such as technical, non-technical, or mixed, and use appropriate visual aids, such as graphs, charts, and diagrams. Presentation skills also involve engaging the audience, handling questions, and overcoming nervousness.    

Interpersonal skills can be improved through honest feedback, workshops, and by asking more questions.  They are also influenced by factors such as personality, culture, and communication style.  Interpersonal skills are crucial for technical professionals who want to communicate their expertise, build relationships, and achieve their goals.
