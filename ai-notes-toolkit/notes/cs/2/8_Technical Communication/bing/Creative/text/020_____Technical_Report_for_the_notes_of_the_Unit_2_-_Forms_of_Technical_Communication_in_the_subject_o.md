### Technical Report for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical report is a document that conveys the results of a scientific or technical research or project to a specific audience.
- A technical report has the following characteristics:
  - It is written for a specific purpose and audience, such as informing, persuading, instructing, or recommending.
  - It is organized logically and clearly, using headings, subheadings, tables, figures, and appendices to present the information.
  - It follows a standard format and style, such as IEEE, APA, or MLA, depending on the discipline and the requirements of the publisher or client.
  - It uses precise, concise, and objective language, avoiding jargon, slang, and personal opinions.
  - It cites and references the sources of information, data, and ideas, using the appropriate citation style and avoiding plagiarism.
- A technical report can have different types and formats, depending on the purpose, audience, and context. Some common types of technical reports are:
  - Feasibility report: evaluates the feasibility of a proposed solution, project, or course of action, and compares it with alternative options.
  - Progress report: updates the status, achievements, problems, and plans of an ongoing project or activity.
  - Laboratory report: describes the methods, results, and conclusions of an experiment or test conducted in a laboratory.
  - Research report: presents the findings, analysis, and implications of a research study or project.
  - Proposal: proposes a solution, project, or course of action to address a problem or need, and provides the rationale, benefits, and costs of the proposal.
  - Instruction manual: provides step-by-step instructions on how to use, operate, or maintain a product, system, or service.
  - User guide: provides information on how to use, operate, or maintain a product, system, or service, and explains the features, functions, and benefits of the product, system, or service.
  - Technical specification: defines the requirements, standards, and characteristics of a product, system, or service, and describes how it will be designed, developed, tested, and delivered.
  - Technical memorandum: communicates technical information, data, or ideas within an organization or to a specific audience, such as a client, colleague, or supervisor.
  - White paper: provides an authoritative and persuasive overview of a topic, issue, or problem, and proposes a solution, project, or course of action.
