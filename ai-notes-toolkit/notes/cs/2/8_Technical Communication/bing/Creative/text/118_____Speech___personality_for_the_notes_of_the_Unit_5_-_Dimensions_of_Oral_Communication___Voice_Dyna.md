### Speech & Personality

- Speech is the ability of a person to effectively communicate verbally.
- Speech is important for personality development because it influences how others perceive and respond to us.
- Speech also helps us express our thoughts, feelings, opinions, and emotions in a clear and appropriate way.
- Speech and personality are interrelated, as one can affect the other. For example, an introvert may not be a confident speaker, while a confident speaker may be perceived as a successful professional.
- Speech and personality are influenced by various factors, such as genetics, environment, education, culture, and brain anatomy .
- Speech and personality can be improved by practicing, learning, and seeking feedback from others.
