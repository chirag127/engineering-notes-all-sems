### Technical style for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical style is the way of writing or speaking that is appropriate for a specific technical context, such as a report, a manual, a presentation, or a website.
- Technical style is influenced by the purpose, audience, and medium of the communication, as well as by the conventions and standards of the field or discipline.
- Technical style aims to achieve clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, and correctness in the communication, while avoiding ambiguity, vagueness, redundancy, jargon, and errors.
- Some general principles of technical style are:

  - Use precise and specific language that conveys the intended meaning and avoids confusion or misinterpretation.
  - Use consistent terminology and notation throughout the communication, and define any unfamiliar terms or symbols.
  - Use active voice and strong verbs to emphasize the agent and the action, and avoid passive voice and weak verbs that obscure the agent and the action.
  - Use parallel structure and transitional words or phrases to show the logical relationship and flow of ideas.
  - Use headings, lists, tables, figures, and other visual aids to organize and highlight the main points and support the text.
  - Use appropriate tone and level of formality that suits the purpose and audience of the communication, and avoid slang, colloquialisms, humor, or personal opinions that may be inappropriate or offensive.
  - Use correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and capitalization, and follow the rules and guidelines of the citation style and format required for the communication.
