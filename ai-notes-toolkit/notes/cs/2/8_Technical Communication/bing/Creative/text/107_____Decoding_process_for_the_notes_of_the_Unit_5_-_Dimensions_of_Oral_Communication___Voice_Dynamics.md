### Decoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Decoding is the process of translating the message from its sign format into meaning.
- Decoding is the reverse of encoding, where the sender uses symbols to translate ideas or concepts into a coded message ready for communication.
- Decoding is performed by the person receiving a message, who converts the message into thoughts by interpreting and understanding the encoded message .
- Decoding is often difficult to achieve because factors such as education, opinions, and emotional states shape how a receiver interprets a message.
- Factors that influence the decoding process include communication channel proficiency, shared mental models, communication noise, and discrepancies in topic knowledge or experience.
- In oral communication, decoding includes listening and understanding the spoken words, tone, gestures, and body language of the speaker.
- Decoding is essential for effective oral communication, as it allows the receiver to comprehend the message, provide feedback, and respond appropriately.
- Decoding can be improved by developing active listening skills, asking clarifying questions, avoiding distractions, and being open-minded and respectful.
