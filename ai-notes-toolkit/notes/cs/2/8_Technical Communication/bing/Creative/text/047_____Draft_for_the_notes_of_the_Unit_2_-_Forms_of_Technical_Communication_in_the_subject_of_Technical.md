### Draft for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information in a clear, concise, and accurate manner to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as reports, proposals, manuals, instructions, presentations, memos, emails, etc.
- The forms of technical communication can be classified into two broad categories: internal and external.
- Internal technical communication is the communication that takes place within an organization, such as between employees, managers, departments, etc. The purpose of internal technical communication is to facilitate the work processes, coordinate the tasks, share the information, and solve the problems.
- External technical communication is the communication that takes place between an organization and its external stakeholders, such as customers, clients, suppliers, regulators, etc. The purpose of external technical communication is to promote the products, services, or image of the organization, persuade the stakeholders, and meet the legal or ethical obligations.
- Some examples of internal technical communication are:
  - Progress reports: These are documents that provide an update on the status, achievements, and challenges of a project or a task.
  - Feasibility studies: These are documents that analyze the viability, benefits, and risks of a proposed solution or a course of action.
  - Technical manuals: These are documents that provide instructions on how to use, operate, maintain, or repair a product or a system.
  - Meeting minutes: These are documents that record the agenda, participants, discussions, decisions, and action items of a meeting.
- Some examples of external technical communication are:
  - Proposals: These are documents that offer a solution, a service, or a product to a potential client or customer, and persuade them to accept it.
  - User guides: These are documents that provide guidance on how to use, operate, or troubleshoot a product or a system for the end-users.
  - Brochures: These are documents that provide an overview of the features, benefits, and specifications of a product or a service for the potential customers or clients.
  - Press releases: These are documents that announce a newsworthy event, achievement, or development of an organization to the media and the public.
