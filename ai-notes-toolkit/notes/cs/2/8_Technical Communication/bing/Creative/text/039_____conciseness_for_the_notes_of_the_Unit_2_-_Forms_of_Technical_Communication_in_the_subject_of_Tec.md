### Conciseness

- Conciseness is the quality of using only the words that are necessary to convey a clear and accurate message.
- Conciseness is important for effective technical communication because it helps the reader to understand the information quickly and easily, and avoids wasting their time and attention.
- Conciseness can be achieved by following some general principles, such as:

  - Eliminating unnecessary words, such as redundancies, fillers, modifiers, and qualifiers.
  - Using precise and specific words, such as concrete nouns, active verbs, and descriptive adjectives and adverbs.
  - Using short and simple sentences, and avoiding complex and compound sentences.
  - Using parallel structure, lists, and headings to organize the information and highlight the main points.
  - Using tables, graphs, charts, and diagrams to present numerical or visual data in a concise and clear way.
  - Revising and editing the document to remove any errors, inconsistencies, or ambiguities.
