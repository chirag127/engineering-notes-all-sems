### Exposition narration for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Exposition narration is a type of narration that explains, informs, or describes something to the audience.
- Exposition narration is often used in technical communication to provide background information, definitions, instructions, or processes that are relevant to the topic or purpose of the document.
- Exposition narration can be classified into four types: definition, classification, comparison and contrast, and cause and effect.
- Definition exposition narration provides the meaning or essence of a term, concept, or phenomenon. It can use examples, synonyms, antonyms, or etymology to clarify the definition.
- Classification exposition narration groups or categorizes things or ideas based on common characteristics or criteria. It can use diagrams, tables, or charts to illustrate the classification scheme.
- Comparison and contrast exposition narration shows the similarities and differences between two or more things or ideas. It can use analogies, metaphors, or similes to highlight the comparison or contrast.
- Cause and effect exposition narration explains the reasons or consequences of an event, action, or situation. It can use logical connectors, such as because, therefore, or as a result, to show the causal relationship.
