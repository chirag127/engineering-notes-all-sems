### Objective for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- To understand the concept and importance of technical communication skills in the engineering profession.
- To learn the principles and techniques of effective technical writing and oral presentation.
- To practice writing various types of technical documents, such as memos, letters, reports, proposals, and manuals.
- To practice designing and delivering various types of technical presentations, such as informative, persuasive, and instructional.
- To develop the skills of using appropriate language, style, tone, and format for different audiences and purposes.
- To develop the skills of using visual aids, graphics, and multimedia to enhance the clarity and impact of technical communication.
- To develop the skills of editing, revising, and proofreading technical documents and presentations.
- To develop the skills of working in teams and collaborating with peers and experts on technical communication projects.
- To develop the skills of using ethical principles and professional standards in technical communication.
