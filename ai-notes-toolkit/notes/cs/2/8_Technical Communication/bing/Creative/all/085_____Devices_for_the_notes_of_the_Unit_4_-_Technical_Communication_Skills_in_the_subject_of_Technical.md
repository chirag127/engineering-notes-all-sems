# Devices for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical communication is the process of making and sharing ideas and information in the workplace as well as the set of genres such as letters, emails, instructions, reports, proposals, websites, and blogs, which comprise the documents you write.
- Technical communication requires the use of various devices to transmit, receive, store, process, and display information.
- Some of the devices for technical communication are:

  - **Telecommunications equipment**: These are devices that enable the exchange of information over long distances using electrical or optical signals. Examples are telephones, radios, televisions, satellites, and fiber-optic cables .
  - **Communication devices**: These are devices that enable the exchange of information over short distances using wireless or wired connections. Examples are smartphones, tablets, laptops, desktops, modems, routers, and printers .
  - **Storage devices**: These are devices that enable the preservation of information for later use. Examples are hard disks, flash drives, CDs, DVDs, and cloud storage.
  - **Processing devices**: These are devices that enable the manipulation and transformation of information using algorithms and software. Examples are microprocessors, memory chips, graphics cards, and scanners .
  - **Display devices**: These are devices that enable the presentation and visualization of information using text, images, sound, or video. Examples are monitors, projectors, speakers, and headphones.

- These devices can be used individually or in combination to create effective technical communication products and systems.
