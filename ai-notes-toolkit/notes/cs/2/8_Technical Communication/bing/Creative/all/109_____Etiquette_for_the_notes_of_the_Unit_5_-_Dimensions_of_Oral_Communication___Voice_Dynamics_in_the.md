# Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Etiquette is the set of rules and norms that govern the appropriate and polite behavior in a given social or professional context.
- Oral communication is the process of expressing ideas, opinions, feelings, and information through speech.
- Voice dynamics are the variations in pitch, volume, rate, and tone of voice that affect the meaning and impact of oral communication.
- The notes of the Unit 5 should cover the following topics:

  - The importance and benefits of oral communication in technical fields.
  - The factors that influence the effectiveness of oral communication, such as clarity, coherence, conciseness, accuracy, relevance, and feedback.
  - The types and purposes of oral communication, such as informative, persuasive, instructional, and interpersonal.
  - The principles and techniques of oral presentation, such as planning, organizing, delivering, and evaluating.
  - The characteristics and functions of voice dynamics, such as pitch, volume, rate, and tone.
  - The strategies and skills to improve voice dynamics, such as breathing, articulation, pronunciation, intonation, and modulation.

- The notes of the Unit 5 should follow these guidelines:

  - Use clear and simple language that is appropriate for the audience and the topic.
  - Use headings, subheadings, bullet points, and numbering to structure the notes and highlight the main points.
  - Use examples, illustrations, diagrams, charts, and tables to support and clarify the information.
  - Use citations and references to acknowledge the sources of information and avoid plagiarism.
  - Use a consistent and professional format and style for the notes, such as font, size, color, alignment, and spacing.
  - Proofread and edit the notes for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and accuracy.
