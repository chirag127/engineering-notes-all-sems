# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

## Introduction

Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose. Technical communication can take various forms, such as manuals, reports, proposals, memos, emails, presentations, websites, blogs, podcasts, etc. Each form of technical communication has its own characteristics, advantages, and limitations.

## Objectives

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Identify the different forms of technical communication and their purposes
- Compare and contrast the features and formats of various forms of technical communication
- Apply the principles of effective technical communication to different forms of technical communication
- Select the appropriate form of technical communication for a given situation and audience

## Forms of Technical Communication

### Manuals

- Manuals are documents that provide instructions or guidance on how to use a product, service, system, or process.
- Manuals can be divided into different types, such as user manuals, installation manuals, maintenance manuals, reference manuals, etc.
- Manuals usually have a clear structure, such as a table of contents, an introduction, a body, and an appendix.
- Manuals should be written in a clear, concise, and consistent style, using simple and direct language, active voice, and imperative mood.
- Manuals should also use visual aids, such as diagrams, illustrations, tables, charts, etc., to enhance the comprehension and retention of the information.

### Reports

- Reports are documents that present the results of an investigation, analysis, or evaluation of a problem, situation, or phenomenon.
- Reports can be classified into different categories, such as informational reports, analytical reports, feasibility reports, progress reports, etc.
- Reports usually have a standard structure, such as a title page, an abstract, an introduction, a body, a conclusion, and a list of references.
- Reports should be written in a formal, objective, and accurate style, using precise and technical language, passive voice, and indicative mood.
- Reports should also use visual aids, such as graphs, figures, tables, etc., to support the claims and arguments made in the text.

### Proposals

- Proposals are documents that propose a solution, a plan, or a course of action to address a problem, a need, or an opportunity.
- Proposals can be divided into two types, such as solicited proposals and unsolicited proposals, depending on whether they are requested by a potential client or not.
- Proposals usually have a similar structure to reports, but they also include a section on the benefits, costs, and timeline of the proposed solution, plan, or action.
- Proposals should be written in a persuasive, confident, and courteous style, using positive and polite language, active voice, and subjunctive mood.
- Proposals should also use visual aids, such as charts, diagrams, tables, etc., to highlight the advantages and feasibility of the proposal.

### Memos

- Memos are short and informal documents that communicate information, instructions, requests, or feedback within an organization or a group.
- Memos can have different purposes, such as informing, instructing, requesting, or responding.
- Memos usually have a simple structure, such as a heading, an opening, a body, and a closing.
- Memos should be written in a clear, concise, and courteous style, using plain and direct language, active voice, and imperative or indicative mood.
- Memos should also use visual aids, such as bullet points, lists, tables, etc., to organize and emphasize the main points.

### Emails

- Emails are electronic messages that communicate information, instructions, requests, or feedback between individuals or groups.
- Emails can have different purposes, such as informing, instructing, requesting, or responding.
- Emails usually have a similar structure to memos, but they also include a subject line, a salutation, and a signature.
- Emails should be written in a clear, concise, and courteous style, using plain and direct language, active voice, and imperative or indicative mood.
- Emails should also use visual aids, such as bullet points, lists, tables, etc., to organize and emphasize the main points.

### Presentations

- Presentations are oral or visual forms of communication that deliver information, instructions, or arguments to an audience.
- Presentations can have different purposes, such as informing, instructing, persuading, or entertaining.
- Presentations usually have a similar structure to reports, but they also include an introduction, a conclusion, and a question and answer session.
- Presentations should be delivered in a clear, confident, and engaging style, using simple and direct language, active voice, and imperative or indicative mood.
- Presentations should also use visual aids, such as slides, images, videos, etc
