# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

## Definition

- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as written, oral, visual, or digital.
- Technical communication can be classified into two main categories: academic and professional.

### Academic Technical Communication

- Academic technical communication is the type of communication that occurs in educational settings, such as schools, colleges, or universities.
- Academic technical communication aims to inform, persuade, or instruct students, teachers, researchers, or peers about a certain topic or field of study.
- Academic technical communication can include:

  - Research papers
  - Essays
  - Reports
  - Theses
  - Dissertations
  - Presentations
  - Posters
  - Journals
  - Books
  - Articles
  - Reviews

### Professional Technical Communication

- Professional technical communication is the type of communication that occurs in workplace settings, such as industries, businesses, or organizations.
- Professional technical communication aims to inform, persuade, or instruct customers, clients, colleagues, managers, or stakeholders about a certain product, service, process, or policy.
- Professional technical communication can include:

  - Manuals
  - Proposals
  - Memos
  - Letters
  - Emails
  - Brochures
  - Flyers
  - Websites
  - Blogs
  - Podcasts
  - Videos
  - Infographics
  - Charts
  - Graphs
  - Tables
