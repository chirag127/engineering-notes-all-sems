- Technical communication skills are the abilities to communicate effectively and efficiently in a technical or professional context, such as engineering, science, or technology.

- Technical communication skills are important for various purposes, such as informing, instructing, persuading, collaborating, and problem-solving.
- Technical communication skills can be improved by following some principles and guidelines, such as:
  - Know your audience and purpose: analyze the needs, expectations, and preferences of your readers or listeners, and tailor your message accordingly.
  - Use clear and concise language: avoid jargon, ambiguity, and unnecessary words, and use simple and direct sentences.
  - Organize your information logically: use headings, subheadings, lists, tables, and other devices to structure your document or presentation, and provide an introduction, a body, and a conclusion.
  - Use appropriate format and design: choose a suitable layout, font, color, and graphics for your document or presentation, and follow the conventions of your genre and discipline.
  - Use reliable and relevant sources: cite and reference the sources of your information, and evaluate their credibility, accuracy, and timeliness.
