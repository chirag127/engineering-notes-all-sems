# 7 Cs of Effective Business Writing

Effective business writing is a skill that can help you communicate your ideas clearly and persuasively. In this note, we will cover the 7 Cs of effective business writing, which are:

- **Clear**: Your writing should have a clear purpose and message. You should avoid ambiguity, jargon, and unnecessary words. You should use simple and direct language that your audience can understand easily.
- **Coherent**: Your writing should have a logical structure and flow. You should use transitions, headings, and paragraphs to organize your ideas and guide your readers. You should avoid gaps, contradictions, and inconsistencies in your writing.
- **Complete**: Your writing should have all the information and details that your readers need to know. You should answer the questions of who, what, when, where, why, and how. You should also provide any supporting evidence, examples, or references that back up your claims.
- **Concise**: Your writing should be brief and to the point. You should avoid repetition, redundancy, and filler words. You should use short sentences and paragraphs, and eliminate any irrelevant or unnecessary information.
- **Consistent**: Your writing should follow the same style, tone, and format throughout. You should use the same terminology, spelling, grammar, and punctuation rules. You should also follow the guidelines and expectations of your genre, purpose, and audience.
- **Correct**: Your writing should be accurate and error-free. You should check your facts, figures, and sources for reliability and validity. You should also proofread and edit your writing for spelling, grammar, and punctuation mistakes.
- **Credible**: Your writing should be trustworthy and convincing. You should use credible and reputable sources, and cite them properly. You should also use a respectful and professional tone, and avoid any bias, exaggeration, or manipulation.
