# Quizzes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. What are the three main types of technical presentations according to the notes?
    - A) Informative, persuasive, and instructional
    - B) Informal, formal, and interactive
    - C) Oral, written, and visual
    - D) Technical, non-technical, and mixed
    - Answer: A

2. What are the four steps of the presentation process according to the notes?
    - A) Planning, preparing, practicing, and presenting
    - B) Analyzing, designing, developing, and delivering
    - C) Researching, organizing, rehearsing, and performing
    - D) All of the above
    - Answer: D

3. What are some of the factors to consider when analyzing the audience for a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) Their background, expectations, and needs
    - B) Their size, location, and diversity
    - C) Their interest, attitude, and knowledge
    - D) All of the above
    - Answer: D

4. What are some of the elements to include in the introduction of a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) A hook, a thesis statement, and a preview of the main points
    - B) A greeting, a self-introduction, and a statement of purpose
    - C) A summary, a question, and a transition
    - D) A and B
    - Answer: D

5. What are some of the strategies to use in the body of a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) Use clear and concise language, avoid jargon and technical terms, and use examples and analogies
    - B) Use signposts, transitions, and repetition to guide the audience and emphasize the key points
    - C) Use visual aids, such as graphs, charts, diagrams, and images, to support and illustrate the main points
    - D) All of the above
    - Answer: D

6. What are some of the elements to include in the conclusion of a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) A review of the main points, a restatement of the thesis statement, and a call to action
    - B) A thank you, a question and answer session, and a closing remark
    - C) A feedback form, a reference list, and a contact information
    - D) A and B
    - Answer: D

7. What are some of the benefits of practicing a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) It helps to improve the delivery, timing, and confidence of the presenter
    - B) It helps to identify and correct any errors, gaps, or weaknesses in the content and structure of the presentation
    - C) It helps to familiarize the presenter with the equipment, environment, and audience of the presentation
    - D) All of the above
    - Answer: D

8. What are some of the skills to demonstrate in the delivery of a technical presentation according to the notes?
    - A) Verbal skills, such as speaking clearly, loudly, and fluently, using appropriate tone, pitch, and pace, and avoiding fillers and slang
    - B) Non-verbal skills, such as making eye contact, using gestures and facial expressions, and maintaining a good posture and appearance
    - C) Visual skills, such as using effective and relevant visual aids, managing the screen and the pointer, and avoiding distractions and clutter
    - D) All of the above
    - Answer: D
