# Notes on Tone for Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics

- Tone is the expression of the speaker's attitude or emotion towards the topic, audience, or situation.
- Tone can be conveyed through various aspects of oral communication, such as word choice, pitch, volume, pace, pauses, gestures, and facial expressions.
- Tone can have a significant impact on the effectiveness and appropriateness of oral communication, as it can influence the listener's perception, understanding, and response to the message.
- Tone can be classified into different types, such as formal, informal, friendly, hostile, polite, rude, confident, hesitant, enthusiastic, bored, etc.
- Tone can also vary depending on the context, purpose, and audience of oral communication, such as academic, professional, personal, social, etc.
- Tone can be adjusted or modified according to the speaker's intention, goal, or feedback from the listener, such as to clarify, emphasize, persuade, inform, entertain, etc.
- Tone can be analyzed and evaluated by considering the following criteria:
  - Appropriateness: Does the tone suit the topic, audience, and situation?
  - Consistency: Does the tone match the speaker's message and nonverbal cues?
  - Clarity: Does the tone convey the speaker's meaning and intention clearly and accurately?
  - Effectiveness: Does the tone achieve the speaker's purpose and elicit the desired response from the listener?
- Tone can be improved or developed by practicing the following skills:
  - Awareness: Be aware of your own tone and how it affects your oral communication.
  - Observation: Observe and learn from the tone of other speakers, especially those who are proficient or experienced in oral communication.
  - Feedback: Seek and receive feedback from others on your tone and how to improve it.
  - Adaptation: Adapt your tone to different contexts, purposes, and audiences, depending on your oral communication goals.
  - Experimentation: Experiment with different tones and see how they affect your oral communication outcomes.
