### Technical style for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Technical writing is a form of writing on a specific topic that requires guidance, instruction, or explanation.
- Technical writing serves a unique purpose and therefore has very different qualities than other types of writing, such as creative writing, academic writing, or business writing.
- Technical writing is a form of communication that professionals use to convey information about specialized topics, such as environmental regulations, computer applications or medical procedures.
- Technical writing is marked by or characteristic of specialization and technical language.
- A technical communications writing style prioritizes the efficient transfer of information and is (almost always) concise, precise, direct, and well organized.
- A technical communications writing style also uses meaningful, precise language and avoids ambiguity, jargon, and unnecessary words.
- A technical communications writing style adapts to the needs and expectations of the audience, the purpose and context of the communication, and the conventions and standards of the field or discipline.
