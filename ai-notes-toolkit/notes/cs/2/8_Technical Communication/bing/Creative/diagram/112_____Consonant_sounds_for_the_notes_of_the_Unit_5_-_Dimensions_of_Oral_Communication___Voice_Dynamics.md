### Consonant sounds for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Consonant sounds are speech sounds that are produced by partially or completely blocking the airflow through the vocal tract  .
- Consonant sounds are represented by letters of the alphabet that are not vowels: A, E, I, O, U and sometimes Y are not consonants.
- Consonant sounds can be classified according to three main features: the place of articulation, the manner of articulation, and the voicing.
- The place of articulation refers to the location of the obstruction in the vocal tract, such as the lips, the teeth, the tongue, the palate, or the throat.
- The manner of articulation refers to the type of obstruction, such as a complete closure, a partial closure, a friction, or a resonance.
- The voicing refers to the presence or absence of vocal cord vibration during the production of the sound.
