### Synopsis Writing

A synopsis is a brief summary of the main points of a text, presentation, or other form of communication. It is usually written for an academic or professional purpose, such as to inform readers, agents, editors, or reviewers about the content and scope of a work. A synopsis should be concise, clear, and accurate, and should highlight the most important and unique aspects of the work.

The following are some steps to write a synopsis for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication:

- Make a list of the key elements of the unit, such as the definition, types, characteristics, purposes, and examples of technical communication.
- Write a good opening sentence that summarizes the main topic and objective of the unit, such as: "This unit introduces the concept of technical communication and its various forms and functions in different contexts and fields."
- Write one or two paragraphs that cover the main points of the unit, using the list of key elements as a guide. For each point, provide a brief explanation and an example or illustration if possible. For example: "Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose, using appropriate language, style, format, and media. Technical communication can take many forms, such as reports, manuals, proposals, memos, emails, presentations, websites, blogs, podcasts, and social media posts. Technical communication has different characteristics depending on the audience, purpose, and context of the communication, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, usability, and ethics. Technical communication serves various purposes, such as to inform, instruct, persuade, or entertain the audience, and to achieve specific goals, such as to solve problems, make decisions, or complete tasks."
- Read through the synopsis and check for clarity, accuracy, completeness, and coherence. Make sure the synopsis covers all the main points of the unit and does not include any irrelevant or redundant information. Make sure the synopsis follows a logical order and transitions smoothly between the points. Make sure the synopsis uses appropriate terminology, grammar, punctuation, and spelling.
- Read through the synopsis again and edit for brevity and style. Make sure the synopsis is concise and does not exceed the word limit or page limit specified by the instructor or the publisher. Make sure the synopsis uses an objective and formal tone and avoids personal opinions, jargon, slang, or colloquialisms. Make sure the synopsis is consistent and follows the formatting and citation guidelines provided by the instructor or the publisher.
