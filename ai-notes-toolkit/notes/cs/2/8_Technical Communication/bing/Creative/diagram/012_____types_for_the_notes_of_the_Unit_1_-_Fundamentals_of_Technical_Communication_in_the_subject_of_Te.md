# Types of Notes

- **Outline notes**: These are notes that summarize the main points and subpoints of a topic in a hierarchical structure.       - Technical communication can be written, oral, visual, or multimodal
      - Technical communication can be used for various purposes, such as informing, instructing, persuading, or entertaining
    - Characteristics and principles of technical communication
      - Technical communication should be clear, concise, accurate, coherent, and appropriate
      - Technical communication should consider the needs, expectations, and preferences of the audience
      - Technical communication should follow the standards and conventions of the field and the genre

      - Technical communication requires using various sources, methods, and tools for gathering, analyzing, and presenting information
      - Technical communication involves applying various rhetorical, ethical, and intercultural strategies to achieve the desired outcome
