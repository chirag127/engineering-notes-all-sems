### Methods for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

- Notes are brief records of information that help you recall, review, and revise the material you have learned.
- There are different methods for taking notes, depending on the purpose, context, and format of the source material.
- Some common methods for taking notes are:

  - **Outline method**: This method involves writing the main points and subpoints of the material in a hierarchical structure, using indentation, bullets, or numbering. This method is useful for organizing and summarizing the material in a logical way.
  - **Cornell method**: This method involves dividing the paper into two columns: one for writing the main ideas and keywords, and the other for writing the details, examples, and questions. This method is useful for identifying and highlighting the important information and testing your understanding of the material.
  - **Mapping method**: This method involves drawing a diagram or a graphic organizer that shows the relationships between the concepts and the details of the material. This method is useful for visualizing and memorizing the material and showing how the parts fit into the whole.
  - **Charting method**: This method involves creating a table or a matrix that compares and contrasts the different aspects of the material. This method is useful for analyzing and synthesizing the material and finding similarities and differences.
  - **Sentence method**: This method involves writing the main points and details of the material in complete sentences, using your own words. This method is useful for paraphrasing and explaining the material and developing your writing skills.
