### Speaking with a purpose

- Speaking with a purpose means having a clear and specific goal for your oral communication, such as informing, instructing, persuading, or entertaining your audience .
- Speaking with a purpose also means adapting your message to suit the needs, interests, and expectations of your audience, as well as the context and occasion of your speech .
- Speaking with a purpose requires you to plan, organize, and deliver your speech effectively, using appropriate language, tone, voice, and nonverbal cues .
- Speaking with a purpose can help you achieve various outcomes, such as:
  - Sharing technical or specialized information with a non-expert or general audience 
  - Convincing an audience to take some specific action or adopt a certain viewpoint 
  - Building rapport and trust with your audience and establishing your credibility and authority 
  - Engaging and entertaining your audience and creating a positive impression 
- To speak with a purpose, you can use the PAM framework, which stands for Purpose, Audience, and Message. This framework helps you to:
  - Identify and convey the purpose or objective of your speech to the audience
  - Analyze and understand your audience, their triggers, likes, and dislikes
  - Craft and deliver a verbal message that is clear, concise, and relevant to your purpose and audience
