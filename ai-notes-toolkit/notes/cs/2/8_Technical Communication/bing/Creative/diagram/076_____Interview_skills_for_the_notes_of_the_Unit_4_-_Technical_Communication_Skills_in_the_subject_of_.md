### Interview skills

- Interview skills are the abilities and techniques that help you perform well in a job interview and communicate your value to a potential employer.
- Interview skills include both verbal and non-verbal communication skills, as well as problem-solving, critical thinking, and interpersonal skills.
- Interview skills are important for technical communicators, who often need to collaborate with subject matter experts, stakeholders, and users, and present complex information in a clear and concise way.
- Some of the interview skills that technical communicators should have are:

  - **Research skills**: Technical communicators should research the company, the role, and the industry before the interview, and prepare relevant questions and examples to demonstrate their knowledge and interest.
  - **Oral communication skills**: Technical communicators should speak clearly, confidently, and respectfully, and use appropriate language and tone for the audience and the situation. They should also listen actively, ask clarifying questions, and summarize key points.
  - **Written communication skills**: Technical communicators should prepare a professional resume, cover letter, and portfolio that showcase their skills and achievements. They should also follow up with a thank-you email after the interview, and highlight their strengths and value proposition.
  - **Technical skills**: Technical communicators should be familiar with the tools, methods, and standards of their field, and be able to explain and demonstrate their technical proficiency and experience. They should also be able to solve technical problems and present solutions in a logical and structured way.
  - **Soft skills**: Technical communicators should display a range of soft skills, such as teamwork, adaptability, creativity, and attention to detail. They should also show their enthusiasm, motivation, and passion for the role and the company.
