### Nuances for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

- Technical communication skills are the abilities to communicate effectively and efficiently in technical contexts, such as engineering, science, technology, or business.
- Technical communication skills include both oral and written communication, as well as visual and digital communication.
- Technical communication skills are essential for professionals who need to convey complex, precise, and accurate information to various audiences, such as colleagues, clients, customers, users, regulators, or the public.
- Technical communication skills require the following competencies:
  - Understanding the purpose, audience, and context of the communication
  - Planning and organizing the content and structure of the communication
  - Choosing the appropriate format, style, tone, and level of detail for the communication
  - Using clear, concise, coherent, and correct language for the communication
  - Incorporating relevant and reliable evidence, data, examples, and illustrations for the communication
  - Designing and using effective visual and digital elements for the communication
  - Revising, editing, and proofreading the communication for quality and accuracy
  - Adhering to the ethical and professional standards and norms of the communication
- Technical communication skills can be applied to various types of documents and presentations, such as reports, proposals, manuals, instructions, specifications, memos, emails, letters, resumes, posters, slides, websites, blogs, podcasts, videos, etc.
- Technical communication skills can be improved by practicing, reviewing, and receiving feedback on the communication, as well as by studying and following the best practices and guidelines of the field.
