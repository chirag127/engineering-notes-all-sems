# Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

## Introduction
- Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take various forms, such as written, oral, visual, or digital.
- Technical communication can be classified into two broad categories: informative and persuasive.

## Informative Technical Communication
- Informative technical communication aims to provide factual, objective, and accurate information to the audience.
- Informative technical communication can be further divided into three types: descriptive, instructional, and analytical.

### Descriptive Technical Communication
- Descriptive technical communication describes the features, characteristics, or properties of an object, process, or phenomenon.
- Examples of descriptive technical communication are definitions, descriptions, classifications, and comparisons.

### Instructional Technical Communication
- Instructional technical communication provides step-by-step directions or guidance on how to perform a task, operate a device, or use a system.
- Examples of instructional technical communication are manuals, procedures, tutorials, and recipes.

### Analytical Technical Communication
- Analytical technical communication evaluates, interprets, or explains data, information, or evidence to draw conclusions, make recommendations, or solve problems.
- Examples of analytical technical communication are reports, proposals, feasibility studies, and reviews.

## Persuasive Technical Communication
- Persuasive technical communication aims to influence the attitudes, beliefs, or actions of the audience.
- Persuasive technical communication can be further divided into three types: argumentative, motivational, and promotional.

### Argumentative Technical Communication
- Argumentative technical communication presents a claim or a position on an issue and supports it with reasons and evidence.
- Examples of argumentative technical communication are essays, editorials, letters, and speeches.

### Motivational Technical Communication
- Motivational technical communication appeals to the emotions, values, or interests of the audience and encourages them to take action or change their behavior.
- Examples of motivational technical communication are motivational speeches, slogans, testimonials, and stories.

### Promotional Technical Communication
- Promotional technical communication promotes or advertises a product, service, organization, or idea to the audience and highlights its benefits, features, or advantages.
- Examples of promotional technical communication are brochures, flyers, posters, commercials, and websites.

## Conclusion
- Technical communication is a diverse and dynamic field that requires various skills, strategies, and techniques to communicate effectively with different audiences and purposes.
- Technical communication can be informative or persuasive, and each type has its own subtypes and examples.
- Technical communication can help you achieve your personal, academic, and professional goals.
