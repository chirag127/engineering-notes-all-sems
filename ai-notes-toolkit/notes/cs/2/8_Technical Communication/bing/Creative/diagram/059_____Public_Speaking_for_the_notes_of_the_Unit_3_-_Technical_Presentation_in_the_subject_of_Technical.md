### Public Speaking for the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation

- A technical presentation is a type of public speaking that aims to inform, educate or persuade an audience about a specific topic, usually related to science, engineering, technology or research.
- A technical presentation requires a clear structure, a well-researched content, a confident delivery and an engaging style to capture the attention and interest of the audience.
- Some tips for preparing and delivering a technical presentation are:

  - Know your subject matter: You should have a thorough understanding of your topic and be able to explain it in simple terms. You should also anticipate possible questions or objections from the audience and prepare your answers in advance.
  - Know your audience: You should tailor your presentation to the level of knowledge, interest and expectations of your audience. You should also use appropriate language, examples and visuals that suit your audience .
  - Configure your IDE: If you are using an integrated development environment (IDE) to demonstrate your code or software, you should make sure it is configured properly and runs smoothly. You should also test your code before the presentation and have a backup plan in case of technical issues.
  - Minimize distractions: You should avoid any unnecessary or irrelevant information, graphics, animations or sounds that may distract the audience from your main message. You should also check the room, the equipment and the lighting before the presentation and eliminate any potential sources of noise or interference .
  - Get the right equipment: You should use a microphone, a projector, a pointer and a remote control to enhance your presentation and make it easier for the audience to follow. You should also have a backup copy of your presentation on a USB drive or an online platform in case of technical issues .
  - Rehearse in advance: You should practice your presentation several times before the actual event, preferably in front of a mirror, a camera or a friend. You should also time yourself and adjust your content and pace accordingly. You should also practice your body language, eye contact, voice and gestures to convey confidence and enthusiasm  .
  - Finally, enjoy the experience: You should try to relax and have fun during your presentation. You should also smile, be friendly and interact with the audience. You should also thank the audience for their attention and invite them to ask questions or give feedback  .
