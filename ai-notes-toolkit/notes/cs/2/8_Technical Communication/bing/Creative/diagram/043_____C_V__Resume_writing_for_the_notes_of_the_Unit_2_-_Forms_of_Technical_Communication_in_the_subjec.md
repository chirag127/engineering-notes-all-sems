### C.V./Resume writing

- A C.V. (curriculum vitae) or a resume is a document that summarizes your education, skills, work experience, and achievements for a potential employer.
- A C.V. or a resume is usually the first impression that you make on a hiring manager, so it is important to make it clear, concise, and professional.
- A C.V. or a resume can have different formats and styles depending on the purpose, the industry, and the country. However, some common elements that most C.V.s or resumes should include are:

  - Contact information: your name, phone number, email address, and optionally your address, social media profiles, and website or portfolio links.
  - Objective or summary: a brief statement that highlights your career goals and qualifications for the specific job or field that you are applying for.
  - Education: a list of your academic degrees, diplomas, certificates, and courses, along with the institutions, dates, and grades or scores that you obtained.
  - Work experience: a list of your relevant work history, starting from the most recent or current position, along with the employer name, location, dates, and responsibilities or achievements that you accomplished.
  - Skills: a list of your relevant skills, such as languages, computer programs, technical abilities, or soft skills, that demonstrate your suitability for the job or field that you are applying for.
  - Awards and honors: a list of any recognitions, prizes, scholarships, or memberships that you have received or belong to, that showcase your achievements or involvement in your field or industry.
  - References: a list of people who can vouch for your work performance, skills, or character, such as former employers, professors, or colleagues, along with their contact information and relationship to you.

- Some tips for writing an effective C.V. or resume are:

  - Tailor your C.V. or resume to the specific job or field that you are applying for, by highlighting the most relevant information and using keywords that match the job description or industry standards.
  - Use a clear and consistent format and style, such as fonts, headings, bullet points, and margins, that make your C.V. or resume easy to read and scan.
  - Use action verbs, quantifiable results, and specific examples to describe your work experience and achievements, such as "managed a team of 10 people", "increased sales by 25%", or "designed a website for a client".
  - Proofread your C.V. or resume for any spelling, grammar, or punctuation errors, and ask someone else to review it for feedback and suggestions.
  - Keep your C.V. or resume concise and relevant, and avoid any unnecessary or irrelevant information, such as personal details, hobbies, or interests, unless they are directly related to the job or field that you are applying for.
