### Considerateness for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Considerateness is one of the seven C's of effective communication . It means being respectful, empathetic, and positive towards the audience and the purpose of the communication. Considerateness is important for oral communication because it helps to build rapport, trust, and understanding with the listeners. Some of the features of considerate communication are:

- Emphasize on "you" approach. Use pronouns and words that show interest and concern for the audience, such as "you", "your", "we", "us", "our", etc. Avoid using "I", "me", "my", "mine", etc. that may sound self-centered or arrogant.
- Empathize with the audience and exhibit interest in the audience. This will stimulate a positive reaction from the audience. Try to understand their needs, expectations, feelings, and perspectives. Show that you care about them and their opinions. Use expressions such as "I understand", "I appreciate", "I agree", etc. to show empathy.
- Show optimism towards your audience. Emphasize on "what is possible" rather than "what is impossible". Lay stress on positive words such as "jovial", "committed", "thanks", "warm", "healthy", "help", etc. Avoid negative words such as "difficult", "problem", "failure", "blame", "complain", etc. that may discourage or offend the audience.
- Be polite and professional in your tone of voice. Use courteous and respectful language and avoid slang, jargon, or vulgar words. Use appropriate titles, names, and salutations. Avoid interrupting, shouting, or arguing with the audience. Apologize for any mistakes or inconveniences and thank the audience for their time and attention.

Considerateness in oral communication can help to achieve the following benefits  :

- It can enhance the credibility and reputation of the speaker and the message. The audience will perceive the speaker as more trustworthy, competent, and reliable.
- It can improve the clarity and comprehension of the message. The audience will be more attentive, interested, and engaged in the communication. They will be able to follow the speaker's logic and arguments more easily.
- It can foster a positive and cooperative relationship between the speaker and the audience. The audience will feel more valued, respected, and appreciated by the speaker. They will be more willing to listen, respond, and provide feedback to the speaker.
