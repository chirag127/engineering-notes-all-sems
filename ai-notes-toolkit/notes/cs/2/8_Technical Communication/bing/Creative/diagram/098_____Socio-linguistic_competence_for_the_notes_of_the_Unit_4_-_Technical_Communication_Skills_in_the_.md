### Socio-linguistic competence

- Socio-linguistic competence is the ability to use and respond to language appropriately, given the setting, the topic, and the relationships among the people communicating.
- Socio-linguistic competence involves understanding the social and cultural context of language use, such as the norms, values, beliefs, and behavioural patterns of a culture.
- Socio-linguistic competence also includes knowing how to vary one's language according to the purpose, audience, and situation, such as choosing formal or informal register, polite or impolite expressions, direct or indirect speech acts, etc.
- Socio-linguistic competence is different from linguistic competence, which is the system of unconscious knowledge that one knows when they know a language, such as grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation.
- Socio-linguistic competence is an important component of communicative competence, which is the ability to use language effectively and appropriately in various contexts.
- Socio-linguistic competence can be developed by exposing oneself to different varieties and styles of language, observing how people use language in different situations, and reflecting on one's own language choices and their effects.
