### Reading & comprehension for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- Reading is the process of extracting meaning from written or printed text by using one's prior knowledge, decoding skills, and comprehension strategies.
- Comprehension is the ability to understand, analyze, synthesize, and evaluate information that is read.
- Reading and comprehension are essential skills for technical communication, which is the process of conveying information in a clear, concise, accurate, and appropriate way for a specific audience and purpose.
- Some of the benefits of reading and comprehension for technical communication are:
  - They help to acquire new knowledge and update existing knowledge in various fields of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM).
  - They enable to critically evaluate the quality, reliability, and validity of different sources of information, such as books, journals, websites, reports, manuals, etc.
  - They facilitate to identify the main ideas, supporting details, arguments, assumptions, and implications of a text, and to compare and contrast them with other texts or perspectives.
  - They assist to apply the information learned from reading to solve problems, make decisions, design products, perform tasks, or communicate effectively with others.
  - They foster to develop one's vocabulary, grammar, style, and tone of writing, as well as to avoid plagiarism and cite sources correctly.
- Some of the strategies for improving reading and comprehension for technical communication are:
  - Before reading, activate prior knowledge, set a purpose, and preview the text to get an overview of its structure, content, and format.
  - During reading, use skimming, scanning, and close reading techniques to locate, select, and focus on the relevant information, and use annotation, summarization, and paraphrasing skills to record and organize the information.
  - After reading, use questioning, inferencing, and evaluating skills to deepen the understanding of the text, and use outlining, mapping, and synthesizing skills to integrate the information with other sources or ideas.
  - Throughout the reading process, use metacognitive skills to monitor and regulate one's own comprehension, and use feedback and revision skills to improve one's own reading and writing performance.
