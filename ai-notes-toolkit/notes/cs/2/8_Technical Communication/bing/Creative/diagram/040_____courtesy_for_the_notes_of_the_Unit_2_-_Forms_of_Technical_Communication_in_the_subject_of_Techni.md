### Courtesy

- Courtesy is the act of being polite, respectful, and considerate of others in communication.
- Courtesy is important for effective technical communication because it helps to establish rapport, trust, and goodwill with the audience, and to avoid misunderstandings, conflicts, and negative emotions.
- Courtesy can be expressed in various ways, such as:
  - Using appropriate titles, names, and pronouns to address the audience.
  - Using positive and constructive language, avoiding harsh, rude, or offensive words.
  - Expressing appreciation, gratitude, and recognition for the audience's time, effort, and feedback.
  - Apologizing sincerely and promptly for any mistakes, errors, or inconveniences caused by the communication.
  - Respecting the audience's privacy, confidentiality, and preferences.
  - Avoiding assumptions, stereotypes, or biases about the audience's background, culture, or values.
  - Being honest, accurate, and transparent in providing information, facts, and evidence.
  - Being responsive, attentive, and helpful in answering questions, addressing concerns, and providing solutions.
  - Being consistent, clear, and concise in organizing, formatting, and presenting the communication.
  - Following the norms, rules, and etiquette of the communication medium, channel, and context.

- Courtesy can enhance the quality, credibility, and effectiveness of technical communication by:
  - Increasing the audience's interest, attention, and engagement with the communication.
  - Building the audience's trust, confidence, and satisfaction with the communication.
  - Creating a positive and professional image and reputation for the communicator and the organization.
  - Strengthening the relationship and collaboration between the communicator and the audience.
  - Reducing the chances of miscommunication, confusion, or conflict with the audience.
  - Improving the feedback and response from the audience.
  - Achieving the purpose and goals of the communication.
