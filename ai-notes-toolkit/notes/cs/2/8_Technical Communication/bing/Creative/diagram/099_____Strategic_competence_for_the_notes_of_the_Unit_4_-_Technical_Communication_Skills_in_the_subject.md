### Strategic competence
- Strategic competence is the ability to use various strategies to achieve communicative or language learning goals, especially when there are difficulties or breakdowns in communication  .
- Strategic competence involves assessing, planning, and executing the most effective means of communication, taking into account the context, the purpose, the audience, and the available resources .
- Strategic competence connects declarative, procedural, and conditional knowledge, which are the types of knowledge that one needs to communicate effectively.
  - Declarative knowledge is the knowledge of facts, concepts, and rules of a language or a domain.
  - Procedural knowledge is the knowledge of how to apply declarative knowledge in specific situations or tasks.
  - Conditional knowledge is the knowledge of when, where, why, and how to use declarative and procedural knowledge appropriately and effectively.
- Strategic competence can be divided into two categories: communication strategies and learning strategies.
  - Communication strategies are the techniques that one uses to overcome communication problems, such as paraphrasing, asking for clarification, using gestures, or switching to another language.
  - Learning strategies are the techniques that one uses to improve one's language skills, such as setting goals, monitoring progress, seeking feedback, or using authentic materials.
- Strategic competence is important for technical communication skills because it helps one to:
  - Adapt to different situations and audiences, such as technical experts, non-experts, or international clients.
  - Use various modes and media of communication, such as oral, written, visual, or digital.
  - Solve communication problems and avoid misunderstandings, such as ambiguity, vagueness, or jargon.
  - Initiate innovation and bring strategic change, such as proposing new ideas, implementing solutions, or evaluating outcomes.
  - Enhance one's language proficiency and communication effectiveness, such as expanding vocabulary, improving grammar, or developing fluency.
