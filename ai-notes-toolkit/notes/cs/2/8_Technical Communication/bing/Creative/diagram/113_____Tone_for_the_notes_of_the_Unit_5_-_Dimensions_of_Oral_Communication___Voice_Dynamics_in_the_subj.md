### Tone

- Tone is the attitude or emotion that a speaker conveys through their voice.
- Tone can affect the meaning and impact of a message, as well as the relationship between the speaker and the listener.
- Tone can be influenced by various factors, such as the speaker's mood, intention, personality, culture, context, and audience.
- Tone can be classified into different types, such as formal, informal, friendly, hostile, polite, rude, confident, hesitant, etc.
- Tone can be modified by using different vocal elements, such as pitch, volume, rate, pause, stress, intonation, etc.
- Tone can also be enhanced by using nonverbal cues, such as facial expressions, gestures, posture, eye contact, etc.

Some tips for using tone effectively in oral communication are:

- Be aware of your own tone and how it affects your message and your listener.
- Adapt your tone to suit your purpose, audience, and situation.
- Use a consistent and appropriate tone throughout your speech or presentation.
- Avoid using a tone that is too monotone, too emotional, too sarcastic, too aggressive, or too passive.
- Use feedback from your listener or other sources to evaluate and improve your tone.
