### Falling Tone

- Falling tone is a type of intonation that involves a downward pitch movement at the end of a sentence or a phrase.
- Falling tone is used to signal the completion of a thought, a statement, or a question that requests information.
- Falling tone can also convey certainty, confidence, finality, or authority.
- Examples of sentences that use falling tone are:

  - I'm going to the library. (statement)
  - Where do you live? (information question)
  - This is the last time I'm telling you. (warning)
  - Please turn off your phone. (request)

- To produce a falling tone, start with a higher pitch and gradually lower it until the end of the sentence or phrase.
- Falling tone can be contrasted with rising tone, which involves an upward pitch movement and can indicate a question, a request for confirmation, a continuation of a thought, or uncertainty.
