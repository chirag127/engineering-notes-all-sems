### Theme clarity

- Theme clarity is the quality of having a clear and consistent main idea or message throughout a piece of technical communication.
- Theme clarity helps the readers to understand the purpose, scope, and main points of the document, and to follow the logical flow of information and arguments.
- Theme clarity can be achieved by using various strategies, such as:
  - Writing a clear and concise title that reflects the main idea of the document.
  - Writing an informative introduction that provides the background, context, and objectives of the document, and states the main idea or thesis statement.
  - Organizing the document into sections and subsections that have clear and descriptive headings and subheadings, and that follow a logical order and structure.
  - Using transitions and signposts to connect the sections and subsections, and to guide the readers through the document.
  - Writing clear and coherent paragraphs that have topic sentences, supporting sentences, and concluding sentences, and that develop one main idea or point per paragraph.
  - Using appropriate language, tone, and style that suit the audience, purpose, and genre of the document, and that avoid ambiguity, jargon, and unnecessary words.
  - Writing a concise and effective conclusion that summarizes the main points of the document, and that provides recommendations, implications, or future directions if applicable.
  - Revising and editing the document to check for clarity, coherence, consistency, accuracy, and correctness.
