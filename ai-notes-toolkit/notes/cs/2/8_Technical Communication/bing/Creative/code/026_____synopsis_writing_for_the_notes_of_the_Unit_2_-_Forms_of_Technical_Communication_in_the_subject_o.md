### Synopsis Writing for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

A synopsis is a brief summary that gives readers an overview of the main points of a text, presentation, or other form of communication. A synopsis can help readers to quickly understand and categorize the content, and it can also help writers to organize and structure their ideas. A synopsis is usually written in the present tense and in the third person, unless otherwise specified by the instructor or the genre.

The following are some steps to write a synopsis for your notes of the unit 2 - forms of technical communication:

1. Make a list of the key elements of the unit. These include the definition, purpose, characteristics, types, and examples of technical communication, as well as the principles and strategies for effective technical communication.
2. Write a good opening sentence that summarizes the main topic and scope of the unit. For example, "This unit introduces the concept of technical communication and its various forms, and provides guidelines for creating clear, concise, and accurate technical documents and presentations."
3. Write one or two sentences for each key element, explaining what it is, why it is important, and how it relates to the other elements. Use transitions and connectors to show the logical flow and connections between the elements. For example, "Technical communication is the process of conveying information and instructions to a specific audience for a specific purpose. It is different from other forms of communication in that it requires a high level of accuracy, clarity, conciseness, and usability. Technical communication can take many forms, such as reports, manuals, proposals, memos, emails, websites, blogs, podcasts, videos, and presentations. Depending on the form, the technical communicator needs to apply different principles and strategies to ensure that the message is effective and appropriate for the audience, context, and goal."
4. Write a concluding sentence that summarizes the main points and highlights the significance or implications of the unit. For example, "By understanding the forms of technical communication and the techniques for producing them, you can improve your communication skills and enhance your professional credibility and competence."
5. Read through the synopsis and check for accuracy, completeness, coherence, and conciseness. Make sure that you have covered all the key elements and that you have not included any unnecessary or irrelevant details. Also, make sure that your synopsis is clear, logical, and consistent, and that it follows the format and style guidelines of your instructor or the genre.
6. Read through the synopsis again and edit and proofread it for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and word choice. Make sure that your synopsis is free of errors and that it uses appropriate and precise language. You can also use tools such as grammar checkers, spell checkers, and dictionaries to help you with this step.
7. Give your synopsis a title that reflects the main topic and scope of the unit. For example, "Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication: A Synopsis".
