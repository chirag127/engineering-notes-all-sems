### Method for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

- Unit 4 covers the topics of oral communication, presentation skills, group discussion, and interview skills.
- Oral communication is the process of expressing ideas, opinions, or information through spoken words. It involves the following elements:
  - Sender: the person who initiates the communication
  - Receiver: the person who receives the communication
  - Message: the content of the communication
  - Channel: the medium through which the communication is transmitted
  - Feedback: the response or reaction of the receiver to the message
  - Noise: any factor that interferes with the communication
  - Context: the situation or environment in which the communication takes place
- Presentation skills are the abilities to plan, prepare, and deliver effective oral presentations to a specific audience and purpose. They involve the following steps:
  - Analyze the audience: identify their needs, expectations, and interests
  - Define the purpose: determine the main goal and objective of the presentation
  - Select the topic: choose a relevant and interesting topic that suits the audience and the purpose
  - Research the topic: gather and evaluate information from credible sources
  - Organize the content: arrange the information in a logical and coherent structure
  - Design the visuals: create and use appropriate visual aids to support the content
  - Prepare the notes: write down the key points and cues for the presentation
  - Rehearse the presentation: practice the delivery, timing, and body language of the presentation
  - Deliver the presentation: use effective verbal and non-verbal communication skills to engage the audience and convey the message
  - Evaluate the presentation: seek and receive feedback from the audience and self-assess the strengths and weaknesses of the presentation
- Group discussion is a collaborative activity in which a small number of people exchange their views and opinions on a given topic or issue. It involves the following skills:
  - Listening: paying attention and showing interest to the speakers
  - Speaking: expressing one's ideas clearly and confidently
  - Questioning: asking relevant and meaningful questions to clarify or challenge the ideas
  - Responding: answering the questions and addressing the comments of the speakers
  - Agreeing: supporting and acknowledging the valid points of the speakers
  - Disagreeing: opposing and refuting the invalid points of the speakers
  - Summarizing: highlighting and concluding the main points of the discussion
- Interview skills are the abilities to prepare and perform well in a formal or informal meeting with one or more interviewers for a specific purpose, such as getting a job, admission, or scholarship. They involve the following aspects:
  - Before the interview: research the organization, the position, and the interviewer; review the resume and the portfolio; anticipate the possible questions and prepare the answers; dress appropriately and arrive on time
  - During the interview: greet and introduce oneself; maintain eye contact and positive body language; answer the questions confidently and honestly; ask relevant and intelligent questions; thank and follow up with the interviewer
  - After the interview: reflect and evaluate the performance; identify the areas of improvement; send a thank-you note to the interviewer
