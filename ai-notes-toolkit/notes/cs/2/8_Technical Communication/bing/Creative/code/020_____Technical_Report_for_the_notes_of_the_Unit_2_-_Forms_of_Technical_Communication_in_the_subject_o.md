### Technical Report

- A technical report is a document that presents and analyzes data, findings, or results of a research, experiment, or project in a clear and objective manner.
- A technical report has a specific purpose, audience, and format, depending on the context and discipline.
- A technical report usually consists of the following sections:

  - Title page: contains the title of the report, the name and affiliation of the author(s), the date of submission, and other relevant information.
  - Abstract: summarizes the main points and conclusions of the report in a concise and informative way.
  - Table of contents: lists the headings and subheadings of the report and their corresponding page numbers.
  - Introduction: introduces the topic, background, objectives, scope, and organization of the report.
  - Literature review: reviews the existing literature and research related to the topic and identifies the gaps or problems that the report aims to address.
  - Methodology: describes the methods, procedures, tools, and materials used to collect and analyze the data or conduct the experiment or project.
  - Results: presents and discusses the findings or outcomes of the data analysis or experiment or project, using tables, graphs, charts, or other visual aids as appropriate.
  - Discussion: interprets and evaluates the results, explains their implications and limitations, and compares them with the literature review and the objectives of the report.
  - Conclusion: summarizes the main points and findings of the report, draws the logical inferences, and provides the recommendations or suggestions for future work or improvement.
  - References: lists the sources that are cited or consulted in the report, using a consistent citation style.
  - Appendices: includes any additional or supplementary information that supports the report, such as raw data, calculations, questionnaires, etc.

- A technical report should follow the general principles of technical communication, such as clarity, accuracy, conciseness, coherence, and ethics.
- A technical report should also adhere to the specific guidelines and requirements of the instructor, the organization, or the publication.
