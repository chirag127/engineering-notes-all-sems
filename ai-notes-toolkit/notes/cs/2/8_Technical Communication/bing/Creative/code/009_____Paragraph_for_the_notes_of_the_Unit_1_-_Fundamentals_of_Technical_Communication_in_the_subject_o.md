### Paragraph for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

- A paragraph is a group of sentences that develops one main idea.
- A paragraph has three parts: a topic sentence, supporting sentences, and a concluding sentence.
- A topic sentence introduces the main idea of the paragraph and tells the reader what the paragraph will be about.
- Supporting sentences provide details, examples, facts, or evidence that support the topic sentence.
- A concluding sentence summarizes the main idea of the paragraph and signals the end of the paragraph.
- A paragraph should be coherent, cohesive, and concise.
- Coherence means that the sentences are logically connected and easy to follow.
- Cohesion means that the sentences are linked by transitional words, phrases, or devices that show the relationship between ideas.
- Conciseness means that the sentences are clear and concise, avoiding unnecessary words or repetition.
- A paragraph should also be consistent in tone, style, and format.
