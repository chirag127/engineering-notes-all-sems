# Encoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Encoding is the process of turning thoughts into communication.
- The encoder is the person who creates the message, adapts it to the receiver, and transmits it across a channel.
- The encoding process is based on the purpose of communication and the relation between the sender and the receiver.
- The encoder uses symbols to communicate their message, such as words, gestures, images, sounds, etc.
- The encoder has to consider the following factors while encoding a message:
  - The context of the communication, such as the topic, the occasion, the setting, etc.
  - The characteristics of the receiver, such as their knowledge, interest, attitude, culture, etc.
  - The channel of communication, such as oral, written, visual, etc.
  - The code of communication, such as the language, the vocabulary, the grammar, the tone, etc.
  - The feedback of the communication, such as the response, the reaction, the interpretation, etc.
- The encoding process can be affected by various barriers, such as noise, distortion, misunderstanding, misinterpretation, etc.
- The encoding process can be improved by using clear, concise, coherent, and courteous communication, as well as by using appropriate nonverbal cues, such as eye contact, facial expressions, gestures, etc.
- The encoding process is essential for effective oral communication, as it helps to convey the message accurately, persuasively, and engagingly to the receiver.
