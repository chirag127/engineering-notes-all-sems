### Techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- A technical presentation is a form of oral communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience, such as experts, managers, customers, or the general public.
- A technical presentation has three main components: the introduction, the body, and the conclusion.
- The introduction should capture the attention of the audience, establish the purpose and scope of the presentation, and preview the main points or outline.
- The body should present the technical information in a clear, logical, and coherent manner, using appropriate visual aids, examples, and evidence to support the main points.
- The conclusion should summarize the main points, restate the purpose and significance of the presentation, and provide a call to action or recommendation if applicable.
- Some techniques for preparing and delivering a technical presentation are:

  - Analyze the audience and the context of the presentation, and tailor the content, tone, and style accordingly.
  - Use the 4Ps model to plan the presentation: purpose, people, place, and point.
  - Use the 4Cs model to structure the presentation: clear, concise, complete, and correct.
  - Use the 4Rs model to revise and rehearse the presentation: review, refine, rehearse, and repeat.
  - Use the 4Es model to engage the audience during the presentation: eye contact, expression, enthusiasm, and energy.
  - Use the 4As model to handle questions and feedback after the presentation: acknowledge, answer, ask, and appreciate.
