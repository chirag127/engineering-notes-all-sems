

### Quizzes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

Here are some quizzes to help you test your knowledge about the technical presentation unit in the subject of technical communication:

1. What is the purpose of a technical presentation?
- To inform the audience about a technical topic.
- To persuade the audience to take a specific action.
- To entertain the audience with technical information.

2. What should be included in the introduction of a technical presentation?
- Background information about the topic.
- The speaker's credentials.
- A summary of what will be covered in the presentation.

3. What are some common visual aids used in technical presentations?
- Slides.
- Handouts.
- Videos.

4. What are some ways to engage the audience during a technical presentation?
- Asking questions.
- Using humor.
- Demonstrating a product or process.

5. What are some common mistakes to avoid during a technical presentation?
- Reading directly from slides or notes.
- Speaking too quickly or too slowly.
- Failing to rehearse beforehand.
