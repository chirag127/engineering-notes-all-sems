

### Impersonal Notes for Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

When it comes to technical presentations, it's important to maintain a level of professionalism and formality. Here are some tips on how to keep your presentation impersonal and effective:

- Stick to the facts: Avoid injecting personal opinions or anecdotes into your presentation. Stick to the relevant data and information that supports your message and objectives.
- Use formal language: Refrain from using slang, contractions, or colloquialisms. Instead, use formal language that is appropriate for a technical presentation.
- Keep it concise: Your audience is likely looking for specific information, so keep your presentation focused and to the point. Avoid lengthy tangents or irrelevant details.
- Use visuals: Visual aids such as graphs, charts, and diagrams can be a powerful way to convey information without relying on personal anecdotes or opinions.
- Avoid emojis: Emojis may be appropriate in personal communication, but they have no place in a technical presentation. Stick to formal language and visuals to convey your message.
- Don't include external links: Instead of relying on external sources, present the information yourself. This will help you maintain control over the information and keep your presentation focused.
- Use markdown format: Markdown is a simple and effective way to format your presentation notes. It can also help you stay organized and focused on your objectives.
- Practice, practice, practice: The more you rehearse your presentation, the more confident and effective you will be. Practice in front of a mirror, with friends or colleagues, or record yourself to identify areas for improvement.

By following these tips, you can create an effective and professional technical presentation that resonates with your audience and achieves your objectives.
