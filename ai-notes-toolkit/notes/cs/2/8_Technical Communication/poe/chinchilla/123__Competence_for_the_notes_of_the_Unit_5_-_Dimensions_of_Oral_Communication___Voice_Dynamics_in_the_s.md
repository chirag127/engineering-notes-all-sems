### Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

Effective oral communication is an essential skill in the field of technical communication. It is imperative to develop competence in oral communication to convey technical information effectively to the audience. Here are some key competencies to keep in mind while communicating orally:

1. Clarity and Conciseness: Clear and concise communication is key to effective oral communication. Use simple language and avoid technical jargon that might confuse the audience. Keep your message clear and to the point.

2. Voice Dynamics: Voice dynamics are an essential aspect of oral communication. Use the appropriate volume, pitch, and tone to convey your message effectively. Use pauses and inflections to emphasize important points.

3. Non-Verbal Communication: Non-verbal communication such as facial expressions, body language, and gestures can convey your message effectively. Use appropriate non-verbal cues to support your message.

4. Listening Skills: Listening is an essential part of oral communication. Listen actively to understand the audience's perspective and respond appropriately. Use paraphrasing to clarify your understanding of the message.

5. Adaptability: Adaptability is an essential skill in oral communication. Be prepared to adapt your communication style to suit the audience's needs and preferences. Adjust your message based on the audience's level of knowledge.

6. Confidence: Confidence is an essential aspect of effective oral communication. Project confidence in your message and delivery to establish credibility with the audience. Practice your delivery to build confidence.

7. Preparation: Preparation is key to effective oral communication. Plan and organize your message, and rehearse your delivery to ensure a smooth and effective presentation.

8. Feedback: Feedback is an essential aspect of oral communication. Seek feedback from the audience to understand their perspective and improve your communication skills.

Developing competence in oral communication is a continuous process. Keep practicing and refining your skills to become an effective communicator in the field of technical communication.
