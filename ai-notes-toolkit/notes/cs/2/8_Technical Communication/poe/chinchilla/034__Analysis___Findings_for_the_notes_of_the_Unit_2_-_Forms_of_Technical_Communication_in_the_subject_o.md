### Analysis & Findings for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

In the field of technical communication, it is important to understand the various forms of communication that are used to convey technical information. The notes from Unit 2 on Forms of Technical Communication provide valuable insights into these communication forms. Here are some key findings and analysis from the notes:

1. Technical Reports: Technical reports are formal documents that provide detailed information on a particular topic or project. They are used to communicate information within an organization or to external stakeholders. Technical reports are structured in a specific format, which includes an introduction, background information, methodology, results, and conclusions.

2. Proposals: Proposals are documents that are used to persuade someone to take a particular course of action. They are commonly used in business settings to pitch ideas or projects to potential clients or investors. Proposals typically include an executive summary, background information, a detailed plan, and a budget.

3. Manuals: Manuals are documents that provide instructions on how to use a particular product or service. They are commonly used in technical fields such as engineering, IT, and healthcare. Manuals are written in a clear and concise manner, with step-by-step instructions and illustrations.

4. Presentations: Presentations are formal talks that are delivered to an audience. They are used to communicate information or persuade someone to take a particular course of action. Presentations can be delivered in various formats, including PowerPoint, Prezi, or Keynote.

5. Emails: Emails are a common form of communication in the workplace. They are used to convey information, ask questions, or provide updates. Emails should be written in a professional manner, with a clear subject line and a concise message.

Overall, the notes from Unit 2 on Forms of Technical Communication provide a comprehensive overview of the various forms of communication used in technical fields. By understanding these forms of communication, technical communicators can effectively convey complex information to their intended audience.
