### Etiquette for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the Subject of Technical Communication

When taking notes for the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication, it is important to follow certain etiquette to ensure that your notes are organized, effective, and helpful for future reference. Here are some tips to keep in mind:

1. Be organized: When taking notes, make sure to keep them organized in a logical manner. Use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to break up the information into manageable chunks. This will make it easier to review your notes later.

2. Be concise: Avoid writing down every single word that the instructor says. Instead, focus on capturing the main points and key ideas. Use abbreviations and symbols to save time and space.

3. Be accurate: It is important to write down information accurately, especially when it comes to technical terms and concepts. If you are unsure about something, ask the instructor for clarification.

4. Be attentive: Pay attention to the instructor's tone, body language, and other nonverbal cues. These can provide important context and help you understand the material better.

5. Be respectful: Avoid distracting others by talking, using your phone, or engaging in other activities during class. Show respect for the instructor and your fellow students by giving your full attention to the lecture.

6. Review your notes: After class, take some time to review your notes and fill in any gaps. This will help you retain the information better and prepare for future exams and assignments.

By following these etiquette tips, you can ensure that your notes are effective and helpful for your studies. Remember, taking good notes is an important skill for success in any academic or professional setting.
