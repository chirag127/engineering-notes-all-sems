### Syllables for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

In this unit, we will explore the crucial aspects of oral communication and delve deeper into the dynamics of voice. Here are the syllables for the notes of this unit:

1. **Introduction to Oral Communication**
   - Definition of Oral Communication
   - Importance of Oral Communication in Technical Communication
   - Types of Oral Communication
   - Barriers to Effective Oral Communication

2. **Dimensions of Oral Communication**
   - Verbal Communication
   - Nonverbal Communication
   - Paralinguistic Features
   - Cultural Influences on Oral Communication

3. **Voice Dynamics**
   - Pitch
   - Tone
   - Volume
   - Rate of Speech
   - Articulation and Pronunciation

4. **Voice Modulation Techniques**
   - Breathing Techniques
   - Voice Projection
   - Voice Inflection
   - Voice Variation
   - Vocal Warm-Up Exercises

5. **Effective Oral Presentations**
   - Planning and Preparation
   - Audience Analysis
   - Structure of Oral Presentations
   - Visual Aids
   - Delivery Techniques

6. **Overcoming Communication Barriers**
   - Listening Skills
   - Responding Skills
   - Feedback and Evaluation
   - Conflict Resolution
   - Cultural Competence

7. **Ethics in Oral Communication**
   - Professionalism in Oral Communication
   - Ethics of Persuasion
   - Ethics of Information Disclosure
   - Ethics of Criticism and Feedback

By studying and understanding these syllables, students will be equipped with the necessary knowledge and skills to become effective and ethical communicators in the technical communication field.
