
### Combination of Expressions for the Notes of Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical Communication involves the use of clear and concise language to explain complex concepts in a way that is accessible to the intended audience.
2. Technical Writing is the process of creating documents that explain technical concepts in a way that is easy to understand. 
3. Technical Writing requires the use of precise language, accurate grammar, and an organized structure.
4. Technical Writing should be written in a style that is appropriate for the intended audience.
5. Technical Writing should use visuals (such as diagrams, charts, and graphs) to help explain complex concepts.
6. Technical Writing should use appropriate formatting to ensure that the document is easy to read.
7. Technical Writing should be proofread and edited to ensure accuracy and clarity.
8. Technical Writing should be presented in a professional manner that is appropriate for the intended audience.
