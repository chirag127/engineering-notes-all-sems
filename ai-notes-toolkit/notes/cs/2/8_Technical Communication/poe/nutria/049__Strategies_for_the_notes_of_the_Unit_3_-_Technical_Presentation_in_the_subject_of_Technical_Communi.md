
### Strategies for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Understand the purpose of the presentation: Before beginning the preparation for a technical presentation, it is important to understand the purpose of the presentation. This will help you to focus on the relevant information and ensure that your presentation is effective. 

2. Research thoroughly: Before beginning the presentation, it is important to research the topic thoroughly. This will ensure that you are aware of the latest developments in the field and can present the most up-to-date information. 

3. Organize the information: Once the research is complete, it is important to organize the information in a logical manner. This will help to ensure that the presentation flows smoothly and that the audience is able to follow the presentation easily. 

4. Choose the right visuals: Visuals are a great way to help the audience understand the information presented. It is important to choose visuals that are appropriate for the topic and that will help the audience to better understand the material. 

5. Practice the presentation: Once the presentation has been prepared, it is important to practice the presentation. This will help to ensure that the presentation is delivered smoothly and that the speaker is comfortable with the material. 

6. Anticipate questions: It is important to anticipate questions that the audience may have during the presentation. This will help to ensure that the speaker is prepared to answer any questions that may arise. 

7. Review and revise: Before delivering the presentation, it is important to review and revise the material. This will help to ensure that the presentation is effective and that the information is accurate.
