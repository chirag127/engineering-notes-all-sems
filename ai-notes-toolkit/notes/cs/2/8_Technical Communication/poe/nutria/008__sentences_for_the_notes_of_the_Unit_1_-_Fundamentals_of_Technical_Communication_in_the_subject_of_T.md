
### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of creating and delivering written and visual messages for the purpose of informing, educating, and/or entertaining an audience.
2. Technical communication is used in a variety of contexts, such as user manuals, help documentation, product specifications, and training materials.
3. Technical communicators must possess a variety of skills, including writing, editing, graphic design, and public speaking.
4. Technical communicators must also be familiar with the tools and technologies used to create and deliver their messages, such as word processing software, page layout software, and web design software.
5. The goal of technical communication is to create content that is accurate, easy to understand, and meets the needs of the intended audience.
