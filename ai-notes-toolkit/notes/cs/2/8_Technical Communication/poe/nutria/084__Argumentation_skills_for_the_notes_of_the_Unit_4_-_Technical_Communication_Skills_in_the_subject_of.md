
### Argumentation Skills

1. Argumentation is a process of constructing and presenting a reasoned case for or against a certain point of view. 
2. Argumentation involves the use of logic and evidence to support a position. 
3. Argumentation is a key skill for technical communicators, as it allows them to effectively present their ideas and opinions. 
4. Argumentation involves analyzing an issue, forming a position, and providing support for that position. 
5. Argumentation requires the use of both inductive and deductive reasoning. 
6. Argumentation should be structured logically, with clear premises and conclusions. 
7. Argumentation should also be supported by evidence, such as facts, statistics, and expert opinions. 
8. Argumentation should be presented in a way that is clear, concise, and persuasive. 
9. Argumentation should also be respectful of other points of view, while still making a strong case for one's own position.
