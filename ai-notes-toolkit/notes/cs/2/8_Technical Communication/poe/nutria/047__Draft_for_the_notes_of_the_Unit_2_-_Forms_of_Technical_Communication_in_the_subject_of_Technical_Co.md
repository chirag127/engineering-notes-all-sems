
### Draft for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication
* Technical Communication is the process of conveying information or instructions to an audience through the use of written, spoken or visual means. 
* There are various forms of technical communication, including reports, manuals, instructions, memos, emails, presentations, videos and more. 
* Technical writers are responsible for creating and editing these documents, as well as ensuring their accuracy and clarity.
* Reports are used to document and analyze data, as well as to present findings and recommendations. Reports can be written in a variety of formats and styles, such as narrative, descriptive, analytical, and tabular. 
* Manuals are written documents that provide instructions to users on how to operate or maintain a product or service. Manuals must be accurate, concise, and easy to understand. 
* Instructions are written documents that provide step-by-step guidance on how to complete a task or process. Instructions should be clear, concise and accurate. 
* Memos are short documents used to communicate information within an organization. Memos should be brief, clear and to the point. 
* Emails are written messages sent electronically to one or more recipients. Emails should be professional, concise and free of errors. 
* Presentations are visual aids used to explain a concept or to provide an overview of a topic. Presentations should be organized, visually appealing and easy to understand. 
* Videos are visual aids used to explain a concept or to provide an overview of a topic. Videos should be well-edited, visually appealing and easy to understand.
