
### Content for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication is the exchange of messages through spoken words. It is a process of sharing ideas, thoughts, and feelings between two or more people.

2. Voice dynamics refer to the different aspects of the sound of a person's voice, including volume, pitch, and tone. It is important to understand how these elements can affect the way a message is received.

3. Technical communication is the process of conveying information related to a specific field or industry. It is used to provide instructions, explain processes, and describe products.

4. Listening is an important part of oral communication. It involves actively paying attention to what is being said and understanding the meaning behind the words.

5. Non-verbal communication is also an important part of oral communication. This includes body language, facial expressions, and gestures.

6. Speaking is the act of producing words and sentences to communicate a message. It is important to be clear and concise when speaking.

7. Voice projection is the ability to project one's voice so that it can be heard clearly by the intended audience. This is an important skill for effective oral communication.

8. Voice modulation is the ability to adjust the volume, pitch, and tone of one's voice in order to convey the intended message.

9. Active listening is the process of actively paying attention to what is being said and responding in a way that shows understanding.

10. Effective communication is the ability to communicate clearly and effectively in order to achieve a desired outcome. It is important to understand the different elements of oral communication in order to communicate effectively.
