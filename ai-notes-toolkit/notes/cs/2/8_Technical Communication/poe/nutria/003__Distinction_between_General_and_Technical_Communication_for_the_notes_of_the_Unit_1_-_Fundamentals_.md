
### Distinction between General and Technical Communication

1. General communication is a broad term that encompasses any form of communication between two or more people, while technical communication is a specialized form of communication used to convey technical information.

2. General communication is often informal and can be used to discuss virtually any topic, while technical communication is more structured and is used to convey specific information related to a particular field or industry.

3. General communication can be used to share opinions, feelings, and experiences, while technical communication is used to share factual information and provide detailed instructions.

4. General communication is often used in everyday conversations, while technical communication is used in professional settings such as engineering, medicine, and computer programming.

5. General communication is often used in informal writing, such as emails, letters, and blog posts, while technical communication is used in formal writing, such as manuals, reports, and specifications.
