
### Techniques for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Understand the purpose of the presentation: Before starting the presentation, it is important to understand the purpose of the presentation and the audience.

2. Choose the right format: Different formats of presentations can be used depending on the purpose, audience, and type of information being presented.

3. Select the content: Content should be carefully chosen to ensure that the main points are communicated in the most effective way.

4. Prepare visuals: Visuals such as charts, diagrams, and pictures can be used to help the audience understand the content better.

5. Practice the presentation: It is important to practice the presentation beforehand to ensure that the presentation flows smoothly and that the content is communicated clearly.

6. Engage the audience: It is important to engage the audience throughout the presentation by asking questions, providing interesting facts, or using humor.

7. Deliver the presentation: When delivering the presentation, it is important to maintain eye contact with the audience, use appropriate body language, and speak clearly.

8. Summarize the presentation: At the end of the presentation, it is important to summarize the main points and provide a conclusion.
