
### C.V./Resume Writing

* A CV (Curriculum Vitae) is a document that outlines an individual's educational and professional qualifications, experiences, and accomplishments. 
* The purpose of a CV is to provide a prospective employer with a comprehensive overview of an individual's skills, qualifications, and experiences. 
* A CV should include the following information: personal details, contact information, education, work experience, skills, interests, and references.
* When writing a CV, it is important to ensure that it is well-structured, concise, and easy to read. 
* It is also important to ensure that the CV is tailored to the job for which you are applying. 
* A resume is a document that outlines an individual's work experience, qualifications, and accomplishments.
* The purpose of a resume is to provide a prospective employer with a comprehensive overview of an individual's work experience, qualifications, and accomplishments. 
* A resume should include the following information: contact information, work experience, education, skills, and references. 
* When writing a resume, it is important to ensure that it is well-structured, concise, and easy to read. 
* It is also important to ensure that the resume is tailored to the job for which you are applying.
