
### Considerations for the Notes of Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is a form of communication used to convey technical information. It is used in a variety of fields such as engineering, computer science, and medicine.
2. Technical communication can take many forms, including books, articles, reports, presentations, and webpages.
3. Technical documents should be well-structured and organized in order to be easily understood.
4. Technical documents should be written in clear and concise language that is appropriate for the intended audience.
5. Technical documents should include visuals such as diagrams and charts to help illustrate concepts.
6. Technical documents should include references to other sources of information to provide additional context.
7. Technical documents should be edited for accuracy and consistency.
8. Technical documents should be reviewed by peers and experts to ensure accuracy and completeness.
