
### Response for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral communication is the exchange of information, ideas, and feelings through spoken words. It is a fundamental part of human interaction and can be used in many different contexts, such as in business, education, and social settings.

2. Voice dynamics refer to the characteristics of a speaker’s voice that can be used to convey meaning. These include pitch, volume, speed, intonation, and emphasis.

3. The use of effective voice dynamics is important in technical communication because it helps to make the speaker’s message more engaging and understandable. It can also help to convey emotion and sincerity.

4. The ability to effectively use voice dynamics is a skill that can be developed through practice and experience. It is important to remember to use a variety of techniques when speaking, such as varying pitch, volume, and speed.

5. It is also important to remember to use pauses and to speak clearly and slowly when addressing a technical audience. This will help to ensure that the audience understands the message and is able to follow the speaker’s train of thought.
