
### Considerateness for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the Subject of Technical Communication

* Respect the other participants in the conversation and maintain a courteous demeanor throughout.
* Listen to the other participants attentively and respond appropriately.
* Avoid interrupting the other participants while they are speaking.
* Be mindful of the other participants' feelings and emotions.
* Speak clearly and enunciate your words properly.
* Be aware of your body language and facial expressions.
* Use appropriate volume, pitch, and tone.
* Speak at a pace that is comfortable for all participants.
* Use pauses and silence to emphasize key points.
* Avoid using jargon and technical language.
* Ask questions to clarify any points that are not understood.
* Summarize the conversation to ensure all participants are on the same page.
