
### Speech & Personality

* Speech is the act of speaking words aloud, while personality is the way someone expresses themselves.
* When speaking, it is important to consider both the content and the delivery.
* The tone of voice, volume, pitch, speed, and pauses all play a role in how a message is interpreted. 
* A speaker's personality can be expressed through their vocal delivery, such as the use of humor, enthusiasm, or sarcasm.
* To achieve successful communication, it is important to consider the audience and the purpose of the message. 
* Voice dynamics can be used to emphasize certain words or phrases, and to create a more engaging experience for the listener.
* Nonverbal communication, such as body language, facial expressions, and gestures, can also be used to convey meaning. 
* To become an effective communicator, it is important to practice and develop verbal and nonverbal communication skills.
