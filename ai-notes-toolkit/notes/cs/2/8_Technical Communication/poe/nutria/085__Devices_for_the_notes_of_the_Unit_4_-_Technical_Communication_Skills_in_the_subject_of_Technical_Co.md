
### Devices for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the Subject of Technical Communication 

1. Computers: Computers are essential for technical communication, as they enable users to create, store, and share documents, images, and other files. Computers can also be used to access the internet, which is a valuable resource for research and communication.

2. Smartphones: Smartphones are increasingly becoming a part of the technical communication process. They allow users to access documents, images, and other files on the go and to communicate with colleagues, customers, and other stakeholders.

3. Tablets: Tablets are a convenient way to access documents, images, and other files, as well as communicate with colleagues, customers, and other stakeholders.

4. Projectors: Projectors can be used to display documents, images, and other files in a large format, making it easier for groups of people to view and discuss them.

5. Videoconferencing: Videoconferencing is an important tool for technical communication, as it allows users to communicate in real-time with colleagues, customers, and other stakeholders.

6. Digital Whiteboards: Digital whiteboards are becoming increasingly popular in the technical communication process, as they allow users to collaborate on documents, images, and other files in real-time.

7. Online Collaboration Tools: Online collaboration tools such as Google Docs and Microsoft Teams are invaluable for technical communication, as they allow users to collaborate on documents, images, and other files in real-time.
