
### Cohesion for the Notes of Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

1. Cohesion refers to the connections between ideas and sentences in a text. It is an important part of effective communication.
2. Cohesion is achieved through the use of linking words and phrases, such as pronouns, conjunctions, and adverbs.
3. Cohesion is also achieved through the use of rhetorical devices, such as repetition, parallelism, and antithesis.
4. Cohesion is important in technical communication because it helps to ensure that the text is clear and easy to understand.
5. Cohesion also helps to ensure that the text is logically organized and that the ideas are connected in a meaningful way.
6. To ensure cohesion in technical communication, writers should use linking words and phrases to connect ideas, use rhetorical devices to emphasize key points, and ensure that the text is logically organized.
