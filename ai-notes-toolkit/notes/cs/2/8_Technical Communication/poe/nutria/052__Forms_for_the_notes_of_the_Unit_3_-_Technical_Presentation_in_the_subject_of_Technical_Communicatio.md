
### Forms for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

1. Technical presentations can be made in various forms, such as: 
    * Oral presentations
    * Written reports
    * Demonstrations
    * Video or multimedia presentations

2. Oral presentations require strong public speaking skills and the ability to capture and hold the audience's attention.

3. Written reports should be well-structured and organized, with clear and concise language.

4. Demonstrations allow the presenter to show the audience how a product or process works.

5. Video or multimedia presentations combine audio, video, graphics, and other elements to create a more engaging and interactive experience.

6. When preparing a presentation, it is important to consider the audience and the purpose of the presentation.

7. Presenters should also be aware of any special considerations, such as time constraints and technical requirements.
