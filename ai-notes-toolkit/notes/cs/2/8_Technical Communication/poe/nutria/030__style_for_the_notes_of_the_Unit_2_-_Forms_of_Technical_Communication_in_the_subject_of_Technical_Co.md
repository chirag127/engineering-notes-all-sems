
### Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is a broad field that encompasses a variety of forms, including technical manuals, instructions, reports, memos, and presentations.
2. Technical manuals are written documents that provide detailed instructions on how to use a product or system. They are often written in a step-by-step format and include illustrations to help the reader understand the instructions.
3. Instructions are written documents that provide detailed information about a particular task or process. They are usually written in a step-by-step format and may include illustrations or diagrams.
4. Reports are written documents that provide a summary of a particular event or situation. Reports may include data, facts, and analysis.
5. Memos are short documents that provide information about a particular topic. They are usually used to communicate information within an organization.
6. Presentations are visual aids that are used to present information to an audience. Presentations can include slides, videos, and other visual elements.
