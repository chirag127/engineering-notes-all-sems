
### Nuances for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication requires the use of precise language and accurate information.
2. It is important to consider the audience when communicating technical information.
3. Technical communication should be clear and concise.
4. Technical writing should be organized in a logical manner.
5. Technical communication should be free of jargon and acronyms that are not commonly understood.
6. It is important to use visuals, such as diagrams, charts, and tables, to help communicate technical information.
7. Technical communication should be written in an appropriate tone for the audience.
8. Technical communication should be proofread and edited for accuracy.
