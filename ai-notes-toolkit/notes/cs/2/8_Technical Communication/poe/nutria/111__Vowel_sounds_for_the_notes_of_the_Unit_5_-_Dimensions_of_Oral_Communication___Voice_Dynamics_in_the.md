
### Vowel sounds for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

* Vowel sounds are the most important building blocks of speech. They are the primary source of vocal expression and communication.
* Vowels are produced by the vibration of the vocal folds and the shape of the mouth and tongue.
* There are 12 vowel sounds in English, each with a different sound and meaning.
* The International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) is a system for transcribing the sounds of language. It is used to represent the vowel sounds of English.
* The IPA symbols for the 12 vowel sounds of English are: /i:/, /ɪ/, /e/, /æ/, /ʌ/, /ɑ:/, /ɔ:/, /u:/, /ʊ/, /ɜ:/, /ə/, and /ɛ/.
* The pitch, volume, and length of a vowel sound can all be varied to express different meanings.
* Voice dynamics refer to the way a speaker uses their voice to convey emotion and meaning. It includes the use of pitch, volume, and emphasis.
* Voice dynamics are important for effective communication in a variety of contexts, such as public speaking, presentations, and interviews.
