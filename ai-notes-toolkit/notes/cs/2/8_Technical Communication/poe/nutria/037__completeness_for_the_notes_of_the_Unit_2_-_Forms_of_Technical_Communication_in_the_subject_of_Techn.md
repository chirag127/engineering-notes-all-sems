
### Completeness for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Completeness is one of the most important aspects of technical communication. It is the ability of a document to provide the reader with all the information they need to understand and act on the document's purpose.

2. The completeness of a document should be judged according to its purpose. For example, a technical manual should include all the information necessary for a user to understand and use the product, while a marketing brochure should provide enough information to make a potential customer interested in the product.

3. In order to ensure completeness, technical communicators must be aware of the needs of the intended audience and the context in which the document will be used. This includes researching the target audience, as well as the subject matter, in order to determine what information should be included.

4. In addition to providing all the necessary information, technical communicators must also ensure that the document is organized and easily navigable. This includes using clear headings, subheadings, and other formatting techniques to make the information easier to find and understand.

5. Finally, technical communicators should use plain language to ensure that the document is accessible to all readers. This includes avoiding jargon and technical terms, as well as using simple language to explain complex concepts.
