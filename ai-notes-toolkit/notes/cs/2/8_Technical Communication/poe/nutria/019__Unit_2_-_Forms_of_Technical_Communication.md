
## Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is a form of communication that is used to convey technical information in a concise, clear and accurate manner. It is often used in engineering, programming, medicine and other scientific fields.

2. Technical communication is typically written in a specific style and format that is designed to make the information easier to understand. This includes using specific terminology, diagrams, charts and tables.

3. Technical communication can take on various forms, including manuals, reports, specifications, instructions, presentations, and online help.

4. Technical writers are responsible for creating technical documents. They must have a deep understanding of the subject matter and be able to communicate it in a way that is clear and concise.

5. Technical documents must be accurate and up-to-date. The writer must also consider the audience and the context in which the document is to be used.

6. Technical communication can also take the form of oral presentations. The presenter must be able to clearly explain the technical information in a way that is easy to understand.
