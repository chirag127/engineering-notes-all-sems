
### Theme clarity for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

* Technical communication is a form of communication that is used to convey technical information in a clear and concise manner.
* It is used to explain complex concepts and processes in a way that is easy to understand.
* Technical communication can take many forms, such as technical reports, manuals, user guides, and even online help.
* The purpose of technical communication is to provide accurate, clear, and concise information that is easy to understand and follow.
* Technical communication should be written in a way that is accessible to its intended audience.
* It should be organized in a logical manner and include relevant visuals to help illustrate complex concepts.
* Technical communication should be written in a style that is appropriate for the intended audience.
* It should be written in a way that is concise and free of jargon. 
* Technical communication should always be proofread and edited for accuracy and clarity.
