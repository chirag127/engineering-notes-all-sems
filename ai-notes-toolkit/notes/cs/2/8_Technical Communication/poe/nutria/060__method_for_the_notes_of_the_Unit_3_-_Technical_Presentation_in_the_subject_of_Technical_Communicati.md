
### Method for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Identify the purpose of the technical presentation.
2. Research the topic thoroughly and generate an organized list of relevant information.
3. Draft an outline of the presentation, focusing on the main points and supporting evidence.
4. Create a visual aid for the presentation, such as a PowerPoint presentation, handout, or poster.
5. Practice the presentation several times to ensure accuracy and confidence.
6. Deliver the presentation in a clear and confident manner, using appropriate body language and vocal techniques.
7. Anticipate and address questions from the audience.
8. Conclude the presentation in a succinct and effective manner.
