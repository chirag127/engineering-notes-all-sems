
### Tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Tone is an important aspect of oral communication and voice dynamics. It is the emotional quality of a speaker's voice, which can be used to convey meaning and emotion.
- Tone can be used to create a sense of urgency, excitement, or authority. It can also be used to express empathy and understanding.
- Tone can be affected by the speaker's physical and emotional state, as well as the context of the conversation.
- The appropriate tone for a given situation will depend on the speaker's purpose, the audience, and the subject matter.
- It is important to be aware of the tone of a conversation and to adjust it accordingly.
- Voice dynamics, such as volume, pitch, and rate of speech, can also affect the tone of a conversation.
- It is important to be aware of the impact of these dynamics on the tone of a conversation and to adjust them accordingly.
