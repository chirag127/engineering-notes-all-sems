
### Impersonal for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Impersonal language is a type of language used in technical documents and presentations that avoids the use of personal pronouns such as “I”, “you” and “we”.
2. This type of language is used to create a more objective and professional tone.
3. It can also help to create a sense of authority and expertise when presenting technical information.
4. Impersonal language is often used in combination with other techniques such as active voice and parallel structure.
5. It is important to remember that the use of personal pronouns is not always wrong and can be used to create a more engaging and conversational tone.
6. When using impersonal language, it is important to be aware of the context and audience to ensure that the tone is appropriate.
