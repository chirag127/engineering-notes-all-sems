
### Clarity for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of conveying information through the use of documents, presentations, and other forms of communication.
2. Technical communication can be used for a variety of purposes, including instruction, explanation, persuasion, and collaboration.
3. Clarity is essential for effective technical communication. Clarity can be achieved through the use of appropriate language, visuals, structure, and formatting.
4. The use of active voice, simple language, and short sentences can help to make a document or presentation more clear and understandable.
5. Visuals, such as diagrams, tables, and charts, can be used to convey complex information in a concise and clear manner.
6. The structure of a document or presentation should be logical and easy to follow.
7. Formatting, such as font size, spacing, and alignment, should be used to make a document or presentation more readable.
8. Technical communication should be tailored to the audience in order to ensure that the message is understood.
