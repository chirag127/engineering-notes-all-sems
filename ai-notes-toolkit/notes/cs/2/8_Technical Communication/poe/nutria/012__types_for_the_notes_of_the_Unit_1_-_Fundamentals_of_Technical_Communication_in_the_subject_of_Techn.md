
### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication
1. Technical communication is the process of exchanging information between two or more people through the use of written, spoken, or visual media. 
2. It includes a wide range of topics, including technical writing, technical editing, technical illustration, technical design, and user experience design. 
3. Technical communication is used to convey complex information in a clear and concise manner. 
4. It is important to consider the audience when communicating technical information, as different audiences may have different levels of understanding and knowledge. 
5. Technical communication is used in a variety of contexts, including educational, professional, and social settings. 
6. Good technical communication requires an understanding of the subject matter and the ability to communicate effectively with the intended audience. 
7. Technical communication is often used to explain complex concepts, procedures, and processes. 
8. Technical communication can be used to create user manuals, software documentation, product specifications, and other technical documents.
