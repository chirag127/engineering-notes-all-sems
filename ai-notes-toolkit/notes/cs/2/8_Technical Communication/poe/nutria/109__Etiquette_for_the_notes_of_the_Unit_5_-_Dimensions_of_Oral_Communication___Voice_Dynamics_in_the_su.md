
### Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Listen carefully and attentively to others when they are speaking.
2. Speak clearly and distinctly, using appropriate volume, pace, and intonation.
3. Avoid using slang and jargon when speaking.
4. Use polite language and maintain an appropriate level of formality.
5. Avoid interrupting others when they are speaking.
6. Respect the opinions and beliefs of others.
7. Avoid speaking in a condescending tone or using derogatory language.
8. Remain aware of cultural differences and avoid any language or behavior that could be seen as offensive.
9. Be aware of your body language and facial expressions when speaking.
10. Ask questions to ensure that you understand what is being said.
