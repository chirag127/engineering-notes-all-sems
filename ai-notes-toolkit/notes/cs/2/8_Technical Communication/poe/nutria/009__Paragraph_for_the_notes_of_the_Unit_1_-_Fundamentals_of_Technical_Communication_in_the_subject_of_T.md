
### Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is the process of creating and exchanging information between two or more people. It involves the use of written, spoken, and visual communication to convey information.

2. Technical communication is used in a variety of contexts, including research, engineering, business, and education.

3. Technical communication is an important skill for professionals in any field, as it allows them to effectively convey their ideas and share their knowledge.

4. Technical communication is also used to create instruction manuals, product specifications, and other documents that help people understand and use products or services.

5. Technical communication is a form of writing that is precise, clear, and concise. It is important to use proper grammar and punctuation, as well as to avoid using jargon or overly complex language.

6. Technical communication should be tailored to the audience, and should include visuals and examples to help readers understand the material.

7. Technical communication should also be organized in a logical manner, with an introduction, body, and conclusion.

8. Technical communication should also be proofread to ensure accuracy and clarity.
