
### Speaking with a Purpose

1. Oral communication is an essential part of technical communication, as it allows for the exchange of information and ideas in a more direct and efficient manner.

2. Voice dynamics are important in oral communication, as they can help convey the intended message and create a more engaging experience for the listener.

3. When speaking with a purpose, it is important to consider the audience and the context of the conversation.

4. Be clear and concise when speaking, and ensure that the message is understood by the listener.

5. Use appropriate language and tone to ensure that the message is received in the way it was intended.

6. Speak with confidence and conviction to show that you are knowledgeable about the topic.

7. Use pauses and silence to emphasize key points and to allow the listener time to process the information.

8. Make sure to listen actively to the listener and ask questions to ensure that the conversation is productive.

9. Speak in an organized and logical manner, and provide examples and evidence to support your points.

10. End the conversation on a positive note, and thank the listener for their time and attention.
