
### The Flow of Communication

1. Communication is a process of exchanging information between two or more people.
2. Communication involves both verbal and nonverbal messages.
3. Technical communication is the process of transmitting technical information, such as instructions, explanations, procedures, and specifications, to a target audience.
4. Technical communication is often used in the fields of engineering, medicine, and computer science.
5. Technical communication involves the use of specialized language, visuals, and other media to convey technical information.
6. Technical communication is used to explain complex ideas and processes in a way that is easy to understand.
7. Technical communication is an important skill for professionals in any field.
8. Technical communication is used to create documents such as manuals, reports, and presentations. 
9. Technical communication is used to share information with colleagues and customers.
10. Technical communication is used to create instructional materials such as user guides and tutorials.
