
### Technical Communication 

Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication 

1. Technical communication is a type of communication used to convey information about a particular topic or subject. It is often used in fields such as engineering, business, and science. 
2. Technical communication is used to explain complex concepts and processes in a clear and concise manner. It is also used to provide instructions on how to use a product or service. 
3. Technical communication can be written, spoken, or visual. Written technical communication includes documents such as manuals, reports, and instructions. 
4. Spoken technical communication includes presentations, lectures, and interviews. Visual technical communication includes diagrams, charts, and graphs. 
5. Technical communication is important because it helps to ensure that all parties involved understand the same information. It also helps to ensure that instructions are followed correctly. 
6. Technical communication is often used to create a shared understanding between experts and non-experts. It can also be used to create a common language between different parties. 
7. Technical communication is used in a variety of fields, including engineering, business, and science. It is also used in the medical field to communicate information about treatments and procedures. 
8. Technical communication is a key skill for anyone working in a technical field. It is also important for anyone who needs to communicate complex information in a clear and concise manner.
