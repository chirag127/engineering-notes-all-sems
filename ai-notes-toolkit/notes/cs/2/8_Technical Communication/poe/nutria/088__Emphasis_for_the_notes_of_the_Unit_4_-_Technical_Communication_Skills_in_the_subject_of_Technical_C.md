
### Emphasis for the Notes of Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in Technical Communication

1. Emphasis is an important tool for technical communication, as it can help to draw attention to important points and help to create a sense of clarity and structure.
2. Emphasis can be achieved through the use of bold, italic, and underline formatting.
3. It can also be achieved through the use of color, symbols, and other graphical elements.
4. Emphasis should be used sparingly, as too much emphasis can be distracting and make text difficult to read.
5. When using emphasis, it is important to consider the context of the text, as different types of emphasis may be more appropriate for different types of documents.
6. Emphasis should also be used in a consistent manner, as this will help to create a sense of unity and flow throughout the text.
