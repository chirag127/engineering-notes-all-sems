
### Strategic Competence for Technical Communication

1. Understanding the purpose of communication: It is important to understand the purpose of each communication, whether it is to inform, persuade, or simply to build relationships.

2. Knowing the audience: It is important to understand the audience before communicating. This includes understanding their background, knowledge level, and interests.

3. Making effective use of language: Effective use of language includes using the right words, tone, and style to ensure that the message is understood.

4. Developing effective communication strategies: Developing effective communication strategies involves planning and anticipating the needs of the audience.

5. Using appropriate media: Different media can be used to communicate, such as written, spoken, visual, or multimedia. It is important to use the most appropriate media for the audience and the message.

6. Adapting to different situations: It is important to be able to adapt to different situations and audiences, such as when communicating in a professional or informal setting.

7. Evaluating communication: It is important to evaluate communication to ensure that the message was received and understood correctly.
