
### Structure for the Notes of Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication is a form of communication that uses technical language to convey information. It is used to explain complex concepts and processes in a way that is easy to understand.

2. There are four main types of technical communication: instructional, informational, persuasive, and narrative.

3. Instructional communication is used to explain how to do something. It can include step-by-step instructions, diagrams, and other visuals to help explain the process.

4. Informational communication is used to provide factual information. It can include descriptions, facts, and diagrams.

5. Persuasive communication is used to convince the reader to take a specific action. It can include arguments, facts, and research.

6. Narrative communication is used to tell a story. It can include stories, anecdotes, and examples.

7. Technical communication is used in a variety of fields, including engineering, science, and technology. It is also used in business, healthcare, and education.

8. Technical communication is an essential skill for any professional who needs to communicate complex information in a way that is easy to understand.
