
### Synopsis Writing for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Synopsis writing is a form of technical communication used to summarize a written work or document in a concise and succinct manner.
2. It is important to be able to identify the main points of a document or written work in order to effectively summarize it in a synopsis.
3. A synopsis should include the main points of the document or written work, as well as any additional information that may be relevant to the topic.
4. Synopsis writing should be clear, concise, and easy to understand.
5. It is important to keep the synopsis focused on the main points of the document or written work, and to avoid including unnecessary details.
6. The synopsis should be written in a formal, objective tone.
7. A synopsis should be written in a way that is easy to read and understand.
8. A well-written synopsis can be used to quickly convey the key points of a document or written work to a reader.
