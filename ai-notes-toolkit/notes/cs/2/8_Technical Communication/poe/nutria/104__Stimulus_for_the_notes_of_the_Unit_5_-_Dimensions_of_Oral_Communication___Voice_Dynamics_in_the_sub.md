
### Stimulus for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

* Oral communication is the exchange of information between two or more people through spoken language. It is a form of communication that is used to convey messages, ideas, and feelings to others. 
* Voice dynamics refer to the way a speaker’s voice is projected and modulated to create an effect or to emphasize certain words or phrases.
* The use of voice dynamics is an important part of oral communication, as it helps to convey the speaker’s message more effectively.
* Different types of voice dynamics can be used to create different effects, such as emphasis, emphasis on certain words or phrases, and to create an atmosphere of authority or confidence.
* Voice dynamics can also be used to create a sense of intimacy, to create a sense of humor, or to create a sense of seriousness.
* In technical communication, voice dynamics are used to convey the importance of a message and to emphasize the points being made. 
* It is important to use voice dynamics effectively in order to ensure that the message is clear and that the speaker is able to convey the information accurately. 
* Voice dynamics can be used to create a sense of urgency or to emphasize the importance of a message. 
* Additionally, voice dynamics can be used to create a sense of trust or to build rapport with the audience. 
* It is important to remember that voice dynamics are not just about volume or tone, but also about the speaker’s ability to use their voice to convey their message. 
* It is important to practice using voice dynamics in order to become an effective communicator.
