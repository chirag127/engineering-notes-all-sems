
### Style for the Notes of Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

1. Writing for technical communication should be clear, concise, and precise.
2. Technical communication should be organized logically and should use an appropriate style for the audience.
3. Use the active voice when writing for technical communication.
4. Use appropriate technical terminology and jargon.
5. Use illustrations, diagrams, and tables to explain concepts and processes.
6. Use formatting techniques such as bolding, italicizing, and highlighting to emphasize important points.
7. Use headings and subheadings to help the reader navigate the document. 
8. Use appropriate grammar and punctuation to ensure clarity and accuracy.
9. Use appropriate citation and referencing techniques to acknowledge sources.
10. Proofread and edit the document to ensure accuracy and clarity.
