
### Essentials for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the Subject of Technical Communication

1. Understand the purpose of the presentation: It is important to understand why the presentation is being given and what the audience needs to take away from it.

2. Know the audience: Knowing the audience will help the presenter tailor their presentation to their needs.

3. Prepare the presentation: It is important to prepare the presentation in advance, so that the presenter can practice and become familiar with the material.

4. Use visuals: Visuals such as slides, diagrams, and videos can help to reinforce the points the presenter is making.

5. Practice the presentation: Practicing the presentation will help the presenter become comfortable with the material and ensure that they are well-prepared for the presentation.

6. Speak clearly and confidently: It is important to speak clearly and confidently when presenting, so that the audience can understand the points being made.

7. Use effective body language: Body language is an important part of any presentation, and can help to reinforce the points being made.

8. Be prepared to answer questions: It is important to be prepared to answer questions that the audience may have.
