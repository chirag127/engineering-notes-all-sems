

## Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics

1. Verbal communication is the exchange of information through the use of words.

2. It is the most common way of communicating and includes speaking, listening and writing.

3. Voice dynamics is the way in which a speaker's voice changes in terms of volume, pitch, speed and tone.

4. Voice dynamics can be used to emphasize certain words or phrases and convey different emotions.

5. Non-verbal communication is also an important component of oral communication. It includes body language, facial expressions and gestures.

6. Non-verbal communication can be used to convey emotions, feelings and attitudes.

7. In order to be an effective communicator, it is important to be aware of both verbal and non-verbal communication.

8. It is also important to be aware of the cultural differences in communication styles.

9. Effective communication can help to build relationships and foster understanding between people.
