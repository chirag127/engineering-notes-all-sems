
### Humour for the Notes of Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in Technical Communication

1. Humour can be used in technical presentations to provide a lighthearted break from the technical content and keep the audience engaged.
2. Humour should be appropriate for the audience, context, and subject matter.
3. Humour can be used to illustrate a point, provide an example, or make a comparison.
4. It is important to be aware of cultural differences when using humour in a presentation.
5. It is important to practice a presentation before delivering it to ensure the humour is effective.
6. Humour should be used sparingly, as too much can be distracting or off-putting.
7. Humour should not be used to make fun of people or situations.
8. When using humour in a presentation, it is important to be aware of the audience's reactions and adjust accordingly.
