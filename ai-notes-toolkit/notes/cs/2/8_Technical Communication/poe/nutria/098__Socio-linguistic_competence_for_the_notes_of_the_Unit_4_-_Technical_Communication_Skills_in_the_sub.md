
### Socio-linguistic competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

* Socio-linguistic competence is the ability to understand and use language in a variety of social contexts. 
* It includes knowledge of the conventions of language use, such as politeness, formality, and appropriateness. 
* It also includes the ability to recognize and interpret non-verbal communication, such as facial expressions, gestures, and body language.
* Socio-linguistic competence is essential for effective communication in the workplace, as it enables people to understand and effectively use the language of their profession.
* To develop socio-linguistic competence, it is important to be aware of the context of the conversation, including the purpose and the audience. 
* It is also important to use language that is appropriate for the situation, and to be aware of the differences between spoken and written language. 
* Finally, it is important to be aware of cultural differences in language use, and to be sensitive to the differences in language use between different groups of people.
