
### Syllables for the Notes of Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in Technical Communication

1. Syllables are the smallest unit of spoken language. They are the individual sounds that make up words.
2. Syllables are important for effective communication as they help to create rhythm, intonation, and emphasis in speech.
3. Syllables also play a role in the pronunciation of words, as they determine the stress placed on certain syllables in a word.
4. In technical communication, syllables are used to create a clear and precise message.
5. The number of syllables in a word can also be used to determine the length of the word and its meaning.
6. Voice dynamics refers to the way in which a speaker changes the volume, pitch, and speed of their speech in order to emphasize certain points or to convey emotion.
7. Voice dynamics can be used to create a more powerful and engaging message.
8. In technical communication, voice dynamics can be used to emphasize key points, convey urgency, and to add clarity to complex topics.
9. The dimensions of oral communication include the use of non-verbal cues such as body language, facial expressions, and gestures.
10. Non-verbal cues can be used to emphasize points, convey emotion, and to create a more engaging presentation.
