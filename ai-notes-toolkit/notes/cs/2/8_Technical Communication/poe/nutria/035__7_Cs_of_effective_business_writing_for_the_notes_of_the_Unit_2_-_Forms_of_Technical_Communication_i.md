
### 7 Cs of Effective Business Writing

1. **Clarity**: Writing should be clear and concise. Avoid ambiguity and unnecessary words.
2. **Completeness**: Writing should be complete and include all relevant information.
3. **Conciseness**: Writing should be concise and to the point.
4. **Consideration**: Writing should be respectful and considerate of the reader.
5. **Correctness**: Writing should be accurate and free of errors.
6. **Courtesy**: Writing should be polite and courteous.
7. **Coherence**: Writing should be coherent and organized.
