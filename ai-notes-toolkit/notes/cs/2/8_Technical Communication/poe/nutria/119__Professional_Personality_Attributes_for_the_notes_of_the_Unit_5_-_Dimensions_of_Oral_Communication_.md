

### Professional Personality Attributes for the Notes of Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in Technical Communication

1. Professionalism: An individual should demonstrate a level of professionalism in their communication that is appropriate for the environment and situation. This includes using appropriate language, maintaining a respectful attitude, and dressing appropriately.

2. Empathy: It is important to be able to recognize and respond to the emotions of the people you are communicating with. This can be done through active listening and understanding the needs and feelings of the other person.

3. Respect: Respect for the other person is essential in any communication. This includes understanding their point of view, being open to their ideas, and avoiding any behavior that could be considered offensive or rude.

4. Clarity: It is important to be clear and concise in your communication. This includes using language that is easily understood, avoiding jargon, and providing relevant information.

5. Honesty: Honesty is essential in any communication. This includes being truthful and open about your opinions and feelings, as well as avoiding any behavior that could be considered deceptive or manipulative.

6. Adaptability: The ability to adapt to different situations and environments is essential in any communication. This includes being aware of different cultures and customs, being prepared to adjust your communication style, and being open to new ideas.
