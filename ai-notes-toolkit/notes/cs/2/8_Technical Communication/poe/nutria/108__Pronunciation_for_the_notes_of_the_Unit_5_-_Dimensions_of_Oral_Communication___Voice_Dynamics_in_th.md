
### Pronunciation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Pronunciation is one of the most important aspects of oral communication, as it helps to ensure that the speaker’s message is clear and understood.
2. The four main elements of pronunciation are: intonation, stress, rhythm, and articulation.
3. Intonation is the rise and fall of the voice when speaking. It helps to convey emotion and meaning in a sentence.
4. Stress is the emphasis placed on certain syllables or words when speaking. This helps to emphasize important points or ideas.
5. Rhythm is the pattern of syllables and words when speaking. It helps to create a sense of flow and continuity in a sentence.
6. Articulation is the way in which words are pronounced. It helps to ensure that words are understood correctly.
7. Voice dynamics are the changes in volume and pitch that are used to convey meaning.
8. These elements of pronunciation can be used to create a powerful and effective communication.
