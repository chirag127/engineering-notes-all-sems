
### Concreteness for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the Subject of Technical Communication

* Concreteness is an important element of effective technical communication. It is used to ensure that the intended audience understands the message being conveyed.
* Concreteness is achieved by using specific, concrete words and phrases rather than general, abstract terms.
* Examples of concrete words and phrases include nouns, verbs, and adjectives that are specific to the topic being discussed.
* Concreteness helps to clarify the message and make it easier for the audience to understand. It also helps to avoid ambiguity and confusion.
* Additionally, concreteness can help to make the message more engaging and memorable for the audience.
* When writing technical documents, it is important to use concrete language that is appropriate for the intended audience. Doing so will help to ensure that the message is understood and remembered.
