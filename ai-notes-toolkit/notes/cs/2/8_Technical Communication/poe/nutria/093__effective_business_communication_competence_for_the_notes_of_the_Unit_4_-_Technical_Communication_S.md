
### Effective Business Communication Competence

1. Understand the purpose of communication: Communication is a process of exchanging information and ideas between two or more people. It is important to understand the purpose of the communication before beginning.

2. Choose the right medium: Different communication mediums such as email, phone, video conferencing, etc. can be used for different types of communication. Choose the right medium for the situation.

3. Use active listening: Active listening is a skill that involves listening carefully and attentively to what the other person is saying. It involves paying attention to the words, tone of voice, and body language of the speaker.

4. Be aware of cultural differences: Different cultures have different norms and values when it comes to communication. It is important to be aware of these differences and to adjust your communication style accordingly.

5. Use clear and concise language: Effective communication requires the use of clear and concise language. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the other person may not understand.

6. Be aware of non-verbal communication: Non-verbal communication such as body language, facial expressions, and gestures can be just as important as verbal communication. Pay attention to non-verbal cues to ensure effective communication.
