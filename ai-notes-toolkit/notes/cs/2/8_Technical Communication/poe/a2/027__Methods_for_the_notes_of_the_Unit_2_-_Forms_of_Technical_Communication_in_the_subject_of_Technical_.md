### Methods for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Memoranda
- Brief, informal written communication within an organization
- Used for routine announcements, requests, and updates
- Uses a direct, concise style with clear subject lines

2. Letters
- More formal than memoranda
- Used for external communication
- Includes standard letter formatting with address block, salutation, body, and closing
- Tone depends on purpose and audience

3. Reports
- Provide information or present findings
- Often highly structured with sections for methods, results, discussion, and conclusions
- Visuals like charts, graphs, images, and diagrams are commonly included to aid understanding
- Varies in formality based on purpose and audience

4. Proposals
- Written to propose a solution to a problem or a course of action
- Includes statement of the problem, objectives, methods, and budget
- Persuades the reader to accept the proposal
- Tone is professional and convincing

5. Manuals and Instructions
- Provide step-by-step directions to complete a task
- Use simple, clear language with logical organization and progression
- Include visuals such as images, diagrams, and screenshots to clarify concepts
- Reading level is typically lower to increase accessibility
