### Argumentation skills for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. What is Argumentation?
- Presenting reasons/evidence/data to support a claim/position.
- Trying to convince readers of a particular point of view.

2. Why is Argumentation important?
- To persuade readers and convince them of your position.
- To solve problems by evaluating alternatives and choosing best options.
- To participate fully in civic and political discussions and debates.

3. Elements of Argumentation
- Claim: The main point you want to make. Your position.
- Evidence: Facts, data, quotes, statistics, examples to support your claim.
- Warrants: Statements that link your evidence to your claim. Explain how/why evidence proves your claim.
- Backing: Additional explanations/info to strengthen your warrants.
- Rebuttals: Anticipate and address counterarguments to your position. Explain how/why opposing positions are flawed or less compelling than yours.

4. Tips for effective Argumentation
- Be logical and structured. Present a clear position/claim.
- Provide solid evidence to support your claim. Credible sources.
- Explain your reasoning (warrants). How evidence proves your claim.
- Address alternatives/opposing views fairly and objectively.
- Use rhetorical strategies like repetition to reinforce your key points.
- Maintain a respectful tone. Avoid personal attacks.
- Keep your writing concise and focused. Do not introduce new points in conclusion.
