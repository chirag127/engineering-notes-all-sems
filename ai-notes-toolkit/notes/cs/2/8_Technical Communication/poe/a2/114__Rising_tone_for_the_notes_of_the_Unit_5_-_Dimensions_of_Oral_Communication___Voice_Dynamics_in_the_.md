### Rising tone for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Rising tone: The upward movement of voice pitch while speaking. It is used to emphasize a point or show excitement.
2. Uses:
- To emphasize an important point in a sentence. e.g. "The most important thing is to finish the work on time."
- To show excitement or interest. e.g. "That's a great idea!"
- To indicate a question. e.g. "Would you like to join us for dinner?"
3. Benefits:
- Keeps the audience engaged by varying tone. A monotonous tone can be boring or make people tune out.
- Draws attention to significant parts of the speech or discussion. The emphasized words stand out.
- Makes conversations and speeches lively and interesting rather than dull.
4. Things to keep in mind:
- Do not overuse rising tone. Using it for every sentence can make you seem insincere or annoying.
- Pair rising tone with appropriate facial expressions and gestures to be more impactful. Tone alone may not suffice.
- Practice to get the right pitch and timing right for optimal effect.
