### Synopsis writing for the notes of Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Memo: Used for interoffice communication. Brief, informal, minimal formatting.
- Purpose: Share information, make requests, or address issues
- Audience: Internal colleagues or team members
- Tone: Professional but conversational

2. Proposal: Present a solution to a problem to persuade stakeholders to take action.
- Purpose: Argue for undertaking a project or course of action
- Audience: Internal or external stakeholders
- Tone: Persuasive and solutions-focused

3. Report: Objective, factual account of research or an analysis of information.
- Purpose: Inform or analyze
- Audience: Internal or external stakeholders
- Tone: Formal, objective, and informational

4. Email: Primary method of professional communication; concise and to the point.
- Purpose: Quickly share information or request input
- Audience: Internal or external colleagues, clients, management, etc.
- Tone: Professional but adaptable to context and relationship
