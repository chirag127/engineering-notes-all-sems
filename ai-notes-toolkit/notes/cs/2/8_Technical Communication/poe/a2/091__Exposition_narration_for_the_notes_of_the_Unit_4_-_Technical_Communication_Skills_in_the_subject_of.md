### Exposition narration for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication involves communicating technical information to people with a wide range of technical understanding.

2. The key aspects of technical communication are:
- Precision: Using precise and accurate language to convey the intended meaning.
- Clarity: Organizing and presenting information in a clear, logical, and understandable way.
- Conciseness: Being succinct and avoiding unnecessary words or information.
- Objectivity: Presenting information in an unbiased, impartial, and balanced way.

3. The technical communication process involves:
- Analyzing the audience and purpose
- Collecting and organizing information
- Writing the first draft
- Reviewing and revising the document
- Editing and proofreading the final draft
- Distributing the final document to the intended audience

4. The characteristics of effective technical communication are:
- Accuracy: Providing correct and verifiable information.
- Completeness: Including all necessary information.
- Concreteness: Using specific and tangible language.
- Consistency: Following a standard style and structure.
- Accessibility: Considering the audience's background, experience, and abilities.

5. The different types of technical communication are:
- Procedures: Step-by-step instructions to complete a task.
- Reports: Formal accounts of information or events.
- Proposals: Documents to present a project or solution and persuade others.
- Letters and memos: Official correspondence to convey information.
- Presentations: Spoken deliveries to share information and ideas.
- Visual aids: Graphics, images, video, and more to enhance understanding.
