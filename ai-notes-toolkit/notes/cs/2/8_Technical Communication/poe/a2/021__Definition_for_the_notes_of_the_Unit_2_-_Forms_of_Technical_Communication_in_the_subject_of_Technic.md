### Definition for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1.  Memoranda or memos - Used for communicating official information within an organization. They are brief and concise.
2.  Letters - Can be formal or informal. Used for external communication with clients, vendors, etc. Formal letters are official and follow a standard structure.
3.  Reports - Used to communicate information or research findings. Can be short (e.g. incident report) or long (e.g. annual report). Contains sections with relevant data, analysis and recommendations.
4.  Proposals - Used to present an idea or solution to a problem. Aims to persuade the reader to take a particular course of action. Contains sections outlining the issue, objectives, methods, budget, etc.
5.  Manuals and instructions - Provide operational or maintenance information. Organized in a logical sequence with illustrations and screenshots to aid understanding.
6.  Formal essays or position papers - Used to present an in-depth analysis or argument on a topic. Requires a formal tone, logical structure and evidence to support key points.
7.  Presentations - Used to communicate information verbally and visually using slides, handouts or props. Tailored to the audience and occasion. A combination of oral and visual communication.
