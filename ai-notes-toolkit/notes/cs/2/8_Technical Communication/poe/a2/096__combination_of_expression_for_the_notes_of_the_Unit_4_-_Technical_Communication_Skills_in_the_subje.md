### Combination of expression for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

1. Technical writing: Writing about technical subjects using concise and clear language to convey information to specific audiences. Some characteristics are:
- Concise: Omit unnecessary words and be precise.
- Clear: Use simple and straightforward language. Avoid ambiguity and explain complex terms.
- Objective: Focus on facts and information, not opinions. Avoid bias.
- Structured: Organize information logically (chronological, comparative, cause/effect, etc.)
- Task-oriented: Help the reader complete a task or understand a concept.

2. Audience analysis: Identifying the intended readers of a technical document and tailoring the content to their needs, knowledge level, and goals. Questions to ask:
- Who is the audience? What are their characteristics?
- What do they need to know? What are their goals or tasks?
- How much do they already understand about the topic? What is their technical background?
- How will they use the information? What format is most appropriate?

3. Information design: Organizing and formatting content to facilitate understanding and usability for the intended audience. Some tips:
- Prioritize important information.
- Use consistent headings and formatting.
- Break up long sections of text with illustrations, tables, or bullet points.
- Choose appropriate language, tone, and style for the audience.
- Remove unnecessary words and be concise.
- Test usability and make improvements.

4. Editing: Reviewing and revising documents to improve the quality, correctness, clarity, and persuasiveness of the writing. Some tips:
- Check for grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors.
- Ensure consistency and compliance with style guidelines.
- Refine word choice and sentence structure.
- Get feedback from others and incorporate suggestions.
- Review for appropriate tone, clarity, and conciseness.
- Test instructions or procedures to ensure accuracy.
