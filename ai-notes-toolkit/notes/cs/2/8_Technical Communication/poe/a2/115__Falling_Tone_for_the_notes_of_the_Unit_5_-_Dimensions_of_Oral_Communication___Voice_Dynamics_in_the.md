### Falling Tone

- A falling tone is a tone of voice where the pitch decreases over the course of a syllable or phrase.
- It is used to indicate that a statement is factual or definite.
- Falling tones are often heard at the end of declarative sentences or phrases to convey completeness or finality.
- Examples:

The sun rises in the east.
Water freezes at 32 degrees Fahrenheit.
TechComm is an important subject.

- In contrast to a rising tone which suggests uncertainty or a question, a falling tone sounds confident and resolute.
- Falling tones are common in technical communication to convey precision and certainty which is valued in this field.
- Practice using a falling tone at the end of sentences or phrases when stating facts or definitive information.
