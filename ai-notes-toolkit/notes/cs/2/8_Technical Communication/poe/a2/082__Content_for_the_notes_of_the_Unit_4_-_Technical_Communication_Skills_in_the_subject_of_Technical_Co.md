### Content for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical communication involves the process of sharing technical information between experts and non-experts. It helps in increasing knowledge and understanding among readers.
2. The basic principles of technical communication are:
- Clarity: The language should be simple, clear and concise. Avoid ambiguity and complexity.
- Completeness: The information should be accurate, adequate and complete. Nothing should be left unclear or incomplete.
- Conciseness: Remove unnecessary words and be brief while communicating the message.
- Courtesy: Maintain a professional tone and be respectful towards the readers.
3. The key skills required for technical communication are:
- Writing: Ability to write in a structured manner while following the standard writing conventions.
- Designing: Ability to design documents, presentations, infographics etc. for easy comprehension.
- Editing: Reviewing and revising content for any errors or scope of improvement.
- Non-verbal communication: Appropriate use of highlighting, formatting and visuals to engage readers.

4. The commonly used formats for technical communication are:
- Memos: Short informal messages distributed within an organization.
- Proposals: Documents to propose an idea or plan and convince the reader.
- Reports: Detailed documents presenting analysis or results of a study or project.
- Procedures: Step-by-step guides to complete a process or task.
- Presentations: Audio-visual material to deliver a message to an audience.

5. The key characteristics of a good technical document are:
- Clear purpose and objective
- Defined audience
- Logical organization
- Concise and consistent language
- Usage of appropriate format and visuals
- Accurate information
- Easy comprehensibility
