### Vowel sounds for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Monophthongs: Vowel sounds produced with a single tongue position. Eg: /i/, /e/, /a/, /o/, /u/.
2. Diphthongs: Vowel sounds produced with a change in tongue position. Eg: /ai/, /au/, /oi/, /ou/.
3. Triphthongs: Vowel sounds produced with two changes in tongue position. Eg: /iau/, /iou/.
4. Neutral vowel: Produced with the tongue in a neutral position. Eg: /ə/.
5. R-colored vowels: Formed with the lips rounded and /r/ sound after the vowel. Eg: /ɜr/, /ɔr/.
6. Nasal vowels: Produced with the soft palate lowered and air flowing through the nose. Eg: /æŋ/, /ɪŋ/, /ɔŋ/.
