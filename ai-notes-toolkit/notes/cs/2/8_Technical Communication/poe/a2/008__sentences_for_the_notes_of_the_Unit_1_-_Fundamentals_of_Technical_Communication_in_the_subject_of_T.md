### What is Technical Communication?

Technical communication is the process of sharing information about technical or specialized topics. The goal is to convey information clearly and concisely to the intended audience. This is achieved through the use of appropriate writing style, organization, and formatting.

### Key Components of Technical Communication

-   **Purpose:** Communicating to inform, instruct, or persuade the audience. Determine the goal of the communication and write to achieve that goal.
-   **Audience:** Identifying the intended audience and their existing knowledge level to tailor the content accordingly. Consider factors like job function, technical background, etc.
-   **Message:** Focusing on key messages and information that needs to be conveyed. Remove unnecessary details and structure the content logically.
-   **Medium:** Choosing the appropriate medium (documentation, video, presentation, etc.) based on the audience and purpose. The medium impacts the style and structure of the communication.
-   **Style:** Using a straightforward writing style with clear language and an active voice. Organize ideas logically and format the content for easy understanding.
-   **Process:** Following a standard process to plan, write, design, and deliver the communication. This includes researching the topic, outlining the content, writing a draft, reviewing, and publishing the final work.

### Attributes of Effective Technical Communication

-   **Clarity:** Using simple and clear language to convey the message. Avoid unnecessary complexity and ambiguity.
-   **Conciseness:** Being precise and keeping the content as brief as possible while still achieving the goal. Remove unnecessary words and repetition.
-   **Correctness:** Ensuring the technical content is factually accurate and verifying facts, figures, and technical details. Using proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
-   **Objectiveness:** Remaining impartial and unbiased. Avoid opinions and subjective language. Focus on facts.
-   **Completeness:** Including all necessary information and details to satisfy the purpose and meet the audience's needs. Avoid missing important details or leaving out key steps or information.
