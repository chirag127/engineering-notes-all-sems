### Encoding process for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication:

1. Oral communication:
- Oral communication refers to the process of expressing ideas verbally.
- It involves the following dimensions:
    - Loudness: Refers to the intensity or volume of the voice. A soft or loud voice can be used depending on the context.
    - Rate: Refers to the speed of speaking. A slow or fast rate can be used depending on the context.
    - Pitch: Refers to the highness or lowness of the voice. Variations in pitch can be used to emphasize important points or to keep the audience engaged.
    - Pauses: Include both filled and unfilled pauses. They are used to provide the audience time to absorb information and to maintain the speaker's poise.

2. Voice dynamics:
- Refers to the variations in the voice that provides emphasis and engages the audience.
- Includes the use of modulation, inflection, and accent.
- Modulation: Variation in pitch, rate, pause, and loudness. Used to provide emphasis and maintain audience interest.
- Inflection: Change in pitch or tone of the voice. Used to convey emotions or emphasis.
- Accent: Stress applied to a syllable or word. Used to highlight important words or points.

3. Applications:
- Oral communication skills and voice dynamics are essential in technical communication for:
    - Delivering presentations
    - Participating in meetings
    - Providing instructions verbally
    - Resolving issues with clients or team members via calls
