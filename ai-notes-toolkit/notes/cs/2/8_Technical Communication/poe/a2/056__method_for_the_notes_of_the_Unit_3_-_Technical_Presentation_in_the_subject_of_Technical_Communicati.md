### Method for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Prepare:
- Understand the topic and scope of the presentation.
- Identify the objectives and key messages to convey.
- Determine the appropriate structure for the presentation.
- Gather relevant information, data, examples, statistics, quotes, stories, etc. to support the key messages.

2. Organize:
- Group related information and structure it logically to support the key messages.
- Determine a clear introduction, body, and conclusion for the presentation.
- Arrange the information in a straightforward flow that is easy to follow.

3. Design:
- Choose an appropriate theme or layout for slides that enhances the presentation content.
- Use a clean, uncluttered slide design with strong headings and limited text.
- Include visuals to illustrate key points, such as photos, graphs, charts, diagrams, etc.
- Ensure color combinations provide adequate contrast and are suitable for the presentation environment.

4. Practice:
- Practice the presentation out loud, ideally in front of others.
- Note the timing for each slide and section to ensure the presentation does not exceed the time limit.
- Anticipate questions and areas that may require more explanation. Practice responses to expected questions.
- Make adjustments to the content and delivery as required based on practice runs.

5. Present:
- Arrive early and ensure any required equipment is set up properly.
- Greet the audience and speak with confidence and enthusiasm.
- Pause periodically for effect and to give the audience time to absorb the information.
- Highlight key messages and end with a strong summary of the main points.
