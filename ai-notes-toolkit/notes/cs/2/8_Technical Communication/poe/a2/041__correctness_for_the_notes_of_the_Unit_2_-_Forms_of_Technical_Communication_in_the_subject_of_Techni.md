### Correctness for the notes of Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Ensure accuracy of information. Double check facts and figures.
2. Use appropriate terminology. Choose words that are precise and accurate.
3. Ensure consistency. Use consistent style for headings, formatting, punctuation, etc.
4. Check for any grammatical, spelling or punctuation errors. Proofread thoroughly.
5. Ensure clarity. Avoid ambiguity and be coherent in your writing style and structure.
6. Credible sources. Cite credible references/sources for any data or information used.
7. Simplicity. Use simple and easy to understand language. Avoid complex words or convoluted sentences.
8. Objectivity. Write in an objective tone by avoiding bias or opinions. Focus on facts.
9. Completeness. Include all necessary information and avoid missing out on key points.

The above points highlight the key things to keep in mind to ensure correctness in technical notes or documentation. Precision, accuracy and consistency are crucial for technical communication to be effective.
