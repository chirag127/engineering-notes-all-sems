### Etiquette for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Be concise and precise. Avoid unnecessary elaboration.
2. Use simple and easy to understand language. Avoid complex terms and jargon.
3. Organize the content in a logical flow and structure. Use headers and bullet points for easier navigation.
4. Highlight key terms, main points and important concepts. This aids in quick revision and reference.
5. Maintain a formal tone as these are study notes. Avoid casual language, contractions and emojis.
6. Cite references and sources for any information that is not your original idea or work. This establishes credibility and authenticity.
7. Keep the notes objective and neutral. Avoid personal opinions and subjective remarks. Focus on facts and concepts.
8. Use active voice as much as possible. This makes the notes more engaging and easier to read and understand.
9. Limit the use of external links. Only include them if required for further reading or reference. The notes should be self-contained as much as possible.
10. Follow the recommended style guide for formatting, citations, punctuation, etc. This ensures consistency and professionalism.
