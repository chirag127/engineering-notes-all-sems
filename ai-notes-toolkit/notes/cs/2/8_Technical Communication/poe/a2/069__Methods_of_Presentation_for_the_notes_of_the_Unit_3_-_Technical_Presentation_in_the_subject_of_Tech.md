### Methods of Presentation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Oral Presentation
- Delivering a talk in front of an audience
- Uses verbal communication with or without visual aids
- Presenter establishes a two-way communication with the audience
- Useful for demonstrating live applications or prototypes

2. Visual Presentations
- Uses visual aids to communicate the topic
- Includes slide presentations, handouts, posters, videos, animations, flip charts, etc.
- Helps keep the audience engaged while presenting a lot of information
- Useful for step-by-step processes or when physical products/samples cannot be showcased

3. Written Presentations
- Information is written and presented in hard copy via reports, documentation, instructions, etc.
- Provides a concrete document for the audience to refer to for details or at a later time
- Useful when presenting intricate information or for complex technical processes

The methods of presentations selected would depend on the topic, audience, location, time available, objectives, etc. A combination of methods can also be used for effective communication. The key is to choose a method that helps achieve the goals of the presentation in a clear and engaging manner.
