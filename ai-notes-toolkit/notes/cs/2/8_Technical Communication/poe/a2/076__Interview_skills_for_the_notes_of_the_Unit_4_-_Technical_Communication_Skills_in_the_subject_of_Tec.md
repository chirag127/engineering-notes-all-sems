### Interview skills

1.  Prepare thoroughly by researching the company and role
2.  Practice your answers to common interview questions
    - Tell me about yourself
    - Why are you interested in this role?
    - Why should we hire you?
    - What are your strengths?
    - Why are you leaving your current job?
3.  Present yourself professionally
    - Dress appropriately
    - Arrive on time
    - Bring copies of your resume, a notepad and pen
    - Make eye contact, smile and maintain an enthusiastic demeanor
4.  Highlight relevant experience and skills
    - Provide specific examples to illustrate your points
    - Show how you are a great fit for the role and the company culture
5.  Ask good questions
    - About the role, team, company, challenges, etc.
    - Shows your interest and enthusiasm
    - Gives you more insight into if the role and company are right for you

Interview preparation and execution are key skills to demonstrate your value as a candidate. With solid self-knowledge and practice discussing your experience, you can feel more confident in your next interview opportunity. Staying professional, engaged and enthusiastic will help you leave a great final impression. Good luck!
