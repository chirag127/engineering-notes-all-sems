### Lateral or Horizontal Communication

- Lateral or horizontal communication occurs between employees or team members at the same level in an organization.
- It is communication between colleagues or peers.
- It is important for coordination, collaboration, and integration between teams or departments.
- It fosters innovation and problem-solving by enabling the free flow of information across teams or departments.
- However, it can lead to gossip or politics and reduce productivity if not properly monitored or controlled.
- Effective lateral communication requires a culture of openness, transparency, and trust between teams and colleagues.
- Managers should encourage lateral communication and put processes in place to enable and monitor it.
