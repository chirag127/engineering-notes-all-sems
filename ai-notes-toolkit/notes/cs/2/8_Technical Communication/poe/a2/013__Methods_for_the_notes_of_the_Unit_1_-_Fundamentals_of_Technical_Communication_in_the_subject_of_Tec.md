### Methods for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Audience Analysis: Analyzing the target audience including their background, knowledge level, age group, etc. to customize the content and language as per the audience. This helps in effective communication.
2. Purpose: Having a clear purpose or objective of the communication. What message has to be conveyed? What is the desired response? Being focused on the purpose leads to an straightforward and efficient communication.
3. Structure: Arranging the content in a logical flow and structure. A well-structured content is easier to comprehend and retains the attention of the audience. Structures like chronological, compare and contrast, cause and effect, etc. can be used based on the purpose.
4. Simplicity: Using simple and easy to understand language, short sentences, and an informal tone as much as possible makes the content accessible and engaging for the audience. Avoid complex words and lengthy paragraphs.
5. Coherence: There must be coherence between ideas and sentences. The flow of thoughts and logic should be consistent throughout the content. Parallel structures and transition words can be utilized to achieve coherence.
6. Conciseness: Being concise while communicating and avoiding unnecessary elaborations saves time and effort of both the sender and receiver. However, being concise should not compromise the clarity of the message.
7. Accuracy: The information or message conveyed should be accurate, correct, and devoid of any errors or misleading information. Appropriate sources and citations can be included for accuracy and credibility.
8. Visuals: Using visuals like images, graphs, charts, videos, etc. enhances the comprehension and engaging quotient of the content. Visuals provide a quick overview of the information and complement the written text.
