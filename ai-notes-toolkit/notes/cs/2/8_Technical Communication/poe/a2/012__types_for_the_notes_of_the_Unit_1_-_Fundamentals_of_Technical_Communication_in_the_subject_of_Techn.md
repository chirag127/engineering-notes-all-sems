### Types of Technical Communication

1. Technical Reports: Detailed written documents containing technical information, data, and analysis on a specific topic. For example, scientific reports, feasibility reports, accident investigation reports, etc.
2. Proposals: Written requests that are designed to persuade the reader to support a particular idea, project, or solution. For example, project proposals, grant proposals, etc.
3. Memos: Short, informal business messages distributed within an organization. Used for a wide variety of purposes like announcements, requests, updates, meeting minutes, etc.
4. Instructions: Step-by-step directions or procedures intended to guide the reader to achieve a specific outcome or goal. For example, software installation instructions, equipment operation guides, task instructions, etc.
5. Specifications: Written descriptions of the essential properties, requirements, and guidelines for a system, device, process, or product. For example, product specifications, interface specifications, etc.
6. Resumes and Cover Letters: Written summaries of one's education, skills, and experience sent with a job application. Cover letters accompany resumes to express interest in a position and highlight relevant qualifications.
