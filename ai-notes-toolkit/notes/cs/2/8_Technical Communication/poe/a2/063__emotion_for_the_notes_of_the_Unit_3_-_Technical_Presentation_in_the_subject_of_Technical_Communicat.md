### Emotion

- Emotions are instinctual reactions to situations and environments. They are complex psychological experiences that involve physiological, behavioral, and cognitive components.
- In technical communication, being aware of the emotional impact of language and design choices can help writers connect with audiences and achieve goals.
- Writers can consider how language, structure, and visual elements may affect audiences emotionally. For example, active voice and simple words can feel engaging and empowering, while stark or high-contrast designs can create a sense of urgency.
- However, emotional appeals should not replace accuracy, evidence, or logic. Effective technical communication balances emotions and facts to achieve clarity and purpose.
- To determine appropriate emotional tones, writers can consider audiences, purposes, contexts, and the nature of the topics or arguments. Subtle, authentic emotional connections tend to be more compelling than exaggerated or insincere emotional language.
