### Stimulus for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Dimensions of Oral Communication
- Content: What is communicated. The actual message - facts, ideas, thoughts, feelings.
- Context: The setting and circumstances in which communication occurs. Includes time, place, and environment.
- Process: How the communication is exchanged. Includes sending and receiving verbal and nonverbal messages.
- Relationship: The nature of the relationship between participants. Includes relative status, roles, and purpose.

2. Voice Dynamics
- Pitch: Highness or lowness of a tone. Variation in pitch helps to emphasize important words or show emotion.
- Volume: Loudness or softness of speech. Appropriate volume is necessary for effective communication. Speaking too loudly or too softly can be ineffective or misunderstood.
- Rate: Speed of speaking. An appropriate rate allows listeners to comprehend the message. Speaking too quickly or too slowly can hinder understanding.
- Pauses: Brief periods of silence. Pauses allow listeners to absorb information and the speaker to organize thoughts or add effect. Inappropriate or excessive pausing can be distracting or confusing.
