### Courtesy for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication

1. Technical manuals - Provide step-by-step instructions to achieve a task, assemble or operate a device or system. They include illustrations, diagrams and images to aid understanding.

2. Proposals - Present a suggested solution to a problem or need. They persuade the reader to adopt the proposed solution. They include detailed explanations of the problem, alternative solutions, costs and benefits.

3. Reports - Provide an informative account of a study, investigation, evaluation or occurrence. They have defined sections for clear communication of data, analysis, discussions and recommendations.

4. Presentations - Share information through verbal commentary and visual aids. They are transient and often accompanied by graphics such as slides, handouts or displays. They are tailored to the audience and occasion.

5. Websites - Provide an interconnected set of web pages with text, images, audio, video and interactive features. They are designed for access via the internet to disseminate information, services, products or resources. Website design considers user experience and accessibility.
