### Essentials for Technical Presentation

1.  Prepare: Do thorough research on the topic. Understand the content and structure the presentation logically. Prepare presentation slides/handouts. Practice the presentation.
2.  Know your audience: Understand the audience profile - their knowledge level, interests, etc. and tailor the presentation accordingly.
3.  Choose an appropriate medium: Decide if you want to use slides, handouts or both. Choose an appropriate design/template and minimize clutter. Use clear language and large font sizes.
4.  Structure logically: Structure the presentation in a logical flow - problem statement, outline of solution, details, examples, call to action, etc. Provide a clear path for the audience.
5.  Share key messages: Highlight the key takeaways and messages you want the audience to remember. Repeat important points and be concise.
6.  Use visual aids effectively: Use slides/handouts effectively to enhance the presentation. Include graphs, charts, images, videos, etc. to make the presentation more engaging and easy to understand.
7.  Be an effective presenter: Speak clearly and with confidence. Make eye contact with the audience. Use hand gestures appropriately to emphasize important points. Pause and vary your tone to keep the audience engaged.
8.  Keep time: Practice the presentation to fit within the specified time limit. If possible, keep some buffer time for questions from the audience.
