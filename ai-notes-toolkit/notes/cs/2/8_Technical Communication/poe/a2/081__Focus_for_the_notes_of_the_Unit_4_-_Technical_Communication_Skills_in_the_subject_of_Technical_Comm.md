Focus for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

### 1. Audience Analysis
- Understanding the audience is critical for effective communication
- Analyzing the audience helps in determining the appropriate tone, medium, style, language, and content for a message
- Factors to consider - Demographics, Culture, Subject knowledge level, etc.

### 2. Adapting Language
- Language should be adapted based on the audience
- Use simple and easy to understand language for novice audiences
- Use technical jargon or formal language for expert audiences
- Avoid complex words and sentence structures if the audience may not understand

### 3. Choosing Appropriate Medium
- Choose a medium that matches the audience and purpose
- Written medium - Emails, Reports, Manuals, etc. suit audiences who would read and refer to the information
- Oral medium - Meetings, Presentations suit audiences who would listen to the information
- Visual medium - Infographics, Videos, etc. suit audiences who would prefer digesting information through visualization

### 4. Structuring Information
- Information should be structured logically to aid understanding
- Use headings and bullet points to break up information into key points
- Arrange points in a sequential or comparative flow
- Ensure coherence and consistency across the structure
