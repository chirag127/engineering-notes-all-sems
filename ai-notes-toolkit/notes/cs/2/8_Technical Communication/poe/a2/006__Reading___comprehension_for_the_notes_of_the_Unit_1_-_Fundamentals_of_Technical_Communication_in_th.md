### Reading & comprehension for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication

1.  Technical communication is a broad field that encompasses any form of communication that exhibits one or more of the following characteristics: Communicating about technical or specialized topics, such as computer applications, medical procedures, or environmental regulations.
2.  Technical communicators research and create information about technical processes or products directed to a targeted audience through various forms of media.
3.  The three major types of technical documents are:
    - Procedural: Describe how to do something (e.g., instructions, procedures, recipes)
    - Descriptive: Describe how something works (e.g., explanations, abstract concepts)
    - Informational: Provide factual information (e.g., reports, proposals, datasheets)
4.  The technical communication process involves:
    - Analyzing the audience and purpose
    - Collecting necessary information
    - Organizing and structuring content
    - Writing the first draft
    - Reviewing and revising the document
    - Editing and proofreading the final draft
5.  The characteristics of effective technical communication are:
    - Clarity: Using straightforward language and a simple style
    - Conciseness: Keeping documents as brief as possible while retaining necessary information
    - Accuracy: Ensuring information is correct, up-to-date, and verified
    - Consistency: Using consistent formatting and tone throughout a document
