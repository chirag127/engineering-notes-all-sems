### Effective Business Communication Competence

* **Clarity**: Be clear and concise in your communication. Avoid ambiguity and convoluted language. Express your thoughts in a simple, straightforward manner using appropriate language.
* ** Conciseness**: Avoid unnecessary elaboration and verbosity. Get to the point quickly and be crisp in your communication. This helps avoid confusion and saves time.
* ** Courteousness**: Be polite, respectful and considerate in your communication. Use phrases like please, thank you, appreciate etc. to show you value the receiver's time and presence.
* ** Correctness**: Check for grammatical, spelling and punctuation errors before communicating. Ensure the facts and details communicated are accurate. This establishes credibility and professionalism.
* ** Completeness**: Provide all necessary information, context, facts, details, reasons etc. to make your communication complete and enable proper understanding and action. Incomplete communication leads to confusion and delays.
* ** Timeliness**: Respond or follow-up in a timely manner. Delayed communication results in frustration, missed deadlines and loss of opportunities. Promptness displays reliability and discipline.

The points cover the key competencies required for effective business communication like clarity, conciseness, courteousness, correctness, completeness and timeliness. Adhering to these competencies will enable professional, efficient and favorable business communication.
