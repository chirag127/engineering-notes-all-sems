### Public Speaking for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Prepare:
- Know your material inside and out. Practice your presentation out loud, ideally in front of a mirror or to friends and family.
- Know your time limit and practice to fit your content into the allotted time.
- Anticipate questions and prepare answers.

2. Connect:
- Start with a story or anecdote to establish a connection with the audience.
- Make eye contact with the audience. Engage with them by asking questions or inviting dialogue.
- Show your passion and enthusiasm for the topic. Let your personality shine through.

3. Structure:
- Organize your content in a clear introduction, body, and conclusion.
- Use headers and bullet points to indicate the structure and flow of information.
- Repeat key themes and points throughout the presentation to reinforce them in the audience's memory.

4. Delivery:
- Speak clearly and with energy and confidence.
- Use hand gestures naturally to emphasize important points.
- Pause occasionally for effect. It gives the audience time to absorb the information and keeps your talk from sounding like a memorized speech.

5. End strong:
- Summarize your main points and overall message.
- End with a call to action if appropriate. Leave the audience with a clear takeaway or something to think about.
- Thank the audience for their time and attention.
