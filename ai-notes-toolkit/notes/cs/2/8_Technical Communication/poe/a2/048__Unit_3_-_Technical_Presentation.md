## Unit 3 - Technical Presentation

1. Prepare:
- Understand the topic and audience
- Determine key points to convey
- Structure presentation and practice
- Ensure tech is working (device, clicker, etc.)

2. Opening:
- Thank organizers and acknowledge audience
- Provide overview of presentation structure
- Express interest/importance of topic

3. Body:
- Stick to key points, don't try to cover too much
- Use visuals (slides, graphics, images) to enhance, not distract
- Describe complex concepts clearly and simply
- Provide concrete examples and statistics to support points
- Highlight key terms and takeaways

4. Close:
- Summarize main points and overall message
- Finish strong and compelling
- Thank audience and note any remaining time for questions

5. Questions:
- Repeat questions before answering for audience
- Keep answers concise and to the point
- Admit if you don't know or will get back to the person
- Don't get defensive if asked a challenging question
