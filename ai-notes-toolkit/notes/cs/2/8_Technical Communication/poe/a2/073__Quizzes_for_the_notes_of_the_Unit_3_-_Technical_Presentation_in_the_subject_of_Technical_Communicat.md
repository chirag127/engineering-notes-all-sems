### Quizzes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

1. What are the key elements of a technical presentation?
- Clearly defined objective or goal
- Logical flow or structure
- Appropriate content (facts, data, examples)
- Visual aids (slides, handouts)
- Confident and clear delivery

2. What are some tips for effective slide design?
- Limit text and use large font sizes (min 24pt)
- Use a clean, simple layout with consistent formatting
- Include visuals (graphs, images, diagrams) to enhance text
- Use a single theme or color scheme throughout
- Ensure good contrast between background and text colors
- Label axes and provide a legend for graphs/charts
- Check alignment, spacing, and proportions

3. How can you prepare to deliver a confident and compelling presentation?
- Know your material inside and out
- Practice your presentation out loud, ideally in front of others
- Start with the structure and main messages before scripting word-for-word
- Include pauses and vary your tone/pace for effect
- Make eye contact with the audience and use confident body language
- Share your passion or enthusiasm for the topic
- Start strong to engage the audience's attention immediately
