### Language as a tool of Communication

1. Language is the primary tool of communication. It is the basis for sharing knowledge and interacting with others.
2. Technical communication relies heavily on language to convey information clearly and effectively. The appropriate use of language is essential to achieving the goals of technical communication.
3. The key aspects of language that are important for technical communication are:
- Vocabulary: Using simple and appropriate words to convey meaning. Avoiding complex or unfamiliar terminology when possible.
- Grammar: Following the rules of grammar to structure sentences and paragraphs in a logical and understandable way.
- Style: Adopting a style appropriate for the audience and purpose. Formal vs informal. Active vs passive voice.
4. In addition to the basic rules of language, technical communication places a strong emphasis on clarity, concision, and precision. The language should be straightforward and to the point, avoiding unnecessary words and complex sentence structures.
5. The constant evolution of language can be challenging for technical communication. New terms, idioms, and styles emerge over time which may or may not be suitable for technical content. Technical writers must remain aware of these changes and their impact on communication.
