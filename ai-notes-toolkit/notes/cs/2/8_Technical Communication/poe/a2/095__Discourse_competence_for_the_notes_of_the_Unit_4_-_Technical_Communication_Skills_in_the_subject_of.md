### Discourse competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Discourse competence refers to the ability to effectively communicate through writing and speech. It involves:
- Knowing the appropriate style, tone, and language to use for the audience and purpose.
- Structuring thoughts in a logical and coherent manner.
- Using transition words/phrases to connect ideas.
- Providing necessary context so others can understand the key messages.
- Avoiding ambiguity or confusion.

2. To develop discourse competence, one should:
- analyze the audience and purpose to determine the appropriate style and tone needed. Tailor language accordingly.
- clearly state the main topic/thesis at the beginning.
- logically sequence arguments/information. Use transition words to link points.
- provide necessary context/background. Explain acronyms/technical terms as needed.
- be concise and avoid redundancies. Choose words carefully to be easily understood.
- get feedback on writing to identify any areas that could be improved.
- read more and analyze pieces of writing to absorb the styles/structures/language.

3. Strong discourse competence allows technical information to be communicated effectively. The writing is clear, concise, and logical. The key messages can be easily identified and understood by the intended audience. This is critical for technical communication.
