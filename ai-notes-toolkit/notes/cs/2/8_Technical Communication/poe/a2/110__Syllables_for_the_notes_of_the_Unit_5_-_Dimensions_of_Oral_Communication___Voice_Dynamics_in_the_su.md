### Syllables for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Voice: The sound produced by humans while speaking. It includes pitch, volume, rate, pauses, etc.
- Pitch: Highness or lowness of a tone. Variation in pitch is used to convey emotions or emphasis.
- Volume: The loudness or softness of the voice. Variation in volume is used to convey emotions or draw attention.
- Rate: The speed of speaking. A steady, clear rate is ideal for effective communication.
- Pauses: Brief stops or silences in speech. Used to provide emphasis or convey effect.

2. Non-verbal communication: Communication without words. Includes facial expressions, hand gestures, body language, etc. It complements or contradicts verbal communication and provides additional context.

3. Presentation skills: Skills required to deliver effective oral presentations. Includes preparation, structuring content, using visual aids, confidence, clarity, etc. Presentations can be informative, persuasive, or motivational.

4. Listening: Active process of receiving, attending to, and understanding auditory information. Barriers to effective listening include distractions, prejudices, etc. Effective listening requires concentration, minimizing distractions, and providing feedback.
