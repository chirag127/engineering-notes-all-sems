### Forms of Technical Communication

- **Memos**: Used for communicating within an organization. They are usually informal and brief. They provide information, requests, updates, or announcements.
- **Reports**: Used to communicate the results of a study or investigation. They are more formal than memos and have a structured format with sections like abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, and conclusions.
- **Proposals**: Used to propose an idea or plan to solve a problem. They persuade the reader to take a specific course of action. They include sections on the problem, objectives, methods, resources, and budget.
- **Manuals and Instructions**: Provide step-by-step guidance on how to complete a task or use a system or product. They use simple and clear language with visuals to aid understanding.
- **Presentations**: Can be oral or visual displays of information to inform, instruct, or persuade an audience. They use rhetorical techniques to engage the audience and audiovisual materials to enhance the message.
- **Technical Descriptions**: Explain a topic using a systematic approach to describe the purpose, functions, specifications, operation, or procedure. They avoid ambiguity and allow the reader to completely understand the topic.
