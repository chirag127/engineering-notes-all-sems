- Explain the Unit 3 is about Technical Presentation. Discuss how technical presentation is a vital skill for technical communicators to convey complex information to the audience.

2. Elements of Technical Presentation
- Slides: Simple layout, limited text, visuals to aid explanation, consistent theme
- Speaking: Clear pronunciation, varied tone, pace as per content
- Content: Logical flow, complexity increased gradually, key terms explained, ample examples
- Handouts: Summarize slides, space for notes

3. Preparing the Presentation
- Understand the audience and purpose
- Select appropriate medium (slides, flipchart, handouts)
- Develop outline with proper flow and sequence
- Include appropriate visual aids to enhance understanding
- Practice and time oneself
- Ensure necessary equipment is available

4. Delivering the Presentation
- Greet audience, introduce self and topic
- Maintain eye contact, smile and confident body language
- Explain slides and connect them into a story
- Highlight key terms and important information
- Keep pace steady and pause after important points
- End strongly and thank the audience

5. Evaluating and Improving
- Take feedback from audience and observe reactions
- Review recording to identify strengths and areas of improvement
- Refine content, design and delivery for future presentations
