### Technical Proposal for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication.

1.  Memorandums - Used for routine communications within an organization. They are usually informal, brief, and direct.
2.  Letters - Can be formal or informal. Used for external communications. Formal letters are more structured with defined sections like letterhead, salutation, body, and closing.
3.  Reports - Detailed documents presenting data, analysis, conclusions, and recommendations on a specific topic. They have a standard structure including an executive summary, introduction, methods, results, discussion, and conclusion section.
4.  Proposals - Documents proposing a solution to a problem or an action to be taken. They should clearly state the problem, objectives, methods, and expected outcomes. Need to convince the reader of the benefits of the proposed solution.
5.  Manuals and Instructions - Provide a step-by-step guide to complete a task. They use simple and clear language with graphical illustrations as needed. The steps should be straightforward and easy to follow.
6.  Presentations - Verbal communication supported by visual aids to engage an audience. The content, design, and delivery of the presentation should be tailored to the audience and goals. They can include slides, handouts, and demonstrations.

The content is written in a formal tone with points presented in a straightforward manner for learning and examination preparation. No feelings, friendliness, emojis, or external links are included as specified. The Markdown format is used for the formatting.
