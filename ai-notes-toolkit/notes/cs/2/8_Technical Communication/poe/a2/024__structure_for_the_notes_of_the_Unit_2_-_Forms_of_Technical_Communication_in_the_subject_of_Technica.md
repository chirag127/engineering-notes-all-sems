### Forms of Technical Communication

1.  Reports - Reports are a common form of technical communication. They are formal documents that convey information or relate findings. Reports may include recommendations or solutions to problems.
2.  Proposals - Proposals recommend a course of action to solve a problem or take advantage of an opportunity. They persuade the reader to accept a point of view or take a specific action.
3.  Manuals - Manuals provide instructions for how to do something or how something works. They contain step-by-step directions for procedures such as assembling a product, performing a task, or using a system.
4.  Technical Papers - Technical papers share new research, experiments, or theoretical work in a technical field. They inform and persuade readers about discoveries, methods, or technologies. They must include a high level of technical detail.
5.  Presentations - Presentations are live, visual displays of information, usually accompanied by a spoken commentary. They are a common way to convey technical information to both expert and non-expert audiences. Presentations may include visual aids such as slides, handouts, or props.
6.  Websites - Websites are an important medium for technical communication. They provide a space to publish a wide range of technical information, including reports, manuals, proposals, papers, and more. Effective technical websites are designed to be easy to use and navigate.
