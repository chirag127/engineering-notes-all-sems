### Distinction between General and Technical Communication

1. Purpose: General communication aims to inform, entertain or persuade. Technical communication aims to inform and instruct.
2. Audience: General communication targets a broad audience. Technical communication targets a specific audience with a particular background and knowledge.
3. Style: General communication uses an informal writing style. Technical communication uses a formal, concise and straightforward writing style.
4. Complexity: General communication deals with simple and everyday concepts and topics. Technical communication deals with specialized and complex concepts and topics.
5. Format: There are no set rules for the format of general communication. Technical communication follows a standard format with headings and lists to aid comprehension.

The above points highlight some of the key distinctions between general and technical communication. Technical communication is a specialized form of writing that is structured, concise and focused on informing or instructing a particular audience about technical concepts or topics.
