### Cohesion for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills

1. Cohesion refers to the continuity and flow of ideas in a piece of writing. It is what ties a text together and gives it meaning.

2. Types of Cohesion:

- Reference: Using pronouns, synonyms, antonyms, etc. to refer to something mentioned earlier in the text. e.g. "The new machine is faster than the old one."
- Substitution: Using words like 'one' or 'it' to substitute a word mentioned earlier. e.g. "John bought a car. It is red in color."
- Ellipsis: Leaving out words that can be understood from the context. e.g. "He went to the store; she went home."
- Conjunction: Using conjunctions like 'and', 'but', 'or', 'so' to join words, phrases or clauses. e.g. "I like tea and coffee."

3. Ways to achieve cohesion:

- Use reference words like pronouns or synonyms to refer back to key terms and ideas
- Use conjunctions and transition words to tie sentences and paragraphs together
- Use repetition of key terms and phrases throughout the text
- Ensure ideas flow in a logical order
- Remove any ambiguities or inconsistencies

4. Importance of Cohesion:

- It makes the text easier to understand and follow
- It gives coherence and unity to the writing
- It makes the writing more persuasive and impactful
- It allows the reader to grasp the relationships between ideas
- It is a key component of effective technical communication
