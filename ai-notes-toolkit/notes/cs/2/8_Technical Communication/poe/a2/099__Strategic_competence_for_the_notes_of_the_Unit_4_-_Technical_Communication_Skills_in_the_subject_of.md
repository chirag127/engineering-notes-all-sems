### Strategic competence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technical Communication Skills in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Identify the audience and purpose:
- Analyze the target audience and the purpose of the communication.
- Understand the background and skills of the audience.
- Determine what information the audience needs and expects.

2. Determine the appropriate medium:
- Choose the appropriate communication channel or medium based on the audience and purpose.
- Select options like verbal, written, visual, etc. as appropriate.
- Consider constraints and accessibility of the medium for the audience.

3. Organize content effectively:
- Structure the content in a logical and coherent flow.
- Use headings and visuals to differentiate key points.
- Sequence information logically by following the typical pattern of introduction, body, and conclusion.

4. Tailor the style and tone:
- Determine the appropriate style and tone that suits the audience and purpose.
- Vary language, approach, and level of detail as needed for the audience.
- Ensure the tone is objective, positive, and respectful.

5. Ensure quality and correctness:
- Check for accuracy and correctness of information.
- Get necessary approvals and feedback.
- Proofread thoroughly to fix any errors or inconsistencies in content, structure, or format.

6. Manage time and resources:
- Create a schedule or timeline for the communication process.
- Allocate necessary resources and monitor progress.
- Be flexible to accommodate any changes or constraints.

This covers the key strategic competencies required to effectively communicate technical information to the appropriate audience. Applying these strategies will enable professional and impactful technical communication.
