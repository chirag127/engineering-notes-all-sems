### Expert Technical Lecture for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Memo: A memo is a brief written message distributed within an organization. It is used for internal communication to convey information, request action or make announcements. Memos are less formal than letters and are typically brief, clear and concise. They include a header with 'To', 'From', 'Date' and 'Subject'.

2. Proposal: A proposal is a formal document that presents an idea and persuades the reader to accept it. It details the plan of action and explains the expected outcomes and benefits. Proposals are used to get approval or funding for a project or idea. They should be clear, specific, logical and persuasive. The structure usually includes an introduction, statement of the problem, objectives, methods, project schedule and budget.

3. Report: A report is a structured document that presents information based on a specific topic or purpose. Reports are used to convey the results, findings or recommendations from an experiment, research or analysis. They should be clear, concise and objective. The structure may include an abstract, introduction, methodology, results, discussion, conclusion and recommendations. Different types of reports include business reports, technical reports, investigative reports and laboratory reports.

4. Email: Email or electronic mail is a method of exchanging messages between people using electronic devices. Emails are an informal means of communication and the tone can be casual but should still be polite and professional in a work scenario. Email messages include a subject line to indicate the topic or purpose and the content is presented in the message body. They are useful for quick communication but may not be the best medium for complex topics or ideas.
