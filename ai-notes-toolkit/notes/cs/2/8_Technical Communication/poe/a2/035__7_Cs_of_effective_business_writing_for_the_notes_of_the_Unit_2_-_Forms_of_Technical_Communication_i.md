### 7 Cs of effective business writing

- Clarity: Be clear and concise. Avoid unnecessary words and complex sentence structures.
- Conciseness: Keep sentences and paragraphs short and to the point. Remove redundancies and unnecessary elaborations.
- Courtesy: Use a respectful tone and avoid being rude or aggressive.
- Completeness: Include all necessary information and sufficient context for the reader to understand the message.
- Concreteness: Use specific examples and tangible language instead of vague words and generalizations.
- Correctness: Ensure there are no grammatical, spelling or punctuation errors.
- Consistency: Use a consistent style and tone throughout the document. Maintain parallelism in lists and headings.

The above points cover key aspects to keep in mind for effective business writing. Writing in a formal tone, being concise while maintaining clarity and completeness, using concrete language and ensuring correctness can make business communications more impactful and professional.
