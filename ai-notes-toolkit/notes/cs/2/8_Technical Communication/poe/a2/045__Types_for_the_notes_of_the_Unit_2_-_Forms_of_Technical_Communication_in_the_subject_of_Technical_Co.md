### Types for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1.  Memoranda or Memos: Used for communicating within an organization. They are short informal letters used for conveying information or requests. They follow a standard structure with headings like To, From, Date, Subject, and Body.
2.  Letters: Used for external communication with people outside an organization. They are formal and follow a standard structure. Different types of letters are inquiry letters, claim letters, complaint letters, etc. They should be polite, concise, and to the point.
3.  Reports: Used to convey specific information or the results of an analysis to others. They follow a standard structure with sections like Terms of Reference, Procedure, Findings, Conclusions, and Recommendations. The content is objective and evidence-based. Different types are laboratory reports, business reports, investigative reports, etc.
4.  Proposals: Used to suggest an idea or course of action to someone. They follow a standard structure with sections like Introduction, Methods, Budget, Timeline, and Conclusion. The content should be persuasive and address the reader's potential concerns or objections.
5.  Resumes or CVs: Used to apply for a job opportunity. A resume is brief (1-2 pages) and focused on relevant work experience, skills, and accomplishments. A CV is more comprehensive (2+ pages) and includes additional details like education, publications, awards, conferences attended, etc. They should be customized for each position applied to.
