### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamentals of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

1. Technical Communication: Technical Communication is a broad field that focuses on the study and practice of communicating in technical fields or contexts. Technical communicators ensure that information about technical concepts or products is conveyed clearly and effectively to users.
2. Types of Technical Documentation: The various types of technical documentation include:
- User manuals: Guide users through operating a product.
- Proposals: Present ideas or solutions to problems.
- Reports: Share research findings or evaluations.
- Specifications: Provide detailed overviews of product attributes and requirements.
- Tutorials: Explain how to perform tasks by giving step-by-step instructions.
3. Attributes of Effective Technical Communication: Some key attributes of effective technical communication are:
- Clarity: Information is presented in a clear, concise, and straightforward manner.
- Accuracy: Information is correct, verifiable, and not misleading.
- Consistency: Use of consistent terminology and formatting.
- Organization: Information is structured in a logical and predictable order.
- Simplicity: Complex information is presented accessibly.
- Courtesy: Considerate of users and their contexts.
