### Etiquette for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the Subject of Technical Communication

Here are some important points to keep in mind when taking notes for the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication:

- Be attentive during the lectures and take notes on the key points that the instructor emphasizes.
- Use shorthand or abbreviations to save time and space, but make sure that you can understand them later.
- Organize your notes in a clear and logical manner. Use headings, bullet points, and numbered lists to make it easier to review and study later.
- Write legibly, so that you can read your notes later.
- Use symbols or highlighters to mark important information that you want to remember or refer back to later.
- Don't try to write down every word that the instructor says. Instead, focus on the main ideas and concepts.
- Review and revise your notes after each lecture to fill in any gaps and ensure that you have a complete understanding of the material.
- If you miss a lecture, try to get notes from a classmate or the instructor to ensure that you don't miss any important information.
- Respect the intellectual property of the instructor and your classmates. Don't share or distribute notes without their permission.
- Be attentive in group discussions and take notes on the key points that are discussed. Make sure to contribute your own ideas and insights to the discussion.
- When presenting your own ideas or findings, use clear and concise language and make sure to cite any sources that you use.
- Practice good listening skills, such as maintaining eye contact and avoiding distractions, to ensure that you understand the information being presented.
