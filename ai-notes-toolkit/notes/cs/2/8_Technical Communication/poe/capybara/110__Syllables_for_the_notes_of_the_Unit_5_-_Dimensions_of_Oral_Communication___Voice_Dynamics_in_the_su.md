### Syllables for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

- Oral communication is the process of exchanging information between two or more people through spoken words.
- The dimensions of oral communication are the various factors that contribute to effective communication, such as tone, pitch, volume, pace, and clarity.
- Voice dynamics refer to the way in which a person uses their voice to convey meaning and emotion.
- Syllables are the building blocks of spoken language and refer to the individual units of sound that make up words.
- Proper pronunciation of syllables is critical to effective oral communication and can greatly impact the listener's ability to understand and interpret the message being conveyed.
- The International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) is a standardized system of symbols that represent the sounds of all human languages, and can be used to aid in the proper pronunciation of syllables.
- Different languages have different syllabic structures, and it is important to be aware of these differences when communicating across cultures or with non-native speakers of a language.
- In addition to proper pronunciation, effective use of voice dynamics can greatly enhance the overall impact of oral communication, including the ability to convey emotion, establish credibility, and engage the listener.
- Voice dynamics can be manipulated through changes in tone, pitch, volume, pace, and emphasis, and can be used to create a more engaging and memorable communication experience.
- Effective use of voice dynamics requires practice and awareness of one's own speaking habits, as well as the ability to read and respond to the nonverbal cues of the listener.
