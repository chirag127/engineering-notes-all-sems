### Competence for the notes of the Unit 5 - Dimensions of Oral Communication & Voice Dynamics in the subject of Technical Communication

In order to become an effective communicator, it is important to develop competence in oral communication and voice dynamics. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- **Clarity of Speech:** It is important to speak clearly and enunciate words properly in order to be understood by the audience. Practice speaking slowly and pausing between sentences to give the audience time to absorb what you are saying.

- **Tone of Voice:** The tone of voice can convey a lot of information about the speaker's emotions and intentions. Use the appropriate tone of voice for the message you are trying to convey, whether it is serious, humorous, or informative.

- **Volume Control:** Adjust the volume of your voice to suit the size of the audience and the acoustics of the room. Speak louder when addressing a large audience, but be careful not to shout, as this can be off-putting.

- **Pitch Control:** Varying the pitch of your voice can add interest and emphasis to your message. Use a higher pitch for excitement or emphasis, and a lower pitch for seriousness or authority.

- **Pace of Speech:** The pace of speech should be adjusted to suit the audience and the content of the message. Speak more slowly when explaining complex concepts, and speed up when delivering simple information.

- **Body Language:** Body language can convey a lot of information about the speaker's confidence and engagement with the audience. Use appropriate hand gestures and facial expressions to reinforce the message you are delivering.

- **Eye Contact:** Maintaining eye contact with the audience can help to establish trust and engagement. Make sure to look at different members of the audience rather than focusing on just one person.

By developing competence in these dimensions of oral communication and voice dynamics, you can become a more effective communicator and engage your audience more effectively.
