### Types for the notes of the Unit 2 - Forms of Technical Communication in the subject of Technical Communication

When it comes to technical communication, it is important to take notes that are clear, concise, and easy to understand. This helps in retaining the information and utilizing it in the future. There are several types of notes that can be taken for technical communication. 

Here are some of the most common types of notes:

1. **Outline Notes** - This type of note-taking involves creating a hierarchical structure of the main ideas and sub-ideas. This helps in organizing the information in a logical and easy-to-understand manner. 

2. **Mind Maps** - Mind maps are visual representations of information that are created by connecting ideas and concepts in a non-linear way. This type of note-taking is useful for brainstorming and generating new ideas. 

3. **Cornell Notes** - This type of note-taking involves dividing the page into three sections - a main section for the notes, a section for the key points, and a section for the summary. This helps in summarizing and synthesizing the information. 

4. **Charting Notes** - Charting notes involve creating tables, charts, or diagrams to represent information. This type of note-taking is useful for presenting information in a visual and organized manner. 

5. **Sentence Notes** - This type of note-taking involves summarizing the information in complete sentences. This helps in retaining the information and understanding the context. 

6. **Annotation Notes** - Annotation notes involve highlighting or underlining important information and adding comments or questions in the margins. This helps in engaging with the material and understanding it better. 

It is important to choose a note-taking method that works best for you and to consistently use it throughout the course. With the right type of notes, technical communication can become less intimidating and more manageable.
