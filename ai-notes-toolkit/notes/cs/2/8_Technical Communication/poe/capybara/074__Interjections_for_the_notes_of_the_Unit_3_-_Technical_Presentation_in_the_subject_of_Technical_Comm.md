### Interjections for the notes of the Unit 3 - Technical Presentation in the subject of Technical Communication

- Ahem: Use this interjection to get the attention of the audience before you start speaking. It is a polite way of indicating that you are about to begin your presentation.
- Excuse me: Use this interjection when you need to interrupt someone, for example, to clarify a point or ask a question. It shows that you respect the other person's time and input.
- Wow: Use this interjection to express surprise, admiration or amazement. It can be used to emphasize a point or to add some enthusiasm to your presentation.
- Um: Use this interjection when you need to pause or gather your thoughts. It is a filler word that can help you avoid awkward silences or long pauses.
- Well: Use this interjection to introduce a new point or to transition to the next topic. It can help you structure your presentation and keep your audience engaged.
- Okay: Use this interjection to signal that you have understood a point or to confirm that everyone is on the same page. It can help you check for comprehension and avoid misunderstandings.
- Right: Use this interjection to express agreement or to acknowledge a good point. It can help you build rapport with your audience and show that you are listening to them.
- So: Use this interjection to summarize your main points or to draw a conclusion. It can help you wrap up your presentation and leave a lasting impression on your audience.

Remember to use interjections appropriately and sparingly, as they can become distracting if overused. Use them to enhance your presentation, not to detract from it.
