### Lateral or Horizontal

In technical communication, there are two main types of communication structures: lateral and vertical. In this section, we will discuss lateral or horizontal communication and its importance in technical communication.

Lateral communication refers to the exchange of information between people of the same level or position within an organization. It usually occurs between peers or colleagues who work in different departments or teams. Some of the important points to consider about lateral communication are:

- Lateral communication helps in sharing information between colleagues who work in different departments or teams. This can increase efficiency and productivity in the workplace.
- Lateral communication can also be used to solve problems or make decisions collectively. This approach can lead to better outcomes as it involves different perspectives and ideas.
- Lateral communication can help in building relationships and teamwork among colleagues. This can create a positive work atmosphere and improve employee satisfaction.
- Lateral communication can be formal or informal, depending on the situation. Formal lateral communication involves structured processes and procedures, while informal lateral communication involves casual interactions and conversations.

In summary, lateral or horizontal communication plays an important role in technical communication. It helps in sharing information, solving problems, building relationships, and improving teamwork among colleagues. As a technical communicator, it is important to understand the different types of communication structures and when to use them effectively.
