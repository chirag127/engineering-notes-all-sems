# Statistical Quality Control (SQC)

Statistical Quality Control (SQC) is a set of statistical techniques used to measure and improve the quality of a product or process. It is a part of the larger process of quality control, which involves inspecting and testing products to ensure that they meet the desired standards. SQC is used to monitor and control the production process to prevent defects and improve the quality of the final product.

Here are some key points to note about SQC:

1. SQC involves the use of statistical methods to monitor and control the quality of a product or process.
2. The goal of SQC is to identify and correct problems in the production process before they result in defective products.
3. SQC techniques include control charts, sampling plans, and process capability analysis.
4. Control charts are used to monitor the production process and detect any changes that may indicate a problem.
5. Sampling plans are used to determine the number of units to be inspected and the criteria for accepting or rejecting a batch of products.
6. Process capability analysis is used to determine if the production process is capable of producing products that meet the desired specifications.

SQC is an important tool for ensuring the quality of products and processes. By using statistical methods to monitor and control the production process, manufacturers can identify and correct problems before they result in defective products, improving the overall quality of the final product.
