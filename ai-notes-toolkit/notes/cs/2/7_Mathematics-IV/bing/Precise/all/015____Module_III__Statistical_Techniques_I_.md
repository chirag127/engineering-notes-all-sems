## Module III: Statistical Techniques I:

Statistical techniques are methods used to collect, analyze, and interpret data. These techniques are used in various fields, including social sciences, business, and engineering, to make informed decisions based on data.

Some common statistical techniques include:

1. **Descriptive statistics:** These techniques are used to summarize and describe data. Common measures include mean, median, mode, range, and standard deviation.

2. **Inferential statistics:** These techniques are used to make inferences about a population based on a sample of data. Common methods include hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and regression analysis.

3. **Probability:** Probability is the study of random events. It is used to model uncertainty and to make predictions about future events.

4. **Sampling:** Sampling is the process of selecting a subset of a population for study. It is used to make inferences about the population based on the sample.

5. **Experimental design:** Experimental design is the process of planning an experiment to ensure that the data collected is valid and reliable. It involves selecting the appropriate sample size, controlling for confounding variables, and choosing the appropriate statistical tests.

These are just a few of the many statistical techniques available. The appropriate technique to use depends on the research question, the type of data collected, and the goals of the analysis.
