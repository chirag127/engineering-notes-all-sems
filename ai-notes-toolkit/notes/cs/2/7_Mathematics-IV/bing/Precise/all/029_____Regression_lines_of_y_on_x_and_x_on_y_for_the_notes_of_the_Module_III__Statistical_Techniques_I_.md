# Regression lines of y on x and x on y

Regression analysis is a statistical technique used to model the relationship between two or more variables. In simple linear regression, we model the relationship between two variables, x and y, using a straight line. The line is called the regression line.

There are two types of regression lines: the regression line of y on x and the regression line of x on y.

## Regression line of y on x

The regression line of y on x is used to predict the value of y for a given value of x. It is calculated using the formula:

y = a + bx

where:
- a is the y-intercept of the line
- b is the slope of the line
- x is the independent variable
- y is the dependent variable

The slope, b, is calculated using the formula:

b = r * (Sy / Sx)

where:
- r is the correlation coefficient between x and y
- Sy is the standard deviation of y
- Sx is the standard deviation of x

The y-intercept, a, is calculated using the formula:

a = ȳ - b * x̄

where:
- ȳ is the mean of y
- x̄ is the mean of x

## Regression line of x on y

The regression line of x on y is used to predict the value of x for a given value of y. It is calculated using the formula:

x = a + by

where:
- a is the x-intercept of the line
- b is the slope of the line
- x is the dependent variable
- y is the independent variable

The slope, b, is calculated using the formula:

b = r * (Sx / Sy)

where:
- r is the correlation coefficient between x and y
- Sy is the standard deviation of y
- Sx is the standard deviation of x

The x-intercept, a, is calculated using the formula:

a = x̄ - b * ȳ

where:
- ȳ is the mean of y
- x̄ is the mean of x

In summary, the regression lines of y on x and x on y are used to model the relationship between two variables, x and y, using a straight line. The formulas for calculating the slope and intercept of the lines are different, depending on whether we are predicting y for a given value of x or x for a given value of y. These lines can be used to make predictions and to understand the relationship between the two variables.
