# Module V: Statistical Techniques III:

1. **Regression Analysis:** Regression analysis is a statistical technique used to investigate the relationship between two or more variables. It is used to predict the value of one variable based on the values of other variables.

2. **Analysis of Variance (ANOVA):** ANOVA is a statistical technique used to determine whether there are significant differences between the means of two or more groups. It is commonly used in experiments where the independent variable has more than two levels.

3. **Factor Analysis:** Factor analysis is a statistical technique used to identify underlying factors or dimensions that explain the correlations among a set of variables. It is commonly used in psychology, sociology, and other social sciences to identify latent variables.

4. **Cluster Analysis:** Cluster analysis is a statistical technique used to group similar objects into clusters. It is commonly used in market research, biology, and other fields to identify natural groupings of data.

5. **Discriminant Analysis:** Discriminant analysis is a statistical technique used to classify objects into two or more groups based on their characteristics. It is commonly used in biology, psychology, and other fields to predict group membership.

6. **Multidimensional Scaling:** Multidimensional scaling is a statistical technique used to represent data in a lower-dimensional space. It is commonly used in psychology, sociology, and other social sciences to visualize the relationships among objects.

7. **Structural Equation Modeling:** Structural equation modeling is a statistical technique used to test theoretical models that specify the relationships among variables. It is commonly used in psychology, sociology, and other social sciences to test hypotheses about the relationships among variables.

8. **Time Series Analysis:** Time series analysis is a statistical technique used to analyze data collected over time. It is commonly used in economics, finance, and other fields to identify trends, patterns, and relationships in time series data.

9. **Survival Analysis:** Survival analysis is a statistical technique used to analyze the time until an event occurs. It is commonly used in medicine, biology, and other fields to analyze the time until death, failure, or other events.

10. **Meta-Analysis:** Meta-analysis is a statistical technique used to combine the results of multiple studies. It is commonly used in medicine, psychology, and other fields to synthesize the results of multiple studies on the same topic.
