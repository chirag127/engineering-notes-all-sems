# Moments

Moments are measures of the shape of a probability distribution. They are used in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS, specifically in Module III: Statistical Techniques I. Here are some key points to remember about moments:

1. The nth moment of a random variable X is defined as the expected value of X^n.
2. The first moment is the mean of the distribution.
3. The second moment is the variance of the distribution.
4. The third moment is the skewness of the distribution, which measures the asymmetry of the distribution.
5. The fourth moment is the kurtosis of the distribution, which measures the "peakedness" of the distribution.
6. Moments can be calculated for both discrete and continuous distributions.
7. Moments can be used to describe the shape of a distribution and to compare different distributions.

These are some of the key points to remember about moments when studying Module III: Statistical Techniques I in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS. It is important to understand the concept of moments and how they are used in statistical analysis.
