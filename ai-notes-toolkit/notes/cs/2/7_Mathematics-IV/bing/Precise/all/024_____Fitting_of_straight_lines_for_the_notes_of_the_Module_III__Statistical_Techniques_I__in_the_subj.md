# Fitting of Straight Lines

Fitting of straight lines is a statistical technique used to find the best linear relationship between two variables. This technique is commonly used in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS, specifically in Module III: Statistical Techniques I. Here are some key points to remember when studying this topic:

1. The goal of fitting a straight line is to find the equation of the line that best describes the relationship between the two variables.
2. This is done by minimizing the sum of the squared differences between the observed values and the values predicted by the line.
3. The most common method for fitting a straight line is the method of least squares.
4. The equation of the fitted line can be used to make predictions about one variable based on the values of the other variable.
5. It is important to check the assumptions of the linear model, such as the normality of the residuals and the homoscedasticity of the errors, before using the fitted line for prediction.
