# Properties of Regression Coefficients

Here are some important properties of regression coefficients for the notes of Module III: Statistical Techniques I in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS:

1. The regression coefficients are independent of the change of origin but not of the change of scale.
2. The two regression lines intersect at the point of averages, i.e., at the point (x̄, ȳ).
3. The regression coefficients are the geometric mean of the two correlation coefficients.
4. The regression coefficients are the arithmetic mean of the two regression coefficients.
5. The regression coefficients are the harmonic mean of the two regression coefficients.
