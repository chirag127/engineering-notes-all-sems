### Probability mass function and Probability density function

- A **probability mass function (PMF)** is a function that gives the probability that a **discrete random variable** is exactly equal to some value.
- A **probability density function (PDF)** is a function that gives the probability that a **continuous random variable** falls within some interval.
- The PMF and PDF are different in that the latter is associated with continuous rather than discrete random variables. A PDF must be integrated over an interval to yield a probability, while a PMF assigns a probability to each point in the sample space .
- The value of the random variable having the largest probability mass or density is called the **mode**.
- The PMF and PDF can be used to describe the distribution of a random variable, and to calculate various statistics such as the mean, variance, and standard deviation.
- The PMF and PDF can also be used to model various phenomena in science, engineering, and social sciences, such as coin tossing, dice rolling, heights, weights, grades, etc.
