### Testing a Hypothesis for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. A hypothesis is an educated guess about the relationship between two variables. It is a statement that can be proved true or false through statistical analysis of data.
2. There are two types of hypotheses:
- Null hypothesis (H0): It is a hypothesis of no difference or no effect. It is a statement of no change or no difference in parameters.
- Alternative hypothesis (H1): It is a hypothesis that suggests a difference or an effect. It is a statement that parameters have changed or that there is a difference.
3. Steps to test a hypothesis:
- State the null and alternative hypotheses.
- Select an appropriate statistical test.
- Determine the critical value to use for evaluating the p-value.
- Calculate the p-value.
- Draw a conclusion:
- If p-value < critical value, reject H0.
- If p-value > critical value, fail to reject H0.
4. While drawing inferences, remember:
- A small p-value indicates strong evidence against the null hypothesis.
- A large p-value indicates weak evidence against the null hypothesis.
- The hypotheses can never be proved, they can only be rejected or not rejected.
- The result of a hypothesis test depends on sample size and variability in data.
