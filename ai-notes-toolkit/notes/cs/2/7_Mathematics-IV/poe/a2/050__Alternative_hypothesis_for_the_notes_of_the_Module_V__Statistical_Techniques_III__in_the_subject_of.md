### Alternative hypothesis for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. The alternative hypothesis (H1) is the opposite of the null hypothesis (H0). It is the hypothesis that the researcher hopes to support or expects to find.
2. The alternative hypothesis states that there is a real effect or difference, that the expected results are unlikely to occur just by chance.
3. The alternative hypothesis is often specified as the research hypothesis that the researcher hopes to support. It may contain one of the following types:
- H1: μ ≠ μ0 (mean is not equal to claimed value)
- H1: μ > μ0 (mean is greater than claimed value)
- H1: μ < μ0 (mean is less than claimed value)
4. The alternative hypothesis is accepted if the observed results are unlikely to occur by chance when the null hypothesis is true. This is often determined using a p-value and significance level. If the p-value is less than the significance level, the alternative hypothesis is accepted.
