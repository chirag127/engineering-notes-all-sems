### Linear and Non Linear Partial Equations of first order for the notes of the Module I: Partial Differential Equations in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Linear Partial Differential Equations of First Order
- A partial differential equation of first order is said to be linear if it is expressible in the form P(x,y,u)dx + Q(x,y,u)dy = R(x,y,u) where P, Q and R are functions of x, y and u.
- Solution: Use separation of variables method.

2. Non Linear Partial Differential Equations of First Order
- A partial differential equation of first order is said to be non linear if it is not of the form P(x,y,u)dx + Q(x,y,u)dy = R(x,y,u).
- Solution: Methods like constant of integration, Bernoulli's equation, Exact differential equation, etc. can be used based on the form of the non linear partial differential equation.
