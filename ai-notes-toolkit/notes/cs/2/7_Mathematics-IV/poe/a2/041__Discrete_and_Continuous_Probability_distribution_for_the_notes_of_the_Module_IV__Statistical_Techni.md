### Discrete and Continuous Probability distribution for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

- Discrete Probability Distribution: Probability distribution where the random variable can take on only countable number of outcomes/values. Eg: Binomial distribution, Poisson distribution, Geometric distribution, Hypergeometric distribution.
- Continuous Probability Distribution: Probability distribution where the random variable can take on any value in an interval. Eg: Normal distribution, Student's t-distribution, Chi-square distribution, Exponential distribution, Uniform distribution.
- Probability Density Function (PDF): For continuous random variables, PDF is a function that describes the probability distribution. The area under the curve of PDF equals 1.
- Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF): Defines the probability that the random variable X takes on a value less than or equal to x. For continuous distribution, CDF increases continuously from 0 to 1 as x increases. For discrete distribution, CDF increases in steps of 1.
- Mean: Measure of central tendency. Can be finite or infinite for various distributions.
- Variance and Standard Deviation: Measure of dispersion which describes the spread of the distribution. Variance is the square of standard deviation.
- Parameters: constants that define the shape/properties of the probability distribution. Eg: μ and σ2 for normal distribution.
- Significant properties and applications of various distributions will be discussed in detail.
