### Introduction for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Measures of Dispersion: Range, Variance, Standard Deviation
- Range: Difference between the largest and smallest observation.
- Variance: Average of the squared deviations from the mean.
- Standard Deviation: Square root of the variance. Used to measure dispersion and determine outliers.

2. Correlation and Regression
- Correlation: Measure of linear association between two quantitative variables.
- Regression: Fitting a straight line to the scatter plot of two variables to determine the relationship.
- Coefficient of Determination (R2): Proportion of variation in one variable that can be explained by the other variable.

3. Analysis of Categorical Data
- Chisquare Test: Test of goodness of fit and test of independence for categorical data.
- Contingency Tables: Organized display of categorical data to analyze relationship between two variables.
