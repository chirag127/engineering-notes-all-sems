### Introduction for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

1. Statistics is a branch of mathematics which deals with the collection, organization, analysis, interpretation and presentation of data.
2. The word statistics is derived from the Latin word “status” meaning collection.
3. Statistics aims at providing information and insights on phenomena or issues by studying numerical information and mathematical models.
4. The major objectives of statistics are:
- Collection of data: The first step in any statistical analysis is collection of data. The data may be collected through surveys, experiments or existing records.
- Organization and presentation of data: The collected data is to be organized systematically for analysis. The data is presented in the form of tables, graphs, charts, etc.
- Analysis of data: The organized data is analyzed to find patterns, relationships, trends, etc. using various statistical techniques like descriptive statistics, inference, regression, etc.
- Drawing conclusions: Appropriate conclusions are drawn from the analysis of data with the help of statistical methods. The conclusions are useful in decision making in various fields.
5. The tools and techniques of statistics are widely used in various fields such as science, engineering, economics, psychology, marketing research, etc. to analyze data and draw meaningful insights.
