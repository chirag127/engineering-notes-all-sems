### Fitting of second degree parabola for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. A second degree parabola is fitted to a given set of data points to analyze the relationship between variables and find the equation of the parabolic curve that best fits the data.
2. The general form of a second degree parabola is y = ax2 + bx + c where a ≠ 0.
3. The method of least squares is used to determine the values of a, b and c.
4. The sum of the squares of the deviations of the points from the parabola is made minimum to get the best fit.
5. The normal equation is solved to obtain the values of a, b and c.
6. The fitted parabolic curve can be used to estimate values for new data or make predictions.
7. The closeness of the fit can be checked using the coefficient of determination, R2 which ranges from 0 to 1. A higher value indicates a better fit.
8. The parabolic curve can also be plotted to visually check the goodness of fit and analyze the relationship between the variables.
