### Measures of central tendency for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Mean: Sum of all the values divided by the total number of values. It is calculated using the formula ΣX/N where ΣX is the sum of all values and N is the number of values. It is also known as average.
2. Median: The middlemost value in the data when arranged in ascending or descending order. If there are two middle values, the median is their average.
3. Mode: The most frequently occurring value in the data. The number that appears most often.

The mean, median and mode are three measures of central tendency that describe the distribution of a data set. The mean is the sum of the values divided by the number of values. The median is the middle value when the data is arranged in order. The mode is the most frequently occurring value.
