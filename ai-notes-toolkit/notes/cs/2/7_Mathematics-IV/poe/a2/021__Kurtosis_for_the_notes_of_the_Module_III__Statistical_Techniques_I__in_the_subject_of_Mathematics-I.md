### Kurtosis for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Kurtosis is a measure of the peakedness or flatness of the probability distribution of a real-valued random variable.
2. A high kurtosis distribution has a sharper peak and longer, fatter tails, while a low kurtosis distribution has a more rounded peak and shorter, thinner tails.
3. The kurtosis of any univariate distribution is defined as the fourth moment around the mean divided by the square of the variance.
4. A distribution with a higher kurtosis than the normal distribution has more of its variance due to infrequent extreme deviations, as opposed to frequent modestly sized deviations.
5. The kurtosis of a normal distribution is 3. Distributions with kurtosis greater than 3 are said to be leptokurtic, while those with kurtosis less than 3 are platykurtic.
6. Kurtosis is a measure of outliers, not shape, and is very sensitive to outliers. Adding or removing outliers can significantly change the kurtosis.
7. Some possible distributions to model different levels of kurtosis are: normal distribution (kurtosis = 3), t-distribution, Laplace distribution, etc.
