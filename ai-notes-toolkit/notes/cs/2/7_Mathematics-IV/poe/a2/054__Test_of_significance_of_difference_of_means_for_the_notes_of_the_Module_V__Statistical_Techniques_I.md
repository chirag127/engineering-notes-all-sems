### Test of significance of difference of means for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS.

1. Null hypothesis ($H_0$): The means of the two populations are equal.
$\\mu_1 = \\mu_2$

2. Alternative hypothesis ($H_1$): The means of the two populations are not equal.
$\\mu_1 \\neq \\mu_2$

3. Test statistic: $t = \\frac{\\bar{x}_1 - \\bar{x}_2}{s_p\\sqrt{1/n_1 + 1/n_2}}$
    - $\\bar{x}_1, \\bar{x}_2$ are sample means
    - $s_p$ is the pooled standard deviation
    - $n_1, n_2$ are sample sizes

4. Degree of freedom: $v = n_1 + n_2 - 2$
    - For large samples($n_1, n_2 \\ge 30$), the distribution of test statistic is approximately normal.
    - For small samples($n_1, n_2 < 30$), the distribution of test statistic is Student's $t$-distribution with $(n_1 + n_2 - 2)$ degree of freedom.

5. Accept $H_0$ if $p$-value $\geslant\\alpha$(Type-I error probability, significance level). Otherwise, accept $H_1$.
    - The $p$-value can be calculated using a statistical calculator or spreadsheet.
    - If $t$ and $v$ are obtained, the $p$-value can also be calculated using the $t$-table or $t$-distribution in statistical software.
