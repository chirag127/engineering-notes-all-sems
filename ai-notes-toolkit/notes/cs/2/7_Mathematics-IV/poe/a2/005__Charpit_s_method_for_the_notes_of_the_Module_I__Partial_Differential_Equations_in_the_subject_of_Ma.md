### Charpit's Method for Partial Differential Equations

1. Partial Differential Equations: equations involving partial derivatives of unknown functions.
2. Order of a PDE: the highest order of derivatives involved.
3. Linear PDEs: differential operators involve only linear operators. Ex: Laplace's equation.
4. Nonlinear PDEs: differential operators involve nonlinear operators. Ex: Burgers' equation.
5. Charpit's Method: A method to solve first order PDEs by reducing to an ordinary differential equation.
6. Steps:
    1. Get the PDE in the form F(x,y,u,∂u/∂x,∂u/∂y) = 0
    2. Find a function φ(x,y) such that F(x,y,φ,∂φ/∂x,∂φ/∂y) = 0
    3. Substitute u = v + φ into the PDE to get an ordinary differential equation with v which can be solved.
    4. Use the solution for v and add φ to get the solution for u.
7. Application: Use Charpit's method to solve the PDE: ∂u/∂x + ∂u/∂y = 0.
