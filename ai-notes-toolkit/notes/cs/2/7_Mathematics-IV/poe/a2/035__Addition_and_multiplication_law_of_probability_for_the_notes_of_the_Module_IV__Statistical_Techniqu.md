### Addition and multiplication law of probability for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in Mathematics-IV KCS.

* Addition Law of Probability: If A and B are two mutually exclusive events, then P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)
For example: Probability of getting a Heart or a Spade = Probability of getting a Heart + Probability of getting a Spade
* Multiplication Law of Probability: If A and B are two independent events, then P(A and B) = P(A) x P(B)
For example: Probability of getting a Heart and drawing an Ace = Probability of drawing a Heart x Probability of drawing an Ace

The above laws are useful in solving complex probability problems by breaking down the problems into simple and basic probability calculations involving independent and mutually exclusive events. These laws form the foundation for further study of conditional probability and Bayes' theorem.
