#### Assumptions

Before conducting a test of significance of difference of means, it is important to check the assumptions. The following assumptions must be met:

- The samples are independent of each other.
- The populations have a normal distribution.
- The variances of the populations are equal.

#### Hypotheses

The null hypothesis, denoted by H0, states that there is no significant difference between the means of the two populations. The alternative hypothesis, denoted by Ha, states that there is a significant difference between the means of the two populations.

H0: μ1 - μ2 = 0
Ha: μ1 - μ2 ≠ 0

where μ1 is the mean of population 1 and μ2 is the mean of population 2.
