## Module I: Partial Differential Equations

Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) are mathematical equations that involve partial derivatives of an unknown function of several variables. PDEs are used to model a wide range of physical phenomena, including fluid flow, heat transfer, and electromagnetic fields. In this module, we will study the basics of PDEs and their applications.

### Topics Covered in this Module:

1. Introduction to Partial Differential Equations
   - Definition of PDEs
   - Classification of PDEs
   - Examples of PDEs

2. First-order PDEs
   - Linear first-order PDEs
   - Quasi-linear first-order PDEs
   - Nonlinear first-order PDEs

3. Second-order PDEs
   - Classification of second-order PDEs
   - Examples of second-order PDEs

4. Boundary Value Problems
   - Dirichlet boundary conditions
   - Neumann boundary conditions
   - Mixed boundary conditions

5. Method of Characteristics
   - Definition of Characteristics
   - Solving first-order PDEs using the Method of Characteristics

6. Separation of Variables
   - Introduction to Separation of Variables
   - Solving second-order homogeneous PDEs using Separation of Variables

7. Fourier Series and Fourier Transform
   - Fourier Series
   - Fourier Transform
   - Applications of Fourier Transform

### Learning Outcomes:

Upon completion of this module, students will be able to:

- Define partial differential equations and classify them according to their properties.
- Solve first-order PDEs using the Method of Characteristics.
- Solve second-order homogeneous PDEs using Separation of Variables.
- Apply Fourier Series and Fourier Transform to solve PDEs.
- Understand the physical applications of PDEs in various fields.

### Conclusion:

Partial Differential Equations are important mathematical tools in modeling physical phenomena. This module covers the basics of PDEs and their applications. By the end of this module, students will have gained a solid understanding of PDEs and will be able to apply various methods to solve them.
