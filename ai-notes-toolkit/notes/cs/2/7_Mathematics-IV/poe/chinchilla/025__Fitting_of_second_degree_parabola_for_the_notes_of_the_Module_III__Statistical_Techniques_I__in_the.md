

### Fitting of Second Degree Parabola

In the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS, one of the topics covered in Module III: Statistical Techniques I is the fitting of second degree parabola. This is an important topic to understand as it involves the use of statistical techniques to analyze data and make predictions.

Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying the fitting of second degree parabola:

1. A second degree parabola is a quadratic function of the form y = ax^2 + bx + c, where a, b, and c are constants.
2. The process of fitting a second degree parabola involves finding the values of a, b, and c that best fit a set of data points.
3. To do this, we use the method of least squares, which involves finding the line that minimizes the sum of the squared differences between the predicted values and the actual values.
4. Once we have found the values of a, b, and c, we can use the parabola to make predictions about future data points.
5. It is important to note that the accuracy of these predictions depends on the quality of the data used to fit the parabola.

In summary, the fitting of second degree parabola is a key topic in Module III: Statistical Techniques I of Mathematics-IV KCS. By understanding the process of fitting a parabola and using the method of least squares, we can analyze data and make predictions with greater accuracy.
