### Classification of linear partial differential equation of second order

In the study of partial differential equations, it is important to classify different types of equations based on their properties. Here, we will focus on the classification of linear partial differential equations of second order.

1. Elliptic Equations:
   - An elliptic equation is a type of partial differential equation in which the solution is smooth and has no singularities.
   - The Laplace equation and the Poisson equation are examples of elliptic equations.
   - In two dimensions, elliptic equations are characterized by a negative discriminant of their second-order terms.

2. Parabolic Equations:
   - A parabolic equation is a type of partial differential equation in which the solution is smooth at a fixed time but may have singularities at other times.
   - The heat equation and the diffusion equation are examples of parabolic equations.
   - In two dimensions, parabolic equations are characterized by a zero discriminant of their second-order terms.

3. Hyperbolic Equations:
   - A hyperbolic equation is a type of partial differential equation in which the solution is smooth and has wave-like behavior.
   - The wave equation and the telegraph equation are examples of hyperbolic equations.
   - In two dimensions, hyperbolic equations are characterized by a positive discriminant of their second-order terms.

4. Mixed Equations:
   - A mixed equation is a type of partial differential equation that exhibits properties of both elliptic and hyperbolic equations.
   - The Helmholtz equation and the Klein-Gordon equation are examples of mixed equations.
   - In two dimensions, mixed equations are characterized by a discriminant of their second-order terms that is neither positive nor negative.

It is important to classify partial differential equations as it allows us to apply appropriate mathematical techniques or methods to solve them. Each type of equation requires a different approach, and knowing the classification helps us choose the most appropriate method.
