

### Regression lines of y on x and x on y for the notes of the Module III: Statistical Techniques I: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Regression analysis is a statistical technique used to find the relationship between a dependent variable (y) and one or more independent variables (x). It is commonly used in various fields like finance, economics, social sciences, and many more. In this module, we will learn about regression lines of y on x and x on y. Here are some key points regarding these regression lines:

#### Regression line of y on x

- The regression line of y on x is a straight line that represents the relationship between the dependent variable y and the independent variable x.
- It is also known as the conditional mean of y given x.
- The equation of the regression line of y on x is given by: y = a + bx, where a is the intercept and b is the slope of the line.
- The intercept a represents the value of y when x is equal to zero.
- The slope b represents the change in y for a unit change in x.
- The regression line of y on x can be used to predict the value of y for a given value of x.
- The difference between the actual value of y and the predicted value of y is known as the residual.

#### Regression line of x on y

- The regression line of x on y is a straight line that represents the relationship between the independent variable x and the dependent variable y.
- It is also known as the conditional mean of x given y.
- The equation of the regression line of x on y is given by: x = a + by, where a is the intercept and b is the slope of the line.
- The intercept a represents the value of x when y is equal to zero.
- The slope b represents the change in x for a unit change in y.
- The regression line of x on y can be used to predict the value of x for a given value of y.
- The difference between the actual value of x and the predicted value of x is known as the residual.

In conclusion, the regression lines of y on x and x on y are important tools in regression analysis. They help us to understand the relationship between the dependent and independent variables and can be used to make predictions. It is important to understand the concepts and equations related to these regression lines for a better understanding of regression analysis.
