3. Calculate the deviations: The next step is to calculate the deviations of the data points from the mean. This involves subtracting the mean from each data point.

4. Square the deviations: The next step is to square the deviations. This is done to make sure that the deviations are positive and to give more weight to larger deviations.

5. Calculate the sum of squared deviations: The next step is to calculate the sum of squared deviations. This involves adding up all the squared deviations.
