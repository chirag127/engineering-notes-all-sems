## Module I: Partial Differential Equations

Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) are mathematical equations that involve multiple variables and their partial derivatives. These equations are used to describe physical phenomena in fields such as physics, engineering, and finance. In this module, we will cover the basics of PDEs and their applications.

Here are some of the key topics that we will cover in this module:

- Classification of PDEs: We will classify PDEs based on their order, linearity, and homogeneity. We will also discuss the difference between elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic PDEs.

- Boundary Conditions: We will learn about the different types of boundary conditions, such as Dirichlet, Neumann, and Robin boundary conditions, and how they are used to solve PDEs.

- Method of Characteristics: This method is used to solve first-order linear PDEs. We will learn how to use this method to find the general solution of a PDE.

- Separation of Variables: This method is used to solve second-order linear PDEs. We will learn how to use this method to find the general solution of a PDE.

- Fourier Series: We will learn about Fourier series and how they can be used to solve PDEs. We will also discuss the convergence of Fourier series.

- Fourier Transform: This is a powerful tool used to analyze signals and functions. We will learn how to use the Fourier transform to solve PDEs.

- Numerical Methods: We will discuss some of the numerical methods used to solve PDEs, such as finite difference methods and finite element methods.

By the end of this module, you will have a solid understanding of the basics of PDEs and their applications. You will be able to classify PDEs based on their order, linearity, and homogeneity, and solve PDEs using various methods such as separation of variables and the Fourier transform. You will also be familiar with some of the numerical methods used to solve PDEs.
