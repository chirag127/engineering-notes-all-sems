### Discrete and Continuous Probability distribution for the notes of the Module IV: Statistical Techniques II: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

Probability distribution refers to the set of all possible outcomes of a random variable and their corresponding probabilities. In this module, we will learn about two types of probability distributions: Discrete and Continuous.

#### Discrete Probability Distribution
A discrete probability distribution is a probability distribution that can only take a finite or countable number of values. Some examples of discrete probability distributions are:

- Binomial Distribution
- Poisson Distribution
- Geometric Distribution

#### Continuous Probability Distribution
A continuous probability distribution is a probability distribution that can take any value within a specific range. Some examples of continuous probability distributions are:

- Normal Distribution
- Exponential Distribution
- Weibull Distribution

#### Properties of Probability Distributions
Probability distributions have some important properties that are worth noting. These properties are:

- Mean: It is the average value of the distribution.
- Variance: It measures the spread of the distribution.
- Skewness: It measures the degree of asymmetry of the distribution.
- Kurtosis: It measures the degree of peakedness of the distribution.

#### Applications of Probability Distributions
Probability distributions have many applications in various fields, some of which are:

- In finance, probability distributions are used to model stock prices and interest rates.
- In physics, probability distributions are used to model the behavior of particles in a system.
- In biology, probability distributions are used to model the distribution of genes in a population.

In conclusion, understanding probability distributions is important for anyone who wants to make informed decisions based on data. By understanding the properties and applications of probability distributions, one can make more accurate predictions and better decisions.
