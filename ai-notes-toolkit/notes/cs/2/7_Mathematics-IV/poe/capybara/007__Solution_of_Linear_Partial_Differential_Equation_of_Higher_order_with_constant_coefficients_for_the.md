### Solution of Linear Partial Differential Equation of Higher Order with Constant Coefficients

In this module, we will learn about the solution of linear partial differential equations of higher order with constant coefficients. Here are the key points to keep in mind:

- A partial differential equation (PDE) is an equation that involves partial derivatives of a function of several variables.
- A linear PDE is a PDE that can be written as a linear combination of partial derivatives of the unknown function and its derivatives.
- The order of a PDE is the highest order of the partial derivatives appearing in the equation.
- The coefficients of a PDE are the constants multiplying the partial derivatives.
- A PDE with constant coefficients is a linear PDE where the coefficients are constants that do not depend on the variables.
- The general solution of a linear PDE with constant coefficients can be written as a linear combination of exponential functions and/or polynomial functions, depending on the roots of the characteristic equation.
- The characteristic equation is a polynomial equation obtained by replacing the derivatives in the PDE with the corresponding variable and solving for the roots.
- If the roots of the characteristic equation are distinct real numbers, then the general solution involves exponential functions.
- If the roots of the characteristic equation are complex conjugate pairs, then the general solution involves complex exponential functions that can be simplified to sine and cosine functions.
- If the characteristic equation has repeated roots, then the general solution involves polynomial functions multiplied by exponential functions.
- The initial and boundary conditions must be specified to determine the constants in the general solution and obtain a particular solution.
- The method of separation of variables can be used to solve some specific types of linear PDEs with constant coefficients, such as the wave equation and the heat equation.

In conclusion, the solution of linear partial differential equations of higher order with constant coefficients involves finding the characteristic equation, determining the roots, and using them to construct the general solution. The initial and boundary conditions must be used to find the constants and obtain a particular solution. The method of separation of variables can be a useful tool for solving specific types of PDEs.
