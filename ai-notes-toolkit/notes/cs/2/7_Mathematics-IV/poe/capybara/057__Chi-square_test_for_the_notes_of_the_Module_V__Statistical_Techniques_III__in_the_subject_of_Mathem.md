### Chi-square test for the notes of the Module V: Statistical Techniques III: in the subject of Mathematics-IV KCS

The chi-square test is a statistical technique that is used to determine if there is a significant difference between the expected and observed values in a distribution. It is widely used in various fields, including mathematics, biology, physics, social sciences, and business.

Here are some key points to understand the chi-square test:

- The chi-square test is based on the concept of a chi-square statistic, which is calculated by comparing the observed and expected frequencies of a categorical variable.
- The test is used to determine if there is a significant difference between the observed and expected frequencies in a distribution.
- The null hypothesis is that there is no significant difference between the observed and expected frequencies, while the alternative hypothesis is that there is a significant difference.
- The level of significance is usually set at 0.05 or 0.01, which means that if the p-value is less than the level of significance, the null hypothesis is rejected.
- The chi-square test can be used for both goodness-of-fit tests and tests of independence.
- Goodness-of-fit tests are used to determine if the observed frequencies in a sample fit a theoretical distribution, while tests of independence are used to determine if there is a relationship between two categorical variables.
- The test can be performed using a chi-square table or a chi-square calculator.
- The chi-square table provides critical values for different degrees of freedom and levels of significance, while the calculator calculates the chi-square statistic and the p-value.
- It is important to note that the chi-square test assumes that the sample is representative of the population and that the expected frequencies are not too small.

Overall, the chi-square test is a useful statistical technique that can be used to determine if there is a significant difference between the expected and observed frequencies in a distribution. It is important to understand the key concepts and assumptions of the test to apply it correctly in various fields of study.
