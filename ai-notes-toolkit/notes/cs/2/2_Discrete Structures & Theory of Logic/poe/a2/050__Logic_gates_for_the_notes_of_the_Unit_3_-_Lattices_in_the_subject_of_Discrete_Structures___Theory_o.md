### Logic gates for the notes of the Unit 3 - Lattices in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. AND gate: Output is high only if both inputs are high. It performs conjunction.
2. OR gate: Output is high if one or both inputs are high. It performs disjunction.
3. NOT gate: Inverts the input. Output is high if input is low and vice versa. It performs negation.
4. NAND gate: Output is high only if both inputs are low. It is a negation of AND gate.
5. NOR gate: Output is high only if both inputs are low. It is a negation of OR gate.
6. XOR gate: Output is high if inputs are different. It performs exclusive disjunction.
7. XNOR gate: Output is high if inputs are same. It is a negation of XOR gate.

The logic gates can be combined to perform complex logic operations to solve problems. They are fundamental building blocks of digital circuits and processors.
