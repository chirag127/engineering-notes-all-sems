### Combination of sets for the notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Union of Sets: The union of two sets A and B is the set of elements which are in A, in B, or in both A and B. It contains all the distinct elements of A and B. It is denoted by A U B.
2. Intersection of Sets: The intersection of two sets A and B is the set of elements which are common to both A and B. It is denoted by A N B.
3. Complement of a Set: The complement of a set A with respect to the universal set U is the set of all elements in U that are not in A. It is denoted by A'.
4. De Morgan's Laws: For any two sets A and B, the following laws hold:
    (A U B)' = A' N B'
    (A N B)' = A' U B'
