### Truth tables for the notes of the Unit 4 - Propositional Logic

1. Truth tables: To determine the truth value of a compound proposition in terms of the truth values of its simpler components.

2. The truth value of a compound proposition depends upon the logical connectives used and the truth values of its components.

3. The possible truth value combinations of the components are exhausted in a truth table.

4. The truth table contains all possible combinations of truth values of the components and the corresponding truth value of the compound proposition.

5. Rules for drawing truth tables:

- List out all the proposition symbols involved in the compound propositions.
- Assign T/F values to the proposition symbols (simple propositions).
- Determine the truth value of the compound propositions using the logical connectives and the truth values of the proposition symbols.
- Check for consistency and completeness.
