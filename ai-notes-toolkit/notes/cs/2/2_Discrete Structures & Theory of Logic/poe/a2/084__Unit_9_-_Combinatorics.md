## Unit 9 - Combinatorics

1. Fundamental Principle of Counting: If a task consists of m steps and each step can be done in n ways, then the total number of ways the task can be completed is n x n x ... x n (m times).
2. Permutations: Arrangements of a given number of objects in a specific order.
- Permutation of n distinct objects = n!.
- Permutation with repetition: p(n,k) = n^k.
3. Combinations: Selection of a subset of objects from a larger set. Order does not matter.
- Combination of n distinct objects taken k at a time: C(n,k) = n! / (k!*(n-k)!).
4. Binomial Coefficients: The coefficients in the expansion of (x + y)^n. C(n,k) = C(n,k) = C(n,n-k).
5. Pascal's Triangle: Shows binomial coefficients in a triangular array. Each entry is the sum of the two above it.
6. Stars and Bars: A technique to count combinations. Divide objects into k subsets (stars) and remaining objects (bars). Count arrangements of stars and bars and multiply.
