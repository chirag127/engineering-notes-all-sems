## Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function

1. Recurrence Relation: A recurrence relation is a mathematical equation that defines a sequence recursively. It relates successive terms of a sequence.

2. Generating Function: A generating function is a function of a variable 't' whose coefficients are the terms of a sequence. It is used to find closed forms or formulas for computing the nth term of a sequence.

3. Uses:
- Recurrence relations and generating functions are used to solve complex problems involving sequences and to find closed-form expressions for the terms of sequences.
- They provide a compact way of encoding the information about a sequence.
- They are useful in analyzing the asymptotic behavior of sequences, like finding bounds on growth rates.

4. Methods to solve:
- Guess and verify method
- Inspection method
- Factoring method
- Evolution of generating functions
