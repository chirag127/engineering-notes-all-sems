### Boolean Algebra for the notes of the Unit 3 - Lattices

1. Boolean algebra deals with the logical relationships between variables that can have only true or false as possible values.
2. The basic operations in Boolean algebra are AND, OR and NOT.
3. AND operation: A AND B is true only if both A and B are true.
4. OR operation: A OR B is true if either A or B or both are true.
5. NOT operation: NOT A is true if A is false and vice versa.
6. Other important laws of Boolean algebra are:
- Commutative laws: A AND B = B AND A; A OR B = B OR A
- Associative laws: (A AND B) AND C = A AND (B AND C); (A OR B) OR C = A OR (B OR C)
- Identity laws: A AND 1 = A; A OR 0 = A
- De Morgan's laws: NOT (A AND B) = NOT A OR NOT B; NOT (A OR B) = NOT A AND NOT B
7. Boolean algebra is useful to simplify logical expressions and prove logical equivalences.
8. It has applications in logic, digital electronics, and computer systems.
