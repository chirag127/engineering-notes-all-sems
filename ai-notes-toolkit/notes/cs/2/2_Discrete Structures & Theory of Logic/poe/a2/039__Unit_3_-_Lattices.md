## Unit 3 - Lattices

1. A lattice is a partially ordered set in which any two elements have a supremum (least upper bound) and an infimum (greatest lower bound).
2. Lattices arise naturally in many applications, including logic, vector spaces, projective geometry, and order theory.
3. A lattice has a unique least element 0 and a unique greatest element 1. Every element a has a least upper bound (join or supremum) with 1, called the "top" of a:
top(a) = {b in L | a <= b}
4. Dually, every element a has a greatest lower bound (meet or infimum) with 0, called the "bottom" of a:
bot(a) = {b in L | b <= a}
5. A lattice is distributive if the operations of join and meet distribute over each other. Formally, for all a, b, and c in L:
a ∧ (b ∨ c) = (a ∧ b) ∨ (a ∧ c)
a ∨ (b ∧ c) = (a ∨ b) ∧ (a ∨ c)
