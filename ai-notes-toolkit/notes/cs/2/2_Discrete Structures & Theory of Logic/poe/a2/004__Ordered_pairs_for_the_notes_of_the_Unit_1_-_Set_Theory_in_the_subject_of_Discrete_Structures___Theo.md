### Ordered pairs for the notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. An ordered pair is a pair of objects where the order of the elements matters. It is denoted as (a, b) where a and b are two distinct objects.
2. The first element of the pair is called the first coordinate and the second element is called the second coordinate.
3. Ordered pairs are used to define relations and functions. The properties of ordered pairs are:
- (a, b) is not same as (b, a)
- (a, b) = (c, d) if and only if a = c and b = d
4. Examples of ordered pairs:
(2, 3), (x, y), ('Apple', 'Banana')
