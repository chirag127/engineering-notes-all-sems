### Multigraphs for the notes of the Unit 7 - Graphs in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. A Multigraph is a graph which is allowed to have multiple edges between two vertices. In other words, loops and parallel edges are permitted in a multigraph.
2. The degree of a vertex in a multigraph is simply the number of edges incident on that vertex. This allows the degree of a vertex to be greater than 1.
3. A walk, trail, or path in a multigraph is similar to that in a graph. The only difference is that a multigraph may contain repeated vertices or edges.
4. A cycle in a multigraph consists of a closed walk where all vertices and edges are distinct except possibly the first and last vertices. The girth of a multigraph is the length of its shortest cycle.
5. The connectivity and components of a multigraph are defined similarly as in graphs with simple edges and are studied in terms of the structure consisting of its underlying graph.
