### Counting Techniques for the notes of the Unit 9 - Combinatorics in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. Fundamental Principle of Counting: If one task can be done in m ways and after doing the first task, a second task can be done in n ways, then the two tasks can be done together in m x n ways.
2. Permutations: Arrangements of distinct objects in a specific order.
- permutations of n distinct objects = n!
3. Combinations: Selections of objects where order is not important.
- combinations of n distinct objects taken r at a time = nCr = n! / (r!(n-r)!)
4. Stars and Bars: A visual method to solve combination problems. Each selection is represented by a star and each non-selection is represented by a bar. The number of combinations is equal to the number of arrangements of stars and bars.
5. Multinomial Coefficient: Generalization of combinations where selections can be made with different counts. Represents the number of ways of selecting x1, x2, ..., xk objects from a set of n distinct objects.
- multinomial coefficient = n! / (x1!x2!...xk!)
