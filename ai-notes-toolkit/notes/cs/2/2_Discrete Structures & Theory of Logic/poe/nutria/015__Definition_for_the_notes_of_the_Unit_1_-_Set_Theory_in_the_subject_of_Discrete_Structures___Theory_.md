

### Definition for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Set Theory in the Subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

* A **set** is a collection of objects, called **elements**, which are distinct from one another. 
* **Set theory** is the branch of mathematics that studies sets and their properties.
* The **union** of two sets is the set of elements that are in either of the two sets.
* The **intersection** of two sets is the set of elements that are in both of the two sets.
* The **complement** of a set is the set of elements that are not in the original set.
* **Venn diagrams** are diagrams that illustrate the relationships between sets.
* **Cartesian products** are sets of ordered pairs of elements from two sets.
* **Relations** are sets of ordered pairs of elements from two sets.
* **Functions** are relations in which each element of one set is associated with exactly one element of the other set.
* **Partial orders** are sets of elements in which a relation of "less than or equal to" is defined.
* **Equivalence relations** are sets of elements in which a relation of "equals" is defined.
* **Induction** is a method of reasoning that can be used to prove theorems about sets.
