
### Cosets for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Algebraic Structures in the Subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. A coset is a set of elements in a group that is equivalent to another set of elements in the same group. 
2. Cosets are used to divide a group into distinct subsets. 
3. The cosets of a group G are the equivalence classes of the group under the action of a subgroup H. 
4. The coset of an element x in a group G is the set of all elements that are equivalent to x under the action of a subgroup H. 
5. The cosets of a group G form a partition of G. 
6. The left and right cosets of a subgroup H in a group G are the sets of elements in G that can be obtained by left and right multiplication of elements in H by elements in G. 
7. The index of a subgroup H in a group G is the number of cosets of H in G. 
8. The Lagrange theorem states that the index of a subgroup H in a group G is equal to the order of the group G divided by the order of the subgroup H. 
9. The coset decomposition theorem states that a group G can be decomposed into a disjoint union of its cosets.
