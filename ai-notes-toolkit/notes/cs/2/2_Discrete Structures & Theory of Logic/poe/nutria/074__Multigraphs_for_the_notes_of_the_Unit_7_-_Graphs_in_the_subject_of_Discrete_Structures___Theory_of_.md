
### Multigraphs for the Notes of Unit 7 - Graphs in the Subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

* A multigraph is a graph in which multiple edges are allowed between the same pair of vertices.
* Multigraphs can be used to represent many different types of relationships between objects, such as social networks, transportation networks, and communication networks.
* Multigraphs can be represented using adjacency matrices, adjacency lists, and incidence matrices.
* Multigraphs can also be used to represent weighted graphs, where the weights represent the strength of the relationship between two vertices.
* In a multigraph, there can be multiple paths between two vertices. This can be used to find the shortest path between two points, or to find the most efficient route in a transportation network.
* Multigraphs can also be used to represent directed graphs, where the edges have a direction associated with them. This can be used to represent a flow of information or resources between two points.
