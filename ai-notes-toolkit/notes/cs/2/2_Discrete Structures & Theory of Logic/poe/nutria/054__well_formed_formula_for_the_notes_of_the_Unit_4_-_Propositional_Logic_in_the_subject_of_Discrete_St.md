
### Well Formed Formula (WFF) for Propositional Logic 

1. A **Well Formed Formula (WFF)** is a logical expression composed of **propositional variables** and **logical operators**. 
2. Propositional variables are symbols that represent propositions and can take on values of either true or false. 
3. Logical operators are symbols that connect propositional variables to form logical expressions. 
4. The most commonly used logical operators are **conjunction (∧)**, **disjunction (∨)**, **negation (¬)**, **implication (→)** and **biconditional (↔)**. 
5. A WFF is said to be **valid** if it is true for all possible combinations of true and false values of its propositional variables.
6. A WFF is said to be **satisfiable** if it is true for at least one combination of true and false values of its propositional variables.
7. A WFF is said to be **unsatisfiable** if it is false for all combinations of true and false values of its propositional variables.
8. **Truth tables** are used to determine the validity, satisfiability and unsatisfiability of a WFF.
