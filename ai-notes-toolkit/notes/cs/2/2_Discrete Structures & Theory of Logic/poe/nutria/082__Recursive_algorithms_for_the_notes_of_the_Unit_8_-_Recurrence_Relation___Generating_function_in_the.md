
### Recursive algorithms for the notes of the Unit 8 - Recurrence Relation & Generating function in the subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. A recursive algorithm is a method of solving a problem by breaking it down into smaller sub-problems and solving them recursively. 
2. Recurrence relations are mathematical equations that describe the relationship between a sequence of numbers. 
3. Generating functions are mathematical expressions used to generate sequences of numbers. 
4. Recursive algorithms can be used to solve problems related to recurrence relations and generating functions. 
5. To solve a problem using a recursive algorithm, we must first identify the base case, which is the simplest form of the problem. 
6. We then use the base case to solve the problem recursively. 
7. The recursive algorithm is then applied to the problem until the base case is reached. 
8. Once the base case is reached, the solution to the problem is obtained.
