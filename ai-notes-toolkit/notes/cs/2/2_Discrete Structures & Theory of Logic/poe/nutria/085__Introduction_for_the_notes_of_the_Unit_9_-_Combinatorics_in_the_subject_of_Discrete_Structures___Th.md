
### Introduction 

1. Combinatorics is a branch of mathematics that deals with the study of finite or countable discrete structures. It is a part of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic.
2. Combinatorics is used to count the number of elements of a finite set and to determine the probability of an event. It is also used to solve optimization problems.
3. Combinatorics can be used to solve problems in engineering, computer science, and operations research.
4. Some of the topics covered in Combinatorics include permutations and combinations, generating functions, recurrence relations, and combinatorial designs.
5. Combinatorics is used to study the structure of graphs, hypergraphs, matroids, and designs. It is also used to study the properties of codes and linear algebraic structures.
6. Combinatorics is used to study the structure of languages, the theory of computing, and the theory of cryptography. It is also used to study the structure of games and puzzles.
