
### Operations on Relations 

1. **Union** of two relations is the combination of the two relations. It is denoted by $A \cup B$ and is defined as:
    $$A \cup B = \{(x, y) \mid (x, y) \in A \text{ or } (x, y) \in B\}$$
2. **Intersection** of two relations is the common elements of the two relations. It is denoted by $A \cap B$ and is defined as:
    $$A \cap B = \{(x, y) \mid (x, y) \in A \text{ and } (x, y) \in B\}$$
3. **Complement** of a relation is the set of all elements not in the relation. It is denoted by $A^C$ and is defined as:
    $$A^C = \{(x, y) \mid (x, y) \notin A\}$$
4. **Inverse** of a relation is a flipped version of the relation. It is denoted by $A^{-1}$ and is defined as:
    $$A^{-1} = \{(y, x) \mid (x, y) \in A\}$$
5. **Cartesian Product** of two relations is the combination of all the elements in the two relations. It is denoted by $A \times B$ and is defined as:
    $$A \times B = \{(x, y) \mid (x, y) \in A \text{ and } (x, y) \in B\}$$
