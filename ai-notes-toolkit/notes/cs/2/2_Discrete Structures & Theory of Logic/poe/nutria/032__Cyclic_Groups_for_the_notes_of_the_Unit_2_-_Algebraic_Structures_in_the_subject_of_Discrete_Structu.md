
### Cyclic Groups
A cyclic group is a group of elements which can be generated by a single element. In other words, a cyclic group is a group of elements which can be expressed as powers of a single element.

A cyclic group of order n is a group of n elements, where each element is the power of a single element.

Examples of cyclic groups include the integers modulo n, the group of units modulo n, and the group of permutations of a set of n elements.

Properties of Cyclic Groups:
1. All cyclic groups are abelian.
2. The order of every element in a cyclic group is a divisor of the order of the group.
3. Every subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic.
4. Every cyclic group is isomorphic to its dual group.
5. The center of a cyclic group is the same as the group itself.
6. Every cyclic group is generated by a single element.
7. The order of a cyclic group is equal to the order of its generator.
8. The order of a cyclic group is equal to the number of elements in the group.
