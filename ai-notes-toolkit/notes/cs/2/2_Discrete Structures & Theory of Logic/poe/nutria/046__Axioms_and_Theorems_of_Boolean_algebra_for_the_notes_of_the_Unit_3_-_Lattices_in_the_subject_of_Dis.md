
### Axioms and Theorems of Boolean Algebra

* **Axiom 1:** The set of Boolean algebra is closed under the operations of union, intersection, and complementation. 
* **Axiom 2:** The set of Boolean algebra is closed under the operation of implication. 
* **Axiom 3:** For every element x of the set of Boolean algebra, x is equal to its own complement. 
* **Theorem 1:** The set of Boolean algebra is distributive over union and intersection. 
* **Theorem 2:** For every element x of the set of Boolean algebra, x is equal to its own double complement. 
* **Theorem 3:** The set of Boolean algebra is distributive over implication and complementation. 
* **Theorem 4:** The set of Boolean algebra is associative over union, intersection, and complementation.
