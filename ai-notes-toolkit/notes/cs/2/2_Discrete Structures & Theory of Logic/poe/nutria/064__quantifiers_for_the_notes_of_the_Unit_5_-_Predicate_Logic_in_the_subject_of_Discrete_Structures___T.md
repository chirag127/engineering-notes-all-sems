

### Quantifiers for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Predicate Logic in the Subject of Discrete Structures & Theory of Logic

1. **Universal Quantifier** - A universal quantifier is a logical operator that is used to indicate that a statement is true for all elements in a given set. It is usually written as an upside-down A, and is usually preceded by the phrase "for all". 

2. **Existential Quantifier** - An existential quantifier is a logical operator that is used to indicate that a statement is true for at least one element in a given set. It is usually written as an upside-down E, and is usually preceded by the phrase "there exists". 

3. **Predicate Logic** - Predicate logic is a type of formal logic that is used to describe relationships between objects and properties. It is a powerful tool for reasoning about the world and can be used to express statements about objects, properties, and relationships in a precise way. 

4. **Discrete Structures** - Discrete Structures are mathematical structures that are composed of discrete elements, such as integers, sets, and graphs. Discrete Structures are used to model and analyze problems in computer science, engineering, and other disciplines. 

5. **Theory of Logic** - The Theory of Logic is a branch of mathematics that deals with the study of logical systems, their properties, and their applications. It is used to analyze the validity of arguments and to determine the truth or falsity of statements.
