### Natural Numbers

- Natural numbers are the numbers that are used for counting and ordering. They are also called positive integers or counting numbers.
- The set of natural numbers is denoted by N = {1, 2, 3, ...}.
- Natural numbers have the following properties:
  - They are infinite, meaning there is no largest natural number.
  - They are discrete, meaning there is no natural number between two consecutive natural numbers.
  - They are well-ordered, meaning every non-empty subset of natural numbers has a least element.
  - They are closed under addition and multiplication, meaning the sum and product of any two natural numbers is also a natural number.
  - They are not closed under subtraction and division, meaning the difference and quotient of any two natural numbers may not be a natural number.
- Natural numbers can be used to define other number systems, such as integers, rational numbers, and real numbers. For example, integers are the set of natural numbers, their negatives, and zero. Rational numbers are the set of fractions that can be written as the ratio of two integers. Real numbers are the set of numbers that can be represented by infinite decimal expansions.
