# Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are problem-solving methods that use shortcuts to produce good-enough solutions given a limited time frame or deadline.
- Heuristics are used to speed up the process of finding a satisfactory solution, where an exhaustive search is impractical.
- Heuristics can be contrasted with algorithms, which are guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but may take longer to do so.
- Heuristics can be used in various fields, including artificial intelligence, where they are used to solve problems that are too complex for exact algorithms.
- Common heuristics include the "hill-climbing" heuristic, which involves iteratively improving a solution until no further improvements can be made, and the "best-first search" heuristic, which involves expanding the most promising node in a search tree first.
- Heuristics can be useful in solving problems, but they can also lead to suboptimal solutions or even failure to find a solution.
- The effectiveness of a heuristic depends on the specific problem and the quality of the heuristic itself.
- Heuristics can be improved through experience and learning, and can be combined with other problem-solving methods to increase their effectiveness.
