## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

1. **Define the problem**: Clearly identify and define the problem that needs to be solved.
2. **Gather information**: Collect relevant information and data related to the problem.
3. **Generate alternatives**: Brainstorm and generate a list of possible solutions to the problem.
4. **Evaluate alternatives**: Analyze the pros and cons of each alternative solution and determine the best course of action.
5. **Choose a solution**: Select the best solution based on the evaluation of alternatives.
6. **Implement the solution**: Put the chosen solution into action.
7. **Monitor and evaluate**: Monitor the results of the implemented solution and evaluate its effectiveness in solving the problem.
