# Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems

## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. **Identify the problem:** The first step in solving any AI problem is to clearly identify and define the problem. This involves understanding the goals and constraints of the problem, as well as the available data and resources.

2. **Gather and preprocess data:** Once the problem has been identified, the next step is to gather and preprocess the data that will be used to train the AI model. This may involve cleaning and transforming the data, as well as selecting relevant features.

3. **Select an appropriate model:** There are many different AI models and algorithms that can be used to solve a given problem. It is important to select a model that is well-suited to the problem at hand, taking into account factors such as the size and complexity of the data, as well as the desired accuracy and speed of the model.

4. **Train the model:** Once the data has been preprocessed and a model has been selected, the next step is to train the model using the data. This involves adjusting the model's parameters to minimize the error between the model's predictions and the true values.

5. **Evaluate the model:** After the model has been trained, it is important to evaluate its performance to ensure that it is making accurate predictions. This can be done by comparing the model's predictions to a set of test data that was not used during training.

6. **Deploy the model:** Once the model has been trained and evaluated, it can be deployed to solve the problem at hand. This may involve integrating the model into a larger system, or making it available as a standalone tool.

7. **Monitor and maintain the model:** AI models can degrade over time as the data and environment change. It is important to monitor the model's performance and make any necessary updates to ensure that it continues to make accurate predictions.
