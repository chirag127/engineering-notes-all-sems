# Knowledge Representation

Knowledge representation is a field of artificial intelligence that focuses on designing computer representations that capture information about the world that can be used to solve complex problems. It is the third unit in the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS.

Some key points to consider when studying knowledge representation are:

1. Knowledge representation is concerned with how knowledge is stored and manipulated within a computer system.
2. It involves the use of various techniques and methods to represent knowledge in a way that can be easily accessed and used by a computer program.
3. Some common knowledge representation techniques include semantic networks, frames, and production rules.
4. The choice of knowledge representation technique depends on the specific problem being solved and the type of knowledge being represented.
5. Knowledge representation is an important aspect of artificial intelligence, as it enables computers to reason and make decisions based on the information they have access to.
