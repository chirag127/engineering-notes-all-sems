# Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

## Events

1. An event is a change of state or a happening that occurs at a specific time and place.
2. Events can be represented using various formalisms, such as predicate logic, temporal logic, or event calculus.
3. In artificial intelligence, events are often used to model dynamic systems and reason about their behavior over time.
4. Event calculus is a formalism for representing and reasoning about events and their effects. It is based on first-order logic and allows for the representation of actions, events, and their effects on the world.
5. Temporal logic is another formalism for representing and reasoning about events and their temporal relationships. It extends propositional logic with temporal operators that allow for the representation of temporal relationships between events.
6. In knowledge representation, events can be used to model complex systems and reason about their behavior over time. This can be useful in various applications, such as planning, scheduling, and diagnosis.
