### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are problem-solving techniques that use a practical approach to find a satisfactory solution, where an exhaustive search is impractical.
- Heuristics are used to speed up the process of finding a solution by reducing the number of alternatives that must be considered.
- Heuristics are often used in artificial intelligence to solve problems that are too complex for traditional algorithms.
- Heuristics can be used to solve problems in many different domains, including scheduling, routing, and game playing.
- Heuristics can be classified into two categories: constructive heuristics and improvement heuristics.
- Constructive heuristics build a solution from scratch, while improvement heuristics improve an existing solution.
- Some common heuristics include the nearest neighbor heuristic, the greedy heuristic, and the hill climbing heuristic.
- Heuristics are not guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but they often produce good solutions in a reasonable amount of time.
- Heuristics can be combined with other techniques, such as local search and genetic algorithms, to improve their performance.
- The effectiveness of a heuristic depends on the problem being solved and the characteristics of the heuristic itself. It is important to choose the right heuristic for the problem at hand.
