### Local Search Algorithms and Optimization Problems

Local search algorithms are a type of heuristic method used for solving computationally hard optimization problems. These algorithms can be applied to problems that can be formulated as finding a solution that maximizes a criterion among a number of candidate solutions .

Local search algorithms work by moving from one solution to another in the space of candidate solutions by applying local changes. This process continues until a solution deemed optimal is found or a time bound is elapsed .

These algorithms are widely applied to numerous hard optimization problems and have been shown to be effective in finding good solutions for many practical problems .
