### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. **CPU (Central Processing Unit)**: The CPU is the primary component of a computer that performs most of the processing. It is responsible for executing instructions and performing calculations. AI applications require a powerful CPU to perform complex calculations quickly.

2. **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)**: A GPU is a specialized electronic circuit designed to rapidly manipulate and alter memory to accelerate the creation of images in a frame buffer intended for output to a display device. GPUs are used in AI applications for their ability to perform parallel processing, which is useful for tasks such as image and video processing.

3. **RAM (Random Access Memory)**: RAM is a type of computer memory that can be accessed randomly. It is used to store data that the CPU is currently processing. AI applications require a large amount of RAM to store and process large amounts of data quickly.

4. **Storage**: Storage devices such as hard drives and solid-state drives are used to store data that is not currently being processed by the CPU. AI applications require a large amount of storage to store the large amounts of data that they use.

5. **Networking**: Networking hardware such as network interface cards and routers are used to connect computers to networks. AI applications often require fast network connections to transfer large amounts of data quickly.

6. **Sensors**: Sensors such as cameras, microphones, and accelerometers are used to collect data from the environment. AI applications use this data to make decisions and interact with the world.

7. **Actuators**: Actuators such as motors and servos are used to control physical systems. AI applications use actuators to interact with the world and perform tasks.

8. **Power Supply**: The power supply provides electrical power to the computer. AI applications require a stable and reliable power supply to function properly.

These are some of the key hardware components that are used in AI applications. Each component plays a critical role in enabling AI systems to function effectively. It is important to ensure that the hardware used in an AI system is powerful enough to meet the demands of the application.
