### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval is a key technology for knowledge management. It deals with the search for information and the representation, storage, and organization of knowledge .
- Artificial intelligence technologies can be applied to information retrieval to improve its effectiveness .
- Concepts surveyed in the application of artificial intelligence to information retrieval include pattern recognition, representation, problem-solving and planning, heuristics, and learning .
- Information retrieval is concerned with search processes in which a user needs to identify a subset of information that is relevant to their information need within a large amount of information .
- AI applications in information retrieval include language models, information extraction, natural language processing, machine translation, and speech .
