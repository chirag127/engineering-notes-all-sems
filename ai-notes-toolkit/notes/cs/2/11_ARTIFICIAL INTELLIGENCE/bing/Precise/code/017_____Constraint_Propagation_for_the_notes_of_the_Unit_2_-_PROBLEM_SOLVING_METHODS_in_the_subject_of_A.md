### Constraint Propagation

Constraint propagation is a technique used in artificial intelligence and constraint programming to reduce the search space of a problem by applying constraints to the variables. It is a form of local consistency enforcement that can be used to solve constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs).

Here are some key points to remember about constraint propagation:

1. Constraint propagation is a process of applying constraints to variables in order to reduce the search space of a problem.
2. It is a form of local consistency enforcement that can be used to solve constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs).
3. Constraint propagation can be used to infer new constraints and to detect inconsistencies in the problem.
4. It can be applied iteratively until no more progress can be made, or until a solution is found.
5. Constraint propagation can be used in combination with other techniques, such as backtracking search, to solve CSPs more efficiently.
