### Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game theory model that extends the concept of a normal-form game to include probabilistic transitions between states. In a stochastic game, players make decisions at each stage, and the game transitions to a new state based on the players' actions and a probabilistic transition function.

Some key points to remember about stochastic games are:

1. Stochastic games are dynamic and involve multiple stages.
2. The game transitions between states based on the players' actions and a probabilistic transition function.
3. The payoffs to the players depend on the state of the game and the actions taken by the players.
4. Stochastic games can be used to model a wide range of strategic interactions, including those in economics, political science, and biology.
5. Solution concepts for stochastic games include Nash equilibrium and subgame perfect equilibrium.

Stochastic games provide a powerful framework for analyzing strategic interactions in dynamic and uncertain environments. They are widely used in the study of artificial intelligence and problem-solving methods.
