### Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

#### Events

1. An event is a change of state or a happening that can be observed.
2. Events can be represented in various ways, including as a set of propositions, as a graph, or as a temporal sequence.
3. Events can be used to represent and reason about the behavior of dynamic systems.
4. Event calculus is a formalism for representing and reasoning about events and their effects.
5. Event calculus can be used to represent and reason about actions, their preconditions, and their effects.
6. Event calculus can also be used to represent and reason about the persistence of properties over time.
7. Event calculus can be used to represent and reason about the interactions between events and the effects of those interactions.
8. Event calculus can be used to represent and reason about the effects of events on the state of the world.
9. Event calculus can be used to represent and reason about the effects of events on the goals of agents.
10. Event calculus can be used to represent and reason about the effects of events on the plans of agents.
