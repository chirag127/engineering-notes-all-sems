### Prolog Programming for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Prolog is a **logic programming language** that has an important role in **artificial intelligence**.
- Unlike many other programming languages, Prolog is intended primarily as a **declarative programming language**.
- In Prolog, logic is expressed as relations, which are called **Facts and Rules**.
- Prolog is usually characterized as a language for programming computers.
- Its inventors described Prolog as a programming language, and its name is an abbreviation for **PROgramming in LOGic**.
- Prolog is used in artificial intelligence programming.
- While implementing the solution for a given problem, instead of specifying the ways to achieve a certain goal in a specific situation, the user needs to specify the situation (rules and facts) and the goal (query).
- Prolog has a role and place in the area of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and in programming-language research.
- Answer Set Prolog is a comparatively new knowledge representation (KR) language with roots in older nonmonotonic logics and the logic programming language Prolog.
- Early proponents of the logical approach to artificial intelligence believed that the classical logical formalism called first-order logic would serve as the basis for the application.
