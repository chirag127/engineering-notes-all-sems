### Information Extraction

Information Extraction (IE) is one of the applications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that is covered in Unit 5 of the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS. It is the process of automatically extracting useful information from unstructured data sources.

Some key points to note about Information Extraction are:

1. It is an AI application that is used to extract structured information from unstructured data sources.
2. It is often used in combination with other AI applications such as Language Models, Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing, Machine Translation, and Speech Recognition .
3. It can be used to process documents faster and simplify operational procedures, with fewer errors .
4. It is a scalable and seamless solution that is simple to operate and easy to manage.
