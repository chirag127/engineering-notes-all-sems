# Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS
## Negotiation and Bargaining

Negotiation and bargaining are processes in which two or more parties attempt to reach an agreement on a matter of mutual interest. In the context of artificial intelligence, negotiation and bargaining can be used by software agents to reach agreements with other agents or with humans.

Some key points to consider when studying negotiation and bargaining in the context of artificial intelligence include:

1. Negotiation and bargaining can be used to resolve conflicts between agents or between agents and humans.
2. Negotiation and bargaining can be used to allocate resources or tasks among agents.
3. Negotiation and bargaining can be used to reach agreements on the terms of a contract or other agreement.
4. Negotiation and bargaining can be used to coordinate the actions of multiple agents.
5. Negotiation and bargaining can be used to reach agreements on the sharing of information or other resources.
6. Negotiation and bargaining can be used to reach agreements on the division of labor or other cooperative tasks.

In summary, negotiation and bargaining are important tools for software agents to reach agreements and coordinate their actions with other agents or with humans. These processes can be used to resolve conflicts, allocate resources, and coordinate actions in a variety of contexts.
