## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter or unit of any subject or topic.
2. It provides an overview of the subject and its importance.
3. It sets the stage for the rest of the material by providing context and background information.
4. It may also include definitions of key terms and concepts.
5. The introduction is an important part of any study material as it helps the reader to understand the scope and purpose of the subject.
6. It is essential to read the introduction carefully to gain a clear understanding of the subject before proceeding to the subsequent chapters or units.
