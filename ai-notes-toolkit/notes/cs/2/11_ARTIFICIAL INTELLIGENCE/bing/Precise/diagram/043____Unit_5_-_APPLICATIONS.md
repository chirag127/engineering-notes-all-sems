## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

1. Applications of mathematics: Mathematics has numerous applications in various fields such as science, engineering, economics, finance, and many more. It is used to solve complex problems, make predictions, and analyze data.

2. Applications of physics: Physics has a wide range of applications in fields such as engineering, medicine, and technology. It is used to design and develop new technologies, improve existing ones, and understand the natural world.

3. Applications of chemistry: Chemistry has many applications in fields such as medicine, agriculture, and industry. It is used to develop new drugs, improve crop yields, and create new materials.

4. Applications of biology: Biology has numerous applications in fields such as medicine, agriculture, and environmental science. It is used to develop new treatments for diseases, improve crop yields, and understand and protect the natural world.

5. Applications of computer science: Computer science has a wide range of applications in fields such as business, science, and entertainment. It is used to develop new software, analyze data, and create new technologies.
