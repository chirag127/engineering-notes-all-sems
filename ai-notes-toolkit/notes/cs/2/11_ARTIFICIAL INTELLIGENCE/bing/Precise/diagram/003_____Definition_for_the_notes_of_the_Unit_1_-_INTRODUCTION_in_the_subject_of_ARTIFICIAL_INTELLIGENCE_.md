### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION
#### Definition of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that deals with the development of intelligent machines that work and react like humans.
2. AI is the simulation of human intelligence processes by computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, and self-correction.
3. AI is the study of how to create computers and computer software that are capable of intelligent behavior.
4. AI is the field of study that focuses on the creation of intelligent agents, where an intelligent agent is a system that perceives its environment and takes actions that maximize its chances of success.
