### Constraint Propagation

Constraint propagation is a technique used in artificial intelligence and constraint programming to reduce the search space of a problem by applying constraints to the variables. This technique is used in problem-solving methods to simplify the problem and make it easier to solve.

Here are some key points to remember about constraint propagation:

1. Constraint propagation is the process of using constraints to reduce the search space of a problem.
2. It is used in artificial intelligence and constraint programming to simplify problems and make them easier to solve.
3. Constraint propagation can be applied to a wide range of problems, including scheduling, resource allocation, and optimization problems.
4. The goal of constraint propagation is to reduce the number of possible solutions to a problem by eliminating values that are inconsistent with the constraints.
5. Constraint propagation can be used in combination with other techniques, such as search and inference, to solve complex problems.
