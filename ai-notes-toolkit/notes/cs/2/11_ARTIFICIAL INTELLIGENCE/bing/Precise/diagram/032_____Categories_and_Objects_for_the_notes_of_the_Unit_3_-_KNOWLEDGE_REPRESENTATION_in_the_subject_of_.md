### Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION
#### Categories and Objects

- In artificial intelligence, knowledge representation is the study of how to represent information about the world in a form that a computer system can use to solve complex tasks.
- Categories and objects are two fundamental concepts in knowledge representation.
- A category is a group of objects that share common characteristics or properties. For example, the category "fruit" includes objects such as apples, bananas, and oranges.
- An object is an instance of a category. For example, a specific apple is an object that belongs to the category "fruit".
- Categories can be organized into hierarchies, where more general categories are at the top and more specific categories are at the bottom. For example, the category "food" is more general than the category "fruit", which is more general than the category "apple".
- Objects can have properties, which are characteristics or attributes that describe the object. For example, an apple object can have properties such as color, size, and taste.
- Knowledge representation systems often use formal languages to represent categories, objects, and their properties. These languages provide a way to define and manipulate complex relationships between categories and objects.
