### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval is a key technology for knowledge management. It deals with the search for information and the representation, storage, and organization of knowledge .
- Artificial intelligence technologies can be applied to information retrieval (IR) .
- A simple model of an information retrieval system provides a framework for subsequent discussion of artificial intelligence concepts and their applicability in information retrieval .
- Concepts surveyed include pattern recognition, representation, problem-solving and planning, heuristics, and learning .
- AI applications include Language Models, Information Retrieval, Information Extraction, Natural Language Processing, Machine Translation, and Speech .
