### Constraint Propagation

Constraint propagation is a technique used in artificial intelligence and constraint programming to reduce the search space of a problem by applying constraints to the variables. This technique is used to solve problems such as scheduling, resource allocation, and optimization.

Here are some key points to remember about constraint propagation:

1. Constraint propagation is the process of using constraints to reduce the search space of a problem.
2. It is used to solve problems such as scheduling, resource allocation, and optimization.
3. The goal of constraint propagation is to reduce the number of possible solutions to a problem, making it easier to find a solution.
4. Constraint propagation can be used in combination with other techniques such as search and inference to solve complex problems.
5. There are several algorithms for constraint propagation, including arc consistency, path consistency, and i-consistency.
