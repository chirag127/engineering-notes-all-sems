## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

1. Knowledge representation is the study of how knowledge about the world can be represented and what kinds of reasoning can be done with that knowledge.
2. It is a fundamental concept in artificial intelligence, as it is the way that an AI system stores and manipulates information.
3. There are several methods of knowledge representation, including:
    - **Semantic Networks**: A graphical representation of concepts and their relationships.
    - **Frames**: A data structure that represents a stereotypical situation.
    - **Rule-Based Systems**: A set of rules that define relationships between concepts and can be used for reasoning.
    - **Ontologies**: A formal representation of a set of concepts and their relationships within a domain.
4. The choice of knowledge representation method depends on the specific problem and the type of reasoning required.
5. Knowledge representation is an active area of research, with ongoing work on developing new methods and improving existing ones.
