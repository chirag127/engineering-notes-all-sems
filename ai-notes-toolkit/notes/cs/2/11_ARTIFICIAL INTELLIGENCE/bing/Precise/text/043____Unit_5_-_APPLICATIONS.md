## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

1. Applications of mathematics in various fields such as science, engineering, economics, and finance.
2. Use of mathematical models to solve real-world problems.
3. Applications of calculus in optimization, rate of change, and modeling.
4. Applications of statistics in data analysis, prediction, and decision making.
5. Applications of linear algebra in computer graphics, machine learning, and cryptography.
6. Applications of discrete mathematics in computer science, operations research, and combinatorics.
7. Applications of geometry in architecture, art, and design.
