### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are problem-solving techniques that help to find a solution more quickly when classic methods are too slow or fail to find an exact solution.
- Heuristics are used to speed up the process of finding a satisfactory solution, where an exhaustive search is impractical.
- Heuristics can be used to solve problems that are difficult to solve by other methods, such as problems with a large search space, incomplete or uncertain information, or limited computation time.
- Heuristics can be classified into two types: constructive heuristics and improvement heuristics.
- Constructive heuristics build a solution from scratch, while improvement heuristics improve an existing solution.
- Examples of heuristics include the nearest neighbor algorithm, the greedy algorithm, and the hill-climbing algorithm.
- Heuristics can be used in various fields, including artificial intelligence, operations research, and computer science.
- Heuristics are not guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but they can often find a good solution in a reasonable amount of time.
- Heuristics can be combined with other techniques, such as exact algorithms or metaheuristics, to improve their performance.
- The effectiveness of a heuristic depends on the problem and the specific heuristic used. It is important to choose the right heuristic for the problem at hand.
