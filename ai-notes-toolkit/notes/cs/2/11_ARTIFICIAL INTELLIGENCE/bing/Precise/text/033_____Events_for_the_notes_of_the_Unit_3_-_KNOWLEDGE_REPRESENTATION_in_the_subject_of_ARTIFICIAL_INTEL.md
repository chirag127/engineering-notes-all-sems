### Events for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge representation is a field of artificial intelligence that focuses on designing computer representations that capture information about the world that can be used to solve complex problems.
2. Knowledge representation techniques include semantic networks, frames, rules, and ontologies.
3. Semantic networks represent knowledge as a graph, where nodes represent concepts and edges represent relationships between concepts.
4. Frames are data structures that represent objects or events and their properties and relationships.
5. Rules are used to represent knowledge in the form of if-then statements.
6. Ontologies are formal representations of knowledge that define the concepts and relationships within a domain.
7. Knowledge representation is important for reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making in artificial intelligence systems.
8. Effective knowledge representation requires the ability to represent incomplete, uncertain, and inconsistent information.
9. Knowledge representation is an active area of research, with ongoing work on developing new techniques and improving existing ones.
