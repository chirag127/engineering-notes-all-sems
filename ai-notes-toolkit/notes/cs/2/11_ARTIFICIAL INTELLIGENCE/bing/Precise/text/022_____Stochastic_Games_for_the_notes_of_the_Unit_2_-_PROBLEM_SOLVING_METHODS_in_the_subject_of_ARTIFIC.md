### Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game theory model that extends the concept of a normal form game to include probabilistic transitions between states. In a stochastic game, the game transitions between different states based on the actions of the players and the outcome of random events. The payoffs to the players depend on the current state of the game and the actions taken by the players.

Some key points to remember about stochastic games are:

- Stochastic games are dynamic and involve multiple stages.
- The game transitions between different states based on the actions of the players and the outcome of random events.
- The payoffs to the players depend on the current state of the game and the actions taken by the players.
- Stochastic games can be used to model a wide range of real-world situations, including economic, political, and social interactions.

Stochastic games are an important tool in the study of artificial intelligence and problem-solving methods. They provide a framework for analyzing complex, dynamic situations and for developing strategies for decision-making under uncertainty. By understanding the principles of stochastic games, AI researchers and practitioners can develop more effective algorithms and systems for solving real-world problems.
