### Ontological Engineering

- Ontological engineering is a field that studies the methods and methodologies for building ontologies, which are formal and explicit specifications of the concepts, properties and relations in a domain of interest .
- Ontologies can be used to enhance the semantic interoperability, reusability and integration of data and information resources, as well as to support automated reasoning and intelligent applications .
- Ontological engineering involves the following activities:
  - Ontology development: the process of creating, modifying, evaluating and maintaining ontologies for a specific purpose or domain.
  - Ontology life cycle: the stages of an ontology from its inception to its retirement, such as specification, design, implementation, evaluation, documentation, evolution and reuse.
  - Ontology methods and methodologies: the principles, guidelines, best practices and standards for ontology engineering, such as ontology design patterns, ontology engineering frameworks and ontology quality metrics.
  - Ontology languages and tools: the formalisms, syntaxes, semantics and software that support the representation, manipulation and reasoning with ontologies, such as OWL, RDF, Protégé and OntoEdit.
