### Information Extraction

- Information extraction (IE) is the process of automatically extracting structured data from unstructured or semi-structured documents, such as text, images, audio, or video.
- IE can be used for various applications, such as:
  - Document analysis: IE can help analyze, search, and store the information contained in documents, such as invoices, contracts, receipts, forms, etc. IE can also help digitize paper-based records and convert them into structured data.
  - Knowledge base construction: IE can help build and update knowledge bases from various sources, such as web pages, news articles, social media posts, etc. IE can also help extract entities, relations, facts, and events from the text and link them to existing knowledge bases.
  - Question answering: IE can help provide answers to natural language questions by extracting relevant information from documents or knowledge bases. IE can also help generate summaries, paraphrases, or explanations for the answers.
  - Sentiment analysis: IE can help identify and extract the opinions, emotions, attitudes, and preferences of the authors or speakers from the text or speech. IE can also help classify the polarity, intensity, and aspect of the sentiment.
  - Information retrieval: IE can help improve the effectiveness and efficiency of information retrieval systems by extracting and indexing the key information from the documents. IE can also help rank and filter the retrieved documents based on the user's query and preferences.
- IE can be performed by using various artificial intelligence (AI) techniques, such as:
  - Optical character recognition (OCR): OCR is the process of converting images of text into machine-readable text. OCR can help extract text from scanned documents, handwritten notes, signs, etc.
  - Natural language processing (NLP): NLP is the process of analyzing and understanding natural language text or speech. NLP can help extract various types of information from the text or speech, such as named entities, keywords, topics, sentiments, etc.
  - Machine learning (ML): ML is the process of learning from data and making predictions or decisions based on the data. ML can help extract information from documents or knowledge bases by using supervised, unsupervised, or semi-supervised learning methods. ML can also help adapt to new domains, languages, or formats of the documents.
  - Computer vision (CV): CV is the process of analyzing and understanding images or videos. CV can help extract information from images or videos, such as faces, objects, scenes, actions, etc.
  - Speech recognition (SR): SR is the process of converting speech into text. SR can help extract information from speech, such as words, phrases, intents, emotions, etc.
