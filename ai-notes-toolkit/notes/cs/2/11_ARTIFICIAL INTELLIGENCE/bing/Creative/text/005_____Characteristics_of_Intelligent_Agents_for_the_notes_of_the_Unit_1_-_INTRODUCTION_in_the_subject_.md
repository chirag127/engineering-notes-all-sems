### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents can be used for various applications, such as information retrieval, decision support, automation, and collaboration. Some of the common characteristics of intelligent agents are:

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct intervention from humans or other agents. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.  
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its own experience and from feedback from its environment. It can improve its performance and behavior over time by adjusting its knowledge, skills, and strategies.  
- **Situativeness**: An intelligent agent can perceive its environment and gather relevant information through sensors or other forms of input. It can also act on its environment and change it through effectors or other forms of output.  
- **Sociability**: An intelligent agent can interact with other agents, humans, or systems through communication, cooperation, coordination, or competition. It can also follow social norms and protocols that govern its interactions.  
- **Goal-orientation**: An intelligent agent can pursue its own goals and objectives that are derived from its preferences, beliefs, and desires. It can also plan and execute actions that are consistent with its goals and rationality.  
- **Knowledge-based**: An intelligent agent can store and manipulate knowledge that represents its beliefs, rules, facts, and concepts. It can also reason and infer new knowledge from existing knowledge and evidence.
