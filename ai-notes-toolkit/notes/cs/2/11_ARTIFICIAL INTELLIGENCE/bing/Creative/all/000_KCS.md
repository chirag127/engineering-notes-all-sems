# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology for service organizations to work more effectively with knowledge. KCS has the following benefits:

- It improves service delivery by capturing, structuring, and reusing knowledge as part of the support process.
- It increases productivity by reducing the need to rediscover or recreate knowledge that already exists.
- It decreases costs by minimizing the time and resources spent on solving repetitive issues.
- It enhances customer satisfaction by providing faster and more accurate solutions.
- It fosters employee engagement by empowering them to contribute and access knowledge.
- It enables organizational learning by creating a feedback loop between knowledge and performance.

KCS is based on four principles:

- Integrate: Knowledge creation and maintenance should be integrated into the workflow of solving issues, not a separate task.
- Evolve: Knowledge should be continuously improved and updated based on demand and usage.
- Collaborate: Knowledge should be shared and validated by a community of experts and peers, not by a central authority.
- Reward: Knowledge participation and quality should be recognized and incentivized, not mandated or enforced.

KCS follows a double-loop process that consists of two practices:

- Solve Loop: This is the practice of creating and using knowledge while solving issues for customers or users. It involves four steps: Capture, Structure, Reuse, and Improve.
- Evolve Loop: This is the practice of measuring and improving the health and effectiveness of the knowledge base and the KCS process. It involves four steps: Content Health, Process Integration, Performance Assessment, and Leadership and Communication.

KCS is not to be confused with KCS, the abbreviation for Kansas City Southern, a US and cross-border railroad company.
