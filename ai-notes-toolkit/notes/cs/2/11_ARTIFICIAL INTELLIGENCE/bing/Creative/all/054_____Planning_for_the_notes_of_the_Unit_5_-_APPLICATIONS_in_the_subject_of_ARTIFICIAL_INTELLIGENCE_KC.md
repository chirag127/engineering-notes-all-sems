# Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

## Introduction
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that studies how to create machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, perception, decision making, and natural language processing.
- AI has many applications in various domains, such as medicine, education, entertainment, business, security, and social good.
- In this unit, we will explore some of the common and emerging applications of AI, such as expert systems, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, machine learning, and artificial neural networks.

## Expert Systems
- An expert system is a computer program that emulates the knowledge and reasoning of a human expert in a specific domain, such as medical diagnosis, legal advice, or engineering design.
- An expert system consists of two main components: a knowledge base and an inference engine.
- The knowledge base stores the facts and rules that represent the domain knowledge of the expert, such as symptoms and diseases, legal precedents and statutes, or design principles and constraints.
- The inference engine applies logical rules to the knowledge base to derive new facts and conclusions, such as diagnosing a patient, recommending a legal action, or generating a design solution.
- An expert system can interact with a user through a natural language interface, such as a dialogue system or a question answering system, to provide explanations and justifications for its reasoning and recommendations.
- Some examples of expert systems are MYCIN, a medical diagnosis system for infectious diseases, DENDRAL, a chemical analysis system for organic compounds, and R1/XCON, a configuration system for computer hardware.

## Natural Language Processing
- Natural language processing (NLP) is the branch of AI that deals with the analysis and generation of natural languages, such as English, Chinese, or Hindi.
- NLP aims to enable machines to understand and communicate with humans using natural languages, as well as to extract useful information and insights from large amounts of natural language data, such as text and speech.
- NLP involves various subtasks, such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, natural language understanding, natural language generation, machine translation, sentiment analysis, text summarization, information extraction, and question answering.
- NLP relies on various techniques and models, such as grammar rules, statistical methods, machine learning algorithms, and neural networks, to process and produce natural language data.
- Some examples of NLP applications are Siri, a virtual assistant that can understand and respond to voice commands, Google Translate, a service that can translate text and speech between different languages, and GPT-3, a neural network that can generate coherent and diverse text on various topics.
