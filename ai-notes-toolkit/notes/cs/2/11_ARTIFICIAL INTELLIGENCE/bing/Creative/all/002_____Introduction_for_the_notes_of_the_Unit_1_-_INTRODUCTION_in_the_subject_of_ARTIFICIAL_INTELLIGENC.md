# Introduction

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that deals with creating machines or software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, natural language processing, computer vision, speech recognition, etc.
- AI can be classified into two main categories: weak AI and strong AI. Weak AI, also known as narrow AI, is the type of AI that is designed to perform a specific task or domain, such as playing chess, driving a car, or diagnosing diseases. Strong AI, also known as general AI or artificial general intelligence (AGI), is the type of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can do, such as understanding natural language, solving complex problems, or creating original art. Weak AI is currently more prevalent and achievable than strong AI, which is still a hypothetical and elusive goal.
- AI can also be classified into four types based on the degree of human-like intelligence and rationality they exhibit: systems that think like humans, systems that act like humans, systems that think rationally, and systems that act rationally. Systems that think like humans are those that try to mimic the cognitive processes and structures of the human mind, such as neural networks, expert systems, or natural language understanding. Systems that act like humans are those that try to behave in a way that is indistinguishable from a human, such as robots, chatbots, or Turing test participants. Systems that think rationally are those that try to follow the principles of logic, mathematics, or optimization, such as theorem provers, decision support systems, or game-playing agents. Systems that act rationally are those that try to achieve their goals in the best possible way, given their knowledge and resources, such as planning systems, autonomous vehicles, or recommender systems.
- AI can also be classified into three levels based on the scope and complexity of the tasks they can perform: artificial narrow intelligence (ANI), artificial general intelligence (AGI), and artificial superintelligence (ASI). ANI is the level of AI that can perform a single or a few tasks at or above human level, such as face recognition, spam filtering, or chess playing. AGI is the level of AI that can perform any task that a human can do, such as natural language understanding, common sense reasoning, or creativity. ASI is the level of AI that can perform any task that a human or any other intelligence can do, and surpass them in speed, accuracy, and efficiency, such as inventing new technologies, solving global problems, or manipulating reality.
- AI is a multidisciplinary field that draws from various domains of knowledge, such as computer science, mathematics, statistics, psychology, linguistics, philosophy, neuroscience, biology, etc. AI also has many subfields and applications, such as machine learning, data mining, computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition, robotics, expert systems, knowledge representation and reasoning, planning and scheduling, search and optimization, etc. AI has a significant impact on various aspects of human society, such as education, health, entertainment, commerce, security, etc.
