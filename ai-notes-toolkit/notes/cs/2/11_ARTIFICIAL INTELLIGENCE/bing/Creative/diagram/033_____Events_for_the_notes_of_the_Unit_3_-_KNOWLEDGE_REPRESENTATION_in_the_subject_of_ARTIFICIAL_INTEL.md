### Events

- Events are the occurrence of things in the real world .
- Events are actions that involve changes in the state of the world or the relationships between entities.
- Events are an essential kind of knowledge in the world, which trigger the spring up of event-centric knowledge representation form like Event KG (EKG).
- Events can be represented by using various techniques, such as:
  - Predicate logic: A formal language that uses symbols and operators to express the logical structure of sentences and events.
  - Scripts: A structured representation of stereotypical sequences of actions that occur in a specific context.
  - Frames: A data structure that organizes information about a concept or an event using slots and values.
  - Ontologies: A formal specification of the concepts, relations, and axioms that define a domain of knowledge.
  - Event calculus: A logic-based formalism that represents events and their effects using fluents, actions, and constraints.
