### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are techniques that are used to solve a problem or to make a decision, where traditional methods would be too time-consuming or impractical .
- Heuristics are often used as an approximation to an ideal solution, where the hope is that the approximate solution is good enough for the specific problem at hand.
- Heuristics are an inevitable and inseparable part of artificial intelligence. They are a computer simulation of the human thinking process, used in situations no known algorithm can reach.
- Heuristics are generally used in conjunction with optimization algorithms to enhance the overall efficiency of the desired results.
- Heuristics can be classified into different types, such as:
  - **Constructive heuristics**: These heuristics build a solution incrementally, adding one element at a time, until a complete solution is obtained or a stopping criterion is met. For example, the greedy algorithm for the traveling salesman problem.
  - **Improvement heuristics**: These heuristics start with an initial solution, and then try to modify it iteratively to improve its quality, until a local or global optimum is reached or a stopping criterion is met. For example, the hill climbing algorithm for the n-queens problem.
  - **Metaheuristics**: These heuristics are general frameworks that can be applied to different problems, and that use other heuristics as subroutines. They aim to escape from local optima and explore the search space more effectively. For example, the simulated annealing algorithm for the knapsack problem.
  - **Hybrid heuristics**: These heuristics combine two or more heuristics of different types, to exploit their strengths and overcome their weaknesses. For example, the genetic algorithm for the graph coloring problem.
