### Moving for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of a machine, program or system to learn and adapt by experience.
- AI can be classified into two types: artificial narrow intelligence (ANI) and artificial general intelligence (AGI).
  - ANI, or weak AI, is a type of AI that can only focus on one specific task or problem at a time. Examples of ANI include speech recognition, face recognition, chess playing, spam filtering, etc.
  - AGI, or strong AI, is the type of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. Examples of AGI include human-like robots, self-aware machines, artificial consciousness, etc. AGI is still a hypothetical concept and has not been achieved yet.
- AI has a multitude of applications in various domains, such as health care, finance, national security, criminal justice, transportation, e-commerce, etc .
  - In health care, AI can be used for diagnosis, treatment, drug discovery, personalized medicine, etc. For example, IBM Watson is a cognitive system that can analyze large amounts of medical data and provide evidence-based recommendations for doctors.
  - In finance, AI can be used for credit scoring, fraud detection, trading, portfolio management, etc. For example, robo-advisors are automated platforms that can provide financial advice and investment services to customers based on their goals and risk preferences.
  - In national security, AI can be used for surveillance, cyberdefense, warfare, counterterrorism, etc. For example, drones are unmanned aerial vehicles that can be controlled remotely or autonomously and can perform various military missions.
  - In criminal justice, AI can be used for crime prediction, prevention, detection, investigation, etc. For example, facial recognition is a technology that can identify and verify a person's identity from a digital image or a video.
  - In transportation, AI can be used for navigation, traffic management, autonomous driving, etc. For example, self-driving cars are vehicles that can operate without human intervention and can sense their environment and make decisions.
  - In e-commerce, AI can be used for product recommendation, customer service, pricing, marketing, etc. For example, Amazon is an online retailer that uses AI to analyze customer behavior and preferences and to offer personalized suggestions and deals.
- AI is evolving rapidly and has many benefits and challenges for society.
  - Some of the benefits of AI include improving efficiency, productivity, quality, innovation, safety, etc.
  - Some of the challenges of AI include ethical, legal, social, economic, security, etc. For example, AI may raise questions about privacy, accountability, fairness, transparency, human dignity, etc.
