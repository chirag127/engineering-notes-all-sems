### AI applications

- AI applications are the systems or software that use artificial intelligence techniques to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, decision making, perception, etc.
- AI applications can be classified into different categories based on the domain, the technique, the function, or the level of intelligence they exhibit.
- Some of the common categories of AI applications are:

  - Expert systems: These are AI applications that emulate the knowledge and reasoning of human experts in a specific domain, such as medicine, law, engineering, etc. They use a knowledge base and an inference engine to provide answers or solutions to user queries or problems. Examples of expert systems are MYCIN, DENDRAL, PROSPECTOR, etc.
  - Natural language processing: These are AI applications that enable computers to understand, analyze, generate, or translate natural languages, such as English, Hindi, Chinese, etc. They use techniques such as parsing, semantic analysis, machine translation, speech recognition, natural language generation, etc. Examples of natural language processing applications are Google Translate, Siri, Alexa, etc.
  - Computer vision: These are AI applications that enable computers to perceive, recognize, analyze, or manipulate visual information, such as images, videos, faces, objects, etc. They use techniques such as image processing, pattern recognition, face detection, object recognition, scene understanding, etc. Examples of computer vision applications are face recognition systems, self-driving cars, medical image analysis, etc.
  - Machine learning: These are AI applications that enable computers to learn from data or experience without explicit programming. They use techniques such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning, deep learning, etc. Examples of machine learning applications are spam filtering, recommender systems, fraud detection, etc.
  - Robotics: These are AI applications that involve the design, construction, operation, or control of machines that can perform physical tasks, such as moving, manipulating, sensing, etc. They use techniques such as planning, navigation, localization, coordination, etc. Examples of robotics applications are industrial robots, service robots, humanoid robots, etc.
  - Artificial neural networks: These are AI applications that use a network of interconnected nodes or units that mimic the structure and function of biological neurons. They can learn from data or experience and perform tasks such as classification, regression, clustering, etc. Examples of artificial neural networks are feedforward neural networks, recurrent neural networks, convolutional neural networks, etc.
  - Fuzzy logic: These are AI applications that use a form of logic that can handle uncertainty, vagueness, or imprecision. They use fuzzy sets, fuzzy rules, and fuzzy inference to model and reason with linguistic or numerical variables that have degrees of membership or truth. Examples of fuzzy logic applications are fuzzy control systems, fuzzy expert systems, fuzzy decision making, etc.
  - Genetic algorithms: These are AI applications that use a form of optimization or search technique that mimics the process of natural evolution. They use a population of candidate solutions that undergo selection, crossover, and mutation to find the optimal or near-optimal solution to a given problem. Examples of genetic algorithms applications are function optimization, scheduling, design, etc.
