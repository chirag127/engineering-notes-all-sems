### Events

- Events are the occurrence of things in the real world .
- Events are the actions which occur in our world.
- Events are an essential kind of knowledge in the world.
- Events trigger the spring up of event-centric knowledge representation form like Event KG (EKG).
- Events can be represented by symbolic representations of objects and events which are not immediately present but exist only in imagination.
- Events can be used to solve complex tasks such as diagnosing a medical condition or having a dialog in a natural language.
- Events can be classified into different types, such as atomic events, composite events, simple events, complex events, etc.
- Events can be related to each other by temporal, causal, or logical relations.
- Events can be extracted from natural language texts, images, videos, or other sources of data.
- Events can be stored, queried, and reasoned with using various techniques of knowledge representation and reasoning .
