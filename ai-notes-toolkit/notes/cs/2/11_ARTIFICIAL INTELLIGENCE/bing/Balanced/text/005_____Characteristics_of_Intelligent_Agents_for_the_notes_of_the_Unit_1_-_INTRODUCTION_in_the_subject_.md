### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents can be used for various applications, such as information retrieval, decision support, automation, and entertainment. Some of the common characteristics of intelligent agents are:

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct intervention from humans or other agents. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing situations and cope with uncertainty and incomplete information.
- **Interaction**: An intelligent agent can communicate and cooperate with other agents, humans, and systems. It can use various modes of interaction, such as natural language, gestures, or graphical user interfaces.
- **Knowledge**: An intelligent agent can store and manipulate knowledge about its environment, its tasks, and itself. It can use various forms of knowledge representation, such as rules, logic, or neural networks.
- **Goal-orientation**: An intelligent agent can pursue its own objectives and act rationally to achieve them. It can also balance multiple and conflicting goals and handle trade-offs and preferences.
