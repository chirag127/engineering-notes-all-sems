### Ontological Engineering

- Ontological Engineering is a field of study that deals with the creation and management of ontologies, which are formal and explicit specifications of the concepts, properties and relations in a domain of interest .
- Ontologies are used to provide a common vocabulary and a shared understanding of a domain, to enable interoperability and integration of heterogeneous data and systems, and to support reasoning and inference over the domain knowledge .
- Ontological Engineering involves the following activities:
  - Ontology development process: the steps and phases for creating an ontology, such as analysis, design, implementation, evaluation, documentation and maintenance.
  - Ontology life cycle: the stages and transitions of an ontology from its inception to its retirement, such as creation, evolution, versioning, reuse and alignment.
  - Methods and methodologies for building ontologies: the principles, guidelines and best practices for ontology engineering, such as ontology design patterns, ontology learning, ontology merging and ontology modularization.
  - Tool suites and languages that support ontologies: the software and standards that facilitate ontology engineering, such as ontology editors, ontology repositories, ontology languages and ontology reasoners.
