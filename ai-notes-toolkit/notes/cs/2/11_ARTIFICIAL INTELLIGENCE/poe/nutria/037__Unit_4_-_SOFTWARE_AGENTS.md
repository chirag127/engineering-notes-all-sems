
## Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS

Software agents are computer programs that are designed to autonomously perform tasks on behalf of a user or organization. They are generally autonomous, meaning they are able to work independently and do not require direct input from a user. Agents may be used to automate mundane tasks, such as web searches, or to provide more complex services, such as customer support or financial advice.

Software agents are typically designed to be intelligent, meaning they can learn from their environment and adapt their behavior accordingly. They can also be designed to be proactive, meaning they can take initiative and act on their own.

Software agents can be divided into two main categories: reactive agents and proactive agents. Reactive agents are designed to respond to events in their environment, while proactive agents are designed to take the initiative and act on their own.

Software agents can be used in a variety of applications, including web search, e-commerce, customer service, health care, finance, and many more. They can also be used to automate mundane tasks, such as web searches, or to provide more complex services, such as customer support or financial advice. In addition, software agents can be used to monitor and control complex systems, such as manufacturing plants or power grids.
