
### Searching with Partial Observations

* Searching with partial observations is a problem solving method used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help find solutions in cases where all of the information is not known. 
* This method is based on the idea that a solution can be found by making small changes and observing the effects of those changes on the problem. 
* It is a type of trial and error approach that is used to help find solutions when all the information is not known.
* In this method, the AI system starts with an initial guess at a solution and then makes small changes to the solution to see what effect they have on the problem. 
* If the changes improve the solution, then the system will continue to make small changes until a solution is found that meets the criteria set by the user.
* This method is often used in AI systems that are trying to solve a problem where all the information is not known. 
* It can be used to help find solutions to problems such as route-finding, scheduling, and game playing.
