
### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI): AI is the science and engineering of making intelligent machines, especially computer programs that can make decisions and take actions. 
2. Knowledge Representation: Knowledge Representation is the process of representing knowledge in a form that can be understood and used by machines. 
3. Machine Learning: Machine Learning is the process of learning from data, usually in the form of patterns and relationships between data points. 
4. Natural Language Processing (NLP): NLP is the process of understanding natural language, such as spoken or written language, and using it to interact with machines. 
5. Robotics: Robotics is the field of engineering and computer science that deals with the design, construction, and operation of robots. 
6. Computer Vision: Computer Vision is the field of computer science that deals with recognizing objects and scenes in images and videos. 
7. Autonomous Agents: Autonomous Agents are computer programs that can act on their own, without human intervention.
