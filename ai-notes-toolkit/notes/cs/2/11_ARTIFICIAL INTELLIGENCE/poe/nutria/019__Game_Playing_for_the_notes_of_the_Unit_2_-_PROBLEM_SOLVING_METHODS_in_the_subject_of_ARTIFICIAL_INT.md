
### Game Playing

Game playing is a form of problem solving in Artificial Intelligence (AI). It involves designing a system that can play a game against an opponent. It is an important area of AI research, as it can be used to test different algorithms and strategies for problem solving.

* Games provide a well-defined structure for problem solving. This makes them ideal for testing AI algorithms.
* Games can be used to evaluate the performance of AI algorithms in a competitive environment.
* Games can provide insight into how humans think and act when faced with a problem.
* Games can be used to develop strategies and tactics for problem solving.
* Games can be used to explore the limits of AI algorithms.
* Games can be used to develop AI algorithms that are more robust and reliable.
