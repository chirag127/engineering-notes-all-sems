
## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

* Knowledge Representation is the process of encoding knowledge and facts into a computer-readable format.
* It is used to store, manipulate, and reason about knowledge and data.
* Knowledge Representation is a key part of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and is used to enable machines to make decisions and solve problems.
* There are many different techniques used for Knowledge Representation, including rule-based systems, semantic networks, frames, and ontologies.
* Rule-based systems are used to represent knowledge as a set of rules or if-then statements.
* Semantic networks are used to represent knowledge as a graph of interconnected nodes and edges.
* Frames are used to represent knowledge as a set of objects and their attributes.
* Ontologies are used to represent knowledge as a hierarchical structure of concepts and relationships.
* Knowledge Representation is an important tool for AI applications, such as natural language processing, expert systems, and robotics.
