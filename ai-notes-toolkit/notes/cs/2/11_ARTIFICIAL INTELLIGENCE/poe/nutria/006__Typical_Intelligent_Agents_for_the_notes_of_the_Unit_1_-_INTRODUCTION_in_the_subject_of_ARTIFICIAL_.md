
### Typical Intelligent Agents

1. **Expert Systems**: Expert systems are computer programs that are designed to emulate the decision-making capabilities of a human expert. Expert systems use a combination of artificial intelligence and knowledge engineering techniques to solve complex problems.

2. **Neural Networks**: Neural networks are computer programs that are designed to mimic the behavior of neurons in the human brain. They are used to solve complex problems, such as pattern recognition, classification, and prediction.

3. **Fuzzy Logic**: Fuzzy logic is a form of artificial intelligence that is based on the concept of fuzzy sets. It is used to solve problems that involve uncertainty and imprecise data.

4. **Genetic Algorithms**: Genetic algorithms are computer programs that use evolutionary principles to solve problems. They use the principles of natural selection and genetic mutation to find solutions to complex problems.

5. **Natural Language Processing**: Natural language processing is a form of artificial intelligence that is used to process natural language. It is used to understand and interpret the meaning of sentences and phrases.
