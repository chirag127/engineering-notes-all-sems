
### Heuristics for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. Heuristics are rules of thumb used to solve problems. They are usually based on experience or intuition and are not guaranteed to work in every case.

2. Heuristics can be used to reduce the search space of a problem by eliminating some possible solutions.

3. Heuristics can be used to guide the search for a solution to a problem by suggesting possible solutions or directions that should be explored.

4. Heuristics are often used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help solve difficult problems.

5. Heuristics can be used to evaluate the quality of a solution by assigning a score to each solution.

6. Heuristics can be used to improve the performance of an AI system by allowing it to make decisions more quickly.

7. Heuristics can be used to help an AI system learn by providing it with feedback on its performance.
