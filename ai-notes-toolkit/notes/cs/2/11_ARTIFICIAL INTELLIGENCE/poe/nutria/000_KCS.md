
# Knowledge Centered Support (KCS)

KCS is a methodology for providing knowledge-based customer support. It focuses on creating, organizing, and sharing knowledge to improve customer service processes. KCS is based on the idea that knowledge should be a core part of the customer service process.

KCS is a methodology that provides a structured approach to creating, organizing, and sharing knowledge. It is based on the idea that knowledge should be a core part of the customer service process.

KCS focuses on the following principles:

1. Knowledge is the most important asset.
2. Knowledge should be created, organized, and shared.
3. Knowledge should be used to improve customer service processes.
4. Knowledge should be used to reduce the cost of customer service.
5. Knowledge should be used to reduce customer effort.
6. Knowledge should be used to improve customer satisfaction.
