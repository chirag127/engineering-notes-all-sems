
### Reasoning with Default Information

* Default reasoning is a form of non-monotonic reasoning which allows conclusions to be drawn even when all premises are not known to be true.
* It is based on the idea that a conclusion can be assumed to be true unless there is evidence to the contrary.
* Default reasoning is used in artificial intelligence (AI) to draw conclusions from incomplete information, and is used in many aspects of AI, such as natural language processing, planning, and knowledge representation.
* Default reasoning is based on the assumption that the information provided is correct and complete.
* Default reasoning is used to draw conclusions from incomplete information, by making assumptions about what is not known.
* Default reasoning is used to infer new facts from existing facts, by making assumptions about what is not known.
* Default reasoning is used to make decisions based on incomplete or uncertain information, by making assumptions about what is not known.
