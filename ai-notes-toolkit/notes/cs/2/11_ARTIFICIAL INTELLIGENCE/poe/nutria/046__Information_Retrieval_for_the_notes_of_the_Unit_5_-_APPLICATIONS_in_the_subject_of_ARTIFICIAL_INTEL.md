
### Information Retrieval 

Information Retrieval is the process of locating relevant documents related to a user's query from a collection of documents. It is a core component of many Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications, such as search engines and question-answering systems.

Information Retrieval can be divided into two main categories:

* **Unstructured Information Retrieval**: This involves searching through large unstructured collections of documents, such as webpages, to locate relevant documents. This type of retrieval typically relies on keyword-based search techniques.

* **Structured Information Retrieval**: This involves searching through structured collections of documents, such as databases, to locate relevant documents. This type of retrieval typically relies on techniques such as Boolean search and natural language processing.

In addition to these two main categories, there are also other approaches to Information Retrieval, such as the use of ontologies and semantic networks.

Information Retrieval is a key component of many AI applications, such as search engines, question-answering systems, and recommendation systems. It is also used in areas such as data mining, natural language processing, and machine learning.
