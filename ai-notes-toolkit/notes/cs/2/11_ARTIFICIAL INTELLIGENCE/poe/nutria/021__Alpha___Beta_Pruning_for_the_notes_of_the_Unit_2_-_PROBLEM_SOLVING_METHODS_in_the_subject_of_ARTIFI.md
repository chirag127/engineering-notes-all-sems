
### Alpha – Beta Pruning 

Alpha – Beta pruning is an algorithm used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) for game playing. It is a search algorithm that seeks to reduce the number of nodes that are evaluated by the minimax algorithm in its search tree. It is an optimization technique for minimax algorithms.

**Advantages:** 
- Alpha–Beta pruning can reduce the number of nodes that are evaluated by the minimax algorithm. 
- It can also improve the speed of the game playing algorithm by several orders of magnitude. 
- Alpha–Beta pruning can also be used to solve problems with a large search space.

**Disadvantages:**
- Alpha–Beta pruning can be computationally expensive. 
- It can also be difficult to implement correctly. 
- Alpha–Beta pruning is also limited to two-player games.

**Application:**
- Alpha–Beta pruning is used in game playing algorithms such as chess, checkers, and Othello. 
- It is also used in AI-based decision making systems such as expert systems. 
- Alpha–Beta pruning is also used in robotics, natural language processing, and other AI-based applications.
