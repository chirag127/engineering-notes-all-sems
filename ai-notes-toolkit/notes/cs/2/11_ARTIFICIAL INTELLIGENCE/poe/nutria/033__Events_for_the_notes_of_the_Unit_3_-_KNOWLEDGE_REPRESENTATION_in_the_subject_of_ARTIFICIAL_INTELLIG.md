
### Events for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. In 1956, John McCarthy coined the term "Artificial Intelligence" to describe the study of how to make computers think.

2. In 1966, the first AI-based chess-playing program was developed by Arthur Samuel.

3. In 1969, the first expert system was developed, called Dendral.

4. In 1974, the first AI-based natural language processing system was developed, called SHRDLU.

5. In 1979, the first AI-based robotic system was developed, called Shakey.

6. In 1981, the first AI-based computer vision system was developed, called the Stanford Cart.

7. In 1982, the first AI-based machine learning system was developed, called the ID3 algorithm.

8. In 1986, the first AI-based neural network system was developed, called the Backpropagation algorithm.

9. In 1997, the first AI-based autonomous vehicle was developed, called the Stanford Cart.

10. In 2005, the first AI-based computer game playing program was developed, called Deep Blue.
