## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

In this unit, we will be discussing various problem-solving methods that can be used to approach and solve a problem. These methods will help you to become a more efficient problem solver and tackle complex problems with ease. Here are some of the problem-solving methods that we will be covering in this unit:

### 1. Trial and Error Method
- This method involves trying different solutions until a suitable solution is found.
- It is a simple and straightforward method that can be used for solving simple problems.
- However, it can be time-consuming and may not always result in a solution.

### 2. Brainstorming Method
- This method involves generating a large number of ideas and solutions to a problem.
- It encourages creativity and helps to generate new ideas that may not have been considered before.
- It is a useful method for solving complex problems that require innovative solutions.

### 3. Root Cause Analysis Method
- This method involves identifying the underlying cause of a problem.
- It helps to identify the root cause of the problem, which can then be addressed to solve the problem.
- It is a useful method for solving recurring problems.

### 4. Fishbone Diagram Method
- This method involves creating a diagram that identifies the possible causes of a problem.
- It helps to visualize the different factors that may be contributing to the problem.
- It is a useful method for solving complex problems that have multiple contributing factors.

### 5. Pareto Analysis Method
- This method involves identifying the most significant causes of a problem.
- It helps to prioritize the most important factors that need to be addressed to solve the problem.
- It is a useful method for solving problems that have multiple contributing factors.

By learning and applying these problem-solving methods, you will become a more efficient problem solver and be able to tackle complex problems with ease.
