### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. CPUs: Central Processing Units are the brain of any computer system. They are responsible for executing program instructions. CPUs with higher clock speed and number of cores are preferred for running AI applications.
2. GPUs: Graphics Processing Units handle intensive graphical tasks like gaming, video editing, etc. They have a highly parallel architecture suitable for ML algorithms which can be leveraged for faster training and inference. NVIDIA GPUs are most popular for deep learning.
3. TPUs: Tensor Processing Units are specialized AI chips designed by Google for accelerating ML workloads. They offer higher performance and efficiency than CPUs and GPUs for ML tasks.
4. FPGAs: Field Programmable Gate Arrays consist of reconfigurable hardware fabric that can be programmed to implement specific types of computations. They provide low latency and high throughput suitable for applications like image recognition.
5. ASICs: Application Specific Integrated Circuits are customized chips tailored for specific use cases. They offer superior performance and power efficiency but are more expensive to design and manufacture. Some companies like Google and Wave Computing build ASICs for AI.
