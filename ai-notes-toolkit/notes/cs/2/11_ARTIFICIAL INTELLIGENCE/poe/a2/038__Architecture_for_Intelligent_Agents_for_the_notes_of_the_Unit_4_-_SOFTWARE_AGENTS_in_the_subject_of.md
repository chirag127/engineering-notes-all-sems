### Architecture for Intelligent Agents

- Agents are software programs that perform tasks or make decisions in a complex environment.
- An intelligent agent has the following components:
- Perception component: Takes input from the environment via sensors and constructs a perceptual representation of the state of the environment.
- Learning component: Uses data from the environment to learn how to map perceptions to actions that will achieve the agent's goals.
- Memory: Stores information for the agent to use.
- Reasoning component: Uses the perceptual representation, learning components, and memory to determine the appropriate action.
- Motor component: Takes actions on the environment via actuators.
- The types of agents are:
- Simple reflex agents: Choose actions based on current percepts.
- Model-based reflex agents: Use an internal model of the world to determine actions based on percepts.
- Goal-based agents: Have explicit goals and choose actions that will achieve their goals.
- Utility-based agents: Have a measure of the utility of states and choose actions that will lead to higher utility.
