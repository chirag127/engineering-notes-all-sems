### Informed for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Uninformed Search Methods
- Breadth First Search (BFS): Searches shallowly first, then explores deeper
- Depth First Search (DFS): Searches deeply first, then explores wider
- Pros and Cons of Uninformed methods: Easy to implement but can expand nodes that don't lead to goal

2. Heuristic Search Methods
- hill-climbing: Choose neighbour with highest evaluation function
- Beam Search: Expands multiple promising nodes at each step
- A*: Combines cost to reach node and estimated cost to goal
- Pros and Cons: Heuristics can speed up search but may be inaccurate leading to missing optimal solution or getting stuck in local optima

3. Other Problem Solving Techniques
- Means-Ends Analysis: Reduce difference between current state and goal state
- Hill Climbing with Random Restarts: Repeat hill climbing multiple times from random initial states to avoid local optima
- Simulated Annealing: Allows some worsening moves to avoid getting stuck in local optima
- Genetic Algorithms: Maintain a population of solutions and evolve over time using selection, crossover and mutation
