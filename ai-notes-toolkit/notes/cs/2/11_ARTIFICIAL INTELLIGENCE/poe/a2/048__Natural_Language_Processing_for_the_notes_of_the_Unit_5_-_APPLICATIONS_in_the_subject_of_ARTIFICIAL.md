### Natural Language Processing for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of artificial intelligence that deals with the interaction between computers and humans using the natural language.
2. The objectives of NLP are:
- To enable people to interact with computers and systems in their natural language.
- To extract information and meanings from huge volumes of text data.
3. Some of the core tasks of NLP are:
- Automatic text summarization: Generating a shorter summary that retains the key information content and overall meaning of the original text.
- Sentiment analysis: Identifying subjective information and determining the sentiment expressed in a text.
- Text classification: Assigning a set of predefined categories to open-domain text.
- Named entity recognition: Identifying and classifying atomic elements like names of people, organizations, locations, expressions of times, quantities, etc.
- Relationship extraction: Identifying relationships between entities in the text.
- Question answering: Providing precise answers to user questions.
4. Some applications of NLP are:
- Information retrieval and filtering
- Machine translation
- Sentiment analysis
- Speech recognition
- Spelling and grammar correction
- Chatbots and voice assistants
