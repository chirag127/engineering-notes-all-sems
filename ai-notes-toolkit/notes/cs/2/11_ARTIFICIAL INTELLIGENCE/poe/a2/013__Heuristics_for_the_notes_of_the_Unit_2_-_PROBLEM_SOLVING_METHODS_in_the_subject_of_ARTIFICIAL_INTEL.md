### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Hill Climbing - A heuristic search algorithm to find local maxima/minima. Starts with an arbitrary solution and iteratively moves to a neighboring solution that increases/decreases the objective function value until no improvement is possible.
2. Simulated Annealing - A heuristic to avoid getting stuck at local optima. Allows for downhill moves with a probability that decreases over time. Helps explore the solution space more thoroughly than hill climbing.
3. Genetic Algorithms - A heuristic inspired by natural selection and genetics. Solves optimization problems by maintaining a population of candidate solutions and generating new solutions through processes mimicking crossover and mutation. Fitness is used to select candidates for breeding new solutions.
4. Swarm Intelligence - Heuristics that derive inspiration from the collective behavior of decentralized, self-organized systems like insect colonies/bird flocks. Examples - Ant colony optimization, Particle swarm optimization. Based on agents/particles moving around in a search space and cooperating to find good solutions.
5. Stochastic/randomized methods - Heuristics that incorporate randomness to explore the solution space more widely and avoid getting stuck at local optima. Includes simulated annealing, genetic algorithms, and other nature-inspired algorithms with stochastic components. The randomness helps avoid greediness and facilitates jumping out of local optima.
