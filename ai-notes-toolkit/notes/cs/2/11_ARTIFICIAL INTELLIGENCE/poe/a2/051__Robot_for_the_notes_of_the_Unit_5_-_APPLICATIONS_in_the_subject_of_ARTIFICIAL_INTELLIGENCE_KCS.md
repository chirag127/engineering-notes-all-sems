### Robot for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Robotics is the design and construction of robots, which are automated machines that can perform a complex series of actions automatically. Robots are used extensively in many industries today to perform tasks that are difficult, dangerous or repetitive.
2. Some applications of robots are:
- Automotive industry: Robots are used to weld car parts together and paint vehicles. They increase production and quality while reducing costs.
- Space exploration: Robots like the Mars rovers are sent to explore other planets in our solar system. They can go where humans cannot and send back valuable data and images.
- Surgery: Robotic surgery is becoming more common. Robotic systems allow surgeons more precision, control and flexibility while performing complex procedures.
- Warehouse automation: Robots are used in warehouses to move stock around, pack orders and retrieve goods. They increase efficiency and productivity.
- Military: The military uses robots for reconnaissance, surveillance, bomb disposal and combat. They can go into dangerous situations instead of putting human lives at risk.
3. The future of robotics is promising with continued advancements in artificial intelligence, sensors, computing power and mechanical designs. More applications will emerge to automate additional tasks and processes, improve various industries and enhance human lives with robotic assistants and companions. However, ethical concerns surrounding privacy, security and human relationships with robots will need to be seriously considered as the field progresses.
