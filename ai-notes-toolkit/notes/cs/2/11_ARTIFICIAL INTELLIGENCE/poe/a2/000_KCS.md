# KCS - Knowledge Centered Service

1. KCS stands for Knowledge Centered Service. It is a methodology to create, organize and manage knowledge in the form of articles for delivering consistent and high quality support to customers.
2. The key components of KCS are:
- Knowledge base: A collection of articles containing solutions to customer issues and questions.
- Content management system: A system to create, edit, organize and publish knowledge base articles.
- Writers/Editors: People responsible for authoring and maintaining the knowledge base articles.
- Subject matter experts: People with expertise in specific domains who review and contribute to knowledge base articles.
3. The key principles of KCS are:
- Capture: Continuous capturing and documenting of solutions to customer issues.
- Organize: Maintaining a logical organization structure for the knowledge base.
- Share: Making the knowledge base easily accessible to support teams and customers.
- Improve: Continuously reviewing and improving the knowledge base articles based on user feedback.
4. The benefits of implementing KCS are:
- Reduced customer support costs through less repetition and faster resolution of issues.
- Increased customer satisfaction due to fast and consistent service.
- Better utilization of expert knowledge by capturing and sharing it in the knowledge base.
- Scalability of support through the use of an organized knowledge base.
