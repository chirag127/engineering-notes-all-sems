### Language Models for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS.

1. Language models are used to predict the next word in a sentence or a sequence of words. They are trained on a large dataset of text to learn the probabilities of sequences of words.
2. The two types of language models are:
- N-gram models: They consider the previous N words to predict the next word. For example, a bi-gram model considers the previous word to predict the next word.
- Recurrent Neural Network models: They have a memory which captures the context of the entire sentence to predict the next word. LSTM and GRU are examples of RNN models used for language modeling.
3. Language models are used in applications such as:
- Speech recognition: To convert speech to text by predicting the next word in the speech.
- Machine translation: To translate sentences by predicting the next word in the translation.
- Text summarization: To predict key phrases or sentences in the text.
- Autocomplete and spell check: To predict the next word or correct spellings.
