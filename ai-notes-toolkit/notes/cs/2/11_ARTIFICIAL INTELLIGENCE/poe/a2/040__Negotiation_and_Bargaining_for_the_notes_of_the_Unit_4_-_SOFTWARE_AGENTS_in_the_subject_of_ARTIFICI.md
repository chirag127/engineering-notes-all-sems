### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS.

1. Negotiation is a process where two or more parties exchange goods, services, or other valuables to try to agree on a deal that benefits all parties.
2. Bargaining is a type of negotiation where the parties openly propose particular terms, and through compromise and concessions, try to reach agreement.
3. The key steps in negotiation are:
 - Preparation: Gather information, understand interests, determine priorities
 - Opening: First offer, establish range
 - Bargaining: Make concessions, find win-win outcomes
 - Closing: Summarize agreement, commit to action
4. Successful negotiation requires:
 - Clear goals and priorities
 - Knowledge of options and alternatives
 - Understanding other parties' needs
 - Building trust and rapport
 - Claiming value to reach win-win outcomes
 - Communication and active listening skills
5. Software agents can employ strategies such as:
 - Tit-for-tat: Start cooperatively, then mirror other's previous action
 - Generous tit-for-tat: Forgive occasional non-cooperation, then mirror
 - Win-stay, lose-shift: If winning, keep strategy; if losing, switch strategy
 - Gradual concession: Slowly concede on goals to reach agreement
 - Randomization: Include randomness in strategy to be less predictable
