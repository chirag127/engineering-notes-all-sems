### Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Understand the problem
- Identify the goal and objectives of the problem. What exactly needs to be achieved?
- Identify the input and output requirements. What data is given and what needs to be obtained?
- Determine the constraints and assumptions. Are there any restrictions on time, memory, data, etc.?

2. Develop a strategy/approach
- Determine how to break down the complex problem into simpler sub-problems.
- Decide the approach to solve the sub-problems. This could be via algorithms, machine learning, search, reasoning, etc.
- Determine the order/sequence of solving the sub-problems.

3. Implement the strategy
- Choose/develop the appropriate techniques/tools to implement the solution.
- Code/develop the solution.
- Test/validate the solution to ensure it meets the goals. Handle errors/issues.

4. Evaluate and refine
- Check if the solution meets all requirements and constraints.
- Get feedback and refine the solution.
- Generalize the solution if required.
