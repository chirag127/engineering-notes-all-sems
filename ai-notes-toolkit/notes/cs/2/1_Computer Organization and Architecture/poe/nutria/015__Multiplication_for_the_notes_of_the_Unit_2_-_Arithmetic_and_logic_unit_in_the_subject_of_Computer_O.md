4. In a sequentially-controlled architecture, the two numbers are fed into the multiplier circuit one at a time. The output of the circuit is then calculated after each number has been processed.
5. In a parallel-controlled architecture, the two numbers are fed into the multiplier circuit simultaneously. The output of the circuit is then calculated after both numbers have been processed.
6. Multiplication can also be performed using a combination of both sequentially-controlled and parallel-controlled architectures.
7. In order to perform multiplication, the two numbers must be represented in a binary format. This allows the multiplier circuit to process the two numbers and calculate the product of the two numbers.
