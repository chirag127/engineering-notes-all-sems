
### Floating Point Arithmetic Operation

* Floating point arithmetic operations involve calculations with numbers in scientific notation, where a number is represented by two parts: a mantissa and an exponent. 
* The mantissa is the part of the number that contains the significant digits, and the exponent is a power of 10 that determines the magnitude of the number.
* Floating point operations are used to perform calculations with numbers of varying magnitudes.
* The most common operations are addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. 
* Addition and subtraction are relatively straightforward, but multiplication and division can be more complex.
* Multiplication involves multiplying the mantissas and adding the exponents, while division involves dividing the mantissas and subtracting the exponents.
* The result of a floating point operation must be rounded to the nearest representable number.
* Floating point operations can be performed using hardware or software implementations. 
* Hardware implementations are typically more efficient, but software implementations are more flexible and can be used to perform calculations with a greater range of numbers.
