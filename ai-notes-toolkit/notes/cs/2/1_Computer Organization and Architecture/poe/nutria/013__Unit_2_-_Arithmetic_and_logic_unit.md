
## Unit 2 - Arithmetic and Logic Unit

1. Arithmetic logic units (ALUs) are digital circuits that perform arithmetic and logic operations on binary numbers.

2. ALUs are used in a wide variety of digital systems, from simple calculators to complex microprocessors and computers.

3. ALUs are composed of logic gates and other digital circuits that can perform arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

4. ALUs can also perform logic operations such as AND, OR, NOT, XOR, and NAND.

5. ALUs are used to store and manipulate data, as well as to control the flow of data in a digital system.

6. ALUs are designed to operate on a wide range of data types, including integers, floating-point numbers, and Boolean values.

7. ALUs can also be used to perform operations on data stored in memory, such as reading and writing data from and to memory.
