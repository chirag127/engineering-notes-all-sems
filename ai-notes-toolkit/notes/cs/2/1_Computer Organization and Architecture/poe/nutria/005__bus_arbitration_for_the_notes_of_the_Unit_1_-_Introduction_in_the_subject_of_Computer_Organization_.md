
### Bus Arbitration for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Computer Organization and Architecture

1. Bus arbitration is the process of selecting one device at a time on a shared communication bus.
2. It is a vital component of computer architecture, as it ensures that multiple devices can communicate with each other without interference.
3. The most common method of bus arbitration is known as a bus master, where one device is designated as the master and all other devices must wait for permission from the master before they can access the bus.
4. Bus arbitration can be implemented using various protocols, such as token passing, priority based, and round-robin.
5. The arbitration process is typically managed by a bus controller, which is responsible for deciding which device can access the bus.
6. Bus arbitration is used in most modern computers, including personal computers, servers, and embedded systems.
