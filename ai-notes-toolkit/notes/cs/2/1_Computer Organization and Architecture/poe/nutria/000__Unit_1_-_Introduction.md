
## Unit 1 - Introduction 

1. Introduction is an essential part of any study material as it helps to understand the purpose and goal of the topic. 
2. It also helps to introduce the reader to the key concepts and ideas of the topic. 
3. It is important to provide an overview of the material and its main ideas, as well as to provide a summary of the main points. 
4. It is also important to provide a brief description of the topics covered and the objectives of the material. 
5. This introduction should provide the reader with a basic understanding of the material, the topics covered, and the objectives of the material.
