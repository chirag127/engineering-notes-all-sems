
### Bus Architecture 

* A bus is a communication system that transfers data between components inside a computer, or between computers. 
* A bus is made up of three parts: the bus lines, the bus controller, and the bus driver. 
* The bus lines are the physical connections that allow data to be transferred between components. 
* The bus controller is responsible for managing the bus lines and controlling the flow of data. 
* The bus driver is responsible for interpreting the data and sending it to the appropriate device. 
* Bus architectures can be divided into two main categories: parallel and serial. 
* In a parallel bus architecture, the bus lines are used to transfer multiple pieces of data at the same time. 
* In a serial bus architecture, the bus lines are used to transfer one piece of data at a time. 
* Common examples of bus architectures include the ISA bus, the PCI bus, and the USB bus. 
* The ISA bus is an older type of bus architecture that was used in older computers. 
* The PCI bus is a more modern type of bus architecture that is used in most modern computers. 
* The USB bus is a type of bus architecture that is used to connect peripherals to computers.
