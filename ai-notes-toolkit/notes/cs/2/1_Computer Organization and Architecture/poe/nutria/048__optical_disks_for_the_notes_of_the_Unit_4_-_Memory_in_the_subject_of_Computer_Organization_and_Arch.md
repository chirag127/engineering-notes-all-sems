
### Optical Disks

Optical disks are a type of storage media used to store digital data. They use lasers to read and write data and are capable of storing large amounts of information.

* Optical disks are made of a thin plastic layer and a reflective layer.
* They are available in different formats, such as CD-ROM, DVD, Blu-ray, and UHD-BD.
* The data is stored in pits and lands, which are read by a laser.
* The data is encoded in a binary format, which is then translated into readable information.
* Optical disks have the advantage of being able to store large amounts of data in a small space.
* They are also resistant to physical damage, making them a reliable form of data storage.
* However, they are vulnerable to scratches and fingerprints, which can cause data loss.
* Optical disks also have a limited lifespan, as the data can degrade over time.
