
### Functional Units of Digital System and their Interconnections

1. A digital system is composed of multiple functional units, each with a specific purpose. 
2. The functional units are connected to each other to form a complete system. 
3. The major functional units of a digital system are the control unit, arithmetic logic unit (ALU), memory unit, input/output unit, and the bus. 
4. The control unit is responsible for controlling the operations of the system. It reads instructions from the memory and decodes them to generate control signals for other functional units. 
5. The arithmetic logic unit is responsible for performing arithmetic and logical operations. It can add, subtract, multiply, and divide numbers. It can also compare two values and determine whether they are equal, less than, or greater than each other. 
6. The memory unit is responsible for storing data and instructions. It consists of several memory cells, each of which can store one bit of data. 
7. The input/output unit is responsible for receiving inputs from external devices such as keyboards and mice, and sending outputs to external devices such as monitors and printers. 
8. The bus is responsible for connecting the various functional units. It is composed of several wires that carry data, address, and control signals between the functional units.
