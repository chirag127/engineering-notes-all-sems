
### Serial Communication 

* Serial communication is a type of communication used for data transmission between two or more digital devices over a single communication channel. 
* It is used to send data one bit at a time, sequentially, over a communication channel such as a serial port, modem, or network connection. 
* It is commonly used to transfer data between computers, peripherals, and other electronic devices. 
* It is also used to communicate with external devices such as sensors, displays, and other electronic components. 
* Serial communication is used in a variety of applications, including industrial automation, medical equipment, and consumer electronics. 
* In serial communication, data is sent in the form of a stream of bits, usually over a single wire or cable. 
* The data is sent bit by bit, one bit at a time, and is received in the same sequence. 
* Serial communication is used in a variety of protocols, such as RS232, RS485, IEEE 1284, and USB. 
* Serial communication is used to control and monitor devices, transfer data, and send commands.
