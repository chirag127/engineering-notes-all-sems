
### Look Ahead Carries Adder

Look ahead carries adder is a type of adder which is used to add two or more binary numbers. It is an optimized version of the ripple carry adder and is used to reduce the time taken for the addition process.

* Look ahead carries adder is faster than ripple carry adder as it does not require the propagation of carry bit. 
* It works by first looking ahead at the next bit to determine if a carry is required. 
* This is done by first adding the two bits and then looking at the next bit in the sequence to determine if carry is required. 
* The carry bit is then propagated to the next bit in the sequence. 
* This method reduces the time taken by the carry bit to propagate through the entire sequence. 
* Look ahead carries adder is used in arithmetic and logic units of computers to perform arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
