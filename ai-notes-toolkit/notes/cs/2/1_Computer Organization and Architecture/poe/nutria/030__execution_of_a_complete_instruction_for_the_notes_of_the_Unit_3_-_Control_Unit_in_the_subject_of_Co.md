
### Execution of a Complete Instruction for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Control Unit in the Subject of Computer Organization and Architecture

1. A computer's control unit is responsible for the execution of instructions that are stored in memory.
2. The control unit fetches instructions from memory and decodes them, determining what action needs to be taken.
3. The control unit then executes the instruction, which may involve sending signals to other components of the computer, such as the arithmetic logic unit (ALU) or memory.
4. The control unit also keeps track of the current instruction and the next instruction to be executed.
5. The control unit also determines when an instruction is complete and when to move on to the next instruction.
6. The control unit is responsible for the timing of the computer, ensuring that all components are in sync and that instructions are executed in the correct order.
7. The control unit also maintains the program counter, which keeps track of the current instruction and the next instruction to be executed.
8. The control unit is also responsible for managing interrupts, which are signals from external devices that require the processor to take immediate action.
9. Finally, the control unit is responsible for managing the system's power, ensuring that the computer does not draw too much power or shut down unexpectedly.
