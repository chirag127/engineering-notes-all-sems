
### Magnetic Disks

* Magnetic disks are a type of non-volatile memory used to store large amounts of data.
* Magnetic disks are divided into concentric circles called tracks. Each track is divided into sectors.
* A sector is the smallest unit of data that can be stored on a magnetic disk.
* Data is stored on the disk as magnetic patterns.
* Magnetic disks are slower than other types of memory, but they are more reliable and have greater storage capacity.
* Magnetic disks are used in computers, laptops, and other electronic devices. They are also used in external storage devices such as USB drives, external hard drives, and floppy disks.
* Magnetic disks are divided into two categories: hard disks and floppy disks. Hard disks have a higher storage capacity and are more reliable than floppy disks.
* The most common type of magnetic disk is the hard disk drive (HDD). HDDs are used in desktop and laptop computers, and they can store up to several terabytes of data.
* Another type of magnetic disk is the solid-state drive (SSD). SSDs are faster than HDDs, but they are more expensive and have lower storage capacity.
* Magnetic disks are used to store operating systems, application programs, user files, and other data.
