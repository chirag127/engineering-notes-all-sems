## Unit 5 - Input / Output

In this unit, we will learn about Input/Output (I/O) operations in computer systems. Input/Output is an essential aspect of computer systems as it enables data to be exchanged between a computer and its peripherals. By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

1. Define Input/Output operations and their importance in computer systems.
2. Understand the different types of Input/Output devices available in computer systems.
3. Describe the process of Input/Output operations and how they are performed in computer systems.
4. Identify the various Input/Output interfaces used in computer systems.
5. Explain the concept of buffering and its importance in Input/Output operations.
6. Understand the difference between synchronous and asynchronous Input/Output operations.
7. Describe the different Input/Output techniques used in computer systems, such as polling and interrupt-driven I/O.
8. Identify the various Input/Output errors that can occur in computer systems and how they can be handled.
9. Understand the concept of file Input/Output and how it is implemented in computer systems.
10. Describe the different file formats used in computer systems, such as binary and text files.

It is essential to understand Input/Output operations as they are fundamental to the functioning of computer systems. With this knowledge, you can troubleshoot and solve problems related to Input/Output operations, which will help you become a more skilled computer technician.
