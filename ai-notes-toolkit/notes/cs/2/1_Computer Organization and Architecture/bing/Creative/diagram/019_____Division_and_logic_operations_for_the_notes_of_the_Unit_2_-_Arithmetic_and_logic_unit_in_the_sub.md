### Division and logic operations

- Division is the process of finding the quotient and the remainder of two numbers.
- Division can be performed by repeated subtraction, shift and subtract, or restoring and non-restoring methods.
- Logic operations are the basic operations that manipulate the bits of data.
- Logic operations include AND, OR, NOT, XOR, NAND, NOR, and XNOR.
- Logic operations can be implemented by using logic gates, which are electronic circuits that perform the logic functions.
- Logic operations can be used to perform bitwise operations, which operate on individual bits of data, or logical operations, which operate on the truth values of data.
