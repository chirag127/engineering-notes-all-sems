
### Clusters Classifications for the notes of the Unit 3 - Overview of Cluster Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

* Cluster computing is a type of parallel computing that involves a large number of computers connected together to form a single system.
* Clusters can be classified based on the type of hardware used, the type of operating system, and the type of application used.
* In terms of hardware, clusters can be divided into two main types: homogeneous clusters and heterogeneous clusters. Homogeneous clusters use identical hardware components, while heterogeneous clusters use a mix of different hardware components.
* In terms of operating system, clusters can be divided into two main types: shared-memory clusters and distributed-memory clusters. Shared-memory clusters use a single operating system, while distributed-memory clusters use multiple operating systems.
* In terms of application, clusters can be divided into two main types: high-performance computing (HPC) clusters and parallel-processing clusters. HPC clusters are designed to run scientific and engineering applications, while parallel-processing clusters are designed to run large-scale data processing applications. 
* Clusters are used for a variety of applications, including scientific research, data analytics, machine learning, and web hosting.
