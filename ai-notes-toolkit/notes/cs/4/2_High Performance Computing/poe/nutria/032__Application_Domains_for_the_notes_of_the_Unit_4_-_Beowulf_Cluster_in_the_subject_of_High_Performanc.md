
### Application Domains for the Notes of Unit 4 - Beowulf Cluster in High Performance Computing

1. **Scientific Computing** - Beowulf clusters are used to perform computationally intensive simulations and calculations in scientific fields such as physics, chemistry, and engineering. 
2. **Data Analysis** - Beowulf clusters are used to analyze large sets of data, such as in astronomy, bioinformatics, and machine learning.
3. **Data Storage** - Beowulf clusters can be used to store large amounts of data, such as in web hosting and distributed file systems.
4. **Graphics Rendering** - Beowulf clusters can be used to render graphics, such as in video games and 3D animation.
5. **High Performance Computing** - Beowulf clusters are used to perform computationally intensive tasks, such as in weather modeling, cryptography, and financial modeling.
