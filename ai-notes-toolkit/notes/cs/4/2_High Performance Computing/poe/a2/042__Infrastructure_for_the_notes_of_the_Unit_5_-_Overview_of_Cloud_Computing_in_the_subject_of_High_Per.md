The key characteristics of cloud infrastructure are:

1. Scalability: Easily scale up or down by adding or removing resources.
2. Elasticity: Flexibly allocating resources as per demand.
3. Automation: Self-service interfaces to allocate and manage resources.
4. Redundancy: Resources are distributed and redundant to handle failures.

The advantages of cloud infrastructure are:

1. No upfront capital investment in hardware and data centers.
2. Scalability and elasticity to meet variable demands.
