### Peer‐to‐Peer Computing

For the notes of the Unit 1 - Overview of Grid Computing Technology in the subject of High Performance Computing.

- Peer-to-peer (P2P) computing utilizes the resources of multiple computers (peers) to solve a problem. The peers are equal partners that share computing resources among themselves.
- In P2P computing, the peers form a network (P2P network) and do not rely on central servers. The participants/peers function both as suppliers and consumers of resources.
- The key advantages of P2P computing are:
- Scalability: The system can scale to large sizes as new peers join the network.
- Robustness: The system is robust to failures as the tasks can be reassigned to other peers in case of failures.
- Low cost: The system can be built from existing computers and networking infrastructure resulting in low costs.
- Applications of P2P computing include:
- File sharing systems: Napster, Gnutella, BitTorrent, etc.
- Distributed computing systems: SETI@home, Folding@Home, etc.
- VoIP systems: Skype
- Video streaming systems
