### Service Oriented Architecture for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject of High Performance Computing

1. Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a style of software design where applications are built as a suite of interoperable services.
2. Each service has a well-defined interface and provides a requested capability.
3. Services can be reused to build multiple applications and integrated with each other.
4. Services collaborate by sharing messages in standard formats using common communication protocols.
5. The key characteristics of SOA are:
- Services are loosely coupled such that changes in one service does not affect other services.
- Services are reusable across applications and applications are built by integrating services.
- Services interact through open protocols and standards.
- Services are autonomous such that they have control over their data and logic.
- Services are discoverable via registries.
6. The benefits of SOA include:
- Increased interoperability through the use of open standards.
- Increased flexibility and reuse of services.
- Platform and language independence.
- Scalability through geographic distribution of services.
- Evolution of services without impact on consumers.
