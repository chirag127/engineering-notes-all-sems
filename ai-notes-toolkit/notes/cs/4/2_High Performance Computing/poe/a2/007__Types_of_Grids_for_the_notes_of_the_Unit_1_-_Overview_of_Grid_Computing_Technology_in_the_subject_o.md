### Types of Grids

1. Public Grid: A public grid is accessible to anyone over the internet. Resources are donated and managed by the organization running the grid. Users can register and use the resources for their applications. Example: World Community Grid.
2. Private Grid: A private grid is designed for a specific organization. The resources are owned and managed by the organization. Only authorized users within the organization can access the resources. Example: A company using a grid for data processing and simulations.
3. Hybrid Grid: A hybrid grid is a combination of public and private grids. Some resources are from a private grid and some from a public grid. The hybrid model is used to tackle issues like resource shortages or security on either a public or private grid.
