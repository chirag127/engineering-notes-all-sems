### Types of Cloud

1. Public Cloud: Public clouds are owned and operated by a third-party cloud service providers, which deliver their computing resources like servers and storage over the Internet. Pay-as-you-go model. Eg: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud.
2. Private Cloud: Private clouds are operated solely for a single organization. They may be managed by the organization or a third-party and may exist on-premises or off-premises. Eg: OpenStack, VMware vCloud Director.
3. Hybrid Cloud: Hybrid clouds are a composition of two or more clouds (private, public or community) that remain unique entities but are bound together by standardized or proprietary technology that enables data and application portability. Eg: AWS Outposts, Azure Stack.
4. Community Cloud: Community clouds are shared by several organizations and support a specific community that has shared concerns. Eg: Healthcare, Government. They are managed by the organizations or a third-party and may exist on-premises or off-premises.

The types of cloud differ in terms of ownership, size, location, purpose and structure. Choosing a cloud type depends on requirements such as scalability, security, compliance, availability, and cost.
