### Clients

1. A client is a piece of computer hardware or software that accesses a service made available by a server.
2. The server is often (but not always) on another computer system, in which case the client accesses the service by way of a network.
3. The term applies to the role that programs or devices play in the client–server model.
4. Clients are the devices or software that interact with the cloud to access the services and resources provided by the cloud.
5. Clients can be categorized into three types: thin clients, thick clients, and mobile clients.
6. Thin clients are devices with minimal processing power and storage capacity that rely on the cloud to perform most of their processing.
7. Thick clients, also known as fat clients, are devices with more processing power and storage capacity that can perform some processing locally.
8. Mobile clients are devices such as smartphones and tablets that are designed for mobility and can access the cloud from anywhere with an internet connection.
9. Clients can access the cloud through web browsers, mobile apps, or desktop applications.
10. The choice of client depends on the needs of the user and the type of service being accessed.
