### Cluster Grids

- Cluster grids are a type of grid computing that involves a group of computers connected by a local area network (LAN) and working together as a single system .
- Cluster grids are usually homogeneous, meaning that the computers have the same hardware components and the same operating system (OS)  .
- Cluster grids are tightly coupled, meaning that the computers communicate frequently and share resources such as memory, disk space, and processors  .
- Cluster grids are often used for high-performance computing (HPC) applications that require a large amount of processing power and data .
- Cluster grids can be classified into different types based on their architecture, such as load-balancing clusters, high-availability clusters, and high-performance clusters .
- Cluster grids can also be classified into different types based on their topology, such as star clusters, ring clusters, and tree clusters .
- Cluster grids have some advantages over other types of grid computing, such as lower latency, higher reliability, and easier management .
- Cluster grids also have some disadvantages, such as higher cost, lower scalability, and limited heterogeneity .
