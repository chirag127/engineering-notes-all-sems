### Service Oriented Architecture

- Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that focuses on discrete services instead of a monolithic design.
- A service is a self-contained unit of functionality that provides a specific business capability  .
- Services are loosely coupled, meaning they can be used and reused independently of each other  .
- Services communicate with each other using standard protocols and formats, such as XML, SOAP, REST, JSON, etc  .
- Services can be composed and orchestrated to form larger applications that leverage existing functionality and reduce development time and cost  .
- SOA aims to allow users to combine large chunks of functionality to form applications that are built purely from existing services and combining them in an ad hoc manner.
- SOA enables interoperability, scalability, reusability, and agility in software development  .
- SOA is not a specific technology or product, but rather a design philosophy and a set of best practices  .
