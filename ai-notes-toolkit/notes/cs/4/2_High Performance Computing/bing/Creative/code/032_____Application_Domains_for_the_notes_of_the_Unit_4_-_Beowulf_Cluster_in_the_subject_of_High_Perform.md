### Application Domains for the notes of the Unit 4 - Beowulf Cluster in the subject of High Performance Computing

A Beowulf cluster is a type of parallel computing system that consists of a collection of commodity computers connected by a local area network and running a distributed operating system. Beowulf clusters are designed to provide high performance computing at a low cost by exploiting the power of multiple processors working together on a common problem.

Beowulf clusters can be used for a variety of applications that require intensive computation and large data sets. Some of the application domains of Beowulf clusters are:

- Transport phenomena, including fluid dynamics, heat and mass transfer, multi-phase flows, aerodynamics, etc. These applications involve solving complex partial differential equations that describe the behavior of fluids, gases, and solids under various conditions.
- Molecular dynamics, and protein folding. These applications involve simulating the interactions of atoms and molecules at the nanoscale, and predicting the three-dimensional structure of proteins from their amino acid sequences.
- Cellular automata to model phenomena from epidemiology to options trading. These applications involve using simple rules to generate complex patterns and behaviors of discrete entities, such as cells, agents, or markets.
- Graphics: distributed raytracing and rendering. These applications involve creating realistic images of virtual scenes by tracing the paths of light rays and calculating the color and intensity of each pixel.
- Data mining and stream processing. These applications involve analyzing large volumes of data to discover patterns, trends, and anomalies, or processing continuous streams of data in real-time.
- Financial market modeling. These applications involve simulating the behavior of financial markets and instruments, such as stocks, bonds, derivatives, and currencies, under various scenarios and strategies.
- Bioinformatics. These applications involve applying computational methods to biological problems, such as DNA sequence alignment, gene expression analysis, phylogenetic reconstruction, and drug design.
- Nuclear physics. These applications involve modeling the behavior of nuclear reactions, such as fission, fusion, and decay, and the radiation emissions from a hypothetical nuclear catastrophe.
- Medical physics. These applications involve modeling the behavior of beams of protons or other particles used for cancer treatment, and the effects of radiation on human tissues.

Beowulf clusters are scalable to a nearly unlimited number of computers, limited only by the overhead of the network. Beowulf programs are usually written using languages such as C and FORTRAN, and use message passing to achieve parallel computations. Provisioning of operating systems and other software for a Beowulf Cluster can be automated using software, such as Open Source Cluster Application Resources.
