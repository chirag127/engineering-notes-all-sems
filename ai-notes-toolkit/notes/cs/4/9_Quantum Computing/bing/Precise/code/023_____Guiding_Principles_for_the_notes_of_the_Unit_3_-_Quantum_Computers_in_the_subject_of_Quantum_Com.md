### Guiding Principles for the notes of the Unit 3 - Quantum Computers in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Quantum computers are a type of computer that use quantum bits, or qubits, to store and process information.
2. Quantum computers have the potential to solve certain problems much faster than classical computers.
3. Quantum algorithms, such as Shor's algorithm for factoring large numbers, can take advantage of the properties of quantum mechanics to achieve exponential speedup over classical algorithms.
4. Quantum error correction is an important area of research, as quantum computers are susceptible to errors due to decoherence and other sources of noise.
5. There are several different approaches to building a quantum computer, including trapped ions, superconducting circuits, and topological qubits.
6. Quantum computers are still in the early stages of development, and there are many challenges to overcome before they can be widely used.
