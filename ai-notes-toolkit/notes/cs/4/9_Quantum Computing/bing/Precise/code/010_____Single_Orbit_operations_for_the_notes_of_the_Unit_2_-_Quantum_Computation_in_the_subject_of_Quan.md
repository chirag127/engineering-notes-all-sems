### Single Orbit operations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Single-qubit operations are local operations performed using single-qubit gates.
2. Single-qubit gates can be represented by a 2×2 matrix that acts on a 2D qubit vector.
3. The X-gate is one such gate, which switches the amplitudes of |1⟩ and |0⟩ in a qubit, and is often known as a 'bit flip'.
4. Single-qubit operations and CNOT gates can be used to perform any quantum computation.
5. Single-qubit operations can be classified into two categories: Clifford gates and non-Clifford gates.
6. Non-Clifford gates consist only of the T-gate (also known as the π/8 gate).
7. The standard set of single-qubit Clifford gates is included by default in Q#.
