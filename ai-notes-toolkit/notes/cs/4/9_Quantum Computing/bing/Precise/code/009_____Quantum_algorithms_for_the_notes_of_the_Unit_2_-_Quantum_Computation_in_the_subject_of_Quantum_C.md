### Quantum algorithms

Quantum algorithms are algorithms designed to run on quantum computers. They are capable of solving problems faster than classical algorithms running on classical computers. Some well-known examples of quantum algorithms are Grover's algorithm and Shor's algorithm, which yield a polynomial and an exponential speedup, respectively, over their classical counterparts .

One of the goals of quantum computing research is to study which problems can be solved by a quantum computer faster than a classical computer and how large the speedup can be .

Quantum phase estimation (QPE) and ``post-QPE'' methods such as block encoding, quantum signal processing, and quantum singular value transformation are some of the techniques used in quantum algorithms. These techniques have applications in solving eigenvalue problems, linear systems of equations, and differential equations .
