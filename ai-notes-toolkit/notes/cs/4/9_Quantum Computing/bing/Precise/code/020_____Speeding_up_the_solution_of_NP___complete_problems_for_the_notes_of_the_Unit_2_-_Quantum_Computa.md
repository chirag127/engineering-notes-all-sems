### Speeding up the solution of NP – complete problems

- NP-complete problems represent the hardest problems in the NP set.
- A solution to an NP-complete problem can be verified quickly, but there is no known way to find a solution quickly.
- Quantum computers can provide a square-root speedup for NP-complete problems using Grover's algorithm.
- A simple method to obtain the full T(n) to tilde {O}(sqrt {T(n)}) speed-up for most of the many non-trivial exponential time algorithms for NP-hard problems is presented. The method works whenever the widely used technique of recursive decomposition is employed.
- Coping strategies for solving NP-complete problems are relevant to business and industry.
- An algorithm dedicated to solving the well-known NP-complete Ising problem with photonics hardware has been developed by the MIT team.
