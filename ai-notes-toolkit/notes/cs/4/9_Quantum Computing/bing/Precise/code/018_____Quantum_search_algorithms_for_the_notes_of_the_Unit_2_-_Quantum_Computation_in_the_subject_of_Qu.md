### Quantum search algorithms

Quantum search algorithms are a class of quantum algorithms that are used to search for a specific item in an unstructured database. One of the most well-known quantum search algorithms is Grover's algorithm, which was created by Lov Grover in 1996.

- Grover's algorithm is quadratically faster than its classical counterpart, requiring only O(√N) operations to search a database of N items .
- The algorithm works by using a binary function, f(x), where f(x) = 1 if and only if x is the label of the object being searched for .
- Grover's algorithm is optimal, meaning that no faster quantum algorithms for search exist .
- Quantum walks are another powerful tool for building quantum search algorithms .
