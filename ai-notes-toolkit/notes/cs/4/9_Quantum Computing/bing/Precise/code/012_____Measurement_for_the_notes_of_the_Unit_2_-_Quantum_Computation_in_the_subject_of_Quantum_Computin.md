### Measurement

Measurement is an important concept in quantum computation. Here are some key points to remember:

1. Measurement is the process of obtaining information about the state of a quantum system.
2. In quantum mechanics, measurement is described by the projection postulate, which states that the act of measurement projects the state of the system onto one of the eigenstates of the observable being measured.
3. The outcome of a measurement is probabilistic, and the probability of obtaining a particular outcome is given by the square of the amplitude of the corresponding eigenstate.
4. After a measurement, the state of the system is changed to the eigenstate corresponding to the observed outcome.
5. Measurement can also be used to manipulate the state of a quantum system, for example, by performing a measurement in a particular basis to collapse the state into one of the basis states.
6. In quantum computation, measurement is used to extract classical information from a quantum system, for example, by measuring the final state of a quantum algorithm to obtain the result of the computation.
