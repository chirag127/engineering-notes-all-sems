## Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts

1. **Data** refers to raw, unorganized facts that need to be processed. Data can be something simple and seemingly random and useless until it is organized.
2. **Information** is processed, organized, structured or presented data in a given context to make it useful.
3. **Knowledge** is the understanding or awareness gained through the analysis of information.
4. **Wisdom** is the ability to make correct judgments and decisions based on knowledge and experience.
5. **Processing** refers to the transformation of data into information through the use of computer systems or other methods.
6. **Storage** refers to the retention of data and information for future use.
7. **Retrieval** refers to the process of accessing stored data and information.
8. **Transmission** refers to the transfer of data and information from one location to another.
9. **Presentation** refers to the display of data and information in a format that is understandable to the user.
