### Quantum Operations

Quantum operations are the transformations that can be applied to quantum states. These operations are used to manipulate and process quantum information, which is the basic unit of information in quantum computing. Quantum information, like classical information, can be processed using digital computers, transmitted from one location to another, manipulated with algorithms, and analyzed with computer science and mathematics .

The basic unit of information in quantum computing is the qubit, similar to the bit in traditional digital electronics. Unlike a classical bit, a qubit can exist in a superposition of its two "basis" states, which loosely means that it is in both states simultaneously  .

Quantum information theory is an interdisciplinary field merging quantum physics, computer science, information theory, philosophy and cryptography .
