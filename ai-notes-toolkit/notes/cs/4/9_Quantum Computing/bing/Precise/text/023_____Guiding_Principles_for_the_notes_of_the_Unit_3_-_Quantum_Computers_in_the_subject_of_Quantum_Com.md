### Guiding Principles for the notes of the Unit 3 - Quantum Computers in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. The notes should provide a clear and concise overview of the key concepts and principles of quantum computers.
2. The notes should be organized in a logical and coherent manner, with clear headings and subheadings to guide the reader.
3. The notes should include relevant examples and diagrams to illustrate the concepts and principles discussed.
4. The notes should be written in a formal and academic style, using appropriate terminology and avoiding colloquial language.
5. The notes should be accurate and up-to-date, reflecting the latest developments and research in the field of quantum computing.
6. The notes should be written with the target audience in mind, using language and explanations that are accessible to students of quantum computing.
7. The notes should include references to relevant sources and further reading materials for students who wish to explore the topic in greater depth.
8. The notes should be reviewed and revised regularly to ensure their continued relevance and accuracy.
