### Strong Sub Additivity

- Strong subadditivity is a property of the von Neumann entropy, which is a measure of the amount of quantum information in a quantum state.
- The property states that for any tripartite quantum state, the sum of the entropies of any two of the subsystems is greater than or equal to the sum of the entropy of the third subsystem and the entropy of the entire system.
- Mathematically, this can be expressed as: S(A) + S(B) ≥ S(AB) + S(C), where S(X) represents the von Neumann entropy of subsystem X, and ABC represents the entire tripartite system.
- This property is important in the study of quantum error correction, as it provides a bound on the amount of information that can be extracted from a quantum state by measuring only a subset of the system.
- Strong subadditivity is also closely related to other important concepts in quantum information theory, such as the Holevo bound and the quantum mutual information.
