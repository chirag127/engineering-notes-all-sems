# Phase Estimation

Phase estimation is an important algorithm in quantum computation. It is used to estimate the eigenvalue of a unitary operator. Here are some key points to remember about phase estimation:

1. The phase estimation algorithm uses two quantum registers. The first register contains the eigenvector of the unitary operator, and the second register is used to store the estimated phase.

2. The algorithm uses the quantum Fourier transform to estimate the phase. The quantum Fourier transform is applied to the second register, followed by a series of controlled unitary operations.

3. The accuracy of the phase estimation algorithm depends on the number of qubits in the second register. More qubits in the second register result in a more accurate estimate of the phase.

4. The phase estimation algorithm can be used to solve problems such as order finding and factoring large numbers.

5. The phase estimation algorithm is an important building block for many other quantum algorithms, such as Shor's algorithm for factoring large numbers and the quantum algorithm for solving linear systems of equations.
