### Conditions for Quantum Computation

Quantum computation is a rapidly developing field that aims to harness the power of quantum mechanics to perform calculations that classical computers cannot. There are several key requirements that must be met in order to build a functional quantum computer. These include:

1. **Long Coherence Time:** Long qubit coherence times are a prerequisite for quantum computing. This refers to the amount of time that a qubit can maintain its quantum state before being affected by its environment .

2. **High Scalability:** A quantum computer must be able to scale up to a large number of qubits in order to perform complex calculations .

3. **High Fault Tolerance and Quantum Error Correction:** Quantum computers must be able to detect and correct errors that occur during computation .

4. **Ability to Initialize Qubits:** A quantum computer must be able to initialize its qubits to a known state before computation begins .

5. **Universal Quantum Gates:** A quantum computer must have a set of universal quantum gates that can be used to perform any computation .

6. **Efficient Qubit-state Measurement Capability:** A quantum computer must be able to efficiently measure the state of its qubits .

7. **Faithful Transmission of Flying Qubits and Interconversion between Stationary and “Flying” Qubits:** A quantum computer must be able to transmit qubits between different parts of the computer and convert between stationary and “flying” qubits .

These are the seven key requirements for designing effective quantum computing systems . Additionally, the DiVincenzo criteria, proposed by theoretical physicist David P. DiVincenzo in 2000, are also considered necessary conditions for constructing a quantum computer .
