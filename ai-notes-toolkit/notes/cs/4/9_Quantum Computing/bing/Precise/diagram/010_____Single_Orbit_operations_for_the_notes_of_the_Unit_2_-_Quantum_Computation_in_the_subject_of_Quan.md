### Single Orbit operations for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation in the subject of Quantum Computing

- Single-qubit operations are fundamental operations that act as building blocks for quantum algorithms.
- A small set of quantum gates is enough to carry out any quantum computation, these sets are called universal quantum gates.
- Single-qubit quantum gates can be represented by a 2×2 matrix that acts on our 2D qubit vector.
- The X-gate is one such gate, it switches the amplitudes of |1⟩ and |0⟩ in our qubit, for this reason it is often known as a 'bit flip'.
- The valid transformations that we are allowed to perform on a qubit are unitary transformations and measurement.
- The adjoint operation or the complex conjugate transpose is of crucial importance to quantum computing because it is needed to invert quantum transformations.
