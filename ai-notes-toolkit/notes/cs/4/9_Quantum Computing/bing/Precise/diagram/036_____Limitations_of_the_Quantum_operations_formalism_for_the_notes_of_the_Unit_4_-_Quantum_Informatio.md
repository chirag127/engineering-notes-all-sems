### Limitations of the Quantum operations formalism

1. **Limited applicability**: The quantum operations formalism is only applicable to completely positive trace-preserving maps, which limits its applicability to certain types of quantum operations.

2. **Difficulty in characterizing non-Markovian dynamics**: The formalism is well-suited for describing Markovian dynamics, but it becomes more challenging to characterize non-Markovian dynamics using this formalism.

3. **Difficulty in describing continuous-time evolution**: The formalism is well-suited for describing discrete-time evolution, but it becomes more challenging to describe continuous-time evolution using this formalism.

4. **Limited ability to describe correlations**: The formalism is well-suited for describing local operations, but it becomes more challenging to describe correlations between different systems using this formalism.

5. **Difficulty in describing measurements**: The formalism is well-suited for describing unitary operations, but it becomes more challenging to describe measurements using this formalism.
