### Measurement

Measurement is a fundamental concept in quantum computation and is the process of obtaining information about a quantum system. Here are some key points to note about measurement in the context of quantum computation:

1. Measurement is the process of observing a quantum system, which causes it to collapse into one of its possible states.
2. The outcome of a measurement is probabilistic, meaning that the result is not certain but is determined by the probabilities of the possible outcomes.
3. The probabilities of the possible outcomes are determined by the state of the quantum system before the measurement.
4. After a measurement, the state of the quantum system is changed to the state corresponding to the outcome of the measurement.
5. Measurement is an irreversible process, meaning that once a measurement has been made, the original state of the quantum system cannot be recovered.
6. In quantum computation, measurement is often used to extract the result of a computation from a quantum system.

These are some of the key points to remember about measurement in the context of quantum computation. It is an important concept to understand when studying quantum computing.
