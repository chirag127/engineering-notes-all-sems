### Quantum Bits

Quantum Bits, also known as Qubits, are the basic unit of quantum information. They are the quantum version of the classic binary bit, physically realized with a two-state device . A qubit is a two-state (or two-level) quantum-mechanical system, one of the simplest quantum systems displaying the peculiarity of quantum mechanics .

- A quantum bit is any bit made out of a quantum system, like an electron or photon .
- Just like classical bits, a quantum bit must have two distinct states: one representing “0” and one representing “1” .
- A quantum bit can exist in superposition, which means that it can exist in multiple states at once .
- In quantum computing, the valid transformations that we are allowed to perform on a qubit are unitary transformations and measurement .
- The adjoint operation or the complex conjugate transpose is of crucial importance to quantum computing because it is needed to invert quantum transformations .
