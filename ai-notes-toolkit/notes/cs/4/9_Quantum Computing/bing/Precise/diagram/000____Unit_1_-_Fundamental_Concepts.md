## Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts

1. **Data** - Data refers to raw, unorganized facts that need to be processed. Data can be something simple and seemingly random and useless until it is organized.

2. **Information** - When data is processed, organized, structured or presented in a given context so as to make it useful, it is called information.

3. **Knowledge** - Knowledge refers to the practical or theoretical understanding of a subject. It is acquired through experience or education by perceiving, discovering, or learning.

4. **Hardware** - Hardware refers to the physical components of a computer system. It includes the computer itself, as well as peripherals such as the monitor, keyboard, and mouse.

5. **Software** - Software refers to the programs and other operating information used by a computer. It includes the operating system, application programs, and data.

6. **Database** - A database is an organized collection of data, generally stored and accessed electronically from a computer system.

7. **Network** - A network is a group of two or more computer systems linked together. Networks can be used to share resources, exchange files, or allow electronic communications.

8. **Internet** - The Internet is a global system of interconnected computer networks that use the standard Internet protocol suite (TCP/IP) to link devices worldwide.

9. **World Wide Web** - The World Wide Web (WWW) is an information space where documents and other web resources are identified by Uniform Resource Locators (URLs), interlinked by hypertext links, and can be accessed via the Internet.

10. **Programming** - Programming is the process of designing and building an executable computer program to accomplish a specific computing result or to perform a specific task.
