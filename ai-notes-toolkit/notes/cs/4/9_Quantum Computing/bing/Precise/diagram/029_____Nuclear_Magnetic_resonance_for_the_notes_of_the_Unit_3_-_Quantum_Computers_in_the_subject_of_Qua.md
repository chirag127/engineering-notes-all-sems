### Nuclear Magnetic Resonance for the notes of the Unit 3 - Quantum Computers in the subject of Quantum Computing

- Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Quantum Computing (NMRQC) is one of the several proposed approaches for constructing a quantum computer.
- NMRQC uses the spin states of nuclei within molecules as qubits.
- The quantum states are probed through the nuclear magnetic resonances, allowing the system to be implemented as a variation of nuclear magnetic resonance.
- NMR differs from other implementations of quantum computers in that it uses an ensemble of systems, in this case molecules, rather than a single pure state qubit.
- It has recently been shown that NMR can be used to implement small quantum computers using the spin states of nuclei in carefully chosen small molecules.
- A process called NMR spectroscopy that is often used to find and identify small molecules in biological samples such as blood and urine has become a powerful diagnostic tool for medical professionals, helping identify biomarkers of specific diseases and disorders.
