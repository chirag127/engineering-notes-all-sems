
### Constructing Quantum Codes for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

* Quantum error correction is the process of protecting quantum information from errors caused by noise in a quantum system. 
* This is done by encoding the quantum information into a larger quantum system, where the errors can be detected and corrected. 
* Quantum error correction codes are constructed from classical codes, such as the Hamming codes and Reed-Solomon codes. 
* Quantum error correction codes can also be constructed from quantum codes, such as the Shor codes and the Steane codes. 
* The Steane code is the most efficient code for correcting single-qubit errors, while the Shor code is the most efficient code for correcting two-qubit errors. 
* Quantum error correction codes can be used to protect quantum information from noise in a quantum system, and they can also be used to detect and correct errors in a quantum computation.
