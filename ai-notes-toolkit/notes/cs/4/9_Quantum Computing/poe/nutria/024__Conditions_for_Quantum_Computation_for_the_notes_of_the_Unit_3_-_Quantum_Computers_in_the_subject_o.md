
### Conditions for Quantum Computation
1. Quantum computation requires a device that can be in a superposition of two or more states simultaneously. 
2. The device must be able to interact with its environment, allowing the computation to be performed. 
3. The device must be able to measure its own state in order to determine the outcome of the computation. 
4. The device must be able to manipulate its own state in order to perform the computation. 
5. The device must be able to store and retrieve information in order to perform the computation. 
6. The device must be able to control the timing of the computation in order to ensure accuracy.
