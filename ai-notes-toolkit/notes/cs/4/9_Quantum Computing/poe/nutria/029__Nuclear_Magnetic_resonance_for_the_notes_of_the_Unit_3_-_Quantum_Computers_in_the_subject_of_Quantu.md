
### Nuclear Magnetic Resonance

Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) is a technique used to study the structure of molecules. NMR is based on the fact that certain nuclei possess a magnetic moment and can be aligned in an external magnetic field. When exposed to a radiofrequency field, these nuclei can absorb energy and undergo a transition from one state to another. This transition can be detected and used to obtain information about the structure of molecules.

NMR is an important tool in quantum computing as it can be used to measure the state of a quantum system. NMR spectroscopy can be used to measure the energy levels of a system, and to determine the structure of molecules. NMR can also be used to measure the spin of a qubit and to detect errors in a quantum computation. NMR can be used to study the dynamics of a quantum system, and to measure the entanglement between qubits.
