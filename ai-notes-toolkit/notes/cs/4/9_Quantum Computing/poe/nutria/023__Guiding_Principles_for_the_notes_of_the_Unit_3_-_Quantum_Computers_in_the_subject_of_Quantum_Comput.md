
### Guiding Principles for the Notes of Unit 3 - Quantum Computers in Quantum Computing
1. Quantum computing is a rapidly growing field of technology that promises to revolutionize the way we interact with computers.
2. Quantum computers use qubits instead of classical bits to store and process information.
3. Quantum computers are able to process information far faster than classical computers due to the nature of quantum entanglement.
4. Quantum algorithms are used to solve problems that are intractable on classical computers.
5. Quantum computers are able to solve certain problems exponentially faster than classical computers.
6. Quantum computing can be used to solve problems in a variety of fields, including cryptography, machine learning, and optimization.
7. Quantum computers are still in their infancy, and there is much research being done to improve their performance and capabilities.
8. Quantum computers require specialized hardware and software, and are currently expensive to build and maintain.
