
### Quantum Bits for the Notes of Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the Subject of Quantum Computing

1. Quantum bits (qubits) are the fundamental building blocks of quantum computing and are used to represent and store information.

2. A qubit can be in a state of both 0 and 1 at the same time, unlike classical bits which can only be in a state of either 0 or 1. This phenomenon is known as superposition.

3. Entanglement is another important concept in quantum computing. It is a phenomenon where two or more qubits become linked together and the state of one affects the state of the other.

4. Quantum gates are used to manipulate qubits and control the flow of information. They are analogous to logic gates in classical computing.

5. Quantum algorithms are used to solve certain problems much faster than classical algorithms. Examples of such algorithms include Shor's algorithm for factoring large numbers and Grover's algorithm for searching an unordered list.

6. Quantum error correction is used to protect quantum information from errors due to noise and decoherence.

7. Quantum computers have the potential to solve certain problems that are intractable on classical computers. Examples of such problems include protein folding, quantum chemistry and machine learning.
