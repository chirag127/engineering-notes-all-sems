
### Fault – Tolerant Quantum Computation 

1. Fault-tolerant quantum computation is a set of techniques used to protect quantum information from errors caused by environmental noise, decoherence, and other sources of noise.

2. Error correction codes are used to identify and correct errors in quantum information before it is read out. These codes are based on the principles of classical error correction codes and utilize the redundancy of quantum information to identify and correct errors.

3. Quantum error correction relies on the use of entangled qubits to identify and correct errors. Entanglement allows for the identification of errors in the quantum information, as well as the correction of errors.

4. Fault-tolerant quantum computation also relies on the use of quantum gates, which are used to manipulate and control the quantum information. These gates are used to perform operations on the qubits, such as the measurement of the qubits and the application of error correction codes.

5. Fault-tolerant quantum computation requires the use of specialized hardware, such as quantum computers, which are used to store and manipulate the quantum information. Additionally, fault-tolerant quantum computation requires the use of specialized software, such as quantum algorithms, which are used to control the quantum computer.
