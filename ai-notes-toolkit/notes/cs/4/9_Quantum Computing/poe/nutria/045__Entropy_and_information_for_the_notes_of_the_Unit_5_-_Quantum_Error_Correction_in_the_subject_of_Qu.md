
### Entropy and Information

* Entropy is a measure of uncertainty in a system. It is related to the amount of information that can be encoded in a system. 
* In quantum computing, entropy is used to measure the amount of information that can be stored in a quantum system.
* Quantum error correction is a technique used to protect quantum information from errors caused by environmental noise. 
* Error correction codes are used to detect and correct errors in a quantum system. These codes are based on the idea of redundancy, where multiple copies of the same information are stored. 
* Quantum error correction codes use entanglement to encode information in a way that is resistant to errors. 
* Quantum error correction is essential for the development of quantum computers, as it allows for the reliable storage and transmission of quantum information.
