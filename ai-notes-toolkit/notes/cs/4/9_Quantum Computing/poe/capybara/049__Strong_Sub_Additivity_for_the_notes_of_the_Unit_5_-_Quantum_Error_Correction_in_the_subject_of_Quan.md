### Strong Sub Additivity

Strong Sub Additivity (SSA) is a key property of quantum information, and it plays a crucial role in the study of quantum error correction. Here are some important points to keep in mind about SSA:

- SSA is a property of the von Neumann entropy, which is a measure of the amount of information contained in a quantum system.

- Specifically, SSA states that the sum of the von Neumann entropies of three subsystems is greater than or equal to the sum of the von Neumann entropies of any two of the subsystems individually.

- Mathematically, SSA can be written as S(ABC) + S(B) ≤ S(AB) + S(BC), where A, B, and C are subsystems of a larger quantum system.

- SSA is a fundamental property of quantum information, and it has many important applications in the field of quantum computing.

- One of the most important applications of SSA is in the field of quantum error correction. Specifically, SSA plays a key role in the construction of quantum error correcting codes, which are essential for protecting quantum information against errors and decoherence.

- Overall, SSA is a powerful tool for understanding and manipulating quantum information, and it is an essential component of any comprehensive study of quantum error correction.
