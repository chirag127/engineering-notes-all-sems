### Von Neumann

- John Von Neumann was a Hungarian-American mathematician, physicist, and computer scientist who made significant contributions to the fields of mathematics, physics, and computer science.
- He is considered to be one of the most influential mathematicians of the 20th century.
- Von Neumann was a pioneer in the development of digital computers and played a key role in the development of the first electronic digital computer, the ENIAC.
- He also made significant contributions to the field of quantum mechanics, including the development of the mathematical framework for quantum mechanics, known as the Von Neumann algebra.
- Von Neumann was also interested in game theory, and he developed the minimax theorem, which is a fundamental principle in game theory.
- He was awarded the Medal of Freedom in 1956 for his contributions to the field of science and technology.
- Von Neumann's work has had a profound impact on the development of modern computing and quantum mechanics. His legacy continues to inspire and influence researchers in these fields today.
