### Control Operations

Quantum computation requires the ability to manipulate and control qubits. Control operations are essential for this purpose.

#### 1. Single-Qubit Control Operations

Single-qubit control operations are used to manipulate the state of a single qubit.

- **Pauli X, Y, Z gates:** These gates are used to flip a qubit's state along the X, Y, or Z axis.
- **Hadamard gate:** This gate is used to create a superposition state, which is a combination of two basis states.
- **Phase gate:** This gate is used to add a phase to a qubit's state.

#### 2. Two-Qubit Control Operations

Two-qubit control operations are used to manipulate the state of two qubits.

- **CNOT gate:** This gate flips the second qubit's state if the first qubit is in the state |1>.
- **SWAP gate:** This gate swaps the states of two qubits.

#### 3. Multi-Qubit Control Operations

Multi-qubit control operations are used to manipulate the state of multiple qubits.

- **Toffoli gate:** This gate flips the third qubit's state if the first two qubits are in the state |1>.
- **Fredkin gate:** This gate swaps the states of the second and third qubits if the first qubit is in the state |1>.

Control operations are essential for quantum computation, as they allow for the manipulation and control of qubits. Understanding these operations is crucial for the development of quantum algorithms and quantum technology.
