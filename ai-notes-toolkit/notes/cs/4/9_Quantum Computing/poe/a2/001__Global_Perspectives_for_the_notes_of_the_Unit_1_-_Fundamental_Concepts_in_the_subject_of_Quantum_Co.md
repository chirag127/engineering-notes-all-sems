### Global Perspectives for the notes of the Unit 1 - Fundamental Concepts in the subject of Quantum Computing:

1. Quantum Computing is an emerging field of computing which harness the properties of quantum mechanics to solve complex computational problems that are intractable for traditional computers.
2. Quantum bits or 'qubits' can hold values of 0, 1, or a superposition of both, allowing a quantum computer to perform certain calculations much faster than a traditional computer.
3. Qubits can become entangled, meaning their states are dependent on each other, allowing a quantum computer to solve certain problems much more efficiently.
4. The key applications of quantum computing may include machine learning, material design, drug design, optimization and quantum chemistry. However, quantum computing is still in its infancy and there are still many technical challenges to overcome.
5. Some of the leading companies working on quantum computing include Google, IBM, Rigetti Computing, IonQ, Quantum Circuits, Inc. and Microsoft. There is active investment in quantum computing and development of early quantum computers with limited processing power.
6. There are still many open questions around how quantum computing will ultimately impact areas like cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and other fields. More research and development is needed to fully realize the promise of quantum computing.
