### Data Compression for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in Quantum Computing

1. Data compression is a technique of encoding information using fewer bits than the original representation. It reduces redundancy and irrelevant information in data to store or transmit data in an efficient form.
2. Lossless vs Lossy compression:
- Lossless compression: The original data can be perfectly reconstructed from the compressed data. e.g. ZIP files.
- Lossy compression: The original data cannot be perfectly reconstructed from the compressed data. Some amount of data is lost in compression. e.g. MP3 and JPEG.
3. Need for data compression in quantum computing:
- Quantum states are fragile and prone to decoherence which introduces errors. Error correction codes are required to recover the original state which increases the number of quantum bits (qubits) needed.
- Data compression can reduce the number of qubits needed, thereby reducing the complexity of quantum circuits and probability of errors.
4. Approaches to data compression in quantum computing:
- Quantum source coding: Exploits quantum correlations to compress quantum data more than classical methods. e.g. quantum entropy squeezing.
- Quantum channel coding: Uses quantum error-correcting codes to compress quantum information and recover it efficiently. e.g. quantum low-density parity-check codes.
