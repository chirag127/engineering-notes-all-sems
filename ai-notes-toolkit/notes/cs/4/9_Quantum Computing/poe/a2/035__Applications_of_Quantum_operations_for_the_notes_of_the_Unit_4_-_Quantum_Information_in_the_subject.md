### Applications of Quantum operations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Quantum Information in the subject of Quantum Computing:

1. Quantum Teleportation: It is the transfer of quantum state from one particle to another without moving the physical matter. It is done by transmitting classical information rather than the physical particle. Quantum information is transmitted by using quantum entanglement and quantum superposition. It can be used to transfer quantum information in quantum computing and cryptography.

2. Quantum Superdense Coding: It is a technique in which two bits of classical information can be transmitted by sending only one qubit. It exploits the quantum entanglement between two particles to transmit more information using fewer quantum states. It has applications in quantum communication to transmit more information using limited physical resources.

3. Quantum Error Correction: It is a method to protect quantum information from decoherence and other errors. The quantum information is encoded in a redundant way such that errors can be detected and corrected. It is important for building practical quantum computers and quantum communication systems. The most common approaches are to replicate the system and apply majority voting or use special quantum error correcting codes.

4. Quantum Cryptography: It allows secure communication based on the laws of quantum mechanics. The most common example is quantum key distribution which allows two parties to produce a shared random secret key known only to them which can be used to encrypt and decrypt messages. The security is based on the quantum mechanical properties like entanglement and measurement disturbance. It can be used to achieve information theoretically secure communication.
