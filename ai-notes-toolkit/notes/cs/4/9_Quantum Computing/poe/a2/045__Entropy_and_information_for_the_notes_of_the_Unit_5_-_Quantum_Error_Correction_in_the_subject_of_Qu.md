### Entropy and information for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing:

1. Entropy: It is a measure of randomness or uncertainty in a system. Higher entropy means higher disorder and uncertainty. Lower entropy means more order and predictability.
2. Information: It is something that reduces uncertainty or randomness. When some information is gained about a system, its entropy decreases. Information gain leads to more order and less randomness.
3. Quantum information: In quantum mechanics, information is represented by quantum bits or 'qubits'. Qubits can be in superposition of states and show quantum entanglement. This allows quantum information to be more powerful than classical information.
4. Qubit corruption: Qubits can get corrupted or distorted due to interaction with environment. This is called decoherence and it leads to loss of quantum information. To avoid this, quantum error correction techniques are used.
5. Quantum error correction: It is a method to detect and correct quantum errors or decoherence to protect quantum information. It uses quantum redundancy and quantum codes to recover original quantum state from corrupted state. This is crucial for building quantum computers and quantum networks.
