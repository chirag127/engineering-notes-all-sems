### Quantum noise and Quantum Operations

1. Quantum noise: The unwanted disturbance to the quantum system due to the interaction with the environment is known as quantum noise or decoherence. It destroys the quantum nature of the system and converts it into a classical system. The main sources of quantum noise are:
    - Temperature effects: At finite temperatures, the thermal fluctuations cause noise. As the temperature increases, the noise increases.
    - Vibrations: The vibrations of the atoms/molecules of the system or the surroundings can cause decoherence.
    - Electromagnetic radiation: The electromagnetic radiation from the environment can interact with the quantum system and destroy the quantum coherence.
2. Quantum Operations: The quantum operations are the ways/methods to manipulate the quantum state of a quantum system. Some of the important quantum operations are:
    - Unitary operations: The quantum operations which preserve the norm/length of the state vector. Eg.rotation operations, controlled-NOT gate, etc.
    - Measurements: The process of obtaining information about a quantum system by observing it. It causes the collapse of the quantum state into an eigenstate of the observable being measured.
    - Dynamical Decoupling: A technique to reduce the decoherence effects by applying a sequence of control pulses to the system. It helps in improving the quantum coherence.
    - Quantum Error Correction: Methods to detect and correct the errors caused by quantum noise. It is required for building a fault-tolerant quantum computer.
