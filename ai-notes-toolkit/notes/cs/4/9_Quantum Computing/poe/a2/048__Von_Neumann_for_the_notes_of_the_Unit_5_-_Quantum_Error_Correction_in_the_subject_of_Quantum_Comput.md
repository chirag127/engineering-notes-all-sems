### Von Neumann for the notes of the Unit 5 - Quantum Error Correction in the subject of Quantum Computing

1. Von Neumann entropy: It is a measure of randomness or uncertainty of a quantum state. It is defined as S = -tr(ρlogρ) where ρ is the density matrix of the quantum state. It is maximum for maximally mixed state and minimum for pure states.
2. Quantum error correction codes: These are methods to encode quantum information in a redundant way such that errors can be detected and corrected. The basic steps are:
- Encode the quantum state into a larger Hilbert space. This introduces redundancy.
- The encoding is done in such a way that errors will manifest themselves as errors that can be corrected.
- Perform measurements to detect errors and apply correction operations to restore the quantum state to the original code space.
Some common types of quantum error correcting codes are:
- Steane code: Encodes 1 qubit into 7 qubits. Can correct 1 error.
- Shor code: Encodes 1 qubit into 9 qubits. Can correct 1 error.
- Surface code: Encodes 1 qubit into many qubits. Can correct multiple errors. Has high fault-tolerance.
