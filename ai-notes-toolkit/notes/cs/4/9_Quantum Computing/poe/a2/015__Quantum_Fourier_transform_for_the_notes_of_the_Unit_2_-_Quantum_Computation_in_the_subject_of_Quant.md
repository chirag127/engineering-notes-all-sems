### Quantum Fourier transform for the notes of the Unit 2 - Quantum Computation

1. Quantum Fourier transform (QFT) is a key concept in quantum algorithms like Shor's algorithm and quantum phase estimation algorithm.
2. QFT is a linear map that transforms quantum bits (qubits) from the computational basis to the Fourier basis.
3. The Fourier basis consists of states |w> = ∑_(j=0)^(2^n-1) w_j|j> where w is an nth root of unity, w = e^(2*π*i/2^n).
4. QFT can be efficiently implemented on a quantum computer using quantum gates like Hadamard gates and controlled phase shift gates.
5. The inverse quantum Fourier transform (QFT^-1) can be used to map qubits back from the Fourier basis to the computational basis. QFT and QFT^-1 can be defined in terms of each other.
6. QFT plays an important role in solving problems like integer factorization that are believed to be classically hard by using quantum algorithms like Shor's algorithm. QFT helps extract the period of a function in Shor's algorithm.
