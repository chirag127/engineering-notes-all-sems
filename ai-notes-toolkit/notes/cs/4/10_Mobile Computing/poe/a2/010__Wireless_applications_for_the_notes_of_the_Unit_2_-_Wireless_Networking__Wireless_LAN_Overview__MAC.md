### Wireless applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

1. Wireless LANs: Wi-Fi, IEEE 802.11 standards (a, b, g, n, ac)
- Connect devices in local area without wires
- Uses radio frequency to communicate
- Data rates up to 600 Mbps for 802.11ac
- Range up to 100 meters indoor and more with multiple access points
- Security issues and solutions like WPA2, WEP

2. Bluetooth: Short-range radio technology
- Connect nearby devices like headphones, speakers, keyboards
- Data rates up to 24 Mbps for Bluetooth 5
- Limited range of 10 meters
- Used for wireless personal area networks
- Security modes: non-authenticated and authenticated

3. Other wireless technologies: ZigBee, RFID, NFC
- ZigBee: Low data rate, low power consumption, used in smart home devices
- RFID: Used for contactless identification and tracking of objects
- NFC: Enables short-range wireless data exchange between devices in close proximity

4. Comparison of different wireless technologies
- Data rate, range/coverage, power requirements, cost, security
- Select technology based on application requirements
