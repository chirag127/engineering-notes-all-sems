### Data Broadcasting

- Data broadcasting refers to the transmission of data over a wide area to a large number of recipients simultaneously.
- The data is transmitted by the sender and is received by all receivers within range.
- Receivers filter the data and extract information of interest to them.
- Data broadcasting is mainly used to efficiently distribute news, sports results, stock quotes, weather information, etc. to a large number of users.
- The main advantages of data broadcasting are:
  - bandwidth efficiency
  - low cost of transmission as data is sent only once
  - widespread dissemination of information

### Wireless LAN Overview

- A wireless LAN (WLAN) is a flexible data communication system implemented as an extension to or as an alternative for a wired LAN.
- Wireless LANs use radio frequency or infrared technology for communication between devices.
- The major benefits of WLANs are:
  - Mobility - devices can move around and remain connected to the network.
  - Flexibility - easy to reconfigure and install.
  - Lower installation costs - no cabling required.
- The disadvantages of WLANs are:
  - Limited range
  - Interference from other devices
  - Security vulnerabilities
