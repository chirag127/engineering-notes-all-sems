### GPRS for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM:

1. General Packet Radio Service (GPRS) is a packet oriented mobile data service on the 2G and 3G cellular communication system's global system for mobile communications (GSM).
2. It provides data rates from 56 kbit/s up to 114 kbit/s with continuous connection to the internet for mobile phones and computers.
3. It is used for services such as Wireless Application Protocol (WAP) access, Multimedia Messaging Service (MMS), and for Internet communication services such as email and World Wide Web access.
4. GPRS is integrated into GSM Release 97 and newer releases. It allows GSM, TDMA and CDMA based networks to transmit IP packets efficiently.
5. Data is sent in packets, allowing efficient transfer of data and efficient use of bandwidth. Hence, GPRS is considered a 2.5G technology as it provides faster data speeds than the older Circuit Switched Data (CSD) used in 2G networks.
