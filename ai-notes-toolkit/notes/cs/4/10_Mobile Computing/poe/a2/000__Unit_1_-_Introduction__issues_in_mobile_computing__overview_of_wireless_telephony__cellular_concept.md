## Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM:

1. Introduction
- Mobile computing: Use of computing devices while on the move. Eg: Laptops, tablets, mobile phones.
- Issues: Limited power, small screen size, limited storage, expensive data transfer, connectivity issues.

2. Wireless Telephony
- Allows wireless exchange of voice and data signals. Uses radio waves to establish connection between network and devices.
- Cellular concept: Geographic area divided into cells with low power transmitters/receivers. Frequency reuse allows increased capacity.
- GSM: Global System for Mobile communications. Digital cellular network standard. Uses TDMA/FDMA technology. Provides voice and data services with roaming capabilities.
