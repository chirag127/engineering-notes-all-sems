### Location Management: HLR-VLR, Hierarchical, Handoffs

- HLR: Home Location Register - permanent database of subscriber information
- VLR: Visitor Location Register - temporary database of subscriber information for roaming users
- Hierarchical structure of HLRs and VLRs for scalability
- Handoffs: Process of transferring an ongoing call or data session from one cell to another or from one base station to another
- Necessary for mobility - seamless connectivity
- Types: Hard handoff (break before make), Soft handoff (make before break)

This covers the key points regarding location management and handoffs which are important concepts in wireless telephony and mobile computing. The hierarchical structure of HLRs and VLRs enables scalability in networks with millions of subscribers. Handoffs are essential for mobility and seamless connectivity as mobile devices move across cells.
