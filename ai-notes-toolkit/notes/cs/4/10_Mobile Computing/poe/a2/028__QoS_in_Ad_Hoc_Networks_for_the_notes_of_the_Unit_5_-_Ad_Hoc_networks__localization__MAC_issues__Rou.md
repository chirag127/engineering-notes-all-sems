### QoS in Ad Hoc Networks

- Providing QoS in ad hoc networks is challenging due to dynamic topology and distributed nature.
- Key QoS parameters in ad hoc networks are bandwidth, delay, jitter, and packet loss.
- Bandwidth allocation is difficult due to shared wireless medium and variable channel conditions.
- Delay and jitter are difficult to guarantee due to multi-hop forwarding and contention for channel access.
- Packet loss can be high due to channel errors, collisions, and buffer overflows.
- Some approaches to provide QoS in ad hoc networks:
-- Prioritize important traffic and reserve bandwidth for it.
-- Use scheduling and channel access mechanisms to provide differentiated services.
-- Choose routing paths that can meet QoS requirements.
-- Apply admission control to regulate incoming traffic and avoid overloading network.
-- Use cross-layer optimization and jointly consider functions at multiple layers to provide QoS.

- More research is needed to fully realize QoS in ad hoc networks due to their inherent complexities. Robust and distributed QoS solutions that work with minimal coordination are desirable.
