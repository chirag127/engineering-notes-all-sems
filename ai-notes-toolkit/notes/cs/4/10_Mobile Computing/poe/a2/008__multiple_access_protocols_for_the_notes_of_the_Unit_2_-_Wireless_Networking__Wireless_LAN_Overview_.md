### Multiple Access Protocols
- Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA): Devices detect carrier before transmitting. If busy, wait for random time and retry. Can lead to collisions.
- Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (CSMA/CA): Devices use handshaking to reserve medium before transmitting. Avoid collisions. Used in Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.
- Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA): Medium is divided into time slots which are allocated to devices. Synchronization required. Used in GSM, IS-136, PDC, iDEN.
- Frequency Division Multiple Access (FDMA): Medium is divided into frequency bands/channels and allocated to devices. Requires frequency planning. Used in AMPS, TIA/EIA-136, PDC.
- Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA): Distinct codes are assigned to devices and signals spread across spectrum. Allows many signals to share spectrum. Complex receiver required. Used in CDMA2000, WCDMA, HSPA.
