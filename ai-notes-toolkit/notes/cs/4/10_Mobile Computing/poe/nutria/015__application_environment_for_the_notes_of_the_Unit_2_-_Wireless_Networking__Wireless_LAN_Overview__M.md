
### Application Environment for Unit 2 - Wireless Networking

* Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless
  * MAC (Media Access Control) issues: 
    * MAC addresses are the unique identifiers assigned to each device connected to a network 
    * MAC addresses are used to identify the source and destination of a data frame 
    * MAC address filtering is used to control access to a network
  * IEEE 802.11: 
    * 802.11 is a set of standards that define how wireless networks communicate
    * 802.11a, b, g, n, and ac are the different versions of the 802.11 standard
    * 802.11b is the most widely used standard
  * Bluetooth: 
    * Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication
    * It is used for connecting devices such as keyboards, mice, and headphones
  * Wireless: 
    * Wireless networks provide connectivity without the need for cables
    * Wireless networks are typically secured with WPA or WPA2 encryption
