
### Wireless Networks

* Wireless networks are networks that use radio waves to communicate between devices. 
* Wireless networks are used in many different settings, from home networks to large corporate networks. 
* Wireless networks offer many advantages over wired networks, such as portability, convenience, and cost savings. 
* Data management issues are a major concern when using wireless networks, as data can be easily intercepted or corrupted. 
* Data replication for mobile computers is an effective way to ensure data is backed up and secure. 
* Adaptive clustering for mobile networks is a technique used to optimize the performance of wireless networks by grouping devices together and routing traffic more efficiently.
