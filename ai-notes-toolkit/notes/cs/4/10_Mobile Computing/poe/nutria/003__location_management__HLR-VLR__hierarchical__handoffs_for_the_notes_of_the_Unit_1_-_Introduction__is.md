
### Location Management: HLR-VLR, Hierarchical, Handoffs

This section provides an introduction to location management in mobile computing, including an overview of wireless telephony and cellular concepts, as well as the Global System for Mobile Communications (GSM).

* **HLR-VLR**: The Home Location Register (HLR) is a database that stores information about each subscriber, such as their phone number, service profile, and current location. The Visitor Location Register (VLR) is a database that stores information about each subscriber who is currently in the coverage area of the network. 

* **Hierarchical**: Location management is hierarchical in nature, meaning that the HLR and VLR are organized in a hierarchical structure, with the HLR at the top and the VLR at the bottom.

* **Handoffs**: Handoffs are the process of transferring a call from one base station to another as the user moves through the network. The handoff process involves the exchange of information between the HLR and VLR, and is necessary to ensure that the user's call is not dropped as they move.
