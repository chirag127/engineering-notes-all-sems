
### Data Broadcasting for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

- Wireless networking is the practice of connecting two or more computing devices using wireless communication. This can include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and other technologies.
- MAC (Media Access Control) issues refer to the way in which multiple devices connected to a wireless network can access the same media.
- IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards developed by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) for wireless networking.
- Bluetooth is a wireless technology standard used for exchanging data between fixed and mobile devices over short distances.
- Wireless in mobile computing refers to the use of wireless communication technology in portable devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops.
