
### Protocol Stack for Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Bluetooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

* Wireless networking is a form of communication between two or more devices using radio waves.
* The Media Access Control (MAC) layer is responsible for controlling access to the physical network medium.
* The IEEE 802.11 standard is the most widely used wireless LAN protocol. It defines the physical and MAC layers of wireless communication.
* Bluetooth is a short-range wireless communication technology used primarily for connecting devices such as headsets and keyboards to computers.
* Wireless networks are used in a variety of scenarios, such as providing internet access in public places, connecting home networks, and providing access to corporate networks.
