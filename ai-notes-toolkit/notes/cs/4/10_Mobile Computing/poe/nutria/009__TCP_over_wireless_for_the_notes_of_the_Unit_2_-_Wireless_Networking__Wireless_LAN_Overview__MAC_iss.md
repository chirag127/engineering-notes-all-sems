
### TCP over Wireless 

- Wireless networking is the use of wireless technology to transfer data between two or more devices. 
- Wireless LANs (Local Area Networks) use radio waves to enable communication between devices. 
- The IEEE 802.11 standard is a set of standards for wireless LANs. It defines the physical and MAC (Media Access Control) layers of the network. 
- Bluetooth is a wireless technology that allows devices to communicate with each other over short distances. It is often used for connecting peripherals such as keyboards, mice, and headphones to a computer. 
- Wireless networks are used in mobile computing to allow devices to connect to the internet while on the move. 
- TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) is a protocol used for reliable data transmission over a network. 
- TCP can be used in wireless networks to provide reliable data transmission. 
- The MAC layer of the network is responsible for ensuring that data is transmitted reliably and efficiently. 
- In order to ensure reliable data transmission, the MAC layer uses techniques such as error detection and retransmission.
