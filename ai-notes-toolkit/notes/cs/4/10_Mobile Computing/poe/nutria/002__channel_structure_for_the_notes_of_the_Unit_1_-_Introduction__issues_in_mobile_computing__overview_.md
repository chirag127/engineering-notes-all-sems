
### Channel Structure for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction, Issues in Mobile Computing, Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular Concept, GSM:

1. Introduction: Mobile computing is the use of computing devices and services to perform tasks and activities away from a fixed location. It is a form of ubiquitous computing that enables users to access and use applications and data from any location.

2. Issues in Mobile Computing: Mobile computing presents a number of challenges due to the physical limitations of mobile devices, such as limited battery life, small display size, limited memory and processing power, and limited network bandwidth.

3. Overview of Wireless Telephony: Cellular concept is based on the division of the geographic area into small cells, each of which is served by a base station. The base station provides radio coverage to the cell, and is connected to the mobile switching center (MSC) via a fixed network.

4. GSM: Global System for Mobile Communications (GSM) is a digital cellular network that is used for mobile communication. It is the most widely used cellular network in the world and is based on the Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA) technology. GSM provides a variety of services such as voice, data, multimedia, and messaging services.
