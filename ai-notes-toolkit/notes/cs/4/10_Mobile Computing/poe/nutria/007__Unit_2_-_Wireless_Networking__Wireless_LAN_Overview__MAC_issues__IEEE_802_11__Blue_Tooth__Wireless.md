

## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

* Wireless networking is a type of networking technology that allows devices to communicate with each other without the need for physical cables.
* Wireless LAN (Local Area Network) is a type of wireless networking technology that is used to connect computers, laptops, and other devices within a limited area such as a home, office, or campus.
* MAC (Media Access Control) issues are related to the way data is transmitted over a network. The MAC address is a unique identifier for each device on a network, and it is used to control access to the network.
* IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for wireless networking technology. It specifies the physical layer and the media access control layer of wireless networks.
* Bluetooth is a wireless technology that is used to connect devices over short ranges. It is often used to connect wireless headsets and other devices to computers and phones.
* Wireless is a general term that is used to refer to any type of wireless communication. It can refer to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or other types of wireless communication.
