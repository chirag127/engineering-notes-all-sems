
### Applications for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC Issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the Subject of Mobile Computing

1. IEEE 802.11: This is a set of standards created by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) that defines the physical layer, medium access control (MAC), and logical link control (LLC) layers of wireless networks. It is the most widely used wireless network protocol and is used in most Wi-Fi networks.

2. MAC Issues: Medium Access Control (MAC) is a layer of the OSI model that is responsible for controlling access to the wireless medium. It is responsible for the coordination of devices accessing the network, as well as the distribution of frames to the intended destination.

3. Bluetooth: This is a wireless technology that enables short-range communication between two or more devices. It is used for a variety of applications such as transferring files, streaming audio, and connecting devices to the internet.

4. Wireless in Mobile Computing: Wireless networks are used in mobile computing to enable users to access the internet and other services while on the move. Wireless networks are convenient and easy to use, and are becoming increasingly popular for mobile devices.
