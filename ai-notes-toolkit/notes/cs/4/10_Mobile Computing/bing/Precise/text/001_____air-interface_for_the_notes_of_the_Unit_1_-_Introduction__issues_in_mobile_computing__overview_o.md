### Air-Interface

Air-interface is the physical and logical connection between the mobile station and the base station in a wireless communication system. It is the part of the radio transmission path that exists between the mobile station and the base station. The air-interface is responsible for transmitting and receiving signals between the mobile station and the base station.

#### Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM

- Mobile computing refers to the use of computing devices that are portable and can be used while on the move.
- Issues in mobile computing include limited battery life, security concerns, and the need for reliable and fast wireless connectivity.
- Wireless telephony is the transmission of voice and data over a wireless network.
- The cellular concept is a system design approach that divides a large geographic area into smaller cells, each with its own base station.
- GSM (Global System for Mobile Communications) is a standard for digital mobile telephony that is widely used around the world. It is based on the cellular concept and uses digital modulation techniques to transmit voice and data over the air-interface.
