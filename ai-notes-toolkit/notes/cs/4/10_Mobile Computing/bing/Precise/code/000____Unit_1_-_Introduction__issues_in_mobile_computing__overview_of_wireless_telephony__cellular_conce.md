## Unit 1 - Introduction, issues in mobile computing, overview of wireless telephony: cellular concept, GSM:

1. **Introduction:** Mobile computing refers to the use of computing devices that are portable and can be used while on the move. These devices include smartphones, tablets, laptops, and wearable devices. Mobile computing has revolutionized the way we live, work, and communicate.

2. **Issues in mobile computing:** Some of the issues in mobile computing include security, privacy, data management, and connectivity. Mobile devices are vulnerable to security threats such as malware and hacking. Privacy is also a concern as personal information can be accessed by unauthorized parties. Data management is important as mobile devices have limited storage capacity. Connectivity is also an issue as mobile devices rely on wireless networks which may not always be available.

3. **Overview of wireless telephony:** Wireless telephony refers to the use of wireless communication technology to make telephone calls. This technology has evolved over the years and has led to the development of cellular networks.

4. **Cellular concept:** The cellular concept is the foundation of mobile communication. It involves dividing a geographical area into smaller regions called cells. Each cell is served by a base station which transmits and receives signals from mobile devices within the cell.

5. **GSM:** Global System for Mobile Communications (GSM) is a standard for mobile communication. It is widely used in Europe and other parts of the world. GSM uses digital technology and offers features such as voice and data services, text messaging, and roaming. GSM operates in the 900 MHz and 1800 MHz frequency bands.
