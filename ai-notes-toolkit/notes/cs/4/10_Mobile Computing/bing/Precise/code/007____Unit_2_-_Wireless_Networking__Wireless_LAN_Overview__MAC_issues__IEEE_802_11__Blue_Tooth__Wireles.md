## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

1. **Wireless Networking** refers to the use of wireless technology to connect devices to a network, allowing them to communicate and exchange data without the need for physical connections.
2. **Wireless LAN (WLAN)** is a type of wireless network that uses high-frequency radio waves to provide wireless connectivity to devices within a limited area, such as a home, office, or campus.
3. **MAC (Media Access Control)** issues in wireless networking refer to the challenges of coordinating access to the shared wireless medium among multiple devices. This is necessary to prevent collisions and ensure efficient use of the available bandwidth.
4. **IEEE 802.11** is a set of standards for implementing wireless local area network (WLAN) computer communication in the 2.4, 3.6, 5, and 60 GHz frequency bands. It is commonly referred to as Wi-Fi.
5. **Bluetooth** is a short-range wireless technology used for exchanging data between fixed and mobile devices over short distances. It is commonly used for wireless communication between devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops, as well as for wireless peripherals such as keyboards, mice, and headphones.
6. **Wireless** refers to the use of electromagnetic waves, such as radio waves, to transmit information through the air, without the use of wires or other physical connections. It is commonly used for communication between devices, as well as for broadcasting and telecommunications.
