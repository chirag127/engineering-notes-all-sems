### Applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - Ad Hoc networks, localization, MAC issues, Routing protocols, global state routing (GSR), in the subject of Mobile Computing

1. **Ad Hoc Networks**: The ad hoc network architecture can be utilized for continuous business applications and corporate organizations to expand efficiency and benefit. The ad hoc networks can be arranged by their application as Mobile Ad hoc Network (MANET) which is a self-organizing infrastructure-less system of cell phones communicated through wireless connection.
2. **Military Application**: Ad hoc wireless networks can be very useful in establishing communication among a group of soldiers in a battlefield.
3. **Sensor Networks**: Ad hoc networks composed of surviving and added nodes. Activated-on-demand, embedded, pre-deployable sensors.
4. **Rule-based Systems Security**: Quickly adaptable policies for necessary unplanned access.
5. **Grid Technologies**: Uniform access to distributed, heterogeneous computational resources.
