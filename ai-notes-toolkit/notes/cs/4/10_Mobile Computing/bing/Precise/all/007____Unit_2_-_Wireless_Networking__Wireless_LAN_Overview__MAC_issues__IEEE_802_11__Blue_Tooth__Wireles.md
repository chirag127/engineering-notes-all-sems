## Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless

1. **Wireless Networking** refers to the use of wireless technology to connect devices to a network, allowing them to communicate and share data without the need for physical connections.
2. **Wireless LAN (WLAN)** is a type of wireless network that uses high-frequency radio waves to provide wireless connectivity to devices within a limited area, such as a home, office, or campus.
3. **MAC (Media Access Control)** issues in wireless networking refer to the challenges of coordinating access to the shared wireless medium among multiple devices. This is necessary to avoid collisions and ensure efficient use of the available bandwidth.
4. **IEEE 802.11** is a set of standards for implementing wireless LANs. It defines the physical and MAC layers of the technology and includes several variations, such as 802.11a, 802.11b, 802.11g, and 802.11n, each with different characteristics and capabilities.
5. **Bluetooth** is a short-range wireless technology used for personal area networking (PAN). It allows devices to communicate and share data over short distances, typically within 10 meters.
6. **Wireless** refers to the use of electromagnetic waves, such as radio waves, to transmit information over the air, without the need for physical connections. It is used in a wide range of applications, including wireless networking, mobile communications, and satellite communications.
