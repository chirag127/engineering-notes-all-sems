# Applications for the notes of the Unit 2 - Wireless Networking, Wireless LAN Overview: MAC issues, IEEE 802.11, Blue Tooth, Wireless in the subject of Mobile Computing

1. Wireless networking allows for the connection of multiple devices without the need for cables or wires.
2. Wireless LANs (Local Area Networks) provide a flexible and convenient way to connect devices within a limited area, such as a home, office, or campus.
3. MAC (Media Access Control) issues in wireless networking include addressing and channel access, which are essential for the efficient and reliable transmission of data.
4. IEEE 802.11 is a set of standards for implementing wireless LANs and includes several specifications for different frequency bands and data rates.
5. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology used for exchanging data between devices, such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops.
6. Wireless technology is increasingly being used in a variety of applications, including mobile computing, where it allows for the use of portable devices without the need for a physical connection to a network.
