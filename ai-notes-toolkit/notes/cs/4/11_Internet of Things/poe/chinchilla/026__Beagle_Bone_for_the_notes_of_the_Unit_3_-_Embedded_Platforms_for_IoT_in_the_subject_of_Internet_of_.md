### Beagle Bone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Beagle Bone is an embedded platform used for Internet of Things (IoT) applications.
- It is a single-board computer (SBC) that provides a low-cost, low-power, and high-performance solution for building IoT devices.
- Beagle Bone is based on Texas Instruments' Sitara ARM Cortex-A8 processor, which runs at a clock speed of 1 GHz.
- It has 512 MB of RAM and 4 GB of eMMC flash storage, which makes it suitable for running complex applications.
- Beagle Bone has a variety of ports and interfaces, including HDMI, USB, Ethernet, and GPIO, which enables it to interface with various sensors and actuators.
- It also has onboard Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity, which allows it to communicate with other devices wirelessly.
- Beagle Bone comes with Debian Linux pre-installed, which makes it easy to develop software for IoT applications.
- It also supports various programming languages, including Python, C++, and JavaScript, which makes it easy to write code for IoT devices.
- Beagle Bone is an open-source platform, which means that its hardware design and software source code are freely available to the public.
- This makes it easy for developers to modify and customize the platform to suit their specific needs.
- Beagle Bone is widely used in various IoT applications, such as home automation, robotics, and industrial control systems.
- Its low cost, high performance, and open-source nature make it a popular choice for building IoT devices.
