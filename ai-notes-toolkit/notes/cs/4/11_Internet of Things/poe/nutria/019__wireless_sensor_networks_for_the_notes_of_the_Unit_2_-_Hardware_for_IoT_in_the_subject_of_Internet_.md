
### Wireless Sensor Networks for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) are networks of interconnected nodes that use wireless communication to collect and transmit data.
2. WSNs are used for a variety of applications, including environmental monitoring, industrial automation, and military surveillance.
3. Each node in a WSN is typically equipped with a radio transceiver, an antenna, a processor, and a power source.
4. Nodes in a WSN may be stationary or mobile, and may be able to communicate with each other directly or through a base station.
5. WSNs are typically deployed in hostile or remote environments, and must be designed to be robust and reliable.
6. WSNs are a key component of the Internet of Things (IoT), as they enable the collection and transmission of data from sensors and other devices.
