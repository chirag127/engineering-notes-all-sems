
### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

* The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, such as sensors, actuators, and other items embedded with electronics, software, and connectivity, which enable objects to collect and exchange data. 
* IoT can be used to monitor, control, and automate physical objects, such as buildings, vehicles, and machines, which are connected to the internet. 
* IoT architecture consists of the following components: 
    * Sensors and Actuators: These are the physical devices that collect data from the environment and send it to the cloud for processing. 
    * Network: This is the communication medium used to transfer data from the sensors to the cloud. It can be wired or wireless. 
    * Cloud Platform: This is the platform where data is stored and processed. It can be a public, private, or hybrid cloud.
* Application: This is the software application that is used to view and analyze the data collected from the sensors. It can be a web or mobile application. 
* Security: This is the layer of security that is used to protect the data collected from the sensors and the application. It can be a firewall, encryption, or authentication.
