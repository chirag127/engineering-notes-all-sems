
### Smart Cards for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Smart cards are small, portable devices that contain information about a user or device. 
2. They are used to authenticate users and devices to access information or services.
3. Smart cards are typically embedded with a microchip and a secure operating system, and they are usually connected to a network or system via a contactless interface.
4. Smart cards can be used in a variety of applications, including authentication, payment systems, data storage, and access control.
5. Smart cards can also be used to store and transfer data, such as medical records, financial information, and customer loyalty programs.
6. They are often used in Internet of Things (IoT) applications, such as smart home systems, to help manage access control and security.
7. Smart cards can also be used in industrial settings to help control access to machines, equipment, and other resources.
8. Smart cards are a secure and convenient way to store and transfer data, and they are becoming increasingly popular in the world of IoT.
