
## Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

* Embedded platforms are systems that are designed to support Internet of Things (IoT) applications. 
* They are designed to be compact, low power, and cost-effective. 
* Embedded platforms are used in a variety of applications, including home automation, industrial automation, medical devices, and consumer electronics.
* Embedded platforms typically consist of a microcontroller, memory, and peripherals. 
* Microcontrollers are the central processing units of embedded systems and are responsible for executing instructions and controlling peripherals. 
* Memory is used to store instructions and data for the microcontroller. 
* Peripherals are components that are used to interact with the environment, such as sensors, actuators, and communication devices.
* Embedded platforms also include an operating system, which is responsible for managing the system resources and providing an interface for applications.
* Popular operating systems for embedded platforms include Linux, FreeRTOS, and Contiki. 
* To develop applications for embedded platforms, developers use a variety of programming languages, such as C, C++, and Python.
