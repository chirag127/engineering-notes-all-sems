
### Definition of Internet of Things (IoT)

1. Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as sensors, actuators, and other devices, that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other to exchange data and perform tasks.

2. IoT devices can be used to monitor and control physical objects in the environment, such as temperature, humidity, and other environmental conditions.

3. IoT devices can also be used to monitor and control the behavior of people, such as tracking their movements or providing feedback on their activities.

4. IoT devices can also be used to automate processes, such as managing inventory, controlling access to buildings, or providing information about traffic conditions.

5. IoT devices can be used to create new business models, such as providing services based on the data collected from connected devices.
