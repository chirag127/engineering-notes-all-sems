
### Design Challenges for the Notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Security: One of the biggest challenges in the Internet of Things is ensuring that devices remain secure from malicious actors. This includes protecting against data breaches, preventing unauthorized access to devices, and ensuring that data is not stolen or misused.

2. Interoperability: Another challenge for the Internet of Things is ensuring that devices from different manufacturers can communicate with one another. This requires a common set of protocols and standards to be used across the industry.

3. Scalability: As the number of connected devices grows, it is important to ensure that the infrastructure that supports them is able to scale to meet the demand. This includes ensuring that networks can handle the increased traffic, and that data centers are able to store and process the data.

4. Privacy: As more data is collected from connected devices, it is important to ensure that users' privacy is respected. This includes ensuring that data is not shared without the user's consent, and that data is encrypted when it is transmitted.

5. Reliability: The Internet of Things must be reliable in order to be useful. This includes ensuring that devices remain connected, and that data is transmitted accurately and in a timely manner.
