
### Automotive Applications for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Automotive applications of the Internet of Things (IoT) are becoming increasingly important in the modern world.
- Automotive applications of the IoT can be used for a variety of purposes, such as monitoring vehicle performance, providing real-time traffic information, and managing vehicle fleets.
- Automotive IoT applications can also be used to improve safety and security, by providing alerts for potential hazards and enabling remote access to vehicles.
- Automotive IoT applications can also enable connected services, such as remote diagnostics and maintenance, as well as providing access to real-time data about vehicle performance.
- Automotive IoT applications can also provide access to vehicle information, such as fuel levels, tire pressure, and maintenance records.
- Automotive IoT applications can also be used to provide tracking and navigation services, as well as providing access to real-time traffic information.
- Automotive IoT applications can also enable automated driving, by providing access to real-time data about the environment and the vehicle's performance.
