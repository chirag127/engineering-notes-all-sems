
### Raspberry pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation in the United Kingdom.
- It is a low-cost, credit-card sized computer that plugs into a computer monitor or TV, and uses a standard keyboard and mouse.
- The Raspberry Pi is capable of running a wide range of software, including the Linux operating system, programming languages such as Python and Scratch, and office suite programs such as LibreOffice.
- It is also capable of connecting to the internet and other devices through Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
- Raspberry Pi can be used to create an Internet of Things (IoT) platform by connecting sensors, actuators, and other devices to the board.
- The board can also be used to create embedded systems for controlling robotics, home automation, and other applications.
- Raspberry Pi is a popular platform for learning to program, as it provides an inexpensive, easy-to-use platform for experimentation and exploration.
