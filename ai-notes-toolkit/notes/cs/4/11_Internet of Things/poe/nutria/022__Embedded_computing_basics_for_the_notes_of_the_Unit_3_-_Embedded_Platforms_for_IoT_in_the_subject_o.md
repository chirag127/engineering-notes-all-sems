
### Embedded Computing Basics for Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. Embedded computing is a type of computing that is used to control and monitor physical devices, such as embedded systems and Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
2. Embedded systems are computer systems that are designed to perform a specific function within a larger system. They are typically used in applications such as industrial automation, medical devices, and consumer electronics.
3. Embedded systems are typically programmed in a low-level language such as C or assembly language.
4. Embedded systems are often used in IoT devices, which are connected devices that can collect and transmit data over a network.
5. IoT devices typically use embedded systems to control and monitor physical devices in a networked environment.
6. Embedded systems are used in a variety of applications, including industrial automation, medical devices, consumer electronics, and automotive systems.
7. Embedded systems use a variety of components, including microcontrollers, sensors, memory, and communication interfaces.
8. Embedded systems are designed to be highly reliable and efficient, and must often meet strict safety and security requirements.
