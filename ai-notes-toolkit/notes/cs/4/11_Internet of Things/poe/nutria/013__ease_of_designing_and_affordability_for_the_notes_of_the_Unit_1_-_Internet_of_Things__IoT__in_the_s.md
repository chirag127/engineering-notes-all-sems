
### Ease of Designing and Affordability for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the Subject of Internet of Things

* The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or "things" embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity that enable them to collect and exchange data.
* IoT devices are relatively inexpensive, making them affordable for many people.
* Designing an IoT system can be done with minimal technical knowledge, as many IoT platforms provide easy-to-use tools and software for prototyping and development.
* IoT systems are designed to be secure, making them ideal for use in a variety of applications.
* IoT systems are also designed to be highly scalable, allowing for the addition of new devices and features as needed.
* IoT systems are designed to be energy efficient, helping to reduce energy costs and environmental impact.
