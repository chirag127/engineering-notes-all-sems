
### MAC Protocol Survey for Unit 4 - Network & Communication Aspects in IoT

1. Media Access Control (MAC) protocols are the methods used by devices on a network to access the medium of communication.
2. The most common MAC protocol used in the Internet of Things (IoT) is the Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Avoidance (CSMA/CA) protocol.
3. CSMA/CA is a protocol that uses a combination of carrier sensing (listening) and collision avoidance (waiting) to determine when a device can access the medium of communication.
4. CSMA/CA is used in both wired and wireless networks, but is more commonly used in wireless networks due to the lack of a physical connection between devices.
5. The basic principle of CSMA/CA is that each device on the network listens for a free channel before transmitting. If the channel is free, the device will transmit its data. If the channel is not free, the device will wait a certain amount of time before attempting to transmit again.
6. Other MAC protocols used in the IoT include Carrier Sense Multiple Access with Collision Detection (CSMA/CD) and Token Passing.
7. CSMA/CD is a protocol that uses a combination of carrier sensing and collision detection to determine when a device can access the medium of communication.
8. Token Passing is a protocol in which a token is passed from one device to the next, allowing each device to access the medium of communication in turn.
9. MAC protocols are an important part of the network and communication aspects of the Internet of Things, as they allow devices to communicate with each other and access the medium of communication.
