

### Design Principles for Connected Devices for the Notes of Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

1. Connected devices should be designed with security and privacy in mind. This includes the use of encryption and authentication protocols to ensure data is only accessible to authorized users.

2. Connected devices should be designed with the user experience in mind. This includes the use of intuitive user interfaces and the ability to easily configure and update settings.

3. Connected devices should be designed with scalability in mind. This includes the ability to handle large amounts of data and support multiple users and devices.

4. Connected devices should be designed with energy efficiency in mind. This includes the use of low-power components and technologies to minimize energy consumption.

5. Connected devices should be designed with interoperability in mind. This includes the use of open standards and protocols to ensure compatibility with other devices and services.
