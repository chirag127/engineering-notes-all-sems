
## Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT

1. Security: One of the main challenges of the Internet of Things (IoT) is the security of connected devices. IoT devices are vulnerable to cyber-attacks due to their lack of built-in security measures. As a result, hackers can access and manipulate IoT devices, leading to data theft, denial of service, and other malicious activities.

2. Privacy: IoT devices can collect and store large amounts of data, including personal information. As a result, there is a risk of data leakage, which can lead to privacy violations.

3. Interoperability: IoT devices often use different communication protocols and standards, which can lead to compatibility issues between different devices. This can make it difficult for devices to communicate with each other, leading to a lack of interoperability.

4. Scalability: As the number of IoT devices increases, it can become difficult to manage them all. This can lead to scalability issues, making it difficult to maintain the network.

5. Reliability: IoT devices are often unreliable due to their limited processing power and limited battery life. As a result, they can become unresponsive or malfunction, leading to unreliable services.
