
### Digital Sensors for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the Subject of Internet of Things

* Digital sensors are devices that measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, humidity, light, and sound, and convert them into digital signals that can be read and processed by computers.
* Digital sensors are used in a wide range of applications, such as robotics, home automation, medical devices, industrial automation, and the Internet of Things (IoT).
* Digital sensors are usually connected to a microcontroller or computer, which processes the data and sends it to a remote server or cloud service for further analysis.
* Digital sensors come in many different forms, such as contact, non-contact, active, and passive.
* Contact sensors measure physical quantities by directly touching the object or surface being measured. Examples include thermocouples, pressure sensors, and strain gauges.
* Non-contact sensors measure physical quantities without physical contact. Examples include infrared sensors and ultrasonic sensors.
* Active sensors require an external power source to operate, while passive sensors do not.
* Digital sensors can be used to measure a wide range of physical quantities, such as temperature, pressure, humidity, light, and sound. They can also be used to detect motion, detect objects, and measure distances.
