
### Ardunio Platform Boards Anatomy 

1. The Ardunio platform consists of a range of boards, including the Uno, Mega, Nano, and Micro. 
2. The Uno is the most popular board, and is the best choice for beginners. It has 14 digital I/O pins, 6 analog input pins, and a USB port. 
3. The Mega has more digital I/O pins (54) and more analog inputs (16) than the Uno. It also has a USB port. 
4. The Nano is a smaller version of the Uno, and is great for projects that require a smaller form factor. It has 14 digital I/O pins, 8 analog inputs, and a USB port. 
5. The Micro is the smallest and most powerful board in the Ardunio range. It has 20 digital I/O pins, 12 analog inputs, and a USB port. 
6. All of the boards are powered by the same microcontroller and have the same programming language, which makes them interchangeable. 
7. Programming the Ardunio is relatively simple, and can be done using the Ardunio IDE. The Ardunio IDE is a free, open source development environment that can be used to write, compile, and upload code to the Ardunio boards.
