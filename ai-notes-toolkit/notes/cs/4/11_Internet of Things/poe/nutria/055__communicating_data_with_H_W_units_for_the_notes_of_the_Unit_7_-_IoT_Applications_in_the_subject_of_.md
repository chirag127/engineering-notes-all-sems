
### Communicating Data with H/W Units for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

1. Hardware units are the physical components of an Internet of Things (IoT) system. These components are used to collect, store, and process data.
2. Communication between hardware units is essential for the functioning of an IoT system. The data collected by the hardware units must be transmitted to other components of the system.
3. There are several methods of communication between hardware units, including wired and wireless protocols. Wired protocols include Ethernet and USB, while wireless protocols include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Zigbee.
4. Each of these protocols has its own advantages and disadvantages. Ethernet is the most reliable and secure protocol, but it is also the most expensive. Wi-Fi is the most widely used protocol, but it has limited range and is vulnerable to interference.
5. When designing an IoT system, it is important to choose the most appropriate communication protocol for the application. Factors such as cost, reliability, and security must be taken into consideration.
