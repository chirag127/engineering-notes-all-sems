
### Sources of the IoT 

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or "things" that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet. The sources of the IoT can be divided into three main categories: 

1. **Connected Devices**: These are physical objects that are connected to the internet and are capable of transmitting and receiving data. Examples include smartphones, tablets, wearables, home appliances, and connected cars.

2. **Sensors and Actuators**: These are electronic devices that are used to collect data from the environment and to control the environment. Examples include temperature and humidity sensors, pressure sensors, motion sensors, and actuators such as motors and relays. 

3. **Networking Technologies**: These are the technologies that enable the communication between the connected devices and the sensors and actuators. Examples include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, and cellular technologies.
