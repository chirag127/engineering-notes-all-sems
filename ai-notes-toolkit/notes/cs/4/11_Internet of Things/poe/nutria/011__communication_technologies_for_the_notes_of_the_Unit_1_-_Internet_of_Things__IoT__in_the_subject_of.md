
### Communication Technologies for Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

* IoT devices use a range of communication technologies to connect to the Internet and to each other. These include: 
    * Wi-Fi: A wireless local area network (WLAN) technology that uses radio waves to provide high-speed Internet connectivity.
    * Bluetooth: A wireless technology that allows devices to connect to each other over short distances.
    * Cellular Technology: A technology that uses cellular networks to provide Internet connectivity.
    * Near Field Communication (NFC): A technology that uses radio waves to allow devices to communicate with each other over short distances.
    * Radio Frequency Identification (RFID): A technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects.
    * Zigbee: A low-power, low data rate wireless communication protocol used in home automation and other applications.
    * Thread: A low-power, secure, mesh networking protocol designed for home automation and other applications.
    * 6LoWPAN: A low-power wireless communication protocol designed for use in IoT networks.
