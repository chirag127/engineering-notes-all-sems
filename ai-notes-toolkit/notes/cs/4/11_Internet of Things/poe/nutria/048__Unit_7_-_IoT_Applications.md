
## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are systems that connect physical devices to the internet. 
- These systems are used to collect data, automate processes, and improve efficiency. 
- IoT applications can be found in many industries, such as healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, and transportation. 
- IoT systems are composed of three main components: sensors, gateways, and cloud computing. 
- Sensors are used to collect data from the environment. Gateways are used to connect the sensors to the cloud. Cloud computing is used to store and process the data. 
- IoT applications have many benefits, such as improved efficiency, increased safety, and better decision-making. 
- They also have some drawbacks, such as data security and privacy concerns. 
- In order to ensure the successful implementation of IoT applications, organizations must consider the security and privacy of their data.
