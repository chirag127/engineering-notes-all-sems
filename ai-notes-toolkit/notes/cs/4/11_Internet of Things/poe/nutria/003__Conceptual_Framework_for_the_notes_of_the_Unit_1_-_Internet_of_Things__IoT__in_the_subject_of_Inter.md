
### Conceptual Framework for the Notes of Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)
1. Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or “things” embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data. 
2. IoT enables the physical world to be connected to the digital world and to interact with each other. 
3. IoT consists of three main components: the physical objects (“things”), the communication networks, and the software applications. 
4. IoT enables the physical objects to be connected to the digital world and to interact with each other. 
5. IoT can be used to monitor and control physical objects, such as sensors, lights, and machines. 
6. IoT can also be used to collect data from the physical objects and to analyze the data for various purposes. 
7. IoT can be used to create smart cities, smart homes, and smart factories. 
8. IoT can also be used to create connected health systems, connected vehicles, and connected energy systems. 
9. Security is a major concern for IoT, as it can be used to collect and store sensitive data. 
10. IoT can also be used to create new business models and to improve customer experiences.
