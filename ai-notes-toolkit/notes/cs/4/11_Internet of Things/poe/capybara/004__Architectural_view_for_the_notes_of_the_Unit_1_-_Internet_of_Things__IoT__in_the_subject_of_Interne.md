### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

Here are some key points to understand the architectural view for the Unit 1 notes on IoT:

- IoT architecture consists of three layers: perception layer, network layer, and application layer.
- The perception layer is responsible for sensing and collecting data from the physical environment.
- The network layer is responsible for transmitting data from the perception layer to the application layer.
- The application layer is responsible for processing and analyzing the data received from the network layer.
- IoT devices are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other through various communication protocols such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Zigbee.
- IoT devices can be classified into two categories: constrained devices and unconstrained devices.
- Constrained devices have limited processing power, memory, and energy, while unconstrained devices have higher capabilities.
- IoT systems can be centralized, decentralized, or hybrid depending on the requirements of the application.
- The centralized architecture has a single point of control, while the decentralized architecture has multiple points of control.
- The hybrid architecture combines the features of both centralized and decentralized architectures.
- IoT security is a critical concern, and it can be achieved by implementing security measures such as encryption, authentication, and access control.

By understanding these key points, you will be able to comprehend the architectural view of IoT and its components in Unit 1 notes.
