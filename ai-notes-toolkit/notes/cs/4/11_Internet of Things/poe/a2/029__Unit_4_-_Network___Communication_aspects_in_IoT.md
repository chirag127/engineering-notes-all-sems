## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

1. Types of Networks
- Wired networks: Ethernet, LAN, etc.
- Wireless networks: WiFi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc.

2. Communication Protocols
- MQTT: Lightweight messaging protocol ideal for IoT
- HTTP: Used commonly but not efficient for IoT
- CoAP: Lightweight web transfer protocol for constrained devices and networks
- XMPP: Extensible messaging and presence protocol for messaging
- AMQP: Advanced message queuing protocol for messaging

3. Range and coverage
- Bluetooth: 10m range, operates in 2.4GHz band
- WiFi: 30-100m range (indoor), operates in 2.4GHz / 5GHz bands
- Cellular: Wide area coverage with low bandwidth
- Zigbee: 30-100m range, operates in 868/915MHz / 2.4GHz bands
- LoRaWAN: 2-5km range (indoor), up to 15km range (outdoor)

4. Power requirements
- WiFi: High power
- Bluetooth: Low to moderate power
- Zigbee: Low power
- LoRaWAN: Low power

The above points cover the major network types, communication protocols and range/coverage options along with their key characteristics including power requirements which are important aspects to consider for IoT solutions. The selection of the appropriate network and protocol depends on the specific use case requirements.
