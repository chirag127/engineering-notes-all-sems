### IoT Examples for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

1. Smart Home - IoT devices like smart speakers, smart displays, smart thermostats, smart locks, smart lights, etc. are connected to create an automated home. One can control these devices through voice commands or smartphone apps to control the lighting, temperature, security, and other aspects of the home.

2. Smart City - IoT devices and sensors are deployed across a city to collect data and automate processes. This includes smart traffic management (using sensors to monitor traffic and control lights), smart waste management (using sensors to monitor fill levels of bins), smart energy management (using sensors to monitor and optimize energy usage), and more. The data collected can help city officials make data-driven decisions to improve infrastructure and services.

3. Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) - IoT devices and sensors are used in industries to monitor processes, track inventory, monitor machine performance, and automate actions. This improves operational efficiency, reduces waste, increases productivity, and minimizes downtime. For example, sensors can monitor equipment for preventive maintenance and data from equipment and products can be tracked throughout the supply chain.

4. Connected Vehicles - Vehicles are being equipped with IoT devices and sensors to connect to other vehicles and transportation infrastructure. This enables features such as advanced driver assistance systems, vehicle diagnostics, and intelligent routing based on traffic data. The data from connected vehicles can also help with applications such as smart parking, traffic management, and road maintenance.

5. Wearable Devices - IoT devices in the form of wearable devices or accessories like fitness trackers, smart watches, and health monitors are gaining popularity. These devices collect data about a user's activity, health, and other metrics, which can be analyzed to provide personalized feedback and recommendations to users. The data can also be aggregated across groups of users to gain insights into trends in health, fitness, and more.
