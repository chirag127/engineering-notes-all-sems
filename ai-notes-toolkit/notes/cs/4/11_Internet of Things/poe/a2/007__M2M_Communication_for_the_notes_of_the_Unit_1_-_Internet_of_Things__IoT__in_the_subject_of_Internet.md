### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

1. Machine to Machine (M2M) communication refers to the exchange of data between devices through wired or wireless networks without human interaction.
2. M2M communication enables devices to send and receive data and execute actions based on the received data, all without the manual assistance of humans.
3. The key components of an M2M system are:
- Connected devices: These are the machines or devices that generate and/or consume the data. For example, sensors, meters, etc.
- Wireless or wired networks: These provide the communication infrastructure through which the data is transmitted between the connected devices. For example, cellular networks, Wi-Fi, etc.
4. Some of the key applications of M2M communication are:
- Smart cities: Smart lighting, smart waste management, smart parking, etc.
- Smart homes: Home automation, security systems, etc.
- Industrial automation: Monitoring and controlling industrial equipment.
- Telemedicine: Remote monitoring of patients.
- Logistics: Tracking of vehicles and goods.
5. The growth of Internet of Things (IoT) and widespread network connectivity has enabled the expansion of M2M communication leading to the development of massive M2M systems with billions of connected devices.
