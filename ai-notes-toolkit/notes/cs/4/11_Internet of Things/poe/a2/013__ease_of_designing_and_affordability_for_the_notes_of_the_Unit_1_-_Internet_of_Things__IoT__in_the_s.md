### Ease of Designing and Affordability for IoT Notes (Unit 1)

1. IoT devices are easy to design and develop as they require simple sensors and processors that are available at a lower cost. This makes them affordable to build and deploy.
2. Open source hardware platforms like Raspberry Pi, Arduino, etc. provide low-cost hardware options to develop IoT devices and solutions. This reduces the cost of development and makes IoT solutions affordable.
3. Many cloud platforms provide free tiers and affordable paid options to store and process IoT data. This reduces the cost of cloud infrastructure for IoT solutions.
4. Many open source software options are available for IoT solutions that cut down licensing and software costs. This makes IoT solutions more affordable.
5. As the adoption of IoT grows, the costs are expected to go down which will make IoT solutions even more affordable and accessible to more users.

The above points outline how ease of designing and affordability makes IoT solutions accessible to more users and organizations. The use of open source and low-cost options combined with the increasing adoption is enabling the growth of IoT.
