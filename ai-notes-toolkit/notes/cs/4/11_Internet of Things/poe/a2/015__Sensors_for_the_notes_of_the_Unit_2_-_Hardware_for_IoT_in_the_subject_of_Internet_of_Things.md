### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. Temperature Sensor: Measures the temperature and converts it into an electrical signal. It is used to sense the temperature changes in the environment.
2. Humidity Sensor: Measures the amount of moisture present in the air and converts it into an electrical signal. It is used to sense the humidity changes in the environment.
3. Light Sensor: Measures the intensity of light and converts it into an electrical signal. It is used to sense the light intensity changes in the environment.
4. Pressure Sensor: Measures the amount of pressure applied to it and converts it into an electrical signal. It is used to sense the pressure changes in the environment.
5. Motion Sensor: Detects the movement of objects around it and generates an electrical signal. It is used to sense the motion or movement in the environment.
6. Proximity Sensor: Detects the presence of nearby objects without any physical contact and generates an electrical signal. It is used to sense the proximity of objects.

The above points cover the key sensors used in IoT hardware to sense the changes in various environmental parameters like temperature, humidity, light, pressure, motion, proximity, etc. and generate corresponding electrical signals which can be used by IoT devices and systems.
