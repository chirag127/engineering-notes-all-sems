### Other challenges for the notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT

1. Security
- Data transmission over insecure networks
- Vulnerable devices and software
- Lack of security awareness among users and developers
- Distributed systems are difficult to secure
- Many interconnected devices increase attack surface

2. Privacy
- Collection of personal data from sensors and devices
- Use of data for unforeseen purposes
- Anonymity and unlinkability are challenging
- Regulations around data privacy are still evolving

3. Interoperability
- Heterogeneous systems and technologies
- Lack of common standards and protocols
- Vendor lock-in can limit interoperability
- Complexity of system integration and device cooperation

4. Reliability
- Unreliable connectivity and networking
- Resource constraints on devices can affect reliability
- Complex software on constrained devices can be buggy
- Many components and links in an IoT system increase chance of failure

5. Scale
- IoT systems can consist of billions of devices
- Challenging to manage such huge scale
- Bandwidth and storage requirements can be enormous
- Real-time processing of massive data streams is difficult
