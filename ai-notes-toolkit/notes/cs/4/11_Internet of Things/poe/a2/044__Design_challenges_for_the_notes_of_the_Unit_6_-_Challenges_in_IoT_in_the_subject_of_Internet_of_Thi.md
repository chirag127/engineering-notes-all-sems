### Design challenges for the notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things.

1. Scalability - As the number of connected devices increases, it becomes challenging to handle the scalability of the entire IoT system. Managing data flow and networking at such large scale is difficult.
2. Interoperability - There are many vendors manufacturing IoT devices and systems. Getting all the heterogeneous devices and systems to interoperate and exchange data is a major challenge. Standardization of communication protocols and data formats can help solve this.
3. Security - Security is one of the major concerns in IoT. With so many connected devices, the attack surface increases and they can be susceptible to various cyber attacks. Implementing strong authentication and encryption is important.
4. Privacy - Personal and sensitive data is collected by many IoT devices and systems. Ensuring privacy of such data and controlling access to it is difficult. Regulations may be needed to address privacy concerns.
5. Reliability - Many IoT applications like smart grids, smart homes, etc. need reliable functioning. However, the interconnected and vast nature of IoT systems makes it difficult to achieve high reliability. Fault detection and redundant systems can help increase reliability.
6. Data Management - The huge amount of data generated by IoT devices needs to be managed. This includes storing, analyzing, and deriving insights from the data. The scale of data and velocity at which it is generated brings data management challenges. Distributed systems and scalable storage solutions are required to handle IoT data.
