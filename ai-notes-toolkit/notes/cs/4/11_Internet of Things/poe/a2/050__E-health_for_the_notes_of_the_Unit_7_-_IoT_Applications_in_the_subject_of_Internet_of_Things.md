### E-health for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

1. E-health refers to the electronic systems used for health services and information.
2. IoT has enabled advanced e-health systems with sensor devices and connectivity.
3. Wearable devices can monitor health in real-time and provide alerts - heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen levels, etc.
4. Implantable devices can be used to monitor chronic conditions and deliver drugs or stimulate organs.
5. IoT connectivity allows collection of health data and sharing with doctors for diagnosis and treatment.
6. Remote monitoring allows patients to be monitored at home, reducing hospital visits.
7. Access to health information and records becomes easy via IoT systems.
8. Challenges include data security, privacy, device accuracy and interoperability.
9. Regulations around e-health IoT systems are complex due to sensitive data and risk to life.
10. E-health IoT has the potential to improve access and quality of healthcare through continuous monitoring and personalized services.
