## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. Microcontrollers - Microcontrollers are small computers on a single integrated circuit containing a processor core, memory, and programmable input/output peripherals. They are used to control devices and process data in IoT devices. Examples include Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP8266, etc.
2. Sensors - Sensors are used to detect and measure physical parameters of the environment such as temperature, humidity, motion, pressure, etc. and provide input to the microcontroller. Examples include temperature sensors, motion sensors, pressure sensors, etc.
3. Actuators - Actuators are used to perform actions by converting electrical signals from the microcontroller to energy/motion. Examples include motors, lights, pumps, etc.
4. Communication Modules - Communication modules are used for microcontrollers to communicate with other devices over various wired/wireless protocols such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, GSM, etc.
5. Power Supply - A power supply is required to provide power to the microcontroller and other components. It can be through batteries or AC power adapters. Low-power components are preferred for battery-powered IoT devices.
