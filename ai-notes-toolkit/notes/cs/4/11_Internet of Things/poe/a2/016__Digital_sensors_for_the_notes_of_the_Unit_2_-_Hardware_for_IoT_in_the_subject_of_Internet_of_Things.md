### Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things.

1. Temperature sensor:
- Measures ambient temperature and converts it into digital data which can be read by a microcontroller.
- Uses components like thermistors, RTDs, thermocouples, etc. to sense the temperature and convert it into resistance or voltage values which are then converted into digital values.

2. Humidity sensor:
- Measures the amount of moisture present in the air and converts it into digital data which can be read by a microcontroller.
- Uses components like resistive, capacitive sensors which change their values based on the moisture content and get converted into digital data.

3. Pressure sensor:
- Measures the pressure (barometric pressure, differential pressure, etc.) acting on it and converts it into digital data which can be read by a microcontroller.
- Uses components like piezoresistive, capacitive sensors which change their values based on the pressure and get converted into digital data.

4. Light sensor:
- Measures the intensity of light in the environment and converts it into digital data which can be read by a microcontroller.
- Uses components like photodiodes, phototransistors which change their values based on the light intensity and get converted into digital data.
