### Intel Galileo boards for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. Intel Galileo is a microcontroller board based on Intel's 32-bit Intel Quark SoC. It is a good platform for the development and prototyping of low-power Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
2. It comes in two variants:
    - Intel Galileo Gen 1: It has Intel 400MHz 32-bit Intel Quark X1000 processor, 256MB RAM, 100Mbit Ethernet port, SD card slot, USB ports, etc.
    - Intel Galileo Gen 2: It has Intel 400MHz 32-bit Intel Quark SE processor, 512MB RAM, Gigabit Ethernet port, mini-PCIe connectors, USB ports, etc. It has more processing power and capabilities than Galileo Gen 1.
3. The Galileo boards can run various Operating Systems like Linux, Windows 10 IoT core, etc. The Arduino Software (IDE) can be used to develop applications for the Galileo board.
4. The boards have various interfacing options like GPIO, I2C, SPI, UART, USB, Ethernet, etc. to interface with sensors and actuators. A variety of Arduino shields can also be used with the Galileo boards.
5. The Intel Galileo boards are good for prototyping IoT devices and learning embedded system programming. They can be used to build projects like smart meters, home automation systems, wearable devices, etc. Due to more powerful specifications, Galileo Gen 2 has more capabilities and can be used for more complex projects than Gen 1.
