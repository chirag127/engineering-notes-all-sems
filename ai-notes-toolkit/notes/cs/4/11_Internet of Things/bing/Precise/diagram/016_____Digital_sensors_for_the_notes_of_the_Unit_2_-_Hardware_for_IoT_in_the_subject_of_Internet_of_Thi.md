### Digital Sensors for IoT

Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data. They are an essential part of an IoT ecosystem, bridging the digital world to the physical world. These sensors may detect things like temperature, pressure, and motion, and if they are connected to a network, they share data with the network .

- **Direct Interaction with IoT Microcontroller**: Digital sensors interact directly with an IoT microcontroller. If an application requires an analog sensor, the data gathered by that sensor must be converted into a digital format, creating an extra step in the process .

- **Positioning**: All sensors are positioned at the frontend of IoT networks so they can collect data that crosses those networks .

- **Variety in Design**: Sensors vary in design depending on their specific purpose. For example, sensors can function in either digital or analog mode .

- **Applications**: IoT sensors are used in a wide range of applications, including consumer devices, Industry 4.0, and medical applications such as remote patient monitoring .

Digital sensors play a crucial role in the Internet of Things by enabling the collection of data for smarter decisions. They are an essential component of any IoT system and are used in a wide range of applications.
