### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms such as Arduino

#### Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. **Internet of Things (IoT)** refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity that enable these objects to collect and exchange data.

2. **Embedded Platforms** are hardware devices that are designed to run specific software applications and perform specific tasks. These platforms are often used in IoT applications to collect, process, and transmit data from sensors and other connected devices.

3. **Arduino** is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is a popular choice for building IoT projects due to its simplicity, flexibility, and affordability.

4. Arduino boards are equipped with a range of input/output (I/O) pins that can be used to interface with various sensors, actuators, and other electronic components. This makes it easy to build custom IoT devices that can collect and transmit data.

5. There are several different Arduino boards available, each with its own unique features and capabilities. Some popular options include the Arduino Uno, Arduino Mega, and Arduino Nano.

6. In addition to the hardware, Arduino also provides a software development environment that makes it easy to write and upload code to the board. This allows users to quickly and easily develop custom applications for their IoT projects.

7. Overall, Arduino is a versatile and accessible platform that is well-suited for building IoT projects. Its combination of easy-to-use hardware and software, along with its large and active community of users, makes it an excellent choice for anyone looking to get started with IoT.
