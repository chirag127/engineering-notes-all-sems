### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

Sensors are an essential component of the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem. They are used to collect data from the physical world and convert it into digital signals that can be processed by computers. Some common types of sensors used in IoT include:

1. **Temperature sensors:** These sensors measure the temperature of their surroundings and are commonly used in smart home systems, weather monitoring, and industrial processes.

2. **Humidity sensors:** These sensors measure the amount of moisture in the air and are commonly used in smart home systems, agriculture, and weather monitoring.

3. **Light sensors:** These sensors measure the intensity of light and are commonly used in smart home systems, street lighting, and security systems.

4. **Motion sensors:** These sensors detect movement and are commonly used in security systems, smart home systems, and gaming.

5. **Pressure sensors:** These sensors measure the pressure of liquids or gases and are commonly used in industrial processes, weather monitoring, and healthcare.

6. **Sound sensors:** These sensors measure the intensity of sound and are commonly used in security systems, smart home systems, and noise pollution monitoring.

7. **Gas sensors:** These sensors detect the presence of specific gases and are commonly used in industrial processes, air quality monitoring, and safety systems.

Sensors play a crucial role in enabling IoT devices to interact with the physical world and provide valuable data for analysis and decision-making. They are an essential component of the hardware for IoT.
