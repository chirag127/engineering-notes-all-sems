### Design Principles for Connected Devices

Design principles are fundamental pieces of advice for making easy-to-use, pleasurable designs. They represent the accumulated wisdom of researchers and practitioners in design and related fields . Here are some design principles for connected devices:

1. **Interaction with other elements**: The design of a connected device should be well thought out in terms of its interaction with other elements .
2. **Functionality**: Design goes beyond the narrow objective of physical appearance. Product design covers appearance but it also covers product functionality .
3. **Representational State Transfer (REST)**: This is a design principle used on connected devices .
4. **JSON-RPC**: This is another design principle used on connected devices .
5. **Simple object access protocol (SOAP)**: This is a design principle used on connected devices .
6. **XML-RPC**: This is another design principle used on connected devices .
7. **Web Communication Protocols**: These include protocols for connected devices, such as SOAP, REST, HTTP Restful and Web Sockets .
8. **Internet Connectivity Principles**: These include internet connectivity, internet-based communication, IP addressing in IoT, and media access control .
9. **Research the device's purpose and its user base**: The first step in the design process is to research the device's purpose and its user base. To define its main features, designers should think like the device's eventual users to identify how the IoT product will solve pain points and help overcome obstacles .
