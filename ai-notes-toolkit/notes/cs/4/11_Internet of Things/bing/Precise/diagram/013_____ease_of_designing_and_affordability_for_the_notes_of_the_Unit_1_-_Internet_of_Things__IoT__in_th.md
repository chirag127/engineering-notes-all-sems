### Ease of Designing and Affordability for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

1. IoT stands for Internet of Things. It refers to the interconnectedness of physical devices, such as appliances and vehicles, that are embedded with software, sensors, and connectivity which enables these objects to connect and exchange data.
2. Affordability of IoT devices is an important factor to consider when designing connected devices for IoT applications, services, and business processes.
3. The designer must consider the ease in designing the devices' physical, data link, adaption layers, and gateway.
4. IoT proves to be a valuable technology for many organizations and sectors. If organizations can develop and standardize certain aspects of the IoT architecture, they can tap into the full potential of their investments.
5. The future of IoT, and the role that digital design will play, is about empowering and enabling people in ways that previously required a tremendous amount of effort or inherit knowledge.
6. The cost of developing an IoT solution can vary, but a minimum viable product (MVP) version of an IoT system can cost between $30,000-50,000.
7. Affordance is an important design parameter, which refers to the physical quality of an object that suggests to a user the appropriate actions to use it.
