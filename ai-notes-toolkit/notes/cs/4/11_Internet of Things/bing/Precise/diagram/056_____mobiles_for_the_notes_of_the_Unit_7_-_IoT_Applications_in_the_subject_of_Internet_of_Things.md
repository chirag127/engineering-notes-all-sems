### Unit 7 - IoT Applications: Mobiles

1. Mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, are important components of the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem.
2. Mobile devices can act as both sensors and controllers in IoT systems.
3. As sensors, mobile devices can collect data from their built-in sensors, such as GPS, accelerometer, and camera, and transmit the data to other devices or systems for processing and analysis.
4. As controllers, mobile devices can receive data and commands from other devices or systems and act on them, such as displaying information or controlling other devices.
5. Mobile devices can also act as gateways, connecting other devices to the internet and enabling communication between devices and systems.
6. Mobile devices can run IoT applications, allowing users to interact with and control IoT systems through a familiar interface.
7. The widespread adoption of mobile devices and their increasing capabilities make them a key component in the development and deployment of IoT systems.
