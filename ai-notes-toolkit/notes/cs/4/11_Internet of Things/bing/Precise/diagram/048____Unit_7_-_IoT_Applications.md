## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

1. **Smart Homes**: IoT technology is used to automate and control various aspects of a home, such as lighting, temperature, and security systems.
2. **Wearable Devices**: IoT technology is used in wearable devices, such as fitness trackers and smartwatches, to monitor and track various health and fitness metrics.
3. **Smart Cities**: IoT technology is used in smart cities to improve the efficiency and sustainability of urban services, such as transportation, energy, and waste management.
4. **Healthcare**: IoT technology is used in healthcare to improve patient outcomes, reduce costs, and increase efficiency through remote monitoring and telemedicine.
5. **Agriculture**: IoT technology is used in agriculture to improve crop yields, reduce costs, and increase efficiency through precision farming and smart irrigation systems.
6. **Industrial Automation**: IoT technology is used in industrial automation to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and increase safety through predictive maintenance and real-time monitoring.
7. **Transportation**: IoT technology is used in transportation to improve safety, reduce costs, and increase efficiency through real-time traffic monitoring and smart fleet management.
