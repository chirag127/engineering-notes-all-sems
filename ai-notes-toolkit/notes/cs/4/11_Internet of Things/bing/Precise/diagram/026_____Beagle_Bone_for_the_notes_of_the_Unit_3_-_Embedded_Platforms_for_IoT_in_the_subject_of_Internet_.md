### BeagleBone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- BeagleBone is a great IoT device based upon arm architecture and is very portable and affordable.
- BeagleBone Black board is an arm-based development board designed to develop IoT concepts and test them.
- The BeagleBone Black board is the newest member of the BeagleBoard family.
- Boot Linux in under 10 seconds and get started on development in less than 5 minutes with just a single USB cable.
- This board is very useful in developing IoT applications.
- The Beagle family revs as high as 1GHz with flexible peripheral interfaces and a proven ecosystem of feature-rich "Cape" plug-in boards.
- Your Beagle should be running a DHCP server that will provide your computer with an IP address of either 192.168.7.1 or 192.168.6.1, depending on the type of USB network adapter supported by your computer's operating system.
- Your Beagle will reserve 192.168.7.2 or 192.168.6.2 for itself.
- K3s, developed by Rancher, and now an open source CNCF sandbox project, makes running lightweight Kubernetes on these devices a real possibility and opens a world of potential new applications.
