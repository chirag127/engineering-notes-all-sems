### Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)
#### Technology behind IoT

1. **Sensors and Actuators:** These are the devices that collect data from the environment and perform actions based on the data. They are the interface between the physical and digital world.
2. **Connectivity:** IoT devices need to be connected to the internet to send and receive data. This can be achieved through various communication technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, and satellite.
3. **Cloud Computing:** The data collected by IoT devices is often stored and processed in the cloud. This allows for easy access to the data from anywhere in the world and enables the use of powerful computing resources for data analysis.
4. **Data Analytics:** The data collected by IoT devices can be analyzed to gain insights and make informed decisions. This can be done using various data analytics techniques such as machine learning and artificial intelligence.
5. **Security:** Security is a major concern in IoT as the devices collect and transmit sensitive data. Various security measures such as encryption and authentication are used to ensure the safety of the data.
