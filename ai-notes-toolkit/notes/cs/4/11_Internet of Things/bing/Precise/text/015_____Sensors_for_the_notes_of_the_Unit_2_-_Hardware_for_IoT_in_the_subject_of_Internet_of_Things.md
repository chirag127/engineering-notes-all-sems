### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

1. Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment.
2. They are used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, light, sound, and motion.
3. Sensors are an essential component of IoT systems as they provide the data that is used to monitor and control the environment.
4. There are many different types of sensors, including temperature sensors, humidity sensors, light sensors, sound sensors, and motion sensors.
5. Sensors can be connected to IoT devices using various communication protocols such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
6. The data collected by sensors is processed and analyzed by IoT systems to provide insights and enable automation.
7. Sensors are used in a wide range of applications, including smart homes, smart cities, healthcare, and agriculture.
