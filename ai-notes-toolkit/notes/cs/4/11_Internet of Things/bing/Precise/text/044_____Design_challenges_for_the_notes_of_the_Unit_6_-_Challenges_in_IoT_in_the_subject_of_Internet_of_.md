### Design challenges for the notes of the Unit 6 - Challenges in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

1. **Scalability**: As the number of connected devices increases, the IoT system must be able to handle the increased data traffic and processing demands.
2. **Interoperability**: IoT devices from different manufacturers must be able to communicate and work together seamlessly.
3. **Security**: IoT devices must be secure to prevent unauthorized access and protect user data.
4. **Power consumption**: IoT devices often operate on battery power and must be designed to consume as little power as possible to maximize battery life.
5. **Data management**: IoT systems must be able to efficiently collect, store, and process large amounts of data generated by connected devices.
6. **User interface**: IoT devices must have intuitive and user-friendly interfaces to allow users to easily interact with and control them.
7. **Reliability**: IoT systems must be reliable and able to operate without interruption or failure.
8. **Cost**: The cost of IoT devices and systems must be reasonable to encourage widespread adoption.
