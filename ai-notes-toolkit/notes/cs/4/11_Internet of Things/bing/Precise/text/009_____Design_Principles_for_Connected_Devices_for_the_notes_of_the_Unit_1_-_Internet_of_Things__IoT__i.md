### Design Principles for Connected Devices

1. **Interaction with other elements**: The design of connected devices should be well thought out in terms of their interaction with other elements.
2. **Design goes beyond physical appearance**: Design covers not only the physical appearance of the device but also its functionality.
3. **Web Communication Protocols**: Connected devices should use appropriate web communication protocols such as REST, JSON-RPC, SOAP, and XML-RPC.
4. **Internet Connectivity Principles**: Internet connectivity, internet-based communication, IP addressing in IoT, and media access control are important principles for connected devices.
5. **Research the device's purpose and user base**: The first step in the design process is to research the device's purpose and its user base to define its main features.
6. **Think like the device's eventual users**: Designers should think like the device's eventual users to identify how the IoT product will solve pain points and help overcome obstacles.
7. **Design principles are fundamental pieces of advice**: Design principles represent the accumulated wisdom of researchers and practitioners in design and related fields.

These are some of the key design principles for connected devices in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT).
