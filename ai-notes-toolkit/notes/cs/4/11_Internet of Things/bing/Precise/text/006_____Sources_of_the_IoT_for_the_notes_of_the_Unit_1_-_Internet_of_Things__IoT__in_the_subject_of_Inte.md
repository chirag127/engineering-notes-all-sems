### Sources of the IoT

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can collect and exchange data. The sources of IoT data can vary widely, and can include:

1. **Machine data**: This is data generated by assets such as pumps, turbine engines, and drilling rigs. However, machine data alone does not always tell the complete story. By combining data from disparate sources, new insights can be created.

2. **Sensors**: Temperature, flow, pressure, and humidity sensors have become significant sources of industrial IoT data. These sensors can be used for applications such as remote water quality monitoring.

3. **RFID**: Radio-frequency identification (RFID) enables tracking and inventory control, identification in supply chain systems, access to buildings, and road tolls.

4. **Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs)**: WSNs are networks of spatially distributed sensors that can monitor environmental conditions such as temperature, sound, and pollution levels.

These are just a few examples of the many sources of IoT data. By leveraging data from these and other sources, IoT can help to make the planet more sustainable and our lives richer.
