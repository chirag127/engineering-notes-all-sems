## Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. **Introduction:** Embedded platforms are the backbone of IoT devices. They provide the necessary hardware and software to enable the device to interact with the physical world and communicate with other devices or systems.

2. **Types of Embedded Platforms:** There are several types of embedded platforms available for IoT devices, including microcontrollers, single-board computers, and system-on-chip (SoC) platforms.

3. **Microcontrollers:** Microcontrollers are small, low-power computers that are designed to perform specific tasks. They are commonly used in IoT devices because of their low cost and power consumption.

4. **Single-Board Computers:** Single-board computers are small, self-contained computers that are built on a single circuit board. They are more powerful than microcontrollers and are often used in IoT devices that require more processing power.

5. **System-on-Chip (SoC) Platforms:** SoC platforms are integrated circuits that combine multiple components, such as a microprocessor, memory, and peripherals, onto a single chip. They are commonly used in IoT devices because of their small size and low power consumption.

6. **Choosing the Right Platform:** When choosing an embedded platform for an IoT device, it is important to consider factors such as processing power, power consumption, connectivity options, and cost.

7. **Conclusion:** Embedded platforms are an essential component of IoT devices. They provide the necessary hardware and software to enable the device to interact with the physical world and communicate with other devices or systems. There are several types of embedded platforms available, and it is important to choose the right one for the specific needs of the IoT device.
