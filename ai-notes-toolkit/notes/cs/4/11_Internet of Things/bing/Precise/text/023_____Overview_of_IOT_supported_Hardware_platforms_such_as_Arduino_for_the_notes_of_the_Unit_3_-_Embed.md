### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms such as Arduino

1. **Internet of Things (IoT)** refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.

2. **IoT supported hardware platforms** are the physical devices that provide the necessary computing power, connectivity, and other features required to implement IoT solutions.

3. **Arduino** is one of the most popular IoT supported hardware platforms. It is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software.

4. **Arduino boards** are able to read inputs - light on a sensor, a finger on a button, or a Twitter message - and turn it into an output - activating a motor, turning on an LED, publishing something online.

5. **Arduino** provides a low-cost, easy-to-use platform for building IoT devices and applications. It is widely used by hobbyists, educators, and professionals for prototyping and developing IoT solutions.

6. **Other IoT supported hardware platforms** include Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Edison, and Particle Photon, among others. These platforms vary in terms of their capabilities, cost, and ease of use.

7. **Choosing the right IoT supported hardware platform** depends on the specific requirements of the IoT solution being developed, including factors such as processing power, connectivity, power consumption, and cost.

8. **In summary**, IoT supported hardware platforms such as Arduino provide the necessary hardware and software to build and deploy IoT solutions. These platforms vary in their capabilities and cost, and the right platform should be chosen based on the specific requirements of the IoT solution being developed.
