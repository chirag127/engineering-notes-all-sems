### Additions in Arduino

- To add numbers on the Arduino, we use the addition operator (+). The example below shows how to add two numbers together. `int a = 2; int b = 7; int sum; sum = a + b;` .
- Addition is one of the four primary arithmetic operations. The operator + (plus) operates on two operands to produce the sum.
- The syntax for addition is `sum = operand1 + operand2;` where `sum` is a variable of allowed data types: `int`, `float`, `double`, `byte`, `short`, `long` and `operand1` and `operand2` are variables or constants of allowed data types: `int`, `float`, `double`, `byte`, `short`, `long`.
- There is also a convenient shorthand to perform addition on a variable with another constant or variable using the `+=` compound addition operator.
