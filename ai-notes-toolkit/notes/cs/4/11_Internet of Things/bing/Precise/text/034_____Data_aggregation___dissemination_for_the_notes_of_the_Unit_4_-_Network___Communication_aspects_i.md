### Data aggregation & dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- **Data aggregation** refers to the process of collecting and combining data from multiple sources to provide a more comprehensive view of the information.
- In the context of IoT, data aggregation is important because it allows for the analysis of large amounts of data generated by various devices and sensors.
- Data aggregation can be performed at different levels, such as at the device level, the gateway level, or the cloud level.
- **Data dissemination** refers to the distribution of data to the intended recipients.
- In the context of IoT, data dissemination is important because it allows for the delivery of information to the appropriate parties, such as end-users or other devices.
- Data dissemination can be performed using various communication protocols and technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular networks, or satellite communication.
- Both data aggregation and dissemination are important aspects of the network and communication in IoT, as they enable the efficient and effective use of the data generated by IoT devices.
