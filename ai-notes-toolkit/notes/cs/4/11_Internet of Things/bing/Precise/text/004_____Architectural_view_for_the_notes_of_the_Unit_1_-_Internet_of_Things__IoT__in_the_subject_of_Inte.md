### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

1. The architectural view of the Internet of Things (IoT) is a framework that defines the various components and their interactions within an IoT system.
2. The architecture of an IoT system can vary depending on the specific requirements and use case, but typically includes the following components:
    - **Sensors and Actuators:** These are the devices that collect data from the environment and perform actions based on the data.
    - **Connectivity:** This refers to the communication protocols and technologies used to transmit data between the sensors/actuators and the rest of the system.
    - **Data Processing:** This component is responsible for processing the data collected by the sensors and actuators, and may include data filtering, aggregation, and analysis.
    - **Application:** This is the user-facing component of the IoT system, and provides the interface for users to interact with the system and access the data and services provided by the system.
3. The architectural view of an IoT system can also include additional components such as data storage, security, and management, depending on the specific requirements of the system.
4. The architectural view provides a high-level overview of the IoT system, and can be used to guide the design and development of the system. It is important to carefully consider the architectural view when designing an IoT system to ensure that all components are properly integrated and can work together effectively.
