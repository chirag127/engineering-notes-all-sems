### Intel Galileo Boards for IoT

- Intel Galileo is a development board based on Intel x86 architecture and is designed for the maker and education communities .
- It was the first in a line of Arduino-certified development boards and complies with open source software and hardware licenses  .
- Intel released two versions of Galileo, referred to as Gen 1 and Gen 2. These development boards are sometimes called "Breakout boards" .
- The board was discontinued on June 19, 2017 .
- Galileo is a microcontroller board based on the Intel® Quark SoC X1000 Application Processor, a 32-bit Intel Pentium-class system on a chip .
- Important pins such as digital pins, analog, GND pin, power header, UART all pins of Galileo are all in the same place as on the Arduino Uno R3 .
- The Yocto system installed on the Intel Galileo provides a set of libraries especially designed for the IoTDK and the Intel® XDK IoT Edition. These libraries are MRAA and UPM .
- MRAA is a low-level library that offers a translation from the General Purpose Input/Output interfaces to the pins available on Intel Galileo .
- The Intel Development Kit for IoT (IoTDK) is a complete solution to create and test applications for Intel IoT platforms like the Intel® Galileo .
- The complete IoTDK provides software and hardware components for developers to create innovative projects using the Galileo board and the Grove Starter Kit for starter .
