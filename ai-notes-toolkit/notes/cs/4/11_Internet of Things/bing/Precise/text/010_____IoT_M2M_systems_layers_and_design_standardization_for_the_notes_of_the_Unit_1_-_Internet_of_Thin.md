### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- The oneM2M architecture divides IoT functions into three major domains: the application layer, the services layer, and the network layer  .
- The oneM2M architecture gives major attention to connectivity between devices and their applications.
- The structures of communication protocols for digital M2M and IoT systems are conceptually broken into abstract layers, with the most common models having three, four, five, or seven layers.
- Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC), an International Industry Consortium, has also suggested open standards for sensors’ discovery, capabilities, quality, and other aspects with support to geographical information web support.
- The main ETSI IoT standardization activities are conducted at the radio layer in 3GPP (LTE-M, NB-IoT, and EC-GSM-IoT) and at the service layer in oneM2M.
