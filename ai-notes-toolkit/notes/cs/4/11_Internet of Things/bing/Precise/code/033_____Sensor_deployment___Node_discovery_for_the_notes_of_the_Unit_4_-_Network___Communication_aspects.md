### Sensor deployment & Node discovery for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

1. Sensor deployment refers to the process of installing and configuring sensors in an IoT network.
2. The sensors are responsible for collecting data from the environment and sending it to the network for processing.
3. The deployment of sensors must be carefully planned to ensure that the sensors are placed in the optimal locations to collect the necessary data.
4. Node discovery is the process of identifying and locating the nodes in an IoT network.
5. Nodes are the devices that make up the network and can include sensors, actuators, and other devices.
6. Node discovery is important for ensuring that the network is functioning properly and that all nodes are able to communicate with each other.
7. There are several methods for node discovery, including broadcasting, multicasting, and unicast discovery.
8. The choice of node discovery method will depend on the specific requirements of the IoT network.
