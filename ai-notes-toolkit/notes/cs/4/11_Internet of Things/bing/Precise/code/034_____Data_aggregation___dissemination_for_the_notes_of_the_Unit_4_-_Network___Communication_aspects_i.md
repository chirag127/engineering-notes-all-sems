### Data aggregation & dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data aggregation is the process of collecting and combining data from multiple sources to provide a more comprehensive view of the information.
- In the context of IoT, data aggregation is used to collect data from multiple sensors and devices to provide a more complete picture of the environment or system being monitored.
- Data dissemination refers to the distribution of data to the intended recipients.
- In IoT, data dissemination can take many forms, including sending data to a central server for analysis, sending data to other devices for local processing, or sending data to a user's mobile device for real-time monitoring.
- Effective data aggregation and dissemination are critical for the successful operation of IoT systems, as they enable the system to make informed decisions based on the most complete and up-to-date information available.
- There are many challenges associated with data aggregation and dissemination in IoT, including ensuring data accuracy, managing large volumes of data, and ensuring data security and privacy.
- Various techniques and technologies are used to address these challenges, including data filtering, data compression, and encryption.
- It is important for IoT system designers to carefully consider their data aggregation and dissemination strategies to ensure that the system can effectively collect, process, and distribute data to support its intended functions.
