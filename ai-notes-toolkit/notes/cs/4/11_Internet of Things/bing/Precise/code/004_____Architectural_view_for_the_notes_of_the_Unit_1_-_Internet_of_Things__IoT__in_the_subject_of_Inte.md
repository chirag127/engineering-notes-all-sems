### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

1. The architectural view of the Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the different layers and components that make up an IoT system.
2. An IoT system typically consists of the following layers: perception layer, network layer, middleware layer, application layer, and business layer.
3. The perception layer is responsible for collecting data from the physical world through sensors and other devices.
4. The network layer is responsible for transmitting the collected data to the middleware layer through various communication protocols.
5. The middleware layer is responsible for managing and processing the data, as well as providing services such as data storage and device management.
6. The application layer is responsible for delivering the processed data to the end user through various applications and interfaces.
7. The business layer is responsible for the overall management and decision-making of the IoT system, including business models and strategies.
8. Each layer of the IoT architecture plays a crucial role in the overall functioning of the system, and it is important to ensure that all layers are properly integrated and working together.
