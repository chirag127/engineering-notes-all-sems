### Unit 7 - IoT Applications: Mobiles

1. Mobile devices such as smartphones and tablets are key components of the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem.
2. Mobile devices can act as both sensors and controllers in IoT systems, collecting data from the environment and sending commands to other devices.
3. Mobile devices can also serve as user interfaces for IoT systems, allowing users to monitor and control their IoT devices remotely.
4. Mobile operating systems such as Android and iOS have built-in support for IoT connectivity protocols such as Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Wi-Fi, making it easy to connect mobile devices to IoT systems.
5. Mobile devices can also leverage cloud services to store and process IoT data, enabling advanced analytics and machine learning capabilities.
6. Mobile applications can be developed to provide users with customized experiences and interactions with their IoT devices.
7. The widespread adoption of mobile devices and their increasing capabilities make them a powerful tool for IoT applications.
