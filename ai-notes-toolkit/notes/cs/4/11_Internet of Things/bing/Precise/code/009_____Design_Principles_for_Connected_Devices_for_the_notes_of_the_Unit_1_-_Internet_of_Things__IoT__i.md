### Design Principles for Connected Devices

Design principles for connected devices are fundamental pieces of advice for making easy-to-use, pleasurable designs. These principles represent the accumulated wisdom of researchers and practitioners in design and related fields. Here are some key design principles for connected devices:

1. **Interaction with other elements**: The design of a connected device should be well thought out in terms of its interaction with other elements. This goes beyond the narrow objective of physical appearance and also covers product functionality .

2. **Representational State Transfer (REST)**: REST is a web communication protocol for connected devices .

3. **JSON-RPC**: JSON-RPC is a message communication protocol for connected devices .

4. **Simple Object Access Protocol (SOAP)**: SOAP is a message communication protocol for connected devices .

5. **XML-RPC**: XML-RPC is a message communication protocol for connected devices .

6. **HTTP Restful and Web Sockets**: HTTP Restful and Web Sockets are web communication protocols for connected devices .

7. **Internet Connectivity**: Internet connectivity is a key principle for connected devices, including internet-based communication, IP addressing in IoT, and media access control .

8. **Research the device's purpose and user base**: The first step in the design process is to research the device's purpose and its user base. Designers should think like the device's eventual users to identify how the IoT product will solve pain points and help overcome obstacles .

These are some of the key design principles for connected devices. It is important to keep these principles in mind when designing and developing connected devices for the Internet of Things (IoT).
