### Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

#### Technology behind IoT

1. IoT works through a combination of wireless networking technology, physical devices, advanced data analytics and cloud computing.
2. A group of physical devices is wired or wirelessly linked to each other and/or a central area.
3. IoT devices connect to the internet via GSM cellular networks, Wi-Fi, or ethernet.
4. Six IoT technologies are now classified as fairly mature or mainstream: CPUs, MCUs, GPUs, security chips, FPGA, and edge gateways.
5. Four IoT technologies are close to maturity: eSIM, mesh networks, 5G, and Wi-Fi 6.
6. Edge computing refers to the technology used to make smart devices do more than just send or receive data to their IoT platform. It increases the computing power at the edges of an IoT network, reducing communication latency and improving response time.
