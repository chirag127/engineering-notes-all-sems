### Survey routing protocols for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Routing protocols are used to determine the best path for data to travel from one device to another in a network.
- In the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), routing protocols are used to connect various devices and sensors to the internet and to each other.
- There are several routing protocols that are commonly used in IoT networks, including RPL, CORPL, and LOADng.
- RPL (Routing Protocol for Low-Power and Lossy Networks) is a distance-vector routing protocol designed for use in low-power and lossy networks, such as those commonly found in IoT applications.
- CORPL (Constrained On-demand Routing Protocol for Low-Power and Lossy Networks) is an on-demand routing protocol designed for use in low-power and lossy networks. It is based on the AODV (Ad hoc On-Demand Distance Vector) routing protocol.
- LOADng (Lightweight On-demand Ad hoc Distance-vector Routing Protocol - Next Generation) is an on-demand routing protocol designed for use in low-power and lossy networks. It is based on the AODV routing protocol and is intended to be a lightweight alternative to RPL.
- These routing protocols are designed to be energy-efficient and to operate effectively in networks with limited resources, making them well-suited for use in IoT applications.
- The choice of routing protocol will depend on the specific requirements of the IoT network, including factors such as network size, data rates, and power constraints.
