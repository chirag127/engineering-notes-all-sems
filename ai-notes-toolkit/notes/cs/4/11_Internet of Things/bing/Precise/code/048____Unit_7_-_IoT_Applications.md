## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can collect and exchange data. IoT applications are the use cases where IoT technology is applied to solve real-world problems or improve existing processes. Some of the common IoT applications are:

1. **Smart Homes**: IoT technology is used to automate and control home appliances, lighting, heating, and security systems. This can improve energy efficiency, convenience, and safety.

2. **Wearable Devices**: IoT technology is used in wearable devices such as smartwatches and fitness trackers to monitor and track health and fitness data.

3. **Smart Cities**: IoT technology is used to improve urban infrastructure and services such as traffic management, waste management, and public safety.

4. **Industrial IoT**: IoT technology is used in industries such as manufacturing, logistics, and agriculture to improve efficiency, productivity, and safety.

5. **Healthcare**: IoT technology is used in healthcare to monitor and track patient health, improve patient outcomes, and reduce healthcare costs.

These are just a few examples of the many IoT applications that exist. The potential for IoT technology to improve our lives and the world around us is vast and constantly evolving.
