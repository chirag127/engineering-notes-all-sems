### Unit 7 - IoT Applications: Tablets

1. Tablets are portable computing devices that are equipped with touchscreens and are larger than smartphones but smaller than laptops.
2. They are commonly used for browsing the internet, reading e-books, playing games, and watching videos.
3. Tablets can also be used as IoT devices, allowing users to control and monitor other connected devices in their homes or workplaces.
4. Some popular tablet models that can be used for IoT applications include the Apple iPad, Samsung Galaxy Tab, and Amazon Fire.
5. These tablets can run various IoT applications, allowing users to control smart home devices such as thermostats, lights, and security systems.
6. Tablets can also be used to monitor and analyze data from IoT sensors, providing users with valuable insights into their environment.
7. In summary, tablets are versatile devices that can be used for a wide range of IoT applications, making them a valuable tool for anyone interested in the Internet of Things.
