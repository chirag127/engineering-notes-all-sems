### Smart Metering

Smart metering is one of the most prominent IoT solutions and is widely utilized by companies large and small. It is also one of the most predictable from a ROI perspective, so it is only natural for businesses’ first experience with IoT technologies to begin with smart metering .

- **IoT smart metering use cases:** Smart metering is used to remotely monitor and record energy consumption. This allows for more accurate billing and better energy management .

- **Smart Meter System based Internet of Things Applications Programme:** This programme aims to support innovation to determine the feasibility of, and trial, Smart Meter System based IoT sensor devices .

- **Application architecture:** A smart meter is one of the most important devices among all the energy assets. It can be connected to the Azure IoT Central platform, which simplifies the build process when creating an IoT solution .

- **Extensibility options:** There are many options available to build upon and extend the capabilities of smart metering solutions, such as data visualization with ThingsBoard .

Smart metering is a powerful tool that can help businesses and individuals better manage their energy consumption and save money. It is an exciting application of IoT technology that is sure to continue to grow in popularity.
