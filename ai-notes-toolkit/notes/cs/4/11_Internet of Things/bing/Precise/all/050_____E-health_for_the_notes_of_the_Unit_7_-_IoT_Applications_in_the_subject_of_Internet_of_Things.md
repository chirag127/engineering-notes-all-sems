# E-health

E-health refers to the use of electronic means and technologies to deliver health-related services and information. The Internet of Things (IoT) has been applied to various e-Health applications to improve diagnostic tools, such as magnetic resonance imaging, and epigenetic and neuropsychological tests .

## IoT Applications in E-health

1. **Wearable Technology**: The most common IoT applications in the market are smart bands or fitness trackers, and smartwatches. These devices represent wearable technology in healthcare that can be useful for people .

2. **Remote Physiological Monitoring**: IoT technology has the potential to create a pervasive environment for monitoring patient health and safety, as well as improving how physicians deliver care .

3. **Personalized Attention**: Devices in the form of wearables like fitness bands and other wirelessly connected devices like blood pressure and heart rate monitoring cuffs, glucometer, etc. give patients access to personalized attention .

4. **Big Data Analytics**: Big Data Analytics based IoT technologies can be used in eHealth to improve healthcare services and applications .

5. **Cloud and Edge Computing**: Cloud and Edge computing can be used for IoT eHealth to improve the delivery of healthcare services and applications .

6. **5G and IoT**: 5G and IoT can be exploited for eHealth to improve healthcare services and applications .

7. **Internet of Medical Things (IoMT)**: IoMT refers to the integration of medical devices and applications with IoT technology. IoMT has numerous applications in e-health systems, including the use of fog and cloud computing .
