### Unit 7 - IoT Applications: Tablets

Tablets are portable computing devices that are larger than smartphones but smaller than laptops. They are commonly used for browsing the internet, reading e-books, playing games, and watching videos. In the context of IoT applications, tablets can serve as a user interface for controlling and monitoring IoT devices.

1. **Home automation:** Tablets can be used to control smart home devices such as lights, thermostats, and security systems. Users can access a home automation app on their tablet to remotely control these devices and monitor their status.

2. **Healthcare:** Tablets can be used in healthcare settings to monitor patient health and track medication schedules. For example, a tablet can be used to display real-time data from a patient's wearable health monitor, or to remind a patient to take their medication.

3. **Industrial automation:** Tablets can be used in industrial settings to monitor and control machinery and equipment. For example, a tablet can be used to display real-time data from sensors on a production line, or to remotely control a robotic arm.

4. **Retail:** Tablets can be used in retail settings to enhance the shopping experience. For example, a tablet can be used to display product information, or to allow customers to place orders and make payments.

These are just a few examples of how tablets can be used in IoT applications. The versatility and portability of tablets make them a valuable tool for interacting with IoT devices in a wide range of settings.
