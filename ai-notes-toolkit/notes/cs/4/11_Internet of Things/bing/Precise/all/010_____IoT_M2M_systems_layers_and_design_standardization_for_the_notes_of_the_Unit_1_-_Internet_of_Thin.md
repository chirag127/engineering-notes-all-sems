# IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and exchange data. Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication is a key component of IoT, allowing devices to communicate with each other without human intervention.

To promote efficient M2M communication systems and IoT, ETSI and 13 other founding members launched oneM2M as a global initiative in 2012. The oneM2M architecture divides IoT functions into three major domains: the application layer, the services layer, and the network layer  .

- **Application layer**: The oneM2M architecture gives major attention to connectivity between devices and their applications.

- **Services layer**: The services layer is responsible for providing services to the application layer.

- **Network layer**: The network layer is responsible for providing connectivity between devices.

The oneM2M architecture promotes interoperability through IT-friendly APIs and supports a wide range of IoT technologies. The Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC), an International Industry Consortium, has also suggested open standards for sensors’ discovery, capabilities, quality, and other aspects with support to geographical information web support.

ETSI's main IoT standardization activities are conducted at the radio layer in 3GPP (LTE-M, NB-IoT, and EC-GSM-IoT) and at the service layer in oneM2M.
