### Digital Sensors

Digital sensors are electronic devices that convert physical quantities into digital signals. These sensors are used in various applications, including the Internet of Things (IoT). Here are some key points to note about digital sensors:

1. Digital sensors are used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, pressure, and light intensity.
2. These sensors use a microprocessor or microcontroller to convert the measured physical quantity into a digital signal.
3. Digital sensors are more accurate and reliable than analog sensors because they are less susceptible to noise and interference.
4. Digital sensors can be easily interfaced with other digital devices, such as computers and microcontrollers, for data processing and analysis.
5. Digital sensors are widely used in IoT applications, such as smart homes, smart cities, and industrial automation.

In summary, digital sensors are essential components of IoT hardware, providing accurate and reliable measurements of physical quantities that can be used to control and monitor various systems and processes.
