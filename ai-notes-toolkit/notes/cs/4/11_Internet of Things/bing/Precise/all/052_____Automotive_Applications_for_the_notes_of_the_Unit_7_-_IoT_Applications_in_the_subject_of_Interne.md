# Unit 7 - IoT Applications: Automotive Applications

The Internet of Things (IoT) has opened doors to countless opportunities for the automobile industry, having helped improve performance, design, and even lower costs while ensuring the highest quality. Industry 4.0 has allowed for expanded IoT implementation via blending network technology with artificial intelligence.

Here are some of the most common applications of IoT in the automotive sector:

1. **Fleet management**: IoT enables remote fleet management through three main technologies — RFID, GPS, and OBD-II. Real-time location monitoring of the fleet is one of the key features of IoT-enabled fleet management.

2. **Vehicle tracking**: Fleet operators and owners use IoT to get a holistic view of their inventory.

3. **Connected cars**: IoT has enabled the development of connected cars, which can communicate with other vehicles and infrastructure to improve safety and efficiency on the road.

4. **Automotive maintenance system**: IoT can be used to monitor the health of vehicles and schedule maintenance when necessary.

5. **Autonomous vehicles**: IoT is playing a key role in the development of autonomous vehicles, which can operate without human intervention.

6. **In-vehicle infotainment and telematics**: IoT has enabled the development of advanced infotainment and telematics systems, which can provide drivers and passengers with a wide range of information and entertainment options.

7. **Theft detection during the maintenance and manufacturing processes**: IoT can be used to detect and prevent theft during the maintenance and manufacturing processes.

8. **Driver behavioral management**: IoT can be used to monitor driver behavior and provide feedback to improve safety and efficiency on the road.

These are just some of the many ways in which IoT is changing the face of the automotive industry. As technology continues to advance, we can expect to see even more innovative applications of IoT in this sector.
