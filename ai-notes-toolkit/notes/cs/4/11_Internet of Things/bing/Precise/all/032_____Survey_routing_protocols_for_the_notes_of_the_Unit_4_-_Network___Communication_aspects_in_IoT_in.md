### Survey routing protocols for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

1. **Routing Protocols in IoT**: The chapter discusses some standard and non-standard protocols that are used for routing in IoT applications. Low-Power and Lossy Networks (RPL) is the most commonly used routing protocols in IoT. Cognitive RPL (CORPL) is a non-standard extension of RPL that is designed for cognitive networks.

2. **RPL**: RPL, which is typically used for resource-constrained devices in IoT networks, is focused on finding the best routing path in LLNs and is classified as a distance vector and resource routing protocol. The RPL algorithm supports multipoint-to-point (MP2P), point-tomultipoint (P2MP), and point-to-point (P2P) data flows.

3. **M2M Communication**: The machine-to-machine (M2M) communication is an enabler technology for internet of things (IoT) that provides communication between machines and devices without human intervention.

4. **Protocols and Standards for IoT**: The rapid growth in technology and internet connected devices has enabled Internet of Things (IoT) to be one of the important fields in computing. A survey of protocols and standards for IoT is available.
