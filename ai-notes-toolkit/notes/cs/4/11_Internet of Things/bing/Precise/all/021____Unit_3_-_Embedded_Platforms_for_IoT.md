## Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT

1. **Introduction:** Embedded platforms are the hardware and software systems that are designed to perform specific tasks within a larger system. In the context of IoT, embedded platforms are used to collect, process, and transmit data from sensors and other devices.

2. **Types of Embedded Platforms:** There are several types of embedded platforms that are commonly used in IoT applications, including microcontrollers, single-board computers, and system-on-chip (SoC) devices.

3. **Microcontrollers:** Microcontrollers are small, low-power computers that are designed to perform specific tasks. They are commonly used in IoT applications to collect and process data from sensors and other devices.

4. **Single-Board Computers:** Single-board computers are small, self-contained computers that are built on a single circuit board. They are commonly used in IoT applications to collect, process, and transmit data from sensors and other devices.

5. **System-on-Chip (SoC) Devices:** SoC devices are integrated circuits that combine multiple components, such as a microprocessor, memory, and input/output interfaces, onto a single chip. They are commonly used in IoT applications to collect, process, and transmit data from sensors and other devices.

6. **Choosing an Embedded Platform:** When choosing an embedded platform for an IoT application, it is important to consider factors such as power consumption, processing power, connectivity options, and cost.

7. **Conclusion:** Embedded platforms play a critical role in IoT applications by providing the hardware and software systems needed to collect, process, and transmit data from sensors and other devices. There are several types of embedded platforms to choose from, and the right choice will depend on the specific requirements of the IoT application.
