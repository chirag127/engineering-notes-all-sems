# Unit 5 - Programming the Arduino

## Additions in Arduino

- To add numbers on the Arduino, we use the addition operator (+) .
- The example below shows how to add two numbers together:
```c
int a = 2;
int b = 7;
int sum;
sum = a + b;
```
- Addition is one of the four primary arithmetic operations .
- The operator + (plus) operates on two operands to produce the sum .
- The syntax for addition is `sum = operand1 + operand2;` .
- The `sum` variable can be of data types: `int`, `float`, `double`, `byte`, `short`, `long` .
- The `operand1` and `operand2` can be a variable or constant of data types: `int`, `float`, `double`, `byte`, `short`, `long` .
- There is also a convenient shorthand to perform addition on a variable with another constant or variable using the `+=` compound addition operator .
