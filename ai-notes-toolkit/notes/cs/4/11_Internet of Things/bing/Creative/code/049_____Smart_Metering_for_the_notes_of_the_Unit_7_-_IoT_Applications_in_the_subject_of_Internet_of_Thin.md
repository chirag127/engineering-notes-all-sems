### Smart Metering

- Smart metering is the monitoring of resource consumption, such as energy, water, gas, etc., through modern metering devices connected to the Internet via IoT technology.
- Smart metering devices, also known as smart meters, can record and communicate energy consumption data to utilities for monitoring and other use cases, such as billing and demand response.
- Smart metering can provide benefits for both utility companies and their consumers, such as:
  - Improved accuracy and efficiency of meter reading and billing .
  - Reduced operational and maintenance costs for utilities .
  - Enhanced visibility and control of energy usage and costs for consumers .
  - Increased awareness and engagement in energy conservation and management .
  - Enabled integration of renewable energy sources and smart grid applications .
- Smart metering can be implemented using various IoT technologies, such as:
  - Low Power Wide Area Network (LPWAN) connectivity, which can provide long-range, low-cost, and low-power wireless communication for smart meters.
  - Embedded SIM (eSIM) cards, which can provide flexible and secure cellular connectivity for smart meters without the need for physical SIM swapping.
  - IoT platforms, which can provide data collection, processing, visualization, and analytics for smart metering applications  .
  - IoT dashboards, which can provide interactive and customizable user interfaces for smart metering data visualization and management .
- Smart metering is one of the most important and widespread IoT applications for utilities and smart cities, as it can improve the efficiency, reliability, and sustainability of resource distribution and consumption  .
