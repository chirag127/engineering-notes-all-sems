### Intel Galileo boards

- Intel Galileo boards are development boards based on Intel x86 architecture and are designed for the maker and education communities.
- Intel Galileo boards are compatible with Arduino shields designed for the Uno R3 and can run Arduino sketches as well as Linux applications .
- Intel Galileo boards are powered by the Intel® Quark SoC X1000, a 32-bit Intel Pentium-class system on a chip that operates at up to 400 MHz and supports 256 MB of DDR3 RAM and 8 MB of NOR flash memory  .
- Intel Galileo boards have a range of features and interfaces, such as:
  - 14 digital input/output pins, of which 6 can be used as PWM outputs and 4 as analog inputs
  - A mini-PCI Express slot, a micro-SD card slot, a USB host port, a USB client port, and an Ethernet port
  - A 6-pin 3.3V USB TTL UART header, a JTAG header, and an ICSP header
  - A reset button, a power button, and a 5V power jack
  - A 10-pin connector for Intel® Edison module (only for Gen 2 board)
- Intel released two versions of Galileo boards, referred to as Gen 1 and Gen 2, which have some differences in hardware and software support .
- Intel discontinued the Galileo boards on June 19, 2017 and no longer provides technical support or software updates for them .
