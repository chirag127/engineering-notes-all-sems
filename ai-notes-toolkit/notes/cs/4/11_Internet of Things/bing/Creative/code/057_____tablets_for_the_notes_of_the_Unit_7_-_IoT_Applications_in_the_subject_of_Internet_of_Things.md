### Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of IoT devices and systems that provide value to users, businesses, or society.
- IoT devices are physical objects that are connected to the internet and can sense, communicate, or actuate data or commands.
- IoT systems are the networks of IoT devices and the software and services that enable their functionality and interoperability.
- Tablets are portable computing devices that have touchscreens and can run various applications.
- Tablets can be used for IoT applications in different ways, such as:

  - **Monitoring and controlling IoT devices**: Tablets can act as user interfaces for IoT devices, allowing users to access, visualize, and manipulate data or commands from the devices. For example, a tablet can be used to monitor and control a smart thermostat, a smart lock, or a smart pill dispenser.
  - **Collecting and processing IoT data**: Tablets can act as edge devices for IoT systems, collecting and processing data from IoT sensors or devices before sending it to the cloud or other destinations. For example, a tablet can be used to collect and analyze data from a wearable device, a smart meter, or a drone.
  - **Enhancing IoT functionality**: Tablets can act as complementary devices for IoT systems, enhancing their functionality or user experience. For example, a tablet can be used to provide voice or gesture control for IoT devices, to display augmented reality or virtual reality content for IoT applications, or to enable secure and reliable communication for IoT systems.
- Some examples of IoT applications that use tablets are:

  - **Smart home**: Tablets can be used to monitor and control various smart home devices, such as lights, cameras, speakers, or appliances. Users can also use tablets to access smart home services, such as entertainment, security, or energy management.
  - **Smart healthcare**: Tablets can be used to collect and process health data from IoT devices, such as blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, or pill dispensers. Users can also use tablets to access health services, such as telemedicine, medication reminders, or wellness coaching.
  - **Smart agriculture**: Tablets can be used to monitor and control various smart agriculture devices, such as soil sensors, irrigation systems, or drones. Users can also use tablets to access agriculture services, such as crop management, pest control, or weather forecasting.
  - **Smart education**: Tablets can be used to enhance the learning experience of students and teachers, using IoT devices such as smart boards, smart pens, or smart glasses. Users can also use tablets to access education services, such as interactive content, personalized feedback, or collaborative learning.
