### Data aggregation and dissemination for the notes of the Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Data aggregation and dissemination are two fundamental services in Internet of Things (IoT) networks .
- Data aggregation is the process of collecting, filtering, and summarizing data from multiple sources or nodes in the IoT network.
- Data dissemination is the process of transmitting data from a source (e.g., a base station, a cloud server, or a sensor node) to one or more destinations (e.g., other nodes, applications, or users) in the IoT network .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be performed at different levels of the IoT network, such as the edge, the fog, or the cloud.
- Data aggregation and dissemination can have different objectives and challenges, such as reducing network traffic, saving energy, improving data quality, ensuring security and privacy, and supporting heterogeneous devices and protocols  .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can be coordinated using different techniques and algorithms, such as machine learning, optimization, game theory, and distributed computing .
- Data aggregation and dissemination can enable various applications and services in the IoT, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, and smart industry .
