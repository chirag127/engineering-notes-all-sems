### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- IoT/M2M systems are composed of devices, networks, platforms, and applications that enable communication and data exchange between machines and things.
- IoT/M2M systems can be divided into three major domains: the application layer, the service layer, and the network layer .
- The application layer is the domain where the end-user applications and services are implemented and executed. It provides the user interface and the business logic for the IoT/M2M scenarios.
- The service layer is the domain where the common functionalities and capabilities for IoT/M2M systems are provided. It offers standardized APIs and protocols for the application layer and the network layer to interact and interoperate.
- The network layer is the domain where the connectivity and transport mechanisms for IoT/M2M systems are provided. It supports various types of networks and technologies, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LoRa, etc.
- One of the main challenges for IoT/M2M systems is to achieve interoperability and scalability across different domains, platforms, and technologies. Therefore, standardization is essential to ensure compatibility and harmonization of IoT/M2M systems.
- One of the main initiatives for IoT/M2M standardization is oneM2M, a global partnership project that aims to develop a common service layer for IoT/M2M systems  .
- OneM2M defines a set of functional entities, interfaces, and protocols for the service layer, such as the Application Entity (AE), the Common Services Entity (CSE), and the oneM2M Resource Model.
- OneM2M also defines a set of service layer capabilities, such as device management, data management, security, discovery, subscription, notification, etc.
- OneM2M supports various types of application layer protocols, such as HTTP, CoAP, MQTT, etc., and various types of network layer protocols, such as 3GPP, IEEE, OMA, etc.
- OneM2M is designed to be flexible, modular, and extensible, allowing for the integration of existing and emerging IoT/M2M technologies and platforms.
- Other standardization initiatives for IoT/M2M systems include the Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC), which focuses on the geospatial aspects of IoT/M2M systems, and the IEEE, which focuses on the technical aspects of IoT/M2M systems, such as communication protocols, security, and quality of service.
