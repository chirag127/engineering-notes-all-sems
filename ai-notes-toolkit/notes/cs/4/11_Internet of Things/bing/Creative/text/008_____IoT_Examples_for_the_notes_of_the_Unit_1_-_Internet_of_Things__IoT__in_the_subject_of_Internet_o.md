### IoT Examples

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical devices or things that are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity to exchange data and communicate over the internet. IoT has many applications and benefits in various sectors and domains, such as:

- **Connected cars**: IoT enables vehicles to communicate with each other, with the infrastructure, and with the cloud, providing features such as navigation, safety, entertainment, diagnostics, and remote control. Examples of connected car platforms are Airbiquity, Tesla, and Google's Waymo.
- **Smart appliances**: IoT enables household appliances to be controlled and monitored remotely, as well as to optimize their energy consumption and performance. Examples of smart appliances are Samsung's Family Hub refrigerator, LG's ThinQ washer and dryer, and Amazon's Alexa-enabled devices.
- **Connected security systems**: IoT enables home and business owners to monitor and protect their properties from intruders, fire, flood, and other hazards, using sensors, cameras, alarms, and notifications. Examples of connected security systems are Ring, Nest, and SimpliSafe.
- **Smart agriculture equipment**: IoT enables farmers to improve their crop yield, reduce water and fertilizer waste, and monitor their livestock, using sensors, drones, robots, and artificial intelligence. Examples of smart agriculture equipment are John Deere's precision farming solutions, CropX's soil sensors, and Cainthus's facial recognition for cows.
- **Connected retail**: IoT enables retailers to enhance their customer experience, optimize their inventory and supply chain, and increase their sales and revenue, using sensors, RFID tags, beacons, digital signage, and analytics. Examples of connected retail are Amazon Go, Walmart's smart shopping carts, and Zara's smart dressing rooms.
- **Connected healthcare monitors**: IoT enables patients and healthcare providers to monitor and manage various health conditions, such as diabetes, heart disease, and COVID-19, using wearable devices, implants, smart pills, and telemedicine. Examples of connected healthcare monitors are Fitbit, Dexcom's glucose monitor, and Medtronic's pacemaker.
- **Connected manufacturing equipment**: IoT enables manufacturers to improve their productivity, quality, and efficiency, as well as to reduce their costs and risks, using sensors, robots, augmented reality, and predictive maintenance. Examples of connected manufacturing equipment are Siemens's MindSphere, GE's Predix, and Bosch's IoT Suite.
- **Connected cities**: IoT enables urban planners and citizens to improve their livability, sustainability, and resilience, using smart lighting, traffic management, waste management, and public safety. Examples of connected cities are Barcelona, Singapore, and Amsterdam.
