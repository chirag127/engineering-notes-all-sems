### Raspberry pi for the notes of the Unit 3 - Embedded Platforms for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost, single-board computer the size of a credit card that allows people from different backgrounds and levels of expertise to experience and learn to compute  .
- Raspberry Pi uses what's called a system on a chip, which integrates the CPU and GPU in a single integrated circuit, with the RAM, USB ports, and other components soldered onto the board for an all-in-one package.
- Raspberry Pi can plug into a computer monitor or TV, and use a standard keyboard and mouse. It can run various operating systems, such as Linux, Windows, or Android .
- Raspberry Pi can also interact with the physical world through its 40-pin GPIO header, which allows it to connect to sensors, actuators, cameras, and other devices .
- Raspberry Pi is suitable for embedded platforms for IoT because it is:
  - Affordable: Raspberry Pi costs from $25 to $75, depending on the model and features .
  - Accessible: Raspberry Pi is widely available and supported by a large community of users and developers .
  - Adaptable: Raspberry Pi can run various applications and programs, from web servers to gaming consoles, from robotics to smart home systems .
  - Educational: Raspberry Pi can help people of all ages to learn computing, programming, and electronics, as well as to develop creativity and problem-solving skills .
