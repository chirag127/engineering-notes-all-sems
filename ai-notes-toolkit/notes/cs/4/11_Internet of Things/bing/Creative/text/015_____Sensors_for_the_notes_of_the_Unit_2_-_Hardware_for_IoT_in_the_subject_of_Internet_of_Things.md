### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, motion, light, sound, etc., and convert them into electrical signals that can be processed by IoT systems .
- Sensors are essential components of IoT applications, as they provide the data that enables smarter decisions and actions based on the real-world conditions .
- Sensors can be classified into different types based on their function, output, technology, or application domain .
- Some of the common types of sensors for IoT are :
  - Temperature sensors: These sensors measure the amount of heat generated from an area or an object. They are widely used in environmental monitoring, smart home, healthcare, agriculture, etc.
  - Proximity sensors: These sensors detect the presence or absence of objects near the sensor without physical contact. They are used in security, automation, robotics, automotive, etc.
  - Pressure sensors: These sensors detect changes in a gas or liquid. They are used in industrial, medical, aerospace, automotive, etc.
  - Water quality sensors: These sensors measure the physical and chemical properties of water, such as pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, etc. They are used in environmental monitoring, water management, agriculture, etc.
  - Chemical and gas sensors: These sensors measure the concentration of specific chemicals or gases in the air or liquid. They are used in environmental monitoring, industrial safety, healthcare, etc.
  - Infrared sensors: These sensors detect the infrared radiation emitted by objects or sources. They are used in thermal imaging, night vision, motion detection, etc.
  - Smoke sensors: These sensors detect the presence of smoke or fire. They are used in fire alarm, security, safety, etc.
  - Motion sensors: These sensors detect the movement or position of objects or people. They are used in security, automation, gaming, healthcare, etc.
  - Humidity sensors: These sensors measure the amount of moisture in the air or an object. They are used in environmental monitoring, smart home, agriculture, etc.
  - Light sensors: These sensors measure the intensity or color of light. They are used in ambient lighting, optical communication, imaging, etc.
  - Sound sensors: These sensors measure the frequency or amplitude of sound waves. They are used in noise detection, voice recognition, acoustic sensing, etc.
  - Biomedical sensors: These sensors measure the physiological parameters of living beings, such as heart rate, blood pressure, glucose level, etc. They are used in healthcare, fitness, wellness, etc.
  - Accelerometers: These sensors measure the acceleration or change in speed, direction, and intensity of movement. They are used in motion detection, vibration analysis, navigation, etc.
  - Gyroscopes: These sensors measure the angular velocity or orientation of an object. They are used in navigation, stabilization, gaming, etc.
