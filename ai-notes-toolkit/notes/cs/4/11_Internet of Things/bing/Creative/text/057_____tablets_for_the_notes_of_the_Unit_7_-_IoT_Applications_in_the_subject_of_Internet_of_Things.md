### Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of IoT devices and systems that provide value to users, businesses, or society.
- Tablets are portable computing devices that have touchscreens, wireless connectivity, and various sensors and features.
- Tablets can be used for various IoT applications, such as:
  - **Smart pill dispensers**: Tablets can communicate with IoT-enabled pill dispensers that monitor and control the dosage and timing of medication for patients. Tablets can also display reminders, alerts, and health information for the users and caregivers.
  - **IoT Communicator**: Tablets can act as an interface for IoT devices and systems that use the MQTT protocol to exchange data. Tablets can display process data, alarms, and notifications from IoT devices in a clear and user-friendly way.
  - **Sensor network access**: Tablets can provide access to sensor networks that collect and transmit data from various IoT devices, such as environmental sensors, smart meters, or industrial machines. Tablets can streamline in-field diagnostics, maintenance, monitoring, and setup of sensor networks.
  - **Touchless transactions**: Tablets can enable touchless transactions, such as contactless payment and data collection and tracking, by using NFC, QR codes, or biometric authentication. Tablets can improve the convenience, security, and hygiene of transactions for customers and businesses.
  - **IoT Managed Tablet**: Tablets can be customized and managed by IoT service providers to provide controlled access to specific business applications, such as inventory management, field service, or digital signage. Tablets can also be remotely updated, monitored, and secured by the IoT service providers.

- Tablets for IoT applications should have the following features and capabilities:
  - **Wireless connectivity**: Tablets should support various wireless standards, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, NFC, or RFID, to communicate with IoT devices and systems.
  - **Security**: Tablets should have encryption, authentication, and authorization mechanisms to protect the data and privacy of the users and IoT devices and systems.
  - **Battery life**: Tablets should have long-lasting batteries that can support the continuous use and communication of IoT applications.
  - **Sensors and features**: Tablets should have sensors and features that can enhance the functionality and usability of IoT applications, such as cameras, microphones, speakers, GPS, accelerometers, gyroscopes, or biometric scanners.
  - **User interface**: Tablets should have user-friendly and intuitive interfaces that can display and interact with the data and information from IoT devices and systems. Tablets should also support voice, gesture, or stylus input for convenience and accessibility.
