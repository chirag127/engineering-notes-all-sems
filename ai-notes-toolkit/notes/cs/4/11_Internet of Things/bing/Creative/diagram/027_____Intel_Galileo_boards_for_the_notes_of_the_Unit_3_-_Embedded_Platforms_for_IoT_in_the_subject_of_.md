### Intel Galileo boards

- Intel Galileo boards are development boards based on Intel x86 architecture and are designed for the maker and education communities.
- Intel Galileo boards are compatible with Arduino shields designed for the Uno R3 and can run Arduino sketches as well as Linux applications .
- Intel Galileo boards are based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000, a 32-bit Intel Pentium-class system on a chip with 16 KB cache and 400 MHz clock speed .
- Intel Galileo boards have 256 MB of DDR3 RAM, 8 MB of NOR flash memory, a micro SD slot, a mini PCI Express slot, a 10/100 Ethernet port, a USB host port, a USB client port, and a serial port .
- Intel Galileo boards have 14 digital input/output pins, 6 analog input pins, a PWM output pin, an ICSP header, and 4 UARTs .
- Intel Galileo boards can be powered by a 5V DC power supply or a micro USB cable .
- Intel Galileo boards can be programmed using the Arduino IDE, the Intel XDK IoT Edition, or the Intel System Studio IoT Edition .
- Intel Galileo boards can communicate with other devices using Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, or cellular modules via the mini PCI Express slot or the USB host port .
- Intel Galileo boards can be used for various IoT applications such as smart home, smart agriculture, robotics, wearable devices, etc .
- Intel Galileo boards are discontinued as of June 19, 2017.
