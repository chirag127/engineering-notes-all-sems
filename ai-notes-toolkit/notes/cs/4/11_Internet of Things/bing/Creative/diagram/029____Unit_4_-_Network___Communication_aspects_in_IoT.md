## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

- IoT devices communicate with other devices, applications, and services using various network and communication protocols  .
- IoT devices can use local communication methods such as Bluetooth, Zigbee, Wi-Fi, or cellular to connect with neighboring devices or gateways.
- Gateways are devices that translate and re-transmit information, typically linking local device networks to the Internet.
- IoT devices can also use application protocols such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, or AMQP to define how information content is transported over the network.
- IoT devices mostly use IPv6, which is the latest internet protocol version, to transfer data and communicate over the Internet. Each IoT device has an IP address and networking is the key aspect in the Internet of Things.
- The communication layer provides service to the network layer, which is responsible for routing and addressing the data packets.
- The network and communication protocols for IoT devices should be secure and reliable, as well as efficient and scalable.
- IoT devices should also be able to adapt to different network conditions and requirements, such as bandwidth, latency, power consumption, and mobility.
- IoT networks can be classified into different types based on the range, topology, and architecture, such as personal area networks (PAN), local area networks (LAN), wide area networks (WAN), or low power wide area networks (LPWAN) .
- IoT networks can also be designed and optimized for specific IoT verticals, such as smart cities, factories, healthcare, retail, etc..
