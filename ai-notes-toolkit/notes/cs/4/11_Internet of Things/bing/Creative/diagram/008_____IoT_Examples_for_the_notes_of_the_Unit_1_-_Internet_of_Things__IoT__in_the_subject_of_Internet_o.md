### IoT Examples

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that can collect, process, exchange and utilize data interrelatedly, via the internet or other communications networks. IoT has many different applications and benefits for various domains and industries. Here are some examples of IoT devices and systems that are commonly used or being developed:

- **Connected cars**: These are vehicles that can communicate with other vehicles, infrastructure, cloud services and drivers through sensors, cameras, GPS, onboard computers and wireless technologies. Connected cars can provide features such as navigation, entertainment, safety, diagnostics, maintenance and remote control .
- **Smart appliances**: These are household devices that can be controlled and monitored remotely via smartphones, tablets or voice assistants. Smart appliances can provide features such as energy efficiency, convenience, security and automation  . Examples of smart appliances include refrigerators, ovens, washing machines, air conditioners, lights and speakers.
- **Connected security systems**: These are systems that can detect and prevent intrusions, fire, smoke, gas leaks and other hazards in homes and buildings. Connected security systems can provide features such as real-time alerts, video surveillance, remote access, emergency response and automation . Examples of connected security systems include cameras, alarms, locks, sensors and smoke detectors.
- **Smart agriculture equipment**: These are devices that can monitor and optimize the growth and health of crops and livestock. Smart agriculture equipment can provide features such as precision farming, irrigation, pest control, animal tracking and automation. Examples of smart agriculture equipment include drones, sensors, robots, RFID tags and smart collars.
- **Connected retail**: This is the use of IoT devices and systems to enhance the shopping experience and operations of retailers and customers. Connected retail can provide features such as personalized recommendations, inventory management, payment processing, customer loyalty and analytics. Examples of connected retail include smart shelves, digital signage, smart mirrors, smart carts and smart tags.
- **Connected healthcare monitors**: These are devices that can measure and track the vital signs and health conditions of patients and users. Connected healthcare monitors can provide features such as remote diagnosis, treatment, prevention, wellness and fitness . Examples of connected healthcare monitors include smart watches, blood pressure monitors, glucose meters, ECG monitors and wearable patches.
- **Connected manufacturing equipment**: These are machines and tools that can perform and optimize the production and maintenance of goods and services. Connected manufacturing equipment can provide features such as quality control, energy efficiency, predictive maintenance, asset management and automation . Examples of connected manufacturing equipment include robots, sensors, actuators, RFID tags and smart meters.
- **Connected cities**: These are urban areas that can use IoT devices and systems to improve the quality of life and sustainability of their residents and visitors. Connected cities can provide features such as traffic management, public safety, environmental monitoring, waste management and smart lighting. Examples of connected cities include smart parking, smart streetlights, smart bins, smart meters and smart grids.
