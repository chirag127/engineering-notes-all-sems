### Automotive Applications

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable data collection and communication. IoT has various applications in different domains, such as healthcare, agriculture, smart cities, and manufacturing. One of the domains that has been significantly transformed by IoT is the automotive industry. IoT applications in the automotive industry can improve design, performance, safety, efficiency, and customer satisfaction. Some of the use cases of IoT applications in the automotive industry are:

- **Fleet management**: IoT can help fleet operators and owners monitor the location, speed, fuel consumption, mileage, and maintenance status of their vehicles in real-time. This can help optimize routes, reduce costs, improve safety, and enhance customer service. IoT can also enable remote diagnostics and updates for the vehicles, as well as driver behavior analysis and feedback .
- **Vehicle tracking**: IoT can help track the location and movement of individual vehicles, such as cars, bikes, or scooters, using GPS and other sensors. This can help owners locate their vehicles, prevent theft, recover stolen vehicles, and share their vehicles with others. IoT can also enable pay-per-use or pay-per-mile models for vehicle rental or insurance .
- **Connected cars**: IoT can help connect cars to the internet, other cars, and the infrastructure, enabling various features and services, such as navigation, entertainment, emergency assistance, traffic information, parking assistance, and smart charging. IoT can also enable vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication, which can improve road safety, traffic efficiency, and environmental sustainability  .
- **Automotive maintenance system**: IoT can help monitor the condition and performance of the vehicle components, such as engine, brakes, tires, and battery, using sensors and cloud computing. This can help detect and prevent faults, schedule maintenance, and reduce downtime. IoT can also enable predictive maintenance, which can anticipate and prevent failures before they occur, using machine learning and data analytics  .
- **Autonomous vehicles**: IoT can help enable self-driving cars, which can operate without human intervention, using sensors, cameras, radars, lidars, and artificial intelligence. Autonomous vehicles can offer various benefits, such as improved safety, convenience, mobility, and productivity. IoT can also enable platooning, which is the coordination of multiple autonomous vehicles to form a group or a convoy, which can reduce fuel consumption, emissions, and traffic congestion  .
- **In-vehicle infotainment and telematics**: IoT can help provide various entertainment and information services to the drivers and passengers, such as music, video, games, news, weather, and social media, using internet connectivity and touchscreens. IoT can also enable telematics, which is the transmission of data from the vehicle to a remote server, which can provide various services, such as insurance, roadside assistance, diagnostics, and analytics  .
- **Theft detection during the maintenance and manufacturing processes**: IoT can help prevent and detect theft of vehicle parts or components during the maintenance and manufacturing processes, using RFID tags, sensors, and cameras. This can help reduce losses, improve quality control, and ensure compliance.
- **Driver behavioral management**: IoT can help monitor and analyze the driving behavior of the drivers, such as speed, acceleration, braking, steering, and lane changing, using sensors, cameras, and machine learning. This can help provide feedback, coaching, and incentives to the drivers, as well as adjust the insurance premiums, fuel consumption, and maintenance costs according to the driving behavior .
