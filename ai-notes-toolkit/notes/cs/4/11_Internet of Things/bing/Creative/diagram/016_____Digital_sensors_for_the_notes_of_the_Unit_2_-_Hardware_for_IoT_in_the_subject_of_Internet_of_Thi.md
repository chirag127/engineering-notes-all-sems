### Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data in a digital format .
- Digital sensors are connected to a network and share data with the network, bridging the digital world to the physical world .
- Digital sensors interact directly with an IoT microcontroller, eliminating the need for an extra step of converting analog data to digital data.
- Digital sensors are classified based on the work they perform, such as temperature sensors, pressure sensors, motion sensors, light sensors, etc .
- Digital sensors are used in various applications of IoT, such as consumer devices, industry 4.0, medical applications, smart cities, etc  .
- Digital sensors enable the IoT to collect data for smarter decisions, automation, optimization, and innovation   .
