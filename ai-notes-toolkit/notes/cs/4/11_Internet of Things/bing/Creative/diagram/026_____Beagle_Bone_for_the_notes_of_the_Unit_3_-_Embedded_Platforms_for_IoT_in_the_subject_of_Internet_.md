### Beagle Bone

Beagle Bone is a series of low-cost, open-source, single-board computers (SBCs) that run Linux and can be used for various embedded applications, such as robotics, 3D printing, gaming, and machine learning. Beagle Bone is developed by the BeagleBoard.org Foundation, a non-profit organization that supports the open hardware and software community.

Some of the features and benefits of Beagle Bone are:

- It has a small size (about the size of a credit card) and a low power consumption (about 2W).
- It has a powerful processor (ARM Cortex-A8 or A15) that can run up to 1 GHz and support various operating systems, such as Android, Ubuntu, Debian, and Angstrom.
- It has a large amount of memory (512 MB or 1 GB of RAM and 4 GB of eMMC flash storage) and a microSD card slot for additional storage.
- It has a rich set of peripherals and interfaces, such as USB, Ethernet, HDMI, audio, serial, SPI, I2C, CAN, PWM, ADC, and GPIO. It also supports various expansion boards, called capes, that can add functionality, such as LCD, camera, WiFi, Bluetooth, GPS, and sensors.
- It has a large and active community of developers and users who provide support, documentation, tutorials, and projects.

Some of the examples of Beagle Bone products are:

- BeagleBone Black: The most popular and widely used Beagle Bone product, which has a 1 GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512 MB of RAM, 4 GB of eMMC flash storage, and a micro HDMI port.
- BeagleBone Green: A variant of BeagleBone Black that replaces the HDMI port with two Grove connectors, which are compatible with Seeed Studio's Grove sensors and modules.
- BeagleBone Green Wireless: A variant of BeagleBone Green that adds WiFi and Bluetooth connectivity.
- BeagleBone Blue: A variant of BeagleBone Black that is designed for robotics and has a built-in IMU, motor drivers, encoders, servos, and battery management.
- BeagleBone AI: A variant of BeagleBone Black that has a 1.5 GHz ARM Cortex-A15 processor, 1 GB of RAM, 16 GB of eMMC flash storage, and a dual-core DSP and dual-core PRU for accelerated machine learning applications.
