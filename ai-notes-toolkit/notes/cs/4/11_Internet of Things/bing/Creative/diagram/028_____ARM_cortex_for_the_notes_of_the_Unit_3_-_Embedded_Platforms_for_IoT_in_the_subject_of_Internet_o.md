### ARM cortex

- ARM cortex is a group of 32-bit and 64-bit RISC ARM processor cores licensed by Arm Holdings  .
- ARM cortex cores are intended for application use in embedded systems, such as microcontrollers, ASICs, ASSPs, FPGAs, and SoCs  .
- ARM cortex cores are divided into four profiles: A, R, M, and S.
  - Cortex-A profile: for high-performance and complex applications, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, servers, etc  .
  - Cortex-R profile: for real-time and safety-critical applications, such as automotive, industrial, medical, etc .
  - Cortex-M profile: for low-cost and energy-efficient applications, such as sensors, IoT devices, wearables, etc  .
  - Cortex-S profile: for secure and trusted applications, such as secure boot, secure storage, etc .
- ARM cortex cores support various features and extensions, such as:
  - Thumb instruction set: a 16-bit compressed instruction set for code density and performance.
  - NEON technology: a SIMD engine for multimedia and signal processing.
  - Helium technology: a MVE (M-Profile Vector Extension) for efficient vector processing.
  - Security extensions: TrustZone for Cortex-A and Cortex-M profiles, for hardware isolation and protection of sensitive data and code.
  - No-execute page protection: a mechanism to prevent execution of data as code.
  - Large Physical Address Extension (LPAE): a feature to support up to 1 TB of physical memory.
  - Armv8-R and Armv8-M: the latest architectures for Cortex-R and Cortex-M profiles, with support for 64-bit and 32-bit modes, and various enhancements.
