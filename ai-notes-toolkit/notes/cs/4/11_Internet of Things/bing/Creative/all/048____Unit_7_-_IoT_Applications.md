## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of the Internet of Things, which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable data collection and communication.
- IoT applications can bring a lot of value to various domains, such as industry, commerce, health, agriculture, and environment, by providing insights, automation, efficiency, and security.
- Some examples of IoT applications are:

  - **Smart home**: IoT devices can be used to control and monitor various aspects of a home, such as lighting, temperature, security, entertainment, and appliances. For example, a smart thermostat can adjust the temperature based on the occupancy and preferences of the residents, or a smart doorbell can send a notification and a video feed to the owner's smartphone when someone rings the bell .
  - **Smart city**: IoT devices can be used to improve the quality of life and the management of urban services, such as transportation, energy, water, waste, and public safety. For example, a traffic camera can detect congestion and adjust the traffic lights accordingly, or a smart meter can measure and report the electricity consumption of a building .
  - **Smart industry**: IoT devices can be used to optimize the production, operation, and maintenance of industrial assets, such as machines, equipment, vehicles, and infrastructure. For example, a sensor can monitor the vibration and temperature of a motor and alert the operator when it needs repair, or a RFID tag can track the location and status of a product in a supply chain .
  - **Smart health**: IoT devices can be used to enable remote health monitoring and emergency notification systems. These health monitoring devices can range from blood pressure and heart rate monitors to advanced devices capable of monitoring specialized implants, such as pacemakers, Fitbit electronic wristbands, or advanced hearing aids .
  - **Smart agriculture**: IoT devices can be used to enhance the productivity, quality, and sustainability of agricultural practices, such as crop cultivation, animal husbandry, and irrigation. For example, a soil moisture sensor can measure the water level and trigger the irrigation system when needed, or a drone can capture aerial images and analyze the crop health and yield .
  - **Smart environment**: IoT devices can be used to measure and protect the natural environment, such as air, water, soil, and wildlife. For example, a air quality sensor can detect the level of pollutants and send alerts to the authorities and the public, or a wildlife tracker can monitor the movement and behavior of endangered species .
