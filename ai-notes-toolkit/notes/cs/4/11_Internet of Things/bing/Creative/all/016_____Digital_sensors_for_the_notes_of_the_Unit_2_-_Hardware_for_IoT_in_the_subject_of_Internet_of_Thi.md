# Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data in a digital format.
- Digital sensors are essential for enabling the Internet of Things (IoT) by bridging the physical world and the digital world.
- Digital sensors can measure various physical phenomena such as temperature, pressure, motion, humidity, gas, light, sound, etc .
- Digital sensors can communicate with other devices or networks using wired or wireless protocols such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, ZigBee, LoRa, etc .
- Digital sensors can be integrated into various applications such as consumer devices, industry 4.0, smart cities, healthcare, agriculture, etc  .
- Digital sensors can enable smarter decisions, improved efficiency, enhanced safety, and reduced costs for IoT systems  .

Some examples of digital sensors for IoT are:

- Temperature sensor: A device that detects the heat energy produced by an object or a nearby area and converts it into a digital signal. Temperature sensors can be used for monitoring and controlling the temperature of devices, rooms, buildings, etc.
- Smoke sensor: A device that detects the presence of smoke in the air and converts it into a digital signal. Smoke sensors can be used for fire detection and prevention in homes, industries, etc.
- Motion sensor: A device that detects the movement of an object or a person and converts it into a digital signal. Motion sensors can be used for security, automation, and energy saving purposes in IoT systems.
- Humidity sensor: A device that detects the amount of moisture in the air and converts it into a digital signal. Humidity sensors can be used for monitoring and controlling the humidity of devices, rooms, buildings, etc.
- Pressure sensor: A device that detects the force exerted by a fluid or a gas and converts it into a digital signal. Pressure sensors can be used for monitoring and controlling the pressure of systems and devices that are pressure propelled, such as pumps, valves, compressors, etc.
- Gas sensor: A device that detects the concentration of a specific gas in the air and converts it into a digital signal. Gas sensors can be used for detecting and preventing gas leaks, air quality monitoring, and environmental sensing in IoT systems.
- IR sensor: A device that detects the infrared radiation emitted or reflected by an object and converts it into a digital signal. IR sensors can be used for measuring the distance, speed, or presence of an object, as well as for remote control and communication in IoT systems.
- Accelerometer sensor: A device that detects the acceleration or vibration of an object and converts it into a digital signal. Accelerometer sensors can be used for measuring the orientation, tilt, or motion of an object, as well as for detecting shocks, impacts, or falls in IoT systems.
