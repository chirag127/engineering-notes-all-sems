## Unit 7 - IoT Applications

- IoT applications are the use cases of the Internet of Things, which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable data collection and communication.
- IoT applications can bring a lot of value in our lives by improving efficiency, productivity, safety, and convenience in various domains such as industry, commerce, agriculture, healthcare, and smart cities.
- Some examples of IoT applications are:

  - **Smart lighting**: IoT devices can control the brightness and color of the lights based on the time of the day, the occupancy of the room, the weather, or the user's preferences. This can reduce energy consumption and enhance the ambiance of the space .
  - **Smart parking**: IoT sensors can detect the availability of parking spaces and guide the drivers to the nearest vacant spot. This can save time, fuel, and money for the drivers and increase the revenue for the parking operators .
  - **Smart agriculture**: IoT devices can monitor the soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and crop health and provide real-time feedback to the farmers. This can help optimize irrigation, fertilization, pest control, and harvesting practices and increase crop yield and quality .
  - **Smart healthcare**: IoT devices can measure various vital signs of the patients such as blood pressure, heart rate, glucose level, and oxygen saturation and transmit the data to the doctors or caregivers. This can enable remote health monitoring, emergency notification, and personalized treatment .
