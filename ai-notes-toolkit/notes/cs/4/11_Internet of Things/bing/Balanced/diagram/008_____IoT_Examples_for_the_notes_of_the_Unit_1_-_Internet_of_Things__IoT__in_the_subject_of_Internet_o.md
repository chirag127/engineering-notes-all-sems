### IoT Examples

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that can collect, process, exchange and utilize data interrelatedly, via the internet or other communications networks. IoT has many different applications in various domains, such as transportation, home automation, healthcare, agriculture, retail, manufacturing and smart cities. Here are some examples of IoT devices and systems that illustrate the potential and diversity of IoT:

- **Connected cars**: Cars that can communicate with other vehicles, infrastructure, cloud services and drivers through sensors, cameras, GPS, onboard computers and wireless technologies. Connected cars can offer features such as navigation, entertainment, safety, diagnostics, maintenance and remote control .
- **Smart appliances**: Appliances that can be controlled and monitored remotely via smartphones, tablets or voice assistants. Smart appliances can also adjust their settings and operations based on user preferences, environmental conditions, energy consumption and other factors. Examples of smart appliances include refrigerators, ovens, washing machines, air conditioners and coffee makers .
- **Connected security systems**: Security systems that can detect and alert users of intrusions, fire, smoke, gas leaks, water leaks and other hazards. Connected security systems can also allow users to access live video feeds, lock and unlock doors, arm and disarm alarms, and control lights and thermostats from anywhere .
- **Smart agriculture equipment**: Equipment that can monitor and optimize crop growth, soil quality, water usage, pest control and harvest. Smart agriculture equipment can also automate tasks such as irrigation, fertilization, spraying and harvesting. Examples of smart agriculture equipment include drones, sensors, robots and smart greenhouses .
- **Connected retail**: Retail systems that can enhance customer experience, inventory management, supply chain optimization and marketing. Connected retail systems can also provide personalized recommendations, offers and loyalty programs based on customer behavior, preferences and location. Examples of connected retail systems include smart shelves, digital signage, smart mirrors, smart carts and smart payment terminals .
- **Connected healthcare monitors**: Healthcare devices that can measure and transmit vital signs, blood pressure, glucose levels, heart rate, oxygen saturation and other health indicators. Connected healthcare monitors can also provide feedback, alerts and reminders to patients and caregivers. Examples of connected healthcare monitors include wearable devices, implantable devices, smart patches and smart pills .
- **Connected manufacturing equipment**: Manufacturing equipment that can collect and analyze data on production processes, quality, efficiency, energy consumption and maintenance. Connected manufacturing equipment can also enable predictive maintenance, remote control, fault detection and optimization. Examples of connected manufacturing equipment include sensors, robots, smart machines and smart factories .
- **Connected cities**: Cities that can leverage IoT to improve public services, infrastructure, safety, environment and quality of life. Connected cities can also enable smart transportation, smart energy, smart waste management, smart lighting and smart governance. Examples of connected cities include Barcelona, Singapore, Amsterdam and Seoul .
