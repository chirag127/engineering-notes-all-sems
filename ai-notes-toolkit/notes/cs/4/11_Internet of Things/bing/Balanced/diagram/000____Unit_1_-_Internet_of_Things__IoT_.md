## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communications networks .
- The IoT can be used for various purposes, such as remote monitoring, control, automation, analysis, and optimization of physical systems, processes, and environments.
- The IoT can also enable new services, applications, and business models that leverage the data collected and processed by the connected devices.
- The IoT can be classified into different domains, such as smart home, smart city, smart industry, smart agriculture, smart health, smart mobility, and smart energy.
- The IoT can also be categorized into different layers, such as perception, network, middleware, application, and business.
- The IoT faces various challenges, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and energy efficiency.
- The IoT requires various technologies, such as sensors, actuators, communication protocols, cloud computing, edge computing, artificial intelligence, and blockchain.
