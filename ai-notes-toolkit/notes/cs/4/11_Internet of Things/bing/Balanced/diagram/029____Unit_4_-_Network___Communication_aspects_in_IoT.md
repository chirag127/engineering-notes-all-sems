## Unit 4 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT

- IoT devices are anything that can connect to the internet and communicate with other devices, applications, and services.
- IoT devices use different local communication methods to speak with neighboring devices, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, Z-Wave, etc.
- IoT devices also use different application protocols to define how information content is transported, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc.
- IoT devices often rely on gateways to translate and re-transmit information, typically linking local device networks to the internet.
- IoT devices mostly use IPv6, which is the latest internet protocol version, to transfer data and communicate over the internet. Each IoT device has an IP address and networking is the key aspect in the Internet of Things.
- IoT communication can be classified into different types based on the direction, distance, and topology of the data flow, such as device-to-device, device-to-cloud, device-to-gateway, etc.
- IoT communication also requires certain features and challenges, such as security, reliability, scalability, interoperability, etc.
