### Beagle Bone

Beagle Bone is a series of low-cost, open-source, single-board Linux computers that can be used for embedded applications such as Internet of Things (IoT). Some of the features and benefits of Beagle Bone are:

- It has a small size (about the size of a credit card) and a low power consumption (about 2W).
- It has a high-performance processor (ARM Cortex-A8 or A15) that can run various operating systems such as Android, Ubuntu, Debian, etc.
- It has plenty of input/output (I/O) interfaces and expansion capabilities, such as USB, Ethernet, HDMI, serial, analog, digital, etc. It can also be augmented with plug-in boards called capes that provide additional functionality such as sensors, displays, wireless, etc.
- It has a large and active community of developers and users who provide support, documentation, tutorials, projects, etc.

Some of the examples of Beagle Bone products are:

- BeagleBone Black: The most popular and widely used Beagle Bone product. It has a 1GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512MB of RAM, 4GB of eMMC flash storage, and a 3D graphics accelerator. It costs about $60 and can be purchased from various online retailers such as eBay, Amazon, etc. 
- BeagleBone Green: A variant of BeagleBone Black that has two Grove connectors for easy connection of sensors and actuators. It also has a lower power consumption and a lower price ($70) than BeagleBone Black. It is produced by Seeed Studio and can be purchased from their website or other online retailers. 
- BeagleBone AI: A Beagle Bone product that is designed for embedded machine learning applications. It has a 1.5GHz ARM Cortex-A15 processor, 1GB of RAM, 16GB of eMMC flash storage, and a dual-core DSP and dual-core GPU for accelerated computation. It costs about $100 and can be purchased from various online retailers such as eBay, Amazon, etc. 
- PocketBeagle: A Beagle Bone product that is the smallest and cheapest ($25) in the series. It has a 1GHz ARM Cortex-A8 processor, 512MB of RAM, and a microSD card slot for storage. It has a minimal I/O interface and can be used for simple and portable projects. 

Beagle Bone products can be used for various IoT applications such as smart home, robotics, automation, gaming, etc. Some of the examples of Beagle Bone projects are:

- 3D printer: A project that uses a BeagleBone Black and a cape to control a 3D printer and monitor its status via a web interface.
- Arcade game: A project that uses a BeagleBone Black and a cape to create a retro arcade game console that can play various classic games.
- Robot: A project that uses a BeagleBone Green Wireless and a cape to create a robot that can be controlled via a smartphone app.
