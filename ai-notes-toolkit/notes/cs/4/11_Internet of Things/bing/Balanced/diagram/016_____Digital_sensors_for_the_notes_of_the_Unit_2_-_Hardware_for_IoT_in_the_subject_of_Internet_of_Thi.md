### Digital sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data in a digital format .
- Digital sensors are connected to a network and share data with the network, bridging the digital world to the physical world .
- Digital sensors can be classified based on the type of data they measure, such as temperature, pressure, motion, light, sound, etc .
- Digital sensors can also be classified based on the type of communication protocol they use, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, LoRa, etc .
- Digital sensors have various applications in IoT, such as consumer devices, industry 4.0, medical devices, smart cities, etc  .
- Digital sensors have some challenges in IoT, such as power consumption, data security, data quality, interoperability, etc .
