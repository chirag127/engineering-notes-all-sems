### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- Internet of Things (IoT) is a term that refers to the interconnection of physical objects with embedded sensors, software, and other technologies that enable them to communicate and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet or other networks .
- IoT can be seen as the integration of people, processes, and technology with connectable devices and sensors that allow remote monitoring, control, manipulation, and analysis of trends of such devices.
- IoT can be applied to various domains and sectors, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart health, smart industry, smart transportation, smart energy, and smart environment.
- IoT can provide various benefits, such as improved efficiency, productivity, convenience, safety, security, quality, and innovation.
- IoT can also pose various challenges, such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and ethics.
