### Mobiles for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- Mobiles are devices that can communicate wirelessly with other devices or networks, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, etc.
- Mobiles can act as both IoT devices and IoT applications, depending on their role and functionality in the IoT system.
- As IoT devices, mobiles can use their built-in sensors, such as GPS, camera, microphone, accelerometer, etc., to collect and transmit data to other devices or cloud platforms.
- As IoT applications, mobiles can use their graphical user interface, such as touch screen, buttons, etc., to interact with other IoT devices or cloud platforms, such as sending commands, receiving notifications, configuring settings, etc.
- Mobiles can use different wireless technologies to connect to the IoT system, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, NFC, etc.
- Mobiles can also use different protocols and standards to communicate with the IoT system, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc.
- Mobiles can benefit from the IoT system in various ways, such as enhancing user experience, improving efficiency, reducing costs, increasing security, etc.
- Mobiles can also face some challenges in the IoT system, such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, etc.

Some examples of mobile IoT applications are:

- Electric bikes tracking: Mobiles can use NB-IoT to track the location, status, and usage of shared electric bikes, which can help city councils to manage the bikes more effectively and reduce theft, accidents, and fire hazards.
- Smoke and gas detectors: Mobiles can use NB-IoT to monitor the smoke and gas levels in buildings, which can help to prevent fire and gas leaks, and alert the users and authorities in case of emergencies.
- Asset tracking: Mobiles can use LTE-M or NB-IoT to track the location and condition of various assets, such as vehicles, containers, equipment, etc., which can help to optimize the supply chain, inventory management, and predictive maintenance.
- Mobile IoT-based apps: Mobiles can use various IoT technologies to enhance the functionality and performance of different industries, such as construction, mining, manufacturing, energy, etc., by providing predictive maintenance, asset tracking, inventory management, supply chain monitoring, etc.
- Mobile IoT app development: Mobiles can use various tools and platforms to develop and deploy IoT applications, such as Azure IoT Central, IoT PnP app, etc., which can help to simplify the IoT app development process and reduce the time and cost .
