### Smart Metering

- Smart metering is the monitoring of resource consumption, such as energy, water, gas, etc., through modern metering devices connected to the Internet via IoT technology.
- Smart metering IoT devices are typically referred to as smart meters. They record and communicate energy consumption data to utilities for monitoring and other use cases, such as billing and demand response.
- Smart metering IoT devices can use various connectivity options, such as LPWAN, cellular, or Wi-Fi, to transmit data to the cloud or a central server.
- Smart metering IoT devices can also use eSIMs, which are embedded SIM cards that can be remotely provisioned and managed, to enable flexibility and interoperability across different network operators and regions.
- Smart metering IoT devices can provide in-depth analysis of resource consumption patterns, trends, and anomalies, which can help utilities and consumers optimize their usage, reduce costs, and improve efficiency.
- Smart metering IoT devices can also enable smart city applications, such as smart grid, smart water, and smart gas, which can enhance the quality and reliability of utility services, as well as the environmental and social benefits.
