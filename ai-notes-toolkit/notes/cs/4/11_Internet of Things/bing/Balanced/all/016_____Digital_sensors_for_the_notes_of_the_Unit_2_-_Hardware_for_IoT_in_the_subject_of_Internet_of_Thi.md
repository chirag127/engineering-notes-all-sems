# Digital Sensors for IoT

- Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data in a digital format .
- Digital sensors can interact directly with an IoT microcontroller, which simplifies the data processing and communication.
- Digital sensors are further differentiated based on the work they perform, such as temperature, pressure, motion, light, sound, humidity, etc .
- Digital sensors enable the Internet of Things (IoT) by collecting the data for smarter decisions and bridging the digital world to the physical world .
- Digital sensors can be used in various applications, such as consumer devices, industry 4.0, medical devices, smart home, smart city, etc  .
- Digital sensors can be integrated with other components, such as wireless modules, microcontrollers, batteries, displays, etc, to form IoT devices .
- Digital sensors can be prototyped using IoT sensor kits, such as Infineon's XENSIV™ connected sensor kit (CSK), which provides various sensors, including radar, environmental sensors, and others.
