# E-health

E-health is a term that refers to the use of electronic processes and communication technologies to support health and health-related fields. E-health can include various applications, such as:

- Telehealth: the delivery of health care services and information remotely, using devices such as computers, tablets, smartphones, etc. Telehealth can enable access to health care for people who live in rural or remote areas, or who have mobility or transportation issues. Telehealth can also reduce costs and improve efficiency and quality of care. Examples of telehealth services are telemedicine, teleconsultation, telemonitoring, tele-education, etc.
- Electronic health records (EHRs): the digital storage and exchange of health information among health care providers, patients, and other authorized parties. EHRs can improve the coordination and continuity of care, reduce errors and duplication, enhance patient safety and privacy, and facilitate research and public health surveillance. Examples of EHR systems are Epic, Cerner, Meditech, etc.
- Mobile health (m-health): the use of mobile devices and wireless technologies to support health and wellness. M-health can enable self-management of health conditions, promote healthy behaviors, provide health education and awareness, and support emergency response and disaster relief. Examples of m-health applications are fitness trackers, health apps, text messaging, etc.
- Health information systems (HIS): the collection, analysis, and dissemination of health data and information for decision making and policy development. HIS can support health system planning, management, evaluation, and improvement, as well as health research and innovation. Examples of HIS components are health indicators, health registries, health surveys, health databases, etc.
- Health informatics: the interdisciplinary field that applies information and communication technologies to health and health care. Health informatics can improve the quality, safety, efficiency, and effectiveness of health care delivery and outcomes, as well as the generation and dissemination of health knowledge. Examples of health informatics domains are clinical informatics, public health informatics, consumer health informatics, etc.
