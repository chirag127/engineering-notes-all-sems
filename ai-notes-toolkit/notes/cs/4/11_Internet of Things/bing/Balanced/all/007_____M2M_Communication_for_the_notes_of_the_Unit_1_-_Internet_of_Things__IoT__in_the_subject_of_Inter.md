# M2M Communication

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine. It refers to the automated exchange of data between machines, devices, or systems without human intervention.
- M2M is a broad concept that encompasses many different technologies, such as wireless sensor networks, RFID, cellular, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, ZigBee, etc.
- M2M is the foundation for IoT, which is a specific subset of M2M that connects smart devices over IP-based networks and enables data analysis, visualization, and integration with other systems.
- M2M communication has many applications in various domains, such as smart grid, smart home, smart city, industrial automation, healthcare, transportation, agriculture, etc.
- M2M communication can provide benefits such as improved efficiency, reduced costs, enhanced safety, better customer service, and new business opportunities.
