# IoT Examples

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that can collect, process, exchange and utilize data interrelatedly, via the internet or other communications networks. IoT has many different applications and benefits for various domains and industries. Here are some examples of IoT devices and systems that are commonly used or being developed:

- **Connected cars**: These are vehicles that can communicate with other vehicles, infrastructure, cloud services and drivers through sensors, cameras, GPS, onboard computers and wireless technologies. Connected cars can provide features such as navigation, entertainment, safety, diagnostics, maintenance and remote control .
- **Smart appliances**: These are household devices that can be controlled and monitored remotely via smartphones, tablets or voice assistants. Smart appliances can provide features such as energy efficiency, convenience, security and automation  . Examples of smart appliances include refrigerators, ovens, washers, dryers, lights, thermostats and cameras.
- **Connected security systems**: These are systems that can detect and prevent intrusions, fire, smoke, gas leaks and other hazards in homes and buildings. Connected security systems can provide features such as real-time alerts, video surveillance, remote access, emergency response and automation  . Examples of connected security systems include alarms, locks, sensors, cameras and smoke detectors.
- **Smart agriculture equipment**: These are devices that can monitor and optimize the growth, health and yield of crops and livestock. Smart agriculture equipment can provide features such as precision farming, irrigation, pest control, animal tracking and automation . Examples of smart agriculture equipment include drones, sensors, cameras, RFID tags and sprinklers.
- **Connected retail**: This is the use of IoT devices and systems to enhance the shopping experience and operations of retailers and customers. Connected retail can provide features such as inventory management, product recommendation, payment, loyalty, customer service and analytics . Examples of connected retail include smart shelves, beacons, digital signage, smart carts and smart mirrors.
- **Connected healthcare monitors**: These are devices that can measure and track the vital signs, health conditions and activities of patients and users. Connected healthcare monitors can provide features such as remote diagnosis, treatment, prevention, wellness and fitness . Examples of connected healthcare monitors include wearable devices, implantable devices, smart patches and smart pills.
- **Connected manufacturing equipment**: These are machines and tools that can perform and optimize the production, quality and maintenance of goods and services. Connected manufacturing equipment can provide features such as predictive maintenance, process control, asset management and automation  . Examples of connected manufacturing equipment include robots, sensors, cameras, RFID tags and 3D printers.
- **Connected cities**: These are urban areas that can use IoT devices and systems to improve the efficiency, sustainability and livability of their infrastructure and services. Connected cities can provide features such as traffic management, waste management, environmental monitoring, public safety and smart lighting . Examples of connected cities include smart parking, smart meters, smart bins, smart streetlights and smart CCTV.
