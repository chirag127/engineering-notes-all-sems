### Tablets for the notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the subject of Internet of Things

- IoT applications are the use cases of IoT devices and systems that provide value and benefits to various domains and industries.
- Tablets are portable computing devices that have touchscreens and wireless connectivity, and can run various software applications.
- Tablets can be used for IoT applications in different ways, such as:
  - As a user interface to control and monitor IoT devices and systems, such as smart home appliances, industrial machines, or medical equipment.
  - As a data collection and analysis tool to gather and process information from IoT sensors and devices, such as environmental conditions, health parameters, or asset tracking.
  - As a communication and collaboration device to share and exchange data and insights with other users and stakeholders, such as customers, suppliers, or managers.
- Some examples of IoT applications that use tablets are:
  - Smart pill dispensers that use IoT-enabled tablets to remind patients to take their medications, track their adherence, and send alerts to caregivers.
  - IoT Communicator that uses tablets and smartphones to securely transfer process data from IoT devices to the cloud, and display them in a clear overview.
  - IoT Managed Tablet that uses tablets with managed IoT cellular connectivity to provide controlled access to specific business applications, such as inventory management, field service, or point of sale.
  - Rugged tablets that use IoT connectivity to withstand harsh environments and support various applications, such as military, construction, or mining.
