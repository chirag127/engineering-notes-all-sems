### Smart Metering

- Smart metering is the monitoring of resource consumption, such as energy, water, gas, etc., through modern metering devices connected to the Internet via IoT technology.
- Smart metering devices, also known as smart meters, can record and communicate energy consumption data to utilities for monitoring and other use cases, such as billing and demand response.
- Smart metering can provide benefits for both utility companies and their consumers, such as:
  - Improved accuracy and efficiency of meter reading and billing.
  - Reduced operational and maintenance costs for utilities.
  - Enhanced customer service and satisfaction.
  - Increased awareness and control of energy usage and costs for consumers.
  - Enabled integration of renewable energy sources and smart grid applications  .
- Smart metering can be implemented using various IoT technologies, such as:
  - Low Power Wide Area Network (LPWAN) connectivity, which can provide long-range, low-power, and low-cost wireless communication for smart meters.
  - Embedded SIM (eSIM) cards, which can provide flexible and secure cellular connectivity for smart meters, allowing them to switch between different network operators and regions.
  - IoT platforms, which can provide data collection, processing, visualization, and analytics for smart metering applications, allowing utilities and consumers to access and manage the metering data in real-time  .
  - IoT dashboards, which can provide graphical and interactive displays of smart metering data, allowing users to monitor and control their energy consumption and costs .
