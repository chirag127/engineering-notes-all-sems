### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- IoT/M2M systems are composed of interconnected devices, applications, and services that communicate and exchange data over the Internet or other networks.
- IoT/M2M systems can be divided into three major domains: the application layer, the service layer, and the network layer .
- The application layer is the domain where the end-user applications and services reside, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, etc. The application layer interacts with the service layer through standardized interfaces and protocols.
- The service layer is the domain where the common functions and capabilities of IoT/M2M systems are provided, such as device management, data management, security, discovery, etc. The service layer abstracts the heterogeneity and complexity of the network layer and provides a unified platform for the application layer.
- The network layer is the domain where the physical and logical connectivity of IoT/M2M devices and systems are established, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc. The network layer enables the data transmission and routing among the devices and systems.
- IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization aim to provide interoperability, scalability, security, and reliability for the IoT/M2M systems across different domains, platforms, and technologies.
- Some of the prominent organizations and initiatives that are involved in IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization are:
  - oneM2M: a global partnership project that develops technical specifications and standards for the service layer of IoT/M2M systems .
  - ETSI: a European standards organization that develops standards for the radio layer and the service layer of IoT/M2M systems, such as LTE-M, NB-IoT, EC-GSM-IoT, and Smart M2M.
  - OGC: an international industry consortium that develops standards for the sensors and geospatial information in IoT/M2M systems, such as Sensor Web Enablement and SensorThings API.
  - NIST: a US federal agency that develops standards and guidelines for the cyber-physical systems and the Internet of Things, such as the CPS Framework and the IoT-Enabled Smart City Framework.
