### Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- A conceptual framework is a set of ideas, principles, and assumptions that guide the design, development, and implementation of a system or a solution.
- A conceptual framework for IoT aims to provide a common understanding of the key concepts, components, and relationships of IoT systems and applications.
- A conceptual framework for IoT can help to address the challenges and opportunities of IoT, such as heterogeneity, interoperability, scalability, security, privacy, and value creation.
- A conceptual framework for IoT can also help to identify the research gaps and directions for future work in IoT.
- There are different conceptual frameworks for IoT proposed by various researchers and organizations, depending on the perspective, scope, and objective of the framework.
- Some examples of conceptual frameworks for IoT are:

  - The four-layer conceptual framework for context-aware networks by  , which consists of physical entities layer, context service layer, context management layer, and context application layer. This framework aims to achieve dynamic and personalized services based on the context information of the physical entities and the users.
  - The resource-based view conceptual framework for IoT impact on supply chain management by , which consists of IoT resources, internal supply chain capabilities, external supply chain challenges, and supply chain competitiveness. This framework aims to understand how IoT resources can enhance the supply chain capabilities and performance in the face of external challenges.
  - The theoretical framework and conceptual model for IoT adoption and implementation by , which consists of IoT characteristics, IoT adoption factors, IoT implementation factors, and IoT outcomes. This framework aims to explain the factors that influence the adoption and implementation of IoT and the outcomes that result from it.
