### Designing of smart street lights in smart city

- Smart street lights are LED luminaires that can be remotely controlled, monitored, and adjusted via a wireless network.
- Smart street lights can provide energy savings, reduce maintenance costs, improve public safety, and enable other smart city applications .
- The design of smart street lights involves the following steps:
  - Define the lighting requirements and standards for different road types, locations, and scenarios.
  - Select the appropriate LED luminaires, sensors, and controllers that meet the performance, quality, and reliability criteria.
  - Install the smart street lights and connect them to a wireless communication network, such as LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, or Wi-Fi.
  - Configure the smart street lights to operate according to the desired schedules, dimming levels, and triggers.
  - Monitor and manage the smart street lights via a cloud-based software platform that provides real-time data, analytics, and alerts.
  - Maintain and upgrade the smart street lights as needed, using remote diagnostics and over-the-air updates.
- The design of smart street lights should also consider the following aspects :
  - Integrate the smart street lights with other smart city systems, such as traffic management, environmental monitoring, and emergency response.
  - Involve the stakeholders, such as municipalities, utilities, citizens, and private partners, in the planning, implementation, and evaluation of the smart street lighting project.
  - Ensure the security, privacy, and interoperability of the smart street lighting network and data.
  - Evaluate the environmental, social, and economic impacts and benefits of the smart street lighting project.
