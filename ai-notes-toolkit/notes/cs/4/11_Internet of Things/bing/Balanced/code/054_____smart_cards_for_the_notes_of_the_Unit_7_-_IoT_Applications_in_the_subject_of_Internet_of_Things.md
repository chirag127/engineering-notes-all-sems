### Smart Cards for the Notes of the Unit 7 - IoT Applications in the Subject of Internet of Things

- A smart card is a physical device that contains a microprocessor or a microcontroller that can store and process data securely .
- Smart cards can be used for various applications in IoT, such as authentication, encryption, key management, payment, access control, and identity management .
- Some examples of smart card applications in IoT are:
  - Smart watches, fitness trackers, and healthcare monitors that use IoT SIM cards to connect to cellular networks and exchange data with cloud services.
  - Smart meters, smart locks, and smart home devices that use smart cards to authenticate users and devices, and to encrypt and decrypt data transmissions .
  - Smart transportation systems, smart parking, and smart city services that use smart cards to enable payment, ticketing, and access control.
- Smart cards can provide several benefits for IoT applications, such as:
  - Enhanced security and privacy by preventing unauthorized access, tampering, and data breaches .
  - Improved convenience and user experience by enabling contactless, mobile, and online transactions .
  - Reduced costs and complexity by simplifying the deployment, management, and maintenance of IoT devices and networks .
