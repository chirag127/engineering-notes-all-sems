### Conceptual Framework for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- A conceptual framework is a set of ideas, principles, and assumptions that guide the design and implementation of a system or a solution.
- A conceptual framework for IoT aims to provide a common understanding of the key concepts, components, and relationships of IoT systems and applications.
- A conceptual framework for IoT can help to address the challenges and opportunities of IoT, such as heterogeneity, scalability, interoperability, security, privacy, and value creation.
- A conceptual framework for IoT can also help to identify the gaps and needs for further research and development in the IoT domain.
- There are different conceptual frameworks for IoT proposed by various researchers and organizations, depending on their perspectives and objectives.
- Some examples of conceptual frameworks for IoT are:

  - The four-layer conceptual framework for context-aware networks by  , which consists of physical layer, virtualization layer, context service layer, and application layer. This framework aims to achieve dynamic and personalized services based on context information from the physical entities and the environment.
  - The resource-based view conceptual framework for IoT impact on supply chain management by , which argues that IoT resources (such as sensors, actuators, data, and analytics) empower internal supply chain capabilities (such as visibility, agility, and collaboration) to address external challenges (such as uncertainty, complexity, and competition) and enhance supply chain competitiveness.
  - The theoretical framework and conceptual model for IoT adoption and implementation by , which integrates the technology-organization-environment framework, the unified theory of acceptance and use of technology, and the technology readiness and acceptance model. This framework aims to explain the factors that influence the adoption and implementation of IoT solutions in different contexts and sectors.

- A conceptual framework for IoT can be useful for students, researchers, practitioners, and policymakers who are interested in learning and developing IoT systems and applications.
