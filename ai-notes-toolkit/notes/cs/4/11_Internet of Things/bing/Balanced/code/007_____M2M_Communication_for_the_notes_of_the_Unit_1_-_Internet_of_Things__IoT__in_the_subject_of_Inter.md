### M2M Communication for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT) in the subject of Internet of Things

- M2M stands for Machine to Machine communication, which is the exchange of data between two or more devices without human intervention.
- M2M is a key component of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software and connectivity that enable them to collect, process and share data.
- M2M communication can be achieved through various methods, such as serial connection, powerline connection, or wireless communication.
- M2M communication has many applications in different domains, such as smart grid, smart home, smart city, industrial automation, healthcare, transportation, agriculture, etc.
- M2M communication can provide benefits such as improved efficiency, reduced costs, enhanced customer service, increased safety, and better decision making.
- M2M communication also faces some challenges, such as interoperability, security, privacy, scalability, and reliability.
