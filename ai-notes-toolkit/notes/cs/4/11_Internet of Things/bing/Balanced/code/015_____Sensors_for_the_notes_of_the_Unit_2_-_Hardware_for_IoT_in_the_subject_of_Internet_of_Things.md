### Sensors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT in the subject of Internet of Things

- Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, motion, light, sound, etc., and convert them into electrical signals.
- Sensors are essential components of IoT systems, as they enable the collection of data from the physical world and the communication of this data to the cloud or other devices via wireless networks.
- Sensors can be classified into different types based on their function, output, principle, or application. Some of the common types of sensors for IoT are:

  - Temperature sensors: These sensors measure the amount of heat generated from an area or an object. They are widely used in IoT applications such as smart home, smart agriculture, smart health, etc. Some examples of temperature sensors are thermocouples, thermistors, and infrared sensors.
  - Proximity sensors: These sensors detect the presence or absence of objects near the sensor without physical contact. They are useful for IoT applications such as security, automation, robotics, etc. Some examples of proximity sensors are ultrasonic sensors, capacitive sensors, and magnetic sensors.
  - Pressure sensors: These sensors detect changes in a gas or liquid. They are used for IoT applications such as weather monitoring, industrial control, automotive, etc. Some examples of pressure sensors are piezoelectric sensors, strain gauges, and optical sensors.
  - Water quality sensors: These sensors measure parameters such as pH, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, conductivity, etc., of water. They are used for IoT applications such as environmental monitoring, water management, agriculture, etc. Some examples of water quality sensors are electrochemical sensors, optical sensors, and biosensors.
  - Chemical and gas sensors: These sensors measure the concentration of specific chemicals or gases in the air or liquid. They are used for IoT applications such as air quality monitoring, industrial safety, health care, etc. Some examples of chemical and gas sensors are metal oxide sensors, electrochemical sensors, and optical sensors.
  - Infrared sensors: These sensors detect infrared radiation emitted or reflected by objects. They are used for IoT applications such as remote sensing, thermal imaging, motion detection, etc. Some examples of infrared sensors are pyroelectric sensors, thermopiles, and bolometers.
  - Smoke sensors: These sensors detect the presence of smoke or fire. They are used for IoT applications such as fire alarm, security, etc. Some examples of smoke sensors are ionization sensors, photoelectric sensors, and thermal sensors.
  - Motion sensors: These sensors detect the movement or position of objects or people. They are used for IoT applications such as smart home, security, gaming, etc. Some examples of motion sensors are accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers.
  - Light sensors: These sensors measure the intensity or color of light. They are used for IoT applications such as smart lighting, display, imaging, etc. Some examples of light sensors are photodiodes, phototransistors, and color sensors.
  - Sound sensors: These sensors measure the amplitude or frequency of sound waves. They are used for IoT applications such as voice recognition, noise detection, music, etc. Some examples of sound sensors are microphones, piezoelectric sensors, and ultrasonic sensors.
  - Humidity sensors: These sensors measure the amount of water vapor in the air or other gases. They are used for IoT applications such as weather monitoring, agriculture, health care, etc. Some examples of humidity sensors are capacitive sensors, resistive sensors, and thermal sensors.
  - Biomedical sensors: These sensors measure physiological parameters such as heart rate, blood pressure, glucose level, etc., of humans or animals. They are used for IoT applications such as health care, fitness, wellness, etc. Some examples of biomedical sensors are electrocardiogram sensors, blood pressure sensors, and glucose sensors.
  - Accelerometers: These sensors measure acceleration, which is the change in speed, direction, and intensity of movement. They are used for IoT applications such as navigation, gesture recognition, vibration detection, etc. Some examples of accelerometers are piezoelectric sensors, capacitive sensors, and MEMS sensors.
