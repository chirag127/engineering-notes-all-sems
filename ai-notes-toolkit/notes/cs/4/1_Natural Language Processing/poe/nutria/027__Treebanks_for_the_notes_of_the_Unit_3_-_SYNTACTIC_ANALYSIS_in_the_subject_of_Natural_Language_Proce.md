
### Treebanks for the Notes of the Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Treebanks are collections of annotated sentences in a given language that are used to train and evaluate natural language processing systems.
* Syntactic analysis is the process of analyzing a sentence to determine its grammatical structure and meaning.
* Treebanks provide a large amount of annotated data which is essential for training and evaluating natural language processing systems.
* Treebanks are usually manually annotated with part-of-speech tags, syntactic labels, and other linguistic features.
* Treebanks can be used to develop models for syntactic analysis, such as dependency parsers, constituency parsers, and phrase structure parsers.
* Treebanks can also be used to develop models for semantic analysis, such as semantic role labeling and semantic parsing.
* Treebanks are also used to develop models for natural language generation and natural language understanding.
