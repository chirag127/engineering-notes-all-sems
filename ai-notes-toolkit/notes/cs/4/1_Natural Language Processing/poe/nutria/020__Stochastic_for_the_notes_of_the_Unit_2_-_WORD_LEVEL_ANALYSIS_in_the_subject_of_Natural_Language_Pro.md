
### Stochastic for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Stochastic methods are a type of algorithms used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to analyze text data. 
* These methods are used to identify patterns in text and to make predictions about the content of the text. 
* Stochastic methods are based on the idea of probability and randomness. 
* They make use of statistical techniques such as Maximum Likelihood Estimation and Bayesian Inference to make predictions about the content of the text. 
* Stochastic methods are used in a variety of tasks such as part-of-speech tagging, sentiment analysis, and text categorization. 
* Stochastic methods are also used to generate text by using Markov models. 
* These methods are efficient and can be used to analyze large amounts of data.
