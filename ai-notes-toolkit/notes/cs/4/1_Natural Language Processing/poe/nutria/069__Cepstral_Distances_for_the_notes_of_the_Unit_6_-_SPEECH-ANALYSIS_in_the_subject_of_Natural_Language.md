
### Cepstral Distances for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Cepstral Distances are a measure of similarity between two speech signals. They are used in Natural Language Processing to compare the content of two different speech signals.

* Cepstral Distances are calculated by taking a Fourier transform of the speech signal and then computing the distance between the resulting spectra. 
* Cepstral Distances are used to compare the content of two different speech signals. 
* Cepstral Distances are used in speech recognition and text-to-speech systems to measure the similarity between two signals. 
* Cepstral Distances can also be used to measure the amount of distortion in a signal, or to detect the presence of noise in a signal.
