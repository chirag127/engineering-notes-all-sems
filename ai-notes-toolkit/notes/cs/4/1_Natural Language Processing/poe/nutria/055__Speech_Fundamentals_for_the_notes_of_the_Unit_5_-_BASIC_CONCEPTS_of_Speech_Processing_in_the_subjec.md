
### Speech Fundamentals for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Speech processing is the study of how to analyze, modify and synthesize speech.
- Speech recognition is the process of automatically recognizing spoken words.
- Speech synthesis is the process of generating speech from text.
- Acoustic modeling is the process of building a model of the sound of a language.
- Language modeling is the process of building a model of the structure of a language.
- Text-to-speech (TTS) is the process of generating speech from text.
- Automatic speech recognition (ASR) is the process of recognizing spoken words.
- Natural language processing (NLP) is the process of analyzing and understanding natural language.
- Voice recognition is the process of recognizing a speaker's identity.
- Voice authentication is the process of verifying a speaker's identity.
