
### Interpolation and Backoff for the Notes of Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in Natural Language Processing

* Interpolation is a technique used to estimate the probability of a word occurring in a given context. It is based on the assumption that the probability of a word occurring in a given context is related to its probability of occurring in similar contexts. 

* Backoff is a technique used to reduce the probability of a word occurring in a given context. It is based on the assumption that the probability of a word occurring in a given context is related to its probability of occurring in less similar contexts.

* Interpolation and backoff are used together in language models to improve the accuracy of the language model. Interpolation is used to increase the probability of words occurring in more similar contexts, while backoff is used to decrease the probability of words occurring in less similar contexts.

* Interpolation and backoff can also be used to improve the accuracy of a language model by smoothing out the probability distribution of words. This is done by assigning a higher probability to words that occur more often in the training corpus, and a lower probability to words that occur less often.
