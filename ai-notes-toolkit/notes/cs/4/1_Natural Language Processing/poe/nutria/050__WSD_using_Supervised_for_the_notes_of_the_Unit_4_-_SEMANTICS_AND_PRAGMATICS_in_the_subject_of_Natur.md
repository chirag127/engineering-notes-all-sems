
### WSD using Supervised for the Notes of Unit 4 - Semantics and Pragmatics in Natural Language Processing

* Word Sense Disambiguation (WSD) is the process of determining the meaning of a word in a given context. It is used to resolve ambiguity in natural language processing tasks, such as machine translation, text summarization and question answering.
* Supervised WSD is a method of WSD that uses manually labeled training data to build a model for disambiguating words in a text. The model is then used to classify words in unseen texts.
* Semantics is the study of the meaning of words and phrases in a language. It is concerned with how words and phrases are used to convey meaning and how they are related to each other.
* Pragmatics is the study of how words and phrases are used in context. It is concerned with the social and cultural aspects of language use, such as how speakers use language to express their intentions, convey information, and influence the behavior of others.
* Natural Language Processing (NLP) is an interdisciplinary field that combines linguistics, computer science, and artificial intelligence to study how computers can understand and generate natural language. It is used in applications such as chatbots, machine translation, text summarization, and question answering.
