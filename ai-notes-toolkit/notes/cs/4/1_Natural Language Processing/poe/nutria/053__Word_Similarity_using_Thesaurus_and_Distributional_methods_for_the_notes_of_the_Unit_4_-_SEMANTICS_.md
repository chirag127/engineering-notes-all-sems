
### Word Similarity using Thesaurus and Distributional methods for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Word similarity is a key component of natural language processing (NLP) and is used to measure the degree of similarity between two words. 
2. Thesaurus-based methods are commonly used to measure word similarity. These methods rely on the relationships between words in a thesaurus or dictionary to measure the similarity between words. 
3. Distributional methods use the context in which words appear to measure the similarity between words. These methods use the co-occurrence of words in a corpus to measure the similarity between words. 
4. Semantics and pragmatics are two important aspects of language that are used to understand how words are used in a specific context. 
5. Semantics is the study of the meaning of words and how they are used in a language. Pragmatics is the study of how language is used in a particular context. 
6. Word similarity can be used to help determine the meaning of a word in a specific context. This can be used to improve the accuracy of natural language processing applications.
