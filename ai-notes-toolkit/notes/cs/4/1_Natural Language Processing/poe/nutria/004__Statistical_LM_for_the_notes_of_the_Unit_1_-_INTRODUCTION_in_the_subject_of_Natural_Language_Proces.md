
### Statistical Language Models for Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in Natural Language Processing

1. A Statistical Language Model (SLM) is a probabilistic model which assigns a probability to a sequence of words. 
2. SLMs are used to measure the likelihood of a given sentence or phrase, and can be used for a variety of tasks such as machine translation, speech recognition, and text summarization.
3. SLMs are based on the assumption that the probability of a word is dependent on the words that came before it. 
4. The most common type of SLM is the n-gram model, which looks at the n-1 words that came before a given word to calculate the probability of that word. 
5. Other types of SLMs include neural network models, which use neural networks to calculate the probability of a word, and recursive models, which look at the context of a sentence to calculate the probability of a word. 
6. SLMs can be used to generate sentences, as well as to identify mistakes in sentences. 
7. SLMs are an important tool in Natural Language Processing and are used in many applications.
