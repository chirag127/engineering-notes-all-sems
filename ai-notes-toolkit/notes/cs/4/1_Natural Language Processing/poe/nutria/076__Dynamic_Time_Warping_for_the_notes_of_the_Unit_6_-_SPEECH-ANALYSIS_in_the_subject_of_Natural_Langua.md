
### Dynamic Time Warping for the Notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Dynamic Time Warping (DTW) is an algorithm used to compare two sequences that may vary in time or speed.
* DTW is used in speech recognition, handwriting recognition, and music recognition.
* It works by finding the optimal alignment between two sequences by minimizing the accumulated cost of the differences between them.
* The goal of DTW is to find the minimum cost path that aligns two sequences.
* DTW is a non-linear approach, meaning that it takes into account the temporal variations between two sequences.
* It is an efficient algorithm that can be used to compare two sequences of different lengths.
* DTW can be used to measure the similarity between two sequences by calculating the distance between them.
* DTW can be used to cluster similar sequences together and detect outliers.
* DTW can be used to detect patterns in time series data.
* DTW can be used to find the optimal alignment between two sequences, such as in speech recognition.
