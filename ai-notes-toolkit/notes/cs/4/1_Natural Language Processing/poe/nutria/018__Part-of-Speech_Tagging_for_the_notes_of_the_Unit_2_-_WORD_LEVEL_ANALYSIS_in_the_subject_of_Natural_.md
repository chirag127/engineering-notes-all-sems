
### Part-of-Speech Tagging

Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging is a process of assigning a part-of-speech label to each word in a sentence. It is used to identify the syntactic or morphological category of a word in a sentence. The most common categories are nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, interjections, and determiners. 

POS tagging can be used to identify the subject and object of a sentence, as well as to identify the role of each word in a sentence. It can also be used to identify the root form of words, such as verbs, nouns, and adjectives.

POS tagging is an important part of natural language processing (NLP), as it allows for more accurate analysis of the structure of a sentence. It is also used in machine translation, information retrieval, and text summarization.
