
### Review Of Digital Signal Processing Concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Digital signal processing (DSP) is the manipulation of signals in the digital domain. It is a branch of mathematics and engineering that deals with the analysis, synthesis, and manipulation of signals.

2. DSP is used in a wide variety of applications, including audio and video processing, speech and language processing, image processing, radar and sonar signal processing, communications, and control systems.

3. DSP algorithms can be implemented in hardware or software, or a combination of both. In hardware, DSP algorithms are implemented as custom integrated circuits or programmable logic devices. In software, DSP algorithms are implemented as computer programs.

4. DSP algorithms are typically designed to perform specific tasks, such as filtering, correlation, convolution, modulation, and other signal-processing operations.

5. DSP algorithms can be used to process speech signals, such as for speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speech coding.

6. DSP algorithms can also be used to process non-speech signals, such as for audio compression, audio equalization, and audio effects.
