
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is a way of introducing a topic or subject to an audience. It is typically the first part of a presentation, lecture, or discussion. It is important to provide an introduction that is engaging and informative in order to capture the attention of the audience. 
2. An effective introduction should provide an overview of the topic, explain why the topic is important, and provide any necessary background information. It should also provide an outline of what will be discussed in the body of the presentation. 
3. Introductions can take many forms, such as a story, a joke, or a quote. It is important to choose an introduction that is appropriate for the audience and the topic. 
4. Introductions are important for setting the tone for the rest of the presentation. They should be concise and to the point in order to capture the attention of the audience and provide an effective transition into the body of the presentation.
