
### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the Notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* **Dictionary:** A dictionary is a collection of words and their definitions. It provides a comprehensive list of words and their meanings, as well as information about grammar, usage, and pronunciation. 

* **Thesaurus:** A thesaurus is a collection of words and their synonyms. It provides a comprehensive list of words and their synonyms, as well as information about their usage and context.

* **Semantics:** Semantics is the study of the meaning of words, phrases, and sentences. It focuses on how words are used to convey meaning in different contexts.

* **Pragmatics:** Pragmatics is the study of how language is used in a social and cultural context. It focuses on how language is used to communicate and how it is interpreted by others.
