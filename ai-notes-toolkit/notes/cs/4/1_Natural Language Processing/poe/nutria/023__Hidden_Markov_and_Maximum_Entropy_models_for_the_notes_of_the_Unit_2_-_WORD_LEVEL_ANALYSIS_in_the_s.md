
### Hidden Markov and Maximum Entropy models for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

* Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are probabilistic models used to model sequential data. They are used in many different tasks such as speech recognition, language modeling and machine translation.

* Maximum Entropy models (MaxEnt) are probabilistic models used to model the probability distribution of a given set of data. They are used in many different tasks such as text classification, sentiment analysis and machine translation.

* HMMs and MaxEnt models can be used for word-level analysis in natural language processing. HMMs can be used to model the probability of a word given its context, while MaxEnt models can be used to model the probability of a word given its context and any other features of the text.

* HMMs are based on the assumption that the probability of a word depends only on its context, while MaxEnt models allow for the inclusion of additional features, such as the part-of-speech of the word or the length of the sentence.

* HMMs and MaxEnt models can be used to improve the accuracy of natural language processing tasks such as text classification and sentiment analysis. They can also be used to improve the accuracy of machine translation systems.
