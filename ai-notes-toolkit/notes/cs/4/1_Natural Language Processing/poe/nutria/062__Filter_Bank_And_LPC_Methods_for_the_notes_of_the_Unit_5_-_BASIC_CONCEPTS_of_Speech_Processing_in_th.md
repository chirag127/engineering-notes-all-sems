
### Filter Bank and LPC Methods 

* Filter Bank methods involve decomposing a signal into different frequency bands. This is done by passing the signal through a bank of filters. Each filter is designed to pass a specific range of frequencies and block other frequencies. 

* Linear Predictive Coding (LPC) is a method of representing a signal by a set of linear equations. It is used to predict the short-term behaviour of a signal by analyzing the signal's past values. LPC is used in speech processing to model the vocal tract of a speaker. 

* Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs) are a set of coefficients derived from the filter bank output. They are used to represent the spectral characteristics of a signal. They are used in speech recognition systems to represent the spectral characteristics of a speaker's voice.
