
### Implementation Issues for Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in Natural Language Processing
1. One of the main challenges in speech modeling is the lack of labeled data. Speech data is often not labeled and must be manually labeled, which is a time-consuming and expensive process.
2. Another challenge is the variability of speech. Speech can vary in terms of accent, intonation, and speed, which can make it difficult to accurately model.
3. Speech recognition systems can be vulnerable to background noise, which can interfere with the accuracy of the model.
4. Speech recognition systems can also be vulnerable to language variation, as different languages have different rules and conventions.
5. Speech recognition models can be sensitive to the speaker's identity, as different speakers have different vocal characteristics.
6. Finally, speech recognition systems can be slow to respond, as they must process a large amount of data before providing a response.
