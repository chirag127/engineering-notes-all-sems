
### Thematic Roles for the Notes of Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS

1. Thematic roles are the semantic roles that describe the relationships between a verb and its arguments.
2. Thematic roles are used to describe how a sentence is structured semantically.
3. The most common thematic roles are Agent (doer of the action), Patient (receiver of the action), Stimulus (cause of the action), and Theme (object of the action).
4. Thematic roles can also be used to describe the relationships between two nouns, such as in a possessive relationship.
5. Thematic roles can be used to infer the meaning of a sentence and to understand the context of a conversation.
6. Pragmatics is the study of how language is used in context, and how it is interpreted by the listener.
7. Pragmatics looks at how language is used to convey meaning and how language is used to create and maintain relationships.
8. Pragmatics also looks at how language is used to express emotions and how language is used to influence the listener.
9. Pragmatics is important for Natural Language Processing because it helps machines understand the context of a conversation and the underlying meaning of a sentence.
