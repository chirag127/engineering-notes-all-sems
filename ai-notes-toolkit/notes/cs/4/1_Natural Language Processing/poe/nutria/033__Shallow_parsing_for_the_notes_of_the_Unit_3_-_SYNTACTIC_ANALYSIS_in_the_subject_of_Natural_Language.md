
### Shallow Parsing for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Shallow parsing, also known as light parsing or chunking, is a procedure used in natural language processing to analyze the structure of a sentence. 
* It is a form of syntactic analysis that breaks down a sentence into its component parts, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, etc. 
* Shallow parsing is used to identify and extract the main components of a sentence, such as the subject, verb, and object. 
* It is different from deep parsing, which is a more complex process that attempts to identify the underlying grammatical structure of a sentence. 
* Shallow parsing is often used in natural language processing applications such as question answering, machine translation, and text summarization. 
* It is also used in information extraction, where it is used to identify and extract specific pieces of information from a sentence. 
* Shallow parsing can be implemented using various techniques, such as rule-based or statistical methods. 
* Rule-based methods rely on a set of predefined rules to identify the components of a sentence, while statistical methods use machine learning algorithms to identify the components of a sentence.
