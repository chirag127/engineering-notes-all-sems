

## Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing

1. Speech Processing: Speech processing is the study and practice of converting spoken language into a digital representation. It involves the analysis, synthesis, and manipulation of sound signals.

2. Speech Recognition: Speech recognition is the process of recognizing spoken language by a computer. It is used to convert spoken language into text, and is used in a variety of applications such as voice-activated commands, dictation, and automated customer service.

3. Speech Synthesis: Speech synthesis is the process of generating speech from a text or a set of parameters. It is used to generate a computer-generated voice that can be used for a variety of purposes, such as text-to-speech applications and voice-activated commands.

4. Speech Analysis: Speech analysis is the process of analyzing the sound signals of a spoken language. It involves the use of algorithms to detect patterns in the sound signals, and is used in applications such as voice recognition and speech synthesis.

5. Speech Coding: Speech coding is the process of compressing speech signals for efficient transmission and storage. It is used to reduce the size of audio files, and is used in applications such as digital audio broadcasting and voice over IP.
