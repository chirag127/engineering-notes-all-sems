
### Hidden Markov Models for the Notes of the Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are probabilistic models used to model temporal and sequential data.
* HMMs are used in a variety of applications, including speech recognition, speech synthesis, and natural language processing.
* HMMs are composed of two components: an underlying Markov chain and a set of hidden states.
* The Markov chain is used to model the probability of transitioning from one state to another.
* The hidden states are used to model the probability of observing a particular observation at each state.
* HMMs are trained using the Baum-Welch algorithm, which uses the forward-backward algorithm to estimate the parameters of the model.
* HMMs can be used to generate sequences of observations, such as speech or text, by sampling from the underlying Markov chain.
