
### Word Senses

* Word senses refer to the different meanings of a word in a language. 
* A single word can have multiple meanings, and each of these meanings can be referred to as a distinct word sense.
* Word senses are typically organized into a hierarchy, with each level representing a more specific meaning of the word. 
* For example, the word "bank" can refer to a financial institution, the edge of a river, or a large pile of something. 
* Word senses can be identified through the use of semantic analysis, which is a process of analyzing the context in which a word is used to determine its meaning.
* Semantic analysis is an important part of Natural Language Processing (NLP), as it helps computers to understand the meaning of words and sentences in natural language. 
* Semantics and pragmatics are two closely related fields of linguistics that study the meaning of words and sentences in natural language. 
* Semantics focuses on the denotative meaning of words, while pragmatics focuses on the connotative meaning of words. 
* Pragmatics also studies how language is used in context, and how context affects the meaning of words and sentences.
