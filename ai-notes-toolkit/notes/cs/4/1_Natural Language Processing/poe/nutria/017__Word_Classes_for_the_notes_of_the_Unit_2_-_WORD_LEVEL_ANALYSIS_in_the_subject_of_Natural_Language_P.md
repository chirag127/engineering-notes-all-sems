
### Word Classes for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Word Level Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Nouns: Nouns are words that refer to people, places, things, ideas, or qualities. Examples of nouns include: car, apple, happiness, freedom.

2. Verbs: Verbs are words that describe an action or state of being. Examples of verbs include: run, think, be.

3. Adjectives: Adjectives are words that describe or modify nouns or pronouns. Examples of adjectives include: big, green, happy.

4. Adverbs: Adverbs are words that modify or describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Examples of adverbs include: quickly, very, often.

5. Prepositions: Prepositions are words that link nouns, pronouns, and phrases to other words in a sentence. Examples of prepositions include: in, on, over.

6. Conjunctions: Conjunctions are words that join together words, phrases, or clauses. Examples of conjunctions include: and, or, but.

7. Interjections: Interjections are words that express emotion or surprise. Examples of interjections include: oh, wow, yikes.
