
### Time Alignment And Normalization for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Time alignment is the process of synchronizing audio signals. It is used to align speech signals with each other for analysis purposes. 
- Normalization is the process of adjusting the amplitude of a signal to a specific level. This is done to make sure that different audio signals have similar amplitude levels. 
- Time alignment and normalization are important steps in speech analysis. They ensure that the audio signals are properly aligned and normalized before they are analyzed. 
- Time alignment is done by using a time-domain algorithm to find the best alignment between two signals. The algorithm looks for the best match between two signals and then adjusts the alignment accordingly. 
- Normalization is done by adjusting the amplitude of the signal to a specific level. This is done to make sure that all signals have the same amplitude level. 
- Time alignment and normalization are important steps in speech analysis. They ensure that the audio signals are properly aligned and normalized before they are analyzed. This helps to improve the accuracy of the speech analysis.
