
### Optimal State Sequence for the Notes of Unit 7 - Speech Modeling in Natural Language Processing

1. Speech modeling is the process of mapping speech signals to linguistic units, such as words, syllables, and phonemes.
2. Optimal state sequence (OSS) is a form of speech modeling that uses hidden Markov models (HMMs) to represent the probability of a sequence of speech units.
3. HMMs are composed of a set of states, each of which is associated with a probability distribution over the set of possible speech units.
4. The goal of OSS is to find the most likely sequence of states that produces a given sequence of speech units.
5. OSS algorithms typically use a combination of dynamic programming and probabilistic search techniques to find the optimal state sequence.
6. OSS algorithms can be used to improve the accuracy of speech recognition systems by providing more accurate models of speech units.
