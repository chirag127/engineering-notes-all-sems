
## Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING

1. Speech modeling is the process of representing speech signals in a format that can be used for analysis and understanding. It is a key component of speech recognition, natural language processing and other areas of artificial intelligence.

2. Speech models are typically built using statistical methods such as Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) and Gaussian Mixture Models (GMMs). These models are used to represent the various characteristics of speech, such as pitch, duration, and energy.

3. Speech models can also be used for speech synthesis, which is the process of generating synthetic speech from text. This is typically done using a concatenative synthesis model, which combines segments of existing speech to create new speech.

4. Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken words into text. This is typically done using a speech recognition system, which uses a speech model to recognize the spoken words.

5. Speech recognition systems can be used for a variety of applications, such as voice-controlled systems, automated customer service systems, and speech-to-text translation systems.

6. Natural language processing (NLP) is the process of understanding and interpreting natural language. It is typically used to create intelligent systems that can interpret and respond to natural language queries.

7. NLP systems use speech models to interpret spoken queries, and can also use text models to interpret written queries. NLP systems can be used for a variety of applications, such as virtual assistants, automated customer service systems, and automated translation systems.
