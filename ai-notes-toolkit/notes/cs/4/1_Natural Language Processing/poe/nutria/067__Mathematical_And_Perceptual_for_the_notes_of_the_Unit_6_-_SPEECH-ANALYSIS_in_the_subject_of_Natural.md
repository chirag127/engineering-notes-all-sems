
### Mathematical And Perceptual for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Speech Analysis is the process of analyzing speech signals to extract information about the speaker, the language, and the content of the speech.

2. The mathematical and perceptual aspects of speech analysis involve the use of algorithms to analyze the acoustic features of speech and the use of human perception to interpret the results.

3. Acoustic features of speech include pitch, intensity, spectrum, and duration. These features can be used to identify the speaker, language, and content of speech.
