
### Probabilistic CFG for the Notes of Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

* Probabilistic Context-Free Grammars (PCFGs) are a type of grammar used to model the syntax of natural language.
* PCFGs are probabilistic in nature, meaning that they assign probabilities to each of their rules. This allows them to capture the ambiguity of natural language and make better predictions about the structure of a sentence.
* PCFGs are composed of a set of production rules, which specify how a sentence can be constructed from its constituent parts. For example, a rule might specify that a noun phrase can be followed by a verb phrase.
* PCFGs can also be used to generate parse trees, which represent the structure of a sentence. These parse trees can then be used to identify the syntactic roles of words in a sentence, such as subject, object, and verb.
* PCFGs are a powerful tool for natural language processing, and are used in many applications such as machine translation, information extraction, and question answering.
