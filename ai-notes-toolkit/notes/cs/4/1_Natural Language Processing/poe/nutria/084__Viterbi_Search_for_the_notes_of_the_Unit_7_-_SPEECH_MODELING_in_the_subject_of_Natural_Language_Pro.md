
### Viterbi Search for the Notes of Unit 7 - SPEECH MODELING in Natural Language Processing

* Viterbi Search is an algorithm used for finding the most likely sequence of hidden states for a given sequence of observations.
* It is used in speech recognition, where the hidden states are the words in the vocabulary, and the observations are the acoustic signals.
* The algorithm works by constructing a trellis, which is a graph that represents the probability of being in each state at each time step.
* The algorithm then uses dynamic programming to find the most likely path through the trellis.
* The Viterbi algorithm is used in speech recognition systems to find the most likely sequence of words given the acoustic signals.
* It is also used in other areas such as machine translation, handwriting recognition, and natural language processing.
