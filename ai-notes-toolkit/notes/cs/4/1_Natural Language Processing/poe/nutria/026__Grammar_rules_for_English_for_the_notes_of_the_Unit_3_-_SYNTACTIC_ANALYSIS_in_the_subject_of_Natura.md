
### Grammar rules for English for the notes of the Unit 3 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing
1. Syntactic analysis is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence in order to determine the meaning of the sentence.
2. Syntactic analysis is based on the rules of grammar and the structure of a language.
3. Syntactic analysis involves breaking down sentences into their component parts and understanding how these parts interact to form meaning.
4. Syntax is the study of the rules that govern the way words are arranged in sentences to form meaningful utterances.
5. Sentences can be broken down into phrases, clauses, and words.
6. A phrase is a group of related words that does not contain a subject and a verb.
7. A clause is a group of related words that contains a subject and a verb.
8. A word is the smallest unit of meaning in a sentence.
9. Grammar rules determine the order of words in a sentence and the way in which they are combined to form meaningful utterances.
10. Grammatical categories, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs, help to identify the function of each word in a sentence.
