
### Articulatory Phonetics for the notes of the Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Articulatory phonetics is the study of how speech sounds are produced and articulated by the human vocal apparatus. 
- It is concerned with the physical properties of speech sounds (such as place of articulation, manner of articulation, and voicing) and their physiological production. 
- Articulatory phonetics is closely related to phonology, which is the study of the sound patterns in language. 
- The primary focus of articulatory phonetics is on the production of speech sounds, while phonology focuses on the abstract patterns of sounds in language. 
- Articulatory phonetics is also closely related to acoustics, which is the study of sound waves and their propagation through air and other media. 
- Articulatory phonetics is used to describe the physical properties of speech sounds and to explain how they are produced. 
- It is also used to study the physiological and neurological processes involved in the production of speech sounds.
