
### PLP And MFCC Coefficients for the notes of the Unit 6 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- PLP (Perceptual Linear Prediction) is a speech analysis technique used to extract the spectral envelope of a speech signal. It is based on the linear prediction of the speech signal.

- MFCC (Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients) is a speech analysis technique used to extract the spectral envelope of a speech signal. It is based on the mel-frequency scale and is used to represent the spectral envelope of a speech signal.

- Both PLP and MFCC are used to represent the spectral envelope of a speech signal. The difference between the two is that PLP is based on the linear prediction of the speech signal, while MFCC is based on the mel-frequency scale.

- PLP and MFCC can be used to analyze the spectral envelope of a speech signal in order to identify words and phrases in the signal. This can be used for speech recognition and speech synthesis applications.

- PLP and MFCC can also be used to analyze the prosodic features of a speech signal. This can be used for applications such as emotion recognition and speaker identification.
