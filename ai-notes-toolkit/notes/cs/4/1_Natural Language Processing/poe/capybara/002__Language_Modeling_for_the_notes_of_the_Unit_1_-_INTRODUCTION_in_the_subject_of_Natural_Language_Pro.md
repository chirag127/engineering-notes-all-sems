### Language Modeling for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Language modeling is an essential topic in Natural Language Processing (NLP) that involves predicting words or sequences of words in a language. It is a statistical approach to language that helps computers to understand human language and generate meaningful responses.

Here are some key points to keep in mind when learning about language modeling in NLP:

- Language modeling involves predicting the probability of a sequence of words in a language.
- It is used in various NLP tasks such as speech recognition, machine translation, text summarization, and more.
- N-gram models are commonly used in language modeling, where N represents the number of words to predict. For example, a 2-gram model predicts the probability of the next word based on the previous word.
- The perplexity score is used to evaluate the performance of a language model. It measures how well the model predicts the next word in a sentence.
- The neural network-based language models such as Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and Transformers have shown significant improvements in language modeling tasks.
- Language modeling has several challenges, such as handling out-of-vocabulary words, long-term dependencies, and dealing with noisy data.

In conclusion, language modeling is a crucial area of study in NLP that helps computers to understand human language and generate meaningful responses. By understanding the key concepts and challenges in language modeling, you can build effective models for various NLP tasks.
