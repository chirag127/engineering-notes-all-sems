## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

In this unit, we will be focusing on word level analysis, which is a crucial aspect of natural language processing. Word level analysis involves breaking down a sentence into its constituent words and analyzing each word individually. This is important because it allows us to extract meaningful insights from text data and perform various text-based tasks such as sentiment analysis, topic modeling, and more.

Here are some key concepts that you should be familiar with when it comes to word level analysis:

### 1. Tokenization

Tokenization is the process of breaking down a sentence or a piece of text into individual words or tokens. This is the first step in any word level analysis task. Tokenization can be done using various techniques such as whitespace tokenization, punctuation-based tokenization, and more.

### 2. Stemming

Stemming is the process of reducing a word to its base or root form. This is useful for reducing the number of unique words in a text corpus and simplifying the analysis process. There are various stemming algorithms such as Porter stemming, Snowball stemming, and more.

### 3. Lemmatization

Lemmatization is similar to stemming but instead of reducing a word to its base form, it reduces it to its dictionary form or lemma. This is a more advanced technique than stemming as it considers the context of the word and its part of speech. Lemmatization is useful for tasks such as text classification and information retrieval.

### 4. Part-of-speech Tagging

Part-of-speech tagging is the process of identifying the grammatical category of each word in a sentence. This is useful for tasks such as text simplification, sentiment analysis, and more. There are various part-of-speech tagging techniques such as rule-based tagging, stochastic tagging, and more.

### 5. Named Entity Recognition

Named Entity Recognition (NER) is the process of identifying and categorizing named entities in a text corpus such as people, organizations, and locations. This is useful for tasks such as information extraction and text summarization. There are various NER techniques such as rule-based NER, machine learning-based NER, and more.

In conclusion, word level analysis is a crucial aspect of natural language processing and involves various techniques such as tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, part-of-speech tagging, and named entity recognition. By understanding these concepts, you will be able to perform various text-based tasks and extract meaningful insights from text data.
