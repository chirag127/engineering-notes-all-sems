### Viterbi Search

Viterbi Search is an algorithm used for finding the most likely sequence of hidden states in a Hidden Markov Model (HMM). This algorithm is widely used in speech recognition systems and other natural language processing applications.

Here are some key points to understand about Viterbi Search:

- Viterbi Search is used to find the most likely sequence of hidden states in a Hidden Markov Model (HMM), given an observation sequence.
- The algorithm works by computing the probability of each possible state sequence, and then choosing the one with the highest probability.
- Viterbi Search is a dynamic programming algorithm, which means that it uses the results of previous computations to speed up the current computation.
- The algorithm is named after its inventor, Andrew Viterbi, who developed it in the 1960s for use in decoding convolutional codes in digital communications.
- In speech recognition systems, Viterbi Search is used to find the most likely sequence of phonemes or words that corresponds to a given speech signal.
- Viterbi Search can also be used in other natural language processing applications, such as part-of-speech tagging and named entity recognition.
- One limitation of Viterbi Search is that it assumes that the HMM is in a known state, and does not account for uncertainty in the model parameters.
- Despite this limitation, Viterbi Search remains a popular and effective algorithm in the field of natural language processing.

Overall, Viterbi Search is a powerful tool for finding the most likely sequence of hidden states in an HMM, and is widely used in speech recognition and other natural language processing applications.
