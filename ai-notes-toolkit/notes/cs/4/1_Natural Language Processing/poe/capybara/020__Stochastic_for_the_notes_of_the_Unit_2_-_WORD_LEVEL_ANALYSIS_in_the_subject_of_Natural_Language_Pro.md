### Stochastic for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

Stochastic processes are fundamental to Natural Language Processing. Here are some key points to understand stochastic processes in the context of word level analysis:

- Stochastic processes are probabilistic methods used to analyze language.
- In word level analysis, stochastic models are used to predict the probability of a word occurring in a sentence or document.
- One popular stochastic model is the n-gram model, which predicts the probability of a word based on the previous n-1 words in a sentence or document.
- Another popular stochastic model is the Hidden Markov Model (HMM), which predicts the probability of a sequence of words based on a set of hidden states.
- Stochastic models can be used for a variety of tasks, including language modeling, speech recognition, and machine translation.
- Stochastic models require large amounts of training data to accurately predict word probabilities.
- Stochastic models can be improved by incorporating additional linguistic features, such as part-of-speech tags or syntactic structures.

Overall, understanding stochastic processes is essential for word level analysis in Natural Language Processing. By utilizing stochastic models, we can more accurately predict word probabilities and improve our language processing tasks.
