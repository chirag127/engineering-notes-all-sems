### Evaluating N-grams for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

When it comes to word level analysis in Natural Language Processing, N-grams are an important concept to understand. N-grams are essentially contiguous sequences of N words from a given text. In this unit, we will be evaluating the usefulness of N-grams in word level analysis. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- N-grams are used in a variety of applications, such as text prediction, spell checking, and machine translation.
- The choice of N in N-grams is important, as it determines the size of the contiguous sequence of words. Smaller values of N may capture more local context, while larger values may capture more global context.
- Unigrams (N=1) are the simplest type of N-gram, consisting of single words. They are useful for tasks such as word frequency analysis.
- Bigrams (N=2) are the most common type of N-gram, consisting of pairs of words. They are useful for tasks such as collocation analysis and language modeling.
- Trigrams (N=3) are less common, but still useful in certain applications. They consist of sequences of three words and can capture more complex relationships between words.
- The effectiveness of N-grams in word level analysis depends on the specific task and domain. In some cases, higher order N-grams may be necessary to capture the relevant information.
- N-gram models can be evaluated using metrics such as perplexity, which measures the ability of the model to predict unseen text. Cross-validation can also be used to evaluate the generalization performance of the model.

In conclusion, N-grams are an important tool in word level analysis and can be used in a variety of applications. However, the choice of N and the specific evaluation metrics used will depend on the specific task and domain of interest.
