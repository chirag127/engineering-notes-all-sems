## Unit 5 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing

This unit covers the basic concepts of speech processing, which includes the following:

### 1. Speech Recognition

Speech recognition refers to the process of converting spoken words into text form. It involves the following steps:

- Acoustic signal processing
- Feature extraction
- Acoustic modeling
- Language modeling
- Decoding

### 2. Text-to-Speech Synthesis

Text-to-speech synthesis is the process of converting written text into spoken words. It involves the following steps:

- Text analysis
- Linguistic processing
- Acoustic modeling
- Waveform generation

### 3. Speaker Recognition

Speaker recognition refers to the process of identifying a person based on their voice. It involves the following steps:

- Feature extraction
- Speaker modeling
- Verification or identification

### 4. Speech Enhancement

Speech enhancement is the process of improving the quality of speech signals by removing unwanted noise or distortion. It involves the following techniques:

- Noise reduction
- Echo cancellation
- Dereverberation

### 5. Speech Coding

Speech coding is the process of compressing speech signals for efficient transmission and storage. It involves the following techniques:

- Pulse code modulation (PCM)
- Linear predictive coding (LPC)
- Code excited linear prediction (CELP)

These basic concepts of speech processing are essential for understanding the technology behind speech recognition, text-to-speech synthesis, speaker recognition, speech enhancement, and speech coding.
