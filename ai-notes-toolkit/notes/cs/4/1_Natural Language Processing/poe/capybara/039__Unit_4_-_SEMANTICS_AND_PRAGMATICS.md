## Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS

Semantics and pragmatics are two important branches of linguistics that deal with the meaning of language. Semantics is concerned with the meaning of words and sentences, while pragmatics is concerned with the way language is used in context.

### Semantics

1. Semantics is the study of meaning in language.
2. It deals with the relationship between words, phrases, and sentences, and their corresponding meanings.
3. It is concerned with the literal meaning of words and sentences, as well as their connotations and implications.
4. It also deals with the meaning of words in different contexts and the way in which meaning can change over time.
5. Semantics helps us understand how language works and how we use it to communicate effectively.

### Pragmatics

1. Pragmatics is the study of language use in context.
2. It deals with the way language is used to achieve different goals and objectives.
3. It is concerned with the social and cultural factors that influence language use.
4. Pragmatics helps us understand how people use language to convey meaning beyond the literal meaning of words and sentences.
5. It also helps us understand how language is used to express politeness, make requests, and convey emotions.

In conclusion, semantics and pragmatics are important branches of linguistics that help us understand the meaning of language and how it is used in different contexts. By studying these areas, we can become better communicators and gain deeper insights into the ways in which language influences our lives.
