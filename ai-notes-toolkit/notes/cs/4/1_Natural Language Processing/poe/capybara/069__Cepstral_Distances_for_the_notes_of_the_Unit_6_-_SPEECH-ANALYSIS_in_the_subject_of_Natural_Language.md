- Cepstral distances are commonly used in speaker recognition, where they can help distinguish between different speakers by comparing the cepstral distances of their speech signals.

- There are several methods for calculating cepstral distances, including the Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs) method, which is widely used in speech processing applications.

- Cepstral distances can also be used in speech synthesis to compare the similarity of a synthesized speech signal to a target speech signal, which can help improve the quality of the synthesized speech.

- In addition to speaker recognition and speech synthesis, cepstral distances are used in a variety of other speech analysis applications, including speech recognition, speech coding, and speech enhancement.
