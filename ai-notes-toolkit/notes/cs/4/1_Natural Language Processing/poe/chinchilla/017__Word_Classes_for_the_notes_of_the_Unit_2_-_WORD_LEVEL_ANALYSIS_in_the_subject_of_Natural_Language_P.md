### Word Classes

In Natural Language Processing (NLP), it is important to be able to identify the different types of words in a sentence. By doing so, we can better understand the meaning of the text and apply various NLP techniques such as sentiment analysis and named entity recognition. Here are the different word classes that we need to know:

1. Nouns: These are words that refer to people, places, things, or ideas. Examples include "dog," "London," and "happiness."

2. Verbs: These are words that describe an action or state of being. Examples include "run," "sing," and "is."

3. Adjectives: These are words that describe or modify nouns. Examples include "red," "happy," and "tall."

4. Adverbs: These are words that describe or modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Examples include "quickly," "very," and "often."

5. Pronouns: These are words that are used in place of a noun. Examples include "he," "she," and "they."

6. Prepositions: These are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. Examples include "in," "on," and "with."

7. Conjunctions: These are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. Examples include "and," "but," and "or."

8. Interjections: These are words or phrases that express strong emotions or reactions. Examples include "wow," "ouch," and "yikes."

It is important to note that some words can belong to multiple word classes depending on their usage in a sentence. For example, "run" can be a verb ("I run every morning") or a noun ("It was a long run").

By understanding the different word classes, we can better analyze text and apply NLP techniques to extract useful information.
