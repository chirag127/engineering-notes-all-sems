## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

In this unit, we will be discussing word level analysis, which is a crucial aspect of natural language processing. Word level analysis involves breaking down text into individual words and examining them in detail to gain insights into their meanings and context. Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying word level analysis:

1. Tokenization: Tokenization is the process of breaking down text into individual units, or tokens, such as words or punctuation marks. Tokenization is a crucial first step in word level analysis, as it allows us to examine each word in isolation.

2. Stemming: Stemming is the process of reducing words to their base or root forms. This is useful for analyzing text in a more efficient manner, as it allows us to group together words with similar meanings.

3. Lemmatization: Lemmatization is similar to stemming, but instead of reducing words to their base forms, it reduces them to their dictionary forms. This is useful for analyzing text in a more accurate manner, as it takes into account the various inflections that words can have.

4. Part-of-speech (POS) tagging: POS tagging involves labeling each word in a text with its corresponding part of speech, such as noun, verb, adjective, etc. This is useful for understanding the syntactic structure of a sentence and for identifying patterns in how words are used.

5. Named entity recognition (NER): NER involves identifying and classifying named entities in a text, such as people, organizations, and locations. This is useful for understanding the context in which words are used and for extracting relevant information from text.

6. Word embeddings: Word embeddings are numerical representations of words that capture their meanings and relationships with other words. This is useful for analyzing text in a more sophisticated manner, such as for sentiment analysis or text classification.

In summary, word level analysis involves breaking down text into individual words and examining them in detail to gain insights into their meanings and context. By understanding the various techniques and tools used in word level analysis, we can gain a deeper understanding of natural language and how it can be analyzed and processed.
