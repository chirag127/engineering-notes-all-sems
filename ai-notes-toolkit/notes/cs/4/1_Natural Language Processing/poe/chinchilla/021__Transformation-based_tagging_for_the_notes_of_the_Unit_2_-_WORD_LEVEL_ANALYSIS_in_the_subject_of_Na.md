### Transformation-based tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing.

In natural language processing, tagging is the process of assigning a label or tag to each word in a text corpus. These tags can be used to identify the part of speech, named entities, or any other relevant information related to the words in the text. One of the popular methods for tagging is Transformation-based tagging.

Here are some key points to understand Transformation-based tagging:

1. Transformation-based tagging is a machine learning approach that uses a set of transformational rules to assign tags to words in a text corpus.

2. The process starts with a set of tagged training data, which is used to learn the transformation rules. These rules are then applied to untagged text to assign tags to each word.

3. The transformational rules are learned using a process called error-driven learning. This involves iteratively applying the rules to the training data, evaluating the accuracy of the tags assigned, and adjusting the rules to improve the accuracy.

4. One advantage of Transformation-based tagging is that it can handle unseen words or words that are not present in the training data by using the context of the surrounding words to assign a tag.

5. Another advantage is that it can handle complex tagging tasks such as identifying named entities or detecting specific patterns in the text.

6. However, the accuracy of Transformation-based tagging depends on the quality of the training data and the complexity of the transformational rules. It may require significant effort to create a well-tagged training corpus and to design effective transformational rules.

In summary, Transformation-based tagging is a powerful machine learning technique for assigning tags to words in a text corpus. It can handle complex tagging tasks and unseen words, but requires a well-tagged training corpus and effective transformational rules to achieve high accuracy.
