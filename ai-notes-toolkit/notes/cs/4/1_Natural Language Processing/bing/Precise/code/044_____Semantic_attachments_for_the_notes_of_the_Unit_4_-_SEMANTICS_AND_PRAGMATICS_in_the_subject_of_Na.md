### Semantic attachments for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Semantics is the study of meaning in language.
2. Pragmatics is the study of how context influences the interpretation of meaning.
3. Semantic attachments are a way to represent the meaning of a sentence or phrase in a formal language, such as first-order logic.
4. Semantic attachments can be used to translate natural language sentences into a formal representation that can be used for reasoning and inference.
5. Semantic attachments are often used in natural language processing tasks such as question answering, information extraction, and machine translation.
6. The process of creating semantic attachments involves identifying the meaning of words and phrases in a sentence and mapping them to a formal representation.
7. This process can be challenging due to the ambiguity and complexity of natural language.
8. Techniques such as word sense disambiguation and anaphora resolution can be used to help resolve ambiguity and improve the accuracy of semantic attachments.
9. The use of semantic attachments can improve the performance of natural language processing systems by enabling them to reason about the meaning of text and make inferences based on that meaning.
