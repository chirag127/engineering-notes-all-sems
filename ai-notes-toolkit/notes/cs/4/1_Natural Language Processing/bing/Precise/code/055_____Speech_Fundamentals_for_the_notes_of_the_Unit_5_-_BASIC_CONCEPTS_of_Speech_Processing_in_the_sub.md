### Speech Fundamentals

Speech is the vocalized form of human communication. It is based on the production and perception of sounds used in spoken language. Here are some key concepts related to speech fundamentals:

1. **Phonetics**: The study of the physical properties of sounds, including how they are produced, transmitted, and perceived.
2. **Phonology**: The study of the sound patterns of a language, including the rules for combining sounds to form words and phrases.
3. **Articulatory phonetics**: The study of how speech sounds are produced by the movement of the articulators, such as the lips, tongue, and vocal cords.
4. **Acoustic phonetics**: The study of the physical properties of speech sounds, including their frequency, amplitude, and duration.
5. **Perceptual phonetics**: The study of how speech sounds are perceived by the listener, including how they are distinguished from one another and how they are grouped into categories.
6. **Prosody**: The study of the patterns of stress, intonation, and rhythm in speech, which convey meaning beyond the individual words.
7. **Speech synthesis**: The artificial production of human speech, typically using computer algorithms to generate speech sounds.
8. **Speech recognition**: The process of converting spoken language into text, typically using computer algorithms to analyze the acoustic properties of speech.

These are some of the fundamental concepts related to speech processing in the field of Natural Language Processing. Understanding these concepts is essential for developing effective speech processing systems.
