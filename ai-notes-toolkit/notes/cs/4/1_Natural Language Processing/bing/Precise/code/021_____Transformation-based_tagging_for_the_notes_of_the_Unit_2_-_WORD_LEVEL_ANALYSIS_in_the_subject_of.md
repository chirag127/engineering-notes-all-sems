### Transformation-based tagging for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Transformation-based tagging is a rule-based approach to part-of-speech tagging.
- It was introduced by Eric Brill in 1995.
- The approach involves learning a set of rules that can be applied to a text to assign part-of-speech tags to words.
- The rules are learned by starting with an initial tagging of the text and iteratively applying transformations to improve the accuracy of the tagging.
- The transformations are learned by analyzing errors in the initial tagging and identifying patterns that can be used to correct the errors.
- The approach has been shown to be effective and efficient, and has been widely used in natural language processing applications.
- Some advantages of transformation-based tagging include its simplicity, interpretability, and ability to incorporate linguistic knowledge.
- Some disadvantages include the need for a large amount of training data and the potential for overfitting.
