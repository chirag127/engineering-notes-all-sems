### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Origins and challenges of NLP

- Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of study that focuses on the interactions between human language and computers.
- NLP is a subfield of linguistics, computer science, and artificial intelligence.
- The origins of NLP can be traced back to the 1940s and 1950s, when researchers first began exploring the potential for computers to process and generate human language.
- One of the earliest NLP systems was the Georgetown-IBM experiment in 1954, which automatically translated 60 Russian sentences into English.
- Since then, NLP has evolved significantly, with the development of more advanced algorithms and techniques for processing and understanding human language.
- Despite these advancements, NLP still faces many challenges, including the complexity and ambiguity of human language, the need for large amounts of training data, and the difficulty of developing systems that can understand and generate language in a way that is natural and intuitive for humans.
- These challenges have led to the development of various approaches to NLP, including rule-based, statistical, and neural network-based methods, each with their own strengths and limitations.
- As NLP continues to evolve, researchers are working to overcome these challenges and develop more advanced and sophisticated systems for processing and understanding human language.
