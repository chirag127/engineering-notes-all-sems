### Feature Structures

Feature structures are a way to represent the syntactic properties of linguistic objects, such as words, phrases, and sentences. They are used in syntactic analysis, which is the process of analyzing the grammatical structure of a sentence. Here are some key points to remember about feature structures:

1. Feature structures are composed of attribute-value pairs, where the attribute represents a grammatical category and the value represents the specific property of that category.
2. Feature structures can be nested, allowing for the representation of complex syntactic properties.
3. Unification is the process of combining two feature structures into a single structure, by finding common attributes and merging their values.
4. Feature structures can be used to represent agreement between different parts of a sentence, such as subject-verb agreement.
5. Feature structures can be used in conjunction with grammatical rules to generate and parse sentences.
