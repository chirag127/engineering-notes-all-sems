## Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS

Word level analysis is the process of breaking down text into individual words and analyzing their meaning and usage. This can be done through various techniques such as:

1. **Tokenization**: This is the process of breaking down text into individual words or tokens. This is usually done by splitting the text at spaces or punctuation marks.

2. **Stemming**: This is the process of reducing words to their base or root form. For example, the word "running" can be reduced to its base form "run". This is useful for grouping words with similar meanings.

3. **Lemmatization**: This is similar to stemming, but instead of reducing words to their base form, it reduces them to their dictionary form. For example, the word "better" would be reduced to its dictionary form "good".

4. **Part-of-speech tagging**: This is the process of identifying the part of speech of each word in a text. This can be useful for understanding the grammatical structure of a sentence.

5. **Named entity recognition**: This is the process of identifying and classifying named entities in a text, such as people, organizations, and locations.

These techniques can be used to analyze text at the word level and extract useful information and insights. They are commonly used in natural language processing and text mining applications.
