### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- A **dictionary** is a collection of words and their definitions, often listed alphabetically.
- A **thesaurus** is a reference work that lists words grouped together according to similarity of meaning, containing synonyms and sometimes antonyms.
- Both dictionaries and thesauri are important tools for natural language processing, as they provide information about the meanings and relationships between words.
- In the context of semantics and pragmatics, dictionaries and thesauri can be used to understand the meaning of words and phrases in a given text, and to generate alternative expressions with similar meanings.
- Dictionaries and thesauri can also be used to disambiguate words with multiple meanings, by providing context-specific definitions and synonyms.
- In natural language processing, dictionaries and thesauri can be used in tasks such as text classification, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.
- There are many different types of dictionaries and thesauri, including general-purpose dictionaries, specialized dictionaries for specific fields or languages, and computational dictionaries and thesauri designed for use in natural language processing.
- When using dictionaries and thesauri in natural language processing, it is important to choose the right resource for the task at hand, and to be aware of the limitations and potential biases of the chosen resource.
