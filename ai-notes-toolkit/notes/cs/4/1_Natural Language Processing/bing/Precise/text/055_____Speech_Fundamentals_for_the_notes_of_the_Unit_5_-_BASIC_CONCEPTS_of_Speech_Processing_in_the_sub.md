### Speech Fundamentals

1. **Speech Production**: Speech is produced by the movement of the articulators, such as the lips, tongue, and vocal cords, to shape the airflow from the lungs into specific sounds.
2. **Phonetics**: The study of the physical properties of speech sounds, including their articulation, acoustic properties, and auditory perception.
3. **Phonology**: The study of the abstract, mental representations of speech sounds and the rules for combining them.
4. **Prosody**: The study of the patterns of stress, intonation, and rhythm in speech.
5. **Speech Perception**: The process by which the brain interprets and understands speech sounds.
6. **Speech Recognition**: The process of automatically recognizing spoken words and phrases using a computer.
7. **Speech Synthesis**: The process of generating artificial speech using a computer.
8. **Speech Coding**: The process of representing speech signals in a digital format for transmission or storage.
9. **Speech Enhancement**: The process of improving the quality of speech signals, often by reducing noise or other distortions.
10. **Speech Analysis**: The process of extracting information from speech signals, such as speaker identification or emotion recognition.

These are some of the fundamental concepts in the field of speech processing, which is a subfield of natural language processing. These concepts are essential for understanding the basic principles of how speech is produced, perceived, and processed by humans and machines.
