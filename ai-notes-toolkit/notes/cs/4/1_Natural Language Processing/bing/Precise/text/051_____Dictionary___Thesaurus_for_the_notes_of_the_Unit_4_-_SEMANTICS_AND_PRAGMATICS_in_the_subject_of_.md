### Dictionary & Thesaurus

- A **dictionary** is a reference book that contains an alphabetical list of words, with information given for each word, usually including meaning, pronunciation, and etymology.
- A **thesaurus** is a reference book that lists words grouped together according to similarity of meaning, containing synonyms and sometimes antonyms.
- In the context of Natural Language Processing, dictionaries and thesauri can be used for tasks such as word sense disambiguation, text classification, and information retrieval.
- Word sense disambiguation is the process of identifying the correct sense of a word in context, which can be aided by the use of a dictionary or thesaurus.
- Text classification involves assigning a text to one or more predefined categories, and can be improved by using a thesaurus to expand the feature space.
- Information retrieval is the process of finding relevant information in a large collection of documents, and can be enhanced by using a thesaurus to expand query terms.
- Both dictionaries and thesauri can be used to improve the performance of natural language processing systems by providing additional information about the words being processed.
