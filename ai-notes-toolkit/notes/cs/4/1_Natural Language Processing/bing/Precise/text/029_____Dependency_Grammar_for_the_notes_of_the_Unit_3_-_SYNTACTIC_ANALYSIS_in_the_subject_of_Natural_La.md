### Dependency Grammar

- Dependency grammar is a type of syntactic analysis in natural language processing.
- It focuses on the relationships between words in a sentence, rather than the phrase structure.
- The basic idea is that words in a sentence are connected by directed links, called dependencies, which represent the grammatical relationships between the words.
- Each dependency has a head, which is the word that governs the relationship, and a dependent, which is the word that is being modified by the head.
- Dependency grammar can be used to analyze the syntactic structure of a sentence and to extract information about the relationships between the words in the sentence.
- It is particularly useful for languages with free word order, where the position of words in a sentence is not as important as the relationships between them.
- Dependency grammar has been used in a variety of natural language processing tasks, including parsing, machine translation, and information extraction.
