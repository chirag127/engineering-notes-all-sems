### Rule-based for the notes of the Unit 2 - WORD LEVEL ANALYSIS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- Rule-based approaches to natural language processing involve the use of manually crafted rules to analyze and understand text.
- These rules can be based on linguistic knowledge, such as grammar and syntax, or on domain-specific knowledge.
- Rule-based approaches can be effective for well-defined tasks with a limited scope, such as named entity recognition or part-of-speech tagging.
- However, they can be difficult to scale to more complex tasks or to adapt to new domains or languages.
- Rule-based approaches can also be brittle, meaning that they may fail to produce correct results if the input text does not conform to the expected format or if the rules are not comprehensive enough.
- Despite these limitations, rule-based approaches remain an important tool in natural language processing and are often used in combination with other approaches, such as machine learning.
