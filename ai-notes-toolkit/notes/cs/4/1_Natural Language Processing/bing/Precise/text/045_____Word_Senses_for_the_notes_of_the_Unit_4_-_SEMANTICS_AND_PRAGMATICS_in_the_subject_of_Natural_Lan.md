### Word Senses

- Word senses refer to the different meanings that a word can have in different contexts.
- In natural language processing, word sense disambiguation is the task of determining which sense of a word is used in a given sentence.
- Word senses can be represented using a variety of techniques, including dictionary definitions, example sentences, and semantic networks.
- WordNet is a commonly used lexical database that organizes words into sets of synonyms called synsets, each representing a distinct concept.
- Word sense disambiguation can be performed using a variety of techniques, including rule-based methods, supervised learning, and unsupervised learning.
- Word sense disambiguation is an important task in natural language processing, as it can improve the accuracy of tasks such as machine translation, information retrieval, and text summarization.
