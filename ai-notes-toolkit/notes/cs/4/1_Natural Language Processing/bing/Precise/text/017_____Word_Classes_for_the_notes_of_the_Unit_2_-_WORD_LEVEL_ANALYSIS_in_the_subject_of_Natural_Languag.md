### Word Classes

Word classes, also known as parts of speech, are categories of words that have similar grammatical properties. Common word classes include nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections. In natural language processing, word classes can be used to analyze and understand the structure of sentences.

1. **Nouns** are words that represent people, places, things, or ideas. Examples include "book," "John," and "happiness."
2. **Verbs** are words that describe actions or states of being. Examples include "run," "is," and "think."
3. **Adjectives** are words that describe or modify nouns. Examples include "red," "happy," and "large."
4. **Adverbs** are words that describe or modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Examples include "quickly," "very," and "well."
5. **Pronouns** are words that take the place of nouns. Examples include "he," "she," and "it."
6. **Prepositions** are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. Examples include "in," "on," and "with."
7. **Conjunctions** are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. Examples include "and," "but," and "or."
8. **Interjections** are words that express emotion or surprise. Examples include "oh," "wow," and "ouch."

These are the basic word classes that are commonly used in natural language processing. Understanding these classes and their properties can help in analyzing and processing text data.
