### WSD using Supervised for the notes of the Unit 4 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of Natural Language Processing

- **Word Sense Disambiguation (WSD)** is the task of identifying the correct sense of a word in context.
- **Supervised WSD** is a type of WSD that uses labeled training data to learn a model that can disambiguate word senses.
- In supervised WSD, a classifier is trained on a set of labeled examples, where each example consists of a word in context and its correct sense.
- The classifier then uses this information to predict the sense of new instances of the word in context.
- Supervised WSD methods can achieve high accuracy, but require a large amount of labeled training data.
- Common approaches to supervised WSD include decision trees, naive Bayes, and support vector machines.
- Supervised WSD can be applied to a variety of NLP tasks, including machine translation, information retrieval, and text classification.
