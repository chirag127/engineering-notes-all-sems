### VGG

- VGG stands for **Visual Geometry Group**, which is the name of the research group at Oxford University that proposed the VGG models in 2014.
- VGG models are **deep convolutional neural networks** that consist of multiple layers of **3x3 convolutional filters** followed by **max pooling layers** and **fully connected layers** at the end .
- VGG models are designed to **increase the depth** of the network by using **small filters** and **reducing the spatial dimensions** of the feature maps after each pooling layer .
- VGG models are widely used for **image classification** and **recognition** tasks, as they achieve high accuracy on large-scale datasets such as ImageNet .
- VGG models have different variants, such as VGG11, VGG16, and VGG19, which differ in the **number of convolutional layers** and **parameters** .
- VGG models are **simple** and **uniform** in their architecture, which makes them easy to implement and understand .
- VGG models are also **modular** and **adaptable**, as they can be **pre-trained** on one dataset and **fine-tuned** on another dataset for different tasks .
- VGG models have some **limitations**, such as **large size**, **high computational cost**, and **overfitting** . Therefore, newer models such as SqueezeNet, GoogleNet, and ResNet have been proposed to overcome these challenges .
