### Backpropagation and regularization for the notes of the Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS in the subject of Deep Learning

In this unit, we will be focusing on backpropagation and regularization techniques for deep neural networks. Below are the key points that you should understand:

#### Backpropagation:
- Backpropagation is a technique used to train neural networks by computing the gradients of the loss function with respect to each parameter in the network.
- It involves propagating the errors from the output layer back to the input layer, and then updating the weights based on the computed gradients.
- The process of backpropagation involves two key steps: forward propagation and backpropagation.
- Forward propagation involves computing the output of the neural network given an input, while backpropagation involves computing the gradients of the loss function with respect to each parameter in the network.

#### Regularization:
- Regularization is a technique used to prevent overfitting in neural networks.
- It involves adding a penalty term to the loss function that encourages the network to learn simpler models.
- There are two common types of regularization techniques: L1 regularization and L2 regularization.
- L1 regularization involves adding the sum of the absolute values of the weights to the loss function, while L2 regularization involves adding the sum of the squares of the weights to the loss function.
- Regularization can also be achieved through techniques such as dropout, which randomly drops out neurons during training to prevent overfitting.

By understanding backpropagation and regularization techniques, you will be able to train deep neural networks that generalize well to new data and avoid overfitting.
