### Linear models (SVMs and Perceptrons) for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning.

- Linear models are simple yet powerful algorithms used in machine learning.
- They are used for classification and regression tasks.
- Support Vector Machines (SVMs) and Perceptrons are two popular linear models used in deep learning.

#### Support Vector Machines (SVMs)

- SVMs are used for classification tasks.
- They create a boundary that separates the different classes in the data.
- The boundary is created in such a way that the margin between the boundary and the closest data points is maximized.
- SVMs can handle both linear and non-linear data by using kernel functions.
- SVMs are very effective for high-dimensional data.

#### Perceptrons

- Perceptrons are used for classification tasks.
- They are based on the concept of artificial neurons that are inspired by the biological neurons in the brain.
- Perceptrons are used to create a decision boundary that separates the different classes in the data.
- They are simple to understand and implement.
- Perceptrons are a good starting point for learning about neural networks.

#### Differences between SVMs and Perceptrons

- SVMs create a margin that maximizes the distance between the boundary and the closest data points, while Perceptrons create a boundary that separates the data points.
- SVMs can handle non-linear data by using kernel functions, while Perceptrons are limited to linearly separable data.
- SVMs are effective for high-dimensional data, while Perceptrons are simple and easy to understand.

In conclusion, Linear models such as SVMs and Perceptrons are simple yet powerful algorithms that are used in machine learning for classification and regression tasks. SVMs are used for creating a boundary that separates the different classes in the data, while Perceptrons are used to create a decision boundary that separates the data points. They are both effective in their respective areas and are great starting points for learning about machine learning and deep learning.
