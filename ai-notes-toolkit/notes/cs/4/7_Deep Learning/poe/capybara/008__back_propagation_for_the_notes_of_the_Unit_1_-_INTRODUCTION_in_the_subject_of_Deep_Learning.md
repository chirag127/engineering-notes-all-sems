### Back Propagation for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Deep Learning

In deep learning, back propagation is a widely used algorithm that is used to train neural networks. It is an important concept to understand in order to gain a strong foundation in this field. Here are some key points to note about back propagation:

- Back propagation is a supervised learning algorithm that is used to train deep neural networks.
- It is used to update the weights of the neural network based on the error between the predicted output and the actual output.
- The algorithm works by calculating the gradient of the error with respect to the weights of the network using the chain rule of calculus.
- The gradient is then used to update the weights of the network in the opposite direction of the gradient, with the goal of minimizing the error.
- Back propagation is an iterative algorithm, which means that it is run multiple times over the training data until the error is minimized to an acceptable level.
- The algorithm can suffer from the problem of vanishing gradients, which occurs when the gradient becomes very small as it is propagated through the layers of the network. This can lead to slower learning and poor performance.
- To overcome the problem of vanishing gradients, techniques such as weight initialization, activation functions, and batch normalization can be used.
- Back propagation is a powerful algorithm that has enabled the development of deep learning models that can achieve state-of-the-art performance on a wide range of tasks, including image recognition, natural language processing, and speech recognition.

In conclusion, back propagation is a fundamental concept in the field of deep learning, and it is important to understand its key principles and techniques to effectively train neural networks. By mastering back propagation, you can develop powerful models that can achieve impressive results on a wide range of tasks.
