### Intro to Neural Nets

Neural Networks are a fundamental component of Deep Learning. They are modeled after the structure and function of the human brain, which makes them one of the most powerful and flexible tools for machine learning. Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying Neural Nets:

- Neural Nets are made up of interconnected nodes, called neurons, that work together to perform complex computations.
- Each neuron takes in input from other neurons and produces an output based on a set of weights and biases.
- The weights and biases are adjusted during training to minimize the difference between the predicted output and the actual output.
- Neural Nets can be used for a wide range of tasks, including image recognition, natural language processing, and speech recognition.
- The more layers a Neural Net has, the more complex the computations it can perform.
- Neural Nets can be trained using a variety of techniques, including backpropagation, stochastic gradient descent, and convolutional Neural Nets.
- Neural Nets have revolutionized the field of machine learning and are widely used in industry and research.

In conclusion, understanding Neural Nets is essential for anyone interested in Deep Learning. By studying their structure and function, you can gain a deep understanding of how they work and how to use them effectively in your own projects.
