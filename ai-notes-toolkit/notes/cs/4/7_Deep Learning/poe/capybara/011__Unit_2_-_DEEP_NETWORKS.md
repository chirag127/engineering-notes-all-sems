## Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS

Deep networks are a type of machine learning algorithms that are used for various purposes such as image recognition, natural language processing, and voice recognition. In this unit, we will learn about the basics of deep networks and their working principles. Here are some important points about deep networks:

- Deep networks are also known as deep learning or neural networks. They consist of multiple layers of neurons that process the input data and produce the output.
- The input data is fed into the first layer of the network, which processes it and passes it onto the next layer. Each subsequent layer processes the data further until the final output is produced.
- The layers in deep networks can be of different types such as convolutional layers, pooling layers, and fully connected layers. Each layer has a specific function in processing the input data.
- Deep networks use backpropagation algorithm to adjust the weights of the neurons in each layer during the training phase. This helps the network to learn from the input data and improve its accuracy over time.
- One of the key advantages of deep networks is their ability to learn complex features from the input data automatically. This makes them suitable for tasks such as image and speech recognition, where traditional machine learning algorithms may not be effective.
- Deep networks require a large amount of training data to learn from. This can be a challenge in some cases as collecting and labeling data can be time-consuming and expensive.
- Deep networks are computationally expensive to train and require specialized hardware such as GPUs to speed up the training process.
- There are several popular deep network architectures such as Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), and Deep Belief Networks (DBNs). Each architecture has its own strengths and weaknesses and is suited for specific types of tasks.

In conclusion, deep networks are a powerful tool in the field of machine learning that can learn complex patterns and features from the input data. They have several advantages over traditional machine learning algorithms but also require a large amount of training data and specialized hardware to train effectively.
