
### What a shallow network computes for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of Deep Learning

- A shallow network is a type of neural network which consists of one or more layers of neurons, with each layer having a single input and output.
- It is used for basic tasks such as classification and regression.
- The input layer takes in data and passes it to the hidden layer.
- The hidden layer is responsible for processing the data and passing it to the output layer.
- The output layer produces the output, which is then used to make predictions or decisions.
- The neurons in the hidden layer are connected to each other in a way that allows them to learn from the data.
- The weights and biases of the neurons are adjusted in order to optimize the network's performance.
- The shallow network can be used for a variety of tasks such as image recognition, language processing, and natural language processing.
