
## Unit 2 - DEEP NETWORKS

1. Deep networks are a type of artificial neural network (ANN) that contain multiple hidden layers of neurons. They are used for complex tasks such as image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous driving.

2. Deep networks are composed of multiple layers of neurons, each of which performs a specific task. The first layer receives input data, the intermediate layers process the data, and the final layer produces output.

3. Each layer of neurons is connected to the next layer via weights and biases. The weights and biases are adjusted during the training process in order to optimize the network's performance.

4. Deep networks can be trained using supervised learning, unsupervised learning, or reinforcement learning. Supervised learning is used for tasks such as image classification, while unsupervised learning is used for tasks such as clustering.

5. Deep networks can be used for a variety of tasks such as image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous driving. They are also used for tasks such as recommendation systems and fraud detection.
