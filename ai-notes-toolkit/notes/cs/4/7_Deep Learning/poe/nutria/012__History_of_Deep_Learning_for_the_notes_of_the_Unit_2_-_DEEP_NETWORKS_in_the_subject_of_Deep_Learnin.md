
### History of Deep Learning 

1. Deep learning is a subset of machine learning, which is a subset of artificial intelligence. It is a form of artificial intelligence that uses neural networks to learn from data, rather than relying on pre-programmed rules.

2. Deep learning was first introduced in the mid-1980s, but it wasn’t until the 2000s that it began to gain traction.

3. In 2006, Geoffrey Hinton, a professor at the University of Toronto, and two of his students developed a deep learning algorithm called “deep belief networks”. This marked the beginning of the deep learning revolution.

4. Since then, deep learning has become increasingly popular, and has been applied to a wide range of tasks such as image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous driving.

5. Deep learning algorithms are now being used in many industries, including healthcare, finance, and retail.

6. Deep learning has also been used to create powerful artificial intelligence systems that can outperform humans in certain tasks, such as playing the game Go.
