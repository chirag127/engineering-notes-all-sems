
### Computational & Artificial Neuroscience for the notes of the Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION in the subject of Deep Learning

1. Computational and Artificial Neuroscience (CAN) is a field of research that focuses on understanding the functions of the brain and developing artificial intelligence (AI) systems that can replicate and exceed the capabilities of the human brain.
2. CAN combines the fields of neuroscience and AI to understand how the brain works and how AI systems can be designed to mimic or exceed the capabilities of the human brain.
3. CAN focuses on understanding the mechanisms of learning and memory, as well as developing algorithms and architectures that enable AI systems to learn from data.
4. CAN is closely related to the field of machine learning, which is the study of algorithms and techniques that enable computers to learn from data.
5. Optimization and generalization are two of the key concepts in CAN. Optimization refers to the process of finding the best solution to a problem, while generalization refers to the ability of an AI system to apply its knowledge to new situations.
6. CAN research has been used to develop AI systems that can recognize objects and faces, understand natural language, and make decisions based on complex data.
7. CAN research is also being used to develop AI systems that can simulate the behavior of the human brain, such as neural networks and deep learning systems.
8. CAN research has the potential to revolutionize the field of AI and create powerful AI systems that can exceed the capabilities of the human brain.
