
### Batch Normalization 

Batch normalization is a technique used to normalize the inputs of a deep neural network. It is used to reduce the internal covariate shift, which is the phenomenon of the distribution of the inputs to a layer of a neural network changing during training. This can lead to slower learning and poorer generalization. 

Batch normalization works by normalizing each batch of inputs before passing it through the layer. This means that each input is normalized with respect to the mean and standard deviation of the batch. This helps to reduce the internal covariate shift and leads to faster learning and better generalization. 

Batch normalization also has the benefit of making the network more robust to changes in the input distribution. This is because the normalization is done with respect to the batch, rather than the entire dataset. This makes the network more resilient to changes in the input distribution, as the normalization is done with respect to the batch, rather than the entire dataset. 

Batch normalization is also used to reduce the internal covariate shift in the output of a deep neural network. This is done by normalizing the outputs of each layer with respect to the mean and standard deviation of the batch. This helps to reduce the internal covariate shift and leads to faster learning and better generalization. 

Batch normalization has become an important technique in deep learning, as it helps to reduce the internal covariate shift and leads to faster learning and better generalization. It is also used to make the network more robust to changes in the input distribution.
