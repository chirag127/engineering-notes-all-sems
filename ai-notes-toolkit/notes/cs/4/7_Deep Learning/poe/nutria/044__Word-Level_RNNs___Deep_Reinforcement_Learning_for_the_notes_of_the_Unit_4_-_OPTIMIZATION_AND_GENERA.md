
### Word-Level RNNs

* Word-level RNNs are recurrent neural networks (RNNs) that are used to process words or characters in a sequence.
* Word-level RNNs are capable of learning the context of words in a sentence and can be used for a variety of tasks such as language modeling, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.
* Word-level RNNs can be used to generate text by predicting the next word in a sentence given the previous words.

### Deep Reinforcement Learning

* Deep reinforcement learning (DRL) is a type of machine learning algorithm that enables agents to learn how to interact with an environment by trial and error.
* DRL combines deep learning and reinforcement learning to enable agents to learn from their own experiences.
* DRL can be used to solve complex tasks such as playing games, controlling robots, and navigating autonomous vehicles.
