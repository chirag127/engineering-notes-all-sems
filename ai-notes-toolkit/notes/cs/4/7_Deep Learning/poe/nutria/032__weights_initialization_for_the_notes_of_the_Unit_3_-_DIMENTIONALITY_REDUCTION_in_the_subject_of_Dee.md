
### Weights Initialization for Unit 3 - Dimensionality Reduction in Deep Learning

* Weights initialization is the process of setting the initial values of the weights of a neural network. 
* It is an important step in training a deep neural network as it affects the performance of the model.
* There are several methods for initializing weights, including random initialization, Xavier initialization, and He initialization.
* Random initialization is the simplest method, where weights are randomly initialized from a normal distribution. 
* Xavier initialization is a method for initializing weights that helps to keep the variance of the outputs of each layer constant. 
* He initialization is a method for initializing weights that helps to keep the variance of the outputs of each layer close to the variance of its inputs. 
* It is important to note that the choice of weights initialization method should be based on the type of neural network and the type of data.
