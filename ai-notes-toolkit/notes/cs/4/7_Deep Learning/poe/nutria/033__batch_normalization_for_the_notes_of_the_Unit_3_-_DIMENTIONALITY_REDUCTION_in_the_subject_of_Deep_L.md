
### Batch Normalization 

- Batch normalization is a technique used to normalize the inputs of a neural network layer for each mini-batch. 
- It helps to reduce the internal covariate shift, which is a change in the distribution of the layer's inputs caused by the change in parameters during training.
- Batch normalization also helps to increase the stability of the model, reduce the time required to train the model, and reduce the need for regularization. 
- It can be used in combination with other regularization techniques such as dropout to further improve the performance of the model.
- Batch normalization works by normalizing the inputs to the layer by subtracting the mean and dividing by the standard deviation. 
- This normalization helps to ensure that the inputs to the layer have a mean of zero and a standard deviation of one. 
- The normalization also helps to reduce the internal covariate shift, which can lead to improved performance of the model.
