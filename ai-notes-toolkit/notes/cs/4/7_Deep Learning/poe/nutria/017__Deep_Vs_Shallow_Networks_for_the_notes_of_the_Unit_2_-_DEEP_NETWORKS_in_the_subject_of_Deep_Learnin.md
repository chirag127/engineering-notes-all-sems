
### Deep Vs Shallow Networks

* Deep networks are artificial neural networks with multiple layers of neurons. They are used to solve complex problems such as image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous vehicle navigation.

* Shallow networks are artificial neural networks with only one or two layers of neurons. They are used to solve simpler problems such as linear regression and classification.

* Deep networks are more powerful than shallow networks because they can capture more complex patterns in data. They can also learn more abstract features from data, which helps them generalize better to new data.

* Training deep networks is more computationally expensive than training shallow networks, because there are more parameters to optimize.

* Deep networks can be more prone to overfitting than shallow networks, since they can learn too much from the training data and fail to generalize to new data.

* Regularization techniques such as dropout and weight decay can be used to reduce overfitting in deep networks.

* Deep networks are more suitable for complex tasks such as image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous vehicle navigation. Shallow networks are more suitable for simpler tasks such as linear regression and classification.
