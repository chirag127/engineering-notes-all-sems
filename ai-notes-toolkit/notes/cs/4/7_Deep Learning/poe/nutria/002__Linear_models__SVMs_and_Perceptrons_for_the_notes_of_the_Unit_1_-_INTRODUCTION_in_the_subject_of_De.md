
### Linear Models (SVMs and Perceptrons)

* Linear models are a type of supervised machine learning algorithm used for classification and regression tasks.
* Support Vector Machines (SVMs) are a type of linear model that uses a hyperplane to separate data points into two classes.
* Perceptrons are a type of linear model that uses a linear combination of weights and inputs to classify data points into two classes.
* Both SVMs and Perceptrons can be used for both classification and regression tasks.
* SVMs are more effective in higher-dimensional spaces, while Perceptrons are more effective in lower-dimensional spaces.
* SVMs are more robust to outliers and can handle non-linear relationships between features, while Perceptrons are more sensitive to outliers and cannot handle non-linear relationships.
* Both SVMs and Perceptrons are used in deep learning applications such as image recognition and natural language processing.
