
### AlexNet

AlexNet is a convolutional neural network (CNN) developed by Alex Krizhevsky, Geoffrey Hinton and Ilya Sutskever in 2012. It is one of the most influential neural networks in the field of deep learning and is widely used for image classification tasks.

AlexNet consists of 8 layers: 5 convolutional layers, 2 fully connected layers and a softmax layer. It is trained on a large dataset called ImageNet, which contains millions of images labeled with 1000 different classes.

The first layer of AlexNet is a convolutional layer with 96 kernels of size 11×11 and a stride of 4. This layer is followed by a max pooling layer with a size of 3×3 and a stride of 2. The next three convolutional layers have 256, 384 and 256 kernels respectively, with a size of 5×5 and a stride of 1.

The fifth convolutional layer is followed by two fully connected layers with 4096 neurons each and a softmax layer with 1000 neurons.

AlexNet is a deep learning model that has been successful in image classification tasks. It is a good starting point for learning about convolutional neural networks and deep learning.
