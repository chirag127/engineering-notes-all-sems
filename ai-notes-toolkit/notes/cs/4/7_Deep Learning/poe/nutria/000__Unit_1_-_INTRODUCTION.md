
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first step to any subject. It provides an overview of the subject, its importance, and its key concepts. 
2. It helps the reader to understand the basic concepts, terms, and definitions related to the subject. 
3. It also helps the reader to understand the structure of the subject and the relationships between different concepts. 
4. Furthermore, it helps to familiarize the reader with the tools and techniques used in the subject. 
5. Introduction helps the reader to understand the scope and objectives of the subject. 
6. It also provides the reader with the necessary background knowledge to understand the subject better.
