
## Unit 4 - OPTIMIZATION AND GENERALIZATION

* Optimization is the process of making something as good as possible in terms of a specific set of criteria, such as speed, cost, or efficiency. 
* Generalization is the process of making something broadly applicable, as opposed to specific to a certain situation. 
* Optimization and generalization can be used together to create more efficient and robust solutions. 
* Optimization algorithms are used to find the best possible solution to a given problem. Examples include linear programming, dynamic programming, and heuristics. 
* Generalization algorithms are used to create models that can be applied to a wide range of problems. Examples include decision trees, neural networks, and support vector machines. 
* Optimization and generalization can be used together in order to create more efficient and robust solutions. For example, a neural network can be optimized to perform better on a specific task, while a decision tree can be generalized to make it more applicable to a wider range of problems.
