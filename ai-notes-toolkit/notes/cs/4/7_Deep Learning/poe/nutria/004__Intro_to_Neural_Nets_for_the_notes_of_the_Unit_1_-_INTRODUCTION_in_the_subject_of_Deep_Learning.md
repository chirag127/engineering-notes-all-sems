
### Intro to Neural Nets

* Neural networks are a type of artificial intelligence (AI) that are modeled after the human brain. They are composed of a large number of interconnected processing elements, called neurons, which communicate with each other to solve complex tasks.
* Neural networks are used to identify patterns in large datasets, such as recognizing objects in images or understanding natural language.
* Neural networks are composed of an input layer, one or more hidden layers, and an output layer.
* The input layer accepts data as input, which is then passed to the hidden layers. The hidden layers process the data and pass it to the output layer, which produces a result.
* Training a neural network involves adjusting the weights of the connections between neurons, which is done using a technique called backpropagation.
* Deep learning is a type of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple hidden layers to learn complex patterns from large datasets. It is used for a variety of tasks, such as image recognition and natural language processing.
