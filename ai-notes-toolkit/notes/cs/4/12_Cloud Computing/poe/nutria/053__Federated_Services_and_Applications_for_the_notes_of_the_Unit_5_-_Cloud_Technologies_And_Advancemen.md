
### Federated Services and Applications 

* Federated services are cloud computing services that are provided by multiple cloud providers. 
* These services can be used to provide a wide range of cloud solutions, such as storage, computing, networking, and application services. 
* They are often used to provide a high level of scalability, availability, and security to businesses. 
* Federated services are typically used when a single cloud provider is unable to meet the needs of a business. 
* In addition to providing scalability, availability, and security, federated services can also provide a range of other benefits, such as cost savings and improved performance. 
* Hadoop is a popular open-source framework for distributed computing that is used to store and process large amounts of data. 
* Hadoop is often used in combination with federated services to provide a high level of scalability and performance. 
* Hadoop can be used to store and process data from multiple cloud providers, allowing businesses to take advantage of the benefits of multiple cloud providers. 
* Hadoop can also be used to provide a range of advanced analytics and machine learning capabilities, allowing businesses to gain valuable insights from their data.
