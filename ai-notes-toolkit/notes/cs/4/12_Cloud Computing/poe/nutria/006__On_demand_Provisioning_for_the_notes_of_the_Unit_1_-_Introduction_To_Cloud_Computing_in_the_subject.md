
### On‐demand Provisioning for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction To Cloud Computing in the subject of Cloud Computing

1. On-demand provisioning is a cloud computing service that allows users to access computing resources, such as storage, processing power, and networking, on an as-needed basis.

2. This service is typically provided by a cloud provider, such as Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

3. On-demand provisioning is beneficial for businesses because it allows them to scale up or down their computing resources quickly and easily, depending on their needs.

4. Additionally, on-demand provisioning is cost-effective since businesses only pay for the resources they use.

5. On-demand provisioning also provides businesses with more control over their computing resources, as they can choose the type, quantity, and quality of the resources they need.

6. Furthermore, on-demand provisioning offers businesses the flexibility to switch between different cloud providers if necessary.

7. In conclusion, on-demand provisioning is a useful and cost-effective cloud computing service that allows businesses to access computing resources on an as-needed basis.
