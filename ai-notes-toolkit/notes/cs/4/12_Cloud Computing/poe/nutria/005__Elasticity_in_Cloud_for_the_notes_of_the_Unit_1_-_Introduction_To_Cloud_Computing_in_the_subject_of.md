
### Elasticity in Cloud

* Elasticity in cloud computing refers to the ability of a system to scale up or scale down its resources based on the demands of the user.
* It allows the user to quickly and easily increase or decrease the amount of resources allocated to their application.
* This allows for a more efficient use of resources and cost savings for the user.
* Elasticity can be used for both vertical and horizontal scaling. Vertical scaling involves increasing or decreasing the amount of computing power allocated to an application. Horizontal scaling involves increasing or decreasing the number of servers used to run an application.
* Cloud providers typically offer a range of elasticity options that allow the user to choose the most appropriate level of scaling for their application.
* Elasticity can also be used to automate the deployment of applications, allowing for faster deployment times and less manual intervention.
* Elasticity is an important feature of cloud computing and is essential for businesses that require a flexible and cost-effective computing solution.
