### Open Stack

- Open Stack is an open source cloud computing platform software that controls large pools of compute, storage, and networking resources throughout a data center  .
- Open Stack is deployed as an Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) model, which allows users to provision and manage cloud resources through APIs or a web-based dashboard .
- Open Stack consists of a set of software components that provide common services for cloud infrastructure, such as identity, image, compute, network, block storage, object storage, orchestration, telemetry, and more .
- Open Stack is developed and maintained by a global community of contributors, who collaborate through various projects, working groups, and events .
- Open Stack is widely used by enterprises, service providers, research organizations, and government agencies, as it offers scalability, flexibility, and interoperability for cloud applications and services .
