# Future of Federation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cloud Technologies And Advancements Hadoop in the subject of Cloud Computing

- Federation is a feature of Hadoop 2.x that allows a cluster to scale horizontally by adding more NameNodes, each managing a separate namespace and storage pool .
- Federation improves the performance, availability, and isolation of HDFS by distributing the metadata load and avoiding single points of failure .
- Federation also enables the integration of different types of storage devices and file systems, such as SSDs, HDDs, and object stores, into a unified namespace.
- Federation is backward compatible with the existing HDFS architecture and configuration, and does not require any changes to the client applications or the data nodes.
- Federation is an ongoing project that aims to address the future challenges and demands of big data analytics, such as real-time processing, streaming, machine learning, and artificial intelligence.
- Federation is expected to evolve with the cloud-based technologies and platforms, such as AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, that offer scalable, elastic, and cost-effective storage and computing services.
- Federation is also expected to leverage the advancements in hardware and software technologies, such as NVMe, RDMA, erasure coding, and compression, to improve the performance, efficiency, and reliability of HDFS .
