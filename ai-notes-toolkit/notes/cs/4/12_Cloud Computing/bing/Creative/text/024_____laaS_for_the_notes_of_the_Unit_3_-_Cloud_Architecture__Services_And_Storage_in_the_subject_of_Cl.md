### IaaS

- IaaS stands for Infrastructure-as-a-Service, which is a form of cloud computing that delivers fundamental compute, network, and storage resources to consumers on-demand, over the internet, and on a pay-as-you-go basis  .
- IaaS allows consumers to access and manage infrastructure resources such as servers, storage, networks, firewalls, load balancers, etc. without having to purchase, install, or maintain them in their own premises or data centers.
- IaaS provides consumers with flexibility, scalability, reliability, and cost-efficiency, as they can provision and release resources according to their needs, and only pay for what they use.
- IaaS is suitable for various use cases, such as hosting websites, applications, databases, data analytics, testing and development, disaster recovery, etc.
- IaaS is one of the four types of cloud services, along with software as a service (SaaS), platform as a service (PaaS), and serverless.
- Some examples of IaaS providers are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), IBM Cloud, etc.
