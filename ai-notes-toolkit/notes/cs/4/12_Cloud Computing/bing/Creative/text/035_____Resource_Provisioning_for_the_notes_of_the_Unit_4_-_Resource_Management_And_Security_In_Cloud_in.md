### Resource Provisioning

- Resource provisioning is the process of allocating and delivering cloud resources and services to a customer according to their needs and demands .
- Resource provisioning is an important aspect of cloud computing, as it enables the cloud provider to offer scalable, elastic, and on-demand services to the customer, and the customer to pay only for the resources they use .
- Resource provisioning can be conducted using one of three delivery models: Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), or Software as a Service (SaaS).
  - IaaS: The customer provisions virtual machines, storage, networks, and other low-level resources from the cloud provider, and has full control over them .
  - PaaS: The customer provisions application platforms, development tools, middleware, and other high-level resources from the cloud provider, and has limited control over them .
  - SaaS: The customer provisions software applications, such as email, CRM, or ERP, from the cloud provider, and has no control over them .
- Resource provisioning can be performed using different methods, such as manual, automated, or dynamic .
  - Manual: The customer requests the resources from the cloud provider, and the cloud provider allocates them manually.
  - Automated: The customer specifies the resources they need, and the cloud provider allocates them automatically using predefined policies or scripts .
  - Dynamic: The customer and the cloud provider agree on the service level objectives (SLOs) and the service level agreements (SLAs), and the cloud provider allocates the resources dynamically based on the current demand and performance .
- Resource provisioning can be challenging in a cloud-edge computing environment, where the resources are distributed across the cloud and the edge devices, such as smartphones, tablets, or IoT devices.
  - The challenges include: heterogeneity, mobility, latency, bandwidth, security, and reliability of the edge devices.
  - The solutions include: using containerization, orchestration, virtualization, and optimization techniques to manage the resources efficiently and effectively.
