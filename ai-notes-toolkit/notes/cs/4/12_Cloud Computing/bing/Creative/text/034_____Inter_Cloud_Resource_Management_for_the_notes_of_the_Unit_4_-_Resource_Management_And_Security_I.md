### Inter Cloud Resource Management

- Inter-Cloud Resource Management is the process of managing the resources of multiple clouds that are interconnected and interdependent.
- Inter-Cloud Resource Management aims to achieve scalability, reliability, efficiency, and interoperability among different cloud service providers and users.
- Inter-Cloud Resource Management can be classified into four types:

  - Federation Clouds: A federation cloud is a kind of inter-cloud where several cloud service providers willingly link their cloud infrastructures together to exchange resources. Cloud service providers in the federation trade resources in an open manner.
  - Broker Clouds: A broker cloud is a kind of inter-cloud where a third-party entity acts as an intermediary between cloud service providers and users. The broker cloud provides services such as resource discovery, negotiation, allocation, and monitoring.
  - Multi-Cloud Services: A multi-cloud service is a kind of inter-cloud where a single service is composed of multiple cloud services from different providers. The multi-cloud service can leverage the best features and capabilities of each cloud service provider.
  - Multi-Cloud Libraries: A multi-cloud library is a kind of inter-cloud where a uniform cloud API is used to access different cloud services. The multi-cloud library simplifies the development and deployment of cloud applications across multiple clouds.

- Inter-Cloud Resource Management faces several challenges, such as:

  - Resource heterogeneity: Different cloud service providers may have different types, formats, and configurations of resources, such as compute, storage, network, and software. This makes it difficult to discover, compare, and integrate resources across different clouds.
  - Resource availability: Different cloud service providers may have different levels of resource availability, reliability, and performance. This makes it difficult to ensure the quality of service and the service level agreements of inter-cloud applications.
  - Resource security: Different cloud service providers may have different security policies, mechanisms, and standards. This makes it difficult to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and resources across different clouds.
  - Resource governance: Different cloud service providers may have different legal, regulatory, and ethical requirements and constraints. This makes it difficult to comply with the laws and regulations of different jurisdictions and to respect the rights and preferences of different stakeholders.

- Inter-Cloud Resource Management requires several techniques, such as:

  - Resource discovery: The process of finding and identifying the available resources across different clouds that match the requirements and preferences of the users and applications.
  - Resource negotiation: The process of establishing and agreeing on the terms and conditions of the resource usage and sharing between the cloud service providers and the users and applications.
  - Resource allocation: The process of assigning and distributing the resources across different clouds to the users and applications according to the negotiated terms and conditions.
  - Resource monitoring: The process of observing and measuring the status and performance of the resources across different clouds and reporting any anomalies or violations.
  - Resource adaptation: The process of adjusting and optimizing the resource usage and sharing across different clouds according to the changing conditions and demands.

- Inter-Cloud Resource Management can benefit from several frameworks, such as:

  - Framework of Resource Management for Intercloud Computing: A framework that proposes a layered architecture and a set of components and functions for inter-cloud resource management, such as resource discovery, resource negotiation, resource allocation, resource monitoring, and resource adaptation.
  - Cloud Adoption Framework: A framework that provides best practices and guidance for organizing and managing Azure resources effectively, such as management groups, subscriptions, resource groups, and resources.
  - Intercloud: A platform that provides fast, secure, and compliant access to multiple clouds and networks, such as AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and IBM Cloud.
