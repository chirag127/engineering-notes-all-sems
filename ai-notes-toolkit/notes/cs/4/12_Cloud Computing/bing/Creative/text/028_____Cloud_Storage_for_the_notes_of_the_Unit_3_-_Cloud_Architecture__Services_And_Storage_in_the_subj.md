### Cloud Storage

- Cloud storage is a mode of computer data storage in which digital data is stored on servers in off-site locations   .
- The servers are maintained by a third-party provider who is responsible for hosting, managing, and securing data stored on its infrastructure .
- Users upload data to servers via an internet connection, where it is saved on a virtual machine on a physical server  .
- Users can access data anytime from any location and easily share it with those who are granted permission .
- Cloud storage also offers a way to back up data to facilitate recovery off-site .
- Cloud storage can be classified into four types: public, private, hybrid, and multi-cloud .
  - Public cloud storage: data is stored on servers owned by a cloud service provider and accessed by users over the public internet .
  - Private cloud storage: data is stored on servers owned or leased by a single organization and accessed by users over a private network or the internet .
  - Hybrid cloud storage: data is stored on a combination of public and private cloud servers and accessed by users over a secure connection .
  - Multi-cloud storage: data is stored on servers from multiple cloud service providers and accessed by users over the internet .
- Cloud storage has many benefits, such as scalability, cost-effectiveness, reliability, security, and accessibility   .
  - Scalability: cloud storage allows users to increase or decrease the amount of storage space they need according to their demand   .
  - Cost-effectiveness: cloud storage eliminates the need for users to purchase, maintain, and upgrade their own storage hardware and software   .
  - Reliability: cloud storage ensures that data is always available and protected from hardware failures, natural disasters, and human errors   .
  - Security: cloud storage encrypts data in transit and at rest and provides various authentication and authorization mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access   .
  - Accessibility: cloud storage enables users to access data from any device and any location with an internet connection   .
