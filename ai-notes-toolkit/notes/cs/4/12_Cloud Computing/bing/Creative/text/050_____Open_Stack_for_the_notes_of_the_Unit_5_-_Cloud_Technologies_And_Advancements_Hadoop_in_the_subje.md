### OpenStack

- OpenStack is an open source cloud computing platform that provides common services for cloud infrastructure  .
- OpenStack consists of several components that manage different aspects of the cloud, such as compute, storage, networking, identity, image, orchestration, etc.
- OpenStack can be deployed as infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS) in both public and private clouds, where users can provision and manage virtual servers and other resources through APIs or a web-based dashboard .
- OpenStack is developed and supported by a large and diverse community of organizations and individuals, who contribute code, documentation, testing, and feedback to the project.
- OpenStack is designed to be scalable, modular, and interoperable, allowing users to customize and integrate their cloud solutions with various technologies and vendors .
