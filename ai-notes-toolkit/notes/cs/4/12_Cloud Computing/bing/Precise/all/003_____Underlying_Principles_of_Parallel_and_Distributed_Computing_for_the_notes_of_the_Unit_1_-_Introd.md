### Underlying Principles of Parallel and Distributed Computing

1. The terms parallel computing and distributed computing are used interchangeably .
2. Parallel computing implies a tightly coupled system .
3. Distributed systems refer to a wider class of systems, including those that are tightly coupled .
4. Parallel computing is characterized by homogeneity of components (Uniform Structure) .
5. In systems implementing parallel computing, all the processors share the same memory, communication medium, and network .
6. The processors communicate with each other with the help of shared memory .
7. Distributed systems, on the other hand, have their own memory and processors .
8. Parallel computing is often used in tandem with distributed computing .
9. Parallel computing on a single computer uses multiple processors to process tasks in parallel, whereas distributed parallel computing uses multiple computing devices to process those tasks .
10. Parallel and distributed computing occurs across many different topic areas in computer science, including algorithms, computer architecture, networks, operating systems, and software engineering .
