### Public, Private and Hybrid Clouds

- **Public Cloud** is cloud computing that’s delivered via the internet and shared across organizations. It is an environment made available over the internet, that anyone can subscribe to and then access. It supports multitenancy, like an apartment. Anyone can pay their share and use it for themselves.

- **Private Cloud** is cloud computing that is dedicated solely to your organization. As the name implies, it is the infrastructure used by just one organization.

- **Hybrid Cloud** is any environment that uses both public and private clouds. It is a combination of the two other types. Which deployment method depends on your business needs.
