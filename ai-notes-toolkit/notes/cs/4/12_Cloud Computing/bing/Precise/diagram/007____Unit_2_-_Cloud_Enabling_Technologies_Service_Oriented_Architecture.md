## Unit 2 - Cloud Enabling Technologies Service Oriented Architecture

Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an architectural style that supports service orientation. It is a way of designing, developing, deploying, and managing enterprise systems where business needs and technical solutions are closely aligned.

1. SOA is based on the concept of a service, which is a self-contained, modular unit of functionality that can be accessed and used by other systems or applications.
2. SOA promotes loose coupling between services, meaning that services are designed to be independent of each other and can be changed or updated without affecting other services.
3. SOA enables the reuse of services, which can reduce development time and costs.
4. SOA supports interoperability between different systems and technologies, making it easier to integrate systems and share data.
5. SOA can improve the agility of an organization by allowing for the quick and easy creation of new business processes and applications by combining existing services.

In the context of cloud computing, SOA can be used to design and develop cloud-based services and applications. Cloud services can be accessed and used by other systems or applications, regardless of their location or technology. This can enable organizations to take advantage of the scalability, flexibility, and cost savings offered by cloud computing.
