# Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering

Software engineering is the application of engineering principles to the design, development, and maintenance of software. It involves the use of systematic, disciplined, and quantifiable approaches to the development, operation, and maintenance of software.

Here are some key points to remember for the review of software engineering:

1. Software engineering is a discipline that applies engineering principles to the development of software.
2. The goal of software engineering is to produce high-quality software that meets the needs of its users, within budget and on time.
3. Software engineering involves the use of systematic, disciplined, and quantifiable approaches to the development, operation, and maintenance of software.
4. The software development process typically includes requirements analysis, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance.
5. Software engineering principles include modularity, abstraction, information hiding, and separation of concerns.
6. Software engineering practices include the use of version control, testing, and continuous integration.
7. Software engineering methodologies include agile, waterfall, and spiral development.
