### Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

1. Validation is the process of evaluating a system or component during or at the end of the development process to determine whether it satisfies the specified requirements.
2. It is a technique used to ensure that the developed software meets the customer's needs and expectations.
3. Validation is carried out by performing a series of tests on the software to ensure that it behaves as expected under different conditions.
4. The goal of validation is to ensure that the software is fit for its intended purpose and meets the needs of the users.
5. Validation is an important part of the software development process as it helps to identify and correct any issues before the software is released to the users.
6. There are several techniques used for validation, including functional testing, system testing, and acceptance testing.
7. Validation is an ongoing process and should be carried out throughout the development process to ensure that the software is of high quality and meets the needs of the users.
