### Inspection for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Inspection is a type of peer review that involves a structured and formal process of reviewing software artifacts, such as code, design, and documentation. The goal of inspection is to identify and correct defects early in the software development process, before they become more costly to fix.

Some key points to remember about inspection are:

1. Inspection is a formal process that involves a team of reviewers who follow a structured process to review software artifacts.
2. The goal of inspection is to identify and correct defects early in the software development process.
3. Inspection can be applied to various software artifacts, including code, design, and documentation.
4. Inspection can help improve the quality of software by catching defects early, before they become more costly to fix.
5. Inspection can also help improve the development process by identifying areas for improvement and providing feedback to the development team.

In summary, inspection is an important tool in software engineering that can help improve the quality of software and the efficiency of the development process. It is a formal and structured process that involves a team of reviewers working together to identify and correct defects in software artifacts. By catching defects early, inspection can help reduce the cost and time required to develop high-quality software.
