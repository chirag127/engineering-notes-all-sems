### Failure
- Failure is an event that occurs when the delivered service of a software system deviates from its expected behavior.
- It is the inability of a system or component to perform its required functions within specified performance requirements.
- Failure can occur due to various reasons such as incorrect implementation, design flaws, hardware malfunctions, and human error.
- In software engineering, failure is often used to refer to the observable effects of faults or defects in the system.
- The impact of failure can range from minor inconvenience to catastrophic consequences, depending on the nature and criticality of the system.
- To minimize the risk of failure, software systems are subjected to rigorous testing and quality assurance processes.
- Failure analysis is an important aspect of software engineering, as it helps to identify the root causes of failure and implement corrective measures to prevent similar failures in the future.
- Failure is an inevitable part of the software development process, and it is important to learn from failures and continuously improve the software development process to reduce the likelihood of future failures.
