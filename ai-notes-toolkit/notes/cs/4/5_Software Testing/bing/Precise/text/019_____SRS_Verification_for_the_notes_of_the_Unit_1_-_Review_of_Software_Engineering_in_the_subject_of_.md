### SRS Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- SRS stands for Software Requirements Specification.
- SRS Verification is the process of ensuring that the SRS document accurately reflects the requirements of the software.
- The SRS document should be reviewed by all stakeholders, including developers, testers, and end-users, to ensure that it is complete, consistent, and unambiguous.
- SRS Verification can be done through various techniques such as inspection, walkthrough, and peer review.
- The goal of SRS Verification is to identify and correct any errors or omissions in the SRS document before the development process begins.
- A well-verified SRS document can help to reduce the risk of misunderstandings and miscommunications during the development process, leading to a higher quality software product.
