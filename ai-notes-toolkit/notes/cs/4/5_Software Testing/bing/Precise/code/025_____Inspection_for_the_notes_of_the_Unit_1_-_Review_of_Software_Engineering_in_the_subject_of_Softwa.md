### Inspection for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Inspection is a type of peer review where the work product is examined by a team of individuals to identify defects and areas for improvement. It is a formal process that involves a structured meeting where the team follows a defined process to review the work product.

Some key points to remember about inspection are:

1. Inspection is a formal process that involves a structured meeting.
2. The goal of inspection is to identify defects and areas for improvement in the work product.
3. Inspection is a type of peer review and is typically performed by a team of individuals.
4. The team follows a defined process to review the work product.
5. Inspection can be applied to various types of work products, including code, design documents, and test cases.

Inspection is an important part of the software engineering process as it helps to improve the quality of the work product and reduce the number of defects. It is a proactive approach to defect prevention and can save time and effort in the long run by identifying and addressing issues early in the development process.
