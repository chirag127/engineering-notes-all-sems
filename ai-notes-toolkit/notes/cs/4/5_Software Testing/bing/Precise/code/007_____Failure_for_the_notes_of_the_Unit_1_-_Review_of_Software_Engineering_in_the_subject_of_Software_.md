### Failure

- Failure is an event that occurs when the delivered service of a software system deviates from its expected behavior.
- Failure is the inability of a system or component to perform its required functions within specified performance requirements.
- Failure can occur due to various reasons such as incorrect implementation, incorrect requirements, incorrect design, or incorrect testing.
- Failure can have serious consequences such as loss of data, loss of revenue, or even loss of life.
- Failure can be detected through testing, monitoring, or user feedback.
- Failure can be prevented or mitigated through proper design, implementation, testing, and maintenance.
- Failure can be classified into different types such as functional failure, performance failure, security failure, or usability failure.
- Failure can be measured in terms of its severity, frequency, and impact.
- Failure can be managed through proper risk management, incident management, and problem management processes.
- Failure is an important aspect of software engineering and should be carefully considered throughout the software development lifecycle.
