### Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

1. Validation is the process of evaluating a system or component during or at the end of the development process to determine whether it satisfies the specified requirements.
2. It is a technique used to ensure that the developed software meets the user's needs and expectations.
3. Validation is performed by executing the software under realistic conditions and comparing the results with the expected outcomes.
4. It is an important part of the software development process, as it helps to identify and correct errors and defects before the software is released to the end-users.
5. Validation can be performed using various techniques, such as testing, inspection, and review.
6. In the context of software engineering, validation is often used in conjunction with verification, which is the process of ensuring that the software meets the specified design and technical requirements.
7. Together, validation and verification help to ensure that the software is of high quality and meets the needs of its users.
