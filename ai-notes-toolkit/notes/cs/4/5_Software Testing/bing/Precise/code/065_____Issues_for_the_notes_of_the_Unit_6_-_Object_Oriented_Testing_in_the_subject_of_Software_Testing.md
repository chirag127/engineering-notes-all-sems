### Issues for the notes of the Unit 6 - Object Oriented Testing in the subject of Software Testing

1. **Object-oriented testing** is a testing technique that is applied to object-oriented software.
2. Object-oriented testing is different from traditional testing methods because it focuses on the testing of objects and their interactions with each other.
3. Some of the challenges of object-oriented testing include the identification of objects, the determination of their states, and the testing of their interactions.
4. Object-oriented testing requires a different approach to test case design, test execution, and test result analysis.
5. Object-oriented testing techniques include state-based testing, interaction-based testing, and scenario-based testing.
6. State-based testing focuses on the testing of the states of objects and their transitions between states.
7. Interaction-based testing focuses on the testing of the interactions between objects.
8. Scenario-based testing focuses on the testing of the behavior of the system as a whole, by simulating real-world scenarios.
9. Object-oriented testing can be more complex than traditional testing methods, but it can also be more effective in identifying defects in object-oriented software.
10. Object-oriented testing is an important part of the software development process, and it is essential for ensuring the quality of object-oriented software.
