### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews

- Software quality assurance (SQA) is the process of ensuring that a software product meets the quality standards and expectations of the stakeholders, such as customers, users, developers, and regulators.
- SQA involves various activities throughout the software development life cycle (SDLC), such as quality planning, quality reviews, process tailoring, metrics, verification, validation, and change management .
- Quality planning is the activity of defining the quality goals, objectives, criteria, and methods for the software project, and documenting them in a quality assurance plan (QAP) .
- Quality reviews are the activities of checking, inspecting, evaluating, and auditing the software products and processes to ensure their compliance with the quality standards and procedures, and to identify and resolve any defects, issues, or risks .
- Process tailoring is the activity of adapting and customizing the software development processes and methods to suit the specific needs, characteristics, and constraints of the software project, such as scope, size, complexity, domain, technology, and resources .
- Metrics are the quantitative and qualitative measures of the software products and processes, such as size, complexity, functionality, reliability, usability, efficiency, maintainability, and customer satisfaction, that are used to monitor, control, and improve the software quality .
- Verification is the activity of ensuring that the software products and processes conform to the specified requirements and standards, such as functional, non-functional, and regulatory requirements.
- Validation is the activity of ensuring that the software products and processes meet the actual needs and expectations of the stakeholders, such as customers, users, and regulators.
- Change management is the activity of managing and controlling the changes to the software products and processes, such as requirements, design, code, test, and configuration, to ensure their traceability, consistency, and quality .

- Tailoring SQA program by reviews means adjusting and optimizing the SQA activities, such as quality planning, quality reviews, process tailoring, metrics, verification, validation, and change management, based on the feedback and results of the quality reviews .
- The benefits of tailoring SQA program by reviews are:

  - It improves the effectiveness and efficiency of the SQA activities by focusing on the most relevant and critical aspects of the software quality .
  - It reduces the cost and time of the software development by preventing and detecting defects early, and by minimizing rework and waste .
  - It enhances the customer and user satisfaction by delivering software products that meet their needs and expectations, and by ensuring their quality throughout the SDLC .
  - It increases the confidence and trust of the stakeholders, such as developers, managers, and regulators, by demonstrating the compliance and quality of the software products and processes .

- The steps of tailoring SQA program by reviews are:

  - Define the quality goals, objectives, criteria, and methods for the software project, and document them in a QAP .
  - Perform quality reviews on the software products and processes at various stages of the SDLC, such as requirements, design, code, test, and deployment, and record the findings, issues, and recommendations .
  - Analyze the results of the quality reviews, and identify the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats of the software quality .
  - Adjust and optimize the SQA activities, such as quality planning, quality reviews, process tailoring, metrics, verification, validation, and change management, based on the analysis of the quality reviews, and update the QAP accordingly .
  - Monitor and control the SQA activities, and measure and report the software quality metrics, such as defect density, defect removal efficiency, test coverage, and customer satisfaction .
