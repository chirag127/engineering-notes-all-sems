# Test for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

- The test consists of 10 multiple-choice questions based on the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing.
- Each question has four options, only one of which is correct.
- The test is timed and has a duration of 15 minutes.
- The test is scored out of 10 points, with one point for each correct answer and zero points for each incorrect or skipped answer.
- The test covers the following topics from the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing:

  - The definition and objectives of software engineering.
  - The software development life cycle (SDLC) and its phases.
  - The software process models and their characteristics.
  - The software quality attributes and metrics.
  - The software verification and validation techniques and activities.
  - The software testing concepts and principles.
  - The software testing levels and types.
  - The software testing strategies and methods.
  - The software testing tools and techniques.
  - The software testing standards and best practices.

- The test is designed to assess the understanding and application of the concepts and techniques of software engineering and software testing.
- The test is intended to help the students prepare for the exams and improve their knowledge and skills in the subject of software testing.
