### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews

- Software quality assurance (SQA) is the process of ensuring that a software program meets the quality goals and standards defined by the stakeholders, such as functionality, reliability, usability, security, etc.
- SQA involves various activities and techniques, such as planning, testing, inspection, review, audit, measurement, analysis, improvement, etc.
- Reviews are one of the most important and effective techniques for SQA, as they allow the identification and correction of defects and issues in the software artifacts (such as requirements, design, code, test cases, etc.) before they become costly and risky to fix in later stages of development or operation.
- Reviews can be classified into different types, such as informal reviews, walkthroughs, technical reviews, inspections, etc., depending on the purpose, scope, format, participants, roles, and outcomes of the review process.
- Tailoring is the process of adapting and modifying a standard or generic process, method, or technique to suit the specific needs and characteristics of a particular project, organization, or domain.
- Tailoring SQA program by reviews means selecting and applying the most appropriate and effective types of reviews for the software project, considering factors such as the project size, complexity, criticality, schedule, budget, quality goals, standards, regulations, stakeholder expectations, etc.
- Tailoring SQA program by reviews can help to achieve the following benefits:
  - Improve the quality of the software products and processes by detecting and eliminating defects and issues early and efficiently.
  - Reduce the cost and time of development and maintenance by avoiding rework, errors, failures, and delays caused by poor quality.
  - Increase the customer satisfaction and confidence by delivering software products that meet or exceed their requirements and expectations.
  - Enhance the communication and collaboration among the project team and stakeholders by involving them in the review process and sharing feedback and suggestions.
  - Support the continuous improvement and learning of the software development process and practices by identifying and applying best practices and lessons learned from the review outcomes.
