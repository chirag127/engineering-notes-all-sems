### Testing Process

- The testing process is a set of activities that aim to verify and validate the quality of a software product or system.
- The testing process consists of four main phases: planning, analysis, design, and execution.
- Planning: In this phase, the test objectives, scope, strategy, resources, schedule, and risks are defined and documented.
- Analysis: In this phase, the test requirements, specifications, and criteria are identified and analyzed. The test basis, such as the software requirements, design, and code, are also reviewed and verified.
- Design: In this phase, the test cases, test data, test procedures, and test environment are designed and prepared. The test cases are derived from the test basis and the test criteria, and they specify the inputs, expected outputs, and execution conditions for each test.
- Execution: In this phase, the test cases are executed on the test environment, and the actual outputs are compared with the expected outputs. The test results are recorded and reported, and any defects or deviations are identified and tracked. The test execution may be manual or automated, depending on the test strategy and tools.
