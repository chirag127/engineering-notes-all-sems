### Exploratory Testing for the notes of the Unit 5 - Software Testing Activities in the subject of Software Testing

1. Exploratory Testing is a hands-on approach to software testing. In this technique, testers actively control the design of tests as those tests are performed and use information gained while testing to design new and better tests.
2. The key characteristics of Exploratory Testing are:
- Tests are designed as they are executed.
- The focus is on discovery and rely on the tester's intuition and past experience.
- May be time-boxed, using a session-based approach.
- The results are documented as tests are executed.
3. The benefits of Exploratory Testing are:
- It is a highly flexible technique that can be applied at any stage of development.
- It can be more effective than scripted testing at finding certain classes of bugs.
- It takes less preparation time than other methods.
- It is a great approach for risk-based testing.
4. The challenges of Exploratory Testing are:
- It relies heavily on the skill and expertise of the individual tester.
- It can be difficult to ensure consistent coverage and to reproduce bugs.
- There is a risk of missing the bugs that scripted tests are designed to find.
- Test results and coverage may be harder to report on.
