### Code coverage prioritization technique for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing.

1. Code coverage prioritization technique focuses on covering the most important and critical parts of the code first and then moving on to the less important parts.
2. This technique is useful when there is limited time available for regression testing. It helps to maximize the coverage of important areas of the code within the given time constraints.
3. The steps involved in this technique are:

- Identify the critical modules or functions of the software that need to be tested. These can be the modules that were frequently modified or the core functionality modules.
- Allocate a major portion of the testing time to test these critical modules.
- Once the critical parts are covered, move on to test the other modules. The time allotted to the remaining modules will depend on how much time is left after testing the critical parts.

4. The advantages of this technique are:

- It increases the probability of detecting faults in the most important parts of the software.
- It makes effective utilization of limited testing time.

5. The disadvantages of this technique are:

- Less important modules may not get sufficient testing.
- It is difficult to identify the most critical modules or functions of the software. The criticality may differ based on the perspectives of different stakeholders.
