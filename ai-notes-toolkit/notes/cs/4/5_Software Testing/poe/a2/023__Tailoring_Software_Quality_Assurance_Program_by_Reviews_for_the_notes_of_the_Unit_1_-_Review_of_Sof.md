### Tailoring Software Quality Assurance Program by Reviews for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

1. Software reviews are a form of verification and validation techniques to evaluate software products to identify faults and improve quality.
2. Reviews are a quality assurance technique performed at various stages of software development life cycle such as requirements review, design review, code review, and test plan review.
3. Reviews identify faults early in the software development life cycle and are cost effective compared to debugging and testing.
4. Different types of reviews are:
- Walkthrough: Lead by author, focus on comprehension
- Technical review: Focus on standards, logic, and consistency
- Inspection: Meticulous, follow formal process, identify faults
5. Benefits of reviews:
- Early detection of faults
- Improves quality and reduces rework
- Cost effective
- Provides feedback and training
- Ensures adherence to standards and guidelines
