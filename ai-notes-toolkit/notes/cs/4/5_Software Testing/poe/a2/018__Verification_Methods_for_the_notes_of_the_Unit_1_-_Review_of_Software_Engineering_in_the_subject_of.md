### Verification Methods for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

1.  Inspection - Manual checking of documents such as requirements, design or code. A group of people check for errors, omissions, incompleteness, inconsistencies, violations of standards etc.
2.  Walkthrough - A meeting where a person leads members of a development team and other interested parties through a document to gather information and establish a common understanding.
3.  Technical Review - A peer group discussion of a technical work such as requirements specification, design or code. Goals include removing defects and improving the technical content.
4.  Desk checking - Individual checking of work products by peers or managers for conformance to standards or procedures and detection of errors.

The above verification methods are systematic checks done on the various work products produced during software development to identify and remove defects and improve quality. These help evaluate conformance to standards and correctness thereby verifying that the work products meet their specified requirements.
