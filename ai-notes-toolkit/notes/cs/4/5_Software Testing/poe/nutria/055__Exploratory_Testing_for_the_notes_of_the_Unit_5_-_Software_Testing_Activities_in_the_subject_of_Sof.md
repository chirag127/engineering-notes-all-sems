
### Exploratory Testing
Exploratory Testing is a software testing technique that involves testing without any pre-defined test cases. It is often used in agile software development to quickly find defects and explore the system's features. Exploratory Testing is an effective way to uncover bugs that may have been missed by traditional test cases.

Exploratory Testing is a process of learning about the system and finding defects as the tester explores the system. It is an iterative process that involves the tester actively exploring the system and making decisions about what to do next. The tester will often use a combination of techniques such as test case design, test data analysis, and heuristics to uncover new defects.

Exploratory Testing is an important part of the software testing process and can be used to uncover defects that may have been missed by other methods. It is a highly effective way to quickly find defects and explore the system's features.
