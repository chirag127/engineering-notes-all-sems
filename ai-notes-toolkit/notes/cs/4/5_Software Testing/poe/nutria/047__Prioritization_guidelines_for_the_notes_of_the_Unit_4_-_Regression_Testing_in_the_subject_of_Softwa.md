
### Prioritization Guidelines for Unit 4 - Regression Testing in Software Testing
1. Identify the areas of the application that are most vulnerable to change and require frequent testing.
2. Prioritize regression tests based on the impact of the changes made to the application.
3. Prioritize tests based on the complexity of the application, the number of changes made, and the expected results.
4. Consider the cost of running the tests, both in terms of time and resources.
5. Determine the scope of the regression tests to ensure that all areas of the application are tested.
6. Monitor the results of the regression tests to identify any potential issues.
7. Use automation tools to streamline the regression testing process.
