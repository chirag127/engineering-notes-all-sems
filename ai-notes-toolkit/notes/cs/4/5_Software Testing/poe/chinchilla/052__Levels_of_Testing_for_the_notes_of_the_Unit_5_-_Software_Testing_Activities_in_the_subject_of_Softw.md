### Levels of Testing

Testing is an essential part of software development that ensures the quality of the software product. There are different levels of testing that a software product goes through before release. Each level of testing has its objectives, methods, and deliverables. In this section, we will discuss the various levels of testing that software goes through.

1. **Unit Testing**: Unit testing is the first level of testing. It is a white-box testing technique that tests individual units of the software product. The objective of unit testing is to identify defects early in the development cycle. Unit testing is performed by developers and is automated. The deliverables of unit testing are test cases, test results, and defect reports.

2. **Integration Testing**: Integration testing is the next level of testing. It is a black-box testing technique that tests the interaction between different modules of the software product. The objective of integration testing is to identify defects in the interfaces between modules. Integration testing is performed by developers and testers. The deliverables of integration testing are test cases, test results, and defect reports.

3. **System Testing**: System testing is the next level of testing. It is a black-box testing technique that tests the entire software product as a whole. The objective of system testing is to verify that the software product meets the specified requirements. System testing is performed by testers. The deliverables of system testing are test cases, test results, and defect reports.

4. **Acceptance Testing**: Acceptance testing is the final level of testing. It is a black-box testing technique that tests the software product from the end-user's perspective. The objective of acceptance testing is to ensure that the software product meets the user's requirements. Acceptance testing is performed by the end-users or the customer. The deliverables of acceptance testing are test cases, test results, and defect reports.

In conclusion, software goes through different levels of testing before release. Each level of testing has its objectives, methods, and deliverables. The levels of testing are unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing. Testing ensures the quality of the software product and helps in identifying defects early in the development cycle.
