### Oracles for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing

Software testing is an essential process in software engineering that ensures the quality and reliability of software products. During the testing process, testers use various techniques and tools to check the correctness and completeness of software functionalities. One of the critical aspects of software testing is the use of oracles. Oracles are the mechanisms that help testers to determine whether the software behaves correctly or not. In this section, we will discuss the oracles used in software testing.

#### What are Oracles?

An oracle is a mechanism that helps to determine the correctness of the software. It is a source of information that is used to compare the actual output of the software with the expected output. Oracles can be categorized into two types: manual oracles and automated oracles.

#### Manual Oracles

Manual oracles are the oracles that require human intervention to determine the correctness of the software. Manual oracles are often used in exploratory testing or when the expected output is not well defined. Some of the examples of manual oracles are:

- Specifications
- User manuals
- Business rules
- Domain knowledge

#### Automated Oracles

Automated oracles are the oracles that are used to determine the correctness of the software automatically. Automated oracles are often used in regression testing or when the expected output is well defined. Some of the examples of automated oracles are:

- Test scripts
- Expected outcomes
- Comparison of actual and expected outputs
- Code coverage analysis

#### Selection of Oracles

The selection of oracles depends on the type of testing and the nature of the software. In some cases, manual oracles may be more appropriate, while in other cases, automated oracles may be more suitable. The selection of oracles should be based on the following factors:

- Type of testing
- Nature of the software
- Availability of resources
- Time and budget constraints

#### Conclusion

Oracles are the mechanisms that help to determine the correctness of the software. They are essential in software testing because they help to ensure the quality and reliability of software products. The selection of oracles depends on the type of testing and the nature of the software. Testers should choose the appropriate oracles based on the available resources, time, and budget constraints.
