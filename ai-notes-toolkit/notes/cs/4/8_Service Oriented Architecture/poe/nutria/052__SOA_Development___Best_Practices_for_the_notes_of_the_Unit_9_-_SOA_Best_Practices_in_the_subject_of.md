
### SOA Development – Best Practices 
1. Designing a service-oriented architecture should begin with a clear understanding of the business requirements.
2. Services should be designed to be loosely coupled and highly cohesive.
3. Services should be designed with a focus on scalability, reliability, and performance.
4. Services should be designed to be independent and stateless.
5. Services should be designed to be secure, with appropriate authentication and authorization in place.
6. Services should be designed with an appropriate level of abstraction.
7. Services should be designed to be extensible and maintainable.
8. Services should be designed to be interoperable with other services.
9. Services should be designed with a well-defined versioning strategy.
10. Services should be designed to be highly available and fault tolerant.
