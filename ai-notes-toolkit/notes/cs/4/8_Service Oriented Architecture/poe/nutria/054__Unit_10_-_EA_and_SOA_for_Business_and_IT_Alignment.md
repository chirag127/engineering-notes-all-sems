
## Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment

1. Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a comprehensive approach to the design, planning, implementation, and management of an organization's IT infrastructure. It is designed to align the organization's IT capabilities with its business objectives. 
2. Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an approach to designing and developing software that enables applications to be built using loosely coupled services. It is a way of breaking down a complex system into smaller, more manageable components that can be reused and combined in different ways. 
3. EA and SOA are complementary approaches to IT alignment. EA provides a high-level view of the organization's IT capabilities and how they should be structured to meet its business goals. SOA provides a more detailed view of how these capabilities should be implemented and maintained. 
4. EA and SOA can be used to improve the alignment between an organization's IT capabilities and its business goals. By designing an IT infrastructure that is tailored to the organization's needs, EA and SOA can help to ensure that the organization's IT capabilities are better able to support its business objectives. 
5. EA and SOA can also help to reduce costs by making the organization's IT infrastructure more efficient and reducing the amount of time and money spent on maintenance and support. 
6. EA and SOA can also help to improve the agility of the organization's IT infrastructure by allowing for more rapid changes and more flexibility in how the IT infrastructure is managed. 
7. Finally, EA and SOA can help to improve the security of the organization's IT infrastructure by providing better control over access to sensitive data and systems.
