

### Need for Business and IT Alignment for the Notes of Unit 10 - EA and SOA for Business and IT Alignment in the Subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a type of software architecture that allows different components of a system to communicate with each other in a loosely coupled manner.

2. Business and IT alignment is the process of ensuring that the goals and objectives of the business are in line with the IT infrastructure and processes.

3. Without business and IT alignment, the IT infrastructure and processes are not optimized to support the goals and objectives of the business, which can lead to inefficiencies and higher costs.

4. The Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a framework that helps to ensure that the business and IT are aligned. It provides a comprehensive view of the IT infrastructure and processes, and helps to identify areas where business and IT alignment can be improved.

5. EA and SOA can be used together to create a system that is optimized for the goals and objectives of the business. This will help to reduce costs and improve efficiency.
