
### SOA Governance – Best Practices 

- Develop a governance strategy that outlines the roles, responsibilities and decision-making processes for SOA initiatives. 
- Establish a review process for SOA initiatives and ensure that the review process is documented and enforced. 
- Ensure that the SOA governance process is integrated with existing organizational processes, such as change and release management. 
- Develop service-level agreements (SLAs) that define the expected performance and availability of services. 
- Ensure that the SLAs are monitored and enforced. 
- Establish policies and procedures to ensure that services are secure and compliant with relevant regulations. 
- Establish a process for evaluating and selecting service providers. 
- Establish a process for monitoring the performance of services and providers. 
- Establish a process for resolving disputes between service providers and consumers. 
- Establish a process for managing the lifecycle of services. 
- Establish a process for retiring services.
