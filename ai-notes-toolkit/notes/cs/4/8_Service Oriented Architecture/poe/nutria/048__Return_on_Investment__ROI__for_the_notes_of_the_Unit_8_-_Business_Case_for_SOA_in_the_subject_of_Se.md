
### Return on Investment (ROI) for the notes of the Unit 8 - Business Case for SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. Return on Investment (ROI) is a measure of the financial benefit that an organization can expect to receive from a particular investment.
2. ROI is calculated by dividing the expected return from an investment by the amount of money invested.
3. ROI is an important factor when making decisions about investments, as it helps to determine whether the expected return is sufficient to justify the cost of the investment.
4. Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an approach to software development that focuses on the creation of services that can be reused across different applications.
5. SOA enables organizations to create applications that are more efficient and cost-effective, as the same services can be used multiple times.
6. The ROI of SOA depends on the cost of the services and the cost of the applications that use the services.
7. In order to maximize the ROI of SOA, organizations should ensure that the services are well-designed, well-tested, and well-documented.
8. Organizations should also ensure that the applications that use the services are well-designed and optimized for performance.
9. By properly utilizing SOA, organizations can reduce the cost of development and increase the efficiency of their applications, resulting in a higher ROI.
