
### Service Design-time Governance

1. Service Design-time Governance is a set of processes and policies that help organizations manage the design and development of services.

2. The main goal of Service Design-time Governance is to ensure that services are designed and implemented in a way that meets the organization's needs and goals.

3. Service Design-time Governance involves the following activities: 
    - Establishing design standards and guidelines
    - Defining service requirements
    - Developing service models
    - Developing service contracts
    - Testing services
    - Deploying services

4. Service Design-time Governance also includes the following elements:
    - Service Lifecycle Management
    - Service Level Agreements
    - Security
    - Performance
    - Availability
    - Scalability

5. By following Service Design-time Governance, organizations can ensure that their services are designed and implemented in a way that meets their business needs and goals.
