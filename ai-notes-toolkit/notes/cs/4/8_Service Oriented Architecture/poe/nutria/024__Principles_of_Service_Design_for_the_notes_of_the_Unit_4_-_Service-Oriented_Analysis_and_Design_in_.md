
### Principles of Service Design 

1. Service-oriented architecture (SOA) is a distributed computing approach that focuses on the use of services to enable the sharing of data and applications across an organization. 
2. Services are self-contained, modular, and loosely coupled components that provide a set of well-defined operations. 
3. Service design is the process of designing a service to meet the needs of the customer and the organization. 
4. Service design involves understanding the customer’s needs, designing the service to meet those needs, and testing the service to ensure it meets the customer’s expectations. 
5. Service design should consider the customer’s experience, the technology used to deliver the service, and the cost of delivering the service. 
6. Service design should also consider the business goals of the organization, the organizational structure, and the resources available for delivering the service. 
7. Service design should be iterative and involve feedback from customers and stakeholders throughout the process. 
8. Service design should also consider the service’s scalability, reliability, security, and maintainability.
