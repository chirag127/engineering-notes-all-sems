
### Standards and Guidelines for SOA

1. Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a software design approach that enables the development of applications by combining various services.
2. Each service is an independent, self-contained unit that provides a specific set of functions.
3. Services are designed to be loosely coupled, allowing them to be replaced or upgraded independently of other services.
4. SOA encourages the use of open standards and protocols, such as web services, to facilitate communication between services.
5. SOA focuses on the reusability of services, allowing them to be used in multiple applications.
6. SOA enables the development of applications that are scalable, reliable, and secure.
7. Microservices Architecture (MSA) is an implementation of SOA that focuses on the development of small, independent services.
8. Each microservice is a self-contained unit that provides a single function.
9. Microservices are designed to be loosely coupled and independently deployable.
10. MSA encourages the use of open standards and protocols, such as web services, to facilitate communication between services.
11. MSA focuses on the reusability of services, allowing them to be used in multiple applications.
12. MSA enables the development of applications that are scalable, reliable, and secure.
