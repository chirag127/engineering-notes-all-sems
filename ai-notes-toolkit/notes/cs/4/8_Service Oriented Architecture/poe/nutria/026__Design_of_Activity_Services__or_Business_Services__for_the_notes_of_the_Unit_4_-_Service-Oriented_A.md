
### Design of Activity Services (or Business Services) 

* Activity services are services that represent the business activities of an organization. 
* Activity services are typically modeled as a collection of related operations, each of which performs a single task. 
* Activity services are typically implemented as a single unit of work, meaning that all of the operations are executed in a single transaction. 
* Activity services can be implemented using a variety of technologies, such as web services, message-oriented middleware, and service-oriented architecture (SOA). 
* Activity services should be designed to be loosely coupled, meaning that they should be able to be changed or replaced without affecting other services. 
* Activity services should be designed to be secure, reliable, and scalable. 
* Activity services should be designed to be stateless, meaning that they should not maintain any state information between requests. 
* Activity services should be designed to be composable, meaning that they should be able to be combined to create more complex services. 
* Activity services should be designed to be interoperable, meaning that they should be able to communicate with other services, regardless of the technology used to implement them.
