
### Build a Case for SOA 

1. Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is an approach to software development that emphasizes the use of services to achieve business objectives.

2. SOA allows for the reuse of components, making it easier to develop new applications and services.

3. SOA provides a level of flexibility and scalability that is not possible with traditional software development approaches.

4. SOA provides a way to integrate disparate applications and services, allowing for the sharing of data and services.

5. SOA enables organizations to quickly respond to changing business needs by allowing for the modification of existing services or the creation of new services.

6. SOA can reduce costs associated with software development and maintenance.

7. SOA can provide better security by allowing for the implementation of security policies at the service level.

8. SOA can increase the speed of development by allowing developers to focus on the business logic of the application rather than the underlying technology.
