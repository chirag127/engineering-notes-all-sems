
### Emergence of MSA for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction: SOA and MSA Basics in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

* Service Oriented Architecture (SOA) is a type of software architecture that enables applications to communicate with each other over a network. It is based on the concept of services, which are self-contained units of functionality that can be accessed remotely.
* Microservices Architecture (MSA) is an evolution of SOA, which is based on the idea of breaking down large, monolithic applications into smaller, self-contained services that can be deployed independently.
* MSA allows for greater scalability and flexibility, as services can be deployed on different platforms and scaled up or down depending on demand.
* MSA also enables faster development cycles, as services can be developed and deployed independently of each other.
* MSA also enables greater resilience, as services can be deployed on different platforms and scaled up or down depending on demand.
* In addition, MSA can also provide greater security, as services can be isolated from each other and access can be restricted to specific services.
