
### Concepts for the notes of the Unit 7 - Big Data and SOA in the subject of Service Oriented Architecture

1. **Service Oriented Architecture** (SOA) is a software architecture that enables the design of distributed applications, allowing them to be more efficient and reusable.

2. **Big Data** is a term used to describe large volumes of data that can be analyzed to gain insights and make better decisions.

3. **Data Integration** is the process of combining data from multiple sources into a single repository.

4. **Data Security** is the process of protecting data from unauthorized access, manipulation, or destruction.

5. **Data Quality** is the process of ensuring that data is accurate, complete, and up-to-date.

6. **Data Governance** is the process of managing the collection, storage, and usage of data in an organization.

7. **Data Privacy** is the process of protecting personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
