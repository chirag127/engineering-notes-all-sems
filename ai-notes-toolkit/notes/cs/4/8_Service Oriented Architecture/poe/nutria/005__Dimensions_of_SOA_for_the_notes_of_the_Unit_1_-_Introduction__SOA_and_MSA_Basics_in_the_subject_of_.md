
### Dimensions of SOA

1. **Service Interoperability:** The ability of services to interact with each other, regardless of platform, language, or architecture.

2. **Service Reusability:** The ability to reuse services in different applications to reduce development time and cost.

3. **Service Autonomy:** The ability of services to operate independently of other services and to manage their own state.

4. **Service Loose Coupling:** The degree to which services are independent and can be modified, replaced, or removed without impacting other services.

5. **Service Abstraction:** The ability of services to hide their implementation details and focus on their functionality.

6. **Service Composability:** The ability to compose services into larger applications.
