
### Drivers for SOA 
1. Increased agility - SOA allows for faster changes to the system by allowing services to be replaced and updated quickly and easily. 
2. Improved scalability - SOA allows for applications to be scaled up or down quickly and easily to meet the needs of the business.
3. Reduced complexity - SOA reduces complexity by breaking down large applications into smaller, more manageable services.
4. Increased reusability - SOA allows for services to be reused across multiple applications, reducing development time.
5. Improved reliability - SOA allows for services to be monitored and maintained, reducing downtime and improving reliability.
6. Increased security - SOA allows for services to be secured and protected from unauthorized access.
7. Improved interoperability - SOA allows for services to be integrated with other systems, enabling communication and collaboration.
