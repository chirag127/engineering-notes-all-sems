### Provenance of goods

- Provenance of goods refers to the **chain of custody** of a product from the point of origin to the point of consumption .
- Provenance of goods is important for ensuring the **authenticity**, **quality**, **safety**, and **sustainability** of products, as well as preventing **fraud** and **counterfeiting**  .
- Blockchain is a technology that can provide **transparency**, **accuracy**, **security**, and **efficiency** for tracking and verifying the provenance of goods   .
- Blockchain is a distributed ledger that records transactions in a **decentralized**, **immutable**, and **verifiable** way, using **cryptography** and **consensus** mechanisms .
- Blockchain can enable the provenance of goods by:
  - Creating a **digital identity** for each product or asset, using **smart tags**, **barcodes**, **QR codes**, or **RFID chips**  .
  - Storing the **metadata** of each product or asset, such as **origin**, **ownership**, **location**, **condition**, **quality**, **certification**, and **history**  .
  - Linking the **digital identity** and the **metadata** of each product or asset to a **unique hash** on the blockchain  .
  - Allowing the **stakeholders** of the supply chain, such as **producers**, **distributors**, **retailers**, **consumers**, and **regulators**, to access and update the **metadata** of each product or asset, using **smart contracts**  .
  - Providing a **traceable** and **auditable** record of the **movement** and **status** of each product or asset, from the source to the destination  .
- Some examples of blockchain applications for the provenance of goods are:
  - **Everledger**, a platform that uses blockchain to track and verify the provenance of **diamonds**, **wine**, and **luxury goods**.
  - **Provenance**, a platform that uses blockchain to provide **social** and **environmental** information about the provenance of **food**, **fashion**, and **furniture**.
  - **Walmart**, a retailer that uses blockchain to trace the provenance of **leafy greens** and **pork** from **farm** to **fork**.
