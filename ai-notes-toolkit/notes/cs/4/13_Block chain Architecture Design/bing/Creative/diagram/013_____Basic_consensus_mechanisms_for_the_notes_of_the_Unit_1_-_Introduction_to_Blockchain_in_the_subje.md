### Basic consensus mechanisms

- A consensus mechanism is any method used to achieve agreement, trust, and security across a decentralized computer network .
- Consensus mechanisms play an essential part of securing information by encrypting it and using automated group verification .
- Consensus mechanisms are the methodologies used to protect networks from malicious behaviour and hacking attacks.
- In the context of blockchains and cryptocurrencies, some of the most prevalent consensus mechanisms are:
  - Proof-of-work (PoW): A system that requires network validators (or miners) to solve complex mathematical puzzles in order to create new blocks and verify transactions. The first validator to solve the puzzle gets rewarded with newly minted coins and transaction fees. PoW is used by Bitcoin, Ethereum, and many other cryptocurrencies  .
  - Proof-of-stake (PoS): A system that selects network validators based on the amount of coins they stake (or lock up) in the network. The more coins a validator stakes, the higher their chance of being chosen to create new blocks and verify transactions. Validators get rewarded with transaction fees, but not with newly minted coins. PoS is used by Cardano, Polkadot, and Ethereum 2.0  .
  - Proof-of-authority (PoA): A system that assigns network validators based on their reputation and identity. Validators are trusted entities that have been vetted by the network and have to follow certain rules and standards. Validators get rewarded with transaction fees, but not with newly minted coins. PoA is used by some private or permissioned blockchains, such as VeChain and POA Network.
  - Proof-of-space (PoSpace): A system that requires network validators (or farmers) to allocate a certain amount of disk space to store data in order to create new blocks and verify transactions. The more disk space a validator allocates, the higher their chance of being chosen to create new blocks and verify transactions. Validators get rewarded with newly minted coins and transaction fees. PoSpace is used by Chia and Filecoin.
  - Proof-of-burn (PoB): A system that requires network validators to destroy (or burn) a certain amount of coins in order to create new blocks and verify transactions. The more coins a validator burns, the higher their chance of being chosen to create new blocks and verify transactions. Validators get rewarded with newly minted coins and transaction fees. PoB is used by Slimcoin and Counterparty.
