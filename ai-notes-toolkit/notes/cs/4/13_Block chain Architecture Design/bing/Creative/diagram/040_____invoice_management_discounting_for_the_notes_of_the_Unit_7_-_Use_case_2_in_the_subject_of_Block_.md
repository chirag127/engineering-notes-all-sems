### Invoice Management Discounting

- Invoice discounting is a funding option available to small businesses to tide over cashflow vagaries.
- Under the invoice discounting arrangement, the supplier (business) uses the account receivable as collateral to access instant funds to improve the cash flow position.
- The supplier pays a fee to the bank (or the financier) for this service, and the bank collects the full amount from the customer (debtor) when the invoice is due.
- Invoice discounting is a market with a double-digit potential growth rate over the next years in Europe and worldwide.
- The main benefit of invoice discounting is the acceleration of cash flow from customers to suppliers: suppliers get advance payments from the bank rather than waiting for the customers to pay.

### Blockchain Enabled Invoice Discounting

- Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that enables secure and transparent transactions among multiple parties without intermediaries.
- Blockchain can be used to enhance the invoice discounting process by providing the following features :
  - Digitization of invoices and verification of their authenticity and uniqueness
  - Elimination of on-site audits of receivables and debtors, of receivables' notification and debtors' verification, and of month-end reconciliation processes
  - Reduction of fraud, errors, and disputes
  - Improvement of trust and security among the involved parties
  - Optimization of liquidity and cash flow management
  - Automation of smart contracts and payments
- Blockchain enabled invoice discounting can be implemented using different architectures, such as :
  - A private blockchain network, where only the authorized parties can access and validate the transactions
  - A public blockchain network, where anyone can access and validate the transactions, but the data is encrypted and anonymized
  - A hybrid blockchain network, where a combination of private and public blockchains is used to achieve the desired level of privacy and scalability
- Blockchain enabled invoice discounting can also leverage other technologies, such as :
  - Artificial intelligence and machine learning, to analyze the data and provide insights and recommendations
  - Internet of things, to track the physical goods and services and trigger the payments
  - Cloud computing, to store and process the data and provide scalability and flexibility
