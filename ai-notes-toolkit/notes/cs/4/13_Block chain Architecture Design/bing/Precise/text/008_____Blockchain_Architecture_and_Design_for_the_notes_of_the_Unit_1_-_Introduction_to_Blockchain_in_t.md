### Blockchain Architecture and Design

Blockchain architecture is the design and structure of a blockchain system. It includes the selection of frameworks and platforms to meet business objectives, and the coordination of various teams, including business, network, infrastructure, DevOps, and testing, to ensure the success of the project.

The architecture of a blockchain system includes several elements that participate in a network, managing, validating, and adding new entries to the database. These elements include nodes, blocks, and transactions.

- **Nodes** are users or computers that have a complete copy of the blockchain ledger.
- **Blocks** are data structures used for keeping a set of transactions.
- **Transactions** are the smallest building blocks of a blockchain system and can include records, information, and other data.

The design of a blockchain system involves working on functional specifications, UX/UI designs, and the architectural plan as the base of its development. An event-driven architecture incorporating a messaging framework, tethered to dedicated components for handling blockchain state-changing and state-collecting operations, is a prevalent approach for choreographing blockchain-dependent business logic in blockchain-based applications.
