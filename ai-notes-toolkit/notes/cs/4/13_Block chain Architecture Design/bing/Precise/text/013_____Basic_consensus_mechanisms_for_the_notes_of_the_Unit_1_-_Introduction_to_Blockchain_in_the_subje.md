### Basic Consensus Mechanisms

- A consensus mechanism is any method used to achieve agreement, trust, and security across a decentralized computer network.
- In the context of blockchains and cryptocurrencies, proof-of-work (PoW) and proof-of-stake (PoS) are two of the most prevalent consensus mechanisms.
- Consensus mechanisms play an essential part in securing information by encrypting it and using automated group verification.
- Some other consensus mechanisms that provide interesting alternatives are Proof Of Activity, Proof Of Burn, Proof Of Elapsed Time, and Proof Of Capacity.
