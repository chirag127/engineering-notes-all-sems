### Digital Identity for the Notes of the Unit 8 - Use Case 3 in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

- Digital identity refers to the online representation of an individual or organization.
- Blockchain technology can be used to create a decentralized digital identity architecture.
- The blockchain system features an inherent design by maintaining data in a highly encrypted fashion. The blockchain also caters to digital signatures, consensus algorithms, and cryptographic hash functions to protect user identities from breaches and thefts .
- Current architectures to validate, certify, and manage identity are based on centralized, top-down approaches that rely on trusted authorities and third-party operators. A decentralized digital identity architecture, on the other hand, allows for more control and privacy for the user .
- The main components of a blockchain architecture include nodes, transactions, and consensus algorithms .
- Blockchain-based platforms can be used to issue, manage, and verify digital credentials, providing individuals and organizations with a security-rich hub for credentials accumulated over a lifetime .
- When designing a blockchain architecture for a digital identity use case, it is important to consider architecture drivers and explore the design space of the architecture to ensure that the relevant design decisions are made .
