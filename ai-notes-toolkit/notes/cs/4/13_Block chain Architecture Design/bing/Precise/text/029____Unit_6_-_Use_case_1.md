## Unit 6 - Use case 1

1. A use case is a methodology used in system analysis to identify, clarify, and organize system requirements.
2. Use case 1 is the first use case in a series of use cases that describe the interactions between a user and a system to achieve a specific goal.
3. The goal of use case 1 is to define the requirements for a specific functionality of the system.
4. The use case is typically represented as a diagram or flowchart, showing the steps involved in achieving the goal.
5. The use case also includes a description of the actors involved, the preconditions and postconditions, and any exceptions or alternative flows.
6. Use case 1 is an important tool for communicating the requirements of the system to stakeholders and for ensuring that the system meets the needs of its users.
7. By defining the requirements in a clear and organized manner, use case 1 helps to ensure that the system is developed in a way that meets the needs of its users and achieves its intended goals.
