### Provenance of goods for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Provenance refers to the chain of custody of a product from the point of origin to the point of consumption .
2. Blockchain technology can improve transparency into the details of the provenance of consumer goods .
3. Blockchain technology can also improve accuracy in asset tracking throughout its journey in the supply chain .
4. Blockchain technology can provide full visibility of goods-related data and transactions, facilitate tracking of products provenance, help assure goods authenticity and origin, prevent fraud and counterfeiting.
5. Provenance is a key feature in the supply chain, allowing supply chain practitioners to easily see who possessed properties and when they did so.
6. Common areas of application for blockchain provenance include art, luxury goods, land ownership, and supply chain information.
