### Protocols for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain is a distributed database that maintains a continuously growing list of records, called blocks, which are linked and secured using cryptography.
2. Each block contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, a timestamp, and transaction data.
3. By design, a blockchain is resistant to modification of the data.
4. Once recorded, the data in any given block cannot be altered retroactively without the alteration of all subsequent blocks, which requires the consensus of the network majority.
5. Blockchain technology is often associated with cryptocurrencies, such as Bitcoin, but it has many other potential applications, including supply chain management, voting systems, and identity verification.
6. The key features of blockchain technology include decentralization, immutability, and transparency.
7. Decentralization refers to the fact that there is no central authority controlling the blockchain network.
8. Immutability means that once data is recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be altered.
9. Transparency means that all participants in the network can view the transactions recorded on the blockchain.
10. Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize many industries by enabling secure, transparent, and efficient transactions without the need for intermediaries.
