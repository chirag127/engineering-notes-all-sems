### Capital Markets

Capital markets are financial markets where long-term debt or equity-backed securities are bought and sold. These markets channel the wealth of savers to those who can put it to long-term productive use, such as companies or governments making long-term investments.

#### Unit 6 - Use Case 1: Blockchain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize the capital markets industry by providing a secure and transparent way to record and transfer ownership of assets.
2. Blockchain can reduce the need for intermediaries, such as brokers and clearinghouses, which can lower transaction costs and increase efficiency.
3. Smart contracts, which are self-executing contracts with the terms of the agreement between buyer and seller being directly written into lines of code, can be used to automate the settlement process.
4. Blockchain can also improve the transparency and accuracy of record-keeping, reducing the risk of errors and fraud.
5. Several companies and organizations are already exploring the use of blockchain in capital markets, including the Australian Securities Exchange, which is working on a blockchain-based replacement for its current clearing and settlement system.
