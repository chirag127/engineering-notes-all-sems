### Visibility for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Visibility refers to the ability to view and track transactions and data on a blockchain network.
2. In a public blockchain, all transactions and data are visible to all participants on the network.
3. In a private blockchain, visibility can be restricted to certain participants through the use of permissions and access controls.
4. Visibility is an important aspect of blockchain architecture design as it impacts the level of transparency and accountability in the system.
5. In the context of Use case 2 in Unit 7, visibility can be used to ensure that all relevant parties have access to the necessary information to make informed decisions and take appropriate actions.
6. The level of visibility required will depend on the specific use case and the needs of the participants involved.
7. It is important to carefully consider the trade-offs between visibility and privacy when designing a blockchain system.
