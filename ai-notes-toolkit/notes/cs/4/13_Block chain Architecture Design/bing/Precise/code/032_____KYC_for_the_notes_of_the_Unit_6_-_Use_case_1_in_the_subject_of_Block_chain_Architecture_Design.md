### KYC for the notes of the Unit 6 - Use case 1 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- KYC stands for Know Your Customer.
- It is a process of verifying the identity of customers and assessing their potential risks for money laundering or financing terrorism.
- KYC is a mandatory requirement for financial institutions and other regulated companies.
- The process involves collecting and verifying information such as name, date of birth, address, and identification numbers.
- Blockchain technology can be used to create a secure and decentralized system for storing and sharing KYC information.
- This can reduce the cost and time required for KYC compliance and improve the customer experience.
- Blockchain-based KYC systems can also enhance data security and privacy by allowing customers to control access to their personal information.
- Several blockchain-based KYC solutions have been proposed and are being developed by companies and organizations around the world.
