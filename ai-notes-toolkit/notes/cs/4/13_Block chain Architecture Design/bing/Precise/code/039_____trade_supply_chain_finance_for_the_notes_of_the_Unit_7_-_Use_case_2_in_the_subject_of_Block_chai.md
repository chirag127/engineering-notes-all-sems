### Unit 7 - Use case 2: Trade/Supply Chain Finance

1. Trade finance refers to the financial instruments and products that are used by companies to facilitate international trade and commerce.
2. Supply chain finance, on the other hand, is a set of technology-based business and financing processes that link the various parties in a transaction, in order to lower financing costs and improve business efficiency.
3. Blockchain technology can be used to improve the transparency and efficiency of trade and supply chain finance.
4. By using blockchain, all parties involved in a trade transaction can have access to a single, shared version of the truth, reducing the risk of fraud and errors.
5. Smart contracts can be used to automate the execution of trade finance agreements, reducing the need for intermediaries and lowering costs.
6. Blockchain can also improve the traceability of goods, allowing buyers and sellers to track the movement of goods along the supply chain, reducing the risk of counterfeiting and improving the efficiency of the supply chain.
7. Overall, the use of blockchain in trade and supply chain finance has the potential to increase transparency, reduce costs, and improve the efficiency of international trade.
