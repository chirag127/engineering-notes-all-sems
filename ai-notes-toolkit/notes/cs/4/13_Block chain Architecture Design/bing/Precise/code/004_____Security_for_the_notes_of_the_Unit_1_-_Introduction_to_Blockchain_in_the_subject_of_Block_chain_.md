### Security for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Blockchain in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. Blockchain technology is designed to be secure by design.
2. It uses cryptographic techniques to ensure the integrity and authenticity of data.
3. Each block in the chain contains a cryptographic hash of the previous block, which ensures that the data in the chain cannot be altered retroactively.
4. The decentralized nature of the blockchain also makes it resistant to attacks, as there is no single point of failure.
5. Transactions on the blockchain are verified by a network of nodes, which ensures that the data is accurate and has not been tampered with.
6. The use of smart contracts can also enhance the security of the blockchain, as they can be used to automate the execution of transactions and enforce certain conditions.
7. Overall, the security features of the blockchain make it a reliable and trustworthy platform for storing and transmitting data.
