### Design goals for the notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

1. To provide a comprehensive understanding of the concept of permissioned blockchains and their role in the blockchain ecosystem.
2. To explain the key differences between permissioned and permissionless blockchains, and the advantages and disadvantages of each.
3. To explore the various use cases and applications of permissioned blockchains, including in industries such as finance, supply chain management, and healthcare.
4. To examine the technical architecture and design of permissioned blockchains, including consensus mechanisms, smart contracts, and access control.
5. To discuss the challenges and limitations of permissioned blockchains, and potential solutions to overcome these challenges.
6. To provide practical examples and case studies of permissioned blockchain implementations, to illustrate the real-world impact and potential of this technology.
7. To equip students with the knowledge and skills to critically evaluate the suitability of permissioned blockchains for various use cases, and to design and implement permissioned blockchain solutions.
