### Provenance of goods for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Provenance refers to the history of the ownership and location of an object or commodity.
- In the context of blockchain architecture design, provenance of goods refers to the use of blockchain technology to track and verify the origin, authenticity, and movement of goods.
- Blockchain technology allows for the creation of a tamper-proof, decentralized ledger that records the history of transactions and ownership of goods.
- This can be used to provide transparency and traceability in supply chains, allowing consumers to verify the origin and authenticity of the goods they purchase.
- Provenance can also help to prevent fraud and counterfeiting by providing a secure and verifiable record of the movement of goods.
- In summary, the use of blockchain technology for the provenance of goods can provide transparency, traceability, and security in supply chains, benefiting both consumers and businesses.
