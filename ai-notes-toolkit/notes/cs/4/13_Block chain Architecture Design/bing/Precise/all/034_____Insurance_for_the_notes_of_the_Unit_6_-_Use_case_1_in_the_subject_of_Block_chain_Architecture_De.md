### Insurance - Unit 6, Use Case 1: Blockchain Architecture Design

1. Insurance is a contract, represented by a policy, in which an individual or entity receives financial protection or reimbursement against losses from an insurance company.
2. Blockchain technology can be used to improve the insurance industry by increasing transparency, reducing fraud, and streamlining processes.
3. Smart contracts can be used to automate the claims process, reducing the need for manual intervention and increasing efficiency.
4. Blockchain can also be used to securely store and share customer data, reducing the risk of data breaches and improving customer trust.
5. The use of blockchain in the insurance industry is still in its early stages, but it has the potential to revolutionize the way insurance is bought, sold, and managed.
