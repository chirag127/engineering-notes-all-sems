# Unit 7 - Use Case 2: Visibility in Blockchain Architecture Design

- Visibility refers to the ability to view and track transactions and data on a blockchain network.
- In a public blockchain, all transactions and data are visible to all participants on the network.
- In a private blockchain, visibility can be restricted to specific participants through the use of permissions and access controls.
- Visibility is an important consideration in the design of a blockchain architecture, as it can impact the level of trust and transparency in the network.
- The level of visibility required will depend on the specific use case and the needs of the participants on the network.
- For example, in a supply chain use case, visibility may be important for tracking the movement of goods and ensuring transparency in the supply chain.
- In a financial use case, visibility may be important for regulatory compliance and auditability.
- The design of the blockchain architecture should take into account the desired level of visibility and implement appropriate mechanisms to achieve it.
