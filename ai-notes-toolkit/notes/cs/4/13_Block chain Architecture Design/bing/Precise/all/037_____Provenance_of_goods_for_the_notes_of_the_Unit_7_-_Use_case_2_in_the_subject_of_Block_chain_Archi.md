# Provenance of goods

Provenance refers to the chain of custody of a product from the point of origin to the point of consumption . Blockchain technology can be used to improve transparency into the details of the provenance of consumer goods . This technology can also improve accuracy in asset tracking throughout its journey in the supply chain .

When used for provenance and traceability, blockchain provides full visibility of goods-related data and transactions, facilitates tracking of products provenance, helps assure goods authenticity and origin, prevent fraud and counterfeiting .

Provenance is a key feature in the supply chain. With blockchain technology, it is possible to know exactly where raw materials or goods came from and where they are in the supply chain . Supply chain practitioners can easily see who possessed properties and when they did so using provenance .

The most common areas of application for blockchain provenance include art, luxury goods, land ownership, and supply chain information .
