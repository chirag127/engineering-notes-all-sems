# Blockchain Cryptography

Blockchain cryptography is a critical component of blockchain technology, which is used to secure the data on the blockchain network. Here are some key points to note about blockchain cryptography in the context of Unit 8 - Use case 3 in the subject of Blockchain Architecture Design:

1. Cryptography is used to secure the data on the blockchain network by encrypting it, making it difficult for unauthorized parties to access or manipulate the data.

2. Cryptography is also used to verify the identity of the parties involved in a transaction on the blockchain network, ensuring that only authorized parties can access and manipulate the data.

3. Cryptography is used to ensure the integrity of the data on the blockchain network, by ensuring that the data cannot be altered without the knowledge and consent of the parties involved.

4. Cryptography is used to ensure the non-repudiation of transactions on the blockchain network, by ensuring that once a transaction has been recorded on the blockchain, it cannot be denied or disputed.

5. Cryptography is used to ensure the confidentiality of the data on the blockchain network, by ensuring that only authorized parties can access the data.

6. Cryptography is used to ensure the availability of the data on the blockchain network, by ensuring that the data is always accessible to authorized parties.

7. Cryptography is used to ensure the resilience of the blockchain network, by ensuring that the network can withstand attacks and continue to function even in the face of adversity.

In summary, blockchain cryptography is a critical component of blockchain technology, which is used to secure the data on the blockchain network and ensure its integrity, confidentiality, availability, and resilience. It is an essential topic to understand for anyone studying blockchain architecture design.
