### Blockchain in Trade/Supply Chain

Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize the trade and supply chain industry by improving transparency, traceability, and reducing administrative costs. Here are some key points to consider:

1. A blockchain supply chain can help participants record price, date, location, quality, certification, and other relevant information to more effectively manage the supply chain .
2. The core technology of the blockchain is the decentralized ledger, which records and protects transaction data shared among multiple parties .
3. Companies using blockchain technology can expect to see several key benefits, including improved efficiency, as it relies on a shared network infrastructure .
4. Blockchain can help in changing and improving supply chains, it is a distributed and decentralized ledger for recording transactions in a verifiable way that cannot be tampered with .
5. Blockchain in supply chain can help make partners of businesses agree upon and share important pieces of information without involving a middle man to check on everything. Blockchains synchronizes transactions and data along with the whole network, and all the participants verify the calculations and works of the others .
6. Blockchain provides transparency and increases trust with multi-party visibility of digital events across your supply chain .
