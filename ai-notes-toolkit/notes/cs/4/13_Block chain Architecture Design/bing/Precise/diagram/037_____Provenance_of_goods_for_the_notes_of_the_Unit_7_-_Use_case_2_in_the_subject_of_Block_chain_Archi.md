### Provenance of goods for the notes of the Unit 7 - Use case 2 in the subject of Block chain Architecture Design

- Provenance refers to the origin or source of a particular good or product.
- In the context of blockchain architecture design, provenance is used to track and verify the authenticity of goods as they move through the supply chain.
- Blockchain technology allows for the creation of a tamper-proof, decentralized ledger that records the movement of goods from their origin to their final destination.
- This ledger can be accessed by all parties involved in the supply chain, providing transparency and accountability.
- By using blockchain technology, it is possible to verify the authenticity of goods and prevent the sale of counterfeit or fraudulent products.
- This use case is particularly relevant in industries such as luxury goods, pharmaceuticals, and food, where the provenance of goods is of utmost importance.
- Blockchain technology can also be used to track the environmental and social impact of goods, providing consumers with information about the sustainability and ethical practices of the companies they purchase from.
- Overall, the use of blockchain technology for the provenance of goods has the potential to increase transparency, accountability, and trust in the supply chain.
