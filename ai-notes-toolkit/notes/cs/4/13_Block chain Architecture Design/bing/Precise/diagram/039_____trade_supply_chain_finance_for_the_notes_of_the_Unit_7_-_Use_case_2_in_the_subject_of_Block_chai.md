### Unit 7 - Use Case 2: Trade/Supply Chain Finance

Trade finance refers to the financial instruments and products that are used by companies to facilitate international trade and commerce. Supply chain finance, on the other hand, is a set of technology-based business and financing processes that link the various parties in a transaction, including the buyer, seller, and financing institution, in order to lower financing costs and improve business efficiency.

Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize trade and supply chain finance by providing a secure, transparent, and tamper-proof platform for the exchange of information and value. Some of the benefits of using blockchain in trade and supply chain finance include:

1. Increased transparency and traceability: Blockchain technology allows all parties involved in a transaction to have real-time access to the same information, which can help to increase transparency and traceability in the supply chain.

2. Reduced risk of fraud: The use of smart contracts and the immutability of the blockchain can help to reduce the risk of fraud in trade finance transactions.

3. Improved efficiency and reduced costs: Blockchain technology can help to automate many of the processes involved in trade finance, which can help to improve efficiency and reduce costs.

4. Increased access to financing: By providing a secure and transparent platform for the exchange of information, blockchain technology can help to increase access to financing for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) that may have previously been unable to access traditional trade finance products.

Overall, the use of blockchain technology in trade and supply chain finance has the potential to provide significant benefits to all parties involved in international trade and commerce. It is an area of active development and innovation, and is likely to continue to evolve and grow in the coming years.
