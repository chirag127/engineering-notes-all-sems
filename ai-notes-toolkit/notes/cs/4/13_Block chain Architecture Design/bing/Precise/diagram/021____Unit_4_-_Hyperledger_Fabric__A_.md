## Unit 4 - Hyperledger Fabric (A)

Hyperledger Fabric is a blockchain framework implementation and one of the Hyperledger projects hosted by The Linux Foundation. It is designed to be a foundation for developing applications or solutions with a modular architecture.

Some key features of Hyperledger Fabric include:
- **Permissioned membership**: Hyperledger Fabric is a permissioned blockchain network, meaning that the participants are known to each other and have been granted permission to join the network.
- **Modular architecture**: Hyperledger Fabric has a modular architecture, allowing for flexibility in the design and implementation of applications and solutions.
- **Chaincode**: Chaincode is the term used for smart contracts in Hyperledger Fabric. It is a program that runs on the blockchain and defines the business logic for managing the shared ledger.
- **Channels**: Channels are a mechanism for creating private communication between a subset of participants in the network. This allows for confidential transactions and data sharing between specific parties.
- **Endorsement policies**: Endorsement policies define the set of peers that must endorse a transaction before it can be committed to the ledger. This allows for flexibility in the consensus mechanism and can be customized for different use cases.

Hyperledger Fabric is a popular choice for enterprise blockchain solutions due to its permissioned membership, modular architecture, and support for confidential transactions. It is used in a variety of industries, including finance, supply chain, and healthcare.
