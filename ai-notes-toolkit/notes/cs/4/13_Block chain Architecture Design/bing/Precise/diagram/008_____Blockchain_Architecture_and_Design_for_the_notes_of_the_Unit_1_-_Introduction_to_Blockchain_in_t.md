### Blockchain Architecture and Design

Blockchain architecture refers to the design and structure of a blockchain system. It includes the selection of frameworks and platforms to meet business objectives, and the coordination of various teams, including business, network, infrastructure, DevOps, and testing, to ensure the success of the project .

The design and use cases of blockchain architecture involve certain elements participating in a network where these elements manage, validate, and add new entries to the database . With the dramatic transformation of blockchain technology in recent years, industries have become capable of securing large amounts of user data with fewer efforts and resources .

The development of blockchain architecture requires professional blockchain developers, as it is a complicated and decentralized platform . The design process includes working on functional specifications, UX/UI designs, and the architectural plan as the base of its development .

The blockchain architecture consists of elements such as nodes, blocks, and transactions . A node is a user or computer that has a complete copy of the blockchain ledger. A block is a data structure used for keeping a set of transactions. A transaction is the smallest building block of a blockchain system and can include records, information, etc .

An event-driven architecture incorporating a messaging framework, tethered to dedicated components for handling blockchain state-changing and state-collecting operations, is a prevalent approach for choreographing blockchain-dependent business logic in blockchain-based applications .
