## Unit 5 - Hyperledger Fabric (B)

Hyperledger Fabric is a blockchain framework initially developed by IBM and now maintained by the Linux Foundation. It is designed to provide a modular architecture that can be used by a wide range of industries and applications.

Some of the key features of Hyperledger Fabric include:

- **Modularity:** Hyperledger Fabric is designed to be modular, allowing developers to build and customize their blockchain networks to meet specific requirements. This modularity also makes it easier to maintain and upgrade the network over time.

- **Privacy:** Hyperledger Fabric allows for private transactions, where only the parties involved in a transaction can see the details of that transaction. This is important for applications where confidentiality is critical.

- **Scalability:** Hyperledger Fabric is designed to be highly scalable, allowing for large-scale blockchain networks with high transaction rates.

- **Flexibility:** Hyperledger Fabric can be used to create a wide range of blockchain-based applications, from supply chain management to digital identity systems.

- **Smart Contracts:** Hyperledger Fabric supports smart contracts, which are self-executing contracts with the terms of the agreement between buyer and seller being directly written into lines of code.

- **Consensus:** Hyperledger Fabric uses a modular consensus mechanism, allowing developers to choose the consensus algorithm that best suits their application.

- **Permissioned Network:** Hyperledger Fabric is a permissioned network, meaning that participants must be authorized to join the network and access its resources. This is important for applications where trust and security are critical.

In summary, Hyperledger Fabric is a powerful blockchain framework that provides a range of features and capabilities for building secure, scalable, and flexible blockchain networks. Its modular architecture, privacy features, and support for smart contracts make it a popular choice for a wide range of industries and applications.
