### Design Goals for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

When studying permissioned blockchains, it is important to take note of the key design goals that should be considered in order to ensure that the blockchain system is secure, efficient, and effective. Here are some of the design goals that should be kept in mind when creating notes for Unit 3 of the Blockchain Architecture Design course:

1. **Security:** As with any blockchain system, security is a top priority. Notes should emphasize the importance of establishing a secure network, which includes the use of strong cryptographic algorithms and protocols, and the implementation of access controls to prevent unauthorized access.

2. **Efficiency:** Permissioned blockchains are designed to be faster and more efficient than public blockchains. Notes should explain how this is achieved through the use of consensus algorithms that prioritize speed and scalability over decentralization.

3. **Flexibility:** Permissioned blockchains are often used in enterprise settings, which means they must be flexible enough to accommodate a wide range of business needs. Notes should highlight the importance of creating a flexible system that can be easily customized to meet the specific requirements of different organizations.

4. **Transparency:** While permissioned blockchains are not as transparent as public blockchains, they still need to provide a certain level of transparency in order to build trust among participants. Notes should explain how transparency can be achieved through the use of auditing and reporting mechanisms.

5. **Privacy:** In some cases, participants may want to keep certain information private. Notes should discuss the importance of implementing privacy controls, such as the use of private transactions and encryption, to protect sensitive data.

6. **Interoperability:** Permissioned blockchains may need to interact with other blockchain systems or legacy systems. Notes should explain the importance of creating a system that is interoperable with other systems, and the challenges that may arise when trying to achieve this.

7. **Governance:** Permissioned blockchains require a governance structure to ensure that the system runs smoothly and that disputes can be resolved fairly. Notes should explain the importance of establishing a governance framework that is transparent, democratic, and accountable.

By keeping these design goals in mind, students can gain a deeper understanding of the key considerations that go into creating a secure and effective permissioned blockchain system.
