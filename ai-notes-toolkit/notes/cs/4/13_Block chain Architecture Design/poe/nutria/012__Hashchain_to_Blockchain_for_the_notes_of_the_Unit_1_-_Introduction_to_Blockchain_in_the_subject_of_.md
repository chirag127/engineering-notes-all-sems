
### Hashchain to Blockchain 

1. Hashchain is a type of distributed ledger technology (DLT) that uses a hash function to link together transactions in a chain. 
2. Hashchain is similar to blockchain in that it is a decentralized, immutable, and secure system of record keeping. 
3. The main difference between hashchain and blockchain is that hashchain does not use a consensus mechanism to validate transactions. 
4. Instead, it uses a hash function to link transactions together in a chain. 
5. Hashchain is a simpler and less expensive way to store data compared to blockchain. 
6. Hashchain is used for applications such as digital asset management, identity management, and smart contracts. 
7. Hashchain is also used for distributed data storage and distributed computing.

9. It also covers the different types of blockchain networks, such as permissioned and permissionless networks.
