
### Design Goals for the Notes of Unit 3 - Permissioned Blockchains in the Subject of Blockchain Architecture Design

1. Understand the concept of permissioned blockchains, and how they differ from permissionless blockchains.
2. Analyze the advantages and disadvantages of permissioned blockchains.
3. Explain the components of a permissioned blockchain, including nodes, consensus algorithms, and smart contracts.
4. Understand how permissioned blockchains can be used to improve security, privacy, scalability, and performance.
5. Describe the use cases of permissioned blockchains in the enterprise and public sector.
6. Evaluate the legal and regulatory implications of permissioned blockchains.
7. Explore the potential applications of permissioned blockchains in various industries.
