
### Digital Money to Distributed Ledgers 

1. Digital money is a type of currency that exists only in digital form and is not backed by any physical assets. It is created, stored, and transferred electronically. Examples of digital money include cryptocurrencies and virtual currencies.

2. A distributed ledger is a type of database that is shared across multiple nodes or computers. Transactions are recorded in the ledger and stored in a decentralized manner, meaning that no single node or entity is responsible for maintaining the ledger.

3. Blockchain technology is a type of distributed ledger technology (DLT) that uses cryptographic algorithms to secure and validate transactions. It is the underlying technology behind cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin and Ethereum.

4. Blockchains are composed of blocks, which are collections of transactions that have been validated by a consensus mechanism. Each block is linked to the previous block, forming a chain of blocks.

5. Smart contracts are digital contracts that are stored on the blockchain and are self-executing. They are used to facilitate, verify, and enforce the performance of a contract between two or more parties.

6. Decentralized applications (Dapps) are applications that are built on top of a blockchain and are open source, meaning that anyone can view and contribute to the code. They are typically used to create decentralized markets, exchanges, and crowdfunding platforms.
