
## Unit 4 - Hyperledger Fabric (A)

1. Hyperledger Fabric is an open source, permissioned blockchain platform developed by the Linux Foundation. It is designed for enterprise-grade applications that require high throughput, data privacy, and confidentiality.

2. Hyperledger Fabric is based on a modular architecture that allows components, such as consensus and membership services, to be plug-and-play. This allows organizations to customize their blockchain networks according to their specific needs.

3. Hyperledger Fabric is composed of several components, including a consensus protocol, a membership service provider, a smart contract language, and a data store. These components work together to ensure that transactions are securely and efficiently processed.

4. Hyperledger Fabric also supports the development of smart contracts, which are computer programs that are executed on the blockchain. Smart contracts allow organizations to automate business processes and reduce the need for manual intervention.

5. Hyperledger Fabric is designed to be secure, scalable, and interoperable. It is also designed to be compliant with existing regulatory requirements and industry standards.
