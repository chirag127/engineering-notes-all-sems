
### Land Records and Other Kinds of Record Keeping between Government Entities for the Notes of the Unit 8 - Use Case 3 in the Block Chain Architecture Design

* Land records are documents that provide evidence of ownership and transfer of land between parties.
* Record keeping between government entities is essential for tracking and maintaining legal documents, such as contracts, titles, deeds, and other documents related to land ownership.
* Block chain technology can be used to create a secure, decentralized, and immutable ledger of records, which can help reduce the cost and complexity of record keeping between government entities.
* Block chain technology can provide a secure platform for the storage and exchange of land records, as well as other types of records, such as financial records, health records, and legal records.
* Block chain technology can also be used to create smart contracts, which are digital contracts that are automatically enforced by the block chain network.
* Block chain technology can also be used to create secure digital identities, which can be used to securely store and transfer land records between parties.
* Block chain technology can also be used to create secure digital signatures, which can be used to verify the authenticity of land records and other documents.
