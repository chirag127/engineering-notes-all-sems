

### Blockchain Architecture and Design

1. Blockchain is a digital ledger technology that enables secure, transparent, and immutable record-keeping of digital transactions.
2. A blockchain is a distributed digital ledger that is maintained by a network of computers. It records data in a secure, immutable, and verifiable way.
3. Each transaction is cryptographically secured and linked to the previous transaction, forming an immutable chain of records.
4. The distributed nature of the blockchain makes it resistant to tampering and manipulation.
5. The blockchain is a decentralized system, with no single point of control or failure.
6. Transactions on the blockchain are validated by a consensus mechanism, such as Proof-of-Work or Proof-of-Stake.
7. Smart contracts are self-executing contracts that are written in code and stored on the blockchain.
8. Smart contracts enable automated execution of transactions based on predefined conditions.
9. Blockchains can be public, private, or permissioned.
10. Public blockchains are open to anyone and are usually decentralized.
11. Private blockchains are managed by a single entity or group and are usually centralized.
12. Permissioned blockchains are managed by a group of entities and are usually hybrid in nature.
