
### KYC for the Notes of Unit 6 - Use Case 1 in Block Chain Architecture Design

1. KYC stands for “Know Your Customer” and is a process used by financial institutions to verify the identity of their customers.
2. This process is important for banks and other financial institutions to meet their regulatory requirements and to prevent money laundering and other fraudulent activities.
3. The KYC process involves collecting and verifying information about the customer such as their name, address, date of birth, and other identifying information.
4. The customer is also required to provide documents such as a passport or driver’s license to prove their identity.
5. The customer’s information is then stored in a secure database and used for future transactions.
6. Block chain technology can be used to improve the KYC process by making it more secure and efficient.
7. Block chain technology allows for the secure storage and sharing of customer data, as well as the ability to track and verify transactions in real time.
8. This makes it difficult for criminals to access and use customer data for illegal activities.
9. Block chain technology can also be used to automate the KYC process, making it faster and more efficient.
10. This can help financial institutions meet their regulatory requirements and reduce the costs associated with manual KYC processes.
