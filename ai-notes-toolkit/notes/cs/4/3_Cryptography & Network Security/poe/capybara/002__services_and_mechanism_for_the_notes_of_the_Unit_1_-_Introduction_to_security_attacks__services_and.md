### Services and Mechanism for the Notes of Unit 1 - Cryptography & Network Security

In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to cryptography and network security:

#### 1. Introduction to Security Attacks, Services, and Mechanism
- Discuss the different types of security attacks such as passive attacks, active attacks, insider attacks, and outsider attacks.
- Explain the basic security services such as confidentiality, integrity, availability, and authentication.
- Describe the different security mechanisms such as encryption, hashing, digital signatures, and access control.

#### 2. Classical Encryption Techniques
- Discuss the basic concepts of cryptography and the different types of encryption techniques.
- Explain substitution ciphers and transposition ciphers in detail.
- Describe the different types of cryptanalysis such as brute-force attacks, frequency analysis, and known plaintext attacks.
- Discuss steganography and its use in hiding messages.

#### 3. Stream and Block Ciphers
- Explain stream ciphers and their use for data transmission.
- Describe the principles of block ciphers and their advantages over stream ciphers.
- Discuss Shannon's theory of confusion and diffusion and its importance in developing strong block ciphers.
- Explain the fiestal structure of block ciphers and its role in data encryption.
- Describe the Data Encryption Standard (DES) and its strength in securing data.
- Explain the idea of differential cryptanalysis and its use in breaking DES.
- Discuss the different block cipher modes of operation such as Electronic Codebook (ECB), Cipher Block Chaining (CBC), Cipher Feedback (CFB), and Output Feedback (OFB).
- Describe Triple DES and its use in improving the security of DES.

By studying these topics, you will gain a thorough understanding of the principles and techniques used in cryptography and network security. These concepts will be essential for securing data and protecting against security attacks in the digital age.
