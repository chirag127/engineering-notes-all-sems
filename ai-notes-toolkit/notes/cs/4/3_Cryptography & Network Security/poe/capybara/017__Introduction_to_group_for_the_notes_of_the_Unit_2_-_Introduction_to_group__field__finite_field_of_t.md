### Introduction to Group Theory

Group theory is an important branch of mathematics that has many applications, including in cryptography and network security. In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

1. Field Theory
    - Introduction to fields and finite fields of the form GF(p).
2. Modular Arithmetic
    - Definition of modular arithmetic and its properties.
3. Prime and Relative Prime Numbers
    - Definition of prime and relative prime numbers and their properties.
4. Extended Euclidean Algorithm
    - Algorithm for finding the greatest common divisor of two numbers.
5. Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) Encryption and Decryption
    - Overview of the AES encryption and decryption algorithm.
6. Fermat’s and Euler’s Theorem
    - Theorem that relates the totient function to modular arithmetic.
7. Primarily Testing
    - Methods for determining whether a number is prime.
8. Chinese Remainder Theorem
    - Theorem that allows us to solve systems of linear congruences.
9. Discrete Logarithmic Problem
    - Problem of finding the exponent to which a given number must be raised to obtain another given number.
10. Principals of Public Key Crypto Systems
    - Overview of public key cryptography and its applications.
11. RSA Algorithm
    - Algorithm for public key encryption and decryption.
12. Security of RSA
    - Discussion of the security of the RSA algorithm and possible attacks.

By studying these topics, you will gain a solid understanding of group theory and its applications in cryptography and network security.
