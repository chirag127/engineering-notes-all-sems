### Triple DES

- Triple DES (3DES or TDES) is a symmetric-key block cipher, which applies the Data Encryption Standard (DES) cipher algorithm three times to each data block .
- Triple DES provides a relatively simple method of increasing the key size of DES to protect against brute-force attacks, without the need to design a completely new block cipher algorithm.
- Triple DES uses three 56-bit keys, resulting in a total key length of 168 bits. However, due to the meet-in-the-middle attack, the effective security of 3DES is only 112 bits.
- Triple DES can operate in various modes, such as Electronic Codebook (ECB), Cipher Block Chaining (CBC), Cipher Feedback (CFB), Output Feedback (OFB), and Counter (CTR).
- Triple DES is considered to be more secure than DES, but less efficient and slower than newer block ciphers, such as AES .
- Triple DES is still widely used in applications that require backward compatibility with legacy systems that use DES, such as banking, payment, and government sectors .
