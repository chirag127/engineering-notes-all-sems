##### 9. To determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid.

1. Take a clean and dry U-tube viscosity manometer. Fill one arm with the given liquid and the other with water.
2. Note the levels of liquid surfaces in both the arms. Let h be the difference in level.
3. Incline the manometer slightly and note the time (t) taken for the meniscus to travel over a certain distance (d) in the liquid under study.
4. Repeat the experiment for water and note the time taken (t0) for the meniscus to travel the same distance (d).
5. Calculate the coefficient of viscosity (ƞ) using the relation:

ƞ = ρgd/ht

Where,
ρ = Density of liquid
g = Acceleration due to gravity

The coefficient of viscosity has no unit. A liquid with high viscosity will have a higher coefficient of viscosity. This method can be used to determine the coefficient of viscosity of liquids that do not react with glass.
