##### 4. To verify Stefan’s law by electric method.

1. Stefan’s law states that the total radiation emitted by a black body is proportional to the fourth power of its absolute temperature.
2. The apparatus consists of a platinum strip heated by an electric current. The platinum strip acts as a black body.
3. The temperature of the platinum strip is measured by a thermocouple. The radiation emitted by the strip is measured by a bolometer.
4. The current through the platinum strip is gradually increased. The temperature and the radiation emitted are noted for different currents.
5. A graph is plotted between the temperature and the radiation. It is found to be a straight line passing through the origin. This verifies Stefan’s law.
6. The proportionality constant of the graph gives the Stefan’s constant which depends only on the material of the radiating body and not on its shape and size.
