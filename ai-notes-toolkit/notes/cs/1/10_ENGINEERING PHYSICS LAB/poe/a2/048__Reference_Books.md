## Reference Books

1. Concepts of Physics by H.C Verma - This book covers all topics of Physics in detail with illustrative examples and problems. It is suitable for students preparing for entrance exams like IIT-JEE.
2. Fundamentals of Physics by Halliday, Resnick and Walker - This is a comprehensive textbook covering all topics of Physics. It provides thorough theoretical explanations as well as real-world applications. It contains examples and practice problems with solutions.
3. Understanding Physics by DC Pandey - This is another popular book for understanding concepts of Physics with a problem-solving approach. It contains objective questions and answers that help in strengthening the understanding of theories and formulas.
4. Formula Book for Physics by M. N. Avadhanulu - This book contains all important formulas of Physics for quick reference. The formulas are arranged chapter-wise and topic-wise for easy look-up. It is a handy book for last-minute revision before exams.
5. Objective Physics by DC Pandey - This book contains a large number of objective questions from all topics of Physics including special topics like Nano and Quantum Physics. It is useful for students preparing for competitive exams. The questions are based on the latest exam patterns.

The above books cover Physics from basic to advanced level and will be helpful references for study and practice. Let me know if you would like me to elaborate on any of the books or provide other recommendations.
