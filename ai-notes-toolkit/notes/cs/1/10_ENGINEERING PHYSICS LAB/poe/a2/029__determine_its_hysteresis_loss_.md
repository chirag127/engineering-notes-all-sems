##### Determine its hysteresis loss:

1. Hysteresis loss occurs in ferromagnetic materials due to delayed response to changes in magnetic field. When the magnetic field is increasing, the magnetization of the material lags behind the magnetic field. Similarly, when the magnetic field is decreasing, the magnetization decreases at a slower rate. This results in a loop called hysteresis loop.
2. The area of the hysteresis loop represents the energy loss per cycle called hysteresis loss. The magnitude of hysteresis loss depends on the material properties and frequency of the applied magnetic field.
3. Hysteresis loss can be reduced by using soft magnetic materials which have narrow hysteresis loops. Also, reducing the frequency of the applied magnetic field will decrease the hysteresis loss. However, both these techniques have their own limitations in practical applications based on the design requirements.
4. Determining the hysteresis loss quantitatively requires the magnetic properties of the material such as saturation magnetization and coercivity. The area of the hysteresis loop can be approximated using analytical expressions or numerical methods. Based on the frequency of the applied field, the total power loss due to hysteresis can be calculated.
