##### 3. To determine the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using polarimeter.

1. Take a clean dry polarimeter tube and fill it with freshly prepared and filtered sugar solution.

2. Place the tube in the polarimeter and adjust the instrument to zero reading.

3. Note the percentage of sugar in the solution by multiplying the Specific gravity with 0.26.

4. Note the reading in the polarimeter scale. Each division in the scale corresponds to 1 degree.

5. Calculate the specific rotation as:

Specific rotation = Observed rotation x Concentration in g/ml

Length of the tube in decimetres x Density in g/ml

6. The specific rotation value for cane sugar is +66.5. The observed value should be close to this if the solution is pure cane sugar.

7. Higher value indicates the presence of more left rotating impurities like glucose. Lower value indicates the presence of more right rotating substances like fructose.
