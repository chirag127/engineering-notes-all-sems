##### 3. To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current:

1. Take a long straight conductor carrying current I.
2. Consider a point P at a distance r from the conductor along its axis.
3. Magnetic field at P due to the current in the conductor is given by:
B = (μ0/2π)I/r
4. As we move away from the conductor along its axis, the distance r increases.
5. According to the above equation, as r increases, the magnetic field B decreases.
6. Hence, the magnetic field due to a current-carrying conductor decreases with increasing distance along its axis.
