##### 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

1. Set up the single slit diffraction apparatus with a He-Ne laser, a narrow slit and a screen.
2. Measure the slit width 'd' with the help of a micrometer scale. Record the value.
3. Switch on the laser and direct the laser beam perpendicularly towards the slit.
4. On varying the slit width, note down the maxima and minima observed on the screen. Measure the distances of the various maxima from the central bright maxima.
5. Calculate the wavelength of the laser light using the formula,
mλ = d(sinθm)
Where,
m = 0, 1, 2, 3.... for maxima and minima
θm = Angles corresponding to maxima/minima
d = Slit width
λ = Wavelength of light
6. Repeat the experiment for different slit widths and calculate the wavelength each time. Take the average of measured wavelengths as the correct wavelength.
