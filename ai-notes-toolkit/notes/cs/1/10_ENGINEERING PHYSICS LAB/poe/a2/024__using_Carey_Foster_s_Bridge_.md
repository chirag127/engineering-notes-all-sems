##### Using Carey Foster's Bridge

- Carey Foster's Bridge is a Wheatstone bridge circuit used to measure resistance.
- It contains four resistances - R1, R2, R3 and RX (unknown resistance).
- R1 = R2 and R3 is a variable resistance.
- When R3 is adjusted so that the potential difference between points A and B is zero, RX = R3.
- This balanced condition is independent of the supply voltage, hence the circuit can be used to measure unknown resistance.
- The bridge is useful for measuring resistances above 1 ohm. For lower resistances, the wire resistances in the circuit become significant and affect the measurement.
- The circuit provides high accuracy and sensitivity in measurement. With a sensitive galvanometer, even small changes in RX can be detected.
