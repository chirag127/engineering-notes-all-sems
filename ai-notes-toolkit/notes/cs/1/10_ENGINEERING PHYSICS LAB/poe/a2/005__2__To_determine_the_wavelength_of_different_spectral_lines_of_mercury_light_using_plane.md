#### 2. To determine the wavelength of different spectral lines of mercury light using plane diffraction grating

1. Take a plane diffraction grating and mount it on a graduated circle.
2. Take a mercury lamp and place it perpendicular to one face of the grating.
3. Allow the light from the mercury lamp to fall on the grating and obtain the diffraction spectrum on a screen.
4. Note down the angles of diffraction corresponding to different spectral lines of mercury.
5. Use the grating formula, nλ = d(sin(i) + sin(r)), to calculate the wavelength of different spectral lines of mercury.

Where,
n = Order of diffraction
d = Spacing between the lines of the grating
i = Incidence angle
r = Angle of diffraction

6. Compare the calculated wavelengths with standard wavelengths of mercury spectral lines.
