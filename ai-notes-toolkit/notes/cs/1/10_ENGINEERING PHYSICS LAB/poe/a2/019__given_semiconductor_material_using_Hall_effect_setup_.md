##### Hall effect setup in a given semiconductor material:

1. Take a rectangular piece of given semiconductor material (e.g. n-type Germanium).
2. Attach electrodes (contacts) at opposite ends to pass current. These are current contacts.
3. Attach electrodes (contacts) on the top and bottom surfaces in the middle. These are known as Hall contacts and are used to measure Hall voltage.
4. Pass electric current I through the semiconductor from the current contacts.
5. Apply a magnetic field B perpendicular to the direction of current flow using permanent magnets.
6. Measure the voltage VH between the Hall contacts using a high impedance voltmeter.
7. Calculate the Hall coefficient RH using the relation: RH = VH/BI
8. Determine the type of charge carriers (electrons/holes) based on the sign of Hall coefficient and their density using the relation: n = 1/eRH

The Hall effect setup can be used to determine the sign, density and mobility of charge carriers in a given semiconductor material. The measurements can be done at varying magnetic field strengths and current densities to obtain more information about the properties of charge carriers in the material.
