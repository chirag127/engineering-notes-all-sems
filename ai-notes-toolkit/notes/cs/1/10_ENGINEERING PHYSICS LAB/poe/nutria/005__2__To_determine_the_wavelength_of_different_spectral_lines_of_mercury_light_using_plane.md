
##### 2. To determine the wavelength of different spectral lines of mercury light using plane

1. The wavelength of light emitted by mercury can be determined by using a plane grating.
2. A plane grating is a device that consists of a number of closely spaced, parallel, equidistant grooves.
3. When light passes through the grooves, it is diffracted into its component wavelengths.
4. The angle of diffraction is related to the wavelength of the light.
5. By measuring the angle of diffraction, the wavelength of the light can be determined.
6. This method can be used to determine the wavelength of the spectral lines of mercury light.
