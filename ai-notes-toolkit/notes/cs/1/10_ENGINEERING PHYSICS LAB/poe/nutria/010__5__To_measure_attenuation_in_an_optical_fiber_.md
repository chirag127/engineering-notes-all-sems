
##### 5. To measure attenuation in an optical fiber

1. Attenuation in an optical fiber can be measured using an optical time-domain reflectometer (OTDR).
2. OTDR works by sending a short pulse of light into the fiber and then measuring the amount of light that is reflected back.
3. The amount of light that is reflected back is proportional to the amount of attenuation in the fiber.
4. OTDR can measure the attenuation of a single fiber or a group of fibers.
5. OTDR can also be used to measure the length of the fiber and the location of any breaks or defects in the fiber.
