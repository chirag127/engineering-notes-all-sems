
##### Transmission Grating

* Transmission gratings are optical components used to separate light into its component wavelengths. 
* They work by diffracting light into its component wavelengths, which can then be used to analyze the spectrum of the light. 
* Transmission gratings are made up of a series of closely spaced, parallel, and equidistant grooves, which are etched or ruled onto a substrate. 
* The grooves act as diffraction gratings, causing the light to be split into its component wavelengths. 
* The spacing of the grooves is typically between 500 and 1500 lines per millimeter, and the grating can be blazed to optimize the diffraction efficiency. 
* Transmission gratings can be used in a variety of applications, such as spectroscopy, optical communications, and astronomy.
