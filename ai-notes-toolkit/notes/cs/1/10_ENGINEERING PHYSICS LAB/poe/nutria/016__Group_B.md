

#### Group B

1. Group B of the periodic table consists of elements with atomic numbers from 21 to 30.
2. These elements are characterized by having two electrons in their outermost shell.
3. Group B elements are also known as transition elements.
4. The most common elements in this group are iron, nickel, cobalt and copper.
5. These elements are important for their use in the production of steel and other alloys.
6. Other uses of Group B elements include catalysts, electrical conductors, and magnets.
7. Group B elements are also essential for life, as they are involved in the metabolism of proteins and other molecules.
