
##### 4. To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance

1. When two lenses are placed in contact with each other, the focal length of the combination is equal to the sum of the focal length of the individual lenses. 
2. When two lenses are separated by a distance, the focal length of the combination is equal to the sum of the focal length of the individual lenses, divided by one plus the product of the focal length of each lens and the distance between them.
3. This formula can be used to calculate the focal length of the combination of two lenses: 

F = (F1 + F2) / (1 + (F1 * F2 * d))

Where: 

F = Focal length of the combination

F1 = Focal length of first lens

F2 = Focal length of second lens

d = Distance between the two lenses
