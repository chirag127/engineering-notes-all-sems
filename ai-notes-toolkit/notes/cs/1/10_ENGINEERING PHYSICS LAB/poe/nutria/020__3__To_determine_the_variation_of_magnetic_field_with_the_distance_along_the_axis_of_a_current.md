

##### 3. To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current
1. The magnetic field produced by a current-carrying wire decreases as the distance from the wire increases.
2. The magnetic field produced by a long, straight wire carrying a current can be determined by using the equation B = μo*I/2πr, where B is the magnetic field strength, μo is the permeability of free space (4π x 10^-7 Tm/A), I is the current in the wire and r is the distance from the wire.
3. The magnetic field strength is proportional to the current in the wire, and inversely proportional to the distance from the wire.
4. The direction of the magnetic field can be determined using the right-hand rule.
