
##### 9. To draw hysteresis (B-H curve) of a specimen in the form of a transformer and to

1. Hysteresis is the phenomenon of a material's magnetization or demagnetization not being directly proportional to the applied magnetic field.
2. The hysteresis loop of a specimen in the form of a transformer is a graph that shows the relationship between the applied magnetic field (B) and the magnetization (H) of the specimen.
3. The hysteresis loop of a transformer can be drawn by plotting the magnetization against the applied magnetic field.
4. The shape of the hysteresis loop is determined by the properties of the material used in the transformer.
5. Generally, the hysteresis loop of a transformer follows a curved path, which is known as the B-H curve.
6. The B-H curve of a transformer can be used to determine the magnetic properties of the transformer, such as its coercivity, remanence, and maximum energy product.
