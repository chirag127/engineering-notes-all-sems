
### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

1. Practical Physics is a book written by K. K. Dey and B. N. Dutta, and published by Kalyani Publishers in New Delhi.
2. This book provides a comprehensive introduction to the subject of practical physics, with topics ranging from basic concepts such as force, motion, and energy to more advanced topics such as thermodynamics, optics, and electricity.
3. The book is written in a clear and concise manner, with diagrams and illustrations to aid understanding.
4. It includes a variety of exercises and problems to help students master the material.
5. The book is suitable for students studying physics at the undergraduate level, as well as those studying for competitive examinations such as IIT-JEE and NEET.
