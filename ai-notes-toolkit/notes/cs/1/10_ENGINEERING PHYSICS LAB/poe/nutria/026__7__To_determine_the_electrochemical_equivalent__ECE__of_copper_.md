
##### 7. To determine the electrochemical equivalent (ECE) of copper

1. The electrochemical equivalent (ECE) of a substance is the amount of the substance that is oxidized or reduced when one ampere of current is passed through it for one second.
2. The electrochemical equivalent (ECE) of copper is determined by passing a current of 1 ampere for 1 second through a solution of copper sulphate.
3. The amount of copper deposited or oxidized at the electrodes is measured in terms of its mass.
4. The mass of copper deposited or oxidized at the electrodes is then divided by the time taken for the deposition or oxidation to take place. This gives the value of the electrochemical equivalent (ECE) of copper.
5. The electrochemical equivalent (ECE) of copper is equal to the amount of copper deposited or oxidized at the electrodes per unit time.
