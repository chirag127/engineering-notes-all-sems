
##### 8. To determine the wavelength of sodium light with the help of Fresnel’s bi-prism
1. Fresnel’s bi-prism is an optical device used to split a beam of light into two beams of equal intensity. 
2. When a beam of monochromatic light is incident on the bi-prism, two beams of equal intensity are formed, one of which is deviated through an angle θ.
3. The angle θ is related to the wavelength of the incident light, and can be calculated using the formula: λ = λ0 sinθ.
4. To determine the wavelength of sodium light with the help of Fresnel’s bi-prism, a beam of sodium light is incident on the bi-prism and the angle θ is measured. 
5. The wavelength of the incident light can then be calculated using the formula λ = λ0 sinθ, where λ0 is the known wavelength of sodium light.
