
##### Focal Length of Combination of Lenses

1. Focal length is the distance between the lens and the image plane when the object is at infinity.
2. The focal length of a combination of lenses is equal to the sum of the focal lengths of the individual lenses.
3. The focal length of a combination of lenses can also be calculated using the formula: 
    $$F = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{f_1} + \frac{1}{f_2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{f_n}}$$
where $f_1$, $f_2$, $\cdots$, $f_n$ are the focal lengths of the individual lenses.
4. The focal length of a combination of lenses can also be calculated using the lens maker's equation:
    $$\frac{1}{F} = \frac{1}{f_1} + \frac{1}{f_2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{f_n}$$
5. The effective focal length of a combination of lenses is equal to the sum of the magnifications of the individual lenses.
6. The effective focal length of a combination of lenses can also be calculated using the formula: 
    $$F_{eff} = f_1 + f_2 + \cdots + f_n \times M_1 \times M_2 \times \cdots \times M_n$$
where $f_1$, $f_2$, $\cdots$, $f_n$ are the focal lengths of the individual lenses and $M_1$, $M_2$, $\cdots$, $M_n$ are the magnifications of the individual lenses.
