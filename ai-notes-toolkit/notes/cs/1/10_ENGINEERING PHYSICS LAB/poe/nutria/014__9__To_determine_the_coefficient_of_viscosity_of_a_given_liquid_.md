
##### 9. To determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid

1. Prepare the liquid sample for testing.
2. Place the liquid sample in a viscometer.
3. Measure the time it takes for the liquid to flow through the viscometer.
4. Calculate the coefficient of viscosity of the liquid using the formula: η = F/A, where F is the force of the liquid and A is the area of the viscometer.
5. Record the results and compare them to the expected values.
