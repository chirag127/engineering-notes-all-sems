

#### CO CO Statement Bloom's

1. The Cognitive-Constructivist Statement of Bloom's Taxonomy (CO CO) is a revision of the original Bloom's Taxonomy of Educational Objectives.

2. The CO CO statement defines six cognitive processes that are essential for learning: remembering, understanding, applying, analyzing, evaluating, and creating.

3. The CO CO statement also identifies three domains of learning: cognitive, affective, and psychomotor.

4. The cognitive domain focuses on the development of intellectual abilities, such as knowledge, comprehension, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation.

5. The affective domain focuses on the development of attitudes, values, and beliefs.

6. The psychomotor domain focuses on the development of physical skills, such as manual dexterity and coordination.

7. The CO CO statement emphasizes the importance of both cognitive and affective processes in learning.

8. The CO CO statement also emphasizes the importance of considering the context in which learning takes place.
