
#### CO-1 Apply the Principle of Interference and Diffraction to Find the Wavelength of

1. Interference of light occurs when two light waves overlap or combine. When the two waves are in phase, they add together to form a wave with a larger amplitude. When they are out of phase, they subtract from each other and form a wave with a smaller amplitude.

2. Diffraction occurs when a wave passes through a narrow opening or around an obstacle. The wave spreads out, and the wavelength decreases.

3. The wavelength of a light wave can be found by measuring the distance between successive maxima or minima. This is known as the fringe spacing.

4. The fringe spacing is related to the wavelength by the equation:

λ = d/n

where λ is the wavelength, d is the fringe spacing, and n is the order of the interference or diffraction pattern.

5. In an interference pattern, n is an integer. In a diffraction pattern, n is related to the angle of diffraction.
