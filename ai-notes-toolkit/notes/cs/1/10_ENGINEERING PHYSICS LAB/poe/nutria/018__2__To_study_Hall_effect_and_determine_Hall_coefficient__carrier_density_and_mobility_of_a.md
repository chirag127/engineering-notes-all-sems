
##### 2. To study Hall effect and determine Hall coefficient, carrier density and mobility of a

1. Hall effect is a phenomenon in which a transverse electric field is generated in a conductor when exposed to a magnetic field in the perpendicular direction.

2. Hall coefficient is a physical quantity which is used to measure the strength of the Hall effect. It is defined as the ratio of the transverse electric field to the product of the current density and the applied magnetic field.

3. Carrier density is the number of charge carriers per unit volume of a material. It is an important parameter that determines the electrical conductivity of a material.

4. Mobility is the measure of how easily charge carriers can move through a material. It is defined as the ratio of the drift velocity of the charge carriers to the applied electric field.

5. The Hall effect can be used to measure the carrier density and mobility of a material. This is done by measuring the Hall voltage when a current is passed through a sample in the presence of a magnetic field. The Hall coefficient, carrier density and mobility can then be calculated from the Hall voltage.
