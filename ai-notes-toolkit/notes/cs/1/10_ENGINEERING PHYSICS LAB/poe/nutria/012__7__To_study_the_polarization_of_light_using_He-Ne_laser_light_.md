

##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light

1. Polarization of light is a phenomenon in which the direction of the electric field of a light wave oscillates in a single plane.
2. The He-Ne laser is a type of laser which produces light in a single wavelength and is used to study light polarization.
3. The He-Ne laser is a gas laser which uses a mixture of helium and neon gas to produce the light.
4. The light emitted by the He-Ne laser is linearly polarized, meaning that the electric field of the light wave oscillates in a single plane.
5. The polarization of light can be studied by using polarizers, which are devices which allow only light with a certain polarization to pass through.
6. The He-Ne laser can be used to study the effect of polarizers on light. For example, by using two polarizers, the intensity of the light can be reduced to zero.
7. The He-Ne laser can also be used to study the effect of birefringent materials on light. Birefringent materials are materials which have different indices of refraction for light polarized in different directions.
8. The He-Ne laser can also be used to study the effect of optical activity on light, which is the ability of a material to rotate the plane of polarization of light.
