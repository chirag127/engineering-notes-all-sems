## To Study Hall Effect and Determine Hall Coefficient, Carrier Density and Mobility of a

The Hall effect is a phenomenon where a magnetic field influences the motion of charged particles. This effect is used to measure the properties of semiconductors and other materials. In this experiment, we will study the Hall effect to determine the Hall coefficient, carrier density, and mobility of a semiconductor sample.

### Materials Required

- Semiconductor sample
- Power supply
- Magnet
- Gaussmeter
- Current source
- Voltage source
- Resistance meter
- Ammeter

### Procedure

1. Take the semiconductor sample and connect it to a power supply.
2. Apply a magnetic field to the sample using a magnet.
3. Measure the magnetic field strength using a gaussmeter.
4. Connect a current source to the sample and measure the current using an ammeter.
5. Connect a voltage source across the sample and measure the voltage using a voltmeter.
6. Measure the resistance of the sample using a resistance meter.
7. Repeat steps 4-6 with varying magnetic fields.
8. Plot a graph of magnetic field strength versus voltage.
9. Determine the Hall coefficient from the slope of the graph.
10. Calculate the carrier density using the equation: n = 1/(q*R_H)
11. Calculate the mobility using the equation: μ = σ/(q*n)

### Calculations

- Hall coefficient: R_H = V_H/(I*B)
- Carrier density: n = 1/(q*R_H)
- Mobility: μ = σ/(q*n)

where V_H is the Hall voltage, I is the current, B is the magnetic field strength, q is the charge of an electron, and σ is the conductivity of the sample.

### Results

The Hall coefficient, carrier density, and mobility can be determined from the experimental data and calculations. These parameters provide valuable information about the semiconductor sample and its properties. The results can be compared to the known values of similar materials to validate the accuracy of the experiment.

### Conclusion

The Hall effect is a useful tool for measuring the properties of semiconductors and other materials. By studying the Hall effect, we can determine the Hall coefficient, carrier density, and mobility of a sample. These parameters provide valuable information about the sample and its properties, which can be used in various applications.
