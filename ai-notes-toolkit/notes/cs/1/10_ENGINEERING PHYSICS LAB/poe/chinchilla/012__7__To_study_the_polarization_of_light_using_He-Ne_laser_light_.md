##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

In this experiment, we will use a He-Ne laser light to study the polarization of light. The aim of this experiment is to understand the concept of polarization and how it can be used in various applications.

The following are the steps involved in the experiment:

1. Set up the He-Ne laser: Start by setting up the He-Ne laser and aligning it properly. The laser should be placed on a stable surface and aligned so that the beam is straight and parallel to the table.

2. Introduce a polarizer: Introduce a polarizer into the path of the laser beam. The polarizer should be placed at a 45-degree angle to the beam. This will allow us to study the polarization of the light.

3. Observe the polarization of the light: Observe the light that passes through the polarizer. Rotate the polarizer and observe how the intensity of the light changes. This will help us understand the concept of polarization and how it affects the intensity of light.

4. Introduce a second polarizer: Introduce a second polarizer into the path of the light after it passes through the first polarizer. This polarizer should be placed at a 90-degree angle to the first polarizer.

5. Observe the polarization of the light after passing through two polarizers: Observe the light that passes through the second polarizer. Rotate the second polarizer and observe how the intensity of the light changes. This will help us understand how two polarizers can be used to study the polarization of light.

6. Analyze the results: Analyze the results of the experiment and draw conclusions about the polarization of light. This will help us understand how polarization can be used in various applications such as 3D movies or polarized sunglasses.

In conclusion, the study of polarization of light using He-Ne laser light is an important experiment that helps students understand the concept of polarization and its various applications. By following the steps outlined above, students can gain a better understanding of this important concept.
