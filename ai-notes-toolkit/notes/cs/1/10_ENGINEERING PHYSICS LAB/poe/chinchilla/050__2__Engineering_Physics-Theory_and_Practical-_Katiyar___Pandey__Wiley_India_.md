### 2. Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

In this book, you will gain knowledge about the fundamentals of physics and their practical applications in engineering. The book is divided into two sections: theory and practical. Here are some of the topics covered in each section:

#### Theory:

1. Mechanics: This section covers the laws of motion, work-energy theorem, conservation of energy, and rotational dynamics.

2. Waves and Optics: Here, you will learn about wave motion, interference, diffraction, polarization, and optical instruments.

3. Electricity and Magnetism: This section covers Coulomb's law, electric field, Gauss's law, electric potential, capacitance, current, resistance, and magnetic effects of current.

4. Modern Physics: In this section, you will learn about special theory of relativity, photoelectric effect, atomic structure, and nuclear physics.

#### Practical:

1. Mechanics: Here, you will learn about the measurement of length, time, and mass, and their practical applications.

2. Waves and Optics: This section covers the measurement of wavelength, frequency, and speed of sound, and their practical applications.

3. Electricity and Magnetism: In this section, you will learn about the measurement of resistance, capacitance, and current, and their practical applications.

4. Modern Physics: Here, you will learn about the measurement of Planck's constant, photoelectric effect, and the study of radioactivity.

In addition to the above topics, the book also covers experiments related to the above topics, which will help you understand the concepts better. Overall, this book is an excellent resource for engineering students who want to gain a strong foundation in physics and its practical applications in engineering.
