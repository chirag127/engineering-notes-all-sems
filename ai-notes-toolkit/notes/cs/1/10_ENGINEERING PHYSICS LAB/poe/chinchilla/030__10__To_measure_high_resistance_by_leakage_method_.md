##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

Measuring high resistance is a crucial task in various industries and applications. The leakage method is one of the ways to measure high resistance accurately. In this method, the resistance of an insulator is measured by applying a voltage and measuring the current that flows through it.

Here are the steps to measure high resistance by the leakage method:

1. Prepare the equipment: You will need a high-voltage source, a sensitive ammeter, and the object whose resistance you want to measure.

2. Connect the high voltage source: Connect the high voltage source to the object whose resistance you want to measure.

3. Measure the current: Measure the current that flows through the object using the sensitive ammeter.

4. Calculate the resistance: Calculate the resistance of the object using Ohm's law. The resistance can be calculated by dividing the voltage applied to the object by the current that flows through it.

5. Repeat the measurement: Repeat the measurement multiple times to ensure accuracy.

6. Take precautions: Take necessary precautions while performing the measurement, such as wearing protective gear, grounding the object, and keeping a safe distance from the high-voltage source.

7. Record the results: Record the results of the measurement accurately, including the voltage applied, current measured, and calculated resistance.

By following these steps, you can measure high resistance accurately using the leakage method. It is important to note that this method is suitable for measuring resistances in the range of 1 megaohm to 100 gigaohms.
