#### CO-3 Verify different established laws with the help of optical and electrical

There are several established laws in the fields of optics and electricity that can be verified through experiments and observations. Some of these laws include:

1. **Ohm's Law**: This law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage applied across it, provided the temperature remains constant. This can be verified by measuring the current and voltage across a conductor and plotting a graph to show the linear relationship.

2. **Snell's Law**: This law describes the relationship between the angles of incidence and refraction when light passes through a boundary between two different media. This can be verified by shining a light through a transparent medium and measuring the angles of incidence and refraction.

3. **Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction**: This law states that a change in the magnetic field within a closed loop of wire will induce an electromotive force (EMF) in the wire. This can be verified by moving a magnet near a coil of wire and measuring the induced EMF.

4. **The Law of Reflection**: This law states that the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection when light is reflected off a smooth surface. This can be verified by shining a light onto a smooth surface and measuring the angles of incidence and reflection.

These are just a few examples of the many established laws in the fields of optics and electricity that can be verified through experiments and observations. It is important to understand and verify these laws in order to have a deeper understanding of the principles that govern the behavior of light and electricity.
