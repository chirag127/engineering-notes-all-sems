### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- Practical Physics is a book written by K. K. Dey and B. N. Dutta.
- It is published by Kalyani Publishers, New Delhi.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to the practical aspects of physics.
- It covers a wide range of topics, including mechanics, heat, light, sound, electricity, and magnetism.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand for students.
- It includes numerous examples, illustrations, and exercises to help students apply the concepts they have learned.
- Practical Physics is an essential resource for students studying physics at the undergraduate level.
