### At the end of the course, students will be able to:

1. Demonstrate an understanding of the key concepts and principles of the subject matter.
2. Apply the knowledge and skills acquired in the course to solve problems and make informed decisions.
3. Communicate effectively using the language and terminology of the subject matter.
4. Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
5. Think critically and creatively to analyze and evaluate information and ideas.
6. Demonstrate a commitment to ongoing learning and professional development.
