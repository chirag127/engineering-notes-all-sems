##### 3. To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current

The magnetic field produced by a current-carrying wire can be determined using the Biot-Savart law. This law states that the magnetic field at a point is directly proportional to the current and inversely proportional to the square of the distance from the wire.

To determine the variation of the magnetic field with distance along the axis of a current-carrying wire, the following steps can be followed:

1. Set up the apparatus, including a current-carrying wire, a magnetic field sensor, and a power supply.
2. Turn on the power supply and adjust the current to the desired value.
3. Place the magnetic field sensor at different distances along the axis of the wire and record the magnetic field readings.
4. Plot a graph of the magnetic field against the distance from the wire.
5. From the graph, it can be observed that the magnetic field decreases as the distance from the wire increases.

This experiment can be used to verify the inverse square law relationship between the magnetic field and the distance from the wire. It can also be used to determine the magnetic field strength at different points along the axis of the wire.
