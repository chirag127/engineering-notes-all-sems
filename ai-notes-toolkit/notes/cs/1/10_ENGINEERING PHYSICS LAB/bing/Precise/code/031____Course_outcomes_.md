## Course outcomes:
- Course outcomes are statements that describe the knowledge, skills, and abilities that students should have acquired by the end of a course.
- They are used to guide the design of the course, including the selection of course content, teaching methods, and assessments.
- Course outcomes should be specific, measurable, and achievable within the scope of the course.
- They should be aligned with the overall goals of the program and the institution.
- Course outcomes can be used to evaluate the effectiveness of the course and to identify areas for improvement.
- They can also be used to communicate the value of the course to students, employers, and other stakeholders.
- Course outcomes should be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure that they remain relevant and aligned with the needs of students and the broader community.
