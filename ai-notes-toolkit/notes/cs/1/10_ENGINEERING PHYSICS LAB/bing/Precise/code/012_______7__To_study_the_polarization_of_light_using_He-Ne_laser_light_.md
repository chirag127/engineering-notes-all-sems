##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

Polarization refers to the orientation of the oscillations of an electromagnetic wave. Light is an electromagnetic wave, and its oscillations can be oriented in different directions. When light is polarized, its oscillations are oriented in a specific direction.

A He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as the lasing medium. The light emitted by a He-Ne laser is highly coherent and monochromatic, making it ideal for studying the polarization of light.

To study the polarization of light using a He-Ne laser, the following steps can be followed:

1. Set up the He-Ne laser and turn it on, allowing it to warm up for a few minutes.
2. Place a polarizer in the path of the laser beam. A polarizer is an optical device that transmits light of a specific polarization and blocks light of other polarizations.
3. Rotate the polarizer and observe the changes in the intensity of the laser beam. The intensity of the laser beam will vary as the polarizer is rotated, depending on the orientation of the polarizer relative to the polarization of the laser light.
4. Record the intensity of the laser beam as a function of the angle of the polarizer.
5. Analyze the data to determine the polarization of the laser light.

By following these steps, one can study the polarization of light using a He-Ne laser. This experiment can provide valuable insights into the properties of polarized light and its interactions with optical devices.
