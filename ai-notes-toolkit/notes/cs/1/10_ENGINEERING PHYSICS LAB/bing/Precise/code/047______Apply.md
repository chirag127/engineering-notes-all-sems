#### Apply

- The term "apply" can have several meanings depending on the context in which it is used.
- It can mean to make a formal request, usually in writing, for something such as a job, admission to a school, or a loan.
- It can also mean to put or spread something, such as a substance or a force, onto or over something else.
- In mathematics, it can mean to use a rule or formula to calculate or solve a problem.
- In computer science, it can refer to the process of using a function or operation to a set of data or arguments.
- In general, the term "apply" refers to the act of putting something into action or practice.
