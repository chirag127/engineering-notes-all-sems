#### Apply

1. The term "apply" can refer to the act of putting something into operation or use, such as applying a rule or a principle.
2. It can also refer to the act of making a formal request or submission, such as applying for a job or a loan.
3. In the context of mathematics, "apply" can refer to the act of using a mathematical operation or function to a given value or set of values.
4. In the context of computer science, "apply" can refer to the act of calling a function with a given set of arguments.
5. In general, "apply" refers to the act of putting something into action or use, or making a request or submission.
