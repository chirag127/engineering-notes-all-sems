##### 9. To determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid.

Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. The coefficient of viscosity is a property of a fluid that determines the amount of internal resistance to flow. There are several methods to determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid, including:

1. **Ostwald viscometer:** This method involves measuring the time it takes for a fixed volume of liquid to flow through a narrow tube under the influence of gravity. The coefficient of viscosity can be calculated using the Hagen-Poiseuille equation.

2. **Falling sphere viscometer:** This method involves measuring the terminal velocity of a sphere falling through a liquid. The coefficient of viscosity can be calculated using Stokes' law.

3. **Rotational viscometer:** This method involves measuring the torque required to rotate a spindle at a constant speed in a liquid. The coefficient of viscosity can be calculated using the relationship between torque, spindle geometry, and rotational speed.

Each of these methods has its own advantages and limitations, and the choice of method depends on the specific properties of the liquid being tested and the accuracy required for the measurement. It is important to carefully calibrate the equipment and follow standard procedures to obtain accurate results.
