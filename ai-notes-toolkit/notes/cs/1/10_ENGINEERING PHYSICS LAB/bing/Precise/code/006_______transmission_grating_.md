##### Transmission Grating

- Transmission gratings are designed to disperse incident light at specific angles. 
- Angular dispersion is a function of angle of incidence and groove spacing. 
- Dispersion increases as angle of incidence increases or as groove spacing decreases .
- Transmission gratings are manufactured in the same way as ruled reflection gratings in which a replica of a master grating is created by duplicating its grooved surface with a thin, vacuum deposition-coated layer, except they have no reflective overcoat .
- Transmission gratings offer low polarization sensitivity as opposed to reflection gratings because incident light is not reflected by a mirror coating such as aluminum .
- Since light is transmitted through the grating, transmission gratings can be used in compact, in-line configurations .
- Transmission gratings can be volume Bragg gratings or surface relief gratings .
- Transmission gratings operate to angularly disperse incident light into a spectrum .
