##### 2. To determine the wavelength of different spectral lines of mercury light using plane

- Mercury light is a type of light source that emits light when an electric current is passed through mercury vapor.
- The light emitted by mercury light contains several distinct spectral lines, each corresponding to a specific wavelength of light.
- To determine the wavelength of these spectral lines, a device called a spectrometer can be used.
- A spectrometer works by separating light into its constituent wavelengths using a diffraction grating or a prism.
- The light is then focused onto a detector, which measures the intensity of light at each wavelength.
- By analyzing the intensity of light at each wavelength, the wavelengths of the different spectral lines of mercury light can be determined.
- This information can be used for various applications, such as calibrating other instruments or studying the properties of mercury vapor.
