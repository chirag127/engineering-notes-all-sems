##### 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

1. The experiment involves shining a He-Ne laser beam through a single slit and observing the diffraction pattern produced on a screen.
2. The distance between the central maximum and the first minimum of the diffraction pattern is measured.
3. The slit width and the distance between the slit and the screen are also measured.
4. Using the formula for single slit diffraction, the wavelength of the He-Ne laser light can be calculated.
5. The formula for single slit diffraction is: λ = (a * sinθ)/m, where λ is the wavelength of the light, a is the width of the slit, θ is the angle of diffraction, and m is the order of the minimum.
6. By substituting the measured values into the formula, the wavelength of the He-Ne laser light can be determined.
