#### CO-3 Verify different established laws with the help of optical and electrical

There are several established laws in the field of optics and electricity that can be verified through experiments and observations. Some of these laws include:

1. **Ohm's Law**: This law states that the current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage applied across it, provided the temperature remains constant. This law can be verified by measuring the current and voltage across a conductor and plotting a graph to show the linear relationship between the two.

2. **Snell's Law**: This law describes the relationship between the angles of incidence and refraction when light passes through a boundary between two different media. This law can be verified by measuring the angles of incidence and refraction when light passes through a transparent material such as glass or water.

3. **Kirchhoff's Laws**: These laws describe the relationship between the currents and voltages in an electrical circuit. Kirchhoff's Current Law states that the sum of the currents entering a node in a circuit is equal to the sum of the currents leaving the node. Kirchhoff's Voltage Law states that the sum of the voltage drops around a closed loop in a circuit is equal to the sum of the voltage rises. These laws can be verified by measuring the currents and voltages in a circuit and checking that they satisfy the equations.

4. **Law of Reflection**: This law states that the angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection when light is reflected from a smooth surface. This law can be verified by measuring the angles of incidence and reflection when light is reflected from a mirror or other smooth surface.

These are just a few examples of the many established laws in the field of optics and electricity that can be verified through experiments and observations. By verifying these laws, we can gain a better understanding of the fundamental principles that govern the behavior of light and electricity.
