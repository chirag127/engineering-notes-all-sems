##### 8. To determine the wavelength of sodium light with the help of Fresnel’s bi-prism.

Fresnel's bi-prism is an optical instrument used to determine the wavelength of light. It consists of two prisms placed base to base, forming a bi-prism. When a beam of light passes through the bi-prism, it is split into two beams, which interfere with each other to produce an interference pattern.

To determine the wavelength of sodium light using Fresnel's bi-prism, the following steps can be followed:

1. Set up the bi-prism experiment by placing the bi-prism in the path of a collimated beam of sodium light.
2. Adjust the position of the bi-prism and the eyepiece to obtain a clear interference pattern.
3. Measure the distance between the central bright fringe and a fringe of known order.
4. Calculate the fringe width using the formula: fringe width = (distance between fringes) / (order of fringe).
5. Use the formula for the wavelength of light in a bi-prism experiment to calculate the wavelength of sodium light: wavelength = (fringe width * distance between slit and eyepiece) / (distance between virtual sources).

By following these steps, the wavelength of sodium light can be accurately determined using Fresnel's bi-prism. It is important to note that the accuracy of the measurement depends on the precision of the measurements and the quality of the experimental setup.
