#### Apply

1. The word "apply" can have several meanings, depending on the context in which it is used.
2. It can mean to make a formal request, usually in writing, for something such as a job, a loan, or admission to a school or university.
3. It can also mean to put or spread something onto a surface, such as applying paint to a wall or applying lotion to the skin.
4. Another meaning of "apply" is to use or make use of something, such as applying knowledge or skills to solve a problem.
5. In mathematics, "apply" can mean to use a mathematical operation or formula to a given set of numbers or variables.
6. In general, "apply" refers to the act of putting something into action or practice, or making use of something for a specific purpose.
