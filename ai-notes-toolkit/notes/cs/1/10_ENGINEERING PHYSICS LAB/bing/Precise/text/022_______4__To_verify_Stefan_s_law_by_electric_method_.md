##### 4. To verify Stefan’s law by electric method.

Stefan's law states that the total energy radiated per unit surface area of a black body in unit time is directly proportional to the fourth power of the absolute temperature of the black body. The law can be verified by the electric method, which involves the following steps:

1. A black body is heated by passing an electric current through it, and its temperature is measured using a thermometer.
2. The temperature of the black body is gradually increased by increasing the electric current, and the corresponding increase in the energy radiated by the black body is measured using a radiometer.
3. A graph is plotted between the fourth power of the absolute temperature of the black body and the energy radiated by it.
4. If the graph is a straight line passing through the origin, it confirms that the energy radiated by the black body is directly proportional to the fourth power of its absolute temperature, thus verifying Stefan's law.
