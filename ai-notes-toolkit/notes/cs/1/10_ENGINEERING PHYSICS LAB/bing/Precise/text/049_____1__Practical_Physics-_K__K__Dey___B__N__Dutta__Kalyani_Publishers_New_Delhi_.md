### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- "Practical Physics" is a book written by K. K. Dey and B. N. Dutta.
- It is published by Kalyani Publishers, New Delhi.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to practical physics experiments and measurements.
- It covers a wide range of topics, including mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, and magnetism.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, with numerous diagrams and illustrations to aid understanding.
- It is a valuable resource for students studying physics at the undergraduate level.
- The book provides detailed instructions for performing experiments and taking measurements, as well as guidance on how to analyze and interpret data.
- It is a useful reference for both students and instructors.
