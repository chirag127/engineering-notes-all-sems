##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

- Polarization refers to the orientation of the oscillations of an electromagnetic wave, such as light.
- Light can be polarized by passing it through a polarizing filter, which only allows light waves oscillating in a specific direction to pass through.
- A He-Ne laser emits light that is already polarized, due to the nature of the laser's construction and operation.
- The polarization of the He-Ne laser light can be studied by passing it through a polarizing filter and observing the intensity of the transmitted light.
- By rotating the polarizing filter, the transmitted intensity will vary, reaching a maximum when the filter's transmission axis is aligned with the polarization direction of the laser light, and a minimum when the transmission axis is perpendicular to the polarization direction.
- This experiment can be used to demonstrate the concept of polarization and to measure the polarization direction of the He-Ne laser light.
