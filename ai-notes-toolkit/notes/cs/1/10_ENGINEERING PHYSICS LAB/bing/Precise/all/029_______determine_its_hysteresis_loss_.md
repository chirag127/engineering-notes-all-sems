##### Determine its Hysteresis Loss

Hysteresis loss is the energy lost in the form of heat when a magnetic material is subjected to a changing magnetic field. It is a measure of the energy dissipated due to the magnetic hysteresis of the material. Here are the steps to determine the hysteresis loss of a magnetic material:

1. Obtain a sample of the magnetic material and measure its mass and volume.
2. Subject the sample to a changing magnetic field by placing it in a coil of wire carrying an alternating current.
3. Measure the magnetic field strength and the magnetic flux density of the sample at various points in the cycle.
4. Plot the magnetic field strength against the magnetic flux density to obtain the hysteresis loop of the material.
5. Calculate the area enclosed by the hysteresis loop. This area represents the energy lost per cycle due to hysteresis.
6. Multiply the area of the hysteresis loop by the frequency of the alternating current to obtain the hysteresis loss per unit time.
7. Divide the hysteresis loss per unit time by the volume of the sample to obtain the hysteresis loss per unit volume.
