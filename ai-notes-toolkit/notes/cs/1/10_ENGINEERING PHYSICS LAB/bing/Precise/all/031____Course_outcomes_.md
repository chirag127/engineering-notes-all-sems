## Course outcomes:

1. Understanding of the subject matter: Students will gain a thorough understanding of the course material and be able to apply the concepts and principles learned in the course to real-world situations.

2. Development of critical thinking skills: Students will learn to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information to make informed decisions and solve problems.

3. Improvement of communication skills: Students will learn to effectively communicate their ideas and thoughts through written and oral presentations.

4. Enhancement of teamwork and collaboration skills: Students will learn to work effectively in groups and collaborate with others to achieve common goals.

5. Acquisition of practical skills: Students will gain hands-on experience and develop practical skills relevant to their field of study.

6. Preparation for further study or career: Students will be prepared for further study or entry into the workforce in their chosen field.

7. Personal growth and development: Students will have the opportunity to explore their interests, develop their strengths, and grow as individuals.
