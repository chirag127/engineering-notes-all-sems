#### Apply

1. The term "apply" can refer to the act of putting something into operation or use, such as applying a rule or principle.
2. It can also refer to the act of making a formal request or submission, such as applying for a job or a loan.
3. In the context of mathematics or computing, "apply" can refer to the act of using a function or operation to a set of values or arguments.
4. In the context of physical sciences, "apply" can refer to the act of exerting force or pressure on an object, such as applying pressure to a wound.
5. In general, "apply" refers to the act of putting something into action or practice, or making use of something for a specific purpose.
