##### 6. To study the resonance condition of a series LCR circuit.

A series LCR circuit consists of an inductor (L), a capacitor (C), and a resistor (R) connected in series. The resonance condition of a series LCR circuit occurs when the inductive reactance (XL) equals the capacitive reactance (XC). At resonance, the circuit behaves as a purely resistive circuit, and the current in the circuit is maximum.

The resonance condition can be mathematically expressed as:

XL = XC
2πfL = 1 / (2πfC)
f = 1 / (2π√(LC))

Where f is the resonant frequency, L is the inductance, and C is the capacitance.

The resonance condition can be experimentally verified by measuring the current in the circuit as the frequency of the applied voltage is varied. At resonance, the current in the circuit will be maximum.

In summary, the resonance condition of a series LCR circuit occurs when the inductive reactance equals the capacitive reactance, resulting in a purely resistive circuit with maximum current. This condition can be mathematically expressed and experimentally verified.
