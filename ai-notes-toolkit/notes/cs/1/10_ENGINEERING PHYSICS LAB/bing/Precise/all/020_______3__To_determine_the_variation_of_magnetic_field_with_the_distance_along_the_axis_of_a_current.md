##### 3. To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current

The magnetic field produced by a current-carrying wire can be determined using the Biot-Savart law. This law states that the magnetic field at a point is proportional to the current, the length of the wire element, and the inverse square of the distance from the wire element to the point.

To determine the variation of the magnetic field with distance along the axis of a current-carrying wire, the following steps can be followed:

1. Set up the experiment by placing a current-carrying wire along the axis of a magnetic field sensor.
2. Measure the magnetic field at different distances from the wire along the axis.
3. Plot the magnetic field strength against the distance from the wire.
4. Analyze the graph to determine the relationship between the magnetic field strength and the distance from the wire.

The graph should show that the magnetic field strength decreases as the distance from the wire increases. This relationship can be described mathematically using the inverse square law, which states that the magnetic field strength is inversely proportional to the square of the distance from the wire.
