### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- "Practical Physics" is a book written by K. K. Dey and B. N. Dutta.
- It is published by Kalyani Publishers, New Delhi.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to practical physics experiments and measurements.
- It covers a wide range of topics, including mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, magnetism, and modern physics.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, with an emphasis on experimental techniques and data analysis.
- It is a valuable resource for students and teachers of physics, as well as for anyone interested in conducting physics experiments.
