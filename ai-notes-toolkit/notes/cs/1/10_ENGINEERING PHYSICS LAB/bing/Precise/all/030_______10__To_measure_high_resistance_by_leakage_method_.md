##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method

The leakage method is a technique used to measure high resistance values. This method is based on the principle of measuring the leakage current flowing through the insulation of a conductor. The following are the steps involved in measuring high resistance by the leakage method:

1. The conductor whose resistance is to be measured is connected to a high voltage source.
2. The leakage current flowing through the insulation of the conductor is measured using a sensitive ammeter.
3. The resistance value is calculated by dividing the applied voltage by the measured leakage current.

It is important to note that the leakage method is only suitable for measuring high resistance values, as the leakage current is very small for low resistance values. Additionally, the accuracy of the measurement depends on the accuracy of the ammeter used to measure the leakage current.
