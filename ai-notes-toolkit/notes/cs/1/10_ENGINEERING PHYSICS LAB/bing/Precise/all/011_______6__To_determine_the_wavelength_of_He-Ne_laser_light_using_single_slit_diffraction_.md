##### 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

1. The experiment is set up by shining a He-Ne laser light through a single slit and onto a screen.
2. The diffraction pattern produced on the screen is observed and measured.
3. The distance between the central maximum and the first minimum on either side is measured.
4. The slit width and the distance between the slit and the screen are also measured.
5. Using the formula for single slit diffraction, the wavelength of the He-Ne laser light can be calculated.
6. The formula for single slit diffraction is: λ = (a * sinθ) / m, where λ is the wavelength, a is the slit width, θ is the angle of diffraction, and m is the order of the minimum.
7. The angle of diffraction can be calculated using the distance between the central maximum and the first minimum, and the distance between the slit and the screen.
8. By substituting the measured values into the formula, the wavelength of the He-Ne laser light can be determined.
