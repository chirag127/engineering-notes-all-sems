#### CO-2 Compute and analyze various electrical and electronic properties of a given

1. **Resistance**: The property of a material to resist the flow of electric current. It is measured in ohms (Ω) and can be calculated using Ohm's Law, which states that the resistance is equal to the voltage divided by the current.
2. **Capacitance**: The ability of a material to store electric charge. It is measured in farads (F) and is determined by the distance between the plates of a capacitor, the area of the plates, and the dielectric constant of the material between the plates.
3. **Inductance**: The property of a coil of wire to resist changes in electric current flowing through it. It is measured in henries (H) and is determined by the number of turns in the coil, the cross-sectional area of the coil, and the magnetic permeability of the core material.
4. **Impedance**: The total opposition to the flow of alternating current (AC) in an electric circuit. It is measured in ohms (Ω) and is a combination of resistance and reactance, which is the opposition to the flow of AC due to capacitance and inductance.
5. **Conductance**: The ability of a material to conduct electric current. It is the reciprocal of resistance and is measured in siemens (S).
6. **Transconductance**: The ratio of the change in the output current of a device to the change in the input voltage. It is measured in siemens (S) and is commonly used to characterize the performance of field-effect transistors (FETs).
7. **Permittivity**: The ability of a material to polarize in response to an applied electric field. It is measured in farads per meter (F/m) and is a measure of the material's ability to store electric charge.
8. **Permeability**: The ability of a material to support the formation of a magnetic field within itself. It is measured in henries per meter (H/m) and is a measure of the material's ability to conduct a magnetic field.
