### 3. Engineering Physics Practical- S K Gupta ( Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- Engineering Physics Practical is a book written by S K Gupta and published by Krishna Prakashan Meerut.
- The book is intended for students studying engineering and covers practical aspects of physics.
- It includes experiments and practical exercises to help students understand the concepts of physics and apply them in an engineering context.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for students to follow and understand.
- It is a valuable resource for students preparing for exams or for anyone looking to improve their understanding of physics in an engineering context.
