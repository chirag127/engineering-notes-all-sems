# 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

## Aim
To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

## Theory
- Diffraction is the phenomenon of bending of light around the edges of an obstacle or an aperture.
- When a monochromatic light beam passes through a narrow slit, it forms a diffraction pattern on a screen placed at some distance from the slit.
- The diffraction pattern consists of a bright central maximum and a series of alternate bright and dark fringes on either side of the central maximum.
- The angular width of the central maximum is given by the formula:

    `theta = 2 * lambda / a`

    where `theta` is the angular width, `lambda` is the wavelength of light, and `a` is the width of the slit.

- The linear width of the central maximum on the screen is given by the formula:

    `y = 2 * D * lambda / a`

    where `y` is the linear width, `D` is the distance between the slit and the screen, and `a` and `lambda` are the same as before.

- By measuring the width of the central maximum and the distance between the slit and the screen, we can calculate the wavelength of light using the above formula.

## Apparatus
- He-Ne laser source
- Single slit
- Screen
- Meter scale
- Vernier calipers

## Procedure
- Set up the apparatus as shown in the diagram below:

    ```
    |Laser| -> |Slit| -> |Screen|
    ```

- Adjust the slit width to about 0.1 mm using the vernier calipers.
- Measure the distance between the slit and the screen using the meter scale. Record this value as `D`.
- Turn on the laser and observe the diffraction pattern on the screen.
- Measure the width of the central maximum using the meter scale. Record this value as `y`.
- Repeat the above steps for different values of slit width and record the corresponding values of `y`.
- Calculate the wavelength of light using the formula:

    `lambda = y * a / (2 * D)`

    where `y`, `a`, and `D` are the measured values.

- Take the average of the calculated values of `lambda` and report it as the final result.
