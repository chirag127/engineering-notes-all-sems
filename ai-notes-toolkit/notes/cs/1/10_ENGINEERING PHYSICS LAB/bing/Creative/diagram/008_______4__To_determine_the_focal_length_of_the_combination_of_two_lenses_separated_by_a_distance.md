##### 4. To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance

- When two lenses are placed in contact, the focal length of the combination is given by the formula:

  1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2

  where f is the focal length of the combination, f1 and f2 are the focal lengths of the individual lenses.

- When two lenses are separated by a distance d, the focal length of the combination is given by the formula:

  1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/f1f2

  where f, f1 and f2 are the same as before, and d is the distance between the lenses.

- To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance, we can use the following steps:

  - Measure the focal lengths of the individual lenses using a distant object or a convex lens.
  - Place the two lenses on an optical bench with a distance d between them.
  - Place an object at a suitable distance from the first lens and adjust the position of a screen until a clear image is formed on it.
  - Measure the object distance u and the image distance v from the first lens.
  - Use the lens formula 1/v - 1/u = 1/f1 to calculate the equivalent focal length f1 of the first lens and the combination.
  - Use the formula 1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/f1f2 to calculate the focal length f of the combination.
  - Repeat the experiment for different values of d and f1 and verify the results.
