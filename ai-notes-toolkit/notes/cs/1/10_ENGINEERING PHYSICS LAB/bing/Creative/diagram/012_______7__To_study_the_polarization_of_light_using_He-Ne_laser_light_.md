##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

- Polarization of light is the phenomenon of restricting the vibrations of the electric field vector of light in a particular direction.
- He-Ne laser light is a type of coherent light source that emits light of a single wavelength (632.8 nm) and a single polarization state (linearly polarized).
- To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light, we need the following apparatus: a He-Ne laser, a polarizer, an analyzer, a photodetector, and a digital multimeter.
- The experimental setup is as follows:

```
|He-Ne laser| --> |Polarizer| --> |Analyzer| --> |Photodetector| --> |Digital multimeter|
```

- The polarizer is a device that transmits only the component of light that is parallel to its transmission axis, and blocks the component that is perpendicular to it.
- The analyzer is another polarizer that is placed after the first one, and can be rotated to change its transmission axis.
- The photodetector is a device that converts the intensity of light into an electric current, which can be measured by the digital multimeter.
- The procedure is as follows:

  - Turn on the He-Ne laser and adjust its position so that the beam is incident on the polarizer.
  - Set the transmission axis of the polarizer to be horizontal, and the transmission axis of the analyzer to be vertical. This means that the two polarizers are crossed, and no light should pass through them.
  - Place the photodetector after the analyzer, and connect it to the digital multimeter. Note the reading of the current, which should be zero or very small.
  - Rotate the analyzer by an angle θ, and note the reading of the current again. Repeat this for different values of θ from 0° to 180°, and record the data in a table.
  - Plot a graph of the current (I) versus the angle (θ), and observe the shape of the curve.
  - Use the Malus' law to verify the relation between the current and the angle: I = I0 cos^2 θ, where I0 is the maximum current when the transmission axes of the polarizers are parallel.
  - Calculate the degree of polarization of the He-Ne laser light using the formula: P = (Imax - Imin) / (Imax + Imin), where Imax and Imin are the maximum and minimum currents respectively.
