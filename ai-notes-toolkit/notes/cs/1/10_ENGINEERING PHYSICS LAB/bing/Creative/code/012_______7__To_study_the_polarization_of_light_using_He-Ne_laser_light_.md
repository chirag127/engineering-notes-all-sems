##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

- Polarization of light is the phenomenon of restricting the vibrations of the electric field vector of a light wave to a certain plane or direction.
- He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that produces coherent and monochromatic light of wavelength 632.8 nm in the red part of the visible spectrum.
- The polarization properties of He-Ne laser depend on the cavity design, the mode structure, the anisotropy of the gain medium, and the mode competition.
- To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser, the following steps are required:
  - Set up a He-Ne laser source and a power supply on an optical bench.
  - Place a polarizer in front of the laser beam and rotate it to adjust the intensity of the transmitted light.
  - Place a photodetector behind the polarizer and connect it to a voltmeter or an oscilloscope to measure the intensity of the polarized light.
  - Record the intensity of the polarized light as a function of the angle of the polarizer.
  - Plot the intensity versus the angle and observe the sinusoidal variation of the polarized light.
  - Repeat the experiment with different He-Ne lasers and compare the results.
- The expected outcome of the experiment is that the intensity of the polarized light will vary as I = I0 cos^2 θ, where I0 is the maximum intensity, and θ is the angle between the polarization axis of the polarizer and the polarization direction of the laser beam.
- The polarization of the He-Ne laser beam is linear, making it ideal for polarization-sensitive applications such as interferometry, holography, optical communication, and optical metrology.
