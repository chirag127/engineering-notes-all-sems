# Engineering Physics Practical by S K Gupta (Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- This book is a comprehensive guide for engineering students to perform physics experiments in the laboratory.
- The book covers various topics such as optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics, modern physics, and mechanics.
- The book provides detailed instructions, diagrams, observations, calculations, and results for each experiment.
- The book also includes viva voce questions, model test papers, and practical record sheets for the students to prepare for the examinations.
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with examples and illustrations to enhance the understanding of the concepts.
- The book is based on the latest syllabus and guidelines of various engineering universities and boards.

Some of the experiments covered in the book are:

- Determination of wavelength of monochromatic light by Newton's rings method.
- Determination of refractive index of a prism by minimum deviation method.
- Determination of specific rotation of a sugar solution by polarimeter.
- Determination of resistance of a galvanometer by half deflection method.
- Determination of magnetic field along the axis of a current carrying coil by Stewart and Gee's method.
- Determination of Planck's constant by photoelectric effect.
- Determination of e/m of an electron by Thomson's method.
- Determination of Young's modulus of a wire by Searle's method.
- Determination of coefficient of viscosity of a liquid by Poiseuille's method.
- Determination of acceleration due to gravity by Kater's pendulum.
