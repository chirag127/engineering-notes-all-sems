##### Transmission Grating

A transmission grating is a type of diffraction grating that transmits light rather than reflecting it. It consists of a periodic pattern of slits or grooves on a transparent substrate that diffracts the incident light into different directions according to the wavelength and the grating equation .

Some features and applications of transmission gratings are:

- Transmission gratings have low polarization sensitivity, unlike reflection gratings, because the light is not reflected by a metallic coating.
- Transmission gratings can be used in compact, in-line configurations, because the light does not need to be folded back by a mirror.
- Transmission gratings can be either surface relief gratings or volume Bragg gratings. Surface relief gratings have grooves on the surface of the substrate, while volume Bragg gratings have periodic variations of refractive index inside the substrate.
- Transmission gratings can be used for spectral analysis, wavelength division multiplexing, laser pulse compression, pulse shaping, and beam steering.
- Transmission gratings can achieve high diffraction efficiency and low wavefront distortion by using precise lithographic fabrication techniques.
