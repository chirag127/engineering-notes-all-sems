##### 1. To determine the wavelength of sodium light by Newton’s ring experiment.

- Newton's ring experiment is a method of measuring the wavelength of light using the phenomenon of interference.
- The setup consists of a plano-convex lens of large radius of curvature placed on a plane glass plate. A monochromatic light source, such as a sodium lamp, is used to illuminate the lens from above. A viewing telescope is used to observe the interference pattern formed by the light reflected from the upper and lower surfaces of the air film between the lens and the plate.
- The interference pattern consists of concentric bright and dark rings, called Newton's rings, centered at the point of contact of the lens and the plate. The radius of the rings depends on the thickness of the air film, which varies with the distance from the point of contact.
- The wavelength of the light can be determined by measuring the radius of the rings and applying the following formula:

where λ is the wavelength, t is the thickness of the air film, θ is the angle of incidence, R is the radius of curvature of the lens, r_m and r_(m+1) are the radii of the mth and (m+1)th dark rings, respectively, and m is the order of the ring.

- To perform the experiment, the following steps are followed:

  - Adjust the sodium lamp, the lens-plate system, and the viewing telescope such that the lamp is at the focal point of the lens, the lens and the plate are in contact at the center, and the telescope is focused on the interference pattern.
  - Measure the diameter of several dark rings using a traveling microscope attached to the telescope. Record the values of D_m and D_(m+1) for different values of m.
  - Calculate the radii of the rings using r = D/2.
  - Plot a graph of r^2 versus m and find the slope of the line. The slope is equal to 2Rλ.
  - Calculate the value of λ using the formula λ = slope/(2R), where R is the radius of curvature of the lens, which can be obtained from the manufacturer or by using a spherometer.
  - Repeat the experiment for different values of m and find the average value of λ. Compare the result with the standard value of 589 nm for sodium light.
