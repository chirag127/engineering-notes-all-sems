#### Group B

- Group B is a group of bacteria that are commonly found in the human body, especially in the digestive, urinary, and reproductive tracts.
- Group B bacteria can cause infections in some people, especially pregnant women, newborns, and people with weakened immune systems.
- Group B infections can be serious and sometimes life-threatening, such as sepsis, meningitis, pneumonia, and bone and joint infections.
- Group B infections can be prevented by screening pregnant women for the bacteria and giving them antibiotics during labor if they test positive.
- Group B infections can be treated with antibiotics, but some strains may be resistant to certain drugs.
- Group B infections can be diagnosed by taking samples of blood, urine, spinal fluid, or other body fluids and testing them for the presence of the bacteria.
