#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to make use of something or to put something into effect.
- Apply can also mean to request or seek something, such as a job, a grant, or a visa.
- Apply can also mean to cover or spread something over a surface, such as paint, cream, or glue.
- Apply can also mean to be relevant or appropriate to a situation, such as a rule, a principle, or a theory.
- Some examples of apply in sentences are:

  - You can apply this formula to any problem of this type.
  - She applied for a scholarship to study abroad.
  - He applied a thin layer of paint to the wall.
  - This law does not apply to minors.
