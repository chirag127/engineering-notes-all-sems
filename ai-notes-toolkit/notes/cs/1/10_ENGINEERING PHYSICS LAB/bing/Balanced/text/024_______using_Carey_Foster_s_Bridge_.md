##### Using Carey Foster's Bridge

- Carey Foster's bridge is a bridge circuit used to measure medium resistances, or to measure small differences between two large resistances.
- It is a variant of the Wheatstone bridge, which consists of four arms with four resistances connected in a loop, and a galvanometer to detect the null point.
- The main difference between the Wheatstone bridge and the Carey Foster's bridge is that one of the arms of the latter is replaced by a uniform resistance wire with a sliding contact.
- The resistance wire is calibrated by measuring its resistance per unit length using a standard resistance and a known length of the wire.
- To measure an unknown resistance using the Carey Foster's bridge, the following steps are followed :
  - The unknown resistance is connected in one of the arms of the bridge, and a known resistance is connected in the opposite arm.
  - The sliding contact is moved along the resistance wire until the galvanometer shows zero deflection, indicating the null point.
  - The length of the wire between the fixed and the sliding contacts is noted, and the resistance of this length is calculated using the calibration factor.
  - The unknown resistance is then given by the formula: R = R' + (l - l')/k, where R is the unknown resistance, R' is the known resistance, l and l' are the lengths of the wire at the null points with and without the unknown resistance, and k is the resistance per unit length of the wire.
- The advantages of the Carey Foster's bridge are :
  - It can measure medium resistances with high accuracy and sensitivity.
  - It can measure small differences between two large resistances with ease.
  - It can eliminate the effect of contact resistance and temperature variation on the measurement.
- The applications of the Carey Foster's bridge are :
  - It can be used to measure the resistivity of materials and the specific resistance of wires.
  - It can be used to compare the emfs of two cells and the capacities of two condensers.
  - It can be used to calibrate other bridge circuits and instruments.
