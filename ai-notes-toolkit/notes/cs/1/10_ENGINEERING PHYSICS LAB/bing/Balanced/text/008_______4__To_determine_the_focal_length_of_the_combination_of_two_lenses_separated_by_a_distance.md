##### 4. To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance

- The focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance is given by the formula:

  1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/f1f2

  where f is the focal length of the combination, f1 and f2 are the focal lengths of the individual lenses, and d is the distance between them.

- To determine the focal length of the combination experimentally, the following steps can be followed:

  - Set up a light source, a screen, and a lens holder on an optical bench.
  - Place the first lens on the lens holder and adjust its position until a clear image of the light source is formed on the screen. Measure the distance between the lens and the screen, which is the focal length of the first lens (f1).
  - Place the second lens on the lens holder behind the first lens and adjust its position until a clear image of the light source is formed on the screen again. Measure the distance between the second lens and the screen, which is the focal length of the second lens (f2).
  - Measure the distance between the two lenses (d).
  - Use the formula to calculate the focal length of the combination (f).
  - Repeat the experiment with different combinations of lenses and distances and record the results.
