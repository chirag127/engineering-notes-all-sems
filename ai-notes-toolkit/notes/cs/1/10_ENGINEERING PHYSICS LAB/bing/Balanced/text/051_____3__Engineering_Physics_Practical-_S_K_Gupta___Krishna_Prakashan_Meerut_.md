### 3. Engineering Physics Practical- S K Gupta ( Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- This is a book that contains practical experiments and exercises related to engineering physics.
- The book covers topics such as optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics, mechanics, and modern physics.
- The book provides detailed instructions, diagrams, observations, calculations, and results for each experiment.
- The book also includes viva voce questions, multiple choice questions, and numerical problems for revision and practice.
- The book is designed to help engineering students develop their experimental skills and understanding of physical concepts and principles.
- The book is divided into 12 chapters, each focusing on a specific topic or theme of engineering physics. The chapters are:

  1. Measurement of Physical Quantities
  2. Errors and Uncertainties in Measurements
  3. Mechanics
  4. Properties of Matter
  5. Heat and Thermodynamics
  6. Optics
  7. Electricity and Magnetism
  8. Electronics
  9. Atomic and Nuclear Physics
  10. Solid State Physics
  11. Laser and Fiber Optics
  12. Nanotechnology and Nanomaterials
