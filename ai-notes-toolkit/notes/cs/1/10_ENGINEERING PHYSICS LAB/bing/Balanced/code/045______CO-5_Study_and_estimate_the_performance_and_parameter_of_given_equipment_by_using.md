#### CO-5 Study and estimate the performance and parameter of given equipment by using

- This topic is about how to use different methods and tools to evaluate the technical and economic aspects of a given equipment or system.
- Some of the objectives of this topic are:
  - To understand the concept and importance of techno-economic modeling for new technology development.
  - To learn how to develop a process model and calculate equipment-sizing parameters for a given equipment or system.
  - To learn how to estimate capital and operating costs for a given equipment or system.
  - To learn how to build a user interface for a techno-economic model and perform sensitivity analysis and optimization.
- Some of the methods and tools that can be used for this topic are:
  - Techno-economic modeling: A technique that combines process modeling, cost estimation, and economic analysis to evaluate the feasibility and profitability of a new technology or system.
  - Parameter estimation: A technique that uses observed data to estimate the unknown parameters of a model or distribution that describes the data.
  - Life data analysis: A technique that uses failure data to estimate the reliability and lifetime characteristics of a product or system.
- Some of the examples of equipment or systems that can be studied and estimated by using these methods and tools are:
  - A solar photovoltaic system: A system that converts sunlight into electricity using solar cells and other components.
  - A heat exchanger: A device that transfers heat between two or more fluids with different temperatures.
  - A gas turbine: A device that converts the energy of a high-pressure gas into mechanical power or electricity.
