#### Monochromatic and Polychromatic Light

- Monochromatic light is light that has only one wavelength or frequency. It is pure and uniform in color. Examples of monochromatic light sources are lasers, LEDs, and sodium lamps  .
- Polychromatic light is light that has more than one wavelength or frequency. It is composed of a mixture of colors. Examples of polychromatic light sources are white light, sunlight, and incandescent lamps  .
- Monochromatic and polychromatic light can be distinguished by using a prism or a diffraction grating. These devices can split polychromatic light into its constituent colors, while monochromatic light remains unchanged.
- Monochromatic and polychromatic light have different applications in science and technology. For instance, monochromatic light is used for spectroscopy, interferometry, holography, and optical communication, while polychromatic light is used for photography, colorimetry, and solar energy .
