#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to put something into use or practice, or to request something formally.
- Apply can have different meanings depending on the context and the object of the verb.
- Some common meanings and examples of apply are:

  - To use something for a specific purpose or situation. For example:
    - You can apply this cream to your skin to reduce inflammation.
    - He applied his knowledge of physics to solve the problem.
    - She applied the brakes to stop the car.
  - To request something, usually in writing, such as a job, a loan, a scholarship, etc. For example:
    - He applied for a visa to travel to Canada.
    - She applied to several universities for admission.
    - They applied for a grant to fund their project.
  - To be relevant or valid in a particular case or situation. For example:
    - The same rules apply to everyone, regardless of their status.
    - This offer only applies to new customers.
    - The law does not apply to minors.
