### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- This is a book on experimental physics for undergraduate students of science and engineering.
- It covers various topics such as mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics and modern physics.
- It contains detailed descriptions of experiments, diagrams, tables, graphs and calculations.
- It also provides theoretical background, error analysis, precautions and references for each experiment.
- It is designed to help students develop practical skills, conceptual understanding and scientific attitude.
- It is based on the syllabus of various universities and examination boards in India.
