##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

- High resistance is the resistance of a circuit or a device that is much higher than the normal operating resistance, usually in the order of megaohms or gigaohms.
- High resistance can be caused by factors such as corrosion, oxidation, moisture, dirt, or insulation breakdown.
- High resistance can affect the performance and safety of electrical systems, as it can reduce the current flow, increase the voltage drop, generate heat, and cause leakage currents.
- Leakage current is the unwanted current that flows through or across the insulation of a circuit or a device, due to the presence of high resistance.
- Leakage current can be measured by using a leakage tester, which is a device that applies a high voltage (usually in the range of 500 V to 5 kV) across the circuit or the device under test, and measures the current that flows through or across the insulation.
- The leakage tester can also calculate the resistance of the insulation by using Ohm's law: R = V / I, where R is the resistance, V is the voltage, and I is the current.
- The leakage method is a technique of measuring high resistance by using a leakage tester.
- The leakage method can be used to measure the resistance of various devices, such as capacitors, transformers, cables, motors, generators, etc.
- The leakage method has some advantages and disadvantages, such as:

  - Advantages:
    - It can measure very high resistance values, up to several teraohms.
    - It can detect the presence and location of faults or defects in the insulation, such as cracks, punctures, or moisture.
    - It can provide information about the quality and condition of the insulation, such as its dielectric strength, breakdown voltage, or polarization index.
  - Disadvantages:
    - It requires a high voltage source, which can be dangerous if not handled properly.
    - It can damage the insulation or the device under test, if the applied voltage is too high or too long.
    - It can be affected by external factors, such as temperature, humidity, or noise.
