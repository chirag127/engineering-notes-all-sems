##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

- Polarization of light is the phenomenon of restricting the vibrations of the electric vector of a transverse electromagnetic wave to a certain plane or direction.
- A polarizer is a device that can produce polarized light from unpolarized light by selectively transmitting or reflecting the electric vector components in a specific direction.
- A He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon as the active medium and emits a red beam of light with a wavelength of 632.8 nm in air.
- A He-Ne laser can be used to study the polarization of light by passing the laser beam through different types of polarizers and observing the changes in the intensity and direction of the beam.
- The polarization properties of a He-Ne laser depend on the cavity design, the mode structure, the anisotropy and the mode competition of the laser.
- A linearly polarized He-Ne laser has a single mode of oscillation with a fixed direction of the electric vector, while a randomly polarized He-Ne laser has multiple modes of oscillation with varying directions of the electric vector.
- The experiment to study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light involves the following steps:

  - Set up the He-Ne laser, a power supply, a polarizer, an analyzer and a photodetector on an optical bench.
  - Align the laser, the polarizer and the analyzer such that the laser beam passes through them and reaches the photodetector.
  - Adjust the angle of the polarizer and the analyzer and measure the intensity of the transmitted beam using the photodetector.
  - Plot a graph of the intensity versus the angle of the analyzer and observe the variation of the intensity with respect to the polarization angle.
  - Repeat the experiment with different types of polarizers, such as linear, circular and elliptical, and compare the results.
