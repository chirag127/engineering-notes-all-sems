##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum.

- A compound pendulum is a rigid body that can oscillate about a horizontal axis passing through it.
- The time period of a compound pendulum depends on its length, mass, and the distance of the axis of oscillation from its center of mass.
- The formula for the time period of a compound pendulum is:

```
T = 2π √(I/mgd)
```

where T is the time period, I is the moment of inertia of the pendulum about the axis of oscillation, m is the mass of the pendulum, g is the acceleration due to gravity, and d is the distance of the axis of oscillation from the center of mass.

- To determine the value of g using a compound pendulum, the following steps are followed:

  - Measure the mass and the length of the pendulum.
  - Suspend the pendulum from a rigid support and set it into small oscillations.
  - Measure the time for 20 oscillations using a stopwatch and repeat the experiment for different values of d.
  - Calculate the average time period for each value of d and plot a graph of T^2 versus d.
  - The graph will be a straight line passing through the origin. The slope of the graph will be:

  ```
  slope = 4π^2I/mg
  ```

  - Calculate the moment of inertia of the pendulum using the formula:

  ```
  I = mL^2/12
  ```

  where L is the length of the pendulum.

  - Substitute the values of I, m, and slope in the formula for slope and solve for g.
  - The value of g obtained from the experiment will be close to the theoretical value of 9.8 m/s^2.
