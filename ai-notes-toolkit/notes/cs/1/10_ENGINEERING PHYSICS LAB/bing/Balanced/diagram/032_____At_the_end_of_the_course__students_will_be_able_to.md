### At the end of the course, students will be able to

- Identify and explain the main concepts and principles of the subject matter.
- Apply the acquired knowledge and skills to solve problems and perform tasks related to the course objectives.
- Analyze and evaluate information from various sources and perspectives, and synthesize their own arguments and conclusions.
- Communicate effectively and professionally in written and oral forms, using appropriate terminology and formats.
- Demonstrate ethical awareness and responsibility in their academic and professional endeavors.
- Collaborate and cooperate with others in diverse and intercultural settings, and respect different opinions and perspectives.
- Reflect on their own learning process and outcomes, and identify areas for improvement and further development.
