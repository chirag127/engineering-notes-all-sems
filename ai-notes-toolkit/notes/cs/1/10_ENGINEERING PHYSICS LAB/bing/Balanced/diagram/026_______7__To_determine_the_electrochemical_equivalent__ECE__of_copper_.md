##### 7. To determine the electrochemical equivalent (ECE) of copper.

  - Connect the electrodes to a battery or a power supply and a rheostat in series. Also connect an ammeter and a stopwatch in the circuit.

  - Repeat the experiment for different values of current and time and record the observations in a table.
