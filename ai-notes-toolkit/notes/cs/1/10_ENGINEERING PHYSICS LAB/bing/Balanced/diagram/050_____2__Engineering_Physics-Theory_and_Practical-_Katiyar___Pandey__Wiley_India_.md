### 2. Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

- This is a book written by A.K. Katiyar and C.K. Pandey, published by Wiley India in 2017    .
- It is based on the revised syllabus of Engineering Physics-I (NAS-101), Engineering Physics-II (NAS-201) and Practical (NAS-151/251) of Uttar Pradesh Technical University (UPTU), Lucknow  .
- It covers the topics of mechanics, optics, thermodynamics, quantum physics, solid state physics, nuclear physics, and nanotechnology, with relevant examples and applications   .
- It also includes practical experiments based on the theory, with detailed procedures, observations, calculations, and results   .
- It aims to provide a comprehensive and clear understanding of the fundamental concepts of physics and their applications in engineering   .
- It is suitable for undergraduate engineering students of various branches, as well as for teachers and researchers in the field of physics and engineering   .
