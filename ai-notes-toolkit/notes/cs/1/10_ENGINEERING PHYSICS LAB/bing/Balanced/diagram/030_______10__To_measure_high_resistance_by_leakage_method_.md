##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

- High resistance is the resistance of a material or a device that is greater than 1 MΩ (megaohm).
- High resistance can be measured by the leakage method, which is based on the principle of charging and discharging a capacitor through the resistance to be measured.
- The leakage method consists of the following steps:

  1. Connect a capacitor of known capacitance C in series with the high resistance R and a DC voltage source V.
  2. Close the switch S to charge the capacitor to the voltage V. Note the time t1 when the switch is closed.
  3. Open the switch S to disconnect the voltage source and let the capacitor discharge through the resistance R. Note the time t2 when the switch is opened.
  4. Measure the voltage across the capacitor Vc at a known time interval t after opening the switch S.
  5. Apply the formula R = C * ln(V/Vc) / t to calculate the high resistance R, where ln is the natural logarithm function.

- The leakage method is suitable for measuring high resistance in the range of 10 MΩ to 1000 GΩ (gigaohm).
- The leakage method has the advantages of simplicity, accuracy, and low cost.
- The leakage method has the disadvantages of being affected by the leakage currents of the capacitor and the measuring instruments, and requiring a stable and constant voltage source.
