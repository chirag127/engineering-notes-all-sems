##### 5. To determine resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance

- Resistance is the property of a material that opposes the flow of electric current through it. It is measured in ohms (Ω).
- Resistance per unit length is the ratio of resistance to the length of a conductor. It is measured in ohms per meter (Ω/m).
- Specific resistance or resistivity is the resistance of a unit length and unit cross-sectional area of a material. It is measured in ohm-meter (Ωm).
- The resistance of a conductor depends on its length, cross-sectional area, and material. The formula for resistance is:

  R = ρL/A

  where R is the resistance, ρ is the resistivity, L is the length, and A is the cross-sectional area of the conductor.

- To determine the resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance, we need the following apparatus:

  - A resistance wire of known length and diameter
  - A meter bridge
  - A galvanometer
  - A jockey
  - A battery
  - A rheostat
  - A key
  - A resistance box
  - A screw gauge
  - A meter scale

- The procedure is as follows:

  - Measure the length and diameter of the resistance wire using the meter scale and the screw gauge respectively. Calculate the cross-sectional area of the wire using the formula A = πd^2/4, where d is the diameter.
  - Connect the resistance wire between the terminals A and B of the meter bridge. Connect the galvanometer between the terminals C and D of the meter bridge. Connect the jockey to the galvanometer. Connect the battery, the rheostat, and the key in series with the resistance box between the terminals A and C of the meter bridge.
  - Adjust the rheostat to obtain a suitable current in the circuit. Close the key and slide the jockey along the wire AC until the galvanometer shows zero deflection. Note the balancing length l1 of the wire AC.
  - Interchange the positions of the resistance wire and the resistance box in the circuit. Repeat the same procedure and note the balancing length l2 of the wire AC.
  - Calculate the mean balancing length l of the wire AC using the formula l = (l1 + l2)/2.
  - Calculate the resistance per unit length of the wire using the formula R/L = r/l, where R is the resistance of the wire, L is the length of the wire, r is the resistance of the resistance box, and l is the mean balancing length of the wire AC.
  - Calculate the specific resistance of the wire using the formula ρ = RA/L, where R is the resistance of the wire, A is the cross-sectional area of the wire, and L is the length of the wire.
