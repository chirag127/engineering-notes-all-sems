### Slip-torque characteristics for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Slip-torque characteristics are an essential aspect to understand when it comes to electrical machines. Here are some key points to consider:

- Slip-torque characteristics refer to the relationship between the torque and slip of the machine.
- Slip is the difference between the synchronous speed and the actual speed of the rotor.
- Torque is the turning force produced by the machine.
- The slip-torque characteristics of a machine can be represented graphically as a curve.
- The curve typically shows that as the slip increases, the torque also increases until it reaches a maximum point known as the breakdown torque.
- Once the breakdown torque is reached, the torque decreases as the slip continues to increase.
- The point at which the breakdown torque is reached is also known as the maximum torque point.
- The slope of the curve before the maximum torque point is known as the pullout torque.
- The pullout torque is the maximum torque that can be sustained by the machine without stalling.
- The shape of the slip-torque curve can vary depending on the type of machine and its operating conditions.
- Slip-torque characteristics are essential for understanding the performance of electrical machines, particularly in applications where high torque is required, such as in industrial settings.

Understanding slip-torque characteristics is critical for electrical engineers working with electrical machines. By analyzing the slip-torque curve, engineers can determine the maximum torque that a machine can produce without stalling and make informed decisions about its use in various applications.
