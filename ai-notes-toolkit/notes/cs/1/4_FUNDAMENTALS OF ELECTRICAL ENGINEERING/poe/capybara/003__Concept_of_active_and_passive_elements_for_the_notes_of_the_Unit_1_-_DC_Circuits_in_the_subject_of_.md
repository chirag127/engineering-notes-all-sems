### Concept of active and passive elements for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING.

In the domain of electrical engineering, various components are used in electrical circuits, which can be divided into two categories, active and passive elements. Understanding the difference between these two types of elements and their behavior is crucial in the design and analysis of electrical circuits. Here are some key points to keep in mind for the concept of active and passive elements:

#### Active Elements:

- Active elements are those elements that are capable of generating energy or power.
- These components can increase the voltage or current level of a signal.
- Examples of active elements include generators, transistors, and amplifiers.
- Active elements require an external source of energy to operate and maintain their output.

#### Passive Elements:

- Passive elements are those elements that cannot generate energy or power.
- These components can only decrease the voltage or current level of a signal.
- Examples of passive elements include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.
- Passive elements do not require an external source of energy to operate.

#### Comparison:

- The primary difference between active and passive elements is that active elements can generate energy, while passive elements cannot.
- Active elements require an external source of energy to operate, while passive elements do not.
- Active elements can increase the voltage or current level of a signal, while passive elements can only decrease it.
- Active elements are used in signal amplification and power generation, while passive elements are used in signal filtering and shaping.

Understanding the concept of active and passive elements provides a foundation for the analysis and design of electrical circuits. It is essential to be familiar with the behavior of these components and their characteristics to solve complex problems in electrical engineering.
