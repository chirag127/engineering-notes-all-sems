### Applications of DC Motors (Simple Numerical Problems)

DC motors have a wide range of applications in various industries and are extensively used in many appliances. In this section, we will discuss some of the common applications of DC motors and their numerical problems.

1. Electric Vehicles: DC motors are widely used in electric vehicles as they provide high starting torque, which is necessary for vehicles to move from a stationary position. The motor's speed can be controlled by varying the voltage applied to it.

2. Industrial Machinery: DC motors are used in various industrial machinery, such as drilling machines, conveyor systems, and cranes. The speed of the motor can be controlled to adjust the speed of the machines.

3. Robotics: DC motors are extensively used in robotics to control the motion and speed of the robots. They are also used in the arms of robots for lifting and moving objects.

4. Power Tools: DC motors are used in power tools, such as drills, saws, and grinders. The motor's speed can be adjusted to control the tool's speed and torque.

5. Home Appliances: DC motors are used in various home appliances, such as fans, vacuum cleaners, and refrigerators. They provide high efficiency and better control over the appliances' speed.

Now, let's discuss some simple numerical problems related to DC motors.

1. A DC motor generates a torque of 50 N-m at a speed of 1500 rpm. The power input to the motor is 10 kW. Calculate the motor's efficiency.

2. A DC motor has a resistance of 0.2 Ω and an armature current of 50 A. If the motor's back emf is 200 V, find the motor's speed.

3. A DC motor has a back emf of 220 V. If the motor is connected to a 220 V supply, and its armature resistance is 0.5 Ω, calculate the current flowing through the motor.

4. A DC shunt motor has a rated voltage of 220 V and a rated current of 10 A. If the motor's resistance is 4 Ω, find the motor's speed when the armature current is 8 A.

5. A DC series motor has a resistance of 0.2 Ω, and the armature current is 50 A. If the motor's back emf is 100 V, find the motor's speed.

These are some of the common applications of DC motors and simple numerical problems related to them. It's essential to understand these concepts thoroughly to solve more complex problems in the future.
