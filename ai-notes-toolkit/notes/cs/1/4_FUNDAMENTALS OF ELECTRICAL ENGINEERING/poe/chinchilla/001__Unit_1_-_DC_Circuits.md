## Unit 1 - DC Circuits

In this unit, we will cover the basics of DC circuits, including the fundamental principles and laws governing the behavior of these circuits. By the end of the unit, you should be able to:

- Define the terms voltage, current, resistance, and power in the context of DC circuits.
- Understand the relationship between these terms and how they apply to Ohm's Law.
- Calculate the voltage, current, or resistance in a circuit using Ohm's Law.
- Understand the concept of series and parallel circuits and how to calculate the equivalent resistance of each type.
- Analyze basic series and parallel circuits and calculate the current, voltage, and power in each component.
- Understand Kirchhoff's Laws and apply them to analyze more complex circuits.
- Understand the concept of circuit analysis using nodal and mesh analysis techniques.

To successfully learn this material and prepare for exams, it is important to:

- Take notes during lectures and read the assigned textbook chapters.
- Practice solving problems and doing calculations using the concepts covered in class.
- Work through example problems in the textbook and in class.
- Participate in class discussions and ask questions if you are unsure about a concept.
- Utilize online resources, such as video tutorials or practice problems, to reinforce your understanding of the material.
- Seek help from your instructor or tutors if you are struggling with any of the concepts.

Remember, practice makes perfect! The more you work with the concepts covered in this unit, the more comfortable you will become with DC circuits and the better prepared you will be for exams.
