### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

In electrical engineering, it is essential to understand the voltage and current relations in different circuit connections. Two commonly used connections in single-phase AC circuits are the star and delta connections. Here are some important points to keep in mind regarding the voltage and current relations in these connections:

#### Star Connection

1. In a star connection, the three-phase windings of a transformer or motor are connected to a common point, which is usually grounded. 

2. The voltage across each phase winding is equal to the line voltage divided by the square root of three. 

3. The line current in a star connection is equal to the phase current. 

4. The phase current lags the corresponding phase voltage by 30 degrees in a star connection. 

5. The line current lags the corresponding line voltage by the same angle as the phase current.

#### Delta Connection

1. In a delta connection, the three-phase windings are connected in a closed loop, forming a triangle. 

2. The line voltage in a delta connection is equal to the phase voltage multiplied by the square root of three. 

3. The line current in a delta connection is equal to the phase current multiplied by the square root of three. 

4. The phase current lags the corresponding phase voltage by 30 degrees in a delta connection. 

5. The line current lags the corresponding line voltage by the same angle as the phase current.


It is important to understand the voltage and current relations in star and delta connections for the steady-state analysis of single-phase AC circuits. By considering these relations, you can easily calculate the voltage and current values in different parts of the circuit and analyze their behavior.
