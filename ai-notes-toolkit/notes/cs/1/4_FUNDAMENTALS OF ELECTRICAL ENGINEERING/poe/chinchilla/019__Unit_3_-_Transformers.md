## Unit 3 - Transformers

Transformers are a type of neural network architecture that has gained popularity in recent years due to their effectiveness in natural language processing tasks. Here are some important points to keep in mind when studying transformers:

- Transformers were introduced in a 2017 paper by Vaswani et al. titled "Attention Is All You Need." They are based on the idea of attention, which allows the network to focus on specific parts of the input sequence when making predictions.
- The basic building blocks of a transformer are the self-attention mechanism and the feedforward network. The self-attention mechanism allows the network to compute a weighted sum of the input sequence, where the weights are determined based on the similarity between each element in the sequence. The feedforward network applies a linear transformation to each element in the sequence and then applies a non-linear activation function.
- Transformers can be used for a variety of natural language processing tasks, including machine translation, text classification, and language modeling. They have been shown to outperform traditional recurrent neural network architectures like LSTMs and GRUs on many of these tasks.
- One key advantage of transformers is their ability to process input sequences in parallel, which makes them much faster than traditional recurrent neural networks. This is because the self-attention mechanism allows the network to compute the similarity between all pairs of elements in the sequence simultaneously.
- Another advantage of transformers is their ability to handle variable-length input sequences. This is because the self-attention mechanism allows the network to compute the weights for each element in the sequence independently of the other elements.
- Transformers have several hyperparameters that need to be tuned in order to achieve optimal performance. These include the number of layers in the network, the number of attention heads, the size of the feedforward network, and the dropout rate.
- Pretrained transformer models like BERT and GPT-2 have become popular in recent years due to their ability to achieve state-of-the-art performance on a variety of natural language processing tasks. These models are trained on massive amounts of text data and can be fine-tuned on specific tasks with relatively little additional training data.
- Finally, it's worth noting that transformers are not limited to natural language processing tasks. They can be applied to any type of sequential data, including time series data and audio signals.
