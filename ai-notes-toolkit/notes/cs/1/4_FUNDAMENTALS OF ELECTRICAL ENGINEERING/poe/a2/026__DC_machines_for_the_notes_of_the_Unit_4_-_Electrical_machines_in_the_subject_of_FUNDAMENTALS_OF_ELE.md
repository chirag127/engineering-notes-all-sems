### DC machines

- DC machines are electric motors and generators that run on direct current electricity.
- The main components of a DC machine are:

1. Stator - The stationary part that produces a stationary magnetic field.
2. Rotor - The rotating part that rotates within the stator.
3. Commutator - A device that converts alternating current to direct current.
4. Brushes - Contacts that transmit electric current to the rotor windings through the commutator.

- Based on the type of excitation, DC machines are classified into:
- Separately excited DC machine - The field windings are externally excited by a separate DC source.
- Self-excited DC machine - The field windings are excited by the armature current.
- Further classified into - Series, shunt, and compound DC machines based on the interconnections of field and armature windings.
- DC machines are widely used in applications requiring variable speed control and high starting torque such as electric locomotives, cranes, elevators, etc.
