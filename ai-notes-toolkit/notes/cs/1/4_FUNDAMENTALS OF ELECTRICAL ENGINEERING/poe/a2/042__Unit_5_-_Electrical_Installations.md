## Unit 5 - Electrical Installations

1. Introduction
- Electrical installation refers to the wiring and associated electrical equipment installed in a building.
- It is designed to provide electrical power and lighting safely while protecting against shock and fire hazards.

2. Types of Circuits
- Series Circuit: Consists of a single path for the current to flow. If the circuit is broken at any point, the current stops flowing and the devices connected in series will not work.
- Parallel Circuit: Consists of multiple paths for the current to flow. The current flowing through each device adds up to the total current. If the circuit breaks at one point, the remaining devices will continue to function. Household circuits are usually parallel circuits.

3. Electrical Safety
- Ensure circuits are properly wired with appropriate gauges of wires for the amperage. Thinner wires may overheat and cause fires if too much current flows through them.
- Proper earthing or grounding must be provided for all appliances for shock protection.
- Protective devices like fuses and circuit breakers must be installed to prevent overload and short circuits.
- Keep electrical devices and wiring away from moisture and water to prevent electric shock.
