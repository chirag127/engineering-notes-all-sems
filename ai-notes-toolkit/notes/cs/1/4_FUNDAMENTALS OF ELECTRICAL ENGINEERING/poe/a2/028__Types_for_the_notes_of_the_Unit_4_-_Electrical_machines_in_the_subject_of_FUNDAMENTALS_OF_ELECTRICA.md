### Types for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. DC Machines
- DC Generator: Converts mechanical energy into DC electrical energy.
- DC Motor: Converts DC electrical energy into mechanical energy.

2. Synchronous Machines
- Alternator: Converts mechanical energy into AC electrical energy.
- Synchronous Motor: Converts AC electrical energy into mechanical energy.

3. Asynchronous Machines
- Induction Motor: Converts AC electrical energy into mechanical energy.

The above points cover the major types of electrical machines which generate or convert electrical and mechanical forms of energy. The working principles and construction details of each machine can be studied in detail under each type.
