### Introduction of Switch Fuse Unit (SFU)

- Switch Fuse Unit (SFU) is a device used for protection of electrical circuits.
- It contains a switch and a fuse.
- The switch is used to control the supply to the load.
- The fuse protects the circuit from overcurrent and acts as a safety device.
- If the current in the circuit exceeds the rated value of the fuse, the fuse blows or melts, thereby opening the circuit and disconnecting the supply to the load.
- This protects the load and wiring from damage due to overcurrent.
- SFUs are available with different ratings of switches and fuses to suit various applications and load requirements.
- They provide convenience as both switch and fuse are housed in a single enclosure.
