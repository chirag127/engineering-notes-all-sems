### Concept of Resonance in series & parallel circuits

- Resonance occurs in an AC circuit when the inductive and capacitive reactances are equal in magnitude but cancel each other.
- This happens at a particular frequency called the resonant frequency.
- At resonance, the impedance of the circuit is minimum and the current is maximum. This results in maximum power transfer and high voltage across the elements.
- In a series resonant circuit, the resonant frequency is given by 1/2π√LC. At resonance, Xl = Xc and Z is minimum. The current is maximum and voltage across elements is maximum.
- In a parallel resonant circuit, the resonant frequency is also given by 1/2π√LC. At resonance, Z is minimum and current through the elements is maximum. The voltage across the elements is minimum.
- Resonance is useful in tuning circuits and in power transmission at high frequencies using resonant transformers.
