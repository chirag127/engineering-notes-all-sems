### Cables and Bus-bars for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. Cables
- Cables are used to transmit electric power or signals between two points.
- Made of one or more wires which are insulated or sheathed.
- Classified into types based on application - power cables, control cables, instrumentation cables, etc.
- Power cables further classified into types based on voltage - low voltage, medium voltage, high voltage, extra high voltage cables.
- Properties - current carrying capacity, voltage rating, flexibility, insulation resistance, etc. depend on type and application.

2. Bus-bars
- Bus-bars are solid copper or aluminum bars used to carry substantial amounts of electric current.
- Installed in switchboards, distribution boards, panel boards, etc.
- Have high conductivity and ampacity.
- Size and material depends on the amount of current to be carried.
- Proper supports and insulation required based on voltages.
- Earthing bus-bar provides a path for fault current to flow to the earth.
