### Principle of operation and introduction to methods of starting for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. Principle of operation
- Electrical machines convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice-versa.
- The working principle involves electromagnetic induction wherein rotating conductors in a magnetic field generate an electromotive force or changing magnetic field due to current-carrying conductors produces motion.
- Based on the type of energy conversion, electrical machines are classified into motors and generators.

2. Methods of starting
- The starting methods control the variation of voltage and current during the starting period to accelerate the machine gradually and minimize stresses. The commonly used methods are:
- Direct on-line starter: Simple but causes high starting current. Used for small motors.
- Star-delta starter: Initially, the motor windings are connected in star and then reconnected in delta which reduces the starting voltage and current.
- Auto-transformer starter: A transformer with variable tap-changing windings is used to vary the voltage during the starting period.
- Rheostatic starter: Resistance is inserted in series with the motor during the starting period and gradually cut-off to accelerate the motor. Efficient but causes power loss in the resistor.
