### Applications (Numerical problems related to slip only)

1. A 4-pole induction motor has 50 Hz supply and runs at 1440 rpm. Calculate slip and rotor speed.
- Supply frequency, f = 50 Hz
- Synchronous speed, Ns = 120xf = 6000 rpm
- Given speed of rotor, N = 1440 rpm
- Slip, s = (Ns - N)/Ns = (6000 - 1440)/6000 = 0.76
- Rotor speed, Nr = Ns(1 - s) = 6000(1 - 0.76) = 1440 rpm

2. A 4-pole induction motor runs at 900 rpm with a slip of 0.5. Calculate supply frequency.
- Given rotor speed, Nr = 900 rpm
- Given slip, s = 0.5
- Ns = Nr/(1 - s) = 900/0.5 = 1800 rpm
- Supply frequency, f = (120 x Ns)/4 = 120 x 1800/4 = 900 Hz

3. The rotor speed of a 4-pole induction motor is 1350 rpm. When supply frequency is increased by 10%, calculate new rotor speed and slip.
- Given rotor speed, Nr = 1350 rpm
- Original supply frequency, f =?
- Increased frequency, f' = 1.1f
- Synchronous speed for increased frequency, N's = 120f' = 132f
- New rotor speed, N'r = N's(1 - s') = 132f(1 - s')
- Given N'r = 1350
- 132f(1 - s') = 1350
- f = 50 Hz (calculating from Nr = 1350 rpm and 4 poles)
- f' = 1.1 x 50 = 55 Hz
- N's = 120 x 55 = 6600 rpm
- s' = (N's - N'r)/N's = (6600 - 1350)/6600 = 0.795
- N'r = 6600(1 - 0.795) = 1350 rpm
