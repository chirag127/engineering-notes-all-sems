### ELCB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- ELCB stands for Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker.
- It is a safety device used in electrical installations with high earth impedance to prevent severe electric shock.
- It detects small stray voltages on the metal enclosures of electrical equipment and cuts off the supply within 30 milliseconds.
- It has a test button to check its proper operation.
- It has two or three poles to disconnect both the phase lines and the neutral line.
- The operating current of ELCB is usually 5 mA or 30 mA which is very small and will not allow any current to leak to earth and cause danger.
- The response time of an ELCB should not be more than 30 milliseconds in order to provide efficient protection against electric shock.
- The ELCB should be placed on the outgoing feeder from the distribution board and should be wired in conjunction with the supply fuse or MCB.
- ELCB provides maximum safety and is highly sensitive to earth faults. Hence it is widely used in homes, hospitals, hotels, etc for protection against electric shock.
