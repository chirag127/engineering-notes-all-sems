### Apparent, active & reactive power

* Apparent power (S) - It is the total power drawn by the AC circuit from the supply source. It is the product of root mean square (rms) current and voltage. (S = IV)
* Active power (P) - It is the actual power consumed by the load to perform useful work. It is the product of rms current and voltage in phase with each other. (P = IV Cos φ)
* Reactive power (Q) - It is the power consumed by the load's inductive or capacitive reactance. It is the product of rms current and voltage 90° out of phase with each other. (Q = IV Sin φ)
* Power factor (cos φ) - It is the ratio of active power to apparent power. It indicates how effectively the apparent power is being used. (cos φ = P/S)

The above points cover the key concepts of apparent, active and reactive power along with power factor for the steady state analysis of single phase AC circuits. The formulas and relationships between these powers are also included for understanding the concepts and applying in solving numerical problems.
