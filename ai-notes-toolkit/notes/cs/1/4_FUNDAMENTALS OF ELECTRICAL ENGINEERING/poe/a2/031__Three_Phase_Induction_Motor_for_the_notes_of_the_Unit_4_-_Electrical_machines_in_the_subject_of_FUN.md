### Three Phase Induction Motor

- Three phase induction motors are the most commonly used type of AC motor.
- They are reliable, efficient, rugged and inexpensive.
- The three phase induction motor has three windings spaced 120 degrees apart in the stator. Three phase power is applied to the stator windings which creates a rotating magnetic field.
- The rotor is made of conductor bars that are short circuited at each end. The rotating magnetic field induces currents in the rotor bars which in turn produce their own magnetic field. The interaction between the magnetic fields of the stator and rotor produce the force that rotates the rotor.
- The rotation speed of the induction motor depends on the frequency of the AC power and the number of pole pairs in the stator. The torque depends on the current in the stator windings and the strength of the magnetic fields.
- By varying the voltage and frequency supplied to the stator windings, the speed and torque of the induction motor can be controlled. This is known as variable frequency drive control and is commonly used for motor control.
- The induction motor is not 100% efficient because some of the power supplied is wasted in heating the rotor bars. However, induction motors are still very efficient at around 85-95% at full load.
- The induction motor is very rugged and reliable as it has a simple construction with no brushes or commutator. It is the workhorse of the industrial world providing efficient and economical motion in many applications.
