### Types of Wires

1. Copper wires
- Made of copper which is a good conductor
- Two types: solid and stranded
- Stranded wires are more flexible and used for appliances
- Solid wires are more rigid and used for electrical panels

2. Aluminum wires
- Made of aluminum which is lighter and cheaper than copper
- Not as good a conductor as copper so larger diameter needed
- Can loosen connections and become hazardous, so not preferred now

3. Insulated vs non-insulated wires
- Wires are insulated with plastic or rubber covering for safety
- Non-insulated wires can be hazardous and cause short circuits
- Always use insulated wires for electrical installations

4. Sheathed cables
- Individual insulated wires bundled into one outer sheath
- Provides protection and easy installation
- Different types like Romex, BX, EMT, etc. used for different purposes
