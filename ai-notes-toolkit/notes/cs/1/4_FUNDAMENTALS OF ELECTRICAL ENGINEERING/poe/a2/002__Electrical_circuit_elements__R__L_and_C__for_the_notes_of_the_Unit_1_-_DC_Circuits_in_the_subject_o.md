### Electrical circuit elements (R, L and C)

- Resistor (R): It opposes the flow of current and converts electrical energy into heat. The amount of opposition is measured in ohms (Ω).
- Inductor (L): It opposes the change in current flow. The opposition to current change is due to the magnetic field generated by the flowing current. The amount of opposition is measured in henries (H).
- Capacitor (C): It opposes the change in voltage. The opposition to voltage change is due to the electric field generated by the charges accumulated on the plates. The amount of opposition is measured in farads (F).

These circuit elements follow the following equations:

- V=IR (Resistor)
- V=Ldi/dt (Inductor)
- I=Cdv/dt (Capacitor)

The properties of these elements affect the flow of electric charge in the circuit. A proper combination of these elements is required to build various circuits to serve different purposes.
