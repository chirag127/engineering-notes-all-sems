### EMF equation of generator and torque equation of motor

**Generator EMF equation:**

- EMF generated (E) is directly proportional to the rate of change of flux linkage (dΦ/dt)
- E = -dΦ/dt
- Polarity of generated EMF depends on the direction of change of flux linkage

**Motor torque equation:**

- Torque (T) is directly proportional to the product of flux linkage (Φ) and current (I)
- T = kΦI
- Direction of torque depends on the relative directions of flux linkage and current
- The constant k depends on the machine parameters like number of turns, etc.

The equations can be used to analyze the working of electrical machines and calculate the generated EMF and developed torque. The notes can be referred to understand the concept and formulas for exams and self-study.
