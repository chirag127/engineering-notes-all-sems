### Concept of active and passive elements for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- Active elements: Elements that can produce energy or voltage such as batteries, generators, etc. are known as active elements. They can produce energy on their own.
- Passive elements: Elements that cannot produce energy or voltage on their own such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, etc. are known as passive elements. They can only store or dissipate energy.
- A circuit must have a complete path or loop to allow current to flow. This complete path includes a voltage source and a load. The voltage source can be an active element such as a battery or it can be a passive element such as a resistor with the voltage applied externally. The load can be resistive, capacitive or inductive depending on the purpose of the circuit.
- In DC circuits, the current flows in one direction whereas in AC circuits the current reverses its direction periodically. The fundamental elements exhibit different characteristics for DC and AC circuits which must be considered while analyzing circuits.
