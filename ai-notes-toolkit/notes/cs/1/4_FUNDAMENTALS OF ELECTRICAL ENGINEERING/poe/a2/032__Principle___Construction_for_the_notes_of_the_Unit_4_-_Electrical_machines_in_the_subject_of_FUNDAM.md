### Principle & Construction

1. DC Machines
- Principle: Conversion of electrical energy into mechanical energy and vice-versa by rotating coils in a magnetic field (motor) or rotating magnets near coils (generator).
- Construction: Stator (field magnets), Rotor (armature coils), Commutator (switching device), Brushes ( contacts for commutator)
- Types: Series, Shunt, Compound

2. Synchronous Machines
- Principle: Rotor coils rotate synchronously with rotating magnetic field. No slip.
- Construction: Stator (excitation coils), Rotor (armature coils)
- Types: Alternator (Generator), Motor

3. Induction Machines
- Principle: Rotor coils cut rotating magnetic field and induce current. Rotor lags behind magnetic field (slip).
- Construction: Stator (excitation coils), Rotor (armature coils)
- Types: Induction Motor, Induction Generator
