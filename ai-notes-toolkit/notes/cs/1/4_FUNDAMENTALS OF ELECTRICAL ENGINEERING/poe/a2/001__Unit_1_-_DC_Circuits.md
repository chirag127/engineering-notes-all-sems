## Unit 1 - DC Circuits

1. DC Circuit: A circuit consisting of a voltage source, wires, and electronic components that provides a path for direct current.
2. Voltage: The amount of "push" provided by a battery or voltage source. It is measured in volts (V).
3. Current: The rate of electron flow through a conductor, measured in amperes (A).
4. Resistance: A material's opposition to the flow of current. It is measured in ohms (Ω).
5. Ohm's Law: A formula that describes the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance in a DC circuit: V=IR.
6. Series Circuit: A circuit in which all components are connected in a continuous path so the same current flows through all components.
7. Parallel Circuit: A circuit in which components are arranged so the same voltage is applied to all components, but the current divides into separate paths.
