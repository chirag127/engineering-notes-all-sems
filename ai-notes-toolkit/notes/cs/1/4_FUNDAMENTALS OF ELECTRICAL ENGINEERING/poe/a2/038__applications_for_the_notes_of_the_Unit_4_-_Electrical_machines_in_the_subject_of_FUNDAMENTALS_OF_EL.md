### Applications for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines

1.  Motors: Electrical motors are used in various applications such as fans, refrigerators, washing machines, vacuum cleaners, etc. The working principle of an electric motor is based on the magnetic effect of electric current. The motors convert electrical energy into mechanical energy.
2.  Generators: Generators work on the principle of electromagnetic induction. They convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. Generators are used in power plants to generate electricity. The generators use renewable energy sources such as moving water, wind, steam, etc. to produce electricity.
3.  Transformers: Transformers are used to increase or decrease an alternating current (AC) voltage. They work on the principle of mutual induction. Transformers are used in power transmission lines to convert high transmission voltages into lower distribution voltages. They are also used in electronic circuits to convert voltage levels.
4.  Relays: Relays are electromechanical switches that are used to control high voltage and high current circuits using low power signals. They are used in various applications such as telephone exchanges, automatic controls, protection circuits, etc. The working principle of a relay is based on electromagnetic attraction between the electromagnet and armature.
