### Applications of DC Motors (Simple Numerical Problems)

1.  Elevators: DC motors are used to drive the pulleys and cables that lift elevators. The speed of the elevator is controlled by varying the supply voltage to the DC motor.
2.  Electric locomotives: DC motors are used in electric locomotives to power the wheels and control the speed. The speed is controlled by varying the supply voltage.
3.  Electric vehicles: DC motors are used in electric vehicles to power the wheels. The speed is controlled by varying the supply voltage.
4.  Hoists and cranes: DC motors are used to power hoists and cranes to lift heavy loads. The speed is controlled by varying the supply voltage.
5.  Conveyor belts: DC motors are used to drive conveyor belts at a required speed. The speed is controlled by varying the supply voltage.

**Numerical Problems**

1. A 200V DC motor draws 10A when running at 1000 rpm. Find the torque developed by the motor.

* Given: V = 200V, I = 10A, N = 1000rpm
* To find: Torque
* Formula: Torque = (PL)/nm
* P = VI = 200 x 10 = 2000W
* L = (1000/60) x 2pi = 523 rad/sec
* n = 1000/60 = 16.67 (rpm to rad/sec)
* m = 7
* Torque = (2000 x 523)/112 = 47 N-m

2. A DC motor draws a current of 15A from a 150V supply and develops 75N-m of torque. Find the speed of the motor in rpm.

* Given: V = 150V, I = 15A, T = 75 N-m
* To find: Speed (rpm)
* Formula: nm = PL/T
* P = VI = 150 x 15 = 2250W
* L = 2pi/60
* n = (2250 x L)/T = (2250 x 2pi/60)/75 = 1500/7 = 214 rad/sec
* Speed (rpm) = (214/2pi) x 60 = 1000rpm
