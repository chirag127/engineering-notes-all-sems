
### Concept of Linearity for the Notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

1. Linearity is a fundamental concept in electrical engineering and is used to describe the relationship between current, voltage, and resistance.

2. In a linear circuit, the current is proportional to the voltage, and the resistance is constant. This means that if the voltage is increased, the current will increase proportionally.

3. Linearity is an important concept to understand when working with DC circuits because it allows us to make predictions about the behavior of the circuit.

4. We can use Ohm's Law to calculate the current in a linear circuit. Ohm's Law states that the current (I) is equal to the voltage (V) divided by the resistance (R).

5. It is important to note that linearity only applies to DC circuits. AC circuits are not linear and require different methods of analysis.
