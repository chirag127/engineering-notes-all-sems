
### Types of Wires for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

* Wires are used in electrical installations to connect components and devices. 
* The type of wire used depends on the application and the voltage and current requirements. 
* Common types of wires used in electrical installations include copper, aluminum, and steel. 
* Copper wires are the most common and are used for both low and high voltage applications. They are strong and durable, and offer good electrical conductivity. 
* Aluminum wires are also used for low and high voltage applications, but are not as durable as copper wires. They are also not as conductive as copper, but are cheaper and lighter. 
* Steel wires are used in high voltage applications, and are the strongest and most durable of the three types. However, they are not as conductive as copper or aluminum. 
* In addition to these three types, there are also special types of wires used in specific applications, such as fiber optic cables and coaxial cables.
