
### Analysis of single phase AC Circuits consisting R-L-C combination (Series and Parallel)

* Single phase AC circuits consist of a combination of resistors, inductors, and capacitors connected in series and/or parallel.
* The analysis of single phase AC circuits requires the application of Kirchhoff's Voltage and Current Laws, as well as Ohm's Law.
* In a series circuit, the current is the same throughout the circuit, while the voltage and impedance vary depending on the components in the circuit.
* In a parallel circuit, the voltage is the same throughout the circuit, while the current and impedance vary depending on the components in the circuit.
* The impedance of a single phase AC circuit is a complex quantity and can be expressed in terms of its magnitude and phase angle.
* The magnitude of the impedance is determined by the resistive and reactive components of the circuit, while the phase angle is determined by the inductive and capacitive components of the circuit.
* The power dissipated in a single phase AC circuit is determined by the magnitude and phase angle of the impedance.
* The total power dissipated in a single phase AC circuit is equal to the sum of the power dissipated in each component.
