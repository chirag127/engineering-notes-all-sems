
### Regulation and Efficiency for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Transformers in the Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

1. Transformers are electrical devices used to transfer electrical energy from one circuit to another, usually at different voltages.
2. Regulation is the ability of a transformer to maintain a constant voltage ratio between its primary and secondary windings over a range of load conditions.
3. Efficiency is the ratio of the power delivered to the load to the power supplied by the source.
4. Transformers are typically rated in terms of their maximum power handling capacity, regulation and efficiency.
5. The regulation of a transformer is affected by the load current, the winding resistance, the leakage inductance and the core losses.
6. The efficiency of a transformer is affected by the load current, the winding resistance, the leakage inductance, the core losses, and the copper losses in the windings.
7. Transformers can be constructed with either a single winding or multiple windings, and can be configured in either a series or parallel connection.
8. Transformers can be used for either step-up or step-down voltage transformation.
9. Transformers can also be used for isolation purposes, such as protecting sensitive electronic equipment from power surges.
