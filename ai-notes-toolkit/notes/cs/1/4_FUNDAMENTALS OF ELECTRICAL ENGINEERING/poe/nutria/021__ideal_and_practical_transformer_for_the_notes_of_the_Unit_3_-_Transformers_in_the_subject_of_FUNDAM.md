
### Ideal and Practical Transformer

* A transformer is an electrical device that is used to transfer electrical energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic induction.
* Transformers are used in a variety of applications, including power generation, transmission, and distribution, as well as in industrial and consumer electronics.
* The basic principle of a transformer is that it takes an alternating current (AC) input and produces an output voltage that is proportional to the input voltage.
* The core of a transformer is made up of two or more coils of wire, which are wound around a magnetic core.
* The primary coil, or winding, is connected to the input voltage and the secondary coil, or winding, is connected to the output voltage.
* The magnetic flux created by the primary coil induces an alternating current in the secondary coil.
* Transformers are available in a variety of sizes and shapes, and can be used to step up or step down the voltage in a circuit.
* In addition to voltage transformation, transformers can also be used to isolate circuits from one another, to provide impedance matching between circuits, and to provide galvanic isolation between circuits.
