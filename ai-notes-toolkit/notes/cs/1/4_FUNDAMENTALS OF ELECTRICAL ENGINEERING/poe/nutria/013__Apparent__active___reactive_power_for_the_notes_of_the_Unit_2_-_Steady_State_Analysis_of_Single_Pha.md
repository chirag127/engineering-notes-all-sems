
### Apparent, active & reactive power for the notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

* Apparent power (S) is the total power consumed in an AC circuit and is expressed in volt-amperes (VA). It is the vector sum of active power (P) and reactive power (Q). 
* Active power (P) is the real power consumed in an AC circuit and is expressed in watts (W). It is the power used to do work. 
* Reactive power (Q) is the imaginary power consumed in an AC circuit and is expressed in volt-amperes reactive (VAR). It is the power used to store energy in the form of an electric field. 
* The power factor (PF) is the ratio of active power to apparent power and is a measure of how efficiently the power is being used. 
* The power triangle is a graphical representation of the relationship between active power, reactive power, and apparent power. 
* The impedance triangle is a graphical representation of the relationship between the resistance, reactance, and impedance of an AC circuit.
