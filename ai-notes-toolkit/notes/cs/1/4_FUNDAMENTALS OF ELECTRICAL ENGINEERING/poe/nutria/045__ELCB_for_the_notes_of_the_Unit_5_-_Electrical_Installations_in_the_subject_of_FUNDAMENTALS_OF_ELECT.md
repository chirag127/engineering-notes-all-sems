
### ELCB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

* An Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker (ELCB) is a safety device used in electrical installations to protect against electric shock. It detects small leakage current flowing to earth and interrupts the circuit.
* ELCBs are designed to trip quickly and reliably when an earth leakage fault is detected, thus providing a high level of protection against electric shock.
* ELCBs are used in both domestic and commercial applications and are available in a range of sizes and ratings to suit the individual requirements of the installation.
* The installation of an ELCB is a legal requirement in many countries and is essential in protecting against electric shock and other potential hazards.
* ELCBs should be tested regularly to ensure they are functioning correctly. Testing should be carried out by a qualified electrician and should include a full inspection of the installation and the ELCB itself.
