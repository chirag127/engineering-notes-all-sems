
### Concept of Resonance in Series & Parallel Circuits

* Resonance occurs when the impedance of a circuit is at its minimum, resulting in maximum current flow.
* In a series circuit, the total impedance of the circuit is equal to the sum of the individual impedances of the components.
* In a parallel circuit, the total impedance of the circuit is equal to the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals of the individual impedances of the components.
* At resonance, the total impedance of the circuit is at its minimum, resulting in maximum current flow.
* Resonance is used to tune circuits to a specific frequency, allowing for maximum current flow at that frequency.
* Resonance can also be used to reduce the power consumption of a circuit by reducing the total impedance.
