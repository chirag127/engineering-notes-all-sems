
### Types for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. Electric Motors: Electric motors are machines that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy. They are used to power a wide range of applications, from household appliances to large industrial machinery.
2. Generators: Generators are machines that convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. They are used to produce electricity for homes and businesses.
3. Transformers: Transformers are machines that convert electrical energy from one voltage level to another. They are used to increase or decrease the voltage of an electrical circuit.
4. Alternators: Alternators are machines that convert mechanical energy into alternating current (AC) electrical energy. They are used in vehicles to produce electricity for the vehicle's electrical system.
5. Induction Motors: Induction motors are machines that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy. They are used in a variety of applications, from powering electric vehicles to industrial machinery.
6. DC Motors: DC motors are machines that convert direct current (DC) electrical energy into mechanical energy. They are used in a variety of applications, from powering small electric vehicles to industrial machinery.
