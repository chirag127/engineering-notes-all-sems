
### Form and Peak Factors for the Notes of Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits

* Form factor is the ratio of the root mean square value of an alternating current (RMS) to the maximum or peak value of the current. It is a measure of the waveform distortion and is expressed as a number between 0 and 1.
* Peak factor is the ratio of the peak value of an alternating current (RMS) to the maximum or peak value of the current. It is a measure of the waveform distortion and is expressed as a number between 0 and 1.
* Average power in an AC circuit is equal to the product of the RMS voltage, the RMS current and the power factor.
* The power factor of an AC circuit is the ratio of the real power to the apparent power, which is the product of the RMS voltage and the RMS current.
* The impedance of an AC circuit is the ratio of the RMS voltage to the RMS current. It is a measure of the opposition to the flow of current in the circuit.
* The reactance of an AC circuit is the ratio of the peak voltage to the peak current. It is a measure of the opposition to the flow of current in the circuit.
