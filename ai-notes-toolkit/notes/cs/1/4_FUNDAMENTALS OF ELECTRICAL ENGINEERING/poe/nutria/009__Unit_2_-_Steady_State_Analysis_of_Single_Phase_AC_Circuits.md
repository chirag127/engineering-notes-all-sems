

## Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits

* AC circuits can be classified into two types: single-phase and three-phase. 
* Single-phase AC circuits are used for residential and small commercial applications. 
* The steady-state analysis of single-phase AC circuits involves the use of complex numbers, phasors, and impedance parameters. 
* The voltage and current waveforms in a single-phase AC circuit can be represented by a sinusoidal waveform. 
* The peak values of the voltage and current waveforms are known as the peak voltage and peak current, respectively. 
* The frequency of the AC waveform is determined by the number of cycles per second, or Hertz (Hz). 
* The phase angle between the voltage and current waveforms is known as the phase angle. 
* The impedance of an AC circuit is the ratio of the voltage to the current. 
* The reactance of an AC circuit is the opposition to the flow of current due to the inductive and capacitive elements in the circuit. 
* The power in an AC circuit can be calculated using the power triangle and the power factor.
