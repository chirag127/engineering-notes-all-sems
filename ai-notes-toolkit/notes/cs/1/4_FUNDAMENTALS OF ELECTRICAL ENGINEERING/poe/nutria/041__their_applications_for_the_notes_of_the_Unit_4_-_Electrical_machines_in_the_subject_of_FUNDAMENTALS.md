
### Applications of Electrical Machines

1. Electric motors are used in a wide variety of applications, including powering industrial machinery, household appliances, elevators, and electric vehicles.
2. Generators are used to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. Common applications include power plants, wind turbines, and hydroelectric dams.
3. Transformers are used to increase or decrease the voltage of an electrical signal. They are commonly used in power distribution systems.
4. Electric drives are used to control the speed and torque of electric motors. They are used in a variety of industrial applications, such as robotics and machine tools.
5. Electric relays are used to control the flow of electricity in circuits. They are used in a variety of applications, such as controlling motors and lighting systems.
6. Electric actuators are used to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy. They are used in a variety of applications, such as controlling valves and opening doors.
