
### Introduction of Switch Fuse Unit (SFU)

* A Switch Fuse Unit (SFU) is a type of circuit breaker that is designed to protect electrical circuits from overloading and short circuits. 
* SFUs are typically installed in homes and businesses to provide protection against circuit overloads and short circuits. 
* SFUs are usually composed of two components: a switch and a fuse. The switch is used to turn the circuit on and off, while the fuse is used to detect any excessive current and disconnect the circuit if the current exceeds a predetermined value. 
* SFUs offer a number of advantages over other types of circuit breakers, such as being able to detect and disconnect a circuit quickly in the event of an overload or short circuit. 
* SFUs are also relatively inexpensive and easy to install, making them a popular choice for residential and commercial applications.
