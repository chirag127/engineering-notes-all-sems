
### Power Factor for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

1. Power factor (PF) is a measure of how effectively electrical energy is being used in an AC circuit.

2. PF is defined as the ratio of the real power (P) to the apparent power (S) in an AC circuit. 

3. It is expressed as a number between 0 and 1, where 1 is the ideal power factor.

4. Low power factor can cause problems such as increased current, voltage drop, and reduced efficiency.

5. To improve power factor, capacitors can be used to store energy and release it when needed.

6. Power factor can also be improved by using more efficient motors and other electrical equipment.
