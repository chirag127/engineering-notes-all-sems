
### Equivalent Circuit for Unit 3 - Transformers in Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering
1. A transformer is an electrical device that transfers energy from one electrical circuit to another by means of electromagnetic induction.
2. The primary and secondary windings of a transformer are magnetically coupled, and the ratio of their number of turns determines the transformation ratio.
3. The equivalent circuit of a transformer consists of a primary winding, a secondary winding, and a magnetizing inductance.
4. The magnetizing inductance is the inductance of the transformer core, which is represented by an inductor in the equivalent circuit.
5. The primary winding of the transformer is represented by a voltage source and a series impedance, while the secondary winding is represented by a current source and a series impedance.
6. The impedances of the primary and secondary windings of the transformer are determined by the resistance of the windings and the leakage reactance of the transformer core.
7. The equivalent circuit of a transformer can be used to determine the voltage and current relationships between the primary and secondary windings of the transformer.
