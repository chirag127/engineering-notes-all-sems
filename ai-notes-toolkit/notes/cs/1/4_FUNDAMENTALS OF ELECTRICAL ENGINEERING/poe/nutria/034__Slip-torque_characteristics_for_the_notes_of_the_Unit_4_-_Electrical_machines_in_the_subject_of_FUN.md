
### Slip-torque characteristics for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

* Slip-torque is the torque produced by an electrical machine when it is operating at a speed that is different from its synchronous speed. 
* Slip-torque characteristics of an electrical machine depend on the type of machine, the number of poles, the load, and the speed. 
* Slip-torque can be calculated by subtracting the synchronous torque from the total torque. 
* The slip-torque characteristics of a machine are usually determined by testing the machine under various conditions. 
* Slip-torque characteristics are important in applications such as electric motors and generators, where the speed of the machine must be controlled in order to achieve the desired performance. 
* Slip-torque characteristics can also be used to determine the efficiency of an electrical machine.
