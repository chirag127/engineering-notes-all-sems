
### Representation of Sinusoidal waveforms – Average and effective values for the notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

* Sinusoidal waveforms are periodic, alternating current (AC) signals that can be represented by a sine or cosine function.
* The average value of a sinusoidal waveform is the average of its instantaneous values over a given period. It is also known as the DC offset.
* The effective (or root mean square) value of a sinusoidal waveform is the square root of the average of the squares of its instantaneous values over a given period.
* The effective value of a sinusoidal waveform is always greater than its average value.
* The effective value of a sinusoidal waveform can be calculated by multiplying its peak value by 0.707.
