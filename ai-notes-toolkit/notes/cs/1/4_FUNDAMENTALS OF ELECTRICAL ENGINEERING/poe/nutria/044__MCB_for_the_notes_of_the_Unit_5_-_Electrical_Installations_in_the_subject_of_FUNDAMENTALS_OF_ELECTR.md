
### MCB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. A Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB) is a device used to protect electrical circuits from damage due to overload or short circuit.
2. It is a type of electrical switch that automatically trips when the current flowing through it exceeds a predetermined limit.
3. MCBs are commonly used in residential, commercial and industrial applications.
4. MCBs are designed to protect against overloads, short circuits and ground faults.
5. MCBs are typically rated according to their current capacity, which is the maximum current they can safely carry.
6. MCBs are available in different sizes and ratings, and are rated for different types of loads.
7. MCBs are typically installed in the main electrical panel, and may also be installed in sub-panels and other locations.
8. MCBs are typically tested and certified to meet applicable safety standards.
9. When selecting an MCB, it is important to select one that is suitable for the application and has the appropriate rating.
