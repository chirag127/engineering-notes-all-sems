
### Principle & Construction for the Notes of Unit 4 - Electrical Machines

* Electrical machines are devices used to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa.
* They can be divided into two categories: rotary machines and linear machines.
* Rotary machines include electric motors, generators, and alternators.
* Linear machines include transformers and solenoids.
* The basic principle of operation of all electrical machines is based on the law of electromagnetic induction.
* The three main components of an electrical machine are the stator, rotor, and commutator.
* The stator is the stationary part of the machine and is usually made of coils of insulated wire wound around a laminated steel core.
* The rotor is the rotating part of the machine and is usually made of a coil of insulated wire wound around a laminated steel core.
* The commutator is a switch that controls the current flow in the rotor.
* The stator and rotor are connected to each other through an electrical circuit.
* The stator and rotor are connected to the commutator and the current flow is controlled by the commutator.
* The current flow in the stator and rotor creates a magnetic field, which in turn creates a torque on the rotor.
* This torque causes the rotor to rotate, thus producing mechanical energy.
