
### Types of Electrical Machines

1. **DC Machines**: DC machines are the most commonly used electrical machines in the industry. They are used to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy, and vice versa. The DC machines are classified into two main types:

* **Synchronous Machines**: Synchronous machines are used for generating and transmitting electrical power. These machines are used in power plants and other large scale industries.

* **Induction Machines**: Induction machines are used for smaller scale applications. They are used in household appliances, electric motors, and other small scale applications.

2. **AC Machines**: AC machines are used for generating and transmitting electrical power. These machines are used in power plants and other large scale industries. The AC machines are classified into three main types:

* **Synchronous Machines**: Synchronous machines are used for generating and transmitting electrical power. These machines are used in power plants and other large scale industries.

* **Induction Machines**: Induction machines are used for smaller scale applications. They are used in household appliances, electric motors, and other small scale applications.

* **Transformer Machines**: Transformer machines are used to convert electrical energy from one voltage level to another. These machines are used in power plants and other large scale industries.
