
### Concept of Active and Passive Elements for the Notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

* Active elements are components in a circuit that require an external source of energy, such as a battery or power supply, in order to function. Examples of active elements include transistors, resistors, capacitors, and inductors. 
* Passive elements are components in a circuit that do not require an external source of energy to function. Examples of passive elements include resistors, capacitors, and inductors. 
* A DC circuit is a circuit that only contains direct current (DC) components. Examples of components that may be found in a DC circuit include resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transistors. 
* Resistors are passive elements that are used to control the flow of current in a circuit. They come in many different shapes and sizes, and can be used to control the voltage and current in a circuit. 
* Capacitors are passive elements that are used to store electrical energy. They come in many different shapes and sizes, and can be used to control the voltage and current in a circuit. 
* Inductors are passive elements that are used to store energy in the form of a magnetic field. They come in many different shapes and sizes, and can be used to control the voltage and current in a circuit. 
* Transistors are active elements that are used to control the flow of current in a circuit. They come in many different shapes and sizes, and can be used to control the voltage and current in a circuit.
