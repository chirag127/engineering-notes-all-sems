
### Voltage and Current Sources for the Notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

* Voltage sources are devices that are capable of producing a constant voltage. Examples of voltage sources include batteries, generators, and power supplies.
* Current sources are devices that are capable of producing a constant current. Examples of current sources include current generators, current mirrors, and current amplifiers.
* Voltage sources and current sources can be used in combination to create a circuit that produces a desired output.
* Voltage sources and current sources can also be used to control the amount of current and voltage in a circuit. This is known as voltage and current regulation.
* Voltage and current sources are important components in the design of electrical circuits. They are used to control the flow of electricity and to ensure that the circuit operates as desired.
