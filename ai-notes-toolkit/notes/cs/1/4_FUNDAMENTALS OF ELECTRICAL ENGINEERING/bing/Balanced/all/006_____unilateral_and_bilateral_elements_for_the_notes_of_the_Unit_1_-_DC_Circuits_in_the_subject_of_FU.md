# Unilateral and Bilateral Elements

- Unilateral and bilateral elements are the two different types of electrical / electronic circuit elements on the basis of their V-I characteristics on reversal of voltage polarity.
- Unilateral elements are those that allow the current in only one direction and offer different impedance in different directions of current flow . Examples: Diodes, Transistors, etc.
- Bilateral elements are those that allow the current in both directions and offer the same impedance in either direction of current flow . Examples: Resistors, Inductors, Capacitors, etc.
- Unilateral and bilateral circuits are the circuits that contain only unilateral or only bilateral elements, or a combination of both, respectively.
- Unilateral and bilateral circuits have different properties and applications, such as:
  - Unilateral circuits are used for rectification, switching, modulation, etc. Bilateral circuits are used for filtering, impedance matching, signal processing, etc.
  - Unilateral circuits are non-linear and asymmetric. Bilateral circuits are linear and symmetric.
  - Unilateral circuits are more sensitive to noise and distortion. Bilateral circuits are more robust and stable.
  - Unilateral circuits are more difficult to analyze and design. Bilateral circuits are simpler and easier to handle.
