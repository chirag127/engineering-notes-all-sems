# Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

- A three-phase system can be connected in two different configurations: star (or Y) and delta (or Δ).
- In a star connection, one end of each phase winding is connected to a common point called the neutral point, and the other end is connected to the line terminal. The line voltage is the voltage between any two line terminals, and the phase voltage is the voltage between any line terminal and the neutral point.
- In a delta connection, each phase winding is connected between two line terminals, forming a closed loop. The line voltage is the same as the phase voltage, and the line current is the current flowing in any line.
- The voltage and current relations in star and delta connections are as follows:

## Star Connection

- Line voltage = √3 × phase voltage
- Line current = phase current
- Power = 3 × phase voltage × phase current × power factor

## Delta Connection

- Line voltage = phase voltage
- Line current = √3 × phase current
- Power = 3 × phase voltage × phase current × power factor

- The power transmitted by both the connections is the same, as long as the phase voltage and phase current are the same in both cases.
