# Unit 4 - Electrical machines

- Electrical machines are devices that use electromagnetic forces to convert electrical energy to mechanical energy or vice versa.
- Electrical machines can be classified into three main types: transformers, generators and motors.
- Transformers are electrical machines that transfer electrical power from one circuit to another without changing the frequency, but with a different voltage and current.
- Generators are electrical machines that convert mechanical power to electrical power by inducing an electromotive force in a coil of wire that rotates in a magnetic field.
- Motors are electrical machines that convert electrical power to mechanical power by creating a torque on a shaft that rotates in a magnetic field.
- Electrical machines can also be classified based on the type of current they use: direct current (DC) or alternating current (AC).
- DC machines are electrical machines that operate on DC power sources, such as batteries or rectifiers. They have commutators and brushes to change the direction of the current in the coil as it rotates.
- AC machines are electrical machines that operate on AC power sources, such as generators or inverters. They have slip rings and brushes to transfer the current to the coil as it rotates.
- AC machines can be further classified into synchronous machines and induction machines.
- Synchronous machines are AC machines that rotate at a constant speed that is proportional to the frequency of the AC power source. They have permanent magnets or electromagnets to create the magnetic field.
- Induction machines are AC machines that rotate at a speed that is slightly less than the synchronous speed. They have no magnets, but rely on the induction of a magnetic field in the rotor by the stator.
- The principles and analysis of electrical machines involve the concepts of magnetic circuits, electromechanical energy conversion, equivalent circuits, power and torque, efficiency and losses, and speed control.
- The design of electrical machines involves the selection of materials, dimensions, winding configurations, cooling methods, and protection devices.
