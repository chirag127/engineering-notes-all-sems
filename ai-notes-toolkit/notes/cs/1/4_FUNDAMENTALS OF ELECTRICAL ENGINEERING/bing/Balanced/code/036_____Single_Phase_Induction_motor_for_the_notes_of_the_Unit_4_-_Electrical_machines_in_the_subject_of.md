### Single Phase Induction Motor

A single phase induction motor is a type of electric motor that operates on single phase alternating current. It is similar to a three phase induction motor in physical appearance, except that the stator has two windings instead of three, and the rotor is a squirrel cage type with no electrical connection to the stator .

Some of the main features of a single phase induction motor are:

- It has no starting torque, because the stator produces a pulsating magnetic field, not a rotating one .
- It requires a special arrangement to make it self-starting, such as a capacitor, a shaded pole, or a split phase winding .
- It has low efficiency, low power factor, and high starting current compared to a three phase induction motor .
- It is used for low power applications, such as fans, pumps, blowers, washing machines, etc .

Some of the common types of single phase induction motors are:

- Capacitor start induction motor: It has a capacitor connected in series with the auxiliary winding, which creates a phase difference between the main and auxiliary windings, and produces a rotating magnetic field at starting .
- Capacitor run induction motor: It has a capacitor connected in parallel with the auxiliary winding, which improves the power factor and reduces the starting current of the motor .
- Permanent split capacitor motor: It has a capacitor permanently connected in parallel with the auxiliary winding, which makes it suitable for variable speed and reversible applications .
- Shaded pole induction motor: It has a copper ring or a short-circuited coil attached to a part of each pole, which creates a shaded area and a phase difference between the shaded and unshaded parts of the pole, and produces a rotating magnetic field at starting .
- Split phase induction motor: It has a high resistance and low inductance auxiliary winding, which creates a phase difference between the main and auxiliary windings, and produces a rotating magnetic field at starting. The auxiliary winding is disconnected by a centrifugal switch after the motor reaches a certain speed .
