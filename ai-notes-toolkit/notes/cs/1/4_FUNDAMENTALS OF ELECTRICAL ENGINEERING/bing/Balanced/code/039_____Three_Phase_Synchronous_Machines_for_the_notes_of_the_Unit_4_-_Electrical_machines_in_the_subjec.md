### Three Phase Synchronous Machines

- A three phase synchronous machine is a type of electric machine that can operate as either a generator or a motor.
- It consists of a stator and a rotor. The stator has a three phase winding that is connected to the AC supply. The rotor has a DC field winding that is excited by a separate DC source or by a self-excited system.
- The rotor field produces a magnetic flux that rotates at a constant speed, called the synchronous speed. The synchronous speed is determined by the number of poles and the frequency of the AC supply, as given by the equation:

`N_s = 120f/P`

where N_s is the synchronous speed in rpm, f is the frequency in Hz, and P is the number of poles.

- The stator winding also produces a rotating magnetic flux that has the same speed and frequency as the rotor flux. The interaction of the two fluxes generates a torque that causes the rotor to rotate at the synchronous speed, in synchronism with the stator flux.
- A three phase synchronous machine can be classified into two types: cylindrical rotor or round rotor, and salient pole or projecting pole. The cylindrical rotor has a smooth and uniform surface, while the salient pole has protruding poles on the rotor surface. The cylindrical rotor is used for high speed applications, such as steam turbines, while the salient pole is used for low speed applications, such as hydro turbines and single phase motors.
- A three phase synchronous machine can be operated in two modes: as a generator or as a motor. When the rotor is driven by a prime mover, such as a turbine, the machine acts as a generator and converts mechanical energy into electrical energy. When the rotor is supplied by a DC source, the machine acts as a motor and converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
- A three phase synchronous generator can be connected to a three phase load or to a three phase power system. The voltage and frequency of the generator are controlled by the rotor field excitation and the speed of the prime mover. The power and power factor of the generator are controlled by the load and the power system. The generator can operate at different power factors, such as lagging, leading, or unity, depending on the load and the system requirements.
- A three phase synchronous motor can be started by various methods, such as damper winding, pony motor, or variable frequency supply. The motor can operate at a constant speed and a constant power factor, which can be adjusted by varying the rotor field excitation. The motor can also operate at a variable speed by using a variable frequency supply.
