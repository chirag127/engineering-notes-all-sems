### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

- A three-phase system can be connected in two different styles: star or delta .
- In a star connection, the three phase windings are connected in such a way that one end of each winding is joined together to form a common neutral point, and the other end of each winding is connected to the line conductor .
- In a delta connection, the three phase windings are connected in such a way that the end of one winding is connected to the start of another winding, forming a closed loop, and each junction point is connected to the line conductor .
- The line voltage is the voltage between any two line conductors, and the phase voltage is the voltage across any phase winding.
- The line current is the current flowing in any line conductor, and the phase current is the current flowing in any phase winding.
- In a star connection, the line current is equal to the phase current, whereas in a delta connection, the line current is equal to root three times of the phase current .
- In a star connection, the line voltage is equal to root three times of the phase voltage, whereas in a delta connection, the line voltage is equal to the phase voltage .
- The power transmitted by both the connections is the same, given that the line voltage and the line current are the same.
- The star connection is suitable for low voltage applications, and the delta connection is suitable for high voltage applications.
- The star connection can be derived in both 3 phase 4 wire and 3 phase 3 wire systems, whereas the delta connection can be derived only in 3 phase 3 wire systems.
