## Unit 5 - Electrical Installations

- Electrical installations are the construction or installation of electrical wiring and the permanent attachment or installation of electrical products in or on any structure that is not itself an electrical product.
- Electrical installations also include the installation, repair, alteration and maintenance of electrical conductors, fittings, devices and fixtures for heating, lighting or power purposes, regardless of the voltage.
- Electrical installations must comply with the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) standards, which provide guidelines for the design, installation, inspection and maintenance of low to medium-voltage electrical installations.
- Electrical installations require professional and licensed installers who are insured and background checked, to ensure the safety and quality of the work.
- Electrical installations can be classified into different types, such as residential, commercial, industrial, or public, depending on the purpose and location of the installation.
- Electrical installations can also be categorized into different levels, such as low voltage (LV), medium voltage (MV), or high voltage (HV), depending on the voltage range of the installation.
- Electrical installations involve various components, such as wires, cables, switches, sockets, outlets, circuit breakers, fuses, transformers, generators, motors, lights, appliances, and other electrical devices and fixtures.
- Electrical installations must follow the principles of electrical engineering, such as Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, power factor, voltage drop, current flow, resistance, impedance, capacitance, inductance, and other electrical concepts and calculations.
