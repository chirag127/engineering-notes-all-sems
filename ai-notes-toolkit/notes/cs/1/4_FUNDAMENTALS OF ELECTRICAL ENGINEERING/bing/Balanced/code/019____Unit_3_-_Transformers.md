## Unit 3 - Transformers

- A transformer is a device that converts alternating current (AC) from one state to another. It can be designed to “step up” or “step down” the voltage and current in the process, which is very useful for various applications  .
- A transformer works on the principle of magnetic induction. It consists of two or more coils of wire, called the primary and secondary coils, that are wound around a common core, usually made of iron or steel .
- When an AC voltage is applied to the primary coil, it creates a varying magnetic field in the core, which induces a varying voltage in the secondary coil. The ratio of the primary and secondary voltages depends on the number of turns of wire in each coil .
- A transformer can be classified into different types based on its construction, function, and application. Some common types are:
  - Step-up transformer: A transformer that increases the voltage from the primary to the secondary coil. It has more turns of wire in the secondary coil than in the primary coil. It is used to raise the voltage from electric generators so that electric power can be transmitted over long distances.
  - Step-down transformer: A transformer that decreases the voltage from the primary to the secondary coil. It has fewer turns of wire in the secondary coil than in the primary coil. It is used to reduce the voltage of conventional power circuits to operate low-voltage devices, such as doorbells and toy electric trains.
  - Isolation transformer: A transformer that has the same number of turns of wire in the primary and secondary coils, and thus the same voltage. It is used to isolate two circuits from each other, and to provide safety and protection from electric shocks and surges.
  - Auto-transformer: A transformer that has only one coil of wire, with a tap that divides it into two parts. The part connected to the input voltage is the primary, and the part connected to the output voltage is the secondary. It is used to provide small changes in voltage, and to save space and cost.
  - Current transformer: A transformer that measures the current in a circuit. It has a primary coil that is connected in series with the circuit, and a secondary coil that is connected to a meter or a relay. It is used to monitor and control the current flow, and to protect the circuit from overcurrent.
  - Potential transformer: A transformer that measures the voltage in a circuit. It has a primary coil that is connected in parallel with the circuit, and a secondary coil that is connected to a meter or a relay. It is used to monitor and control the voltage level, and to protect the circuit from overvoltage.
