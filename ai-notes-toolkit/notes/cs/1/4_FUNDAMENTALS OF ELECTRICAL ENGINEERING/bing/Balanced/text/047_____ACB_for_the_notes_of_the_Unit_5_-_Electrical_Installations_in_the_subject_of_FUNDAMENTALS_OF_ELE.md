### ACB

- ACB stands for Air Circuit Breaker, which is an electrical device used to provide overcurrent and short-circuit protection for electric circuits over 800 Amps to 10kA   .
- ACB operates at atmospheric pressure in air, where air-blast as an arc quenching medium .
- ACB is used in low voltage applications below 450V, such as distribution panels , and also in high voltage applications up to 15kV.
- ACB has three main components: a fixed contact, a moving contact and an arc runner .
- ACB has two types of operating mechanisms: spring operated and pneumatic .
- ACB has three types of arc extinction methods: plain air break, air chute and axial blast .
- ACB has several advantages, such as high speed of operation, low maintenance, high reliability, easy replacement and low noise  .
- ACB has some disadvantages, such as high cost, large size, high air pressure requirement, and possibility of fire hazard .
- ACB has various applications, such as industrial plants, power stations, substations, switchboards, and motor control centers  .
