### Concept of active and passive elements

- Active and passive elements are two types of electronic circuit elements that differ in their ability to supply or consume energy.
- Active elements are those that can produce electrical energy or provide amplification to a circuit. They can control the direction and magnitude of current or voltage in a circuit. They have the function of gain for electronic circuits. Examples of active elements are batteries, generators, transistors, op-amps, etc.    
- Passive elements are those that can only receive, store, or dissipate electrical energy. They cannot control the current or voltage in a circuit on their own. They do not have the function of gain for electronic circuits. Examples of passive elements are resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, etc.    
- Active and passive elements are used for different purposes in electrical circuits. Active elements are used for current and voltage control, signal processing, amplification, oscillation, etc. Passive elements are used for energy storage, discharge, filtering, impedance matching, etc.

|---------------------------------------|----------------|-----------------|

| Impervious in nature i.e. cannot supply energy to the circuit | No | Yes |
| Controls current direction | Yes | No |
| Have function of gain for electronic circuits | Yes | No |
| Used for current control and voltage control, signal processing, amplification, oscillation, etc. | Yes | No |
| Used for energy storage, discharge, filtering, impedance matching, etc. | No | Yes |
