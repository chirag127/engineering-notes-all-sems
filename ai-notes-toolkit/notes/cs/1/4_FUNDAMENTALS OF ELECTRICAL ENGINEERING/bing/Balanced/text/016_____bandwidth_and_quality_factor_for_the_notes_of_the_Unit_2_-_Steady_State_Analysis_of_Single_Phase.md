### Bandwidth and Quality Factor for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

- Bandwidth of a resonant circuit is the range of frequencies over which the current or voltage amplitude is equal to or greater than 70.7% of its maximum value .
- Quality factor of a resonant circuit is a dimensionless quantity that measures how underdamped the circuit is, or how narrow the bandwidth is in relation to the resonant frequency .
- The quality factor of an AC circuit is related to bandwidth as inversely proportional, i.e., Q = f_r / BW, where f_r is the resonant frequency and BW is the bandwidth .
- The quality factor and the bandwidth of a resonant circuit depend on whether the circuit is series or parallel. For a series circuit, Q = X_L / R and BW = R / L, where X_L is the inductive reactance, R is the resistance, and L is the inductance. For a parallel circuit, Q = R / X_L and BW = L / RC, where C is the capacitance.
- A high Q resonant circuit has a narrow bandwidth and a sharp resonance peak, whereas a low Q resonant circuit has a wide bandwidth and a flat resonance peak .
