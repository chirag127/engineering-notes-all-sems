### Voltage and Current Sources

Voltage and current sources are fundamental components of electrical circuits. They are used to provide power to the circuit and to control the flow of current.

1. **Voltage sources** provide a constant voltage across their terminals, regardless of the current flowing through them. Examples of voltage sources include batteries and power supplies.

2. **Current sources** provide a constant current through their terminals, regardless of the voltage across them. Examples of current sources include photovoltaic cells and constant current generators.

3. Voltage and current sources can be either independent or dependent. Independent sources provide a fixed voltage or current, while dependent sources provide a voltage or current that is controlled by another variable in the circuit.

4. In DC circuits, voltage and current sources are typically represented by symbols in circuit diagrams. The symbol for a voltage source is a circle with a "+" sign inside, while the symbol for a current source is a circle with an arrow pointing in the direction of the current flow.

5. Voltage and current sources are essential for the analysis and design of DC circuits. They allow us to control the flow of current and to provide power to the circuit components.
