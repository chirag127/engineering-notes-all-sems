### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

#### Star Connection:
- In a star connection, one end of each winding is connected to a common point, called the neutral point.
- The phase voltage is the voltage across each winding.
- The line voltage is the voltage between any two lines.
- The line voltage is equal to the square root of 3 times the phase voltage.
- The phase current is the current flowing through each winding.
- The line current is equal to the phase current.

#### Delta Connection:
- In a delta connection, the windings are connected end-to-end to form a closed loop.
- The phase voltage is the voltage across each winding.
- The line voltage is equal to the phase voltage.
- The phase current is the current flowing through each winding.
- The line current is equal to the square root of 3 times the phase current.
