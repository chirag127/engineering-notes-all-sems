### Cables and Bus-bars

Cables and bus-bars are used in electrical installations to distribute electrical power. Here are some key points to note:

1. **Cables** are insulated conductors used to transmit electrical power from one point to another. They are available in different sizes and types, depending on the voltage level and current-carrying capacity required.

2. **Bus-bars** are thick strips of copper or aluminum used to distribute electrical power within switchgear, distribution boards, and substations. They are designed to carry large currents and are typically uninsulated.

3. The selection of cables and bus-bars depends on several factors, including the voltage level, current-carrying capacity, and the environment in which they will be installed.

4. Cables and bus-bars must be properly sized and installed to ensure the safe and efficient operation of the electrical installation.

5. Proper maintenance of cables and bus-bars is essential to prevent failures and ensure the reliability of the electrical installation.
