## Unit 5 - Electrical Installations

1. Electrical installations refer to the fixed electrical equipment that is supplied through the electrical wiring system of a building or structure.
2. Electrical installations include equipment such as lighting fixtures, power outlets, switches, and circuit breakers.
3. Electrical installations must comply with local and national electrical codes and regulations to ensure safety and proper functioning.
4. Electrical installations are typically performed by licensed electricians who have the necessary training and expertise to install and maintain electrical systems.
5. Proper electrical installations are essential for the safe and efficient operation of electrical equipment and appliances.
6. Electrical installations can vary in complexity, from simple installations in residential homes to complex installations in commercial and industrial buildings.
7. Regular maintenance and inspection of electrical installations is important to ensure their continued safe and efficient operation.
8. Upgrades and improvements to electrical installations can improve energy efficiency and reduce energy costs.
