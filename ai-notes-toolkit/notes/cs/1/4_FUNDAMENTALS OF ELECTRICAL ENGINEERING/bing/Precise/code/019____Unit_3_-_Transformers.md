## Unit 3 - Transformers

Transformers are electrical devices that transfer electrical energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic induction. They are used to increase or decrease the voltage of an alternating current (AC) power supply.

Some key points to remember about transformers are:

1. Transformers operate on the principle of electromagnetic induction.
2. They consist of two or more coils of wire wrapped around a magnetic core.
3. The primary coil is connected to the input power supply, while the secondary coil is connected to the output load.
4. When an AC voltage is applied to the primary coil, it creates a magnetic field that induces a voltage in the secondary coil.
5. The ratio of the number of turns in the primary coil to the number of turns in the secondary coil determines the voltage transformation ratio.
6. Transformers can be used to step-up or step-down the voltage of an AC power supply.
7. They are widely used in power transmission and distribution systems, as well as in electronic devices such as power adapters and chargers.
