### Form and Peak Factors

- In the steady-state analysis of single-phase AC circuits, sinusoidal waveforms are represented using average and effective values, form and peak factors.
- The peak-to-peak value of an alternating quantity is the value from its positive peak to its negative peak.
- The average value of an alternating quantity is the arithmetic mean of all the values over a complete cycle.
- The form factor is the ratio of the effective value to the average value of an alternating quantity.
- The peak factor is the ratio of the maximum value to the effective value of an alternating quantity.
