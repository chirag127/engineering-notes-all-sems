### Types of Batteries

Batteries can be broadly divided into two major types:
1. Primary Cell / Primary battery
2. Secondary Cell / Secondary battery

Based on the application of the battery, they can be classified again. They are:
- Household Batteries: These are the types of batteries which are more likely to be known to the common man.

The following are the types of batteries that are explained with their uses:
1. Lead-acid batteries
2. Nickel-cadmium batteries (Ni-Cd)
3. Nickel-metal hybrid batteries (Ni-MH)
4. Lithium-ion batteries (Li-ion)
5. Alkaline batteries
6. Zinc-carbon batteries
7. Coin cell batteries
8. Zinc-air cells
9. Sealed lead-acid batteries.
