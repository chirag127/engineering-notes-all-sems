# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Electrical engineering is a branch of engineering that deals with the study and application of electricity, electronics, and electromagnetism. Here are some fundamental concepts in electrical engineering:

1. **Electric charge:** Electric charge is a fundamental property of matter that results from the presence or absence of electrons. It is measured in coulombs (C).

2. **Electric current:** Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor. It is measured in amperes (A).

3. **Voltage:** Voltage, also known as electric potential difference, is the measure of the electric potential energy per unit charge in an electrical circuit. It is measured in volts (V).

4. **Resistance:** Resistance is the measure of the opposition to the flow of electric current through a conductor. It is measured in ohms (Ω).

5. **Ohm's Law:** Ohm's Law states that the current through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points.

6. **Power:** Power is the rate at which energy is transferred or used. In electrical systems, it is measured in watts (W).

7. **Circuits:** An electric circuit is a closed path through which electric current flows. It consists of various components such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors.
