## Unit 5 - Electrical Installations

1. Electrical installations refer to the fixed electrical equipment that is supplied through the electrical wiring system of a building or structure.
2. These installations include items such as lighting fixtures, power outlets, switches, and circuit breakers.
3. Electrical installations must be designed and installed in accordance with local and national electrical codes to ensure safety and proper operation.
4. The design of an electrical installation takes into account factors such as the intended use of the building, the electrical load requirements, and the location of the electrical equipment.
5. Proper installation of electrical equipment is essential to prevent electrical hazards such as electric shock, fire, and electrocution.
6. Regular maintenance and inspection of electrical installations is necessary to ensure their continued safe and efficient operation.
7. Electrical installations can be upgraded or modified to accommodate changes in the electrical load requirements or to improve energy efficiency.
8. Qualified electricians should be consulted for the design, installation, and maintenance of electrical installations.
