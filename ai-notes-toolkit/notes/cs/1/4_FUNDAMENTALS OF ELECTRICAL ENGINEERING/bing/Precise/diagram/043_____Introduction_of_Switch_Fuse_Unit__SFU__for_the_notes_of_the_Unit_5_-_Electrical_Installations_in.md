### Introduction of Switch Fuse Unit (SFU)

A Switch Fuse Unit (SFU) is an electrical device used for protection and isolation of electrical circuits. It is a combination of a switch and a fuse unit, where the switch is used to isolate the circuit and the fuse is used to protect the circuit from overcurrent.

Some key points to note about SFUs are:

1. SFUs are used in low voltage electrical installations.
2. They are used to protect and isolate electrical circuits.
3. SFUs are available in different current ratings to suit the needs of different circuits.
4. The fuse unit in an SFU can be easily replaced in case of a fuse blowout.
5. SFUs are commonly used in industrial and commercial electrical installations.
