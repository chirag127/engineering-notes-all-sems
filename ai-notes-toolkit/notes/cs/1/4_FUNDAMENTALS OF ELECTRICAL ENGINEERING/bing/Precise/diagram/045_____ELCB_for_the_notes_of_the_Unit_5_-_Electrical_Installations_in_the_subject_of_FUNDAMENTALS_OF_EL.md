### ELCB

- ELCB stands for Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker.
- It is a safety device used in electrical installations with high Earth impedance to prevent electric shocks .
- It detects small stray voltages on the metal enclosures of electrical equipment and interrupts the circuit if a dangerous voltage is detected .
- The main function of an ELCB is to prevent shock while electrical installations through high Earth impedance .
- It identifies tiny stray voltages on top of the electrical equipment with a metal enclosure and disrupts the circuit if a hazardous voltage is identified .
- It monitors the leakage current that flows out of the circuit through any unintended path .
- It cannot protect against overloading or short circuit current .
