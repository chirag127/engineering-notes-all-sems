### Concept of Active and Passive Elements

#### Unit 1 - DC Circuits

##### Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

An electrical element is considered active if it is capable of generating energy, while a passive element is one that consumes or stores energy.

- **Active Elements:** These elements are capable of generating energy in the form of voltage or current. Examples of active elements include batteries, generators, and operational amplifiers. Active elements are typically used to provide power to a circuit.

- **Passive Elements:** These elements consume or store energy, but do not generate it. Examples of passive elements include resistors, capacitors, and inductors. Passive elements are used to control the flow of current or voltage in a circuit, or to store energy for later use.

In summary, active elements generate energy while passive elements consume or store it. These concepts are fundamental to the understanding of DC circuits in electrical engineering.
