### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

In the subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering, Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits, the voltage and current relations in star and delta connections are important concepts to understand.

#### Star Connection

In a star connection, the phase voltage is equal to the line voltage divided by the square root of 3. The phase current is equal to the line current.

#### Delta Connection

In a delta connection, the phase voltage is equal to the line voltage. The phase current is equal to the line current divided by the square root of 3.

These relations can be derived using Kirchhoff's Voltage Law and Kirchhoff's Current Law. It is important to understand these relations when analyzing single phase AC circuits.
