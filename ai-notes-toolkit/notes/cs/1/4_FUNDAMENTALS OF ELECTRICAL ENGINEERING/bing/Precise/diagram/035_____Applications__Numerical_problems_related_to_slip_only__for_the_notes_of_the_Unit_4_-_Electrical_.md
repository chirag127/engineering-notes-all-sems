### Applications (Numerical problems related to slip only)

1. **Calculating slip:** Slip is the difference between the synchronous speed and the rotor speed of an induction motor, expressed as a percentage of the synchronous speed. It can be calculated using the formula: `Slip(%) = (Ns - Nr) / Ns * 100`, where Ns is the synchronous speed and Nr is the rotor speed.

2. **Determining rotor speed:** Given the synchronous speed and slip of an induction motor, the rotor speed can be determined using the formula: `Nr = Ns * (1 - Slip(%)/100)`.

3. **Determining synchronous speed:** Given the rotor speed and slip of an induction motor, the synchronous speed can be determined using the formula: `Ns = Nr / (1 - Slip(%)/100)`.

4. **Example problem:** An induction motor has a synchronous speed of 1800 RPM and a slip of 4%. What is the rotor speed of the motor?
    - Solution: Using the formula `Nr = Ns * (1 - Slip(%)/100)`, we can calculate the rotor speed as `Nr = 1800 * (1 - 4/100) = 1728 RPM`.
