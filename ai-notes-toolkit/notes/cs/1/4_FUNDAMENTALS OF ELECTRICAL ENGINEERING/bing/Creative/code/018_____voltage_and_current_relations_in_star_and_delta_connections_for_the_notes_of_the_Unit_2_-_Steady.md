### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

- A three-phase system can be connected in two different styles: star or delta.
- In a star connection, the three phase windings are connected in such a way that one end of each winding is joined together to form a common neutral point, and the other end of each winding is connected to the line conductor. The line conductors are also called the line voltages or the line currents.
- In a delta connection, the three phase windings are connected in such a way that the end of one winding is connected to the start of another winding, forming a closed loop. The line conductors are connected to the junctions of the windings. The line voltages or the line currents are the same as the phase voltages or the phase currents across each winding.
- The voltage and current relations in star and delta connections are as follows:

#### Star Connection

- The line voltage is equal to the root three times of the phase voltage, i.e., V_L = √3 V_P
- The line current is equal to the phase current, i.e., I_L = I_P
- The power factor is the same for the line and the phase, i.e., cosφ_L = cosφ_P
- The total power delivered by the three-phase system is equal to the sum of the powers delivered by each phase, i.e., P_T = 3 V_P I_P cosφ_P = √3 V_L I_L cosφ_L

#### Delta Connection

- The line voltage is equal to the phase voltage, i.e., V_L = V_P
- The line current is equal to the root three times of the phase current, i.e., I_L = √3 I_P
- The power factor is the same for the line and the phase, i.e., cosφ_L = cosφ_P
- The total power delivered by the three-phase system is equal to the sum of the powers delivered by each phase, i.e., P_T = 3 V_P I_P cosφ_P = √3 V_L I_L cosφ_L

- Both star and delta connections can deliver the same amount of power to the load, but they have different advantages and disadvantages depending on the application. For example, star connection is preferred for long-distance transmission as it reduces the line voltage and the line losses, while delta connection is preferred for high-power applications as it allows higher current and lower impedance.
