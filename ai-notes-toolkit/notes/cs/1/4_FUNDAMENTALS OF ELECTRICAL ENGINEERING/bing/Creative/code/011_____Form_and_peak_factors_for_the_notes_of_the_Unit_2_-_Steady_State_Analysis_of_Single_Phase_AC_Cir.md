### Form and peak factors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- Form factor is a parameter used in describing AC waveforms and is given by the ratio between the RMS value of the alternating quantity and the average value.
- RMS value is the effective value of an AC waveform that produces the same heating effect as a DC voltage of the same magnitude.
- Average value is the arithmetic mean of the positive half cycle of an AC waveform.
- Peak factor is also called as crest factor and is defined as the ratio of the maximum value to the RMS value of an AC waveform.
- Maximum value is the highest attained value by an AC waveform during a half cycle measured from the zero line.
- The form factor and peak factor depend on the shape of the AC waveform and are different for different types of waveforms.
- For a sinusoidal waveform, the form factor is 1.11 and the peak factor is 1.414.
- For a square waveform, the form factor and peak factor are both 1.
- For a triangular waveform, the form factor is 1.15 and the peak factor is 1.732.
- The form factor and peak factor are useful in determining the quality and efficiency of AC power transmission and distribution.
