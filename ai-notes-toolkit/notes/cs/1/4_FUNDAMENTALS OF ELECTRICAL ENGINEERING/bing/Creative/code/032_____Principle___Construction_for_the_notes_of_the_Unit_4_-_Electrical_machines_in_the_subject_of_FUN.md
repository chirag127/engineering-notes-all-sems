### Principle and Construction of Electrical Machines

- Electrical machines are devices that convert mechanical energy to electrical energy and vice versa.
- The principle of electromagnetic induction, discovered by Michael Faraday in 1831-1832, is the basis of most electrical machines.
- According to Faraday's law, an electromotive force (EMF) is generated in an electrical conductor that encircles a varying magnetic flux.
- The magnetic flux can be varied by changing the current in the coil, the position of the coil, or the strength of the magnetic field.
- Electrical machines can be classified into two main types: generators and motors.
- Generators convert mechanical power to electrical power by rotating a coil in a magnetic field, which induces an EMF in the coil.
- Motors convert electrical power to mechanical power by applying a current to a coil in a magnetic field, which creates a torque that rotates the coil.
- Electrical machines can also be classified based on the type of current they use: direct current (DC) or alternating current (AC).
- DC machines use a commutator and brushes to switch the direction of the current in the coil, so that the torque is always in the same direction.
- AC machines use a rotating magnetic field that changes the direction of the current in the coil, so that the torque is synchronized with the frequency of the AC supply.
- The construction of electrical machines consists of some common components, such as yoke, pole, field coil, armature, commutator, and brushes.
- The yoke is the outer frame that supports and protects the machine.
- The pole is the part that produces the magnetic field, either by permanent magnets or by electromagnets.
- The field coil is the winding that carries the current that creates the magnetic field.
- The armature is the part that carries the induced EMF or the applied current, depending on whether the machine is a generator or a motor.
- The commutator is the device that reverses the direction of the current in the armature, in case of DC machines.
- The brushes are the contacts that connect the armature to the external circuit.
- The construction of electrical machines also depends on the specific design and application of the machine, such as the number of poles, the shape of the armature, the type of winding, the cooling system, etc .
