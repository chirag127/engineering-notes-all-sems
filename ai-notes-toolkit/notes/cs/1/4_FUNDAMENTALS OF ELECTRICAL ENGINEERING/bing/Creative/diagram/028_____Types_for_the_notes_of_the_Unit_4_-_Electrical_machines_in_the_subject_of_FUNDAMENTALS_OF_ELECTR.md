### Types of Electrical Machines

Electrical machines are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa. They can be classified into two main categories: static and dynamic.

- Static electrical machines are devices that do not have any moving parts and operate on the principle of electromagnetic induction. They are used to transfer electrical power from one circuit to another with a change in voltage, current, or frequency. The most common example of a static electrical machine is a transformer .
- Dynamic electrical machines are devices that have rotating parts and operate on the principle of electromechanical energy conversion. They are used to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy or vice versa. The most common examples of dynamic electrical machines are motors and generators .

Dynamic electrical machines can be further classified into three types based on the type of current they use or produce: direct current (DC), alternating current (AC), or both.

- DC machines are devices that use or produce direct current, which is a unidirectional and constant flow of electric charge. They have a commutator and brushes to switch the direction of current in the rotor coil. They are used for applications that require constant speed, torque, or power. The most common example of a DC machine is a permanent magnet DC motor .
- AC machines are devices that use or produce alternating current, which is a bidirectional and periodic flow of electric charge. They have a stator and a rotor that create a rotating magnetic field. They are used for applications that require variable speed, torque, or power. The most common examples of AC machines are synchronous machines and induction machines .
- Synchronous machines are devices that have a constant speed of rotation that is proportional to the frequency of the supply voltage. They can operate as motors or generators. They have a rotor that is either excited by a DC source or has permanent magnets. They are used for applications that require high efficiency, power factor correction, or constant frequency. The most common example of a synchronous machine is an alternator .
- Induction machines are devices that have a variable speed of rotation that depends on the load and the slip. They can operate as motors or generators. They have a rotor that is either wound or has a cage of conductors. They are used for applications that require low cost, ruggedness, or easy maintenance. The most common example of an induction machine is a squirrel cage induction motor .
