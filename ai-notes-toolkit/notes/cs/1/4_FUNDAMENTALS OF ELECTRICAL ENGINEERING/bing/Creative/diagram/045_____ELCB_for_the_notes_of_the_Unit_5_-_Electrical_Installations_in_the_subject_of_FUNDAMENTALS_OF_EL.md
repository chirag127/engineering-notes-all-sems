### ELCB

- ELCB stands for Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker   .
- It is a safety device used in electrical installations with high Earth impedance to prevent shock  .
- It detects small stray voltages on the metal enclosures of electrical equipment, and interrupts the circuit if a dangerous voltage is detected  .
- It is a specialised type of latching relay that has a building's incoming mains power connected through its switching contacts so that the ELCB disconnects the power when earth leakage is detected.
- It detects fault currents from live to the Earth (ground) wire within the installation it protects.

#### Types of ELCB

- There are two types of ELCB: voltage-operated and current-operated  .
- Voltage-operated ELCB: It measures the voltage difference between the Earth and the metal body of the equipment. It operates when this voltage exceeds a preset value  .
- Current-operated ELCB: It measures the current difference between the live and neutral wires. It operates when this difference exceeds a preset value, indicating a leakage to the Earth  .
- Current-operated ELCB is more sensitive and reliable than voltage-operated ELCB  .

#### Advantages of ELCB

- ELCB provides protection against electrical shock and fire hazards caused by earth leakage  .
- ELCB can detect very low levels of leakage current that may not trip a conventional circuit breaker  .
- ELCB can isolate the faulty circuit quickly and prevent further damage to the equipment and the installation  .
- ELCB can be manually or automatically reset after clearing the fault  .

#### Disadvantages of ELCB

- ELCB may not work properly if the Earth wire is broken or disconnected  .
- ELCB may cause nuisance tripping due to external factors such as lightning, static electricity, or capacitive coupling  .
- ELCB may not detect leakage currents that do not flow to the Earth, such as between live and neutral wires or between two live wires  .
- ELCB may be affected by the quality and condition of the Earth connection  .

#### Applications of ELCB

- ELCB is widely used in domestic, commercial, and industrial electrical installations  .
- ELCB is especially suitable for applications where the equipment or the users may come in contact with the Earth, such as wet areas, outdoor sockets, metal appliances, etc  .
- ELCB is also used for protection of sensitive electronic devices that may be damaged by leakage currents  .
