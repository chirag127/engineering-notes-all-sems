# Form and peak factors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- Form factor is a parameter used in describing AC waveforms and is given by the ratio of the RMS value to the average value.
- RMS value is the effective value of an AC waveform that produces the same heating effect as a DC voltage of the same magnitude.
- Average value is the arithmetic mean of the positive half cycle of an AC waveform.
- Peak factor is also called as crest factor and is defined as the ratio of the peak value to the RMS value of an AC waveform.
- Peak value is the maximum value attained by an AC waveform in either positive or negative direction.
- Peak-to-peak value is the difference between the positive and negative peak values of an AC waveform.
- Form factor and peak factor are dimensionless quantities and depend on the shape of the AC waveform .
- For a sinusoidal waveform, the form factor is 1.11 and the peak factor is 1.414.
- For a square waveform, the form factor and the peak factor are both 1.
- For a triangular waveform, the form factor is 1.15 and the peak factor is 1.732.
