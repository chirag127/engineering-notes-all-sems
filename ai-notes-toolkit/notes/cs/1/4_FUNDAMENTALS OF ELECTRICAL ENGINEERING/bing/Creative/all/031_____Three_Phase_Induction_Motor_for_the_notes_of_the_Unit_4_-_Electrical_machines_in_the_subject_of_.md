# Three Phase Induction Motor

A three phase induction motor is a type of AC motor that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy by using the principle of electromagnetic induction. It is one of the most commonly used motors in various industrial applications.

## Working Principle

A three phase induction motor works on the following principle:

- A three phase AC supply is given to the stator windings, which creates a rotating magnetic field (RMF) in the air gap. The RMF has a constant magnitude and rotates at a synchronous speed (Ns), which depends on the supply frequency (f) and the number of poles (p) of the stator.

- The RMF induces an electromotive force (EMF) in the rotor conductors, which are either short-circuited or connected to an external resistance. The induced EMF causes a current to flow in the rotor conductors, which in turn produces a magnetic field in the rotor.

- The interaction between the stator RMF and the rotor magnetic field creates a torque on the rotor, which makes it rotate. The rotor speed (Nr) is always less than the synchronous speed (Ns), and the difference is called the slip (s).

- The slip is a measure of how much the rotor lags behind the stator RMF. The slip can be expressed as:

  s = (Ns - Nr) / Ns

- The slip varies from 0 to 1, depending on the load and the rotor resistance. A higher slip means a higher torque and a lower speed, and vice versa.

## Types of Three Phase Induction Motors

There are two main types of three phase induction motors, based on the construction and connection of the rotor:

- Squirrel cage induction motor: This type of motor has a rotor that consists of a cylindrical core with bars of copper or aluminum embedded in slots. The bars are short-circuited at both ends by end rings, forming a cage-like structure. The rotor has no external connection, and the induced current flows through the bars and the end rings. This type of motor is simple, rugged, and cheap, but has a low starting torque and a poor speed control.

- Slip ring or wound rotor induction motor: This type of motor has a rotor that consists of a laminated core with coils of insulated wire wound on it. The coils are connected to slip rings, which are mounted on the rotor shaft. The slip rings are connected to an external resistance or a rheostat, which can be varied to control the rotor resistance. This type of motor has a high starting torque and a better speed control, but is more complex, expensive, and requires more maintenance.

## Applications of Three Phase Induction Motors

Three phase induction motors are widely used in various industrial applications, such as:

- Fans, blowers, pumps, compressors, conveyors, elevators, cranes, hoists, etc.
- Machine tools, lathes, drills, mills, etc.
- Textile, paper, cement, steel, chemical, etc. industries.
- Electric traction, electric vehicles, etc.
