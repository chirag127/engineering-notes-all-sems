- The height of a pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the apex to the base plane.
- The slant height of a pyramid is the distance from the apex to the midpoint of any edge of the base.
- The lateral edge of a pyramid is the edge that connects the apex to a vertex of the base.
- The lateral surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the areas of the lateral faces.
- The total surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the lateral surface area and the base area.
