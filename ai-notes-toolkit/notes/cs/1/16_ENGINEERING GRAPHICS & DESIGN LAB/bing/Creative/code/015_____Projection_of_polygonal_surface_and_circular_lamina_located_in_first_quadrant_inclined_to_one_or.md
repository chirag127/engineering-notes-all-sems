### Projection of polygonal surface and circular lamina located in first quadrant inclined to one or both reference planes

- A polygonal surface is a plane surface bounded by straight lines. A circular lamina is a plane surface bounded by a circle.
- A plane surface can be located in any of the four quadrants of the reference planes. The first quadrant is the space above the horizontal plane (HP) and in front of the vertical plane (VP).
- A plane surface can be inclined to one or both of the reference planes. The inclination of a plane surface is the angle between the plane surface and the reference plane.
- To draw the projection of a plane surface, the following steps are followed:
  - Assume the initial position of the plane surface such that it is parallel to one of the reference planes and perpendicular to the other. Draw the true shape and size of the plane surface on the reference plane to which it is parallel. This is called the plan or the elevation of the plane surface, depending on whether it is parallel to the HP or the VP.
  - Rotate the plane surface about one of its edges or corners until it attains the given inclination to the reference plane. The edge or corner about which the rotation takes place is called the axis of rotation. The angle of rotation is equal to the angle of inclination. Draw the new position of the plane surface and its projection on the reference plane. This is called the auxiliary plan or the auxiliary elevation of the plane surface, depending on whether it is parallel to the HP or the VP.
  - Project the auxiliary plan or the auxiliary elevation on the other reference plane. This is called the final plan or the final elevation of the plane surface, depending on whether it is projected on the HP or the VP.
  - Show the axis of rotation, the angle of inclination, and the dimensions of the plane surface in the final plan and the final elevation.
- To draw the projection of a polygonal surface, the same steps are followed, except that the true shape and size of the polygonal surface is drawn by joining the vertices of the polygon with straight lines.
- To draw the projection of a circular lamina, the same steps are followed, except that the true shape and size of the circular lamina is drawn by drawing a circle with the given radius. The axis of rotation can be the center or any point on the circumference of the circle. The projection of the circular lamina on the reference plane will be an ellipse, unless the circular lamina is parallel or perpendicular to the reference plane. The major and minor axes of the ellipse can be determined by using the method of change of position or the method of auxiliary circle.
