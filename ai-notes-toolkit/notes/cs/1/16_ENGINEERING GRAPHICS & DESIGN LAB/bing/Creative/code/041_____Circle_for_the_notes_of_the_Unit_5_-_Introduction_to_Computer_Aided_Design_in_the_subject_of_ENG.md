### Circle

A circle is a two-dimensional shape that consists of all the points that are equidistant from a fixed point called the center. The distance from the center to any point on the circle is called the radius. The circle has no corners or edges and is a closed and curved shape.

Some of the properties of a circle are:

- The diameter of a circle is the longest chord of a circle and is equal to twice the radius .
- A chord of a circle is a line segment that joins any two points on the circle .
- A secant of a circle is a line that intersects the circle at two points .
- A tangent of a circle is a line that touches the circle at one point and is perpendicular to the radius at that point .
- An arc of a circle is a part of the circle between two points on the circle .
- A sector of a circle is a region bounded by two radii and an arc .
- A segment of a circle is a region bounded by a chord and an arc .
- The circumference of a circle is the length of the boundary of the circle and is equal to 2πr, where r is the radius .
- The area of a circle is the measure of the region enclosed by the circle and is equal to πr^2^, where r is the radius .
- Equal chords of a circle subtend equal angles at the center  .
- The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by the same arc at any point on the circle .
- The angle subtended by a semicircle at any point on the circle is a right angle .
- The perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects the chord  .
- The angle in a semicircle is a right angle .
- The opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary .
- The sum of the angles in a cyclic quadrilateral is 360 degrees .
- The angle between a tangent and a chord is equal to the angle subtended by the chord in the alternate segment .
- Two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal in length and subtend equal angles at the center .

Some examples of circles in real life are:

- A lollipop
- A coin
- A pizza
- A CD
