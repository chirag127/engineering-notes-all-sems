### Chamfer

- A chamfer is an angle on the edge of a workpiece.
- It is often created at a 45° angle between two adjoining right-angled faces.
- It is used for protecting the chamfered object as well as anyone who might come in contact with the object.
- It can also improve the appearance, function, and assembly of the object.
- Chamfers can be applied to the outside of the part, where a hole breaks through a surface, or where any two surfaces meet.
- Chamfers can be classified into different types, such as symmetrical, asymmetrical, circular, or linear.
- Chamfers can be created by various methods, such as machining, grinding, filing, or molding.
- Chamfers can be measured by using a chamfer gage, a protractor, or a coordinate measuring machine.
- Chamfers can be represented by using a symbol or a notation on a technical drawing.
