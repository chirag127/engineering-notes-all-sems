### Projection of solids like prisms

- A solid is a three-dimensional object that has length, breadth and height. A prism is a solid that has two congruent and parallel faces called bases and the other faces are parallelograms called lateral faces.
- A projection of a solid is a representation of the solid on a plane, such as a sheet of paper. There are different types of projections, such as orthographic, isometric, oblique, perspective, etc.
- Orthographic projection is a method of showing the true shape and size of a solid on two or more planes that are perpendicular to each other. The most common planes are the horizontal plane (HP) and the vertical plane (VP).
- Isometric projection is a method of showing the three-dimensional shape of a solid on a plane, such as a sheet of paper. It is obtained by tilting the solid 45 degrees to the HP and 35.26 degrees to the VP, and then projecting the solid parallel to the plane.
- To draw the orthographic projection of a prism, we need to know the following information:
  - The type and size of the base of the prism.
  - The height of the prism.
  - The position and inclination of the axis of the prism with respect to the HP and the VP.
- To draw the isometric projection of a prism, we need to know the following information:
  - The type and size of the base of the prism.
  - The height of the prism.
  - The orientation of the prism with respect to the isometric axes.
- The steps to draw the orthographic projection of a prism are as follows :
  - Draw the front view of the prism on the VP, showing the true shape and size of the base and the height of the prism.
  - Draw the top view of the prism on the HP, showing the true shape and size of the base and the projection of the height of the prism.
  - Draw the side view of the prism on a plane perpendicular to both the HP and the VP, showing the true height of the prism and the projection of the base of the prism.
  - Label the views and the dimensions as required.
- The steps to draw the isometric projection of a prism are as follows :
  - Draw the isometric axes on the plane, such that the x-axis is at 30 degrees to the horizontal, the y-axis is at -30 degrees to the horizontal, and the z-axis is vertical.
  - Draw the isometric outline of the base of the prism on the plane, using the isometric scale and the isometric angles.
  - Draw the isometric outline of the top of the prism on the plane, parallel to the base and at a distance equal to the height of the prism.
  - Join the corresponding vertices of the base and the top of the prism to form the lateral faces of the prism.
  - Label the projection and the dimensions as required.
