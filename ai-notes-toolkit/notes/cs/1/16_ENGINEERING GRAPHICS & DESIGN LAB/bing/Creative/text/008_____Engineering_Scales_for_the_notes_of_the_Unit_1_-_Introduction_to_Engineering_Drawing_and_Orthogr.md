### Engineering Scales

- Engineering scales are rulers that produce ratio drawings of objects or plans.
- Engineers use engineering scales for drafting and blueprint creation  .
- Engineering scales are commonly 300 mm long, which is a little more than 12 inches.
- Engineering scales have extra space on the ends to provide a safety margin in case of wear and tear.
- Engineering scales are shaped like long triangular prisms, with each side of each point marked with a different scale for measuring.
- Engineering scales have six scales on each prism, divided into decimalized fractions of an inch.
- The six scales on an engineering scale are 1:10, 1:20, 1:30, 1:40, 1:50 and 1:60, unless otherwise stated.
- The first graduation on an engineering scale is the zero mark.
- To use an engineering scale, one has to align the zero mark with the starting point of the measurement, and read the corresponding value on the desired scale.
- Engineering scales are useful for measuring things in land development and site construction plans.
