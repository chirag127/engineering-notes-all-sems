### Cone

- A cone is a three-dimensional solid that has a circular base and a single vertex, called the apex.
- The lateral surface of a cone is the curved surface that connects the base and the apex.
- The height of a cone is the perpendicular distance from the base to the apex.
- The slant height of a cone is the length of the segment from the apex to any point on the base circle.
- The base angle of a cone is the angle between the slant height and the height.

![Cone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6e/Cone_%28geometry%29.svg/1200px-Cone_%28geometry%29.svg.png)

- A cone can be classified into two types: right cone and oblique cone.
- A right cone is a cone whose apex is aligned with the center of the base circle.
- An oblique cone is a cone whose apex is not aligned with the center of the base circle.

![Right cone and oblique cone](https://www.mathplanet.com/Oldsite/media/45483/cone1.png)

- A cone can also be classified into two types based on the size of the base angle: acute cone and obtuse cone.
- An acute cone is a cone whose base angle is less than 90 degrees.
- An obtuse cone is a cone whose base angle is greater than 90 degrees.

![Acute cone and obtuse cone](https://www.mathplanet.com/Oldsite/media/45483/cone2.png)

- A section of a cone is the intersection of a plane and the cone.
- A section of a cone can have different shapes depending on the position and orientation of the cutting plane.
- Some common sections of a cone are:

  - Circle: when the cutting plane is parallel to the base and passes through the cone.
  - Ellipse: when the cutting plane is oblique to the base and the axis and passes through the cone.
  - Parabola: when the cutting plane is parallel to the axis and passes through the cone.
  - Hyperbola: when the cutting plane is oblique to the base and the axis and does not pass through the cone.

![Sections of a cone](https://www.mathplanet.com/Oldsite/media/45483/cone3.png)

- The development of a surface of a solid is the unfolding of the surface into a plane figure.
- The development of a surface of a cone is a sector of a circle whose radius is the slant height and whose angle is equal to the circumference of the base divided by the slant height.
- The development of a surface of a cone can be obtained by cutting along a slant height and unfolding the lateral surface.

![Development of a surface of a cone](https://www.mathplanet.com/Oldsite/media/45483/cone4.png)
