## Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

- Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel to communicate technical information, such as design specifications, dimensions, tolerances, materials, and manufacturing processes.
- Engineering drawing follows a set of standards and conventions to ensure clarity, accuracy, and uniformity of the drawings.
- Engineering drawing can be classified into two types: pictorial and orthographic.
- Pictorial drawing is a type of drawing that shows the object as it appears to the eye, with three-dimensional perspective and foreshortening. Pictorial drawing is useful for visualization, presentation, and illustration purposes, but it may not convey the exact shape, size, and details of the object.
- Orthographic drawing is a type of drawing that shows the object as it is projected on two or more planes perpendicular to each other, without perspective or foreshortening. Orthographic drawing is used for precise and detailed representation of the object, and it consists of two or more views of the object, such as front view, top view, and side view.
- Orthographic projection is a method of projecting the features of a three-dimensional object onto two-dimensional planes, such as horizontal plane, vertical plane, and profile plane. Orthographic projection follows a set of rules and principles, such as projection lines, projection symbols, first-angle projection, and third-angle projection.
- Orthographic projection is also known as multiview projection, because it shows the object from different directions and angles. Each view of the object shows only two dimensions, such as length and width, or height and width, or length and height. The third dimension is obtained by combining the information from two or more views.
- Orthographic projection is the basis of engineering drawing, and it is essential for engineers and technical personnel to understand and interpret orthographic drawings correctly. Orthographic projection helps to analyze the shape, size, and features of the object, and to communicate the design specifications and requirements to others.
