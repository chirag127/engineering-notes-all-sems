### Cylinder

A cylinder is a solid that has two circular bases of equal size and shape, and a curved surface that connects them. The axis of the cylinder is the line segment that joins the centers of the bases. The height of the cylinder is the distance between the bases. A cylinder is a type of prism, since it has two parallel and congruent bases.

Some of the topics related to cylinders in engineering graphics are:

- Projection of cylinders: This is the process of drawing the views of a cylinder on a plane, such as the horizontal plane (HP) or the vertical plane (VP). Depending on the orientation of the axis of the cylinder, the projection may show an ellipse, a circle, or a rectangle. The projection of a cylinder can be done by using the change of position method, which involves rotating the cylinder about its axis or a perpendicular line until the axis is parallel or perpendicular to the plane of projection.
- Section of cylinders: This is the process of cutting a cylinder by a plane and drawing the shape of the cross-section. The section plane can be parallel, perpendicular, or inclined to the axis of the cylinder. The shape of the section can be a circle, an ellipse, a rectangle, or a parabola. The true shape of the section can be found by projecting the section on a plane parallel to the section plane.
- Interpenetration of cylinders: This is the process of finding the curve of intersection between two cylinders that cross each other. The curve of intersection can be a straight line, an ellipse, a hyperbola, or a pair of straight lines. The interpenetration of cylinders can be done by using the method of auxiliary planes, which involves drawing the sections of the cylinders by planes perpendicular to the axes of the cylinders and finding the points of intersection.
- Isometric view of cylinders: This is the process of drawing a 3D representation of a cylinder on a 2D plane, such as a paper. The isometric view of a cylinder shows the true shape and size of the base, but not the height. The isometric view of a cylinder can be done by using the isometric scale, which is a ratio that converts the true dimensions of the cylinder to the isometric dimensions. The isometric scale for a cylinder is 0.816.
