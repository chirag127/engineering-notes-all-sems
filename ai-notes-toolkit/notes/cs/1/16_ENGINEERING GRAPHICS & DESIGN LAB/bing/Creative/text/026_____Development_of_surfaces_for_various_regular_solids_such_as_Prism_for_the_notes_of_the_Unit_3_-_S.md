### Development of surfaces for various regular solids such as Prism

- A regular solid is a solid whose faces are congruent regular polygons and whose vertices have the same degree (number of faces meeting at a vertex).
- A prism is a regular solid whose bases are congruent polygons and whose lateral faces are parallelograms.
- The development of a surface of a solid is the shape of a plain sheet that by proper folding could be converted into the shape of the concerned solid .
- To develop the surface of a prism, we need to know the shape and dimensions of the base and the height of the prism.
- The steps to develop the surface of a prism are as follows:
  - Draw the base of the prism as a regular polygon on a plain sheet.
  - Draw a line perpendicular to one of the sides of the base and mark the height of the prism on it.
  - Draw a line parallel to the base through the height mark and extend it on both sides.
  - Draw lines perpendicular to the parallel line from the vertices of the base and extend them until they meet the parallel line. These lines are the lateral edges of the prism.
  - Draw the other base of the prism by joining the points where the lateral edges meet the parallel line. This base is congruent to the first base.
  - Cut out the shape along the outer edges of the base and the lateral edges.
  - Fold the shape along the base and the lateral edges to form the prism.

- The development of the surface of a prism can be used to find the surface area of the prism by measuring the length and width of the plain sheet.
- The development of the surface of a prism can also be used to make models of the prism by using cardboard, paper or other materials.
