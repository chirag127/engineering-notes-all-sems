### Trim

- Trim is a command in computer aided design (CAD) software that allows the user to cut or remove unwanted parts of an object or a drawing.
- Trim can be used to create new shapes or modify existing ones by trimming the intersecting or overlapping segments of different objects.
- Trim can also be used to extend or shorten an object to meet the edge of another object.

#### How to use the Trim command in AutoCAD   

- Click on the Trim icon or type TRIM and press Enter on the keyboard.
- Select the objects that will act as the boundaries or cutting edges for the trim operation. These can be lines, circles, arcs, polylines, etc. Press Enter to confirm the selection.
- Select the objects or segments that you want to trim. You can select them individually, drag a freehand selection path, or pick two points to specify a crossing fence. Press Enter to finish the command.
- Alternatively, you can use the Quick mode to trim objects by selecting all objects as boundaries and then selecting the objects to be trimmed.
- You can also use the options in the command line to change the trim mode, select a different boundary, or undo the last trim.

#### Advantages of using the Trim command in CAD

- The Trim command can help you create complex shapes or modify existing ones by removing unwanted parts or segments.
- The Trim command can save you time and effort by simplifying the drawing process and reducing the number of steps required to achieve the desired result.
- The Trim command can improve the accuracy and quality of your drawings by ensuring that the objects are trimmed or extended to the exact edge of another object.
