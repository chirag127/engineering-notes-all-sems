### Orthographic Projection

Orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is commonly used in engineering drawing to show the shape and size of an object from different views. Orthographic projection is also known as orthogonal projection .

Some features of orthographic projection are:

- It uses parallel lines that are perpendicular to the plane of the drawing .
- It shows the true shape and size of the object in each view .
- It requires at least two views to represent an object, usually a front view and a top view .
- It can also include a side view, a sectional view, or an auxiliary view to show more details of the object .
- It follows a set of conventions and standards to ensure clarity and consistency of the drawings .
