### Sections of Solids

- A section of a solid is the shape obtained by cutting the solid by a plane, called the section plane or cutting plane, and removing the portion between the plane and the observer.
- Sections of solids are used to show the hidden or internal features of an object in technical drawings.
- To draw the sectional views of solids, the following steps are followed:
  - Draw the projections of the solid in the given position and orientation.
  - Draw the section plane in the front view or the top view as per the given conditions.
  - Mark the points where the section plane intersects the edges or faces of the solid and name them with letters.
  - Draw the sectional view by projecting the points from the view where the section plane is seen as a line to the view where the section plane is seen as a plane.
  - Shade the cut surfaces of the solid in the sectional view to indicate the material removed.
  - Draw the true shape of the section by projecting the points from the sectional view to a plane perpendicular to the section plane.
- The types of solids that are commonly encountered in engineering graphics are prisms, pyramids, cylinders and cones.
- The types of section planes that are commonly used are parallel, perpendicular and inclined to the principal planes.
- The types of sections that are commonly drawn are full section, half section, offset section and broken-out section.
