### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A section of a solid is the shape obtained by cutting the solid with a plane, called the section plane or cutting plane.
- The true shape of the section is the shape of the section as seen perpendicular to the section plane.
- The development of a surface of a solid is the unfolding or unrolling of the surface on a plane.
- The development of a surface is useful for making patterns for sheet metal work, paper models, etc.
- The development of a surface can be obtained by drawing the edges of the solid on a plane and joining them appropriately.
- A pyramid is a solid whose base is a polygon and whose lateral faces are triangles with a common vertex, called the apex.
- The development of the lateral surface of a pyramid is a set of triangles joined along their bases.
- The development of the lateral surface of a pyramid can be obtained by drawing the slant edges of the pyramid on a plane and joining them to form triangles.
- The development of the base of a pyramid is the same as the shape of the base.
- The development of the whole surface of a pyramid is the combination of the development of the lateral surface and the base.
- A section of a pyramid is the shape obtained by cutting the pyramid with a plane, called the section plane or cutting plane.
- The true shape of the section of a pyramid depends on the position and orientation of the section plane.
- The true shape of the section of a pyramid can be obtained by drawing the intersection points of the section plane and the edges of the pyramid on a plane perpendicular to the section plane and joining them appropriately.
