### Chamfer

- A chamfer is an **angled cut** on the edge or corner of a part .
- Chamfers are used for various purposes, such as:
  - Improving part strength by reducing stress concentration .
  - Removing burrs and sharp edges that may cause injury or damage .
  - Easing assembly and fitting of parts by providing clearance and alignment .
  - Enhancing aesthetics and appearance of the part .
- Chamfers can be classified into two types: **corner chamfers** and **edge chamfers**.
  - Corner chamfers are applied to the intersection of two or more surfaces, such as the vertex of a cube.
  - Edge chamfers are applied to a single surface, such as the end of a cylinder.
- Chamfers can be dimensioned by specifying the **length** of one edge and the **angle** to it, or by specifying the **length** of both edges .
- Chamfers are often assumed to have a **45-degree angle**, unless otherwise stated.
- Chamfers can be measured by using a **triangle calculator**, a **protractor**, or a **chamfer gage**.
- Chamfers can be created by using various tools and methods, such as:
  - Milling cutters, drills, or reamers.
  - Hand files or deburring tools.
  - Computer-aided design (CAD) software, such as PTC Creo .
