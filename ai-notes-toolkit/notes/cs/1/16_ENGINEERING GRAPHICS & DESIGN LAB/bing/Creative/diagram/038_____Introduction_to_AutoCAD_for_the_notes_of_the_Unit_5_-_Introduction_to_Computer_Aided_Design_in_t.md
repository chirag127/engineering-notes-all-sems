### Introduction to AutoCAD

- AutoCAD is a commercial computer-aided design (CAD) and drafting software application developed and marketed by Autodesk .
- AutoCAD was first released in December 1982 as a desktop app running on microcomputers with internal graphics controllers .
- AutoCAD enables engineers, designers and architects to produce 2D and 3D models using computers.
- AutoCAD started as a design tool for engineers and architects, but is now used by other professionals as well, such as interior designers, landscape architects, industrial designers, etc.
- AutoCAD has various features and tools that help users to create, modify, annotate, dimension, and plot drawings.
- AutoCAD supports multiple file formats, such as DWG, DXF, DWF, PDF, etc.
- AutoCAD also has several extensions and add-ons that enhance its functionality and customization, such as AutoLISP, Visual Basic for Applications, ObjectARX, etc .
- AutoCAD is available for Windows, Mac OS, and mobile platforms .
