### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex.
- The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, and the number of faces is equal to the number of sides of the base.
- The axis of a pyramid is the perpendicular line segment joining the vertex and the center of the base.
- The height of a pyramid is the length of the axis, and the slant height is the distance from the vertex to the midpoint of any edge of the base.
- The lateral surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the areas of the triangular faces, and the total surface area is the sum of the lateral surface area and the area of the base.
- A section of a pyramid is the intersection of the pyramid with a plane that cuts through it.
- A section plane can be parallel, perpendicular, or inclined to the base or the axis of the pyramid.
- The true shape of the section is the shape of the section as seen from a direction perpendicular to the section plane.
- The development of a pyramid is the process of unfolding the lateral surface of the pyramid on a plane.
- The development of a pyramid consists of a series of triangles that are joined at their bases along the edges of the base of the pyramid.
- The development of a pyramid can be used to find the lateral surface area of the pyramid, or to make a paper model of the pyramid.
