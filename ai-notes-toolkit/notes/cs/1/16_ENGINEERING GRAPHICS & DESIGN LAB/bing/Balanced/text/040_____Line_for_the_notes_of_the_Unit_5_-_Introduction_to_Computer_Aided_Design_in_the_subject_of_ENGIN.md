### Line for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A line is a basic element of computer aided design (CAD) that represents a continuous sequence of points in a two-dimensional or three-dimensional space.
- A line can be defined by two endpoints, a direction vector, or a parametric equation.
- A line can have different properties, such as color, thickness, style, and layer.
- A line can be modified by using commands such as move, copy, rotate, scale, trim, extend, offset, and mirror.
- A line can be used to create other geometric entities, such as polygons, circles, arcs, splines, and curves.
- A line can also be used to define dimensions, annotations, constraints, and relationships in a CAD model.
