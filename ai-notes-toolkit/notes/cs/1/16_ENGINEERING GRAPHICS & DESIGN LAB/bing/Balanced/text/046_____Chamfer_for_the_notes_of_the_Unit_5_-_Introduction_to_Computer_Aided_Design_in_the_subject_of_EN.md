### Chamfer

- A chamfer is an angled cut on the edge or corner of a part .
- Chamfers are used for a variety of reasons, such as part strength, burr removal, ease of assembly, and aesthetics .
- Chamfers can be classified into two types: corner chamfers and edge chamfers.
- Corner chamfers are applied to the intersection of two or more surfaces, such as a cube or a cylinder.
- Edge chamfers are applied to a single surface, such as a plate or a sheet.
- Chamfers can be dimensioned by specifying the length of one edge and the corresponding angle to it, or by specifying the distance from the original edge to the chamfered edge and the angle or the length of the chamfered edge.
- Chamfers can be created using different tools, such as milling cutters, drills, files, or chamfering machines .
- Chamfers can be measured using different tools, such as protractors, calipers, gages, or chamfer angle calculators.
- Chamfers can be represented on technical drawings using a symbol that consists of two lines forming an angle, with the chamfer distance and angle written inside or outside the symbol.
