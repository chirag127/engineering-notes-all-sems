### Cone

- A cone is a three-dimensional solid that has a circular base and a single vertex, called the apex.
- The lateral surface of a cone is the curved surface that connects the base and the apex.
- The height of a cone is the perpendicular distance from the base to the apex.
- The slant height of a cone is the length of the segment from the apex to any point on the edge of the base.
- The radius of a cone is the radius of the circular base.
- The volume of a cone is given by the formula `V = (1/3)πr^2h`, where `r` is the radius and `h` is the height.
- The lateral surface area of a cone is given by the formula `L = πrs`, where `r` is the radius and `s` is the slant height.
- The total surface area of a cone is given by the formula `A = πr(r + s)`, where `r` is the radius and `s` is the slant height.

- A section of a cone is the intersection of a plane and the cone.
- The shape of the section depends on the position and orientation of the plane.
- Some possible sections of a cone are:

  - A circle, if the plane is parallel to the base and passes through it.
  - An ellipse, if the plane is parallel to the base but does not pass through it.
  - A parabola, if the plane is parallel to the axis of the cone and passes through the apex.
  - A hyperbola, if the plane is not parallel to the axis of the cone or the base and passes through both nappes of the cone.
  - A point, if the plane passes through the apex but not the base.
  - A line, if the plane is tangent to the cone at a point on the edge of the base.

- The development of a surface of a solid is the process of unfolding the surface into a plane figure.
- The development of a surface can be used to find the true shape and size of the surface, as well as to make patterns for cutting and folding the solid.
- The development of the lateral surface of a cone is a sector of a circle, with the central angle equal to the slant angle of the cone and the radius equal to the slant height of the cone.
- The development of the base of a cone is a circle with the same radius as the base of the cone.
- The development of the whole surface of a cone is the combination of the development of the lateral surface and the base, with the base attached to the curved edge of the sector.
