### Introduction to AutoCAD

- AutoCAD is a **commercial computer-aided design (CAD) and drafting software application**  that enables engineers, designers and architects to produce 2D and 3D models using computers.
- AutoCAD was developed and marketed by **Autodesk**, a leader in 3D design, engineering and entertainment software .
- AutoCAD was first released in **December 1982** as a desktop app running on microcomputers with internal graphics controllers .
- AutoCAD is the **starting of modeling and designing on a digital platform**. Prior to the intent of this application, people used to design and create sketches and models by hand.
- AutoCAD has various **tools and commands** that help users to create and modify 2D and 3D drawings, such as **Draw, Modify, Dimension, Annotate, Layout, Block, Hatch, etc**.
- AutoCAD also supports **customization and programming** using **AutoLISP, Visual Basic, .NET, etc** to extend its functionality and automate repetitive tasks.
- AutoCAD is widely used in various **industries and domains**, such as **architecture, engineering, construction, manufacturing, animation, etc** .
- AutoCAD is compatible with various **file formats**, such as **DWG, DXF, DWF, PDF, etc** to exchange data with other applications and platforms.
