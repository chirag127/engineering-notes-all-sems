### Offset for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Offset is a technique of creating a parallel or concentric copy of an object or a curve in computer aided design (CAD).
- Offset can be used to create outlines, borders, margins, gaps, clearances, or offsets for machining operations.
- Offset can be applied to various types of objects or curves, such as lines, circles, arcs, splines, polylines, or polygons.
- Offset can be specified by a distance, a factor, or a direction.
- Offset can be positive or negative, depending on the direction of the copy relative to the original object or curve.
- Offset can be performed by using commands, tools, or menus in different CAD software, such as AutoCAD, SolidWorks, or SketchUp.
- Offset can be modified, edited, or deleted after creation, depending on the CAD software and the type of object or curve.
- Offset can be combined with other CAD operations, such as trim, extend, fillet, chamfer, or mirror, to create complex shapes or designs.
