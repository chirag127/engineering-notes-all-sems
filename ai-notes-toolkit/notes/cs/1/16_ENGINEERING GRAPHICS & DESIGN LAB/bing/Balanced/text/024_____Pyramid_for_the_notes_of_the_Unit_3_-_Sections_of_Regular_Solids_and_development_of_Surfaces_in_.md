### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex.
- The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, and the number of faces is equal to the number of sides of the base.
- The axis of a pyramid is the perpendicular line joining the vertex and the center of the base.
- The slant height of a pyramid is the distance from the vertex to the midpoint of any edge of the base.
- The height of a pyramid is the distance from the vertex to the base plane.
- The lateral surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the areas of the triangular faces, and the total surface area is the sum of the lateral surface area and the base area.
- The volume of a pyramid is one-third of the product of the base area and the height.

- A section of a pyramid is the intersection of the pyramid with a cutting plane that passes through it.
- The cutting plane can be parallel, perpendicular, or inclined to the base or the axis of the pyramid.
- The true shape of the section is the shape of the section as seen from a direction perpendicular to the cutting plane.
- To draw the true shape of the section, the following steps are followed:
  - Draw the front and top views of the pyramid in the first angle projection, showing the cutting plane and the section line.
  - Project the section line to the auxiliary plane perpendicular to the cutting plane, and draw the auxiliary view of the pyramid.
  - In the auxiliary view, measure the distances of the vertices of the section from the reference line, and transfer them to the true shape plane parallel to the cutting plane.
  - Join the points in the true shape plane to get the true shape of the section.

- The development of a pyramid is the process of unfolding the lateral surface of the pyramid on a plane.
- The development of a pyramid can be obtained by cutting the pyramid along some of its edges and laying it flat on a plane.
- To draw the development of a pyramid, the following steps are followed:
  - Draw the front and top views of the pyramid in the first angle projection, showing the base and the slant edges.
  - Draw a horizontal line as the base line of the development, and mark the length of the base edge on it.
  - From the center of the base line, draw a perpendicular line as the axis line of the development, and mark the slant height of the pyramid on it.
  - Divide the base edge into equal parts, and draw radial lines from the center of the base line to the division points.
  - From the division points, draw perpendicular lines to the axis line, and mark the lengths of the slant edges on them.
  - Join the points on the perpendicular lines to get the development of the pyramid.
