### Development of surfaces for various regular solids such as Prism

- Development of surfaces is the process of finding the shape of a flat sheet that can be folded to form the surface of a given solid.
- Regular solids are solids whose faces are congruent regular polygons and whose vertices are all the same.
- Prism is a regular solid whose bases are congruent polygons and whose lateral faces are rectangles.
- To develop the surface of a prism, we need to know the shape and dimensions of the base and the height of the prism.
- The steps to develop the surface of a prism are:

  1. Draw the base of the prism on a flat sheet of paper.
  2. Draw a line perpendicular to one of the sides of the base and mark the height of the prism on it.
  3. Draw a rectangle with the same length as the side of the base and the same height as the prism, adjacent to the base.
  4. Repeat step 3 for all the sides of the base, making sure that the rectangles are connected at the corners.
  5. Cut out the shape formed by the base and the rectangles.
  6. Fold the rectangles along the sides of the base to form the lateral faces of the prism.
