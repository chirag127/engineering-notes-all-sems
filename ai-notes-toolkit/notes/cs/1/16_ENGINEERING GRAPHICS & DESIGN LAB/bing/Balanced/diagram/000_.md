# Header Diagram

- A header diagram is a graphical representation of the structure and layout of a web page or a document.
- A header diagram shows the hierarchy of the elements in the page or document, such as headings, subheadings, paragraphs, images, links, etc.
- A header diagram can help you to organize your content, improve readability, and enhance accessibility.
- A header diagram can also help you to identify the main topic, the subtopics, and the supporting details of your page or document.

## How to create a header diagram

- To create a header diagram, you need to use a tool that can generate a graphical outline of your page or document, such as Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or an online tool like [this one](https://www.writemaps.com/).
- You need to identify the main heading of your page or document, which is usually the title or the name of the topic. This is the highest level of your header diagram, and it should be at the top of the page.
- You need to identify the subheadings of your page or document, which are the main sections or categories of your topic. These are the second level of your header diagram, and they should be below the main heading, indented to the right.
- You need to identify the paragraphs, images, links, or other elements that belong to each subheading. These are the third level of your header diagram, and they should be below the subheadings, indented further to the right.
- You can repeat this process for any lower levels of your header diagram, such as sub-subheadings, sub-paragraphs, etc., as long as they are relevant and necessary for your page or document.

## Example of a header diagram

Here is an example of a header diagram for a page about the history of the internet.

```
History of the Internet
  Introduction
    - What is the internet and how does it work?
    - Why is the internet important and how has it changed the world?
  Early Development
    - The origins of the internet in the 1950s and 1960s
    - The ARPANET project and the first network of computers
    - The development of packet switching and TCP/IP protocols
  Expansion and Growth
    - The emergence of the World Wide Web in the 1980s and 1990s
    - The rise of browsers, search engines, and online services
    - The impact of the internet on communication, education, and commerce
  Challenges and Opportunities
    - The issues of security, privacy, and censorship on the internet
    - The role of the internet in social movements and political activism
    - The future of the internet and the potential of new technologies
  Conclusion
    - A summary of the main points and the significance of the topic
    - A call to action or a question for further research
```
