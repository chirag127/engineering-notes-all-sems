# Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design

## What is Computer Aided Design (CAD)?

- Computer Aided Design (CAD) is a manufacturing process that allows us to digitally create 2D drawings or 3D models of future products.
- CAD helps designers and engineers visualize a product’s construction, before fabricating it.
- CAD can also be used for analysis, simulation, optimization, and documentation of the product design.

## Why is CAD important for Engineering Graphics and Design?

- CAD is important for Engineering Graphics and Design because it enables the creation of accurate, realistic, and complex geometries that are difficult or impossible to achieve by hand.
- CAD also allows the manipulation, modification, and transformation of the geometries using various tools and commands.
- CAD can facilitate the communication and collaboration among different stakeholders involved in the product development process, such as designers, engineers, manufacturers, and customers.
- CAD can improve the quality, efficiency, and productivity of the product design and manufacturing process, by reducing errors, costs, and time.

## How to use CAD for Engineering Graphics and Design?

- To use CAD for Engineering Graphics and Design, one needs to learn the fundamentals of engineering graphics using solid modeling, as well as the specific software package that supports CAD.
- Solid modeling is a technique of representing a solid object using mathematical equations and geometric primitives, such as points, lines, curves, surfaces, and solids.
- Solid modeling can be used for conceptual design, detailed design, and engineering analysis of the product.
- Some examples of CAD software packages are Creo Parametric, SolidWorks, AutoCAD, CATIA, and Inventor .
- Each CAD software package has its own interface, features, functions, and commands that need to be learned and mastered.
- A typical CAD workflow involves the following steps:
  - Define the design problem and the requirements
  - Sketch the basic shape and dimensions of the product
  - Create the 3D model of the product using various tools and commands
  - Modify and refine the 3D model as needed
  - Apply materials, colors, textures, and lighting to the 3D model
  - Perform analysis, simulation, optimization, and verification of the 3D model
  - Generate 2D drawings, views, and annotations of the 3D model
  - Export and share the 3D model and the 2D drawings with others
