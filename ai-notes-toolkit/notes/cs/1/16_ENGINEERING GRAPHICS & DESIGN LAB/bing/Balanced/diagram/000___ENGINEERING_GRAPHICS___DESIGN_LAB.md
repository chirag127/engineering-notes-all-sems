# Engineering Graphics and Design Lab

Engineering graphics and design lab is a course that introduces the students to the principles and techniques of engineering drawing, computer-aided design (CAD), and engineering design. The course covers the following topics:

- Engineering design process and problem-solving methods
- Spatial visualization and orthographic projection
- Pictorial sketching and freehand drawing
- Dimensioning and tolerancing
- Geometric construction and descriptive geometry
- Sectional views and auxiliary views
- Multiview drawings and working drawings
- Introduction to CAD software and 3D solid modeling
- Assembly modeling and exploded views
- Engineering graphics standards and conventions

The course objectives are:

- To develop the graphic skills for communication of concepts, ideas, and design of engineering products
- To expose the students to the existing national and international standards related to technical drawings
- To enhance the students' ability to use CAD software for creating, editing, and analyzing 3D models
- To apply the engineering design process and methods to solve engineering problems

The course consists of lectures and laboratory sessions. The lectures provide the theoretical background and the laboratory sessions provide the hands-on experience and practice. The students are expected to complete assignments, quizzes, projects, and exams. The course grading is based on the following criteria:

- Assignments and quizzes: 30%
- Projects: 30%
- Midterm exam: 20%
- Final exam: 20%

The course prerequisites are:

- Basic knowledge of geometry, algebra, and trigonometry
- Basic computer skills and familiarity with Windows operating system
- Access to a computer with CAD software installed (preferably SolidWorks or AutoCAD)
