### Engineering Scales

- Engineering scales are used to measure and draw objects in engineering drawings with accurate dimensions and proportions.
- Engineering scales are based on ratios or fractions that relate the actual size of the object to the size of the drawing.
- Engineering scales can be classified into three types: plain scales, diagonal scales, and vernier scales.
- Plain scales are the simplest type of scales that show two units of measurement, such as meters and centimeters, on a single line. Plain scales can be used to measure and draw lengths, areas, and volumes of objects.
- Diagonal scales are a type of plain scales that have additional diagonal lines to show smaller subdivisions of the units, such as millimeters. Diagonal scales can be used to measure and draw more precise dimensions of objects.
- Vernier scales are a type of scales that have a movable scale that slides along a fixed scale. Vernier scales have two sets of graduations, one on the fixed scale and one on the movable scale, that are slightly different in length. Vernier scales can be used to measure and draw very accurate dimensions of objects by reading the coincidence of the graduations on both scales.
