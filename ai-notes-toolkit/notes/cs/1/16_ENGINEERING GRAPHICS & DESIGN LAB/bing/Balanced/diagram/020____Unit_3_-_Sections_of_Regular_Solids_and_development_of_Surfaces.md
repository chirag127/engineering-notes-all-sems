## Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces

- A section of a solid is the shape obtained by cutting the solid with a plane.
- A section plane is the plane that cuts the solid.
- A sectional view is the view of the solid as seen from the direction of the section plane.
- A true shape of a section is the shape of the section as seen from a direction perpendicular to the section plane.
- A development of a surface is the process of unfolding the surface of a solid and laying it flat on a plane.
- A development of a solid is the process of unfolding all the surfaces of a solid and laying them flat on a plane.

### Sections of Regular Solids

- Regular solids are solids that have regular shapes and sizes, such as cubes, prisms, pyramids, cylinders, cones, and spheres.
- To draw the sectional view and the true shape of a section of a regular solid, the following steps are followed:
  - Draw the front view and the top view of the solid in the first angle or third angle projection, as per the given dimensions and orientation.
  - Draw the section plane on the front view and the top view, as per the given inclination and position.
  - Mark the points where the section plane intersects the edges of the solid on the front view and the top view.
  - Join the points on the front view to get the sectional view of the solid.
  - Project the points from the front view to the side view, perpendicular to the reference line.
  - Join the points on the side view to get the true shape of the section of the solid.
  - Shade the sectional view and the true shape of the section with diagonal lines to indicate the cut surface.
