### Rectangle

- A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles.
- A rectangle can be defined by two opposite vertices or by its length and width.
- The length of a rectangle is the longer side and the width is the shorter side.
- The perimeter of a rectangle is the sum of the lengths of all four sides: P = 2(L + W).
- The area of a rectangle is the product of the length and the width: A = L * W.
- The diagonal of a rectangle is the line segment that connects two opposite vertices. The length of the diagonal can be found by using the Pythagorean theorem: D = sqrt(L^2 + W^2).
- A rectangle is a special case of a parallelogram, where all angles are 90 degrees.
- A rectangle is also a special case of a trapezoid, where both pairs of opposite sides are parallel and equal in length.
- A square is a special case of a rectangle, where all four sides are equal in length and all angles are 90 degrees.
