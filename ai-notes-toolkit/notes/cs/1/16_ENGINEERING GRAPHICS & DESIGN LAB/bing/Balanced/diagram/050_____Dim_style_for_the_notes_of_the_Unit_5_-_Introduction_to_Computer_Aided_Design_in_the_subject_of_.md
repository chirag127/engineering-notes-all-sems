### Dim style for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Computer-aided design (CAD) is the use of computers to aid in the creation, modification, analysis, or optimization of a design.
- CAD software is used to increase the productivity of the designer, improve the quality of design, improve communications through documentation, and to create a database for manufacturing .
- CAD can be used for a wide range of projects in various industries, such as metal fabrication, carpentry, and 3D printing.
- CAD can involve 2D drawings or 3D models of future products, which can help designers and engineers visualize a product's construction, before fabricating it .
- CAD can also provide computational foundations and methods for design, such as conceptualization, synthesis, realization, and evaluation.
- CAD can be integrated with other technologies, such as computer-aided engineering (CAE), computer-aided manufacturing (CAM), and computer-aided design and drafting (CADD) .
- CAD can have various advantages, such as reducing errors, saving time and cost, enhancing creativity, and facilitating collaboration .
- CAD can also have some challenges, such as requiring specialized skills, software, and hardware, maintaining compatibility and interoperability, and ensuring security and privacy .
