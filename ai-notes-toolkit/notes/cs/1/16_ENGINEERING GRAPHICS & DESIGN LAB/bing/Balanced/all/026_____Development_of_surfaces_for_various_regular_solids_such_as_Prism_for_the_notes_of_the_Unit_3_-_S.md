# Development of Surfaces for Various Regular Solids such as Prism

- Development of surfaces is a technique of representing the shape of a three-dimensional object on a plane surface by unfolding or unrolling its surfaces.
- Development of surfaces is useful for designing and making patterns for sheet metal work, paper craft, cardboard models, etc.
- Development of surfaces can be done by different methods, such as parallel-line method, radial-line method, triangulation method, and approximate method.
- Parallel-line method is used for developing prisms and cylinders, in which all the edges or generators of the lateral surfaces are parallel to each other.
- Radial-line method is used for developing pyramids and cones, in which the edges or generators of the lateral surfaces converge at a point (the vertex or the axis).
- Triangulation method is used for developing solids with curved surfaces, such as spheres, torus, etc., in which the surface is divided into a number of triangular facets and then developed as a series of triangles.
- Approximate method is used for developing solids with complex or irregular shapes, such as ellipsoids, paraboloids, etc., in which the surface is approximated by a number of polygons and then developed as a series of polygons.

## Development of Surfaces for Prism

- A prism is a polyhedron with two congruent and parallel polygonal bases and rectangular lateral faces.
- The development of a prism can be obtained by the parallel-line method, as follows:

1. Draw the front view and the top view of the prism, showing the true shape and size of the base and the height of the prism.
2. Draw a horizontal line AB equal to the perimeter of the base polygon. This line will be the base line of the development.
3. Divide the base polygon into a number of equal parts by drawing radial lines from the center to the vertices. Number the vertices in a clockwise or anticlockwise direction.
4. From the points of division on the base line AB, draw perpendiculars equal to the height of the prism. These lines will be the edges of the lateral faces in the development.
5. Join the ends of the perpendiculars by parallel lines to form the lateral faces in the development. These lines will be parallel to the base line AB.
6. Transfer the numbers of the vertices from the base polygon to the corresponding points on the base line AB and the parallel lines in the development.
7. Cut along the base line AB and the parallel lines, and fold along the perpendiculars to form the prism.
