# Engineering Graphics and Design Lab

Engineering graphics and design lab is a course that introduces the students to the principles and techniques of engineering drawing, computer-aided design (CAD), and engineering design. The course covers the following topics:

- Engineering design process and problem-solving methods
- Spatial visualization and orthographic projection
- Pictorial sketching and freehand drawing
- Dimensioning and tolerancing
- Geometric construction and descriptive geometry
- Sectional views and auxiliary views
- Multiview drawings and working drawings
- Solid modeling and parametric design
- Assembly modeling and exploded views
- Engineering graphics standards and conventions
- Engineering graphics communication and documentation

The course consists of lectures and laboratory sessions, where the students apply the concepts and skills learned in the lectures to solve engineering problems using CAD software and manual drawing tools. The course also involves design projects, where the students work in teams to design and model engineering products or systems.

The course objectives are:

- To develop the students' graphic skills for communication of concepts, ideas, and design of engineering products
- To expose the students to existing national and international standards related to technical drawings
- To enhance the students' spatial reasoning and visualization abilities
- To familiarize the students with the use of CAD software and its applications in engineering design
- To foster the students' creativity and innovation in engineering design
- To cultivate the students' teamwork and collaboration skills in engineering design

The course outcomes are:

- The students will be able to apply the engineering design process and problem-solving methods to engineering problems
- The students will be able to create and interpret engineering drawings using orthographic projection, pictorial sketching, dimensioning, tolerancing, sectional views, auxiliary views, and multiview drawings
- The students will be able to use geometric construction and descriptive geometry to solve spatial problems
- The students will be able to create and modify solid models and assembly models using parametric design and CAD software
- The students will be able to communicate and document their engineering design solutions using engineering graphics standards and conventions
- The students will be able to demonstrate their creativity and innovation in engineering design projects
- The students will be able to work effectively in teams and present their engineering design solutions to peers and instructors

The course assessment is based on:

- Quizzes and exams
- Laboratory assignments and reports
- Design projects and presentations
- Class participation and attendance

The course prerequisites are:

- Basic knowledge of mathematics and physics
- Basic computer skills
- Access to a computer with CAD software installed
- Drawing tools and materials

The course references are:

- Engineering Graphics Essentials with AutoCAD 2020 Instruction by Kirstie Plantenberg
- Engineering Design Graphics: Sketching, Modeling, and Visualization by James Leake and Jacob Borgerson
- Engineering Graphics with SolidWorks 2020 by David Planchard
- Engineering Graphics with AutoCAD 2020 by James Bethune
- Engineering Design: A Project-Based Introduction by Clive Dym and Patrick Little
- Engineering Design: An Introduction by John Karsnitz, Stephen O'Brien, and John Hutchinson
