# Projection of Point

- Projection in engineering drawing is a technique for representing a three-dimensional object on a two-dimensional drawing.
- A point is the simplest geometric element that has no dimensions, only a position in space.
- To project a point onto a plane, we need to draw a line of sight from the point to the plane and mark the point where the line intersects the plane. This point is called the projection of the point.
- The projection of a point depends on the position of the point relative to the plane and the orientation of the plane relative to the observer.
- There are two types of projections in engineering drawing: parallel projection and perspective projection.
- Parallel projection is a projection where the lines of sight are assumed to be parallel to each other and perpendicular to the plane. This type of projection preserves the true shape and size of the object, but distorts the depth and perspective.
- Perspective projection is a projection where the lines of sight converge at a single point called the vanishing point. This type of projection creates a realistic impression of the object, but alters the shape and size of the object.

- In engineering drawing, parallel projection is more commonly used than perspective projection, because it is easier to construct and measure.
- There are two main systems of parallel projection: orthographic projection and oblique projection.
- Orthographic projection is a parallel projection where the plane is perpendicular to one of the principal axes of the object. This type of projection shows the true shape and size of the object along that axis, but hides the other dimensions.
- Oblique projection is a parallel projection where the plane is inclined to one or more of the principal axes of the object. This type of projection shows the true shape and size of the object along the plane, but distorts the other dimensions.

- In engineering drawing, orthographic projection is more commonly used than oblique projection, because it is more accurate and standardized.
- There are two main methods of orthographic projection: first-angle projection and third-angle projection.
- First-angle projection is a method of orthographic projection where the object is placed in the first quadrant, between the plane and the observer. This type of projection shows the front view on the right, the top view on the bottom, and the left view on the left.
- Third-angle projection is a method of orthographic projection where the object is placed in the third quadrant, behind the plane and the observer. This type of projection shows the front view on the left, the top view on the top, and the right view on the right.

- In engineering drawing, the choice of projection method depends on the convention and preference of the region or industry. In general, first-angle projection is more widely used in Europe and Asia, while third-angle projection is more widely used in North America and Australia.
- To indicate the projection method used in a drawing, a symbol is placed in the bottom right corner of the drawing. The symbol consists of two truncated cones, one inside the other, representing the first and third quadrants. The cone that is shaded indicates the projection method used. - To summarize, projection of point is a technique for representing a three-dimensional point on a two-dimensional plane. There are different types and methods of projection, each with its own advantages and disadvantages. In engineering drawing, parallel projection, especially orthographic projection, is the most common type of projection, and the projection method used should be indicated by a symbol on the drawing.
