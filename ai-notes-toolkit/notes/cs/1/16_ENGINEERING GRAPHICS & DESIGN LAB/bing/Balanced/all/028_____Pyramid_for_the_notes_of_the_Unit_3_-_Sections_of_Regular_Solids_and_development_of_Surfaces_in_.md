# Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex.
- The development of a pyramid is the process of unfolding its lateral faces on a plane surface.
- The development of a pyramid can be obtained by drawing the true lengths and true inclinations of the slant edges from the vertex.
- The development of a pyramid can be used to find the surface area, the shape of the cut sections, and the dimensions of the sheet metal required to make the pyramid.

## Steps to draw the development of a pyramid

- Draw the front view and the top view of the pyramid in the first angle projection, showing the base, the axis, and the slant edges.
- Draw the true shape of the base by projecting it on a plane parallel to the base and perpendicular to the reference plane (H.P. or V.P.).
- Draw the true length of the axis by projecting it on a plane perpendicular to the axis and parallel to the reference plane.
- Draw the true length and true inclination of a slant edge by projecting it on a plane containing the axis and perpendicular to the reference plane.
- Draw an arc with the true length of the slant edge as the radius and the vertex as the center. Divide the arc into as many parts as the number of faces of the pyramid.
- Draw the development of the base by transferring the true shape of the base from the front view or the top view to the development plane. The base should be placed along the arc such that one of its sides coincides with a part of the arc.
- Draw the development of the lateral faces by joining the vertices of the base to the vertex of the pyramid along the arc. The lateral faces should be triangles with the base as one side and the slant edge as the hypotenuse.
- The development of the pyramid is complete.

## Example: Development of a square pyramid

- Given: A square pyramid of base 40 mm and height 60 mm rests on its base on the H.P. with one edge of the base parallel to the V.P.
- Required: To draw the development of the pyramid.

![Development of a square pyramid](https://i.imgur.com/7wZwJ5a.png)

- Steps:
  - Draw the front view and the top view of the pyramid as shown in the figure. Label the base as ABCD and the vertex as O. Mark the center of the base as E.
  - Draw the true shape of the base by projecting it on the V.P. as a square of 40 mm side. Label it as A'B'C'D'.
  - Draw the true length of the axis by projecting it on the H.P. as a line of 60 mm length. Label it as O'E'.
  - Draw the true length and true inclination of a slant edge by projecting it on the H.P. as a line of 70.71 mm length and 45° angle with the V.P. Label it as O'A'.
  - Draw an arc with O as the center and O'A' as the radius. Divide the arc into four equal parts and label them as 1, 2, 3, and 4.
  - Draw the development of the base by transferring the square A'B'C'D' to the development plane along the arc. The side A'B' should coincide with the part 1-2 of the arc.
  - Draw the development of the lateral faces by joining the vertices A', B', C', and D' to the vertex O along the arc. The lateral faces should be isosceles triangles with the base as A'B', B'C', C'D', and D'A' and the slant edge as O'A', O'B', O'C', and O'D'.
  - The development of the pyramid is complete.
