### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex.
- The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, such as a square, a triangle, a pentagon, etc.
- The height of a pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the vertex to the base plane.
- The slant height of a pyramid is the distance from the vertex to the midpoint of an edge of the base.
- The lateral surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the areas of the triangular faces, excluding the base.
- The total surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the lateral surface area and the base area.

#### Sections of Regular Solids

- A section of a solid is the shape obtained by cutting the solid with a plane, called the section plane or the cutting plane.
- The section plane can be parallel, perpendicular, or inclined to the base or the axis of the solid.
- The true shape of the section is the shape of the section as seen from a direction perpendicular to the section plane.
- The apparent shape of the section is the shape of the section as seen from a direction parallel to the axis of the solid.
- To draw the true shape of the section of a pyramid, the following steps are followed:

  - Draw the front view and the top view of the pyramid, showing the section plane and the cutting points on the edges of the pyramid.
  - Draw a line perpendicular to the section plane from the vertex of the pyramid and mark the point where it intersects the section plane as O.
  - Draw a line parallel to the base of the pyramid from O and mark the points where it intersects the cutting points on the edges of the pyramid as A, B, C, etc.
  - Draw a polygon ABC... with the same number of sides as the base of the pyramid and the same lengths as OA, OB, OC, etc.
  - This polygon is the true shape of the section of the pyramid.

#### Development of Surfaces

- The development of a surface of a solid is the unfolded or flattened shape of the surface when it is cut along some lines and laid out on a plane.
- The development of a surface is useful for making patterns or templates for cutting and bending materials to form the solid.
- The development of a surface should have the same dimensions and shape as the actual surface of the solid.
- To draw the development of the lateral surface of a pyramid, the following steps are followed:

  - Draw the front view and the top view of the pyramid, showing the slant heights and the base edges of the pyramid.
  - Draw a line AB equal to the base edge of the pyramid and mark the midpoint as O.
  - Draw a semicircle with radius equal to the slant height of the pyramid and center O.
  - Divide the semicircle into equal parts, corresponding to the number of faces of the pyramid.
  - Draw radii from O to the points of division and mark them as OA, OB, OC, etc.
  - Draw perpendiculars from A, B, C, etc. to AB and mark the points of intersection as A', B', C', etc.
  - Draw triangles OAA', OBB', OCC', etc. with the same angles as the triangular faces of the pyramid.
  - This figure is the development of the lateral surface of the pyramid.
