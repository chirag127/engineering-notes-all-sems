### Offset for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- An offset section is a type of section view that shows the internal details of a part that are not aligned on a straight line.
- An offset section is created by using a cutting plane that is bent or offset at one or more 90-degree angles to include the features that need to be shown.
- An offset section is useful for complex parts that have several important features that cannot be sectioned using a straight cutting plane.
- The rules for creating an offset section are:
  - All the bends of the section line must be 90 degrees.
  - The bent sections must not be shown as discrete sections while creating a hatch pattern to show that section.
  - The section line must be labeled with the word OFFSET and an arrow indicating the direction of viewing.
  - The section view must be aligned with the corresponding view of the part.
