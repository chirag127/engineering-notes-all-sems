### Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Computer Aided Design (CAD) is the use of computer software to design and document a product's design process.
2. CAD software is used to create 2D and 3D models of products, as well as technical drawings and other design documents.
3. CAD software can be used to simulate the behavior of a product under different conditions, allowing designers to test and optimize their designs before manufacturing.
4. CAD software can also be used to generate toolpaths for computer-controlled manufacturing equipment, such as CNC machines.
5. CAD software is widely used in industries such as architecture, engineering, and product design.
6. Some popular CAD software packages include AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and CATIA.
7. CAD software can improve the efficiency and accuracy of the design process, as well as facilitate collaboration between team members.
8. CAD software can also help to reduce the time and cost of bringing a product to market by streamlining the design and manufacturing process.
