### Rectangle

A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles. It is a type of parallelogram, and is also a type of polygon. The opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel and equal in length, and the diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length.

In computer-aided design (CAD), rectangles are commonly used as basic geometric shapes for creating more complex designs. They can be created using various tools and commands, such as the rectangle tool or the line tool.

Here are some key points to remember when working with rectangles in CAD:

1. To create a rectangle, you need to specify two opposite corners.
2. You can also create a rectangle by specifying its center point and dimensions.
3. Rectangles can be modified by changing the length of their sides or by moving their vertices.
4. You can use various commands to manipulate rectangles, such as rotate, scale, or mirror.
5. Rectangles can be combined with other geometric shapes to create more complex designs.
