### Dimensioning

Dimensioning is the process of adding measurements to a technical drawing. It is an essential part of creating clear and accurate drawings that can be used for manufacturing or construction. Here are some key points to remember when dimensioning:

1. **Units**: The units of measurement should be clearly stated, either in the title block or near the measurements themselves.
2. **Clarity**: Dimensions should be placed in a clear and unambiguous manner, avoiding any confusion or misinterpretation.
3. **Placement**: Dimensions should be placed outside the view, unless there is not enough space. In that case, they can be placed inside the view, but should not cross or obscure any lines.
4. **Spacing**: Adequate spacing should be provided between dimensions to avoid crowding and ensure readability.
5. **Alignment**: Dimensions should be aligned with the dimension lines and grouped together in a logical manner.
6. **Abbreviations**: Standard abbreviations should be used for units of measurement and other common terms.
7. **Arrows**: Arrowheads should be used to indicate the extent of the dimension and should touch the extension lines.
8. **Extension lines**: Extension lines should be used to extend the feature being dimensioned to the dimension line.
9. **Dimension lines**: Dimension lines should be thin, unbroken lines with arrowheads at each end.
10. **Leaders**: Leaders should be used to connect notes or dimensions to the relevant feature.
