### Lettering for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Lettering is an important part of engineering drawing.
2. It is used to convey information and instructions on the drawing.
3. Lettering should be clear, legible, and uniform in size and style.
4. There are two main types of lettering used in engineering drawing: single-stroke gothic and vertical gothic.
5. Single-stroke gothic lettering is made up of simple, single strokes, while vertical gothic lettering is made up of vertical strokes.
6. Both types of lettering can be written in either uppercase or lowercase letters.
7. The size of the lettering should be chosen based on the scale of the drawing and the amount of information that needs to be conveyed.
8. It is important to practice lettering to improve consistency and legibility.
9. Guidelines can be used to help align the letters and ensure that they are of uniform size.
10. The use of lettering templates or stencils can also help to improve consistency and speed up the lettering process.
