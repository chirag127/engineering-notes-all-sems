### Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

1. **Engineering Drawing**: Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel associated with the engineering profession. It is used to convey ideas and information about the design, construction, and operation of objects and systems.

2. **Orthographic Projections**: Orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is a form of parallel projection, where all the projection lines are orthogonal to the projection plane.

3. **Types of Orthographic Projections**: There are two types of orthographic projections: first-angle projection and third-angle projection. In first-angle projection, the object is placed in the first quadrant, while in third-angle projection, the object is placed in the third quadrant.

4. **Importance of Orthographic Projections**: Orthographic projections are important because they provide a clear and accurate representation of an object. They are commonly used in engineering and technical drawings to convey information about the shape, size, and position of an object.

5. **Applications of Orthographic Projections**: Orthographic projections are used in a variety of applications, including mechanical engineering, architecture, and product design. They are used to create detailed drawings of objects and systems, and to communicate design information to manufacturers and builders.

6. **Conclusion**: In conclusion, engineering drawing and orthographic projections are important tools for engineers and technical personnel. They provide a clear and accurate way to communicate design information and are widely used in a variety of applications. Understanding these concepts is essential for anyone working in the engineering profession.
