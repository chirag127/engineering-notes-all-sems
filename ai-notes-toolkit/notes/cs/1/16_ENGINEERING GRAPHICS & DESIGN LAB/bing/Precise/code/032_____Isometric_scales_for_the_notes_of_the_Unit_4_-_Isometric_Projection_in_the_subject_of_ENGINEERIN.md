### Isometric scales for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
- In isometric projection, the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angles between them are 120 degrees.
- Isometric scales are used to measure distances in isometric projection.
- An isometric scale is a scale that has been reduced by a factor of 1/√2 (0.707) in order to represent the foreshortening of the isometric axes.
- To construct an isometric scale, a regular scale is divided into equal parts and then each part is reduced by a factor of 1/√2.
- Isometric scales can be used to measure distances along the isometric axes and to determine the true size of an object in isometric projection.
- Isometric scales are useful in technical drawing and engineering design, as they allow for the accurate representation of three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
