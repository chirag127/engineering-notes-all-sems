### Projection of Lines

Projection of lines is a fundamental concept in engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It is the first topic in Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Projection of lines refers to the representation of a 3D line on a 2D plane.
2. The projection of a line can be obtained by drawing perpendiculars from the endpoints of the line to the plane of projection.
3. The points where the perpendiculars meet the plane of projection are called the projections of the endpoints of the line.
4. The line joining the projections of the endpoints is called the projection of the line.
5. The projection of a line can be seen as the shadow of the line on the plane of projection when a light source is placed perpendicular to the plane.
6. The length of the projection of a line is equal to the length of the line multiplied by the cosine of the angle between the line and the plane of projection.
7. The angle between the line and its projection is called the angle of inclination.
8. The projection of a line can be used to determine the true length of the line and the angle it makes with the plane of projection.
