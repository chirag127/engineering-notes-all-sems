### Isometric projections of simple and combination of solids for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is a type of axonometric projection, where the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angles between them are 120 degrees.

1. Isometric projection of simple solids:
- Simple solids such as cubes, cylinders, cones, and spheres can be represented in isometric projection.
- To draw an isometric projection of a simple solid, first draw an isometric box that encloses the solid.
- Then, draw the solid within the box, using the box's edges as guides.

2. Isometric projection of combination of solids:
- Combination of solids can also be represented in isometric projection.
- To draw an isometric projection of a combination of solids, first draw an isometric box that encloses the entire combination.
- Then, draw each solid within the box, using the box's edges as guides.

Isometric projection is a useful tool for engineers and designers, as it allows them to represent complex three-dimensional objects in a clear and concise manner. It is commonly used in technical drawings, diagrams, and illustrations.
