## Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design

Computer Aided Design (CAD) refers to the use of computer software to assist in the creation, modification, analysis, or optimization of a design. CAD software is used to increase the productivity of the designer, improve the quality of design, improve communications through documentation, and to create a database for manufacturing.

Some of the benefits of using CAD software include:
- Increased speed and accuracy of design
- Ability to create and test virtual prototypes
- Improved communication and collaboration
- Reduced design and manufacturing costs
- Increased design flexibility

CAD software is used in a variety of industries, including architecture, engineering, and product design. Some popular CAD software programs include AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and CATIA.

CAD software typically includes tools for 2D and 3D modeling, drafting, and visualization. These tools allow designers to create detailed and accurate models of their designs, which can then be used for analysis, simulation, and manufacturing.

In summary, CAD is an essential tool for modern design and manufacturing, providing numerous benefits and capabilities to designers and engineers. It is widely used across a range of industries and is an important part of the design and manufacturing process.
