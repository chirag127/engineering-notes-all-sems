### Pyramids for the notes of the Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Pyramids are a type of solid that can be projected in engineering graphics.
2. A pyramid is a polyhedron formed by connecting a polygonal base and a point, called the apex, by triangular faces.
3. The projection of a pyramid can be done when the axis is inclined to one of the principal planes by rotating the object method .
4. The projection of a pyramid can be done by resting one of its base edges on the HP and making an angle with the HP .
5. The projection of a pyramid can also be done by lying a triangular face on the HP and keeping the axis parallel to the VP .
6. The projection of a pyramid can be done by having an edge of its base on the ground and the axis inclined at an angle to the HP .
7. The projection of a pyramid can be done by using the auxiliary plane method .
8. The projection of a pyramid can be done by using the square pyramid method .
9. The projection of a pyramid can be done by using the hexagonal pyramid method .
