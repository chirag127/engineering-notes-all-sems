### Scales for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. **Introduction to Engineering Drawing:** Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel to convey technical information. It is a means of communication between the design and manufacturing stages of a product.

2. **Orthographic Projections:** Orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is a form of parallel projection, where the view direction is orthogonal to the projection plane.

3. **Scales:** In engineering drawing, scales are used to represent the size of an object in a drawing. The scale is the ratio of the size of the object in the drawing to its actual size. There are several types of scales used in engineering drawing, including full size, reduced size, and enlarged size.

4. **Full Size Scale:** A full size scale is a 1:1 scale, where the size of the object in the drawing is the same as its actual size.

5. **Reduced Size Scale:** A reduced size scale is used when the object is too large to be represented at full size in the drawing. The scale is less than 1:1, and the size of the object in the drawing is smaller than its actual size.

6. **Enlarged Size Scale:** An enlarged size scale is used when the object is too small to be represented at full size in the drawing. The scale is greater than 1:1, and the size of the object in the drawing is larger than its actual size.

7. **Selection of Scale:** The selection of the scale to be used in a drawing depends on several factors, including the size of the object, the level of detail required, and the size of the drawing sheet.

8. **Representing Scale:** The scale used in a drawing is typically represented using a scale bar or a scale ratio. A scale bar is a graphical representation of the scale, while a scale ratio is a numerical representation of the scale.
