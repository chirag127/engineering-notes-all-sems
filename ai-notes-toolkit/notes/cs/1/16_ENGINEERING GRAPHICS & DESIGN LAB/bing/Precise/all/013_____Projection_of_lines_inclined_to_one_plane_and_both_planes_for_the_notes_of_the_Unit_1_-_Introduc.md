### Projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections, the topic of projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes is an important concept to understand.

1. A line is said to be inclined to a plane when it is not perpendicular to the plane.
2. When a line is inclined to one plane, its projection on that plane will appear shorter than its true length.
3. When a line is inclined to both the horizontal and vertical planes, its projections on both planes will appear shorter than its true length.
4. The angle between the line and the plane is called the angle of inclination.
5. The true length of the line can be determined by using the principles of orthographic projection.
6. The true angle of inclination can also be determined by using the principles of orthographic projection.

It is important to understand these concepts in order to accurately represent and interpret engineering drawings. These principles can be applied to various types of engineering drawings, including mechanical, architectural, and electrical drawings. Understanding the projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes is essential for accurate and effective communication in the field of engineering.
