## Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel associated with the engineering profession. It is a means of communication between the design and manufacturing departments. The main purpose of engineering drawing is to convey the design ideas and technical information to the manufacturing department in the form of drawings.

Orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is a form of parallel projection, where the view direction is orthogonal to the projection plane, resulting in each plane of the scene appearing in affine transformation on the viewing surface.

The following are some key points to remember when working with orthographic projections:

1. Orthographic projections are used to represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
2. The view direction is orthogonal to the projection plane.
3. Each plane of the scene appears in affine transformation on the viewing surface.
4. Orthographic projections are commonly used in engineering and technical drawings.

In summary, engineering drawing is a means of communication between the design and manufacturing departments, and orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. These concepts are fundamental to the field of engineering and technical drawing.
