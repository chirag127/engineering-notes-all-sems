# Lettering for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Lettering is an important part of engineering drawing and is used to convey information on the drawing.
2. It is important to use clear and legible lettering to ensure that the information is easily understood.
3. There are two main types of lettering used in engineering drawing: single-stroke gothic and vertical gothic.
4. Single-stroke gothic lettering is created using a single stroke for each line of the letter. It is commonly used for notes, dimensions, and titles.
5. Vertical gothic lettering is created using vertical strokes and is commonly used for section and detail labels.
6. It is important to use consistent lettering throughout the drawing to ensure clarity and readability.
7. The size of the lettering should be appropriate for the scale of the drawing and the intended audience.
8. It is recommended to use a lettering guide or template to ensure consistency and accuracy in lettering.
9. Practice is important to improve lettering skills and to develop a personal style that is clear and legible.
10. Good lettering is an essential skill for any engineer or designer and can greatly improve the clarity and effectiveness of engineering drawings.
