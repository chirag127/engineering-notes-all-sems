# Sections of Solids

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 3 covers the topic of Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces. Here are some key points to remember about Sections of Solids:

1. A section of a solid is the shape obtained when a solid is cut by a plane.
2. The plane that cuts the solid is called the cutting plane or section plane.
3. The shape of the section depends on the orientation of the cutting plane relative to the solid.
4. The section can be represented graphically by drawing the intersection of the cutting plane with the solid.
5. Different types of sections can be obtained by varying the orientation of the cutting plane.
6. Common types of sections include longitudinal, transverse, and oblique sections.
7. The development of a surface is the process of unfolding a 3D shape into a 2D shape.
8. The development of a surface is used to create patterns for making 3D objects from flat materials.
