# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Engineering Graphics & Design Lab is a course that introduces students to the fundamental principles of engineering graphics and design. The course covers the following topics:

1. Introduction to engineering graphics and design, including the use of drawing instruments, lettering, and dimensioning.
2. Orthographic projection, including multi-view drawings and sectional views.
3. Pictorial drawings, including isometric, oblique, and perspective drawings.
4. Dimensioning and tolerancing, including geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T).
5. Auxiliary views and descriptive geometry, including the use of true length, true shape, and point view methods.
6. Computer-aided design (CAD) and drafting, including the use of CAD software to create and modify engineering drawings.

The course is designed to provide students with the skills and knowledge necessary to create and interpret engineering drawings and to use CAD software effectively. Students will have the opportunity to apply the concepts learned in the course through hands-on lab exercises and projects.
