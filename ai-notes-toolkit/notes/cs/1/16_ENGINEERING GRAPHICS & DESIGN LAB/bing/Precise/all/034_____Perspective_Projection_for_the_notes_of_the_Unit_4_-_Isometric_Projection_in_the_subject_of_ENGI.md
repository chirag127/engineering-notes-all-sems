# Perspective Projection

Perspective projection is a type of graphical projection used in engineering graphics and design. It is a method of representing three-dimensional objects on a two-dimensional plane, such as a piece of paper or a computer screen. In perspective projection, the lines of sight from the object to the viewer's eye converge at a single point, called the vanishing point. This creates the illusion of depth and makes the object appear more realistic.

Some key points to remember about perspective projection are:

1. Perspective projection is used to represent 3D objects on a 2D plane.
2. The lines of sight from the object to the viewer's eye converge at a single point, called the vanishing point.
3. Perspective projection creates the illusion of depth and makes the object appear more realistic.
4. There are different types of perspective projection, including one-point, two-point, and three-point perspective.
5. The choice of perspective projection depends on the position of the viewer relative to the object and the desired level of realism.
