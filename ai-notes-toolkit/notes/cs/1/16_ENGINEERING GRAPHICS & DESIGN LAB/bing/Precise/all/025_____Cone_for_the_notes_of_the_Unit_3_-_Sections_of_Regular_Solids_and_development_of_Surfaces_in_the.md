# Cone

A cone is a three-dimensional geometric shape that tapers smoothly from a flat base (usually circular) to a point called the apex or vertex.

## Sections of a Cone

When a plane cuts a cone, the resulting shape is called a section of the cone. The shape of the section depends on the angle and position of the cutting plane.

- If the cutting plane is parallel to the base of the cone, the section is a circle.
- If the cutting plane is perpendicular to the base of the cone and passes through the apex, the section is a triangle.
- If the cutting plane is perpendicular to the base of the cone but does not pass through the apex, the section is a parabola.
- If the cutting plane is at an angle to the base of the cone, the section is an ellipse.

## Development of the Surface of a Cone

The development of the surface of a cone is the process of unfolding the cone into a flat shape. The development of the surface of a cone is a sector of a circle. The radius of the circle is equal to the slant height of the cone, and the angle of the sector is equal to the angle between the slant height and the base of the cone.

To develop the surface of a cone, follow these steps:

1. Draw a circle with a radius equal to the slant height of the cone.
2. Measure the angle between the slant height and the base of the cone.
3. Draw a sector of the circle with the same angle.
4. Cut out the sector and roll it into a cone shape, aligning the straight edges.
