# Isometric Scales

Isometric scales are used in isometric projection, which is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. Isometric projection is commonly used in engineering and technical drawings. In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, isometric scales are covered in Unit 4 - Isometric Projection.

Here are some key points to remember about isometric scales:

1. Isometric scales are used to measure distances in isometric projection.
2. In isometric projection, the three axes are equally inclined to the plane of projection, and the angles between them are 120 degrees.
3. To construct an isometric scale, the true lengths are first reduced to their isometric lengths, which are shorter than the true lengths.
4. The isometric scale is then constructed by dividing the isometric length by the number of divisions on the true length scale.
5. Isometric scales can be used to measure distances along the isometric axes, as well as to find the isometric length of a line.
