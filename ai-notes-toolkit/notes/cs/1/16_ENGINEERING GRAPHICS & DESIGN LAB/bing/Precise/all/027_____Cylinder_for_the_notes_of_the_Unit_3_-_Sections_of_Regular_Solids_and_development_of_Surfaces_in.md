### Cylinder

A cylinder is a three-dimensional geometric shape that has two parallel, congruent bases that are usually circular in shape and are connected by a curved surface. The axis of the cylinder is the line segment that connects the centers of the two bases and is perpendicular to the bases.

In the context of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, the following points are important to note:

1. The section of a cylinder by a plane parallel to its base is a circle equal in size to the base of the cylinder.
2. The section of a cylinder by a plane perpendicular to its axis is a rectangle.
3. The development of the lateral surface of a right circular cylinder is a rectangle.
4. The development of the whole surface of a right circular cylinder consists of two circles and a rectangle.

These are some of the key points to remember when studying the cylinder in the context of sections of regular solids and development of surfaces. It is important to practice drawing and visualizing these concepts to fully understand them.
