### Creation of engineering models and their presentation in standard 2D blueprint form

1. Engineering models are created using computer-aided design (CAD) software. This software allows engineers to create, modify, analyze, and optimize designs.
2. The CAD software can generate 2D blueprints from the 3D models. These blueprints are used to communicate the design to others, such as manufacturers or builders.
3. The blueprints are created in a standard format, which includes dimensions, annotations, and symbols. This standardization ensures that the design can be understood by anyone familiar with the blueprint format.
4. The 2D blueprints can be printed or shared electronically. They are used to guide the manufacturing or construction process.
5. The use of CAD software and standardized blueprints improves the accuracy and efficiency of the design process. It also allows for easier collaboration and communication between team members.
