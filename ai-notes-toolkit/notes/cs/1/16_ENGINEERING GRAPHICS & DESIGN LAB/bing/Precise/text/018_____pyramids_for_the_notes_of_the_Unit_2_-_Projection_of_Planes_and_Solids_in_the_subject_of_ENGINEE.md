### Pyramids

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common point called the apex.
- In the context of engineering graphics and design, the projection of pyramids is an important topic.
- The projection of a pyramid involves representing the 3D object on a 2D plane.
- The axis of the pyramid can be inclined to one plane by using the change of position method .
- The section of a pyramid in a simple vertical position (axis perpendicular to the horizontal plane alone) can be obtained by planes either inclined to the horizontal plane or the vertical plane alone. This gives the true shape of the section .
- For example, a square pyramid of base 30mm and height 60mm can rest with one of its base edges on the horizontal plane. The axis of the pyramid can make an angle of 45° with the horizontal plane .
