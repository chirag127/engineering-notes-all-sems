### Lettering for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Lettering is an important part of engineering drawing.
2. It is used to convey information and instructions on the drawing.
3. Lettering should be clear, legible, and uniform in size and style.
4. There are two types of lettering used in engineering drawing: single-stroke gothic and vertical gothic.
5. Single-stroke gothic lettering is made with a single stroke of the pen or pencil and is commonly used for notes and dimensions.
6. Vertical gothic lettering is made with multiple strokes and is used for titles and headings.
7. The size of the lettering should be appropriate for the scale of the drawing and the intended audience.
8. The spacing between letters and words should be consistent and visually pleasing.
9. Guidelines may be used to ensure uniformity in the size and alignment of the lettering.
10. Practice and attention to detail are important in achieving high-quality lettering on engineering drawings.
