## Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

1. **Engineering Drawing**: Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel associated with the engineering profession. It is a means of communication between the engineer and the manufacturer, and between the engineer and his associates.
2. **Purpose of Engineering Drawing**: The main purpose of engineering drawing is to convey the design ideas and technical information from the engineer to the manufacturer or other technical personnel. It is used to accurately and unambiguously define the shape, size, and other details of an object or a system.
3. **Orthographic Projections**: Orthographic projection is a method of representing a three-dimensional object in two dimensions. It is a type of parallel projection, where the view direction is orthogonal to the projection plane. In orthographic projection, the object is viewed from several directions, usually the front, top, and side views.
4. **Types of Orthographic Projections**: There are two types of orthographic projections: first-angle projection and third-angle projection. In first-angle projection, the object is placed in the first quadrant, while in third-angle projection, the object is placed in the third quadrant.
5. **Importance of Orthographic Projections**: Orthographic projections are important in engineering drawing because they provide a clear and accurate representation of an object or a system. They are used to communicate the design ideas and technical information from the engineer to the manufacturer or other technical personnel.
