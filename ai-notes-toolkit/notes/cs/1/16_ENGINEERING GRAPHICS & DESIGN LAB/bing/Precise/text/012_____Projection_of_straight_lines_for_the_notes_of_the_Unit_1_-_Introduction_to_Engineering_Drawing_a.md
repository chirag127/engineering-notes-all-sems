### Projection of Straight Lines

Projection of straight lines is a fundamental concept in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, specifically in Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections. Here are some key points to remember:

1. A straight line can be defined as the shortest distance between two points.
2. In engineering drawing, straight lines are projected onto different planes to create two-dimensional representations of three-dimensional objects.
3. The projection of a straight line onto a plane is also a straight line.
4. The length and orientation of the projected line depend on the angle between the original line and the plane of projection.
5. Orthographic projection is a commonly used method for projecting straight lines onto planes. In this method, the projection is made perpendicular to the plane of projection.
6. The front, top, and side views of an object are created using orthographic projection.
7. It is important to carefully choose the planes of projection to accurately represent the object being drawn.

These are some of the key concepts to remember when studying the projection of straight lines in the context of engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It is important to practice drawing and visualizing these concepts to fully understand and apply them.
