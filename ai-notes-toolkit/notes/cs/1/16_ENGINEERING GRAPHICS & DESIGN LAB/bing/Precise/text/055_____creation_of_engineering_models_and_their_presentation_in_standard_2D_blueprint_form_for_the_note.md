### Creation of Engineering Models and their Presentation in Standard 2D Blueprint Form

Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB KCS

1. Engineering models are created using computer-aided design (CAD) software. This software allows engineers to create 3D models of their designs, which can then be used to create 2D blueprints.
2. The 2D blueprints are created by projecting the 3D model onto a 2D plane. This is done using standard projection techniques, such as orthographic or isometric projection.
3. The resulting 2D blueprint will show the object as if it were viewed from a specific angle, with all of its features and dimensions accurately represented.
4. These 2D blueprints are then used by manufacturers to create the physical object. They provide all of the necessary information, such as dimensions, tolerances, and materials, to ensure that the object is manufactured accurately.
5. Standard 2D blueprint form refers to the use of standard symbols and conventions to represent the various features of the object. This includes the use of dimension lines, section views, and hatching to represent different materials.
6. The use of standard 2D blueprint form ensures that the blueprint can be easily understood by anyone familiar with the conventions, regardless of their background or training.
7. In summary, the creation of engineering models and their presentation in standard 2D blueprint form is an essential part of the design process. It allows engineers to accurately communicate their designs to manufacturers, ensuring that the final product meets the desired specifications.
