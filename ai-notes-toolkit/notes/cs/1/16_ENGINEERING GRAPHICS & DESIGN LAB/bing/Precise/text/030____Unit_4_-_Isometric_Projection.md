## Unit 4 - Isometric Projection

Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is a type of axonometric projection, which is a category of parallel projection techniques used to create a pictorial drawing of an object.

Some key points to remember about isometric projection are:

1. Isometric projection is a form of graphical projection, which is a technique used to represent three-dimensional objects on a two-dimensional plane.
2. In isometric projection, the most commonly used form of axonometric projection, the direction of viewing is such that the three axes of space appear equally foreshortened, and the angles between them are 120 degrees.
3. Isometric projection is used in engineering and technical drawing to represent objects in a way that is easy to visualize and understand.
4. Isometric projection can also be used in video games and other forms of digital media to create a 3D effect on a 2D screen.
5. Isometric projection is not the same as isometric view, which is a perspective projection technique that is also used to represent 3D objects in 2D.
