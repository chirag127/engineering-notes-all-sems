### Projection of Lines

Projection of lines is a fundamental concept in engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It is the first topic in Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Projection of lines refers to the representation of a three-dimensional line on a two-dimensional plane, such as a piece of paper or a computer screen.
2. This is achieved by drawing the line in such a way that it appears to be perpendicular to the plane of projection.
3. The line is then projected onto the plane, creating a two-dimensional representation of the original three-dimensional line.
4. This technique is used to create technical drawings and blueprints, which are essential for the design and construction of buildings, machines, and other objects.
5. There are two main types of projection used in engineering drawing: parallel projection and perspective projection.
6. Parallel projection is used when the object being drawn is relatively close to the plane of projection, while perspective projection is used when the object is farther away.
7. Understanding the principles of projection of lines is essential for anyone studying engineering drawing and orthographic projections.
