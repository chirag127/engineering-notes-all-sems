### Cylinder and Cone when the Axis is Inclined to One Reference Plane by Change of Position Method

#### Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the Subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab

1. The change of position method is used to project a cylinder or cone when its axis is inclined to one of the reference planes.
2. In this method, the cylinder or cone is first positioned such that its axis is parallel to one of the reference planes and perpendicular to the other.
3. The projections on the reference planes are then obtained in this position.
4. The cylinder or cone is then rotated about an axis perpendicular to the reference plane to which its axis was parallel, until its axis is inclined at the desired angle to the reference plane.
5. The projections on the reference planes are then obtained in this new position.
6. This method is useful when the angle of inclination of the axis to the reference plane is known.
