### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common point called the apex.
- The base of a pyramid can be any polygon, including a square, rectangle, triangle, or hexagon.
- The triangular faces of a pyramid are called lateral faces.
- The height of a pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the base to the apex.
- A regular pyramid is a pyramid whose base is a regular polygon and whose lateral faces are congruent isosceles triangles.
- The slant height of a regular pyramid is the distance from the center of the base to the midpoint of an edge of the base.
- The lateral area of a regular pyramid is the sum of the areas of its lateral faces.
- The total surface area of a regular pyramid is the sum of the lateral area and the area of the base.
- The volume of a pyramid is one-third the product of the area of the base and the height.
