### Hatch
Hatch is a tool used in computer-aided design (CAD) to fill an enclosed area or selected objects with a pattern or solid color. It is commonly used in technical drawings, such as engineering and architectural plans, to represent different materials or textures.

Some key points to remember about hatch in CAD are:
- Hatch patterns can be predefined or custom.
- The scale and angle of the hatch pattern can be adjusted.
- Hatch can be associative, meaning it will update if the boundary changes.
- The hatch command can also be used to fill an area with a solid color or gradient.

Hatch is an important tool in CAD as it helps to convey information about the materials and textures used in a design. It can also add visual interest to a technical drawing. In the context of the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, hatch can be used to represent different materials in a design, such as concrete, wood, or metal. It can also be used to represent different textures, such as a rough or smooth surface.
