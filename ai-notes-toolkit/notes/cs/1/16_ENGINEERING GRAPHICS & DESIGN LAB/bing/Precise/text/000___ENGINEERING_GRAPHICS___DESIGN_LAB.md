# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Engineering Graphics and Design Lab is a course that introduces students to the fundamental principles of technical drawing and design. The course covers topics such as:

1. **Orthographic Projection:** This is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is commonly used in engineering and architecture to create detailed technical drawings.

2. **Isometric Projection:** This is another method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is commonly used to create pictorial drawings that show an object's overall shape and proportions.

3. **Dimensioning and Tolerancing:** This involves adding dimensions and tolerances to technical drawings to specify the size and allowable variation of an object's features.

4. **Sectional Views:** These are used to show the internal details of an object that are not visible from the outside.

5. **Auxiliary Views:** These are used to show features that are not parallel to the primary planes of projection.

6. **Computer-Aided Design (CAD):** This is the use of computer software to create, modify, analyze, and optimize technical drawings and designs.

The course typically includes both theoretical instruction and hands-on practice in the lab, where students learn to use drafting tools and CAD software to create technical drawings and designs. The skills learned in this course are essential for many fields of engineering and design.
