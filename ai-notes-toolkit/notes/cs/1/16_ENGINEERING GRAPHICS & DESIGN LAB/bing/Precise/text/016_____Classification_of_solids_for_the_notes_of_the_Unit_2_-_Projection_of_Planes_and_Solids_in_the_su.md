### Classification of Solids

Solids can be classified into two main groups: Polyhedra and Solids of Revolution.

1. **Polyhedra**: A Polyhedra is defined as a solid bounded by planes called faces which meet in straight lines called edges. They can be further classified into:
    - Regular Polyhedra
    - Prisms
    - Pyramids

2. **Solids of Revolution**: When a solid is generated by revolutions of a plane figure about a fixed line (Axis) then such solids are named as solids of revolution. Solids of revolutions may be of following types:
    - Cylinder
    - Cone
    - Sphere
    - Ellipsoid
    - Paraboloid
    - Hyperboloid

Solids can also be broadly classified into two types: crystalline solids and amorphous solids. A crystalline solid is a substance whose constituent particles possess regular orderly arrangement e.g. Sodium chloride, sucrose, diamond etc.
