### Perspective Projection

Perspective projection is a type of graphical projection used in engineering graphics and design to represent three-dimensional objects on a two-dimensional plane. It is used to create a realistic representation of an object as it would appear to an observer from a particular viewpoint.

Some key points to remember about perspective projection are:

1. Perspective projection is used to create a realistic representation of an object as it would appear to an observer from a particular viewpoint.
2. In perspective projection, the lines of projection converge at a single point, called the vanishing point.
3. The distance between the observer and the object, as well as the position of the observer, affects the appearance of the object in perspective projection.
4. Perspective projection can be classified into one-point, two-point, and three-point perspective, depending on the number of vanishing points used.
5. Perspective projection is commonly used in art, architecture, and engineering to create realistic representations of objects.
