### Right Regular Solids and Auxiliary Views for the True Shape of the Sections such as Prism

Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab

1. A right regular solid is a polyhedron with all faces congruent regular polygons and all solid angles congruent.
2. The five Platonic solids are the only right regular solids: tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron.
3. An auxiliary view is an orthographic view taken in a direction other than one of the six principal views.
4. Auxiliary views are used to show the true shape and size of inclined or oblique surfaces.
5. The true shape of a section is the shape of the intersection of a cutting plane with the solid.
6. To determine the true shape of a section, an auxiliary view is drawn perpendicular to the cutting plane.
7. A prism is a polyhedron with two parallel, congruent polygonal faces and rectangular lateral faces.
8. The true shape of a section of a prism can be determined using an auxiliary view.
9. The development of a surface is the process of unfolding a 3D shape into a 2D pattern.
10. The development of a surface can be used to create a pattern for cutting and folding a sheet of material to form the 3D shape.
