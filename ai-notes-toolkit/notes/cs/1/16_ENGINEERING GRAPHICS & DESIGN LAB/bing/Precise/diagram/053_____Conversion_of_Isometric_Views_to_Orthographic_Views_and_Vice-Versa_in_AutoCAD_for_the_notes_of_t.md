### Conversion of Isometric Views to Orthographic Views and Vice-Versa in AutoCAD

Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Isometric views are 3D representations of objects, while orthographic views are 2D representations.
2. In AutoCAD, it is possible to convert between isometric and orthographic views using the `VIEWBASE` command.
3. To convert an isometric view to an orthographic view, first, select the isometric view and then enter the `VIEWBASE` command. Select the `From model space` option and then select the desired orthographic view.
4. To convert an orthographic view to an isometric view, first, select the orthographic view and then enter the `VIEWBASE` command. Select the `From model space` option and then select the desired isometric view.
5. It is important to note that the conversion process may result in some loss of detail or accuracy, as the two types of views represent the object in different ways.
6. It is recommended to practice the conversion process to become familiar with the steps and potential issues that may arise.
