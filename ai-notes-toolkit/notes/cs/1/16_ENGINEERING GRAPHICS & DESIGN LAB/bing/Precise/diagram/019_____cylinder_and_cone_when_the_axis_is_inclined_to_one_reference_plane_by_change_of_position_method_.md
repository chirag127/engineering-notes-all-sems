### Cylinder and Cone when the Axis is Inclined to One Reference Plane by Change of Position Method

#### Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. When the axis of a cylinder or cone is inclined to one of the reference planes, the true shape of the base can be obtained by the change of position method.
2. In this method, the solid is rotated about one of its edges until the axis is parallel to the reference plane.
3. The true shape of the base can then be projected onto the reference plane.
4. The projections of the solid can be obtained by projecting the points of the solid onto the reference planes.
5. This method can be used to obtain the projections of the solid when the axis is inclined to one of the reference planes and the base is either parallel or perpendicular to the other reference plane.
