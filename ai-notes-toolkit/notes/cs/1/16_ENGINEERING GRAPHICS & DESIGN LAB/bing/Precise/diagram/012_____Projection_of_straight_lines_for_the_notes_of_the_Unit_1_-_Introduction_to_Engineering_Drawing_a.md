### Projection of Straight Lines

Projection of straight lines is a topic in Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. A straight line can be defined as the shortest distance between two points.
2. In engineering drawing, the projection of a straight line refers to the representation of the line on a plane.
3. The projection of a straight line can be done using various methods, including parallel projection and perspective projection.
4. In parallel projection, the line is projected onto the plane along parallel lines, resulting in a true representation of the line's length and direction.
5. In perspective projection, the line is projected onto the plane along converging lines, resulting in a distorted representation of the line's length and direction.
6. The choice of projection method depends on the purpose of the drawing and the desired level of accuracy.
7. It is important to understand the principles of projection in order to accurately represent straight lines in engineering drawings.
