#### Hatch

- Hatch is a tool used in computer-aided design (CAD) to fill an enclosed area or selected objects with a pattern or solid color.
- Hatching is used to represent different materials, textures, or shading in technical drawings.
- In CAD software, hatching can be applied to 2D drawings or 3D models.
- There are various types of hatching patterns available, including standard, custom, and user-defined patterns.
- Hatching can be applied to a single object or a group of objects.
- The scale, angle, and spacing of the hatching pattern can be adjusted to achieve the desired effect.
- Hatching can also be used to add visual interest or emphasis to a drawing.
