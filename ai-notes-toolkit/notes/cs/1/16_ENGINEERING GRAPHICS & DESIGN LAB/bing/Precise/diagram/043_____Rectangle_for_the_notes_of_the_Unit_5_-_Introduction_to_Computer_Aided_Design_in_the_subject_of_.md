### Rectangle

A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles. It is a type of parallelogram, and is also a type of polygon. The opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel and equal in length, and the diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length.

In the context of computer-aided design (CAD), a rectangle can be created using various tools and commands. In many CAD programs, there is a specific tool for creating rectangles, which allows the user to specify the length and width of the rectangle, as well as its position and orientation.

Some key points to remember when working with rectangles in CAD include:
- The opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel and equal in length.
- The diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length.
- Rectangles can be created using specific tools and commands in CAD programs.
- The length, width, position, and orientation of a rectangle can be specified when creating it in CAD.
