### Projection of polygonal surface and circular lamina located in first quadrant inclined to one or both reference planes

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids, the projection of polygonal surface and circular lamina located in the first quadrant inclined to one or both reference planes is an important topic.

1. A polygonal surface is a flat surface that is made up of multiple straight sides. A circular lamina is a flat, circular surface.
2. When a polygonal surface or circular lamina is located in the first quadrant, it means that it is positioned in the positive x and y directions from the origin of the coordinate system.
3. When a polygonal surface or circular lamina is inclined to one or both reference planes, it means that it is not perpendicular to the reference planes.
4. The reference planes are the horizontal plane (HP) and the vertical plane (VP).
5. The projection of a polygonal surface or circular lamina onto the reference planes is the shadow or outline of the surface or lamina as seen from the reference planes.
6. The projection of a polygonal surface or circular lamina onto the HP is called the top view, while the projection onto the VP is called the front view.
7. The angle of inclination of the surface or lamina to the reference planes affects the shape and size of the projections.
8. The projections can be drawn using various methods, such as the first angle projection method or the third angle projection method.
