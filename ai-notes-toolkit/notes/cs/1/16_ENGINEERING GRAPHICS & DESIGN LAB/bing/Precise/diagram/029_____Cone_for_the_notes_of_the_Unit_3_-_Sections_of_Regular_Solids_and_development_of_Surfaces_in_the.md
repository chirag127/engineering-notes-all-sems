### Cone
A cone is a three-dimensional geometric shape that tapers smoothly from a flat base to a point called the apex or vertex.

#### Sections of a Cone
- A cone can be cut by a plane to produce different sections.
- If the plane is perpendicular to the base of the cone, the section will be a circle.
- If the plane is at an angle to the base of the cone, the section will be an ellipse.
- If the plane passes through the apex of the cone, the section will be a triangle.

#### Development of the Surface of a Cone
- The development of the surface of a cone is the process of unfolding the surface of the cone into a flat plane.
- The development of the surface of a cone is a sector of a circle.
- The radius of the circle is equal to the slant height of the cone.
- The angle of the sector is equal to the angle subtended by the base of the cone at the apex.
