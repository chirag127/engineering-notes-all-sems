### Projection of Point

Projection of a point is the representation of a point in a two-dimensional plane. In the context of engineering drawing and orthographic projections, it is the process of representing a three-dimensional point on a two-dimensional plane, such as a piece of paper or a computer screen.

Here are some key points to remember when projecting a point:

1. The point is represented by its coordinates in the three-dimensional space.
2. The projection is achieved by drawing lines from the point to the plane of projection, perpendicular to the plane.
3. The point where the line intersects the plane is the projection of the point.
4. The projection of a point can be represented using different methods, such as first-angle projection or third-angle projection.
5. The choice of projection method depends on the conventions and standards used in the field of engineering or design.

In summary, the projection of a point is a fundamental concept in engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It allows the representation of three-dimensional objects in a two-dimensional plane, which is essential for communication and visualization in the field of engineering and design.
