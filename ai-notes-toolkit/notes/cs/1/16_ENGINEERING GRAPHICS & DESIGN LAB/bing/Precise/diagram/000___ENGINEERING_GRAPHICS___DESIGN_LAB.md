# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Engineering Graphics and Design Lab is a course that introduces students to the fundamental principles of engineering graphics and design. The course covers topics such as:

1. Technical drawing: Students learn the basics of technical drawing, including orthographic projection, isometric projection, and dimensioning.

2. Computer-aided design (CAD): Students learn how to use CAD software to create 2D and 3D models of engineering designs.

3. Design process: Students learn about the design process, including problem definition, ideation, prototyping, and testing.

4. Engineering materials: Students learn about the properties and selection of engineering materials.

5. Manufacturing processes: Students learn about common manufacturing processes, including casting, forming, machining, and joining.

The course typically includes both lectures and hands-on lab sessions, where students have the opportunity to apply the concepts they have learned to real-world design problems. The course is designed to help students develop the skills and knowledge necessary to communicate their ideas effectively and to create high-quality engineering designs.
