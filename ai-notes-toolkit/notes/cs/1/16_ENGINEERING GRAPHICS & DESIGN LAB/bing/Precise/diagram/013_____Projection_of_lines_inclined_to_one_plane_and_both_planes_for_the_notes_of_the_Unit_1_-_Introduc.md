### Projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections, the projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes is an important topic.

1. **Projection of lines inclined to one plane**: When a line is inclined to one of the principal planes, its projections on that plane will appear as a line, while its projection on the other plane will appear as a point.
2. **Projection of lines inclined to both planes**: When a line is inclined to both the principal planes, its projections on both planes will appear as lines.
3. **True length and true inclination**: To determine the true length and true inclination of the line, it is necessary to construct an auxiliary plane perpendicular to the line. The projection of the line on this auxiliary plane will show the true length and true inclination of the line.
4. **Methods for determining true length and true inclination**: There are several methods for determining the true length and true inclination of a line, including the rotating line method, the auxiliary plane method, and the four-center method.

It is important to understand and practice these concepts in order to accurately represent and interpret engineering drawings.
