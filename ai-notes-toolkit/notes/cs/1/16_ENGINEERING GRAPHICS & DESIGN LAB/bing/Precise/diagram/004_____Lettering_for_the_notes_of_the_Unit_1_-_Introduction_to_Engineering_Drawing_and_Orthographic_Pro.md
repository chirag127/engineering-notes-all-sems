### Lettering for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Lettering is an important part of engineering drawing.
2. It is used to convey information and instructions on the drawing.
3. Lettering should be clear, legible, and uniform in size and style.
4. The recommended lettering style for engineering drawings is the vertical Gothic style.
5. The size of the letters should be chosen based on the scale of the drawing and the amount of information to be conveyed.
6. The spacing between letters and words should be consistent and appropriate for the size of the letters.
7. The use of upper and lower case letters, as well as the use of bold and italic styles, can be used to convey emphasis and hierarchy of information.
8. Proper lettering is essential for clear and effective communication in engineering drawings.
