### Fillet

A fillet is a curved surface that connects two surfaces that meet at an angle. In the context of computer-aided design (CAD), a fillet is a tool that allows the user to create a smooth, rounded transition between two surfaces. Fillets are commonly used in engineering and design to reduce stress concentrations and improve the appearance of a part.

Here are some key points to remember about fillets in CAD:

1. Fillets can be added to both internal and external corners of a part.
2. The size of the fillet is determined by the radius of the arc that forms the curved surface.
3. Fillets can be added to 2D sketches or 3D models.
4. In most CAD software, the fillet tool can be accessed from the modify or edit menu.
5. When creating a fillet, the user can specify the radius of the fillet and select the edges or faces to which the fillet will be applied.
6. Fillets can be previewed before being applied to the model, allowing the user to make adjustments as needed.
7. Fillets can be edited or removed after they have been applied to the model.
