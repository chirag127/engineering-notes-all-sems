### Orthographic representation of perspective views — Plane figures and simple solids — Visual Ray Method

Orthographic representation is a technique used to represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is commonly used in technical drawing and engineering graphics to convey the shape and size of an object.

- Plane figures and simple solids are two types of objects that can be represented using orthographic projection.
- Plane figures are two-dimensional shapes such as circles, squares, and triangles.
- Simple solids are three-dimensional objects such as cubes, spheres, and cylinders.
- The visual ray method is a technique used to create an orthographic projection of an object.
- This method involves drawing lines, called visual rays, from the object to the plane of projection.
- The points where the visual rays intersect the plane of projection are used to create the orthographic representation of the object.

This technique is useful for creating accurate and detailed drawings of objects, and is commonly used in engineering graphics and design. It is an important concept to understand for students studying isometric projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.
