### Transformation of Projections

Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Transformation of projections refers to the process of converting a 3D object into a 2D representation on a plane.
2. This is achieved through the use of projection techniques, such as orthographic, isometric, and perspective projections.
3. Orthographic projections represent the object as it appears from the front, top, and side views.
4. Isometric projections represent the object as it appears from a corner view, with all three dimensions equally foreshortened.
5. Perspective projections represent the object as it appears to the eye, with the size of the object decreasing as it recedes into the distance.
6. These projection techniques are used in computer-aided design (CAD) to create technical drawings and visualizations of 3D objects.
7. CAD software allows for the easy manipulation and transformation of projections, enabling designers to quickly create and modify technical drawings.
8. Understanding the principles of transformation of projections is essential for creating accurate and effective technical drawings in the field of engineering graphics and design.
