### Circle

In the Unit 5 of Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB, Circle is an essential element. Here are some important points to note about circles in CAD:

- A circle is a closed curve that is defined by a set of points that are equidistant from a fixed point, called the center of the circle.
- In CAD, circles are defined by their center point and radius. The center point is the point in the plane that is equidistant from all points on the circle, and the radius is the distance from the center point to any point on the circle.
- To draw a circle in CAD, you can use the Circle tool. You can either specify the center point and radius or use other methods to define the circle, such as by specifying three points on the circle or by specifying the diameter of the circle.
- You can also modify circles in CAD by changing their radius, center point, or other properties. You can use the Modify tool to do this.
- In addition to drawing circles, you can also use circles in other CAD operations, such as creating arcs or filleting corners.
- Circles are important in many areas of engineering and design, such as in the design of gears, bearings, and other mechanical components.
- Understanding how to draw and modify circles in CAD is an important skill for anyone working in engineering or design.

In conclusion, circles are an essential element in CAD, and understanding how to draw and modify them is an important skill for anyone working in engineering or design.
