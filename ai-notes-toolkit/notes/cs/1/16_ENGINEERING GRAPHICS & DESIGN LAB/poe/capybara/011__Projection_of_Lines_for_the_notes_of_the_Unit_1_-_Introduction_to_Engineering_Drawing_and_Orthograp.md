### Projection of Lines

In the field of engineering drawing and orthographic projections, the projection of lines plays a crucial role. Here are some key points to understand the concept of projection of lines:

- Projection of a line is the process of determining the points at which it intersects with a given plane or surface.
- The projection of a line can be either perpendicular or oblique to the given plane or surface.
- In perpendicular projection, the projection of the line is perpendicular to the given plane or surface. This type of projection is used in orthographic projections.
- In oblique projection, the projection of the line is not perpendicular to the given plane or surface. This type of projection is used in pictorial projections.
- The projection of a line can be shown using different symbols, such as dots or dashes, depending on the type of projection used.
- The projection of a line can be classified as a visible line or a hidden line based on whether it is visible or hidden in the final drawing.
- In engineering drawing, the projection of a line is used to create accurate and detailed drawings of objects and structures.
- The projection of lines is an important concept in understanding the principles of orthographic projections, which are used to create 2D representations of 3D objects.
- Understanding the projection of lines is essential for engineers and designers to accurately communicate their designs and ideas. 

In conclusion, the projection of lines is a fundamental concept in the field of engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It is a crucial tool for engineers and designers to accurately communicate their designs and ideas through drawings and plans.
