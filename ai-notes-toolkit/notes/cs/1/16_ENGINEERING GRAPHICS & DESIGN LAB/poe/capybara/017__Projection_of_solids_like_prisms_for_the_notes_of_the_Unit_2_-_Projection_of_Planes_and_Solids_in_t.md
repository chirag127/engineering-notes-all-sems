### Projection of Solids like Prisms

In Engineering Graphics and Design Lab, you will come across the concept of projection of solids like prisms. This is an important topic that you need to understand thoroughly in order to excel in your exams. Here are some key points to help you understand the topic better:

- A prism is a solid figure that has two parallel and congruent polygonal faces, and rectangular faces that connect them.
- There are different types of prisms, such as triangular prisms, rectangular prisms, hexagonal prisms, and so on.
- In projection, we represent the three-dimensional object on a two-dimensional plane.
- To project a prism, we need to consider its front view, top view, and side view.
- The front view of a prism is a rectangular shape, while the top view is the same as the base of the prism.
- The side view of the prism shows the height of the prism and the shape of the base.
- To project a prism, we first draw the front view, then the top view and finally the side view.
- In the front view, we project the height of the prism perpendicular to the base, and in the top view, we project the height of the prism parallel to the base.
- The side view shows the height and the shape of the base of the prism.
- By projecting the three views of the prism, we can get a complete and accurate representation of the solid figure on a two-dimensional plane.

In summary, understanding the concept of projection of solids like prisms is essential for anyone studying Engineering Graphics and Design Lab. By following the above points, you can gain a better understanding of this topic and prepare yourself for your exams.
