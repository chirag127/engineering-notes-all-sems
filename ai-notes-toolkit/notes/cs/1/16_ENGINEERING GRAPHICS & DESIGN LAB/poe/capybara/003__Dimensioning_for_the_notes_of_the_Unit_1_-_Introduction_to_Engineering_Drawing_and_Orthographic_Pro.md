### Dimensioning

Dimensioning is one of the most important aspects of engineering drawing. It involves the process of adding measurements to a drawing, which helps in communicating the size and shape of a product. The following are some of the points related to dimensioning that you should keep in mind while studying the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB:

- Dimensioning should be done in a clear, concise, and consistent manner. It should be easy to read and understand.
- The dimension lines should be placed outside the object, and the measurements should be written between the extension lines.
- The dimension should be located in such a way that it does not interfere with other lines on the drawing. It should be placed in a visible location.
- The arrows at the end of the dimension line should point towards the object being measured.
- The units of measurement should be clearly stated, and they should be consistent throughout the drawing.
- The tolerances should be included with the dimensions, and they should be clearly identified.
- The dimensions should be given in the correct order, and they should be arranged in a logical manner.
- The dimensions should be appropriate for the intended use of the drawing. They should be accurate enough for the purpose of the drawing.
- The dimensioning should be done in accordance with the standards and conventions used in the industry or organization.
- The dimensioning should be reviewed by a qualified person to ensure that it is accurate and complete.

In conclusion, dimensioning is an important aspect of engineering drawing that helps in communicating the size and shape of a product. It should be done in a clear, concise, and consistent manner, and it should be reviewed by a qualified person to ensure its accuracy and completeness.
