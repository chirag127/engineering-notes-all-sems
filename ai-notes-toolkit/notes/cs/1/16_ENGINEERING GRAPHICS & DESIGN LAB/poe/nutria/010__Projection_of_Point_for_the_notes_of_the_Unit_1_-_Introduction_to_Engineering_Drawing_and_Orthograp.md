
### Projection of Point

1. A point is defined as a zero-dimensional object that has no length, width, or depth. 
2. In engineering graphics and design, a point is represented by a dot.
3. A point can be projected in three different ways: orthographic projection, isometric projection, and oblique projection.
4. Orthographic projection is the most common method used to project points in engineering drawings. It involves projecting a point onto a two-dimensional plane.
5. Isometric projection is used to project a point onto a three-dimensional plane. It is commonly used to create isometric views of objects.
6. Oblique projection is used to project a point onto a two-dimensional plane, but at an angle. This is commonly used to create oblique views of objects.
7. In orthographic projection, points can be projected onto the front view, top view, and side view.
8. In isometric projection, points can be projected onto the isometric view.
9. In oblique projection, points can be projected onto the oblique view.
10. All three projections of points can be used to create engineering drawings.
