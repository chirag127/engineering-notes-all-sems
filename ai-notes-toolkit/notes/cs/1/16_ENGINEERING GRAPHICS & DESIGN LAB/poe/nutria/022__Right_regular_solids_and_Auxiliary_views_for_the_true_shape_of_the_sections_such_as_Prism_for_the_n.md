
### Right Regular Solids and Auxiliary Views for the True Shape of Sections

* Right regular solids are three-dimensional shapes that have all faces the same size and shape, and all angles the same. Examples of right regular solids include cubes, pyramids, and prisms.
* Auxiliary views are used to show the true shape of sections such as prisms. These views are created by rotating the object around an axis and then drawing the resulting shape.
* In order to draw a prism in the true shape, several steps must be taken. First, the base and top of the prism must be drawn. Then, the sides of the prism must be drawn using the auxiliary views. Finally, the edges of the prism must be drawn using the auxiliary views.
* In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces focuses on the use of auxiliary views to draw the true shape of sections such as prisms.
