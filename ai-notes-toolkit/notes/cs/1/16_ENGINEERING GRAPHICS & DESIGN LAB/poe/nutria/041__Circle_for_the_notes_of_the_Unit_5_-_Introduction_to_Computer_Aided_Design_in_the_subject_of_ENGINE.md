
### Circle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

* A circle is a two-dimensional shape with a curved line that forms a closed loop.
* It is a regular polygon with an infinite number of sides and is one of the most basic shapes in geometry. 
* A circle can be defined by its center point and radius or by its diameter.
* The circumference of a circle is the length of the outside of the circle and is calculated using the formula `c = 2 * pi * r`, where `r` is the radius of the circle and `pi` is the mathematical constant.
* The area of a circle is the amount of space inside the circle and is calculated using the formula `A = pi * r^2`, where `r` is the radius of the circle and `pi` is the mathematical constant.
* In Computer Aided Design (CAD), circles are used to create shapes and objects. CAD software can be used to draw circles and manipulate them in a variety of ways, such as scaling, rotating, and stretching.
