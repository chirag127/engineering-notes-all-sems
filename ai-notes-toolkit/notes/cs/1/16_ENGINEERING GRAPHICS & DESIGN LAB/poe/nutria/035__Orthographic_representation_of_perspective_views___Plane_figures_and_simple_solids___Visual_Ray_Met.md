
### Orthographic representation of perspective views — Plane figures and simple solids — Visual Ray Method

Orthographic representation of perspective views is a method of representing a three-dimensional object in two dimensions. It is used to create drawings of objects that are seen from different angles. Plane figures and simple solids are the objects that are represented in this way.

The Visual Ray Method is a specific approach to orthographic representation. It involves tracing rays from the object to the plane of projection, and then drawing the object from the perspective of these rays. This method is commonly used in engineering graphics and design lab to create drawings of isometric projections.

The following steps are used to create an isometric projection using the Visual Ray Method:

1. Draw a horizon line on the plane of projection.
2. Select three points on the object and trace three visual rays from each point to the plane of projection.
3. Connect the points on the plane of projection to create an isometric projection.
4. Draw the object from the perspective of the visual rays.

The Visual Ray Method is a useful tool for creating accurate and detailed drawings of objects from different angles. It is an essential skill for engineers and designers who need to create drawings of isometric projections.
