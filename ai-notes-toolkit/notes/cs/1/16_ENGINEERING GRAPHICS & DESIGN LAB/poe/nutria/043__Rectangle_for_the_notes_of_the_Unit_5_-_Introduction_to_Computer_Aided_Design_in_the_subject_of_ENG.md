
### Rectangle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. A rectangle is a two-dimensional geometric shape with four sides and four angles. 
2. Rectangles can be drawn in either a horizontal or vertical orientation. 
3. The length of the sides of a rectangle is always equal. 
4. The angles of a rectangle are always 90 degrees.
5. In Computer Aided Design (CAD), rectangles are used to create objects such as windows, doors and walls. 
6. CAD rectangles can be manipulated to create more complex shapes, such as circles and polygons. 
7. CAD rectangles can also be used to create patterns and textures. 
8. CAD rectangles can be used to create precise measurements and dimensions for objects.
