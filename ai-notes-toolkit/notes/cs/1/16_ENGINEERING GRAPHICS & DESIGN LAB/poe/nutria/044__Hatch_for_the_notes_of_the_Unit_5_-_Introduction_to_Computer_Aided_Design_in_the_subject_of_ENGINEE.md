
### Hatch for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

* Hatch is a powerful tool in Computer Aided Design (CAD) that allows a user to fill an area with a pattern. 
* The pattern can be a variety of lines, shapes, or textures that can be used to represent different materials or objects.
* Hatch patterns can be used to create a variety of effects, from shading a drawing to representing a material or texture.
* Hatch patterns can be customized with different line types, line weights, and colors.
* The angle of the hatch pattern can also be adjusted to create different effects.
* In Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, hatch patterns are used to represent different materials or objects, such as wood, metal, concrete, and glass. 
* Hatch patterns can also be used to represent different textures, such as smooth, rough, and textured. 
* The use of hatch patterns can make a drawing more realistic and help to convey information about the materials or objects represented in a drawing.
