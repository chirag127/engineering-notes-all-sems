
### Sections of Solids

- A solid is a three-dimensional object that has length, breadth and height. 
- Regular solids are those whose faces are all the same shape and size. 
- The six regular solids are the cube, tetrahedron, octahedron, dodecahedron, icosahedron and prism. 
- The development of a surface is the process of drawing the surface of a solid on a plane. 
- The two methods used for developing surfaces are the orthographic projection method and the oblique projection method. 
- The orthographic projection method is used to draw the front, top, side and bottom views of a solid. 
- The oblique projection method is used to draw the oblique view of a solid.
