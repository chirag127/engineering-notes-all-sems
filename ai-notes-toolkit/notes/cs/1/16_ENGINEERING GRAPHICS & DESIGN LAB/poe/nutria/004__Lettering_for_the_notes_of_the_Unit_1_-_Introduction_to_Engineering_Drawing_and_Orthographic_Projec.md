
### Lettering for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Lettering is the process of writing letters, numbers, symbols, and other characters on a drawing.
2. In engineering drawings, lettering should be neat, legible, and consistent in size, style, and orientation.
3. Lettering should be done with a drafting pencil and ruler, and should be placed in a specific location on the drawing.
4. The size of the lettering should be between 0.5mm and 3.5mm.
5. The orientation of the lettering should be horizontal, vertical, or inclined at a 45-degree angle.
6. The lettering should be placed in the title block or in the drawing area.
7. The lettering should be done in block letters and should not be connected.
8. The lettering should be done in capital letters, except for the first letter of a sentence, which should be in a capital letter.
9. The lettering should be placed in the order of the lines and points on the drawing.
10. The lettering should be done in the same style throughout the drawing.
