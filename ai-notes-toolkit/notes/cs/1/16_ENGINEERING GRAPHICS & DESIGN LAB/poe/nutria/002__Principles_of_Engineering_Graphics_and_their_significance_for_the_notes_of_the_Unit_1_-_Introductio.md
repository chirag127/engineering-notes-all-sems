
### Principles of Engineering Graphics and their significance for the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections 

1. Engineering graphics are a set of principles used to represent and communicate ideas and information in the engineering field. 
2. It is an essential tool for engineers to understand and visualize complex systems and problems. 
3. Orthographic projections are a type of engineering graphics that are used to represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
4. Engineering graphics are used to create technical drawings that are used in the design, manufacture and assembly of products.
5. They are used to convey information about the design, dimensions, materials and processes required to produce the product.
6. Engineering graphics are also used to create diagrams and schematics that are used to explain the operation of a system or process. 
7. The principles of engineering graphics are used to create drawings that are accurate, clear and concise. 
8. These principles include the use of conventions, such as lines, symbols and labels, to represent different components of a system. 
9. The use of standards and conventions ensures that all drawings are consistent and can be easily understood by engineers. 
10. The principles of engineering graphics are essential for the successful completion of engineering projects.
