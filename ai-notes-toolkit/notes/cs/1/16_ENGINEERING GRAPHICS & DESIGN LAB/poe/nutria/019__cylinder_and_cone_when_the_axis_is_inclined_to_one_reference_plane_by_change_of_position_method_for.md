
### Cylinder and Cone when Axis is Inclined to One Reference Plane by Change of Position Method

* When the axis of a cylinder or cone is inclined to one reference plane, the projection of the figure can be obtained by the change of position method.
* This method involves rotating the figure about its axis until it is in the desired position of projection.
* The figure should then be projected in the required direction.
* The projection of the inclined cylinder or cone is made up of two parts, the inclined surface and the end view.
* The end view is obtained by projecting the end circle of the cylinder or cone in the direction perpendicular to the reference plane.
* The inclined surface is obtained by projecting the curved surface of the cylinder or cone in the direction perpendicular to the axis of the figure.
* The projection of the inclined surface is obtained by first drawing the true shape of the inclined surface and then projecting it in the required direction.
