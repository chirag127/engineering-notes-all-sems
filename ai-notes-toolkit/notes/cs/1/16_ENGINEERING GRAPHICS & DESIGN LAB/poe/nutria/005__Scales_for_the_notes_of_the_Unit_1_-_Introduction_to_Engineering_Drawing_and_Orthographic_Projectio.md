2. The most commonly used scales are the English and Metric scales.
3. The English scale is typically used for measurements in feet and inches, while the Metric scale is used for measurements in meters and centimeters. 
4. It is important to use the correct scale when creating a drawing, as this will ensure that the measurements are accurate.
5. Orthographic projections are drawings that show the exact shape and size of an object from different angles. 
6. These projections are used to create detailed drawings of objects, which can then be used to create physical models or used as a reference for further designs. 
7. In order to create an accurate orthographic projection, the scale must be chosen carefully. 
8. The scale should be large enough to show the details of the object, but small enough to fit the entire object on the page.
