
### Projection of Lines

1. A projection of a line is a line drawn on a plane which is parallel to the line of sight.
2. The two most common types of projections of lines are orthographic and oblique.
3. Orthographic projection is the most common type of projection used in engineering drawings. It is used to represent objects in three dimensions on a two-dimensional plane.
4. In orthographic projection, the line of sight is perpendicular to the plane of projection.
5. In oblique projection, the line of sight is not perpendicular to the plane of projection.
6. In engineering graphics, the most common type of oblique projection is Cavalier projection.
7. In Cavalier projection, the line of sight is inclined at an angle of 45° to the plane of projection.
8. The lines in an orthographic projection are drawn parallel to the plane of projection.
9. The lines in an oblique projection are drawn at an angle to the plane of projection.
10. The lines in a Cavalier projection are drawn at an angle of 45° to the plane of projection.
