
### Projection of Lines Inclined to One Plane and Both Planes

1. Lines inclined to one plane are those that make an angle other than 90° with the plane.
2. Lines inclined to both planes are those that make an angle other than 90° with both the planes.
3. The projection of such lines can be determined by the following steps:
    * Step 1: Draw the true size of the inclined line in the appropriate plane of projection.
    * Step 2: Mark the angle of inclination with reference to the plane of projection.
    * Step 3: Determine the direction of projection.
    * Step 4: Draw the projection of the line in the plane of projection.
4. The projection of a line inclined to both planes is obtained by projecting the line in both the planes of projection.
5. The projection of the inclined line can be obtained by the following steps:
    * Step 1: Draw the true size of the inclined line in the appropriate plane of projection.
    * Step 2: Mark the angle of inclination with reference to the plane of projection.
    * Step 3: Determine the direction of projection in both the planes.
    * Step 4: Draw the projection of the line in both the planes of projection.
