### Pyramids

1. A pyramid is a polyhedron formed by connecting a polygonal base and a point, called the apex.
2. The slant height of a pyramid is the distance between the apex and the base.
3. The height of a pyramid is the length of a line segment from the apex perpendicular to the base.
4. The lateral surface of a pyramid consists of triangles with a common base. The area of the lateral surface is equal to (1/2)bh, where b is the length of the base and h is the slant height.
5. The volume of a pyramid is (1/3)Ah, where A is the area of the base and h is the height of the pyramid.
6. Types of pyramids:
- Triangular pyramid: Base is a triangle, apex is a point.
- Square pyramid: Base is a square, apex is a point.
- Pentagonal pyramid: Base is a pentagon, apex is a point.

7. To draw the projections of a pyramid:
- Draw the plan and front view of the base.
- Project the apex onto the vertical and horizontal planes.
- Join the projections of the apex to the corresponding corners of the base.
- The lines joining the apex to the base represent the slant heights of the faces of the pyramid.
