### Cylinder

1. A cylinder is a three-dimensional solid shape with two parallel bases that are circles.
2. The surface of a cylinder is made up of two circular bases and one rectangular surface that wraps around and connects the edges of the two bases.
3. To draw a cylinder:
 - Draw two concentric circles to represent the bases
 - Draw two parallel lines to represent the height of the cylinder
 - Connect the outer edges of the circles with straight lines to create the side surface
4. To find the surface area of a cylinder:
 - Calculate the area of one circular base
 - Calculate the area of the side surface (perimeter x height)
 - Add the area of the base and side surface
5. To find the volume of a cylinder:
 - Calculate the area of one circular base
 - Multiply the area of the base by the height of the cylinder
