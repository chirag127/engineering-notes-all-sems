### Development of surfaces for various regular solids such as Prism for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Prism:
    - A prism is a polyhedron comprising two parallel faces and other faces joining them.
    - The parallel faces are known as bases and other faces are known as lateral faces.
    - **Development of lateral surface:**
        - The lateral surface can be developed into a rectangle by wrapping a sheet around the prism.
        - The dimensions of the developed lateral surface are:
            - Length = Perimeter of base
            - Breadth = Height of prism

2. Cube:
    - A cube is a regular solid with 6 equal square faces.
    - **Development of surface:**
        - The surface of a cube can be developed into a square sheet by unfolding along one edge.
        - The dimensions of the developed surface are:
            - Side of square = Side of cube

3. Cylinder:
    - A cylinder is a surface generated by revolving a rectangle about one of its sides.
    - It has circular bases and a curved lateral surface.
    - **Development of lateral surface:**
        - The lateral surface can be developed into a rectangle by wrapping a sheet around the cylinder.
        - The dimensions of the developed lateral surface are:
            - Length = Circumference of base
            - Breadth = Height of cylinder
