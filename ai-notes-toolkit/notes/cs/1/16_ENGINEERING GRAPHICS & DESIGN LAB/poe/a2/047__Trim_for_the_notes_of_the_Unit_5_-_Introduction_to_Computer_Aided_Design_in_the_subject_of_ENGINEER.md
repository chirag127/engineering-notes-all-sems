### Trim

1. Trim is a command used to remove the unwanted portions of an object.
2. It is used to create precise shapes by removing the unwanted lines or portions.
3. The trim command can trim off the endpoints or portions of multiple intersecting lines.
4. To trim a line, select the trim command and then select the lines to trim and press enter.
5. The portion of the line which is to be removed will be highlighted. Then press enter to remove the highlighted portion.
6. While trimming, the cutting line should intersect the line which is to be trimmed.
7. The trim command works only on 2D lines and cannot be used to trim 3D objects.
8. The trimmed line will merge with the cutting or boundary line.
9. The trim options like 'Select objects' and 'Select boundary objects' can be used to select the objects to trim and boundary lines respectively.
