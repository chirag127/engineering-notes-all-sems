### Projection of Lines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Lines:
- Straight lines: Lines having no change in direction. Represented by arrowheads.
- Broken lines: Lines consisting of dashes and gaps. Used to indicate hidden detail or construction lines.
- Dimension lines: Thin continuous lines. Used to indicate measurements.
- Centre lines: Thin continuous lines. Used to indicate symmetry or centre of circular features.
- Extension lines: Thin continuous lines extending out from the drawing. Used to indicate dimensions.

2. Projections:
- Plan: Top view projection onto a horizontal plane.
- Elevation: Side view projection onto a vertical plane.
- Section: View showing an internal feature of an object.

3. Viewing planes:
- Horizontal plane (HP): Parallel to the ground. Plan view projected onto it.
- Vertical plane (VP): Perpendicular to the HP. Elevation view projected onto it.
- Profile plane (PP): Perpendicular to the object's side. Used to obtain a true shape of an inclined surface.

4. Projection rules:
- A line/point is projected as a line/point.
- A straight line remains straight after projection.
- The ratio of dimensions is maintained after projection.
- The angle between two lines is maintained after projection.
