### Pyramid

1. A pyramid is a polyhedron formed by connecting a polygonal base and a point, called the apex.
2. The slant height of a pyramid is the distance between the apex and the base.
3. The lateral faces of a pyramid are triangles with a common base.
4. The apex of a pyramid lies on the perpendicular bisector of the base.
5. The triangular pyramid has an equilateral triangle as base and a vertex that is the midpoint of the base.
6. The square pyramid and pentagonal pyramid have a square and regular pentagon respectively as base.
7. To draw the development of a pyramid:
    - Draw the base of the pyramid.
    - Draw slant height of the pyramid.
    - Divide the base into equal parts.
    - Join the apex to the division points on the base.
    - The area occupied inside the figure gives the development of the pyramid.
