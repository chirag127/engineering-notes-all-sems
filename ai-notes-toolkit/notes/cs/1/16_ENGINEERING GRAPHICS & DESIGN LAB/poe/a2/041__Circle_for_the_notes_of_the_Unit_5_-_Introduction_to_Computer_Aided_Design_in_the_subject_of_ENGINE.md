### Circle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Circle:
- A circle is a two-dimensional shape consisting of all points in a plane that are equidistant from a given point, the centre.
- The diameter of a circle is the distance across the circle passing through the centre.
- The radius of a circle is the distance from the centre out to the edge of the circle.
- Circumference: The distance around the outside of the circle. Calculated using the formula: 2*pi*r, where r is the radius.

2. Parts of a circle:
- Centre: The central point of the circle.
- Radius: The distance from the centre to the edge of the circle.
- Diameter: The distance across the circle passing through the centre. Diameter = 2 x Radius.
- Arc: A section of the circumference of the circle.
- Chord: A line segment whose endpoints lie on the circle.
- Secant: A line that intersects a circle at two points.
- Tangent: A line that touches a circle at only one point.

3. Computer Aided Design (CAD):
- CAD is the use of computer software to create technical drawings of physical products.
- CAD software replaces manual drafting techniques.
- The main advantages of CAD over manual drafting are:
-- Increased productivity.
-- Superior quality and accuracy of drawings.
-- Easier modification and revision of drawings.
-- Easier data sharing between teams and departments.
-- Easier generation of multiple views and sections of 3D models.
