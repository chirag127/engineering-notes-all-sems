### Scales for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Engineering drawings are drawn to scale to represent the exact shape and size of the object. 
2. The scale is the ratio of the dimension on the drawing to the actual dimension of the object. 
3. For example, if a line on the drawing has a length of 6cm and represents an actual length of 60cm, then the scale of the drawing is 1:10. 
4. The scales commonly used in engineering drawing are:
- 1:1 - Full size scale
- 1:2
- 1:5
- 1:10
- 1:20
- 1:50
- 1:100
5. Choosing an appropriate scale:
- For large objects - smaller scales like 1:50 or 1:100
- For small objects - larger scales like 1:2 or 1:5
- Depends on the size of the paper and the purpose of the drawing
