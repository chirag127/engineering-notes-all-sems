## Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces.

1. Regular Solids:
- A regular solid is one which has equal sides and equal angles.
- There are 5 regular solids namely cube, tetrahedron, octahedron, dodecahedron and icosahedron.
- A cube has 6 equal sides and 8 vertices. A tetrahedron has 4 triangular faces. An octahedron has 8 faces. A dodecahedron has 12 pentagonal faces and an icosahedron has 20 triangular faces.

2. Sections of Regular Solids:
- A section is the intersection of a solid with a plane.
- The sections of a cube by a plane parallel to its face are squares. The sections by a plane intersecting 2 opposite edges are rectangles.
- The sections of a tetrahedron by a plane are triangles.
- The sections of an octahedron by a plane are squares or triangles.
- The sections of a dodecahedron and icosahedron are pentagons or triangles.

3. Development of surfaces:
- The development of a surface is the unfolding of a 3D shape into a 2D plane without any overlaps.
- Only regular solids made of identical faces can be developed. Irregular solids cannot be developed.
- The development of a cube results in a square. The development of a tetrahedron results in 4 isosceles triangles. The development of an octahedron results in 2 squares and 4 isosceles triangles.
