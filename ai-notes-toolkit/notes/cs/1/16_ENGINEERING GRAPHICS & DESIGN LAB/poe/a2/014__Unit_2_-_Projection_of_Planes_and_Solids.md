## Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids

1. Projection of a point: The projection of a point on a plane is the foot of the perpendicular drawn from the point to the plane.
2. Projection of a line: The projection of a line on a plane is the line obtained by projecting each point of the given line on the plane.
3. Types of projections: The two main types of projections are:
(a) Orthographic projection: It is a parallel projection in which the projecting lines are perpendicular to the projecting plane.
(b) Perspective projection: It is a projection in which the projecting lines converge to a point called the centre of projection.
4. Projection of solids: The projection of a solid on a plane is the figure obtained by projecting all the points of the solid on the plane. The projection will depend on the relative position of the solid and the plane. The different cases are:
(a) When the plane is parallel to one face of the solid
(b) When the plane intersects the solid
(c) When the solid is inclined to the plane
