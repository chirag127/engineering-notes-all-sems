## Unit 4 - Isometric Projection

Isometric projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects on a two-dimensional surface. It is a type of axonometric projection in which the three axes are equally foreshortened and the angles between any two of them are 120 degrees. Isometric projection is widely used in engineering drawings, architectural drawings, and other technical illustrations.

In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to isometric projection:

1. Introduction to Isometric Projection
    - Definition and history
    - Advantages and disadvantages
    - Types of axonometric projections

2. Isometric Projection Principles
    - Orthographic projection and its relationship to isometric projection
    - Isometric axes and planes
    - Isometric scale and its application

3. Isometric Drawing Techniques
    - Isometric drawing tools and materials
    - Isometric drawing conventions
    - Isometric drawing exercises and examples

4. Isometric Projection Applications
    - Isometric projection in engineering and architectural drawings
    - Isometric projection in technical illustrations and diagrams
    - Isometric projection in computer graphics and animation

5. Isometric Projection Standards
    - ISO and ANSI standards for isometric projection
    - Dimensioning and labeling in isometric drawings
    - Common errors and mistakes in isometric projection

In conclusion, isometric projection is an essential tool for technical drawing and visualization of three-dimensional objects. This unit will provide you with the fundamental principles, techniques, applications, and standards of isometric projection that will enable you to create accurate and professional isometric drawings.
