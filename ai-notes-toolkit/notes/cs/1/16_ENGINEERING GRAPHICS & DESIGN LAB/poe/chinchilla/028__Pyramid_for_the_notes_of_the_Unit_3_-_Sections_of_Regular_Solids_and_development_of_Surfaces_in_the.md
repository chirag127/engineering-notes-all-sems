### Pyramid

A pyramid is a three-dimensional geometric shape that has a base and triangular faces that converge to a single point at the top. It is a regular solid and is classified as a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces.

#### Types of Pyramids

There are various types of pyramids based on the shape of the base. Some common types are:

- Square Pyramid: A pyramid with a square base and four triangular faces.
- Rectangular Pyramid: A pyramid with a rectangular base and four triangular faces.
- Triangular Pyramid: A pyramid with a triangular base and three triangular faces.
- Pentagonal Pyramid: A pyramid with a pentagonal base and five triangular faces.

#### Sections of Pyramid

Pyramids can be sectioned to reveal their internal structure. The most common sections are:

- Horizontal Section: A section made by a plane that is parallel to the base of the pyramid.
- Vertical Section: A section made by a plane that passes through the apex of the pyramid and is perpendicular to the base.

#### Development of Pyramid Surfaces

The development of a pyramid surface involves the process of unfolding the surface of the pyramid onto a flat surface. The development can be done using the following steps:

1. Draw the base of the pyramid and mark the apex.
2. Draw the triangular faces of the pyramid by connecting the apex to the vertices of the base.
3. Cut the triangular faces along their edges and unfold them onto a flat surface.
4. The unfolded triangles will form a two-dimensional shape that represents the development of the pyramid surface.

#### Applications of Pyramid

Pyramids are commonly used in architecture and construction as a decorative element or as a structural component. In addition, they are also used in mathematics and geometry to teach concepts related to regular solids and three-dimensional shapes.
