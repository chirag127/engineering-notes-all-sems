### Cylinder and Cone When the Axis is Inclined to One Reference Plane by Change of Position Method

In Engineering Graphics and Design Lab, the projection of planes and solids is an important topic to learn. In this unit, we will focus on the cylinder and cone when the axis is inclined to one reference plane by change of position method. Here are some important points to remember:

#### Cylinder:
- When the axis of the cylinder is inclined to one reference plane, the projection of the cylinder would be an ellipse on that plane.
- The direction of the axis must be indicated with the help of an arrow.
- The ellipse would be a true size projection of the base of the cylinder.
- The height of the cylinder would be projected as a line perpendicular to the base ellipse.
- The length of the line would be equal to the height of the cylinder.
- The line would be inclined to the plane of projection at the same angle as that of the axis of the cylinder.

#### Cone:
- When the axis of the cone is inclined to one reference plane, the projection of the cone would be an ellipse on that plane.
- The direction of the axis must be indicated with the help of an arrow.
- The major axis of the ellipse would be equal to the diameter of the base of the cone.
- The minor axis of the ellipse would be equal to the slant height of the cone.
- The height of the cone would be projected as a line perpendicular to the base ellipse.
- The length of the line would be equal to the height of the cone.
- The line would be inclined to the plane of projection at the same angle as that of the axis of the cone.

By understanding the above points, one can easily project the cylinder and cone when the axis is inclined to one reference plane by change of position method. It is important to practice these projections to get a better understanding of the topic.
