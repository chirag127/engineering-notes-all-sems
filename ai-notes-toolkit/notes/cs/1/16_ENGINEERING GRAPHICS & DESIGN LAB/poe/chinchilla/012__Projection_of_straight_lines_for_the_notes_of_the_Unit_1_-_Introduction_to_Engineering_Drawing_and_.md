### Projection of Straight Lines

In Engineering Graphics and Design Lab, it is important to understand the concept of Projection of Straight Lines. This topic is covered in the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections. Here are some key points to remember:

- A straight line can be projected onto a plane by drawing a perpendicular line from each point on the line to the plane.
- The projection of the line on the plane is the set of all the perpendicular lines from the points on the line to the plane.
- The projection of a straight line can be either parallel or inclined to the plane.
- In the case of a parallel projection, the projection of the line on the plane is a line that is parallel to the original line.
- In the case of an inclined projection, the projection of the line on the plane is a line that is inclined to the original line.
- The projection of a line can be drawn using either the First Angle Projection method or the Third Angle Projection method.
- In First Angle Projection, the object is placed in the first quadrant and the observer is located in the third quadrant. In Third Angle Projection, the object is placed in the third quadrant and the observer is located in the first quadrant.
- The projection of a line can also be drawn using the Isometric Projection method, which is a 3D projection method that shows the object from three different angles.
- To draw the projection of a line using Isometric Projection, first draw the isometric axes and then project the line onto the isometric plane.
