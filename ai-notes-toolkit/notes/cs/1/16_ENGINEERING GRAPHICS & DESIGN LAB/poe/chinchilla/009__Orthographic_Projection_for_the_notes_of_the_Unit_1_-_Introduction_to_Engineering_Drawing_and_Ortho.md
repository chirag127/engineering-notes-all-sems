### Orthographic Projection for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Orthographic Projection is a technique used in engineering drawing to create a 2D representation of a 3D object. It is an essential skill for engineers, architects, and designers to communicate their ideas and designs effectively. Here are some important points to keep in mind while learning about Orthographic Projection:

1. Orthographic Projection is a method of creating 2D drawings of 3D objects.
2. It is also known as multiview projection or engineering projection.
3. In Orthographic Projection, the object is viewed from different angles and projected onto a 2D plane.
4. The different views of the object are usually drawn on separate planes, such as the front, top, and side views.
5. The views are drawn with parallel lines, which means that there is no perspective and the object appears flat.
6. Orthographic Projection is used to show the exact shape and size of each part of an object.
7. It is often used in technical drawings, such as architectural plans, engineering diagrams, and machine designs.
8. There are two types of Orthographic Projection: First Angle Projection and Third Angle Projection.
9. In First Angle Projection, the object is placed in the first quadrant of the projection plane, while in Third Angle Projection, the object is placed in the third quadrant.
10. It is important to understand the projection symbol used in each type of projection to avoid confusion.
11. Orthographic Projection also requires the use of projection lines, dimension lines, and section lines to accurately represent the object.
12. The projection lines show the direction of projection, while dimension lines indicate the size of the object.
13. Section lines are used to show the internal structure of the object.
14. Orthographic Projection requires a good understanding of geometry and spatial relationships.
15. It is essential to practice drawing different views of objects using Orthographic Projection to develop the skill.

Learning Orthographic Projection is an important part of engineering drawing and design. By mastering this technique, engineers, architects, and designers can accurately communicate their ideas and designs to others. It is a fundamental skill that is used in a wide range of industries and applications.
