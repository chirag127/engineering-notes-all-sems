## Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids

In this unit, we will be studying about the projection of planes and solids. This is an important topic in engineering and mathematics, and is used extensively in fields such as architecture, construction, and design. In this unit, we will be covering the following topics:

1. Introduction to Projection of Planes and Solids
   * Definition of Projection
   * Types of Projections
   * Orthographic Projection
   * Isometric Projection

2. Projection of Planes
   * Definition of Plane
   * Basic Geometrical Constructions
   * Orthographic Projection of Planes
   * Isometric Projection of Planes

3. Projection of Solids
   * Definition of Solid
   * Basic Geometrical Constructions
   * Orthographic Projection of Solids
   * Isometric Projection of Solids

4. Sectional Views
   * Definition of Sectional Views
   * Types of Sectional Views
   * Orthographic Sectional Views
   * Isometric Sectional Views

5. Development of Surfaces
   * Definition of Development of Surfaces
   * Basic Geometrical Constructions
   * Orthographic Development of Surfaces
   * Isometric Development of Surfaces

6. Applications of Projection of Planes and Solids
   * Architecture and Design
   * Engineering and Manufacturing
   * Construction and Surveying
   * Animation and Special Effects

It is important to understand the concepts and principles covered in this unit thoroughly, as they form the basis for many advanced topics in engineering and mathematics. Practice problems and exercises are also provided to reinforce your understanding of the material. By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

* Understand the concept of projection and its applications
* Apply basic geometrical constructions to project planes and solids
* Create orthographic and isometric projections of planes and solids
* Understand sectional views and their types
* Create orthographic and isometric sectional views
* Understand the concept of development of surfaces and apply it to orthographic and isometric projections
* Apply the principles of projection of planes and solids to real-world applications.
