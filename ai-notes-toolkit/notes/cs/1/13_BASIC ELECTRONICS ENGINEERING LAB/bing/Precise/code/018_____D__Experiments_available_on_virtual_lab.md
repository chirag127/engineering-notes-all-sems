## (D) Experiments available on virtual lab

Virtual labs provide a platform for students to conduct experiments and simulations online. There are a variety of virtual labs available that cater to different subjects and learning styles. Here are some examples of virtual labs and the experiments they offer:

1. **Virtual Chemistry Experiments** - A collection of interactive web-based chemistry tutorials .
2. **PraxiLabs** - Offers virtual experiments that adapt to different learning styles, including progress tracking, walkthrough videos, lab manuals, enrichment documents, immediate feedback, MCQ quizzes, and more .
3. **VRLab Academy** - Offers more than 200 science experiments available on VR and PC, designed to increase the ability to learn through virtual memory .
4. **American Chemical Society** - Offers virtual chemistry experiments and simulations, including the exploration of the chemistry of aspirin, average atomic mass, solubility, and freezing point depression .
5. **National Science Digital Library: ChemCollective Virtual Labs** - Offers a deep dive into chemistry with virtual labs .
6. **PBS: Nova Labs** - A platform for teens and older learners to explore and examine cutting-edge issues .
7. **Mystery Science** - Offers a wealth of virtual science labs, activities, and experiments for students in elementary school, covering topics such as weather, astronomy, humans, and animals .
8. **Molecular Workbench** - Offers virtual lab activities for the classroom .

These are just a few examples of the many virtual labs available. Students can choose the virtual lab that best suits their needs and learning style.
