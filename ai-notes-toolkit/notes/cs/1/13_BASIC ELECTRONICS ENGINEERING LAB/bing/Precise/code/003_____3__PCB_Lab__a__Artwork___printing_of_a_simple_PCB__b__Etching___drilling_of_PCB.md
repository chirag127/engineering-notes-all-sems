### 3. PCB Lab: 
a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB:
- PCB stands for Printed Circuit Board.
- The first step in creating a PCB is to design the artwork.
- This can be done using a software program such as Eagle or Altium.
- The artwork is then printed onto a transparent film using a laser printer.
- This film is used as a mask during the etching process.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:
- The etching process removes unwanted copper from the PCB.
- This is done by applying an etchant solution to the board.
- The etchant reacts with the copper, dissolving it and leaving behind the desired circuit pattern.
- After etching, the PCB is drilled to create holes for components to be mounted.
- The drilling is done using a CNC machine or a drill press.
