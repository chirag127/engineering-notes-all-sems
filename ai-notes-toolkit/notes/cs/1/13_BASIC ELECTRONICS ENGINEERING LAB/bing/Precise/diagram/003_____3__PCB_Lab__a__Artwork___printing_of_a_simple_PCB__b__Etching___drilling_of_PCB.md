### 3. PCB Lab:
a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB:
- PCB stands for Printed Circuit Board.
- The first step in creating a PCB is to design the artwork.
- The artwork is a representation of the desired circuit connections.
- The artwork can be created using a software program or by hand.
- Once the artwork is complete, it is printed onto a transparent film.
- The film is then used to transfer the circuit pattern onto the PCB material.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:
- Etching is the process of removing unwanted copper from the PCB material.
- This is done by applying an etchant solution to the PCB.
- The etchant solution dissolves the copper, leaving only the desired circuit pattern.
- After etching, the PCB is rinsed and dried.
- Drilling is the process of creating holes in the PCB for component leads and vias.
- A drill press or CNC machine is used to drill the holes in the PCB.
- The PCB is then ready for component placement and soldering.
