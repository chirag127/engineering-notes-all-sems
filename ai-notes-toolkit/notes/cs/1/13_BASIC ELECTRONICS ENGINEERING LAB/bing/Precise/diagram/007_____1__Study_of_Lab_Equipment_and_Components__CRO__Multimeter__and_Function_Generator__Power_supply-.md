### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- **CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope)**: A CRO is an electronic device that displays electrical signals on a screen. It is used to measure the voltage, frequency, and phase of an electrical signal.
- **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a device that measures electrical quantities such as voltage, current, and resistance. It can be used to troubleshoot electrical circuits and test batteries.
- **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that produces different types of electrical waveforms such as sine, square, and triangle waves. It is used to test and calibrate electronic equipment.
- **Power Supply**: A power supply is a device that converts electrical energy from one form to another. It is used to provide a stable and constant source of power to electronic devices.
- **Active Components**: Active components are electronic components that require an external source of power to operate. Examples include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.
- **Passive Components**: Passive components are electronic components that do not require an external source of power to operate. Examples include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.
- **Bread Board**: A breadboard is a device used to build and test electronic circuits. It allows the user to easily connect and disconnect components without the need for soldering.
