### Part B Study of Lab Equipment’s and Components: CRO, Multi meter, Function Generator, Power supplyActive, Passive Components and Bread Board

1. **CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope):** A CRO is an electronic test instrument that displays electrical signals graphically, usually as a voltage waveform. It is used to observe the change of an electrical signal over time, such as voltage or current.
2. **Multimeter:** A multimeter is a handheld device that combines several measurement functions in one unit. It can be used to measure voltage, current, and resistance.
3. **Function Generator:** A function generator is a type of electronic test equipment that produces various types of electrical waveforms over a wide range of frequencies. It is used to test the response of circuits to common input signals.
4. **Power Supply:** A power supply is an electronic device that supplies electric power to an electrical load. It converts one form of electrical energy to another and regulates the voltage and current of the output power.
5. **Active Components:** Active components are electronic components that require a source of energy to operate, usually in the form of a DC voltage. Examples include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.
6. **Passive Components:** Passive components are electronic components that do not require a source of energy to operate. Examples include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.
7. **Breadboard:** A breadboard is a construction base for prototyping of electronics. It allows the user to create temporary circuits and test them before making permanent connections.
