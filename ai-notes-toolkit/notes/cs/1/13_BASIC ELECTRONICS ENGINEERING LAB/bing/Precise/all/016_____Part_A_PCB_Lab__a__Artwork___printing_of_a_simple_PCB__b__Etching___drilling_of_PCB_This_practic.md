### Part A PCB Lab:

a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB:
1. The first step in creating a PCB is to design the artwork.
2. This can be done using a computer-aided design (CAD) program.
3. The artwork is then printed onto a transparent film using a laser printer.
4. The film is used to create a photomask, which is used to transfer the design onto the PCB.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:
1. The PCB is then etched to remove the unwanted copper, leaving only the desired circuit pattern.
2. This is done by immersing the PCB in an etching solution, such as ferric chloride or ammonium persulfate.
3. After etching, the PCB is rinsed and dried.
4. The next step is to drill holes for the components.
5. This is done using a drill press or a CNC machine.
