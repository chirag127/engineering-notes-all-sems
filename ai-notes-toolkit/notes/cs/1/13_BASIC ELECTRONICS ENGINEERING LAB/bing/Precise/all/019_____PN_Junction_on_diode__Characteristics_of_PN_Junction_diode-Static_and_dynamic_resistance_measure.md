### PN Junction on diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode-Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph

A PN junction diode is a two-terminal electronic device that allows the flow of current in only one direction. It is formed by joining a p-type semiconductor material with an n-type semiconductor material.

#### Static Resistance

Static resistance of a diode is the ratio of the DC voltage applied across the diode to the DC current flowing through it. It can be calculated using Ohm's law, which states that the resistance is equal to the voltage divided by the current.

#### Dynamic Resistance

Dynamic resistance of a diode is the ratio of the small change in voltage to the corresponding small change in current at a particular operating point. It can be calculated by taking the derivative of the voltage with respect to the current.

#### Measurement from Graph

The static and dynamic resistance of a diode can be measured from its current-voltage (I-V) characteristic graph. The static resistance can be calculated by taking the ratio of the voltage to the current at a particular operating point. The dynamic resistance can be calculated by taking the slope of the tangent to the I-V curve at the operating point.
