### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- **CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope)**: A CRO is an electronic device that displays electrical signals on a screen. It is used to measure and analyze waveforms of varying frequencies and amplitudes.

- **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a device that measures electrical quantities such as voltage, current, and resistance. It can be used to troubleshoot electrical circuits and test batteries, among other things.

- **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that produces various types of electrical waveforms over a range of frequencies. It is used to test and calibrate other electronic devices.

- **Power Supply**: A power supply is a device that converts electrical energy from one form to another. It is used to provide a stable and regulated source of power to electronic devices.

- **Active Components**: Active components are electronic components that require a source of energy to operate. Examples include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

- **Passive Components**: Passive components are electronic components that do not require a source of energy to operate. Examples include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

- **Bread Board**: A breadboard is a device used to prototype electronic circuits. It allows for the easy and temporary connection of electronic components without the need for soldering.

These are some of the basic lab equipment and components that are commonly used in electronics laboratories. It is important to have a good understanding of their functions and uses in order to effectively design and test electronic circuits.
