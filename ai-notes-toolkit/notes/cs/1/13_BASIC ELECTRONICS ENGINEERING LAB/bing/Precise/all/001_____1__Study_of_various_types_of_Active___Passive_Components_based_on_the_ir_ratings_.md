### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings

Active and passive components are the two main types of electronic components. They are distinguished by their ability to amplify signals or control the flow of current.

- **Active Components:** These components rely on a source of energy and can control the flow of current. They are capable of amplifying signals and are commonly used in electronic circuits. Examples of active components include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

- **Passive Components:** These components do not require a source of energy to operate and cannot amplify signals. They are used to store, filter, or regulate the flow of current in a circuit. Examples of passive components include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

The ratings of these components are important as they determine their performance and suitability for use in a particular circuit. Some common ratings for active and passive components include:

- **Power Rating:** This refers to the maximum amount of power that a component can handle without being damaged. It is typically measured in watts.

- **Voltage Rating:** This refers to the maximum voltage that a component can withstand without being damaged. It is typically measured in volts.

- **Current Rating:** This refers to the maximum current that a component can handle without being damaged. It is typically measured in amperes.

- **Resistance Rating:** This refers to the resistance of a component, typically measured in ohms. It is an important rating for passive components such as resistors.

- **Capacitance Rating:** This refers to the capacitance of a component, typically measured in farads. It is an important rating for passive components such as capacitors.

- **Inductance Rating:** This refers to the inductance of a component, typically measured in henries. It is an important rating for passive components such as inductors.

It is important to carefully consider the ratings of active and passive components when designing and building electronic circuits to ensure that they operate safely and effectively.
