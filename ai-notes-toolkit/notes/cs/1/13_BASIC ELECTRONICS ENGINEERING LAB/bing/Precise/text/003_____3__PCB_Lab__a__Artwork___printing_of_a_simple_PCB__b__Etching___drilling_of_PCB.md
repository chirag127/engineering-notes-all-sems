### 3. PCB Lab: a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB. b. Etching & drilling of PCB

PCB stands for Printed Circuit Board. It is a board that electrically connects electronic components using conductive tracks, pads, and other features etched from copper sheets laminated onto a non-conductive substrate.

a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB:
1. The first step in creating a PCB is to design the circuit and create the artwork.
2. The artwork is a representation of the desired copper pattern on the PCB.
3. The artwork can be created using a software program or by hand.
4. Once the artwork is complete, it is printed onto a transparent film or paper.
5. The printed artwork is then used to create a photomask, which is used to transfer the pattern onto the PCB.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:
1. The next step in creating a PCB is to etch the copper pattern onto the board.
2. This is done by coating the board with a photosensitive material and exposing it to UV light through the photomask.
3. The exposed areas of the photosensitive material harden, while the unexposed areas remain soft.
4. The board is then developed, which removes the soft, unexposed areas of the photosensitive material, leaving the hardened areas to protect the copper underneath.
5. The board is then etched, which removes the unprotected copper, leaving only the desired copper pattern.
6. After etching, the board is drilled to create holes for the electronic components to be mounted.
