### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings

Active and passive components are the two main types of electronic components. They are distinguished by their ability to amplify signals or control the flow of current.

- **Active Components:** These components rely on a source of energy and are capable of amplifying signals or controlling the flow of current. Examples of active components include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

- **Passive Components:** These components do not require a source of energy to operate and cannot amplify signals. They are used to store, filter, or regulate electric energy. Examples of passive components include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

The ratings of these components are based on their ability to handle power, voltage, and current. For example, a resistor's power rating indicates the maximum amount of power it can dissipate without being damaged, while its voltage rating indicates the maximum voltage it can withstand. Similarly, a capacitor's voltage rating indicates the maximum voltage it can withstand, while its capacitance value indicates the amount of electric charge it can store.

It is important to choose components with appropriate ratings for the intended application to ensure reliable and safe operation of the electronic circuit.
