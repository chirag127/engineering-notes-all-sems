### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- **CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope)**: A CRO is an electronic instrument used to display and analyze electrical signals. It displays the waveform of the signal on a screen, allowing the user to measure its amplitude, frequency, and other characteristics.

- **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a device used to measure electrical quantities such as voltage, current, and resistance. It can be either analog or digital and can have a variety of functions, including continuity testing and diode testing.

- **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that produces various types of electrical waveforms. These waveforms can be used to test and calibrate other electronic equipment. The waveforms produced by a function generator can be of various shapes, including sine, square, and triangle.

- **Power Supply**: A power supply is a device that provides electrical power to other devices. It converts the AC power from the wall outlet into DC power that can be used by electronic devices. Power supplies can be either linear or switching.

- **Active Components**: Active components are electronic components that require a source of energy to operate. Examples of active components include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

- **Passive Components**: Passive components are electronic components that do not require a source of energy to operate. Examples of passive components include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

- **Bread Board**: A breadboard is a device used to prototype electronic circuits. It allows the user to quickly and easily connect components together without the need for soldering. Breadboards are reusable and can be used to test and debug circuits before they are permanently soldered together.
