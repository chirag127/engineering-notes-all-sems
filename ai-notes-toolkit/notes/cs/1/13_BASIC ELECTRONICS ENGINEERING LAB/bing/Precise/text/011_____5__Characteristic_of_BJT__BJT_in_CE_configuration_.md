### 5. Characteristics of BJT: BJT in CE configuration

1. **Current Gain:** The current gain of a BJT in CE configuration is the ratio of the collector current to the base current. It is denoted by β.
2. **Voltage Gain:** The voltage gain of a BJT in CE configuration is the ratio of the change in output voltage to the change in input voltage. It is denoted by Av.
3. **Input Impedance:** The input impedance of a BJT in CE configuration is the ratio of the change in input voltage to the change in input current. It is denoted by Zi.
4. **Output Impedance:** The output impedance of a BJT in CE configuration is the ratio of the change in output voltage to the change in output current. It is denoted by Zo.
5. **Power Gain:** The power gain of a BJT in CE configuration is the product of the current gain and the voltage gain. It is denoted by Ap.
6. **Phase Shift:** The phase shift of a BJT in CE configuration is 180 degrees. This means that the output signal is inverted with respect to the input signal.
7. **Frequency Response:** The frequency response of a BJT in CE configuration is determined by the internal capacitances of the transistor and the external circuit components. It is typically characterized by a low-frequency cutoff, a midband gain, and a high-frequency cutoff.
