### PN Junction on diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode-Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph

A PN junction diode is a two-terminal electronic device that allows the flow of current in only one direction. It is formed by joining a p-type semiconductor material with an n-type semiconductor material.

#### Static Resistance

Static resistance of a diode is the ratio of the DC voltage across the diode to the DC current flowing through it. It can be measured by plotting the voltage-current characteristics of the diode and taking the ratio of the voltage to the current at a particular point on the graph.

#### Dynamic Resistance

Dynamic resistance of a diode is the ratio of the small change in voltage to the corresponding small change in current at a particular operating point on the voltage-current characteristics of the diode. It can be measured by drawing a tangent to the curve at the operating point and taking the reciprocal of the slope of the tangent.

#### Measurement from Graph

To measure the static and dynamic resistance of a diode from its voltage-current characteristics graph, the following steps can be followed:

1. Plot the voltage-current characteristics of the diode on a graph paper.
2. To measure the static resistance at a particular operating point, note the voltage and current values at that point and take their ratio.
3. To measure the dynamic resistance at a particular operating point, draw a tangent to the curve at that point and take the reciprocal of the slope of the tangent.
