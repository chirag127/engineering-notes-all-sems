# 3. Applications of PN Junction diode: Half & Full wave rectifier Measurement of Vrms, Vdc, and ripple factor.

- A PN junction diode is a semiconductor device that allows current to flow in one direction only. It has two terminals, anode and cathode, and a junction between p-type and n-type materials.
- A rectifier is a circuit that converts alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC). Rectifiers are used in power supplies, radio receivers, and other electronic devices that require DC voltage.
- A half-wave rectifier is a rectifier that uses only one diode and passes only one half-cycle of the AC input voltage to the output. The other half-cycle is blocked by the reverse-biased diode. The output voltage is pulsating DC, which has a lot of ripple (fluctuation).
- A full-wave rectifier is a rectifier that uses two or four diodes and passes both half-cycles of the AC input voltage to the output. The diodes are arranged in such a way that the polarity of the output voltage is always the same. The output voltage is also pulsating DC, but with less ripple than the half-wave rectifier.
- Vrms is the root mean square value of the AC input voltage. It is a measure of the effective voltage that produces the same power as the AC voltage. Vrms is equal to 0.707 times the peak voltage of the AC input.
- Vdc is the average value of the DC output voltage. It is a measure of the average power delivered by the rectifier. Vdc is equal to the area under the output voltage curve divided by the time period of the AC input.
- Ripple factor is the ratio of the root mean square value of the AC component of the output voltage to the DC component of the output voltage. It is a measure of the smoothness of the output voltage. Ripple factor is equal to the square root of the ratio of the mean square value of the output voltage to the square of the average value of the output voltage minus one.
- The formulas for Vrms, Vdc, and ripple factor for half-wave and full-wave rectifiers are given below:

| Rectifier | Vrms | Vdc | Ripple factor |
|-----------|------|-----|---------------|
| Half-wave | Vp/2 | Vp/π | 1.21 |
| Full-wave | Vp/√2 | 2Vp/π | 0.48 |

- Where Vp is the peak value of the AC input voltage.
