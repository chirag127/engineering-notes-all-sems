# 2. P-N Junction diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode - Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph.

- A P-N junction diode is a semiconductor device that consists of a p-type and an n-type region separated by a depletion region where charge is stored.
- A P-N junction diode has the ability to rectify electric current, that is, to allow current to flow in one direction only .
- A P-N junction diode has various nonlinear current-voltage characteristics, depending on the biasing condition .
- A P-N junction diode can also transform light energy into electrical energy or vice versa, depending on the application .

## Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph

- The static resistance of a P-N junction diode is the ratio of the applied voltage to the current at a given point on the current-voltage characteristic curve.
- The static resistance can be calculated by drawing a straight line from the origin to the point of interest on the curve and finding the slope of the line.
- The static resistance is also called the DC resistance or the average resistance.
- The dynamic resistance of a P-N junction diode is the ratio of the small change in voltage to the corresponding small change in current at a given operating point on the curve.
- The dynamic resistance can be calculated by drawing a tangent to the curve at the operating point and finding the slope of the tangent.
- The dynamic resistance is also called the AC resistance or the differential resistance.
- The dynamic resistance is usually much smaller than the static resistance for a P-N junction diode.

## Example

- Consider the following current-voltage characteristic curve of a P-N junction diode:

![PN junction diode curve](https://www.electronics-tutorials.ws/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/diode3.gif)

- The static resistance at point A, where the voltage is 0.7 V and the current is 5 mA, can be calculated by drawing a line from the origin to point A and finding the slope:

![PN junction diode static resistance](https://i.imgur.com/6lZp6fN.png)

- The slope of the line is 0.7 V / 0.005 A = 140 ohms, which is the static resistance at point A.
- The dynamic resistance at point A can be calculated by drawing a tangent to the curve at point A and finding the slope:

![PN junction diode dynamic resistance](https://i.imgur.com/1y1w7yT.png)

- The slope of the tangent can be estimated by taking two points near point A, such as B and C, and finding the ratio of the change in voltage to the change in current:

![PN junction diode dynamic resistance calculation](https://i.imgur.com/9l4w4yG.png)

- The slope of the tangent is (0.75 V - 0.65 V) / (0.006 A - 0.004 A) = 50 ohms, which is the dynamic resistance at point A.
