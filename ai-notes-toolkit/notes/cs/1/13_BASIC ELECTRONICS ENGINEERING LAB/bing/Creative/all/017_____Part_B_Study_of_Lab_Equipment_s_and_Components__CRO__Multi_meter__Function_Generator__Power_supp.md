# Part B Study of Lab Equipment’s and Components: CRO, Multi meter, Function Generator, Power supplyActive, Passive Components and Bread Board

- The objective of this part is to study various electronic components and design of various electronic circuits using lab equipment and components such as CRO, multimeter, function generator, power supply, active and passive components and bread board .
- CRO stands for cathode ray oscilloscope. It is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure voltage, current, frequency, phase and other parameters of a signal.
- Multimeter is a device that can measure multiple electrical quantities such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, inductance, etc. It can be analog or digital. It has a selector switch to choose the desired quantity and range, and a display to show the measured value.
- Function generator is a device that can generate different types of electrical signals such as sine, square, triangular, sawtooth, etc. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, duty cycle and phase of the signal. It has knobs and switches to control the parameters and output terminals to connect to the circuit.
- Power supply is a device that can provide a constant or variable voltage or current to a circuit. It can be AC or DC. It has a transformer, a rectifier, a filter and a regulator to convert the mains voltage to the desired output. It has a display to show the output value and terminals to connect to the circuit.
- Active components are those that can amplify or control the flow of electric current in a circuit. They require an external power source to operate. Examples are transistors, diodes, LEDs, ICs, etc.
- Passive components are those that can store or dissipate electric energy in a circuit. They do not require an external power source to operate. Examples are resistors, capacitors, inductors, etc.
- Bread board is a device that can be used to make temporary connections between electronic components without soldering. It has a grid of holes and metal strips that connect the components. It has power rails to supply voltage and current to the circuit.
- These test equipment and components can be demonstrated online from any lab of ECE department or physical mode is only option depending on the availability and accessibility of the resources. Online mode may require simulation software or remote access to the lab equipment. Physical mode may require proper safety and precaution measures.
