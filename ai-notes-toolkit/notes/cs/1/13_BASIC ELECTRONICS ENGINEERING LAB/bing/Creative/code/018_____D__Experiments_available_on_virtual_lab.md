## Experiments available on virtual lab

- Virtual lab is a platform that allows users to perform experiments online without the need of physical equipment or facilities.
- Virtual lab can simulate various scientific phenomena and processes, such as physics, chemistry, biology, engineering, mathematics, etc.
- Virtual lab can provide interactive feedback, visualization, data analysis, and assessment tools to enhance the learning experience and outcomes.
- Some examples of experiments available on virtual lab are:

  - **Physics**: Pendulum, Projectile motion, Newton's laws, Optics, Electricity and magnetism, etc.
  - **Chemistry**: Acid-base titration, Chemical equilibrium, Electrochemistry, Organic synthesis, etc.
  - **Biology**: Cell structure and function, DNA extraction, Enzyme kinetics, Photosynthesis, etc.
  - **Engineering**: Circuit design, Control systems, Fluid mechanics, Robotics, etc.
  - **Mathematics**: Graphing functions, Calculus, Linear algebra, Probability and statistics, etc.

- Virtual lab can be accessed through various websites, applications, or software, such as:

  - **PhET Interactive Simulations**: A collection of over 150 free simulations for science and math education, developed by the University of Colorado Boulder. https://phet.colorado.edu/
  - **Labster**: A platform that offers immersive and realistic virtual lab simulations for biology, chemistry, physics, and engineering. https://www.labster.com/
  - **Wolfram Demonstrations Project**: A website that hosts thousands of interactive demonstrations for various topics, powered by Wolfram Mathematica. https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/
  - **Virtual Labs**: A project initiated by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, to provide online lab experiments for various disciplines. http://www.vlab.co.in/
