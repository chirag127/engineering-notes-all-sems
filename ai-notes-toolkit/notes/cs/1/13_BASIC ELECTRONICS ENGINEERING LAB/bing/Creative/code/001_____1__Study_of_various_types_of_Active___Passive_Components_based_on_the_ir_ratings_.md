### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings.

- Active components are those that can generate or amplify electrical signals, such as transistors, diodes, LEDs, and integrated circuits. Passive components are those that can only store or dissipate electrical energy, such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers.
- Active and passive components can be classified based on their ratings, which are the maximum values of voltage, current, power, or frequency that they can handle without damage or degradation. Ratings are usually specified by the manufacturer and depend on the material, design, and operating conditions of the component.
- Some examples of ratings for active and passive components are:

  - Transistors: Collector-emitter voltage (Vce), collector current (Ic), power dissipation (Pc), and frequency (f).
  - Diodes: Peak inverse voltage (PIV), forward current (If), power dissipation (Pd), and frequency (f).
  - LEDs: Forward voltage (Vf), forward current (If), power dissipation (Pd), and wavelength (λ).
  - Integrated circuits: Supply voltage (Vcc), input/output voltage (Vin/Vout), input/output current (Iin/Iout), power dissipation (Pd), and frequency (f).
  - Resistors: Resistance (R), power rating (P), and tolerance (T).
  - Capacitors: Capacitance (C), voltage rating (V), and tolerance (T).
  - Inductors: Inductance (L), current rating (I), and resistance (R).
  - Transformers: Primary/secondary voltage (Vp/Vs), primary/secondary current (Ip/Is), turns ratio (Np/Ns), and frequency (f).

- The ratings of active and passive components are important to consider when designing or analyzing electronic circuits, as they determine the performance, efficiency, and reliability of the components and the circuit as a whole. Exceeding the ratings of a component can cause it to overheat, burn out, or fail. Therefore, it is advisable to choose components with ratings that are higher than the expected values of voltage, current, power, or frequency in the circuit, and to provide adequate cooling and protection for the components.
