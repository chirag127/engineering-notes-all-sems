### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- The objective of this topic is to study various electronic components and design of various electronic circuits like power supply, audio and power amplifiers.
- The following are some of the lab equipment and components that are commonly used in electronics experiments:

  - **CRO**: A cathode ray oscilloscope (CRO) is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure the voltage, frequency, phase, and shape of the signal. It consists of a cathode ray tube (CRT), a vertical and a horizontal amplifier, a time base generator, and a trigger circuit .
  - **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a device that can measure different electrical quantities such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, and continuity. It can be either analog or digital. It consists of a display, a selector switch, and two probes .
  - **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that can produce different types of periodic signals such as sine, square, triangle, and sawtooth waves. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and duty cycle of the signal. It consists of a waveform selector, a frequency control, an amplitude control, and an output terminal .
  - **Power Supply**: A power supply is a device that converts the alternating current (AC) from the mains to a direct current (DC) of a desired voltage and current. It can be either fixed or variable. It consists of a transformer, a rectifier, a filter, a regulator, and an output terminal .
  - **Active Components**: Active components are those that can amplify or control the flow of electric current. They require an external source of power to operate. Some examples of active components are transistors, diodes, integrated circuits, and optoelectronic devices .
  - **Passive Components**: Passive components are those that can store or dissipate electric energy. They do not require an external source of power to operate. Some examples of passive components are resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers .
  - **Bread Board**: A bread board is a device that allows the connection of electronic components without soldering. It consists of a plastic board with rows and columns of holes and metal strips underneath. The holes are connected by the metal strips in a specific pattern. The components are inserted into the holes and connected by jumper wires .

  - **CRO**: A CRO can be used to observe and analyze the behavior of various electronic circuits and signals. It can also be used to measure the parameters of the signals such as peak-to-peak voltage, root mean square (RMS) voltage, frequency, and phase difference .
  - **Multimeter**: A multimeter can be used to measure the values of various electrical quantities such as voltage, current, resistance, and capacitance. It can also be used to test the continuity of a circuit or a component .
  - **Function Generator**: A function generator can be used to provide different types of input signals to various electronic circuits and devices. It can also be used to test the frequency response, bandwidth, and distortion of the circuits and devices .
  - **Power Supply**: A power supply can be used to provide a stable and regulated DC voltage and current to various electronic circuits and devices. It can also be used to vary the voltage and current according to the requirements of the circuits and devices .
