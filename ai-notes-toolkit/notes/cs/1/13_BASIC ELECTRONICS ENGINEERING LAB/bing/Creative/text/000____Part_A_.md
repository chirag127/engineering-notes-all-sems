## Part A:

- Part A is the first section of the exam paper.
- It consists of 10 multiple choice questions, each worth 1 mark.
- The questions cover the topics of the course syllabus, such as data structures, algorithms, complexity analysis, recursion, sorting, searching, hashing, trees, graphs, etc.
- The questions are designed to test the basic understanding and application of the concepts and techniques learned in the course.
- The questions may have one or more correct answers. The candidate has to select the best answer among the given options.
- There is no negative marking for wrong answers or unattempted questions.
- The candidate has to mark the answer on the OMR sheet provided with the question paper.
- The candidate has to complete Part A in 15 minutes.
