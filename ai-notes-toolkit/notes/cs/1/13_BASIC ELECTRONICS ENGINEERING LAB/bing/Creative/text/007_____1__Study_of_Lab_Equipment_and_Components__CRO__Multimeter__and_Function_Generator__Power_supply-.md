### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- The objective of this course is to study various electronic components and design of various electronic circuits like power supply, audio and power amplifiers .
- The lab equipment and components used in this course are:

  - **CRO**: A cathode ray oscilloscope (CRO) is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure the voltage, frequency, phase, and other characteristics of the signal .
  - **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a device that can measure different electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, etc. It can be either analog or digital .
  - **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that can produce different types of periodic signals, such as sine, square, triangle, etc. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and shape of the signal  .
  - **Power supply**: A power supply is a device that provides a constant and regulated voltage or current to a circuit. It can be either AC or DC, and can have different output levels .
  - **Active Components**: Active components are those that can amplify or control the flow of electric current in a circuit. They require an external power source to operate. Examples are transistors, diodes, integrated circuits, etc  .
  - **Passive Components**: Passive components are those that can store or dissipate electric energy in a circuit. They do not require an external power source to operate. Examples are resistors, capacitors, inductors, etc  .
  - **Bread Board**: A bread board is a device that allows the user to create temporary circuits by inserting wires and components into holes. It is useful for testing and prototyping circuits without soldering  .

- The procedure to study the lab equipment and components is as follows:

  - Identify the different parts and functions of each equipment and component.
  - Connect the equipment and components to the power supply and the bread board as per the circuit diagram.
  - Use the multimeter to measure the voltage, current, resistance, or capacitance of the components or the circuit.
  - Use the function generator to generate different types of signals and apply them to the circuit.
  - Use the CRO to observe and analyze the waveform of the input and output signals of the circuit.
  - Record the observations and calculations in a tabular form.
  - Draw the graphs and diagrams of the signals and the circuit.
  - Compare the theoretical and experimental results and discuss the sources of error and deviation.
