### Part B Study of Lab Equipment’s and Components: CRO, Multi meter, Function Generator, Power supplyActive, Passive Components and Bread Board

- **CRO**: A cathode ray oscilloscope (CRO) is an electronic device that displays the variation of an electrical signal over time on a screen. It can measure voltage, frequency, phase difference, and other parameters of a signal. It consists of a cathode ray tube (CRT), a time base generator, a vertical and a horizontal amplifier, and a trigger circuit.
- **Multimeter**: A multimeter is an instrument that can measure multiple electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, and continuity. It can be analog or digital, and can have different types of probes and leads for different applications. It can be used to test circuits, batteries, components, and devices.
- **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that can produce different types of waveforms, such as sine, square, triangular, and sawtooth. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and phase of the output signal. It can be used to test and calibrate electronic equipment, such as amplifiers, filters, and oscilloscopes.
- **Power supply**: A power supply is a device that converts AC mains voltage to DC voltage for electronic circuits. It can be fixed or variable, and can have different levels of regulation and protection. It can provide different voltages and currents for different components and devices.
- **Active components**: Active components are electronic components that can amplify, oscillate, or switch an electrical signal. They require an external power source to operate. Examples of active components are transistors, diodes, integrated circuits, and optoelectronic devices.
- **Passive components**: Passive components are electronic components that can store, dissipate, or filter an electrical signal. They do not require an external power source to operate. Examples of passive components are resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers.
- **Breadboard**: A breadboard is a device that allows the prototyping of electronic circuits without soldering. It consists of a plastic board with holes and metal strips that connect the holes. It can be used to insert and remove components and wires easily and quickly.
