### Part A PCB Lab: a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB. b. Etching & drilling of PCB

This practical is not possible by virtual lab. It will be conducted only in physical mode.

The objective of this practical is to design and fabricate a simple printed circuit board (PCB) using the following steps:

a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB.

- A PCB is a thin board made of fiberglass, epoxy or other materials that has copper tracks on one or both sides to connect electronic components.
- The artwork is the design of the PCB layout, which shows the position and size of the components, pads, holes and tracks on the board.
- The artwork can be drawn by hand or using a software tool such as Eagle, KiCad, Proteus, etc.
- The artwork can be printed on a transparent sheet or film using a laser printer or a photocopier.
- The printed artwork is then aligned and placed on top of a copper-clad board, which is a board coated with a thin layer of copper on one or both sides.
- The copper-clad board is then exposed to ultraviolet (UV) light, which transfers the artwork to the board. The exposed areas of copper become hardened and resistant to etching, while the unexposed areas remain soft and can be removed by etching.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB.

- Etching is the process of removing the unwanted copper from the board, leaving only the desired tracks and pads.
- Etching can be done using a chemical solution such as ferric chloride or ammonium persulfate, which dissolves the copper in the unexposed areas.
- The board is immersed in the etching solution and agitated until the unwanted copper is completely removed. The board is then rinsed with water and dried.
- Drilling is the process of making holes in the board for mounting the components and connecting the tracks on different layers of the board.
- Drilling can be done using a hand drill or a drill press, with a suitable drill bit size and speed.
- The board is clamped or fixed on a flat surface and the holes are drilled according to the artwork. The board is then cleaned and inspected for any defects.
