### 3. Applications of PN Junction diode: Half & Full wave rectifierMeasurement of Vrms, Vdc, and ripple factor.

- A PN junction diode is a semiconductor device that allows current to flow in one direction only. It has two terminals, anode and cathode, and a junction between them.
- A rectifier is a circuit that converts alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC). Rectifiers are used in power supplies, radio receivers, and other electronic devices that require DC voltage.
- A half-wave rectifier is a rectifier that uses only one diode and passes only one half-cycle of the AC input voltage to the output. The other half-cycle is blocked by the diode. The output voltage is pulsating DC, which has a lot of fluctuations and gaps.
- A full-wave rectifier is a rectifier that uses two diodes and passes both half-cycles of the AC input voltage to the output. The diodes are connected in such a way that they reverse the polarity of the negative half-cycle and make it positive. The output voltage is also pulsating DC, but it has less fluctuations and gaps than the half-wave rectifier.
- Vrms is the root mean square value of the AC input voltage. It is the effective value of the AC voltage that produces the same heating effect as a DC voltage of the same magnitude. Vrms can be calculated by dividing the peak value of the AC voltage by the square root of 2.
- Vdc is the average value of the DC output voltage. It is the value of the DC voltage that is equivalent to the area under the curve of the pulsating DC voltage. Vdc can be calculated by multiplying the peak value of the AC voltage by a factor that depends on the type of rectifier. For half-wave rectifier, the factor is 0.318, and for full-wave rectifier, the factor is 0.636.
- Ripple factor is a measure of the amount of AC component present in the DC output voltage. It is the ratio of the root mean square value of the AC component to the average value of the DC component. Ripple factor can be calculated by dividing the root mean square value of the AC component by the average value of the DC component. For half-wave rectifier, the ripple factor is 1.21, and for full-wave rectifier, the ripple factor is 0.482.
