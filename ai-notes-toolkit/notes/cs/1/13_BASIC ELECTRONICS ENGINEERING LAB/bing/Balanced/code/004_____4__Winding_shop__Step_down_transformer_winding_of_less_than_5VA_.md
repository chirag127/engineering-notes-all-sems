### 4. Winding shop: Step down transformer winding of less than 5VA.

- A step down transformer is a device that reduces the voltage from the primary winding to the secondary winding.
- A step down transformer winding of less than 5VA means that the transformer can deliver a maximum of 5 watts of power to the load connected to the secondary winding.
- The winding shop is the place where the transformer windings are made by wrapping insulated copper wire around a magnetic core.
- The steps involved in winding a step down transformer of less than 5VA are:

  - Select the appropriate wire gauge and length for the primary and secondary windings, based on the desired turns ratio, voltage, current and resistance specifications.
  - Cut the wire to the required length and strip the insulation from the ends.
  - Wind the primary winding around the core, starting from one end and leaving some wire length for the terminals. Secure the winding with tape or glue.
  - Wind the secondary winding around the same core, over the primary winding, in the opposite direction. Leave some wire length for the terminals and secure the winding with tape or glue.
  - Solder the terminals of the primary and secondary windings to the corresponding pins or leads of the transformer casing or board.
  - Test the transformer for continuity, polarity, voltage, current and power output using a multimeter and a load resistor.
