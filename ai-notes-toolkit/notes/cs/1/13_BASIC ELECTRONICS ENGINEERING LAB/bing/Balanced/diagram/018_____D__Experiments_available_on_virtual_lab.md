## (D) Experiments available on virtual lab

- A virtual lab is a simulated environment that allows users to perform experiments or activities without the need for physical equipment, materials, or space.
- Virtual labs can be used for various purposes, such as education, research, training, testing, or entertainment.
- Some examples of experiments available on virtual lab are:

  - **Physics**: Users can explore the concepts and phenomena of mechanics, optics, electricity, magnetism, thermodynamics, waves, and more. For instance, users can measure the acceleration due to gravity, observe the interference of light, build circuits, create magnetic fields, etc.
  - **Chemistry**: Users can conduct experiments involving chemical reactions, properties, and structures. For instance, users can balance chemical equations, titrate acids and bases, synthesize organic compounds, etc.
  - **Biology**: Users can investigate the processes and functions of living organisms, such as cells, tissues, organs, systems, and ecology. For instance, users can observe the structure of a cell, dissect a frog, measure the rate of photosynthesis, etc.
  - **Mathematics**: Users can practice and apply mathematical concepts and skills, such as algebra, geometry, calculus, statistics, and more. For instance, users can solve equations, graph functions, find derivatives and integrals, analyze data, etc.
  - **Computer Science**: Users can learn and practice programming languages, algorithms, data structures, and more. For instance, users can write and run code, debug errors, implement sorting and searching methods, etc.
