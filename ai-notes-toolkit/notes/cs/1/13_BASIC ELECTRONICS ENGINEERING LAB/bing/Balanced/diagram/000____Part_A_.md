## Part A

- Part A is the first section of a document or a project that provides an overview of the main objectives, scope, methods, and results.
- Part A usually consists of the following sub-sections:
  - Introduction: This sub-section introduces the topic, the background, the motivation, and the research questions or hypotheses.
  - Literature Review: This sub-section reviews the existing literature on the topic, identifies the gaps, and summarizes the main findings and contributions.
  - Methodology: This sub-section describes the research design, the data sources, the data collection methods, the data analysis methods, and the ethical considerations.

  - Discussion: This sub-section discusses the implications, the limitations, the strengths, and the weaknesses of the results, and compares them with the literature review.
