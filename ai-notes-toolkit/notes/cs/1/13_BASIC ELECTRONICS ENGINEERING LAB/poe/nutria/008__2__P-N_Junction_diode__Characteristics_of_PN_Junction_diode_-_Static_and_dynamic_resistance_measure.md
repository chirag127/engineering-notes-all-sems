
### 2. P-N Junction diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode - Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph

* A PN junction diode is a semiconductor device composed of a P-type and N-type semiconductor material. 
* The P-type material is positively charged and the N-type material is negatively charged. 
* When a voltage is applied across the diode, electrons flow from the N-type material to the P-type material and holes flow from the P-type material to the N-type material. 
* The characteristics of the PN junction diode can be studied by measuring the static and dynamic resistance of the diode. 
* The static resistance of the diode is measured by plotting the current versus voltage (I-V) curve. 
* The dynamic resistance of the diode is measured by plotting the voltage versus time (V-t) curve. 
* The dynamic resistance of the diode is determined by the slope of the V-t curve. 
* The static and dynamic resistance of the diode can be used to calculate the voltage drop across the diode and the power dissipated by the diode.
