
### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board

* Cathode Ray Oscilloscope (CRO): A CRO is an electronic instrument that uses a beam of electrons to measure and display the magnitude of electrical signals. It is used to measure the frequency, amplitude, and shape of electrical signals.

* Multimeter: A multimeter is an electronic device that is used to measure voltage, current, and resistance. It can also be used to measure capacitance, inductance, and temperature.

* Function Generator: A function generator is an electronic device that generates electrical signals with different waveforms. It is used to generate signals with different frequencies, amplitudes, and shapes.

* Power Supply: A power supply is an electronic device that converts electrical energy into other forms of energy. It is used to supply power to electronic circuits.

* Active Components: Active components are electronic components that require a power source to operate. Examples of active components are transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

* Passive Components: Passive components are electronic components that do not require a power source to operate. Examples of passive components are resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

* Breadboard: A breadboard is a device used to build and test electronic circuits. It consists of a board with a grid of holes into which components can be inserted.
