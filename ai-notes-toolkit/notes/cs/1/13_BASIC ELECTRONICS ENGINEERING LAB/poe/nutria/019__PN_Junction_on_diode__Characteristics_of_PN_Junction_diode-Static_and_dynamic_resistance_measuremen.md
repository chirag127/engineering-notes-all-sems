
### PN Junction on diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode-Static and Dynamic Resistance Measurement from Graph

1. A PN junction diode is a two-terminal semiconductor device that consists of a p-type semiconductor and an n-type semiconductor.

2. The p-type semiconductor is positively charged and the n-type semiconductor is negatively charged.

3. The junction between the p-type and n-type semiconductors is called the PN junction.

4. When a voltage is applied across the PN junction, current can flow through the diode.

5. The static resistance of the diode can be measured by plotting the current versus voltage graph.

6. The dynamic resistance of the diode can be measured by plotting the current versus frequency graph.

7. The dynamic resistance of the diode is higher than the static resistance.

8. The dynamic resistance of the diode decreases with increasing frequency.

9. The dynamic resistance of the diode is inversely proportional to the applied voltage.
