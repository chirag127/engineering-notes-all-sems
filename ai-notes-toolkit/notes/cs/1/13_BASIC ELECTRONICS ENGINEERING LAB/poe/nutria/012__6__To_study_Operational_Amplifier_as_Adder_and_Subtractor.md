
### 6. To study Operational Amplifier as Adder and Subtractor
- An operational amplifier (op-amp) is a DC-coupled high-gain electronic voltage amplifier with a differential input and, usually, a single-ended output. 
- An op-amp can be used as an adder or subtractor depending on the circuit configuration. 
- When used as an adder, the output of the op-amp is the sum of the input voltages. 
- When used as a subtractor, the output of the op-amp is the difference between the input voltages. 
- In both cases, the gain of the amplifier is determined by the feedback resistor network. 
- An op-amp can also be used as a voltage comparator. In this application, the op-amp compares two input voltages and produces an output voltage that is either high or low, depending on the polarity of the difference between the input voltages.
