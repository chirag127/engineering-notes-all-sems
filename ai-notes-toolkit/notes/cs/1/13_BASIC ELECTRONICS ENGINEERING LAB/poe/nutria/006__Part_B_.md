
## Part B:

1. Formality is an important part of communication. It is used to show respect and professionalism.
2. Formal language should be used in professional settings such as business meetings, job interviews, and academic settings.
3. CommonMark Markdown is a writing style that is used to format text. It uses symbols to denote different types of text such as headings, lists, and links.
4. Emojis should be avoided in formal contexts as they can be seen as informal and unprofessional.
5. External links should not be included in formal writing as they can be distracting and take the reader away from the main point.
