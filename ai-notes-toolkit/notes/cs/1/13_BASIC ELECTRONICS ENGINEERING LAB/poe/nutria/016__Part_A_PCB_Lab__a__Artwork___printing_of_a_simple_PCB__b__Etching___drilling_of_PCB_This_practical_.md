
### Part A PCB Lab:
1. Artwork & Printing of a Simple PCB: 
    * Artwork is the preparation of a printed circuit board (PCB) for etching and drilling. It involves creating the necessary design on a computer, then printing it out onto a substrate material.
    * The printed artwork is then transferred to a copper-clad board, which is then ready for etching.
2. Etching & Drilling of PCB:
    * Etching is the process of removing unwanted copper from the PCB using a chemical solution.
    * Drilling is the process of making holes in the PCB. This is done using a drill press or a CNC machine.
