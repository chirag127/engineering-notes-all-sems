
### Applications of PN Junction Diode: Half & Full Wave Rectifier

1. Half wave rectifier:
    - A half wave rectifier is a type of rectifier circuit which converts an AC input voltage into a DC output voltage.
    - It is composed of a single diode connected to the load resistor in series. 
    - In this type of rectifier, only one half of the AC voltage waveform is rectified, hence the name.
    - The output voltage of a half wave rectifier is a pulsating DC voltage.
2. Full wave rectifier:
    - A full wave rectifier is a type of rectifier circuit which converts an AC input voltage into a DC output voltage.
    - It is composed of two diodes connected to the load resistor in series. 
    - In this type of rectifier, both the positive and negative halves of the AC voltage waveform are rectified, hence the name.
    - The output voltage of a full wave rectifier is a smooth DC voltage.
3. Measurement of Vrms, Vdc, and ripple factor:
    - Vrms (Root Mean Square) is the effective value of the AC voltage. It is the equivalent DC voltage that will produce the same heating effect as the AC voltage.
    - Vdc (Direct Current) is the DC voltage obtained after rectification.
    - Ripple factor is the ratio of the root mean square value of the AC components of the output voltage to the DC component. It is a measure of the amount of AC voltage present in the output of the rectifier.
