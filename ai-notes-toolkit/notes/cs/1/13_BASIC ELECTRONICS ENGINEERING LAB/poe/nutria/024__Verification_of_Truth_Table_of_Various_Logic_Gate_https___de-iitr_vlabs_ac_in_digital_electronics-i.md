
### Verification of Truth Table of Various Logic Gate

1. A logic gate is a device that performs a logical operation on one or more inputs and produces a single output.
2. Logic gates are the building blocks of digital circuits and are used to process and manipulate digital signals.
3. Logic gates are classified into two categories: combinational logic gates and sequential logic gates.
4. Combinational logic gates are used to implement Boolean functions and are characterized by their truth tables.
5. A truth table is a table that shows the output of a logic gate for all possible combinations of its inputs.
6. The most commonly used logic gates are AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, and XOR.
7. The truth table for each logic gate can be used to verify its operation.
8. The AND gate produces an output of 1 only when both its inputs are 1.
9. The OR gate produces an output of 1 when either of its inputs is 1.
10. The NOT gate produces an output of 0 when its input is 1 and an output of 1 when its input is 0.
11. The NAND gate produces an output of 0 only when both its inputs are 1.
12. The NOR gate produces an output of 0 when either of its inputs is 1.
13. The XOR gate produces an output of 1 when either of its inputs is 1 and an output of 0 when both its inputs are 0.
