

### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings

* Active components are electrical components that require a power source to operate. Examples include transistors, diodes, integrated circuits, and relays.
* Passive components are electrical components that do not require a power source to operate. Examples include resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers.
* The ratings of active components are usually expressed in terms of voltage, current, power, and temperature.
* The ratings of passive components are usually expressed in terms of resistance, capacitance, inductance, and power.
* The ratings of active components must be taken into consideration when selecting components for a circuit.
* The ratings of passive components must also be taken into consideration when selecting components for a circuit.
* It is important to ensure that components are rated for the correct voltage, current, and temperature before they are used.
