### 3. PCB Lab:

a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB:

1. Design the circuit diagram for a simple circuit like an LED flasher.
2. Convert the circuit diagram into PCB layout using PCB design software like Fritzing or Eagle.
3. Print the PCB layout on a glossy paper using a laser printer.
4. Align and stick the printed PCB layout on the copper clad board and get it laminated.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:

1. Immerse the laminated PCB in Ferric Chloride solution.
2. Check regularly for the progress of etching. Once done, remove and wash the PCB.
3. Drill holes on the PCB using a mini drilling machine for mounting components.
4. The PCB is now ready for component mounting and soldering.
