### To study Operational Amplifier as Adder and Subtractor

1. Operational Amplifier (OA) can be configured to perform arithmetic operations like addition, subtraction, integration, differentiation, etc.
2. OA as adder: When two inputs are applied to the input terminals, the output is the sum of the inputs. This configuration is known as summing amplifier or adder.
3. OA as subtractor: When one input is applied to inverting input terminal and other to non-inverting input terminal, the output is the difference between the two inputs. This configuration is known as difference amplifier or subtractor.
4. To configure OA as adder or subtractor, apply input signals to appropriate terminals and analyze the output.
5. The closed loop gain of adder and subtractor can be made unity by proper selection of feedback resistors.
6. The input impedance depends on the input terminal (inverting or non-inverting) to which the input is applied. The output impedance is low as it is a voltage follower configuration.
7. Some applications of adder and subtractor are analog computers, instrumentation, wave shaping circuits, etc.
