### 6. To study Operational Amplifier as Adder and Subtractor

- An op-amp can be configured to perform addition and subtraction of input voltages.
- For addition: Connect both inputs to the input voltages to be added. The output will be the sum of the two input voltages.
- For subtraction: Connect one input to the minuend voltage and the other input to the subtrahend voltage. The output will be the difference between the two input voltages.
- The applications of op-amp as adder and subtractor are:
- - Analog computers
- - Function generators
- - Waveform generators
- - Active filters
- - Instrumentation amplifiers
