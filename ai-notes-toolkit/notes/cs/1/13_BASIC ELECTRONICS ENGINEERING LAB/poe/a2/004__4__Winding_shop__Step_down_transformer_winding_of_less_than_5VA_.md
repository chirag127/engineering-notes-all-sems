### 4. Winding shop: Step down transformer winding of less than 5VA.

1. Gather the materials: Enameled copper wire, transformer core, insulation tape, etc.
2. Wind the primary coil:
- Start with the thicker wire
- Wind tightly and evenly around one section of the core
- Leave enough wire to connect to the power source
3. Wind the secondary coil:
- Use thinner wire
- Wind around the opposite section of the core
- Leave enough wire to connect the load
4. Insulate the coils: Wrap insulation tape around each coil and between the coils to prevent short circuits.
5. Connect the coils: Connect the primary coil to the power source and the secondary coil to the load.
6. Test the transformer: Power on the transformer and test that the output voltage is appropriate for the load.
