### 2. Identification of various types of Printed Circuit Boards (PCB) and soldering Techniques.

1. Single sided PCB: Contains copper tracks on one side of the board. Components are soldered on one side.
2. Double sided PCB: Contains copper tracks on both sides of the board. Through hole plating connects the two layers. Allows higher component density.
3. Multi-layer PCB: Contains three or more layers of copper tracks separated by insulation. Allows high speed signals and high component density.
4. Flexible PCB: Made of flexible material like polyimide. Used in applications where rigid PCBs cannot be employed.
5. Rigid Flex PCB: A mix of rigid and flexible PCBs allowing flexible areas to connect rigid sections.

Soldering techniques:

1. Manual soldering: Using a soldering iron and solder wire/solder paste.
2. Reflow soldering: Place solder paste on pads, place components and pass through a reflow oven.
3. Wave soldering: Pass the PCB over a wave of molten solder to solder components on one side.
4. Selective soldering: Soldering specific points on the PCB using soldering iron, solder pot or reflow.
