### 5. Soldering shop:

1. Soldering of Resistor in PCB:
- Clean the PCB and component pads with solvent.
- Place the resistors on the PCB with correct polarity.
- Apply soldering flux on the joints.
- Heat one pad of resistor and apply solder.
- When solder melts and coats the pad, remove soldering iron.
- Hold the resistor in place for few seconds as the solder hardens.
- Repeat the same process for other pad of resistor.

2. Disordering of Resistor in PCB:
- Heat one soldered pad of resistor with soldering iron.
- When solder melts, use solder wick or solder sucker to remove the solder.
- Gently remove the resistor from the PCB.
- Repeat the same process to remove solder from other pad and completely remove the resistor.
