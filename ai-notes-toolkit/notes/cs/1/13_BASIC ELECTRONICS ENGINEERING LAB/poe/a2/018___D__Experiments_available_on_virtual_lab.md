## (D) Experiments available on virtual lab.

1. Ohm's Law: To verify Ohm's Law which states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage applied across it, provided temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.
2. Resistivity of a wire: To determine the resistivity of a given wire specimen. Resistivity is a measure of how strongly a material opposes the flow of electric current.
3. Resistance of a wire: To find the resistance of a uniform wire using a meter bridge and to verify the relation between resistance, length and cross-sectional area of the wire. Resistance is directly proportional to length and inversely proportional to cross-sectional area.
4. Effect of temperature on resistance: To study the effect of temperature on the resistance of a conductor. The resistance of a conductor decreases with increase in temperature.
5. Combination of resistors: To verify the laws of combination of resistors in series and parallel and to calculate equivalent resistance of combinations.
6. Charge and discharge of a capacitor: To study the charge and discharge characteristics of a capacitor. A capacitor charges through a resistor and discharges through a resistor.
7. RC circuits: To study the charging and discharging of a capacitor through a resistor and to verify exponential growth and decay laws. Time constant is the product of resistance and capacitance.

The above points describe some of the key experiments available on a virtual physics lab to understand concepts related to electrical circuits and components. The experiments can be performed virtually to observe results and analyze data to understand the theories and laws.
