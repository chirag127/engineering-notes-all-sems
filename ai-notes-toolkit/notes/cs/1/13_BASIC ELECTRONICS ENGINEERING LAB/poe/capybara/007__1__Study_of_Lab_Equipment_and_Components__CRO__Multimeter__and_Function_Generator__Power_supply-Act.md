### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board

In a laboratory setting, it is essential to have a proper understanding of the equipment and components used in experiments. The following points provide an overview of the study of lab equipment and components.

#### CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope)

- A CRO is an electronic measuring instrument used to display various signals graphically.
- It consists of a cathode ray tube (CRT) and an electron gun that produces a beam of electrons.
- The beam of electrons is deflected by the magnetic field produced by the coils around the CRT.
- The CRO is used to measure voltage, current, and frequency, and can display waveforms, such as sine, square, and triangular.
- The various controls on a CRO include the time base, trigger, and vertical deflection.

#### Multimeter

- A multimeter is an electronic measuring instrument used to measure voltage, current, and resistance.
- It consists of a display screen, probes, and a selection dial to choose the desired measurement.
- The probes are used to connect the multimeter to the circuit or component being measured.
- The multimeter can also measure continuity and diode voltage.

#### Function Generator

- A function generator is an electronic device that produces various waveforms, such as sine, square, and triangular.
- It is used to test and troubleshoot electronic circuits and components.
- The function generator can also produce complex waveforms, such as pulse and ramp, and can modulate the amplitude or frequency of the waveform.

#### Power Supply

- A power supply is an electronic device that provides electrical energy to an electronic circuit or component.
- There are two types of power supplies: active and passive.
- Active power supplies convert AC (alternating current) to DC (direct current) and can regulate the voltage and current output.
- Passive power supplies are essentially transformers that step down the voltage of the AC input.

#### Passive Components

- Passive components are electronic components that do not require an external power source.
- Examples of passive components include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.
- Resistors are used to limit the flow of current in a circuit.
- Capacitors are used to store electrical energy and can filter out unwanted frequencies in a circuit.
- Inductors are used to store electrical energy in a magnetic field.

#### Bread Board

- A breadboard is a device used to create temporary circuits for testing and prototyping.
- It consists of a board with holes for inserting electronic components and metal strips for connecting the components.
- The breadboard allows for easy experimentation and modification of circuits without the need for soldering.

In conclusion, a proper understanding of lab equipment and components is essential for conducting experiments and troubleshooting electronic circuits. The above points provide a comprehensive overview of the study of CRO, multimeter, function generator, power supply, passive components, and breadboard.
