### 3. PCB Lab: a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB. b. Etching & drilling of PCB

In this lab session, we will learn about creating a printed circuit board (PCB) through artwork and printing. We will also cover the process of etching and drilling the PCB. 

a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB:

1. To start with, we need to create the artwork for the PCB using a software tool like Eagle or Altium. 
2. Once the artwork is ready, we need to print it onto a transparency film using a laser printer. 
3. Next, we need to prepare the copper-coated board by cleaning it thoroughly and applying a layer of photoresist material on it. 
4. We will then place the transparency film on top of the photoresist-coated board and expose it to UV light. 
5. The areas where the UV light is blocked by the black ink on the transparency film will remain unexposed. 
6. We will then develop the board by washing it in a solution that removes the unexposed photoresist material. 
7. The exposed copper is left behind, creating the circuit pattern on the board. 
8. Finally, we will etch the board to remove any remaining copper that is not part of the circuit pattern. 

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:

1. Once the circuit pattern is created on the board, we need to etch it to remove any excess copper. 
2. Etching can be done using a solution of ferric chloride or ammonium persulfate. 
3. The board is immersed in the solution until the excess copper is removed, leaving only the circuit pattern behind. 
4. After etching, the board is washed and dried. 
5. Next, we need to drill holes in the board to accommodate the electronic components. 
6. We will use a drill to make holes in the board at the appropriate locations. 
7. Finally, the board is cleaned again and is ready to be populated with electronic components. 

In conclusion, this lab session provides a basic understanding of the process involved in creating a simple PCB. By following the steps outlined above, one can create their own PCB and build electronic circuits that meet their specific requirements.
