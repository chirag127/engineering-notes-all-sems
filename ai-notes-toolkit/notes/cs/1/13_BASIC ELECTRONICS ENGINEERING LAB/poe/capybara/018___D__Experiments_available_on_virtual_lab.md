## (D) Experiments available on virtual lab

Virtual labs provide a unique opportunity for students to conduct experiments in a simulated environment. Here are some experiments that are available on virtual lab:

- **Chemistry experiments:** Virtual labs offer a variety of chemistry experiments that cover topics such as acids and bases, stoichiometry, and thermochemistry. These experiments allow students to observe chemical reactions and perform calculations without the need for physical equipment.

- **Physics experiments:** Virtual labs also offer physics experiments that cover topics such as optics, electricity and magnetism, and mechanics. These experiments allow students to study the properties of light, electricity, and motion without the need for physical equipment.

- **Biology experiments:** Virtual labs provide biology experiments that cover topics such as cell biology, genetics, and ecology. These experiments allow students to observe the behavior of cells and organisms and to study the relationships between different organisms and their environment.

- **Computer science experiments:** Virtual labs offer computer science experiments that cover topics such as programming, data structures, and algorithm analysis. These experiments allow students to develop programming skills and to test the efficiency of algorithms.

- **Engineering experiments:** Virtual labs also provide engineering experiments that cover topics such as materials science, fluid mechanics, and thermodynamics. These experiments allow students to study the properties of materials and to design and test engineering systems.

In conclusion, virtual labs offer a wide range of experiments that cover different areas of science and engineering. They provide a safe and convenient way for students to study and learn without the need for physical equipment.
