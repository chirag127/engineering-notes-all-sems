### 2. P-N Junction diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode - Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph.

The P-N Junction diode is a semiconductor device that is commonly used in electronic circuits. It is a two-terminal device that allows current to flow in only one direction. The following are the characteristics of the PN Junction diode:

#### Static Resistance Measurement:
- The static resistance of a PN Junction diode is the resistance of the diode when it is not conducting.
- It can be measured using a multimeter in the ohmmeter mode.
- The static resistance of a PN Junction diode depends on the doping concentration of the P and N regions, as well as the temperature.

#### Dynamic Resistance Measurement:
- The dynamic resistance of a PN Junction diode is the resistance of the diode when it is conducting.
- It can be measured using a curve tracer, which plots the current-voltage (I-V) characteristic curve of the diode.
- The dynamic resistance of a PN Junction diode depends on the doping concentration of the P and N regions, as well as the temperature.

#### Graphical Representation of Static and Dynamic Resistance:
- The static and dynamic resistance of a PN Junction diode can be represented graphically using the I-V characteristic curve.
- The I-V characteristic curve is a plot of the diode's current versus voltage for a range of input voltages.
- The slope of the I-V characteristic curve at any point gives the dynamic resistance of the diode.
- The static resistance of the diode can be calculated by measuring the resistance of the diode at zero bias voltage.

In conclusion, the P-N Junction diode has both static and dynamic resistance, which can be measured using different methods. The I-V characteristic curve is a useful tool for representing the static and dynamic resistance of the diode, and it can be used to calculate the resistance of the diode at different bias voltages.
