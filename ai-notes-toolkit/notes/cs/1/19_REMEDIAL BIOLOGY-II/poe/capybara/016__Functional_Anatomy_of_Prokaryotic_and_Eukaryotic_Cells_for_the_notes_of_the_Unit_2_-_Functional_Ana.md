### Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells

#### Prokaryotic Cells
- Prokaryotic cells are unicellular organisms that lack a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
- They are small in size, typically ranging from 0.5 to 5 micrometers in diameter.
- The cell is bounded by a plasma membrane that encloses the cytoplasm.
- The cytoplasm contains ribosomes, which are responsible for protein synthesis.
- The DNA in prokaryotic cells is typically organized into a single circular chromosome that is located in the nucleoid region of the cell.
- Prokaryotic cells may also have plasmids, small circular pieces of DNA that can replicate independently of the main chromosome.
- Some prokaryotic cells have external structures such as flagella, pili, and capsules that aid in movement, attachment, and protection.

#### Eukaryotic Cells
- Eukaryotic cells are more complex than prokaryotic cells and contain a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
- They are typically larger in size, ranging from 10 to 100 micrometers in diameter.
- The cell is bounded by a plasma membrane that encloses the cytoplasm.
- The cytoplasm contains a variety of organelles, including the nucleus, mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi apparatus, lysosomes, and peroxisomes.
- Each organelle has a specific function within the cell.
- The DNA in eukaryotic cells is organized into multiple linear chromosomes that are located within the nucleus.
- Eukaryotic cells may also have external structures such as cilia and flagella that aid in movement.

#### Comparison of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells
| Prokaryotic Cells | Eukaryotic Cells |
| --- | --- |
| Lack a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles | Contain a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles |
| Small in size, typically ranging from 0.5 to 5 micrometers in diameter | Larger in size, typically ranging from 10 to 100 micrometers in diameter |
| DNA is typically organized into a single circular chromosome located in the nucleoid region | DNA is organized into multiple linear chromosomes located within the nucleus |
| Cytoplasm contains ribosomes responsible for protein synthesis | Cytoplasm contains a variety of organelles with specific functions |
| May have external structures such as flagella, pili, and capsules | May have external structures such as cilia and flagella |

In conclusion, prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells differ in size, complexity, and organization of genetic material. Understanding the functional anatomy of these cells is important for studying various biological processes and diseases.
