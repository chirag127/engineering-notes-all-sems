## Unit 4 - Energy Utilization

### Structure of Mitochondria
- Mitochondria are organelles found in eukaryotic cells.
- They are responsible for producing ATP, the main energy currency of the cell.
- The structure of mitochondria consists of an outer membrane, an intermembrane space, an inner membrane, and a matrix.

### Cellular Respiration
- Cellular respiration is a process that occurs in the mitochondria of eukaryotic cells.
- It involves the conversion of glucose and other compounds into ATP.
- The process is broken down into three stages: Glycolysis, the Kreb cycle, and the Electron Transport System (ETS).

### Relationship of Carbohydrate Metabolism to Other Compounds
- Carbohydrate metabolism is closely related to the metabolism of other compounds, such as lipids and proteins.
- These compounds can also be used as sources of energy in cellular respiration.

### Glycolysis
- Glycolysis is the first stage of cellular respiration.
- It takes place in the cytoplasm of the cell and does not require oxygen.
- The process involves the breakdown of glucose into pyruvate, which can then be used in the next stage of respiration.

### Formation of Acetyl Co-A
- Acetyl Co-A is a molecule that is formed from pyruvate during the second stage of cellular respiration.
- The molecule is then used in the Kreb cycle to produce ATP.

### Kreb Cycle
- The Kreb cycle is the second stage of cellular respiration.
- It takes place in the mitochondria and involves the breakdown of acetyl Co-A.
- The process produces ATP and other molecules that are used in the Electron Transport System.

### Electron Transport System and Oxidative Phosphorylation
- The Electron Transport System is the final stage of cellular respiration.
- It takes place in the inner membrane of the mitochondria and involves the transfer of electrons between molecules.
- The process produces ATP through a process called oxidative phosphorylation.

### ATP
- ATP is the main energy currency of the cell.
- It is produced during cellular respiration and is used in a variety of cellular processes.

### Factors Affecting Respiration
- Respiration can be affected by a variety of factors, such as temperature, pH, and the availability of oxygen.
- Changes in these factors can affect the efficiency of cellular respiration and the production of ATP.
