
### Unit 2 - Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells: 

* Prokaryotic cells are much smaller than eukaryotic cells, ranging in size from 0.2 to 2.0 μm in diameter. 
* Prokaryotic cells have a simple, single-layered cell wall that provides structural support and protection. 
* Prokaryotic cells can have a variety of shapes, including rods, spheres, and spirals. 
* Eukaryotic cells are much larger than prokaryotic cells, ranging from 10–100 μm in diameter. 
* Eukaryotic cells have a complex, multi-layered cell wall that provides structural support and protection. 
* Eukaryotic cells are typically round or oval in shape. 
* The structure of a cell is closely related to its function. 
* Prokaryotic cells contain a single, circular chromosome located in the nucleoid region. 
* Prokaryotic cells also contain ribosomes, which are responsible for protein synthesis. 
* Eukaryotic cells contain multiple linear chromosomes located in the nucleus. 
* Eukaryotic cells also contain organelles, which are specialized structures that perform specific functions. 
* The endomembrane system is responsible for the movement of proteins and lipids within the cell. 
* The cytoskeleton is responsible for the movement of the cell itself.
