
### Basic Idea of Domain Bacteria

* Bacteria are single-celled microorganisms that belong to the domain Bacteria. 
* They are prokaryotic organisms, meaning that their cells do not have a nucleus or other membrane-bound organelles.
* Bacteria can be classified into two major groups: Proteobacteria and Non-Proteobacteria. 
* Proteobacteria are further divided into five subgroups: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, and Epsilon. 
* Non-Proteobacteria are further divided into Gram-positive and Gram-negative bacteria. 
* Lichens, algae, protozoa, and helminthes are also classified as bacteria. 
* Viral structures and viral multiplication are also important topics related to bacteria. 
* Bacteria play an important role in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals. 
* Microbiology is the study of microorganisms, including bacteria.
