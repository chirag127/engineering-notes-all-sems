

### Proteobacteria

* Proteobacteria are a major group of bacteria that includes Escherichia coli and other pathogens, as well as many beneficial species.
* Proteobacteria are divided into five major classes: alpha, beta, gamma, delta and epsilon.
* Proteobacteria are Gram-negative, meaning they have an outer membrane with a thin peptidoglycan layer.
* Proteobacteria are commonly found in soil, water, and the human gut.
* Proteobacteria are important in the production of industrial chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and bioremediation.
* Proteobacteria are also important in the nitrogen cycle, as they are able to convert atmospheric nitrogen into a form usable by other organisms.
* Proteobacteria are also a major component of lichens, algae, and protozoa.
* Viral structures and viral multiplication are also associated with Proteobacteria.
