

### Integration of Metabolism

1. **Catabolic and Anabolic Reactions:** Catabolic reactions involve the breakdown of large molecules into smaller molecules, releasing energy in the process. Anabolic reactions involve the building of large molecules from smaller molecules, requiring energy in the process. Enzymes are proteins that catalyze biochemical reactions, and are essential for metabolic processes. 

2. **Energy Production:** Carbohydrate metabolism involves the breakdown of carbohydrates into smaller molecules, releasing energy in the process. Lipid metabolism involves the breakdown of lipids into smaller molecules, releasing energy in the process. Protein catabolism involves the breakdown of proteins into smaller molecules, releasing energy in the process. 

3. **Metabolic Diversity:** Metabolic pathways involve the sequential processing of molecules, which can be either anabolic or catabolic. Different organisms have different metabolic pathways, enabling them to use different sources of energy and nutrients. 

4. **Integration of Metabolism:** Integration of metabolism is the process of combining different metabolic pathways to generate energy, synthesize molecules, and build complex structures. Integration of metabolism is essential for the survival of organisms, as it enables them to adapt to changing environments.
