
### Helminthes

Helminthes, also known as parasitic worms, are multicellular organisms that typically infect humans and other animals. They are classified as either flatworms or roundworms.

**Brief History of Microbiology**

Microbiology is the study of microorganisms, which are tiny organisms that can only be seen with a microscope. Microbiology has been studied since the 16th century, when Antonie van Leeuwenhoek first observed bacteria and other microorganisms.

**Types of Microorganisms**

Microorganisms include bacteria, viruses, fungi, protozoa, and helminthes. Bacteria are single-celled organisms that can exist in a wide variety of environments. Viruses are small particles of genetic material that can replicate only inside a host cell. Fungi are eukaryotic organisms that can exist in the form of single cells or colonies. Protozoa are single-celled eukaryotic organisms that can move independently.

**Basic Idea of Domain Bacteria, Proteobacteria, Non Proteobacteria Gram –ve and Gram +ve Bacteria**

Bacteria are classified into two domains: Bacteria and Archaea. Bacteria are further divided into two groups: Proteobacteria and non-Proteobacteria. Proteobacteria are further divided into five classes: Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, and Epsilon. Non-Proteobacteria are further divided into four classes: Cytophaga-Flavobacteria-Bacteroides, Spirochaetes, Chlorobi, and Firmicutes. Gram-positive bacteria have a thick peptidoglycan layer in their cell wall, while Gram-negative bacteria have a thin peptidoglycan layer.

**Lichens, Algae, Protozoa**

Lichens are composite organisms composed of a fungus and an alga. Algae are photosynthetic organisms that can exist in a wide variety of environments. Protozoa are single-celled eukaryotic organisms that can move independently.

**Viral Structures, Viral Multiplication**

Viruses are small particles of genetic material that can replicate only inside a host cell. Viruses have a capsid, which is a protein shell that encloses the genetic material. Viruses replicate by injecting their genetic material into a host cell and hijacking the cell's machinery to produce more viruses.

**Role of Microorganisms in the Production of Industrial Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals**

Microorganisms can be used in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals. For example, bacteria can be used to produce enzymes, antibiotics, and other industrial chemicals. Fungi can be used to produce antibiotics, steroids, and other pharmaceuticals.
