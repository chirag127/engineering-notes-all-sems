

### Enzymes for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Catabolic & Anabolic Reactions: Enzymes, Energy Production and Carbohydrate Metabolism

* Enzymes are proteins that act as catalysts in biochemical reactions. They speed up the rate of a reaction by lowering the activation energy needed for a reaction to take place. 
* Enzymes are specific to the reaction they catalyze, meaning that each enzyme can catalyze only one type of reaction.
* Catabolic reactions are those that break down large molecules into smaller molecules and release energy. Anabolic reactions are those that build up large molecules from smaller molecules and require energy. 
* Enzymes are involved in both catabolic and anabolic reactions.
* Carbohydrate metabolism is the process by which carbohydrates are broken down into smaller molecules and used for energy production. 
* Lipid and protein catabolism involve the breakdown of lipids and proteins into smaller molecules, which can then be used for energy production.
* The energy production mechanism involves the breakdown of molecules to form ATP (adenosine triphosphate), which is the energy currency of the cell. 
* Metabolic diversity refers to the variety of metabolic pathways that exist in different organisms. 
* Pathways of energy use involve the use of energy from catabolic reactions to power anabolic reactions. 
* Integration of metabolism is an important concept in the subject of Remedial Biology-II KCS, as it involves understanding how different metabolic pathways are connected and how they interact with each other.
