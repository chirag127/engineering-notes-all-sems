
### Arrangement of Bacterial Cells 

* Bacterial cells are typically spherical, rod-shaped, or spiral-shaped. 
* The arrangement of bacterial cells can vary, depending on the species. 
* Spherical bacterial cells, known as cocci, can be arranged singly, in pairs, in chains, or in clusters. 
* Rod-shaped bacterial cells, known as bacilli, can be arranged singly, in pairs, in short chains, or in long chains. 
* Spiral-shaped bacterial cells, known as spirilla, can be arranged in a variety of ways, including spirals, helices, and even clusters. 
* The arrangement of bacterial cells can be used to identify different species. 
* The arrangement of bacterial cells can also affect the ability of the cells to interact with their environment and to reproduce.
