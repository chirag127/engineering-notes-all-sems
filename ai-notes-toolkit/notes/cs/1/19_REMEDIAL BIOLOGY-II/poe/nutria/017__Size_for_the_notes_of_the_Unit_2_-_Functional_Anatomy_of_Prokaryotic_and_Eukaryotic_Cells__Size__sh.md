
### Size for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells: 

* Prokaryotic cells are typically very small, ranging from 0.2 to 2.0 micrometers in diameter. 
* Eukaryotic cells are typically larger, ranging from 10 to 100 micrometers in diameter. 
* Bacterial cells are typically spherical or rod-shaped, while eukaryotic cells can be spherical, rod-shaped, or flat. 
* Bacterial cells are typically arranged in clusters or chains, while eukaryotic cells are typically arranged in sheets or layers. 
* The structure and function of cells vary greatly depending on the type of cell. For example, prokaryotic cells have a cell wall and a single, circular chromosome, while eukaryotic cells have a nucleus and multiple linear chromosomes. 
* The structure and function of cells are important to understand in order to properly diagnose and treat diseases. Understanding the structure and function of cells can also help in the development of new treatments and therapies.
