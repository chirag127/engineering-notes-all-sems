### Catabolic & anabolic reactions for the notes of the Unit 3 - Catabolic & anabolic reactions: enzymes, energy production and carbohydrate metabolism. Lipid & protein catabolism, Energy production mechanism, metabolic diversity & pathways of energy use. Integration of metabolism in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-II KCS.

- Catabolic reactions: Breakdown of complex molecules into simpler ones. Releases energy. e.g. cellular respiration.
- Anabolic reactions: Synthesis of complex molecules from simpler ones. Requires energy. e.g. photosynthesis.
- Enzymes: Biological catalysts that speed up metabolic reactions. Have specific 3D shapes that fit with substrates.
- Energy production: Cellular respiration produces ATP from oxidation of glucose and other nutrients. Photosynthesis produces ATP and NADPH from light energy.
- Carbohydrate metabolism: Glycolysis breaks down glucose into pyruvate. Citric acid cycle and electron transport chain produce more ATP. Gluconeogenesis synthesizes glucose from pyruvate.
- Lipid catabolism: Lipids broken down into fatty acids and glycerol. Beta-oxidation breaks down fatty acids into acetyl-CoA.
- Protein catabolism: Proteins broken down into amino acids. Amino groups removed and used to synthesize other compounds. Remaining carbon skeletons converted to intermediates of citric acid cycle.
- Metabolic diversity: Variety of pathways enables organisms to use different nutrients for energy based on availability. Allows adaptation to environment.
- Energy use: ATP powers cellular processes. NADPH reduces equivalents transfer redox potential energy. Mechanisms regulate energy production and use.
