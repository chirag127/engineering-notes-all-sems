### Metabolic diversity & pathways of energy use

- Catabolic reactions: Breakdown of complex molecules into simpler ones releasing energy. eg. cellular respiration.
- Anabolic reactions: Synthesis of complex molecules from simpler ones requiring energy. eg. Photosynthesis.
- Enzymes: Biological catalysts that speed up metabolic reactions. They are protein in nature.
- Energy production: Via oxidative phosphorylation in mitochondria. It produces 38 ATP molecules via electron transport chain.
- Carbohydrate metabolism: Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, Pentose phosphate pathway. They produce energy, precursors for amino acids and nucleic acids.
- Lipid catabolism: Beta oxidation pathway. It produces energy and acetyl CoA.
- Protein catabolism: Produces amino acids which enter various metabolic pathways.
- Metabolic diversity: Presence of multiple pathways for utilizing nutrients reflecting adaptation to environment.
- Pathways of energy use: ATP is the universal energy currency. It powers cellular work.
