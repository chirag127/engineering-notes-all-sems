## Unit 4 - Energy Utilization:

- Structure of mitochondria: Double membrane organelle, inner membrane folded into cristae to increase surface area, matrix space containing enzymes and DNA.
- Cellular respiration: Process of oxidizing nutrients to produce ATP, includes glycolysis, Krebs cycle and electron transport system.
- Relationship of carbohydrate metabolism to other compounds: Carbohydrates broken down to pyruvate which enters Krebs cycle, produces NADH and FADH2 used in electron transport system, Krebs cycle also produces other precursor molecules for biosynthesis of other compounds.
- Glycolysis: Conversion of glucose to pyruvate, produces small amount of ATP and NADH, occurs in cytoplasm.
- Formation of acetyl co-A: Pyruvate oxidized to acetyl co-A which enters Krebs cycle, requires coenzymes NAD+ and CoA.
- Krebs cycle: Acetyl co-A oxidized to produce CO2, NADH and FADH2, occurs in matrix of mitochondria.
- Electron transport system and oxidative phosphorylation: NADH and FADH2 transfer electrons to electron carriers, energy used to pump protons and produce ATP, occurs in inner membrane of mitochondria.
- ATP: Adenosine triphosphate, primary energy currency of cells used to power cellular processes.
- Factors affecting respiration: Available oxygen, nutrients, enzymes, protons, ATP utilization, temperature, pH.
