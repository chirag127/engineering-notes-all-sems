### Basic idea of domain bacteria for the notes of the Unit 1 - Brief history of microbiology, Types of microorganisms, Basic idea of domain bacteria, proteobacteria, non proteobacteria Gram –ve and Gram +ve bacteria, lichens, algae, protozoa, helminthes, viral structures, viral multiplication, Role of microorganisms in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-II KCS.

* Domain bacteria: Unicellular prokaryotic microorganisms. Lack membrane bound organelles. Cell wall made up of peptidoglycan. Reproduce asexually by binary fission. Mostly aerobic or anaerobic. Examples: E.coli, Bacillus, Staphylococcus etc.
* Based on cell wall structure: Gram +ve bacteria: Thick peptidoglycan layer. Do not retain crystal violet stain. Examples: Staphylococcus, Streptococcus. Gram -ve bacteria: Thin peptidoglycan layer. Retain crystal violet stain. Examples: E.coli, Salmonella, Pseudomonas etc.
* Based on shape: Cocci (spherical), Bacilli (rod shaped), Spirilla (spiral shaped), Spirochetes (corkscrew shaped)
* Based on metabolism: Heterotrophs: Depend on organic carbon compounds as energy source. Autotrophs: Capable of fixing inorganic carbon and synthesizing organic compounds. Examples: Cyanobacteria. Chemolithotrophs: Derive energy from inorganic compounds. Photoautotrophs: Use light as energy source.
* Other types of microorganisms: Lichens (symbiotic association of algae and fungi), Algae (photosynthetic eukaryotes), Protozoa (unicellular eukaryotic animals), Helminthes (multicellular parasitic worms), Viruses (obligate intracellular parasites).
* Industrial and pharmaceutical applications: Production of enzymes, antibiotics, vitamins, biofertilizers, biopolymers etc.
