### Energy Production

- Cellular respiration: Breakdown of glucose to produce ATP. Anaerobic respiration in absence of oxygen produces less ATP. Aerobic respiration in presence of oxygen produces more ATP.
- Glycolysis: conversion of glucose to pyruvate. Produces 2 ATP, 2 NADH. Occurs in cytoplasm.
- Krebs cycle: pyruvate converted to acetyl CoA which enters Krebs cycle. Produces 2 ATP, 6 NADH, 2 FADH2. Occurs in mitochondria matrix.
- Electron transport chain: NADH and FADH2 transfer electrons to produce 34 ATP. Chemiosmotic process. Occurs in mitochondria inner membrane.
- Fermentation: Anaerobic breakdown of glucose to produce ATP in absence of oxygen. Examples: Lactic acid fermentation, Alcoholic fermentation. Produces less ATP than cellular respiration.
- Other sources: Beta oxidation of fatty acids, breakdown of proteins and amino acids. Produce lesser amounts of ATP than carbohydrates.
- ATP is the main energy currency of cells and is used to drive various cellular processes. The integration and coordination of catabolic and anabolic pathways is regulated by enzymes and hormones to meet the energy demands of cells.
