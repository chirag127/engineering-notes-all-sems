### Lipid & protein catabolism

- Lipids are catabolized through β-oxidation to produce acetyl CoA which enters TCA cycle.
- Triglycerides are first broken down into glycerol & fatty acids by lipases.
- Fatty acids undergo β-oxidation in mitochondria to produce acetyl CoA.
- Odd chain fatty acids produce propionyl CoA which is converted to succinyl CoA.
- Proteins are catabolized by proteases to produce amino acids which are deaminated to produce urea & enter TCA cycle or glycolysis.
- Amino groups are removed by transamination or deamination reactions.
- Ammonia produced is converted to urea in urea cycle for excretion.
- Catabolism of lipids & proteins provides energy & precursors for gluconeogenesis & other anabolic pathways.

Energy production mechanism, metabolic diversity & pathways of energy use. Integration of metabolism

- Cellular respiration & fermentation produce energy in the form of ATP from catabolism of carbohydrates, lipids & proteins.
- Cellular respiration is more efficient & produces more ATP than fermentation. It involves glycolysis, TCA cycle & electron transport chain.
- Metabolism is diverse & integrated. Substrates & products of one pathway are precursors/products of other pathways. This coordination is regulated by enzymes, hormones & cell's energy demands.
- ATP produced is used for various cellular processes like growth, division, transport, etc. Any excess ATP is stored as macromolecules like glycogen or fat.
