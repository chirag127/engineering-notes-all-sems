### Structure of mitochondria

- Mitochondria are double membrane-bound organelles found in the cytoplasm of eukaryotic cells.
- The outer membrane is smooth while the inner membrane is folded into inward folds called cristae that project into the matrix.
- The intermembrane space is the narrow space between the outer membrane and inner membrane.
- The matrix is the dense gel-like fluid enclosed by the inner membrane.
- The inner membrane contains enzymes and proteins required for synthesis of ATP through chemiosmosis.
- The cristae increase the surface area for the enzymes and proteins involved in cellular respiration.
- The number and shape of cristae varies depending on the energy requirements of the cell.
