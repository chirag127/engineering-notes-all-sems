## Unit 1 - Brief history of microbiology

- Antonie van Leeuwenhoek observed microorganisms in 1676 using his single-lens microscope
- Robert Hooke observed cellular structures in cork in 1665
- Louis Pasteur disproved spontaneous generation and proposed biogenesis - organisms arise from pre-existing organisms
- Robert Koch proposed criteria to establish causative relationship between microbes and disease in 1890s leading to germ theory of disease

## Types of microorganisms

- Bacteria: prokaryotic, unicellular, lacking membrane-bound organelles
- Archaea: prokaryotic, adapted to extreme environments
- Fungi: eukaryotic, obtain nutrients by absorption
- Protozoa: eukaryotic, unicellular, motile
- Algae: eukaryotic, photosynthetic
- Viruses: acellular, obligate intracellular parasites
- Helminths: multicellular worms
- Lichens: symbiotic association between fungi and algae
