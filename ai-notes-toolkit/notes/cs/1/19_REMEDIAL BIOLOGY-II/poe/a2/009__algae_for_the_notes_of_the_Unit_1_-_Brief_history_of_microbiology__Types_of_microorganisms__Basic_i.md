### Algae

- Algae are eukaryotic organisms that contain chlorophyll and carry out photosynthesis.
- They are found in aquatic as well as terrestrial environments.
- Based on their shape and structure, algae are classified into three main types:

1. Chlorophyta or green algae: They are mainly freshwater algae that are green in color due to the dominance of chlorophyll-a and chlorophyll-b. Eg: Ulothrix, spirogyra, volvox, etc.

2. Phaeophyta or brown algae: They are marine algae that have chlorophyll-a, chlorophyll-c, fucoxanthin (brown colored pigment) and carotenoids. Eg: Laminaria, Macrocystis, Sargassum, etc.

3. Rhodophyta or red algae: They are marine algae that contain chlorophyll-a, phycobiliproteins (red colored pigments) and carotenoids. Eg: Porphyra, Gracilaria, Gelidium, etc.

- Algae play an important role in aquatic food chains and are also commercially important as sources of food, fertilizers, and pharmaceutical products.
- They are also used as bioindicators to monitor water pollution.
