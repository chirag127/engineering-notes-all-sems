### Role of microorganisms in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals

- Microorganisms are used in industry to produce a variety of organic compounds including acids, growth stimulants, and enzymes .
- In some cases, the production results from an apparent accident in nature where an organism manufactures many thousands of times the amount necessary for its own metabolism .
- In the industrial process, microorganisms act like chemical factories. To be effective, they should liberate a large amount of a single product that can be efficiently isolated and purified .
- The microorganisms should be easy to maintain and cultivate, and should have genetic stability with infrequent mutations .
- Industrial microorganisms are used to produce many things, including food, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, and construction materials .
- Microorganisms can be genetically modified or engineered to aid in large-scale production .
