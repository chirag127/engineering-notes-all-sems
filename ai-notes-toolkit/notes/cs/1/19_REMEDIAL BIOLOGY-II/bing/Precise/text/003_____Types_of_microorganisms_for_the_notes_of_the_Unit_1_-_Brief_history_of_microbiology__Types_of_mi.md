### Types of Microorganisms

Microorganisms are divided into seven types: bacteria, archaea, protozoa, algae, fungi, viruses, and multicellular animal parasites (helminths). Each type has a characteristic cellular composition, morphology, mean of locomotion, and reproduction.

1. **Bacteria**: Microbiology came into being largely through studies of bacteria.
2. **Archaea**: Archaea are single-celled microorganisms that are similar to bacteria in size and shape.
3. **Protozoa**: Protozoa are unicellular eukaryotic microorganisms.
4. **Algae**: The cells of eukaryotic microbes are similar to plant and animal cells in that their DNA is enclosed within a nucleus.
5. **Fungi**: Fungi are eukaryotic organisms that include yeasts, molds, and mushrooms.
6. **Viruses**: Viruses are acellular microorganisms that are not composed of cells.
7. **Multicellular animal parasites (helminths)**: Helminths are multicellular parasitic worms.
