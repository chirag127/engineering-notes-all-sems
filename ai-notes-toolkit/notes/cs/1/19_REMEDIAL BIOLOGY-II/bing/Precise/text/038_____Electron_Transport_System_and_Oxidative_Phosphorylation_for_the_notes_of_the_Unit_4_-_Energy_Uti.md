### Electron Transport System and Oxidative Phosphorylation

- Oxidative phosphorylation is linked to a process known as electron transport.
- The electron transport system (also called the Electron Transport Chain, or ETC), located in the inner mitochondrial membrane, transfers electrons donated by the reduced molecules NADH and FADH2 through a series of electrons acceptors, to oxygen.
- Electrons are passed from one member of the transport chain to another in a series of redox reactions.
- Energy released in these reactions is captured as a proton gradient, which is then used to make ATP in a process called chemiosmosis.
- Together, the electron transport chain and chemiosmosis make up oxidative phosphorylation.
