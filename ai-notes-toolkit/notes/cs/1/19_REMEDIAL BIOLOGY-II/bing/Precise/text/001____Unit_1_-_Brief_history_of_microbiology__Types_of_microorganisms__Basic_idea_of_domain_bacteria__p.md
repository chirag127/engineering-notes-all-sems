## Unit 1 - Brief history of microbiology, Types of microorganisms, Basic idea of domain bacteria, proteobacteria, non proteobacteria Gram –ve and Gram +ve bacteria, lichens, algae, protozoa, helminthes, viral structures, viral multiplication, Role of microorganisms in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals

1. **Brief history of microbiology:** Microbiology is the study of microorganisms, which are unicellular or cell-cluster microscopic organisms. The field of microbiology can be traced back to the 1670s when Antonie van Leeuwenhoek, known as the "father of microbiology," first observed microorganisms using a microscope of his own design.
2. **Types of microorganisms:** There are several types of microorganisms, including bacteria, viruses, fungi, protozoa, and algae.
3. **Basic idea of domain bacteria:** Bacteria are single-celled prokaryotic organisms that belong to the domain Bacteria. They are found in almost every habitat on Earth and play important roles in various ecosystems.
4. **Proteobacteria:** Proteobacteria is a major phylum of gram-negative bacteria that includes a wide variety of pathogens, such as Escherichia coli and Salmonella.
5. **Non-proteobacteria Gram-negative bacteria:** Non-proteobacteria Gram-negative bacteria include several phyla of bacteria that are not closely related to Proteobacteria but share the characteristic of having a gram-negative cell wall.
6. **Gram-positive bacteria:** Gram-positive bacteria are a group of bacteria that have a thick peptidoglycan layer in their cell wall and stain purple when subjected to the Gram stain procedure.
7. **Lichens:** Lichens are symbiotic associations between a fungus and a photosynthetic partner, usually an alga or a cyanobacterium.
8. **Algae:** Algae are a diverse group of aquatic organisms that have the ability to conduct photosynthesis.
9. **Protozoa:** Protozoa are single-celled eukaryotic organisms that are classified as protists.
10. **Helminthes:** Helminthes are parasitic worms that belong to the phylum Platyhelminthes (flatworms) or Nematoda (roundworms).
11. **Viral structures:** Viruses are non-living infectious agents that consist of a nucleic acid core (DNA or RNA) surrounded by a protein coat.
12. **Viral multiplication:** Viruses replicate by taking over the host cell's machinery and using it to produce new virus particles.
13. **Role of microorganisms in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals:** Microorganisms play a crucial role in the production of various industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals, such as antibiotics, vaccines, and enzymes.
