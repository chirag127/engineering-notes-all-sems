### Lichens

- Lichens are a complex life form that is a symbiotic partnership of two separate organisms, a fungus and an alga.
- The dominant partner is the fungus, which gives the lichen the majority of its characteristics, from its thallus shape to its fruiting bodies.
- The alga can be either a green alga or a blue-green alga, otherwise known as cyanobacteria.
- There are about 15,000 species of lichens found worldwide and occur in a variety of environmental conditions.
- Lichens are pioneer species, among the first living things to grow on bare rock or areas denuded of life by a disaster.
- Lichens may have to compete with plants for access to sunlight, but because of their small size and slow growth, they thrive in places where higher plants have difficulty growing.
