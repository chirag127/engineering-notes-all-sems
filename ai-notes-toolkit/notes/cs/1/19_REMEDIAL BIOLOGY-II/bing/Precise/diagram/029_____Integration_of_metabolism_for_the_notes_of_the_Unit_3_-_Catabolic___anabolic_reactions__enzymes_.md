### Integration of metabolism

Unit 3 - Catabolic & anabolic reactions: enzymes, energy production and carbohydrate metabolism. Lipid & protein catabolism, Energy production mechanism, metabolic diversity & pathways of energy use. Integration of metabolism in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-II KCS

1. **Catabolic reactions** are the set of metabolic pathways that break down molecules into smaller units to release energy. These reactions involve the breakdown of complex organic molecules, such as carbohydrates, lipids, and proteins, into simpler molecules, such as carbon dioxide, water, and ammonia.

2. **Anabolic reactions** are the set of metabolic pathways that construct molecules from smaller units. These reactions require energy and are the opposite of catabolic reactions. Anabolic reactions involve the synthesis of complex molecules, such as proteins, nucleic acids, and polysaccharides, from simpler molecules.

3. **Enzymes** are biological catalysts that speed up the rate of chemical reactions in the body. They are essential for metabolism, as they allow the body to carry out chemical reactions quickly and efficiently.

4. **Energy production** in the body occurs through the breakdown of nutrients, such as carbohydrates, lipids, and proteins. This process releases energy in the form of ATP (adenosine triphosphate), which is used by cells to carry out their functions.

5. **Carbohydrate metabolism** involves the breakdown of carbohydrates into simpler molecules, such as glucose, which can be used by cells for energy. This process involves several steps, including glycolysis, the citric acid cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation.

6. **Lipid catabolism** involves the breakdown of lipids, such as fats and oils, into simpler molecules, such as fatty acids and glycerol. These molecules can then be used by cells for energy or for the synthesis of other molecules.

7. **Protein catabolism** involves the breakdown of proteins into amino acids, which can be used by cells for energy or for the synthesis of other molecules.

8. **Energy production mechanisms** in the body include the breakdown of nutrients, such as carbohydrates, lipids, and proteins, to release energy in the form of ATP. This process involves several steps, including glycolysis, the citric acid cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation.

9. **Metabolic diversity** refers to the variety of metabolic pathways that exist in different organisms. These pathways allow organisms to use different sources of energy and to adapt to different environments.

10. **Pathways of energy use** in the body include the breakdown of nutrients, such as carbohydrates, lipids, and proteins, to release energy in the form of ATP. This energy is then used by cells to carry out their functions.

11. **Integration of metabolism** refers to the coordination of the various metabolic pathways in the body. This allows the body to efficiently use energy and to maintain homeostasis. Integration of metabolism is essential for the proper functioning of the body.
