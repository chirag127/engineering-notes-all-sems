### Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells

Unit 2 - Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells: Size, shape, and arrangement of bacterial cells. Structure and function of cells in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-II KCS

1. Prokaryotic cells are smaller and simpler in structure than eukaryotic cells.
2. Prokaryotic cells lack a nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles.
3. The DNA of prokaryotic cells is located in the nucleoid region.
4. Prokaryotic cells have a cell wall that provides shape and protection.
5. The size, shape, and arrangement of bacterial cells can vary greatly.
6. Eukaryotic cells are larger and more complex than prokaryotic cells.
7. Eukaryotic cells have a nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles.
8. The DNA of eukaryotic cells is located within the nucleus.
9. Eukaryotic cells may or may not have a cell wall.
10. The structure and function of cells can vary greatly among different types of eukaryotes.
