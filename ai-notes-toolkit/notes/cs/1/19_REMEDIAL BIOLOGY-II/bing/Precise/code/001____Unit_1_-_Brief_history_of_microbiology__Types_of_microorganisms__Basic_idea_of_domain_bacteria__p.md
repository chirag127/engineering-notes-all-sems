## Unit 1 - Brief history of microbiology, Types of microorganisms, Basic idea of domain bacteria, proteobacteria, non proteobacteria Gram –ve and Gram +ve bacteria, lichens, algae, protozoa, helminthes, viral structures, viral multiplication, Role of microorganisms in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals

- **Microbiology** is the study of microorganisms, which are unicellular or cell-cluster microscopic organisms. This includes eukaryotes such as fungi and protists, and prokaryotes such as bacteria and certain algae.
- The field of microbiology can be traced back to the 1670s when Antonie van Leeuwenhoek, known as the "father of microbiology," first observed microorganisms using a microscope of his own design.
- **Types of microorganisms** include bacteria, archaea, fungi, protists, viruses, and microscopic animals such as rotifers.
- **Bacteria** are prokaryotic microorganisms that belong to the domain Bacteria. They are typically a few micrometers in length and have a wide range of shapes, including spheres, rods, and spirals.
- **Proteobacteria** is a major phylum of gram-negative bacteria that includes a wide variety of pathogens, such as Escherichia coli, Salmonella, and Helicobacter pylori.
- **Non-proteobacteria** gram-negative bacteria include the phyla Bacteroidetes, Fusobacteria, and Spirochaetes, among others.
- **Gram-negative** bacteria have a cell wall composed of a thin layer of peptidoglycan surrounded by an outer membrane, while **Gram-positive** bacteria have a thicker peptidoglycan layer and no outer membrane.
- **Lichens** are symbiotic associations between a fungus and a photosynthetic partner, usually an alga or a cyanobacterium.
- **Algae** are a diverse group of aquatic organisms that have the ability to conduct photosynthesis.
- **Protozoa** are unicellular eukaryotic organisms that are classified as protists.
- **Helminths** are parasitic worms that belong to the phylum Platyhelminthes (flatworms) or Nematoda (roundworms).
- **Viral structures** vary widely, but all viruses have a nucleic acid genome surrounded by a protein coat called a capsid.
- **Viral multiplication** involves the replication of the viral genome and the production of new viral particles within a host cell.
- **Microorganisms** play important roles in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals, including antibiotics, vaccines, and enzymes.
