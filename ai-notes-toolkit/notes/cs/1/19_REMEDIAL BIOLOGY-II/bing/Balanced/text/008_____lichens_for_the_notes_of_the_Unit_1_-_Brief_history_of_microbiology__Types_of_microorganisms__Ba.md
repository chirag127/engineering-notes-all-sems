### Lichens

- Lichens are **simple, slow-growing plants** that typically form a **low crusty, leaflike, or branching growth** on rocks, walls, and trees .
- Lichens are **composite organisms** that consist of a **symbiotic association** of algae (usually green) or cyanobacteria and fungi (mostly ascomycetes and basidiomycetes) .
- Lichens are **found worldwide** and occur in a variety of environmental conditions.
- Lichens have different forms, such as **foliose** (leaflike), **crustose** (crusty), or **fruticose** (shrublike) .
- Lichens often play an important part in the **weathering of rocks** and include some that are sources of **natural dyes**.
- Lichens are also used as **indicators of air quality**, as they are sensitive to pollutants.
- Lichens have some **medicinal properties**, such as antibacterial, antifungal, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant effects.
- Lichens are also a source of **food and shelter** for some animals, such as insects, birds, and mammals.
