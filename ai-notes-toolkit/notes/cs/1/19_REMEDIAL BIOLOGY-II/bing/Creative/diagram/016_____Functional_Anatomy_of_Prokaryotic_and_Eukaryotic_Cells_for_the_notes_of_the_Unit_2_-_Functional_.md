# Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells

## Size, shape, and arrangement of bacterial cells

- Bacterial cells are prokaryotic cells, which means they lack a nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles .
- Bacterial cells are typically much smaller than eukaryotic cells, ranging from 0.2 to 10 micrometers in diameter .
- Bacterial cells can have various shapes, such as spherical (cocci), rod-shaped (bacilli), spiral (spirilla), comma-shaped (vibrios), or corkscrew-shaped (spirochetes) .
- Bacterial cells can also form different arrangements, such as pairs (diplo-), chains (strepto-), clusters (staphylo-), or tetrads (groups of four) .
