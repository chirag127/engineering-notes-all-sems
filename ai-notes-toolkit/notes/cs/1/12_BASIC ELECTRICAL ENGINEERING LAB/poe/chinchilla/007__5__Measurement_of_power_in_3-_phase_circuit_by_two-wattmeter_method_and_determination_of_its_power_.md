##### Introduction
In three-phase electrical systems, the measurement of power is crucial for determining the efficiency and performance of the system. One of the methods used for measuring power in three-phase circuits is the two-wattmeter method. This method is used for both star and delta connected loads and can also be used to determine the power factor of the circuit.

##### Two-Wattmeter Method
The two-wattmeter method is a technique used to measure power in three-phase circuits. This method involves the use of two wattmeters, which are connected to the circuit in such a way that they measure the power in two different phases. The readings obtained from the two wattmeters are then used to calculate the total power in the circuit.

##### Star Connected Load
For a star connected load, the two wattmeters are connected as follows:
- Wattmeter 1 is connected between the phase A and neutral.
- Wattmeter 2 is connected between the phase B and neutral.

The total power in the circuit can be calculated using the following formula:
- Total power = W1 + W2

Where W1 and W2 are the readings obtained from wattmeter 1 and wattmeter 2, respectively.

##### Delta Connected Load
For a delta connected load, the two wattmeters are connected as follows:
- Wattmeter 1 is connected between phase A and phase B.
- Wattmeter 2 is connected between phase B and phase C.

The total power in the circuit can be calculated using the following formula:
- Total power = (W1 + W2) x √3

Where W1 and W2 are the readings obtained from wattmeter 1 and wattmeter 2, respectively.

##### Power Factor
The power factor of a circuit is a measure of the efficiency of the circuit in converting electrical power into useful work. The power factor can be calculated using the readings obtained from the two wattmeters. The power factor can be calculated using the following formula:

- Power Factor = (W1 - W2) / (W1 + W2)

Where W1 and W2 are the readings obtained from wattmeter 1 and wattmeter 2, respectively.

##### Conclusion
The two-wattmeter method is a simple and effective method for measuring power in three-phase circuits.
