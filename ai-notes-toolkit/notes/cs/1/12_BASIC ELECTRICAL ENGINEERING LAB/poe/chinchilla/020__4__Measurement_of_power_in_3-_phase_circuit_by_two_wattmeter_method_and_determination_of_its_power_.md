#### Measurement of power in 3-phase circuit by two wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

Power measurement is an important aspect of electrical engineering. In a three-phase circuit, power can be measured by using the two wattmeter method. This method is commonly used for measuring power in both star and delta connected loads. Let's take a look at the steps involved in this method.

1. **Connection of Wattmeters:** The first step is to connect the two wattmeters to the three-phase circuit. The wattmeters are connected in such a way that one is connected between the first and second phases, and the other is connected between the second and third phases. The third phase is not connected to any of the wattmeters. 

2. **Measurement of Wattmeter Readings:** Once the wattmeters are connected, the next step is to measure their readings. The wattmeter connected between the first and second phases will measure the power consumed by the load connected between those two phases. Similarly, the wattmeter connected between the second and third phases will measure the power consumed by the load connected between those two phases. 

3. **Calculation of Total Power:** The total power consumed by the three-phase load can be calculated using the formula:
   `Total Power = W1 + W2 + 3VILcos(θ)`
   where W1 and W2 are the readings of the two wattmeters, V is the line voltage, I is the line current, and θ is the phase angle between the voltage and current.

4. **Calculation of Power Factor:** The power factor of the three-phase load can be determined using the formula:
   `Power Factor = (W1 - W2) / (W1 + W2)`
   where W1 and W2 are the readings of the two wattmeters.

5. **Star Connection:** In a star connected load, the line current is equal to the phase current. Therefore, the power factor can be calculated directly using the above formula.

6. **Delta Connection:** In a delta connected load, the line current is √3 times the phase current. Therefore, the power factor calculated using the above formula needs to be multiplied by √3 to get the correct value.

In conclusion, the two wattmeter method is a simple and effective way to measure power in three-phase circuits. By using this method, we can also determine the power factor of the load, which is an important parameter in electrical engineering.
