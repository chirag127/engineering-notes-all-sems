#### 4. Connection and measurement of power consumption of a fluorescent lamp (tube light)

Fluorescent lamps or tube lights are widely used for various lighting applications due to their energy-efficient nature. Understanding how to connect and measure their power consumption is crucial for their proper usage. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

1. Connection of fluorescent lamp:
   - Fluorescent lamps require a ballast to regulate the current flow and provide the necessary voltage for starting and running the lamp.
   - The ballast should be compatible with the lamp's wattage and type (T5, T8, etc.).
   - The lamp and ballast should be connected according to the manufacturer's instructions.
   - It is important to ensure that the lamp is securely seated in the lamp holder before switching on the power.

2. Measuring power consumption:
   - Power consumption can be measured using a wattmeter or a multimeter.
   - To measure the power consumption, the lamp should be connected to the meter in series with the power supply.
   - The meter should be set to measure the AC voltage and current in the circuit.
   - The power consumption can be calculated using the formula P = V x I, where P is the power, V is the voltage, and I is the current.
   - It is important to measure the power consumption of the lamp regularly to ensure that it is operating within its rated power and to monitor any changes in the lamp's performance.

3. Factors affecting power consumption:
   - The power consumption of a fluorescent lamp depends on various factors such as the lamp's wattage, ballast type and efficiency, and ambient temperature.
   - The power consumption may increase if the lamp is operated at a higher voltage than its rated voltage.
   - The lamp's power consumption may decrease with age due to the degradation of the lamp's phosphor coating.
   - The ambient temperature can affect the lamp's power consumption, with higher temperatures leading to increased power consumption.

In conclusion, understanding how to connect and measure the power consumption of a fluorescent lamp is essential for its proper functioning and maintenance. By following the manufacturer's instructions, using the appropriate equipment, and monitoring the lamp's performance, it is possible to ensure efficient and safe operation of the lamp.
