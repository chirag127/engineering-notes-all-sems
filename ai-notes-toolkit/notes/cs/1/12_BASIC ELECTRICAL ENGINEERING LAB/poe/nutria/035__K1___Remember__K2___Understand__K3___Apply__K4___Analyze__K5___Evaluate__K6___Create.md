

### K1 – Remember
- Remembering is the ability to recall and recall information.
- It involves the use of memory strategies such as repetition, organization, and visualization.
- Remembering is important for understanding concepts and retaining knowledge.

### K2 – Understand
- Understanding is the ability to comprehend and interpret information.
- It involves the use of higher-order thinking skills such as analysis, synthesis, and evaluation.
- Understanding is important for problem solving and making decisions.

### K3 – Apply
- Applying is the ability to use knowledge and skills in a new context.
- It involves the use of practical skills such as problem solving and critical thinking.
- Applying is important for developing new solutions and adapting to changing environments.

### K4 – Analyze
- Analyzing is the ability to break down information into its component parts.
- It involves the use of analytical skills such as comparison, classification, and deduction.
- Analyzing is important for understanding complex concepts and making sense of data.

### K5 – Evaluate
- Evaluating is the ability to assess the quality of information.
- It involves the use of evaluation skills such as judgment, reflection, and comparison.
- Evaluating is important for making informed decisions and determining the accuracy of information.

### K6 – Create
- Creating is the ability to generate new ideas and solutions.
- It involves the use of creative skills such as imagination, innovation, and experimentation.
- Creating is important for developing new products and services and advancing the state of knowledge.
