
#### Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Level

- **Remember**: Students will recall or recognize facts, terms, concepts, and answers.
- **Understand**: Students will demonstrate understanding of facts, terms, concepts, and answers.
- **Apply**: Students will use information to solve problems in new or familiar situations.
- **Analyze**: Students will break down information into parts and recognize relationships among the parts.
- **Evaluate**: Students will make judgments about the value of ideas or materials.
- **Create**: Students will put elements together to form a coherent or functional whole; to generate a new pattern or structure.
