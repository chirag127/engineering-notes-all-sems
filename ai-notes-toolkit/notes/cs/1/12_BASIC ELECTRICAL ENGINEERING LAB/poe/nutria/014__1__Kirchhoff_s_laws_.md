
#### 1. Kirchhoff's Laws
1. **Kirchhoff's Current Law** (KCL): The algebraic sum of currents entering and leaving any node (junction) is zero.

2. **Kirchhoff's Voltage Law** (KVL): The algebraic sum of the voltages around any closed loop is zero.

3. **Kirchhoff's Junction Rule** (KJR): The sum of currents entering a junction is equal to the sum of currents leaving the junction.

4. **Kirchhoff's Loop Rule** (KLR): The algebraic sum of the voltage drops in any closed loop is equal to the algebraic sum of the emf's in the same loop.
