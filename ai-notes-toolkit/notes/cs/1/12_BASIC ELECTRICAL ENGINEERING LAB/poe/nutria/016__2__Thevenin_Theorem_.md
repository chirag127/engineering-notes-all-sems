
##### 2. Thevenin Theorem

* Thevenin Theorem states that any linear, active, two-terminal network can be reduced to an equivalent circuit consisting of a single voltage source in series with a single impedance. 
* The equivalent voltage source is the open-circuit voltage at the two terminals and the equivalent impedance is the impedance looking into the two terminals when all sources are turned off. 
* The Thevenin equivalent circuit can be used to find the current through any load connected between the two terminals. 
* Thevenin Theorem can be used to simplify complex electrical networks and reduce the number of components needed to analyze a circuit. 
* Thevenin Theorem can also be used to calculate the voltage across a component in a circuit when the current through the component is known.
