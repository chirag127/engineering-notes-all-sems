
##### 3. Study of phenomenon of resonance in RLC series circuit and obtain resonant frequency

1. Resonance is the phenomenon of increased amplitude of oscillations in an RLC series circuit when the frequency of the applied voltage is equal to the resonant frequency of the circuit.

2. The resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit is determined by the values of the inductance (L), capacitance (C) and resistance (R) in the circuit.

3. The resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit is given by the formula: 

$$f_r = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$$

4. The resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit can be calculated by substituting the values of the inductance, capacitance and resistance into the above formula.

5. The resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit can also be determined experimentally by measuring the frequency at which the amplitude of oscillations in the circuit is maximum.
