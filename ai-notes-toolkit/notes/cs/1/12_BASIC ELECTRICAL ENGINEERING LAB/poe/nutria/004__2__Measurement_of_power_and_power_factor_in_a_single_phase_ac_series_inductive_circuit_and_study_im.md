

##### 2. Measurement of power and power factor in a single phase ac series inductive circuit and study improvement of power factor using capacitor

1. Power factor is the ratio between the real power and the apparent power in an AC circuit.

2. In a single-phase AC series inductive circuit, the power factor is always less than one.

3. The power factor in a single-phase AC series inductive circuit can be improved by adding a capacitor in parallel with the load.

4. The value of the capacitor should be calculated based on the load current, the line voltage, and the power factor desired.

5. The power factor can be measured by using a wattmeter or a power factor meter.

6. The wattmeter is used to measure the real power in the circuit, while the power factor meter is used to measure the power factor.

7. The power factor can be improved by adding a capacitor in parallel with the load. The capacitor should be sized based on the load current, line voltage, and desired power factor.
