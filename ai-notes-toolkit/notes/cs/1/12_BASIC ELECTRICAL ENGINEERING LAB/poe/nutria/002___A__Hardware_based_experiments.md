
#### (A) Hardware based experiments

1. Hardware based experiments involve the use of physical hardware to measure and record data. 
2. This type of experiment is typically used to study physical phenomena, such as the behavior of a system or the properties of a material.
3. Common hardware used in experiments include sensors, transducers, and other measuring devices, as well as computers, microcontrollers, and other data-logging systems.
4. Experiments can be conducted in a laboratory setting or in the field.
5. In order to ensure accuracy and reliability, experiments must be carefully designed, taking into account factors such as the type of hardware being used and the environment in which the experiment is being conducted.
6. Data collected from hardware based experiments must be carefully analyzed in order to draw meaningful conclusions.
