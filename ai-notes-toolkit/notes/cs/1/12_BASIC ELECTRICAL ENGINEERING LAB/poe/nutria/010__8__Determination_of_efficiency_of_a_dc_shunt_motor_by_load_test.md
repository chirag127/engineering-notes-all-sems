
##### 8. Determination of efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test

1. A dc shunt motor is an electric motor that works on direct current (DC) and has its field windings connected in parallel to the armature windings.
2. The efficiency of a dc shunt motor can be determined by a load test.
3. During a load test, the motor is connected to a DC power supply and its speed and torque are measured while the load is varied.
4. The efficiency of the motor is calculated by comparing the input power to the output power.
5. The input power is calculated by multiplying the voltage, current and power factor of the motor.
6. The output power is calculated by multiplying the torque and speed of the motor.
7. The efficiency of the motor is calculated by dividing the output power by the input power.
8. The efficiency of the motor can be improved by using a higher voltage power supply and by increasing the power factor of the motor.
