##### 9. To study running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions.

- Connect the three phase induction motor to the three phase supply.
- Note down the specifications of the motor such as voltage, current, number of poles, etc.
- Run the motor in the forward direction by connecting the supply to the stator winding terminals.
- Measure and record the speed of the motor using a tachometer in the forward direction.
- Reverse the direction of the motor by interchanging the connections of any two supply lines.
- Measure and record the speed of the motor in the reverse direction using the tachometer.
- Compare the speed in both forward and reverse directions. The speed in the reverse direction will be slightly less than the forward speed.
- The difference in speeds in both directions is due to the structural asymmetry and winding inductances of the motor.
