#### (A) Hardware based experiments.

1. Ohm's law experiment:
- Take a voltage source, ampere meter, voltmeter and a resistor of known value.
- Connect them in a circuit and note down the current and voltage readings.
- Calculate the resistance using V=IR and verify with the known resistance value.
- This verifies Ohm's law which forms the basis for analysis of electrical circuits.

2. Study of volt-ampere characteristics of a diode:
- Take a diode and connect it to a voltage source and ampere meter.
- Note the current readings for various voltages applied.
- Plot a graph with voltage on x-axis and current on y-axis.
- This gives the volt-ampere characteristic of diode which is needed to analyze diode based circuits.

3. Study of volt-ampere characteristics of a transistor:
- Take a transistor and connect it to a voltage source and ampere meter.
- Note the current readings for various voltages applied.
- Plot a graph with voltage on x-axis and current on y-axis.
- This gives the volt-ampere characteristic of transistor which is needed to analyze transistor based circuits.
