##### CO 4 Calculate efficiency of a single phase transformer and DC machine. K4

1. Single phase transformer efficiency
- Copper loss = I^2*R
- Iron loss = Kh*f*Bm
- Efficiency = Output power/Input power
- Input power = Output power + Losses
- Losses = Copper loss + Iron loss
- Calculate efficiency for different loads and draw efficiency vs load curve.

2. DC machine efficiency
- Copper loss = I^2*R
- Iron loss = Kh*f*Bm
- Mechanical loss = Friction and windage losses
- Efficiency = Output power/Input power
- Input power = Output power + Losses
- Losses = Copper loss + Iron loss + Mechanical loss
- Calculate efficiency for different loads and draw efficiency vs load curve.
