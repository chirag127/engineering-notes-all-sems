#### CO 5 Perform experiments on speed measurement and reversal of direction of three phase induction motor and Identify the type of DC and AC machines based on their construction. K4

- Speed measurement of 3 phase induction motor:
-- Connect the 3 phase supply to the induction motor.
-- Note down the frequency of supply and number of poles of the motor.
-- Calculate the synchronous speed(Ns) using the formula: Ns=120xf/P
-- Run the motor and measure the actual speed(Na) using a tachometer.
-- Compare the actual and synchronous speed to check the slip and torque developed.

- Reversing the direction of 3 phase induction motor:
-- Interchange any two supply lines to reverse the direction of rotation.
-- The sequence of interchanging supply lines will decide the direction of rotation.

- Identifying DC machines:
-- Based on field excitation: Separately excited, Series and Shunt.
-- Based on commutation: Brush type - Brushless.

- Identifying AC machines:
-- Based on rotor construction: Induction motor, Synchronous motor.
