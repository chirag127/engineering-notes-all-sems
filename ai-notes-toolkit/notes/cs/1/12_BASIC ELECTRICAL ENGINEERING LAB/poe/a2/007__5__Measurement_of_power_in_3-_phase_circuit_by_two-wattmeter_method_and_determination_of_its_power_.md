##### 5. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two-wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

- Connect the 3-phase supply to the load in star or delta connection.
- Connect two wattmeters in the two lines of the 3-phase supply. The third phase is not connected to any wattmeter.
- Note down the readings of both wattmeters.
- Calculate the power as:
Power = Wattmeter 1 reading + Wattmeter 2 reading
- The power factor can be calculated as:
Power factor = Wattmeter 1 reading / Total power
- Draw the circuit diagram and mark the wattmeter readings and power calculations.
- The power factor can be lagging, leading or unity depending on whether the wattmeter readings are unbalanced or same.
- Compare the power factor for star and delta connected loads and note down the observations.
- The two-wattmeter method can only be used for balanced 3-phase loads. For unbalanced loads, the 3-wattmeter method is used.
