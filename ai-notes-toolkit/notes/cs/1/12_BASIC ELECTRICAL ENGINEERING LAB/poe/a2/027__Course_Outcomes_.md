#### Course Outcomes:

1. Learn the core concepts and fundamentals of software engineering.
- Understand the software development lifecycle models.
- Gain hands-on experience with the software engineering processes.
- Learn to apply design principles and patterns to solve problems.
- Gain proficiency in at least one programming language.
- Learn to evaluate and apply software engineering technologies and tools.
- Develop skills to work effectively on teams to build software solutions.

2. Learn to apply technical skills to solve real-world problems.
- Analyze complex, ill-defined problems and apply programming techniques to achieve solutions.
- Design, develop, test, and debug software applications/systems.
- Recognize and address ethical, legal, and social issues that may impact systems.

3. Develop professional and interpersonal skills that are crucial for software engineers.
- Communicate effectively, both written and oral.
- Exhibit leadership abilities and teamwork skills.
- Continue learning and stay up-to-date with advancements in the field.
- Practice technical writing to create reports, documentation, and specifications.

4. Broaden knowledge of other disciplines that interact with software engineering.
- Understand the role of software engineering in scientific and business domains.
- Recognize the connections between software engineering and other engineering fields.
- Recognize the influence of technologies from other fields on software engineering.
