##### 10. Demonstration of cut-out sections of machines: dc machine, three phase induction machine, single-phase induction machine and synchronous machine.

1. DC Machine
- Cut section of DC machine showing armature core with slots to carry armature conductors, commutator to reverse current direction, field poles with field coils and brushes.
- Explain the function of each part.

2. Three phase induction machine
- Cut section showing stator with three phase windings and rotor with squirrel cage or winding.
- Explain the operation of three phase induction machine based on rotating magnetic field and induction of current in rotor.

3. Single-phase induction machine
- Cut section showing single phase winding on stator and squirrel cage on rotor. Explain the split ring arrangement to introduce phase difference for rotating magnetic field.
- Explain the operation based on double revolving field theory.

4. Synchronous machine
- Cut section showing field poles, field winding, armature core with 3 phase windings mounted on rotor shaft.
- Explain the operation based on rotating magnetic field produced by armature currents and constant magnetic field produced by field currents.
