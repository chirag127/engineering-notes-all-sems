##### 2. Measurement of power and power factor in a single phase ac series inductive circuit and study improvement of power factor using capacitor.

1. Measurement of power in an inductive ac circuit:
- Active power (P) = V x I x Cosθ
- Reactive power (Q) = V x I x Sinθ
- Apparent power (S) = V x I
- Power factor (PF) = Cosθ

Where V is supply voltage, I is current and θ is the phase angle between V and I.

2. Power factor improvement using capacitor:
- A capacitor is connected in parallel with the inductive load.
- This compensates the reactive power component of the load and improves the power factor.
- The value of the capacitor is chosen such that it cancels the effect of inductive reactance of the load. This results in a minimum phase difference between current and voltage and hence improves the power factor.

3. Benefits of improved power factor:
- Reduced kVA rating of equipment and supply systems.
- Increased efficiency.
- Fewer voltage drops.
- Lesser heating of equipment.
- Lower electricity bills.
