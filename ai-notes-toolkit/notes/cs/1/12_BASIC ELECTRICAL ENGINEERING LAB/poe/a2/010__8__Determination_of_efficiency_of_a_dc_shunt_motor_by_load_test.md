##### 8. Determination of efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test.

- Connect the dc shunt motor to a dc supply and a suitable load like a rheostat.
- Note down the reading of armature current (Ia) and field current (If) along with the load voltage (V) and load current (Il) for different loads.
- For each load, calculate the input power (Pi) and output power (Po).
- Calculate the efficiency (eta) using the formula:

eta = (Po/Pi) x 100

- Plot a graph between efficiency and load current. The maximum point in the graph gives the maximum efficiency of the motor.
- The losses in the motor can be determined from the input and output power at different loads.
- This method, though practical, does not give accurate results due to stray load losses. More precise methods like Hopkinson's method or loading back method should be used for accurate determination of motor efficiency.
