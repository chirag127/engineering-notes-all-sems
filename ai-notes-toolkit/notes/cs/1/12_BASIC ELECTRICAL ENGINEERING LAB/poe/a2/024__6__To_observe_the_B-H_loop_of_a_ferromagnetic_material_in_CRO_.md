##### 6. To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in CRO.

1. Connect the CRO in X-Y mode.
2. Connect the material (ferromagnetic) whose B-H loop is to be studied in series with X-coil of CRO. Connect a variable resistance in series.
3. Connect a DC power supply in series with Y-coil of CRO and variable resistance.
4. Start increasing the current gradually through ferromagnetic material and note the corresponding magnetic field and magnetic induction values by noting the deflections produced in X and Y coils of CRO respectively.
5. Note down the readings of magnetic field and magnetic induction values corresponding to the increasing and decreasing current values to get the complete B-H loop.
6. The shape of the B-H loop gives an idea about the magnetic properties of the material. A rectangular loop indicates ideal ferromagnetic behavior. The actual shape is a hysteresis loop.

The above points explain the procedure to observe and plot the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material using a CRO in X-Y mode. The shape and specifics of the B-H loop can reveal details about the magnetic characteristics of the material. This experiment helps understand ferromagnetic behavior and hysteresis.
