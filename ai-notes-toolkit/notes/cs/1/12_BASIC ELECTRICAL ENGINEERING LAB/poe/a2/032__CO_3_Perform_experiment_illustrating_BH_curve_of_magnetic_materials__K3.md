##### CO 3 Perform experiment illustrating BH curve of magnetic materials. K3

- Take a toroid of ferromagnetic material like soft iron. Wind a coil of insulated copper wire over it. Connect the coil to a battery and an ammeter.
- Note the ammeter reading for different values of applied voltage. Note the corresponding magnetic field using a hall probe or magnetometer.
- Plot a graph between magnetic field B and magnetization M. The curve so obtained is the BH curve or hysteresis loop.
- The curve is not a straight line. It consists of a lower branch when B increases and an upper branch when B decreases. The curves meet at two points representing coercivity and remanence.
- Coercivity is the value of reversing magnetic field. Remanence is the magnetization left after removing the magnetizing field. The area of the loop represents energy loss during magnetization and demagnetization.
- The experiment illustrates the magnetic properties like hysteresis, coercivity and remanence in magnetic materials which are used in devices like transformers, motors, relays, etc.
