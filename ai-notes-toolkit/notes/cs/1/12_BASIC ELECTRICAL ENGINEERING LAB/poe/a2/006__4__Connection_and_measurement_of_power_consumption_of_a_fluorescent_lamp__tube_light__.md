##### 4. Connection and measurement of power consumption of a fluorescent lamp (tube light).

1. Connect the fluorescent lamp (tube light) to a power supply with a voltmeter and ammeter in series with proper connections as shown in the circuit diagram.

2. Note down the voltage across the lamp and current through the lamp. These values can be noted from the voltmeter and ammeter readings respectively.

3. Calculate the power consumption of the fluorescent lamp using the formula:
Power (in watts) = Voltage (in volts) x Current (in amperes)

4. Repeat the measurements and calculations for different ratings of fluorescent lamps. Tabulate the readings and calculated power consumptions.

5. Plot a graph with power consumption on the y-axis and rating of the fluorescent lamp on the x-axis. The nature of the graph will be a straight line passing through the origin since power consumption is directly proportional to the rating of the fluorescent lamp.
