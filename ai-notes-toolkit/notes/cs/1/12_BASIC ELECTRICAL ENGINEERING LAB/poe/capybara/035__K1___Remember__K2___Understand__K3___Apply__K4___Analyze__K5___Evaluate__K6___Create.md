#### K1 – Remember
- This level requires the student to recall or retrieve previously learned information.
- Techniques such as repetition, mnemonics, and flashcards can help in retaining information.
- Memorization is an essential skill for subjects such as history, language, and sciences.

#### K2 – Understand
- This level requires the student to comprehend or explain the meaning of the information.
- Techniques such as note-taking, summarization, and visualization can help in understanding complex concepts.
- Understanding is an essential skill for subjects such as math, literature, and philosophy.

#### K3 – Apply
- This level requires the student to use the information in a new or different context.
- Techniques such as problem-solving, experimentation, and simulation can help in applying the learned knowledge.
- Application is an essential skill for subjects such as engineering, business, and computer science.

#### K4 – Analyze
- This level requires the student to break down or examine the information to understand its parts and relationships.
- Techniques such as comparison, classification, and deduction can help in analyzing information.
- Analysis is an essential skill for subjects such as law, psychology, and social sciences.

#### K5 – Evaluate
- This level requires the student to make judgments or decisions based on the information and criteria.
- Techniques such as critical thinking, decision-making, and reflection can help in evaluating information.
- Evaluation is an essential skill for subjects such as ethics, politics, and arts.

#### K6 – Create
- This level requires the student to use the information to generate new ideas, products, or solutions.
- Techniques such as brainstorming, design thinking, and prototyping can help in creating new things.
- Creation is an essential skill for subjects such as entrepreneurship, innovation, and arts.
