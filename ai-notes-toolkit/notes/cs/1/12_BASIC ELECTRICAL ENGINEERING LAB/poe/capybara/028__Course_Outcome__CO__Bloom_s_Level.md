#### Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Level

Bloom’s level is a framework used to categorize educational goals and objectives. It is a tool used by educators to design assessments, create lesson plans, and evaluate student learning. There are six levels of Bloom’s taxonomy: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. Each level corresponds to a different cognitive process that students must engage in to achieve the desired learning outcomes. Below are the course outcomes for each level of Bloom’s taxonomy:

##### Remembering
- Students will be able to recall important concepts, facts, and information related to the course material.
- Students will be able to memorize and recite key terms, definitions, and formulas.
- Students will be able to identify and label important elements within the course material.

##### Understanding
- Students will be able to explain the meaning of key concepts and ideas.
- Students will be able to summarize important information and ideas from the course material.
- Students will be able to compare and contrast different ideas and concepts within the course material.

##### Applying
- Students will be able to use their understanding of the course material to solve problems and complete tasks.
- Students will be able to apply course concepts and principles to real-world scenarios.
- Students will be able to demonstrate their ability to use the course material in practical applications.

##### Analyzing
- Students will be able to break down complex ideas and concepts into smaller parts.
- Students will be able to identify patterns and relationships within the course material.
- Students will be able to evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of different arguments and theories presented in the course material.

##### Evaluating
- Students will be able to make judgments about the quality and value of the course material.
- Students will be able to evaluate the effectiveness of different strategies and approaches used within the course material.
- Students will be able to critique and analyze different viewpoints presented in the course material.

##### Creating
- Students will be able to use their knowledge and understanding of the course material to create new ideas, products, or solutions.
- Students will be able to design and implement original projects and presentations based on the course material.
- Students will be able to generate new ways of thinking about the course material and its applications.
