##### 9. To study running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions.

In order to understand the running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor, it is important to follow the guidelines mentioned below:

- Firstly, connect a three phase induction motor to a three phase AC supply. Ensure that the motor is properly grounded.
- Start the motor and measure the initial speed using a tachometer. Record this value.
- Now, reverse the direction of the motor by interchanging any two of the three supply phase wires. The motor should now run in the opposite direction.
- Measure the speed of the motor again using the tachometer. Record this value as well.
- Repeat the above steps multiple times to get an average value for both the forward and reverse directions.
- Analyze the data collected to understand the running characteristics and speed reversal of the three phase induction motor.
- Make a note of any differences observed in the motor's performance while running in the forward and reverse directions.
- Also, study the effects of changing the supply frequency and voltage on the motor's performance.
- Finally, draw conclusions based on the data collected and observations made during the experiment.

By following the above guidelines and thoroughly analyzing the data collected, one can gain a clear understanding of the running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor.
