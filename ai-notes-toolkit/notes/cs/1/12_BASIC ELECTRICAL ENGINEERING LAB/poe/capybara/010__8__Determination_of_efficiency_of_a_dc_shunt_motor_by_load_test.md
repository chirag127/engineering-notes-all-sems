##### 8. Determination of efficiency of a DC shunt motor by load test

In order to determine the efficiency of a DC shunt motor, a load test is performed. The load test is carried out by applying a mechanical load to the motor and measuring the corresponding electrical parameters.

The following points explain the procedure for determining the efficiency of a DC shunt motor by load test:

1. Firstly, the motor is connected to a DC power supply and its rated voltage is applied.

2. The speed of the motor is measured using a tachometer.

3. A suitable load is applied to the motor and the current drawn by the motor is measured using an ammeter.

4. The voltage across the motor terminals is measured using a voltmeter.

5. The power input to the motor is calculated using the formula P = VI, where P is the power input, V is the voltage across the motor terminals, and I is the current drawn by the motor.

6. The output power of the motor is calculated using the formula Pout = Tω, where Pout is the output power, T is the torque developed by the motor, and ω is the angular speed of the motor.

7. The efficiency of the motor is calculated using the formula η = Pout/Pin x 100%, where η is the efficiency of the motor, Pout is the output power of the motor, and Pin is the power input to the motor.

8. The load is varied and the above steps are repeated to obtain efficiency values at different loads.

9. A graph is plotted with load on the x-axis and efficiency on the y-axis.

10. The maximum efficiency point is determined from the graph.

11. The efficiency of the motor at the maximum efficiency point is noted as the overall efficiency of the motor.

By performing a load test on a DC shunt motor, its efficiency can be determined accurately. This information can be used for selecting the appropriate motor for a specific application or for optimizing the performance of an existing motor.
