##### 7. Determination of (i) Voltage ratio (ii) polarity and (iii) efficiency by load test of a single phase Transformer.

1. Load test is an important test conducted on a single phase transformer to determine various parameters such as voltage ratio, polarity, and efficiency.

2. Voltage ratio is the ratio of the output voltage to the input voltage of the transformer.

3. The load test involves connecting the primary winding of the transformer to a source of alternating current and the secondary winding is connected to a load.

4. The load is gradually increased, and the corresponding values of input current, output current, and voltage are measured.

5. By measuring the input and output voltage and current, the voltage ratio of the transformer can be calculated.

6. Polarity of a transformer refers to the direction of the induced voltage in the secondary winding with respect to the primary winding.

7. To determine the polarity of the transformer, a low voltage battery is connected to the primary winding, and the direction of the induced voltage in the secondary winding is observed.

8. If the induced voltage is in the same direction as the battery voltage, then the polarity is said to be additive. If the induced voltage is in the opposite direction, then the polarity is said to be subtractive.

9. Efficiency of a transformer is defined as the ratio of output power to input power.

10. The efficiency of the transformer can be calculated by measuring the input and output power and then taking the ratio of the two.

11. The load test is an essential test for determining the various parameters of a single phase transformer, and it is essential to conduct this test to ensure the proper functioning of the transformer.
