##### CO 5 Perform experiments on speed measurement and reversal of direction of three phase induction motor and Identify the type of DC and AC machines based on their construction. K4

- Speed measurement of three phase induction motor:

  - A three phase induction motor is a type of AC machine that converts electrical power into mechanical power by creating a rotating magnetic field in the stator and inducing currents in the rotor.
  - The speed of the rotor is proportional to the frequency of the supply voltage and the number of poles in the stator, but it is always less than the synchronous speed of the stator field due to the slip factor.
  - The speed measurement of a three phase induction motor can be done by using a tachometer, a stroboscope, or a frequency meter.
  - A tachometer is a device that measures the rotational speed of a shaft by counting the number of pulses generated by a sensor attached to the shaft or by measuring the voltage induced in a coil wrapped around the shaft.
  - A stroboscope is a device that produces flashes of light at a variable frequency and synchronizes them with the rotation of the shaft. By adjusting the frequency of the flashes until they appear to be stationary, the speed of the shaft can be determined.
  - A frequency meter is a device that measures the frequency of the induced voltage in the rotor or the slip ring of the motor. The frequency of the induced voltage is equal to the slip frequency, which is the difference between the synchronous frequency and the rotor frequency.

- Reversal of direction of three phase induction motor:

  - A three phase induction motor can be reversed by changing the phase sequence of the supply voltage to the stator. This can be done by using a reversing switch or a star-delta starter.
  - A reversing switch is a device that interchanges any two phases of the supply voltage, thus reversing the direction of the rotating magnetic field in the stator and the induced currents in the rotor.
  - A star-delta starter is a device that connects the stator windings in star configuration for starting and in delta configuration for running. By changing the star-delta connection, the phase sequence of the supply voltage can be reversed, thus reversing the direction of the motor.

- Identification of type of DC and AC machines based on their construction:

  - A DC machine is a type of electrical machine that converts electrical power into mechanical power or vice versa by using a commutator and brushes to change the direction of the current in the armature.
  - An AC machine is a type of electrical machine that converts electrical power into mechanical power or vice versa by using alternating currents in the stator and the rotor to create a rotating magnetic field.
  - The type of DC and AC machines can be identified based on their construction features, such as the type of armature, the type of field winding, the type of rotor, and the type of cooling system.
  - The type of armature can be classified as lap wound or wave wound, depending on the way the armature coils are connected to the commutator segments. A lap wound armature has multiple parallel paths for the current, while a wave wound armature has only two paths for the current.
  - The type of field winding can be classified as shunt wound, series wound, compound wound, or separately excited, depending on the way the field coils are connected to the supply voltage and the armature. A shunt wound field winding is connected in parallel with the armature, a series wound field winding is connected in series with the armature, a compound wound field winding is a combination of shunt and series windings, and a separately excited field winding is connected to a separate source of voltage.
  - The type of rotor can be classified as squirrel cage or wound rotor, depending on the way the rotor conductors are arranged. A squirrel cage rotor has short-circuited bars of copper or aluminum embedded in slots on the rotor surface, while a wound rotor has coils of wire wound on the rotor and connected to slip rings on the shaft.
  - The type of cooling system can be classified as self-cooled, fan-cooled, or water-cooled, depending on the way the heat generated by the machine is dissipated. A self-cooled machine relies on natural convection and radiation, a fan-cooled machine uses a fan attached to the shaft or the frame to circulate air, and a water-cooled machine uses water jackets or tubes to transfer heat to a cooling tower or a heat exchanger.
