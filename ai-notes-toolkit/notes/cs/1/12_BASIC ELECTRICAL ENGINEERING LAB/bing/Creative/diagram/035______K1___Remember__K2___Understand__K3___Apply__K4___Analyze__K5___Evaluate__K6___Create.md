#### K1 – Remember, K2 – Understand, K3 – Apply, K4 – Analyze, K5 – Evaluate, K6 – Create

- These are the six levels of cognitive learning according to Bloom's taxonomy, a framework for classifying educational objectives and outcomes.
- The levels are arranged in a hierarchy, from lower-order to higher-order thinking skills, and each level requires the mastery of the previous one.
- The levels are:

  - **K1 – Remember**: The ability to recall facts, terms, definitions, concepts, or procedures from memory. Examples of verbs: define, list, name, identify, label, recognize, recall, state, repeat, memorize.
  - **K2 – Understand**: The ability to explain, interpret, summarize, paraphrase, classify, compare, or exemplify the meaning of information or ideas. Examples of verbs: describe, explain, summarize, paraphrase, illustrate, classify, compare, contrast, convert, infer, predict, exemplify.
  - **K3 – Apply**: The ability to use learned knowledge or skills in new or familiar situations or contexts. Examples of verbs: apply, use, demonstrate, solve, calculate, implement, perform, execute, operate, practice, relate, employ, adapt, adjust, modify.
  - **K4 – Analyze**: The ability to break down complex information or problems into smaller parts, identify their relationships, and examine their implications or assumptions. Examples of verbs: analyze, examine, investigate, explore, scrutinize, differentiate, distinguish, discriminate, categorize, identify, separate, compare, contrast, outline, map, diagram, deconstruct, question, infer, deduce, induce.
  - **K5 – Evaluate**: The ability to judge, critique, assess, appraise, or justify the value, quality, relevance, effectiveness, or adequacy of information, arguments, claims, or outcomes based on criteria or standards. Examples of verbs: evaluate, judge, critique, assess, appraise, review, rate, rank, grade, measure, test, check, verify, validate, argue, defend, support, justify, challenge, question, dispute, debate, recommend, propose, select, choose, decide, prioritize, optimize.
  - **K6 – Create**: The ability to generate, produce, design, construct, synthesize, or integrate new or original ideas, products, solutions, or outcomes that are novel, appropriate, and useful. Examples of verbs: create, generate, produce, design, construct, build, assemble, synthesize, integrate, invent, develop, formulate, compose, write, make, originate, devise, plan, organize, propose, initiate, implement, execute, launch, innovate, improvise, adapt, modify, improve, enhance, refine, revise, edit, transform, remix, reorganize, restructure, rearrange, recombine, reformat, expand, elaborate, extend.

- The diagram shows that the higher levels of cognitive learning require more complex and sophisticated mental processes than the lower levels, and that the lower levels are the foundation for the higher levels.
