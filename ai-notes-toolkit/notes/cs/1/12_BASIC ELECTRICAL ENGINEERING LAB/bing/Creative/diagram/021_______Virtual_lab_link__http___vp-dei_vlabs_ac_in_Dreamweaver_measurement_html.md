##### Virtual lab

- A virtual lab is a computer-based simulation of a real laboratory environment that allows students to perform experiments and learn concepts without the need for physical equipment, materials, or space.
- Virtual labs can be used to supplement or replace traditional lab activities, enhance student engagement and understanding, and provide access to resources that may be otherwise unavailable or costly.
- Virtual labs can also offer benefits such as safety, flexibility, feedback, and repeatability.

##### Dreamweaver measurement

- Dreamweaver is a web development software that allows users to create and edit websites using a graphical user interface or a code editor.
- Dreamweaver measurement is a virtual lab that teaches students how to use various measuring devices and techniques for electrical power measurement, such as ammeters, voltmeters, wattmeters, and power factor meters.
- Dreamweaver measurement is hosted by the Virtual Power Laboratory of the Dayalbagh Educational Institute (DEI) in India, and can be accessed online at http://vp-dei.vlabs.ac.in/Dreamweaver/measurement.html
- Dreamweaver measurement consists of four modules: introduction, theory, procedure, and simulation. Each module provides information, instructions, and interactive elements to help students learn the concepts and skills of power measurement.
- Dreamweaver measurement also provides quizzes, references, and feedback to assess students' learning outcomes and performance.
