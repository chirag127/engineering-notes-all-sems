##### 8. Determination of efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test

- A dc shunt motor is a type of dc motor where the field winding is connected in parallel with the armature winding. This means that the field current is independent of the armature current and the speed is relatively constant.
- The efficiency of a dc shunt motor is the ratio of the output power to the input power. The output power is the mechanical power delivered by the motor to the load, and the input power is the electrical power supplied to the motor from the source.
- To determine the efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test, the following steps are followed:

  1. Connect the dc shunt motor to a suitable load, such as a brake drum or a dynamometer, and measure the torque and speed of the motor. The output power can be calculated as P_out = T * omega, where T is the torque and omega is the angular speed.
  2. Connect an ammeter and a voltmeter to measure the armature current and voltage, and another ammeter to measure the field current. The input power can be calculated as P_in = V_a * I_a + V_f * I_f, where V_a and I_a are the armature voltage and current, and V_f and I_f are the field voltage and current.
  3. Calculate the efficiency as eta = P_out / P_in * 100%. Repeat the measurements for different values of load and plot the efficiency versus output power curve.
  4. The maximum efficiency occurs when the variable losses are equal to the constant losses. The variable losses are proportional to the armature current squared, and the constant losses are the sum of the field copper loss, the armature core loss, and the friction and windage losses.
  5. The efficiency can be improved by reducing the losses, such as using better materials, increasing the cross-sectional area of the windings, laminating the core, and lubricating the bearings.
