##### 9. To study running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions.

- A three phase induction motor is a type of electric motor that converts alternating current (AC) power into mechanical power by creating a rotating magnetic field in the stator and inducing currents in the rotor.
- The speed of the motor depends on the number of poles (p) and the frequency (f) of the AC supply, according to the formula: `N = 120f/p`, where N is the synchronous speed in revolutions per minute (rpm).
- The rotor speed is slightly less than the synchronous speed, due to the slip (s), which is the difference between the synchronous speed and the rotor speed, expressed as a fraction of the synchronous speed: `s = (N - n)/N`, where n is the rotor speed in rpm.
- The direction of rotation of the motor is determined by the phase sequence of the AC supply, which is the order in which the three phases reach their peak values. For example, if the phase sequence is R-Y-B, then the motor rotates in the clockwise direction, and if the phase sequence is R-B-Y, then the motor rotates in the anti-clockwise direction.
- To reverse the direction of rotation of the motor, the phase sequence of the AC supply must be reversed, by interchanging any two of the three phase wires. For example, if the phase sequence is R-Y-B, then interchanging R and B will make the phase sequence R-B-Y, and the motor will rotate in the anti-clockwise direction.
- To record the speed of the motor in both directions, a tachometer can be used, which is a device that measures the rotational speed of a shaft or a wheel. The tachometer can be attached to the motor shaft or the load shaft, and the speed can be read from the display or the dial.

  - Switch on the supply and observe the direction of rotation of the motor. Note down the phase sequence and the speed of the motor, using the tachometer.
  - Switch off the supply and interchange any two of the three phase wires. Switch on the supply and observe the direction of rotation of the motor. Note down the phase sequence and the speed of the motor, using the tachometer.
