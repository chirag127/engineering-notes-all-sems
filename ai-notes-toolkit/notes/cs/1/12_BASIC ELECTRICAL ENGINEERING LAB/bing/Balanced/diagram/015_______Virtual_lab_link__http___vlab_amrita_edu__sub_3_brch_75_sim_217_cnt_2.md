##### Virtual lab

A virtual lab is a simulation-based laboratory that allows students to perform experiments and learn concepts online, without the need for physical equipment or facilities. Virtual labs can provide remote access to labs in various disciplines of science and engineering, and cater to different levels of learners. Virtual labs can also enhance the quality and accessibility of education, especially for students who have limited resources or opportunities to visit real labs.

Some of the points to note about virtual labs are:

- Virtual labs use state-of-the-art computer simulation technology to create realistic and interactive environments and scenarios for experiments.
- Virtual labs can offer feedback, guidance, and assessment to students as they perform the experiments, and also allow them to repeat, modify, or customize the experiments as per their needs and interests.
- Virtual labs can supplement or complement the traditional lab sessions, and provide students with a variety of learning modes, such as visual, auditory, kinesthetic, and collaborative.
- Virtual labs can also foster inquiry-based learning, problem-solving skills, and scientific literacy among students, and expose them to current and emerging topics and technologies in their fields of study.

One of the examples of virtual labs is the Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Virtual Lab, which is a collaborative initiative of Amrita University and other institutions, supported by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The Amrita Virtual Lab offers over 220 simulation-based experiments in various domains, such as physics, chemistry, biology, biotechnology, computer science, and electrical engineering. The Amrita Virtual Lab also provides animations, videos, graphics, and theoretical explanations to enhance the learning experience of the students. The Amrita Virtual Lab can be accessed through the link: http://vlab.amrita.edu/ .
