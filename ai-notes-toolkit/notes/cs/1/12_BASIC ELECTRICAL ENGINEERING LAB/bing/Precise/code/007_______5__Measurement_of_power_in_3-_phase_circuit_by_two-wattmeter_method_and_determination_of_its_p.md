##### 5. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two-wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

The two-wattmeter method is used to measure the power in a three-phase circuit. This method is applicable for both balanced and unbalanced loads, and for both star and delta connected loads.

The procedure for measuring power using the two-wattmeter method is as follows:
1. Connect the two wattmeters as shown in the diagram, with one wattmeter connected between phase A and the neutral, and the other wattmeter connected between phase B and the neutral.
2. The readings of the two wattmeters are then added to obtain the total power consumed by the load.
3. The power factor of the load can be determined by using the formula: power factor = (W1 - W2) / (W1 + W2), where W1 and W2 are the readings of the two wattmeters.

This method is applicable for both star and delta connected loads. For a star connected load, the neutral point of the load is connected to the neutral of the supply, while for a delta connected load, the neutral point of the load is not connected to the neutral of the supply.

In summary, the two-wattmeter method is a simple and effective way to measure the power in a three-phase circuit, and to determine the power factor of the load. It is applicable for both balanced and unbalanced loads, and for both star and delta connected loads.
