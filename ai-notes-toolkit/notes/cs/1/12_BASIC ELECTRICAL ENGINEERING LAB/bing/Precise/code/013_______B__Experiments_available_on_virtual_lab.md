#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab
Virtual labs provide a platform for students to perform experiments and learn in a simulated environment. There are many virtual labs available that offer a wide range of experiments for students to perform. Here are some examples of virtual labs and the experiments they offer:

1. **Virtual Chemistry Experiments** - A collection of interactive web-based chemistry tutorials .
2. **PraxiLabs** - Offers virtual experiments that adapt to any learning style, including progress tracking, walkthrough videos, lab manuals, enrichment documents, immediate feedback, MCQ quizzes, and much more .
3. **VRLab Academy** - Offers more than 200 science experiments available on VR and PC .
4. **American Chemical Society** - Offers virtual chemistry and simulations, including the chemistry of aspirin, average atomic mass, solubility with rock candy, and freezing point depression with road salt .
5. **Mystery Science** - Offers a wealth of virtual science labs, activities, and experiments for students in elementary school, covering topics such as weather, astronomy, humans, and animals .
6. **National Science Digital Library** - Offers ChemCollective Virtual Labs, which provide a deep dive into chemistry .

These are just a few examples of the many virtual labs available that offer a wide range of experiments for students to perform and learn from.
