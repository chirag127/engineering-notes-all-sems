##### 6. To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in CRO.

- The B-H loop is a graphical representation of the relationship between the magnetic field strength (H) and the magnetic flux density (B) of a ferromagnetic material.
- The B-H loop is also known as the hysteresis loop.
- A ferromagnetic material is a material that exhibits strong magnetism in the presence of an external magnetic field.
- A CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope) is an electronic instrument that can be used to display and analyze the waveform of electronic signals.
- To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in a CRO, the material is subjected to an alternating magnetic field.
- The CRO displays the B-H loop as a closed curve on the screen.
- The shape of the B-H loop provides information about the magnetic properties of the ferromagnetic material, such as its coercivity and remanence.
- Coercivity is the measure of the magnetic field strength required to reduce the magnetization of a ferromagnetic material to zero.
- Remanence is the measure of the residual magnetization of a ferromagnetic material after the external magnetic field has been removed.
- Observing the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in a CRO is an important technique in the study of magnetism and magnetic materials.
