##### 3. Study of phenomenon of resonance in RLC series circuit and obtain resonant frequency.

- Resonance is a phenomenon that occurs in RLC series circuits when the inductive reactance and capacitive reactance are equal in magnitude.
- At resonance, the impedance of the circuit is purely resistive, and the current in the circuit is at its maximum value.
- The resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit is given by the formula: `f = 1 / (2 * pi * sqrt(L * C))`, where `f` is the resonant frequency, `L` is the inductance, and `C` is the capacitance.
- To obtain the resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit, the values of the inductance and capacitance must be known.
- The resonant frequency can be determined experimentally by measuring the current in the circuit as the frequency of the applied voltage is varied.
- At the resonant frequency, the current in the circuit will be at its maximum value.
- The phenomenon of resonance has many practical applications, such as in the tuning of radio receivers and the design of filters in electronic circuits.
