##### 4. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

The two wattmeter method is a common method used to measure power in a three-phase circuit. This method involves the use of two wattmeters to measure the power consumed by a three-phase load. The power factor of the load can also be determined using this method.

The two wattmeter method can be used for both star and delta connected loads. The procedure for measuring power and determining the power factor is slightly different for each type of connection.

For a star connected load, the two wattmeters are connected between the line and the neutral. The readings of the two wattmeters are then added together to obtain the total power consumed by the load. The power factor can be determined by dividing the total power by the product of the line voltage and the line current.

For a delta connected load, the two wattmeters are connected between the lines. The readings of the two wattmeters are then added together to obtain the total power consumed by the load. The power factor can be determined by dividing the total power by the product of the line voltage and the line current.

In summary, the two wattmeter method is a useful and accurate method for measuring power in a three-phase circuit and determining its power factor for both star and delta connected loads. It is important to follow the correct procedure for the type of load being measured to ensure accurate results.
