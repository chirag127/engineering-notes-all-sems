##### CO 2 Demonstrate the behavior of AC circuits connected to single phase AC supply and measure power in single phase as well as three phase electrical circuits. K4

- An AC circuit is an electrical circuit that is powered by an alternating current (AC) source.
- A single-phase AC supply is an electric power supply that uses a single sinusoidal voltage waveform.
- The behavior of an AC circuit connected to a single-phase AC supply can be demonstrated by analyzing the voltage, current, and power in the circuit.
- The power in a single-phase AC circuit can be measured using a wattmeter.
- A three-phase electrical circuit is a type of polyphase system that uses three conductors carrying alternating currents of the same frequency and amplitude, but with a phase difference of 120 degrees between them.
- The power in a three-phase electrical circuit can be measured using a three-phase power meter or by measuring the power in each phase and summing the results.
- It is important to understand the behavior of AC circuits connected to single-phase and three-phase AC supplies in order to design and operate electrical systems effectively.
