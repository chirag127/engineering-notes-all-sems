#### K1 – Remember, K2 – Understand, K3 – Apply, K4 – Analyze, K5 – Evaluate, K6 – Create

These are the six levels of Bloom's Taxonomy, a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives. 

1. **K1 – Remember**: This level involves recalling information from memory. It includes recognizing and recalling facts, terms, basic concepts, and answers.
2. **K2 – Understand**: This level involves understanding the meaning of information. It includes interpreting, exemplifying, classifying, summarizing, inferring, comparing, and explaining.
3. **K3 – Apply**: This level involves using information in a new situation. It includes executing and implementing.
4. **K4 – Analyze**: This level involves breaking information into parts to understand its structure. It includes differentiating, organizing, and attributing.
5. **K5 – Evaluate**: This level involves making judgments based on criteria and standards. It includes checking and critiquing.
6. **K6 – Create**: This level involves putting elements together to form a new whole. It includes generating, planning, and producing.

These levels can be used to design educational activities and assessments, and to evaluate the effectiveness of educational programs. They can also be used by learners to guide their own learning and to assess their own progress.
