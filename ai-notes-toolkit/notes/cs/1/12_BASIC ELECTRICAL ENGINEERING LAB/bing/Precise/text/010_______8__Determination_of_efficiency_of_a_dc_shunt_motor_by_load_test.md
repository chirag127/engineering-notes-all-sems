##### 8. Determination of efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test

The efficiency of a DC shunt motor can be determined by performing a load test. The load test involves measuring the input power, output power, and losses of the motor at different load conditions. The steps to determine the efficiency of a DC shunt motor by load test are as follows:

1. Connect the DC shunt motor to a suitable load and power supply.
2. Measure the input voltage and current to the motor using a voltmeter and ammeter, respectively.
3. Measure the speed of the motor using a tachometer.
4. Calculate the input power to the motor by multiplying the input voltage and current.
5. Calculate the output power of the motor by multiplying the torque and speed of the motor.
6. Calculate the losses in the motor by subtracting the output power from the input power.
7. Calculate the efficiency of the motor by dividing the output power by the input power and multiplying by 100%.

The efficiency of the motor can be plotted against the load to determine the optimal operating conditions for the motor. It is important to note that the efficiency of a DC shunt motor will vary with the load and operating conditions. Therefore, it is important to perform the load test at different load conditions to accurately determine the efficiency of the motor.
