##### 4. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

- The two wattmeter method is used to measure the total power in a three-phase circuit.
- This method is applicable to both balanced and unbalanced three-phase loads.
- Two wattmeters are connected to the circuit, with one wattmeter measuring the power in one phase and the other wattmeter measuring the power in another phase.
- The sum of the readings of the two wattmeters gives the total power in the circuit.
- The power factor of the circuit can be determined by the phase angle between the voltage and current of the circuit.
- For a star-connected load, the power factor is given by the cosine of the phase angle between the line voltage and line current.
- For a delta-connected load, the power factor is given by the cosine of the phase angle between the phase voltage and phase current.
- The two wattmeter method is a simple and accurate way to measure the power and power factor in a three-phase circuit.
