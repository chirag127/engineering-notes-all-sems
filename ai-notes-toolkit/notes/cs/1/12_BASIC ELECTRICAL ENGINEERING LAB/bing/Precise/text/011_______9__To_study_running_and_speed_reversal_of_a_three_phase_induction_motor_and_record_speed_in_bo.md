##### 9. To study running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions.

- A three-phase induction motor is an AC electric motor that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
- The motor consists of a stator, which is a stationary part, and a rotor, which is a rotating part.
- The stator has a three-phase winding, which produces a rotating magnetic field when a three-phase AC supply is applied.
- The rotating magnetic field induces an electromotive force in the rotor, which causes it to rotate.
- The speed of the motor can be controlled by varying the frequency of the AC supply.
- To reverse the direction of rotation of the motor, the phase sequence of the AC supply must be changed.
- This can be achieved by interchanging any two of the three-phase supply connections to the motor.
- The speed of the motor in both directions can be recorded using a tachometer or a stroboscope.
