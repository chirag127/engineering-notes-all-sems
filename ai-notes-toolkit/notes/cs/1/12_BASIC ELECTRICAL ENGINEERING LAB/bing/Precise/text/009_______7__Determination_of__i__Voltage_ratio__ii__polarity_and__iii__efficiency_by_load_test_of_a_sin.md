##### 7. Determination of (i) Voltage ratio (ii) polarity and (iii) efficiency by load test of a single phase Transformer

A single-phase transformer is an electrical device that is used to transfer electrical energy from one circuit to another by means of electromagnetic induction. The voltage ratio, polarity, and efficiency of a single-phase transformer can be determined by performing a load test.

i. **Voltage Ratio**: The voltage ratio of a transformer is the ratio of the secondary voltage to the primary voltage. It can be determined by measuring the primary and secondary voltages of the transformer while it is under load and calculating the ratio.

ii. **Polarity**: The polarity of a transformer refers to the direction of the induced voltage in the secondary winding relative to the primary winding. It can be determined by performing a polarity test, which involves connecting the primary and secondary windings in series and measuring the resulting voltage.

iii. **Efficiency**: The efficiency of a transformer is the ratio of the output power to the input power. It can be determined by measuring the input and output power of the transformer while it is under load and calculating the ratio.

During a load test, the transformer is connected to a load and the primary and secondary voltages, as well as the input and output power, are measured. These measurements can then be used to calculate the voltage ratio, polarity, and efficiency of the transformer. It is important to note that the load test should be performed under normal operating conditions to ensure accurate results.
