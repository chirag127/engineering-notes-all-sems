- A partial derivative is a derivative where we hold some variables constant and differentiate a function with respect to one variable .
- For example, if f(x,y) = xy + x^2y, then the partial derivative of f with respect to x is f_x(x,y) = y + 2xy, and the partial derivative of f with respect to y is f_y(x,y) = x + x^2.
- To find the partial derivative of a function, we treat the other variables as constants and use the usual rules of differentiation, such as the power rule, the product rule, the quotient rule, and the chain rule .
- The notation for partial derivatives can vary, but some common symbols are:

  - f_x(x,y) or \frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x,y) for the partial derivative of f with respect to x
  - f_y(x,y) or \frac{\partial f}{\partial y}(x,y) for the partial derivative of f with respect to y
  - f_{xx}(x,y) or \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x^2}(x,y) for the second partial derivative of f with respect to x
  - f_{yy}(x,y) or \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y^2}(x,y) for the second partial derivative of f with respect to y
  - f_{xy}(x,y) or \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x \partial y}(x,y) for the mixed partial derivative of f with respect to x and y
  - f_{yx}(x,y) or \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial y \partial x}(x,y) for the mixed partial derivative of f with respect to y and x

- Partial derivatives are useful for studying the rate of change of a function along different directions, such as the slope of a surface or the sensitivity of a physical quantity .
- Partial derivatives also play an important role in multivariable calculus, such as finding the gradient, the divergence, the curl, and the Laplacian of a function.
