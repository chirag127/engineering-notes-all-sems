### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

- **Solid state lasers** use a solid material as the active medium, where the laser light is generated. One example of a solid state laser is the **Ruby laser**.
- The Ruby laser uses a synthetic ruby crystal as the active medium, which is pumped with a high-intensity light source to produce laser light.
- The ruby crystal is made of aluminum oxide, with some of the aluminum ions replaced by chromium ions. The chromium ions absorb the pump light and emit light at a wavelength of 694.3 nm, in the red part of the spectrum.
- Ruby lasers are typically pulsed, meaning they emit light in short bursts, rather than continuously.
- **Gas lasers** use a gas as the active medium. One example of a gas laser is the **Helium-Neon (He-Ne) laser**.
- The He-Ne laser uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as the active medium, contained in a glass tube. The gas mixture is excited by an electric discharge, causing the neon atoms to emit light at a wavelength of 632.8 nm, in the red part of the spectrum.
- He-Ne lasers are typically continuous-wave, meaning they emit light continuously, rather than in pulses.
- Both Ruby and He-Ne lasers are commonly used in scientific research, as well as in various industrial and medical applications.
