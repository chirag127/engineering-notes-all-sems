# Step Index and Graded Index Fibers

## Introduction
Step index and graded index fibers are two types of optical fibers used in fiber optics. They differ in the way the refractive index of the core changes from the center to the cladding.

## Step Index Fiber
- A step index fiber has a core with a constant refractive index and a sharp decrease in the index of refraction at the cladding .
- Step index fiber is often used for short distance communication .
- Step index fibers are less expensive to manufacture than graded index fibers .
- They have a higher numerical aperture, which allows them to collect light from a wider range of angles .
- Step index fibers have higher dispersion .

## Graded Index Fiber
- A graded index fiber has a core where the refractive index is maximum at the center and decreases gradually towards the core-cladding interface .
- Graded index fiber is only of one type, that is multi mode fiber .
- In graded index fiber, the refractive index of the core gradually lowers towards the cladding, which causes light to travel faster in lower refractive index .

## Conclusion
In summary, step index and graded index fibers are two types of optical fibers used in fiber optics. They differ in the way the refractive index of the core changes from the center to the cladding. Step index fibers are often used for short distance communication and are less expensive to manufacture than graded index fibers. Graded index fibers have a core where the refractive index decreases gradually towards the core-cladding interface.
