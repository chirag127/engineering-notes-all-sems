### Principles of Laser Action

1. **Population Inversion:** For laser action to occur, the number of atoms in the excited state must be greater than the number of atoms in the ground state. This condition is known as population inversion.

2. **Optical Feedback:** The laser cavity must be designed to provide optical feedback, which is achieved by placing two mirrors at either end of the laser medium. The mirrors reflect the light back and forth, allowing it to pass through the laser medium multiple times and stimulate further emission of photons.

3. **Stimulated Emission:** When an atom in the excited state is struck by a photon with the same energy as the energy difference between the excited state and the ground state, the atom can release a second photon and return to the ground state. This process is known as stimulated emission, and it is the key to laser action.

4. **Coherence:** The photons emitted by a laser are coherent, meaning that they are all in phase with each other and have the same frequency. This coherence is what gives laser light its unique properties, such as the ability to produce a narrow, intense beam of light.
