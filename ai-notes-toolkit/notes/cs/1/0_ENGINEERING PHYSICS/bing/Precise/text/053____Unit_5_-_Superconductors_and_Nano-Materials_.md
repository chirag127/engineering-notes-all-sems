## Unit 5 - Superconductors and Nano-Materials:

1. **Nanosuperconductors** are superconducting materials developed at the scale of a nanometer.
2. Superconductivity occurs when a quantum condensate of paired electrons (Cooper pairs) is formed.
3. In nanoparticles and other small particles, energy levels are quantized.
4. Superconducting properties have been conferred on other nanostructures such as anodized alumina arrays by coating in superconducting materials.
5. A popular choice of coating is YBa2Cu3Ox.
6. Superconducting materials include fullerenes, borides, pnictides or iron-based chalcogen superconductors ides, alloys and cuprate oxides.
7. The book "Superconducting Materials: Fundamentals, Synthesis and Applications" covers numerous classes of superconducting materials and assists as a fundamental resource on the developed methodologies and techniques involved in the synthesis, processing, and characterization of superconducting materials.
