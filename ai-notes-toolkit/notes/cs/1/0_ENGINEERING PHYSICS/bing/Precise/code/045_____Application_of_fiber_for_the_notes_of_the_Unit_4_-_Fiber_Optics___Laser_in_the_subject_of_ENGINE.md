### Application of fiber

Fiber optics is the science of transmitting data, voice, and images by the passage of light through thin, transparent fibers. It has a wide range of applications due to its inherent advantages over copper conductors. Some of the major application areas of optical fibers are:

1. **Communications** - Voice, data, and video transmission are the most common uses of fiber optics. It has virtually replaced copper wire in long-distance telephone lines and is used to link computers within local area networks .

2. **Medical Industry** - Optical fibers are used in the medical industry for a variety of applications, including endoscopy, light therapy, and surgical procedures .

3. **Defense** - Fiber optics are used for data transmission in high-level data security fields of military and aerospace applications. These are used in wirings in aircraft, hydrophones for SONARs and Seismics applications .

4. **Industries** - Optical fibers are used in industries for a variety of applications, including industrial control systems and avionic systems .

5. **Broadcasting** - Fiber optics are used in broadcasting for the transmission of high-quality audio and video signals .

6. **Lighting and Decorations** - Optical fibers are used for lighting and decorations, including architectural lighting, landscape lighting, and decorative lighting .

7. **Mechanical Inspections** - Optical fibers are used for mechanical inspections, including the inspection of hard-to-reach areas and the inspection of mechanical components .
