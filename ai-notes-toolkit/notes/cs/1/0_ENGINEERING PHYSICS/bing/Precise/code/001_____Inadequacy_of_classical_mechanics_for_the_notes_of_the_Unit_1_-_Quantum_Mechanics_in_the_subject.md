### Inadequacy of classical mechanics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Classical mechanics is a branch of physics that explains the motion of celestial bodies such as planets, stars, and microscopic terrestrial bodies that move with non-relativistic speed. However, classical mechanics has its limitations and inadequacies, particularly in the region of atomic dimensions. Some of the inadequacies of classical mechanics are:

1. **Blackbody Radiation**: Classical mechanics could not explain the observed spectra of black body radiation .
2. **Photoelectric Effect**: Classical mechanics could not explain the photoelectric effect.
3. **Hydrogen Emission**: Classical mechanics could not explain the hydrogen emission of light.

These problems within classical physics led to the development of quantum theory. Quantum mechanics is a fundamental theory in physics that provides a description of the physical properties of nature at the scale of atoms and subatomic particles. It is the foundation of all quantum physics including quantum chemistry, quantum field theory, quantum technology, and quantum information science.
