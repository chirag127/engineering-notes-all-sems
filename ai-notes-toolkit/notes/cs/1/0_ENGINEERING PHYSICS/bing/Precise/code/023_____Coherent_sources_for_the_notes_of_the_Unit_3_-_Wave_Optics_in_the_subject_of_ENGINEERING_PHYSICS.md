### Coherent sources for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Coherent sources are sources of light that emit waves of the same frequency and constant phase difference.
- The waves from coherent sources interfere constructively and destructively to produce an interference pattern.
- Coherent sources can be created by dividing a single light source into two or more beams using a beam splitter or by using two or more lasers that are phase-locked.
- Examples of coherent sources include lasers, masers, and some gas discharge lamps.
- Coherent sources are important in many applications, including holography, interferometry, and optical communications.
