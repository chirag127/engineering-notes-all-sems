### Physical interpretation of wave function

- The wave function, at a particular time, contains all the information that anybody at that time can have about the particle.
- The wave function itself has no physical interpretation. It is not measurable.
- However, the square of the absolute value of the wave function has a physical interpretation.
- There is no physical meaning of wave function as it is not a quantity which can be observed. Instead, it is complex.
- For light, the wave-function has the physical meaning of propagating two orthogonal fields: an electric field and a magnetic field. This wave function is a real number.
- A clue to the physical meaning of the wavefunction Ψ(x, t) is provided by the two-slit interference of monochromatic light that behaves as electromagnetic waves. The wavefunction of a light wave is given by E(x, t), and its energy density is given by |E|2, where E is the electric field strength.
