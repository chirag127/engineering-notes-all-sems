### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

#### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser)
- A ruby laser is a three-level solid-state laser.
- The gain medium is a solid at room temperature.
- Optical pumping technique is used to supply energy to the laser medium.
- Optical pumping is a technique in which light is used as an energy source to raise electrons from a lower energy level to a higher energy level.
- In solid-state laser materials, the atoms responsible for generating laser light are first excited to higher energy states through the absorption of photons.
- The way in which these atoms relax from their excited states determines the quality and quantity of laser light produced.

#### Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)
- A gas laser is used to create a continuous laser beam.
- High coherence, high directionality, and high monocromacity beams can be achieved using gas lasers.
- The output power of a gas laser is usually measured in milliwatts.
- The first He-Ne gas laser was developed by Ali Javan and coworkers.
- The He-Ne laser has a wavelength of 632.8 nm and a linewidth of only 10kHz.
