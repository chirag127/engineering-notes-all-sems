### Laser applications

Laser is an acronym for light amplification by stimulated emission of radiation. It is a device that produces a coherent beam of light with high intensity and low divergence. Lasers have many applications in various fields of science, engineering, medicine, industry, and entertainment. Some of the common laser applications are:

- **Transmission and processing of information**: Lasers can be used to transmit and process information in various ways, such as optical fiber communication, optical data storage, barcode scanning, holography, laser printing, and optical computing.
- **Precise delivery of energy**: Lasers can be used to deliver energy precisely and selectively to a target, such as laser cutting, welding, drilling, marking, engraving, soldering, and surface treatment of materials. Lasers can also be used for medical applications, such as laser surgery, vision correction, dentistry, dermatology, and cosmetic treatment.
- **Alignment, measurement, and imaging**: Lasers can be used to align, measure, and image objects and phenomena, such as laser leveling, laser rangefinding, laser interferometry, laser spectroscopy, laser microscopy, laser scanning, and laser display.
