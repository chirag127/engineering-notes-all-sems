## Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser

### Fiber Optics
- Fiber optics are thin strands of glass or plastic that can transmit light signals over long distances.
- Fiber optics have many advantages over conventional copper wires, such as higher bandwidth, lower attenuation, immunity to electromagnetic interference, and security.
- Fiber optics are widely used in telecommunications, internet, cable TV, medical imaging, and sensing applications.

### Laser
- Laser is an acronym for Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation.
- Laser is a device that produces a coherent and monochromatic beam of light by stimulating the atoms or molecules of a medium to emit photons in a specific direction.
- Laser has many properties that make it useful for various applications, such as high intensity, narrow beam divergence, high directionality, and tunable wavelength.
- Laser can be classified into different types based on the medium, the wavelength, the mode of operation, and the output power.
- Laser can be used for various purposes, such as cutting, welding, drilling, printing, scanning, surgery, communication, measurement, and entertainment.
