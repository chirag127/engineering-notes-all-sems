### Application of fiber optics

Fiber optics are thin, transparent fibers that can transmit light signals over long distances with minimal loss or interference. They have many applications in various fields, such as:

- **Communication**: Fiber optics are widely used for voice, data, and video transmission, as they offer high bandwidth, low attenuation, immunity to electromagnetic interference, and security. Fiber optics are used for telephone lines, internet cables, cable TV, and optical networks .
- **Medical**: Fiber optics are used for various medical purposes, such as endoscopy, surgery, dentistry, imaging, and sensing. Fiber optics can provide illumination, visualization, and delivery of laser beams or other therapeutic agents to the target tissues. Fiber optics can also be used to measure physiological parameters, such as blood pressure, temperature, or oxygen saturation .
- **Defence**: Fiber optics are used for data transmission in high-level data security fields of military and aerospace applications. They are used for wiring in aircraft, hydrophones for SONARs and seismic applications, and sensors for surveillance and reconnaissance. Fiber optics are also used for laser weapons and countermeasures .
- **Industries**: Fiber optics are used for various industrial applications, such as process control, automation, inspection, and measurement. Fiber optics can provide reliable and accurate data transmission in harsh environments, such as high temperature, pressure, or vibration. Fiber optics can also be used for laser cutting, welding, drilling, and marking of materials .
- **Broadcasting**: Fiber optics are used for transmitting audio and video signals for broadcasting purposes, such as radio, television, and internet. Fiber optics can offer high quality, low noise, and long distance transmission of signals, as well as flexibility and scalability of the network .
- **Lighting and Decorations**: Fiber optics are used for various lighting and decorative purposes, such as architectural, artistic, and entertainment applications. Fiber optics can provide colorful, dynamic, and energy-efficient lighting effects, as well as create optical illusions and patterns. Fiber optics can also be used for fiber optic art, clothing, and jewelry .
- **Mechanical Inspections**: Fiber optics are used for inspecting the internal structure and condition of various mechanical systems, such as pipelines, engines, turbines, and boilers. Fiber optics can provide high-resolution images and videos of the inaccessible or hazardous areas, as well as detect any defects, cracks, or leaks .
