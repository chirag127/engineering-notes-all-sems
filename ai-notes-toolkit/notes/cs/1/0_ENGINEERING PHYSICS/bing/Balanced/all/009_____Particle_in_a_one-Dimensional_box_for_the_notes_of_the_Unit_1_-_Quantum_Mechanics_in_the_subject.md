# Particle in a one-dimensional box

- A particle in a one-dimensional box is a fundamental quantum mechanical approximation describing the translational motion of a single particle confined inside an infinitely deep well from which it cannot escape   .
- The walls of a one-dimensional box may be seen as regions of space with an infinitely large potential energy.
- The particle can only move along a straight line (the x-axis) with impenetrable barriers at either end (x = 0 and x = L).
- The potential energy of the particle is zero inside the box and infinite outside the box   .
- The Schrödinger equation for the particle is given by:

![\frac{-\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2} = E\psi](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=%5Cfrac%7B-%5Chbar%5E2%7D%7B2m%7D%5Cfrac%7Bd%5E2%5Cpsi%7D%7Bdx%5E2%7D%20%3D%20E%5Cpsi)

where ![m](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=m) is the mass of the particle, ![E](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=E) is the energy of the particle, and ![\psi](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=%5Cpsi) is the wavefunction of the particle   .
- The boundary conditions for the wavefunction are:

![\psi(0) = \psi(L) = 0](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=%5Cpsi(0)%20%3D%20%5Cpsi(L)%20%3D%200)

which means that the wavefunction vanishes at the walls of the box   .
- The general solution of the Schrödinger equation is:

![\psi(x) = A\sin(kx) + B\cos(kx)](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=%5Cpsi(x)%20%3D%20A%5Csin(kx)%20%2B%20B%5Ccos(kx))

where ![A](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=A) and ![B](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=B) are constants and ![k](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=k) is a wave number related to the energy by:

![k = \frac{\sqrt{2mE}}{\hbar}](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=k%20%3D%20%5Cfrac%7B%5Csqrt%7B2mE%7D%7D%7B%5Chbar%7D)

   .
- Applying the boundary conditions, we find that:

![B = 0](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=B%20%3D%200)

and

![kL = n\pi](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=kL%20%3D%20n%5Cpi)

where ![n](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=n) is a positive integer   .
- The energy of the particle is then quantized and given by:

![E_n = \frac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2mL^2}](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=E_n%20%3D%20%5Cfrac%7Bn%5E2%5Cpi%5E2%5Chbar%5E2%7D%7B2mL%5E2%7D)

where ![n](https://render.githubusercontent.com/render/math?math=n) is the quantum number of the energy level   .
