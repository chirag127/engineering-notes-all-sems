### Laser applications

Laser is an acronym for light amplification by stimulated emission of radiation. It is a device that produces a coherent beam of light with high intensity and low divergence. Lasers have many applications in various fields of science, engineering, medicine, industry, and entertainment. Some of the common laser applications are:

- **Transmission and processing of information**: Lasers are used to transmit and process information in optical communication systems, such as fiber optics, optical disks, barcode scanners, printers, holography, and optical computing. Lasers can also be used to encode, store, and retrieve data in various media, such as CDs, DVDs, and Blu-ray discs .
- **Precise delivery of energy**: Lasers are used to deliver energy precisely and selectively to a target, such as in laser cutting, welding, drilling, marking, engraving, and machining of various materials. Lasers can also be used to heat, melt, vaporize, or ionize matter, such as in laser ablation, laser annealing, laser sintering, and laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy. Lasers can also be used to generate high temperatures and pressures, such as in laser fusion, laser ignition, and laser propulsion .
- **Alignment, measurement, and imaging**: Lasers are used to align, measure, and image objects, such as in laser leveling, laser rangefinding, laser interferometry, laser scanning, laser microscopy, and laser spectroscopy. Lasers can also be used to create and manipulate light, such as in laser pointers, laser shows, laser cooling, laser trapping, and laser tweezers  .
- **Medical and biological applications**: Lasers are used to diagnose, treat, and prevent various diseases and disorders, such as in laser surgery, laser therapy, laser dentistry, laser dermatology, and laser ophthalmology. Lasers can also be used to modify biological tissues, such as in gene therapy, stem cell therapy, and tissue engineering. Lasers can also be used to study biological processes, such as in fluorescence microscopy, confocal microscopy, and optical coherence tomography.
- **Other applications**: Lasers have many other applications, such as in laser weapons, laser security, laser art, laser entertainment, laser cleaning, laser printing, and laser engraving.
