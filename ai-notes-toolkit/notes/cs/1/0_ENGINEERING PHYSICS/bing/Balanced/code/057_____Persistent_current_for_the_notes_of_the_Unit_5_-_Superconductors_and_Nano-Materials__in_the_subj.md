### Persistent current for the notes of the Unit 5 - Superconductors and Nano-Materials: in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Persistent current is the current which flows forever in a superconductor without any external power source.
- Persistent current is possible and observed in superconductors due to quantum effects.
- Persistent current can be induced in a superconducting ring by placing it in a magnetic field above its critical temperature, cooling it below its critical temperature, and then removing the magnetic field .
- Persistent current is determined by the energy of the superconductor, which takes into account the bulk excitations, the tunneling across the junction, and the energies of the edge states.
- Persistent current is widely used in the form of superconducting magnets, which have applications in medical imaging, particle accelerators, and magnetic levitation.
