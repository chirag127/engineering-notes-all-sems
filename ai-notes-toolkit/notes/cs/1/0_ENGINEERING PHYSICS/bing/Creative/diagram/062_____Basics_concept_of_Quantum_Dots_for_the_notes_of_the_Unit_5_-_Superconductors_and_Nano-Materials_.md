### Basics concept of Quantum Dots

- Quantum dots (QDs) are **nanoscale particles** of **semiconducting material** that have **optical and electronic properties** that differ from those of larger particles as a result of **quantum mechanics** .
- Quantum dots can be **embedded in cells or organisms** for various experimental purposes, such as **labeling proteins**.
- Quantum dots can be **defined by lithographically patterned gate electrodes**, or by **etching on two-dimensional electron gases** in semiconductor heterostructures.
- Quantum dots can have **lateral dimensions** between **20 and 100 nm**.
- Quantum dots can also be **small regions of one material buried in another** with a larger band gap.
- Quantum dots show **quantum effects** because they are so little. This means that **electrons inside the dot are trapped** and can only occupy **defined energy levels**.
- Quantum dots can **emit light** of different colors depending on their **size and shape**.
- Quantum dots can be used for **applications** such as **solar cells, LEDs, lasers, transistors, biosensors, quantum computing, and medical imaging** .
