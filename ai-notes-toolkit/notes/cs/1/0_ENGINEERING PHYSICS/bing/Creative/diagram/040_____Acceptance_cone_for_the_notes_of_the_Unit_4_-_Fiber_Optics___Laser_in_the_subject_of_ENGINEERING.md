### Acceptance cone

- The acceptance cone is the cone-shaped region of space around the optical fiber, within which the light rays can enter the fiber and be propagated through it .
- The acceptance cone is derived by rotating the acceptance angle, which is the maximum angle with the axis of the optical fiber at which light can enter the fiber, around the fiber axis .
- The acceptance cone depends on the numerical aperture (NA) of the optical fiber, which is a measure of the light-gathering ability and resolution of the fiber.
- The numerical aperture is defined as the sine of the acceptance angle, multiplied by the refractive index of the medium surrounding the fiber .
- The numerical aperture can also be expressed as the square root of the difference between the squares of the refractive indices of the core and the cladding of the fiber .
- The larger the acceptance cone, the larger the numerical aperture, which means that the fiber can accept and propagate more light.
- The acceptance cone is important for coupling light into the optical fiber from a light source, such as a laser or an LED.
