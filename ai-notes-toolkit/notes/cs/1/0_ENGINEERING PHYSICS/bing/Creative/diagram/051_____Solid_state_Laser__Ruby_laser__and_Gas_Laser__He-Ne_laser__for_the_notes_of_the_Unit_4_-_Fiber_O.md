### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser)

- A solid state laser is a laser that uses a solid material as the gain medium, where the light is amplified. 
- A ruby laser is a type of solid state laser that uses a synthetic ruby crystal as the gain medium.  
- Ruby laser is the first successful laser developed by Theodore H. Maiman at Hughes Research Laboratories on May 16, 1960.  
- Ruby laser produces pulses of coherent visible light at a wavelength of 694.3 nm, which is a deep red color, with a very narrow linewidth of 0.53 nm.  
- Ruby laser is a three level laser, which means that the electrons in the ruby crystal can occupy three energy levels. The laser action occurs between the second and the ground level.  
- The ruby crystal is rod-shaped and has a polished surface. It is surrounded by a helical flash lamp that provides optical pumping. The ends of the rod are coated with silver, which act as mirrors. One of the mirrors is partially transparent, which allows the laser beam to exit.  
- The ruby laser has a low efficiency of about 0.1%, which means that only a small fraction of the input energy is converted into laser output.  
- The ruby laser has a high threshold, which means that a high input energy is required to start the laser action.  
- The ruby laser has a short lifetime, which means that the ruby crystal degrades over time due to the high temperature and stress.  
- The ruby laser has applications in holography, dermatology, and laser spectroscopy.  

### Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

- A gas laser is a laser that uses a gas as the gain medium, where the light is amplified. 
- A He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon gas as the gain medium.  
- He-Ne laser is one of the most common and widely used lasers, as it is simple, reliable, and low-cost.  
- He-Ne laser produces continuous wave (CW) of coherent light at a wavelength of 632.8 nm, which is a bright red color, with a very narrow linewidth of 0.002 nm.  
- He-Ne laser is a four level laser, which means that the electrons in the gas mixture can occupy four energy levels. The laser action occurs between the third and the second level.  
- The gas mixture is contained in a glass tube with metal electrodes at the ends. A high voltage is applied across the electrodes, which creates an electric discharge that excites the gas atoms. The ends of the tube are coated with dielectric material, which act as mirrors. One of the mirrors is partially transparent, which allows the laser beam to exit.  
- The He-Ne laser has a moderate efficiency of about 0.1%, which means that only a small fraction of the input energy is converted into laser output.  
- The He-Ne laser has a low threshold, which means that a low input energy is required to start the laser action.  
- The He-Ne laser has a long lifetime, which means that the gas mixture does not degrade over time and can be replenished easily.  
- The He-Ne laser has applications in metrology, alignment, scanning, communication, and laser printing.
