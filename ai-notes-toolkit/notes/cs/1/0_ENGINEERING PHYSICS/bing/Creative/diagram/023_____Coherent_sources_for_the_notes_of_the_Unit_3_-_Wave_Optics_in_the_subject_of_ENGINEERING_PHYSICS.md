### Coherent sources

- Coherent sources are sources that emit waves with the same frequency, wavelength and phase or having a constant phase difference .
- Coherent sources produce sustained interference patterns when the waves superimpose and the positions of maxima and minima are fixed .
- Coherent sources are required for the phenomenon of interference of light, which is the superposition of two or more light waves .
- Coherent sources can be obtained by using a single source and splitting it into two parts by using a beam splitter, a prism, a mirror or a thin film .
- Coherent sources can also be obtained by using two independent sources that are synchronized by a common oscillator or a feedback mechanism.
- Coherent sources have applications in holography, laser technology, optical communication, metrology and spectroscopy .
