### Laser applications

Laser is an acronym for light amplification by stimulated emission of radiation. It is a device that produces a coherent beam of light with high intensity and low divergence. Lasers have many applications in various fields of science, engineering, medicine, industry, and entertainment. Some of the common laser applications are:

- **Transmission and processing of information**: Lasers are used to transmit and process information in optical communication systems, such as fiber optics, optical disks, barcode scanners, laser printers, and holography. Lasers can also be used to store and retrieve information in optical memory devices, such as CD-ROMs and DVDs.
- **Precise delivery of energy**: Lasers are used to deliver energy precisely and selectively to a target, such as in laser surgery, laser welding, laser cutting, laser drilling, laser marking, and laser engraving. Lasers can also be used to generate high temperatures and pressures, such as in laser fusion, laser heating, and laser propulsion .
- **Alignment, measurement, and imaging**: Lasers are used to align and measure objects and distances, such as in laser leveling, laser rangefinding, laser interferometry, and laser spectroscopy. Lasers can also be used to create and display images, such as in laser scanning, laser projection, laser microscopy, and laser photography .
- **Medical applications**: Lasers are used to diagnose and treat various diseases and conditions, such as in laser eye surgery, laser dentistry, laser dermatology, laser oncology, and laser therapy. Lasers can also be used to enhance the appearance and function of the body, such as in laser hair removal, laser tattoo removal, laser liposuction, and laser skin resurfacing.
- **Military and security applications**: Lasers are used to enhance the performance and accuracy of weapons and defense systems, such as in laser sights, laser guidance, laser weapons, and laser countermeasures. Lasers can also be used to detect and identify threats and targets, such as in laser radar, laser illumination, laser fingerprinting, and laser spectroscopy.
