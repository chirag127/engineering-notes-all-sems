### Necessity of extended sources for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Wave optics, also called physical optics, is a branch of optics that deals with the study of various phenomena such as polarization, diffraction, interference and other occurrences where ray approximation of geometric optics cannot be done.
- Wave optics describes the connection between waves and rays of light, and explains how light behaves as a form of energy that travels through a medium in the form of transverse waves.
- Wave optics is an important topic for engineering physics, as it helps to understand the nature and properties of light, and its applications in various fields such as communication, imaging, spectroscopy, holography, etc.
- The notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS cover the following topics:
  - Superposition of harmonic oscillations and the physics of traveling and standing waves.
  - EM wave equations and the eikonal equation for light rays.
  - Superposition of waves, interference, optical interferometry and coherence.
  - Diffraction of light, Fraunhofer and Fresnel diffraction, and the resolving power of optical instruments .
  - Polarization of light, Brewster's law, Malus' law, polarizers and analyzers, and optical activity .

  - To gain a deeper understanding of the concepts and principles of wave optics, and to apply them to solve numerical problems and derivations.
  - To learn about the latest developments and innovations in the field of wave optics, and to explore its applications in various domains of engineering and technology.
  - To revise and reinforce the key points and formulas of wave optics, and to practice more questions and examples for better preparation and performance in the exams.
