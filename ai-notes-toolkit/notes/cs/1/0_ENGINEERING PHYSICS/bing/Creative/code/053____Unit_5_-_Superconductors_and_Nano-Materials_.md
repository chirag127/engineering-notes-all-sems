## Unit 5 - Superconductors and Nano-Materials:

- Superconductors are materials that can conduct electricity without any resistance when they are cooled below a certain critical temperature.
- Nano-materials are materials that have at least one dimension in the range of 1 to 100 nanometers, where quantum effects can influence their properties.
- Superconductors and nano-materials are related because superconductivity can be enhanced or modified by reducing the size of the material to the nanoscale  .
- Some examples of superconducting nano-materials are:
  - Carbon nanotubes coated with superconducting materials such as MgB2 or NbSe2.
  - Anodized alumina arrays coated with superconducting materials such as YBa2Cu3Ox.
  - Lead nanoparticles that exhibit the Meissner effect at higher magnetic fields than bulk lead.
  - Iron-based chalcogenide nanoparticles that show enhanced superconducting transition temperatures.
- Some applications of superconducting nano-materials are:
  - Quantum computing and information processing using superconducting qubits.
  - Magnetic resonance imaging and spectroscopy using superconducting quantum interference devices (SQUIDs).
  - Energy storage and transmission using superconducting wires and coils.
  - Nanoscale sensors and detectors using superconducting nanowires.
