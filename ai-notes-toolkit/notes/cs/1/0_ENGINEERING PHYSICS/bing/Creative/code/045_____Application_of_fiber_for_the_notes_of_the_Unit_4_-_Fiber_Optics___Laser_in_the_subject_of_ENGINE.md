# Application of fiber optics

Fiber optics are thin, flexible strands of glass or plastic that can transmit light signals over long distances with minimal loss or interference. Fiber optics have many advantages over conventional copper wires, such as higher bandwidth, lower attenuation, immunity to electromagnetic interference, lighter weight, and greater security.

Fiber optics have a wide range of applications in various fields, such as:

- **Communication**: Fiber optics are used to transmit voice, data, and video signals over long distances and at high speeds. Fiber optics are the backbone of the internet, telephone, and cable TV networks. Fiber optics can also be used for local area networks (LANs), metropolitan area networks (MANs), and wide area networks (WANs)  .
- **Medical**: Fiber optics are used for various medical purposes, such as endoscopy, laparoscopy, laser surgery, dentistry, and biomedical sensing. Fiber optics can provide high-resolution images, illumination, and laser beams to diagnose and treat various diseases and conditions. Fiber optics can also be used to monitor vital signs, blood pressure, temperature, and oxygen levels  .
- **Defense**: Fiber optics are used for military and aerospace applications, such as communication, surveillance, navigation, radar, and missile guidance. Fiber optics can provide secure, reliable, and jam-resistant communication and data transmission. Fiber optics can also be used for optical sensors, fiber lasers, and fiber optic gyroscopes  .
- **Industrial**: Fiber optics are used for various industrial applications, such as process control, automation, inspection, measurement, and quality control. Fiber optics can provide real-time data, feedback, and control signals to various machines and devices. Fiber optics can also be used for optical fiber sensors, fiber optic thermometers, and fiber Bragg gratings  .
- **Broadcasting**: Fiber optics are used for broadcasting applications, such as television, radio, and internet. Fiber optics can provide high-quality, high-definition, and digital signals to various media platforms and devices. Fiber optics can also be used for studio-to-transmitter links, satellite links, and remote broadcasting  .
- **Lighting and Decorations**: Fiber optics are used for lighting and decorations, such as architectural lighting, landscape lighting, stage lighting, and display lighting. Fiber optics can provide colorful, flexible, and energy-efficient lighting effects. Fiber optics can also be used for decorative items, such as fiber optic lamps, curtains, chandeliers, and art  .
- **Mechanical Inspections**: Fiber optics are used for mechanical inspections, such as checking the internal conditions of pipes, tanks, engines, and other hard-to-reach places. Fiber optics can provide clear and detailed images, videos, and measurements of various components and defects. Fiber optics can also be used for optical fiber scopes, borescopes, and periscopes  .
