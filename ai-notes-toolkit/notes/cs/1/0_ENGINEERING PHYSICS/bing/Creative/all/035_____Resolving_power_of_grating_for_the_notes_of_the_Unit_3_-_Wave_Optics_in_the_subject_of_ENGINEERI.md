# Resolving Power of Grating

- Resolving power of grating is its ability to form two separate maxima of two wavelengths which are very close to each other.
- It is defined as the ratio of the wavelength of one of the spectral line to the difference in the wavelengths between them such that the two lines appear to be just resolved.
- Thus, resolving power of grating is given by R.P. = λ/dλ, where λ is the mean wavelength and dλ is the smallest difference between two wavelengths that can be resolved .
- Resolving power of grating depends on the order of diffraction, the number of lines per unit length of the grating, and the width of the grating .
- The expression for resolving power of grating can be derived as follows:

  - Consider a grating with N lines per unit length and a total width of b = N/a, where a is the distance between two adjacent lines.
  - Let λ and λ + dλ be two wavelengths that are just resolved in the nth order of diffraction.
  - The condition for constructive interference for these wavelengths is:

    - nλ = a(sinθ + sinα)
    - n(λ + dλ) = a(sinθ' + sinα)

  - where θ and θ' are the angles of diffraction, and α is the angle of incidence.
  - Subtracting these two equations and neglecting higher powers of dλ, we get:

    - ndλ = a(cosθ - cosθ')dθ

  - The resolving power of grating is the reciprocal of the smallest value of dλ that can be resolved, which occurs when dθ is the angular width of the diffraction peak.
  - The angular width of the diffraction peak can be approximated by the Rayleigh criterion, which states that two peaks are just resolved when the maximum of one peak coincides with the first minimum of the other peak .
  - The first minimum of the diffraction peak occurs when the path difference between the rays from the two ends of the grating is equal to λ, which gives:

    - λ = a(sinθ + sinα) - a(sinθ - sinα)
    - λ = 2asinα

  - Therefore, the angular width of the diffraction peak is:

    - dθ = λ/b

  - Substituting this value in the expression for ndλ, we get:

    - ndλ = a(cosθ - cosθ')λ/b

  - Dividing both sides by λ and rearranging, we get:

    - R.P. = λ/dλ = nN(cosθ - cosθ')/cosθ

  - This is the expression for the resolving power of grating   .
