# Coherent sources for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Wave optics is the branch of physics that studies the nature and behavior of light as a wave phenomenon.
- Interference is the phenomenon of superposition of two or more waves that results in the formation of regions of constructive and destructive interference.
- Coherent sources are the sources that emit waves with the same frequency, wavelength and phase or having a constant phase difference.
- Coherent sources are necessary for producing sustained interference patterns, where the positions of maxima and minima are fixed.
- Examples of coherent sources are lasers, which produce monochromatic, parallel and unbroken wave chains, and two slits illuminated by a single source of light, which act as secondary coherent sources.
- Incoherent sources are the sources that emit waves with different frequencies, wavelengths and phases or having a random phase difference.
- Incoherent sources produce irregular interference patterns, where the positions of maxima and minima are constantly changing due to the fluctuations in the phase difference.
- Examples of incoherent sources are ordinary light sources, such as the sun, stars, lamps, etc., which produce polychromatic, divergent and broken wave chains.
- Coherence can be classified into two types: temporal coherence and spatial coherence.
- Temporal coherence is the measure of the correlation between the phases of two waves at different times or locations along the same direction of propagation.
- Temporal coherence depends on the frequency spectrum of the source and the path difference between the waves.
- Temporal coherence is related to the coherence time, which is the time interval over which the phase difference between two waves remains constant.
- Spatial coherence is the measure of the correlation between the phases of two waves at different locations across the wavefront.
- Spatial coherence depends on the size and shape of the source and the distance from the source.
- Spatial coherence is related to the coherence length, which is the distance over which the phase difference between two waves remains constant.
- Coherence and interference are important concepts in wave optics, as they help us understand the properties and applications of light as a wave.
- Some applications of coherence and interference are holography, interferometry, diffraction gratings, thin film interference, etc.
