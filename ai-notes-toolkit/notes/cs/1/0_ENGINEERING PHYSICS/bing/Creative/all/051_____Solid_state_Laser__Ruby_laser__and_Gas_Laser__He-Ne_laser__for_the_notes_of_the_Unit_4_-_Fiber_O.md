# Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

## Solid state Laser (Ruby laser)

- A solid state laser is a laser that uses a solid material as the gain medium, rather than a liquid or a gas.
- A ruby laser is a type of solid state laser that uses a synthetic ruby crystal as the gain medium .
- Ruby laser is the first successful laser developed by Theodore H. Maiman at Hughes Research Laboratories on May 16, 1960 .
- Ruby laser produces pulses of coherent visible light at a wavelength of 694.3 nm, which is a deep red color, with a very narrow linewidth of 0.53 nm .
- Ruby laser is a three level laser, which means that the electrons in the ruby crystal can occupy three different energy levels .
- The ruby crystal is usually rod-shaped and has a cylindrical cavity along its axis. The cavity is coated with highly reflective mirrors at both ends, forming an optical resonator .
- The ruby crystal is optically pumped by a flash lamp that emits a broad spectrum of light. The light excites the electrons in the ruby crystal from the ground state to the highest energy level .
- The electrons in the highest energy level quickly decay to the intermediate energy level, which is the metastable state. The electrons in the metastable state have a longer lifetime and can accumulate in large numbers .
- When the population inversion between the metastable state and the ground state is achieved, the stimulated emission of photons occurs. The photons travel back and forth in the cavity, amplifying the light and forming a laser beam .
- The laser beam exits the cavity through one of the mirrors, which is partially transparent. The other mirror is fully reflective and reflects the light back into the cavity .
- Ruby laser has a low efficiency and a low repetition rate. It can only produce short pulses of light with a duration of a few milliseconds and a frequency of a few hertz .
- Ruby laser has applications in holography, spectroscopy, medicine, and laser ranging .

## Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

- A gas laser is a laser that uses a gas as the gain medium, rather than a solid or a liquid.
- A helium-neon laser (He-Ne laser) is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as the gain medium.
- He-Ne laser is one of the most common and widely used lasers. It was invented by Ali Javan, William Bennett, and Donald Herriott at Bell Labs in 1961.
- He-Ne laser produces continuous wave (CW) coherent light at a wavelength of 632.8 nm, which is a bright red color, with a very narrow linewidth of 0.0015 nm.
- He-Ne laser is a four level laser, which means that the electrons in the gas mixture can occupy four different energy levels.
- The gas mixture is contained in a glass tube that has a cylindrical cavity along its axis. The cavity is coated with highly reflective mirrors at both ends, forming an optical resonator.
- The gas mixture is electrically pumped by a direct current (DC) power supply that applies a high voltage across the tube. The electric field ionizes the gas atoms and creates a plasma.
- The plasma consists of free electrons and positive ions. The electrons collide with the helium atoms and excite them to higher energy levels. The excited helium atoms then collide with the neon atoms and transfer their energy to them.
- The neon atoms are excited to various energy levels, some of which are metastable. The electrons in the metastable states have a longer lifetime and can accumulate in large numbers.
- When the population inversion between the metastable states and the lower energy states is achieved, the stimulated emission of photons occurs. The photons travel back and forth in the cavity, amplifying the light and forming a laser beam.
- The laser beam exits the cavity through one of the mirrors, which is partially transparent. The other mirror is fully reflective and reflects the light back into the cavity.
