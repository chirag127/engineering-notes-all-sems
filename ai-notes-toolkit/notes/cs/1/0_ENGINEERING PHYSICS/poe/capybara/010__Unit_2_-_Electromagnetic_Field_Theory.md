## Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory

Electromagnetic Field Theory is an important branch of physics that deals with the study of the behavior of electric and magnetic fields. This unit covers various aspects of electromagnetic field theory, including:

- **Maxwell's Equations:** These equations describe the behavior of electromagnetic fields and form the foundation of electromagnetic field theory. They are a set of four differential equations that relate the electric and magnetic fields to their sources.

- **Electromagnetic Waves:** Electromagnetic waves are waves that consist of oscillating electric and magnetic fields. They are the means by which electromagnetic energy is transported through space. Electromagnetic waves can be divided into different categories based on their frequency and wavelength.

- **Electrostatics:** Electrostatics is the study of static electric fields. It deals with the behavior of charges that are at rest or are in motion at a constant velocity. Some of the topics covered in electrostatics include Coulomb's law, electric field, electric potential, and capacitance.

- **Magnetostatics:** Magnetostatics is the study of static magnetic fields. It deals with the behavior of magnetic fields that are at rest or are in motion at a constant velocity. Some of the topics covered in magnetostatics include Ampere's law, magnetic field, magnetic flux density, and magnetic circuits.

- **Electrodynamics:** Electrodynamics is the study of the dynamic behavior of electric and magnetic fields. It deals with the behavior of charges that are in motion, as well as the behavior of electromagnetic waves. Some of the topics covered in electrodynamics include Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction, Lenz's law, and electromagnetic radiation.

- **Boundary Value Problems:** Boundary value problems are mathematical problems that involve finding the solution to a differential equation subject to certain boundary conditions. In electromagnetic field theory, boundary value problems are used to determine the behavior of electric and magnetic fields in different situations, such as in the presence of conductors or dielectrics.

Overall, the study of electromagnetic field theory is important for understanding a wide range of phenomena, from the behavior of electric and magnetic fields to the operation of electronic devices. By mastering the concepts covered in this unit, you will be well-equipped to analyze and solve problems related to electromagnetic fields.
