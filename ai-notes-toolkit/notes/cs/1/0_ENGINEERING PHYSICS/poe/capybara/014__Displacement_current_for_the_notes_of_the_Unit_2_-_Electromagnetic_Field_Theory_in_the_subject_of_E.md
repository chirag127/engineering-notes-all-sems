### Displacement current

#### Definition

- Displacement current is a concept in electromagnetism that was introduced by James Clerk Maxwell in 1861.
- It is defined as the time rate of change of electric flux density or electric displacement in a region of space.
- Displacement current is a term used to describe the flow of electric charges that are not carried by electrons, but rather are due to the changing electric field in a region of space.

#### Importance

- Displacement current plays a crucial role in the formulation of Maxwell's equations, which describe the behavior of electromagnetic fields.
- It is an essential component of the electromagnetic wave equation, which describes the propagation of electromagnetic waves in free space.
- The existence of displacement current also helps to explain the behavior of capacitors and other electrical devices.

#### Mathematical expression

- The mathematical expression for displacement current is given by the equation:
```
∂D/∂t = ε0 ∂E/∂t
```
where D is the electric displacement, E is the electric field, ε0 is the permittivity of free space, and t is time.

#### Relationship with conduction current

- Displacement current and conduction current are two types of electric current that coexist in a region of space.
- Conduction current is the flow of electric charges through a conductor, while displacement current is the flow of electric charges that are not carried by electrons.
- The sum of the displacement current and conduction current is equal to the total current in a circuit, as described by Kirchhoff's current law.

#### Applications

- Displacement current has numerous applications in various fields, including telecommunications, electrical engineering, and physics.
- It is used in the design and operation of capacitors, antennas, and other electrical devices.
- Displacement current also plays a vital role in the generation and transmission of electromagnetic waves, which are used in radio communication, television, and other forms of wireless communication.
