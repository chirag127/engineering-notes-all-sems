## Unit 3 - Wave Optics

Wave optics is a branch of optics that studies the behavior of light as waves. It explores the properties of light waves and their interactions with other waves and with matter. This unit covers the following topics:

1. Introduction to wave optics:
    - Definition of wave optics
    - Wave-particle duality of light
    - Electromagnetic spectrum
    - Types of waves
    - Properties of waves

2. Wave nature of light:
    - Huygens' principle
    - Interference of light waves
    - Young's double-slit experiment
    - Diffraction of light waves
    - Fraunhofer diffraction

3. Polarization of light:
    - Definition of polarization
    - Types of polarization
    - Polarization by reflection and refraction
    - Malus' law
    - Polarizers

4. Optical instruments:
    - Microscopes
    - Telescopes
    - Interferometers
    - Spectrometers

5. Applications of wave optics:
    - Holography
    - Fiber optics
    - Lasers
    - Optical communication
    - Optical sensors

In summary, wave optics is an important area of study in optics that helps us understand the wave-like behavior of light and its interactions with matter. It has many applications in fields such as telecommunications, medicine, and manufacturing.
