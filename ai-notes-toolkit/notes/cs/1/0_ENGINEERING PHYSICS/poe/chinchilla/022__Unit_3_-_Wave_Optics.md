## Unit 3 - Wave Optics

In this unit, we will study the wave nature of light and its interactions with matter. We will explore the concepts of interference, diffraction, and polarization of light, and how they can be applied in various fields.

### 1. Wave Characteristics of Light
- Light as an electromagnetic wave
- Wave properties of light: wavelength, frequency, amplitude, phase
- Wavefront, ray, and Huygens' principle
- Superposition principle and interference

### 2. Interference of Light
- Coherent sources of light
- Conditions for interference: path difference, phase difference
- Types of interference: constructive and destructive
- Interference in thin films and Newton's rings

### 3. Diffraction of Light
- Diffraction grating and its applications
- Single-slit and double-slit diffraction
- Fraunhofer and Fresnel diffraction
- Diffraction-limited resolution

### 4. Polarization of Light
- Polarization by reflection, refraction, and scattering
- Polarizers and their applications
- Circular and elliptical polarization
- Optical activity and chiral molecules

### 5. Applications of Wave Optics
- Interferometry in precision measurements
- Holography and its applications
- Fiber optics and communication
- Spectroscopy and its applications in various fields

In conclusion, wave optics is an essential branch of optics that describes the wave-like behavior of light and its interactions with matter. Understanding the concepts of interference, diffraction, and polarization is crucial in various fields, including physics, engineering, and medicine. By mastering the concepts presented in this unit, you will have the necessary knowledge to understand and apply wave optics in various applications.
