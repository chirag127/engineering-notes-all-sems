### Time-dependent and time-independent Schrodinger wave equations for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Quantum mechanics is a fundamental theory in physics that describes the behavior of particles at the atomic and subatomic scales. One of the key concepts in quantum mechanics is the wave function, which describes the probability of finding a particle in a particular state.

The Schrodinger wave equation is a fundamental equation in quantum mechanics that describes the behavior of a particle in terms of its wave function. There are two forms of the Schrodinger wave equation: the time-dependent and the time-independent forms. Let's take a closer look at these two forms.

#### Time-dependent Schrodinger wave equation

The time-dependent Schrodinger wave equation describes the evolution of a particle's wave function over time. It is given by the following equation:

$$ i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \Psi(\mathbf{r},t) = \hat{H} \Psi(\mathbf{r},t) $$

where $\hbar$ is the reduced Planck constant, $\hat{H}$ is the Hamiltonian operator, $\Psi(\mathbf{r},t)$ is the wave function, and $t$ is time.

The time-dependent Schrodinger wave equation is used to determine the time evolution of a particle's wave function in the presence of a potential energy field. This equation is essential in understanding the behavior of particles in systems such as atoms, molecules, and solids.

#### Time-independent Schrodinger wave equation

The time-independent Schrodinger wave equation describes the stationary states of a particle's wave function. It is given by the following equation:

$$ \hat{H}\Psi(\mathbf{r}) = E\Psi(\mathbf{r}) $$

where $\hat{H}$ is the Hamiltonian operator, $\Psi(\mathbf{r})$ is the wave function, and $E$ is the energy.

The time-independent Schrodinger wave equation is used to determine the energy levels and wave functions of a particle in a potential energy field. This equation is essential in understanding the electronic structure of atoms and molecules.

In summary, the Schrodinger wave equation is a fundamental equation in quantum mechanics that describes the behavior of particles in terms of their wave functions. The time-dependent Schrodinger wave equation describes the time evolution of a particle's wave function, while the time-independent Schrodinger wave equation describes the stationary states of a particle's wave function. These equations are essential in understanding the behavior of particles in systems such as atoms, molecules, and solids.
