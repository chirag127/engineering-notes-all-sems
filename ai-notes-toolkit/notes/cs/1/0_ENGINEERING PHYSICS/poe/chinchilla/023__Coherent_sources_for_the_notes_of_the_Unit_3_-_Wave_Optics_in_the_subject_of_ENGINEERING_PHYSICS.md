### Coherent sources for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS:

Wave optics is a branch of physics that deals with the behavior of light waves. In this unit, we will study the concept of coherent sources, which are essential in understanding the interference of light waves.

Coherent sources are those sources of light waves that have a constant phase difference between them. These sources emit waves that have a fixed phase relationship, which means that the crest of one wave corresponds to the crest of the other wave and vice versa.

Here are some coherent sources that are commonly used in wave optics:

1. LASER: Laser stands for Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation. It is a highly coherent source of light that emits light waves with a fixed phase relationship. Laser beams are monochromatic, which means they have a single wavelength, and they are highly directional, which means they can be focused to a small spot.

2. LED: LED stands for Light Emitting Diode. It is also a coherent source of light, but it is less coherent than a laser. LED emits light waves with a fixed phase relationship, but the phase difference between the waves is not constant over time. LED is used in applications where high coherence is not required.

3. Mercury Vapor Lamp: Mercury vapor lamp is a type of gas-discharge lamp that emits light waves in the ultraviolet and visible range. It is a coherent source of light, but its coherence is lower than that of laser and LED. Mercury vapor lamps are used in applications where high-intensity light is required.

4. Sodium Vapor Lamp: Sodium vapor lamp is a type of gas-discharge lamp that emits light waves in the yellow-orange range. It is a highly coherent source of light, but it is not monochromatic. Sodium vapor lamps are used in applications where a high-intensity yellow-orange light is required.

In conclusion, coherent sources are essential in understanding the interference of light waves. Laser, LED, mercury vapor lamp, and sodium vapor lamp are some of the coherent sources that are commonly used in wave optics. Understanding the nature of these sources and their properties is essential in the study of wave optics.
