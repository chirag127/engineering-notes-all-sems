
### Maxwell Equations in Vacuum

1. Gauss's Law: $$ \nabla \cdot \vec{E} = \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$$
2. Faraday's Law: $$\nabla \times \vec{E} = -\frac{\partial \vec{B}}{\partial t}$$
3. Ampere's Law: $$\nabla \times \vec{B} = \mu_0 \vec{j} + \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \vec{E}}{\partial t}$$

### Maxwell Equations in Conducting Medium

1. Gauss's Law: $$ \nabla \cdot \vec{E} = \frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$$
2. Faraday's Law: $$\nabla \times \vec{E} = -\frac{\partial \vec{B}}{\partial t} - \vec{j}$$
3. Ampere's Law: $$\nabla \times \vec{B} = \mu_0 \vec{j} + \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \vec{E}}{\partial t}$$
