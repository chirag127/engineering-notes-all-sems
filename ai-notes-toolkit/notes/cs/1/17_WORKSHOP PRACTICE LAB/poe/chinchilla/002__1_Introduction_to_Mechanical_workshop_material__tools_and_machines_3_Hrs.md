### 1 Introduction to Mechanical Workshop Material, Tools and Machines 3 Hrs

In this section, we will introduce you to the basic materials, tools, and machines commonly used in mechanical workshops. The knowledge gained from this section will provide you with a foundation for understanding and working in a mechanical workshop environment. 

1.1 Materials 
- Materials used in mechanical workshops include metals, plastics, and composites. 
- Metals are the most commonly used material and can be categorized into ferrous and non-ferrous metals. 
- Ferrous metals include iron, steel, and cast iron, while non-ferrous metals include aluminum, copper, and brass. 
- Plastics and composites are also used in some applications due to their lightweight and corrosion-resistant properties. 

1.2 Tools 
- Hand tools are essential in mechanical workshops and include tools such as hammers, pliers, wrenches, and screwdrivers. 
- Power tools are also used to increase efficiency and include tools such as drills, grinders, and saws. 
- Measuring tools such as micrometers, calipers, and gauges are used to ensure precision in machining and fabrication. 
- Safety equipment such as gloves, goggles, and ear protection should always be worn when using tools. 

1.3 Machines 
- Machines used in mechanical workshops include lathes, milling machines, and drill presses. 
- Lathes are used to turn cylindrical parts, while milling machines are used to cut complex shapes and profiles. 
- Drill presses are used to accurately drill holes in materials. 
- Other machines such as welding machines and plasma cutters are used for welding and cutting metals. 

In conclusion, understanding the materials, tools, and machines commonly used in mechanical workshops is essential for anyone interested in working in this environment. Make sure to familiarize yourself with the basic concepts and safety procedures before working with any of these tools or machines.
