#### Introduction to Patterns

- Patterns are physical models or templates used to create cavities or shapes in a mould.
- They are made of materials such as wood, metal or plastic.
- Patterns are used in foundry processes to create a mold that can be filled with molten material to create a part.

#### Pattern Allowances

- Pattern allowances are the dimensional changes made to the pattern to compensate for the contraction of the material during solidification.
- These allowances ensure that the final product has the desired dimensions and surface finish.
- The allowances are based on the material being cast and the specific casting process used.

#### Ingredients of Moulding Sand

- Moulding sand is a mixture of sand, clay, water and other additives used to create moulds for casting.
- The sand used is typically silica sand, which is high in silica content and has a consistent grain size.
- The clay is used as a binder to hold the sand together and can be bentonite, kaolin or other types of clay.
- Additives such as coal dust or sawdust are added to improve the permeability of the sand.

#### Melting Furnaces

- Melting furnaces are used to melt the materials used in casting.
- These furnaces can be electric or gas-fired and can reach temperatures of up to 2000°C.
- The type of furnace used depends on the material being melted and the volume of material needed.

#### Foundry Tools and Their Purposes

- Foundry tools are used to prepare the moulds, create the pattern and remove the casting from the mould.
- Some common tools include:

  - Rammers and trowels for compacting the sand in the mould.
  - Shovels and scoops for moving sand.
  - Crucibles for melting the material.
  - Ladles for pouring the molten material into the mould.
  - Mallets and chisels for shaping the pattern.

#### Demo of Mould Preparation and Aluminum Casting

- A demo of mould preparation and aluminum casting can be performed to understand the process firsthand.
- The demonstration will involve creating a pattern, preparing the sand mould, melting the aluminum and pouring it into the mould.
- The finished product will be a small aluminum part.

#### Practice - Study and Preparation of Mould for Plastic

- To practice the skills learned, a study and preparation of a mould for plastic can be performed.
- This will involve creating a pattern, preparing the mould and melting the plastic to fill the mould.
- The finished product will be a small plastic part.
