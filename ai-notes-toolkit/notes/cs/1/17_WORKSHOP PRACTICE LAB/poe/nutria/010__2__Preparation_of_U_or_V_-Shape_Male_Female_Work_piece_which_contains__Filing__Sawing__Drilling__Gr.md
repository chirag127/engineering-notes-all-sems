
#### 2. Preparation of U or V -Shape Male Female Work piece

1. Filing: Filing is the process of removing material from a workpiece to create a desired shape or finish. It involves using a file, which is a metal tool with a series of small teeth, to remove material from the surface of the workpiece.

2. Sawing: Sawing is the process of cutting material into two or more pieces using a saw. It can be used to cut a variety of materials, including wood, metal, plastic, and stone.

3. Drilling: Drilling is the process of creating a hole in a workpiece by using a drill bit. The drill bit is a rotating tool that is used to cut into the material.

4. Grinding: Grinding is the process of removing material from a workpiece using an abrasive wheel. The abrasive wheel is a rotating wheel that is used to cut away material from the surface of the workpiece.
