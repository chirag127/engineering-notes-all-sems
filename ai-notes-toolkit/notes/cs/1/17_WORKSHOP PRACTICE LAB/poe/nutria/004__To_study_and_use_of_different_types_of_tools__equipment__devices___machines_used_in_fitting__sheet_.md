
#### To study and use of different types of tools, equipment, devices & machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section

* Fitting: Tools such as hammers, pliers, wrenches, screwdrivers, and chisels are commonly used in fitting.
* Sheet Metal: Sheet metal tools such as shears, snips, notchers, and presses are used to cut, shape, and form sheet metal.
* Welding: Welding equipment such as welding torches, welding rods, and safety equipment are essential for welding.
