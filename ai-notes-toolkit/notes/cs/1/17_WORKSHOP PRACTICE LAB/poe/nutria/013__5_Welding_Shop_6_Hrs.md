
### 5 Welding Shop 6 Hrs

1. Welding is a fabrication process that involves the use of heat to join metal components together. 
2. It is a highly skilled craft that requires specialized tools and equipment.
3. The most common welding processes are arc welding, gas welding, and oxy-fuel welding. 
4. In order to become a certified welder, one must pass a welding certification test and complete 6 hours of welding shop time.
5. Welding shop time is supervised by a qualified welding instructor and involves the use of welding tools, safety equipment, and welding techniques. 
6. During the welding shop time, the student will learn about the different types of welding processes, welding terminology, and welding safety practices. 
7. The student will also practice welding on various types of metal, such as aluminum, stainless steel, and mild steel. 
8. Upon completion of the welding shop time, the student will receive a certification that is valid for two years.
