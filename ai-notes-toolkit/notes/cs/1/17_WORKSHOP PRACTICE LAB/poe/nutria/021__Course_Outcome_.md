
### Course Outcome:

1. Understand the fundamental principles and concepts of computer science.
2. Develop the ability to design, implement, and evaluate computer-based solutions to problems.
3. Gain an understanding of the ethical and social implications of computer technology and its applications.
4. Acquire the skills necessary to effectively communicate with computer professionals and non-computer professionals.
5. Develop the ability to work collaboratively on teams.
6. Develop the capacity to think critically and creatively, and to reason logically.
7. Understand the impact of computer technology on society, and be able to use it responsibly.
