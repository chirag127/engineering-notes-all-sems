1. CNC stands for Computer Numerical Control, and is a type of manufacturing process that uses computer-controlled machines to fabricate parts from a variety of materials. 
2. CNC machines are used in a wide range of industries, from aerospace to automotive, and are used to make parts for everything from medical implants to furniture.
3. CNC machines are operated by a computer program that is used to control the machine's movements and operations. The program is typically written in a language called G-code.
4. CNC machines require a skilled operator to ensure that the machine is working correctly and that the finished parts meet the required specifications.
5. CNC machines can be used to make parts from a variety of materials, including metals, plastics, and composites.
6. CNC machines are used in a variety of manufacturing processes, including milling, drilling, and turning.
