
#### 1. Workshop Practice, H S Bawa, McGraw Hill
1. Workshop Practice provides a comprehensive introduction to the basics of workshop technology.
2. It covers topics such as tools and machines used in a workshop, materials used for manufacturing, and the principles of workshop design.
3. It also covers topics such as safety and health considerations, quality control, and the use of computers in the workshop.
4. The book is written by H S Bawa and published by McGraw Hill.
5. It is an essential resource for students and professionals in the field of workshop technology.
