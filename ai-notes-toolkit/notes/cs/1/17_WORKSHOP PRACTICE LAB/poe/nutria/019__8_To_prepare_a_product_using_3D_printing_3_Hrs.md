
### 8 To Prepare a Product Using 3D Printing 3 Hrs

1. 3D printing is a process of making three dimensional solid objects from a digital file.
2. 3D printing is achieved using additive processes, where successive layers of material are laid down in different shapes.
3. The most common 3D printing material is plastic, although other materials such as metal, ceramic and composite materials can also be used.
4. 3D printing is used in many industries for prototyping and production, including automotive, aerospace, medical, dental and consumer products.
5. In order to prepare a product using 3D printing, a 3D model must first be created. This can be done using computer aided design (CAD) software or 3D scanning.
6. Once the 3D model is created, it must be prepared for printing. This involves slicing the model into layers and generating a G-code file, which contains instructions for the 3D printer.
7. The G-code file is then sent to the 3D printer, which reads the instructions and prints the model layer by layer.
8. Depending on the size and complexity of the model, 3D printing can take anywhere from a few minutes to several hours.
