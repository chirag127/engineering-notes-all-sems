

#### Introduction to BI Standards and Reading of Welding Drawings

1. The International Institute of Welding (IIW) is a global professional organization that develops and promotes standards for welding and related technologies.
2. The IIW provides guidance on welding processes and procedures, including the use of welding symbols to communicate welding instructions.
3. Welding drawings provide a clear and concise description of the welding process, and the symbols used to represent the welding operations.
4. To understand welding drawings, it is important to be familiar with the basic symbols and terms used in welding, such as butt joint, lap joint, TIG welding, and MIG welding.
5. Butt joints are used to join two pieces of metal together, while lap joints are used to join two pieces of metal that overlap each other.
6. TIG welding is a welding process that uses a tungsten electrode to create an electric arc between the electrode and the metal being welded.
7. MIG welding is a welding process that uses a consumable wire electrode to create an electric arc between the electrode and the metal being welded.
8. Both TIG and MIG welding require the use of proper safety gear and procedures to ensure the safety of the welder.
