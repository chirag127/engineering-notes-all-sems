# Fitting Shop

- Fitting shop is a place where metal components are fabricated by cutting, filing, drilling, tapping, etc.
- Fitting shop tools can be classified into three categories: measuring tools, marking tools, and cutting tools.
- Measuring tools are used to measure the dimensions and angles of the workpieces. Examples are steel rule, caliper, micrometer, protractor, etc.
- Marking tools are used to mark the lines and points on the workpieces for cutting and drilling. Examples are scriber, center punch, divider, etc.
- Cutting tools are used to remove the excess material from the workpieces by cutting, filing, drilling, tapping, etc. Examples are hacksaw, chisel, file, drill, tap, etc.
- Fitting shop operations can be classified into four categories: sawing, filing, drilling, and tapping.
- Sawing is the process of cutting the workpiece into desired shape and size by using a hacksaw. The hacksaw consists of a blade with teeth and a frame to hold the blade. The blade is selected according to the material and thickness of the workpiece. The sawing operation is done by holding the workpiece in a vice and moving the hacksaw back and forth along the marked line.
- Filing is the process of smoothing and finishing the workpiece by using a file. The file consists of a steel bar with rows of teeth on its surface. The file is selected according to the shape and size of the workpiece. The filing operation is done by holding the workpiece in a vice and moving the file across the surface of the workpiece in one direction only.
- Drilling is the process of making holes in the workpiece by using a drill. The drill consists of a cutting tool with a pointed tip and spiral grooves on its body. The drill is selected according to the diameter and depth of the hole. The drilling operation is done by holding the workpiece in a vice and rotating the drill by using a drilling machine or a hand drill.
- Tapping is the process of cutting internal threads in the workpiece by using a tap. The tap consists of a cutting tool with a tapered end and spiral flutes on its body. The tap is selected according to the size and pitch of the thread. The tapping operation is done by holding the workpiece in a vice and rotating the tap by using a tap wrench or a tapping machine.
