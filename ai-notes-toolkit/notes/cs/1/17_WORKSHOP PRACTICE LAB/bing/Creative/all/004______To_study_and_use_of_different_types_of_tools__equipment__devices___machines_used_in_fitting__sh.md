# To study and use of different types of tools, equipment, devices & machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section.

Fitting, sheet metal and welding are three common processes of metalworking that involve cutting, shaping and joining metal parts. Each process requires different types of tools, equipment, devices and machines to perform the tasks efficiently and safely. Here are some of the main types of tools, equipment, devices and machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section:

## Fitting tools, equipment, devices and machines

Fitting is the process of assembling, adjusting and repairing metal parts by using hand tools and measuring instruments. Some of the common fitting tools, equipment, devices and machines are:

- **Hammers**: These are used to strike metal parts or tools to drive them into place, shape them or remove them. There are different types of hammers, such as ball peen, cross peen, claw, sledge and soft-faced hammers.
- **Files**: These are used to smooth, shape and finish metal surfaces by removing small amounts of material. There are different types of files, such as flat, round, half-round, triangular, square and needle files.
- **Chisels**: These are used to cut, split or chip metal parts by striking them with a hammer. There are different types of chisels, such as cold, hot, flat, cross-cut and diamond-point chisels.
- **Saws**: These are used to cut metal parts into desired shapes and sizes. There are different types of saws, such as hacksaw, coping saw, fret saw and power saw.
- **Drills**: These are used to make holes in metal parts by rotating a cutting tool called a drill bit. There are different types of drills, such as hand drill, electric drill, drill press and radial drill.
- **Taps and dies**: These are used to make internal and external threads on metal parts by cutting grooves on them. Taps are used to make internal threads, while dies are used to make external threads.
- **Screwdrivers**: These are used to tighten or loosen screws on metal parts by turning them. There are different types of screwdrivers, such as flat, Phillips, Torx and Allen screwdrivers.
- **Wrenches**: These are used to tighten or loosen nuts and bolts on metal parts by applying torque on them. There are different types of wrenches, such as open-end, box-end, adjustable, socket and torque wrenches.
- **Pliers**: These are used to grip, bend, twist or cut metal parts or wires by applying pressure on them. There are different types of pliers, such as combination, long-nose, diagonal, slip-joint and locking pliers.
- **Vices**: These are used to hold metal parts securely while working on them. There are different types of vices, such as bench, pipe, machine and hand vices.
- **Clamps**: These are used to join or hold metal parts temporarily while working on them. There are different types of clamps, such as C-clamp, G-clamp, F-clamp and spring clamp.
- **Measuring instruments**: These are used to measure the dimensions, angles, distances and other properties of metal parts. There are different types of measuring instruments, such as ruler, caliper, micrometer, protractor, vernier, gauge and level.

## Sheet metal tools, equipment, devices and machines

Sheet metal is the process of forming, cutting and bending metal sheets into various shapes and sizes. Some of the common sheet metal tools, equipment, devices and machines are:

- **Shears**: These are used to cut metal sheets into desired shapes and sizes. There are different types of shears, such as hand, power, guillotine and swing-beam shears.
- **Punches and dies**: These are used to make holes, slots, notches and other shapes on metal sheets by applying force on them. There are different types of punches and dies, such as simple, compound, progressive and turret punches and dies.
- **Presses**: These are used to apply pressure on metal sheets to form them into various shapes. There are different types of presses, such as hydraulic, mechanical, pneumatic and arbor presses.
- **Brakes**: These are used to bend metal sheets along a straight line or an angle. There are different types of brakes, such as hand, power, box-and-pan and press brakes.
