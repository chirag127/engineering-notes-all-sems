### 2 Machine shop 3 Hrs

- A machine shop is a place where various machines and tools are used to cut, shape, and form metal or other materials into desired shapes and sizes.
- Machine shops can perform various operations, such as drilling, milling, turning, grinding, welding, and assembling.
- Machine shops can also produce custom-made parts, prototypes, or products according to specifications or drawings.
- Machine shops require skilled workers, such as machinists, operators, programmers, and engineers, who can operate and maintain the machines and tools safely and efficiently.
- Machine shops also need to follow quality standards, safety regulations, and environmental guidelines to ensure the quality and safety of the products and the workers.
- Some of the common machines and tools used in machine shops are:

  - Lathes: Machines that rotate a workpiece around a fixed axis and use cutting tools to remove material and create cylindrical or conical shapes.
  - Milling machines: Machines that use rotary cutters to remove material from a workpiece and create flat or curved surfaces.
  - Drilling machines: Machines that use drill bits to create holes in a workpiece.
  - Grinding machines: Machines that use abrasive wheels to remove material from a workpiece and create smooth or rough surfaces.
  - Welding machines: Machines that use heat or electricity to join two or more metal pieces together.
  - Measuring tools: Tools that are used to measure the dimensions, angles, or other characteristics of a workpiece, such as rulers, calipers, micrometers, gauges, etc.
  - Hand tools: Tools that are used to perform manual tasks, such as hammers, wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers, etc.
