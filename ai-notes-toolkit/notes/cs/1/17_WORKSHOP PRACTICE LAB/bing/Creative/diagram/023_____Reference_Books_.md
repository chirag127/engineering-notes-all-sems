### Reference Books:

- Reference books are books that provide information on a specific topic or subject, such as dictionaries, encyclopedias, atlases, handbooks, manuals, etc.
- Reference books are usually not meant to be read cover to cover, but rather to be consulted for specific facts, data, definitions, or explanations.
- Reference books are often organized alphabetically, chronologically, topically, or thematically, depending on the nature and scope of the information they contain.
- Reference books are usually authoritative, reliable, and credible sources of information, as they are written by experts, edited by publishers, and reviewed by peers.
- Reference books are useful for finding background information, overview, summary, or context on a topic, as well as for verifying facts, figures, dates, or spellings.
- Reference books are available in print or online formats, and some online reference books are updated regularly to reflect the latest information.
