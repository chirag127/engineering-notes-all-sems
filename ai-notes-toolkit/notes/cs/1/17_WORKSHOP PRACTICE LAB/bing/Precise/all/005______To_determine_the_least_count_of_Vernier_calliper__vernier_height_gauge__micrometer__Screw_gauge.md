#### To determine the least count of Vernier calliper, vernier height gauge, micrometer (Screw gauge) and take different reading over given metallic pieces using these instruments.

1. **Vernier Calliper**: The least count of a vernier calliper is the difference between the value of one main scale division and the value of one vernier scale division. To calculate the least count, divide the value of one main scale division by the total number of vernier scale divisions.

2. **Vernier Height Gauge**: The least count of a vernier height gauge is calculated in the same way as that of a vernier calliper. Divide the value of one main scale division by the total number of vernier scale divisions.

3. **Micrometer (Screw Gauge)**: The least count of a micrometer is the smallest distance that can be measured with the instrument. It is equal to the pitch of the screw, which is the distance moved by the spindle per revolution, divided by the total number of divisions on the thimble.

To take different readings over given metallic pieces using these instruments, follow these steps:

1. **Vernier Calliper**: Place the metallic piece between the jaws of the vernier calliper and close the jaws until they touch the piece. Read the main scale and add the vernier scale reading to obtain the final measurement.

2. **Vernier Height Gauge**: Place the metallic piece on a flat surface and lower the vernier height gauge until it touches the piece. Read the main scale and add the vernier scale reading to obtain the final measurement.

3. **Micrometer (Screw Gauge)**: Place the metallic piece between the anvil and the spindle of the micrometer and rotate the thimble until the piece is held firmly. Read the main scale and add the thimble reading to obtain the final measurement.
