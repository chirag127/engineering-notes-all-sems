# Course Outcome:

A course outcome is a statement that describes the knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to have acquired by the end of a course. Course outcomes are used to:

1. Define the goals and objectives of a course.
2. Communicate expectations to students.
3. Guide the development of course content and assessments.
4. Evaluate the effectiveness of a course in achieving its goals.

Course outcomes should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. They should be aligned with the overall program outcomes and the institution's mission and goals. Course outcomes should be regularly reviewed and updated to ensure that they remain relevant and effective in achieving the desired learning outcomes for students.
