#### To study and use of different types of tools, equipment, devices & machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section

Fitting, sheet metal, and welding are important processes in the manufacturing industry. These processes require the use of various tools, equipment, devices, and machines to achieve the desired results. Below is a list of some of the common tools, equipment, devices, and machines used in fitting, sheet metal, and welding section:

1. **Fitting tools:** These include tools such as hammers, chisels, files, hacksaws, and hand reamers. These tools are used for cutting, shaping, and finishing metal parts.

2. **Sheet metal tools:** These include tools such as snips, shears, punches, and dies. These tools are used for cutting, bending, and shaping sheet metal.

3. **Welding equipment:** This includes equipment such as welding machines, welding torches, and welding electrodes. These are used for joining metal parts together through the process of welding.

4. **Measuring and marking tools:** These include tools such as rulers, calipers, and scribers. These tools are used for measuring and marking metal parts to ensure accuracy and precision.

5. **Machines:** These include machines such as lathes, milling machines, and drill presses. These machines are used for cutting, shaping, and drilling metal parts.

It is important to note that the specific tools, equipment, devices, and machines used in fitting, sheet metal, and welding section may vary depending on the specific task and the materials being used. It is important to use the appropriate tools and equipment for the task at hand to ensure safety and efficiency.
