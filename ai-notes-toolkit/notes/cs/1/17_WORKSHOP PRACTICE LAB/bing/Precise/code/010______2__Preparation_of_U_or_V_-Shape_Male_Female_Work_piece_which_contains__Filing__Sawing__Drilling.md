#### 2. Preparation of U or V -Shape Male Female Work piece which contains: Filing, Sawing, Drilling, Grinding.

The preparation of U or V-shaped male and female work pieces involves several steps, including filing, sawing, drilling, and grinding. These steps are necessary to achieve the desired shape and fit of the work pieces.

1. **Filing:** Filing is the process of removing material from the work piece using a file. This is done to smooth out rough edges and to shape the work piece to the desired form.

2. **Sawing:** Sawing is the process of cutting the work piece to the desired size and shape using a saw. This is typically done using a band saw or a hacksaw.

3. **Drilling:** Drilling is the process of creating holes in the work piece using a drill. This is necessary for the assembly of the male and female work pieces.

4. **Grinding:** Grinding is the process of removing material from the work piece using an abrasive wheel. This is done to smooth out rough surfaces and to achieve a precise fit between the male and female work pieces.

These steps must be performed carefully and accurately to ensure that the male and female work pieces fit together properly. Proper preparation of the work pieces is essential for the success of the final product.
