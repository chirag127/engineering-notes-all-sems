#### 3. Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2, by HazraChoudhary , Media promoters and Publications

- Workshop Practice is a two-volume book set written by HazraChoudhary and published by Media Promoters and Publications.
- The books provide a comprehensive guide to workshop practices and techniques.
- Volume 1 covers topics such as hand tools, measuring instruments, marking and layout, fitting, sheet metal work, and carpentry.
- Volume 2 covers topics such as welding, forging, foundry, machine tools, and electrical and electronic workshop practices.
- The books are written in a clear and concise manner, with numerous illustrations and diagrams to aid understanding.
- They are suitable for students, apprentices, and practicing engineers who wish to improve their knowledge and skills in workshop practices.
- The books are widely used as reference material in technical schools and colleges.
