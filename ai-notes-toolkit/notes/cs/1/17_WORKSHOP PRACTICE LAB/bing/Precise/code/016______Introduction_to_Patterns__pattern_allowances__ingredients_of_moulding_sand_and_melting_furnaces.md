#### Introduction to Patterns, Pattern Allowances, Ingredients of Moulding Sand and Melting Furnaces. Foundry Tools and Their Purposes Demo of Mould Preparation and Aluminum Casting Practice – Study and Preparation of Mould for Plastic

1. **Patterns**: A pattern is a model or replica of the object to be cast. It is used to create a cavity in the moulding sand, into which molten metal is poured to produce a casting.
2. **Pattern Allowances**: Pattern allowances are small adjustments made to the pattern to compensate for changes that occur during the casting process. These include shrinkage allowance, draft allowance, machining allowance, and distortion allowance.
3. **Ingredients of Moulding Sand**: Moulding sand is a mixture of sand, clay, and water. The sand provides the structure for the mould, while the clay binds the sand particles together. Water is added to make the sand mixture pliable.
4. **Melting Furnaces**: Melting furnaces are used to melt the metal that will be poured into the mould. There are several types of melting furnaces, including cupola furnaces, induction furnaces, and electric arc furnaces.
5. **Foundry Tools**: Foundry tools are used to prepare the mould and handle the molten metal. These include moulding boxes, rammers, sprue cutters, and tongs.
6. **Demo of Mould Preparation and Aluminum Casting Practice**: In this demo, a mould is prepared using a pattern and moulding sand. Molten aluminum is then poured into the mould to produce a casting. The casting is then removed from the mould and any excess material is trimmed away.
7. **Study and Preparation of Mould for Plastic**: In this practice, a mould is prepared for casting plastic. The process is similar to that used for metal casting, but the materials and techniques used may be different.
