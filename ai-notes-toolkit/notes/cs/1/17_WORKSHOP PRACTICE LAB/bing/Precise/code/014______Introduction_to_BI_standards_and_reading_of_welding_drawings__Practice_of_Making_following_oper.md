#### Introduction to BI standards and reading of welding drawings

BI standards refer to the standards set by the British Institute of Standards for welding and fabrication. These standards provide guidelines for the design, execution, and inspection of welded joints and structures.

Reading welding drawings is an essential skill for anyone involved in the welding and fabrication industry. Welding drawings provide detailed information about the dimensions, materials, and welding processes to be used in the fabrication of a welded structure.

In order to read welding drawings, one must be familiar with welding symbols, dimensions, and welding terminology. Welding symbols are used to indicate the type of weld, its size, and its location on the drawing. Dimensions provide information about the size and shape of the welded structure, while welding terminology refers to the specific welding processes and techniques used.

#### Practice of Making following operations

1. **Butt Joint**: A butt joint is a type of joint where two pieces of material are joined end to end. This type of joint is commonly used in welding and can be created using various welding techniques such as TIG or MIG welding.

2. **Lap Joint**: A lap joint is a type of joint where two pieces of material are overlapped and joined together. This type of joint is commonly used in welding and can be created using various welding techniques such as TIG or MIG welding.

3. **TIG Welding**: TIG welding, also known as Gas Tungsten Arc Welding (GTAW), is a welding process that uses a non-consumable tungsten electrode to produce the weld. This type of welding is commonly used for welding thin materials and for creating precise welds.

4. **MIG Welding**: MIG welding, also known as Gas Metal Arc Welding (GMAW), is a welding process that uses a consumable wire electrode to produce the weld. This type of welding is commonly used for welding thicker materials and for creating strong welds.

These are some of the basic operations and techniques used in welding and fabrication. It is important to practice these techniques in order to become proficient in the art of welding.
