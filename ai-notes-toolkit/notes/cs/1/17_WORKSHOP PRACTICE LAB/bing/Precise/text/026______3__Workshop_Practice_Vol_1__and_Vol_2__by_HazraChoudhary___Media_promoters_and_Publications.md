#### 3. Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2, by HazraChoudhary , Media promoters and Publications

- Workshop Practice is a two-volume book set written by HazraChoudhary and published by Media Promoters and Publications.
- The books provide a comprehensive guide to workshop practices and techniques.
- Volume 1 covers topics such as workshop safety, hand tools, measuring instruments, and machine tools.
- Volume 2 delves into more advanced topics such as welding, sheet metal work, and heat treatment.
- The books are written in a clear and concise manner, making them suitable for both beginners and experienced practitioners.
- The books are widely used as reference material in technical schools and colleges.
- The books are well-illustrated with diagrams and photographs to aid understanding.
- The books are regularly updated to keep pace with the latest developments in workshop practice.
