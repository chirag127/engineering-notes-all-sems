## S. No. Mechanical Workshop Duration

1. Introduction to Mechanical Workshop: The duration of this workshop can vary depending on the depth of the topics covered. A basic introduction to mechanical workshop can take anywhere from a few hours to a full day.

2. Basic Hand Tools and their Usage: This workshop can take anywhere from half a day to a full day, depending on the number of tools covered and the level of detail in their usage.

3. Machine Tools and their Operation: The duration of this workshop can vary greatly depending on the number of machine tools covered and the level of detail in their operation. A basic introduction to machine tools can take anywhere from a full day to several days.

4. Welding and Fabrication: This workshop can take anywhere from a few days to a week, depending on the level of detail in the welding and fabrication techniques covered.

5. Computer-Aided Design and Manufacturing: The duration of this workshop can vary greatly depending on the software used and the level of detail in the design and manufacturing processes covered. A basic introduction to CAD/CAM can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks.

6. Robotics and Automation: This workshop can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks, depending on the level of detail in the robotics and automation technologies covered.

7. Quality Control and Inspection: The duration of this workshop can vary depending on the level of detail in the quality control and inspection techniques covered. A basic introduction to quality control and inspection can take anywhere from a few hours to a full day.

8. Maintenance and Repair: This workshop can take anywhere from a few days to a week, depending on the level of detail in the maintenance and repair techniques covered.

9. Health and Safety: The duration of this workshop can vary depending on the level of detail in the health and safety practices covered. A basic introduction to health and safety in a mechanical workshop can take anywhere from a few hours to a full day.

10. Project Management: This workshop can take anywhere from a few days to several weeks, depending on the level of detail in the project management techniques covered.
