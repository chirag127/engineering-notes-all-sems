#### To study and use of different types of tools, equipment, devices & machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section

Fitting, sheet metal, and welding are important processes in the manufacturing industry. These processes require the use of various tools, equipment, devices, and machines to achieve the desired results. Below is a list of some of the commonly used tools, equipment, devices, and machines in these sections:

1. **Fitting tools:** These include tools such as hammers, chisels, files, hacksaws, and hand reamers. These tools are used for cutting, shaping, and finishing metal parts.
2. **Sheet metal tools:** These include tools such as snips, punches, and shears. These tools are used for cutting and shaping sheet metal.
3. **Welding equipment:** This includes equipment such as welding machines, welding torches, and welding electrodes. These are used for joining metal parts together using heat and pressure.
4. **Measuring devices:** These include devices such as calipers, micrometers, and dial indicators. These devices are used for measuring the dimensions of metal parts.
5. **Machines:** These include machines such as lathes, milling machines, and drill presses. These machines are used for cutting, shaping, and drilling metal parts.

It is important to have a good understanding of these tools, equipment, devices, and machines in order to use them effectively and safely in fitting, sheet metal, and welding processes. Proper training and practice are essential for achieving proficiency in their use.
