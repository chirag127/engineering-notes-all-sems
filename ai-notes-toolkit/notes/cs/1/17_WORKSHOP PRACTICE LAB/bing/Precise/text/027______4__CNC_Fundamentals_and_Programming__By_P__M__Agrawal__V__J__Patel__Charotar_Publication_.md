#### 4. CNC Fundamentals and Programming, By P. M. Agrawal, V. J. Patel, Charotar Publication

CNC stands for Computer Numerical Control. It is a technology that uses computer-controlled machines to perform various manufacturing operations. CNC machines are widely used in industries such as aerospace, automotive, and electronics.

Some of the key concepts covered in this book include:

1. Introduction to CNC machines: This section provides an overview of the different types of CNC machines, their components, and their applications.

2. CNC programming: This section covers the basics of CNC programming, including G-code and M-code, and provides examples of CNC programs.

3. CNC tooling: This section discusses the different types of tooling used in CNC machines, including cutting tools, tool holders, and workholding devices.

4. CNC operations: This section covers the various operations that can be performed on a CNC machine, including milling, turning, drilling, and tapping.

5. CNC machine maintenance: This section discusses the importance of regular maintenance of CNC machines and provides guidelines for maintaining and troubleshooting CNC machines.

Overall, this book provides a comprehensive introduction to the fundamentals of CNC technology and its applications in manufacturing. It is a valuable resource for students, engineers, and technicians who are interested in learning more about CNC machines and their programming.
