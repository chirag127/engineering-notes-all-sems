### Reference Books:

- Reference books are a type of non-fiction book that provides information on a specific subject.
- They are often used for research or to gain a deeper understanding of a topic.
- Examples of reference books include encyclopedias, dictionaries, thesauruses, almanacs, and atlases.
- Reference books are typically organized in a way that makes it easy to find the information you are looking for.
- They are often considered authoritative sources of information and are frequently updated to ensure accuracy.
- Reference books can be found in libraries, bookstores, and online.
- It is important to evaluate the credibility of a reference book before using it as a source of information.
