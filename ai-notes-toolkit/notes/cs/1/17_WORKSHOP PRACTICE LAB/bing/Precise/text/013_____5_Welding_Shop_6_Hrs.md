### 5 Welding Shop 6 Hrs

1. Welding is a fabrication process that joins two or more pieces of metal or thermoplastics by heating the surfaces to their melting points and applying pressure to fuse them together.
2. Welding is used in a variety of industries, including construction, manufacturing, and repair.
3. There are several types of welding, including arc welding, gas welding, and resistance welding.
4. Welding safety is important and includes wearing protective gear, such as gloves, a welding helmet, and a respirator, and following proper procedures to prevent accidents.
5. A welding shop is a facility where welding services are provided. A 6-hour welding shop session may include instruction, practice, and the completion of a welding project.
