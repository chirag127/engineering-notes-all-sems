#### 2. Preparation of U or V -Shape Male Female Work piece which contains: Filing, Sawing, Drilling, Grinding.

The preparation of U or V -Shape Male Female Work piece involves several steps, including filing, sawing, drilling, and grinding. These steps are necessary to achieve the desired shape and fit of the work piece.

1. **Filing:** Filing is the process of removing excess material from the work piece using a file. This is done to smooth out rough edges and to shape the work piece to the desired form.

2. **Sawing:** Sawing is the process of cutting the work piece to the desired size and shape using a saw. This is typically done using a band saw or a hacksaw.

3. **Drilling:** Drilling is the process of creating holes in the work piece using a drill. This is necessary for the assembly of the work piece, as well as for the attachment of other components.

4. **Grinding:** Grinding is the process of removing excess material from the work piece using an abrasive wheel. This is done to achieve a smooth finish and to remove any burrs or rough edges.

These steps must be performed in the correct order and with the appropriate tools to achieve the desired result. Care must be taken to ensure that the work piece is properly aligned and secured during each step to prevent damage or inaccuracies.
