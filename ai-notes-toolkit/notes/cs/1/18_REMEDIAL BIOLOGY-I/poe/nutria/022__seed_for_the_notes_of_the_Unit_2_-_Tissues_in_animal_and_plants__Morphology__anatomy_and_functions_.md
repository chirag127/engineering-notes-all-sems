1. Tissues in Animal and Plants: Tissues are groups of cells that are organized to perform a specific function in an organism. In plants, tissues are divided into two main categories: meristematic and permanent tissues. Meristematic tissues are composed of actively dividing cells and are found in areas of growth. Permanent tissues are composed of cells that are no longer dividing and are responsible for the majority of a plant's functions.

2. Morphology: Morphology is the study of the form and structure of plants. It is used to identify the different parts of a plant and how they are arranged.

3. Anatomy: Anatomy is the study of the internal structure of plants. It is used to identify the different organs of a plant and how they are arranged.

4. Functions of Different Parts of Plants:
- Root: The root is responsible for anchoring the plant in the soil and absorbing water and nutrients from the soil.
- Stem: The stem is responsible for transporting water and nutrients from the root to the leaves.
- Leaf: The leaf is responsible for photosynthesis, which is the process of converting light energy into chemical energy.
- Inflorescence: The inflorescence is responsible for producing flowers.
- Flower: The flower is responsible for producing fruits and seeds.
- Fruit: The fruit is responsible for protecting the seeds and dispersing them away from the parent plant.
- Seed: The seed is responsible for germinating and producing a new plant.
