
### Stem for the notes of the Unit 2 - Tissues in animal and plants, Morphology, anatomy and functions of different parts of plants: Root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS

1. Tissues: Tissues are groups of cells that work together to perform a specific function. Plants have two types of tissues: meristematic and permanent. Meristematic tissues are actively dividing cells that are responsible for growth. Permanent tissues are specialized for a specific function and are found in the root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed.

2. Morphology: Morphology is the study of the form and structure of plants. It includes the study of the shape, size, and arrangement of organs and tissues.

3. Anatomy: Anatomy is the study of the internal structure of plants. It includes the study of organs, tissues, and cells.

4. Root: The root is the organ of a plant that anchors the plant into the ground and absorbs water and nutrients from the soil. It is composed of the root cap, root hairs, and the root vascular system.

5. Stem: The stem is the main support structure of the plant. It is composed of the epidermis, cortex, vascular cylinder, and pith.

6. Leaf: The leaf is the main organ of photosynthesis in plants. It is composed of the blade, petiole, and stipules.

7. Inflorescence: The inflorescence is a cluster of flowers on a single stem.

8. Flower: The flower is the reproductive organ of the plant. It is composed of the sepals, petals, stamens, and pistil.

9. Fruit: The fruit is the mature ovary of the plant. It is composed of the pericarp, placenta, and seeds.

10. Seed: The seed is the reproductive unit of the plant. It is composed of the embryo, endosperm, and seed coat.
