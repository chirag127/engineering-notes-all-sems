
### Cell Cycle

Cell cycle is a sequence of events that occur in a cell during its lifetime. It includes the processes of growth, DNA replication, and cell division.

#### Alleles and Genes

Alleles are alternative forms of a gene, which can be inherited from a parent. Genes are the units of heredity that are located on chromosomes and are responsible for the characteristics of an organism. 

#### Mendelian Experiments

Mendelian experiments are experiments conducted by Gregor Mendel to study the inheritance of traits. His experiments showed that traits are inherited in a predictable manner, according to the laws of segregation and independent assortment.

#### Cell Cycle (Elementary Idea)

Cell cycle is divided into two main phases: interphase and mitosis. During interphase, the cell grows, replicates its DNA, and prepares for mitosis. During mitosis, the cell divides into two daughter cells.

#### Mitosis and Meiosis

Mitosis is the process of cell division that produces two genetically identical daughter cells. Meiosis is a form of cell division that produces four genetically unique daughter cells.

#### Techniques to Study Mitosis and Meiosis

There are various techniques used to study mitosis and meiosis, such as light microscopy, fluorescence microscopy, and electron microscopy. These techniques allow scientists to observe the various stages of the cell cycle in detail.

#### Origin of Life

The origin of life is still a mystery. Scientists have proposed various hypotheses to explain the origin of life, such as the primordial soup hypothesis and the panspermia hypothesis.

#### Evidences for Biological Evolution

There is a large body of evidence that supports the theory of biological evolution. Examples of evidence include the fossil record, biogeography, comparative anatomy, and molecular biology.

#### Mechanism of Evolution

Evolution is driven by two main mechanisms: mutation and recombination. Mutation is a change in the genetic material of an organism, while recombination is the process of exchanging genetic material between two chromosomes.

#### Natural Selection

Natural selection is the process by which organisms that are better adapted to their environment are more likely to survive and reproduce. It is one of the main mechanisms of evolution.

#### Types of Natural Selection

There are several types of natural selection, such as directional selection, stabilizing selection, and disruptive selection. Each type of selection affects the population in different ways.
