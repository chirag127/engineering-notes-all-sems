
### Eukaryotic Cells

* Eukaryotic cells are the most complex type of cells and are found in plants, animals, fungi and protists. 
* They are much larger than prokaryotic cells and have a complex internal structure. 
* Eukaryotic cells have a nucleus, which contains the genetic material, and other organelles, such as mitochondria, chloroplasts, and lysosomes, which are surrounded by a membrane. 
* The nucleus is surrounded by a double membrane, known as the nuclear envelope, which contains pores that allow molecules to enter and exit. 
* The cytoplasm of eukaryotic cells contains a variety of structures, including ribosomes, Golgi apparatus, endoplasmic reticulum, and cytoskeleton. 
* The cell membrane is a selectively permeable membrane that regulates the flow of molecules into and out of the cell. 
* The cell membrane is composed of a phospholipid bilayer and proteins. 
* The cell membrane also contains cholesterol, which helps to stabilize the membrane. 
* Cell organelles are specialized structures that perform specific functions in the cell. 
* Mitochondria are organelles that produce energy for the cell. 
* Chloroplasts are organelles that are responsible for photosynthesis in plants. 
* Lysosomes are organelles that contain enzymes that break down molecules. 
* The endoplasmic reticulum is an organelle that is responsible for the synthesis and transport of proteins and lipids. 
* The Golgi apparatus is an organelle that is responsible for the packaging and transport of molecules. 
* The cytoskeleton is a network of proteins that gives the cell its shape and helps it to move. 
* Cell signaling is the process by which cells communicate with each other. 
* Cells use chemical signals to send and receive messages. 
* Compound microscopes are used to view cells and other small objects. 
* The basic chemical constituents of living bodies include proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, nucleic acids, and water.
