
### Root 

A root is a plant organ that anchors the plant in the soil and absorbs water and nutrients from the soil. It is the part of the plant that is usually underground and not visible.

* Tissues in Animal and Plants: Animal tissues are composed of cells and are grouped into four main types: epithelial, muscle, connective, and nervous. Plant tissues are composed of cells and are divided into three main types: dermal, vascular, and ground.

* Morphology: The root morphology of a plant depends on the species and environment. Roots can be either taproots, which are a single large root, or fibrous, which are many small roots.

* Anatomy: The anatomy of a root includes the root cap, root hairs, and the root apical meristem. The root cap protects the root from mechanical damage and the root hairs absorb water and minerals. The root apical meristem is the actively growing region of the root where cells divide and differentiate into different root tissues. 

* Functions: The main functions of the root are to anchor the plant in the soil, absorb water and minerals, and store nutrients. The root also plays an important role in the transport of water and minerals from the soil to the rest of the plant.
