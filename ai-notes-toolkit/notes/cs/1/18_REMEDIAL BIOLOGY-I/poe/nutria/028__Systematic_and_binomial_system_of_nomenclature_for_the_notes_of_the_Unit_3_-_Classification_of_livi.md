
### Systematic and binomial system of nomenclature for the notes of the Unit 3 - Classification of living organisms

* Systematic and binomial system of nomenclature is a two-part naming system used to classify living organisms.
* The two parts of the name are the genus and the species.
* The Five Kingdom Classification is used to classify organisms into five major groups: Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Protista and Monera.
* Major groups are further divided into subgroups according to their characteristics.
* The principles of classification in each kingdom are based on the similarities and differences between organisms.
* The Concept of animal and plant classification in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS is based on the evolutionary history of the organisms, the number of characteristics shared between them, and the diversity of their structures and functions.
