
### Evidences for Biological Evolution 

1. Fossil Record: The fossil record is a record of the changes in the species over time. It shows that species have evolved and changed over time.

2. Comparative Anatomy: Comparative anatomy is the study of the similarities and differences between different species. It shows that species have evolved from a common ancestor.

3. Molecular Biology: Molecular biology is the study of the structure and function of molecules. It shows that species have similar molecules and that these molecules have evolved over time.

4. Genomics: Genomics is the study of the structure and function of genes. It shows that species have similar genes and that these genes have evolved over time.

5. Embryology: Embryology is the study of the development of an embryo. It shows that species have similar development processes and that these processes have evolved over time.

6. Natural Selection: Natural selection is the process by which species adapt to their environment. It shows that species have adapted to their environment and that these adaptations have evolved over time.
