
### Concept of Animal and Plant Classification

* Classification of living organisms is the process of grouping organisms based on their similarities and differences.
* The five kingdom classification system is the most widely accepted system of classification. The five kingdoms are Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Protista, and Monera. 
* Major groups within the Animalia kingdom include vertebrates (animals with a backbone) and invertebrates (animals without a backbone). 
* Major groups within the Plantae kingdom include non-vascular plants (plants without a vascular system) and vascular plants (plants with a vascular system).
* Principles of classification in each kingdom include characteristics such as cell type, body structure, and nutrition type.
* Systematic nomenclature is the process of assigning scientific names to organisms, using a two-part Latin name. This two-part name is known as the binomial system of nomenclature.
* The concept of animal and plant classification is important in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS, as it provides the basis for understanding the diversity of life.
