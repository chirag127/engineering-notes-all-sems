
### Cell Organelles and Their Function

* Cell Membrane: The cell membrane is a thin, semi-permeable barrier that surrounds the cell and separates it from its environment. It is composed of proteins and lipids that allow certain substances to enter and exit the cell. 
* Nucleus: The nucleus is the control center of the cell and contains the genetic material of the cell. It is surrounded by a membrane and contains the chromosomes and other organelles. 
* Mitochondria: Mitochondria are the powerhouses of the cell. They produce energy by breaking down sugar molecules and releasing energy. 
* Ribosomes: Ribosomes are small organelles that are responsible for protein synthesis. They are composed of ribonucleic acid (RNA) and proteins. 
* Endoplasmic Reticulum: The endoplasmic reticulum is a network of membranes that are responsible for transporting proteins and lipids throughout the cell. 
* Golgi Apparatus: The Golgi apparatus is a series of membranes that are responsible for packaging and transporting proteins and lipids. 
* Lysosomes: Lysosomes are organelles that contain digestive enzymes that are responsible for breaking down molecules. 
* Vacuoles: Vacuoles are membrane-bound organelles that are responsible for storing molecules and waste products. 
* Chloroplasts: Chloroplasts are organelles that are found in plant cells and are responsible for photosynthesis.
