
### Leaf: 

* Leaf is the major organ of photosynthesis in plants and it is responsible for the production of food for the plant. 
* Leaf is composed of two layers, the upper epidermis and the lower mesophyll. 
* The upper epidermis is a single layer of cells that is covered with a waxy cuticle to protect the leaf from water loss. 
* The lower mesophyll is composed of two layers of cells, the palisade layer and the spongy layer. 
* The palisade layer is composed of tightly packed cells that contain chloroplasts and are responsible for the absorption of light energy for photosynthesis. 
* The spongy layer is composed of loosely packed cells that contain air spaces that allow for the exchange of gases. 
* The veins of the leaf are responsible for the transport of water and nutrients to the leaf and the products of photosynthesis from the leaf. 
* Leaf morphology, anatomy and functions vary depending on the species of the plant.
