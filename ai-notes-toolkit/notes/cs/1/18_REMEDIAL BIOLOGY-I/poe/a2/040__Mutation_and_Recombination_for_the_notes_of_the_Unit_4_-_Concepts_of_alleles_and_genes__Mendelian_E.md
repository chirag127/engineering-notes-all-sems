### Mutation and Recombination

- Mutation: Changes in the nucleotide sequence of DNA that can be inherited by offspring. It results in altered genetic information.
- Types of mutations:
    - Point mutation: Change in a single base pair. Eg: Substitution, insertion, deletion.
    - Frameshift mutation: Insertion or deletion of nucleotides that alter the reading frame.
    - Chromosomal mutations: Changes in chromosome structure like deletions, duplications, rearrangements.
- Causes of mutations: Errors during DNA replication or cell division, exposure to mutagens like UV radiation and chemicals.
- Recombination: Rearrangement of genetic material between two strands of DNA. It leads to new combinations of alleles and increases genetic variation.
- Types: Homologous recombination and site-specific recombination.
- Importance: It repairs damaged DNA, increases genetic diversity and facilitates evolution.
