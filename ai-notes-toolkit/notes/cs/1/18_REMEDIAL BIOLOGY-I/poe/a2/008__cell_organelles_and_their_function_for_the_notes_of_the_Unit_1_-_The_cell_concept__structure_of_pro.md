### Cell Organelles and Their Function

1. Nucleus: Control center of the cell that contains DNA and directs cell activities.
2. Mitochondria: Powerhouse of the cell that generates energy for cellular processes.
3. Ribosomes: Site of protein synthesis where proteins are made.
4. Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER): Transport network that transports materials within the cell. There are two types:
- Rough ER: Has ribosomes attached that are involved in protein synthesis.
- Smooth ER: Does not have ribosomes and is involved in lipid and carbohydrate metabolism.
5. Golgi apparatus: Packaging and delivery system that transports substances out of the cell.
6. Lysosomes: Digestive organelles that contain enzymes to break down waste and cellular debris.
7. Vacuoles: Storage organelles that store water, nutrients, waste, and enzymes. Common in plant cells.
8. Cytoskeleton: Network of filaments that maintains cell shape, enables movement, and transports materials. Includes microtubules, actin filaments, and intermediate filaments.
9. Cell Membrane: Outer layer that controls what enters and exits the cell. Made of a lipid bilayer with embedded proteins.
