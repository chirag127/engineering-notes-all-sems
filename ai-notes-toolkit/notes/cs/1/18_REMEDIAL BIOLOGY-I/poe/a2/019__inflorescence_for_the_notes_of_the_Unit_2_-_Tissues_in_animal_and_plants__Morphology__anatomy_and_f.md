### Inflorescence

- Inflorescence refers to the arrangement of flowers on the floral axis.
- Based on the arrangement, inflorescences are classified into:
-- Racemose: Flowers are arranged along a common elongated floral axis. e.g. Raceme, spike, corymb, umbel, etc.
-- Cymose: The main axis ends in a flower, and the lateral flowers are developed backwards. e.g. Cyme, helicoid cyme, etc.
- Advantages of inflorescence:
-- It helps in efficient pollination by promoting cross-pollination.
-- It helps in the collective dispersal of seeds.
-- It provides mechanical support to flowers.
