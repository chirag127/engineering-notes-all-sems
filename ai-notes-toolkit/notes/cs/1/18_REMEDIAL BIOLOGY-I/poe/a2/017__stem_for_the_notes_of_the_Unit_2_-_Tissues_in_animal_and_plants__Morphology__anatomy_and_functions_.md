### Stem for the notes of the Unit 2 - Tissues in animal and plants, Morphology, anatomy and functions of different parts of plants: Root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed.

1. Tissues in animals
- Epithelial tissue: Lines the outer surfaces of organs and blood vessels, involved in secretion and absorption
- Connective tissue: Binds and supports other tissues; e.g. blood, bone, cartilage
- Muscle tissue: Contracts to produce movement
- Nervous tissue: Transmits signals between different parts of the body

2. Tissues in plants
- Meristematic tissue: Responsible for growth and cell division
- Permanent tissue: Dermal, vascular and ground tissue
- Dermal tissue: Outer covering; protects inner tissues
- Vascular tissue: Transports water and nutrients; xylem and phloem
- Ground tissue: Fills space between dermal and vascular tissue; stores nutrients

3. Morphology, anatomy and functions of plant parts

Root:
- Anchor plant and absorb water/minerals
- Taproot: Carrot shape; fibrous: bushy roots

Stem:
- Connects roots to leaves; transports water/nutrients
- Herbaceous: Soft, green; woody: hard, permanent
