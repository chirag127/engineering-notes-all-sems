### Anatomy and functions of different parts of plants

**Root**:

- Anchor the plant to the soil and absorb water and minerals
- Primary root: First root that grows downwards from the radicle
- Secondary roots: Branch out from primary root, increase surface area for absorption
- Root hairs: Tiny extensions of epidermal cells to increase surface area for absorption
- Root cap: Protects tender apex of root as it grows through soil

**Stem**:

- Transport water and minerals upwards and carbohydrates downwards
- Herbaceous stem: Soft, green, found in annual plants; contains chlorophyll
- Woody stem: Thick, hard, found in perennial plants; contains lignin
- Nodes: Points where leaves attach
- Internodes: Stem segments between nodes
- Vascular bundles: Conduct water, minerals, and carbohydrates; arranged in a ring in cross-section

**Leaf**:

- Carry out photosynthesis
- Flat, thin, and broad to absorb maximum light
- Epidermis: Outer layer that protects inner tissues
- Stomata: Microscopic pores that allow gas exchange
- Mesophyll: Internal spongy and palisade layers containing chlorophyll where photosynthesis occurs
- Vascular bundles: Veins that transport water, minerals, and carbohydrates
