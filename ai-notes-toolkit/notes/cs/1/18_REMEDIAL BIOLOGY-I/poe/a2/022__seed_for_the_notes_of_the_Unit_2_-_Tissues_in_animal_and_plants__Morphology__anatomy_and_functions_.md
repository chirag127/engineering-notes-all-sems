### Seed for the notes of the Unit 2 - Tissues in animal and plants, Morphology, anatomy and functions of different parts of plants: Root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed. in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS.

1. Tissues:
- Plant tissues: Meristematic and permanent tissues (parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma, xylem and phloem)
- Animal tissues: Epithelial, connective, muscular and nervous tissues

2. Root:
- Origin - Radicle of seed embryo
- Functions - Absorption of water and minerals, anchorage and storage
- Modifications - Taproot, fibrous, adventitious, aerial
- Regions - Root cap, meristematic zone, elongation zone, maturation zone

3. Stem:
- Functions - Bearing leaves, flowers and fruits, conduction and storage
- Modifications - Herbaceous, woody, climbing, underground
- Regions - Nodes and internodes, leaf scars, lenticels
- Internal structure - Epidermis, cortex, stele (vascular bundles, pith)
