## Unit 1 - The cell concept, structure of prokaryotic, eukaryotic cells, plant cells and animal cells

- Cell: Basic structural and functional unit of life. Cells are microscopic and composed of cellular components surrounded by a plasma membrane.
- Prokaryotic cells: Cells without a nucleus, e.g. bacteria. Have naked DNA and lack membrane-bound organelles.
- Eukaryotic cells: Cells with a nucleus enclosed by a nuclear membrane, e.g. animal and plant cells. Have DNA contained within the nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
- Plant cells: Eukaryotic cells with a cell wall made of cellulose, chloroplasts and a large central vacuole. Cell wall provides structure and rigidity. Chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis. Vacuole stores nutrients and helps maintain cell shape.
- Animal cells: Eukaryotic cells without a cell wall, chloroplasts or vacuoles. Have mitochondria which produce ATP via cellular respiration. Shape maintained by cytoskeleton.
