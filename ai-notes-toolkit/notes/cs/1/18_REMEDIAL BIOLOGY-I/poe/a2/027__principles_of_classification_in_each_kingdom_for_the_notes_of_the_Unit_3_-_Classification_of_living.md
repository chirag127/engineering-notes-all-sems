### Principles of classification in each kingdom

**Monera (Prokaryotes):**

- Unicellular prokaryotic organisms
- Cell structure: Lack nucleus and membrane bound organelles
- Reproduction: Asexual (binary fission)
- Examples: Bacteria, Blue green algae

**Classification criteria:**

- Cell structure: Prokaryotic/Eukaryotic
- Nutrition: Autotrophic/Heterotrophic
-Adaptations: Structural, physiological and reproductive

**Protista (Eukaryotes):**

- Unicellular/multicellular eukaryotic organisms
- Cell structure: Presence of nucleus and membrane bound organelles
- Reproduction: Asexual (binary fission, budding, fragmentation)/Sexual
- Examples: Amoeba, Plasmodium, Euglena, etc.

**Classification criteria:**

- Cell structure: Prokaryotic/Eukaryotic
- Mode of nutrition: Autotrophic/Heterotrophic/Mixotrophic
- Locomotion: Cilia/Flagella/Pseudopodia/Non-motile
