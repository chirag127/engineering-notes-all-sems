### Five kingdom classification for the notes of the Unit 3 - Classification of living organisms (Five kingdom classification, major groups and principles of classification in each kingdom), Systematic and binomial system of nomenclature, Concept of animal and plant classification in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS.

1. Five kingdom classification: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia
- Monera: Prokaryotic cells, eg. Bacteria
- Protista: Eukaryotic unicellular, eg. Amoeba, Plasmodium
- Fungi: Eukaryotic heterotrophic, eg. Mushroom, Aspergillus
- Plantae: Eukaryotic autotrophic, eg. Plants, Algae
- Animalia: Eukaryotic heterotrophic, eg. Animals

2. Major groups under each kingdom:
- Monera: Archaebacteria, Eubacteria
- Protista: Protozoa, Algae
- Fungi: Zygomycota, Ascomycota, Basidiomycota
- Plantae: Bryophyta, Pteridophyta, Gymnosperms, Angiosperms
- Animalia: Porifera, Cnidaria, Platyhelminthes, Nematoda, Mollusca, Arthropoda, Echinodermata, Chordata

3. Principles of classification:
- Similarity: Based on morphological, anatomical and physiological similarities
- Homology: Based on evolutionary relationships and common descent
- Hierarchy: Classificatory categories are arranged in a hierarchy: Kingdom, Phylum, Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species

4. Systematic and binomial system of nomenclature:
- Genus and species: Two word Latin name for each organism
- Eg. Homo sapiens (Genus, Species)

5. Concept of animal and plant classification:
- Based on fundamental features and evolutionary relationships
- Aids in identification, communication and understanding biodiversity
