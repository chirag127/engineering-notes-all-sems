### Tissues in animal and plants

- Tissues are groups of similar cells that work together to perform a specific function.
- There are four main types of animal tissues: epithelial, connective, muscle, and nervous.
- There are three main types of plant tissues: epidermis, ground tissue, and vascular tissue.
- Some examples of animal tissues are blood, cartilage, muscle, and nerve.
- Some examples of plant tissues are xylem, phloem, parenchyma, and sclerenchyma.
- Animal tissues are mostly made of living cells, while plant tissues have many dead cells.
- Animal tissues do not have cell walls, while plant tissues have cell walls made of cellulose.
- Animal tissues are specialized for functions such as movement, communication, and protection, while plant tissues are specialized for functions such as transport, support, and photosynthesis .
