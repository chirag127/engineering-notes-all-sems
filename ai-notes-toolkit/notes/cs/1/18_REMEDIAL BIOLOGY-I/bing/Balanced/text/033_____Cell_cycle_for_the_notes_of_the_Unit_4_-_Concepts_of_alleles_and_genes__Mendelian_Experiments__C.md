### Cell cycle

- The cell cycle is an ordered series of events involving cell growth and cell division that produces two new daughter cells  .
- The cell cycle can be thought of as the life cycle of a cell.
- The cell cycle consists of four main phases: G1, S, G2, and M   .
- G1 phase: The first gap phase, where the cell grows in size and synthesizes proteins and organelles   .
- S phase: The synthesis phase, where the cell replicates its DNA and duplicates its chromosomes   .
- G2 phase: The second gap phase, where the cell prepares for mitosis by checking the accuracy of DNA replication and making any necessary repairs   .
- M phase: The mitotic phase, where the cell divides its nucleus and cytoplasm into two daughter cells   .
- The cell cycle is regulated by various checkpoints and molecules that ensure the proper timing and sequence of events   .
- The cell cycle is essential for growth, development, repair, and reproduction of cells   .
