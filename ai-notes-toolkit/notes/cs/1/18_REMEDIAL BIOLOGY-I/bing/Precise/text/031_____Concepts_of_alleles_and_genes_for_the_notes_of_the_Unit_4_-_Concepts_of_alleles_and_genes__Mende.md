### Concepts of alleles and genes

1. An allele is a variant form of a gene that arises by mutation and is found at the same place on a chromosome.
2. Genes are segments of DNA that contain the instructions for making proteins, which in turn determine the traits and characteristics of an organism.
3. Each gene has a specific location on a chromosome, called its locus, and can exist in different forms, or alleles.
4. The alleles of a gene can influence the expression of a trait in different ways, such as dominant or recessive inheritance.
5. In diploid organisms, such as humans, each individual has two copies of each gene, one inherited from each parent.
6. The combination of alleles for a particular gene in an individual is called the genotype, while the physical expression of the trait is called the phenotype.
7. The interaction between alleles, as well as the influence of environmental factors, can result in a wide range of phenotypic variation within a population.
