### Techniques to study mitosis and meiosis

- **Drawing**: Drawing both processes out on paper is a good practice exercise to deepen your understanding of mitosis and meiosis. Don’t forget to label the phases, and write a brief description of what is occurring in each.

- **Squash preparation of onion root tips**: This is a simple and common experiment to demonstrate mitosis in plant cells. You need to cut off some onion root tips, stain them with a dye that binds to DNA, and squash them on a slide. Then, you can observe the different stages of mitosis under a microscope.
- **Slide preparation of grasshopper testes**: This is an example of an experiment to demonstrate meiosis in animal cells. You need to dissect a chloroformed grasshopper and take out its testes. Then, you need to fix them in a solution that preserves the cells, stain them with a dye that binds to DNA, and mount them on a slide. Then, you can observe the different stages of meiosis under a microscope.
