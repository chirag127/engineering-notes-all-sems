### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation;

Here are some techniques that can be used when listening to Ted Talk audio with script reading to improve pronunciation:

- Listen to the audio several times and pay attention to the speaker's pronunciation of words.
- Repeat the audio yourself, paying close attention to your own pronunciation of the words.
- Use a dictionary to look up any words that you are unsure of, and practice pronouncing them correctly.
- Record yourself reading the script and listen back to it. This will help you identify areas where you need to improve your pronunciation.
- Practice reading the script out loud, focusing on pronouncing each word clearly and accurately.
- Use a phonetic alphabet to help you understand the pronunciation of difficult words. This can be especially helpful if you are learning English as a second language.
- Practice speaking slowly and deliberately. This will help you to enunciate each word clearly and make your speech more understandable.
- Use online resources, such as pronunciation videos or audiobooks, to supplement your learning and practice.
- Work with a tutor or language exchange partner to receive feedback on your pronunciation and practice speaking with a native speaker. 

By consistently applying these techniques, you can improve your pronunciation skills and become a more confident and effective speaker.
