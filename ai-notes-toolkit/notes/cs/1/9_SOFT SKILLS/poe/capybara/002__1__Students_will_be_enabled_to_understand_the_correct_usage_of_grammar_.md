### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

In order to communicate effectively, it is essential to have a good grasp of grammar. Here are some key points that will enable students to understand the correct usage of grammar:

- **Parts of Speech**: Students should have a clear understanding of the different parts of speech, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, and conjunctions. They should learn how to identify these parts of speech in sentences and understand their functions.

- **Sentence Structure**: Students should be able to identify the subject and predicate in a sentence and understand the different sentence structures, such as simple, compound, and complex sentences. They should also learn how to avoid common sentence errors, such as sentence fragments and run-on sentences.

- **Punctuation**: Proper punctuation is essential for conveying meaning in written communication. Students should understand the correct usage of punctuation marks, such as commas, periods, question marks, exclamation points, and quotation marks. They should also learn how to use punctuation to create clear and concise sentences.

- **Verb Tenses**: Understanding verb tenses is crucial for conveying the correct meaning in a sentence. Students should learn how to use the different verb tenses, such as present, past, and future, and understand the different forms of irregular verbs.

- **Agreement**: Subject-verb agreement and pronoun-antecedent agreement are important for ensuring that sentences are grammatically correct. Students should learn how to match the subject and verb in a sentence and ensure that pronouns agree with their antecedents in gender and number.

By mastering these key concepts, students will be well-equipped to communicate effectively and confidently in both written and spoken communication.
