### 2006

The year 2006 marked several significant events in various fields. Below are some of the notable events that happened in 2006.

#### Politics

- Ellen Johnson Sirleaf became the first female elected head of state in Africa, as she was inaugurated as the President of Liberia on January 16, 2006.
- The United Nations Security Council established the International Criminal Tribunal for Darfur on March 31, 2006, to prosecute individuals responsible for war crimes and crimes against humanity in the Darfur region of Sudan.
- The United States Senate confirmed Samuel Alito as an associate justice of the Supreme Court of the United States on January 31, 2006.

#### Science and Technology

- The New Horizons spacecraft was launched on January 19, 2006, with the mission to study Pluto and the Kuiper belt.
- Twitter was launched on July 15, 2006, as a social media platform that allows users to send and receive short messages called tweets.
- NASA's Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter was launched on August 12, 2006, with the mission to study the atmosphere and surface of Mars.

#### Sports

- The 2006 Winter Olympics were held in Turin, Italy, from February 10 to 26, 2006.
- The 2006 FIFA World Cup was held in Germany from June 9 to July 9, 2006, with Italy winning the championship.
- The 2006 Commonwealth Games were held in Melbourne, Australia, from March 15 to 26, 2006.

#### Entertainment

- The popular social networking website, Facebook, became available to everyone on September 26, 2006.
- The Disney Channel Original Movie, High School Musical, premiered on January 20, 2006, and became a cultural phenomenon.
- The James Bond film, Casino Royale, was released on November 14, 2006, with Daniel Craig as the new James Bond.

#### Natural Disasters

- On May 27, 2006, a magnitude 6.3 earthquake struck the Indonesian island of Java, killing more than 5,700 people.
- Hurricane Katrina hit the Gulf Coast of the United States on August 29, 2005, but its effects were still felt in 2006 as the government continued recovery efforts.

These are just some of the significant events that happened in 2006. It was a year full of accomplishments, challenges, and advancements across various fields.
