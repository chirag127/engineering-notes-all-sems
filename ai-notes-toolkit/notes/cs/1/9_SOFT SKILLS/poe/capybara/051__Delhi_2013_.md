### Delhi 2013

Delhi is the capital city of India and is known for its rich history, diverse culture, and vibrant food scene. In 2013, Delhi witnessed several significant events that had a lasting impact on the city's landscape and people. Here are some of the key highlights from Delhi in 2013:

- **The Delhi gang rape case:** In December 2012, a brutal gang rape and murder of a 23-year-old woman took place in Delhi, which sparked nationwide protests and outrage. In 2013, the trial of the accused began, and all the accused were found guilty and sentenced to death.

- **The Delhi assembly elections:** In December 2013, the Delhi assembly elections were held, and the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) emerged as the single largest party with 28 seats out of 70. However, they fell short of a clear majority, and the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) declined to form the government, paving the way for a new election in 2015.

- **The Commonwealth Games scam:** In 2010, Delhi hosted the Commonwealth Games, and several allegations of corruption and mismanagement surfaced. In 2013, the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) filed an FIR against several officials, including the former chairman of the organizing committee, Suresh Kalmadi.

- **The Uttarakhand floods:** In June 2013, heavy rainfall caused devastating floods and landslides in the north Indian state of Uttarakhand. Delhi played a crucial role in the relief efforts, with the government and several NGOs providing aid and support to the affected areas.

- **The Delhi high court verdict on Section 377:** In 2009, the Delhi high court had decriminalized consensual homosexual acts between adults, but the Supreme Court had overturned the verdict in 2013. This decision sparked widespread protests and outrage, with many activists calling for a repeal of Section 377, which criminalizes homosexuality in India.

Overall, 2013 was a tumultuous year for Delhi, with several significant events that had far-reaching consequences. These events highlight the city's complex social and political landscape and its role as a global hub for culture, politics, and commerce.
