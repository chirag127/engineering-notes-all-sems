### 5. Students will be able to build up personal traits that will make the transition from institution.

As students prepare to transition from their academic life to the professional world, it is essential that they develop certain personal traits that will help them succeed in their careers. The following are some of the personal traits that students can cultivate during their time in institution:

- **Self-discipline**: Students who possess self-discipline are able to manage their time effectively and prioritize their tasks. They are able to resist distractions and stay focused on their goals. Developing self-discipline can be achieved through setting goals, creating schedules, and sticking to them.

- **Communication skills**: Good communication skills are essential for success in any field. Students who are able to communicate effectively can express their ideas clearly, listen actively, and build strong relationships with their colleagues. Communication skills can be enhanced through public speaking, writing, and participating in group discussions.

- **Critical thinking**: Critical thinking is the ability to analyze information and make reasoned judgments. Students who possess critical thinking skills are able to evaluate arguments and evidence, identify biases and assumptions, and make informed decisions. Critical thinking can be developed through questioning assumptions, considering multiple perspectives, and analyzing data.

- **Adaptability**: In today's fast-paced and ever-changing world, it is essential for students to be adaptable. Students who possess adaptability are able to adjust to new situations, learn new skills, and embrace change. Adaptability can be developed through exposure to new experiences, seeking out challenges, and learning from failures.

- **Leadership skills**: Leadership skills are essential for success in many fields. Students who possess leadership skills are able to motivate and inspire others, make decisions, and take responsibility for their actions. Leadership skills can be developed through participating in group projects, taking on leadership roles in student organizations, and seeking out mentorship opportunities.

By developing these personal traits, students will be better equipped to make the transition from institution to the professional world. They will be able to communicate effectively, think critically, and adapt to new situations, which are essential skills for success in any field.
