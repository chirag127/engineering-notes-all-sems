### Speaking Style: Content and Sequencing

When it comes to public speaking, it is not just about what you say, but how you say it. The right speaking style, content and sequencing can make all the difference in delivering a successful speech or presentation. Here are some tips to keep in mind:

#### Speaking Style

- Speak clearly and at a moderate pace to ensure that your message is understood by everyone in the audience. 
- Use appropriate pauses to emphasize important points and give the audience time to absorb what you are saying. 
- Vary your tone and pitch to keep the audience engaged and interested in what you are saying. 
- Maintain eye contact with the audience to build trust and connection. 
- Avoid filler words like "um" and "ah" as they can distract from your message and make you appear less confident.

#### Content

- Start with a strong opening that grabs the audience's attention and sets the tone for the rest of your speech. 
- Use stories, examples, and anecdotes to illustrate your points and make them more relatable to the audience. 
- Keep your message simple and focused to avoid overwhelming the audience with too much information. 
- Use visual aids, such as slides or handouts, to reinforce your message and help the audience understand complex concepts. 
- End with a clear and concise conclusion that summarizes your main points and leaves a lasting impression on the audience.

#### Sequencing

- Organize your speech in a logical and easy-to-follow sequence to help the audience understand the flow of your message. 
- Use transitions between different sections of your speech to keep the audience engaged and show the connection between different ideas. 
- Group similar ideas together to make your message more coherent and easier to remember. 
- Use repetition to reinforce key points and make them more memorable for the audience. 
- End with a strong call to action that encourages the audience to take action based on your message.

By following these tips for speaking style, content, and sequencing, you can deliver a powerful and effective speech or presentation that resonates with your audience and leaves a lasting impression.
