## Prescribed Books:

Here are the prescribed books for your studies:

- "Principles of Economics" by N. Gregory Mankiw
- "Organizational Behavior" by Stephen P. Robbins and Timothy A. Judge
- "Introduction to Psychology" by James W. Kalat
- "Computer Networks" by Andrew S. Tanenbaum and David J. Wetherall
- "Marketing Management" by Philip Kotler and Kevin Lane Keller
- "Fundamentals of Financial Management" by Eugene F. Brigham and Joel F. Houston
- "Database Systems: Design, Implementation, and Management" by Carlos Coronel and Steven Morris
- "Business Ethics: Concepts and Cases" by Manuel G. Velasquez

Please note that these books have been carefully selected to provide you with a comprehensive understanding of the subjects. We recommend that you read each book thoroughly and take notes to aid in your studies. Additionally, these books may be supplemented with additional resources provided by your professors or instructors.
