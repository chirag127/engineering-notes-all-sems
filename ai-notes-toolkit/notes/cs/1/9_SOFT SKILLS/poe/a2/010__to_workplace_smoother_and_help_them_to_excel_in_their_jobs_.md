### Tips to excel at workplace

1. Communicate effectively
- Listen actively and take notes in meetings
- Be articulate while speaking and writing
- Share relevant information with colleagues proactively

2. Meet deadlines consistently
- Prioritize high impact tasks
- Break down large tasks into smaller milestones
- Ask for help if needed to avoid delays

3. Take initiative and look for opportunities
- Look for ways to go above and beyond your core responsibilities
- Propose process improvements and automation opportunities
- Ask for additional responsibilities and look for growth opportunities

4. Develop technical skills
- Keep learning and stay up-to-date with developments in your domain
- Take online courses or pursue formal certifications to strengthen your skills
- Contribute to open-source projects or start a side project to apply your skills

5. Build a strong relationship with your manager
- Meet regularly and communicate openly
- Understand your manager's priorities and how you can help
- Meet or exceed the expectations set by your manager
