### 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,

- Effective communication is essential to success in life, work, and relationships.
- Practical communication involves understanding the audience, choosing appropriate language, using proper medium, and ensuring clarity, concision, and correctness.
- Barriers to effective communication include information overload, language, cultural differences, emotions, and physical disabilities.
- To be an effective communicator, improve listening skills, be an engaged speaker, choose appropriate communication channels, and ensure proper feedback.
- Written communication requires structuring messages appropriately, using simple and concise language, checking for clarity and correctness, and matching style and tone to the audience and purpose.
- Presentations require preparation, organizing content logically, using visual aids appropriately, and practicing to build confidence and engage the audience.
- In the digital age, be aware of the pros and cons of communication via technology and implement cyber etiquette to ensure respectful communication.
