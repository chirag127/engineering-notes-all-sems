### 1. Write professionally in simple and correct English.

1. Use simple and clear language: Avoid complex words and convoluted sentence structures. Write in a straightforward manner using simple and easy to understand language. This allows the reader to comprehend the content easily without struggling with complex vocabulary or winding sentences.
2. Check grammar and spelling: Poor grammar and spelling mistakes reduce the credibility of the writer and reflect poorly on the content. Proofread thoroughly to ensure there are no errors. Get help from tools like spell check and grammar check.
3. Vary sentence structure: Using a variety of sentence structures makes writing more engaging and pleasant to read. Do not use all short sentences or all long, complex sentences. A mix of both creates a good flow and rhythm.
4. Avoid colloquial language: Informal expressions, slang, and idioms are not appropriate for formal writing. Keep the tone professional and avoid conversational language.
5. Ensure consistency: Use consistent tone, style, and voice throughout the writing. Do not switch between formal and informal or vary the style dramatically. This confuses the reader and reduces coherence.
