### Application of 4 A’s; Avoid; Alter; Access; Adapt

1. Avoid: Completely staying away from the activity, location or material which is causing the disability or barrier.
Eg: Avoiding stairs and using lifts or ramps. Avoiding crowded places for people with sensory disabilities etc.

2. Alter: Changing or modifying the environment, tools, equipment etc to enable participation.
Eg: Providing wheelchair ramps instead of stairs. Providing braille instructions on elevators. Modifying workplace furniture and equipment for people with restricted mobility etc.

3. Access: Providing means to approach and make use of a facility or system.
Eg: Providing wheelchair accessibility in public buildings. Providing audio and visual alerts for people with sensory disabilities. Providing accessibility features like screen readers, captioning etc for people with visual and hearing disabilities respectively.

4. Adapt: Adjusting attitudes, practices, procedures, equipment, tools or the environment to meet the needs of people with disabilities.
Eg: Adjusting work timings for employees with disabilities. Allowing service animals for people with disabilities. Providing alternatives to handwritten exams or interviews for people with certain disabilities. Training staff to be sensitive towards people with disabilities.

The 4 A’s - Avoid, Alter, Access and Adapt help in making reasonable adjustments and providing equal opportunities to people with disabilities. Applying these principles help create an accessible and inclusive environment for people with diverse disabilities.
