### Unit-4: Presentation and Interaction Skills 8

1. Eye Contact: Make eye contact with the audience. It helps to engage them and build a connection. However, do not stare at one person for too long. Move your gaze to different members of the audience.

2. Body Language: Have an open and relaxed posture. Do not cross your arms or appear tense. Use natural hand gestures to emphasize important points. Move around the stage or space to appear more dynamic.

3. Voice: Speak clearly by pronouncing words correctly. Vary your tone to keep the audience engaged, but do not be too dramatic. Project your voice to the back of the room so everyone can hear you.

4. Content: Ensure your presentation has a clear introduction, body, and conclusion. Structure your content logically and keep slides uncluttered with limited text. Include examples and stories to illustrate your key points. Practice your presentation several times to boost confidence and timing.

5. Handling Questions: Anticipate potential questions and prepare answers in advance. Repeat the question before answering so all can hear. If you do not know the answer, do not make up an response. Say you will get back to the person with a response or refer them to additional resources with more information.
