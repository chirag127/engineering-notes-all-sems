### Responding; Mental health at work place: Managing Stress; Techniques:

1. Take short breaks: Taking short breaks can help reduce stress and rejuvenate the mind. Step away from work for a few minutes and stretch, go for a walk or just relax.
2. Practice deep breathing: Deep breathing is very effective in reducing stress and anxiety. Taking a few minutes to sit quietly and practice deep, slow breathing can help calm the mind and body.
3. Limit workload: Having too much work or unrealistic deadlines can contribute to stress. Learn to delegate when possible and say no if you are taking on more than you can handle.
4. Stay positive: Having a positive and optimistic outlook can help reduce stress and make tasks seem more manageable. Try to maintain an 'I can do this' attitude.
5. Eat healthy and exercise: Eating a healthy, balanced diet and getting regular exercise is important for both physical and mental wellbeing. Exercise is a great outlet for your stress and also releases feel good hormones called endorphins.
6. Get enough sleep: Lack of sleep can increase stress. Aim for 7 to 8 hours of quality sleep each night to allow your body and mind to recharge, leaving you better equipped to handle stressful situations.
7. Seek professional help if needed: If you are experiencing persistent or severe stress, it is a good idea to seek professional help from a psychologist or counsellor. They can help you develop strategies to better deal with stressful situations and overcome negative thoughts or emotions.
