### 10. Soft Skills for Everyone. Jeff Butterfield, Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd; New Delhi 2017.

1. Communication Skills
- Listening: Paying attention to understand the message
- Speaking: Sharing your message clearly and concisely
- Writing: Conveying your message in a structured manner

2. Critical Thinking
- Asking questions
- Challenging assumptions
- Seeing multiple perspectives
- Making informed judgments

3. Problem-Solving
- Identifying the problem and root cause
- Generating multiple solutions
- Evaluating options objectively
- Taking action and reviewing results

4. Collaboration
- Sharing responsibilities and work
- Accepting inputs and feedback
- Compromising when required
- Helping others and building on diverse perspectives

5. Leadership
- Having a vision and communicating it
- Inspiring and motivating teams
- Recognizing and rewarding good work
- Taking ownership and being accountable
