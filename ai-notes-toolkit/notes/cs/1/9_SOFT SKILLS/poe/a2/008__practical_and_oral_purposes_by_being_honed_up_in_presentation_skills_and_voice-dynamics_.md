### Practical and Oral Purposes by being Honed up in Presentation Skills and Voice-Dynamics

1. Presentation skills:
- Maintaining eye contact with the audience
- Using hand gestures appropriately to emphasize important points
- Speaking clearly and confidently
- Using slides effectively to compliment the talk, not repeat what is being said
- Keeping the content concise and relevant to the topic

2. Voice dynamics:
- Speaking at an appropriate pace, not too fast or too slow
- Projecting the voice to reach the entire audience
- Using varying tones and emphasis to keep the audience engaged
- Pausing briefly after making key points for the information to sink in
- Using enthusiasm and energy to convey passion for the topic

The above points highlight how honing presentation skills and voice dynamics can help serve practical and oral purposes. By being cognizant of these techniques, one can become a more effective communicator whether giving a work presentation or speech. A polished delivery can keep audiences attentive and convey information with impact. With continuous practice, these skills can become second nature.
