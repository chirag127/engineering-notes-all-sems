### Unit-3: Reading and Writing Skills: 8

1. Read extensively: Read books, newspapers, magazines, journals, blogs, etc. to expand vocabulary and gain knowledge. Wide reading helps in improving comprehension skills and speed reading.

2. Read actively: Don't just skim through the text. Read with a pen and paper to note down important points. Summarize and paraphrase what you read in your own words. This makes you an active reader.

3. Read purposefully: Read with a purpose to achieve some goal like learning a new skill, hobby or acquiring information on a particular topic. Focus on themes and key ideas rather than every word.

4. Improve concentration: Avoid distractions while reading. Focus on understanding the text rather than just rushing through it. Take short breaks in between to reflect on what you read. This improves comprehension and recall.

5. Use reference materials: Refer to a dictionary or thesaurus while reading to understand the meaning of difficult words and find suitable alternatives. This expands vocabulary and enhances understanding.

6. Summarize and synthesize: Summarize what you read in your own words to reinforce learning. Synthesize and make connections between different ideas or arguments presented in the text. This demonstrates analytical and evaluative skills.

7. Share your insights: Share your thoughts on what you read with others through discussions or write a review. This helps in gaining a new perspective and retaining information for a longer time.

8. Practice regularly: Set aside time each day exclusively for reading and writing. Regular practice would improve speed, skills and confidence in reading and writing.
