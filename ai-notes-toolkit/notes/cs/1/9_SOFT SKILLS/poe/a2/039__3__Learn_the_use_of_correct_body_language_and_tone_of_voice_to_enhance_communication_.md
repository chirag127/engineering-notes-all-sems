### 3. Learn the use of correct body language and tone of voice to enhance communication.

1. Maintain eye contact: Making eye contact is important to appear engaged and interested. Eye contact shows you are listening and focused on the conversation. However, do not stare or make the other person uncomfortable.

2. Lean in slightly: Leaning in slightly shows you are engaged in the conversation. However, do not invade the other person's personal space. Lean in only slightly to show interest.

3. Nod occasionally: Nodding occasionally or saying "uh-huh" shows you are listening to the other person. Nod to encourage them to continue speaking and show you understand.

4. Open and relaxed body posture: Having an open and relaxed body posture makes you appear friendly and engaged. Do not cross your arms or appear tense. Relax your shoulders and have an open stance.

5. Appropriate facial expressions: Using appropriate facial expressions shows you understand the emotions of the conversation. Smile when appropriate to appear warm, or look concerned if the situation calls for it. However, do not overdo it and come across as insincere.

6. Match tone of voice: Matching the tone of voice of the other person shows you are engaged in the conversation. If they speak excitedly, increase your energy. If they are serious, have a serious and lower tone of voice. Matching tone shows you identify with them and the topic.

The above points describe using appropriate body language and tone of voice to appear engaged and enhance communication. Applying these techniques helps to build rapport and convey understanding. However, be sincere and do not overdo any of the techniques. Use them subtly to appear genuinely interested in the conversation.
