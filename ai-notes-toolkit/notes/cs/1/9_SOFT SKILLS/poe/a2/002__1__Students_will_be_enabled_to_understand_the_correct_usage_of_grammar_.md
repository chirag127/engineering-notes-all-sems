### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

1. Subject-verb agreement: Students will learn to ensure subject and verb agree in number. For example: "The team have won the match" is incorrect, it should be "The team has won the match".

2. Use of appropriate tense: Students will learn to use correct tense forms for sentences. For example: "I go to the market daily" is incorrect, it should be "I went to the market daily". Past tense should be used for a daily activity that has stopped.

3. Use of appropriate pronoun: Students will learn to use correct pronouns as per the context. For example: "Everyone must do their work" is incorrect, it should be "Everyone must do his/her work". When referring to a singular subject, singular pronouns should be used.

4. Use of appropriate punctuation: Students will learn the correct usage of punctuation such as commas, full stops, question marks, etc. to bring clarity in communication. For example: "How are you" should be "How are you?" with a question mark at the end.

5. Parallel construction: Students will learn to use parallel structure for words, phrases, and clauses that are parallel or alike. For example: "She is intelligent, caring and humours" is incorrect, it should be "She is intelligent, caring and humorous". Same forms of adjectives should be used.

Through consistent practice and application, students will be able to master the key grammar concepts and use correct grammar in speech and writing.
