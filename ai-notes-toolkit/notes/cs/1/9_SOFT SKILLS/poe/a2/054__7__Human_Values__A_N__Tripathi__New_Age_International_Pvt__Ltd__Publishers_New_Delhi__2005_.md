### 7. Human Values, A.N. Tripathi, New Age International Pvt. Ltd. Publishers New Delhi, 2005.

- According to the author A.N. Tripathi, human values are the ideals and principles that govern the conduct of human beings. They are concerned with the question of what is right or wrong for human beings.
- The author lists the following as key human values:
    - Truth: Being honest and sincere. Accepting facts and reality.
    - Righteousness: Being fair and just. Showing integrity in words and actions.
    - Peace: Showing harmony and absence of conflict. Promoting mutual understanding.
    - Love: Feeling of affection, care and concern towards others. Accepting people as they are.
- Tripathi argues that human values are universal and eternal. They are not affected by time, space, society or circumstances. However, their relative importance to individuals may vary based on personality and situation.
- To conclude, the author emphasizes the importance of human values in providing direction to human thoughts and actions. Only by adhering to human values can one achieve happiness and make progress.
