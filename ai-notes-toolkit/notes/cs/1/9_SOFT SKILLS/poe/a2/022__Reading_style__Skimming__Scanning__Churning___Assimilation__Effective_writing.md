### Reading style: Skimming; Scanning; Churning & Assimilation, Effective writing

**Skimming:**

- Roughly scanning the text to get an overview of the structure and content.
- Reading headings, introductions and conclusions.
- Glancing at diagrams/images and their captions.
- Not reading in detail.
- Used to quickly grasp the overall meaning.

**Scanning:**

- Searching for specific information.
- Looking for key terms or names.
- Reading selectively.
- Not reading everything - focusing on locating relevant details.

**Churning:**

- Rereading sections of text for better understanding.
- Going back over confusing sections.
- Reflecting on what has been read to reinforce learning and memory.

**Assimilation:**

- Making sense of what has been read by interpreting and reflecting on the information.
- Linking new information to prior knowledge.
- Considering implications and applications.
- This is necessary for effective and long-term learning.

**Effective writing:**

- Have a clear purpose and structure.
- Use an appropriate style and tone for the audience and purpose.
- Ensure key terms are defined.
- Use signposting language to indicate the development of ideas.
- Vary sentence structure.
- Check for grammar, spelling and punctuation errors.
- Ensure flow and coherence.
