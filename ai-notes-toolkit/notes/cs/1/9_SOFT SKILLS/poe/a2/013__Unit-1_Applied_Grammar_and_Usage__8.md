### Unit-1 Applied Grammar and Usage: 8

1. Use of Articles:
- Definite article 'the' is used before singular and plural nouns to refer to a particular thing or things.
- Indefinite articles 'a' and 'an' are used before singular nouns to refer to one thing of a kind.

2. Use of Subject-Verb Agreement:
- A singular subject takes a singular verb and a plural subject takes a plural verb.
- Eg: The boy (subject) is (verb) playing. The boys (subject) are (verb) playing.

3. Use of Tenses:
- Present Tense: To express an action happening currently or in the present.
- Past Tense: To express an action that happened in the past.
- Future Tense: To express an action that will happen in the future.

4. Use of Prepositions:
- Prepositions are used to relate a noun or a pronoun to other words in a sentence.
- Common prepositions: at, in, on, under, over, after, before, into, onto, etc.
- The correct preposition is chosen based on the context and the relationship expressed.
