### Management

1. Planning: Determining organizational goals and the actions to achieve them. This includes both strategic and operational planning.

- Strategic planning: Long-term plans to achieve broader goals. Eg. Expansion into new markets.
- Operational planning: Short-term plans to achieve specific actions. Eg. Meeting production targets.

2. Organizing: Designing the structure of work and the relationships among jobs and groups to achieve the goals. This involves defining jobs, grouping jobs, assigning people, and delegating authority.

3. Leading: Using influence to motivate employees to achieve goals. This includes communicating, resolving conflicts, recognizing good work, coaching, etc.

4. Controlling: Monitoring performance and making adjustments to achieve goals. This includes measuring progress, comparing to plans, and taking corrective action if required.

The key to effective management is implementing all four functions simultaneously and continuously to achieve optimal results. A deficiency in one area can have significant negative consequences for an organization.
