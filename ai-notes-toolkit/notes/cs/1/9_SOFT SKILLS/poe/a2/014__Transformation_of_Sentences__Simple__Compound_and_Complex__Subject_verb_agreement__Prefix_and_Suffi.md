### Transformation of Sentences:

1. Simple Sentence: It contains only one independent clause. e.g. "I am reading a book."

2. Compound Sentence: It contains two independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction. e.g. "I am reading a book, but I am unable to concentrate."

3. Complex Sentence: It contains an independent clause and one or more dependent clauses. e.g. "As I am tired, I am unable to concentrate on the book which I am reading."

### Subject-Verb Agreement:

1. Subject and verb must agree in number. e.g. "He goes to school."; "They go to school."

2. Subject and verb must agree in person. e.g. "I write articles."; "You/He/She writes articles."; "They write articles."

3. Collective nouns may take singular or plural verb depending on the sense. e.g. "The team hasfinished its work." (singular); "The team are divided in their opinion." (plural)
