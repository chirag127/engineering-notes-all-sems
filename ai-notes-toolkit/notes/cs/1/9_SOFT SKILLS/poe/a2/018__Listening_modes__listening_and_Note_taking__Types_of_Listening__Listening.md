### Listening modes, listening and Note taking, Types of Listening, Listening

**Listening modes**

- Active listening: Paying full attention to the speaker and understanding the message.
- Selective listening: Listening only to things that interest you and ignoring the rest.
- Passive listening: Hearing the words but not making any effort to understand the meaning.

**Listening and note taking**

- Listen for key terms and main ideas and note them down.
- Use abbreviations and symbols when possible.
- Organize notes by using headings and lists.
- Review and elaborate on notes within 24 hours.

**Types of listening**

- Informational listening: To gather information or facts.
- Critical listening: To evaluate and analyze information.
- Therapeutic listening: To help solve emotional problems.
- Persuasive listening: To influence or convince someone.

**Benefits of effective listening**

- Avoid misunderstandings and conflicts.
- Build trust and strong relationships.
- Gain different perspectives.
- Acquire new skills and knowledge.
- Foster creativity and problem solving.
