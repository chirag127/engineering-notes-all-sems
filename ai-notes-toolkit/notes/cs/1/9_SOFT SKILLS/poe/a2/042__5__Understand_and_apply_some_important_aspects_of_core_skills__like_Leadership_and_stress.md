### 5. Understand and apply some important aspects of core skills, like Leadership and stress

1. Leadership
- Lead by example: Set a good example through your own behavior and actions. Model the kinds of behavior and accomplishments you want to see from others.
- Communicate effectively: Share your vision and goals clearly. Provide feedback and recognize good work.
- Empower others: Delegate responsibilities and trust others to get the work done. Give people autonomy to work independently and make decisions.
- Take responsibility: Accept accountability for decisions and actions. Admit mistakes and take ownership of issues and solutions.

2. Handling stress
- Identify the sources of stress: Pinpoint the major causes of stress in your life. Then you can work to reduce or eliminate the sources when possible.
- Practice relaxation techniques: Try deep breathing, meditation, yoga, or massage to relax your body and mind. Making relaxation a daily habit can help reduce stress levels.
- Maintain a healthy lifestyle: Exercise regularly, eat healthy foods, get enough sleep, and avoid excessive alcohol or caffeine. Taking good care of your physical and emotional health will boost your resilience to stress.
- Adapt to circumstances: Learn to accept what you cannot change. Be flexible and willing to adapt to change what you can influence or control. Having a positive and realistic outlook can help reduce stress and anxiety.
