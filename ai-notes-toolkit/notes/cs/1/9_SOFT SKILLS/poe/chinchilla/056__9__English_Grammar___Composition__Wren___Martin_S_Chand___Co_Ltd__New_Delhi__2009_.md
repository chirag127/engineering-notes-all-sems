### 9. English Grammar & Composition, Wren & Martin S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi, 2009

This book is a comprehensive guide to English grammar and composition, designed to help students master the language and improve their writing skills. Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying this book:

1. Parts of Speech: The book covers the eight parts of speech in detail - nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections. Each part of speech is explained with examples and exercises to reinforce learning.

2. Tenses: The book explains the twelve tenses in English - simple present, present continuous, present perfect, present perfect continuous, simple past, past continuous, past perfect, past perfect continuous, simple future, future continuous, future perfect, and future perfect continuous. Each tense is explained with examples and exercises.

3. Active and Passive Voice: The book explains how to use active and passive voice in sentences, and provides exercises to reinforce learning.

4. Direct and Indirect Speech: The book covers direct and indirect speech, and provides examples and exercises to help students understand the differences between the two.

5. Punctuation: The book covers all aspects of punctuation, including commas, full stops, semicolons, colons, apostrophes, and quotation marks. There are exercises to reinforce learning.

6. Sentence Structure: The book covers the structure of sentences, including simple, compound, complex, and compound-complex sentences. There are examples and exercises to help students understand the differences between each type of sentence.

7. Paragraph Writing: The book provides guidance on how to write effective paragraphs, covering topics such as topic sentences, supporting sentences, and concluding sentences.

8. Essay Writing: The book covers the basics of essay writing, including how to develop a topic, how to structure an essay, and how to write an effective introduction and conclusion.

9. Vocabulary: The book includes a section on vocabulary development, providing tips on how to expand one's vocabulary and exercises to reinforce learning.

By studying this book, students can gain a comprehensive understanding of English grammar and composition, and improve their reading, writing, and communication skills.
