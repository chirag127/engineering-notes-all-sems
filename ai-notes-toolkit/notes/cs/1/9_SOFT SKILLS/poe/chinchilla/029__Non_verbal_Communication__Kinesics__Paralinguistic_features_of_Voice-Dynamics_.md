### Non Verbal Communication: Kinesics, Paralinguistic Features of Voice-Dynamics

Nonverbal communication is a type of communication where messages are conveyed without using words. It involves the use of body language, facial expressions, gestures, posture, eye contact, and voice dynamics. This type of communication is equally important as verbal communication and can be used to convey emotions, attitudes, and thoughts. 

In this section, we will focus on two important aspects of nonverbal communication: Kinesics and Paralinguistic features of Voice-Dynamics. 

### Kinesics 

Kinesics is the study of body language, including facial expressions, gestures, and posture. It is an important aspect of nonverbal communication, as it can convey emotions and attitudes that words cannot. 

Here are some types of kinesics: 

- Facial expressions: Facial expressions can convey a wide range of emotions, including happiness, sadness, anger, surprise, and fear. For example, a smile can indicate happiness, while a frown can indicate sadness or disapproval. 
- Gestures: Gestures are movements of the hands and arms that can convey meaning. For example, pointing can indicate direction, while waving can indicate greeting or farewell. 
- Posture: Posture refers to the way we hold our bodies, including the position of our arms, legs, and torso. It can convey confidence, relaxation, or tension. 

### Paralinguistic Features of Voice-Dynamics

Paralinguistic features of voice-dynamics refer to the non-verbal aspects of speech, such as tone of voice, volume, and inflection. These features can convey emotions, attitudes, and intentions. 

Here are some types of paralinguistic features of voice-dynamics: 

- Tone of voice: Tone of voice refers to the pitch and inflection of our speech. It can convey emotions such as anger, happiness, or sadness. 
- Volume: Volume refers to the loudness or softness of our speech. It can convey confidence, assertiveness, or shyness. 
- Inflection: Inflection refers to the way we vary the pitch of our speech. It can convey emphasis, sarcasm, or uncertainty. 

In conclusion, nonverbal communication is an important aspect of communication, and kinesics and paralinguistic features of voice-dynamics are two important aspects of nonverbal communication. By understanding these aspects, we can improve our communication skills and better understand the messages that others are trying to convey.
