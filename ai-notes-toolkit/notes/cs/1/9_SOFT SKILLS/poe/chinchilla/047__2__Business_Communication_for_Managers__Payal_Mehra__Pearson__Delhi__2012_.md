### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

In today's fast-paced business environment, effective communication is essential for managers to achieve their goals and objectives. Here are some key points to keep in mind when it comes to business communication:

1. Importance of Communication: Communication is the lifeblood of any business organization. Effective communication helps in building relationships with customers, suppliers, employees, and other stakeholders. It also helps in promoting teamwork, resolving conflicts, and making informed decisions.

2. Types of Communication: There are two types of communication - verbal and non-verbal. Verbal communication includes face-to-face conversations, phone calls, video conferencing, and presentations. Non-verbal communication includes body language, gestures, facial expressions, and silence.

3. Communication Channels: Managers need to choose the right communication channels based on the message they want to convey and the audience they want to reach. Some common communication channels include emails, memos, reports, newsletters, social media, and intranet.

4. Communication Skills: Good communication skills are essential for managers to effectively convey their message and persuade their audience. Some key communication skills include listening, speaking, writing, and presenting.

5. Barriers to Communication: There are several barriers to effective communication, such as language barriers, cultural differences, physical barriers, and emotional barriers. Managers need to be aware of these barriers and take steps to overcome them.

6. Communication Strategies: Managers need to develop effective communication strategies to achieve their goals and objectives. Some common communication strategies include setting clear goals, using appropriate language, providing feedback, and being empathetic.

7. Ethics in Communication: Managers need to communicate ethically and responsibly. They should avoid unethical practices such as lying, misrepresenting information, and manipulating their audience.

8. Communication Technology: Advances in communication technology have made it easier for managers to communicate with their stakeholders. However, managers need to be aware of the potential pitfalls of relying too heavily on technology and ensure that their communication remains human and personal.

In conclusion, effective communication is essential for managers to achieve their goals and objectives. By understanding the key points of business communication, managers can develop effective communication strategies and build strong relationships with their stakeholders.
