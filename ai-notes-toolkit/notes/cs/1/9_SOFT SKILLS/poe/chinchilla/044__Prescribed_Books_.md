## Prescribed Books:

When preparing for exams, it is essential to have the right books at hand. Here are some prescribed books to help you with your studies:

- **Mathematics:** "NCERT Mathematics Textbook" by RD Sharma or RS Aggarwal's "Quantitative Aptitude for Competitive Examinations."
- **Physics:** "Concepts of Physics" by HC Verma, "University Physics" by Sears and Zemansky, or "Fundamentals of Physics" by Halliday, Resnick, and Walker.
- **Chemistry:** "NCERT Chemistry Textbook" by OP Tandon, "Physical Chemistry" by Peter Atkins, or "Organic Chemistry" by Morrison and Boyd.
- **Biology:** "NCERT Biology Textbook" by SC Verma or "Biology: A Global Approach" by Campbell and Reece.
- **English:** "Word Power Made Easy" by Norman Lewis, "High School English Grammar and Composition" by Wren and Martin, or "The Elements of Style" by William Strunk Jr. and E.B. White.
- **History:** "India's Struggle for Independence" by Bipan Chandra, "A Brief History of Modern India" by Rajiv Ahir, or "The Wonder That Was India" by A.L. Basham.
- **Geography:** "Certificate Physical and Human Geography" by Goh Cheng Leong, "Oxford School Atlas" by Oxford University Press, or "India: A Comprehensive Geography" by D.R. Khullar.
- **Economics:** "Indian Economy" by Ramesh Singh, "Macroeconomics" by N. Gregory Mankiw, or "Microeconomics" by Paul Krugman and Robin Wells.

Remember that these books are just suggestions, and you should choose the ones that suit your learning style and exam requirements. Make sure to read them thoroughly and take notes to help you with your studies. Good luck!
