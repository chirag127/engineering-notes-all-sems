### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

In order to effectively communicate in any language, it is important to have a solid grasp of grammar. The following points will help students understand the correct usage of grammar:

1. Understand the basic parts of speech - Nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections. Learn their definitions and how they are used in sentences.

2. Learn about tenses - There are three main tenses: past, present, and future. Understand how to form each tense and when to use them in sentences.

3. Study subject-verb agreement - Make sure that the subject and verb in a sentence agree in number and person.

4. Learn about sentence structure - Understand the basic structure of a sentence and how to use punctuation to separate and combine ideas.

5. Practice using correct punctuation - Master the use of commas, periods, colons, semicolons, and apostrophes.

6. Understand common grammar mistakes - Learn to avoid common mistakes such as using the wrong verb tense, subject-verb disagreement, and using incorrect pronouns.

7. Read and write regularly - Reading and writing regularly will help you to better understand grammar rules and how they are used in context.

8. Seek feedback - Ask your teacher or peers to review your writing and provide feedback on your grammar usage.

By following these points, students can develop a strong foundation in grammar and effectively communicate their ideas in written and spoken language.
