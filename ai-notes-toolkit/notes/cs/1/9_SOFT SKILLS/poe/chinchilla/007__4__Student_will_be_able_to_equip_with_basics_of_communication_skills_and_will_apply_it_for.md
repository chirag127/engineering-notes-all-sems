### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for

In order to become an effective communicator, it is essential to master the basics of communication skills. The following points will help students equip themselves with the necessary skills to communicate effectively:

1. Understanding the purpose of communication: Communication is a means of conveying information, ideas, thoughts, and feelings to others. Students should understand the purpose of communication and tailor their message accordingly.

2. Listening skills: Effective communication is not just about conveying a message but also about actively listening to others. Students should develop their listening skills by paying attention, asking questions, and reflecting on what is being said.

3. Verbal communication: Students should learn to express their thoughts clearly and concisely. This includes using appropriate language, tone, and body language to convey their message effectively.

4. Written communication: In today's digital age, written communication is an essential skill. Students should learn to write clearly and concisely, using proper grammar, punctuation, and formatting.

5. Non-verbal communication: Non-verbal communication includes body language, facial expressions, and tone of voice. Students should learn to use non-verbal cues effectively to convey their message and interpret the non-verbal cues of others.

6. Feedback: Feedback is an essential component of effective communication. Students should learn to give and receive feedback constructively, in a manner that is respectful and supportive.

7. Cultural sensitivity: In a diverse world, it is essential to be culturally sensitive and aware of the differences in communication styles and customs. Students should learn to communicate effectively with people from different cultures and backgrounds.

By mastering these essential communication skills, students will be able to apply them effectively in various contexts, including personal, academic, and professional settings. Effective communication is a critical skill that will benefit students throughout their lives.
