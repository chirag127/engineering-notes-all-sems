# 2. Students will be able to converse well with effective speaking and listening skills in English.

- To converse well in English, students need to develop both their speaking and listening skills.
- Speaking skills involve the ability to produce clear, coherent, and appropriate speech in different contexts and for different purposes.
- Listening skills involve the ability to comprehend, interpret, and respond to spoken messages in various situations and with different accents and dialects.
- Some of the factors that affect the development of speaking and listening skills are:

  - The level of exposure to the target language and culture
  - The motivation and attitude of the learner
  - The feedback and correction from the interlocutor or the teacher
  - The opportunities and strategies for practice and interaction
  - The awareness and use of linguistic and pragmatic features of the language
  - The confidence and self-esteem of the speaker and listener

- Some of the strategies that can help students improve their speaking and listening skills are:

  - Listening to authentic and diverse materials, such as podcasts, videos, songs, speeches, etc.
  - Speaking with native and non-native speakers of English, both online and offline
  - Participating in various communicative tasks, such as role-plays, debates, presentations, interviews, etc.
  - Recording and reviewing one's own speech and identifying areas of improvement
  - Seeking and providing constructive feedback and correction
  - Using dictionaries, grammar books, and online resources to learn new vocabulary, expressions, and structures
  - Practicing pronunciation, intonation, stress, and rhythm
  - Developing metacognitive and metalinguistic skills, such as planning, monitoring, evaluating, and reflecting on one's own performance
  - Setting realistic and achievable goals and tracking one's progress
