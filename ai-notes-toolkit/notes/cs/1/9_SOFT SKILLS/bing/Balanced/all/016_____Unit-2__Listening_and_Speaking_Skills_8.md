# Unit-2: Listening and Speaking Skills

- Listening and speaking skills are essential for effective communication in any language.
- Listening is the process of receiving, understanding, and interpreting spoken messages.
- Speaking is the process of producing and delivering spoken messages.
- Listening and speaking skills are interrelated and interdependent. They influence and support each other in various ways.
- Listening and speaking skills can be improved by practicing various strategies and techniques, such as:

  - Setting a purpose and a goal for listening or speaking.
  - Activating prior knowledge and making predictions about the topic.
  - Using contextual clues and non-verbal cues to aid comprehension or expression.
  - Asking and answering questions to clarify or elaborate on the message.
  - Providing feedback and summarizing the main points of the message.
  - Reflecting on and evaluating one's own listening or speaking performance.

- Listening and speaking skills can be applied to various contexts and purposes, such as:

  - Academic listening and speaking: listening to lectures, presentations, or discussions; participating in seminars, tutorials, or group projects; giving oral reports, speeches, or debates.
  - Social listening and speaking: listening to conversations, stories, or jokes; engaging in small talk, casual chat, or gossip; expressing opinions, feelings, or emotions; making requests, suggestions, or complaints.
  - Professional listening and speaking: listening to instructions, directions, or announcements; conducting interviews, meetings, or negotiations; making phone calls, announcements, or presentations; persuading, convincing, or influencing others.
