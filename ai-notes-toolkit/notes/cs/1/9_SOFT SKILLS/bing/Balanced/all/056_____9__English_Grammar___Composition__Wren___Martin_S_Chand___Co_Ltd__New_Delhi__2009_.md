# 9. English Grammar & Composition, Wren & Martin S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi, 2009.

- This is a book that covers the fundamentals of English grammar and composition for students of various levels.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part I deals with grammar and Part II deals with composition.
- Part I consists of 69 chapters that cover topics such as parts of speech, phrases and clauses, sentence structure, punctuation, spelling, vocabulary, and usage.
- Part II consists of 19 chapters that cover topics such as paragraph writing, essay writing, letter writing, precis writing, comprehension, expansion, and note making.
- The book also contains exercises, examples, illustrations, and appendices for reference and practice.
- The book is written in a clear, concise, and systematic manner, with ample examples and explanations.
- The book is suitable for students preparing for various examinations, such as school, college, and competitive exams, as well as for general readers who want to improve their English skills.
