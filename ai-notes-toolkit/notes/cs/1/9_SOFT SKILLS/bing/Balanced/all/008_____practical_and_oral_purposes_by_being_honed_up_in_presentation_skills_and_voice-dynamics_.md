# Practical and oral purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics

- Presentation skills and voice-dynamics are important for practical and oral purposes, such as delivering speeches, lectures, reports, interviews, or pitches.
- Presentation skills refer to the ability to plan, prepare, and deliver effective and engaging presentations that convey the intended message and achieve the desired outcome.
- Voice-dynamics refer to the use of vocal elements, such as volume, pitch, tone, pace, and pauses, to enhance the delivery and impact of the presentation.
- Some of the benefits of being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics are:

  - It can increase the confidence and credibility of the speaker, as well as the interest and attention of the audience.
  - It can improve the clarity and coherence of the presentation, as well as the retention and recall of the information by the audience.
  - It can help the speaker to adapt to different situations, audiences, and purposes, as well as to handle questions and feedback effectively.
  - It can enhance the speaker's personal and professional development, as well as the reputation and image of the organization or institution they represent.

- Some of the ways to be honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics are:

  - Analyze the purpose, audience, and context of the presentation, and tailor the content and delivery accordingly.
  - Structure the presentation with a clear introduction, body, and conclusion, and use transitions and signposts to guide the audience.
  - Use appropriate and relevant visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, or images, to support and illustrate the presentation.
  - Practice the presentation aloud, preferably in front of a mirror, a recorder, or a friend, and seek feedback and suggestions for improvement.
  - Pay attention to the voice-dynamics, such as speaking loudly and clearly, varying the pitch and tone, modulating the pace and pauses, and using emphasis and intonation.
  - Maintain eye contact, facial expressions, and body language that convey confidence, enthusiasm, and rapport with the audience.
  - Anticipate and prepare for possible questions and objections, and respond to them politely and confidently.
