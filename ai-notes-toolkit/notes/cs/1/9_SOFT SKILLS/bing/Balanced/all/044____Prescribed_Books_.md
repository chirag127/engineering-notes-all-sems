## Prescribed Books:

- Prescribed books are the books that are recommended by the syllabus or the instructor for a particular course or subject.
- Prescribed books are usually selected based on their relevance, quality, and availability for the course content and objectives.
- Prescribed books are intended to provide the students with the essential knowledge, concepts, and skills that they need to master the course or subject.
- Prescribed books may also include supplementary materials, such as exercises, case studies, examples, and references, that can enhance the students' learning and understanding.
- Prescribed books may vary depending on the level, discipline, and institution of the course or subject. Some courses or subjects may have more than one prescribed book, while others may have none.
- Prescribed books are not the only source of information or learning for the students. They should be complemented by other resources, such as lectures, notes, online materials, and additional readings, that can broaden the students' perspective and exposure to the course or subject.
- Prescribed books should be used effectively and efficiently by the students. They should be read carefully, critically, and actively, with attention to the main ideas, arguments, and evidence. They should also be reviewed, summarized, and applied to the course or subject context and goals.
