# ### and impact

- ### is a symbol that is used to indicate a heading or a subheading in a markdown document. It is also known as a hash sign or a pound sign.
- Impact is a noun or a verb that means a forceful contact or onset, a significant or major effect, or to have an effect or influence on something or someone.
- ### and impact are not synonyms or interchangeable words. They have different meanings and uses in different contexts.
- Some examples of how to use ### and impact are:

  - To create a heading in a markdown document, use one ### at the beginning of the line. To create a subheading, use two ### at the beginning of the line. For example:

    ```
    # This is a heading
    ## This is a subheading
    ```

  - To describe the effect of an action or an event on something or someone, use impact as a noun or a verb. For example:

    ```
    The impact of the pandemic on the economy was severe.
    The pandemic impacted the economy severely.
    ```

  - To express the idea or desire behind an action, use intent as a noun. To express the effect an action has on a person or a community, use impact as a noun. For example:

    ```
    His intent was to help the poor, but his impact was to create dependency.
    ```
