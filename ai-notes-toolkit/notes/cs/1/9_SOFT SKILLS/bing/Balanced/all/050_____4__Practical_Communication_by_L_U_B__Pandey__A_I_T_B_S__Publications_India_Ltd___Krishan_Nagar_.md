# 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- This is a book that covers the process and practice of communication skills in various contexts and situations.
- The book aims to help the readers develop their ability to communicate effectively and confidently in different settings, such as academic, professional, social and personal.
- The book consists of 16 chapters, each focusing on a specific aspect of communication, such as listening, speaking, reading, writing, non-verbal communication, intercultural communication, group communication, presentation skills, interview skills, etc.
- The book provides theoretical concepts, practical examples, exercises, activities, tips and guidelines for improving communication skills.
- The book also includes a glossary of key terms, a bibliography of useful references and an index for easy access to the topics.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, professionals and anyone who wants to enhance their communication skills.
