# 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

- This is a textbook that aims to equip readers with the necessary skills required to be an effective communicator and a successful professional in the competitive world.
- The book covers various topics such as personality development, soft skills, communication skills, resume writing, group discussion, interview skills, presentation skills, business etiquette, and corporate culture.
- The book also provides practical tips, examples, exercises, self-assessment tests, and case studies to help readers apply the concepts and techniques in real-life situations.
- The book is divided into 15 chapters, each focusing on a specific aspect of personality development and soft skills. The chapters are as follows:

  - Chapter 1: Personality Development: A Must for Leadership and Career Growth
  - Chapter 2: Soft Skills: Demanded by Every Employer
  - Chapter 3: Communication Skills: Spoken English, Phonetics, Accent, Intonation
  - Chapter 4: Your Resume or Curriculum Vitae: The First Step Forward
  - Chapter 5: Group Discussion: A Test of Your Soft Skills
  - Chapter 6: Interview Skills: The Final Hurdle
  - Chapter 7: Presentation Skills: A Key to Success
  - Chapter 8: Business Etiquette: The Art of Politeness
  - Chapter 9: Corporate Culture: The Way Things Work
  - Chapter 10: Teamwork: A Prerequisite for Organizational Success
  - Chapter 11: Leadership Skills: A Quality of the Few
  - Chapter 12: Time Management: A Skill for Life
  - Chapter 13: Stress Management: A Necessity for Well-being
  - Chapter 14: Conflict Management: A Challenge for Everyone
  - Chapter 15: Ethics and Values: The Foundation of Character

- The book is written by Barun K. Mitra, who is a former professor of English and communication skills at IIT Kharagpur. He has over 40 years of experience in teaching, training, and consulting in the fields of communication, personality development, and soft skills. He is also the author of several books and articles on these topics.
