# 2006

- 2006 was a common year that started on a Sunday in the Gregorian calendar.
- It was the 2006th year of the Common Era (CE) and Anno Domini (AD) designations, the 6th year of the 3rd millennium, the 6th year of the 21st century, and the 7th year of the 2000s decade.
- Some of the major events that occurred in 2006 were:

  - The Winter Olympics were held in Turin, Italy, from February 10 to 26.
  - The International Astronomical Union redefined the term "planet", demoting Pluto to a dwarf planet on August 24.
  - North Korea conducted its first nuclear test on October 9, sparking international condemnation and sanctions.
  - Saddam Hussein, the former president of Iraq, was executed by hanging on December 30, after being convicted of crimes against humanity by an Iraqi court.
  - Other notable events in 2006 included the Lebanon War, the FIFA World Cup in Germany, the Montara oil spill in Australia, the Sago Mine disaster in the United States, the Mumbai train bombings in India, and the death of former Yugoslav leader Slobodan Milosevic.
