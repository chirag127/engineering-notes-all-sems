### Chronological etc, Official and Business Letter writing, Agenda, Notices

- Chronological etc is a type of resume format that lists the work experience and education of a person in reverse chronological order, starting from the most recent to the oldest. This format is suitable for people who have a consistent and stable work history, and who want to highlight their skills and achievements.
- Official and business letter writing is a form of communication that follows a formal and professional tone, structure, and layout. Official and business letters are used for various purposes, such as making inquiries, requests, complaints, invitations, proposals, orders, confirmations, etc. Official and business letters typically include the following elements: sender's address, date, inside address, salutation, subject line, body, complimentary close, signature, and enclosures (if any).
- Agenda is a list of items or topics that are to be discussed or accomplished in a meeting or event. An agenda helps to organize and plan the meeting or event, and to inform the participants of the objectives, expectations, and responsibilities. An agenda usually includes the following elements: title, date, time, location, attendees, objectives, topics, time allocation, and action items.
- Notices are brief and formal announcements that inform the public or a specific group of people about something important or relevant, such as an event, a change, a policy, a deadline, etc. Notices are usually written in a clear, concise, and direct manner, and include the following elements: heading, date, body, signature, and designation. Notices are often displayed on notice boards, websites, newspapers, or other media.
