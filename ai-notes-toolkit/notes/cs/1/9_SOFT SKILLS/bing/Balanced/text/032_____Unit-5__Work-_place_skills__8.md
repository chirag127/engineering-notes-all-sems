### Unit-5: Workplace skills: 8

- Workplace skills are the abilities and competencies that enable employees to perform their tasks effectively and efficiently in different work environments.
- Workplace skills can be classified into three categories: technical skills, interpersonal skills, and personal skills.
- Technical skills are the specific knowledge and abilities required to perform a certain job or task, such as operating a machine, using a software, or applying a mathematical formula.
- Interpersonal skills are the skills that enable employees to communicate, collaborate, and interact with others in a positive and respectful manner, such as listening, speaking, writing, negotiating, and resolving conflicts.
- Personal skills are the skills that reflect the individual's personality, attitude, and work ethic, such as self-motivation, time management, problem-solving, creativity, and adaptability.
- Workplace skills are important for employees to achieve their goals, meet the expectations of their employers and customers, and adapt to the changing demands and challenges of the work environment.
- Workplace skills can be developed and improved through various methods, such as formal education, training, mentoring, coaching, feedback, self-assessment, and practice.
