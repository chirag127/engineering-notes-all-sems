## Prescribed Books:

- Prescribed books are the books that are recommended by the syllabus or the instructor for a particular course or subject.
- Prescribed books are usually selected based on their relevance, quality, and availability for the course objectives and learning outcomes.
- Prescribed books may contain the core concepts, theories, principles, and examples that are essential for the course content and assessment.
- Prescribed books may also provide additional information, perspectives, and references that can enrich the learning experience and stimulate critical thinking.
- Prescribed books are not the only source of information for a course or subject. Students are encouraged to consult other sources such as journals, websites, podcasts, videos, etc. to supplement their learning and broaden their knowledge.
- Prescribed books may vary depending on the level, discipline, and institution of the course or subject. Some courses may have more than one prescribed book, while others may have none.
- Prescribed books may be available in different formats such as print, digital, or audio. Students should choose the format that suits their learning style, preference, and accessibility.
- Prescribed books may have different editions, versions, or translations. Students should check with their instructor or syllabus to ensure that they are using the correct or latest edition, version, or translation of the prescribed book.
