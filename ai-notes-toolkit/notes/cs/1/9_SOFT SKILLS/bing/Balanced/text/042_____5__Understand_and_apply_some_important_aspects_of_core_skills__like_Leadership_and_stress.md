### 5. Understand and apply some important aspects of core skills, like Leadership and stress

- Leadership is the ability to influence, motivate, and inspire others to achieve a common goal or vision.
- Some of the key skills of effective leaders are: communication, emotional intelligence, decision making, problem solving, delegation, and feedback.
- Communication is the process of exchanging information, ideas, and emotions with others, using verbal and non-verbal cues.
- Emotional intelligence is the ability to recognize, understand, and manage one's own and others' emotions, and to use them appropriately in different situations.
- Decision making is the ability to choose the best course of action among various alternatives, considering the available information, risks, and consequences.
- Problem solving is the ability to identify, analyze, and resolve issues or challenges, using logical and creative thinking.
- Delegation is the ability to assign tasks and responsibilities to others, based on their skills, abilities, and availability, and to monitor and support their performance.
- Feedback is the ability to give and receive constructive and timely information about one's own and others' performance, behavior, and outcomes, and to use it for improvement and learning.

- Stress is the physical and psychological response to a perceived threat or challenge, which can affect one's health, well-being, and performance.
- Some of the common sources of stress are: work demands, personal issues, change, uncertainty, and conflict.
- Some of the common signs and symptoms of stress are: headaches, muscle tension, fatigue, insomnia, anxiety, irritability, depression, and low self-esteem.
- Some of the strategies to cope with and manage stress are: relaxation, exercise, meditation, breathing, time management, prioritization, goal setting, positive thinking, social support, and seeking professional help.
