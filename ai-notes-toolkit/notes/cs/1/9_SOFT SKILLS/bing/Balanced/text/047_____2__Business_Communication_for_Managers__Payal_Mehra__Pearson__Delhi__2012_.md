### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

- This is a book written by Payal Mehra, an Associate Professor of Communication at the Indian Institute of Management Lucknow.
- The book is published by Pearson Publishing and was released in February 2012.
- The book is a student-friendly, practical and example-driven book that gives students a thorough knowledge of business communication, covering all the major communication topics included in MBA syllabi across the country  .
- The book begins by briefly analysing the various theories of communication. It demonstrates methods of effective communication in different business situations, such as interpersonal communication, group communication, cross-cultural communication, oral communication, written communication, and non-verbal communication.
- The book also covers topics such as business etiquette, listening skills, presentation skills, report writing, business correspondence, and crisis communication.
- The book includes numerous examples, case studies, exercises, and self-assessment tests to help students apply the concepts and skills learned in the book.
- The book is suitable for MBA students, managers, and professionals who want to improve their communication skills in the business context.
