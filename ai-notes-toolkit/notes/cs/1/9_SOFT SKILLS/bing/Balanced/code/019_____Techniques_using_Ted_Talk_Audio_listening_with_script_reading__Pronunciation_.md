### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation

- Ted Talks are short, engaging, and informative presentations on various topics, delivered by experts or enthusiasts in their fields.
- Ted Talks can be used as a resource for improving English listening and pronunciation skills, as well as expanding one's vocabulary and knowledge.
- Some techniques for using Ted Talk audio listening with script reading and pronunciation are:

  - Choose a Ted Talk that interests you and matches your level of English proficiency. You can browse by topic, duration, language, or rating on the Ted website or app.
  - Listen to the Ted Talk once without reading the script, and try to understand the main idea and the speaker's tone and attitude. You can also take notes of any unfamiliar words or phrases.
  - Listen to the Ted Talk again, this time with the script. You can find the transcript by clicking on the "Transcript" button below the video. You can also turn on the subtitles if you prefer. Pay attention to how the speaker pronounces the words, stresses the syllables, and uses intonation and pauses.
  - Repeat the Ted Talk aloud, following the speaker's pronunciation and rhythm. You can use the "Interactive Transcript" feature to highlight the words as they are spoken. You can also slow down or speed up the playback speed if you need to.
  - Record yourself repeating the Ted Talk, and compare your pronunciation with the speaker's. You can use a voice recorder app or a website like Vocaroo to record and play back your voice. You can also ask for feedback from a native speaker or a teacher if possible.
  - Review the vocabulary and expressions from the Ted Talk, and try to use them in your own sentences or conversations. You can use a dictionary or a website like Quizlet to look up the meanings and examples of the words. You can also create flashcards or quizzes to test yourself.
