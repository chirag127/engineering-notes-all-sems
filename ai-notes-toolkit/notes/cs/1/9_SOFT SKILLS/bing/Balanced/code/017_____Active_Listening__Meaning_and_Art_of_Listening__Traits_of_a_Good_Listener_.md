### Active Listening: Meaning and Art of Listening, Traits of a Good Listener

- Active listening is a skill that involves paying full attention to the speaker, understanding their message, and responding appropriately.
- Active listening is different from passive listening, which is simply hearing what the speaker says without engaging with them or showing interest.
- Active listening is important for effective communication, building rapport, resolving conflicts, and learning new information.
- Active listening requires the following steps:
  - Prepare to listen: Remove any distractions, such as phone, TV, or noise, and focus on the speaker. Adopt a positive and open-minded attitude, and avoid making judgments or assumptions.
  - Show interest: Use nonverbal cues, such as eye contact, nodding, smiling, and leaning forward, to indicate that you are listening and interested. You can also use verbal cues, such as "uh-huh", "I see", or "go on", to encourage the speaker to continue.
  - Listen attentively: Pay attention to the speaker's words, tone, body language, and emotions. Try to understand their perspective, feelings, and intentions. Do not interrupt, argue, or criticize the speaker, even if you disagree with them.
  - Clarify and summarize: Ask questions to clarify any points that are unclear, or to elicit more details or examples. Summarize what the speaker has said in your own words, and check if you have understood them correctly. For example, "So, what you are saying is...", or "Let me see if I got this right..."
  - Respond appropriately: Give feedback to the speaker, such as expressing your opinion, feelings, or suggestions, in a respectful and constructive way. Acknowledge the speaker's feelings and views, and avoid imposing your own agenda or solutions. For example, "I appreciate your honesty", or "I understand how you feel, but have you considered..."
- Traits of a good listener are:
  - Empathy: The ability to put oneself in the speaker's shoes and understand their emotions and experiences.
  - Patience: The ability to listen without interrupting, rushing, or losing interest.
  - Open-mindedness: The ability to listen without bias, prejudice, or preconceived notions.
  - Respect: The ability to listen without judging, criticizing, or belittling the speaker.
  - Curiosity: The ability to listen with a genuine interest and desire to learn from the speaker.
