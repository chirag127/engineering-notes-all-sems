### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

- This is a book written by Payal Mehra, an Associate Professor of Communication at the Indian Institute of Management Lucknow.
- The book is intended for MBA students and covers all the major communication topics included in MBA syllabi across the country.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I - Fundamentals of Business Communication, Part II - Business Writing, Part III - Oral Communication, and Part IV - Communication in Special Situations.
- Some of the topics covered in the book are: communication process and models, barriers to communication, non-verbal communication, business letters, memos, reports, proposals, presentations, meetings, interviews, negotiations, cross-cultural communication, and crisis communication  .
- The book is student-friendly, practical and example-driven, and uses case studies, exercises, and activities to enhance the learning outcomes   .
- The book also provides tips and guidelines for effective communication skills, such as listening, speaking, writing, and reading   .
- The book is published by Pearson, a leading educational publisher, and has two editions: the first edition was published in 2012 and the second edition was published in 2016 .
