### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for

- Effective communication in personal and professional settings.
- Developing rapport and trust with others.
- Expressing thoughts and feelings clearly and confidently.
- Listening actively and empathetically to others.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Resolving conflicts and disagreements peacefully.
- Adapting communication style and tone to different situations and audiences.
- Using appropriate verbal and non-verbal cues to enhance communication.
- Avoiding common communication barriers and pitfalls.
- Improving communication skills through practice and reflection.
