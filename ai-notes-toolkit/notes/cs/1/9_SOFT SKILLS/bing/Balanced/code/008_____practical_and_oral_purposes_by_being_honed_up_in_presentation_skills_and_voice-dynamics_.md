### Practical and oral purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- Presentation skills are the abilities to communicate effectively and confidently in front of an audience, using various tools and techniques to convey a message or information.
- Voice-dynamics are the aspects of vocal expression that influence the listener's perception and interpretation of the speaker's message, such as tone, pitch, volume, pace, and emotion.
- Both presentation skills and voice-dynamics are important for practical and oral purposes, such as:
  - Giving speeches, lectures, or seminars
  - Delivering reports, proposals, or findings
  - Participating in meetings, discussions, or debates
  - Teaching, instructing, or coaching
  - Persuading, influencing, or motivating others
  - Entertaining, inspiring, or captivating audiences
- To be honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics, one should:
  - Know the purpose, audience, and context of the presentation
  - Prepare the content, structure, and visual aids of the presentation
  - Practice the delivery, timing, and feedback of the presentation
  - Use appropriate language, gestures, and eye contact
  - Adjust the voice-dynamics according to the message, mood, and situation
  - Avoid vocal distractions, such as filler words, monotony, or hesitation
  - Seek constructive criticism and improvement opportunities
