### 9. English Grammar & Composition, Wren & Martin S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi, 2009.

This is a book that covers the fundamentals of English grammar and composition. It is divided into two parts: Part I deals with the principles of grammar, and Part II deals with the methods of composition. The book is intended for students who are preparing for various examinations that test their knowledge of English.

Some of the topics covered in the book are:

- The sentence and its parts: This chapter explains the basic structure of a sentence and the different kinds of words that make up a sentence, such as nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
- The phrase and the clause: This chapter explains the difference between a phrase and a clause, and the different types of phrases and clauses, such as noun phrases, verb phrases, adjective phrases, adverb phrases, prepositional phrases, participial phrases, gerund phrases, infinitive phrases, noun clauses, adjective clauses, and adverb clauses.
- The parts of speech: This chapter explains the functions and forms of the eight parts of speech in detail, with examples and exercises.
- The noun: This chapter explains the kinds, number, gender, case, and agreement of nouns, and the rules for forming plurals and possessives of nouns.
- The pronoun: This chapter explains the kinds, number, gender, case, and agreement of pronouns, and the rules for using personal, reflexive, emphatic, demonstrative, interrogative, relative, indefinite, and distributive pronouns.
- The verb: This chapter explains the kinds, number, person, tense, voice, mood, and agreement of verbs, and the rules for using finite and non-finite verbs, auxiliaries and modals, transitive and intransitive verbs, active and passive voice, indicative, imperative, subjunctive, and conditional mood, and simple, continuous, perfect, and perfect continuous tenses.
- The adjective: This chapter explains the kinds, degree, and comparison of adjectives, and the rules for using attributive, predicative, and appositive adjectives, and adjectives of quality, quantity, number, and order.
- The adverb: This chapter explains the kinds, degree, and comparison of adverbs, and the rules for using adverbs of manner, place, time, frequency, degree, reason, affirmation, negation, and interrogation.
- The preposition: This chapter explains the kinds and uses of prepositions, and the rules for using simple, compound, and phrasal prepositions, and prepositions of place, time, direction, and relation.
- The conjunction: This chapter explains the kinds and uses of conjunctions, and the rules for using coordinating, subordinating, and correlative conjunctions, and conjunctions of addition, contrast, cause, effect, condition, concession, and purpose.
- The interjection: This chapter explains the nature and uses of interjections, and the rules for using interjections to express emotions, feelings, or attitudes.
- The analysis of sentences: This chapter explains the methods of analyzing sentences into their parts and clauses, and the rules for identifying the subject, predicate, complement, object, and adjunct of a sentence, and the principal, subordinate, and coordinate clauses of a complex or compound sentence.
- The synthesis of sentences: This chapter explains the methods of synthesizing sentences from their parts and clauses, and the rules for combining simple sentences into complex or compound sentences, and complex or compound sentences into simple sentences, using various connectors and modifiers.
- The transformation of sentences: This chapter explains the methods of transforming sentences from one form to another, and the rules for changing the voice, mood, tense, degree, number, person, and gender of sentences, and for converting affirmative, negative, interrogative, exclamatory, and imperative sentences into each other, and for expanding or contracting sentences using various devices.
- The punctuation and capitalization: This chapter explains the rules and principles of punctuation and capitalization, and the uses of various punctuation marks, such as the comma, the semicolon, the colon, the full stop, the question mark, the exclamation mark, the quotation marks, the apostrophe, the hyphen, the dash, the brackets, the parentheses, and the ellipsis.
- The composition: This chapter explains the steps and techniques of composition, and the types and formats of various forms of composition, such as the paragraph, the essay, the letter, the précis, the report, the dialogue, the speech, the story, and the article.
