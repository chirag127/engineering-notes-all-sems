### 10. Soft Skills for Everyone. Jeff Butterfield, Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd; New Delhi 2017.

- This book is a guide to developing and enhancing the soft skills that are essential for personal and professional success in today's world.
- Soft skills are the interpersonal, communication, problem-solving, and self-management abilities that enable people to work effectively and harmoniously with others.
- The book covers 10 key soft skills that are relevant for everyone, regardless of their background, education, or career goals. These are:
  - Self-awareness: The ability to recognize and understand one's own emotions, strengths, weaknesses, values, and motives.
  - Self-regulation: The ability to control one's impulses, emotions, and behaviors, and to adapt to changing situations and demands.
  - Motivation: The drive and enthusiasm to pursue one's goals and interests, and to overcome challenges and setbacks.
  - Empathy: The ability to understand and share the feelings, thoughts, and perspectives of others, and to respond appropriately and compassionately.
  - Social skills: The ability to communicate effectively and respectfully with others, to build and maintain positive relationships, and to work cooperatively and collaboratively in teams.
  - Communication skills: The ability to express oneself clearly and confidently in oral and written forms, to listen actively and attentively, and to give and receive constructive feedback.
  - Problem-solving skills: The ability to identify, analyze, and resolve problems in a logical and creative way, and to make sound decisions based on relevant information and criteria.
  - Critical thinking skills: The ability to think critically and objectively about issues and situations, to evaluate arguments and evidence, and to avoid biases and fallacies.
  - Creativity and innovation skills: The ability to generate and implement new and original ideas, to find novel and effective solutions, and to embrace change and diversity.
  - Learning skills: The ability to learn from one's own and others' experiences, to seek and use feedback, to acquire and update knowledge and skills, and to pursue lifelong learning and development.
- The book provides practical tips, examples, exercises, and self-assessments to help readers develop and improve their soft skills. It also offers guidance on how to apply these skills in various contexts, such as education, work, and personal life.
