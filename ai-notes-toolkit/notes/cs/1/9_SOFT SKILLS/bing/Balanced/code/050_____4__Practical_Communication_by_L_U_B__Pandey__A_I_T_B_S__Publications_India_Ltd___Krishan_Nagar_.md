# 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- This book is a manual of practical communication skills for students and professionals who need to communicate effectively in various situations.
- The book covers the following topics:

  - Communication: Process and Practice
    - This chapter introduces the concept and importance of communication, the elements and models of communication, the types and levels of communication, and the barriers and strategies of communication.
  - Listening and Speaking Skills
    - This chapter explains the role and skills of listening and speaking in communication, the types and purposes of listening and speaking, the factors affecting listening and speaking, and the techniques of improving listening and speaking skills.
  - Reading and Writing Skills
    - This chapter discusses the role and skills of reading and writing in communication, the types and purposes of reading and writing, the factors affecting reading and writing, and the methods of enhancing reading and writing skills.
  - Non-verbal Communication
    - This chapter describes the nature and significance of non-verbal communication, the types and functions of non-verbal communication, the principles and rules of non-verbal communication, and the ways of improving non-verbal communication skills.
  - Interpersonal Communication
    - This chapter defines and analyzes interpersonal communication, the factors influencing interpersonal communication, the stages and styles of interpersonal communication, and the skills and strategies of effective interpersonal communication.
  - Group Communication
    - This chapter explores the concept and characteristics of group communication, the types and functions of groups, the roles and norms of group members, the process and problems of group communication, and the skills and techniques of successful group communication.
  - Public Communication
    - This chapter examines the concept and features of public communication, the types and purposes of public communication, the steps and principles of public communication, and the skills and tips of effective public communication.
  - Professional Communication
    - This chapter reviews the concept and scope of professional communication, the types and modes of professional communication, the ethics and etiquette of professional communication, and the skills and tools of competent professional communication.

- The book is based on the latest syllabus and guidelines of the Uttar Pradesh Technical University, Lucknow  .
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with examples, illustrations, exercises, and summaries to facilitate learning and practice.
- The book is suitable for students and professionals of engineering, management, and other disciplines who want to improve their communication skills and confidence.
