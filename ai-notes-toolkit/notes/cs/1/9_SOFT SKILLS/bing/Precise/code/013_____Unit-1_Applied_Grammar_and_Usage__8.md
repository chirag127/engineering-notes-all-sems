### Unit-1 Applied Grammar and Usage: 8

1. **Subject-Verb Agreement:** The verb must agree with its subject in number and person. For example, "She writes" but "They write."
2. **Tenses:** Tenses are used to indicate the time of an action or state of being. There are three main tenses: present, past, and future.
3. **Active and Passive Voice:** In the active voice, the subject performs the action. In the passive voice, the subject receives the action. For example, "The cat chased the mouse" (active) vs. "The mouse was chased by the cat" (passive).
4. **Modifiers:** Modifiers are words or phrases that provide additional information about the noun or verb they modify. For example, "The **quick** brown fox" or "She sings **beautifully**."
5. **Pronouns:** Pronouns are words that take the place of a noun. For example, "She" instead of "Mary" or "It" instead of "the book."
6. **Prepositions:** Prepositions are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. For example, "The book is **on** the table" or "She arrived **after** dinner."
7. **Conjunctions:** Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. For example, "She likes pizza **and** ice cream" or "I will go to the store **if** I have time."
8. **Interjections:** Interjections are words or phrases that express strong emotion or surprise. For example, "Wow!" or "Oh no!"
