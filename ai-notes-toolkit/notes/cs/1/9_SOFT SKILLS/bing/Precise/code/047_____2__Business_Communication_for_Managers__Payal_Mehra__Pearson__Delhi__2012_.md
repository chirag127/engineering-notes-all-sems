### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

- Business Communication for Managers is a book written by Payal Mehra and published by Pearson Publishing in February 2012 .
- Payal Mehra is an Associate Professor of Communication at the Indian Institute of Management Lucknow, a premier business school of India with 17 years of teaching, training and research experience .
- The book is student-friendly, practical and example-driven  .
- It gives students a thorough knowledge of business communication, covering all the major communication topics included in MBA syllabi across the country  .
- The second edition of the book begins by briefly analyzing the various theories of communication and demonstrates methods of effective communication .
