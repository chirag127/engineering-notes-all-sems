### 2. Students will be able to converse well with effective speaking and listening skills in English.

- Effective speaking skills involve the ability to articulate thoughts and ideas clearly and concisely.
- Good pronunciation, intonation, and stress are important aspects of effective speaking.
- Effective listening skills involve the ability to understand and interpret spoken language accurately.
- Active listening, which involves giving full attention to the speaker and providing feedback, is an important aspect of effective listening.
- Both speaking and listening skills can be improved through practice and exposure to the language.
- Engaging in conversations, participating in group discussions, and giving presentations are some ways to improve speaking and listening skills.
- It is also important to be aware of cultural differences and to use appropriate language and non-verbal communication when conversing in English.
