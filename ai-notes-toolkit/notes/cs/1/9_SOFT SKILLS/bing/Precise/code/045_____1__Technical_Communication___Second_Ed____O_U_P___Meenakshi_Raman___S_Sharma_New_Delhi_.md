### Technical Communication, Second Edition by Meenakshi Raman and S. Sharma, published by Oxford University Press, New Delhi

- Technical Communication is a comprehensive textbook designed for students of undergraduate and postgraduate courses in engineering and management.
- The book covers the essentials of technical communication, including the writing process, document design, and oral presentations.
- It also provides practical guidance on writing reports, proposals, and technical descriptions, as well as on conducting research and documenting sources.
- The second edition has been updated to reflect the latest trends and practices in technical communication, and includes new chapters on writing for the web and social media, and on creating visual aids.
- The book is accompanied by a companion website that provides additional resources for students and instructors.
