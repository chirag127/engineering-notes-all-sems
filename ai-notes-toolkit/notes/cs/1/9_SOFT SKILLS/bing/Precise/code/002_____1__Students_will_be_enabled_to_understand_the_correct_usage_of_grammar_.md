### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

- Grammar is the set of rules that govern the structure of sentences in a language.
- Understanding grammar is essential for effective communication and writing.
- Students can learn grammar through a variety of methods, including studying grammar rules, practicing writing and speaking, and receiving feedback from teachers or peers.
- Learning grammar can help students improve their writing skills, express their ideas more clearly, and communicate more effectively.
- Some common grammar topics that students may study include parts of speech, verb tense, subject-verb agreement, and punctuation.
- By mastering grammar, students can gain confidence in their ability to communicate and write effectively.
