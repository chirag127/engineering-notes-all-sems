### 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- "Practical Communication" is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey.
- It is published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.
- The publisher is located in Krishan Nagar.
- The book focuses on practical aspects of communication.
- It provides information and guidance on effective communication in various settings.
- The book is intended for individuals who wish to improve their communication skills.
- It covers topics such as verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, and public speaking.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to enhance their communication abilities.
