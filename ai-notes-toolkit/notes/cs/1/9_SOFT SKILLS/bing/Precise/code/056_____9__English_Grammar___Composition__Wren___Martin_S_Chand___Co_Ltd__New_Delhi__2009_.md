### 9. English Grammar & Composition, Wren & Martin S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi, 2009.

- "English Grammar & Composition" is a book written by Wren & Martin and published by S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi in 2009.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to English grammar and composition.
- It covers all the essential topics of English grammar, including parts of speech, tenses, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, and more.
- The book also includes chapters on composition, including essay writing, letter writing, and précis writing.
- It is widely used as a reference book for students preparing for exams, as well as for general readers looking to improve their English language skills.
- The book is known for its clear explanations, numerous examples, and exercises for practice.
- It is available in both paperback and hardcover editions.
