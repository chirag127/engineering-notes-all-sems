### 6. Public Speaking, William S. Pfeiffer, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

- "Public Speaking" is a book written by William S. Pfeiffer and published by Pearson in Delhi in 2012.
- The book focuses on the art of public speaking and provides tips and techniques for delivering effective speeches.
- Some of the key topics covered in the book include understanding your audience, organizing your speech, using visual aids, and managing stage fright.
- The book also includes practical exercises and real-life examples to help readers improve their public speaking skills.
- Overall, "Public Speaking" by William S. Pfeiffer is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their public speaking abilities.
