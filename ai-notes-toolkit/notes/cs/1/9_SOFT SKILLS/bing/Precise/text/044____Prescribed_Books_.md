## Prescribed Books:
- Prescribed books are the books that are recommended by an educational institution or a board of education for students to read and study.
- These books are selected by a panel of experts and are considered to be the most relevant and important for the students to learn the subject.
- Prescribed books are usually mandatory for students to read and are often used as the basis for exams and assessments.
- The content of prescribed books is carefully curated to meet the learning objectives of the course and to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the subject.
- Prescribed books can be found in libraries, bookstores, and online. Students are usually required to purchase their own copies of the prescribed books.
- It is important for students to read and study the prescribed books thoroughly as they provide the foundation for their learning and success in the course.
