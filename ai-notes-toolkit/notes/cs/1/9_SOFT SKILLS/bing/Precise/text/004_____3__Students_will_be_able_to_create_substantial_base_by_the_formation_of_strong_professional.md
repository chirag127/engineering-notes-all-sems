### 3. Students will be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional

- Students can create a strong professional base by developing skills and knowledge in their field of interest.
- This can be achieved through a combination of formal education, practical experience, and self-directed learning.
- Building a strong professional network can also help students establish a solid foundation for their careers.
- Participating in internships, mentorship programs, and professional organizations can provide valuable opportunities for students to gain experience and make connections in their field.
- By actively seeking out and engaging in these opportunities, students can build a strong professional base that will serve them well throughout their careers.
