### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

- Grammar is the set of rules that govern the composition of sentences, phrases, and words in any given language.
- Understanding grammar is essential for effective communication, as it helps to convey the intended meaning accurately.
- Students can learn grammar through various methods, such as studying grammar rules, practicing exercises, and reading and writing texts.
- It is important for students to learn the correct usage of grammar, as it can improve their writing and speaking skills, and help them to express themselves clearly and effectively.
- By mastering grammar, students can also improve their reading comprehension and their ability to analyze and interpret texts.
- In summary, understanding the correct usage of grammar is an important skill for students to develop, as it can enhance their communication abilities and overall academic performance.
