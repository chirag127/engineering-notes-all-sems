### 1. Technical Communication, (Second Ed.); O.U.P., Meenakshi Raman & S.Sharma New Delhi

- Technical Communication: Principles and Practice is a comprehensive textbook tailored to meet the requirements of undergraduate students of engineering at Gujarat Technological University .
- The book is authored by Meenakshi Raman and Sangeeta Sharma and published by Oxford University Press .
- The second revised edition of the book provides a more balanced and exhaustive coverage of the subject .
- Meenakshi Raman, PhD, is presently Group Leader of the Humanities and Management Group at the Goa Campus of Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani .
- Sangeeta Sharma is also an author of the book .
