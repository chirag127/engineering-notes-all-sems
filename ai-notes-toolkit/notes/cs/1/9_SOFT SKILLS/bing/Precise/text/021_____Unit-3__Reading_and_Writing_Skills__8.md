### Unit-3: Reading and Writing Skills: 8

1. Reading and writing skills are essential for effective communication and academic success.
2. Reading skills involve the ability to comprehend, analyze, and interpret written text.
3. Writing skills involve the ability to express thoughts and ideas clearly and coherently in written form.
4. Developing strong reading and writing skills requires practice and dedication.
5. Strategies for improving reading skills include active reading, summarizing, and note-taking.
6. Strategies for improving writing skills include planning, drafting, revising, and editing.
7. Reading and writing skills are interconnected and can be improved simultaneously through activities such as journaling and essay writing.
8. Strong reading and writing skills can enhance critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving abilities.
