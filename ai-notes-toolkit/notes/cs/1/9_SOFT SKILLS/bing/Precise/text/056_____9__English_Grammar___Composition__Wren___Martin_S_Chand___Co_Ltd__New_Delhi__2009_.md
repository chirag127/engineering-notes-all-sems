### 9. English Grammar & Composition, Wren & Martin S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi, 2009.

- English Grammar & Composition is a book written by Wren & Martin and published by S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi in 2009.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to English grammar and composition.
- It covers various topics such as parts of speech, tenses, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, and more.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- It is a popular choice among students and teachers for learning and teaching English grammar and composition.
- The book includes numerous examples and exercises to help readers practice and improve their skills.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their English grammar and composition skills.
