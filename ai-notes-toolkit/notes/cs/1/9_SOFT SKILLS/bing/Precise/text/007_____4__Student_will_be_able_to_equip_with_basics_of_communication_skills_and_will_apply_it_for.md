### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for:

1. **Effective communication**: Students will learn the basics of effective communication, including active listening, clear and concise expression, and appropriate body language. This will enable them to communicate their ideas and thoughts effectively in personal and professional settings.

2. **Interpersonal relationships**: Students will learn how to build and maintain positive relationships with others through effective communication. This includes understanding the importance of empathy, respect, and trust in building strong interpersonal relationships.

3. **Conflict resolution**: Students will learn how to use effective communication to resolve conflicts in a constructive manner. This includes understanding the importance of finding common ground, expressing one's own needs and concerns, and actively listening to the other party.

4. **Teamwork**: Students will learn how to use effective communication to work collaboratively with others in a team setting. This includes understanding the importance of clear and open communication, active listening, and constructive feedback in achieving common goals.

5. **Presentation skills**: Students will learn how to effectively present their ideas and thoughts to others in a clear and engaging manner. This includes understanding the importance of preparation, organization, and delivery in making effective presentations.

By equipping themselves with the basics of communication skills, students will be able to apply these skills in various personal and professional settings, leading to more successful and fulfilling interactions with others.
