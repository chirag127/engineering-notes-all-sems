### 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

- "English Grammar & Usage" is a book written by R.P.Sinha and published by Oxford University Press in New Delhi in 2005.
- The book covers various aspects of English grammar and usage, providing readers with a comprehensive guide to the language.
- It is intended for students, teachers, and anyone interested in improving their knowledge of English grammar and usage.
- The book is organized into chapters, each covering a specific topic related to grammar and usage.
- Topics covered in the book include parts of speech, sentence structure, verb tenses, punctuation, and common errors in usage.
- The book also includes exercises and quizzes to help readers test their knowledge and understanding of the material.
- Overall, "English Grammar & Usage" by R.P.Sinha is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their proficiency in the English language.
