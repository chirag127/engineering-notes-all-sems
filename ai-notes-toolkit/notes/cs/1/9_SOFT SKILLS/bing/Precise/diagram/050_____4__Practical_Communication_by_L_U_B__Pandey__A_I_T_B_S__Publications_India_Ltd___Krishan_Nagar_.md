### 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- "Practical Communication" is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.
- The book focuses on practical aspects of communication and aims to improve the communication skills of its readers.
- The book covers various topics related to communication, including verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, and effective writing.
- The book is intended for students, professionals, and anyone who wants to improve their communication skills.
- The book is available for purchase from A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in Krishan Nagar.
