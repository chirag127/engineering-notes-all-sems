### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

- Grammar is the set of rules that govern the composition of sentences, phrases, and words in any given language.
- Understanding grammar is essential for effective communication, as it helps to convey the intended meaning accurately.
- Students can improve their grammar skills by studying the rules and practicing their application through exercises and writing assignments.
- Some common areas of grammar that students may need to focus on include subject-verb agreement, verb tense, and the use of pronouns.
- There are many resources available to help students improve their grammar, including textbooks, online tutorials, and grammar software.
- By mastering the correct usage of grammar, students can improve their writing skills, express themselves more clearly, and communicate more effectively.
