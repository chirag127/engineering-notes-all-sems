## Course Objectives:

1. To provide a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. To develop critical thinking and analytical skills.
3. To enhance the ability to apply theoretical concepts to real-world scenarios.
4. To foster independent learning and research skills.
5. To prepare students for further academic or professional pursuits in the field.
6. To promote active engagement and participation in class discussions and activities.
7. To encourage collaboration and teamwork among students.
8. To assess student progress and provide feedback for improvement.
9. To instill a sense of responsibility and ethical behavior in the field of study.
10. To inspire a lifelong love for learning and intellectual curiosity.
