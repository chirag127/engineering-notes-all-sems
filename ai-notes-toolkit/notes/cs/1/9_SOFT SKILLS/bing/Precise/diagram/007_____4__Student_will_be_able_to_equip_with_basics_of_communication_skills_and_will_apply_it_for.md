### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for:

1. **Effective communication:** Students will learn the basics of effective communication, including active listening, clear and concise expression, and appropriate body language. This will enable them to communicate their ideas and thoughts effectively in personal and professional settings.

2. **Interpersonal relationships:** By learning the basics of communication, students will be able to build and maintain healthy interpersonal relationships. This includes understanding the importance of empathy, respect, and trust in relationships.

3. **Conflict resolution:** Students will learn how to use communication skills to resolve conflicts in a constructive manner. This includes understanding the importance of finding common ground, expressing emotions in a healthy way, and finding mutually beneficial solutions.

4. **Teamwork:** Effective communication is essential for successful teamwork. Students will learn how to communicate effectively within a team, including understanding the importance of clear roles and responsibilities, active listening, and constructive feedback.

5. **Leadership:** Effective communication is a key skill for leaders. Students will learn how to use communication skills to inspire and motivate others, build trust and credibility, and effectively delegate tasks and responsibilities.

By equipping themselves with the basics of communication skills, students will be able to apply these skills in various personal and professional settings, leading to more successful and fulfilling interactions and relationships.
