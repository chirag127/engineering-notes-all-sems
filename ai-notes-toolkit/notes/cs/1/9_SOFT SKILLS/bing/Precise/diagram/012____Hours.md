## Hours

- An hour is a unit of time equal to 60 minutes or 3,600 seconds.
- It is one of the seven base units in the International System of Units (SI).
- The symbol for hour is "h".
- The hour is commonly used to measure time duration, such as the length of a movie or the duration of a flight.
- The hour is also used to measure time of day, with 24 hours in a day.
- The concept of the hour can be traced back to ancient civilizations, such as the Egyptians, who divided the day into 24 parts.
- The hour is also used in various timekeeping systems, such as Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) and local time zones.
- Daylight Saving Time (DST) is a system that adjusts the official time forward by one hour during the summer months to make better use of natural daylight.
- The hour is also used in various expressions, such as "rush hour" and "happy hour".
