### Reading style: Skimming; Scanning; Churning & Assimilation, Effective writing

- **Skimming** is a reading technique that involves quickly going through the text to get an overall idea of its content. It is useful when you need to quickly understand the main points of a text without going into details.

- **Scanning** is another reading technique that involves quickly searching for specific information in a text. It is useful when you need to find a particular piece of information without reading the entire text.

- **Churning** is the process of actively engaging with the text to understand it deeply. It involves reading the text carefully, taking notes, and thinking critically about the content.

- **Assimilation** is the process of integrating new information into your existing knowledge. It involves making connections between the new information and what you already know, and using this to deepen your understanding of the text.

- **Effective writing** is the ability to communicate your ideas clearly and concisely in writing. It involves organizing your thoughts, using appropriate language and tone, and following the conventions of grammar and punctuation.

These reading and writing techniques are essential skills for students and professionals alike, as they help to improve comprehension, retention, and communication of information. It is important to practice and develop these skills to become an effective reader and writer.
