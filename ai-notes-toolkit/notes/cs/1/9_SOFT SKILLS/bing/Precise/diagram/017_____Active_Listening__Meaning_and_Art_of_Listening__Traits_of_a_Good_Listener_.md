### Active Listening: Meaning and Art of Listening, Traits of a Good Listener

Active listening is a communication technique that involves fully concentrating, understanding, responding, and remembering what is being said. It is an essential skill for effective communication and building strong relationships.

The art of listening involves paying attention to both verbal and non-verbal cues, such as body language and tone of voice. It also involves providing feedback to the speaker, such as nodding or asking clarifying questions, to show that you are engaged in the conversation.

Some traits of a good listener include:
- Being present and focused on the conversation
- Avoiding distractions
- Providing non-judgmental feedback
- Asking open-ended questions
- Paraphrasing what the speaker has said to show understanding
- Being empathetic and showing genuine interest in the speaker's perspective.

By practicing active listening, one can improve their communication skills, build stronger relationships, and better understand the perspectives of others. It is a valuable skill in both personal and professional settings.
