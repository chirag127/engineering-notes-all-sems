### Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012

Business Communication for Managers is a book written by Payal Mehra and published by Pearson in Delhi in 2012. The book focuses on the importance of effective communication in the business world, particularly for managers.

Some key points from the book include:

1. Effective communication is essential for successful management and leadership.
2. Managers must be able to communicate clearly and concisely with their team members, superiors, and other stakeholders.
3. Good communication skills can help managers build strong relationships, resolve conflicts, and motivate their team.
4. The book covers various forms of communication, including verbal, nonverbal, and written communication.
5. It also discusses the role of technology in business communication and how it can be used effectively.

Overall, Business Communication for Managers is a valuable resource for managers looking to improve their communication skills and become more effective leaders.
