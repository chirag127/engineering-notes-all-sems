### 2006

2006 was a year of many significant events. Here are some of the top news stories and history events that happened in 2006:

- President George W. Bush signed the USA Patriot Act into law.
- Democrats gained control of both houses of Congress during the midterm elections.
- The Dow Jones Industrial Average closed above 12,000 for the first time in its 110-year history.
- Del Monte announced that it would end pineapple production in Hawaii because it was no longer economically feasible to grow pineapple in the state.
- 243 people were injured in pro-democracy protest in Nepal after Nepali security forces open fire on protesters against King Gyanendra.
- An earthquake struck Java, Indonesia at 5:53:58 AM local time (22:53:58 UTC May 26) devastating Bantul and the city of Yogyakarta, killing over 6,600 people.
- The 2006 Winter Olympics were held in Turin, Italy.
- A massive mudslide occurred in Southern Leyte, Philippines killing an estimated 1,126 people.
- Explosions occurred at the al-Askari Shrine in Samara, Iraq.
