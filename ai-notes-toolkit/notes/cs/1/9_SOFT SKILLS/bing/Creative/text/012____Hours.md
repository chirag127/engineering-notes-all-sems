## Hours

- Hours are units of time that measure the duration of events or intervals.
- One hour is equal to 60 minutes or 3600 seconds.
- Hours are commonly used to divide the day into 24 equal parts, starting from midnight and ending at midnight the next day.
- Hours can also be expressed in different formats, such as 12-hour clock (AM/PM) or 24-hour clock (military time).
- Hours can be converted to other units of time, such as days, weeks, months, years, etc. by using appropriate conversion factors.
- Hours can be used to measure various aspects of human activity, such as work, sleep, leisure, travel, etc.
