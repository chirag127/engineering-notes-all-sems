### 10. Soft Skills for Everyone. Jeff Butterfield, Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd; New Delhi 2017.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to developing and enhancing soft skills, which are the personal and interpersonal qualities that enable one to communicate effectively, work well with others, and achieve personal and professional goals.
- The book covers 10 key soft skills: communication, teamwork, problem-solving, critical thinking, professionalism, ethics, leadership, lifelong learning, diversity, and career management.
- The book provides a clear and concise explanation of each soft skill, along with practical examples, self-assessment exercises, and tips for improvement.
- The book also offers guidance on how to apply soft skills in various contexts, such as academic, workplace, and social settings.
- The book is suitable for students, educators, and professionals who want to enhance their soft skills and achieve success in their personal and professional lives.
