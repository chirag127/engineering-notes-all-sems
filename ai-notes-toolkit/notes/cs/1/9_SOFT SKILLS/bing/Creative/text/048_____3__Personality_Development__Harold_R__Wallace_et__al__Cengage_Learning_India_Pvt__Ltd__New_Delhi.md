### 3. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace et. al, Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd; New Delhi

- Personality development is the process of enhancing one's self-awareness, self-esteem, self-confidence, self-regulation, and self-actualization.
- Personality development is influenced by various factors, such as biological, psychological, social, and environmental.
- Personality development can be achieved through various methods, such as learning, training, coaching, mentoring, counseling, and feedback.
- Personality development has various benefits, such as improving one's personal and professional relationships, performance, productivity, creativity, and well-being.
- Personality development can be measured by various tools, such as personality tests, behavioral observations, self-reports, and feedback surveys.
