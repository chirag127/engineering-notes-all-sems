### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

- This is a book written by **Payal Mehra**, an Associate Professor of Communication at the Indian Institute of Management Lucknow.
- The book is published by **Pearson Publishing** and was released in **February 2012**.
- The book is a **student-friendly, practical and example-driven** book that gives students a thorough knowledge of business communication, covering all the major communication topics included in MBA syllabi across the country  .
- The book begins by briefly analysing the various theories of communication. It demonstrates methods of effective communication in different contexts, such as interpersonal, group, organizational, intercultural, and mass communication. It also covers topics such as business writing, presentation skills, listening skills, non-verbal communication, and ethics in communication.
- The book is divided into **four parts** and **twenty chapters**. The parts are:

  - Part I: Introduction to Business Communication
  - Part II: Interpersonal Communication
  - Part III: Organizational Communication
  - Part IV: Mass Communication

- The book is suitable for MBA students, managers, and professionals who want to improve their communication skills and enhance their career prospects. It is also useful for trainers and instructors who want to design and deliver effective communication courses. The book is accompanied by a companion website that provides additional resources and exercises for the readers.
