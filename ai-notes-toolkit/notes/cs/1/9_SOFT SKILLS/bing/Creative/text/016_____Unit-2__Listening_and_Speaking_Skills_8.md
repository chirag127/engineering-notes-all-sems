### Unit-2: Listening and Speaking Skills

- Listening and speaking skills are essential for effective communication in any language.
- Listening is the process of receiving, understanding, and interpreting spoken messages.
- Speaking is the process of producing and delivering spoken messages.
- Listening and speaking skills are interrelated and interdependent. They influence and support each other in various ways.
- Listening and speaking skills can be improved by practicing various strategies and techniques, such as:

  - Setting a purpose and a goal for listening or speaking.
  - Activating prior knowledge and making predictions about the topic.
  - Using context clues and non-verbal cues to infer meaning and tone.
  - Asking and answering questions to clarify and check understanding.
  - Paraphrasing and summarizing the main ideas and details of a spoken message.
  - Giving and receiving feedback to improve performance and accuracy.
  - Using appropriate vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, and intonation to convey and comprehend messages.
  - Using various types of listening and speaking tasks, such as:

    - Listening for gist, specific information, or inference.
    - Listening to different genres and sources, such as dialogues, speeches, podcasts, or videos.
    - Speaking for different purposes and audiences, such as describing, narrating, persuading, or informing.
    - Speaking in different formats and situations, such as monologues, conversations, presentations, or interviews.
