### Unit-3: Reading and Writing Skills: 8

- This unit focuses on developing the skills of reading and writing in academic contexts.
- Reading skills include the ability to comprehend, analyze, synthesize, and evaluate texts of different genres, purposes, and levels of difficulty.
- Writing skills include the ability to produce clear, coherent, and well-organized texts that follow the conventions of academic writing, such as structure, style, tone, citation, and referencing.
- The unit covers the following topics:

  - Reading strategies: how to use different techniques to improve reading comprehension and efficiency, such as skimming, scanning, predicting, inferring, summarizing, and annotating.
  - Reading critically: how to identify the main idea, supporting details, arguments, evidence, and assumptions of a text, and how to evaluate its reliability, validity, and relevance.
  - Reading across texts: how to compare and contrast texts on the same or related topics, and how to synthesize information from multiple sources.
  - Writing process: how to plan, draft, revise, edit, and proofread a piece of academic writing, and how to use feedback to improve writing quality.
  - Writing genres: how to write different types of academic texts, such as essays, reports, reviews, summaries, and critiques, and how to adapt the content, structure, and language to the specific genre, audience, and purpose.
  - Writing mechanics: how to use correct grammar, punctuation, spelling, and vocabulary in academic writing, and how to avoid common errors and plagiarism.
  - Writing style: how to use appropriate tone, voice, and register in academic writing, and how to use cohesive devices, transitions, and modifiers to create coherence and clarity.
  - Writing citation and referencing: how to use different citation and referencing styles, such as APA, MLA, and Harvard, and how to integrate sources effectively and ethically in academic writing.
