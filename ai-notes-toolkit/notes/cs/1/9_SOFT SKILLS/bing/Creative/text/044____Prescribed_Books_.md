## Prescribed Books:

- Prescribed books are the books that are recommended or required by an academic institution, a course instructor, or an examination board for students to read and study.
- Prescribed books are usually chosen based on their relevance, quality, and availability for a specific subject, level, or curriculum.
- Prescribed books may have different purposes, such as:
  - Providing an overview or introduction to a topic or field of study.
  - Explaining key concepts, theories, methods, or applications of a subject.
  - Presenting original research, findings, arguments, or perspectives on a topic.
  - Offering examples, exercises, problems, or case studies for practice or assessment.
  - Supplementing or complementing other learning materials, such as lectures, notes, slides, or videos.
- Prescribed books may have different formats, such as:
  - Textbooks, which are comprehensive and systematic books that cover a subject in detail and are designed for teaching and learning purposes.
  - Monographs, which are focused and in-depth books that deal with a specific aspect or issue of a subject and are usually based on scholarly research.
  - Anthologies, which are collections of selected works by different authors on a common theme or topic and are often edited by one or more experts.
  - Handbooks, which are concise and practical books that provide essential information, guidance, or instructions on a subject or skill.
  - Reference books, which are authoritative and reliable books that provide factual information, data, or definitions on a subject and are often arranged alphabetically or systematically.
