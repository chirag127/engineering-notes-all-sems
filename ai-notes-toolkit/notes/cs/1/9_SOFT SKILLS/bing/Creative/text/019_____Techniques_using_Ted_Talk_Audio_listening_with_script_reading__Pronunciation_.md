### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation

- Ted Talk is a platform that features speakers from various fields and backgrounds who share their ideas and insights on various topics.
- Ted Talk audio listening with script reading is a technique that can help learners improve their listening comprehension, vocabulary, and pronunciation skills.
- Pronunciation is the way a word or a language is spoken, or the manner in which someone utters a word.
- Some of the techniques using Ted Talk audio listening with script reading and pronunciation are:

  - Choose a Ted Talk that interests you and matches your level of proficiency. You can browse the topics and categories on the Ted website or use the search function to find a suitable talk.
  - Download or stream the audio of the talk and the transcript. You can find the transcript on the Ted website or use a third-party tool to generate one.
  - Listen to the audio once without reading the transcript. Try to understand the main idea and the key points of the talk. You can also take notes or summarize what you heard.
  - Listen to the audio again while reading the transcript. Pay attention to the words, phrases, and expressions that the speaker uses. You can also highlight or underline the words or phrases that you don't know or want to learn.
  - Look up the meaning and pronunciation of the words or phrases that you highlighted or underlined. You can use a dictionary, a thesaurus, or an online tool to find the definitions and the audio pronunciations. You can also write down the words or phrases in a notebook or a flashcard for later review.
  - Listen to the audio again and repeat after the speaker. Try to imitate the speaker's intonation, stress, and rhythm. You can also record yourself and compare your pronunciation with the speaker's.
  - Listen to the audio again and shadow the speaker. Shadowing is a technique that involves speaking along with the speaker as closely as possible. You can also pause the audio and repeat the sentences or phrases that you find difficult or challenging.
  - Listen to the audio again and answer some comprehension questions. You can use the questions that are provided on the Ted website or create your own questions based on the talk. You can also discuss the talk with a partner or a group and share your opinions and perspectives.
