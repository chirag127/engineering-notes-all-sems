### 3. Students will be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional

- A substantial base is the foundation of a successful career that enables students to achieve their goals and aspirations.
- A strong professional is someone who has the skills, knowledge, attitude, and ethics that are valued and respected in their field of work.
- To create a substantial base by the formation of strong professional, students need to:
  - Identify their strengths, interests, and passions and align them with their career choices.
  - Seek opportunities to learn and grow from various sources, such as formal education, online courses, mentors, peers, and industry experts.
  - Develop a portfolio of evidence that showcases their achievements, skills, and competencies in their chosen domain.
  - Build a network of contacts and relationships that can support them in their career development and advancement.
  - Demonstrate professionalism and integrity in their work and interactions with others, such as employers, colleagues, clients, and customers.
  - Seek feedback and reflect on their performance and progress, and identify areas for improvement and learning.
  - Adapt to changing situations and challenges, and embrace innovation and creativity in their work.
