### Unit-5: Work- place skills: 8

- This unit covers the following topics:

  - Communication skills: how to communicate effectively and professionally in the workplace, including verbal, non-verbal, written, and digital communication.
  - Teamwork skills: how to collaborate and cooperate with others in a team, including roles, responsibilities, conflict resolution, and feedback.
  - Problem-solving skills: how to identify, analyze, and solve problems in the workplace, using critical thinking, creativity, and decision-making techniques.
  - Time management skills: how to plan, prioritize, and execute tasks efficiently and effectively, using tools such as calendars, schedules, and deadlines.
  - Self-management skills: how to manage oneself in the workplace, including self-awareness, self-regulation, self-motivation, and self-improvement.

- The learning objectives of this unit are:

  - To understand the importance and benefits of effective communication, teamwork, problem-solving, time management, and self-management skills in the workplace.
  - To develop and practice the communication, teamwork, problem-solving, time management, and self-management skills in various workplace scenarios and contexts.
  - To evaluate and reflect on one's own communication, teamwork, problem-solving, time management, and self-management skills and identify areas for improvement.
