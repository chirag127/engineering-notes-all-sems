# The topic is

- The topic is a general term for the subject or theme of a text, speech, or conversation.
- A topic can be broad or narrow, depending on the scope and purpose of the communication.
- A topic can be expressed explicitly or implicitly, depending on the context and the audience.
- A topic can be chosen by the speaker or writer, or assigned by a teacher, editor, or moderator.
- A topic can be informative, persuasive, entertaining, or expressive, depending on the genre and mode of the communication.
- A topic can be related to other topics, either by similarity, contrast, or connection.
- A topic can be developed by using various strategies, such as definition, description, comparison, contrast, cause and effect, problem and solution, or argument and evidence.
- A topic can be supported by using various sources, such as facts, statistics, examples, anecdotes, quotations, or citations.
- A topic can be organized by using various structures, such as chronological, spatial, topical, or logical.
- A topic can be evaluated by using various criteria, such as relevance, clarity, coherence, accuracy, or originality.
