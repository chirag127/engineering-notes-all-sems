### 3. Learn the use of correct body language and tone of voice to enhance communication.

- Body language and tone of voice are important aspects of communication that convey non-verbal messages and emotions.
- Body language refers to the gestures, postures, facial expressions, eye contact, and physical distance that one uses while communicating.
- Tone of voice refers to the pitch, volume, speed, and quality of one's speech that indicate one's attitude, mood, and intention.
- Using correct body language and tone of voice can enhance communication by:
  - Showing interest, respect, and empathy for the other person.
  - Creating rapport and trust with the other person.
  - Clarifying and reinforcing the verbal message.
  - Avoiding misunderstandings and conflicts.
  - Expressing one's personality and emotions appropriately.
- Some examples of correct body language and tone of voice are:
  - Maintaining eye contact to show attention and confidence.
  - Nodding and smiling to show agreement and appreciation.
  - Leaning forward and tilting the head to show curiosity and engagement.
  - Using open and relaxed gestures to show friendliness and openness.
  - Speaking clearly and calmly to show respect and professionalism.
  - Modulating the pitch and volume to emphasize important points and convey emotions.
  - Varying the speed and pauses to create interest and suspense.
  - Using a warm and friendly quality to show sincerity and enthusiasm.
