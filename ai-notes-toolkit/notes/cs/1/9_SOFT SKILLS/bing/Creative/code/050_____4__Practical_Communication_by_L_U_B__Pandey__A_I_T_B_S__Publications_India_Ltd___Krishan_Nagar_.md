### 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- This is a book on communication skills for students and professionals.
- It covers various aspects of communication, such as process, practice, types, barriers, listening, speaking, writing, reading, and non-verbal communication.
- It also provides guidelines and exercises for effective communication in different situations, such as interviews, meetings, presentations, group discussions, and public speaking.
- It is based on the latest syllabus of U.P. Technical University and other technical institutions.
- It is written in a simple and lucid language, with examples, diagrams, and illustrations.
- It is divided into 12 chapters, each with a summary, review questions, and practical assignments.
- The chapters are:

  1. Introduction to Communication
  2. Communication Process
  3. Communication Practice
  4. Types of Communication
  5. Barriers to Communication
  6. Listening Skills
  7. Speaking Skills
  8. Writing Skills
  9. Reading Skills
  10. Non-verbal Communication
  11. Communication in Specific Situations
  12. Communication and Personality Development

- The book is intended to help the readers improve their communication skills and confidence in various contexts.
