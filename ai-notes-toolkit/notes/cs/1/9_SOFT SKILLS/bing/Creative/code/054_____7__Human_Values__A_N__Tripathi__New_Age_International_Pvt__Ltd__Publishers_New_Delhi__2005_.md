### 7. Human Values, A.N. Tripathi, New Age International Pvt. Ltd. Publishers New Delhi, 2005.

- This book is a comprehensive and systematic study of human values in the context of contemporary challenges and opportunities.
- It offers a fresh interpretation of human values as the collectivity of all those values which enrich the different dimensions of life- material, societal, psychological, aesthetic, ethical and spiritual .
- A harmonious realisation of all these values helps us grow as truly and fully human persons.
- The book synthesises the insights of various disciplines such as philosophy, psychology, sociology, religion, art and literature to present a holistic and integrated vision of human values .
- The book also explores the sources, development, classification and evaluation of human values, as well as the role of education, culture, media and law in promoting or hindering them .
- The book also examines some of the major contemporary issues and problems that affect human values, such as violence, terrorism, corruption, consumerism, environmental degradation, human rights, gender justice and globalisation .
- The book proposes some practical and constructive ways to foster and cultivate human values in personal and social life, such as value education, value clarification, value analysis, value dialogue, value action and value audit .
- The book is intended for students, teachers, researchers and general readers who are interested in the study and practice of human values .
