### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation

- Ted Talk is a platform that features speakers from various fields and backgrounds who share their ideas and insights on various topics.
- Ted Talk audio listening with script reading is a technique that can help learners improve their listening comprehension, vocabulary, and pronunciation skills.
- The technique involves listening to a Ted Talk audio while reading the transcript of the speech, which can be found on the Ted Talk website or app.
- The benefits of this technique are:

  - It can help learners catch the details and nuances of the speech that they might miss if they only listen or only read.
  - It can help learners compare their own pronunciation with the speaker's pronunciation and identify areas of improvement.
  - It can help learners learn new words and expressions from the context and usage of the speech.
  - It can help learners improve their critical thinking and analytical skills by following the speaker's arguments and perspectives.

- The steps of this technique are:

  - Choose a Ted Talk that interests you and matches your level of proficiency.
  - Listen to the audio once without reading the transcript to get the main idea and gist of the speech.
  - Listen to the audio again while reading the transcript and pause whenever you encounter a word or phrase that you don't understand or want to learn more about. Look up the meaning and pronunciation of the word or phrase and write it down in your notebook.
  - Listen to the audio a third time and try to repeat after the speaker as closely as possible. Pay attention to the speaker's intonation, stress, and rhythm. Record yourself and compare your pronunciation with the speaker's pronunciation.
  - Review the transcript and your notes and summarize the main points and takeaways of the speech in your own words.
