### 2. Demonstrate active listening with comprehension, and the ability to write clear and well-structured emails and proposals.

Active listening is a skill that involves paying attention to what the speaker is saying, showing interest and empathy, and providing feedback and clarification. Active listening can improve communication, understanding, and rapport in various situations, such as work, education, and personal relationships. Some of the benefits of active listening are:

- It can help you learn new information and perspectives from others.
- It can help you avoid misunderstandings and conflicts by clarifying the speaker's message and intent.
- It can help you build trust and rapport with the speaker by showing respect and appreciation.
- It can help you improve your own speaking and writing skills by observing how others communicate effectively.

Some of the techniques of active listening are:

- Use nonverbal cues to show your attention and interest, such as eye contact, nodding, smiling, and leaning forward.
- Use verbal cues to acknowledge and encourage the speaker, such as "I see", "I understand", "That's interesting", and "Tell me more".
- Ask open-ended questions to elicit more information and details from the speaker, such as "What do you think about ...?", "How did you feel when ...?", and "What are some of the challenges you faced ...?".
- Ask clarifying questions to check your understanding and avoid assumptions, such as "What do you mean by ...?", "Can you give me an example of ...?", and "Can you repeat that ...?".
- Paraphrase and summarize what the speaker has said to confirm your comprehension and show your appreciation, such as "So, what you are saying is ...", "Let me see if I got this right ...", and "That sounds like ...".
- Provide constructive feedback and suggestions to the speaker, if appropriate, such as "I agree with your point of view ...", "I have a different opinion ...", and "Have you considered ...?".

Email writing is a skill that involves composing and sending electronic messages to communicate with others for various purposes, such as requesting information, giving instructions, making inquiries, expressing opinions, and so on. Email writing can be used in various contexts, such as work, education, and personal relationships. Some of the benefits of email writing are:

- It can help you communicate quickly and efficiently with others across different locations and time zones.
- It can help you create a record of your communication and track your progress and outcomes.
- It can help you convey your message clearly and concisely by using appropriate language, tone, and format.
- It can help you establish and maintain a professional and respectful relationship with your recipients by following the email etiquette and norms.

Some of the techniques of email writing are:

- Use a clear and descriptive subject line to capture the attention and interest of your recipients and indicate the purpose and content of your email, such as "Request for feedback on project report", "Reminder of meeting agenda and minutes", and "Thank you for your cooperation and support".
- Use a proper salutation and closing to address and sign off your email, depending on the level of formality and familiarity with your recipients, such as "Dear Mr./Ms./Dr. ...", "Hello ...", "Hi ...", "Sincerely", "Best regards", and "Cheers".
- Use a polite and courteous tone to convey your message and express your emotions, such as "Please", "Thank you", "I apologize", "I appreciate", and "I hope".
- Use clear and concise language to communicate your message and avoid ambiguity and confusion, such as "I am writing to ...", "I would like to ...", "I need ...", and "I request ...".
- Use proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation to ensure the accuracy and readability of your email, such as "I have attached ...", "Please find ...", "Please let me know ...", and "Please reply by ...".
- Use appropriate format and layout to organize and present your email, such as using paragraphs, bullet points, headings, and fonts to separate and highlight your main points, and using attachments, links, and images to support and illustrate your message.
