## Unit 1 - Environment and Ecosystem

### Environment
- **Definition:** Environment refers to the surroundings, which includes both living and non-living things, that affect an organism's life and development.
- **Types of Environment:** There are two types of environment: natural environment and human-made environment.
- **Components of Environment:** The components of the environment are air, water, soil, living organisms, and physical factors such as temperature, light, and pressure.
- **Segments of Environment:** There are five segments of the environment: atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, biosphere, and anthroposphere.
- **Scope and Importance:** The environment plays a crucial role in the survival of all living organisms, including humans. It provides natural resources, regulates the climate, and influences the overall well-being of the planet.
- **Need for Public Awareness:** Public awareness is essential to promote environmental protection, conservation, and sustainable development. It involves educating the public about the importance of the environment and encouraging them to adopt environmentally friendly practices.

### Ecosystem
- **Definition:** An ecosystem is a community of living organisms (plants, animals, and microorganisms) interacting with each other and their non-living environment.
- **Types of Ecosystem:** There are two types of ecosystems: terrestrial and aquatic ecosystems.
- **Structure of Ecosystem:** The structure of the ecosystem includes producers (plants), consumers (animals), and decomposers (bacteria and fungi).
- **Food Chain:** A food chain represents the flow of energy and nutrients from one organism to another. It starts with a producer and ends with a top predator.
- **Food Web:** A food web is a network of interconnected food chains and represents the complex interactions between different organisms in an ecosystem.
- **Ecological Pyramid:** An ecological pyramid shows the relative amount of energy or biomass at each trophic level in an ecosystem.
- **Balance Ecosystem:** A balanced ecosystem maintains a stable population of different species and ensures the survival of all living organisms.
- **Effects of Human Activities:** Human activities such as food, shelter, housing, agriculture, industry, mining, transportation, economic and social security have a significant impact on the environment. They cause pollution, deforestation, loss of biodiversity, and climate change.
- **Environmental Impact Assessment:** Environmental Impact Assessment is a process that evaluates the potential environmental impacts of a proposed project or development.
- **Sustainable Development:** Sustainable development is a development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs. It involves promoting economic growth, social development, and environmental protection.
