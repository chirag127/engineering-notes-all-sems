

## Unit 3 - Pollution and their Effects; Public Health Aspects of Environmental; Water Pollution, Air Pollution, Soil Pollution, Noise Pollution, Solid Waste Management

* Pollution is the introduction of contaminants into the environment that cause harm or discomfort to humans and other living organisms.
* Air pollution is the release of harmful chemicals into the atmosphere, such as carbon dioxide, sulfur dioxide, and nitrogen oxides. It can cause respiratory illnesses, heart disease, and even death.
* Water pollution is the contamination of water bodies, such as lakes, rivers, oceans, and groundwater, with harmful substances. It can lead to the spread of disease, the destruction of aquatic life, and the contamination of drinking water.
* Soil pollution is the contamination of soil with toxic chemicals, such as pesticides, fertilizers, and industrial waste. It can lead to the destruction of crops and the contamination of groundwater.
* Noise pollution is the excessive noise caused by human activities, such as construction, traffic, and industrial operations. It can cause hearing loss, stress, and sleep disturbances.
* Solid waste management is the process of collecting, treating, and disposing of solid waste in a safe and efficient manner. It includes the collection, transportation, and disposal of solid waste, as well as the recycling of materials for reuse.
