## Unit 3 - Pollution and their Effects; Public Health Aspects of Environmental; Water Pollution, Air Pollution, Soil Pollution, Noise Pollution, Solid waste management.

### Water Pollution

- discharge of untreated sewage and industrial waste into water bodies.
- effects: harmful chemicals enter food chain, diseases spread, loss of biodiversity.
- solutions: treat sewage and waste before disposing, curb dumping of waste, conserve water.

### Air Pollution

- emission of harmful gases and particles from vehicles and industries.
- effects: respiratory diseases, global warming, acid rain, reduced visibility.
- solutions: use of cleaner fuels, afforestation, public transport, emission controls.

### Soil Pollution

- excessive use of fertilizers and pesticides kills useful microorganisms.
- effects: loss of soil fertility, entry of chemicals into food chain.
- solutions: organic farming, waste management, bioremediation.

### Noise Pollution

- excessive noise from vehicles, machines, etc.
- effects: hearing loss, stress, sleep disturbance, reduced productivity.
- solutions: use of noise absorbing materials, minimise use of loud devices, traffic rules.

### Solid Waste Management

- excessive garbage generation and improper disposal.
- effects: spread of diseases, clogs drains and water bodies, loss of land space.
- solutions: segregate waste, composting, recycling, incineration, landfills.
