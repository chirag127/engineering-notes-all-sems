### Introduction to Wireless Communication

- Wireless communication is the transmission of voice and data without cable or wires.
- Wireless communication uses electromagnetic signals, such as radio waves, to broadcast information from sending facilities to intermediate and end-user devices.
- Wireless communication has many advantages, such as mobility, scalability, flexibility, and cost-effectiveness.
- Wireless communication has many applications, such as cellular networks, satellite systems, wireless LANs, Bluetooth, RFID, and wireless sensor networks.
- Wireless communication has many challenges, such as interference, noise, fading, multipath, security, and power consumption.
- Wireless communication requires a good understanding of the fundamentals of communication engineering, such as signal processing, modulation, coding, channel models, and diversity techniques.
