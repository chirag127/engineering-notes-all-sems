## Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering

- Communication engineering is a field that focuses on supporting systems that transfer information from one place to another.
- Communication engineering involves the following basic concepts  :
  - Signals, spectra, and bandwidth: Signals are the physical representation of information, spectra are the frequency components of signals, and bandwidth is the range of frequencies that a signal occupies.
  - Attenuation, distortion, and noise: Attenuation is the loss of signal power due to transmission medium, distortion is the alteration of signal shape or amplitude due to non-linear effects, and noise is the unwanted random fluctuations in signal that degrade its quality.
  - Filtering, equalizing, and companding: Filtering is the process of removing unwanted frequency components from a signal, equalizing is the process of compensating for the distortion caused by the transmission medium, and companding is the process of compressing and expanding the dynamic range of a signal to reduce the effects of noise.
  - Modulation and multiplexing: Modulation is the process of changing the characteristics of a carrier signal according to the information signal, and multiplexing is the process of combining multiple signals into one signal for transmission over a common medium.
  - Information measurement, source and channel coding, channel capacity and Shannon theorems: Information measurement is the quantification of the amount of information in a signal, source coding is the process of reducing the redundancy in a signal, channel coding is the process of adding redundancy to a signal to improve its reliability, channel capacity is the maximum rate of information that can be transmitted over a channel, and Shannon theorems are the fundamental limits of communication systems.
  - Baseband data transmission, digital modulation and spread-spectrum systems: Baseband data transmission is the transmission of digital signals without modulation, digital modulation is the modulation of digital signals using discrete values of carrier signal, and spread-spectrum systems are the systems that use a wide bandwidth to transmit signals with low power and high security.
  - Local area networks and architecture and performance: Local area networks are the networks that connect devices within a limited geographical area, architecture is the structure and organization of a network, and performance is the measure of how well a network meets the requirements of the users and applications.
  - Layered network architecture, protocols, switching techniques, TCP/IP, traffic engineering and basic capacity analysis: Layered network architecture is the division of network functions into hierarchical layers, protocols are the rules and procedures that govern the communication between network entities, switching techniques are the methods of transferring data from one network node to another, TCP/IP is the suite of protocols that defines the internet, traffic engineering is the optimization of network resources and performance, and basic capacity analysis is the estimation of the maximum amount of data that can be transmitted over a network.
