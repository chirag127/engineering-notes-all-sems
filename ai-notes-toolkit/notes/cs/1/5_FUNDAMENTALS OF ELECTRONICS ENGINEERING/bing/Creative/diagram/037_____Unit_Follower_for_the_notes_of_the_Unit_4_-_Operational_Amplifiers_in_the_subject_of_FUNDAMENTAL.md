### Unit Follower

- A unit follower is a type of operational amplifier (op-amp) circuit that has a **unity gain**. This means that the output voltage is equal to the input voltage.
- A unit follower is also called a **voltage follower**, an **op-amp buffer**, or a **unity-gain amplifier**  .
- A unit follower is created by **directly connecting** the output of the op-amp to the **inverting input** (-) and applying the input voltage to the **non-inverting input** (+)  .
- A unit follower is a simple and useful op-amp circuit that can **isolate** circuits from each other, **prevent** loading effects, and **improve** impedance matching   .
- A unit follower has a very **high input impedance** and a very **low output impedance**. This means that it can **draw** very little current from the input source and **deliver** a large current to the output load   .
- A unit follower has a **stable output voltage** that is **independent** of the load resistance. It can also **follow** the input voltage **accurately** and **instantly**   .
- A unit follower can be used in various applications, such as **signal buffering**, **voltage level shifting**, **active filtering**, **impedance transformation**, and **biomedical instrumentation**   .
