### Common Emitter Configuration

- The common emitter configuration is the most widely used transistor configuration.
- It is used when large current gain is needed.
- The input signal is applied between the base and emitter terminals while the output signal is taken between the collector and emitter terminals.
- The base is the input terminal, the collector is the output terminal and the emitter is the common terminal for both input and output .
- The common emitter amplifier has medium input and output impedance levels.
- The current gain and voltage gain of the common emitter amplifier are medium.
- The common emitter amplifier is typically used as a voltage amplifier.
- The common emitter amplifier can be biased using two-supply emitter bias or voltage divider bias.
- The common emitter amplifier can drive a speaker with an audio frequency signal.
