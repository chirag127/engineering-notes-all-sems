### Summing Amplifier

- A summing amplifier is an op amp circuit that can combine numbers of input signals to a single output that is the weighted sum of the applied inputs   .
- The summing amplifier is one variation of inverting amplifier. In inverting amplifier there is only one voltage signal applied to the inverting input as shown below:

![Inverting amplifier](https://www.electrical4u.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/inverting-amplifier.png)

- In summing amplifier, there are two or more voltage signals applied to the inverting input through different resistors as shown below:

![Summing amplifier](https://www.electrical4u.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/summing-amplifier.png)

- The output voltage of the summing amplifier is given by the formula  :

![Output voltage formula](https://www.electrical4u.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/02/summing-amplifier-formula.png)

- Where Rf is the feedback resistor and R1, R2, R3, ... are the input resistors. V1, V2, V3, ... are the input voltages.
- The output voltage is negative because the op amp is in inverting mode. The output voltage is proportional to the sum of the input voltages, with each input voltage scaled by the ratio of the feedback resistor to the corresponding input resistor .
- The summing amplifier can be used for various applications, such as audio mixing, digital to analog conversion, voltage to current conversion, etc .
