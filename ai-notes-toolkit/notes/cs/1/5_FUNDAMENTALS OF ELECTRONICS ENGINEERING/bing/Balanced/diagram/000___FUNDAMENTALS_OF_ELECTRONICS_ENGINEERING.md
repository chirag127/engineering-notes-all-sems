# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Electronics engineering is a branch of engineering that deals with the design, development and testing of electronic systems and products. Electronics engineers work with various components and devices that manipulate electric signals, such as resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, integrated circuits, microcontrollers, sensors, etc. Electronics engineering has many applications in various fields, such as telecommunications, science, healthcare, government, and military .

Some of the basic concepts and topics that are essential for electronics engineering are:

- Electricity: Electricity is the flow of electric charge through a conductor. There are two types of electrical signals, those being alternating current (AC), and direct current (DC). AC is the type of electricity that comes from the power grid and changes direction periodically. DC is the type of electricity that comes from batteries and solar cells and flows in one direction. AC and DC have different properties and applications in electronics.
- Circuits: A circuit is a complete and closed path through which electric current can flow. A circuit consists of a power source, such as a battery or a generator, and one or more components, such as resistors, capacitors, diodes, etc. that control the flow and behavior of the current. A circuit can be analyzed using various laws and methods, such as Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, Thevenin's theorem, etc. to determine the voltage, current, power, and resistance at any point in the circuit .
- Resistance: Resistance is a measure of how much a component or a material opposes the flow of electric current. Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω) and depends on factors such as the length, cross-sectional area, temperature, and material of the conductor. Resistance can be used to limit the current, divide the voltage, or generate heat in a circuit. Resistors are components that have a fixed or variable resistance and are used for various purposes in electronic circuits .
- Series Vs. Parallel: Series and parallel are two ways of connecting components in a circuit. In series, the components are connected end to end, so that the same current flows through all of them. In parallel, the components are connected across the same two points, so that the voltage across them is the same. The total resistance, current, and voltage in a series or parallel circuit can be calculated using different formulas. Series and parallel connections have different effects on the circuit performance and behavior .
- Basic Components: There are many types of components that are used in electronic circuits, each with a specific function and symbol. Some of the basic components are:

  - Capacitors: Capacitors are components that store electric charge and energy. Capacitors consist of two conductive plates separated by an insulator, such as air, paper, or ceramic. Capacitors have the ability to block DC and pass AC, and are used for filtering, smoothing, timing, and coupling in electronic circuits. Capacitors are measured in farads (F) and have different types, such as electrolytic, ceramic, film, etc. depending on the material and construction .
  - Diodes: Diodes are components that allow current to flow in one direction only. Diodes consist of a semiconductor material, such as silicon or germanium, that has two terminals, called anode and cathode. Diodes have a threshold voltage, called the forward voltage, that must be applied across them for the current to flow. Diodes are used for rectification, switching, protection, and regulation in electronic circuits. Diodes have different types, such as LED, Zener, Schottky, etc. depending on the function and characteristics .
  - Transistors: Transistors are components that can amplify or switch electric signals. Transistors consist of three terminals, called base, collector, and emitter, and two regions of semiconductor material, called N-type and P-type, that are arranged in different configurations, such as NPN or PNP. Transistors can operate in different modes, such as cut-off, saturation, or active, depending on the voltage and current applied to the terminals. Transistors are used for amplification, switching, oscillation, and logic in electronic circuits. Transistors have different types, such as BJT, FET, MOSFET, etc. depending on the structure and operation .
