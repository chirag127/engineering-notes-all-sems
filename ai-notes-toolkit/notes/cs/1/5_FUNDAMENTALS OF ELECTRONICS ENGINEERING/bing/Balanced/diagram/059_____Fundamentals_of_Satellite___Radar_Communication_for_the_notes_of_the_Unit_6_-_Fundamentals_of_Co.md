### Fundamentals of Satellite & Radar Communication

- Satellite communication is the use of artificial satellites to transmit and receive signals for voice, video and data communications. Satellite communication has the advantages of global coverage, high bandwidth, reliability and scalability.
- Satellite communication systems consist of four main components: an uplink Earth station, a satellite, a downlink Earth station and a user terminal. The uplink Earth station transmits the desired signal to the satellite, which amplifies and changes the frequency of the signal and transmits it back to Earth. The downlink Earth station receives the signal and delivers it to the user terminal.
- Satellite communication systems can be classified by the orbit of the satellite, the frequency band of the signal, the type of service provided and the architecture of the network. Some common types of satellite communication systems are geostationary satellites, low Earth orbit satellites, medium Earth orbit satellites, direct broadcast satellites, high throughput satellites, very small aperture terminals and satellite phones .
- Radar communication is the use of radio waves to detect and locate objects by measuring the time delay and direction of the reflected signal. Radar communication has the applications of air traffic control, navigation, surveillance, weather forecasting, missile guidance and collision avoidance.
- Radar communication systems consist of a transmitter, an antenna, a receiver and a display. The transmitter generates an electromagnetic signal that is radiated into space by the antenna. When the signal strikes an object, it gets reflected or reradiated in many directions. The antenna receives the echo signal and sends it to the receiver, which amplifies and processes the signal. The display shows the information about the object, such as its distance, speed, direction and shape.
- Radar communication systems can be classified by the frequency of the signal, the type of modulation, the type of scanning, the type of detection and the type of processing. Some common types of radar communication systems are continuous wave radar, pulsed radar, Doppler radar, frequency modulated continuous wave radar, phased array radar, monopulse radar, bistatic radar and synthetic aperture radar.
