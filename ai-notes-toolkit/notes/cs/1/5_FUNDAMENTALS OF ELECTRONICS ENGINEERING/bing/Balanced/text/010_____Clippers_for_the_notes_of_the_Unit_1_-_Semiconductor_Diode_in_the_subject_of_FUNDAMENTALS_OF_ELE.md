### Clippers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- A semiconductor diode is a diode made of semiconductor material, most often silicon. It is a device for controlling electrical currents so that they only flow the desired way.
- A diode has two terminals: an anode and a cathode. The anode is the positive terminal and the cathode is the negative terminal. The anode is connected to the p-type region of the semiconductor and the cathode is connected to the n-type region.
- A diode allows current to flow easily from the anode to the cathode, but severely restricts current from flowing in the opposite direction. This is because the p-n junction of the diode acts as a barrier for the reverse current.
- A diode is also known as a rectifier because it can change alternating current (AC) into pulsating direct current (DC). This is useful for many applications that require a constant voltage or current source.
- A clipper is a circuit that uses one or more diodes to clip or limit the voltage or current of a signal. A clipper can be used to protect a circuit from overvoltage, to remove unwanted parts of a signal, or to shape a signal into a desired form.
- A clipper can be classified into two types: series clipper and parallel clipper. A series clipper has the diode connected in series with the load, while a parallel clipper has the diode connected in parallel with the load.
- A clipper can also be classified into four types based on the polarity and level of clipping: positive clipper, negative clipper, biased positive clipper, and biased negative clipper. A positive clipper clips the positive half of the input signal, while a negative clipper clips the negative half. A biased clipper adds a DC voltage to the input signal before clipping it.
- A clipper can be designed using different components, such as resistors, capacitors, batteries, and Zener diodes. A Zener diode is a special type of diode that can conduct in both directions when the voltage across it exceeds a certain value, called the Zener voltage. A Zener diode can be used to clip a signal at a fixed voltage level.
