# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- Electronics engineering is a branch of engineering that deals with the design, development and testing of electronic systems and products that use digital or analog components.
- Electronic systems and products can be used for various applications such as telecommunications, science, healthcare, government, and military.
- Electronics engineering requires a solid understanding of the basic concepts and principles of electricity, circuits, components, and devices.
- Some of the basic concepts and principles of electronics engineering are:

  - Electricity: Electricity is the flow of electric charge through a conductor. There are two types of electrical signals, those being alternating current (AC), and direct current (DC). AC is the type of electricity that comes from the wall outlet and changes direction periodically. DC is the type of electricity that comes from batteries and flows in one direction only.
  - Circuits: A circuit is a complete and closed path through which electric current can flow. A circuit consists of a power source, such as a battery or a generator, and one or more components, such as resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, etc., that are connected by wires or traces.
  - Resistance: Resistance is the measure of how much a component or a material opposes the flow of electric current. Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω) and is calculated by dividing the voltage (V) across the component by the current (I) through it. Resistance can be used to control the amount of current in a circuit or to create voltage drops.
  - Series Vs. Parallel: Series and parallel are two ways of connecting components in a circuit. In a series connection, the components are connected end to end, so that the same current flows through all of them. The total resistance of a series connection is the sum of the individual resistances. In a parallel connection, the components are connected across each other, so that the same voltage is applied to all of them. The total resistance of a parallel connection is the reciprocal of the sum of the reciprocals of the individual resistances.
  - Basic Components: Some of the basic components used in electronics engineering are:

    - Resistors: Resistors are components that have a fixed or variable resistance. Resistors are used to limit the current, divide the voltage, or create heat in a circuit. Resistors are color-coded to indicate their resistance value and tolerance.
    - Capacitors: Capacitors are components that store electric charge and energy. Capacitors are used to filter, smooth, or store voltage, or to create timing or frequency effects in a circuit. Capacitors are labeled with their capacitance value and voltage rating.
    - Diodes: Diodes are components that allow current to flow in one direction only. Diodes are used to rectify, regulate, or protect voltage, or to create light or sound in a circuit. Diodes are marked with a stripe or a band to indicate their polarity.
    - Transistors: Transistors are components that can amplify or switch electric signals. Transistors are used to create logic, memory, or power functions in a circuit. Transistors have three terminals: the base, the collector, and the emitter, and are identified by their type and number.
