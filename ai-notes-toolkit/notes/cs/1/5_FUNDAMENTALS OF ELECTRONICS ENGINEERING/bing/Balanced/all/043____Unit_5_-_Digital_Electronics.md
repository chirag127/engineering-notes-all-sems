# Unit 5 - Digital Electronics

- Digital electronics is the branch of electronics that deals with the representation and manipulation of data in digital form.
- Digital data is composed of discrete values, usually represented by binary digits (bits) of 0 and 1.
- Digital electronic circuits are usually made from large assemblies of logic gates, often packaged in integrated circuits.
- Logic gates are devices that perform basic logical operations on one or more input signals and produce a single output signal.
- The basic types of logic gates are AND, OR, and NOT gates. They can be combined to form more complex gates such as NAND, NOR, XOR, and XNOR gates.
- Digital electronic circuits can perform various functions such as arithmetic, logic, memory, communication, and control.
- Some examples of digital electronic devices are microprocessors, microcontrollers, digital signal processors, counters, registers, flip-flops, multiplexers, and encoders.
- Digital electronics has many advantages over analog electronics, such as higher speed, accuracy, reliability, and scalability.
- Digital electronics also has some disadvantages, such as higher power consumption, noise susceptibility, and quantization error.
- Digital electronics is a fundamental subject for electrical engineering and computer science students, as it provides the basis for understanding and designing digital systems.
