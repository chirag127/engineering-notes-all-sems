### Zener Diodes breakdown mechanism (Zener and avalanche)

Zener diodes are heavily doped p-n junction diodes. When the diode is reverse biased, there is a certain voltage at which the diode breaks down and current flows through the diode. The breakdown mechanism of Zener diodes can be classified into two types: Zener breakdown and avalanche breakdown. Let's discuss these mechanisms in detail.

#### Zener Breakdown

Zener breakdown occurs in heavily doped diodes, where the electric field at the junction is very high. When the reverse voltage is increased, the electric field at the junction increases, and at a certain voltage, the covalent bonds of the junction break down. This phenomenon is known as Zener breakdown. After Zener breakdown, the diode starts conducting in reverse direction.

#### Avalanche Breakdown

Avalanche breakdown occurs in lightly doped diodes, where the electric field is not high enough to cause Zener breakdown. When the reverse voltage is increased, the electric field at the junction creates free electrons and holes, which collide with other atoms and create more free electrons and holes. This process is known as carrier multiplication. When the number of free electrons and holes increases, the current through the diode increases rapidly. This phenomenon is known as avalanche breakdown.

#### Zener Diode Characteristics

The Zener diode can be operated in reverse breakdown region, and it has a unique feature that it maintains a constant voltage across its terminals, even if the current through the diode changes. This feature makes Zener diodes useful in voltage regulation circuits.

#### Applications of Zener Diodes

Zener diodes are used in voltage regulator circuits, voltage reference circuits, and overvoltage protection circuits. They are also used in waveform generation circuits, where they are used to clip or limit the amplitude of a signal.

#### Conclusion

Zener diodes are heavily doped p-n junction diodes that exhibit a unique breakdown mechanism. They can be operated in reverse breakdown region and maintain a constant voltage across their terminals. Zener diodes find applications in voltage regulation circuits, voltage reference circuits, overvoltage protection circuits, and waveform generation circuits.
