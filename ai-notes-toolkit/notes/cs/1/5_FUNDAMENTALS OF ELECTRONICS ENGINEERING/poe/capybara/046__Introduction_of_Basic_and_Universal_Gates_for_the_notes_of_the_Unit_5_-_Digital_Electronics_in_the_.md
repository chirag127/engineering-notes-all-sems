### Introduction of Basic and Universal Gates

In the field of digital electronics, gates play a crucial role in designing and building digital circuits. Gates are the fundamental building blocks of digital circuits, and they are responsible for taking input signals, processing them, and producing output signals. In this section, we will introduce the basic and universal gates used in digital electronics.

#### Basic Gates

Basic gates are the simplest types of gates and are used to perform basic logical operations. There are three types of basic gates: 

1. **NOT Gate**: The NOT gate (also known as an inverter) takes a single input signal and produces its complement as output. The NOT gate is represented by the symbol "!" or "¬".

2. **AND Gate**: The AND gate takes two input signals and produces an output signal that is the logical AND of the input signals. The AND gate is represented by the symbol "&" or "∧".

3. **OR Gate**: The OR gate takes two input signals and produces an output signal that is the logical OR of the input signals. The OR gate is represented by the symbol "|" or "∨".

#### Universal Gates

Universal gates are gates that can be used to implement any logical function. There are two types of universal gates:

1. **NAND Gate**: The NAND gate is a combination of the NOT and AND gates. It produces an output signal that is the logical NAND of the input signals. The NAND gate is represented by the symbol "¬&" or "↑".

2. **NOR Gate**: The NOR gate is a combination of the NOT and OR gates. It produces an output signal that is the logical NOR of the input signals. The NOR gate is represented by the symbol "¬|" or "↓".

In conclusion, basic gates perform simple logical operations, while universal gates can be used to implement any logical function. Understanding the behavior of these gates is essential in designing and building digital circuits.
