### Need of Modulation and Typical Applications for the Notes of Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering

In the field of communication engineering, modulation is a crucial technique used to transmit information over long distances. Here are some of the reasons why modulation is necessary:

- **Bandwidth Utilization:** Modulation helps in the efficient use of available bandwidth by allowing multiple signals to be transmitted simultaneously.
- **Noise Reduction:** Modulation helps in reducing the effect of noise on the transmitted signal. By modulating the signal, the information is spread over a wider frequency range, making the signal less prone to noise.
- **Increased Range:** By modulating the signal, the transmitted signal can travel longer distances without significant attenuation.

Here are some of the typical applications of modulation:

- **Radio Broadcasting:** Modulation is extensively used in radio broadcasting to transmit audio signals over long distances. The audio signal is first modulated onto a carrier wave, which is then transmitted over the airwaves.
- **Television Broadcasting:** Television broadcasting also uses modulation to transmit audio and video signals over long distances. The video signal is modulated onto a carrier wave, which is then transmitted to the television receiver.
- **Satellite Communication:** Modulation is used in satellite communication to transmit signals from the earth to the satellite and vice versa. The signal is first modulated onto a carrier wave, which is then transmitted to the satellite. The satellite then retransmits the signal back to the earth.
- **Digital Communication:** Modulation is also used in digital communication to transmit digital signals over long distances. The digital signal is first modulated onto a carrier wave and then transmitted over the communication channel.

In conclusion, modulation is an essential technique in the field of communication engineering. It helps in efficient utilization of available bandwidth, reduces the effect of noise on the transmitted signal, and increases the range of the transmitted signal. The typical applications of modulation include radio broadcasting, television broadcasting, satellite communication, and digital communication.
