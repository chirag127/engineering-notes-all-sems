### Clippers

In electronic circuits, clippers are also known as limiters or amplitude selectors. Clippers are used to remove a portion of the waveform above or below a certain level. Clippers are frequently used in voltage regulation circuits to limit the voltage that is supplied to a device. Clippers come in two types: series and shunt.

Here are some key points to keep in mind about clippers:

- Clippers are used to limit the voltage level of a waveform.
- Series clippers are connected in series with the signal source, whereas shunt clippers are connected in parallel with the signal source.
- Clippers are used in voltage regulation circuits to limit the voltage that is supplied to a device.
- Clippers can be used to protect devices from voltage spikes and transient events.
- Clippers can be constructed with diodes, zener diodes, and other semiconductor devices.
- Clippers are simple circuits that can be easily implemented with a few components.
- Clippers are used in audio amplifiers to limit the signal level and prevent distortion.
- Clippers can be used to extract a portion of a waveform by clipping off the unwanted portion.
- Clippers can be used to generate square waves and other waveforms.

Overall, clippers are an important component in electronic circuits, and a good understanding of their operation is essential for any electronics engineer.
