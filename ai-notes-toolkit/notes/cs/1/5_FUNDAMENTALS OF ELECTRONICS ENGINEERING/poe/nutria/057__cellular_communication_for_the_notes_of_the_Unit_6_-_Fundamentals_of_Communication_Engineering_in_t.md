
### Cellular Communication

* Cellular communication is a form of communication that uses radio waves to transmit data between two or more points.
* It is used to provide mobile phone service, as well as wireless internet access.
* Cellular communication is based on the concept of frequency reuse, which allows multiple users to share the same frequency in different geographic areas.
* Cellular networks are divided into cells, each of which is served by a base station.
* The base station transmits and receives signals from the mobile user.
* Cellular communication systems use different modulation techniques and multiple access schemes such as Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA), Frequency Division Multiple Access (FDMA), and Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA).
* Cellular communication systems are designed to provide reliable communication in the presence of noise and interference.
* To ensure the quality of the signal, cellular communication systems use various techniques such as power control, handoff, and diversity techniques.
