
### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. Operational amplifiers (Op-amps) are electronic devices that are used to amplify small electrical signals. 
2. Op-amps are used in a variety of applications, including signal conditioning, active filters, and voltage regulation. 
3. Op-amps are composed of several transistors, resistors, and capacitors that are connected together to form an integrated circuit. 
4. The input signal is applied to the two input terminals of the op-amp, and the output is taken from the output terminal. 
5. The gain of the op-amp is determined by the ratio of the resistors connected to the input terminals. 
6. The gain of the op-amp can be adjusted by changing the values of the resistors. 
7. Op-amps can be used to amplify signals in both AC and DC circuits. 
8. Op-amps are used in a wide range of applications, such as audio amplifiers, instrumentation amplifiers, and voltage regulators.
