
### Op-Amp Basics for the Notes of Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering

* An operational amplifier (Op-Amp) is an integrated circuit that is used to amplify an input signal. 
* Op-Amps are used in many applications, such as amplifying audio signals, voltage regulation and signal filtering.
* Op-Amps can be used in both analog and digital circuits.
* The basic components of an Op-Amp are the input and output terminals, the power supply terminals, and the feedback loop.
* The input terminals of an Op-Amp are the non-inverting (positive) and inverting (negative) terminals. 
* The output terminal provides the output voltage of the Op-Amp.
* The power supply terminals provide the power for the Op-Amp.
* The feedback loop is used to control the gain of the Op-Amp.
* The gain of an Op-Amp is determined by the ratio of the feedback resistor to the input resistor.
* Op-Amps can be used to amplify both AC and DC signals.
* Op-Amps can be used in both linear and non-linear applications.
