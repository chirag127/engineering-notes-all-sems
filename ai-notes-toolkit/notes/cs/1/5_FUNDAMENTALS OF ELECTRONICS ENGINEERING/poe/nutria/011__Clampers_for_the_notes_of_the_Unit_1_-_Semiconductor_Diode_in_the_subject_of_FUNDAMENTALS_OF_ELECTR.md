
### Clampers for the Notes of Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering

1. A clamped circuit is a circuit where a diode is connected in series with a capacitor and a resistor. 
2. The main purpose of this circuit is to limit the voltage across the capacitor to a certain value. 
3. This allows for the capacitor to be charged and discharged in a controlled manner. 
4. When the diode is forward biased, the capacitor is charged to the value of the voltage source. 
5. When the diode is reverse biased, the capacitor is discharged to the value of the voltage source. 
6. Clamped circuits are used in many applications such as voltage regulation, power supply design, and signal processing. 
7. Clampers can also be used to limit the voltage across a diode, which can help to protect the diode from damage.
