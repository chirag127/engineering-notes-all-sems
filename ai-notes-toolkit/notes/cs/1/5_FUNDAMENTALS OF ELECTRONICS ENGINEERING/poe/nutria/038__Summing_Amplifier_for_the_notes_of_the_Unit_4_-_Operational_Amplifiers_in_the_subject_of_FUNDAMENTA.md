
### Summing Amplifier 

1. A summing amplifier is an operational amplifier circuit which is used to add or subtract multiple inputs. 
2. It is a type of voltage adder circuit which can be used to add multiple DC voltages together. 
3. Summing amplifiers are used in many applications such as audio mixing, signal conditioning, and so on. 
4. The summing amplifier consists of an operational amplifier and two or more input resistors, which are connected to the inverting and non-inverting inputs of the op-amp. 
5. The inputs are connected to the inverting input of the op-amp through the input resistors, and the non-inverting input is connected to ground. 
6. The output of the summing amplifier is the sum of the input voltages. 
7. The gain of the summing amplifier can be adjusted by changing the value of the input resistors. 
8. The summing amplifier can be used to add both AC and DC voltages. 
9. The summing amplifier is a very useful circuit for signal conditioning and other applications.
