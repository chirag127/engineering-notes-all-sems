
### Zener Diodes breakdown mechanism (Zener and avalanche)

* Zener diodes are a type of semiconductor diode that are designed to operate in the breakdown region. 
* Zener breakdown occurs when the electric field in the diode exceeds the breakdown voltage of the diode material, causing current to flow through the diode. 
* Avalanche breakdown occurs when a large number of electrons are accelerated by an electric field and collide with atoms in the diode material, releasing additional electrons and creating a chain reaction. 
* Zener diodes are used in voltage regulation circuits to provide a stable voltage reference. They are also used in power supplies, clamp circuits, signal processing and other applications. 
* Zener and avalanche breakdown both have their advantages and disadvantages. Zener breakdown is more reliable and has a lower breakdown voltage, while avalanche breakdown has a higher current carrying capacity and can be used in higher voltage applications.
