

### Integrator for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering

* An integrator is an electronic circuit that produces an output voltage that is proportional to the integral of the input voltage. 
* Integrators are used in signal processing, control systems, and other applications where a signal must be integrated over time. 
* An operational amplifier (Op-Amp) is a type of integrated circuit that is used to amplify a signal. 
* An Op-Amp can be used as an integrator by connecting the input signal to one of its inputs and connecting the output to the other input. 
* Op-Amp integrators are used in a variety of applications, such as signal conditioning, signal filtering, and waveform shaping. 
* The output of an Op-Amp integrator is the integral of the input signal, and the gain of the integrator is determined by the feedback resistor. 
* Op-Amp integrators are also used to generate sawtooth and triangular waveforms.
