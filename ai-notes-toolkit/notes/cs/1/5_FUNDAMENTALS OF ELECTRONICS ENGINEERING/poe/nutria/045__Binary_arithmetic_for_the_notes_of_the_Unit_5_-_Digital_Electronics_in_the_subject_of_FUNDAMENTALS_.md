
### Binary Arithmetic 

Binary arithmetic is an essential concept in digital electronics engineering. It is the basis for the operations performed by digital computers and other digital systems. This section will provide a basic overview of binary arithmetic and its applications. 

* Binary numbers are composed of two digits - 0 and 1. 
* Binary arithmetic is based on the binary number system, which is a base-2 number system. 
* Binary arithmetic operations include addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. 
* Binary addition is performed by adding the two binary numbers, bit by bit. 
* Binary subtraction is performed by subtracting the two binary numbers, bit by bit. 
* Binary multiplication is performed by multiplying the two binary numbers, bit by bit. 
* Binary division is performed by dividing the two binary numbers, bit by bit. 
* Binary arithmetic is used in digital computers and other digital systems to perform calculations and operations. 
* Binary arithmetic is also used in digital signal processing to perform operations on digital signals.
