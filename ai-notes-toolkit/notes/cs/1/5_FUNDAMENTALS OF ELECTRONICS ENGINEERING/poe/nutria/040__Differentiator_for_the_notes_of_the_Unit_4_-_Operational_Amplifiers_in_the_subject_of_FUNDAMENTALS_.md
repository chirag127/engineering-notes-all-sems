* An operational amplifier (op-amp) is an electronic device used to amplify electrical signals. 
* A differentiator is an op-amp circuit that produces an output voltage that is proportional to the rate of change of the input voltage. 
* Differentiators are used to detect changes in the input signal and to amplify them. 
* The output of a differentiator is a voltage that is proportional to the rate of change of the input voltage. 
* The output of a differentiator is usually in the form of a voltage spike when the input voltage changes suddenly. 
* Differentiators can be used to detect changes in the input signal, such as a sudden change in the input voltage or a sudden change in the frequency of the input signal. 
* Differentiators can also be used to amplify the input signal, such as to increase the sensitivity of an oscilloscope.
