
### Ideal and Practical Diodes for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the Subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING 

1. A diode is an electronic component that allows current to flow in one direction only. It is composed of two terminals, the anode and the cathode. 
2. The ideal diode is a device that has zero resistance when the anode is more positive than the cathode, and infinite resistance when the anode is less positive than the cathode. 
3. The practical diode has a small forward voltage drop, which is the voltage across the diode when current is flowing in the forward direction. 
4. The reverse breakdown voltage of a practical diode is the voltage at which the diode begins to conduct in the reverse direction. 
5. The junction capacitance of a practical diode is the capacitance between the anode and cathode of the diode. 
6. The junction leakage current of a practical diode is the current that flows through the diode when the anode is more positive than the cathode. 
7. The temperature coefficient of a practical diode is the change in the diode’s characteristics with temperature. 
8. The reverse recovery time of a practical diode is the time it takes for the diode to switch from the reverse conduction state to the forward conduction state. 
9. The power dissipation of a practical diode is the power dissipated by the diode when current is flowing through it.
