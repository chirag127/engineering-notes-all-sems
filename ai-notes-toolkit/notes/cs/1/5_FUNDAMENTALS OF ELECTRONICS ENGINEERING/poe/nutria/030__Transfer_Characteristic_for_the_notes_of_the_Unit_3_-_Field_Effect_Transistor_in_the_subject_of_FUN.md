
### Transfer Characteristic for the notes of the Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. Field Effect Transistors (FETs) are three-terminal devices that are used to control current flow.
2. FETs are voltage-controlled devices, meaning that their current flow is determined by the voltage applied to one of their terminals.
3. FETs can be used to amplify signals, and are also used as switches.
4. The transfer characteristic of a FET is a graph that shows the relationship between the input voltage and the output current.
5. The transfer characteristic of a FET is usually represented by an S-shaped curve.
6. The S-shaped curve is a result of the FET's gate-source voltage, which determines the current flow through the FET.
7. The transfer characteristic of a FET can be used to calculate the FET's gain, which is the ratio of the output current to the input voltage.
8. The transfer characteristic of a FET can also be used to calculate the FET's cutoff frequency, which is the frequency at which the FET's gain drops to zero.
