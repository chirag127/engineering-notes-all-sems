
### Need of Modulation and Typical Applications for the Notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the Subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

* Modulation is a process of changing the characteristics of a signal in order to transmit it over a distance or to reduce its bandwidth.
* The purpose of modulation is to reduce the amount of noise in a signal and to make it easier to transmit.
* Modulation can be used in a variety of applications, such as radio and television broadcasting, cellular communication, and satellite communication.
* In radio and television broadcasting, modulation is used to encode a signal for transmission over a distance.
* In cellular communication, modulation is used to encode a signal for transmission over a cellular network.
* In satellite communication, modulation is used to encode a signal for transmission over a satellite link.
* Modulation can also be used in digital communication, such as in digital audio, digital video, and digital data transmission.
* Typical applications of modulation include amplitude modulation (AM), frequency modulation (FM), phase modulation (PM), and quadrature amplitude modulation (QAM).
* AM is used in radio broadcasting and is the most commonly used type of modulation.
* FM is used in radio broadcasting and is used to reduce noise in the signal.
* PM is used in digital communication and is used to reduce the bandwidth of a signal.
* QAM is used in digital communication and is used to increase the data rate of a signal.
