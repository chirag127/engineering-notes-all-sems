
## Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers

* An operational amplifier (op-amp) is an integrated circuit that performs mathematical operations on an input signal. 
* Op-amps are used in a wide variety of applications, such as amplifying signals, filtering signals, and performing mathematical operations like addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
* The most common type of op-amp is the voltage-controlled amplifier, which amplifies the difference between two input voltages.
* Op-amps can also be used to create more complex circuits, such as comparators, oscillators, and active filters.
* Op-amps can be used in combination with other components, such as resistors and capacitors, to create more complex circuits.
* Op-amps can be used to create feedback loops, which can be used to control the gain of the amplifier, or to create oscillators and filters.
* Op-amps can be used to create analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog converters, which can be used to convert between analog and digital signals.
