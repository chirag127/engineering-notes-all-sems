### Zener Diode as Shunt Regulator

- A Zener diode acts as a shunt voltage regulator when it is operated in the breakdown region.
- In shunt voltage regulation, the Zener diode is connected in parallel with the load.
- The voltage across the Zener diode remains constant at the breakdown voltage. Hence, any increase in supply voltage appears across the Zener diode and the voltage across the load remains constant.
- This is suitable when the current drawn by the load is small. The Zener diode can draw high currents in the breakdown region and adequate heat sinking becomes necessary.
- The series resistance Rs is included to limit the short circuit current from the power supply in case of failure of the Zener diode.
- The value of Rs is chosen to allow sufficient current through the Zener diode under normal conditions.
- The value of Rs should be low enough not to cause a significant voltage drop and affect the load regulation.
