### Common Collector Configuration

- In common collector configuration, the collector terminal is common to both input and output. The input is applied between the base and emitter terminals and the output is taken across the collector and emitter terminals.
- The input characteristics are forward biased and the output characteristics are reverse biased.
- The voltage gain is less than 1 and the current gain is greater than 1. This configuration provides high input impedance and low output impedance.
- The common collector configuration is also known as emitter follower configuration. It acts as a voltage buffer by isolating the load resistance from the input resistance.
- The biases are provided using battery or power supply. The base bias stabilizes the operating point.
- The applications of common collector configuration include voltage amplification, impedance matching and current boosting.
