### Common Base for the notes of the Unit 2 - Bipolar Junction Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- In common base configuration, the base terminal of the transistor acts as the input terminal and the collector terminal acts as the output terminal.
- The input voltage or current is applied between the emitter and base terminals and the output is taken across the collector and base terminals.
- The common base configuration provides voltage amplification. It offers high input impedance and low output impedance.
- The voltage gain in the common base configuration is equal to -RC/RE where RC is the collector resistance and RE is the emitter resistance.
- As the input is applied between the emitter and base, the circuit exhibits non-inverting nature. The output voltage will either increase or decrease in the same direction as the input voltage.
- The common base configuration is rarely used in comparison to the common emitter and common collector configurations due to its low voltage and current gains. However, it provides high speed of operation and wide bandwidth.
