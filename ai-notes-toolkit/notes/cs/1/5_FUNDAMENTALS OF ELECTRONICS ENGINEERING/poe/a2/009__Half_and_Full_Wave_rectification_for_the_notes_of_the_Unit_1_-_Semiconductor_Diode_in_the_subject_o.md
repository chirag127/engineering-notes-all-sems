### Half and Full Wave rectification

**Half Wave Rectification:**

- Only one half of the AC wave is passed, the other half is blocked.
- A diode is connected in series with the load.
- The diode conducts only during the positive half cycle and blocks during the negative half cycle.
- The output is a series of positive pulses.
- The average output voltage is less than the peak voltage of the AC input.
- Low efficiency and high ripple factor.

**Full Wave Rectification:**

- The complete AC wave is rectified.
- A centre-tapped transformer and two diodes are used.
- During the positive half cycle, the top diode conducts and during the negative half cycle, the bottom diode conducts.
- The output is a full wave with magnitude equal to the peak voltage of the input AC wave.
- Higher efficiency and lower ripple factor than half wave rectification.
- The output DC voltage is higher and the ripple factor is lower.
