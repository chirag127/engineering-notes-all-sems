### Different Generations and Standards in Cellular Communication Systems

1. 1G - First Generation Analog Systems
- Introduced in 1980s.
- Used analog technology.
- Had low capacity and was expensive.
- Examples: AMPS, TACS.

2. 2G - Second Generation Digital Systems
- Introduced in 1990s.
- Used digital technology which provided higher capacity and security.
- Examples: GSM, CDMA.

3. 2.5G - Enhancements to 2G
- Provided faster data speeds than 2G.
- Example: GPRS.

4. 3G - Third Generation Systems
- Introduced in late 1990s and 2000s.
- Offered higher data speeds and the ability to support multimedia services.
- Examples: UMTS, CDMA2000.

5. 4G - Fourth Generation Systems
- Currently in use. Introduced in 2008.
- Offer significantly faster data speeds than 3G and lower latency.
- Example: LTE.

6. 5G - Fifth Generation Systems
- Currently being developed and tested.
- Will provide extremely fast speeds, high capacity, low latency, and ability to connect a huge number of devices.
- Rollout starting from 2020.
