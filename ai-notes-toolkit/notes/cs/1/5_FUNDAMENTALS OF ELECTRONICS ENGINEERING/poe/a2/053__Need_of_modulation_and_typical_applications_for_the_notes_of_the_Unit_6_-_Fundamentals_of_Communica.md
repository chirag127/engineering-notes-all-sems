### Need of modulation and typical applications

1. The information signal such as voice or data often has a bandwidth much less than the carrier frequency. Modulation is done to combine the information signal and carrier signal to transmit the information.
2. Modulation increases the bandwidth of the information signal and shifts it to a higher frequency band which can be effectively transmitted.
3. Demodulation is done at the receiver to extract the information signal from the received modulated signal.
4. Some common modulation techniques are:
 - Amplitude Modulation (AM) - Variation in amplitude of the carrier wave as per the information signal. Used for AM radio broadcasting.
 - Frequency Modulation (FM) - Variation in frequency of the carrier wave as per the information signal. Used for FM radio broadcasting to get high quality audio transmission.
 - Phase Modulation (PM) - Variation in phase of the carrier wave as per the information signal. Used for wireless data transmission.
