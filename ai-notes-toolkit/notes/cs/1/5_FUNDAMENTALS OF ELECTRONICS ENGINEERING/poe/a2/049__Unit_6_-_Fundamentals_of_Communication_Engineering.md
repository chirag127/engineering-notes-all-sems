## Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering

1. Introduction
- Communication engineering involves the application of engineering principles to the transmission of information.
- It uses electromagnetic waves to transmit and receive data or information.

2. Communications Systems
- Consists of a transmitter, channel, and receiver.
- The transmitter converts the original signal into an electromagnetic wave and transmits it.
- The channel is the medium through which the signal travels from the transmitter to the receiver.
- The receiver converts the received electromagnetic wave back into the original signal.

3. Modulation
- The process of modifying a carrier wave in accordance with the information signal is known as modulation.
- The two types of modulation used are analog and digital modulation.
- In analog modulation, the carrier wave is modified continuously in accordance with the information signal. In digital modulation, the carrier wave is modified in discrete steps.
