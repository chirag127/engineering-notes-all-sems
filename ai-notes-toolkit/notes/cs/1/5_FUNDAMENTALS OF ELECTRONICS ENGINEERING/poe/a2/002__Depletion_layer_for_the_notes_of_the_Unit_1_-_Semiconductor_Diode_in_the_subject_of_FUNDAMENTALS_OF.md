### Depletion layer for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- When a P-N junction is formed by joining p-type and n-type semiconductors, the free electrons from n-type region diffuse into p-type region and the holes from p-type region diffuse into n-type region.
- This results in the formation of a region across the junction that is depleted of charge carriers. This region is called depletion layer.
- The depletion layer has a built-in potential that opposes the flow of charge carriers. The depletion layer width varies with the amount of doping. Higher the doping, thinner the depletion layer.
- At equilibrium, the depletion layer has a fixed width. When reverse bias is applied, the width increases and when forward bias is applied, the width decreases.
- The depletion layer is responsible for the rectification action and other important characteristics of the P-N junction diode.
