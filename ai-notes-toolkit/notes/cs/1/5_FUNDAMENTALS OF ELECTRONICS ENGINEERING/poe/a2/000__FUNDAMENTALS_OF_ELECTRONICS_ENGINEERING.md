# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. Introduction
- Electronics is the study of how electrical devices control the flow of electrons.
- Electronics engineering is the application of physics and mathematics to address problems associated with the transmission of information via electronic devices.

2. Electrical Components
- Resistors: Components that resist the flow of current. They control voltage and current levels.
- Capacitors: Components that store electrical charge. They are used to filter signals and block DC current.
- Inductors: Components that store electrical energy in a magnetic field. They are used in AC circuits to filter signals and block high-frequency alternating current.
- Diodes: Components that allow current to flow in only one direction. They are used to convert alternating current to direct current.
- Transistors: Components that control the flow of electrons. They are the fundamental building blocks of all modern electronic devices.

3. Circuits
- Series circuits: Current flows through each component one after the other. Voltage is shared across all components.
- Parallel circuits: Components are arranged in branches so current has multiple paths. Voltage is the same across all components.
- Combination circuits: Include both series and parallel components. The relationships between voltage, current, and resistance must be analyzed for the entire circuit.

4. Applications
- Computers
- Telecommunications systems
- Biomedical devices
- Robotics
- Transportation systems
- Entertainment devices
