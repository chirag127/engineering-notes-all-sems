### K3

1. K3 refers to Knowledge, Skills and Attitudes - the three broad areas of learning.
2. Knowledge refers to information and facts. Skills refers to the ability to do something. Attitudes refer to ways of thinking and feeling.
3. A comprehensive learning experience should focus on developing all three areas - knowledge, skills and attitudes - in learners. Merely focusing on knowledge transfer is not sufficient.
4. For example, learning about environmental conservation (knowledge) should be accompanied by skills to implement conservation measures and attitudes such as environmental stewardship.
5. Evaluating learning should also assess all three areas, not just knowledge. Questions, tasks and observations can assess skills and attitudes, not just knowledge.
6. The K3 framework is a simple but powerful way to design and assess learning in a holistic manner. It ensures that learning experiences are comprehensive and balanced, addressing the cognitive, psychomotor and affective domains.
