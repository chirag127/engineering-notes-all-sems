### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

1. Adipic acid
- Adipic acid is prepared industrially by the oxidation of cyclohexane with nitric acid.
- The oxidation reaction is carried out using a cobalt or vanadium catalyst at 150-200°C under pressure.
- The product is purified by crystallization to get adipic acid.

2. Paracetamol
- Paracetamol is prepared by the acetylation of p-aminophenol.
- p-Aminophenol is reacted with acetic anhydride in the presence of acid catalyst like hydrochloric acid or sulphuric acid.
- The reaction mixture is neutralized and paracetamol is precipitated out.
- It is filtered, dried and recrystallized to get pure paracetamol.
