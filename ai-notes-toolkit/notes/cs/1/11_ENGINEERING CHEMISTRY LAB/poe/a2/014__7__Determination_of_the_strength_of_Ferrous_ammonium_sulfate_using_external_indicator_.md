### 7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

1. Take about 0.5g of ferrous ammonium sulfate and dissolve it in 50ml of distilled water in a conical flask.
2. Add 2-3 drops of potassium dichromate solution (0.1N) as external indicator to the above solution. The solution turns reddish-brown.
3. Titrate the above solution against potassium permanganate solution (0.1N) until the reddish-brown color just disappears.
4. Note down the titre value. Each 1ml of potassium permanganate is equivalent to 0.0056g of ferrous ammonium sulfate.
5. Calculate the percentage strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate sample.

The above method determines the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate by redox titration using potassium permanganate as the titrant and potassium dichromate as the external indicator. The ferrous iron gets oxidized to ferric iron by potassium permanganate. On reaching the endpoint, the reddish-brown color of dichromate just disappears. The percentage strength of the given sample can be calculated from the titre value.
