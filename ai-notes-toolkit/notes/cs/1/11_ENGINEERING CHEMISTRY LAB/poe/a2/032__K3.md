### K3

1. K3 is an enzyme which catalyzes the conversion of creatine to phosphocreatine in the presence of Mg2+ ions and ATP.
2. The full form of K3 is Creatine Kinase isoenzyme-3. It is a phosphoryl transferase enzyme mainly found in the brain and cardiac tissues.
3. The role of K3 is to generate phosphocreatine which acts as a high-energy storage molecule. The phosphocreatine is utilized to produce ATP from ADP during cellular respiration for meeting the high energy demands of the brain and heart.
4. The K3 enzyme follows a ping-pong mechanism wherein ATP as a substrate binds first to release pyrophosphate and produce phosphocreatine. The Mg2+ ions and creatine bind next to regenerate the K3 enzyme.
5. Deficiency or mutations in the K3 enzyme can lead to muscle weakness or improper brain and heart functioning. Increased K3 levels in the blood can indicate muscle damage or kidney problems.
