### 4. Determination of pH by titrimetric method.

- Take the acidic or basic solution of known concentration in a conical flask.
- Add 2-3 drops of indicator to it. For acidic solutions, use indicators like methyl orange or bromothymol blue. For basic solutions, use indicators like phenolphthalein or methyl red.
- Connect the conical flask to a burette containing standard solution of opposite nature (basic if acidic solution is taken and vice versa).
- Titrate the solution with standard solution till the colour of indicator changes. Note the initial and final readings on the burette.
- Calculate the concentration of unknown solution using the formula:

Normality of unknown solution = (Normality of standard solution) x (Volume of standard solution consumed/Volume of unknown solution taken)

- Calculate pH using the formula:

pH = -log(1/Normality of acid or base)

- The pH can also be determined graphically using pH curves. Plot pH on y-axis and volume of standard solution on x-axis. The point of intersection of the curve and volume corresponds to pH of the solution.
