## Course Outcomes:

1. Learn the fundamentals of machine learning and deep learning.
2. Understand the mathematical and conceptual background of machine learning methods.
3. Gain practical experience with machine learning libraries and frameworks (e.g. scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras).
4. Apply machine learning methods to tasks such as:
    - Classification (e.g. predict whether an email is spam or not)
    - Regression (e.g. predict housing prices)
    - Clustering (e.g. customer segmentation)
    - Dimensionality reduction (e.g. visualize high-dimensional data)
5. Reflect on the applications, limitations and ethical implications of machine learning.
6. Prepare for a career as a machine learning engineer or data scientist.

The course covers the theoretical foundations as well as practical aspects of machine learning with a focus on deep neural networks and modern deep learning frameworks. Students will learn how to apply machine learning to various tasks and reflect on the ethical and social implications of this technology.
