# CO-5 Estimate the rate constant of reaction. K3

### Point 1: Identify the rate law
- Write the rate law expression for the given chemical reaction. The rate law contains concentration terms of reactants raised to their respective reaction orders.
- Note down the reaction orders of each reactant. These can be determined from the stoichiometric coefficients of the balanced chemical equation.

### Point 2: Collect concentration and time data
- Perform the chemical reaction experimentally and collect concentration data of reactants with respect to time.
- Measure the concentrations of reactants at regular time intervals. The time intervals should be short enough to capture the rapid changes in concentration at the start of the reaction.

### Point 3: Plot concentration vs time graph
- Plot a graph of concentration of reactants vs time. The slope of the graph gives the rate of reaction at a particular concentration.
- Note that the rate of reaction would be the highest at the start of the reaction as the concentrations of reactants is the highest. The rate decreases with time as the concentrations of reactants decrease.

### Point 4: Calculate rate constant
- Using the rate law expression and collected data of concentrations and corresponding rates, calculate the rate constant value.
- The units of rate constant are the inverse of concentration terms present in the rate law.
