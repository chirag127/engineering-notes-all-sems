### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample.

1. Take 100 ml of the given water sample in a conical flask.
2. Add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to it.
3. Titrate the solution with 0.1 N HCl solution from a burette until the pink color just disappears.
4. Note the volume of HCl solution consumed.
5. Repeat the titration two more times and note the readings.
6. Calculate the mean of three readings.
7. Calculate the alkalinity in mg/L as CaCO3 using the formula:
Alkalinity (mg/L) = Volume of HCl used x Equivalent weight of carbonate x 100 / Volume of sample taken
