# CO2: Measure molecular/system properties

## Surface tension
- Surface tension is the tendency of liquid surfaces to shrink into the minimum surface area possible.
- It is caused by cohesive forces between molecules in the surface layer.
- Surface tension of CO2 decreases with increasing temperature. At critical temperature (31.1°C), surface tension is zero and liquid and gas phases have same density.
- Surface tension can be measured using ring method or Wilhelmy plate method.

## Viscosity
- Viscosity is resistance of a fluid to flow. It is a measure of internal friction between layers of fluid.
- Viscosity of CO2 decreases with increasing temperature. It approaches zero at critical point.
- Viscometers are used to measure viscosity of fluids. Capillary viscometers and rotating spindle viscometers are commonly used.
