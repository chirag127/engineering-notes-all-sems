### CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

1. Spectrophotometer: Used to measure the amount of light absorbed by a chemical substance at a particular wavelength. It is used to determine the concentration of a known substance in a solution by generating a calibration curve.
2. pH Meter: Used to measure the pH of a solution. It contains electrodes that sense the hydrogen ion concentration and display the pH value.
3. Conductivity Meter: Used to measure the electrical conductivity of a solution. It passes an electric current through the solution and measures its conductivity. The conductivity depends on the presence of ions in the solution.
4. Titrator: Used to determine the concentration of an unknown substance through titration. It can deliver a precise volume of titrant into the analyte solution while detecting the endpoint of the titration.
5. Centrifuge: Used to separate components of a mixture based on density through rapid rotation. The denser components move outward in the rotating tube and get separated. It is commonly used to separate precipitates, cells, and proteins.
