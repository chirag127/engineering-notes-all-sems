### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

**Adipic acid:**

1. Oxidation of cyclohexanol or cyclohexanone with nitric acid
2. Hydration of butadiene followed by oxidation

**Properties:** White crystalline solid; mp 152°C; solubility in water - 0.5%; solubility in organic solvents - good; main use - production of nylon.

**Paracetamol:**

1. Nitration of phenol followed by amidinization with ammonia
2. Reduction with concentrated ammonium formate

**Properties:** White crystalline solid; mp 169-170°C; sparingly soluble in water; freely soluble in organic solvents; main use - analgesic and antipyretic.
