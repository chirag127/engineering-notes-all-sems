### 3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of:

1. pH: Measure of acidity or basicity of a solution. Range from 0 to 14. 0-7 acidic, 7 neutral, 7-14 basic. pH scales is logarithmic. Measured using pH meter or pH paper which changes color depending on pH.

2. Surface tension: Tendency of liquid surfaces to contract and have the smallest surface area possible. Caused by cohesive forces between molecules. Measured using du Noüy ring method or capillary rise method. Higher surface tension means higher cohesion between molecules. Important property for functioning of living organisms.

3. Viscosity: Resistance of a fluid to flow. Thicker liquids have higher viscosity. Depends on intermolecular forces between molecules and their size. Measured using viscometers like Ostwald viscometers or rotating disk viscometers. Higher viscosity means higher friction between layers of fluid and slower flow. Important for flow of fluids in living organisms and industrial processes.
