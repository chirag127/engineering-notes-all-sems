# 7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

- Take accurately weighed quantity of pure Ferrous ammonium sulfate crystals.
- Dissolve it in distilled water to make its solution of known concentration.
- Take 10ml of this solution in a conical flask and add 2-3 drops of Potassium dichromate solution as external indicator.
- Titrate this against 0.1N Potassium permanganate solution.
- Note the point when reddish tinge is just noticeable. This is the end point.
- Note the burette reading. This will give you volume of Potassium permanganate solution used.
- Use this to calculate the molarity or percentage purity of the given sample of Ferrous ammonium sulfate.
- The intensity of reddish tinge indicates the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution with higher strength giving darker colour.
- Thus, by using an external indicator, strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate can be determined.
