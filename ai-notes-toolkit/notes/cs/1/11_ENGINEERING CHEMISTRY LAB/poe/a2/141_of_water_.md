# Water

## Properties

1. Colorless, odorless liquid that covers 70% of the Earth's surface
2. Excellent solvent due to polar nature of the water molecule
3. High heat capacity and latent heat of vaporization allow for temperature stabilization
4. Density changes with temperature, allowing for convection currents

## Important Roles

1. Solvent for biological processes such as photosynthesis and cellular respiration
2. Transportation medium for nutrients, metabolites, and waste products
3. Temperature stabilizer through high heat capacity
4. Reactant or product in chemical and physical weathering of rocks
5. Shaping force through erosion and deposition in the formation of landscapes
