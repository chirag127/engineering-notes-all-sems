##### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic

1. Adipic acid is an organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is a white crystalline solid.

2. Adipic acid is the most important commercially available aliphatic dicarboxylic acid. It is mainly used to produce nylon, polyurethanes, and adipate esters.

3. The industrial synthesis of adipic acid uses benzene and butadiene as precursors. The overall process consists of several steps:

- Hydrogenation of butadiene to produce butane-1,4-diol
- Oxidation of butane-1,4-diol to the diacid, thereby producing adipic acid
- Separation and purification

4. The hydrogenation is catalyzed by nickel. The oxidation uses an aqueous solution of sodium hypochlorite or a mixture of nitric and acetic acids. The purified adipic acid is crystallized to give the final product.
