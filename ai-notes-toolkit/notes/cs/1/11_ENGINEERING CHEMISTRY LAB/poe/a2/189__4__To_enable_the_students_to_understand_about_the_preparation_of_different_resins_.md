### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins.

1. Polyester Resin:

- Polyester resin is prepared by the esterification reaction between polyhydric alcohols and polybasic acids.
- The commonly used polyhydric alcohols are glycerol and ethylene glycol. The commonly used polybasic acids are phthalic anhydride and maleic anhydride.
- This resin is mixed with styrene to make it solidify upon curing. Styrene acts as a solvent and diluent and also takes part in the cross-linking reaction.
- These resins are used in the manufacture of glass fiber reinforced plastics.

2. Epoxy Resin:

- Epoxy resins are prepared by the reaction between epichlorohydrin and bisphenol-A.
- This resin requires a curing agent like polyamines or polyamine adducts to solidify upon curing through a cross-linking reaction.
- Epoxy resins have good mechanical, adhesive and chemical resistance properties. Hence, they are widely used as surface coatings, adhesives and potting compounds.

3. Vinyl Ester Resin:

- Vinyl ester resins are prepared by the reaction between epoxy resin and unsaturated monocarboxylic acid.
- The commonly used unsaturated acids are methacrylic acid and acrylic acid.
- These resins have the positive properties of both epoxy and polyester resins. They have good mechanical and chemical resistance properties along with better adhesion to substrates.
- Hence, they are used in chemical plants, water treatment applications and for making corrosion-resistant structures.
