### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol.

Adipic acid:

1. Conversion of cyclohexanol to cyclohexanone: Cyclohexanol is oxidized to cyclohexanone using chromic acid.
2. Oxidation of cyclohexanone to adipic acid: Cyclohexanone is further oxidized to adipic acid using nitric acid.

Paracetamol:

1. Nitration of phenol: Phenol is nitrated with concentrated nitric acid to produce p-nitrophenol.
2. Reduction of p-nitrophenol to p-aminophenol: p-Nitrophenol is reduced to p-aminophenol using iron filings and hydrochloric acid.
3. Acetylation of p-aminophenol to paracetamol: p-Aminophenol is acetylated using acetic anhydride to produce paracetamol.
