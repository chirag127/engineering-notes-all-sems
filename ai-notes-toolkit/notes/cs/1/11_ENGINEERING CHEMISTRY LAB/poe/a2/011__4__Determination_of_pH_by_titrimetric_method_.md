### 4. Determination of pH by titrimetric method.

- Take a known volume of solution whose pH is to be determined.
- Add 2-3 drops of pH indicator to it. (For acidic range use indicators like methyl orange/litmus etc. and for basic range use phenolphthalein etc.)
- Titrate the solution with standard alkali (like NaOH) or acid (like HCl) solutions.
- Note down the burette reading when the indicator just changes the color. This is called end point.
- From the volume of standard solution required to reach end point and its concentration, determine the concentration of unknown solution.
- Finally, determine the pH using pH scale.
