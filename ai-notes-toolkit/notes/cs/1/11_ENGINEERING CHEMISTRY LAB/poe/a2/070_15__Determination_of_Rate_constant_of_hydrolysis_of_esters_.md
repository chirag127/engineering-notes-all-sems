# 15. Determination of Rate constant of hydrolysis of esters.

1. Esters undergo acid or base catalyzed hydrolysis. The rate of reaction can be determined by measuring the amount of ester hydrolyzed per unit time.
2. The rate of hydrolysis is measured under pseudo-first order conditions, i.e. with [ester]<<[water] and [acid/base] constant.
3. The rate law can be written as:

rate = k [ester]

4. Integrating the rate law, we get:

[ester]t = [ester]0 e-kt

5. Plot of log[ester] vs time gives a straight line with slope -k. The value of k can be determined from the slope.
6. The slope can also be determined by measuring initial rate (rate at t=0), i.e. - d[ester]/dt|t=0, and [ester]0. This gives:

k = -d[ester]/dt|t=0/[ester]0

7. The hydrolysis is carried out under controlled conditions of temperature and pH. This allows determination of temperature and pH dependence of the rate constant. The activation energy and other kinetic parameters can also be determined.
