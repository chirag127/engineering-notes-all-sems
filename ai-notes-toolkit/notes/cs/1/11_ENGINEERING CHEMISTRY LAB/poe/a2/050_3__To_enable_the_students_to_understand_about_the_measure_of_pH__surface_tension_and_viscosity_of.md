### 3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of

1. pH
- pH is a measure of acidity or basicity of an aqueous solution. It is measured using a pH meter or pH paper.
- pH values range from 0 to 14. A value of 7 is neutral. Lower values indicate acidity and higher values indicate basicity.
- The pH of a solution depends on the concentration of hydrogen ions (H+) in it. Higher [H+] means lower pH and vice versa.

2. Surface Tension
- Surface tension is a measure of the cohesive forces between the molecules of a liquid. It causes the surface of a liquid to act like an elastic membrane.
- It is measured using a surface tension meter and is expressed in units of force per unit length (N/m).
- Liquids with higher surface tension have a greater tendency to contract and occupy the smallest surface area possible. This property allows liquids to form droplets.

3. Viscosity
- Viscosity is a measure of a liquid's resistance to flow. It is a measure of the internal friction between layers of a moving liquid.
- It is measured using a viscometer and is expressed in units of force times time per area (Pa•s).
- Liquids with higher viscosity flow more slowly compared to liquids with lower viscosity. The viscosity of a liquid depends on the size and shape of its molecules.
