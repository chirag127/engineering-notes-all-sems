# Life Skills for Success
1. Communication Skills
- Verbal communication: Speak clearly and concisely; listen actively; ask questions
- Written communication: Write clearly and concisely; be aware of tone and style
2. Problem-Solving Skills
- Identify the problem thoroughly before solving
- Brainstorm multiple solutions and evaluate the pros and cons of each
- Think creatively and logically
3. Critical Thinking Skills
- Ask clarifying questions and seek out missing information
- Challenge assumptions and evaluate arguments and evidence objectively
- Seek out diverse perspectives and look for complexities and nuances
4. Technical Skills
- Stay up-to-date with developments in your field of interest or industry
- Apply technical knowledge to solve problems; learn new tools and software as needed
5. Time Management Skills
- Prioritize important tasks; don't get distracted by less important tasks
- Create schedules and to-do lists and hold yourself accountable
- Learn to say 'no' when taking on more work will lead to being overwhelmed
