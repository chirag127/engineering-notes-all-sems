# Level

## Point 1
- Level refers to the position of something in a scale of measurement or method of ranking.
- The level indicates the relative magnitude of something as compared to other similar things.
- A level is a relative measure which denotes higher or lower positions in a hierarchy.

## Point 2
- Levels help understand a hierarchical structure and aid comparisons.
- In any system or organization, levels indicate the relative importance or complexity of components.
- The levels of a multi-storey building indicate the relative positions of floors. Higher levels are positioned above lower levels.
- Levels are used to denote stages or phases in a process. For e.g. different levels of education such as primary, secondary and higher secondary levels.

## Point 3
- The term 'level' implies a vertical structure with components arranged in a top-down manner in order of increasing rank or complexity.
- Level is a key concept in progression as it represents the stage of advancement in a process. Completing one level unlocks the next level which is at a higher position in the structure.
- In exams, passing at a certain percentage score is required to achieve a level of performance. Higher levels require better performance to be achieved.
