### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample.

1. Take 100 ml of the water sample in a conical flask.
2. Add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to it.
3. Titrate the solution with 0.1N HCl using a burette until the pink color disappears. Note down the volume of HCl used.
4. Repeat the titration with another 100 ml of water sample and note down the volume of HCl used.
5. The alkalinity of water sample is calculated as:

Alkalinity (mg/L as CaCO3) = (Volume of HCl used (ml) x Concentration of HCl (N) x Equivalent weight of CaCO3 (50)) / Volume of water sample (L)

The alkalinity determines the quantitative capacity of water to neutralize acids. It indicates the buffering capacity of water against acidification. The main causes of alkalinity in natural waters are bicarbonates, carbonates, hydroxides, and borates of calcium, magnesium, sodium, and potassium.
