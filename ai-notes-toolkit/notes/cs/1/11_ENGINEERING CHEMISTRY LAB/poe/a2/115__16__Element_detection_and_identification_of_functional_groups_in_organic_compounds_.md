### 16. Element detection and identification of functional groups in organic compounds.

1. Elemental analysis:
- Carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen and halogens can be estimated using elemental analysers.
- Oxygen can be estimated by difference.
- Sulfur can be estimated by burning the compound in a stream of oxygen and absorbing the SO2 formed in a solution of hydrogen peroxide.

2. Functional group tests:
- Aldehydes give positive iodoform test and tollens' test.
- Ketones do not give iodoform test but give positive tollens' test after oxidation to aldehydes.
- Carboxylic acids give positive tests for aldehydes and also give brisk effervescence with carbonates/bicarbonates.
- Alcohols give positive tests for aldehydes after oxidation and also give positive lucas test.
- Amines give positive tests for aldehydes after oxidation and also give positive ninhydrin test.

Points to note:
- The above tests should be performed on dilute solutions of compounds in appropriate solvents.
- The test results should be checked by performing control experiments with known compounds.
- Proper precautions should be taken while handling and testing organic compounds as many of them are irritants/toxic/flammable.
