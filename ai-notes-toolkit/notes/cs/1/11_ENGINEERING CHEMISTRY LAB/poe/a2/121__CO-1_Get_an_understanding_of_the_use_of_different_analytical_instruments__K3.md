### CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

1. Spectroscopy
- Used to analyze composition of a sample by observing interaction between light and matter.
- Types: UV-Visible spectroscopy, Infrared spectroscopy, Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy, etc.
- Used to determine functional groups, structure of molecules, concentrations of compounds, etc.

2. Chromatography
- Used to separate components of a mixture for qualitative and quantitative analysis.
- Types: Thin layer chromatography, Column chromatography, Gas chromatography, High performance liquid chromatography, etc.
- Used for separating and analyzing mixtures, identifying compounds, purifying compounds, etc.

3. X-Ray Diffraction
- Used to analyze atomic and molecular structure of a substance.
- Produces a diffraction pattern which can be used to measure interplanar spacings and bond angles.
- Used to analyze crystal structure and identify crystalline compounds.
