### 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

- Take about 0.5g of pure ferrous ammonium sulphate crystals. Dissolve it in 50ml distilled water. Add 10-15 drops of dilute sulphuric acid. This forms the internal standard solution.
- Take 0.5g of Potassium dichromate crystals and dissolve in 100ml distilled water. This forms the primary standard solution. 
- Titrate the primary standard solution with the internal standard solution until a persistent violet or blue color is obtained. This is the end point.
- Note down the volumes of internal standard solution required to reach the end point. Repeat the titration 2-3 times and take the average.
- Calculate the strength/molarity of the primary standard solution (Potassium dichromate) using the formula, Strength (M) = (Volume of internal solution)/(Volume of external solution x Equivalent weight).
- The determined strength can be used to determine the unknown concentration of anotherIron(II) salt solution by direct titration.
