# Artificial Intelligence

## What is Artificial Intelligence?

1. AI is the simulation of human intelligence in machines like computers.
2. It is a branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines that can learn, reason, and carry out tasks like humans.
3. The core goals of AI include:

- Reasoning: Ability to solve problems, draw inferences and make decisions.
- Knowledge: Representation and management of what the system knows.
- Planning: Coming up with a series of actions to achieve a goal.
- Learning: Improving performance from experience.
- Natural language processing: Ability to understand human language.
- Perception: Ability to use input from sensors like vision, speech, etc.

4. The major types of AI are:

- Narrow AI: Specialized in a specific task like playing chess or solving equations. Most AI today is narrow AI.
- General AI: A machine with human-level intelligence that can perform any intellectual task a human can. It does not exist yet.
- Artificial Superintelligence: Hypothetical machine that surpasses human intelligence. Also does not exist yet and is difficult to predict if or when it may be developed.

5. AI has a wide range of applications including:

- Automation: Automating tasks like customer service, transportation, etc.
- Healthcare: Diagnosing diseases, suggesting treatments, robotic surgery, etc.
- Education: Personalized learning, grading, feedback, etc.
- Manufacturing: Optimizing production, quality control, etc.
