# Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

## Formaldehyde

- Formaldehyde is an organic compound with the formula CH2O.
- It is a colorless gas with a pungent smell.
- It is mainly used in the production of industrial resins and as a preservative in medical laboratories and mortuaries.
- It is also used in the production of fertilizer, paper, plywood, and urea-formaldehyde resins.

## Urea Formaldehyde Resin

- Urea formaldehyde resin is a non-transparent, thermosetting resin or plastic obtained by chemical combination of urea and formaldehyde.
- It is used for the production of adhesives and coatings.
- UF resins have good chemical resistance and durability. Hence, they are used to make molded products with impact strength.
- However, they have some disadvantages like formaldehyde emission and low water resistance.
