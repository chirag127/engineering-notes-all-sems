### 7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

1. Take about 0.5 g of pure dry ferrous ammonium sulfate crystals in a clean dry flask.
2. Add 50 ml of distilled water and shake well till the salt completely dissolves.
3. Add 2-3 drops of potassium dichromate solution as external indicator. The colour changes to reddish-brown.
4. titrate the solution against N/10 potassium permanganate solution till the colour of the solution becomes bluish-green.
5. Note down the titre value.
6. Repeat the experiment two more times and take the average of the three titre values.
7. Calculate the percentage strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate using the formula: %Strength = ( titre value x Normality of KMnO4 x Equivalent weight of FeSO4 x 100)/Weight of sample taken.
