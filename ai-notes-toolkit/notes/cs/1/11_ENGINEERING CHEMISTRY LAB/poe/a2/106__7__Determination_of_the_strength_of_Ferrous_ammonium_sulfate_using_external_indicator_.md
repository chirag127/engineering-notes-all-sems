### 7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

1. Take about 0.5 g of pure recrystallized ferrous ammonium sulfate in a clean and dry conical flask.
2. Add 20 mL of distilled water and heat the solution gently till the salt dissolves completely.
3. Allow the solution to cool and then add 2-3 drops of potassium dichromate solution (0.1 N). A reddish-orange color appears.
4. Now add ammonia solution dropwise with shaking till the color just disappears.
5. Note down the volume of ammonia solution added.
6. Repeat the titration with a fresh solution of ferrous ammonium sulfate. The volume of ammonia solution should be the same as before.
7. If not, correct the strength of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution and repeat.
8. The strength of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution is directly proportional to the volume of ammonia solution added.
