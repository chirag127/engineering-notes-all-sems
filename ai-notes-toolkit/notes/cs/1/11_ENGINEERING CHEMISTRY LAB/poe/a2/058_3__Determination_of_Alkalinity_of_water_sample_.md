### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample.

- Take 100ml of given water sample in a conical flask
- Add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator and shake well
- Titrate the solution with 0.1N HCl from a burette until the first permanent pink colour appears
- Note down the burette reading
- Repeat the titration and note down the reading again
- Calculate the mean of both the readings and note it down
- Alkalinity (mg/L as CaCO3) = ( burette reading (ml) x 0.1N HCl x 50 ) / volume of sample taken (ml)
- The alkalinity is expressed as mg/L of equivalent CaCO3

This process is used to determine the alkalinity of a water sample which gives an idea of the basic components present in it like bicarbonates, carbonates and hydroxides of metals like calcium and magnesium. A higher alkalinity indicates higher presence of basic ions which determines the buffering capacity of water to neutralize acids.
