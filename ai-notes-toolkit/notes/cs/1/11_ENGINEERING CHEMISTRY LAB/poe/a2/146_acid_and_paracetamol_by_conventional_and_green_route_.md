# Acid and Paracetamol by Conventional and Green Route

## Conventional Route

1. Acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin) is produced by acetylation of salicylic acid. Salicylic acid is obtained from sodium salicylate which is produced synthetically from phenol and carbon dioxide.
2. Paracetamol is produced by nitration of phenol to form p-nitrophenol which is then reduced to p-aminophenol. This is then acetylated using acetic anhydride to form paracetamol.

## Green Route

1. Salicylic acid can be obtained from willow tree bark via extraction which is a renewable source. This can then be acetylated to produce aspirin.
2. p-aminophenol used to produce paracetamol can be obtained from tyrosine which is derived from biomass. Tyrosine is converted to p-hydroxyphenyl pyruvic acid which is then converted to p-aminophenol. This is then acetylated using acetic anhydride to form paracetamol via a green route.
