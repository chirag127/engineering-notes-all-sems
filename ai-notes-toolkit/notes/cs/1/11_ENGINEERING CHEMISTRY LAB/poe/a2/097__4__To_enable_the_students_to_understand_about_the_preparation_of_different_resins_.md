### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins.

1. Epoxy Resins:
- Epoxy resins are prepared by reacting epichlorohydrin with bisphenol-A.
- This produces a three-dimensional cross-linked polymer which has high mechanical, chemical and heat resistance properties.
- The properties of epoxy resins can be varied by using different molecular weight between cross-links and different bisphenol-A to epichlorohydrin ratios.

2. Polyester Resins:
- Polyester resins are prepared by the reaction of dibasic organic acids and dihydric alcohols.
- Common acids used are phthalic anhydride and maleic anhydride and common dihydric alcohols are propylene glycol and dipropylene glycol.
- Polyester resins can be either orthophthalic, isophthalic or terephthalic based on the type of dibasic acid used.
- Polyester resins have good chemical resistance but poor temperature resistance.

3. Vinyl Ester Resins:
- Vinyl ester resins are prepared by the reaction of epoxy resins with unsaturated monocarboxylic acids.
- This produces a resin which has the mechanical properties of epoxy resins and chemical resistance and temperature resistance of polyester resins.
- The important properties of vinyl ester resins can be varied by using different types of epoxy resins and unsaturated monocarboxylic acids.
