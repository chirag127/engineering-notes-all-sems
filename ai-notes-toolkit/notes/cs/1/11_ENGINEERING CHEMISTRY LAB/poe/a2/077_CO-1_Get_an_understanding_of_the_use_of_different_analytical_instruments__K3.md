# CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

**Spectroscopy**

- Spectroscopy is used to analyze the interaction of electromagnetic radiation with matter.
- It is used to identify and quantify the chemical components of a sample.
- Commonly used spectroscopic techniques are UV-Vis spectroscopy, IR spectroscopy, NMR spectroscopy, and Mass spectroscopy.

UV-Vis spectroscopy: Used to study electronic transitions in molecules that absorb ultraviolet and/or visible light. It can be used to determine the concentration of a substance in solution or analyze the degree of conjugation in a molecule.

IR spectroscopy: Used to study vibrational transitions in molecules that absorb infrared light. It can be used to identify functional groups in organic molecules and analyze variations in molecular structure.

NMR spectroscopy: Used to study nuclear spin transitions in molecules placed in a magnetic field. It can be used to determine molecular structure, analyze purity and quantify the components of a mixture.

Mass spectroscopy: Used to measure the mass-to-charge ratio of ions to identify the chemical composition of a molecule. It can be used to determine the molecular weight of a compound and elucidate the structure of an unknown substance.

**Chromatography**

- Chromatography is used to separate mixtures into their individual components.
- Commonly used chromatographic techniques are column chromatography, gas chromatography, high performance liquid chromatography, and thin layer chromatography.
