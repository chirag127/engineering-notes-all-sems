# Synthesis of Organic Compounds: Adipic Acid

Adipic acid is an organic compound synthesized industrially from cyclohexanol and cyclohexanone. The synthesis involves the following steps:

1. Oxidation of cyclohexanol and cyclohexanone to form a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol. This is achieved using nitric acid as the oxidizing agent.
2. The oxidized mixture is then heated under reflux with sodium hydroxide to form sodium adipate. This is a nucleophilic addition reaction.
3. Sodium adipate is then acidified using sulphuric acid to precipitate adipic acid.
4. The precipitated adipic acid is filtered, washed and dried to obtain the final product.

Adipic acid synthesis is an important bulk industrial process to produce the monomer for nylon synthesis. The properties and structure of adipic acid allow it to be a versatile intermediate in organic syntheses of polymers and esters. A thorough understanding of this process enables students to understand nucleophilic addition reactions and oxidation processes in organic synthesis.
