### 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid.

1. Take a clean and dry burette. Fill it with the given liquid.
2. Take a clean glass plate and attach an iron stand with clamp on its edge.
3. Attach a thin glass tube (capillary tube) with the clamp in vertical position such that its lower end just touches the surface of liquid in burette.
4. Adjust the height of burette such that the liquid just enters the capillary tube. Note the difference in level of liquid inside and outside the capillary tube. This difference in level is the measure of surface tension of the liquid.
5. The formula to calculate surface tension is:

Surface tension = hρg/2r

Where,
h = Difference in level of liquid inside and outside capillary tube
ρ = Density of liquid
g = Acceleration due to gravity
r = Radius of capillary tube

6. Repeat the experiment for different capillary tubes and take the average to get the correct value of surface tension.
