### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic

- Adipic acid is an organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is a white crystalline solid. Adipic acid is the most common commercially available dicarboxylic acid.
- Synthesis of adipic acid: The industrial synthesis of adipic acid uses a process called the nitric acid oxidation of cyclohexanol. In this process, cyclohexanol is oxidized using nitric acid to produce adipic acid. The cyclohexanol may be derived from benzene. This synthesis process is highly efficient and involves low waste.
- Properties: Adipic acid is a mild irritant to skin and the respiratory tract. As a straight-chain aliphatic acid, it is moderately water-soluble but organic-soluble. It has a high boiling point due to its high molecular weight. The pKa values of its two carboxylic acid groups are 4.4 and 5.4.
- Uses: The primary use of adipic acid is as a monomer for the production of nylon, specifically nylon 6-6. It is also used in the production of polyurethane, plasticizers, lubricants and food additives.
