### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route.

1. Adipic acid:
- Synthesis by oxidation of cyclohexanol or cyclohexanone (conventional route)
- Synthesis by bio-based routes using renewable materials such as glucose, fatty acids, etc. (green route)
2. Paracetamol:
- Synthesis by nitration of phenol followed by reduction (conventional route)
- Synthesis by peroxyacid oxidation of p-aminophenol (green route)

The conventional routes typically use harsh conditions and toxic chemicals whereas the green routes use renewable and eco-friendly raw materials and mild reaction conditions. Understanding the synthesis of important organic compounds by both conventional and green routes will enable the students to appreciate the need for sustainable and eco-friendly industrial processes.
