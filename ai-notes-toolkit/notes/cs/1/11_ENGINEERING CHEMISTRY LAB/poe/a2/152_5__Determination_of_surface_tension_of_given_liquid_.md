##### 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid.

1. Select a clean beaker/container and fill it with the given liquid. Ensure the beaker walls are clean and dry.
2. Carefully drop a small metal ball or needle into the liquid and measure the diameter of the depression caused on the surface. This depression is caused due to the surface tension of the liquid.
3. Use the formula: Surface tension = mg/2πr
   Where,
   m = Mass of the metal ball/needle
   g = Acceleration due to gravity
   r = Radius of the depression
4. Calculate the surface tension value and record it.
5. Repeat the experiment 2-3 times and take the average to get the accurate surface tension value of the given liquid.
6. Tabulate and compare the surface tension values of different liquids to study the effect of intermolecular forces on surface tension.
