# 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

1. Bleaching powder is calcium hypochlorite (Ca(OCl)2) and contains available chlorine.
2. Available chlorine is the amount of chlorine that can be liberated by the action of an acid. It is determined by titration with standard sodium thiosulfate solution.
3. Procedure:
    1. Weigh exactly 1 g of powdered bleaching powder and transfer to a conical flask.
    2. Add 50 mL of distilled water and shake for 2 minutes. Let it settle.
    3. Add 2 mL of 5% potassium iodide solution. This liberates iodine from iodide which is equivalent to the available chlorine in bleaching powder.
    4. Titrate the liberated iodine with 0.1 M sodium thiosulfate until the yellow color fades.
    5. Add 1 mL of starch indicator and continue titration until the blue-black color disappears.
    6. Note the titre value.
4. Calculation:
    Available chlorine (%) = (Titre value x 0.003545 x 100)/Weight of bleaching powder (in grams)
