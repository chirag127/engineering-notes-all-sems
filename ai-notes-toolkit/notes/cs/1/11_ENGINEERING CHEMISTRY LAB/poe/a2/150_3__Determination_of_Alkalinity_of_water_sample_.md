### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample.

1. Take 100ml of the given water sample in a conical flask.
2. Add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to it.
3. Titrate the solution with 0.1N HCl till the pink color disappears.
4. Note down the volume of HCl consumed.
5. Calculate the alkalinity in terms of mg/L of CaCO3 using the formula:

Alkalinity (mg/L) = (Volume of HCl x Normality of HCl x Eq.wt. of CaCO3) / Volume of sample (L)

6. The alkalinity is expressed in terms of equivalent per liter (eq/L) or parts per million (ppm) or milliequivalents per liter (meq/L).
