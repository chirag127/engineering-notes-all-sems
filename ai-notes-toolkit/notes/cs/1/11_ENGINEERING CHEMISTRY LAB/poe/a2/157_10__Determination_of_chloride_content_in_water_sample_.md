# 10. Determination of chloride content in water sample

1.  Take 100 mL of given water sample in a conical flask.
2.  Add 3-4 drops of potassium chromate indicator to the sample. The water sample turns yellow.
3.  Titrate the water sample against 0.1 M silver nitrate solution taken in a burette.
4.  Shake the conical flask gently after each addition of silver nitrate solution.
5.  Continue titration till the yellow color of potassium chromate just disappears. Note down the burette reading.
6.  Calculate the chloride content in the water sample using the following formula:
    Cl<sup>-</sup> (in mg/L) = (Burette reading × Molarity of AgNO<sub>3</sub> × 35.45) / Volume of water sample in liters

7.  Repeat the experiment thrice and take the average of chloride content to get the correct result.
