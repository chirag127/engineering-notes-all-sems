### 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid.

1. Take a clean undamaged capillary tube of uniform bore. Clean and dry it thoroughly.
2. Dip one end of the capillary tube in the given liquid whose surface tension is to be determined.
3. Measure the height (h) to which the liquid rises in the capillary tube.
4. Use the formula:
Surface tension (γ) = ρgh
Where,
ρ = Density of liquid
g = Acceleration due to gravity
h = Height to which liquid rises in the capillary tube
5. Calculate the surface tension of the given liquid using the above formula and known values of density and acceleration due to gravity.
