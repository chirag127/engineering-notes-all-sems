### 15. Determination of Rate constant of hydrolysis of esters.

1. Esters are hydrolyzed by acids and alkalis. The rate of hydrolysis can be determined by measuring the amount of ester hydrolyzed at different time intervals.
2. The rate of reaction is directly proportional to the concentration of ester. Therefore, the plot of log of concentration of ester vs time gives a straight line.
3. The slope of the straight line gives the rate constant of hydrolysis of ester.
4. The effect of temperature, pH and catalysts on the rate of hydrolysis can also be studied to get the activation energy and other constants.
5. The rate constant determined in this way helps to decide the suitability of an ester as a flavoring agent or polymer. Esters with high rate constants get hydrolyzed quickly and hence are not suitable for applications requiring stability.
