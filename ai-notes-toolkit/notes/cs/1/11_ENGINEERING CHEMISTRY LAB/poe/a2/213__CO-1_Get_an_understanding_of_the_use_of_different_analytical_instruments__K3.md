### CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

1. Spectroscopy
- Measures interaction of light and matter to identify chemical composition
- Types: UV-visible, IR, NMR, Mass spectrometry
- Used to identify functional groups, purity of compounds, structure elucidation

2. Chromatography
- Separates analytes based on differential distribution between mobile and stationary phases
- Types: Thin layer, column, gas chromatography
- Used for separation, identification, quantification of compounds in a mixture

3. Thermal analysis
- Measures physical changes of a substance with change in temperature
- Types: DSC, TGA, DTA
- Used to study transitions like glass transitions, melting points, decomposition temperatures

4. Microscopy
- Produces magnified visual or digital images of objects
- Types: Optical, electron, atomic force, scanning probe microscopy
- Used to study morphology, topography at micro and nano scale levels
