# CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

1. Spectroscopy
- Measures how light interacts with matter to determine composition of a sample.
- Types: Atomic absorption spectroscopy, Atomic emission spectroscopy, Infrared spectroscopy, Raman spectroscopy, Ultraviolet-visible spectroscopy.
- Used to identify and quantify atoms or molecules in a sample.

2. Chromatography
- Used to separate and analyze compounds in a mixture.
- Works on the principle of differential migration of components over a stationary phase.
- Types: Gas chromatography, High performance liquid chromatography, Thin layer chromatography.
- Used for purification, identification and quantification of compounds.

3. X-Ray diffraction
- Measures intensity of X-Rays scattered by a sample to determine its crystal structure.
- Requires sample to be crystalline solid.
- Used to determine atomic arrangement of a crystal and hence its structure.

4. Mass spectrometry
- Measures mass-to-charge ratio of ions to identify the chemical composition of a compound.
- Types: Time-of-flight mass spectrometry, Quadrupole mass spectrometry, Ion trap mass spectrometry, Fourier transform ion cyclotron resonance mass spectrometry.
- Used for determination of molecular weight and structure of a compound.
