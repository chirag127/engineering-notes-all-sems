# 15. Determination of Rate constant of hydrolysis of esters

1. Take a known concentration of ester say ethyl acetate in water.
2. Note the initial rate of reaction by titrating the reaction mixture against standard NaOH solution at different time intervals.
3. Plot a graph of concentration of ethyl acetate versus time. The graph will be exponential in nature.
4. Calculate the rate constant (k) from the slope of the graph.
5. The effect of temperature, catalyst and substituent on the rate can be studied to understand the mechanism of reaction.
6. The rate expression can be deduced by measuring the rate at different initial concentrations. A first order reaction with respect to ester is usually found.
