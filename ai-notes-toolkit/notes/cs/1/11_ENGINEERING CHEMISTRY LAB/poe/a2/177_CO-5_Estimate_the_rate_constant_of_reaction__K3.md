# CO-5 Estimate the rate constant of reaction. K3

## Points:

1. The rate constant, k, of a chemical reaction can be estimated from the experimental data of rate of reaction under certain conditions.
2. The rate law expression contains the rate constant, k, whose value can be calculated if the concentrations of reactants and the rate of reaction are known at a particular instant.
3. The units of rate constant depend upon the order of the reaction. For a first order reaction, unit is s^-1, for a second order reaction, unit is M^-1s^-1 and for a third order reaction, unit is M^-2s^-1.
4. The value of rate constant depends upon the nature of reactants, temperature and presence of catalyst. The rate constant increases with increase in temperature according to Arrhenius equation.
5. A plot of log k vs 1/T gives a straight line with negative slope. The activation energy can be calculated from the slope. A higher value of activation energy indicates that high energy is required to activate the reactants.
