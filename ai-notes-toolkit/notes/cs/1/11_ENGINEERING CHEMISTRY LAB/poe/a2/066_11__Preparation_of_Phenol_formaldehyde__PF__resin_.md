# 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin.

* Phenol and formaldehyde are the two monomers used to prepare phenol formaldehyde resin.
* The reaction is carried out in alkaline medium (pH 8-10) using sodium hydroxide as catalyst.
* The phenol and formaldehyde are taken in 1:1 to 1:2 molar ratio. Excess formaldehyde is used to achieve higher crosslinking.
* The reaction mixture is heated to 80-90°C with continuous stirring. As the reaction proceeds, the viscosity of the reaction mixture increases.
* Once the desired viscosity is achieved, the reaction mixture is cooled and neutralized using acid. The resin is then filtered, packaged and sold.
* The properties of the PF resin can be modified by using various molar ratios of phenol and formaldehyde and also by using various catalysts and reaction conditions.
* The PF resin finds applications in manufacturing of electrical equipment, adhesives and various engineered wood products like plywood, laminates, etc.
