### 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

1. Take about 0.5 g of pure ferrous ammonium sulphate crystals and dissolve in 100 mL distilled water in a conical flask.
2. Add 5 mL of concentrated sulphuric acid and heat the solution gently till white fumes of sulphur trioxide appear. Cool the solution. This reduces Fe^3+ ions to Fe^2+ ions.
3. Prepare 0.2 M potassium dichromate solution.
4. Add the dichromate solution from a burette to the ferrous ammonium sulphate solution in the conical flask with gentle shaking, till the blue-black colour of ferrous ammonium sulphate just disappears.
5. Note the volume of dichromate solution used. Repeat the titration two more times and take the average.
6. From the average volume of dichromate solution used, calculate the molarity of the dichromate solution.
