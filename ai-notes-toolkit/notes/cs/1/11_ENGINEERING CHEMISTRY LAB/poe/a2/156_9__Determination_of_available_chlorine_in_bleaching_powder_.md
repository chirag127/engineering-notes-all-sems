# 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

- Bleaching powder is calcium hypochlorite (Ca(OCl)2) and contains available chlorine.
- Available chlorine is the amount of chlorine present in bleaching powder that can be used for bleaching.
- Bleaching powder sample is taken in a flask and added with distilled water.
- Acidified potassium iodide solution is added to the flask. This converts the available chlorine to hypochlorous acid which then liberates iodine from potassium iodide.
- The liberated iodine is titrated against sodium thiosulphate solution using starch as indicator.
- At the endpoint, the blue-black colour of the starch-iodine complex just disappears.
- The amount of iodine liberated, which is equivalent to the available chlorine, is calculated to determine the available chlorine percentage in the bleaching powder sample.
