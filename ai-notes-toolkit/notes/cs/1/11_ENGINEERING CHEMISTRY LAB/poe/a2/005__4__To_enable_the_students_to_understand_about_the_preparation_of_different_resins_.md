### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins.

1. Melamine-formaldehyde resin (MF resin):
- Melamine and formaldehyde are the monomers used.
- They are heated in a reactor in presence of acid/base catalyst.
- The two monomers react to form a three-dimensional crosslinked structure.
- It forms a thermosetting resin which cures on heating.
- Used for making high-quality decorative laminates.

2. Urea-formaldehyde resin (UF resin):
- Urea and formaldehyde are the monomers used.
- They are heated in a reactor in presence of acid/base catalyst.
- The two monomers react to form a three-dimensional crosslinked structure.
- It forms a thermosetting resin which cures on heating.
- Used as wood adhesives in plywood and particle boards.

3. Phenol-formaldehyde resin (PF resin):
- Phenol and formaldehyde are the monomers used.
- They are heated in a reactor in presence of acid catalyst.
- The phenol reacts with formaldehyde to form a three-dimensional crosslinked structure.
- It forms a thermosetting resin which cures on heating.
- Used for making decorative laminates and as adhesives.
