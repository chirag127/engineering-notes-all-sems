### 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity of water.

1. Chloride content:
- Chlorides are ions of chlorine which is added to water during its journey through soil and rocks.
- High chloride content makes water unsuitable for drinking as it gives a salty taste.
- Chloride content can be tested using silver nitrate solution which gives a white precipitate of silver chloride if chlorides are present.

2. Hardness of water:
- Hardness is caused due to the presence of calcium and magnesium salts.
- Temporary hardness is due to bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium which can be removed by boiling.
- Permanent hardness is due to sulphates and chlorides of calcium and magnesium and cannot be removed by boiling.
- Hard water requires more soap for washing and can form scale in boilers and pipes.
- Hardness can be tested using soap solution or EDTA solution.

3. Alkalinity of water:
- Alkalinity is due to the presence of carbonates, bicarbonates and hydroxides of sodium, potassium, calcium and magnesium.
- High alkalinity leads to bitter taste and irritation to eyes.
- Alkalinity can be tested using acid base indicators like phenolphthalein or methyl orange.
- Release of CO2 during acid addition identifies bicarbonate alkalinity.
