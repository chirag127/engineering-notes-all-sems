# Solar System

* The Sun is at the center of our solar system. It contains 99.8% of the mass of the solar system.
* The 8 planets in order of distance from the Sun are:

1. Mercury - Small, rocky planet, closest to Sun
2. Venus - Rocky planet with thick atmosphere and extreme surface temperatures
3. Earth - Only planet known to harbor life, with oceans and oxygen-rich atmosphere
4. Mars - Rocky red planet with polar ice caps
5. Jupiter - Largest planet, a gas giant with prominent red spot
6. Saturn - Gas giant known for prominent ring system made of particles of ice and rock
7. Uranus - Ice giant with sideways tilted magnetic field
8. Neptune - Ice giant, furthest from the Sun

* Beyond Neptune lies a collection of icy objects and debris called the Kuiper Belt.
* Pluto is a dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt. It is smaller than Earth's moon and has an eccentric, oval-shaped orbit.
* The solar system formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago from a giant cloud of hydrogen, helium, and particles of heavier elements. The Sun and planets coalesced out of this material due to gravity.
