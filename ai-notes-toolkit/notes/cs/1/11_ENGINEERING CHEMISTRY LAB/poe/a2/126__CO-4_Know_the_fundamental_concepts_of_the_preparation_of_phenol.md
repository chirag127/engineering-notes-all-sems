### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol.

1. Cumene process:
- Propylene is reacted with benzene in the presence of acid catalyst to produce cumene (isopropylbenzene).
- Cumene is then oxidized to produce cumene hydroperoxide.
- Cumene hydroperoxide is then cleaved in the presence of acid to produce phenol and acetone.

2. Raschig process:
- Anisole (methoxybenzene) is dehydrogenated in the presence of copper catalyst to produce phenol and methanol.
- The process suffers from over-oxidation of anisole leading to low yields of phenol.

3. Hydrolysis of chlorobenzene:
- Chlorobenzene is reacted with sodium hydroxide solution under high pressure and temperature to produce phenol and sodium chloride.
- The process suffers from side reactions leading to low yields and production of sodium phenoxide which is difficult toseparate.

4. Oxidation of hydrocarbons:
- Benzene or butane is oxidized in the presence of oxygen or air to produce phenol.
- The process requires high temperatures and suffers from over-oxidation of the hydrocarbons leading to low yields of phenol. The separation of products is also difficult.
