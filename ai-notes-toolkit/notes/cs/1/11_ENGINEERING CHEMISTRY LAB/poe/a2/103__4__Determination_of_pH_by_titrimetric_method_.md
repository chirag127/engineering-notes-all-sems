### 4. Determination of pH by titrimetric method.

1. Take a known volume of the solution whose pH is to be determined.
2. Add a few drops of pH indicator to it. Select the indicator depending upon the expected pH range of the solution.
3. Titrate the solution with a standard solution of acid/base.
4. Note the endpoint of the titration by observing the color change of the pH indicator.
5. Calculate the pH using the formula:
pH = pKw - log[conc. of H+/conc. of OH-]

Where pKw is the negative logarithm of the ionization constant of water.
Conc. of H+ can be determined from the volume and molarity of the acid/base used.
Conc. of OH- can be determined from the ionization equilibrium of water.

This titrimetric method is based on the neutralization reaction between the unknown solution and the standard acid/base solution. The pH at the equivalence point gives the pH of the solution. Proper selection of the pH indicator and the type of standard solution (acid/base) is crucial for accurate determination of pH.
