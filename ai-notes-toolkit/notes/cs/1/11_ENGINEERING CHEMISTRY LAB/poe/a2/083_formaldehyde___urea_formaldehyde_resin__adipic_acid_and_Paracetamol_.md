Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin:
- Formaldehyde is an organic compound with the formula CH2O. It is a colorless gas with a pungent smell.
- Urea formaldehyde resin is a polymer that is formed by a chemical reaction between urea and formaldehyde. It is a thermosetting polymer that is strong, hard, and heat-resistant.
- Uses: Adhesives in plywood & particleboard, coatings, binders, etc.

Adipic Acid:
- Adipic acid is a white crystalline solid organic compound. It is slightly soluble in water and soluble in organic solvents.
- It is produced from a process called oxo synthesis using cyclohexanol & cyclohexanone as precursors.
- Uses: Nylon production (nylon 6,6), polyurethane foams, plasticizers, etc.

Paracetamol:
- Paracetamol is an over-the-counter analgesic & antipyretic drug. It is a white crystalline solid that is slightly soluble in water.
- It acts by inhibiting COX enzymes that produce prostaglandins which are inflammation-causing lipid compounds.
- Uses: Pain relief & fever reduction. It is generally safe in recommended doses but can cause liver damage in overdose.
