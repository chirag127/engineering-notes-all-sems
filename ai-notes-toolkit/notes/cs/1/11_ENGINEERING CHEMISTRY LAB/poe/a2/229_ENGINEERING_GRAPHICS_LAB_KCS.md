# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS LAB KCS

1. Introduction
- Engineering Graphics is a tool which is used to communicate technical ideas and information through graphical representation.
- It plays an important role in engineering design and manufacture.
- In this lab, various tools and techniques used in Engineering Graphics will be studied.

2. Drawing Instruments
- Drawing board: Used as a base to draw. Made of well seasoned softwood or plywood.
- Drawing sheet: Sheet on which drawing is made. Comes in different sizes like A0,A1,A2, etc.
- Scales: Used to measure distances in drawing. Different types like plain scale, engineer's scale, etc. are used.
- Drawing pencils: Used for making lines of varying thickness. HB, 2H, 2B, etc. are commonly used.
- Colour pencils: Used to indicate different materials in the drawing.
- Erasers: Used to remove unwanted lines.
- Set squares: Used to draw parallel lines and other geometrical constructions at specific angles.

3. Geometrical Constructions
- How to construct points, lines and circles using drawing instruments.
- Division of lines and circles into equal parts.
- Properties of triangles, quadrilaterals and circles.
- Tangents and normals to circles.

4. Orthographic Projections
- Multiview drawings of 3D objects shown using orthographic projections.
- First angle and third angle projection methods studied.
- Conversion between different projections.
- Sections and conventions used in engineering drawings.
