# 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer.

## Aim:
To determine the viscosity of a given liquid using viscometer.

## Apparatus Required:
- Viscometer
- Stopwatch
- Given liquid sample

## Procedure:
1. Clean the viscometer and rinse it with the liquid sample.
2. Fill the viscometer with the liquid sample.
3. Note the time taken for the liquid to flow through the viscometer. This is the flow time (t).
4. Calculate the viscosity (η) using the formula:
η = K × t
Where, K is the viscometer constant.

## Precautions:
1. Ensure there are no air bubbles in the viscometer.
2. Note the time taken for the liquid meniscus to pass through the two marks accuratelly.
3. Keep the viscometer vertically for the liquid to flow freely under gravity.
