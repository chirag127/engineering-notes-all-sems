# 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid

### Aim:
To determine the surface tension of a given liquid by capillary rise method.

### Apparatus required:
- Capillary tube
- Given liquid (water, ethanol, etc.)
- Ruler

### Procedure:
1. Clean and dry the capillary tube.
2. Immerse one end of the capillary tube in the given liquid.
3. Measure the height (h) to which the liquid rises in the capillary tube.
4. Repeat the experiment 3-4 times and take the average of the readings.

### Calculation:
Surface tension (γ) = ρgh
Where,
ρ = Density of liquid
g = Acceleration due to gravity
h = Height of liquid rise

### Precautions:
1. Use clean and dry capillary tube for accurate results.
2. Handle the capillary tube carefully to avoid breakage.
3. Measure the height of rise carefully at the meniscus.

### Result:
Surface tension of given liquid is determined.
