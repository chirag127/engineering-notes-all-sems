### 12. Preparation of Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin.

- Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin is prepared by condensation polymerization of urea and formaldehyde.
- The reaction is carried out in aqueous medium and in the presence of an acid catalyst like hydrochloric acid or sulfuric acid.
- The molar ratio of urea to formaldehyde (U:F) is 1:2 and the solution is heated to 60-70°C.
- As the reaction progresses, the viscosity of the reaction mixture increases.
- When the desired viscosity is reached, the reaction is stopped by neutralizing the acid with sodium hydroxide and cooling the mixture.
- The resin is isolated by precipitating in a nonsolvent like acetone or methanol.
- The precipitated resin is filtered, washed and dried.
- The molecular weight of resin can be increased by further heating the resin under alkaline conditions which leads to condensation polymerization.
- The important characteristics of UF resin are:

1. Low cost.
2. Fast curing.
3. Good adhesion to wood and paper.
4. Good water resistance.
5. Emits formaldehyde which is toxic.

- UF resin finds applications in manufacturing wood-based composite boards like plywood, laminated boards, particleboards, etc.
