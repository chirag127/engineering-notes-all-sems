### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer.

1. Take the viscometer and clean it properly.
2. Fill the outer jacket of viscometer with the liquid whose viscosity is to be determined.
3. Fill the inner tube of viscometer with the same liquid.
4. Note the time taken by the liquid to flow from upper mark to lower mark on the viscometer. This time is known as flow time (t).
5. Calculate the viscosity (η) using the relation:
    η = K × t
    Where, K is the viscometer constant.
6. The viscosity is expressed in poise or centipoise (cP).
