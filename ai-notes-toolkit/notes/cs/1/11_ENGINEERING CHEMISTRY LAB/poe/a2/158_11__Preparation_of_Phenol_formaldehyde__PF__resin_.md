# 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin

1. Phenol and formaldehyde are the two monomers used to prepare phenol formaldehyde resin.
2. The manufacturing process involves two steps:
	i. Reacting phenol and formaldehyde under basic conditions to produce a low molecular weight pre-polymer called resole.
	ii. Further condensing the resole under acid conditions to produce a high molecular weight 3-dimensional network polymer called novolac.
3. The molar ratio of phenol to formaldehyde and the type of catalyst used controls the properties of the final resin.
	- Resole: Mol ratio 1:1 to 1:2; Catalyst: Base like Sodium hydroxide
	- Novolac: Mol ratio >1:1; Catalyst: Acid like Oxalic acid or Sulfuric acid
4. PF resin is widely used as adhesives, laminates, paints, coatings and molding compounds due to properties like good insulation, heat resistance, dimensional stability, etc.
