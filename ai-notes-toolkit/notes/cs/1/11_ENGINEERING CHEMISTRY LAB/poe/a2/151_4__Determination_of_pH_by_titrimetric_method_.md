### 4. Determination of pH by titrimetric method.

- Take a known volume of the given solution in a conical flask.
- Add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to it.
- Titrate the solution with 0.1 M NaOH till the first permanent pink color appears.
- Note down the burette reading. This gives the volume of NaOH used (V1 ml).
- Repeat the titration with another portion of the given solution. Note down the burette reading (V2 ml).
- Take the average of the two readings (V = (V1 + V2) / 2).
- Calculate the pH using the formula: pH = -log(V/10), where V is the average volume of NaOH in ml.

The pH can be determined titrimetrically by performing a basic titration and using an appropriate indicator. The end point is identified when the indicator changes color and the volume of the basic titrant used gives the hydrogen ion concentration which can be converted to pH. The above steps describe the procedure to determine pH of a given solution using titrimetric method.
