

# List of Experiments

1. The Mpemba Effect: This experiment studies the phenomenon in which hot water can freeze faster than cold water.

2. The Boyle's Law Experiment: This experiment studies the relationship between the pressure and volume of a gas.

3. The Bernoulli's Principle Experiment: This experiment studies the relationship between the pressure, velocity, and height of a fluid.

4. The Archimedes' Principle Experiment: This experiment studies the relationship between the buoyant force and the volume of an object submerged in a fluid.

5. The Faraday's Law of Induction Experiment: This experiment studies the relationship between the electric current and the magnetic field.

6. The Ohm's Law Experiment: This experiment studies the relationship between the current and voltage in an electrical circuit.

7. The Doppler Effect Experiment: This experiment studies the phenomenon in which the frequency of a sound wave changes as the source moves relative to the observer.
