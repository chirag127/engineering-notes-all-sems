
# CO-3 Measure the Hardness and Alkalinity of the Water. K3

1. Hardness of water is the measure of the amount of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, present in the water.

2. Alkalinity of water is the measure of the ability of the water to neutralize acids, and is typically expressed as the concentration of calcium carbonate (CaCO3) in the water.

3. Hardness can be measured using a variety of methods, including titration, atomic absorption spectroscopy, and colorimetry.

4. Alkalinity can be measured using a variety of methods, including titration, ion chromatography, and colorimetry.

5. Hardness and alkalinity are important parameters to consider when evaluating the suitability of water for drinking, irrigation, and other uses.
