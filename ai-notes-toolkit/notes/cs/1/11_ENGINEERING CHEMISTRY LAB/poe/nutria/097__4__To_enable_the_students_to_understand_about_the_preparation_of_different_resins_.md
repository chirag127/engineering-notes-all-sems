
### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins

* Resins are materials that are used to form strong and durable bonds between surfaces. They are often used in the construction and repair of furniture, automobiles, and other objects.
* Resins are typically made up of a combination of materials, including polymers, monomers, and other chemicals. The combination of these materials determines the properties of the resin and its ability to form a strong bond.
* Resins can be prepared in a variety of ways, depending on the desired properties of the final product. For example, some resins may be heated and cooled to form a solid material, while others may be mixed with solvents to form a liquid material.
* The preparation of different resins involves the selection of the appropriate materials, the correct combination of these materials, and the precise application of heat and/or pressure. It is important to understand the properties of the different materials and the desired outcome of the resin in order to achieve the desired results.
