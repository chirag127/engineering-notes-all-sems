

### Synthesis of Organic Compounds such as Adipic

1. Organic compounds are molecules that contain carbon and hydrogen atoms and can also include other elements such as oxygen, nitrogen, and sulfur. 
2. Adipic acid is an organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is a white, crystalline, odorless solid. 
3. Synthesis of adipic acid involves the oxidation of cyclohexanol with nitric acid. This reaction is catalyzed by vanadium (V) oxide, and the product of the reaction is cyclohexanone. 
4. Cyclohexanone is then oxidized with nitric acid and catalyzed by vanadium (V) oxide to form adipic acid. 
5. Adipic acid can be used to make nylon, polyurethanes, and other polymers. It is also used in the production of food additives, pharmaceuticals, and other industrial chemicals.
