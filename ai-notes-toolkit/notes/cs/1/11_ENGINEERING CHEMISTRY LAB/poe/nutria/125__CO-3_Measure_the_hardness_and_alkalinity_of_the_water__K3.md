3. Hardness and alkalinity of water can be measured using a titration method. This involves adding a standard acid solution to the water sample until the pH of the solution reaches a certain level. 
4. The amount of acid required to reach the desired pH is then used to calculate the hardness and alkalinity of the water sample. 
5. The hardness of water is typically expressed as milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate (CaCO3).
