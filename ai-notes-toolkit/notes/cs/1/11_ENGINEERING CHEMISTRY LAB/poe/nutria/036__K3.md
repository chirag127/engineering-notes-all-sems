
### K3

1. K3 is a term used to refer to the three most important elements of knowledge management: knowledge creation, knowledge sharing, and knowledge application.

2. Knowledge creation is the process of generating new ideas and insights that can be used to improve an organization's operations. It involves research, experimentation, and analysis.

3. Knowledge sharing is the process of transferring knowledge from one person to another. It involves communication, collaboration, and networking.

4. Knowledge application is the process of using knowledge to solve problems and make decisions. It involves problem-solving, decision-making, and implementation.
