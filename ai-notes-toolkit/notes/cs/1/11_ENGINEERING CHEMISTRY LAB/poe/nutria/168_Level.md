
# Level

A level is a type of measurement used to measure the height or depth of an object. It is used in a variety of fields, such as architecture, engineering, surveying, and construction.

- Levels are typically made of a long, straight, rigid rod with a spirit level attached to one end.
- The spirit level is a device that contains a bubble of air which is used to indicate a horizontal or vertical plane.
- Levels are used to ensure that a surface is level or plumb, or to measure the height of an object.
- They are also used to measure the angle of a slope.
- Levels are available in different sizes and accuracy levels, depending on the application.
- Levels are typically made of metal or plastic, and are often equipped with a handle for easy carrying.
