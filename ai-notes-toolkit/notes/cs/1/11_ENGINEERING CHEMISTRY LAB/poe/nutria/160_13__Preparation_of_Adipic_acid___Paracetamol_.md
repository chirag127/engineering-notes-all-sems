
# 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

* Adipic acid and paracetamol are two important organic compounds used in the manufacture of pharmaceuticals, plastics and dyes.
* Adipic acid is a white, crystalline solid with a melting point of 152°C. It is prepared by the oxidation of cyclohexanol with nitric acid.
* Paracetamol is a white, crystalline solid with a melting point of 169°C. It is prepared by the reaction of phenol with acetic anhydride in the presence of an acid catalyst.
* Both adipic acid and paracetamol are used in the production of various pharmaceuticals, plastics and dyes. Adipic acid is used in the production of nylon, while paracetamol is used in the production of pain relievers.
* Adipic acid and paracetamol can also be used in the synthesis of other organic compounds, such as esters, amides and amines.
