
# CO2 Measurement of Molecular and System Properties such as Surface Tension

1. Carbon dioxide (CO2) is a non-polar molecule composed of two oxygen atoms and one carbon atom.

2. The molecular structure of CO2 is linear, and the molecule has no net dipole moment.

3. The molecular weight of CO2 is 44.01 g/mol and its molar mass is 0.044 kg/mol.

4. The boiling point of CO2 is -78.5°C and its melting point is -56.6°C.

5. The density of CO2 is 1.98 kg/m3 at 0°C and 1.5 kg/m3 at 25°C.

6. The surface tension of CO2 is 0.042 N/m at 25°C.

7. The viscosity of CO2 is 0.0014 Ns/m2 at 25°C.

8. The solubility of CO2 in water is 0.25 g/L at 25°C.
