
# 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

* Analytical instruments are used to measure and analyze physical and chemical properties of substances. 
* Analytical instruments can be used to measure properties such as temperature, light, pressure, and chemical composition. 
* Analytical instruments are used in a variety of fields such as biochemistry, geology, and engineering. 
* Common analytical instruments include spectrophotometers, chromatographs, and mass spectrometers. 
* Analytical instruments are used to analyze samples of materials to determine their composition and properties. 
* Analytical instruments are used to detect and measure the presence of various substances in a sample. 
* Analytical instruments can be used to measure physical properties such as temperature, pressure, and light. 
* Analytical instruments are used to measure chemical properties such as pH, concentrations, and molecular structure. 
* Analytical instruments are used to analyze the structure and composition of a sample. 
* Analytical instruments are used to identify and quantify unknown substances in a sample.
