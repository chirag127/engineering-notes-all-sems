
# A Liquid

A liquid is a state of matter that has a relatively low viscosity and conforms to the shape of its container. It has a definite volume, but no definite shape. Liquids are sometimes referred to as "fluids", although this term is often used to refer to all states of matter.

1. Liquids have a definite volume but no definite shape.
2. Liquids are less viscous than solids and gases.
3. Liquids are sometimes referred to as "fluids".
4. Liquids flow and can take the shape of their container.
5. Liquids have a surface tension which creates a film on the surface of the liquid.
6. Liquids have a relatively low compressibility.
7. Liquids are capable of conveying forces, such as pressure and shear.
8. Liquids can be mixed together to form solutions or suspensions.
