

# 7. Determination of the Strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate using External Indicator

1. Ferrous ammonium sulfate is a type of compound used in laboratory experiments and other scientific applications.

2. It is important to accurately determine the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate, as this can affect the outcome of an experiment.

3. An external indicator can be used to determine the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate. This involves adding a known amount of the indicator to a known amount of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution and observing the color change.

4. The color change will indicate the concentration of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution. The color change will be more intense for higher concentrations.

5. The concentration of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution can then be determined using a calibration curve.

6. It is important to note that the external indicator used must be compatible with the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution, as some indicators may not be suitable.
