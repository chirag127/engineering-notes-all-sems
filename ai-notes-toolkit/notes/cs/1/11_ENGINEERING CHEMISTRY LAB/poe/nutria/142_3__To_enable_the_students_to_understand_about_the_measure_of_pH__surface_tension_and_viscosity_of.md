
### To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of

- pH is a measure of the acidity or alkalinity of a solution. It is measured on a scale of 0 to 14, with 0 being the most acidic and 14 being the most alkaline.
- Surface tension is a measure of the elasticity of a liquid's surface. It is measured in millinewtons per meter (mN/m).
- Viscosity is a measure of a liquid's resistance to flow. It is measured in centipoise (cP).
