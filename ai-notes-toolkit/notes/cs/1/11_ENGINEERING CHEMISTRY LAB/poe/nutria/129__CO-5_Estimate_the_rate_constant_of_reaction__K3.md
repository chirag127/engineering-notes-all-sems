
### CO-5 Estimate the rate Constant of Reaction. K3

1. The rate constant of a reaction is a measure of the rate at which reactants are converted to products. 
2. It is usually expressed as the rate of the reaction per unit of concentration of the reactants. 
3. The rate constant of a reaction is dependent on the temperature and the type of reaction. 
4. For a given reaction, the rate constant can be calculated using the Arrhenius equation, which is given by:

K = Ae^(-Ea/RT)

Where:

A = Pre-exponential factor

Ea = Activation energy

R = Universal gas constant

T = Temperature in Kelvin

5. The rate constant can also be determined experimentally by measuring the rate of reaction at different temperatures. 
6. The rate constant can then be used to calculate the rate of reaction at other temperatures. 
7. Knowing the rate constant of a reaction is important in designing and controlling chemical processes, as well as predicting the products of a reaction.
