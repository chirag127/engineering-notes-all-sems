
### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer

1. Viscosity is the measure of a fluid's resistance to flow.
2. A viscometer is an instrument used to measure the viscosity of a fluid.
3. The most common type of viscometer is the rotational viscometer, which measures the torque required to rotate a spindle immersed in the fluid.
4. To use a rotational viscometer, the spindle is immersed in the fluid and a known torque is applied to the spindle.
5. The spindle is then rotated at a constant speed and the torque required to maintain the rotation is measured.
6. The viscosity of the fluid is then calculated from the measured torque and the known parameters of the spindle.
7. The viscosity of a given liquid can also be determined by other methods, such as capillary viscometers, falling sphere viscometers, orifice viscometers, and rheometers.
