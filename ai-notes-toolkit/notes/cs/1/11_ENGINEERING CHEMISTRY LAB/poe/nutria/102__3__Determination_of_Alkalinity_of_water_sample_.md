
### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of Water Sample

1. Alkalinity is the ability of a water sample to neutralize an acid.
2. Alkalinity is measured in terms of milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate (CaCO3).
3. Alkalinity can be determined by titration with a standard acid solution.
4. The endpoint of the titration is determined by a color indicator.
5. The total alkalinity of a water sample is calculated by subtracting the acid volume from the base volume.
6. The result is then multiplied by the equivalent weight of calcium carbonate to determine the alkalinity.
