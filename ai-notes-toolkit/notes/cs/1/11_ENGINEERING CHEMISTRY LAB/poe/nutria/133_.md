
# The History of the Internet

1. The Internet began in the 1960s as a project of the United States Department of Defense, known as ARPANET.

2. In the 1970s, the idea of a global computer network was developed by a group of computer scientists, including Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn.

3. In the 1980s, the National Science Foundation (NSF) established the NSFNET, which allowed for the connection of many different networks.

4. In the 1990s, the World Wide Web (WWW) was invented by Tim Berners-Lee, and the first web browser, Mosaic, was released.

5. In the 2000s, broadband internet became widely available, and the number of users of the internet around the world began to skyrocket.

6. Today, the internet is used for a variety of purposes, including communication, entertainment, commerce, and education.
