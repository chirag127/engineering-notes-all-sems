
### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route

* Synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol can be achieved through conventional and green route. 
* Conventional synthesis of organic compounds involves the use of chemical reagents and solvents such as mineral acids and organic solvents. These processes generate hazardous by-products and can be energy intensive. 
* Green synthesis, on the other hand, uses environmentally friendly reagents, catalysts and solvents, and is more cost-effective and energy-efficient.
* Adipic acid is an important industrial chemical used in the production of nylon and polyurethane. It can be synthesized through conventional route, which involves the oxidation of cyclohexanol with nitric acid, or through green synthesis, which uses aqueous hydrogen peroxide as oxidizing agent.
* Paracetamol is a widely used drug for the treatment of mild to moderate pain and fever. It can be synthesized through conventional route, which involves the reaction of phenol with acetic anhydride, or through green synthesis, which uses aqueous hydrogen peroxide as oxidizing agent.
