
# A Liquid
A liquid is a type of matter that has a constant volume but takes the shape of its container. It has a distinct surface and is able to flow. Liquids are made up of particles that are in constant motion and have a low level of attraction to each other.

1. Properties of a Liquid
    * Liquids have a fixed volume but take the shape of their container.
    * Liquids have a distinct surface.
    * Liquids are able to flow.
    * Liquids are made up of particles that are in constant motion.
    * Liquids have a low level of attraction to each other.

2. Examples of Liquids
    * Water
    * Alcohol
    * Oil
    * Juice
    * Milk
    * Mercury
