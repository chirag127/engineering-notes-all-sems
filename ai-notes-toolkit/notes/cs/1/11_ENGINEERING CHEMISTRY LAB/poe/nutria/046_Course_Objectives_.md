

# Course Objectives:

1. Understand the principles and fundamentals of programming
2. Develop an understanding of the core concepts of programming languages
3. Learn the fundamentals of data structures and algorithms
4. Develop basic problem-solving and debugging skills
5. Develop an understanding of the principles of software engineering
6. Develop an understanding of the principles of computer networks
7. Learn the fundamentals of web development
8. Develop an understanding of the principles of database design and management
9. Develop an understanding of the principles of user interface design
10. Develop an understanding of the principles of operating systems and computer architecture
