
### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample

1. Alkalinity is the measure of the capacity of water to neutralize acids.
2. Alkalinity can be determined by titration with a standard acid solution.
3. The titration is usually done with a pH meter and burette.
4. The end point of the titration is determined by a pH indicator.
5. The alkalinity of the sample is calculated from the volume of acid used and the normality of the acid.
