
### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

1. An analytical instrument is an instrument used to measure and analyze physical properties of a sample.
2. The types of analytical instruments include spectroscopy, chromatography, microscopy, and mass spectrometry.
3. Spectroscopy is the study of the interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation. It is used to identify and quantify chemical components in a sample.
4. Chromatography is a technique used to separate mixtures of substances into their individual components.
5. Microscopy is the study of small objects using a microscope. It is used to study the structure and composition of a sample.
6. Mass spectrometry is the analysis of mixtures of molecules based on their mass-to-charge ratio. It is used to identify and quantify chemical components in a sample.
7. Analytical instruments are used to measure and analyze physical properties such as composition, structure, and concentration of a sample.
