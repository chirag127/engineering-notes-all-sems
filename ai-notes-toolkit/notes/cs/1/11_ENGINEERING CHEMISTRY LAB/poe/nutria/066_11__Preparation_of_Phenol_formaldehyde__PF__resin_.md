
# 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) Resin

1. Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin is a thermosetting plastic material produced by the reaction of phenol and formaldehyde in the presence of an acid catalyst.

2. The reaction is exothermic and the product is a viscous liquid.

3. The reaction is usually carried out in aqueous solution and the pH is maintained at around 4.5 by the addition of acid.

4. The reaction is complete when the viscosity of the reaction mixture reaches a predetermined value.

5. The product is then neutralized with a base and the pH is adjusted to around 7.

6. The product is then cooled and moulded into the desired shape.

7. The product can then be cured at an elevated temperature to give the desired properties.
