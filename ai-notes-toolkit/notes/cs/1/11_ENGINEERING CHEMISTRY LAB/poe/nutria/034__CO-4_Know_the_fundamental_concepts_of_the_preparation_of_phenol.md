

### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol

1. Phenol is an organic compound with the chemical formula C6H5OH. It is a colorless, volatile, flammable liquid with a characteristic odor. 
2. Phenol can be prepared by several methods, including the Williamson ether synthesis, the Grignard reaction, and the Kolbe-Schmitt reaction. 
3. The Williamson ether synthesis involves the reaction of an alkyl halide with an alcohol in the presence of a base, such as sodium hydroxide, to form an ether. 
4. The Grignard reaction involves the reaction of an alkyl or aryl halide with a Grignard reagent (a magnesium halide) to form an alcohol. 
5. The Kolbe-Schmitt reaction involves the reaction of an acid with a carbonate or bicarbonate to form an organic compound. 
6. Phenol can also be prepared by the oxidation of benzene or other aromatic compounds.
