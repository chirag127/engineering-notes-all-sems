
# Acid and Paracetamol by Conventional and Green Route

* Acid and paracetamol can be synthesized by conventional and green routes. 
* The conventional route involves the use of a strong acid, such as sulfuric acid, to convert paracetamol into its acid form. 
* The green route utilizes an enzyme-catalyzed reaction to convert paracetamol into its acid form. 
* The green route is more environmentally friendly and uses fewer resources than the conventional route. 
* The green route also produces fewer byproducts, which can be more easily disposed of. 
* The green route is more expensive than the conventional route, but is becoming more cost-effective as technology advances.
