
### Course Outcomes Bloom's

1. Understand the basic principles of Bloom's Taxonomy and its application in educational settings.
2. Demonstrate an understanding of the various levels of Bloom's Taxonomy and how they relate to each other.
3. Explain the importance of Bloom's Taxonomy in the development of learning objectives.
4. Utilize Bloom's Taxonomy to create effective learning objectives that are measurable and attainable.
5. Analyze and evaluate student performance using the various levels of Bloom's Taxonomy.
6. Design and implement instruction that is based on Bloom's Taxonomy.
7. Develop assessment instruments that measure student learning outcomes based on Bloom's Taxonomy.
