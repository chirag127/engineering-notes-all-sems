
## 2. Determination of Hardness of Water Sample by EDTA Method

1. Hardness of water is an important physical property that is determined by the concentration of Ca2+ and Mg2+ ions present in water.

2. EDTA (Ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid) is a complexing agent used to determine the hardness of water by titration.

3. The EDTA titration method involves the addition of EDTA in a known concentration to a sample of water containing Ca2+ and Mg2+ ions, which then react with the EDTA to form a complex.

4. The end-point of the titration is determined by the use of an indicator, such as Eriochrome Black T, which changes color when all of the Ca2+ and Mg2+ ions have been complexed.

5. The total hardness of the water sample can then be calculated by multiplying the concentration of EDTA used in the titration by the volume of the sample.
