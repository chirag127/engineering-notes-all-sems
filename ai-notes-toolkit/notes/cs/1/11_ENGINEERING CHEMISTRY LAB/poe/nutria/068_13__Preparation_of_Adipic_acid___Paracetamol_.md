
# 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

1. Adipic acid is an organic compound used in the production of polymers, such as nylon and polyurethane. It can also be used in the production of pharmaceuticals, food additives, and other industrial products.
2. Paracetamol is an analgesic drug used to treat mild to moderate pain and fever. It is available in various forms, including tablets, capsules, and liquids.
3. Adipic acid and paracetamol can be prepared through a combination of chemical reactions. The first step involves the reaction of an aldehyde with ammonia to form an imine. This imine is then reacted with an acid chloride to form an amide. 
4. The amide is then reacted with acetic anhydride to form a carboxylic acid. This acid is then reacted with hydrazine to form adipic acid.
5. To prepare paracetamol, the adipic acid is then reacted with phenol in the presence of an acid catalyst. This reaction produces paracetamol and carbon dioxide.
