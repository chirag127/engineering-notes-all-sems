

### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer

1. Viscometers are instruments used to measure the viscosity of a liquid.
2. The viscosity of a liquid can be determined by measuring the time it takes for a given volume of the liquid to flow through a capillary tube.
3. The viscosity of a liquid is usually expressed in terms of centipoise, which is a measure of the liquid's resistance to flow.
4. To determine the viscosity of a given liquid using a viscometer, a sample of the liquid is placed in the viscometer and the time it takes for the liquid to flow through the capillary tube is measured.
5. The viscosity of the liquid can then be calculated using the equation: Viscosity = Time/Volume.
6. The viscosity of a liquid can also be determined using a rotational viscometer, which measures the torque required to rotate a spindle immersed in the liquid.
7. The viscosity of a liquid can also be determined using a falling ball viscometer, which measures the time it takes for a ball to fall through a given volume of the liquid.
8. The viscosity of a liquid can also be determined using a bubble viscometer, which measures the time it takes for a bubble to rise through a given volume of the liquid.
