
# A Liquid

A liquid is a state of matter characterized by a lack of fixed shape and an ability to flow. It is one of the four fundamental states of matter, along with solids, gases, and plasma. A liquid is a nearly incompressible fluid that conforms to the shape of its container but retains a (nearly) constant volume independent of pressure. 

Some of the most common liquids include water, oil, alcohol, and juice. Liquids are composed of tiny vibrating particles of matter, such as atoms, held together by intermolecular bonds. Like solids, the molecules in a liquid have a regular structure and move at different speeds. 

## Properties of a Liquid

- Liquids can flow and take the shape of their container. 
- The volume of a liquid remains constant, regardless of pressure. 
- The surface tension of a liquid is the result of the cohesive forces between molecules. 
- The viscosity of a liquid is the measure of its resistance to flow. 
- The boiling point of a liquid is the temperature at which it vaporizes. 
- The freezing point of a liquid is the temperature at which it solidifies.
