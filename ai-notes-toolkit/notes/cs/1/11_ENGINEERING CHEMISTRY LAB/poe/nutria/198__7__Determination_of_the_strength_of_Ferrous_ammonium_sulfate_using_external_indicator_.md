
### 7. Determination of the Strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate Using External Indicator

1. Ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) is a salt of ferrous iron and ammonium sulfate.
2. FAS can be used as a reagent in various chemical reactions, such as in the determination of the strength of a solution.
3. An external indicator is a chemical compound that changes color when it comes into contact with another compound.
4. In the determination of the strength of FAS, an external indicator is used to detect the presence of FAS in a solution.
5. The indicator is added to the solution and the resulting color change indicates the strength of the FAS solution.
6. The color change of the indicator is compared to a color chart to determine the strength of the FAS solution.
7. The strength of the FAS solution can then be used to determine the amount of FAS needed for a particular reaction.
