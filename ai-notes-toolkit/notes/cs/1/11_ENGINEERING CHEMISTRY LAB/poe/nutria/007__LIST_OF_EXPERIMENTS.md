
## List of Experiments
1. Boyle's Law Experiment: This experiment was conducted by Robert Boyle in 1662 and is used to illustrate the relationship between pressure and volume of a gas.
2. Faraday's Law of Induction Experiment: This experiment was conducted by Michael Faraday in 1831 and is used to demonstrate the production of an electric current in a closed circuit when a magnetic field is applied.
3. Ohm's Law Experiment: This experiment was conducted by Georg Ohm in 1827 and is used to illustrate the relationship between current, voltage, and resistance in an electrical circuit.
4. Avogadro's Law Experiment: This experiment was conducted by Amedeo Avogadro in 1811 and is used to illustrate the relationship between the volume of a gas and its amount.
5. Charles' Law Experiment: This experiment was conducted by Jacques Charles in 1787 and is used to illustrate the relationship between temperature and volume of a gas.
6. Graham's Law of Diffusion Experiment: This experiment was conducted by Thomas Graham in 1851 and is used to illustrate the relationship between the rate of diffusion of two gases and their molecular weights.
7. Dalton's Law of Partial Pressure Experiment: This experiment was conducted by John Dalton in 1801 and is used to illustrate the relationship between the total pressure of a gas and the partial pressure of its components.
