
### 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity of water

1. Chloride content: Chloride is a common anion found in water. It is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) and is an indicator of water quality. High levels of chloride can be a sign of pollution or contamination.

2. Hardness: Hardness is a measure of the amount of calcium and magnesium dissolved in water. It is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) and is an indicator of water quality. High levels of hardness can cause scaling and staining of plumbing fixtures.

3. Alkalinity: Alkalinity is a measure of the buffering capacity of water. It is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) and is an indicator of water quality. High levels of alkalinity can cause corrosion of pipes and other plumbing fixtures.
