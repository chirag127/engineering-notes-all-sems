

# 9. Determination of Available Chlorine in Bleaching Powder

1. Bleaching powder is a white powder composed of calcium chloride, calcium hydroxide, and sodium hypochlorite.

2. It is used in the bleaching of textiles and paper products and as a disinfectant.

3. The available chlorine content of bleaching powder can be determined by titration with a standard solution of sodium thiosulfate.

4. A known volume of the bleaching powder solution is taken in a conical flask and titrated against the standard solution of sodium thiosulfate until the solution turns pale yellow.

5. The amount of sodium thiosulfate required for the titration is a measure of the available chlorine content of the bleaching powder.

6. The available chlorine content of the bleaching powder is calculated by multiplying the amount of sodium thiosulfate used for the titration with a factor of 0.086.
