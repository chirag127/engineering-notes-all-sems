
### 9. Determination of Available Chlorine in Bleaching Powder

- Bleaching powder is a white or yellowish powder composed of calcium hypochlorite (Ca(OCl)₂). 
- It is used as a disinfectant and bleaching agent in many industries.
- The available chlorine in bleaching powder can be determined by a titration method. 
- The sample is dissolved in water and then titrated with a standard sodium thiosulphate solution. 
- The end point of the titration is determined by the appearance of the yellow colour of iodine. 
- The amount of available chlorine present in the sample is calculated from the volume of sodium thiosulphate used in the titration. 
- The results obtained from this method are usually expressed as a percentage of available chlorine.
