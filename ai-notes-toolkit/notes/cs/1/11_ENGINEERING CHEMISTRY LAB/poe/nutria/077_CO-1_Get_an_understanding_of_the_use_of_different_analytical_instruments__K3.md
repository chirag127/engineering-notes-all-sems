
# CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

1. Analytical instruments are used to measure, analyze, and interpret physical and chemical properties of substances. 
2. Examples of analytical instruments include spectrophotometers, chromatographs, refractometers, and microscopes. 
3. Spectrophotometers measure the amount of light that is absorbed or emitted by a sample. 
4. Chromatographs are used to separate and identify components of a sample. 
5. Refractometers measure the refractive index of a sample, which can be used to identify and quantify substances. 
6. Microscopes are used to observe and identify particles in a sample. 
7. Analytical instruments are used in a variety of fields, including medicine, chemistry, and engineering.
