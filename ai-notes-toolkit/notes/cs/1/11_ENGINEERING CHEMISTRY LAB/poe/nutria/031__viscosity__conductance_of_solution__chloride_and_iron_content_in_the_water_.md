
### Viscosity, Conductance of Solution, Chloride and Iron Content in the Water

- Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is a measure of the internal friction of a fluid that is caused by the attraction between its molecules. The higher the viscosity, the slower the fluid flows.
- Conductance of a solution is a measure of its ability to conduct electrical current. It is proportional to the concentration of ions in the solution and is inversely proportional to the solution's resistance.
- Chloride is an anion found in many natural and synthetic compounds. It is present in seawater and is the most abundant anion in the oceans. It is also found in many minerals and soil.
- Iron is an essential nutrient for plants and animals. It is found in many foods and is an important component of hemoglobin, the molecule that carries oxygen in the blood. Iron is also found in many soils and is an important component of many industrial processes.
