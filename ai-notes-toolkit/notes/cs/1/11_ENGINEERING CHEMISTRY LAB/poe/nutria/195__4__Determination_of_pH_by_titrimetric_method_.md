
### 4. Determination of pH by Titrimetric Method

* Titrimetric methods involve the use of a titrant, which is a solution of known concentration, to determine the concentration of an unknown solution.
* In this method, the pH of the unknown solution is determined by titrating it with a standard solution of a base or an acid.
* The endpoint of the titration is determined by a color change, by the use of an indicator, or by the use of a pH meter.
* The pH of the unknown solution can then be calculated from the volume of titrant used and the concentration of the titrant.
