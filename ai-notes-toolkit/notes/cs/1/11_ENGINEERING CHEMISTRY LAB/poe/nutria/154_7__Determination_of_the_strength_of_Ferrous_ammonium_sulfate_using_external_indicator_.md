
# 7. Determination of the Strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate Using External Indicator

* Ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) is a compound composed of iron and ammonium sulfate. 
* It is commonly used as a reducing agent in organic chemistry. 
* The strength of FAS can be determined using an external indicator. 
* The indicator used for this purpose is potassium permanganate (KMnO4). 
* The KMnO4 is added to a solution of FAS, and the color of the solution is observed. 
* If the solution turns pink, it indicates that FAS is present, and the strength of FAS can be determined by the intensity of the pink color. 
* The intensity of the pink color is directly proportional to the strength of FAS. 
* The strength of FAS can also be determined by titration with a standard solution of KMnO4.
