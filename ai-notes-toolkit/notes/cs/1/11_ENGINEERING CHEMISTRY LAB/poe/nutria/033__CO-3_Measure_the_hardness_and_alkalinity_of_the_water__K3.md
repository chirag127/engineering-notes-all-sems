
### CO-3 Measure the hardness and alkalinity of the water. K3

1. Hardness of water is a measure of the amount of calcium and magnesium ions present in it.
2. Alkalinity of water is a measure of the amount of carbonate, bicarbonate, hydroxide, and other alkaline substances present in it.
3. Hardness of water is usually measured in parts per million (ppm) or milligrams per liter (mg/L).
4. Alkalinity of water is usually measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L).
5. Hardness of water can be determined by using a titration process with a calcium chloride solution.
6. Alkalinity of water can be determined by using a titration process with a sodium hydroxide solution.
7. High levels of hardness and alkalinity in water can cause scaling and staining of plumbing fixtures, and can also interfere with the effectiveness of detergents.
8. It is important to regularly test the hardness and alkalinity of water to ensure that it is safe for use.
