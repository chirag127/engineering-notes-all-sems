
### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

1. Adipic acid is a white crystalline solid with a molecular formula of C6H10O4. It is an important industrial chemical used in the manufacture of nylon, polyurethane, and other synthetic materials.
2. Paracetamol is a widely used analgesic and antipyretic drug. It is used to treat pain and fever.
3. The preparation of adipic acid involves the oxidation of cyclohexanol with nitric acid. The reaction is carried out in a sulfuric acid medium.
4. The preparation of paracetamol involves the reaction of phenol with acetic anhydride in the presence of an acid catalyst. The product is then purified by recrystallization.
5. Both adipic acid and paracetamol are important chemicals with a wide range of applications. Adipic acid is used in the manufacture of nylon and other synthetic materials, while paracetamol is used as an analgesic and antipyretic drug.
