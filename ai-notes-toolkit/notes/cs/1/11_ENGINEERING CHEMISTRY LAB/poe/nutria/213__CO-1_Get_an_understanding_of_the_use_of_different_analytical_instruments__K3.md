
### CO-1 Get an Understanding of the Use of Different Analytical Instruments. K3

1. Analytical instruments are used to measure and analyze physical and chemical properties of substances.
2. These instruments can measure properties such as mass, temperature, pressure, pH, viscosity, electrical conductivity, and optical properties.
3. They can be used to identify and quantify the components of a sample, as well as to compare the properties of different samples.
4. Some of the most commonly used analytical instruments include spectrophotometers, chromatographs, refractometers, and microscopes.
5. Each instrument has its own set of advantages and disadvantages, so it is important to choose the right instrument for the job.
6. Proper maintenance and calibration of the instrument is also essential for accurate and reliable results.
