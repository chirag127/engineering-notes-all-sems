
### 15. Determination of Rate constant of Hydrolysis of Esters

* Rate constant of hydrolysis of esters is determined by monitoring the disappearance of the ester and the formation of the acid and alcohol products of the reaction. 
* The rate constant of hydrolysis is calculated by plotting the logarithm of the concentration of the ester against time. 
* The rate of hydrolysis is also affected by the presence of catalysts and other factors such as temperature, solvent, and pH. 
* The rate constant of hydrolysis can be used to predict the stability of esters in a particular environment. 
* The rate constant of hydrolysis can be determined using a variety of methods such as spectrophotometric, chromatographic, and calorimetric techniques. 
* The rate constant of hydrolysis can also be used to develop a kinetic model of the reaction.
