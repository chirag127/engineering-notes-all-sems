
### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol

1. Phenol is an aromatic organic compound, consisting of a hydroxyl group (-OH) attached to a benzene ring.
2. Phenol can be prepared by the oxidation of benzene, either by direct oxidation or by hydrolysis of benzene sulfonates.
3. The direct oxidation of benzene to phenol is usually done with air or oxygen in the presence of a catalyst such as manganese dioxide.
4. The hydrolysis of benzene sulfonates involves the reaction of benzene sulfonates with water in the presence of an acid catalyst.
5. Phenol can also be prepared from the hydrolysis of chlorobenzene in the presence of an acid catalyst.
6. Phenol can also be prepared from the dehydration of cresols in the presence of an acid catalyst.
7. Phenol can also be prepared from the oxidation of cumene in the presence of a catalyst such as manganese dioxide.
8. Phenol can also be prepared by the hydrolysis of phenol ethers.
9. Phenol can be purified by distillation and recrystallization.
