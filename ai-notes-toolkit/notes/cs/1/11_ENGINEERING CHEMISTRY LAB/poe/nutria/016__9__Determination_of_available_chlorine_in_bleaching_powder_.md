
### 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

1. Bleaching powder is a white powder made from calcium chloride and sodium hypochlorite.
2. It is used as a disinfectant and bleaching agent in laundry and other household cleaning products.
3. The available chlorine in bleaching powder can be determined by titration with sodium thiosulfate solution.
4. A known amount of bleaching powder is dissolved in water and titrated with sodium thiosulfate solution.
5. The number of moles of sodium thiosulfate required to neutralize the bleaching powder is equal to the number of moles of available chlorine in the sample.
6. The concentration of available chlorine in the sample can be calculated by dividing the number of moles of available chlorine by the volume of the sample.
