

### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of Water Sample

1. Alkalinity is the measure of the capacity of water to neutralize an acid.
2. Alkalinity is an important parameter in water quality and is used to indicate the amount of dissolved minerals in the water.
3. Alkalinity is usually expressed in milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate (CaCO3).
4. The alkalinity of water can be determined using a titration method.
5. The titration method involves adding a known amount of acid to the water sample and then titrating it with a base such as sodium hydroxide (NaOH) until the sample reaches a pH of 8.3.
6. The amount of acid required to reach this pH is then used to calculate the alkalinity of the sample.
7. The alkalinity of the sample can also be determined by using a colorimetric method.
8. This method involves adding a reagent to the water sample and then measuring the absorbance of the solution at a particular wavelength.
9. The absorbance is then used to calculate the alkalinity of the sample.
