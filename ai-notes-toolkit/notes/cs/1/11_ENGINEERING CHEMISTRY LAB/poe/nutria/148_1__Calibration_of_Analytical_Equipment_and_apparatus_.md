
# 1. Calibration of Analytical Equipment and Apparatus 

* Calibration is the process of verifying that an instrument is properly measuring a physical quantity.
* It is important to ensure that the instrument is correctly calibrated in order to obtain accurate results.
* Calibration can be done in two ways: by comparison to a standard or using a calibration curve.
* When calibrating using a standard, the instrument is compared to a known reference material.
* When calibrating using a calibration curve, a series of measurements are taken at known concentrations and a graph is constructed.
* The instrument is then adjusted until the readings match the graph.
* Calibration should be done regularly to ensure accuracy of results.
* In addition, it is important to document the results of the calibration for future reference.
