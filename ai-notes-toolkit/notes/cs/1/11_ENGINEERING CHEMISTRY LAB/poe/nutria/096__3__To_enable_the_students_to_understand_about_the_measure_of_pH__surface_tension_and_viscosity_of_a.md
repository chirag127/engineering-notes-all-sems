
### 3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of a liquid:

1. **pH** is a measure of the acidity or alkalinity of a solution. It is measured on a scale of 0 to 14, with 0 being the most acidic and 14 being the most alkaline.

2. **Surface tension** is a measure of the cohesion of molecules in a liquid. It is measured in dynes per centimeter (dyn/cm).

3. **Viscosity** is a measure of the internal friction of a liquid. It is measured in centipoise (cP).
