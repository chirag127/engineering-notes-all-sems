
## Course Outcomes:
- Develop an understanding of the fundamental principles of computer science and engineering, including algorithms, data structures, operating systems, and computer architecture. 
- Learn to design, implement, and evaluate computer-based systems, processes, components, and programs.
- Develop an understanding of the basic principles of software engineering, including software design, software development, and software testing.
- Acquire the skills to use modern software engineering tools and techniques.
- Learn to apply mathematics, science, and engineering to solve problems in computer science and engineering.
- Learn to identify, analyze, and solve complex computing problems.
- Develop an understanding of the ethical and social implications of computing and its applications.
