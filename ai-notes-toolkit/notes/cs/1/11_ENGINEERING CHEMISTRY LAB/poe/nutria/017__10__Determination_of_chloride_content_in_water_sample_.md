

### 10. Determination of Chloride Content in Water Sample

1. Chloride content in water samples can be determined using a variety of methods, such as titration, gravimetric analysis, and colorimetric analysis.
2. Titration is a quantitative analytical method used to determine the concentration of a known reactant in a solution. It involves adding a known amount of a reagent to a solution until the reaction is complete.
3. Gravimetric analysis is a method of analyzing the mass of an unknown substance in a sample. This method involves weighing a sample before and after a reaction to determine the mass of the unknown substance.
4. Colorimetric analysis is a method of analyzing the concentration of a substance in a sample by measuring the intensity of light absorbed or emitted by the sample.
5. Chloride content in water samples can also be determined using ion-selective electrodes. This method involves measuring the electrical potential of the sample to determine the concentration of chloride ions.
