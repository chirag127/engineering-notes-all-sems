
### 2. Determination of Hardness of Water Sample by EDTA Method

1. Hardness of water can be determined by titration with a chelating agent such as EDTA.
2. EDTA reacts with metal ions in water to form a complex that can be titrated.
3. The endpoint of the titration is determined by an indicator such as Eriochrome Black T.
4. The amount of EDTA used in the titration is proportional to the concentration of metal ions in the sample.
5. The total hardness of a sample can be calculated by multiplying the molarity of EDTA used in the titration by a factor of 5.
6. The EDTA method for determining water hardness is accurate and reliable, but it requires a skilled operator and specialized equipment.
