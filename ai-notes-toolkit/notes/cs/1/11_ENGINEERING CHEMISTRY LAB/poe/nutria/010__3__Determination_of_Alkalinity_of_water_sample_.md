
### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample

* Alkalinity is a measure of the ability of water to neutralize acids and is expressed in terms of the equivalent concentration of calcium carbonate. 
* Alkalinity can be determined by titration with a strong acid, such as hydrochloric acid or sulfuric acid, using a pH meter or color indicator. 
* The end point of the titration is indicated by either a change in the pH of the solution or the appearance of a color indicator. 
* The alkalinity of the sample can then be calculated using the volume of acid added and the titrant concentration. 
* The alkalinity of natural water samples can be determined through the use of a carbonate-bicarbonate titration. 
* This method involves adding a known amount of acid to the sample and titrating until the pH of the sample reaches a certain level. 
* The alkalinity can then be calculated from the volume of acid added and the titrant concentration.
