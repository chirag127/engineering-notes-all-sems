
### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route.

1. Organic compounds are molecules that contain carbon and hydrogen atoms, and are found in living organisms.
2. Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is used in the production of nylon, polyurethanes and other synthetic materials.
3. Paracetamol is a widely used analgesic and antipyretic drug.
4. Conventional synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol involves the use of toxic chemicals and solvents.
5. Green synthesis of organic compounds is a less hazardous and more environmentally friendly approach that uses natural catalysts such as enzymes and microorganisms.
6. Green synthesis of adipic acid and paracetamol has been successfully demonstrated using biocatalysts such as lipases and oxidases.
7. Green synthesis of organic compounds is an important area of research, and has potential applications in the pharmaceutical and chemical industries.
