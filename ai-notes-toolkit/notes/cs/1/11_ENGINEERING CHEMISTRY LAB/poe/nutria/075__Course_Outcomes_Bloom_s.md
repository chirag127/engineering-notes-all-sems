
# Course Outcomes Bloom's

* Understand the fundamentals of Bloom's Taxonomy and the different levels of cognitive thinking
* Identify the different cognitive processes and skills that can be applied to different types of tasks
* Analyze the different levels of Bloom's Taxonomy and the associated cognitive processes
* Evaluate the effectiveness of different levels of Bloom's Taxonomy and the associated cognitive processes
* Demonstrate the ability to apply Bloom's Taxonomy and the associated cognitive processes to different types of tasks
* Create and implement a plan that effectively applies Bloom's Taxonomy and the associated cognitive processes to different types of tasks
