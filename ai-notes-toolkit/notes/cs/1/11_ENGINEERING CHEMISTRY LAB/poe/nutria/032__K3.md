
### K3
- K3 is a mathematical field that studies algebraic varieties.
- Algebraic varieties are geometric objects defined by polynomial equations.
- Examples of algebraic varieties include circles, ellipses, hyperbolas, and parabolas.
- K3 surfaces are a special class of algebraic varieties that have been extensively studied.
- K3 surfaces have many interesting properties, such as being Calabi-Yau manifolds and having a rich moduli space.
- K3 surfaces are important in string theory and mirror symmetry, and have applications in other areas of mathematics and physics.
