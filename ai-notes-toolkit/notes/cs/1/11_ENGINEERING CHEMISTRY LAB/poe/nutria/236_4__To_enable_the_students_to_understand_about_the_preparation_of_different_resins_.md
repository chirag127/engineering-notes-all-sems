

#### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins

* Resins are natural or synthetic polymers that are used for a variety of applications, such as coatings, adhesives, and composites.
* The preparation of different resins depends on the type of resin used. For example, epoxy resins are prepared by mixing an epoxide with an amine, while polyester resins are prepared by reacting an alcohol with an acid.
* The curing process is an important part of resin preparation. Curing involves heating or chemically reacting the resin to form a strong and durable material.
* Resins can also be modified with additives to improve their performance. Additives such as fillers, pigments, and catalysts can be added to resins to improve their properties.
* The properties of a resin can be further modified by manipulating the curing process. For example, adjusting the temperature and time of curing can change the properties of a resin.
