
### 16. Element detection and Identification of Functional Groups in Organic Compounds

1. Element Detection: Element detection involves the use of various chemical tests to identify the presence of specific elements in organic compounds. Common tests used for element detection include flame tests, acid-base reactions, and spectroscopy.

2. Functional Group Identification: Functional groups are specific groups of atoms that are responsible for the chemical properties of an organic compound. Common functional groups include alcohols, aldehydes, carboxylic acids, and amines. Functional group identification involves the use of various chemical tests to identify the presence of specific functional groups in organic compounds. Common tests used for functional group identification include color tests, infrared spectroscopy, and nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy.
