
# ENGINEERING CHEMISTRY LAB

1. Engineering Chemistry is the study of the chemistry of materials used in engineering applications. 
2. It involves the study of the physical, chemical and biochemical properties of materials used in engineering. 
3. The engineering chemistry lab is used to study the various properties of materials used in engineering processes. 
4. In the lab, experiments are conducted to determine the physical and chemical properties of materials such as metals, plastics, ceramics and composites. 
5. The lab also studies the interaction of materials with different environments, such as with different temperatures, pH levels, and chemical or biological agents. 
6. The lab also studies the corrosion behavior of materials and the effect of corrosion on materials. 
7. The lab also studies the behavior of materials in extreme environments, such as high temperatures, high pressures, and in the presence of radiation. 
8. The lab also studies the behavior of materials in different manufacturing processes, such as casting, welding, and machining. 
9. The lab also studies the behavior of materials in different service environments, such as in automotive, aerospace, and marine applications. 
10. The lab also studies the behavior of materials in different environmental conditions, such as in the presence of humidity, dust, and other contaminants.
