
# 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a Solution

1. Cell conductance is a measure of the ability of a solution to conduct an electrical current.
2. It is important to understand the concept of cell conductance in order to accurately measure and interpret the results of electrical experiments.
3. Cell conductance is dependent on the temperature, concentration, and type of ions present in the solution.
4. To measure the cell conductance of a solution, an electrical current is passed through the solution and the resulting voltage is measured.
5. The cell conductance is then calculated from the voltage and current values.
6. The cell conductance of a solution can be used to determine the concentration of ions present in the solution.
7. It is also used to study the behavior of ions in solutions and to calculate the electrical properties of solutions.
