2. It is expressed as the rate of change of concentration of a reactant or product with respect to time.
3. The rate constant can be determined by measuring the rate of change of a reactant or product over time.
4. The rate constant is related to the activation energy of a reaction, and is an important factor in determining the rate of reaction.
5. The rate constant can be calculated from the Arrhenius equation, which relates the rate constant to the activation energy and temperature.
6. The rate constant is also related to the reaction order, which is the number of reactants involved in the reaction.
7. The rate constant can be used to calculate the rate of reaction, which is the rate at which reactants are converted to products.
8. The rate constant can also be used to calculate the equilibrium constant, which is the ratio of the rate of the forward reaction to the rate of the reverse reaction.
