
### 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid

1. Surface tension is a property of liquids that results from the cohesive forces between molecules. 
2. Surface tension is responsible for the shape of liquid droplets, as well as for the ability of some insects (e.g. water striders) to walk on the surface of a body of water. 
3. The surface tension of a given liquid can be determined using a variety of methods, including the use of a tensiometer, an instrument that measures the force required to draw a liquid into a capillary tube. 
4. The force required to draw the liquid into the tube is a measure of the surface tension. 
5. Other methods of determining surface tension include the use of a ring, a drop weight, and a Wilhelmy plate. 
6. In each of these methods, the force required to draw the liquid into a given shape is a measure of the surface tension.
