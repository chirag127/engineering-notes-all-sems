
### 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) Resin

1. Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin is a thermosetting synthetic polymer produced from the reaction of phenol and formaldehyde.
2. The reaction of phenol and formaldehyde is known as a condensation reaction, which occurs when two molecules combine to form a larger molecule, with the release of a small molecule such as water or methanol.
3. The reaction is typically carried out in the presence of an acid catalyst, such as sulfuric acid, to speed up the reaction.
4. The reaction is exothermic, meaning that it produces heat.
5. The resulting resin is a solid, insoluble material with a high molecular weight.
6. The properties of the resin depend on the ratio of phenol to formaldehyde used in the reaction, as well as the type of acid catalyst and the reaction conditions.
7. The resin is often used as an adhesive, coating or molding material in a variety of industrial applications.
