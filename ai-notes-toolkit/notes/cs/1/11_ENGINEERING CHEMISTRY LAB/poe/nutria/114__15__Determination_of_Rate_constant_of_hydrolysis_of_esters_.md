
### 15. Determination of Rate constant of Hydrolysis of Esters

1. Hydrolysis of esters is a chemical reaction in which an ester molecule is broken down into a carboxylic acid and an alcohol by the addition of a molecule of water.
2. The rate constant of hydrolysis of esters is a measure of the speed of the reaction and can be determined by measuring the change in concentration of the reacting species over time.
3. The rate constant of hydrolysis of esters can be determined experimentally by monitoring the disappearance of the ester and the appearance of the carboxylic acid and alcohol product.
4. The rate constant can also be determined using a mathematical model that takes into account the reactants, products, and rate constants of the reaction.
5. The rate constant of hydrolysis of esters can be used to predict the rate of reaction and the amount of product that will be produced.
