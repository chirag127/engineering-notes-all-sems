
### Formaldehyde and Urea Formaldehyde Resin
- Formaldehyde is a colorless, flammable gas with a strong odor. It is used in the manufacture of plastics, resins, and other products. Urea formaldehyde resin is a thermosetting plastic made from urea and formaldehyde. It is used in the production of adhesives, coatings, and other products.

### Adipic Acid
- Adipic acid is an organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is an odourless, white, crystalline solid that is soluble in water. Adipic acid is used in the production of nylon, polyurethane, and other products.

### Paracetamol
- Paracetamol is an analgesic drug used to relieve pain and reduce fever. It is commonly used to treat headaches, muscle aches, and other minor aches and pains. Paracetamol is available in the form of tablets, capsules, and syrups.
