
### 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) Resin
1. Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin is a thermosetting resin that is used in various applications such as adhesives, coatings, and laminates.
2. The resin is prepared by the reaction of phenol and formaldehyde in the presence of an acid or base catalyst.
3. The reaction is carried out in an aqueous solution at a temperature of 70-80°C.
4. The reaction is exothermic and the temperature must be controlled to prevent the reaction from getting out of control.
5. The reaction is complete when the mixture reaches a pH of 7.5.
6. The resin is then precipitated and washed with water to remove any unreacted reactants.
7. The resin is then dried and ground into a powder for use in various applications.
