
## K3

1. K3 is an abbreviation for the 3rd generation of the [Kelvin scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelvin), a temperature scale used in science and engineering.
2. The Kelvin scale is based on the concept of absolute zero, the temperature at which all thermal motion ceases.
3. The Kelvin scale is an absolute temperature scale, meaning that temperatures on the Kelvin scale are independent of any external reference point.
4. The Kelvin temperature scale starts from absolute zero, which is defined as 0 K.
5. One degree Kelvin (1 K) is equal to one degree Celsius (1 °C).
6. The boiling point of water is 373.15 K (100 °C).
7. The freezing point of water is 273.15 K (0 °C).
8. The triple point of water, the temperature and pressure at which solid, liquid, and vapor phases of water can exist in equilibrium, is 273.16 K (0.01 °C).
