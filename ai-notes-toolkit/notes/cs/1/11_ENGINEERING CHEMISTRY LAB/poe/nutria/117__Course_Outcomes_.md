
## Course Outcomes:
- Students will be able to explain the fundamentals of computer science and its major branches. 
- Students will be able to understand the major components of a computer system and how they interact. 
- Students will be able to analyze algorithms and evaluate their complexity. 
- Students will be able to design and implement programs using appropriate programming languages. 
- Students will be able to understand the principles of software engineering and apply them to the design and development of software. 
- Students will be able to apply the principles of database design and management to create and manipulate databases. 
- Students will be able to apply the principles of networking and distributed computing to the design and implementation of computer systems. 
- Students will be able to understand the principles of artificial intelligence and apply them to the development of intelligent systems.
