
# 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

1. Analytical instruments are used to measure physical and chemical properties of samples.
2. These instruments are used in a variety of fields, such as chemistry, biology, engineering, and medicine.
3. Common analytical instruments include spectrometers, chromatographs, calorimeters, and mass spectrometers.
4. Analytical instruments are important tools for research, development, and quality control.
5. Analytical instruments measure properties such as composition, structure, and concentration.
6. The accuracy and precision of analytical instruments depend on the type of instrument and the sample being analyzed.
7. Proper operation and maintenance of analytical instruments is essential for accurate results.
