

# 10. Determination of Chloride Content in Water Sample

1. Chloride is an anion found in many water sources, including natural surface and groundwater, and is commonly used to measure water quality.

2. The most common method for determining chloride content in water samples is the titration method. This method involves adding a known amount of a base, such as sodium hydroxide, to the sample and then titrating it with a standard solution of hydrochloric acid.

3. The amount of hydrochloric acid needed to titrate the sample to a neutral pH indicates the concentration of chloride ions in the sample.

4. The chloride content of water can be used to measure its quality, as high levels of chloride can indicate the presence of pollutants such as sewage or industrial waste.

5. It is important to note that the accuracy of the titration method is affected by the presence of other ions in the sample, such as sodium and calcium, as these can interfere with the titration process.
