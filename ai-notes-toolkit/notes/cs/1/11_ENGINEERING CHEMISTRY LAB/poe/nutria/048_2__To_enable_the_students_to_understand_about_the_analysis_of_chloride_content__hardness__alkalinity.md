
## To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity

1. Chloride content can be determined by titration with silver nitrate solution. The end point of the titration is determined by the presence of a silver chloride precipitate.
2. Hardness of water can be determined by titration with EDTA. The end point of the titration is indicated by the presence of a pink-violet color.
3. Alkalinity of water can be determined by titration with hydrochloric acid. The end point of the titration is indicated by the presence of a yellow color.
