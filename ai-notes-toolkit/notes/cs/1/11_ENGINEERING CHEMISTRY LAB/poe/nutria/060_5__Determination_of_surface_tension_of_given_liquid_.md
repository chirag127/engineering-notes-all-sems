

### 5. Determination of Surface Tension of a Given Liquid

1. Surface tension is a property of liquids that causes them to resist an external force, acting to draw the liquid's surface into a concave shape.

2. Surface tension can be measured by a variety of methods, including the use of a surface tensiometer, a device that measures the force required to pull a needle through a given liquid.

3. To measure the surface tension of a given liquid, the needle of the surface tensiometer is pulled through the liquid at a constant speed.

4. The force required to pull the needle through the liquid is then measured and used to calculate the surface tension of the liquid.

5. The surface tension of a given liquid can also be determined by measuring the maximum height of a liquid column that can be supported by the liquid's surface tension. This is known as the capillary rise method.

6. Knowing the surface tension of a given liquid can be useful for a variety of applications, such as the design of medical devices and the study of the behavior of liquids in various environments.
