
### 7. Determination of the Strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate Using External Indicator

1. Ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) is a chemical compound used in a variety of applications, including as an oxidizing agent, a reagent for organic synthesis, and a source of iron in nutrition.

2. FAS can be determined by titration with an external indicator. This method is based on a reaction between FAS and the indicator, which produces a color change that can be used to determine the concentration of FAS.

3. An appropriate indicator for FAS titration must be chosen. Commonly used indicators for FAS titration include methyl orange, phenolphthalein, and bromocresol green.

4. The endpoint of the titration is determined by the color change of the indicator. The endpoint is the point at which the indicator changes color and indicates the completion of the reaction.

5. The strength of the FAS solution can then be determined by calculating the molarity of the FAS solution. This is done by dividing the amount of FAS used in the titration by the volume of the solution.
