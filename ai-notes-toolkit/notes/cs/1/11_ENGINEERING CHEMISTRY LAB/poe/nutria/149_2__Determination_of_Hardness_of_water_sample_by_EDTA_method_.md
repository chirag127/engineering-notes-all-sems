
## 2. Determination of Hardness of Water Sample by EDTA Method

1. Hardness of water can be determined by titration with EDTA (Ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid).
2. EDTA forms a complex with metal ions in the water sample, making them soluble and thus, reducing the hardness of the sample.
3. The titration of EDTA with the water sample is done using a standardized solution of EDTA, which is usually 0.01 M.
4. The titration is performed until the endpoint is reached, which is usually indicated by a color change.
5. The amount of EDTA used in the titration is a measure of the hardness of the water sample.
6. The hardness of the water sample can be calculated using the following formula:

Hardness (in ppm) = (volume of EDTA used (in mL)) x (concentration of EDTA (in M)) x (equivalent weight of calcium carbonate (in g/mol)) x (1000)
