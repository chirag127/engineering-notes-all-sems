
### 3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of a liquid

* pH is a measure of the acidity or alkalinity of a liquid, measured on a scale from 0 to 14. A neutral pH is 7, and a liquid with a pH below 7 is considered acidic, while one with a pH above 7 is considered basic or alkaline.
* Surface tension is a measure of the cohesive forces between liquid molecules that cause a liquid to act as if it has an elastic surface. It is measured in units of force per unit length. 
* Viscosity is a measure of a liquid's resistance to flow. It is measured in units of force per unit area. Higher viscosity liquids are more resistant to flow than lower viscosity liquids.
