
# Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

* Formaldehyde is a colorless and flammable gas at room temperature. It is used in the production of many industrial and consumer products, such as building materials, adhesives, and plastics.
* Urea formaldehyde resin is a thermosetting plastic made from urea and formaldehyde. It is used in the production of molded products, such as furniture and kitchenware, and insulation materials.
* Adipic acid is a white, crystalline organic compound used in the production of nylon and polyurethanes. It is also used as a food preservative and flavoring agent. 
* Paracetamol is an analgesic and antipyretic drug used to treat pain and fever. It is available in both oral and intravenous forms.
