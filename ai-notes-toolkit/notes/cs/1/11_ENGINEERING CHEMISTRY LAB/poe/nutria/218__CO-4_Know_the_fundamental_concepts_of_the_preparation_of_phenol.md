
### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol

1. Phenol is an organic compound that is commonly used in the manufacture of plastics, pharmaceuticals, and other products.
2. The most common method of preparing phenol is by the hydrolysis of cumene.
3. Cumene is an alkylbenzene that is produced from the catalytic reforming of petroleum.
4. The hydrolysis of cumene is conducted in an acidic medium, such as sulfuric acid, and results in the formation of phenol and acetone.
5. The reaction is highly exothermic and must be carefully controlled to prevent a runaway reaction.
6. The yield of phenol is usually around 80%, with the remainder being acetone and other byproducts.
7. The purity of the phenol can be increased by recrystallisation, distillation, and other purification processes.
