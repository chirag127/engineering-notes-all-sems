
# CO-5 Estimate the rate constant of reaction. K3

1. The rate constant of a reaction, K3, is a measure of the speed of a chemical reaction.
2. It is a measure of how quickly a reaction takes place and is related to the activation energy of the reaction.
3. The rate constant is usually expressed as a reciprocal of time, such as seconds, minutes, or hours.
4. The rate constant can be calculated by measuring the change in the concentration of a reactant or product over time.
5. The rate constant can also be calculated by measuring the rate of a reaction at different temperatures and using the Arrhenius equation.
6. The rate constant can be used to predict the rate of a reaction at different temperatures.
7. The rate constant can also be used to compare the rates of different reactions.
