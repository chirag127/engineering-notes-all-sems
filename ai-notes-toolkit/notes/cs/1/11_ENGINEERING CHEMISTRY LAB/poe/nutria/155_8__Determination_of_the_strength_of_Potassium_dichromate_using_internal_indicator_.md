
# 8. Determination of the Strength of Potassium Dichromate Using Internal Indicator

1. Potassium dichromate is an inorganic compound with the formula K2Cr2O7.
2. It is a strong oxidizing agent, used in a variety of applications such as in the manufacture of dyes, leather tanning, and in analytical chemistry.
3. The strength of Potassium dichromate can be determined by using an internal indicator.
4. The internal indicator used for this purpose is a solution of methyl orange in 0.1 M HCl.
5. The sample of Potassium dichromate is titrated with the internal indicator solution until the end point is reached.
6. The end point is determined by the change in colour of the indicator solution from orange to yellow.
7. The strength of the Potassium dichromate solution is calculated using the following equation:

Strength of Potassium dichromate (M) = (Volume of internal indicator used (mL)) / (Volume of Potassium dichromate sample (mL))
