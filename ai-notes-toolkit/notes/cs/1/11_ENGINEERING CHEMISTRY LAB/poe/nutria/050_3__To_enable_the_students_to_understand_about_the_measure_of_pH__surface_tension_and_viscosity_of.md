
### To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of

* pH is a measure of the acidity or alkalinity of a solution, usually measured on a scale from 0 to 14. A pH of 7 is considered neutral, a pH below 7 is acidic, and a pH above 7 is alkaline.
* Surface tension is the measure of the cohesive forces between molecules of a liquid that cause it to act as if its surface were a stretched elastic membrane.
* Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is determined by the amount of internal friction between the molecules of a fluid and is affected by temperature, pressure and the type of fluid.
