
# Topic: Solar System

1. The Solar System is made up of the Sun, eight planets and their moons, dwarf planets and other small bodies such as asteroids and comets. 
2. The Sun is the center of the Solar System, and all the planets and other bodies orbit around it.
3. The planets in the Solar System are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
4. Each planet has its own unique characteristics, such as size, composition, atmosphere and temperature.
5. The moons of the planets are also varied and interesting, with some having atmospheres and others being covered in ice and dust.
6. Dwarf planets, like Pluto, are smaller than planets but larger than asteroids and comets.
7. Asteroids are small rocky bodies that orbit the Sun, while comets are icy bodies that have a tail of dust and gas when they approach the Sun.
8. The Solar System is part of a larger system called the Milky Way galaxy, which contains billions of stars and planets.
