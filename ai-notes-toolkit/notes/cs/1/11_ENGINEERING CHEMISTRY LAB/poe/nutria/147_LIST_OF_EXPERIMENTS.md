

# List of Experiments

1. The Mpemba effect: This is an experiment which investigates the phenomenon where hot water can freeze faster than cold water.

2. The Bernoulli Principle: This experiment examines how an increase in the speed of a fluid results in a decrease in pressure.

3. The Law of Conservation of Mass: This experiment seeks to demonstrate how mass is conserved in a chemical reaction.

4. Boyle's Law: This experiment investigates the relationship between the pressure and volume of a gas.

5. The Ideal Gas Law: This experiment seeks to demonstrate the relationship between pressure, volume, and temperature of a gas.

6. The Doppler Effect: This experiment investigates how the frequency of a sound wave changes when the source is moving.

7. Archimedes Principle: This experiment examines the relationship between the buoyant force and the weight of an object in a fluid.
