
### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

1. Adipic acid is an organic compound that is used in the production of nylon. It is a white, crystalline solid with a slightly acidic taste.

2. Paracetamol is an analgesic and antipyretic drug used to treat mild to moderate pain and fever.

3. Adipic acid and paracetamol can be prepared from benzene, an aromatic hydrocarbon. 

4. The first step in the preparation of adipic acid is the oxidation of benzene to benzoic acid. This is done by treating benzene with oxygen in the presence of a catalyst such as manganese dioxide.

5. The benzoic acid is then heated with concentrated nitric acid, forming adipic acid and water.

6. Paracetamol can be prepared by treating adipic acid with acetic anhydride and sodium acetate. This reaction produces paracetamol, acetic acid, and sodium adipate. 

7. The products are then separated and purified. The paracetamol is then recrystallized to obtain a pure product.
