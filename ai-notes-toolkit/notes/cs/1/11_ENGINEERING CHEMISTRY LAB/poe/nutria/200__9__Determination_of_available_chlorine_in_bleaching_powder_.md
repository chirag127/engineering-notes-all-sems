
### 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

1. Bleaching powder is a compound containing chlorine, which is used as a bleaching agent in the textile industry.
2. The available chlorine in bleaching powder can be determined by titration with standard sodium thiosulfate solution.
3. The reaction between the bleaching powder and sodium thiosulfate solution is as follows:
Cl2 + 2Na2S2O3 → 2NaCl + Na2S4O6
4. The end point of the titration is determined by adding a few drops of starch indicator solution to the titrated solution.
5. The end point is marked by the appearance of a blue colour in the titrated solution.
6. The available chlorine in the bleaching powder is calculated from the volume of sodium thiosulfate solution consumed.
