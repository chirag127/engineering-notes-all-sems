
# Course Outcomes:
- Develop an understanding of the foundations of computer science and the principles underlying the design of computer systems.
- Demonstrate the ability to design, implement, and analyze algorithms and data structures.
- Develop an understanding of the principles of operating systems, including process management, memory management, and input/output.
- Demonstrate the ability to program in a high-level language, such as Java, C++, or Python.
- Develop an understanding of the principles of computer networks, including the fundamentals of communication, routing, and switching.
- Demonstrate the ability to design and implement software systems.
- Develop an understanding of the principles of databases, including the fundamentals of data storage and retrieval.
- Demonstrate the ability to design and implement user interfaces.
- Develop an understanding of the principles of artificial intelligence, including the fundamentals of machine learning and natural language processing.
