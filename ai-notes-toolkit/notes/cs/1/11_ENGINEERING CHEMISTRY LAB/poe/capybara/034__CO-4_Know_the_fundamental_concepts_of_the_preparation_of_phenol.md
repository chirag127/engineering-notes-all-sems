### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol.

Phenol is a highly important compound used in various industries for the production of resins, plastics, pharmaceuticals, and other chemicals. It is obtained from various sources using different methods. In this section, we will discuss the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol.

1. From Benzene:
Phenol is obtained from benzene by the cumene process. In this process, benzene is reacted with propylene to form cumene in the presence of an acid catalyst. The cumene is oxidized to form cumene hydroperoxide, which is then cleaved to form phenol and acetone.

2. From Toluene:
Phenol can also be obtained from toluene by the Raschig-Hooker process. In this process, toluene is oxidized to form benzyl chloride, which is then hydrolyzed to form benzyl alcohol. The benzyl alcohol is then oxidized to form benzaldehyde, which is further oxidized to form benzoic acid. The benzoic acid is then decarboxylated to form phenol.

3. From Coal Tar:
Phenol can also be obtained from coal tar. Coal tar is subjected to fractional distillation to obtain various fractions. The fraction containing phenol is then subjected to further purification processes to obtain pure phenol.

4. From Chlorobenzene:
Phenol can also be obtained from chlorobenzene by the Dow process. In this process, chlorobenzene is reacted with sodium hydroxide to form sodium phenoxide, which is then acidified to form phenol.

In conclusion, phenol can be obtained from various sources using different methods. The choice of method depends on the availability and cost of the starting material, as well as the desired purity of the final product.
