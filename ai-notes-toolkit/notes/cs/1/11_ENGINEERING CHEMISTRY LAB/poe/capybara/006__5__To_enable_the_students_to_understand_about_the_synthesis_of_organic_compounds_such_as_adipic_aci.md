### 5. Synthesis of Organic Compounds by Conventional and Green Route

Organic compounds are molecules that contain carbon atoms bonded to hydrogen atoms, along with other elements such as oxygen, nitrogen, sulfur, and halogens. These compounds are essential for life, as they are the building blocks of many biomolecules, including proteins, carbohydrates, and nucleic acids.

The synthesis of organic compounds can be done by conventional and green routes. The conventional method involves the use of hazardous chemicals and solvents, which are harmful to the environment and human health. On the other hand, the green route involves the use of renewable resources and environmentally friendly solvents, which are safer and more sustainable.

Here are some points to help you understand the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route:

#### Synthesis of Adipic Acid

Conventional Route:
- Adipic acid is traditionally synthesized by the oxidation of cyclohexane with nitric acid, which produces nitrous oxide as a byproduct.
- This process is energy-intensive, and the nitrous oxide produced is a potent greenhouse gas that contributes to climate change.
- The high demand for adipic acid has led to the development of alternative routes, such as the use of benzene and naphthalene as starting materials.

Green Route:
- Adipic acid can be synthesized by the oxidation of cyclohexene with hydrogen peroxide, which produces water as a byproduct.
- This process is more energy-efficient and produces less waste compared to the conventional route.
- The use of renewable resources such as biomass-derived cyclohexene can further enhance the sustainability of this process.

#### Synthesis of Paracetamol

Conventional Route:
- Paracetamol is synthesized by the nitration of phenol with nitric acid, followed by reduction with hydrogen gas.
- This process is hazardous due to the use of nitric acid, which is highly corrosive and toxic.
- The reduction step also requires high pressure and temperature conditions, which can be dangerous.

Green Route:
- Paracetamol can be synthesized by the direct acylation of phenol with acetic anhydride, which produces acetic acid as a byproduct.
- This process is more environmentally friendly and safer compared to the conventional route.
- The use of renewable resources such as lignin-derived phenol can further enhance the sustainability of this process.

In conclusion, the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol can be done by conventional and green routes. The green route offers a more sustainable and safer alternative to the traditional methods, which rely on hazardous chemicals and solvents. By understanding the principles behind these processes, students can contribute to the development of more sustainable and environmentally friendly methods for the synthesis of organic compounds.
