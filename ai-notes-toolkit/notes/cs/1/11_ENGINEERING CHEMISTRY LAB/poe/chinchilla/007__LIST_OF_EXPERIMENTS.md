## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

Here is a list of experiments that are commonly conducted in science and engineering:

1. Acid-Base Titration: This experiment is used to determine the concentration of an acid or a base solution by titrating it against a standardized solution of the opposite type.

2. Spectrophotometry: This experiment is used to measure the absorbance or transmission of light by a sample in the ultraviolet, visible or infrared regions of the electromagnetic spectrum.

3. Chromatography: This experiment is used to separate and analyze the components of a mixture based on their differences in chemical properties such as solubility, polarity, or size.

4. Electrolysis: This experiment is used to split a compound into its constituent elements using an electric current.

5. Enzyme Kinetics: This experiment is used to study the rate of enzyme-catalyzed reactions by measuring the change in concentration of the substrate or the product over time.

6. DNA Extraction: This experiment is used to isolate DNA from cells or tissues for further analysis or manipulation.

7. Microscopy: This experiment is used to observe and analyze objects that are too small to be seen by the naked eye, such as cells, microorganisms, or particles.

8. Newton's Laws: This experiment is used to study the fundamental principles of motion and force described by Isaac Newton's laws.

9. Boyle's Law: This experiment is used to study the relationship between the pressure and volume of a gas at a constant temperature, as described by Boyle's law.

10. Ohm's Law: This experiment is used to study the relationship between the voltage, current, and resistance of an electrical circuit, as described by Ohm's law.

These experiments are fundamental to many fields of science and engineering and can provide valuable insights into the workings of the world around us.
