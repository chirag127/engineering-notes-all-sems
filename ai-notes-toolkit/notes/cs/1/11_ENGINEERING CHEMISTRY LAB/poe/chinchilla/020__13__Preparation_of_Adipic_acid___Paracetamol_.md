### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

Adipic acid and Paracetamol are two important chemicals used in various industries. Here are the methods for their preparation:

#### Preparation of Adipic acid:

1. Oxidation of Cyclohexane: Cyclohexane is oxidized using nitric acid and air to produce adipic acid. The reaction is exothermic and produces a large amount of heat. The yield of adipic acid is around 60-70%.

2. Oxidation of Cyclohexene: Cyclohexene is oxidized using potassium permanganate and sulfuric acid to produce adipic acid. The yield is around 90%.

3. Oxidation of Cyclohexanol: Cyclohexanol is oxidized using sodium hypochlorite or potassium dichromate to produce adipic acid. The yield is around 70-80%.

#### Preparation of Paracetamol:

1. Nitration of Phenol: Phenol is nitrated using nitric acid and sulfuric acid to produce 4-nitrophenol. The yield is around 90%. 

2. Reduction of 4-nitrophenol: 4-nitrophenol is reduced using sodium borohydride to produce 4-aminophenol. The yield is around 80-90%.

3. Acetylation of 4-aminophenol: 4-aminophenol is acetylated using acetic anhydride to produce Paracetamol. The yield is around 80-90%.
