# Preparation of Adipic Acid / Paracetamol

Adipic acid and paracetamol are important organic compounds that are widely used in various industries. Adipic acid is commonly used in the production of nylon, while paracetamol is a widely used pain reliever. In this article, we will discuss the preparation of adipic acid and paracetamol.

## Preparation of Adipic Acid

Adipic acid can be prepared by the following process:

1. Oxidation of cyclohexene with nitric acid to produce adipic acid.
2. The reaction is carried out in the presence of a catalyst, usually vanadium pentoxide or a mixture of nitric acid and sulfuric acid.
3. The reaction mixture is then washed with water to remove impurities and dried to obtain adipic acid.

C6H10 + 2 HNO3 → HOOC(CH2)4COOH + 2 NO2 + H2O

2. Reduction of 4-nitrophenol with sodium borohydride to produce 4-aminophenol.
3. Acetylation of 4-aminophenol with acetic anhydride to produce paracetamol.
