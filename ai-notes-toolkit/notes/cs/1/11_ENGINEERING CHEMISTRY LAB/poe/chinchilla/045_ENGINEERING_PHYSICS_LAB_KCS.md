# ENGINEERING PHYSICS LAB KCS

## Introduction

The Engineering Physics Lab is intended to provide hands-on experience to the students in various concepts of physics and engineering. The lab is equipped with a variety of instruments and devices to carry out experiments in different areas of physics such as mechanics, optics, electricity, and magnetism.

## Lab Safety

Before starting any experiment, it is important to follow the lab safety guidelines to ensure that the experiment is carried out safely. The following points should be kept in mind:

- Wear appropriate lab attire, including lab coats, safety goggles, and closed-toe shoes.
- Ensure that all equipment is clean and in good working condition before use.
- Follow the instructions of the lab instructor carefully and ask questions if anything is unclear.
- Do not touch any live electrical equipment without proper training and supervision.
- Handle chemicals with care and dispose of them properly according to the lab guidelines.

## Experiments

The following are some of the experiments that can be carried out in the Engineering Physics Lab:

### Mechanics

- Measurement of the coefficient of static friction between two surfaces.
- Measurement of the coefficient of kinetic friction between two surfaces.
- Study of simple pendulum and determination of its period.
- Measurement of the acceleration due to gravity using a simple pendulum or a free-fall experiment.
- Study of the behavior of springs and determination of the spring constant.

### Optics

- Measurement of the focal length of a convex lens.
- Measurement of the refractive index of a glass prism.
- Study of interference and diffraction of light.
- Study of polarization of light.

### Electricity and Magnetism

- Measurement of the resistance of a wire using the Wheatstone bridge method.
- Measurement of the internal resistance of a cell using a potentiometer.
- Study of the behavior of capacitors and determination of their capacitance.
- Study of the behavior of inductors and determination of their inductance.
- Measurement of the magnetic field strength using a Hall probe.

## Conclusion

The Engineering Physics Lab is an essential component of the engineering curriculum, providing hands-on experience to the students in various areas of physics and engineering. By following the lab safety guidelines and carrying out the experiments carefully, the students can gain a deeper understanding of the concepts and principles of physics.
