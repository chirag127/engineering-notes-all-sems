### 12. Preparation of Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin.

Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin is a widely used thermosetting adhesive in the wood industry, as well as in the manufacturing of electrical appliances, laminates, and textiles. It is a cost-effective adhesive with excellent adhesive properties and water resistance. Here are the steps for preparing UF resin:

1. Materials required: Urea, formaldehyde, catalyst (such as ammonium chloride or sodium hydroxide), water, and a mixing vessel.

2. Measure the required amount of urea and formaldehyde according to the desired ratio. The most common ratio is 1:1.2 (urea: formaldehyde).

3. Add the measured urea and formaldehyde to the mixing vessel.

4. Add the catalyst to the mixture. The amount of catalyst required depends on the type and concentration of the catalyst used. The most commonly used catalysts are ammonium chloride and sodium hydroxide.

5. Mix the contents thoroughly using a mechanical stirrer. The mixture will become cloudy initially, but it will clear up as the reaction proceeds.

6. Heat the mixture using a heating mantle or a water bath. The reaction is exothermic, and the temperature will increase as the reaction proceeds.

7. Maintain the temperature within the range of 60-80°C, depending on the desired properties of the UF resin. A lower temperature will result in a resin with better water resistance, while a higher temperature will result in a resin with better heat resistance.

8. Continue heating the mixture until the desired viscosity is achieved. The viscosity of the UF resin can be controlled by adjusting the amount of water added to the mixture.

9. Once the desired viscosity is achieved, cool the mixture to room temperature.

10. The UF resin is now ready for use. It can be stored for several weeks in a cool and dry place.

In conclusion, the preparation of UF resin involves mixing urea, formaldehyde, and a catalyst, heating the mixture to a desired temperature, and controlling the viscosity by adding water. The resulting resin is a cost-effective adhesive with excellent adhesive properties and water resistance, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.
