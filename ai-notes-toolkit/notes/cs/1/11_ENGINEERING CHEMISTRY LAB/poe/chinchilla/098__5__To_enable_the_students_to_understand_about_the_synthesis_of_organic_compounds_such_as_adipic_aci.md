### 5. Synthesis of Organic Compounds: Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

In this section, we will discuss the synthesis of two important organic compounds: adipic acid and paracetamol. We will explore both conventional and green routes for the synthesis of these compounds.

#### Adipic Acid

Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is widely used in the production of nylon. It is also used as a food additive and in the production of various other chemicals. Here are the synthesis methods for adipic acid:

##### Conventional Route

1. Oxidation of cyclohexane: In this method, cyclohexane is oxidized using nitric acid and air to produce a mixture of adipic acid and other by-products. The mixture is then separated and purified to obtain pure adipic acid.

2. Oxidation of cyclohexene: In this method, cyclohexene is oxidized using potassium permanganate to produce a mixture of adipic acid and other by-products. The mixture is then separated and purified to obtain pure adipic acid.

##### Green Route

1. Bio-based production: In this method, adipic acid is produced from renewable resources such as glucose or other plant-based materials. The process involves the fermentation of the materials to produce the required intermediate compounds, which are then converted into adipic acid.

2. Electrochemical reduction: In this method, adipic acid is produced by the electrochemical reduction of carbon dioxide. The process involves the use of renewable energy sources and has low carbon footprint.

#### Paracetamol

Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a widely used pain reliever and fever reducer. Here are the synthesis methods for paracetamol:

##### Conventional Route

1. Nitration of phenol: In this method, phenol is nitrated using nitric acid and sulfuric acid to produce 4-nitrophenol. The 4-nitrophenol is then reduced to 4-aminophenol using sodium borohydride. The 4-aminophenol is then acetylated to produce paracetamol.

##### Green Route

1. Direct acylation of phenol: In this method, phenol is directly acylated using acetic anhydride in the presence of a catalyst to produce paracetamol. The process has lower environmental impact and produces less waste.

2. Biocatalytic synthesis: In this method, paracetamol is synthesized using enzymes as catalysts. The process is environmentally friendly and produces high yields of pure paracetamol.

In conclusion, the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol can be achieved using both conventional and green routes. While the conventional routes have been widely used, the green routes are gaining popularity due to their lower environmental impact and use of renewable resources.
