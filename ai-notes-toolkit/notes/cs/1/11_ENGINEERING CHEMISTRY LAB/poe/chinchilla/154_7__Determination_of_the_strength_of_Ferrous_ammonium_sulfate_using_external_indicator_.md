### Determination of the Strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate Using External Indicator

In analytical chemistry, the determination of the strength of a chemical compound is an important aspect of quality control. In this experiment, we will determine the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate, which is a common reagent used in analytical chemistry.

#### Materials Required
- Ferrous ammonium sulfate
- Potassium permanganate
- Sulfuric acid
- Distilled water
- Burette
- Pipette
- Conical flask
- White tile
- External indicator (sodium oxalate)

#### Procedure
1. Weigh accurately 1.6g of ferrous ammonium sulfate into a conical flask.
2. Add 25mL of concentrated sulfuric acid and swirl the flask gently to dissolve the ferrous ammonium sulfate.
3. Dilute the solution to 250mL with distilled water and mix well.
4. Pipette 25mL of the diluted solution into a conical flask.
5. Add 25mL of distilled water to the same flask.
6. Add a small amount of external indicator (sodium oxalate) to the flask.
7. Titrate the solution with potassium permanganate solution until a faint pink color persists for 30 seconds.
8. Record the volume of potassium permanganate solution used.

#### Calculations
The strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate can be calculated using the following formula:

Strength (in g/L) = (Volume of potassium permanganate x Molarity of potassium permanganate x 0.0056) / Volume of ferrous ammonium sulfate used in mL

Where:
- Volume of potassium permanganate is the volume of potassium permanganate solution used in mL.
- Molarity of potassium permanganate is the molarity of the potassium permanganate solution.
- Volume of ferrous ammonium sulfate used in mL is the volume of ferrous ammonium sulfate solution used in the titration.

#### Precautions
- Use accurate measuring instruments to ensure the precision of the experiment.
- Handle the chemicals with care and follow the safety guidelines.
- Rinse the burette and pipette with distilled water before use.
- Use a white tile to observe the color change during titration.
- Titrate the solution slowly to avoid overshooting the end point.

#### Conclusion
In conclusion, the determination of the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate using an external indicator is a simple and reliable method. By accurately measuring the volume of potassium permanganate solution used, we can calculate the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate. This experiment is useful in ensuring the quality control of ferrous ammonium sulfate in analytical chemistry.
