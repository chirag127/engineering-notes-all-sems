### 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a Solution

In the field of chemistry, the conductance of a solution is an important property that helps to understand the behavior of ions in a solution. The conductance of a solution can be measured using a device called a conductivity cell. Here are some important points to keep in mind when determining the cell conductance of a solution:

1. A conductivity cell is a device that measures the ability of a solution to conduct electricity. The cell consists of two electrodes, which are usually made of platinum, and a solution that is placed between the electrodes.

2. The conductivity of a solution is directly proportional to the concentration of ions present in the solution. This means that a solution with a high concentration of ions will have a higher conductivity than a solution with a low concentration of ions.

3. The cell conductance is measured in siemens per meter (S/m) or millisiemens per centimeter (mS/cm). The unit of measure used depends on the type of conductivity cell being used.

4. To determine the cell conductance of a solution, the solution is placed in the conductivity cell, and a current is passed through the electrodes. The voltage drop across the electrodes is measured, and the conductivity of the solution is calculated using Ohm's law.

5. Ohm's law states that the conductance of a solution is equal to the current passing through the electrodes divided by the voltage drop across the electrodes. This formula can be used to calculate the cell conductance of a solution.

6. The temperature of the solution can also affect its conductivity. Therefore, it is important to measure the temperature of the solution when determining its conductivity.

7. The cell constant is a factor that is used to convert the conductivity of a solution to its actual conductivity. The cell constant depends on the geometry of the conductivity cell being used and needs to be calibrated before use.

8. When determining the cell conductance of a solution, it is important to use a clean conductivity cell and to rinse the electrodes with distilled water before use. Any impurities or contaminants on the electrodes can affect the accuracy of the measurement.

In conclusion, the determination of cell conductance of a solution is an important technique in the field of chemistry. It helps to understand the behavior of ions in a solution and can be used to measure the concentration of ions present in a solution. By following the guidelines mentioned above, accurate measurements of cell conductance can be obtained.
