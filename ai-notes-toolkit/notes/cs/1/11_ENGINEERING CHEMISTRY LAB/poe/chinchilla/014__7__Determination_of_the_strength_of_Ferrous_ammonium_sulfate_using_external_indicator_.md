### 7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

Ferrous ammonium sulfate is a chemical compound commonly used in analytical chemistry as a reducing agent, especially in the determination of the concentration of potassium permanganate. The strength of this compound can be determined by using an external indicator, which is a substance that changes color in response to the presence of a certain chemical. Here are the steps for determining the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using an external indicator:

1. Prepare a standard solution of potassium permanganate of known concentration.
2. Weigh accurately about 0.2 g of Ferrous ammonium sulfate and dissolve it in distilled water in a 250 ml volumetric flask.
3. Add a few drops of dilute sulfuric acid to the solution to prevent the oxidation of the Ferrous ion.
4. Add a few crystals of the external indicator, which is generally potassium ferricyanide. The indicator will react with the Ferrous ion in the solution and form a blue-colored complex.
5. Titrate the solution with the standard solution of potassium permanganate until the blue color disappears. This indicates that all of the Ferrous ion has been oxidized to Ferric ion by the potassium permanganate.
6. Record the volume of the standard solution required for titration.
7. Repeat the above steps two more times and calculate the average volume of the standard solution required for titration.
8. From the volume of the standard solution required for titration, calculate the strength of the Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution in terms of grams per liter.

It is important to note that the external indicator used in this method should be carefully chosen to ensure that it does not interfere with the reaction between Ferrous ammonium sulfate and potassium permanganate. Additionally, the solution should be titrated immediately after the addition of the external indicator to avoid any color changes due to oxidation of the Ferrous ion.

In conclusion, the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate can be accurately determined using an external indicator and a standard solution of potassium permanganate. This method is commonly used in analytical chemistry and is an important technique for determining the concentration of reducing agents.
