## Course Outcomes:

Upon completion of this course, you will be able to:

- Demonstrate a thorough understanding of the fundamental principles and concepts of the subject matter.
- Apply critical thinking skills to analyze and solve problems related to the subject matter.
- Communicate effectively through written and verbal means, using appropriate terminology and style.
- Develop and execute a research project related to the subject matter, including data collection, analysis, and interpretation.
- Identify and analyze ethical issues related to the subject matter, and demonstrate an understanding of ethical principles and standards.
- Collaborate effectively with peers in team-based activities, including group projects and discussions.
- Use appropriate technology and tools to enhance learning and communication.
- Demonstrate an appreciation for the cultural and historical context of the subject matter, and its relevance to contemporary issues.
- Develop a broad and deep understanding of the subject matter, and its interdisciplinary connections to other fields of study.
- Prepare for further study or employment in a related field, by acquiring the skills, knowledge, and experience necessary for success.
