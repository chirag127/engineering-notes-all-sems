### 15. Determination of Rate constant of hydrolysis of esters

In this experiment, we will determine the rate constant of hydrolysis of ethyl acetate using alkaline hydroxide solution. The reaction is given as follows:

Ethyl acetate + Hydroxide ion → Ethanol + Acetate ion

The rate of the reaction can be expressed as:

Rate = k [Ethyl acetate] [Hydroxide ion]

Where k is the rate constant of the reaction.

**Experimental Procedure:**

1. Prepare a solution of 0.1 M sodium hydroxide (NaOH) by dissolving 4 g of NaOH in 100 mL of distilled water.

2. In a 250 mL beaker, take 25 mL of 0.1 M NaOH solution.

3. Add a few drops of phenolphthalein indicator to the solution. The solution will turn pink.

4. Take 5 mL of ethyl acetate in a clean and dry test tube.

5. Add 20 mL of distilled water to the test tube.

6. Cover the test tube with a stopper and shake it vigorously for 1 minute.

7. Quickly add the ethyl acetate solution to the NaOH solution in the beaker and start the stop watch.

8. Stir the solution and observe the change in colour from pink to colourless.

9. Note down the time taken for the reaction to complete.

10. Repeat the experiment with different concentrations of ethyl acetate solution (2 mL, 3 mL, 4 mL, 5 mL) keeping the volume of NaOH solution constant.

**Calculations:**

1. Calculate the moles of NaOH used in the reaction.

2. Calculate the moles of ethyl acetate used in the reaction.

3. Calculate the initial concentration of ethyl acetate.

4. Calculate the rate constant (k) using the formula:

k = 2.303/t log (C0/Ct)

Where t is the time taken for the reaction to complete, C0 is the initial concentration of ethyl acetate, and Ct is the concentration of ethyl acetate at time t.

**Precautions:**

1. Use clean and dry glassware for the experiment.

2. Use distilled water for making the solutions.

3. Do not touch the NaOH solution with bare hands.

4. Use a stop watch to measure the time accurately.

5. Keep the volume of NaOH solution constant while changing the concentration of ethyl acetate solution.

6. Perform the experiment in a well-ventilated area.

7. Dispose of the waste properly.
