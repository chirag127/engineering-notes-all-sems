### 8. Determination of the Strength of Potassium Dichromate using Internal Indicator

Potassium dichromate is a common oxidizing agent used in many chemical reactions. It is important to determine the strength of the solution to ensure that the correct amount is used in a reaction. The strength of potassium dichromate can be determined using an internal indicator.

Here are the steps involved in the determination of the strength of potassium dichromate using an internal indicator:

1. Prepare the potassium dichromate solution by dissolving a known amount of potassium dichromate in distilled water. The concentration of the solution should be around 0.1M.

2. Add a small amount of an internal indicator to the solution. The internal indicator used is diphenylamine. The amount of the indicator added should be around 0.1g.

3. Titrate the solution against a standard solution of ferrous ammonium sulfate. The ferrous ammonium sulfate solution should also be prepared with a concentration of 0.1M.

4. The titration should be carried out in the presence of sulfuric acid. The concentration of sulfuric acid used should be around 1M.

5. During the titration, the potassium dichromate solution will react with the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution. The diphenylamine indicator will change color from blue to green when all the ferrous ammonium sulfate is consumed.

6. Note down the volume of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution required to reach the end point of the titration. The volume of the potassium dichromate solution used for the titration should also be noted.

7. Calculate the strength of the potassium dichromate solution using the formula:

Strength of potassium dichromate = (Volume of ferrous ammonium sulfate solution used x Strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate solution)/Volume of potassium dichromate solution used

8. Repeat the titration two more times to obtain an average value for the strength of the potassium dichromate solution.

9. Record all the observations and calculations in a tabular form.

By following these steps, the strength of potassium dichromate can be determined accurately using an internal indicator. This method is widely used in laboratories for the analysis of chemical compounds.
