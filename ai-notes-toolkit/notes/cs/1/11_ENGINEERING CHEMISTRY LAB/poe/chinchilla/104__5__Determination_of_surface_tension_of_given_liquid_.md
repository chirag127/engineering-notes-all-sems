### 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid.

Surface tension is defined as the force acting per unit length at the surface of a liquid, in the direction perpendicular to the surface. The surface tension of a liquid depends on factors such as temperature, pressure, and the nature of the liquid.

In this experiment, we will determine the surface tension of a given liquid using the capillary rise method. The capillary rise method is based on the fact that a liquid will rise in a capillary tube due to the surface tension of the liquid.

#### Materials Required:
- A capillary tube
- A beaker
- A stopwatch
- A ruler
- The given liquid

#### Procedure:
1. Fill the beaker with the given liquid.
2. Insert the capillary tube into the liquid in the beaker, ensuring that the capillary tube is completely submerged in the liquid.
3. Observe the liquid rise in the capillary tube due to surface tension.
4. Measure the height of the liquid rise in the capillary tube using the ruler.
5. Repeat the above steps for at least three different capillary tubes.
6. Calculate the average height of the liquid rise in the capillary tube.
7. Calculate the surface tension of the given liquid using the formula: 

   Surface tension = (2 x density x g x h) / (diameter of capillary tube x height of rise)

   where, 
   density = density of the liquid
   g = acceleration due to gravity
   h = average height of the liquid rise in the capillary tube


#### Precautions:
- Ensure that the capillary tube is completely submerged in the liquid.
- Use the same diameter capillary tube for all measurements.
- Take at least three readings to ensure accuracy.
- Ensure that the capillary tube is clean and free from any impurities.

By following the above procedure and taking the necessary precautions, the surface tension of a given liquid can be determined using the capillary rise method.
