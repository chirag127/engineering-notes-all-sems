# ENGINEERING CHEMISTRY LAB

Engineering Chemistry lab is an essential component of the engineering curriculum. It provides a platform for students to understand the basic principles of chemistry and their applications in engineering. Here are some key points to keep in mind while studying for the Engineering Chemistry Lab exams:

## Safety Precautions

Chemistry labs can be hazardous, and safety precautions must be taken seriously. Here are some safety precautions that must be followed in the Engineering Chemistry lab:

- Wear appropriate lab attire, including lab coat, gloves, and safety glasses.
- Handle chemicals with care and make sure to read the safety instructions before starting any experiment.
- Keep the lab workspace clean and organized to avoid accidents.
- Always use a fume hood when handling volatile chemicals.
- In case of any accidents or emergencies, inform the lab supervisor immediately.

## Laboratory Techniques

In Engineering Chemistry Lab, students learn various laboratory techniques that are essential for conducting experiments. Here are some of the laboratory techniques that you should be familiar with:

- Titration: A process of measuring the concentration of a solution by adding a reagent that reacts with the solute.
- Distillation: A method of separating a mixture of two or more liquids based on their boiling points.
- Chromatography: A method of separating and analyzing chemical compounds based on their properties.
- Spectroscopy: A technique used to study the interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation.

## Laboratory Experiments

Engineering Chemistry Lab involves performing experiments to understand the principles of chemistry and their applications in engineering. Here are some of the common experiments that you will perform in the lab:

- Acid-base titration: A technique used to determine the concentration of an acid or base solution.
- Synthesis of aspirin: A process of synthesizing aspirin from salicylic acid and acetic anhydride.
- Identification of unknown compounds: A process of identifying the unknown compounds using various laboratory techniques.
- Electroplating: A process of depositing a metal coating onto a metal surface using an electric current.

## Lab Reports

Lab reports are an essential component of the Engineering Chemistry lab. You will be required to submit a lab report for each experiment that you perform in the lab. Here are some key points to keep in mind while writing a lab report:

- The report should include a brief introduction to the experiment and its objectives.
- The report should include a detailed description of the experimental procedure, including any observations and measurements.
- The report should include a discussion of the results and their implications.
- The report should include a conclusion that summarizes the findings of the experiment and their significance.

Engineering Chemistry Lab is an important subject that requires both theoretical and practical knowledge. By following the safety precautions, mastering laboratory techniques, performing experiments, and writing lab reports, you can excel in this subject and apply the principles of chemistry in engineering.
