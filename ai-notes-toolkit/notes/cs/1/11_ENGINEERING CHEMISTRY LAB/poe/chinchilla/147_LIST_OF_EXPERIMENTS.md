# List of Experiments

The following is a list of experiments that are commonly performed in various scientific disciplines:

1. Acid-Base Titration: A technique used to determine the concentration of an acid or a base in a solution.

2. Chromatography: A method used to separate and identify different components in a mixture based on their physical and chemical properties.

3. Electrolysis: A process in which an electric current is passed through a solution to cause a chemical reaction, often used to produce or separate elements.

4. Enzyme Catalysis: The study of the effects of enzymes on chemical reactions, often used to understand the mechanisms of biological processes.

5. Gas Chromatography: A technique used to separate and identify different components of a gas mixture based on their physical and chemical properties.

6. Gravimetric Analysis: A method used to determine the amount of a substance in a sample by measuring its weight.

7. Mass Spectrometry: A technique used to identify the chemical composition of a sample based on the mass-to-charge ratio of its ions.

8. Polymerization: The process of combining monomers to form a polymer, often used to produce plastics and other synthetic materials.

9. Spectrophotometry: A method used to measure the absorption or transmission of light by a sample, often used to determine the concentration of a substance in a solution.

10. X-ray Crystallography: A technique used to determine the three-dimensional structure of molecules by analyzing their patterns of diffraction of X-rays.

These experiments are fundamental to many fields of science and are often used to provide insight into the behavior of matter and energy. They are an essential part of laboratory education and play a critical role in the advancement of scientific knowledge.
