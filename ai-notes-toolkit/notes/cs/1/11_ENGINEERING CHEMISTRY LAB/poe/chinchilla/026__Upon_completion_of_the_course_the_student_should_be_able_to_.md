### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

1. Understand the fundamental concepts and principles of the subject matter.
2. Analyze and interpret complex information related to the course.
3. Apply critical thinking and problem-solving skills to solve real-world problems.
4. Demonstrate proficiency in using relevant tools and technologies related to the course.
5. Communicate effectively in both written and oral formats.
6. Work collaboratively with peers to achieve common goals.
7. Evaluate the ethical implications of decisions related to the course.
8. Demonstrate a solid understanding of the historical and cultural context of the subject matter.
9. Develop a lifelong learning attitude to continue building knowledge and skills related to the course.
10. Apply the knowledge gained from the course to future academic and professional pursuits.

As you complete this course, you will have a thorough understanding of the subject matter that will equip you with the necessary skills and knowledge to pursue your academic and professional goals. You will have the ability to analyze complex information, problem-solve, collaborate with peers, and communicate effectively. You will also have an understanding of the historical and cultural context of the subject matter, and how it relates to ethical decision-making. Furthermore, you will have developed a lifelong learning attitude that will help you continue building upon the knowledge and skills you have gained from this course.
