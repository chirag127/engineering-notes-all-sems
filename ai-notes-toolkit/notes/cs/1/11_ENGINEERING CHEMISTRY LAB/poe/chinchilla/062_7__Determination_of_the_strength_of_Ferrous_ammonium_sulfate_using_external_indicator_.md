####### Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

Ferrous ammonium sulfate is a commonly used chemical in laboratory experiments. It is an important reagent for various analytical procedures, including the determination of the strength of solutions. In this experiment, we will use an external indicator to determine the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution. Here are the steps involved:

1. Prepare the Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution by dissolving a known amount of the chemical in water. The solution should be properly labeled and stored in a clean container.

2. Choose an external indicator that is suitable for this experiment. Methyl orange or phenolphthalein can be used as indicators, depending on the pH of the solution.

3. Prepare the indicator solution by dissolving a small amount of the indicator in water. The concentration of the indicator solution should be low enough to not interfere with the chemical reaction.

4. Take a small amount of the Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution in a clean flask and add a few drops of the indicator solution. The solution will change color based on the strength of the Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution.

5. Titrate the solution by adding a standard solution of potassium permanganate or potassium dichromate until the color of the solution changes. Note the volume of the standard solution required for the color change.

6. Repeat the titration for at least three times to obtain accurate results. Calculate the average volume of the standard solution required for the color change.

7. Use the average volume and the concentration of the standard solution to calculate the strength of the Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution.

8. Record the results and calculate the percentage error to determine the accuracy of the experiment.

By following these steps, we can determine the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution using an external indicator. This experiment is important for analytical chemistry and can be used to determine the strength of other solutions as well.
