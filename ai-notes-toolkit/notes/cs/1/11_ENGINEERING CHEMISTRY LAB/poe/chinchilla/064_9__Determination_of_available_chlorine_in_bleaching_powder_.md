#### Determination of Available Chlorine in Bleaching Powder

Bleaching powder, also known as calcium hypochlorite, is a common household chemical used for disinfecting and bleaching purposes. It is important to determine the available chlorine content in bleaching powder as it can affect its effectiveness in disinfecting and bleaching.

Here are the steps to determine the available chlorine content in bleaching powder:

1. Weigh 1 gram of bleaching powder and dissolve it in 100 ml of distilled water in a volumetric flask.
2. Add a few drops of potassium iodide solution to the solution in the flask. This will react with the available chlorine in the bleaching powder and liberate iodine.
3. Titrate the liberated iodine with a standard solution of sodium thiosulphate until the solution turns pale yellow.
4. Add a few drops of starch solution to the solution in the flask. This will form a blue color complex with the iodine, indicating the end-point of the titration.
5. Continue titrating with the sodium thiosulphate solution until the blue color disappears, indicating the completion of the reaction.
6. Note down the volume of sodium thiosulphate solution used for the titration.
7. Calculate the available chlorine content in the bleaching powder using the following formula:

   Available chlorine (%) = (Volume of sodium thiosulphate × Normality of sodium thiosulphate × 35.5 × 100) / Weight of bleaching powder

   Where, 
   - Volume of sodium thiosulphate is the volume used for the titration
   - Normality of sodium thiosulphate is the molarity of the solution multiplied by the number of electrons involved in the reaction (in this case, 2)
   - 35.5 is the atomic weight of chlorine
   - Weight of bleaching powder is the weight of the sample taken for the experiment.

8. Repeat the experiment three times and take the average of the calculated values to get a more accurate result.

By following these steps, one can determine the available chlorine content in bleaching powder and ensure its effectiveness in disinfecting and bleaching.
