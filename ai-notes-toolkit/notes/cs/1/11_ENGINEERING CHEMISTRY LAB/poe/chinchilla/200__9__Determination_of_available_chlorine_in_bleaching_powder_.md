### 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

Bleaching powder is a common chemical used for disinfecting and bleaching purposes. It contains various active chemicals, including available chlorine, which is responsible for its bleaching properties. The available chlorine content in bleaching powder can be determined through a simple chemical analysis. Here are the steps involved in determining the available chlorine content in bleaching powder:

1. Preparation of standard sodium thiosulphate solution: Dissolve 25g of sodium thiosulphate in 1L of distilled water to prepare a 0.1N solution.

2. Preparation of bleaching powder solution: Weigh 1g of bleaching powder and dissolve it in 100mL of distilled water.

3. Titration: Take 10mL of the bleaching powder solution in a conical flask and add 10mL of 6N HCl. Titrate the solution with the standard sodium thiosulphate solution using starch as an indicator. Carry out the titration until the blue color of the starch-iodine complex disappears.

4. Calculation: The available chlorine content in the bleaching powder can be calculated using the following formula:

 Available chlorine (%) = [(B - A) × N × 1.785] / W

Where,
B = Volume of sodium thiosulphate solution used for titration.
A = Volume of sodium thiosulphate solution used for blank titration.
N = Normality of sodium thiosulphate solution.
W = Weight of bleaching powder taken.

5. Interpretation: The percentage of available chlorine content in the bleaching powder is obtained from the calculation. This value can be compared with the standard value of available chlorine content in bleaching powder to determine its quality.

It is essential to ensure accurate measurements and precise titration for obtaining reliable results. It is also important to follow proper safety measures while handling chemicals and equipment. By following these steps, the available chlorine content in bleaching powder can be determined accurately and efficiently.
