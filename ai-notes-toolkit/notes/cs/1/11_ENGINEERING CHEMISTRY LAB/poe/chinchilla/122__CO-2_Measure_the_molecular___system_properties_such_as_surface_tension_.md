### CO-2 Measure the molecular / system properties such as surface tension

In the study of CO-2, it is important to measure the molecular and system properties such as surface tension. Here are some key points to consider when measuring these properties:

1. Surface tension is the force that holds the surface of a liquid together and is measured in units of force per unit length. It is a measure of the attraction between molecules at the surface of a liquid.

2. To measure surface tension, several methods can be used, including the drop weight method, the Wilhelmy plate method, and the pendant drop method.

3. The drop weight method involves measuring the weight of a drop of liquid as it falls from a pipette. The surface tension can then be calculated from the weight and the size of the drop.

4. The Wilhelmy plate method involves suspending a thin plate in the liquid and measuring the force required to detach it from the surface. The force is then used to calculate the surface tension.

5. The pendant drop method involves suspending a droplet of liquid from a needle and measuring the shape of the droplet. The shape can be used to calculate the surface tension.

9. Refractive index is a measure of how much a substance bends light and is important in the study of CO-2 as it affects the ability to detect CO-2 in reservoirs using optical techniques.
