### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer

Viscosity is the measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is an important property of fluids and is used in various fields such as engineering, medicine, and food industry. In this experiment, we will learn how to determine the viscosity of a given liquid using a viscometer.

#### Materials Required

- Viscometer
- Stopwatch
- Liquid sample
- Thermometer
- Beaker
- Stirrer

#### Procedure

1. Take the given liquid sample in a beaker and measure its temperature using a thermometer. Note down the temperature.

2. Pour the liquid into the viscometer up to the marked level.

3. Insert the spindle into the viscometer and start the stopwatch.

4. Stir the liquid using the stirrer and wait until the spindle reaches a steady state.

5. Record the time taken for the spindle to make a complete rotation or travel a certain distance in the liquid. Note down the time as t1.

6. Repeat the above steps for at least three times and calculate the average time taken by the spindle, which is denoted by t.

7. Now, take the viscometer out of the liquid and measure the temperature of the liquid again. Note down the temperature as T.

8. Calculate the viscosity of the liquid using the following formula: Viscosity = K (t - t0) (T + 273.15), where K is the calibration constant of the viscometer and t0 is the time taken by the spindle to travel a certain distance in an empty viscometer at the same temperature as the liquid.

#### Precautions

- Clean the viscometer properly before using it.
- Use a clean and dry beaker to hold the liquid sample.
- Stir the liquid gently to avoid air bubbles.
- Measure the temperature of the liquid accurately.
- Repeat the experiment at least three times to get an accurate result.

#### Conclusion

In this experiment, we learned how to determine the viscosity of a given liquid using a viscometer. The viscosity of a liquid depends on its temperature and molecular structure. This experiment can be used to determine the viscosity of various fluids and can be useful in industries such as oil and gas, pharmaceuticals, and food processing.
