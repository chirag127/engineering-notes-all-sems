# 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution

The determination of cell conductance of a solution is an essential aspect of electrochemistry. It is used to study the ionic behavior of a solution and helps to understand the electrolytic properties of the solution. The following are the steps involved in determining the cell conductance of a solution:

1. Preparation of Electrochemical Cell - The first step is to prepare the electrochemical cell. The cell consists of two electrodes, i.e., the anode and cathode, and an electrolyte solution. The electrodes should be made of a suitable material that does not corrode in the electrolyte solution.

2. Calibration of Conductivity Meter - The conductivity meter is calibrated using standard solutions of known conductivity. This step is essential to ensure the accuracy of the measurements.

3. Measurement of Conductivity - The conductivity of the solution is measured using the calibrated conductivity meter. The electrodes are dipped into the solution, and the conductivity meter is connected to the circuit. The conductivity of the solution is measured by the meter.

4. Calculation of Cell Conductance - The cell conductance is calculated using the formula:

        G = k / L

        Where G is the cell conductance, k is the conductivity of the solution, and L is the distance between the electrodes.

5. Calculation of Specific Conductance - The specific conductance of the solution is calculated using the formula:

        K = G / c

        Where K is the specific conductance, G is the cell conductance, and c is the concentration of the solution.

6. Determination of Equivalent Conductance - The equivalent conductance is calculated using the formula:

        Λ = K / C

        Where Λ is the equivalent conductance, K is the specific conductance, and C is the concentration of the solution.

In conclusion, the determination of cell conductance of a solution is an essential aspect of electrochemistry. It helps to understand the electrolytic properties of the solution and provides insights into the ionic behavior of the solution. The above steps should be followed to ensure accurate measurements of the cell conductance of the solution.
