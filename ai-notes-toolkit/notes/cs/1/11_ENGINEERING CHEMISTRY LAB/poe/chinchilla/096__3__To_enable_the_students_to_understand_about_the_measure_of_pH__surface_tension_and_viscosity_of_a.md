### 3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of a liquid.

In this section, we will discuss the three important properties of a liquid: pH, surface tension, and viscosity. These properties are crucial in understanding the behavior of liquids in various applications, such as in chemistry, physics, and engineering.

#### 1. pH

pH is a measure of the acidity or basicity of a liquid. It is an important property in many chemical and biological processes, such as in the human body and in the environment. The pH scale ranges from 0 to 14, with 7 being neutral, below 7 being acidic, and above 7 being basic. 

#### 2. Surface Tension

Surface tension is the property of a liquid that causes its surface to behave like a stretched elastic membrane. It is measured by the force required to stretch or deform the surface of the liquid. Surface tension is important in many applications, such as in the formation of droplets and bubbles, and in the behavior of fluids in porous materials.

#### 3. Viscosity

Viscosity is the measure of a liquid's resistance to flow. It is determined by the internal friction of the liquid as it flows. Viscosity is an important property in many applications, such as in the flow of fluids in pipes, the movement of blood in the body, and the behavior of liquids in lubrication.

In conclusion, the understanding of pH, surface tension, and viscosity of a liquid is essential in many scientific and engineering applications. It is important to measure and understand these properties to optimize the performance of liquids in various processes.
