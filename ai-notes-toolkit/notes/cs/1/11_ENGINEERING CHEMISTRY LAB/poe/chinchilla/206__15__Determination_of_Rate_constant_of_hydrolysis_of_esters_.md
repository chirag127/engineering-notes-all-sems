### 15. Determination of Rate constant of hydrolysis of esters

In this experiment, we will determine the rate constant of hydrolysis of esters using acid-catalyzed hydrolysis of ethyl acetate.

#### Materials Required
- Ethyl acetate
- Dilute hydrochloric acid
- Sodium hydroxide solution
- Phenolphthalein indicator
- Distilled water
- Burette
- Conical flask
- Measuring cylinder
- Stop watch
- Pipette
- White tile

#### Procedure

1. Prepare a solution of 0.1 M hydrochloric acid by diluting 10 mL of concentrated hydrochloric acid to 1 L with distilled water.

2. Prepare a solution of 0.1 M sodium hydroxide by dissolving 4 g of sodium hydroxide in 1 L of distilled water.

3. Take 100 mL of 0.1 M hydrochloric acid in a conical flask.

4. Add a few drops of phenolphthalein indicator to the conical flask.

5. Add 2 mL of ethyl acetate to the conical flask.

6. Mix the contents of the conical flask thoroughly.

7. Start the stopwatch and swirl the contents of the conical flask.

8. Record the time taken for the pink color of the phenolphthalein indicator to disappear.

9. Repeat the experiment by varying the volume of ethyl acetate from 1 mL to 5 mL.

10. Prepare a graph of the time taken for the pink color of the phenolphthalein indicator to disappear against the volume of ethyl acetate added.

11. Determine the rate constant of hydrolysis of ethyl acetate using the slope of the graph.

#### Calculations

- The rate of hydrolysis of ethyl acetate is given by the equation:
  ```
  Rate = k [ethyl acetate] [HCl]
  ```
  where k is the rate constant, [ethyl acetate] is the concentration of ethyl acetate and [HCl] is the concentration of hydrochloric acid.

- The concentration of hydrochloric acid is 0.1 M.

- The concentration of ethyl acetate is given by the equation:
  ```
  [ethyl acetate] = (volume of ethyl acetate added / total volume of solution) x 0.1 M
  ```

- Calculate the rate of hydrolysis of ethyl acetate for each volume of ethyl acetate added.

- Plot a graph of the rate of hydrolysis of ethyl acetate against the concentration of ethyl acetate.

- Determine the slope of the graph.

- The slope of the graph gives the rate constant of hydrolysis of ethyl acetate.

#### Precautions

- Use accurate measuring instruments to measure the volumes of the solutions.

- Make sure that the contents of the conical flask are thoroughly mixed before starting the stopwatch.

- Use distilled water to prepare the solutions.

- Handle the chemicals with care and follow the safety precautions.

- Use a white tile to observe the color change of the phenolphthalein indicator.

#### Conclusion

In this experiment, we have determined the rate constant of hydrolysis of esters using acid-catalyzed hydrolysis of ethyl acetate. The rate constant can be used to predict the rate of hydrolysis of other esters under similar conditions. This experiment demonstrates the importance of rate constants in predicting the rate of chemical reactions.
