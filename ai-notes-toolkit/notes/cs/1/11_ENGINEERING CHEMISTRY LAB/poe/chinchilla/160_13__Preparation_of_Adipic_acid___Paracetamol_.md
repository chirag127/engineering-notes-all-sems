# 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

Adipic acid and Paracetamol are important organic compounds with various applications in industries and medicine. Here are the methods for their preparation:

## Preparation of Adipic acid

Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is widely used in the production of nylon, polyurethane, and other synthetic fibers. It is prepared by the oxidation of cyclohexene with a mixture of nitric acid and sulfuric acid.

1. In a round-bottom flask, add 50 mL of cyclohexene and 50 mL of concentrated nitric acid.
2. Slowly add 50 mL of concentrated sulfuric acid while stirring the mixture.
3. Heat the mixture to 140-150°C and maintain the temperature for 4-5 hours while stirring.
4. Cool the mixture and pour it into a separatory funnel.
5. Extract the adipic acid with 50 mL of distilled water.
6. Wash the extracted adipic acid with 2-3 portions of 50 mL of distilled water.
7. Dry the adipic acid using a desiccator and obtain the pure compound.

## Preparation of Paracetamol

Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a common pain reliever and fever reducer. It is prepared by the nitration of phenol followed by reduction of the nitro group.

1. In a round-bottom flask, add 100 mL of concentrated sulfuric acid and 20 g of phenol.
2. Slowly add 15 mL of concentrated nitric acid while stirring the mixture.
3. Heat the mixture to 80-90°C and maintain the temperature for 2-3 hours while stirring.
4. Cool the mixture and pour it into a separatory funnel.
5. Extract the crude paracetamol with 50 mL of distilled water.
6. Add 20 g of sodium hydroxide to the extracted crude paracetamol and stir the mixture.
7. Collect the precipitated paracetamol by filtration.
8. Wash the paracetamol with 2-3 portions of 50 mL of distilled water.
9. Dry the paracetamol using a desiccator and obtain the pure compound.

These methods are widely used in industries and research laboratories for the preparation of Adipic acid and Paracetamol.
