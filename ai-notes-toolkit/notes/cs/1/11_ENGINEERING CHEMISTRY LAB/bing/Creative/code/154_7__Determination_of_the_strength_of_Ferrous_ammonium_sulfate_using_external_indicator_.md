# Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator

## Aim
To determine the strength of a given solution of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using an external indicator.

## Principle
Ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) is a salt of iron (II) that can be oxidized by a standard solution of potassium permanganate (KMnO4) in acidic medium. The reaction is:

5FeSO4(NH4)2SO4.6H2O + KMnO4 + 8H2SO4 -> 5Fe2(SO4)3 + K2SO4 + 2(NH4)2SO4 + 6MnSO4 + 24H2O

The end point of the titration is detected by using an external indicator, such as starch solution, which forms a blue-black complex with iodine. A small amount of potassium iodide (KI) is added to the FAS solution, which reacts with a trace of KMnO4 to produce iodine:

2KMnO4 + 10KI + 8H2SO4 -> 6K2SO4 + 2MnSO4 + 8H2O + 5I2

The iodine liberated is then titrated with a standard solution of sodium thiosulfate (Na2S2O3), which reduces it to iodide:

I2 + 2Na2S2O3 -> 2NaI + Na2S4O6

The end point of this titration is detected by adding a few drops of starch solution, which turns blue-black in the presence of iodine. When all the iodine is consumed, the blue-black color disappears.

## Procedure
- Pipette out 10 mL of the given FAS solution into a conical flask and add 10 mL of 1 M sulfuric acid and 2 g of KI crystals.
- Titrate the solution with the given KMnO4 solution until a faint pink color persists for 30 seconds.
- Note down the volume of KMnO4 used as V1.
- Add 2 mL of starch solution to the flask and continue the titration until the blue-black color disappears.
- Note down the volume of KMnO4 used as V2.
- Repeat the above steps for two more trials and take the average of V1 and V2.

## Calculations
- The strength of FAS solution can be calculated by using the following formula:

Strength of FAS = (V1 - V2) x N x 0.392 / V

where V1 and V2 are the average volumes of KMnO4 used in the two titrations, N is the normality of KMnO4 solution, 0.392 is the equivalent weight of FAS, and V is the volume of FAS solution taken for titration.

- The percentage purity of FAS can be calculated by using the following formula:

Percentage purity of FAS = (Strength of FAS / Theoretical strength of FAS) x 100

where the theoretical strength of FAS is 3.92 g/100 mL.
