### 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

Bleaching powder is a compound of calcium hypochlorite [Ca(OCl)<sub>2</sub>] and calcium chloride [CaCl<sub>2</sub>]. It is used as a disinfectant and a bleaching agent. The available chlorine in bleaching powder is the amount of chlorine that can be liberated when the powder is treated with dilute acids. The available chlorine is expressed as a percentage of the mass of the bleaching powder.

The principle of the determination is based on the reaction of bleaching powder with excess iodide in acidic medium to produce iodine, which is then titrated with a standard solution of sodium thiosulfate (hypo) using starch as an indicator.

The reactions involved are:

Ca(OCl)<sub>2</sub> + 2HCl → CaCl<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O + Cl<sub>2</sub>

Cl<sub>2</sub> + 2KI → 2KCl + I<sub>2</sub>

I<sub>2</sub> + 2Na<sub>2</sub>S<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> → Na<sub>2</sub>S<sub>4</sub>O<sub>6</sub> + 2NaI

The procedure for the determination is as follows:

- Weigh accurately about 1 g of bleaching powder and transfer it to a 250 mL conical flask.
- Add about 100 mL of distilled water and shake well to dissolve the powder.
- Add 10 mL of 10% potassium iodide solution and 10 mL of 1 M hydrochloric acid and swirl the flask to mix the contents.
- Titrate the liberated iodine with 0.1 N sodium thiosulfate solution using a burette until the solution becomes pale yellow.
- Add a few drops of starch solution as an indicator and continue the titration until the blue color disappears.
- Record the volume of sodium thiosulfate solution used as V<sub>1</sub> mL.
- Repeat the procedure with a blank titration using distilled water instead of bleaching powder and record the volume of sodium thiosulfate solution used as V<sub>2</sub> mL.
- Calculate the percentage of available chlorine in the bleaching powder using the formula:

% available chlorine = (V<sub>2</sub> - V<sub>1</sub>) x N x 35.45 x 100 / W

where N is the normality of sodium thiosulfate solution, W is the mass of bleaching powder taken in grams, and 35.45 is the equivalent mass of chlorine.
