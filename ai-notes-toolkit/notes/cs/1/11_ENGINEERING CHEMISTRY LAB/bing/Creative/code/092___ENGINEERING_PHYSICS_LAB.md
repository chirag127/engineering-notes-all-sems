# Engineering Physics Lab

- Engineering Physics Lab is a course that introduces students to the basic concepts and methods of experimental physics.
- The course aims to develop the skills of observation, measurement, analysis, and interpretation of physical phenomena.
- The course also exposes students to various instruments and techniques used in physics experiments, such as oscilloscopes, multimeters, lasers, optical fibers, etc.
- The course consists of several experiments that cover topics such as mechanics, optics, electricity, magnetism, and modern physics.
- The experiments are designed to illustrate the principles and applications of physics in engineering and technology.
- The course requires students to perform the experiments in groups, record the data and observations, and write formal lab reports.
- The course also requires students to follow the safety rules and ethical standards of scientific research.

Some of the experiments that are typically included in the Engineering Physics Lab course are:

- Measurement of physical quantities and errors
- Verification of Hooke's law and Young's modulus
- Determination of the coefficient of viscosity of a liquid
- Verification of the laws of reflection and refraction
- Determination of the focal length of a lens
- Study of interference and diffraction of light
- Determination of the wavelength of a laser
- Study of polarization of light
- Study of the characteristics of a solar cell
- Study of the Hall effect
- Study of the photoelectric effect
- Study of the radioactive decay of a source
