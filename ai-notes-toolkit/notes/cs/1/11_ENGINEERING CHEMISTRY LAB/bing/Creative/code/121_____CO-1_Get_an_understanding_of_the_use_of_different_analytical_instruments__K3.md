### CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

- Analytical instruments are devices that are used to measure, analyze, or identify the chemical, physical, or biological properties of a sample or a substance .
- Analytical instruments can be classified into different categories based on their principles, applications, or techniques, such as spectroscopy, mass spectrometry, electrochemical analysis, thermal analysis, separation analysis, microscopy, and hybrid technologies .
- Analytical instruments have various applications in different fields, such as pharmaceutical, food-processing, chemical, clinical, environmental, forensic, life science, and industrial sectors  .
- Analytical instruments can help in determining the composition, structure, quality, purity, identity, concentration, activity, or function of a sample or a substance  .
- Analytical instruments can also help in monitoring, controlling, or optimizing a process or a product.
- Analytical instruments can provide qualitative or quantitative information, or both, depending on the type and purpose of the analysis.
- Analytical instruments can operate in different modes, such as manual, semi-automatic, or fully automatic, depending on the level of automation and user interface.
- Analytical instruments can have different features, such as accuracy, precision, sensitivity, selectivity, speed, resolution, range, or reliability, depending on the performance and specifications of the device .
- Analytical instruments can have different advantages and limitations, such as cost, availability, maintenance, calibration, validation, or safety, depending on the design and operation of the device .
