# A liquid

- A liquid is one of the four states of matter, along with solid, gas, and plasma.
- A liquid is a fluid that can flow and take the shape of its container, but has a constant volume.
- A liquid has a definite surface, unlike a gas, but can change its shape, unlike a solid.
- A liquid is composed of molecules that are loosely bound by intermolecular forces, such as hydrogen bonds, dipole-dipole interactions, or van der Waals forces.
- A liquid can undergo phase transitions to a solid (freezing) or a gas (evaporation or boiling) depending on the temperature and pressure.
- A liquid can also form mixtures with other liquids (solutions) or solids (suspensions or colloids) depending on the solubility and polarity of the substances involved.
- A liquid has some properties that depend on its specific composition, such as density, viscosity, surface tension, boiling point, and freezing point.
- A liquid has some properties that are common to all fluids, such as pressure, buoyancy, and Bernoulli's principle.
