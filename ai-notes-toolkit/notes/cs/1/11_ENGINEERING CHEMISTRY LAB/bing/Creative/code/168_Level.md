# Level

- A level is a concept that measures the amount of something in a system or a process.
- A level can be quantitative or qualitative, depending on the type of measurement used.
- A level can be absolute or relative, depending on the reference point or the scale used.
- A level can be static or dynamic, depending on the rate of change or the variation over time.
- A level can be discrete or continuous, depending on the granularity or the smoothness of the measurement.

## Examples of levels

- The level of water in a tank is a quantitative, absolute, dynamic and continuous level. It can be measured in liters, gallons, or cubic meters, and it changes over time depending on the inflow and outflow of water.
- The level of difficulty of a game is a qualitative, relative, static and discrete level. It can be measured in terms of easy, medium, hard, or expert, and it does not change over time unless the player changes the settings.
- The level of happiness of a person is a qualitative, relative, dynamic and continuous level. It can be measured in terms of sad, neutral, happy, or ecstatic, and it changes over time depending on the person's mood and circumstances.
