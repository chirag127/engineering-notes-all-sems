# CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

- Analytical instruments are devices that measure, analyze, or monitor physical, chemical, or biological properties of samples, materials, or systems.
- Different analytical instruments have different uses, such as:
  - Spectroscopy: the study of the interaction of matter and electromagnetic radiation. Spectroscopic instruments can measure the absorption, emission, reflection, or scattering of light by a sample, and provide information about its structure, composition, or concentration.
  - Chromatography: the separation of a mixture into its components based on their different affinities to a stationary phase and a mobile phase. Chromatographic instruments can separate and identify the components of a complex mixture, such as drugs, pollutants, or biomolecules.
  - Mass spectrometry: the determination of the mass-to-charge ratio of ions generated from a sample. Mass spectrometric instruments can identify the molecular formula, structure, or isotopic composition of a sample, or quantify the amount of a substance in a sample.
  - Electrochemistry: the study of the relationship between electricity and chemical reactions. Electrochemical instruments can measure the electrical potential, current, or resistance of a sample, and provide information about its redox state, pH, conductivity, or electrochemical activity.
  - Microscopy: the use of a microscope to magnify and observe objects that are too small to be seen by the naked eye. Microscopic instruments can reveal the morphology, structure, or function of cells, tissues, or materials at different scales and resolutions.
