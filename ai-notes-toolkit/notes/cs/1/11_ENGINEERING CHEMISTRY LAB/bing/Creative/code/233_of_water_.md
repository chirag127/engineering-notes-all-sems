# Water

Water is a substance composed of the chemical elements hydrogen and oxygen and existing in gaseous, liquid, and solid states. It is one of the most plentiful and essential of compounds. A tasteless and odourless liquid at room temperature, it has the important ability to dissolve many other substances.

Water is a tiny molecule. It consists of three atoms: two of hydrogen and one of oxygen. Water molecules cling to each other because of a force called hydrogen bonding. It's the reason why water can do amazing things.

Water is a shape-shifter. It exists in three states on Earth: liquid, gas, and solid. Liquid water is the most common form of water on Earth. It covers about 71% of the Earth's surface in oceans, lakes, rivers, and other bodies of water. Gas water is also known as water vapor. It is the invisible gas that forms when water evaporates or boils. Water vapor is present in the air and can form clouds, fog, rain, snow, and other types of precipitation. Solid water is also known as ice. It is the frozen form of water that occurs when water freezes below 0°C (32°F). Ice can form glaciers, icebergs, snowflakes, hail, and frost.

Water is the liquid that makes life on Earth possible. As water cycles from the air to the land to the sea and back again, water shapes our planet — and nearly every aspect of our lives. All living things, from tiny cyanobacteria to giant blue whales, need water to survive. Water is essential for many biological processes, such as metabolism, transport, regulation, and synthesis.

Water is also a valuable resource for human activities, such as agriculture, industry, energy, and recreation. Water is used for irrigation, drinking, sanitation, hygiene, cooking, cleaning, cooling, heating, and more. Water is also a source of renewable energy, such as hydroelectricity, tidal power, and wave power.

Water is a precious and finite resource that needs to be conserved and protected. Water pollution, overuse, climate change, and population growth are some of the major threats to water quality and availability. Water scarcity, waterborne diseases, water conflicts, and water insecurity are some of the major challenges that affect millions of people around the world. Water management, water treatment, water conservation, water education, and water cooperation are some of the possible solutions to ensure water for all.

Water is a fascinating and complex substance that has many properties and functions. Water is essential for life, health, and well-being. Water is a vital part of our planet, our environment, and our society. Water is a wonder of nature and a wonder of science. Water is water.
