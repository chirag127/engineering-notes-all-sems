### 1. Calibration of Analytical Equipment and apparatus

Calibration is the process of evaluating and adjusting the precision and accuracy of measurement equipment. It is important to calibrate analytical equipment and apparatus to ensure the quality and reliability of the results obtained from them. Calibration also helps to maintain a safe working environment and comply with the regulatory standards.

Some of the steps involved in calibration of analytical equipment and apparatus are:

- Make an annual schedule for calibration of each instrument or equipment, indicating the dates when they are to be calibrated. The frequency of calibration may depend on the usage, the manufacturer's recommendations, and the in-house standard operating procedures .
- Use appropriate standards and reference materials for calibration. The standards should be traceable to national or international standards and have a known uncertainty. The reference materials should be stable, homogeneous, and representative of the samples to be analyzed .
- Follow the calibration procedures specified by the manufacturer or the in-house standard operating procedures. The procedures should include the steps to prepare the instrument or equipment, the calibration method, the acceptance criteria, and the documentation requirements .
- Record the calibration results and any adjustments made to the instrument or equipment. The records should include the date, time, operator, standard or reference material used, calibration method, calibration data, uncertainty, and any corrective actions taken .
- Label the instrument or equipment with the calibration status, date, and expiry. The label should be visible and durable. The instrument or equipment should not be used beyond the expiry date or if the calibration status is invalid .
- Review the calibration results and monitor the performance of the instrument or equipment. If the results are not satisfactory or the performance is deteriorating, the instrument or equipment should be recalibrated or repaired as soon as possible .
