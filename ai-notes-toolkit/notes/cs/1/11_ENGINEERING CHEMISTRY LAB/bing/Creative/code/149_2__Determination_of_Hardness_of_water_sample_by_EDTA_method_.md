# 2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method.

- Hardness of water is the measure of the concentration of calcium and magnesium ions in water.
- Hard water causes scaling, corrosion, and reduced efficiency of boilers, pipes, and appliances.
- EDTA (ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid) is a chelating agent that forms stable complexes with metal ions, such as Ca2+ and Mg2+.
- EDTA method is a titrimetric method that uses a standard solution of EDTA to titrate a water sample against an indicator that changes color at the end point.
- The indicator used is Eriochrome Black T (EBT), which forms a wine-red complex with free Ca2+ and Mg2+ ions in water.
- As EDTA is added, it reacts with the metal ions and releases the EBT, which turns blue.
- The end point is reached when the last trace of red color disappears and the solution becomes blue.
- The hardness of water can be calculated from the volume of EDTA used and its concentration.

## Procedure:

- Pipette out 50 mL of the water sample into a conical flask.
- Add 2 mL of buffer solution (pH 10) and 2 drops of EBT indicator.
- Titrate the solution with 0.01 M EDTA from a burette until the color changes from wine-red to blue.
- Note down the burette reading at the end point.
- Repeat the titration with two more aliquots of the water sample and take the average of the three readings.

## Calculation:

- The hardness of water is expressed in terms of ppm (parts per million) of CaCO3 equivalent.
- 1 ppm of CaCO3 equivalent = 1 mg of CaCO3 in 1 L of water.
- The molar mass of CaCO3 is 100 g/mol.
- The molar mass of EDTA is 292.24 g/mol.
- The molarity of EDTA is 0.01 mol/L.
- The stoichiometry of the reaction between EDTA and Ca2+ or Mg2+ is 1:1.

- Therefore, the hardness of water (in ppm) can be calculated by the formula:

`Hardness = (Volume of EDTA x Molarity of EDTA x 1000 x 100) / Volume of water sample`

- For example, if the average volume of EDTA used is 12.5 mL, then the hardness of water is:

`Hardness = (12.5 x 0.01 x 1000 x 100) / 50`

`Hardness = 500 ppm`
