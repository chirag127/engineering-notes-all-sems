# Course Outcomes

- Course outcomes are statements that describe what students are expected to know and be able to do by the end of a course.
- Course outcomes are usually derived from the course objectives, which are the broad goals of the course aligned with the program and institutional missions.
- Course outcomes are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) indicators of student learning.
- Course outcomes should be aligned with the course content, activities, assessments, and feedback methods to ensure coherence and validity.
- Course outcomes should be communicated to the students at the beginning of the course and throughout the course to guide their learning and self-assessment.
- Course outcomes should be evaluated at the end of the course to measure the extent of student learning and the effectiveness of the course design and delivery.
- Course outcomes should be reviewed and revised periodically based on the feedback from students, instructors, peers, and external stakeholders to ensure continuous improvement and quality assurance.
