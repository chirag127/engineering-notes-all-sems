# LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

An experiment is a scientific procedure that aims to test a hypothesis, answer a question, or demonstrate a known fact. Experiments usually involve manipulating one or more variables and measuring their effects on other variables.

Some examples of experiments are:

- Testing the effect of different fertilizers on plant growth.
- Measuring the reaction time of humans under different stimuli.
- Comparing the efficiency of different solar panels under different weather conditions.
- Investigating the relationship between temperature and pressure of a gas.
- Determining the pH of different solutions using indicators.
- Observing the behavior of animals in different environments.
- Studying the effect of music on memory and learning.
- Evaluating the performance of different algorithms on a given task.
- Exploring the properties of magnets and electricity.
- Demonstrating the law of conservation of energy using a pendulum.
