# CO2: Molecular and System Properties

## Molecular Properties

- CO2 is a linear molecule with a bond angle of 180 degrees and a bond length of 116.3 pm.
- CO2 has a molecular weight of 44.0095 g/mol and a molar volume of 22.26 L/mol at STP.
- CO2 is a non-polar molecule with a dipole moment of zero and a quadrupole moment of 4.67 D Å.
- CO2 has a vibrational frequency of 1333 cm-1 for the symmetric stretching mode and 2349 cm-1 for the asymmetric stretching mode.
- CO2 has a rotational constant of 0.3902 cm-1 and a centrifugal distortion constant of 1.35 x 10-6 cm-1.

## System Properties

- CO2 is a gas at standard conditions, but can be liquefied or solidified under high pressure or low temperature.
- CO2 has a critical point at 304.25 K and 73.8 bar, and a triple point at 216.58 K and 5.185 bar.
- CO2 has a density of 1.977 kg/m3 at STP, and a specific weight of 19.41 N/m3 at STP.
- CO2 has a surface tension of 0.0126 N/m at the triple point, and decreases with increasing temperature and pressure .
- CO2 has a viscosity of 0.0148 mPa s at STP, and increases with increasing pressure and decreases with increasing temperature.
- CO2 has a thermal conductivity of 0.0166 W/m K at STP, and increases with increasing temperature and pressure.
- CO2 has a specific heat capacity of 0.844 kJ/kg K at STP, and varies with temperature and pressure.
- CO2 has a latent heat of vaporization of 200 kJ/kg at the triple point, and decreases with increasing temperature and pressure.
- CO2 has a latent heat of fusion of 184 kJ/kg at the triple point, and decreases with increasing pressure.
