# CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

- Analytical instruments are devices that are used to measure, analyze, or identify the chemical, physical, or biological properties of a sample or a substance .
- Analytical instruments can be classified into different categories based on the principle or technique of analysis, such as spectroscopy, mass spectrometry, electrochemistry, thermal analysis, separation analysis, microscopy, etc .
- Analytical instruments have various applications in different fields, such as pharmaceutical, food-processing, chemical, clinical, environmental, forensic, life science, etc .
- Analytical instruments can help in determining the composition, structure, function, quality, purity, safety, or performance of a sample or a substance .
- Analytical instruments can also help in solving problems, developing new methods, validating results, or monitoring processes .
- Analytical instruments can be used in the laboratory or in the field, depending on the type, size, portability, and accuracy of the instrument .
- Analytical instruments can be operated manually or automatically, depending on the level of automation, control, and data processing of the instrument.
