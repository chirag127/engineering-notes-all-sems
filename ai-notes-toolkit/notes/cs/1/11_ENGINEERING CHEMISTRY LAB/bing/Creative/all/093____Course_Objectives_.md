## Course Objectives:

- The main objective of this course is to provide students with a comprehensive overview of the principles and practices of artificial intelligence (AI).
- The course will cover the following topics:
  - The history and foundations of AI, including its goals, challenges, and ethical issues.
  - The methods and techniques of AI, such as search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.
  - The applications and limitations of AI, such as expert systems, game playing, speech recognition, face detection, self-driving cars, and social robots.
- The course will also help students to develop the following skills:
  - The ability to analyze and solve problems using AI techniques and tools.
  - The ability to design and implement AI systems and agents using programming languages and frameworks.
  - The ability to evaluate and compare the performance and effectiveness of different AI approaches and algorithms.
  - The ability to communicate and collaborate with others on AI-related projects and tasks.
