# 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

- Bleaching powder is a compound of calcium, chlorine and oxygen with the formula Ca(OCl)Cl. It is used as a disinfectant and a bleaching agent.
- Available chlorine is the amount of chlorine that can be liberated from bleaching powder when it reacts with dilute acids. It is expressed as a percentage of the mass of bleaching powder.
- The reaction of bleaching powder with dilute acid is as follows:

  Ca(OCl)Cl + H2SO4 → CaSO4 + H2O + Cl2

- The available chlorine can be determined by titrating the chlorine gas evolved with a standard solution of sodium thiosulfate (Na2S2O3) using starch as an indicator. The reaction is as follows:

  Cl2 + 2Na2S2O3 → 2NaCl + Na2S4O6

- The procedure for the determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder is as follows:

  - Weigh about 0.5 g of bleaching powder and transfer it to a 250 mL conical flask.
  - Add about 100 mL of distilled water and shake well to dissolve the bleaching powder.
  - Add 10 mL of dilute sulfuric acid and stopper the flask immediately.
  - Allow the reaction to complete and the chlorine gas to escape.
  - Transfer the solution to a 250 mL volumetric flask and make up the volume with distilled water.
  - Pipette out 25 mL of the solution into a 250 mL conical flask and add 2 g of potassium iodide (KI).
  - Titrate the liberated iodine with 0.1 N sodium thiosulfate solution using starch as an indicator.
  - Note the volume of sodium thiosulfate solution used at the end point.
  - Repeat the titration with a blank solution prepared by adding 10 mL of dilute sulfuric acid and 2 g of potassium iodide to 25 mL of distilled water.
  - Calculate the percentage of available chlorine in the bleaching powder sample using the following formula:

    % available chlorine = (B - S) x N x 35.46 x 100 / W

    where B is the volume of sodium thiosulfate solution used for the blank titration, S is the volume of sodium thiosulfate solution used for the sample titration, N is the normality of sodium thiosulfate solution, W is the weight of bleaching powder taken, and 35.46 is the equivalent weight of chlorine.
