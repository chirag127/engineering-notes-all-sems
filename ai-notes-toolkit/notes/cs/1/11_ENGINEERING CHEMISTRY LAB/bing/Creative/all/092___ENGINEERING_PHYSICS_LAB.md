# Engineering Physics Lab

Engineering physics lab is a course that introduces students to the basic concepts and methods of experimental physics. The course aims to develop the skills of observation, measurement, analysis, and interpretation of physical phenomena. The course also exposes students to various instruments and techniques used in physics experiments.

Some of the topics covered in engineering physics lab are:

- Measurement of physical quantities and error analysis
- Newton's laws of motion and applications
- Conservation of energy and momentum
- Simple harmonic motion and resonance
- Waves and sound
- Optics and interference
- Electricity and magnetism
- Semiconductor devices and circuits

Some of the experiments performed in engineering physics lab are:

- Measurement of length, mass, and time using vernier calipers, screw gauge, and stopwatch
- Verification of Hooke's law and determination of Young's modulus of a wire
- Determination of the coefficient of friction and the angle of repose
- Verification of the principle of conservation of momentum using a ballistic pendulum
- Study of simple pendulum and determination of acceleration due to gravity
- Study of forced and damped oscillations using a spring-mass system
- Study of standing waves and determination of the speed of sound in air
- Study of diffraction and interference of light using a single slit, a double slit, and a diffraction grating
- Study of polarization of light using a polarizer and an analyzer
- Study of Ohm's law and Kirchhoff's laws using a voltmeter and an ammeter
- Study of the characteristics of a diode and a transistor using a multimeter and an oscilloscope
- Study of the logic gates and their applications using a breadboard and an LED

The engineering physics lab course helps students to:

- Understand the theoretical principles of physics and their experimental verification
- Develop the ability to design, conduct, and report physics experiments
- Learn the proper use and handling of various instruments and equipment
- Enhance the problem-solving and analytical skills
- Appreciate the role and relevance of physics in engineering and technology
