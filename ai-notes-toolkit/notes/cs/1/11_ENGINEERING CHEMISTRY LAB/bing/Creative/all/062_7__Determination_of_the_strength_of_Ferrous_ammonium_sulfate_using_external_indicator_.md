# Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator

- Ferrous ammonium sulfate, also known as Mohr's salt, is a double salt of iron(II) sulfate and ammonium sulfate, with the formula (NH4)2Fe(SO4)2·6H2O.
- It is often used as a standard solution for titrations of reducing agents, such as oxalic acid, potassium permanganate, or potassium dichromate.
- The strength of a ferrous ammonium sulfate solution can be determined by titrating it with a standard solution of potassium permanganate, using an external indicator such as starch or potassium ferricyanide.

(NH4)2Fe(SO4)2 + KMnO4 + H2SO4 -> Fe2(SO4)3 + K2SO4 + MnSO4 + NH4HSO4 + H2O

- The end point of the titration is marked by the appearance of a faint pink color in the solution, due to the excess of potassium permanganate.
- The external indicator is used to detect the end point more accurately, as the color change is very faint and may be affected by the presence of other substances in the solution.
- Starch is used as an external indicator when the concentration of potassium permanganate is low, as it forms a blue-black complex with iodine, which is produced by the reaction of potassium permanganate and potassium iodide in acidic medium.
- Potassium ferricyanide is used as an external indicator when the concentration of potassium permanganate is high, as it forms a brown precipitate of Prussian blue with iron(III), which is produced by the oxidation of iron(II) by potassium permanganate.
- The procedure for the determination of the strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator is as follows:

1. Prepare a standard solution of potassium permanganate by dissolving a known mass of KMnO4 in distilled water and standardizing it against a primary standard, such as sodium oxalate or sodium thiosulfate.

4. Titrate the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution with the standard potassium permanganate solution, stirring constantly, until the end point is reached.
5. Note down the volume of potassium permanganate used and repeat the titration for two more times to get concordant readings.
