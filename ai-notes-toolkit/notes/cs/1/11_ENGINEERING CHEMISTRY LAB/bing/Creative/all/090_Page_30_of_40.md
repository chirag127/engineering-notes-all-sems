## The topic is: How to write a formal email

- A formal email is a type of written communication that follows certain conventions of etiquette, grammar, and style.
- A formal email is usually used for professional or academic purposes, such as applying for a job, requesting information, or making a complaint.
- A formal email should have a clear and concise subject line that summarizes the main purpose of the email.
- A formal email should start with a polite salutation, such as "Dear Mr./Ms./Dr./Professor (last name)" or "To whom it may concern".
- A formal email should introduce yourself and state the reason for writing in the first paragraph.
- A formal email should provide relevant details and evidence to support your request, inquiry, or argument in the following paragraphs.
- A formal email should end with a courteous closing, such as "Sincerely", "Best regards", or "Thank you for your time and attention".
- A formal email should include your full name and contact information below the closing.
- A formal email should follow the standard rules of grammar, punctuation, and spelling, and avoid informal language, slang, or abbreviations.
- A formal email should be proofread and edited before sending to ensure clarity, accuracy, and professionalism.
