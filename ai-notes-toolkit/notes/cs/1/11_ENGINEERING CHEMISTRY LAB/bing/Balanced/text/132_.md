# The topic is

- The topic is a general term for the subject or theme of a text, speech, or conversation.
- The topic can be expressed in different ways, such as a word, a phrase, a question, or a statement.
- The topic can be identified by looking for the main idea or the central point that the text, speech, or conversation is about.
- The topic can be different from the title, which is a brief label that summarizes the topic or attracts the reader's attention.
- The topic can be related to the purpose, audience, and context of the text, speech, or conversation.
- The topic can be narrowed down or broadened by adding or removing details, examples, or subtopics.
- The topic can be developed by using various strategies, such as definition, description, comparison, contrast, classification, cause and effect, problem and solution, or argument and persuasion.
