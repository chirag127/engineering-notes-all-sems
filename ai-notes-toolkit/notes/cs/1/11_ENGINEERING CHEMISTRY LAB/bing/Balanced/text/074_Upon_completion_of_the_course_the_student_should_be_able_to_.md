# Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Demonstrate an understanding of the basic concepts and principles of the subject matter.
- Apply the acquired knowledge and skills to solve problems and perform tasks related to the course objectives.
- Analyze and evaluate information and arguments from various sources and perspectives.
- Communicate effectively and appropriately in oral and written forms.
- Collaborate with others and work independently in a variety of settings and contexts.
- Reflect on their own learning process and outcomes and identify areas for improvement.
- Demonstrate ethical, professional, and social responsibility in their academic and personal endeavors.
