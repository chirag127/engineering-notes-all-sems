# CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol

- Phenol is an aromatic compound with a hydroxyl group (-OH) attached to a benzene ring.
- Phenol is also known as carbolic acid or hydroxybenzene.
- Phenol has a pungent smell and is corrosive to the skin and eyes.
- Phenol is used as an antiseptic, disinfectant, and precursor for many industrial chemicals and drugs.

## Preparation of phenol

- Phenol can be prepared from various sources, such as coal tar, benzene, cumene, and chlorobenzene.
- Some of the common methods of preparation of phenol are:

### From coal tar

- Coal tar is a by-product of the destructive distillation of coal.
- Coal tar contains about 1% of phenol along with other aromatic compounds.
- Phenol can be extracted from coal tar by fractional distillation, followed by washing with sodium hydroxide solution to remove acidic impurities.
- The phenol can then be purified by crystallization or distillation.

### From benzene

- Benzene can be converted to phenol by electrophilic substitution with nitric acid, followed by reduction with tin and hydrochloric acid.
- The reaction steps are:

  - Benzene + Nitric acid -> Nitrobenzene + Water
  - Nitrobenzene + Tin + Hydrochloric acid -> Phenol + Ammonium chloride

- Alternatively, benzene can be converted to phenol by sulfonation with concentrated sulfuric acid, followed by hydrolysis with steam.
- The reaction steps are:

  - Benzene + Sulfuric acid -> Benzenesulfonic acid + Water
  - Benzenesulfonic acid + Steam -> Phenol + Sulfur dioxide

### From cumene

- Cumene is an alkylbenzene with an isopropyl group attached to a benzene ring.
- Cumene can be oxidized to cumene hydroperoxide, which can be cleaved to phenol and acetone by treatment with dilute acid.
- This method is known as the cumene process and is widely used in the industrial production of phenol and acetone.
- The reaction steps are:

  - Cumene + Oxygen -> Cumene hydroperoxide
  - Cumene hydroperoxide + Dilute acid -> Phenol + Acetone

### From chlorobenzene

- Chlorobenzene is an aromatic compound with a chlorine atom attached to a benzene ring.
- Chlorobenzene can be hydrolyzed to phenol by heating with sodium hydroxide solution under high pressure.
- This method is known as the Dow process and is used to produce phenol from waste chlorobenzene.
- The reaction steps are:

  - Chlorobenzene + Sodium hydroxide -> Sodium phenoxide + Sodium chloride
  - Sodium phenoxide + Water -> Phenol + Sodium hydroxide
