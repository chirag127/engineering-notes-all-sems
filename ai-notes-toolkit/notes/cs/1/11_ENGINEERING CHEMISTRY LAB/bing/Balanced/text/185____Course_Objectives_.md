## Course Objectives:

- To provide an overview of the principles and practices of artificial intelligence (AI).
- To introduce the main concepts and techniques of AI, such as search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.
- To develop the skills and abilities to apply AI methods and tools to solve real-world problems.
- To foster the critical thinking and ethical awareness of the opportunities and challenges of AI in society.
