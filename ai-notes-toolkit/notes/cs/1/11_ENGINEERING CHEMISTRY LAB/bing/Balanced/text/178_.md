# The topic is

- The topic is a general term for the subject matter or theme of a conversation, text, or discourse.
- The topic can be expressed explicitly or implicitly, depending on the context and the purpose of the communication.
- The topic can be identified by using various strategies, such as looking for keywords, phrases, titles, headings, summaries, questions, or main ideas.
- The topic can be classified into different types, such as factual, opinion, descriptive, narrative, persuasive, informative, or argumentative.
- The topic can be narrowed down or broadened by using different criteria, such as scope, relevance, interest, complexity, or specificity.
- The topic can be developed by using different methods, such as brainstorming, researching, outlining, drafting, revising, or editing.
