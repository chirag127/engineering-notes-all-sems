# The topic is

- The topic is a general term for the subject or theme of a conversation, text, or speech.
- The topic can be expressed explicitly or implicitly, depending on the context and the purpose of the communication.
- The topic can be identified by looking for clues such as keywords, main ideas, summaries, titles, headings, questions, or statements of purpose.
- The topic can be narrowed down or broadened by adding or removing details, examples, subtopics, or perspectives.
- The topic can be related to other topics by finding similarities, differences, causes, effects, or connections.
- The topic can be evaluated by considering its relevance, importance, accuracy, validity, or significance.
