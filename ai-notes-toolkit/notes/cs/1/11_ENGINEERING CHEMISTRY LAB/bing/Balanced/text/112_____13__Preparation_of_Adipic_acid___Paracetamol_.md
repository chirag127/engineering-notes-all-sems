### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

- Adipic acid is the organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is the most important dicarboxylic acid, mainly used as a precursor for the production of nylon.
- Adipic acid can be prepared from a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol called KA oil, the abbreviation of ketone-alcohol oil .
- The KA oil is oxidized with nitric acid to give adipic acid, via a multistep pathway .
- The oxidation involves the formation of nitrosonium ion (NO+), which reacts with the KA oil to form a nitroso compound.
- The nitroso compound undergoes a rearrangement to form a cyclic ketone, which is further oxidized to a lactone.
- The lactone is hydrolyzed to a dicarboxylic acid, which is decarboxylated to adipic acid.

- Paracetamol is the common name for acetaminophen, the organic compound with the formula C8H9NO2. It is a widely used analgesic and antipyretic drug.
- Paracetamol can be prepared from phenol by acetylation with acetic anhydride.
- The acetylation involves the nucleophilic attack of the phenol oxygen on the carbonyl carbon of the acetic anhydride, forming an ester and acetic acid.
