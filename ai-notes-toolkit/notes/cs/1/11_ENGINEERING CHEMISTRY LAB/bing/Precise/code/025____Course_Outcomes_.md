## Course Outcomes:
- Define the objectives and goals of the course.
- Identify the skills and knowledge that students are expected to acquire by the end of the course.
- Describe the methods of assessment used to evaluate student learning.
- Outline the course content and the sequence of topics to be covered.
- Specify the instructional materials and resources required for the course.
- Discuss the teaching strategies and techniques used to facilitate student learning.
- Explain how the course outcomes align with the program outcomes and institutional goals.
- Provide a clear and concise summary of the course outcomes for students to refer to throughout the course.
