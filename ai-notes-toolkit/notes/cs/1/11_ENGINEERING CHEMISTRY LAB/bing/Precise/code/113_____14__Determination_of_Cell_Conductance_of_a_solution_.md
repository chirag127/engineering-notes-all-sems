### 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution

Cell conductance is a measure of a solution's ability to conduct an electric current. It is determined by the concentration and mobility of ions in the solution. Here are the steps to determine the cell conductance of a solution:

1. Prepare the solution whose conductance is to be measured.
2. Clean the conductivity cell with distilled water and dry it.
3. Fill the cell with the solution.
4. Connect the cell to a conductivity meter.
5. Switch on the conductivity meter and allow it to stabilize.
6. Record the conductivity reading displayed on the meter.
7. Repeat the measurement with fresh samples of the solution to obtain an average value.

The cell conductance is calculated by multiplying the conductivity reading by the cell constant, which is determined by calibrating the cell with a solution of known conductivity. The cell constant is the ratio of the distance between the electrodes to the area of the electrodes. The unit of cell conductance is Siemens (S).
