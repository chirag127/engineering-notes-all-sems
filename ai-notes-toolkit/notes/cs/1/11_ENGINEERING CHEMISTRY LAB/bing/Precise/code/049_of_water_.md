# Water

Water is a transparent, tasteless, odorless, and nearly colorless chemical substance, which is the main constituent of Earth's streams, lakes, and oceans, and the fluids of most living organisms. It is vital for all known forms of life, even though it provides no calories or organic nutrients.

- Water is a liquid at standard temperature and pressure.
- Water covers 71% of the Earth's surface.
- Water is essential for the survival of all known forms of life.
- Water is a polar molecule, meaning it has a partial positive charge near the hydrogen atoms and a partial negative charge near the oxygen atom.
- Water has a high heat capacity, meaning it can absorb a lot of heat before its temperature rises.
- Water has a high surface tension, allowing it to form droplets and to support small objects on its surface.
- Water is a universal solvent, meaning it can dissolve many substances.
- Water is involved in many chemical reactions, including hydrolysis and hydration.
- Water exists in three states: solid (ice), liquid, and gas (water vapor).
- Water can exist in all three states at the same time in a state known as the triple point.
