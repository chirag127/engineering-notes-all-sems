# Synthesis of Adipic Acid

Adipic acid is a substance used for the preparation of nylon, polyurethanes, and other products of commercial interest. The industrial synthesis of adipic acid uses nitric acid oxidation of a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol termed “KA oil” (ketone and alcohol-oil).

1. Traditional feedstock for the synthesis of adipic acid is benzene. Benzene is hydrogenated using Ni-Al2O3 catalyst at 370–800 psi to cyclohexane.
2. Cyclohexane is then oxidized using Co catalyst to a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol.
3. This mixture is then converted to adipic acid using ammonium vanadate and nitric acid, with the help of a catalyst.

This procedure generates nitrous oxide, a substance that increases greenhouse gases, damages the ozone layer, and causes acid rain.
