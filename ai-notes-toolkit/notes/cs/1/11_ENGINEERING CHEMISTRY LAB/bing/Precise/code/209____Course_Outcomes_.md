## Course Outcomes:

1. Understand the fundamental concepts and principles of the subject matter.
2. Develop the ability to apply the knowledge and skills acquired in the course to solve problems and make informed decisions.
3. Demonstrate the ability to communicate effectively, both orally and in writing.
4. Develop critical thinking and analytical skills.
5. Demonstrate the ability to work effectively in a team.
6. Develop an appreciation for the ethical and social implications of the subject matter.
7. Demonstrate the ability to engage in lifelong learning and professional development.
