### 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution

Cell conductance is a measure of a solution's ability to conduct an electric current. It is determined by the concentration and mobility of ions in the solution. Here are the steps to determine the cell conductance of a solution:

1. Prepare the solution whose conductance is to be measured.
2. Clean the conductivity cell with distilled water and dry it.
3. Fill the cell with the solution and ensure that the electrodes are completely immersed.
4. Connect the cell to a conductivity meter and switch it on.
5. Allow the reading to stabilize and record the conductance value displayed on the meter.
6. Repeat the measurement with different concentrations of the solution to study the variation of conductance with concentration.

It is important to calibrate the conductivity meter with a standard solution of known conductance before taking measurements. The cell constant of the conductivity cell should also be determined to ensure accurate results.
