### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer

Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is defined as the ratio of the shear stress to the shear rate. A viscometer is an instrument used to measure the viscosity of a fluid. There are several types of viscometers, including capillary, rotational, and falling ball viscometers.

To determine the viscosity of a given liquid using a viscometer, the following steps can be followed:

1. Prepare the viscometer according to the manufacturer's instructions.
2. Fill the viscometer with the liquid to be tested.
3. Start the viscometer and record the time it takes for the liquid to flow through the viscometer.
4. Calculate the viscosity of the liquid using the appropriate formula for the type of viscometer being used.

It is important to note that the viscosity of a liquid can be affected by factors such as temperature and pressure. Therefore, it is important to conduct the test under controlled conditions to ensure accurate results.
