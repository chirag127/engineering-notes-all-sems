# Course Objectives

- To provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
- To equip students with the necessary skills and knowledge to apply the concepts learned in real-life situations.
- To develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
- To encourage independent learning and self-directed study.
- To prepare students for further academic study or professional development in the field.
- To foster an appreciation for the subject and its relevance to various aspects of life.
