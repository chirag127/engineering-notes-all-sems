### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route.

- Adipic acid is an important organic compound that is used in the production of nylon. It can be synthesized through a conventional route, which involves the oxidation of cyclohexanol or cyclohexanone with nitric acid. This process produces large amounts of nitrous oxide, a greenhouse gas that contributes to climate change.

- A green route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the use of hydrogen peroxide as an oxidizing agent, which produces water as the only byproduct. This process is more environmentally friendly and sustainable.

- Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a widely used pain reliever and fever reducer. It can be synthesized through a conventional route, which involves the nitration of phenol followed by the reduction of the resulting nitro compound.

- A green route for the synthesis of paracetamol involves the use of enzymes, such as peroxidases, to catalyze the reaction. This process is more environmentally friendly and produces fewer byproducts.

- Green chemistry is an approach to chemical synthesis that aims to reduce the environmental impact of chemical processes. It involves the use of sustainable and renewable resources, the reduction of waste and the use of safer and less toxic chemicals.

- The synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol through green routes is an example of how green chemistry can be applied to reduce the environmental impact of chemical processes. It is important for students to understand the principles of green chemistry and how it can be applied in the synthesis of organic compounds.
