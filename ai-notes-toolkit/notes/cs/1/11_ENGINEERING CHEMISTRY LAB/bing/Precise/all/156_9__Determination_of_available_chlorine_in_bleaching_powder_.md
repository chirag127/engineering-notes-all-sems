# Determination of Available Chlorine in Bleaching Powder

1. Bleaching powder is a white powder that is mainly used as a disinfectant and as a bleaching agent.
2. It is also known as calcium hypochlorite and its chemical formula is Ca(OCl)2.
3. The effectiveness of bleaching powder depends on the amount of available chlorine it contains.
4. Available chlorine is the amount of chlorine that is available to react with other substances.
5. The determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder is important to ensure its effectiveness as a disinfectant and as a bleaching agent.
6. The available chlorine in bleaching powder can be determined by titrating a known amount of bleaching powder with a standard solution of sodium thiosulfate.
7. The reaction between sodium thiosulfate and available chlorine produces iodine, which can be titrated with a standard solution of sodium thiosulfate.
8. The amount of sodium thiosulfate used in the titration is used to calculate the amount of available chlorine in the bleaching powder.
9. The result is expressed as a percentage of available chlorine in the bleaching powder.
