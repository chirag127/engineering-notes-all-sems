# K3

- K3 is a synthetic, artificially produced form of vitamin K that doesn’t occur naturally .
- This is unlike the other two forms of vitamin K — vitamin K1, known as phylloquinone, and vitamin K2, called menaquinone .
- Vitamin K3 may be converted into K2 in your liver .
- Vitamin K3 is also known as menadione .
- Vitamin K is important for blood clotting and bone health .
- Although vitamin K3 has demonstrated anticancer and antibacterial properties in test-tube studies, it has potential side effects .
- K3 is also the name of a form used by the IRS, called Schedule K-3 (Form 1065) .
