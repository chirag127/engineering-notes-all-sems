### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample

Alkalinity is a measure of the ability of water to neutralize acids. It is an important parameter in water quality analysis, as it provides information about the buffering capacity of water. Here are the steps to determine the alkalinity of a water sample:

1. Collect a water sample in a clean container.
2. Measure the pH of the sample using a pH meter or pH test strips.
3. If the pH is above 8.3, the sample is considered to have high alkalinity.
4. If the pH is between 4.5 and 8.3, titrate the sample with a standard acid solution, such as sulfuric acid or hydrochloric acid, using a burette.
5. Record the volume of acid required to lower the pH to 4.5.
6. Calculate the alkalinity of the sample using the formula: Alkalinity (mg/L as CaCO3) = (Volume of acid used * Normality of acid * 50,000) / Volume of sample.

This is a basic overview of the process of determining the alkalinity of a water sample. It is important to follow standard procedures and use calibrated equipment to ensure accurate results.
