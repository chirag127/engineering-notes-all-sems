### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample

Alkalinity is a measure of the ability of water to neutralize acids. It is an important parameter in determining the quality of water for various uses. Here are the steps to determine the alkalinity of a water sample:

1. Collect a water sample in a clean container.
2. Measure the pH of the water sample using a pH meter or pH test strips.
3. If the pH is above 7, the water is alkaline.
4. To determine the exact alkalinity, titrate the water sample with a standard acid solution, such as sulfuric acid or hydrochloric acid.
5. The volume of acid required to bring the pH of the water sample to a specific value, usually 4.5, is used to calculate the alkalinity of the water sample.
6. The alkalinity is usually expressed in milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate (CaCO3).

It is important to note that the alkalinity of water can vary depending on the source and the presence of certain minerals and substances in the water. Regular testing and monitoring of water alkalinity is important to ensure its suitability for various uses.
