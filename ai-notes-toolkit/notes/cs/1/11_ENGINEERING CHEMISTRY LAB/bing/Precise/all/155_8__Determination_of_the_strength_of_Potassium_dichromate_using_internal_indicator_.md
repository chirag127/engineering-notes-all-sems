# Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator

1. Potassium dichromate is a primary standard substance that can be used to determine the strength of a solution.
2. An internal indicator is used to determine the end point of the titration.
3. The internal indicator used in this experiment is potassium iodide.
4. The reaction between potassium dichromate and potassium iodide produces iodine, which can be observed as a color change.
5. The iodine produced is then titrated with sodium thiosulfate until the solution becomes colorless, indicating the end point of the titration.
6. From the volume of sodium thiosulfate used, the strength of the potassium dichromate solution can be calculated.
