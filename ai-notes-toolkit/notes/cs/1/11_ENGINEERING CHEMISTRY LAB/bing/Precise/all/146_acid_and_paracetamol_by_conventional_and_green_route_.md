# Acid and Paracetamol by Conventional and Green Route

1. Paracetamol, also known as N-acetyl-p-aminophenol (PAR), and acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin, ASA) can be synthesized using principles of green chemistry.
2. An alternative route for the synthesis of these compounds involves solvent-free synthesis combined with microwave-energy irradiation  .
3. In contrast, the conventional method of synthesizing paracetamol involves the use of solvents and may not be as environmentally friendly.
4. Green chemistry aims to reduce the environmental impact of chemical processes by minimizing the use of hazardous substances and maximizing atom economy.
5. For example, in a green synthesis of acetaminophen from phenol, the solvent from step two is kept to help maximize atom economy.
