## Course Outcomes:

1. Understanding of the subject matter: Students will gain a thorough understanding of the course material and be able to apply the concepts and theories to real-world situations.

2. Critical thinking: Students will develop the ability to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information to make informed decisions and solve problems.

3. Communication skills: Students will improve their ability to communicate effectively, both orally and in writing, and to present their ideas clearly and persuasively.

4. Collaboration: Students will learn to work effectively in teams, to collaborate with others, and to build consensus.

5. Professionalism: Students will develop a strong sense of professionalism, including ethical behavior, responsibility, and accountability.

6. Lifelong learning: Students will develop the skills and habits necessary for lifelong learning, including the ability to adapt to new situations and to continue to grow and develop professionally.

7. Global perspective: Students will gain an understanding of global issues and the interconnectedness of the world, and will develop the ability to think and act globally.

8. Technological proficiency: Students will become proficient in the use of technology and will be able to use it effectively to enhance their learning and professional development.

9. Creativity and innovation: Students will develop the ability to think creatively and to generate new ideas and approaches, and will learn to apply innovative solutions to complex problems.

10. Leadership: Students will develop the skills and qualities necessary to become effective leaders, including the ability to inspire and motivate others, to set and achieve goals, and to make difficult decisions.
