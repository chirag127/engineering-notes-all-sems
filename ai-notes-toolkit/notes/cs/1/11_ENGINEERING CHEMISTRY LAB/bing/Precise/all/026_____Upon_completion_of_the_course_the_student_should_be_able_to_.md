### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

1. Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. Apply the knowledge and skills acquired in the course to solve problems and make informed decisions.
3. Communicate effectively using the terminology and concepts of the subject.
4. Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
5. Critically evaluate information and arguments, and form well-reasoned opinions.
6. Demonstrate ethical and responsible behavior in academic and professional settings.
7. Engage in lifelong learning and professional development.
