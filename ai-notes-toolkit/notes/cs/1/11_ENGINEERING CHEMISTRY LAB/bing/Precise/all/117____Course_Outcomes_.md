## Course Outcomes:

1. Understanding the fundamental concepts and principles of the subject.
2. Developing the ability to apply the learned concepts and principles to solve problems.
3. Enhancing critical thinking and analytical skills.
4. Improving communication skills through written and oral presentations.
5. Developing the ability to work effectively in a team.
6. Gaining an appreciation for the importance of lifelong learning and professional development.
7. Acquiring the skills and knowledge necessary to pursue further studies or a career in the field.
