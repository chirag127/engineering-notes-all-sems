### 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator

1. Potassium dichromate is an oxidizing agent that can be used to determine the strength of a solution.
2. An internal indicator is used to determine the endpoint of the titration.
3. The internal indicator used in this experiment is diphenylamine sulfonic acid.
4. The diphenylamine sulfonic acid reacts with the excess potassium dichromate to produce a blue color, indicating the endpoint of the titration.
5. The strength of the potassium dichromate solution can be calculated using the volume of the solution used and the concentration of the analyte.
6. This method is commonly used in analytical chemistry to determine the strength of oxidizing agents.
