# 5. Synthesis of Organic Compounds: Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

Adipic acid and paracetamol are two important organic compounds that can be synthesized through both conventional and green routes. Here, we will discuss the synthesis of these compounds through both methods.

## Adipic Acid

Adipic acid is an important dicarboxylic acid used in the production of nylon. It can be synthesized through both conventional and green routes.

### Conventional Route

The conventional route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the oxidation of cyclohexane with nitric acid. This process produces nitrous oxide, a potent greenhouse gas, as a byproduct.

### Green Route

The green route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the oxidation of cyclohexene with hydrogen peroxide in the presence of a tungsten catalyst. This process produces water as the only byproduct, making it a more environmentally friendly option.

## Paracetamol

Paracetamol is a widely used analgesic and antipyretic drug. It can also be synthesized through both conventional and green routes.

### Conventional Route

The conventional route for the synthesis of paracetamol involves the nitration of phenol followed by the reduction of the nitro group to an amino group and the acetylation of the amino group.

### Green Route

The green route for the synthesis of paracetamol involves the direct acetylation of para-aminophenol with acetic anhydride in the presence of a solid acid catalyst. This process produces fewer byproducts and is considered a more environmentally friendly option.

In conclusion, the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol can be achieved through both conventional and green routes. The green routes offer more environmentally friendly options with fewer byproducts. It is important for students to understand these methods and their implications for the environment.
