## Course Outcomes:

- Define and explain the key concepts and principles of the course subject.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the course material through application and analysis.
- Develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills related to the course subject.
- Communicate effectively in written and oral forms about the course subject.
- Collaborate effectively with others to achieve common goals related to the course subject.
- Demonstrate ethical and professional behavior in the context of the course subject.
- Apply the knowledge and skills gained in the course to real-world situations.
- Evaluate and reflect on personal learning and growth throughout the course.
