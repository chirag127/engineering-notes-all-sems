### 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator

1. Potassium dichromate is a primary standard substance that can be used to determine the strength of a solution.
2. The strength of potassium dichromate can be determined using an internal indicator.
3. An internal indicator is a substance that changes color at the end point of a titration.
4. In the case of potassium dichromate, the internal indicator used is usually diphenylamine sulfonate.
5. Diphenylamine sulfonate changes color from colorless to blue at the end point of the titration.
6. The end point of the titration is reached when the blue color persists for at least 30 seconds.
7. The strength of the potassium dichromate solution can then be calculated using the volume of the solution used and the concentration of the titrant.
8. This method is accurate and reliable for determining the strength of potassium dichromate solutions.
