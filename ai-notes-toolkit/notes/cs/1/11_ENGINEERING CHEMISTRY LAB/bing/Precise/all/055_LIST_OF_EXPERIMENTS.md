# List of Experiments

1. **The Milgram Experiment:** A series of social psychology experiments conducted by Stanley Milgram in the 1960s, which measured the willingness of study participants to obey an authority figure who instructed them to perform acts that conflicted with their personal conscience.
2. **The Stanford Prison Experiment:** A social psychology study conducted by Philip Zimbardo in 1971, which investigated the psychological effects of perceived power, focusing on the struggle between prisoners and prison officers.
3. **The Asch Conformity Experiment:** A series of studies conducted by Solomon Asch in the 1950s, which demonstrated the power of conformity in groups and the influence of the majority on individual behavior.
4. **The Pavlov's Dog Experiment:** A study conducted by Ivan Pavlov in the 1890s, which investigated classical conditioning and the association of stimuli with reflex responses.
5. **The Marshmallow Test:** A study conducted by Walter Mischel in the 1960s and 1970s, which investigated delayed gratification and the ability of children to resist the temptation of an immediate reward in favor of a larger reward later on.
