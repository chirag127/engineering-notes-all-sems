# Analytical Instruments

Analytical instruments are devices used to measure, analyze, and determine the physical and chemical properties of a sample. These instruments are commonly used in scientific research, quality control, and medical diagnosis.

1. **Fundamental Concepts of Analytical Instruments:**
    - Analytical instruments are designed to provide accurate and precise measurements of a sample's properties.
    - These instruments use various techniques and methods to analyze the sample, including spectroscopy, chromatography, and mass spectrometry.
    - The choice of analytical instrument and technique depends on the type of sample being analyzed and the information required.
    - Proper calibration and maintenance of analytical instruments are essential to ensure accurate and reliable results.
    - Data analysis and interpretation are critical components of the analytical process, and require a thorough understanding of the instrument and the underlying principles of the technique used.
