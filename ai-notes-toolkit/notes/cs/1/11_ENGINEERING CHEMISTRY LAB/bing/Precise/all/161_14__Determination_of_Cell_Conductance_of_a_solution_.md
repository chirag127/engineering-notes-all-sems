# 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution

Cell conductance is a measure of a solution's ability to conduct an electric current. It is determined by the concentration and mobility of ions in the solution. Here are the steps to determine the cell conductance of a solution:

1. Prepare the solution to be tested by dissolving a known amount of solute in a known volume of solvent.
2. Set up the conductivity cell by connecting it to a conductivity meter.
3. Rinse the conductivity cell with distilled water and dry it.
4. Fill the conductivity cell with the solution to be tested.
5. Turn on the conductivity meter and wait for the reading to stabilize.
6. Record the conductivity reading displayed on the meter.
7. Repeat the measurement with different concentrations of the solution to obtain a calibration curve.
8. Use the calibration curve to determine the cell conductance of the solution.

It is important to note that the temperature of the solution can affect its conductivity, so it is recommended to perform the measurement at a constant temperature. Additionally, the conductivity cell should be calibrated regularly to ensure accurate measurements.
