### Scope for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

- The notes of this unit aim to provide an overview of the concepts and applications of measurement and mechatronics in mechanical engineering.
- The notes cover the following topics:
  - Measurement: The process of obtaining quantitative information about a physical phenomenon or system using instruments and methods.
  - Mechatronics: The integration of mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering to design and control systems that involve sensors, actuators, and feedback.
  - Sensors: Devices that convert physical quantities, such as force, temperature, pressure, etc., into electrical signals that can be processed and displayed.
  - Actuators: Devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions, such as motion, force, torque, etc., to perform a desired task.
  - Feedback: The process of comparing the actual output of a system with the desired output and adjusting the input accordingly to reduce the error.
  - Control systems: Systems that use feedback to regulate the behavior of a system or process according to a set of rules or objectives.
  - Types of control systems: Open-loop and closed-loop control systems, proportional, integral, and derivative (PID) controllers, etc.
  - Applications of measurement and mechatronics: Examples of measurement and mechatronics systems in various fields of mechanical engineering, such as robotics, automation, manufacturing, biomedical, automotive, aerospace, etc.
- The notes are intended to help the students to:
  - Understand the basic principles and components of measurement and mechatronics systems.
  - Analyze and design simple measurement and mechatronics systems using mathematical models and diagrams.
  - Perform experiments and simulations to test and evaluate the performance of measurement and mechatronics systems.
  - Appreciate the role and importance of measurement and mechatronics in modern engineering and technology.
