### Scope

- The scope of this unit is to introduce the basic concepts and principles of measurement and mechatronics, which are essential for mechanical engineers.
- Measurement is the process of obtaining quantitative information about a physical phenomenon or system using instruments or devices.
- Mechatronics is the integration of mechanical, electrical, electronic, and computer engineering to design and control complex systems that involve sensors, actuators, and feedback.
- The topics covered in this unit are:

  - Measurement systems: components, characteristics, errors, and standards.
  - Sensors and transducers: types, working principles, applications, and selection criteria.
  - Signal conditioning and processing: amplification, filtering, modulation, demodulation, analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog conversion, and data acquisition.
  - Mechatronic systems: examples, design process, and modeling techniques.
  - Actuators and controllers: types, working principles, applications, and selection criteria.
  - Feedback and control: concepts, types, stability, and performance analysis.

- The learning outcomes of this unit are:

  - To understand the basic concepts and principles of measurement and mechatronics.
  - To identify and describe the components and characteristics of measurement systems.
  - To select and use appropriate sensors and transducers for different measurement applications.
  - To apply signal conditioning and processing techniques to enhance the quality and usability of measurement data.
  - To recognize and explain the examples and applications of mechatronic systems.
  - To design and model simple mechatronic systems using engineering tools and methods.
  - To choose and operate suitable actuators and controllers for mechatronic systems.
  - To analyze and evaluate the feedback and control of mechatronic systems.
