### Introduction to Mechatronic Systems

- A mechatronic system is a system that integrates mechanical, electrical, electronic, and software components to perform a specific function or task .
- A mechatronic system can be characterized by versatility and flexibility, which are related to the operation capability and performance of the system.
- A mechatronic system typically consists of four main elements: sensors, information processing, actuators, and mechanical (physical) change.
- Sensors are devices that measure physical quantities, such as temperature, pressure, force, displacement, etc., and convert them into electrical signals .
- Information processing is the process of analyzing, processing, and controlling the signals from the sensors, using hardware and software components, such as microcontrollers, microprocessors, logic circuits, etc .
- Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into mechanical actions, such as motion, force, torque, etc., and apply them to the mechanical system .
- Mechanical (physical) change is the result of the interaction between the actuators and the mechanical system, such as position, velocity, acceleration, etc.
- A mechatronic system can be classified into different types, such as open-loop, closed-loop, feedback, feedforward, etc., depending on the control strategy and the system dynamics .
- A mechatronic system can be found in various applications, such as robotics, automotive, aerospace, biomedical, industrial, etc .
- A mechatronic system can provide reliable, efficient, and intelligent solutions for complex problems, by combining the advantages of different disciplines  .
