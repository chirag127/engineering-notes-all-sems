### Working principles of hydraulic turbines (Pelton Wheel and Francis) and pumps (Centrifugal and Reciprocating) and their classifications and hydraulic lift

- A hydraulic turbine is a device that converts the kinetic and potential energy of water into mechanical work.
- A hydraulic pump is a device that converts the mechanical energy of an engine or motor into hydraulic energy.
- Both hydraulic turbines and pumps operate on the principle of fluid mechanics, which states that the change in momentum or pressure of a fluid causes a force on the device .

#### Hydraulic turbines

- Hydraulic turbines are classified into two main types: impulse and reaction turbines.
- Impulse turbines are those in which the water jet strikes the buckets or blades of the turbine at high speed, and the pressure of the water remains constant throughout the process.
- Reaction turbines are those in which the water flows through the runner of the turbine, and the pressure of the water decreases as it passes through the blades.
- Pelton wheel is an example of an impulse turbine, while Francis turbine is an example of a reaction turbine.

##### Pelton wheel

- Pelton wheel consists of a circular wheel with a number of spoon-shaped buckets mounted on its rim, and one or more nozzles that direct the water jet tangentially to the wheel.
- The water jet impinges on the buckets and changes its direction, thus transferring its momentum to the wheel and making it rotate.
- The speed of the water jet should be equal to half the speed of the wheel for maximum efficiency.
- Pelton wheel is suitable for high head and low flow applications.

##### Francis turbine

- Francis turbine consists of a spiral-shaped casing that encloses a runner with a number of curved blades, and a guide vane mechanism that controls the flow and direction of the water entering the runner.
- The water enters the runner radially and leaves it axially, thus creating a pressure difference across the blades and making the runner rotate.
- The speed of the water at the inlet should be equal to the speed of the runner for maximum efficiency.
- Francis turbine is suitable for medium head and medium flow applications.

#### Hydraulic pumps

- Hydraulic pumps are classified into two main types: positive displacement and non-positive displacement pumps.
- Positive displacement pumps are those in which a fixed amount of fluid is displaced or delivered in each cycle of the pumping component, regardless of the resistance or pressure in the system.
- Non-positive displacement pumps are those in which the flow rate of the fluid depends on the resistance or pressure in the system, and the pumping component does not have a fixed displacement.
- Centrifugal pump is an example of a non-positive displacement pump, while reciprocating pump is an example of a positive displacement pump.

##### Centrifugal pump

- Centrifugal pump consists of a volute-shaped casing that encloses an impeller with a number of curved blades, and a suction and a delivery pipe that connect the pump to the reservoir.
- The impeller is driven by an engine or motor, and creates a vacuum at the pump inlet, allowing the atmospheric pressure to force the fluid from the reservoir into the pump.
- The impeller then imparts kinetic energy to the fluid, and the volute casing converts it into pressure energy, thus delivering the fluid to the system.
- Centrifugal pump is suitable for low head and high flow applications.

##### Reciprocating pump

- Reciprocating pump consists of a cylinder with a piston and a valve mechanism, and a suction and a delivery pipe that connect the pump to the reservoir.
- The piston is driven by an engine or motor, and performs a to-and-fro motion inside the cylinder, creating a suction and a delivery stroke.
- During the suction stroke, the piston moves away from the valve, creating a vacuum in the cylinder, and the valve opens, allowing the fluid to enter the cylinder from the reservoir.
- During the delivery stroke, the piston moves towards the valve, compressing the fluid in the cylinder, and the valve closes, forcing the fluid to exit the cylinder and enter the system.
- Reciprocating pump is suitable for high head and low flow applications.
