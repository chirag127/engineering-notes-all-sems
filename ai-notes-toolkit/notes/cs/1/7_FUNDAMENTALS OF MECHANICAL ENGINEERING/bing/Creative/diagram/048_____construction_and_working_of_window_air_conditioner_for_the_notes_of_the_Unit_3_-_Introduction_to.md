### Construction and Working of Window Air Conditioner

A window air conditioner is a type of room air conditioner that is mounted on a window or through a wall. It is a small unit that has a capacity of 0.5 to 3 tons of refrigeration (TOR). It uses a vapor compression cycle to cool and dehumidify the air in the room.

The main components of a window air conditioner are:

- **Compressor**: It is a hermetic type compressor that compresses the refrigerant vapor (usually R-22 or R-410A) to a high pressure and temperature. It is located at the back of the unit, near the condenser.
- **Condenser**: It is a finned tube heat exchanger that transfers the heat from the refrigerant to the ambient air. It is located at the back of the unit, along with the compressor. A propeller type fan is used to draw air from the atmosphere and to circulate it through the condenser coil.
- **Drier**: It is a filter that removes moisture and impurities from the refrigerant. It is located between the condenser and the capillary tube.
- **Capillary tube**: It is a thin tube that acts as a throttling device to reduce the pressure and temperature of the refrigerant. It is located between the drier and the evaporator.
- **Evaporator**: It is a finned tube heat exchanger that absorbs the heat from the room air and cools and dehumidifies it. It is located at the front of the unit, along with the blower. A centrifugal type blower is used to draw air from the room and to force it through the evaporator coil.
- **Expansion valve**: It is a valve that controls the flow of the refrigerant from the evaporator to the compressor. It is located between the evaporator and the compressor.

The working of a window air conditioner is as follows:

- The refrigerant vapor at high pressure and temperature is supplied by the compressor to the condenser.
- The refrigerant condenses in the condenser, releasing heat to the ambient air. The refrigerant becomes a high pressure liquid.
- The refrigerant passes through the drier, where it is filtered and dried.
- The refrigerant passes through the capillary tube, where it undergoes a pressure and temperature drop. The refrigerant becomes a low pressure liquid.
- The refrigerant enters the evaporator, where it evaporates, absorbing heat from the room air. The refrigerant becomes a low pressure vapor.
- The refrigerant passes through the expansion valve, where it is regulated to maintain a constant pressure difference between the evaporator and the compressor. The refrigerant returns to the compressor, completing the cycle.
- The room air is drawn by the blower and passes through a filter, which removes dust and dirt. The air then passes through the evaporator, where it is cooled and dehumidified. The air is then delivered to the room through a grille or a duct.

The advantages of a window air conditioner are:

- It is easy to install and remove, as it does not require any ductwork or piping.
- It is compact and portable, as it occupies less space and can be moved from one room to another.
- It is cheaper and more energy efficient than a central air conditioning system, as it cools only the required space and does not waste energy on cooling the unused areas.
- It is suitable for small rooms, apartments, offices, shops, etc., where individual cooling is required.
