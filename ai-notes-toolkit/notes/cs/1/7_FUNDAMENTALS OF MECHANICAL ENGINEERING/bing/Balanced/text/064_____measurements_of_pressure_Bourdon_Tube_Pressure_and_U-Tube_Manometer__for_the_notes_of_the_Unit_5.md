### Measurements of pressure (Bourdon Tube Pressure and U-Tube Manometer)

- Pressure is the measure of the force applied by a gas or liquid on a surface.
- Pressure can be measured using different devices, such as manometers and pressure gauges.
- A manometer is a device that uses a column of fluid (usually mercury or water) to measure the pressure difference between two points .
- A U-tube manometer is a type of manometer that consists of a U-shaped tube filled with fluid and connected to the two points whose pressure difference is to be measured .
- The U-tube manometer works on the principle of hydrostatic equilibrium, which states that the pressure at any point in a fluid at rest is the same in all directions.
- The U-tube manometer can measure the pressure difference between two points by comparing the heights of the fluid columns in the two arms of the tube .
- The pressure difference measured by a U-tube manometer can be calculated by the formula: pd = γ h = ρ g h, where pd is the pressure difference, γ is the specific weight of the fluid, ρ is the density of the fluid, g is the acceleration due to gravity, and h is the height difference between the fluid columns.
- A pressure gauge is a device that uses a mechanical element (such as a spring, a diaphragm, or a Bourdon tube) to measure the pressure of a fluid by converting it into a displacement or a force .
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge is a type of pressure gauge that uses a curved or coiled metal tube (called a Bourdon tube) to measure the pressure of a fluid .
- The Bourdon tube pressure gauge works on the principle of elastic deformation, which states that the shape of a material changes when a force is applied to it .
- The Bourdon tube pressure gauge can measure the pressure of a fluid by measuring the change in the shape of the Bourdon tube, which is connected to a pointer or a dial that indicates the pressure value .
- The Bourdon tube pressure gauge can measure the absolute, gauge, or differential pressure of a fluid, depending on the reference pressure used .
- The Bourdon tube pressure gauge can measure the pressure of a fluid with high precision and easy reading advantages, and can be compatible with almost every application.
