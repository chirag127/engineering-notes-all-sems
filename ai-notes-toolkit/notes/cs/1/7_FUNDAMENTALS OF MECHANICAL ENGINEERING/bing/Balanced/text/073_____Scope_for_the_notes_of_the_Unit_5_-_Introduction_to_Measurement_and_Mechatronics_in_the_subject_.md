### Scope

- The scope of measurement and mechatronics is to integrate the principles of mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering to design and develop smart systems that can sense, control, and interact with the physical world .
- Measurement and mechatronics can be applied to various domains, such as manufacturing, robotics, automotive, aerospace, biomedical, energy, and consumer electronics  .
- Measurement and mechatronics can enable the development of innovative products and solutions that can improve the performance, efficiency, reliability, safety, and sustainability of engineering systems .
- Measurement and mechatronics can also provide opportunities for research and development, as well as interdisciplinary collaboration and learning, in the fields of sensors, signal processing, control, automation, artificial intelligence, and machine learning  .
