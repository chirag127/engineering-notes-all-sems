### Calibration

- Calibration is the process of comparing the measurement values delivered by a device under test (DUT) with those of a calibration standard of known accuracy .
- Calibration is done to determine the error or verify the accuracy of the DUT's unknown value.
- Calibration can also be defined as the operation that establishes a relation between the quantity values with measurement uncertainties provided by measurement standards and the corresponding indications with associated measurement uncertainties of the calibrated instrument or secondary standard.
- Calibration is essential for ensuring the quality and reliability of measurements and reducing the uncertainty and errors in the measurement results.
- Calibration can be classified into two types: static calibration and dynamic calibration.
  - Static calibration is the calibration of a DUT when it is not in motion or under constant conditions.
  - Dynamic calibration is the calibration of a DUT when it is in motion or under varying conditions.
- Calibration can be performed in different ways, such as:
  - Direct comparison: The DUT is compared with a single reference standard of the same quantity and unit.
  - Indirect comparison: The DUT is compared with a reference standard of a different quantity or unit, but related by a known function or equation.
  - Substitution: The DUT is replaced by a reference standard of the same quantity and unit, and the difference in the indications is measured.
  - Transfer: The DUT is compared with a reference standard that has been previously calibrated against a higher-level reference standard.
- Calibration can be expressed in different ways, such as:
  - Calibration factor: The ratio of the reference standard value to the DUT value.
  - Calibration curve: The graphical representation of the relation between the reference standard values and the DUT values.
  - Calibration equation: The mathematical expression of the relation between the reference standard values and the DUT values.
  - Calibration certificate: The document that contains the information about the calibration process and the results.
