### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

- Fluid mechanics is the branch of science that deals with the behavior of fluids (liquids and gases) at rest and in motion.
- Fluid mechanics has a wide range of applications in engineering, such as aerodynamics, hydraulics, lubrication, blood flow, weather prediction, etc.
- Fluid mechanics can be divided into two main subfields: fluid statics and fluid dynamics.
- Fluid statics is the study of fluids at rest or in equilibrium. It involves the concepts of pressure, buoyancy, hydrostatic forces, etc.
- Fluid dynamics is the study of fluids in motion. It involves the concepts of velocity, acceleration, flow rate, viscosity, Reynolds number, Bernoulli's principle, etc.
- Fluid dynamics can be further classified into laminar flow and turbulent flow, depending on the degree of randomness and disorder in the fluid motion.
- Laminar flow is the smooth and orderly flow of fluid, where the fluid particles move in parallel layers or streamlines. It occurs at low velocities and low Reynolds numbers.
- Turbulent flow is the chaotic and irregular flow of fluid, where the fluid particles move in random and unpredictable directions. It occurs at high velocities and high Reynolds numbers.
- In this unit, we will learn the basic concepts and principles of fluid mechanics and their applications in engineering problems. We will also learn how to analyze and solve fluid mechanics problems using various methods and tools.
