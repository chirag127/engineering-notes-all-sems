### Measurements of Pressure (Bourdon Tube Pressure and U-Tube Manometer)

- Pressure is the force exerted by a fluid per unit area on a surface.
- Pressure can be measured by various devices, such as manometers, pressure gauges, and pressure transducers.
- A manometer is a device that uses a column of liquid to measure the difference in pressure between two points.
- A U-tube manometer is a type of manometer that consists of a U-shaped tube filled with a liquid of known density.
- A U-tube manometer can measure the pressure difference between two points by measuring the height difference of the liquid columns in the two arms of the tube.
- The pressure difference measured by a U-tube manometer can be calculated by the formula:

  $$p_d = \gamma h = \rho g h$$

  where $p_d$ is the pressure difference, $\gamma$ is the specific weight of the liquid, $\rho$ is the liquid density, $g$ is the gravitational acceleration, and $h$ is the height difference.
- A U-tube manometer can measure both positive and negative pressure differences, depending on which arm of the tube is connected to the higher or lower pressure point.
- A U-tube manometer can measure the absolute pressure of a point by connecting one arm of the tube to a vacuum chamber.
- A U-tube manometer can measure the gauge pressure of a point by connecting one arm of the tube to the atmospheric pressure.
- A U-tube manometer can measure the differential pressure of two points by connecting both arms of the tube to the two points.
- A U-tube manometer can be calibrated by using a known pressure difference and measuring the corresponding height difference.
- A U-tube manometer has some advantages and disadvantages, such as:

  - Advantages: simple, inexpensive, accurate, and versatile.
  - Disadvantages: limited by the density and height of the liquid, sensitive to temperature changes, and prone to parallax errors.

- A pressure gauge is a device that uses a mechanical element to measure the pressure of a fluid.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge is a type of pressure gauge that uses a curved metal tube to measure the pressure of a fluid.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge works by the principle that the tube tends to straighten when subjected to internal pressure.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge can measure the pressure of a fluid by measuring the displacement of the free end of the tube, which is connected to a pointer or a dial.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge can measure the absolute pressure of a fluid by sealing the tube in a vacuum chamber.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge can measure the gauge pressure of a fluid by exposing the tube to the atmospheric pressure.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge can measure the differential pressure of two fluids by connecting the tube to a diaphragm that separates the two fluids.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge can be calibrated by using a known pressure and measuring the corresponding displacement of the tube.
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge has some advantages and disadvantages, such as:

  - Advantages: robust, reliable, easy to read, and compatible with almost every application.
  - Disadvantages: limited by the elasticity and strength of the tube, affected by vibration and shock, and subject to hysteresis and wear.
