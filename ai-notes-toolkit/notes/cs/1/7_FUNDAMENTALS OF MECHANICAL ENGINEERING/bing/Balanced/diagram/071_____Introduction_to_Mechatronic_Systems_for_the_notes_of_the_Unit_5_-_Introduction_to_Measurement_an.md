### Introduction to Mechatronic Systems

- A mechatronic system is a system that integrates mechanical, electrical, electronic, and software components to perform a specific function or task.
- A mechatronic system can be characterized by versatility and flexibility, which are related to the operation capability and performance of the system.
- A mechatronic system typically consists of four main elements: sensors, information processing, actuators, and mechanical (physical) change.
- Sensors are devices that measure physical quantities, such as temperature, pressure, position, speed, etc., and convert them into electrical signals.
- Information processing is the process of analyzing, processing, and controlling the signals from the sensors, using hardware and software components, such as microcontrollers, microprocessors, digital signal processors, etc.
- Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into mechanical actions, such as force, torque, displacement, etc., and apply them to the mechanical system.
- Mechanical (physical) change is the result of the interaction between the actuators and the mechanical system, such as motion, deformation, vibration, etc.
