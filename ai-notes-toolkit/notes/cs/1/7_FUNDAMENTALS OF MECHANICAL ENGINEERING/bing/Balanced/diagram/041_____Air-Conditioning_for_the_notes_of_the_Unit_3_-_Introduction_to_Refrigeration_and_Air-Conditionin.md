### Air-Conditioning

- Air-conditioning is the process of treating air so as to control simultaneously its temperature, humidity, purity and distribution to meet the requirements of the conditioned space, such as comfort and health of human beings and other needs of the situation.
- Air-conditioning can also be defined as the science which deals with supply and maintaining desirable internal atmospheric condition irrespective of external condition.
- Air-conditioning is a member of a family of systems and techniques that provide heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC).
- The basic working principle of an air-conditioner is based on the laws of thermodynamics. An air-conditioner operates using the refrigeration cycle. Specific refrigerants are needed as the working fluid in the refrigeration cycle. An air-conditioner goes through 4 processes; compression, condensation, expansion, and evaporation .

- The main types of air-conditioning systems are:

  - Window air-conditioner: A self-contained unit that is installed in a window or a wall and cools a single room.
  - Split air-conditioner: A system that consists of two units, one indoor and one outdoor, that are connected by refrigerant pipes and electrical wires. The indoor unit contains the evaporator and the fan, while the outdoor unit contains the compressor, the condenser and the expansion valve. Split air-conditioners can cool one or more rooms.
  - Central air-conditioner: A system that uses ducts to distribute cooled air to multiple rooms or zones. The main components of a central air-conditioner are the air handler, the evaporator coil, the condenser unit and the thermostat. Central air-conditioners can also provide heating by reversing the refrigeration cycle.
