### Mass Flow Rate (Venturi Meter and Orifice Meter)

Mass flow rate is the amount of mass of a fluid passing through a cross-sectional area per unit time. It is usually denoted by m-dot and has the units of kg/s.

Venturi meter and orifice meter are devices that are used to measure the mass flow rate of a fluid by creating a pressure difference across a constriction in the flow path. The pressure difference is proportional to the square of the fluid velocity, according to the Bernoulli's equation. The mass flow rate can then be calculated by using the continuity equation and the fluid density.

Venturi meter is a device that consists of a converging section, a throat, and a diverging section. The fluid velocity increases in the converging section, reaches a maximum at the throat, and decreases in the diverging section. The pressure difference between the inlet and the throat is measured by a manometer or a differential pressure transmitter. The mass flow rate can be calculated by using the following formula:

m-dot = A1 * sqrt(2 * rho * (P1 - P2) / (1 - (A2 / A1)^2))

where A1 and A2 are the cross-sectional areas of the inlet and the throat, rho is the fluid density, and P1 and P2 are the pressures at the inlet and the throat.

Orifice meter is a device that consists of a thin plate with a hole in the middle, inserted in the flow path. The fluid velocity increases as it passes through the hole, creating a pressure difference between the upstream and the downstream of the plate. The pressure difference is measured by a manometer or a differential pressure transmitter. The mass flow rate can be calculated by using the following formula:

m-dot = Cd * A2 * sqrt(2 * rho * (P1 - P2))

where Cd is the discharge coefficient, A2 is the cross-sectional area of the hole, rho is the fluid density, and P1 and P2 are the pressures at the upstream and the downstream of the plate.

Some of the differences between venturi meter and orifice meter are:

- Venturi meter is more accurate than orifice meter, because it has a lower discharge coefficient variation and a lower head loss .
- Venturi meter is more expensive than orifice meter, because it has a more complex shape and requires more material and fabrication .
- Venturi meter is more suitable for measuring the mass flow rate of fluids with high viscosity, low density, or high Reynolds number, because it has a lower pressure recovery and a lower tendency to cause cavitation or erosion  .
- Orifice meter is more suitable for measuring the mass flow rate of fluids with low viscosity, high density, or low Reynolds number, because it has a higher pressure recovery and a higher sensitivity to small pressure differences  .
