### Comfort Conditions

- Comfort conditions are the atmospheric conditions that are suitable or comfortable for human health and well-being.
- Comfort conditions depend on various factors, such as temperature, humidity, air movement, dust particle level, odor level, etc.
- Comfort conditions can be achieved by controlling the physical properties of air using cooling, heating, humidification, and dehumidification processes.
- Comfort conditions can vary depending on the individual preferences, clothing, activity level, and climatic zone of the occupants.
- Comfort conditions can be measured by using instruments such as thermometers, hygrometers, anemometers, etc.
- Comfort conditions can be maintained by using refrigeration and air-conditioning systems that can regulate the heat and moisture exchange between the indoor and outdoor air .
- Comfort conditions can be specified by using standards such as ASHRAE Standard 55, which defines the acceptable thermal environments for human occupancy.
