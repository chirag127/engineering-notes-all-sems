### Working principles of hydraulic turbines (Pelton Wheel and Francis) and pumps (Centrifugal and Reciprocating) and their classifications and hydraulic lift

- A hydraulic turbine is a device that converts the kinetic and potential energy of water into mechanical work.
- A hydraulic pump is a device that converts the mechanical energy of an engine or motor into hydraulic energy.
- The working principle of a hydraulic turbine is based on Newton's law of motion, which states that a force is directly proportional to the change in momentum of a fluid.
- The working principle of a hydraulic pump is based on the principle of displacement, which states that for each rotating cycle of the pumping component, the same amount of liquid is displaced or delivered.

#### Pelton Wheel Turbine
- A Pelton wheel turbine is a type of impulse turbine, which means that the water jet strikes the buckets of the wheel at high speed and transfers its momentum to the wheel.
- The Pelton wheel turbine consists of a nozzle, a runner, a casing, and a brake nozzle.
- The nozzle converts the pressure energy of water into kinetic energy and directs a high-velocity jet of water onto the buckets of the runner.
- The runner is a wheel with a number of spoon-shaped buckets mounted on its rim.
- The casing is a circular chamber that encloses the runner and collects the water after it strikes the buckets.
- The brake nozzle is a device that regulates the flow of water through the nozzle and controls the speed of the runner.
- The Pelton wheel turbine is suitable for high head and low flow applications.

#### Francis Turbine
- A Francis turbine is a type of reaction turbine, which means that the water flows through the runner and changes its pressure and velocity as it passes through the blades.
- The Francis turbine consists of a spiral casing, a guide vane, a runner, and a draft tube.
- The spiral casing is a volute-shaped chamber that distributes the water uniformly around the runner.
- The guide vane is a device that regulates the flow of water and directs it onto the blades of the runner at the optimum angle.
- The runner is a wheel with a number of curved blades mounted on its hub.
- The draft tube is a conical pipe that connects the runner to the tailrace and converts the kinetic energy of the water into pressure energy.
- The Francis turbine is suitable for medium head and medium flow applications.

#### Centrifugal Pump
- A centrifugal pump is a type of rotodynamic pump, which means that the fluid is accelerated by a rotating impeller and then discharged by the centrifugal force.
- The centrifugal pump consists of a casing, an impeller, a suction pipe, and a delivery pipe.
- The casing is a volute-shaped chamber that encloses the impeller and collects the fluid after it leaves the impeller.
- The impeller is a wheel with a number of curved vanes mounted on its hub.
- The suction pipe is a pipe that connects the pump to the reservoir and delivers the fluid to the impeller.
- The delivery pipe is a pipe that connects the pump to the system and discharges the fluid from the pump.
- The centrifugal pump is suitable for low head and high flow applications.

#### Reciprocating Pump
- A reciprocating pump is a type of positive displacement pump, which means that the fluid is transferred by the periodic motion of a piston or a plunger in a cylinder.
- The reciprocating pump consists of a cylinder, a piston or a plunger, a suction valve, a delivery valve, a suction pipe, and a delivery pipe.
- The cylinder is a cylindrical chamber that houses the piston or the plunger.
- The piston or the plunger is a solid or hollow rod that moves back and forth in the cylinder.
- The suction valve is a one-way valve that opens when the piston or the plunger moves away from it and allows the fluid to enter the cylinder.
- The delivery valve is a one-way valve that opens when the piston or the plunger moves towards it and allows the fluid to exit the cylinder.
- The suction pipe is a pipe that connects the pump to the reservoir and delivers the fluid to the suction valve
