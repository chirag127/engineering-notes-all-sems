### Evolution for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

- Mechatronics is an interdisciplinary branch of engineering that focuses on the integration of mechanical, electrical and electronic engineering systems, and also includes a combination of robotics, electronics, computer science, telecommunications, systems, control, and product engineering.
- Mechatronics aims to develop intelligent electro-mechanical systems that can sense, actuate, and control physical phenomena.
- Mechatronics involves the application of principles of electromechanical energy conversion, which is the process of transforming electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa.
- Mechatronics also involves the use of photovoltaics, which is the conversion of light into electricity using semiconducting materials.
- Mechatronics has evolved from the traditional fields of mechanical engineering and electronics engineering, which have been influenced by the advances in microelectronics, microprocessors, sensors, actuators, and software engineering.
- Mechatronics has applications in various domains, such as automotive, aerospace, biomedical, industrial automation, robotics, renewable energy, and smart systems .
- Mechatronics requires a multidisciplinary approach to design, analysis, optimization, and implementation of complex systems, which involves the use of mathematical modeling, simulation, control theory, signal processing, and computer programming.
- Mechatronics also requires a systems perspective, which considers the interactions and interdependencies among the components and subsystems of a mechatronic system, as well as the external environment and the user requirements.
- Mechatronics is a dynamic and evolving field, which responds to the changing needs and challenges of the society and the industry, and offers opportunities for innovation and creativity .
