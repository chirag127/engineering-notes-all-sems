### Density

- Density is the measurement of how tightly a material is packed together. It is an intensive property, which means it does not depend on the amount of substance.  
- Density is defined as the mass per unit volume of a material substance. The formula for density is:     

  `ρ = m / V`

  where ρ is the density, m is the mass, and V is the volume.

- The SI unit of density is kilogram per cubic meter (kg/m<sup>3</sup>). It is also frequently represented in the cgs unit of grams per cubic centimeter (g/cm<sup>3</sup>).  
- Density can be used to identify a substance, to calculate the mass of a given volume of a substance, or to determine the volume of a given mass of a substance. 
- Density can vary with temperature and pressure, as both factors affect the volume of a substance. Generally, density decreases as temperature increases and increases as pressure increases.
