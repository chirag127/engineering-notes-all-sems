### Pressure

- Pressure is the physical force exerted on an object per unit area. 
- Pressure is denoted by the capital letter P or the lowercase letter p. 
- The basic formula for pressure is P = F / A, where F is the force and A is the area.      
- The SI unit of pressure is the Pascal (Pa), where 1 Pa = 1 N/m^2, i.e. one Newton per square meter. 
- Pressure can be measured using various instruments, such as barometers, manometers, and pressure gauges. 
- Pressure can vary depending on the state of matter, temperature, and altitude. 
- Pressure can cause deformation, stress, strain, and fluid flow in objects and fluids. 
- Pressure can be classified into different types, such as atmospheric pressure, gauge pressure, absolute pressure, and hydrostatic pressure.
