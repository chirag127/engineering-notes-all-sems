# Hybrid electric vehicles

- A hybrid electric vehicle (HEV) is a type of hybrid vehicle that combines a conventional internal combustion engine (ICE) system with an electric propulsion system (hybrid vehicle drivetrain)  .
- The presence of the electric powertrain is intended to achieve either better fuel economy than a conventional vehicle or better performance  .
- A hybrid electric vehicle cannot be plugged in to charge the battery. Instead, the battery is charged through regenerative braking and by the internal combustion engine .
- There are different types of hybrid electric vehicles, such as series hybrids, parallel hybrids, series-parallel hybrids, mild hybrids, and plug-in hybrids  .
- Series hybrids have only an electric motor to drive the wheels, and the ICE acts as a generator to charge the battery  .
- Parallel hybrids have both an ICE and an electric motor connected to the transmission, and they can work together or separately to propel the vehicle  .
- Series-parallel hybrids can operate as either series or parallel hybrids depending on the driving conditions  .
- Mild hybrids have a small electric motor that assists the ICE, but cannot drive the vehicle by itself  .
- Plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) are a combination of gasoline and electric vehicles, so they have a battery, an electric motor, a gasoline tank, and an internal combustion engine. PHEVs use both gasoline and electricity as fuel sources. They can be plugged in to an external power source to charge the battery, and they can also use regenerative braking and the ICE to charge the battery  .
- Hybrid electric vehicles have several advantages over conventional vehicles, such as lower emissions, higher fuel efficiency, reduced dependence on fossil fuels, and improved performance   .
- Hybrid electric vehicles also have some challenges, such as higher initial cost, complexity of the hybrid system, battery weight and size, and limited driving range on electric mode   .
- Some examples of hybrid electric vehicles are Toyota Prius, Honda Insight, Ford Fusion Hybrid, Hyundai Sonata Hybrid, Kia Niro Plug-In Hybrid, and Chevrolet Volt   .
