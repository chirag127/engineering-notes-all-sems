# Scope of Measurement and Mechatronics

- Measurement and mechatronics are two interrelated fields of engineering that deal with the design, development and application of systems that integrate mechanical, electrical, electronic and software components.
- Measurement is the process of obtaining, processing and interpreting data about physical quantities, such as temperature, pressure, displacement, force, etc. Measurement systems consist of sensors, transducers, signal conditioning circuits, data acquisition devices, and display or recording devices.
- Mechatronics is the synergistic combination of mechanical engineering, electronics, control engineering, and computer science, applied to the design of intelligent machines and systems. Mechatronics systems include robots, automated manufacturing systems, smart appliances, biomedical devices, etc.
- The scope of measurement and mechatronics is very broad and covers various domains of engineering and science, such as:

  - Industrial automation and control: Mechatronics systems are widely used to automate and optimize industrial processes, such as assembly, packaging, welding, machining, etc. Measurement systems are essential to monitor and control the quality, efficiency and safety of these processes.
  - Robotics and artificial intelligence: Mechatronics systems are the basis of robotics, which is the branch of engineering that deals with the design, construction and operation of machines that can perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously. Measurement systems provide the feedback and sensory information that enable robots to interact with their environment and perform tasks such as navigation, manipulation, recognition, etc.
  - Automotive and aerospace engineering: Mechatronics systems are involved in the design and development of vehicles and aircrafts, such as cars, trucks, buses, trains, planes, helicopters, rockets, etc. Measurement systems are used to monitor and control the performance, reliability and safety of these vehicles and aircrafts, such as engine, transmission, suspension, braking, steering, etc.
  - Biomedical engineering and health care: Mechatronics systems are applied to the design and development of medical devices and equipment, such as prosthetics, implants, surgical instruments, diagnostic tools, etc. Measurement systems are used to measure and analyze the physiological parameters and functions of the human body, such as blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, etc.
  - Consumer electronics and entertainment: Mechatronics systems are integrated into various consumer electronic devices and products, such as mobile phones, computers, cameras, gaming consoles, etc. Measurement systems are used to provide the user interface and functionality of these devices and products, such as touch screen, motion sensor, camera, microphone, etc.

- The scope of measurement and mechatronics is constantly expanding and evolving, as new technologies, applications and challenges emerge in the fields of engineering and science. Mechatronics engineers and measurement specialists are in high demand in various industries and sectors, such as manufacturing, transportation, energy, defense, health care, education, etc.
