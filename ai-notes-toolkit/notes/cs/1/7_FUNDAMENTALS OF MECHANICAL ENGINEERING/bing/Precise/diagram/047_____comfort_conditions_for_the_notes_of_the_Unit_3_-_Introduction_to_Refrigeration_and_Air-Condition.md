### Comfort Conditions

In Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning of the subject FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING, comfort conditions refer to the environmental conditions that are conducive to human comfort and well-being. These conditions include:

1. **Temperature:** The temperature of the air should be maintained within a comfortable range, typically between 20-25°C (68-77°F).
2. **Humidity:** The relative humidity of the air should be maintained within a comfortable range, typically between 40-60%.
3. **Air Movement:** The air should be circulated at a comfortable rate, typically between 0.15-0.25 m/s (30-50 fpm).
4. **Air Quality:** The air should be free of pollutants and contaminants, and should be maintained at a comfortable level of freshness.
5. **Noise:** The noise level should be maintained within a comfortable range, typically below 55 dB(A).
6. **Lighting:** The lighting should be adequate for the tasks being performed, and should be maintained at a comfortable level.

These comfort conditions are important for maintaining the health and well-being of individuals, and are typically achieved through the use of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems. These systems are designed to control the temperature, humidity, air movement, air quality, noise, and lighting within a building or space to provide a comfortable and healthy environment for its occupants.
