### Resultant of force system- concurrent and non-concurrent coplanar forces

#### Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

#### FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. A force system is a collection of forces acting on a body or a group of bodies.
2. The resultant of a force system is a single force that has the same effect on the body as the original force system.
3. Concurrent forces are forces that act on a body at the same point.
4. Non-concurrent forces are forces that act on a body at different points.
5. Coplanar forces are forces that lie in the same plane.
6. The resultant of a concurrent coplanar force system can be found by adding the forces vectorially.
7. The resultant of a non-concurrent coplanar force system can be found by finding the moment of each force about a point and then finding the resultant moment. The line of action of the resultant force passes through the point where the resultant moment is zero.
8. The magnitude and direction of the resultant force can be found using the principles of statics.
