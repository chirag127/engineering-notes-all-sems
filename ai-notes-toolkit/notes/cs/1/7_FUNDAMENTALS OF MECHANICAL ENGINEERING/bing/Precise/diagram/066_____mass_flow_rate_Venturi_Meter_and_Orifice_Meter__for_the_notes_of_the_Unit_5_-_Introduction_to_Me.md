### Mass Flow Rate (Venturi Meter and Orifice Meter)

Mass flow rate is the mass of a substance which passes through a given surface per unit time. It is typically measured in kilograms per second (kg/s). The mass flow rate can be determined using devices such as a Venturi meter or an orifice meter.

#### Venturi Meter

A Venturi meter is a device used to measure the flow rate of a fluid through a pipe. It consists of a short, converging section followed by a short, diverging section. The fluid's velocity increases as it flows through the converging section, causing a decrease in pressure. The pressure difference between the upstream and downstream sections of the meter can be used to determine the flow rate.

#### Orifice Meter

An orifice meter is another device used to measure the flow rate of a fluid through a pipe. It consists of a flat orifice plate with a hole in the center, placed in the flow path. As the fluid flows through the orifice, its velocity increases, causing a decrease in pressure. The pressure difference between the upstream and downstream sections of the meter can be used to determine the flow rate.
