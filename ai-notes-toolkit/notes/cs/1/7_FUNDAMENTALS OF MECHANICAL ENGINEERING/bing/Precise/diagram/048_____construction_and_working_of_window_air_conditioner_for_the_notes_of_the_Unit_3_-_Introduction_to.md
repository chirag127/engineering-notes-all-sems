### Construction and Working of Window Air Conditioner

A window air conditioner is a small unit with a capacity of 1/2 TOR to 3 TOR, mounted in a window or through a wall . It consists of several components, including a compressor, a condenser, a drier, a filter, a capillary tube, and an evaporator  .

The working cycle of a window air conditioner is the vapor compression cycle . An electric motor drives the fan to circulate air through the condenser coil and runs a blower to deliver either cool air to the room or suck air from it before forcing it through the evaporator coil, which cools as well as dehumidifies the air  .

The air circuit of a window air conditioner consists of 3 major air circulation systems: room air, outdoor air, and refrigerant air . The air from the room is drawn over the evaporator fan (also called the indoor fan) through a filter, which removes dirt, dust, and impurities. The fan used may be a propeller type or a centrifugal type .

The refrigerant vapor at high pressure and temperature is supplied by the compressor to the condenser. A propeller type fan is used to draw air from the atmosphere and to circulate it through the condenser coil .

Installation of a window air conditioner involves lowering the upper window down on top of the air conditioner, holding it in place, and expanding the air conditioner wings to close the gap on each side . An electrical connection is also required .
