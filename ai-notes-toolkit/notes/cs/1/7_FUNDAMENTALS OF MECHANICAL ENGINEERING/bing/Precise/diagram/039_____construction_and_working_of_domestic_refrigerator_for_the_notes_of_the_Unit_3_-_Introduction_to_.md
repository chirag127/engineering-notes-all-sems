### Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning

#### Construction and Working of Domestic Refrigerator

A domestic refrigerator works on the principle of vapor compression cycle. The main components of a domestic refrigerator are:

1. **Evaporator**: The evaporator is located in the freezer box in the form of a coil. The liquid refrigerant is evaporated in the evaporator by absorbing heat from the contents of the domestic refrigerator in the cabinet.
2. **Compressor**: The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas.
3. **Condenser**: The coils on the back of the refrigerator let the hot refrigerant gas dissipate its heat.
4. **Capillary tube**: The high-pressure liquid flows through the expansion valve.
5. **Refrigerant**: The system works on closed cyclic operation with the help of a heat transfer fluid called 'refrigerant'.

The working principle of a domestic refrigerator is as follows:

1. The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas.
2. The coils on the back of the refrigerator let the hot refrigerant gas dissipate its heat.
3. The high-pressure liquid flows through the expansion valve.
4. The liquid immediately boils and vaporizes, its temperature dropping to about -25°F.
5. As the cold gas flows through the expansion coils (inside the refrigerator) it makes the inside cold by absorbing heat.
