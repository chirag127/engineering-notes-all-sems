### Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles
#### Chargers
- Chargers are devices used to replenish the energy stored in rechargeable batteries.
- Chargers for electric vehicles (EVs) come in different types, with varying power levels and charging speeds.
- Level 1 chargers, also known as trickle chargers, use a standard 120V AC outlet and can take several hours to fully charge an EV battery.
- Level 2 chargers use a 240V AC outlet and can charge an EV battery in a few hours.
- DC fast chargers, also known as Level 3 chargers, use direct current (DC) and can charge an EV battery to 80% capacity in less than an hour.
- The type of charger used can affect the charging time and cost of charging an EV.
- Some EVs come with an onboard charger, while others require an external charger to be installed.
- Charging infrastructure is an important consideration for the widespread adoption of EVs.
