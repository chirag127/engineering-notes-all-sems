### Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning

#### Meaning and Application

1. **Refrigeration** is the process of removing heat from a space or substance to lower its temperature and maintain it below the ambient temperature.
2. **Air-Conditioning** is the process of treating air to control its temperature, humidity, cleanliness, and distribution to meet the requirements of the conditioned space.
3. The application of refrigeration and air-conditioning is widespread and includes areas such as food preservation, comfort cooling, and industrial processes.
4. Refrigeration and air-conditioning systems use the principles of thermodynamics, heat transfer, and fluid mechanics to achieve their desired effects.
5. The basic components of a refrigeration or air-conditioning system include a compressor, condenser, expansion device, and evaporator.
6. The refrigerant, a substance that absorbs and releases heat, circulates through these components to transfer heat from the space or substance being cooled to the outside environment.
7. The selection of the appropriate refrigerant and system design is critical to the efficient and effective operation of the refrigeration or air-conditioning system.
