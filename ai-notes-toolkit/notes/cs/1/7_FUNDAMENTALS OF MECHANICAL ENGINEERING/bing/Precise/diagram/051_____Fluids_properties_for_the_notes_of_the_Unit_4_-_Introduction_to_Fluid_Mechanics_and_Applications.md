### Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications

#### Fluid Properties

1. **Density**: The mass per unit volume of a fluid is called its density. It is denoted by the symbol ρ (rho). The unit of density is kg/m³.

2. **Viscosity**: Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is denoted by the symbol μ (mu). The unit of viscosity is Pa.s.

3. **Surface Tension**: Surface tension is the property of a liquid that allows it to resist an external force. It is denoted by the symbol σ (sigma). The unit of surface tension is N/m.

4. **Vapor Pressure**: Vapor pressure is the pressure exerted by a gas that is in equilibrium with its condensed phases (solid or liquid) at a given temperature in a closed system. The unit of vapor pressure is Pa.

5. **Compressibility**: Compressibility is the ability of a fluid to change its volume in response to a change in pressure. It is denoted by the symbol β (beta). The unit of compressibility is 1/Pa.
