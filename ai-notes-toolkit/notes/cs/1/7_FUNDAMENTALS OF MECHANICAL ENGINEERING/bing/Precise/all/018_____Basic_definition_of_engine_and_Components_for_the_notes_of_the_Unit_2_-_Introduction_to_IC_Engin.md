### Basic definition of engine and Components for the notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

An engine is a machine designed to convert one form of energy into mechanical energy. The internal combustion engine (IC engine) is a type of engine that burns fuel inside the engine to produce mechanical power. The electric vehicle (EV) engine, on the other hand, converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.

The main components of an IC engine include:
1. Cylinder block: The main body of the engine where the combustion takes place.
2. Piston: A cylindrical component that moves up and down inside the cylinder.
3. Connecting rod: Connects the piston to the crankshaft.
4. Crankshaft: Converts the linear motion of the piston into rotational motion.
5. Cylinder head: Seals the top of the cylinder and contains the valves and spark plug.
6. Valves: Control the flow of air and fuel into the cylinder and the flow of exhaust gases out of the cylinder.
7. Spark plug: Ignites the air-fuel mixture inside the cylinder.
8. Fuel injector: Injects fuel into the cylinder.

The main components of an EV engine include:
1. Electric motor: Converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
2. Battery: Stores electrical energy.
3. Controller: Controls the flow of electrical energy from the battery to the motor.
4. Transmission: Transfers the mechanical power from the motor to the wheels.
5. Regenerative braking system: Converts the kinetic energy of the vehicle into electrical energy and stores it in the battery.
