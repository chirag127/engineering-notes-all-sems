### Fluids properties for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. **Density**: The density of a fluid is defined as its mass per unit volume. It is typically represented by the Greek letter rho (ρ) and is measured in kilograms per cubic meter (kg/m³).

2. **Viscosity**: Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is typically represented by the Greek letter mu (μ) and is measured in pascal-seconds (Pa·s).

3. **Surface Tension**: Surface tension is the property of a liquid that allows it to resist an external force. It is typically represented by the Greek letter gamma (γ) and is measured in newtons per meter (N/m).

4. **Compressibility**: Compressibility is the ability of a fluid to change its volume in response to a change in pressure. It is typically represented by the Greek letter kappa (κ) and is measured in reciprocal pascals (1/Pa).

5. **Vapor Pressure**: Vapor pressure is the pressure exerted by a gas that is in equilibrium with its condensed phases (solid or liquid) at a given temperature in a closed system. It is typically measured in pascals (Pa).

6. **Buoyancy**: Buoyancy is the upward force exerted by a fluid on an object that is partially or completely immersed in it. It is equal to the weight of the fluid displaced by the object.

7. **Pressure**: Pressure is the force exerted per unit area. It is typically represented by the letter P and is measured in pascals (Pa).

8. **Temperature**: Temperature is a measure of the average kinetic energy of the particles in a substance. It is typically represented by the letter T and is measured in kelvins (K).

9. **Flow Rate**: Flow rate is the volume of fluid that passes through a given surface per unit time. It is typically represented by the letter Q and is measured in cubic meters per second (m³/s).

10. **Turbulence**: Turbulence is the chaotic movement of fluid particles. It is characterized by irregular and unpredictable flow patterns.
