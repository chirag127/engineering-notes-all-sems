### Drives

Drives are the means by which power is transmitted from the engine to the wheels of a vehicle. There are several types of drives used in vehicles, including:

1. **Chain drive:** A chain drive is a system that transmits power through a chain that is wrapped around two or more sprockets. This type of drive is commonly used in motorcycles and bicycles.

2. **Belt drive:** A belt drive is a system that transmits power through a belt that is wrapped around two or more pulleys. This type of drive is commonly used in automobiles and other vehicles.

3. **Shaft drive:** A shaft drive is a system that transmits power through a shaft that is connected to the engine and the wheels. This type of drive is commonly used in automobiles and other vehicles.

4. **Electric drive:** An electric drive is a system that uses an electric motor to transmit power to the wheels. This type of drive is commonly used in electric vehicles.

Each type of drive has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of drive depends on the specific requirements of the vehicle.
