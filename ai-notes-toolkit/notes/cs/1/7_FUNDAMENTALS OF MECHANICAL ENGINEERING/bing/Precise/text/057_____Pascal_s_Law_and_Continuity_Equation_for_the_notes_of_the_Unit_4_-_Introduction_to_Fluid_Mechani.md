### Pascal’s Law and Continuity Equation

Pascal's Law states that pressure exerted on a fluid in a closed container is transmitted equally and undiminished to every point in the fluid and to the walls of the container. This law is the basis for hydraulic systems, where a small force applied to a small area can produce a large force on a larger area.

The Continuity Equation is a mathematical expression that describes the conservation of mass in fluid flow. It states that the mass flow rate into a control volume must equal the mass flow rate out of the control volume, plus the rate of change of mass within the control volume. This equation is used to analyze fluid flow in pipes and other systems.
