### Measurements of Pressure: Bourdon Tube Pressure and U-Tube Manometer

#### Bourdon Tube Pressure Gauge:
- A Bourdon tube pressure gauge is a device used to measure the pressure of fluids.
- It consists of a curved tube, called the Bourdon tube, which is connected to a pointer.
- When the pressure inside the tube increases, the tube tends to straighten, causing the pointer to move.
- The movement of the pointer is proportional to the change in pressure.

#### U-Tube Manometer:
- A U-tube manometer is a device used to measure the pressure of fluids.
- It consists of a U-shaped tube filled with a liquid, usually mercury or water.
- The pressure of the fluid is applied to one end of the tube, causing the liquid to rise on that side and fall on the other side.
- The difference in height between the two sides of the tube is proportional to the pressure of the fluid.

These are two common methods for measuring pressure in the field of mechanical engineering. They are both widely used in the study of fluid mechanics and are important tools for understanding the behavior of fluids under different conditions.
