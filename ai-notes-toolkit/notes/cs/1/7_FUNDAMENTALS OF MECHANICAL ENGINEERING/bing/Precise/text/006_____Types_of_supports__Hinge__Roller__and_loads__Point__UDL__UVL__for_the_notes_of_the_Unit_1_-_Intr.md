### Types of supports (Hinge, Roller) and loads (Point, UDL, UVL) for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

#### Supports
- Supports are used to connect a body to its surroundings and to transmit forces between them.
- There are two main types of supports: hinge and roller.

##### Hinge Support
- A hinge support allows rotation but prevents translation in any direction.
- It is also known as a pin support or a pinned support.

##### Roller Support
- A roller support allows rotation and translation in one direction but prevents translation in the other direction.
- It is also known as a rocker support.

#### Loads
- Loads are forces that act on a body.
- There are three main types of loads: point, uniformly distributed load (UDL), and uniformly varying load (UVL).

##### Point Load
- A point load is a force that acts at a single point on a body.
- It is also known as a concentrated load.

##### Uniformly Distributed Load (UDL)
- A uniformly distributed load is a force that is distributed evenly over a length or area of a body.
- It is also known as a distributed load.

##### Uniformly Varying Load (UVL)
- A uniformly varying load is a force that varies linearly over a length or area of a body.
- It is also known as a triangular load.
