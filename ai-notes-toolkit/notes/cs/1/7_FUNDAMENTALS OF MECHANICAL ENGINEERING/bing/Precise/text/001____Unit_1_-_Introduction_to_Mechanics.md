## Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

Mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the motion of objects and the forces that cause that motion. It is divided into two main parts: kinematics and dynamics.

1. **Kinematics** is the study of motion without considering the forces that cause it. It deals with concepts such as position, velocity, and acceleration.
2. **Dynamics** is the study of motion and the forces that cause it. It includes concepts such as force, mass, and energy.

Some important principles in mechanics include Newton's laws of motion, the conservation of energy, and the conservation of momentum. These principles help us understand and predict the motion of objects in a wide range of situations.

Mechanics has many practical applications, including the design of machines, the study of the motion of planets and other celestial bodies, and the analysis of the motion of fluids. It is a fundamental subject in physics and engineering.
