## Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics

1. **Measurement** is the process of determining the value of a physical quantity by comparing it with a known standard.
2. **Mechatronics** is a multidisciplinary field that combines mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering to design and develop advanced systems and products.
3. Mechatronics systems often involve the use of sensors and actuators to measure and control physical quantities.
4. Common measurement techniques include the use of scales, rulers, and gauges to measure physical dimensions, and the use of thermometers, pressure sensors, and flow meters to measure temperature, pressure, and flow rate, respectively.
5. Mechatronics systems often involve the use of microcontrollers and other embedded systems to process sensor data and control actuators.
6. Examples of mechatronics systems include robots, automated manufacturing systems, and advanced consumer products such as smart home appliances and autonomous vehicles.
7. The design of mechatronics systems often involves the use of computer-aided design (CAD) and simulation tools to model and analyze system behavior.
8. The field of mechatronics is rapidly evolving, with new technologies and applications emerging on a regular basis.
