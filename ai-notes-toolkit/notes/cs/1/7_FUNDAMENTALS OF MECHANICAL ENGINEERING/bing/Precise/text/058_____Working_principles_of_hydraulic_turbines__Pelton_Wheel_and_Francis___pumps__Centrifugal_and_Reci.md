### Working principles of hydraulic turbines (Pelton Wheel and Francis) & pumps (Centrifugal and Reciprocating) and their classifications and hydraulic lift for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

- **Hydraulic turbines** are machines that convert the energy of flowing water into mechanical energy. The two main types of hydraulic turbines are the **Pelton Wheel** and the **Francis turbine**.
- The **Pelton Wheel** is an impulse turbine, which means that it uses the kinetic energy of the water to turn the wheel. The water is directed onto the wheel's blades, which are shaped like cups, and the force of the water causes the wheel to turn.
- The **Francis turbine** is a reaction turbine, which means that it uses both the kinetic and potential energy of the water to turn the wheel. The water flows through the turbine, and the pressure difference between the inlet and outlet causes the wheel to turn.
- **Hydraulic pumps** are machines that convert mechanical energy into hydraulic energy. The two main types of hydraulic pumps are **centrifugal pumps** and **reciprocating pumps**.
- **Centrifugal pumps** use a rotating impeller to create a flow of fluid. The fluid is drawn into the pump and then expelled by the centrifugal force generated by the rotation of the impeller.
- **Reciprocating pumps** use a piston or plunger to create a flow of fluid. The piston or plunger moves back and forth, drawing fluid into the pump on the intake stroke and expelling it on the discharge stroke.
- **Hydraulic lifts** use the principles of hydraulics to lift heavy loads. A hydraulic lift typically consists of a platform or cage that is raised and lowered by a hydraulic cylinder. The cylinder is filled with hydraulic fluid, and the pressure of the fluid is used to raise and lower the platform.
- Hydraulic turbines, pumps, and lifts are classified based on their design, operating principles, and intended applications. For example, hydraulic turbines can be classified as impulse or reaction turbines, and hydraulic pumps can be classified as positive displacement or dynamic pumps.
