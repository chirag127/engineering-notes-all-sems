### Construction and Working of Domestic Refrigerator

A domestic refrigerator is a household appliance that is used to store food and drinks at low temperatures. The main components of a domestic refrigerator are the evaporator, compressor, condenser, and capillary tube. The system works on a closed cyclic operation with the help of a heat transfer fluid called 'refrigerant' .

1. **Evaporator**: The evaporator is located in the freezer box in the form of a coil. The liquid refrigerant is evaporated in the evaporator by absorbing heat from the contents of the domestic refrigerator in the cabinet .
2. **Compressor**: The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas .
3. **Condenser**: The coils on the back of the refrigerator let the hot refrigerant gas dissipate its heat .
4. **Capillary tube**: The high-pressure liquid flows through the expansion valve .

The working principle of a domestic refrigerator is as follows: The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas, which then flows through the condenser coils on the back of the refrigerator, where it dissipates its heat. The high-pressure liquid then flows through the expansion valve, where it immediately boils and vaporizes, its temperature dropping to about -25°F. As the cold gas flows through the expansion coils (inside the refrigerator), it makes the inside cold by absorbing heat .
