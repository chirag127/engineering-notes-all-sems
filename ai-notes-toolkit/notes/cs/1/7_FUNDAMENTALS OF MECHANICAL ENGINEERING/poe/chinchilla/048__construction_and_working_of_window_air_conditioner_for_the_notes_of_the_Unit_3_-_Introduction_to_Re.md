### Construction and Working of Window Air Conditioner

A window air conditioner is a self-contained unit that is designed to cool a single room. It is a popular choice for residential and small commercial spaces because of its affordability and easy installation. Let's dive into the construction and working of a window air conditioner:

#### Construction

1. Compressor: It is the heart of the air conditioner unit, responsible for compressing and circulating the refrigerant through the system.

2. Evaporator: It is the component that absorbs heat from the room and cools the air.

3. Condenser: It releases the heat absorbed by the refrigerant and expels it outside the room.

4. Expansion valve: It controls the flow of refrigerant into the evaporator.

5. Fan: It circulates the air in the room over the evaporator coil, which cools the air.

6. Thermostat: It senses the room temperature and controls the compressor and fan to maintain the desired temperature.

#### Working

1. The compressor compresses the refrigerant, which then flows through the condenser where it releases the heat absorbed from the room.

2. The cooled refrigerant then flows through the expansion valve, which controls the flow of refrigerant into the evaporator.

3. The evaporator absorbs heat from the room and cools the air, which is circulated by the fan.

4. The thermostat senses the room temperature and controls the compressor and fan to maintain the desired temperature.

5. The cycle repeats until the desired temperature is achieved.

In summary, a window air conditioner works by removing heat from the room and expelling it outside. It does this by circulating refrigerant through a closed system that includes a compressor, evaporator, condenser, expansion valve, fan, and thermostat. Understanding the construction and working of a window air conditioner is essential for proper installation, maintenance, and repair of the unit.
