### Free Body Diagram for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering

In mechanics, free body diagrams (FBDs) are an essential tool to analyze and understand the behavior of an object under the influence of external forces. FBDs help to simplify complex problems and provide a visual representation of the forces acting on the object.

Here are some key points to remember while drawing a free body diagram:

1. Identify the object: The first step is to identify the object or system that you want to analyze. This could be a single object or a group of objects that are interacting with each other.

2. Isolate the object: Draw the object in isolation, separating it from its surroundings. This helps to focus on the forces acting on the object and avoid confusion.

3. Draw all external forces: Identify all the external forces acting on the object and draw them as arrows pointing away from the object. Examples of external forces include weight, normal force, tension, friction, and air resistance.

4. Label the forces: Label each force with a brief description of what it represents. For example, weight could be labeled as "W" and normal force as "N".

5. Choose a coordinate system: Choose a coordinate system for the object, which could be a Cartesian coordinate system or a polar coordinate system. This helps to determine the components of the forces acting on the object.

6. Resolve forces: Resolve each force into its components along the chosen coordinate system. This helps to analyze the forces acting in different directions.

7. Sum up forces: Sum up all the forces acting on the object in each direction. This helps to determine the net force acting on the object.

8. Apply Newton's laws of motion: Apply Newton's laws of motion to analyze the behavior of the object. This helps to determine the acceleration of the object under the influence of the net force acting on it.

In conclusion, free body diagrams are an essential tool to analyze and understand the behavior of an object under the influence of external forces in mechanics. By following these key points, one can draw an accurate and informative FBD for any object or system.
