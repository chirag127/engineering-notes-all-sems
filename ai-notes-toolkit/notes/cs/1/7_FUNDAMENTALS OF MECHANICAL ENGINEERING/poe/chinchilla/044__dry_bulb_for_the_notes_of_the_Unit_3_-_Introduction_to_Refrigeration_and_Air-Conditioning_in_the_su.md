### Dry Bulb

In the field of refrigeration and air conditioning, the term "dry bulb" is used to refer to the temperature of air that is measured using a thermometer that does not have any moisture on it. Here are some key points to remember about dry bulb:

- Dry bulb temperature is the temperature of air as measured by an ordinary thermometer that is not moistened.
- It is a basic measure of air temperature and is the most commonly used temperature measurement in the field of air conditioning and refrigeration.
- Dry bulb temperature is used in calculating the sensible heat of air, which is the heat that is added or removed from the air without changing its moisture content.
- The dry bulb temperature is used to determine the cooling load of a space or building, which is the amount of heat that must be removed from the space to achieve a comfortable indoor environment.
- It is also used to determine the performance of air conditioning and refrigeration systems, as it is an important factor in determining the efficiency of these systems.
- The dry bulb temperature is measured using a thermometer that is not moistened, and is typically measured in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
- The dry bulb temperature can be affected by a number of factors, including the humidity level, the amount of solar radiation, and the presence of other heat sources or sinks in the environment.
- In air conditioning and refrigeration systems, the dry bulb temperature is often measured in conjunction with the wet bulb temperature, which is a measure of the temperature of air as measured by a thermometer that is moistened with water.
- Together, the dry bulb and wet bulb temperature can be used to determine the relative humidity of the air, which is an important factor in determining the comfort level of the indoor environment.
- In summary, the dry bulb temperature is a fundamental measurement in the field of refrigeration and air conditioning, and is used to determine the cooling load, performance, and efficiency of air conditioning and refrigeration systems.
