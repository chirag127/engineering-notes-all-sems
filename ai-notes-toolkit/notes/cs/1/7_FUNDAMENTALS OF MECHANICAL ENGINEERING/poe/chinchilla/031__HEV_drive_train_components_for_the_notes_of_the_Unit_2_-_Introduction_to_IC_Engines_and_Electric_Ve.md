### HEV Drive Train Components

In a Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV), the drive train consists of several components that work together to deliver power from the engine(s) to the wheels. Here are the main components of an HEV drive train:

1. Internal Combustion Engine (ICE): An ICE is the primary power source in an HEV. It is used to generate electricity and/or power the wheels directly. The engine can be a gasoline or diesel engine, and it is usually smaller than the engine in a traditional car.

2. Electric Motor: The electric motor is used to provide additional power to the wheels when needed, and it also acts as a generator to recharge the battery. The motor is usually powered by a battery pack or by the ICE.

3. Transmission: The transmission is used to transfer power from the engine and/or electric motor to the wheels. In an HEV, the transmission is usually a continuously variable transmission (CVT), which allows for smooth and efficient power delivery.

4. Battery Pack: The battery pack is used to store electricity to power the electric motor and other components of the car, such as the air conditioning and headlights. The battery pack is usually located in the rear of the car, and it can be charged through regenerative braking or by plugging it in to an external power source.

5. Power Control Unit (PCU): The PCU is responsible for managing the flow of power between the engine, electric motor, and battery pack. It also controls the transmission and other components of the car.

6. Regenerative Braking System: The regenerative braking system is used to capture energy from the wheels when the car is braking, and it converts that energy into electricity to recharge the battery. This helps to improve the overall efficiency of the car.

7. Inverter: The inverter is used to convert the DC electricity from the battery pack into AC electricity to power the electric motor. It also converts the AC electricity from the electric motor into DC electricity to recharge the battery.

These are the main components of an HEV drive train. Understanding how these components work together is key to understanding how an HEV operates and how to maintain it.
