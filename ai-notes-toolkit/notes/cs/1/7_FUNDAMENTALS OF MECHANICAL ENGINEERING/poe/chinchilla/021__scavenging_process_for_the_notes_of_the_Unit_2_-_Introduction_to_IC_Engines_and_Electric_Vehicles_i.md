### Scavenging Process for the Notes of Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the Subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering

The scavenging process is an important aspect of internal combustion engines. It involves the removal of exhaust gases from the combustion chamber and the introduction of fresh air or fuel-air mixture for the next cycle of combustion. In this unit, we will study the scavenging process in detail. Here are some important points to remember:

- Scavenging is the process of removing the burnt gases from the combustion chamber and replacing them with fresh air or fuel-air mixture.
- The scavenging process is important because it affects the performance and efficiency of the engine.
- There are two types of scavenging processes: internal and external scavenging.
- Internal scavenging is used in two-stroke engines, where the incoming fuel-air mixture enters the combustion chamber and pushes the exhaust gases out through the exhaust port.
- External scavenging is used in four-stroke engines, where the exhaust gases are removed through the exhaust valve and the incoming fuel-air mixture enters through the intake valve.
- In two-stroke engines, the scavenging process is controlled by the position of the piston and the design of the ports.
- In four-stroke engines, the scavenging process is controlled by the camshaft and the opening and closing of the intake and exhaust valves.
- The scavenging process can be affected by factors such as engine speed, temperature, pressure, and the design of the engine.
- Improper scavenging can lead to poor engine performance, high fuel consumption, and increased emissions.
- There are various techniques used to improve the scavenging process, such as using tuned exhaust systems, variable valve timing, and turbocharging.
- The scavenging process is an important aspect of engine design and plays a crucial role in the performance and efficiency of internal combustion engines.

In conclusion, the scavenging process is an important aspect of internal combustion engines and plays a crucial role in their performance and efficiency. It is essential to understand the different types of scavenging processes and the factors that affect them to design and operate efficient engines.
