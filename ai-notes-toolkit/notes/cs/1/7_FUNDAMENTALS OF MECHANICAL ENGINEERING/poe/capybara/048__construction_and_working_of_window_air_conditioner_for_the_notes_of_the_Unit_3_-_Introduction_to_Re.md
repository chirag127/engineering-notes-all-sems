### Construction and Working of Window Air Conditioner

Window air conditioners are widely used in households and small offices due to their compact size, ease of installation, and cost-effectiveness. In this section, we will discuss the construction and working of a window air conditioner.

#### Construction

A window air conditioner consists of the following components:

1. **Compressor:** It compresses the refrigerant gas and pumps it to the condenser.

2. **Condenser:** It is a heat exchanger that removes heat from the refrigerant gas and converts it into a high-pressure liquid.

3. **Expansion Valve:** It is a small orifice that regulates the flow of refrigerant from the high-pressure side to the low-pressure side.

4. **Evaporator:** It is a heat exchanger located inside the room that absorbs heat from the room and converts the liquid refrigerant into a low-pressure gas.

5. **Fan:** It circulates the air inside the room and blows it over the evaporator and condenser.

6. **Filter:** It filters out dust and other impurities from the air before it enters the evaporator.

#### Working

The working of a window air conditioner can be explained in the following steps:

1. The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas and pumps it to the condenser.

2. The condenser removes heat from the refrigerant gas and converts it into a high-pressure liquid.

3. The high-pressure liquid refrigerant passes through the expansion valve, where it is throttled to a low-pressure liquid.

4. The low-pressure liquid refrigerant enters the evaporator, where it absorbs heat from the room and converts into a low-pressure gas.

5. The fan blows the air over the evaporator, where the heat is absorbed, and the cooled air is blown back into the room.

6. The low-pressure gas refrigerant is then pumped back to the compressor, where the cycle repeats.

In conclusion, a window air conditioner uses the principles of refrigeration to cool the air inside a room. It consists of various components such as compressor, condenser, expansion valve, evaporator, fan, and filter. Understanding the construction and working of a window air conditioner is essential for anyone studying refrigeration and air conditioning.
