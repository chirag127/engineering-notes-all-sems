### Construction and Working of Two stroke and four stroke SI & CI engine

IC engines are widely used in automobiles, generators, and other machinery. The two major types of IC engines are two-stroke and four-stroke engines. In this unit, we will discuss the construction and working of these engines in detail.

#### Two-stroke SI engine

- A two-stroke SI engine is a type of internal combustion engine that completes a power cycle in only two strokes of the piston.
- The engine has a crankcase, which serves as a pump for the air-fuel mixture.
- The air-fuel mixture is drawn into the crankcase during the upward stroke of the piston and compressed during the downward stroke.
- During the next upward stroke of the piston, the compressed air-fuel mixture is forced into the combustion chamber through the transfer ports.
- The fuel is ignited by the spark plug, and the expanding gases push the piston down, completing the power stroke.
- The exhaust gases are then expelled through the exhaust port during the upward stroke of the piston.

#### Four-stroke SI engine

- A four-stroke SI engine is a type of internal combustion engine that completes a power cycle in four strokes of the piston.
- The engine has four main components: an intake valve, a compression stroke, a power stroke, and an exhaust valve.
- During the first stroke, the intake valve opens, and the piston moves downward, drawing in the air-fuel mixture.
- During the second stroke, the piston moves upward, compressing the air-fuel mixture.
- During the third stroke, the spark plug ignites the air-fuel mixture, causing an explosion that pushes the piston downward, producing power.
- During the fourth stroke, the exhaust valve opens, and the piston moves upward, expelling the exhaust gases.

#### Two-stroke CI engine

- A two-stroke CI engine is a type of internal combustion engine that completes a power cycle in only two strokes of the piston.
- The engine has no separate pump for the air-fuel mixture, and the fuel is injected directly into the combustion chamber.
- During the upward stroke of the piston, the fuel is injected into the combustion chamber, and the air is compressed.
- During the downward stroke of the piston, the fuel is ignited, and the expanding gases push the piston down, completing the power stroke.
- The exhaust gases are then expelled through the exhaust port during the upward stroke of the piston.

#### Four-stroke CI engine

- A four-stroke CI engine is a type of internal combustion engine that completes a power cycle in four strokes of the piston.
- The engine has four main components: an intake valve, a compression stroke, a power stroke, and an exhaust valve.
- During the first stroke, the intake valve opens, and the piston moves downward, drawing in the air.
- During the second stroke, the piston moves upward, compressing the air.
- During the third stroke, the fuel is injected into the combustion chamber, and the air-fuel mixture is ignited, causing an explosion that pushes the piston downward, producing power.
- During the fourth stroke, the exhaust valve opens, and the piston moves upward, expelling the exhaust gases.

In conclusion, the construction and working of two-stroke and four-stroke SI & CI engines are different. Understanding the differences between these engines is crucial to the design and operation of IC engines.
