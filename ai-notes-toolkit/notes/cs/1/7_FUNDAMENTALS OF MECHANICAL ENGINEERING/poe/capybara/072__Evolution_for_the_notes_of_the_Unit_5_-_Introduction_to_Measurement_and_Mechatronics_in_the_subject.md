### Evolution

- The evolution of measurement dates back to ancient civilizations where people used body parts as a reference for measurement.
- The standardization of measurement units started in the 18th century during the French Revolution where the metric system was introduced.
- The introduction of electronic devices and computers revolutionized the field of measurement and introduced the concept of mechatronics.
- The use of sensors and actuators in mechatronics has improved the accuracy and precision of measurements.
- The development of microprocessors and microcontrollers has made it possible to automate the measurement process and integrate it with other systems.
- The evolution of measurement has led to the development of modern technologies such as robotics, automation, and control systems.
- The integration of measurement and mechatronics has opened up new possibilities in various fields such as manufacturing, healthcare, and space exploration.
- The future of measurement and mechatronics is expected to bring even more advancements in the field of engineering and technology.
