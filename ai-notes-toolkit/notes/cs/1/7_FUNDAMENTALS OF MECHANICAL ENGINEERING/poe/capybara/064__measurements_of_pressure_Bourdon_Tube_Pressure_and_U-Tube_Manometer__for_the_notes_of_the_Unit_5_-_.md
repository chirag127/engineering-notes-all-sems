### Measurements of Pressure (Bourdon Tube Pressure and U-Tube Manometer)

In the field of mechanical engineering, pressure measurement is a crucial aspect, and two of the most commonly used devices for measuring pressure are Bourdon tube pressure gauges and U-tube manometers. Here are some important points to keep in mind while studying the measurements of pressure:

- **Bourdon Tube Pressure Gauge**
    - A Bourdon tube pressure gauge is a mechanical device used for measuring pressure.
    - It consists of a curved, tube-like structure that is flattened into an oval or elliptical shape.
    - As the pressure increases, the tube tries to return to its original, circular shape, which is then translated into the movement of the gauge's needle.
    - Bourdon tube pressure gauges can be used to measure both high and low pressures, depending on their design.
    - They are commonly used in applications like hydraulic systems, steam boilers, and gas cylinders.

- **U-Tube Manometer**
    - A U-tube manometer is a device used for measuring pressure or pressure differences.
    - It consists of a U-shaped tube filled with a liquid, such as water or mercury.
    - One end of the tube is connected to the pressure source, while the other end is open to the atmosphere.
    - The difference in pressure between the two ends of the tube causes the liquid to move and create a height difference between the two sides of the U-tube.
    - The height difference is then used to calculate the pressure difference.
    - U-tube manometers are commonly used in applications like measuring blood pressure, measuring vacuum pressure, and measuring the pressure drop across filters.

These are some of the basic points to keep in mind while studying the measurements of pressure using Bourdon tube pressure gauges and U-tube manometers. Understanding the working principles and applications of these devices is crucial for any mechanical engineer, and it is important to have a clear understanding of these concepts to excel in the field.
