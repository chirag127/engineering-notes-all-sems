
### Strain (Bonded and Unbonded Strain Gauge)

Strain is a measure of the deformation of a material due to an applied force. Strain gauges are devices used to measure strain in a material. They can be either bonded or unbonded.

**Bonded Strain Gauge**

A bonded strain gauge is a device that is directly attached to the material being tested. It is made up of a thin metal foil, which is attached to the material using an adhesive. The strain gauge is then connected to a measuring device, such as a strain gauge amplifier, which measures the change in resistance of the foil as the material is strained.

**Unbonded Strain Gauge**

An unbonded strain gauge is a device that is not attached directly to the material being tested. Instead, it is attached to a support structure, such as a steel frame, and the strain in the material is measured indirectly by measuring the strain in the support structure. This type of strain gauge is often used in applications where the material being tested cannot be directly accessed, such as in pipes and tanks.
