
### Wet Bulb

* The wet bulb temperature is the temperature that is read by a thermometer when it is covered with a wet cloth or wick.
* The wet bulb temperature is lower than the dry bulb temperature because the evaporation of water from the wet cloth or wick causes cooling.
* The wet bulb temperature is used to measure the humidity of the air.
* In air-conditioning and refrigeration, the wet bulb temperature is used to calculate the cooling capacity of the system.
* The wet bulb temperature is also used to calculate the dew point temperature, which is the temperature at which the air is saturated with water vapor.
