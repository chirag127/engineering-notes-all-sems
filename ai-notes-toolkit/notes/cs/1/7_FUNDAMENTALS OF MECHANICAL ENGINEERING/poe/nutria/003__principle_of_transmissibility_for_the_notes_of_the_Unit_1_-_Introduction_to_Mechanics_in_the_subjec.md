
### Principle of Transmissibility

1. Transmissibility is a mechanical engineering principle that states that the forces and moments acting on a system are equal to the forces and moments transmitted to the system. 
2. This principle is used to analyze the behavior of machines and systems under load, and to determine the forces and moments that the components of a system must withstand. 
3. Transmissibility can be used to calculate the forces and moments that are transmitted through a system when a given load is applied. 
4. It is also used to determine the effects of friction, inertia, and other forces on a system. 
5. Transmissibility is an important concept in the study of mechanics, and it is essential for understanding the behavior of machines and systems under load.
