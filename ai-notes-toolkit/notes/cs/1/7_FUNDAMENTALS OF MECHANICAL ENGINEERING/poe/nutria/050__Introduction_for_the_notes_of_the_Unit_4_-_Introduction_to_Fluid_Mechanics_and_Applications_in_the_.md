
### Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications 

1. Fluid mechanics is a branch of physics that deals with the behavior of fluids (liquids and gases) and their interactions with surfaces. 
2. It is a sub-discipline of mechanics, which is the study of the effects of forces on objects. 
3. Fluid mechanics is an important part of mechanical engineering, as it is used to understand and design systems involving liquids and gases. 
4. Fluid mechanics is used to analyze and design a wide range of systems, from aircraft to water treatment plants. 
5. In fluid mechanics, different properties of fluids such as pressure, velocity, and density are studied. 
6. Fluid dynamics is the study of the motion of fluids and their effects on solid objects. 
7. It involves the study of the forces that act on a fluid and the behavior of the fluid in response to those forces. 
8. Applications of fluid mechanics include aircraft design, the design of water treatment plants, and the design of pipelines and other systems involving the transport of fluids.
