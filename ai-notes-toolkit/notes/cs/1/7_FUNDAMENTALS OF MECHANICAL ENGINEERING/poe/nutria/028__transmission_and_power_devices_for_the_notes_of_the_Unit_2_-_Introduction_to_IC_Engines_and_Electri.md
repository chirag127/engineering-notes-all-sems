
### Transmission and Power Devices for Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

* Internal Combustion Engines (ICEs) are powered by a variety of fuel sources, including gasoline, diesel, and natural gas. The fuel is combusted inside the engine, creating power which is then used to move the vehicle. 
* Electric Vehicles (EVs) are powered by electric motors which draw power from a battery or fuel cell. EVs are more efficient than ICEs and can be powered by renewable energy sources.
* Transmissions are used to transfer power from the engine to the wheels. Manual transmissions are operated by the driver, while automatic transmissions shift gears without the driver's input. 
* Power devices are used to control the amount of power generated by the engine, and can include throttles, governors, and turbochargers.
