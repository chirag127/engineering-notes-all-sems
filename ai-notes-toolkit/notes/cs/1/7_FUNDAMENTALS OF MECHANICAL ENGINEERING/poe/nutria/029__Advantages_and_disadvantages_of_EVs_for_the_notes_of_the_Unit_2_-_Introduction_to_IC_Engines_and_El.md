
### Advantages and Disadvantages of EVs

**Advantages:**
- Low cost of operation due to lower fuel costs.
- Low emissions and no need for exhaust systems.
- Quieter operation than traditional internal combustion engines.
- Improved acceleration and torque compared to internal combustion engines.
- Improved range and charging times due to advances in battery technology.

**Disadvantages:**
- High initial costs due to the cost of the battery.
- Limited range compared to traditional internal combustion engines.
- Longer charging times than traditional internal combustion engines.
- Limited availability of charging points in some areas.
- High maintenance costs due to the complexity of the technology.
