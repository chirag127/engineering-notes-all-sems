
### Construction and Working of Window Air Conditioner

1. Window air conditioners are self-contained units that are usually installed in a window or wall opening.
2. The main components of a window air conditioner are the compressor, condenser, expansion valve, and evaporator.
3. The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas, which increases its temperature and pressure.
4. The hot, high-pressure gas then flows through the condenser, where it is cooled and converted back into a liquid.
5. The liquid then passes through the expansion valve and is evaporated in the evaporator.
6. The evaporator absorbs heat from the air in the room, cooling it down.
7. The cooled air is then blown back into the room.
8. The refrigerant gas then returns to the compressor, where the cycle begins again.
