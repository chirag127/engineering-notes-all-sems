
### Comfort Conditions for Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

* Comfort conditions are defined as the desired environment for human comfort, which is usually a temperature range between 18-25 degrees Celsius and a relative humidity between 40-60%.
* Refrigeration and air-conditioning systems are used to create these comfort conditions.
* Refrigeration systems use a refrigerant to absorb heat from the environment and transfer it to another location, thus cooling the environment.
* Air-conditioning systems use a refrigerant to absorb heat from the environment and transfer it to an outdoor unit, thus cooling the environment.
* The temperature and humidity of the environment can be controlled using a thermostat, which is a device that senses the temperature and humidity of the environment and adjusts the cooling system accordingly.
* Proper maintenance and servicing of the refrigeration and air-conditioning systems is essential to ensure that the comfort conditions are maintained.
