
### Refrigeration for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a space, object or substance in order to lower its temperature.
2. Air-conditioning is the process of controlling the temperature, humidity, air circulation and air cleanliness of an environment.
3. Refrigeration and air-conditioning systems are used to maintain comfortable temperatures in buildings, transportation vehicles and storage facilities.
4. Refrigeration and air-conditioning systems use a variety of components, including compressors, evaporators, condensers and expansion devices.
5. Refrigerants are substances that are used to transfer heat from one location to another.
6. The efficiency of a refrigeration or air-conditioning system is determined by the type of refrigerant used, the size of the system and the amount of heat it must remove.
7. Proper maintenance and servicing of a refrigeration or air-conditioning system is essential for its efficient operation.
