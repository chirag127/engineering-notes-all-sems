
### Resultant of Force System - Concurrent and Non-Concurrent Coplanar Forces 

1. A force system is a combination of two or more forces acting on a body. 
2. The resultant of a force system is the single force that has the same effect as the combination of the forces. 
3. When the forces in a system are concurrent, they all pass through the same point. 
4. The resultant of a concurrent force system can be determined by the parallelogram law of forces or the triangle law of forces. 
5. The parallelogram law states that the resultant of two concurrent forces is equal to the diagonal of the parallelogram formed by the two forces. 
6. The triangle law states that the resultant of two concurrent forces is equal to the sum of the two forces and the angle between them. 
7. When the forces in a system are non-concurrent, they do not pass through the same point. 
8. The resultant of a non-concurrent force system can be determined by the polygon law of forces. 
9. The polygon law states that the resultant of two non-concurrent forces is equal to the sum of the two forces and the angle between them. 
10. The magnitude and direction of the resultant of a force system can be determined by drawing a free body diagram.
