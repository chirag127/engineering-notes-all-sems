
### Poisson's Ratio

* Poisson's ratio is a dimensionless number that describes the ratio of the transverse contraction strain to the axial extension strain in the direction of the applied load. 
* It is denoted by the Greek symbol $\nu$ and is defined as the negative ratio of transverse strain $\epsilon_t$ to axial strain $\epsilon_a$:

$$\nu = -\frac{\epsilon_t}{\epsilon_a}$$

* Poisson's ratio is a measure of the material's compressibility and is an important parameter in the determination of the elastic constants of a material.
* It is also used to calculate the stress-strain relationship in a material and is used in the design of mechanical components.
* The value of Poisson's ratio for most materials lies between 0 and 0.5, with most metals having a value of around 0.3.
