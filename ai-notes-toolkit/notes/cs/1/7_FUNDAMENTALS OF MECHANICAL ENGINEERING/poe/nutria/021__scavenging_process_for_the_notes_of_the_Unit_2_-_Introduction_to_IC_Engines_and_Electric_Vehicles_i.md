
### Scavenging Process for the Notes of Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Scavenging is the process of removing exhaust gases from the cylinders of an internal combustion engine. 
2. It is important for the efficient operation of an engine as it allows for fresh air to be drawn into the cylinders for the combustion process.
3. The scavenging process is generally accomplished by a two-stroke cycle engine, which uses the piston to draw in the fresh air and push out the exhaust gases. 
4. In a four-stroke cycle engine, the scavenging process is achieved by the use of a positive displacement blower or a turbocharger. 
5. The scavenging process is also used in electric vehicles to remove the heat generated by the motor during operation. 
6. The scavenging process helps to ensure that the motor does not overheat, which can lead to damage and reduced efficiency. 
7. The scavenging process also helps to reduce emissions from the vehicle, as the exhaust gases are removed before they are released into the atmosphere.
