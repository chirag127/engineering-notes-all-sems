
### Coefficient of Performance for Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning

1. The coefficient of performance (COP) is a measure of the efficiency of a refrigeration system or air conditioning system. It is defined as the ratio of the cooling capacity of the system to the power input.

2. The COP can be calculated using the following formula: COP = Cooling Capacity / Power Input.

3. The higher the COP, the more efficient the system is. Generally, a COP of 3 or higher is considered to be very efficient.

4. The COP of a system can be improved by increasing the efficiency of the compressor, improving the insulation of the system, and using a higher pressure refrigerant.

5. The COP of a system can also be affected by the ambient temperature, the size of the system, and the type of refrigerant used.
