
### Factor of Safety in Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

* Factor of Safety (FoS) is an important concept in mechanical engineering, as it helps engineers ensure the safety and reliability of their designs.
* FoS is defined as the ratio of the ultimate strength of a material to the working stress it is subjected to.
* In order to calculate the FoS, engineers must consider the material properties, the geometry of the component, and the type of loading it will be subjected to.
* It is important to note that FoS is not a static value, and can change depending on the type of loading, the material properties, and the geometry of the component.
* FoS is typically expressed as a number greater than 1, with higher values indicating a higher margin of safety.
* When designing a component, engineers must consider the FoS and ensure that the component is designed to withstand the expected loading conditions.
