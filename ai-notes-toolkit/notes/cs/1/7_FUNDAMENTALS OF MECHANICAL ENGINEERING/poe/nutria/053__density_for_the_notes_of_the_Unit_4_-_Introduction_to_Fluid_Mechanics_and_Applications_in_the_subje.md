
### Density for Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering

* Density is a measure of mass per unit volume. It is an intrinsic physical property of a material and is usually expressed in kilograms per cubic meter (kg/m3).
* Density is an important factor in determining the behavior of fluids, as it affects the speed of sound, pressure, and other properties.
* The density of a fluid is determined by its composition, temperature, and pressure.
* Density is an important factor in determining the stability of a fluid, as well as its ability to mix with other fluids.
* In fluid mechanics, density is used to calculate the force of gravity, buoyancy, and other forces.
* Density is also used to calculate the Reynolds number, which is a measure of the relative importance of inertia and viscous forces in a flow.
* Density is also used to calculate the pressure drop in a pipe or channel, and the friction factor in a pipe.
