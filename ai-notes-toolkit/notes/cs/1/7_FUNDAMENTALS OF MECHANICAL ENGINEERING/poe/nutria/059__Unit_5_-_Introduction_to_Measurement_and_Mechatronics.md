
## Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics

* Measurement is the process of determining the size, amount, or degree of something. It is used in a variety of fields, including engineering, physics, chemistry, and mathematics.
* Mechatronics is the combination of mechanics and electronics. It is a field of engineering that combines mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering to create systems that are more efficient and reliable.
* Measurement systems are used to measure physical quantities, such as temperature, pressure, and force. They can also be used to measure abstract quantities, such as time, speed, and distance.
* Mechatronic systems are used to control and monitor mechanical processes. They can be used to control robots, industrial machines, and other automated systems.
* Measurement and mechatronic systems are used in a variety of industries, including automotive, aerospace, medical, and manufacturing. They are also used in research and development.
