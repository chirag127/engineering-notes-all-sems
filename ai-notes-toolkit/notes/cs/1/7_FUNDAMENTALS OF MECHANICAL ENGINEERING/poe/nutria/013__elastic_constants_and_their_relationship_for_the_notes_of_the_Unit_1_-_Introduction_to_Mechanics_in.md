
### Elastic Constants and Their Relationship

1. Elastic constants are physical quantities used to describe the mechanical behavior of a material when it is subjected to a force.
2. The most common elastic constants are Young's modulus (E), shear modulus (G), and Poisson's ratio (ν).
3. Young's modulus (E) is a measure of the stiffness of a material, and is equal to the ratio of the tensile stress to the strain.
4. Shear modulus (G) is a measure of the resistance of a material to shear stress. It is equal to the ratio of the shear stress to the shear strain.
5. Poisson's ratio (ν) is a measure of the lateral contraction of a material when it is subjected to a tensile force. It is equal to the ratio of the lateral strain to the longitudinal strain.
6. The relationship between the elastic constants is given by the following equation:

$$E = 2G(1 + \nu)$$
