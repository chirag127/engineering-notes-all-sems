
### Force (Proving Ring)

- Proving ring is a device used to measure the force applied to an object.
- It is a type of dynamometer, which is a device used to measure forces and torques.
- The proving ring works by applying a known force to an object and measuring the resulting displacement or deformation.
- Proving rings are used in a variety of applications, such as testing the strength of materials, measuring the force of a motor, and calibrating other force measuring instruments.

### Torques (Prony Brake Dynamometer)

- Prony brake dynamometers are devices used to measure the torque applied to an object.
- They work by applying a known torque to an object and measuring the resulting displacement or deformation.
- Prony brake dynamometers are used in a variety of applications, such as testing the strength of materials, measuring the torque of a motor, and calibrating other torque measuring instruments.
- Prony brake dynamometers are also commonly used in automotive and aerospace applications.
