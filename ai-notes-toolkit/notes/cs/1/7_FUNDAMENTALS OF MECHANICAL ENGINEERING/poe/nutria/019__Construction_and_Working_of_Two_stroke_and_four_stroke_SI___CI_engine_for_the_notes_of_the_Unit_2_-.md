
### Construction and Working of Two stroke and four stroke SI & CI engine

* Two-stroke engines are internal combustion engines that complete the entire four-stroke cycle in only two strokes of the piston. They are simpler and lighter than four-stroke engines and can produce more power for their size.
* Four-stroke engines are the most common type of internal combustion engine. They complete the four-stroke cycle in four piston strokes, which includes intake, compression, combustion, and exhaust. Four-stroke engines are more efficient than two-stroke engines and have a longer lifespan.
* The SI (Spark Ignition) engine is an internal combustion engine that uses a spark plug to ignite the fuel-air mixture. It is most commonly used in gasoline-powered vehicles.
* The CI (Compression Ignition) engine is an internal combustion engine that uses compression to ignite the fuel-air mixture. It is most commonly used in diesel-powered vehicles.
* In a two-stroke SI engine, the intake, compression, combustion, and exhaust occur in two piston strokes. The intake stroke draws in a fuel-air mixture, which is then compressed in the compression stroke. The spark plug ignites the fuel-air mixture, and the combustion stroke pushes the piston down. The exhaust stroke then pushes out the exhaust gases.
* In a four-stroke SI engine, the intake, compression, combustion, and exhaust occur in four piston strokes. The intake stroke draws in a fuel-air mixture, which is then compressed in the compression stroke. The spark plug ignites the fuel-air mixture, and the combustion stroke pushes the piston down. The exhaust stroke then pushes out the exhaust gases.
* In a two-stroke CI engine, the intake, compression, combustion, and exhaust occur in two piston strokes. The intake stroke draws in a fuel-air mixture, which is then compressed in the compression stroke. The compression of the fuel-air mixture causes it to ignite, and the combustion stroke pushes the piston down. The exhaust stroke then pushes out the exhaust gases.
* In a four-stroke CI engine, the intake, compression, combustion, and exhaust occur in four piston strokes. The intake stroke draws in a fuel-air mixture, which is then compressed in the compression stroke. The compression of the fuel-air mixture causes it to ignite, and the combustion stroke pushes the piston down. The exhaust stroke then pushes out the exhaust gases.
