
### Dry Bulb

* The dry bulb temperature is the temperature of the air as measured by a thermometer exposed to the air, but shielded from radiation and moisture. 
* It is the most common form of measurement used in meteorology and HVAC applications. 
* In HVAC applications, the dry bulb temperature is used to calculate the humidity ratio and the dew point temperature. 
* The dry bulb temperature is also used to calculate the sensible heat load, which is the amount of heat energy that needs to be added or removed from a space to maintain a comfortable environment. 
* The dry bulb temperature can be measured with a thermometer, or it can be estimated by measuring the wet bulb temperature and then subtracting the wet bulb depression.
