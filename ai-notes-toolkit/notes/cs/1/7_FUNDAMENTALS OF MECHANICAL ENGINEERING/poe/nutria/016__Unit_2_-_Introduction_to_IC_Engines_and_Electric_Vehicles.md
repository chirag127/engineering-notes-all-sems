
## Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

1. Internal Combustion Engines (ICE) are engines that burn fuel to generate power. They are used in a variety of applications, including automobiles, boats, aircraft, and other machinery.
2. ICEs can be powered by gasoline, diesel, or other fuels. They are typically more efficient than electric engines, but produce more emissions.
3. Electric Vehicles (EVs) are powered by electric motors and batteries. They are becoming increasingly popular due to their low emissions and high efficiency.
4. EVs are becoming more affordable and have a range of features that make them attractive to consumers. They are also becoming more widely available in many countries.
5. EV technology is constantly evolving, with new features and improvements being made all the time. This includes advances in battery technology, charging infrastructure, and autonomous driving technology.
6. ICEs and EVs both have advantages and disadvantages, and the decision of which type of vehicle to buy depends on individual needs and preferences.
