### Ton of Refrigeration

- One ton of refrigeration is the amount of cooling obtained by melting one ton of ice in 24 hours. It is equal to 288,000 BTU/day or 12,000 BTU/hr.
- It is a unit of capacity and not actual work done. It only specifies the amount of heat that can be removed by the refrigeration system.
- One ton of refrigeration is equal to the heat removal required to freeze one ton of water at 32°F (0°C) into ice at 32°F (0°C) in 24 hours.
- It is commonly used as a measure of the capacity of refrigeration machines and air conditioning systems. Larger the ton of refrigeration rating, greater is the capacity of the system to remove heat.
- The amount of refrigeration in terms of tons required depends upon factors like difference between desired and surrounding temperatures, heat leakage, etc.
