### Varignon's theorem

Varignon's theorem states that the moment of a force about any point is equal to the sum of the moments of its components about the same point.

* Prove Varignon's theorem given a force **F** acting at a distance **d** from a point O and resolved into two rectangular components **F1** and **F2** acting at distances **d1** and **d2** from O respectively.
* Moment of **F** about O = **Fd**
* Moments of **F1** and **F2** about O are **F1d1** and **F2d2** respectively.
* By theorem, **Fd = F1d1 + F2d2**
* Hence proved.

Varignon's theorem is useful in analyzing the moment of a force and finding the net moment of concurrent forces about a point. It simplifies the task of finding the net moment to finding the sum of moments of individual components.
