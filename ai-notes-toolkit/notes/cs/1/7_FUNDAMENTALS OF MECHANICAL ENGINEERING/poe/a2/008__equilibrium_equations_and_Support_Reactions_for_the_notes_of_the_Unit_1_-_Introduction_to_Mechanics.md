### Equilibrium Equations and Support Reactions

- Equilibrium equations: The sum of forces and sum of moments acting on a body must be zero for equilibrium.
- Sum of forces in x-direction = 0
- Sum of forces in y-direction = 0
- Sum of moments about any point = 0
- Support reactions: The reactions at the supports/constraints of a structure can be determined using equilibrium equations.
- Number of unknown support reactions <= number of equilibrium equations
- If more number of unknowns, the system is statically indeterminate and additional equations are required to solve for reactions.
- For 2D structures with pin, roller and fixed supports, the reactions can be determined by applying equilibrium equations.
- For 3D frames, the 3D equilibrium equations can be applied to determine the support reactions.
