### HEV drive train components

1. Internal Combustion Engine (ICE): The ICE is the primary source of power in a hybrid electric vehicle. It powers the wheels via a transmission and driveshaft.
2. Electric Motor: The electric motor provides supplemental power to the wheels and also acts as a generator during braking to recharge the batteries.
3. Power Split Device: The power split device combines and splits the power from the ICE and electric motor. It is usually a planetary gear set that allows for varying power splits.
4. Generator: The generator is connected to the ICE and produces electricity to charge the batteries when the ICE is running. It is essentially an electric motor running in reverse.
5. Batteries: The batteries store energy from the ICE and electric motor for use when needed. They provide power to the electric motor to supplement the ICE.
6. Inverter: The inverter converts the DC power from the batteries to AC to drive the electric motor. It also converts AC from the electric motor to DC to charge the batteries.

The components work together in a coordinated fashion controlled by the vehicle's computer to achieve optimal efficiency and performance. The power split between the ICE and electric motor can be varied based on driving conditions to maximize fuel economy. The HEV drive train provides the benefits of both gasoline and electric vehicles.
