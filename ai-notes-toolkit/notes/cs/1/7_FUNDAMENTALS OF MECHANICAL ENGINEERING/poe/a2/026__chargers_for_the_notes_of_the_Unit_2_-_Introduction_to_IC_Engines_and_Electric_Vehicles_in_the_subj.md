### Chargers for IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

1. IC Engines
- Electrical system: Battery, alternator, voltage regulator, spark plugs, ignition coil, etc.
- Battery: Lead-acid battery, maintains voltage during engine cranking and provides power to electrical equipment when the alternator is not functioning.
- Alternator: Generates alternating current (AC) electricity to charge the battery and power the electrical system. Belt or gear driven by the engine.
- Voltage regulator: Controls the alternator's output voltage to prevent overcharging or undercharging the battery.

2. Electric Vehicles
- Traction battery: Lithium-ion battery, supplies electric energy to the drive motor.
- On-board charger: Converts AC power to DC power to charge the traction battery.
- Off-board charger: Charging station with an AC-DC converter, charges the EV battery.
- Fast charging: Uses high-power chargers to charge the EV battery up to 80% capacity in 30 minutes.
