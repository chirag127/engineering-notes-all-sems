### Dew Point Temperatures

- Dew point temperature is the temperature at which the water vapor in air condenses into liquid water.
- As the temperature of air decreases, its capacity to hold water vapor decreases. When the air reaches its saturation point, the water vapor starts condensing into liquid water. The temperature at which this condensation occurs is known as the dew point temperature.
- The dew point temperature depends on the amount of water vapor present in the air. Air with higher humidity will have a higher dew point temperature than dry air.
- Knowledge of dew point temperatures is important in refrigeration and air conditioning applications as it determines the possibility of condensation forming on cold surfaces. Condensation can be avoided by maintaining surfaces above the dew point temperature of the surrounding air.
- Dew point temperatures can be measured using dew point meters or can be calculated from measurements of dry bulb and relative humidity.
