# FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Mechanics
- Statics - Study of forces acting on bodies at rest
- Dynamics - Study of forces acting on bodies in motion
- Kinematics - Study of motion without considering forces
- Kinetics - Study of forces that produce or change motion

2. Materials Science
- Properties of materials - Strength, ductility, hardness, toughness, etc.
- Classification of materials - Metals, polymers, ceramics, composites, etc.
- Processing of materials - Casting, welding, machining, etc.

3. Thermodynamics
- Laws of thermodynamics - Conservation of energy, entropy, etc.
- Heat engines - Carnot cycle, Rankine cycle, etc.
- Refrigeration and air conditioning

4. Mechanism and Machines
- Kinematic analysis of mechanisms - Velocity, acceleration, etc.
- Gears, cams, belts, etc.
- Vibrations - Free and forced vibrations, resonance, etc.
