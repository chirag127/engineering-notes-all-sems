### Poisson’s ratio for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

- Poisson’s ratio (ν) is the ratio of lateral strain to axial strain for an elastic material under uniaxial stress.
- It is a dimensionless quantity and has no units.
- For most materials, the value of Poisson's ratio ranges from 0 to 0.5.
- Materials with Poisson's ratio equal to 0 are incompressible while materials with Poisson's ratio equal to 0.5 have maximum compressibility.
- Poisson's ratio can be either positive or negative. For most materials, it is positive indicating lateral expansion upon stretching and lateral contraction upon compression.
- The sign of Poisson's ratio determines whether a material will bulge or neck under uniaxial loading.
- The value of Poisson's ratio affects the stiffness and strength of a material and is important in determining many properties of materials and structures.
