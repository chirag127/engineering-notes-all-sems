### Hookes' law

- Hooke's law states that the extension produced in an elastic body is directly proportional to the load applied, provided the elastic limit is not exceeded.
- The law can be expressed mathematically as:
F = kx
Where,
F = Force applied
x = Extension produced
k = Constant of proportionality (Spring constant)
- The spring constant depends on the properties and geometry of the material.
- Hooke's law is valid for most solid materials as long as the elastic limit is not exceeded.
- The law breaks down when the elastic limit is exceeded and the material suffers permanent deformation.
- Applications of Hooke's law include:
-- Spring
-- Stress-strain analysis of materials
-- Force sensors
-- Weighing machines
