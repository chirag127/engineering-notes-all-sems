## Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics

1. Measurement - The process of quantifying experience of the physical world. All measurements have some amount of uncertainty. Different measuring devices have different amounts of precision and accuracy.
2. Dimensional Analysis - A technique to check if an equation is dimensionally correct. It is done by checking that the units on both sides of the equation are the same. If not, the equation is wrong.
3. Significant Figures - The digits in a measurement that are meaningful. Trailing zeros are not significant if they are placeholders. Rules for calculating with significant figures:
    - Multiply/Divide: Result has the same # of significant figures as the measurement with the fewest.
    - Add/Subtract: Result has the same # of decimal places as the measurement with the fewest.
4. Error Analysis - Evaluating uncertainty in measurements and calculations. Sources of error include instrument precision, personal bias, environment, etc. Error is the uncertainty in a measurement, often denoted with +/-.
5. Mechatronics - The synergistic combination of mechanical engineering, electronic engineering, and software engineering. Mechatronic systems have mechanical, electrical, and computer components that are integrated to achieve a desired objective. Sensors and actuators are connected to a microprocessor that controls the system. Mechatronics enables more functionality, precision, and automation.
