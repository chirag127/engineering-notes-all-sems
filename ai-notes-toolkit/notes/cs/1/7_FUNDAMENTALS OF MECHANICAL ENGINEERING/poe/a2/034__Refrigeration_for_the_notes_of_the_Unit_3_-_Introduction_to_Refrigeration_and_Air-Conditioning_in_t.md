### Refrigeration

1. Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a low-temperature reservoir and rejecting it to a high-temperature reservoir.
2. The low-temperature reservoir is called the refrigerated space. The high-temperature reservoir is called the heat sink.
3. Refrigeration is used to maintain a lower temperature of a substance or an enclosed space by extracting heat. This process is used for food preservation and air conditioning or cooling applications.
4. The most common refrigerants are haloalkanes, also known as freons, like R-12, R-22, R-134a, etc. Ammonia and CO2 are also used as refrigerants for some applications.
5. The four primary components required for any refrigeration system are a compressor, a condenser, an expansion valve, and an evaporator. The refrigeration effect is produced due to the evaporation and condensation of the refrigerant.
6. The compression refrigeration cycle is the most commonly used system and involves circulating refrigerated vapor-compression cycle. The vapor-compression cycle includes evaporation, compression, condensation, expansion, and returns to evaporation. This cyclical process transfers heat from the refrigerated space to the heat sink.
