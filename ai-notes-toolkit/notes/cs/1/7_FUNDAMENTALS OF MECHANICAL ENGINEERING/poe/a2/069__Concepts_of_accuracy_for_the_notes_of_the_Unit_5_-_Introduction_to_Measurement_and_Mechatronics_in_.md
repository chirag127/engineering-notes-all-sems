### Concepts of accuracy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Accuracy - The closeness of a measured or calculated quantity to its actual (true) value. It reflects the degree of veracity of a measurement.
2. Precision - The closeness of agreement between independent test results obtained under stipulated conditions. It reflects the degree of reproducibility or repeatability of measurements.
3. Errors - The differences between measurements or calculated values and true values.
4. Systematic error - An error that is consistently in the same direction. It can be eliminated by calibration.
5. Random error - An error that varies in magnitude and direction and follows no pattern. It cannot be eliminated but can only be reduced by repeating measurements and using statistical techniques.
6. Resolution - The smallest change in input that produces a detectable change in output. It depends on the least count of the measuring instrument.
7. Sensitivity - The ratio of change in output to the corresponding change in input. It is a measure of the responsiveness or gain of a measuring system.
