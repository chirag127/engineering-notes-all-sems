### Basic definition of engine and Components for the notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

- An engine is a machine that converts the chemical energy stored in fuel into mechanical energy.
- The main components of an engine are:
    - Fuel system: Supplies fuel to the engine. Includes fuel tank, fuel pump, fuel filters, fuel injectors, carburetor, etc.
    - Air intake system: Supplies air to the engine. Includes air filter, air intake manifold, etc.
    - Exhaust system: Removes combustion byproducts from the engine. Includes exhaust manifold, catalytic converter, muffler, etc.
    - Ignition system: Provides spark to ignite the air-fuel mixture. Includes spark plugs, ignition coil, crankshaft sensor, camshaft sensor, etc.
    - Lubrication system: Supplies oil to lubricate the engine's moving parts. Includes oil pump, oil filter, oil pan, etc.
    - Cooling system: Removes excess heat from the engine. Includes water pump, radiator, fan, thermostat, etc.
    - Starting system: Starts the engine. Includes starter motor, battery, ignition switch, etc.
- The working of an engine involves intake, compression, power, and exhaust strokes which repeat in a cycle. The 4-stroke engine completes one cycle in two rotations of the crankshaft while the 2-stroke engine completes one cycle in a single rotation of the crankshaft.
