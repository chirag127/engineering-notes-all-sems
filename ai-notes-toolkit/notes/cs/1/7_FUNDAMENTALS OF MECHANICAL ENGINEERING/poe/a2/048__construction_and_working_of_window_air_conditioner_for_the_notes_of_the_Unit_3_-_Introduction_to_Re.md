### Construction and working of window air conditioner

- A window air conditioner unit has integral parts like compressor, condenser coil, expansion valve, evaporator coil and a fan.
- The compressor sucks the refrigerant vapor from the evaporator and compresses it. The high-pressure vapor then moves to the condenser coil.
- The condenser coil releases the heat to the surroundings with the help of a fan. The refrigerant changes its state from vapor to liquid.
- The liquid refrigerant then passes through the expansion valve where its pressure decreases. It enters the evaporator coil as a low-pressure liquid-vapor mixture.
- The evaporator coil absorbs the heat from the room with the help of a fan. The liquid refrigerant evaporates by absorbing this heat. The evaporation of the liquid refrigerant leads to cooling.
- The evaporated refrigerant vapor then enters the compressor to repeat the cycle.
- The regulation of the desired temperature takes place with the help of a thermostat that controls the operation of the compressor.

The construction and working of a window air conditioner unit involves the key components and a refrigeration cycle to provide cooling to a room. The thermostat regulates the temperature as per the user requirements. The fans help in heat exchange between the coils and the surroundings.
