### Advantages and disadvantages of EVs

Advantages:

- Environment friendly - Zero direct emissions
- Lower operating costs - Electricity is cheaper than gas
- Quieter ride - Absence of combustion reduces noise
- Lower maintenance - Fewer moving parts compared to ICE vehicles
- Quick acceleration - High torque at low RPM

Disadvantages:

- Higher upfront costs - Batteries are expensive
- Limited range - Limited energy density of batteries reduces range
- Long charging times - Can take hours to fully charge the batteries
- Reliance on electricity - Need robust charging infrastructure
- Performance degradation - Batteries lose capacity over time
