### Wet Bulb

- The wet bulb temperature is the temperature read by a thermometer whose bulb is covered by a wet wick.
- As the water on the wick evaporates, it cools the bulb. The rate of evaporation depends on the humidity and air temperature.
- Lower the humidity, greater is the evaporation and lower is the wet bulb temperature.
- Higher the air temperature, greater is the evaporation and lower is the wet bulb temperature.
- The difference between the dry bulb temperature and wet bulb temperature is an indicator of humidity. Larger the difference, lower is the humidity.
- Wet bulb temperature is used to determine the relative humidity using psychometric charts.
- It is also used in cooling towers and air conditioning systems to determine the effectiveness of evaporative cooling.
