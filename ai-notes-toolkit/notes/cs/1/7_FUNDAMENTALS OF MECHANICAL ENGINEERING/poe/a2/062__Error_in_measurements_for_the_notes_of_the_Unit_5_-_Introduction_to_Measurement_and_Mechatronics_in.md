### Error in measurements for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Types of Errors
- Systematic Error: Caused due to flaws in the measuring equipment or by the observer. It affects all measurements in a consistent manner, either positive or negative. Eg. Zero error, calibration error.
- Random Error: Caused due to unpredictable and uncontrollable changes in measuring conditions. Eg. Due to vibrations. It can be reduced by repeating measurements and taking average.

2. Sources of Error
- Human errors: Inaccurate reading of instrument, parallax error.
- Instrument errors: Zero error, least count error.
- Environmental errors: Due to temperature, humidity, vibrations, etc.

3. Minimizing Errors
- Use precise instruments and maintain them properly.
- Take repeated readings and calculate average.
- Ensure good working conditions minimizing environmental errors.
- Proper techniques to be followed, avoid parallax errors, take accurate readings, etc.
- Calculate percentage error and ensure it is within acceptable limits.
