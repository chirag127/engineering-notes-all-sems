### Density

- Density is defined as the mass per unit volume of a substance. It is a physical property that can be used to characterize a fluid.
- The SI unit of density is kg/m3.
- Density can be calculated using the formula:

Density = Mass / Volume

- For a homogeneous substance, density is the same throughout the volume and does not vary with location.
- The density of a fluid depends on pressure and temperature. As pressure or temperature increases, density decreases.
- Density is a useful property for identifying substances and determining whether a fluid will float or sink in another fluid. A fluid with higher density will sink in a fluid with lower density.
- The density of water is 1,000 kg/m3 at 4°C. Materials with density higher than 1,000 kg/m3 will sink in water while those with lower density will float.
