# Values, Gross & Net Calorific Value, Determination of Calorific Value by Bomb Calorimeter

## Values
- Values are principles or standards of behavior that are considered important or worthwhile.
- They can be personal, cultural, or societal and can vary between individuals and groups.
- Values can influence decision-making and behavior.

## Gross & Net Calorific Value
- Calorific value is the amount of heat energy released when a substance is burned.
- Gross calorific value (GCV) is the total amount of heat released when a substance is burned, including the heat of condensation of the water vapor produced.
- Net calorific value (NCV) is the amount of heat released when a substance is burned, excluding the heat of condensation of the water vapor produced.
- NCV is typically lower than GCV because it does not include the heat of condensation of the water vapor produced.

## Determination of Calorific Value by Bomb Calorimeter
- A bomb calorimeter is a device used to measure the calorific value of a substance.
- The substance is placed in a sealed container (the "bomb") and burned in an oxygen-rich environment.
- The heat released by the combustion is absorbed by the surrounding water and the temperature rise is measured.
- The calorific value can be calculated from the temperature rise and the heat capacity of the calorimeter.
