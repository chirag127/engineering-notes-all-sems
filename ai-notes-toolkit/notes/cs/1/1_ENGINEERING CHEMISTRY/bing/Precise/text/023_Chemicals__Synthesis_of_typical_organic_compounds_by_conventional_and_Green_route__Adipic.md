# Chemicals, Synthesis of typical organic compounds by conventional and Green route (Adipic Acid)

- Adipic acid is an organic compound that can be synthesized through both conventional and green routes.
- The conventional route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the use of benzene as the starting material. This process requires high pressure and temperature .
- In contrast, the green route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the use of glucose as the starting material. This process requires less temperature and pressure .
- Green chemistry techniques have been applied to synthesize both well-known chemical compounds by more sustainable routes and completely new materials .
- Green techniques in organic chemistry include reactions of C–H bond activation, fluorous, solid-supported, bio- and asymmetric catalysis and synthesis, use of water and other green solvents (in particular, ionic liquids (ILs)) or without any solvent, microwave-, ultrasound- and ultraviolet (UV)-assisted reactions, and use of flow reactors .
- The ideology of Green Chemistry calls for the development of new chemical reactivities and reaction conditions that can potentially provide benefits for chemical syntheses in terms of resource and energy efficiency, product selectivity, operational simplicity, and health and environmental safety .
