### 5. Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna, Mc Graw Hill Education, New Delhi

- "Engineering Chemistry" is a book written by O.G. Palanna and published by Tata McGraw Hill Publishing Pvt.Ltd. in New Delhi in 2009 .
- The book is meant for first-year undergraduate students of engineering and provides a comprehensive exposure to the fundamentals of Engineering Chemistry .
- The presentation is lucid, the language is simple, and the flow of topics is logical, along with relevant concepts that help students acquire a good command of the basics of the subject .
- The book has received positive reviews for its content and presentation  .
