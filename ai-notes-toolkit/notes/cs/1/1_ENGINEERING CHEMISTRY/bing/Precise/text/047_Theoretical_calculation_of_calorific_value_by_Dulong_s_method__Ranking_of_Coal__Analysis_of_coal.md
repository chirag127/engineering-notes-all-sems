# Theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong’s method, Ranking of Coal, Analysis of coal

## Theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong’s method
- The various formulae for calculating calorific values for coals from ultimate analyses depend essentially on a proposition due to Dulong.
- The heat of combustion of an organic compound is nearly equal to the heats of combustion of the elements in it, multiplied by their percentage content in the compound in question .

## Ranking of Coal
- Coal is ranked based on its degree of transformation from the original organic material into carbon.
- The higher the carbon content, the higher the rank of the coal.
- The ranking of coal, from lowest to highest, is lignite, sub-bituminous, bituminous, and anthracite.

## Analysis of coal
- Coal analysis is the process of determining the composition and properties of coal.
- It is used to determine the quality of the coal and its suitability for specific applications.
- Coal analysis can include measurements of the coal's moisture, volatile matter, ash, sulfur, and carbon content, as well as its heating value and other properties.
