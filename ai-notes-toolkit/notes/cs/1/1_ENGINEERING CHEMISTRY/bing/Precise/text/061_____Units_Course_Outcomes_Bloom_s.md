### Units Course Outcomes Bloom’s

1. **Units**: Units are a standard of measurement used to express physical quantities. They provide a common language for scientists and engineers to communicate their findings and ideas.

2. **Course Outcomes**: Course outcomes are the specific knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to achieve by the end of a course. They are used to assess the effectiveness of the course and to guide future improvements.

3. **Bloom’s Taxonomy**: Bloom’s Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives. It is used to design learning activities and assessments that promote higher-order thinking skills. The taxonomy consists of six levels: remembering, understanding, applying, analyzing, evaluating, and creating.
