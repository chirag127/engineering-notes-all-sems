# Thiokol, Nylon, Buna-N and Buna-S and their environmental impact on society, Speciality

- **Nylon** is the world's first entirely synthetic polymer fiber, introduced by the DuPont company in 1938. It is known for its strength, durability, and flexibility.
- **Buna-N**, also known as **nitrile rubber**, is a synthetic rubber derived from acrylonitrile (ACN) and butadiene. It is resistant to oil, fuel, and other chemicals.
- **Buna-S**, now known as **styrene butadiene rubber (SBR)**, was being produced in large quantities in Germany by 1935.
- **Thiokol** is not mentioned in the search results.
