### 1. To enable the students to understand about the Chemistry of Atomic and Molecular structure, Chemistry of:

- Atomic structure refers to the fundamental structure of an atom, including its nucleus and electrons.
- The nucleus is made up of protons and neutrons, and is surrounded by electrons in shells or orbitals.
- The number of protons in the nucleus determines the atomic number and the element to which the atom belongs.
- The arrangement of electrons in the atom's outermost shell determines its chemical properties and reactivity.
- Molecular structure refers to the arrangement of atoms in a molecule and the chemical bonds that hold them together.
- The type and strength of chemical bonds, as well as the shape of the molecule, determine its properties and reactivity.
- Understanding the chemistry of atomic and molecular structure is essential for predicting and explaining chemical reactions and the properties of substances.
