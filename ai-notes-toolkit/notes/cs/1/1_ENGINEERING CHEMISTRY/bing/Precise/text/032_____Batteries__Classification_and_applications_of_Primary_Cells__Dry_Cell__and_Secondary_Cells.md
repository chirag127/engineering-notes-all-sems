### Batteries; Classification and applications of Primary Cells (Dry Cell) and Secondary Cells

A battery is a combination of many cells, which can be divided into two types: primary and secondary cells .

- **Primary Cells**: A primary cell is a battery that is designed to be used only one time. It creates an electric current through an irreversible electrochemical reaction occurring in the cell. When a primary cell is discharged, it is discarded, and not recharged again like the secondary cell “rechargeable battery” . Dry cells and (most) alkaline batteries are examples of primary batteries .

- **Secondary Cells**: The second type of battery is rechargeable and is called a secondary battery. Examples of secondary batteries include nickel-cadmium (NiCd), lead acid, and lithium-ion batteries .
