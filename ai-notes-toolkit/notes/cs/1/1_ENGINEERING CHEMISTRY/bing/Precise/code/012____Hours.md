## Hours

- An hour is a unit of time equal to 60 minutes or 3,600 seconds.
- It is one of the seven base units in the International System of Units (SI).
- The symbol for hour is "h".
- The hour is commonly used to measure the duration of events, to express the time of day, and to measure the amount of time spent on a task.
- The concept of the hour has been used since ancient times, with the earliest known reference to the hour being in the writings of the ancient Egyptians.
- The hour is divided into 60 minutes, and each minute is divided into 60 seconds.
- The hour is also used to measure angles, with one hour being equal to 15 degrees.
- The hour is used in both the 12-hour and 24-hour clock systems.
- In the 12-hour clock system, the day is divided into two 12-hour periods, with the first period running from midnight to noon and the second period running from noon to midnight.
- In the 24-hour clock system, the day is divided into 24 hours, with each hour being numbered from 0 to 23.
- The 24-hour clock system is commonly used in military, aviation, and other fields where precise timekeeping is essential.
