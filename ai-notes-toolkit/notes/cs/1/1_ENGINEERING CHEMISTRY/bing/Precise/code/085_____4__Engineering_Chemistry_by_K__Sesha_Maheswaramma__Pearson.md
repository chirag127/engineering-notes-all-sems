### 4. Engineering Chemistry by K. Sesha Maheswaramma, Pearson

Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by K. Sesha Maheswaramma and published by Pearson. The book covers the fundamental concepts of chemistry and their applications in engineering. Some of the topics covered in the book include:

1. Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding
2. States of Matter
3. Chemical Thermodynamics
4. Electrochemistry
5. Chemical Kinetics
6. Surface Chemistry
7. Coordination Compounds
8. Organic Chemistry
9. Polymers
10. Environmental Chemistry

This book is intended for undergraduate students in engineering and provides a comprehensive introduction to the subject of chemistry in the context of engineering. It is written in a clear and concise manner and includes numerous examples and illustrations to aid in understanding the material. The book also includes end-of-chapter problems to help students test their knowledge and understanding of the concepts presented.
