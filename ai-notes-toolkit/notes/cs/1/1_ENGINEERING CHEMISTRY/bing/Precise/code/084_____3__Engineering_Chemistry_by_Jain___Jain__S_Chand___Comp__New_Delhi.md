### 3. Engineering Chemistry by Jain & Jain, S.Chand & Comp, New Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry is a book written by Jain and Jain and published by Dhanpat Rai Publishing Company .
- It is one of the primary textbooks for the course CY100 Engineering Chemistry for Kerala Technological University (KTU) .
- The original textbook may cost up to Rs.425 .
- The 17th edition of the book has been made more informative and comprehensive .
- This book has been prepared specially for students, keeping in view the new syllabus of various reputed universities throughout the country .
- It is written exclusively for students of all branches of engineering, keeping in view their professional requirements .
- The book is divided into 26 chapters .
- The 16th edition of the book is also available in paperback format on Amazon.in .
