### CO-2 Apply the fundamental concepts of determination of structure with various spectral techniques

Spectral techniques are used to determine the structure of molecules by analyzing the interaction of electromagnetic radiation with matter. Some of the fundamental concepts of determination of structure with various spectral techniques are:

1. **Infrared (IR) Spectroscopy**: This technique measures the absorption of infrared radiation by a molecule. The absorption of infrared radiation causes changes in the vibrational energy of the molecule, which can be used to determine the functional groups present in the molecule.

2. **Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) Spectroscopy**: This technique measures the absorption of radiofrequency radiation by the nuclei of atoms in a molecule. The absorption of radiofrequency radiation causes changes in the spin state of the nuclei, which can be used to determine the chemical environment of the nuclei and the connectivity of atoms in the molecule.

3. **Ultraviolet-Visible (UV-Vis) Spectroscopy**: This technique measures the absorption of ultraviolet and visible radiation by a molecule. The absorption of ultraviolet and visible radiation causes changes in the electronic energy of the molecule, which can be used to determine the presence of conjugated systems and chromophores in the molecule.

4. **Mass Spectrometry (MS)**: This technique measures the mass-to-charge ratio of ions produced by the molecule. The mass-to-charge ratio of the ions can be used to determine the molecular weight and the elemental composition of the molecule.

These are some of the fundamental concepts of determination of structure with various spectral techniques. Each technique provides different information about the molecule, and a combination of these techniques can be used to determine the complete structure of the molecule.
