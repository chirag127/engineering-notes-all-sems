### Water Technology

#### Sources of Water
Water is a vital resource for life and is obtained from various sources. Some of the common sources of water are:
1. Surface water: This includes water from sources such as rivers, lakes, and reservoirs.
2. Groundwater: This is water that is found underground in aquifers and is obtained through wells and boreholes.
3. Rainwater: This is water that is collected from precipitation and can be harvested for use.
4. Sea water: This is water from the ocean and can be made potable through desalination.

#### Impurities in Water
Water from natural sources is rarely pure and often contains various impurities. Some common impurities found in water are:
1. Physical impurities: These include suspended particles such as sand, clay, and silt.
2. Chemical impurities: These include dissolved minerals such as calcium and magnesium, which can cause hardness in water.
3. Biological impurities: These include microorganisms such as bacteria, viruses, and algae.

#### Hardness of Water
Hardness in water is caused by the presence of dissolved minerals such as calcium and magnesium. There are two types of hardness in water:
1. Temporary hardness: This is caused by the presence of bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium and can be removed by boiling the water.
2. Permanent hardness: This is caused by the presence of sulfates and chlorides of calcium and magnesium and cannot be removed by boiling. It can be removed by methods such as ion exchange or reverse osmosis.

#### Boiler Troubles
Boiler troubles refer to problems that can occur in boilers due to the presence of impurities in the water used. Some common boiler troubles are:
1. Scaling: This is the deposition of minerals on the inner surfaces of the boiler, which can reduce heat transfer and efficiency.
2. Corrosion: This is the deterioration of the boiler material due to chemical reactions with the water and impurities.
3. Priming and foaming: This is the carryover of water with the steam, which can cause problems in the steam system.
