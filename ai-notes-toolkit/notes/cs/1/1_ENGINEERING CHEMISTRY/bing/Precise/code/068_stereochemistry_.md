# Stereochemistry

Stereochemistry is a subdiscipline of chemistry that involves the study of the relative spatial arrangement of atoms that form the structure of molecules and their manipulation. It is also known as 3D chemistry, where the prefix "stereo" means "three-dimensionality".

The study of stereochemistry focuses on the relationships between stereoisomers, which by definition have the same molecular formula and sequence of bonded atoms. A property of molecules that is dependent on their handedness is called chirality.

Some important concepts in stereochemistry include:
- Chirality
- Enantiomers
- Diastereomers
- Assigning configurations using the R,S system
- Optical activity
- Fischer projections
