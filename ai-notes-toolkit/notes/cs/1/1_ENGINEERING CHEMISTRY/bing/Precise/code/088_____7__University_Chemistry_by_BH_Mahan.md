### 7. University Chemistry by BH Mahan

- University Chemistry by BH Mahan is a comprehensive textbook for undergraduate students studying chemistry.
- The book covers fundamental concepts in chemistry, including atomic structure, chemical bonding, and chemical reactions.
- It also includes chapters on thermodynamics, kinetics, and electrochemistry.
- The book is known for its clear explanations and numerous examples and practice problems.
- It is widely used as a reference for students preparing for exams in chemistry.
- The book is written in a formal style and is well-organized, making it easy to navigate and find information.
- Overall, University Chemistry by BH Mahan is a valuable resource for students studying chemistry at the university level.
