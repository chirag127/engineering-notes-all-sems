### Corrosion
- Corrosion is the process of deterioration of metal caused by the action of chemicals or electro-chemicals present in the surrounding atmosphere.
- It is the chemical or electrochemical destruction of metals and alloys in the presence of an environment to form undesirable compounds.
- Corrosion processes are the reactions of metals with environmental organisms.
- It is the process of a body gradually deteriorating due to the attack of air gases on the metal's surface.
- Corrosion is a major problem especially in the construction industry where various metals are used for structural purposes.

### Types of Corrosion
- Erosion Corrosion: caused by mechanical abrasion due to the movement between corrosive fluids and the metal surface.
- Uniform Corrosion: corrosion readily identifiable by ordinary visual examination.
- Pitting Corrosion: corrosion readily identifiable by ordinary visual examination.
- Crevice Corrosion: corrosion readily identifiable by ordinary visual examination.
- Fillform Corrosion: corrosion readily identifiable by ordinary visual examination.
- Pack Rust Corrosion: corrosion readily identifiable by ordinary visual examination.
- Galvanic Corrosion: corrosion readily identifiable by ordinary visual examination.
- Lamellar Corrosion: corrosion readily identifiable by ordinary visual examination.
- Erosion Corrosion: corrosion requiring supplementary means of examination.
- Cavitation Corrosion: corrosion requiring supplementary means of examination.
- Fretting Corrosion: corrosion requiring supplementary means of examination.
- Intergranular Corrosion: corrosion requiring supplementary means of examination.

### Cause of Corrosion
- The action of air, moisture, or a chemical reaction (such as an acid) on the metal surface.
- The attack of air gases on the metal's surface.
- The movement between corrosive fluids and the metal surface.

### Corrosion Prevention
- Corrosion reactions can be used to advantage in technological processes such as pickling, etching, chemical, and electrochemical polishing and machining, etc.
- Any reaction of a metal with its environment must be regarded as a corrosion process irrespective of whether it is used to advantage or not.
