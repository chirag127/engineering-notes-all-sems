### CO-5 Develop the understanding of Chemical structure of polymers and its effect on their various properties

1. Polymers are large molecules made up of repeating units called monomers.
2. The chemical structure of a polymer refers to the arrangement of its monomer units and the type of bonds that hold them together.
3. The chemical structure of a polymer can affect its properties such as strength, elasticity, melting point, and solubility.
4. For example, polymers with strong covalent bonds between their monomer units tend to have high melting points and are resistant to solvents.
5. On the other hand, polymers with weak intermolecular forces between their monomer units tend to have low melting points and are soluble in certain solvents.
6. The arrangement of monomer units in a polymer can also affect its properties. For example, polymers with a linear arrangement of monomer units tend to be strong and rigid, while polymers with a branched arrangement tend to be more flexible.
7. The presence of functional groups in a polymer can also affect its properties. For example, the presence of polar functional groups can increase the solubility of a polymer in polar solvents.
8. In summary, the chemical structure of a polymer plays a crucial role in determining its properties and behavior. Understanding the relationship between the chemical structure and properties of polymers is essential for their effective use in various applications.
