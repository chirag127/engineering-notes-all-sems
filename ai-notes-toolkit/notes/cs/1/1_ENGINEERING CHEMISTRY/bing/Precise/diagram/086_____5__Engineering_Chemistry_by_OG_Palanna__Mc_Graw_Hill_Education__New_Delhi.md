### 5. Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna, Mc Graw Hill Education, New Delhi

Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna is a comprehensive textbook published by Mc Graw Hill Education, New Delhi. The book covers various topics in the field of engineering chemistry, including:

1. Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding
2. States of Matter
3. Chemical Thermodynamics
4. Electrochemistry
5. Chemical Kinetics
6. Surface Chemistry
7. Coordination Compounds
8. Nuclear Chemistry
9. Solid State Chemistry
10. Materials Chemistry

The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for students to understand the concepts. It is a valuable resource for students studying engineering chemistry and related fields.
