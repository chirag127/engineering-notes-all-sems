## Reference Books:

Reference books are a type of non-fiction book that provides information on a specific subject. They are designed to be consulted for specific information rather than read from cover to cover. Some common types of reference books include:

1. **Dictionaries:** Provide definitions and meanings of words.
2. **Encyclopedias:** Provide general knowledge on a wide range of subjects.
3. **Atlases:** Provide maps and geographical information.
4. **Almanacs:** Provide statistical and factual information on a variety of subjects.
5. **Handbooks:** Provide practical information and instructions on a specific subject.
6. **Directories:** Provide lists of names, addresses, and other contact information.
7. **Bibliographies:** Provide lists of books and other resources on a specific subject.

Reference books are often used in research and academic settings to provide reliable and authoritative information on a subject. They can be found in libraries, bookstores, and online. It is important to evaluate the credibility of the source when using reference books for research.
