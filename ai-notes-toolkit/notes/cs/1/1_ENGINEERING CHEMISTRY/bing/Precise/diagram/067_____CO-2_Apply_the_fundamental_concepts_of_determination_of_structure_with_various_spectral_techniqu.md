### CO-2 Apply the fundamental concepts of determination of structure with various spectral techniques

1. Spectral techniques are used to determine the structure of molecules by analyzing the interaction of electromagnetic radiation with matter.
2. Some common spectral techniques include infrared (IR) spectroscopy, nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy, and mass spectrometry (MS).
3. In IR spectroscopy, the absorption of infrared radiation by a molecule causes vibrations in its chemical bonds. The resulting spectrum can be used to identify functional groups and determine the molecule's overall structure.
4. NMR spectroscopy uses the interaction of radio waves with the nuclei of atoms to determine the chemical environment of those atoms within a molecule. This information can be used to deduce the molecule's structure.
5. MS measures the mass-to-charge ratio of ions produced by ionizing a sample. The resulting mass spectrum can provide information about the molecular weight and structure of the sample.
6. These techniques can be used in combination to provide a more complete picture of a molecule's structure.
7. It is important to have a fundamental understanding of these techniques in order to accurately interpret the data and determine the structure of a molecule.
