### 5. To enable the students to understand detailed concepts related to polymers, Polymerization, Polymer Blends

Polymers are large molecules made up of repeating subunits called monomers. These monomers are chemically bonded together to form long chains or networks. Polymers can be found in nature, such as in proteins and DNA, or they can be synthesized in a laboratory.

Polymerization is the process of chemically bonding monomers together to form a polymer. There are two main types of polymerization: addition polymerization and condensation polymerization. In addition polymerization, monomers are added to the growing polymer chain one at a time, with no byproducts being produced. In condensation polymerization, monomers react with each other to form a polymer, with a small molecule, such as water, being produced as a byproduct.

Polymer blends are mixtures of two or more polymers. These blends can be created to combine the desirable properties of each individual polymer, resulting in a material with improved characteristics. Polymer blends can be made by physically mixing the polymers together, or by chemically bonding them together to form a copolymer.
