### Units Course Outcomes Bloom’s

1. **Units**: Units are a standard of measurement used to express physical quantities. They provide a common language for scientists to communicate their findings and compare results.
2. **Course Outcomes**: Course outcomes are the specific knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to achieve by the end of a course. They are used to assess the effectiveness of the course and to guide future improvements.
3. **Bloom’s Taxonomy**: Bloom’s Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives. It is used to design learning activities and assessments that promote higher-order thinking skills. The taxonomy is organized into six levels, from lower-order thinking skills such as remembering and understanding to higher-order thinking skills such as analyzing, evaluating, and creating.
