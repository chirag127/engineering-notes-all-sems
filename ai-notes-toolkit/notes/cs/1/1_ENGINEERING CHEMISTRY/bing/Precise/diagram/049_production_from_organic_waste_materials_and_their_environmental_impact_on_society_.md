# Production from Organic Waste Materials and their Environmental Impact on Society

Organic waste materials can be used to produce compost, which can be used to enrich soil and reduce the need for chemical fertilizers . However, when organic waste is disposed of in landfills, it can have a negative impact on the environment. The anaerobic decomposition of organic materials in landfills produces methane, a potent greenhouse gas with a global warming potential approximately 85 times higher than carbon dioxide over a 20-year time period . Landfills are one of the top emitters of man-made methane emissions in the United States .

Organic waste feedstocks of aquatic, agricultural, forestry, and terrestrial animal origin are especially suitable for valorization . By composting wasted food and other organics, methane emissions are significantly reduced . However, toxic waste can pollute groundwater, soil, surface water, and air, causing problems for humans and environmental health . Gases like CO2 and methane, which are by-products of many production processes, have an adverse impact on our environment, increasing the speed of global warming .

In conclusion, the production of compost from organic waste materials can have a positive impact on the environment by reducing the need for chemical fertilizers and reducing methane emissions. However, the disposal of organic waste in landfills can have a negative impact on the environment by producing methane, a potent greenhouse gas. It is important to manage waste through reuse, recycling, storage, treatment, energy recovery, and/or disposal or other releases to the environment .
