# Determination of Hardness and Alkalinity, Numerical problems

## Hardness
Hardness is a measure of the concentration of calcium and magnesium ions in water. It is usually expressed in milligrams per liter (mg/L) as calcium carbonate (CaCO3). There are two types of hardness: carbonate hardness (CH) and noncarbonate hardness (NCH). The total hardness (TH) is the sum of carbonate hardness and noncarbonate hardness: TH = CH + NCH .

## Alkalinity
Alkalinity is a measure of the capacity of water to neutralize acids. It is usually expressed in milligrams per liter (mg/L) as calcium carbonate (CaCO3). Carbonate hardness has a relationship with alkalinity: CH in meq/L = [Alk in meq/L] and CH in °dH = 809 × [Alk in meq/L] .
