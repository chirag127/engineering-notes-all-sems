# Values, Gross & Net Calorific Value, Determination of Calorific Value by Bomb Calorimeter

## Values
Values are principles or standards of behavior that are considered important or worthwhile. They can be personal, cultural, or societal, and they can vary from one individual or group to another.

## Gross & Net Calorific Value
Calorific value is the amount of heat energy released when a substance is burned. It is usually measured in units of energy per unit of mass, such as joules per gram or calories per gram.

Gross calorific value (GCV) is the total amount of heat energy released when a substance is burned, including the heat energy contained in the water vapor produced during combustion. Net calorific value (NCV) is the amount of heat energy released when a substance is burned, excluding the heat energy contained in the water vapor produced during combustion.

## Determination of Calorific Value by Bomb Calorimeter
A bomb calorimeter is a device used to measure the calorific value of a substance. It consists of a sealed container, known as a bomb, which is filled with oxygen and the substance to be tested. The bomb is then placed in a water bath, and the substance is ignited. The heat energy released during combustion is transferred to the water, causing its temperature to rise. The temperature rise is measured, and the calorific value of the substance can be calculated using the known heat capacity of the water and the mass of the substance burned.
