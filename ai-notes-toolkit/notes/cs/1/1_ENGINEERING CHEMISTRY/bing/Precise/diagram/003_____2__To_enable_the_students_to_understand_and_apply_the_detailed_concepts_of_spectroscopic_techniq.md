### 2. To enable the students to understand and apply the detailed concepts of spectroscopic techniques:

- Spectroscopy is the study of the interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation.
- Spectroscopic techniques are used to analyze the chemical composition and structure of materials.
- There are several types of spectroscopy, including atomic absorption, atomic emission, infrared, Raman, ultraviolet-visible, and X-ray fluorescence.
- Each type of spectroscopy uses a different part of the electromagnetic spectrum and provides different information about the sample being analyzed.
- Spectroscopic techniques can be used in a variety of fields, including chemistry, physics, biology, and environmental science.
- To apply spectroscopic techniques, students must understand the principles of the technique and how to interpret the data obtained from the analysis.
- Students should also be familiar with the instrumentation used for spectroscopic analysis and how to properly calibrate and operate the equipment.
- With a solid understanding of spectroscopic techniques, students can use these methods to analyze a wide range of materials and gain valuable insights into their composition and structure.
