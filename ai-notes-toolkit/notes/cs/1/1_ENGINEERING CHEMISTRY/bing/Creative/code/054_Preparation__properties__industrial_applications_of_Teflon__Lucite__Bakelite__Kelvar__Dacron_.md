# Preparation, properties, industrial applications of Teflon, Lucite, Bakelite, Kelvar, Dacron

## Teflon
- Teflon is a synthetic polymer of tetrafluoroethylene (C2F4) with the formula (C2F4)n.
- It is prepared by free radical polymerization of tetrafluoroethylene in a water medium with a catalyst such as ammonium persulfate or sodium persulfate.
- It has the following properties:
  - It is a white solid compound at room temperature with a density of about 2.2 g/cm3.
  - It has a high melting point of about 600 K and is thermally stable even at very high temperatures.
  - It is chemically inert and resistant to most acids, bases, solvents, and oxidizing agents. The only chemicals that can affect it are alkali metals and some fluorinating agents.
  - It has a low coefficient of friction and is non-sticky, non-wetting, and self-lubricating.
  - It has a low water absorption capacity and is hydrophobic and oleophobic.
  - It has good electrical insulation properties and a high dielectric strength.
- It has the following industrial applications:
  - It is used as a coating material for non-stick cookware, bakeware, and utensils.
  - It is used as a sealant, gasket, and lubricant in various mechanical and chemical processes.
  - It is used as a protective layer for electrical wires, cables, and connectors.
  - It is used as a fabric protector and stain repellent for clothing, carpets, and upholstery.
  - It is used as a component of medical devices, implants, and surgical instruments.

## Lucite
- Lucite is a trade name for polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA), a transparent thermoplastic polymer of methyl methacrylate (C5H8O2) with the formula (C5H8O2)n.
- It is prepared by free radical polymerization of methyl methacrylate in a bulk or suspension process with a catalyst such as benzoyl peroxide or azobisisobutyronitrile.
- It has the following properties:
  - It is a clear, colorless, and glossy solid with a density of about 1.2 g/cm3.
  - It has a glass transition temperature of about 105 °C and a melting point of about 160 °C. It is thermoplastic and can be molded, extruded, or cast into various shapes and forms.
  - It is resistant to weathering, UV radiation, and most chemicals. It can be dyed, painted, or coated with other materials.
  - It has a high refractive index of about 1.49 and a low birefringence. It is optically transparent and transmits about 92% of visible light.
  - It has good mechanical strength, stiffness, and impact resistance. It can be machined, drilled, cut, or polished with ease.
  - It has poor electrical conductivity and is a good insulator. It also has low flammability and smoke emission.
- It has the following industrial applications:
  - It is used as a substitute for glass in windows, skylights, lenses, screens, and displays.
  - It is used as a material for signs, sculptures, furniture, jewelry, and art objects.
  - It is used as a component of dental prosthetics, implants, and orthodontic appliances.
  - It is used as a matrix for composite materials such as carbon fiber, fiberglass, or kevlar.
