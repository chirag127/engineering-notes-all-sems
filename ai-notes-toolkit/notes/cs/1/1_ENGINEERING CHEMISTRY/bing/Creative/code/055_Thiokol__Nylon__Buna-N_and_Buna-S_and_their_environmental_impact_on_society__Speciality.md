# Thiokol, Nylon, Buna-N and Buna-S and their environmental impact on society, Speciality

- Thiokol is a synthetic rubber made from ethylene dichloride and sodium polysulfide. It is used as a sealant, adhesive, and binder for solid rocket propellants. It has high resistance to heat, cold, and chemicals. It was invented by Joseph C. Patrick and Nathan Mnookin in 1926.
- Nylon is a synthetic polymer fiber made from coal, water, and air. It is used for clothing, carpets, parachutes, and other products. It has high strength, durability, and flexibility. It was invented by Wallace Carothers at DuPont in 1935.
- Buna-N is a synthetic rubber made from acrylonitrile and butadiene. It is also known as nitrile rubber or NBR. It is used for hoses, seals, gloves, and other products. It has high resistance to oil, fuel, and other chemicals. It was invented by IG Farben scientists in 1931.
- Buna-S is a synthetic rubber made from styrene and butadiene. It is also known as styrene butadiene rubber or SBR. It is used for tires, footwear, conveyor belts, and other products. It has good abrasion resistance and low cost. It was invented by IG Farben scientists in 1929.

## Environmental impact

- Thiokol has a low environmental impact compared to other synthetic rubbers. It is biodegradable and does not release harmful gases when burned. However, it can still cause pollution if not disposed of properly. It can also pose health risks to workers who handle it without proper protection.
- Nylon has a high environmental impact compared to other synthetic fibers. It is not biodegradable and requires a lot of energy and water to produce. It also releases greenhouse gases and microplastics when manufactured and washed. It can affect the wildlife and ecosystems that ingest or encounter it.
- Buna-N has a moderate environmental impact compared to other synthetic rubbers. It is not biodegradable and can leach toxic substances into the soil and water. It can also emit volatile organic compounds (VOCs) when heated or exposed to sunlight. It can cause respiratory and skin problems to humans and animals that come in contact with it.
- Buna-S has a high environmental impact compared to other synthetic rubbers. It is not biodegradable and can generate a lot of waste and pollution. It can also release carcinogenic substances such as benzene and styrene when burned or degraded. It can harm the health and environment of the people and places that produce and use it.

## Speciality

- Thiokol's speciality is its ability to withstand extreme temperatures and pressures. It is ideal for applications that require high performance and reliability, such as aerospace and defense. It is also resistant to oxidation and corrosion, which makes it suitable for long-term storage and use.
- Nylon's speciality is its versatility and adaptability. It can be blended with other fibers, dyed, and treated to achieve different properties and effects. It can also be spun, woven, or molded into various shapes and forms. It is suitable for applications that require lightness, elasticity, and durability, such as textiles and engineering.
- Buna-N's speciality is its resistance to oil and fuel. It is one of the most widely used synthetic rubbers in the automotive and industrial sectors. It can also withstand high temperatures and abrasion, which makes it suitable for applications that require toughness and stability, such as hoses and seals.
- Buna-S's speciality is its low cost and availability. It is one of the most common and cheapest synthetic rubbers in the market. It can also be modified and improved by adding fillers, additives, and copolymers. It is suitable for applications that require mass production and economy, such as tires and footwear.
