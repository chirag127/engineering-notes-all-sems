### K3

- K3 is a type of **synthetic cannabinoid** that mimics the effects of THC, the main psychoactive component of cannabis.
- K3 is also known as **Spice, K2, Black Mamba, Bombay Blue, Genie, Zohai, Yucatan Fire, Skunk, and Moon Rocks**.
- K3 is usually sold as a **herbal mixture** that can be smoked or vaporized, or as a **liquid** that can be used in e-cigarettes or other devices.
- K3 is **illegal** in many countries, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and most of Europe, because of its potential for abuse and harm.
- K3 can cause **serious health risks**, such as:

  - Rapid heart rate, high blood pressure, chest pain, and heart attack
  - Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and kidney damage
  - Anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, psychosis, and suicidal thoughts
  - Seizures, tremors, muscle spasms, and loss of consciousness
  - Addiction, withdrawal, and dependence

- K3 can also interact with other drugs, such as alcohol, opioids, stimulants, and antidepressants, and increase the risk of overdose and death.
- K3 is **not detected** by standard drug tests, which makes it appealing to some users who want to avoid detection.
- K3 is **not safe** and should be avoided by anyone who values their health and well-being.
