### K3

- K3 is a type of surface mount technology (SMT) component package that is used for integrated circuits (ICs).
- K3 stands for **Kilopass 3**, which is a trademark of Kilopass Technology Inc., a company that specializes in non-volatile memory (NVM) solutions.
- K3 packages have a **rectangular shape** with **32 pins** on each side, for a total of **128 pins**. The pins are arranged in a **0.5 mm pitch** grid, meaning the distance between the centers of adjacent pins is 0.5 mm.
- K3 packages have a **body size** of **11 mm x 11 mm**, and a **height** of **1.4 mm**. The **die size** (the area of the silicon chip inside the package) is **7.5 mm x 7.5 mm**.
- K3 packages are designed for **high-density** and **low-power** applications, such as **smart cards**, **security chips**, **embedded systems**, and **IoT devices**. They can store up to **4 Mb** of data in a single chip, using a proprietary **anti-fuse** technology that is resistant to hacking and tampering.
- K3 packages are compatible with standard SMT equipment and processes, and can be soldered onto printed circuit boards (PCBs) using **reflow soldering**. They have a **lead-free** and **halogen-free** design, and comply with the **RoHS** and **WEEE** environmental regulations.
