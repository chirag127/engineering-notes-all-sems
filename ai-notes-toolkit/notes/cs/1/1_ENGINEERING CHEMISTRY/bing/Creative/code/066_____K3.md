### K3

- K3 is a type of surface mount device (SMD) LED that is used for backlighting and general illumination applications.
- K3 stands for **K**ingbright **3**mm LED, which is the brand name and the size of the LED package.
- K3 LEDs have a flat top and a rectangular shape, which allows them to be mounted close together on a printed circuit board (PCB) and create a uniform light output.
- K3 LEDs have a wide viewing angle of 120 degrees, which means they can be seen from different directions without losing brightness or color quality.
- K3 LEDs are available in various colors, such as red, green, blue, yellow, white, and RGB (red, green, and blue combined).
- K3 LEDs have a low power consumption, high brightness, long lifespan, and high reliability, which makes them suitable for various applications, such as LCD monitors, TVs, laptops, keyboards, cameras, signs, indicators, and decorative lighting.
