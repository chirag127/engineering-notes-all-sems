### Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by SS Dara and SS Umare for students of all branches of engineering, keeping in view their professional requirements.
- The book is divided into 26 chapters, covering topics such as water and its treatment, fuels and combustion, polymers and composites, corrosion and its control, lubricants and additives, electrochemistry and batteries, cement and concrete, nanomaterials and nanotechnology, green chemistry and engineering, and environmental engineering.
- The book is designed to provide a clear and concise explanation of the fundamental concepts, principles, and applications of engineering chemistry, with numerous examples, solved problems, and exercises.
- The book also includes a glossary of important terms, a list of abbreviations and symbols, and a bibliography of useful references.
- The book is published by S. Chand & Co Ltd, a leading publisher of educational books in India, with a history of more than 75 years.
- The book is available in paperback and e-book formats, and has received positive reviews from students and teachers alike.
