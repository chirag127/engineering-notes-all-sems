### Graphite and Fullerene; Introduction, Structure and Applications

- Graphite and fullerene are two forms of carbon that have different structures and properties.
- Graphite is a soft, black, metallic material that consists of layers of hexagonal rings of carbon atoms. Graphite is a good conductor of electricity and heat, and has a high melting point. Graphite is used in pencils, lubricants, batteries, electrodes, and as a moderator in nuclear reactors.
- Fullerene is a family of carbon molecules that have hollow, cage-like shapes. The most common and stable fullerene is C60, also known as buckminsterfullerene or buckyball, which has 60 carbon atoms arranged in a sphere. Other fullerenes include C70, C76, C84, and nanotubes, which are cylindrical fullerenes. Fullerene is a poor conductor of electricity, but can act as a semiconductor or a superconductor under certain conditions. Fullerene has a high tensile strength and a low density. Fullerene is used in nanotechnology, drug delivery, solar cells, sensors, and catalysts .
- Graphite and fullerene have a similar structure in that they both consist of hexagonal rings of carbon atoms, but they differ in the number and arrangement of the rings. Graphite has an infinite number of rings that form flat sheets, while fullerene has a finite number of rings that form curved surfaces. Graphite has only hexagonal rings, while fullerene has pentagonal and sometimes heptagonal rings as well. Graphite has a planar structure, while fullerene has a three-dimensional structure .
- Graphite and fullerene have different applications because of their different properties. Graphite is useful for applications that require high conductivity, high temperature resistance, and low friction, while fullerene is useful for applications that require high strength, low weight, and novel electronic properties. Graphite and fullerene are also used to synthesize graphene, which is a single layer of graphite and has exceptional properties such as high electrical and thermal conductivity, high mechanical strength, and high surface area. Graphene is used in electronics, composites, water purification, sensors, batteries, and supercapacitors .
