# Processing industry, Oil & gas industry and Pulp & paper industries

## Processing industry
- A processing industry is a type of industry that transforms raw materials or intermediate products into finished goods or services.
- Examples of processing industries include food and beverage, textile, chemical, pharmaceutical, metal, and petroleum industries.
- Processing industries can be classified into two main types: continuous and batch processing.
  - Continuous processing involves a constant flow of materials through a series of operations, such as in oil refining or paper making.
  - Batch processing involves processing a fixed amount of materials at a time, such as in baking or brewing.

## Oil & gas industry
- The oil & gas industry is a type of processing industry that explores, extracts, transports, refines, and markets crude oil and natural gas and their derivatives.
- The oil & gas industry can be divided into three main segments: upstream, midstream, and downstream.
  - Upstream involves the exploration and production of oil and gas from reservoirs, wells, and fields.
  - Midstream involves the transportation and storage of oil and gas, such as by pipelines, tankers, and terminals.
  - Downstream involves the refining and processing of oil and gas into fuels, petrochemicals, and other products, such as gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, plastics, and fertilizers.

## Pulp & paper industry
- The pulp & paper industry is a type of processing industry that produces pulp, paper, and paperboard from wood and other raw materials, such as recycled paper, agricultural residues, and non-wood fibers.
- The pulp & paper industry can be divided into two main segments: pulp production and paper production.
  - Pulp production involves the separation of cellulose fibers from lignin and other impurities in wood or other raw materials, using mechanical, chemical, or biological methods.
  - Paper production involves the formation, pressing, drying, and finishing of paper or paperboard from pulp, using additives, coatings, and treatments to enhance the properties and quality of the final product.
