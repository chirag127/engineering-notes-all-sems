### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Define the basic concepts and terminology of artificial intelligence, such as agents, environments, rationality, search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.
- Apply various search algorithms, such as uninformed search, informed search, local search, adversarial search, and constraint satisfaction, to solve problems that require finding optimal or near-optimal solutions.
- Design and implement knowledge-based systems, such as propositional logic, first-order logic, inference rules, resolution, and logic programming, to represent and reason with facts and rules.
- Explain and compare different planning techniques, such as classical planning, partial-order planning, hierarchical planning, and planning under uncertainty, to generate sequences of actions that achieve given goals.
- Understand and apply the basic principles and methods of machine learning, such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning, neural networks, and deep learning, to analyze data and learn from experience.
- Demonstrate the ability to use natural language processing techniques, such as tokenization, stemming, parsing, semantics, and pragmatics, to process and understand natural language texts and generate natural language responses.
- Describe and evaluate the main approaches and challenges of computer vision, such as image processing, feature extraction, object recognition, face detection, and scene understanding, to perceive and interpret visual information from images and videos.
- Identify and analyze the main components and issues of robotics, such as sensors, actuators, kinematics, localization, mapping, navigation, and coordination, to design and program robots that can perform tasks in the real world.
- Discuss the ethical, social, and philosophical implications of artificial intelligence, such as the Turing test, the Chinese room argument, the singularity, the alignment problem, and the impact of AI on human society and values.
