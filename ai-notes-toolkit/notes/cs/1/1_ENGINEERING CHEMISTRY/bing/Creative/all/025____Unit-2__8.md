## Unit-2: 8

- Unit-2: 8 is a topic that covers the following concepts:
  - The definition and properties of logarithms and exponential functions.
  - The laws of logarithms and how to use them to simplify expressions and solve equations.
  - The applications of logarithms and exponential functions in various fields such as science, engineering, finance, and statistics.
  - The graphs of logarithmic and exponential functions and their transformations.
  - The inverse relationship between logarithms and exponentials and how to use it to switch between different forms of equations.
  - The natural logarithm and the natural exponential function and their properties and applications.
  - The common logarithm and the common exponential function and their properties and applications.
  - The change of base formula and how to use it to convert between different bases of logarithms and exponentials.
  - The logarithmic and exponential models and how to use them to fit data and make predictions.
