# 6. Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawala, Dhanpat Rai Publishing Comp, New Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawala is a widely recommended textbook for students of all branches of engineering, covering the syllabus of various Indian universities    .
- The book is divided into 26 chapters, covering topics such as water and its treatment, fuels and combustion, polymers and composites, corrosion and its prevention, lubricants and their applications, electrochemistry and batteries, phase rule and alloys, analytical techniques and instrumentation, and nanomaterials and nanotechnology.
- The book provides a comprehensive and systematic presentation of the concepts and principles of engineering chemistry, with emphasis on the applications and relevance of chemistry in engineering.
- The book also contains numerous solved examples, illustrations, tables, diagrams, and exercises to enhance the understanding and problem-solving skills of the students.
- The book is updated with the latest developments and trends in the field of engineering chemistry, such as green chemistry, biodegradable polymers, fuel cells, solar cells, and nanobiotechnology.
- The book is suitable for both theory and laboratory courses, as well as for self-study and reference.
