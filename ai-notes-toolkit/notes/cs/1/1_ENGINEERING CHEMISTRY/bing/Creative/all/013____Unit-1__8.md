## Unit-1: 8

- Unit-1: 8 is a topic that covers the basic concepts of data structures and algorithms.
- Data structures are ways of organizing and storing data in a computer memory, such as arrays, lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, etc.
- Algorithms are step-by-step procedures for solving a problem or performing a task, such as sorting, searching, traversing, etc.
- The main objectives of studying data structures and algorithms are to:
  - Understand the trade-offs between different data structures and algorithms in terms of time and space complexity, correctness, and efficiency.
  - Learn how to design and implement data structures and algorithms using a programming language, such as C, C++, Java, Python, etc.
  - Apply data structures and algorithms to solve real-world problems, such as web development, data analysis, cryptography, etc.
- The main topics covered in Unit-1: 8 are:
  - Introduction to data structures and algorithms, including definitions, examples, and applications.
  - Analysis of algorithms, including asymptotic notation, recurrence relations, and master theorem.
  - Linear data structures, including arrays, lists, stacks, and queues, and their operations, implementations, and applications.
  - Non-linear data structures, including trees, graphs, and heaps, and their operations, implementations, and applications.
  - Sorting and searching algorithms, including selection sort, insertion sort, bubble sort, merge sort, quick sort, heap sort, linear search, binary search, and hashing, and their complexity, implementation, and applications.
