# Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd

Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by SS Dara and SS Umare for students of all branches of engineering. The book covers the basic concepts and applications of chemistry in various fields of engineering, such as materials, fuels, corrosion, water treatment, nanotechnology, etc. The book is divided into 26 chapters, each with a summary, review questions, and numerical problems. The book also contains appendices on units and conversions, symbols and abbreviations, and common chemical reactions. The book is published by S Chand & Co Ltd, a leading publisher of educational books in India.

Some of the main features of the book are:

- It provides a clear and concise explanation of the fundamental principles of chemistry and their relevance to engineering problems.
- It includes numerous examples, illustrations, diagrams, and tables to enhance the understanding of the concepts and applications.
- It covers the latest developments and trends in the field of engineering chemistry, such as nanomaterials, green chemistry, biofuels, etc.
- It follows the syllabus of various universities and technical boards for the first year engineering courses.
- It is suitable for self-study and reference for students and professionals.

- Atomic structure and chemical bonding

- Electrochemistry and corrosion

- Fuels and combustion

- Lubricants and adhesives
- Cement and concrete
- Ceramics and refractories
- Metals and alloys

- Environmental chemistry and pollution control
- Nanoscience and nanotechnology
- Green chemistry and engineering
- Biochemistry and biotechnology
- Nuclear chemistry and energy

- Inorganic chemistry and reactions
