# Level

- A level is a tool or device that is used to measure or indicate whether a surface is horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb).
- A level can also refer to the horizontal or vertical position of a surface or object, or the degree of inclination of a line or plane relative to the horizontal or vertical.
- There are different types of levels, such as spirit levels, laser levels, water levels, digital levels, and electronic levels, each with their own advantages and disadvantages.
- A level is commonly used in construction, engineering, surveying, carpentry, and other fields that require precise alignment or measurement of angles and distances.
- A level can help to ensure that structures, objects, or instruments are properly aligned, balanced, or calibrated, which can improve their functionality, stability, or accuracy.
