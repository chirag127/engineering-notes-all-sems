## Hours

- Hours are units of time that measure the duration of events or intervals.
- One hour is equal to 60 minutes or 3600 seconds.
- Hours are commonly used to divide the day into 24 equal parts, starting from midnight or noon.
- Hours can also be expressed in different formats, such as 12-hour clock, 24-hour clock, or decimal time.
- Hours can be abbreviated as h, hr, or hrs. For example, 3 hours can be written as 3h, 3hr, or 3hrs.
- Hours can be converted to other units of time using simple formulas. For example, to convert hours to days, divide by 24. To convert hours to weeks, divide by 168. To convert hours to months, divide by 730. To convert hours to years, divide by 8760.
