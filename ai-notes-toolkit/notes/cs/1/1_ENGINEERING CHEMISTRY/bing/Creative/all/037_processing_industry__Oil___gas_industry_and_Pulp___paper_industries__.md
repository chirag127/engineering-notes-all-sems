# Processing Industry, Oil & Gas Industry and Pulp & Paper Industries

## Processing Industry

- A processing industry is any industry that involves a mechanical, chemical, thermal or other means of alteration of either organic or inorganic materials, or a combination of both, to create, manufacture, fabricate, and/or package a product for use on site or for distribution or sale off-site.
- Examples of processing industries include agricultural, horticultural, forestry, fisheries, gypsum, mineral / aggregate, and aggregate- based, petrochemical, rubber, or pharmaceutical products or combination of products.
- A processing industry is also known as a process industry, which is defined as an industry, such as the chemical or petrochemical industry, that is concerned with the processing of bulk resources into other products.
- A processing industry is a branch of process manufacturing, which is a production method that creates goods by combining supplies, ingredients or raw materials using a formula or recipe.
- Process manufacturing is frequently used in industries that produce bulk quantities of goods, such as food, beverages, refined oil, gasoline, pharmaceuticals, chemicals and plastics.

## Oil & Gas Industry

- The oil and gas industry is a processing industry that is involved in the exploration, extraction, refining, transportation, and marketing of petroleum and natural gas products.
- The oil and gas industry is divided into three major sectors: upstream, midstream and downstream.
- The upstream sector is responsible for finding and producing crude oil and natural gas from underground or underwater reservoirs.
- The midstream sector is responsible for transporting and storing the crude oil and natural gas from the production sites to the refineries and processing plants.
- The downstream sector is responsible for refining the crude oil and natural gas into various products, such as gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, lubricants, petrochemicals, and plastics.
- The oil and gas industry is one of the largest and most influential industries in the world, as it provides the primary source of energy for many countries and industries.

## Pulp & Paper Industries

- The pulp and paper industries are processing industries that are involved in the production of pulp, paper, paperboard, and other cellulose-based products from wood or other raw materials.
- The pulp and paper industries are divided into two main stages: pulping and papermaking.
- The pulping stage is responsible for converting the raw materials, such as wood chips, into pulp, which is a fibrous material that can be used to make paper.
- The pulping stage can use different methods, such as mechanical, chemical, or semi-chemical, depending on the type and quality of the raw materials and the desired properties of the pulp.
- The papermaking stage is responsible for forming, pressing, drying, and finishing the pulp into paper or paperboard products, such as writing paper, printing paper, packaging paper, tissue paper, and cardboard.
- The papermaking stage can use different machines, such as fourdrinier, cylinder, or twin-wire, depending on the type and quality of the pulp and the desired properties of the paper or paperboard.
- The pulp and paper industries are important for many sectors, such as education, communication, publishing, packaging, hygiene, and construction.
