# Preparation, properties, industrial applications of Teflon, Lucite, Bakelite, Kelvar, Dacron

## Teflon

- Teflon is the trade name of **polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE)**, a synthetic polymer of tetrafluoroethylene (C2F4).
- Teflon is prepared by the **polymerization** of tetrafluoroethylene in the presence of a **catalyst** such as perfluoropropionyl fluoride or perfluoroisobutylene.
- Teflon has the following properties:
  - It is a **white solid** compound at room temperature.
  - Its **density** is about 2200 kg/m3 or 2.2 g/cm3.
  - Its **melting point** is 600 K or 327 °C.
  - It is **chemically resistant** to most substances, except alkali metals and some fluorinated compounds.
  - It shows good **resistance** to heat and low temperature.
  - It has a low **water absorption** capacity and a low **coefficient of friction**.
- Teflon has the following industrial applications:
  - It is used as a **coating** material for non-stick cookware, electrical wires, cables, bearings, seals, gaskets, etc.
  - It is used as a **lubricant** for machinery, valves, pumps, etc.
  - It is used as a **fabric** for clothing, carpets, tents, etc.
  - It is used as a **membrane** for filtration, separation, gas exchange, etc.

## Lucite

- Lucite is the trade name of **polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA)**, a synthetic polymer of methyl methacrylate (C5H8O2).
- Lucite is prepared by the **polymerization** of methyl methacrylate in the presence of a **catalyst** such as benzoyl peroxide or azobisisobutyronitrile.
- Lucite has the following properties:
  - It is a **transparent** and **colorless** solid at room temperature.
  - Its **density** is about 1190 kg/m3 or 1.19 g/cm3.
  - Its **glass transition temperature** is 105 °C.
  - It is **thermoplastic**, meaning it can be molded and reshaped by heating and cooling.
  - It is **resistant** to ultraviolet light, weathering, and chemicals, except strong acids and bases.
  - It has a high **refractive index** of 1.49, making it suitable for optical applications.
- Lucite has the following industrial applications:
  - It is used as a **substitute** for glass in windows, lenses, screens, signs, etc.
  - It is used as a **material** for sculptures, jewelry, furniture, toys, etc.
  - It is used as a **component** for dental prosthetics, implants, artificial nails, etc.
