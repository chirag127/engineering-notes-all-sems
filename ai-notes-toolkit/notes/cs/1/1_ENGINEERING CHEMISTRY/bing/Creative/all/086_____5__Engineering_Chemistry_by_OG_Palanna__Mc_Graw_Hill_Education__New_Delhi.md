# Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna, Mc Graw Hill Education, New Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by O.G. Palanna, a professor of chemistry at the National Institute of Technology, Karnataka, India.
- The book is meant for the first-year undergraduate students of engineering, and covers the fundamentals of engineering chemistry in a lucid and logical manner.
- The book has 16 chapters, covering topics such as atomic structure, chemical bonding, phase rule, electrochemistry, corrosion, fuels, polymers, water treatment, nanomaterials, and environmental chemistry.
- The book also includes numerous solved examples, exercises, multiple choice questions, and laboratory experiments to help the students understand and apply the concepts of engineering chemistry.
- The book is published by McGraw Hill Education, a leading global provider of educational materials and services, and has been reprinted several times since its first edition in 2009.
- The book is widely used and recommended by various engineering colleges and universities in India and abroad, and has received positive reviews from students and teachers alike.
