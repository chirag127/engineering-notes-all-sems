# Engineering Chemistry by Rath & Singh, 2nd Edition, Cengage Learning India Pvt Ltd Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook that covers all topics taught to the first-year undergraduate students of all engineering disciplines offered by various Indian universities .
- The book follows the syllabi of Biju Patnaik University of Technology (BPUT), KIIT (Deemed University), and SOA (Deemed University) in the state of Orissa.
- The book covers topics from diverse branches of chemistry, viz., general, inorganic, organic, and analytical chemistry in addition to a topic, nanomaterials, from material science .
- The book aims to provide a comprehensive and systematic coverage of the theory behind the concepts and their applications.
- The book has a neat and student-friendly visual presentation with rich pedagogy to support and illustrate the material for easy and effective learning.
- The book has a variety of solved and unsolved problems/exercises to help reinforce learning and boost the confidence of the learner/reader.
- The book is divided into 10 chapters, each with an introduction, objectives, summary, and references.
- The chapters are as follows:

  - Chapter 1: Water and Its Treatment
  - Chapter 2: Polymers and Polymerization
  - Chapter 3: Fuels and Combustion
  - Chapter 4: Corrosion and Its Control
  - Chapter 5: Phase Rule and Its Applications
  - Chapter 6: Electrochemistry and Batteries
  - Chapter 7: Chemical Kinetics and Catalysis
  - Chapter 8: Spectroscopy and Instrumental Methods of Analysis
  - Chapter 9: Organic Reactions and Mechanisms
  - Chapter 10: Nanomaterials and Their Applications
