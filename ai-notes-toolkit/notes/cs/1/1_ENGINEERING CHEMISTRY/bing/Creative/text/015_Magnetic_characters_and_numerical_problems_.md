# Magnetic Characteristics and Numerical Problems

- Magnetic characteristics are the properties of a material that determine its behavior in a magnetic field.
- Some of the magnetic characteristics are:
  - Magnetic permeability: The ratio of the magnetic flux density to the magnetic field intensity in a material. It measures how easily a material can be magnetized by an external field.
  - Magnetic susceptibility: The ratio of the magnetization to the magnetic field intensity in a material. It measures how much a material responds to an external field.
  - Magnetic hysteresis: The phenomenon of retaining some magnetization even after the external field is removed. It is caused by the alignment of magnetic domains in a material.
  - Magnetic saturation: The state of a material when all its magnetic domains are aligned in the same direction by a strong external field. It is the maximum possible magnetization of a material.
  - Magnetic coercivity: The minimum external field required to reduce the magnetization of a material to zero. It measures how resistant a material is to demagnetization.
  - Magnetic retentivity: The residual magnetization of a material after the external field is removed. It measures how much a material retains its magnetization.

- Numerical problems are the problems that involve calculations using the magnetic characteristics and other parameters of a material or a system in a magnetic field.
- Some of the numerical problems are:
  - Finding the magnetic flux density, magnetic field intensity, magnetization, magnetic permeability, or magnetic susceptibility of a material or a system in a given magnetic field.
  - Finding the magnetic hysteresis loop, magnetic saturation, magnetic coercivity, or magnetic retentivity of a material or a system in a varying magnetic field.
  - Finding the magnetic force, torque, or energy of a material or a system in a magnetic field.
  - Finding the magnetic induction, magnetic potential, or magnetic dipole moment of a material or a system in a magnetic field.
  - Finding the magnetic flux, magnetic flux linkage, or magnetic reluctance of a material or a system in a magnetic circuit.
