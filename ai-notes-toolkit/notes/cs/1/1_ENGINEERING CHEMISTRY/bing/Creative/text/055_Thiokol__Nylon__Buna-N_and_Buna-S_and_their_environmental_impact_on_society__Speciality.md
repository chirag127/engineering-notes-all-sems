# Thiokol, Nylon, Buna-N and Buna-S and their environmental impact on society, Speciality

- Thiokol is a synthetic rubber made from the co-polymerization of ethylene dichloride and sodium polysulfide. It is used as a sealant, adhesive, and binder for rocket propellants, gaskets, and coatings. It is also known as polysulfide rubber or Thiokol rubber.
- Nylon is a synthetic polymer made from the condensation of diamines and dicarboxylic acids. It is used as a fiber for textiles, carpets, ropes, and parachutes. It is also used as a plastic for gears, bearings, and casings. It is the first entirely synthetic fiber invented by DuPont in 1938.
- Buna-N is a synthetic rubber made from the co-polymerization of acrylonitrile and butadiene. It is also known as nitrile rubber or NBR. It is used as a seal, hose, and glove material for oil, fuel, and chemical resistance. It is also used as a medical and dental material for gloves and tubing.
- Buna-S is a synthetic rubber made from the co-polymerization of styrene and butadiene. It is also known as styrene butadiene rubber or SBR. It is used as a tire, conveyor belt, and shoe sole material for abrasion and wear resistance. It is also used as a latex substitute for paints and adhesives.

## Environmental impact

- Thiokol is a non-biodegradable material that can persist in the environment for a long time. It can also release toxic gases such as hydrogen sulfide and sulfur dioxide when burned or decomposed. It can pose a threat to human health and wildlife if not properly disposed of or recycled.
- Nylon is a non-biodegradable material that can accumulate in landfills and oceans. It can also release greenhouse gases such as nitrous oxide and carbon dioxide when produced or incinerated. It can contribute to global warming and climate change if not reduced or reused.
- Buna-N is a non-biodegradable material that can leach harmful chemicals such as acrylonitrile and butadiene into the soil and water. It can also emit carcinogenic substances such as benzene and 1,3-butadiene when heated or exposed to sunlight. It can cause cancer and other diseases if not handled or stored safely.
- Buna-S is a non-biodegradable material that can degrade into microplastics and pollute the environment. It can also release volatile organic compounds such as styrene and butadiene into the air. It can cause respiratory and neurological problems if not controlled or ventilated.

## Speciality

- Thiokol is a special synthetic rubber that has excellent elasticity, flexibility, and resistance to low temperatures, oxidation, and ozone. It can withstand extreme conditions and stresses without cracking or deteriorating. It is ideal for aerospace and marine applications.
- Nylon is a special synthetic polymer that has high strength, durability, and resistance to abrasion, heat, and chemicals. It can be spun into fine filaments or molded into various shapes and sizes. It is versatile and adaptable for various industrial and consumer applications.
- Buna-N is a special synthetic rubber that has high resistance to oil, fuel, and other chemicals. It can swell or shrink depending on the type and concentration of the solvent. It is selective and specific for different applications that require chemical compatibility and stability.
- Buna-S is a special synthetic rubber that has high resistance to abrasion, wear, and aging. It can be blended with other rubbers or fillers to modify its properties and performance. It is flexible and customizable for different applications that require mechanical strength and durability.
