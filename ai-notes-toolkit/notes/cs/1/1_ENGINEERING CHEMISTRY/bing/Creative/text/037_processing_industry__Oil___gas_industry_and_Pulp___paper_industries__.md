# Processing industry, Oil & gas industry and Pulp & paper industries

## Processing industry

- A processing industry is a type of industry that transforms raw materials or intermediate products into finished goods or services.
- Examples of processing industries include food and beverage, textile, chemical, pharmaceutical, metal, and petroleum industries.
- Processing industries can be classified into two main types: continuous and batch processing.
  - Continuous processing involves a constant flow of materials through a series of operations, such as in oil refining or paper making.
  - Batch processing involves processing discrete quantities of materials in separate batches, such as in baking or brewing.

## Oil & gas industry

- The oil & gas industry is a global sector that explores, produces, transports, refines, and markets crude oil, natural gas, and petroleum products.
- The oil & gas industry can be divided into three main segments: upstream, midstream, and downstream.
  - Upstream involves the exploration and production of oil and gas from reservoirs, wells, and fields.
  - Midstream involves the transportation and storage of oil and gas, such as by pipelines, tankers, and terminals.
  - Downstream involves the refining and processing of oil and gas into fuels, lubricants, chemicals, and other products, as well as the distribution and marketing of these products to end-users.
- The oil & gas industry is influenced by various factors, such as supply and demand, geopolitics, environmental regulations, technological innovations, and price fluctuations.

## Pulp & paper industry

- The pulp & paper industry is a sector that produces pulp, paper, and paperboard from wood and other raw materials, such as recycled paper, agricultural residues, and non-wood fibers.
- The pulp & paper industry can be divided into four main segments: pulp, paper, paperboard, and tissue.
  - Pulp is the fibrous material that is produced by mechanically or chemically separating wood or other fibers, and is used as the main ingredient for paper and paperboard.
  - Paper is a thin sheet of material that is made by forming a pulp slurry into a web and drying it, and is used for various purposes, such as writing, printing, packaging, and hygiene.
  - Paperboard is a thicker and stiffer type of paper that is made by laminating or compressing multiple layers of paper, and is used for applications that require more strength and durability, such as boxes, cartons, and containers.
  - Tissue is a soft and absorbent type of paper that is made by creping a thin sheet of paper, and is used for products that require high softness and absorbency, such as toilet paper, facial tissue, and napkins.
- The pulp & paper industry is affected by various factors, such as consumer demand, environmental sustainability, energy efficiency, and digitalization.
