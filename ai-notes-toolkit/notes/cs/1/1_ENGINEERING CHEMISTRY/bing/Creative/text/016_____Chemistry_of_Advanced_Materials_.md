### Chemistry of Advanced Materials:

- Chemistry of advanced materials is the study of the design, synthesis, characterization and application of materials that have novel properties and functions.
- Advanced materials include nanomaterials, biomaterials, energy materials, smart materials, functional materials and more.
- The study of advanced materials in chemistry has helped to develop synthetic chemicals, the interplay between the structure of materials, the processing methods to manipulate them and the resulting properties and performance.
- Some of the principles and concepts involved in chemistry of advanced materials are:

  - Molecular self-assembly: the spontaneous organization of molecules into ordered structures by non-covalent interactions.
  - Supramolecular chemistry: the chemistry of molecular assemblies and intermolecular bonds that are beyond the scope of individual molecules.
  - Crystal engineering: the design and synthesis of molecular crystals with desired properties and functions.
  - Nanotechnology: the manipulation of matter at the nanoscale (1-100 nm) to create new materials and devices with novel properties and functions.
  - Biomimetics: the imitation of natural systems and processes to create new materials and devices with enhanced performance and functionality.
  - Green chemistry: the design of chemical products and processes that reduce or eliminate the use and generation of hazardous substances.

- Some of the applications of advanced materials in chemistry are:

  - Nanomedicine: the use of nanomaterials for diagnosis, imaging, drug delivery, therapy and biosensing of diseases.
  - Renewable energy: the use of energy materials such as solar cells, fuel cells, batteries, supercapacitors and thermoelectrics for converting and storing energy from renewable sources.
  - Sensors and actuators: the use of smart materials and functional materials that can sense and respond to external stimuli such as light, temperature, pressure, electric field, magnetic field, etc.
  - Catalysis: the use of nanomaterials and supramolecular assemblies as catalysts for enhancing the rate and selectivity of chemical reactions.
  - Electronics and photonics: the use of nanomaterials and molecular crystals for creating new devices and systems for information processing, communication, storage and display.
