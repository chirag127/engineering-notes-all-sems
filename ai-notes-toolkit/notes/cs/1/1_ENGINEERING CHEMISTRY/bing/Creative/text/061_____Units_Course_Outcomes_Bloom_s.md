### Units Course Outcomes Bloom’s

- The course outcomes of units are the specific learning objectives that students are expected to achieve by the end of the course.
- The course outcomes of units are aligned with the Bloom's taxonomy of cognitive domains, which classifies learning into six levels: knowledge, comprehension, application, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation.
- The course outcomes of units are written in measurable and observable terms, using action verbs that correspond to the appropriate level of Bloom's taxonomy.
- The course outcomes of units are used to guide the design of the curriculum, the assessment methods, and the feedback strategies for the course.
- The course outcomes of units are communicated to the students at the beginning of the course, and are reviewed periodically throughout the course to monitor the students' progress and achievement.
- The course outcomes of units are evaluated at the end of the course, using various tools and techniques, such as rubrics, portfolios, tests, quizzes, projects, etc.
- The course outcomes of units are reviewed and revised regularly, based on the feedback from the students, the instructors, and the external stakeholders, such as employers, professional bodies, accreditation agencies, etc.
