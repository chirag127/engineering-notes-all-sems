# Organometallic Compounds (RMgX and LiAlH4)

- Organometallic compounds are compounds that contain a metal-carbon bond, R-M.
- RMgX and LiAlH4 are two important types of organometallic compounds that are widely used in organic synthesis.
- RMgX is also known as a Grignard reagent, named after Victor Grignard, who discovered them in 1900. X is a halogen atom (Cl, Br, I) and R is an alkyl or aryl group.
- LiAlH4 is also known as lithium aluminium hydride, a powerful reducing agent that can reduce various carbonyl groups to alcohols.

## Properties and Preparation of RMgX

- RMgX are usually prepared by reacting an alkyl or aryl halide with magnesium metal in an ether solvent, such as diethyl ether or tetrahydrofuran (THF) .
- RMgX are highly reactive and must be kept away from moisture, oxygen, and acidic compounds, as they can react violently with them .
- RMgX exist as tetrahedral complexes with the ether solvent molecules, as shown below for methylmagnesium chloride:

![Methylmagnesium chloride complex](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/5a/MeMgCl-THF-2D.png/220px-MeMgCl-THF-2D.png)

- RMgX are strong nucleophiles and bases, and can attack electrophilic carbon atoms, such as those in carbonyl groups, epoxides, alkyl halides, and carbon dioxide  .
- RMgX can also react with other metal halides to form organometallic compounds of different metals, such as RLi, RCu, and RZn .

## Properties and Preparation of LiAlH4

- LiAlH4 is usually prepared by reacting lithium hydride with aluminium chloride in an ether solvent.
- LiAlH4 is a white solid that can ignite spontaneously in air, and must be stored under an inert atmosphere.
- LiAlH4 is a very strong reducing agent that can reduce various carbonyl groups to alcohols, such as aldehydes, ketones, esters, amides, and carboxylic acids .
- LiAlH4 can also reduce other functional groups, such as nitriles, imines, oximes, and nitro groups .
- LiAlH4 reacts with water to produce hydrogen gas and lithium hydroxide, and with alcohols to produce alkoxides and aluminium alkoxides .
