## Unit-1: 8

- Unit-1: 8 is a topic in mathematics that covers the following subtopics:
  - Exponents and radicals
  - Scientific notation
  - Rational and irrational numbers
  - Real numbers and their properties
  - Operations with real numbers
  - Order of operations and simplifying expressions
- The main objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand the meaning and rules of exponents and radicals
  - Convert between standard form and scientific notation
  - Classify numbers as rational or irrational and locate them on the number line
  - Identify and apply the properties of real numbers
  - Perform operations with real numbers using the correct order of operations
  - Simplify algebraic expressions by combining like terms and using the distributive property
- Some key terms and definitions in this unit are:
  - Exponent: a number that indicates how many times a base is multiplied by itself
  - Radical: a symbol that indicates the root of a number
  - Scientific notation: a way of writing very large or very small numbers using a power of 10
  - Rational number: a number that can be written as a fraction of two integers
  - Irrational number: a number that cannot be written as a fraction of two integers
  - Real number: a number that can be located on the number line
  - Property: a rule that applies to all real numbers
  - Order of operations: a set of rules that determines the order in which calculations are performed
  - Expression: a combination of numbers, variables, and operations
  - Term: a single number, variable, or product of numbers and variables
  - Like terms: terms that have the same variable and exponent
  - Distributive property: a property that states that a(b + c) = ab + ac
