# Industrial Process

- An industrial process is a series of operations or steps that transform raw materials or inputs into finished products or outputs.
- Industrial processes can involve physical, chemical, biological, or mechanical changes, or a combination of these.
- Industrial processes can be classified into two main types: continuous and batch.
  - Continuous processes operate without interruption and produce a steady stream of output. Examples of continuous processes are oil refining, paper making, and electricity generation.
  - Batch processes operate in discrete cycles and produce a finite amount of output per cycle. Examples of batch processes are baking, brewing, and pharmaceutical manufacturing.
- Industrial processes can have various objectives, such as increasing efficiency, reducing costs, improving quality, enhancing safety, or minimizing environmental impact.
- Industrial processes can be optimized by applying various methods, such as lean manufacturing, six sigma, total quality management, or statistical process control.
