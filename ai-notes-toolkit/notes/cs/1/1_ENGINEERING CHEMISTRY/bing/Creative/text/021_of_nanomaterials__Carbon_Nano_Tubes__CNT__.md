# Nanomaterials, Carbon Nano Tubes (CNT)

- Nanomaterials are materials that have at least one dimension in the range of 1 to 100 nanometers (nm).
- Carbon nanomaterials are a family of carbon allotropes, consisting of different shapes and sizes of carbon atoms arranged in various ways.
- Carbon nano tubes (CNT) are one type of carbon nanomaterials that are formed by rolling up a two-dimensional hexagonal lattice of carbon atoms into a hollow cylindrical structure .
- CNTs can be classified into single-wall carbon nanotubes (SWCNTs) and multi-wall carbon nanotubes (MWCNTs), depending on the number of layers of carbon atoms in the tube wall.
- CNTs have unique physical and chemical properties, such as high strength, stiffness, electrical and thermal conductivity, and surface area .
- CNTs have potential applications in various fields, such as electronics, nanomedicine, energy, sensors, composites, and coatings  .
