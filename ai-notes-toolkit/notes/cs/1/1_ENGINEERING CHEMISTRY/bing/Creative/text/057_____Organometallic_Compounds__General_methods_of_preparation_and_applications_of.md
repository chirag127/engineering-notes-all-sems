### Organometallic Compounds: General methods of preparation and applications

- Organometallic compounds are compounds that contain one or more metal-carbon bonds.
- They are classified according to the number and type of metal-carbon bonds, such as ionic, covalent, or mixed.
- They have diverse physical and chemical properties, depending on the nature of the metal and the organic ligand.

#### General methods of preparation

- Most organometallic compounds can be synthesized by using one of four M-C bond forming reactions :
  - Reaction with metal and transmetallation: The net reaction of an electropositive metal M and a halogen-substituted hydrocarbon is
    $$M + R-X \rightarrow M^+X^- + R^- \rightarrow M-R + X^-$$
    where R is an organic group and X is a halogen. This reaction can also be used to exchange one metal for another, such as
    $$M-R + M'-X \rightarrow M-X + M'-R$$
    where M and M' are different metals.
  - Metal displacement: The reaction of a less electropositive metal M with an organometallic compound of a more electropositive metal M' is
    $$M + M'-R \rightarrow M-R + M'$$
    This reaction can be used to prepare organometallic compounds of transition metals, such as
    $$Fe + 2Li-C_5H_5 \rightarrow Fe(C_5H_5)_2 + 2Li$$
  - Metathesis: The reaction of two organometallic compounds with different metals is
    $$M-R + M'-R' \rightarrow M-R' + M'-R$$
    This reaction can be used to prepare organometallic compounds with different organic groups, such as
    $$Li-CH_3 + Mg-C_2H_5 \rightarrow Li-C_2H_5 + Mg-CH_3$$
  - Hydrometallation: The reaction of a metal hydride M-H with an unsaturated organic compound is
    $$M-H + R-R' \rightarrow M-R + H-R'$$
    where R-R' is a double or triple bond. This reaction can be used to prepare organometallic compounds with alkenyl or alkynyl groups, such as
    $$Li-H + CH_2=CH_2 \rightarrow Li-CH_2CH_2 + H_2$$

#### General applications

- Organometallic compounds have many applications in the field of chemistry. Some of them are listed below:
  - Organometallic compounds are used as homogeneous catalysts in certain commercial chemical reactions, such as hydroformylation, hydrogenation, polymerization, and metathesis.
  - They can be used as stoichiometric reagents for both research-oriented and industrial chemical reactions, such as cross-coupling, C-H activation, and carbonylation.
  - They can be used as precursors for the synthesis of other organometallic compounds, such as metal carbonyls, metal clusters, and metal-organic frameworks.
  - They can be used as intermediates for the synthesis of organic compounds, such as alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, and carboxylic acids.
  - They can be used as probes for the study of the structure and bonding of metal complexes, such as NMR, IR, and X-ray spectroscopy.
