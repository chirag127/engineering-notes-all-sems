# Paris

Paris is the capital and most populous city of France, with an estimated population of 2,165,423 residents in 2019 in an area of more than 105 km² (41 sq mi), making it the fourth-most populated city in the European Union as well as the 30th most densely populated city in the world in 2022. Paris is a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture, and is often regarded as the most romantic city in the world.

Some of the main points to know about Paris are:

- Paris is situated in the north-central part of France, along the Seine River, which divides the city into two parts: the Right Bank and the Left Bank.
- Paris has a rich and turbulent history, dating back to the 3rd century BCE, when it was founded by a Celtic tribe called the Parisii. It became the capital of the Frankish kingdom in the 6th century CE, and later the seat of the French monarchy, the French Revolution, and the French Empire.
- Paris is home to many iconic landmarks, such as the Eiffel Tower, the Arc de Triomphe, the Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Louvre Museum, and the Palace of Versailles. It also has many museums, art galleries, parks, gardens, and monuments that showcase its cultural and artistic heritage.
- Paris is known for its cuisine, which includes dishes such as croissants, macarons, frog legs, steak and frites, eclairs, and French onion soup. It also has a reputation for its wine, cheese, and chocolate, which are popular souvenirs for tourists.
- Paris is a major hub for fashion, hosting the Paris Fashion Week twice a year, and being the birthplace of many famous designers, such as Coco Chanel, Christian Dior, and Yves Saint Laurent. It also has many boutiques, department stores, and flea markets that offer a variety of styles and trends.
- Paris is a vibrant and diverse city, with many neighborhoods, districts, and quarters that have their own character and atmosphere. Some of the most famous ones are Montmartre, the Latin Quarter, the Marais, and the Champs-Élysées.
- Paris is a popular destination for travelers, attracting more than 30 million visitors annually. It offers many attractions, activities, and events for different interests and preferences, such as sightseeing, shopping, dining, nightlife, entertainment, and romance.
