# When used as engineering materials. Understanding the applications of specific polymers and Chemistry

- Polymers are large molecules composed of repeating units called monomers, which are linked together by covalent bonds.
- Polymers can be classified into natural and synthetic polymers, depending on their origin and composition.
- Natural polymers are derived from biological sources, such as proteins, cellulose, starch, and rubber. Synthetic polymers are artificially created by chemical reactions, such as plastics, nylon, and silicone.
- Polymers have diverse physical and chemical properties, such as hardness, flexibility, solubility, biodegradability, and thermal stability, which depend on their molecular structure, size, and arrangement.
- Polymers have many applications in engineering and technology, such as:
  - Aircraft, aerospace, and sports equipment: Polymers are used to make lightweight, strong, and durable materials, such as carbon fiber, epoxy resin, and polyurethane foam.
  - 3D printing plastics: Polymers are used to create complex shapes and structures by depositing layers of molten or solidified material, such as polylactic acid, acrylonitrile butadiene styrene, and polyethylene terephthalate.
  - Biopolymers in molecular recognition: Polymers are used to design molecules that can bind to specific targets, such as enzymes, antibodies, and DNA, for applications in biotechnology, medicine, and nanotechnology.
  - Polymers in holography: Polymers are used to create holograms, which are three-dimensional images formed by the interference of light waves, for applications in security, entertainment, and data storage.
  - Organic polymers used in water purification: Polymers are used to remove contaminants from water, such as heavy metals, organic pollutants, and microorganisms, by adsorption, filtration, or coagulation.
  - Printed circuit board substrates: Polymers are used to make the base material for electronic circuits, such as polyimide, epoxy, and polytetrafluoroethylene, which provide electrical insulation, mechanical support, and thermal stability.
  - Green chemicals: Polymers and biopolymers: Polymers are used to make environmentally friendly chemicals, such as biodegradable plastics, biofuels, and biocatalysts, which reduce the use of fossil fuels, toxic substances, and waste generation.
  - Polymeric biomolecules: Polymers are used to study and manipulate biological molecules, such as DNA, RNA, and proteins, which are essential for life and perform various functions in cells.
