## Course Objectives:

- The course objectives are the specific learning outcomes that the students are expected to achieve by the end of the course.
- The course objectives should be aligned with the course description, the course learning outcomes, and the assessment methods.
- The course objectives should be clear, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.
- The course objectives should be written using action verbs that indicate the level of cognitive, affective, or psychomotor skills that the students will demonstrate.
- The course objectives should be communicated to the students at the beginning of the course and throughout the course as a guide for their learning.
- The course objectives should be evaluated periodically to ensure that they are appropriate, realistic, and consistent with the course content and expectations.
