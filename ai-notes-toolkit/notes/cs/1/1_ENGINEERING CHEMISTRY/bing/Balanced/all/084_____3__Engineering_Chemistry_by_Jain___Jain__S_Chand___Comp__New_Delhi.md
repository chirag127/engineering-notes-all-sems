# Engineering Chemistry by Jain & Jain, S.Chand & Comp, New Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by Jain & Jain and published by S.Chand & Comp, New Delhi.
- The book covers the basic concepts and applications of chemistry in various fields of engineering, such as materials, energy, environment, and biotechnology.
- The book is divided into 16 chapters, each with a summary, review questions, and numerical problems.
- The chapters are as follows:

  1. Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding
  2. Periodic Properties and Hydrogen
  3. s-Block Elements
  4. p-Block Elements
  5. d- and f-Block Elements
  6. Coordination Compounds and Organometallics
  7. Chemical Thermodynamics
  8. Chemical Kinetics and Catalysis
  9. Electrochemistry
  10. Solutions and Colligative Properties
  11. Phase Equilibria and Phase Rule
  12. Chemical Equilibria and Ionic Equilibria
  13. Water and its Treatment
  14. Fuels and Combustion
  15. Polymers and Composites
  16. Corrosion and its Prevention

- The book is suitable for undergraduate students of engineering and technology, as well as for those preparing for competitive examinations.
