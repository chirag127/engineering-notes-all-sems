# Paris

Paris is the capital and most populous city of France, with an estimated population of 2,165,423 residents in 2019 in an area of more than 105 km² (41 sq mi), making it the fourth-most populated city in the European Union as well as the 30th most densely populated city in the world in 2022. Paris is a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture, and is often regarded as the most romantic city in the world.

Some of the main points to know about Paris are:

- Paris is situated in the north-central part of France, along the Seine River, which divides the city into two parts: the Right Bank and the Left Bank.
- Paris has a rich and turbulent history, dating back to the 3rd century BCE, when it was founded by a Celtic tribe called the Parisii. Paris was the capital of the Frankish kingdom, the French monarchy, the First French Republic, the First French Empire, and the Third French Republic, and witnessed many important events such as the French Revolution, the Reign of Terror, the Napoleonic Wars, the Paris Commune, the World Wars, and the May 1968 protests.
- Paris is home to many iconic landmarks, monuments, museums, and cultural sites, such as the Eiffel Tower, the Arc de Triomphe, the Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Louvre Museum, the Musée d'Orsay, the Sacré-Cœur Basilica, the Palace of Versailles, and the Champs-Élysées.
- Paris is also known for its distinctive architecture, which reflects its historical and artistic influences, such as the Gothic, Renaissance, Baroque, Neoclassical, Haussmannian, Art Nouveau, Art Deco, Modernist, and Postmodernist styles.
- Paris is a major hub for fashion, design, and luxury, hosting many prestigious events such as the Paris Fashion Week, the Paris Haute Couture Week, and the Paris Biennale. Paris is also the headquarters of many renowned fashion houses, such as Chanel, Dior, Louis Vuitton, Hermès, and Yves Saint Laurent.
- Paris is famous for its culinary scene, which offers a variety of dishes, from traditional French cuisine to international specialties. Some of the most popular foods to try in Paris are macarons, croissants, frog legs, steak and frites, eclairs, hot chocolate, French onion soup, and madeleines. Paris is also a great place to buy souvenirs, such as cheese, dried sausage, French chocolate, French macarons, wine, a marinière, a beret, or French candy.
- Paris has a vibrant and diverse cultural life, with many festivals, concerts, theaters, operas, cinemas, art galleries, libraries, and bookstores. Paris is also the birthplace or the residence of many famous artists, writers, musicians, philosophers, and scientists, such as Victor Hugo, Voltaire, Émile Zola, Marcel Proust, Charles Baudelaire, Claude Monet, Edgar Degas, Auguste Rodin, Pierre and Marie Curie, Édith Piaf, Coco Chanel, Jean-Paul Sartre, Simone de Beauvoir, and many more.
