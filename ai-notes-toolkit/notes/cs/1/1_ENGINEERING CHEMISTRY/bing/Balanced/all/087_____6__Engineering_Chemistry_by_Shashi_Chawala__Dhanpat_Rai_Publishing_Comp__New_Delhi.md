# Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawla

- Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawla is a textbook for engineering students of various branches, covering the syllabus of engineering chemistry courses offered by Indian universities  .
- The book is divided into 26 chapters, covering topics such as atomic structure, chemical bonding, phase rule, solutions, electrochemistry, corrosion, polymers, fuels, water, instrumental methods of analysis, and environmental chemistry .
- The book aims to provide a clear and concise explanation of the fundamental concepts and principles of chemistry, with an emphasis on their applications in engineering problems.
- The book also contains numerous solved examples, illustrations, tables, and diagrams to aid the understanding of the topics .
- The book is widely recommended in most engineering colleges of India, and has received positive reviews from students and teachers for its comprehensive coverage, simple language, and logical presentation .
