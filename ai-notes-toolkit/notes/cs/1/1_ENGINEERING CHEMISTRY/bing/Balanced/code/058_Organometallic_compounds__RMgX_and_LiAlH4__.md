# Organometallic Compounds (RMgX and LiAlH4)

- Organometallic compounds are compounds that contain a metal-carbon bond, R-M.
- RMgX and LiAlH4 are two important types of organometallic compounds that are used in organic synthesis as nucleophiles.
- RMgX is also known as a Grignard reagent, named after Victor Grignard, who discovered them in 1900.
- RMgX is formed by the reaction of an alkyl or aryl halide with magnesium metal in an ether solvent.
- LiAlH4 is also known as lithium aluminium hydride, and is formed by the reaction of lithium hydride with aluminium chloride.
- RMgX and LiAlH4 can react with various organic carbonyl compounds, such as aldehydes, ketones, esters, and acid chlorides, to form new carbon-carbon bonds and alcohols.
- RMgX and LiAlH4 are strong bases and nucleophiles, and can also react with other electrophiles, such as water, alcohols, amines, and halogens.
- RMgX and LiAlH4 are sensitive to moisture and air, and must be handled under inert conditions.
