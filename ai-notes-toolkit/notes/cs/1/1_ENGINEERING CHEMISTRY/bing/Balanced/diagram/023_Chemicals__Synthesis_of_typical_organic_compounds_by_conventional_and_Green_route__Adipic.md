# Synthesis of Adipic Acid by Conventional and Green Route

Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid with the formula HOOC(CH2)4COOH. It is mainly used as a precursor for the production of nylon 6,6.

## Conventional Route

The conventional route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the oxidation of benzene with nitric acid to produce nitrobenzene, followed by hydrogenation to produce cyclohexane, and then further oxidation with nitric acid to produce adipic acid. This process requires high temperature and pressure, and generates large amounts of nitrous oxide, a greenhouse gas, as a by-product.

## Green Route

The green route for the synthesis of adipic acid uses glucose as a renewable starting material, instead of benzene. Glucose can be obtained from biomass, such as cellulose or starch. The green route involves two main steps: the oxidation of glucose to glucaric acid, and the dehydration of glucaric acid to adipic acid. The oxidation of glucose can be catalyzed by platinum nanoparticles supported on carbon nanotubes, and the dehydration of glucaric acid can be catalyzed by solid acid catalysts, such as zeolites or sulfated zirconia. This process requires lower temperature and pressure, and does not produce nitrous oxide as a by-product.
