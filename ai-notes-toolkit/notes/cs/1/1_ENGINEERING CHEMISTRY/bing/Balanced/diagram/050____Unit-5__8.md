## Unit-5: 8

- This unit covers the topic of **8-bit microprocessors**, which are electronic devices that can perform arithmetic and logic operations on 8-bit binary data.
- The unit introduces the basic concepts of microprocessor architecture, instruction set, addressing modes, and interfacing techniques.
- The unit also discusses the features and applications of some popular 8-bit microprocessors, such as the **Intel 8085**, the **Motorola 6800**, and the **Zilog Z80**.
- The unit consists of the following subtopics:

  - **5.1 Introduction to 8-bit microprocessors**: This subtopic explains the definition, functions, and components of a microprocessor, and compares the advantages and disadvantages of 8-bit microprocessors with other types of microprocessors.
  - **5.2 Intel 8085 microprocessor**: This subtopic describes the architecture, instruction set, addressing modes, and interfacing techniques of the Intel 8085 microprocessor, which is one of the most widely used 8-bit microprocessors in the industry.
  - **5.3 Motorola 6800 microprocessor**: This subtopic describes the architecture, instruction set, addressing modes, and interfacing techniques of the Motorola 6800 microprocessor, which is another popular 8-bit microprocessor that has a similar structure and functionality to the Intel 8085 microprocessor.
  - **5.4 Zilog Z80 microprocessor**: This subtopic describes the architecture, instruction set, addressing modes, and interfacing techniques of the Zilog Z80 microprocessor, which is an enhanced version of the Intel 8085 microprocessor that offers more features and capabilities.
  - **5.5 Comparison of 8-bit microprocessors**: This subtopic compares the features, performance, and applications of the Intel 8085, the Motorola 6800, and the Zilog Z80 microprocessors, and highlights their similarities and differences.
