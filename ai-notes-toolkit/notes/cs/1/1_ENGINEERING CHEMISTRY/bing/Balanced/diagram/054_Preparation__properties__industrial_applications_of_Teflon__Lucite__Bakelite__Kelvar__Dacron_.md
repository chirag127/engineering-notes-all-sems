# Teflon
- Teflon is the trade name of **polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE)**, a synthetic polymer of tetrafluoroethylene (C2F4).
- Teflon is prepared by **free radical polymerization** of tetrafluoroethylene in a water emulsion at high pressure and temperature, using a peroxide or persulfate initiator.
- Teflon has the following properties:
  - It is a **white solid** compound at room temperature.
  - Its density is about **2200 kg/m3** or **2.2 g/cm3**.
  - Its melting point is **600 K**.
  - It is a **chemical resistance** compound, the only chemicals that can affect it are alkali metals.
  - It shows good resistance towards **heat and low temperature**.
  - It has a **low water absorption** capacity.
  - It has a **low coefficient of friction**, making it **non-sticky** and **non-wetting**.
- Teflon has the following industrial applications:
  - It is used as a **coating** for non-stick cookware, electrical wires, pipes, valves, seals, bearings, and gaskets.
  - It is used as a **lubricant** for machinery, gears, and chains.
  - It is used as a **fabric** for clothing, carpets, tents, and protective suits.
  - It is used as a **biomaterial** for artificial joints, heart valves, and vascular grafts.

# Lucite
- Lucite is the trade name of **polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA)**, a synthetic polymer of methyl methacrylate (C5H8O2).
- Lucite is prepared by **free radical polymerization** of methyl methacrylate in a bulk or suspension process, using a peroxide or azo initiator.
- Lucite has the following properties:
  - It is a **transparent** and **colorless** solid at room temperature.
  - Its density is about **1190 kg/m3** or **1.19 g/cm3**.
  - Its glass transition temperature is **105 °C**.
  - It is **resistant** to ultraviolet light, weathering, and chemicals, except for strong acids and bases.
  - It has a **high refractive index** of **1.49**, making it **optically clear** and **shiny**.
  - It has a **high tensile strength** of **65 MPa**, but a **low impact strength** of **1.6 kJ/m2**.
- Lucite has the following industrial applications:
  - It is used as a **material** for windows, lenses, screens, signs, displays, furniture, jewelry, and art.
  - It is used as a **coating** for paints, varnishes, and adhesives.
  - It is used as a **molding** for dental prosthetics, implants, and orthodontic appliances.
  - It is used as a **fiber** for textiles, wigs, and optical fibers.
