## Hours

- An hour is a unit of time that is equal to 60 minutes or 3,600 seconds.
- The symbol for hour is h or hr.
- There are 24 hours in a day, which is the time it takes for the Earth to rotate once around its axis.
- The hour is divided into smaller units, such as half an hour (30 minutes), quarter of an hour (15 minutes), and minute (60 seconds).
- The hour is also used to measure angles, with one hour being equal to 15 degrees of arc.
- The hour is based on the sexagesimal system, which uses the number 60 as its base. This system was developed by the ancient Babylonians and is still used for measuring time and angles today.
- The hour is not a standard unit in the International System of Units (SI), but it is accepted for use with SI.
