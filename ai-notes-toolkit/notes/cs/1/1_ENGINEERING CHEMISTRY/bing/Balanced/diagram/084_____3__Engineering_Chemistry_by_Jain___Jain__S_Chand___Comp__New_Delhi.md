Engineering Chemistry by Jain & Jain, S.Chand & Comp, New Delhi is a textbook for engineering students of various branches. It covers the following topics:

- Atoms and molecules: This unit deals with the structure and properties of atoms and molecules, such as atomic number, mass number, isotopes, isobars, molecular orbital theory, hybridization, bond order, etc.
- Chemical bonding and molecular structure: This unit explains the types and theories of chemical bonding, such as ionic, covalent, metallic, hydrogen, coordinate, and van der Waals bonds, VSEPR theory, valence bond theory, etc.
- Periodic properties of elements: This unit discusses the trends and variations of periodic properties of elements, such as atomic and ionic radii, ionization energy, electron affinity, electronegativity, etc.
- Chemical thermodynamics: This unit introduces the concepts and laws of thermodynamics, such as system, surroundings, state functions, enthalpy, entropy, Gibbs free energy, etc. It also covers the applications of thermodynamics in chemical reactions, such as Hess's law, Kirchhoff's equation, etc.
- Chemical kinetics: This unit deals with the rate and mechanism of chemical reactions, such as rate law, order, molecularity, rate constant, activation energy, Arrhenius equation, collision theory, etc. It also covers the factors affecting the rate of reaction, such as concentration, temperature, catalyst, etc.
- Chemical equilibrium: This unit explains the concept and criteria of chemical equilibrium, such as equilibrium constant, Le Chatelier's principle, etc. It also covers the types of chemical equilibrium, such as homogeneous, heterogeneous, acid-base, solubility, complexation, etc.
- Electrochemistry: This unit covers the principles and applications of electrochemistry, such as electrochemical cells, galvanic cells, electrolytic cells, standard electrode potentials, Nernst equation, Faraday's laws, etc. It also covers the topics of corrosion, batteries, fuel cells, etc.
- Solutions: This unit discusses the properties and types of solutions, such as ideal and non-ideal solutions, colligative properties, Raoult's law, Henry's law, etc. It also covers the methods of expressing the concentration of solutions, such as molarity, molality, normality, etc.
- Phase rule: This unit introduces the phase rule and its applications, such as one-component and two-component systems, phase diagrams, eutectic point, etc.
- Colloids: This unit deals with the classification and properties of colloids, such as lyophilic and lyophobic colloids, Tyndall effect, Brownian movement, electrophoresis, coagulation, etc. It also covers the methods of preparation and purification of colloids, such as peptization, dialysis, etc.
- Polymers: This unit covers the concepts and types of polymers, such as monomers, degree of polymerization, addition and condensation polymers, etc. It also covers the properties and applications of polymers, such as molecular weight, crystallinity, glass transition temperature, etc.
- Fuels: This unit discusses the classification and characteristics of fuels, such as solid, liquid, and gaseous fuels, calorific value, combustion, etc. It also covers the analysis and evaluation of fuels, such as proximate and ultimate analysis, bomb calorimeter, etc.
- Water: This unit covers the sources and properties of water, such as hardness, alkalinity, dissolved oxygen, etc. It also covers the methods of treatment and purification of water, such as coagulation, filtration, disinfection, ion exchange, reverse osmosis, etc.
- Environmental chemistry: This unit deals with the sources and effects of environmental pollution, such as air, water, soil, and noise pollution, greenhouse effect, ozone depletion, acid rain, etc. It also covers the methods of prevention and control of environmental pollution, such as catalytic converters, scrubbers, bioremediation, etc.
