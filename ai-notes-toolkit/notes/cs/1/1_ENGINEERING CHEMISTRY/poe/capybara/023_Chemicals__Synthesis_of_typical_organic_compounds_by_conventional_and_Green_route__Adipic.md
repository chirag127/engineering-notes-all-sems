# Chemicals: Synthesis of Typical Organic Compounds by Conventional and Green Route (Adipic)

Organic compounds are widely used in various industries such as pharmaceuticals, polymers, and agrochemicals. Adipic acid is one such organic compound that is used in the manufacturing of nylon, polyurethane, and other polymers. The synthesis of adipic acid can be done through both conventional and green routes. In this section, we will discuss the synthesis of adipic acid through both these routes.

## Conventional Route

The conventional route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the following steps:

1. Oxidation of cyclohexane: Cyclohexane is oxidized using a mixture of concentrated nitric acid and air to form cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol.
2. Formation of adipic acid: The mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol is oxidized using nitric acid and air to form adipic acid.

The conventional route has some disadvantages such as the use of concentrated nitric acid, which is hazardous and can cause environmental pollution. Moreover, the process involves the production of large amounts of waste products.

## Green Route

The green route for the synthesis of adipic acid involves the following steps:

1. Formation of cyclohexene: Cyclohexene is formed by the dehydration of cyclohexanol.
2. Epoxidation of cyclohexene: Cyclohexene is epoxidized using hydrogen peroxide and a titanium silicalite catalyst to form cyclohexene oxide.
3. Formation of adipic acid: Cyclohexene oxide is hydrolyzed to form adipic acid.

The green route is a more environmentally friendly method as it eliminates the use of nitric acid and produces fewer waste products. Moreover, the use of a titanium silicalite catalyst makes the process more efficient and selective.

In conclusion, the synthesis of adipic acid can be done through both conventional and green routes. However, the green route is a more sustainable and environmentally friendly method that produces fewer waste products and eliminates the use of hazardous chemicals.
