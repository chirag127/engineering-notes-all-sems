### 3. Engineering Chemistry by Jain & Jain, S.Chand & Comp, New Delhi

Engineering Chemistry is an essential subject for engineering students. The book Engineering Chemistry by Jain & Jain, S.Chand & Comp, New Delhi provides a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter. The following are some of the important topics covered in the book:

- **Atomic Structure:** The book covers the basic atomic structure and explains the electronic configuration of atoms. It also covers the types of bonding in molecules and the concept of hybridization.

- **Chemical Thermodynamics:** The book explains the concept of thermodynamics and covers the laws of thermodynamics. It also covers the application of thermodynamics in chemical reactions.

- **Electrochemistry:** The book covers the basics of electrochemistry, including electrochemical cells, galvanic cells, and electrolysis. It also explains the applications of electrochemistry in industries.

- **Corrosion and Corrosion Control:** The book explains the types of corrosion and the factors affecting corrosion. It also covers the methods of corrosion control, including cathodic protection and coatings.

- **Water Treatment:** The book covers the basics of water treatment, including the types of impurities in water and the methods of water purification. It also explains the process of water softening and the methods of disinfection.

- **Fuels and Combustion:** The book covers the types of fuels, including solid, liquid, and gaseous fuels. It also explains the principles of combustion and the types of combustion chambers.

- **Polymers:** The book covers the basics of polymers, including their classification, synthesis, and properties. It also covers the applications of polymers in industries.

In conclusion, Engineering Chemistry by Jain & Jain, S.Chand & Comp, New Delhi is an excellent book for engineering students who want to gain a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter. The book covers all the important topics and provides practical applications of the concepts discussed.
