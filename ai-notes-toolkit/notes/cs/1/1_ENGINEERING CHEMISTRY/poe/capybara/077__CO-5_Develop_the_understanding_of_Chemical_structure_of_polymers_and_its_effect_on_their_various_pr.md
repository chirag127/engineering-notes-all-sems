### CO-5 Develop the understanding of Chemical structure of polymers and its effect on their various properties.

Polymers are large molecules composed of repeating structural units, or monomers. The chemical structure of polymers plays a crucial role in determining their physical and chemical properties. In this section, we will discuss the chemical structure of polymers and how it affects their various properties.

1. **Polymerization:** Polymerization is the process of combining monomers to form a polymer. There are two types of polymerization: addition polymerization and condensation polymerization.

2. **Addition Polymerization:** In addition polymerization, the monomer units are simply added to the growing polymer chain without the elimination of any byproducts. Addition polymers are usually made from unsaturated monomers such as ethylene, propylene, and styrene.

3. **Condensation Polymerization:** In condensation polymerization, the monomers react with each other to form a polymer and a small molecule, such as water, is eliminated. Condensation polymers are usually made from monomers that have two functional groups, such as diacids and diamines.

4. **Chain Length:** The length of the polymer chain affects the physical and chemical properties of the polymer. Longer chains result in higher melting points and greater strength.

5. **Branching:** Branching occurs when a side chain is attached to the main polymer chain. Branching reduces the polymer's melting point and increases its flexibility.

6. **Cross-linking:** Cross-linking occurs when two or more polymer chains are joined together by covalent bonds. Cross-linking increases the polymer's strength, stiffness, and resistance to heat and chemicals.

7. **Stereochemistry:** The stereochemistry of a polymer refers to the spatial arrangement of the monomer units in the polymer chain. Stereochemistry affects the polymer's crystallinity and solubility.

8. **Copolymerization:** Copolymerization is the process of combining two or more different monomers to form a polymer. Copolymers often exhibit unique properties that are different from those of the individual homopolymers.

In conclusion, the chemical structure of polymers plays a vital role in determining their physical and chemical properties. Understanding the chemical structure of polymers is essential in designing and developing new polymers with specific properties for various applications.
