
# Processing Industry, Oil & Gas Industry and Pulp & Paper Industries

1. Processing Industry: This industry encompasses a broad range of activities that involve the transformation of raw materials into products that are ready for consumption. This includes activities such as refining, packaging, and distribution. 

2. Oil & Gas Industry: This industry involves the extraction, refining, and production of oil and gas. It includes activities such as exploration, drilling, and production of oil and gas. 

3. Pulp & Paper Industry: This industry involves the production of paper and other paper-based products. It includes activities such as the production of pulp and paper, bleaching, and the production of paper products.
