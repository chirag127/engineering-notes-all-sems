
### 4. To Enable Students to Understand and Apply Detailed Concepts of Water Source, Water Impurities, and Hardness

1. Water Source: Water sources can be classified as surface water, such as lakes and rivers, or ground water, such as aquifers. Surface water is more exposed to contamination from pollutants, while ground water is more protected, but can still contain pollutants.

2. Water Impurities: Water impurities can be classified as organic or inorganic. Organic impurities include bacteria, viruses, and other microorganisms, while inorganic impurities include minerals, metals, and other chemicals.

3. Hardness: Hardness is a measure of the amount of calcium and magnesium in the water. Hard water can cause scale buildup in pipes and appliances, and can also affect the taste of the water.
