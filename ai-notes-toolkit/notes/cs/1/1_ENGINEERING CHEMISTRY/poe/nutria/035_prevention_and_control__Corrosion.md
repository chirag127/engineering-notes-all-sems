
# Prevention and Control of Corrosion

* Corrosion is the gradual destruction of materials (usually metals) by chemical and/or electrochemical reaction with their environment.
* Corrosion can be prevented or controlled by using corrosion-resistant materials, such as stainless steel, or by applying protective coatings, such as paint.
* Corrosion can also be prevented or controlled by using inhibitors, such as inhibitors that form a protective film on the surface of the metal.
* Corrosion can also be prevented or controlled by using cathodic protection, which involves using an external electrical current to reduce the corrosion rate of the metal.
* Corrosion can also be prevented or controlled by using sacrificial anodes, which are materials that are more easily corroded than the metal that is being protected.
* Corrosion can also be prevented or controlled by using coatings, such as galvanizing, which involves coating the metal with a layer of zinc.
* Corrosion can also be prevented or controlled by using environmental control, such as controlling the temperature and humidity of the environment.
