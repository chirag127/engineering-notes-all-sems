
# Paris (POP)

* Paris (POP) is an international agreement signed by 196 countries in December 2015 to combat climate change and its effects.
* It is the first legally binding universal agreement on climate change, which aims to reduce global emissions and keep global temperature rise this century well below 2°C.
* The agreement sets out a global action plan to reduce emissions and adapt to the impacts of climate change. It also includes provisions for developing countries to receive financial and technological support to adapt to climate change.
* The agreement also includes a global stocktake every five years to assess collective progress towards achieving the long-term goal of the agreement.
* The Paris Agreement is an important step forward in the global effort to tackle climate change and its impacts.
