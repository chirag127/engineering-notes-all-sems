
### CO-4 Develop understanding of the sources, impurities and hardness of water, apply the concepts of

1. Sources of Water: Water can come from a variety of sources, including rivers, lakes, streams, wells, springs, and glaciers.

2. Impurities in Water: Water may contain a variety of impurities, such as minerals, organic matter, bacteria, and other pollutants.

3. Hardness of Water: Hardness of water is determined by the amount of dissolved minerals, such as calcium and magnesium, in the water.

4. Concepts of Water Quality: Water quality is determined by a variety of factors, including pH, turbidity, conductivity, and dissolved oxygen. Water quality can be improved by treating the water with chemicals and/or filtration.
