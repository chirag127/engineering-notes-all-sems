
## Course Outcomes:
* Develop an understanding of the fundamental principles of computer science and programming.
* Learn to design and implement algorithms and data structures to solve problems.
* Develop an understanding of the principles of software engineering and design.
* Develop an understanding of the principles of distributed systems and networks.
* Develop an understanding of the principles of operating systems and databases.
* Develop an understanding of the principles of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and natural language processing.
* Demonstrate the ability to develop and implement a software project.
