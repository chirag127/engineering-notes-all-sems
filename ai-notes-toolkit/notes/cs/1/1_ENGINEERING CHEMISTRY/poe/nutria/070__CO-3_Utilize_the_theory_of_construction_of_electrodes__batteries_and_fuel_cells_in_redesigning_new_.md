
### CO-3 Utilize the theory of construction of electrodes, batteries and fuel cells in redesigning new engineering

1. Electrodes are components of a battery or fuel cell that allow the transfer of electrons from one side of the cell to the other.
2. Batteries are devices that store electrical energy and use it to power electronic devices.
3. Fuel cells are electrochemical devices that convert chemical energy into electrical energy.
4. The construction of electrodes, batteries and fuel cells involves the selection of materials and the design of the components.
5. The selection of materials includes the choice of active materials, current collectors, separators, and electrolytes.
6. The design of the components includes the selection of the shape, size, and configuration of the components.
7. The performance of the electrodes, batteries and fuel cells is affected by the design of the components and the selection of materials.
8. The performance of the electrodes, batteries and fuel cells can be improved by redesigning the components and selecting better materials.
9. Redesigning the components and selecting better materials can lead to the development of new engineering applications.
