
### Chemistry of Engineering Materials:
- Engineering materials are materials that have been developed and engineered to meet specific requirements or objectives. 
- These materials are typically designed to be strong, lightweight, and/or cost-effective.
- The chemical composition of engineering materials plays a major role in determining their properties and performance.
- Common types of engineering materials include metals, ceramics, polymers, and composites.
- Metals are materials composed of elements such as iron, aluminum, and copper. 
- Ceramics are materials composed of inorganic compounds such as silicates, oxides, and carbides.
- Polymers are materials composed of long chains of molecules and include plastics and rubbers.
- Composites are materials composed of two or more distinct materials that are combined to create a material with improved properties.
- The chemical composition of engineering materials affects their strength, hardness, ductility, and other physical properties.
- The chemical composition also affects the materials' thermal, electrical, and magnetic properties.
- The chemical composition of engineering materials also affects their corrosion resistance and other chemical properties.
