
### 2. Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd.

1. Engineering Chemistry is a branch of chemistry that focuses on the properties and uses of chemicals in engineering applications.
2. It is an important part of the engineering curriculum, as it provides an understanding of the physical and chemical principles that govern the behavior of materials in engineering applications.
3. The topics covered in Engineering Chemistry include thermodynamics, electrochemistry, corrosion, materials science, and catalysis.
4. The book by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd. covers all the topics in detail and provides a comprehensive overview of the subject.
5. It also includes a variety of examples and exercises to help students understand the concepts better.
6. The book is suitable for undergraduate engineering students and those preparing for competitive examinations.
