
### 3. To enable the students to understand and apply the concepts related to Electrochemistry, Batteries, Corrosion

1. Electrochemistry: 
    * Electrochemistry is the study of the relationship between electrical energy and chemical energy. It involves the study of the production of electricity from chemical reactions and the use of electrical energy to cause chemical reactions.
    * Electrochemistry is used in the study of batteries, fuel cells, electrolysis, corrosion, and the production of electrical energy from chemical sources.
2. Batteries: 
    * Batteries are devices that store electrical energy and convert it into chemical energy.
    * Batteries are composed of two or more electrochemical cells connected in series or parallel.
    * The chemical reactions in the cells produce electrical energy, which can be used to power electronic devices.
3. Corrosion: 
    * Corrosion is a process in which a material is attacked by chemical reactions with its environment.
    * Corrosion is caused by the presence of oxygen, water, and other chemicals that react with the material.
    * The corrosion process can be slowed down by using protective coatings or other methods.
