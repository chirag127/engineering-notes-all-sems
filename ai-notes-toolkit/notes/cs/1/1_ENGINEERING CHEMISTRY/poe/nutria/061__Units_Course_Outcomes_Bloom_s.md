
### Units Course Outcomes Bloom's

1. Understand the fundamentals of Bloom's Taxonomy, including the six levels of cognitive learning and their associated verbs.
2. Recognize how to apply Bloom's Taxonomy to create course outcomes that are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
3. Construct course outcomes that are measurable and assessable using Bloom's Taxonomy.
4. Identify the appropriate level of cognitive learning for each course outcome.
5. Utilize Bloom's Taxonomy to create course objectives that are aligned with course outcomes.
6. Analyze the implications of Bloom's Taxonomy on the design and delivery of courses.
