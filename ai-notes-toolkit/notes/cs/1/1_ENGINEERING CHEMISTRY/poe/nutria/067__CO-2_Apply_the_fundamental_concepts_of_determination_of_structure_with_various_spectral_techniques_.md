
### CO-2 Apply the fundamental concepts of determination of structure with various spectral techniques and

1. Carbon dioxide (CO2) is a molecule composed of two oxygen atoms and one carbon atom.
2. The structure of CO2 is linear, with a carbon atom in the center and two oxygen atoms at the ends.
3. Spectroscopic techniques can be used to study the structure of CO2.
4. Infrared spectroscopy can be used to determine the bond lengths and angles of CO2.
5. Nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy can be used to determine the chemical shifts of the atoms in CO2.
6. Mass spectrometry can be used to determine the mass of the molecules in the sample, as well as the relative abundance of different isotopes.
7. X-ray diffraction can be used to determine the structure of molecules in the solid state.
8. Ultraviolet-visible spectroscopy can be used to determine the absorption of light by molecules in solution.
