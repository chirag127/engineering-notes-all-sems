
# Stereochemistry
Stereochemistry is the branch of chemistry that studies the spatial arrangement of atoms within molecules. It is an important part of organic chemistry and is used to identify compounds, elements, and other molecules.

## Properties of Stereochemistry
1. Stereochemistry is based on the three-dimensional arrangement of atoms within a molecule.
2. Stereochemistry is used to classify compounds based on their molecular structure.
3. Stereoisomers are molecules with the same molecular formula but different arrangements of atoms in space.
4. Chiral molecules are those that are not superimposable on their mirror image.
5. Chirality is determined by the presence of a chiral center, which is an atom with four different substituents attached.
6. Stereochemistry can be used to determine the absolute configuration of a molecule.
7. Stereochemistry is also used to predict the reactivity and reactivity of a molecule.
