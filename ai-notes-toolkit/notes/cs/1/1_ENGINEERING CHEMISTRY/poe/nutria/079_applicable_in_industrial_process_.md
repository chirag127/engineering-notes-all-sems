
# Applicable Industrial Process

1. Automation: Automation is the use of machines, control systems and information technologies to reduce the need for human work in the production of goods and services. Automation has been used in a wide variety of industries, from manufacturing to healthcare, to increase productivity and reduce costs.

2. Robotics: Robotics is the branch of technology that deals with the design, construction, operation, and application of robots, as well as computer systems for their control, sensory feedback, and information processing. Robotics is used in a wide variety of industries, from manufacturing to healthcare, to automate tasks that are too difficult or dangerous for humans to perform.

3. Data Analysis: Data analysis is the process of examining, transforming, and modeling data with the goal of discovering useful information, informing conclusions, and supporting decision-making. Data analysis is used in a wide variety of industries, from finance to healthcare, to uncover patterns and trends in data that can be used to make better decisions.

4. Artificial Intelligence: Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of a computer system to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and translation between languages. AI is used in a wide variety of industries, from finance to healthcare, to automate tasks that would otherwise require human intelligence.
