
### CO-5 Develop the understanding of Chemical structure of polymers and its effect on their various properties

1. Polymers are large molecules made up of repeating structural units. Each structural unit is known as a monomer. 
2. Polymers can be natural or synthetic. Natural polymers are derived from plants and animals, while synthetic polymers are man-made. 
3. The chemical structure of a polymer affects its physical and chemical properties. 
4. The physical properties of a polymer are mainly determined by its molecular weight, its degree of crystallinity, and its glass transition temperature. 
5. The chemical properties of a polymer are mainly determined by its chemical structure, its degree of crystallinity, and its glass transition temperature. 
6. The degree of crystallinity of a polymer affects its mechanical properties, such as its tensile strength and modulus. 
7. The glass transition temperature of a polymer affects its thermal properties, such as its heat deflection temperature and thermal conductivity. 
8. The chemical structure of a polymer affects its chemical properties, such as its solubility and reactivity.
