
# Advanced Materials

* Liquid Crystals: Liquid crystals are a state of matter that lies between solid and liquid phases. They are composed of molecules that are arranged in an ordered, but not completely rigid, arrangement. They are characterized by anisotropic physical properties, including optical, electrical, and thermal properties. 

* Nanomaterials: Nanomaterials are materials that are composed of particles that are smaller than 100 nanometers in size. They have unique physical, chemical, and biological properties that differ from larger materials. 

* Graphite & Fullerenes: Graphite is a form of carbon that is composed of layers of hexagonal lattices. Fullerenes are a form of carbon that is composed of spherical or cylindrical molecules. Both of these materials have unique properties, such as high electrical and thermal conductivity. 

* Green Chemistry: Green chemistry is a set of principles that are designed to reduce or eliminate the use of hazardous materials and processes in the production of chemicals. It is based on the principles of reducing waste, increasing efficiency, and preventing pollution.
