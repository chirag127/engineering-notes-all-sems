
### Spectroscopic Techniques and Applications: Elementary idea and Simple Applications

* Spectroscopy is the study of the interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation. It is used to determine the structure and composition of materials.
* The most common spectroscopic techniques are absorption, emission, and reflection spectroscopy.
* Absorption spectroscopy is used to study the absorption of light by a material and is used to determine the concentration of certain elements in a sample.
* Emission spectroscopy is used to study the emission of light from a material and is used to determine the composition of a sample.
* Reflection spectroscopy is used to study the reflection of light from a material and is used to determine the surface properties of a sample.
* Spectroscopy can also be used to study the structure of molecules, the motion of particles, and the properties of matter at a microscopic level.
* Spectroscopic techniques have numerous applications in fields such as medicine, chemistry, biology, and engineering. For example, spectroscopy can be used to detect and identify chemicals in the environment, to study the structure of proteins and other biological molecules, and to study the properties of materials.
