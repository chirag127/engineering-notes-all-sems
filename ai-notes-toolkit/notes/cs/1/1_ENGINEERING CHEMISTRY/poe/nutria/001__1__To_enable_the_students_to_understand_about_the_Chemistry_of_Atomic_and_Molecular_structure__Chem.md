
### 1. To enable the students to understand about the Chemistry of Atomic and Molecular structure, Chemistry of

- Atoms are the basic building blocks of matter and are composed of protons, neutrons, and electrons.
- The number of protons in an atom defines its atomic number and determines its chemical element.
- Electrons are found in different energy levels around the nucleus, and the arrangement of electrons in an atom determines its chemical properties.
- Molecules are two or more atoms held together by chemical bonds.
- Chemical bonds are formed when atoms share or transfer electrons.
- The chemical properties of a molecule are determined by the arrangement of its atoms and the types of bonds between them.
