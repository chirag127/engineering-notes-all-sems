
## Unit-1: 8

1. The term “formal language” refers to a style of writing and speaking that is used in academic, professional, or legal contexts. This type of language is characterized by its precision, clarity, and lack of emotion. 
2. Formal language is often used in documents such as contracts, laws, and official documents. It is also used in speeches and presentations to give them a more professional tone. 
3. Formal language should be used when writing business emails, memos, reports, and other documents. It should also be used when speaking in a professional setting, such as a job interview or a business meeting. 
4. Formal language should be used to convey information in a clear and concise manner. It should avoid using slang, jargon, and colloquialisms. 
5. Formal language should be used when writing for an audience that is unfamiliar with the topic. For example, when writing an academic paper, it is important to use formal language that is appropriate for the audience. 
6. Formal language should be used when writing for a specific purpose. For example, when writing a legal document, it is important to use language that is precise and accurate. 
7. Formal language should be used when communicating with someone in a position of authority. It is important to show respect and professionalism when communicating with someone in a higher position. 
8. Formal language should be used when writing for a formal occasion. For example, when writing a wedding invitation, it is important to use a formal tone.
