
### 2. To enable the students to understand and apply the detailed concepts of spectroscopic techniques and

1. Spectroscopic techniques are used to measure the interaction of electromagnetic radiation with matter.
2. Spectroscopy is used to identify different elements and molecules in a sample, as well as to measure the concentration of the sample.
3. The different types of spectroscopic techniques include absorption spectroscopy, emission spectroscopy, and Raman spectroscopy.
4. Absorption spectroscopy measures the amount of light that is absorbed by a sample.
5. Emission spectroscopy measures the amount of light that is emitted from a sample.
6. Raman spectroscopy measures the amount of light that is scattered by a sample.
7. The different types of spectroscopic techniques can be used to measure the composition, structure, and properties of a sample.
8. The different types of spectroscopic techniques can also be used to identify unknown compounds and to monitor chemical reactions.
