
### Batteries; Classification and applications of Primary Cells (Dry Cell) and Secondary Cells

* Batteries are devices that store and convert chemical energy into electrical energy.
* Primary cells, also known as dry cells, are batteries that cannot be recharged and are used once. Examples of primary cells include alkaline batteries, carbon-zinc batteries, and lithium batteries.
* Secondary cells are batteries that can be recharged and used multiple times. Examples of secondary cells include lead-acid batteries, nickel-cadmium batteries, and lithium-ion batteries.
* Primary cells are used in many everyday devices, such as flashlights, remote controls, and clocks.
* Secondary cells are used in devices that require a large amount of energy, such as electric vehicles, power tools, and UPS systems.
