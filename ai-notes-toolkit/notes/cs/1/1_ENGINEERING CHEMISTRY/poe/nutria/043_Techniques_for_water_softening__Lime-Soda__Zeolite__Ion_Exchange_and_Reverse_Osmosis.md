
# Techniques for Water Softening

* Lime-Soda: This method is also known as the soda-ash process and involves adding lime (calcium hydroxide) and soda (sodium carbonate) to water. This process increases the pH of the water, which causes the hardness-causing ions to precipitate out.

* Zeolite: Zeolite is a naturally occurring mineral that is used to soften water. It works by exchanging sodium ions for calcium and magnesium ions, thus removing the hardness-causing ions from the water.

* Ion Exchange: This process involves passing water through a resin bed that contains sodium ions. The hardness-causing ions are exchanged for sodium ions, thus softening the water.

* Reverse Osmosis: This process involves passing water through a semi-permeable membrane. The membrane traps the hardness-causing ions, thus softening the water.
