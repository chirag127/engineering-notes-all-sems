
# Organometallic compounds (RMgX and LiAlH4)

Organometallic compounds are a class of compounds that contain a metal-carbon bond. The most common organometallic compounds are RMgX and LiAlH4.

**RMgX**

RMgX compounds are a type of organometallic compound that contains a metal-carbon bond. The metal is typically a transition metal, such as ruthenium, rhodium, palladium, osmium, or iridium. The carbon is typically an alkyl group, such as methyl, ethyl, or propyl.

**LiAlH4**

LiAlH4 is an organometallic compound that contains a metal-carbon bond. The metal is lithium and the carbon is an alkyl group, typically an alkyl halide. LiAlH4 is used in organic synthesis as a reducing agent. It can reduce aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, and other organic compounds.
