
### Polymers; Classification, Polymerization processes, Thermosetting and Thermoplastic

Polymers are large molecules composed of repeating structural units. These molecules can be natural or synthetic and can vary in size and complexity. Polymers are classified according to the type of monomer units they are composed of and the type of polymerization process used to create them.

* Classification: 
  * Natural polymers: These polymers are found in nature and are composed of monomers that are linked together by covalent bonds. Examples include proteins, DNA, cellulose, and rubber. 
  * Synthetic polymers: These polymers are created in the laboratory from monomers that are linked together by covalent bonds. Examples include polyethylene, polystyrene, and nylon.

* Polymerization Processes:
  * Addition polymerization: This is a process in which monomers are joined together by covalent bonds to form a polymer. This process can be catalyzed by either an acid or a base.
  * Condensation polymerization: This is a process in which two monomers are joined together by covalent bonds to form a polymer. This process is usually catalyzed by an acid or a base.

* Thermosetting and Thermoplastic Polymers:
  * Thermosetting polymers: These polymers are formed by the condensation polymerization of monomers. Once these polymers have been formed, they cannot be reshaped or reformed. Examples include epoxy and polyurethane.
  * Thermoplastic polymers: These polymers are formed by the addition polymerization of monomers. These polymers can be reshaped and reformed by applying heat. Examples include polyethylene and polystyrene.
