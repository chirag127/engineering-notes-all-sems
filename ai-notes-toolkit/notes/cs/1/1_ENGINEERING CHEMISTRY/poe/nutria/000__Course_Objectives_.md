
## Course Objectives:

1. Understand the fundamentals of computer science and programming
2. Develop an understanding of the principles of software engineering
3. Develop an understanding of the principles of operating systems
4. Learn how to design and develop algorithms
5. Develop an understanding of data structures and their applications
6. Learn how to design and develop efficient and secure software
7. Understand the principles of computer networks and their applications
8. Develop an understanding of the principles of database systems
9. Learn how to develop software applications for various platforms
10. Develop an understanding of the principles of artificial intelligence and machine learning
