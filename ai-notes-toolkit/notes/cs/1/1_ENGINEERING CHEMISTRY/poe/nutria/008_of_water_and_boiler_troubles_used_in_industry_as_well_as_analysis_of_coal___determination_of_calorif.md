
# Water and Boiler Troubles Used in Industry
1. Boiler problems can arise due to a number of reasons, including:
    - Poor maintenance
    - Improper installation
    - Faulty equipment
    - Contaminated water
2. Boiler problems can be divided into two categories:
    - Primary problems, which are caused by the boiler itself
    - Secondary problems, which are caused by external factors
3. Common boiler problems include: 
    - Leaks
    - Corrosion
    - Boiler overpressure
    - Low water level
    - Sludge buildup
4. To prevent boiler problems, it is important to carry out regular maintenance and inspections.

# Analysis of Coal and Determination of Calorific Values
1. Calorific value is the amount of heat released by a unit of coal when it is burned.
2. Calorific values can be determined by analyzing the coal's chemical composition.
3. The analysis of coal involves testing the coal's moisture content, ash content, volatile matter content, and sulfur content.
4. The results of the analysis are used to calculate the calorific value of the coal.
5. The calorific value is then used to determine the efficiency of the coal in producing heat.
