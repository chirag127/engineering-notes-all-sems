
# Important Materials Used as Liquid Crystals

1. Calamitic Liquid Crystals: These are the most commonly used liquid crystals, and are composed of rod-like molecules that are arranged in a helical structure. They are typically used in displays, such as those found in televisions and mobile phones.

2. Discotic Liquid Crystals: These liquid crystals are composed of disk-shaped molecules which are arranged in a columnar structure. They are generally used in optical devices, such as lenses and prisms.

3. Thermotropic Liquid Crystals: These liquid crystals are composed of molecules that are arranged in a lamellar structure. They are typically used in thermometers and temperature-sensitive switches.

4. Lyotropic Liquid Crystals: These liquid crystals are composed of molecules that are arranged in a micellar structure. They are usually used in detergents and surfactants.
