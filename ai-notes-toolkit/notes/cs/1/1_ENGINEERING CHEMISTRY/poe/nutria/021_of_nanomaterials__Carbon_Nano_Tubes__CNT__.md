
# Carbon Nano Tubes (CNTs)

CNTs are nanomaterials composed of rolled sheets of carbon atoms. They are hollow cylinders with diameters of 1-50 nanometers and lengths of up to several millimeters. CNTs have unique properties that make them attractive for use in a variety of applications, including electronics, energy storage, and biomedical devices.

1. Structure: CNTs are composed of a single layer of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice. This structure gives CNTs a high degree of strength and flexibility.

2. Properties: CNTs have a high electrical conductivity, thermal conductivity, and surface-to-volume ratio, making them ideal for use in electronics and energy storage applications. They also have a high degree of chemical stability and are resistant to corrosion.

3. Applications: CNTs have been used in a variety of applications, including electronics, energy storage, and biomedical devices. They have been used as field emitters in flat panel displays, as electrodes in lithium-ion batteries, and as sensors in medical devices.
