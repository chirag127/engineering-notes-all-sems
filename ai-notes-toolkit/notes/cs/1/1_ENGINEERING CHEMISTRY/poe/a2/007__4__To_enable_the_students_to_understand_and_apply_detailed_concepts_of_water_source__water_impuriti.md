### 4. To enable the students to understand and apply detailed concepts of water source, water impurities, hardness

1. Water Source
- Rainwater: Collected from roofs and stored in tanks. Contains dissolved gases and particulate matter. Needs treatment before consumption.
- Surface water: Lakes, rivers, streams. Contain microorganisms, sediments and other pollutants. Requires intensive treatment.
- Groundwater: From wells and borewells. Contains dissolved salts and minerals. May need treatment depending on levels of impurities.

2. Water Impurities
- Microorganisms: Bacteria, viruses, protozoa. Cause water-borne diseases. Removed using disinfection methods like chlorination.
- Particulate matter: Clay, silt, sand, rust. Removed using filtration.
- Dissolved gases: Oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide. Removed by boiling/aeration/degasification.
- Dissolved minerals: Calcium, magnesium, iron. Cause scaling and affect taste. Removed using softeners and reverse osmosis.

3. Water Hardness
- Temporary: Due to dissolved bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium. Can be removed by boiling.
- Permanent: Due to dissolved chlorides and sulfates of calcium and magnesium. Cannot be removed by boiling. Requires water softeners.
- Hard water forms scale in boilers and pipes. Reduces lathering ability of soap. Softening is required for domestic and industrial use.
