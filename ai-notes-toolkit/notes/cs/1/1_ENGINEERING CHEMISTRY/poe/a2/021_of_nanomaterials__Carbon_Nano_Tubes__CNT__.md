# Carbon Nano Tubes (CNT)

1. Carbon Nano Tubes (CNTs) are allotropes of carbon with a cylindrical nanostructure.
2. They were discovered in 1991 by Japanese scientist Sumio Iijima.
3. CNTs have unique properties such as high tensile strength, high thermal conductivity, high electrical conductivity, etc.
4. They are classified as single-walled CNTs (SWCNTs) and multi-walled CNTs (MWCNTs) based on the number of cylindrical layers.
5. The applications of CNTs include electronics, optics, materials science, biology, etc.
6. The large-scale production of CNTs is challenging due to difficulties in controlling the geometry and properties.
7. The toxicity of CNTs is still under investigation as they may pose risks to human health and the environment.
