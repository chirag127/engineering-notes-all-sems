# Acid, Paracetamol and Environmental impact of Green chemistry

## Acid

- An acid is a substance that produces hydrogen ions (H+) in solution, which lowers the pH.
- Acids taste sour and can cause burns.
- Common acids: Hydrochloric acid (HCl), Sulfuric acid (H2SO4), Nitric acid (HNO3), etc.
- Uses: Manufacturing fertilizers, batteries, medicines, etc.

## Paracetamol

- Paracetamol is a pain reliever and fever reducer.
- Chemical name: N-acetyl-p-aminophenol or APAP.
- Works by inhibiting cyclooxygenase enzymes and reducing prostaglandin production in the brain.
- Common brand names: Tylenol, Crocin, Calpol, etc.
- Overdose can cause liver damage. Dosage should be followed carefully.

## Environmental impact of Green chemistry

- Green chemistry aims to reduce pollution and use sustainable processes.
- Fewer toxic solvents and less energy consumption.
- Recycling of resources and use of renewable feedstocks.
- Biodegradable and less toxic byproducts.
- Economic benefits through innovative technologies and lesser costs of waste treatment.
- Overall benefits to the society through a cleaner environment and lesser health hazards.
