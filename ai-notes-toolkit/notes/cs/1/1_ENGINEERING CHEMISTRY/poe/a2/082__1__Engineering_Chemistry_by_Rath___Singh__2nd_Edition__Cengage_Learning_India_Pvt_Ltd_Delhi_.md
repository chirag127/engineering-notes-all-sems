### 1. Engineering Chemistry by Rath & Singh, 2nd Edition, Cengage Learning India Pvt Ltd Delhi.

- The book covers all major topics of Engineering Chemistry in detail with ample examples and illustrations.
- It begins with an introduction to basic concepts of chemistry followed by the topics of atomic structure, chemical bonding, and molecular structure.
- It then moves on to cover the topics of states of matter, solutions, chemical equilibrium, acids and bases, chemical kinetics, surface chemistry, and electrochemistry.
- The book also includes chapters on chemical thermodynamics, corrosion and its prevention, polymers, and nanomaterials.
- Each chapter contains a large number of solved and unsolved problems to help students understand the concepts thoroughly.
- The content is presented in a simple and straightforward manner with emphasis on understanding the basic principles.
- It is a comprehensive book for undergraduate students of engineering preparing for various competitive exams.
