### CO-2 Apply the fundamental concepts of determination of structure with various spectral techniques and

1. Mass Spectrometry
- Measures mass-to-charge ratio of ions to determine molecular weight and structure of a compound.
- Fragmentation pattern analyzed to deduce structural information.
- Isotopic distribution can confirm presence/absence of certain elements.

2. Infrared Spectroscopy
- Measures vibration frequencies of bonds in a molecule.
- Functional groups have characteristic absorption frequencies that can be used to identify their presence.
- Absorption peaks provide information about types of bonds and symmetry of molecules.

3. Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy
- Measures magnetic properties of certain nuclei (e.g. 1H, 13C) in a molecule in a magnetic field.
- Chemical shifts are determined based on surrounding electron density and can be used to identify structures.
- Spin-spin coupling patterns provide information about neighboring groups.
- Integral ratios correlate to the number of adjacent nuclei.

4. Ultraviolet-Visible Spectroscopy
- Measures electronic transitions of molecules upon absorption of UV-visible light.
- The energy of absorbed light can be used to determine conjugation and unsaturated bonds.
- Shoulder peaks and hyperchromic effects can reveal additional structural features.

The above points cover the key spectral techniques and the fundamental concepts for determining molecular structure. The techniques provide complementary information that can be combined to deduce the complete structure of a molecule.
