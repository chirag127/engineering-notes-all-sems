### K4

1. K4 or Potassium-40 is a radioactive isotope of potassium which has a half-life of 1.248 billion years.
2. It makes up 0.012% of the total amount of potassium found in nature.
3. K4 decays into calcium-40 through beta decay and also emits gamma rays.
4. The long half-life of K4 allows it to persist since the formation of the Earth, making it a valuable tool for determining the age of rocks and dating geological events.
5. The decay of K4 in rocks and minerals can be used in radiometric dating to determine the age of geological samples from 100,000 years to several billion years old.
6. K4 is one of the isotopes of potassium which is essential for plant growth and is also important for human health in required quantities. However, higher levels of radiation from excessive K4 are harmful.
7. K4 is used industrially as a tracer to detect leaks and in density gauges, however precautions must be taken due to its radioactivity.
