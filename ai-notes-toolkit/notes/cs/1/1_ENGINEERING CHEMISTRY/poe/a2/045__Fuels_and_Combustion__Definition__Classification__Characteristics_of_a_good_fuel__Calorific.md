### Fuels and Combustion:

- Definition: Fuel is a substance which when heated in air, produces combustion and serves as a source of energy. Combustion is a chemical reaction between a fuel and an oxidant that releases energy.

- Classification: Fuels can be classified into solid, liquid and gaseous fuels. Example: Solid - coal, wood; Liquid - petrol, diesel; Gaseous - LPG, CNG, biogas.

- Characteristics of a good fuel:

1. High calorific value: It should produce a large amount of energy per unit mass.
2. Safe and economical: It should be safe to produce, store and transport. It should be easily available and inexpensive.
3. Uniform composition: It should have a uniform and consistent composition so that it can produce a predictable amount of heat safely.

- Calorific value: It is the amount of heat liberated by the complete combustion of a unit quantity of a fuel. It is expressed in kJ/kg or kcal/kg. A higher calorific value indicates a better fuel.
