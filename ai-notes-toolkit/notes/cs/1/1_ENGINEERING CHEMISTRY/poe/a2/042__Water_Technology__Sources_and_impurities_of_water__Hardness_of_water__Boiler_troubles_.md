### Water Technology: Sources and impurities of water, Hardness of water, Boiler troubles,

Sources of water:

- Rainwater: Collected from rooftops and stored in tanks. Contains dissolved gases and organic matter. Needs treatment before use.
- Surface water: Rivers, lakes, reservoirs. Contains suspended particles and dissolved salts. Needs extensive treatment.
- Groundwater: Wells and tube wells. Contains dissolved salts and gases. May need treatment depending on usage.

Impurities in water:

- Suspended particles: Sand, clay, rust, organic matter. Removed using filtration.
- Dissolved salts: Calcium, magnesium, sodium, chloride, sulphate. Removed using ion exchange or distillation.
- Dissolved gases: Oxygen, carbon dioxide. Removed using degasification.
- Microorganisms: Bacteria, viruses. Removed using disinfection (chlorination, ozonation).

Hardness of water:

- Temporary hardness: Due to dissolved calcium and magnesium hydrogen carbonates. Removed by boiling or addition of lime.
- Permanent hardness: Due to dissolved calcium and magnesium sulfates and chlorides. Not removed by boiling. Removed using ion exchange.

Boiler troubles:

- Scale formation: Due to precipitation of calcium and magnesium salts. Reduced using water softeners or acid dosing.
- Corrosion: Due to presence of dissolved oxygen. Reduced by deaeration or addition of corrosion inhibitors.
- Foaming: Due to presence of suspended particles and dissolved salts. Reduced using filtration and defoaming agents.
