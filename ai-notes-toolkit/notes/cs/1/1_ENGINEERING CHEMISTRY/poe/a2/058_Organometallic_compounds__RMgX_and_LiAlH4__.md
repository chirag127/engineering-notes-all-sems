# Organometallic compounds (RMgX and LiAlH4)

## RMgX

- RMgX are organometallic compounds where R is an alkyl/aryl group and X is a halide.
- They are strong nucleophiles due to the partial negative charge on the R group.
- They are used as strong bases to deprotonate alcohols and acids.
- Grignard reagents are useful in carbon-carbon bond formation reactions like the Grignard reaction.

## LiAlH4

- LiAlH4 is a strong hydride donor which can produce the hydride ion (H-).
- It is used to reduce ketones, aldehydes, esters, acids, etc. by addition of H-.
- The reduction is a multi-step mechanism involving alkoxide and aldehyde intermediates.
- LiAlH4 produces a more complete reduction compared to other hydride donors like NaBH4.
- However, it is more dangerous to work with due to hydrogen gas evolution and reactivity with protic solvents and water.
