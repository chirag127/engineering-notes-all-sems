### Materials Chemistry:

1. Materials - Substances that are used to produce useful products and structures. They have properties that can be modified for specific applications.
2. Properties of Materials - Chemical, physical, mechanical, electrical, thermal, optical, etc. properties that can be tailored for applications through composition and processing.
3. Composition - Elements and compounds that make up the material and their proportions. Changing composition can alter properties.
4. Processing - Methods used to produce a material from its composition such as heating, mixing, molding, etc. Processing influences the characteristics and performance of a material.
5. Characterization - Techniques used to experimentally measure the properties of a material such as X-ray diffraction, spectroscopy, thermal analysis, electron microscopy, etc. Characterization relates properties to composition and processing.
6. Structure - Arrangement of atoms or molecules in a material. The structure, ranging from the atomic to macro scale, determines properties and how a material responds to its environment.
7. Performance - How a material behaves in an application. Performance depends on the specific properties of a material and how it responds to environmental conditions such as stress, temperature, humidity, etc.
8. Selection - Choosing a material based on its properties and performance to meet the requirements of an application. Selection involves properly characterizing the environmental conditions and determining which properties are most important for optimal performance.
