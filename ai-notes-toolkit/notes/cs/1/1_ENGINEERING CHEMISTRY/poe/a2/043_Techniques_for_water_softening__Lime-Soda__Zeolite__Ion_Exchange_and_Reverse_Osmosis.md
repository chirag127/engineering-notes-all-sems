# Techniques for water softening

## Lime-Soda process

1. Lime is added to hard water which reacts with carbon dioxide to form calcium carbonate precipitate.
2. Sodium carbonate is added which reacts with magnesium salts to form magnesium carbonate precipitate.
3. The precipitates are filtered out, leaving behind soft water.

Advantages:
- Inexpensive
- Removes both calcium and magnesium salts

Disadvantages:
- Large volume of precipitates generated
- pH of treated water is high (11-12) and corrosive

## Zeolite process

1. Zeolite is a sodium aluminosilicate that exchanges sodium ions for calcium and magnesium ions in hard water.
2. The zeolite gets saturated over time and needs to be regenerated with a sodium chloride solution.

Advantages:
- Compact process
- No outside additions or sludge generation

Disadvantages:
- More expensive than lime-soda process
- Sodium ions introduced can affect downstream processes or corrosion
