### CO-4 Develop understanding of the sources, impurities and hardness of water, apply the concepts of

1. Sources of water:
- Rainwater: Collected directly or from streams, rivers. Contains dissolved gases and pollutants from air.
- Surface water: Lakes, rivers, dams. Contain impurities and microorganisms from runoff and sewage. Require treatment.
- Groundwater: Aquifers, wells, boreholes. Isolated from pollution but can contain minerals and salts.
- Seawater: Requires desalination for use.

2. Impurities in water:
- Sediments: Sand, clay, silt. Settle down on standing, removed by filtration.
- Microorganisms: Bacteria, viruses, protozoa. Cause diseases. Removed by disinfection using chlorine or boiling.
- Inorganic compounds: Salts, heavy metals. Toxic, removed using reverse osmosis or distillation.
- Organic compounds: Pesticides, detergents. Carcinogenic, removed using activated carbon or oxidation.

3. Hardness of water:
- Temporary: Due to bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium. Soap inefficient, precipitates on heating. Removed by boiling or precipitation.
- Permanent: Due to chlorides and sulfates of calcium and magnesium. Not removed by boiling, requires water softeners.
