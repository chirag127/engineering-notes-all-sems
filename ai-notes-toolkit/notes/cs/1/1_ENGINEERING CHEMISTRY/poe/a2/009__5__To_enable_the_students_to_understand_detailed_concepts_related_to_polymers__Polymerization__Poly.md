### 5. To enable the students to understand detailed concepts related to polymers, Polymerization, Polymer Blends.

1. Polymers: Polymers are long chain macromolecules formed by repeated linkage of monomers. They have high molecular weight. Based on origin they are natural or synthetic. Based on structure they are homopolymers or copolymers. Based on morphology they are thermosetting or thermoplastic.

2. Polymerization: It is a process of combining monomers to form polymers. It can be addition or condensation polymerization. In addition polymerization, double bond of monomer breaks and forms single bonds with other monomers. In condensation polymerization, two monomers combine with elimination of small molecules like water. Polymerization can be catalyzed or uncatalyzed.

3. Copolymers: When two or more different monomers undergo polymerization, the resulting polymer is called copolymer. The properties depend on composition and sequence distribution of monomers. It can be random, alternating, block or graft copolymers.

4. Polymer Blends: Polymer blends are mixtures of two or more polymers. They are prepared by mixing molten polymers or by dissolving them in a common solvent and removing the solvent. The properties can be tailored by combining desirable properties of different polymers. They can be compatible or incompatible based on interactions between polymer chains.
