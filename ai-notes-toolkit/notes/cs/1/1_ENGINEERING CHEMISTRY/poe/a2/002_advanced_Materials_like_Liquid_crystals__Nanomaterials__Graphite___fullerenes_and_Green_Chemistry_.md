# Advanced Materials

## Liquid Crystals

- Liquid crystals are materials that have properties between those of liquids and crystals. They flow like liquids but their molecules are oriented in ordered patterns like solids.
- They are commonly used in displays like calculators, watches, laptops, and TVs. The optical properties of liquid crystals can be easily manipulated by electric fields, allowing them to be switched between transparent and opaque states.
- The two most common types are nematic and smectic. Nematic liquid crystals have molecules aligned in the same direction, while smectic liquid crystals have molecules arranged in layers.

## Nanomaterials

- Nanomaterials are materials with structure on the nanoscale, having unique properties due to quantum effects. They have a high surface area to volume ratio and include nanoparticles, nanotubes, and nanowires.
- Carbon nanotubes are tubes of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice. They have exceptional mechanical and electrical properties and are used in electronics, optics, and materials science.
- Graphene is a two-dimensional sheet of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice. It is the thinnest and lightest material and also has exceptional mechanical, thermal, and electrical properties. It is used in electronics, optics, and energy storage.
