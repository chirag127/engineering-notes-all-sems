# Important Materials Used as Liquid Crystals

1. Nematic Liquid Crystals
- Rod-like organic molecules that can align in the same direction.
- Exhibit fluidity and ordered molecular arrangement.
- Used in LCDs for displays due to high electrical anisotropy.

2. Smectic Liquid Crystals
- Rod-like molecules arranged in layers with molecules aligned within layers.
- more ordered than nematics with distinctly separate layers.
- Used in fast electro-optic switching applications.

3. Cholesteric Liquid Crystals
- Rod-like molecules helically arranged with periodic variation in refractive index.
- Exhibit selective reflection of light resulting in iridescent colors.
- Used in temperature & pressure sensors, reflective displays, etc.
