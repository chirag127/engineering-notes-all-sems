### Organometallic Compounds: General methods of preparation and applications of

1. Organometallics are compounds that contain carbon-metal bonds. They are widely used in organic synthesis due to their unique reactivities.
2. The most common metals in organometallics are Li, Mg, Al, Zn, B, Si, Sn, Pb. The carbon can be bound to the metal in many oxidation states. The most common are:
- M-C single bonds (e.g. Grignard reagents, organolithiums)
- M=C double bonds (e.g. acetaldehyde enolate)
- Metal-olefin complexes (e.g. Cp2ZrCl2)
3. Commonly used methods for organometallic preparation:
- Direct reaction of a metal with an organic substrate (e.g. Li/R-H, Mg/R-X)
- Transmetalation - reaction of organometallic with another metal or salt (e.g. R-Li + R'-CuBr → R'-Li + R-CuBr)
- Reductive coupling - reduction of two organic groups using a metal (e.g. Cp2TiCl2 + 2 R'-H → Cp2Ti(R')2 + HCl)
4. Applications:
- Synthesis of alcohols from carbonyl compounds (e.g. Grignard reaction)
- Synthesis of alkenes from alkyl halides (e.g. Peterson olefination)
- Carbon-carbon bond forming reactions (e.g. Kumada coupling)
- Polymerization of alkenes (e.g. Ziegler-Natta catalyst)
