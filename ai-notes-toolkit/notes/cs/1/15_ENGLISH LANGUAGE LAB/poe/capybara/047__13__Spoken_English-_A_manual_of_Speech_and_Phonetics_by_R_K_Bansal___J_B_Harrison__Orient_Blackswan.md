### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

Spoken English is an essential skill that is required in today's world. It is not only important for communication but also for career growth. Here are some key points from the book 'Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics' by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi, that can help you improve your spoken English:

1. Pronunciation: Pronunciation is the key to effective communication. Pay attention to the way words are pronounced and try to imitate it. The book provides a comprehensive guide to English phonetics and pronunciation.

2. Stress and Intonation: Stress and intonation are important aspects of spoken English. Stress is the emphasis given to certain syllables in a word, while intonation is the rise and fall of the voice. Understanding stress and intonation can help you convey your message effectively.

3. Vocabulary: A good vocabulary is essential for effective communication. The book provides a list of commonly used words and phrases along with their meanings.

4. Grammar: Good grammar is essential for clear communication. The book provides a comprehensive guide to English grammar.

5. Fluency: Fluency is the ability to speak English smoothly and confidently. The book provides exercises and tips to improve your fluency.

6. Body Language: Body language plays an important role in communication. The book provides tips on how to use body language effectively.

7. Listening Skills: Good listening skills are essential for effective communication. The book provides tips on how to improve your listening skills.

8. Practice: Practice is the key to improving your spoken English. The book provides exercises and activities to help you practice and improve your spoken English.

In conclusion, 'Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics' by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi, is an excellent guide to improving your spoken English. By following the tips and exercises provided in the book, you can improve your pronunciation, stress and intonation, vocabulary, grammar, fluency, body language and listening skills.
