## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

### Part 1: Written Communication Skills
- In this section, students will learn how to write professional emails, memos, reports, and other types of business communication.
- They will learn how to structure their writing, use appropriate vocabulary and tone, and tailor their message to their audience.
- Students will also learn the importance of proofreading and editing their work to ensure accuracy and clarity.

### Part 2: Oral Communication Skills
- In this section, students will learn how to give effective presentations, participate in group discussions, and conduct professional meetings.
- They will learn how to organize their thoughts, use body language and gestures to enhance their message, and engage their audience.
- Students will also learn how to handle difficult questions and conflicts in a professional manner.

Overall, the Professional Communication Lab is designed to help students develop the essential communication skills needed to succeed in the business world. By mastering both written and oral communication, students will be well-equipped to communicate effectively with colleagues, clients, and stakeholders in any industry.
