### 2. Conversational Skills for Interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics.

The ability to communicate effectively during an interview is crucial for job seekers. In order to be successful, it is important to have strong conversational skills and to understand the role that nonverbal communication, or kinesics, plays in the interview process. Here are some tips for developing strong conversational skills for interviews:

1. Prepare beforehand: Before the interview, research the company and the position you are applying for. This will help you to be more confident and articulate during the interview.

2. Be attentive to nonverbal cues: Kinesics plays a significant role in the interview process. Pay attention to your body language, eye contact, and posture. Also, be aware of the interviewer's nonverbal cues, such as facial expressions and body language.

3. Listen actively: Active listening is an important conversational skill. Listen to the interviewer's questions carefully and respond thoughtfully. Avoid interrupting the interviewer or making assumptions about what they are asking.

4. Be concise: Avoid rambling or going off on tangents. Be concise and to the point when answering questions.

5. Use appropriate language: Use clear and concise language when answering questions. Avoid using slang, jargon, or technical terms that the interviewer may not understand.

6. Practice, practice, practice: Practice your conversational skills prior to the interview. Practice with a friend or family member, or record yourself answering common interview questions.

In conclusion, strong conversational skills are essential for a successful interview. By preparing beforehand, being attentive to nonverbal cues, actively listening, being concise, using appropriate language, and practicing, you can develop the skills needed to ace your next interview.
