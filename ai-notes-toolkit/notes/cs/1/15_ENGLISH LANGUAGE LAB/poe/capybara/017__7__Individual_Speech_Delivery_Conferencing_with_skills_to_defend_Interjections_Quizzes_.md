### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

When it comes to delivering a speech or participating in a conference, it is important to have the necessary skills to defend interjections and quizzes. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Be prepared: Before delivering a speech or participating in a conference, it is important to thoroughly prepare for the event. This includes researching the topic, practicing your delivery, and anticipating potential questions or interjections.

- Stay focused: During your speech or conference, it is important to stay focused and on-topic. Avoid getting sidetracked by irrelevant interjections or quizzes, and always steer the conversation back to the main point.

- Listen actively: When someone interjects or quizzes you, it is important to actively listen to their question or comment. This shows that you respect their input and are willing to engage in a dialogue.

- Respond confidently: When defending interjections or answering quizzes, it is important to respond confidently and clearly. Use examples or evidence to support your points, and avoid becoming defensive or confrontational.

- Stay respectful: Even if you strongly disagree with an interjection or quiz, it is important to remain respectful and professional. Avoid using dismissive or derogatory language, and always treat others with courtesy and consideration.

By developing these skills, you can become a more effective communicator and a stronger participant in speeches and conferences. Remember to stay prepared, focused, and respectful, and you will be well-equipped to defend interjections and answer quizzes with confidence and poise.
