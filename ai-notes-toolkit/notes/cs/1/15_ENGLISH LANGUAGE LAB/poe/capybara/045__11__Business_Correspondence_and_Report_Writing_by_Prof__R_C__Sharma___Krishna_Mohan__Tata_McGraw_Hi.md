### 11. Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan, Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd., 2001, New Delhi.

In business communication, writing proficiency is essential. Business correspondence and report writing is a crucial aspect of the communication process. Proficiency in business writing enables the writer to convey information accurately, concisely, and effectively. 

Here are some key points to keep in mind while writing business correspondence and reports:

1. Understand your audience: Before writing, it is crucial to understand the target audience. The language, tone, and style of writing should be suitable for the reader.

2. Be clear and concise: Clarity and brevity are essential in business communication. Use simple and precise language to convey the message effectively.

3. Use appropriate tone and style: The tone and style of writing should be formal and professional. Avoid using slang or colloquial language.

4. Plan before writing: A well-structured document is easy to read and understand. Plan the document and organize the information logically.

5. Proofread and edit: Before sending the document, proofread and edit it for errors, grammar, and spelling mistakes.

6. Use proper formatting: Use proper formatting techniques to make the document look professional. Use headings and subheadings to organize the information.

7. Use visuals: Use visuals such as graphs and charts to present data effectively.

8. Write an effective conclusion: A conclusion should summarize the key points and provide recommendations or actions.

9. Use appropriate salutations and closings: Use appropriate salutations and closings depending on the formality of the document and the relationship with the recipient.

10. Follow-up: Follow-up after sending the document to ensure that the recipient has received and understood the information.

In conclusion, business correspondence and report writing are essential skills in today's business world. By following the above guidelines, one can write effective and professional documents that convey the message accurately and concisely.
