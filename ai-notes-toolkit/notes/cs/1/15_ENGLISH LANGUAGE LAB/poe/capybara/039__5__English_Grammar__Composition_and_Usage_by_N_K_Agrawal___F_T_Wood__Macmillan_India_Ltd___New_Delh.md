### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

English Grammar:

- Grammar is the study of the structure of language and its rules.
- In English grammar, there are eight parts of speech: nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
- Nouns are words that name people, places, things, or ideas.
- Verbs are words that show action or a state of being.
- Adjectives are words that describe or modify nouns or pronouns.
- Adverbs are words that describe or modify verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.
- Pronouns are words that take the place of nouns.
- Prepositions are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence.
- Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses.
- Interjections are words that express strong feelings or emotions.

Composition:

- Composition is the act of putting thoughts and ideas into written form.
- In English composition, it is important to have a clear thesis statement that expresses the main idea of the essay.
- The essay should be well-organized, with each paragraph focusing on a specific point.
- Transitions should be used to connect ideas and paragraphs.
- Sentences should be varied in length and structure.
- Active voice is preferred over passive voice.
- The use of cliches and jargon should be avoided.
- Proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation are essential.

Usage:

- Usage refers to the proper and effective use of language.
- It includes using appropriate vocabulary, tone, and style for the audience and purpose.
- It also involves using language in a clear and concise manner, avoiding ambiguity and redundancy.
- Common usage errors include using incorrect verb tense, subject-verb agreement, and pronoun reference.
- It is important to proofread and edit written work for errors in usage.
