### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

In this course, students will learn about the basic objective of communication skills and specific dimensions such as reading, writing, listening, thinking, and speaking. The following points will help students understand the basic concepts of the course:

- Communication skills are essential for success in both personal and professional life. Good communication skills can help individuals express their ideas and thoughts effectively, build relationships, and solve problems.
- The first dimension of communication skills is reading. Students will learn how to read effectively and understand different types of texts such as academic articles, books, and news articles.
- The second dimension is writing. Students will learn how to write effectively for different purposes such as academic writing, business writing, and creative writing. They will learn how to structure their writing, use appropriate language, and convey their ideas effectively.
- The third dimension is listening. Students will learn how to listen actively, understand different perspectives, and respond appropriately. They will also learn how to use different listening techniques such as paraphrasing and summarizing.
- The fourth dimension is thinking. Students will learn how to think critically, analyze information, and make informed decisions. They will learn how to identify biases, evaluate evidence, and develop logical arguments.
- The fifth dimension is speaking. Students will learn how to speak effectively in different contexts such as public speaking, group discussions, and interviews. They will learn how to use appropriate body language, tone, and language to convey their message effectively.

Overall, this course will provide students with the necessary skills to communicate effectively in different contexts and achieve success in their personal and professional life.
