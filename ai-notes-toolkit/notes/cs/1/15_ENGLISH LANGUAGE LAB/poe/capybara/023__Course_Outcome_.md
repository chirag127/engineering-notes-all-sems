## Course Outcome:

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand and explain the fundamental concepts of the subject matter.
- Analyze and evaluate various theories, models, and principles related to the subject.
- Apply theoretical knowledge to real-world scenarios and problem-solving situations.
- Develop critical thinking and analytical skills to assess and interpret information related to the subject.
- Demonstrate effective communication skills, both in oral and written form, to present ideas and arguments related to the subject.
- Work collaboratively with peers and engage in group discussions and activities to enhance learning outcomes.
- Utilize appropriate research methods and techniques to gather, analyze, and interpret data related to the subject matter.
- Demonstrate ethical and professional conduct in all aspects of learning and academic work.
- Develop self-awareness and reflective skills to evaluate one's own learning and personal growth in relation to the subject matter.
