### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi

English Grammar and Composition by Wren & Martin is a comprehensive guide to the English language. It covers all aspects of grammar and composition, including parts of speech, sentence structure, and punctuation. Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying this book:

- The book is divided into two parts: Part I covers grammar, while Part II covers composition.
- Part I begins with an introduction to the English language and then covers the eight parts of speech: nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
- Each chapter includes clear explanations of the concepts, followed by exercises to help you practice what you've learned.
- Part I also covers sentence structure, including subjects, predicates, phrases, and clauses.
- The book then moves on to punctuation, covering everything from commas and semicolons to quotation marks and apostrophes.
- Part II covers composition, including the writing process, paragraph development, and essay writing.
- The book includes numerous examples and exercises to help you master the concepts.
- It is important to work through the exercises and practice writing on your own to reinforce what you've learned.
- The book is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills, whether for academic or professional purposes.

In summary, English Grammar and Composition by Wren & Martin is a comprehensive guide to the English language that covers all aspects of grammar and composition. It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
