### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

In today's world, knowing English language has become a necessity for students, especially for those who aspire to pursue higher studies or work in multinational companies. It is therefore important to provide them with the necessary language proficiency skills to help them achieve their goals. One way to achieve this is through software based learning. Here are some key points to consider:

- Software based learning can provide students with a personalized learning experience, where they can learn at their own pace and according to their own level of understanding.

- Such software can provide a variety of activities and exercises, including reading, writing, listening, and speaking, to help students develop their language skills in a comprehensive manner.

- Software based learning can also provide instant feedback to students, helping them to identify their mistakes and improve their language proficiency in a more efficient manner.

- By using software based learning, students can access learning materials and resources anytime and anywhere, without the need for physical books or classroom settings. This can be especially beneficial for students who have limited access to educational resources.

- Software based learning can be cost-effective, as it eliminates the need for expensive textbooks and classroom materials.

- However, it is important to note that software based learning should not be the sole means of providing English language proficiency to students. It should be used in conjunction with other methods, such as classroom instruction and interactive learning activities.

In conclusion, software based learning can be an effective way to provide English language proficiency to students. It can provide a personalized learning experience, a variety of exercises and activities, instant feedback, accessibility, and cost-effectiveness. However, it is important to use it in conjunction with other methods to ensure that students receive a comprehensive and well-rounded language education.
