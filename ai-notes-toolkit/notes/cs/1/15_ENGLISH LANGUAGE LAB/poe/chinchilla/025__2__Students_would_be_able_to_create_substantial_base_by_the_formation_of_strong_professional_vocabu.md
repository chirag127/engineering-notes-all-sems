### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

When it comes to building a strong professional vocabulary, students need to focus on expanding their knowledge and usage of words that are commonly used in professional settings. By improving their vocabulary, students can enhance their communication skills and become more effective in their academic and professional pursuits. Here are some tips and strategies that can help students create a substantial base of professional vocabulary:

1. Read extensively: The more you read, the more exposure you get to different words and phrases in context. Reading different types of texts, such as academic journals, business reports, and professional blogs, can help you build your vocabulary in specific fields of interest. You can also read books on vocabulary building and grammar to improve your overall language proficiency.

2. Use a dictionary: When you encounter a new word, make it a habit to look it up in a dictionary or thesaurus. This will not only help you understand the meaning of the word but also its usage in different contexts.

3. Learn word roots and affixes: Many words in English have a root word or affix that can help you understand their meaning. For example, the prefix "dis-" means not or opposite, so words like "dislike" and "disagree" mean not liking or not agreeing. By learning these roots and affixes, you can quickly decode new words and expand your vocabulary.

4. Practice using new words: Use new words in your writing and speaking to reinforce your understanding and retention of them. You can also make flashcards or use vocabulary apps to quiz yourself on new words and their meanings.

5. Attend workshops and seminars: Many schools and universities offer workshops and seminars on vocabulary building and communication skills. These can be great opportunities to learn new strategies and practice your skills in a supportive environment.

By following these tips and strategies, students can create a substantial base of professional vocabulary that can be applied in different platforms and modes of communication. With a strong vocabulary, students can become more confident and effective in their academic and professional pursuits, and improve their overall language proficiency.
