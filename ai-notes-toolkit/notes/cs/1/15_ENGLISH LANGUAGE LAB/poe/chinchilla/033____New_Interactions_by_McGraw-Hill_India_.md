### • New Interactions by McGraw-Hill India

McGraw-Hill India has introduced new interactions that aim to enhance the learning experiences of students. These interactions are designed to help students engage with the content in a more interactive and effective way. Here are some of the new interactions by McGraw-Hill India:

- Interactive eBooks: The interactive eBooks are designed to make learning more engaging and interactive. With the help of interactive elements like videos, animations, and quizzes, students can now learn and test their knowledge at the same time.

- Adaptive Learning: The adaptive learning feature uses data analytics to provide customized learning experiences to students. This feature helps students identify their strengths and weaknesses and provides personalized content to improve their learning outcomes.

- Virtual Labs: The virtual lab feature provides students with a hands-on learning experience by simulating real-world experiments. This feature helps students understand complex concepts in a more practical and engaging way.

- Gamification: The gamification feature uses game-like elements to motivate students to learn and engage with the content. This feature helps students stay motivated and interested in learning.

- Augmented Reality: The augmented reality feature uses technology to provide students with a more immersive and interactive learning experience. This feature helps students visualize complex concepts in a more engaging way.

Overall, these new interactions by McGraw-Hill India aim to transform the way students learn and engage with the content. By providing more interactive and personalized learning experiences, McGraw-Hill India is helping students achieve better learning outcomes and preparing them for the future.
