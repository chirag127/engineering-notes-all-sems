### Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics

This manual is a comprehensive guide to learning spoken English effectively. The book covers various aspects of spoken English, such as speech sounds, stress, intonation, and rhythm. The following points provide an overview of the topics covered in the manual:

1. Introduction to Spoken English: The manual begins with an introduction to spoken English and its importance. It discusses how spoken English differs from written English and the various situations where spoken English is used.

2. Speech Sounds: The manual covers the basic speech sounds of English, including vowels, consonants, and diphthongs. It explains how to produce each sound and provides examples of words that contain these sounds.

3. Word Stress: The manual explains the concept of word stress and how it affects the meaning of a word. It provides guidelines for identifying and placing stress on the correct syllable in a word.

4. Sentence Stress: The manual discusses the importance of sentence stress and how it can change the meaning of a sentence. It provides guidelines for placing stress on the correct words in a sentence.

5. Intonation: The manual explains the concept of intonation and how it can convey different meanings in spoken English. It provides guidelines for using rising and falling intonation to convey different emotions and attitudes.

6. Rhythm: The manual discusses the importance of rhythm in spoken English and how it can affect the clarity of speech. It provides guidelines for using the correct rhythm and timing in speaking.

7. Connected Speech: The manual covers the concept of connected speech and how it affects the pronunciation of words in a sentence. It provides guidelines for using connected speech to speak more fluently and naturally.

8. Pronunciation Practice: The manual includes exercises and activities for practicing pronunciation, stress, intonation, and rhythm. It also provides audio recordings for listening and practicing.

Overall, this manual is an excellent resource for anyone looking to improve their spoken English skills. It provides a thorough understanding of the various aspects of spoken English and offers practical guidelines for using them effectively.
