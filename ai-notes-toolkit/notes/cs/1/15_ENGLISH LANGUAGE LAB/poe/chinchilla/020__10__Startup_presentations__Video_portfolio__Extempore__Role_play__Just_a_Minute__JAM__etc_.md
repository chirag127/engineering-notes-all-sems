### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

1. Startup presentations: 
   - A startup presentation is a pitch made to potential investors, clients, or partners to convince them to invest in or collaborate with the startup. 
   - It typically includes information about the team, the product or service, the market opportunity, and the financial projections. 
   - A good startup presentation should be concise, clear, and compelling. 

2. Video portfolio: 
   - A video portfolio is a collection of videos that showcase your skills, experience, and achievements. 
   - It can be used as a marketing tool to promote yourself to potential clients or employers. 
   - A good video portfolio should be well-produced, engaging, and relevant to the target audience. 

3. Extempore: 
   - Extempore is a public speaking exercise in which the speaker is given a topic and a limited amount of time to prepare a speech. 
   - The speaker is expected to deliver a coherent and persuasive speech without any prior preparation. 
   - This exercise is often used to test the speaker's ability to think on their feet and communicate effectively under pressure. 

4. Role play: 
   - Role play is a simulation exercise in which participants act out a scenario in a fictional or real-life setting. 
   - It is often used in training and development programs to improve communication, problem-solving, and conflict resolution skills. 
   - A good role play scenario should be relevant to the participants' work or personal life and provide opportunities for learning and growth. 

5. Just a Minute (JAM): 
   - Just a Minute (JAM) is a public speaking exercise in which the speaker is given a topic and one minute to speak on the topic without hesitation, repetition, or deviation. 
   - The goal of this exercise is to improve the speaker's ability to communicate concisely and effectively. 
   - JAM is often used in schools and corporate training programs to develop communication skills and build confidence.
