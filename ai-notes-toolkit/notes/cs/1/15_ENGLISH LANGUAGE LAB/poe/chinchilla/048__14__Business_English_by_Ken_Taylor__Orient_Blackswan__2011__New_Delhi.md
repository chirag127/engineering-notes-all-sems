### 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi.

Business English is a crucial aspect of communication in the corporate world. It involves the use of appropriate language, tone, and style to convey messages effectively. Ken Taylor's book 'Business English' published by Orient Blackswan in 2011 provides a comprehensive guide to mastering the language for business purposes. Here are some key takeaways from the book:

1. Importance of clarity in communication: Clear communication is essential in business English. Ambiguous language can lead to misunderstandings and confusion. The book emphasizes the need for clarity in both written and spoken communication.

2. Use of formal language: Business English requires the use of formal language. The book provides guidance on the appropriate use of grammar, vocabulary, and sentence structure in formal contexts.

3. Writing effective emails: Emails are a primary mode of communication in the business world, and writing effective emails is crucial. The book provides tips on how to structure emails, use appropriate language, and avoid common errors.

4. Conducting meetings: Meetings are an important aspect of business communication. The book provides guidance on how to conduct meetings effectively, including the use of appropriate language, tone, and structure.

5. Making presentations: Presentations are another crucial aspect of business communication. The book provides tips on how to structure and deliver effective presentations, including the use of visuals and appropriate language.

6. Negotiating and persuading: Negotiation and persuasion are essential skills in business. The book provides guidance on how to use language effectively to negotiate and persuade in a business context.

7. Writing reports: Reports are an important form of written communication in business. The book provides guidance on how to structure and write effective reports, including the use of appropriate language and tone.

In conclusion, Ken Taylor's book 'Business English' provides a comprehensive guide to mastering the language for business purposes. The book emphasizes the importance of clarity, formal language, and effective communication in various business contexts, including emails, meetings, presentations, negotiation, and report writing. By following the guidance provided in the book, readers can improve their business communication skills and achieve success in the corporate world.
