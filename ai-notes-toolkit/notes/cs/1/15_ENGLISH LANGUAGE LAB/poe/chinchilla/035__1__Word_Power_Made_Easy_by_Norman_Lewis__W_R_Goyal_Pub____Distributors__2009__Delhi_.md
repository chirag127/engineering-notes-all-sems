### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

Word Power Made Easy is a comprehensive vocabulary-building book authored by Norman Lewis. It is a must-read book for anyone who wants to improve their vocabulary and communication skills. Here are some key points that you should keep in mind while studying this book:

1. The book is divided into three sections: Building Your Vocabulary, Using Words Correctly, and A Program for Further Growth.

2. The first section, Building Your Vocabulary, contains various chapters that focus on different aspects of vocabulary-building such as synonyms, antonyms, word roots, prefixes, suffixes, and so on.

3. The second section, Using Words Correctly, deals with the proper usage of words in different contexts such as in writing, speaking, and formal communication.

4. The third section, A Program for Further Growth, provides a roadmap for improving your vocabulary further by suggesting various resources and techniques such as reading, writing, and using a dictionary.

5. The book contains numerous exercises, quizzes, and tests to help you assess your progress and reinforce your learning.

6. The language used in the book is simple and easy to understand. However, it is recommended that you have a good grasp of basic English grammar before you start studying this book.

7. The book is not meant to be read in one go. It is recommended that you read a chapter or two every day and practice the exercises given at the end of each chapter.

8. The book is suitable for anyone who wants to improve their vocabulary, regardless of their age or educational background.

9. The book is widely available in bookstores and online. Make sure to get the latest edition to ensure that you have the most up-to-date information.

In conclusion, Word Power Made Easy is an excellent book for anyone who wants to improve their vocabulary and communication skills. By following the tips and techniques given in this book, you can significantly enhance your language skills and become a more effective communicator.
