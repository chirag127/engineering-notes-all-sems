### 2-Computer assisted software based Language Learning:

Computer-assisted language learning (CALL) is a method of language teaching and learning through the use of computers and technology. Here are some key points to understand about computer-assisted software-based language learning:

1. CALL is an effective way to learn a new language. It provides learners with opportunities to practice reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills in a variety of contexts.

2. CALL software can provide individualized learning experiences that are tailored to a learner's strengths and weaknesses.

3. CALL software can provide instant feedback and corrections to learners, which helps them to learn more quickly and efficiently.

4. CALL software can also provide learners with opportunities to interact with native speakers of the language they are learning.

5. There are a wide variety of CALL software programs available, ranging from simple vocabulary drills to complex virtual reality simulations.

6. Some popular CALL software programs include Rosetta Stone, Duolingo, and Babbel.

7. When choosing a CALL software program, it is important to consider factors such as the level of interactivity, the quality of the content, and the cost.

8. It is also important to remember that CALL software should be used in conjunction with other language learning activities, such as reading, writing, and speaking with native speakers.

9. Finally, it is important to remember that while CALL software can be an effective tool for language learning, it is not a substitute for real-life language practice and interaction with native speakers.
