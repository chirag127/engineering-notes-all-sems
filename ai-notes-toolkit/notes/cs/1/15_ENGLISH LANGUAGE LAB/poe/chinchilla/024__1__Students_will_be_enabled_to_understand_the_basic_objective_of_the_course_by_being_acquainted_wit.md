### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

In order to understand the basic objective of this course, students will be introduced to various dimensions of communication skills. These dimensions include:

1. **Reading:** Students will learn how to read effectively and comprehend written material. They will be taught strategies to enhance their reading abilities, such as skimming, scanning, and note-taking.

2. **Writing:** Students will develop their writing skills by learning how to structure their writing, use appropriate language and grammar, and convey their ideas effectively. They will also learn how to use different writing styles for different purposes, such as academic writing, business writing, and creative writing.

3. **Listening:** Students will learn how to be active listeners and how to understand and interpret spoken messages. They will also develop their note-taking skills and learn how to ask questions to clarify information.

4. **Thinking:** Students will be taught critical thinking skills, including how to analyze information, evaluate arguments, and make logical conclusions. They will also learn how to think creatively and generate ideas.

5. **Speaking:** Students will develop their speaking skills by learning how to deliver presentations, participate in group discussions, and communicate effectively in different contexts. They will also learn how to use non-verbal communication, such as body language and tone of voice, to enhance their message.

By being acquainted with these specific dimensions of communication skills, students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course. They will also be equipped with the necessary tools to improve their communication abilities, which will be useful in both their academic and professional lives.
