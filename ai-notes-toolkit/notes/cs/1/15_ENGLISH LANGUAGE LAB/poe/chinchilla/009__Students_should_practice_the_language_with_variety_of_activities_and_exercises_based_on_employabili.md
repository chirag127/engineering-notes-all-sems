#### Importance of Practicing Language with Employability Skills

In today's competitive world, it is essential for students to be proficient in multiple languages to enhance their employability skills. Here are some points that highlight the importance of practicing language with employability skills:

1. Interactive and Communicative Practical: Language learning should not be restricted to just reading and writing. Students should engage in interactive and communicative practical sessions that involve speaking and listening skills. This will help them develop their communication skills, which is a critical employability skill.

2. Emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication: Oral presentation and spoken communication skills are essential in the workplace. Students should practice their language skills through various activities that emphasize these skills. This will help them build confidence and improve their ability to articulate their thoughts effectively.

3. International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.): Learning the International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.) can help students improve their pronunciation and accent. This is an essential employability skill, as clear and accurate communication is crucial in various professions.

4. Variety of Activities and Exercises: Students should practice the language through various activities and exercises, such as role-plays, group discussions, debates, and presentations. These activities will help them develop their problem-solving, critical thinking, and teamwork skills, which are highly valued by employers.

5. Based on Employability Skills: Language learning should not be isolated from other employability skills. Students should practice language through exercises that are based on employability skills, such as time management, goal setting, and teamwork. This will help them develop a holistic set of skills that will make them more employable.

In conclusion, practicing language with employability skills is crucial for students to enhance their employability skills and succeed in the competitive job market. By engaging in interactive and communicative practical sessions, emphasizing oral presentation and spoken communication skills, learning International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.), practicing through a variety of activities and exercises, and integrating language learning with other employability skills, students can develop a holistic set of skills that will help them stand out in the workplace.
