### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

When it comes to delivering a theme presentation or keynote presentation, it is important to use correct methodologies of argumentation to ensure that your message is delivered effectively. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

1. Identify your audience: Before you start preparing your presentation, it is important to identify your audience. This will help you tailor your message and argument to their needs and interests.

2. Define your objective: Your presentation should have a clear objective. This will help you stay focused and ensure that your argument is relevant and persuasive.

3. Research your topic: To make a convincing argument, you need to research your topic thoroughly. This will help you support your argument with facts, statistics, and other evidence.

4. Use a clear structure: A well-structured presentation is easier to follow and more persuasive. Use a clear structure that includes an introduction, main points, and a conclusion.

5. Use visuals: Visual aids can help reinforce your argument and make your presentation more engaging. Use graphs, charts, and images to support your argument.

6. Use persuasive language: Use strong, persuasive language to make your argument more convincing. Use active verbs and avoid passive language.

7. Address counterarguments: Anticipate counterarguments and address them in your presentation. This will help you strengthen your argument and show that you have considered all perspectives.

8. Use repetition: Repetition can be a powerful tool in reinforcing your message. Use key phrases and concepts throughout your presentation to reinforce your argument.

9. Practice: Practice your presentation to ensure that you are confident and comfortable with your message. This will help you deliver your argument more effectively.

In summary, delivering a theme presentation or keynote presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation requires careful planning, research, and practice. By following these key points, you can ensure that your presentation is clear, persuasive, and effective in achieving your objectives.
