### 2. Conversational Skills for Interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics.

In order to ace an interview and secure a job, it is important to possess strong conversational skills, especially in a professional setting. Here are some key points to keep in mind when practicing conversational skills for interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics.

1. Start with a firm handshake: When you meet your interviewer, offer a firm handshake and maintain eye contact. This conveys confidence and professionalism.

2. Watch your body language: Kinesics, or the study of body language, is an important aspect of communication. Avoid slouching or fidgeting, as this can make you appear nervous or uninterested. Sit up straight, maintain a relaxed posture, and use appropriate hand gestures to emphasize your points.

3. Listen attentively: Active listening is key in any conversation, but especially in an interview. Pay attention to what the interviewer is saying, and respond thoughtfully to their questions. Take a moment to gather your thoughts before answering, and always answer truthfully.

4. Avoid filler words: Filler words such as "um" and "like" can make you appear less confident and less professional. Take a moment to think before speaking, and eliminate filler words from your vocabulary.

5. Use appropriate language: Avoid using slang or overly casual language in a professional setting. Instead, use formal language and avoid using contractions.

6. Be concise: When answering interview questions, be concise and to the point. Avoid rambling or going off on tangents, as this can make you appear unprepared or disorganized.

7. Ask questions: At the end of the interview, be sure to ask questions about the company or the position. This shows that you are interested and engaged, and can help you stand out from other candidates.

By keeping these tips in mind and practicing them in a Professional Communication Lab setting, you can improve your conversational skills and increase your chances of success in an interview. Remember to pay attention to your body language, listen attentively, use appropriate language, and be concise and confident in your responses.
