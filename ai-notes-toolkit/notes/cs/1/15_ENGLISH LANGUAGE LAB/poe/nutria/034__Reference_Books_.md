
## Reference Books:
- **The C Programming Language** by Brian W. Kernighan and Dennis M. Ritchie: A classic for all programmers, this book provides a comprehensive introduction to the C language and its features.
- **Compilers: Principles, Techniques, and Tools** by Alfred V. Aho, Monica S. Lam, Ravi Sethi, and Jeffrey D. Ullman: This book provides a comprehensive introduction to the theory and practice of compiler construction.
- **The Art of Computer Programming** by Donald E. Knuth: This book is considered to be the definitive work on the subject of computer programming.
- **Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective** by Randal E. Bryant and David R. O'Hallaron: This book provides an in-depth look at the internal workings of computer systems from the perspective of a programmer.
- **Algorithms** by Robert Sedgewick and Kevin Wayne: This book provides an introduction to the design and analysis of algorithms, with a focus on the practical aspects of algorithm design.
