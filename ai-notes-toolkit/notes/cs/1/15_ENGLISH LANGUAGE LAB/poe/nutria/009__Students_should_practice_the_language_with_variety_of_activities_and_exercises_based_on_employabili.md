
#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills

* Develop an understanding of International Phonetic Alphabets (IPA) and its application in oral presentation/spoken communication  
* Practice pronunciation and intonation of words and sentences in the target language
* Develop listening skills to comprehend spoken language
* Develop speaking skills to communicate effectively in the target language
* Develop reading skills to understand written language
* Develop writing skills to communicate effectively in the target language
* Practice interactive and communicative activities to develop confidence in speaking the target language
* Develop an understanding of cultural nuances and conventions in communication
* Practice problem-solving and critical thinking skills to apply the target language in real-world scenarios.
