

### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar

1. Students should be familiar with the basic rules of English grammar, such as parts of speech, sentence structure, and punctuation.
2. Students should be able to identify and correct errors in their writing, including spelling, grammar, punctuation, and usage.
3. Students should be able to recognize common errors in grammar and usage and be able to correct them.
4. Students should be able to apply the rules of grammar to their writing and be able to recognize when they have made mistakes.
5. Students should be able to recognize and use the appropriate tone and style for different types of writing.
6. Students should be able to use appropriate language for different audiences.
7. Students should be able to use resources such as dictionaries and grammar books to help them with their writing.
