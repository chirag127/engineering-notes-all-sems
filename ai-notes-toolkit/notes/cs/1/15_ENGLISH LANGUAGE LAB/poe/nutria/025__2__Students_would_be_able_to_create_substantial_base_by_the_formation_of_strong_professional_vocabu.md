
### 2. Students would be able to create a substantial base by the formation of a strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes such as Comprehension, Reading, Writing, and Speaking:

* Developing a strong professional vocabulary is essential for students to be able to communicate effectively in different contexts.
* Having a strong professional vocabulary helps students to understand and comprehend written and spoken language in a variety of settings.
* By having a strong professional vocabulary, students can use language more accurately and fluently in different settings such as work, school, and other contexts.
* Students can use their professional vocabulary to communicate more effectively in conversations, presentations, and other forms of discourse.
* Having a strong professional vocabulary also helps students to understand and interpret written and spoken language in a variety of settings, such as in literature, news, and other media.
