
### 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi

1. Personality development is the process of developing and enhancing one’s personality. It involves developing strong self-awareness, self-esteem, and social skills.

2. It is important to understand that personality development is not about changing one’s personality, but rather about growing and developing it.

3. Soft skills are the skills that are related to how one interacts with others. These skills include communication, problem-solving, decision-making, and teamwork.

4. Barun K. Mitra’s book, “Personality Development & Soft Skills”, provides an in-depth look at the topics of personality development and soft skills.

5. The book covers topics such as self-awareness, self-esteem, interpersonal skills, communication, problem-solving, decision-making, and teamwork.

6. It also provides practical strategies for developing and improving these skills.

7. The book is written in an accessible and engaging style, and provides a comprehensive overview of the topics.
