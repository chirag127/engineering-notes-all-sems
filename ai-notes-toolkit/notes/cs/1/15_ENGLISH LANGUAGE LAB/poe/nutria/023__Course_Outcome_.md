
## Course Outcome:
1. Understand the fundamentals of computer science, including algorithms, data structures, and programming languages.
2. Develop an understanding of the design principles of software systems and the ability to apply these principles to the development of software.
3. Acquire knowledge and skills related to the analysis, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance of software applications.
4. Learn to use a variety of software development tools and techniques.
5. Develop an understanding of the ethical and social implications of computer technology.
6. Develop the ability to communicate effectively, both orally and in writing, about computing topics.
