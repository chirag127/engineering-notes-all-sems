
#### Software based self-guided learning to provide the required English language proficiency to students from an employability and career readiness standpoint
- Software should be designed to align with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and deliver a CEFR level - B2 upon completion.
- Students should be provided with a comprehensive range of self-guided learning tools and activities to develop their language proficiency.
- These tools and activities should include, but not be limited to, reading, writing, listening, speaking, grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation.
- Students should be given ample opportunity to practice and apply their language skills in a variety of contexts.
- Students should be provided with feedback and support to help them develop their language skills.
- Students should be given the opportunity to evaluate their own progress and achievements.
- The software should be designed to be user-friendly and engaging, with a range of interactive features and visuals.
