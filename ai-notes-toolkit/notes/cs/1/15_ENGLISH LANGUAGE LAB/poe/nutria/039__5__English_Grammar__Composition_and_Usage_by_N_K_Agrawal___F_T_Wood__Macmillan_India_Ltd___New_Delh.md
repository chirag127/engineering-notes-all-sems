
### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi

- English Grammar is the set of rules and principles that govern the use of the English language.
- Composition is the art of putting words together in a meaningful and effective way. It includes the use of grammar, punctuation, and other stylistic elements.
- Usage refers to the way in which words are used in context. It includes the use of idioms, colloquialisms, and other forms of language that are specific to a particular region or culture.
- N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood's book provides an in-depth study of English grammar, composition, and usage. It covers topics such as sentence structure, parts of speech, punctuation, and other aspects of the English language.
- The book also provides examples and exercises to help readers practice their grammar and composition skills.
- Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi is the publisher of the book and provides a comprehensive resource for those who want to improve their English language skills.
