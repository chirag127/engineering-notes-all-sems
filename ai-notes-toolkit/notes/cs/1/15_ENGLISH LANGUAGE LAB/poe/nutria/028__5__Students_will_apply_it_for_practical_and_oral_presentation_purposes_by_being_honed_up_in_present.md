
### 5. Students will apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics. 

1. Developing interpersonal communication skills: 
    * Active listening 
    * Asking questions 
    * Nonverbal communication 
    * Giving and receiving feedback 
    * Negotiation 
2. Developing a positive attitude: 
    * Maintaining a positive outlook 
    * Setting realistic goals 
    * Acknowledging successes 
    * Embracing failure 
3. Developing voice dynamics: 
    * Controlling the volume and tone of voice 
    * Speaking with clarity and accuracy 
    * Using pauses, emphasis and inflection 
    * Using appropriate language 
4. Developing presentation skills: 
    * Preparing and rehearsing presentations 
    * Using visual aids 
    * Engaging with the audience 
    * Handling questions 
5. Professional competence: 
    * Understanding the context of the presentation 
    * Understanding the audience 
    * Understanding the purpose of the presentation 
    * Understanding the message and how to communicate it 
    * Being aware of cultural and other differences
