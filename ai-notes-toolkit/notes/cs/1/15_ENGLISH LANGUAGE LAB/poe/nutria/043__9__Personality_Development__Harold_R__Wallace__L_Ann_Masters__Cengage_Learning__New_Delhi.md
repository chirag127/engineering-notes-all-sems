
### 9. Personality Development
Harold R. Wallace &L.Ann Masters, Cengage Learning, New Delhi

1. Personality is a set of psychological characteristics that make an individual unique.

2. Personality development is the process of developing and growing one's personality. It includes the development of positive characteristics such as self-awareness, self-esteem, communication skills, empathy, and resilience.

3. Personality development is influenced by many factors, including genetics, environment, culture, and experiences.

4. Personality development is a lifelong process that begins in childhood and continues throughout adulthood.

5. Personality development can be enhanced through activities such as self-reflection, self-expression, and goal setting.

6. Personality development can help individuals become more successful in their personal and professional lives.
