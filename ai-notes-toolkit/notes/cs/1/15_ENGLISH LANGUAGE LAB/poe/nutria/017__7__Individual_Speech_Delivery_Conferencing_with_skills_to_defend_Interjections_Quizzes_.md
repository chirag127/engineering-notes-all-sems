
### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with Skills to Defend Interjections/Quizzes

* Effective speech delivery is an important skill for any individual who wishes to communicate effectively. 
* It is important to be aware of the different strategies that can be used to defend against interjections and quizzes.
* Preparation is key to effective speech delivery. It is important to practice the speech beforehand, and to be familiar with the content.
* When delivering a speech, it is important to maintain eye contact with the audience, and to use gestures and body language to emphasize points.
* It is important to be confident and to speak clearly. This will help to ensure that the audience is engaged and paying attention.
* It is also important to be aware of the potential for interjections and quizzes. If a question is asked, it is important to respond calmly and confidently.
* It is important to be prepared to answer any questions that may arise during the speech. It is important to be aware of the potential for interjections and quizzes, and to be prepared to answer them.
* It is also important to be aware of the potential for criticism and to be prepared to defend against it. It is important to be confident in one's abilities and to be able to respond to criticism in a constructive manner.
