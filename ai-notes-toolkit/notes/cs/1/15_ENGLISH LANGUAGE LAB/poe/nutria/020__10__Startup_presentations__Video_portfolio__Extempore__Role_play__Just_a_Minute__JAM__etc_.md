
### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

1. Startup presentations: This is a method of presenting a business idea or plan to potential investors in order to secure funding. It involves presenting the business idea in a clear and concise manner, highlighting the potential return on investment.

2. Video portfolio: A video portfolio is a collection of videos that demonstrate a person's skills, experience, and accomplishments. It can be used for job applications, to showcase a portfolio of work, or to demonstrate a product or service.

3. Extempore: Extempore is a type of public speaking in which the speaker is given a topic and must speak on it without any preparation. It is a great way to test a person's ability to think on their feet and to express their ideas in an articulate manner.

4. Role play: Role play is an acting exercise in which participants take on the roles of characters and act out a scene. It can be used to explore a particular issue or to practice communication or negotiation skills.

5. Just a Minute (JAM): JAM is a game in which participants are given a topic and must speak on it for one minute without any preparation. It is a great way to practice public speaking and to develop quick thinking skills.
