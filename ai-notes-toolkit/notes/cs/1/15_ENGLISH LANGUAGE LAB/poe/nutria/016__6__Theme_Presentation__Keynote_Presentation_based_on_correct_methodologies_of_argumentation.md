

### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

1. Identify a theme: The first step in creating a successful keynote presentation is to identify a theme that resonates with the audience. The theme should be relevant to the topic and should be something that the audience can relate to.

2. Research: After identifying a theme, the next step is to research the topic. This can be done by reading books, articles, and other sources of information related to the topic.

3. Outline: Once the research is completed, the presenter should create an outline of the presentation. This should include an introduction, main points, and a conclusion.

4. Visuals: Visuals are an important part of any presentation. They can help to illustrate the points that are being made and can be used to help the audience understand the topic more clearly.

5. Delivery: The presenter should practice the presentation multiple times before delivering it in front of an audience. This will help to ensure that the presentation is delivered smoothly and that all points are made clearly.

6. Argumentation: Argumentation is an important part of any presentation. The presenter should be prepared to defend their points and provide evidence to support their claims. This will help to ensure that the audience is engaged and that the presentation is successful.
