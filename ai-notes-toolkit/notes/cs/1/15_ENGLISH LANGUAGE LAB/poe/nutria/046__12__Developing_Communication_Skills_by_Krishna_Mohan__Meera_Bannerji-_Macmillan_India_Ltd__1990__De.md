
### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi

1. Effective communication is an important skill for success in both personal and professional life. 
2. Good communication involves the ability to both speak and listen effectively.
3. Communication is the process of conveying information from one person or group to another.
4. It is important to understand the different types of communication, such as verbal, non-verbal, written, and visual.
5. Verbal communication involves using words to express ideas, feelings, and thoughts.
6. Non-verbal communication involves using gestures, facial expressions, and body language to convey messages.
7. Written communication involves using written words to convey messages.
8. Visual communication involves using images, diagrams, and other visuals to convey messages.
9. Communication skills can be developed through practice, such as speaking in front of a mirror or participating in group activities.
10. Active listening is an important part of communication, as it allows the listener to better understand the speaker’s message.
11. It is important to be aware of cultural differences when communicating with people from different backgrounds.
12. Developing effective communication skills can help people to better connect with others and build strong relationships.
