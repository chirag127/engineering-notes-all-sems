
### 9. Comprehension Skills based on Reading and Listening Practical’s on a model Audio

1. Comprehension skills are the ability to understand and interpret information that is presented in various formats such as text, audio, and video.

2. Reading and listening comprehension skills can be developed through practice and repetition.

3. To develop these skills, it is important to read and listen to a variety of material, including both fiction and non-fiction.

4. Practicing listening comprehension skills can be done by listening to audio recordings such as podcasts, speeches, and lectures.

5. Practicing reading comprehension skills can be done by reading books, articles, and other written material.

6. To improve comprehension skills, it is important to take notes while reading or listening and to review them afterwards.

7. It is also important to ask questions while reading or listening to material, as this will help to clarify any misunderstandings.

8. Finally, it is important to practice comprehension skills on a regular basis in order to retain and improve them.
