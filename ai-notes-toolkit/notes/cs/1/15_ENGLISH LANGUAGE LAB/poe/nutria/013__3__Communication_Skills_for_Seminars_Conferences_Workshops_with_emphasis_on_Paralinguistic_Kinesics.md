
### 3. Communication Skills for Seminars/Conferences/Workshops with emphasis on Paralinguistic/Kinesics

* Paralinguistic communication involves the use of vocal intonation, body language, and facial expressions to convey meaning.
* Kinesics is the study of how people use body language to communicate, including gestures, posture, and facial expressions.
* It is important to be aware of the cultural differences in how people communicate.
* Good communication skills involve speaking clearly and confidently, listening actively, and utilizing appropriate non-verbal cues.
* It is important to be aware of the audience and the context of the conversation.
* When speaking in a seminar, conference, or workshop, it is important to maintain eye contact with the audience, use a strong voice, and maintain an open body language.
* It is also important to use pauses and vary the intonation of your voice to emphasize key points.
* When listening, it is important to maintain eye contact and use non-verbal cues such as nodding to show that you are paying attention.
