

### 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

* Business English is a specialized form of English used in professional and commercial settings. 
* It focuses on the language and vocabulary used in business settings, including meetings, negotiations, and presentations.
* It also covers topics such as etiquette, communication styles, and cultural awareness.
* The book provides a comprehensive introduction to the subject, covering topics such as writing emails, making presentations, and understanding business jargon. 
* It also includes exercises and activities to help readers practice and improve their business English.
* The book is written by Ken Taylor, an experienced business English teacher and author of several other books on the subject. 
* It is published by Orient Blackswan, a leading publisher of educational books in India. 
* The book was first published in 2011 and is now in its second edition.
