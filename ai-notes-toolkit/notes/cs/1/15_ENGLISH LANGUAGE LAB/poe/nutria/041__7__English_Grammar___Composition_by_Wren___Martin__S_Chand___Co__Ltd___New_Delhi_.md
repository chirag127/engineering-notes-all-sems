
### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi

- English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin is a comprehensive book for high school students that covers the entire English grammar. 
- It is written in a simple and lucid language and is an essential book for learning the basics of English grammar.
- The book is divided into two parts, namely, ‘Grammar’ and ‘Composition’. 
- The ‘Grammar’ section covers topics such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, interjections, tenses, active and passive voice, and so on. 
- The ‘Composition’ section covers topics such as letter writing, essay writing, précis writing, and so on. 
- The book also includes a section on ‘Correct Usage’ which explains the correct usage of words and phrases. 
- The book is published by S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.
