
### 4. Students will be Made to Evaluate the Correct and Error-Free Writing

1. Students should become well-versed in the rules of English grammar to be able to evaluate writing accurately.
2. Students should also cultivate a relevant technical style of communication and presentation, both for their work place and for academic purposes.
3. A strong understanding of grammar and technical writing will help students to produce more accurate and error-free writing.
4. Furthermore, students will be better able to recognize errors in the writing of others and be able to offer constructive feedback.
