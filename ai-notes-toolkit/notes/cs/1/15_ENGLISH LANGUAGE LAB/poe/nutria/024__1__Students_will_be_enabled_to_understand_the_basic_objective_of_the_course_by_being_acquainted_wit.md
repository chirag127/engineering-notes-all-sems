
### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- Reading: Students will be taught to read and comprehend various types of texts, and to identify main ideas and supporting details.
- Writing: Students will be taught to express their thoughts and ideas through writing, and to use different writing styles and formats.
- Listening: Students will be taught to listen actively and critically, and to recognize different types of verbal and non-verbal communication.
- Thinking: Students will be taught to think critically and analytically, and to evaluate and synthesize information.
- Speaking: Students will be taught to communicate effectively and confidently in both formal and informal contexts.
