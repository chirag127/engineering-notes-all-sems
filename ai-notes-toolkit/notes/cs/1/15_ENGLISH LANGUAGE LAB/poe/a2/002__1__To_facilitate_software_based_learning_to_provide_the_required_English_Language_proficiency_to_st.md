### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

1. Software based learning: Software applications and tools can be leveraged to provide an interactive and engaging learning experience for students to learn English. This includes tools for vocabulary building, grammar practice, comprehension exercises, etc. Students can learn at their own pace using these tools and get immediate feedback on their progress.
2. Personalized learning: The learning experience can be personalized for each student based on their current proficiency levels. The software can assess the student's current skills and tailor the learning content and pace accordingly. This personalized approach is more effective for student learning.
3. Constant feedback: Students get constant feedback on their learning through scores, ratings, and progress reports. This helps them understand their strong and weak areas and work on improving their skills. The feedback loop aids in better retention of learning.
4. interactive learning: Using software tools enables interactive learning methods such as quizzing, puzzles, games, etc. This fosters student engagement and interest in learning the language. Interactive and engaging methods can enhance learning outcomes.
5. Consistency: Software-based learning provides a consistent learning experience. The tools and content don't get fatigued or vary in quality. This results in steady progress and achievement of learning goals. The consistency is not dependent on human trainers or teachers.

The above points highlight how software-based learning can be leveraged to provide the required English language proficiency to students in a structured, personalized, interactive, and consistent manner. With the aid of technology, students can learn at their own pace and achieve mastery.
