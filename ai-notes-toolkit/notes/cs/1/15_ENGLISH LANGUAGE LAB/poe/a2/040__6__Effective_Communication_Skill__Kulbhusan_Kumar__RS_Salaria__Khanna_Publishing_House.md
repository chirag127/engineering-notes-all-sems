### 6. Effective Communication Skill

* Be Clear: Communicate in a simple, straightforward manner. Avoid ambiguity and confusion. Choose appropriate words and be concise.
* Be Brief: Avoid unnecessary verbosity and be precise while communicating. Listeners have a short attention span, so keep your message brief and to the point.
* Be Positive: Use a positive tone and attitude while communicating. This makes you appear confident and helps to keep the audience engaged. Criticism and negativity can discourage the listener.
* Be Courteous: Show respect towards the listener through tone of voice and choice of words. Politeness and courtesy go a long way in having a constructive communication.
* Be Patient: Avoid interrupting the speaker or finishing their sentences. Allow them to speak freely and communicate their thoughts. Patience is important for effective two-way communication.
* Use Appropriate Body Language: Facial expressions, hand gestures, and posture/stance are important to reinforce your message. Engage your body to appear open, friendly and confident.
* Listen Actively: Pay attention to the speaker and understand their perspective. Provide feedback and ask questions. This helps avoid confusion and leads to better understanding.
* Provide Feedback: Summary and paraphrasing what the speaker said shows you were listening and gives them a chance to clarify. Ask questions if needed and share your thoughts to make communication two-way.

The above points are from the book 'Effective Communication Skill' by Kulbhusan Kumar and R.S. Salaria, published by Khanna Publishing House.
