### 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi.

1. Personality refers to the unique ways in which a person thinks, feels and behaves. It is the sum total of physical, mental and emotional characteristics that define an individual.
2. Soft skills refer to abilities such as communication skills, emotional intelligence, creativity, critical thinking, etc. that complement technical skills and are crucial to success in life and career.
3. Personality development helps individuals realize their full potential, improve their interaction with others and adapt to change. It enables them to lead a more productive and satisfying life.
4. The book discusses the importance of developing a positive personality and acquiring soft skills. It provides practical tips and techniques on various aspects of personality development and soft skills such as:
- Enhancing self-awareness
- Building confidence
- Developing communication skills
- Managing emotions
- Thinking positively
- Embracing change
- Working with others
- Continuous self-improvement

5. By following the principles and practices outlined in the book, one can develop a pleasant personality, strengthen interpersonal skills and achieve personal and professional excellence. The book serves as a useful guide for students, professionals and those interested in self-development.
