### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes.

1. Prepare thoroughly: Study the topic in depth. Anticipate questions and prepare answers. Practice the speech/presentation several times. Being well-prepared boosts confidence.
2. Make eye contact: Engage the audience by making eye contact. Look at specific members of the audience as you speak to make a personal connection and help keep everyone engaged.
3. Show passion: Speak with enthusiasm and energy to keep the audience interested. Share your passion for the topic. Even if the topic is complex, show that you find it interesting.
4. Project confidence: Stand up straight, smile, and use confident body language and tone of voice. Even if you are nervous on the inside, appearing self-assured on the outside can help you win over an audience.
5. Address challenges: If faced with difficult questions or comments, stay calm and composed. Acknowledge the point, and explain your perspective. Refer back to your preparation to reinforce your key points. If you do not know the answer, it is fine to say you will get back to the person with a response.
6. Conclude strongly: End with a key summary point or call to action. Finish with energy and confidence to leave a lasting impact. Thank the audience for their time and attention.
