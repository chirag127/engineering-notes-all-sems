### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

1. Reading
- Recognizing the main idea and supporting details
- Understanding logical relationships between ideas
- Making inferences and drawing conclusions
- Understanding text structure (chronological, compare-contrast, cause-effect, problem-solution)
- Adjusting reading speed and strategies based on purpose and complexity of text

2. Writing
- Organizing thoughts in a logical flow
- Using specific and concise language
- Varying sentence structure
- Applying appropriate tone and style based on audience and purpose
- Summarizing and paraphrasing information from sources
- Developing coherent paragraphs and essays

3. Listening
- Paying attention to the speaker and avoiding distractions
- Understanding the main ideas, important details, and sequence of information
- Avoiding interrupting the speaker or finishing their sentences
- Asking relevant follow-up questions
- Summarizing and responding appropriately based on the context and purpose

4. Thinking
- Analyzing information objectively and logically
- Seeing relationships between ideas and interpreting information
- Evaluating arguments and claims
- Asking thoughtful questions
- Challenging assumptions and considering alternative perspectives
- Applying knowledge from different areas to solve problems in new ways

5. Speaking
- Communicating a clear main message or point of view
- Organizing thoughts in a logical flow
- Using appropriate tone, pace, and pauses
- Varying language, vocabulary, and sentence structure
- Adapting style and content based on audience and purpose
- Asking for feedback and confirming understanding
