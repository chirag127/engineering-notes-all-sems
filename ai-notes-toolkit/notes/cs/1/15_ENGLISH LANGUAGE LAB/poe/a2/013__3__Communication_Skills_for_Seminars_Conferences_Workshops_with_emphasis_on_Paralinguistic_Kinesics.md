### 3. Communication Skills for Seminars/Conferences/Workshops with emphasis on Paralinguistic/Kinesics.

1. Posture:
- Stand up straight with shoulders back and chest open.
- Make eye contact with the audience.
- Use hand gestures appropriately to emphasize important points.

2. Facial Expressions:
- Smile occasionally to appear open and friendly.
- Nod along while listening to show you are engaged.
- Raise eyebrows for emphasis on significant statements.

3. Tone of Voice:
- Speak clearly by pronouncing words correctly and projecting your voice.
- Vary your tone, speed, and pauses to maintain audience interest.
- Increase volume and energy to stress important topics or conclusions.

4. Clothing and Hygiene:
- Dress professionally in formal business attire.
- Practice good hygiene like showering and wearing deodorant.
- Appearance makes a first impression, so be well-groomed.

The above points cover key paralinguistic and kinesic communication skills to utilize when conducting seminars, conferences, or workshops. Demonstrating these techniques will make you appear more confident, credible, and engaging to attendees. Always prepare thoroughly on the material and practice your delivery for optimal results.
