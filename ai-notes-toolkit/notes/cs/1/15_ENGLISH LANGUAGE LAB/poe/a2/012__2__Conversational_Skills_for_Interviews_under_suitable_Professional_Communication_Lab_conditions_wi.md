### 2. Conversational Skills for Interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics.

1. Maintain eye contact: Make eye contact during the interview. Brief to moderate eye contact indicates interest, confidence and involvement in the discussion. Avoid staring or not making any eye contact.

2. Body language: Display positive body language like leaning slightly forward, keeping an open and relaxed posture, smiling occasionally etc. Avoid negative body language such as crossed arms, frowning, looking distracted or tense.

3. Tone of voice: Speak in a confident and clear tone. Modulate your voice appropriately based on the context. Avoid speaking in a monotonic tone or being too loud/soft spoken.

4. Listen actively: Listen to the interviewer actively by maintaining eye contact, nodding occasionally and repeating parts of the question before answering to confirm your understanding. This indicates you are engaged in the discussion.

5. Be enthusiastic: Display enthusiasm and energy in your gestures, facial expressions and tone of voice. This portrays your passion for the work and confidence. However, avoid being overly dramatic.

6. Be concise: Provide concise answers to questions. Avoid rambling or being too verbose. Structure your answers appropriately with a clear introduction, body and conclusion.

7. Ask questions: It is appropriate to ask questions to the interviewer regarding the role, team, company etc. This indicates your interest and enthusiasm. However, avoid asking too many questions or questions whose answers you can easily find online.
