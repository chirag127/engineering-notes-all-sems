### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

1. Teach the basic rules of English grammar like subject-verb agreement, article usage, tense forms, modal usage, etc. This will help students understand the structure of sentences and correct word order.
2. Give multiple examples of correct and incorrect usage of grammar in sentences and ask students to identify the errors. This will help them apply the grammar rules in practical sentences.
3. Provide grammar worksheets and exercises like fill in the blanks, sentence reordering or restructuring, grammar quizzes, etc. This will help reinforce the grammar concepts and rules learned.
4. Give students paragraphs or essays with grammar errors and ask them to proofread and correct the errors. This will help them identify grammar mistakes in a larger context and apply the learning.
5. Train students to do a thorough proofread of their own writing to apply the grammar skills and ensure correct and error-free writing. Regular practice will make them well versed in English grammar.
