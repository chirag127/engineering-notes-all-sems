## LIST OF PRACTICALS

1. Ohm's Law Experiment
- Connect a resistor, voltmeter, ammeter and a variable voltage source in series.
- Vary the voltage and note down the current and resistance readings to verify Ohm's Law (V=IR).

2. Series and Parallel Circuits
- Connect resistors in series and parallel combinations.
- Measure the total resistance in each case and verify the relationships:
-- Resistance in series: Rtotal = R1 + R2 + ...
-- Resistance in parallel: 1/Rtotal = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + ...

3. Transistor Circuits
- Build common transistor circuits like CE amplifier, CB amplifier and transistor switch.
- Measure the input and output characteristics to understand the transistor's operating regions.

4. Op-Amp Circuits
- Build inverting and non-inverting op-amp amplifier circuits.
- Measure the voltage gain and verify that it matches the theoretical gain.
- Build op-amp comparator and op-amp Schmitt trigger circuits and demonstrate their working.
