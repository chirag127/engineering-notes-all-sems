### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

1. Reading
- Understanding the main ideas and key details of a written text
- Drawing inferences from a text and comprehending implicit ideas
- Identifying the author's purpose, tone, and point of view

2. Writing
- Writing concisely, logically, and coherently
- Developing a thesis statement and supporting points that build an argument
- Using specific, relevant examples to illustrate key ideas
- Editing and revising drafts for grammar, spelling, word choice, and flow

3. Listening
- Understanding speaker's key themes, purpose, or goal
- Identifying supporting details and implied information
- Distinguishing fact from opinion
- Asking questions to verify understanding

4. Thinking
- Organizing and analyzing information logically
- Seeing connections across ideas
- Evaluating arguments and claims for flaws or inaccuracies
- Coming up with original inferences or solutions to problems

5. Speaking
- Sharing information with clarity and confidence
- Tailoring language and tone for different audiences and contexts
- Asking questions and engaging listeners to encourage discussion
- Defending a position or perspective politely and respectfully

The content aims to highlight key aspects of each communication skill that students will develop and practice through the course. The overarching goal is to enable students to effectively read, write, listen, think, and speak for academic and professional purposes.
