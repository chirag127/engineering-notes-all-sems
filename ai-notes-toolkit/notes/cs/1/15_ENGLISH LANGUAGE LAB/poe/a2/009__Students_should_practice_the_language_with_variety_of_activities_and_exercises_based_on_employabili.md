#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills. Interactive and Communicative Practical with emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication, based on International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.).

    - Communication skills - Both written and oral presentations, debates, group discussions, etc.
    - Teamwork skills - Group projects, role-plays, simulations, etc.
    - Problem-solving skills - Case studies, projects, etc.
    - Critical thinking skills - Analysis of texts, debates, etc.
- Language learning should incorporate interactive and communicative techniques focusing on spoken communication.
- Pronunciation should be taught based on the International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.) which provides a set of symbols to represent distinct sounds in language. Learning the correct pronunciation is essential for effective communication.
- Such practical and interactive techniques will make language learning more engaging, relevant and effective in developing proficiency and skills.
