### 9. Comprehension Skills based on Reading and Listening Practical’s on a model Audio

1. Listen carefully to the audio and note down the key points. Some of the things to keep in mind while listening:
- Note down the main topic of the audio.
- Note down the key ideas and arguments.
- Note down examples if any.
- Note down the sequence of ideas.

2. Once the audio is over, organize the points in a logical flow. See if you can connect the points and frame good paragraphs.
3. Read the passage thorougly and understand the main theme or topic. Some tips for effective reading comprehension:
- Read actively by asking questions about the text.
- Annotate and underline key terms and main ideas.
- Summarize and paraphrase sections of the text.
- Make connections between the text and your own experiences or other texts you have read.

4. Compare your notes from listening with the main points from reading. See if you have captured the essence of the audio and the passage. This will help evaluate how well you have understood the content.
5. Inferences and analysis: Go beyond the literal meanings and interpret the implications and analyse the arguments or points of view. Apply your critical thinking skills to comprehend the content thoroughly.
