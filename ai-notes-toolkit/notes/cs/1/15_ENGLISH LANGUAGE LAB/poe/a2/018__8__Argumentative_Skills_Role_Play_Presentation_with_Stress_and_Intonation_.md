### 8. Argumentative Skills/Role Play Presentation with Stress and Intonation

1. What are argumentative skills?
- The ability to logically and rationally convince someone of your point of view.
- Backing up your statements with solid evidence and reasoning.
- Addressing counterarguments and opposing views fairly and objectively.

2. What is role play presentation?
- Acting out a scenario or task to simulate a real-life situation.
- Presenting or pitching something to others by assuming a particular role or position.
- Allows you to demonstrate and practice key skills in a safe space.

3. Why is stress and intonation important?
- Stress refers to emphasizing certain words or syllables in a sentence to convey meaning.
- Intonation refers to the rise and fall of your voice to express emotions or ask questions.
- Using stress and intonation effectively helps to engage the audience and keep them interested.
- It makes your presentation dynamic, compelling and persuasive.
