# Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- The "Manual of Practical Communication" is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in 2013.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to effective communication, covering various aspects of verbal and non-verbal communication.
- It provides practical tips and techniques for improving communication skills in personal and professional settings.
- The book is divided into chapters, each focusing on a specific aspect of communication, such as listening, speaking, writing, and body language.
- It includes exercises and activities to help readers practice and improve their communication skills.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills, whether for personal or professional reasons.
