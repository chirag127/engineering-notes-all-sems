### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

- Word Power Made Easy is a book written by Norman Lewis.
- It was published by W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors in 2009 in Delhi.
- The book is designed to help readers improve their vocabulary and language skills.
- It provides a step-by-step method for increasing knowledge and mastery of the English language.
- The book is divided into a number of chapters, each focusing on a specific aspect of vocabulary building.
- It includes exercises and quizzes to help readers test their progress and reinforce their understanding of the material.
- Word Power Made Easy is widely regarded as a classic in the field of vocabulary building and is a popular choice for students and professionals alike.
