### 11. Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan, Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd., 2001, New Delhi.

- Business correspondence refers to the exchange of information in a written format for the process of business activities.
- Report writing is the process of organizing and presenting information in a written format for a specific audience and purpose.
- The book by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan provides a comprehensive guide to effective business correspondence and report writing.
- It covers various types of business correspondence, including letters, memos, emails, and reports.
- The book also provides guidelines for effective writing, including clarity, conciseness, and tone.
- It includes examples and exercises to help readers improve their writing skills.
- The book is published by Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd. and was released in 2001 in New Delhi.
