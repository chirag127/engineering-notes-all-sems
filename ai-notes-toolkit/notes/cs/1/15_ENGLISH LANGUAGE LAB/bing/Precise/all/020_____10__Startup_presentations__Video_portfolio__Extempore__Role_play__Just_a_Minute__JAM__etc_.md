### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

1. **Startup presentations** are presentations given by entrepreneurs to pitch their business ideas to potential investors, customers, or partners. These presentations typically include information about the business model, target market, financial projections, and team members.

2. A **video portfolio** is a collection of videos that showcase an individual's skills, talents, and accomplishments. Video portfolios are commonly used by artists, designers, and other creative professionals to demonstrate their work to potential clients or employers.

3. **Extempore** is a type of public speaking where the speaker is given little or no time to prepare their speech. The speaker must rely on their knowledge, quick thinking, and improvisation skills to deliver a coherent and engaging speech on the spot.

4. **Role play** is a type of simulation where participants act out different roles in a fictional scenario. Role play is commonly used in training and education to help individuals develop their communication, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.

5. **Just a Minute (JAM)** is a public speaking game where participants are given one minute to speak on a given topic without hesitation, repetition, or deviation. JAM is a fun and challenging way to improve one's public speaking and improvisation skills.

These are just a few examples of the many different types of presentations and public speaking activities that individuals can engage in to develop their communication skills and showcase their talents. Each of these activities has its own unique challenges and benefits, and individuals can choose the ones that best suit their needs and goals.
