# 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

- English grammar is a set of rules that govern the structure of sentences and the use of words in the English language.
- To write correctly and without errors, it is important for students to have a good understanding of these rules.
- Grammar rules include the proper use of punctuation, verb tense, subject-verb agreement, and sentence structure.
- Students can improve their grammar skills by studying grammar rules, practicing writing, and receiving feedback from teachers or peers.
- There are many resources available to help students learn and practice English grammar, including textbooks, online resources, and grammar exercises.
- By being well-versed in the rules of English grammar, students can improve their writing skills and communicate more effectively in written form.
