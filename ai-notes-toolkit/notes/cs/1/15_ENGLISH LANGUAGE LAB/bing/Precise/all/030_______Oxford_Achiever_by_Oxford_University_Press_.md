# Oxford Achiever by Oxford University Press

- Oxford Achiever is a learning platform developed by Oxford University Press (China) Ltd., covering key subjects: English, Chinese and Mathematics.
- It has over 45,000 questions, diverse learning aspects and varied question types; which contribute to a flexible learning approach.
- It is a self-learning system for objective-specific online assessment and individualized learning.
- It includes a Diagnostic Placement Test to place students at the right starting level to learn according to their own aptitude/ability in each subject area.
- It is a comprehensive app-based learning and assessment system developed with the aim of building students’ language skills and subject knowledge along an individualized, self-paced learning path.
- It is a total e-learning solution for effective app/computer-based teaching and learning.
