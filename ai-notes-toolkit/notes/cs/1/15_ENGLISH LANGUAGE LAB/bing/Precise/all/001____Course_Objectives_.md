## Course Objectives:

1. To provide a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. To develop critical thinking and analytical skills.
3. To enhance the ability to apply theoretical concepts to real-world scenarios.
4. To foster independent learning and research skills.
5. To encourage active participation and engagement in class discussions and activities.
6. To prepare students for further academic or professional pursuits in the field.
7. To promote the development of effective communication and collaboration skills.
8. To instill a sense of ethical responsibility and social awareness.
