# 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi

Personality Development & Soft Skills is a book written by Barun K. Mitra and published by Oxford University Press in 2012 in New Delhi. The book focuses on the development of an individual's personality and the acquisition of soft skills. Some of the key points covered in the book include:

1. The importance of self-awareness and self-improvement in personality development.
2. The role of communication skills, body language, and etiquette in personal and professional interactions.
3. Techniques for developing positive thinking, emotional intelligence, and stress management.
4. The significance of time management, goal setting, and motivation in achieving personal and professional success.
5. The impact of leadership, teamwork, and conflict resolution on personal and professional growth.
