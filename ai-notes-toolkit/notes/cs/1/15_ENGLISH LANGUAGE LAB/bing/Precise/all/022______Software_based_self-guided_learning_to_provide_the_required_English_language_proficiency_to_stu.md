#### Software-based self-guided learning for English language proficiency

- Software-based self-guided learning can provide students with the necessary English language proficiency for employability and career readiness.
- The software should align with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and deliver a CEFR level of B2 upon completion.
- CEFR is a widely recognized standard for describing language proficiency and is used by employers and educational institutions to assess the language skills of individuals.
- A CEFR level of B2 indicates that the individual can understand the main ideas of complex text on both concrete and abstract topics, including technical discussions in their field of specialization.
- The individual can also interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers quite possible without strain for either party.
- The software can provide a structured and personalized learning experience for students, allowing them to progress at their own pace and focus on areas where they need improvement.
- The use of technology can also provide students with access to a wide range of multimedia resources and interactive learning tools, making the learning experience more engaging and effective.
- In summary, software-based self-guided learning can provide students with the necessary English language proficiency for employability and career readiness, aligned with the CEFR standards and delivering a CEFR level of B2 upon completion.
