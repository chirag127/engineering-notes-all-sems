### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

- **Startup presentations** are presentations given by entrepreneurs to pitch their business ideas to potential investors, customers, or partners. These presentations typically include information about the business concept, target market, financial projections, and growth strategy.

- A **video portfolio** is a collection of videos that showcase an individual's skills, talents, and accomplishments. Video portfolios are commonly used by artists, performers, and other creative professionals to demonstrate their abilities to potential clients or employers.

- **Extempore** is a form of impromptu speaking where the speaker is given little or no preparation time before delivering a speech on a given topic. Extempore speaking is often used as a training exercise to improve public speaking skills and quick thinking.

- **Role play** is an activity where participants act out scenarios or situations in order to practice or demonstrate skills, or to explore different perspectives. Role play is commonly used in training and education, as well as in therapy and conflict resolution.

- **Just a Minute (JAM)** is a game where participants are challenged to speak on a given topic for one minute without hesitation, repetition, or deviation. JAM is often used as a training exercise to improve public speaking skills and quick thinking.

These are all effective methods for improving communication skills, building confidence, and showcasing one's abilities. They can be used in a variety of settings, including education, training, and professional development.
