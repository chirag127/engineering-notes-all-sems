### 8. Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists, Sangeeta Sharma et.al. PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd, 2011, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to effective communication for engineers and scientists.
- It covers various aspects of communication, including written, oral, and visual communication.
- The book provides practical tips and techniques for improving communication skills, including how to write clear and concise technical reports, deliver effective presentations, and communicate effectively with colleagues and clients.
- The authors, Sangeeta Sharma et.al., are experts in the field of communication and have extensive experience in teaching and training engineers and scientists.
- Published by PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd in 2011, this book is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills in a technical or scientific field.
