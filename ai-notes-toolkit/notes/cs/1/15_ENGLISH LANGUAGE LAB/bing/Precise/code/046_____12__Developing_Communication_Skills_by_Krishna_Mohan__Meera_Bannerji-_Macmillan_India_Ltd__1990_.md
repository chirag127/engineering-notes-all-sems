### Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi

- "Developing Communication Skills" is a book written by Krishna Mohan and Meera Banerjee and published by Macmillan India Ltd. in 1990, Delhi .
- The book is designed to help anyone who wants to improve their skills in communication .
- It guides the reader with their choice of words, body language, and confidence .
- The book works towards improving a person’s oral as well as written communication .
- It is primarily designed as a text for advanced level courses in communication skills .
- However, it would prove equally useful as an effective aid to professionals in performing their day-to-day communicative tasks .
