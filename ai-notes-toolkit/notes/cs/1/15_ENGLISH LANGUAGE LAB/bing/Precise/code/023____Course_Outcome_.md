## Course Outcome:

A course outcome is a statement that describes the knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to have acquired by the end of a course. Course outcomes are used to:

1. Define the goals and objectives of a course.
2. Communicate the expectations of the course to students.
3. Guide the development of course content and assessments.
4. Evaluate the effectiveness of the course in achieving its goals.

Course outcomes should be specific, measurable, and achievable within the scope of the course. They should be aligned with the overall program outcomes and the mission of the institution. Course outcomes should be regularly reviewed and updated to ensure that they remain relevant and effective in achieving the goals of the course.
