### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

1. **Preparation**: Prepare your speech or presentation thoroughly. Know your material inside and out, and anticipate potential questions or interjections.
2. **Confidence**: Speak with confidence and conviction. This will help you maintain control of the conversation and convey your message effectively.
3. **Active Listening**: Listen carefully to any interjections or questions. This will help you understand the concerns or objections of your audience and respond appropriately.
4. **Stay Calm**: Remain calm and composed, even if the interjection or question is challenging or confrontational. This will help you maintain control of the situation and respond effectively.
5. **Address the Concern**: Address the concern or objection directly and respectfully. Provide a clear and concise response, using evidence or examples to support your position.
6. **Stay on Topic**: Stay focused on the topic at hand and avoid getting sidetracked by unrelated issues.
7. **Be Respectful**: Treat your audience with respect, even if you disagree with their perspective. This will help foster a productive and respectful dialogue.

These are some key skills and strategies for effectively delivering a speech or presentation and defending against interjections or quizzes. By following these guidelines, you can effectively communicate your message and engage with your audience in a productive and respectful manner.
