### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

- "English Grammar, Composition and Usage" is a book written by N.K.Agrawal and F.T.Wood.
- It is published by Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.
- The book covers various aspects of English grammar, composition, and usage.
- It is a comprehensive guide for students and learners of the English language.
- The book provides clear explanations and examples to help readers understand and apply the rules of English grammar and usage.
- It also includes exercises and practice materials to help readers improve their skills in writing and composition.
- The book is suitable for learners of all levels, from beginners to advanced students.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their proficiency in the English language.
