### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- The "Manual of Practical Communication" is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in 2013.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to effective communication in various practical situations.
- It covers topics such as verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, public speaking, and interpersonal communication.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, with numerous examples and practical tips to help readers improve their communication skills.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to enhance their ability to communicate effectively in personal and professional settings.
