### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- **Reading:** Students will learn how to read effectively, including techniques for improving comprehension, speed, and retention.
- **Writing:** Students will learn how to write clearly and concisely, with a focus on organization, grammar, and style.
- **Listening:** Students will learn how to listen actively and critically, including techniques for improving concentration and understanding.
- **Thinking:** Students will learn how to think critically and creatively, including techniques for problem-solving and decision-making.
- **Speaking:** Students will learn how to speak confidently and persuasively, with a focus on delivery, body language, and vocal variety.

By mastering these skills, students will be better equipped to communicate effectively in both personal and professional settings.
