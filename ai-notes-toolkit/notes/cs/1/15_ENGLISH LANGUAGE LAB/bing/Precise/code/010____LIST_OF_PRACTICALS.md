## LIST OF PRACTICALS

1. **Preparation of standard solutions**: This practical involves preparing solutions of known concentrations, which can be used as a reference for future experiments.
2. **Acid-base titrations**: This practical involves determining the concentration of an unknown acid or base by titrating it against a standard solution of known concentration.
3. **Redox titrations**: This practical involves determining the concentration of an unknown oxidizing or reducing agent by titrating it against a standard solution of known concentration.
4. **Gravimetric analysis**: This practical involves determining the amount of a substance present in a sample by measuring the mass of a precipitate formed.
5. **Spectrophotometry**: This practical involves measuring the absorbance of a solution at a specific wavelength to determine the concentration of a substance present in the solution.
6. **Chromatography**: This practical involves separating the components of a mixture based on their different affinities for a stationary and mobile phase.
7. **Synthesis of compounds**: This practical involves synthesizing a compound by carrying out a chemical reaction and purifying the product.
8. **Qualitative analysis**: This practical involves identifying the presence of specific ions or functional groups in a sample by carrying out chemical tests.
9. **Thermochemistry**: This practical involves measuring the heat released or absorbed during a chemical reaction to determine the enthalpy change of the reaction.
10. **Electrochemistry**: This practical involves studying the relationship between electricity and chemical reactions by carrying out experiments involving electrochemical cells.
