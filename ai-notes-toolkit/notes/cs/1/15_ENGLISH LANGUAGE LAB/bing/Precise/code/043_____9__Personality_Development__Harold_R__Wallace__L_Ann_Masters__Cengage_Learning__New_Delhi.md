### 9. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace & L.Ann Masters, Cengage Learning, New Delhi

Personality development is the process of enhancing one's character and improving one's behavior and attitude towards life. It is a continuous process that involves the development of various aspects of an individual's personality, such as emotional, intellectual, social, and moral development.

The book "Personality Development" by Harold R. Wallace and L.Ann Masters, published by Cengage Learning in New Delhi, provides a comprehensive guide to the subject. Some of the key points covered in the book include:

1. Understanding the concept of personality and its various components.
2. The role of nature and nurture in shaping an individual's personality.
3. The importance of self-awareness and self-reflection in personal growth.
4. Techniques for developing emotional intelligence and managing emotions effectively.
5. The role of communication and interpersonal skills in building healthy relationships.
6. Strategies for developing a positive attitude and building resilience.
7. The importance of goal setting and personal planning in achieving success.
8. Techniques for managing stress and maintaining a healthy work-life balance.

Overall, the book provides a comprehensive and practical guide to the subject of personality development, with a focus on developing the skills and attitudes necessary for personal and professional success. It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their personal and professional lives.
