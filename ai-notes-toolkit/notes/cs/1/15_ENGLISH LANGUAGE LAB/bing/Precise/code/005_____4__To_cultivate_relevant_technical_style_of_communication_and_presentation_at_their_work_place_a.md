### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

To cultivate a relevant technical style of communication and presentation at the workplace and for academic uses, one should consider the following points:

1. **Understand the audience**: It is important to understand the audience and their level of technical knowledge. This will help in tailoring the communication and presentation to their needs and level of understanding.

2. **Use clear and concise language**: Technical communication should be clear and concise. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the audience may not understand.

3. **Use visuals**: Visuals such as diagrams, charts, and graphs can help in conveying technical information more effectively.

4. **Organize the information**: The information should be organized in a logical and easy-to-follow manner. This will help the audience to understand the information better.

5. **Practice**: Practice makes perfect. It is important to practice the technical communication and presentation to improve one's skills.

By following these points, one can cultivate a relevant technical style of communication and presentation at the workplace and for academic uses.
