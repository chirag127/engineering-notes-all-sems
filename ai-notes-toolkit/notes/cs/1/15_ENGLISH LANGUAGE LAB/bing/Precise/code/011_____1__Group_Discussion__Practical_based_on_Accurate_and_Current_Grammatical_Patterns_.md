### 1. Group Discussion: Practical based on Accurate and Current Grammatical Patterns

Group discussion is a practical activity that involves a group of people discussing a topic or issue. It is an effective way to improve communication skills, critical thinking, and teamwork. In order to have a successful group discussion, it is important to use accurate and current grammatical patterns. Here are some points to consider:

- **Use correct grammar**: It is important to use correct grammar during a group discussion to effectively communicate your ideas and thoughts. Incorrect grammar can lead to confusion and misunderstandings.

- **Stay up-to-date with current grammatical patterns**: Language is constantly evolving, and it is important to stay up-to-date with current grammatical patterns. This will help you to communicate effectively and avoid using outdated or incorrect grammar.

- **Practice**: The best way to improve your grammar is to practice. Participating in group discussions, reading, and writing are all effective ways to improve your grammar.

- **Ask for feedback**: Don't be afraid to ask for feedback on your grammar during a group discussion. This will help you to identify areas for improvement and work on them.

- **Use resources**: There are many resources available to help you improve your grammar, such as grammar books, online resources, and language courses. Make use of these resources to improve your grammar and communication skills.

In summary, using accurate and current grammatical patterns is important for effective communication during a group discussion. Practice, feedback, and the use of resources can help you to improve your grammar and communication skills.
