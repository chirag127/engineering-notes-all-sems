### Oxford Achiever by Oxford University Press

- Oxford Achiever is a learning platform developed by Oxford University Press (China) Ltd.
- It covers key subjects: English, Chinese and Mathematics.
- It has over 45,000 questions, diverse learning aspects and varied question types.
- It is a self-learning system for objective-specific online assessment and individualized learning.
- It has a Diagnostic Placement Test to place students at the right starting level to learn according to their own aptitude/ability in each subject area.
- It is a total e-learning solution for effective app/computer-based teaching and learning.
- It is characterized by a sound pedagogical approach and aims to build students’ language skills and subject knowledge along an individualized, self-paced learning path.
