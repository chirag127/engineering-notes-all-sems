### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

- The formation of a strong professional vocabulary is essential for students to succeed in their academic and professional careers.
- By developing a substantial base of vocabulary, students can effectively communicate their ideas and thoughts through various modes such as comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking.
- A strong vocabulary enables students to express themselves clearly and concisely, making it easier for them to convey their message to their audience.
- The application of a professional vocabulary can be done at different platforms, including academic institutions, professional organizations, and social media.
- By mastering the use of a professional vocabulary, students can enhance their credibility and increase their chances of success in their chosen field.
