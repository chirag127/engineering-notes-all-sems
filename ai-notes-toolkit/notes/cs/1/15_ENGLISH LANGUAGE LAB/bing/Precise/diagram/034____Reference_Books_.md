## Reference Books:

- Reference books are a type of non-fiction book that provides information on a specific subject.
- They are often used for research or to gain a deeper understanding of a topic.
- Examples of reference books include encyclopedias, dictionaries, thesauri, almanacs, and atlases.
- Reference books are usually organized in a way that makes it easy to find the information you need.
- They often include an index, table of contents, and/or glossary to help you locate specific information.
- Reference books are typically not meant to be read cover-to-cover, but rather to be consulted as needed.
- They are often found in libraries and can be a valuable resource for students, researchers, and anyone seeking to learn more about a particular subject.
