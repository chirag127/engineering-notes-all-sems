### 4. English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to English grammar and usage.
- It is written by R.P. Sinha and published by Oxford University Press in 2005.
- The book is intended for students and teachers of English as a second language.
- It covers all aspects of English grammar, including parts of speech, sentence structure, and punctuation.
- The book also includes a section on common errors in English usage and how to avoid them.
- It is written in a clear and concise style, making it easy to understand and use.
- The book is widely used as a reference for students preparing for exams in English language and literature.
- It is an essential resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
