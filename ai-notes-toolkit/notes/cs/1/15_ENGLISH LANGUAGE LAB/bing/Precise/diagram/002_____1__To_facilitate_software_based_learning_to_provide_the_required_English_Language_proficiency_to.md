### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

- Software-based learning can provide a flexible and interactive learning experience for students to improve their English language proficiency.
- It can provide personalized learning experiences tailored to the individual needs and abilities of each student.
- It can provide immediate feedback and progress tracking, allowing students to monitor their own progress and adjust their learning strategies accordingly.
- It can provide a wide range of multimedia resources, including audio, video, and interactive exercises, to enhance the learning experience.
- It can provide opportunities for collaborative learning and peer interaction, allowing students to practice their language skills with others.
- It can provide a cost-effective and scalable solution for providing English language instruction to large numbers of students.
