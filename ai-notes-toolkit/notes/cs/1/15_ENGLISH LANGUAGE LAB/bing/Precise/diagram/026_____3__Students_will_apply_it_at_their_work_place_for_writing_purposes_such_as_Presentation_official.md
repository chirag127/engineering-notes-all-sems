### 3. Students will apply it at their work place for writing purposes such as Presentation/official drafting/administrative communication and use it for document/project/report/research paper writing.

- Markdown is a lightweight markup language that is easy to learn and use.
- It is a popular choice for writing and formatting text for various purposes, including presentations, official drafting, administrative communication, and document/project/report/research paper writing.
- Markdown allows users to format text using simple syntax, making it easy to create professional-looking documents without the need for specialized software.
- By learning and using Markdown, students can improve their writing skills and produce high-quality documents for their work.
- Markdown is widely used in various industries and is a valuable skill for students to have as they enter the workforce.
