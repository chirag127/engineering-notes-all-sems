### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013

- "Practical Communication Process & Practice" is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in Krishna Nagar, Delhi in 2013.
- The book is a reference for technical communication and is listed as a recommended reading in the syllabus of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University.
- The book is aimed at improving the communication skills of its readers and provides practical tips and techniques for effective communication.
