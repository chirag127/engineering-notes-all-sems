### 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi.

1. Personality Development & Soft Skills is a book written by Barun K. Mitra and published by Oxford University Press in 2012 in New Delhi.
2. The book focuses on the development of an individual's personality and the acquisition of soft skills.
3. Soft skills refer to a set of interpersonal and social skills that are essential for personal and professional success.
4. The book provides practical tips and techniques for improving communication skills, emotional intelligence, and leadership qualities.
5. It also covers topics such as time management, stress management, and conflict resolution.
6. The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and apply the concepts discussed.
7. It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their personal and professional development.
8. The book has received positive reviews for its practical approach and comprehensive coverage of the subject matter.
9. It is widely used as a reference for courses and training programs on personality development and soft skills.
10. Overall, Personality Development & Soft Skills is a must-read for anyone looking to enhance their personal and professional growth.
