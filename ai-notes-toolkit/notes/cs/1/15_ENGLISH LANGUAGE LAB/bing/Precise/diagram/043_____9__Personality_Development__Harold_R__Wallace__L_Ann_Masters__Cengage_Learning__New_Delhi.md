### 9. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace & L.Ann Masters, Cengage Learning, New Delhi

Personality development is the process of developing and enhancing one's personality. It involves improving one's self-awareness, self-confidence, and self-esteem, as well as developing positive habits and behaviors. Personality development is important because it can help individuals achieve their goals and lead a more fulfilling life.

Harold R. Wallace and L.Ann Masters' book, published by Cengage Learning in New Delhi, provides a comprehensive guide to personality development. The book covers various aspects of personality development, including:

1. Understanding the concept of personality and its various components.
2. Identifying and overcoming negative personality traits.
3. Developing positive habits and behaviors.
4. Improving self-awareness, self-confidence, and self-esteem.
5. Setting and achieving personal goals.

This book is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their personality and lead a more fulfilling life. It provides practical advice and guidance on how to develop and enhance one's personality.
