### New Interactions by McGraw-Hill India

- New Interactions is a solution that utilizes McGraw Hill’s leading learning platform, Connect, to offer flexibility in creating the right balance of approaches for teaching style.
- It supports important skills across several areas, building not only language proficiency but also systematically developing academic skills.
- The course also focuses on developing skills essential for students to be ready for the future workforce through a focus on group work and collaboration.
- New Interactions utilizes McGraw Hill’s Connect platform to provide an adaptive experience for every user, meaning that every student receives a personalized pathway which maximizes learning, boosts confidence, and improves engagement.
- Globally, New Interactions has been one of the most recognized and respected EAP series since its initial creation almost 30 years ago. The course is built on solid pedagogy and best-practice principles, combined with ground-breaking technology provided by McGraw Hill’s award-winning Connect platform.
