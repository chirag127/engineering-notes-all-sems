### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

- Word Power Made Easy is a book written by Norman Lewis and published by W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors in 2009 in Delhi.
- The book is designed to help readers improve their vocabulary and language skills.
- It is divided into a number of chapters, each focusing on a different aspect of language learning.
- The book includes a variety of exercises and quizzes to help readers test their knowledge and reinforce their understanding of the material.
- It is a popular choice for students preparing for exams or for anyone looking to improve their language skills.
