### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

- English Grammar & Composition is a book written by Wren & Martin and published by S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to English grammar and composition.
- It covers all the essential topics of English grammar, including parts of speech, tenses, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, and more.
- The book also includes chapters on composition, including essay writing, letter writing, and précis writing.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- It is a popular choice among students and teachers alike, and is widely used as a reference book for English grammar and composition.
- The book is available in both paperback and hardcover editions.
