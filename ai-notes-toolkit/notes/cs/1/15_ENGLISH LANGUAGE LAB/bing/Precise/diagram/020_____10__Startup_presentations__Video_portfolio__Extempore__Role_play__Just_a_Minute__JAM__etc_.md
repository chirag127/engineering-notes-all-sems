### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

- **Startup presentations** are presentations given by entrepreneurs to pitch their business ideas to potential investors, customers, or partners. These presentations typically include information about the business model, target market, financial projections, and team.

- **Video portfolios** are collections of videos that showcase an individual's skills, talents, and accomplishments. They are often used by artists, designers, and other creative professionals to demonstrate their abilities to potential clients or employers.

- **Extempore** is a type of speech or presentation given without prior preparation. The speaker is given a topic and must speak on it for a set amount of time, often with little or no notes. This type of presentation is often used to test a speaker's ability to think on their feet and communicate effectively.

- **Role play** is a type of exercise in which participants act out scenarios or situations in order to practice or demonstrate skills. Role play is often used in training and education to help individuals develop communication, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.

- **Just a Minute (JAM)** is a game in which participants must speak on a given topic for one minute without hesitation, repetition, or deviation. This game is often used to improve public speaking skills and quick thinking.

These are all effective methods for individuals to showcase their skills, talents, and ideas in a professional setting. They can be used in a variety of contexts, including job interviews, business meetings, and networking events.
