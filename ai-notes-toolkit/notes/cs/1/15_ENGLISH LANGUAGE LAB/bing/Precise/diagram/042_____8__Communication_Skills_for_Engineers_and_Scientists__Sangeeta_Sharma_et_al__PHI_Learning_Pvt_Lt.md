### 8. Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists, Sangeeta Sharma et.al. PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd, 2011, New Delhi.

- "Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists" is a comprehensive book for undergraduate students of engineering and science .
- The book comprises chapters on technical report writing, communication breakdown, listening, business letters, advertising, communicating without words, and group discussions .
- The book is authored by Sangeeta Sharma and Binod Mishra .
- The book is aimed at helping engineers and scientists express themselves better, as they are often engrossed in the technicalities of the mechanized world .
- The book is available in both print and digital formats .
