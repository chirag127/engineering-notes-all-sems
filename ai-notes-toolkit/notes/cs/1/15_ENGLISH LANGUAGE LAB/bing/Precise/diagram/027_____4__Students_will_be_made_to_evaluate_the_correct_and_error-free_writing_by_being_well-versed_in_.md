### 4. Students will be made to evaluate the correct and error-free writing by being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivate relevant technical style of communication &presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- To evaluate correct and error-free writing, students must have a strong understanding of the rules of English grammar.
- This includes knowledge of sentence structure, punctuation, and spelling.
- Students must also be able to identify and correct common grammatical errors.
- In addition to grammar, students must also cultivate a relevant technical style of communication and presentation.
- This involves using appropriate language, tone, and format for the intended audience and purpose.
- Technical communication skills are essential for effective communication in the workplace and for academic uses.
- By being well-versed in the rules of English grammar and technical communication, students will be able to evaluate and produce correct and error-free writing.
