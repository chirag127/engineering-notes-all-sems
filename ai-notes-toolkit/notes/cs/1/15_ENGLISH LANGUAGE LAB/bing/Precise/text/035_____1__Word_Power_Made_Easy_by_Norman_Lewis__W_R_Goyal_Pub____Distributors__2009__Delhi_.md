### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

- Word Power Made Easy is a comprehensive vocabulary-building system created by legendary grammarian Norman Lewis.
- It provides a simple, step-by-step method to increase your knowledge and mastery of the English language.
- It is the best and quickest means to a better vocabulary in the English language.
- The book is broadly divided into three sections.
- By way of simple techniques, the author has presented easy tips for improving one's vocabulary and word skills.
- After having created awareness about the new words and their meaning, he then goes onto explain how to make their correct usage.
- It is useful for anybody who wants to improve one’s vocabulary.
- The first edition was released in the year 2009 and became popular soon after.
