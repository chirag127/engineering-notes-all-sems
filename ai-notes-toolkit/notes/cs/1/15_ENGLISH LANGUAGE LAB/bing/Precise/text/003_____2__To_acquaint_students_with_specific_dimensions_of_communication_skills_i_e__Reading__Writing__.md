### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- **Reading**: Reading is an essential communication skill that enables individuals to interpret and understand written information. It involves recognizing written symbols, comprehending their meaning, and making connections between them to construct meaning. Reading skills can be improved through regular practice, exposure to diverse texts, and the use of active reading strategies.

- **Writing**: Writing is the ability to express thoughts, ideas, and information in written form. It involves organizing and structuring information, using appropriate vocabulary and grammar, and effectively conveying the intended message. Writing skills can be improved through regular practice, feedback, and the use of writing tools such as outlines and drafts.

- **Listening**: Listening is the ability to accurately receive and interpret messages in the communication process. It involves paying attention to the speaker, understanding the message, and providing feedback. Listening skills can be improved through active listening techniques, such as maintaining eye contact, asking questions, and providing feedback.

- **Thinking**: Thinking is the ability to process information, form opinions, and make decisions. It involves analyzing information, evaluating evidence, and making logical connections. Thinking skills can be improved through critical thinking exercises, exposure to diverse perspectives, and the use of problem-solving techniques.

- **Speaking**: Speaking is the ability to convey information and express thoughts and ideas through spoken language. It involves organizing and structuring information, using appropriate vocabulary and grammar, and effectively conveying the intended message. Speaking skills can be improved through regular practice, feedback, and the use of public speaking techniques such as body language and vocal variety.

These five dimensions of communication skills are interconnected and essential for effective communication. By improving their skills in these areas, students can become more effective communicators and better equipped to navigate personal, academic, and professional situations.
