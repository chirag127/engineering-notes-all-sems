### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- Technical communication is a type of communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience for a specific purpose.
- Technical communication can take many forms, including written, visual, and oral communication.
- In the workplace, technical communication is often used to convey information about products, services, or processes to customers, colleagues, or other stakeholders.
- In academic settings, technical communication is often used to present research findings or to explain complex concepts to students or peers.
- To cultivate a relevant technical style of communication and presentation, it is important to understand the needs and expectations of your audience.
- This may involve using clear and concise language, incorporating visual aids or diagrams, and organizing information in a logical and easy-to-follow manner.
- It is also important to use appropriate tone and level of formality, depending on the context and audience.
- By developing strong technical communication skills, individuals can effectively convey complex information and ideas, and enhance their professional and academic success.
