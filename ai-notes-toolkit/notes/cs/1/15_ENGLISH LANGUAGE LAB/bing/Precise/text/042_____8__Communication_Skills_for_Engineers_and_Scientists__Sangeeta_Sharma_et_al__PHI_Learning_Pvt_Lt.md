### 8. Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists, Sangeeta Sharma et.al. PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd, 2011, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to effective communication for engineers and scientists.
- It covers various aspects of communication, including written, oral, and visual communication.
- The book provides practical tips and techniques for improving communication skills, including how to write technical reports, give presentations, and communicate with non-technical audiences.
- It also discusses the importance of effective communication in the workplace and how it can impact career success.
- The book is written by Sangeeta Sharma and other authors and was published by PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd in 2011 in New Delhi.
- It is a valuable resource for engineers and scientists looking to improve their communication skills.
