## Course Outcome:

- A course outcome is a statement that describes the knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to have acquired by the end of a course.
- Course outcomes are used to measure the effectiveness of a course in achieving its intended goals.
- Course outcomes are typically developed by the instructor and are based on the course content and objectives.
- Course outcomes should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound.
- Course outcomes should be aligned with the program outcomes and institutional goals.
- Course outcomes can be used to assess student learning and to improve the course design and delivery.
- Course outcomes can be communicated to students to help them understand the expectations and goals of the course.
- Course outcomes can be used to evaluate the effectiveness of teaching methods and to identify areas for improvement.
- Course outcomes can be used to support accreditation and program review processes.
- Course outcomes can be used to guide the development of course materials and assessments.
