### 1. To facilitate software-based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

- Software-based learning can provide students with interactive and engaging learning experiences.
- It can offer personalized learning paths, allowing students to learn at their own pace and level.
- Software-based learning can provide immediate feedback, allowing students to identify their strengths and weaknesses and work on improving their skills.
- It can offer a wide range of multimedia resources, such as videos, audio recordings, and interactive exercises, to cater to different learning styles.
- Software-based learning can provide students with the opportunity to practice their language skills in a safe and controlled environment.
- It can offer a cost-effective and flexible solution for students who may not have access to traditional language classes.
- Software-based learning can provide students with the opportunity to learn from native speakers and expose them to authentic language use.
- It can offer a convenient and flexible solution for students who may have busy schedules or live in remote areas.
