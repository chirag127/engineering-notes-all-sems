### 3. Students will apply it at their work place for writing purposes such as Presentation/official drafting/administrative communication and use it for document/project/report/research paper writing.

- Markdown is a lightweight markup language that is easy to learn and use.
- It is widely used for formatting text in various applications, including presentations, official drafting, administrative communication, and document/project/report/research paper writing.
- Markdown allows users to format text using simple syntax, making it easy to create professional-looking documents without the need for specialized software or knowledge.
- By learning and using Markdown, students can improve their writing skills and create well-formatted documents for various purposes at their workplace.
- Markdown is particularly useful for creating documents that need to be shared or collaborated on, as it is platform-independent and can be easily converted to other formats such as HTML or PDF.
- Overall, the use of Markdown can enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of written communication in the workplace.
