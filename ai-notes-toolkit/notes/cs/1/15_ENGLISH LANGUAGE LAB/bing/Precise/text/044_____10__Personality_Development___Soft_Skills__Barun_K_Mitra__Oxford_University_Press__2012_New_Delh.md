### 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi.

- Personality Development & Soft Skills is a book written by Barun K. Mitra and published by Oxford University Press in 2012 in New Delhi.
- The book focuses on the development of an individual's personality and the acquisition of soft skills.
- Soft skills refer to a set of interpersonal and communication skills that are essential for success in the workplace and in personal relationships.
- The book provides practical tips and advice on how to improve one's personality and develop soft skills.
- Some of the topics covered in the book include effective communication, time management, leadership, and teamwork.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner and is suitable for individuals of all ages and backgrounds.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their personal and professional development.
