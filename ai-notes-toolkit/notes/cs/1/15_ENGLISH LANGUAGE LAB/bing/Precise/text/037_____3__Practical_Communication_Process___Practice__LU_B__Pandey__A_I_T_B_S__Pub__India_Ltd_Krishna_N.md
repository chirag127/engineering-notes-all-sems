### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013

- "Practical Communication: Process and Practice" is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in Krishan Nagar, Delhi in 2013 .
- The book is a guide to effective communication and is intended for students and professionals who want to improve their communication skills.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, including the process of communication, the role of language in communication, and the importance of listening and feedback in effective communication.
- The book also provides practical tips and exercises to help readers improve their communication skills.
