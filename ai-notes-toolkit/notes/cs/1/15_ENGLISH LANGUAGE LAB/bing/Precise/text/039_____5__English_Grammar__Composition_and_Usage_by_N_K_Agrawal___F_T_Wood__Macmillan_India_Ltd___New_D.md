### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to English grammar, composition, and usage.
- It is authored by N.K.Agrawal and F.T.Wood and published by Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.
- The book covers various aspects of the English language, including grammar rules, sentence structure, and vocabulary.
- It also includes sections on composition, including tips on how to write effectively and clearly.
- The book is designed to be accessible to learners of all levels and can be used as a reference or study guide.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
