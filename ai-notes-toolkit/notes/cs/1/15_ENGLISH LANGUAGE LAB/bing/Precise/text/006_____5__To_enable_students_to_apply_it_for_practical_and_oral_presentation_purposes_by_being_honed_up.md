### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- Presentation skills and voice-dynamics are essential for students to effectively communicate their ideas and knowledge in practical and oral presentations.
- By honing their presentation skills, students can learn to organize their thoughts, use visual aids effectively, and engage their audience.
- Voice-dynamics, including tone, pitch, and volume, can help students convey their message with clarity and confidence.
- Practice and feedback are important for students to improve their presentation skills and voice-dynamics.
- By mastering these skills, students can apply them in practical and oral presentations, as well as in other areas of their academic and professional lives.
