### 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a book by Ken Taylor, published by Orient Blackswan in 2011, that aims to help learners improve their communication skills in various business contexts.
- The book is divided into 50 units, each focusing on a specific aspect of business English, such as meetings, negotiations, presentations, emails, reports, etc.
- Each unit consists of an introduction, a main text, a vocabulary section, a grammar section, a practice section, and a summary.
- The book covers topics such as business etiquette, cultural differences, formal and informal language, idioms, phrasal verbs, and common errors.
- The book also provides tips and advice on how to use appropriate tone, style, and register in different situations, as well as how to avoid misunderstandings and miscommunication.
- The book is suitable for intermediate to advanced learners of English who want to improve their business English skills for professional or academic purposes.
- The book can be used as a self-study material or as a supplementary resource for classroom teaching.
