### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

- Software based learning is a form of education that uses computer programs, applications, or online platforms to deliver instruction, assessment, feedback, and support to students.
- Software based learning can provide the required English Language proficiency to students by:
  - Offering personalized and adaptive learning paths that match the students' needs, goals, and preferences.
  - Providing interactive and engaging activities that stimulate the students' interest, motivation, and participation.
  - Incorporating multimedia elements such as audio, video, images, and animations that enhance the students' exposure, comprehension, and retention of the language.
  - Enabling instant and automated feedback that guides the students' progress and identifies their strengths and weaknesses.
  - Facilitating self-paced and flexible learning that allows the students to learn at their own time, place, and pace.
  - Supporting collaborative and social learning that fosters the students' communication, cooperation, and interaction with peers and instructors.
  - Integrating gamification and rewards that motivate the students to complete tasks, achieve goals, and earn badges and points.
  - Providing access to a variety of resources and materials that cover different aspects and levels of the language.
  - Tracking and reporting the students' performance and outcomes that measure their improvement and proficiency.
