### 9. Comprehension Skills based on Reading and Listening Practical’s on a model Audio

- Comprehension skills are the ability to understand and interpret the meaning of texts and spoken language.
- Reading and listening are two of the most common and important ways of acquiring comprehension skills.
- Reading and listening practicals are exercises that involve reading or listening to a model audio and answering questions based on the content, structure, and purpose of the text or speech.
- A model audio is a recorded speech or dialogue that is used as an example or reference for learning comprehension skills.
- The following are some steps and tips for doing reading and listening practicals on a model audio:

  - Before listening or reading, activate your prior knowledge and make predictions about the topic, genre, and purpose of the model audio.
  - While listening or reading, pay attention to the main idea, supporting details, and key words of the model audio. Use strategies such as summarizing, paraphrasing, and questioning to check your understanding and clarify any doubts.
  - After listening or reading, review and evaluate your comprehension by answering the questions given. Use evidence from the model audio to support your answers. Compare your answers with the answer key or a peer and identify any gaps or errors in your comprehension.
  - Reflect on your performance and identify your strengths and weaknesses in comprehension skills. Set goals and plan for improvement.
