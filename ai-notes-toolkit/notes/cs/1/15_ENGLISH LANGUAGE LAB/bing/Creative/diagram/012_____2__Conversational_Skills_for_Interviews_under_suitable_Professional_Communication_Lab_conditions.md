### 2. Conversational Skills for Interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics

- Conversational skills are the ability to communicate effectively and appropriately with others in a variety of situations, such as interviews.
- Interviews are formal interactions between an interviewer and an interviewee, where the interviewer evaluates the interviewee's suitability for a job, a course, a scholarship, or any other opportunity.
- Professional communication lab conditions are the settings and equipment that simulate a real interview environment, such as a quiet room, a microphone, a camera, a computer, and a projector.
- Kinesics is the study of body language, such as gestures, facial expressions, eye contact, posture, and movement, that convey meaning and emotion in communication.
- Conversational skills for interviews under suitable professional communication lab conditions with emphasis on kinesics involve the following aspects:

  - Preparing for the interview: researching the organization, the role, and the interviewer, reviewing your resume and portfolio, anticipating possible questions and answers, practicing your speech and body language, dressing appropriately, and arriving on time.
  - Starting the interview: greeting the interviewer with a smile and a handshake, introducing yourself, making eye contact, and expressing your interest and enthusiasm for the opportunity.
  - Answering the questions: listening attentively, clarifying the question if needed, giving clear, concise, and relevant responses, providing examples and evidence, avoiding filler words and negative statements, and using appropriate gestures and facial expressions to support your message.
  - Asking the questions: preparing some questions in advance, showing your curiosity and knowledge about the organization and the role, avoiding personal or inappropriate questions, and using polite and respectful language.
  - Ending the interview: thanking the interviewer for their time and consideration, reiterating your interest and suitability for the opportunity, asking about the next steps, and following up with a thank-you email or note.
