#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills. Interactive and Communicative Practical with emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication, based on International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.)

- Language is a vital skill for employability in the globalized world. It enables people to communicate effectively, collaborate with others, and express their ideas and opinions.
- To master a language, students need to practice it with a variety of activities and exercises that are relevant, engaging, and challenging. These activities and exercises should be based on employability skills, such as teamwork, problem-solving, critical thinking, creativity, and intercultural awareness.
- Interactive and communicative practicals are one of the best ways to practice the language with employability skills. These practicals involve students in real-life situations, tasks, and projects that require them to use the language for a specific purpose and audience. They also encourage students to interact and communicate with each other, as well as with native speakers or experts, if possible.
- Oral presentation and spoken communication are essential components of interactive and communicative practicals. They allow students to demonstrate their language proficiency, knowledge, and skills in a clear and confident manner. They also help students to develop their pronunciation, intonation, stress, and rhythm, which are important for effective communication.
- To improve their oral presentation and spoken communication, students should learn and practice the International Phonetic Alphabet (I.P.A.). The I.P.A. is a system of symbols that represent the sounds of any language in the world. It helps students to recognize, produce, and transcribe the sounds of the language accurately and consistently. It also helps students to avoid confusion and misunderstanding caused by spelling variations, dialect differences, and accent variations.
- Here is an example of an interactive and communicative practical with emphasis on oral presentation and spoken communication, based on I.P.A.:

  - Topic: Introduce yourself and your career goals to a potential employer.
  - Objective: To practice self-introduction, resume writing, and interview skills in the language.
  - Activity: Students work in pairs. One student plays the role of a job seeker, and the other plays the role of an employer. The job seeker prepares a resume and a self-introduction in the language, using the I.P.A. symbols to indicate the pronunciation of their name and other key words. The employer prepares some questions to ask the job seeker in the interview. The pair then conducts a mock interview, using the language and the I.P.A. symbols. They record their interview and transcribe it using the I.P.A. symbols. They then exchange feedback on their performance, focusing on their language use, pronunciation, and employability skills.
