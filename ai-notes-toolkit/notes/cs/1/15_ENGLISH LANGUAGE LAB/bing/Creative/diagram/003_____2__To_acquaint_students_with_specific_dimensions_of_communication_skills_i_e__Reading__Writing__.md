### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- Communication skills are essential for academic and professional success, as well as for personal and social development.
- Communication skills can be classified into five main dimensions: reading, writing, listening, thinking and speaking.
- Reading is the ability to comprehend and interpret written texts of various genres, purposes and levels of difficulty.
- Writing is the ability to produce clear, coherent and accurate texts for different audiences, contexts and purposes.
- Listening is the ability to understand and respond to spoken messages of various types, modes and situations.
- Thinking is the ability to analyze, evaluate, synthesize and apply information and ideas from various sources and perspectives.
- Speaking is the ability to express oneself effectively and appropriately in oral communication, using suitable language, tone and style.

- Each dimension of communication skills has its own subskills, strategies and techniques that can be learned and practiced.
- Developing communication skills requires a holistic and integrated approach, involving cognitive, affective and behavioral aspects.
- Communication skills can be enhanced by engaging in various activities, such as reading widely, writing regularly, listening actively, thinking critically and creatively, and speaking confidently and respectfully.
