### 8. Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists, Sangeeta Sharma et.al. PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd, 2011, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide for undergraduate students of engineering and science to improve their technical communication skills.
- The book covers various aspects of technical communication, such as technical report writing, communication breakdown, listening, business letters, advertising, communicating without words, and group discussions.
- The book also provides tips and examples for effective oral and written communication, presentation skills, resume writing, and interview skills.
- The book aims to help students develop their communication skills for academic and professional purposes, and to enhance their confidence and competence in communication.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part A focuses on the principles and practice of technical communication, and Part B provides exercises and activities for practice and assessment.
