### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

- This book is divided into four parts: Part I - Grammar, Part II - Composition, Part III - Usage, and Part IV - Appendices.
- Part I - Grammar covers the basic rules and principles of English grammar, such as parts of speech, sentence structure, verb forms, tenses, voice, mood, clauses, phrases, punctuation, and spelling.
- Part II - Composition deals with the skills and techniques of writing different types of compositions, such as essays, letters, reports, summaries, précis, and paragraphs. It also provides guidance on how to plan, organize, draft, revise, and edit one's writing.
- Part III - Usage focuses on the correct and effective use of English in various contexts and situations, such as formal and informal communication, academic and professional writing, and spoken and written expression. It also covers common errors, idioms, synonyms, antonyms, homonyms, and vocabulary building.
- Part IV - Appendices contains additional information and resources for reference and practice, such as model compositions, sample letters, word lists, grammar exercises, and answers to the exercises.

- This book is suitable for students, teachers, and learners of English who want to improve their grammar, composition, and usage skills. It is also useful for preparing for various examinations and tests that require a good command of English.
