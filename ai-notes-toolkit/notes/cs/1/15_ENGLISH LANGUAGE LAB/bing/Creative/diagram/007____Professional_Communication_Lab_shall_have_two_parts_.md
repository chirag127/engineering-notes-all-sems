## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

- Part A: This part consists of oral communication skills and focuses on the following aspects:

  - Phonetics: It covers the basics of speech sounds, such as vowels, consonants, stress, intonation, and pronunciation.
  - Listening Skills: It covers the techniques of active listening, such as attending, comprehending, retaining, and responding.
  - Speaking Skills: It covers the skills of effective speaking, such as clarity, fluency, coherence, accuracy, and appropriateness.
  - Presentation Skills: It covers the skills of preparing and delivering oral presentations, such as planning, organizing, rehearsing, and using visual aids.
  - Group Discussion: It covers the skills of participating and leading group discussions, such as initiating, contributing, summarizing, and moderating.
  - Interview Skills: It covers the skills of facing and conducting interviews, such as preparing, answering, asking, and following up.

- Part B: This part consists of written communication skills and focuses on the following aspects:

  - Grammar: It covers the rules and conventions of standard English grammar, such as parts of speech, tenses, voice, and sentence structure.
  - Vocabulary: It covers the strategies of building and using a rich and varied vocabulary, such as word formation, collocations, synonyms, and antonyms.
  - Reading Skills: It covers the skills of comprehending and analyzing different types of texts, such as factual, inferential, evaluative, and critical.
  - Writing Skills: It covers the skills of producing different types of texts, such as descriptive, narrative, expository, and argumentative.
  - Business Communication: It covers the skills of writing formal and professional documents, such as letters, emails, memos, reports, and proposals.
  - Technical Communication: It covers the skills of writing technical and scientific documents, such as manuals, instructions, specifications, and research papers.
