- This topic is about how students can use their knowledge and skills to deliver effective and engaging presentations in various contexts and situations.
- Presentation skills are the abilities to plan, prepare, deliver, and evaluate a presentation using appropriate tools, techniques, and strategies.
- Voice-dynamics are the aspects of vocal expression that affect the quality, tone, and impact of the speaker's message, such as volume, pitch, pace, intonation, and emphasis.
- To apply this topic for practical and oral presentation purposes, students need to:

  - Use appropriate visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, images, or videos, to enhance their message and support their arguments.
