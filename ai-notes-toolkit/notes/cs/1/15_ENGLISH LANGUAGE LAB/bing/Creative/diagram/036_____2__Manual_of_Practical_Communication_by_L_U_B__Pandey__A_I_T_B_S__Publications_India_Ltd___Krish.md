### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- This is a book that covers various aspects of communication skills, such as listening, speaking, reading, writing, and non-verbal communication.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I deals with the basics of communication, such as the process, types, barriers, and models of communication. Part II focuses on the oral communication skills, such as pronunciation, conversation, presentation, and group discussion. Part III covers the written communication skills, such as grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, and style. Part IV discusses the non-verbal communication skills, such as body language, gestures, and facial expressions.
- The book is designed for students and professionals who want to improve their communication skills in various situations and contexts. The book provides practical examples, exercises, and tips to help the readers apply the concepts and techniques of effective communication.
- The book is written by L.U.B. Pandey, a professor of English and communication skills at the Institute of Engineering and Technology, Lucknow, India. He has authored several books and articles on communication skills, technical writing, and English language teaching. He is also a member of the Board of Studies of Technical Communication at the Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow, India .
- The book is published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd., a leading publisher of books on engineering, management, medical, and general subjects. The publisher is based in Krishan Nagar, Delhi, India .
