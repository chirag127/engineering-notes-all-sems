## LIST OF PRACTICALS

- A practical is a hands-on activity that allows students to apply and demonstrate their theoretical knowledge and skills in a specific subject or field.
- Practicals can take various forms, such as experiments, simulations, fieldwork, projects, or case studies.
- Practicals can have different purposes, such as developing practical skills, testing hypotheses, collecting data, solving problems, or creating products.
- Practicals can be assessed in different ways, such as by observation, report, presentation, portfolio, or reflection.
- Practicals can have different benefits, such as enhancing student engagement, motivation, understanding, creativity, and collaboration.
- Practicals can also have some challenges, such as requiring adequate resources, time, supervision, guidance, and feedback.
- Practicals can be designed and implemented following some general principles, such as aligning with the learning outcomes, providing clear instructions and expectations, scaffolding the learning process, ensuring safety and ethical standards, and evaluating the effectiveness and impact.
