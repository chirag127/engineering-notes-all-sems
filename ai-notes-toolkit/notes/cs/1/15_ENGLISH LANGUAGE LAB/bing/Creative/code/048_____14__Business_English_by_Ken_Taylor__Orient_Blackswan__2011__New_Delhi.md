# 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a book by Ken Taylor that aims to help learners improve their communication skills in various business contexts.
- The book is divided into 50 units, each focusing on a specific topic or skill, such as meetings, negotiations, presentations, emails, reports, etc.
- Each unit consists of four sections: introduction, language focus, practice, and review.
- The introduction provides the background and objectives of the unit, as well as some useful tips and advice.
- The language focus presents the key vocabulary, grammar, and expressions related to the topic or skill, with examples and explanations.
- The practice section offers a variety of exercises and activities to help learners apply and consolidate what they have learned.
- The review section summarizes the main points and provides further practice and feedback.
- The book also includes a glossary, an answer key, and a CD-ROM with audio recordings of the dialogues and exercises.
- The book is suitable for intermediate to advanced learners of English who want to improve their business communication skills in a practical and effective way.
- The book is published by Orient Blackswan, a leading publisher of academic and educational books in India.
