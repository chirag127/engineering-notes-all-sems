### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation is a type of speech that aims to convey a central message or idea to the audience, usually in a persuasive or inspirational manner.
- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation should have a clear and specific purpose, a well-defined audience, a coherent structure, a compelling introduction, a convincing body, and a memorable conclusion.
- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation should also follow the correct methodologies of argumentation, which are the principles and techniques of reasoning and communicating effectively to support a claim or a position.
- Some of the correct methodologies of argumentation are:

  - Using logical appeals, such as facts, statistics, examples, analogies, and syllogisms, to establish the validity and soundness of the arguments.
  - Using ethical appeals, such as credibility, authority, honesty, and values, to establish the trustworthiness and character of the speaker and the arguments.
  - Using emotional appeals, such as stories, anecdotes, metaphors, and imagery, to elicit the feelings and emotions of the audience and to connect with them on a personal level.
  - Using rhetorical devices, such as repetition, parallelism, contrast, and rhetorical questions, to enhance the style and impact of the speech.
  - Using counterarguments, such as acknowledging, refuting, or conceding, to address the opposing views and to strengthen the speaker's position.
  - Using fallacies, such as ad hominem, straw man, false dilemma, and slippery slope, to avoid or expose the errors and flaws in the reasoning and the arguments.

- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation should also consider the following aspects:

  - The context and occasion of the speech, such as the time, place, setting, and expectations of the audience and the speaker.
  - The tone and mood of the speech, such as the level of formality, the attitude, and the emotion that the speaker wants to convey and evoke.
  - The delivery and presentation of the speech, such as the voice, gestures, eye contact, and visual aids that the speaker uses to enhance the message and the engagement of the audience.

- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation is an effective and powerful way of communicating a central message or idea to the audience, if it follows the correct methodologies of argumentation and considers the various aspects of the speech.
