### Oxford Achiever by Oxford University Press

- Oxford Achiever is a learning platform developed by Oxford University Press (China) Ltd., covering key subjects: English, Chinese and Mathematics.
- It is a comprehensive app-based learning and assessment system that aims to build students’ language skills and subject knowledge along an individualized, self-paced learning path.
- It is a total e-learning solution for effective app/computer-based teaching and learning and is characterized by:
  - Diagnostic Placement Test: Place students at the right starting level to learn according to their own aptitude/ability in each subject area .
  - Learning Path: Provide students with a personalized learning path based on their performance in the Diagnostic Placement Test .
  - Learning Zone: Offer students a variety of interactive exercises and activities to reinforce and consolidate their learning of key concepts and skills .
  - Assessment Zone: Assess students’ progress and mastery of the learning objectives through periodic tests and quizzes .
  - Report Zone: Track students’ performance and learning outcomes through detailed reports and analytics .
  - Teacher Zone: Enable teachers to monitor students’ progress, assign tasks, provide feedback and customize the learning content.
- It has over 45,000 questions, diverse learning aspects and varied question types; which contribute to a flexible learning approach.
- It is compatible with Android devices and can be downloaded from the Google Play Store or the Oxford University Press website  .
