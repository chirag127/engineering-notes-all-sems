### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

This book is a comprehensive guide to the process and practice of effective communication in various contexts and situations. It covers the following topics:

- The nature, scope, and importance of communication
- The communication process and its elements
- The barriers to communication and how to overcome them
- The types and modes of communication
- The principles and skills of communication
- The communication strategies and styles
- The communication ethics and etiquette
- The communication in different settings, such as interpersonal, group, organizational, public, mass, and cross-cultural
- The communication tools and technologies, such as letters, memos, reports, proposals, presentations, emails, blogs, social media, etc.
- The communication challenges and opportunities in the 21st century

The book is designed to help the readers develop their communication competence and confidence in various domains of personal, professional, and social life. It provides theoretical concepts, practical examples, exercises, activities, case studies, and self-assessment tests to enhance the learning outcomes. It also includes sample documents, formats, templates, and checklists to help the readers apply the communication principles and skills in real-life situations.
