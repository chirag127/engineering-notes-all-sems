## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

- Part A: This part consists of oral communication skills and focuses on the following aspects:

  - Phonetics: The study of speech sounds, their production, transcription and classification.
  - Pronunciation: The correct and standard way of speaking a language, following the rules of stress, intonation and rhythm.
  - Accent: The distinctive way of pronouncing a language, influenced by the speaker's native language, region, education and social class.
  - Fluency: The ability to speak smoothly and confidently, without hesitation, repetition or pauses.
  - Vocabulary: The range and variety of words and expressions that a speaker can use effectively and appropriately in different contexts and situations.
  - Grammar: The system and structure of a language, including the rules of word formation, sentence construction and punctuation.
  - Listening: The skill of understanding and interpreting spoken language, paying attention to the speaker's tone, mood, purpose and intention.
  - Speaking: The skill of expressing oneself clearly and coherently in spoken language, using appropriate vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation and fluency.
  - Presentation: The skill of delivering a formal or informal speech or talk on a given topic, using suitable visual aids, body language and voice modulation.
  - Group Discussion: The skill of participating in a collaborative exchange of ideas and opinions on a given topic, following the norms of turn-taking, politeness and relevance.
  - Interview: The skill of facing a formal or informal conversation with one or more interviewers, answering their questions and asking relevant questions, demonstrating one's suitability for a job, course or position.

- Part B: This part consists of written communication skills and focuses on the following aspects:

  - Reading: The skill of comprehending and analyzing written texts, identifying the main idea, supporting details, tone, purpose and intention of the writer.
  - Writing: The skill of producing clear and coherent written texts, using appropriate vocabulary, grammar, punctuation and style.
  - Letter Writing: The skill of composing formal or informal letters for different purposes and audiences, following the conventions of format, layout, salutation and closure.
  - Email Writing: The skill of composing formal or informal emails for different purposes and audiences, following the conventions of format, subject line, greeting and signature.
  - Report Writing: The skill of writing a factual and objective account of an event, situation, problem or issue, following the structure of introduction, body and conclusion.
  - Proposal Writing: The skill of writing a persuasive and detailed plan of action for a project, task or idea, following the structure of introduction, background, objectives, methodology, expected outcomes, budget and timeline.
  - Resume Writing: The skill of writing a brief and concise summary of one's personal, educational and professional qualifications and achievements, following the format of contact information, objective, education, work experience, skills and references.
  - Technical Writing: The skill of writing specialized and technical documents for a specific audience and purpose, such as manuals, instructions, specifications, proposals, reports, etc.
