### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

- Individual speech delivery is the process of presenting a speech to an audience, either in person or online, using verbal and nonverbal communication skills.
- Conferencing is the process of engaging in a dialogue with the audience, either during or after the speech, to clarify, elaborate, or respond to questions or comments.
- Interjections are interruptions or comments from the audience that may challenge, contradict, or support the speaker's message.
- Quizzes are questions or tests from the audience that may assess the speaker's knowledge, understanding, or credibility on the topic.
- Skills to defend interjections/quizzes are strategies or techniques that the speaker can use to address or counter the audience's interjections or quizzes in a respectful, confident, and effective way.

Some of the skills to defend interjections/quizzes are:

- Acknowledge the interjection/quiz and thank the audience for their interest or participation.
- Repeat or restate the interjection/quiz to ensure that you and the audience understand it correctly.
- Answer the interjection/quiz directly and concisely, using facts, evidence, or examples to support your point.
- If you do not know the answer, admit it honestly and promise to follow up later or refer the audience to a credible source.
- If the interjection/quiz is irrelevant, off-topic, or inappropriate, politely redirect the audience to the main focus or purpose of your speech.
- If the interjection/quiz is hostile, aggressive, or disrespectful, remain calm and professional, and avoid personal attacks or emotional reactions.
- If the interjection/quiz is positive, constructive, or complimentary, express your appreciation and use it as an opportunity to reinforce or expand on your message.
- Transition back to your speech and resume your delivery, using a signpost, a summary, or a preview to guide the audience.
