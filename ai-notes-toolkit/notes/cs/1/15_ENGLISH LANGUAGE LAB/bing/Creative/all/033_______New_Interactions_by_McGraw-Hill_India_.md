# New Interactions by McGraw-Hill India

- New Interactions is a series of textbooks for English language learners from elementary to advanced levels.
- The series aims to develop the four skills of listening, speaking, reading and writing, as well as grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation.
- The series follows a communicative approach that encourages learners to interact with authentic texts and tasks in meaningful contexts.
- The series consists of six levels: New Interactions 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6. Each level has a student book, a workbook, a teacher's edition, an audio CD and a DVD.
- The series covers a variety of topics and themes that are relevant and interesting to learners, such as culture, technology, health, travel, education and careers.
- The series provides learners with opportunities to practice and apply their language skills in different modes and formats, such as dialogues, interviews, articles, reports, emails, blogs and podcasts.
- The series also offers learners with strategies and tips to improve their language learning and performance, such as setting goals, self-assessing, reflecting, summarizing and reviewing.
- The series is aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and the Global Scale of English (GSE) standards, which are widely recognized and accepted by educational institutions and employers around the world.
