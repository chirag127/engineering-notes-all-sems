# 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

- Individual speech delivery is the process of presenting a speech to an audience, either in person or online, without the assistance of a co-speaker or a moderator.
- Conferencing is the process of engaging in a dialogue with an audience, either in person or online, with the assistance of a co-speaker or a moderator, who may ask questions, provide feedback, or facilitate discussion.
- Skills to defend interjections/quizzes are the abilities to respond effectively to interruptions, challenges, or inquiries from the audience or the moderator during a speech or a conference.
- Some of the skills to defend interjections/quizzes are:

  - Anticipating possible interjections/quizzes and preparing responses in advance.
  - Listening attentively and respectfully to the interjections/quizzes and acknowledging them.
  - Clarifying the purpose and scope of the interjections/quizzes and asking for more information if needed.
  - Providing relevant, accurate, and concise answers that address the interjections/quizzes and support the main points of the speech or the conference.
  - Referring to credible sources or evidence to back up the answers and citing them properly.
  - Using appropriate language, tone, and nonverbal cues to convey confidence, respect, and professionalism.
  - Inviting further questions or comments from the audience or the moderator and thanking them for their participation.
