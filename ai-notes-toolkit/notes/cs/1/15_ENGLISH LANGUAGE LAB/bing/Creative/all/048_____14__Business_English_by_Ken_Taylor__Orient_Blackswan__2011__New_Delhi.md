# 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a self-study book for learners of English who want to improve their communication skills in business contexts.
- The book contains 50 units, each focusing on a specific aspect of business English, such as meetings, presentations, negotiations, emails, reports, etc.
- Each unit consists of a brief introduction, a main text, a set of exercises, and a key to the exercises.
- The main text provides authentic examples of business English in use, with explanations of key vocabulary, grammar, and expressions.
- The exercises help the learners to practice and consolidate their understanding of the main text, as well as to develop their own skills in business English.
- The book also includes a glossary of business terms, a list of useful phrases, and an index of topics and grammar points.
- The book is suitable for intermediate to advanced learners of English who want to improve their business English for professional or academic purposes.
- The book can be used either as a self-study material or as a supplementary resource for classroom teaching.
- The book is written by Ken Taylor, who is a trainer and consultant in business communication and intercultural skills.
- The book is published by Orient Blackswan, a leading publisher of academic and educational books in India.
