## Course Outcome:

- A course outcome is a statement that describes what students should be able to do or demonstrate by the end of a course.
- Course outcomes are usually derived from the course objectives, which are the broad goals or purposes of the course.
- Course outcomes are more specific, measurable, and observable than course objectives. They should align with the course content, activities, and assessments.
- Course outcomes should use action verbs that indicate the level of cognitive, affective, or psychomotor skills that students are expected to achieve.
- Course outcomes should be clear, concise, and consistent. They should avoid vague or ambiguous terms, such as understand, appreciate, or learn.
- Course outcomes should be realistic and attainable within the scope and duration of the course. They should also be challenging and meaningful for students.
- Course outcomes should be communicated to students at the beginning of the course and throughout the course. They should also be used to evaluate student learning and provide feedback.
- Course outcomes should be reviewed and revised periodically to ensure their relevance, accuracy, and alignment with the course objectives, content, activities, and assessments.
