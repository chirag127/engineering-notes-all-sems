# Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists

- Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists is a book written by Sangeeta Sharma, Binod Mishra and Savita Mishra, and published by PHI Learning Pvt. Ltd. in 2011.
- The book aims to provide comprehensive and student-friendly guidance on various aspects of technical communication that students of engineering and science should be familiar with.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part A covers the planning, designing and drafting of documents for a broad range of situations and applications, such as technical reports, business letters, resumes, proposals, and presentations. Part B covers the communication breakdown, listening, advertising, communicating without words, and group discussions, which are essential skills for effective communication in the professional world.
- The book also includes numerous examples, exercises, case studies, and illustrations to help the students apply the concepts and techniques of technical communication in various contexts.
- The book is based on the following principles and objectives :
  - To help the students develop their communication skills in both oral and written modes.
  - To enable the students to communicate effectively with different audiences, such as peers, superiors, clients, and the general public.
  - To equip the students with the knowledge and skills of various forms and formats of technical communication, such as reports, letters, proposals, and presentations.
  - To enhance the students' awareness and understanding of the ethical, social, and cultural aspects of communication.
  - To foster the students' creativity and critical thinking skills in communication.
  - To prepare the students for the challenges and opportunities of the globalized and competitive professional environment.
