# Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi

- This book is a guide to equip readers with all the necessary skills required to be an effective communicator in the increasingly competitive professional space .
- The book covers various topics such as personality development, self-awareness, self-motivation, emotional intelligence, interpersonal skills, communication skills, presentation skills, group discussion skills, interview skills, resume writing, and business etiquette .
- The book also provides tips and techniques to enhance one's confidence, creativity, leadership, and decision-making abilities .
- The book is designed to serve as a textbook that addresses the requirements of students of any discipline who are on the threshold of starting their careers .
- The book would prove equally beneficial to young managers who have already had a taste of corporate life .
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I: Personality Development, Part II: Communication Skills, Part III: Soft Skills, and Part IV: Career Skills .
- The book is written in a simple and lucid style, with examples, illustrations, exercises, and case studies to make the learning process interactive and engaging .
- The book also includes a glossary of key terms, a bibliography of useful references, and an index for easy access to the topics .
