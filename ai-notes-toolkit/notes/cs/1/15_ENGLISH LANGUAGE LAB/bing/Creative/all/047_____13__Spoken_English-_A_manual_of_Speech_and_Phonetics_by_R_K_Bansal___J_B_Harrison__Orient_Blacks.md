# Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to the pronunciation and intonation of spoken English for learners and teachers.
- It covers the basic concepts and principles of phonetics, phonology, and prosody, as well as the practical aspects of speech production, perception, and transcription.
- It also provides exercises and activities to help learners improve their spoken English skills and develop their awareness of the sounds and patterns of the language.
- The book is divided into four parts:

  - Part I: Introduction to Speech and Phonetics
    - This part introduces the basic terminology and concepts of speech and phonetics, such as the organs of speech, the classification of sounds, the phonetic alphabet, and the features of vowels and consonants.
  - Part II: The Sounds of English
    - This part describes and illustrates the sounds of English, including the phonetic symbols, the articulation, the distribution, and the spelling of each sound. It also explains the concepts of stress, rhythm, and intonation, and how they affect the pronunciation of words and sentences.
  - Part III: Transcription and Analysis
    - This part provides guidelines and examples for transcribing and analyzing spoken English, using the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA). It also introduces the concepts of phonemic and allophonic transcription, and the notation of stress, rhythm, and intonation.
  - Part IV: Exercises and Activities
    - This part contains a variety of exercises and activities to help learners practice and improve their spoken English skills, such as discrimination, identification, production, and imitation of sounds, words, and sentences. It also includes some diagnostic tests and remedial exercises for common pronunciation problems.

- The book is suitable for learners and teachers of English at various levels, from intermediate to advanced. It can be used as a self-study material or as a supplementary text for classroom instruction. It can also serve as a reference book for researchers and professionals who are interested in the study of spoken English.
