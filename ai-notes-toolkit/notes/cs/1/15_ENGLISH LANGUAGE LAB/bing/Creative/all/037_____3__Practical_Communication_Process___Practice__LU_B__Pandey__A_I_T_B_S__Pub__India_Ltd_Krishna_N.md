# 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to the process and practice of effective communication in technical and professional contexts.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, such as:
  - The nature and scope of communication, its types, models, barriers, and principles.
  - The skills and strategies of listening, speaking, reading, writing, and non-verbal communication.
  - The formats and styles of various types of technical documents, such as letters, memos, reports, proposals, manuals, etc.
  - The methods and techniques of oral presentation, group discussion, interview, and negotiation.
  - The use of visual aids, graphics, and multimedia in communication.
  - The ethical and legal issues in communication, such as plagiarism, copyright, and confidentiality.
- The book also provides practical exercises, examples, and case studies to illustrate the concepts and applications of communication.
- The book is intended for students and professionals of engineering, management, and other technical fields who need to communicate effectively in their academic and professional settings.
