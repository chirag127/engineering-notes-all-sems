# 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

- This is a comprehensive book on English grammar and composition that covers the basics of grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, and writing skills.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part I deals with the essentials of grammar, such as parts of speech, phrases, clauses, sentences, and tenses. Part II covers the aspects of composition, such as narration, description, argument, letter writing, precis writing, and essay writing.
- The book also provides exercises and tests for practice and revision, along with answers and explanations for reference.
- The book is suitable for students of various levels, from high school to college, as well as for competitive exams and general readers who want to improve their English language skills.
- The book is written in a clear and simple style, with examples and illustrations to make the concepts easy to understand and apply.
- The book is one of the most popular and widely used books on English grammar and composition in India and abroad, and has been revised and updated regularly to keep pace with the changing trends and needs of the learners.
