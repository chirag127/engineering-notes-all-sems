### 4. English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi.

This book is a comprehensive guide to the rules and principles of English grammar and usage. It covers the following topics:

- The parts of speech and their functions in sentences
- The types and structures of sentences and clauses
- The formation and use of nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections
- The rules and exceptions of verb tenses, aspects, moods, and voices
- The agreement of subjects and verbs, and of pronouns and antecedents
- The use of articles, determiners, quantifiers, and modifiers
- The punctuation and capitalization of sentences and phrases
- The common errors and pitfalls of grammar and usage
- The stylistic and rhetorical aspects of grammar and usage
- The differences and similarities between British and American English

The book is divided into 20 chapters, each with a clear introduction, examples, exercises, and summaries. The book also includes appendices on irregular verbs, spelling rules, abbreviations, and symbols. The book is suitable for students, teachers, and learners of English as a second or foreign language. It is also a useful reference for writers, editors, and speakers of English.
