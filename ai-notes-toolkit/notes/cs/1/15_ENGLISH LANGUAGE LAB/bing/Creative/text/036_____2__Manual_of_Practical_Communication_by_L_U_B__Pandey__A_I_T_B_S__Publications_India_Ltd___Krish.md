### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- This is a book that aims to help students develop their communication skills in various contexts and situations.
- The book covers topics such as communication process, communication barriers, listening skills, non-verbal communication, oral communication, written communication, technical communication, business communication, and intercultural communication.
- The book provides theoretical concepts, practical examples, exercises, and activities to enhance the learning outcomes of the students.
- The book also includes a glossary of key terms, a bibliography of relevant sources, and an index for easy reference.
- The book is suitable for undergraduate and postgraduate students of engineering, management, and other disciplines, as well as for professionals who want to improve their communication skills.
