### 6. Effective Communication Skill, Kulbhusan Kumar, RS Salaria, Khanna Publishing House

- Effective Communication Skill is a book written by Kulbhusan Kumar and RS Salaria, and published by Khanna Publishing House in 2018    .
- The book aims to provide the readers with the essential skills and techniques to communicate effectively in various contexts, such as academic, professional, social and personal   .
- The book covers the following topics:

  - Introduction to Communication: Definition, types, models, barriers and principles of communication  .
  - Verbal Communication: Elements, features, functions and strategies of verbal communication  .
  - Non-Verbal Communication: Types, functions, cues and signals of non-verbal communication  .
  - Listening Skills: Importance, types, barriers and techniques of effective listening  .
  - Writing Skills: Process, types, formats and styles of writing for different purposes and audiences  .
  - Reading Skills: Types, strategies and benefits of reading for comprehension and analysis  .
  - Speaking Skills: Types, features, techniques and tips for speaking confidently and persuasively  .
  - Presentation Skills: Planning, preparing, delivering and evaluating oral presentations  .
  - Group Communication: Characteristics, types, roles and dynamics of group communication  .
  - Interpersonal Communication: Definition, factors, skills and strategies of interpersonal communication  .
  - Intercultural Communication: Definition, challenges, dimensions and skills of intercultural communication  .
  - Professional Communication: Definition, scope, ethics and etiquette of professional communication  .
  - Communication Technology: Types, advantages, disadvantages and implications of communication technology  .

- The book is designed for the students of engineering, management, commerce and other disciplines who need to develop their communication skills for academic and professional success   .
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with examples, illustrations, exercises, activities and self-assessment tests to enhance the learning outcomes   .
- The book is based on the latest syllabus and guidelines of various universities and boards   .
