### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

- This is a book that covers the basics of communication skills and techniques for technical and professional purposes.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I - Communication Process and Principles, Part II - Communication Skills and Strategies, Part III - Communication in Specific Contexts, and Part IV - Communication for Career Development.
- Some of the topics covered in the book are:

  - The nature, functions, and types of communication
  - The barriers and facilitators of effective communication
  - The verbal and non-verbal aspects of communication
  - The listening and feedback skills
  - The communication styles and strategies for different situations and audiences
  - The communication skills for group discussions, presentations, interviews, and meetings
  - The communication skills for writing reports, proposals, letters, memos, and emails
  - The communication skills for using visual aids, graphics, and multimedia
  - The communication skills for intercultural and cross-cultural communication
  - The communication skills for ethical and social responsibility
  - The communication skills for career planning and development

- The book is written in a simple and clear language, with examples, exercises, and case studies to illustrate the concepts and practices.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, and professionals who want to improve their communication skills and competence.
