### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

- The objective of this topic is to help students improve their writing skills by learning and applying the rules of English grammar.
- Writing is a form of communication that requires clarity, accuracy, and coherence. Writing errors can affect the meaning, credibility, and effectiveness of the message.
- English grammar is the system of rules that governs how words and sentences are structured, used, and punctuated in the language. Grammar rules cover various aspects of writing, such as word order, verb tense, agreement, voice, mood, modifiers, pronouns, articles, prepositions, conjunctions, and punctuation.
- To use the correct and error-free writing, students need to be well versed in the rules of English grammar. This means that they need to:
  - Understand the basic concepts and terms of grammar, such as parts of speech, phrases, clauses, sentences, and types of sentences.
  - Identify and avoid common grammatical errors, such as fragments, run-ons, comma splices, subject-verb agreement, pronoun-antecedent agreement, verb tense consistency, misplaced and dangling modifiers, parallelism, and faulty coordination and subordination.
  - Apply the appropriate grammar rules to different types of writing, such as academic, professional, creative, and informal writing.
  - Use various resources and tools to check and improve their grammar, such as dictionaries, thesauruses, style guides, online grammar checkers, and feedback from peers and instructors.
- To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar, instructors can use various strategies and methods, such as:
  - Providing clear and explicit explanations and examples of grammar rules and their applications in writing.
  - Designing exercises and activities that allow students to practice and reinforce their grammar skills in different contexts and genres of writing.
  - Giving constructive and specific feedback on students' writing, pointing out their strengths and weaknesses in grammar, and suggesting ways to improve their accuracy and clarity.
  - Encouraging students to revise and edit their writing, using the feedback and resources available to them, and to reflect on their progress and challenges in grammar.
  - Creating a supportive and collaborative learning environment, where students can share their writing, ask questions, and learn from each other's mistakes and successes in grammar.
