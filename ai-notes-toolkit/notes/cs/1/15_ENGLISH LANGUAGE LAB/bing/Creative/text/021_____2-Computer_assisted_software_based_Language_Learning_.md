### 2-Computer assisted software based Language Learning:

- Computer assisted software based language learning (CASLL) is a subset of computer assisted language learning (CALL), which is an approach to teaching and learning languages that uses computers and other technologies to present, reinforce, and assess material to be learned, or to create environments where teachers and learners can interact with one another and the outside world.
- CASLL focuses on the use of software applications that are specifically designed for language learning purposes, such as vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, reading, writing, listening, and speaking. Some examples of CASLL software are Rosetta Stone, Duolingo, Fluenz, and Lingoda.
- CASLL has several advantages and disadvantages in enhanced language learning. Some of the advantages are:
  - It can provide individualized and adaptive instruction that suits the learner's level, pace, and preferences.
  - It can offer immediate and consistent feedback and correction that can enhance the learner's motivation and confidence.
  - It can expose the learner to authentic and diverse language input and output from various sources and contexts.
  - It can facilitate the development of autonomous and self-directed learning skills that can foster lifelong language learning.
- Some of the disadvantages are:
  - It can be expensive and require access to reliable and compatible devices and internet connection.
  - It can be limited in its ability to capture the complexity and variability of natural language and human interaction.
  - It can be prone to technical glitches and errors that can disrupt the learning process.
  - It can be isolating and impersonal and reduce the opportunities for social and cultural exchange and awareness.
- CASLL is a dynamic and evolving field that requires constant research and evaluation to ensure its effectiveness and quality. Some of the current issues and trends in CASLL are:
  - The integration of artificial intelligence and machine learning to enhance the interactivity and adaptability of CASLL software.
  - The incorporation of gamification and storytelling elements to increase the engagement and enjoyment of CASLL software.
  - The exploration of multimodal and mobile CASLL software that can leverage the features and functions of different devices and platforms.
  - The investigation of the impact and implications of CASLL software on the learner's cognitive, affective, and social aspects of language learning.
