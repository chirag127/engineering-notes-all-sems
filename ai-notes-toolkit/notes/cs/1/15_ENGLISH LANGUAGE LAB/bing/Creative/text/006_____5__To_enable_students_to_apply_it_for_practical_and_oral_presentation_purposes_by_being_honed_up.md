### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- This objective aims to help students use their knowledge and skills in real-life situations, such as giving presentations, speeches, or interviews.
- Presentation skills are the abilities to plan, prepare, deliver, and evaluate effective oral presentations using appropriate language, structure, and visual aids.
- Voice-dynamics are the aspects of vocal delivery that affect the audience's attention, interest, and understanding, such as volume, pitch, tone, pace, and pauses.
- To achieve this objective, students should:

  - Identify the purpose, audience, and context of their presentations and tailor their content and style accordingly.
  - Organize their ideas in a clear and logical way, using an introduction, body, and conclusion, and transitions between them.
  - Support their main points with relevant and credible evidence, examples, or anecdotes, and cite their sources properly.
  - Use appropriate language, grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation for their presentations, and avoid jargon, slang, or filler words.
  - Use effective visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, or images, to enhance their presentations and make them more engaging and memorable.
  - Practice their presentations beforehand, rehearsing their speech, timing, and gestures, and seeking feedback from others.
  - Deliver their presentations confidently, using eye contact, facial expressions, body language, and voice-dynamics to communicate their message and connect with their audience.
  - Evaluate their presentations after delivery, reflecting on their strengths and weaknesses, and identifying areas for improvement.
