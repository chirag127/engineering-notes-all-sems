### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

- This topic is about the importance of developing a strong professional vocabulary for students who want to communicate effectively in various contexts and situations.
- Professional vocabulary refers to the words and phrases that are specific to a particular field, industry, or occupation, such as medicine, law, engineering, business, etc.
- Professional vocabulary can help students to:
  - Understand and analyze complex texts and concepts related to their field of study or interest.
  - Express their ideas and opinions clearly and persuasively in academic and professional settings.
  - Demonstrate their knowledge and skills in written and oral assessments, such as essays, reports, presentations, interviews, etc.
  - Build their credibility and confidence as experts or learners in their chosen domain.
- To create a substantial base of professional vocabulary, students need to:
  - Identify and learn the key terms and definitions that are relevant to their field of study or interest.
  - Use various sources and tools to expand their vocabulary, such as dictionaries, thesauruses, glossaries, online databases, etc.
  - Practice using their vocabulary in different modes and platforms, such as comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking exercises, quizzes, games, etc.
  - Review and revise their vocabulary regularly to consolidate their learning and avoid forgetting.
  - Seek feedback and guidance from their teachers, peers, mentors, or experts to improve their vocabulary usage and accuracy.
