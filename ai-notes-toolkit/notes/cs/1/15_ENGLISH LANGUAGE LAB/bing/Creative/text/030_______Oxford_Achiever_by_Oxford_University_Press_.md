### Oxford Achiever by Oxford University Press

- Oxford Achiever is a learning platform developed by Oxford University Press (China) Ltd., covering key subjects: English, Chinese and Mathematics.
- It is a comprehensive app-based learning and assessment system that aims to build students’ language skills and subject knowledge along an individualized, self-paced learning path.
- It is a total e-learning solution for effective app/computer-based teaching and learning and is characterized by:
  - Diagnostic Placement Test: Place students at the right starting level to learn according to their own aptitude/ability in each subject area .
  - Learning Path: Provide students with a personalized learning path based on their performance in the Diagnostic Placement Test .
  - Learning Modules: Offer students interactive and engaging learning activities that cover diverse learning aspects and varied question types .
  - Progress Report: Track students’ learning progress and performance with detailed and instant feedback .
  - Teacher Dashboard: Enable teachers to monitor students’ learning progress, assign homework, and generate reports.
- It has over 45,000 questions, covering different levels of difficulty and cognitive skills.
- It is compatible with Android and Windows devices and can be accessed online or offline  .
