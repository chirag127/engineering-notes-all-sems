### 9. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace &L.Ann Masters, Cengage Learning, New Delhi

- This is a book that covers the topics of personal development, soft skills, critical thinking skills, and work ethics for students and professionals.
- The book aims to help readers recognize the importance of these skills and attitudes in today's workplace and in life.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I: Understanding Yourself, Part II: Communicating Effectively, Part III: Developing Career Skills, and Part IV: Developing Life Skills.
- Some of the main topics covered in the book are:

  - Self-awareness and self-esteem
  - Personality types and preferences
  - Learning styles and strategies
  - Goal setting and time management
  - Listening and speaking skills
  - Writing and presentation skills
  - Teamwork and leadership skills
  - Problem solving and decision making skills
  - Ethics and social responsibility
  - Diversity and cross-cultural communication
  - Career planning and development
  - Job search and interview skills
  - Workplace etiquette and professionalism
  - Stress management and wellness
  - Financial literacy and consumer skills

- The book uses a variety of features to engage readers and enhance learning, such as:

  - Learning objectives and summaries
  - Self-assessment quizzes and exercises
  - Case studies and scenarios
  - Role plays and simulations
  - Critical thinking questions and activities
  - Web resources and references
  - Glossary and index

- The book is written by Harold R. Wallace and L. Ann Masters, who are both experienced educators and authors in the fields of personal development and career education.
- The book is published by Cengage Learning, a leading provider of educational materials and solutions for higher education and professional markets.
