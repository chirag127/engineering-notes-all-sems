### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes.

- Individual speech delivery is the process of presenting a speech to an audience, either in person or online, using verbal and nonverbal communication skills.
- Conferencing is the process of engaging in a dialogue with the audience, either during or after the speech, to clarify, elaborate, or respond to questions or comments.
- Skills to defend interjections/quizzes are the abilities to handle interruptions, challenges, or tests from the audience or the evaluator, using logic, evidence, and persuasion.

Some points to consider for individual speech delivery/conferencing with skills to defend interjections/quizzes are:

- Prepare and practice your speech beforehand, considering the purpose, audience, and context of your presentation.
- Use an appropriate tone, volume, pace, and pronunciation for your speech, and avoid filler words, slang, or jargon.
- Use eye contact, gestures, facial expressions, and posture to enhance your message and connect with your audience.
- Use visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, or images, to support your speech and make it more engaging and memorable.
- Anticipate possible questions or objections from your audience or the evaluator, and prepare responses that address them effectively and respectfully.
- Listen actively and attentively to the feedback or queries from your audience or the evaluator, and acknowledge their points of view.
- Use evidence, examples, statistics, or testimonials to back up your claims and arguments, and cite your sources properly.
- Use rhetorical devices, such as repetition, parallelism, analogy, or metaphor, to make your speech more persuasive and impactful.
- Summarize your main points and restate your thesis or call to action at the end of your speech, and thank your audience for their attention and participation.
