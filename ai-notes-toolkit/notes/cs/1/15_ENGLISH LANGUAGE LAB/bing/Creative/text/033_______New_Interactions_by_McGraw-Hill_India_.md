### New Interactions by McGraw-Hill India

- New Interactions is a series of textbooks for English language learners from elementary to advanced levels.
- The series aims to develop the four skills of listening, speaking, reading and writing, as well as grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation.
- The series follows a communicative approach that encourages learners to interact with authentic texts and tasks in meaningful contexts.
- The series consists of 12 books, each with a student's book, a workbook, a teacher's manual and an audio CD.
- The series covers a variety of topics and themes, such as culture, technology, health, environment, travel, education and careers.
- The series provides learners with opportunities to practice and improve their language skills through a range of activities, such as dialogues, role plays, discussions, presentations, projects, games and quizzes.
- The series also offers learners with self-assessment tools, such as checklists, portfolios, progress tests and feedback forms, to monitor their own learning and progress.
- The series is designed to meet the needs and interests of learners from different backgrounds and levels, and to prepare them for academic and professional success in the global context.
