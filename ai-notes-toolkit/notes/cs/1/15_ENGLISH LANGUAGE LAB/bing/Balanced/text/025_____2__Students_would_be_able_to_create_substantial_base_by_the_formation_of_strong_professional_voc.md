### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

- This topic is about the importance of developing a strong professional vocabulary for students who want to communicate effectively in various contexts and situations.
- Professional vocabulary refers to the words and phrases that are specific to a particular field, industry, or occupation, such as medicine, law, engineering, business, etc.
- Professional vocabulary can help students to:
  - Understand and interpret complex texts and documents related to their field of study or interest.
  - Express their ideas and opinions clearly and persuasively in academic and professional settings.
  - Demonstrate their knowledge and competence in their chosen discipline or career path.
  - Build rapport and credibility with their peers, instructors, employers, clients, and other stakeholders.
- To create a substantial base of professional vocabulary, students need to:
  - Identify and learn the key terms and concepts of their field or domain, as well as their definitions, synonyms, antonyms, and usage examples.
  - Expand their vocabulary by reading and listening to authentic and relevant sources, such as textbooks, journals, articles, podcasts, lectures, presentations, etc.
  - Practice using their vocabulary in different modes and platforms, such as comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking exercises, quizzes, tests, assignments, projects, reports, essays, etc.
  - Review and revise their vocabulary regularly and monitor their progress and improvement.
