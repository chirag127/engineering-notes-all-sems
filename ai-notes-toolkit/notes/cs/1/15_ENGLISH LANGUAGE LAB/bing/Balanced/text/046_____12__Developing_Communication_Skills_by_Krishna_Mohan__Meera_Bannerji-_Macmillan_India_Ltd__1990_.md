### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

- This is a book that aims to help anyone who wants to improve their skills in communication, both oral and written.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, such as:
  - The nature and process of communication
  - The barriers and aids to communication
  - The types and modes of communication
  - The skills and strategies of communication
  - The applications and contexts of communication
- The book is divided into four parts:
  - Part I: Communication: An Introduction
  - Part II: Communication Skills
  - Part III: Communication Strategies
  - Part IV: Communication in Practice
- The book provides examples, exercises, activities, and case studies to illustrate and practice the concepts and skills of communication.
- The book also offers tips, guidelines, checklists, and summaries to help the reader review and apply the communication skills and strategies.
- The book is designed as a text for advanced level courses in communication skills, but it can also be used by professionals and general readers who want to enhance their communication abilities.
