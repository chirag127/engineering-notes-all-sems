### 4. Presentation Skills for Technical Paper/Project Reports/ proposals based on proper Stress and Intonation Mechanics

- Presentation skills are the abilities to communicate effectively and confidently with an audience, using appropriate verbal and non-verbal cues, visual aids, and structure.
- Technical paper/project reports/proposals are types of academic or professional documents that present the results of research, analysis, design, or implementation of a specific topic or problem.
- Stress and intonation mechanics are the aspects of speech that convey emphasis, emotion, and attitude through variations in loudness, pitch, and duration of syllables and words.
- To improve presentation skills for technical paper/project reports/proposals based on proper stress and intonation mechanics, some tips are:

  - Plan and prepare the content, structure, and delivery of the presentation according to the purpose, audience, and context.
  - Use clear and concise language, avoid jargon and technical terms that may confuse the audience, and define any acronyms or abbreviations.
  - Use appropriate visual aids, such as graphs, charts, diagrams, images, or videos, to support the main points and illustrate the data or concepts.
  - Use proper stress and intonation mechanics to highlight the key words, phrases, and sentences, to express the tone and mood of the message, and to signal the transitions and connections between the parts of the presentation.
  - Practice the presentation several times, preferably in front of a mirror, a recorder, or a friend, and get feedback on the content, structure, and delivery.
  - During the presentation, maintain eye contact with the audience, use gestures and facial expressions to enhance the communication, and modulate the voice to avoid monotony and boredom.
  - Anticipate and address any questions or comments from the audience, and thank them for their attention and participation.
