### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

- This is a book that covers the basics of communication skills and techniques for technical and professional purposes.
- The book is divided into four units: Communication Process and Principles, Communication Skills, Communication Techniques, and Communication Practice.
- The book aims to help the readers develop their communication competence and confidence in various situations and contexts.
- Some of the topics covered in the book are:

  - The nature, functions, and types of communication
  - The barriers and facilitators of communication
  - The verbal and non-verbal aspects of communication
  - The listening and feedback skills
  - The interpersonal and group communication skills
  - The presentation and public speaking skills
  - The report and proposal writing skills
  - The technical and scientific writing skills
  - The business and professional writing skills
  - The communication ethics and etiquette

- The book also provides exercises, activities, examples, and case studies to help the readers apply the concepts and skills learned.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, and professionals who want to improve their communication abilities and effectiveness.
