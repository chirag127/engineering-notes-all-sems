### 2-Computer assisted software based Language Learning:

- Computer assisted software based language learning (CASLL) is a subset of computer assisted language learning (CALL), which is an approach to teaching and learning languages that uses computers and other technologies to present, reinforce, and assess material to be learned, or to create environments where teachers and learners can interact with one another and the outside world.
- CASLL focuses on the use of software applications that are specifically designed for language learning purposes, such as interactive multimedia, games, simulations, adaptive learning systems, speech recognition, natural language processing, etc.
- CASLL can offer several benefits for language learners, such as:
  - Increased motivation, engagement, and autonomy.
  - Individualized and personalized learning paths and feedback.
  - Enhanced exposure to authentic and varied language input and output.
  - Improved linguistic and communicative skills and competencies.
- CASLL can also pose some challenges and limitations, such as:
  - Technical issues and accessibility problems.
  - Lack of human interaction and social presence.
  - Need for teacher training and guidance.
  - Potential mismatch between learner needs and software design.
- CASLL is a dynamic and evolving field that requires constant research and development to keep up with the changing needs and preferences of language learners and teachers, as well as the advances in technology and pedagogy.
