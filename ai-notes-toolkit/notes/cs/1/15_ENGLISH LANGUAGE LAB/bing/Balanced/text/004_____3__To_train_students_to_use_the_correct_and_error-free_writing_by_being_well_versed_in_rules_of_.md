### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

- The objective of this topic is to help students improve their writing skills by learning and applying the rules of English grammar.
- Writing is a form of communication that requires clarity, accuracy, and coherence. Writing errors can affect the meaning, credibility, and effectiveness of the message.
- English grammar is the set of rules that govern how words and sentences are structured, used, and punctuated in the language. Grammar rules cover various aspects of writing, such as word order, verb tense, agreement, voice, mood, modifiers, pronouns, articles, prepositions, conjunctions, and punctuation.
- By being well versed in rules of English grammar, students can avoid common writing errors, such as fragments, run-ons, comma splices, subject-verb disagreement, misplaced or dangling modifiers, pronoun reference or agreement errors, incorrect verb tense or form, parallelism errors, and punctuation errors.
- To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar, the following steps can be followed:

  - Teach the students the basic concepts and terms of grammar, such as parts of speech, sentence structure, phrases, clauses, and types of sentences.
  - Provide the students with examples and exercises to practice identifying and correcting grammar errors in their own and others' writing.
  - Give the students feedback and guidance on their grammar performance and progress, and encourage them to revise and edit their writing accordingly.
  - Expose the students to various types of texts and genres that demonstrate effective and appropriate use of grammar in different contexts and purposes.
  - Assess the students' grammar knowledge and skills through quizzes, tests, and writing assignments, and provide them with constructive and specific feedback.
