## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

- Part A: This part consists of oral communication skills, which include listening, speaking, and presentation skills. The objectives of this part are to:
  - Enhance the students' ability to comprehend and respond to various types of spoken texts, such as lectures, interviews, conversations, etc.
  - Develop the students' proficiency in using appropriate language, tone, and style for different speaking purposes and contexts, such as formal, informal, academic, professional, etc.
  - Train the students to plan, prepare, and deliver effective oral presentations on various topics, using suitable audio-visual aids and techniques.
  - Assess the students' performance and provide constructive feedback and suggestions for improvement.
- Part B: This part consists of written communication skills, which include reading, writing, and documentation skills. The objectives of this part are to:
  - Enhance the students' ability to comprehend and analyze various types of written texts, such as articles, reports, essays, etc.
  - Develop the students' proficiency in using appropriate language, grammar, and vocabulary for different writing purposes and contexts, such as descriptive, narrative, expository, argumentative, etc.
  - Train the students to plan, draft, revise, and edit various types of written documents, such as letters, emails, memos, resumes, proposals, etc.
  - Assess the students' performance and provide constructive feedback and suggestions for improvement.
