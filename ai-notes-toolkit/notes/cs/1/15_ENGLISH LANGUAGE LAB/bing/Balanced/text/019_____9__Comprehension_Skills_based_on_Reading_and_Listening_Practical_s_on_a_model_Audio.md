### 9. Comprehension Skills based on Reading and Listening Practical’s on a model Audio

- Comprehension skills are the ability to understand and interpret information from various sources, such as texts, speeches, images, etc.
- Reading and listening are two of the most common and important sources of information in academic and professional contexts.
- Reading comprehension is the ability to read a text and extract its main idea, supporting details, inferences, and implications.
- Listening comprehension is the ability to listen to an audio and identify its purpose, tone, structure, and key points.
- Practicing reading and listening comprehension skills can help improve one's vocabulary, grammar, critical thinking, and communication skills.
- A model audio is a sample audio that is used for teaching or testing purposes. It can be a lecture, a dialogue, a speech, or any other type of spoken discourse.
- A model audio can be used to assess one's listening comprehension skills by asking questions based on the content, context, and language of the audio.
- Some of the questions that can be asked are:
  - What is the main topic or theme of the audio?
  - What is the speaker's purpose or goal in delivering the audio?
  - What is the speaker's tone or attitude towards the topic or the audience?
  - How is the audio organized or structured? What are the main parts or sections of the audio?
  - What are the main points or arguments that the speaker makes in the audio?
  - What are the supporting details or examples that the speaker provides to back up the main points or arguments?
  - What are the inferences or implications that the speaker draws or suggests from the main points or arguments?
  - What are the rhetorical devices or strategies that the speaker uses to persuade or inform the audience?
  - What are the unfamiliar words or expressions that the speaker uses in the audio? What do they mean in the context of the audio?
