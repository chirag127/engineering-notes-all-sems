#### Software based self-guided learning to provide the required English language proficiency to students from an employability and career readiness standpoint. The software should align to Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and deliver a CEFR level – B2 upon completion.

- Software based self-guided learning is a form of education that uses computer programs and applications to deliver personalized and interactive instruction to learners, without the need for a teacher or a classroom.
- The software can adapt to the learner's level, pace, preferences, and goals, and provide feedback, guidance, and support along the way.
- The software can also track the learner's progress and performance, and generate reports and certificates to demonstrate the learner's achievements and competencies.
- The software can be accessed anytime and anywhere, as long as the learner has a device and an internet connection, making it convenient and flexible for the learner.
- The software can also incorporate various multimedia elements, such as audio, video, images, animations, games, and simulations, to enhance the learner's engagement and motivation.
- The software can also offer social features, such as chat, forums, peer review, and collaboration, to foster a sense of community and interaction among learners.
- The software can also integrate with other platforms and tools, such as e-books, podcasts, webinars, and online assessments, to provide a comprehensive and diverse learning experience for the learner.
- The software can also be customized and updated according to the learner's needs and preferences, and the latest trends and developments in the field of study.
- The software can also be aligned to various standards and frameworks, such as the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), to ensure the quality and validity of the software and the learner's outcomes.
- The software can also be designed to provide the required English language proficiency to students from an employability and career readiness standpoint, by focusing on the skills and knowledge that are relevant and applicable to the workplace and the professional context.
- The software can also deliver a CEFR level – B2 upon completion, which means that the learner can:

  - Understand the main ideas of complex texts on both concrete and abstract topics, including technical discussions in their field of specialization.
  - Interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers quite possible without strain for either party.
  - Produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects and explain a viewpoint on a topical issue giving the advantages and disadvantages of various options.
