### 13. Spoken English- A Manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- This is a book for students who wish to improve their pronunciation of English and acquire the correct patterns of stress, rhythm and intonation  .
- The book covers the following topics:
  - The organs of speech and their functions
  - The sounds of English and their symbols
  - The phonetic transcription of words and sentences
  - The syllable structure and word stress in English
  - The features of connected speech, such as assimilation, elision, liaison, juncture and intonation
  - The pronunciation of English in different varieties, such as British, American, Indian and International
  - The common errors and difficulties in pronouncing English
  - The practice exercises and drills for improving pronunciation
- The book comes with a CD that contains audio recordings of the sounds, words, sentences and dialogues used in the book .
- The book is written in a simple and clear style, with examples, diagrams, charts and tables to illustrate the points .
- The book is suitable for students of various levels, from beginners to advanced, and can be used for self-study or in the classroom  .
