### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

- This is a book that aims to help readers improve their vocabulary and master the English language.
- The book is divided into three main sections: Part One: Getting Off to a Good Start, Part Two: Gaining Increased Momentum, and Part Three: Finishing with a Feeling of Complete Success.
- Each section consists of several chapters that cover different aspects of vocabulary building, such as word roots, prefixes, suffixes, synonyms, antonyms, word analysis, word formation, word usage, spelling, pronunciation, and etymology.
- The book also provides exercises, quizzes, tests, and reviews to help readers practice and reinforce their learning.
- The book is written in a clear, simple, and engaging style, with examples, anecdotes, and humor to make the learning process enjoyable and effective.
- The book is suitable for students, professionals, and anyone who wants to enrich their vocabulary and communication skills.
