### 9. Comprehension Skills based on Reading and Listening Practical’s on a model Audio

- Comprehension skills are the ability to understand and interpret the meaning of texts and spoken language.
- Reading and listening are two of the most common and important ways of acquiring comprehension skills.
- Reading and listening practicals are exercises that involve reading or listening to a model audio and answering questions based on the content, structure, and purpose of the text or speech.
- A model audio is a recorded speech or dialogue that is used as an example or reference for learning and practicing comprehension skills.
- The following are some steps and tips for doing reading and listening practicals on a model audio:

  - Before listening or reading, activate your prior knowledge and make predictions about the topic, genre, and purpose of the model audio.
  - While listening or reading, pay attention to the main idea, supporting details, and key words of the model audio. Use strategies such as skimming, scanning, summarizing, and paraphrasing to help you understand the text or speech.
  - After listening or reading, check your comprehension by answering questions based on the model audio. The questions may be factual, inferential, evaluative, or creative. Use evidence from the text or speech to support your answers.
  - Review your answers and compare them with the answer key or feedback. Identify your strengths and weaknesses and areas for improvement.
