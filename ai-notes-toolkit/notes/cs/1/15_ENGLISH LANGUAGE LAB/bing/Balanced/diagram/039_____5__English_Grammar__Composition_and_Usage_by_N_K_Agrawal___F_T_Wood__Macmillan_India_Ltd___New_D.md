### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to the rules and principles of English grammar, composition and usage.
- It covers the following topics in detail:

  - Parts of speech: nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions and interjections.
  - Sentence structure: subject, predicate, object, complement, modifier, clause and phrase.
  - Types of sentences: simple, compound, complex and compound-complex sentences.
  - Tenses: present, past and future tenses, and their forms and uses.
  - Voice: active and passive voice, and their rules and transformations.
  - Mood: indicative, imperative, subjunctive and conditional moods, and their forms and functions.
  - Modals: modal verbs, their meanings and uses.
  - Concord: subject-verb agreement, and noun-pronoun agreement.
  - Direct and indirect speech: rules and methods of changing direct speech into indirect speech, and vice versa.
  - Punctuation: the use of commas, semicolons, colons, apostrophes, quotation marks, hyphens, dashes, brackets, ellipses and full stops.
  - Spelling: common spelling rules and exceptions, and tips to avoid spelling errors.
  - Vocabulary: word formation, synonyms, antonyms, homonyms, homophones, homographs, prefixes, suffixes, roots, idioms, phrases and proverbs.
  - Composition: types of composition, such as essays, letters, reports, summaries, précis, articles, speeches, dialogues and stories, and their formats, styles and techniques.
  - Usage: common errors and pitfalls in English grammar, composition and usage, and how to avoid or correct them.

- The book also provides exercises, examples, illustrations, tables, charts and diagrams to help the learners practice and master the concepts and skills of English grammar, composition and usage.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, professionals and anyone who wants to improve their English language skills.
