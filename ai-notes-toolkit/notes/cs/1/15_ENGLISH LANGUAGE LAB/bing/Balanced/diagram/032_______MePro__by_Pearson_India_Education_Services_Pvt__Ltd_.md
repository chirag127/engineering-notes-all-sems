### MePro by Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd.

- MePro is a digital English language learning platform that aims to transform English language learning for students and professionals.
- MePro uses artificial intelligence and adaptive learning to personalize the learning journey of each learner based on their proficiency level, goals, and interests.
- MePro offers a comprehensive curriculum that covers listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, as well as grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation.
- MePro also provides feedback, analytics, and gamification to motivate and engage learners throughout their learning journey.
- MePro is a product of Pearson, the world's leading learning company that has an expertise in creating legacy products in English language teaching and catering to over 100 million learners around the world .
- MePro is suitable for learners of different age groups, educational backgrounds, and professional domains, as it offers a variety of courses and modules to suit different needs and preferences.
- MePro can be accessed online through a web browser or a mobile app, and can be used for self-study or blended learning.
- MePro is available for purchase through Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd., a subsidiary of Pearson that provides educational products and services in India .
- MePro has been recognized for its innovation and excellence by various awards and accolades, such as the EdTech Review Award 2019, the India Education Awards 2019, and the Bright Spot Award 2020 .
