## List of Practicals

A list of practicals is a document that specifies the experiments or activities that students have to perform in a laboratory course. The purpose of a list of practicals is to help students learn the theoretical concepts and skills through hands-on experience and observation. A list of practicals usually contains the following information:

- The title of the practical, which should be concise and descriptive of the main objective or phenomenon to be studied.
- The aim of the practical, which should state the specific learning outcome or question to be answered by the experiment or activity.
- The apparatus or materials required for the practical, which should include the names and quantities of the equipment, instruments, chemicals, specimens, etc. that are needed to conduct the experiment or activity.
- The procedure or method of the practical, which should provide clear and detailed instructions on how to set up, perform, and record the experiment or activity. The procedure should also include any safety precautions, warnings, or tips that are relevant to the practical.
- The observations or results of the practical, which should present the data or findings that are obtained from the experiment or activity. The observations or results should be recorded in an appropriate format, such as tables, graphs, diagrams, charts, etc. The observations or results should also include any calculations, analyses, or interpretations that are necessary to answer the aim of the practical.
- The conclusion or discussion of the practical, which should summarize the main findings or outcomes of the experiment or activity. The conclusion should also compare the results with the expected or theoretical values, explain any sources of error or uncertainty, and suggest any improvements or extensions for the practical.
