### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to the process and practice of effective communication in technical and professional contexts.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, such as:
  - The nature and scope of communication, its types, models, barriers, and principles.
  - The skills and strategies for oral communication, such as listening, speaking, presenting, interviewing, and participating in group discussions and meetings.
  - The skills and strategies for written communication, such as planning, drafting, revising, editing, and formatting various types of documents, such as letters, memos, reports, proposals, and manuals.
  - The skills and strategies for non-verbal communication, such as body language, gestures, facial expressions, eye contact, and voice modulation.
  - The skills and strategies for intercultural communication, such as understanding cultural differences, avoiding stereotypes, and adapting to diverse audiences and situations.
  - The skills and strategies for ethical communication, such as respecting confidentiality, avoiding plagiarism, and following codes of conduct and professional standards.
- The book also provides practical exercises, examples, case studies, and self-assessment tests to help the readers apply and evaluate their communication skills.
- The book is intended for students, teachers, and professionals who want to improve their communication competence and confidence in various fields and domains.
