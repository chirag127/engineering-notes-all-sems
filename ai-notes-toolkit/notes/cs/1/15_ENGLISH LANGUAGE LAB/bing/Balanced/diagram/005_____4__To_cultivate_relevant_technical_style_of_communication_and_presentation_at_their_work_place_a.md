### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- Technical style of communication and presentation refers to the use of clear, concise, accurate and objective language to convey information or persuade an audience about a specific topic.
- Technical style of communication and presentation is important for both work place and academic purposes, as it helps to:
  - Establish credibility and authority as a professional or a researcher.
  - Communicate complex ideas or data in a simple and understandable way.
  - Avoid ambiguity, confusion or misunderstanding among the readers or listeners.
  - Achieve the desired goals or outcomes of the communication or presentation.
- To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation, one should:
  - Know the purpose, audience and context of the communication or presentation.
  - Use appropriate format, structure, tone and style for the communication or presentation.
  - Use precise and specific vocabulary, terminology and definitions for the communication or presentation.
  - Use logical and coherent arguments, evidence and examples to support the communication or presentation.
  - Use visual aids, such as graphs, charts, tables, diagrams, images, etc., to enhance the communication or presentation.
  - Use correct grammar, spelling, punctuation and citation for the communication or presentation.
  - Revise, edit and proofread the communication or presentation before finalizing it.
  - Seek feedback and suggestions from others to improve the communication or presentation.
