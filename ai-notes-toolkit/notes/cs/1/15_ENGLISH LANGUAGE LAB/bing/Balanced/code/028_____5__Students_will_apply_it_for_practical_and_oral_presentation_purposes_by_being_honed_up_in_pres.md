### 5. Students will apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics. They will apply techniques for developing interpersonal communication skills and positive attitude leading to their professional competence.

This topic covers the following points:

- The importance of practical and oral presentation skills for students in various academic and professional contexts.
- The key elements of effective presentation skills, such as planning, preparation, delivery, audience analysis, visual aids, and feedback.
- The role of voice-dynamics in enhancing the clarity, expressiveness, and persuasiveness of oral communication.
- The techniques for improving voice-dynamics, such as breathing, articulation, intonation, pitch, volume, pace, and pauses.
- The benefits of developing interpersonal communication skills and positive attitude for students in their personal and professional relationships.
- The strategies for developing interpersonal communication skills and positive attitude, such as active listening, empathy, assertiveness, conflict resolution, and self-confidence.
