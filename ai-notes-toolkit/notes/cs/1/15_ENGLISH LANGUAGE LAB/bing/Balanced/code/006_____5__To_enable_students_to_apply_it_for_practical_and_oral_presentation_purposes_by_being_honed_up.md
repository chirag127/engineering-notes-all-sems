### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- This is a learning objective that aims to help students use their knowledge and skills in real-life situations, such as giving presentations, speeches, or demonstrations.
- Presentation skills are the abilities to plan, prepare, and deliver effective and engaging presentations that convey the intended message and achieve the desired outcome.
- Voice-dynamics are the aspects of vocal expression that influence the listener's perception and response, such as volume, pitch, tone, pace, and pauses.
- To achieve this objective, students need to:
  - Understand the purpose, audience, and context of their presentation.
  - Choose an appropriate topic, format, and structure for their presentation.
  - Research and gather relevant and reliable information and evidence to support their main points and arguments.
  - Organize and outline their presentation using clear and logical transitions and signposts.
  - Design and use visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, or images, to enhance their presentation and support their verbal communication.
  - Practice and rehearse their presentation, paying attention to their voice-dynamics, body language, eye contact, and gestures.
  - Deliver their presentation confidently, clearly, and concisely, using appropriate language and style for their audience and purpose.
  - Engage their audience by asking questions, inviting feedback, and responding to comments or queries.
  - Evaluate their presentation and reflect on their strengths and areas for improvement.
