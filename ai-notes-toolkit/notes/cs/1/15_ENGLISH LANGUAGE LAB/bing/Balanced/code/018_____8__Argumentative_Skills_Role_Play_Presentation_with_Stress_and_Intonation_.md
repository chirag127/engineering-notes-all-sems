### 8. Argumentative Skills/Role Play Presentation with Stress and Intonation

- Argumentative skills are the ability to present and defend a position or opinion using logical reasoning and evidence.
- Role play presentation is a technique of simulating a real-life situation or scenario using dialogue and acting skills.
- Stress and intonation are the ways of using the voice to convey meaning, emotion, and emphasis in speech.
- To improve your argumentative skills/role play presentation with stress and intonation, you can follow these steps:

  - Identify the purpose and audience of your argument/presentation. What are you trying to achieve and who are you trying to persuade or inform?
  - Research and gather relevant information and evidence to support your argument/presentation. Use credible sources and cite them properly.
  - Organize your argument/presentation into a clear structure with an introduction, body, and conclusion. Use transitions and signposts to guide your listeners.
  - Develop a strong thesis statement or main claim that summarizes your position or opinion. Make sure it is debatable, specific, and clear.
  - Acknowledge and refute the opposing views or counterarguments. Show why your argument/presentation is more valid, reasonable, or convincing than the other side.
  - Use appropriate language and tone for your argument/presentation. Avoid fallacies, emotional appeals, and personal attacks. Use formal, polite, and respectful language.
  - Use stress and intonation to enhance your argument/presentation. Stress the important words or syllables to show emphasis or contrast. Use intonation to show the attitude, mood, or emotion of your speech. Use rising intonation for questions, falling intonation for statements, and fall-rise intonation for uncertainty or surprise.
  - Practice your argument/presentation before delivering it. Record yourself and listen to your voice. Check for clarity, fluency, accuracy, and coherence. Get feedback from others and improve your performance.
