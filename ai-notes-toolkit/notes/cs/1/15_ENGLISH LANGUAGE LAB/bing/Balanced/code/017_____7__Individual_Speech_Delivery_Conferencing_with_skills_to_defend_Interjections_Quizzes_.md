### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

- Individual speech delivery is the process of presenting a speech to an audience, either in person or online, using verbal and nonverbal communication skills.
- Conferencing is the process of engaging in a dialogue with the audience, either during or after the speech, to clarify, elaborate, or respond to questions or comments.
- Skills to defend interjections/quizzes are the abilities to handle interruptions, challenges, or tests from the audience or the evaluator, using logic, evidence, and rhetoric.
- Some of the skills to defend interjections/quizzes are:
  - Anticipating possible objections or questions and preparing answers in advance.
  - Acknowledging the interjection or quiz politely and respectfully, without being defensive or hostile.
  - Addressing the interjection or quiz directly and concisely, using relevant facts, examples, or arguments.
  - Restating or summarizing the main point or thesis of the speech, if necessary, to reinforce the message.
  - Inviting further feedback or questions from the audience, if appropriate, to show openness and confidence.
  - Transitioning back to the speech or concluding the speech, if applicable, using a clear and coherent structure.
