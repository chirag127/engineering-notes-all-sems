# 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi.

- This is a textbook that addresses the requirements of students of any discipline who are on the threshold of starting their careers. The book would also prove beneficial to young managers who have already had a taste of corporate life  .
- The book aims to provide crucial insights into various facets of developing one's personality, as well as to improve written, verbal, and non-verbal communication skills.
- The book covers topics such as self-awareness, self-management, dealing with people, communication skills, presentation skills, group dynamics, leadership skills, conflict management, etiquette and manners, and professional image  .
- The book adopts a learner-centric approach and provides ample examples, case studies, activities, and self-assessment tools to help the readers apply the concepts to real-life situations  .
- The book also includes a section on soft skills for the digital age, which discusses the impact of technology on communication and personality development.
- The second edition of the book, published in 2019, has been revised and updated to incorporate the latest trends and developments in the field of personality development and soft skills.
