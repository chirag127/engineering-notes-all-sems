## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

- Part A: Language Lab
  - This part aims to enhance the language skills of the students, such as listening, speaking, reading and writing.
  - This part will cover topics such as phonetics, pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, paragraph writing, comprehension, summarizing, note-taking, etc.
  - This part will use various audio-visual aids and software tools to facilitate the learning process.
  - This part will have both individual and group activities to promote interactive and collaborative learning.
- Part B: Soft Skills Lab
  - This part aims to develop the soft skills of the students, such as communication, presentation, teamwork, leadership, problem-solving, critical thinking, creativity, etc.
  - This part will cover topics such as verbal and non-verbal communication, interpersonal and intrapersonal communication, public speaking, group discussion, debate, interview, resume writing, email etiquette, etc.
  - This part will use various role-plays, simulations, case studies, games, and exercises to enhance the learning outcomes.
  - This part will have both formative and summative assessments to evaluate the students' performance and feedback.
