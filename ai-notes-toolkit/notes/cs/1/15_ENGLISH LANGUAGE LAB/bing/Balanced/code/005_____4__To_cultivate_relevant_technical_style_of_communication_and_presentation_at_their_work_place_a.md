### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

Technical style of communication and presentation is the use of clear, concise, and accurate language and visuals to convey technical information to a specific audience and purpose. Technical style of communication and presentation can be cultivated by following some general guidelines, such as:

- Analyze the audience, purpose, and context of the communication or presentation. Consider the needs, expectations, background, and preferences of the audience, as well as the goals, objectives, and constraints of the communication or presentation. Adapt the tone, style, format, and content accordingly.
- Organize the information logically and coherently. Use headings, subheadings, lists, tables, charts, graphs, diagrams, and other visuals to structure the information and highlight the main points. Use transitions, signposts, summaries, and previews to guide the audience through the communication or presentation.
- Use clear, concise, and accurate language. Avoid jargon, slang, vague, ambiguous, or redundant words, and complex or lengthy sentences. Use active voice, parallel structure, and appropriate verb tense and mood. Use correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and capitalization. Define any technical terms, acronyms, or abbreviations that the audience may not be familiar with.
- Use appropriate and effective visuals. Choose visuals that support the message, purpose, and audience of the communication or presentation. Use visuals that are clear, relevant, accurate, consistent, and ethical. Use labels, captions, legends, titles, and sources to explain and cite the visuals. Integrate the visuals with the text and refer to them in the communication or presentation.
- Revise and edit the communication or presentation. Check the communication or presentation for clarity, coherence, accuracy, completeness, and correctness. Seek feedback from peers, instructors, or experts. Use tools such as spell checkers, grammar checkers, and plagiarism checkers. Proofread the communication or presentation before submitting or delivering it.
