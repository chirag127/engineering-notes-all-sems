# Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- This is a book that covers various aspects of communication skills, such as listening, speaking, reading, writing, and non-verbal communication.
- The book is divided into 12 chapters, each focusing on a specific topic or skill, such as communication process, barriers to communication, oral communication, written communication, technical communication, business communication, and so on.
- The book provides theoretical concepts, practical examples, exercises, and self-assessment tests to help the readers improve their communication skills.
- The book is intended for students, teachers, professionals, and anyone who wants to enhance their communication abilities in different contexts and situations.
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with clear explanations and illustrations. It also includes references and suggestions for further reading at the end of each chapter.
