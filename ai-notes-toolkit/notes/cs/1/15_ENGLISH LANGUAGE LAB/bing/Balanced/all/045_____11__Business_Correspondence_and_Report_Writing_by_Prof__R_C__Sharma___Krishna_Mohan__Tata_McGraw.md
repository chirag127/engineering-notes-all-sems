# 11. Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan, Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd., 2001, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to effective business communication in various contexts and formats.
- The book covers the following topics:

  - The principles and techniques of business correspondence, such as letters, memos, emails, faxes, etc.
  - The types and purposes of business reports, such as analytical, informational, feasibility, progress, etc.
  - The process and structure of report writing, such as planning, collecting, organizing, analyzing, presenting, and documenting data and information.
  - The style and tone of business writing, such as clarity, conciseness, coherence, courtesy, correctness, etc.
  - The use of visual aids, such as tables, charts, graphs, diagrams, etc., to enhance the readability and impact of business documents.
  - The ethical and legal aspects of business communication, such as plagiarism, confidentiality, copyright, etc.

- The book also provides numerous examples, exercises, and case studies to illustrate the concepts and skills of business communication.
- The book is suitable for students, professionals, and managers who want to improve their business writing skills and knowledge.
