# Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists

- This is a book written by Sangeeta Sharma, Binod Mishra and Savita Mishra, published by PHI Learning Pvt. Ltd. in 2011.
- The book aims to help undergraduate students of engineering and science develop effective communication skills for various professional situations and applications.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part A covers the basics of technical communication, such as technical report writing, communication breakdown, listening, business letters, advertising, and non-verbal communication. Part B covers the advanced topics of technical communication, such as group discussions, presentations, interviews, resumes, proposals, and research papers.
- The book provides numerous examples, exercises, case studies, and illustrations to help the students practice and apply the concepts and skills learned from the book.
- The book also includes a glossary of technical terms, a bibliography of references, and an index of topics for easy access and revision.
