## LIST OF PRACTICALS

- A practical is a hands-on activity that allows students to apply and demonstrate their theoretical knowledge and skills in a specific subject or field.
- Practicals can take various forms, such as experiments, simulations, fieldwork, projects, case studies, demonstrations, or exercises.
- Practicals can have different purposes, such as developing practical skills, enhancing conceptual understanding, testing hypotheses, solving problems, collecting data, or evaluating outcomes.
- Practicals can be conducted individually or in groups, depending on the nature and objectives of the activity.
- Practicals can be assessed in different ways, such as by observation, feedback, reports, portfolios, presentations, or tests.
- Practicals can be integrated into the curriculum in different ways, such as by aligning them with the learning outcomes, linking them with the theoretical content, or embedding them in the assessment scheme.
- Practicals can have different benefits for students, such as increasing their motivation, engagement, confidence, creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, or communication skills.
