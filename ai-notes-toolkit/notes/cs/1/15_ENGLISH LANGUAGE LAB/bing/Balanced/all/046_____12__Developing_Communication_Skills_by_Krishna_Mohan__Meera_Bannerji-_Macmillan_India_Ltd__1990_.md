# 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

- This book is a text for advanced level courses in communication skills, but it can also help professionals in their daily communication tasks.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, such as:
  - The nature and process of communication, and the barriers and strategies to overcome them.
  - The types and functions of communication, and the skills and techniques to use them effectively.
  - The principles and practices of oral communication, such as listening, speaking, and presenting.
  - The features and conventions of written communication, such as grammar, vocabulary, style, and format.
  - The modes and methods of business communication, such as letters, memos, reports, and proposals.
  - The role and importance of non-verbal communication, such as body language, gestures, and facial expressions.
  - The challenges and opportunities of intercultural communication, and the ways to adapt and adjust to different cultures and contexts.
- The book provides examples, exercises, and activities to help the reader apply the concepts and skills learned.
- The book also includes a glossary of key terms, a bibliography of useful references, and an index for easy access.
