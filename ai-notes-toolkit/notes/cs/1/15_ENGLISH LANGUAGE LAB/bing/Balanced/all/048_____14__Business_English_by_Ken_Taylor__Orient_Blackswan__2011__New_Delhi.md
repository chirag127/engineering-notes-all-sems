# 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a self-study book for learners of English who want to improve their communication skills in business contexts.
- The book contains 50 units, each focusing on a specific aspect of business English, such as meetings, presentations, negotiations, emails, reports, etc.
- Each unit consists of four parts: a warm-up activity, a reading text, a language focus section, and a practice section.
- The warm-up activity helps the learners activate their prior knowledge and introduces the topic of the unit.
- The reading text provides authentic examples of business English in use and exposes the learners to relevant vocabulary and expressions.
- The language focus section explains the grammar, pronunciation, and style points that are related to the topic of the unit.
- The practice section gives the learners an opportunity to apply what they have learned and check their progress.
- The book also includes a glossary of key terms, a reference section with useful phrases and tips, and an answer key for self-correction.
- The book is suitable for intermediate to advanced learners of English who want to improve their business English skills in a variety of situations.
- The book can be used as a standalone course or as a supplement to other business English materials.
