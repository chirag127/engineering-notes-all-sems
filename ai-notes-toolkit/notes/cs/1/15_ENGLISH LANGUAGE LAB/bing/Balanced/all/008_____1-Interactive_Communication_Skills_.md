# 1-Interactive Communication Skills:

- Interactive communication skills are the abilities that enable a person to effectively exchange information, ideas, feelings, and feedback with others in various contexts, such as face-to-face, online, or in groups.
- Interactive communication skills include verbal and non-verbal communication, listening, questioning, clarifying, summarizing, paraphrasing, empathizing, and giving and receiving feedback.
- Interactive communication skills are essential for building rapport, trust, and understanding with others, as well as for resolving conflicts, negotiating, persuading, influencing, and collaborating.
- Interactive communication skills can be improved by practicing the following strategies:
  - Use appropriate language, tone, and volume for the situation and the audience.
  - Use clear, concise, and coherent messages that avoid jargon, slang, and ambiguity.
  - Use open-ended questions that invite elaboration and exploration, rather than closed-ended questions that limit the response.
  - Use active listening skills that show attention, interest, and respect, such as eye contact, nodding, paraphrasing, and reflecting feelings.
  - Use positive and constructive feedback that focuses on the behavior, not the person, and that offers specific suggestions for improvement, rather than vague or negative criticism.
  - Use empathy and emotional intelligence to understand and respond to the emotions, needs, and perspectives of others, and to manage your own emotions and reactions.
  - Use assertive communication that expresses your thoughts, feelings, and opinions in a respectful and confident manner, without being aggressive or passive.
  - Use non-verbal communication that supports and enhances your verbal communication, such as gestures, facial expressions, posture, and eye contact.
  - Use appropriate communication tools and channels that suit the purpose, context, and audience of your communication, such as email, phone, video call, or face-to-face meeting.
  - Use feedback and reflection to evaluate and improve your communication skills, by seeking and giving feedback, and by reviewing and analyzing your communication performance and outcomes.
