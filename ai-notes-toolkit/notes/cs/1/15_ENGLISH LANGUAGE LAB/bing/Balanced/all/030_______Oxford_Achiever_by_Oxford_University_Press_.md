# Oxford Achiever by Oxford University Press

- Oxford Achiever is a comprehensive web-based learning and assessment system developed by Oxford University Press.
- It is designed to enhance students' language skills and knowledge across key learning areas in the English medium curriculum.
- It covers the subjects of English, Mathematics, Science, Computer Studies, and General Studies for primary and secondary levels.
- It provides interactive and engaging learning materials, such as animations, videos, games, and quizzes, that are aligned with the latest curriculum and assessment standards.
- It also offers diagnostic and formative assessments, as well as instant feedback and reports, to help teachers and students monitor their progress and identify their strengths and weaknesses.
- It supports blended learning and flipped classroom approaches, as well as self-directed and collaborative learning.
- It can be accessed anytime and anywhere through the internet, using computers, tablets, or smartphones.
