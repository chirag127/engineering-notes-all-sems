# 9. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace & L.Ann Masters, Cengage Learning, New Delhi

- Personality development is the process of enhancing one's self-concept, self-esteem, self-efficacy, and self-regulation through various psychological, social, and environmental factors.
- Personality development can be influenced by both internal and external factors, such as genetics, temperament, cognition, emotions, motivation, values, beliefs, attitudes, culture, family, peers, education, work, media, and life events.
- Personality development can be assessed by various methods, such as personality tests, self-reports, behavioral observations, interviews, and projective techniques.
- Personality development can be facilitated by various strategies, such as goal setting, self-monitoring, feedback, reinforcement, modeling, coaching, mentoring, counseling, and therapy.
- Personality development can have various outcomes, such as improved self-awareness, self-acceptance, self-confidence, self-expression, self-control, self-regulation, self-improvement, self-actualization, and well-being.
