# 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to the pronunciation and intonation of spoken English for learners and teachers.
- It covers the basic concepts and principles of phonetics, phonology, and prosody, as well as the practical aspects of speech production, perception, and transcription.
- It also provides exercises, drills, and activities to help learners improve their spoken English skills and confidence.
- The book is divided into four parts:
  - Part I: Introduction to Speech and Phonetics
    - This part introduces the basic terminology and concepts of speech and phonetics, such as the organs of speech, the classification of sounds, the phonetic alphabet, and the features of vowels and consonants.
  - Part II: The Sounds of English
    - This part describes the sounds of English in detail, with examples, diagrams, and charts. It covers the phonetic and phonemic systems of English, the stress and rhythm patterns, the weak forms and contractions, the assimilation and elision processes, and the intonation contours.
  - Part III: Transcription and Analysis
    - This part explains how to transcribe and analyze spoken English using the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA). It provides guidelines and conventions for transcription, as well as exercises and practice passages for transcription and analysis.
  - Part IV: Teaching and Learning Spoken English
    - This part offers practical suggestions and techniques for teaching and learning spoken English, such as the use of audio-visual aids, the correction of errors, the development of listening and speaking skills, and the evaluation of spoken performance.
