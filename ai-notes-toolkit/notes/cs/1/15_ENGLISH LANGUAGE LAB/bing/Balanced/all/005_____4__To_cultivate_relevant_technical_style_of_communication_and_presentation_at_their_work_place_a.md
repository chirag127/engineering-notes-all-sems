# 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- Technical style of communication and presentation refers to the use of clear, concise, accurate and objective language to convey information or persuade an audience about a specific topic.
- Technical style of communication and presentation is important for both work place and academic uses, as it helps to:
  - Establish credibility and authority as a professional or a scholar.
  - Communicate effectively with different audiences, such as colleagues, clients, supervisors, instructors, peers, etc.
  - Achieve specific purposes, such as informing, instructing, explaining, proposing, recommending, etc.
  - Organize and structure information logically and coherently.
  - Use appropriate formats, conventions, and citations for different types of documents or presentations.
  - Avoid ambiguity, confusion, errors, and misunderstandings.
- To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation, one should:
  - Identify the purpose, audience, and context of the communication or presentation.
  - Conduct thorough research and analysis of the topic and the relevant sources of information.
  - Plan and outline the main points, arguments, evidence, and examples to support the purpose.
  - Draft and revise the content, using clear, concise, accurate and objective language, and following the appropriate format, convention, and citation style.
  - Use visual aids, such as graphs, charts, tables, diagrams, etc., to enhance the communication or presentation, and explain them clearly and accurately.
  - Practice and rehearse the communication or presentation, and seek feedback from others.
  - Deliver the communication or presentation confidently, professionally, and respectfully, and respond to questions or comments from the audience.
