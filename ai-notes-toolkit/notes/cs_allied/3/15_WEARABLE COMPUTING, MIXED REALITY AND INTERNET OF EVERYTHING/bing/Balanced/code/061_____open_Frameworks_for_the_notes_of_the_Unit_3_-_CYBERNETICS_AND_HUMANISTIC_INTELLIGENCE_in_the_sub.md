# Open Frameworks

- Open Frameworks is an open source C++ toolkit for creative coding.
- It is designed to assist the creative process by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation.
- It wraps together several commonly used libraries, including OpenGL, GLEW, GLUT, libtess2 and cairo for graphics, rtAudio, PortAudio or FMOD for audio input and output, FreeType for fonts, FreeImage for image saving and loading, Quicktime, GStreamer and videoInput for video playback and grabbing and Poco for a variety of utilities.
- It supports as many development environments and operating systems as possible, such as Microsoft Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android and Emscripten .
- It can be used for various applications, such as interactive installations, generative art, computer vision, data visualization, games, augmented reality, mixed reality and wearable computing.
- It has a large and active community of users and contributors, who share their code, projects, tutorials and documentation on the official website, forum and GitHub repository .
