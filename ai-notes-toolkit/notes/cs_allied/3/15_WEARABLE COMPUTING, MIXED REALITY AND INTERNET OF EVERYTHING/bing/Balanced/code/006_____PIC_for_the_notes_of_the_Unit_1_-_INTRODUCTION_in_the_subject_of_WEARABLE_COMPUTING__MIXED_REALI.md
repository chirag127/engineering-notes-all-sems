### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- PIC stands for **P**rogrammable **I**nterface **C**ontroller, which is a type of microcontroller that can be used to control various devices and systems, such as wearable computers, mixed reality devices and internet of things applications.
- A wearable computer is a device that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing, and that can perform various functions, such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, etc. Examples of wearable computers are smart watches, smart glasses, fitness trackers, etc.
- Mixed reality is a term that describes the combination of real and virtual environments, where physical and digital objects can coexist and interact with each other. Examples of mixed reality devices are virtual reality headsets, augmented reality glasses, holographic displays, etc.
- Internet of things is a concept that refers to the network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, actuators, software and connectivity, and that can exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet. Examples of internet of things applications are smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
- PIC can be used to control wearable computers, mixed reality devices and internet of things applications by providing input and output interfaces, processing data, executing instructions, communicating with other devices and systems, etc.
- PIC can also be programmed to perform specific tasks and functions according to the requirements and preferences of the user, such as adjusting the display, sensing the environment, responding to commands, etc.
