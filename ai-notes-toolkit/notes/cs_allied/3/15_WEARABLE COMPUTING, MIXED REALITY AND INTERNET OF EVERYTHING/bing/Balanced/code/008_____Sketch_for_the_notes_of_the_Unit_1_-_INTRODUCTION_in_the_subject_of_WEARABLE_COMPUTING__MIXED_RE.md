### Sketch for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is a field of technology that involves creating electronic devices that can be worn on the body, either as accessories or as part of clothing .
- Wearable devices can have various functions, such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, or interacting with the user or the environment.
- Wearable devices are part of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of physical objects that can collect, exchange, and process data using embedded sensors, software, and wireless connectivity .
- IoT enables new applications and services that can improve the quality of life, health, education, entertainment, and productivity of individuals and society .
- Mixed reality (MR) is a type of technology that combines aspects of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) to create immersive and interactive experiences that blend the real and the virtual worlds.
- MR can be achieved using various devices, such as head-mounted displays, smart glasses, projectors, or smartphones.
- MR can enhance the perception, cognition, and interaction of the user with the physical and digital environments, and enable new forms of education, entertainment, communication, and collaboration.
- Wearable computing, IoT, and MR are interrelated and complementary technologies that can create synergies and opportunities for innovation and value creation.
- However, they also pose challenges and risks, such as privacy, security, ethics, usability, and social acceptance, that need to be addressed by researchers, developers, and users .
