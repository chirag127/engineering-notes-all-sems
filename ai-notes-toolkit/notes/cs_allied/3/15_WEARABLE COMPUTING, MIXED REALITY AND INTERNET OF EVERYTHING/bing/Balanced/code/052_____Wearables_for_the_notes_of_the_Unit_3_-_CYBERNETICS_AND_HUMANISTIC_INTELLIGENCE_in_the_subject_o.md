### Wearables for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearables are devices that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing or accessories, and that have sensing, computing, and communication capabilities.
- Wearables can be classified into three categories based on their level of integration with the human body: attached, implanted, and ingested.
- Wearables can enable various applications such as health monitoring, fitness tracking, entertainment, education, social interaction, and augmented reality.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines, and it studies the feedback loops that regulate their behavior and adaptation.
- Cybernetics can be applied to the design and analysis of wearables, as they can be seen as cybernetic systems that interact with the human body and the environment.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is a concept that defines intelligence as the result of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- HI can be embodied by wearable computing, as it allows the human to augment their natural capabilities and extend their perception and cognition through the wearable device.
- HI can be contrasted with artificial intelligence (AI), which aims to create machines that can perform tasks that require human intelligence, and with natural intelligence (NI), which is the intelligence that arises from the natural evolution of living organisms.
- HI can be measured by the degree of embodiment, engagement, and empowerment that the wearable device provides to the human user.
- HI can be enhanced by the principles of personalization, participation, and protection, which aim to make the wearable device more suitable, interactive, and secure for the human user.
