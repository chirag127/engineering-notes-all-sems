### Audio
- Audio is a form of data that represents sound waves or speech signals in digital or analog formats.
- Audio can be used for various purposes in wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything, such as:
  - Providing feedback, instructions, alerts, or notifications to the user or other devices.
  - Enabling voice-based interaction, recognition, or control of the system or application.
  - Enhancing the user experience, immersion, or engagement with the environment or content.
  - Transmitting or receiving information, data, or commands from other sources or devices.
- Audio can be processed, synthesized, analyzed, or manipulated by software or hardware components, such as:
  - Microphones, speakers, headphones, or earbuds for capturing or delivering audio signals.
  - Sound cards, audio codecs, or audio processors for converting, encoding, or decoding audio signals.
  - Audio libraries, frameworks, or APIs for providing audio functionalities, such as playback, recording, mixing, filtering, or effects.
  - Audio engines, tools, or editors for creating, editing, or generating audio content, such as music, sound effects, or speech.
  - Audio recognition, synthesis, or analysis systems for performing tasks, such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, speech translation, sound classification, or sound localization.
- Audio can be integrated, combined, or coordinated with other modalities, such as vision, haptics, or gestures, to create multimodal or crossmodal interfaces, applications, or experiences.
- Audio can pose some challenges or limitations for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything, such as:
  - Audio quality, latency, or bandwidth issues due to the constraints of the devices, networks, or environments.
  - Audio privacy, security, or safety concerns due to the potential exposure, interception, or misuse of the audio data or content.
  - Audio interference, noise, or distraction problems due to the presence of other sounds or sources in the surroundings or the system.
  - Audio accessibility, usability, or compatibility issues due to the diversity, preferences, or needs of the users or devices.
