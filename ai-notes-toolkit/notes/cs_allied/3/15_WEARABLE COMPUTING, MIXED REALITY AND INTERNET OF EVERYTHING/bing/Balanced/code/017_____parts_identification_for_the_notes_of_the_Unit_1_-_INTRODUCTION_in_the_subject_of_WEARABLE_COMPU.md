### Parts identification

- Wearable computing: A wearable device is a computer that can be worn on the body, either as an accessory or as part of the clothing. Wearable devices can perform various functions, such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, or interacting with the user or the environment. Examples of wearable devices are smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, head-mounted displays, smart clothing, etc.

- Mixed reality: Mixed reality (MR) is a type of immersive technology that combines the real and virtual worlds, creating a hybrid environment where physical and digital objects coexist and interact. MR can be seen as a spectrum that ranges from augmented reality (AR), where digital information is overlaid on the real world, to virtual reality (VR), where the user is fully immersed in a simulated environment. Examples of MR devices are Microsoft HoloLens, Magic Leap, Oculus Quest, etc.

- Internet of Everything: Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the network of people, things, data, and processes that are connected through the Internet. IoE extends the idea of Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects that can communicate and exchange data with each other or with other devices. IoE aims to create more value and opportunities by integrating data analytics, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and other technologies into the network. Examples of IoE applications are smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
