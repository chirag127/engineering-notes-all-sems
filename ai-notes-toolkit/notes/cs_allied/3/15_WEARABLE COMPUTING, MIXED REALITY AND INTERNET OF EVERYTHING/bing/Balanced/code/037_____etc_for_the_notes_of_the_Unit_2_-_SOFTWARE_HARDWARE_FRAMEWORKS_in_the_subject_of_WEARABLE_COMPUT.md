### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

- This unit covers the basic concepts and principles of software and hardware frameworks for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything applications.
- Software frameworks are sets of tools, libraries, protocols and standards that provide a common platform for developing and running applications.
- Hardware frameworks are collections of devices, sensors, actuators, interfaces and communication modules that enable the interaction between the physical and digital worlds.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - Software frameworks for wearable computing, such as Android Wear, Apple WatchOS, Tizen, Pebble OS, etc.
  - Software frameworks for mixed reality, such as Unity, Unreal Engine, Vuforia, ARCore, ARKit, etc.
  - Software frameworks for internet of everything, such as MQTT, CoAP, RESTful APIs, WebSockets, etc.
  - Hardware frameworks for wearable computing, such as smartwatches, smart glasses, smart clothing, smart jewelry, etc.
  - Hardware frameworks for mixed reality, such as head-mounted displays, handheld devices, projection systems, etc.
  - Hardware frameworks for internet of everything, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, sensors, actuators, etc.
  - The advantages and disadvantages of different software and hardware frameworks for various applications and scenarios.
  - The challenges and opportunities of integrating software and hardware frameworks for creating immersive and interactive experiences.
