# PHP/Web for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- PHP is a server scripting language, and a powerful tool for making dynamic and interactive Web pages.
- PHP runs on various platforms (Windows, Linux, Unix, Mac OS X, etc.) and is compatible with almost all servers used today (Apache, IIS, etc.).
- PHP supports a wide range of databases and is easy to learn and runs efficiently on the server side.
- PHP can be used to create web applications that interact with wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything technologies.
- Wearable computing is the use of devices that are worn on the body or in direct contact with the skin, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, necklaces, and virtual reality headsets.
- Wearable computing devices can connect to the internet of things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies to collect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet.
- Wearable computing devices can also enable mixed reality (MR), which is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where digital content can be projected into the physical space around the user and interact with it.
- Mixed reality can also be achieved by using spatial computing, which is the ability to map and understand the 3D environment and track the user's position and orientation in it.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines.
- Cybernetics can be applied to study the interactions and feedback loops between wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything technologies and their users, as well as the social and ethical implications of these technologies.
- Humanistic intelligence is the ability to augment human intelligence and creativity by using wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything technologies as extensions of the human mind and body.
- Humanistic intelligence can enable new forms of communication, expression, learning, and collaboration, as well as new challenges and opportunities for human development and well-being .
