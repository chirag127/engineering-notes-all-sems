### Desktop

- A desktop is a computer system that consists of a monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, and a central processing unit (CPU) that is usually housed in a separate case.
- A desktop is designed to be used on a desk or a table, and it is not very portable or mobile.
- A desktop can run various operating systems, such as Windows, Linux, or macOS, depending on the user's preference and needs.
- A desktop can perform various tasks, such as word processing, web browsing, gaming, video editing, and more, depending on the hardware and software specifications and capabilities.
- A desktop can be connected to other devices, such as printers, scanners, speakers, cameras, and more, through wired or wireless connections.
- A desktop can be customized and upgraded by changing or adding components, such as the CPU, the memory, the storage, the graphics card, and more, to improve the performance or functionality of the system.
