### Saving data and preferences for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, etc. These devices can collect, process, and transmit data about the user and the environment, as well as provide feedback and services to the user.
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual and augmented reality elements can interact with the real world and vice versa.
- Internet of everything is the concept of connecting people, processes, data, and things through the internet, enabling new levels of intelligence, efficiency, and innovation.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines, which can be applied to the design and analysis of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything systems.
- Humanistic intelligence is the ability of humans to enhance their cognitive and perceptual capabilities through the use of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything technologies.
- Saving data and preferences for these technologies is important for several reasons, such as:
  - To ensure the accuracy, reliability, and security of the data collected and transmitted by the devices  .
  - To protect the privacy and confidentiality of the user and the data, especially when it involves sensitive information such as health, location, or personal identity .
  - To enable the personalization and customization of the devices and the services they provide, according to the user's needs, preferences, and goals  .
  - To facilitate the integration and interoperability of the devices and the data with other systems and platforms, such as cloud computing, artificial intelligence, or social networks  .
- Some of the methods and techniques for saving data and preferences for these technologies are:
  - Using encryption, authentication, and authorization mechanisms to protect the data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure .
  - Using data compression, aggregation, and filtering techniques to reduce the size and complexity of the data, and to remove any unnecessary or redundant information .
  - Using data synchronization, backup, and recovery techniques to ensure the consistency and availability of the data across different devices and platforms .
  - Using data analysis, mining, and visualization techniques to extract useful insights and patterns from the data, and to present them in an understandable and actionable way .
  - Using user modeling, profiling, and preference elicitation techniques to capture and represent the user's characteristics, behaviors, and preferences, and to adapt the devices and the services accordingly .
