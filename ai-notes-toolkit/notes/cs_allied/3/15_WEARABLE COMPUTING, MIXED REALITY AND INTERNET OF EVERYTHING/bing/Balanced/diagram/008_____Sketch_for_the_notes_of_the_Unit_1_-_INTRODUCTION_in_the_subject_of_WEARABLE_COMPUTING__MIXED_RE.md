# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

## Wearable Computing

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body to perform various functions, such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, or interacting with the environment or other devices.
- Wearable devices can be classified into different categories, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, smart clothing, smart jewelry, or virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) headsets.
- Wearable devices can connect to the internet or other devices through wireless technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC, or cellular networks.
- Wearable devices can provide various benefits, such as convenience, personalization, health monitoring, entertainment, productivity, or social interaction.
- Wearable devices can also pose some challenges, such as privacy, security, usability, battery life, or social acceptance.

## Mixed Reality

- Mixed reality (MR) is a type of immersive technology that combines the physical and digital worlds, creating a hybrid reality where virtual and real objects coexist and interact.
- MR can be achieved by using devices that overlay digital content onto the user's view of the real world, such as smart glasses or head-mounted displays (HMDs), or by using devices that project digital content onto the physical environment, such as holograms or spatial computing.
- MR can enable various applications, such as education, training, gaming, entertainment, art, tourism, or healthcare.
- MR can also create some challenges, such as technical limitations, user experience, ethical issues, or social implications.

## Internet of Everything

- Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that extends the Internet of Things (IoT) by including not only the connections between things, but also the connections between people, data, and processes, creating a network of intelligence and value across the networked environment.
- IoE can be enabled by various technologies, such as cloud computing, big data analytics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, or blockchain.
- IoE can provide various opportunities, such as innovation, efficiency, productivity, sustainability, or social good.
- IoE can also pose some challenges, such as complexity, security, privacy, governance, or ethics.
