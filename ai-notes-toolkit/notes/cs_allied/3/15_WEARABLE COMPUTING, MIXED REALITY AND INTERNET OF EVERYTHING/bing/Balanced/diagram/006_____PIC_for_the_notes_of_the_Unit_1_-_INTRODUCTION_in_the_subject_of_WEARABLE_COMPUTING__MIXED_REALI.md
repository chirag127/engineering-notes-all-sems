### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the field of technology that deals with electronic devices that can be worn on the user's body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, glasses, headsets, etc.  
- Wearable devices can have various functions, such as sensing, processing, communicating, displaying, or interacting with the user or the environment. 
- Mixed reality is the field of technology that combines the real and the virtual worlds, creating a hybrid environment where physical and digital objects can coexist and interact.  
- Mixed reality devices can include augmented reality (AR) devices, which overlay digital information on the real world, or virtual reality (VR) devices, which create a fully immersive simulation of a different world.  
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is the concept that encompasses the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things through the internet, enabling new capabilities, experiences, and opportunities.  
- Internet of Everything devices can include sensors, actuators, cameras, robots, smart appliances, etc. that can collect, transmit, analyze, or act on data from the physical or digital world.
