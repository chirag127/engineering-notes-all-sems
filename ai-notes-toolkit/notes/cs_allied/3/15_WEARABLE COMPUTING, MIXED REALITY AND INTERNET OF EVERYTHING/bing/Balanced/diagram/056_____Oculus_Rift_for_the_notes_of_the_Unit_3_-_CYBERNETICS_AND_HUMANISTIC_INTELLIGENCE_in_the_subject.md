### Oculus Rift

- Oculus Rift is a line of virtual reality headsets developed and manufactured by Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms.
- Oculus Rift was invented by VR enthusiast Palmer Luckey, who later founded Oculus VR with Brendan Iribe.
- Oculus Rift connects to a gaming PC or laptop and uses a pair of goggles and headphones to create an immersive VR experience.
- Oculus Rift was released on March 28, 2016, after a successful Kickstarter campaign in 2012.
- Oculus Rift was discontinued in 2020 and replaced by Oculus Rift S, which has improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics.
- Oculus Rift S is compatible with the Oculus software, which allows users to find friends, discover VR apps and games, and configure their hardware.
- Oculus Rift is an example of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything, as it integrates digital and physical worlds, connects to the internet, and enhances human capabilities .
