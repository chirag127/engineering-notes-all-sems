### Live Network Feeds for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- Live network feeds are streams of data that are continuously updated and transmitted over a network, such as the internet, to provide real-time information and interaction.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms, machines, and social systems. It studies the principles of feedback, adaptation, and self-organization in complex systems.
- Humanistic intelligence is a concept that describes the integration of human and artificial intelligence through wearable computing and mixed reality. It emphasizes the role of human creativity, intuition, and emotion in intelligent systems.
- Wearable computing is the use of devices that are worn on the body, such as smartwatches, glasses, or clothing, to augment human capabilities and experiences. It enables ubiquitous and natural interaction with the digital world.
- Mixed reality is the blending of physical and virtual environments, where real and digital objects coexist and interact. It encompasses both augmented reality, which enhances the real world with virtual elements, and virtual reality, which immerses the user in a simulated world.
- Internet of everything is the extension of the internet to connect not only people and devices, but also data, processes, and things. It enables new applications and services that leverage the power of networked intelligence and collaboration.

Some points to note about live network feeds for the unit 3 are:

- Live network feeds can be used to enhance the cybernetic feedback loop between the human and the machine, by providing real-time data and control signals that can modify the behavior and performance of both.
- Live network feeds can also enable humanistic intelligence, by allowing the user to access and share information and experiences across different modalities and contexts, such as vision, sound, touch, and location.
- Live network feeds can facilitate wearable computing and mixed reality, by providing the necessary data and communication channels to support the interaction and integration of physical and virtual worlds.
- Live network feeds can contribute to the internet of everything, by enabling the connection and coordination of diverse and distributed entities, such as sensors, actuators, devices, people, and things.
