- Wearable technology is any kind of electronic device designed to be worn on the user's body, such as jewelry, accessories, medical devices, and clothing or elements of clothing.
- Wearable devices can have processing or communications capabilities, such as microprocessors, batteries, and connectivity to the internet, so the collected data can be synced with other electronics, such as mobile devices or laptops.
- Wearable devices are an example of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of physical objects that can exchange data through the internet without requiring human intervention .
- Wearable IoT can have various applications in different domains, such as health, sports and daily activity, tracking and localization, and safety.
- Wearable IoT can also enable mixed reality, which is a combination of real and virtual environments that can interact with each other.
- Wearable IoT faces many challenges and opportunities, such as battery life, security, privacy, user acceptance, and cellular IoT (CIoT), which is a technology that can provide low-power, wide-area, and reliable connectivity for IoT devices .
