### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Basic electronics is the study of the principles and applications of electronic devices and circuits, such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits.
- Basic electronics is essential for understanding and designing wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything systems, which are composed of various sensors, actuators, displays, and communication modules that interact with the physical and digital world.
- Some of the topics covered in basic electronics are:
  - Voltage, current, power, and energy: the fundamental quantities that describe the behavior and performance of electronic circuits and devices.
  - Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, and Thevenin's theorem: the basic rules and methods for analyzing and simplifying electronic circuits.
  - Series and parallel circuits: the common configurations of connecting electronic components and their effects on voltage, current, and resistance.
  - AC and DC circuits: the difference between alternating current (AC) and direct current (DC) and how to convert and use them in electronic systems.
  - Passive and active components: the difference between components that do not require external power to operate (such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors) and those that do (such as diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits).
  - Analog and digital signals: the difference between continuous and discrete signals and how to process and convert them using electronic devices and circuits.
  - Logic gates and Boolean algebra: the basic building blocks of digital electronics and how to use them to perform logical operations and create complex functions.
  - Sensors and actuators: the devices that can measure and control physical quantities, such as temperature, pressure, light, sound, motion, and force, and how to interface them with electronic circuits and systems.
  - Displays and communication modules: the devices that can output and transmit information, such as LEDs, LCDs, speakers, microphones, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and RFID, and how to use them in wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything applications.
