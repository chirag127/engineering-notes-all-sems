### Microcontrollers for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- A microcontroller is a small computer on a single integrated circuit that can be programmed to perform various tasks.
- A microcontroller consists of a central processing unit (CPU), memory, input/output (I/O) ports, timers, serial communication interfaces, analog-to-digital converters, and other peripherals.
- A microcontroller is typically used to control devices or systems that require low power consumption, high reliability, and real-time performance, such as wearable devices, sensors, robots, and embedded systems.
- A microcontroller can be programmed using various languages, such as assembly, C, C++, Python, or Arduino.
- A microcontroller can communicate with other devices or systems using various protocols, such as serial, I2C, SPI, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, or ZigBee.
- A microcontroller can be interfaced with various sensors, actuators, displays, and other components using various hardware frameworks, such as breadboards, shields, modules, or development boards.
- Some examples of microcontrollers are Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, STM32, PIC, and AVR.
