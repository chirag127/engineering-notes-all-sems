### Oculus Rift

- Oculus Rift is a line of **virtual reality headsets** developed and manufactured by Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms.
- Oculus Rift allows users to experience **immersive 3D environments** and interact with them using **hand controllers**.
- Oculus Rift connects to a **gaming PC or laptop** via a cable and requires **external sensors** to track the user's head and hand movements .
- Oculus Rift was released in **March 2016** after a successful **Kickstarter campaign** in 2012, which raised over **$2.4 million** from backers.
- Oculus Rift was discontinued in **April 2020** and replaced by **Oculus Rift S**, which has improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics.
- Oculus Rift is an example of **cybernetics** and **humanistic intelligence**, as it enhances the user's perception and interaction with the virtual world, and creates a feedback loop between the user and the system.
