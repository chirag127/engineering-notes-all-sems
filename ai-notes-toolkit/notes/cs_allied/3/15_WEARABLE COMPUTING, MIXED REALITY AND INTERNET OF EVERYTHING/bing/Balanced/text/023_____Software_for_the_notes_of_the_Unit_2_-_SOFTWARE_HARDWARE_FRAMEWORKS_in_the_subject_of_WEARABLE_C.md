### Software for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Software hardware frameworks are the systems and tools that enable the development and deployment of applications for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything.
- Wearable computing is the integration of computing devices into clothing or accessories that can be worn by users, such as smart watches, glasses, or earbuds.
- Mixed reality is the combination of real and virtual worlds, where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real time, such as augmented reality or virtual reality.
- Internet of everything is the network of physical objects, people, and processes that are connected and communicate through the internet, such as smart homes, cities, or industries.
- Some examples of software hardware frameworks for these domains are:

  - UbiVis: A framework that tackles the challenges of using different hardware for mixed reality experiences, such as synchronization, calibration, and collaboration.
  - Azure IoT: A platform that provides cloud and edge computing services for internet of everything applications, such as digital twins, mixed reality, and autonomous systems.
  - MRCP: A framework that offers a vendor-neutral, open-source, cross-platform, mixed reality software for interacting with headless IoT devices, such as smart parking meters or locks.
