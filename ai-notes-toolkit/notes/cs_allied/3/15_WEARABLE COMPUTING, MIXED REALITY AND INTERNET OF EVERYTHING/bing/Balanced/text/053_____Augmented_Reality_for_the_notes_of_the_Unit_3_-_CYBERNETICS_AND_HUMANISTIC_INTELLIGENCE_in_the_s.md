### Augmented Reality

- Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that superimposes a computer-generated image on a user's view of the real world, thus providing a composite view.
- AR can span multiple sensory modalities, including visual, auditory, haptic, somatosensory and olfactory.
- AR can be used to enhance the real world with artificial or real information, such as electromagnetic radio waves overlaid in exact alignment with where they actually are in space.
- AR can be delivered via various devices, such as smartphones, tablets, head-mounted displays, smart glasses, projectors, etc.
- AR can have various applications, such as entertainment, education, gaming, tourism, navigation, medical, industrial, military, etc.
- AR can create immersive and interactive experiences that can enrich the user's perception and understanding of the real world.
