### Database interface for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Cybernetics is the study of feedback, communication and control systems in living organisms, machines and society.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is a new signal processing framework in which (1) intelligence arises by having the human being in the feedback loop of the computational process, and (2) the processing apparatus is inextricably intertwined with the natural capabilities of the human mind and body .
- Wearable computing is the use of computerized devices that can be worn on the body, such as smart glasses, smart watches, smart clothing, etc .
- Mixed reality (MR) is the blending of physical and digital worlds, where real and virtual objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the network of physical objects, people, processes and data that are connected and communicate through the internet.
- A database interface is a software component that allows users to interact with a database, such as creating, querying, updating and deleting data.
- The notes of the unit 3 cover the following topics:

  - The history and evolution of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, and their applications in various domains, such as medicine, education, art, entertainment, etc .
  - The principles and challenges of wearable computing, such as power consumption, user interface, privacy, security, etc .
  - The concepts and technologies of mixed reality, such as virtual reality, augmented reality, holograms, etc.
  - The benefits and risks of internet of everything, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, etc.
  - The design and implementation of a database interface for storing and retrieving data related to wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything, such as user preferences, sensor data, location data, etc.
