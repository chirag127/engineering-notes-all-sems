### Live Network Feeds for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- Live network feeds are streams of data that are continuously updated and transmitted over a network, such as the internet, to provide real-time information or interaction.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines, based on the principles of feedback, adaptation, and self-organization.
- Humanistic intelligence is the ability to use wearable computing devices to augment human capabilities and enhance human experience, based on the principles of embodiment, mediation, and expression.
- Some of the topics that can be covered in this unit are:

  - The history and evolution of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, from the early pioneers such as Norbert Wiener, Claude Shannon, and Steve Mann, to the current trends and challenges in the field.
  - The concepts and applications of network science, network theory, and network analysis, as tools to understand the structure and dynamics of complex systems, such as the brain, the society, and the internet.
  - The examples and implications of live network feeds in various domains, such as neuroscience, social theory, art, and education, and how they can enable brain-inspired intelligence, network governance, networked creativity, and networked learning.
  - The ethical, social, and cultural issues and opportunities that arise from the use of live network feeds, such as privacy, security, trust, identity, empowerment, and participation.
