### etc for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the integration of computing devices and sensors into clothing and accessories that can be worn on the body.
- Mixed reality is the blending of real and virtual worlds to create new environments and experiences where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real time.
- Internet of Everything is the concept of connecting people, processes, data and things to the internet and each other, enabling new levels of intelligence, efficiency and innovation.
- Software hardware frameworks are the architectures and platforms that enable the development and deployment of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything applications and systems.
- Some examples of software hardware frameworks are:
  - Arduino: an open-source hardware and software platform for creating interactive electronic projects using microcontrollers, sensors, actuators and other components.
  - Raspberry Pi: a low-cost, credit-card sized computer that can run various operating systems and can be used for various projects involving computing, robotics, internet of things, etc.
  - Unity: a cross-platform game engine and development environment that can create 2D and 3D applications and games for various platforms, including mixed reality devices such as Microsoft HoloLens and Oculus Rift.
  - TensorFlow: an open-source software library for machine learning and artificial intelligence that can be used for various applications such as computer vision, natural language processing, speech recognition, etc.
  - AWS IoT: a cloud-based platform that provides various services and tools for connecting and managing internet of things devices and applications, such as device registry, device shadow, device gateway, rules engine, etc.
