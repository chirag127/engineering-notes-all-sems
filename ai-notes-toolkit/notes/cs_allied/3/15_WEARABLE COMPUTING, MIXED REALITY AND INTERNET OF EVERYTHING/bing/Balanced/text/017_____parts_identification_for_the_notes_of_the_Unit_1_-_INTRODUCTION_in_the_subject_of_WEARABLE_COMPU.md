### Parts identification for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing: A branch of computer science that deals with the design and development of wearable devices that can be worn on the body or embedded in clothing or accessories. Wearable devices can perform various functions such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, or interacting with the user or the environment.
- Mixed reality: A hybrid of virtual reality and augmented reality that combines digital and physical elements to create a realistic and immersive experience. Mixed reality can be achieved by using head-mounted displays, holograms, projection mapping, or other technologies that overlay or blend virtual content with the real world.
- Internet of everything: A concept that extends the internet of things to include people, processes, data, and things that are connected and interact through the internet. Internet of everything enables new applications and services that can improve efficiency, productivity, safety, and quality of life. Internet of everything can also generate large amounts of data that can be analyzed and used for decision making, innovation, and optimization.
