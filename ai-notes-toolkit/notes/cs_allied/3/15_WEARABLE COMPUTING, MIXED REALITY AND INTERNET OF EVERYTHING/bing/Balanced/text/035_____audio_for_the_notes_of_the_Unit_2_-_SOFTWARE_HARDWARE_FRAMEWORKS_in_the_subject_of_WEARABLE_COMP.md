### Audio for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that are worn on the body or attached to clothing, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets.
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where digital content can interact with the real environment and the user can manipulate both.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the network of connections between people, things, data, and processes that provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment.
- The software hardware frameworks for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything are the platforms, tools, and standards that enable the development, deployment, and integration of these technologies.
- Some examples of software hardware frameworks are:
  - Android Wear: a version of Google's Android operating system designed for smartwatches and other wearables.
  - Magic Leap One: a wearable mobile computer that uses spatial computing to create mixed reality experiences.
  - AWS IoT: a cloud platform that provides services and tools for connecting and managing devices, data, and applications in the internet of things.
  - Ericsson IoT Accelerator: a cloud-based platform that offers connectivity management, device management, data management, and application enablement for the internet of things.
  - Wearable Sensor Platform: a framework that integrates wearable sensors, data processing, and communication modules for health and wellness applications.
