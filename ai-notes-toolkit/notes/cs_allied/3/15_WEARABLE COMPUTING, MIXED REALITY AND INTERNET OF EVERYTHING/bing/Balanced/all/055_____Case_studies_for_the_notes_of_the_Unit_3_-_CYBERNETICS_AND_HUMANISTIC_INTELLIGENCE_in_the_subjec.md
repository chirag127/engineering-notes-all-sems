# Case studies for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Cybernetics is the study of feedback, communication and control systems in living organisms, machines and society. It aims to understand and improve the interaction and adaptation of complex systems.
- Humanistic intelligence is a concept proposed by Steve Mann, who is considered the father of wearable computing. It is defined as intelligence that arises when a human is part of the feedback loop of a computational process in which the human and computer are inextricably intertwined. It emphasizes the human brain as the best neural network and the wearable computer as a tool to augment and extend human capabilities.
- Wearable computing is the design and implementation of computing devices that can be worn on the body, such as glasses, watches, clothing, jewelry, etc. It enables continuous, personal and context-aware interaction with information and the environment. It can also support augmented reality, virtual reality and mixed reality applications, which create immersive and interactive experiences that blend the physical and digital worlds.
- Internet of everything is a term that refers to the network of physical objects, people, processes and data that are connected and communicate with each other through the internet. It enables new forms of sensing, analysis, optimization and automation of various domains, such as health, education, entertainment, transportation, etc.

Some case studies that illustrate the concepts and applications of cybernetics, humanistic intelligence, wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything are:

- **Digital Eye Glass**: This is a wearable computing device that resembles a pair of glasses, but has a camera, a display, a microphone, a speaker and a processor. It can perform various functions, such as capturing and displaying images, videos, texts, sounds, etc., augmenting the user's vision with information and graphics, recognizing faces and objects, translating languages, etc. It can also connect to the internet and other devices, such as smartphones, computers, etc. It is an example of humanistic intelligence, as it enhances the user's perception and cognition by integrating the computer and the human in a feedback loop. It is also an example of mixed reality, as it overlays digital content on the physical world. Some examples of digital eye glass devices are Google Glass, Microsoft HoloLens, etc.    
- **HoloLearn**: This is a mixed reality application that uses the Microsoft HoloLens device to provide educational and training support for people with neuro-developmental disorders, such as autism, dyslexia, etc. It allows the user to interact with holograms that represent concepts, tasks, scenarios, etc., that are relevant to their learning goals and needs. It also provides feedback, guidance and encouragement to the user through voice, sound and visual cues. It is an example of cybernetics, as it monitors and adapts to the user's behavior and performance, and provides appropriate stimuli and responses. It is also an example of internet of everything, as it connects the user, the device, the application and the data through the internet.
