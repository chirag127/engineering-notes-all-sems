# Oculus Rift

Oculus Rift is a line of virtual reality headsets that connect to a gaming PC or laptop and allow the user to experience immersive and interactive 3D environments. The Oculus Rift was invented by Palmer Luckey, who later founded Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms (formerly Facebook). The first consumer version of the Oculus Rift was released in 2016 and was discontinued in 2020, replaced by the Oculus Rift S, which has improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics. The Oculus Rift S is compatible with the Oculus software, which provides access to a variety of VR apps, games, and experiences.

Some of the features and benefits of the Oculus Rift are:

- It uses a pair of OLED displays with a resolution of 1080 x 1200 pixels per eye and a refresh rate of 90 Hz, which provide a wide field of view and a smooth and realistic visual experience.
- It has integrated headphones that deliver spatial audio, which creates a sense of depth and direction for the sound sources in the VR environment.
- It has a positional tracking system that uses external sensors (called Constellation) to track the movement of the headset and the controllers (called Touch) in a 3D space, which enables the user to move around and interact with the virtual world.
- It has a comfortable and adjustable design that fits different head sizes and shapes, and can accommodate glasses wearers. It also has a sliding mechanism that allows the user to adjust the distance between the lenses and the eyes, which improves the focus and clarity of the image.
- It has a library of VR content that ranges from games and simulations to social and educational applications, which can be accessed through the Oculus software or other platforms such as SteamVR. Some of the popular titles include Beat Saber, Half-Life: Alyx, Asgard's Wrath, and The Climb.
- It has a community of VR enthusiasts and developers who create and share new VR experiences and provide feedback and support to each other.

The Oculus Rift is an example of a wearable computing device that uses mixed reality technology to create a humanistic intelligence, which is the integration of human and artificial intelligence. The Oculus Rift allows the user to augment their perception and cognition with the VR environment, which can enhance their learning, creativity, and entertainment. The Oculus Rift also enables the user to connect and communicate with other users in the VR space, which can foster social and emotional intelligence. The Oculus Rift is part of the Internet of Everything, which is the network of physical and digital objects that are interconnected and exchange data and services. The Oculus Rift can access and transmit data from the Internet, such as online content, user profiles, and preferences, which can enrich the VR experience and personalize it for the user. The Oculus Rift can also interact with other devices and sensors, such as cameras, microphones, and motion controllers, which can provide additional input and output for the VR system.
