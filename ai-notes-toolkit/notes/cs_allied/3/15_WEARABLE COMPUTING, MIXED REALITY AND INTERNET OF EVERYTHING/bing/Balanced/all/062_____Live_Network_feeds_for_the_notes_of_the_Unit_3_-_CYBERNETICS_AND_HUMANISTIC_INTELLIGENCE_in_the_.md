# Live Network Feeds for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- Live network feeds are streams of data that are continuously transmitted over a network, such as the internet, to provide real-time information or interaction to the users.
- Live network feeds can be used for various purposes in wearable computing, such as:
  - Providing context-awareness and personalization to the users based on their location, activity, preferences, and environment.
  - Enhancing the user's perception and cognition by augmenting the reality with relevant information, graphics, sounds, or haptics.
  - Enabling social and collaborative interactions among the users and other agents, such as other wearables, smart objects, or cloud services.
  - Supporting learning and creativity by allowing the users to access, create, and share knowledge and experiences.
- Live network feeds can be classified into different types based on the source, content, and format of the data, such as:
  - Sensor feeds: Data from various sensors embedded in the wearable devices or the environment, such as cameras, microphones, accelerometers, GPS, etc.
  - Media feeds: Data from various media sources, such as images, videos, audio, text, etc.
  - Semantic feeds: Data that are annotated with metadata, such as tags, categories, keywords, etc., to provide meaning and structure to the data.
  - Social feeds: Data that are generated by or related to the users and their social networks, such as status updates, messages, comments, likes, etc.
  - Service feeds: Data that are provided by various online services, such as web APIs, databases, cloud platforms, etc.
- Live network feeds can be processed and presented to the users in different ways, such as:
  - Filtering: Selecting the relevant and useful data from the large and noisy data streams based on the user's context and preferences.
  - Aggregating: Combining and summarizing the data from multiple sources to provide a comprehensive and coherent view of the data.
  - Visualizing: Displaying the data in a graphical or symbolic form that is easy to understand and interpret by the user.
  - Interacting: Allowing the user to manipulate, query, or annotate the data using various input and output modalities, such as voice, gesture, touch, etc.
- Live network feeds can pose various challenges and opportunities for wearable computing, such as:
  - Privacy and security: Protecting the user's personal and sensitive data from unauthorized access, misuse, or leakage, while ensuring the user's trust and control over the data.
  - Quality and reliability: Ensuring the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, and consistency of the data, while coping with the uncertainty, ambiguity, and noise in the data.
  - Scalability and efficiency: Handling the large volume, velocity, and variety of the data, while optimizing the resource consumption and performance of the wearable devices and the network.
  - User experience and engagement: Providing the user with meaningful, relevant, and enjoyable data, while avoiding information overload, distraction, or annoyance.
