# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

## Measurements for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, or virtual reality headsets, to collect and analyse data, provide feedback, or enhance the user's experience .
- Mixed reality is a type of immersive technology that combines the physical and digital worlds, allowing the user to interact with both real and virtual objects in the same space. Mixed reality devices, such as Magic Leap One or HoloLens 2, are capable of augmented reality, spatial computing, and multitasking.
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is a network of connections between people, things, data, and processes that provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment. IoE encompasses the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the interconnection of devices and sensors that collect and exchange data  .
- The main objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand the concepts and applications of wearable computing, mixed reality, and IoE.
  - Learn about the benefits and challenges of these technologies for individuals, organizations, and society.
  - Explore the current and future trends and developments in these fields.
  - Evaluate the ethical, legal, and social implications of these technologies.
