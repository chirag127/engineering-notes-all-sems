# Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is a field of technology that involves creating electronic devices that can be worn on the body, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, glasses, headsets, clothing, etc.
- Wearable devices can have various functions, such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, interacting, etc. They can also be connected to other devices or networks, such as smartphones, laptops, or the internet, to exchange data or services.
- Mixed reality is a term that encompasses both augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR). AR is the technology that overlays digital information or objects on the real world, while VR is the technology that creates a fully immersive and simulated environment for the user.
- Mixed reality devices can be wearable, such as head-mounted displays (HMDs), glasses, or contact lenses, or non-wearable, such as projectors, screens, or holograms. They can also be connected to other devices or networks, such as cameras, sensors, controllers, or the internet, to enhance the user's experience or interaction.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is a concept that extends the internet of things (IoT) to include not only physical devices, but also people, data, and processes. IoE is the network of networks that connects everything and everyone, enabling new possibilities and opportunities for innovation, efficiency, and value creation.
- IoE devices can be wearable, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, glasses, etc., or non-wearable, such as sensors, actuators, cameras, etc. They can also be connected to other devices or networks, such as smartphones, laptops, or the internet, to collect, analyze, and share data or services.

## Learning objectives

- To understand the basic concepts and definitions of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
- To learn about the different types, components, and functions of wearable devices, mixed reality devices, and IoE devices.
- To explore the applications, opportunities, and challenges of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything in various domains and scenarios.
- To appreciate the role of basic electronics in designing, developing, and testing wearable devices, mixed reality devices, and IoE devices.
