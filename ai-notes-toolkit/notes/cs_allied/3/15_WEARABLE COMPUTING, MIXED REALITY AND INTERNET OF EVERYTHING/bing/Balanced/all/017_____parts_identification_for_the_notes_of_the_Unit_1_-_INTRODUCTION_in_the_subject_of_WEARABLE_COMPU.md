# Parts Identification

- Wearable computing is a field of computer science that deals with the design, development and application of wearable devices that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing or accessories.
- Wearable devices can perform various functions, such as sensing, computing, communicating, displaying, interacting, or augmenting the user's capabilities or experiences.
- Wearable devices can be classified into different categories based on their form factor, functionality, application domain, or user interface.
- Some examples of wearable devices are smart watches, smart glasses, smart clothing, smart jewelry, fitness trackers, head-mounted displays, body-worn cameras, wearable sensors, etc.
- Mixed reality is a field of computer science that deals with the combination of real and virtual environments, where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- Mixed reality can be seen as a spectrum that ranges from augmented reality, where virtual objects are overlaid on the real world, to virtual reality, where the user is fully immersed in a synthetic environment.
- Mixed reality can be achieved by using various technologies, such as computer vision, graphics, audio, haptics, tracking, etc.
- Mixed reality can be used for various purposes, such as entertainment, education, training, simulation, gaming, art, etc.
- Some examples of mixed reality devices are Microsoft HoloLens, Oculus Rift, HTC Vive, Google Cardboard, etc.
- Internet of Everything is a field of computer science that deals with the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things through the internet, enabling new capabilities, opportunities, and experiences.
- Internet of Everything can be seen as an extension of the Internet of Things, where not only physical devices, but also humans and other entities can communicate and exchange information through the internet.
- Internet of Everything can be achieved by using various technologies, such as cloud computing, big data, artificial intelligence, machine learning, blockchain, etc.
- Internet of Everything can be used for various purposes, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, smart transportation, smart industry, etc.
- Some examples of Internet of Everything devices are smartphones, tablets, laptops, smart speakers, smart TVs, smart appliances, smart meters, smart cars, etc.
