### Push and Pull

Push and pull are two fundamental concepts in the field of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence, particularly in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.

1. **Push** refers to the act of sending information or data from one device or system to another, without the recipient actively requesting it. This can be seen in the form of notifications, alerts, or updates that are automatically sent to a user's device.

2. **Pull**, on the other hand, refers to the act of actively requesting information or data from a device or system. This can be seen in the form of a user actively searching for information, or requesting an update from a device or system.

In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, push and pull can be used to facilitate seamless interaction and communication between devices, systems, and users. For example, a wearable device may push notifications to a user's smartphone, while the user may pull information from the device to view their fitness data or control their smart home devices.

Understanding the concepts of push and pull is essential for designing effective and user-friendly systems in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
