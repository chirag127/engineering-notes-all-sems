### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Basic Electronics is the study of electronic components, circuits, and systems used in the design and application of electronic devices.
2. It includes the study of passive components such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors, as well as active components such as diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits.
3. Basic Electronics also covers the principles of circuit analysis and design, including Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Laws, and Thevenin's and Norton's Theorems.
4. Understanding Basic Electronics is essential for the design and development of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything devices, as these technologies rely heavily on electronic components and systems.
5. Wearable computing refers to the integration of computing devices into clothing or accessories that can be worn on the body.
6. Mixed reality is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time.
7. The Internet of Everything is the concept of connecting any device with an on/off switch to the Internet and/or to each other, allowing for the exchange of data and the creation of smart systems.
8. Basic Electronics plays a crucial role in the development of these technologies, as it provides the foundation for the design and implementation of the electronic systems that enable their functionality.
