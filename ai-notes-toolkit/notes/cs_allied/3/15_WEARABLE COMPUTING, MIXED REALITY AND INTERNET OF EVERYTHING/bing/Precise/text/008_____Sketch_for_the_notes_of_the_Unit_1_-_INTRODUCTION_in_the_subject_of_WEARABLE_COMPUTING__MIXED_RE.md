### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. **Wearable Computing** refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories to enhance the functionality and convenience of the wearer.
2. **Mixed Reality** is a hybrid of virtual and augmented reality, where virtual and physical objects coexist and interact in real-time.
3. **Internet of Everything** is the concept of connecting all devices and objects to the internet, allowing them to communicate and interact with each other to improve efficiency and convenience.
4. These technologies are rapidly evolving and have the potential to revolutionize the way we live, work, and interact with the world around us.
5. This unit will provide an introduction to these technologies, their applications, and their potential impact on society.
