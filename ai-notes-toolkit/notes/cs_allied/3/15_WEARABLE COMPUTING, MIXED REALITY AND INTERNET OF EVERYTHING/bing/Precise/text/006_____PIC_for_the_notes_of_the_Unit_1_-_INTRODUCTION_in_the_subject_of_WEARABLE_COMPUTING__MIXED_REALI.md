### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- PIC stands for Peripheral Interface Controller.
- It is a family of microcontrollers developed by Microchip Technology.
- PIC microcontrollers are popular in the development of embedded systems, including wearable computing devices.
- PIC microcontrollers are known for their ease of use, low cost, and wide availability.
- They are commonly used in the development of wearable devices such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and health monitors.
- PIC microcontrollers can interface with a variety of sensors and other components, making them ideal for use in wearable computing applications.
- In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, PIC microcontrollers can play a crucial role in the development of innovative and advanced devices.
