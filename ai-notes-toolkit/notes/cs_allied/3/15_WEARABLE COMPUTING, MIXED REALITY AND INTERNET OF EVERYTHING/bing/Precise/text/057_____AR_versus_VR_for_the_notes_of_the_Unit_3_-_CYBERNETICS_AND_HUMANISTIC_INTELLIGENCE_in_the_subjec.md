### AR versus VR

- **AR** stands for **Augmented Reality**. It is a technology that enhances or augments the real world by overlaying digital information on top of it. This can be achieved through the use of devices such as smartphones, tablets, or AR glasses.
- **VR** stands for **Virtual Reality**. It is a technology that creates a completely immersive and simulated environment for the user. This is achieved through the use of devices such as VR headsets or VR rooms.
- The main difference between AR and VR is the level of immersion. While AR enhances the real world, VR creates a completely new one.
- AR has a wide range of applications, including gaming, education, and marketing. It can be used to provide additional information or enhance the user's experience in the real world.
- VR, on the other hand, is mainly used for gaming and entertainment. It can also be used for training and simulation, as it allows users to experience situations that would be difficult or dangerous to replicate in the real world.
- Both AR and VR have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with technology and the world around us. However, they are still in the early stages of development and have yet to reach their full potential.
