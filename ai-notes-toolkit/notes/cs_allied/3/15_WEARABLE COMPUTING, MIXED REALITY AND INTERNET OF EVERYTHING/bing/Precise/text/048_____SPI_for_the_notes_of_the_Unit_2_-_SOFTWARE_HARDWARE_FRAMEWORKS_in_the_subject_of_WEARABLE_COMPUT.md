### SPI

- **SPI** stands for **Serial Peripheral Interface** and it is a **synchronous serial communication protocol** that provides full-duplex communication at very high speeds.
- It is a **master-slave type protocol** that provides a simple and low-cost interface between a microcontroller and its peripherals.
- In typical SPI bus mode, only one master device can control multiple independent slave devices.
- The master device is responsible for setting the clock frequency.
- One unique benefit of SPI is the fact that data can be transferred without interruption.
- Typical applications of SPI include Secure Digital cards and liquid crystal displays.
