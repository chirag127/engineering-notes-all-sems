### Software for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing frameworks are collections of electronic modules, components, materials, embedded software, and applications for user interfaces and data analytics.
- These frameworks are used in research projects and assemble work from students and PhDs over the years.
- Some parts of the framework include haptic displays and actuators.
- The Open Wearable Computing Framework is being developed as a central, easily wearable, and hardware-independent computing unit that gives access to an ICT environment.
- Wearable computing software frameworks can be used for intelligence augmentation research and applications.
- These frameworks allow for the easy building of smart glasses apps, relying on built-in voice command, speech recognition, computer vision, UI, sensors, smartphone connection, NLP, facial recognition, database, cloud connection, and more.
- Body Sensor Networks (BSNs) have become prominent in research and industry as a powerful enabler of novel applications in human-centered domains.
