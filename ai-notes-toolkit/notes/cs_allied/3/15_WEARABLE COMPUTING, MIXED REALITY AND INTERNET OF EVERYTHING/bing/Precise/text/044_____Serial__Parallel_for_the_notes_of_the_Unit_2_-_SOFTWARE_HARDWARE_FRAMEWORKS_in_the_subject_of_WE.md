### Serial & Parallel

Serial and parallel are two types of communication methods used in the transmission of data between devices.

1. **Serial communication** is the process of sending data one bit at a time, sequentially, over a communication channel or computer bus. This is in contrast to parallel communication, where several bits are sent as a whole, on a link with several parallel channels.

2. **Parallel communication** is a method of transmitting several data signals simultaneously over multiple channels. This is in contrast to serial communication, where data is sent one bit at a time over a single channel.

In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, both serial and parallel communication methods can be used to transmit data between devices. The choice of communication method depends on factors such as the amount of data being transmitted, the speed of transmission required, and the capabilities of the devices involved.
