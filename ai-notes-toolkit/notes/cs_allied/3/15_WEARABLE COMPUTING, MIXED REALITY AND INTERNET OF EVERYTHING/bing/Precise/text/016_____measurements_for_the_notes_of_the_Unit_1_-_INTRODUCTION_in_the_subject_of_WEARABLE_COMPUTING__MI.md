### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Measurements in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing devices are electronic devices that are worn on the body, either as an accessory or as part of the clothing. These devices can perform many of the same computing tasks as mobile phones and laptop computers. Measurements in wearable computing include the size and weight of the device, battery life, processing power, and sensor accuracy.

2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time. Measurements in mixed reality include the field of view, resolution, refresh rate, and tracking accuracy.

3. Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that extends the Internet of Things (IoT) by including people, processes, and data in addition to connected devices. Measurements in IoE include the number of connected devices, the amount of data generated, and the speed and reliability of data transmission.

These are some of the key measurements in the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything. These measurements are important for evaluating the performance and capabilities of devices and systems in these fields.
