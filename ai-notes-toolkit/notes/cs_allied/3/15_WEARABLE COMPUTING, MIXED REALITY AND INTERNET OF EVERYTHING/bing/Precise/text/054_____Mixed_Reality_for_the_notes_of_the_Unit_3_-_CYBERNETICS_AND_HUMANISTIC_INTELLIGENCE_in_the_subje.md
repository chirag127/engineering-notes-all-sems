### Mixed Reality

Mixed Reality (MR) is a technology that combines the real and virtual worlds to create a hybrid environment where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real-time. It is a form of augmented reality that allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world.

Some key points to consider when studying Mixed Reality for Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING are:

1. Mixed Reality is a combination of real and virtual worlds, creating a hybrid environment.
2. It allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world.
3. Mixed Reality is a form of augmented reality.
4. It has applications in various fields such as gaming, education, and healthcare.
5. Wearable devices such as head-mounted displays are commonly used to experience Mixed Reality.
