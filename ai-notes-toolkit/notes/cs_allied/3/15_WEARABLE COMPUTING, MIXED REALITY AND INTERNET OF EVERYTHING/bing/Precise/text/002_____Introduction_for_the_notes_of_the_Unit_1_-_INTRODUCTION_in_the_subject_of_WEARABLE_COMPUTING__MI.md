### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Wearable computing refers to the use of small, portable electronic devices that are worn on the body or attached to clothing.
2. These devices can perform a wide range of functions, from tracking fitness and health data to providing navigation and communication capabilities.
3. Mixed reality is a technology that combines elements of both virtual and augmented reality, allowing users to interact with digital content in the real world.
4. The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of all devices and objects, allowing them to communicate and share data with each other.
5. These technologies have the potential to revolutionize the way we live, work, and interact with the world around us.
