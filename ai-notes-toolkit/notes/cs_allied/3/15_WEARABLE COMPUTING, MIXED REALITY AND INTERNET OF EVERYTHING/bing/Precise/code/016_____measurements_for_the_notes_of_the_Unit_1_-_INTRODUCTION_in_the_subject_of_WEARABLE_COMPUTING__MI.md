### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Measurements in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

1. Wearable Computing: 
    - Measurements in wearable computing involve the use of sensors and other devices to collect data about the user's physical and physiological state.
    - Common measurements include heart rate, body temperature, and movement data.
    - These measurements can be used to track fitness, monitor health, and provide personalized feedback to the user.

2. Mixed Reality:
    - Measurements in mixed reality involve the use of sensors and other devices to collect data about the user's physical environment and interactions with virtual objects.
    - Common measurements include position, orientation, and movement data.
    - These measurements can be used to create immersive and interactive experiences for the user.

3. Internet of Everything:
    - Measurements in the Internet of Everything involve the collection of data from a wide variety of connected devices and sensors.
    - Common measurements include environmental data, such as temperature and humidity, as well as data from smart home devices, such as energy usage and security data.
    - These measurements can be used to improve efficiency, increase safety, and provide personalized experiences for the user.
