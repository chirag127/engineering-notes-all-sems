### AR versus VR for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- **AR** stands for **Augmented Reality**. It is a technology that superimposes a computer-generated image on a user's view of the real world, thus providing a composite view.
- **VR** stands for **Virtual Reality**. It is a technology that creates a simulated environment, which can be similar to or completely different from the real world.
- The main difference between AR and VR is the level of immersion. VR is a fully immersive experience, where the user is completely surrounded by a virtual environment. AR, on the other hand, enhances the real world by adding virtual elements to it.
- AR and VR have different applications. AR is often used in gaming, education, and marketing, while VR is used in gaming, entertainment, and training.
- Both AR and VR have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with technology and the world around us. However, they are still in the early stages of development and have yet to reach their full potential.
- In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, AR and VR can play a significant role in enhancing our ability to interact with and control the technology around us.
