### Iterative Coding Methodology

Iterative coding methodology is a software development process where the development is broken down into smaller, more manageable parts known as iterations. Each iteration is a complete development loop resulting in a release of a working product. This approach allows for continuous feedback and refinement of the product.

Here are some key points to note about iterative coding methodology:

1. Iterative coding methodology is an approach to software development that emphasizes the breaking down of the overall development process into smaller, more manageable parts known as iterations.

2. Each iteration is a complete development loop, including planning, design, coding, testing, and review.

3. The goal of each iteration is to produce a working product that can be released to the customer for feedback.

4. The feedback from the customer is then used to refine the product in the next iteration.

5. This approach allows for continuous feedback and refinement of the product, resulting in a more refined and higher quality final product.

6. Iterative coding methodology is often used in agile software development methodologies such as Scrum and Extreme Programming (XP).

7. This approach is particularly useful for complex projects where requirements may change or evolve over time.

8. Iterative coding methodology can help to reduce the risk of project failure by allowing for early detection and correction of issues.

9. It can also help to improve customer satisfaction by involving the customer in the development process and allowing for continuous feedback.

10. Overall, iterative coding methodology is a flexible and adaptive approach to software development that can help to improve the quality and success of software projects.
