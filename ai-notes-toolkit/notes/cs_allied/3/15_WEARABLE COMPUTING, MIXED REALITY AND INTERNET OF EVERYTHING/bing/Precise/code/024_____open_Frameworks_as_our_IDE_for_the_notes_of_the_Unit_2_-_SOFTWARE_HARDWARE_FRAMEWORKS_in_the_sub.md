### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS: openFrameworks as our IDE

- openFrameworks is an open-source C++ toolkit designed to assist the creative process by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation.
- It is commonly used for developing applications in the fields of art, design, and research.
- openFrameworks is designed to work as a general-purpose glue, and wraps together several commonly used libraries under a tidy interface.
- It can be used to develop standalone applications, as well as to develop plugins for other software such as Max/MSP or Adobe Creative Suite.
- openFrameworks is cross-platform and can be run on various operating systems including Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.
- It provides a simple and intuitive API for working with graphics, sound, video, computer vision, networking, and more.
- openFrameworks is community-driven and has a large and active community of developers and artists contributing to its development and sharing their work with others.
- It is a powerful tool for developing interactive and multimedia applications, and is widely used in the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
