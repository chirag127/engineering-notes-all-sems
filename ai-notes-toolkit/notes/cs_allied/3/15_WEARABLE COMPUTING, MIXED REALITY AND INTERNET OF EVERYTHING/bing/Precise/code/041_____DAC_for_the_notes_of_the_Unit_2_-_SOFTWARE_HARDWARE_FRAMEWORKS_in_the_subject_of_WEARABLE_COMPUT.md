### DAC

DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is a device that converts digital signals into analog signals. In the context of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING, DAC is an important component in the hardware framework.

Some key points to note about DAC are:

1. DAC is used to convert digital data into an analog signal that can be used by analog devices such as speakers, headphones, and amplifiers.
2. The quality of the DAC can affect the quality of the audio output.
3. There are different types of DACs, including R-2R ladder DACs, weighted resistor DACs, and delta-sigma DACs.
4. The resolution of a DAC is determined by the number of bits it can process. A higher resolution DAC can produce a more accurate analog signal.
5. DACs are commonly used in audio and video playback devices, as well as in data acquisition and control systems.
