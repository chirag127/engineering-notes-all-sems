### Microcontrollers

Microcontrollers are small computers that are embedded in a variety of electronic devices. They are designed to perform specific tasks and are often used in applications such as wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.

Some key points to consider when studying microcontrollers for Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING are:

1. Microcontrollers are typically low-power devices that are designed to operate in a wide range of environments.
2. They often include a variety of peripherals, such as timers, analog-to-digital converters, and communication interfaces, that allow them to interact with other devices.
3. Microcontrollers are typically programmed using a high-level language, such as C or C++, and are often used in conjunction with a real-time operating system.
4. The choice of microcontroller for a particular application will depend on factors such as the required processing power, memory size, and peripheral requirements.
5. Microcontrollers are widely used in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, where they provide the computational power and connectivity required to support these applications.
