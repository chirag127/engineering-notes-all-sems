### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Raspberry Pi

- Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation.
- The original model became popular for its low cost and small size, making it suitable for use in education, home automation, and other projects.
- The Raspberry Pi runs a variety of operating systems, including Linux-based distributions such as Raspbian, and Microsoft Windows 10 IoT Core.
- The Raspberry Pi has a Broadcom system on a chip (SoC) with an integrated ARM-compatible central processing unit (CPU) and on-chip graphics processing unit (GPU).
- The Raspberry Pi Foundation provides several resources, including documentation and software, to help users get started with the Raspberry Pi and learn how to use it for various projects.
- The Raspberry Pi has been used in a wide range of applications, including media centers, game consoles, digital signage, and robotics.
- The Raspberry Pi has also been used in education, with many schools and universities using it to teach programming and computer science concepts.
- The Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of users and developers, who share their projects and ideas on the official Raspberry Pi website and other online forums.
