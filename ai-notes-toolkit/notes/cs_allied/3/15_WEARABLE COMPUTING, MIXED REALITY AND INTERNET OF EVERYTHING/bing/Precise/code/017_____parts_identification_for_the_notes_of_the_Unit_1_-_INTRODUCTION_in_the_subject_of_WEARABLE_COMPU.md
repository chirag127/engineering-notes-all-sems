### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Parts Identification

Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything are all emerging technologies that are changing the way we interact with the world around us. Here are some key parts that are important for understanding these technologies:

1. **Sensors**: These are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They can measure things like temperature, light, sound, and motion.

2. **Actuators**: These are devices that convert electrical signals into physical movement. They can be used to control things like motors, lights, and speakers.

3. **Processors**: These are the brains of the system. They take in data from the sensors, process it, and then send commands to the actuators.

4. **Wireless communication**: This allows the system to communicate with other devices and the internet. It can use technologies like Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.

5. **Power sources**: These provide the energy needed to run the system. They can include batteries, solar panels, and kinetic energy harvesters.

6. **Displays**: These are used to present information to the user. They can include screens, projectors, and augmented reality glasses.

7. **Input devices**: These allow the user to interact with the system. They can include buttons, touchscreens, and voice recognition.

These are just some of the key parts that make up wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything systems. Understanding these parts and how they work together is essential for understanding these technologies.
