### History for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing refers to the use of computer technology that is integrated into clothing or accessories and can be worn on the body.
- The concept of wearable computing has been around for several decades, with early examples including calculator watches and hearing aids.
- In the 1990s, the development of more advanced wearable computers, such as head-mounted displays and body-worn sensors, began to gain momentum.
- Mixed reality is a term that refers to the merging of real and virtual worlds to create new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real-time.
- The concept of mixed reality has been around since the 1970s, with early examples including virtual reality and augmented reality technologies.
- The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things through the use of the internet.
- The term "Internet of Everything" was first coined by Cisco in 2012, and it is seen as an extension of the Internet of Things (IoT) concept.
- The IoE is expected to have a significant impact on various industries, including healthcare, transportation, and manufacturing, by enabling the collection and analysis of large amounts of data to improve efficiency and decision-making.
