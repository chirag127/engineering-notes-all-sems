### History for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. The concept of Mixed Reality was first introduced in 1994 by Paul Milgram and Fumio Kishino. They introduced the idea of exploring the concept of a virtuality continuum and the taxonomy of visual displays .
2. The history of wearable computing devices can be divided into three main time periods: Early Wearable Technology (1700–1907s), Wearable Computing using Modern Electronics (1960–2005), and Advanced Commercial Wearable Computing (2006—present).
3. By 1994, a wearable computer capable of continuously recording interactions with people and devices was developed. This was called “Forget-Me-Not” and it featured wireless transmitters.
4. Humans have always adorned their bodies with gadgetry — be it for show, for utility, or both.
