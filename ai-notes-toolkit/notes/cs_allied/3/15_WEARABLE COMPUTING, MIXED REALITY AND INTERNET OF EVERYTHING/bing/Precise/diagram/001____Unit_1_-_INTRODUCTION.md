## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter or unit of any subject or topic.
2. It provides an overview of the subject or topic being studied.
3. It sets the foundation for the rest of the course by introducing key concepts, theories, and principles.
4. It is important to pay attention to the introduction as it helps to understand the context and scope of the subject or topic.
5. The introduction may also include the objectives and goals of the course, as well as the expected outcomes for the students.
6. It is recommended to take notes and actively engage with the material presented in the introduction to ensure a solid understanding of the subject or topic.
