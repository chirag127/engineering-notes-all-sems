### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

#### Wearable Computing
- Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories.
- These devices can perform a range of functions, from simple tasks such as displaying the time to more complex functions such as monitoring health and fitness.
- Wearable computing devices include smartwatches, fitness trackers, and smart glasses.

#### Mixed Reality
- Mixed reality is a technology that combines elements of virtual reality and augmented reality.
- It allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world, creating a seamless blend of the physical and digital worlds.
- Mixed reality has applications in gaming, entertainment, and education, among other fields.

#### Internet of Everything
- The Internet of Everything (IoE) refers to the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things through the internet.
- It is an extension of the Internet of Things (IoT), which focuses on the interconnection of devices.
- IoE has the potential to revolutionize industries such as healthcare, transportation, and manufacturing by enabling new levels of automation and efficiency.
