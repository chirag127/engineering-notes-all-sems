### Introduction

Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories that can be worn on the body.
2. Mixed reality is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time.
3. The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that extends the Internet of Things (IoT) to include people, processes, data, and things, all connected and communicating with each other to create new value and opportunities.
4. These technologies are rapidly evolving and have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with the world around us.
5. This unit will provide an introduction to these concepts and explore their potential applications and implications.
