### Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

- **openFrameworks** is an open-source C++ toolkit designed to assist the creative process by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation.

- **Humanistic Intelligence (HI)** is intelligence that arises when a human is part of the feedback loop of a computational process in which the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.

- Over the past 20 years, **wearable computing** has emerged as the perfect tool for embodying humanistic intelligence.

- Wearable computing allows for the creation of **mixed reality** environments, where the physical and digital worlds are seamlessly integrated.

- The **Internet of Everything** (IoE) is a concept that refers to the interconnection of all devices and objects, allowing them to communicate and share data with each other.

- The combination of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything has the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with technology and the world around us.

- By incorporating humanistic intelligence into these technologies, we can create more intuitive and natural interfaces, allowing for a more seamless integration of technology into our daily lives.
