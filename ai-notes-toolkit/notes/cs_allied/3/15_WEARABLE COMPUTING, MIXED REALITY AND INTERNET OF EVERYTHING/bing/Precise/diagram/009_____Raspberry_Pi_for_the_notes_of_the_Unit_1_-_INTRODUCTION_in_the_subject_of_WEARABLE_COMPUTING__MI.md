### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, credit card-sized computer that can be connected to a computer monitor or TV using HDMI, and uses a standard keyboard and mouse. It can run a host of operating systems, such as Raspbian (Debian Linux), Android, Windows 10, IoT Core, etc. 

Raspberry Pi can provide the right platform for experimentation when combined with the Internet of Things, which involves sensors, devices, cloud, data, etc. It can be used in weather security and temperature sensors, as a central controller for other low-powered controllers, and as a media center with simple input/output variations. 

Raspberry Pi computers have been put in almost everything, including clothing and devices to keep on your person, creating what are called wearables. You can now get kits and products that act as wearables, allowing you to upgrade your fashion sense with a bit of Pi. 

Some wearable Raspberry Pi projects include Google Glass, the privacy-breaching prototype and later limited release headset equipped with augmented reality.
