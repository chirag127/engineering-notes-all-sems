### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

PIC stands for Peripheral Interface Controller. It is a family of microcontrollers developed by Microchip Technology. PIC microcontrollers are popular in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything due to their versatility and ease of use.

Some key points to note about PIC microcontrollers are:
- They are available in a wide range of sizes and capabilities, making them suitable for a variety of applications.
- They are known for their low power consumption, making them ideal for battery-powered devices.
- They have a rich set of peripherals, including timers, analog-to-digital converters, and communication interfaces, allowing them to interface with a wide range of sensors and other devices.
- They are easy to program, with a large community of developers and a wealth of resources available online.

In the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, PIC microcontrollers can be used to:
- Collect and process data from sensors, such as accelerometers, gyroscopes, and heart rate monitors.
- Control actuators, such as motors and displays, to provide feedback to the user.
- Communicate with other devices, such as smartphones and cloud services, to share data and receive commands.

Overall, PIC microcontrollers are a powerful and versatile tool for developing wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything applications. They provide a solid foundation for building complex systems, while remaining accessible to developers of all skill levels.
