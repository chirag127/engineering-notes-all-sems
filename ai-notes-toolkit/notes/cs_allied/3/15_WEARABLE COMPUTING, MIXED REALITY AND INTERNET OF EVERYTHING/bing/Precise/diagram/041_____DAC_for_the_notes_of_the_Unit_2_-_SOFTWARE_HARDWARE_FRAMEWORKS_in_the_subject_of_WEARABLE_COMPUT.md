### DAC

DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is a device that converts digital signals into analog signals. In the context of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING, DACs are important because they allow digital devices to interact with the analog world.

Some key points to remember about DACs are:

1. DACs convert digital signals into analog signals.
2. They are used in many applications, including audio and video playback, and control systems.
3. The accuracy of a DAC is determined by its resolution, which is the number of bits used to represent the analog signal.
4. There are several types of DACs, including binary-weighted, R-2R ladder, and sigma-delta DACs.
5. The choice of DAC depends on the application and the required accuracy and speed.
