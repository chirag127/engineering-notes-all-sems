### ADC for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

ADC stands for Analog-to-Digital Converter. It is an electronic circuit that measures a real-world signal (such as temperature, pressure, acceleration, and speed) and converts it to a digital representation of the signal.

- ADCs are used in various applications, including wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
- ADCs can be used to measure and convert various real-world signals, such as temperature, pressure, acceleration, and speed, into digital signals that can be processed by computers and other digital devices.
- ADCs are essential components in the development of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, as they enable these technologies to interact with the physical world.
