### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

In the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything, the software hardware frameworks play a crucial role. Here are some key points to consider:

1. **Wearable Computing** refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories to enhance the user's experience and capabilities.
2. **Mixed Reality** is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real-time.
3. **Internet of Everything** is the concept of connecting any device with an on and off switch to the Internet and/or to each other, including people, processes, data, and things.
4. These technologies require a combination of hardware and software frameworks to function effectively.
5. Hardware frameworks include sensors, processors, displays, and communication modules that enable the collection, processing, and transmission of data.
6. Software frameworks provide the necessary tools and platforms for the development and deployment of applications and services that utilize the data collected by the hardware.
7. Together, these frameworks enable the creation of advanced wearable, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything systems that can enhance the user's experience and capabilities.
