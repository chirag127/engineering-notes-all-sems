### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Parts Identification

Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything are all emerging technologies that are changing the way we interact with the world around us. Here are some key parts that are essential to these technologies:

1. **Sensors**: These are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment, such as temperature, light, or motion. They are used in wearable devices to track activity, monitor health, and provide feedback to the user.

2. **Processors**: These are the brains of the device, responsible for processing the data collected by the sensors and making decisions based on that data. They are essential for the functioning of wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and the Internet of Everything.

3. **Displays**: These are the screens or other visual interfaces that present information to the user. They can range from small screens on wearable devices to large, immersive displays in mixed reality systems.

4. **Connectivity**: This refers to the ability of the device to connect to other devices and networks, such as the Internet or a local network. This is essential for the functioning of the Internet of Everything, as it allows devices to communicate with each other and share data.

5. **Power**: Wearable devices, mixed reality systems, and the Internet of Everything all require power to function. This can come from batteries, external power sources, or even energy harvesting technologies.

6. **User Interface**: This refers to the way the user interacts with the device, such as through touch, voice, or gesture controls. The user interface is an essential part of the user experience and can greatly affect the usability of the device.

These are just some of the key parts that are essential to the functioning of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything. As these technologies continue to evolve, new parts and components will undoubtedly be developed to enhance their capabilities.
