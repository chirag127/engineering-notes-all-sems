### Desktop
- A desktop is a personal computer that is designed to be used at a single location, typically on a desk or table.
- Desktops are typically larger and more powerful than laptops or other portable devices.
- They are commonly used for tasks such as word processing, web browsing, and gaming.
- Desktops can be customized and upgraded more easily than laptops, allowing users to add more memory, storage, or processing power as needed.
- Desktops typically have more ports and expansion slots than laptops, allowing for the connection of multiple peripherals such as printers, scanners, and external hard drives.
- Desktops are often less expensive than laptops with similar specifications, making them a popular choice for home and office use.
- Desktops are available in a variety of form factors, including towers, all-in-ones, and small form factor PCs.
- Desktops typically run on either the Windows or macOS operating system, although Linux is also a popular choice for some users.
- Desktops are an important part of the hardware framework in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, as they provide the processing power and storage needed to run complex applications and store large amounts of data.
