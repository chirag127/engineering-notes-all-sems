# Oculus Rift

Oculus Rift is a line of virtual reality headsets developed and manufactured by Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms. It was released on March 28, 2016 . The Oculus Rift was invented by VR enthusiast Palmer Luckey, who later went on to found Oculus VR with Brendan Iribe, who became CEO . The Oculus Rift itself is a pair of virtual reality goggles, complete with headphones, that connect to a gaming PC or laptop .
