# Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE
## AR versus VR

- **AR** stands for **Augmented Reality**. It is a technology that enhances or augments the real world by overlaying digital information on top of it.
- **VR** stands for **Virtual Reality**. It is a technology that creates a completely immersive and simulated environment for the user to experience.
- The main difference between AR and VR is the level of immersion. While VR completely replaces the user's reality with a simulated one, AR enhances the user's reality by adding digital information to it.
- AR can be experienced through devices such as smartphones, tablets, and AR glasses, while VR requires specialized headsets or goggles.
- AR has a wide range of applications, including gaming, education, and marketing. VR, on the other hand, is mainly used for gaming and entertainment, but also has potential in fields such as therapy and training.
- Both AR and VR have the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with technology and the world around us.
