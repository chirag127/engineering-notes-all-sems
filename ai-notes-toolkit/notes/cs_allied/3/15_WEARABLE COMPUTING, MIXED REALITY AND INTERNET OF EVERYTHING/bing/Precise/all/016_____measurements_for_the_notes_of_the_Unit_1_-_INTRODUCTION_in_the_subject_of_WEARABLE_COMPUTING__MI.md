# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Measurements in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything are emerging fields that involve the integration of advanced technologies into our daily lives.
- Measurements play a crucial role in these fields, as they provide the data needed to accurately track, monitor, and analyze various aspects of the user's experience.
- In Wearable Computing, measurements can include biometric data such as heart rate, body temperature, and movement, as well as environmental data such as temperature, humidity, and air quality.
- In Mixed Reality, measurements can include tracking the user's position, orientation, and movement, as well as the position and orientation of virtual objects in the environment.
- In the Internet of Everything, measurements can include data from a wide range of connected devices and sensors, such as smart home appliances, security systems, and environmental monitoring systems.
- Accurate and reliable measurements are essential for the development and implementation of effective Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything systems. They provide the foundation for data analysis, decision making, and user feedback.
