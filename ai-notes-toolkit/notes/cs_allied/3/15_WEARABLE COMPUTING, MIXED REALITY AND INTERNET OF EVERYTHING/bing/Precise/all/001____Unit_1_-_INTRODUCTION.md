## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter or unit of any subject or topic.
2. It provides an overview of the subject or topic being discussed.
3. It sets the foundation for the rest of the subject or topic.
4. It is important to read and understand the introduction before proceeding to the rest of the subject or topic.
5. The introduction may include definitions, background information, and key concepts.
6. It may also provide a brief history of the subject or topic.
7. The introduction is an essential part of any subject or topic and should not be skipped or overlooked.
8. It is important to take notes and review the introduction to ensure a thorough understanding of the subject or topic.
