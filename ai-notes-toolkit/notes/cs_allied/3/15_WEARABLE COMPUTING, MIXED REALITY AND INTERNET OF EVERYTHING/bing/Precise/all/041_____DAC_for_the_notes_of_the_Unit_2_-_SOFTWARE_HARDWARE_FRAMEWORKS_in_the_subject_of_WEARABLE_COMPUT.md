# DAC

DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is a device that converts digital signals into analog signals. In the context of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING, DAC is an important component as it allows digital devices to interact with the analog world.

Some key points to remember about DAC are:

1. DAC converts digital signals into analog signals.
2. It is used in various applications such as audio and video playback, and signal processing.
3. The quality of the DAC can affect the quality of the output signal.
4. There are different types of DACs, including resistor ladder DACs, current steering DACs, and sigma-delta DACs.
5. The resolution of a DAC is determined by the number of bits it can process.
