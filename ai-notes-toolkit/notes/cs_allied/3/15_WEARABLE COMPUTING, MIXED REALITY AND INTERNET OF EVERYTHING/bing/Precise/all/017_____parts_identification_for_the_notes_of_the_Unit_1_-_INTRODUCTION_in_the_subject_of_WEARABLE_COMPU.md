# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### Parts Identification

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing refers to the integration of advanced electronic technologies into clothing and accessories that can be worn on the body. These devices can perform a wide range of functions, from simple information display to complex sensing and processing.

2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality is a technology that combines elements of both virtual reality and augmented reality. It allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world, creating a seamless blend of the physical and digital environments.

3. Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that describes the interconnection of all devices and objects, allowing them to communicate and share data with each other. This includes not only traditional computing devices, but also everyday objects such as appliances, vehicles, and infrastructure.

These three technologies are rapidly advancing and have the potential to revolutionize the way we live, work, and interact with the world around us. They are the key components of the emerging field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
