# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### Raspberry Pi

- Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation.
- The original model became popular for its low cost and small size, making it suitable for use in education, robotics, and home automation.
- The Raspberry Pi runs on a variety of operating systems, including Linux-based distributions such as Raspbian and Ubuntu, as well as Microsoft Windows 10 IoT Core.
- The Raspberry Pi has a range of models, each with different specifications and capabilities.
- The Raspberry Pi can be used for a variety of projects, including media centers, game consoles, and home automation systems.
- The Raspberry Pi Foundation provides extensive documentation and support for the Raspberry Pi, making it accessible to users of all skill levels.
- The Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of users and developers, who share projects, ideas, and support through online forums and social media.
