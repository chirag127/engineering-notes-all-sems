# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### Mobile phones and similar devices

- Mobile phones, also known as cell phones or smartphones, are portable devices that allow users to make phone calls, send text messages, and access the internet.
- Mobile phones have become an integral part of daily life for many people, providing a convenient way to stay connected with others and access information on the go.
- Similar devices include tablets, smartwatches, and other wearable technology that offer similar functionality to mobile phones.
- These devices often run on mobile operating systems such as iOS or Android and can support a wide range of applications.
- Mobile phones and similar devices have revolutionized the way we communicate, access information, and interact with the world around us.
- They have also paved the way for new technologies such as wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
