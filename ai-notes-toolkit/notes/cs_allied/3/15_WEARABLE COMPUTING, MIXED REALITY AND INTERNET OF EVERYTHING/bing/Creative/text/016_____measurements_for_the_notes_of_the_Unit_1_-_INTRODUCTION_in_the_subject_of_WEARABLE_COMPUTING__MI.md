### Measurements for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the field of technology that involves devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, and virtual reality headsets. These devices can collect and analyse data, communicate with other devices, and provide various services to the user. Wearable computing is closely related to the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity to exchange data with other devices and systems  .
- Mixed reality (MR) is a type of immersive technology that combines elements of both virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR). VR creates a fully artificial environment that the user can interact with, while AR overlays digital information onto the real world. MR blends the two, allowing the user to see and manipulate virtual objects that are integrated with the physical environment. MR can be achieved by using wearable devices such as head-mounted displays (HMDs) or smart glasses that can project holograms or 3D models onto the user's field of view .
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is a broader concept than IoT, as it encompasses not only the network of things, but also the network of people, data, and processes. IoE aims to provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment, by leveraging the power of data analytics, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and machine learning. IoE can enable new applications and services that can enhance the quality of life, productivity, efficiency, and innovation in various domains, such as healthcare, education, transportation, manufacturing, and entertainment .
