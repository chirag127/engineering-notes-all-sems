### Push and Pull for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- Push and pull are two different approaches to managing information flow and communication in cybernetic systems.
- Push systems initiate production or action as a reaction to present demand, while pull systems initiate production or action in anticipation of future demand.
- Push systems are more suitable for situations where the demand is predictable, stable, and homogeneous, while pull systems are more suitable for situations where the demand is uncertain, dynamic, and heterogeneous.
- Push systems tend to be more centralized, top-down, and directive, while pull systems tend to be more decentralized, bottom-up, and adaptive.
- Push systems rely on predefined rules, procedures, and schedules, while pull systems rely on feedback, learning, and self-organization.
- Push systems can achieve efficiency, consistency, and standardization, while pull systems can achieve flexibility, responsiveness, and customization.
- Examples of push systems in cybernetics are:
  - GitOps agents that orchestrate deployment to external targets, such as VMs and bare metal.
  - Marketing strategies that create demand for products or services by pushing them to potential customers, such as advertising, promotion, and sales.
  - Security practices that enforce compliance and prevent vulnerabilities by pushing policies and controls to users and systems, such as firewalls, IDS sensors, and training.
- Examples of pull systems in cybernetics are:
  - Algorithmic platforms that co-define visibility and power by pulling user-generated content and data, such as social media, search engines, and recommender systems.
  - Marketing strategies that satisfy demand for products or services by pulling them from potential customers, such as word-of-mouth, referrals, and reviews.
  - Security practices that enable resilience and recovery by pulling resources and capabilities from users and systems, such as backups, encryption, and incident response.
- Push and pull systems can work together in cybernetics to balance the trade-offs and complement the strengths of each approach. For example, a hybrid system can use a push strategy to create awareness and interest, and a pull strategy to generate action and loyalty. Alternatively, a hybrid system can use a pull strategy to sense and respond to changes, and a push strategy to plan and execute actions.
