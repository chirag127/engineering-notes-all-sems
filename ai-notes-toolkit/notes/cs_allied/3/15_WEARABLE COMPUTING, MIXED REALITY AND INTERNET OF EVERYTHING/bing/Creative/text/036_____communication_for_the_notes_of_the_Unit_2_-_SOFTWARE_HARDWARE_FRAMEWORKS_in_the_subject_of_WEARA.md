### Communication for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Communication is the process of exchanging information and data between different devices, systems, or people.
- Communication in wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything involves the use of various technologies and protocols to enable seamless and interactive communication across different domains and platforms.
- Some of the key aspects of communication in these fields are:

  - Wearable computing: Wearable computing refers to the use of wearable devices that provide constant access to computing and communication resources. Wearable devices can be used as communication tools to enable remote collaboration, social interaction, and information sharing. For example, WearCom is a wearable communication space that allows multiple remote people to communicate with a wearable computer user using virtual reality techniques.
  - Mixed reality: Mixed reality refers to the blending of the real world and the virtual world, creating immersive experiences where 3D digital images can be represented alongside physical objects. Mixed reality can enhance communication by providing rich and contextual information, augmenting human senses and capabilities, and enabling new forms of interaction and collaboration. For example, mixed reality can be used to create virtual avatars, holograms, and telepresence systems that can communicate with people across different locations .
  - Internet of everything: Internet of everything refers to the interconnection of people, processes, data, and things through the internet. Internet of everything can enable communication by providing ubiquitous and pervasive access to information and services, facilitating data exchange and analysis, and creating new opportunities for innovation and value creation. For example, internet of everything can be used to connect wearable devices and sensors with other devices and physical things, creating a network of smart and responsive objects that can communicate and interact with each other .

- Some of the challenges and issues of communication in these fields are:

  - Security and privacy: Communication in these fields involves the transmission and storage of sensitive and personal data, which can pose risks of unauthorized access, misuse, or leakage. Security and privacy measures are needed to protect the data and the users from malicious attacks, breaches, or violations.
  - Compatibility and interoperability: Communication in these fields involves the use of diverse and heterogeneous devices, systems, and platforms, which can have different standards, protocols, and formats. Compatibility and interoperability issues can arise when the devices, systems, and platforms are not able to communicate or work with each other effectively or efficiently.
  - User experience and acceptance: Communication in these fields involves the use of novel and complex technologies and interfaces, which can have different effects on the user's perception, cognition, and behavior. User experience and acceptance issues can arise when the technologies and interfaces are not user-friendly, intuitive, or comfortable, or when they cause negative impacts such as distraction, confusion, or discomfort.
