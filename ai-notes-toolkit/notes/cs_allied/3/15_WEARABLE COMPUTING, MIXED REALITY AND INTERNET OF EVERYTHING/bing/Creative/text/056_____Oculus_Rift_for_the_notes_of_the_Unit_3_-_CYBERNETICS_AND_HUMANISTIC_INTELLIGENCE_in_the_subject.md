### Oculus Rift

- Oculus Rift is a line of **virtual reality headsets** developed and manufactured by Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms.
- Oculus Rift was invented by **Palmer Luckey**, a VR enthusiast, who later founded Oculus VR with **Brendan Iribe**, who became CEO.
- Oculus Rift connects to a **gaming PC or laptop** and uses **sensors** and **controllers** to track the user's head and hand movements .
- Oculus Rift was released on **March 28, 2016**, after a successful **Kickstarter campaign** in 2012 that raised over $2.4 million.
- Oculus Rift was discontinued in **2020**, and replaced by **Oculus Rift S**, which has improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics .
- Oculus Rift is one of the most popular and influential VR headsets, and has a large library of **VR apps, games, and experiences** available on the Oculus store and other platforms .
- Oculus Rift is an example of **cybernetics and humanistic intelligence**, as it creates a **mixed reality** environment that enhances the user's perception, cognition, and interaction with the virtual world.
