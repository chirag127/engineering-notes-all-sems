### Audio for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Software hardware frameworks are the tools and platforms that enable the development and deployment of applications for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything (IoT).
- Wearable computing refers to the integration of computing devices and sensors into clothing or accessories that can be worn by users, such as smart watches, glasses, headsets, etc.
- Mixed reality (MR) refers to the blending of physical and digital worlds, where users can interact with both real and virtual objects and environments, such as augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR).
- Internet of everything (IoT) refers to the network of physical objects and devices that are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other and with cloud services, such as smart home appliances, vehicles, industrial machines, etc.
- Some of the common software hardware frameworks for wearable computing, mixed reality and IoT are:

  - Azure IoT: A cloud-based platform that provides various services and tools for building, managing and securing IoT solutions, such as device provisioning, data ingestion, analytics, edge computing, digital twins, etc.
  - UbiVis: A framework that enables ubiquitous mixed reality applications, where users can access and manipulate virtual content from different devices and platforms, such as smartphones, tablets, head-mounted displays, etc.
  - MRCP: A secure mixed reality framework for the IoT, that allows users to interact with headless IoT devices (devices without screens or keyboards) using voice and gesture commands, such as smart parking meters, smart locks, etc.
  - Unity: A cross-platform game engine and development environment that supports various platforms and devices for creating immersive and interactive 3D and 2D applications, such as games, simulations, AR and VR experiences, etc.
  - Arduino: An open-source hardware and software platform that consists of microcontroller boards, sensors, actuators, and a programming language and IDE, that can be used for creating various IoT projects and prototypes, such as wearable devices, robots, etc.
