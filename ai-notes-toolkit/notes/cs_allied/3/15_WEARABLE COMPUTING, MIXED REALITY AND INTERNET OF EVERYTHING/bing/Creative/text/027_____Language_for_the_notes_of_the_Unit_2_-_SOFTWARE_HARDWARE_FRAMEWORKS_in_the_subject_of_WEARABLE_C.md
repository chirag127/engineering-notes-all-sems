### Language for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Software hardware frameworks are systems that combine software and hardware components to enable the development and deployment of applications that involve wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything.
- Wearable computing is the use of wearable devices that can sense, process and communicate data, and provide feedback to the user or the environment.
- Mixed reality is the blending of physical and virtual worlds, where real and digital objects coexist and interact in real time.
- Internet of everything is the concept of connecting any device, object or entity to the internet and to each other, enabling data exchange, analysis and automation.
- Some examples of software hardware frameworks are:
  - MIRELA: a high-level language and a rapid prototyping framework dedicated to systems where virtual and digital objects coexist in the same environment and interact in real time.
  - UbiVis: a framework that tackles challenges emerging from using different hardware for the current mixed reality experience, such as hardware compatibility, synchronization and collaboration.
  - Azure IoT: a cloud platform that provides services and tools for building, managing and securing IoT applications, such as digital twins, mixed reality and autonomous systems.
  - XRI: a mixed-reality internet-of-things architectural framework that aims to enable the seamless integration of physical and virtual worlds, and support the development of immersive and interactive applications.
- Some challenges and opportunities of software hardware frameworks are:
  - Designing user interfaces and interactions that are intuitive, natural and adaptive to different contexts and devices.
  - Ensuring the security, privacy and trustworthiness of the data and devices involved in the applications.
  - Leveraging the computational and communication capabilities of the cloud and the edge to enhance the performance and scalability of the applications.
  - Exploring the potential of software hardware frameworks for various domains and scenarios, such as education, entertainment, health, industry and smart cities.
