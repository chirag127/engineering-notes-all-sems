### PHP/Web for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- PHP is a server-side scripting language that can create dynamic and interactive web pages.
- PHP can run on various platforms, such as Windows, Linux, Unix, Mac OS X, etc.
- PHP can work with different web servers, such as Apache, IIS, etc.
- PHP can connect to various databases, such as MySQL, Oracle, PostgreSQL, etc.
- PHP is free and easy to learn.
- PHP can be embedded in HTML or used as a standalone file.
- PHP can handle forms, cookies, sessions, file uploads, and other web features.
- PHP can also use object-oriented programming, exception handling, namespaces, and other advanced concepts.

- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines.
- Cybernetics studies the principles of feedback, adaptation, self-organization, and learning in complex systems.
- Cybernetics can be applied to various domains, such as biology, psychology, sociology, engineering, management, etc.
- Cybernetics can also help to understand the nature of human intelligence, cognition, and behavior.
- Cybernetics can also provide methods and tools for designing and evaluating wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything systems.

- Wearable computing is the integration of computing devices and sensors into clothing and accessories that can be worn by humans.
- Wearable computing can provide various functions, such as health monitoring, fitness tracking, navigation, communication, entertainment, etc.
- Wearable computing can also enhance human capabilities, such as perception, memory, cognition, and interaction.
- Wearable computing can also create new forms of expression, such as augmented reality, virtual reality, and mixed reality.

- Mixed reality is the combination of real and virtual environments that can be experienced by humans.
- Mixed reality can create immersive and interactive experiences that can augment or transform the perception of reality.
- Mixed reality can also enable new forms of communication, collaboration, and learning across physical and digital spaces.
- Mixed reality can also challenge the boundaries between human and machine, self and others, and physical and virtual.

- Internet of everything is the network of physical and digital objects that can communicate and exchange data with each other and with humans.
- Internet of everything can enable various applications, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart industries, smart agriculture, etc.
- Internet of everything can also provide new opportunities for innovation, efficiency, and sustainability.
- Internet of everything can also raise new challenges and risks, such as privacy, security, ethics, and governance.
