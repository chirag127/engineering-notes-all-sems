### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is a field of technology that involves creating electronic devices that can be worn on the body, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, etc.
- Wearable devices can collect and analyze data, communicate with other devices, and provide feedback or services to the user or the environment.
- Wearable devices are an example of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is a network of physical objects that can interact with each other and the internet through sensors, software, and connectivity.
- IoT enables data exchange, automation, and optimization of various processes and applications in different domains, such as health, sports, entertainment, education, security, etc.
- Mixed reality (MR) is a type of immersive technology that combines elements of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) to create a hybrid experience of the real and the virtual worlds.
- MR can enhance the perception and interaction of the user with the environment, and enable new forms of communication, learning, and entertainment.
- MR can be achieved by using various devices, such as head-mounted displays, smart glasses, smartphones, tablets, etc.
- Basic electronics is the study of the principles and applications of electronic components, circuits, and systems that are essential for creating and operating wearable, IoT, and MR devices.
- Basic electronics covers topics such as voltage, current, resistance, Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, power, energy, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, amplifiers, oscillators, filters, etc.
- Basic electronics also involves using tools and techniques for designing, testing, and troubleshooting electronic circuits and systems, such as breadboards, multimeters, oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, simulation software, etc.
