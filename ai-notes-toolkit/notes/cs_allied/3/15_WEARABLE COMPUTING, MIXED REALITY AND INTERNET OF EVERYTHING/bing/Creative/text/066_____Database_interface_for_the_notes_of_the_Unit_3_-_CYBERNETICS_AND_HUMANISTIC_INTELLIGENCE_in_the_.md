### Database interface for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is a new signal processing framework in which (1) intelligence arises by having the human being in the feedback loop of the computational process, and (2) the processing apparatus is inextricably intertwined with the natural capabilities of the human mind and body.
- Wearable computing is the use of portable devices that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing, such as smart glasses, smart watches, smart earbuds, etc.
- Mixed reality (MR) is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where real and virtual objects coexist and interact in real time.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the concept of connecting people, processes, data, and things to the internet and each other, enabling new levels of collaboration, innovation, and efficiency.
- A database interface is a software component that allows users to interact with a database, such as creating, querying, updating, or deleting data.
- A database interface for the notes of the Unit 3 would allow users to store, retrieve, and manipulate the information related to the topics of cybernetics, humanistic intelligence, wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
- Some possible features of a database interface for the notes of the Unit 3 are:

  - A user-friendly graphical user interface (GUI) that allows users to create, edit, and delete notes using text, images, audio, video, or other multimedia formats.
  - A search function that allows users to find notes by keywords, categories, tags, dates, or other criteria.
  - A synchronization function that allows users to access and update their notes across different devices, such as laptops, smartphones, tablets, or wearable devices.
  - A sharing function that allows users to collaborate and communicate with other users who are studying the same subject, such as sending messages, comments, feedback, or questions.
  - A recommendation function that suggests relevant notes, resources, or activities to users based on their learning goals, preferences, or progress.
  - A gamification function that motivates and rewards users for their learning achievements, such as badges, points, levels, or leaderboards.
  - A personalization function that adapts the interface and the content to the user's learning style, pace, and needs, such as providing different modes, themes, or difficulty levels.
  - A feedback function that provides users with timely and constructive feedback on their learning performance, such as quizzes, tests, or self-assessments.
  - A analytics function that tracks and visualizes the user's learning data, such as time spent, notes created, notes viewed, notes shared, notes rated, etc.
