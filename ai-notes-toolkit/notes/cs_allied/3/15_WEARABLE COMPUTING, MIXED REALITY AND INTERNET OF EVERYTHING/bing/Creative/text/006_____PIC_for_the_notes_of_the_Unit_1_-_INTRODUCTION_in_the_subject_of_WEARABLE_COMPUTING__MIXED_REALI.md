### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the field of computer science that deals with designing, developing, and using wearable devices that can sense, process, and communicate information .
- Mixed reality is the field of computer science that deals with creating and manipulating digital content that is integrated with the physical environment and can be interacted with in real time .
- Internet of everything is the field of computer science that deals with connecting people, processes, data, and things through the internet and enabling new applications and services .
- PIC is an acronym for **P**roblem, **I**dea, and **C**ontribution, which is a framework for writing research papers and proposals in computer science .
- A PIC statement consists of three parts:
  - Problem: The motivation and context of the research, the gap or challenge that needs to be addressed, and the significance and impact of the problem.
  - Idea: The main concept or approach that the research proposes to solve the problem, the novelty and originality of the idea, and the assumptions and limitations of the idea.
  - Contribution: The expected outcomes and benefits of the research, the evidence and evaluation of the idea, and the comparison and contrast with existing solutions.
- A PIC statement should be concise, clear, and compelling, and should answer the following questions:
  - What is the problem and why is it important?
  - What is the idea and how is it different from existing solutions?
  - What is the contribution and how is it validated?
- A PIC statement can be used to structure the introduction and abstract of a research paper, as well as the summary and pitch of a research proposal.
