### Wearables for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearables are devices that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing, accessories, or implants, that have sensing, computing, or communication capabilities.
- Wearables can enable various applications, such as health monitoring, fitness tracking, augmented reality, virtual reality, social networking, entertainment, gaming, education, and more.
- Wearables can also enhance human capabilities, such as perception, cognition, memory, communication, and expression, by providing continuous and personalized feedback, guidance, or assistance.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines, that studies the principles of feedback, adaptation, and self-organization.
- Cybernetics can be applied to understand and design complex systems, such as biological, social, ecological, or technological systems, that involve interactions between humans and machines, or among machines themselves.
- Cybernetics can also be used to create artificial intelligence, that is, machines or systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, or creativity.
- Humanistic intelligence is a concept that defines intelligence as arising from the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and the computer are inextricably intertwined .
- Humanistic intelligence is different from artificial intelligence, which aims to create machines or systems that can mimic or surpass human intelligence, or natural intelligence, which is the intelligence inherent in living organisms.
- Humanistic intelligence is based on the idea that humans are the best source of information and inspiration for computational processes, and that computational processes can augment and amplify human intelligence and creativity.
- Humanistic intelligence can be embodied by wearable computing, which is the perfect tool for creating a seamless and symbiotic relationship between humans and machines .
- Wearable computing can provide humanistic intelligence by enabling continuous and natural interaction, personalization and customization, context-awareness and adaptation, and expression and communication.
