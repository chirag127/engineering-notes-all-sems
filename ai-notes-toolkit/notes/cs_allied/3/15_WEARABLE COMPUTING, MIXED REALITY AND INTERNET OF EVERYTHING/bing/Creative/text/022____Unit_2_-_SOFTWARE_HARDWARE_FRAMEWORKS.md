## Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

- This unit covers the basic concepts and components of software and hardware frameworks that enable the development and deployment of artificial intelligence (AI) applications.
- Software frameworks are collections of libraries, tools, and standards that provide a common platform for developing and running AI applications. Examples of software frameworks for AI include TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, Scikit-learn, etc.
- Hardware frameworks are architectures and devices that optimize the performance and efficiency of AI applications. Examples of hardware frameworks for AI include GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, ASICs, neuromorphic chips, etc.
- The main objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand the role and benefits of software and hardware frameworks for AI.
  - Compare and contrast different types of software and hardware frameworks for AI.
  - Identify the key features and components of some popular software and hardware frameworks for AI.
  - Apply the appropriate software and hardware frameworks for different AI tasks and scenarios.
