### Software for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Software hardware frameworks are the tools and platforms that enable the development and deployment of applications for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything (IoT).
- Wearable computing is the integration of computing devices into clothing or accessories that can be worn by users, such as smart watches, glasses, headsets, etc.
- Mixed reality is the blending of physical and digital worlds, where users can interact with both real and virtual objects and environments, such as augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR).
- Internet of everything is the extension of internet connectivity to everyday objects and devices, such as sensors, actuators, cameras, etc., that can communicate and exchange data with each other and the cloud.
- Some examples of software hardware frameworks for wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything are:

  - **Azure IoT**: A cloud-based platform that provides services and tools for connecting, managing, and securing IoT devices and solutions. It also supports digital twins, mixed reality, and autonomous systems applications.
  - **UbiVis**: A framework that enables ubiquitous mixed reality applications that can run on different hardware devices, such as smartphones, tablets, head-mounted displays, etc. It also provides solutions for displaying virtual content in collaborative MR-applications.
  - **MRCP**: A secure mixed reality framework for the internet of things that allows users to interact with headless IoT devices using a cross-platform, open-source, vendor-neutral MR software. It also provides proofs of concepts for smart parking meter and smart lock applications.
  - **Mixed Reality Toolkit (MRTK)**: A cross-platform, open-source framework that provides components and features for building MR applications for Windows Mixed Reality, HoloLens, and other devices. It also supports input, spatial awareness, UI, interactions, and more.
