# Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

## Wearable Computing

- Wearable computing is the use of devices that can be worn on the body to perform various functions, such as communication, health monitoring, fitness tracking, entertainment, etc.
- Wearable devices can be classified into different categories, such as smartwatches, smart glasses, smart clothing, smart jewelry, etc.
- Wearable devices can connect to the internet and other devices through wireless technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC, etc.
- Wearable devices can collect and analyze data from the user's body, environment, and behavior, and provide feedback, guidance, or recommendations to the user or other devices.
- Wearable devices can also interact with the physical world through sensors, actuators, displays, speakers, etc.
- Wearable devices can enable new applications and services, such as augmented reality, virtual reality, mixed reality, spatial computing, etc.

## Mixed Reality

- Mixed reality (MR) is a type of immersive technology that combines elements of both virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR).
- VR creates a fully artificial environment that replaces the user's real surroundings, while AR overlays digital information or objects on top of the user's real environment.
- MR blends the real and the virtual worlds, creating a hybrid reality where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real time.
- MR can be achieved through different devices, such as head-mounted displays (HMDs), smart glasses, smartphones, tablets, etc.
- MR can provide a more natural and intuitive way of interacting with the digital world, as well as enhancing the user's perception and cognition of the physical world.
- MR can enable new applications and services, such as education, entertainment, gaming, healthcare, tourism, etc.

## Internet of Everything

- Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that extends the Internet of Things (IoT) by including not only devices, but also people, data, and processes in the network of connections.
- IoT refers to the network of physical objects or things that are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity, enabling them to collect and exchange data with other devices or systems.
- IoE adds the human and organizational dimensions to IoT, creating a more comprehensive and intelligent network that can provide value and insights to all the stakeholders.
- IoE can be achieved through different technologies, such as cloud computing, big data analytics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, etc.
- IoE can enable new applications and services, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart agriculture, smart healthcare, smart education, etc.
