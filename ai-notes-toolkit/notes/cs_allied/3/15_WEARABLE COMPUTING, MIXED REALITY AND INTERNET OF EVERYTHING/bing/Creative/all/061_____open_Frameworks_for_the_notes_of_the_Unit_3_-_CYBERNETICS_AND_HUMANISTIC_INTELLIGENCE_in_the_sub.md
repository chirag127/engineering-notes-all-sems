# Open Frameworks for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is the intelligence that arises when a human is part of the feedback loop of a computational process in which the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- Wearable computing is the application of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence to create wearable devices that augment human capabilities and experiences.
- Mixed reality (MR) is the blending of physical and digital worlds, where physical and virtual objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the network of physical and virtual objects that are connected and communicate data over the internet.
- Open frameworks are software platforms that provide a set of tools and libraries for developing applications in a specific domain, such as wearable computing, mixed reality, or internet of everything.
- Some examples of open frameworks for wearable computing are:
  - Arduino: a hardware and software platform for creating interactive electronic devices.
  - Android Wear: an operating system for smartwatches and other wearable devices that run on Android.
  - OpenCV: a library of computer vision algorithms and functions for image processing, face detection, object recognition, etc.
- Some examples of open frameworks for mixed reality are:
  - Unity: a game engine and development platform for creating 2D and 3D games and interactive content for various platforms, including VR and AR.
  - Unreal Engine: a game engine and development platform for creating high-fidelity graphics and immersive experiences for various platforms, including VR and AR.
  - A-Frame: a web framework for building VR and AR scenes using HTML and JavaScript.
- Some examples of open frameworks for internet of everything are:
  - MQTT: a lightweight messaging protocol for small sensors and mobile devices that communicate over the internet.
  - Node-RED: a flow-based programming tool for wiring together devices, APIs, and online services.
  - Firebase: a platform for developing web and mobile applications that provide real-time data synchronization, authentication, storage, and hosting.
