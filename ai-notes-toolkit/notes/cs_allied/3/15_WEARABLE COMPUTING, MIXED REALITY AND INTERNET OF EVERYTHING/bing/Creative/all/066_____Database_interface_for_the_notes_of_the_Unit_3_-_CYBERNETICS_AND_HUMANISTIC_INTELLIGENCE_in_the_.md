# Database interface for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living beings and machines.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is a new signal processing framework in which intelligence arises by having the human being in the feedback loop of the computational process, and the processing apparatus is inextricably intertwined with the natural capabilities of the human mind and body.
- Wearable computing is the use of portable devices that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing, and that can sense, process, and communicate information.
- Mixed reality (MR) is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual and augmented reality are combined to create new environments and visualizations.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the network of physical objects, people, processes, and data that are interconnected and can exchange information and services.

Some of the main points to note are:

- Wearable computing can embody HI by creating a symbiotic relationship between the human and the computer, where the human provides the context, creativity, and intuition, and the computer provides the speed, memory, and accuracy.
- Wearable computing can enable new forms of communication through collective connected HI, where multiple users can share their sensory experiences and collaborate in real time.
- Wearable computing can also enhance the human perception and interaction with the physical and digital worlds, by providing mixed reality interfaces that can overlay, augment, or replace the natural sensory inputs and outputs.
- Wearable computing can leverage the IoE to access and integrate data and services from various sources, such as sensors, actuators, cloud computing, social media, and smart objects.
- Wearable computing can also contribute to the IoE by generating and transmitting data and services from the human body and behavior, such as biometrics, emotions, gestures, and preferences.
