# Creative Coding Platforms

Creative coding is a form of artistic expression that uses programming languages and technologies to create interactive and generative digital artworks. Creative coding platforms are tools and environments that enable and support creative coding practices. Some of the features and benefits of creative coding platforms are:

- They provide a simplified and intuitive syntax and interface for coding, often based on popular languages such as JavaScript, Python, or C++.
- They offer a rich set of libraries and frameworks for graphics, sound, animation, data visualization, physical computing, and more.
- They allow for live coding, which is the process of writing and modifying code while it is running, enabling immediate feedback and experimentation.
- They foster a collaborative and open culture of sharing, remixing, and learning from other creative coders, through online communities, galleries, and workshops.
- They are accessible and inclusive, catering to different levels of experience, backgrounds, and interests, and often supporting multiple platforms and devices.

Some examples of creative coding platforms are:

- **p5.js**: A JavaScript library that is based on the core principles of Processing, a popular creative coding language and environment. p5.js enables creative coding on the web, using the HTML5 canvas element and other web technologies. p5.js also has a web editor that allows users to write and run code in the browser, and share their sketches online.
- **Tynker**: A leading K-12 creative coding platform that teaches children of all ages the fundamentals of coding, logic, and problem-solving through fun and engaging projects and games. Tynker also integrates with popular platforms such as Minecraft, Lego, and Arduino, and supports coding with voice, touch, and physical gestures.
- **Scratch**: A free creative coding platform and online community that allows children of all ages to code, share, and remix their own stories, games, and animations. Scratch is translated into 50+ languages and available both online and offline. Scratch also supports physical computing with devices such as the Raspberry Pi, micro:bit, and Lego WeDo.
- **openFrameworks**: A creative coding framework designed and developed for prototyping and developing robust performant visual and interactive applications. openFrameworks is based on C++ and uses OpenGL for graphics rendering. openFrameworks also supports a variety of platforms, such as Windows, Linux, macOS, iOS, Android, and Raspberry Pi.
- **OpenProcessing**: A creative coding platform and online community that allows users to create and share sketches using Processing or p5.js. OpenProcessing also features a gallery of curated and popular sketches, a classroom mode for teachers and students, and a subscription service for more advanced features.
