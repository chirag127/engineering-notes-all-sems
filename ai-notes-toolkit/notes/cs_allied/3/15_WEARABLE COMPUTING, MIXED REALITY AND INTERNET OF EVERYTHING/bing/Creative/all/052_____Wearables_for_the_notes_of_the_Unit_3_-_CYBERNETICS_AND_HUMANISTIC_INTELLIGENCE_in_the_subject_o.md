# Wearables for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearables are devices that can be worn on the body or integrated into clothing or accessories, and that have sensing, computing, and communication capabilities.
- Wearables can be classified into three categories based on their level of integration with the human body: attached, implanted, and ingested.
- Wearables can enable various applications such as health monitoring, fitness tracking, entertainment, gaming, education, social interaction, and augmented reality.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines, and the study of feedback loops, self-regulation, and adaptation.
- Cybernetics can be applied to the design and analysis of wearables, as they involve the interaction and coordination of human and artificial systems.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is a concept that defines intelligence as the result of a human being in the feedback loop of a computational process, where the human and computer are inextricably intertwined.
- HI can be embodied by wearable computing, which is the perfect tool for creating a symbiotic relationship between the human and the machine, and enhancing the human's natural capabilities and experiences.
- HI can be contrasted with artificial intelligence (AI), which is the intelligence of a machine that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, and natural intelligence (NI), which is the intelligence of a living organism that can adapt to its environment and learn from its experience.
- HI can be measured by the degree of coupling between the human and the computer, the level of awareness and control of the feedback loop, and the extent of personalization and customization of the wearable system.
- HI can be improved by designing wearables that are comfortable, unobtrusive, intuitive, expressive, and ethical, and that respect the human's privacy, autonomy, and dignity.
