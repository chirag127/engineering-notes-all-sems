### Oculus Rift

- Oculus Rift is a line of virtual reality headsets developed and manufactured by Oculus VR, a division of Meta Platforms.
- Oculus Rift was invented by VR enthusiast Palmer Luckey, who later founded Oculus VR with Brendan Iribe.
- Oculus Rift connects to a gaming PC or laptop and uses a pair of goggles with headphones to create an immersive VR experience.
- Oculus Rift was released on March 28, 2016, after a successful Kickstarter campaign in 2012.
- Oculus Rift was discontinued in 2020 and replaced by Oculus Rift S, which has improved optics, tracking, and ergonomics.
- Oculus Rift uses external sensors and controllers to track the user's head and hand movements and translate them into VR.
- Oculus Rift has a library of VR apps, games, and experiences that can be downloaded from the Oculus software or other platforms.
- Oculus Rift is compatible with Windows 10 and requires a minimum system specification of an Intel i3-6100 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 processor, 8 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 470 graphics card.
- Oculus Rift is one of the most popular and influential VR headsets in the market, and has been used for various applications such as entertainment, education, training, and social interaction.
