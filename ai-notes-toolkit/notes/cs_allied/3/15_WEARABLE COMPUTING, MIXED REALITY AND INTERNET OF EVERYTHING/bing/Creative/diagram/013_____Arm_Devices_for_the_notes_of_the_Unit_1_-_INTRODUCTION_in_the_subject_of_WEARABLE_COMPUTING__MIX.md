- Arm devices are wearable computing devices that are worn on the arm or wrist, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart bracelets, etc.

- Arm processors are designed to provide high performance, energy efficiency, and scalability for various applications, including wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
- Arm processors are licensed by Arm to various chip makers, who then customize and integrate them into their own products. Some examples of Arm processors used in arm devices are:
  - Cortex-M series: These are microcontroller-class processors that are optimized for low-cost, low-power, and real-time applications. They are widely used in fitness trackers, smart bracelets, and other simple wearables.

  - Cortex-R series: These are real-time-class processors that are designed for high reliability, safety, and responsiveness. They are used in specialized wearables, such as medical devices, industrial controllers, and automotive systems.
