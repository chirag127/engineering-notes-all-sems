## Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

- This unit covers the basic concepts and components of software and hardware frameworks that enable the development and deployment of AI applications.
- Software frameworks are collections of libraries, tools, and standards that provide a common platform and structure for developing software applications.
- Hardware frameworks are collections of devices, components, and interfaces that provide a common platform and structure for executing software applications.
- Some examples of software frameworks for AI are TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, Scikit-learn, etc.
- Some examples of hardware frameworks for AI are CPUs, GPUs, TPUs, FPGAs, ASICs, etc.
- Software and hardware frameworks have different advantages and disadvantages in terms of performance, scalability, flexibility, compatibility, etc.
- Choosing the appropriate software and hardware frameworks for a given AI problem depends on various factors such as the type and size of the data, the complexity and architecture of the model, the available resources and budget, the desired accuracy and speed, etc.
- Software and hardware frameworks are constantly evolving and improving to meet the increasing demands and challenges of AI applications.
