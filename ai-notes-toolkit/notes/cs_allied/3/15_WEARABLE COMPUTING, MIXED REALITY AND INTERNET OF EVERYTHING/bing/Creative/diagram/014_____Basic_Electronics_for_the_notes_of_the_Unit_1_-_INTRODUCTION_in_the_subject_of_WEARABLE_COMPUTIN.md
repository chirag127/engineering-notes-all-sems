### Basic Electronics
- Basic electronics is the study of the principles and applications of electronic devices and circuits, such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, amplifiers, oscillators, filters, etc.
- Basic electronics involves understanding the concepts of voltage, current, resistance, power, energy, Kirchhoff's laws, Ohm's law, Thevenin's theorem, Norton's theorem, etc.
- Basic electronics also involves learning how to analyze, design, and troubleshoot electronic circuits using various methods and tools, such as circuit diagrams, schematic symbols, truth tables, logic gates, Boolean algebra, Karnaugh maps, etc.

### Wearable Computing
- Wearable computing is the field of designing and developing electronic devices that can be worn on the user's body, such as smart watches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, smart clothing, etc.
- Wearable devices can collect and analyze data, communicate with other devices, and provide feedback or response to the user, using sensors, microprocessors, batteries, and wireless connectivity.
- Wearable computing is an example of the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects that can interact with each other and the environment through the internet.
- Wearable computing has many applications and benefits in various domains, such as health, sports, entertainment, education, security, etc.

### Mixed Reality
- Mixed reality (MR) is the combination of real and virtual worlds, where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
- MR is a spectrum that includes augmented reality (AR), which is the overlay of digital information on the real world, and virtual reality (VR), which is the immersion of the user in a fully synthetic environment.
- MR devices can be wearable, such as head-mounted displays (HMDs), or non-wearable, such as projectors, screens, or holograms.
- MR technologies can create immersive and interactive experiences for the user, and can be used for various purposes, such as gaming, education, training, tourism, art, etc.

### Internet of Everything
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is the concept of connecting not only things, but also people, data, and processes, through the internet, to create more value and opportunities for various stakeholders.
- IoE is an extension of IoT, which focuses on the connectivity of things, and adds the dimensions of people, data, and processes, to enable more intelligence, efficiency, and innovation.
- IoE involves the use of various technologies, such as cloud computing, big data analytics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, blockchain, etc., to enable the exchange and processing of information among different entities.
- IoE has many potential applications and benefits in various sectors, such as healthcare, education, transportation, agriculture, manufacturing, etc.
