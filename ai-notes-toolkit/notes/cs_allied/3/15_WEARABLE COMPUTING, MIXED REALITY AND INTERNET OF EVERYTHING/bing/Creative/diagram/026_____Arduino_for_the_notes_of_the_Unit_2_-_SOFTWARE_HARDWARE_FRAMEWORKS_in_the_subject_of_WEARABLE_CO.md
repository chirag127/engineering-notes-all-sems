### Arduino for Wearable Computing

- Arduino is an open-source platform that consists of a hardware board and a software environment for creating interactive electronic projects.
- Arduino can be used for wearable computing, which is the integration of computing devices into clothing and accessories that can sense, process, and communicate data.
- Arduino offers various boards and modules that are designed for wearable computing, such as the Arduino Gemma, the Arduino LilyPad, and the Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense .
- Arduino Gemma is a small circular board that can be sewn into fabric with conductive thread and connected to sensors and actuators. It has an ATtiny85 microcontroller, a USB port, and an RGB LED.
- Arduino LilyPad is a series of boards that are shaped like flowers and can be stitched onto textiles with conductive thread. They have various sensors, actuators, and modules, such as light, temperature, sound, vibration, GPS, and Bluetooth.
- Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense is a compact board that has a 9-axis inertial measurement unit, a microphone, a temperature, humidity and pressure sensor, a gesture sensor, and a Bluetooth Low Energy module. It can be used for applications that require motion, sound, and environmental sensing.
- Arduino Wearables is a book that provides a guide to getting started with Arduino and wearable computing. It covers the basics of Arduino programming, electronics, and sewing, and presents 10 inspiring projects to make, learn from, and build upon.
- Some examples of Arduino wearables projects are:

  - Arduino Lightsaber: a DIY lightsaber that uses an Arduino MKR Zero, a NeoPixel LED strip, a speaker, and a LiPo battery. It can play sound effects and change colors according to the motion of the blade.
  - Nailz: a wearable input system for smart devices that uses touch-sensitive fingernails, called Nailz. It uses an Arduino Nano, a capacitive touch sensor, a Bluetooth module, and a vibration motor. It can detect different gestures and commands, such as tapping, sliding, and rotating.
  - Smart Glasses: a pair of glasses that can display information and notifications from a smartphone. It uses an Arduino Nano, an OLED display, a Bluetooth module, and a push button. It can show the time, date, weather, messages, and calls.
