### Mixed Reality

- Mixed reality is a medium consisting of immersive computer-generated environments in which elements of a physical and virtual environment are combined.
- Mixed reality is a blend of physical and digital worlds, unlocking natural and intuitive 3D human, computer, and environmental interactions.
- Mixed reality is the next wave in computing followed by mainframes, PCs, and smartphones. Mixed reality is going mainstream for consumers and businesses. It liberates us from screen-bound experiences by offering instinctual interactions with data in our living spaces and with our friends.
- Mixed reality that incorporates haptics has sometimes been referred to as Visuo-haptic mixed reality.
- Mixed reality can be classified into two types: augmented reality and virtual reality. Augmented reality is the overlay of digital content onto the real world, while virtual reality is the immersion of the user in a fully synthetic environment.
- Mixed reality can be achieved by various technologies, such as head-mounted displays, projectors, cameras, sensors, and controllers. Mixed reality can also be enhanced by cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and 5G networks.
- Mixed reality can have various applications, such as education, entertainment, gaming, health care, tourism, manufacturing, and military. Mixed reality can provide immersive learning experiences, realistic simulations, interactive storytelling, remote collaboration, and enhanced situational awareness.
