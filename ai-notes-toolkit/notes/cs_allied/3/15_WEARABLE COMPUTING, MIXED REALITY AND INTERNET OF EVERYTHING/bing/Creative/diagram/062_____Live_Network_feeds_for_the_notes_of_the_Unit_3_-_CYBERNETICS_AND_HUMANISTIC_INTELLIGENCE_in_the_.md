### Live Network Feeds for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

- Live network feeds are streams of data that are continuously updated and transmitted over a network, such as the Internet, to provide real-time information or interaction.
- Cybernetics is the science of communication and control in living organisms and machines, especially the study of feedback loops and self-regulation.
- Humanistic intelligence (HI) is a concept proposed by Steve Mann, a pioneer of wearable computing, that defines intelligence as the ability to adapt to changing situations by having a human in the feedback loop of a computational process, in which the human and the computer are inseparably intertwined.
- Wearable computing is the field of designing and developing wearable devices that augment or enhance human capabilities, such as perception, cognition, communication, and expression.
- Mixed reality (MR) is the blending of physical and digital worlds, where real and virtual objects coexist and interact in real time, creating new forms of immersive experiences.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the extension of the Internet of things (IoT), which refers to the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity, to include people, processes, and data, creating a more interconnected and intelligent system.

Some of the main points to note about the relationship between these concepts are:

- Live network feeds enable wearable computing devices to access and share data from various sources, such as sensors, cameras, social media, and cloud services, and to provide feedback and guidance to the user in real time.
- Live network feeds also facilitate mixed reality applications, such as augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR), by allowing the user to overlay digital information or images on the physical environment, or to immerse themselves in a simulated world, respectively.
- Cybernetics provides the theoretical foundation and the principles for designing and evaluating wearable computing and mixed reality systems, such as feedback, adaptation, learning, and self-organization.
- Humanistic intelligence is the ultimate goal and the guiding philosophy of wearable computing and mixed reality, as it aims to empower the human user and to enhance their natural abilities, rather than to replace or surpass them.
