### Audio for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything are emerging technologies that aim to create immersive, interactive and connected experiences for users across different domains and contexts.
- These technologies require a combination of software and hardware components that enable the creation, delivery and consumption of digital content and services in various forms and modalities, such as visual, auditory, haptic, etc.
- Software hardware frameworks are the tools and platforms that facilitate the development and deployment of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything applications, by providing common functionalities, standards, APIs and interfaces for different devices, sensors, networks and cloud services.
- Some examples of software hardware frameworks are:

  - **Azure IoT**: A cloud-based platform that provides a comprehensive set of services and solutions for connecting, managing and securing IoT devices and data, as well as enabling advanced analytics, AI and mixed reality applications.
  - **Frontiers in Virtual Reality**: A peer-reviewed open access journal that publishes original research articles, reviews and perspectives on the latest developments and challenges in virtual reality, augmented reality, mixed reality and related fields.
  - **Ubiquitous Mixed Reality Framework (UMRF)**: A software framework that enables the development of ubiquitous mixed reality applications across dynamically adaptable device configurations, by providing a common data model, communication protocol and interaction mechanism for heterogeneous devices and systems.

- The benefits of using software hardware frameworks are:

  - They reduce the complexity and cost of developing and deploying wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything applications, by abstracting the low-level details and providing high-level functionalities and interfaces.
  - They increase the interoperability and compatibility of different devices, systems and services, by following common standards and protocols and supporting cross-platform development and deployment.
  - They enhance the user experience and satisfaction, by enabling seamless integration, adaptation and interaction across different devices, modalities and environments.

- The challenges of using software hardware frameworks are:

  - They may introduce performance and security issues, by adding additional layers of abstraction and communication and relying on external services and networks.
  - They may limit the flexibility and creativity of developers and users, by imposing certain constraints and assumptions on the design and implementation of applications and content.
  - They may require constant updating and maintenance, by coping with the rapid evolution and diversity of hardware and software systems and technologies.
