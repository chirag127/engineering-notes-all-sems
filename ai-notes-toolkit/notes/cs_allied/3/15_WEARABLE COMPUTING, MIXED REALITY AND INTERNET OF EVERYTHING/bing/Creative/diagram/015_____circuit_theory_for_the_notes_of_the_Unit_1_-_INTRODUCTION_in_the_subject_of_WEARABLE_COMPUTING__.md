### Circuit Theory for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Circuit theory is the study of the behavior of electrical circuits, which are composed of components such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, and sources of voltage and current.
- Circuit theory can be used to analyze and design various types of circuits, such as analog, digital, linear, nonlinear, active, passive, AC, DC, etc.
- Circuit theory can also be applied to the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything, which are emerging technologies that integrate computing, communication, and sensing into everyday objects, environments, and experiences.
- Wearable computing refers to the use of wearable devices, such as smartwatches, glasses, clothing, or implants, that can perform various functions, such as monitoring health, providing information, enhancing perception, or enabling interaction.
- Mixed reality refers to the combination of real and virtual worlds, where physical and digital objects coexist and interact in real time. Mixed reality can be achieved by using devices such as head-mounted displays, projectors, cameras, or sensors, that can create, capture, or augment the reality.
- Internet of everything refers to the network of people, processes, data, and things, that are connected and exchange information through the internet. Internet of everything can enable new capabilities, richer experiences, and unprecedented economic opportunities for businesses, individuals, and countries.
- Circuit theory can help to understand, design, and optimize the performance, functionality, and efficiency of the devices, systems, and networks that are involved in wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything. For example, circuit theory can help to:
  - Determine the power consumption, battery life, and charging methods of wearable devices.
  - Design the signal processing, data transmission, and display circuits of mixed reality devices.
  - Analyze the communication, security, and scalability issues of internet of everything networks.
