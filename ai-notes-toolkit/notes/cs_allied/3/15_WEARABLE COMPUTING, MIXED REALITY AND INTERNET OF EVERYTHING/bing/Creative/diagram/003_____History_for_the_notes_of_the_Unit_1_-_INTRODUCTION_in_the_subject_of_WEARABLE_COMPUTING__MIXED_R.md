# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

## History of Wearable Computing

- Wearable computing is the field of technology that involves devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, glasses, headsets, rings, etc.
- The term "wearable computer" was coined by Edward O. Thorp and Claude Shannon in 1961, when they developed a concealed device to predict the outcome of roulette games.
- The first wearable computer that was publicly demonstrated was Steve Mann's rig in 1980, which consisted of a backpack-mounted computer, a head-mounted display, and a camera .
- In the 1990s, several wearable computer systems were developed by researchers at MIT, Carnegie Mellon, Georgia Tech, and other institutions, using various components such as Private Eye displays, Twiddler keyboards, and wireless networks .
- In the 2000s, wearable computing became more popular and commercialized, with products such as Nike+ (2006), Fitbit (2009), Google Glass (2013), Apple Watch (2015), and Oculus Rift (2016)  .
- The current trends and challenges in wearable computing include improving the battery life, user interface, privacy, security, and social acceptance of the devices, as well as exploring new applications and domains such as health, education, entertainment, and augmented reality   .
