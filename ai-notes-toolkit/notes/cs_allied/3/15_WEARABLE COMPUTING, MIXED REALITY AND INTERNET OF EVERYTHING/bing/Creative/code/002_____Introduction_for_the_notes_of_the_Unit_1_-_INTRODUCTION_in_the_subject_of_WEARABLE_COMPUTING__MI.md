### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the field of computer science that deals with the design, development and application of wearable devices that can be worn on the body or embedded in clothing or accessories.
- Wearable devices can provide various functions such as sensing, computing, communication, display, interaction, health monitoring, entertainment, etc.
- Wearable computing can be classified into three categories: passive, active and interactive. Passive wearable devices only sense and record data, active wearable devices perform some computation and feedback, and interactive wearable devices allow bidirectional communication and control between the user and the device or the environment.
- Mixed reality is the field of computer science that deals with the creation and manipulation of digital content that is integrated with the physical world in real time.
- Mixed reality can be classified into four categories: augmented reality, augmented virtuality, virtual reality and mediated reality. Augmented reality enhances the physical world with digital content, augmented virtuality enhances the digital world with physical content, virtual reality creates a fully immersive digital world, and mediated reality modifies the perception of the physical world through digital filters or effects.
- Internet of Everything is the field of computer science that deals with the interconnection and integration of people, processes, data and things through the internet and other networks.
- Internet of Everything can enable various applications such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, smart transportation, smart education, smart industry, etc.
- Internet of Everything can be classified into four domains: networked connections, data analytics, cloud computing and cognitive computing. Networked connections provide the infrastructure and protocols for data transmission and communication, data analytics provide the methods and tools for data processing and extraction of insights, cloud computing provide the services and resources for data storage and computation, and cognitive computing provide the capabilities and techniques for data understanding and decision making.
