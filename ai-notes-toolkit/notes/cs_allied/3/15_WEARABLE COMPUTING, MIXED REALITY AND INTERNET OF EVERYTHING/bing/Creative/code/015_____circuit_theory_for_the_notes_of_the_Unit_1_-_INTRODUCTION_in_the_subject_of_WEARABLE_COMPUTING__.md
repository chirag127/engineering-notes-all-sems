### Circuit Theory for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Circuit theory is the branch of electrical engineering that deals with the analysis and design of electric circuits, which are composed of passive and active components such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, transistors, diodes, etc.
- Circuit theory is based on the principles of Kirchhoff's laws, Ohm's law, Thevenin's theorem, Norton's theorem, superposition, mesh analysis, nodal analysis, etc.
- Circuit theory is essential for understanding and developing wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything devices and applications, which are based on the integration of sensors, actuators, processors, communication modules, and power sources in small and wearable form factors.
- Wearable computing is the study or practice of inventing, designing, building, or using miniature body-borne computational and sensory devices. Wearable computers may be worn under, over, or in clothing, or may also be themselves clothes. Wearable computing enables ubiquitous and context-aware computing, human-computer interaction, and augmented reality.
- Mixed reality is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations, where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time. Mixed reality can be achieved by using various technologies such as head-mounted displays, projectors, cameras, sensors, etc. Mixed reality can enhance the perception and interaction of users with their surroundings, and enable immersive and collaborative experiences.
- Internet of everything (IoE) is the concept of connecting people, things, data, and processes through network intelligence . It is an extension or superset of the internet of things (IoT), which focuses on the connectivity of physical objects. IoE can create new capabilities, richer experiences, and unprecedented economic opportunities for businesses, individuals, and countries. IoE can enable smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart education, smart agriculture, etc.
