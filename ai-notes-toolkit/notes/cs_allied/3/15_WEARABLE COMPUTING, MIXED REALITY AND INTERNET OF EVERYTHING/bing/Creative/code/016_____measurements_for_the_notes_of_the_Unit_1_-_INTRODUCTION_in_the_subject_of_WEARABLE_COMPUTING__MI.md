# Measurements for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Wearable computing is the field of technology that involves devices that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses, virtual reality headsets, etc. These devices can collect and analyse data, communicate with other devices, and provide various services to the user  .
- Mixed reality is the blending of the physical and digital worlds, where virtual objects can interact with real environments and vice versa. Mixed reality devices, such as HoloLens or Magic Leap, can create immersive and interactive experiences that augment the user's perception and cognition .
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is the network of connections between people, things, data, and processes that provide general intelligence and improved cognition across the networked environment. IoE encompasses the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity, as well as the Internet of Humans (IoH), which is the network of people using wearable and mobile devices to access and share information .
- The main objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand the basic concepts and definitions of wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything.
  - Learn about the history and evolution of these technologies and their applications in various domains.
  - Explore the current trends and challenges in these fields and their implications for the future.
  - Develop the skills and knowledge to design, develop, and evaluate wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything systems and solutions.
