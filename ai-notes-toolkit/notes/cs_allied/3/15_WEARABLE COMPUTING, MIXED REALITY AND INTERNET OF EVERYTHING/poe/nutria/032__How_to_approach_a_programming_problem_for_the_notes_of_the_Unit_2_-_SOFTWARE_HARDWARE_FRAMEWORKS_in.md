
### How to approach a programming problem 

1. Understand the problem: Take the time to read and understand the problem. Identify the inputs and outputs, and any constraints that may be present.

2. Analyze the problem: Break down the problem into smaller pieces and analyze each piece. Consider how the pieces fit together and how they can be implemented.

3. Design a solution: Design a solution that meets the requirements of the problem. Consider the algorithms, data structures, and other tools that may be used to solve the problem.

4. Implement the solution: Write the code to implement the solution. Test the code to ensure it meets the requirements.

5. Analyze the results: Analyze the results to ensure they meet the requirements. If not, go back to step 3 and design a new solution.
