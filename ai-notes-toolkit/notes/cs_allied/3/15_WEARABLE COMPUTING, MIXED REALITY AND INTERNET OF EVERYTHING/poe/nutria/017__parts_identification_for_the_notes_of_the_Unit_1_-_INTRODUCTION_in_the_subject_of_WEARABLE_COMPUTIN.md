
### Parts Identification for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing is a type of technology that is designed to be worn on the body. It can include devices such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, augmented reality glasses, and virtual reality headsets.

2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality is a combination of augmented and virtual reality. It allows users to interact with virtual objects in the real world, and vice versa.

3. Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything (IoE) is the concept of connecting everyday objects to the internet. This includes devices such as smart TVs, lightbulbs, thermostats, and home security systems.
