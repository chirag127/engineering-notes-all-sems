
### SPI for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* SPI stands for Serial Peripheral Interface, which is a synchronous serial communication protocol used for short-distance communication, primarily in embedded systems.
* SPI allows for full-duplex communication between two devices by using four wires: a clock line (SCLK), a slave select line (SS), a master output/slave input line (MOSI), and a master input/slave output line (MISO).
* SPI is used to connect peripherals such as sensors, displays, and memory chips to a microcontroller.
* SPI is a popular protocol for communication between microcontrollers and other components, such as sensors, displays, and memory chips.
* SPI is relatively simple to use, and is supported by many microcontroller platforms.
* SPI is a popular choice for communication between microcontrollers and other components because of its simplicity and low overhead.
