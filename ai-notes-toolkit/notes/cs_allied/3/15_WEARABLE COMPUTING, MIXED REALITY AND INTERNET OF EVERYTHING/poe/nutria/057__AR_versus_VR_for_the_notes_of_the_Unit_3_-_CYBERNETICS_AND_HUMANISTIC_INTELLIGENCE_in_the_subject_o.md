
### AR versus VR

* Augmented reality (AR) is a technology that superimposes digital content onto the real world. It can be used to enhance the user experience by providing additional information or visuals to the physical environment.

* Virtual reality (VR) is a technology that creates a simulated environment for the user. It can be used to create immersive experiences that allow users to interact with virtual objects and environments.

* AR and VR both have their advantages and disadvantages. AR is more accessible and can be used in a variety of contexts, while VR is more immersive and can create more realistic experiences.

* AR and VR can be used to enhance the user experience in a variety of ways, such as providing additional information or visuals to the physical environment. They can also be used to create immersive experiences that allow users to interact with virtual objects and environments.
