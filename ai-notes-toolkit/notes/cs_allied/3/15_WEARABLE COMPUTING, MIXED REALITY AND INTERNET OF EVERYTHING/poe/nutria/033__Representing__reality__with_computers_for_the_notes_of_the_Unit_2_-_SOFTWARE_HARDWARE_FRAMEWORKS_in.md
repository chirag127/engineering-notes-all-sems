
### Representing “Reality” with Computers 

* Computers are used to represent reality in many ways, such as through virtual reality (VR), augmented reality (AR), and mixed reality (MR) technologies. 
* VR is a computer-generated simulation of a 3D environment, which can be explored and interacted with by a user. AR is the integration of digital information with the user’s environment in real time. MR is a combination of both VR and AR, allowing users to experience a hybrid of real and virtual worlds. 
* Wearable computing devices, such as smart watches and fitness trackers, are used to interact with computers and the physical world. These devices can be used to measure and monitor physical activity, as well as to provide notifications and reminders. 
* The Internet of Everything (IoE) is an interconnected network of physical objects, such as sensors, machines, and people, that are able to communicate and exchange data. IoE enables smarter decision making, improved efficiency, and greater convenience. 
* Software and hardware frameworks are used to create and manage applications for wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything. These frameworks provide the tools and APIs needed to develop and deploy applications.
