
### Software for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

* Wearable computing refers to the use of computer technology to create devices that can be worn on the body. These devices are typically used for data collection, communication, and entertainment.
* Hardware frameworks are the physical components of a system, such as processors, memory, and other components.
* Mixed reality is an augmented reality technology that combines virtual objects with real-world environments. It allows users to interact with virtual objects as if they were real.
* The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that refers to the connection of people, processes, data, and things. It enables the sharing of data and information across a network of devices.
