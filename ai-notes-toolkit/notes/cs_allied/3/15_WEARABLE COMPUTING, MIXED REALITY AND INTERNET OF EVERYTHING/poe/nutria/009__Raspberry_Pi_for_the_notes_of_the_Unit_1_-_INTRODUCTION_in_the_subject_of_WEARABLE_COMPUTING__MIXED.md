
### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer that can be used to create a wide range of projects. It is a popular tool for makers, hobbyists, and students, and is often used in wearable computing, mixed reality, and Internet of Everything applications.

Raspberry Pi has a range of features, including:

- A Broadcom BCM2837 64-bit quad-core ARM Cortex-A53 processor running at 1.4GHz
- 1GB RAM
- 4 USB ports
- HDMI output
- Ethernet port
- 40-pin GPIO header
- MicroSD card slot
- Camera and display interface
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity

Raspberry Pi is a great platform for creating a variety of projects, from home automation systems to robots and games. It can also be used to create Internet of Things (IoT) devices, such as smart home sensors and connected appliances.
