
### Unit 2 - Software Hardware Frameworks

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing is a technology that integrates computing devices into clothing and accessories. It can be used to monitor and track a user’s physical activity, such as heart rate, temperature, and other biometric data. Wearable computing can also be used for communication, entertainment, and navigation.

2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality (MR) combines the physical and virtual worlds to create a new form of reality. It includes technologies such as augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and mixed reality (MR). MR allows users to interact with both digital and physical environments, and can be used to create immersive experiences.

3. Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that describes the connection of people, processes, data, and things. It enables the collection and sharing of data across multiple networks and devices. IoE is an emerging technology that can be used to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and create new opportunities.
