
### Mobile phones and similar devices

* Mobile phones are handheld devices used to make and receive calls, send text messages, access the internet, and perform other functions. 
* Smartphones are a type of mobile phone with added features such as a touchscreen, internet access, and applications (apps). 
* Wearable computing is the use of technology embedded in clothing, accessories, and other items worn on the body. 
* Mixed reality combines the physical environment with virtual objects in an interactive way. 
* The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for collecting and exchanging data. 
* Smartphones, wearables, and IoT devices can be used together to create immersive and interactive experiences. 
* These devices can also be used to monitor health, control home appliances, and more.
