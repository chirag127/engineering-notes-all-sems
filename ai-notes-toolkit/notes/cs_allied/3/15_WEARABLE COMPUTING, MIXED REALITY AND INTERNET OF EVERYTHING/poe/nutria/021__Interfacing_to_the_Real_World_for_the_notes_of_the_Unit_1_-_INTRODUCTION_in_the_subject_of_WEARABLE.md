
### Interfacing to the Real World 

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computing is the use of technology that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and augmented reality glasses. Wearable computing can provide real-time data, such as location, heart rate, and movement patterns. 
2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality combines the physical and digital worlds by blending the real and virtual worlds together. This technology uses computer-generated images to interact with the physical world, such as augmented reality and virtual reality. 
3. Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything (IoE) is a concept that connects people, processes, data, and things. It enables the sharing of data between physical and digital devices, allowing them to interact with each other and create new opportunities.
