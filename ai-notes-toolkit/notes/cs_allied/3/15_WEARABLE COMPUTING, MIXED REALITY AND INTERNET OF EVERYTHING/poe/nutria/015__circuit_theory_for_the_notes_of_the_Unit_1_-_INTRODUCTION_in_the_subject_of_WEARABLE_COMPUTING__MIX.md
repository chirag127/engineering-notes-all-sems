
### Circuit Theory for Unit 1 - Introduction in Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

1. Circuit theory is a fundamental concept in electrical engineering and computer science, and is essential to creating and understanding wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything.
2. Circuit theory is based on the idea of creating a circuit of components that can be used to send and receive electrical signals.
3. Components in a circuit can include transistors, resistors, capacitors, and diodes, among other components.
4. Circuits can be used to create digital logic circuits, which are the basis for computers.
5. Circuits can also be used to create analog circuits, which are used to process signals from sensors and other sources.
6. Wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything all rely on circuits to process data and create useful output.
7. To understand circuits and how to use them in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, it is important to understand the basics of electricity and how it works.
8. It is also important to understand the basics of components and how they work together in a circuit.
9. Finally, it is important to understand how to design and build circuits, as well as how to troubleshoot and debug them.
