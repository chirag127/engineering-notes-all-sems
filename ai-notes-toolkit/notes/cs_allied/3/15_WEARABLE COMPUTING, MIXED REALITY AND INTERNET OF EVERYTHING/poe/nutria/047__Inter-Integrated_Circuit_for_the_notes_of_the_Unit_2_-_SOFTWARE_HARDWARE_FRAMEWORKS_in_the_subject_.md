
### Inter-Integrated Circuit (I2C)

* I2C is a hardware protocol used for communication between integrated circuits. 
* It is a two-wire serial communication protocol, which means that two wires are used for data transfer. 
* It is used to connect multiple devices, such as sensors, displays, and microcontrollers, to each other in a single system. 
* It is a multi-master protocol, meaning that any device can initiate a communication session with any other device. 
* I2C is used in a wide variety of applications, including wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Things. 
* It is a low-power, low-speed protocol, which makes it ideal for battery-powered devices. 
* I2C is a synchronous protocol, meaning that the data is transmitted in a predetermined clock frequency. 
* The I2C protocol is simple to implement and requires minimal hardware resources. 
* It is also relatively inexpensive and easy to debug.
