### Laptop for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

In the modern world, laptops have become an essential tool for students to take notes and complete assignments. When it comes to the subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything, having a reliable laptop is crucial. Here are some key features to consider when selecting a laptop for this subject:

1. Processor: A powerful processor is essential to handle the heavy workloads of software development and virtual reality applications. Look for a laptop with an Intel Core i5 or i7 processor or an AMD Ryzen 5 or 7 processor.

2. Graphics card: In this subject, students will be working with virtual and augmented reality applications that require high-quality graphics. A dedicated graphics card, such as NVIDIA GeForce or AMD Radeon, is a must-have.

3. RAM: To handle the demands of software development and virtual reality applications, a minimum of 8GB of RAM is recommended. However, for optimal performance, look for a laptop with 16GB or even 32GB of RAM.

4. Storage: Students will need ample storage space for their notes, assignments, and software development projects. Consider a laptop with a solid-state drive (SSD) for faster load times and smoother performance.

5. Display: When working with virtual and augmented reality applications, having a high-quality display is crucial. Look for a laptop with a Full HD (1920x1080) or higher resolution and a refresh rate of at least 60Hz.

6. Connectivity: Students will need to connect to the internet and other devices frequently. Consider a laptop with Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0, and multiple USB ports.

7. Battery life: When attending classes or working on assignments, students need a laptop with long battery life. Look for a laptop with a battery life of at least 8 hours.

In summary, selecting the right laptop for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING requires careful consideration of the processor, graphics card, RAM, storage, display, connectivity, and battery life. By choosing a laptop with these key features, students can ensure they have a reliable and high-performance machine to support their studies.
