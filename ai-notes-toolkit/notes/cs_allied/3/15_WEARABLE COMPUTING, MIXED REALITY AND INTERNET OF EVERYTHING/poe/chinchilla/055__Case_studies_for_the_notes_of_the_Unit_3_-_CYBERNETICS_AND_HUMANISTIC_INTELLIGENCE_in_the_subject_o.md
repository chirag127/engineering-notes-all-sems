### Case Studies for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cybernetics and Humanistic Intelligence in the Subject of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality and Internet of Everything

1. Smart Glasses by Google
- Google Glass is a wearable device that provides an augmented reality experience to users.
- It allows users to access information hands-free and provides a new way of interacting with the world.
- The device uses voice commands and gestures to control the interface and display information.
- It has a camera that can take photos and record videos, and can be used for video conferencing and navigation.
- The device has potential applications in healthcare, education, and entertainment industries.

2. Wearable Fitness Trackers by Fitbit
- Fitbit is a wearable device that tracks physical activity, sleep patterns, and heart rate.
- It uses sensors to monitor the wearer's movements and provides real-time feedback on their performance.
- The device can be synced with a mobile app and allows users to set goals, track progress, and compete with friends.
- It has potential applications in the healthcare industry for monitoring patients with chronic conditions.

3. Smart Clothing by Hexoskin
- Hexoskin is a smart clothing brand that uses sensors to monitor biometric data.
- The clothing can track heart rate, respiratory rate, and activity levels.
- It provides real-time feedback on the wearer's performance and can be synced with a mobile app.
- The clothing has potential applications in sports training, healthcare, and military industries.

4. Smartwatches by Apple
- Apple Watch is a wearable device that provides a range of features beyond telling time.
- It has a variety of health and fitness tracking functions, including monitoring heart rate, activity levels, and sleep patterns.
- The device can be used to make phone calls, send messages, and access apps.
- It has potential applications in the healthcare industry for monitoring patients with chronic conditions and for telemedicine.

5. Smart Contact Lenses by Verily
- Verily is a subsidiary of Alphabet (Google's parent company) that is developing smart contact lenses.
- The lenses can measure glucose levels in tears, which could be used for diabetes management.
- They can also be used for vision correction and as a platform for augmented reality.
- The lenses have potential applications in the healthcare industry for monitoring patients with diabetes and for providing a new way of interacting with the world.

6. Virtual Reality Therapy by Psious
- Psious is a virtual reality therapy platform that uses immersive technology to treat mental health disorders.
- It provides a safe and controlled environment for patients to confront their fears and anxieties.
- The platform can be customized to treat a variety of conditions, including phobias, anxiety, and PTSD.
- It has potential applications in the healthcare industry for treating mental health disorders and for improving patient outcomes.

In conclusion, wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything technologies have the potential to revolutionize many industries, including healthcare, sports, and entertainment. These case studies illustrate the diverse range of applications and opportunities that these technologies offer. As the field continues to evolve, it will be important to consider both the technical and humanistic aspects of these innovations.
