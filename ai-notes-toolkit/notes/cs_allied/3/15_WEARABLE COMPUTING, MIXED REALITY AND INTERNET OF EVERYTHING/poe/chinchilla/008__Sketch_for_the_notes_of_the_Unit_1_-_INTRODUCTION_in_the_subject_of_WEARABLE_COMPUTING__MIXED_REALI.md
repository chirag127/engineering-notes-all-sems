### Sketch for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

In this unit, we will be introduced to the exciting world of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything (IoE). We will explore the various technologies and concepts that make up these fields and learn how they are transforming the way we interact with the world around us. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Wearable computing refers to the use of technology that can be worn on the body, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and augmented reality glasses. These devices can collect data about the wearer and their environment, and provide useful information and services.

- Mixed reality involves the blending of the physical and virtual worlds, creating a new form of reality that can be experienced through devices such as virtual reality headsets and augmented reality glasses. This technology has the potential to revolutionize industries such as entertainment, education, and healthcare.

- The Internet of Everything refers to the network of connected devices and objects that are part of our daily lives, from smartphones and laptops to home appliances and cars. This network enables the collection and sharing of vast amounts of data, which can be used to improve efficiency, productivity, and quality of life.

- The development of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything is being driven by advances in technologies such as sensors, wireless communication, and artificial intelligence. These technologies are enabling new forms of interaction between humans and machines, and new ways of experiencing and understanding the world around us.

- There are many challenges and opportunities associated with these fields, including issues related to privacy, security, and ethics. As we explore these technologies, it is important to consider their implications for society and to develop responsible and sustainable approaches to their development and use.

Overall, the fields of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything are rapidly evolving and have the potential to transform the way we live, work, and play. In this unit, we will delve deeper into these exciting areas and explore their potential for innovation and impact.
