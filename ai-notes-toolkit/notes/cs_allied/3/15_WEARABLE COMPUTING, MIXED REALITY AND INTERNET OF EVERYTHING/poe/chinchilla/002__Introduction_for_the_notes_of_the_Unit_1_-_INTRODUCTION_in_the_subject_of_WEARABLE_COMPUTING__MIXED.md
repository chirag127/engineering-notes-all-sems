#### Definition and Overview

- Wearable Computing: It is a technology that enables users to wear electronic devices on their bodies, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and virtual reality headsets. These devices can help users in various ways, such as monitoring their health, tracking their fitness goals, and providing them with augmented reality experiences.
- Mixed Reality: It is a technology that combines elements of virtual reality and augmented reality. It allows users to interact with digital objects in the real world, blurring the line between the physical and digital worlds.
- Internet of Everything: It is a concept that refers to the integration of physical devices, sensors, and software applications with the internet. This integration allows devices to communicate with each other and share data, creating a more connected world.

#### Evolution of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything

- Wearable Computing: The concept of wearable computing dates back to the 1960s, but it wasn't until the 2000s that it started gaining mainstream attention. Today, wearable devices have become an integral part of our daily lives, and the market for these devices is expected to grow significantly in the coming years.
- Mixed Reality: The concept of mixed reality was first introduced in the 1990s, but it wasn't until the development of advanced sensors and displays that it started gaining popularity. Today, mixed reality is being used in various industries, such as healthcare, education, and entertainment.
- Internet of Everything: The concept of the internet of everything emerged in the early 2000s, and it has been rapidly evolving ever since. Today, the internet of everything is being used in various industries, such as manufacturing, transportation, and healthcare.

- Wearable Computing: Wearable devices are being used in various industries, such as healthcare, fitness, and entertainment. They are also being used to enhance the shopping experience by providing personalized recommendations and enabling mobile payments.
- Mixed Reality: Mixed reality is being used in various industries, such as healthcare, education, and entertainment. It is also being used to enhance the shopping experience by providing virtual try-on experiences and product visualization.

- Mixed Reality: One of the biggest challenges in mixed reality is to create devices that are affordable and accessible to the general public. The future of mixed reality is expected to be focused on the development of more advanced sensors and the integration of artificial intelligence.
