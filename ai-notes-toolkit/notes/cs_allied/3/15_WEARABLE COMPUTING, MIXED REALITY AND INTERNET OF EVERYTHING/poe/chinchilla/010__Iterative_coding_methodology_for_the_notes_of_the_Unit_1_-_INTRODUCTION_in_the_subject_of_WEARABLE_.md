### Iterative coding methodology for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

In the world of software development, iterative coding methodology is a popular approach that emphasizes on continuous improvement and adaptation throughout the development process. This methodology has proven to be effective in developing complex systems where requirements are not fully understood at the beginning of the project. In this unit, we will discuss the iterative coding methodology and its relevance in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.

Here are the key points to understand about iterative coding methodology:

- Iterative coding methodology involves breaking down the development process into smaller iterations or cycles, where each cycle includes planning, designing, coding, testing, and evaluating.
- The goal of each iteration is to produce a working product increment that can be tested and evaluated by the stakeholders.
- The feedback received from stakeholders during the evaluation phase is used to refine and improve the product increment in the subsequent iterations.
- This process continues until the final product is developed, meeting all the requirements and expectations of the stakeholders.
- Iterative coding methodology is particularly suitable for developing complex systems where requirements are not fully understood at the beginning of the project.
- It enables the development team to adapt to changing requirements and incorporate feedback from stakeholders throughout the development process.
- It also promotes collaboration and communication between team members, as well as with stakeholders.
- The use of iterative coding methodology can result in a more flexible and resilient product that can evolve and adapt to changing circumstances and requirements.
- The key principles of iterative coding methodology include flexibility, adaptability, collaboration, communication, and continuous improvement.

In conclusion, iterative coding methodology is an effective approach for developing complex systems in the context of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything. It enables the development team to adapt to changing requirements and incorporate feedback from stakeholders throughout the development process, resulting in a more flexible and resilient product.
