### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a small, affordable computer that was developed in the UK by the Raspberry Pi Foundation. It was designed to be a tool for teaching computer science and programming to students, but has since become popular for a wide range of applications, including in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything. Here are some key points to know about the Raspberry Pi:

- The Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer that is about the size of a credit card. It runs on a Linux-based operating system and has a range of input and output ports, including HDMI, USB, and GPIO pins which can be used to connect sensors and other devices.
- The Raspberry Pi has a relatively low processing power and memory compared to traditional desktop computers, but it is still capable of running a wide range of applications, including web servers, media centers, and even games.
- The Raspberry Pi is affordable, with prices starting at around $35 for the latest model. This makes it an accessible tool for students and hobbyists who want to experiment with computing and programming.
- The Raspberry Pi can be used in a wide range of applications related to wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything. For example, it can be used to power sensors and other devices in a wearable computing project, or to run software for a mixed reality headset. It can also be used as a hub for internet of everything devices, such as smart home sensors and appliances.
- The Raspberry Pi has a large and supportive community of users and developers, who have created a wide range of resources and tutorials for getting started with the device. This includes software libraries, hardware add-ons, and project ideas.
- To get started with the Raspberry Pi, you will need to purchase the device itself, as well as some basic peripherals such as a power supply, keyboard, and mouse. You will also need a microSD card to install the operating system, and may want to purchase additional sensors and devices depending on your project goals.
- There are several different models of Raspberry Pi available, each with slightly different specifications and capabilities. The latest model, the Raspberry Pi 4, offers the most processing power and memory, and can be used for more demanding applications. However, earlier models may be sufficient for simpler projects, and may be more affordable.
