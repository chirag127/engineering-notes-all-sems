### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, credit-card-sized computer that plugs into a computer monitor or TV, and uses a standard keyboard and mouse. It is a small and affordable device that can be used for a variety of purposes, including learning to program, running applications, and building electronic projects.

Here are some key points about Raspberry Pi:

- Raspberry Pi was first introduced in 2012, and since then it has become increasingly popular among hobbyists, students, and professionals.
- The device is powered by a Broadcom BCM2835 system-on-a-chip, which includes a 700 MHz ARM11 processor, 512 MB of RAM, and a VideoCore IV GPU.
- The latest version of Raspberry Pi is the Raspberry Pi 4, which was released in 2019. It features a 1.5 GHz quad-core ARM Cortex-A72 processor, up to 8 GB of RAM, and support for dual 4K displays.
- Raspberry Pi runs on a variety of operating systems, including Raspbian, Ubuntu, and Windows 10 IoT Core. It can also run a variety of applications, including web browsers, media players, and programming environments like Scratch and Python.
- Raspberry Pi is often used for educational purposes, as it provides an affordable and accessible way to learn about programming, electronics, and computer science. It is also used in a variety of projects, including home automation, robotics, and internet of things (IoT) devices.
- Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of users and developers, who share projects, tutorials, and resources on the Raspberry Pi website and forums. This makes it easy to find help and support when working with Raspberry Pi.

Overall, Raspberry Pi is a powerful and versatile device that can be used for a wide range of purposes. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced programmer, it provides an affordable and accessible way to learn about software and hardware frameworks, and to build your own projects in the world of wearable computing, mixed reality, and internet of everything.
