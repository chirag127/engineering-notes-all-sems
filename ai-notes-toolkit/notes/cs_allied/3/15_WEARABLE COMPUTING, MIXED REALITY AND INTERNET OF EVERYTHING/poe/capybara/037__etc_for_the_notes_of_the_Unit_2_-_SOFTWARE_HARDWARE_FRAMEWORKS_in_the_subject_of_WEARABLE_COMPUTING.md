### Unit 2: SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS

In this unit, we will study the software and hardware frameworks used in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything. Here are the key points to remember:

- Wearable computing devices require specific software and hardware frameworks to operate effectively. These frameworks include operating systems, sensors, and communication modules.
- The most common operating systems used in wearable computing include Android, iOS, and Linux. Each of these operating systems has its strengths and weaknesses, and developers must choose the best one for their specific needs.
- Sensors are an essential part of wearable computing devices. These sensors include accelerometers, gyroscopes, and heart rate monitors. They are used to collect data and provide information to the user.
- Communication modules are used to connect wearable devices to the internet, other devices, and networks. Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks are the most common communication modules used in wearable computing.
- Mixed reality devices require specialized hardware, such as cameras and sensors, to create a virtual environment. These devices also require specific software frameworks, such as Unity and Unreal Engine, to create and render virtual objects.
- The internet of everything relies on a network of interconnected devices and systems. These devices include sensors, actuators, and controllers. They rely on specific hardware and software frameworks to communicate and work together effectively.
- In summary, wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything require specific software and hardware frameworks to function effectively. Developers must choose the best frameworks for their needs based on factors such as performance, compatibility, and cost.
