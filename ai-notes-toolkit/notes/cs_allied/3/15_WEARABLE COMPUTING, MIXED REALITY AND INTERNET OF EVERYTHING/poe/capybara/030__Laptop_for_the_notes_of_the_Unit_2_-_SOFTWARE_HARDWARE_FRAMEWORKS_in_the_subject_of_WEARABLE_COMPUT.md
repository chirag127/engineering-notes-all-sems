### Laptop for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING.

In this unit, we will be learning about software hardware frameworks in the subject of wearable computing, mixed reality and internet of everything. To take proper notes and keep up with the course, it is important to have a reliable laptop. Here are some points to consider when choosing a laptop for this purpose:

- Processing power: Look for a laptop with a powerful processor such as Intel Core i5 or i7 to ensure smooth performance while running software and applications related to the course. 
- Memory: A minimum of 8GB RAM is recommended to ensure that the laptop can handle multitasking and running resource-intensive applications without any lag. 
- Storage: A laptop with 256GB or more storage capacity is recommended to store notes, projects, and assignments. 
- Battery life: Look for a laptop with a battery life of at least 6-8 hours to ensure that you can attend classes and take notes without worrying about running out of charge.
- Display: A larger display, preferably 14 inches or more, is recommended to comfortably read and take notes. 
- Graphics: A dedicated graphics card is not necessary for this course, but a laptop with an integrated graphics card such as Intel HD Graphics will suffice. 
- Connectivity: Make sure that the laptop has WiFi and Bluetooth connectivity to access online resources and transfer files wirelessly. 
- Operating system: Windows or Mac OS are both suitable for this course. Choose the one you are most comfortable with. 

It is also recommended to invest in a good quality laptop bag or sleeve to protect your device while transporting it to and from classes. With these points in mind, you can choose a laptop that will meet your needs for this course and beyond.
