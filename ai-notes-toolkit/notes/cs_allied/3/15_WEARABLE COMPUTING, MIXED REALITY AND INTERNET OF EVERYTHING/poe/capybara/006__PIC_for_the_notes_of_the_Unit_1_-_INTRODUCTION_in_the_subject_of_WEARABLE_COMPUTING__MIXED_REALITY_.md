### PIC for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

In this unit, we will be introduced to the exciting field of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything. Here are some points to keep in mind:

- Wearable Computing refers to the technologies that enable people to wear computing devices as accessories, such as smartwatches, fitness trackers, and augmented reality glasses.
- Mixed Reality (MR) is a combination of the physical and virtual worlds, where digital information is overlaid onto the physical environment. MR can enhance the user's experience by providing them with additional information, such as directions or product information.
- Internet of Everything (IoE) is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet, allowing them to interact with each other and with people. IoE includes devices such as smart thermostats, security systems, and even cars.
- The field of Wearable Computing, Mixed Reality, and Internet of Everything has the potential to transform the way we live, work, and interact with each other. It can lead to better health outcomes, more efficient work processes, and more personalized experiences.
- Some of the challenges associated with this field include privacy concerns, security risks, and the need for standardization of technologies.
- This unit will cover the basics of these technologies, their applications, and their impact on society. We will also explore some of the ethical and social implications of these technologies.
