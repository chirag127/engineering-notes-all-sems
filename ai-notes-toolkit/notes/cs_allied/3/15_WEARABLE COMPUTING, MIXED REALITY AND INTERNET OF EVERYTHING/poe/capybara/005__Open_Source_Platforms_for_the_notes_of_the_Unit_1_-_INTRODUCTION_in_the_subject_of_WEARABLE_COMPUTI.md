### Open Source Platforms for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

In the world of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything, open source platforms play a crucial role. Here are some of the most important open source platforms used in this field:

- **Arduino:** Arduino is an open source electronics platform that is used to create interactive projects. It is widely used in the field of wearable computing and mixed reality to create wearable devices that interact with their environment. Arduino is easy to use and has a large community of users who share their projects and code.

- **Raspberry Pi:** Raspberry Pi is a small, affordable computer that can be used to create a wide range of projects. It is often used in the field of wearable computing and mixed reality to create prototypes and test ideas. Raspberry Pi is also popular for its ease of use and the large community of developers who contribute to its development.

- **Unity3D:** Unity3D is a powerful game engine that is used to create 3D games and interactive applications. It is also used in the field of mixed reality to create virtual and augmented reality experiences. Unity3D is open source and has a large community of developers who contribute to its development.

- **OpenCV:** OpenCV is an open source computer vision library that is used to create applications that can interpret and understand visual data. It is often used in the field of mixed reality to create applications that can recognize and track objects in the real world. OpenCV is widely used and has a large community of developers who contribute to its development.

- **TensorFlow:** TensorFlow is an open source machine learning library that is used to create applications that can learn from data. It is often used in the field of wearable computing and the Internet of Everything to create applications that can analyze data from sensors and other sources. TensorFlow is widely used and has a large community of developers who contribute to its development.

These are just a few examples of the many open source platforms that are used in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the Internet of Everything. By using these platforms, developers can create innovative and powerful applications that can change the way we interact with technology.
