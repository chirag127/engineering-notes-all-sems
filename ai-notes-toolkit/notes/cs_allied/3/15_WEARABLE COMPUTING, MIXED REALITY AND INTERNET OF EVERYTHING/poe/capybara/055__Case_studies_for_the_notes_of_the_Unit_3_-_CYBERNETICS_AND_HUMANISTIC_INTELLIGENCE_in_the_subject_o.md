### Case Studies for the Notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the Subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

In this unit, we will be exploring the various case studies related to cybernetics and humanistic intelligence in the field of wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything. These case studies will help us understand the real-world applications of these technologies and their impact on society.

Here are some of the case studies that we will be discussing:

1. **Google Glass**: Google Glass was a wearable device that allowed users to access information and interact with the world around them. It was one of the first wearable devices to gain mainstream attention. However, due to privacy concerns and the high cost, the device failed to take off.

2. **Microsoft HoloLens**: Microsoft HoloLens is a mixed reality headset that overlays 3D holographic images onto the real world. It has found applications in various fields, including healthcare, education, and entertainment.

3. **Fitbit**: Fitbit is a wearable device that tracks a user's physical activity, heart rate, and sleep patterns. It has become popular among fitness enthusiasts and has helped people lead healthier lifestyles.

4. **Amazon Go**: Amazon Go is a store that uses a combination of sensors, cameras, and machine learning algorithms to enable customers to shop without the need for checkout. It is an example of how the internet of everything can be used to create a seamless shopping experience.

5. **Tesla Autopilot**: Tesla Autopilot is a self-driving feature in Tesla cars that uses sensors, cameras, and advanced algorithms to navigate the vehicle. It has the potential to revolutionize the transportation industry and make roads safer.

These case studies demonstrate the potential of cybernetics and humanistic intelligence in wearable computing, mixed reality, and the internet of everything. As we continue to develop these technologies, it will be important to consider their impact on society and ensure that they are used ethically and responsibly.
