### Mixed Reality for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Mixed reality (MR) is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time.
2. MR makes it possible for physical and digital objects to co-exist and interact in real time. This is achieved by immersing users in a digital world and integrating computer-generated perceptual information into the real world.
3. The key capabilities of MR are:
- Combination of real and virtual worlds
- Interaction between real and virtual objects
- Registration (aligning virtual and real worlds)
- Tracking (determining position/orientation)
4. Examples of MR include smart glasses, head-mounted displays and AR contact lenses. These devices can overlay computer-generated images on the real world or allow users to explore 3D virtual environments.
5. Key applications of MR include:
- Enterprise: Remote collaboration, design and prototyping
- Entertainment: Gaming, sports viewing
- Healthcare: Surgical planning/training, rehabilitation
- Education: Enhanced learning experiences
- Shopping: Virtual product trials and previews
