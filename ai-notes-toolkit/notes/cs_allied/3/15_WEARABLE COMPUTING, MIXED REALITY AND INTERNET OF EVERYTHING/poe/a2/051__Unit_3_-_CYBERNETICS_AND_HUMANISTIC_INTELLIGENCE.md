## Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE

1. Cybernetics: Cybernetics is the study of self-governed systems. It is concerned with exploring the ways in which the control of systems influences their behaviors. In cybernetics, the focus is on how systems regulate themselves through feedback loops and adapt to their environments. The core idea in cybernetics is that the design of control systems can be applied broadly, to mechanical, physical, biological, or social systems.

2. Humanistic Intelligence: Humanistic intelligence is the study of human intelligence that focuses on the emotional, creative, intuitive, and spiritual aspects of human intelligence. It emphasizes human experiences, perspectives, and values, rather than just cognitive processes. The key perspectives in humanistic intelligence include:

    - Holism: Viewing individuals as whole beings rather than just the sum of their parts. Humanistic intelligence focuses on people's overall sense of purpose and meaning.
    - Subjectivity: Recognizing the importance of human experiences, perceptions, feelings, and beliefs. There is an emphasis on how people construct their own realities.
    - Growth and self-actualization: Encouraging individuals to reach their full potential through creativity, spontaneity, problem solving, and a grasp of existential questions. Personal growth and fulfillment are key goals.
    - Free will: Believing that people can choose their paths in life and direct their behavior. They have agency and are not just subject to external determinants.
    - Positive regard: Having an attitude of warmth, caring, and non-judgment toward others. People's potential for growth is nurtured through unconditional positive regard.
