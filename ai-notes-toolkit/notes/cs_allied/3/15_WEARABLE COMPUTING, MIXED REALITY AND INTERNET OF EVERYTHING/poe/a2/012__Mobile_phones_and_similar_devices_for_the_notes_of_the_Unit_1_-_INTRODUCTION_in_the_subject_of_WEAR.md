### Mobile phones and similar devices

- Mobile phones are handheld devices primarily used for mobile voice and data communication over a cellular network of base stations known as cell towers.
- Modern smartphones incorporate the functionality of other portable mobile devices like PDAs, MP3 players, digital cameras and navigation systems.
- Smartphones have Internet connectivity and also offer applications for productivity, entertainment and social networking.
- Devices like tablets, phablets and wearable devices like smart watches and head mounted displays can also connect to cellular networks and Wi-Fi, incorporate various features of smartphones and in some cases accept SIM cards.
- The growing capabilities and decreasing costs of these devices are enabling new modes of working and learning as well as access to information, entertainment, social connectivity and transactions.
- These technologies raise new opportunities but also complex questions about privacy, security, addiction, inclusion and societal impacts that researchers and designers are studying.
