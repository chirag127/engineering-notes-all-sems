### Representing “reality” with computers

- Computers can represent reality through data and models of the real world
- Data captures aspects of the real world as numbers, text, images, audio, etc.
- Models are abstract representations of reality that can be simulated and used to make predictions or decisions
- Capturing data and building models of reality allows us to:
  - Analyze the world and gain insights (e.g. patterns in data)
  - Control and interact with the world (e.g. autonomous systems)
  - Create simulated worlds (e.g. virtual/augmented reality)
- However, all representations of reality with computers are limited approximations
- They depend on the data and models we choose to build, which are constrained by:
  - Available sensors and data (we can’t sense everything about the world)
  - Algorithms and computing power (models are simplified)
  - Human choices, biases, and understanding (we decide what to measure and how to model)
- As a result, we must be aware of the limitations of digital representations of reality if we are to use them responsibly and ethically
