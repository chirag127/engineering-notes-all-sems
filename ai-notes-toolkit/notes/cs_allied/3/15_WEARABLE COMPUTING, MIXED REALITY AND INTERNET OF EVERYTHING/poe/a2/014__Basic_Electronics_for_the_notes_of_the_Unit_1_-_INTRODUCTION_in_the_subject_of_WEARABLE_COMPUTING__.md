### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

1. Electric charge: Electric charge is a fundamental property of matter that causes it to experience a force when placed in an electromagnetic field. Positive and negative charges attract each other, while charges of the same sign repel each other.

2. Current: The flow of electric charge is called an electric current. It is measured in amperes (A). Materials can be classified into conductors, insulators, and semiconductors based on how easily they conduct electric current.

3. Voltage: Voltage is the amount of "push" that drives a current. It is measured in volts (V). A higher voltage means a higher push and causes current to flow more easily through a circuit. Voltage is analogous to pressure in a water circuit.

4. Resistance: The extent to which a material opposes the flow of current is called resistance. It is measured in ohms (?). Materials with high resistance have few charge carriers available to conduct current, while low resistance means current can flow more freely. Resistance causes the electrical energy to dissipate as heat.

5. Circuit: A complete path that allows current to flow is called an electric circuit. If the circuit is complete, it is called a closed circuit, while an incomplete path is an open circuit. Electric devices use circuits to control the flow of current and perform various functions.
