### Microcontrollers for the notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE HARDWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Microcontrollers are integrated circuits that contain a processor core, memory, and programmable I/O peripherals.
- They are embedded as a component in a larger device for controlling and monitoring its functions.
- Microcontrollers are used in automatically controlled products and devices, such as automobile engine control systems, implantable medical devices, remote controls, office machines, appliances, power tools, and toys.
- By reducing the size and cost compared to a design that uses a separate microprocessor, memory, and input/output devices.
- Microcontrollers make it more economical to digitally control mechanical and other devices and thus allow more devices to be digitally controlled.
- Examples of popular microcontrollers include:
    - Arduino boards that use various microcontrollers such as ATmega328.
    - Raspberry Pi that uses ARM-based processors.
    - Intel Galileo that uses Quark SoC X1000.
    - Basic Stamp microcontroller that uses PIC microcontroller.
