### Arm Devices

- Arm processors are RISC architecture based CPUs designed by Arm Holdings.
- They are widely used in smartphones, tablets, wearables and IoT devices due to their low power consumption and cost efficiency.
- Some popular Arm CPU architectures are:
-- ARM Cortex-A series: Used in applications requiring high performance such as smartphones and tablets. Examples are Cortex-A53, Cortex-A73, Cortex-A78.
-- ARM Cortex-M series: Used in microcontroller applications requiring real-time processing and low power consumption. Examples are Cortex-M0, Cortex-M3, Cortex-M4.
-- ARM Cortex-R series: Used in applications requiring high reliability and real-time processing. Example is Cortex-R5.
- The instruction set architecture of Arm CPUs is 32-bit or 64-bit ARMv7 or ARMv8 based. The 32-bit architectures are more widely used in low-end devices while 64-bit are used in high-end devices requiring more memory.
- Some benefits of using Arm processors are:
-- Low power consumption leading to longer battery life.
-- Small die size leading to lower cost.
-- Support for a wide range of operating systems such as Android, iOS, Windows, Linux, etc.
-- Large ecosystem of third party support for development.
