### Advanced I/O for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI): Direct communication pathway between the brain and an external device. It senses brain signals and translates them into commands that control the device. Examples: EEG, fMRI, Infrared Spectroscopy.
- Neuromorphic Computing: Inspired by neurobiology. Uses analog circuits/memristors to mimic neuro-biological architectures present in the nervous system. Examples: IBM's TrueNorth, SpiNNaker machine.
- Augmented/Virtual Reality (AR/VR): Immersive technologies that enhance our perception of reality. AR overlays digital information on the real world via devices like smart glasses. VR creates an entirely artificial environment using headsets. Examples: Microsoft HoloLens (AR), Oculus Rift (VR).
- Wearable Computing: Smart electronic devices worn on the body that provide computing capabilities. Examples: Smart watches, fitness trackers, smart clothing.
- Ubiquitous/Pervasive Computing: Embedded computational power in everyday objects and environments. Making computing available anywhere and anytime. Examples: Smart homes, smart cities.
