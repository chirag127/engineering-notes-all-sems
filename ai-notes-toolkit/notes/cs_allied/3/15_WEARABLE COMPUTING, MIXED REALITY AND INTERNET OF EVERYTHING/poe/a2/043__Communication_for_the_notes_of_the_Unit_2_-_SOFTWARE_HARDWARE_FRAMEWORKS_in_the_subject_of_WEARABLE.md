### Communication
- Wireless networks: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular networks enable communication between devices.
- Protocols: Communication between devices follow protocols like HTTP, MQTT, CoAP, etc. These define rules for exchanging data.
- APIs: Applications interact with each other through APIs. APIs define interfaces and messaging formats.
- Cloud: The cloud provides a centralized infrastructure for communication, data storage and computing needs of IoT systems. Devices connect to cloud servers to access capabilities and share data.
- Messaging: Devices communicate by sending and receiving messages in appropriate formats. Message queues and brokers help decouple sender and receiver.
- Security: Communication channels must be secure through methods like encryption, authentication, access control, etc. to protect data and device integrity and privacy.
