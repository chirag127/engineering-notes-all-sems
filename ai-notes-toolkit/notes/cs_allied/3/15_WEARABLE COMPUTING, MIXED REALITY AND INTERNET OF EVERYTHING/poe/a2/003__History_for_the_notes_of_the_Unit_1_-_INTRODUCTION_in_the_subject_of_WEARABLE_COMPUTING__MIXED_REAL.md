### History for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING

- The concept of wearable computing and smart fabrics has been around since the 1960s. However, due to limitations in computing power, battery technology and material science, wearable devices did not gain mainstream popularity until the 2010s.
- In the 1970s, the first wireless headphones and wearable calculators were created. In the 1980s, wearable glucose monitors and fitness trackers were invented.
- In the 1990s and 2000s, more powerful and compact sensors, microprocessors and wireless technologies enabled new wearable devices. Notable devices included Bluetooth headsets, smart watches and wearable fitness trackers.
- In 2012, Google released Google Glass, one of the first smart glasses. In 2014, the Samsung Gear Smartwatch was released, followed by the Apple Watch in 2015.
- The release of these and other smart wearable devices has accelerated the growth of the Internet of Things and increased public awareness of smart fabrics, smart clothing and other smart wearable technologies.
- Continued innovation in hardware, computing, batteries and materials is enabling new types of smart wearable devices and smart fabrics. The integration of artificial intelligence, augmented reality and virtual reality into wearable computing is enabling new mixed reality experiences.
- The potential for smart wearable technologies to transform various industries and human capabilities is enormous. However, there are also risks and ethical concerns surrounding privacy, data security, inequality of access and over-reliance on technology that must be addressed.
