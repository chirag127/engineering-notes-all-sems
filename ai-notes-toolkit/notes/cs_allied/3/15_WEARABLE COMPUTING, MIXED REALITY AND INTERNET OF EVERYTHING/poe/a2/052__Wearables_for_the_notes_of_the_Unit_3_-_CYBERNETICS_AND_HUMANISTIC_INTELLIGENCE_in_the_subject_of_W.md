### Wearables for the notes of the Unit 3 - CYBERNETICS AND HUMANISTIC INTELLIGENCE in the subject of WEARABLE COMPUTING, MIXED REALITY AND INTERNET OF EVERYTHING:

1. Wearable devices: Wearable devices refer to electronic technologies or computers that are incorporated into items that can be comfortably worn on the body. These wearable devices can perform various tasks like monitoring health, providing feedback, displaying information etc. Some examples of wearable devices are smart watches, smart glasses, fitness trackers, health monitoring devices etc.

2. Types of wearable devices: The major types of wearable devices are:

a) Smart watches: Smart watches are wrist-worn devices that can perform tasks like displaying notifications, tracking health metrics like steps taken, heart rate etc., controlling music, making payments etc. Some popular smart watch brands are Apple Watch, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Fitbit Versa etc.

b) Smart glasses: Smart glasses are eye-worn devices that can display information in the user's field of view using augmented reality. Some applications of smart glasses include seeing notifications, navigation, translating text etc. Some brands that manufacture smart glasses are Google Glass, Microsoft Hololens, Snap Spectacles etc.

c) Fitness trackers: Fitness trackers are wrist-worn devices that can track metrics related to a user's fitness like steps taken, calories burnt, heart rate, sleep quality etc. This data can help users track their activity levels and make improvements. Some popular fitness tracker brands are Fitbit, Xiaomi Mi Band, Samsung Galaxy Fit etc.

3. Applications of wearable devices: Some potential applications of wearable devices are:

a) Healthcare: Wearable devices can be used to continuously monitor health metrics and detect health issues early. They can be used to monitor conditions like heart disease, diabetes, sleep disorders etc.

b) Fitness: Wearable fitness trackers can help track physical activity levels and provide motivation to achieve fitness goals.

c) Enterprise: Wearable devices can be used for hands-free data access and completion of tasks in industries like manufacturing, warehousing etc. They can provide employees with information quickly and increase productivity.

d) Entertainment: Smart glasses can be used to provide immersive augmented reality and virtual reality experiences for entertainment.

e) Communication: Wearable devices with augmented reality can be used to facilitate communication through gestures, translations etc.
