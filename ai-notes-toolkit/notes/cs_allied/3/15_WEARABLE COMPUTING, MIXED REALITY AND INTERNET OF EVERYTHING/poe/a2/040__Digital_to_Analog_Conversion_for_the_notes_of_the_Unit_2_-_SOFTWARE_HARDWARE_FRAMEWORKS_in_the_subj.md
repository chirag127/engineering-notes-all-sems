### Digital to Analog Conversion

- Digital to analog conversion is the process of converting a digital signal into an analog signal.
- It is done by digital to analog converters (DAC) which take in a digital input and produce an analog output.
- The digital input is a sequence of numerical values represented by bits. The DAC converts these bits into a voltage or current.
- Higher the number of bits used, higher is the precision of analog output. Most common types of DACs are resistor ladder network, oversampling converters and delta-sigma converters.
- DACs are used in audio equipment, measuring systems, generating analog signals etc. to produce an analog output from a digital input.
- The key parameters for DACs are resolution, sampling rate, linearity and noise. Resolution determines analog output precision, sampling rate determines maximum output frequency, linearity determines accurate conversion and noise affects signal distortion.
