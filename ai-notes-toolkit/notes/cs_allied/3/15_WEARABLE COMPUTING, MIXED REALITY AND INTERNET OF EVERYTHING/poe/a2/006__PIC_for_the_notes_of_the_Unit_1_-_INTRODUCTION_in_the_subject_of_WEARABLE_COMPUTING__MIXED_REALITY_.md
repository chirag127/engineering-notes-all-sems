### PIC

1. Wearable Computing: Wearable computers are small computing devices that are worn on the body, either as an accessory or as part of the material of the user's clothing. They are hands-free devices with sensors, interfaces and networking capabilities. Examples include smart watches, smart glasses, fitness trackers, etc.

2. Mixed Reality: Mixed reality (MR) is the merging of real and virtual worlds to produce new environments and visualizations where physical and digital objects co-exist and interact in real time. It combines aspects of augmented reality and virtual reality. Using MR headsets or smart glasses, digital information can be overlaid onto the real world.

3. Internet of Everything: The Internet of Everything (IoE) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and machines via the internet. It enables everyday things and devices to send and receive data. With IoE, more devices and systems are being connected to the internet beyond computers and smartphones, allowing them to be smarter and more efficient. IoE leads to integration of technologies like cloud computing, big data analytics, etc.

4. Potential Applications: Wearable computing, mixed reality and Internet of Everything have potential applications in various areas such as healthcare, education, manufacturing, transportation, etc. They enable information access, data capture, monitoring, automation, etc. which can improve experiences, increase efficiencies and enhance safety. However, there are also risks and challenges to address around privacy, security, technical issues, health effects, etc.
