
### Applying Spatial Relationships, Functions, and Models for the Notes of the Unit 2 - RFID False Authentications in the Subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

1. Spatial relationships are the relationships between objects in a physical space. These relationships can be used to describe the layout of a space, the movement of objects within it, and the relationships between objects.

2. Functions are mathematical equations that describe the behavior of objects in a space. They can be used to calculate the position, velocity, and acceleration of objects, as well as the forces that act on them.

3. Models are mathematical representations of physical objects. They can be used to represent the structure, shape, and behavior of objects in a space.

4. RFID false authentications are a type of attack on radio frequency identification (RFID) systems. In this attack, an attacker attempts to gain access to a system by sending false authentication signals to the system.

5. Big data analytics for IoT KCS is the process of using big data analytics to analyze and interpret data from Internet of Things (IoT) devices. This data can be used to gain insights into how IoT devices are being used, and to identify potential security vulnerabilities in the system.
