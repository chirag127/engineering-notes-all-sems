2. The neural networks are designed to identify and classify RFID signals based on their characteristics, such as frequency, amplitude, and phase.
3. The neural networks can be trained to recognize the characteristics of genuine RFID signals, as well as those of false authentications.
4. The neural networks can also be used to detect anomalies in RFID signals, such as those caused by malicious actors.
5. Adaptive neural networks can also be used to help identify and classify RFID tag types, which can be used to detect counterfeit RFID tags.
6. Adaptive neural networks can also be used to detect changes in RFID signals over time, which can help identify changes in the environment that could be used to compromise the security of RFID-enabled systems.
