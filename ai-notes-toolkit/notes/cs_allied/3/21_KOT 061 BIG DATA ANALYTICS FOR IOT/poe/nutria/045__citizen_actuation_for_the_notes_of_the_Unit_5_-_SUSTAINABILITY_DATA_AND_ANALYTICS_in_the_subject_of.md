
### Citizen Actuation for the Notes of Unit 5 - Sustainability Data and Analytics in KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

1. Citizen actuation is a process where citizens are actively involved in the decision-making process of a particular issue.

2. Citizen actuation involves gathering data from citizens, using data analytics to identify trends and patterns, and using the results to inform decisions.

3. Citizen actuation can be used to identify and address sustainability issues, such as energy consumption, water usage, and waste management.

4. Citizen actuation can be used to identify and address environmental issues, such as air pollution, land use, and biodiversity.

5. Citizen actuation can also be used to identify and address social issues, such as poverty, inequality, and access to education.

6. Citizen actuation can be used to identify and address economic issues, such as job creation, economic growth, and wealth distribution.

7. Citizen actuation can be used to identify and address political issues, such as government transparency, public policy, and public participation.

8. Citizen actuation can be used to identify and address technological issues, such as access to information, the use of artificial intelligence, and the development of smart cities.
