
### Intelligent Transportation Systems and Wireless Access inVehicular Environment Technology for Developing Smart Cities

1. Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) are systems that use information and communication technologies to improve the efficiency and safety of transportation. They are used to monitor, control, and manage traffic, as well as to provide information to travelers.

2. Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment (WAVE) technology is a set of standards that enables wireless communication between vehicles and roadside units. This technology is used to support safety-critical applications, such as collision avoidance and traffic management.

3. Smart cities use a variety of technologies, such as ITS and WAVE, to improve the efficiency and safety of transportation. These technologies are used to monitor and control traffic, as well as to provide real-time information to travelers.

4. Developing smart cities requires a comprehensive approach that includes the integration of ITS and WAVE technologies. This integration allows for the sharing of data between vehicles and roadside units, which enables the development of more efficient and safer transportation systems.
