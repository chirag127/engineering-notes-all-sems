
### Spatial Dimensions of Big Data for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID FALSE AUTHENTICATIONS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

1. Big data is characterized by its volume, velocity, variety, and veracity.
2. Spatial big data is characterized by the geographic location of the data.
3. Spatial big data can be used to analyze patterns and trends in physical locations.
4. Radio frequency identification (RFID) is a technology that uses radio waves to identify objects.
5. RFID is commonly used in access control systems, such as door locks.
6. False authentications can occur when an RFID tag is not properly authenticated.
7. False authentications can lead to unauthorized access to secure areas.
8. To prevent false authentications, security measures such as encryption and authentication protocols must be implemented.
