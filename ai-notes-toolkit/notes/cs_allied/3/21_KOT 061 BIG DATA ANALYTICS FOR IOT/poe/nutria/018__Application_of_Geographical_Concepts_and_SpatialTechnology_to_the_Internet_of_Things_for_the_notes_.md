
### Application of Geographical Concepts and SpatialTechnology to the Internet of Things for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID FALSE AUTHENTICATIONS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

1. Geographical concepts provide the foundation for understanding the spatial relationships between objects. This is especially important for the Internet of Things (IoT), where objects are often located in different physical locations.

2. Spatial technology is used to help visualize and analyze data related to the physical location of objects. This includes Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Global Positioning Systems (GPS), and Remote Sensing (RS).

3. RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) is a technology used to identify objects and track their movements. It is commonly used in IoT applications, such as tracking the location of vehicles or goods in a supply chain.

4. False authentications can occur when RFID tags are not properly authenticated. This can lead to inaccurate location data, and can potentially be exploited by malicious actors.

5. To prevent false authentications, organizations should ensure that their RFID tags are properly authenticated, and that their security measures are up to date. Additionally, organizations should be aware of the risks associated with RFID technology, and take steps to mitigate them.
