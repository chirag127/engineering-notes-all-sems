## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

I. Data collection and analysis
- Data can be collected from various internal and external sources like sales reports, customer feedback forms, environmental sensors, etc.
- The collected data needs to be cleaned and organized for analysis. Removing duplicates, filling in missing values, transforming variables, etc. are some of the data cleaning steps.
- Various data analysis techniques like regression, clustering, and segmentation can be applied to gain business insights and make decisions.
- Data visualization is important to present the insights in an easy to understand format like charts, graphs, infographics, etc.

II. Sustainability metrics and KPIs
- Certain metrics are required to measure the sustainability performance of a business. Some examples are carbon footprint, water usage, waste generation, etc.
- Relevant key performance indicators or KPIs can be set for sustainability metrics to measure progress and achieve targets. For e.g. 10% reduction in carbon emissions annually.
- Sustainability metrics and KPIs should be tracked regularly and necessary actions should be taken if the targets are not being met.

III. Sustainability data analytics applications
- Sustainability data analytics can be used in various business applications like:
-- Optimizing and reducing resource consumption. E.g. energy and water usage analytics.
-- Managing supply chain sustainability. E.g. sustainable procurement analytics.
-- Innovating and developing sustainable products. E.g. lifecycle assessment of products.
-- Reporting and disclosing sustainability performance. E.g. creating sustainability reports.
