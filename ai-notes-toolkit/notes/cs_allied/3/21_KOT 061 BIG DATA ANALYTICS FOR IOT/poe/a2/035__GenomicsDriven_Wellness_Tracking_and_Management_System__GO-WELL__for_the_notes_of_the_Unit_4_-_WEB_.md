### GenomicsDriven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a genomics-driven wellness tracking and management system which utilizes an individual's genetic data to provide personalized wellness recommendations.
- It analyzes a person's genetic variants which are associated with health risks and requirements of specific nutrients. Based on the analysis, it provides customized wellness plans including nutritional requirements, lifestyle changes and screening recommendations.
- The wellness plans get continuously updated based on the person's health data, genetic data, physical activities, diet logs, etc. tracked using various IoT devices and wearables. This enables proactive prevention of potential health conditions and lifelong optimized wellness management.
- The massive amount of personalized data is analyzed using big data analytics and machine learning algorithms to gain insights and suggest impactful interventions.
- GO-WELL enhances an individual's health outcomes and reduces healthcare costs through early risk detection and personalized precision interventions. It enables web-enhanced wellness with the convergence of genomics, IoT and big data analytics.
