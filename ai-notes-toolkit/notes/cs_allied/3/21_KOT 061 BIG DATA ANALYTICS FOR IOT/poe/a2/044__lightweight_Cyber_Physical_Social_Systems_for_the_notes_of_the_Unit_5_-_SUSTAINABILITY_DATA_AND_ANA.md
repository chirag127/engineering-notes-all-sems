### Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber Physical Social Systems (CPSS) integrate computing, networking, and physical components with human social dynamics.
- They enable advanced monitoring, coordination, and control of critical systems and promote social interactions.
- Lightweight CPSS have fewer computational, storage, sensing, and networking resources. They are suitable for resource-constrained environments and applications.
- Some examples of lightweight CPSS applications include:
-- Smart homes/offices with basic automation and energy management.
-- Neighborhoods with environment monitoring and emergency response systems.
-- Factories with basic automation and tracking systems.
- The key characteristics of lightweight CPSS are:
-- Limited computing, storage, sensing, and networking capabilities.
-- Focus on basic monitoring, coordination, and control functionalities.
-- Involve human social interactions and decisions.
-- Built using low-cost components and constrained protocols.
- The research challenges in lightweight CPSS include:
-- Developing efficient algorithms and techniques under limited resources.
-- Handling imperfect and uncertain data from limited-capability sensors and human inputs.
-- Incorporating human factors and social interactions with lightweight computing/networking.
-- Ensuring trust, security, privacy, and reliability with limited capabilities.
-- Enabling interoperability among heterogeneous lightweight CPSS.
