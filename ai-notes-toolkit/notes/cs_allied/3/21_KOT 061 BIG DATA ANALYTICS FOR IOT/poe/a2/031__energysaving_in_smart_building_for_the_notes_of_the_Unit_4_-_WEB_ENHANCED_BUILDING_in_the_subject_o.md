### energysaving in smart building

1. Smart meters - Smart meters provide real-time energy consumption data which helps in monitoring energy usage and identifying areas of waste. This data can be used to develop energy saving strategies.
2. Smart plugs - Smart plugs allow remotely controlling and scheduling the power supply to devices. This can be used to automate the switching off of devices when not in use to reduce idle energy consumption.
3. Smart thermostats - Smart thermostats allow programming and adjusting the temperature based on occupancy and usage. This can optimize the heating/cooling and lead to energy savings. The temperature can be lowered when occupants are away or sleeping and increased when occupants are active to maintain comfort.
4. Smart lighting - Smart lighting allows dimming or switching off lights automatically when not needed. Motion sensors and ambient light sensors can be used to detect occupancy and daylight levels to control the lights and reduce energy usage. Timers can also be used to switch off lights when not required.
5. Building energy management systems - Building energy management systems collect data from smart devices and meters to monitor and optimize the energy consumption of a building. The data can be analyzed to identify opportunities for automation and energy savings which can then be implemented through controls. This leads to an efficient and sustainable energy management of smart buildings.
