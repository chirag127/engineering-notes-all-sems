### Intelligent Transportation Systems and Wireless Access inVehicular Environment Technology for Developing Smart Cities

1. Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)
- Integrate information and communication technologies with transportation infrastructure and vehicles to improve safety, mobility and efficiency.
- Examples: Traffic management systems, public transit systems, emergency management systems, roadway maintenance systems, etc.
- Sensors, cameras, GPS and wireless communications are used to collect and share data.
- The data is analyzed to provide customized information and make transportation smarter.

2. Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment (WAVE)
- A communication protocol standard to allow vehicles and roadside infrastructure to exchange data over wireless networks.
- Facilitates vehicle-to-vehicle and vehicle-to-infrastructure communication to improve traffic efficiency and safety.
- Uses IEEE 802.11p at the physical and medium access control layers and the IEEE 1609 standards for the upper layers.
- The information exchanged includes vehicle speed, location, braking, traffic signals, road hazards, etc.

3. Developing Smart Cities
- ITS and WAVE can be integrated into smart cities to gain several benefits:
-- Reduced traffic congestion and travel time.
-- Improved road safety through collision avoidance and emergency management systems.
-- Monitoring and optimization of parking, public transit, road maintenance, etc.
-- Environmental benefits through reduced emissions and energy consumption.
-- Better mobility and accessibility for all including disabled and elderly people.
- Seamless integration of these technologies into transportation infrastructure and vehicles is essential to realize the full potential of smart cities.
