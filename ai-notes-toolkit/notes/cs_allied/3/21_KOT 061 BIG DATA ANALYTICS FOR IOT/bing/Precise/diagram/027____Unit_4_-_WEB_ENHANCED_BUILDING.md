## Unit 4 - WEB ENHANCED BUILDING

Web-enhanced buildings are structures that have been designed and built to incorporate advanced technology and connectivity features. These features can include:

1. High-speed internet connectivity: Web-enhanced buildings are equipped with high-speed internet connections, allowing tenants to access the internet at fast speeds.

2. Smart building systems: These buildings may also incorporate smart building systems, such as automated lighting, heating, and cooling systems, which can be controlled remotely.

3. Advanced security systems: Web-enhanced buildings may also feature advanced security systems, such as surveillance cameras and access control systems, to ensure the safety of tenants.

4. Energy-efficient design: Many web-enhanced buildings are designed to be energy-efficient, incorporating features such as solar panels and green roofs to reduce their environmental impact.

Overall, web-enhanced buildings provide tenants with a modern, connected, and secure living or working environment. They are becoming increasingly popular as technology continues to advance and the demand for connected living and working spaces grows.
