### Citizen Actuation

Citizen actuation refers to the involvement of citizens in the collection, analysis, and use of data to improve sustainability. This can be achieved through various means, including:

1. **Participatory sensing:** This involves citizens using their personal devices, such as smartphones, to collect data on various sustainability indicators, such as air quality, noise levels, and traffic congestion.

2. **Crowdsourcing:** This involves citizens contributing data to a common platform, where it can be analyzed and used to inform decision-making.

3. **Community-based monitoring:** This involves citizens working together to monitor and report on sustainability issues in their local area.

4. **Citizen science:** This involves citizens participating in scientific research, often by collecting and analyzing data.

Citizen actuation can help to improve the accuracy and completeness of sustainability data, as well as increasing public engagement and awareness of sustainability issues. It can also help to empower citizens to take action to improve sustainability in their local area.
