### Potential Stakeholders and Their Complex Relationships to Data and Analytics Applications

When it comes to data and analytics applications, there are several potential stakeholders that may have complex relationships with these technologies. These stakeholders may include:

1. **Data providers:** These are the individuals or organizations that provide the data used in analytics applications. They may have concerns about how their data is being used, who has access to it, and how it is being protected.

2. **Data analysts:** These are the individuals who analyze the data and create insights from it. They may have concerns about the accuracy and completeness of the data, as well as the tools and techniques used to analyze it.

3. **Decision-makers:** These are the individuals or organizations that use the insights generated by data analytics to make decisions. They may have concerns about the reliability and validity of the insights, as well as the ethical implications of using data to make decisions.

4. **End-users:** These are the individuals or organizations that are affected by the decisions made using data analytics. They may have concerns about how their personal data is being used, as well as the fairness and transparency of the decision-making process.

5. **Regulators:** These are the government agencies or other organizations that oversee the use of data and analytics. They may have concerns about the protection of personal data, as well as the ethical and legal implications of using data to make decisions.

These stakeholders may have complex relationships with data and analytics applications, as their interests and concerns may not always align. It is important for organizations to consider the perspectives of all stakeholders when developing and implementing data and analytics applications. This can help to ensure that these technologies are used in a responsible and sustainable manner.
