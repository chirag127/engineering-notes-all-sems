### YA TRAP

YA TRAP is a technique used to detect false authentication attempts in RFID systems. It is a part of the Unit 2 - RFID False Authentications in the subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS.

1. YA TRAP stands for "Yet Another TRAP" and is a method for detecting false authentication attempts in RFID systems.
2. It works by monitoring the communication between the RFID reader and the RFID tag, and detecting any anomalies that may indicate a false authentication attempt.
3. YA TRAP is an effective way to prevent unauthorized access to RFID systems and to protect the data stored on RFID tags.
4. It is an important topic in the study of Big Data Analytics for IoT, as it helps to ensure the security and integrity of data in IoT systems.
