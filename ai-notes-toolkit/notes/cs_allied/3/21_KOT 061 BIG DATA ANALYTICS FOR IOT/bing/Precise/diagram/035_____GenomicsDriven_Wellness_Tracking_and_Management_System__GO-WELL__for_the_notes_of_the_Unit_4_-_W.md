### GenomicsDriven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a system that utilizes genomics data to track and manage an individual's wellness.
- It is a part of Unit 4 - WEB ENHANCED BUILDING in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS.
- The system uses an individual's genomic information to provide personalized recommendations for diet, exercise, and lifestyle choices.
- By analyzing an individual's genetic makeup, GO-WELL can identify potential health risks and provide preventative measures to reduce the likelihood of developing certain conditions.
- The system also allows for the tracking of an individual's progress towards their wellness goals, providing feedback and support along the way.
- GO-WELL represents a shift towards a more personalized approach to healthcare, utilizing the latest advancements in genomics and big data analytics to improve overall health and wellbeing.
