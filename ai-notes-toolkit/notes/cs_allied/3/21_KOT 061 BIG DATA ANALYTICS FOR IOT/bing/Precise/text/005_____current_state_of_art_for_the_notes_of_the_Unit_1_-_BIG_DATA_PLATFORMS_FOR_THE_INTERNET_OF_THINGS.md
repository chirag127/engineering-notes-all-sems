### Current State of Art for the Notes of the Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the Subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Big Data is a term used to describe large and complex data sets that traditional data processing methods are unable to handle.
- Big Data frameworks and libraries are constantly evolving to meet the needs of businesses and organizations that need to process and analyze large amounts of data.
- Big Data architecture is the design that serves as the foundation for big data systems. It provides a framework for addressing the needs and scope of business users.
- Big Data-as-a-Service (BDaaS) is a framework that provides access to big data tools and technologies on a subscription basis.
- Big Data analytics techniques are used to extract valuable insights from large and complex data sets. These techniques are constantly evolving to meet the needs of businesses and organizations.
- Big Data systems can be benchmarked using a variety of methods, including Map-Reduce based system benchmarking, SQL-based analytical system benchmarking, NoSQL-based database benchmarking, and Big graph system benchmarking.
