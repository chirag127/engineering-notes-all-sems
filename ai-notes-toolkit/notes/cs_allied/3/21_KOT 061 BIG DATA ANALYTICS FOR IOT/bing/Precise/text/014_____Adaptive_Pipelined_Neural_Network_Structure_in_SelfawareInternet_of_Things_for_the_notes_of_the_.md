### Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network Structure in Self-aware Internet of Things

- Adaptive neural networks are proposed as a solution to some of the challenges in self-healing systems, such as monitoring the system and environment, mapping a suitable solution and adapting the system accordingly.
- A novel application of a modified Pipelined Recurrent Neural Network is proposed in this field with experiments aimed to assess its applicability to online.
- Recursive neural networks have been proposed and used to solve the main challenges of self-healing, such as monitoring, interpretation, resolution, and adaptation.
- A modified pipelined neural network is introduced to fulfill the requirements in this field. This technique is examined and tested with different fields.
