### Citizen Actuation

Citizen actuation refers to the involvement of citizens in data science and analytics to drive sustainability. This can be achieved through the empowerment of citizen data science (CDS), which requires a comprehensive ecosystem that includes people, tools, data, and processes.

- Citizen data scientists can boost an organization’s business value and analytics maturity; however, in most cases, their capabilities remain underutilized.
- As the power of data science provides organizations with differentiating competitive advantages, the demand for talent is rising. Supply, meanwhile, remains too scarce to meet that demand.
- Data and analytics leaders must provide an environment that integrates and supports citizen data scientists by leveraging their skills and maximizing their contribution.
- Citizen data science scales machine learning and data science efforts across the organization.
- The analytics capabilities and use cases enable data and analytics leaders to pursue CDS efforts in innovative and impactful ways.

Citizen actuation can help make improvements with issues related to environmental, social, and economic sustainability. For the analytics-minded and socially conscious, there are multiple new paths in which to launch a career in this Age of Information.
