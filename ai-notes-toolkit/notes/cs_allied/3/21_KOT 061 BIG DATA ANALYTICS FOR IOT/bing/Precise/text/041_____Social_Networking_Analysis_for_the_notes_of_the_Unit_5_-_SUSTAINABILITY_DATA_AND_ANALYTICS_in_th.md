### Social Networking Analysis

Social Networking Analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of network and graph theories. It is a key technique in the field of sustainability data and analytics, particularly in the context of Big Data Analytics for IoT (KOT 061).

Here are some key points to consider when studying SNA for Unit 5 - Sustainability Data and Analytics:

1. SNA can be used to identify and analyze patterns of relationships between individuals, groups, and organizations.
2. It can help to uncover hidden connections and relationships that may not be immediately apparent.
3. SNA can be used to analyze the flow of information and resources within a network, and to identify key influencers and decision-makers.
4. It can also be used to identify potential areas for intervention or improvement, and to evaluate the effectiveness of interventions.
5. SNA is a powerful tool for understanding complex systems and can be applied to a wide range of sustainability issues, including energy use, resource management, and waste reduction.
