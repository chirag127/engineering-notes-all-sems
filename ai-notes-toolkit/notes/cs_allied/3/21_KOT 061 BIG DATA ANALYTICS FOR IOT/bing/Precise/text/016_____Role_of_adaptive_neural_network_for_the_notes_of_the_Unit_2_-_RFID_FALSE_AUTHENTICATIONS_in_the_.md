### Role of adaptive neural network for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID FALSE AUTHENTICATIONS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Adaptive neural networks play a key role in the development of intelligent manufacturing and the proliferation and communication in IoT.
- They can be used to identify and filter false-positives from the RFID data stream.
- An observer-based adaptive neural network control can be used for nonlinear strict-feedback CPSs subject to false data injection attacks.
- Self-adaptive RFID authentication is a promising technology for the Internet of Things.
