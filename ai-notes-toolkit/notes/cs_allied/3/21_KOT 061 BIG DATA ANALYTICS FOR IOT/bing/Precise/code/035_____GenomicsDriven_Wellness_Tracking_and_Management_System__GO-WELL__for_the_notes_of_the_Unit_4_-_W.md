### GenomicsDriven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a system that incorporates genomic data into health information systems to provide personalized medicine and wellness tracking.
- It is an emerging technology in the field of health information systems.
- The system is designed to track and manage wellness using genomic data.
- The design principles of the system are based on several concepts, including personalized medicine and the omics revolution.
- The system allows for the incorporation of genomic data into health information systems to provide personalized medicine and wellness tracking.
