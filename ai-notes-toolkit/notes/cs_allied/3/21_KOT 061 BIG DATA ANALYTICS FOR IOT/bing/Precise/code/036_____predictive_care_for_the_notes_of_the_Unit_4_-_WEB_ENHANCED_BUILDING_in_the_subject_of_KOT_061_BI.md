### Predictive Care

Predictive care is a proactive approach to maintenance and repair of equipment and systems. It involves the use of data analytics and machine learning to predict when maintenance or repair is needed, before a failure occurs. This can help to prevent downtime and increase the efficiency of the equipment or system.

Here are some key points to consider when studying predictive care for Unit 4 - WEB ENHANCED BUILDING in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS:

1. Predictive care can be applied to a wide range of equipment and systems, including HVAC, lighting, and security systems in buildings.
2. Data is collected from sensors and other sources to monitor the performance and condition of the equipment or system.
3. This data is analyzed using machine learning algorithms to identify patterns and predict when maintenance or repair is needed.
4. Predictive care can help to reduce downtime and increase the efficiency of the equipment or system.
5. It can also help to reduce maintenance costs and extend the lifespan of the equipment or system.
