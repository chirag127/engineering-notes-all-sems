### Citizen Actuation

- Citizen actuation is the activation of a human being as the mechanism by which a control system acts upon the environment.
- It is a way of closing the loop started by citizen sensing, which is the collection and analysis of data from citizens about events in their surrounding environment.
- Citizen actuation aims to make the citizen reports an actionable item, by sending a task to suitable occupants of an environment to complete.
- Citizen actuation can be used for smart environments, such as smart buildings, smart cities, or smart grids, to improve their efficiency, sustainability, and resilience.
- Citizen actuation can also empower citizens to participate in the decision-making and governance of their environment, by providing them with feedback, incentives, and rewards.
- Citizen actuation requires a framework that can identify the relevant events, select the appropriate actuation channels, match the tasks with the suitable citizens, and monitor the task execution and outcomes.
- Citizen actuation also poses some challenges, such as ensuring the privacy and security of the citizen data, motivating and engaging the citizens to perform the tasks, and balancing the trade-offs between automation and human intervention .
- Citizen actuation is an emerging concept that has the potential to transform the way we interact with our environment, and to create new opportunities for innovation and social good .
