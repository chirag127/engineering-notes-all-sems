### Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network Structure in Self-aware Internet of Things

- Self-aware Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that aims to enable IoT devices to monitor, interpret, and adapt to their own state and environment in a dynamic and autonomous manner.
- Self-aware IoT devices can improve their reliability, performance, and efficiency by adjusting their behavior and configuration according to the changing context and requirements.
- Adaptive neural networks are proposed as a solution to some of the challenges of self-aware IoT, such as monitoring the system and environment, mapping a suitable solution, and adapting the system accordingly.
- A novel application of a modified Pipelined Recurrent Neural Network (PRNN) is proposed in this chapter with experiments aimed to assess its applicability to online learning and adaptation in self-aware IoT.
- A PRNN is a type of recurrent neural network that consists of multiple stages of processing units, each with its own memory and feedback loop, connected in a pipeline structure.
- A PRNN can learn temporal patterns and sequences from data streams and perform parallel and distributed computation, which makes it suitable for IoT applications.
- The proposed modified PRNN introduces two main features: adaptive learning rate and adaptive structure.
- The adaptive learning rate allows the PRNN to adjust the speed of learning based on the error and the confidence of the prediction.
- The adaptive structure allows the PRNN to add or remove stages dynamically based on the complexity and the nonlinearity of the data.
- The experiments show that the modified PRNN can achieve better accuracy and lower computational cost than the original PRNN and other neural network models on different datasets.
- The modified PRNN can also adapt to concept drift and noise in the data streams, which are common challenges in IoT scenarios.
- The modified PRNN can be applied to various self-aware IoT domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, and smart grids.
- The modified PRNN can provide a flexible and scalable framework for self-aware IoT systems that can learn and adapt to the dynamic and uncertain environment.
