### Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber Physical Social Systems (CPSS) are systems that integrate cyber components (such as computation, communication, and sensing) with physical components (such as machines, devices, and humans) and social components (such as networks, behaviors, and preferences) .
- CPSS can be used for various applications, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart grids, smart healthcare, smart transportation, and smart manufacturing .
- Lightweight CPSS are CPSS that have low resource requirements, such as energy, memory, bandwidth, and computation, and can operate efficiently and reliably in dynamic and uncertain environments .
- Lightweight CPSS can be achieved by using various techniques, such as:
  - Distortion-based security: a method that introduces controlled distortion to the communicated data to protect it from adversaries, while preserving the inference quality for legitimate users .
  - Edge computing: a paradigm that offloads computation and storage tasks from the cloud to the edge devices, such as sensors, actuators, and gateways, to reduce latency, bandwidth, and energy consumption .
  - Data fusion: a process that combines data from multiple sources to obtain a more accurate and comprehensive representation of the system state and behavior .
  - Data analytics: a process that applies various techniques, such as machine learning, data mining, and statistics, to extract useful information and insights from large and complex data sets .
  - Social network analysis: a process that studies the structure, dynamics, and interactions of social networks, such as users, groups, communities, and platforms, to understand their patterns, behaviors, and preferences .
- Lightweight CPSS can provide various benefits, such as:
  - Improving the efficiency, monitoring, controllability, and reliability of constructed facilities, such as buildings, bridges, and roads .
  - Enhancing the quality, accessibility, and affordability of services, such as healthcare, education, and entertainment .
  - Increasing the resilience, security, and sustainability of critical infrastructures, such as power grids, water systems, and transportation networks .
  - Promoting the social welfare, participation, and innovation of individuals and communities .
