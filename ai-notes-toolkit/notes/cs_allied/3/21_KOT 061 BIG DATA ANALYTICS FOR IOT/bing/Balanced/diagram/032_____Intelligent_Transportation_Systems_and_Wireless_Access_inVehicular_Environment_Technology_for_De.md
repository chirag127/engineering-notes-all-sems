### Intelligent Transportation Systems and Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment Technology for Developing Smart Cities

- Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) are the application of advanced technologies to improve the efficiency, safety, and sustainability of transportation systems in smart cities .
- ITS involve the use of sensors, communication devices, data analytics, and artificial intelligence to collect, process, and share information among different transportation modes, infrastructure, and users .
- ITS can enable various use cases, such as traffic and mobility management, port modernization, vehicle-to-everything (V2X) communication, electric vehicle charging, and auto-routing and scheduling for smart railways.
- Wireless Access in Vehicular Environment (WAVE) is a set of standards and protocols for wireless communication between vehicles and roadside infrastructure in ITS  .
- WAVE is based on the IEEE 802.11p standard, which defines the physical and medium access control layers for wireless communication in the 5.9 GHz band  .
- WAVE also includes the IEEE 1609 family of standards, which define the security, networking, and application layers for ITS applications and management messages  .
- WAVE can support various services, such as safety, traffic efficiency, infotainment, and environmental monitoring, by enabling the exchange of data among vehicles and infrastructure  .
- WAVE can also enable cooperative and autonomous driving, by allowing vehicles to share their location, speed, direction, and intentions with other vehicles and infrastructure  .
- WAVE can enhance the performance and reliability of ITS applications, by providing low latency, high bandwidth, and broadcast capabilities for wireless communication in vehicular environments  .
- WAVE can also complement other wireless technologies, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth, by providing dedicated and secure channels for ITS applications  .
- WAVE can contribute to the development of smart cities, by enabling the integration of vehicles and infrastructure into a connected and intelligent transportation system, which can improve the mobility, safety, and sustainability of urban transportation  .
