- Spatial relationships, functions, and models are methods of data analytics that are used to analyze geographic patterns and location-based information in the context of IoT applications.
- Spatial relationships are the associations or connections between spatial objects, such as points, lines, polygons, or regions. Spatial relationships can be topological (e.g., adjacency, containment, intersection), directional (e.g., north, south, east, west), or metric (e.g., distance, area, angle).
- Spatial functions are operations that manipulate spatial objects or attributes, such as creating buffers, clipping, intersecting, unioning, or measuring. Spatial functions can be used to perform spatial analysis, such as finding the nearest neighbor, the shortest path, or the optimal location.
- Spatial models are representations of spatial phenomena or processes, such as spatial interpolation, regression, classification, or clustering. Spatial models can be used to predict, explain, or simulate spatial patterns or behaviors, such as spatial autocorrelation, spatial heterogeneity, or spatial dependence.
