# Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS

- Big data platforms are software systems that enable the processing, storage, analysis, and visualization of large and complex data sets generated by various sources, such as sensors, devices, machines, or applications.
- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, actuators, and communication capabilities, and that can interact with each other and with other systems over the internet.
- Big data platforms for the IoT are designed to handle the challenges and opportunities of IoT data, such as high volume, velocity, variety, veracity, and value.
- Big data platforms for the IoT can be classified into three types: connectivity platforms, application platforms, and analytics platforms.
  - Connectivity platforms provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. Among popular connectivity protocols and standards for the IoT are MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN and more.
  - Application platforms provide software tools and services to develop, deploy, and manage IoT applications that run on the connected devices or on the cloud. They also provide features such as device management, security, authentication, authorization, and integration. Among popular application platforms for the IoT are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, Artik from Samsung Electronics, Cisco Cloud Connect, Salesforce Cloud, Watson and BlueMix from IBM, OpenStack .
  - Analytics platforms provide data processing, storage, and analysis capabilities to extract insights and value from the IoT data. They also provide features such as data ingestion, cleansing, transformation, aggregation, querying, visualization, and machine learning. Among popular analytics platforms for the IoT are Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark, Apache Storm, Apache Kafka, Apache Flink, Apache Cassandra, MongoDB, Elasticsearch, and Nexus Integra .
- Big data platforms for the IoT enable various benefits and applications, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart industries, smart healthcare, smart agriculture, smart energy, smart transportation, and more. They also enable new business models and opportunities, such as predictive maintenance, remote monitoring, asset tracking, demand response, and more.
