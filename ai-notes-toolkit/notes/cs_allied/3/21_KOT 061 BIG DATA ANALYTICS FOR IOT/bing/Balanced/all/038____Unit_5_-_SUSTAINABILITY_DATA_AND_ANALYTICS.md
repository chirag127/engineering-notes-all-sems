## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

- This unit introduces the concepts and methods of sustainability data and analytics, which are essential for measuring, monitoring, and improving the environmental, social, and economic performance of organizations and systems.
- The unit covers the following topics:

  - The definition and scope of sustainability data and analytics, and the main sources and types of data available for sustainability assessment and reporting.
  - The principles and standards of sustainability accounting and reporting, such as the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB), and the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD).
  - The techniques and tools for collecting, processing, analyzing, and visualizing sustainability data, such as life cycle assessment (LCA), carbon footprinting, material flow analysis (MFA), and geographic information systems (GIS).
  - The applications and challenges of sustainability data and analytics in various domains and sectors, such as energy, water, waste, transport, agriculture, and health.
  - The future trends and opportunities of sustainability data and analytics, such as big data, artificial intelligence, blockchain, and the internet of things (IoT).

- The learning outcomes of this unit are:

  - To understand the concepts and methods of sustainability data and analytics, and their relevance and importance for sustainability management and decision making.
  - To identify and evaluate the sources and types of sustainability data available for different purposes and contexts, and their quality and limitations.
  - To apply the principles and standards of sustainability accounting and reporting, and to prepare and communicate sustainability reports using appropriate indicators and frameworks.
  - To use the techniques and tools for collecting, processing, analyzing, and visualizing sustainability data, and to interpret and communicate the results and insights.
  - To recognize and address the applications and challenges of sustainability data and analytics in various domains and sectors, and to explore the future trends and opportunities of sustainability data and analytics.
