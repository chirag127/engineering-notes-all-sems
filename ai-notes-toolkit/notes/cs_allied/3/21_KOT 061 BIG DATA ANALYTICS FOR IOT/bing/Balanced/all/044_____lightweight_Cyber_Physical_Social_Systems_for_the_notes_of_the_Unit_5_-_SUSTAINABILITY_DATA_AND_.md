# Lightweight Cyber Physical Social Systems

- Cyber-physical systems (CPS) are systems that integrate computation, communication, sensing, and actuation with physical processes .
- Cyber-physical-social systems (CPSS) are systems that extend CPS by incorporating social networks and human interactions  .
- Lightweight CPSS are systems that aim to reduce the complexity, cost, and resource consumption of CPSS by using efficient algorithms, protocols, and architectures  .
- Some of the benefits of lightweight CPSS are:
  - Improved security and privacy of data and operations .
  - Enhanced performance and scalability of CPSS applications .
  - Reduced energy consumption and environmental impact .
- Some of the challenges of lightweight CPSS are:
  - Handling the heterogeneity and diversity of devices, networks, and users .
  - Ensuring the reliability and robustness of CPSS under uncertainties and disturbances .
  - Balancing the trade-offs between quality of service and resource efficiency .
- Some of the applications of lightweight CPSS are:
  - Smart homes and buildings  .
  - Smart healthcare and wellness  .
  - Smart transportation and mobility  .
