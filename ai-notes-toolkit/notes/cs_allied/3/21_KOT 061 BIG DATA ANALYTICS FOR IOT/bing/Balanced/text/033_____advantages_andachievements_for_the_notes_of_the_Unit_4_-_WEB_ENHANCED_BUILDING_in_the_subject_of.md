### Advantages and Achievements for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Web Enhanced Building in the Subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

- Web enhanced building is the application of IoT and big data analytics to improve the performance, efficiency, and sustainability of buildings.
- Some of the advantages of web enhanced building are:
  - Reduced energy consumption and carbon footprint by optimizing heating, cooling, lighting, and ventilation systems based on real-time data from sensors and smart meters.
  - Improved occupant comfort and productivity by adjusting indoor environmental quality parameters such as temperature, humidity, air quality, and noise levels according to user preferences and feedback.
  - Enhanced security and safety by detecting and preventing unauthorized access, fire, gas leaks, water leaks, and other hazards using cameras, motion sensors, smoke detectors, and alarms.
  - Increased asset value and operational efficiency by monitoring and maintaining the condition and performance of building equipment and infrastructure using predictive analytics and preventive maintenance.
  - Enabled new business models and revenue streams by providing value-added services to tenants, customers, and visitors such as personalized recommendations, dynamic pricing, and loyalty programs.
- Some of the achievements of web enhanced building are:
  - The Korea Superconducting Tokamak Advanced Research (KSTAR) facility, which is a nuclear fusion reactor, uses IoT and big data analytics to monitor and control the plasma temperature, pressure, and magnetic field, as well as to optimize the fusion performance and safety.
  - The Microsoft Azure IoT platform, which is a cloud-based service that enables the development and deployment of IoT solutions, provides various tools and features for building smart buildings, such as Azure Digital Twins, Azure IoT Central, Azure IoT Hub, and Azure IoT Edge.
  - The Amazon Kinesis Firehose and Amazon QuickSight, which are AWS services that enable the capture, transformation, and visualization of streaming data from IoT devices, allow the creation of dashboards and reports for monitoring and analyzing the IoT data from smart buildings.
