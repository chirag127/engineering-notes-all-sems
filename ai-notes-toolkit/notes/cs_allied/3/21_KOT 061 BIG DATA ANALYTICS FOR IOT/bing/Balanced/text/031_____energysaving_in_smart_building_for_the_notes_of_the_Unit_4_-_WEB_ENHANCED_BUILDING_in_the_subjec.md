### Energy Saving in Smart Building

- A smart building is a building that uses advanced technologies and sensors to monitor and control various aspects of its operation, such as lighting, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, security, and occupancy.
- A smart building can improve energy efficiency by reducing energy consumption, increasing renewable energy generation, and optimizing energy management.
- Some of the benefits of smart buildings are:
  - Lower carbon footprint and greenhouse gas emissions
  - Lower operational and maintenance costs
  - Higher occupant comfort and productivity
  - Higher asset value and marketability
- Some of the technologies and strategies that can enable energy saving in smart buildings are:
  - Smart HVAC systems: These systems can adjust the temperature and airflow according to the occupancy, weather, and preferences of the occupants. They can also use waste heat recovery, variable speed drives, and demand-controlled ventilation to save energy .
  - Smart lighting: These systems can dim or turn off the lights when there is enough natural light or no occupancy. They can also use LED bulbs, daylight harvesting, and occupancy sensors to save energy .
  - Smart windows: These windows can change their transparency or tint according to the sunlight and temperature. They can also use electrochromic, thermochromic, or photochromic materials to save energy.
  - Smart energy supply: These systems can generate and store renewable energy on-site, such as solar, wind, or geothermal energy. They can also use smart grids, microgrids, or battery storage to save energy .
  - Smart energy management: These systems can monitor and analyze the energy consumption and generation of the building. They can also use artificial intelligence, machine learning, or cloud computing to optimize the energy performance and reduce the energy demand .
  - Smart space management: These systems can track the occupancy and usage of the building spaces. They can also use smart locks, cameras, or RFID tags to save energy .
