### GenomicsDriven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a concept of a health information system that uses genomic, clinical, environmental, and behavioral data to provide predictive and personalized care for patients .
- GO-WELL aims to engage patients in their own wellness management by providing them with feedback, recommendations, and alerts based on their genomic profile and risk factors .
- GO-WELL leverages the advances in genomics, bioinformatics, and data analytics to interpret the complex interactions between genes, environment, and lifestyle in disease mechanisms .
- GO-WELL is designed to be web-enhanced, meaning that it can be accessed through various devices and platforms, such as smartphones, tablets, computers, and wearable sensors .
- GO-WELL is also designed to be interoperable, meaning that it can exchange and integrate data from different sources and systems, such as electronic health records, genomic databases, and social media .
- GO-WELL is proposed as a case study for prostate cancer, which is a common and complex disease that has both genetic and environmental influences .
- GO-WELL can help prostate cancer patients to monitor their condition, track their treatment outcomes, and prevent potential complications by using genomic biomarkers, clinical indicators, and lifestyle factors .
- GO-WELL can also help prostate cancer patients to participate in research and clinical trials, and to share their data and experiences with other patients and healthcare providers .
- GO-WELL is an example of how emerging technologies can transform health information systems and services to become more effective and efficient    .
