# Current State of Art for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Big Data Platforms for the Internet of Things

- Big Data Platforms for the Internet of Things (IoT) are software systems that enable the collection, processing, analysis, and visualization of large amounts of data generated by IoT devices and sensors.
- Big Data Platforms for the IoT can be classified into three main categories: connectivity platforms, application platforms, and analytics platforms .
- Connectivity platforms provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. Among popular connectivity protocols and standards for the IoT are MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN and more.
- Application platforms provide the tools and services to develop, deploy, and manage IoT applications. They can also provide device management, data integration, security, and user interface features. Some examples of application platforms are AWS IoT, Azure IoT, Google Cloud IoT, IBM Watson IoT, and Salesforce IoT .
- Analytics platforms provide the capabilities to store, process, and analyze large volumes of IoT data. They can also provide machine learning, artificial intelligence, and visualization features. Some examples of analytics platforms are Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark, Apache Kafka, Apache Flink, and Nexus Integra .
- The main challenges and research directions in Big Data Platforms for the IoT are scalability, reliability, security, privacy, interoperability, and real-time processing.
