# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology that aims to improve service delivery and knowledge management in service organizations . Some of the main features and benefits of KCS are:

- It integrates the creation and maintenance of knowledge articles with the resolution of customer issues, making knowledge a by-product of service delivery.
- It empowers service agents to capture, structure, reuse, and improve knowledge articles based on their interactions with customers and their feedback.
- It enables service organizations to leverage the collective experience and expertise of their agents, reducing the dependency on individual experts and increasing the consistency and quality of service.
- It improves the efficiency and effectiveness of service delivery, reducing costs, increasing customer satisfaction, and enhancing service levels.
- It fosters a culture of collaboration, learning, and innovation among service agents, encouraging them to share and improve their knowledge and skills.

KCS is based on a set of principles, practices, and techniques that guide service organizations in implementing and sustaining the methodology. Some of the key elements of KCS are:

- The KCS Solve Loop, which describes the process of capturing, structuring, reusing, and improving knowledge articles during service delivery.
- The KCS Evolve Loop, which describes the process of measuring, managing, and maturing the knowledge base and the KCS practices over time.
- The KCS Adoption Model, which provides a roadmap for service organizations to adopt and optimize KCS in four phases: planning, adoption, proficiency, and leverage.
- The KCS Practices Guide, which provides detailed guidance on how to apply the KCS principles and practices in various service scenarios and contexts.
- The KCS Verified and Aligned programs, which provide standards and recognition for service organizations and technology vendors that demonstrate their adherence and alignment to the KCS methodology.

KCS is also supported by a global community of practitioners, researchers, and consultants who share their knowledge and experience through various channels and events.
