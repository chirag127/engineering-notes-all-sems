# Toward Web Enhanced Building Automation Systems

- Building automation systems (BAS) are systems that control and monitor the mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems in a building, such as heating, ventilation, air conditioning, lighting, security, fire alarms, etc.
- BAS can improve the energy efficiency, comfort, safety and security of buildings, as well as reduce the operational and maintenance costs.
- However, traditional BAS are often proprietary, isolated and incompatible with each other, making it difficult to integrate them with other systems or devices, or to access and analyze the data they generate.
- Web enhanced building automation systems (WEBAS) are systems that leverage the web technologies and standards to enable the interoperability, connectivity and intelligence of BAS.
- WEBAS can benefit from the Internet of Things (IoT) paradigms, which aim to connect objects to the Internet and provide functionalities like machine-to-machine communications, data processing and analytics, and service discovery and composition.
- WEBAS can also benefit from the semantic web technologies, which aim to provide a common framework for data representation, integration and reasoning, and to enable the discovery and use of web resources based on their meaning and context.
- Some of the challenges and opportunities of WEBAS are:

  - How to discover and query the devices and services in a building based on their capabilities, properties and location, and not only on their network address or identifier.
  - How to integrate and harmonize the heterogeneous data from different BAS and other sources, such as weather, occupancy, user preferences, etc., and to provide a unified and meaningful view of the building state and performance.
  - How to apply data analysis and machine learning techniques to extract useful insights and knowledge from the data, such as patterns, anomalies, correlations, predictions, etc., and to support decision making and optimization.
  - How to design and implement web-based user interfaces and applications that can provide intuitive and personalized access and control of the building systems and devices, and that can visualize and communicate the data and information in an effective and engaging way.
