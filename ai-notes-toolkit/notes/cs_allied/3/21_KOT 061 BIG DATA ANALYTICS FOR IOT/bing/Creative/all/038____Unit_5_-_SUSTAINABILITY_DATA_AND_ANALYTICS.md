## Unit 5 - SUSTAINABILITY DATA AND ANALYTICS

- This unit introduces the concepts and methods of sustainability data and analytics, which are essential for measuring, monitoring, and improving the environmental, social, and economic performance of organizations and systems.
- The unit covers the following topics:

  - The definition and scope of sustainability data and analytics, and the main sources and types of data available for sustainability assessment and reporting.
  - The principles and frameworks of sustainability accounting and reporting, such as the triple bottom line, the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
  - The techniques and tools of sustainability data analysis, such as descriptive statistics, data visualization, indicators, indices, benchmarks, and dashboards.
  - The applications and challenges of sustainability data and analytics in various domains and sectors, such as energy, water, waste, transportation, agriculture, and health.
- The unit aims to provide students with the knowledge and skills to:

  - Identify and collect relevant sustainability data from various sources and formats, such as databases, surveys, sensors, and social media.
  - Apply appropriate sustainability accounting and reporting standards and frameworks to organize and present sustainability data in a clear and consistent manner.
  - Analyze and interpret sustainability data using various techniques and tools to generate meaningful insights and recommendations for decision making and improvement.
  - Evaluate and communicate the results and limitations of sustainability data and analytics to various stakeholders and audiences, such as managers, investors, regulators, and consumers.
