### Role of metadata in fog computing

- Metadata is data that describes other data, such as its source, format, quality, and context.
- Metadata can help fog computing to optimize data analytics by storing information closer to the data source for real-time analysis .
- Metadata can also help fog computing to decide whether to process the data from multiple data sources using its resources or to send it to the cloud for further analysis.
- Metadata can enable fog computing to manage data efficiently and effectively, such as by filtering, aggregating, caching, and indexing data.
- Metadata can also facilitate data security, privacy, and provenance in fog computing, such as by encrypting, anonymizing, and auditing data.
