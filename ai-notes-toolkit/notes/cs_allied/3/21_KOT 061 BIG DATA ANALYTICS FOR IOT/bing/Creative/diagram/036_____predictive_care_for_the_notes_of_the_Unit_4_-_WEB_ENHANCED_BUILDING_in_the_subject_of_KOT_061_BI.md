### Predictive care

Predictive care is the application of predictive analytics and IoT to improve the quality and efficiency of healthcare services. Predictive care can help to:

- Monitor the condition and performance of medical equipment and devices, and prevent failures or downtime by scheduling maintenance or repairs in advance .
- Detect early signs of patient deterioration or complications in the hospital or at home, and alert the medical staff or caregivers to intervene promptly .
- Identify patients who are at risk of developing chronic diseases or experiencing adverse events, and provide personalized interventions or recommendations to prevent or mitigate them .

Some of the benefits of predictive care are:

- Reduced costs and waste by optimizing the use of resources and avoiding unnecessary or redundant tests or treatments.
- Improved patient outcomes and satisfaction by enhancing the quality and safety of care and reducing hospital readmissions or complications.
- Increased operational efficiency and productivity by automating and streamlining workflows and processes.
- Enhanced decision making and planning by providing actionable insights and forecasts based on data and evidence.
