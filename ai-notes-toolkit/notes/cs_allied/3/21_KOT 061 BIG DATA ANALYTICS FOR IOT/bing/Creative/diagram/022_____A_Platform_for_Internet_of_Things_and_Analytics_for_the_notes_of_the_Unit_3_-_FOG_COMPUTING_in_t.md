### A Platform for Internet of Things and Analytics

- Fog computing is a computing architecture in which a series of nodes receives data from IoT devices in real time .
- These nodes perform real-time processing of the data that they receive, with millisecond response time.
- The nodes periodically send analytical summary information to the cloud.
- Fog computing is one of the promising solutions for handling the big data produced by the IoT, which are often security-critical and time-sensitive .
- Massive IoT data analytics by a fog computing structure is emerging and requires extensive research for more proficient knowledge and smart decisions .
- Fog computing enables the interplay between real time analytics and batch analytics, the tight interaction between sensors and actuators, the distributed data management and the geo-distribution of applications.
- Fog computing also provides benefits such as low latency, bandwidth savings, mobility support, location awareness, scalability and privacy.
- Fog computing can support various IoT applications such as smart cities, smart grids, smart health, smart transportation, smart manufacturing and smart agriculture.
