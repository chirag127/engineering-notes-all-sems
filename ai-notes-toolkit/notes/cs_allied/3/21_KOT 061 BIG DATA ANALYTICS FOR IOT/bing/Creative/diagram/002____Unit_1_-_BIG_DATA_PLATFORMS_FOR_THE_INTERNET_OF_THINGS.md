## Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or things that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies to collect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet.
- Big Data is a term that refers to the large volume, variety, velocity, and veracity of data that is generated by IoT devices and other sources, and that requires advanced techniques and technologies to store, process, and analyze.
- Big Data platforms are software systems that provide the infrastructure and tools to manage, integrate, and analyze Big Data from IoT and other sources, and to extract valuable insights and knowledge from it.
- Big Data platforms for IoT can be classified into three main categories: connectivity platforms, cloud platforms, and analytics platforms .
- Connectivity platforms provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. Among popular connectivity protocols and standards for the Internet of Things are MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN and more.
- Cloud platforms provide the storage, computing, and networking resources to host and scale IoT applications and services in a distributed and elastic manner. They also offer various services and APIs to facilitate the development and deployment of IoT solutions. Some of the most popular cloud platforms for creating Internet of Things systems are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, Artik from Samsung Electronics, Cisco Cloud Connect, Salesforce Cloud, Watson and BlueMix from IBM, OpenStack .
- Analytics platforms provide the methods and tools to process, analyze, and visualize Big Data from IoT and other sources, and to apply machine learning, artificial intelligence, and other techniques to discover patterns, trends, anomalies, and predictions. Some of the most widely used analytics platforms for IoT are Apache Hadoop, Apache Spark, Apache Kafka, Apache Storm, Apache Flink, Apache NiFi, Apache Cassandra, MongoDB, Elasticsearch, and more .
- Big Data platforms for IoT enable various applications and benefits across different domains and industries, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart manufacturing, smart healthcare, smart agriculture, smart energy, smart transportation, and more.
