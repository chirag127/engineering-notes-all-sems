## Unit 4 - WEB ENHANCED BUILDING

- A web enhanced building is a building that uses web technologies to improve its functionality, efficiency, and user experience.
- A web enhanced building can have different levels of web integration, from web-enhanced courses that use online platforms to supplement traditional classroom instruction, to web-enhanced building automation systems that use Internet of Things (IoT) devices to monitor and control various aspects of the building environment .
- Some benefits of web enhanced building are:
  - Increased accessibility and convenience for users, who can access information and services anytime and anywhere via the web.
  - Reduced operational costs and energy consumption for building owners and managers, who can optimize the performance and maintenance of the building systems using web-based data and analytics.
  - Enhanced security and safety for occupants and visitors, who can benefit from web-enabled features such as access control, surveillance, and emergency response.
  - Improved learning outcomes and satisfaction for students and instructors, who can use web-based tools and resources to enrich the course content and interaction.
- Some challenges of web enhanced building are:
  - Compatibility and interoperability issues between different web standards, protocols, and devices, which may require additional integration and adaptation efforts.
  - Privacy and security risks associated with web-based data collection and transmission, which may expose sensitive information and compromise the integrity of the building systems.
  - Reliability and availability issues of web-based services and applications, which may depend on the quality and stability of the network and the cloud infrastructure.
  - User acceptance and adoption issues of web-based innovations, which may require training, education, and incentives to overcome resistance and skepticism.
