- Big data platforms are software systems that enable the collection, processing, analysis, and visualization of large and complex data sets generated by the Internet of Things (IoT) devices and applications.
- Big data platforms can be classified into three types: connectivity platforms, cloud platforms, and analytics platforms.
- Connectivity platforms provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. Among popular connectivity protocols and standards for the Internet of Things are MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN and more.
- Cloud platforms provide storage, computing, and networking resources to host and manage IoT applications and data. They also offer various services such as device management, data ingestion, data processing, data integration, data analytics, data visualization, and security. Some of the most popular cloud platforms for creating Internet of Things systems are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, Artik from Samsung Electronics, Cisco Cloud Connect, Salesforce Cloud, Watson and BlueMix from IBM, and OpenStack  .
- Analytics platforms provide tools and techniques to extract insights and value from IoT data. They can perform various types of analytics such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive, and cognitive. They can also apply various methods such as machine learning, deep learning, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, computer vision, and more. Some of the examples of big data analytics platforms for the Internet of Things are Nexus Integra, Splunk, Cloudera, Hortonworks, SAP HANA, and Oracle .
