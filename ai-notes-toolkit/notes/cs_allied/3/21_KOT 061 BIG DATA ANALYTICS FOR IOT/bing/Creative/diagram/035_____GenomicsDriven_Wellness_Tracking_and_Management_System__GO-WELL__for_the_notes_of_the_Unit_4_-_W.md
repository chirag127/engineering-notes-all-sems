### GenomicsDriven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a concept that aims to provide predictive and personalized care for patients by integrating genomic, clinical, environmental, and behavioral data .
- GO-WELL uses big data analytics and web technologies to collect, store, analyze, and visualize the data from various sources, such as electronic health records, genomic tests, wearable sensors, and mobile applications .
- GO-WELL leverages the advances in genomics and bioinformatics to identify the genetic variations that are associated with disease risk, prognosis, and response to treatment .
- GO-WELL also considers the environmental and behavioral factors that can influence the gene expression and disease outcomes, such as lifestyle, diet, stress, and exposure to pollutants .
- GO-WELL provides a web-based platform that enables the patients to access their own data, receive personalized recommendations, and communicate with their healthcare providers .
- GO-WELL also supports the healthcare providers to monitor the patients' health status, provide feedback, and adjust the treatment plans according to the data .
- GO-WELL is designed to be scalable, interoperable, secure, and user-friendly, and to comply with the ethical and legal standards of data privacy and consent .
- GO-WELL is applied to prostate cancer as a case study, but it can be extended to other diseases and populations .
- GO-WELL is expected to improve the quality and efficiency of healthcare, reduce the costs and risks of overtreatment and undertreatment, and empower the patients to take an active role in their own health and wellness .
