# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology for service organizations to work more effectively with knowledge. It aims to improve service delivery, productivity, cost reduction, and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the key features of KCS are:

- It is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge as a by-product of solving problems.
- It empowers support agents to create and update knowledge articles based on their interactions with customers and their own expertise.
- It uses a demand-driven approach to knowledge creation, meaning that knowledge is created and updated based on the actual needs and feedback of customers and support agents.
- It leverages a peer-review process to ensure the quality and relevance of knowledge articles, rather than relying on a centralized approval process.
- It encourages a collaborative and learning culture within the service organization, where knowledge is shared and improved continuously.

Some of the benefits of KCS are:

- It reduces the time and effort required to resolve customer issues, as support agents can reuse existing knowledge articles or create new ones quickly and easily.
- It improves the consistency and accuracy of service delivery, as knowledge articles are updated and verified by the support agents who use them.
- It increases customer satisfaction and loyalty, as customers can access self-service options or receive faster and better service from support agents.
- It enhances the skills and motivation of support agents, as they can contribute to the knowledge base and learn from their peers.
- It lowers the costs of service delivery, as knowledge articles reduce the need for escalation, rework, and duplication of effort.
