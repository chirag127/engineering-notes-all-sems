### Sustainability Data and Analytics in Cloud-Based M2M Systems

- Machine-to-Machine (M2M) systems are systems that enable communication and data exchange between devices, such as sensors, actuators, and controllers, without human intervention.
- M2M systems can be used for various applications, such as smart buildings, smart grids, smart cities, and smart transportation, that aim to improve the efficiency, reliability, and sustainability of the systems and processes involved.
- Sustainability governance is the process of managing the environmental, social, and economic impacts of the systems and processes, and ensuring that they meet the needs and expectations of the stakeholders, such as owners, operators, users, regulators, and society.
- Cloud-based M2M systems are M2M systems that leverage cloud computing technologies, such as Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS), to provide scalable, flexible, and cost-effective solutions for data collection, storage, processing, and analytics.
- Sustainability data and analytics are the data and methods that enable the measurement, monitoring, evaluation, and improvement of the sustainability performance of the systems and processes.
- Cloud-based M2M systems can provide various benefits for sustainability data and analytics, such as:
  - Enabling the integration and interoperability of heterogeneous data sources and formats, such as sensors, databases, web services, and social media.
  - Providing large-scale and real-time data processing and analytics capabilities, such as stream processing, batch processing, machine learning, and visualization.
  - Supporting the customization and personalization of data and analytics services, such as data filtering, aggregation, transformation, and enrichment, according to the needs and preferences of different stakeholders.
  - Facilitating the collaboration and communication among different stakeholders, such as sharing, commenting, and rating of data and analytics results and insights.
- However, cloud-based M2M systems also pose various challenges for sustainability data and analytics, such as:
  - Ensuring the quality, reliability, and security of the data and analytics services, such as data validation, verification, cleaning, and encryption.
  - Managing the complexity and diversity of the data and analytics workflows, such as data provenance, lineage, and metadata management.
  - Balancing the trade-offs and conflicts among different sustainability dimensions and objectives, such as energy efficiency, carbon footprint, user satisfaction, and cost optimization.
  - Addressing the ethical, legal, and social issues related to the data and analytics services, such as data ownership, privacy, consent, and accountability.
