### Predictive care

Predictive care is the application of predictive analytics and IoT technologies to improve the quality and efficiency of healthcare services. Predictive care can help to:

- Monitor the condition and performance of medical equipment and devices, and prevent failures or downtime by scheduling maintenance or repairs in advance .
- Detect early signs of patient deterioration or complications in the hospital or at home, and alert the medical staff or caregivers to intervene promptly .
- Identify patients who are at risk of developing chronic diseases or experiencing adverse events, and provide personalized interventions or recommendations to prevent or mitigate them .

Predictive care can benefit healthcare providers, patients, and payers by:

- Reducing operational costs and improving resource utilization by optimizing maintenance schedules and avoiding unnecessary repairs or replacements .
- Enhancing patient safety and satisfaction by preventing adverse outcomes, improving diagnosis and treatment, and reducing hospital readmissions .
- Improving clinical outcomes and quality of life by enabling proactive and preventive care, and supporting self-management and behavior change .

Predictive care requires the following components:

- IoT sensors and devices that collect and transmit data about the equipment or the patient, such as temperature, vibration, humidity, light, blood pressure, heart rate, glucose level, etc.   .
- Data storage and processing platforms that store and analyze the data using advanced algorithms and models, such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, or statistical methods   .
- Data visualization and communication tools that present the results and insights of the analysis to the end-users, such as dashboards, reports, alerts, or notifications   .

Some examples of predictive care use cases are:

- Predictive maintenance and IoT in healthcare: All the healthcare equipment should undergo regular inspection. Predictive maintenance, in turn, helps avoid service interruption due to unplanned device services. For instance, Philips e-Alert is a solution that monitors the condition of MRI systems and sends notifications to the service team if any issue is detected.
- Predictive analytics in healthcare: Predictive analytics can help to identify patients who are likely to develop certain conditions or experience adverse events, and provide timely interventions or recommendations. For instance, Philips eCareCoordinator is a solution that uses predictive analytics to monitor patients in the ICU and the general ward, and alert the clinicians if any deterioration is detected.
- IoT and the promise of predictive health: IoT and predictive analytics can augment the doctor-patient conversation and diagnoses with knowledge based on data. IoT in the healthcare market has more than doubled in the past five years, and shows no sign of slowing down. For instance, Philips HealthSuite is a platform that connects devices, data, and people to enable personalized and predictive health.
