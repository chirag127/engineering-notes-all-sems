### Self-healing systems for the notes of the Unit 2 - RFID False Authentications in the subject of KOT 061 Big Data Analytics for IoT KCS

- RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) is a technology that uses radio waves to identify objects or people by attaching tags or chips to them.
- RFID tags can store information such as ID, location, status, and history of the objects or people they are attached to.
- RFID readers can scan the tags and communicate with a central database or system to access or update the information.
- RFID technology has many applications in various domains such as healthcare, retail, logistics, security, and IoT (Internet of Things).
- However, RFID technology also faces some challenges and risks, such as false authentication, interference, privacy, and security issues.
- False authentication is a type of attack where an unauthorized RFID reader or tag tries to access or manipulate the information of a legitimate RFID system.
- False authentication can cause serious problems such as data theft, fraud, impersonation, sabotage, or denial of service.
- To prevent or mitigate false authentication, RFID systems need to implement some countermeasures, such as encryption, authentication protocols, physical protection, or anomaly detection.
- Self-healing systems are systems that can detect, diagnose, and repair faults or errors in their components or processes without human intervention.
- Self-healing systems can improve the reliability, availability, and performance of RFID systems by automatically recovering from false authentication attacks or other failures.
- Self-healing systems can use various techniques such as redundancy, reconfiguration, adaptation, or learning to achieve self-healing capabilities.
- Self-healing systems can also leverage big data analytics and IoT technologies to collect, process, and analyze large amounts of data from RFID systems and other sources to identify and resolve problems.
- Self-healing systems can benefit RFID systems by reducing operational costs, enhancing customer satisfaction, increasing security, and enabling innovation.
