### Predictive care
- Predictive care is the application of predictive analytics to healthcare, using data from various sources to anticipate and prevent health problems, improve outcomes, and reduce costs .
- Predictive care can be applied to different domains, such as equipment maintenance, patient monitoring, disease prevention, and personalized medicine   .
- Predictive care relies on IoT sensors and devices to collect and transmit data about various parameters, such as temperature, vibration, humidity, light, heart rate, blood pressure, glucose level, etc.   
- Predictive care uses machine learning and artificial intelligence to analyze the data and generate insights, predictions, and recommendations based on patterns, trends, and anomalies   .
- Predictive care can help to improve the quality, efficiency, and safety of healthcare services, by enabling early detection and intervention, reducing errors and downtime, optimizing resources and workflows, and enhancing patient satisfaction and engagement    .
