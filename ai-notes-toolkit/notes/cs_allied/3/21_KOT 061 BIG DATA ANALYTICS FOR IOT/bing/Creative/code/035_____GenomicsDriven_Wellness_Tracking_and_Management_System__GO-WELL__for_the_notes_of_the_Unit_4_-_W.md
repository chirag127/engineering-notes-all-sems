### Genomics Driven Wellness Tracking and Management System (GO-WELL)

- GO-WELL is a concept of a health information system that uses genomic, clinical, environmental, and behavioral data to provide predictive and personalized care for patients .
- GO-WELL aims to engage patients in their own wellness management by providing them with feedback, recommendations, and alerts based on their genomic profile and risk factors .
- GO-WELL leverages the advances in genomics, bioinformatics, big data analytics, and web technologies to integrate and analyze heterogeneous data sources and generate actionable insights .
- GO-WELL consists of four main components: data acquisition, data integration, data analysis, and data presentation .
- Data acquisition involves collecting genomic, clinical, environmental, and behavioral data from various sources, such as electronic health records, personal health records, wearable devices, sensors, and social media .
- Data integration involves harmonizing, standardizing, and linking the data from different sources and formats, using ontologies, vocabularies, and identifiers .
- Data analysis involves applying various methods and techniques, such as machine learning, data mining, statistics, and visualization, to discover patterns, associations, and predictions from the data .
- Data presentation involves delivering the results of the data analysis to the patients and the clinicians, using web-based interfaces, dashboards, reports, and alerts .
- GO-WELL can be applied to various diseases and conditions, such as prostate cancer, diabetes, obesity, and cardiovascular diseases, where genomic, environmental, and behavioral factors play a significant role .
- GO-WELL can benefit both the patients and the clinicians, by enabling them to monitor, manage, and improve their wellness, prevent or delay the onset of diseases, and personalize their treatments .
