### Fog Computing

- Fog computing is a decentralized computing infrastructure that uses edge devices to perform computation, storage, and communication locally and over the Internet backbone  .
- Fog computing is also known as edge computing or fogging .
- Fog computing aims to bring the advantages and power of the cloud closer to where data is created and acted upon, especially for Internet of Things (IoT) applications  .
- Fog computing is not a substitute, but a complement to cloud computing. Fog computing is more energy-efficient and responsive than cloud computing .
- Fog computing can support various applications such as smart cities, smart grids, smart health, smart transportation, and smart manufacturing .
- Fog computing can provide benefits such as low latency, high bandwidth, real-time processing, data privacy, security, and scalability  .
- Fog computing faces challenges such as resource management, interoperability, standardization, and trust .
- IEEE adopted the fog computing standards proposed by OpenFog Consortium.
