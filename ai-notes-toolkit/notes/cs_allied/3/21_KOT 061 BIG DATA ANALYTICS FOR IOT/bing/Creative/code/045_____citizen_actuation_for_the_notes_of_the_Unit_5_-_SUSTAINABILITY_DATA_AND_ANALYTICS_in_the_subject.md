### Citizen Actuation for Smart Environments

- Citizen actuation is the activation of a human being as the mechanism by which a control system acts upon the environment.
- Citizen actuation is a way of closing the loop started by citizen sensing, which is the collection and analysis of data from citizens about events in their surrounding environment.
- Citizen actuation aims to make the reports from citizen sensing an actionable item, by sending a task to suitable occupants of an environment to complete .
- Citizen actuation can be used for various purposes, such as improving energy efficiency, enhancing safety and security, promoting social interaction, and increasing awareness and engagement .
- Citizen actuation requires a framework that can identify the relevant actors, select the appropriate actuation method, deliver the task request, monitor the task execution, and provide feedback and incentives .
- Citizen actuation can be implemented using different methods, such as notifications, gamification, nudging, persuasion, and social influence .
- Citizen actuation can benefit from citizen automation, which is the use of automation tools by non-technical business users to perform tasks that would otherwise require IT support.
- Citizen automation can enable citizen actuation by providing a platform for creating, testing, and deploying actuation tasks, as well as a governance model for managing the risks and challenges of automation.
- Citizen actuation can also leverage existing enterprise automation functions, such as robotic process automation, intelligent automation, and cognitive automation, to enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of actuation tasks.
- Citizen actuation is a promising approach for smart environments, as it can harness the collective intelligence and action of citizens to achieve sustainability goals and improve the quality of life  .
