# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology that aims to improve service delivery and knowledge management in service organizations. Some of the main features and benefits of KCS are:

- It integrates the creation and maintenance of knowledge articles with the resolution of customer issues, making knowledge a by-product of service delivery.
- It empowers service agents to capture, structure, reuse, and improve knowledge articles based on their interactions with customers and their feedback.
- It enables service organizations to leverage the collective experience and expertise of their agents, reducing the dependency on a few experts and improving the consistency and quality of service.
- It reduces the cost and time of service delivery, increases customer satisfaction and loyalty, and enhances the learning and development of service agents.
- It follows a set of principles, practices, and techniques that are based on the KCS methodology and the KCS v6 Practices Guide .

Some of the key concepts and components of KCS are:

- The KCS Loop, a cycle of four phases that describe the activities and outcomes of KCS: Capture, Structure, Reuse, and Improve.
- The KCS Solve and Evolve processes, which define the roles and responsibilities of service agents and knowledge workers in creating, using, and maintaining knowledge articles.
- The KCS Article Quality Index (AQI), a set of criteria and guidelines that measure the quality and effectiveness of knowledge articles.
- The KCS Licensing Model, a framework that defines the levels of proficiency and permissions for service agents and knowledge workers in KCS.
- The KCS Adoption Model, a roadmap that helps service organizations plan, implement, and sustain KCS in their environment.

KCS is a registered service mark of the Consortium for Service Innovation , a non-profit organization that promotes innovation and collaboration in service delivery. KCS is widely adopted and recognized as a best practice in the service industry, and is compatible with other service frameworks such as ITIL and Agile .
