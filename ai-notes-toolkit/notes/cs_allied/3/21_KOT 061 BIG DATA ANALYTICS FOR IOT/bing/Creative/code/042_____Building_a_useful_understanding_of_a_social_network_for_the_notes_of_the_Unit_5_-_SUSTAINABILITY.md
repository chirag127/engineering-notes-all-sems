### Building a useful understanding of a social network

- A social network is a set of actors (such as individuals, groups, or organizations) and the relationships or interactions among them.
- Social network analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of networks and graph theory.
- SNA can help to understand how social networks influence health, behavior, communication, innovation, and other outcomes.
- SNA can also be applied to networks outside of the societal realm, such as the Internet, biological systems, transportation networks, etc .
- To conduct SNA, one needs to collect data on the nodes (actors) and the ties (relationships) of a network, and represent them as a graph or a matrix.
- SNA uses various metrics and methods to measure and analyze the properties and patterns of a network, such as centrality, density, clustering, community detection, etc .
- SNA can provide useful insights for decision making, intervention design, policy evaluation, and network management .
- SNA can also be combined with other data sources and methods, such as big data analytics, machine learning, natural language processing, etc., to enhance the understanding of complex and dynamic social phenomena.
