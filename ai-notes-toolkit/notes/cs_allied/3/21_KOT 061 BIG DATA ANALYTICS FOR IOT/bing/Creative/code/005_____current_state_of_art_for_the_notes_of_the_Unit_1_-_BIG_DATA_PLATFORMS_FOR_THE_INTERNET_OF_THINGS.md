### Current state of art for the notes of the Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of KOT 061 BIG DATA ANALYTICS FOR IOT KCS

- Big data platforms for the Internet of Things (IoT) are software systems that enable the collection, processing, analysis, and visualization of large and complex data sets generated by IoT devices and applications.
- Big data platforms for the IoT can be classified into three main categories:
  - **Cloud platforms**: These are platforms that provide scalable and elastic computing, storage, and networking resources on demand over the internet. Cloud platforms can offer various services for IoT data management, such as data ingestion, streaming, storage, analytics, machine learning, and visualization. Some examples of cloud platforms for IoT are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, IBM Watson and BlueMix, and Salesforce Cloud .
  - **Connectivity platforms**: These are platforms that provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. Connectivity platforms can support different protocols and standards for IoT data transmission, such as MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN, and more.
  - **Application platforms**: These are platforms that provide tools and frameworks for developing, deploying, and managing IoT applications. Application platforms can offer various features for IoT application development, such as device management, data processing, analytics, machine learning, visualization, security, and integration. Some examples of application platforms for IoT are ThingWorx, Oracle IoT, GE Predix, and Bosch IoT Suite.
- Big data platforms for the IoT can offer various benefits for businesses and organizations, such as:
  - **Improved operational efficiency**: By collecting and analyzing IoT data, businesses can optimize their processes, reduce costs, increase productivity, and enhance quality.
  - **Enhanced customer experience**: By collecting and analyzing IoT data, businesses can understand their customers' needs, preferences, and behaviors, and offer personalized and tailored products and services.
  - **New business opportunities**: By collecting and analyzing IoT data, businesses can discover new insights, patterns, and trends, and create new value propositions, revenue streams, and business models.
- Big data platforms for the IoT also face various challenges and research directions, such as:
  - **Scalability and performance**: As the number and variety of IoT devices and applications increase, the volume, velocity, and variety of IoT data also increase, posing challenges for the scalability and performance of big data platforms for the IoT.
  - **Security and privacy**: As IoT data can contain sensitive and personal information, the security and privacy of big data platforms for the IoT are crucial to prevent unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure of IoT data.
  - **Interoperability and integration**: As IoT devices and applications can use different protocols, standards, and formats for data transmission and representation, the interoperability and integration of big data platforms for the IoT are essential to enable seamless and consistent data exchange and analysis.
  - **Quality and reliability**: As IoT data can be noisy, incomplete, inconsistent, or inaccurate, the quality and reliability of big data platforms for the IoT are important to ensure the validity and correctness of IoT data and analytics results.
