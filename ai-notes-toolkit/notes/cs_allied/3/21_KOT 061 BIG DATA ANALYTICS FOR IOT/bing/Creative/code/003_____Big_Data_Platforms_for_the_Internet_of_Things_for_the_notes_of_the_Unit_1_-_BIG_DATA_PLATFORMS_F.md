### Big Data Platforms for the Internet of Things

- Big data platforms are software systems that enable the collection, storage, processing, analysis, and visualization of large and complex data sets generated by various sources, such as sensors, devices, machines, or applications, that are connected to the internet of things (IoT) .
- IoT is a network of physical objects that can communicate and interact with each other and with other systems through the internet, using embedded sensors, actuators, and software .
- Big data platforms for IoT can be classified into three main types: connectivity platforms, cloud platforms, and analytics platforms  .

#### Connectivity Platforms
- Connectivity platforms provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. 
- Among popular connectivity protocols and standards for the IoT are MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN and more .
- Connectivity platforms can also provide device management, security, and authentication features to ensure the reliability and integrity of the data transmission .

#### Cloud Platforms
- Cloud platforms provide the infrastructure and services to store, process, and access the data collected by the IoT devices in a scalable, elastic, and cost-effective way. 
- Cloud platforms can also offer various tools and frameworks to develop, deploy, and manage IoT applications and services .
- The most popular cloud platforms for creating IoT systems are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, Artik from Samsung Electronics, Cisco Cloud Connect, Salesforce Cloud, Watson and BlueMix from IBM, OpenStack .

#### Analytics Platforms
- Analytics platforms provide the techniques and tools to extract insights and value from the data generated by the IoT devices, using methods such as data mining, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and visualization .
- Analytics platforms can also enable the integration and fusion of data from different sources and formats, such as structured, unstructured, or streaming data .
- Analytics platforms can help to improve the decision making, optimization, and automation of various IoT applications and domains, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart manufacturing, smart health, smart agriculture, and more .
