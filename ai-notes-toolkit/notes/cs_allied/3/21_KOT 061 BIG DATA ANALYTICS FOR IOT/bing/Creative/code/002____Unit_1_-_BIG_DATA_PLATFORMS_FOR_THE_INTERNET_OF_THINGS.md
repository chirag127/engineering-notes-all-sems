## Unit 1 - BIG DATA PLATFORMS FOR THE INTERNET OF THINGS

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that can communicate and exchange data with each other and with the cloud.
- Big Data is a term that refers to the large volume, variety, velocity, and veracity of data that is generated by IoT devices and applications.
- Big Data platforms are software systems that enable the collection, storage, processing, analysis, and visualization of Big Data from IoT sources.
- Big Data platforms for the IoT can be classified into three main types: connectivity platforms, cloud platforms, and analytics platforms .
- Connectivity platforms provide communication technologies to connect physical objects with the data center (on-premise or cloud) and transmit information between them. Among popular connectivity protocols and standards for the IoT are MQTT, DDS, AMQP, Bluetooth, ZigBee, WiFi, Cellular, LoRaWAN and more.
- Cloud platforms provide the network’s storage location and computing resources to handle the Big Data from the IoT. They also offer various services and tools to manage, secure, and scale the IoT applications. Some of the most popular cloud platforms for creating IoT systems are Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, Artik from Samsung Electronics, Cisco Cloud Connect, Salesforce Cloud, Watson and BlueMix from IBM, OpenStack .
- Analytics platforms provide the methods and techniques to extract meaningful insights and value from the Big Data generated by the IoT. They can perform various types of analysis, such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, prescriptive, and cognitive. They can also use advanced technologies, such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, and natural language processing, to enable smart and autonomous decision making for the IoT .
- Big Data platforms for the IoT are essential for enabling the digital transformation of various domains and industries, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart manufacturing, smart healthcare, smart agriculture, and more.
- Big Data platforms for the IoT are also subject to various challenges and research directions, such as data quality, data security, data privacy, data interoperability, data governance, data ethics, and data visualization.
