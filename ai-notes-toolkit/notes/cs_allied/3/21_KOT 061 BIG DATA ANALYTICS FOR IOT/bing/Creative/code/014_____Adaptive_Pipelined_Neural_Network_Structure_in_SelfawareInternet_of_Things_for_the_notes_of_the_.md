### Adaptive Pipelined Neural Network Structure in Self-aware Internet of Things

- Self-aware Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that aims to enable IoT devices to monitor, interpret, and adapt to their own states and environments in a dynamic and autonomous manner.
- Self-aware IoT devices can improve their reliability, performance, and efficiency by adjusting their behavior and configuration according to the changing context and requirements.
- Adaptive neural networks are proposed as a solution to some of the challenges of self-aware IoT, such as monitoring, interpretation, resolution, and adaptation.
- Adaptive neural networks are neural networks that can modify their structure and parameters during the learning process, based on the feedback from the environment or the task.
- A novel application of a modified Pipelined Recurrent Neural Network (PRNN) is proposed in this chapter with experiments aimed to assess its applicability to online learning and adaptation in self-aware IoT.
- PRNN is a type of recurrent neural network that consists of multiple stages, each with its own input, output, and hidden layers, connected in a pipelined fashion.
- PRNN can process sequential data in a parallel and distributed manner, and can learn long-term dependencies and temporal patterns.
- The modified PRNN introduces two mechanisms to enable adaptation: pruning and growing.
- Pruning is the process of removing unnecessary or redundant connections or neurons from the network, based on a threshold or a criterion, to reduce the complexity and improve the generalization of the network.
- Growing is the process of adding new connections or neurons to the network, based on a trigger or a condition, to increase the capacity and expressiveness of the network.
- The modified PRNN can adapt its structure and parameters online, based on the error signal and the input data, to cope with the dynamic and non-stationary nature of the IoT environment.
- The experiments show that the modified PRNN can achieve better performance and accuracy than the conventional PRNN and other baseline models, such as Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) and Elman Recurrent Neural Network (ERNN), on various tasks, such as time series prediction, classification, and anomaly detection.
- The modified PRNN can also handle concept drift, noise, and outliers in the data, and can adjust its complexity and size according to the task difficulty and data availability.
- The modified PRNN can be applied to various domains and scenarios of self-aware IoT, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart grids, smart health, and smart manufacturing.
- The modified PRNN can provide a flexible and scalable framework for online learning and adaptation in self-aware IoT, and can enable IoT devices to achieve self-management, self-optimization, and self-healing.
