### Robot

- A robot is a machine that can perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously, often with the help of sensors, actuators, and controllers.
- A robot can be classified into different types based on its shape, size, mobility, functionality, and intelligence.
- Some common types of robots are industrial robots, service robots, social robots, humanoid robots, and mobile robots.

### Applications of Artificial Intelligence in Robotics

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that deals with creating machines or software that can perform tasks that require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, and decision making.
- AI can be applied to robotics to enhance the capabilities and performance of robots in various domains and scenarios.
- Some of the applications of AI in robotics are:

  - **Self-driving cars**: AI enables self-driving cars to perceive their surroundings, navigate complex traffic situations, and avoid collisions using sensors, cameras, and deep learning algorithms. Examples of self-driving cars are Tesla, Waymo, and Uber .
  - **Autonomous drones**: AI enables autonomous drones to fly without human intervention, perform tasks such as delivery, surveillance, and mapping, and coordinate with other drones using computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. Examples of autonomous drones are Amazon Prime Air, DJI, and Skydio.
  - **Humanoid robots**: AI enables humanoid robots to mimic human appearance, behavior, and interaction using facial recognition, speech synthesis, and natural language understanding. Examples of humanoid robots are Sophia, Pepper, and Atlas.
  - **Autonomous farming equipment**: AI enables autonomous farming equipment to perform tasks such as planting, harvesting, and spraying using GPS, image processing, and machine learning. Examples of autonomous farming equipment are John Deere, Blue River Technology, and Harvest Automation.
  - **Entertainment robots**: AI enables entertainment robots to create and display content such as films, video games, animation, and cartoons using computer graphics, generative adversarial networks, and neural style transfer. Examples of entertainment robots are Disney Imagineering, DeepMind, and OpenAI.
