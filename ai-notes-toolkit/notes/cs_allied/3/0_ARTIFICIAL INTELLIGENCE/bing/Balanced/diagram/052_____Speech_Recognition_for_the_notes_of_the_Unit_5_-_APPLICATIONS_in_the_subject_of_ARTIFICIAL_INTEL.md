### Speech Recognition

Speech recognition is a significant part of artificial intelligence (AI) applications. AI is a machine’s ability to mimic human behaviour by learning from its environment. Speech recognition enables computers and software applications to “understand” what people are saying, which allows them to process information faster and with high accuracy.

Some of the main points to note about speech recognition are:

- Speech recognition converts spoken words to machine-readable input.
- Speech recognition relies on sophisticated AI that transforms voice inputs into large volumes of machine-readable information. One of the key benefits of AI is that it can become more accurate with every use session by learning from the exceptions and errors that arise.
- Speech recognizers are made up of a few components, such as the speech input, feature extraction, feature vectors, a decoder, and a word output. The decoder leverages acoustic models, a pronunciation dictionary, and language models to determine the appropriate output.
- Speech recognition can be classified into two types: speaker-dependent and speaker-independent. Speaker-dependent speech recognition requires the user to train the system with their voice, while speaker-independent speech recognition can recognize any speaker without prior training.
- Speech recognition can also be classified into two modes: isolated word recognition and continuous speech recognition. Isolated word recognition recognizes one word at a time, while continuous speech recognition recognizes natural speech with pauses and overlaps.
- Speech recognition can be used for various applications, such as voice assistants, voice search, voice typing, voice control, voice authentication, voice translation, and voice analytics .
