### Events

- Events are the **occurrence of things in the real world** .
- Events are the **actions which occur in our world**.
- Events are an important element as they are the **initial thing to be considered in knowledge representation** .
- Events can be described by their **trigger, participant, object, location, and time**.
- Events can be represented by **logical formulas, frames, scripts, or event calculus**.
- Events can be used to reason about **causality, temporality, and counterfactuals**.
