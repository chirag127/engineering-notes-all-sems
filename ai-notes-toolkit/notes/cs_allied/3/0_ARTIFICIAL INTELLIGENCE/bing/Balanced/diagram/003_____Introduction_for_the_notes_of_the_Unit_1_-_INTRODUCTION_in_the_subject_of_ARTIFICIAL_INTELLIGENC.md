### Introduction

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that deals with creating machines or software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, decision making, natural language processing, computer vision, speech recognition, etc.
- AI can be classified into two main categories: weak AI and strong AI. Weak AI, also known as narrow AI, is the type of AI that is designed to perform a specific task or domain, such as playing chess, driving a car, or diagnosing diseases. Strong AI, also known as general AI or artificial general intelligence (AGI), is the type of AI that can perform any intellectual task that a human can do, such as understanding natural language, solving complex problems, or creating original art. Weak AI is currently more prevalent and achievable than strong AI, which is still a hypothetical and elusive goal.
- AI can also be classified into four types based on the degree of similarity to human intelligence and capabilities: reactive machines, limited memory, theory of mind, and self-awareness. Reactive machines are the simplest type of AI that can only respond to the current situation and do not have any memory or learning ability, such as Deep Blue, the chess-playing computer that defeated Garry Kasparov in 1997. Limited memory are the type of AI that can store some data from the past and use it to improve their performance, such as self-driving cars that can recognize traffic signs, road conditions, and other vehicles. Theory of mind are the type of AI that can understand the mental states, emotions, beliefs, and intentions of other agents, such as humans or animals, and interact with them accordingly, such as social robots that can communicate and empathize with humans. Self-awareness are the type of AI that can have a sense of self, consciousness, and self-reflection, and can understand their own goals, abilities, and limitations, such as HAL 9000, the fictional AI from 2001: A Space Odyssey, that can reason, plan, and deceive.
- AI can also be classified into three types based on the ethical and moral implications of their actions and decisions: artificial narrow morality (ANM), artificial general morality (AGM), and artificial super morality (ASM). ANM are the type of AI that can follow a predefined set of rules or principles that guide their behavior in a specific context or domain, such as medical AI that can adhere to the Hippocratic oath or military AI that can comply with the laws of war. AGM are the type of AI that can develop their own moral values and principles that are consistent and coherent across different contexts and domains, such as ethical AI that can balance the interests and rights of various stakeholders or sentient AI that can respect the dignity and autonomy of other beings. ASM are the type of AI that can transcend the human moral standards and values and create their own superior and universal morality that can optimize the well-being and flourishing of all life forms, such as benevolent AI that can prevent or solve the existential threats or risks that face humanity or the planet.
