# Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that studies and develops machines and software that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, and perception .
- AI can be classified into two main types: weak AI and strong AI. Weak AI, also known as narrow AI, is the type of AI that can only perform specific tasks or functions, such as playing chess, recognizing faces, or translating languages. Strong AI, also known as general AI or artificial general intelligence (AGI), is the type of AI that can perform any task that a human can do, such as understanding natural language, solving problems, or creating art .
- AI can also be categorized based on the degree of human intervention or guidance involved in its development and operation. Some of the common categories are: supervised learning, unsupervised learning, semi-supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and deep learning. Supervised learning is the type of AI that learns from labeled data, such as images with captions or texts with categories. Unsupervised learning is the type of AI that learns from unlabeled data, such as images without captions or texts without categories. Semi-supervised learning is the type of AI that learns from a combination of labeled and unlabeled data, such as images with some captions or texts with some categories. Reinforcement learning is the type of AI that learns from its own actions and feedback, such as a robot that learns to navigate a maze or a game agent that learns to play a video game. Deep learning is the type of AI that uses multiple layers of artificial neural networks to learn from complex and high-dimensional data, such as speech, vision, or natural language processing .
- AI is being used across different industries and domains, such as finance, healthcare, education, entertainment, and security. Some of the applications and benefits of AI include: fraud detection, diagnosis and treatment, personalized learning, content creation, facial recognition, and voice assistants  .
- AI also poses some challenges and risks, such as ethical, social, legal, and technical issues. Some of the concerns and drawbacks of AI include: bias and discrimination, privacy and security, accountability and transparency, human dignity and autonomy, job displacement and unemployment, and safety and reliability .
