# Perception

- Perception is the process of acquiring, analyzing, and interpreting sensory information in order to gain an understanding of the environment .
- Perception involves both sensing and understanding the information that is gathered.
- Perception is a key component of artificial intelligence (AI), as it enables AI systems to interact with the world, make decisions, take action, and ultimately achieve a goal .
- Perception can be divided into two main categories: machine perception and computer vision .

## Machine perception

- Machine perception is the capability of a computer to take in and process sensory information in a way that’s similar to how humans perceive the world.
- Machine perception may rely on sensors that mimic common human senses, such as cameras, microphones, touch sensors, etc.
- Machine perception also involves applying AI techniques, such as machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, etc., to analyze and interpret the sensory data.
- Machine perception can be used for various applications, such as speech recognition, face recognition, gesture recognition, emotion recognition, etc.

## Computer vision

- Computer vision is a subfield of machine perception that focuses on processing and understanding visual information, such as images and videos.
- Computer vision involves applying AI techniques, such as computer graphics, image processing, pattern recognition, etc., to analyze and interpret the visual data.
- Computer vision can be used for various applications, such as object detection, face detection, scene understanding, optical character recognition, etc.
