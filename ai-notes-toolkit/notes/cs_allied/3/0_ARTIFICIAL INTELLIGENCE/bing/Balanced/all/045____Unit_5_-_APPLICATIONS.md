## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- This unit covers some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains, such as natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, games, and social media.
- The objectives of this unit are to:
  - Understand the basic concepts and techniques of natural language processing, such as tokenization, parsing, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.
  - Learn how computer vision enables machines to perceive and understand visual information, such as face detection, object recognition, and scene understanding.
  - Explore the challenges and opportunities of robotics, such as navigation, manipulation, coordination, and human-robot interaction.
  - Appreciate the role of artificial intelligence in games, such as search, planning, adversarial reasoning, and reinforcement learning.
  - Analyze the impact of artificial intelligence on social media, such as recommender systems, content moderation, and fake news detection.
- The topics covered in this unit are:
  - Natural language processing
  - Computer vision
  - Robotics
  - Games
  - Social media
