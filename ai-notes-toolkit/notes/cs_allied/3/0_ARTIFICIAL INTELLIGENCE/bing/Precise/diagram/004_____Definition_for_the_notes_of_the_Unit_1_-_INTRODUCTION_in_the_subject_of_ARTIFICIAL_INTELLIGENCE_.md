### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Definition of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines that work and react like humans.
2. AI is the simulation of human intelligence processes by computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, and self-correction.
3. AI is the development of computer systems that are able to perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.
4. AI is the study of how to create computers and computer software that are capable of intelligent behavior.
5. AI is the field of study that focuses on the creation of intelligent agents, which are systems that can reason, learn, and act autonomously.
