### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Heuristics are problem-solving methods that use shortcuts to produce good-enough solutions given a limited time frame or deadline.
2. Heuristics can be contrasted with algorithms, which are guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but may take longer to do so.
3. Heuristics are often used in artificial intelligence to solve problems that are too complex or time-consuming to solve using traditional methods.
4. Common heuristics used in artificial intelligence include hill climbing, best-first search, and simulated annealing.
5. Heuristics can be used to solve problems in a variety of domains, including scheduling, planning, and game playing.
6. Heuristics can be evaluated based on their effectiveness, efficiency, and simplicity.
7. Heuristics can be combined with other problem-solving methods, such as constraint satisfaction, to produce more powerful solutions.
8. Heuristics can be adapted and improved over time based on their performance in solving problems.
