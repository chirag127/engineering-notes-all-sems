### Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS: Moving

1. **Robotics**: AI can be used to develop intelligent robots that can move and perform tasks autonomously. These robots can be used in various industries such as manufacturing, healthcare, and transportation.
2. **Autonomous vehicles**: AI can be used to develop self-driving cars that can move and navigate on their own. This technology has the potential to revolutionize transportation and reduce the number of accidents on the road.
3. **Gaming**: AI can be used to develop intelligent non-player characters (NPCs) that can move and interact with the player in a realistic manner. This can enhance the gaming experience and make games more immersive.
4. **Virtual assistants**: AI can be used to develop virtual assistants that can move and interact with the user through a screen or a device. These assistants can help users with tasks such as scheduling, reminders, and information retrieval.
5. **Prosthetics**: AI can be used to develop intelligent prosthetics that can move and function like a natural limb. This can improve the quality of life for people with disabilities.
