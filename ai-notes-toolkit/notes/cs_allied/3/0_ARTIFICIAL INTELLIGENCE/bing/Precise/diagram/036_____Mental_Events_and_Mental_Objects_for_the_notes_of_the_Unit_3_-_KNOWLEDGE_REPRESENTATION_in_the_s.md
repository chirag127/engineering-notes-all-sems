### Mental Events and Mental Objects

Mental events are occurrences that happen within the mind. Examples include thoughts, feelings, decisions, dreams, and realizations. These events often make up the conscious life that are associated with cognitive function.

Mental objects, on the other hand, refer to the beliefs and knowledge that agents have constructed. These agents can deduce new beliefs and knowledge based on their existing mental objects.

In the context of Artificial Intelligence, some believe that mental events are not limited to human thought but can be associated with animals and artificial intelligence as well. AI technologies are learning and recognizing human emotions, and using that knowledge to improve various aspects of society, including health care, transportation, education, and finance .

To create mental models themselves, two key mechanisms have been suggested and applied successfully in AI research: Reinforcement and self-supervised learning, which require observing and acting on the environment.
