### Speech Recognition

Speech recognition is a technology that can identify words and phrases in spoken language and convert them into readable text. It is driven by artificial intelligence and is one of the applications of AI. 

Some of the components of speech recognizers are:
- Speech input
- Feature extraction
- Feature vectors
- Decoder
- Word output

The decoder leverages acoustic models, a pronunciation dictionary, and language models to determine the appropriate output.

Some of the applications of speech recognition are:
- Game playing
- Understanding natural language
- Computer vision
- Expert systems
- Interactive Voice Response (IVR)
- Analytics

Interactive Voice Response (IVR) is one of the oldest speech recognition applications and allows customers to reach the right agents or resolve their problems via voice commands. Analytics involves the transcription of thousands of phone calls between customers and agents to help identify common call patterns and issues.
