### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information Extraction (IE) is one of the AI applications covered in Unit 5 of the subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS.
- IE is the process of automatically extracting useful information from unstructured data sources.
- IE is often used in combination with other AI applications such as Language Models, Information Retrieval, Natural Language Processing, Machine Translation, and Speech Recognition .
- IE can be used to process documents faster and simplify operational procedures, resulting in fewer errors and increased efficiency .
