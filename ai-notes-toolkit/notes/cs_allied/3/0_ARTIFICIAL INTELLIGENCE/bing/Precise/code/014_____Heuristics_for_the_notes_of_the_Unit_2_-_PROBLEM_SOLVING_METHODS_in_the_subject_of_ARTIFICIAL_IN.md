### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

Heuristics are techniques used to solve problems more quickly when classic methods are too slow, or for finding an approximate solution when classic methods fail to find any exact solution. They are rules of thumb, educated guesses, intuitive judgments or simply common sense. Heuristics can be mental shortcuts that ease the cognitive load of making a decision.

Some common heuristics used in problem solving methods in Artificial Intelligence are:

1. **Means-ends analysis**: This heuristic involves breaking down the problem into smaller sub-problems and solving them one by one until the main problem is solved.

2. **Working backwards**: This heuristic involves starting from the goal state and working backwards to the initial state to find a solution.

3. **Hill climbing**: This heuristic involves moving in the direction of increasing value or utility until a solution is reached.

4. **Best-first search**: This heuristic involves expanding the most promising node in a search tree first, based on an evaluation function.

5. **Genetic algorithms**: This heuristic involves using techniques inspired by natural evolution, such as selection, crossover, and mutation, to find a solution to a problem.

These are just a few examples of heuristics used in problem solving methods in Artificial Intelligence. There are many more heuristics that can be used depending on the specific problem at hand. It is important to note that heuristics do not guarantee a correct solution, but they can often provide a good enough solution in a reasonable amount of time.
