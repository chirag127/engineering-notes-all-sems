### AI Applications

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has a wide range of applications in various fields. Here are some of the most common applications of AI:

1. **Healthcare:** AI is used in healthcare for tasks such as diagnosing diseases, predicting patient outcomes, and developing personalized treatment plans.
2. **Finance:** AI is used in finance for tasks such as fraud detection, risk management, and investment advice.
3. **Transportation:** AI is used in transportation for tasks such as route optimization, traffic management, and autonomous vehicles.
4. **Retail:** AI is used in retail for tasks such as inventory management, personalized marketing, and customer service.
5. **Manufacturing:** AI is used in manufacturing for tasks such as quality control, predictive maintenance, and supply chain management.
