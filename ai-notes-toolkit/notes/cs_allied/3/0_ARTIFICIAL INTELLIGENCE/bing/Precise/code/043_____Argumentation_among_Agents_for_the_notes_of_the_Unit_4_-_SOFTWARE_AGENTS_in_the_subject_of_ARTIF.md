### Argumentation among Agents

Argumentation is a form of reasoning that involves the exchange of arguments between agents. It is a key component of multi-agent systems, where agents interact with each other to achieve their goals. In the context of software agents, argumentation can be used to resolve conflicts, make decisions, and negotiate.

Here are some key points to consider when studying argumentation among agents in the context of artificial intelligence:

1. Argumentation involves the exchange of arguments between agents. An argument is a claim that is supported by evidence or reasoning.

2. Argumentation can be used to resolve conflicts between agents. For example, if two agents have conflicting goals, they can use argumentation to reach a mutually acceptable solution.

3. Argumentation can also be used to make decisions. Agents can use argumentation to weigh the pros and cons of different options and make a decision based on the strength of the arguments.

4. Argumentation can be used to negotiate. Agents can use argumentation to reach agreements on issues such as resource allocation or task assignment.

5. Argumentation can be formal or informal. Formal argumentation involves the use of logical rules and structured arguments, while informal argumentation is more flexible and can involve the use of persuasion and rhetoric.

6. Argumentation can be cooperative or competitive. In cooperative argumentation, agents work together to reach a common goal, while in competitive argumentation, agents try to persuade each other to adopt their point of view.

7. Argumentation can be used in a variety of domains, including law, politics, and business.

In summary, argumentation is a powerful tool that can be used by software agents to interact with each other in a multi-agent system. It can be used to resolve conflicts, make decisions, and negotiate, and can be formal or informal, cooperative or competitive. It is an important topic to study in the field of artificial intelligence.
