### Information Retrieval

Information retrieval is the process of obtaining information resources relevant to an information need from a collection of information resources. It is a field of study concerned with searching for documents, for information within documents, and for metadata about documents, as well as that of searching structured storage, relational databases, and the World Wide Web.

Here are some key points to remember about Information Retrieval:

1. Information retrieval is used in a variety of applications, including web search engines, recommender systems, and digital libraries.
2. The goal of information retrieval is to provide the user with relevant information in response to a query.
3. Information retrieval systems use a variety of techniques to determine the relevance of documents, including keyword matching, natural language processing, and machine learning.
4. Evaluation of information retrieval systems is typically done using measures such as precision, recall, and F-measure.
5. Information retrieval is an interdisciplinary field, drawing on computer science, linguistics, and cognitive psychology.
