### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are problem-solving methods that use shortcuts to produce good-enough solutions given a limited time frame or deadline.
- Heuristics are used to speed up the process of finding a satisfactory solution, where an exhaustive search is impractical.
- Heuristics can be contrasted with algorithms, which are guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but may take longer to do so.
- Heuristics can be used in various fields, including artificial intelligence, where they are used to solve complex problems that do not have an easily identifiable solution.
- Some common heuristics used in artificial intelligence include hill climbing, best-first search, and the A* algorithm.
- Heuristics can be useful in situations where the optimal solution is not necessary, or where a quick solution is needed.
- However, heuristics can also lead to suboptimal solutions or even failure to find a solution, so it is important to use them judiciously.
- In summary, heuristics are problem-solving methods that use shortcuts to produce good-enough solutions quickly, but may not always find the optimal solution. They are commonly used in artificial intelligence to solve complex problems.
