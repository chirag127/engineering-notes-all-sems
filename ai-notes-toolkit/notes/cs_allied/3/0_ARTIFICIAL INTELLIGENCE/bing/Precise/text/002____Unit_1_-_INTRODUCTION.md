## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first part of any study material or book.
2. It provides an overview of the topic and sets the stage for the rest of the material.
3. It is important to read the introduction carefully as it provides the context for the rest of the material.
4. The introduction may include background information, definitions of key terms, and an outline of the material to be covered.
5. A well-written introduction can help the reader understand the purpose and scope of the material and can also help to engage the reader's interest.
