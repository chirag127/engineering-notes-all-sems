# KCS (Knowledge-Centered Service)

- KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, also known as Knowledge-Centered Support.
- KCS is a methodology where support teams provide real-time customer, system, or employee support, while also creating and maintaining documentation as part of the same process.
- The goal of KCS is to get the in-depth knowledge of IT teams out of their heads and onto the page, creating detailed documentation that employees, system users, and new or less experienced engineers can use without constantly bombarding the service desk with the same requests.
- KCS operationalizes knowledge management concepts into existing workflows, holistically improving customer experience, employee engagement, and organizational learning.
