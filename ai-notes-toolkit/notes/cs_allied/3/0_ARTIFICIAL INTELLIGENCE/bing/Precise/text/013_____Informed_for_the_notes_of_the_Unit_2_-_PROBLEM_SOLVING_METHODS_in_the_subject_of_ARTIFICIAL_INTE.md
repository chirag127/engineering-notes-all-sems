### Informed for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Informed search is a search strategy that uses problem-specific knowledge to find solutions more efficiently than an uninformed search strategy.
2. Informed search algorithms use heuristics, which are problem-specific rules of thumb, to guide the search process.
3. Examples of informed search algorithms include greedy best-first search, A* search, and iterative deepening A* search.
4. Informed search algorithms can significantly reduce the search space and the time required to find a solution, compared to uninformed search algorithms.
5. However, the effectiveness of an informed search algorithm depends on the quality of the heuristic used. A good heuristic should provide an accurate estimate of the cost of reaching the goal from a given state.
6. Informed search algorithms are widely used in artificial intelligence and other fields, such as operations research and computer science, to solve complex problems.
