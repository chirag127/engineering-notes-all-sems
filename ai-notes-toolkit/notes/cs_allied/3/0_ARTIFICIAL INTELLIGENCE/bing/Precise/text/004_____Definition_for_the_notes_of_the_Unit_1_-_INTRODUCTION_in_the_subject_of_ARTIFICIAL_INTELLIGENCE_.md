### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that deals with the development of intelligent machines that work and react like humans.
2. AI is concerned with the design of algorithms, or sets of rules, that can take data inputs and provide an output that helps the machine make decisions or predictions.
3. AI systems can be trained to perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.
4. AI is often used in applications such as natural language processing, image recognition, and autonomous vehicles.
5. AI can be classified into two main categories: narrow or weak AI, which is designed to perform a specific task, and general or strong AI, which has the ability to perform any intellectual task that a human can.
6. AI technologies include machine learning, deep learning, and neural networks.
7. AI has the potential to revolutionize many industries, including healthcare, finance, and transportation.
8. However, there are also concerns about the potential negative impacts of AI, such as job displacement and the potential for AI systems to be used for malicious purposes.
