### Trust and Reputation in Multi-agent systems

Trust and reputation are key ingredients in most multi-agent systems. Many different metrics have been proposed in academic literature to measure trust and reputation in multi-agent systems. Some of these metrics include those outlined by Marsh, Sen et al., as well as SPORAS, that used in eBay, and certified reputation .

Consistent and accurate understanding of trust and reputation in multi-agent systems is a prerequisite for evaluating system state and determining any needed corrective actions that preserve continued safe operation .

Trust and reputation are central to effective interactions in open multi-agent systems (MAS) in which agents, that are owned by a variety of stakeholders, continuously enter and leave the system .

Some trust computation models for secured communication in multiagent systems include SecuredTrust and TRUMMAR - A trust model for mobile agent systems based on reputation .
