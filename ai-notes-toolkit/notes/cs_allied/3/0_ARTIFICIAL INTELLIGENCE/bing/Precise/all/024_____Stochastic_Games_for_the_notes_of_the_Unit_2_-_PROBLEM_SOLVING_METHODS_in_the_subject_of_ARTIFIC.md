# Stochastic Games

Stochastic games are a type of game in which the outcome is determined by both the actions of the players and chance elements. These games are used to model situations in which the outcome is uncertain and depends on the actions of multiple agents.

Some key points to remember about stochastic games are:

- Stochastic games are used to model situations in which the outcome is uncertain and depends on the actions of multiple agents.
- The outcome of a stochastic game is determined by both the actions of the players and chance elements.
- Stochastic games can be used to model a wide range of real-world situations, including economic, political, and social interactions.
- In a stochastic game, players must make decisions based on incomplete information and must take into account the uncertainty of the outcome.
- Stochastic games can be solved using a variety of methods, including game theory and reinforcement learning.
