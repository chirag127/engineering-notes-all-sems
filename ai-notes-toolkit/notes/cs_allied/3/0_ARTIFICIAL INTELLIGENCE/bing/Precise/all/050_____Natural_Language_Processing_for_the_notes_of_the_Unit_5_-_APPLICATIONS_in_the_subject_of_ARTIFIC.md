# Natural Language Processing

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that focuses on the interaction between humans and computers using natural language. The goal of NLP is to enable computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language.

Here are some key points to remember about NLP:

1. NLP is used in a variety of applications, including language translation, sentiment analysis, speech recognition, and text summarization.
2. NLP involves several tasks, such as tokenization, parsing, and named entity recognition.
3. NLP techniques can be divided into two categories: rule-based and statistical.
4. Rule-based NLP techniques rely on a set of hand-crafted rules to extract meaning from text.
5. Statistical NLP techniques, on the other hand, use machine learning algorithms to learn from data and make predictions.
6. NLP is a challenging field due to the complexity and ambiguity of human language.
7. Despite these challenges, NLP has made significant progress in recent years, thanks to advances in machine learning and the availability of large amounts of data.
