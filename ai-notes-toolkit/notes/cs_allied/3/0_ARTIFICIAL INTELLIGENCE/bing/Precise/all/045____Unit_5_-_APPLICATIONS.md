## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

1. Applications refer to the use of technology, knowledge, and skills to achieve a specific purpose or solve a problem.
2. Applications can be found in various fields, including medicine, engineering, business, and entertainment.
3. In medicine, applications can include the use of medical devices, software, and procedures to diagnose, treat, and prevent illnesses.
4. In engineering, applications can include the design and construction of machines, structures, and systems to solve practical problems.
5. In business, applications can include the use of software and technology to improve efficiency, productivity, and profitability.
6. In entertainment, applications can include the use of technology to create and distribute music, movies, and video games.
7. The development of new applications often involves collaboration between experts in different fields, including scientists, engineers, and designers.
8. The use of applications can have a significant impact on society, improving the quality of life and driving economic growth.
