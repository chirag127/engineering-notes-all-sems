# Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Heuristics are techniques used to solve problems that are difficult to solve using traditional methods.
- Heuristics are often used in artificial intelligence to find solutions to problems that are too complex to be solved using brute-force methods.
- Heuristics are not guaranteed to find the optimal solution, but they can often find a good solution in a reasonable amount of time.
- Heuristics can be used to solve problems in many different domains, including search, optimization, and game playing.
- Some common heuristics used in artificial intelligence include hill climbing, simulated annealing, and genetic algorithms.
- Heuristics can be used in combination with other techniques, such as constraint satisfaction, to solve complex problems.
- Heuristics can be designed to be domain-specific, taking advantage of the unique characteristics of the problem at hand.
- The effectiveness of a heuristic can depend on the specific problem being solved and the quality of the heuristic itself.
- Heuristics can be evaluated using various metrics, such as solution quality, runtime, and scalability.
- The use of heuristics in artificial intelligence is an active area of research, with new techniques and improvements being developed regularly.
