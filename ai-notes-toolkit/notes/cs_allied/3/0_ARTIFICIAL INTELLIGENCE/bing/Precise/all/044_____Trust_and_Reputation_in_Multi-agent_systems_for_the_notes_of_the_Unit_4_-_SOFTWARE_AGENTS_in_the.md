# Trust and Reputation in Multi-agent systems

Trust and reputation are central to effective interactions in open multi-agent systems (MAS) in which agents, that are owned by a variety of stakeholders, continuously enter and leave the system. Trust and reputation are a key ingredient to most multi-agent systems, and as such, many different metrics have been proposed in academic literature .

1. **Metrics**: This paper describes 5 such metrics: those outlined by Marsh, Sen et al., as well as SPORAS, that used in eBay, and certified reputation .
2. **Consistent and accurate understanding**: Consistent and accurate understanding of trust and reputation in multi-agent systems is a prerequisite for evaluating system state and determining any needed corrective actions that preserve continued safe operation.
3. **Simulation results**: This strategy enables agents to treat previously unencountered agents with caution, communicate about the trustworthiness of other agents, and evaluate past communication for deception. We discuss the details of our simulation results and the impact of various parameterizations and communication.
