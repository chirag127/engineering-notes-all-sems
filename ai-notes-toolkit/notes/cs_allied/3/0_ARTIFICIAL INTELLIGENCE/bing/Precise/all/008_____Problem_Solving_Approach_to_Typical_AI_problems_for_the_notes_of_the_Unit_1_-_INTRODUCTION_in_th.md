# Problem Solving Approach to Typical AI problems

Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. **Define the problem:** Clearly state the problem and its objectives. Identify the desired outcome and the constraints that must be taken into account.
2. **Analyze the problem:** Break down the problem into smaller, more manageable sub-problems. Identify the key components and relationships between them.
3. **Develop a plan:** Create a step-by-step plan to solve the problem. This may involve developing algorithms, heuristics, or other problem-solving techniques.
4. **Implement the plan:** Execute the plan, making sure to follow each step carefully and accurately.
5. **Evaluate the results:** Assess the outcome of the plan to determine if the problem has been solved. If not, revise the plan and try again.
6. **Iterate:** Continue to refine the plan until the problem is solved to satisfaction.

This approach can be applied to a wide range of AI problems, including machine learning, natural language processing, and robotics. By following these steps, one can systematically tackle complex problems and develop effective solutions.
