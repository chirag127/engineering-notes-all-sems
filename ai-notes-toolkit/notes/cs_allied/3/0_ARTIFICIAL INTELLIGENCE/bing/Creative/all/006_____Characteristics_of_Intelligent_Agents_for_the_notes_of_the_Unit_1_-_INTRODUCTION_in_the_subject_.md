# Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents can be used for various applications in artificial intelligence, such as information retrieval, decision support, data mining, and customer service. 

Some of the common characteristics of intelligent agents are:

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct human intervention. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.  
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing situations and environments.  
- **Sociability**: An intelligent agent can interact with other agents, humans, and systems in a cooperative and coordinated manner. It can communicate, negotiate, and collaborate with others to achieve common or individual goals.  
- **Situatedness**: An intelligent agent is embedded in a specific environment and can sense and act on it. It can perceive its surroundings through sensors or other forms of input, and affect its environment through actuators or other forms of output.  
- **Goal-oriented**: An intelligent agent has a set of goals or objectives that guide its behavior. It can select and execute actions that are expected to achieve its goals or maximize its utility.  
- **Knowledge-based**: An intelligent agent has a representation of its domain knowledge and can reason about it. It can use its knowledge to plan, infer, and solve problems. It can also update its knowledge based on new information or feedback.
