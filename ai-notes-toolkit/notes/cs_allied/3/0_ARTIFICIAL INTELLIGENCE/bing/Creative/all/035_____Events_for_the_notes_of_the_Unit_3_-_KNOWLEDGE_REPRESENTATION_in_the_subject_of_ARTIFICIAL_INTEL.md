# Events

- Events are one of the types of knowledge representation in artificial intelligence (AI) that capture information about the world in a form that a computer system can use to solve complex tasks .
- Events are occurrences or happenings that involve changes of state, time, location, or participants.
- Events can be represented by various formalisms, such as logic, frames, scripts, schemas, or neural networks .
- Events can be classified into different types, such as atomic, complex, generic, specific, planned, or unplanned.
- Events can be related to each other by various relations, such as causality, temporality, spatiality, or similarity.
- Events can be enhanced with external commonsense knowledge, such as the intents and emotions of the event participants, the preconditions and postconditions of the event, or the typicality and frequency of the event.
- Events can be used for various applications, such as script event prediction, event extraction, event coreference, event summarization, or event question answering.
