# Machine Translation

Machine translation is a sub-field of artificial intelligence that investigates the use of software to translate text or speech from one natural language to another . Machine translation can provide text translations based on computer algorithms without human involvement.

Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

- The history and evolution of machine translation
- The challenges and limitations of machine translation
- The types and methods of machine translation
- The evaluation and quality of machine translation
- The applications and future of machine translation
