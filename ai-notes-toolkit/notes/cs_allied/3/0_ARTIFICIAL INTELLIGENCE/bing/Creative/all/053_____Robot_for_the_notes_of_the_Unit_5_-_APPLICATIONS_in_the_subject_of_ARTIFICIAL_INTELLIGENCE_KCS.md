# Robot Applications in Artificial Intelligence

Robotics is the branch of science and engineering that deals with the design, construction, operation and control of machines that can perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously. Artificial intelligence is the branch of computer science that deals with the creation and study of systems that can exhibit intelligent behavior, such as learning, reasoning, planning and decision making.

Artificial intelligence in robotics aids models in learning to perform specific tasks and makes robots more intelligent in a variety of environments. Robotics has applications in the entertainment industry, including films, video games, animation, and cartoons. Robots are also extremely useful in situations where a lot of repetition is required, such as manufacturing, assembly, inspection and testing. Moreover, robots can perform tasks that are dangerous, difficult or impossible for humans, such as exploration, rescue, surveillance, surgery and warfare.

Some of the major applications of artificial intelligence in robotics are:

- **Self-driving cars**: These are vehicles that can navigate and drive themselves without human intervention, using sensors, cameras, maps, GPS and AI algorithms. Self-driving cars can improve road safety, reduce traffic congestion, save fuel and provide convenience and comfort to passengers. Some of the companies that are developing self-driving cars are Tesla, Waymo, Uber, Lyft and Cruise .
- **Autonomous drones**: These are aerial vehicles that can fly and perform tasks without human control, using AI, computer vision, navigation and communication systems. Autonomous drones can be used for various purposes, such as delivery, photography, mapping, agriculture, inspection, security and entertainment. Some of the companies that are developing autonomous drones are Amazon, Google, DJI, Skydio and Zipline .
- **Humanoid robots**: These are robots that resemble and mimic human appearance, movement and behavior, using AI, natural language processing, speech recognition, computer vision and emotion recognition. Humanoid robots can interact with humans in natural and social ways, and can be used for education, entertainment, research, health care and companionship. Some of the examples of humanoid robots are Sophia, Pepper, Atlas and Asimo .
- **Autonomous farming equipment**: These are machines that can perform agricultural tasks without human supervision, using AI, sensors, GPS, computer vision and machine learning. Autonomous farming equipment can increase crop yield, reduce labor cost, optimize resource use and protect the environment. Some of the examples of autonomous farming equipment are Harvest Automation, Blue River Technology, John Deere and FarmWise .
- **Intelligent robots**: These are robots that can learn from their own experience and adapt to dynamic situations, using AI, reinforcement learning, deep learning, neural networks and cognitive architectures. Intelligent robots can perform complex and creative tasks, such as playing games, composing music, solving problems and generating art. Some of the examples of intelligent robots are AlphaGo, DeepMind, OpenAI and Microsoft AI Lab .
