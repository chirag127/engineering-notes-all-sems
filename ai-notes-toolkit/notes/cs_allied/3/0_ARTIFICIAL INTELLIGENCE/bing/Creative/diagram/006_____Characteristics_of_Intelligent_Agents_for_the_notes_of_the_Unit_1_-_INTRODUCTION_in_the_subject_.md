### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents can be used for various applications in artificial intelligence, such as information access, decision support, repetitive tasks, and entertainment.

Some of the common characteristics of intelligent agents are:

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct human intervention. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing situations and environments.
- **Interaction**: An intelligent agent can communicate and cooperate with other agents, humans, and systems. It can use various forms of communication, such as natural language, gestures, or protocols.
- **Knowledge**: An intelligent agent can store and manipulate knowledge about its domain, its environment, and itself. It can use various forms of knowledge representation, such as rules, facts, or ontologies.
- **Goal-orientation**: An intelligent agent can pursue one or more goals that guide its behavior. It can also generate subgoals and plans to achieve its goals.
- **Rationality**: An intelligent agent can act in a way that maximizes its expected utility, given its knowledge, goals, and preferences. It can also reason logically and probabilistically about its actions and their consequences.
