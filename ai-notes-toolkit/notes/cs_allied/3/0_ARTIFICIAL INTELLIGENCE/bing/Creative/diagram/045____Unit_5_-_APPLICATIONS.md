## Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS

- In this unit, we will learn about some of the applications of artificial intelligence in various domains and industries.
- We will explore how AI can be used to solve problems, improve efficiency, enhance user experience, and create new opportunities in different fields.
- We will also discuss some of the challenges and limitations of AI, as well as the ethical and social implications of its use.
- The topics covered in this unit are:

  - AI for Healthcare
  - AI for Education
  - AI for Business
  - AI for Entertainment
  - AI for Social Good
  - AI for Security
  - AI for Environment
  - AI for Art and Creativity
  - AI for Future

- By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

  - Identify some of the use cases and benefits of AI in various domains and industries.
  - Explain some of the challenges and limitations of AI in different contexts and scenarios.
  - Evaluate some of the ethical and social issues and risks associated with AI applications.
  - Reflect on the current and future impact of AI on society and humanity.
