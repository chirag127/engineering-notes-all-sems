### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

- Information retrieval (IR) is the process of finding relevant information from a large collection of documents, such as web pages, books, articles, etc., based on a user's query.
- Artificial intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that aims to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, natural language processing, etc..
- AI methods are employed throughout the standard IR process and for novel value added services, such as personalization, summarization, question answering, etc..
- The main steps in the IR process are:
  - Document analysis: the process of transforming the raw documents into a structured representation that can be indexed and searched, such as tokens, terms, vectors, etc. AI methods such as natural language processing, text mining, and machine learning can be used to perform tasks such as tokenization, stemming, stop word removal, part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, etc..
  - Indexing: the process of creating data structures that allow efficient retrieval of documents based on the query terms, such as inverted files, posting lists, term frequency-inverse document frequency (tf-idf) weights, etc. AI methods such as latent semantic indexing, probabilistic indexing, and neural network models can be used to capture the semantic relationships between terms and documents, and to improve the retrieval performance.
  - Query processing: the process of interpreting the user's query and transforming it into a suitable representation for matching with the indexed documents, such as query expansion, query reformulation, query translation, etc. AI methods such as natural language understanding, query classification, query suggestion, and query rewriting can be used to improve the query effectiveness and user satisfaction.
  - Retrieval: the process of finding and ranking the documents that are relevant to the query, based on some similarity or relevance measure, such as cosine similarity, vector space model, language model, etc. AI methods such as learning to rank, relevance feedback, and collaborative filtering can be used to learn from the user's preferences and behavior, and to provide personalized and adaptive retrieval results.
  - Evaluation: the process of measuring the quality and effectiveness of the IR system, based on some criteria such as precision, recall, F-measure, mean average precision, normalized discounted cumulative gain, etc. AI methods such as information retrieval evaluation metrics, evaluation frameworks, and evaluation campaigns can be used to compare and benchmark different IR systems and methods.

- Some examples of applications of AI in IR are:
  - Web search engines: the most popular and widely used IR systems that allow users to find information from the vast and dynamic web, such as Google, Bing, Yahoo, etc. Web search engines use AI methods such as crawling, indexing, ranking, query processing, natural language processing, machine learning, etc. to provide fast, accurate, and relevant results to the user's queries.
  - Question answering systems: IR systems that aim to provide direct and concise answers to natural language questions, rather than a list of documents, such as Siri, Alexa, Cortana, etc. Question answering systems use AI methods such as natural language understanding, knowledge representation, reasoning, inference, etc. to analyze the question, retrieve relevant information, and generate the answer.
  - Recommender systems: IR systems that aim to provide personalized suggestions of items or services that the user might be interested in, based on the user's profile, preferences, and behavior, such as Amazon, Netflix, Spotify, etc. Recommender systems use AI methods such as collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, hybrid filtering, machine learning, etc. to learn from the user's feedback and to provide tailored recommendations.
  - Text summarization systems: IR systems that aim to provide a concise and coherent summary of a document or a collection of documents, such as news articles, scientific papers, reviews, etc. Text summarization systems use AI methods such as natural language generation, text simplification, text compression, text abstraction, etc. to extract the main points, reduce the redundancy, and generate the summary.
