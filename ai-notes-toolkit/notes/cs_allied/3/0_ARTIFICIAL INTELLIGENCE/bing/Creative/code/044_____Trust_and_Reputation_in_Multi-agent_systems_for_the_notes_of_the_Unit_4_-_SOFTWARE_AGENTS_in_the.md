### Trust and Reputation in Multi-Agent Systems

- Trust and reputation are important concepts for effective interactions in open multi-agent systems (MAS), where agents may have different goals, preferences, and behaviors, and may enter and leave the system dynamically .
- Trust is the subjective belief that an agent has about the expected behavior of another agent in a given context . Trust can be based on direct experiences, recommendations from other agents, or default values.
- Reputation is the collective opinion that a group of agents has about the behavior of another agent, based on their own or others' experiences . Reputation can be used to infer the trustworthiness of an agent that has not been encountered before, or to update the trust level after an interaction.
- Trust and reputation models aim to provide mechanisms for agents to assess, manage, and use trust and reputation information in their decision making and coordination processes . Trust and reputation models can vary in terms of the representation, computation, aggregation, and dissemination of trust and reputation values.
- Some examples of trust and reputation models are:

  - Marsh's model: A formal model that defines trust as a function of basic, general, and situational trust components, and uses a calculus to compute trust values based on utility and importance factors.
  - Sen et al.'s model: A simulation-based model that uses a learning strategy to update trust values based on direct interactions and communication with other agents, and incorporates a deception detection mechanism to identify untrustworthy agents.
  - SPORAS: A reputation-based model that uses a scoring system to assign reputation values to agents based on their feedback ratings, and adjusts the values according to the number and recency of ratings.
  - eBay's model: A reputation-based model that uses a feedback mechanism to collect positive, negative, and neutral ratings from buyers and sellers, and displays the aggregate ratings as feedback scores and percentages.
  - Certified reputation: A reputation-based model that uses a third-party certification authority to verify the identity and credentials of agents, and to issue certificates that attest to their reputation levels.
