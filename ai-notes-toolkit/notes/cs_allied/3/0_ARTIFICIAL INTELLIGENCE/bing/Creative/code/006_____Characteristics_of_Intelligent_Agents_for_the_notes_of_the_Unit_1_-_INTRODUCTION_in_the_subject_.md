# Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

An intelligent agent is a computer software system that can perceive its environment, act rationally, and learn from its experience. Intelligent agents can be used for various applications, such as information retrieval, decision support, automation, and entertainment. 

Some of the common characteristics of intelligent agents are:

- **Autonomy**: An intelligent agent can act independently and without direct human intervention. It can control its own actions and internal state based on its goals and preferences.  
- **Adaptivity**: An intelligent agent can learn from its experience and improve its performance over time. It can also adapt to changing situations and cope with uncertainty and incomplete information.  
- **Situativeness**: An intelligent agent can perceive its environment and gather relevant information through sensors or other forms of input. It can also act on its environment and affect it through actuators or other forms of output.  
- **Sociability**: An intelligent agent can interact with other agents, humans, or systems through communication, cooperation, or coordination. It can also follow social norms and protocols and respect the rights and obligations of others.  
- **Goal-orientation**: An intelligent agent can pursue its own objectives and preferences, which may be given by the user or derived from its own knowledge and beliefs. It can also plan and execute actions that are consistent with its goals and rationality.  
- **Knowledge-based**: An intelligent agent can store and manipulate knowledge about its environment, itself, and other agents. It can also use reasoning and inference mechanisms to draw conclusions and make decisions based on its knowledge.
