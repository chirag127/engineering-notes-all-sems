### Alpha for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to problem-solving methods in Artificial Intelligence:

1. Problem Definition:
   - Understanding the problem and its requirements
   - Defining the problem in a clear and concise manner
   - Identifying the constraints and assumptions in the problem

2. Search Algorithms:
   - Understanding the concept of search algorithms
   - Types of search algorithms: uninformed and informed
   - Comparison of different search algorithms: BFS, DFS, UCS, A*

3. Heuristic Functions:
   - Definition and importance of heuristic functions
   - Admissibility and consistency of heuristic functions
   - Examples of heuristic functions: Manhattan distance, Euclidean distance

4. Constraint Satisfaction Problems:
   - Understanding the concept of CSPs
   - Constraint propagation and backtracking search
   - Examples of CSPs: Sudoku, N-Queens

5. Adversarial Search:
   - Introduction to adversarial search
   - Minimax algorithm and its variants
   - Alpha-beta pruning and its significance
