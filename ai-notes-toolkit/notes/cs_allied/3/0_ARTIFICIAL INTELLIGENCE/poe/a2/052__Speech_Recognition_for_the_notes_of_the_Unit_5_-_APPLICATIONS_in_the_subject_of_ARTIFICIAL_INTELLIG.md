### Speech Recognition for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Speech recognition is the inter-disciplinary subfield of computational linguistics that develops methodologies and technologies that enables the recognition and translation of spoken language into text by computers.
2. It incorporates knowledge and research in acoustics, computer science, signal processing, linguistics, and patterns recognition.
3. The core of automatic speech recognition is to determine the most probable corresponding text given the acoustic signal. It requires extracting a set of acoustic features from the raw audio signal and finding the closest match with the models.
4. The steps involved in Speech Recognition are:
- Speech waveform segmentation - Dividing the continuous speech signal into distinct utterances.
- Feature extraction - Converting the raw speech signal into a set of features which contains linguistic information.
- Acoustic modeling - Finding the phonetic transcription of the speech signal based on the extracted features.
- Language modeling - Using the phonetic transcription and other contextual information to find the most probable word sequence.
- Decoding - Combining the output of acoustic model and language model to find the best matching text for the speech signal.
5. Speech recognition has a wide range of applications such as voice assistants, dictation systems, virtual assistants, speech to text converters, etc. The high demand for speech-enabled applications and devices is driving innovation and advancement in this field.
