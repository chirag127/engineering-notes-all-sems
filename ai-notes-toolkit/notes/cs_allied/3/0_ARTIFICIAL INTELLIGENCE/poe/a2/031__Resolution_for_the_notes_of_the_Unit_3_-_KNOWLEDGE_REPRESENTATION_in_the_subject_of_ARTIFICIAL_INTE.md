### Resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation:
- Representing knowledge in a computer-interpretable format.
- Needed for machines to communicate with humans and understand the knowledge provided by humans.
- Examples: Logic, Frames, Rules, Semantic Networks, Conceptual Graphs, etc.

2. Logic:
- Propositional Logic: Propositions and logical connectives (AND, OR, NOT, etc.).
- First Order Logic: Quantifiers (FOR ALL, EXISTS) and predicates with variables.
- Used to represent precise and unambiguous knowledge.
- Useful for automated reasoning but limited to representing static knowledge.

3. Frames:
- Represent knowledge in terms of objects and their attributes.
- Objects (frames) are related to other frames via various relationships.
- Flexible way of representing knowledge but lacks precision.
- Useful in expert systems and natural language processing.
