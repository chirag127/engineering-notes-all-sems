### Hardware for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1.  CPUs: Central Processing Units are the brain of a computer system. They are responsible for executing program instructions. More powerful CPUs with multiple cores are preferred for AI applications to enable parallel processing and faster execution of machine learning algorithms.
2.  GPUs: Graphics Processing Units are specialized electronic circuits designed to rapidly manipulate and alter memory to accelerate the creation of images in a frame buffer intended for output to a display. GPUs are more effective than CPUs for implementing machine learning algorithms and are often used in AI applications. They are optimized for parallel processing and carrying out repetitive tasks efficiently.
3.  TPUs: Tensor Processing Units are specialized AI accelerators designed by Google to accelerate machine learning workloads. They are optimized for training and inference of machine learning models, especially neural networks. TPUs are faster and more efficient than GPUs for ML tasks.
4.  FPGA: Field Programmable Gate Arrays are semiconductor devices that are configured after manufacturing. They enable hardware acceleration and can be optimized for specific machine learning algorithms. FPGAs provide more flexibility than GPUs but are more difficult to program. They are used to accelerate ML applications that do not change the model frequently.
