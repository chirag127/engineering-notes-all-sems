### Informed for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Problem Solving Methods
- Means to reach the goals and subgoals to solve a problem.
- Types:
    - Search: Blind search, informed search
    - Logic: Resolution, backward chaining, forward chaining
    - Constraint satisfaction: Local search, global constraints
- Search methods:
    - Blind search: Unorganized searching, inefficient
    - Informed search: Heuristic function provides guidance, more efficient than blind search
    - Examples: Breadth-first search, depth-first search, hill climbing, beam search
- Logic methods:
    - Resolution: Automated theorem prover, starts with contradiction and resolves it by repeated application of resolution rule
    - Backward chaining: Starts with goals and works backwards towards initial state to find solutions
    - Forward chaining: Starts with initial state and works forwards by chaining facts to goals
- Constraint satisfaction methods:
    - Local search: Evaluates neighbors of a solution and moves to better neighbor solutions
    - Global constraints: Constraints imposed on all variables together along with local constraints on individual variables for effective search

2. Applications of problem solving methods
- Automated theorem proving
- Game playing (chess, sudoku, crossword, etc.)
- Scheduling and planning
- Diagnosis
- Robot motion planning
- Natural language processing
