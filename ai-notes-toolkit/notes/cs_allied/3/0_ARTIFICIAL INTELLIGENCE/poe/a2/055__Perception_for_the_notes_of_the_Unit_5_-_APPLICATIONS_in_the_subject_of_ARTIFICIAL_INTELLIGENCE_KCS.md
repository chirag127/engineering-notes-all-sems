### Perception for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Perception refers to the process of using input from sensors (such as cameras, microphones, wireless signals, and lidar) to deduce aspects of the world.
2. Machine perception is the ability of computer systems to use input from sensors to deduce aspects of the world. This is a key part of artificial intelligence systems that interface with the physical world.
3. Some of the tasks machine perception systems can do include:
- Recognizing objects, faces or scenes in images.
- localizing and tracking objects in video.
- Recognizing speech and translating speech to text.
- Detecting anomalous or unusual patterns in sensor data that could indicate a malfunctioning system or a security threat.
4. Machine perception systems need to be trained on massive amounts of data to learn how to interpret the world. Some of the machine learning approaches used include deep learning, computer vision and natural language processing.
5. There are still many challenges remaining for machine perception including handling varied/poor quality sensor input and limited data scenarios, dealing with adversarial attacks, and achieving human-level understanding of perception.
