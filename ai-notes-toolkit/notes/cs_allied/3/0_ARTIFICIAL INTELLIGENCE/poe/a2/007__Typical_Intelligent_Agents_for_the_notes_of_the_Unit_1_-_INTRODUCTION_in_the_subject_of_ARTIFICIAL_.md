### Typical Intelligent Agents

1. Expert Systems: These are systems that contain stored knowledge and inferential engine to derive solutions to problems in a specialized domain. They are designed to mimic human experts in specific domains like medical diagnosis, technical troubleshooting, etc. The knowledge is represented in the form of rules.

2. Fuzzy Systems: These systems are designed to handle imprecise or uncertain input. They are designed with if-then rules but the crisp values are replaced by fuzzy linguistic variables like high, medium, low, etc. They are used in applications where human expertise and judgment is important like control systems.

3. Neural Networks: These are systems inspired by biological neural networks. They are designed to recognize patterns and learn complex relationships between inputs and outputs. They consists of interconnected nodes which must be trained on large amounts of data to learn how to perform a task like pattern recognition or classification. They are used in applications like image classification, speech recognition, etc.

4. Robotics: Robots are physical agents that can perform a variety of tasks in the real world. They contain sensors to perceive the environment, actuators to manipulate the environment and a controller to coordinate the actions. They are used extensively in manufacturing, space exploration, etc. The goal is to create general purpose intellignt robots that can perform human tasks.
