### Information Retrieval for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information Retrieval (IR) is the process of obtaining relevant documents or information from a collection in response to a query.
2. The key steps in IR are:
- Indexing: Indexing is the process of extracting and storing features like words, terms and concepts from documents. This data is structured and stored in an index.
- Query Processing: The user query is processed and relevant terms are extracted from it.
- Searching: The index is searched to find documents that contain terms similar to the query terms.
- Ranking: The retrieved documents are ranked in the order of their relevance to the user query.
3. IR Evaluation measures:
- Precision: Percentage of retrieved documents that are relevant.
- Recall: Percentage of relevant documents that are retrieved.
- F-measure: Harmonic mean of precision and recall.
4. Types of IR systems:
- Boolean Retrieval: Uses Boolean logic (AND, OR, NOT) to match query terms and index terms.
- Vector Space Model: Documents and queries are represented as vectors and similarity is computed using cosine similarity.
- Probabilistic Retrieval: Models the probability of relevance of a document given the query.
- Language Models: Models the probability of generating a query given a document.
