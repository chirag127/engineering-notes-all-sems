### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information Extraction is the task of automatically extracting structured information from unstructured and/or semi-structured documents.

2. The key steps in Information Extraction are:

- Document segmentation: Splitting the text into paragraphs, sentences, or tokens.
- Named Entity Recognition: Identifying and classifying named entities into pre-defined categories such as person names, organizations, locations, dates, etc.
- Relation and Event extraction: Extracting semantic relations between entities and events with participants.
- Coreference resolution: Resolving references to the same entities or mentions.

3. The applications of Information Extraction are:

- Gathering business intelligence and market research data.
- Tracking financial and business events by analyzing news articles.
- Extracting data from medical records and scientific papers.
- Facilitating search and navigation support by automatically summarizing web content.
- Enhancing user experience by extracting structured data and relevant information.

4. The challenges in Information Extraction are:

- Lack of formally structured data which makes the task difficult.
- Ambiguity and variability in natural language which leads to inconsistencies.
- Domain dependence makes porting models to new domains hard.
- Scale and efficiency owing to the volume of data and required throughput.

5. Some approaches to Information Extraction are:

- Rule-based approaches using handcrafted rules and patterns.
- Machine learning approaches using supervised, unsupervised, and semi-supervised learning.
- Hybrid approaches combining rule-based and machine learning techniques.
- Deep learning approaches using various neural network architectures.
