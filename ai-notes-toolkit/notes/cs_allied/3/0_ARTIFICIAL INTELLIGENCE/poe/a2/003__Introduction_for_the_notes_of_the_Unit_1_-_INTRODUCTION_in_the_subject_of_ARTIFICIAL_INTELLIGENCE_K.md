### Introduction

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science that attempts to build machines that can think and act intelligently, similar to a human.
2. AI is a diverse field that includes machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and more. The ultimate goal is to create general artificial intelligence - a machine that can perform any intellectual task that a human can. However, we are not there yet.
3. Some examples of AI today include:
 - Siri, Alexa, and other virtual assistants that can understand speech and respond to questions.
 - Image recognition software that can identify people or objects in pictures.
 - Self-driving cars that can perceive and navigate the environment around them.
4. The progress of AI has been rapid in recent years, due to advances in deep learning, neural networks, and the availability of massive amounts of data and increased computing power.
5. The future of AI is promising but uncertain. There are hopes for continued progress in the field and for AI to benefit society with applications such as improved healthcare. However, there are also risks and ethical concerns about bias, privacy, and safety that must be addressed.
