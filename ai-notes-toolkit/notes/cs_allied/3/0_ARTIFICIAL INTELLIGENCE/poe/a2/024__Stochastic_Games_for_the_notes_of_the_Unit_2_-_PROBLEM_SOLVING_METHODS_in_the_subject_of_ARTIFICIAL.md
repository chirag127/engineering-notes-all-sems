### Stochastic Games

- Stochastic games are a generalization of Markov decision processes (MDPs) to multiple agents.
- In stochastic games, there are multiple players (agents), each with their own set of actions and rewards.
- The dynamics of the game depend on the joint actions of the agents and stochastic elements.
- Agents do not know the actions chosen by other agents and have to plan based on their beliefs about the other agents.
- Finding optimal strategies in stochastic games is difficult and there may not exist a pure Nash equilibrium.
- Applications of stochastic games include modeling competition and cooperation in multiagent systems with uncertainty.
