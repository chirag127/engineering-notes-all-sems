### Heuristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Hill Climbing - It is a local search technique. It starts with an initial solution and then tries to find a better solution by making small incremental changes to the current solution. If the change results in a better solution, it is retained, otherwise the change is undone. This process is repeated until no further improvements can be made.
2. Simulated Annealing - It is a randomized local search technique. It allows for occasional worsening of solutions to avoid getting stuck in local optima. The probability of accepting worse solutions is decreased over time using a temperature parameter (analogous to the cooling of metals in the annealing process).
3. Genetic Algorithms - They are search algorithms based on the principles of genetics and natural selection. They start with a population of random solutions and evolve them over multiple generations to find the optimal solution. The genetic operators like crossover, mutation are used to guide the search process.
4. Particle Swarm Optimization - It is a population-based search algorithm inspired by the social behavior of birds flocking or fish schooling. The system is initialized with a population of random solutions called particles. Each particle then moves through the search space by following the current optimum particles. The particles have memory of their previous best position as well as the global best position which guides their search.
