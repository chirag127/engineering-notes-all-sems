### Characteristics of Intelligent Agents

1. Sense the environment: Intelligent agents sense the environment through sensors like cameras, microphones, etc. They take inputs from the environment through sensors.

2. Perceive the state: Agents perceive the current state of the environment from the inputs received from the sensors. They process the inputs to understand the current state.

3. Have goals: Agents have some goals or objectives to achieve. These goals drive the behavior of the agents. The agents perform actions to achieve the goals.

4. Act in the environment: Based on the current state and goals, the agents perform actions that lead to changes in the environment. The actions are performed through actuators like motors, speakers, etc.

5. Adapt to the changes: Intelligent agents adapt to changes in the environment. They perceive the new state of the environment after every action and change their actions accordingly to achieve the goals.

The characteristics of sensing the environment, perceiving the state, having goals, acting in the environment, and adapting to changes are essential for any intelligent agent. Based on these characteristics, agents interact with the dynamic environment and achieve their objectives.
