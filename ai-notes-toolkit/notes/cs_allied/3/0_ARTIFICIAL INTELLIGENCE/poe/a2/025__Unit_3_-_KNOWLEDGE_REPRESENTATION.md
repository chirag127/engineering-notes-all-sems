## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

1. Knowledge Representation -

- Knowledge Representation is a field of artificial intelligence research aimed at representing knowledge in symbols to make it understandable to computers.
- It is about representing knowledge in a format that computers can use to solve complex tasks.
- The core idea is to represent knowledge in a declarative form through some language or graphical notation.
- The key tasks involved are:
-- Acquiring knowledge from various sources.
-- Representing the knowledge in a suitable data structure.
-- Reasoning about the knowledge to derive new facts or conclusions.
-- Updating the knowledge base from new information.

2. Need for Knowledge Representation -

- To enable intelligent systems to use the knowledge. Machines cannot understand human languages directly. So the knowledge needs to be represented in a formal language.
- To share knowledge between multiple systems. The common formats for knowledge representation enable knowledge sharing.
- To provide a mechanism for automated reasoning. The logical nature of the formal languages used provides a basis for automated deduction.
- To separate the knowledge from the algorithms. The knowledge is represented independently and the algorithms use the representation to perform various tasks.

3. Key Requirements -

- The format should be unambiguous. The meaning should be clear and not open to multiple interpretations.
- The format should be capable of representing various types of knowledge. It should support representations of objects, concepts, relationships, etc.
- The language should be capable of logical reasoning. It should be based on well-defined semantics to enable automated deduction.
- It should be usable by machine systems. The format should be decipherable by computers for further processing.
- It should be capable of extension to incorporate new knowledge. The representation schema should be extendable to add more knowledge.
