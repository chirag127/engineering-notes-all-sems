### Categories and Objects for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Categories:
- Classes or types of objects/entities in a domain
- Represented by sets or collections of objects
- Example: Animal, Vehicle, Furniture, etc.

2. Objects:
- Instances or members of a category
- Represented by individual entities
- Example: Cat, Car, Table, etc.

3. Type hierarchies:
- Categories can be organized into hierarchies
- A category at a higher level is more general than categories lower down
- Example: Animal > Mammal > Cat
- Used to represent subsumption relationship between categories

4. Properties:
- Characteristics or attributes of categories and objects
- Example: Color, Size, Weight, etc. for objects
- Can be inherited from supercategories
- Useful for defining constraints and inferences
