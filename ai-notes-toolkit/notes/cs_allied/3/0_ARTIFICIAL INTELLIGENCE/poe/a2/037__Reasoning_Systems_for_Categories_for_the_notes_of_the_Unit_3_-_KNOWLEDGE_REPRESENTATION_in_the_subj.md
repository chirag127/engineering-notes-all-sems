### Reasoning Systems for Categories for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Propositional Logic - It deals with propositions that are either true or false. The propositions are combined using logical connectives like and, or, not, implies, etc. to form logical expressions. These logical expressions are evaluated to derive conclusions.
2. Predicate Logic - It is an extension of propositional logic. It includes variables and quantifiers in addition to propositions and connectives. Quantifiers like for all and there exists are used to refer to groups or classes of individuals. It can represent more complex relationships between objects/classes.
3. Semantic Networks - It is a graphical structure used to represent knowledge. Concepts/objects are represented as nodes and relationships between them are represented as links. The nodes and links can have attributes associated with them to represent additional information. It provides a means to visualize relationships between different concepts.
4. Frames - It is a knowledge representation technique based on the idea of frames/templates. A frame is used to store relevant attributes/information about a concept. New frames can inherit information from existing parent frames. Frames make it easy to store stereotypical/typical knowledge about a concept and its attributes.
5. Rules - It represents knowledge in the form of condition-action rules (IF-THEN rules). The IF part specifies the conditions and the THEN part specifies the consequences/actions. Rules are useful for representing procedural knowledge and can be used to infer new conclusions from known facts.
