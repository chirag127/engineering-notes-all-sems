
## Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION

1. Knowledge Representation is the process of encoding knowledge in a form that can be used by a computer to reason, plan, or make decisions.

2. Knowledge Representation involves representing objects, their attributes, and relationships between them. This includes representing facts, rules, and other forms of knowledge.

3. Knowledge Representation is a critical aspect of Artificial Intelligence, as it enables computers to understand and reason about the world.

4. Knowledge Representation techniques include logic-based representations, semantic networks, frames, and ontologies.

5. Logic-based representations are based on First-Order Logic and are used to represent factual knowledge.

6. Semantic networks are graphical representations of knowledge which are used to represent relationships between concepts.

7. Frames are used to represent objects and their attributes, as well as the relationships between them.

8. Ontologies are used to represent the knowledge of a domain, including the objects, attributes, and relationships between them.
