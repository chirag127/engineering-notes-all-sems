
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first step in learning any subject. It is a fundamental part of understanding a topic and provides the foundation for future learning.
2. Introduction provides an overview of the subject and outlines the main points that will be discussed in greater detail.
3. It is important to understand the key concepts and terminology related to a subject before proceeding with further study.
4. Introduction should be written in a clear and concise manner, using language that is appropriate for the intended audience.
5. It is important to remember that introduction should not be overly long or complex, as this can lead to confusion and a lack of understanding.
