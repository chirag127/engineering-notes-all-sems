
### Heuristics for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Problem Solving Methods in the Subject of Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. Heuristics are strategies and techniques used to solve problems in Artificial Intelligence.
2. Heuristics can be divided into two categories: domain-specific heuristics and general heuristics.
3. Domain-specific heuristics are strategies and techniques that are tailored to a specific problem, while general heuristics are strategies and techniques that can be used in any problem-solving context.
4. Heuristics can be used to identify the best solution to a problem by evaluating the available options.
5. Heuristics can also be used to evaluate the performance of a system or algorithm, by measuring its accuracy and efficiency.
6. Heuristics can be used to generate new solutions to a problem by combining existing solutions.
7. Heuristics can also be used to optimize existing solutions, by finding ways to reduce the complexity or improve the efficiency of a system.
8. Heuristics can be used to identify potential areas of improvement in a system, by evaluating the existing system and identifying potential areas of improvement.
