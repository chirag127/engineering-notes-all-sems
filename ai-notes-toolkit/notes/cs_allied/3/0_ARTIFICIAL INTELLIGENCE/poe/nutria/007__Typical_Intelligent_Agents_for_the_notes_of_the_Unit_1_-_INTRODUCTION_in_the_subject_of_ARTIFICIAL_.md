
### Typical Intelligent Agents

1. Expert Systems: Expert systems are computer programs that simulate the decision-making ability of a human expert. They use artificial intelligence techniques such as knowledge representation, automated reasoning, and machine learning to solve complex problems.

2. Natural Language Processing (NLP): Natural language processing is a field of computer science, artificial intelligence, and linguistics concerned with the interactions between computers and human (natural) languages. NLP enables computers to understand, interpret, and manipulate human language.

3. Robotics: Robotics is the branch of mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and computer science that deals with the design, construction, operation, and application of robots. Robots are used in a variety of applications, from manufacturing to medical to military.

4. Neural Networks: Neural networks are computing systems modeled after the human brain. They are composed of interconnected neurons, which are capable of learning from and adapting to their environment. Neural networks are used in a variety of applications, including image recognition and natural language processing.

5. Fuzzy Logic: Fuzzy logic is a form of artificial intelligence that deals with reasoning that is approximate rather than precise. It is based on the concept of "fuzzy sets", which are sets of objects that can have partially defined boundaries. Fuzzy logic is used in a variety of applications, including control systems and pattern recognition.
