
### Game Playing 
Game playing is an important problem solving method in Artificial Intelligence. It is used to create computer programs that can play games such as chess, checkers, and tic-tac-toe. In this method, the computer is given a set of rules and a goal, and it must find the best way to achieve the goal.

Game playing algorithms are used to solve problems such as finding the best move in a game, predicting the outcome of a game, and finding the best strategy for a game. These algorithms are based on search techniques, heuristics, and game theory. 

Search techniques are used to find the best move in a game. Heuristics are used to evaluate the moves and predict the outcome of the game. Game theory is used to find the best strategy for a game. 

Game playing algorithms are used in a variety of applications such as computer gaming, artificial intelligence, robotics, and autonomous systems. They are also used in medical, military, and financial applications.
