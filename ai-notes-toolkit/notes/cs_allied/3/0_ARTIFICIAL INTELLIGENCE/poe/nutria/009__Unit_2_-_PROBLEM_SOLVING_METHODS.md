
## Unit 2 - PROBLEM SOLVING METHODS

1. Problem solving is a process that involves breaking down a complex problem into smaller, more manageable components.
2. It is important to identify the root cause of a problem before attempting to solve it.
3. Brainstorming is a problem-solving technique that involves generating ideas or solutions to a problem in an open and creative manner.
4. Systematic problem solving is a structured approach to problem solving that involves breaking down the problem into smaller parts and then analyzing each part in detail.
5. The scientific method is a systematic approach to problem solving that involves making observations, forming hypotheses, testing hypotheses, and drawing conclusions.
6. Decision making is the process of making choices among alternative courses of action.
7. The decision-making process involves gathering information, evaluating alternatives, and making a choice based on the available information.
8. Critical thinking is a process of analyzing and evaluating information in order to draw logical conclusions.
9. Creative problem solving is a process of using imagination and innovation to come up with new ideas and solutions to problems.
10. Algorithms are a set of instructions that can be used to solve a problem.
