
### Categories and Objects for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Knowledge Representation: Knowledge representation is the process of representing knowledge in a logical form that can be used by computers. It is the process of representing information in a way that can be understood by a computer.

2. Categories: Categories are the basic building blocks of knowledge representation. They are used to represent objects, events, and relationships between them. Categories are used to organize information into meaningful groups.

3. Objects: Objects are the entities that are represented in a knowledge representation system. They can be physical objects, abstract concepts, or events.

4. Relationships: Relationships are the connections between objects. They can be used to represent the relationships between objects, events, and other concepts.

5. Rules: Rules are used to represent the relationships between objects and events. They are used to define how objects and events interact with each other.

6. Semantic Networks: Semantic networks are used to represent the relationships between objects and events in a knowledge representation system. They are used to represent the relationships between objects and events in a more intuitive way.
