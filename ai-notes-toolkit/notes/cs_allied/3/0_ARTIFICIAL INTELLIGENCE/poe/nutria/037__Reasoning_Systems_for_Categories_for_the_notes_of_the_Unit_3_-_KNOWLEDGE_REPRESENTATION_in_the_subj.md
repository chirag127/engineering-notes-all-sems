
### Reasoning Systems for Categories

1. Deductive Reasoning: Deductive reasoning is a type of reasoning that moves from general to specific. It begins with a general statement or hypothesis and works towards a specific conclusion. Deductive reasoning is often used in problem solving and decision making.

2. Inductive Reasoning: Inductive reasoning is a type of reasoning that moves from specific to general. It begins with a specific statement or observation and works towards a general conclusion. Inductive reasoning is often used in scientific research and data analysis.

3. Abductive Reasoning: Abductive reasoning is a type of reasoning that moves from observation to hypothesis. It begins with an observation and works towards a hypothesis that explains the observation. Abductive reasoning is often used in medical diagnosis and legal reasoning.

4. Analogical Reasoning: Analogical reasoning is a type of reasoning that moves from one example to another. It begins with an example and works towards a similar example. Analogical reasoning is often used in literature and philosophy.
