
### Planning for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Introduction to Applications of Artificial Intelligence: Overview of how Artificial Intelligence has been applied in various fields, such as healthcare, finance, robotics, and more.
2. Machine Learning: Overview of supervised and unsupervised learning techniques and how they are used in Artificial Intelligence applications.
3. Natural Language Processing (NLP): Overview of how NLP is used to interpret and generate natural language.
4. Computer Vision: Overview of how computer vision is used to interpret images and videos.
5. Robotics: Overview of how robots are used in Artificial Intelligence applications.
6. Autonomous Vehicles: Overview of how Artificial Intelligence is used to power autonomous vehicles.
7. Expert Systems: Overview of how expert systems are used to solve complex problems.
8. Neural Networks: Overview of how neural networks are used to model complex problems.
9. Conclusion: Summary of the topics discussed in this unit and how they relate to Artificial Intelligence applications.
