

### Informed Problem Solving Methods in Artificial Intelligence KCS

1. Informed problem solving is a method of problem solving which utilizes knowledge about the problem space to identify potential solutions. 
2. It is used in Artificial Intelligence (AI) to create algorithms which can identify and solve problems in an efficient manner.
3. Informed problem solving is based on the idea that knowledge of the problem space can be used to identify potential solutions. 
4. It is often used in AI to create algorithms which can identify and solve problems quickly and accurately.
5. Informed problem solving methods use heuristics, or rules of thumb, to identify potential solutions. 
6. Heuristics are based on experience and are used to narrow down the search space and identify potential solutions. 
7. Informed problem solving methods are often used in conjunction with other AI techniques such as search algorithms, constraint satisfaction, and machine learning.
8. Informed problem solving is an important part of AI research and has been used to solve a variety of problems in fields such as robotics, natural language processing, and computer vision.
