
### Information Extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - APPLICATIONS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Information Extraction (IE) is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that deals with extracting structured information from unstructured or semi-structured sources.
2. IE systems can be used to extract information from text, audio, video, and other sources.
3. IE systems are used in a variety of applications, including information retrieval, question answering, text summarization, opinion mining, and machine translation.
4. IE systems typically use a combination of natural language processing (NLP) techniques and machine learning algorithms to extract relevant information from a given source.
5. IE systems can also be used to extract facts from web pages and documents, such as names, dates, locations, and other entities.
6. IE systems can also be used to identify relationships between entities, such as person-organization, person-location, and organization-location relationships.
7. IE systems can be used to generate structured knowledge graphs that represent the relationships between entities in a given source.
8. IE systems can also be used to generate structured summaries of text documents.
