
### Argumentation among Agents for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

1. Argumentation is a process of reasoning in which two or more agents attempt to reach a conclusion by exchanging arguments.
2. Argumentation among agents is used to represent the process of decision-making in multi-agent systems.
3. Argumentation can be used to represent the beliefs, preferences, and goals of the agents.
4. Argumentation can be used to represent the process of negotiation among agents.
5. Argumentation can be used to represent the process of problem-solving among agents.
6. Argumentation can be used to represent the process of conflict resolution among agents.
7. Argumentation can be used to represent the process of coordination among agents.
8. Argumentation can be used to represent the process of consensus building among agents.
