
### Forward Chaining 

Forward chaining is a type of logical inference used in artificial intelligence (AI) and expert systems. It is a form of data-driven reasoning in which the system works from known facts to draw conclusions. Unlike backward chaining, which starts with the goal and works backward to determine what needs to be done to reach the goal, forward chaining starts with the facts and works forward to determine the goal.

- It can also be used to generate new knowledge from existing knowledge.
- Forward chaining is used in many AI applications, such as natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision.
