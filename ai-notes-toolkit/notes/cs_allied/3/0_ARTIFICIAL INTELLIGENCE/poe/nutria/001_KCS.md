
# Knowledge Centered Support (KCS)

KCS is a methodology for improving customer service and support by providing a consistent, repeatable, and documented approach to problem solving. It was developed by the Consortium for Service Innovation and is based on the principle that knowledge is the most important asset in providing customer service.

KCS is focused on creating, sharing, and using knowledge to improve customer service. It emphasizes collaboration and knowledge sharing among customer service teams to ensure that customer service agents have access to the most up-to-date and accurate information.

KCS involves the following steps:

1. Identify the problem: Identify the customer’s problem and the root cause of the issue.

2. Search for solutions: Search for existing solutions to the problem.

3. Create a solution: If a solution is not found, create a new solution.

4. Test the solution: Test the solution to ensure that it works.

5. Document the solution: Document the solution and make it available to other customer service agents.

6. Monitor the solution: Monitor the solution to ensure that it is still effective.

KCS is an effective way to improve customer service and support by ensuring that customer service agents have access to the most up-to-date and accurate information. By following the steps outlined above, customer service teams can create, share, and use knowledge to quickly and effectively resolve customer issues.
