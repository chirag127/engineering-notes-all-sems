
# Artificial Intelligence

1. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that aims to create intelligent machines that can think and act like humans.

2. AI can be used to solve complex problems that are too difficult or time-consuming for humans to solve. AI systems can be used to analyze large amounts of data, recognize patterns and make decisions.

3. AI systems are divided into two categories: weak AI and strong AI. Weak AI systems are limited in their capabilities and can only solve specific problems. Strong AI systems are more powerful and can solve a wide range of problems.

4. AI systems can be trained using Machine Learning (ML) algorithms. ML algorithms use data to learn how to recognize patterns and make decisions.

5. AI systems can be used in a variety of applications, including robotics, natural language processing, computer vision and autonomous vehicles.
