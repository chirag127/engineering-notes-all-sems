
### Negotiation and Bargaining for the notes of the Unit 4 - SOFTWARE AGENTS in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

* Negotiation and bargaining is a process of communication between two or more parties to reach an agreement. 
* It is used in many areas such as business, politics, and law. 
* In the context of Artificial Intelligence, negotiation and bargaining are used to enable agents to interact and reach a mutually beneficial agreement. 
* Negotiation and bargaining involve two or more agents communicating in order to reach an agreement on a certain issue. 
* The agents need to decide on the objectives, constraints, and preferences that will be used in the negotiation. 
* The negotiation process involves the agents exchanging information and making offers until an agreement is reached. 
* Negotiation and bargaining algorithms are used to enable agents to reach an agreement without the need for human intervention. 
* These algorithms use techniques such as game theory, utility theory, and heuristics to determine the best course of action for each agent. 
* Negotiation and bargaining can be used to solve problems such as resource allocation, task assignment, and conflict resolution.
