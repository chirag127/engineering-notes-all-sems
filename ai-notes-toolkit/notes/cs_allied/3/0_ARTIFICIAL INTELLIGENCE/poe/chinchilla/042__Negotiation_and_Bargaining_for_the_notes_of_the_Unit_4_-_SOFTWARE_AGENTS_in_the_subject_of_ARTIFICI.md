### Negotiation and Bargaining

In the context of software agents, negotiation and bargaining refer to the process of two or more agents communicating with each other to reach an agreement that benefits all parties involved. Negotiation and bargaining are essential in multi-agent systems where agents have conflicting goals and interests.

#### Types of Negotiation

There are two main types of negotiation in software agents:

1. **Distributive Negotiation:** This type of negotiation is also known as zero-sum negotiation. In this type of negotiation, the agents involved try to maximize their own benefits while minimizing the benefits of the other agents. Distributive negotiation is usually used when there is a fixed amount of resources to be distributed among the agents.

2. **Integrative Negotiation:** This type of negotiation is also known as win-win negotiation. In this type of negotiation, the agents involved try to find a solution that benefits all parties involved. Integrative negotiation is usually used when the agents involved have compatible goals and interests.

#### Bargaining Strategies

There are several bargaining strategies that agents can use during negotiation:

1. **Tit-for-Tat:** In this strategy, an agent starts by cooperating with the other agent, and then mirrors the other agent's behavior in subsequent interactions. This strategy is effective in building trust between agents.

2. **Gradual Concession:** In this strategy, an agent starts with a high demand and then gradually makes concessions until an agreement is reached. This strategy is effective in situations where the agents involved have different preferences.

3. **Boulware and Gandhian:** Boulware strategy involves sticking to a fixed demand, while Gandhian strategy involves making large concessions. These strategies are effective in situations where the agents involved have different preferences.

4. **Random Walk:** In this strategy, an agent randomly selects a demand and makes a concession if the other agent rejects the demand. This strategy is effective in situations where the agents involved have limited information about each other.

#### Negotiation Protocols

Negotiation protocols are a set of rules that agents follow during negotiation. Some negotiation protocols include:

1. **Contract Net Protocol:** In this protocol, a manager agent sends out a request for proposals to other agents. The agents then submit proposals and the manager agent selects the best proposal.

2. **Auction Protocol:** In this protocol, an auctioneer agent sells a resource to the highest bidder among other agents.

3. **Negotiation Tree Protocol:** In this protocol, agents use a decision tree to negotiate over a set of issues. The agents then reach an agreement by selecting a path through the decision tree.

In conclusion, negotiation and bargaining are important aspects of software agent behavior. By using different negotiation strategies and protocols, agents can reach mutually beneficial agreements and maximize their benefits.
