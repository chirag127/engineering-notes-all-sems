### Introduction

In this unit, we will be introducing the subject of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and exploring its various aspects. AI is a rapidly growing field that has the potential to significantly impact the way we live and work. This unit will provide an overview of the following:

1. Definition of Artificial Intelligence: We will be defining what AI is and what makes it different from traditional computer programs.

2. Brief History of AI: We will be exploring the history of AI, including key milestones and important figures in the field.

3. Applications of AI: We will be examining the various applications of AI, including machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and more.

4. Ethics and Implications of AI: We will be discussing the ethical concerns that arise with the development and implementation of AI, including issues related to privacy, bias, and job displacement.

5. Future of AI: We will be exploring the potential future developments in AI and how they may impact various industries and society as a whole.

Overall, this unit will provide a foundation for understanding the basics of AI and its potential impact on our world. It will also set the stage for further exploration of the subject in subsequent units.
