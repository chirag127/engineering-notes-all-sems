### Events for the notes of the Unit 3 - KNOWLEDGE REPRESENTATION in the subject of ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE KCS

In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to knowledge representation in artificial intelligence:

1. Propositional Logic
   - Syntax and Semantics
   - Entailment and Validity
   - Inference rules
   - Resolution
   - Knowledge Engineering
2. First-order Logic
   - Syntax and Semantics
   - Unification and Substitution
   - Inference rules
   - Resolution
   - Knowledge Engineering
3. Semantic Networks
   - Nodes and Arcs
   - Inheritance
   - Frames and Scripts
   - Knowledge Engineering
4. Frames and Scripts
   - Structure and Components
   - Inheritance and Default Values
   - Knowledge Engineering
5. Rule-based Systems
   - Production Rules
   - Forward Chaining and Backward Chaining
   - Conflict Resolution
   - Knowledge Engineering
6. Ontologies
   - Conceptualization and Specification
   - Reasoning and Inference
   - Knowledge Engineering

To understand knowledge representation, it is important to grasp the concepts and techniques used in each of the topics mentioned above. It is also important to understand the strengths and weaknesses of each technique and when to use them.
