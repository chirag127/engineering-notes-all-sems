## Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture)

Neural Networks are a type of machine learning algorithm that is inspired by the functioning of the human brain. In this unit, we will introduce the concept of Neural Networks and their architecture.

### Introduction to Neural Networks

- Neural Networks are a type of machine learning algorithm that is designed to recognize patterns in data.
- They are inspired by the functioning of the human brain, where neurons process information and communicate with each other.
- Neural Networks are used in a variety of applications such as image recognition, speech recognition, and natural language processing.
- They can learn from examples and improve their performance over time.

### Architecture of Neural Networks

- The architecture of a Neural Network refers to the way in which the neurons are organized and connected.
- The basic building block of a Neural Network is the neuron, which receives input and produces output.
- Neurons are organized into layers, where each layer performs a specific function.
- The input layer receives the input data, and the output layer produces the output.
- The layers between the input and output layers are called hidden layers, and they perform intermediate computations.
- Each neuron in a layer is connected to all the neurons in the previous layer, and the strength of the connection is determined by a weight.
- The output of each neuron is determined by the weighted sum of its inputs, which is then passed through an activation function.

### Types of Neural Networks

- There are several types of Neural Networks, each with a different architecture and function.
- Feedforward Neural Networks are the most basic type, where the data flows from the input to the output layer without feedback.
- Recurrent Neural Networks are designed to process sequential data, where the output at each time step is fed back as input to the next time step.
- Convolutional Neural Networks are designed for image and video recognition tasks, where the input data has a spatial structure.
- Autoencoders are used for unsupervised learning tasks, where the network learns to encode and decode the input data.

In conclusion, Neural Networks are a powerful machine learning algorithm that can learn from examples and recognize patterns in data. The architecture of a Neural Network determines how the neurons are organized and connected, and there are several types of Neural Networks designed for different tasks.
