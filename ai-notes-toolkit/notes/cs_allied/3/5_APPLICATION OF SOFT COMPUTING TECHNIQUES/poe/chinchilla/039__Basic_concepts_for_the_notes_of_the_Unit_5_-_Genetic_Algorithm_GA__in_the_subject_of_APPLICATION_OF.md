### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

Genetic Algorithm (GA) is a popular evolutionary algorithm that is inspired by the natural evolution process. It is an optimization technique that is widely used to solve complex problems in various fields like engineering, economics, biology, and more.

Here are the basic concepts that you need to understand to learn Genetic Algorithm:

#### 1. Chromosomes

Chromosomes are the building blocks of the Genetic Algorithm. They are the solution representation of the problem. In a GA, a solution is represented by a string of characters, which is called a chromosome. Each character in the chromosome represents a parameter of the solution.

#### 2. Fitness Function

The fitness function is a crucial part of the GA. It is used to evaluate the fitness of a chromosome. The fitness function takes a chromosome as input and returns a fitness value. The fitness function is designed in a way that the higher the fitness value, the better the solution.

#### 3. Population

A population is a set of chromosomes that represent the solutions to the problem. In a GA, the population is initialized randomly, and then the optimization process starts.

#### 4. Selection

Selection is the process of selecting the fittest chromosomes from the population for the next generation. The selection process is based on the fitness value of the chromosomes. The fitter chromosomes have a higher chance of being selected for the next generation.

#### 5. Crossover

Crossover is a genetic operator that is used to create new offspring from two parent chromosomes. In the crossover process, a portion of the chromosome from one parent is exchanged with a portion of the chromosome from the other parent to create a new offspring.

#### 6. Mutation

Mutation is a genetic operator that is used to introduce new genetic material into the population. In the mutation process, a random change is made to a chromosome to create a new solution.

#### 7. Termination

Termination is the process of stopping the GA when a satisfactory solution is found. The termination criteria can be a maximum number of generations, a minimum fitness value, or a time limit.

In conclusion, Genetic Algorithm is a powerful optimization technique that can solve complex problems in various fields. The basic concepts of GA include chromosomes, fitness function, population, selection, crossover, mutation, and termination. Understanding these concepts is crucial to master Genetic Algorithm.
