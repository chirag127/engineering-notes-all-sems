
## Unit 1 - Neural Networks-I (Introduction & Architecture)

1. Neural networks are a type of artificial intelligence (AI) that is modeled after the human brain. They are composed of neurons and layers of connections, which allow them to learn from data and make decisions.

2. Neural networks are used in a variety of applications, including image recognition, natural language processing, robotics, and autonomous driving.

3. Neural networks are composed of neurons, which are connected in layers. The input layer receives data from the outside world, the hidden layers process the data, and the output layer produces the result.

4. Each neuron takes in an input and produces an output, which is then passed to the next neuron in the layer. The neurons in the hidden layers use mathematical operations to process the data and create a result.

5. Training a neural network involves adjusting the weights of the connections between the neurons. This is done by providing the network with labeled data and adjusting the weights until the network produces the desired result.

6. Neural networks can be supervised, unsupervised, or a combination of both. Supervised neural networks are trained with labeled data, while unsupervised neural networks are trained without labels.

7. Deep learning is a type of neural network that uses multiple layers of neurons to process data. It is used in many applications, including image recognition, natural language processing, and autonomous driving.
