

## Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA)

* Genetic Algorithms (GA) are a type of evolutionary algorithm that use natural selection processes to generate solutions to optimization problems.
* GA uses a population of candidate solutions and applies selection pressure to them over multiple generations.
* Each candidate solution is represented by a set of parameters, or a chromosome.
* Selection pressure is applied by evaluating each candidate solution according to a fitness function.
* The fittest candidates are selected to form the next generation of solutions.
* The process of selection and reproduction is repeated until a satisfactory solution is found.
* GA can be used to solve a variety of problems, from scheduling and routing, to feature selection and parameter optimization.
