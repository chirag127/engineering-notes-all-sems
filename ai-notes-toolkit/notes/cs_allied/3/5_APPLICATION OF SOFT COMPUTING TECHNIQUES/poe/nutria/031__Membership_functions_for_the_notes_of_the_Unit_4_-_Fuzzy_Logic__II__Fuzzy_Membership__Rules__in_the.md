
### Membership Functions for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Fuzzy Logic –II (Fuzzy Membership, Rules) in the Subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

* Fuzzy membership functions are used to represent a range of values for a given input. 
* The most common type of membership function is the triangular membership function, which is represented by three points: the lower boundary, the peak, and the upper boundary. 
* Other types of membership functions include trapezoidal, Gaussian, and sigmoid. 
* Fuzzy rules are used to define the relationships between fuzzy sets. 
* Fuzzy rules are written in the form of IF-THEN statements, where the IF part is the antecedent and the THEN part is the consequent. 
* Fuzzy rules can be used to define the behavior of a fuzzy system, such as a fuzzy controller. 
* Fuzzy logic can be used to approximate non-linear functions, such as those found in control systems. 
* Fuzzy logic can also be used to solve complex problems that are difficult to solve with traditional methods.
