

### Genetic Operators for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm (GA) in the Subject of Application of Soft Computing Techniques

1. Selection: Selection is the process of selecting individuals from a population to be used in the next generation. Selection is based on fitness, which is a measure of how well an individual is adapted to the environment. 

2. Crossover: Crossover is a genetic operator that combines two parent solutions to create a new solution. Crossover is used to create new solutions by combining the features of two or more existing solutions.

3. Mutation: Mutation is a genetic operator that randomly changes one or more bits in a solution. Mutation is used to introduce randomness into a population and to create new solutions.

4. Elitism: Elitism is a technique used to ensure that the best solutions in a population are not lost during the evolutionary process. Elitism is used to preserve the best solutions in a population and to ensure that the population does not get stuck in a local optimum.
