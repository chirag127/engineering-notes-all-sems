
### Generational Cycle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm(GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

1. Genetic algorithms are a type of optimization algorithm that uses the principles of natural selection to find the best solution to a problem. 
2. The process of a genetic algorithm is divided into a series of steps, known as the generational cycle.
3. The first step of the generational cycle is to generate an initial population of solutions to the problem. These solutions are randomly generated and can be represented as a set of numbers, strings, or other data structures.
4. After the initial population is generated, each individual in the population is evaluated using a fitness function. This fitness function assigns a score to each individual in the population based on how well it solves the problem.
5. After the fitness scores are calculated, the individuals in the population are selected for reproduction. This selection process is based on the fitness scores, with individuals with higher scores being more likely to be selected.
6. After the selection process, the individuals in the population are combined to create new individuals. This process is called crossover, and it involves combining the genetic information of two individuals to create a new individual with characteristics of both parents.
7. After crossover, the new individuals are subjected to a process called mutation. This process randomly changes the genetic information of the new individuals, which can help to create more diverse solutions.
8. After mutation, the new individuals are added to the population and the process repeats until a solution to the problem is found or the maximum number of generations has been reached.
