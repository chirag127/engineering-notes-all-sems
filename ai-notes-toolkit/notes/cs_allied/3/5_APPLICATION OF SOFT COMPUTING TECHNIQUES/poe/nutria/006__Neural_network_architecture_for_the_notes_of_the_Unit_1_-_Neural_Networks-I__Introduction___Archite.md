
### Neural Network Architecture

1. **Neural Networks** are computing systems that are modeled after the human brain, and are capable of performing complex tasks such as recognizing patterns, making decisions, and learning from data.

2. **Architecture** refers to the structure and organization of a neural network. It is composed of layers of neurons, which are connected to each other and to the input and output layers.

3. **Input Layer** is the first layer of a neural network and is responsible for receiving data from the outside world. It is composed of neurons that take in the input data and pass it to the hidden layers.

4. **Hidden Layers** are the layers between the input and output layers. They are composed of neurons that process the data and pass it to the output layer.

5. **Output Layer** is the last layer of a neural network and is responsible for producing the final output. It is composed of neurons that take the processed data from the hidden layers and produce the desired output.

6. Neural networks can be trained using various algorithms such as backpropagation, reinforcement learning, and evolutionary algorithms.

7. Neural networks can be used for a variety of applications such as image recognition, natural language processing, and robotics.
