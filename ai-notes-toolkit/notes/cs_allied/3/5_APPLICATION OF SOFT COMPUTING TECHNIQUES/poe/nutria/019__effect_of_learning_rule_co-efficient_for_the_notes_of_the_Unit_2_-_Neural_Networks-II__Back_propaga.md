
### Effect of Learning Rule Co-efficient for Unit 2 - Neural Networks-II (Back Propagation Networks)

1. Learning rule co-efficients are used to determine the rate at which a neural network learns from its environment.
2. They are used to adjust the weights of the neurons in the network, allowing for more accurate predictions.
3. The learning rule co-efficient is usually set to a value between 0 and 1.
4. A higher learning rule co-efficient will result in faster learning, but also increased risk of overfitting.
5. A lower learning rule co-efficient will result in slower learning, but also increased robustness.
6. It is important to choose the right learning rule co-efficient for the task at hand, as it can have a large effect on the accuracy of the network's predictions.
