
### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Genetic Algorithm (GA) in the subject of APPLICATION OF SOFT COMPUTING TECHNIQUES

* Genetic Algorithms (GAs) are evolutionary algorithms that are inspired by the principles of natural selection and genetics.
* GAs are used to solve optimization problems, such as finding the best solution to a given problem.
* GAs are based on the idea of survival of the fittest, where the fittest individuals survive and reproduce, while the weaker ones die off.
* GAs use a population of individuals (solutions) and apply genetic operators such as crossover and mutation to generate new solutions.
* The fitness of each solution is evaluated and the best solutions are selected for reproduction.
* The process is repeated until an optimal solution is obtained.
* GAs are used in a wide variety of applications, such as machine learning, image processing, and robotics.
