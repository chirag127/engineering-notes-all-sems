### Central Limit Theorem

The Central Limit Theorem is an important concept in statistics that describes the behavior of the means of a large number of independent and identically distributed random variables. This theorem is used in various fields, including AI, ML, and data science. Here are some key points about the Central Limit Theorem:

1. The Central Limit Theorem states that the sum or average of a large number of independent and identically distributed random variables will converge to a normal distribution, regardless of the underlying distribution.

2. The Central Limit Theorem is important because it allows us to make inferences about the population mean based on the sample mean, even if the population is not normally distributed.

3. The Central Limit Theorem can be applied to discrete or continuous random variables, as long as the sample size is large enough.

4. The Central Limit Theorem assumes that the random variables are independent and identically distributed, which means that each variable has the same probability distribution and is not affected by the other variables.

5. The Central Limit Theorem can be used to calculate confidence intervals and hypothesis tests for the population mean.

6. The Central Limit Theorem has practical applications in various fields, such as finance, economics, and physics.

In conclusion, the Central Limit Theorem is a fundamental concept in statistics that is widely used in AI, ML, and data science. By understanding this theorem, we can make accurate inferences about the population mean based on the sample mean, even if the underlying distribution is not normal.
