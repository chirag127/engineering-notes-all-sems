### Triangle Inequality

In vector spaces, the triangle inequality is a property that relates the lengths of vectors to the sum of their lengths. It is an important property that is used in many applications.

The triangle inequality states that for any vectors **u** and **v** in a vector space:

1. The length of the sum of two vectors **u** and **v** is less than or equal to the sum of the lengths of **u** and **v**. This can be written as:

   ||**u + v**|| ≤ ||**u**|| + ||**v**||
