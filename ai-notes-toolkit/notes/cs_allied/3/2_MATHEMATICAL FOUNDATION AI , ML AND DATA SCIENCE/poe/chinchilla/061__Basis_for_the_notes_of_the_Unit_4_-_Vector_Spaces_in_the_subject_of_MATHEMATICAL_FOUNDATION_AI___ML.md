### Basis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

In this unit, we will learn about vector spaces, which are fundamental to many areas of mathematics and are also important in machine learning and data science. Here are the key points to keep in mind:

- A vector space is a collection of vectors that can be added together and multiplied by scalars (numbers). These operations must satisfy certain properties, such as associativity and distributivity.
- The vectors in a vector space can be represented as columns or rows of numbers, or as geometric objects in space. The choice of representation depends on the context and the problem at hand.
- A basis for a vector space is a set of vectors that can be used to represent any vector in the space. In other words, every vector in the space can be written as a linear combination of the basis vectors, with unique coefficients.
- A basis is not unique, but it has a well-defined dimension, which is the number of vectors in the basis. Two bases for the same vector space must have the same dimension.
- The standard basis for a vector space is the set of vectors with one component equal to 1 and all other components equal to 0. This basis is usually used for Euclidean spaces, which are vector spaces with a notion of distance and angle.
- A linear transformation is a function that maps vectors from one vector space to another, while preserving certain properties such as linearity and continuity. Linear transformations can be represented by matrices, and they play a central role in many applications of vector spaces, such as image processing and data compression.
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors are important concepts in linear algebra, which describe how a linear transformation stretches or shrinks certain directions in a vector space. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors can be used to simplify computations and to understand the behavior of linear systems.

By mastering the concepts of vector spaces and linear transformations, you will be able to tackle a wide range of problems in mathematics, machine learning, and data science.
