## Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics is the branch of statistics that deals with the analysis and interpretation of data. It involves the use of various techniques to summarize and describe data in a meaningful way. In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

### 1.1 Types of Data

Data can be classified into two major types:

- **Qualitative data:** This type of data describes the characteristics or attributes of a population, such as gender, marital status, or occupation. It is also known as categorical data.

- **Quantitative data:** This type of data consists of numerical values that represent measurements or counts of a population, such as height, weight, or age. It is also known as numerical data.

### 1.2 Measures of Central Tendency

Measures of central tendency are used to describe the central or typical value of a dataset. The three most commonly used measures of central tendency are:

- **Mean:** The arithmetic average of a dataset.

- **Median:** The middle value of a dataset when arranged in order.

- **Mode:** The value that appears most frequently in a dataset.

### 1.3 Measures of Dispersion

Measures of dispersion are used to describe the amount of variability or spread in a dataset. The three most commonly used measures of dispersion are:

- **Range:** The difference between the maximum and minimum values in a dataset.

- **Variance:** The average of the squared differences from the mean.

- **Standard deviation:** The square root of the variance.

### 1.4 Graphical Representation of Data

Graphical representation of data is a visual way to present data that helps in the interpretation and understanding of data. Some commonly used graphs are:

- **Histogram:** A graph that displays the distribution of a dataset.

- **Bar chart:** A graph that displays the frequency of categorical data.

- **Box plot:** A graph that displays the distribution of numerical data.

### 1.5 Probability Distributions

Probability distributions are used to describe the probability of occurrence of different outcomes in a random process. Some commonly used probability distributions are:

- **Normal distribution:** A symmetric distribution that is commonly used to model many natural phenomena.

- **Binomial distribution:** A distribution that describes the probability of occurrence of a certain number of successes in a fixed number of trials.

- **Poisson distribution:** A distribution that describes the probability of occurrence of a certain number of events in a fixed interval of time.

In conclusion, descriptive statistics is a fundamental tool for analyzing and interpreting data. The techniques covered in this unit are essential for understanding the characteristics of a dataset and for making informed decisions based on data.
