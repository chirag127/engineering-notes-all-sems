### Bivariate Correlation

Bivariate correlation is a statistical method used to measure the relationship between two variables. In this unit, we will learn about bivariate correlation in the context of descriptive statistics in the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Bivariate correlation is used to determine the strength and direction of the relationship between two variables.
- The strength of the relationship is measured by the correlation coefficient, which varies between -1 and 1.
- A positive correlation coefficient indicates a positive relationship between the variables, while a negative correlation coefficient indicates a negative relationship.
- A correlation coefficient of 0 indicates no relationship between the variables.
- Bivariate correlation is often used in data analysis to identify patterns and trends in the data.
- It is important to note that correlation does not imply causation. Just because two variables are correlated, it does not mean that one variable causes the other.
- Bivariate correlation can be visualized using a scatter plot, which shows the relationship between the variables.
- Outliers in the data can affect the correlation coefficient, so it is important to identify and handle them appropriately.
- Bivariate correlation can be calculated using various methods, including Pearson correlation and Spearman correlation.

In conclusion, bivariate correlation is a useful tool for analyzing the relationship between two variables. As we delve deeper into the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science, it is important to have a strong understanding of bivariate correlation and its applications.
