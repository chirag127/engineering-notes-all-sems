- The QR decomposition is a factorization of a matrix A into a product A = QR, where Q is an orthogonal matrix and R is an upper triangular matrix.
- The QR decomposition is commonly used to solve linear least squares problems and to perform orthogonalization of a set of vectors.
- The Householder transformation is a particular type of reflection that can be used to construct an orthogonal matrix Q.
- The reflection matrix H is defined by H = I - 2uu^T / ||u||^2, where u is a vector of length n.
- The Householder transformation can be used iteratively to construct the orthogonal matrix Q from a set of vectors.
