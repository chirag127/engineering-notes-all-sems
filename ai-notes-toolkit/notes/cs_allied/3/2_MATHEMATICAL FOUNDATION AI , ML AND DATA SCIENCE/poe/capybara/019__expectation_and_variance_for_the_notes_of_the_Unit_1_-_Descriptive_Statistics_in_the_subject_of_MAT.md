### Expectation and Variance for the notes of the Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Descriptive statistics is a crucial aspect of data analysis, which helps in understanding and summarizing data. In this unit, we will learn about expectation and variance, which are two important measures used in descriptive statistics.

#### Expectation

Expectation is a measure of the central tendency of a random variable. It is also known as the mean or the average. The expectation of a random variable X is denoted by E(X) and is calculated as:

E(X) = Σ [xi * P(xi)]

where xi is the value of the random variable and P(xi) is the probability of xi.

#### Variance

Variance is a measure of the spread or dispersion of a random variable. It is denoted by Var(X) and is calculated as:

Var(X) = E[(X - μ)^2]

where μ is the mean of the random variable X.

The variance of a random variable X can also be calculated as:

Var(X) = E(X^2) - [E(X)]^2

#### Properties of Expectation and Variance

1. Linearity: E(aX + bY) = aE(X) + bE(Y) and Var(aX + bY) = a^2Var(X) + b^2Var(Y), where a and b are constants.

2. Independence: If X and Y are independent random variables, then E(XY) = E(X)E(Y) and Var(X + Y) = Var(X) + Var(Y).

3. Additivity: If X1, X2, ..., Xn are independent random variables, then E(X1 + X2 + ... + Xn) = E(X1) + E(X2) + ... + E(Xn) and Var(X1 + X2 + ... + Xn) = Var(X1) + Var(X2) + ... + Var(Xn).

In conclusion, understanding the concepts of expectation and variance is essential for data analysis. These measures help in summarizing the data and making informed decisions.
