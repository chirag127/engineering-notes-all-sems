### Subspace for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

Vector spaces are fundamental in the study of mathematics and its applications in AI, ML, and data science. In this unit, we will focus on subspaces of vector spaces.

Here are the key points to understand subspaces:

- A subspace of a vector space V is a subset of V that is itself a vector space under the same operations of addition and scalar multiplication as V.
- To check if a subset W of V is a subspace of V, we need to verify the following three conditions:
  - The zero vector of V is in W.
  - W is closed under vector addition, meaning that if u and v are in W, then u + v is also in W.
  - W is closed under scalar multiplication, meaning that if u is in W and a is a scalar, then au is also in W.
- The intersection of any number of subspaces of V is also a subspace of V.
- The span of a set of vectors in V is the smallest subspace of V that contains all those vectors.
- The dimension of a subspace W of V is the maximum number of linearly independent vectors in W. It is always less than or equal to the dimension of V.
- A proper subspace of V is a subspace of V that is not equal to V itself.

Here are some examples of subspaces:

- The set of all solutions to a homogeneous system of linear equations is a subspace of the vector space of all solutions to the corresponding non-homogeneous system.
- The set of all polynomials of degree at most n is a subspace of the vector space of all polynomials.
- The set of all symmetric matrices is a subspace of the vector space of all matrices.

In summary, subspaces are important concepts in the study of vector spaces. Understanding subspaces allows us to classify and analyze the structure of vector spaces and their applications in AI, ML, and data science.
