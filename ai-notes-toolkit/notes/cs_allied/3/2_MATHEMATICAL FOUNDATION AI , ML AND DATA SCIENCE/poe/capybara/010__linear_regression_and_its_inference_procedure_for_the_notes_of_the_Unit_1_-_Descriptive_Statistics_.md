### Linear Regression and its Inference Procedure

Linear regression is one of the most commonly used statistical techniques for modeling the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. It is a powerful tool that is widely used in the field of data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. In this unit, we will learn about the inference procedure for linear regression.

Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Linear regression is a statistical method for modeling the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables.
- The dependent variable is the outcome or response variable, while the independent variables are the predictors or explanatory variables.
- The basic idea behind linear regression is to find the line of best fit that describes the relationship between the dependent and independent variables.
- The line of best fit is obtained by minimizing the sum of the squared errors between the observed values and predicted values.
- The slope and intercept of the line of best fit can be estimated using the least squares method.
- The least squares method involves finding the values of slope and intercept that minimize the sum of the squared errors.
- Once the line of best fit has been obtained, we can use it to make predictions about the dependent variable based on the values of the independent variable(s).
- The inference procedure for linear regression involves testing the hypothesis that the slope of the line of best fit is equal to zero.
- The null hypothesis is that there is no relationship between the dependent and independent variables.
- The alternative hypothesis is that there is a relationship between the dependent and independent variables.
- To test the hypothesis, we use the t-test, which compares the estimated slope to its standard error.
- If the t-statistic is greater than the critical value, we reject the null hypothesis and conclude that there is a significant relationship between the dependent and independent variables.

In conclusion, linear regression is a powerful tool for modeling the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. The inference procedure for linear regression involves testing the hypothesis that there is no relationship between the dependent and independent variables. By understanding this procedure, we can make more accurate predictions and draw more meaningful conclusions from our data.
