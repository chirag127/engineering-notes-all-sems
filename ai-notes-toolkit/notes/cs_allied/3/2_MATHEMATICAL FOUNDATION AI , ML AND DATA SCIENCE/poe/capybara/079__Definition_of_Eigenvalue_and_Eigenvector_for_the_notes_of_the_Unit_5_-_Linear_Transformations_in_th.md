### Definition of Eigenvalue and Eigenvector:

Eigenvalue and Eigenvector are important concepts in Linear Algebra, which are used in various fields like data science, machine learning and artificial intelligence. Here are some important points to understand these concepts:

1. An Eigenvector is a non-zero vector that remains in the same direction after a linear transformation. In other words, the Eigenvector is scaled by a factor, known as Eigenvalue, when it is transformed.

2. Eigenvalue represents the scalar value by which an Eigenvector is scaled when it is transformed. It is represented by the symbol λ.

3. The Eigenvalue and Eigenvector are always calculated together, and they are important in solving systems of linear equations.

4. The Eigenvectors are usually normalized to have a unit length, which makes them easier to work with.

5. Eigenvectors corresponding to different Eigenvalues are linearly independent, and they form a basis for the space in which the transformation takes place.

6. Eigenvectors corresponding to the same Eigenvalue can be linearly dependent, and they form a subspace of the space in which the transformation takes place.

7. Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors are used in various applications, such as image compression, principal component analysis, and machine learning algorithms like PCA and SVD.

In summary, Eigenvalue and Eigenvector are important concepts in Linear Algebra, which play a crucial role in various fields like data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. Understanding these concepts is important for solving systems of linear equations and for developing efficient algorithms for data analysis.
