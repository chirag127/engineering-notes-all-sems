### Inner Product for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Inner product is a function that takes two vectors and returns a scalar. It is a measure of similarity between two vectors.
2. The inner product of two vectors u and v is denoted by ⟨u,v⟩ or u•v or uTv.
3. Properties of Inner Product:
- ⟨u,v⟩ is linear in u: ⟨αu,v⟩ = α⟨u,v⟩ for any scalar α.
- ⟨u,v⟩ is linear in v: ⟨u,αv⟩ = α⟨u,v⟩ for any scalar α.
- ⟨u,u⟩ ≥ 0 and ⟨u,u⟩ = 0 if and only if u = 0.
- ⟨u,v⟩ = ⟨v,u⟩ (Symmetry property).
4. The angle between two vectors u and v is given by θ = cos−1(⟨u,v⟩/‖u‖‖v‖). This gives the similarity between the vectors. Larger the inner product, smaller the angle between the vectors and vice-versa.
5. Orthogonal vectors: Two vectors u and v are said to be orthogonal if their inner product is zero i.e. ⟨u,v⟩ = 0. They are perpendicular to each other.
