### Measures of probability for the notes of the Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Probability - Probability is a measure of the likelihood that an event will occur. It is quantified as a number between 0 and 1.
2. Relative frequency - The relative frequency of an event is the number of times the event occurs divided by the total number of trials or observations. It is an estimate of the probability of an event.
3. Odds - The odds of an event is the ratio of the probability that the event will occur to the probability that it will not occur. It is calculated as:
Odds = Probability / (1 - Probability)
4. Conditional probability - The conditional probability of an event A given that another event B has occurred is the probability that event A will occur given that event B has already occurred. It is calculated as:
P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B)
