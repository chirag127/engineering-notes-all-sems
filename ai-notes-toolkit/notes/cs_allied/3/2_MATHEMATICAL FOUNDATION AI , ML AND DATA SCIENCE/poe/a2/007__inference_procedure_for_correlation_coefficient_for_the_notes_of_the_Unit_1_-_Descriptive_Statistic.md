### Inference Procedure for Correlation Coefficient

- Calculate the sample correlation coefficient, r, between the two variables.
- Determine the degrees of freedom, df = n - 2, where n is the sample size.
- Determine the critical values for the given significance level, α, from the correlation coefficient distribution table. The critical values are denoted by rcv.
- If the calculated r value lies in between -rcv and +rcv, then we fail to reject the null hypothesis (H0: ρ = 0). This means the variables are uncorrelated.
- If the calculated r value is less than -rcv or greater than +rcv, then we reject the null hypothesis. This means the variables are correlated either positively or negatively respectively.
