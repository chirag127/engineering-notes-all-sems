### Orthogonalization by Householder Transformations (QR)

1. Householder transformations generate a sequence of orthogonal matrices that can be used to zero out elements below the main diagonal of a matrix.
2. This is a key step in the QR decomposition and allows us to transform a matrix A into an upper triangular matrix R.
3. The Householder transformation involves a reflection about a plane/hyperplane generated by a vector u. The vector u is designed to zero out a specific element of the matrix.
4. The Householder transformation matrix H_u is of the form:
H_u = I - 2uu^T/u^Tu
5. By applying Householder transformations that zero out one element at a time, we can ultimately arrive at an upper triangular R.
6. The QR decomposition is numerically stable and useful for calculating eigenvalues/eigenvectors and solving systems of linear equations.
7. TheHouseholder transformation is a smart way to generate orthogonal transformations to zero out elements and simplify matrices. It plays an important role in linear algebra and numerical linear algebra.
