### Subspace

Notes for Unit 4 - Vector Spaces

- A subspace of a vector space V is a subset W of V that is a vector space with the vector addition and scalar multiplication inherited from V.
- The zero vector 0 must be in W.
- If v and w are in W, then v+w must be in W.
- If v is in W and c is a scalar, then cv must be in W.
- A subspace is a vector space that is a subset of another vector space.
- The subset of vectors spanning a subspace is linearly independent.
- A subspace must contain the zero vector and be closed under vector addition and scalar multiplication.
- Subspaces are useful for simplifying problems and focusing on relevant vectors.
- To show that W is a subspace:
    - Show 0 is in W
    - Show v,w in W implies v+w in W
    - Show v in W, c scalar implies cv in W
- Examples:
    - {0} is a subspace of any vector space
    - Span of a set of vectors is a subspace
    - Entire vector space V is a subspace of itself (called the trivial subspace)
