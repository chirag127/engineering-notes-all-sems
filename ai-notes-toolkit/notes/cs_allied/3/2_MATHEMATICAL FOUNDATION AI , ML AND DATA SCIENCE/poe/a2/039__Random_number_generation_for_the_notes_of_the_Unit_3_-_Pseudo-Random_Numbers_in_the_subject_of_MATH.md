### Random number generation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers

1. Random number generation: The process of generating a sequence of numbers or symbols that lacks any pattern, i.e. appear random.
2. Pseudo-random number: A random number generated by a deterministic algorithm. It is not truly random but produces outputs that pass certain statistical tests for randomness.
3. Uses of pseudo-random numbers:
    - Simulations like Monte Carlo simulations
    - Creating artificial data for training ML models
    - Cryptography
    - Sampling methods like bootstrap sampling
4. Properties of good pseudo-random number generators:
    - Uniformity: Numbers should be uniformly distributed
    - Independence: Numbers should be independent of previous numbers
    - Period: Sequence should have a long period before repeating
    - Speed: Generator should be fast
5. Examples of pseudo-random number generators:
    - Linear congruential generator (LCG)
    - Mersenne twister
    - Python's random module
