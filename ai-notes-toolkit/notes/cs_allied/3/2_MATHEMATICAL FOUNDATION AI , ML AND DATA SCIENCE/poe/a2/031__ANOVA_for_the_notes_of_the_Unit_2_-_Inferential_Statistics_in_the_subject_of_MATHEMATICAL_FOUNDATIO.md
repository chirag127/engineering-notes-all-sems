### ANOVA for the notes of the Unit 2 - Inferential Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI , ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. ANOVA stands for Analysis of Variance. It is a statistical technique to compare means of two or more groups in order to determine if there is a significant difference between them.
2. ANOVA tests the null hypothesis that the means of all groups are equal. If the p-value is less than the significance level, we reject the null hypothesis and conclude that at least one of the means is different.
3. The steps involved in ANOVA are:
- State the null and alternate hypothesis
- Select the significance level
- Collect the samples and calculate the means of each group
- Calculate the total variation between the groups and within the groups
- Calculate the F-statistic and p-value
- Draw inferences based on p-value
4. ANOVA has applications in various fields like science, engineering, psychology, etc. to compare group means and understand if a factor has a significant effect on the variable under study.
5. The key assumptions of ANOVA are:
- The samples are independent of each other
- The populations follow a normal distribution
- The variances of populations are equal
