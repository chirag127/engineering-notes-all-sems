
### Importance Sampling for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

1. Importance Sampling is a technique used in Monte Carlo integration to estimate the value of a certain integral.

2. It is used to reduce the variance of a Monte Carlo estimator by assigning higher probability to regions of the integration domain where the integrand is more likely to be large.

3. The technique is based on the observation that the integrand can be written as a product of two functions: a sampling function and an importance function.

4. The sampling function is used to sample points from the domain of integration, while the importance function is used to assign higher probability to those points where the integrand is likely to be large.

5. The importance sampling technique is especially useful when the integrand is difficult to sample directly, or when the integrand is not known explicitly.

6. Pseudo-random numbers are used to generate the samples for importance sampling. These numbers are generated using a deterministic algorithm, but appear to be random.

7. Pseudo-random numbers are used in many applications, including Monte Carlo integration, cryptography, and simulation.
