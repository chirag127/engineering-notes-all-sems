
### Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)

1. Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) is a type of computational method used to generate random numbers for use in AI, Machine Learning, and Data Science.
2. MCMC algorithms are based on Markov Chains, which are a type of mathematical structure that can be used to model the behavior of a system over time.
3. MCMC algorithms use a random number generator to sample from a probability distribution, which is then used to generate a sequence of random numbers.
4. MCMC algorithms are used to sample from complex probability distributions, such as those used in Bayesian Statistics.
5. MCMC algorithms can be used to estimate parameters in a model, such as the parameters of a neural network.
6. MCMC algorithms can also be used to sample from a distribution in order to generate samples for use in simulation and data analysis.
7. MCMC algorithms are used in many fields, including finance, engineering, and medicine.
