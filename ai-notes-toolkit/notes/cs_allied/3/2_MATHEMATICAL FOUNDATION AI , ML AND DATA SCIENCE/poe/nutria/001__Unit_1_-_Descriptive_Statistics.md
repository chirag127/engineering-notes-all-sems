
## Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics 

* Descriptive statistics is a branch of statistics that deals with summarizing and organizing data. 
* It is used to describe and interpret data in order to gain insight into the underlying population.
* Descriptive statistics can be used to describe the properties of a single variable or multiple variables.
* Common descriptive statistics include measures of central tendency such as mean, median, and mode, as well as measures of variability such as range, variance, and standard deviation.
* Descriptive statistics can also be used to create graphical representations of data, such as histograms, box plots, and scatter plots.
* Descriptive statistics can be used to identify relationships between variables, such as correlation and regression.
