
### Variance Reduction for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

1. Variance reduction is a technique used to reduce the amount of randomness in a statistical sample.
2. The goal of variance reduction is to reduce the variability of a sample so that the results are more consistent and reliable.
3. Pseudo-random numbers are numbers that are generated using a deterministic algorithm, but appear to be random.
4. Pseudo-random numbers can be used to generate samples with a reduced amount of variance.
5. Variance reduction techniques can be used to reduce the variance of a pseudo-random number generator.
6. Common variance reduction techniques include stratified sampling, antithetic variates, and Latin Hypercube Sampling.
7. Stratified sampling divides the sample into multiple sub-samples and then randomly samples from each sub-sample.
8. Antithetic variates involve generating two samples from the same source and then combining them in a way that reduces the variance.
9. Latin Hypercube Sampling involves randomly sampling from a set of points that are evenly distributed across the sample space.
10. Variance reduction techniques can be used to improve the accuracy of predictions and reduce the amount of data needed to make reliable predictions.
