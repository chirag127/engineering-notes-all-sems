
### Random Variables for Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science

* A random variable is a variable whose value is determined by chance. It is an abstract concept used to model unpredictable phenomena. 
* Random variables can be discrete or continuous. A discrete random variable takes on a finite or countable number of distinct values, while a continuous random variable takes on an infinite number of possible values.
* Examples of discrete random variables include the number of heads in a series of coin flips, the number of cars passing through an intersection in a given hour, and the number of people in a room. Examples of continuous random variables include the time it takes for a car to travel a certain distance, the temperature at a certain location, and the amount of rainfall in a given area.
* In descriptive statistics, random variables are used to measure the characteristics of a population or a sample. They can be used to calculate measures of central tendency such as the mean, median, and mode, as well as measures of variability such as the standard deviation and range.
