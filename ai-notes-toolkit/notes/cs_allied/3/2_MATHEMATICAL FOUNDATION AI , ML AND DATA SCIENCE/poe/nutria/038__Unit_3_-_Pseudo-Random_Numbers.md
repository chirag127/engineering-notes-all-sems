

## Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers

Pseudo-random numbers are numbers generated by a computer algorithm that appear to be random, but are actually generated in a predictable way. Pseudo-random numbers are used in various applications, such as cryptography, simulations, and gaming.

* Pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs) are used to generate sequences of numbers that appear random, but are actually generated in a predictable way.
* PRNGs are deterministic algorithms, meaning that the same sequence of numbers will be generated each time the algorithm is run.
* PRNGs are often used in cryptography, simulations, and gaming.
* PRNGs are based on mathematical principles and use mathematical operations to generate a sequence of numbers.
* PRNGs typically use a seed value to start the sequence, which can be used to generate the same sequence of numbers again.
* PRNGs may use linear congruential generators, which use a sequence of mathematical operations to generate a sequence of numbers.
* PRNGs may also use cryptographic hash functions, which are used to generate a sequence of numbers that are difficult to predict.
* PRNGs are often tested for randomness using statistical tests, such as the Chi-Square test.
