
## Unit 2 - Inferential Statistics

* Inferential statistics is a branch of statistics that focuses on drawing conclusions from data that are subject to random variation. 
* It is used to make predictions and assess the strength of evidence in scientific research.
* It uses probability theory to draw inferences from sample data to estimate population parameters.
* It uses methods such as hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, and regression analysis to make inferences.
* It is used to test hypotheses about populations and make decisions about them.
* It can also be used to compare different groups of data and make predictions about future data.
