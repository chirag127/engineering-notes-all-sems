
### Measures of Central Tendency for Descriptive Statistics

1. **Mean**: The mean (or average) is the sum of all the values in a dataset divided by the number of values. It is the most common measure of central tendency.

2. **Median**: The median is the middle value in a dataset when the values are arranged in order from smallest to largest. It is less affected by outliers than the mean.

3. **Mode**: The mode is the most frequent value in a dataset. If no value appears more than once, the dataset has no mode.
