
### Correlation for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the Subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science 

1. Correlation is a measure of the linear relationship between two variables. It is represented by a number between -1 and 1, where -1 indicates a perfect negative correlation and 1 indicates a perfect positive correlation. 
2. Correlation is used to determine if two variables are related to each other and can be used to predict one variable from the other. 
3. Correlation can be calculated using the Pearson’s correlation coefficient, which is a measure of the linear relationship between two variables. 
4. Correlation is affected by outliers and should be used with caution when outliers are present. 
5. Correlation does not imply causation, meaning that a correlation between two variables does not necessarily mean that one variable causes the other. 
6. Correlation can be used to identify clusters or groups in a dataset. 
7. Correlation can be used to identify trends in a dataset. 
8. Correlation can be used to identify potential relationships between variables.
