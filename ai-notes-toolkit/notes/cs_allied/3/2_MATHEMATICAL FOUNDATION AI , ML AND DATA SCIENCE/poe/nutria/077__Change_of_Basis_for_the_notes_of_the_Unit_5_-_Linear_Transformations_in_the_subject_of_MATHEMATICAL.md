
### Change of Basis for the Unit 5 - Linear Transformations

1. Change of basis is a concept used in linear algebra that allows us to represent a vector in different coordinate systems. 
2. A basis is a set of vectors that span a vector space and can be used to represent any vector in the vector space. 
3. The change of basis matrix, denoted by $P$, is used to transform a vector from one basis to another. 
4. The change of basis matrix is calculated by computing the inverse of the matrix formed by the basis vectors of the original basis. 
5. The change of basis matrix can be used to calculate the transformation matrix that maps a linear transformation from one basis to another. 
6. The change of basis matrix can also be used to calculate the inverse of a linear transformation. 
7. The change of basis matrix can be used to calculate the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a linear transformation. 
8. The change of basis matrix can be used to calculate the matrix representation of a linear transformation in different bases.
