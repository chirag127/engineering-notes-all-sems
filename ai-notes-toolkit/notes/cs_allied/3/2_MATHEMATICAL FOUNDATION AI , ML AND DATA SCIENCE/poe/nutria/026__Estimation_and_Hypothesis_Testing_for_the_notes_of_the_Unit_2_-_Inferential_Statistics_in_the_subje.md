
### Estimation and Hypothesis Testing 

Estimation and hypothesis testing are two important concepts in inferential statistics. Estimation involves the use of sample data to estimate population parameters. Hypothesis testing involves the use of sample data to test a hypothesis about a population parameter. 

* Estimation involves the use of sample data to estimate population parameters, such as the population mean, population proportion, and population standard deviation. 
* Hypothesis testing involves the use of sample data to test a hypothesis about a population parameter. In a hypothesis test, the null hypothesis is a statement about the population parameter, and the alternative hypothesis is the statement that is being tested. 
* The test statistic is used to compare the observed data with the expected data. The test statistic is calculated using the sample data, and the p-value is calculated from the test statistic. 
* The p-value is used to determine whether the null hypothesis should be rejected or not. If the p-value is less than the significance level, the null hypothesis is rejected. 
* In addition to hypothesis testing, inferential statistics also includes confidence intervals, which are used to estimate population parameters with a certain level of confidence. 
* Finally, inferential statistics also includes correlation and regression analysis, which are used to investigate the relationship between two or more variables.
