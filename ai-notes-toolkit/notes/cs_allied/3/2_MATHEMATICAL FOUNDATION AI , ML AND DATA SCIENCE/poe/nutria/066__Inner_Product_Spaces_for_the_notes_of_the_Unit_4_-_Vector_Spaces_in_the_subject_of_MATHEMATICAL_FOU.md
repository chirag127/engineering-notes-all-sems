

### Inner Product Spaces

Inner product spaces are an important concept in the study of mathematical foundations of AI, ML and Data Science. They are a type of vector space which allows for the calculation of inner products between two vectors. Inner product spaces have the following properties:

1. **Closed under addition and scalar multiplication**: If two vectors, _x_ and _y_, are in an inner product space, then the sum of the two vectors, _x + y_, and any scalar multiple of the vectors, _ax_ and _ay_, are also in the inner product space. 

2. **Non-degeneracy**: The inner product of two vectors in an inner product space is non-zero, i.e. _<x, y> ≠ 0_.

3. **Symmetry**: The inner product of two vectors in an inner product space is symmetric, i.e. _<x, y> = <y, x>_.

4. **Positive definiteness**: The inner product of a vector with itself in an inner product space is always greater than or equal to zero, i.e. _<x, x> ≥ 0_.

Inner product spaces are used in many areas of AI, ML and Data Science, such as in optimization, machine learning and data analysis. They are also used to define distance and similarity measures between vectors, which can be used to compare different data points.
