
### Bayes’ Theorem for the Notes of Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Bayes' Theorem is a mathematical tool used to calculate probabilities and make decisions.
2. It is named after the English mathematician Thomas Bayes (1702-1761).
3. It is based on the idea of conditional probability, which is the probability of an event given that another event has already occurred.
4. Bayes' Theorem states that the probability of an event A, given that event B has occurred, is equal to the probability of event B given that event A has occurred, multiplied by the probability of event A, divided by the probability of event B.
5. This can be expressed mathematically as:

P(A|B) = P(B|A) * P(A) / P(B)

6. Bayes' Theorem can be used to calculate the probability of an event given the probability of other related events.
7. For example, it can be used to calculate the probability of a disease given the probability of its symptoms.
8. It can also be used to calculate the probability of a hypothesis given the evidence for or against it.
9. Bayes' Theorem is an important tool in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science.
10. It is used in many applications, such as natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.
