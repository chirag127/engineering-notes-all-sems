
### Definition of Eigenvalue and Eigenvector 

* **Eigenvalue**: An eigenvalue is a scalar that is associated with a linear transformation. It is a measure of the amount of stretching or shrinking that occurs when the transformation is applied. 

* **Eigenvector**: An eigenvector is a vector that is associated with a linear transformation. It is a vector that is not changed by the transformation and is used to describe the direction of the transformation.
