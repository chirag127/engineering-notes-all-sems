
### Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality

The Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality is an important mathematical result that is used in the study of vector spaces. It states that for any two vectors *u* and *v*, the following inequality holds:

$$ \lvert \langle u, v \rangle \rvert \leq \lVert u \rVert \lVert v \rVert $$

where $\langle u, v \rangle$ is the inner product of *u* and *v*, and $\lVert u \rVert$ and $\lVert v \rVert$ are the magnitudes of *u* and *v*, respectively.

The Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality is a useful tool for proving other results in vector spaces. For example, it can be used to prove the Triangle Inequality, which states that for any three vectors *u*, *v* and *w*, the following inequality holds:

$$ \lVert u + v \rVert \leq \lVert u \rVert + \lVert v \rVert $$

The Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality is also used in the study of linear algebra, where it can be used to prove the existence of solutions to certain systems of linear equations.
