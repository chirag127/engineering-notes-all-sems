### Diagrammatic representation of data

- Diagrammatic representation of data is a way of presenting numerical data using graphs, charts, diagrams, or pictures.
- Diagrammatic representation of data helps to summarize, compare, and visualize the data in an effective and easy way.
- Diagrammatic representation of data can be classified into two types: univariate and bivariate.
- Univariate diagrams are used to display the distribution of a single variable, such as frequency distribution, histogram, bar chart, pie chart, line chart, etc.
- Bivariate diagrams are used to display the relationship between two variables, such as scatter plot, regression line, correlation coefficient, etc.
- Some of the advantages of diagrammatic representation of data are:
  - It makes the data more attractive and appealing to the audience.
  - It helps to highlight the important features and trends of the data.
  - It facilitates the comparison and analysis of the data.
  - It aids the memory and recall of the data.
- Some of the disadvantages of diagrammatic representation of data are:
  - It may not be able to show the exact values and details of the data.
  - It may be misleading or biased if the scale, proportion, or color is not chosen properly.
  - It may require more time and effort to construct and interpret than tabular representation of data.
