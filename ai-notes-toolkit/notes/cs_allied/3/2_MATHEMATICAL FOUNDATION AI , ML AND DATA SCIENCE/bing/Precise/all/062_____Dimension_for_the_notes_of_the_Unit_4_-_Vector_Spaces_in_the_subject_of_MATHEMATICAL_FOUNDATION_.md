# Dimension

Dimension is a fundamental concept in vector spaces, which is a topic in Unit 4 of the subject Mathematical Foundation for AI, ML, and Data Science. Here are some key points to remember about dimension:

1. Dimension is the number of vectors in a basis for a vector space.
2. A basis for a vector space is a set of linearly independent vectors that span the space.
3. The dimension of a vector space is unique, meaning that all bases for the space have the same number of vectors.
4. The dimension of a subspace is always less than or equal to the dimension of the larger space.
5. The dimension of the null space and the row space of a matrix add up to the number of columns of the matrix.
6. The rank of a matrix is the dimension of its row space.
7. The dimension of the column space of a matrix is equal to the rank of the matrix.
