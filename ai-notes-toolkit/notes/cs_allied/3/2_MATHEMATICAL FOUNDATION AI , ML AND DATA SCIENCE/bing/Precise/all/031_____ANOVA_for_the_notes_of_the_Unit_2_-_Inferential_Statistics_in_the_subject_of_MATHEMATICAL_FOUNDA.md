# ANOVA

ANOVA, or Analysis of Variance, is a statistical method used to test the differences between two or more means. It is commonly used in the field of inferential statistics, which is a branch of statistics that deals with drawing conclusions from data. ANOVA is often used in the field of AI, ML, and Data Science as it provides a way to determine if there are significant differences between groups.

Here are some key points to remember about ANOVA:

1. ANOVA is used to compare the means of two or more groups.
2. The null hypothesis in ANOVA is that all group means are equal.
3. The alternative hypothesis is that at least one group mean is different from the others.
4. ANOVA uses an F-test to determine if there is a significant difference between group means.
5. The F-test compares the variance between groups to the variance within groups.
6. If the F-test is significant, it means that there is evidence to suggest that at least one group mean is different from the others.
7. ANOVA can be used with both between-subjects and within-subjects designs.
8. ANOVA can also be used with more than two groups.
