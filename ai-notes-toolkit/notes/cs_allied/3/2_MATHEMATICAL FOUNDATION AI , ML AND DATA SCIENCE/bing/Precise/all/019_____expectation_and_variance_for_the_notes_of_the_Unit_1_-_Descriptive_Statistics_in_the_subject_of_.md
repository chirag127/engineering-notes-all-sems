# Expectation and Variance

Expectation and variance are two important concepts in descriptive statistics, which is a part of the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science.

## Expectation

- Expectation, also known as expected value, is a measure of the central tendency of a random variable.
- It is calculated by taking the weighted average of all possible values that the random variable can take, where the weights are the probabilities of the respective values.
- The formula for the expectation of a discrete random variable X is given by: E(X) = ∑[x * P(X=x)], where the sum is taken over all possible values of X.
- For a continuous random variable, the expectation is given by the integral of the product of the value and its probability density function.

## Variance

- Variance is a measure of the spread of a random variable.
- It is calculated by taking the average of the squared differences between each value of the random variable and its expected value.
- The formula for the variance of a random variable X is given by: Var(X) = E[(X - E(X))^2].
- The standard deviation is the square root of the variance and is another commonly used measure of spread.

These concepts are important for understanding and describing the behavior of random variables and are widely used in the fields of AI, ML and Data Science.
