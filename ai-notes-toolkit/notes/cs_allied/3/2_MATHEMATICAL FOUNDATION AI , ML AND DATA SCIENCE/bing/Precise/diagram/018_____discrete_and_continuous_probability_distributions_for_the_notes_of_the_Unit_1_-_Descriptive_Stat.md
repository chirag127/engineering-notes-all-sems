### Discrete and Continuous Probability Distributions

#### Unit 1 - Descriptive Statistics in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

- Probability distributions describe the likelihood of different outcomes in an experiment.
- There are two types of probability distributions: discrete and continuous.
- A discrete probability distribution is used to model a random variable that can take on a finite or countably infinite number of values.
- A continuous probability distribution is used to model a random variable that can take on any value within a continuous range.
- Examples of discrete probability distributions include the binomial distribution, the Poisson distribution, and the geometric distribution.
- Examples of continuous probability distributions include the normal distribution, the exponential distribution, and the uniform distribution.
- The choice of probability distribution depends on the nature of the data being analyzed and the research question being addressed.
- Probability distributions are used in a variety of applications, including statistical inference, hypothesis testing, and predictive modeling.
