### Length for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

1. Vector spaces are a fundamental concept in linear algebra.
2. A vector space is a set of vectors that can be added together and multiplied by scalars to produce new vectors.
3. The set of all linear combinations of a set of vectors is called the span of the set.
4. A set of vectors is linearly independent if no vector in the set can be written as a linear combination of the others.
5. The dimension of a vector space is the number of vectors in a basis for the space.
6. A subspace of a vector space is a subset that is closed under addition and scalar multiplication.
7. The intersection of two subspaces is also a subspace.
8. The sum of two subspaces is the smallest subspace containing both subspaces.
9. The direct sum of two subspaces is the set of all vectors that can be written uniquely as the sum of a vector from each subspace.
10. The rank of a matrix is the dimension of the column space of the matrix.
11. The nullity of a matrix is the dimension of the null space of the matrix.
12. The rank-nullity theorem states that the rank of a matrix plus the nullity of the matrix is equal to the number of columns of the matrix.
