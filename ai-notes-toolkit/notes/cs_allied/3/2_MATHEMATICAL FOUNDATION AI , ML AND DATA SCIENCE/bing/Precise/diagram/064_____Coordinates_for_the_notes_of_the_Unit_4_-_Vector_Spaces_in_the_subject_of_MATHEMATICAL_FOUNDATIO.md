### Unit 4 - Vector Spaces

#### Coordinates

1. A **coordinate system** is a method of assigning an n-tuple of numbers to each point in an n-dimensional space.
2. The **standard coordinate system** for a vector space is the system in which each vector is represented by its components with respect to the standard basis.
3. The **coordinates** of a vector with respect to a given basis are the coefficients of the linear combination of the basis vectors that equals the given vector.
4. The **change of coordinates** matrix is used to convert the coordinates of a vector from one basis to another.
5. The **dimension** of a vector space is the number of vectors in any basis for the space.
6. The **rank** of a matrix is the dimension of the column space of the matrix.
7. The **nullity** of a matrix is the dimension of the null space of the matrix.
