### Orthogonalization by Householder Transformations (QR)

Orthogonalization is the process of constructing a set of orthogonal vectors from a given set of vectors. One method of orthogonalization is the use of Householder transformations, also known as QR decomposition.

QR decomposition is a method of decomposing a matrix into the product of an orthogonal matrix Q and an upper triangular matrix R. This decomposition is useful in many applications, including solving linear systems, computing eigenvalues, and least squares regression.

The Householder transformation is an orthogonal transformation that can be used to zero out all but one element in a column of a matrix. This transformation is performed by constructing a Householder matrix, which is an orthogonal matrix that reflects a vector about a hyperplane.

The process of orthogonalization by Householder transformations involves applying a series of Householder transformations to a given matrix to transform it into an upper triangular matrix. This upper triangular matrix is the R matrix in the QR decomposition, and the product of the Householder matrices used in the process is the Q matrix.
