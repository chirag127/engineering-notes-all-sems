### Markov Chain Monte Carlo (McMC)

Markov Chain Monte Carlo (McMC) is a method for generating samples from a probability distribution. It is used in the field of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science for generating pseudo-random numbers. Here are some key points to note about McMC:

1. McMC is a class of algorithms that use Markov Chains to generate samples from a probability distribution.
2. The Markov Chain is constructed in such a way that its stationary distribution is the target distribution that we want to sample from.
3. McMC algorithms are iterative, meaning that they generate samples by moving from one state to another in the Markov Chain.
4. The most common McMC algorithms are the Metropolis-Hastings algorithm and the Gibbs sampler.
5. McMC is used in a wide range of applications, including Bayesian statistics, statistical physics, and machine learning.
6. One of the main advantages of McMC is its ability to sample from complex, high-dimensional distributions.
