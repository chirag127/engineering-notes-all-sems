### Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors

Eigenvalues and eigenvectors are important concepts in the study of linear transformations in the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML, and Data Science. They are used to understand the behavior of linear transformations and to find solutions to systems of linear equations.

- **Eigenvalues**: An eigenvalue is a scalar that is associated with a linear transformation of a vector space. It is a value that, when a given matrix is multiplied by a vector, results in a scalar multiple of that vector. In other words, if `A` is a matrix and `v` is a vector, then `λ` is an eigenvalue of `A` if `Av = λv`.

- **Eigenvectors**: An eigenvector is a nonzero vector that, when multiplied by a given matrix, results in a scalar multiple of itself. In other words, if `A` is a matrix and `v` is a vector, then `v` is an eigenvector of `A` if `Av = λv` for some scalar `λ`.

- **Finding Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors**: To find the eigenvalues of a matrix, one can use the characteristic polynomial of the matrix. The characteristic polynomial is defined as the determinant of the matrix `A - λI`, where `I` is the identity matrix. The roots of the characteristic polynomial are the eigenvalues of the matrix. Once the eigenvalues are found, the eigenvectors can be found by solving the equation `Av = λv` for each eigenvalue `λ`.

- **Applications**: Eigenvalues and eigenvectors have many applications in the field of AI, ML, and Data Science. They are used in principal component analysis, which is a technique for reducing the dimensionality of data. They are also used in the PageRank algorithm, which is used by search engines to rank web pages.

In summary, eigenvalues and eigenvectors are important concepts in the study of linear transformations and have many applications in the field of AI, ML, and Data Science. They provide insight into the behavior of linear transformations and can be used to solve systems of linear equations.
