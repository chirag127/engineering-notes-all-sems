## Transformations

1. Transformations are mathematical functions that map one set of values to another set of values.
2. In the context of pseudo-random numbers, transformations are used to generate random numbers from a given distribution.
3. Common transformations used in pseudo-random number generation include the Box-Muller transformation and the inverse transform method.
4. The Box-Muller transformation is used to generate normally distributed random numbers from uniformly distributed random numbers.
5. The inverse transform method is used to generate random numbers from any continuous distribution by using its inverse cumulative distribution function.
6. Transformations are an important tool in the generation of pseudo-random numbers and are widely used in AI, ML, and data science applications.
