### Random Number Generation

Random number generation is a process of generating a sequence of numbers that cannot be predicted better than by a random chance. It is a crucial component in many applications, including cryptography, statistical sampling, and computer simulations.

#### Pseudo-Random Numbers

Pseudo-random numbers are numbers that are generated using deterministic algorithms, but appear to be random. These algorithms start with a seed value and generate a sequence of numbers that have statistical properties similar to truly random numbers.

#### Applications of Pseudo-Random Numbers

Pseudo-random numbers have many applications in computer science, including:

1. Cryptography: Pseudo-random numbers are used to generate keys for encryption and decryption.
2. Statistical Sampling: Pseudo-random numbers are used to select a representative sample from a population.
3. Computer Simulations: Pseudo-random numbers are used to simulate random events in computer models.
