### Definition of Eigenvalue and Eigenvector

In the context of Unit 5 - Linear Transformations in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE, the definition of Eigenvalue and Eigenvector is as follows:

- An **eigenvalue** is a scalar value that, when multiplied by a given vector, results in a linear transformation of that vector that is equivalent to the original vector being scaled by the same scalar value.

- An **eigenvector** is a non-zero vector that, when multiplied by a given square matrix, results in a linear transformation of that vector that is equivalent to the original vector being scaled by a scalar value, known as the eigenvalue.

In other words, if `A` is a square matrix, `v` is an eigenvector of `A` and `λ` is the corresponding eigenvalue, then the following equation holds true:

```
Av = λv
```

where `Av` represents the linear transformation of the vector `v` by the matrix `A`, and `λv` represents the scaling of the vector `v` by the scalar value `λ`.

Eigenvalues and eigenvectors have important applications in many fields, including AI, ML, and data science. They are used to analyze and understand the properties of linear transformations, and can provide valuable insights into the behavior of complex systems.
