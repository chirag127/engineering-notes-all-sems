### Triangle Inequality

The Triangle Inequality is a fundamental concept in the study of Vector Spaces, which is covered in Unit 4 of the subject Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science. Here are some key points to remember about the Triangle Inequality:

1. The Triangle Inequality states that for any vectors u and v, the magnitude of their sum is less than or equal to the sum of their magnitudes. In other words, ||u + v|| ≤ ||u|| + ||v||.
2. This inequality can be derived geometrically by considering the triangle formed by the vectors u, v, and u + v.
3. The Triangle Inequality can also be proven algebraically using the Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality.
4. The Triangle Inequality is useful in many applications, including the study of metric spaces and the convergence of sequences in normed vector spaces.
5. The Triangle Inequality can be generalized to hold for any number of vectors, not just two.

These are some of the key points to remember about the Triangle Inequality. It is an important concept to understand when studying Vector Spaces in the subject of Mathematical Foundation AI, ML and Data Science.
