## Unit 3 - Pseudo-Random Numbers

1. Pseudo-random numbers are numbers that appear to be random but are generated by a deterministic algorithm.
2. These numbers are not truly random, but they can be used in many applications where true randomness is not required.
3. Pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs) are algorithms that generate a sequence of numbers that are statistically random.
4. PRNGs are initialized with a seed value, which determines the sequence of numbers that will be generated.
5. The same seed value will always produce the same sequence of numbers.
6. PRNGs are used in many applications, including computer simulations, cryptography, and gaming.
7. There are many different PRNG algorithms, each with its own strengths and weaknesses.
8. It is important to choose the right PRNG algorithm for the specific application.
9. Some common PRNG algorithms include the linear congruential generator, the Mersenne Twister, and the Blum Blum Shub algorithm.
10. PRNGs can be tested for randomness using statistical tests, such as the chi-squared test and the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test.
