### Estimation and Hypothesis Testing

Estimation and hypothesis testing are two important concepts in inferential statistics, which is a branch of statistics that deals with making inferences about a population based on a sample of data.

1. **Estimation** involves using sample data to estimate a population parameter, such as the mean or proportion. There are two types of estimation: point estimation and interval estimation.
    - Point estimation involves calculating a single value that is the best estimate of the population parameter.
    - Interval estimation involves calculating an interval within which the population parameter is likely to fall, with a certain level of confidence.

2. **Hypothesis testing** is a method for testing a claim or hypothesis about a population parameter, such as the mean or proportion, based on a sample of data. The basic steps involved in hypothesis testing are:
    - Formulate the null and alternative hypotheses.
    - Choose an appropriate test statistic and significance level.
    - Calculate the test statistic and p-value.
    - Make a decision based on the p-value and significance level.

These concepts are important for making inferences about a population based on a sample of data, and are commonly used in many fields, including AI, ML, and data science. They provide a framework for making decisions and drawing conclusions based on data.
