### Unit 4 - Vector Spaces: Coordinates

1. A **coordinate system** is a way to assign a unique set of numbers to each point in a vector space.
2. The **standard basis** for a vector space is a set of vectors that can be used to represent any vector in the space as a linear combination of the basis vectors.
3. The **coordinates** of a vector with respect to a given basis are the coefficients of the linear combination that represents the vector in terms of the basis vectors.
4. The **change of basis** is the process of expressing a vector in terms of a new basis by finding the coordinates of the vector with respect to the new basis.
5. The **transition matrix** is a matrix that can be used to convert the coordinates of a vector from one basis to another.
6. The **dimension** of a vector space is the number of vectors in any basis for the space.
7. The **rank** of a matrix is the dimension of the column space of the matrix.
8. The **nullity** of a matrix is the dimension of the null space of the matrix.
