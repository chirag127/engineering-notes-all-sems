### Dimension for the notes of the Unit 4 - Vector Spaces in the subject of MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATION AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

- Dimension is a fundamental concept in vector spaces.
- It refers to the number of vectors in a basis for the vector space.
- A basis is a set of linearly independent vectors that span the vector space.
- The dimension of a vector space is unique and well-defined.
- The dimension of a vector space can be finite or infinite.
- The dimension of a subspace is always less than or equal to the dimension of the vector space it is contained in.
- The rank of a matrix is equal to the dimension of the column space of the matrix.
- The nullity of a matrix is equal to the dimension of the null space of the matrix.
- The rank-nullity theorem states that the rank of a matrix plus the nullity of the matrix is equal to the number of columns of the matrix.
- The dimension of a vector space can be used to determine the number of solutions to a system of linear equations.
