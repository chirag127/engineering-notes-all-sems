### Change of Basis

- A basis of a vector space is a set of linearly independent vectors that spans the space.
- Given a basis for a vector space, any vector in the space can be written as a unique linear combination of the basis vectors.
- A change of basis is the process of expressing a vector in terms of a different basis.
- To change the basis of a vector, we need to find the coordinates of the vector with respect to the new basis.
- This can be done by solving a system of linear equations or by using matrix algebra.
- The matrix that transforms the coordinates of a vector from one basis to another is called the change of basis matrix.
- The change of basis matrix is invertible, and its inverse matrix can be used to transform the coordinates of a vector from the new basis back to the original basis.
- Change of basis is useful in many applications, including diagonalization of matrices and simplification of linear transformations.
