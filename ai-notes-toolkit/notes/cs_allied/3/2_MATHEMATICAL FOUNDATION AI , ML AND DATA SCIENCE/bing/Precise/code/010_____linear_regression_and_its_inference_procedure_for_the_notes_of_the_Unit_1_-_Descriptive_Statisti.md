### Linear Regression and its Inference Procedure

Linear regression is a statistical method used to model the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables. It is commonly used in predictive modeling and forecasting.

The inference procedure for linear regression involves the following steps:

1. **Model specification:** The first step is to specify the form of the model, which includes selecting the independent variables and the functional form of the relationship between the dependent and independent variables.

2. **Estimation:** The next step is to estimate the parameters of the model using a sample of data. This is typically done using the method of least squares, which minimizes the sum of squared residuals.

3. **Hypothesis testing:** Once the model has been estimated, hypothesis tests can be performed to determine the statistical significance of the estimated parameters. This involves testing whether the estimated coefficients are significantly different from zero.

4. **Model validation:** The final step is to validate the model by checking its assumptions and assessing its predictive performance. This can be done by examining the residuals and using measures such as the coefficient of determination (R-squared) and the mean squared error (MSE).
