- A vector space or a linear space is a set of objects called vectors, which can be added together and multiplied by numbers called scalars  .
- Scalars are usually real numbers, but can also be complex numbers or elements of any field .
- A field is a set of numbers that has two operations, usually called addition and multiplication, that satisfy certain properties.
- A vector space has two operations: vector addition and scalar multiplication, which must satisfy certain properties or axioms   .
- The properties or axioms of a vector space are:

  - Closure under vector addition: If u and v are vectors in V, then u + v is also in V.
  - Commutativity of vector addition: If u and v are vectors in V, then u + v = v + u.
  - Associativity of vector addition: If u, v and w are vectors in V, then (u + v) + w = u + (v + w).
  - Existence of additive identity: There exists a vector 0 in V such that 0 + v = v for any vector v in V.
  - Existence of additive inverse: For any vector v in V, there exists a vector -v in V such that v + (-v) = 0.
  - Closure under scalar multiplication: If c is a scalar and v is a vector in V, then c * v is also in V.
  - Distributivity of scalar multiplication over vector addition: If c is a scalar and u and v are vectors in V, then c * (u + v) = c * u + c * v.
  - Distributivity of scalar addition over scalar multiplication: If c and d are scalars and v is a vector in V, then (c + d) * v = c * v + d * v.
  - Associativity of scalar multiplication: If c and d are scalars and v is a vector in V, then c * (d * v) = (c * d) * v.
  - Existence of multiplicative identity: There exists a scalar 1 such that 1 * v = v for any vector v in V.

- A vector space is characterized by its dimension, which is the number of independent directions in the space.
- Two vector spaces with the same dimension have the same vector-space structure, meaning that they are isomorphic.
- Isomorphic means that there is a one-to-one and onto correspondence between the elements of the two vector spaces that preserves the operations of vector addition and scalar multiplication.
- A subspace of a vector space is a subset of the vector space that is also a vector space under the same operations .
- A subspace must contain the zero vector, be closed under vector addition and scalar multiplication, and inherit the properties or axioms of the vector space .
- A linear combination of vectors is a sum of scalar multiples of the vectors .
- A span of a set of vectors is the set of all linear combinations of the vectors .
- A basis of a vector space is a set of linearly independent vectors that span the vector space .
- Linearly independent means that none of the vectors can be written as a linear combination of the others .
- The dimension of a vector space is the number of vectors in any basis of the vector space .
- A coordinate system of a vector space is a way of assigning a unique ordered list of scalars, called coordinates, to each vector in the vector space, based on a chosen basis .
- A linear transformation from one vector space to another is a function that maps vectors to vectors, preserves vector addition and scalar multiplication, and is well-defined by its action on a basis .
