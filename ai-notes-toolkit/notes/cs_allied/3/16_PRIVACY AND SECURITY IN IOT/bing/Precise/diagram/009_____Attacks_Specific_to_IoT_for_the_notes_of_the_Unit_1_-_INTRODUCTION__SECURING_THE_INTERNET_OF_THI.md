### Attacks Specific to IoT

1. **Botnet Attacks:** IoT devices can be compromised and used as part of a botnet to launch Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks.
2. **Man-in-the-Middle Attacks:** IoT devices can be vulnerable to man-in-the-middle attacks, where an attacker intercepts and alters the communication between two devices.
3. **Physical Attacks:** IoT devices can be physically tampered with or stolen, allowing attackers to gain access to sensitive information or control of the device.
4. **Eavesdropping:** IoT devices can be vulnerable to eavesdropping, where attackers intercept and listen in on the communication between devices.
5. **Malware:** IoT devices can be infected with malware, allowing attackers to gain control of the device or steal sensitive information.
6. **Data Theft:** IoT devices can be vulnerable to data theft, where attackers steal sensitive information stored on the device or transmitted by the device.
7. **Device Hijacking:** IoT devices can be hijacked, allowing attackers to take control of the device and use it for malicious purposes.
8. **Identity Theft:** IoT devices can be used to steal the identity of the user, allowing attackers to impersonate the user and carry out fraudulent activities.
