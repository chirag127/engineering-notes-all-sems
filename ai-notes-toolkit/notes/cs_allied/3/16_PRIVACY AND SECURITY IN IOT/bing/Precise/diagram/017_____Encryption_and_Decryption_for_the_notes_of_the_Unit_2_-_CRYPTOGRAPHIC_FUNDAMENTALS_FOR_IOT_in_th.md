# Unit 2 - Cryptographic Fundamentals for IoT
## Encryption and Decryption

1. Encryption is the process of converting plaintext into ciphertext, which is unreadable without the proper key.
2. Decryption is the reverse process, where ciphertext is converted back into plaintext using the proper key.
3. Encryption and decryption are used to protect the confidentiality of data, especially when it is transmitted over an insecure network such as the internet.
4. There are two main types of encryption: symmetric and asymmetric.
5. Symmetric encryption uses the same key for both encryption and decryption. Examples of symmetric encryption algorithms include AES and DES.
6. Asymmetric encryption, also known as public key encryption, uses a pair of keys: one for encryption and one for decryption. The encryption key is made public, while the decryption key is kept private. Examples of asymmetric encryption algorithms include RSA and ECC.
7. In the context of IoT, encryption and decryption can be used to protect the data transmitted between devices and the cloud, as well as the data stored on the devices themselves.
8. It is important to choose strong encryption algorithms and properly manage the keys to ensure the security of the data.
