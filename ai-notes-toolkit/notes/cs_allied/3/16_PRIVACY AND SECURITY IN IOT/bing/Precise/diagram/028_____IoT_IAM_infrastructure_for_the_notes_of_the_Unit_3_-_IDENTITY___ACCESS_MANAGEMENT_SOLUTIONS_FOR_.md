### IoT IAM infrastructure

Identity and Access Management (IAM) is an important aspect of IoT security. IAM is focused on identifying people and managing access to different data types, such as sensitive data, non-sensitive data, or device data .

One of the key components of IoT IAM infrastructure is Public Key Infrastructure (PKI). PKI is used to secure IAM deployments for the IoT  . PKI provides a secure way to authenticate and authorize users and devices, and to manage access to data and resources.

Some IoT platforms, such as AWS IoT, provide their own IAM solutions. AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) is an AWS service that helps an administrator securely control access to AWS resources. IAM administrators control who can be authenticated (signed in) and authorized (have permissions) to use AWS IoT resources . AWS IoT also supports identity-based policies, which allow administrators to specify allowed or denied actions and resources, as well as the conditions under which actions are allowed or denied .

In summary, IoT IAM infrastructure is an essential component of IoT security, providing a way to authenticate and authorize users and devices, and to manage access to data and resources. Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) is a key component of IoT IAM infrastructure, and some IoT platforms provide their own IAM solutions.
