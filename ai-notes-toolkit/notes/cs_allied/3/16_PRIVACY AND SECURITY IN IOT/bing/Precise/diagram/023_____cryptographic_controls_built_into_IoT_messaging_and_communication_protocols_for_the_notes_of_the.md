### Cryptographic Controls Built into IoT Messaging and Communication Protocols

1. **Transport Layer Security (TLS)**: TLS is a cryptographic protocol that provides secure communication over a network. It is commonly used in IoT messaging and communication to provide confidentiality, integrity, and authentication.

2. **Advanced Encryption Standard (AES)**: AES is a symmetric encryption algorithm used to encrypt data in transit and at rest. It is commonly used in IoT messaging and communication to provide confidentiality.

3. **Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC)**: ECC is an asymmetric encryption algorithm used to provide secure key exchange and digital signatures. It is commonly used in IoT messaging and communication to provide authentication and non-repudiation.

4. **Message Authentication Code (MAC)**: MAC is a cryptographic function used to verify the integrity and authenticity of a message. It is commonly used in IoT messaging and communication to provide integrity and authentication.

5. **Key Management**: Key management refers to the secure generation, storage, and distribution of cryptographic keys. It is an essential component of IoT messaging and communication to ensure the security of cryptographic operations.

These are some of the cryptographic controls built into IoT messaging and communication protocols. They provide a range of security services, including confidentiality, integrity, authentication, and non-repudiation, to ensure the privacy and security of IoT devices and their data.
