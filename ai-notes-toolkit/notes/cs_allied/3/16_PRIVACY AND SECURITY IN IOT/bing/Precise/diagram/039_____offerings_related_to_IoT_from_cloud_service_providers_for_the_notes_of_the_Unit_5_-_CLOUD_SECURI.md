### Offerings related to IoT from cloud service providers

Cloud service providers offer a range of services and platforms for the Internet of Things (IoT). These services enable easy connectivity for devices, real-time data analysis, endpoint management, high-speed messaging, and more. Here are some of the top cloud platforms for IoT:

1. **Thingworx 8 IoT Platform**: Thingworx is one of the leading IoT platforms for industrial companies, which provides easy connectivity for devices.
2. **Microsoft Azure IoT Suite**: Microsoft Azure provides multiple services to create IoT solutions. It enhances your IoT solution with features such as security, analytics, and device management.
3. **Google Cloud’s IoT Platform**: Google Cloud offers a fully managed IoT platform that enables you to securely connect, manage, and ingest data from globally dispersed devices.
4. **IBM Watson IoT Platform**: IBM Watson IoT Platform is a fully managed, cloud-hosted service that makes it simple to derive value from your IoT devices.
5. **AWS IoT Platform**: AWS IoT provides a range of services that enable you to easily and securely connect and manage billions of devices, process, analyze and act on the data they generate, and create applications that interact with the physical world.
6. **Cisco IoT Cloud Connect**: Cisco IoT Cloud Connect is a mobility cloud-based software suite that offers a complete set of services to enable the rapid deployment of IoT services.
7. **Salesforce IoT Cloud**: Salesforce IoT Cloud is a platform that empowers businesses to connect data from the Internet of Things (IoT) with customer data to create more engaging customer experiences.
8. **Kaa IoT Platform**: Kaa is an enterprise-grade IoT platform for device management, data collection, analytics, and visualization, remote control, software updates, and more.
9. **Oracle Integrated Cloud for IoT**: Oracle offers real-time Internet of Things data analysis, endpoint management, and high-speed messaging where the user can get real-time notification directly on their devices. Oracle IoT cloud service is a Platform as a Service (PaaS), cloud-based offering that helps you to make critical business decisions.

These are just some of the many offerings related to IoT from cloud service providers. Each platform has its own unique features and capabilities, so it is important to carefully evaluate your needs and requirements before selecting a platform for your IoT solution.
