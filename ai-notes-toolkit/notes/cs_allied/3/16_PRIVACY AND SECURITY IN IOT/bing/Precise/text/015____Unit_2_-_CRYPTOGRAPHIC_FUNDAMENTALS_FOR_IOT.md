## Unit 2 - Cryptographic Fundamentals for IoT

1. **Introduction to Cryptography:** Cryptography is the practice of secure communication in the presence of third parties. It involves techniques for secure storage and transmission of data.

2. **Symmetric Encryption:** Symmetric encryption uses the same key for both encryption and decryption. It is fast and efficient for large amounts of data.

3. **Asymmetric Encryption:** Asymmetric encryption uses a pair of keys, one for encryption and one for decryption. It is slower than symmetric encryption but provides better security.

4. **Hash Functions:** A hash function takes an input and produces a fixed-size output. It is used for verifying the integrity of data.

5. **Digital Signatures:** A digital signature is a mathematical scheme for verifying the authenticity of digital messages or documents.

6. **Key Management:** Key management involves the generation, storage, distribution, and revocation of cryptographic keys.

7. **Cryptographic Protocols:** Cryptographic protocols are used to secure communication over a network. Examples include SSL/TLS and SSH.

8. **IoT Security:** IoT devices often have limited resources and require lightweight cryptographic algorithms. Security measures must be implemented to protect against threats such as data breaches and unauthorized access.

9. **Conclusion:** Cryptography is an essential component of IoT security. It provides mechanisms for secure communication, data integrity, and authentication. Proper key management and the use of appropriate cryptographic protocols are crucial for ensuring the security of IoT devices.
