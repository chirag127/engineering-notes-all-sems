### Authorization with Publish / Subscribe schemes

#### Unit 3 - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR IOT

#### PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

- Publish/Subscribe (Pub/Sub) is a messaging pattern where publishers send messages to topics, and subscribers receive messages based on the topics they have subscribed to.
- In the context of IoT, Pub/Sub can be used to facilitate communication between devices and applications.
- Authorization is the process of determining whether a user or device has the right to access a resource or perform an action.
- In a Pub/Sub scheme, authorization can be implemented by controlling access to topics.
- For example, a device may be authorized to publish messages to a specific topic, while an application may be authorized to subscribe to that topic and receive messages.
- This can be achieved through the use of access control lists (ACLs) or role-based access control (RBAC).
- ACLs define the permissions for specific users or devices, while RBAC assigns permissions based on roles.
- In both cases, the goal is to ensure that only authorized parties can publish or subscribe to a given topic.
- This helps to protect the confidentiality and integrity of the data being transmitted, and can also help to prevent unauthorized access or control of IoT devices.
