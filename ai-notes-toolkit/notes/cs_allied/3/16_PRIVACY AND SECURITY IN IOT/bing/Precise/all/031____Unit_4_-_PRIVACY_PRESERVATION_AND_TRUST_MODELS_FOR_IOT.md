## Unit 4 - PRIVACY PRESERVATION AND TRUST MODELS FOR IOT

1. **Introduction:** With the increasing use of IoT devices, privacy preservation and trust models have become important topics of discussion. These devices collect and transmit large amounts of data, which can be sensitive in nature. It is important to ensure that this data is protected and used in a trustworthy manner.

2. **Privacy Preservation:** Privacy preservation in IoT refers to the protection of personal data collected by IoT devices. This can be achieved through various methods such as data encryption, access control, and data minimization.

3. **Trust Models:** Trust models are used to establish trust between different entities in an IoT system. These models can be based on various factors such as past behavior, reputation, and recommendations from other trusted entities.

4. **Challenges:** There are several challenges associated with privacy preservation and trust models in IoT. These include the large number of devices, the heterogeneity of these devices, and the need for real-time processing of data.

5. **Conclusion:** Privacy preservation and trust models are important aspects of IoT systems. They help to ensure that data is protected and used in a trustworthy manner. However, there are several challenges that need to be addressed in order to effectively implement these concepts in IoT systems.
