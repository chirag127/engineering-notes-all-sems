# IoT Node Authentication

IoT Node Authentication is an important means to ensure the security of IoT devices. It is essential to distinguish the identity types of IoT nodes located in different positions and authenticate the identity of unknown nodes when they break into the pre-set area .

- **Physical Layer Authentication Mechanism**: A physical layer authentication mechanism based on convolution neural network (CNN) can be used to realize the secure access authentication of IoT nodes and regional intrusion detection with low resource consumption. This mechanism uses the deep characteristics of channel state information (CSI) to identify sending nodes in different locations .

- **Hybrid Blockchain-Based Identity Authentication Scheme**: Traditional IoT identity authentication protocols usually rely on trusted third parties. However, many IoT environments do not allow such conditions, and are prone to single point failure. A hybrid blockchain-based identity authentication scheme can be used for multi-WSN environments to ensure the security of IoT nodes .

- **IoT Authentication and Authorization**: IoT authentication and authorization are essential components of cybersecurity, whether consumers implement them on smart home devices or an enterprise on hundreds of IoT devices that track and monitor large-scale workflows and resources. At their core, IoT devices simply connect to share data .

- **IoT Device Authentication Methods**: IoT device authentication can secure networks by ensuring that devices only have access and permission to do exactly what they need. Without one standard authentication process, IT admins must consider the type of IoT device, its location and what data it transmits to pick the right method .
