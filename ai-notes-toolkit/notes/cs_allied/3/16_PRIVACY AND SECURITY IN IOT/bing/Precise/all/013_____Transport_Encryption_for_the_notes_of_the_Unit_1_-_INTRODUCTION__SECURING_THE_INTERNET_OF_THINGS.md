### Transport Encryption

Transport encryption is a method of protecting data that is transmitted over a network, such as the internet. It is used to ensure that the data remains confidential and cannot be intercepted or tampered with by unauthorized parties.

Here are some key points to consider when studying transport encryption for the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT:

1. Transport encryption is used to secure data in transit, meaning that it is protected while it is being transmitted from one device to another.
2. Common protocols used for transport encryption include SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) and TLS (Transport Layer Security). These protocols use cryptographic algorithms to encrypt the data being transmitted.
3. Transport encryption is important for protecting sensitive information, such as financial data, personal information, and login credentials, from being intercepted by attackers.
4. Transport encryption can also help to prevent man-in-the-middle attacks, where an attacker intercepts and alters the data being transmitted between two parties.
5. It is important to use strong encryption algorithms and to keep the encryption software up to date to ensure that the data remains secure.
