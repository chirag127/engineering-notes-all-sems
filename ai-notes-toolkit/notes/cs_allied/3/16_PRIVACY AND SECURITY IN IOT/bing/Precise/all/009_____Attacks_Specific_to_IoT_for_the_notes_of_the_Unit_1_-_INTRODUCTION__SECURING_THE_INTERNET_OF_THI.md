# Attacks Specific to IoT

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and exchange data. Due to the nature of IoT devices, they are vulnerable to a variety of attacks. Here are some common attacks specific to IoT:

1. **Denial of Service (DoS):** This type of attack aims to disrupt the normal functioning of an IoT device or network by overwhelming it with traffic or requests.
2. **Malware:** Malware is a type of software designed to harm or exploit IoT devices. It can be used to gain unauthorized access, steal data, or disrupt the normal functioning of the device.
3. **Passive Wiretapping:** This type of attack involves intercepting the communication between IoT devices to gather sensitive information without being detected.
4. **Structured Query Language Injection (SQLi):** This type of attack targets IoT devices that use a database by injecting malicious code into the database server to gain unauthorized access or manipulate data.
5. **Wardriving:** This type of attack involves searching for Wi-Fi networks by a person in a moving vehicle to gain unauthorized access to IoT devices connected to the network.
6. **Zero-day exploits:** These are attacks that exploit previously unknown vulnerabilities in IoT devices or software before the vendor has had a chance to release a patch.
7. **Privilege escalation:** Attackers could exploit bugs, unpatched vulnerabilities, critical design problems, or even operating system oversights in an IoT device to obtain unauthorized access to the network.
8. **Botnets:** IoT devices are particularly vulnerable to malware because they don’t have the same security mechanisms as traditional computing devices. Attackers can use malware to create a network of compromised IoT devices, known as a botnet, to carry out large-scale attacks.
9. **Ransomware:** IoT devices can also be targeted by ransomware attacks, where attackers encrypt the data on the device and demand payment in exchange for the decryption key.

These are just some of the common attacks specific to IoT. It is important for organizations and individuals to take appropriate measures to secure their IoT devices and networks to prevent these types of attacks.
