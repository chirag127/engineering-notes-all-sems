### Cryptographic Controls Built into IoT Messaging and Communication Protocols

Cryptographic controls are essential for ensuring the security and privacy of data transmitted between IoT devices. These controls are built into IoT messaging and communication protocols to provide secure communication between devices. Some of the cryptographic controls built into IoT messaging and communication protocols include:

1. **Encryption:** Encryption is the process of converting plaintext data into ciphertext, which is unreadable without the proper decryption key. Encryption is used to protect the confidentiality of data transmitted between IoT devices.

2. **Authentication:** Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a device or user. This is important for ensuring that only authorized devices and users can access the data transmitted between IoT devices.

3. **Integrity:** Integrity controls are used to ensure that data transmitted between IoT devices has not been tampered with or altered in transit. This is typically achieved through the use of cryptographic hash functions, which generate a unique digital fingerprint of the data.

4. **Key Management:** Key management is the process of generating, storing, and distributing cryptographic keys. This is important for ensuring that the keys used for encryption, authentication, and integrity are properly managed and protected.

These are some of the cryptographic controls built into IoT messaging and communication protocols to provide secure communication between devices. It is important to understand and implement these controls to ensure the security and privacy of data transmitted between IoT devices.
