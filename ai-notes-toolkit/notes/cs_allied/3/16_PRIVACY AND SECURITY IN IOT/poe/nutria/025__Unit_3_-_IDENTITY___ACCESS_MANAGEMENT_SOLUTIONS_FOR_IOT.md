
## Unit 3 - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR IOT

1. Identity and Access Management (IAM) solutions are designed to help organizations securely manage user access to their networks and applications.
2. IAM solutions can provide authentication and authorization of users, as well as secure communications and data access.
3. IAM solutions can also help organizations ensure compliance with applicable regulations and standards.
4. IAM solutions can be deployed on-premise or in the cloud, depending on the organization's needs.
5. IAM solutions can be used to manage user access to IoT devices and applications, as well as user access to data generated by those devices.
6. IAM solutions can help organizations protect against unauthorized access to their networks and applications.
7. IAM solutions can also be used to monitor user activity and detect suspicious activity.
8. IAM solutions can help organizations ensure that users are authenticated and authorized to access the data and applications they need.
9. IAM solutions can help organizations ensure that users are not able to access data or applications they are not authorized to access.
10. IAM solutions can help organizations ensure that data is kept secure and that user access is managed in a secure and compliant manner.
