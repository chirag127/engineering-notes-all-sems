
### Security Requirements in IoT

1. Authentication: Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user or device before allowing access to a system or network. Authentication can be done using passwords, biometrics, or digital certificates.

2. Authorization: Authorization is the process of granting access to a user or device after authentication. Authorization is based on the user's identity and the resources they are allowed to access.

3. Encryption: Encryption is the process of encoding data so that it can only be read by the intended recipient. Encryption is used to protect data from unauthorized access and to ensure that data remains confidential.

4. Data Integrity: Data integrity is the assurance that data has not been modified or corrupted in any way. Data integrity is essential for ensuring that data is accurate and reliable.

5. Access Control: Access control is the process of restricting access to resources based on an individual's identity and the resources they are allowed to access. Access control is used to protect data from unauthorized access and to ensure that data is only accessed by authorized users.

6. Network Security: Network security is the process of protecting a network from unauthorized access and malicious activity. Network security is essential for protecting data from being accessed by unauthorized users.

7. Risk Management: Risk management is the process of identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks to a system or network. Risk management is essential for ensuring that data is secure and protected from unauthorized access.
