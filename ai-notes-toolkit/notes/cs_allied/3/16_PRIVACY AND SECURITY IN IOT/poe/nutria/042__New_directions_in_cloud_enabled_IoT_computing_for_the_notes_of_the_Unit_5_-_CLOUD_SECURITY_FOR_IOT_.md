
### New Directions in Cloud Enabled IoT Computing

1. Cloud computing is a rapidly growing technology that enables the storage, processing, and analysis of large datasets in a distributed, virtualized environment.
2. This technology has enabled the development of a new generation of Internet of Things (IoT) applications, which are characterized by their ability to collect, store, and analyze data from connected devices in real-time.
3. Cloud-enabled IoT applications have the potential to revolutionize many industries, including healthcare, transportation, and home automation.
4. However, cloud-enabled IoT applications also pose significant security risks, as they require the transmission of sensitive data across multiple networks.
5. To ensure the security of cloud-enabled IoT applications, organizations must implement robust security measures, including authentication, authorization, encryption, and access control.
6. Additionally, organizations should monitor their cloud-enabled IoT applications for any suspicious activity and respond quickly to any potential threats.
7. Finally, organizations should regularly audit their cloud-enabled IoT applications to ensure that their security measures remain up to date and effective.
