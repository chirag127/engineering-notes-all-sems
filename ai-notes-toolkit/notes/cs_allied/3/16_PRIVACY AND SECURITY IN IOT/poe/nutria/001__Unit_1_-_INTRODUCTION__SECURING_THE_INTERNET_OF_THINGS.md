
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as devices, sensors, and actuators, that are connected to the internet. 
2. IoT devices are increasingly being used to control and monitor physical systems, from home appliances to industrial processes. 
3. As the number of connected devices grows, so does the risk of malicious actors taking control of these devices and using them to launch cyber-attacks. 
4. To protect IoT devices from such attacks, it is important to use secure authentication protocols, encrypt data, and use secure communication protocols. 
5. Additionally, it is important to use secure software and hardware designs, as well as secure operating systems and applications. 
6. Finally, it is important to use secure networking protocols and architectures, such as virtual private networks (VPNs) and firewalls, to protect the network from malicious actors.
