
### Self-Organizing Things for the Notes of the Unit 4 - PRIVACY PRESERVATION AND TRUST MODELS FOR IOT in the Subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

1. Self-Organizing Things (SOTs) are networks of connected devices that use machine learning algorithms to self-organize and optimize their behavior.
2. SOTs can be used to improve the security and privacy of Internet of Things (IoT) devices by providing a secure and trustable communication layer between them.
3. SOTs use distributed ledger technology (DLT) to provide a secure, transparent and trustable communication layer between devices.
4. SOTs use cryptography to ensure secure communication between devices, as well as to protect the data stored on them.
5. SOTs use privacy-preserving protocols to ensure that data is kept private and secure.
6. SOTs use trust models to ensure that devices are trustworthy and that data is not tampered with.
7. SOTs use distributed consensus mechanisms to ensure that data is not corrupted or manipulated.
8. SOTs use distributed storage systems to ensure that data is stored securely and reliably.
9. SOTs use decentralized identity systems to ensure that devices are identified and authenticated.
10. SOTs use distributed computing systems to ensure that data is processed securely and efficiently.
