
### Cipher Suites for the Notes of Unit 2 - Cryptographic Fundamentals for IoT in Privacy and Security in IoT

- A cipher suite is a set of algorithms used to secure a network connection by encrypting data transmitted between two systems.
- It is typically composed of four algorithms: a key exchange algorithm, an authentication algorithm, a symmetric encryption algorithm, and a message authentication code (MAC) algorithm.
- The key exchange algorithm is used to securely exchange cryptographic keys between the two systems.
- The authentication algorithm is used to authenticate the identity of the two systems.
- The symmetric encryption algorithm is used to encrypt data transmitted between the two systems.
- The MAC algorithm is used to ensure the integrity of the data transmitted between the two systems.
- The most commonly used cipher suites are TLS 1.2 and TLS 1.3, which use the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) for symmetric encryption and the SHA-2 family of hash functions for the MAC algorithm.
