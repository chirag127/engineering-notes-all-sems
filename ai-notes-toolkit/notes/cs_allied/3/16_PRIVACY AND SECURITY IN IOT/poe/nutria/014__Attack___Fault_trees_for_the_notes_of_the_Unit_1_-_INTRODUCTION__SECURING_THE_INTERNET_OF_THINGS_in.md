
### Attack & Fault Trees

* Attack Trees: Attack trees are diagrams used to analyze potential threats to a system. They are used to identify possible attack vectors, and to identify countermeasures that can be used to protect the system. Attack trees are used to identify the most effective ways to protect a system by breaking down the attack into its component parts, and then analyzing each part to determine the most effective countermeasures.

* Fault Trees: Fault trees are diagrams used to analyze potential failures in a system. They are used to identify potential causes of failure, and to identify countermeasures that can be used to prevent or mitigate the failure. Fault trees are used to identify the most effective ways to protect a system by breaking down the failure into its component parts, and then analyzing each part to determine the most effective countermeasures.
