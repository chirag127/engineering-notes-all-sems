
### Cryptographic Primitives and their Role in IoT

1. Cryptographic primitives are fundamental algorithms used to secure data in communication systems. 
2. Cryptographic primitives are used in many applications such as authentication, encryption, digital signatures and hashing.
3. Cryptographic primitives are used to protect data in Internet of Things (IoT) systems by ensuring secure communication between IoT devices and preventing unauthorized access to data.
4. Cryptographic primitives are used to provide confidentiality, integrity and authentication of data in IoT systems.
5. Cryptographic primitives are used to ensure secure communication between IoT devices by encrypting data before sending it over the network.
6. Cryptographic primitives are used to authenticate IoT devices by verifying the identity of the sender of data.
7. Cryptographic primitives are used to ensure data integrity by verifying the integrity of data before it is sent over the network.
8. Cryptographic primitives are used to protect data from unauthorized access by using access control mechanisms such as authentication, authorization and encryption.
