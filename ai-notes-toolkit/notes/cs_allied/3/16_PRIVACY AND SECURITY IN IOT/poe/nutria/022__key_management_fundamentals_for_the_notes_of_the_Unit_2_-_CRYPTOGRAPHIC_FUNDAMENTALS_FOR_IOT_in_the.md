

### Key Management Fundamentals for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Cryptographic Fundamentals for IoT in the Subject of Privacy and Security in IoT

1. Key management is the process of generating, storing, protecting, and managing cryptographic keys.
2. Cryptographic keys are used to secure data and communications by encrypting and decrypting messages.
3. Key management involves creating, storing, and protecting cryptographic keys in a secure manner.
4. Key management also involves the secure distribution of keys to authorized users.
5. Key management systems should be designed to ensure that only authorized users have access to the keys.
6. Key management systems should also ensure that the keys are not compromised or exposed to unauthorized users.
7. Key management systems should also provide an audit trail to ensure that any changes to the keys are tracked and recorded.
8. Key management systems should also be designed to be resilient to attacks such as brute force attacks, dictionary attacks, and other types of attacks.
9. Key management systems should also be designed to be secure against physical attacks, such as theft or destruction of the keys.
10. Key management systems should also be designed to ensure that the keys are not lost or forgotten.
