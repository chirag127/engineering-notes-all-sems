
## Unit 4 - PRIVACY PRESERVATION AND TRUST MODELS FOR IOT

1. Privacy preservation is a process of protecting the privacy of individuals from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of their personal data. It is important for the Internet of Things (IoT) because it allows for the secure transmission of data from devices to other devices or to a central server.

2. Trust models for IoT provide the necessary security measures for authentication, authorization, and access control. These models are designed to ensure that only authorized users can access the data and that the data is securely transmitted.

3. Cryptography is an important tool for privacy preservation in IoT. It is used to encrypt data and ensure that only authorized users can decrypt it. Cryptography also helps to ensure the integrity of data and prevent data tampering.

4. Data anonymization is another method used to protect the privacy of individuals. It is a process of removing personal information from data sets to make them anonymous. This allows data to be used for research and analytics without compromising the privacy of individuals.

5. Privacy-preserving technologies such as differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, and secure multi-party computation are also important tools for preserving privacy in IoT. These technologies allow data to be securely shared without compromising the privacy of individuals.

6. Finally, trust models for IoT should also include measures for detecting and responding to security threats. This includes the use of intrusion detection systems, firewalls, and other security measures. These measures help to ensure that data is securely transmitted and that unauthorized access is prevented.
