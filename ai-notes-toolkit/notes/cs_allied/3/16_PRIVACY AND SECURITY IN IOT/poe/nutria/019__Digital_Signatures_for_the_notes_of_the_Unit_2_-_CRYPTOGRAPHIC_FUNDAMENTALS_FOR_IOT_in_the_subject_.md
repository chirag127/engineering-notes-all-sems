
### Digital Signatures for the notes of the Unit 2 - CRYPTOGRAPHIC FUNDAMENTALS FOR IOT in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

- A digital signature is a cryptographic technique used to authenticate the identity of a sender.
- It is used to ensure that a message or document is genuine and has not been modified in transit.
- Digital signatures are based on public-key cryptography, which uses two keys: a public key and a private key.
- The public key is used to encrypt data and verify the digital signature, while the private key is used to decrypt the data and sign documents.
- Digital signatures provide a way to verify the authenticity and integrity of a message or document.
- They are also used to prove non-repudiation, meaning that the sender of the message cannot deny that they sent it.
- Digital signatures are an important part of security in the Internet of Things (IoT), as they can help to ensure that data is transmitted securely and reliably.
