### Random number generation for the notes of the Unit 2 - CRYPTOGRAPHIC FUNDAMENTALS FOR IOT in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

Random number generation is a crucial aspect of cryptography as it helps in creating secure communication between two parties. Here are some points related to random number generation for the notes of Unit 2 - Cryptographic Fundamentals for IoT in the subject of Privacy and Security in IoT:

- Random number generation is used in cryptographic protocols to create keys, nonces, and initialization vectors.
- The security of cryptographic protocols depends on the quality of the random numbers generated. If the random numbers are predictable, then the protocol can be easily attacked.
- Pseudo-random number generators (PRNGs) are used to generate random numbers in software. They use a deterministic algorithm and a seed to generate a sequence of seemingly random numbers.
- Hardware random number generators (HRNGs) generate random numbers using physical processes such as thermal noise, radioactive decay, or atmospheric noise. They are considered more secure than PRNGs as they are not based on a deterministic algorithm.
- A common method for generating random numbers is to use a combination of a PRNG and an HRNG. The PRNG is used to generate the seed for the HRNG, and the HRNG then generates the random numbers.
- The quality of the random numbers generated by an HRNG can be evaluated using statistical tests such as the NIST Statistical Test Suite.
- It is important to ensure that the random numbers generated are not biased towards certain values or patterns. This can be achieved by using a good quality HRNG and by performing statistical tests on the generated numbers.
- In IoT systems, where resources are limited, it is important to use efficient algorithms for random number generation. This can be achieved by using lightweight cryptographic algorithms such as the Trivium stream cipher or the Grain family of ciphers.
- In conclusion, random number generation is a critical aspect of cryptography, and the quality of the generated numbers determines the security of the cryptographic protocols. It is important to use a combination of PRNGs and HRNGs to generate random numbers and to perform statistical tests to ensure their quality. In IoT systems, efficient algorithms should be used to ensure optimal use of resources.
