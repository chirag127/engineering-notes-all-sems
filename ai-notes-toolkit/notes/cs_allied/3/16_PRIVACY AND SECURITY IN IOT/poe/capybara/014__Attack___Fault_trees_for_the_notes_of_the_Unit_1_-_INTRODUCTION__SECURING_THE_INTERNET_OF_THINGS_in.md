### Attack & Fault Trees

The following are the key points to keep in mind when studying Attack & Fault Trees for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT:

- Attack trees are diagrams that show how an attacker could exploit vulnerabilities in a system to achieve their objectives. They are used to identify potential attack vectors and to prioritize security measures.
- Fault trees are diagrams that show how a system could fail due to a combination of component failures. They are used to identify potential points of failure and to prioritize reliability measures.
- Both attack and fault trees are useful tools for analyzing the security and reliability of IoT systems.
- When creating an attack or fault tree, it is important to consider all possible attack scenarios or failure modes, and to identify the critical components or vulnerabilities that would allow an attacker to succeed or a failure to occur.
- Once the critical components or vulnerabilities have been identified, appropriate security or reliability measures can be implemented to mitigate the risks.
- Attack and fault trees are living documents and should be updated regularly to reflect changes in the system, such as new vulnerabilities, components or functionality.
- A well-designed attack or fault tree can help to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities before they are exploited, and can help to prioritize security and reliability measures to protect against them.
