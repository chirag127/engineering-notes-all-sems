## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS

In this unit, we will be discussing the importance of securing the Internet of Things (IoT). Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- The IoT is a network of physical devices, vehicles, and other objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and network connectivity. This allows them to collect and exchange data.
- The IoT has the potential to revolutionize many industries, from healthcare to transportation. However, it also poses significant security risks.
- The more devices that are connected to the internet, the more opportunities there are for hackers to gain access to sensitive information or cause harm.
- Some common security threats to the IoT include: unauthorized access, data breaches, malware, and denial-of-service attacks.
- Securing the IoT requires a multi-layered approach. This includes: encryption, authentication, access control, and regular software updates.
- As the IoT continues to grow, it is important for individuals and organizations to prioritize security in their devices and networks. This will help to prevent potential breaches and ensure the safety of both individuals and businesses.

Overall, securing the IoT is a complex and ongoing process. However, with the right tools and strategies in place, it is possible to mitigate risks and protect against potential threats.
