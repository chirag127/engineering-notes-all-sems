## Unit 4 - PRIVACY PRESERVATION AND TRUST MODELS FOR IOT

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a rapidly growing network of interconnected devices that can collect and transmit data in real-time. While this connectivity brings significant benefits to our daily lives, it also raises concerns around privacy and security. In this unit, we will explore the concept of privacy preservation and trust models for IoT, and how they can be used to address these concerns.

### Privacy Preservation in IoT

Privacy preservation is an essential aspect of IoT, as it involves the protection of personal data collected by IoT devices. The following are some of the methods used for privacy preservation in IoT:

- Data anonymization: This involves removing any personally identifiable information (PII) from the collected data. By doing so, it becomes difficult to identify an individual from the data.

- Data encryption: This involves converting the data into a code that can only be decrypted by someone with the appropriate key. This helps to protect the data from unauthorized access.

- Access control: This involves limiting access to the data collected by IoT devices to authorized individuals or systems. This helps to prevent unauthorized access to the data.

### Trust Models for IoT

Trust models are used to establish trust between different entities in IoT networks. The following are some of the trust models used in IoT:

- Direct trust: This involves establishing trust between two entities based on their direct interactions. For example, if a user interacts with an IoT device and has a positive experience, they are more likely to trust the device in the future.

- Indirect trust: This involves establishing trust between two entities based on the trust relationships they have with other entities. For example, if two devices are both trusted by a third device, they are more likely to trust each other.

- Reputation-based trust: This involves establishing trust based on the reputation of an entity. For example, if a device has a good reputation for security and privacy, it is more likely to be trusted by other entities.

### Conclusion

Privacy preservation and trust models are crucial for ensuring the security and privacy of IoT networks. By implementing these methods, we can protect the personal data collected by IoT devices and establish trust between different entities in the network. As the use of IoT continues to grow, it is essential to prioritize privacy and security to ensure the safe and responsible use of this technology.
