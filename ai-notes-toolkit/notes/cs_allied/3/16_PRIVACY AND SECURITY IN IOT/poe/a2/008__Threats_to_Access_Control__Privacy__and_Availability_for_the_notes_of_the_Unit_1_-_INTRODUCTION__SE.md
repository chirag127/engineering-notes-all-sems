### Threats to Access Control, Privacy, and Availability

- Access control: IoT devices may have weak or default passwords, allowing unauthorized access. Malware can also be installed to gain access.
- Privacy: Data from IoT devices can be monitored and tracked, violating privacy. Personal information can be inferred from data and activities tracked by IoT devices.
- Availability: IoT botnets can conduct DDoS attacks using thousands of compromised IoT devices, disrupting networks and services. Ransomware can also lock users out of their IoT devices until a ransom is paid.
- Additional threats: Physical tampering, data manipulation, and software vulnerabilities also pose risks to IoT security and trustworthiness.
