### Lightweight and robust schemes for Privacy protection for the notes of the Unit 4 - PRIVACY PRESERVATION AND TRUST MODELS FOR IOT

1. Homomorphic Encryption: It is a form of encryption that allows computations to be carried out on ciphertext and generate an encrypted result which, when decrypted, matches the result of the operations as if they had been performed on the plaintext. This enables processing of data without decrypting it, thereby maintaining privacy.

2. Secure Multiparty Computation: It is a method by which multiple parties can jointly compute a function over their private inputs without revealing any private information other than the output of the function. This allows collaborating parties to obtain the output of a computation on their combined private data without revealing their individual inputs.

3. Differential Privacy: It is a framework that ensures privacy-preserving data analysis by adding random noise to aggregate statistics from a database. It limits the ability to infer the presence or absence of individuals within a dataset by restricting the amount of information that can be learned about any individual from the aggregate statistics. It allows useful computations to be performed on the data while preserving privacy.

4. Zero-Knowledge Proofs: It is an interactive method by which a party (the prover) can convince another party (the verifier) that a statement is true without revealing any other information. The verifier will be convinced of the prover's knowledge of a secret without learning anything apart from the validity of the statement. This enables verification of credentials and proofs without compromising privacy.
