## Unit 4 - PRIVACY PRESERVATION AND TRUST MODELS FOR IOT

1. Privacy Preservation in IoT:
- Data anonymization - removing personal identifiers from data
- Data masking - altering sensitive data to preserve privacy
- Encryption - using keys to convert data to unreadable form
- Access control - restricting access to data based on privileges
- Policy enforcement - implementing and monitoring compliance to privacy policies

2. Trust Models in IoT:
- Direct trust - devices trust each other if they are in the same domain
- Third-party trust - external authorities like certification agencies provide trust
- Reputation systems - devices get trust ratings based on their past interactions
- Blockchain - a distributed ledger to track and verify interactions between devices
- Machine learning - identifying and isolating malicious or compromised devices

3. Challenges in IoT Privacy and Trust:
- Heterogeneity of devices and networks
- Scalability to billions of devices and transactions
- Constraints on device resources like power, storage and computing capabilities
- Vulnerabilities that can be exploited to gain access to data and resources
- Lack of governance and standardization across IoT ecosystems
