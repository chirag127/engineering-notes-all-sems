### Insecure Access Control for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: SECURING THE INTERNET OF THINGS in the subject of PRIVACY AND SECURITY IN IOT

- Many IoT devices have insecure default passwords and no mechanism to change passwords. This allows unauthorized access.
- Many IoT devices do not have access control mechanisms like authentication and authorization. This allows any device to access the IoT device and its data or functionality.
- If access control is present, it is often based on weak mechanisms like hardcoded keys or IDs which can be easily compromised.
- APIs provided by IoT devices may not have proper access control, allowing unauthorized data access or control of devices.
- IoT devices and their data may be accessible via the Internet without any access control, allowing for easy unauthorized access.
- Shared IoT networks may allow devices to access each other due to lack of proper network segmentation and access control. This can lead to compromised devices attacking other devices on the network.
