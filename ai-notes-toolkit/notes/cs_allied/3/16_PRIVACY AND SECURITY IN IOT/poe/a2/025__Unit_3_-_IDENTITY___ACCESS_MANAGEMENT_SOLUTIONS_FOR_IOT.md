## Unit 3 - IDENTITY & ACCESS MANAGEMENT SOLUTIONS FOR IOT

1. Introduction to IAM for IoT
- IoT devices have identities and require access control
- Traditional IAM solutions not suited for scale and heterogeneity of IoT
- Requirements for IoT IAM: Scalability, heterogeneity, constrained devices, automated onboarding and provisioning

2. IoT Device Identities
- Unique identifiers for IoT devices: IDs, serial numbers, MAC addresses, etc.
- Types of identities: Device identities, Gateway identities, Service identities
- Identity lifecycle management: Onboarding, authentication, authorization, attribute management, revocation

3. Authorization and Access Control
- Role-based access control (RBAC) and attribute-based access control (ABAC) for IoT
- OAuth 2.0/OpenID Connect for REST APIs and MQTT
- X.509 certificates for security and access control
- Single sign-on (SSO) and federated identity management

4. Emerging Standards and Solutions
- OCF, OIC, and IPSO Alliance standards for device interoperability and security
- ISO/IEC 27001 and other standards for IoT security and privacy
- Commercial IAM solutions from companies like Ping Identity, Forgerock, Azure Active Directory, etc.
- Open-source IAM software like Keycloak
