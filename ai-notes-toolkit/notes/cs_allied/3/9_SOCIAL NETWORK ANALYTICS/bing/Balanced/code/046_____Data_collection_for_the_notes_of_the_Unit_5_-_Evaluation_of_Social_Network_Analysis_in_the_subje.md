### Data collection for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis

- Data collection is a crucial phase of social network analysis, as it determines the quality and validity of the network data and the subsequent analysis.
- Data collection methods for social network analysis can be classified into two main types: socio-centric and ego-centric.
- Socio-centric data collection involves identifying and surveying all the members of a predefined network, such as a group, an organization, or a community. This type of data collection allows for a comprehensive and complete representation of the network structure and the relationships among its members.
- Ego-centric data collection involves surveying a sample of individuals and asking them to report on their personal networks, such as their friends, family, or colleagues. This type of data collection allows for a flexible and diverse representation of the network structure and the relationships among different types of actors.
- The choice of data collection method depends on the research question, the network boundary, the availability of resources, and the ethical considerations.
- The most common data collection technique for social network analysis is web-based surveys, as they allow for customized and interactive questions, easy data entry and analysis, and low cost and time.
- Other data collection techniques include interviews, observations, archival records, tracking devices, and online data mining, each with their own advantages and limitations.
- Data collection for social network analysis should follow the principles of validity, reliability, and ethics, and should address the challenges of sampling, measurement, and missing data.
