### Visualization and Analysis for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis

- Social network analysis (SNA) is the study of structure, and how it influences health, and it is based on theoretical constructs of sociology and mathematical foundations of graph theory.
- Structure refers to the regularities in the patterning of relationships among individuals, groups and/or organizations.
- SNA uses various methods to measure and describe the properties of network actors, subgroups and complete networks.
- Visualization is an important aspect of SNA, as it helps to display the network structure and attributes in a graphical form.
- Visualization also facilitates qualitative interpretation of network data and communication of network insights .
- SNA software tools are used to change the layout, colors, size and other properties of the network representation.
- Some SNA software tools can also perform predictive analysis and community detection .
- Evaluation of SNA involves assessing the validity, reliability and usefulness of the network measures and methods.
- Evaluation also involves comparing the results of SNA with other methods and data sources, and identifying the limitations and challenges of SNA.
- Evaluation can help to improve the quality and rigor of SNA, and to inform the design and implementation of network interventions.
