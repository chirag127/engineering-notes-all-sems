# SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Social network analytics is the application of social network analysis (SNA) to various types of data, such as social media, communication, collaboration, and transaction data, to gain insights into the patterns and behaviors of social actors and their relationships .
- Social network analytics can help answer questions such as:
  - Who are the most influential or central actors in a network?
  - How are information, resources, or opinions diffused or transmitted in a network?
  - How are groups or communities formed or maintained in a network?
  - How are network structures related to individual or collective outcomes or performance?
- Social network analytics can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Marketing: to identify potential customers, influencers, or advocates, and to measure the effectiveness of campaigns or strategies.
  - Public health: to understand the spread of diseases, behaviors, or interventions, and to design effective prevention or treatment programs.
  - Organization: to assess the collaboration, communication, or innovation among employees, teams, or departments, and to improve the organizational culture or performance.
- Social network analytics involves the following steps:
  - Data collection: to obtain the relevant data from various sources, such as surveys, interviews, observations, or online platforms, and to ensure the data quality and validity.
  - Data preparation: to transform the data into a network format, such as a matrix or a graph, and to define the nodes (actors) and the edges (relationships) of the network.
  - Data analysis: to apply various methods and techniques, such as descriptive statistics, visualization, centrality measures, clustering algorithms, or network models, to explore and explain the network characteristics and dynamics.
  - Data interpretation: to derive meaningful and actionable insights from the analysis results, and to communicate them effectively to the relevant stakeholders or audiences.
