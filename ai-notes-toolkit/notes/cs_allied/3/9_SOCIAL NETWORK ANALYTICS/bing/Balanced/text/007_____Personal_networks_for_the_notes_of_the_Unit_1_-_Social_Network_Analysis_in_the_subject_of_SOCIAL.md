### Personal networks

- Personal networks are subsets of social networks that focus on the relationships and interactions of a specific individual, group, or entity.
- Personal network analysis is the application of social network analysis methods and techniques to personal networks.
- Personal network analysis can help to study and understand various aspects of social life, such as social support, social capital, personal identity, social influence, and social mobility .
- Personal network analysis involves collecting data on the personal network members (also called alters) of a focal individual (also called ego), such as their attributes, ties, and roles.
- Personal network analysis also involves measuring and analyzing the structure, composition, and function of personal networks, such as their size, density, diversity, centrality, cohesion, and multiplexity .
- Personal network analysis can be used for different purposes, such as describing personal networks, comparing personal networks, explaining personal network outcomes, and intervening in personal networks.
- Personal network analysis can be conducted using various methods and tools, such as surveys, interviews, diaries, observations, experiments, software, and visualization.
- Personal network analysis can benefit from interdisciplinary and comparative perspectives, as personal networks vary across cultures, contexts, and domains.
