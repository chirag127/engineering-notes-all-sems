### Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Web-based networks are networks that are hosted on the web and connect people and organizations through various online platforms and channels.
- Web semantics is the study of the meaning and structure of the data and information on the web, such as web pages, hyperlinks, tags, and metadata.
- Social network applications are applications that use web-based networks to enable social interactions, communication, and information sharing among users and groups.
- Social network analytics is the field of data analytics that uses web semantics and network science to understand and analyze the patterns, dynamics, and outcomes of social network applications.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - Web-based social network models and types, such as blogs, microblogs, social media, social bookmarking, social news, social Q&A, etc.
  - Web semantics concepts and techniques, such as ontologies, RDF, SPARQL, OWL, etc.
  - Social network analysis methods and metrics, such as centrality, density, clustering, community detection, influence, diffusion, etc.
  - Social network analysis tools and software, such as Netlytic, Gephi, NodeXL, Hootsuite, IBM Watson, etc.
  - Social network analysis applications and use cases, such as marketing, recommender systems, sentiment analysis, crisis management, etc.
