### The macro-structure of social networks

- A social network is a social structure made up of a set of social actors (such as individuals or organizations), sets of dyadic ties, and other social interactions between actors.
- The macro-structure of social networks refers to the overall pattern of relationships (network structure) at the level of the whole network or large subgroups of actors.
- The macro-structure of social networks can be analyzed using various methods and measures, such as density, centrality, clustering, modularity, community detection, etc.
- The macro-structure of social networks can reveal important properties and functions of the network, such as cohesion, fragmentation, polarization, diffusion, influence, etc.
- The macro-structure of social networks can be influenced by various factors, such as the characteristics of the actors, the types of ties, the context of the network, the dynamics of the network, etc.
- The macro-structure of social networks can be applied to different levels of analysis, such as the meso level (between the macro and the micro levels) or the micro level (the behavior of the individual actors) .
- The macro-structure of social networks can be studied using different theoretical perspectives, such as the social network perspective, the complex network perspective, the social capital perspective, the social influence perspective, etc .
