# Ontological Representation of Social Individuals

- Ontology is the study of the nature and categories of being and existence.
- Social ontology is the subfield of ontology that deals with the entities, properties and kinds that arise from social interaction, such as social groups, norms, roles, institutions, etc.
- Ontological representation of social individuals is the process of describing and modeling the characteristics, relationships and activities of human agents in a social context, using a formal language or framework.
- Ontological representation of social individuals can be useful for various purposes, such as:
  - Knowledge sharing and reuse among different applications and domains.
  - Knowledge processing and reasoning by intelligent systems and agents.
  - Knowledge capturing and communication by humans and machines.
  - Knowledge discovery and analysis by data mining and network science techniques.
- Ontological representation of social individuals can be done at different levels of abstraction and granularity, depending on the scope and goal of the representation. For example:
  - At the micro level, one can represent the personal information and preferences of an individual, such as name, age, gender, hobbies, etc.
  - At the meso level, one can represent the social network and interactions of an individual, such as friends, colleagues, contacts, messages, etc.
  - At the macro level, one can represent the social roles and affiliations of an individual, such as profession, organization, community, etc.
- Ontological representation of social individuals can be based on different paradigms and formats, depending on the requirements and standards of the representation. For example:
  - The Friend-of-a-Friend (FOAF) ontology is an OWL based format for representing personal information and an individual’s social network.
  - The Social Network Analysis Ontology (SNAO) is an RDF based format for representing social network data and analysis.
  - The Social Media Ontology (SMO) is an OWL based format for representing social media data and activities.
  - The Social Role Ontology (SRO) is an OWL based format for representing social roles and their properties.
