# Evaluation of web-based social network extraction

Web-based social network extraction is the process of identifying and describing the actors and their relations from online resources, such as web pages, social media platforms, blogs, etc.  . It is a useful technique for web mining and social network analysis applications, such as community detection, sentiment analysis, recommendation systems, etc. .

However, web-based social network extraction faces many challenges and limitations, such as:

- The heterogeneity and complexity of web data, which may require different methods and tools for different sources and formats .
- The scalability and efficiency of the extraction algorithms, which may need to handle large amounts of data and update the network dynamically .
- The accuracy and reliability of the extracted network, which may depend on the quality and validity of the web data and the extraction methods .
- The ethical and legal issues of the extraction process, which may involve privacy, security, and consent of the web users and data owners .

Therefore, web-based social network extraction needs to be evaluated carefully and systematically, using appropriate criteria and metrics. Some possible evaluation methods are:

- Supervised methods, which compare the extracted network with a ground truth or a reference network, and measure the similarity or difference between them . For example, precision, recall, F-measure, etc.
- Unsupervised methods, which assess the extracted network based on its own properties and characteristics, and measure the quality or usefulness of the network . For example, density, modularity, centrality, etc.
- User-based methods, which involve human feedback and judgment on the extracted network, and measure the satisfaction or preference of the users . For example, surveys, ratings, rankings, etc.

Each evaluation method has its own advantages and disadvantages, and may be suitable for different scenarios and objectives. Therefore, it is important to choose the appropriate evaluation method based on the research question and the data source . Moreover, it is advisable to use multiple evaluation methods and compare the results, to obtain a comprehensive and robust evaluation of the web-based social network extraction .
