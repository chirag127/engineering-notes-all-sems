### Sesame

- Sesame is an **open source RDF database** with support for **RDFS inferencing and querying**.
- It offers a large scale of tools to developers to leverage the power of RDF and RDF Schema.
- It is a **Java framework**, there is also a **python wrapper**.
- Sesame can be used to **build Semantic Web applications with social network features**.
- Sesame has a **generic architecture** that consists of four layers: **storage, access, query, and application**.
- Sesame provides a **Java API** for accessing and manipulating RDF data, as well as a **HTTP protocol** for remote access.
- Sesame supports **multiple query languages**, such as **SPARQL, SeRQL, and RDQL**.
- Sesame can be integrated with other frameworks, such as **Elmo** and **GraphUtil**, to provide higher-level abstractions and functionalities for social-semantic applications.
- Sesame is used by several projects, such as **Flink**, which is a social network of the Semantic Web community .
