### Personal networks

- Personal networks are subsets of social networks that focus on the relationships and interactions of a specific individual, usually called the ego .
- Personal network analysis is the process of collecting, measuring, and analyzing data on the personal networks of individuals or groups .
- Personal network analysis can be used to study and understand various aspects of social life, such as social support, social capital, social influence, social identity, and social behavior .
- Personal network analysis can be conducted using various methods, such as surveys, interviews, observations, diaries, or online platforms.
- Personal network analysis can be performed at different levels of analysis, such as the ego level, the dyad level, the triad level, or the network level.
- Personal network analysis can be applied to various domains and contexts, such as health, education, work, family, migration, or online communities.
