### Comparison to the Entity/Relationship (E/R) model and the relational model

- The E/R model and the relational model are two popular data models in DBMS  .
- The E/R model is an entity-specific data model that describes the data with entity sets, relationship sets and attributes  .
- The relational model is a table-specific data model that represents the data in the form of tables (or relations) with rows (or tuples) and columns (or attributes)  .
- The E/R model is used to model the logical view of the system from a data perspective, while the relational model is used to implement the physical view of the system from a storage perspective .
- The E/R model is more abstract and conceptual, while the relational model is more concrete and operational .
- The E/R model supports the concepts of entities, relationships, attributes, keys, cardinalities, participation constraints, generalization, specialization, aggregation and composition .
- The relational model supports the concepts of relations, tuples, attributes, keys, domains, integrity constraints, relational algebra, relational calculus and normalization .
- The E/R model can be converted into the relational model by mapping the entity sets, relationship sets and attributes to the tables, rows and columns respectively .
- The E/R model is more suitable for designing and modeling the data requirements of a system, while the relational model is more suitable for storing and manipulating the data in a system .
- The E/R model is more expressive and flexible, while the relational model is more standardized and efficient .
