# SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Social network analytics is the process of investigating and analyzing social structures and interactions through the use of networks and graph theory.
- Social network analytics can be applied to various domains, such as sociology, psychology, communication, marketing, public health, computer science, and business .
- Social network analytics can help to understand the patterns, dynamics, and outcomes of social relationships and behaviors, such as information diffusion, influence, trust, collaboration, and innovation .
- Social network analytics can also help to optimize the design and performance of social media platforms, such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and LinkedIn, by measuring and improving user engagement, satisfaction, and loyalty.
- Social network analytics involves the following steps :
  - Define the research question and the scope of the analysis.
  - Identify and collect the relevant data sources, such as social media posts, comments, likes, shares, follows, etc.
  - Clean, preprocess, and transform the data into a network representation, such as a graph, a matrix, or a table.
  - Apply network analysis methods and techniques, such as centrality, density, clustering, community detection, etc., to measure and visualize the network properties and characteristics.
  - Interpret and communicate the results and insights, and provide recommendations or actions based on the findings.
