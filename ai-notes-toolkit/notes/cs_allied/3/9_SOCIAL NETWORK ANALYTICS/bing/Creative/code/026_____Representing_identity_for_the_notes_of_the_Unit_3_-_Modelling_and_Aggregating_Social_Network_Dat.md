### Representing identity for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Social network analysis (SNA) is the process of investigating social structures through the use of networks and graph theory.
- Networks and graphs are mathematical models that consist of nodes (individual actors, people, or things within the network) and edges (relationships or interactions) that connect them.
- Representing identity in social networks involves defining the attributes and features of the nodes and edges, as well as the structure and dynamics of the network as a whole.
- Some common ways of representing identity in social networks are:

  - **Node attributes**: These are the characteristics or properties of the nodes, such as name, age, gender, location, occupation, interests, etc. Node attributes can be categorical, numerical, or textual.
  - **Edge attributes**: These are the characteristics or properties of the edges, such as type, weight, direction, duration, frequency, etc. Edge attributes can also be categorical, numerical, or textual.
  - **Network structure**: This is the pattern or shape of the network, such as the number of nodes and edges, the density, the degree distribution, the clustering coefficient, the centrality measures, the community detection, etc. Network structure can reveal the global and local properties of the network, such as connectivity, hierarchy, homophily, influence, etc .
  - **Network dynamics**: This is the change or evolution of the network over time, such as the formation, dissolution, or modification of nodes and edges, the diffusion of information, the emergence of trends, the detection of anomalies, etc. Network dynamics can capture the temporal and causal aspects of the network, such as stability, variability, feedback, etc .

- Representing identity in social networks can have many applications, such as:

  - **Social media analysis**: This involves analyzing the content and behavior of users on online platforms, such as Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, etc. Social media analysis can help understand the interests and opinions of users, discover patterns and trends from the interactions between users, and mine the events that take place on the platforms.
  - **Text analysis**: This involves analyzing the content and structure of textual data, such as documents, articles, books, emails, etc. Text analysis can help extract the topics and sentiments of the texts, identify the authors and sources of the texts, and construct the networks of the entities and concepts mentioned in the texts.
  - **Sensor network analysis**: This involves analyzing the data and communication of sensor devices, such as cameras, radars, satellites, etc. Sensor network analysis can help monitor the physical environment, detect and track the objects and events of interest, and optimize the performance and security of the network.
