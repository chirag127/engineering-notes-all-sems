# SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

- Social network analytics is the application of social network analysis (SNA) to various types of data, such as social media, communication, collaboration, and transaction data, to gain insights into the patterns and behaviors of social actors and their relationships .
- Social network analytics can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Understanding the structure and dynamics of social networks, such as communities, cliques, bridges, brokers, influencers, and outliers .
  - Measuring the social capital, trust, reputation, and influence of social actors within and across networks .
  - Identifying the key drivers and barriers of information diffusion, innovation adoption, and behavior change in social networks .
  - Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and malicious activities in social networks .
  - Optimizing the design and performance of social media platforms, campaigns, and interventions based on network metrics and feedback .
- Social network analytics involves the following steps :
  - Data collection: Gathering and preprocessing the relevant data from various sources, such as social media platforms, surveys, web analytics, and databases.
  - Network construction: Defining the nodes (social actors) and edges (relationships) of the network, and assigning attributes and weights to them based on the data.
  - Network analysis: Applying network measures, models, and algorithms to analyze the network structure, properties, and processes, such as centrality, density, clustering, homophily, reciprocity, and diffusion.
  - Network visualization: Creating graphical representations of the network and its features, such as graphs, matrices, maps, and dashboards, to facilitate exploration and communication of the results.
  - Network interpretation: Drawing conclusions and implications from the network analysis and visualization, and validating them with domain knowledge and additional data sources.
