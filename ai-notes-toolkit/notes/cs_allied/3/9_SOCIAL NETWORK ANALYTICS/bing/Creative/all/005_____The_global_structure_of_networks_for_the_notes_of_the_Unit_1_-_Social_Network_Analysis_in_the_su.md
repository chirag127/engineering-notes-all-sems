# The global structure of networks

- Social network analysis (SNA) is a general study of the social network using network and graph theory concepts  .
- A social network is a set of actors (nodes) and the relationships (edges) between them  .
- SNA aims to understand the behavior of individuals, the pattern of relationships, and the interactions between the two levels .
- SNA can be applied to various domains, such as social media, information diffusion, health, business, education, politics, and history    .
- The global structure of networks refers to the regularities in the patterning of relationships among actors, groups, and/or organizations.
- The global structure of networks can be characterized by different properties, such as size, density, diameter, degree distribution, clustering coefficient, centrality, modularity, and community detection  .
- The global structure of networks can influence the outcomes of individual and collective behavior, such as diffusion, influence, cooperation, innovation, and resilience    .
- SNA can help to identify the key actors, groups, and positions in a network, as well as the gaps, bridges, and boundaries between them    .
- SNA can also help to compare and contrast different networks, and to evaluate the effects of network interventions    .
