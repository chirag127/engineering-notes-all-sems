# Blogs and Online Communities

- A blog is an online journal or informational website that offers regularly updated content (blog post) about a topic.
- A blog post is written in an informal or conversational style and presents information in reverse chronological order.
- A blog can be run by an individual, group, or corporation.
- A blog can have different types, such as microblogging, where the amount of information in a single element is smaller, and liveblogging, where an ongoing event is blogged about in real time.
- A blog can also have different formats, such as text, audio, video, or images.
- A blog can allow readers to comment, share, or subscribe to the content.
- A blog can be used for various purposes, such as personal expression, education, entertainment, marketing, or social activism.

- An online community is a group of people with common interests, and who use the Internet tools and spaces (e.g., blogs, websites, and email) to communicate, create something together, and pursue common interests over time.
- An online community can have different types, such as interest-based, practice-based, identity-based, or place-based.
- An online community can have different structures, such as hierarchical, networked, or hybrid.
- An online community can have different features, such as membership, profiles, content, interaction, norms, and reputation.
- An online community can have different benefits, such as social support, learning, collaboration, innovation, or civic engagement.

- Blogs and online communities are related in several ways:
  - Blogs are among the major platforms on which online communities form.
  - Blogs can facilitate the communication, creation, and sharing of content among online community members.
  - Blogs can reflect the interests, practices, identities, or places of online community members.
  - Blogs can influence the norms, reputation, and engagement of online community members.
  - Blogs can also be part of larger online communities, such as social networks, forums, or wikis.
