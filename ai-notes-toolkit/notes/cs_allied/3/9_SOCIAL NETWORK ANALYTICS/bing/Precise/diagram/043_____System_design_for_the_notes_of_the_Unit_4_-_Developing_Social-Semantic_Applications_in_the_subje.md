### System Design for Developing Social-Semantic Applications

Unit 4: Social Network Analytics

1. **Identify the requirements**: Determine the goals and objectives of the social-semantic application, and identify the data sources and user needs that must be addressed.
2. **Design the data model**: Develop a data model that captures the relationships and attributes of the entities in the social network, and supports the desired functionality of the application.
3. **Select the appropriate technologies**: Choose the technologies and tools that best support the data model and the desired functionality of the application.
4. **Implement the system**: Develop the application using the chosen technologies and tools, and integrate it with the data sources and other systems as needed.
5. **Test and evaluate**: Test the application to ensure that it meets the requirements and performs as expected, and evaluate its effectiveness in achieving the desired goals and objectives.
