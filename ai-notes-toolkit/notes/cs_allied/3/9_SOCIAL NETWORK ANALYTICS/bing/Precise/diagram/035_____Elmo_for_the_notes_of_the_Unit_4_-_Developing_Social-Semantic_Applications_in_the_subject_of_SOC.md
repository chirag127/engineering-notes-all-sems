### Unit 4 - Developing Social-Semantic Applications: Elmo

- Elmo is a deep learning-based natural language processing model developed by Allen Institute for Artificial Intelligence.
- It stands for Embeddings from Language Models and is designed to improve the performance of NLP tasks by generating contextualized word representations.
- Unlike traditional word embeddings, which assign a fixed vector to each word, Elmo generates word representations that are functions of the entire input sentence.
- This allows the model to capture the meaning of words in different contexts, leading to improved performance on a wide range of NLP tasks.
- Elmo has been shown to significantly improve the state-of-the-art for several NLP tasks, including question answering, named entity recognition, and sentiment analysis.
- The model is pre-trained on a large corpus of text and can be fine-tuned for specific tasks, making it a powerful tool for developing social-semantic applications.
