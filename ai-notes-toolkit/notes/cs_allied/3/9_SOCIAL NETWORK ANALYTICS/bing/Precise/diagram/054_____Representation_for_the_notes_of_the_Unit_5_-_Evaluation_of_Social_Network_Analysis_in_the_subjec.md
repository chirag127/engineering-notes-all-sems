### Representation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Evaluation of Social Network Analysis involves assessing the effectiveness of the methods and techniques used in analyzing social networks.
2. It is important to evaluate the results of Social Network Analysis to ensure that the conclusions drawn are accurate and reliable.
3. Evaluation can be done through various methods such as comparing the results with known ground truth, cross-validation, and sensitivity analysis.
4. Comparing the results with known ground truth involves comparing the results of the analysis with data that is known to be true. This can help to assess the accuracy of the analysis.
5. Cross-validation involves dividing the data into training and testing sets and using the training set to build the model and the testing set to evaluate its performance.
6. Sensitivity analysis involves testing the robustness of the results by varying the parameters of the analysis and observing the changes in the results.
7. Evaluation of Social Network Analysis is crucial in ensuring that the analysis provides meaningful and actionable insights.
