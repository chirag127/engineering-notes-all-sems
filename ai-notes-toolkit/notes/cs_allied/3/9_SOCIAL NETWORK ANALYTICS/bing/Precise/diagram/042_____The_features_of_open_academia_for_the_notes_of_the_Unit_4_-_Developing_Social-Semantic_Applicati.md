### The features of open academia for the notes of the Unit 4 - Developing Social-Semantic Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Open Access: Open academia promotes open access to research and scholarly materials, making them freely available to the public.
2. Collaboration: Open academia encourages collaboration among researchers, scholars, and students, allowing them to share their knowledge and work together on projects.
3. Transparency: Open academia values transparency in research and scholarly activities, promoting the sharing of data, methods, and results.
4. Reproducibility: Open academia supports the reproducibility of research, encouraging researchers to share their data and methods so that others can verify and build upon their work.
5. Innovation: Open academia fosters innovation by providing an open and collaborative environment for researchers, scholars, and students to share their ideas and work together on new projects.
6. Inclusivity: Open academia promotes inclusivity by making research and scholarly materials accessible to a wider audience, including those who may not have access to traditional academic resources.
7. Interdisciplinarity: Open academia encourages interdisciplinary research and collaboration, allowing researchers from different fields to work together and share their knowledge.
