### Electronic discussion networks

Electronic discussion networks are online platforms that allow users to communicate and share information with each other. These networks can be used for various purposes, including social networking, knowledge sharing, and collaboration.

In the context of Social Network Analytics, electronic discussion networks can be used as a source of data for network analysis. By collecting data from these networks, researchers can study the structure and dynamics of social networks, and gain insights into the behavior and interactions of individuals within these networks.

Some key concepts and measures used in network analysis of electronic discussion networks include:

- **Global structure of networks**: This refers to the overall structure and properties of the network, such as its size, density, and degree distribution.
- **Macro-structure of social networks**: This refers to the patterns of connections between groups of individuals within the network, such as the presence of clusters or communities.
- **Personal networks**: This refers to the network of connections surrounding a single individual, and can provide insights into their social behavior and relationships.

Web semantics plays an important role in the analysis of electronic discussion networks. By using ontologies and other semantic technologies, researchers can represent and reason about the knowledge contained within these networks, and extract meaningful insights from the data.

In summary, electronic discussion networks are an important source of data for Social Network Analytics, and the use of web semantics can enhance the analysis of these networks.
