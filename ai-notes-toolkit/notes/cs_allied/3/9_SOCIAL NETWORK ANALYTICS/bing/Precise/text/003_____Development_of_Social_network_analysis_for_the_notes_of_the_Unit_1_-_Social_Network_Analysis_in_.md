### Development of Social Network Analysis

- Social network analysis is a research method developed primarily in sociology and communication science, which focuses on patterns of relations among people and among groups such as organizations and states.
- The origin and historical development of social network analysis (SNA) dates to the end of the nineteenth century with the precursor work of Auguste Comte. However, its evolution as a methodology owes much to mathematics, namely, to graph theory.
- As the Web connects people and organizations, it can host social networks. Therefore, social network analysis has been used to study Web hyperlinks.
- Social Network Analysis (SNA) is a promising yet underutilized tool in the international development field. SNA entails collecting and analyzing data to characterize and visualize social networks, where nodes represent network members and edges connecting nodes represent relationships or exchanges among them.
