### Open Academia

Open Academia is an organization that offers a wide range of services aimed at supporting independent publishing. They provide full support as an experienced and strong back office working to ensure a professional online presence for the journal. They also provide support to university presses, libraries, and other academic units looking to publish open access .

Some of the services offered by Open Academia include:
- Partnering with Public Knowledge Project (PKP) to provide a complete production and publication service that includes processing manuscripts through copyediting, typesetting, proofreading (liaising with authors), online publication, indexing, and archiving  .
- Providing a flexible and highly configurable system for managing digital publishing, as traditional publishing houses have a hard time keeping up with the evolving and gaining momentum of Open Access publishing .

Another example of an open access library is Academia.edu, which has over 40 million academic papers that have been uploaded by more than 12 million researchers around the world .
