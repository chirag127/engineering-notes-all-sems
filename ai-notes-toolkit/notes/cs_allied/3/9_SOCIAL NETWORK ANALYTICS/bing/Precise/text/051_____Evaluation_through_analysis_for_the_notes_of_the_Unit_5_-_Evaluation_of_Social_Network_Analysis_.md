### Evaluation through analysis for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Evaluation through analysis is a crucial step in the process of Social Network Analysis (SNA).
2. It involves the use of various analytical techniques to assess the effectiveness and impact of social networks.
3. Some common methods used for evaluation through analysis include centrality measures, density measures, and clustering coefficients.
4. Centrality measures help to identify the most influential or important nodes in a network.
5. Density measures provide information about the overall connectedness of the network.
6. Clustering coefficients measure the extent to which nodes in a network tend to cluster together.
7. These analytical techniques can be used to evaluate the effectiveness of social networks in achieving their intended goals.
8. For example, a high density and clustering coefficient may indicate a strong and cohesive community, while a low centrality measure may suggest that the network is not effectively reaching its target audience.
9. Evaluation through analysis is an important tool for researchers and practitioners in the field of SNA, as it provides valuable insights into the functioning and impact of social networks.
