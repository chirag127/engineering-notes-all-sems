### The features of open academia for the notes of the Unit 4 - Developing Social-Semantic Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Open Access: Open academia promotes open access to research and scholarly materials, making them freely available to the public.
2. Collaboration: Open academia encourages collaboration among researchers, scholars, and students, facilitating the sharing of knowledge and ideas.
3. Transparency: Open academia values transparency in research, including the sharing of data, methods, and results.
4. Reproducibility: Open academia promotes the reproducibility of research, allowing others to verify and build upon the work of others.
5. Innovation: Open academia fosters innovation by providing a platform for the exchange of ideas and the development of new research methods and technologies.
6. Inclusivity: Open academia strives to be inclusive, providing opportunities for researchers and scholars from diverse backgrounds to participate and contribute.
7. Interdisciplinarity: Open academia encourages interdisciplinary research, bringing together researchers from different fields to address complex problems.
8. Public Engagement: Open academia values public engagement, seeking to involve the public in the research process and to communicate research findings to a broad audience.
