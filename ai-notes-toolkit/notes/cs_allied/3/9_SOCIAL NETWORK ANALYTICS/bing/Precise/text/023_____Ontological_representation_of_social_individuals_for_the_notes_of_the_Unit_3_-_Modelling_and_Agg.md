### Ontological representation of social individuals

Ontological representation of social individuals is a topic in the field of social ontology, which is the study of the nature and properties of the social world. Social ontology is concerned with analyzing the various entities in the world that arise from social interaction.

- Ontological individualism is the thesis that social or historical groups, processes, and events are nothing more than complexes of individuals and individual actions .
- Social ontology includes the basic entities, properties, and kinds studied by the social sciences. There are two kinds of social entities: social individuals and social complexes or collectives.
- The Friend-of-a-Friend (FOAF) ontology is an OWL-based format for representing personal information and an individual’s social network.
