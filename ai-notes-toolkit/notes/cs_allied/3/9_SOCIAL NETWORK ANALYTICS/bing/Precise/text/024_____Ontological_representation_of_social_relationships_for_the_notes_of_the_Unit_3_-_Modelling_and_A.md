### Ontological representation of social relationships

- Ontological representation of social networks such as Friend of a Friend (FOAF) is a popular method in applications .
- However, these ontologies still lack the ability to support the automated integration of social information on a semantic basis and capture established concepts in social network analysis .
- The social relationship representation model consists of 4 layers .
- Layer 1 is the fundamental, where each social network application has its relationship data represented by ontology and conceptual graph .
- In layer 2, relevant social relationship datasets or user concerns are integrated into a united representation form based on the context .
- Social relationships are described in FOAF as RDF statements with a subject and an object of type foaf:Person and a predicate that is a subproperty of the foaf:knows relationship .
- Qualifying a statement in RDF requires reification and for this purpose a minimal vocabulary is provided by the RDF Schema specification .
