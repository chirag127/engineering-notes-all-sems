### Electronic discussion networks

Electronic discussion networks are a type of social media platform that allows users to communicate and share information with each other. These networks can be used for a variety of purposes, including socializing, sharing news and information, and collaborating on projects.

In the context of Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS, electronic discussion networks can be used to collect social network data from Internet sources. This data can then be analyzed using network analysis techniques to better understand the structure and behavior of social networks.

Some key concepts and measures in network analysis include the global structure of networks, the macro-structure of social networks, and personal networks. These concepts can be used to analyze data collected from electronic discussion networks and other sources to gain insights into the behavior of social networks.

Overall, electronic discussion networks are an important source of data for social network analysis and can provide valuable insights into the behavior of social networks. They are one of the many tools available to researchers and analysts studying social networks and the Semantic Web.
