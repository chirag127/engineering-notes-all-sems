### The features of open academia for the notes of the Unit 4 - Developing Social-Semantic Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Open Access: Open academia promotes open access to research and scholarly materials, making them freely available to the public.
2. Collaboration: Open academia encourages collaboration among researchers and scholars, allowing them to share their knowledge and work together on projects.
3. Transparency: Open academia values transparency in research, making data, methods, and results openly available for review and scrutiny.
4. Reproducibility: Open academia promotes reproducibility in research, allowing others to verify and build upon the work of others.
5. Public Engagement: Open academia encourages engagement with the public, allowing researchers and scholars to share their work with a wider audience and receive feedback from the community.
6. Innovation: Open academia fosters innovation by providing an open and collaborative environment for researchers and scholars to explore new ideas and approaches.
7. Inclusivity: Open academia promotes inclusivity by providing equal access to research and scholarly materials, regardless of an individual's background or resources.
