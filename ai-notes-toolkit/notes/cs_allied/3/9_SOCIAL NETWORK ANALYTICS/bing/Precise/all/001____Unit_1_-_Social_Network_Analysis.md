## Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis

Social Network Analysis (SNA) is a research technique that focuses on identifying and comparing the relationships within and between individuals, groups, and organizations.

1. **Concepts and Measures**: SNA involves the use of various concepts and measures to analyze social networks. Some of these include centrality, density, and clustering coefficient.

2. **Data Collection**: Data for SNA can be collected through various methods such as surveys, interviews, and observation.

3. **Visualization**: SNA often involves the use of visual representations of networks to help identify patterns and relationships.

4. **Applications**: SNA has a wide range of applications in fields such as sociology, anthropology, and business.

5. **Limitations**: SNA has its limitations, including the potential for incomplete data and the difficulty in establishing causality.

6. **Software**: There are various software programs available for conducting SNA, including UCINet, Gephi, and NodeXL.

7. **Future Directions**: SNA is a rapidly evolving field, with ongoing research into new methods and applications. Some potential future directions include the use of machine learning and the analysis of online social networks.
