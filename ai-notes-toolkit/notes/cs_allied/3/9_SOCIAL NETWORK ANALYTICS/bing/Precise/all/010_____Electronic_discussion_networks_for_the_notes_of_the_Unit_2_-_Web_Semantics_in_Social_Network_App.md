### Electronic Discussion Networks

Electronic discussion networks are a type of social media platform that allows users to participate in online discussions and share information. These networks can be used for a variety of purposes, including professional networking, academic collaboration, and personal communication.

In the context of Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS, electronic discussion networks can be used to collect social network data from internet sources. This data can then be analyzed using network analysis techniques to gain insights into the structure and behavior of social networks.

Some key concepts and measures in network analysis include the global structure of networks, the macro-structure of social networks, and personal networks. These concepts can be used to understand the relationships between individuals and groups within a social network.

Overall, electronic discussion networks provide a valuable source of data for social network analysis and can help researchers and analysts better understand the dynamics of social networks.
