### Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

In the world of social network analytics, web-based networks play a crucial role in understanding the behavior and interactions of individuals in online communities. Here are some important points about web-based networks that you should know:

- Web-based networks refer to social networks that are formed online and are based on web technologies.
- These networks are built on top of the web infrastructure and are accessible through web browsers, making them widely available to users around the world.
- Web-based networks use web semantics to describe the relationships and interactions between users, which can be analyzed to gain insights into user behavior and community dynamics.
- Web semantics refer to the meaning and interpretation of data on the web, and are used to create structured data that can be easily processed and analyzed by machines.
- Web-based networks can be analyzed using social network analysis (SNA) techniques, which involve the study of social structures and relationships within a network.
- SNA can help us understand the patterns of communication and interaction between users, as well as the roles and positions that users occupy within a network.
- Web-based networks can also be analyzed using natural language processing (NLP) techniques, which involve the study of language use and communication patterns within a network.
- NLP can help us understand the content and sentiment of user messages, as well as the topics and themes that are discussed within a network.
- Web-based networks can be visualized using network visualization tools, which provide a graphical representation of the network structure and relationships between users.
- Network visualization can help us identify patterns and clusters within a network, as well as the influential users and communities within a network.

In summary, web-based networks are an important area of study in social network analytics, and can provide valuable insights into the behavior and interactions of individuals in online communities. By using web semantics, SNA, NLP, and network visualization techniques, we can gain a deeper understanding of the social structures and relationships within web-based networks, and use this knowledge to inform decision-making and improve user experiences.
