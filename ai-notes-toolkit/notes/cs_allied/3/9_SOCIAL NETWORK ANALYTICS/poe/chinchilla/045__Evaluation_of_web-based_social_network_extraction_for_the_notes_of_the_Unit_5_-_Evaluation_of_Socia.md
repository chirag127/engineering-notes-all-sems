### Evaluation of web-based social network extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

Social network analysis has become a popular method for understanding the structure and dynamics of social systems. With the increasing popularity of online social networks, web-based social network extraction has become an important research area. In this unit, we will evaluate the effectiveness of web-based social network extraction and its role in social network analysis.

Here are some points to consider when evaluating web-based social network extraction:

1. Accuracy: The accuracy of web-based social network extraction is critical to the success of social network analysis. The extracted network should accurately represent the connections between individuals in the social network. The accuracy can be evaluated by comparing the extracted network with the actual network and by analyzing the errors.

2. Scalability: Web-based social network extraction should be able to handle large amounts of data. The scalability of the extraction method is important, as social networks can contain millions of nodes and edges. The scalability can be evaluated by analyzing the performance of the extraction method on large datasets.

3. Robustness: Web-based social network extraction should be robust to noise and errors in the data. The extraction method should be able to handle missing data, incorrect data, and noisy data. The robustness can be evaluated by analyzing the performance of the extraction method on noisy datasets and by comparing the results with other extraction methods.

4. Efficiency: Web-based social network extraction should be efficient and fast. The extraction method should be able to extract the social network in a reasonable amount of time. The efficiency can be evaluated by analyzing the performance of the extraction method on large datasets and by comparing the results with other extraction methods.

5. Privacy: Web-based social network extraction should protect the privacy of individuals in the social network. The extraction method should not expose sensitive information about individuals in the network. The privacy can be evaluated by analyzing the privacy policies of the extraction method and by comparing the results with other extraction methods.

In conclusion, web-based social network extraction is an important research area for social network analysis. The effectiveness of the extraction method can be evaluated based on its accuracy, scalability, robustness, efficiency, and privacy. By evaluating these factors, we can determine the reliability and usefulness of the extracted social network for social network analysis.
