### Electronic discussion networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS.

1. Electronic discussion networks refer to online platforms where people can discuss, converse, debate or exchange information on a variety of topics. These networks allow people to connect, communicate and collaborate via computers and network connections.

2. Some examples of electronic discussion networks are online forums, message boards, newsgroups, mailing lists, blogs, social media platforms like Reddit, Facebook groups, etc. These networks facilitate asynchronous communication where participants can share their views or comments whenever they want without requiring real-time interaction.

3. The content shared on these networks can include text, images, videos, audio, etc. The participants can start their own discussion threads or comment on existing threads. They can choose to restrict discussions to specific topics or keep them open-ended. The networks may be public, private or a mix of both.

4. For studying web semantics in social network applications, electronic discussion networks provide a rich source of data to analyze conversational patterns, topics of discussion, sentiment analysis, spread of information, user interactions, etc. By understanding the structure and content of communications on these networks, insights can be gained into user behaviors, interests, and relationships. The data can be used to improve applications and services.

5. However, there are challenges involved in collecting and analyzing data from electronic discussion networks. The informal style of communication can make it difficult to process the language. The scale of data and variety of topics can also pose issues. Privacy concerns have to be considered when using data from private networks. Appropriate techniques and tools are required to handle these challenges.
