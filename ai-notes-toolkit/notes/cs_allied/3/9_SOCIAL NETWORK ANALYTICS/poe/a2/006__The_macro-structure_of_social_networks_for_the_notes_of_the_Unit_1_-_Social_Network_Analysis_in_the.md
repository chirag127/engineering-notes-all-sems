### The macro-structure of social networks for the notes of the Unit 1 - Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS.

1. Nodes: Individual actors or entities in the network. For example, people, organizations, groups, computers, websites, etc.
2. Ties (also called edges or links): The relationships or interactions between the nodes. For example, friendship, communication, membership, etc.
3. Networks: The pattern of ties between the nodes. The nodes plus the ties form a social network.
4. Subnetworks: Connected parts of the whole network. For example, subgroups where the members know each other.
5. Density: The number of ties in a network relative to the maximum possible number of ties. A dense network has many ties, a sparse network has few ties. Density affects how fast something can spread in a network.
6. Centrality: The relative importance of a node in a network. There are different measures of centrality, such as degree centrality, betweenness centrality, and closeness centrality. Nodes with high centrality are influential in the network.
