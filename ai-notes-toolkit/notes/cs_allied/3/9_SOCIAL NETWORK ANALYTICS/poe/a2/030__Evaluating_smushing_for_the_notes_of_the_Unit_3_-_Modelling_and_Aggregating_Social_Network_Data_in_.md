### Evaluating smushing for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Smushing is a technique used to aggregate networks by combining nodes and edges. It is done to reduce the complexity of a large network and focus on macro-level patterns.
2. The pros of smushing include:
- Simplification of complex networks for analysis
- Identification of meta-communities and clusters
- Understanding network patterns at a high level
3. The cons of smushing include:
- Loss of detail as nodes and edges are combined
- Inability to analyze micro-level patterns
- Prone to biases if not done carefully and objectively
4. When evaluating smushing, factors to consider are:
- The level of aggregation (high vs low)
- The criteria used for combining nodes and edges
- The purpose of the analysis and what patterns need to be focused on
- The inherent biases in the smushing process and how to minimize them
5. Ultimately, smushing should be done judiciously by balancing the needs to simplify the network and retain important details. The evaluation of smushing depends on the analytical goals and the network in consideration.
