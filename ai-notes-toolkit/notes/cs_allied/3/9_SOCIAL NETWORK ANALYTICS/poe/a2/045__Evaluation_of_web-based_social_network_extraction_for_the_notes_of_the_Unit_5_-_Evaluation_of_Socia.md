### Evaluation of web-based social network extraction for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS:

1. Web-based social networks: Websites and applications that allow individuals to create profiles, connect with others, post messages, images, and videos, and share various types of content. Some popular examples are Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.
2. Web-based social network extraction: The process of extracting data from web-based social networks to analyze the structure and dynamics of the networks and understand the interactions and relationships between users. This extracted data can be used to study various aspects of user behavior, information spread, influence, communities, etc.
3. Challenges in extraction: The main challenges in extracting data from web-based social networks are:
- Constantly changing APIs and policies: The APIs and data policies of the networks keep changing, making existing extraction methods fail frequently. New methods need to be developed continuously to extract data from the changing interfaces.
- Scale of data: The networks contain huge amounts of data in the form of posts, connections, multimedia, and more. Extracting and processing this immense volume of data is a major challenge.
- Ethical issues: There are ethical concerns regarding the privacy of users and use of their data. Proper consent and anonymization are necessary while extracting and using social network data.
4. Applications of extraction: Extracted social network data can be used to:
- Study network structure and dynamics
- Analyze influence, information spread, and relationships
- Detect communities and clusters
- Target advertising and personalized recommendations
- Monitor public opinion and events
- Predict behavior and trends
