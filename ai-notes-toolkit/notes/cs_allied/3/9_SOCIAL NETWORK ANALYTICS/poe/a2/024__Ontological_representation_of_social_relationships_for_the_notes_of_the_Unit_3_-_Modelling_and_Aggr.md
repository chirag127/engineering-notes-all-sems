### Ontological representation of social relationships for the notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS.

- Ontology is a formal representation of knowledge as a set of concepts within a domain and the relationships between those concepts.
- Ontologies are used to reason about the entities in that domain and the relationships between them.
- In social network analysis, ontologies are useful to represent the social relationships and interactions between actors.
- Some of the ways to represent social relationships ontologically are:
-- Representing relationships as links between actors/nodes with types like friend, family, colleague, etc.
-- Representing relationships with properties like strength, duration, etc.
-- Representing complex relationships like relative power between actors.
-- Representing dynamic relationships that change over time.
- Ontological representation of social relationships enables richer analysis and reasoning about networks which can provide deeper insights.
- However, ontological representation also introduces complexity which needs to be handled effectively for practical applications.
