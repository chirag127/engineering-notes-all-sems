### Web-based networks for the notes of the Unit 2 - Web Semantics in Social Network Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

1. Web-based social networks: These networks are based on the World Wide Web. Examples include Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, and Instagram. Users create profiles, connect with other users, post status updates, upload photos or videos, send messages, join interest-based communities, events, and groups.

2. Features of web-based social networks:
- Profiles: Users create profiles with personal information like name, photo, location, interests, etc.
- Connections: Users connect with other users by sending and accepting connection requests. These connections form the social graph of the network.
- Sharing status updates: Users share quick updates, ideas, thoughts, opinions, etc. in the form of status messages, tweets, posts, etc.
- Uploading and sharing media: Users upload and share photos, videos, links, documents, etc.
- Creating and joining communities: Users can create and join communities, events, and groups centered around interests, topics, locations, etc.
- Messaging: Users can send private or group messages to other users.

3. Data generated on web-based social networks: These networks generate a huge amount of data in the forms of profiles, connections, status updates, shared media, messages, and interactions. This data is very useful to analyze user behavior, interests, preferences, relationships, influence, trends, etc. The analysis of this data has emerged as a research field known as social network analysis.
