### Data acquisition for the notes of the Unit 5 - Evaluation of Social Network Analysis in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS.

1. Define the research questions and hypotheses:
- What are the research questions and hypotheses that the social network analysis will help to answer? Formulating the right research questions and hypotheses is key to acquiring the appropriate data to conduct the social network analysis.

2. Decide the units of analysis:
- Who or what are the nodes that will be connected in the social network? This could be individuals, organizations, groups, etc. Determining the appropriate units of analysis is necessary to acquire data on the correct entities and relationships between them.

3. Determine methods of data collection:
- How will the data on the nodes and relationships between them be collected? Some options include surveys, interviews, archival records, digital trace data, ethnographic observations, etc. The methods of data collection must be selected based on the research questions, hypotheses, and units of analysis.

4. Obtain necessary permissions and consent:
- If the data collection involves human subjects, it is necessary to obtain proper consent and permissions as per ethical guidelines. This may require approval from an ethics review board.

5. Collect and prepare the data:
- Collect the data using the determined methods. Then, prepare the data for social network analysis by encoding node attributes and relationships between nodes in a suitable format. The data must be cleaned and preprocessed before network analysis can be performed.
