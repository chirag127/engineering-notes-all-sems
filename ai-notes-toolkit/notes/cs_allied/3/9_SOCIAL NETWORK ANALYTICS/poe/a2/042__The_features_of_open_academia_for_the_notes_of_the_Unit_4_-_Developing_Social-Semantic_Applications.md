### The features of open academia for the notes of the Unit 4 - Developing Social-Semantic Applications in the subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS.

1. Open access to research papers: Research papers are made freely available to everyone without any restrictions. This promotes sharing and growth of knowledge.
2. Open data sharing: Datasets are shared openly for reproducibility and further analysis by other researchers. This speeds up research and innovation.
3. Open source tools: Tools and software used for research are open source which means that their code is openly shared and can be used, modified and distributed by anyone. This decreases dependency and increases participation.
4. Open peer review: Reviews of research papers are openly shared which makes the review process more transparent and accountable. This decreases biases and increases trust in the system.
5. Open educational resources: Learning materials like videos, texts, forums etc are freely available for everyone. This enables affordable access to education and learning.

The above points highlight the key features of open academia which aim to make research and education more collaborative, transparent and accessible to all. This fosters a spirit of openness and sharing which is beneficial for the growth of individuals and society as a whole.
