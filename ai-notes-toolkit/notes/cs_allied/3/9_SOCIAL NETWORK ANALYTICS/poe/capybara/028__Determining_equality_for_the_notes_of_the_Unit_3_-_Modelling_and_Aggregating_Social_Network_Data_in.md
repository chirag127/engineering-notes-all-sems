### Determining Equality for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data in the Subject of SOCIAL NETWORK ANALYTICS

When analyzing social network data, determining the equality of notes is crucial to understanding the overall network structure. Here are some methods for determining equality:

- Degree centrality: This method determines the number of connections each note has in the network. The more connections a note has, the higher its degree centrality. 
- Betweenness centrality: This method measures the number of times a note acts as a bridge between other notes in the network. A note with high betweenness centrality is important in connecting different parts of the network.
- Closeness centrality: This method measures the distance of a note to all other notes in the network. The closer a note is to other notes, the higher its closeness centrality.
- Eigenvector centrality: This method calculates the importance of a note based on the importance of the notes it is connected to. A note with many connections to important notes will have higher eigenvector centrality.

It is important to use multiple methods to determine equality, as different methods may reveal different insights about the network. By analyzing the equality of notes, we can gain a better understanding of the overall structure and importance of each note in the social network.
