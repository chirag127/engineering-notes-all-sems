## Unit 3 - Modelling and Aggregating Social Network Data

In this unit, we will learn about the modelling and aggregating of social network data. The topics we will cover include:

- Introduction to Social Network Analysis (SNA)
    - Definition of SNA
    - Importance of SNA
    - Key concepts in SNA
    
- Types of Social Networks
    - Formal and Informal Networks
    - Directed and Undirected Networks
    - Multiplex and Multilevel Networks
    
- Modelling Social Networks
    - Nodes and Edges
    - Degree and Centrality
    - Clustering and Cohesion
    - Structural Holes and Brokerage
    
- Aggregating Social Networks
    - Group-level Analysis
    - Blockmodelling
    - Community Detection
    - Role Analysis
    
- Applications of Social Network Analysis
    - Organizational Analysis
    - Marketing Analysis
    - Political Analysis
    - Health Analysis
    
In summary, this unit will provide an overview of social network analysis, its key concepts, and the different types of social networks. Additionally, we will learn about the modelling and aggregating of social network data, and explore its applications in various fields.
