### Design for the notes of the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Here are some tips on how to design effective notes for the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization:

1. Use headings and subheadings to organize your notes. This will make it easier to find information later and to review the material.

2. Include diagrams and illustrations to help you visualize the concepts. You can draw these yourself or find them online, but make sure they are relevant to the content.

3. Use bullet points and short sentences to keep your notes concise and easy to read. Avoid long paragraphs and complex sentences.

4. Use abbreviations and symbols to save time and space. For example, you can use "w/" for "with" or "≈" for "approximately".

5. Summarize the key points at the end of each section. This will help you to review the material later and to remember the most important concepts.

6. Use color coding to highlight important information. For example, you can use red for definitions, blue for examples, and green for key concepts.

7. Leave plenty of white space on the page to make your notes easier to read. Don't cram too much information onto one page.

8. Review your notes regularly to help you remember the material. This will also help you to identify any gaps in your understanding.

By following these tips, you can create effective and efficient notes for the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization. Good luck with your studies!
