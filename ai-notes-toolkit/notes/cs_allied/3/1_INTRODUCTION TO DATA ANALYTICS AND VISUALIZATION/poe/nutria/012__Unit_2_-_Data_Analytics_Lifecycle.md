
## Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. The data analytics lifecycle is a process that is used to analyze data in order to make meaningful decisions. It typically involves several steps, including data collection, data preparation, data analysis, and interpretation of results.

2. Data collection is the process of gathering data from various sources. This data can be collected from internal or external sources, such as databases, surveys, and online sources.

3. Data preparation is the process of cleaning and formatting the data in order to make it easier to analyze. This step typically involves removing outliers, transforming the data into a suitable format, and creating additional variables.

4. Data analysis is the process of using statistical methods and algorithms to uncover insights from the data. This step typically involves exploring the data, identifying patterns, and testing hypotheses.

5. Interpretation of results is the process of making sense of the insights that have been uncovered. This step typically involves communicating the results to stakeholders and making decisions based on the findings.
