
### Stochastic Search Methods

Stochastic search methods are a class of algorithms used to solve optimization problems. These methods are based on random sampling and are used to find the optimal solution to a given problem.

* **Monte Carlo Methods**: Monte Carlo methods are a type of stochastic search methods that use random sampling to find an optimal solution. Monte Carlo methods are used in many fields, such as finance, engineering, and data science. 

* **Simulated Annealing**: Simulated annealing is an optimization technique that uses random sampling to find an optimal solution. It is based on the physical process of annealing, which is used to reduce the energy of a system.

* **Genetic Algorithms**: Genetic algorithms are a type of stochastic search methods that use random sampling to find an optimal solution. These algorithms are based on the principles of natural selection and are used to solve complex problems.

* **Particle Swarm Optimization**: Particle swarm optimization is a stochastic search method that uses random sampling to find an optimal solution. It is based on the behavior of swarms of animals, such as birds or bees.
