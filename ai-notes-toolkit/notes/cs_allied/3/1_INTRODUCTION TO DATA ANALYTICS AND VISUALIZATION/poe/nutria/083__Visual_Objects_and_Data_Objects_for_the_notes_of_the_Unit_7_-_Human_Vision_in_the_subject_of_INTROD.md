

### Visual Objects and Data Objects

1. Visual objects are physical entities that can be seen and experienced with the human senses. Examples of visual objects include plants, animals, people, buildings, and other physical objects.

2. Data objects are representations of physical objects in a digital format. Data objects are usually stored in a structured format, such as a database, and can be manipulated and analyzed with the help of software. Examples of data objects include images, videos, audio files, text documents, spreadsheets, and other digital formats.
