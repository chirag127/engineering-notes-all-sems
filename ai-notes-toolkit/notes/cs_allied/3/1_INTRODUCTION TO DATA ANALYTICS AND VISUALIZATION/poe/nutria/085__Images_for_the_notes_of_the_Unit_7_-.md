
### Images for the Notes of Unit 7

1. Images are a powerful tool for conveying information quickly and effectively.
2. They can be used to illustrate concepts, explain processes, and show relationships between different elements.
3. Images can also be used to create a visual impact and add interest to a presentation or document.
4. When creating images for notes, it is important to consider the purpose of the image and the audience.
5. Images should be clear, concise, and relevant to the topic.
6. It is also important to consider the size and resolution of the image, as well as the format in which it will be displayed.
7. Images should also be properly credited and include copyright information, if applicable.
