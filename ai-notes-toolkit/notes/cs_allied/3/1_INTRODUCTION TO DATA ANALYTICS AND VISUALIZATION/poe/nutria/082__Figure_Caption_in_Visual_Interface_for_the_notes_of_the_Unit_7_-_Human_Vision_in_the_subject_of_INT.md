
### Figure Caption in Visual Interface

1. A figure caption is a brief description of a figure, typically included beneath the visual representation in a report, paper, or other document. 
2. Figure captions are used to enable the reader to better understand the information presented in the figure. 
3. The purpose of a figure caption is to provide additional context or explanation for the figure, and to help the reader understand the information presented in the figure. 
4. A good figure caption should be concise and clear, and should provide enough information to enable the reader to understand and interpret the figure without having to refer to other sources. 
5. When writing a figure caption, it is important to include the figure number, title, source, and any other relevant information. 
6. Additionally, it is important to use consistent formatting for all figure captions in a document. 
7. Figure captions are a valuable tool for conveying information in a visual format, and can be used to enhance the understanding of a document.
