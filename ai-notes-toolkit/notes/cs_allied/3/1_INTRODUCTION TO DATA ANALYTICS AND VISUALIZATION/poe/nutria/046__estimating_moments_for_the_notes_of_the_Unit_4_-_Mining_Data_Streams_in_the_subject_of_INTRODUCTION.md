
### Estimating Moments for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the Subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Estimation of moments refers to the process of calculating certain statistical properties of a given data set. 
2. The most commonly used moments are the mean, variance, and standard deviation. 
3. These moments can be calculated from a sample or from the entire population. 
4. The sample moments are usually estimated using the sample mean, sample variance, and sample standard deviation. 
5. The population moments are usually estimated using the population mean, population variance, and population standard deviation. 
6. In the context of data streams, moments can be estimated using various techniques such as sliding window, reservoir sampling, and sketching. 
7. Sliding window is a method of estimating moments over a fixed window size. 
8. Reservoir sampling is a method of estimating moments over a randomly chosen window size. 
9. Sketching is a method of estimating moments over a small subset of data points. 
10. Estimating moments from data streams is an important task in data mining and can be used to identify patterns and trends in the data.
