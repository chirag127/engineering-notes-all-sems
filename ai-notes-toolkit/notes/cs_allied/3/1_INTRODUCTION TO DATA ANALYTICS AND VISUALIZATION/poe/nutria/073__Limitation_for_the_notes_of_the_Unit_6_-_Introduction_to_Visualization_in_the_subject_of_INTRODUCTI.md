
### Limitation for the Notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the Subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Visualizations are limited by the data used to create them. If the data is incomplete or inaccurate, the visualizations created from it will be similarly limited.

2. Visualizations can be subject to misinterpretation if the audience is not familiar with the data or the techniques used to create the visualization.

3. Visualizations can be difficult to interpret when there is too much data or when data points are too close together.

4. Visualizations can be difficult to interpret when the data is not presented in a logical order.

5. Visualizations can be difficult to interpret when the data points are too far apart.

6. Visualizations can be difficult to interpret when the data points are not clearly labeled.

7. Visualizations can be difficult to interpret when the data is presented in multiple formats.

8. Visualizations can be difficult to interpret when the data is presented in multiple colors.
