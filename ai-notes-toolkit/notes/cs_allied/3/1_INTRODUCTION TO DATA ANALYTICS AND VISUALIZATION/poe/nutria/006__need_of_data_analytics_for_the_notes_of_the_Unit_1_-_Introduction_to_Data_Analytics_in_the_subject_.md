
### Need of Data Analytics for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the Subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Data analytics is becoming increasingly important in today's world. It is used in a variety of fields, from business to healthcare, and is becoming an essential tool for understanding and making decisions. In the field of Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization, data analytics is essential for understanding the data that is collected and making informed decisions.

1. Data analytics can help to identify patterns and trends in data that would otherwise be difficult to spot.
2. Data analytics can help to identify relationships between different variables and uncover correlations.
3. Data analytics can help to identify anomalies in data, such as outliers or errors.
4. Data analytics can help to identify the most important factors that influence a certain outcome.
5. Data analytics can help to identify problems and opportunities in the data.
6. Data analytics can help to optimize processes and operations.
7. Data analytics can help to make predictions about future trends.
8. Data analytics can help to create more efficient and effective visualizations.
