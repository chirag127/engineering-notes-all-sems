

### Data Preparation for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the Subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Data preparation is the process of transforming data from its raw form into a format that is suitable for analysis.
2. It involves data cleaning, which is the process of removing or correcting inaccurate or incomplete data.
3. Data transformation is the process of converting data from one form to another. This includes scaling, normalizing, and aggregating data.
4. Data integration is the process of combining data from multiple sources into a single dataset.
5. Data enrichment is the process of adding additional data to existing datasets.
6. Data validation is the process of checking data for accuracy and consistency.
7. Data visualization is the process of creating visual representations of data, such as charts and graphs.
8. Data mining is the process of discovering patterns and relationships in data.
9. Data warehousing is the process of storing and managing large amounts of data.
