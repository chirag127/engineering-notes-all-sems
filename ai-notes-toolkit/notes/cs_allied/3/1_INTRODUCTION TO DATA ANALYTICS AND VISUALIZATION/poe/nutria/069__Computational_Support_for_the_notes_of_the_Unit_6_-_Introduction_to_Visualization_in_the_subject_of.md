
### Computational Support for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Computational support for data visualization involves using various computer algorithms and software tools to analyze and interpret data in order to generate meaningful visual representations.
2. Visualization is an important part of data analysis, as it allows us to quickly identify patterns and trends in data that may not be apparent in the raw data.
3. Data visualization tools can help to identify correlations between variables, spot outliers, and provide an overall better understanding of the data.
4. Some of the most popular data visualization tools include Tableau, Microsoft Power BI, and QlikView.
5. Data visualization can also be used to create interactive and engaging visualizations, such as interactive maps and charts.
6. It is important to remember that data visualization is only as good as the data that is being visualized, so it is important to ensure that the data is accurate and up-to-date.
