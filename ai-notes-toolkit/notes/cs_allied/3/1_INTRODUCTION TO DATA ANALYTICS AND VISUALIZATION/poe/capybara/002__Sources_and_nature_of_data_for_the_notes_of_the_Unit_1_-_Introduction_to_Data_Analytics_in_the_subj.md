### Sources and nature of data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

Data analytics is the process of examining and interpreting data to draw conclusions and insights from it. For this process, it is important to have access to data that is reliable, relevant and accurate. In this unit, we will explore the sources and nature of data that is used in data analytics.

Here are the different sources and nature of data used in data analytics:

1. Primary Data Sources: Primary data sources are the original sources of data that have not been previously collected. These sources can be surveys, interviews, observations, experiments, or any other method of collecting data directly from the source.

2. Secondary Data Sources: Secondary data sources are data that have already been collected by someone else. These sources can be internal or external to the organization. Examples of secondary data sources include government data, industry reports, news articles, and social media activity.

3. Big Data: Big data refers to large volumes of data that are generated at high velocities and in a variety of formats. This data is often unstructured and requires specialized tools and techniques to analyze it.

4. Open Data: Open data refers to data that is freely available to the public. This data is often published by government organizations, non-profits, and other entities with the goal of increasing transparency and accountability.

5. Streaming Data: Streaming data refers to real-time data that is generated continuously. This data is often used in industries such as finance, healthcare, and transportation to monitor and respond to events as they happen.

Data can also be categorized based on its nature:

1. Structured Data: Structured data refers to data that is organized in a specific format such as a database, spreadsheet or table. This data is easy to analyze using traditional methods such as SQL queries.

2. Unstructured Data: Unstructured data refers to data that does not have a specific format. This data can include text, images, audio, and video. Analyzing unstructured data requires specialized techniques such as natural language processing and machine learning.

In conclusion, data analytics relies on the availability of reliable, relevant and accurate data. Data can come from a variety of sources such as primary and secondary data, big data, open data, and streaming data. Data can also be categorized based on its nature as structured or unstructured. Understanding the sources and nature of data is important for effective data analytics.
