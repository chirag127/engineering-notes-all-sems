### Introduction to Streams Concepts

In Unit 4 of the subject Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization, we will be exploring the topic of Mining Data Streams. Before we dive into the technical aspects of this topic, it is important to have a clear understanding of streams concepts. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- A stream is a sequence of data items that are generated continuously over time.
- Streaming data is typically unbounded, meaning that there is no fixed amount of data to be processed.
- Streams can be either unstructured or structured. Unstructured streams contain data that does not conform to a specific schema, while structured streams have a defined schema.
- Streams can be processed in real-time or near real-time, which means that the processing must happen quickly and efficiently.
- Stream processing involves performing computations on the data items in the stream as they arrive, rather than storing the data and processing it later.
- Stream processing can be used for a variety of applications, including fraud detection, real-time analytics, and monitoring systems.

By understanding these basic concepts of streams, we can better appreciate the challenges and opportunities presented by mining data streams. In the upcoming units, we will delve deeper into the technical aspects of stream processing and explore various algorithms and techniques for mining data streams.
