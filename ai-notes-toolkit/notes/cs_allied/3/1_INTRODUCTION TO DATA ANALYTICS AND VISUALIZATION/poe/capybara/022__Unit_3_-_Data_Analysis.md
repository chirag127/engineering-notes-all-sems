## Unit 3 - Data Analysis

In this unit, we will delve into the world of data analysis. We will cover various methods and techniques used to analyze and interpret data. The following are the key topics that will be covered in this unit:

1. Data Collection: 
   - Understand the process of collecting data and its importance in analysis.
   - Learn about different data collection methods, such as surveys, interviews, and observations.
   
2. Data Cleaning: 
   - Learn how to identify and handle missing data.
   - Understand the importance of data consistency and how to handle inconsistencies.
   - Familiarize yourself with different data cleaning techniques, such as imputation and outlier detection.
   
3. Data Visualization: 
   - Learn how to represent data through various visualizations, such as graphs, charts, and maps.
   - Understand the importance of visualizations in conveying complex information.
   - Familiarize yourself with different visualization tools and techniques, such as matplotlib and Tableau.
   
4. Descriptive Statistics: 
   - Learn how to summarize and describe data using measures such as mean, median, and mode.
   - Understand the concept of variability and its measures, such as range and standard deviation.
   
5. Inferential Statistics: 
   - Learn how to make inferences about a population based on a sample.
   - Understand the concept of probability and its applications in statistical inference.
   - Familiarize yourself with different statistical tests, such as t-tests and ANOVA.
   
6. Regression Analysis: 
   - Learn how to model and analyze relationships between variables.
   - Understand the concept of correlation and its measures, such as the correlation coefficient.
   - Familiarize yourself with different types of regression models, such as linear and logistic regression.

By the end of this unit, you should have a solid foundation in data analysis and be able to apply the techniques and methods covered to real-world data. Good luck!
