### Computational Support for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Visualization is the graphical representation of information and data. It makes complex data more understandable, spot patterns and insights easily.
2. The basic steps in visualization are:
- Gather the data
- Prepare the data
- Choose the right visualization
- Design the visualization
- Share or present the visualization
3. Some commonly used visualization types are:
- Bar charts: To compare categories
- Line charts: To show trends over time
- Pie charts: To show proportions of a whole
- Scatter plots: To show relationship between two variables
- Heat maps: To show data density
- Maps: To display geographical data
4. Tools for data visualization:
- Excel: Inbuilt charts and graphs
- Tableau: Interactive data visualization software with drag and drop features
- Power BI: Business analytics service by Microsoft for data modeling and visualization
- Seaborn and Matplotlib: Python libraries for data visualization
- D3.js: JavaScript library for creating custom interactive data visualizations
