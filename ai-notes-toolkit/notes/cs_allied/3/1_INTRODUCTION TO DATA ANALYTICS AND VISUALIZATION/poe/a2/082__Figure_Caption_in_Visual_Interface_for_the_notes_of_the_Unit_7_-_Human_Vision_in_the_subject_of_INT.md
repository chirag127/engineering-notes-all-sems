### Figure Caption in Visual Interface for the notes of the Unit 7 - Human Vision

1. Figure Captions provide context to the visualizations and images in the interface. They describe what the visualization or image is depicting.
2. Effective Figure Captions are brief but descriptive. They should explain the key insights or takeaways from the visualization or image.
3. Figure Captions are important for accessibility as screen readers use them to describe the visualization or image to users with visual impairments. Without a descriptive caption, the visualization or image would be useless for such users.
4. Misleading or poorly written Figure Captions can confuse the readers and miscommunicate the insights from the data. Hence, it is important to be accurate and concise while writing the captions.
5. The placement of the Figure Caption is ideally right below the visualization or image. This ensures easy association of the caption with the corresponding visual element.

The above points cover the key things to keep in mind regarding Figure Captions in visual interfaces. They enhance the accessibility, understandability, and communication of insights through visualization and images. With accurate and concise captions, visual interfaces can be more useful and impactful.
