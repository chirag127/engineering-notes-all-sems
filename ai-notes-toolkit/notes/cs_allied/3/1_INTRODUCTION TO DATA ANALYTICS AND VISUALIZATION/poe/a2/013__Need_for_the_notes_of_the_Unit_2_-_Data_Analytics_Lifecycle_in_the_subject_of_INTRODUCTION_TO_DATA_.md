### Need for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

1. Data Analytics Lifecycle refers to the process of analyzing data to discover useful information and derive insights from it. It consists of multiple steps which are iterative in nature.
2. The steps in the Data Analytics Lifecycle are:
- data collection: gathering relevant data from various sources
- data preparation: cleaning, organizing and structuring the data
- data exploration: summarizing the data and finding patterns
- data modeling: applying models to analyze the data
- evaluation and validation: evaluating model performance and validity of results
- deployment: implementing the analytical results to derive business value
3. Going through the lifecycle multiple times by refining the steps leads to optimal results. The insights and experiences gained in one iteration can feed into the next iteration.
4. Understanding the Data Analytics Lifecycle is essential to comprehend how data analytics projects are executed and to apply the concepts and techniques effectively to solve problems. It provides a systematic approach to leveraging data to enable data-driven decisions.
