# INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Data Analytics: The process of examining data and discovering patterns to make better decisions. It involves cleaning, transforming, modeling and mining the data to uncover hidden patterns, unknown correlations, market trends, customer preferences and other useful information.

2. Data Visualization: The presentation of data in a pictorial or graphical format. It makes data more visually appealing, easier to understand and spot trends. Charts, graphs, maps, infographics, etc are used to represent data visually.

3. Importance:
- Gain business insights: Analyze data to understand customer behavior, sales patterns, fraud, etc and make strategic decisions.
- Spot trends: Identify trends and patterns in data to take actions proactively.
- Communicate insights: Convey insights and findings to stakeholders through data visualization.
- Automate processes: Use machine learning and AI for automation of data analytics and data visualization processes.

4. Applications:
- Marketing: Understand customer segments, buying patterns, effectiveness of campaigns, etc.
- Sales: Analyze sales data to optimize revenue, forecast sales, understand peaks and drops, etc.
- Finance: Detect fraud, forecast revenue and expenses, assess risks, etc.
- Healthcare: Predict disease outbreaks, personalized patient care, optimize processes, etc.
- Transportation: Optimize routes, forecast demand and delays, track vehicles, etc.

5. Tools: Excel, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, Python, R, SAS, etc.
