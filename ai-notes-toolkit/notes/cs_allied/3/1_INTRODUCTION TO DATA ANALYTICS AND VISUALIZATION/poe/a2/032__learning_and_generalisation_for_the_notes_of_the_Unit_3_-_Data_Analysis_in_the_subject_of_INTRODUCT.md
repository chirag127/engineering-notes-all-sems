### Learning and Generalisation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Data Analysis

1. Data Analysis is the process of inspecting, cleansing, transforming and modeling data with the goal of discovering useful information, informing conclusions and supporting decision making.
2. Data Analysis is used to uncover patterns, trends, correlations or other insights in data that can provide benefits to an organization.
3. The key steps in data analysis are:
- Gathering data: Obtaining raw data that needs to be analyzed. This can come from various sources such as observations, experiments, surveys, etc.
- Cleaning data: Identifying and correcting errors or other issues with the data to make it accurate and consistent. This may involve filtering out unwanted data or imputing missing data.
- Exploring data: Summarizing the key characteristics of the data and identifying patterns or anomalies. This is to gain an initial sense of the information and determine if additional data is needed.
- Modeling data: Applying statistical or machine learning models to the data to discover patterns and relationships that can be used to solve problems or gain insights. Different modeling techniques may be tested and refined.
- Interpreting results: Evaluating the output from data analysis and determining key findings, insights or decisions that can be made from the results. The implications and limitations are also considered.
4. Generalization refers to using the insights from an analysis of sample data to infer properties of an entire population. However, there are risks of overgeneralization or drawing inaccurate conclusions if the sample is not representative. Data analysis results should be validated and interpreted cautiously before generalizing to a wider population.
