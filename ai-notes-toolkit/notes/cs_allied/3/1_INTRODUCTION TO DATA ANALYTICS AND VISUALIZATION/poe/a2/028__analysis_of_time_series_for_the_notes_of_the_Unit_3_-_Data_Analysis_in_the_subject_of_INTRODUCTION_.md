### Analysis of Time Series

1. Time series data: Data collected over regular time intervals forms time series data. Examples include stock prices, weather data, sales data, etc.
2. Components of time series: A time series can be decomposed into trend, seasonal variation, and noise/random variation.
- Trend: Long-term increase or decrease in the data
- Seasonal variation: Fluctuations that occur periodically every year/month/week, etc. For example, sales may be higher in December due to Christmas season.
- Noise/random variation: Random fluctuations in the data due to various unpredictable factors.
3. Time series analysis: The analysis of time series data to understand the components and patterns in the data. Some of the key techniques used are:
- Smoothing: Reducing noise/random variations to identify trend/seasonal components
- Decomposition: Separating the time series into trend, seasonal, and noise components
- Forecasting: Predicting future values based on the patterns in the historical data
- Anomaly detection: Identifying anomalies/outliers in the usual pattern of the time series data
