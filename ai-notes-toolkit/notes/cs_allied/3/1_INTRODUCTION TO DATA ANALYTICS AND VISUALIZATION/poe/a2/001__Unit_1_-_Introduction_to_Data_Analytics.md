## Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

1. What is Data Analytics?
- Data Analytics is the process of examining data and identifying patterns to discover useful information and insights to help make better decisions.
- It involves applying analytical techniques and tools to raw data and transforming it into meaningful information.
- The ultimate goal is to discover hidden patterns, unknown correlations, market trends, customer preferences, and other information that can provide competitive advantages.

2. Why is Data Analytics Important?
- It helps identify opportunities and risks. By analyzing data, businesses can uncover hidden patterns and insights to spot market opportunities and potential risks.
- It enables faster and more accurate decisions. With data-driven insights, organizations can make faster and more accurate decisions on key business matters.
- It optimizes key business metrics. Data Analytics can help optimize key metrics such as increasing revenue, reducing costs, improving operational efficiency, enhancing customer experience, etc.
- It provides a competitive advantage. In today's data-driven world, data analytics can provide organizations with a competitive advantage to outperform competitors.

3. Data Analytics Process
- The key steps in the data analytics process are:
- Identifying the business problem or goal.
- Collecting and organizing the relevant data.
- Cleaning and preparing the data.
- Applying data analysis and data mining techniques.
- Interpreting the results and drawing conclusions.
- Taking action and measuring the impact.
