### Issues for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Choosing appropriate visualization technique: It is important to choose the right visualization technique to represent the data. Choosing a wrong technique can lead to misunderstanding or misinterpretation of the data. Some factors to consider while choosing a visualization technique are:
- Type of data: Categorical, numerical, geospatial, temporal, etc.
- Number of variables: Univariate, bivariate or multivariate data
- Purpose of visualization: Exploration, communication or both
- Audience: Experts or general public

2. Designing effective visualizations: Effective data visualizations should be:
- Clear: They should communicate the insights or patterns in the data clearly to the audience. The visual elements should not be confusing or cluttered.
- Accurate: The visualization should represent the data accurately without any distortion or bias.
- Visually appealing: The visual design including colors, fonts, white space, etc. should be pleasant and engaging for the audience.
- Purposeful: The visualization should serve the purpose for which it was created, whether exploration, communication or both.

3. Avoiding visualization pitfalls: Some common mistakes to avoid when creating data visualizations are:
- Using 3D effects just for attracting attention rather than adding value
- Using too much ink or visual elements which clutter the visualization
- Misusing scales (linear vs log), axes and legends which can distort the data
- Not considering colorblind users while choosing colors
- Not considering the limitations of the visualization software or tools which can restrict creativity
