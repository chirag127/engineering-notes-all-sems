### Various phases of data analytics lifecycle

Data analytics lifecycle is a framework that guides the process of transforming data into insights that can be used to achieve business goals. It consists of six phases that are based on the CRISP-DM methodology :

- **Business understanding**: This phase involves defining the problem or opportunity, identifying the objectives and requirements, and establishing the success criteria for the data analytics project.
- **Data understanding**: This phase involves collecting, exploring, and describing the data that is relevant to the problem or opportunity. It also involves assessing the quality, quantity, and availability of the data.
- **Data preparation**: This phase involves cleaning, transforming, and integrating the data into a suitable format for analysis. It also involves selecting, sampling, and partitioning the data for modeling and validation.
- **Modeling**: This phase involves applying various analytical techniques and algorithms to the data to discover patterns, relationships, and insights. It also involves selecting, testing, and comparing different models to find the best fit for the data and the problem.
- **Evaluation**: This phase involves evaluating the performance, accuracy, and validity of the models and the insights. It also involves assessing the alignment of the models and the insights with the business objectives and the success criteria.
- **Deployment**: This phase involves deploying the models and the insights into the operational environment, where they can be used to support decision making and action. It also involves monitoring, maintaining, and updating the models and the insights as the data and the business context change.

These six phases are not necessarily sequential or linear. They can be iterative, parallel, or cyclical, depending on the nature and scope of the data analytics project. The data analytics lifecycle can be adapted and customized to suit different types of data, problems, and goals.
