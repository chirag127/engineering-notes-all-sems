### Real Time Sentiment Analysis

- Real time sentiment analysis is a machine learning technique that automatically recognizes and extracts the sentiment in a text whenever it occurs .
- Sentiment is the subjective opinion or attitude expressed by a person towards a topic, such as a brand, product, service, event, or person.
- Sentiment analysis can be used to analyze live social and online feeds, such as comments, posts, reviews, tweets, chats, etc., and derive insights from the text for various applications, such as customer service, marketing, reputation management, product development, etc .
- The process of sentiment analysis follows these four steps:
  - Breaking down the text into components: sentences, phrases, tokens, and parts of speech.
  - Identifying each phrase and component.
  - Assigning a sentiment score to each phrase with plus or minus points.
  - Combining scores for a final sentiment analysis.
- The sentiment score can be either binary (positive or negative), ternary (positive, negative, or neutral), or multiclass (such as happy, sad, angry, etc.).
- The challenges of real time sentiment analysis include dealing with large and unstructured data, handling noise and ambiguity, capturing context and sarcasm, and adapting to different domains and languages.
- The techniques of real time sentiment analysis include rule-based methods, which use predefined rules and lexicons to assign sentiment scores, and machine learning methods, which use supervised or unsupervised algorithms to learn from labeled or unlabeled data.
- Some examples of real time sentiment analysis tools are MonkeyLearn, Repustate, and Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Insights  .
