### Need of data analytics

- Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to gain insights and support decision making.
- Data analytics is needed for various reasons, such as:

  - To understand the patterns, trends, and relationships in data and derive meaningful information from it.
  - To improve the efficiency, quality, and performance of processes, products, and services by optimizing the use of resources and minimizing the risks and costs.
  - To enhance the customer satisfaction, loyalty, and retention by providing personalized and customized solutions and recommendations.
  - To innovate and create new products, services, and business models by discovering new opportunities and solving complex problems.
  - To comply with the regulatory and ethical standards and ensure the security and privacy of data and information.
  - To gain a competitive advantage and increase the market share and profitability by leveraging the data and information assets.
