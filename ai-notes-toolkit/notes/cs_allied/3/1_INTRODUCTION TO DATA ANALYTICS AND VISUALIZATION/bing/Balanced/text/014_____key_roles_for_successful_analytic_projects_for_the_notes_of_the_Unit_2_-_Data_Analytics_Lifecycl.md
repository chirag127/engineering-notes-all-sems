### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

According to the Data Analytics Lifecycle, a successful analytic project requires the collaboration of different roles and skills. Some of the key roles are:

- **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also approves the budget, scope, and timeline of the project and ensures alignment with the strategic goals of the organization.
- **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication and collaboration among the project team and stakeholders, manages the risks and issues, and monitors the progress and performance of the project .
- **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized . A business user can also provide feedback and validation to the project team throughout the project lifecycle.
- **Business intelligence analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can collect, analyze, and report on data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, or web applications. This person can also create and maintain dashboards, reports, and visualizations to present the data in an understandable and actionable way . A business intelligence analyst can also help the project team to define the key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics to measure the success of the project.
- **Database administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can design, implement, and maintain the databases that store and manage the data for the project. This person can also ensure the security, integrity, availability, and performance of the databases and troubleshoot any issues that may arise . A database administrator can also help the project team to access and query the data from the databases using various tools and languages.
- **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can build, test, and deploy the data pipelines and workflows that transform, integrate, and process the data from various sources and formats. This person can also use various tools and frameworks to handle big data, such as Hadoop, Spark, or Kafka . A data engineer can also help the project team to ensure the quality, reliability, and scalability of the data and the data pipelines.
- **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced analytical techniques, such as machine learning, statistics, or optimization, to discover insights, patterns, or predictions from the data. This person can also use various tools and languages, such as Python, R, or SQL, to perform data analysis and modeling . A data scientist can also help the project team to formulate and test hypotheses, evaluate and interpret the results, and communicate the findings and recommendations.

These roles are not mutually exclusive and may overlap or vary depending on the size, scope, and complexity of the project. However, having a clear understanding of the roles and responsibilities of each team member can help to ensure a successful analytic project.
