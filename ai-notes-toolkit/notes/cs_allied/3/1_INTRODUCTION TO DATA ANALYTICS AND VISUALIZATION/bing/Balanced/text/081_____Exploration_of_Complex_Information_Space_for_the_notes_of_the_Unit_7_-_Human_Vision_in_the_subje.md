### Exploration of Complex Information Space

- Complex information spaces are information spaces that are not only large but also heterogeneous in their structure, meaning that they contain different types of data, such as text, images, graphs, etc.
- Exploration of complex information spaces is the process of interacting with data in order to extract meaningful information, such as patterns, trends, outliers, etc. 
- Exploration of complex information spaces requires presentation techniques that provide intuitive orientation aids and navigation techniques, such as overviews, zooming, filtering, etc. 
- Exploration of complex information spaces also requires information visualization techniques that represent data in graphical or symbolic forms, such as maps, charts, diagrams, etc.  
- Exploration of complex information spaces can benefit from information technology (IT) applications that increase safety, efficiency, performance, or decrease cost, such as vehicle health management, automated assembly, or automated operations.
- Exploration of complex information spaces is relevant for human space exploration, as it can help to design, operate, and analyze space missions, such as the Apollo missions, the International Space Station, or the Mars exploration program.
