### Analysis of Time Series

- A time series is a sequence of data points indexed or listed in time order, such as the daily closing value of a stock market index, the monthly sales of a product, or the yearly temperature of a city.
- Time series analysis is the process of analyzing a time series to extract meaningful statistics and characteristics of the data, such as trends, seasonality, cycles, and irregular fluctuations  .
- Time series analysis can be used to better clean, understand, and forecast the data, as well as to identify patterns, anomalies, and causal relationships .
- Time series analysis may use a range of models that can process the time series, such as moving averages, exponential smoothing, autoregressive integrated moving average (ARIMA), and neural networks.
- Time series analysis can be classified into two types: descriptive and inferential.
  - Descriptive analysis summarizes the main features and patterns of the data using graphs, tables, and statistics, such as mean, variance, autocorrelation, and spectral density.
  - Inferential analysis tests hypotheses and estimates parameters using statistical methods, such as regression, correlation, and hypothesis testing.
- Time series analysis can be applied to various domains and fields, such as economics, finance, marketing, engineering, medicine, and social sciences  .
