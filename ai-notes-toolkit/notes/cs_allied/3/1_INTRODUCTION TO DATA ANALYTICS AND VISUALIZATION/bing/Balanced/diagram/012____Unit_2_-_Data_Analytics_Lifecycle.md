## Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

The data analytics lifecycle is a framework for planning and executing data analysis projects. It consists of six phases:

1. **Discovery**: This phase involves identifying the business problem, defining the objectives and scope of the analysis, and gathering the relevant data sources and stakeholders.
2. **Data preparation**: This phase involves cleaning, transforming, and integrating the data into a suitable format for analysis. It also involves exploring the data to understand its characteristics, quality, and distribution.
3. **Model planning**: This phase involves selecting the appropriate analytical techniques and tools to address the business problem, such as regression, classification, clustering, or visualization. It also involves designing and testing the analytical models and validating their assumptions and results.
4. **Model building**: This phase involves applying the analytical models to the data and evaluating their performance and accuracy. It also involves refining and optimizing the models and documenting their outcomes and limitations.
5. **Communicating results**: This phase involves presenting and communicating the results and insights of the analysis to the stakeholders and decision-makers. It also involves explaining the methodology, assumptions, and limitations of the analysis and providing recommendations and action plans based on the results.
6. **Operationalizing**: This phase involves deploying and implementing the analytical models and solutions into the business processes and systems. It also involves monitoring and evaluating the impact and effectiveness of the solutions and updating and maintaining them as needed.
