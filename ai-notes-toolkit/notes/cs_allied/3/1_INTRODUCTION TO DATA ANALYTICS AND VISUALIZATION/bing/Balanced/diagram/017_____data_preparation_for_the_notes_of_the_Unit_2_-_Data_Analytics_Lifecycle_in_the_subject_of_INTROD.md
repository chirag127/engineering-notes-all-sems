### Data Preparation

Data preparation is the process of transforming raw data into a format that is suitable for analysis and modeling. Data preparation is an essential stage in the data analytics lifecycle, which consists of six phases: business understanding, data understanding, data preparation, modeling, evaluation, and deployment  .

Some of the tasks involved in data preparation are:

- Accessing, loading, and structuring data from various sources, such as databases, files, web services, etc.
- Purging, filtering, and aggregating data to remove outliers, duplicates, errors, and irrelevant records.
- Unifying, joining, and merging data from different sources or tables to create a single view of the data.
- Adjusting, transforming, and enriching data to standardize formats, handle missing values, create new variables, and apply business rules.
- Exploring, profiling, and visualizing data to understand its characteristics, quality, and distribution.

Data preparation requires a scalable and flexible platform that can handle large and complex data sets, such as an analytical sandbox. An analytical sandbox is a dedicated environment where data analysts and data scientists can process, manipulate, and experiment with data without affecting the operational systems or the production data warehouse.

Data preparation is an iterative and interactive process that involves frequent feedback and validation from the business stakeholders and the data experts. Data preparation can consume up to 80% of the time and resources in a data analytics project , but it is crucial for ensuring the accuracy, reliability, and usefulness of the analytical results.
