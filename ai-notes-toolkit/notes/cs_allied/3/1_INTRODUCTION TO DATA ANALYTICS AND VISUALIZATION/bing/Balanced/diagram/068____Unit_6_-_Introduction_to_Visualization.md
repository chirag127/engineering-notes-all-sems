## Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization

- Visualization is the process of creating and displaying graphical representations of data or information.
- Visualization can help users to explore, analyze, communicate, and understand data or information more effectively and efficiently.
- Visualization can be classified into different types based on the purpose, audience, data, and technique of the visualization.
- Some common types of visualization are:

  - **Charts**: Charts are graphical representations of numerical data, such as bar charts, pie charts, line charts, scatter plots, etc. Charts can show trends, patterns, distributions, comparisons, and relationships among data values.
  - **Maps**: Maps are graphical representations of spatial data, such as geographic locations, regions, routes, etc. Maps can show the distribution, density, distance, and direction of spatial data, as well as overlay other data attributes on the map.
  - **Diagrams**: Diagrams are graphical representations of abstract or conceptual data, such as processes, structures, systems, hierarchies, etc. Diagrams can show the components, connections, flows, and functions of abstract or conceptual data, such as flowcharts, network diagrams, tree diagrams, etc.
  - **Infographics**: Infographics are graphical representations of complex or diverse data, such as facts, statistics, stories, etc. Infographics can combine different types of visualization, such as charts, maps, diagrams, icons, text, etc., to convey a message or a story to the audience.
  - **Dashboards**: Dashboards are graphical representations of multiple data sources, such as key performance indicators, metrics, alerts, etc. Dashboards can provide an overview, summary, or snapshot of the data sources, as well as allow users to interact with the data, such as filtering, drilling down, zooming in, etc.

- Visualization can be created and displayed using different tools and platforms, such as software applications, web browsers, mobile devices, etc.
- Visualization can be evaluated and improved based on different criteria, such as accuracy, clarity, aesthetics, usability, etc.
