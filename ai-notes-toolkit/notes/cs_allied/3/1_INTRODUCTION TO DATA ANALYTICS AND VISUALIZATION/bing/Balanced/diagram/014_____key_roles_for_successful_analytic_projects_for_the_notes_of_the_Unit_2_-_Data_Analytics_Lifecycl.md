### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

According to the web sources   , some of the key roles for successful analytic projects are:

- **Project sponsor**: This is the person who initiates and funds the project, provides the business requirements and defines the core problem to be solved. The project sponsor also approves the project scope, timeline and budget, and ensures the alignment of the project with the strategic goals of the organization.
- **Project manager**: This is the person who oversees the execution of the project, coordinates the project team and stakeholders, monitors the progress and quality of the project deliverables, and manages the risks and issues that may arise during the project lifecycle. The project manager also communicates the project status, achievements and challenges to the project sponsor and other relevant parties.
- **Business user**: This is the person who understands the domain area and the business context of the project, and usually benefits from the results of the project. The business user can consult and advise the project team on the value and relevance of the project outputs, and how they can be operationalized and applied in the real world. The business user can also provide feedback and validation on the project outcomes and recommendations.
- **Business intelligence analyst**: This is the person who collects, cleans, transforms and analyzes the data related to the project, using various tools and techniques such as SQL, Excel, Tableau, Power BI, etc. The business intelligence analyst can also create and maintain data models, dashboards, reports and visualizations that support the project objectives and insights.
- **Database administrator (DBA)**: This is the person who manages the data sources, databases and data warehouses that store and provide the data for the project. The DBA is responsible for ensuring the security, availability, performance and integrity of the data, as well as designing and implementing data backup and recovery strategies. The DBA can also assist the project team with data access, extraction and loading processes.
- **Data engineer**: This is the person who builds, tests and maintains the data pipelines and infrastructure that enable the ingestion, processing and delivery of large-scale and complex data for the project. The data engineer uses various programming languages, frameworks and platforms such as Python, Java, Spark, Hadoop, AWS, etc. to create and optimize data workflows and architectures that support the project needs and challenges.
- **Data scientist**: This is the person who applies advanced statistical and machine learning methods and algorithms to the data, in order to discover patterns, generate insights and make predictions that address the project problem and questions. The data scientist uses various programming languages, libraries and tools such as R, Python, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, etc. to create and evaluate data science models and solutions that support the project goals and outcomes. The data scientist can also communicate and present the data science findings and recommendations to the project team and stakeholders, using clear and compelling narratives and visualizations.
