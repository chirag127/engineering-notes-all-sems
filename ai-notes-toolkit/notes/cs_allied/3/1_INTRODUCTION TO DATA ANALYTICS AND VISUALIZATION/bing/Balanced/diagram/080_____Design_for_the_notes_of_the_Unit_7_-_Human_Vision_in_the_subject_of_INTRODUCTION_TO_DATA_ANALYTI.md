# Unit 7 - Human Vision

## Introduction
- Human vision is the process of perceiving and interpreting visual information from the environment.
- Human vision is influenced by biological, psychological, and environmental factors.
- Human vision is relevant for data analytics and visualization because it affects how we perceive, understand, and communicate data.

## The Eye and the Visual System
- The eye is the main organ of vision that captures light and converts it into electrical signals.
- The eye has several components, such as the cornea, the iris, the pupil, the lens, the retina, and the optic nerve.
- The retina is the inner layer of the eye that contains photoreceptors, which are cells that respond to light.
- There are two types of photoreceptors: rods and cones.
  - Rods are more sensitive to light and work better in low-light conditions, but they cannot distinguish colors.
  - Cones are less sensitive to light and work better in bright-light conditions, but they can distinguish colors.
- The optic nerve is the bundle of nerve fibers that carries the signals from the retina to the brain.
- The visual system is the part of the brain that processes and interprets the signals from the eye.
- The visual system has several regions, such as the primary visual cortex, the secondary visual cortex, and the association areas.
- The primary visual cortex is the first region that receives the signals from the optic nerve and performs basic processing, such as edge detection and orientation.
- The secondary visual cortex is the second region that receives the signals from the primary visual cortex and performs more complex processing, such as shape recognition and motion detection.
- The association areas are the regions that receive the signals from the secondary visual cortex and perform higher-level processing, such as object recognition and face recognition.

## Visual Perception and Cognition
- Visual perception is the process of interpreting and understanding the visual information from the visual system.
- Visual perception is influenced by several factors, such as attention, memory, context, and expectations.
- Attention is the process of selecting and focusing on certain aspects of the visual information and ignoring others.
- Memory is the process of storing and retrieving the visual information for later use.
- Context is the process of using the background knowledge and the situation to interpret the visual information.
- Expectations are the process of using the prior beliefs and the goals to interpret the visual information.
- Visual cognition is the process of applying the visual perception to perform various tasks, such as reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, and communication.
- Visual cognition is influenced by several factors, such as domain knowledge, cognitive style, and cognitive load.
- Domain knowledge is the process of using the specific knowledge and the expertise to perform the tasks.
- Cognitive style is the process of using the preferred way of thinking and learning to perform the tasks.
- Cognitive load is the process of managing the amount and the complexity of the information to perform the tasks.

## Data Visualization and Human Vision
- Data visualization is the process of creating and using visual representations of data to communicate and explore data.
- Data visualization is based on the principles and the techniques of human vision, such as perception, cognition, attention, memory, context, and expectations.
- Data visualization aims to enhance the human vision by using various elements, such as color, shape, size, position, orientation, texture, and motion.
- Data visualization also aims to reduce the cognitive load by using various methods, such as aggregation, filtering, sorting, grouping, and highlighting.
- Data visualization can be classified into different types, such as tables, charts, graphs, maps, diagrams, and dashboards.
- Data visualization can be evaluated by using different criteria, such as accuracy, clarity, efficiency, effectiveness, and aesthetics.
