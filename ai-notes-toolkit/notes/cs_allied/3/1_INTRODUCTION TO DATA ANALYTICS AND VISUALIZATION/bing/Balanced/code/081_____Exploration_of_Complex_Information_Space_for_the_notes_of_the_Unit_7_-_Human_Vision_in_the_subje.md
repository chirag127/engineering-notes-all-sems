# Exploration of Complex Information Space

- Complex information spaces are information spaces that are not only large but also heterogeneous in their structure, meaning they contain different types of data and relationships.
- Exploration of complex information spaces is the process of interacting with data in order to extract meaningful information, such as patterns, trends, outliers, or insights .
- Exploration of complex information spaces requires presentation techniques that provide intuitive orientation aids and navigation techniques, such as:
  - Information visualizations that represent data in graphical or symbolic forms, such as charts, graphs, maps, or diagrams .
  - Interaction facilities that allow the user to browse, search, filter, zoom, or select data in an information space .
  - Cluster analysis that groups data into meaningful categories based on similarity or distance measures, such as hierarchical, partitioning, or density-based methods.
  - Visual exploration that combines information visualizations, interaction facilities, and cluster analysis to enable the user to navigate in a 3D space and discover hidden structures or relationships in the data.
- Exploration of complex information spaces has applications in various domains, such as:
  - Human space exploration, where information technology (IT) can increase the safety, efficiency, performance, and cost-effectiveness of missions by providing vehicle health management, automated assembly, and automated operations.
  - Business intelligence, where information exploration can help decision makers to analyze large and diverse data sets, such as customer behavior, market trends, or financial transactions.
  - Scientific research, where information exploration can help researchers to understand complex phenomena, such as climate change, gene expression, or social networks.
