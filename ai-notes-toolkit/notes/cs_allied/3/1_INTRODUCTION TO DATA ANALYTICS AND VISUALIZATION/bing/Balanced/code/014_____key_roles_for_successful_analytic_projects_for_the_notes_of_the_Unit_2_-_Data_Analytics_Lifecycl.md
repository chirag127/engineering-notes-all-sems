### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

According to the Data Analytics Lifecycle, a successful analytic project requires the collaboration of different roles and skills to achieve the desired outcomes. Some of the key roles are:

- **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also approves the budget, timeline, and resources for the project and ensures alignment with the strategic goals of the organization.
- **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication and collaboration among the project team and stakeholders, manages the risks and issues, and monitors the progress and performance of the project.
- **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. A business user can also provide feedback and validation of the data, analysis, and recommendations.
- **Business Intelligence Analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can collect, integrate, and analyze data from various sources and present it in a meaningful and actionable way. This person can also design and develop dashboards, reports, and visualizations to communicate the insights and findings to the stakeholders.
- **Database Administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can manage and maintain the data sources, databases, and data warehouses that are used for the project. This person can also ensure the security, availability, and quality of the data, as well as perform backup and recovery operations.
- **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can design and build the data pipelines, platforms, and architectures that are needed for the project. This person can also implement the data ingestion, transformation, and processing methods, as well as optimize the performance and scalability of the data systems.
- **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced statistical and machine learning techniques to the data to discover patterns, trends, and relationships, and generate predictive and prescriptive models. This person can also evaluate and validate the models, as well as communicate the results and recommendations to the stakeholders.

These roles are not mutually exclusive and may overlap or vary depending on the size, scope, and complexity of the project. However, having a clear understanding of the roles and responsibilities of each team member can help ensure a successful analytic project.
