# Unit 7 - Human Vision in Data Analytics and Visualization

- Human vision is the process of perceiving and interpreting visual information from the environment.
- Data analytics and visualization are techniques to transform data into graphical representations that can reveal patterns, trends, and insights.
- Human vision plays a crucial role in data analytics and visualization, as it enables the users to explore, analyze, and communicate data effectively.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The characteristics of human vision, such as color perception, visual attention, preattentive processing, and gestalt principles.
  - The design principles for data visualization, such as data-ink ratio, chart junk, lie factor, and visual encoding.
  - The types of data visualization, such as tables, charts, graphs, maps, and dashboards.
  - The tools and techniques for data visualization, such as Excel, Tableau, Power BI, R, and Python.
  - The challenges and opportunities for data visualization, such as big data, interactive visualization, storytelling, and vision analytics.
