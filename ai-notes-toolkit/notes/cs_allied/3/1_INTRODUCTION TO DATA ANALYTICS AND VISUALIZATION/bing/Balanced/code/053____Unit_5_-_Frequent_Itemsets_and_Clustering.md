## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

- This unit covers two important topics in data mining: finding frequent itemsets and clustering data points.
- Frequent itemsets are sets of items that occur together frequently in a transactional database, such as a supermarket or an online store. They can reveal interesting patterns and associations among the items, such as market basket analysis or recommendation systems.
- Clustering is the process of grouping data points into meaningful clusters based on their similarity or distance. Clustering can help discover the underlying structure and patterns of the data, such as customer segmentation or image compression.

### Learning Outcomes

- By the end of this unit, you should be able to:
  - Explain the concepts and applications of frequent itemsets and association rules.
  - Apply the Apriori algorithm and its variations to find frequent itemsets and association rules from a transactional database.
  - Evaluate the quality and usefulness of association rules using various measures, such as support, confidence, lift, and conviction.
  - Explain the concepts and applications of clustering and cluster analysis.
  - Apply different clustering techniques, such as k-means, hierarchical, and density-based clustering, to cluster data points based on their similarity or distance.
  - Evaluate the quality and validity of clustering results using various measures, such as silhouette coefficient, Dunn index, and Davies-Bouldin index.
