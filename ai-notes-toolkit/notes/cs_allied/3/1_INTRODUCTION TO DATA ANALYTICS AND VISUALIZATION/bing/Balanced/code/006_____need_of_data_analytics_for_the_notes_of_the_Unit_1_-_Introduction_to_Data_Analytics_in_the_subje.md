### Need of data analytics

Data analytics is the science of analyzing raw data to make conclusions about that information. Data analytics help a business optimize its performance, perform more efficiently, maximize profit, or make more strategically-guided decisions.

The need of data analytics arises from the following factors:

- The abundance of data being generated by various sources, such as social media, sensors, e-commerce, etc. Data analytics can help extract valuable insights from this data and use it for competitive advantage.
- The complexity and uncertainty of the business environment, which requires data-driven decision making to reduce risks, improve quality, and increase customer satisfaction.
- The innovation and differentiation of products and services, which requires data analytics to understand customer needs, preferences, and behavior, and to create personalized and customized solutions.
- The efficiency and effectiveness of business processes, which requires data analytics to identify bottlenecks, optimize resources, and monitor performance.

Some of the benefits of data analytics are:

- It can help improve customer loyalty and retention by providing personalized and relevant offers, recommendations, and feedback.
- It can help increase revenue and profitability by identifying new opportunities, markets, and segments, and by optimizing pricing and promotion strategies.
- It can help reduce costs and waste by improving operational efficiency, quality, and productivity, and by detecting and preventing fraud and errors.
- It can help enhance innovation and creativity by generating new ideas, insights, and solutions, and by testing and validating hypotheses.

Data analytics can be applied to various domains and industries, such as:

- Marketing and sales: to understand customer behavior, preferences, and needs, and to design and evaluate marketing campaigns and strategies.
- Finance and accounting: to analyze financial data, such as transactions, statements, and reports, and to provide financial forecasting, risk management, and compliance.
- Healthcare and medicine: to analyze medical data, such as records, images, and tests, and to provide diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases and conditions.
- Education and learning: to analyze educational data, such as grades, attendance, and feedback, and to provide personalized and adaptive learning, assessment, and guidance.
- Manufacturing and engineering: to analyze production data, such as sensors, machines, and materials, and to provide quality control, maintenance, and optimization of processes and systems.

Data analytics can be performed using various techniques and tools, such as:

- Descriptive analytics: to summarize and visualize data using statistics, charts, graphs, and dashboards.
- Diagnostic analytics: to explore and explain data using queries, filters, drill-downs, and correlations.
- Predictive analytics: to forecast and estimate data using regression, classification, clustering, and machine learning.
- Prescriptive analytics: to recommend and optimize data using optimization, simulation, and decision analysis.

Data analytics requires various skills and competencies, such as:

- Data literacy: to understand and communicate data using appropriate terminology, concepts, and methods.
- Data collection: to identify and acquire data from various sources, such as databases, files, web, and APIs.
- Data cleaning: to prepare and transform data by removing errors, outliers, duplicates, and missing values.
- Data analysis: to apply appropriate techniques and tools to analyze data and generate insights and conclusions.
- Data visualization: to present and communicate data using effective and appealing charts, graphs, and dashboards.
- Data storytelling: to narrate and explain data using clear and compelling language, context, and logic.

Data analytics is a dynamic and evolving field that offers many opportunities and challenges for both individuals and organizations. Data analytics can help improve decision making, performance, and outcomes, and can create value and impact for various stakeholders. Data analytics is a key skill and competency for the 21st century.
