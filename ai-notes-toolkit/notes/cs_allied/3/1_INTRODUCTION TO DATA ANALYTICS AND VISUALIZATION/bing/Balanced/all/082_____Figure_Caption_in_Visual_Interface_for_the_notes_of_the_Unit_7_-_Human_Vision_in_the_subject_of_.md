### Figure Caption in Visual Interface

- A figure caption is a text that accompanies and explains a graphical element in a visual interface, such as a chart, a map, a diagram, or an image.
- A figure caption can provide additional information, context, or interpretation for the graphical element, such as its source, its purpose, its main message, or its key features.
- A figure caption can also guide the user's attention to specific aspects of the graphical element, such as highlighting important patterns, trends, outliers, or comparisons.
- A figure caption should be concise, clear, and accurate, and avoid unnecessary or redundant details that can distract or confuse the user.
- A figure caption should be placed close to the graphical element, preferably below it, and aligned with its left edge.
- A figure caption should use a smaller font size than the main text, and a different font style or color to distinguish it from the graphical element.
- A figure caption should follow a consistent format and style throughout the visual interface, and use appropriate punctuation and capitalization rules.
- A figure caption should be numbered if there are multiple graphical elements in the visual interface, and referenced in the main text by their numbers.
