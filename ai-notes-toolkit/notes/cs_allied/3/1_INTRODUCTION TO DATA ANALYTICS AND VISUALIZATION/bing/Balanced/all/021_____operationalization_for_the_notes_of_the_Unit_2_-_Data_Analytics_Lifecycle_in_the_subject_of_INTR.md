# Operationalization

- Operationalization is the final phase of the data analytics lifecycle, where the results of the analysis are deployed and integrated into the business processes or systems.
- Operationalization involves the following steps:
  - Developing a deployment plan that specifies how the analytical outputs will be delivered, accessed, and used by the end-users or stakeholders.
  - Testing and validating the analytical outputs to ensure they meet the quality, accuracy, and performance standards.
  - Deploying the analytical outputs to the target environment, such as a dashboard, a report, a web application, or a data product.
  - Monitoring and evaluating the analytical outputs to measure their impact, effectiveness, and value for the business or organization.
  - Updating and maintaining the analytical outputs to incorporate feedback, new data, or changing requirements.
- Operationalization requires collaboration and communication among the data analysts, the business users, the IT staff, and the management, to ensure a smooth and successful deployment and integration of the analytical outputs.
- Operationalization also requires documentation and training to ensure the analytical outputs are understood and used properly by the end-users or stakeholders.
