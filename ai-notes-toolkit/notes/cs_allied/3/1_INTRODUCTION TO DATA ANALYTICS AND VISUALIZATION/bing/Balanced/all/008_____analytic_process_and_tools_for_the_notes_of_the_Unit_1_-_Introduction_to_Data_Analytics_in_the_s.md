# Analytic Process and Tools

- Analytic process is a systematic approach to identify, collect, analyze, and communicate data and information to support decision making and problem solving.
- Analytic tools are software applications, techniques, or methods that enable the analytic process and help to generate insights from data.
- The analytic process typically involves the following steps:
  - Define the problem or question: What is the goal or purpose of the analysis? What are the key questions or issues to be addressed?
  - Obtain and prepare the data: What are the sources and types of data available? How can the data be accessed, cleaned, transformed, and integrated?
  - Explore and analyze the data: What are the patterns, trends, relationships, or anomalies in the data? What are the appropriate techniques or methods to apply to the data?
  - Interpret and communicate the results: What are the main findings or conclusions from the analysis? How can the results be presented or visualized in a clear and effective way?
  - Evaluate and refine the analysis: How well does the analysis answer the problem or question? How can the analysis be improved or validated?
- Some examples of analytic tools are:
  - Spreadsheet software: A program that allows users to organize, manipulate, and perform calculations on data in rows and columns, such as Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
  - Database management system: A software system that stores, manages, and retrieves data from a structured collection of tables, such as MySQL or Oracle.
  - Business intelligence software: A software system that provides interactive dashboards, reports, and charts to visualize and explore data, such as Tableau or Power BI.
  - Statistical software: A software system that performs statistical analysis and tests on data, such as R or SPSS.
  - Data mining software: A software system that applies machine learning, artificial intelligence, or other techniques to discover patterns or relationships in large and complex data sets, such as RapidMiner or Weka.
  - Data visualization software: A software system that creates graphical representations of data, such as charts, maps, or diagrams, to communicate information or insights, such as D3.js or Plotly.
