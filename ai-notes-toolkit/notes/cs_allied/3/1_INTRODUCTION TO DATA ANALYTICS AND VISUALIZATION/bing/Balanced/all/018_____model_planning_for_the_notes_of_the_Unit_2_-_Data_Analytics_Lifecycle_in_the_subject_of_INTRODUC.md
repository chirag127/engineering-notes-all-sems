# Model Planning

- Model planning is the third phase of the data analytics lifecycle, after data preparation and data exploration.
- The goal of model planning is to determine the appropriate techniques and methods to analyze the data and answer the business questions or objectives.
- Model planning involves selecting the variables or features that are relevant for the analysis, choosing the appropriate algorithms or models to apply, and defining the evaluation metrics or criteria to measure the performance of the models.
- Model planning can be done using various tools and techniques, such as:

  - Statistical methods, such as hypothesis testing, correlation analysis, regression analysis, ANOVA, etc.
  - Machine learning methods, such as classification, clustering, association rules, decision trees, neural networks, etc.
  - Data mining methods, such as text mining, sentiment analysis, web mining, etc.
  - Visualization methods, such as charts, graphs, maps, dashboards, etc.

- Model planning requires a good understanding of the data, the business problem, and the analytical objectives. It also requires creativity, experimentation, and iteration to find the best solution.
