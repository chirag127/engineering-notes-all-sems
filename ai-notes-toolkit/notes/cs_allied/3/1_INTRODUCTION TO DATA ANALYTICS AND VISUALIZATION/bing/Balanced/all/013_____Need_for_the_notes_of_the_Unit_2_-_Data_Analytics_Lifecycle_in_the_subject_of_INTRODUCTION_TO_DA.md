# Need for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- The notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle are important for the students who are studying the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION because they cover the following topics:

  - The definition and scope of data analytics and its applications in various domains and industries.
  - The steps and phases of the data analytics lifecycle, such as business understanding, data understanding, data preparation, modeling, evaluation, and deployment.
  - The challenges and best practices of each phase of the data analytics lifecycle, such as defining the problem statement, identifying the data sources, cleaning and transforming the data, selecting and applying the appropriate analytical techniques, validating and interpreting the results, and communicating and implementing the findings.
  - The tools and techniques that are used in each phase of the data analytics lifecycle, such as data collection, data exploration, data visualization, data mining, machine learning, statistical analysis, and reporting.
  - The ethical and legal issues that are involved in data analytics, such as data privacy, data security, data quality, data ownership, and data governance.

- The notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle can help the students to:

  - Understand the concepts and principles of data analytics and its role in solving real-world problems and creating value for businesses and society.
  - Develop the skills and competencies that are required for performing data analytics tasks, such as data manipulation, data analysis, data modeling, data evaluation, and data communication.
  - Apply the knowledge and skills of data analytics to various case studies and scenarios that are relevant to their fields of interest and specialization.
  - Prepare for the assessments and examinations that are based on the learning outcomes and objectives of the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION.
