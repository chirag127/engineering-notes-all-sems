# Data Preparation for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

- Data preparation is the process of collecting, processing, and cleansing data for use in analytics and business intelligence.
- Data preparation is one of the six phases of the data analytics lifecycle, which is a systematic method for managing data to achieve corporate objectives.
- The data analytics lifecycle consists of the following phases   :
  - **Discovery**: This phase involves understanding the business issue, defining the project objectives, and identifying the data sources and requirements.
  - **Data preparation**: This phase involves accessing, loading, structuring, purging, unifying, adjusting, and transforming data into an analytical sandbox, which is a scalable platform for data analysis.
  - **Model planning**: This phase involves selecting the appropriate techniques, methods, and algorithms for data analysis, and creating test designs to validate the models.
  - **Model building**: This phase involves applying the selected techniques, methods, and algorithms to the data in the analytical sandbox, and assessing the model outcomes and quality.
  - **Communicating results**: This phase involves presenting and visualizing the model results, findings, and insights to the stakeholders, and getting feedback and approval for the next steps.
  - **Operationalizing**: This phase involves deploying the models into production, monitoring their performance, and updating them as needed.
- Data preparation is a crucial phase in the data analytics lifecycle, as it affects the quality and accuracy of the subsequent phases .
- Data preparation requires the following steps :
  - **Accessing data**: This step involves identifying and connecting to the relevant data sources, such as databases, files, web services, etc., and extracting the data for analysis.
  - **Loading data**: This step involves transferring the data from the sources to the analytical sandbox, and ensuring the data is compatible and consistent with the sandbox environment.
  - **Structuring data**: This step involves organizing the data into a suitable format and structure for analysis, such as tables, columns, rows, etc., and defining the data types, labels, and metadata.
  - **Purging data**: This step involves removing the data that is irrelevant, redundant, or erroneous for the analysis, such as duplicates, outliers, missing values, etc.
  - **Unifying data**: This step involves combining and integrating the data from different sources, and resolving any conflicts or inconsistencies in the data, such as different formats, scales, units, etc.
  - **Adjusting data**: This step involves modifying the data to meet the specific needs and objectives of the analysis, such as aggregating, filtering, sorting, grouping, etc.
  - **Transforming data**: This step involves applying various functions and operations to the data to enhance its quality and usefulness for the analysis, such as normalizing, scaling, binning, encoding, etc.
