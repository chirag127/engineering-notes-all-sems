# Characteristics of Data

Data is any form of information that has been gathered and organized in a meaningful format that can be processed further . Data can be quantitative (numerical) or qualitative (categorical) and can be used for various purposes such as analysis, decision making, visualization, etc. Data quality is an important aspect of data that affects its usability and reliability. Data quality can be measured by five characteristics:

- **Accuracy**: The degree to which data is free of errors and conforms to the intended meaning and purpose. Accurate data should be precise, valid, and consistent across different sources and systems .
- **Completeness**: The degree to which data has all the required and expected values and attributes. Complete data should not have missing, null, or blank values that may affect the analysis or interpretation of the data.
- **Reliability**: The degree to which data can be trusted and depended on for its intended use. Reliable data should be consistent, verifiable, and unbiased. Reliability can be affected by factors such as the source, method, and frequency of data collection .
- **Relevance**: The degree to which data is appropriate and useful for the specific context and needs of the user. Relevant data should be timely, current, and aligned with the objectives and goals of the analysis or decision making .
- **Uniqueness**: The degree to which data is distinct and free of duplicates or redundancies. Unique data should have a clear and consistent identification and representation of the entities and attributes. Uniqueness can improve the efficiency and effectiveness of data processing and analysis .

Data can also be described by two other characteristics that relate to its distribution and variability :

- **Central tendency**: The measure of the average or typical value of the data. Central tendency can be calculated by different statistics such as mean, median, or mode. Central tendency can help to summarize and compare the data .
- **Dispersion**: The measure of the spread or variation of the data. Dispersion can be calculated by different statistics such as range, standard deviation, or variance. Dispersion can help to understand the diversity and variability of the data .
