# Discovery for the notes of the Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- Data Analytics Lifecycle defines the roadmap of how data is generated, collected, processed, used, and analyzed to achieve business goals.
- It offers a systematic way to manage data for converting it into information that can be used to fulfill organizational and project goals.
- The data analytics lifecycle describes the process of conducting a data analytics project, which consists of six key steps based on the CRISP-DM methodology.
- According to Paula Muñoz, a Northeastern alumna, these steps include:
  - Understanding the business issue: This step involves defining the problem or opportunity, identifying the objectives and success criteria, and establishing the project scope and plan.
  - Understanding the data set: This step involves collecting the data from various sources, assessing the quality and quantity of the data, and exploring the data characteristics and relationships.
  - Preparing the data: This step involves cleaning the data, handling missing values and outliers, transforming the data, and creating new variables or features.
  - Exploratory analysis: This step involves applying descriptive and inferential statistics, visualizing the data, and identifying patterns and insights.
  - Validation: This step involves building and testing predictive or prescriptive models, evaluating their performance and accuracy, and selecting the best model for the business problem.
  - Deployment: This step involves implementing the model in the real-world scenario, monitoring its results and outcomes, and updating or refining the model as needed.
- The data analytics lifecycle is based on the popular CRISP-DM analytics process model, which is an open-standard analytics model developed by IBM.
- The data analytics lifecycle is not a linear process, but rather an iterative and flexible one, where feedback and learning can be incorporated at any stage.
- The data analytics lifecycle can help data analysts and business stakeholders to collaborate effectively, communicate clearly, and deliver value from data.
