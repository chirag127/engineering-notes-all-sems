# Unit 7 - Human Vision in Data Analytics and Visualization

## Introduction

- Human vision is the process of perceiving and interpreting visual information from the environment.
- Data analytics and visualization are the techniques of transforming data into graphical representations that can reveal patterns, trends, and insights.
- Human vision plays a crucial role in data analytics and visualization, as it determines how effectively and efficiently the visual information can be processed and communicated.

## Objectives

- To understand the basic principles and characteristics of human vision and perception.
- To apply the knowledge of human vision and perception to design effective and engaging data visualizations.
- To evaluate and critique data visualizations based on human vision and perception criteria.

## Topics

- Human vision system: the anatomy and physiology of the eye, the visual pathway, and the visual cortex.
- Visual perception: the processes and mechanisms of attention, perception, memory, and cognition that influence how we see and interpret visual information.
- Visual variables: the properties and attributes of graphical elements that can encode data, such as position, size, shape, color, orientation, and texture.
- Visual design principles: the guidelines and best practices for creating clear, coherent, and compelling data visualizations, such as contrast, alignment, balance, hierarchy, and gestalt principles.
- Visual analytics: the methods and tools for exploring, analyzing, and communicating data using interactive and dynamic visualizations, such as dashboards, charts, maps, and networks.
