# Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

According to the Data Analytics Lifecycle, a successful analytic project requires the collaboration of different roles and skills. Some of the key roles are:

- **Project sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for the start of the project and provides all the requirements for the project and defines the core business problem. The project sponsor also approves the budget, timeline, and resources for the project. The project sponsor is usually a senior executive or a business leader who has the authority and influence to drive the project forward.  

- **Project manager**: The project manager ensures that key milestones and objectives are met on time and at the expected quality. The project manager also coordinates the communication, collaboration, and integration of the project team and stakeholders. The project manager is usually a skilled professional who has experience in managing complex and cross-functional projects.  

- **Business user**: A business user is someone who understands the domain area and usually benefits from the results. This person can consult and advise the project team on the context of the project, the value of the results, and how the outputs will be operationalized. A business user is usually a business analyst, a line manager, or a deep subject matter expert.  

- **Business Intelligence Analyst**: A business intelligence analyst is someone who can collect, analyze, and report on data from various sources and systems. This person can also design and develop dashboards, reports, and visualizations to communicate the insights and recommendations to the business users and stakeholders. A business intelligence analyst is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of data warehousing, data modeling, and data visualization tools.  

- **Database Administrator (DBA)**: A database administrator is someone who can manage, maintain, and optimize the databases and data platforms that store and process the data for the project. This person can also ensure the security, availability, and performance of the data systems. A database administrator is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of database administration, data engineering, and data governance.  

- **Data engineer**: A data engineer is someone who can design, build, and test the data pipelines and workflows that transform, integrate, and prepare the data for analysis. This person can also implement the data quality and validation checks, as well as the data ingestion and extraction processes. A data engineer is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of data engineering, data architecture, and data programming.  

- **Data scientist**: A data scientist is someone who can apply advanced analytical techniques and methods, such as machine learning, statistics, and optimization, to discover patterns, trends, and insights from the data. This person can also develop and deploy predictive models, algorithms, and solutions that address the business problem and provide value to the business users and stakeholders. A data scientist is usually a technical expert who has knowledge of data science, data analysis, and data programming.  

These roles are not mutually exclusive and may overlap or vary depending on the size, scope, and complexity of the project. However, they are essential for ensuring the success and quality of the analytic project.
