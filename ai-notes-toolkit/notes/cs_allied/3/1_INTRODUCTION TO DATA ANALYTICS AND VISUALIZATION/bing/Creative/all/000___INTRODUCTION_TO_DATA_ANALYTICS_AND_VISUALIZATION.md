# INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- Data analytics is the process of collecting, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting data to gain insights and support decision-making.
- Data visualization is the presentation of data in a visual format, such as charts, graphs, and maps, to tell a meaningful story and communicate information effectively.
- Data analytics and visualization are closely related and complementary skills that can help businesses and organizations to understand their data, identify patterns and trends, and communicate their findings to various audiences.
- Data analytics and visualization can be applied to various domains and industries, such as finance, marketing, healthcare, education, and social sciences.
- Data analytics and visualization require a combination of technical, analytical, and creative skills, as well as knowledge of various tools and techniques, such as Excel, R, Python, SQL, Tableau, and Power BI  .
- Data analytics and visualization follow a general process that involves the following steps:
  - Define the problem or question
  - Collect and clean the data
  - Explore and analyze the data
  - Visualize and communicate the results
  - Evaluate and refine the solution
- Data analytics and visualization are essential skills for the 21st century, as they enable data-driven decision making, innovation, and storytelling .
