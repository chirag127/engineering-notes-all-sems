# Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

The data analytics lifecycle is a framework for planning and executing data analysis projects. It consists of six phases:

- **Discovery**: This phase involves identifying the business problem, defining the objectives and scope of the analysis, and gathering the relevant data sources and stakeholders.
- **Data preparation**: This phase involves cleaning, transforming, and integrating the data into a suitable format for analysis. It also involves exploring the data to understand its characteristics, quality, and distribution.
- **Model planning**: This phase involves selecting the appropriate analytical techniques and tools for the data and the problem. It also involves creating and validating hypotheses, and designing experiments or tests to evaluate them.
- **Model building**: This phase involves applying the selected techniques and tools to the data, and generating insights, patterns, or predictions. It also involves assessing the accuracy, validity, and reliability of the results, and refining the models as needed.
- **Communicate results**: This phase involves presenting and visualizing the results to the stakeholders, and explaining the methods, assumptions, and limitations of the analysis. It also involves providing recommendations, actions, or solutions based on the results.
- **Operationalize**: This phase involves deploying the models or solutions into production, and monitoring and evaluating their performance and impact. It also involves updating, maintaining, and improving the models or solutions as the data and the problem evolve.
