# Case studies for the notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- Mining data streams is the process of extracting knowledge structures, such as models and patterns, from continuous and unbounded streams of information .
- Data streams pose many challenges for data mining, such as high volume, high velocity, concept drift, limited memory, and real-time processing .
- Some applications of data stream mining include network monitoring, sensor networks, web clickstream analysis, social media analysis, and fraud detection .
- Here are some case studies of data stream mining from different domains:

## Case study 1: Stream data mining classification

- This case study presents a stream data mining classification system that uses a sliding window technique to handle concept drift and a decision tree algorithm to build a classifier.
- The system is applied to a real-world dataset of network intrusion detection, which consists of network traffic data collected from a simulated network environment.
- The system is evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, and F-measure metrics, and compared with other stream data mining classification methods.
- The results show that the system achieves high performance and adapts well to the changing data distribution.

## Case study 2: Improvised methods for tackling big data stream mining challenges

- This case study proposes a holistic data stream mining approach that integrates different components of data stream mining, such as feature selection, ensemble learning, and concept drift detection, with the aim of improving the prediction performance.
- The approach is applied to two real-world datasets of electricity price prediction and stock market prediction, which consist of time-series data with high volatility and non-stationarity.
- The approach is evaluated using root mean square error, mean absolute error, and mean absolute percentage error metrics, and compared with other data stream mining methods.
- The results show that the approach outperforms the existing methods and handles the challenges of big data stream mining effectively.

## Case study 3: An update on global mining land use

- This case study presents an updated dataset of global mining land use, which covers both active and inactive mining sites across different commodities and countries.
- The dataset is derived from two previous datasets that mapped the major mine sites worldwide and the global extent of mining, using satellite imagery and other sources of information.
- The dataset is analyzed using spatial statistics, such as area, density, and fragmentation, and compared with other land use datasets, such as cropland and urban areas.
- The results show that mining land use has increased significantly over time and has a considerable impact on the environment and society.
