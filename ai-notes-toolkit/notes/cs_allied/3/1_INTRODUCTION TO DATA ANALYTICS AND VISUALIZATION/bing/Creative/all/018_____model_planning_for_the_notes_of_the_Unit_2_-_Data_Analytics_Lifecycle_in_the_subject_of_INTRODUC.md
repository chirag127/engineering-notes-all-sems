# Model Planning

- Model planning is the third phase of the data analytics lifecycle, following the data preparation phase.
- The goal of model planning is to determine the appropriate techniques and methods to analyze the data and address the business problem or opportunity.
- Model planning involves selecting and applying various statistical or machine learning models to the data, such as regression, classification, clustering, association, etc.
- Model planning also involves choosing the relevant features or variables to include in the models, as well as the evaluation metrics to measure the performance and accuracy of the models.
- Model planning requires a good understanding of the data, the business context, and the analytical objectives, as well as the assumptions and limitations of the models.
- Model planning may involve testing and comparing different models or approaches to find the best fit for the data and the problem.
- Model planning may also involve tuning or optimizing the parameters or hyperparameters of the models to improve their results.
- Model planning may require iterating or revising the previous phases of the data analytics lifecycle, such as data preparation or problem definition, based on the feedback or insights from the models.
- Model planning is an important and challenging phase of the data analytics lifecycle, as it can have a significant impact on the quality and value of the analytical outcomes.
