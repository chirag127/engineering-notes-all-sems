### Unit 7 - Human Vision in Data Analytics and Visualization

- Human vision is the process of perceiving and interpreting visual information from the environment.
- Data analytics and visualization are techniques that use graphical representations of data to facilitate understanding, communication and decision making.
- Human vision and data visualization are closely related, as both rely on the principles of visual perception, cognition and communication.
- Some of the topics covered in this unit are:

  - The characteristics and limitations of human vision, such as acuity, color vision, depth perception, attention and visual memory.
  - The design principles and guidelines for effective data visualization, such as data-ink ratio, chart junk, gestalt laws, pre-attentive attributes and visual encoding.
  - The types and functions of data visualization, such as exploratory, explanatory, descriptive, inferential, predictive and prescriptive.
  - The common data visualization techniques and tools, such as tables, charts, graphs, maps, dashboards and interactive visualizations.
  - The evaluation methods and criteria for data visualization, such as usability, aesthetics, accuracy, clarity and insightfulness.

- The main objectives of this unit are:

  - To understand the role and importance of human vision in data analytics and visualization.
  - To apply the knowledge of human vision to design and evaluate effective data visualizations.
  - To explore and compare different data visualization techniques and tools for various data types and tasks.
  - To develop critical thinking and creativity skills for data visualization.
