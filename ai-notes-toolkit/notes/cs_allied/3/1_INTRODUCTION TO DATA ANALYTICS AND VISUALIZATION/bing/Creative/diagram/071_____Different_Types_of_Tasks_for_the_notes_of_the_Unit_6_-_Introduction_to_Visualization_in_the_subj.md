### Different Types of Tasks

- Data visualization is the process of creating graphical representations of data to communicate information, insights, and patterns effectively.
- Data visualization tasks can be classified into four main types: **exploratory**, **explanatory**, **presentational**, and **analytical**.
- **Exploratory tasks** are those that aim to discover new knowledge or generate hypotheses from the data. They involve interacting with the data, filtering, sorting, zooming, drilling down, and comparing different views. Examples of exploratory tasks are:
  - Finding outliers or anomalies in the data
  - Identifying clusters or groups of similar data points
  - Detecting trends or changes over time
- **Explanatory tasks** are those that aim to convey a specific message or answer a specific question from the data. They involve selecting, highlighting, annotating, and emphasizing the most relevant or important aspects of the data. Examples of explanatory tasks are:
  - Showing the relationship or correlation between two variables
  - Comparing the performance or ranking of different categories or groups
  - Illustrating the distribution or proportion of a variable
- **Presentational tasks** are those that aim to communicate the results or findings of a data analysis to a specific audience. They involve designing, organizing, and formatting the visual elements to create a clear, concise, and compelling story. Examples of presentational tasks are:
  - Creating a dashboard or report that summarizes the key insights or outcomes of a data analysis
  - Developing a slide deck or infographic that narrates the main points or arguments of a data analysis
  - Producing a poster or flyer that showcases the highlights or recommendations of a data analysis
- **Analytical tasks** are those that aim to apply mathematical or statistical methods to the data to derive new information or knowledge. They involve transforming, modeling, or testing the data using various techniques and tools. Examples of analytical tasks are:
  - Calculating descriptive or inferential statistics from the data
  - Performing regression or classification on the data
  - Applying clustering or dimensionality reduction on the data
