# Need of Data Analytics

Data analytics is the science of analyzing raw data to make conclusions about that information. Data analytics can help a business optimize its performance, perform more efficiently, maximize profit, or make more strategically-guided decisions. Data analytics can also reveal trends and metrics that would otherwise be lost in the mass of information.

Some of the reasons why data analytics is needed are:

- Data analytics can help businesses understand their customers better, such as their preferences, behavior, needs, and satisfaction. This can help businesses tailor their products, services, and marketing strategies to meet customer expectations and increase loyalty and retention.
- Data analytics can help businesses identify and solve problems, such as operational inefficiencies, quality issues, fraud, or risks. This can help businesses improve their processes, reduce costs, enhance quality, and mitigate risks.
- Data analytics can help businesses discover new opportunities, such as new markets, segments, products, or services. This can help businesses innovate, diversify, and grow their revenue streams and competitive advantage.
- Data analytics can help businesses make better decisions, such as strategic planning, resource allocation, budgeting, or forecasting. This can help businesses align their goals, actions, and outcomes, and measure their performance and impact.

Data analytics is a broad term that encompasses many diverse types of data analysis. Any type of information can be subjected to data analytics techniques to get insight that can be used to improve things. Some of the common types of data analytics are:

- Descriptive analytics: This type of analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using historical data. It can answer questions such as what, when, where, and how. For example, descriptive analytics can show the sales trends, customer demographics, or website traffic of a business.
- Diagnostic analytics: This type of analytics explains why something has happened in the past using historical data. It can answer questions such as why and how. For example, diagnostic analytics can show the root causes, correlations, or factors that influenced the sales, customer satisfaction, or website performance of a business.
- Predictive analytics: This type of analytics predicts what is likely to happen in the future using historical and current data. It can answer questions such as what if and how likely. For example, predictive analytics can show the sales forecasts, customer churn rates, or website conversion rates of a business.
- Prescriptive analytics: This type of analytics suggests what actions should be taken in the future using historical, current, and future data. It can answer questions such as what and how. For example, prescriptive analytics can show the optimal pricing, product recommendations, or marketing campaigns for a business.

Data analytics is a valuable skill that can be applied to various domains and industries, such as finance, healthcare, education, retail, manufacturing, or government. Data analytics can also create new career opportunities, such as data analyst, data scientist, data engineer, or business analyst. Data analytics requires a combination of technical, analytical, and business skills, such as:

- Data collection: This skill involves gathering data from various sources, such as databases, files, APIs, web scraping, or surveys.
- Data cleaning: This skill involves preparing data for analysis, such as removing errors, duplicates, outliers, or missing values.
- Data manipulation: This skill involves transforming data for analysis, such as aggregating, filtering, sorting, or joining data.
- Data visualization: This skill involves presenting data for analysis, such as creating charts, graphs, dashboards, or reports.
- Data analysis: This skill involves applying data analytics techniques for analysis, such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, or prescriptive analytics.
- Data interpretation: This skill involves drawing insights and conclusions from data analysis, such as identifying patterns, trends, or anomalies.
- Data communication: This skill involves communicating data analysis results to stakeholders, such as using storytelling, reports, or presentations.

Data analytics is a powerful tool that can help businesses and organizations leverage their data to gain insights, make decisions, and drive actions. Data analytics is also a dynamic and evolving field that requires continuous learning and adaptation to new technologies, methods, and challenges. Data analytics is a key component of data science, which is the interdisciplinary field of using scientific methods, processes, algorithms, and systems to extract knowledge and insights from data. Data science also includes other aspects, such as data mining, machine learning, artificial intelligence, or big data. Data analytics is the foundation of data science, and data science is the future of data analytics.
