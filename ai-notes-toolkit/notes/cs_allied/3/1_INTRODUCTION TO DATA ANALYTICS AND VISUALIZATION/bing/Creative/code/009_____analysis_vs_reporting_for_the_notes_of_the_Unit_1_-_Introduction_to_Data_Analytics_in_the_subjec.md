### Analysis vs Reporting

- Reporting is the process of organizing data into informational summaries in order to monitor how different areas of a business are performing .
- Analysis is the process of exploring data and reports to extract meaningful insights, which can be used to better understand and improve business performance .
- Some of the key differences between reporting and analysis are :
  - Required skills: Analysis requires more advanced skills than reporting, such as knowledge of different analytical models, statistical techniques, and business problems. Reporting requires basic skills of data organization, presentation, and communication.
  - Order of operations: Reporting is usually done before analysis, as it provides the data and summaries that can be used for analysis. Analysis is usually done after reporting, as it uses the reports to generate insights and recommendations.
  - Time needed to implement: Reporting is usually faster and easier to implement than analysis, as it involves less complex and more standardized processes. Analysis is usually slower and harder to implement than reporting, as it involves more complex and customized processes.
  - Ease of automation: Reporting is usually more suitable for automation than analysis, as it involves repetitive and predictable tasks. Analysis is usually less suitable for automation than reporting, as it involves creative and contextual tasks.
  - Impact on strategy: Analysis usually has more impact on strategy than reporting, as it provides actionable insights and recommendations that can influence business decisions. Reporting usually has less impact on strategy than analysis, as it provides descriptive summaries and facts that can inform business decisions.
  - Data context: Analysis usually requires more data context than reporting, as it involves interpreting and explaining the data and its implications. Reporting usually requires less data context than reporting, as it involves displaying and summarizing the data and its features.
- Reporting and analysis are both important aspects of data analytics, as they complement each other and provide different benefits for a business .
  - Reporting helps to monitor the current state of a business, track its progress, and identify any issues or opportunities.
  - Analysis helps to understand the root causes of a business problem, explore different scenarios and solutions, and optimize the business performance and outcomes.
