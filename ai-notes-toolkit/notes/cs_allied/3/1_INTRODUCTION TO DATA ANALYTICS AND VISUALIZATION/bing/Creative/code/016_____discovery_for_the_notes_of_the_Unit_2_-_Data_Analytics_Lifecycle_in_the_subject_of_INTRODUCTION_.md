### Discovery

Discovery is the first phase of the data analytics lifecycle, which defines the roadmap of how data is generated, collected, processed, used, and analyzed to achieve business goals . In this phase, the following tasks are performed:

- Understanding the business issue: This involves identifying the problem or opportunity that the data analytics project aims to address, defining the objectives and scope of the project, and establishing the success criteria and metrics.
- Understanding the data set: This involves collecting the relevant data sources, exploring the data quality and availability, and assessing the data suitability and feasibility for the project.
- Preparing the data: This involves cleaning, transforming, integrating, and formatting the data to make it ready for analysis.

The discovery phase is crucial for setting the foundation and direction of the data analytics project, as it helps to align the data with the business needs and expectations. The discovery phase also helps to identify the potential risks and challenges that may arise during the project, and plan for the appropriate mitigation strategies.
