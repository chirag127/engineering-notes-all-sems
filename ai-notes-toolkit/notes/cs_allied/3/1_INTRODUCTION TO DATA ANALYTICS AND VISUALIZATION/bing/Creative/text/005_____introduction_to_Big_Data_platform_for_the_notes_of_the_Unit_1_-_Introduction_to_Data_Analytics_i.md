### Introduction to Big Data Platform

- A big data platform is a structured data storage system for massive volumes of information  .
- To store aggregated data sets, big data platforms combine software and hardware solutions for data management  .
- These data sets are typically kept in the cloud  .
- A big data platform enables organizations to develop, deploy, operate and manage a big data infrastructure/environment.
- A big data platform also supports the use of advanced analytic techniques against large data sets, including structured/unstructured data and streaming/batch data.
- Some examples of big data platforms are Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services, IBM Cloud, etc .
- Big data platforms have key features such as scalability, reliability, security, performance, integration, and flexibility  .
- Big data platforms have use cases in various domains such as e-commerce, healthcare, finance, social media, education, etc  .
