### Introduction to streams concepts

- A data stream is an existing, continuous, ordered (implicitly by entrance time or explicitly by timestamp) chain of items.
- Data streams capture the data processing needs of today, where random access is expensive and a single scan algorithm is preferred.
- Data streams can come from various sources, such as equipment sensors, clickstreams, social media feeds, stock market quotes, app activity, and more.
- Data streams can be processed and analyzed in real time, or near real time, to extract insights, filter, transform, or enhance the data.
- Streaming analytics is the continuous processing and analysis of big data in motion.
- Streaming analytics can enable various use cases, such as fraud detection, anomaly detection, sentiment analysis, real-time recommendations, and more.
- Streaming analytics requires a streaming data architecture that consists of several components, such as data sources, data ingestion, stream processing, data storage, and data consumption.
- Streaming data architecture can leverage various technologies, such as Apache Kafka, Apache Spark, Apache Flink, Apache Beam, and more.
