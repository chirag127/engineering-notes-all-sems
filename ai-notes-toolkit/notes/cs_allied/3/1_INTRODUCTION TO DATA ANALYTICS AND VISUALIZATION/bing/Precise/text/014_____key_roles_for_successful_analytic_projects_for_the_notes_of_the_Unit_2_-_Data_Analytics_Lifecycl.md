### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

1. **Project Sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for providing the vision, direction, and resources for the project. They are the champion of the project and ensure that it aligns with the organization's goals and objectives.

2. **Project Manager**: The project manager is responsible for the day-to-day management of the project. They ensure that the project is completed on time, within budget, and to the required quality standards.

3. **Data Scientist**: The data scientist is responsible for analyzing the data and developing the models and algorithms that drive the analytics. They have a deep understanding of the data and the tools and techniques used to analyze it.

4. **Data Engineer**: The data engineer is responsible for the design, construction, and maintenance of the data infrastructure. They ensure that the data is properly stored, processed, and made available to the data scientist and other stakeholders.

5. **Business Analyst**: The business analyst is responsible for understanding the business requirements and translating them into technical requirements for the data scientist and data engineer. They act as a bridge between the business and technical teams.

6. **Subject Matter Expert**: The subject matter expert has deep knowledge of the domain in which the project is being undertaken. They provide valuable insights and guidance to the project team.

7. **End User**: The end user is the ultimate consumer of the analytics. They use the insights generated by the project to make better decisions and improve business outcomes.

These are some of the key roles that are essential for the success of an analytics project. Each role brings unique skills and expertise to the project, and it is important to have the right mix of people to ensure that the project is successful.
