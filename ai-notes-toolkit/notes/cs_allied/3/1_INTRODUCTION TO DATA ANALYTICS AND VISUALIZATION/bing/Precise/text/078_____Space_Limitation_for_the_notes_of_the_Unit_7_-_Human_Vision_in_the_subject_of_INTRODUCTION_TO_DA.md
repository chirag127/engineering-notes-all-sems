### Space Limitation for the notes of the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Space limitation refers to the limited amount of space available for displaying visual information.
2. This can be a challenge when creating visualizations, as there may not be enough space to display all the necessary information.
3. Techniques such as zooming, panning, and filtering can be used to overcome space limitations.
4. It is important to consider space limitations when designing visualizations, as overcrowding can make it difficult for the user to understand the information being presented.
5. Effective use of space can enhance the readability and effectiveness of a visualization.
6. Space limitations can also be addressed through the use of interactive visualizations, which allow the user to explore the data in more detail.
7. The design of a visualization should take into account the available space and the amount of information that needs to be displayed.
