### Learning and Generalisation

- Learning is the process of acquiring new knowledge, skills, or behaviors through experience, study, or instruction.
- Generalisation is the ability to apply learned knowledge or skills to new situations or contexts.
- In the context of data analysis, learning refers to the process of building models from data to make predictions or decisions.
- Generalisation is the ability of a model to perform well on new, unseen data.
- A key goal in data analysis is to build models that have good generalisation performance, meaning they can accurately predict outcomes for new data.
- Overfitting is a common problem in data analysis, where a model is too complex and fits the training data too well, resulting in poor generalisation performance.
- Techniques such as cross-validation and regularisation can be used to improve the generalisation performance of a model.
- In summary, learning and generalisation are important concepts in data analysis, as they relate to the ability of a model to accurately predict outcomes for new data.
