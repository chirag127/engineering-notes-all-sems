### Discovery
The discovery phase is the first phase of the Data Analytics Lifecycle. In this phase, the following tasks are performed:

1. **Identifying the business problem:** The first step in the discovery phase is to identify the business problem that needs to be solved. This involves understanding the business objectives and the key performance indicators (KPIs) that need to be improved.

2. **Determining the data requirements:** Once the business problem has been identified, the next step is to determine the data requirements. This involves identifying the data sources and the data elements that are required to solve the business problem.

3. **Assessing the current situation:** The next step is to assess the current situation. This involves understanding the current state of the data, the data quality, and the data governance processes.

4. **Formulating the initial hypothesis:** Based on the understanding of the business problem, the data requirements, and the current situation, an initial hypothesis is formulated. This hypothesis will be tested and refined in the subsequent phases of the Data Analytics Lifecycle.

The discovery phase is a critical phase in the Data Analytics Lifecycle as it sets the foundation for the rest of the lifecycle. It is important to spend sufficient time and effort in this phase to ensure that the subsequent phases are successful.
