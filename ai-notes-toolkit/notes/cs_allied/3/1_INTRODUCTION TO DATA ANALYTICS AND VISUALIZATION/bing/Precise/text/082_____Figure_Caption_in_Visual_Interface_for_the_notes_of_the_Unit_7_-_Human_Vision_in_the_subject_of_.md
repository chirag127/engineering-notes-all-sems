### Figure Caption in Visual Interface

1. Figure captions are an important element in visual interfaces, especially in data analytics and visualization.
2. They provide a brief description of the content of the figure, including any relevant information such as the data source, units of measurement, and any other pertinent details.
3. Figure captions should be clear, concise, and informative, allowing the reader to quickly understand the content of the figure without having to refer to the main text.
4. In many cases, figure captions are placed below the figure, but they can also be placed above or to the side of the figure, depending on the design of the visual interface.
5. Figure captions should be formatted consistently throughout the document, using the same font, size, and style as the main text.
6. In some cases, figure captions may also include additional information such as a key to symbols or colors used in the figure, or a reference to a related figure or table.
7. Figure captions play a crucial role in helping the reader to interpret and understand the data presented in the figure, and should be carefully crafted to maximize their effectiveness.
