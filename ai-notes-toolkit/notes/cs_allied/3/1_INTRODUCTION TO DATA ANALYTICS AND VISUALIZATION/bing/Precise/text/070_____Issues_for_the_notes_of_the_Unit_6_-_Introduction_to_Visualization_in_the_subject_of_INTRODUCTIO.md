### Issues for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. **Data Quality**: The quality of the data used for visualization is crucial. Inaccurate or incomplete data can lead to misleading visualizations.
2. **Data Preparation**: Data must be properly prepared and cleaned before visualization. This includes dealing with missing values, outliers, and data transformation.
3. **Choice of Visualization**: The choice of visualization technique must be appropriate for the data and the message being conveyed. Using the wrong visualization can lead to confusion or misinterpretation of the data.
4. **Design**: The design of the visualization must be clear and easy to understand. Poor design can make it difficult for the viewer to interpret the data.
5. **Interactivity**: Interactive visualizations can allow the user to explore the data in more detail, but can also add complexity and may require more technical expertise to create.
6. **Ethics**: The visualization must be created and presented in an ethical manner, avoiding manipulation or misrepresentation of the data.
