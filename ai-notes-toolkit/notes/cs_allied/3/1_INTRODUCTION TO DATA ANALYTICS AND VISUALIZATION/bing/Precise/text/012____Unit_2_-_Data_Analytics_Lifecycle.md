## Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

The Data Analytics Lifecycle is a framework for conducting data analysis projects. It consists of several phases, each with its own set of tasks and objectives. The phases of the Data Analytics Lifecycle are:

1. **Discovery**: In this phase, the analyst works with the business stakeholders to understand the business problem and determine the objectives of the analysis. This includes identifying the data sources and the questions that need to be answered.

2. **Data Preparation**: In this phase, the analyst collects, cleans, and transforms the data to prepare it for analysis. This includes dealing with missing or incomplete data, removing outliers, and converting data into a format suitable for analysis.

3. **Model Planning**: In this phase, the analyst selects the appropriate analytical techniques and tools to use for the analysis. This includes identifying the variables and the relationships between them, and selecting the appropriate statistical methods.

4. **Model Building**: In this phase, the analyst builds the analytical models using the selected techniques and tools. This includes training the models on the data, testing their performance, and refining them as necessary.

5. **Communicating Results**: In this phase, the analyst presents the results of the analysis to the business stakeholders. This includes visualizing the data, explaining the findings, and making recommendations based on the results.

6. **Operationalizing**: In this final phase, the analyst works with the business stakeholders to implement the recommendations and integrate the analytical models into the business processes. This includes monitoring the performance of the models and making adjustments as necessary.

Each phase of the Data Analytics Lifecycle is important and builds upon the previous phases. By following this framework, analysts can ensure that their data analysis projects are well-structured and produce actionable insights for the business.
