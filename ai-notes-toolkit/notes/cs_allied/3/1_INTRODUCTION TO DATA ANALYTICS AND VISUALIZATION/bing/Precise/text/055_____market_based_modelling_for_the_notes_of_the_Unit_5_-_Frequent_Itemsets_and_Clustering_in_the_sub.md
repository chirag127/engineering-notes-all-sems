### Market Based Modelling

Market based modelling is a technique used in data analytics and visualization to analyze and understand the relationships between items in a market. This technique is commonly used in the field of market basket analysis, where the goal is to identify patterns and relationships between items that are frequently purchased together.

Some key points to consider when studying market based modelling include:

1. Market based modelling is used to analyze transactional data, where each transaction represents a set of items purchased together.
2. The goal of market based modelling is to identify frequent itemsets, which are sets of items that frequently appear together in transactions.
3. One common approach to market based modelling is the use of association rules, which are rules that describe the relationships between items in a market.
4. Clustering is another technique that can be used in market based modelling to group similar items together based on their relationships with other items.
5. Market based modelling can be used to inform business decisions, such as product placement and promotion, by providing insights into the relationships between items in a market.
