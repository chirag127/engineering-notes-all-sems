### Limitation for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. **Data Quality**: The quality of the data being visualized is crucial to the accuracy and effectiveness of the visualization. Poor quality data can lead to misleading or incorrect visualizations.

2. **Data Complexity**: The complexity of the data can also be a limitation. Large and complex datasets can be difficult to visualize effectively, and may require advanced techniques or tools to be properly represented.

3. **Design Choices**: The design choices made when creating a visualization can also impact its effectiveness. Poor design choices, such as inappropriate color schemes or cluttered layouts, can make it difficult for the viewer to understand the information being presented.

4. **Viewer Perception**: The perception of the viewer can also be a limitation. Different viewers may interpret the same visualization in different ways, and cultural or personal biases can impact how the information is understood.

5. **Technical Limitations**: There may also be technical limitations to consider, such as the capabilities of the software or hardware being used to create or view the visualization.
