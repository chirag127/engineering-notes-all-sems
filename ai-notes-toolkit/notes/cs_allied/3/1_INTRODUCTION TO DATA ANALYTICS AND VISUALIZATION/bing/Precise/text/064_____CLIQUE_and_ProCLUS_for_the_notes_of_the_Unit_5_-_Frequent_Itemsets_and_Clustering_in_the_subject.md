### CLIQUE and ProCLUS

CLIQUE (CLustering In QUEst) and ProCLUS (PROjected CLUStering) are two algorithms used for clustering in data mining.

- CLIQUE is an algorithm for identifying dense clusters in subspaces of high dimensional data. It automatically finds subspaces of the highest dimensionality such that high-density clusters exist in those subspaces.

- ProCLUS is an algorithm for projected clustering, which is a type of subspace clustering. It finds clusters in subspaces of the data by first identifying a set of cluster representatives, and then assigning points to clusters based on their distance to the representatives in the subspace.

These algorithms are used in the context of frequent itemsets and clustering, which is a topic in the subject of Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization. They can be useful for finding patterns and relationships in large datasets.
