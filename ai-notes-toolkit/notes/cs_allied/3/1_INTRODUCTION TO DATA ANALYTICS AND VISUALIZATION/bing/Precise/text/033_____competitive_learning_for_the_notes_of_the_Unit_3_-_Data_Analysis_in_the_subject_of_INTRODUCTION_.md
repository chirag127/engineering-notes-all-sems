### Competitive Learning

Competitive learning is a type of learning that can be applied to data analysis. The goal of competitive analysis is to provide the information needed to make data-driven business decisions, from product offerings to marketing. A competitive analysis helps in identifying and evaluating competitors’ strategies and apparent future actions.

Here are some key points to consider when conducting a competitive analysis:

1. The goal of a competitive analysis is to provide information that can be used to make data-driven business decisions.
2. A competitive analysis helps to learn more about competitors and find ways to perform better than them.
3. Competitive analysis can help in identifying and evaluating competitors’ strategies and apparent future actions.
4. The marginal value of learning from customer data can remain high even after a large customer base has been acquired, leading to significant competitive advantages.
