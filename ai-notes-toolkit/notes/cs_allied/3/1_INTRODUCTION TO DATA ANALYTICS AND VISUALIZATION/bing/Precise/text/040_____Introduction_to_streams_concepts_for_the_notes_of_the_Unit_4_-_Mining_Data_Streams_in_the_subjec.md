### Introduction to Streams Concepts

1. A stream is a sequence of data elements that are made available over time.
2. Streams are often used to represent data that is continuously generated by a source, such as sensor data, log files, or social media feeds.
3. In the context of data analytics and visualization, streams are used to process and analyze data in real-time.
4. Stream processing involves the use of algorithms and techniques to extract insights and knowledge from data as it is generated.
5. Common stream processing tasks include filtering, aggregation, and pattern detection.
6. Stream processing is often used in applications such as fraud detection, real-time analytics, and monitoring.
7. Stream processing can be challenging due to the large volume and high velocity of data, as well as the need for low-latency processing.
8. Techniques such as sampling, sketching, and approximation algorithms are often used to address these challenges.
9. Stream processing systems are designed to be scalable and fault-tolerant, and often make use of distributed computing and data storage technologies.
