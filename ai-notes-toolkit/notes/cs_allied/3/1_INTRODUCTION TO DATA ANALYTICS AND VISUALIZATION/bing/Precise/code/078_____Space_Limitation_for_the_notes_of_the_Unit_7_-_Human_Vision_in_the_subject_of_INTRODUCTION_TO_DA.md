### Space Limitation for the notes of the Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Space limitation refers to the limited amount of space available for displaying visual information.
2. This can be a challenge when working with large datasets or complex visualizations, as there may not be enough space to display all the information in a clear and concise manner.
3. Techniques such as filtering, aggregation, and zooming can be used to overcome space limitations and effectively display large amounts of data.
4. It is important to consider the available space when designing visualizations, as overcrowding can make it difficult for the user to interpret the information.
5. Effective use of space can enhance the readability and interpretability of visualizations, making it easier for the user to gain insights from the data.
