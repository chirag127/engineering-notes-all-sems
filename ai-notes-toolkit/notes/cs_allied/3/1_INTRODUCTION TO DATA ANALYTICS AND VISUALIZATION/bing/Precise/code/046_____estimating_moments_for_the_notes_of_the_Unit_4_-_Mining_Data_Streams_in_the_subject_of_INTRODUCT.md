### Estimating Moments for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the Subject of Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization

1. **Moments** are statistical measures that describe the shape of a data distribution.
2. The **first moment** is the mean, which measures the central tendency of the data.
3. The **second moment** is the variance, which measures the spread of the data.
4. The **third moment** is the skewness, which measures the asymmetry of the data distribution.
5. The **fourth moment** is the kurtosis, which measures the peakedness of the data distribution.
6. In the context of data streams, it is often necessary to estimate moments in an online fashion, as the data is continuously arriving.
7. One common approach for estimating moments in data streams is the **AMS algorithm**, which uses random sampling to estimate the moments of the data stream.
8. Another approach is the **Count-Sketch algorithm**, which uses a sketch data structure to estimate the moments of the data stream.
9. These algorithms can provide accurate estimates of the moments of the data stream while using only a small amount of memory.
