### Time Limitations

In the context of Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION, time limitations refer to the constraints on the amount of time available for the human visual system to process information.

1. The human visual system has a limited capacity to process information, and this capacity is affected by the amount of time available for processing.
2. When information is presented rapidly or for a short duration, the visual system may not have enough time to fully process the information, leading to reduced comprehension and retention.
3. Time limitations can also affect the ability to make accurate judgments and decisions based on visual information.
4. To mitigate the effects of time limitations, it is important to present information in a clear and organized manner, and to allow sufficient time for the visual system to process the information.
5. Techniques such as pre-attentive processing, which involves the rapid and automatic processing of visual information, can also help to overcome time limitations.
6. In the context of data analytics and visualization, it is important to consider time limitations when designing visualizations and presenting data to ensure that the information is effectively communicated to the viewer.
