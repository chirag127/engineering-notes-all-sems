### Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

The data analytics lifecycle is a framework for carrying out data analytics projects. It consists of several phases that guide the process from start to finish. These phases are:

1. **Discovery**: In this phase, the problem or opportunity is identified and defined. The scope of the project is determined, and the resources required are identified.

2. **Data Preparation**: This phase involves collecting, cleaning, and transforming the data to be used in the analysis. This may involve dealing with missing or inconsistent data, and converting data into a format that can be used for analysis.

3. **Model Planning**: In this phase, the data is analyzed to determine the most appropriate model or technique to use. This may involve selecting variables, choosing a model type, and determining the parameters of the model.

4. **Model Building**: In this phase, the model is built and tested. This may involve training the model on a subset of the data, and then evaluating its performance on a separate validation set.

5. **Communicating Results**: In this phase, the results of the analysis are communicated to stakeholders. This may involve creating visualizations, reports, or presentations to convey the findings.

6. **Operationalizing**: In this final phase, the model is deployed and integrated into the organization's operations. This may involve automating the model, or incorporating it into a larger system.

Each phase of the data analytics lifecycle is important, and skipping any of them can lead to suboptimal results. By following this framework, organizations can ensure that their data analytics projects are carried out in a structured and effective manner.
