### Figure Caption in Visual Interface

1. Figure captions are an important element in visual interfaces, especially in the context of data analytics and visualization.
2. They provide a brief description of the visual content, such as a chart or graph, and help the viewer understand the information being presented.
3. Figure captions should be concise, clear, and informative, and should accurately reflect the content of the visual.
4. They should be placed in close proximity to the visual, usually below it, and should be easily distinguishable from the main text.
5. Figure captions can also include additional information, such as the source of the data, the units of measurement, or any relevant notes or explanations.
6. In the context of data analytics and visualization, figure captions can help the viewer interpret the data and draw meaningful conclusions.
7. They can also provide context and background information, and can help the viewer compare and contrast different visualizations.
8. Overall, figure captions are an essential component of effective visual communication in data analytics and visualization.
