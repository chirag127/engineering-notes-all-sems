### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

In order to successfully carry out an analytic project, several key roles must be fulfilled. These roles include:

1. **Project Sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for providing the necessary resources and support for the project. They also help to define the project's goals and objectives.

2. **Project Manager**: The project manager is responsible for overseeing the project and ensuring that it is completed on time and within budget. They also coordinate the efforts of the project team.

3. **Data Scientist**: The data scientist is responsible for analyzing the data and developing models to extract insights and make predictions. They also help to identify the most appropriate data sources and analytical techniques.

4. **Data Engineer**: The data engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining the data infrastructure. They also ensure that the data is properly stored, processed, and made available to the data scientist.

5. **Business Analyst**: The business analyst is responsible for translating the insights and predictions generated by the data scientist into actionable recommendations for the business. They also help to communicate the results of the analysis to stakeholders.

6. **Domain Expert**: The domain expert provides subject matter expertise and helps to ensure that the analysis is grounded in the realities of the business. They also help to validate the results of the analysis.

These roles are critical for the success of an analytic project, and it is important to ensure that the right individuals are selected to fill them. Each role requires a unique set of skills and expertise, and it is important to carefully consider the qualifications of potential candidates before making a selection.
