## Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering

1. **Frequent Itemsets**: Frequent itemsets are sets of items that appear together frequently in a dataset. They are used in market basket analysis to identify items that are commonly purchased together.
2. **Association Rule Mining**: Association rule mining is the process of discovering interesting relationships between variables in large databases. It is used to identify patterns and relationships between items in a dataset.
3. **Apriori Algorithm**: The Apriori algorithm is a popular algorithm for mining frequent itemsets. It uses a bottom-up approach, where frequent subsets are extended one item at a time and groups of candidates are tested against the data.
4. **Clustering**: Clustering is the process of grouping similar objects together. It is used in data analysis to identify patterns and relationships in data.
5. **K-means Clustering**: K-means clustering is a popular clustering algorithm that partitions a dataset into k clusters, where k is a user-specified parameter. It iteratively assigns each data point to the cluster with the nearest mean and recalculates the means of the clusters until convergence.
6. **Hierarchical Clustering**: Hierarchical clustering is a clustering algorithm that builds a hierarchy of clusters by either merging smaller clusters into larger ones or by splitting larger clusters into smaller ones. It can be visualized using a dendrogram.
