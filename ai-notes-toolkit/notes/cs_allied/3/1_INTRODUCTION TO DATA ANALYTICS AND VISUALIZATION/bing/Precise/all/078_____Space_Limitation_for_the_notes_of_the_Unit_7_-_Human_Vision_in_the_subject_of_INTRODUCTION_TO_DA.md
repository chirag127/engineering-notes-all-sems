# Unit 7 - Human Vision: Space Limitation

- Human vision is limited by the physical space available for the display of visual information.
- This limitation can be overcome by using techniques such as zooming, panning, and scrolling to navigate through large datasets.
- Another approach is to use data reduction techniques such as aggregation, filtering, and sampling to reduce the amount of data that needs to be displayed.
- Data visualization tools can also help to overcome space limitations by providing interactive and dynamic visualizations that allow users to explore and analyze large datasets.
- Effective use of color, shape, and size can also help to convey information in a limited space.
- It is important to consider the trade-off between the amount of information displayed and the ease of interpretation when designing visualizations to overcome space limitations.
