# Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization

1. **Visualization** refers to the representation of data or information in a visual format, such as a chart, graph, or diagram.
2. The goal of visualization is to **communicate** information clearly and effectively to the audience.
3. Visualization can help to **identify patterns, trends, and relationships** in the data that may not be easily discernible from raw data.
4. There are many **tools and techniques** available for creating visualizations, including specialized software and programming languages.
5. When creating a visualization, it is important to consider the **audience** and the **message** that you want to convey.
6. Effective visualizations should be **clear, concise, and easy to understand**.
7. Common types of visualizations include **bar charts, line graphs, scatter plots, and pie charts**.
8. The choice of visualization type will depend on the **type of data** and the **message** that you want to convey.
9. It is important to **label** the axes and provide a **legend** to help the audience understand the visualization.
10. **Color** can be used to highlight important information or to distinguish between different data series.
