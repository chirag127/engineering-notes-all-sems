# Unit 7 - Human Vision

## Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization

### Design

1. Human vision is an important aspect to consider when designing visualizations.
2. The human eye is capable of detecting a wide range of colors, but is more sensitive to certain colors than others.
3. The use of color in visualizations should be carefully considered to ensure that the information is effectively communicated.
4. The use of contrast, size, and position can also help to draw attention to important information.
5. The design of visualizations should also take into account the limitations of human vision, such as the inability to focus on multiple areas at once.
6. The use of interactive elements, such as zooming and panning, can help to overcome these limitations and allow the user to explore the data in more detail.
7. The design of visualizations should also consider the intended audience and their level of expertise, as well as the context in which the visualization will be used.
8. The use of clear and concise labels and annotations can help to ensure that the information is easily understood.
9. The design of visualizations should be tested with users to ensure that the information is effectively communicated and that the visualization is easy to use.
10. The use of established design principles and guidelines can help to ensure that visualizations are effective and user-friendly.
