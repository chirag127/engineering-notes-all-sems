# Images for the notes of the Unit 7

- Images are visual representations of information, data or knowledge.
- They can be used to convey complex information in a simple and easy-to-understand manner.
- Images can be in the form of photographs, drawings, diagrams, charts, graphs, maps, etc.
- They can be used to supplement text, making it easier for the reader to understand the content.
- Images can be used to break up large blocks of text, making the content more visually appealing and easier to read.
- They can also be used to provide visual examples or illustrations of concepts discussed in the text.
- When using images in notes, it is important to ensure that they are relevant to the content and are of good quality.
- Images should be properly labeled and captioned to provide context and additional information.
- It is also important to properly cite the source of the image if it is not original content.
