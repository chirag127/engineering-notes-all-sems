# Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

In the context of the Data Analytics Lifecycle, there are several key roles that are essential for the success of an analytic project. These roles include:

1. **Project Sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for providing the necessary resources and support for the project. They are also responsible for ensuring that the project aligns with the overall goals and objectives of the organization.

2. **Project Manager**: The project manager is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of the project. They are responsible for ensuring that the project is completed on time, within budget, and to the desired level of quality.

3. **Data Scientist**: The data scientist is responsible for analyzing the data and developing models to extract insights and make predictions. They are also responsible for communicating the results of their analysis to the project team and stakeholders.

4. **Data Engineer**: The data engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining the data infrastructure that supports the project. This includes tasks such as data ingestion, data transformation, and data storage.

5. **Business Analyst**: The business analyst is responsible for understanding the business context of the project and translating business requirements into technical requirements. They are also responsible for ensuring that the project delivers value to the organization.

6. **Subject Matter Expert**: The subject matter expert is responsible for providing domain-specific knowledge and expertise to the project team. They help to ensure that the project is grounded in the realities of the business and that the results of the analysis are relevant and actionable.

These roles are critical for the success of an analytic project, and it is important to ensure that the right people are in place to fulfill these roles. Each role brings a unique set of skills and expertise to the project, and it is important to ensure that there is effective collaboration and communication between the different roles to ensure the success of the project.
