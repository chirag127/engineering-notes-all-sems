# Competitive Learning

Competitive learning is a type of unsupervised learning in which the neurons in a neural network compete with each other to become active. It is used in data analysis to cluster data into different groups based on their similarity.

Here are some key points to note about competitive learning:

- The goal of competitive learning is to provide information that can be used to make data-driven business decisions, from product offerings to marketing .
- Competitive analysis helps in identifying and evaluating competitors’ strategies and apparent future actions .
- The more data a machine learning algorithm processes, the “smarter” it becomes, allowing for more accurate predictions .
- When the marginal value of learning from customer data remains high even after a very large customer base has been acquired, products and services tend to have significant competitive advantages .
