# Unit 7 - Human Vision: Time Limitations

- Human vision has certain time limitations that affect the perception of visual information.
- The human eye can only perceive changes in visual stimuli that occur within a certain time frame.
- For example, the human eye can perceive changes in light intensity that occur within 1/100th of a second.
- This means that if a visual stimulus changes faster than this time frame, the human eye may not be able to perceive the change.
- This time limitation is known as the critical flicker fusion frequency (CFF).
- The CFF varies depending on the individual and the conditions, such as the level of light and the location of the stimulus on the retina.
- The CFF is also affected by factors such as age, fatigue, and the use of certain medications.
- Understanding the time limitations of human vision is important in the design of visual displays and interfaces, as it can affect the readability and effectiveness of the information presented.
