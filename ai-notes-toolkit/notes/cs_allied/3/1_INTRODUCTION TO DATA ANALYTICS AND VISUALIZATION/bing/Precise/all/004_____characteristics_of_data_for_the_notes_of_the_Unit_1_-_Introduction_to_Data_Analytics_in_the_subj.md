### Characteristics of Data

Data is a collection of facts, figures, and statistics that can be processed to produce information. The following are some of the characteristics of data:

1. **Accuracy**: Data should be accurate and free from errors. Inaccurate data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

2. **Relevance**: Data should be relevant to the problem or question being addressed. Irrelevant data can lead to confusion and incorrect conclusions.

3. **Timeliness**: Data should be up-to-date and reflect the current situation. Outdated data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

4. **Completeness**: Data should be complete and include all relevant information. Incomplete data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

5. **Consistency**: Data should be consistent and not contain contradictions. Inconsistent data can lead to confusion and incorrect conclusions.

6. **Accessibility**: Data should be easily accessible to those who need it. Inaccessible data can lead to delays and missed opportunities.

7. **Understandability**: Data should be presented in a clear and understandable manner. Confusing or unclear data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

These characteristics are important for ensuring that data is of high quality and can be used effectively in data analytics and visualization.
