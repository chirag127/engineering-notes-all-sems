### Analysis vs Reporting

#### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

In the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION, it is important to understand the difference between analysis and reporting.

- **Analysis** involves examining data to uncover insights, trends, and patterns. It is a process of exploring and interpreting data to extract meaningful information and draw conclusions.

- **Reporting**, on the other hand, involves presenting data in an organized and summarized manner. It is a process of communicating information, often in the form of tables, charts, or graphs.

- While reporting provides a snapshot of what has happened, analysis goes deeper to explain why it happened and what can be done about it.

- Reporting is often a prerequisite for analysis, as it provides the data that is needed for analysis.

- Analysis is more complex and requires more expertise than reporting. It involves using statistical and analytical tools to make sense of the data.

- Both analysis and reporting are important components of data analytics and visualization. Reporting helps to communicate information, while analysis helps to uncover insights and drive decision-making.

In summary, analysis and reporting are two distinct but related processes in data analytics and visualization. Reporting presents data in an organized manner, while analysis explores and interprets the data to extract meaningful insights. Both are important for making informed decisions based on data.
