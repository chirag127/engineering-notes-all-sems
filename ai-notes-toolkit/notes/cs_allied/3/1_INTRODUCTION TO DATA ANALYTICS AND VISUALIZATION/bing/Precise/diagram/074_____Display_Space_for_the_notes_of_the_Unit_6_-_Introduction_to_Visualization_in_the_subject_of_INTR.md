### Display Space for the notes of the Unit 6 - Introduction to Visualization in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Visualization is the graphical representation of data and information.
2. It is used to communicate information clearly and effectively through graphical means.
3. Visualization can help to identify patterns, trends, and relationships that might go undetected in text-based data.
4. There are many types of visualizations, including charts, graphs, maps, and diagrams.
5. The choice of visualization depends on the type of data being represented and the message being conveyed.
6. Effective visualization requires careful consideration of the design, including the use of color, size, and shape to convey information.
7. Visualization tools and software can help to create and customize visualizations.
8. Visualization is an important tool in data analytics and can help to support decision-making and problem-solving.
