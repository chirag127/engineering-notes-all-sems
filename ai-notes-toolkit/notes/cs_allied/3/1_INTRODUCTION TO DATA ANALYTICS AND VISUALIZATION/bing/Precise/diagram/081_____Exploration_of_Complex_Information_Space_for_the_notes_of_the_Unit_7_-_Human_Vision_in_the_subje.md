### Exploration of Complex Information Space

Unit 7 - Human Vision in the subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

1. Complex information spaces refer to large, multi-dimensional data sets that can be challenging to navigate and understand.
2. Effective exploration of complex information spaces requires the use of appropriate tools and techniques to help users make sense of the data.
3. Human vision plays a crucial role in the exploration of complex information spaces, as it is the primary means by which we perceive and interpret visual information.
4. The human visual system is highly adept at detecting patterns and relationships in data, making it well-suited for tasks such as identifying trends, clusters, and outliers.
5. Visual analytics is a field that combines the strengths of human vision with the power of computational analysis to enable the exploration of complex information spaces.
6. Techniques such as interactive visualization, dimensionality reduction, and machine learning can be used to help users navigate and make sense of complex data sets.
7. By leveraging the strengths of human vision and computational analysis, it is possible to gain new insights and understanding from complex information spaces.
