### Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams: Stock Market Predictions

1. Stock market data analysis needs the help of artificial intelligence and data mining techniques .
2. The volatility of stock prices depends on gains or losses of certain companies .
3. Many people consider stock market prediction as gambling. However, it is possible to generate constructive patterns by the analysis of stock prices .
4. Data mining techniques can be applied on past and present financial data to generate patterns and decision-making algorithms .
5. Datasets for stock market prediction can be found on websites like Kaggle. These datasets have many columns like date, open, close, high, low, and turnover, etc .
6. High and low are the boundaries of the stock prices, where a close column gives the closing value for the end of the day .
7. The traditional method of accessing real-time market data requires firms to co-locate in data centers, purchase and maintain physical hardware .
8. Precisely predicting stocks is essential for investors to gain enormous profits. However, the volatility of the market makes this kind of prediction highly difficult .
9. Data Mining and Machine Learning could be used to guide an investor’s decisions .
10. The stock market contains rich, valuable and considerable data, and these data need careful analysis for good decisions to be made that can lead to increases in the efficiency of a business .
11. Data mining techniques offer data processing tools and applications used to enhance decision-maker decisions .
