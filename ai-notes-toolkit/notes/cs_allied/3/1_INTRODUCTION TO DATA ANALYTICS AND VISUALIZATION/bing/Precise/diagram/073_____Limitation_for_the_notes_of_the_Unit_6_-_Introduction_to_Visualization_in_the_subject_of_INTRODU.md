### Limitations of Visualization

1. **Data Quality**: The quality of the data being visualized is crucial for the accuracy and effectiveness of the visualization. Poor data quality can lead to misleading or inaccurate visualizations.

2. **Data Complexity**: Visualizations can become cluttered and difficult to interpret when dealing with large and complex datasets. It is important to carefully select and organize the data to be visualized to avoid overwhelming the viewer.

3. **Perception and Interpretation**: Different viewers may perceive and interpret the same visualization differently. It is important to consider the intended audience and their background knowledge when designing a visualization.

4. **Design Choices**: The design choices made when creating a visualization, such as the choice of color, scale, and layout, can greatly impact its effectiveness. Poor design choices can make a visualization difficult to understand or misleading.

5. **Technical Limitations**: There may be technical limitations to the types of visualizations that can be created, such as the capabilities of the software or hardware being used.

6. **Time and Resources**: Creating effective visualizations can be time-consuming and may require specialized knowledge and resources. This can limit the ability to quickly create and share visualizations.
