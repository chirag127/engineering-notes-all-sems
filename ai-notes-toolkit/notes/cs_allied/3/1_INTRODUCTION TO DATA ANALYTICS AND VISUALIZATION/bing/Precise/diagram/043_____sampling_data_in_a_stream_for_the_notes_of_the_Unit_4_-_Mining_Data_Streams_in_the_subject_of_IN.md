### Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams
#### Sampling Data in a Stream

1. Sampling is a technique used to extract a subset of data from a larger dataset, or data stream, for analysis.
2. Sampling can be used to reduce the amount of data that needs to be processed, making it more manageable and efficient to analyze.
3. There are several sampling techniques that can be used when dealing with data streams, including simple random sampling, systematic sampling, and stratified sampling.
4. Simple random sampling involves selecting a random subset of the data stream for analysis.
5. Systematic sampling involves selecting every nth element from the data stream for analysis.
6. Stratified sampling involves dividing the data stream into different subgroups, or strata, and then selecting a sample from each stratum for analysis.
7. The choice of sampling technique will depend on the characteristics of the data stream and the goals of the analysis.
8. It is important to ensure that the sample is representative of the larger dataset, or data stream, in order to draw valid conclusions from the analysis.
