### Market Based Modelling

Market based modelling is a technique used in data analytics and visualization to analyze and understand customer behavior and preferences. It is commonly used in the field of retail and marketing to identify patterns and trends in customer purchasing behavior.

Here are some key points to consider when studying market based modelling:

1. Market based modelling is used to analyze transaction data to identify patterns and trends in customer purchasing behavior.
2. This technique can help businesses to better understand their customers and make informed decisions about product offerings and marketing strategies.
3. Market based modelling can be used to identify frequent itemsets, which are sets of items that are commonly purchased together by customers.
4. Clustering techniques can also be used in market based modelling to group customers with similar purchasing behavior.
5. Market based modelling can provide valuable insights into customer behavior and preferences, which can help businesses to improve their products and services and increase customer satisfaction.
