# Estimating Moments for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the Subject of INTRODUCTION TO DATA ANALYTICS AND VISUALIZATION

- Moments are statistical measures that describe the shape of a data distribution.
- The first moment is the mean, which measures the central tendency of the data.
- The second moment is the variance, which measures the spread of the data.
- The third moment is the skewness, which measures the asymmetry of the data distribution.
- The fourth moment is the kurtosis, which measures the "peakedness" of the data distribution.
- In the context of mining data streams, estimating moments can be useful for understanding the underlying distribution of the data and for detecting changes in the data distribution over time.
- There are several algorithms for estimating moments in data streams, including the AMS algorithm, the Count-Sketch algorithm, and the Count-Min algorithm.
- These algorithms use sketch data structures to provide approximate estimates of the moments with a small amount of memory.
- The accuracy of the estimates can be improved by using multiple independent sketches and combining their results.
- Estimating moments in data streams is an active area of research, with ongoing work on developing new algorithms and improving the accuracy of existing algorithms.
