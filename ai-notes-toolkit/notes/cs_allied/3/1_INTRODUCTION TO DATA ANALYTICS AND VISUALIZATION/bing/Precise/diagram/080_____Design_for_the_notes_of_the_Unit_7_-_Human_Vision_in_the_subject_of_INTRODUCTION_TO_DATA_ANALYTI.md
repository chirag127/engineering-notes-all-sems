### Unit 7 - Human Vision

#### Introduction to Data Analytics and Visualization

1. Human vision is the primary sense used in data visualization.
2. The human visual system is highly developed and can quickly interpret and understand visual information.
3. The design of data visualizations should take into account the capabilities and limitations of human vision.

#### Perception and Cognition

1. Perception refers to the process of interpreting sensory information.
2. Cognition refers to the mental processes involved in understanding and making sense of information.
3. The design of data visualizations should consider both perception and cognition to effectively communicate information.

#### Color Perception

1. Color is an important aspect of data visualization design.
2. The human visual system can perceive a wide range of colors, but there are limitations to color perception.
3. The use of color in data visualizations should be carefully considered to effectively communicate information.

#### Visual Encoding

1. Visual encoding refers to the use of visual elements to represent data.
2. Common visual encodings include position, length, angle, and color.
3. The choice of visual encoding should be appropriate for the data and the intended message.

#### Gestalt Principles

1. The Gestalt principles describe how the human visual system organizes visual information.
2. These principles include proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, and symmetry.
3. The design of data visualizations should consider the Gestalt principles to effectively communicate information.

#### Conclusion

1. Human vision is a complex and highly developed sense that plays a crucial role in data visualization.
2. The design of data visualizations should take into account the capabilities and limitations of human vision, as well as principles of perception and cognition.
3. Effective data visualization design can greatly enhance the communication of information.
