# Area of a given shape

- The area of a shape is the amount of space that it covers in a flat surface. Different shapes have different formulas for calculating their areas.
- To find the area of a given shape, we need to read the shape name and the appropriate values from the standard input. The standard input is the source of data that the program receives, such as the keyboard or a file.
- Here are the formulas and examples for finding the area of a rectangle, a triangle and a circle.

## Area of a rectangle

- A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides equal and right angles.
- The area of a rectangle is given by the formula: `area = length * width`, where length and width are the measurements of the sides of the rectangle.
- To find the area of a rectangle, we need to read the shape name, the length and the width from the standard input. For example, if the input is:

```
rectangle
10
5
```

- Then the output is:

```
The area of the rectangle is 50 square units.
```

## Area of a triangle

- A triangle is a three-sided shape with three angles that add up to 180 degrees.
- The area of a triangle is given by the formula: `area = (base * height) / 2`, where base and height are the measurements of the base and the perpendicular height of the triangle.
- To find the area of a triangle, we need to read the shape name, the base and the height from the standard input. For example, if the input is:

```
triangle
12
8
```

- Then the output is:

```
The area of the triangle is 48 square units.
```

## Area of a circle

- A circle is a shape with all points at the same distance from the center.
- The area of a circle is given by the formula: `area = pi * radius * radius`, where pi is a constant value that is approximately equal to 3.14, and radius is the measurement of the distance from the center to any point on the circle.
- To find the area of a circle, we need to read the shape name and the radius from the standard input. For example, if the input is:

```
circle
5
```

- Then the output is:

```
The area of the circle is 78.5 square units.
```
