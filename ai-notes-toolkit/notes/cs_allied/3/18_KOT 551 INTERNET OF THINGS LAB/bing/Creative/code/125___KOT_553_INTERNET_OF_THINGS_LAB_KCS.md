# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- This is a course offered by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University for the third year students of Computer Science and Engineering (IoT) branch.
- The course aims to provide practical exposure to the students on the concepts, principles and challenges of Internet of Things (IoT).
- IoT is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, actuators, and communication devices that can interact with each other and the environment through the Internet.
- The course covers the following topics:

  - Introduction to IoT and its applications
  - Hardware devices and sensors used for IoT
  - Communication protocols and standards for IoT
  - Cloud computing and data analytics for IoT
  - Security and privacy issues in IoT
  - IoT project development and deployment

- The course has a total of 50 marks, divided into 30 marks for internal assessment and 20 marks for external assessment.
- The internal assessment consists of 10 marks for attendance, 10 marks for lab performance, and 10 marks for lab records.
- The external assessment consists of a practical exam of 20 marks, where the students have to demonstrate their IoT projects.
- The course requires the students to have basic knowledge of programming, networking, and electronics.
- The course also requires the students to have access to IoT devices and sensors, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP8266, etc.
- The course is designed to help the students to develop skills and competencies in IoT, such as:

  - Analyzing and designing IoT solutions for various domains
  - Programming and interfacing IoT devices and sensors
  - Communicating and transferring data among IoT devices and the cloud
  - Processing and visualizing IoT data using cloud services and tools
  - Securing and protecting IoT devices and data from cyberattacks
  - Testing and deploying IoT projects in real-world scenarios
