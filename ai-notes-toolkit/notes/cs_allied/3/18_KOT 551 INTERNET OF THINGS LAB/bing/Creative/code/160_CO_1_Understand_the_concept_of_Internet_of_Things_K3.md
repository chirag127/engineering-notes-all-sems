# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of physical objects or devices that have sensors, processing ability, software and other technologies that enable them to communicate and exchange data with other devices and systems over the Internet or other networks .
- IoT allows information to be sent and received from objects and devices that are embedded in everyday objects, such as fixtures and kitchen appliances.
- IoT can be used for various purposes, such as remote monitoring, status, manipulation and evaluation of trends of such devices.
- IoT can also enable smart applications and services that can improve the quality of life, efficiency, productivity, security and sustainability of various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, etc.
- IoT can also pose challenges and risks, such as privacy, security, interoperability, scalability, reliability, etc. that need to be addressed and managed.
