# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

## Introduction

The Internet of Things (IoT) is defined as the network of physical objects, things that are embedded, with sensors, software and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with, other devices and systems over the internet. IoT can enable various applications such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, etc.

The IoT Lab is a resource for students and faculty to experiment with the latest devices and datasets for research and applications. The lab provides access to various IoT devices, such as sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, cameras, etc., and platforms, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, NodeMCU, etc. The lab also provides guidance and support for developing IoT projects, such as data collection, processing, analysis, visualization, etc.

## Objectives

The objectives of the IoT Lab are:

- To introduce the basic concepts and principles of IoT, such as architecture, protocols, standards, security, etc.
- To familiarize the students with various IoT devices, platforms, and tools, such as sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, Arduino, Raspberry Pi, NodeMCU, etc.
- To enable the students to design, implement, and test IoT projects, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, etc.
- To enhance the students' skills in data collection, processing, analysis, and visualization using IoT devices and platforms
- To encourage the students to explore the latest trends and challenges in IoT research and applications

## Syllabus

The syllabus of the IoT Lab covers the following topics:

- Introduction to IoT: Definition, characteristics, applications, architecture, protocols, standards, security, etc.
- IoT Devices: Sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, cameras, etc.
- IoT Platforms: Arduino, Raspberry Pi, NodeMCU, etc.
- IoT Tools: Programming languages, libraries, frameworks, IDEs, etc.
- IoT Projects: Smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, etc.
- IoT Data: Collection, processing, analysis, and visualization

## Evaluation

The evaluation of the IoT Lab is based on the following criteria:

- Attendance and participation: 10%
- Lab assignments: 40%
- Project report and presentation: 50%
