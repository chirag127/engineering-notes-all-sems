# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of physical objects or devices that have sensors, software, and other technologies that enable them to communicate and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet or other networks .
- IoT can be used for various purposes, such as remote monitoring, control, automation, optimization, security, and analytics of devices and systems.
- IoT can also integrate people, processes, and technology to create value and improve efficiency, productivity, and quality of life.
- IoT can be applied to various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart health, smart manufacturing, smart transportation, smart energy, and smart environment.
- IoT can be classified into different types, such as consumer IoT, industrial IoT, enterprise IoT, and public IoT, based on the users, applications, and characteristics of the devices and systems.
- IoT can be composed of different components, such as devices, sensors, actuators, gateways, networks, platforms, applications, and services, that work together to collect, process, store, and transmit data.
- IoT can face various challenges, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, reliability, and sustainability, that need to be addressed to ensure its successful implementation and adoption.
