# Area of a given shape

The area of a shape is the amount of space that it covers. Different shapes have different formulas for calculating their areas. In this topic, we will learn how to find the area of three common shapes: rectangle, triangle and circle.

## Rectangle

A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides equal and right angles. To find the area of a rectangle, we need to know its length and width. The formula for the area of a rectangle is:

`Area = length * width`

For example, if a rectangle has a length of 10 cm and a width of 5 cm, its area is:

`Area = 10 * 5`

`Area = 50 cm^2`

## Triangle

A triangle is a three-sided shape with three angles. To find the area of a triangle, we need to know its base and height. The formula for the area of a triangle is:

`Area = (base * height) / 2`

For example, if a triangle has a base of 12 cm and a height of 9 cm, its area is:

`Area = (12 * 9) / 2`

`Area = 54 cm^2`

To read the shape and the values of base and height from standard input, we can use the following pseudocode:

`shape = input("Enter the shape: ")`

`if shape == "triangle":`

`base = input("Enter the base: ")`

`height = input("Enter the height: ")`

`area = (base * height) / 2`

`print("The area of the triangle is", area)`

## Circle

A circle is a shape with a curved boundary that is equidistant from a fixed point called the center. To find the area of a circle, we need to know its radius, which is the distance from the center to the boundary. The formula for the area of a circle is:

`Area = pi * radius^2`

where pi is a constant that is approximately equal to 3.14. For example, if a circle has a radius of 7 cm, its area is:

`Area = pi * 7^2`
