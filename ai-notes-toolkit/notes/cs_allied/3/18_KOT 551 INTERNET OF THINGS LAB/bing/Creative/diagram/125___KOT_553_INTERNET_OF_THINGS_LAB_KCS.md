# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- This is a course offered by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University for the third year students of Computer Science and Engineering (IoT) branch.
- The course aims to provide practical exposure to the students on the concepts, principles and challenges of Internet of Things (IoT) and its applications in various domains.
- The course consists of 10 experiments that cover topics such as hardware devices and sensors, communication protocols, cloud computing, data analytics, security and privacy, smart home and smart city solutions.
- The course also requires the students to design and implement a mini project based on IoT using the knowledge and skills acquired from the experiments.
- The course is evaluated based on the performance of the students in the lab sessions, viva-voce, report writing and project presentation.
- The course outcomes are as follows:
  - CO1: Demonstrate basic concepts, principles and challenges in IoT.
  - CO2: Illustrate functioning of hardware devices and sensors used for IoT.
  - CO3: Apply communication protocols and standards for IoT.
  - CO4: Utilize cloud computing and data analytics platforms for IoT.
  - CO5: Analyze security and privacy issues in IoT.
  - CO6: Develop and deploy IoT solutions for smart home and smart city scenarios.
- The course is inspired by the Internet of Things Lab of Kohler, a company that designs and manufactures smart kitchen and bath products based on the KOHLER® Konnect platform.
- The Internet of Things Lab of Kohler serves as the design and testing ground for the smart home technology that is transforming how we interact with our kitchens and bathrooms.
- The Internet of Things Lab of Kohler also showcases the latest innovations and trends in IoT and provides a collaborative space for the researchers, engineers and designers to work together.
