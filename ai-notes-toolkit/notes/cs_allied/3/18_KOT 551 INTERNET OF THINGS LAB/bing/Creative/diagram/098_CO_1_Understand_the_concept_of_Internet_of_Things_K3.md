# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communications networks .
- IoT devices can include sensors, actuators, cameras, microphones, speakers, displays, wearables, appliances, vehicles, machines, and more.
- IoT devices can communicate with each other, with humans, or with cloud services, using various protocols and standards, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, MQTT, HTTP, etc.
- IoT devices can collect, process, store, and analyze data, using embedded software, edge computing, or cloud computing, to provide various functions and services, such as monitoring, control, automation, optimization, personalization, security, etc.
- IoT devices can be grouped into networks or systems, such as smart home, smart city, smart grid, smart agriculture, smart health, smart industry, etc., to achieve higher-level goals and benefits, such as efficiency, convenience, sustainability, quality, safety, etc.
- IoT devices can also be integrated with other technologies, such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, big data, blockchain, etc., to enhance their capabilities and applications.
