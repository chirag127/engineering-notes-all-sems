# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communications networks .
- IoT devices can range from simple sensors and actuators to complex smart appliances and wearable devices, that can communicate with each other and with other systems or services.
- IoT enables remote monitoring, control, automation, and analysis of data from physical objects, creating new opportunities and challenges for various domains such as industry, agriculture, healthcare, transportation, and smart cities.
- IoT also raises some issues and concerns regarding security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, and ethics, that need to be addressed by the stakeholders involved in the design, development, and deployment of IoT solutions.
