# Area of a given shape

- The area of a shape is the measure of how much space it occupies in a plane.
- Different shapes have different formulas for calculating their areas, depending on their properties and dimensions.
- Here are some common shapes and their area formulas:

## Rectangle

- A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles and opposite sides equal in length.
- The area of a rectangle is given by multiplying its length and width.
- The formula is: `A = l * w`, where `A` is the area, `l` is the length and `w` is the width.
- For example, if a rectangle has a length of 10 cm and a width of 5 cm, its area is `A = 10 * 5 = 50 cm^2`.

## Triangle

- A triangle is a polygon with three sides and three angles.
- The area of a triangle is given by multiplying its base and height and dividing by two.
- The formula is: `A = (b * h) / 2`, where `A` is the area, `b` is the base and `h` is the height.
- For example, if a triangle has a base of 12 cm and a height of 9 cm, its area is `A = (12 * 9) / 2 = 54 cm^2`.

## Circle

- A circle is a shape with all points equidistant from a fixed point called the center.
- The area of a circle is given by multiplying the square of its radius and the constant pi.
- The formula is: `A = pi * r^2`, where `A` is the area, `pi` is approximately 3.14 and `r` is the radius.
- For example, if a circle has a radius of 7 cm, its area is `A = pi * 7^2 = 153.86 cm^2`.
