# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a best practice methodology that aims to improve service delivery, productivity, and customer satisfaction by creating and maintaining documentation as part of the support process .

Some of the main features and benefits of KCS are:

- It is based on the principle of "capture, structure, reuse, and improve" knowledge as a by-product of solving problems .
- It empowers support agents to create, update, and publish knowledge articles without requiring approval from a central authority .
- It enables self-service and collaboration among customers, employees, and support teams by making knowledge accessible and searchable .
- It reduces costs, increases efficiency, and improves quality by avoiding duplication of work and leveraging existing knowledge .
- It fosters a culture of learning and continuous improvement by measuring and rewarding knowledge creation and reuse .

KCS is not to be confused with KCS, the acronym for Kansas City Southern, a US and cross-border railroad company .
