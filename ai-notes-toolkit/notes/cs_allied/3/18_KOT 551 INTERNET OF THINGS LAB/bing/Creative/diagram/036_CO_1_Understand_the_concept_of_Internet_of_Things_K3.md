# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communications networks .
- IoT devices can range from sensors, actuators, cameras, smart phones, smart watches, smart home appliances, wearable devices, vehicles, industrial machines, etc.
- IoT devices can communicate with each other, with cloud servers, with mobile applications, or with other systems using various protocols and standards, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, MQTT, HTTP, etc.
- IoT devices can collect, process, store, and analyze data from their environment, and perform actions based on predefined rules, user commands, or artificial intelligence algorithms.
- IoT devices can enable remote monitoring, control, automation, optimization, and personalization of various applications and services, such as smart cities, smart homes, smart health, smart agriculture, smart manufacturing, smart energy, etc.
- IoT devices can also pose challenges and risks, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, reliability, etc.
