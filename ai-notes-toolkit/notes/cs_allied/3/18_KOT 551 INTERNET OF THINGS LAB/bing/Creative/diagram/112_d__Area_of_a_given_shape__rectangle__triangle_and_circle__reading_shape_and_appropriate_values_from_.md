# Area of a given shape

The area of a shape is the amount of space that it covers. Different shapes have different formulas for calculating their areas. Here are some common shapes and their area formulas:

## Rectangle

A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides equal and right angles. The area of a rectangle is given by multiplying its length and width. The formula is:

`Area = length * width`

For example, if a rectangle has a length of 10 cm and a width of 5 cm, its area is:

`Area = 10 * 5 = 50 cm^2`

## Triangle

A triangle is a three-sided shape with three angles. The area of a triangle is given by multiplying its base and height and dividing by two. The formula is:

`Area = (base * height) / 2`

For example, if a triangle has a base of 12 cm and a height of 9 cm, its area is:

`Area = (12 * 9) / 2 = 54 cm^2`

## Circle

A circle is a shape with a curved boundary that is equidistant from a fixed point called the center. The area of a circle is given by multiplying the square of its radius and a constant called pi. The formula is:

`Area = pi * radius^2`

Pi is an irrational number that is approximately equal to 3.14. For example, if a circle has a radius of 7 cm, its area is:

`Area = 3.14 * 7^2 = 153.86 cm^2`
