# At the end of course, the student will be able to

- Define the main concepts and principles of the course subject.
- Apply the knowledge and skills acquired in the course to solve problems and perform tasks related to the course subject.
- Analyze and evaluate information, arguments, and evidence related to the course subject.
- Communicate effectively and appropriately in written and oral forms about the course subject.
- Demonstrate ethical and professional behavior and attitudes in the course subject.
- Collaborate with others and work independently in the course subject.
- Reflect on their own learning process and outcomes and identify areas for improvement in the course subject.
