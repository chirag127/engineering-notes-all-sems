# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course outcome (CO) is a statement that describes what students are expected to know, understand, or be able to do by the end of a course.
- Bloom's knowledge level (KL) is a classification of cognitive skills that students can demonstrate in learning activities, based on the revised Bloom's taxonomy of educational objectives.
- The six levels of Bloom's KL are: remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, and create.
- Each CO should be aligned with one or more KLs, depending on the complexity and depth of the learning outcome.
- The alignment of COs and KLs helps to design appropriate assessment methods and criteria, as well as to measure the effectiveness of teaching and learning.
- An example of a CO and its corresponding KLs is:

  - CO: Explain the basic concepts and principles of object-oriented programming.
  - KLs: Remember, understand, apply.
