- The topic can be broad or narrow, depending on how much information or detail it covers or excludes.
- The topic can be general or specific, depending on how abstract or concrete it is.
- The topic can be factual or opinionated, depending on how objective or subjective it is.
- The topic can be informative or persuasive, depending on how it aims to inform or influence the audience.
- The topic can be descriptive or explanatory, depending on how it portrays or analyzes the subject matter.
