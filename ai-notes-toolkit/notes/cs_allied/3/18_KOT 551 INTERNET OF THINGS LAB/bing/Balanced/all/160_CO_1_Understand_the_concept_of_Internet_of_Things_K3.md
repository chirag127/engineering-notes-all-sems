# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communications networks .
- IoT devices can include sensors, actuators, cameras, speakers, microphones, GPS, RFID, etc. that can collect, process, and transmit data to other devices or systems.
- IoT devices can also receive commands or instructions from other devices or systems, enabling remote monitoring, control, and automation of various tasks and processes.
- IoT devices can communicate with each other or with a central server or cloud platform, using various protocols and standards, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, MQTT, CoAP, etc.
- IoT devices can form networks of different sizes and scopes, such as personal area networks (PAN), local area networks (LAN), wide area networks (WAN), or global networks (Internet).
- IoT devices can provide various benefits and applications, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart health, smart industry, smart transportation, smart energy, etc.
- IoT devices can also pose various challenges and risks, such as security, privacy, interoperability, scalability, reliability, etc.
