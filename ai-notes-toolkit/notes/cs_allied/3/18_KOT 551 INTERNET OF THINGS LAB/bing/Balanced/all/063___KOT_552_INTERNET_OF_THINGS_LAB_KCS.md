# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the concept of connecting physical devices, sensors, actuators, and other objects to the internet and enabling them to communicate and exchange data with other devices and applications.
- IoT has various applications in different domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, etc.
- IoT devices can generate large amounts of data that can be analyzed and processed using various techniques, such as data analytics, machine learning, big data, etc.
- IoT devices can also be controlled and monitored remotely using web or mobile applications, or through voice or gesture commands.
- IoT devices can also interact with other devices and systems using various protocols, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc.
- IoT devices can also use cloud computing and edge computing to store, process, and access data and services.

## Lab Objectives

- The objective of this lab is to provide hands-on experience in designing, developing, and testing IoT applications using various hardware and software tools.
- The lab will cover the following topics:
  - Introduction to IoT and its components
  - IoT hardware platforms, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, etc.
  - IoT sensors and actuators, such as temperature, humidity, light, motion, etc.
  - IoT communication protocols, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc.
  - IoT cloud platforms, such as AWS IoT, Google Cloud IoT, etc.
  - IoT data analytics and visualization tools, such as Python, R, Tableau, etc.
  - IoT security and privacy issues and solutions
- The lab will consist of various experiments and projects that will require the students to apply the concepts and skills learned in the lab.
- The lab will also encourage the students to explore and innovate new IoT applications and solutions for real-world problems.
