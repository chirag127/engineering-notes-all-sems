# At the end of the course, the student will be able to

- Define the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence, such as agents, search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics.
- Apply various search algorithms, such as uninformed search, informed search, local search, adversarial search, and constraint satisfaction, to solve different types of problems.
- Represent and manipulate knowledge using propositional logic, first-order logic, and other formalisms, such as semantic networks, frames, and ontologies.
- Use different methods of automated reasoning, such as inference rules, resolution, forward chaining, backward chaining, and probabilistic reasoning, to draw conclusions from knowledge bases.
- Design and implement basic planning algorithms, such as state-space planning, heuristic planning, and hierarchical planning, to generate sequences of actions that achieve goals.
- Explain the main concepts and techniques of machine learning, such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, reinforcement learning, neural networks, and deep learning, and apply them to various domains and tasks.
- Analyze and process natural language texts using linguistic models, such as syntax, semantics, pragmatics, and discourse, and implement natural language applications, such as parsing, information extraction, question answering, and dialogue systems.
- Understand the fundamentals of computer vision, such as image formation, feature extraction, segmentation, object recognition, face detection, and optical flow, and use computer vision libraries, such as OpenCV, to perform image analysis and manipulation.
- Describe the main components and challenges of robotics, such as perception, localization, mapping, navigation, control, and coordination, and program robots, such as TurtleBot, to perform simple tasks.
