# The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc.

- Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc. and convert them into electrical signals that can be processed by a microcontroller or a computer.
- Hands on experience in using sensors means that the student should be able to connect, configure, calibrate, and test different types of sensors using appropriate hardware and software tools.
- Hands on experience in using sensors is important for the following reasons:

  - It helps the student to understand the working principle, characteristics, and limitations of various sensors and how they can be applied in different scenarios and applications.
  - It enhances the student's practical skills and problem-solving abilities in designing, building, and debugging sensor-based systems and circuits.
  - It exposes the student to the challenges and opportunities of sensor technology and its integration with other components such as actuators, displays, communication modules, etc.
  - It fosters the student's creativity and innovation in developing new solutions and products using sensors and sensor data.
  - It prepares the student for the future trends and demands of the sensor industry and the Internet of Things (IoT) domain.
