# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is defined as the network of physical objects, things that are embedded, with sensors, software and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with, other devices and systems over the internet.
- IoT has many applications in various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart transportation, smart industry, etc.
- IoT lab is a resource for students and faculty to experiment with the latest devices and datasets for research and applications.
- IoT lab consists of various hardware and software components, such as sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, communication modules, cloud platforms, data analytics tools, etc.
- IoT lab enables students and faculty to learn and practice the following skills and concepts:
  - Design and implement IoT solutions using various hardware and software components.
  - Understand and use various communication protocols and standards for IoT, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, LoRa, etc.
  - Connect and manage IoT devices and data using cloud platforms, such as AWS IoT, Google Cloud IoT, Microsoft Azure IoT, etc.
  - Analyze and visualize IoT data using data analytics tools, such as Python, R, Tableau, etc.
  - Apply security and privacy principles and techniques to IoT systems, such as encryption, authentication, authorization, etc.
  - Explore and evaluate the benefits and challenges of IoT for various domains and applications.
- IoT lab requires students and faculty to follow the following guidelines and procedures:
  - Read and follow the lab manual and instructions carefully.
  - Use the lab equipment and resources responsibly and safely.
  - Do not damage or modify the lab equipment and resources without permission.
  - Report any issues or problems to the lab instructor or supervisor.
  - Complete the lab assignments and projects on time and with quality.
  - Document and present the lab work and results clearly and effectively.
