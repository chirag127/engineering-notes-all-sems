#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

- Wireless data transmission is the process of sending and receiving information without using physical wires or cables.
- Wireless data transmission can be achieved by using electromagnetic waves, such as radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, or laser beams.
- Wireless data transmission can be classified into two types: broadcast and point-to-point.
  - Broadcast wireless transmission is when a single transmitter sends data to multiple receivers within a certain range. Examples of broadcast wireless transmission are radio, television, and Wi-Fi.
  - Point-to-point wireless transmission is when a transmitter sends data to a specific receiver over a dedicated channel. Examples of point-to-point wireless transmission are cellular phones, Bluetooth, and satellite communication.
- Wireless data transmission can have advantages and disadvantages over wired data transmission, depending on the application, environment, and security requirements.
  - Advantages of wireless data transmission include:
    - Mobility and flexibility: Wireless devices can move freely and access data from different locations without being restricted by wires or cables.
    - Scalability and cost-effectiveness: Wireless networks can be easily expanded or reduced by adding or removing devices without requiring additional infrastructure or wiring.
    - Ease of installation and maintenance: Wireless devices can be installed and configured quickly and easily without requiring complex wiring or technical skills.
  - Disadvantages of wireless data transmission include:
    - Interference and noise: Wireless signals can be affected by environmental factors, such as weather, obstacles, or other wireless devices, which can cause distortion, attenuation, or loss of data.
    - Security and privacy: Wireless data transmission can be more vulnerable to unauthorized access, interception, or modification by malicious parties, as wireless signals can be easily detected or captured by anyone within the range.
    - Reliability and performance: Wireless data transmission can have lower data rates, higher latency, or higher error rates than wired data transmission, as wireless signals can be affected by interference, noise, or congestion.
