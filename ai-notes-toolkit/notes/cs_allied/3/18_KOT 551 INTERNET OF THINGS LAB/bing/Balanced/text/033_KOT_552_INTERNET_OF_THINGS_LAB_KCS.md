# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- This course is designed to provide hands-on experience in developing Internet of Things (IoT) applications using various hardware and software platforms.
- The course covers the following topics:
  - Introduction to IoT and its components, such as sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, communication protocols, cloud computing, and data analytics.
  - IoT hardware platforms, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, and NodeMCU, and their features, capabilities, and limitations.
  - IoT software platforms, such as Arduino IDE, Python, Node-RED, MQTT, and Firebase, and their functions, advantages, and disadvantages.
  - IoT application development, such as interfacing sensors and actuators with microcontrollers, sending and receiving data over wireless networks, storing and processing data in the cloud, and visualizing and analyzing data using web and mobile applications.
  - IoT application examples, such as smart home, smart city, smart agriculture, smart health, and smart industry, and their challenges, opportunities, and benefits.
- The course objectives are:
  - To understand the basic concepts and principles of IoT and its components.
  - To learn how to use various IoT hardware and software platforms for developing IoT applications.
  - To gain practical skills in designing, implementing, testing, and deploying IoT applications for different domains and scenarios.
  - To explore the current trends and future directions of IoT and its applications.
- The course outcomes are:
  - The students will be able to explain the key features and components of IoT and its applications.
  - The students will be able to select and use appropriate IoT hardware and software platforms for developing IoT applications.
  - The students will be able to design, implement, test, and deploy IoT applications for different domains and scenarios.
  - The students will be able to evaluate the performance and effectiveness of IoT applications and suggest improvements.
