# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This note is a disclaimer that the instructor has the authority and discretion to change the experiments that are part of the course syllabus or curriculum.
- The instructor may add new experiments, delete existing ones, modify the objectives, methods, or outcomes of the experiments, or tune the parameters, settings, or requirements of the experiments.
- The instructor may do this for various reasons, such as to align the experiments with the course objectives, to update the experiments with the latest developments or technologies, to accommodate the availability of resources or equipment, or to enhance the learning outcomes or experience of the students.
- The instructor may inform the students of the changes in advance or during the course, depending on the nature and extent of the changes.
- The students are expected to follow the instructions of the instructor and adapt to the changes in the experiments as part of their learning process.
