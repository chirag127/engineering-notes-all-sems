# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the concept of connecting physical devices, sensors, actuators, and other objects to the internet and enabling them to communicate and exchange data with each other.
- IoT has various applications in different domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart agriculture, smart health, smart industry, etc.
- IoT devices can collect, process, and transmit data using various protocols, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc.
- IoT devices can also interact with cloud services, such as AWS IoT, Azure IoT, Google Cloud IoT, etc., to store, analyze, and visualize the data.
- IoT devices can also use edge computing to perform some tasks locally, such as filtering, aggregation, or inference, to reduce latency, bandwidth, and energy consumption.
- IoT devices can also use artificial intelligence and machine learning to learn from the data and improve their performance, such as anomaly detection, prediction, or optimization.
- IoT devices can also use blockchain and cryptography to ensure security, privacy, and trust in the data and transactions.
- IoT devices can also use 5G and other wireless technologies to enable faster and more reliable communication and connectivity.

- KOT 552 is a lab course that aims to provide hands-on experience and practical skills in developing and deploying IoT applications using various tools and platforms.
- The course covers the following topics:

  - Introduction to IoT and its components, architecture, and challenges.
  - IoT protocols and standards, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc.
  - IoT platforms and cloud services, such as AWS IoT, Azure IoT, Google Cloud IoT, etc.
  - IoT edge computing and artificial intelligence, such as TensorFlow Lite, AWS Greengrass, Azure IoT Edge, etc.
  - IoT security and blockchain, such as encryption, authentication, authorization, etc.
  - IoT wireless technologies and 5G, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, LoRa, etc.
  - IoT applications and case studies, such as smart home, smart city, smart agriculture, smart health, smart industry, etc.

- The course requires the students to perform various experiments and projects using IoT devices, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, etc., and IoT software, such as Node-RED, Python, etc.
- The course also requires the students to write reports and present their work to the instructor and peers.
- The course evaluates the students based on their performance in the lab sessions, assignments, projects, and viva.
