#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This note indicates that the instructor has the authority and discretion to change the experiments that are part of the course or the lab.
- The instructor may do this for various reasons, such as:
  - To align the experiments with the course objectives and learning outcomes.
  - To update the experiments with the latest developments and technologies in the field.
  - To accommodate the availability of resources and equipment.
  - To adjust the difficulty and complexity of the experiments according to the level and background of the students.
  - To enhance the diversity and variety of the experiments.
- The instructor should inform the students about any changes in the experiments in advance and provide them with the necessary instructions and guidance.
- The students should follow the instructor's directions and feedback regarding the experiments and respect their decisions.
