#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

- Wireless data transmission is the process of sending and receiving information without using physical wires or cables.
- Wireless data transmission can be achieved by using electromagnetic waves, such as radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, or lasers.
- Wireless data transmission can be classified into two types: broadcast and point-to-point.
  - Broadcast wireless transmission involves sending the same data to multiple receivers at the same time, such as radio or television broadcasting.
  - Point-to-point wireless transmission involves sending data to a specific receiver, such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
- Wireless data transmission can be affected by various factors, such as distance, interference, noise, bandwidth, security, and power consumption.
- Wireless data transmission can be used for various applications, such as mobile communication, wireless networking, wireless sensor networks, wireless power transfer, and wireless charging.
