# At the end of course, the student will be able to

- Define the main concepts and principles of the course topic.
- Apply the learned skills and knowledge to solve problems and perform tasks related to the course topic.
- Analyze and evaluate information, arguments, and evidence from various sources and perspectives related to the course topic.
- Communicate effectively and appropriately in oral and written forms using the terminology and conventions of the course topic.
- Demonstrate ethical, professional, and social responsibility in the context of the course topic.
- Collaborate with others and work independently to achieve the learning outcomes of the course topic.
