# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course outcome (CO) is a statement that describes what students are expected to know, understand, or be able to do at the end of a course.
- Bloom's knowledge level (KL) is a classification of the cognitive domain of learning objectives, based on the level of complexity and specificity of the knowledge required.
- Bloom's knowledge level (KL) consists of six categories: remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, and create. Each category represents a different level of cognitive skill and knowledge depth.
- The following table shows the relationship between course outcome (CO) and Bloom's knowledge level (KL), with examples of verbs and learning activities for each category.

| CO | KL | Verbs | Learning Activities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CO1: Recall the basic concepts and principles of programming. | Remember | Define, list, name, recall, recognize, state. | Memorize definitions, terms, and rules. Identify keywords and concepts in a program. |
| CO2: Explain the syntax and semantics of a programming language. | Understand | Describe, explain, interpret, paraphrase, summarize, compare. | Rewrite code in your own words. Explain the meaning and purpose of a program. Compare different programming languages. |
| CO3: Apply programming skills to solve simple problems. | Apply | Apply, demonstrate, execute, implement, use, solve. | Write and run programs to perform specific tasks. Use appropriate data structures and algorithms. Debug and test programs. |
| CO4: Analyze the structure and behavior of a program. | Analyze | Analyze, break down, classify, compare, contrast, differentiate. | Examine the components and logic of a program. Identify errors and bugs. Classify programs based on their features and functions. |
| CO5: Evaluate the quality and efficiency of a program. | Evaluate | Assess, critique, evaluate, judge, justify, validate. | Review and comment on a program. Test and measure the performance and output of a program. Justify your choice of programming tools and techniques. |
| CO6: Create original programs for various purposes and domains. | Create | Compose, construct, create, design, develop, produce. | Generate ideas and plans for a program. Design and implement a program from scratch. Modify and improve existing programs. |
