# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This note implies that the instructor has the authority and flexibility to change the experiments according to the course objectives, the availability of resources, the level of difficulty, and the feedback from the students.
- The instructor may add new experiments to introduce new concepts, techniques, or applications that are relevant to the course.
- The instructor may delete experiments that are outdated, redundant, or too easy or too hard for the students.
- The instructor may modify or tune experiments to adjust the scope, the duration, the instructions, the data, the expected outcomes, or the evaluation criteria of the experiments.
- The instructor should inform the students about any changes in the experiments in advance and provide clear and updated guidelines for the experiments.
- The instructor should also justify the reasons for the changes and explain how they will benefit the students' learning.
