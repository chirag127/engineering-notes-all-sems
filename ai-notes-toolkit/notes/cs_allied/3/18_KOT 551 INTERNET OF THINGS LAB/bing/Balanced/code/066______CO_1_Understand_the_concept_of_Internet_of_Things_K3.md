#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a technology that allows us to add a device to an inert object (such as vehicles, plant electronic systems, roofs, lighting, etc.) that can measure environmental parameters, generate associated data and transmit them through a communications network.
- The IoT is a giant network of connected things and people – all of which collect and share data about the way they are used and about the environment around them.
- The IoT can enable various applications and benefits, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart industry, smart mobility, smart energy, smart security, etc.
- Some examples of IoT devices are:
  - Smart alarm clocks that can work on a specified day and time, turn on the coffee machine and open the windows.
  - Smart thermostats that can adjust the temperature based on the weather and the user's preferences.
  - Smart refrigerators that can monitor the food items and order them online when they are running low.
  - Smart lights that can change the color and brightness based on the mood and the time of the day.
  - Smart cars that can communicate with other vehicles and the infrastructure to avoid accidents and optimize traffic.
  - Smart watches that can track the user's health and fitness data and send alerts to the doctor if needed.
  - Smart planters that can water the plants and monitor their growth and health.
  - Smart cameras that can recognize faces and objects and send notifications to the user.
  - Smart speakers that can play music, answer questions, control other smart devices and perform various tasks using voice commands.
- The IoT relies on several technologies and components, such as sensors, actuators, processors, software, communication protocols, cloud computing, data analytics, artificial intelligence, machine learning, etc.
