# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the concept of connecting physical objects and devices to the internet and enabling them to communicate and exchange data with other devices and applications.
- IoT has many applications in various domains such as smart homes, smart cities, healthcare, agriculture, industry, etc.
- IoT devices collect data about the physical world and make these data available in the Cloud. In the laboratory, such connectivity can be exploited for tasks such as continual monitoring of laboratory environment and instruments, inventory management, and data collection .
- IoT devices can also be used to control and automate laboratory processes, such as adjusting temperature, lighting, ventilation, etc.
- IoT devices can be designed and developed using various hardware and software platforms, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, NodeMCU, etc. These platforms provide different features and capabilities, such as sensors, actuators, wireless communication, programming languages, etc.
- IoT devices can communicate with each other and with the Cloud using different protocols, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc. These protocols have different characteristics and requirements, such as reliability, security, scalability, etc.
- IoT devices can also use different data formats and standards, such as JSON, XML, etc. These formats and standards enable interoperability and compatibility among different devices and applications.
- IoT devices can also use different cloud services and platforms, such as AWS IoT, Google Cloud IoT, etc. These services and platforms provide different functionalities and benefits, such as data storage, data processing, data visualization, data analytics, etc.
- IoT devices can also use different security mechanisms and techniques, such as encryption, authentication, authorization, etc. These mechanisms and techniques ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and devices.
- IoT devices can also use different testing and debugging tools and methods, such as serial monitor, LED indicators, logic analyzer, etc. These tools and methods help to identify and resolve errors and issues in the device design and development.

## Lab Exercises

- The lab exercises for the Internet of Things course are designed to provide hands-on experience and practical skills in IoT device design and development.
- The lab exercises cover the principles of hardware operation and design methodologies through mini-projects centered around Raspberry Pi Zero W, a very low-cost and versatile development board.
- The lab exercises also cover the software development and programming aspects of IoT devices, such as using Python, MQTT, JSON, etc.
- The lab exercises also cover the communication and integration aspects of IoT devices, such as using Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, AWS IoT, etc.
- The lab exercises also cover the security and testing aspects of IoT devices, such as using encryption, authentication, serial monitor, etc.
- The lab exercises are divided into four modules, each consisting of several tasks and challenges. The modules are:

  - Module 1: Introduction to IoT and Raspberry Pi
  - Module 2: Sensors and Actuators
  - Module 3: Communication and Cloud
  - Module 4: Security and Testing

- The lab exercises are designed to be self-paced and collaborative, with guidance and feedback from the instructor and peers.
- The lab exercises are designed to be fun and engaging, with real-world scenarios and applications.
- The lab exercises are designed to be assessed and evaluated, with rubrics and criteria for each task and challenge.
