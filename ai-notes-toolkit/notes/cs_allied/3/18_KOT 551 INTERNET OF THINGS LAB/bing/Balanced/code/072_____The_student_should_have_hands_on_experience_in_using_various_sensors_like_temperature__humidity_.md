### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc. and

- Understand the basic principles and working of different types of sensors.
- Learn how to connect sensors to microcontrollers or computers using appropriate interfaces and protocols.
- Write programs or scripts to read, process, and display sensor data in real time or store it for later analysis.
- Apply sensors to design and implement projects or experiments that demonstrate their applications and limitations in various domains such as environmental monitoring, home automation, security, health, etc.
- Evaluate the performance, accuracy, and reliability of sensors and compare them with other methods of measurement or detection.
