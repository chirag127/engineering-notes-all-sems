# At the end of course, the student will be able to

- Define the main concepts and principles of the course topic.
- Apply the learned skills and knowledge to solve problems and complete tasks related to the course topic.
- Analyze and evaluate the information and arguments presented in the course materials and sources.
- Communicate effectively and professionally using the appropriate language and format for the course topic.
- Demonstrate creativity and critical thinking in developing and presenting their own ideas and solutions related to the course topic.
- Collaborate and cooperate with others in group projects and discussions related to the course topic.
- Reflect on their own learning process and outcomes and identify areas for improvement and further development.
