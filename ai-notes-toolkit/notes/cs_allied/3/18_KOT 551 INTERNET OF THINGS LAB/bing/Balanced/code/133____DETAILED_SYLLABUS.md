## DETAILED SYLLABUS

- A detailed syllabus is a document that outlines the topics, objectives, learning outcomes, assessment methods, and resources for a specific course or module.
- A detailed syllabus can help students to understand the expectations and requirements of the course, as well as to plan their study time and activities accordingly.
- A detailed syllabus can also help instructors to design and deliver the course in a coherent and consistent way, as well as to communicate with students and other stakeholders about the course content and goals.
- A detailed syllabus typically includes the following sections:

  - Course information: This section provides basic information about the course, such as the course title, code, number of credits, prerequisites, instructor name and contact details, office hours, course website, etc.
  - Course description: This section gives an overview of the course, such as the main themes, topics, and questions that will be explored, the learning approach and methods that will be used, and the relevance and importance of the course for the discipline or field of study.
  - Course objectives: This section states the general and specific learning objectives of the course, such as what students will be able to know, understand, do, or demonstrate by the end of the course.
  - Course outcomes: This section describes the expected learning outcomes of the course, such as what students will be able to demonstrate or apply in terms of knowledge, skills, attitudes, or values as a result of completing the course.
  - Course schedule: This section provides a tentative outline of the course content and activities, such as the topics, readings, assignments, quizzes, exams, projects, etc. that will be covered in each week or session of the course. This section may also indicate the due dates, weightings, and grading criteria for each assessment component.
  - Course policies: This section specifies the rules and regulations that apply to the course, such as the attendance, participation, late submission, academic integrity, plagiarism, grading, feedback, communication, etc. policies that students and instructors are expected to follow and respect.
  - Course resources: This section lists the required and recommended materials and resources for the course, such as the textbooks, articles, websites, software, etc. that students will need to access and use for the course. This section may also provide information on how and where to obtain or access these resources.
