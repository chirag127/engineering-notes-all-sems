# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- KOT 552 is a course on the Internet of Things (IoT) Lab offered by the KCS department.
- The course focuses on the practical implementation of IoT concepts and technologies.
- Students will learn to design, develop, and deploy IoT systems using various hardware and software platforms.
- Topics covered in the course may include:
  - Introduction to IoT and its applications
  - Sensors and actuators
  - Microcontrollers and embedded systems
  - Wireless communication protocols
  - Cloud computing and data storage
  - Data analysis and visualization
  - Security and privacy in IoT systems
- The course may involve hands-on projects and assignments to provide students with practical experience in building IoT systems.
- The course is suitable for students with a background in computer science or a related field.
