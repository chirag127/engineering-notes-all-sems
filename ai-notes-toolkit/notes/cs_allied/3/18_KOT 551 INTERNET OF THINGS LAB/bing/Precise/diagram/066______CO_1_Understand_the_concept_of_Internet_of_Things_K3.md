#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

- The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.
- IoT allows objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy, and economic benefit.
- IoT is a rapidly growing field, with an estimated 50 billion connected devices by 2020.
- IoT has the potential to impact many areas of our lives, including transportation, healthcare, agriculture, and energy management.
- IoT devices can range from simple sensors to complex systems, and can include consumer products such as smart home appliances, as well as industrial and commercial systems such as smart grids and smart cities.
- IoT relies on a number of technologies, including wireless communication, cloud computing, and data analytics, to enable the collection, storage, and analysis of data from connected devices.
- Security and privacy are major concerns in the development and deployment of IoT systems, as the large amount of data collected by these devices can potentially be used for malicious purposes.
- The development of IoT requires collaboration between multiple disciplines, including computer science, engineering, and business, to ensure the successful integration of these technologies into our daily lives.
