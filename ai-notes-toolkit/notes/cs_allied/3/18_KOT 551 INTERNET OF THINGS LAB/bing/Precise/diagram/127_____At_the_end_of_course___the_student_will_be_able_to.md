### At the end of the course, the student will be able to:

1. Demonstrate an understanding of the course material and its application to real-world scenarios.
2. Apply critical thinking and problem-solving skills to analyze and evaluate information.
3. Communicate effectively in written and oral forms.
4. Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
5. Demonstrate ethical behavior and social responsibility in personal and professional settings.
6. Use technology effectively to access, organize, and present information.
7. Engage in lifelong learning and professional development.
