# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- The instructor may add new experiments to the curriculum.
- The instructor may delete experiments that are no longer relevant or necessary.
- The instructor may modify experiments to better suit the needs of the students or the course.
- The instructor may tune experiments to make them more effective or efficient.
- It is important for students to be aware of these changes and to adapt accordingly.
- These changes may be made to improve the learning experience for the students.
- Students should communicate with the instructor if they have any concerns or questions about the changes made to the experiments.
