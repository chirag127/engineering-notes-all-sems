# Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

To get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs, the following steps can be taken:

1. Connect the two switches to the input pins of the microcontroller.
2. Connect the two LEDs to the output pins of the microcontroller.
3. Write a program to read the input from the switches and turn on the corresponding LEDs.
4. Upload the program to the microcontroller.
5. Test the circuit by pressing the switches and observing the LEDs.
