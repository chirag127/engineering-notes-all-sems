# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- This statement indicates that the instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments in a course or program.
- The instructor may add new experiments to the course to introduce new concepts or techniques.
- The instructor may delete experiments that are no longer relevant or necessary for the course.
- The instructor may modify or tune existing experiments to improve their effectiveness or relevance.
- These changes may be made to ensure that the course content is up-to-date and meets the learning objectives of the students.
- Students should be aware that the experiments in the course may change and should be prepared to adapt to these changes.
