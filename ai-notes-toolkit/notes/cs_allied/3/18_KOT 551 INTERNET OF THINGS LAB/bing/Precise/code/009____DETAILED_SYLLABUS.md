## DETAILED SYLLABUS

A detailed syllabus is a comprehensive outline of the topics, assignments, and assessments that will be covered in a course. It is typically provided by the instructor at the beginning of the course and serves as a guide for students to understand the expectations and requirements of the course.

A detailed syllabus may include the following information:

1. Course description: A brief overview of the course, including its objectives and goals.
2. Course schedule: A list of topics that will be covered in the course, along with the dates and deadlines for assignments and assessments.
3. Course materials: A list of required and recommended textbooks, articles, and other resources for the course.
4. Grading policy: An explanation of how grades will be calculated, including the weight of assignments and assessments.
5. Attendance policy: The instructor's expectations for attendance and participation in the course.
6. Course policies: Any additional policies or rules specific to the course, such as academic integrity or technology use.

A detailed syllabus is an important tool for students to plan their time and manage their workload throughout the course. It also helps to ensure that all students have a clear understanding of the expectations and requirements of the course.
