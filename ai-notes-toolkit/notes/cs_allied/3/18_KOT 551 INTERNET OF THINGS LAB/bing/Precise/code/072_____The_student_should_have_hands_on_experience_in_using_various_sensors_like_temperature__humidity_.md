### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc.

- **Temperature sensor**: A temperature sensor is a device that measures the temperature of its environment and converts the input data into electronic data to record, monitor, or signal temperature changes.
- **Humidity sensor**: A humidity sensor is a device that measures the relative humidity of the air around it. It works by detecting changes in electrical resistance or capacitance caused by moisture in the air.
- **Smoke sensor**: A smoke sensor is a device that detects the presence of smoke in the air. It works by using a light source and a light detector to measure the amount of light scattered by smoke particles in the air.
- **Light sensor**: A light sensor is a device that measures the amount of light in its environment. It works by converting light energy into an electrical signal that can be measured and recorded.

Having hands-on experience with these sensors can help students understand how they work and how to use them in practical applications. This can be useful in fields such as engineering, environmental science, and meteorology. It can also help students develop skills in data collection, analysis, and interpretation.
