# KOT 554 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- KOT 554 is a course on the Internet of Things (IoT) Lab.
- IoT refers to the interconnection of devices and objects through the internet, allowing them to collect and exchange data.
- The course covers the fundamentals of IoT, including its architecture, protocols, and applications.
- Students will learn how to design, implement, and test IoT systems.
- The course includes hands-on lab sessions where students will work with IoT devices and platforms.
- The course is offered by the KCS department.
