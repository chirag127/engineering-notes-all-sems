# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course.
- Bloom’s Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives into different levels of complexity and specificity.
- The Knowledge Level (KL) of Bloom’s Taxonomy is the first and most basic level, which involves the recall of facts, terms, basic concepts, and answers.
- At the Knowledge Level, students are expected to remember or recognize information, but not necessarily understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, or create with it.
- Examples of verbs used to describe learning objectives at the Knowledge Level include: define, describe, identify, label, list, match, name, outline, recall, recognize, record, repeat, and state.
- Course Outcomes can be written to target different levels of Bloom’s Taxonomy, including the Knowledge Level, to ensure that students are challenged to develop a range of cognitive skills.
- By aligning Course Outcomes with Bloom’s Taxonomy, instructors can design assessments and learning activities that appropriately measure and promote student learning at the desired level of complexity.
