# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share data. In the KOT 551 Internet of Things Lab, students will learn about the following topics:

1. Introduction to IoT: Students will learn about the basic concepts of IoT, including its architecture, protocols, and applications.

2. Sensors and Actuators: Students will learn about different types of sensors and actuators, and how they can be used in IoT applications.

3. Data Acquisition and Processing: Students will learn about data acquisition techniques, and how to process and analyze data collected from IoT devices.

4. IoT Communication Protocols: Students will learn about different communication protocols used in IoT, including MQTT, CoAP, and HTTP.

5. IoT Security: Students will learn about the security challenges in IoT, and how to secure IoT devices and data.

6. IoT Applications: Students will learn about different IoT applications, including smart homes, smart cities, and industrial IoT.

7. IoT Project: Students will work on an IoT project, where they will design and implement an IoT system.

The KOT 551 Internet of Things Lab provides students with hands-on experience in designing and implementing IoT systems, and prepares them for a career in the field of IoT.
