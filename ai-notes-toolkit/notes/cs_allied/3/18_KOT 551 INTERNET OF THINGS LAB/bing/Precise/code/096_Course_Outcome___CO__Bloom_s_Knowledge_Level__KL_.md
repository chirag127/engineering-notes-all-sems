# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course. These outcomes are used to assess the effectiveness of the course and to guide the development of future courses.

Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is a framework for categorizing educational learning objectives into levels of complexity and specificity. The levels are: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. These levels can be used to assess the level of understanding that students have achieved in a course.

- COs are specific to each course and are developed by the instructor or course designer.
- COs should be measurable and achievable within the timeframe of the course.
- Bloom’s KL can be used to assess the level of understanding that students have achieved in a course.
- The levels of Bloom’s KL are hierarchical, with each level building upon the previous one.
- The levels of Bloom’s KL can be used to guide the development of course content and assessments.
