#### Get the status of a bulb at a remote place (on the LAN) through web

1. To get the status of a bulb at a remote place on the LAN through the web, you need to have a smart bulb that is connected to the internet and can be controlled remotely.
2. The smart bulb should be connected to the same LAN as the device you are using to check its status.
3. You can use a web interface or an app provided by the manufacturer of the smart bulb to check its status.
4. Log in to the web interface or app using your credentials.
5. Navigate to the section where you can view the status of your smart devices.
6. Select the smart bulb whose status you want to check.
7. The status of the bulb, whether it is on or off, will be displayed on the screen.
