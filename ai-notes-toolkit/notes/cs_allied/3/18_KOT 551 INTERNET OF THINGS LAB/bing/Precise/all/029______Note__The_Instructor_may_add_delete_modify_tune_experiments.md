- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- These changes may include adding new experiments, deleting existing experiments, modifying the details of the experiments, or tuning the experiments to better fit the needs of the course.
- It is important for students to be aware of these potential changes and to stay up to date with any announcements or updates from the instructor.

- Students should be prepared to adapt to any changes made by the instructor and to approach the experiments with an open and flexible mindset.
