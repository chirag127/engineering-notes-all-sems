#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- These changes may include adding new experiments, deleting existing ones, modifying the details of the experiments, or fine-tuning them to better suit the needs of the course.
- It is important for students to be aware of this, as the experiments are an integral part of the course and their understanding of the subject matter.
- Students should regularly check for updates and changes to the experiments, and adjust their study plans accordingly.
- This flexibility allows the instructor to tailor the course to the needs of the students and ensure that the material is up-to-date and relevant.
