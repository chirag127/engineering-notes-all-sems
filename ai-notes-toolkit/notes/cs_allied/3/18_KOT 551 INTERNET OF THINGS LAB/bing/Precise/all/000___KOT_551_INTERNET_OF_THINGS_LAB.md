# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share data. The IoT Lab course (KOT 551) is designed to provide students with hands-on experience in developing IoT applications.

In this course, students will learn about:

1. The basics of IoT, including its architecture, protocols, and standards.
2. The use of sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers in IoT systems.
3. The design and implementation of IoT applications using cloud platforms and services.
4. The security and privacy issues associated with IoT systems.

The course will involve a series of lab exercises and projects, where students will have the opportunity to apply their knowledge and skills in building IoT systems. The course will also cover the use of various tools and technologies, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and MQTT, in the development of IoT applications.

Overall, the KOT 551 IoT Lab course aims to provide students with a solid foundation in the field of IoT and prepare them for further studies or careers in this rapidly growing field.
