# CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. **Wireless data transmission** refers to the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. **Bluetooth** is a common wireless technology used for short-range data transmission between devices such as smartphones, laptops, and speakers.
3. **Wi-Fi** is another widely used wireless technology that allows devices to connect to a local area network (LAN) and the internet.
4. **Infrared** technology can also be used for wireless data transmission, although it is less common and has a shorter range than Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.
5. **Near Field Communication (NFC)** is another short-range wireless technology that allows devices to exchange data by simply touching them together or bringing them into close proximity.
6. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and be within range of each other.
7. The process of transmitting data wirelessly between devices may vary depending on the technology used, but generally involves establishing a connection between the devices and selecting the data to be transferred.
8. Wireless data transmission has many advantages, including convenience, flexibility, and the ability to transfer data between devices without the need for physical connections.
