# Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

To get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs, you can follow these steps:

1. Connect the two switches to the input pins of a microcontroller or a digital logic circuit.
2. Connect the two LEDs to the output pins of the microcontroller or digital logic circuit.
3. Write a program or design a logic circuit that reads the state of the switches and turns on the corresponding LED when the switch is closed (or pressed).
4. Upload the program to the microcontroller or build the digital logic circuit.
5. Test the circuit by pressing the switches and observing the behavior of the LEDs.

This is a simple example of how to use switches to control the state of LEDs. You can expand on this concept to create more complex circuits and systems.
