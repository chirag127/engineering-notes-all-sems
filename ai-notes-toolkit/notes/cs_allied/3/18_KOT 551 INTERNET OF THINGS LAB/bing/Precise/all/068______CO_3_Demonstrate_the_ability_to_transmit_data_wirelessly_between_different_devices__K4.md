#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. There are several technologies that enable wireless data transmission, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
3. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that is commonly used to connect devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops to peripherals such as headphones, speakers, and keyboards.
4. Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that allows devices to connect to the internet or to other devices on a local area network (LAN) without the use of cables.
5. Cellular networks, such as 3G, 4G, and 5G, allow mobile devices to connect to the internet and to make phone calls and send text messages without the need for a wired connection.
6. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and must be within range of each other or of a wireless access point.
7. The process of transmitting data wirelessly involves encoding the data into a signal that can be transmitted over the airwaves, transmitting the signal, and then decoding the signal back into data at the receiving device.
8. Wireless data transmission has many advantages, including increased mobility, flexibility, and convenience. However, there are also some challenges, such as security and interference, that must be addressed to ensure reliable and secure wireless data transmission.
