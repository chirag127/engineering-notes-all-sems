# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a system of interconnected devices, machines, and objects that are able to collect and exchange data using embedded sensors and network connectivity. The IoT Lab course (KOT 552) focuses on the practical implementation of IoT concepts and technologies.

Some of the key topics covered in this course include:

1. Introduction to IoT and its applications
2. IoT architecture and protocols
3. IoT device programming and interfacing
4. Data acquisition, processing, and visualization
5. IoT security and privacy
6. IoT project development and management

This course provides hands-on experience with IoT technologies and tools, enabling students to design and implement IoT solutions for various applications. Students will work on projects that involve the use of sensors, microcontrollers, and cloud platforms to collect, process, and visualize data.

Overall, the KOT 552 IoT Lab course provides a comprehensive introduction to the field of IoT and prepares students for further study and career opportunities in this rapidly growing field.
