# KOT 554 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- KOT 554 is a course code for the Internet of Things Lab offered by the KCS department.
- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and exchange data.
- In this lab, students will learn how to design, implement, and test IoT systems.
- Topics covered in this lab may include:
    - Introduction to IoT and its applications
    - IoT architecture and protocols
    - IoT device programming and interfacing
    - IoT data management and analytics
    - IoT security and privacy
- This lab provides hands-on experience with IoT technologies and prepares students for careers in the field of IoT.
