# Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

The area of a given shape can be calculated by using the appropriate formula for that shape. The formulas for calculating the area of a rectangle, triangle, and circle are as follows:

1. **Rectangle**: The area of a rectangle can be calculated by multiplying its length by its width. The formula for the area of a rectangle is `A = l * w`, where `A` is the area, `l` is the length, and `w` is the width.

2. **Triangle**: The area of a triangle can be calculated using the formula `A = (b * h) / 2`, where `A` is the area, `b` is the length of the base, and `h` is the height of the triangle.

3. **Circle**: The area of a circle can be calculated using the formula `A = π * r^2`, where `A` is the area, `π` is approximately equal to 3.14, and `r` is the radius of the circle.

To calculate the area of a given shape, one must first identify the shape and then use the appropriate formula to calculate its area. The values for the variables in the formula must be read from the standard, which specifies the units of measurement and the precision of the values.
