#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other objects embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.

1. IoT allows objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy, and economic benefit.
2. IoT is expected to offer advanced connectivity of devices, systems, and services that goes beyond machine-to-machine (M2M) communications and covers a variety of protocols, domains, and applications.
3. IoT is a complex ecosystem that involves various technologies, including wireless communication, sensors, micro-electromechanical systems (MEMS), and embedded systems.
4. IoT has the potential to impact various industries, including healthcare, transportation, agriculture, and manufacturing, by enabling new business models, improving operational efficiency, and enhancing customer experiences.
5. Security and privacy are major concerns in the IoT ecosystem, as the large amount of data collected and exchanged by IoT devices can potentially be vulnerable to cyber attacks and unauthorized access.
