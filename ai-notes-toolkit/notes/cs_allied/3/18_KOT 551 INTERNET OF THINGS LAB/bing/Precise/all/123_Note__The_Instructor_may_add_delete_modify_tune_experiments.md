# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- The instructor may add new experiments to the course.
- The instructor may delete experiments that are no longer relevant or necessary.
- The instructor may modify experiments to better suit the needs of the course or the students.
- The instructor may tune experiments to make them more effective or efficient.
- These changes may be made at any time and are at the discretion of the instructor.
- It is important for students to be aware of these potential changes and to be prepared to adapt to them.
