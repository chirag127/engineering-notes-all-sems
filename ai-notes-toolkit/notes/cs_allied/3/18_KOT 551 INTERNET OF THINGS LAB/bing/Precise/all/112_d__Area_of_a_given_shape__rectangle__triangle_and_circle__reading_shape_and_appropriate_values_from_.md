# Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

The area of a shape is the measure of the region enclosed by the shape. The formulas for calculating the area of common shapes such as rectangles, triangles, and circles are as follows:

1. **Rectangle**: The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width. The formula is `Area = length × width`.

2. **Triangle**: The area of a triangle can be calculated using the formula `Area = (base × height) ÷ 2`, where the base is the length of one side of the triangle and the height is the perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite vertex.

3. **Circle**: The area of a circle is calculated using the formula `Area = π × radius²`, where π is approximately equal to 3.14 and the radius is the distance from the center of the circle to any point on its circumference.

To calculate the area of a given shape, one must first identify the shape and then use the appropriate formula with the given values. For example, to calculate the area of a rectangle with a length of 5 units and a width of 3 units, one would use the formula for the area of a rectangle and substitute the given values to get `Area = 5 × 3 = 15 square units`.
