# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies to exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet.

- IoT devices can range from simple sensors to complex machines, and can include consumer devices such as smart home appliances, wearable technology, and connected vehicles.
- IoT technology enables devices to communicate and interact with each other, allowing for the automation of various tasks and the collection of data for analysis and decision-making.
- IoT has the potential to revolutionize many industries, including healthcare, transportation, and manufacturing, by improving efficiency, reducing costs, and enhancing the user experience.
- The growth of IoT has been driven by advances in technology, such as the development of low-cost sensors and the increasing availability of high-speed internet connectivity.
- Security and privacy are major concerns in the development and deployment of IoT systems, as the large amount of data collected by these devices can be sensitive and potentially vulnerable to cyber attacks.
