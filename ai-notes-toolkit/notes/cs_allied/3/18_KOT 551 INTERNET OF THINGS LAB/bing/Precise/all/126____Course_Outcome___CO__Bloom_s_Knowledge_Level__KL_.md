## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course.
- Bloom’s Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives into different levels of complexity and specificity.
- Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is the first and most basic level of Bloom’s Taxonomy, which focuses on the recall and recognition of information.
- At the Knowledge Level, students are expected to demonstrate their ability to remember and retrieve previously learned material.
- This can include recalling facts, terms, basic concepts, and answers to questions.
- Assessments at the Knowledge Level may include tasks such as defining, listing, identifying, and labeling.
- The Knowledge Level is an important foundation for higher levels of learning, as it provides the necessary information and understanding for more complex cognitive processes.
- In the context of Course Outcomes, the Knowledge Level can be used to assess whether students have achieved a basic understanding of the course material.
- By aligning Course Outcomes with Bloom’s Taxonomy, instructors can ensure that their assessments accurately measure student learning and progress.
