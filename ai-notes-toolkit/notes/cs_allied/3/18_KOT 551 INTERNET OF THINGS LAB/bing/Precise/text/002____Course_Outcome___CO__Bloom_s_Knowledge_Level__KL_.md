## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- Course Outcome (CO) refers to the knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to acquire by the end of a course.
- COs are usually defined in terms of the level of understanding and proficiency that students should achieve in a particular subject area.
- Bloom’s Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives into different levels of complexity and specificity.
- The Knowledge Level (KL) in Bloom’s Taxonomy refers to the lowest level of cognitive learning, where students are expected to recall and remember information.
- The KL is the foundation for higher levels of learning, such as comprehension, application, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation.
- COs can be mapped to different levels of Bloom’s Taxonomy to ensure that students are achieving the desired level of understanding and proficiency in a subject area.
- By aligning COs with Bloom’s Taxonomy, educators can design assessments and instructional strategies that target the appropriate level of cognitive learning for their students.
