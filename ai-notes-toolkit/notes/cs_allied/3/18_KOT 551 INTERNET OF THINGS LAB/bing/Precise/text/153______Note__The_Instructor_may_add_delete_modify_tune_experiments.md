#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- It is important to note that the instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- This means that the instructor may add new experiments, delete existing ones, modify the details of the experiments, or tune them to better fit the needs of the class.
- This flexibility allows the instructor to tailor the experiments to the specific needs and interests of the students, as well as to adapt to any changes in the curriculum or course material.
- As a student, it is important to be aware of this possibility and to be prepared for changes to the experiments throughout the course.
