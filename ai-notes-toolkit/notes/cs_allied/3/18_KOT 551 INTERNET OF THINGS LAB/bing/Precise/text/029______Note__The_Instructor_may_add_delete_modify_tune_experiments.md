#### Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

- The instructor has the authority to make changes to the experiments.
- These changes may include adding new experiments, deleting existing ones, modifying the procedures or tuning the parameters.
- The purpose of these changes is to improve the learning experience for the students.
- It is important for students to be aware of these changes and to adapt accordingly.
- Students should regularly check for updates and communicate with the instructor to ensure they are up to date with the latest information.
