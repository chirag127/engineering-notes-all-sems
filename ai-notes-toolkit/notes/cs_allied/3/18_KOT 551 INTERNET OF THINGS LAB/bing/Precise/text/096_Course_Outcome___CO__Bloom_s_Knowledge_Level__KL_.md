# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

Course Outcome (CO) refers to the expected knowledge, skills, and attitudes that students should possess upon completion of a specific course. COs are typically aligned with the program outcomes and are used to assess the effectiveness of the course in achieving the desired outcomes.

Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL) is a framework for categorizing educational learning objectives into levels of complexity and specificity. The levels include: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. These levels can be used to assess the level of understanding and mastery of a particular subject or topic.

In the context of COs, Bloom’s KL can be used to assess the level of understanding and mastery of the course material. For example, a CO may state that students should be able to apply a particular concept or skill, indicating that the desired level of mastery is at the Applying level of Bloom’s KL.

Some key points to consider when using Bloom’s KL in the context of COs include:

- COs should be specific and measurable, indicating the desired level of mastery using Bloom’s KL.
- COs should be aligned with the program outcomes and the overall goals of the course.
- Assessment methods should be designed to accurately measure the level of mastery indicated by the COs using Bloom’s KL.
- Regular review and revision of COs and assessment methods is necessary to ensure their continued effectiveness in achieving the desired outcomes.

Overall, the use of Bloom’s KL in the context of COs can provide a structured and systematic approach to assessing student learning and mastery of course material. This can help to ensure that the course is effective in achieving the desired outcomes and can provide valuable feedback for ongoing improvement.
