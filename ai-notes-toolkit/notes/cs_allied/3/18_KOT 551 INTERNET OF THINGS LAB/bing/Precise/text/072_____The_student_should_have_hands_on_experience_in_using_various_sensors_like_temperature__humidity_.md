### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc.

1. **Temperature Sensor:** A temperature sensor is a device that measures the temperature of its environment and converts the input data into electronic data to record, monitor, or signal temperature changes.
2. **Humidity Sensor:** A humidity sensor is a device that measures the relative humidity of the air around it. It works by detecting changes in electrical resistance or capacitance caused by changes in humidity.
3. **Smoke Sensor:** A smoke sensor is a device that detects the presence of smoke in the air. It works by using a light source and a light detector to measure the amount of light scattered by smoke particles.
4. **Light Sensor:** A light sensor is a device that measures the intensity of light in its environment. It works by converting light energy into an electrical signal that can be measured and recorded.

Having hands-on experience with these sensors can help students understand how they work and how they can be used in various applications. It can also help them develop practical skills in using and integrating these sensors into different systems.
