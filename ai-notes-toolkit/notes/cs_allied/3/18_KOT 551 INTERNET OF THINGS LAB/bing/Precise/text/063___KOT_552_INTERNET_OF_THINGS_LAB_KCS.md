# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or "things" embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and connectivity to enable these objects to collect and exchange data.
- IoT allows objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit.
- The KOT 552 Internet of Things Lab course is designed to provide students with hands-on experience in developing IoT applications.
- In this course, students will learn about the various components and technologies used in IoT systems, including sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, and communication protocols.
- Students will also learn how to design, implement, and test IoT applications using various hardware and software platforms.
- The course will cover topics such as data acquisition, data processing, data storage, and data visualization.
- Through laboratory exercises and projects, students will gain practical experience in building IoT systems and applications.
- The course is suitable for students with a background in computer science or engineering, and is recommended for students interested in pursuing careers in IoT development or research.
