### Get the status of a bulb at a remote place (on the LAN) through web

1. To get the status of a bulb at a remote place on the LAN through the web, you need to have a smart bulb that is connected to the internet and can be controlled remotely.
2. The smart bulb should be connected to the same LAN as the device you are using to check its status.
3. You can use a web interface or a mobile app provided by the manufacturer of the smart bulb to check its status.
4. Log in to the web interface or mobile app using your credentials.
5. Navigate to the section where you can view the status of your smart bulb.
6. The status of the bulb, whether it is on or off, will be displayed on the screen.
7. You can also use this interface to turn the bulb on or off remotely.
