### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc.

1. **Temperature Sensor**: A temperature sensor is an electronic device that measures the temperature of its environment and converts the input data into electronic data to record, monitor, or signal temperature changes.
2. **Humidity Sensor**: A humidity sensor is an electronic device that measures the moisture content in the air and converts it into an electrical signal. This data can be used to monitor or control the humidity levels in a given environment.
3. **Smoke Sensor**: A smoke sensor is an electronic device that detects the presence of smoke in the air. It is commonly used in fire alarm systems to provide early warning of a potential fire.
4. **Light Sensor**: A light sensor is an electronic device that measures the amount of light in its environment. It is commonly used in automatic lighting systems to control the brightness of lights based on the ambient light levels.

Having hands-on experience with these sensors can help students understand how they work and how they can be used in various applications. This knowledge can be useful in fields such as engineering, environmental monitoring, and home automation.
