#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. There are several methods of wireless data transmission, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cellular networks, and infrared.
3. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that allows devices to communicate with each other over distances of up to 10 meters.
4. Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that uses radio waves to provide high-speed internet and network connections.
5. Cellular networks use radio waves to transmit data between mobile devices and cell towers, allowing for wireless communication over long distances.
6. Infrared is a type of wireless communication that uses infrared light to transmit data between devices.
7. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and be within range of each other or a wireless network.
8. The process of transmitting data wirelessly involves encoding the data into a signal that can be transmitted over the airwaves, transmitting the signal, and then decoding the signal at the receiving device to retrieve the original data.
9. Wireless data transmission has many advantages, including increased mobility, flexibility, and convenience.
10. However, there are also potential security risks associated with wireless data transmission, and it is important to take appropriate measures to protect the data being transmitted.
