# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

- KOT 552 is a course on the Internet of Things (IoT) Lab.
- IoT refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, buildings, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.
- The course covers the basics of IoT, including its architecture, protocols, and applications.
- Students will learn how to design, implement, and test IoT systems using various hardware and software platforms.
- The course also covers the security and privacy issues associated with IoT.
- The lab component of the course provides hands-on experience in building and deploying IoT systems.
- KCS refers to the Knowledge and Competency Statement, which outlines the learning outcomes and competencies expected of students who complete the course.
