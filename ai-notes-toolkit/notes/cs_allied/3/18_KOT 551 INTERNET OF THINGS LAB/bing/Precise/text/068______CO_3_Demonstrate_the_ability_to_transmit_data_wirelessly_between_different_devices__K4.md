#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of wires or cables.
2. There are several technologies that enable wireless data transmission, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
3. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that allows devices to communicate with each other over distances of up to 10 meters. It is commonly used for wireless headphones, speakers, and keyboards.
4. Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that allows devices to connect to the internet and to each other over a local area network. It has a range of up to 100 meters and is commonly used for home and office networks.
5. Cellular networks use radio waves to transmit data between mobile devices and cell towers. They have a much larger range than Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and are commonly used for mobile internet access and voice calls.
6. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and be within range of each other or a wireless access point.
7. The process of transmitting data wirelessly involves encoding the data into a signal that can be transmitted over the air, transmitting the signal, and then decoding the signal back into data at the receiving device.
8. Wireless data transmission has many advantages, including increased mobility, flexibility, and convenience. However, it can also be less secure than wired data transmission and may be subject to interference from other wireless devices.
