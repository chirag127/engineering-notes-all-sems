# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies to connect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet.
- The KOT 551 Internet of Things Lab is a course that provides students with hands-on experience in designing, building, and testing IoT systems.
- In this lab, students will learn about the various components of an IoT system, including sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, and communication protocols.
- Students will also learn how to program IoT devices, collect and analyze data, and develop applications that use IoT data to solve real-world problems.
- The lab will cover topics such as wireless communication, cloud computing, data analytics, and security in IoT systems.
- By the end of the course, students will have a solid understanding of the principles and practices of IoT and will be able to design and implement their own IoT systems.
