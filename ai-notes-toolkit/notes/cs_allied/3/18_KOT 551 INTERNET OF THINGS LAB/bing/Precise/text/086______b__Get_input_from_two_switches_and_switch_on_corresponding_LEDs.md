#### b) Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

1. To get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs, you will need two switches, two LEDs, a microcontroller, and a power source.
2. Connect the switches to the input pins of the microcontroller and the LEDs to the output pins.
3. Write a program for the microcontroller to read the state of the switches and turn on the corresponding LED when the switch is closed.
4. Upload the program to the microcontroller and power it on.
5. When you press one of the switches, the corresponding LED should light up.
