#### d) Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

The area of a shape is the measure of the region enclosed by the shape. The formula for calculating the area of a shape depends on the shape in question. Here are the formulas for calculating the area of a rectangle, triangle, and circle:

1. **Rectangle**: The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width. The formula is `A = l * w`, where `A` is the area, `l` is the length, and `w` is the width.

2. **Triangle**: The area of a triangle can be calculated using the formula `A = (b * h) / 2`, where `A` is the area, `b` is the length of the base, and `h` is the height of the triangle.

3. **Circle**: The area of a circle is calculated using the formula `A = π * r^2`, where `A` is the area, `π` is approximately equal to 3.14, and `r` is the radius of the circle.

To calculate the area of a given shape, one must first identify the shape and then use the appropriate formula with the given values. For example, to calculate the area of a rectangle with a length of 5 units and a width of 3 units, one would use the formula for the area of a rectangle and substitute the given values to get `A = 5 * 3 = 15 square units`.
