#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the transfer of information between two or more devices without the use of physical connections.
2. There are several technologies that enable wireless data transmission, including Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
3. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that is commonly used to connect devices such as smartphones, tablets, and laptops to peripherals such as keyboards, mice, and headphones.
4. Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that allows devices to connect to the internet or to other devices on a local network.
5. Cellular networks, such as 3G, 4G, and 5G, allow mobile devices to connect to the internet and to make phone calls and send text messages.
6. To transmit data wirelessly between different devices, the devices must be equipped with the appropriate wireless technology and must be within range of each other or of a wireless access point.
7. The process of transmitting data wirelessly between different devices typically involves selecting the data to be transmitted, choosing the destination device, and initiating the transfer.
8. Wireless data transmission can be faster and more convenient than wired data transmission, but it can also be less secure and more susceptible to interference.
