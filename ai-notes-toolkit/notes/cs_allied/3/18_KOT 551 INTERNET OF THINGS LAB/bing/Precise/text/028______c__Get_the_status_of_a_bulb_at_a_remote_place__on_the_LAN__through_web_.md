#### c) Get the status of a bulb at a remote place (on the LAN) through web.

1. To get the status of a bulb at a remote place on the LAN through the web, you need to have a smart bulb that is connected to the internet and can be controlled remotely.
2. The smart bulb should be connected to the same local area network (LAN) as the device you are using to check its status.
3. You can use a web interface or a mobile application provided by the manufacturer of the smart bulb to check its status.
4. Once you have logged in to the web interface or mobile application, you can navigate to the section that displays the status of the bulb.
5. The status of the bulb will be displayed, indicating whether it is on or off, its brightness level, and other relevant information.
6. You can also use third-party applications or services that are compatible with your smart bulb to check its status.
7. It is important to ensure that the smart bulb and the device you are using to check its status are connected to the same LAN and that the bulb is properly configured to allow remote access.
