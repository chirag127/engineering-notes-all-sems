#### d) Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

When it comes to calculating the area of shapes, there are three basic shapes that you should know how to calculate: rectangles, triangles, and circles. In this section, we will discuss how to calculate the area of these shapes by reading shapes and appropriate values from standard.

Here are the steps to calculate the area of each shape:

##### 1. Rectangle

To calculate the area of a rectangle, you need to know its length and width. The formula for the area of a rectangle is:

```
Area = Length x Width
```

##### 2. Triangle

To calculate the area of a triangle, you need to know its base and height. The formula for the area of a triangle is:

```
Area = 1/2 x Base x Height
```

##### 3. Circle

To calculate the area of a circle, you need to know its radius. The formula for the area of a circle is:

```
Area = π x (Radius)^2
```

where π (pi) is a mathematical constant that is approximately equal to 3.14.

It is important to note that the units of measurement used for length, width, base, height, and radius should all be the same. For example, if the length of a rectangle is measured in meters, then the width should also be measured in meters.

By using the appropriate formula for each shape, and by reading the shape and appropriate values from standard, you will be able to calculate the area of any given shape with ease.
