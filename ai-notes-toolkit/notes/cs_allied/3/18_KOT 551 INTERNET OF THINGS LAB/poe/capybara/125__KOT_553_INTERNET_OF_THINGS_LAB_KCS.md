# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

In this lab, you will learn about the Internet of Things (IoT) and its applications in various industries. Here are the key points you will cover:

- Understanding the concept of IoT and its components.
- Learning about sensors and their types.
- Understanding the working of microcontrollers and microprocessors.
- Learning about the different protocols used in IoT communication.
- Building IoT applications using Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and other development boards.
- Understanding the cloud infrastructure used in IoT systems.
- Learning about the security challenges faced by IoT systems and how to mitigate them.
- Developing IoT applications using popular cloud platforms like AWS IoT, Azure IoT, and Google Cloud IoT.

Throughout the lab, you will work on hands-on projects to reinforce your understanding of IoT concepts and their practical applications. You will also learn about the latest trends and developments in the field of IoT, including edge computing, machine learning, and blockchain.

By the end of this lab, you will have a solid understanding of IoT and the skills to develop your own IoT applications. This knowledge will be invaluable in various industries, including healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, and transportation.
