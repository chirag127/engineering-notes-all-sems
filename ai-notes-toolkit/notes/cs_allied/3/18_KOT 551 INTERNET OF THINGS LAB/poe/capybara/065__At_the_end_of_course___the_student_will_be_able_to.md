### At the end of the course, the student will be able to:

- Understand the fundamental concepts and principles of the subject matter.
- Analyze and evaluate relevant theories, models, and frameworks related to the subject.
- Apply critical thinking skills to solve complex problems and make informed decisions.
- Communicate effectively using appropriate language and terminology related to the subject.
- Conduct research and analyze data to support arguments and conclusions related to the subject.
- Demonstrate proficiency in using relevant tools, technologies, and methods related to the subject.
- Recognize ethical and professional responsibilities related to the subject and apply them appropriately.
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals related to the subject.
- Develop a deep understanding of the subject matter and its relevance to broader societal and global issues.
- Apply knowledge and skills acquired during the course to real-world situations and contexts.
- Continuously learn and update knowledge and skills related to the subject to stay current with the latest developments and trends.
