#### d) Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

When it comes to geometry, calculating the area of different shapes is an important part of understanding the subject. In this section, we will explore the formulas for calculating the area of a rectangle, triangle, and circle, based on the values provided in the standard.

Here are the key points to keep in mind:

##### 1. Rectangle

- A rectangle is a four-sided shape with two pairs of parallel sides.
- To calculate the area of a rectangle, you need to multiply the length and the width of the rectangle.
- The formula for calculating the area of a rectangle is: `Area = length x width`.
- The values for length and width can be obtained from the standard.

##### 2. Triangle

- A triangle is a three-sided shape with three angles.
- To calculate the area of a triangle, you need to multiply the base of the triangle and the height of the triangle, and then divide the result by two.
- The formula for calculating the area of a triangle is: `Area = (base x height) / 2`.
- The values for base and height can be obtained from the standard.

##### 3. Circle

- A circle is a round shape with no corners or edges.
- To calculate the area of a circle, you need to multiply the radius of the circle by itself, and then multiply the result by pi (π).
- The formula for calculating the area of a circle is: `Area = π x radius^2`.
- The value for the radius can be obtained from the standard.

By using these formulas and the values provided in the standard, you can easily calculate the area of different shapes. It is important to remember the formulas and the units of measurement used for the length, width, base, height, and radius, to ensure accurate calculations.
