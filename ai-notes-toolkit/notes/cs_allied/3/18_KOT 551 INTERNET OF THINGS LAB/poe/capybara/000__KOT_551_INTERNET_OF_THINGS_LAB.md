# KOT 551 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

In this lab, you will learn about the following:

## 1. Introduction to Internet of Things (IoT)
- Definition of IoT
- Applications of IoT
- IoT architecture

## 2. Sensors and Actuators
- Types of Sensors and Actuators
- Interfacing sensors and actuators with IoT devices

## 3. IoT Platforms and Frameworks
- Overview of IoT platforms and frameworks
- Comparison of popular IoT platforms and frameworks

## 4. IoT Communication Protocols
- Types of IoT communication protocols
- Comparison of popular IoT communication protocols

## 5. IoT Security
- Overview of IoT security
- IoT security threats and vulnerabilities
- IoT security measures and best practices

## 6. IoT Data Analytics
- Overview of IoT data analytics
- Techniques for IoT data analysis
- IoT data visualization

## 7. IoT Applications
- IoT applications in various industries
- Case studies of successful IoT implementations

## 8. IoT Project Development
- Overview of IoT project development
- Steps involved in IoT project development
- Examples of IoT projects

By the end of this lab, you will have a comprehensive understanding of Internet of Things and its various components. You will also be equipped with the knowledge and skills to develop your own IoT projects.
