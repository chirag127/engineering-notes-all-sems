
#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the process of sending data between two or more devices without the need for physical cables.
2. Wireless data transmission is typically accomplished through the use of radio waves, microwaves, infrared light, or other electromagnetic radiation.
3. Wireless data transmission can be used for various purposes, such as transferring files, streaming audio and video, and providing internet access.
4. Wireless data transmission can be achieved through several different technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
5. In order to transmit data wirelessly, both the sender and the receiver must have compatible devices and must be within the same range of the signal.
6. Security measures, such as encryption and authentication, must also be in place to ensure the safety of the transmitted data.
