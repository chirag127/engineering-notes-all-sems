
# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data. 

2. IoT is a combination of hardware, software, and communication networks that allow physical objects to interact with each other and with the external environment. 

3. IoT applications are used in a variety of industries such as healthcare, automotive, manufacturing, and retail. 

4. The main components of an IoT system are the sensors, actuators, controllers, and communication networks. 

5. Sensors are used to detect changes in the environment and to collect data. Actuators are used to control the environment and to perform actions. Controllers are used to process the data and to control the actuators. Communication networks are used to connect the sensors, actuators, and controllers. 

6. IoT systems can be used to monitor and control physical objects in real-time. 

7. Security is a major concern in IoT systems, as they are vulnerable to malicious attacks. 

8. To ensure the security of IoT systems, the use of encryption, authentication, and authorization techniques is recommended. 

9. The development of IoT systems requires expertise in hardware, software, and communication networks.
