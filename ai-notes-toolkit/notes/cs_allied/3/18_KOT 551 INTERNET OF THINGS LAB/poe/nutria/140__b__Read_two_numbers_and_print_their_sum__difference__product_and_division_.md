
#### b) Read two numbers and print their sum, difference, product and division.

1. To read two numbers, use the `input()` function to get user input and store it in two variables.
2. To calculate the sum of two numbers, use the `+` operator.
3. To calculate the difference between two numbers, use the `-` operator.
4. To calculate the product of two numbers, use the `*` operator.
5. To calculate the division of two numbers, use the `/` operator.
6. To print the results of the calculations, use the `print()` function.
