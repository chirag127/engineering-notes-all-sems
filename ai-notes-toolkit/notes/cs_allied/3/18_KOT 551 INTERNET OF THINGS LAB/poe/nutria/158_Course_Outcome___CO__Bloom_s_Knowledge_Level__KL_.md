
## Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

1. **Remembering**: This level of Bloom's Taxonomy is the most basic and involves the recall or recognition of specific facts, terms, basic concepts and answers.
2. **Understanding**: At this level, the student must be able to demonstrate a comprehension of facts and ideas by organizing, comparing, translating, interpreting, giving descriptions and stating the main idea.
3. **Applying**: At this level, the student must be able to use learned material in new and concrete situations by implementing a procedure or process to solve a problem.
4. **Analyzing**: At this level, the student must be able to break down material into its components and identify the relationships among the parts.
5. **Evaluating**: At this level, the student must be able to make judgments about the value of ideas or materials based on a set of criteria.
6. **Creating**: At this level, the student must be able to put elements together to form a coherent or functional whole; to reorganize elements into a new pattern or structure.
