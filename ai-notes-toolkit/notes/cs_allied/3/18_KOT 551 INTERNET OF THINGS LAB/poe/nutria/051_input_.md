
# Input
Input is a term used in computing to refer to the data that is sent to a computer for processing. It can be in the form of text, numbers, images, audio, video or other types of data. Input is usually provided by a user, but it can also be generated by a computer program or other device.

Input is received by the computer through a variety of input devices, such as a keyboard, mouse, microphone, scanner, camera, or joystick. The computer then processes the input and produces output, which is the result of the processing.
