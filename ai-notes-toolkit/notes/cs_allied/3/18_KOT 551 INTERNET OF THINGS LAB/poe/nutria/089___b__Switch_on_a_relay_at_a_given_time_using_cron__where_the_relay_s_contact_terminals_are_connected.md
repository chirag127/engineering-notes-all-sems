
#### b) Switch on a relay at a given time using cron, where the relay's contact terminals are connected to a load

1. A relay is an electromagnetic switch which is used to control a large amount of electrical power through a small amount of electrical power. 
2. A cron is a type of software utility used to schedule tasks to run periodically at certain times or dates. 
3. The contact terminals of the relay are connected to a load, which is the device that is being powered by the relay. 
4. To switch on the relay at a given time, the cron must be configured to run a command at the specified time. 
5. The command should be written to turn on the relay, which can be done by setting the relay’s contact terminals to a high voltage level. 
6. Once the command is run, the relay will remain on until the cron is configured to run another command to turn off the relay.
