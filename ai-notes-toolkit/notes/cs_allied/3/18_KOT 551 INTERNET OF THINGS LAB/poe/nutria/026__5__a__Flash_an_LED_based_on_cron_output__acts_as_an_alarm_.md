
#### 5. a) Flash an LED based on cron output (acts as an alarm)
1. Create a `cron` job that will run at the desired time.
2. Connect an LED to the GPIO pins of the Raspberry Pi.
3. Write a script to turn on the LED when the cron job runs.
4. Set the cron job to run at the desired time.
5. The LED will now flash at the desired time, acting as an alarm.
