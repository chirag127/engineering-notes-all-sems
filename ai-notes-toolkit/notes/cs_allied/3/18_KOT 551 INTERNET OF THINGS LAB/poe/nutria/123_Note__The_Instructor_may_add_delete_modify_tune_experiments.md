
# Note: The Instructor may add/delete/modify/tune experiments

1. An instructor may add experiments to the course syllabus in order to enhance the learning experience.
2. An instructor may delete experiments from the course syllabus if they are no longer relevant or necessary.
3. An instructor may modify existing experiments in order to keep the course syllabus up to date.
4. An instructor may tune experiments in order to optimize the learning experience for the students.
