

# CO 3 Demonstrate the Ability to Transmit Data Wirelessly Between Different Devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission refers to the process of sending data between two or more devices without the need for physical cables or wires. 
2. Wireless data transmission is made possible by radio waves, which are electromagnetic waves that travel through the air. 
3. The most common type of wireless data transmission is Wi-Fi, which is used to connect computers and other devices to the internet. 
4. Bluetooth is another type of wireless data transmission that is used to connect devices such as headphones, keyboards, and mice to computers.
5. Infrared is another type of wireless data transmission that is used to transfer data between devices that are close together. 
6. Wireless data transmission can also be used for communication between devices, such as between two computers or between a computer and a smartphone. 
7. Wireless data transmission is becoming increasingly popular as it eliminates the need for cables and wires, making it easier to connect devices.
