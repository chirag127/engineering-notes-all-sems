
# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)
* **Remembering:** This level involves the ability to recall facts, terms, basic concepts and answers.
* **Understanding:** This level requires the ability to comprehend the meaning, translation, interpolation and interpretation of instructions and problems.
* **Applying:** At this level, the learner is able to utilize acquired knowledge, facts, techniques and rules in order to solve given problems.
* **Analyzing:** This level involves breaking down a concept or idea into its component parts so that its organizational structure may be understood.
* **Evaluating:** This level requires the ability to judge the value of material for a given purpose.
* **Creating:** This level involves the ability to put parts together to form a whole and to recognize relationships between facts, terms, and concepts.
