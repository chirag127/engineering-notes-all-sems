

# Area of a Given Shape

* A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides that are equal in length and parallel to each other. The area of a rectangle can be calculated by multiplying the length of the rectangle by its width.
* A triangle is a three-sided shape with three sides of varying lengths. The area of a triangle can be calculated by multiplying the base of the triangle by its height, and then dividing by 2.
* A circle is a two-dimensional shape with a curved line around its circumference. The area of a circle can be calculated by multiplying the radius of the circle by itself, and then multiplying the result by pi (3.14).
