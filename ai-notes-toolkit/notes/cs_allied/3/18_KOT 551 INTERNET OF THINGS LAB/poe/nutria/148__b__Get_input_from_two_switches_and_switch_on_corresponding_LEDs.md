
#### b) Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

1. Connect two switches to the input ports of the microcontroller.
2. Write a program to read the input from the switches.
3. When the input from the switch is detected, turn on the corresponding LED.
4. Use a loop to continuously monitor the switches for input.
5. When the switch is activated, the corresponding LED should turn on.
6. When the switch is deactivated, the corresponding LED should turn off.
7. Test the program to ensure that the LEDs are turning on and off correctly.
