

#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

* The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects or "things" that are embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables them to collect and exchange data. 
* IoT allows objects to be sensed and/or controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy, and economic benefit. 
* IoT devices can be connected to the Internet and send and receive data. They can be used to monitor the environment, track objects, and control everyday objects such as lights, locks, and thermostats. 
* IoT devices are often connected to the cloud, allowing them to be accessed from anywhere. This allows users to control their devices remotely and receive notifications when something happens. 
* IoT devices can also be used to create smart homes, where all of the devices in the home are connected and can be controlled from one central hub. 
* IoT can also be used in the industrial space, where it can be used to monitor and control industrial processes and machinery. 
* IoT is an emerging technology that is transforming the way businesses and consumers interact with the physical world. IoT is enabling new applications, such as connected cars, smart homes, and predictive maintenance, that were not possible before.
