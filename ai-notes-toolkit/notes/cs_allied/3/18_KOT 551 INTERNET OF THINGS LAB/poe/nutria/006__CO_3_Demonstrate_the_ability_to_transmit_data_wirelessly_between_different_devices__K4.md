
#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

1. Wireless data transmission is the process of sending and receiving data over a wireless network. 
2. Wireless data transmission can be achieved through a variety of technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks. 
3. Wireless data transmission is used in a variety of applications, including the transmission of audio and video, file sharing, and remote control. 
4. Wireless data transmission requires a transmitter and a receiver. The transmitter sends data over the air using radio waves, while the receiver receives the data and converts it into a usable format. 
5. Wireless data transmission is faster and more efficient than wired data transmission, and it allows for more flexibility in terms of device placement and movement. 
6. Security is an important consideration when using wireless data transmission, as the data is transmitted over the air and can be intercepted by malicious actors. To protect the data, encryption and authentication protocols can be used.
