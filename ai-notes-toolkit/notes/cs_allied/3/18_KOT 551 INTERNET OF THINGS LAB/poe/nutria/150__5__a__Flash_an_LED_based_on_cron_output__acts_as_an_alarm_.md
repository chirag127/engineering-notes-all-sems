
#### 5. a) Flashing an LED Based on Cron Output (Acts as an Alarm)

1. Understand the concept of cron: Cron is a tool for scheduling tasks to be run periodically. It is commonly used for system administration and automation.

2. Create a cron job: A cron job is a set of commands that are executed at a specific time or on a specific schedule.

3. Connect the LED to the cron job: The LED can be connected to the cron job by wiring it to the appropriate pins on the Raspberry Pi.

4. Configure the cron job: The cron job can be configured to run at a specific time or on a specific schedule.

5. Flash the LED: The LED will flash when the cron job is executed. This can be used as an alarm or a reminder.
