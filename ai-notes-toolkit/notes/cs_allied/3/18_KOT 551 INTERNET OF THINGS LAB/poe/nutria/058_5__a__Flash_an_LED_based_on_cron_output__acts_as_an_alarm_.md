
#### 5. a) Flashing an LED Based on Cron Output (Acts as an Alarm)
1. Cron is a time-based job scheduler in Unix-like computer operating systems. 
2. It is used to schedule tasks to be performed at specific times or intervals. 
3. Cron output can be used to trigger a flashing LED.
4. To do this, the LED needs to be connected to a GPIO pin on the Raspberry Pi.
5. A script can then be written to read the cron output and turn on the LED when the output is high.
6. The LED can be set to flash at specific times or intervals, acting as an alarm.
