
#### b) Read two numbers and print their sum, difference, product and division

1. To read two numbers, start by declaring two variables to store the numbers: `int num1` and `int num2`. 
2. Then, use the `scanf()` function to read the two numbers from the user and store them in the variables: `scanf("%d %d", &num1, &num2);`
3. To print the sum of the two numbers, use the `printf()` function and the `+` operator: `printf("The sum is: %d", num1 + num2);`
4. To print the difference of the two numbers, use the `printf()` function and the `-` operator: `printf("The difference is: %d", num1 - num2);`
5. To print the product of the two numbers, use the `printf()` function and the `*` operator: `printf("The product is: %d", num1 * num2);`
6. To print the division of the two numbers, use the `printf()` function and the `/` operator: `printf("The division is: %d", num1 / num2);`
