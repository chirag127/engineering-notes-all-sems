
### The Student Should Have Hands-On Experience in Using Various Sensors
1. Temperature sensors measure the temperature of their environment.
2. Humidity sensors measure the amount of water vapor in the air.
3. Smoke sensors detect the presence of smoke in the air.
4. Light sensors measure the amount of light in an environment.
5. Motion sensors detect movement in an area.
6. Pressure sensors measure the pressure of an environment.
7. Gas sensors detect the presence of certain gases in the air.
