
# b) Switch on a relay at a given time using cron, where the relay's contact terminals are connected to a load

1. A relay is an electromechanical device that consists of a coil, a set of contact terminals, and an armature. 
2. When the coil is energized, the armature moves, opening or closing the contact terminals. 
3. A cron job is a Linux utility used to schedule tasks to be executed at a certain time. 
4. The cron job can be used to switch on the relay at a given time. 
5. The contact terminals of the relay must be connected to the load that needs to be switched on/off. 
6. When the relay is switched on, the contact terminals will be closed, allowing the current to flow through the load.
