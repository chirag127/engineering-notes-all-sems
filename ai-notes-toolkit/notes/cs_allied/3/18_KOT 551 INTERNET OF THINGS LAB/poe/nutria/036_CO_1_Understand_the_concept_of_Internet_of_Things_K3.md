
# CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a system of interconnected devices, machines, objects, and people that can communicate with each other and share data.

2. IoT devices are typically embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable them to collect and exchange data.

3. IoT devices can be connected to the internet, allowing them to communicate with other devices and systems.

4. IoT technology is used in a variety of applications, including home automation, smart cities, healthcare, industrial automation, and more.

5. IoT technology enables devices to be monitored and controlled remotely, allowing for greater efficiency and convenience.

6. Security is an important consideration when implementing IoT technology, as the data collected and transmitted by IoT devices is often sensitive.
