
#### b) Get input from two switches and switch on corresponding LEDs

1. Connect two switches to the appropriate pins on the microcontroller.
2. Configure the switches as digital input pins.
3. Write a program to read the digital input from the switches.
4. Write a program to switch on the corresponding LEDs based on the digital input from the switches.
5. Test the program to ensure that the correct LEDs are switched on when the switches are pressed.
