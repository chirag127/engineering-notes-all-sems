
#### CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

* Wireless data transmission is the process of sending data from one device to another without the use of physical cables or wires.
* Wireless data transmission can be accomplished through a variety of technologies, including Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, infrared, and radio frequency.
* Wi-Fi is the most widely used wireless data transmission technology. It uses radio waves to send and receive data between two or more devices.
* Bluetooth is another popular wireless data transmission technology. It uses short-range radio waves to connect two or more devices, such as smartphones and computers.
* Infrared and radio frequency are two other technologies used to transmit data wirelessly. Infrared is typically used to send data over short distances, while radio frequency is used for longer-range transmissions.
* To transmit data wirelessly, both the sending and receiving devices must be compatible with the same wireless data transmission technology.
* Security is an important consideration when transmitting data wirelessly. It is important to ensure that the data is encrypted and that the transmission is secure.
