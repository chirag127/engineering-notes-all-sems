
#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects such as vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity. 
2. This allows these objects to collect and exchange data. 
3. IoT enables objects to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure, creating opportunities for more direct integration of the physical world into computer-based systems, and resulting in improved efficiency, accuracy and economic benefit. 
4. IoT can also help to improve the quality of life by providing better access to information, services, and resources. 
5. IoT can be used in various applications such as smart homes, smart cities, health care, industrial automation, and transportation. 
6. IoT devices can be connected to the internet and can communicate with each other to exchange data. 
7. IoT devices can be used to monitor, control, and automate various tasks. 
8. IoT can help to reduce energy consumption, improve safety and security, and increase efficiency. 
9. Security is a major concern with IoT, as it can be vulnerable to cyber-attacks. 
10. It is important to ensure that IoT devices are secure and that the data they exchange is protected.
