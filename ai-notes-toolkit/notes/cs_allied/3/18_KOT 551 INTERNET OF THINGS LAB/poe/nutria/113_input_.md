
# Input
1. In computing, an input is any data, information, or instructions that are provided to a computer or other device in order to produce an output. 
2. Inputs may be provided through a variety of sources, such as a keyboard, mouse, microphone, or camera. 
3. Inputs can also be provided in a variety of formats, such as text, numbers, images, audio, and video. 
4. The device used to provide input to a computer is known as an input device. 
5. Examples of input devices include keyboards, mice, trackballs, touchscreens, microphones, and scanners. 
6. Inputs are usually processed by a computer program, which produces an output. 
7. The output of a program is typically displayed on a screen or sent to an output device, such as a printer or speaker.
