
# Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

* **KL 1: Remembering** - Students should be able to recall information from memory.
* **KL 2: Understanding** - Students should be able to comprehend the meaning of the material.
* **KL 3: Applying** - Students should be able to apply the knowledge and skills they have learned to solve problems.
* **KL 4: Analyzing** - Students should be able to break down the material into its component parts and analyze it.
* **KL 5: Evaluating** - Students should be able to assess the material and make judgments about its validity and usefulness.
* **KL 6: Creating** - Students should be able to use the material to create something new.
