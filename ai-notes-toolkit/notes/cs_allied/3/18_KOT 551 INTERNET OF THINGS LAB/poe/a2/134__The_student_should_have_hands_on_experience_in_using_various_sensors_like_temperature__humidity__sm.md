### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc. and

1. Temperature sensor: Measures the temperature and outputs an analog or digital signal proportional to the temperature. Students should learn to interface the sensor with a microcontroller and display the temperature on a LCD or send the data to a computer.

2. Humidity sensor: Measures the amount of moisture in the air and outputs an analog or digital signal proportional to the humidity. Students should learn to interface the sensor with a microcontroller and display the humidity on a LCD or send the data to a computer.

3. Smoke sensor: Detects smoke or fire and outputs a digital signal. Students should learn to interface the sensor with a microcontroller and activate alarms or relay circuits in case of fire detection.

4. Light sensor: Measures the intensity of light and outputs an analog or digital signal proportional to the light intensity. Students should learn to interface the sensor with a microcontroller and control devices like LEDs based on the light intensity.

The hands on experience with various sensors will help students understand the working principles of different types of sensors and their applications in embedded system projects and products. The knowledge can be applied to create smart and automated systems.
