# Course Outcome (CO)
## Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

KL1: Remembering
- CO1: Recall relevant theories, concepts, models, etc. learned in the course
- CO2: Identify key terms, facts, dates, events, people, etc. relevant to the course topic

KL2: Understanding
- CO3: Explain ideas, concepts, theories, methods, analyses, results, etc. from the course
- CO4: Summarize and organize key information and ideas from the course
- CO5: Compare and contrast different issues, perspectives, methodologies, theories, results, etc. from the course

KL3: Applying
- CO6: Use methods, techniques, concepts, theories, etc. from the course to solve new problems
- CO7: Apply course knowledge and skills to relevant situations

KL4: Analyzing
- CO8: Distinguish between facts and inferences, opinions and judgments in course-related information
- CO9: Analyze complex ideas, arguments, problems, etc. into their component parts
- CO10: Assess the logical consistency, assumptions, implications, etc. of course-related information

KL5: Evaluating
- CO11: Make judgments about the value or merit of theories, arguments, methods, results, etc. from the course
- CO12: Evaluate alternatives or make choices among options based on given criteria

KL6: Creating
- CO13: Generate new and original ideas, solutions, productions, etc. related to the course topic
- CO14: Design, develop, or produce something new that applies or makes use of course knowledge and skills
