### The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc. and

1. Understand the working principle of various sensors like:
- Temperature sensor: Measures temperature and converts it into an electrical signal. Works on the principle of change in resistance with temperature.
- Humidity sensor: Measures humidity and converts it into an electrical signal. Works on the principle of change in capacitance or resistance with humidity.
- Smoke sensor: Detects smoke or fire and converts it into an electrical signal. Works on the principle of detecting scattered light from smoke particles.
- Light sensor: Measures intensity of light and converts it into an electrical signal. Works on the principle of change in resistance or voltage with light intensity.

2. Learn to interface various sensors with microcontrollers/microprocessors and display the sensor outputs.
3. Learn to calibrate the sensors and compensate for errors like hysteresis and drift.
4. Understand applications of various sensors like temperature sensors are used in air conditioners, refrigerators, humidity sensors are used in green houses, smoke sensors are used in fire alarms, light sensors are used in light dimmers, etc.
