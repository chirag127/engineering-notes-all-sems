## Course Outcome ( CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

- CO2: Understand: Demonstrate understanding of facts and ideas. (KL: Understanding)
- CO3: Apply: Apply knowledge and skills to new situations. (KL: Applying)
- CO4: Analyze: Break down information and distinguish between facts and inferences. (KL: Analyzing)
- CO5: Evaluate: Make judgments and decisions based on criteria and standards. (KL: Evaluating)
- CO6: Create: Put parts together to form a coherent or functional whole; create new and original materials or products. (KL: Creating)
