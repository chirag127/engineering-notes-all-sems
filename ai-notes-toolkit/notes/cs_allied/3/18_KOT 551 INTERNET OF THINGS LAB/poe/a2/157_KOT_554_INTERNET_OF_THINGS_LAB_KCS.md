# KOT 554 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

1. Introduction to Internet of Things (IoT):
- IoT refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects/devices via the internet.
- These devices are equipped with sensors, software, and network connectivity that allows them to send and receive data.
- IoT enables these devices to be sensed and controlled remotely across existing network infrastructure.

2. IoT Architecture:
- The IoT system consists of sensors/devices, connectivity, cloud, and applications.
- Sensors/devices collect data and transmit it over connectivity medium to the cloud.
- The cloud stores, analyses, and processes the data.
- Applications provide user interfaces to access IoT data and features.

3. IoT Protocols:
- Different protocols are used for device-to-device and device-to-cloud communication in IoT.
- Common IoT protocols include MQTT, CoAP, AMQP, XMPP, HTTP, etc.
- Selection of a protocol depends on the types of devices/applications and network constraints.
