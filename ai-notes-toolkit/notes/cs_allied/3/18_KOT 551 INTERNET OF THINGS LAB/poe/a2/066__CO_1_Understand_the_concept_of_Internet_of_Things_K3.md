#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

1. Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items that are embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to collect and exchange data.

2. IoT devices are interconnected and can be remotely monitored and controlled over the existing network infrastructure. This connection allows the devices to send and receive data, helping them to work together more efficiently and effectively.

3. The core components of the IoT system are:

- Sensors: Sensors detect and measure physical parameters of the surrounding environment. They collect data and trigger an action.
- Connectivity: The components are connected to the network using wireless protocols like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, etc. This allows the devices to send and receive data.
- Data processing: The data collected by the sensors is sent to the cloud or data centers where it is aggregated, analyzed, and processed.
- User Interface: The processed data is sent to the user interface to display the required information or trigger actions.

4. The applications of IoT are numerous and include smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, healthcare, transportation, and logistics. The IoT aims to make our lives more convenient, efficient, and secure.

5. The challenges in IoT include security, privacy, interoperability, massive data storage and processing requirements, and development of standards. Security is a major concern as the increased connectivity can lead to more vulnerabilities that can be exploited by hackers.
