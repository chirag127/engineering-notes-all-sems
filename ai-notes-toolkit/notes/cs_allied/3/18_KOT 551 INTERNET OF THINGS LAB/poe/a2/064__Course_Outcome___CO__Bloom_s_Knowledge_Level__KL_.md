## Course Outcome ( CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

1. Remembering: Recalling facts, terms, basic concepts and answers without understanding them deeply. Eg: Remembering formulas, definitions, dates, events etc.

2. Understanding: Grasping the meaning of information, being able to translate figures, examples or statements into own words. Eg: Explaining ideas or concepts, summarizing information.

3. Applying: Using information in familiar situations. Eg: Using methods, formulae, concepts to solve problems.

4. Analyzing: Breaking information into parts, examining the relationship between the parts. Eg: Determining why problems occur, making inferences, identifying patterns, categorizing.

5. Evaluating: Making judgements about value of ideas, materials using internal or external criteria. Eg: Assessing validity of information, critiquing work, experiment results or solutions to problems. Evaluating alternatives.

6. Creating: Putting parts together or rearranging information to make a new or original product or clarify ideas. Eg: Formulating hypotheses, designing experiments, planning new approaches to problems. Creating artistic objects or presentations.
