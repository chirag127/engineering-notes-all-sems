## Course Outcome ( CO) Bloom’s Knowledge Level (KL)

1. Remembering: Recall or recognize terms, definitions, facts, ideas, materials, patterns, sequences, methods, etc. previously learned.
- Students will be able to recall and recognize key terms and facts related to the topic.

2. Understanding: Read and comprehend ideas, arguments, or other forms of communication.
- Students will be able to explain ideas or concepts in their own words. They will be able to summarize key points or themes.

3. Applying: Apply or use abstract or concrete concepts, procedures, methods, formulas, principles, laws, generalizations, or theories in new situations.
- Students will be able to use the concepts or methods they have learned in new problem-solving or decision-making situations. They will be able to adapt or apply ideas in new areas or scenarios.

4. Analyzing: Break ideas or communications down into parts, examine and try to understand the relationships among the parts, and determine how the parts relate to one another and to an overall structure or purpose.
- Students will be able to distinguish between relevant and irrelevant information. They will detect logical flaws or inconsistencies in arguments or methods. They will be able to see multiple sides or perspectives on issues or problems.

5. Evaluating: Make judgments about the value of ideas, works, solutions, methods, materials, etc. based on selected criteria.
- Students will be able to assess the implications of ideas or alternative solutions. They will be able to determine the merits of positions or methods. They will be able to determine effectiveness, relevance, significance, worth, etc.

6. Creating: Put together elements or parts in a new pattern or structure to make something original or to make something that did not exist before.
- Students will be able to generate, plan, and produce new or original works, solutions, methods, etc. They will be able to create, design, compose, etc.
