# KOT 552 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

1. Introduction
- Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and appliances via the internet. These devices are equipped with sensors, software, and networking capabilities that allow them to send and receive data.
- IoT Lab allows students to gain hands-on experience with IoT devices and platforms. Students learn how to build IoT systems, connect sensors and actuators, securely access and control devices, store and analyze data, and develop applications.

2. IoT Devices and Protocols
- Commonly used IoT devices include microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and single-board computers like Raspberry Pi.
- Common IoT protocols and standards include MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, and WebSockets for communication and IEEE 802.11 (Wi-Fi), Bluetooth, and Zigbee for wireless networking.

3. IoT Platforms and Software
- IoT platforms facilitate connecting and managing IoT devices and their data. Some popular IoT platforms are AWS IoT, Azure IoT, and Google Cloud IoT.
- Programming languages and software frameworks used for IoT include C/C++, Python, JavaScript, Rust, Arduino IDE, and Node-RED.
