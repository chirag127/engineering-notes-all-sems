# CO 3 Demonstrate the ability to transmit data wirelessly between different devices. K4

- Wireless transmission of data is the transfer of data over a distance without the use of wires or cables. Some commonly used methods for wireless data transmission are:
- Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi is a wireless networking technology that allows devices like laptops, tablets, and smartphones to exchange data wirelessly over a network. Wi-Fi uses radio waves to create wireless network connections known as wireless local area networks or WLANs.
- Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances using radio waves in the frequency range of 2.400 to 2.485 gigahertz from fixed and mobile devices and for creating wireless peripheral networks.
- Infrared: Infrared or IR radiation refers to electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths between 0.75 and 1000 micrometers, longer than visible light but shorter than terahertz radiation. Infrared is used in remote controls, infrared data transmission, thermal imaging and other applications.
- Radio: Radio waves are a type of electromagnetic radiation with wavelengths in the electromagnetic spectrum longer than infrared light. Radio waves are used for wireless radio transmission technologies like AM/FM radio broadcasting, cell phones, two-way radios, wireless computer networks (wireless LANs), etc.
