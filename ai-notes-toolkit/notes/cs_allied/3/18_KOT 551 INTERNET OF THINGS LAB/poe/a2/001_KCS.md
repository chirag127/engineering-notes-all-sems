# KCS

Knowledge-Centered Service

1. KCS is a methodology to capture, organize, and provide relevant information to users.
2. It emphasizes on creating and maintaining reusable information/knowledge.
3. The key components of KCS are:
- Knowledge base: A centralized repository of information.
- Knowledge engineers: People responsible for capturing and organizing information.
- Knowledge managers: People responsible for the optimal use of captured knowledge.
4. The benefits of adopting KCS are:
- Reduced duplication of work.
- Increased productivity and efficiency.
- Improved quality of services and products.
- Decreased dependence on individuals.
- Better leveraging of organizational knowledge.
5. The steps to implement KCS are:
- Identify the key knowledge areas and audiences.
- Capture knowledge in a structured manner.
- Organize and link the captured knowledge.
- Ensure knowledge is accessible to relevant users.
- Continuously improve the knowledge and process.
