#### b) Read two numbers and print their sum, difference, product and division.

1. Take two numbers as input from the user.
2. Calculate their sum: Sum = Number1 + Number2
3. Calculate their difference: Difference = Number1 - Number2
4. Calculate their product: Product = Number1 * Number2
5. Calculate their division: Division = Number1 / Number2
6. Print all the calculated values.

The code will look like:

num1 = int(input("Enter first number: "))
num2 = int(input("Enter second number: "))

sum = num1 + num2
difference = num1 - num2
product = num1 * num2
division = num1 / num2

print("Sum:", sum)
print("Difference:", difference)
print("Product:", product)
print("Division:", division)
