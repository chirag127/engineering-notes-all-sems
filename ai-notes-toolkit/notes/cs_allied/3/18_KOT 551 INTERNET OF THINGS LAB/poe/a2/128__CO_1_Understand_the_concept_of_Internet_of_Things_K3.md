#### CO 1 Understand the concept of Internet of Things K3

1. Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and appliances via the internet. These devices are equipped with sensors, software, and network connectivity that allow them to send and receive data.

2. IoT enables common devices and appliances we use every day to be connected together and exchange information, creating opportunities for integration and automation in homes, businesses, and cities. For example, smart devices like thermostats, lights, door locks, etc. can be connected to the internet so you can control them remotely via a mobile app.

3. The core components of any IoT system are:
- Sensors: To detect and measure physical parameters like temperature, motion, pressure, etc. and convert the measurements into digital data.
- Connectivity: To enable the transfer of data over the internet using WiFi, Bluetooth, cellular data, etc.
- Cloud: To store and process the data received from the sensors and devices. The data can be analyzed to gain useful insights and trigger actions.
- Applications: To interact with the IoT system via desktop or mobile apps to monitor devices, automate tasks, etc.

4. The key benefits of IoT are:
- Increased efficiency: Automate tasks and processes to reduce waste and costs.
- Remote monitoring: Check on devices and systems from anywhere at any time.
- Smart environments: Create smart homes, cities, and workplaces that can adjust to conditions and needs.
- Data-driven insights: Collect and analyze data to uncover patterns and make better decisions.
