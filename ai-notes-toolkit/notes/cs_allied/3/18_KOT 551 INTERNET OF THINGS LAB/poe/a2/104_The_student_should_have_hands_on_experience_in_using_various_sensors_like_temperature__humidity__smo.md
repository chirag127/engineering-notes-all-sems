# The student should have hands on experience in using various sensors like temperature, humidity, smoke, light, etc. and

- Temperature sensor: Measures ambient temperature and can be used to detect variations in temperature. Students can learn to interface the sensor with a microcontroller and display the temperature readings.
- Humidity sensor: Measures relative humidity in the air. Students can learn to interface the sensor and display humidity readings. Excessive humidity can lead to the growth of mold and high temperatures can lead to sweating in humans which can be detected using this sensor.
- Smoke/Gas sensor: Detects smoke or inflammable gases in an area. Students can learn to interface the sensor and trigger an alarm or alert in case of fire or gas leak detection.
- Light sensor: Measures ambient light intensity. Students can learn to interface the sensor and automatically turn ON/OFF lights or other devices based on the light intensity.
- and other sensors: Students can explore other sensors like ultrasonic sensors to measure distance, flame sensors to detect fire, etc. and learn interfacing and applications of the various sensors.

Hands-on experience with interfacing of sensors will help students understand the working principles of different sensors and their applications in real-world projects.
