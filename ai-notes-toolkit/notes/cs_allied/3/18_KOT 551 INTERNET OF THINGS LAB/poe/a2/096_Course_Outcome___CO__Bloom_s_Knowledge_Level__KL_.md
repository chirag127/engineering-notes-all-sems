# Course Outcome (CO)
## Bloom's Knowledge Level (KL)

**KL1: Remembering**

- Recall or recognize terms, facts, and basic concepts
- Locate information in memory or designate materials that serve as sources of information

**KL2: Understanding**

- Translate, interpret, extrapolate, or paraphrase information based on existing knowledge
- Recognize relationships or connections between ideas
- Express, summarize, or paraphrase what is known about a topic

**KL3: Applying**

- Carry out or use a procedure through executing or implementing steps
- Solve a problem or a new situation by applying acquired knowledge, skills, or rules
- Adapt or modify information or procedures to fit a new situation or need

**KL4: Analyzing**

- Detect patterns or structure or break information or concepts into parts
- Determine how parts relate to each other or to an overall structure or purpose
- Make inferences or find evidence to support generalizations

**KL5: Evaluating**

- Assess or determine the value or worth of something
- Form and defend evaluative judgments based on internal evidence or on externally derived criteria
- Evaluate methods or procedures for obtaining data or information

**KL6: Creating**

- Design, compose, or produce something new that is of value
- Put elements together to form a novel, coherent whole or make an original work or contribution
- Construct a complex system or express artistically thoughts, feelings, or technical ideas through language, images, etc.
