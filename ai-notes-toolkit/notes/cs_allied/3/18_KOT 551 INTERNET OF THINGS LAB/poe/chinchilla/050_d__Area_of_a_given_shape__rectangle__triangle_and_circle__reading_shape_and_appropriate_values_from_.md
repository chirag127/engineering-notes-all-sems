# Area of a Given Shape

In this section, we will discuss how to calculate the area of three common geometric shapes: rectangle, triangle, and circle. We will assume that we have access to the appropriate values from standard, such as length, width, base, and radius.

## Rectangle

1. The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width:
   - Area = length x width
   
2. The standard unit for area is square units (e.g., square meters, square feet).

## Triangle

1. The area of a triangle is calculated by multiplying its base by its height and then dividing by 2:
   - Area = (base x height) / 2
   
2. The height of a triangle is the perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite vertex.

## Circle

1. The area of a circle is calculated by multiplying pi (π) by the square of its radius:
   - Area = π x radius^2
   
2. The value of π is approximately 3.14.
3. The radius is the distance from the center of the circle to any point on its perimeter.

Note: When calculating the area of a shape, it is important to use the appropriate units for the given values. For example, if the length and width of a rectangle are given in meters, the area should be expressed in square meters.

Remember to always check your calculations and units to ensure that your answers are accurate and meaningful.
