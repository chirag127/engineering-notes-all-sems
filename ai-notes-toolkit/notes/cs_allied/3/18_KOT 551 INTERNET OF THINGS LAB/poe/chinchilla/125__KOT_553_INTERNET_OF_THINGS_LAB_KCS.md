# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

In this lab course, students will learn about the Internet of Things (IoT) and its applications. The course will focus on hands-on experience with IoT devices and platforms. Here are some key topics that will be covered in this lab:

- Introduction to IoT: Students will learn about the basics of IoT, including its components, architecture, and applications. They will also learn about the challenges and opportunities of IoT.

- IoT devices: Students will work with various IoT devices, including sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers. They will learn how to interface these devices with other systems and platforms.

- IoT platforms: Students will explore different IoT platforms, such as AWS IoT, Azure IoT, and Google Cloud IoT. They will learn how to use these platforms to collect, store, and analyze data from IoT devices.

- IoT applications: Students will develop various IoT applications, such as smart home systems, industrial monitoring systems, and environmental monitoring systems. They will learn how to design and implement these applications using the knowledge and skills they have acquired in the lab.

- IoT security: Students will learn about the security challenges of IoT and how to mitigate them. They will learn about various security technologies and protocols, such as encryption, authentication, and access control.

- IoT standards and protocols: Students will learn about the various standards and protocols used in IoT, such as MQTT, CoAP, and Zigbee. They will learn how to use these standards and protocols to ensure interoperability and compatibility among different IoT devices and platforms.

By the end of this lab course, students will have gained practical experience with IoT devices and platforms. They will be able to design, develop, and deploy IoT applications for various domains. They will also have a solid understanding of the challenges and opportunities of IoT and how to address them.
