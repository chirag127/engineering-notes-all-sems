**Rectangle:**

A rectangle is a four-sided shape with opposite sides being equal in length and parallel to each other. The formula to calculate the area of a rectangle is:

```
Area = Length x Width
```

To calculate the area of a rectangle, we need to know its length and width. These values are typically provided in standard units such as meters, centimeters, or feet.

**Triangle:**

A triangle is a three-sided shape with three angles. To calculate the area of a triangle, we need to know its base and height. The formula to calculate the area of a triangle is:

```
Area = 1/2 x Base x Height
```

The base and height of a triangle are typically provided in standard units such as meters, centimeters, or feet.

**Circle:**

A circle is a shape with a curved boundary that is made up of points that are equidistant from the center. To calculate the area of a circle, we need to know its radius. The formula to calculate the area of a circle is:

```
Area = π x Radius^2
```

The value of π is a constant that equals approximately 3.14. The radius of a circle is typically provided in standard units such as meters, centimeters, or feet.
