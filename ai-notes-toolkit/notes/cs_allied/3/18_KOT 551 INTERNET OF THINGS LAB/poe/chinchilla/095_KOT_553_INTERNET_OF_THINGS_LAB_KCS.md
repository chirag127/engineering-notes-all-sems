# KOT 553 INTERNET OF THINGS LAB KCS

In this lab, students will learn about the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the interconnection of physical devices through the internet. The lab will provide students with hands-on experience with IoT devices, sensors, and data analysis.

The following are the topics covered in KOT 553 Internet of Things Lab KCS:

## 1. Introduction to Internet of Things (IoT)

- Definition of IoT
- Evolution of IoT
- IoT Architecture
- IoT Applications

## 2. IoT Devices and Sensors

- Types of IoT Devices
- Sensors used in IoT
- Communication Protocols for IoT Devices

## 3. IoT Platforms and Cloud Services

- Introduction to IoT Platforms
- Cloud Services for IoT
- IoT Data Analytics

## 4. IoT Applications

- Smart Home Automation
- Industrial IoT
- Healthcare IoT
- Agriculture IoT

## 5. IoT Security and Privacy

- Security Challenges in IoT
- IoT Security Standards
- Privacy Concerns in IoT

## 6. IoT Project Development

- Project Planning and Execution
- IoT Project Design and Development
- IoT Project Testing and Deployment

Through KOT 553 Internet of Things Lab KCS, students will be able to gain practical knowledge of IoT, its applications, and its security concerns. The lab will enable students to develop IoT projects and analyze IoT data.
