#### d) Area of a given shape (rectangle, triangle and circle) reading shape and appropriate values from standard

When it comes to finding the area of a shape, it is essential to know the formula for each shape. In this section, we will learn about the area of three basic shapes, namely, rectangle, triangle and circle.

1. Rectangle:
   - A rectangle is a four-sided figure where opposite sides are equal and parallel.
   - To find the area of a rectangle, we need to know the length and width of the rectangle.
   - The formula to find the area of a rectangle is length x width or l x w.
   - For example, if the length of the rectangle is 10 cm and the width is 5 cm, the area of the rectangle will be 10 cm x 5 cm = 50 cm².

2. Triangle:
   - A triangle is a three-sided figure where the sum of the angles is 180 degrees.
   - To find the area of a triangle, we need to know the base and height of the triangle.
   - The formula to find the area of a triangle is 1/2 x base x height or 1/2 x b x h.
   - For example, if the base of the triangle is 6 cm and the height is 4 cm, the area of the triangle will be 1/2 x 6 cm x 4 cm = 12 cm².

3. Circle:
   - A circle is a round figure where all points on the boundary are equidistant from the center.
   - To find the area of a circle, we need to know the radius of the circle.
   - The formula to find the area of a circle is π x radius² or πr², where π (pi) is a mathematical constant with the approximate value of 3.14.
   - For example, if the radius of the circle is 7 cm, the area of the circle will be π x 7 cm² = 153.94 cm².

It is essential to remember these formulas and understand the concept thoroughly to solve problems related to finding the area of these basic shapes. By practicing more problems, one can become proficient in finding the area of various shapes.
