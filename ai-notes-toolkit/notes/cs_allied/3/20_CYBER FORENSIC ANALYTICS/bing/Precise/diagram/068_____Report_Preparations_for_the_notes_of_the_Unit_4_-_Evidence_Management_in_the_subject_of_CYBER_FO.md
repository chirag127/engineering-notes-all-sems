### Report Preparations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Introduction:** Evidence management is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics. It involves the collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence in a manner that is admissible in a court of law.

2. **Collection of Evidence:** The first step in evidence management is the collection of digital evidence. This involves identifying, locating, and acquiring data from various sources such as computer systems, storage devices, and networks.

3. **Preservation of Evidence:** Once the evidence has been collected, it must be preserved to ensure its integrity and admissibility in court. This involves creating a forensic image of the data and maintaining a chain of custody to document the handling of the evidence.

4. **Analysis of Evidence:** The next step is to analyze the evidence to extract relevant information. This involves using various tools and techniques to recover deleted files, examine file metadata, and identify patterns of activity.

5. **Presentation of Evidence:** The final step is to present the evidence in a manner that is clear, concise, and understandable to a judge or jury. This involves preparing reports, visual aids, and expert testimony to explain the significance of the evidence.

6. **Conclusion:** Evidence management is a critical component of cyber forensic analytics. By following best practices for the collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence, forensic analysts can help ensure that justice is served.
