### Cyber Terrorism

Cyber terrorism is the politically motivated use of computers and information technology to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society . It is a controversial term and some authors opt for a very narrow definition, relating to deployment by known terrorist organizations of disruption attacks against information systems for the primary purpose of creating alarm, panic, or physical disruption . The definition is sometimes expanded to include any cyber attack that intimidates or generates fear in the target population .

Here are some key points to remember about cyber terrorism:
- It is politically motivated.
- It involves the use of computers and information technology.
- Its goal is to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society.
- It can be carried out by known terrorist organizations or by individuals or groups with similar goals.
- It can involve attacks against information systems or the use of the internet to conduct violent acts.
