### Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering digital evidence from electronic devices and networks. It is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics, which involves the application of scientific methods to the investigation of cybercrime.

Some key points to consider when conducting a cyber investigation include:

1. Understanding the nature of the cybercrime: It is important to have a clear understanding of the type of cybercrime that has been committed in order to conduct an effective investigation.

2. Identifying and preserving digital evidence: Digital evidence can be found on a variety of electronic devices and networks. It is important to identify and preserve this evidence in a manner that maintains its integrity and admissibility in court.

3. Analyzing digital evidence: Once digital evidence has been collected, it must be analyzed in order to extract relevant information. This can involve the use of specialized software and techniques.

4. Reporting findings: The results of a cyber investigation must be reported in a clear and concise manner, often in the form of an expert witness report.

Cyber investigation is a complex and challenging field that requires a combination of technical expertise and investigative skills. It is an essential tool in the fight against cybercrime and the protection of digital assets.
