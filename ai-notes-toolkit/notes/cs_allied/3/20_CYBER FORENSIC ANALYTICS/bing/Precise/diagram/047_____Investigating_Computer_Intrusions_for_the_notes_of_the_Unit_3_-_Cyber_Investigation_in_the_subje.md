### Investigating Computer Intrusions

Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Identify the intrusion**: The first step in investigating a computer intrusion is to identify that an intrusion has occurred. This can be done by monitoring system logs, intrusion detection systems, and other security measures.

2. **Contain the intrusion**: Once an intrusion has been identified, it is important to contain it to prevent further damage. This can be done by disconnecting the affected systems from the network, blocking the attacker's IP address, or taking other appropriate measures.

3. **Collect evidence**: In order to investigate the intrusion, it is necessary to collect evidence. This can include system logs, network traffic, and any other relevant data.

4. **Analyze the evidence**: The collected evidence must be analyzed to determine the nature of the intrusion, the methods used by the attacker, and the extent of the damage.

5. **Remediate the damage**: After the analysis is complete, steps must be taken to remediate the damage caused by the intrusion. This can include restoring data from backups, patching vulnerabilities, and improving security measures.

6. **Report the incident**: It is important to report the incident to the appropriate authorities, such as law enforcement or regulatory agencies.

7. **Prevent future intrusions**: Finally, steps should be taken to prevent future intrusions. This can include improving security measures, training employees, and regularly monitoring systems for signs of intrusion.
