### Concepts of Investigation

Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Identification**: The first step in any investigation is to identify the incident or event that needs to be investigated. This could be a security breach, data theft, or any other cybercrime.

2. **Preservation**: Once the incident has been identified, it is important to preserve any evidence that may be relevant to the investigation. This could include logs, system images, or any other digital artifacts.

3. **Collection**: The next step is to collect the evidence in a forensically sound manner. This means that the evidence must be collected in a way that maintains its integrity and can be used in a court of law.

4. **Examination**: After the evidence has been collected, it must be examined to determine its relevance to the investigation. This could involve analyzing log files, reviewing system images, or performing other forensic analysis.

5. **Analysis**: Once the evidence has been examined, it must be analyzed to determine what happened, when it happened, and who was involved. This could involve correlating evidence from multiple sources, reconstructing events, or identifying patterns of behavior.

6. **Presentation**: The final step in any investigation is to present the findings in a clear and concise manner. This could involve writing a report, testifying in court, or presenting the findings to management.
