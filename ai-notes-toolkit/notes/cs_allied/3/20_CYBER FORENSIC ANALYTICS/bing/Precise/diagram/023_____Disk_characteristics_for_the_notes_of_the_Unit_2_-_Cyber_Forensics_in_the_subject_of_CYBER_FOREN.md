### Disk Characteristics

1. **Storage Capacity**: The amount of data that can be stored on a disk.
2. **Data Transfer Rate**: The rate at which data can be transferred to or from the disk.
3. **Access Time**: The time it takes for the disk to locate and retrieve data.
4. **Disk Cache**: A small amount of high-speed memory used to store frequently accessed data.
5. **Spindle Speed**: The rotational speed of the disk, measured in revolutions per minute (RPM).
6. **Form Factor**: The physical size and shape of the disk.
7. **Interface**: The type of connection used to connect the disk to the computer.
8. **Power Consumption**: The amount of power the disk uses during operation.
9. **Noise Level**: The amount of noise the disk generates during operation.
10. **Reliability**: The likelihood of the disk failing or experiencing errors.

These are some of the key characteristics of disks that are relevant to the study of Cyber Forensics. Understanding these characteristics can help in the analysis and recovery of data from disks in a forensic investigation.
