### CERNTin for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- CERNTin is an acronym that stands for Computer Emergency Response Team Information Network.
- It is a network of Computer Emergency Response Teams (CERTs) that work together to respond to computer security incidents.
- CERTs are responsible for responding to computer security incidents, such as hacking, viruses, and other malicious activities.
- CERNTin provides a platform for CERTs to share information and collaborate on incident response.
- The goal of CERNTin is to improve the overall security of computer systems by facilitating communication and cooperation between CERTs.
- CERNTin is an important tool for organizations to protect their computer systems from cyber threats.
- By being part of CERNTin, organizations can receive timely information about threats and vulnerabilities, and can work with other CERTs to respond to incidents.
- CERNTin is an important part of the overall cyber security infrastructure, and plays a critical role in protecting computer systems from cyber threats.
