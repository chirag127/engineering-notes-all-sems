### Trojans and Backdoors

Trojans and backdoors are types of malware that can be used to gain unauthorized access to a computer system. Here are some key points to remember:

1. **Trojans** are malicious programs that are disguised as harmless software. They can be used to steal information, install other malware, or give an attacker remote access to a system.
2. **Backdoors** are hidden entry points into a system that bypass normal security measures. They can be installed by attackers or by legitimate software for maintenance purposes.
3. Trojans and backdoors can be difficult to detect because they often use stealth techniques to hide their presence.
4. It is important to keep software and systems up to date with the latest security patches to reduce the risk of infection.
5. Regularly scanning for malware and using strong passwords can also help to protect against Trojans and backdoors.
