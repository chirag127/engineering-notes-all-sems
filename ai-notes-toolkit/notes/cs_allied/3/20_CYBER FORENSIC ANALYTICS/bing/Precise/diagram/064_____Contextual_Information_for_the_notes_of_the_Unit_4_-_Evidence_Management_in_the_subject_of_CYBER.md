### Contextual Information for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Evidence management is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics.
2. It involves the identification, collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.
3. Digital evidence can include data from various sources such as computer systems, storage devices, and networks.
4. Proper evidence management is essential to ensure the integrity and admissibility of digital evidence in legal proceedings.
5. Chain of custody must be maintained to document the handling of evidence from the time of collection to presentation in court.
6. Standard operating procedures and guidelines must be followed to ensure the proper handling of digital evidence.
7. Tools and techniques used in evidence management must be validated and tested to ensure their reliability and accuracy.
8. Evidence management is a collaborative effort involving multiple parties such as law enforcement, forensic analysts, and legal professionals.
9. Ongoing training and education are necessary to keep up with the rapidly evolving field of digital forensics and evidence management.
