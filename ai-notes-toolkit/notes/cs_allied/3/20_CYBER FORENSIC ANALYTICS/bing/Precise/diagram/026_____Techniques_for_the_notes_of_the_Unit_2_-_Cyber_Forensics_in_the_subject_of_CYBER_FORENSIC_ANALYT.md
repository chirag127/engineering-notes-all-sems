### Techniques for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Data Acquisition:** This technique involves the collection of digital evidence from various sources such as hard drives, mobile devices, and cloud storage. It is important to follow proper procedures to ensure the integrity of the evidence.

2. **Data Analysis:** This technique involves the examination of the collected data to identify and extract relevant information. Various tools and techniques such as keyword searching, file carving, and timeline analysis can be used.

3. **Data Preservation:** This technique involves the proper storage and handling of digital evidence to ensure its integrity and admissibility in a court of law. It is important to follow proper chain of custody procedures and to document all actions taken.

4. **Reporting:** This technique involves the documentation and presentation of the findings of the cyber forensic investigation. It is important to present the information in a clear and concise manner, and to provide supporting evidence for all conclusions drawn.

These are some of the key techniques used in the field of cyber forensics. It is important to have a thorough understanding of these techniques in order to conduct effective cyber forensic investigations.
