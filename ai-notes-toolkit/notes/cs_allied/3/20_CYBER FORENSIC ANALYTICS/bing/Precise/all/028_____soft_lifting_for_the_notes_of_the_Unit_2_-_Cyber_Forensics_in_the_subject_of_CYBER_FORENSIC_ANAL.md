### Soft Lifting

Soft lifting is a term used in the context of software piracy. It refers to the act of purchasing a single copy of software and then downloading it onto more than one computer, despite agreement terms stating the software must only be downloaded once. This often occurs in business or school environments and is usually done to save money. Soft lifting is the most common type of software piracy.

In the context of cyber forensics, soft lifting can be investigated as a form of software piracy. Cyber forensic analysts may use various tools and techniques to gather evidence of soft lifting and present it in a court of law. It is important for businesses and organizations to have proper software licensing management in place to prevent soft lifting and other forms of software piracy.
