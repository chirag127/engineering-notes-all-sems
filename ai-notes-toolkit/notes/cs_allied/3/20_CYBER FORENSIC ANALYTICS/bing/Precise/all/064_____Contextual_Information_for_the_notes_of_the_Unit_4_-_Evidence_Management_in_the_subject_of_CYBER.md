# Contextual Information for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Evidence management is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics.
2. It involves the collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.
3. Digital evidence can include data from various sources such as computer systems, storage devices, and networks.
4. Proper evidence management is essential to ensure the integrity and admissibility of digital evidence in legal proceedings.
5. Chain of custody is an important concept in evidence management, which refers to the chronological documentation of the handling of evidence.
6. It is important to follow standard procedures and guidelines for evidence management to ensure the reliability and validity of the evidence.
7. Failure to properly manage digital evidence can result in the evidence being inadmissible in court or its reliability being questioned.
8. Tools and techniques used in evidence management can include forensic imaging, hashing, and write-blocking.
9. It is important for forensic analysts to stay up-to-date with the latest developments and best practices in evidence management.
