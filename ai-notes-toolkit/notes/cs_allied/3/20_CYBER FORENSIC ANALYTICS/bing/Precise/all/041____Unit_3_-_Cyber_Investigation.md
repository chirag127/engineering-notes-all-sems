## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

1. **Introduction:** Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering digital evidence from electronic devices and networks. It is used to investigate cybercrime, such as hacking, identity theft, and online fraud.

2. **Types of Cybercrime:** There are many types of cybercrime, including hacking, identity theft, online fraud, cyberstalking, and cyberbullying. Each type of cybercrime requires a different approach to investigation.

3. **Digital Evidence:** Digital evidence is any information that is stored or transmitted in digital form and is relevant to a cyber investigation. This can include emails, text messages, social media posts, and other electronic documents.

4. **Investigative Techniques:** There are many techniques used in cyber investigation, including network analysis, malware analysis, and digital forensics. These techniques are used to gather and analyze digital evidence to support an investigation.

5. **Legal Considerations:** Cyber investigation must be conducted in accordance with the law. This includes obtaining the necessary legal permissions, such as search warrants, and following proper procedures for handling and preserving evidence.

6. **Conclusion:** Cyber investigation is a complex and rapidly evolving field. It requires a combination of technical expertise and legal knowledge to effectively investigate and prosecute cybercrime. As technology continues to advance, the techniques and tools used in cyber investigation will continue to evolve.
