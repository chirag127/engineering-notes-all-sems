# Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

### Cyber Investigation

Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering digital evidence from electronic devices and networks. It is used to investigate cybercrimes such as hacking, identity theft, and online fraud.

Some key points to consider when conducting a cyber investigation include:

1. Preservation of evidence: It is important to preserve the integrity of digital evidence by following proper procedures and using appropriate tools.

2. Chain of custody: A chain of custody must be established to document the handling of evidence from the time it is collected until it is presented in court.

3. Analysis of evidence: Digital evidence must be analyzed using specialized tools and techniques to uncover relevant information.

4. Reporting: The results of the investigation must be documented and reported in a clear and concise manner.

Cyber investigation is a complex and challenging field that requires specialized knowledge and skills. It is an essential tool in the fight against cybercrime.
