### Network Components

Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Network Interface Card (NIC):** A hardware component that connects a computer to a network.
2. **Router:** A device that forwards data packets between computer networks.
3. **Switch:** A device that filters and forwards packets between LAN segments.
4. **Hub:** A common connection point for devices in a network.
5. **Modem:** A device that modulates an analog carrier signal to encode digital information, and also demodulates such a carrier signal to decode the transmitted information.
6. **Firewall:** A network security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic based on predetermined security rules.
7. **Wireless Access Point (WAP):** A device that allows wireless devices to connect to a wired network using Wi-Fi or related standards.
8. **Bridge:** A device that connects multiple network segments at the data link layer.
9. **Repeater:** A device that receives a signal and retransmits it at a higher level or higher power.
10. **Gateway:** A device that serves as an entry point from one network to another.

These are some of the key components of a network that are important to understand in the context of cyber forensics. Each component plays a specific role in the functioning of a network and can be a potential point of vulnerability that needs to be secured. Understanding these components and their functions can help in the analysis and investigation of cyber crimes.
