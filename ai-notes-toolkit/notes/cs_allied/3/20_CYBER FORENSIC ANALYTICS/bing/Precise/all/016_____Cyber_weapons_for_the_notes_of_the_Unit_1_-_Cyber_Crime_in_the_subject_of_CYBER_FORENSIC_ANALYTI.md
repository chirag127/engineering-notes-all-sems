# Unit 1 - Cyber Crime: Cyber Weapons

- Cyber weapons are tools and techniques used to attack, disrupt, or defend computer systems and networks.
- These weapons can be used for various purposes, including espionage, sabotage, and warfare.
- Cyber weapons can take many forms, including viruses, worms, Trojan horses, and other types of malware.
- They can also include tools for exploiting vulnerabilities in software and hardware, as well as techniques for social engineering and phishing.
- The use of cyber weapons is often associated with state-sponsored actors, but they can also be used by criminal organizations, hacktivists, and other non-state actors.
- The development and use of cyber weapons is a rapidly evolving field, with new techniques and tools emerging regularly.
- Defending against cyber weapons requires a multi-layered approach, including the use of firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and other security measures, as well as user education and awareness.
- The use of cyber weapons raises many legal and ethical questions, and there is ongoing debate about how to regulate their use and development.
