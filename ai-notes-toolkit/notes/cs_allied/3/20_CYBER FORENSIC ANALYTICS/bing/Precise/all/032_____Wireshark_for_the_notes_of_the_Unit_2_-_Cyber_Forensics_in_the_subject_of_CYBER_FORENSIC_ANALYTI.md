# Wireshark for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Wireshark is a free and very powerful tool, especially in network forensics.
- It provides data single-handedly and is useful in identifying threats in home networks and devices.
- Wireshark is the most widely used network and protocol analyzer and is also an essential tool in the field of network forensics.
- It is an effective open-source tool in the study of network packets and their behavior.
- Wireshark is a network protocol analyzer, or an application that captures packets from a network connection.
- Packet is the name given to a discrete unit of data in a typical Ethernet network.
- Wireshark is the most often-used packet sniffer in the world.
