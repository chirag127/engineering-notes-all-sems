# Concepts of Investigation

Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Identification**: The first step in any investigation is to identify the potential sources of evidence. This includes identifying the devices, systems, and networks that may contain relevant data.

2. **Preservation**: Once the potential sources of evidence have been identified, it is important to preserve the data in a forensically sound manner. This involves creating a forensic image of the data, which is an exact copy of the original data that can be used for analysis.

3. **Collection**: The next step is to collect the data from the identified sources. This involves acquiring the data in a manner that maintains its integrity and authenticity.

4. **Examination**: Once the data has been collected, it is examined to identify relevant evidence. This involves using various forensic tools and techniques to analyze the data and extract relevant information.

5. **Analysis**: The evidence that has been identified is then analyzed to determine its significance and relevance to the investigation. This involves interpreting the data and drawing conclusions based on the evidence.

6. **Presentation**: The final step in the investigation process is to present the findings in a clear and concise manner. This involves preparing a report that summarizes the evidence and the conclusions that have been drawn.

These are the key concepts of investigation in the context of cyber forensic analytics. It is important to follow these steps in a systematic and rigorous manner to ensure that the investigation is conducted in a thorough and effective manner.
