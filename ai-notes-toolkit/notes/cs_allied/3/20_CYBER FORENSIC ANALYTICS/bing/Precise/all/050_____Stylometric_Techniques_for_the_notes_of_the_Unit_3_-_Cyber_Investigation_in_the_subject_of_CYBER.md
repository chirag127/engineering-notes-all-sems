### Stylometric Techniques

Stylometry is the application of the study of linguistic style, usually to written language. It is often used to attribute authorship to anonymous or disputed documents. Stylometry can also be used to identify characteristics of the author, such as gender, age, or nationality.

Some common stylometric techniques used in cyber investigation include:

1. **Word frequency analysis:** This technique involves analyzing the frequency of words used in a document to identify patterns that are unique to a particular author.

2. **Character frequency analysis:** Similar to word frequency analysis, this technique involves analyzing the frequency of characters used in a document to identify patterns that are unique to a particular author.

3. **Vocabulary richness:** This technique involves analyzing the diversity of words used in a document to identify patterns that are unique to a particular author.

4. **Function word analysis:** This technique involves analyzing the use of function words, such as prepositions and conjunctions, to identify patterns that are unique to a particular author.

5. **Sentence length analysis:** This technique involves analyzing the length of sentences in a document to identify patterns that are unique to a particular author.

These are just a few examples of the many stylometric techniques that can be used in cyber investigation. These techniques can be used individually or in combination to help identify the author of a document or to provide other insights into the author's characteristics.
