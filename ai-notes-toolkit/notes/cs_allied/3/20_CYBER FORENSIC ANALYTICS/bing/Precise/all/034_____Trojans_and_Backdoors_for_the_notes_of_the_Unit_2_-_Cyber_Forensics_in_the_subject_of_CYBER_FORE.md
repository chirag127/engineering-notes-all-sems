### Trojans and Backdoors

Trojans and Backdoors are two types of malicious software programs that are used by cyber criminals to gain unauthorized access to a computer system.

- **Trojans** are a type of malware that masquerades as a legitimate program to trick the recipient into running it. Once activated, a trojan can spy on your activities, steal sensitive data, and set up a backdoor for remote access.

- **Backdoors** are a type of Trojan that enables an attacker to gain remote access to a computer and take control of it using a backdoor. Backdoor malware is generally classified as a Trojan.

- The Trojan can make changes in the system or the device of the victim to let other malware or any cyber criminals get into your device or the system.

- In the field of Cyber Forensics, a forensic analysis can be conducted to reveal the presence of a Trojan or Backdoor on a suspect's computer.

It is important to have a good understanding of Trojans and Backdoors in the field of Cyber Forensic Analytics to be able to detect and prevent such attacks.
