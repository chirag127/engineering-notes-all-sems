# Unit 1 - Cyber Crime: Cyber Terrorism

### Definition
- Cyber terrorism is the politically motivated use of computers and information technology to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society .
- It is often defined as any premeditated, politically motivated attack against information systems, programs, and data that threatens violence or results in violence .
- The definition is sometimes expanded to include any cyber attack that intimidates or generates fear in the target population .

### Characteristics
- Cyber terrorism is a controversial term and some authors opt for a very narrow definition, relating to deployment by known terrorist organizations of disruption attacks against information systems for the primary purpose of creating alarm, panic, or physical disruption .
- It is the use of the Internet to conduct violent acts that result in, or threaten, the loss of life or significant bodily harm, in order to achieve political or ideological gains through threat or intimidation .
