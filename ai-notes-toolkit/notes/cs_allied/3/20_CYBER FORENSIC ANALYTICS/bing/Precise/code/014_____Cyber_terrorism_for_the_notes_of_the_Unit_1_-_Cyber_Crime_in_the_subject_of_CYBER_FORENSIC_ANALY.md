### Cyber Terrorism

- Cyber terrorism is the politically motivated use of computers and information technology to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society .
- It is a controversial term and some authors opt for a very narrow definition, relating to deployment by known terrorist organizations of disruption attacks against information systems for the primary purpose of creating alarm, panic, or physical disruption .
- The definition is sometimes expanded to include any cyber attack that intimidates or generates fear in the target population .
- Cyber terrorism can result in, or threaten, the loss of life or significant bodily harm, in order to achieve political or ideological gains through threat or intimidation .
- It is a form of terrorist activities intended to damage or disrupt vital computer systems .
