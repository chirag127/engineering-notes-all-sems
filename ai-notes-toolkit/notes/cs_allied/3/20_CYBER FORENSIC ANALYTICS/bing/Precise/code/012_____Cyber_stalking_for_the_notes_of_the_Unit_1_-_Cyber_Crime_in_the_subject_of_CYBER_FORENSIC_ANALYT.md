### Cyberstalking

Cyberstalking is a form of emotional assault that involves the repeated use of electronic communications to harass or frighten someone. It is a high-tech method of inflicting more pain and is also referred to as “cyberbullying” . Cyberstalking can take many forms, including harassment, embarrassment, and humiliation of the victim . It may include false accusations, defamation, slander, and libel. It may also include monitoring, identity theft, threats, vandalism, solicitation for sex, doxing, or blackmail .

In the United States, the Violence Against Women Act of 2000 placed cyberstalking under the prevue of federal law .

Some key points to remember about cyberstalking are:

- It is a form of emotional assault.
- It involves the repeated use of electronic communications to harass or frighten someone.
- It can take many forms, including harassment, embarrassment, and humiliation of the victim.
- It may include false accusations, defamation, slander, and libel.
- It may also include monitoring, identity theft, threats, vandalism, solicitation for sex, doxing, or blackmail.
- In the United States, it is covered under federal law.
