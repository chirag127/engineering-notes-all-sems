### Trojans and Backdoors

- **Trojans** are malicious programs that are disguised as legitimate software. They are often used to gain unauthorized access to a computer system or to steal sensitive information.
- Trojans can be spread through various means, such as email attachments, malicious downloads, or social engineering attacks.
- Once installed on a system, Trojans can open a **backdoor** that allows an attacker to remotely control the infected computer.
- Backdoors can be used for a variety of purposes, including stealing data, installing additional malware, or launching attacks on other systems.
- To protect against Trojans and backdoors, it is important to keep software and operating systems up to date, use reputable antivirus software, and be cautious when opening email attachments or downloading files from the internet.
- In the context of cyber forensics, it is important to be able to identify and analyze Trojans and backdoors in order to determine the extent of a security breach and to gather evidence for legal proceedings.
