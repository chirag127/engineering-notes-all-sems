### Virus and Worms

Virus and worms are two types of malicious software (malware) that can harm computer systems. They are both self-replicating programs that spread from one computer to another, but they differ in their behavior and the way they spread.

#### Virus
- A virus is a type of malware that attaches itself to a host program or file and spreads by infecting other programs or files on the computer.
- Viruses can cause damage to the computer system by corrupting or deleting files, or by using up system resources, causing the computer to slow down or crash.
- Viruses can spread through various means, such as email attachments, downloads from the internet, or by being transferred from one computer to another via removable media such as USB drives.

#### Worms
- A worm is a type of malware that spreads by exploiting vulnerabilities in computer networks.
- Unlike viruses, worms do not need to attach themselves to a host program or file, and can spread independently.
- Worms can cause damage to the computer system by using up bandwidth and system resources, causing the computer to slow down or crash.
- Worms can spread rapidly through a network, and can infect a large number of computers in a short amount of time.

In conclusion, both viruses and worms are types of malware that can harm computer systems, but they differ in their behavior and the way they spread. It is important to have up-to-date antivirus software and to practice safe computing habits to protect against these threats.
