### DoS for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- DoS stands for Denial of Service.
- It is a type of cyber attack that aims to make a computer or network resource unavailable to its intended users.
- This is typically done by overwhelming the target system with a flood of traffic, causing it to crash or become unresponsive.
- There are several types of DoS attacks, including ping floods, SYN floods, and UDP floods.
- DoS attacks can be carried out by a single individual or a group of attackers, and can be launched from a single computer or a network of compromised machines.
- DoS attacks can have serious consequences for the targeted organization, including loss of revenue, damage to reputation, and loss of customer trust.
- Cyber forensics plays a crucial role in investigating and preventing DoS attacks by identifying the source of the attack and collecting evidence for legal action.
- Preventative measures against DoS attacks include implementing firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and traffic filtering.
