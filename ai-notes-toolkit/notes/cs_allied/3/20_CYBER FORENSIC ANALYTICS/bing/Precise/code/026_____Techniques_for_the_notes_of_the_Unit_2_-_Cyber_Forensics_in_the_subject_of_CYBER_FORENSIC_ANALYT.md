### Techniques for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Acquisition:** This is the first step in the cyber forensic process. It involves the collection of digital evidence from various sources such as hard drives, mobile devices, and cloud storage.

2. **Preservation:** Once the digital evidence has been acquired, it must be preserved in a manner that maintains its integrity and authenticity. This can be achieved through the use of write-blockers and by creating forensic images of the original evidence.

3. **Analysis:** The analysis phase involves the examination of the digital evidence to identify and recover relevant data. This can be done through the use of specialized forensic tools and techniques such as file carving, keyword searching, and timeline analysis.

4. **Presentation:** The final step in the cyber forensic process is the presentation of the findings. This involves the preparation of a report that details the results of the analysis and the conclusions that can be drawn from the evidence.

These are some of the key techniques used in the field of cyber forensics. It is important to note that the specific techniques used may vary depending on the nature of the case and the type of evidence being examined.
