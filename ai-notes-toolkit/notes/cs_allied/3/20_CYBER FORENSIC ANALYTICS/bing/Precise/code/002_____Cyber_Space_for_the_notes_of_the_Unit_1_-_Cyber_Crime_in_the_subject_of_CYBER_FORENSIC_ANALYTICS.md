### Cyber Space

Cyber space refers to the virtual environment where communication over computer networks occurs. It is a domain characterized by the use of electronics and the electromagnetic spectrum to store, modify, and exchange data via networked systems and associated physical infrastructures.

Some key points to consider when studying cyber space in the context of cyber crime and cyber forensic analytics are:

1. Cyber space is a global domain that transcends national borders and is accessible to anyone with an internet connection.
2. The anonymity and vastness of cyber space make it a fertile ground for criminal activities such as hacking, identity theft, and cyber espionage.
3. Law enforcement agencies face significant challenges in investigating and prosecuting cyber crimes due to the transnational nature of cyber space and the difficulty in tracing criminal activities to specific individuals or groups.
4. Cyber forensic analytics is a crucial tool in the fight against cyber crime, as it involves the collection, analysis, and preservation of digital evidence to support investigations and legal proceedings.
5. The field of cyber forensic analytics is constantly evolving to keep pace with the rapid development of technology and the ever-changing tactics of cyber criminals.
