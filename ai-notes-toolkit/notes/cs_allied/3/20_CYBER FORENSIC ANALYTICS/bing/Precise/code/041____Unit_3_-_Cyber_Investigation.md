## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

1. **Introduction:** Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering digital evidence from electronic devices and networks. It is used to investigate cybercrimes, such as hacking, identity theft, and online fraud.

2. **Types of Cybercrimes:** There are many types of cybercrimes, including hacking, identity theft, online fraud, cyberstalking, and cyberbullying. Each type of cybercrime requires a different approach to investigation.

3. **Digital Evidence:** Digital evidence is any information that is stored or transmitted in digital form and is relevant to a cyber investigation. This can include emails, text messages, social media posts, and files stored on a computer or mobile device.

4. **Investigative Techniques:** There are many techniques that can be used in a cyber investigation, including network analysis, malware analysis, and digital forensics. These techniques can help investigators to identify the source of a cyber attack and recover evidence.

5. **Legal Considerations:** Cyber investigations must be conducted in accordance with the law. This includes obtaining the necessary warrants and following proper procedures for collecting and handling evidence.

6. **Conclusion:** Cyber investigation is a complex and rapidly evolving field. It requires a combination of technical expertise and legal knowledge to effectively investigate and prosecute cybercrimes. As technology continues to advance, the importance of cyber investigation will only continue to grow.
