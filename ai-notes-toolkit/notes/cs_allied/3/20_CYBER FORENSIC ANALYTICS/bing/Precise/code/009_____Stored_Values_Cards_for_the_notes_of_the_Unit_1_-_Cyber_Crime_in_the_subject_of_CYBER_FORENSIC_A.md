### Stored Values Cards for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Stored Value Cards (SVCs) are a type of payment card that has a monetary value stored on the card itself.
- SVCs can be used for various purposes, including gift cards, phone cards, and transit cards.
- SVCs can be reloadable or non-reloadable, and can be used at specific merchants or at any merchant that accepts the card brand.
- SVCs can be used anonymously, which makes them attractive for criminal activities such as money laundering and fraud.
- Law enforcement agencies can use forensic techniques to analyze SVCs and track criminal activities.
- SVCs can be seized as evidence in criminal investigations and can provide valuable information about the criminal activities of suspects.
- SVCs can also be used by criminals to store and transfer illicit funds, making them a target for law enforcement agencies.
- The use of SVCs in criminal activities is a growing concern for law enforcement agencies, and efforts are being made to regulate their use and improve their traceability.
