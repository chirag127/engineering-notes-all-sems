### Wireshark

Wireshark is a network protocol analyzer that allows you to see what's happening on your network at a microscopic level. It is commonly used in the field of cyber forensics to analyze network traffic and investigate security incidents.

Here are some key points to remember about Wireshark:

1. Wireshark can capture and display packets in real-time, allowing you to see the data as it is being transmitted over the network.
2. It supports a wide range of protocols, including TCP, UDP, HTTP, and many others.
3. Wireshark has a user-friendly interface that makes it easy to use, even for those without a technical background.
4. It can be used to troubleshoot network issues, identify security threats, and analyze network performance.
5. Wireshark is an open-source tool, meaning that it is free to use and can be modified by anyone.

In summary, Wireshark is a powerful tool for analyzing network traffic and is commonly used in the field of cyber forensics. It is user-friendly, supports a wide range of protocols, and is available for free as an open-source tool.
