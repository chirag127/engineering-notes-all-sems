### Network Investigation

Network investigation is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics and is covered in Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation. It involves the analysis of network traffic and data to identify and investigate potential security incidents or breaches.

Some key points to consider when conducting a network investigation include:

1. Understanding the network architecture and topology: This includes identifying the various devices and systems connected to the network, as well as their roles and functions.

2. Collecting and analyzing network traffic: This involves capturing and analyzing network packets to identify any unusual or suspicious activity.

3. Identifying and investigating potential security incidents: This includes analyzing network logs and other data to identify potential security breaches or incidents.

4. Utilizing specialized tools and techniques: Network investigators may use specialized tools and techniques, such as network sniffers and intrusion detection systems, to aid in their investigations.

5. Collaborating with other teams and departments: Network investigators may need to work closely with other teams and departments, such as IT and legal, to effectively investigate and respond to security incidents.

Overall, network investigation is a critical component of cyber forensic analytics and plays a vital role in identifying and responding to potential security threats. It requires a strong understanding of network architecture and traffic analysis, as well as the ability to utilize specialized tools and techniques to effectively investigate potential incidents.
