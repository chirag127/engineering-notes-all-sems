### Virus and Worms

- **Virus:** A virus is a type of malicious software that is designed to spread from one computer to another by attaching itself to other programs or files. Once it has infected a computer, it can cause damage by deleting or corrupting files, or by using the computer's resources for its own purposes.

- **Worm:** A worm is similar to a virus in that it is a type of malicious software that is designed to spread from one computer to another. However, unlike a virus, a worm does not need to attach itself to other programs or files in order to spread. Instead, it can spread by exploiting vulnerabilities in a computer's operating system or by using social engineering techniques to trick users into downloading and running it.

- **Differences between Virus and Worms:** The main difference between a virus and a worm is the way in which they spread. A virus needs to attach itself to other programs or files in order to spread, while a worm can spread on its own. This means that worms can often spread more quickly and easily than viruses.

- **Prevention:** To prevent infection by viruses and worms, it is important to keep your computer's operating system and software up to date with the latest security patches, to use antivirus software, and to be cautious when downloading files or opening email attachments from unknown sources.

- **Removal:** If your computer is infected with a virus or worm, it is important to remove it as soon as possible to prevent further damage. This can often be done using antivirus software, but in some cases, it may be necessary to seek the help of a computer security professional. It is also important to change any passwords that may have been compromised as a result of the infection.
