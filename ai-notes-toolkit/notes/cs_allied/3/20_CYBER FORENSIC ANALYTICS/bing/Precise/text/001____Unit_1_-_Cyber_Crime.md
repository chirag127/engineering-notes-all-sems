## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

1. **Definition:** Cybercrime refers to criminal activities that are committed using the internet or other forms of digital communication technology.
2. **Types of Cybercrime:** Some common types of cybercrime include hacking, identity theft, phishing, cyberstalking, and online harassment.
3. **Prevention:** To prevent cybercrime, it is important to use strong passwords, keep software and systems up to date, and be cautious when clicking on links or downloading attachments from unknown sources.
4. **Impact:** Cybercrime can have a significant impact on individuals, businesses, and society as a whole. It can result in financial loss, damage to reputation, and loss of personal information.
5. **Legal Consequences:** Many countries have laws in place to combat cybercrime. These laws may include penalties such as fines or imprisonment for those found guilty of committing cybercrime.
