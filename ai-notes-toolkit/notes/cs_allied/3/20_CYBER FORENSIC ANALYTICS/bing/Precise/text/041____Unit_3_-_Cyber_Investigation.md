## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

1. **Introduction:** Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering digital evidence from electronic devices and networks. It is used to investigate cybercrimes such as hacking, identity theft, and online fraud.

2. **Types of Cybercrimes:** Some common types of cybercrimes include hacking, phishing, identity theft, cyberstalking, and online fraud.

3. **Digital Evidence:** Digital evidence is any information that is stored or transmitted in digital form and is relevant to a cyber investigation. This can include emails, text messages, social media posts, and other electronic documents.

4. **Cyber Forensics:** Cyber forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving digital evidence in a manner that is admissible in a court of law. This involves following strict procedures to ensure the integrity of the evidence.

5. **Investigative Techniques:** Some common techniques used in cyber investigations include network analysis, malware analysis, and data recovery.

6. **Legal Considerations:** Cyber investigations must be conducted in accordance with the law. This includes obtaining the necessary warrants and following proper procedures for collecting and handling evidence.

7. **Conclusion:** Cyber investigation is a complex and rapidly evolving field that requires specialized knowledge and skills. It plays a crucial role in the fight against cybercrime and the protection of individuals and organizations from online threats.
