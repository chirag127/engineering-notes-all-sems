### Investigating Computer Intrusions

1. **Identify the type of intrusion**: The first step in investigating a computer intrusion is to identify the type of intrusion that has occurred. This could include malware infections, unauthorized access, or data theft.

2. **Collect evidence**: Once the type of intrusion has been identified, the next step is to collect evidence. This could include system logs, network traffic, and any other relevant data that could help in the investigation.

3. **Analyze the evidence**: After collecting the evidence, it is important to analyze it to determine the cause of the intrusion and to identify any vulnerabilities that may have been exploited.

4. **Contain the intrusion**: Once the cause of the intrusion has been determined, steps should be taken to contain the intrusion and prevent further damage. This could include disconnecting affected systems from the network or applying patches to fix vulnerabilities.

5. **Remediate the intrusion**: After containing the intrusion, steps should be taken to remediate the damage caused by the intrusion. This could include restoring data from backups or repairing any damage to the system.

6. **Prevent future intrusions**: Finally, steps should be taken to prevent future intrusions. This could include implementing stronger security measures, such as firewalls or intrusion detection systems, and educating users on safe computing practices.
