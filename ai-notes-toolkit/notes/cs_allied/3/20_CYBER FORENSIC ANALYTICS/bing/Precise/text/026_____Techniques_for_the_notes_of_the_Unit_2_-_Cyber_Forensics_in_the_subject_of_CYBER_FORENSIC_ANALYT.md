### Techniques for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. **Acquisition:** This technique involves the collection of digital evidence from various sources such as hard drives, mobile devices, and cloud storage. It is important to follow proper procedures to ensure the integrity of the evidence.

2. **Preservation:** Once the evidence is acquired, it is important to preserve it in a manner that prevents tampering or alteration. This can be achieved through the use of write-blockers, checksums, and digital signatures.

3. **Analysis:** The analysis of digital evidence involves the use of various tools and techniques to extract and interpret the data. This can include keyword searches, file carving, and timeline analysis.

4. **Presentation:** The presentation of digital evidence involves the preparation of reports and exhibits that can be used in legal proceedings. It is important to present the evidence in a clear and concise manner that is understandable to non-technical individuals.

5. **Chain of Custody:** Maintaining a proper chain of custody is crucial in ensuring the admissibility of digital evidence in legal proceedings. This involves documenting the handling and transfer of the evidence from the time of acquisition to the time of presentation in court.

These are some of the key techniques used in the field of cyber forensics. It is important to have a thorough understanding of these techniques in order to effectively conduct a cyber forensic investigation.
