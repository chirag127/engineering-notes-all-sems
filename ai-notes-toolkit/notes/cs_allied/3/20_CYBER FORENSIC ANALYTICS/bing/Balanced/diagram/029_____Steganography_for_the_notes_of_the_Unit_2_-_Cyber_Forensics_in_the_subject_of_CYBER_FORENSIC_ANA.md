### Steganography

- Steganography is the art or practice of concealing a message, image, or file within another message, image, or file  .
- Steganography can be used along with cryptography as an extra-secure method in which to protect data.
- Steganography techniques can be applied to various types of media, such as text, image, audio, video, or network traffic .
- The main goal of steganography is to hide the existence of the secret information from human inspection or analysis.
- Steganography can be used for various purposes, such as covert communication, watermarking, authentication, or digital rights management .
- Steganography can be classified into different categories based on the type of cover media, the type of secret information, the embedding method, or the extraction method .
- Some examples of steganography techniques are:
  - Least significant bit (LSB) insertion: replacing the least significant bits of the cover media with the bits of the secret information .
  - Masking and filtering: modifying the cover media in a way that is imperceptible to human vision, such as adding noise or changing the brightness .
  - Transform domain techniques: embedding the secret information in the frequency domain of the cover media, such as using discrete cosine transform (DCT) or discrete wavelet transform (DWT) .
  - Spread spectrum techniques: spreading the secret information across the cover media using a pseudorandom sequence or a secret key .
  - Statistical techniques: exploiting the statistical properties of the cover media to embed the secret information, such as using histogram shifting or pixel value differencing .
  - Distortion techniques: distorting the cover media according to a secret function and measuring the difference between the original and the distorted media to extract the secret information .
  - Steganalysis: the process of detecting the presence of hidden information in a cover media .
- Steganography has been used for centuries, from ancient times to modern times, for various purposes, such as political, military, religious, or personal reasons .
- Steganography has also been associated with some forms of cyber attacks, such as malware delivery, command and control communication, or data exfiltration .
