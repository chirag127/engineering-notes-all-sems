### Evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Evidence management is the process of protecting, preserving, analyzing and presenting digital evidence in a cyber forensic investigation.
- Evidence management involves the following steps :
  - Identification: finding and selecting potential sources of digital evidence related to the cybercrime, such as computers, mobile phones, networks, etc.
  - Collection: acquiring and documenting the digital evidence using proper tools and techniques, such as imaging, hashing, logging, etc.
  - Preservation: storing and safeguarding the digital evidence in a secure and tamper-proof manner, such as using encryption, locking, sealing, etc.
  - Analysis: examining and interpreting the digital evidence using various methods and tools, such as data recovery, data carving, data mining, etc.
  - Presentation: reporting and presenting the digital evidence and the findings of the analysis in a clear and concise manner, such as using charts, graphs, tables, etc.
- Evidence management is important for the security of the cyber forensic analytics because  :
  - It helps to recover, analyze and preserve digital evidence that can be used to prove or disprove the facts of the cybercrime in a court of law.
  - It helps to identify the motive, method and identity of the cybercriminals and their accomplices.
  - It helps to prevent or mitigate the damage caused by the cybercrime and to prevent future attacks.
  - It helps to enhance the credibility and reliability of the cyber forensic investigation and the digital evidence.
  - It helps to comply with the legal and ethical standards and regulations of the cyber forensic domain.
