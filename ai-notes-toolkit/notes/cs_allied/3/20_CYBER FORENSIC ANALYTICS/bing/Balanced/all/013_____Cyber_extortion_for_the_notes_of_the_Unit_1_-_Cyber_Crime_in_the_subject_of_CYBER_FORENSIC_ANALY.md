# Cyber Extortion

- Cyber extortion is an online crime in which hackers hold your data, website, computer systems, or other sensitive information hostage until you meet their demands for payment .
- It often takes the form of ransomware and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, both of which could paralyze your business.
- Ransomware is a type of malware that encrypts your files or locks your devices and demands a ransom for their decryption or release.
- DDoS attacks are a type of cyberattack that floods your network or servers with traffic or requests, overwhelming your resources and preventing legitimate users from accessing your services.
- Cyber extortion can also involve threats to expose or damage your reputation, intellectual property, trade secrets, or customer data if you do not pay the extortionists .
- Cyber extortion is a serious and growing threat to businesses and individuals, as hackers become more sophisticated and demand higher ransoms.
- Cyber extortion can cause significant financial losses, operational disruptions, legal liabilities, and reputational damage to the victims.
- To prevent or mitigate cyber extortion, you should implement the following best practices:
  - Use strong passwords and encryption for your devices and data.
  - Install antivirus software and keep it updated.
  - Backup your data regularly and store it offline or in the cloud.
  - Avoid opening suspicious emails or attachments, or clicking on unknown links.
  - Educate your employees and customers about the risks and signs of cyber extortion.
  - Report any incidents or attempts of cyber extortion to the authorities and your cyber insurance provider.
  - Do not pay the ransom, as it may not guarantee the recovery of your data or the cessation of the attack, and it may encourage further extortion attempts.
