# Cyber Crime

Cyber crime is any illegal activity that involves a computer, a network, or a device connected to the internet. Cyber criminals use various methods and tools to harm individuals, organizations, or governments for their personal, financial, or political gain. Cyber crime can have serious consequences for the victims, such as loss of data, money, reputation, or privacy.

Some of the common types of cyber crime are:

- **Email and internet fraud**: This involves sending deceptive or fraudulent messages to trick the recipients into revealing their personal or financial information, or into paying money for fake products or services. For example, phishing, spoofing, or scam emails.
- **Identity fraud**: This involves stealing and using someone else's personal information, such as name, address, credit card number, or social security number, to commit fraud or other crimes. For example, identity theft, account takeover, or synthetic identity fraud.
- **Theft of financial or card payment data**: This involves stealing and using someone else's financial or card payment data, such as bank account number, debit or credit card number, or PIN, to make unauthorized transactions or withdrawals. For example, skimming, carding, or ATM fraud.
- **Theft and sale of corporate data**: This involves stealing and selling confidential or proprietary data from a company or an organization, such as trade secrets, customer data, or employee data, to competitors or other parties. For example, data breach, data exfiltration, or industrial espionage.
- **Cyberextortion**: This involves demanding money or other benefits from a victim in exchange for not carrying out a threatened cyber attack, or for restoring access to a compromised system or data. For example, ransomware, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack, or sextortion.
- **Cryptojacking**: This involves using someone else's computer, network, or device resources without their consent to mine cryptocurrency, such as Bitcoin or Ethereum. For example, malware, browser-based mining, or cloud mining.
