# Cyber Space

- Cyber space is a term that refers to the virtual environment in which communication over computer networks occurs .
- Cyber space is not a physical location, but rather a conceptual space that exists between different devices and systems that are connected to the Internet or other networks.
- Cyber space can be seen as a global and dynamic domain that is subject to constant change and innovation, and that enables the creation, storage, modification, exchange, sharing, extraction, use, elimination and disruption of information and physical resources .
- Cyber space can also be understood as a cultural phenomenon that reflects the values, norms, practices and identities of the online communities and individuals that inhabit it.
