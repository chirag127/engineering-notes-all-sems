# Cyber terrorism

- Cyber terrorism means the **politically motivated use of computers and information technology to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society**.
- Cyber terrorism can target **vital computer systems** that control infrastructure, communication, transportation, finance, or security.
- Cyber terrorism can result in, or threaten, the **loss of life or significant bodily harm**, as well as **political or ideological gains** through threat or intimidation.
- Cyber terrorism can be carried out by **subnational groups or clandestine agents** who have access to sophisticated hacking tools and techniques.
- Cyber terrorism can pose a serious threat to national and international security, as well as to human rights and civil liberties.
