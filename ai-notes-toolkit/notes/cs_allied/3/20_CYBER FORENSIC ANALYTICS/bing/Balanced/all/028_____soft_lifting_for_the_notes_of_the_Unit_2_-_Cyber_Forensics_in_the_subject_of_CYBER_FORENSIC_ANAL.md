# Soft Lifting

- Soft lifting is the act of purchasing a single copy of software and then downloading it onto more than one computer, despite agreement terms stating the software must only be downloaded once .
- Soft lifting is the most common type of software piracy, which is the unauthorized use or distribution of copyrighted software.
- Soft lifting often occurs in business or school environments and is usually done to save money.
- Soft lifting is also known as softloading or end-user piracy.
- Soft lifting is a violation of the licensing agreement and can have legal consequences for the offenders.
- Soft lifting can also harm the software developers and the software industry by reducing their revenues and incentives to innovate.
- Soft lifting can be prevented by enforcing strict licensing policies, educating users about the risks and ethics of software piracy, and using software protection technologies such as encryption, digital signatures, and activation codes.
