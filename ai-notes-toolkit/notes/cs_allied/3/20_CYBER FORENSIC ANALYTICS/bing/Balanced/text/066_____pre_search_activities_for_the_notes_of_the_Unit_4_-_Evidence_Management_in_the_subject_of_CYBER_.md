### Pre Search Activities for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

- Pre search activities are the steps that cyber forensic experts take before conducting a search for digital evidence in a cyber crime investigation.
- Pre search activities include the following:

  - **Identification**: The first step of cyber forensics experts is to identify what evidence is present, where it is stored, and in which format it is stored. This involves analyzing the nature and scope of the incident, the devices and networks involved, and the potential sources and types of data that may be relevant.
  - **Planning**: The second step of cyber forensics experts is to plan how to conduct the search in a systematic, efficient, and legal manner. This involves defining the objectives and scope of the search, the methods and tools to be used, the personnel and resources required, the legal and ethical issues to be considered, and the documentation and reporting procedures to be followed.
  - **Preparation**: The third step of cyber forensics experts is to prepare for the search by acquiring the necessary authorization, equipment, software, and skills. This involves obtaining search warrants, consent forms, or other legal permissions, securing and testing the hardware and software tools, and training and briefing the team members.
  - **Coordination**: The fourth step of cyber forensics experts is to coordinate with other stakeholders and parties involved in the search, such as law enforcement, lawyers, clients, or other experts. This involves communicating the goals and expectations of the search, establishing the roles and responsibilities of each party, and resolving any conflicts or issues that may arise.

- Pre search activities are important for ensuring the success and validity of the search, as they help to:

  - **Define the scope and objectives of the search**: Pre search activities help to narrow down the focus and direction of the search, and to avoid wasting time and resources on irrelevant or redundant data.
  - **Ensure the legality and ethics of the search**: Pre search activities help to comply with the applicable laws and regulations, and to respect the privacy and rights of the data owners and subjects.
  - **Ensure the reliability and integrity of the evidence**: Pre search activities help to preserve and protect the original state and condition of the data, and to prevent any alteration, damage, or loss of the evidence.
  - **Ensure the efficiency and effectiveness of the search**: Pre search activities help to optimize the use of the available tools and techniques, and to reduce the risks and errors that may occur during the search.
