### Wireshark for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Wireshark is a free and open-source network protocol analyzer that can capture and examine packets from various network interfaces .
- Wireshark can be used for network forensics, which is the process of analyzing network traffic and packets to identify and investigate malicious activities, such as cyberattacks, malware infections, data breaches, etc   .
- Wireshark can help network forensic investigators to:
  - Monitor and troubleshoot network performance and functionality .
  - Filter and search for specific packets based on various criteria, such as source and destination IP addresses, protocols, ports, etc .
  - Decode and dissect packets to reveal their contents and details, such as headers, payloads, checksums, etc .
  - Reconstruct and follow the flow of network sessions, such as TCP streams, HTTP requests, etc .
  - Identify and isolate anomalous or suspicious packets that may indicate network attacks, such as denial-of-service, man-in-the-middle, spoofing, etc   .
  - Extract and analyze evidence from packets, such as files, images, passwords, etc   .
- Wireshark has a graphical user interface (GUI) that displays packets in a tabular format, as well as a command-line interface (CLI) that allows for scripting and automation .
- Wireshark supports hundreds of network protocols and can be extended with plugins and custom dissectors .
- Wireshark is widely used by network professionals, security analysts, forensic examiners, and researchers, as well as by hackers and cybercriminals    .
- Wireshark requires sufficient permissions and knowledge to capture and analyze packets, and may pose some ethical and legal challenges, such as privacy, confidentiality, and consent    .
