### Evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Evidence management is the process of protecting, preserving, and documenting the digital evidence that is collected, examined, and presented in cyber forensics investigations.
- Digital evidence is information stored or transmitted in binary form that may be relied on in court. It can be found on various electronic devices, such as computers, mobile phones, smart devices, etc.
- Evidence management is a critical facet of the criminal justice system, as it ensures the integrity, reliability, and admissibility of the digital evidence in legal proceedings.
- Evidence management involves the following steps:
  - Identification: locating and identifying the potential sources and types of digital evidence relevant to the case.
  - Collection: acquiring the digital evidence using appropriate tools and techniques, such as imaging, hashing, logging, etc.
  - Preservation: securing and storing the digital evidence in a safe and tamper-proof manner, such as using write blockers, encryption, seals, etc.
  - Analysis: examining and interpreting the digital evidence using specialized software and methods, such as data carving, file recovery, malware analysis, etc.
  - Presentation: reporting and presenting the digital evidence and the findings in a clear and concise manner, such as using charts, graphs, tables, etc.
  - Disposition: disposing or returning the digital evidence according to the legal and ethical requirements, such as deleting, destroying, or archiving the data.
- Evidence management requires following the best practices and standards, such as the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) guidelines, the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) standards, the Association of Chief Police Officers (ACPO) principles, etc .
- Evidence management also requires maintaining the chain of custody, which is the documentation of the history and movement of the digital evidence, such as who handled it, when, where, how, and why.
