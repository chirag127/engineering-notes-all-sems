### Cyber Investigation

- Cyber investigation is the process of investigating, analyzing, and recovering forensic data for digital evidence of a crime.
- Cyber investigation can be used to track criminals via the computer, either by investigating computer crimes or by using computer forensics to access records of criminals .
- Cyber investigation can also be used to impose risk and consequences on cyber adversaries, such as hackers, cyber terrorists, cyber spies, and cyber thieves, who compromise the security and integrity of U.S. networks and data.
- Cyber investigation involves the following steps:
  - Identifying the type and scope of the cyber crime, such as hacking, phishing, malware, denial-of-service, extortion, identity theft, fraud, etc.
  - Collecting and preserving digital evidence from various sources, such as computers, mobile devices, network logs, cloud services, social media, etc.
  - Analyzing and interpreting the digital evidence using various tools and techniques, such as encryption, decryption, hashing, data carving, file recovery, etc.
  - Reporting and presenting the findings and conclusions of the cyber investigation to the relevant authorities, such as law enforcement, courts, or clients.
  - Following the legal and ethical standards and procedures for cyber investigation, such as chain of custody, privacy, confidentiality, etc.
