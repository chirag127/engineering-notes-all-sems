### Real evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Real evidence is any physical object that can be directly observed or examined by the court or the jury .
- Real evidence can be used to prove or disprove a fact or a connection between a suspect and a crime .
- Real evidence can also be used to corroborate or contradict other types of evidence, such as testimonial or documentary evidence .
- Examples of real evidence in cyber forensics are:
  - The actual computer, hard drive, mobile phone, or other device that contains digital evidence  .
  - The fingerprints, DNA, or other biometric data that can be extracted from the device or its peripherals.
  - The physical location, condition, or appearance of the device or its components .
  - The cables, connectors, adapters, or other accessories that can be used to connect the device to a network or another device .
- Real evidence must be properly collected, preserved, analyzed, and presented in cyber forensics investigations  .
- The collection of real evidence involves identifying, locating, and seizing the device or its parts, as well as documenting the process and the chain of custody  .
- The preservation of real evidence involves protecting the device or its parts from damage, alteration, or loss, as well as storing them in a secure and controlled environment  .
- The analysis of real evidence involves extracting, interpreting, and verifying the digital data from the device or its parts, as well as identifying any relevant information or patterns  .
- The presentation of real evidence involves preparing, organizing, and displaying the device or its parts, as well as the digital data and the analysis results, in a clear and convincing manner  .
