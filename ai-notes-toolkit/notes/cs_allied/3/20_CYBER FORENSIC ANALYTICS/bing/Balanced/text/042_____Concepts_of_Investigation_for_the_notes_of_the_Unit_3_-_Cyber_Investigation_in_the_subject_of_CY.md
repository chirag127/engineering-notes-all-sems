### Concepts of Investigation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Cyber forensics is a process of extracting data as proof for a crime (that involves electronic devices) while following proper investigation rules to nab the culprit by presenting the evidence to the court.
- Cyber forensics is needed for the investigation of crime and law enforcement. There are cases like hacking and denial of service (DOS) attacks where the computer system is the crime scene. The proof of the crime will be present in the computer system.
- Cyber forensics follows certain procedures to find the evidence to reach conclusions after proper investigation of matters. The procedures that cyber forensic experts follow are:
  - Identification: The first step of cyber forensics experts are to identify what evidence is present, where it is stored, and in which format it is stored.
  - Preservation: The second step is to preserve the evidence in a way that it does not get altered, damaged, or lost. This may involve making copies, securing the original device, or using write blockers.
  - Analysis: The third step is to analyze the evidence using various tools and techniques to extract relevant information and interpret the data. This may involve reverse steganography, decryption, data carving, or timeline analysis.
  - Presentation: The fourth step is to present the findings and conclusions in a clear and concise manner that can be understood by the court or the client. This may involve writing reports, creating charts, or giving testimony.
  - Review: The final step is to review the entire process and evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of the investigation. This may involve identifying the strengths and weaknesses, the lessons learned, and the recommendations for improvement.
- Cyber forensics requires certain skills and advantages to perform the investigation successfully. Some of the skills and advantages are:
  - Technical skills: Cyber forensic experts need to have a strong knowledge of computer systems, networks, operating systems, software, and hardware. They also need to be familiar with various forensic tools and methods to collect and analyze the evidence.
  - Analytical skills: Cyber forensic experts need to have a keen eye for detail and a logical mind to interpret the data and find the hidden patterns and connections. They also need to be able to solve complex problems and think creatively.
  - Communication skills: Cyber forensic experts need to be able to communicate effectively with different audiences, such as law enforcement, lawyers, judges, or clients. They also need to be able to write clear and concise reports and give convincing testimony.
  - Ethical skills: Cyber forensic experts need to follow the ethical principles and standards of the profession, such as integrity, confidentiality, impartiality, and accuracy. They also need to respect the privacy and rights of the individuals and organizations involved in the investigation.
  - Career opportunities: Cyber forensic experts have a high demand and a wide range of career options in various sectors, such as government, law enforcement, private companies, or consulting firms. They also have a rewarding and challenging job that involves solving real-world problems and making a difference in society.
