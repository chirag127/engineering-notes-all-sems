### Jurisprudential Inconsistency

- Jurisprudential inconsistency refers to the lack of coherence or harmony in the application of legal principles and rules to different cases of cybercrime and digital forensics.
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can arise from various sources, such as:
  - The non-local and transnational nature of cybercrime, which challenges the traditional notions of territorial jurisdiction and sovereignty .
  - The intangible and volatile nature of digital evidence, which requires special methods of preservation, collection, analysis and presentation .
  - The evolving and complex nature of cyber technologies and digital objects, which may not fit into the existing legal categories and definitions.
  - The diversity and disparity of legal systems and standards across different countries and regions, which may create conflicts of law and gaps in legal protection.
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can have negative impacts on the effectiveness and legitimacy of cybercrime prevention and prosecution, such as:
  - Reducing the admissibility and reliability of digital evidence in court, which may undermine the rights of the victims and the accused .
  - Creating legal uncertainty and ambiguity for the law enforcement agencies, the digital forensic experts and the judicial authorities, which may hamper the investigation and adjudication process .
  - Eroding the public trust and confidence in the rule of law and the justice system, which may encourage more cybercrime and cyber-attacks .
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can be addressed by various measures, such as:
  - Developing and harmonizing the legal frameworks and standards for cybercrime and digital forensics at the national and international levels, which may enhance the legal certainty and consistency .
  - Adopting and implementing the best practices and guidelines for digital evidence management and digital forensic investigations, which may improve the quality and integrity of digital evidence .
  - Promoting and facilitating the cooperation and coordination among the relevant stakeholders, such as the law enforcement agencies, the digital forensic experts, the judicial authorities and the private sector, which may increase the efficiency and effectiveness of cybercrime response .
