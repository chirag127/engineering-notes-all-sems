### Jurisprudential Inconsistency for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Jurisprudential inconsistency refers to the lack of uniformity or coherence in the legal system or the legal theory that deals with cyber crime and digital forensics.
- Cyber crime challenges the traditional notions of crime and jurisdiction because it is non-local in nature, which throws law enforcement into confusion regarding who should investigate and prosecute the criminal.
- Digital forensics investigations jurisprudence is the theory and philosophy of the study of law and the principles upon which a law is based. For digital evidence to appear at court and be legally admissible, the evidence must be authentic, accurate, complete, and convincing to the jury.
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can arise from various sources, such as:
  - Different legal systems or jurisdictions that have different laws, rules, or standards for cyber crime and digital evidence.
  - Inconsistencies or loopholes in the forensic investigations that contradict or compromise the value or admissibility of the evidence .
  - Evolving nature of cyber technology and digital objects that pose new challenges or questions for the legal system and the legal theory.
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can have negative impacts on the justice system, such as:
  - Undermining the credibility or reliability of the digital evidence or the forensic experts .
  - Creating confusion or uncertainty for the judges, lawyers, or jurors who have to deal with complex or unfamiliar cyber issues .
  - Hindering the cooperation or coordination among different law enforcement agencies or countries that have to work together to combat cyber crime.
- Jurisprudential inconsistency can be addressed or reduced by various measures, such as:
  - Developing or harmonizing the legal frameworks or standards for cyber crime and digital evidence across different jurisdictions or countries.
  - Enhancing the quality or consistency of the forensic investigations or the forensic tools or methods .
  - Educating or training the legal professionals or the public about the cyber issues or the digital evidence .
