### Evidence collection for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Evidence collection is the process of identifying, preserving, and acquiring digital data that can be used in cyber forensics investigations or legal proceedings .
- Evidence collection involves following the principles of digital forensics, such as:
  - Adhering to the legal and ethical standards of the jurisdiction and the organization .
  - Maintaining the integrity and authenticity of the evidence by using proper tools and techniques, such as hashing, encryption, and write protection  .
  - Documenting the evidence collection process and the chain of custody, such as using forms, logs, labels, and reports  .
  - Minimizing the impact of the evidence collection on the original system or device, such as using live or dead forensics methods, depending on the situation  .
- Evidence collection can be performed on various types of digital devices and media, such as computers, mobile phones, networks, cloud services, IoT devices, etc    .
- Evidence collection can be challenging due to the large volume, variety, and volatility of digital data, as well as the potential encryption, obfuscation, or deletion of the data by the suspects or the system    .
- Evidence collection requires proper planning, preparation, and execution, such as:
  - Defining the scope, objectives, and legal authority of the evidence collection   .
  - Identifying the sources, locations, and types of potential evidence   .
  - Selecting the appropriate tools, techniques, and personnel for the evidence collection   .
  - Securing and isolating the scene and the devices to prevent tampering or contamination   .
  - Acquiring the evidence using forensically sound methods, such as imaging, copying, or seizing   .
  - Transporting and storing the evidence in a safe and secure manner   .
- Evidence collection is an essential step in the digital forensics process, as it provides the basis for the subsequent analysis, interpretation, and presentation of the evidence   .
