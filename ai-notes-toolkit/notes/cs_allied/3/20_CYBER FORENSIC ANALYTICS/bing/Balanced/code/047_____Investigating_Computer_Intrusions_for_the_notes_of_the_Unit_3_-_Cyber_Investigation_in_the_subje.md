### Investigating Computer Intrusions

- A computer intrusion is an unauthorized access to a computer system or network that violates the security policies or standard practices of the system or network owner.
- A computer intrusion investigation is a process of identifying, analyzing, and responding to the incidents of computer intrusions, which may involve criminal, civil, or administrative actions.
- A computer intrusion investigation may involve the following steps  :
  - **Notification**: The investigator should notify the appropriate authorities and stakeholders of the intrusion, such as the security division of the local telephone company, the system administrator, the network service provider, the law enforcement agencies, and the legal counsel.
  - **Preservation**: The investigator should preserve the evidence of the intrusion, such as the system logs, network traffic, user accounts, files, and malware. The investigator should also document the steps taken to preserve the evidence, such as the date, time, location, and method of collection.
  - **Identification**: The investigator should identify the source and scope of the intrusion, such as the IP address, hostname, username, password, and access level of the intruder, the entry point, the duration, the frequency, and the impact of the intrusion.
  - **Analysis**: The investigator should analyze the evidence of the intrusion, such as the tools, techniques, and motives of the intruder, the vulnerabilities and weaknesses of the system or network, the damage and loss caused by the intrusion, and the indicators and patterns of the intrusion.
  - **Response**: The investigator should respond to the intrusion, such as by containing, eradicating, and recovering from the intrusion, by reporting, sharing, and disseminating the findings and recommendations of the investigation, by prosecuting, suing, or sanctioning the intruder, and by preventing, detecting, and mitigating future intrusions.
- A computer intrusion investigation may require the collaboration and coordination of various entities, such as the system or network owner, the system administrator, the network service provider, the law enforcement agencies, the legal counsel, the forensic experts, and the intelligence analysts  .
- A computer intrusion investigation may face various challenges and limitations, such as the complexity and diversity of the computer systems and networks, the volatility and fragility of the digital evidence, the anonymity and mobility of the intruders, the jurisdictional and legal issues, and the ethical and privacy concerns.
