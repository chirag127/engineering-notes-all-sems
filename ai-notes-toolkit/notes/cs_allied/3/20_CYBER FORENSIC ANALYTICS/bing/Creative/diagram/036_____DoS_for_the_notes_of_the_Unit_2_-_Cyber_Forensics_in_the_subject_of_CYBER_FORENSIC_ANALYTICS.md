### DoS for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics

- DoS stands for Denial-of-Service, which is a type of cyberattack that aims to prevent authorized users from accessing their personal data or files on a computer or network.
- DoS attacks can be launched by a single or group of computers, often using malicious software or scripts to overload the target system with requests or packets.
- DoS attacks can cause various impacts, such as slowing down the system, crashing the server, consuming bandwidth, exhausting resources, or disrupting services.
- DoS attacks can be classified into different types, such as volumetric, protocol, application layer, distributed, or amplified.
- Cyber forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting digital evidence related to cybercrimes, such as hacking and DoS attacks.
- Cyber forensics is needed to identify the attacker, distinguish legitimate traffic from malicious traffic, and employ mitigation techniques to block or stop the attack .
- Cyber forensics involves various steps, such as identification, preservation, extraction, analysis, and presentation of digital evidence.
- Cyber forensics requires various skills and tools, such as knowledge of operating systems, networks, protocols, encryption, malware, and software or hardware tools for data acquisition, analysis, and reporting.
- Cyber forensics has various advantages, such as enhancing security, deterring cybercriminals, supporting legal actions, and improving incident response.
- Cyber forensics has various challenges, such as encryption, anonymity, volatility, diversity, and legality of digital evidence.
- Cyber forensics is a growing and rewarding career field, with various roles and opportunities, such as cyber forensic analyst, investigator, consultant, or trainer.
