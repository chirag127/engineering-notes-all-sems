### Criminal Behaviour

- Criminal behaviour is any kind of antisocial behaviour that is punishable by law or norms stated by the community .
- Criminal behaviour can be committed by act or omission, and can involve harm to persons, property, or society .
- Criminal behaviour can be classified into different types, such as violent crimes, property crimes, white-collar crimes, cyber crimes, etc.
- Cyber crime is a type of criminal behaviour that involves the use of computers, networks, or digital devices to commit or facilitate illegal activities.
- Cyber crime can include hacking, identity theft, phishing, cyberstalking, cyberbullying, online fraud, cyberterrorism, etc.
- Cyber crime can have various impacts on individuals, organizations, and society, such as financial losses, psychological distress, reputational damage, security breaches, etc.
- Cyber crime can be prevented or reduced by using various measures, such as encryption, antivirus software, firewalls, passwords, awareness, education, etc.
- Cyber crime can be investigated and prosecuted by using various methods, such as digital forensics, cyber law, cyber ethics, etc.
