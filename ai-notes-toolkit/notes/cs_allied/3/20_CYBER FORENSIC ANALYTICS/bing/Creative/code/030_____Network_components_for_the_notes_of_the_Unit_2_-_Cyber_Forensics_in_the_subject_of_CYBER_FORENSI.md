### Network components for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Network components are the devices, systems, and software that enable the transmission and analysis of data over a network.
- Network components can be classified into three categories: network infrastructure, network security, and network forensics.
- Network infrastructure refers to the physical and logical elements that provide the foundation for network communication, such as routers, switches, cables, protocols, and standards.
- Network security refers to the policies, procedures, and tools that protect the network from unauthorized access, misuse, modification, or disruption, such as firewalls, antivirus, encryption, and authentication.
- Network forensics refers to the capture, recording, and analysis of network traffic and events in order to determine the source, nature, and impact of cyberattacks, such as packet sniffers, network analyzers, and forensic software.
- Network components play a vital role in cyber forensics, as they can provide valuable evidence and insights into the activities and behaviors of cybercriminals, victims, and witnesses.
- Network components can also be used to prevent, detect, and respond to cyberattacks, by implementing security measures, monitoring network activity, and performing forensic investigations.
- Network components can be challenged by various factors, such as the complexity, diversity, and volatility of network environments, the sophistication, stealth, and persistence of cyberattacks, and the legal, ethical, and technical issues of network forensics.
