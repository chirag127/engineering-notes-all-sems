### Botnets

- A botnet is a network of compromised computers (bots) that are controlled by an attacker (botmaster) remotely.
- Botnets can be used for various malicious purposes, such as spamming, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, stealing sensitive information, or hosting illegal content.
- Botnet forensics is the science that determines the scope of the breach and applies the methodology to find out the type of the infection .
- Botnet forensics involves a collection of activities, such as collection, identification, detection, acquisition, and attribution of botnet evidence from digital sources .
- Botnet forensics can be performed at different levels, such as malware analysis, network analysis, or host analysis .
- Botnet forensics can use various tools and techniques, such as network security tools, malware analysis tools, machine learning methods, or digital forensic tools   .
- Botnet forensics can help uncover facts related to the cybercrimes specifically on the botnet, such as the botnet architecture, protocol, life cycle, command and control (C&C) server, infection vector, or attack target .
- Botnet forensics can also help identify the botmaster, the botnet size, the botnet activity, or the botnet impact .
- Botnet forensics can provide valuable information for law enforcement, security analysts, or network administrators to prevent, detect, or mitigate botnet attacks .
- Botnet forensics can face various challenges, such as encryption, obfuscation, polymorphism, or evasion techniques used by botnets to avoid detection or analysis .
- Botnet forensics can also be affected by the dynamic and heterogeneous nature of botnets, the large-scale and distributed nature of botnet evidence, or the legal and ethical issues related to botnet investigation .
- Botnet forensics can benefit from the collaboration and coordination of different stakeholders, such as researchers, practitioners, or organizations, to share data, tools, or best practices .
- Botnet forensics can learn from the analysis of some of the biggest botnet attacks to date, such as Conficker, Mirai, Storm, or Zeus.
