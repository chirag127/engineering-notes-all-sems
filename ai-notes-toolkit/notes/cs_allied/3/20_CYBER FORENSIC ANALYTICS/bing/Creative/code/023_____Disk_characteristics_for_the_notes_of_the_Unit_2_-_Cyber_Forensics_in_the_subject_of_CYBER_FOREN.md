### Disk characteristics for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Disk forensics is a sub-branch of cyber forensics that deals with extracting data from storage media such as hard drives, USB devices, flash drives, etc.   
- Disk forensics can retrieve not only active information and deleted data, but also data from unallocated space and slack space for the purpose of investigation. 
- Disk forensics can help to find digital evidence that is stored or transmitted in binary form that may be relied on in court. 
- Disk forensics can help to identify the user's documents, emails, internet history, and any downloaded illicit images that may be relevant to the case. 
- Disk forensics requires the use of disk imaging tools that can create an exact copy of the original disk without altering or damaging it. 
- Disk forensics faces some challenges such as encryption, fragmentation, large volumes of data, and anti-forensic techniques that can hinder the analysis.
