### Physical evidence

Physical evidence is any tangible object or material that can be used to prove or disprove a fact or hypothesis in a cyber forensic investigation. Physical evidence can be collected from various sources, such as computers, mobile devices, removable media, cables, publications, trash, etc. Physical evidence can contain digital evidence, which is information stored or transmitted in binary form that may be relevant to the case .

Some points to note about physical evidence are:

- Physical evidence requires seizing the equipment used by the suspect. Proper authorization should be requested to legally access the equipment.
- Physical evidence should be handled carefully to avoid contamination, damage, or loss. Proper chain of custody should be maintained to document the collection, storage, and transfer of the evidence .
- Physical evidence should be analyzed using appropriate tools and techniques to extract and interpret the digital evidence. The analysis should be conducted in a forensically sound manner, following the principles of integrity, authenticity, and reliability .
- Physical evidence should be presented in a clear and concise manner, using visual aids, reports, and testimony. The evidence should be relevant, reliable, and admissible in court .
