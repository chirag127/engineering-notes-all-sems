## Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics

- Cyber forensics is the application of scientific methods and techniques to collect, preserve, analyze, and present digital evidence in a court of law.
- Cyber forensics can be used to investigate various types of cybercrimes, such as hacking, phishing, malware, identity theft, fraud, cyberstalking, cyberbullying, etc.
- Cyber forensics involves four main phases: acquisition, examination, analysis, and reporting.
  - Acquisition: The process of obtaining digital evidence from various sources, such as computers, mobile devices, networks, cloud services, etc. The evidence must be collected in a forensically sound manner, which means preserving its integrity, authenticity, and chain of custody.
  - Examination: The process of extracting relevant data from the digital evidence using various tools and techniques, such as hashing, imaging, carving, decryption, etc. The data must be verified and validated to ensure its accuracy and reliability.
  - Analysis: The process of interpreting and evaluating the data to identify patterns, anomalies, connections, and implications. The analysis must be objective, logical, and consistent with the facts and circumstances of the case.
  - Reporting: The process of presenting the findings and conclusions of the analysis in a clear, concise, and convincing manner. The report must be written in a professional and ethical manner, following the standards and guidelines of the relevant jurisdiction and organization.
- Cyber forensics requires various skills and knowledge, such as:
  - Technical skills: The ability to use various hardware and software tools and techniques to collect, examine, analyze, and report digital evidence.
  - Legal skills: The knowledge of the laws, regulations, policies, and procedures that govern cyber forensics and cybercrime investigations.
  - Communication skills: The ability to communicate effectively with various stakeholders, such as law enforcement, lawyers, judges, clients, etc.
  - Critical thinking skills: The ability to apply logic, reasoning, and creativity to solve problems and make decisions in cyber forensics.
  - Ethical skills: The awareness of the ethical principles and codes of conduct that guide cyber forensics and cybercrime investigations.
