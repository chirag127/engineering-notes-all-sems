### Cyber terrorism

- Cyber terrorism means the **politically motivated use of computers and information technology to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society**.
- Cyber terrorism can target **vital computer systems** that control critical infrastructure, such as power grids, transportation networks, communication systems, etc.
- Cyber terrorism can also aim to **damage or disrupt** the online services and platforms that people rely on, such as banking, e-commerce, social media, etc.
- Cyber terrorism can result in, or threaten, the **loss of life or significant bodily harm**, as well as economic, social, and psychological impacts.
- Cyber terrorism can be carried out by **subnational groups or clandestine agents** who have ideological, religious, or political agendas .
- Cyber terrorism can be difficult to **prevent, detect, and attribute**, as the attackers can use various techniques to hide their identities, locations, and motives.
- Cyber terrorism can pose a **serious threat** to national and international security, as well as human rights and freedoms.
