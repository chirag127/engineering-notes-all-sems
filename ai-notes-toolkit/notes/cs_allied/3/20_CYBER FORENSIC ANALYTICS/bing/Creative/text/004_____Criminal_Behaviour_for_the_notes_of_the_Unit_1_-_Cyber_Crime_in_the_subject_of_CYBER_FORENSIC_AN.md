### Criminal Behaviour

- Criminal behaviour is the conduct that violates the legal norms and rules of a society, and that is subject to legal sanctions and penalties.
- Criminal behaviour can be classified into different types, such as violent crimes, property crimes, white-collar crimes, organized crimes, cyber crimes, etc.
- Cyber crime is a type of criminal behaviour that involves the use of computers or the internet to commit illegal acts, such as fraud, identity theft, hacking, cyberstalking, cyberbullying, child pornography, etc.
- Cyber crime can be categorized into two main types: cyber-dependent crimes and cyber-enabled crimes.
  - Cyber-dependent crimes are crimes that can only be committed using computers or the internet, such as hacking, denial-of-service attacks, malware, etc.
  - Cyber-enabled crimes are crimes that can be committed without computers or the internet, but are facilitated or enhanced by them, such as fraud, identity theft, online gambling, etc.
- Cyber crime poses various challenges and threats to individuals, organizations, and governments, such as financial losses, reputational damage, privacy breaches, security risks, etc.
- Cyber crime also raises various legal and ethical issues, such as jurisdiction, evidence, investigation, prosecution, prevention, etc.
