### Concepts of Investigation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Cyber forensics is a field that follows certain procedures to find the evidence to reach conclusions after proper investigation of matters.
- Cyber forensics is also known as computer forensics or digital forensics .
- Cyber forensics is needed for the investigation of crime and law enforcement, especially when the computer system is the crime scene or the source of evidence.
- Cyber forensics involves the following steps :
  - Identification: The first step of cyber forensics experts is to identify what evidence is present, where it is stored, and in which format it is stored.
  - Preservation: The second step is to preserve the evidence in a way that prevents any alteration, loss, or damage. This may involve making copies, securing the original device, or using write blockers.
  - Analysis: The third step is to analyze the evidence using various tools and techniques to extract relevant information and interpret the data. This may involve reverse steganography, file hashing, keyword lists, or grep.
  - Presentation: The fourth step is to present the findings and conclusions in a clear and concise manner, using reports, charts, graphs, or testimony. The presentation should follow the legal and ethical standards and be admissible in court.
- Cyber forensics requires certain skills and advantages, such as:
  - Technical skills: Cyber forensics experts need to have a good knowledge of computer hardware, software, networks, operating systems, and encryption methods.
  - Analytical skills: Cyber forensics experts need to have a keen eye for detail, a logical mind, and a problem-solving attitude. They need to be able to identify patterns, anomalies, and hidden data.
  - Communication skills: Cyber forensics experts need to be able to communicate effectively with various stakeholders, such as law enforcement, lawyers, judges, and clients. They need to be able to explain complex technical concepts in simple terms and write clear and accurate reports.
  - Ethical skills: Cyber forensics experts need to follow the code of conduct and the professional standards of their field. They need to respect the privacy and confidentiality of the data and the parties involved. They need to be impartial, honest, and objective in their work.
- Cyber forensics is a challenging and rewarding career that offers various opportunities in different sectors, such as government, law enforcement, corporate, education, or research.
