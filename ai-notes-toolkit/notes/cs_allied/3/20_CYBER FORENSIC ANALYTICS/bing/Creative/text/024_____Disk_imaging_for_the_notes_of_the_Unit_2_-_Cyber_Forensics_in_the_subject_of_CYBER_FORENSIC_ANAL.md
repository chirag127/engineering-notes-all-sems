### Disk imaging for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Disk imaging is the process of creating an electronic copy of a drive (e.g. a hard drive, USB, etc.) that preserves all the data and metadata in a forensically sound manner .
- Disk imaging is a crucial step in cyber forensics, as it allows the examiner to analyze the contents of the drive without altering or damaging the original evidence .
- Disk imaging can be performed using various tools, such as hardware or software imagers, that can read the drive bit-by-bit and write the data to a forensics image format, such as E01, AFF, or RAW  .
- Disk imaging tools should be able to handle different types of drives, such as IDE, SATA, SCSI, USB, etc., and different file systems, such as FAT, NTFS, EXT, etc .
- Disk imaging tools should also be able to verify the integrity of the image by calculating and comparing hash values, such as MD5, SHA-1, or SHA-256, of the source and the destination  .
- Disk imaging tools should be tested and validated by independent organizations, such as NIST, to ensure their reliability and accuracy.
- Disk imaging tools should be used in accordance with the legal and ethical standards of cyber forensics, such as obtaining proper authorization, documenting the process, and preserving the chain of custody .
