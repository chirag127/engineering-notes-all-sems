### Stylometric Techniques for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Stylometry is the statistical analysis of the linguistic features of a text, such as word choice, sentence structure, punctuation, etc., to identify the unique writing style of an author.
- Stylometry can be used for various applications, such as literary studies, historical studies, social studies, information retrieval, and forensic cases and studies.
- In cyber forensics, stylometry can be used to attribute the authorship of digital texts, such as e-mails, blogs, social media posts, etc., by comparing them with known samples of the suspected authors    .
- Stylometry can also be used to detect plagiarism, by identifying changes in writing style within a document that indicate the use of different sources .
- Stylometry techniques can be divided into two categories: feature-based and machine learning-based.
  - Feature-based techniques extract various linguistic features from the text, such as lexical, syntactic, semantic, content-based, and idiosyncratic features, and use them to measure the similarity or difference between texts .
  - Machine learning-based techniques use algorithms to learn the patterns and characteristics of different authors from a large corpus of texts, and use them to classify or cluster new texts according to their authorship.
- Some examples of stylometric tools are:
  - JGAAP: Java Graphical Authorship Attribution Program, which is a software package that allows users to perform feature-based stylometric analysis on various types of texts.
  - Stylo: An R package that provides various functions and methods for stylometric analysis, such as feature extraction, distance measures, clustering, classification, etc.
  - Writeprints: A stylometric approach that collects lexical, content-based, and idiosyncratic features as the baseline, and uses machine learning techniques to identify and compare the writing styles of different authors.
