### CERNTin for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- CERNTin is an acronym for **Cybersecurity Education and Research Network**. It is a platform that provides online courses, webinars, workshops, and certifications on various topics related to cybersecurity, cyber law, and cyber forensics.
- CERNTin aims to promote awareness, education, and research on the emerging challenges and opportunities in the field of cybersecurity, cyber law, and cyber forensics. It also facilitates collaboration and networking among students, professionals, academics, and experts from different sectors and regions.
- CERNTin offers courses on cyber law that cover the following topics:
  - Introduction to cyber law and its scope, objectives, and principles
  - Cybercrime and cyber terrorism: types, causes, effects, and prevention
  - Cybersecurity laws and regulations in the U.S. and other countries: overview, comparison, and analysis
  - Cybersecurity governance and compliance: roles, responsibilities, and best practices of various stakeholders
  - Cybersecurity litigation and insurance: legal issues, challenges, and solutions
  - Cybersecurity ethics and human rights: values, norms, and dilemmas in cyberspace
- CERNTin also provides courses on cyber forensics that cover the following topics:
  - Introduction to cyber forensics and its applications, methods, and tools
  - Digital evidence and its collection, preservation, analysis, and presentation
  - Cyber forensics investigation and reporting: standards, procedures, and techniques
  - Cyber forensics challenges and solutions: technical, legal, and ethical aspects
  - Cyber forensics case studies and scenarios: real-world examples and lessons learned
- CERNTin is a useful resource for anyone who wants to learn more about cyber law and cyber forensics, as well as for those who want to pursue a career or advance their skills in these fields. CERNTin also offers certification exams and credentials for those who complete its courses and demonstrate their knowledge and competence.
