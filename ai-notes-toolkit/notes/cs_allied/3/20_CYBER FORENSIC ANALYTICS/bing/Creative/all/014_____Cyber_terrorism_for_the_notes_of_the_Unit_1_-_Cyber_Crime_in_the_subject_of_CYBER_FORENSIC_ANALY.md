# Cyber terrorism

- Cyber terrorism means the **politically motivated use of computers and information technology to cause severe disruption or widespread fear in society**.
- Cyber terrorism can also be defined as **terrorist activities intended to damage or disrupt vital computer systems**.
- Cyber terrorism can result in, or threaten, the **loss of life or significant bodily harm**.
- Cyber terrorism can be carried out by **subnational groups or clandestine agents**.
- Cyber terrorism can target **information, computer systems, computer programs, and data**.
- Cyber terrorism can use various methods, such as **hacking, denial-of-service attacks, malware, ransomware, phishing, spoofing, etc.**.
- Cyber terrorism can have various impacts, such as **affecting critical infrastructure, national security, public health, economic stability, social cohesion, etc.**.
- Cyber terrorism can be prevented or mitigated by **enhancing cybersecurity, raising awareness, sharing information, cooperating internationally, enforcing laws, etc.**.
