# Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

- Cyber crime is defined as criminal activities carried out by means of computers or the internet.
- Cyber crime can target individuals, corporations, or governments, and can involve fraud, theft, child pornography, identity theft, or privacy violation.
- Cyber crime can be classified into two main categories: cyber-dependent and cyber-enabled crimes.
  - Cyber-dependent crimes are crimes that can only be committed using a computer, such as hacking, denial-of-service attacks, or malware distribution.
  - Cyber-enabled crimes are crimes that can be committed without a computer, but are facilitated or amplified by the use of a computer, such as online fraud, cyberstalking, or cyberbullying.
- Cyber crime poses a serious threat to the security, economy, and social well-being of individuals and societies, and requires effective prevention, detection, and prosecution strategies.
