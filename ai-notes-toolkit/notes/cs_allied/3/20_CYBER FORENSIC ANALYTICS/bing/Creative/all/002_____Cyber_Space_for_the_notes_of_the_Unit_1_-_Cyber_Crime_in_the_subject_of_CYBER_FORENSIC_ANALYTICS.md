# Cyber Space

- Cyber space is a term that refers to the **notional environment** in which **communication over computer networks** occurs .
- Cyber space is also used to describe the **online world** of the Internet and the diverse Internet culture.
- Cyber space is a **global and dynamic domain** that is subject to constant change and is characterized by the combined use of electrons and the electromagnetic spectrum.
- Cyber space enables the creation, storage, modification, exchange, sharing, extraction, use, elimination and disruption of information and physical resources.
- Cyber space is composed of various elements, such as **hardware, software, data, people, processes, and protocols** that interact with each other in complex ways.
- Cyber space is not a physical place, but rather a **virtual reality** that exists in the minds of the users and the devices that connect them.
- Cyber space is influenced by various factors, such as **social, cultural, political, legal, economic, and ethical** aspects that shape the norms and behaviors of the cyberspace actors.
- Cyber space is also a **contested domain**, where different actors have different interests, goals, values, and capabilities, and where conflicts and cooperation can occur.
- Cyber space is a **strategic domain**, where national and international security, defense, and intelligence issues are increasingly relevant and challenging.
- Cyber space is a **transformative domain**, where new opportunities and risks emerge for individuals, organizations, and societies, and where innovation and creativity are essential.
