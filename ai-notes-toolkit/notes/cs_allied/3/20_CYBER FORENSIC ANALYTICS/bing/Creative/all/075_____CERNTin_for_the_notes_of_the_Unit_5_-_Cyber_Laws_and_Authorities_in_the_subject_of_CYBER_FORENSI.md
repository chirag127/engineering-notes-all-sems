# CERNTin

CERNTin is an acronym for **C**yber **E**thics, **R**esponsibility, and **N**etiquette **T**raining **in**itiative. It is a program that aims to educate and empower students, teachers, parents, and communities on the ethical, responsible, and respectful use of digital technologies. CERNTin was launched by the Cybercrime Prevention and Education Center (CPEC) of the University of the Philippines Diliman in 2021. CERNTin covers topics such as cyberbullying, cyberstalking, online privacy, digital citizenship, cyber wellness, and cyber laws.

Some of the objectives of CERNTin are:

- To raise awareness and understanding of the benefits and risks of using digital technologies
- To promote positive online behavior and prevent cybercrime and cyber violence
- To develop critical thinking and digital literacy skills among students and teachers
- To foster a culture of respect, empathy, and responsibility in online interactions
- To encourage collaboration and participation in online communities and platforms
- To support the implementation of the Philippine Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 and other relevant cyber laws and policies

CERNTin offers various activities and resources for its target audiences, such as:

- Online modules and quizzes on cyber ethics, responsibility, and netiquette
- Webinars and workshops on cyber safety and security, digital citizenship, and cyber laws
- Online games and simulations on cyber scenarios and challenges
- Online forums and discussions on cyber issues and solutions
- Online campaigns and contests on cyber advocacy and creativity
- Online mentoring and coaching on cyber wellness and resilience

CERNTin is one of the initiatives that aims to address the growing need for cyber education and awareness in the Philippines and beyond. By providing relevant and engaging learning opportunities, CERNTin hopes to empower its participants to become ethical, responsible, and respectful digital citizens.
