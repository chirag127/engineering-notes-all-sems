# Wireshark for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Wireshark is a free and open-source network protocol analyzer that can capture and examine packets from various network interfaces .
- Wireshark can be used for network forensics, which is the process of analyzing network traffic and packets to identify and investigate malicious activities, such as cyberattacks, malware infections, data breaches, etc.  .
- Wireshark can display the details of each packet, such as the source and destination IP addresses, ports, protocols, payload, checksums, etc. It can also filter, search, and highlight packets based on various criteria, such as protocol, content, color, etc. .
- Wireshark can also decode and reassemble packets into higher-level protocols, such as HTTP, FTP, DNS, etc. It can also extract files, images, and other data from the packets. .
- Wireshark can also generate statistics and graphs to visualize the network traffic and identify patterns, anomalies, and trends. .
- Wireshark can be used for various network forensics tasks, such as:
  - Detecting and analyzing network attacks, such as denial-of-service, man-in-the-middle, spoofing, etc. .
  - Identifying and tracing malicious sources, such as hackers, botnets, malware, etc. .
  - Recovering and preserving digital evidence, such as logs, timestamps, files, etc. .
  - Monitoring and auditing network performance, security, and compliance. .
  - Troubleshooting and debugging network problems, such as errors, failures, misconfigurations, etc. .
- Wireshark can be installed and run on various operating systems, such as Windows, Linux, macOS, etc. It has a graphical user interface (GUI) and a command-line interface (CLI). .
- Wireshark can also be integrated with other tools and frameworks, such as Nmap, Snort, Metasploit, etc. to enhance its functionality and capabilities. .
