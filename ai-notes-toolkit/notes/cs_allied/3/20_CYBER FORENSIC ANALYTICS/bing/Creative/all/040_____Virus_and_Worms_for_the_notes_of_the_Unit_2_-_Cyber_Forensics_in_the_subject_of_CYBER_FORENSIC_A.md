# Virus and Worms

## Introduction

- Virus and worms are two types of malicious software (malware) that can infect and harm computer systems and networks.
- Virus and worms differ in how they spread and what they do to the infected systems.
- Virus and worms can cause various damages, such as deleting or encrypting files, stealing or modifying data, consuming resources, disrupting services, or opening backdoors for other attacks.
- Virus and worms can be detected and removed by using antivirus software, firewall, and other security measures.

## Virus

- A virus is a malware that attaches itself to another executable program or file and executes when the host program or file is run.
- A virus can only spread when the infected program or file is copied or transferred to another system or device, usually by human action.
- A virus can have different types of payloads, such as displaying a message, deleting files, corrupting data, or downloading other malware.
- A virus can be classified into different types based on its infection method, such as file infectors, boot sector infectors, macro viruses, or polymorphic viruses.

## Worm

- A worm is a malware that can replicate itself and spread to other systems or devices without requiring a host program or file or human action.
- A worm can spread through a computer network by exploiting vulnerabilities in software, or by using email attachments, instant messages, or spam emails.
- A worm can also have different types of payloads, such as creating a botnet, consuming bandwidth, crashing systems, or stealing information.
- A worm can be classified into different types based on its propagation method, such as mass-mailing worms, network worms, or internet worms.
