# Undercover Techniques

Undercover techniques are methods used by cybercrime investigators to conceal their identity and purpose while interacting with suspects or targets online. Undercover techniques can help investigators to gather evidence, identify offenders, prevent crimes, and protect victims. Some of the undercover techniques used in cyber investigations are:

- **Creating and operating online undercover personas.** An online undercover persona is a fake identity that an investigator adopts to communicate with suspects or targets online. An online undercover persona should be realistic, consistent, and appropriate for the investigation. An investigator should use a secure device and network to operate an online undercover persona, and avoid revealing any personal or professional information that could compromise the investigation. An investigator should also document and preserve all the interactions and activities of the online undercover persona for evidentiary purposes.

- **Using undercover techniques in online marketplaces and forums.** Online marketplaces and forums are platforms where users can buy, sell, or exchange goods and services, or share information and opinions. Some of these platforms may be used for illegal or malicious purposes, such as selling stolen data, counterfeit products, or illicit drugs, or recruiting or radicalizing potential terrorists. An investigator can use undercover techniques to infiltrate these platforms and collect intelligence, identify suspects, or disrupt criminal activities. An investigator should follow the rules and norms of the platform, and avoid arousing suspicion or drawing attention to themselves.

- **Using undercover techniques in online chat rooms and social media.** Online chat rooms and social media are platforms where users can communicate and interact with each other in real time or asynchronously. Some of these platforms may be used for illegal or malicious purposes, such as grooming or exploiting children, soliciting or distributing child pornography, or planning or coordinating cyberattacks. An investigator can use undercover techniques to monitor, join, or initiate conversations with suspects or targets online, and gather evidence, identify offenders, or prevent crimes. An investigator should be careful not to entrap or coerce the suspects or targets, and to respect their privacy and constitutional rights.

- **Using undercover techniques in cyberattacks and cyber operations.** Cyberattacks and cyber operations are activities that use computer systems or networks to compromise, damage, or disrupt the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information, systems, or services. Some of these activities may be conducted by state-sponsored actors, organized crime groups, hacktivists, or lone wolves. An investigator can use undercover techniques to infiltrate, observe, or counter these activities, and collect intelligence, identify perpetrators, or mitigate threats. An investigator should follow the legal and ethical guidelines for conducting cyberattacks and cyber operations, and avoid causing collateral damage or harm to innocent parties .
