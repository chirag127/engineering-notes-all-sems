# Disk imaging for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Disk imaging is the process of creating an electronic copy of a drive (e.g. a hard drive, USB, etc.) that preserves all the data and metadata in a forensically sound manner .
- Disk imaging is a crucial step in cyber forensics, as it allows the examiner to analyze the evidence without altering or damaging the original source .
- Disk imaging can be performed using various tools, such as hardware or software imagers, that can read the disk from start to finish and write the data to a forensics image format .
- Forensics image formats are designed to store the exact bit-by-bit or bitstream copy of the disk, along with additional information such as hash values, timestamps, compression, encryption, etc .
- Some examples of forensics image formats are: E01, AFF, RAW, ISO, etc.
- Disk imaging tools should be tested and validated to ensure their accuracy, reliability, and compatibility with different types of disks and operating systems.
- Disk imaging tools should also follow the best practices and standards of cyber forensics, such as maintaining the chain of custody, documenting the process, verifying the integrity of the image, etc .
