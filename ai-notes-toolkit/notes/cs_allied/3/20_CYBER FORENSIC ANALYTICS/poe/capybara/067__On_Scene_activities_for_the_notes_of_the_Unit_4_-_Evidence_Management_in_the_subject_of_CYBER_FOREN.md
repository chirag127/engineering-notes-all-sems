### On Scene Activities

In the context of Cyber Forensic Analytics, On Scene Activities refer to the activities that are carried out on the scene of a cybercrime. These activities are vital in collecting and preserving evidence for further analysis. Here are some of the On Scene Activities that are carried out:

1. Documentation of the Scene: Before anything else, the first step in the On Scene Activities is to document the scene. This includes taking photographs and videos of the scene. It is essential to document the scene as it will help in reconstructing the event at a later stage.

2. Identification of Evidence: The next step is to identify the evidence. This includes collecting digital evidence such as computers, laptops, mobile phones, and other electronic devices. It is crucial to identify the evidence and ensure that it is collected and preserved correctly.

3. Preservation of Evidence: Once the evidence is identified, the next step is to preserve it. This includes making sure that the evidence is not tampered with and that it is stored in a safe and secure location. Preservation of evidence is vital as it ensures that the evidence is not lost or destroyed.

4. Chain of Custody: The Chain of Custody is the process of maintaining a record of the evidence from the time it is collected until it is presented in court. It is essential to maintain the Chain of Custody as it ensures the integrity of the evidence.

5. Interviews: Interviews are conducted to gather information from individuals who may have witnessed the crime or have information about it. Interviews are vital in providing context to the evidence and helping investigators understand the crime.

6. Collection of Other Physical Evidence: In addition to digital evidence, physical evidence such as documents, fingerprints, and DNA samples may also be collected. It is essential to collect all relevant physical evidence as it may help in solving the crime.

In conclusion, On Scene Activities are crucial in collecting and preserving evidence for further analysis in Cyber Forensic Analytics. It is essential to follow the proper procedures and guidelines to ensure that the evidence is not tampered with and that it is admissible in court.
