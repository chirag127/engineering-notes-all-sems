### Investigating Computer Intrusions

Investigating computer intrusions is a crucial aspect of cyber forensic analytics. Below are some key points to keep in mind while conducting an investigation:

- **Evidence Collection:** It is important to collect as much evidence as possible during a computer intrusion investigation. This includes digital data, physical devices, network logs, and witness statements. The chain of custody should be strictly maintained to ensure that the evidence is admissible in court.
- **Analysis of Digital Data:** The digital data collected during the investigation should be analyzed thoroughly. This includes examining system logs, examining files for signs of tampering, and analyzing network traffic.
- **Reconstructing the Attack:** Once the evidence has been collected and analyzed, it is important to reconstruct the attack. This involves tracing the attacker's steps and identifying the vulnerabilities that were exploited.
- **Identifying the Attacker:** Identifying the attacker is a critical step in the investigation. This can involve using forensic tools to trace the source of the attack, analyzing network traffic to identify the attacker's IP address, and interviewing witnesses who may have seen the attacker.
- **Reporting and Remediation:** After the investigation is complete, a report should be prepared detailing the findings. Remediation measures should also be taken to prevent similar attacks in the future.

Overall, investigating computer intrusions requires a thorough knowledge of digital forensics and cyber security. It is important to follow proper procedures and maintain a strict chain of custody to ensure that the evidence is admissible in court.
