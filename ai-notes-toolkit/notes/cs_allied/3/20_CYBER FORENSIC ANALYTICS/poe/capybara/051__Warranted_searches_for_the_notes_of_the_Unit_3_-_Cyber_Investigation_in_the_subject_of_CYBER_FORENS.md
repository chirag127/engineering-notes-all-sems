### Warranted searches for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS.

In the field of cyber forensic analytics, the investigation process involves collecting evidence for legal proceedings. Warranted searches are an essential aspect of this process, as they provide the legal authority to gather evidence from digital devices. Here are some key points to keep in mind regarding warranted searches:

- A warranted search is a legal process that allows law enforcement officials to search a person's home, office, or digital devices.
- A search warrant is issued by a judge, who reviews the evidence presented by law enforcement officials and determines if there is probable cause to conduct the search.
- The Fourth Amendment to the United States Constitution protects citizens from unreasonable searches and seizures. A search warrant is necessary to conduct a search that is considered reasonable under the law.
- In the context of cyber forensic investigation, a search warrant authorizes the search and seizure of digital devices, such as computers, smartphones, and tablets, for the purpose of collecting evidence.
- Law enforcement officials must follow specific procedures when conducting a warranted search, such as providing a copy of the search warrant to the person being searched and conducting the search in a reasonable manner.
- Failure to follow these procedures can result in the evidence being deemed inadmissible in court.
- It is important to note that not all searches require a warrant. In some cases, such as when a person gives consent to a search, a warrant may not be necessary.
- However, in the context of cyber forensic investigation, obtaining a search warrant is typically the preferred method for collecting evidence, as it provides a clear legal basis for the search and seizure of digital devices.

In conclusion, warranted searches play a crucial role in the investigation process of cyber forensic analytics. Understanding the legal procedures and requirements surrounding search warrants is essential for conducting a thorough and legally sound investigation.
