### Steganography for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Steganography is the art of hiding a message within another seemingly innocuous message. It is an important concept in the field of cyber forensics as it is often used by criminals to hide their activities and communications from law enforcement. Here are some key points to understand about steganography:

- Steganography involves hiding a message within another message, such as hiding text within an image file.
- The goal of steganography is to make the hidden message undetectable to anyone who is not the intended recipient.
- There are many different methods of steganography, including techniques that modify the least significant bits of a file, or that use specific patterns of pixels in an image to encode a message.
- Steganography can be used for both legal and illegal purposes. For example, it can be used by journalists to protect the identity of sources, or by criminals to communicate without being detected.
- Steganography is often used in conjunction with encryption to provide an additional layer of security. By encrypting the hidden message before it is embedded in the carrier file, it becomes even more difficult for unauthorized parties to access the information.
- Detecting steganography can be a difficult task for cyber forensic analysts. However, there are tools and techniques available that can help identify the presence of hidden messages within files.
- It is important for cyber forensic analysts to be aware of steganography and the various methods that can be used to hide information. By understanding how steganography works, analysts can better identify and investigate criminal activity.

In summary, steganography is a technique used to hide messages within seemingly innocuous files. It can be used for both legal and illegal purposes, and can be difficult to detect. However, with the right tools and techniques, cyber forensic analysts can identify the presence of hidden messages and use this information to investigate criminal activity.
