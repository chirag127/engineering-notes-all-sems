### Hard disk for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

Here are some important points to keep in mind regarding the hard disk for the notes of Unit 2 in Cyber Forensics:

- The hard disk is a crucial component for storing and retrieving data in a computer system. It is also an important source of evidence in cyber forensic investigations.
- When dealing with a hard disk, it is important to follow proper handling procedures to avoid damaging the drive or contaminating the evidence. Careful handling is necessary to preserve the integrity of the data stored on the disk.
- The first step in working with a hard disk is to create a forensic image of the drive. This is a bit-for-bit copy of the entire disk, which can be used for analysis and investigation without altering the original data. 
- During the imaging process, it is important to use a tool that is capable of creating a verified image, which means that the image is checked for errors and verified to be an exact copy of the original disk. 
- Once the image is created, it can be analyzed using various forensic tools to uncover evidence of cybercrime. The analysis process typically involves searching for files, keywords, and other indicators of suspicious activity. 
- It is important to note that the analysis of a hard disk can be a time-consuming process, as there may be large amounts of data to sift through. 
- In addition to analyzing the data on the hard disk, it is also important to examine the physical characteristics of the drive itself, such as the make and model, serial number, and any signs of physical damage or tampering. 
- Overall, the hard disk is a critical component of cyber forensic investigations, and proper handling and analysis procedures are essential to ensure the integrity of the data and the validity of any findings.
