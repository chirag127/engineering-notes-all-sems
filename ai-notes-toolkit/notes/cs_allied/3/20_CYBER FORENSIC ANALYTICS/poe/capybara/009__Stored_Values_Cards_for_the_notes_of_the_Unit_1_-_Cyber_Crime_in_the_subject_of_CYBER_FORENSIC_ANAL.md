### Stored Values Cards

Stored value cards, also known as gift cards, are a type of payment card that contains a stored value of money that can be used for purchases. They are commonly used in retail stores, restaurants, and other businesses as an alternative to cash or credit cards.

#### Types of Stored Value Cards

There are two types of stored value cards: closed-loop and open-loop cards.

- **Closed-loop cards** are cards that can only be used at a specific merchant or chain of merchants. For example, a Starbucks gift card can only be used at Starbucks locations.

- **Open-loop cards** are cards that can be used anywhere that accepts the card brand. For example, a Visa gift card can be used at any merchant that accepts Visa.

#### How Stored Value Cards Work

Stored value cards work by storing a balance of money on the card. When the card is used to make a purchase, the amount of the purchase is deducted from the balance on the card. If the balance becomes low, the card can be reloaded with more money.

Stored value cards can be purchased at the merchant's location or online. They can also be given as gifts or used as incentives for customers.

#### Risks Associated with Stored Value Cards

Stored value cards can be vulnerable to fraud and theft. Here are some risks associated with stored value cards:

- **Counterfeit cards:** Criminals may create counterfeit cards that look like legitimate stored value cards. They can then use these cards to make purchases or sell them to others.

- **Stolen cards:** If a stored value card is stolen, the thief can use the balance on the card to make purchases.

- **Lost cards:** If a stored value card is lost, the balance on the card is lost as well.

#### Investigating Stored Value Cards

In cases of cyber crime, stored value cards can be used to launder money or to purchase illegal goods or services. Investigators may need to track the use of stored value cards to gather evidence.

Here are some ways that investigators can track stored value cards:

- **Transaction records:** Investigators can obtain transaction records from the merchant or card issuer to track the use of a stored value card.

- **Security footage:** If the card was used in person, investigators can obtain security footage to identify the person using the card.

- **Interviews:** Investigators can interview witnesses or suspects to gather information about the use of a stored value card.

#### Conclusion

Stored value cards are a popular form of payment that can be used in a variety of settings. However, they can also be vulnerable to fraud and theft. Investigators may need to track the use of stored value cards in cases of cyber crime. By understanding how stored value cards work and the risks associated with them, investigators can gather evidence and help bring criminals to justice.
