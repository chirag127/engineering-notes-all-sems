### Criminal Behaviour

In the field of cyber forensic analytics, understanding criminal behaviour is crucial when investigating cyber crimes. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Criminal behaviour refers to actions that violate the law and are committed with the intention of causing harm or gain for the perpetrator.
- Cyber criminals often have a motive, such as financial gain, revenge, or political activism.
- Cyber criminals may use a variety of techniques to achieve their goals, including hacking, phishing, and social engineering.
- It is important to understand the psychology behind criminal behaviour to better predict and prevent cyber crimes.
- Some common psychological factors that may contribute to criminal behaviour include greed, impulsivity, and a lack of empathy for others.
- Additionally, environmental factors such as poverty, social isolation, and exposure to violence can also contribute to criminal behaviour.
- Cyber forensic analysts may use various tools and techniques to investigate criminal behaviour, including digital forensics, data analysis, and psychological profiling.
- By understanding criminal behaviour, cyber forensic analysts can better identify and investigate cyber crimes, as well as develop strategies to prevent them from occurring in the first place.
