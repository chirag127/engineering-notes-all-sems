## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

In this unit, we will be discussing the various aspects of cyber investigation. Cyber investigation is the process of investigating cybercrimes, which are crimes that are committed using digital technology. Cyber investigation is a critical component of modern law enforcement, as more and more crimes are being committed online.

### Importance of Cyber Investigation

- Cyber investigation is necessary to identify and apprehend cyber criminals who use digital technology to commit crimes.
- Cyber investigation is essential for the protection of individuals and organizations against cyber attacks.
- Cyber investigation plays a vital role in ensuring cybersecurity and protecting sensitive information.

### Types of Cybercrimes

- Hacking: Unauthorized access to computer systems or networks.
- Malware: The use of malicious software to gain unauthorized access or cause harm.
- Phishing: The use of deceptive emails or websites to obtain sensitive information.
- Identity theft: The theft of personal information for fraudulent purposes.
- Cyberstalking: The use of digital technology to stalk or harass an individual.
- Cyberbullying: The use of digital technology to bully or harass an individual.

### Cyber Investigation Techniques

- Digital Forensics: The process of collecting and analyzing digital evidence to solve crimes.
- Network Analysis: The process of analyzing network traffic to identify suspicious activity.
- Social Engineering: The use of psychological manipulation to obtain sensitive information.
- Open-Source Intelligence: The process of collecting information from publicly available sources.

### Cyber Investigation Process

- Identification of the crime: The first step is to identify the type of cybercrime that has been committed.
- Preservation of evidence: The evidence must be preserved to ensure that it can be used in court.
- Collection of evidence: The evidence must be collected using appropriate methods and techniques.
- Analysis of evidence: The evidence must be analyzed to identify the perpetrator and build a case.
- Presentation of evidence: The evidence must be presented in court to prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt.

### Cyber Investigation Tools

- Encase: A digital forensics tool used to collect and analyze digital evidence.
- Wireshark: A network analysis tool used to analyze network traffic.
- Metasploit: A tool used for penetration testing and vulnerability assessment.
- Nmap: A network mapping and scanning tool used to identify open ports and vulnerabilities.

### Conclusion

Cyber investigation is a critical component of modern law enforcement. It is essential for identifying and apprehending cyber criminals, protecting individuals and organizations against cyber attacks, and ensuring cybersecurity. To be successful in cyber investigation, it is necessary to understand the various types of cybercrimes, use appropriate techniques and tools, and follow a proper investigation process.
