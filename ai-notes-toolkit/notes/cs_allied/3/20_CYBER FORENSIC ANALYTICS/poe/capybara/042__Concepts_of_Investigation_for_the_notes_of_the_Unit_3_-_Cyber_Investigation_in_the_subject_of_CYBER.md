### Concepts of Investigation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

In the field of cyber forensic analytics, investigations are conducted to gather evidence and analyze it to determine the cause and origin of a cybercrime. The following are some of the key concepts of investigation that are important to understand:

- **Chain of Custody:** This refers to the chronological documentation of the transfer of physical or digital evidence from the original source to the investigator. It is important to maintain the chain of custody to ensure the integrity of the evidence and its admissibility in court.

- **Digital Evidence:** This refers to any electronic data that can be used as evidence in a criminal investigation. Examples include email messages, text messages, social media posts, and computer files. Digital evidence is often volatile and can be easily altered or deleted, so it is important to preserve it properly.

- **Forensic Imaging:** This involves creating a bit-for-bit copy of a storage device, such as a hard drive or USB drive, for analysis. Forensic imaging is important because it allows investigators to work with a copy of the evidence and preserve the original device.

- **Metadata:** This refers to the data that describes other data. In the context of cyber forensic analytics, metadata can provide valuable information about the origin and history of digital evidence.

- **Timeline Analysis:** This involves creating a chronological timeline of events related to a cybercrime. Timeline analysis can help investigators understand the sequence of events and identify potential suspects.

- **Volatility:** This refers to the tendency of digital evidence to change or be lost over time. Volatility can be caused by a variety of factors, such as power loss or software updates. It is important for investigators to be aware of volatility and take steps to preserve evidence before it is lost.

By understanding these key concepts of investigation, cyber forensic analysts can conduct effective and thorough investigations of cybercrime.
