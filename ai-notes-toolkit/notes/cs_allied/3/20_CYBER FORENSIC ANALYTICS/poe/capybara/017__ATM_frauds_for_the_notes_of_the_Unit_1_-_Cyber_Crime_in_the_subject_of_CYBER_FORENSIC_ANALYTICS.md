### ATM Frauds

ATM frauds are one of the most common types of cyber crimes. They involve unauthorized access to an individual's bank account through the use of an ATM machine. In this section, we will take a closer look at what ATM frauds are, their types, and how they can be prevented.

#### Types of ATM Frauds

1. **Skimming**: Skimming is the most common type of ATM fraud. It involves the use of a skimming device that is attached to the ATM machine. The device reads the magnetic strip on the debit/credit card and captures the card details. Fraudsters then use this information to make fraudulent transactions.

2. **Card Trapping**: In card trapping, fraudsters insert a thin plastic device into the card slot of an ATM machine. The device traps the card inside the machine, and the fraudsters retrieve it later. They then use the card details for making fraudulent transactions.

3. **Cash Trapping**: Cash trapping involves placing a device inside the cash dispenser slot that traps the cash as it is dispensed. The fraudsters retrieve the cash later.

4. **PIN Theft**: In this type of fraud, fraudsters use a hidden camera or keypad overlay to capture the PIN entered by the user. They then use the card details and PIN to make fraudulent transactions.

#### Prevention of ATM Frauds

1. **Be vigilant**: Always be aware of your surroundings while using an ATM machine. Look for any suspicious devices or people around the machine.

2. **Cover the keypad**: While entering your PIN, cover the keypad with your hand to prevent anyone from seeing it.

3. **Use secure ATMs**: Use ATMs located in secure areas such as inside a bank or a shopping mall.

4. **Check your bank account regularly**: Regularly check your bank account for any suspicious transactions. Report any unauthorized transactions to your bank immediately.

5. **Use EMV cards**: Use cards that have EMV chip technology. These cards are more secure than magnetic strip cards and are less susceptible to skimming.

In conclusion, ATM frauds are a serious issue that can result in financial loss for individuals. By being aware of the types of ATM frauds and taking necessary precautions, we can prevent ourselves from becoming victims of this cyber crime.
