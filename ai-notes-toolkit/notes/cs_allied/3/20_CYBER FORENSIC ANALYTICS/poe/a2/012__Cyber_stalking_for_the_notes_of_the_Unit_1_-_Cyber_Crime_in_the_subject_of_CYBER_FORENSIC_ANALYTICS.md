### Cyber stalking for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Cyber stalking refers to the repeated unwanted behavior directed at a specific person via digital devices and internet-enabled technology.
2. It involves harassing or threatening behavior that is targeted at a specific victim and intended to cause fear or distress.
3. Common cyber stalking behaviors include:
- Sending unwanted, harassing or threatening messages via email, social media, instant messaging or other online means.
- Posting private or personally identifying information about the victim online.
- Tracking the victim's online activities or location and sharing that information with others.
- Impersonating the victim online or creating fake social media profiles in the victim's name.
4. Cyber stalking is a criminal offense and can have severe psychological and emotional impacts on victims. It is important to report any suspicious or harassing online behavior to the appropriate parties.
