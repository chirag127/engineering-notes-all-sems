## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

1. Introduction to Cyber Investigation
- Cyber investigation refers to the process of examining computing devices and systems for potential evidence of illegal activity or security incidents.
- The investigation is conducted by cybersecurity professionals to identify, preserve, analyze and present digital evidence to authorized parties.
- Various tools and techniques are used to conduct cyber investigations, such as data recovery, log analysis, malware analysis, network forensics, etc.

2. Data Collection and Analysis
- Data is collected from various digital sources such as computers, servers, networks, mobile devices, cloud, etc. using appropriate tools and techniques.
- The collected data is then analyzed to look for any suspicious activity, security breaches or illegal actions. Data analysis may reveal hidden connections and patterns in the data to draw conclusions.
- Powerful data analytics and data visualization tools are used to efficiently analyze and interpret the digital evidence. Data is organized and presented in human-readable formats as investigation reports or presentations.

3. Legal and Ethical Considerations
- Cyber investigators must follow proper legal procedures and laws related to search, seizure and analysis of digital evidence. They should have knowledge of cybercrime laws and also privacy laws.
- Investigators must be impartial and unbiased. They should only analyze relevant data as per the law and not access or share any personal or private data unrelated to the investigation.
- The digital evidence analysis process should be meticulous, transparent and reproducible. The tools and techniques used must be validated to maintain high credibility of investigation results.

4. Conclusion
- To summarize, cyber investigation is an important process to look into security incidents and illegal cyber activities. By collecting and analyzing digital data effectively, cyber investigators can reveal hidden patterns and connections to identify potential threats and crimes. However, legal and ethical considerations must be kept in mind during the entire investigation process.
