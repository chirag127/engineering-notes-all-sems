### Cyber terrorism

- Cyber terrorism refers to the use of computers and networks to intimidate or coerce a government or its citizens in furtherance of political or social objectives.
- It involves the use of computer network tools to shut down critical national infrastructures like energy, transportation, government operations, or emergency services.
- The motives of cyber terrorists can be religious, political or ideological. They aim to create fear and disruption among people.
- Cyber terrorists may hack into computer systems to steal and destroy data or take control of critical infrastructure like power grids.
- They may use malware, DDoS attacks or other hacking techniques to carry out their attacks. These attacks can have severe consequences including loss of lives.
- It is difficult to trace the source of cyber terrorism attacks and identify the attackers. This makes cyber terrorism a serious threat to national and international security.
- Countries need to have strong cyber security measures and cyber crime investigation capabilities in place to prevent and deal with potential cyber terrorism attacks. International co-operation is also needed to tackle this borderless threat.
