### Types for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS:

1. Primary Evidence:
- Collected from the original source.
- Directly related to the incident under investigation.
- Examples: Log files, malware samples, memory dumps, etc.

2. Secondary Evidence:
- Derived or collected indirectly from primary evidence.
- Examples: Findings from the analysis of primary evidence like recovered data, timeline of events, etc.

3. Direct Evidence:
- Proves a fact directly without requiring an inference.
- Examples: Admission statements, eyewitness accounts, surveillance footage, etc.

4. Circumstantial Evidence:
- Requires an inference to prove a fact.
- Examples: Opportunity, motive, access, etc.

5. Real Evidence:
- Tangible objects offered as proof in a trial.
- Examples: Weapons, documents, devices, etc.

The evidence should be handled carefully to maintain integrity and authenticity. Proper procedures and tools should be used for evidence acquisition, transportation, storage, and analysis.
