## Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities

1. Cyber Laws - Laws related to cyber space and cyber crimes. Eg. Information Technology Act, 2000 in India which defines cyber crimes and their punishments.
2. Cyber Crime - Any illegal activity committed using computer, computer network or internet. Eg. Hacking, Phishing, Online Fraud etc.
3. Cyber Security - Protection against cyber attacks and cyber crimes. Includes steps to protect data, systems, network etc.
4. Cyber Authorities - Government bodies responsible for cyber security and implementing cyber laws. Eg. CERT-In in India which monitors cyber security threats and issues guidelines.
5. Intermediaries - Third parties who provide services such as hosting, storing data, search engines etc. on the internet. They are also governed under cyber laws for content moderation, privacy etc. Eg. Google, Facebook, Internet Service Providers etc.
