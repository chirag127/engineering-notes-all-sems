### Cyber Criminal profiling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

- Cyber criminals can be classified into following types:
-- Script kiddies: Hackers with less technical expertise who use ready-made scripts or programs to conduct cyber attacks. They are often motivated by thrill seeking or bragging rights in hacker communities.
-- Hacktivists: Hackers who carry out attacks with a social or political message, like defacing government websites or taking them offline. Examples include hacker groups like Anonymous.
-- Organized crime groups: Professional cyber criminal networks that conduct attacks like phishing, ransomware, or trojan malware campaigns purely for financial gain and profit. They operate like legitimate businesses with a structured hierarchy and division of labor.
-- Nation state actors: Government agencies or military groups of a nation that conduct sophisticated cyber espionage or sabotage attacks to gather intelligence or damage adversaries. Examples include groups like APT28 and APT29.
-- Insiders: Employees or former employees of an organization who misuse their access privileges to steal data, conduct sabotage, or carry out fraud for personal gain. Around 50% of cyber attacks are estimated to involve insiders.

- To investigate and attribute cyber attacks, cyber forensic analysts can look at distinct characteristics exhibited by different criminal profiles. For example, script kiddies may leave obvious clues, hacktivists may draw attention to their cause, organized crime may show monetary motivation, and nation states may conduct highly sophisticated attacks. Profiling can help narrow down the list of potential suspects and determine appropriate responses.
