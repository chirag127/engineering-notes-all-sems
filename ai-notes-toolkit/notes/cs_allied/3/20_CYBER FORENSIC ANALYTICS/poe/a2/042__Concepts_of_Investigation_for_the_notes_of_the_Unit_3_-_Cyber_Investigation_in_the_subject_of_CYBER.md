4. Analysis of Digital Evidence: The extracted data and information is analyzed to understand the events and draw conclusions. Timeline analysis and correlation are done to recreate the events. Various logs and artifacts are analyzed to draw meaningful inferences.

5. Reporting of Digital Evidence: A proper report is prepared stating the methods and tools used.
