## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

1. Cyber Crimes - Cyber crimes refer to unlawful acts wherein the computer is either a tool or a target or both. These acts are committed by individuals or organized groups to steal money or data or to gain unauthorized access to a computer system.

2. Types of Cyber Crimes - The major types of cyber crimes are:

- Cyber theft - Stealing money or financial data or intellectual property from individuals or organizations.
- Cyber vandalism - Intentional sabotage of computer systems or networks through acts like malware attacks, defacing websites, etc.
- Cyber fraud - Obtaining money or funds illegally through deceitful means using computers and the internet.
- Cyber stalking - Stalking or harassing a person online through methods like sending threatening messages, sharing private data, etc.
- Phishing attacks - Tricking users into providing personal information or account credentials through malicious emails or messages.
- Ransomware attacks - Gaining access to a computer system and restricting access to it until a ransom is paid.

3. Prevention of Cyber Crimes - The following steps can be taken to prevent cyber crimes:

- Use strong and unique passwords for online accounts and enable two-factor authentication whenever possible.
- Install a reputed antivirus or anti-malware software and keep it updated.
- Browse the internet safely by disabling Flash and Java, avoiding suspicious links and downloads, etc.
- Educate employees on cyber threats and security best practices.
- Report any suspicious activity or cyber crime incidents to the relevant authorities.
- Keep software and operating systems updated with the latest security patches.
