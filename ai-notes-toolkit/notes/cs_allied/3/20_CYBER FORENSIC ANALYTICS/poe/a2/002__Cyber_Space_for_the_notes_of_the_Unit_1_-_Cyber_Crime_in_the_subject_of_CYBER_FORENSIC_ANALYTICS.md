### Cyber Space for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Cyber space refers to the virtual space or environment created by computers and telecommunications networks including the internet. It is a broader term that encompasses the internet, virtual private networks, and other networks.
2. Cyber crimes are criminal activities that are carried out by using computers and telecommunication networks like the internet. These crimes may include fraud, theft of information, harassment, and other illegal activities.
3. Types of cyber crimes:
    - Internet frauds like phishing, spoofing, identity theft, scams
    - Theft of information like financial data, personal data, intellectual property
    - Harassment like cyber stalking, cyber bullying, online defamation
    - Distribution of malicious software like viruses, worms, ransomware
    - Child pornography and online exploitation
    - Terrorism and politically motivated attacks
4. Challenges in investigating cyber crimes:
    - Anonymity of the internet making it difficult to trace the perpetrator
    - Fast pace of technology making it difficult for laws and law enforcement to keep up
    - Global nature of the internet making jurisdiction difficult to determine
    - Volatility of data and artifacts making digital forensics difficult
