### Report Preparations for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Gather and secure digital evidence:
- Identify and locate digital evidence sources like hard drives, emails, logs, etc.
- Secure the evidence by making a bit-by-bit copy of the original using write blockers or forensic imaging tools. This preserves the original evidence.
- Maintain chain of custody records of seized evidence.

2. Analyze and evaluate digital evidence:
- Extract and parse data from digital evidence using forensic tools.
- Correlate different pieces of evidence to understand the timeline of events and actions.
- Evaluate reliability and relevance of evidence using principles like integrity, authenticity, best evidence rule, etc.

3. Prepare investigation report:
- Organize analyzed digital evidence and investigation details in a structured format.
- Include details like incident description, investigation goals, evidence sources, analysis methods, findings, conclusions, recommendations, etc.
- Use formal language and writing style. Follow required report formatting guidelines.
- Proofread the report before finalizing it.

The above points cover the key steps and considerations in preparing a cyber forensics investigation report based on managing and analyzing digital evidence from Unit 4. The report should be prepared in a formal manner with objective analysis and recommendations.
