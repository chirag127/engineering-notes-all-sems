### DDoS Attacks

* DDoS stands for Distributed Denial of Service. It is a type of cyber attack where the attacker floods the target with excessive internet traffic, essentially disrupting its services and making it inaccessible to legitimate users.
* In DDoS attacks, the attacker uses a network of compromised computers (botnet) to send heavy traffic to the target server. This traffic overload consumes the target's available bandwidth and resources, eventually crashing the server.
* The key steps involved in a DDoS attack are:
1. The attacker gains control of a large number of computers (botnet) and installs malware on them to control them remotely.
2. The attacker then directs all the compromised computers to send a flood of traffic to the target server at the same time.
3. This sudden surge of excessive traffic overwhelms the target server, exhausting its resources and causing it to crash or slow down significantly.
* DDoS attacks are illegal and unethical. They are often used for extortion by threatening to disrupt a business's operations. DDoS attacks can have serious negative impacts, including financial losses and damage to reputation. Organizations should implement strong defense mechanisms to mitigate DDoS attacks.
