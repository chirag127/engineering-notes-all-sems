### Secure electronic records for the notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Importance of secure electronic records
- Electronic records are important evidence in cyber crimes
- They can be easily tampered or deleted, so securing them is critical
- Proper write blocking techniques and forensic image acquisition is important to maintain integrity

2. Laws regarding electronic records
- Original evidence cannot be altered or tampered. Only forensic images can be analyzed
- Proper warrants and legal permission is required to acquire electronic data as evidence
- Adherence to laws like Electronic Communications Privacy Act, Stored Communications Act, etc. is necessary

3. Authorities for securing electronic records
- Law enforcement agencies like FBI, police, etc. can secure electronic records with proper legal permission
- Forensic experts and ethical hackers can be hired to acquire forensic images and analyze them
- Judges can pass orders to seize electronic devices and obtain backups/cloud data as required for a case

4. Best practices to secure electronic records
- disconnect device from networks and prohibit remote access
- Use write blockers to acquire forensic images
- Establish chain of custody
- Store originals and images securely with verification and backups
- Extract and analyze data using forensic tools and techniques
- Preserve metadata and generate hashes for verification
