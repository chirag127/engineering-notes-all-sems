### On Scene activities for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Secure the scene: The first and foremost activity at the scene is to secure the crime scene. The scene of the cybercrime should be secured and untouched. Any contamination or tampering of the evidence should be avoided.
2. Identify and isolate systems: The systems involved in the cybercrime incident should be identified and isolated from the network. This is to avoid any further damage and tampering of evidence. The isolated systems can then be analyzed forensically.
3. Take images of evidence: Forensic images or bitstream copies of the storage drives should be taken. This ensures that the original evidence is preserved and the images can be analyzed.
4. Collect additional evidence: Other forensic evidence like logs, malware, credentials, etc should be collected. Volatile data can be collected using live analysis techniques.
5. Maintain chain of custody: It is important to maintain chain of custody of all the evidence collected. This ensures the integrity and admissibility of the evidence in the court of law. Proper logs and documentation should be maintained about the evidence collection process.

The above points cover the major on-scene activities to be performed during evidence management in a cybercrime incident. Preserving the original evidence and maintaining proper chain of custody are very crucial in cyber forensics. The isolated systems and collected evidence can then be analyzed in the forensic lab for further investigation.
