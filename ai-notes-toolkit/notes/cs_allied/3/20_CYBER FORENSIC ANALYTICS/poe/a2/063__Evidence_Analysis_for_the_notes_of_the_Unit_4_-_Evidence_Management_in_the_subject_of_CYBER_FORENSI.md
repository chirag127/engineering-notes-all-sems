### Evidence Analysis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Collect and acquire digital evidence:
- Identify and seize digital devices.
- Make forensic image of digital devices.
- Analyze and extract data from forensic images.

2. Examine and analyze file systems and data:
- Analyze file systems to understand data storage.
- Recover deleted data and hidden files.
- Analyze various types of files like documents, images, databases, etc.

3. Correlate evidence:
- Correlate data from multiple sources.
- Establish relationships between data, devices and individuals.
- Prepare timeline of events and activities.

4. Report and present findings:
- Prepare investigation report.
- Present findings and evidence in the court of law.
- Draw conclusions based on evidence and address investigation objectives.

The above points cover the key steps involved in evidence analysis for cyber forensic investigations to understand the digital evidence and address the investigation objectives. The evidence needs to be properly collected, examined, analyzed, correlated and presented to draw valid conclusions.
