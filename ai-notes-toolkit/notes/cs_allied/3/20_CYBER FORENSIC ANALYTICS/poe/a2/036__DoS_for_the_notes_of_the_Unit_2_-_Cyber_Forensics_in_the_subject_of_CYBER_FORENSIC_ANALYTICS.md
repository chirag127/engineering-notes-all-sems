### DoS

- DoS stands for Denial of Service. It is a type of cyber attack where the attacker floods the target system or network with excessive internet traffic, thereby disrupting its services and making it unavailable to legitimate users.
- The goal of a DoS attack is to deny access to a resource by legitimate users. The common techniques used for DoS attacks are:
 - Flooding the target with excessive packets
 - Sending malformed packets to crash services
 - Spoofing source IP addresses to overwhelm the target
- DoS attacks are easy to conduct but difficult to defend against. Some prevention techniques are:
 - Rate limiting
 - Packet filtering
 - Scrubbing
 - Using firewalls and intrusion prevention systems
 - Distributing resources to handle excess load
- DoS attacks are illegal and unethical as they disrupt services and violate availability. Cyber forensic analysts investigate DoS attacks by analyzing network traffic logs and server logs to find the source and techniques of the attack. Appropriate legal action can then be taken against the attackers.
