### Cyber Investigation

**Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation**

- Collection of digital evidence: Collection of digital evidence from various digital sources like computers, servers, mobiles, cloud, etc. while maintaining integrity of evidence. Following proper procedures and using appropriate tools for collection.
- Analysis of digital evidence: Analyzing the collected digital evidence to find patterns and insights. This includes analysis of file metadata, strings, hex editor, etc. to uncover hidden information and make connections.
- Timeline analysis: Creating timeline of events and actions from digital evidence to understand the sequence of events and find anomalies. This helps in understanding the context and cracking the case.
- Attribution and identification: Attributing the digital evidence to a person or an entity and identifying the source. This is done using various techniques like MAC address, IP address, device identification, etc.
- Reporting and presentation: Preparing reports and presentations to convey the investigation details and findings to the relevant stakeholders. The reports and presentations need to be comprehensive yet easy to understand for non-technical people.

The above points cover the major steps involved in a cyber investigation. The process requires a systematic approach and skilled professionals to handle the digital evidence and draw the right conclusions. Cyber investigations have become very critical with increased cyber crimes and need for tracing the perpetrators.
