### Botnets

- A botnet is a network of compromised computers that are controlled by an attacker to conduct malicious activities, such as sending out spam email, installing malware, or conducting DDoS attacks.
- Computers are usually compromised through malware or viruses that get installed when the user clicks on malicious links, downloads infected files, or uses infected removable media.
- Once compromised, the attacker can control the computer remotely and use it to carry out nefarious tasks along with other compromised computers as a botnet.
- Botnets are appealing to attackers because they can leverage the resources of many computers to conduct attacks and expand their reach. Stopping botnets is challenging because they are decentralized and constantly evolving.
- To defend against botnets, users should be cautious of phishing emails and malicious links, keep systems and software up to date, and use antivirus software. Network operators can monitor for botnet activity and take infected machines offline. Law enforcement works to identify botnet attackers and take down infrastructure.
