### Honey Pots for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. A honeypot is a system that is designed to be attacked or compromised. It is a trap set to detect, deflect, or in some manner counteract attempts at unauthorized use of information systems.
2. Honeypots allow us to learn how attackers work by monitoring their activities in a safe, controlled environment. This helps cybersecurity researchers identify attacker's strategies and better protect systems and data.
3. There are two main types of honeypots:
- Production honeypot: It is placed within the actual production network to detect attacks. It has some vulnerabilities that an attacker can exploit.
- Research honeypot: It is isolated from the production network. It is designed to be compromised to learn attacker's behavior and techniques. The activities are closely monitored.
4. The key advantages of honeypots are:
- They provide opportunities to study attacks and attackers.
- They can detect new attacks and techniques.
- They divert attackers from critical systems and data.
- They can capture and analyze malware samples.
