### Digital Evidence for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Digital evidence refers to any data that can establish identity or prove a fact in an investigation. It can be found on any digital device or storage medium.
2. Types of digital evidence:
    - System artifacts: Logs, activity records, accessed/modified files
    - Application data: Emails, chats, downloaded files, account information
    - Multimedia: Images, videos, audio files
3. Challenges in digital evidence:
    - Voluminous data: Petabytes of data in enterprises and cloud
    - Easily altered: Data can be easily deleted, edited or fabricated
    - Complex formats: Variety of formats, encodings and structures
    - Dynamic nature: Continuous changes to systems, data and applications
4. Best practices for evidence management:
    - Secure the device or system as soon as possible to prevent tampering
    - Make a bit-level image of the storage medium for analysis
    - Use write blockers to prevent modification of evidence
    - Maintain chain of custody logs for evidence handling
    - Store evidence in tamper-proof containers or systems
    - Authenticate users accessing the evidence
    - Ensure analysts are trained and follow standard processes
