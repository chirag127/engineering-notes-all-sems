### Warrantless searches for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Warrantless searches refer to searches conducted by law enforcement officials without obtaining a warrant from a court.
2. These searches are allowed under certain special circumstances like:
- Searches incident to lawful arrest: Officers can conduct warrantless searches of the arrestee's person and the area within his/her immediate control.
- Automobile searches: Officers can search a vehicle without a warrant if they have probable cause to believe that it contains contraband or evidence of a crime.
- Plain view doctrine: If officers are lawfully in a place, they can seize any illegal items that are in plain view.
- Exigent circumstances: Warrantless searches are allowed if officers have an objectively reasonable belief that immediate action is necessary to prevent imminent harm, stop ongoing criminal activity, or prevent the loss/destruction of evidence.
3. Any evidence obtained from warrantless searches that do not fall within the exceptions may be suppressed and excluded from the trial.
4. Warrantless searches raise issues surrounding privacy and the reasonableness of police conduct. Courts attempt to strike a balance between effective law enforcement and protection of civil liberties.
