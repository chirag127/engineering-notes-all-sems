## Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

In this unit, we will be exploring the basics of cyber investigation. Cyber investigation is the process of gathering and analyzing information about digital devices and networks to solve cybercrimes. It involves a combination of technical and investigative skills and is critical in the fight against cybercrime. 

The following are some key concepts and techniques that are important to understand in the context of cyber investigation:

- **Digital Forensics**: This is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving electronic evidence from digital devices such as computers, mobile phones, and storage media. Digital forensics helps investigators to reconstruct events and identify perpetrators of cyber crimes. 

- **Chain of Custody**: This refers to the proper handling of digital evidence to maintain its integrity and reliability. The chain of custody must be established and maintained throughout the investigation process to ensure that the evidence can be used in court.

- **Data Recovery**: This involves the retrieval of deleted, damaged, or hidden data from digital devices. Data recovery techniques are crucial in cyber investigations as they can uncover valuable evidence that could be used to solve crimes.

- **Network Analysis**: This involves the examination of network traffic to identify sources of attacks, determine the scope of the attack, and track the movements of attackers. Network analysis is a critical aspect of cyber investigations as it can help to identify the source of an attack and prevent future attacks.

- **Malware Analysis**: This involves the examination of malicious software to determine its functionality, behavior, and origin. Malware analysis is important in cyber investigations as it can help to identify the source of an attack and prevent future attacks.

- **Social Engineering**: This is the use of psychological manipulation to trick individuals into divulging sensitive information or performing actions that are not in their best interest. Social engineering is a common tactic used by cybercriminals and understanding its techniques is critical in preventing cyber attacks.

In summary, cyber investigation is a critical aspect of the fight against cybercrime. Understanding the concepts and techniques involved in cyber investigation is essential for investigators to effectively gather and analyze electronic evidence, identify perpetrators of cyber crimes, and prevent future attacks.
