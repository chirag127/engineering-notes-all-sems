### Warranted searches for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

In cyber forensic investigations, it is crucial to follow the legal process to gather evidence. This involves obtaining warrants for searches and seizures of digital devices and data. Here are some important points to keep in mind regarding warranted searches:

1. Warrant requirement - In most countries, including the United States, a warrant is required for a search and seizure of digital data. The warrant must be issued by a judge or magistrate and must include specific information about the location to be searched, the items to be seized, and the probable cause for the search.

2. Exceptions to the warrant requirement - There are some exceptions to the warrant requirement, such as exigent circumstances where there is an immediate threat to life or property, or where the evidence is in plain view. However, these exceptions are narrowly defined and should be used cautiously.

3. Types of warrants - There are different types of warrants that can be used in cyber forensic investigations. A search warrant allows the search and seizure of physical devices, while a subpoena or court order can be used to obtain digital data from third-party providers such as email or social media companies.

4. Execution of the warrant - The warrant must be executed properly to avoid any potential issues with admissibility of evidence in court. The search and seizure must be conducted in a reasonable manner, and any digital evidence must be collected and preserved in a forensically sound manner.

5. Challenges to warrants - If a warrant is challenged in court, it is important to have documentation showing that the warrant was obtained properly and executed in a reasonable manner. This includes documentation of the probable cause for the search, the specific items seized, and the methods used to collect and preserve digital evidence.

In conclusion, obtaining and executing warrants for searches and seizures of digital data is an important aspect of cyber forensic investigations. It is important to follow the legal process and document all aspects of the search to ensure that any evidence collected is admissible in court.
