## Unit 1 - Cyber Crime

In this unit, we will be studying about cyber crime, which refers to criminal activities carried out using computers or the internet. Cyber crime has become a major concern in recent years, with the increasing use of technology and the internet in our daily lives. In this unit, we will be covering the following topics:

1. Introduction to Cyber Crime
    - Definition of cyber crime
    - Types of cyber crime
    - Examples of cyber crime

2. Cyber Criminals
    - Who are cyber criminals?
    - Motivations behind cyber crime
    - Profile of cyber criminals

3. Cyber Attacks
    - What are cyber attacks?
    - Types of cyber attacks
    - Examples of cyber attacks

4. Cyber Security
    - What is cyber security?
    - Importance of cyber security
    - Cyber security measures

5. Cyber Laws and Regulations
    - Overview of cyber laws and regulations
    - Cyber crime laws in different countries
    - Importance of cyber laws and regulations

6. Cyber Crime Investigation
    - Process of cyber crime investigation
    - Techniques used in cyber crime investigation
    - Challenges faced in cyber crime investigation

In order to understand cyber crime, it is important to have a basic understanding of computer networks, internet technologies, and security systems. We will also be covering these topics briefly in this unit. It is important to note that cyber crime is a constantly evolving field, and new types of cyber crimes and attacks are being developed every day. Therefore, it is important to stay updated on the latest trends and developments in cyber security.
