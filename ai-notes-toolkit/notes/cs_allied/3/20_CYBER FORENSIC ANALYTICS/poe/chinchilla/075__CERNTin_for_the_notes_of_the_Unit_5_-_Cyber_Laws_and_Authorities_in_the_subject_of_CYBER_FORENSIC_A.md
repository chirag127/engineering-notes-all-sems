In this unit, we will be discussing cyber laws and authorities related to cyber forensic analytics. Cyber laws are essential in ensuring that individuals and organizations are held accountable for their actions in the digital world. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

1. Cyber laws refer to the body of laws that govern the use of electronic communication technologies, such as computers, smartphones, and the internet.

2. Cyber laws are designed to protect individuals, businesses, and governments from cyber threats such as hacking, cybercrime, and cyberterrorism.

3. Cyber laws cover a broad range of topics, including privacy, intellectual property, and data protection.

4. The most important cyber laws that you should be aware of include the following:

   - Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Philippines)
   - Computer Misuse Act 1990 (United Kingdom)
   - Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Singapore)
   - Cybersecurity Information Sharing Act (United States)

5. Cyber authorities are responsible for enforcing cyber laws and investigating cybercrime. Some of the key cyber authorities include:

   - Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) (United States)
   - National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) (United Kingdom)
   - Cyber Security Agency (CSA) (Singapore)
   - National Bureau of Investigation (NBI) (Philippines)

6. Cyber forensic analytics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving electronic data to investigate cybercrime. It involves using specialized tools and techniques to extract digital evidence from electronic devices.

7. The key steps in cyber forensic analytics include the following:

   - Identification of evidence
   - Collection of evidence
   - Preservation of evidence
   - Analysis of evidence
   - Presentation of evidence

8. Cyber forensic analysts play a critical role in investigating cybercrime and assisting law enforcement agencies. They require a combination of technical and analytical skills to be able to analyze digital evidence accurately.

9. Cyber forensic analysts should also have a sound understanding of cyber laws and authorities to ensure that their investigations are conducted within legal boundaries.

In conclusion, understanding cyber laws and authorities is essential in the field of cyber forensic analytics. It is important to be aware of the key cyber laws and authorities and the role they play in protecting individuals, businesses, and governments from cyber threats. Cyber forensic analysts should also have a thorough understanding of cyber laws and authorities to ensure that their investigations are conducted within legal boundaries.
