
### Evidence for the Notes of Unit 4 - Evidence Management in CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Evidence is the foundation of any successful investigation. It is the material facts which are collected and used to support or refute a theory of a case.

2. Evidence can be physical, digital or testimonial. Physical evidence includes items such as documents, clothing, weapons, and any other object that may provide clues to the crime. Digital evidence includes data stored on computers, servers, and other electronic devices. Testimonial evidence is information provided by witnesses or victims.

3. The process of evidence management involves collecting, preserving, analyzing, and presenting evidence in a way that is legally admissible and reliable.

4. It is important to remember that evidence must be handled carefully to ensure its integrity. This means that evidence must be collected and stored in a manner that prevents contamination or alteration.

5. In order to properly manage evidence, investigators must follow a set of procedures and protocols. This includes documenting the chain of custody, which is a record of who had access to the evidence and when.

6. It is also important to ensure that the evidence is properly stored and secured. This includes storing evidence in a secure location and ensuring that only authorized personnel have access to it.

7. In addition to proper storage, evidence must be properly analyzed. This includes using appropriate techniques to determine the authenticity and relevance of the evidence.

8. Finally, evidence must be presented in a manner that is legally admissible. This includes ensuring that the evidence is properly organized and presented in court.
