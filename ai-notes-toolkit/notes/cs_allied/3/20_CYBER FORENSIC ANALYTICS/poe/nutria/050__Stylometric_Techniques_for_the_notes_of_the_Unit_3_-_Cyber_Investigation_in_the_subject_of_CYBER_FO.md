
### Stylometric Techniques for Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation

1. Stylometry is the use of linguistic analysis to identify the author of a text. It is used in cyber forensics to determine the origin of a document or a message.

2. The most common techniques used in stylometry are word frequency analysis, character n-gram analysis, and syntactic analysis.

3. Word frequency analysis looks at the frequency of words used in a text to identify the author. It can be used to compare two texts to determine if they are written by the same author.

4. Character n-gram analysis looks at the sequence of characters used in a text to identify the author. It is used to compare two texts to determine if they are written by the same author.

5. Syntactic analysis looks at the structure of sentences used in a text to identify the author. It can be used to compare two texts to determine if they are written by the same author.

6. Stylometry can also be used to compare different versions of the same text to determine if they are written by the same author. This can be used to identify plagiarism or to determine if a document has been altered.

7. Stylometry can also be used to identify the author of anonymous documents or messages. It can be used to identify the author of a document or message without knowing the author's identity.
