
### On Scene activities for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. It is important to establish a secure chain of custody for any evidence collected at a crime scene. This means that all personnel involved with the evidence must be identified and all actions taken with the evidence must be documented.

2. To ensure that evidence is not compromised, all personnel involved in collecting and handling evidence must wear protective clothing, such as gloves and masks.

3. All evidence must be carefully packaged and labeled in order to maintain its integrity. This includes labeling the evidence with the date and time of collection, the name of the person collecting it, and a description of the evidence.

4. All evidence must be stored in a secure location and kept away from unauthorized personnel.

5. It is important to document all activities related to the collection of evidence, including photographs, sketches, and notes.

6. All evidence must be documented and analyzed in a laboratory setting. This includes photographing and examining the evidence, as well as conducting any necessary tests.

7. All evidence must be securely stored in an evidence locker until it is needed for court proceedings.
