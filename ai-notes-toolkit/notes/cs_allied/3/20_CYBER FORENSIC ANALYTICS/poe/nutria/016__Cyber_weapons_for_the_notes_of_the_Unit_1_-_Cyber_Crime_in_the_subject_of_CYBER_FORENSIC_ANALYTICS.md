
### Cyber Weapons 

1. Cyber weapons are malicious software and hardware tools used to facilitate cyber crime. Examples of cyber weapons include malware, ransomware, distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks, and botnets. 
2. Malware is a type of malicious software designed to damage or disrupt a computer system. It can be used to gain access to confidential data, steal money, or cause destruction.
3. Ransomware is a type of malicious software used to extort money from victims. It works by encrypting files on a victim's computer and demanding payment for the decryption key.
4. DDoS attacks are a type of cyber attack that uses multiple computers to send large amounts of traffic to a target website or server in order to overwhelm it and cause it to crash.
5. Botnets are networks of computers that have been infected with malicious software and can be used to launch cyber attacks. They are often used to send spam or launch DDoS attacks.
