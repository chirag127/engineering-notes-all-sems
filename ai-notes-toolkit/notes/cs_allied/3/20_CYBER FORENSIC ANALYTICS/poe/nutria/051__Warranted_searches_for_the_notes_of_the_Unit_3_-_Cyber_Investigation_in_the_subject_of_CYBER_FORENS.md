
### Warranted Searches for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the Subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

* A **warranted search** is a legal search of a person’s property or premises conducted by law enforcement or other government officials.
* A warrant must be obtained from a court before a search can be conducted. The warrant must specify the type of search to be conducted and the items to be searched.
* A warrant may be issued for a variety of reasons, including suspicion of criminal activity, the seizure of evidence, and the investigation of a crime.
* Warranted searches are conducted in cases of cyber investigations. In such cases, law enforcement or other government officials must obtain a warrant from a court before they can search a person’s electronic devices or premises for evidence of a crime.
* Warranted searches are conducted in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations of the jurisdiction in which the search is being conducted.
* Warranted searches must be conducted in a manner that is respectful of the individual’s rights and privacy.
* Warranted searches must be conducted in a manner that is consistent with the principles of due process and the rule of law.
