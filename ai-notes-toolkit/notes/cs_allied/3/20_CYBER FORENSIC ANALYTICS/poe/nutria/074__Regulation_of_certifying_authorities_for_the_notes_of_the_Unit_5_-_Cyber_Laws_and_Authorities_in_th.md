
### Regulation of Certifying Authorities

1. The certifying authorities are responsible for issuing digital certificates to individuals and organizations.
2. The certifying authorities are regulated by the Information Technology Act, 2000.
3. The certifying authorities must be registered with the Controller of Certifying Authorities (CCA).
4. The CCA is responsible for the registration, monitoring and enforcement of the certifying authorities.
5. The certifying authorities must comply with the rules and regulations laid down by the CCA.
6. The certifying authorities must also ensure that the digital certificates issued to individuals and organizations are secure and reliable.
7. The certifying authorities must also ensure that the digital certificates are not misused or abused.
8. The certifying authorities must also ensure that the digital certificates are not used for any illegal or unauthorized activities.
