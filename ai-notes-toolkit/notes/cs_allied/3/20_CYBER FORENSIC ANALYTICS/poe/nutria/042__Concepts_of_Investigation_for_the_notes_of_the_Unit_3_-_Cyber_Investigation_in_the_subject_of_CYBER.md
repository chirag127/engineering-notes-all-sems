
### Concepts of Investigation for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Investigation Planning: Planning an investigation requires a systematic approach to ensure that all necessary steps are taken to properly investigate a case. It involves determining the scope of the investigation, the goals and objectives of the investigation, the resources needed to complete the investigation, and the timeline for completing the investigation.

2. Evidence Collection: Collecting evidence is a critical step in any investigation. Evidence can be collected from a variety of sources such as digital devices, paper documents, and witness statements. The evidence must be collected in a manner that preserves its integrity and admissibility in court.

3. Evidence Analysis: Once the evidence has been collected, it must be analyzed to determine its relevance to the investigation. This includes determining the origin of the evidence, its authenticity, and its potential use in the investigation.

4. Investigative Reporting: The results of the investigation must be documented in an investigative report. The report must include all relevant evidence, the findings of the investigation, and any recommendations for further action.

5. Investigative Interviews: Interviewing witnesses and suspects is a critical part of any investigation. Interviews must be conducted in a professional and ethical manner, and all relevant information must be documented.
