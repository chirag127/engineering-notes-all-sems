
### Digital Signature for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Cyber Laws and Authorities in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

1. A digital signature is an electronic form of authentication that can be used to verify the identity of the sender of a message or the signer of a document.

2. Digital signatures are created using a combination of public and private key cryptography, where a unique digital signature is generated from the private key of the signer and can be verified using the public key.

3. Digital signatures are often used to authenticate documents like contracts, invoices, and other legal documents.

4. Digital signatures are also used to authenticate the identity of the sender of a message or the signer of a document.

5. In the context of cyber forensic analytics, digital signatures are used to authenticate the identity of the sender of a message or the signer of a document, as well as to verify the integrity of the message or document.

6. Digital signatures can also be used to verify the authenticity of digital evidence collected during a cyber forensic investigation.

7. Cyber laws and authorities are responsible for enforcing laws and regulations related to the use of digital signatures and other forms of authentication.
