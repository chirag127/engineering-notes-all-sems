
### DDoS Attacks

1. A Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack is a type of cyber attack that attempts to make a network or system unavailable by flooding it with malicious traffic.
2. DDoS attacks are commonly used to target websites and other online services, but they can also be used to attack other types of networks, such as gaming networks and financial networks.
3. DDoS attacks can be carried out in several ways, including using botnets, exploiting vulnerabilities in the target system, and using spoofed IP addresses.
4. DDoS attacks can be difficult to detect and mitigate, as they can come from multiple sources and vary in size and complexity.
5. It is important for organizations to have a DDoS protection plan in place in order to protect their networks and systems from such attacks.
6. Common DDoS protection measures include rate limiting, traffic scrubbing, and using a content delivery network (CDN).
