
### Digital device for the notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Cyber forensics is a branch of digital forensics that focuses on the investigation, analysis, and preservation of digital evidence.
2. Digital devices are the physical components of a computer system, such as the motherboard, processor, memory, hard drive, and other components.
3. Digital forensics involves the recovery and analysis of data stored on digital devices, such as computers, phones, and tablets.
4. In order to conduct a successful investigation, a digital forensics analyst must be familiar with the different types of digital devices, their operating systems, and the data they contain.
5. Digital forensics analysts must also be familiar with the various methods of data acquisition, such as imaging, logging, and data carving.
6. It is also important for digital forensics analysts to understand the different types of digital evidence, such as system logs, application data, and file system data.
7. Digital forensics analysts must also be familiar with the different types of evidence analysis, such as timeline analysis, keyword searches, and hash analysis.
8. Finally, digital forensics analysts must be familiar with the different types of evidence preservation, such as write-blocking and encryption.
