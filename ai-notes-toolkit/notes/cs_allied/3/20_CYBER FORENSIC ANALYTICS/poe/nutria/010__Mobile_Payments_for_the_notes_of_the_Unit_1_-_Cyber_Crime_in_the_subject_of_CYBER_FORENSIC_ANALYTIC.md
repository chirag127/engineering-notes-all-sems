
### Mobile Payments for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Mobile payments refer to the use of mobile phones or other mobile devices to purchase goods and services, transfer money, and make payments.

2. Mobile payments are typically made through an app, website, or other online platform. They can also be made through text messages, QR codes, and Near Field Communication (NFC) technology.

3. Mobile payments are becoming increasingly popular due to their convenience, ease of use, and security.

4. Cyber crime is any criminal activity that involves the use of computers, networks, or the internet.

5. Cyber criminals can use mobile payments to commit various types of fraud, such as identity theft, phishing, and money laundering.

6. To protect against cyber crime, it is important to use secure mobile payment services, such as those provided by banks and other financial institutions.

7. It is also important to use strong passwords, two-factor authentication, and other security measures to protect your mobile payments from cyber criminals.
