
### Denial of Service (DoS)

* Denial of Service (DoS) is an attack on a computer system or network that causes a loss of service to users, typically by flooding the target with traffic.
* DoS attacks can be carried out in a variety of ways, including using malware, exploiting security vulnerabilities, and using botnets.
* DoS attacks are often used to disrupt critical services, such as banking or e-commerce websites, or to extort money from companies.
* To protect against DoS attacks, organizations should employ a variety of security measures, including firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and rate-limiting techniques.
