
### Virus and Worms

* Viruses are malicious computer programs that are designed to replicate themselves and spread from one computer to another. They can cause damage to files and system performance.

* Worms are similar to viruses in that they are malicious programs that spread from one computer to another. However, worms do not require a host file to replicate, and they can spread rapidly across a network.

* Both viruses and worms can be used to gain access to a computer system or network, and can be used to launch attacks against other systems.

* Cyber forensic analysts use a variety of techniques to detect and analyze viruses and worms, including reverse engineering, malware analysis, and network traffic analysis.

* Cyber forensic analysts also use techniques such as digital forensics and incident response to investigate the effects of viruses and worms on a system.
