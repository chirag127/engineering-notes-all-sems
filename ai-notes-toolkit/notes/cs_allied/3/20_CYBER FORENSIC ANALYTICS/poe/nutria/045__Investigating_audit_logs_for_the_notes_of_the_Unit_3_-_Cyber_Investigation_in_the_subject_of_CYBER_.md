

### Investigating audit logs for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Audit logs are records of activity that occur on a computer system. They can be used to track user activities, system events, and security-related events. 
2. Cyber investigations often involve the analysis of audit logs to identify suspicious activity or to trace the actions of malicious actors.
3. Audit logs can provide useful information about the time of an event, the user who initiated the event, and the system or application that was affected.
4. Auditing can be used to detect unauthorized access, detect malicious activity, or provide evidence for a criminal investigation.
5. It is important to understand the different types of audit logs, such as system logs, application logs, and security logs, and how they can be used in a cyber investigation.
6. It is also important to understand the different types of audit log analysis, such as log correlation, log analysis, and log review.
7. It is essential to have a comprehensive understanding of the audit logs and the tools available to analyze them.
8. It is important to understand the legal implications of using audit logs in a cyber investigation.
