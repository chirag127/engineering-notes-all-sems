
### Soft Lifting for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the Subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Soft lifting is a technique used in cyber forensics to analyze digital evidence. It involves analyzing data without changing or altering its original form.

2. Soft lifting is a non-destructive process, meaning that the original data is not changed or destroyed in any way.

3. Soft lifting is used to identify patterns and trends in digital evidence. It can be used to uncover hidden information and uncover malicious behavior.

4. Soft lifting can be used to identify the source of a digital file, as well as the time and date it was created or modified.

5. Soft lifting can also be used to identify the type of file, such as an image, video, or audio file.

6. Soft lifting can be used to identify the operating system and software used to create or modify the file.

7. Soft lifting can be used to identify the user who created or modified the file.

8. Soft lifting can be used to uncover malicious activity, such as hacking, phishing, and malware.

9. Soft lifting is an important tool in cyber forensics, as it can provide valuable evidence to help identify and prosecute cyber criminals.
