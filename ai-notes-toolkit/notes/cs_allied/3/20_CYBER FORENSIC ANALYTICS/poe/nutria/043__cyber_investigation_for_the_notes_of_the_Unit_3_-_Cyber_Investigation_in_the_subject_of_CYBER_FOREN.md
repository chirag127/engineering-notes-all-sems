
### Cyber Investigation

1. Cyber investigation is the process of gathering, analyzing, and preserving digital evidence related to cybercrime.
2. Cyber investigators use a variety of methods and tools to identify, collect, and analyze digital evidence, including computer forensics, network forensics, and malware analysis.
3. Cyber investigation is a critical component of cyber security, as it helps to identify the perpetrators of cybercrime and protect organizations from future attacks.
4. Cyber investigators must be familiar with the various types of digital evidence, including files, emails, webpages, and other digital artifacts.
5. Cyber investigators must also be familiar with the various tools used to analyze digital evidence, including forensic software and network analysis tools.
6. Cyber investigators must also be familiar with the legal aspects of cyber investigation, including laws related to privacy and data protection.
7. Cyber investigation is a complex and rapidly evolving field, and cyber investigators must stay up to date on the latest developments in order to effectively protect organizations from cybercrime.
