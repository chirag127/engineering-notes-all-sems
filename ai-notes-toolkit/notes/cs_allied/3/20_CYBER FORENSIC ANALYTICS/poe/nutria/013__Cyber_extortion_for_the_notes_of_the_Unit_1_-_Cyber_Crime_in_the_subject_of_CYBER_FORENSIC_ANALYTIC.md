
### Cyber Extortion 

1. Cyber extortion is a type of cyber crime that involves the use of computer networks to threaten, coerce, and extort money or sensitive information from victims. 
2. It can involve blackmailing victims with the threat of releasing sensitive information or damaging their reputation, or threatening to disrupt their computer systems or data. 
3. Cyber extortion can take many forms, such as ransomware, denial of service attacks, and phishing. 
4. Victims of cyber extortion may be individuals, businesses, or organizations. 
5. Cyber extortion is a serious crime that can have devastating consequences for victims, including financial loss, reputational damage, and emotional distress. 
6. To protect against cyber extortion, it is important to have strong security measures in place, including antivirus software, firewalls, and data encryption. 
7. It is also important to be aware of the signs of cyber extortion, such as suspicious emails or messages, requests for payment, and sudden changes to computer systems. 
8. If you believe you are a victim of cyber extortion, it is important to contact law enforcement immediately.
