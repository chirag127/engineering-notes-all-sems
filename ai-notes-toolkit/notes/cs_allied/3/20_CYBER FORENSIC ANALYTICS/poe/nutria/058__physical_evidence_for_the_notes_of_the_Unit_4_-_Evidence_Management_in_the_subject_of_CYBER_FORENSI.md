4. Evidence management also involves the use of specialized tools and techniques to analyze the evidence. This includes the use of forensic tools such as EnCase, FTK, and X-Ways.

5. It is also important to note that physical evidence must be stored securely in order to prevent tampering or destruction. This includes the use of secure storage facilities and the use of encryption to protect the evidence.
