
### Stored Values Cards for the notes of the Unit 1 - Cyber Crime in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Stored Value Cards (SVCs) are prepaid cards that store a certain amount of money, which can be used to purchase goods and services. 
2. SVCs are convenient for users as they provide access to money without the need for a bank account or credit card. 
3. SVCs are also popular for online purchases, as they can be used to make payments quickly and securely. 
4. However, SVCs can also be used for cyber crime, as criminals can use them to launder money or to purchase goods and services anonymously. 
5. Cyber criminals can also use SVCs to purchase stolen goods and services, or to purchase illegal services such as hacking tools. 
6. Cyber forensic analysts must be aware of the potential risks associated with SVCs and be able to identify suspicious activity related to their use. 
7. Analysts must also have the ability to trace the origin of SVCs and identify the parties involved in their use. 
8. In addition, analysts must be able to identify and analyze evidence related to SVCs and use this information to build a case against cyber criminals.
