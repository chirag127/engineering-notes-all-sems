
### Contextual Information for the notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. Cyber forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting digital evidence in a manner that is legally admissible. 
2. Digital evidence can be used to investigate a variety of cyber crimes, including fraud, identity theft, and intellectual property theft. 
3. Evidence management is the process of handling digital evidence in a way that is secure, reliable, and consistent with legal requirements. 
4. Digital evidence must be collected and stored properly in order to be admissible in court. 
5. Evidence must also be protected from tampering or alteration. 
6. Evidence management includes the use of tools such as digital forensics software and hardware, as well as policies and procedures for handling digital evidence. 
7. Digital forensics investigators must be trained in the proper use of evidence management tools and techniques. 
8. Digital evidence must be preserved in its original form and must not be tampered with or altered in any way. 
9. Digital evidence must be properly documented and stored in a secure location. 
10. Evidence must be kept secure from unauthorized access and must be kept in a manner that is consistent with legal requirements.
