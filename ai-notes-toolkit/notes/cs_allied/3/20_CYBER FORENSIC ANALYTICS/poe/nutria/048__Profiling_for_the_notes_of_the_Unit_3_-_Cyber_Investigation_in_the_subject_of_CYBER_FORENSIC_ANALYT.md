
### Profiling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Cyber Investigation in the subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

* Profiling is the process of gathering information about a person or group of people in order to better understand their behavior and motivations. 
* In the context of cyber investigations, profiling is used to identify the threat actor responsible for a particular attack. 
* It involves gathering data from various sources such as network logs, system logs, social media accounts, and other online sources. 
* This data is then analyzed to determine the attacker’s motives, methods, and capabilities. 
* Profiling can also be used to identify potential targets of future attacks, as well as to identify indicators of compromise (IOCs) that can be used to detect and mitigate future attacks.
