
### Trojans and Backdoors

* A Trojan is a type of malicious software that is disguised as legitimate software. It is designed to gain access to a user's system without their knowledge or consent.

* A backdoor is a way to gain access to a system or program that bypasses the normal authentication process. Backdoors are often installed by malicious actors to gain access to a computer or network without authorization.

* Both Trojans and backdoors can be used to gain access to a system or program without the user's knowledge or consent.

* Trojans and backdoors are commonly used by attackers to gain access to a system or program, steal data, or install malicious software.

* To protect against Trojans and backdoors, users should ensure that their systems are up-to-date with the latest security patches and antivirus software. Additionally, users should be aware of any suspicious activity on their systems, such as unexpected pop-ups or changes in system performance.
