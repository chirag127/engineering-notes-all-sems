
### Pcap Analysis for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Cyber Forensics in the Subject of CYBER FORENSIC ANALYTICS

1. A PCAP (Packet Capture) is a file that contains a snapshot of all the network traffic that occurred on a network at a specific point in time. 
2. PCAP analysis is the process of analyzing the contents of a PCAP file to identify any malicious or suspicious activity.
3. PCAP analysis can be used to investigate a variety of cyber threats, including malware, phishing, and DDoS attacks.
4. PCAP analysis is often used by cyber forensic analysts to examine the evidence of a cyber attack.
5. PCAP analysis can be used to identify the source of an attack, the type of attack, and the methods used to carry out the attack.
6. PCAP analysis can also be used to identify any malicious payloads, such as malware or ransomware, that may have been delivered to the target system.
7. PCAP analysis is a critical component of any cyber forensic investigation and is an important tool for cyber security professionals.
