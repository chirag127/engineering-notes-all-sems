
### Network Evidence for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Evidence Management in the Subject of Cyber Forensic Analytics

* Network evidence is any type of digital evidence that is found on a network or in a networked environment.
* Network evidence can include communications between users, user activities, user profiles, and any other information that is stored on a network or transferred across a network.
* Network evidence can also include digital artifacts such as files, emails, and other digital objects.
* Network evidence can be used to identify the source of a digital crime, to establish a timeline of events, and to uncover evidence of criminal activity.
* Network forensics is the process of collecting, analyzing, and preserving digital evidence found in a networked environment.
* Network forensics is used to investigate cyber crimes, identify malicious activity, and reconstruct events that have occurred on a network.
* Network forensics tools can be used to capture network traffic, analyze network logs, and uncover evidence of malicious activity.
* Network forensics analysts must be knowledgeable about network security, network protocols, and network forensics tools.
