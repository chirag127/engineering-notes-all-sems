### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Knowledge-based systems (KBS) are computer programs that reason and use a knowledge base to solve complex problems.
2. The term "knowledge-based system" is often used to refer to any program that relies on a knowledge base to provide solutions to complex problems.
3. A knowledge base is a collection of facts, rules, and heuristics that the system uses to reason and make decisions.
4. Knowledge-based systems are a type of artificial intelligence (AI) that use a knowledge base to provide solutions to complex problems.
5. Knowledge-based systems are used in a variety of applications, including expert systems, decision support systems, and intelligent tutoring systems.
6. Knowledge-based systems are designed to mimic the problem-solving abilities of human experts in a specific domain.
7. Knowledge-based systems are often used in situations where human expertise is not readily available or where the problem is too complex for a human to solve.
8. Knowledge-based systems can be used to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of decision-making processes.
9. Knowledge-based systems can be used to provide personalized recommendations and advice to users.
10. Knowledge-based systems can be used to automate complex and time-consuming tasks.
