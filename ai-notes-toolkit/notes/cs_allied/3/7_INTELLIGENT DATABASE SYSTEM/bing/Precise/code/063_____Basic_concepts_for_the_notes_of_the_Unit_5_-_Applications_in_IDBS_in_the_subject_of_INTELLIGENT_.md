### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. **Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS)**: An intelligent database system is a database system that uses artificial intelligence techniques to provide intelligent responses to complex queries.
2. **Applications of IDBS**: IDBS can be used in a variety of applications, including decision support systems, expert systems, and data mining.
3. **Decision Support Systems**: Decision support systems are computer-based systems that help decision-makers to make better decisions by providing relevant information and analysis.
4. **Expert Systems**: Expert systems are computer programs that mimic the decision-making abilities of a human expert in a specific domain.
5. **Data Mining**: Data mining is the process of discovering patterns and knowledge from large amounts of data. IDBS can be used to perform data mining tasks, such as classification, clustering, and association rule mining.
6. **Classification**: Classification is the process of assigning a data instance to one of several predefined classes.
7. **Clustering**: Clustering is the process of grouping similar data instances together.
8. **Association Rule Mining**: Association rule mining is the process of discovering relationships between variables in large datasets.
