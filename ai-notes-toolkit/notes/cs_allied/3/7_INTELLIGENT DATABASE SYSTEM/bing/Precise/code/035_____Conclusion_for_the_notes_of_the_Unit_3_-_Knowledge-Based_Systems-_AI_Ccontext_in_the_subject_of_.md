### Conclusion for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- Knowledge-based systems are a type of AI system that uses a knowledge base to solve problems.
- The knowledge base contains facts and rules that represent the knowledge of the domain.
- Inference engines are used to apply the rules to the facts to draw conclusions and make decisions.
- Knowledge-based systems can be used in a variety of applications, including expert systems, decision support systems, and intelligent tutoring systems.
- The development of knowledge-based systems involves knowledge acquisition, knowledge representation, and knowledge manipulation.
- Knowledge-based systems have the potential to improve decision-making and problem-solving in many domains. However, they also have limitations and challenges, such as the difficulty of acquiring and representing knowledge, and the need for ongoing maintenance and updating of the knowledge base.
