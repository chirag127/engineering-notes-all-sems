### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Semantic data models are high-level data models that capture the meaning of data within the context of its interrelationships with other data.
2. They are used to represent the data in a way that is closer to the way humans perceive and understand data.
3. Semantic data models are often used in the design of databases, where they serve as a blueprint for the construction of the database schema.
4. They are also used in the development of knowledge-based systems, where they provide a means of representing the knowledge contained within the system.
5. Semantic data models are based on concepts such as entities, attributes, and relationships.
6. Entities represent objects or concepts in the real world, attributes represent the properties of these entities, and relationships represent the associations between entities.
7. Semantic data models provide a higher level of abstraction than other data models, such as the relational data model or the entity-relationship data model.
8. This higher level of abstraction makes it easier to design and maintain the database, as well as to understand and use the data contained within it.
9. There are several types of semantic data models, including the functional data model, the object-oriented data model, and the binary data model.
10. Each of these types of semantic data models has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of which type to use depends on the specific requirements of the system being developed.
