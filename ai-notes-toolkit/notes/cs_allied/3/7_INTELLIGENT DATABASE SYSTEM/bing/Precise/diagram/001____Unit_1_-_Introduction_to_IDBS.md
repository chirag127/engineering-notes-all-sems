## Unit 1 - Introduction to IDBS

1. IDBS stands for Integrated Data and Business Systems.
2. IDBS is a software company that provides data management and analytics solutions for research and development organizations.
3. The company's products are designed to help scientists and researchers capture, store, analyze, and share data.
4. IDBS was founded in 1989 and is headquartered in Guildford, United Kingdom.
5. The company's solutions are used in various industries, including pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, and chemicals.
6. IDBS's products include E-WorkBook, a cloud-based electronic laboratory notebook, and ActivityBase, a data management and analysis platform for high-throughput screening.
7. The company's solutions are designed to improve collaboration, data integrity, and compliance with regulatory requirements.
8. IDBS has a global customer base and has offices in the United States, Europe, and Asia.
