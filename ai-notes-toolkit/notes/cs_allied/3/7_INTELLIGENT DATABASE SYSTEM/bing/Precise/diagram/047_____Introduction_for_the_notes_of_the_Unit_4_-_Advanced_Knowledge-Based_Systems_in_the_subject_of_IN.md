### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems (AKBS) are computer programs that use artificial intelligence to solve complex problems.
- These systems are designed to mimic the problem-solving abilities of human experts in a particular domain.
- AKBS can be used to solve problems in a wide range of fields, including medicine, finance, and engineering.
- These systems use a variety of techniques, including rule-based systems, case-based reasoning, and machine learning, to solve problems.
- AKBS can be integrated with databases to provide intelligent data analysis and decision-making capabilities.
- In this unit, we will explore the principles and techniques used in the design and implementation of AKBS, and their applications in various domains.
