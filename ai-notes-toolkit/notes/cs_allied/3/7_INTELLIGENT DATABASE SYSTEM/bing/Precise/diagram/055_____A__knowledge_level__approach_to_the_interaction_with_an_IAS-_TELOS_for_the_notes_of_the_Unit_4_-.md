### A 'knowledge level' approach to the interaction with an IAS- TELOS for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- A 'knowledge level' approach to the interaction with an IAS-TELOS refers to the use of a high-level representation of knowledge to facilitate communication between the user and the system.
- TELOS is a knowledge representation language that allows for the representation of complex knowledge structures, including objects, relationships, and rules.
- By using a knowledge level approach, the user can interact with the system in a more natural and intuitive way, as the system is able to understand and reason about the user's requests and commands at a higher level of abstraction.
- This approach can improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the interaction, as the system is able to provide more relevant and accurate responses to the user's queries and commands.
- In the context of advanced knowledge-based systems, the use of a knowledge level approach can also facilitate the integration of multiple sources of knowledge and the development of more sophisticated reasoning capabilities.
