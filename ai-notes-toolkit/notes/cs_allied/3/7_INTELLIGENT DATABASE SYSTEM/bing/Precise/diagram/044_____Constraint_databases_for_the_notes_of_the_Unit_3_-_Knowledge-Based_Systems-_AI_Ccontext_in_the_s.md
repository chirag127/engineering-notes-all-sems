### Constraint databases for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- Constraint databases provide a new view of classic (linear and nonlinear) optimization theory and are especially suited for applications in which possibly infinite sets of continuous data, which have a geometric interpretation, have to be stored in a computer.
- They bring together techniques from a variety of fields, such as logic and model theory, algebraic and computational geometry, and symbolic computation, to the design and analysis of data models and query languages.
- Database constraints are a key feature of database management systems. They ensure that rules defined at data model creation are enforced when the data is manipulated (inserted, updated, or deleted) in a database.
- Constraints allow us to rely on the database to ensure integrity, accuracy, and reliability of the data stored in it.
- Constraint databases have become a relatively mainstream database research topic, with practical as well as theoretical motivations.
- Relational databases are rooted in First Order Logic and Finite Model Theory, as initially envisioned by Codd.
