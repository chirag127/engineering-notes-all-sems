### Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS: Internet Indexing and Retrieval

- Internet indexing and retrieval is the process of organizing and retrieving information from the World Wide Web.
- Search engines use web crawlers to index the content of web pages and store the information in a database.
- When a user enters a query, the search engine retrieves relevant information from its database and presents it to the user.
- The relevance of the information is determined by algorithms that take into account factors such as the content of the web page, the number of times the query terms appear on the page, and the number of links to the page from other sites.
- The process of indexing and retrieval is constantly evolving as search engines strive to improve the accuracy and relevance of their results.
- Some of the challenges faced by search engines include dealing with large amounts of data, handling ambiguous queries, and addressing issues of privacy and security.
- Internet indexing and retrieval is an important application of intelligent database systems, as it allows users to quickly and easily access the vast amount of information available on the web.
