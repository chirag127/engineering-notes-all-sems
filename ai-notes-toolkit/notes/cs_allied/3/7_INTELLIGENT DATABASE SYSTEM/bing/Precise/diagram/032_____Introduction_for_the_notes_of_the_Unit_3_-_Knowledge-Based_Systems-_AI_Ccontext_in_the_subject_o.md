### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Knowledge-based systems (KBS) are computer programs that reason and use a knowledge base to solve complex problems.
2. A knowledge-based system is an artificial intelligence (AI) program that simulates the problem-solving capabilities of a human expert.
3. The primary components of a knowledge-based system are the knowledge base and the inference engine.
4. The knowledge base contains facts, rules, and heuristics about the domain of interest.
5. The inference engine applies logical rules to the knowledge base to deduce new information and draw conclusions.
6. Knowledge-based systems are used in a variety of applications, including expert systems, decision support systems, and intelligent tutoring systems.
7. In the context of intelligent database systems, knowledge-based systems can be used to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of data retrieval and analysis.
