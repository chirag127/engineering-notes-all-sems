### Data mining tasks

- Data mining tasks are the operations that can be performed on the data to extract useful patterns, insights, and knowledge .
- Data mining tasks can be classified into two types: **descriptive** and **predictive**.
  - Descriptive mining tasks define the common features of the data in the database, such as groups, clusters, summaries, associations, and sequential patterns.
  - Predictive mining tasks act on the current information to make predictions about future or unknown events, such as classification, regression, and anomaly detection.
- A data mining system can execute one or more of the above specified tasks as part of data mining.
- Data mining tasks are designed to be semi-automatic or fully automatic and on large data sets to uncover patterns that are not easily detectable by human analysts.
- Data mining tasks are often guided by the business objectives and the data availability of the organization.
- Data mining tasks require a data mining process that involves data preparation, model building, pattern evaluation, and knowledge presentation .
- Data mining tasks can be applied to various domains and industries, such as marketing, finance, healthcare, education, security, and social media .
