### Lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs

- A lexical approach is a method of language teaching and learning that focuses on words and word combinations as the basic units of meaning and communication .
- A large KB (knowledge base) is a collection of facts, rules, and relations that represent the knowledge of a domain or a system.
- Lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs aim to extract and organize the relevant lexical information from natural language texts or other sources, such as corpora, ontologies, or databases .
- Some of the advantages of lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs are:
  - They can capture the richness and variety of natural language expressions and their semantic and pragmatic nuances.
  - They can leverage existing lexical resources, such as dictionaries, thesauri, or wordnets, to enrich and validate the extracted knowledge.
  - They can facilitate the integration and interoperability of different KBs by using common lexical standards and formats.
- Some of the challenges of lexical approaches to the construction of large KBs are:
  - They require sophisticated natural language processing techniques, such as parsing, disambiguation, or semantic analysis, to handle the complexity and ambiguity of natural language texts .
  - They need to deal with the issues of lexical coverage, consistency, and quality, as well as the trade-off between generality and specificity of the extracted knowledge .
  - They have to cope with the dynamic and evolving nature of natural language and knowledge, and the need for updating and maintaining the KBs .
