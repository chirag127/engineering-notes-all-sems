### Basic concepts for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- An **intelligent database system (IDBS)** is a system that manages information (rather than data) in a way that appears natural and informative for users and that goes beyond simple record keeping.
- An IDBS consists of three levels of intelligence: high level tools, the user interface and the database engine.
- The **high level tools** manage data quality and automatically discover relevant patterns in the data with a process called **data mining**.
- The **user interface** provides natural language processing, speech recognition, graphical visualization and multimedia capabilities to facilitate human-computer interaction.
- The **database engine** incorporates artificial intelligence techniques such as rule-based systems, neural networks, fuzzy logic and genetic algorithms to support complex queries, reasoning and learning.
- Some of the applications of IDBS are:
  - **Business intelligence**: IDBS can help businesses analyze data from various sources and provide insights for decision making, forecasting, optimization and risk management.
  - **Healthcare**: IDBS can help healthcare providers and researchers access, integrate and analyze medical data from electronic health records, clinical trials, genomic databases and other sources.
  - **Education**: IDBS can help educators and learners access, organize and personalize educational content from various sources and formats, such as textbooks, videos, podcasts and online courses.
  - **Social media**: IDBS can help users and advertisers understand and influence the behavior and preferences of social media users by analyzing data from posts, comments, likes, shares and other interactions.
  - **Internet of things**: IDBS can help manage and optimize the performance and security of interconnected devices and sensors by collecting, processing and analyzing data from various sources and formats, such as images, videos, audio and text.
