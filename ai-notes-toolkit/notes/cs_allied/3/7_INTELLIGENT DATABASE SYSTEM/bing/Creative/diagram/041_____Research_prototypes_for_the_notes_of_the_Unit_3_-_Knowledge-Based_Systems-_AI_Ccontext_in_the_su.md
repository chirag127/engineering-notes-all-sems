### Research prototypes for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

- A research prototype is a preliminary version of a system that is used to test and evaluate its feasibility, functionality, performance, and usability in a specific domain or problem.
- Research prototypes are often used in the field of artificial intelligence (AI) to explore new ideas, methods, algorithms, and architectures for knowledge-based systems (KBS).
- A knowledge-based system is a system that uses explicit representations of knowledge, such as rules, facts, frames, ontologies, or neural networks, to perform tasks that require human expertise or intelligence.
- Knowledge-based systems can be classified into different types, such as expert systems, case-based reasoning systems, natural language processing systems, machine learning systems, and semantic web systems, depending on the type and source of knowledge, the reasoning mechanism, and the application domain.
- Some examples of research prototypes for knowledge-based systems are:

  - Prototypical knowledge for expert systems: This prototype uses frames to represent typical situations that can be used as a basis for comparison to the actual situation given by the data. The prototype aims to improve the performance and robustness of expert systems by using prototypical knowledge as a form of abstraction and generalization.
  - A prototype knowledge-based simulation support system : This prototype uses a rule-based system to provide guidance and feedback to students who are learning how to use simulation software for industrial engineering problems. The prototype aims to enhance the learning experience and outcomes of the students by using knowledge-based techniques to support simulation modeling and analysis.
  - Geometric knowledge-based systems framework for fingerprint image classification: This prototype uses a geometric approach to represent and manipulate fingerprint images based on their features, such as ridges, bifurcations, and endings. The prototype aims to improve the accuracy and efficiency of fingerprint recognition by using geometric knowledge-based methods to classify and match fingerprint images.
  - Knowledge-based systems journal: This is not a prototype, but a journal that publishes original and innovative research results in the field of artificial intelligence and knowledge-based systems. The journal covers various topics and techniques related to knowledge-based and other artificial intelligence-based systems, such as knowledge representation and reasoning, knowledge engineering and management, knowledge discovery and learning, knowledge integration and fusion, knowledge visualization and explanation, and knowledge-based applications and systems.
  - Research prototypes in design research: This is not a prototype, but a paper that discusses the role and types of research prototypes in design research, which is a field that studies how to design and evaluate systems that are useful, usable, and desirable for human users. The paper proposes a framework for categorizing research prototypes based on their purpose, fidelity, audience, and evaluation, and provides examples of research prototypes from different design research projects.
