- The system is based on the idea of "schemas," which are sets of rules that define the structure and behavior of a database.

- This language allows for complex queries and reasoning, making it easier to build more intelligent and sophisticated databases.

- The Starburst Rule System has been used in a variety of applications, including natural language processing, decision support systems, and expert systems.
