### A Taxonomy of Intelligent Database Systems

Intelligent Database Systems (IDBS) are designed to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of data management. These systems use artificial intelligence techniques to enhance the capabilities of traditional database systems. In this unit, we will explore the taxonomy of IDBS.

Here are the different types of IDBS:

1. Knowledge-Based Systems (KBS): These systems are designed to capture and use domain-specific knowledge to support decision-making. They use expert systems to provide recommendations based on the data.

2. Neural Networks: These systems are inspired by the structure and function of the human brain. They are used for data classification, pattern recognition, and prediction.

3. Fuzzy Logic Systems (FLS): These systems are used to deal with uncertainty and imprecision in data. They use fuzzy sets to represent and manipulate uncertain data.

4. Genetic Algorithms (GAs): These systems use evolutionary algorithms to search for optimal solutions to complex problems. They are used for data mining and optimization.

5. Hybrid Systems: These systems combine multiple AI techniques to improve the performance of IDBS. For example, a hybrid system may combine KBS and neural networks to provide more accurate recommendations.

In conclusion, IDBS is a broad field that encompasses a variety of AI techniques. Understanding the taxonomy of IDBS is important for selecting the right system for a particular application.
