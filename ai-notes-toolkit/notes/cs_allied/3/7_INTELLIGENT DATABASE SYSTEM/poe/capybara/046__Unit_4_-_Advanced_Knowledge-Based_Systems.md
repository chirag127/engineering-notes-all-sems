## Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems

In this unit, we will explore the advanced concepts of knowledge-based systems. We will cover the following topics:

1. Rule-based systems:
    - Inference engine
    - Forward chaining
    - Backward chaining
    - Conflict resolution
2. Expert systems:
    - Knowledge acquisition
    - Knowledge representation
    - Inference engine
    - Explanation facility
3. Fuzzy logic:
    - Membership functions
    - Fuzzy sets
    - Fuzzy rules
    - Fuzzy inference
4. Neural networks:
    - Perceptron
    - Multilayer perceptron
    - Backpropagation algorithm
    - Radial basis function networks
5. Genetic algorithms:
    - Selection
    - Crossover
    - Mutation
    - Fitness function
6. Hybrid systems:
    - Integration of different knowledge-based systems
    - Benefits of hybrid systems
    - Challenges in developing hybrid systems

By the end of this unit, you should be able to understand the advanced concepts of knowledge-based systems and their applications. You will also learn how to develop hybrid systems by integrating different knowledge-based systems. Good luck!
