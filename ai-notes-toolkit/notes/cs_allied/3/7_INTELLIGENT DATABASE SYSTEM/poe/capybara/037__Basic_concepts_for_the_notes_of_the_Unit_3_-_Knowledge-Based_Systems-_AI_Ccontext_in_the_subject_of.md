### Basic Concepts for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Context in the Subject of Intelligent Database System

In this unit, you will learn about knowledge-based systems and AI context in the subject of intelligent database system. Here are some basic concepts that you should know:

- **Knowledge-based system:** It is a type of artificial intelligence system that uses knowledge representation to solve complex problems. It consists of a knowledge base, an inference engine, and a user interface. The knowledge base contains domain-specific information, and the inference engine uses logical reasoning to draw conclusions from the information in the knowledge base.

- **Expert system:** It is a type of knowledge-based system that is designed to solve complex problems in a specific domain. It uses a set of rules and a knowledge base to provide advice and recommendations to users.

- **Rule-based system:** It is a type of expert system that uses a set of rules to make decisions. The rules are based on the knowledge of domain experts and are used to provide advice and recommendations.

- **Knowledge representation:** It is the process of representing knowledge in a way that can be used by a computer system. There are different types of knowledge representation, such as semantic networks, frames, and rules.

- **Inference engine:** It is the part of a knowledge-based system that uses logical reasoning to draw conclusions from the information in the knowledge base. It applies the rules and knowledge in the knowledge base to solve problems and provide recommendations.

- **Ontology:** It is a formal representation of knowledge that defines the concepts and relationships within a particular domain. It is used to create a shared understanding of a domain and to facilitate communication among different systems and users.

- **Artificial intelligence:** It is a branch of computer science that focuses on the development of intelligent machines that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as perception, reasoning, and learning.

In conclusion, knowledge-based systems and AI context are essential concepts in the subject of intelligent database system. Understanding these concepts will help you develop intelligent systems that can solve complex problems in different domains.
