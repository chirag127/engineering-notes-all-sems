### Lexical Approaches to the Construction of Large KBs

In the field of Intelligent Database Systems, there are a variety of approaches to constructing large knowledge bases (KBs). One such approach is the use of lexical methods. Here are some key points to consider:

- Lexical methods involve the use of natural language processing (NLP) techniques to extract knowledge from text.
- One common approach is to use a corpus of documents and extract entities and relationships from the text.
- These entities and relationships can then be used to construct a KB that can be queried and used for various applications.
- Another approach is to use lexical resources, such as WordNet or Wikipedia, to build a KB. These resources provide a structured hierarchy of concepts and relationships that can be used to construct a KB.
- These lexical resources can be combined with NLP techniques to extract additional knowledge from text.
- The construction of large KBs using lexical approaches can be time-consuming and resource-intensive, but it can also be highly accurate and effective.
- One challenge with using lexical approaches is dealing with ambiguity and multiple interpretations of text. This can lead to errors and inconsistencies in the resulting KB.
- To mitigate these challenges, it is important to carefully design and validate the lexical methods being used, and to incorporate human expertise and feedback into the construction process.
- Overall, lexical approaches can be a powerful tool for constructing large KBs in the field of Intelligent Database Systems. However, they require careful consideration and validation to ensure accuracy and effectiveness.
