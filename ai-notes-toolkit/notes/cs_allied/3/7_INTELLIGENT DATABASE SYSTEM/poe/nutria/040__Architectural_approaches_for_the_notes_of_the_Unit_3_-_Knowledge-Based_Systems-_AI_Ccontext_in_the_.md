
### Architectural Approaches for Knowledge-Based Systems

1. **Expert Systems**: Expert systems are computer programs that simulate the decision-making ability of a human expert. They are based on an inference engine that processes a knowledge base to generate a solution to a problem.

2. **Neural Networks**: Neural networks are computer programs that simulate the behavior of neurons in the human brain. They are based on a network of interconnected nodes, each of which performs a simple calculation.

3. **Fuzzy Logic**: Fuzzy logic is a form of many-valued logic in which truth values can range between 0 and 1. It is used to model uncertain or imprecise information and can be used to make decisions in situations where there is incomplete or uncertain data.

4. **Genetic Algorithms**: Genetic algorithms are computer programs that simulate the process of natural selection and evolution. They are used to search for solutions to problems by creating a population of solutions and then selecting the best solutions based on a fitness function.

5. **Case-Based Reasoning**: Case-based reasoning is a form of problem solving in which solutions to new problems are found by retrieving and adapting solutions to similar problems from a case base.
