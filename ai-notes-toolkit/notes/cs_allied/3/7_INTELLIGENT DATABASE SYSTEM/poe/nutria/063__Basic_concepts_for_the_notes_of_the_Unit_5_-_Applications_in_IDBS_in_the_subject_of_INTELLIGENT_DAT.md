
### Basic Concepts for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the Subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Intelligent Database System (IDBS): IDBS is a type of database system that uses artificial intelligence (AI) techniques to improve its performance. IDBS can be used for various applications, such as data mining, decision support, and data warehousing.

2. Data Mining: Data mining is the process of uncovering patterns and relationships in large datasets. It involves the use of techniques such as clustering, classification, and regression to identify meaningful patterns and relationships.

3. Decision Support: Decision support systems are computer-based systems that provide users with the information and analysis they need to make decisions. These systems often use AI techniques such as fuzzy logic, neural networks, and genetic algorithms to analyze data.

4. Data Warehousing: Data warehousing is the process of storing and organizing large amounts of data in a centralized repository. This data can then be used for reporting, analysis, and decision-making.

5. Artificial Intelligence (AI): AI is the science of designing computer systems that can solve problems and make decisions. AI techniques are used in IDBS for data mining, decision support, and data warehousing.
