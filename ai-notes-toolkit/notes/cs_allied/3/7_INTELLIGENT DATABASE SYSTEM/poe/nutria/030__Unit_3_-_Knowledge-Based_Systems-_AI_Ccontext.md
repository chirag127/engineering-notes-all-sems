
## Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext

1. Knowledge-based systems are computer systems that are designed to store and reason with knowledge. 
2. Knowledge-based systems are built around a knowledge base that contains facts, rules, and procedures about a particular domain. 
3. AI-based computer systems use knowledge-based systems to draw conclusions from the data they have stored. 
4. Knowledge-based systems are used in a variety of applications, such as medical diagnosis, natural language processing, and robotics. 
5. Knowledge-based systems are often used in conjunction with other AI techniques, such as machine learning and deep learning. 
6. Knowledge-based systems can be used to automate tasks that require reasoning and decision making. 
7. Knowledge-based systems are often used to provide advice and guidance to users. 
8. The accuracy of knowledge-based systems depends on the quality of the data and the accuracy of the rules used to reason with the data.
