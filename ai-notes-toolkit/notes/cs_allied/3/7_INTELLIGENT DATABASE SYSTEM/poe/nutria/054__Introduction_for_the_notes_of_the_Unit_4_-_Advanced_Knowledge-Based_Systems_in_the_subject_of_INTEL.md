
### Introduction to Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems

1. Knowledge-based systems (KBS) are computer systems that use knowledge to solve problems, analyze data, and make decisions. 
2. KBS can be divided into two categories: rule-based systems and case-based systems. Rule-based systems are based on a set of rules that are used to make decisions or take actions. Case-based systems store and use past experiences to make decisions or take actions. 
3. KBS are used in a wide range of applications, including medical diagnosis, financial planning, and natural language processing. 
4. KBS use a variety of techniques, such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, and expert systems, to acquire, store, and use knowledge. 
5. KBS are often used in conjunction with other technologies, such as databases, to provide a more comprehensive solution.
