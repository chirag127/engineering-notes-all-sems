
### Comparison with other data models for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Semantic data models are more expressive than traditional data models. They allow for more complex relationships between data elements and are able to better capture the meaning of data.
2. Semantic data models are more flexible than traditional data models. They can support multiple interpretations of data and can easily be extended or modified.
3. Semantic data models are better at representing complex relationships between data elements. This makes them better suited for managing large amounts of data.
4. Semantic data models are better at capturing the meaning of data. This makes them more suitable for tasks such as natural language processing, information retrieval and knowledge management.
5. Semantic data models are more difficult to implement than traditional data models. They require more sophisticated tools and techniques, and require more up-front design work.
