### Medical information systems for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Electronic Health Records (EHRs): EHRs are digital versions of a patient's medical history, including details about visits to clinics, hospitals, specialists and more. They allow for instant access to information by authorized users and include medical history, diagnoses, vaccinations, test results, and treatment plans. EHRs can be shared across different healthcare providers and updated in real-time. Benefits include improved accuracy, reduced redundancy of tests, and better coordination of care.

2. Picture Archiving and Communication Systems (PACS): PACS are used to store, retrieve, distribute and display medical images. They provide a centralized archive of medical images from sources like CT scans, X-rays, MRI, etc. Images can be viewed and shared instantly by authorized users. Key benefits include improved workflow, remote access and easy storage and retrieval of large volumes of data.

3. Radiology Information Systems (RIS): RIS help radiology departments schedule, register, and track patients and procedures. They handle the logistical aspects of operating a radiology department, including booking appointments, registering patients, tracking images and reports, billing and more. Benefits include optimized workflow, reduced paperwork and improved efficiency.

4. Laboratory Information Systems (LIS): LIS are used to manage and automate the workflow of medical laboratories. They handle tasks like recording lab orders, entering/tracking test results, generating reports, billing and more. Key benefits include reduced errors, faster turnaround of lab results, and simplified data management.
