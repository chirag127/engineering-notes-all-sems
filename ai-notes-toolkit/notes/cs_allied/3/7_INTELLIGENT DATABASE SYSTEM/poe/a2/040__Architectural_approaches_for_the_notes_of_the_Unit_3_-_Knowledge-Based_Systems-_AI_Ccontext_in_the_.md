### Architectural approaches for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Production system:
- Productions are of the form: IF condition THEN action
- The working memory is matched against the condition part of productions
- If a match occurs, the action part of the matched production is executed
- This cycle continues until no further matches can be made
- Often used to simulate human expertise or perform machine learning tasks

2. Rule-based system:
- Knowledge is encoded in the form of rules (IF-THEN rules)
- The rules are applied to the known facts or data to deduce new facts
- Useful for tasks like classification, diagnosis, etc.
- The major issues are:
- Lack of explanation facility - difficult to determine why a particular rule was fired
- Difficult to handle exceptions and uncertainty
- Scalability can be an issue for large rule-bases

3. Frame-based system:
- Knowledge is represented in terms of frames
- A frame is a data structure used to represent a stereotyped situation
- It consists of a set of slots which can be filled with various values
- A collection of related frames forms a frame-base
- Frame-bases provide a good representation for the hierarchical structure of concepts and contexts found in the world
- However, they lack the inference and reasoning capabilities of production systems and rule-based systems
