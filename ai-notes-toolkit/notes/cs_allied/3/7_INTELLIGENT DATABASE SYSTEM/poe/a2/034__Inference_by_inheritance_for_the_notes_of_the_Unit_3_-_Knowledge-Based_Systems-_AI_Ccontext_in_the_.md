### Inference by inheritance for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Inference by inheritance is a method of deriving new facts from known facts using inheritance hierarchy.
2. Inference by inheritance assumes that properties of a class are also properties of its subclasses.
3. For example, if "bird" is a subclass of "animal" and we know that "all animals have feathers", we can infer that "all birds have feathers" even without an explicit statement.
4. The main steps involved in inference by inheritance are:
- Identify the inheritance hierarchy between classes.
- Identify attributes of superclasses.
- Derive attributes of subclasses by inheriting properties from superclasses.
5. Advantages:
- Reduces the number of statements required as properties can be inherited from superclasses.
- Reflects natural relationships between classes in the real world.
- Can deduce new facts.
6. Disadvantages:
- Difficult to implement for complex hierarchies.
- Incorrect conclusions may arise if inconsistent or incorrect information is present.
- May deduce facts that are not always true. Needs to be used with caution.
