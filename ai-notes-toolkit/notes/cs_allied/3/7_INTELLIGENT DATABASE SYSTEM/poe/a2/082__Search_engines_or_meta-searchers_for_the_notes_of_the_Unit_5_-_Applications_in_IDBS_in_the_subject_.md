### Search engines or meta-searchers for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

-   Search engines are used to search data or information from the databases. Some of the search engines or meta-searchers used for the intelligent database systems are:
-   Boolean search: It uses Boolean operators like AND, OR and NOT to combine the keywords and retrieve the relevant data.
-   Vector space model: It represents documents and queries as vectors and matches them to return relevant results. It considers keywords and their frequencies.
-   Probabilistic model: It uses probability to determine the relevance of the documents with respect to the given query. It considers the likelihood of keywords appearing in the documents.
-   Inference networks: It uses inference rules and hierarchical structure to retrieve and rank the relevant data or information.
-   Neural networks: It is trained on large datasets to learn the patterns and relationships between the keywords and documents to retrieve most relevant results for the given query.
