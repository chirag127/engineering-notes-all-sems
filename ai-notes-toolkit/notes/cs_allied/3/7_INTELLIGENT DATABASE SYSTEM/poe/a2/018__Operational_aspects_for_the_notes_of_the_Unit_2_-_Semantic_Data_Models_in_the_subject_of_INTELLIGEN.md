### Operational aspects for the notes of the Unit 2 - Semantic Data Models in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Semantic Data Models
- Represent data in terms of entities, attributes and relationships.
- Define the meaning of data and how it relates to the real world.
- Examples: Entity-Relationship model, Object-Oriented model, UML class diagram.

2. Entity-Relationship Model
- Entities: Real-world objects with independent existence. Represented by rectangles.
- Attributes: Properties of entities. Represented by ellipses.
- Relationships: Association between entities. Represented by diamonds.
- Cardinality: One (1:1), Many (1:M), Optional (0:1)
- Weak Entity: Dependent on another entity for its existence.
- Conversion to Relational Model: Map entities to tables, attributes to columns and relationships to foreign keys.

3. Characteristics of a good data model
- Accurately represents the data.
- Avoids redundancy.
- Maintains consistency and integrity constraints.
- Supports data manipulation and queries efficiently.
- Expresses relationships effectively.
- Is understandable and maintainable.
