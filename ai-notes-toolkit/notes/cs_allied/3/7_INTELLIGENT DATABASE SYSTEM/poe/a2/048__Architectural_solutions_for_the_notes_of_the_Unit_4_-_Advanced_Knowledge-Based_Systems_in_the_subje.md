### Architectural solutions for the notes of the Unit 4 - Advanced Knowledge-Based Systems in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM.

1. Rule-based systems:
- If-then rules are used to represent knowledge.
- The inference engine matches the rules with the known facts to derive new conclusions.
- Forward chaining and backward chaining are two common control strategies used.
- Examples: Expert systems, business rule management systems.

2. Frame-based systems:
- Knowledge is organized into frames with slots and facets.
- A frame is a data structure used to represent a concept or a stereotype. Slots contain data or other frames.
- Facets are used to specify constraints and aspects of a slot.
- Examples: KL-ONE, Loom.

3. Logic-based systems:
- Use logical expressions to represent knowledge.
- The inference is performed using automated theorem provers.
- Example: Prolog.

4. Neural networks:
- Inspired by biological neural networks.
- Consist of interconnected nodes that operate on numerical data.
- Learning is done by adjusting the strengths of the connections based on comparisons of actual outputs and expected outputs.
- Examples: Multilayer perceptrons, recurrent neural networks, Hopfield networks, etc.
