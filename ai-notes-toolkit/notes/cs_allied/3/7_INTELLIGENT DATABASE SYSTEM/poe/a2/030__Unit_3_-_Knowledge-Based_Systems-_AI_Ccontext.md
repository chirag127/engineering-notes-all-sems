## Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext

1. Knowledge-based systems are a subset of expert systems that rely on a knowledge base of facts and rules to make inferences and come to conclusions.
2. The knowledge base represents knowledge as a set of facts and relationships about a specific domain. It is built by an expert or team of experts to capture knowledge and heuristic decision-making rules in a structured form.
3. The knowledge base must be complete and consistent. Inconsistency can lead to unreliable decisions and conclusions. Verifying and validating the knowledge base is an important part of knowledge-based system development.
4. A knowledge-based system includes an inference engine that interprets the knowledge in the knowledge base to draw conclusions and make recommendations or predictions. The inference engine uses the rules and facts in the knowledge base and the input information to derive solutions.
5. Knowledge-based systems are limited by the amount and accuracy of knowledge that can be encoded. They may not be able to explain the reasoning behind their decisions or handle exceptions and uncertainty well. They also require continuous maintenance as knowledge becomes outdated.
