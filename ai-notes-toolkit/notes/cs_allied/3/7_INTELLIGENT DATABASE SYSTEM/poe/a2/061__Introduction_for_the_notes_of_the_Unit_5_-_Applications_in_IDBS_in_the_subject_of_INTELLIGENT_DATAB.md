### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS

1. Expert Systems: Expert systems are computer programs that contain stored knowledge and inference procedures that are used to solve complex problems in a specific domain. They are designed to mimic human expertise in a narrow domain. The knowledge is represented in the form of if-then rules. The inference engine uses the rules and facts to draw new conclusions.

2. Robotics: Robotics is the design and construction of robots that can perform a variety of tasks automatically with human-like skill. Robots are used extensively in manufacturing for repetitive and dangerous tasks. They are also used in space exploration, surgical procedures, and the military. Robots contain sensors to detect the environment, computer systems to process the sensory information and control the mechanical systems, and actuators that perform the physical tasks.

3. Computer Vision: Computer vision is focused on teaching computers to understand and label what's in images and videos. The goal is to replicate human vision, but on a much larger scale. Some of the core problems in computer vision include object recognition, semantic segmentation, depth estimation, tracking moving objects, etc. It has applications in self-driving cars, face recognition, robotic vision, medical imaging, etc.

4. Natural Language Processing: Natural language processing (NLP) is focused on teaching computers to process and understand human language as it is spoken. The ultimate goal is to enable seamless and efficient communication between humans and computers. Some of the core problems include automatic text summarization, machine translation, relationship extraction, sentiment analysis, speech recognition, etc. It has applications in virtual assistants, machine translation, spam detection, etc.
