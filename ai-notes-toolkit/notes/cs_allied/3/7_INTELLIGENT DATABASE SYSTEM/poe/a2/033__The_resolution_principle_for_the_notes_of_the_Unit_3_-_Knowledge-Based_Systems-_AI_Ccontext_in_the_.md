### The resolution principle for the notes of the Unit 3 - Knowledge-Based Systems- AI Ccontext in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM.

1. Knowledge-based systems are a specific class of expert systems that incorporate a knowledge base. The knowledge base is a repository of facts about a specific domain and inferences or rules about those facts.

2. The knowledge base is separate from the inference engine. The inference engine applies the rules to the knowledge base facts to deduce new facts or make recommendations.

3. The knowledge acquisition bottleneck is a key challenge in building knowledge-based systems. Significant human expertise is required to identify the relevant knowledge and encode it in a form the system can use.

4. Logic is commonly used to represent knowledge and reasoning in expert systems. Production rules, frames, and predicate logic are the primary formats. Uncertainty can be handled using probability.

5. Expert systems have been applied in a wide range of domains, including medical diagnosis, technical troubleshooting, financial analysis, and decision support. However, they have not realized the early predictions of widespread deployment and impact. Key reasons include the knowledge acquisition bottleneck and lack of robustness.

6. Machine learning methods can be used to automatically learn a knowledge base from data. This can potentially help address the knowledge acquisition bottleneck. However, the results may be difficult to interpret and validate relative to human-authored knowledge bases. Ensemble approaches using both human-authored and learned knowledge are also being explored.
