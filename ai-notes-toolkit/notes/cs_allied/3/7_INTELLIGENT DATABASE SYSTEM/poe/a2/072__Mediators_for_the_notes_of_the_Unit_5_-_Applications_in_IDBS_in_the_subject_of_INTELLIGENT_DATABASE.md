### Mediators for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM.

1. Data Mining:
- The process of extracting knowledge from data.
- Finds hidden patterns or unknown correlations in large data sets to predict outcomes or behaviors.
- Classification, Clustering, Association rule learning, Regression, Summarization, etc. are some of the methods.

2. Data Warehousing:
- A collection of datasets organised under a unified schema.
- Provides the source for data mining activities.
- Removes inconsistencies and redundancies from data sources and integrates data from multiple sources.

3. Information Retrieval:
- The task of searching for and retrieving relevant documents or information for a user query.
- Uses techniques like TF-IDF to rank and retrieve the documents.
- Web search engines are examples of Information Retrieval systems.

4. Question Answering Systems:
- Systems that provide specific answers to questions posed to them in natural language.
- Analyze the intent and context of the question and provide accurate answers instead of a list of documents.
- Limited domain knowledge and technical difficulties make them less efficient than a human expert.
