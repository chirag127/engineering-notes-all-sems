### Multi-Agents systems for the notes of the Unit 5 - Applications in IDBS in the subject of INTELLIGENT DATABASE SYSTEM

1. Multi-agent systems are systems composed of multiple interacting intelligent agents.
2. Agents are autonomous entities with some goals or tasks to achieve.
3. Agents can be software or robots and can communicate with each other.
4. The interactions between agents lead to the emergence of global behaviour of the multi-agent system.
5. Some applications of multi-agent systems:
- Coordination of autonomous vehicles
- Logistics and supply chain management
- Decision making in organizations
- Robotics
- E-commerce and auctions

The key properties of multi-agent systems are:
1. Autonomy: Agents operate without the direct intervention of humans or others.
2. Local view: Agents have a local view of the problem and limited information.
3. Decentralization: There is no global system control.
4. Emergence: The global behaviour emerges from the interactions between agents.
5. Adaptability: Multi-agent systems can adapt to changes in the environment or system.

The challenges in multi-agent systems include:
1. Designing mechanisms for coordination and cooperation between self-interested agents.
2. Ensuring convergence to a desirable solution or stable state.
3. Handling imperfect or incomplete information.
4. Preventing or managing conflicts between agents.
5. Achieving scalability to large numbers of agents.
