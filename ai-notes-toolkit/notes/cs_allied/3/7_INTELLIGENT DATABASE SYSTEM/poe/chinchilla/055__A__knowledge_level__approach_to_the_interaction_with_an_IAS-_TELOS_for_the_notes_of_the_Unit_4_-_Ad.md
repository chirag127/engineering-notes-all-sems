1. Understand the basics of an IAS-TELOS - An IAS-TELOS is an expert system that is designed to carry out complex tasks by imitating the decision-making abilities of a human expert. 2. 5. Pay attention to feedback - The IAS-TELOS provides feedback on the queries and results provided. It is important to pay attention to this feedback and use it to refine your queries and improve the quality of the results.

In conclusion, interacting with an IAS-TELOS requires a 'knowledge level' approach that involves understanding the underlying technology, familiarizing oneself with the knowledge representation techniques, learning how to formulate queries, understanding the reasoning process, and paying attention to feedback.
