
### PLP And MFCC Coefficients for the notes of the Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* PLP (Perceptual Linear Prediction) is a method used to analyze speech signals. It is based on the concept of linear prediction, which is used to predict the value of a signal at a given time.

* MFCC (Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficients) is a technique used to analyze the frequency components of a speech signal. It is based on the concept of cepstral analysis, which is used to separate the components of a signal into different frequency bands.

* PLP and MFCC are used together to analyze the speech signal. PLP is used to analyze the frequency components of the signal, while MFCC is used to extract the cepstral coefficients from the signal.

* The coefficients obtained from PLP and MFCC are then used to represent the speech signal in the form of a vector. This vector can then be used for speech recognition and other applications.
