
### Rule-based for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Natural Language Processing 

1. Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of computer science and linguistics that focuses on the interactions between computers and human languages.
2. NLP is used to analyze and understand natural language, such as text, voice, and images.
3. Rule-based NLP is a type of NLP that uses a set of rules to analyze and process natural language.
4. Rule-based NLP systems are typically created by humans, and can be used to process text, generate text, or identify patterns.
5. Rule-based NLP systems are often used to identify and extract information from text, such as dates, names, and locations.
6. Rule-based NLP systems can also be used to generate text, such as summaries, translations, and paraphrases.
7. Rule-based NLP systems can be used to identify and classify text, such as sentiment analysis and topic classification.
