
### Shallow Parsing for the Notes of Unit 2 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Shallow Parsing (also known as Light Parsing or Chunking) is a process of analyzing a sentence to identify the constituent parts (nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc.) and their relationships. 

2. It is a form of syntactic analysis that looks only at the structure of the sentence and does not attempt to determine the meaning of the sentence. 

3. Shallow parsing is used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) applications such as machine translation, information extraction, and question answering.

4. The goal of shallow parsing is to identify the syntactic structure of a sentence by grouping words into phrases. 

5. The output of a shallow parser is typically a shallow tree structure or a set of labeled brackets that represent the syntactic structure of the sentence. 

6. Shallow parsing can be done using a variety of techniques, including rule-based parsing, statistical parsing, and machine learning. 

7. Shallow parsing is an important step in natural language processing because it provides the basis for more complex syntactic analysis.
