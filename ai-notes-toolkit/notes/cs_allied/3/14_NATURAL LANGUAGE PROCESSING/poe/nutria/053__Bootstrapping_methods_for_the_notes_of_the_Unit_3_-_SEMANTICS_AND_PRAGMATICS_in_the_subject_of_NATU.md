
### Bootstrapping methods for the notes of the Unit 3 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Bootstrapping methods are used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to help machines understand and interpret natural language. 
* Bootstrapping methods are based on the idea that machines can learn to interpret language by using existing knowledge and examples. 
* Bootstrapping methods use a combination of linguistic and statistical techniques to identify patterns in language and draw inferences from them.
* Bootstrapping methods are used to build semantic and pragmatic models for understanding language.
* Semantic models are used to identify the meaning of words and phrases in a sentence. 
* Pragmatic models are used to identify the context and intent of a sentence. 
* Bootstrapping methods can be used to identify relationships between words and phrases, such as synonyms, antonyms, and other semantic relationships. 
* Bootstrapping methods can also be used to identify relationships between sentences, such as topic, sentiment, and other pragmatic relationships. 
* Bootstrapping methods can also be used to identify relationships between documents, such as genre, topic, and other semantic and pragmatic relationships.
