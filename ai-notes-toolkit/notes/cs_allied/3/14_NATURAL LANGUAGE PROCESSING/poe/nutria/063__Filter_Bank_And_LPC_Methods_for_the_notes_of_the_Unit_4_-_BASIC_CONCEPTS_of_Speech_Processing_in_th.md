
### Filter Bank and LPC Methods

* A filter bank is a set of band-pass filters that divide the input signal into multiple frequency sub-bands.
* Linear Predictive Coding (LPC) is a method of representing a signal in terms of its linear prediction coefficients.
* Both filter banks and LPC are used in speech processing to represent the signal in terms of its frequency components.
* Filter banks can be used to reduce the amount of data that needs to be processed in a speech signal.
* LPC can be used to estimate the parameters of a signal, such as its pitch, energy, and spectral envelope.
* Both filter banks and LPC can be used to improve the performance of speech recognition systems.
