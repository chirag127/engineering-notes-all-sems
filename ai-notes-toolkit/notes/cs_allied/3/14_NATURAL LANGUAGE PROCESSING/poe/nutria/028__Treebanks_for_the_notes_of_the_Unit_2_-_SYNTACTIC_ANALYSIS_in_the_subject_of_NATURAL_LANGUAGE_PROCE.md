
### Treebanks for the Notes of Unit 2 - Syntactic Analysis in Natural Language Processing

* Treebanks are collections of syntactic annotations of text, usually in the form of a corpus. These collections are used in natural language processing (NLP) to train and test algorithms for syntactic analysis.
* Treebanks provide a structured representation of the syntax of a language, which is essential for understanding the meaning of sentences.
* Treebanks are created by manually annotating sentences with syntactic information, such as part-of-speech tags and phrase structure.
* Treebanks can also be automatically created using statistical methods, such as parsing algorithms.
* Treebanks can be used to evaluate the accuracy of syntactic analysis algorithms, and to compare different algorithms.
* Treebanks are also used to develop and improve natural language processing systems, such as machine translation and dialogue systems.
