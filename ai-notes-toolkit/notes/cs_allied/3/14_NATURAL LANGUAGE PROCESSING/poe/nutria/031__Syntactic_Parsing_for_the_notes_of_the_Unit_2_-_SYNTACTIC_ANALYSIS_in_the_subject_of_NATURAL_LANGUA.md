
### Syntactic Parsing for Unit 2 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING 

1. Syntactic parsing is the process of analyzing a sentence in order to determine its grammatical structure. 
2. It is a form of syntactic analysis that involves breaking down a sentence into its component parts in order to understand the meaning of the sentence.
3. Syntactic parsing is an important part of natural language processing (NLP) because it helps computers to understand the structure and meaning of sentences.
4. Syntactic parsers use a variety of techniques to analyze sentences, including rule-based systems, statistical models, and machine learning algorithms.
5. Syntactic parsers can be used to create parse trees, which are graphical representations of the structure of a sentence. 
6. Parse trees are useful for understanding the meaning of a sentence, as well as for identifying errors in grammar and syntax.
7. Syntactic parsers can also be used to generate natural language processing applications such as machine translation, text summarization, and question answering.
