
### Mathematical And Perceptual for the notes of the Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Speech-Analysis is the process of analyzing the sound of human speech. It is used to identify and classify spoken words and phrases, detect emotions, and measure the accuracy of spoken language.
* Speech-Analysis can be done using mathematical techniques such as Fourier Analysis, Wavelet Analysis, and Spectral Analysis.
* It can also be done using perceptual techniques such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speech coding.
* Speech recognition is the process of converting speech into text. It is used to recognize words, phrases and sentences.
* Speech synthesis is the process of creating artificial speech from text. It is used to generate audio from text.
* Speech coding is the process of compressing and decompressing audio data. It is used to reduce the size of audio files.
* Natural language processing (NLP) is a field of computer science that focuses on understanding and generating human language. It is used to analyze and understand natural language, and to generate text or speech from natural language.
