

### Language Modeling for the Notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Language modeling is the process of predicting the probability of a sequence of words based on a given set of data.

2. It is an essential part of natural language processing (NLP) and is used to improve the accuracy of language understanding and machine translation.

3. Language models are used to assign probabilities to sequences of words, allowing the computer to determine which sequence is the most likely or most probable.

4. Language models can be divided into two categories: n-gram models and recurrent neural networks.

5. N-gram models are statistical models that assign probabilities to sequences of words based on the frequency of occurrence of the words in a given corpus.

6. Recurrent neural networks are neural networks that are designed to process sequences of data. They are used to model the relationships between words in a given sequence.

7. Language models can be used in a variety of applications, such as speech recognition, machine translation, and text summarization.
