
### Probabilistic CFG for the notes of the Unit 2 - SYNTACTIC ANALYSIS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Probabilistic Context-Free Grammars (PCFGs) are a type of formal grammar that assign probabilities to the production rules of a context-free grammar. 
* PCFGs are used to model the syntactic structure of natural language. 
* PCFGs are a type of stochastic grammar, which means they assign a probability to each possible phrase or sentence in a language. 
* PCFGs are often used in natural language processing tasks such as parsing and machine translation. 
* PCFGs are composed of a set of production rules, which describe the possible syntactic structures of a language. 
* Each rule consists of a left-hand side, which is the non-terminal symbol, and a right-hand side, which is a sequence of terminal and non-terminal symbols. 
* The probability of each production rule is determined by counting the number of times it appears in a corpus of sentences. 
* PCFGs can be used to generate random sentences from a given language. 
* PCFGs can also be used to parse sentences, by assigning the highest probability parse tree to a given sentence.
