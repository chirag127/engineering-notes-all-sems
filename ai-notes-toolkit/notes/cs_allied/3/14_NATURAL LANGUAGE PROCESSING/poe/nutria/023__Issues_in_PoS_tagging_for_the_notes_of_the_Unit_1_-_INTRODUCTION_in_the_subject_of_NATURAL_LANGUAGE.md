
### Issues in PoS Tagging for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. One of the major issues in PoS tagging is the ambiguity of words. For example, the word "bank" can refer to a financial institution, a body of water, or an edge of a river. Without context, it can be difficult to accurately assign a PoS tag to this word.

2. Another issue is the presence of unknown words. In some cases, the PoS tagger may not be able to recognize a word and assign it a tag. This can lead to incorrect tagging of the sentence.

3. PoS taggers also have difficulty with words that have multiple meanings. For example, the word "run" can mean to move quickly or to operate. Depending on the context, the PoS tagger may assign the wrong tag to the word.

4. PoS taggers also struggle to tag words that are part of a collocation. For example, the phrase "strong coffee" can be difficult to tag accurately due to the fact that the words "strong" and "coffee" can have different meanings when used in other contexts.

5. Finally, PoS taggers may struggle to tag words in informal language. For example, slang words or words with multiple spellings can be difficult to tag accurately.
