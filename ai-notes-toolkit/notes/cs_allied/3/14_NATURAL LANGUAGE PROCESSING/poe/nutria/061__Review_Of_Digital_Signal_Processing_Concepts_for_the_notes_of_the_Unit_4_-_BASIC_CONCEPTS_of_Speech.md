
### Review Of Digital Signal Processing Concepts for the notes of the Unit 4 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Digital signal processing (DSP) is a field of study that focuses on the processing of digital signals. It is used to analyze and modify signals in order to improve the quality of the signal or to extract useful information from it.
* DSP algorithms are used in a wide range of applications, including audio and video processing, speech recognition, medical imaging, and communication systems.
* Digital signal processing algorithms can be divided into two categories: time-domain algorithms and frequency-domain algorithms.
* Time-domain algorithms analyze the signal in the time domain, which is the domain in which the signal is represented as a function of time. Common time-domain algorithms include filtering, convolution, and correlation.
* Frequency-domain algorithms analyze the signal in the frequency domain, which is the domain in which the signal is represented as a function of frequency. Common frequency-domain algorithms include Fourier analysis, spectral analysis, and wavelet analysis.
* Digital signal processing algorithms can be implemented in both hardware and software. Hardware implementations are often used for real-time applications, while software implementations are used for non-real-time applications.
* Digital signal processing algorithms can be optimized for speed, memory usage, or accuracy, depending on the application.
