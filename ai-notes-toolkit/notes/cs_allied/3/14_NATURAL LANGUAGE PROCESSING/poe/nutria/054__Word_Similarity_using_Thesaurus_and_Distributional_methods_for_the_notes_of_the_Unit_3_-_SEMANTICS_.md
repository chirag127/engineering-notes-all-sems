
### Word Similarity using Thesaurus and Distributional methods

Word similarity is an important concept in the field of natural language processing (NLP). It is used to measure the similarity between two words or phrases and can be used to identify related concepts.

There are two main methods used to measure word similarity: thesaurus-based methods and distributional methods.

#### Thesaurus-based Methods

Thesaurus-based methods use a thesaurus or dictionary to compare the words and measure their similarity. This method is based on the idea that similar words will have similar meanings and thus be found near each other in a thesaurus.

Thesaurus-based methods are relatively simple to use and can be used to quickly find related words or phrases. However, they are limited in their ability to capture the subtle nuances of language.

#### Distributional Methods

Distributional methods measure the similarity between words by looking at how they are used in real-world contexts. This method is based on the idea that words that are used in similar contexts will be more similar than words used in different contexts.

Distributional methods are more complex than thesaurus-based methods and require more data to be accurate. However, they are better at capturing the subtle nuances of language.
