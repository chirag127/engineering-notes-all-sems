
### Normal Forms for Grammar 

* Chomsky Normal Form (CNF) is a type of grammar used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to simplify the structure of sentences. It is a type of context-free grammar that has been transformed into a form where all rules have only two symbols on the right-hand side of the production. 

* Greibach Normal Form (GNF) is a type of grammar used in NLP to simplify the structure of sentences. It is a type of context-free grammar that has been transformed into a form where all rules have a single non-terminal symbol on the left-hand side and a sequence of terminal symbols on the right-hand side. 

* Normal Forms for grammar are used to simplify the task of syntactic analysis in NLP. They allow for the development of efficient algorithms for parsing sentences, which are used to extract the underlying meaning of a sentence. By transforming the grammar into a normalized form, the parser can more easily identify the syntactic structure of the sentence.
