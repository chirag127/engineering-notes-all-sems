
### Acoustics Of Speech Production 
 
1. Speech production is the process of producing human speech. It is a complex process involving the coordination of the respiratory, phonatory, and articulatory systems. 
2. The respiratory system is responsible for providing the air pressure needed to produce speech sounds. This is done by altering the pressure and volume of air in the lungs and controlling the flow of air through the vocal tract. 
3. The phonatory system is responsible for producing the sound waves that make up speech. This is done by modulating the air pressure created by the respiratory system with the vocal folds. 
4. The articulatory system is responsible for shaping the sound waves created by the phonatory system into the desired speech sounds. This is done by controlling the shape and position of the articulators, such as the tongue, lips, and jaw. 
5. The acoustics of speech production is the study of how these three systems work together to produce speech. It involves the study of the physical properties of the sound waves produced by the phonatory system, as well as the study of how the articulators shape these sound waves into speech.
