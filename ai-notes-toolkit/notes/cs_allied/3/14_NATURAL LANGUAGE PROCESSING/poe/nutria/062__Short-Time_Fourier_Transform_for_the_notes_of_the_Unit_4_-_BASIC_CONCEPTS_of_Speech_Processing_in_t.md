
### Short-Time Fourier Transform

The Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) is a powerful tool for analyzing the spectral content of speech signals. It is used to analyze the frequency components of a signal over a short period of time.

The STFT is a mathematical technique that decomposes a signal into its frequency components. It divides a signal into small segments, and then applies the Fourier Transform to each segment to obtain its frequency content. The STFT allows us to analyze the spectral content of a signal over a short period of time.

The STFT is a useful tool for speech processing tasks such as speech recognition, speech synthesis, and speech coding. It can also be used to analyze the spectral content of a signal in order to identify its components.

The STFT is an important tool for natural language processing. It can be used to extract features from speech signals in order to classify and identify spoken words. It can also be used to detect patterns in speech signals, which can be used to improve the accuracy of speech recognition systems.
