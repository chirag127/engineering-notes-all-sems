
### Hidden Markov and Maximum Entropy models

Hidden Markov models (HMMs) are probabilistic models for sequences of observations. They are used to model the probability of a sequence of words or symbols in a language or a sequence of states in a state machine. HMMs are used in natural language processing (NLP) to model the probability of a sequence of words in a language.

Maximum entropy models (MEMs) are probabilistic models for sequences of observations. They are used to model the probability of a sequence of words or symbols in a language or a sequence of states in a state machine. MEMs are used in natural language processing (NLP) to model the probability of a sequence of words in a language. MEMs are more accurate than HMMs and can be used to better model the probability of a sequence of words in a language.

Both HMMs and MEMs are used in natural language processing (NLP) to model the probability of a sequence of words in a language. They can be used to identify parts of speech, identify the most likely sequence of words in a sentence, and identify the most probable sequence of states in a state machine.
