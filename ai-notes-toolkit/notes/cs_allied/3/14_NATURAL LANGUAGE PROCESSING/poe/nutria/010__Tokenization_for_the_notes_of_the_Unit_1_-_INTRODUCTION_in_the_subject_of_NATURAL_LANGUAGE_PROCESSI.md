
### Tokenization for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction in Natural Language Processing

* Tokenization is the process of breaking a stream of text into words, phrases, symbols, or other meaningful elements called tokens.
* It is a common task in natural language processing (NLP) and is used to prepare text for further processing.
* Tokenization can be done in a variety of ways, such as using regular expressions, split functions, or a dictionary.
* Tokenization can also involve more complex tasks such as part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and stemming.
* Tokenization is an important step in the NLP pipeline as it provides the input for further processing such as parsing and semantic analysis.
