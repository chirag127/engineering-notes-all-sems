
### Relations between Senses 

* Semantics is the study of meaning in language, while pragmatics is the study of how language is used in context. 
* Pragmatic meaning is determined by the context in which a sentence is used, while semantic meaning is determined by the literal meaning of words. 
* Semantics is concerned with the literal meaning of words, while pragmatics is concerned with the context in which words are used. 
* When analyzing a sentence, semantic meaning is determined by the meaning of the words, while pragmatic meaning is determined by the context in which the words are used. 
* Semantics and pragmatics are often used together to determine the meaning of a sentence. 
* For example, the sentence "I'm going to the store" could have different meanings depending on the context. If the speaker is standing in the store, the sentence could mean that they are leaving the store. However, if the speaker is standing outside the store, the sentence could mean that they are entering the store. 
* Semantics and pragmatics are both important for understanding the meaning of a sentence.
