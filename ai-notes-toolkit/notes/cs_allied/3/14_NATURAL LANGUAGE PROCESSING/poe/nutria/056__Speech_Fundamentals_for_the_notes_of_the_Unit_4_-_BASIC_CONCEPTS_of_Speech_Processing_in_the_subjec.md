
### Speech Fundamentals

* Speech processing is the study of how to analyze, modify, and synthesize speech signals. It is an important field of study in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). 
* Speech processing involves the analysis of speech signals to detect and identify words and other features of the spoken language. 
* Speech recognition is the process of converting spoken language into text or other forms of data. 
* Speech synthesis is the process of generating speech from text or other forms of data.
* Speech coding is the process of compressing speech signals for transmission over a communication channel.
* Speech enhancement is the process of improving the quality of speech signals.
* Speech recognition and synthesis algorithms are used in many applications such as voice search, voice assistants, and text-to-speech systems.
