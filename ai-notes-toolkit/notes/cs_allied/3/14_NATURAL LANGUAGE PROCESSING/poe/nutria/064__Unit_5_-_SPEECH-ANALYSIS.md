

## Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS

* Speech-analysis is the process of breaking down a spoken language into its individual components.
* This process involves analyzing the sounds, words, phrases, and sentences used in a language, as well as the context in which they are used.
* Speech-analysis can be used to identify patterns in a language, as well as to gain insight into the meaning of a particular phrase or sentence.
* It can also be used to identify differences between two languages, or to compare two dialects of the same language.
* Speech-analysis can be used to detect errors in a speaker's pronunciation, or to identify errors in a written text.
* It can also be used to detect changes in a speaker's speech over time.
* Speech-analysis can be used to detect changes in a speaker's accent or dialect, as well as to identify regional variations in a language.
* Speech-analysis can also be used to identify speakers who are speaking the same language, but with different accents.
