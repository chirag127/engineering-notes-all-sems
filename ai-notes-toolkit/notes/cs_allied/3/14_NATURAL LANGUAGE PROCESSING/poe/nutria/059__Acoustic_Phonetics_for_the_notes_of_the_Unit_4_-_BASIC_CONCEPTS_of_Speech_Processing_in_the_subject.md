
### Acoustic Phonetics for the Notes of the Unit 4 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing in the Subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Acoustic phonetics is the study of the physical properties of speech sounds. It is concerned with how speech sounds are produced, transmitted, and perceived.

* Acoustic phonetics focuses on the physical characteristics of speech sounds, such as their frequency, intensity, and duration. It also examines how these characteristics vary between different speakers and how they are affected by the environment.

* Acoustic phonetics is used to study a variety of different aspects of speech production, including the production of different speech sounds, the recognition of speech sounds, and the perception of speech.

* Acoustic phonetics is an important tool for understanding the acoustic structure of language and for developing speech recognition systems. It is also used to identify and analyze speech disorders.
