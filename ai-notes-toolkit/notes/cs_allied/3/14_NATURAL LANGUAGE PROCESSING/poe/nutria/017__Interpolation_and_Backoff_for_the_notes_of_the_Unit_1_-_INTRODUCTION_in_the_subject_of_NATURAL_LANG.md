
### Interpolation and Backoff

* Interpolation is a method used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to improve the accuracy of a language model by interpolating between multiple models. It is used to combine the predictions of multiple models in order to generate a more accurate estimate of the probability of a word or phrase.

* Backoff is a technique used in NLP to reduce the probability of rare words or phrases. It works by reducing the probability of rare words or phrases in favor of more common words or phrases. This helps to reduce the amount of data needed to train a language model and also improves the accuracy of the model.

* Interpolation and backoff are two techniques used to improve the accuracy of a language model. Interpolation combines the predictions of multiple models while backoff reduces the probability of rare words or phrases in favor of more common words or phrases. Both techniques help to improve the accuracy of the language model and reduce the amount of data needed to train it.
