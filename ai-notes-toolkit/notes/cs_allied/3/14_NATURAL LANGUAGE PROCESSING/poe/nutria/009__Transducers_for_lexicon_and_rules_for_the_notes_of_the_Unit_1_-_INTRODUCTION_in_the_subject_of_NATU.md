
### Transducers for lexicon and rules for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Transducers are used to model the mapping between natural language and its underlying meaning.
* A lexicon is a collection of words and their associated meanings. It is used to help interpret natural language.
* Rules are used to represent the grammar of a language. They are used to determine how words are combined to form sentences that are meaningful and grammatically correct.
* Natural language processing (NLP) is the field of computer science that deals with the analysis of natural language. It is used to understand the meaning of sentences, extract information from text, and generate new sentences.
* NLP has applications in many areas, such as machine translation, speech recognition, question answering, and text summarization.
