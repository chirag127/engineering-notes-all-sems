
### Transformation-based tagging for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

1. Transformation-based tagging (TBT) is a type of natural language processing (NLP) technique that uses a set of rules to determine the correct tags for words in a sentence.

2. TBT uses a set of rules to transform a given sentence into a sequence of tags that represent the words in the sentence. Each rule is used to transform a given sentence into a sequence of tags that represent the words in the sentence.

3. In TBT, the rules are derived from a training corpus. The training corpus is a set of sentences with their corresponding tags. The rules are learned from the training corpus and are used to tag unknown sentences.

4. The rules are applied to unknown sentences in order to predict the tags for each word in the sentence. The rules are applied in a top-down fashion, starting from the most general rule and gradually refining the tags as more specific rules are applied.

5. TBT can be used in a variety of applications, such as part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and sentiment analysis. It is a powerful technique for NLP tasks and is widely used in industry.
