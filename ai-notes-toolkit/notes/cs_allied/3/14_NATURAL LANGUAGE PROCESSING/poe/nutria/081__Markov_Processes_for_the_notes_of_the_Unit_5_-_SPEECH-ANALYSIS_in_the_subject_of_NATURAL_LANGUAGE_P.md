
### Markov Processes for the notes of the Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

* Markov Processes are a type of probabilistic model used to describe the state of a system over time.
* They are used in Natural Language Processing (NLP) to model the probability of a sequence of words or characters.
* A Markov Process is a stochastic process where the future state of the system is determined by the current state, and not by the past states.
* In speech-analysis, Markov Processes are used to model the probability of a sequence of sounds, words, or syllables.
* Markov Models can be used to generate text that is similar to a given corpus.
* Markov Models can also be used to identify patterns in speech, such as pauses, intonation, and other features.
* Markov Models can be used to identify the most likely sequence of words in a given speech.
