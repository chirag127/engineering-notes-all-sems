
## Unit 4 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing

* Speech processing is the process of transforming spoken language into a form that can be understood and analyzed by a computer.
* Speech recognition is the ability of a computer to understand spoken language.
* Speech synthesis is the process of generating speech from text.
* Speech recognition and synthesis are two of the most important components of natural language processing (NLP).
* Speech recognition systems use acoustic models, language models, and other techniques to analyze and identify spoken words.
* Speech synthesis systems use text-to-speech (TTS) technology to generate a spoken version of the text.
* Speech processing techniques are used in many applications such as voice search, voice commands, virtual assistants, and more.
