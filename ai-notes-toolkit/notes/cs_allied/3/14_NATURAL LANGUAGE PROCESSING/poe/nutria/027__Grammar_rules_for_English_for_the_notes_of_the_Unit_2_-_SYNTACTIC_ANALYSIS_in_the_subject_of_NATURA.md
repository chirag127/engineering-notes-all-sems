
### Grammar Rules for English for the Notes of Unit 2 - Syntactic Analysis in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

1. Syntactic analysis is the process of analyzing the structure of a sentence in order to understand its meaning.
2. Syntactic analysis is often used in natural language processing (NLP) to create a parse tree, which is a graphical representation of the syntactic structure of a sentence.
3. Syntactic analysis involves identifying the parts of speech in a sentence (e.g. nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, etc.), as well as the relationships between the parts of speech (e.g. subject-verb agreement, verb tense, etc.).
4. Syntactic analysis also involves identifying the phrase structure of a sentence (e.g. noun phrases, verb phrases, etc.).
5. Syntactic analysis is an important part of understanding natural language, as it helps to identify the meaning of a sentence, as well as its grammatical correctness.
