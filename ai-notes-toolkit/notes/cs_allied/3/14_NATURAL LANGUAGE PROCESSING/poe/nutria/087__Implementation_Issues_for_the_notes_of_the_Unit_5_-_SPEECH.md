
### Implementation Issues for the Unit 5 - SPEECH
1. Speech recognition accuracy can be affected by the speaker's accent, background noise, and other factors.
2. Speech synthesis can be difficult to understand due to natural language processing issues.
3. Natural language processing algorithms may not be able to accurately interpret complex sentences or slang.
4. Speech recognition and synthesis algorithms require a lot of computing power, and are often difficult to implement in real-time applications.
5. Speech recognition and synthesis algorithms can be expensive to develop and maintain.
6. Speech recognition and synthesis algorithms can be difficult to integrate with existing systems.
7. Speech recognition and synthesis algorithms can be difficult to integrate with human-computer interfaces.
8. Speech recognition and synthesis algorithms can be difficult to integrate with other technologies, such as virtual reality or augmented reality.
