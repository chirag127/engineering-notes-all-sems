
## Unit 3 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS

- Semantics is the study of meaning in language, focusing on the meaning of words, phrases, and sentences.
- Pragmatics is the study of how meaning is used in context, focusing on the context in which language is used and the effects of this context on the interpretation of meaning.
- Semantics focuses on the literal meaning of words, phrases, and sentences, while pragmatics focuses on the implied meaning of language.
- Semantics deals with the relationship between words and their literal meanings, while pragmatics deals with how language is used in context and the implications of this use.
