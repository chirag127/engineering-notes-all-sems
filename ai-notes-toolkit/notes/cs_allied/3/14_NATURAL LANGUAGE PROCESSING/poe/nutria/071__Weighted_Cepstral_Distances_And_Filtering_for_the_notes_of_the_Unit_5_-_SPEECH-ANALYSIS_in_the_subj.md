
### Weighted Cepstral Distances And Filtering

* Weighted Cepstral Distances (WCD) is a method of measuring the similarity between two speech signals. It is based on the cepstral coefficients of the two signals.
* WCD is used to compare the voice characteristics of a speaker with a reference. It is used in speech recognition systems to identify a speaker and in speaker verification systems to authenticate a speaker.
* WCD can also be used to filter out noise from speech signals. This is done by comparing the cepstral coefficients of the noisy signal with the cepstral coefficients of a clean reference signal. 
* WCD can also be used to identify and remove background noise from speech signals. This is done by comparing the cepstral coefficients of the noisy signal with the cepstral coefficients of a clean reference signal. 
* WCD can be used to detect and remove reverberation from speech signals. This is done by comparing the cepstral coefficients of the reverberated signal with the cepstral coefficients of a clean reference signal.
