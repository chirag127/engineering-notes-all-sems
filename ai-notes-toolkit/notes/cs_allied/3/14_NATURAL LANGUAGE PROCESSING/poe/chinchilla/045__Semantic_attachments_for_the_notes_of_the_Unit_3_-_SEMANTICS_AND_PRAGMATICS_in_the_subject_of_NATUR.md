### Semantic Attachments for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Semantics and Pragmatics in the Subject of Natural Language Processing

In natural language processing, semantic attachments are used to associate meaning with words and phrases in a sentence. These attachments help in understanding the intended meaning of the text and also assist in various NLP tasks such as Named Entity Recognition, Sentiment Analysis, and Text Classification. Here are some important semantic attachments to note:

1. Part-of-Speech (POS) tags: POS tags are used to identify the part of speech of each word in a sentence, such as noun, verb, adjective, etc. POS tags provide information about the grammatical structure of the sentence and help in identifying the meaning of words in context.

2. Syntactic Dependencies: Syntactic dependencies represent the relationships between words in a sentence, such as subject-verb, object-verb, etc. These dependencies help in understanding the meaning of the sentence and are used in various NLP tasks such as Text Summarization and Question Answering.

3. Word Senses: Words can have multiple senses depending on the context in which they are used. Word senses are used to identify the meaning of a word in a particular context. WordNet is a popular lexical database that provides word senses and is widely used in NLP applications.

4. Named Entity Recognition (NER) Tags: NER tags are used to identify and classify named entities in a sentence, such as person, organization, location, etc. NER is an important task in NLP and is used in various applications such as Information Extraction and Text Classification.

5. Sentiment Analysis Tags: Sentiment analysis tags are used to identify the sentiment of a sentence, such as positive, negative, or neutral. Sentiment analysis is used in various applications such as Social Media Monitoring and Customer Feedback Analysis.

6. Semantic Role Labeling (SRL) Tags: SRL tags are used to identify the semantic roles of each word in a sentence, such as agent, patient, instrument, etc. SRL is used in various NLP tasks such as Text Summarization and Question Answering.

In conclusion, semantic attachments play a crucial role in natural language processing and assist in various NLP tasks. Understanding these attachments is essential for developing effective NLP models and applications.
