### Dictionary & Thesaurus for the notes of the Unit 3 - SEMANTICS AND PRAGMATICS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

In natural language processing, understanding the meaning of words and phrases is essential. Semantics and pragmatics are two fields that deal with meaning in language. Dictionary and thesaurus are two resources that can help in understanding the meaning of words and phrases. In this unit, we will discuss the use of dictionaries and thesauri in natural language processing.

#### Dictionary

A dictionary is a book or an electronic resource that contains words and their meanings. A dictionary can be used for various purposes, such as learning the meaning of a new word or finding synonyms for a word. In natural language processing, dictionaries are used to:

- Look up the meaning of a word: Dictionaries provide the definition of a word, which can help in understanding its meaning in a particular context.
- Identify the part of speech: Dictionaries also provide information about the part of speech of a word, which can help in determining its grammatical role in a sentence.
- Provide examples: Dictionaries often include examples of how a word can be used in a sentence, which can help in understanding its meaning and usage.

#### Thesaurus

A thesaurus is a book or an electronic resource that contains synonyms and antonyms of words. Thesauri are used to:

- Find synonyms: Thesauri provide synonyms for a word, which can help in finding alternative words with similar meanings.
- Find antonyms: Thesauri also provide antonyms for a word, which can help in finding words with opposite meanings.
- Expand vocabulary: Thesauri can be used to expand one's vocabulary by finding new words with similar meanings.

#### Differences between Dictionary and Thesaurus

While both dictionaries and thesauri provide information about words, they serve different purposes. Some differences between dictionaries and thesauri are:

- Dictionaries provide definitions and meanings of words, while thesauri provide synonyms and antonyms.
- Dictionaries are used to understand the meaning of a word, while thesauri are used to find alternative words with similar meanings.
- Dictionaries are used to identify the part of speech of a word, while thesauri do not provide information about the part of speech.

In conclusion, dictionaries and thesauri are useful resources for understanding the meaning of words and phrases in natural language processing. While dictionaries provide definitions and meanings of words, thesauri provide synonyms and antonyms. Both resources can be used to expand one's vocabulary and improve language understanding.
