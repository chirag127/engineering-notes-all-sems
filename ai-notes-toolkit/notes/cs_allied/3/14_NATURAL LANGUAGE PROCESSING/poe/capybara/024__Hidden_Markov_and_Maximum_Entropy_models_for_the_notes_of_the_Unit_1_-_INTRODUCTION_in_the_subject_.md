### Hidden Markov and Maximum Entropy models for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

In the field of Natural Language Processing, statistical models play a crucial role in understanding and processing natural language. Hidden Markov Models (HMM) and Maximum Entropy Models (MaxEnt) are two popular statistical models used in NLP. Let's take a closer look at these models and their applications.

#### Hidden Markov Models (HMM)

- HMM is a statistical model that is commonly used for modeling sequential data, such as speech or text.
- It is a probabilistic model that assumes that the observed data is generated by a hidden process that is not directly observable.
- HMM consists of two types of states:
  - Hidden states: These are the unobservable states that generate the output data. 
  - Observable states: These are the visible states that we can observe.
- HMM is commonly used in speech recognition, part-of-speech tagging, and machine translation.

#### Maximum Entropy Models (MaxEnt)

- MaxEnt is a statistical model used for classification problems in NLP.
- It is also known as logistic regression or log-linear models.
- MaxEnt makes use of the principle of maximum entropy, which states that the model should choose the most uniform distribution that satisfies the observed constraints.
- The model is trained on a set of labeled data and learns the probability distribution of the output variables given the input variables.
- MaxEnt is commonly used in named entity recognition, sentiment analysis, and text classification.

In conclusion, Hidden Markov Models and Maximum Entropy Models are two important statistical models in NLP that are used for different applications. It is important to understand the underlying principles and applications of these models in order to apply them effectively in NLP tasks.
