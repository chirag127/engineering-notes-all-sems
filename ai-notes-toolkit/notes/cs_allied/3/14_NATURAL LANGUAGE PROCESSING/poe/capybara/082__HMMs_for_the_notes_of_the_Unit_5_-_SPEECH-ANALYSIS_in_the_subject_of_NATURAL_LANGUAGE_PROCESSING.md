### HMMs for the notes of the Unit 5 - SPEECH-ANALYSIS in the subject of NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING

- Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are statistical models used for speech recognition and analysis.
- HMMs are based on the assumption that speech can be modeled as a sequence of discrete states.
- The model consists of a set of states, each of which emits a symbol or observation.
- The transition between states is governed by a set of probabilities, known as the transition probabilities.
- The emission probabilities are the probabilities of observing a particular symbol from a given state.
- HMMs have been successfully used for speech recognition, speaker identification, and language modeling.
- In speech recognition, HMMs are used to model the acoustic properties of speech sounds.
- HMMs have been used to build systems that can recognize spoken words and phrases, as well as to transcribe speech into text.
- Speaker identification involves using HMMs to model the unique characteristics of an individual's speech.
- HMMs are also used in language modeling to predict the probability of a sequence of words based on previous words in the sequence.
- HMMs have been used in a variety of applications, including speech recognition, natural language processing, and bioinformatics.
- In conclusion, HMMs are a powerful tool for speech analysis and are widely used in many applications.
