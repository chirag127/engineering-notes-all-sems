## Unit 4 - BASIC CONCEPTS of Speech Processing

The following are some of the basic concepts of speech processing that are commonly used in the field of speech technology:

### 1. Signal processing

Speech signals are processed using various signal processing techniques. These techniques include filtering, noise reduction, and feature extraction. Filtering helps in removing unwanted noise from the signal. Noise reduction techniques are used to reduce background noise. Feature extraction techniques are used to extract important features from the speech signal.

### 2. Speech recognition

Speech recognition is the process of recognizing spoken words from an audio signal. It involves converting the speech signal into text. There are two types of speech recognition systems: isolated word recognition and continuous speech recognition.

### 3. Speaker recognition

Speaker recognition is the process of recognizing an individual based on their voice. It is used in security systems and authentication processes. There are two types of speaker recognition systems: text-dependent and text-independent.

### 4. Speech synthesis

Speech synthesis is the process of generating speech from text. It involves converting written text into spoken words. There are two types of speech synthesis systems: concatenative and parametric.

### 5. Natural language processing

Natural language processing (NLP) is the process of analyzing and understanding human language. It involves tasks such as text classification, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.

### 6. Speech coding

Speech coding is the process of compressing speech signals. It is used to reduce the amount of data required to transmit speech over a communication channel. There are two types of speech coding techniques: waveform coding and parametric coding.

### 7. Speech enhancement

Speech enhancement is the process of improving the quality of speech signals. It involves removing noise and improving the clarity of the speech signal.

### 8. Speech analysis

Speech analysis is the process of analyzing the properties of speech signals. It involves extracting features such as pitch, duration, and intensity.

These are some of the basic concepts of speech processing that are used in the field of speech technology. Understanding these concepts is essential for anyone interested in pursuing a career in speech technology.
