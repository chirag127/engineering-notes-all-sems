### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

In this section, we will cover some basic concepts of electronics that are essential for understanding microcontrollers and their programming. These concepts are crucial for anyone who wants to work with microcontrollers and develop electronic systems.

1. **Voltage, Current, and Resistance**

- Voltage is the measure of electrical potential difference between two points in a circuit. It is measured in volts (V).
- Current is the flow of electric charge in a circuit. It is measured in amperes (A).
- Resistance is the opposition to the flow of electric current in a circuit. It is measured in ohms (Ω).

2. **Ohm's Law**

Ohm's law states that the current through a resistor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points. It can be represented mathematically as:

```
I = V / R
```

Where I is the current in amperes, V is the voltage in volts, and R is the resistance in ohms.

3. **Power**

Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred. In electronics, power is measured in watts (W). The formula for power is:

```
P = V * I
```

Where P is power in watts, V is voltage in volts, and I is current in amperes.

4. **Circuits**

A circuit is a closed path through which electric current flows. There are two types of circuits:

- Series circuit: In a series circuit, the components are connected in a single path so that the current flows through each component in turn.
- Parallel circuit: In a parallel circuit, the components are connected in multiple paths so that the current is divided among the components.

5. **Components**

There are several electronic components that are commonly used in circuits. Some of these components include:

- Resistors: These are used to limit the flow of current in a circuit.
- Capacitors: These are used to store electrical energy and release it when needed.
- Diodes: These are used to allow current to flow in only one direction.
- Transistors: These are used as switches or amplifiers in electronic circuits.

6. **Schematic Diagrams**

A schematic diagram is a graphical representation of an electronic circuit. It uses standardized symbols to represent the different components in a circuit.

By understanding these basic concepts of electronics, you will be better equipped to work with microcontrollers and develop electronic systems.
