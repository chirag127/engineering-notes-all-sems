### History

- The first microcontroller was created in the 1970s by Intel, called the Intel 8048.
- The microcontroller was designed to combine the central processing unit (CPU), memory, and input/output (I/O) interfaces onto a single chip.
- The 8048 was quickly adopted by the industry due to its small size, low cost, and high functionality.
- Over the years, various companies such as Motorola, Texas Instruments, and Atmel entered the microcontroller market, with each bringing their own innovations and improvements.
- The development of microcontrollers led to the creation of embedded systems, which are computer systems designed to perform a specific function within a larger system.
- The use of microcontrollers and embedded systems became widespread in the 1990s, with applications ranging from home appliances to automobiles.
- Today, microcontrollers continue to evolve and improve, with new features such as wireless connectivity and increased processing power being added to meet the demands of modern applications.
