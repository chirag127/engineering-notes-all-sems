### Parts Identification for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the Subject of Programming and Interfacing with Microcontrollers

In this unit, we will be discussing the basic parts of a microcontroller and their functions. It is important to have a good understanding of these parts in order to effectively program and interface with microcontrollers. Below are the parts that we will be covering:

- **Central Processing Unit (CPU):** This is the brain of the microcontroller and is responsible for processing data and executing instructions.

- **Memory:** There are two types of memory in a microcontroller - Random Access Memory (RAM) and Read Only Memory (ROM). RAM is used for temporary storage of data while ROM is used for storing permanent data and program instructions.

- **Input/Output Ports (I/O Ports):** These are the communication channels used to interact with the outside world. They are used for connecting sensors, switches, LEDs, and other peripherals to the microcontroller.

- **Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC):** This part is used for converting analog signals (such as voltage or current) into digital signals that can be processed by the microcontroller.

- **Timers and Counters:** These parts are used for time-keeping and counting events. They are used for tasks such as measuring the time between two events or generating a specific frequency signal.

- **Serial Communication Interfaces:** These are used for communicating with other devices using standard communication protocols such as UART, SPI, and I2C.

- **Interrupt Controller:** This part is responsible for handling interrupts. Interrupts are signals that temporarily stop the normal execution of the program and allow the microcontroller to perform a specific task.

It is important to have a good understanding of these parts in order to effectively program and interface with microcontrollers. In the next units, we will be discussing how to use these parts to develop microcontroller-based projects.
