## Unit 4 - ADVANCED I/O INTERFACING

Advanced I/O interfacing involves the use of various techniques to connect external devices to a microcontroller. In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

1. Serial Communication
   * UART
   * SPI
   * I2C

2. Interrupts
   * Types of interrupts
   * Interrupt service routines (ISRs)
   * Nested interrupts

3. Timers
   * Types of timers
   * Timer interrupts
   * Applications of timers

4. Analog-to-Digital Conversion (ADC)
   * Principles of ADC
   * Types of ADC
   * Applications of ADC

5. Digital-to-Analog Conversion (DAC)
   * Principles of DAC
   * Types of DAC
   * Applications of DAC

6. Pulse Width Modulation (PWM)
   * Principles of PWM
   * Applications of PWM

7. Interfacing with External Memory
   * Types of memories
   * Memory organization
   * Interfacing techniques

8. Interfacing with Displays
   * Types of displays
   * Interfacing techniques

In conclusion, advanced I/O interfacing is an essential aspect of microcontroller programming. By mastering the techniques covered in this unit, you will be able to interface with a wide range of external devices and expand the functionality of your microcontroller-based projects.
