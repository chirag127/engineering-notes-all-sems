### Building peer to peer communication system using Bluetooth &WiFi for the notes of the Unit 5 - IoT, FUTURE AND PERSPECTIVES

1. Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless technology standard for exchanging data over short distances (using UHF radio waves in the frequency range 2.4 to 2.485 gigahertz) from fixed and mobile devices, and building personal area networks (PANs).

2. WiFi: WiFi is a family of wireless networking technologies commonly used for local area networking of devices and internet access, based on the IEEE 802.11 standards. WiFi is used in mobile devices such as laptops, tablets, and smartphones.

3. Peer to peer communication: Peer to peer or P2P communication is a method of device interconnection without the need for a centralized administration authority or server. Devices in a P2P network act as both clients and servers to share files, computing resources, and other services. P2P systems are often used to connect devices via both Bluetooth and WiFi to share and distribute data or services.

4. Building a communication system: To build a peer to peer communication system, the following steps can be followed:

-Enable Bluetooth and WiFi on the devices to be connected
-Pair the Bluetooth devices to create a Bluetooth connection
-Connect to the same WiFi network to establish a WiFi connection
- Choose either Bluetooth or WiFi to establish a peer to peer connection
-Share data or access services over the P2P connection

The peer to peer communication system can allow devices to directly connect and share information which enables efficient device interconnection for IoT systems and other applications. The choice of Bluetooth and/or WiFi can be made based on factors like range, bandwidth, power consumption, etc.
