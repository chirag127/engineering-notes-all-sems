### Communication for the notes of the Unit 3 - HARDWARE COMMUNICATION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

1.  Parallel Communication:
    - Data is transmitted bit by bit simultaneously over multiple wires.
    - Higher data transfer rate but requires more wires.
    - Examples: Parallel bus, parallel port, etc.
2.  Serial Communication:
    - Data is transmitted bit by bit sequentially over a single wire.
    - Lower data transfer rate but requires fewer wires.
    - Examples: UART, SPI, I2C, etc.
3.  Synchronous vs Asynchronous:
    - Synchronous: Data is transmitted in sync with a clock signal. Allows higher speed but more complex.
    - Asynchronous: No separate clock signal. Simpler but lower speed.
4.  Standards:
    - UART: Universal Asynchronous Receiver/Transmitter. Asynchronous serial communication.
    - SPI: Serial Peripheral Interface. Synchronous serial communication. Full-duplex and high speed.
    - I2C: Inter-Integrated Circuit. Synchronous multi-master serial communication.
