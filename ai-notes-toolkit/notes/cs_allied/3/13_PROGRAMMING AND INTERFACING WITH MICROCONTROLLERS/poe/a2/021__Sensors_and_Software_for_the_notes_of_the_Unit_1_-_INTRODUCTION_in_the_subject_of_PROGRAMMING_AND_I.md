### Sensors and Software for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

1. Sensors: Sensors are devices that detect and respond to physical inputs from the environment. They convert the physical quantity into electrical signals which can be measured, recorded and displayed in a useful form. Some commonly used sensors are:
- Temperature sensors: To measure temperature. Ex: Thermistors, Resistance Temperature Detectors (RTDs), Thermocouples, etc.
- Motion sensors: To detect motion or displacement. Ex: PIR sensors, accelerometers, gyroscopes, etc.
- Light sensors: To detect light intensity. Ex: Photoresistors, Photodiodes, etc.
- Pressure sensors: To measure force or pressure. Ex: Strain gauges, capacitive pressure sensors, piezoelectric pressure sensors, etc.

2. Microcontroller and Software: A microcontroller is a tiny computer that is used to control electronic devices and systems. It consists of a processor, memory and input/output peripherals. Software is coded in a programming language understood by the microcontroller to instruct it on what actions to perform based on the inputs. The software controls the microcontroller to acquire data from the sensors, process it and provide meaningful output. Popular microcontrollers used for interfacing with sensors include Arduino, Raspberry Pi, PIC microcontroller, etc.
