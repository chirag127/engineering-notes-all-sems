### Basic Electronics for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Electric Charge: The fundamental property of matter that causes it to experience a force when placed in an electric field. Charge is quantized in units of the elementary charge (e).
2. Current: The rate of flow of electric charge. Measured in amperes (A).
3. Voltage: The amount of "push" provided by a battery or power source to move charge through a circuit. Measured in volts (V).
4. Resistance: A measure of how difficult it is to move charge through a material. Materials can be conductors, semiconductors or insulators. Measured in ohms (?).
5. Ohm's Law: States that current is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance (I=V/R).
6. Series and Parallel Circuits: ways of connecting components in a circuit. In series, the same current flows through all components. In parallel, the voltage is the same across all components.
