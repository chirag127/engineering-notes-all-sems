### History for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

1. The first mechanical computers were developed in the 17th century. They used levers and gears to perform calculations.
2. In the 1940s, the first electronic general-purpose computers were created. They were giant, room-sized machines that could perform calculations thousands of times faster than earlier mechanical calculators.
3. In the 1970s, the microprocessor was invented. Microprocessors are complete computers on a single integrated circuit or chip. They led to the first microcomputers and the first programmer-friendly computers.
4. In the 1980s, the IBM PC and Apple Macintosh popularized the use of the graphical user interface and the computer mouse. This led to an explosion in the sales of personal computers for home and business use.
5. In the late 1990s and 2000s, the growth of the Internet and networks led to new ways of using and interacting with computers, including e-commerce, social networking, and widespread access to information via the World Wide Web.
6. Today, computing devices have become much more advanced, inexpensive, and versatile. They are embedded throughout society in smartphones, vehicles, appliances, and more. Computing has shifted from a focus on processing speed to increased connectivity, mobility, and user experience.
