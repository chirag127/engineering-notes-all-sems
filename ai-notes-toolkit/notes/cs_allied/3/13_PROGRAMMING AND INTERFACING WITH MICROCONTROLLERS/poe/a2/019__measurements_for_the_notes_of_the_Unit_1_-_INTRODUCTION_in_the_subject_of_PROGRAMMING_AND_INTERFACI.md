### Measurements for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1.  Voltage: The amount of "push" or "pressure" in an electrical circuit. Measured in volts (V)
2.  Current: The amount of electric charge flowing through a circuit per unit time. Measured in amperes (A)
3.  Resistance: A material's opposition to the flow of electric current. Measured in ohms (Ω)
4.  Power: The rate at which energy is converted or transferred. Equal to current multiplied by voltage. Measured in watts (W)

The above measurements form the core of understanding any electrical or electronic circuit. Proper understanding of the quantities and their units is essential to designing, analyzing and troubleshooting circuits.
