### Digital vs. Analog circuits

- Digital circuits deal with digital signals that have only two levels of voltage, i.e. high and low or 1 and 0. Analog circuits deal with continuous range of voltages.
- Digital circuits are made up of digital components like transistors, ICs, etc. Analog circuits are made up of components like resistors, capacitors, inductors, etc.
- Digital circuits are more robust and less susceptible to noise. Analog circuits are more prone to noise and interference.
- In digital circuits, signals are interpreted as binary information. In analog circuits, signals are interpreted as continuous information.
- Examples of digital circuits are computers, CPUs, etc. Examples of analog circuits are amplifiers, oscillators, filters, etc.
