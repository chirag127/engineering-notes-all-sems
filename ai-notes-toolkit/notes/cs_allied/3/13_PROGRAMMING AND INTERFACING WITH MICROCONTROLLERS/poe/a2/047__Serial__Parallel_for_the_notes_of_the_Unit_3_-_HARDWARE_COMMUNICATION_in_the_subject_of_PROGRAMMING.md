### Serial & Parallel Communication

- Serial Communication: Data is transmitted one bit at a time, sequentially over a single communication line (wire). Typically uses protocols like UART, I2C, SPI.
    - Pros: Requires fewer pins, less complex wiring.
    - Cons: Slower data transfer rates.
- Parallel Communication: Data is transmitted multiple bits at a time, simultaneously over multiple communication lines (wires). Typically uses buses like GPIO, address/data buses.
    - Pros: Faster data transfer rates.
    - Cons: Requires more pins and more complex wiring.

Notes:
- Choose between serial and parallel based on required speed of data transfer and number of pins available.
- Microcontrollers typically have built-in hardware modules to support serial communication protocols, some may also have parallel communication peripheral modules (like parallel ports).
- Data sheets will specify what communication protocols/buses a microcontroller supports.
