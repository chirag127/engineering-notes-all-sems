
### Communication for the Notes of Unit 3 - Hardware Communication in Programming and Interfacing with Microcontrollers

1. Hardware communication is the process of exchanging data between two or more devices connected to a computer system.

2. It is used to connect various components of a system, such as memory, storage, and input/output (I/O) devices.

3. There are several types of hardware communication protocols, such as Serial, Parallel, USB, Ethernet, and Wireless.

4. Serial communication is the most common form of hardware communication, and is used to connect peripherals to a computer. It is also used to transfer data between two computers.

5. Parallel communication is used to transfer data between two or more devices at the same time. It is typically used to connect printers and scanners to a computer.

6. USB (Universal Serial Bus) is a serial communication protocol used to connect peripherals to a computer. It is the most commonly used connection type for keyboards, mice, and other external devices.

7. Ethernet is a network communication protocol used to connect computers and other network devices. It is commonly used to connect PCs to the Internet.

8. Wireless communication is used to connect devices without the need for a physical connection. It is typically used to connect mobile devices to the Internet or other wireless networks.
