
### Sensors and Software for Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

* Sensors are devices that measure physical parameters such as temperature, pressure, light, and sound. They are used to provide input to microcontrollers and other electronic systems. 
* Software is the instructions that tell a microcontroller what to do. It is usually written in a programming language such as C or Assembly.
* Interfacing is the process of connecting a sensor or other device to a microcontroller. This is usually done using an interface such as an analog-to-digital converter or a serial port.
* Microcontrollers are small, single-chip computers that can be programmed to control a variety of devices. They are commonly used in embedded systems such as robots, appliances, and automobiles.
