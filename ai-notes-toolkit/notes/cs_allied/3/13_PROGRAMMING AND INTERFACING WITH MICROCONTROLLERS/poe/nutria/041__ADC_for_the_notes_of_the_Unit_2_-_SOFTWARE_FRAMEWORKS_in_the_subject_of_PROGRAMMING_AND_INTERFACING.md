
### ADC for the Notes of the Unit 2 - SOFTWARE FRAMEWORKS in the Subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

* An Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC) is a device used to convert an analog signal into a digital signal. 
* The ADC is used to convert analog voltage into digital form.
* An ADC can be used to measure the voltage of a signal.
* ADC is used in many applications, such as measuring temperature, measuring sound levels, and measuring light intensity.
* ADC can be used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, humidity, and current.
* The ADC is used in microcontroller-based systems to convert analog signals into digital signals that can be processed by the microcontroller.
* ADC can be implemented using hardware or software.
* Hardware-based ADC requires external components such as resistors and capacitors.
* Software-based ADC does not require external components, but it is slower than hardware-based ADC.
* ADC is used in embedded systems to convert analog signals into digital signals that can be processed by the microcontroller.
