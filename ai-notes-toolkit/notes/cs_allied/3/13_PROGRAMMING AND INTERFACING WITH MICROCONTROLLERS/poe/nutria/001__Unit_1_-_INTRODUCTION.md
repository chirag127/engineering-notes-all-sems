
## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the beginning of any learning process. It provides an overview of the topic, its objectives, and the key concepts that will be discussed.
2. It is important to understand the basics of the topic before delving into the details. Introduction provides an opportunity to understand the context and the scope of the topic.
3. It is essential to have a clear understanding of the topic before proceeding further. Introduction helps to set the stage for the learning process.
4. Introduction should include the purpose of the topic, the objectives, and the expected outcomes.
5. It should also include any prerequisites that the learner should have prior to beginning the topic.
