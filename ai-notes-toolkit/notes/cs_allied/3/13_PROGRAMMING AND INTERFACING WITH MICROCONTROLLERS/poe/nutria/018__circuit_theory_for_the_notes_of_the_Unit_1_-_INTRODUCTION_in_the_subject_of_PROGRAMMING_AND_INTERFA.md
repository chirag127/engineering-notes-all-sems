

### Circuit Theory for Unit 1 - Introduction in Programming and Interfacing with Microcontrollers

1. Circuit theory is the study of the behavior of electrical circuits. It covers topics such as Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, capacitance, inductance, and more.

2. Electrical circuits are composed of components such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, transistors, and other active devices.

3. The behavior of these components can be described mathematically using Kirchhoff's laws and other equations.

4. Electrical circuits can be used to create a variety of useful devices such as amplifiers, oscillators, filters, and more.

5. Microcontrollers are special types of circuits that can be programmed to perform a variety of tasks.

6. Programming and interfacing with microcontrollers requires a deep understanding of circuit theory and the ability to write code.
