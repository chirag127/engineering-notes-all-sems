
### Serial & Parallel for the notes of the Unit 3 - HARDWARE COMMUNICATION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

1. Serial communication is the process of transmitting data one bit at a time, sequentially, over a communication channel or computer bus. It is used for short-distance data transmission, such as within a building or on a campus.

2. Parallel communication is the simultaneous transmission of multiple bits of data over a number of communication channels or computer buses. It is used for long-distance data transmission, such as between cities or countries.

3. Serial and parallel communication are both used in the field of microcontrollers. Serial communication is used to communicate with peripherals, such as keyboards, mice, and printers, while parallel communication is used to communicate with memory, such as RAM and ROM.

4. Serial communication is typically slower than parallel communication, but requires fewer wires. This makes it more cost-effective for short-distance communication.

5. Parallel communication is typically faster than serial communication, but requires more wires. This makes it more cost-effective for long-distance communication.
