
### Serial UART Communication

Serial UART communication is a form of communication that is used to transfer data between two devices. It is a type of serial communication, meaning that data is sent one bit at a time. UART stands for Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter, and is used to control the flow of data between two devices.

- UART is used to send and receive data between two devices.
- UART communication is asynchronous, meaning that the timing of the data is not synchronized between the two devices.
- UART uses two wires to communicate, one for data and one for a clock signal.
- UART data is sent one bit at a time, at a fixed baud rate.
- UART can be used to control the flow of data between two devices, such as a microcontroller and a computer.
- UART can also be used for communication between two microcontrollers.
