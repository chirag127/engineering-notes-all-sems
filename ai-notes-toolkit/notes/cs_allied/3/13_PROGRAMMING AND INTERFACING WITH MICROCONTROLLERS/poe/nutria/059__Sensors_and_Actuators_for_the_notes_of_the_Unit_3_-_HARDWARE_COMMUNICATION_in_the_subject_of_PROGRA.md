
### Sensors and Actuators for the notes of the Unit 3 - HARDWARE COMMUNICATION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

* Sensors are devices used to detect changes in the environment, such as temperature, light, pressure, sound, and motion. They convert the physical parameters into electrical signals which can be read by a microcontroller.

* Actuators are devices used to control the environment, such as motors, solenoids, and relays. They convert electrical signals from the microcontroller into mechanical action.

* Interfacing with sensors and actuators requires knowledge of the electrical characteristics of the device, such as voltage and current requirements, as well as the protocol used to communicate with it.

* The most common communication protocol used to interface with sensors and actuators is the Serial Peripheral Interface (SPI). SPI is a synchronous serial data protocol that allows a microcontroller to communicate with one or more peripheral devices in a master-slave relationship.

* Interfacing with sensors and actuators also requires knowledge of the physical characteristics of the device, such as its size and shape, as well as the type of output it produces.
