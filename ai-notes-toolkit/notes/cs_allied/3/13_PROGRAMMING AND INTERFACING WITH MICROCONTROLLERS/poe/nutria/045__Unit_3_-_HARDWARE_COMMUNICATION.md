
## Unit 3 - HARDWARE COMMUNICATION

1. Hardware communication is the process of connecting two or more physical devices together in order to exchange data.

2. It involves the use of various physical components, such as cables, connectors, switches, and routers, to enable communication between the devices.

3. There are several different types of hardware communication, including wired, wireless, and optical.

4. Wired communication is the most common form of hardware communication. It involves the use of cables and connectors to connect devices together.

5. Wireless communication is a type of hardware communication that uses radio waves to transmit data between two or more devices.

6. Optical communication is a type of hardware communication that uses light to transmit data. It is often used in high-speed networks and is capable of transmitting data at very high speeds.

7. Hardware communication is an important part of any network, as it allows devices to communicate with each other and exchange data.
