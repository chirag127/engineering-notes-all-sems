
### Safety for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

1. Always wear safety glasses when working with microcontrollers to protect your eyes from flying debris.
2. Wear static-free clothing when handling microcontrollers to reduce the risk of static electricity damaging the device.
3. Always ensure your workspace is well-ventilated and free from dust and debris.
4. Make sure that all power supplies are properly grounded and that all electrical connections are secure.
5. Unplug all power sources before making any changes to the circuit.
6. Use caution when handling sharp objects such as soldering irons and wire cutters.
7. Make sure to follow the manufacturer’s instructions when installing and using the microcontroller.
