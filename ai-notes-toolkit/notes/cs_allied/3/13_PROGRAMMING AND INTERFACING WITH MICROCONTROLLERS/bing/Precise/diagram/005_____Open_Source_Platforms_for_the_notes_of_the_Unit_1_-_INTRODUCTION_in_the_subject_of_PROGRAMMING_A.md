### Open Source Platforms

Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

Programming and Interfacing with Microcontrollers

1. Open source platforms refer to software or hardware systems whose source code or design is made available to the public for use or modification.
2. These platforms are often developed and maintained by a community of contributors and users.
3. Some popular open source platforms for microcontroller programming and interfacing include Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and BeagleBone.
4. These platforms provide a range of tools and resources for developing and prototyping microcontroller-based projects.
5. Open source platforms offer several advantages, including low cost, flexibility, and a large and active community of users and developers.
6. They also provide opportunities for learning and collaboration, making them a popular choice for educational and hobbyist projects.
