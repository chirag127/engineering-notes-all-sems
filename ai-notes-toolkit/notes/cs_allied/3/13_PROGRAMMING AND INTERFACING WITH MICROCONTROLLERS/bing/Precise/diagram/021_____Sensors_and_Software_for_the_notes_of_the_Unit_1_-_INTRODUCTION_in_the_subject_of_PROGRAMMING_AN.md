### Sensors and Software

#### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

- Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment.
- They convert physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, and light into electrical signals that can be processed by a computer.
- Sensors are used in a wide range of applications, including home automation, medical devices, and industrial control systems.
- Software is used to process the data from sensors and make decisions based on that data.
- Programming languages such as C and Python are commonly used to write software for microcontrollers.
- Microcontrollers are small, low-power computers that are used to control sensors and other devices.
- Interfacing with microcontrollers involves connecting sensors and other devices to the microcontroller and writing software to control those devices.
- This unit will provide an introduction to sensors and software for programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
