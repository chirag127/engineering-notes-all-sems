### Unit 3 - HARDWARE COMMUNICATION: Sensors and Actuators

Sensors and actuators are essential components in the field of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers. They enable the microcontroller to interact with the physical world by converting physical parameters into electrical signals and vice versa.

#### Sensors
- Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment.
- They convert physical parameters such as temperature, light, sound, and pressure into electrical signals that can be processed by the microcontroller.
- Common types of sensors include temperature sensors, light sensors, sound sensors, and pressure sensors.

#### Actuators
- Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions.
- They enable the microcontroller to control physical systems by converting electrical signals into mechanical movements or other physical outputs.
- Common types of actuators include motors, solenoids, and LEDs.

In summary, sensors and actuators are essential components in the field of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers. They enable the microcontroller to interact with the physical world by converting physical parameters into electrical signals and vice versa. Sensors detect changes in the environment and convert them into electrical signals, while actuators convert electrical signals into physical actions. Together, sensors and actuators enable the microcontroller to sense and control the physical world.
