### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION
#### Safety
- Safety is an important aspect of programming and interfacing with microcontrollers.
- Microcontrollers are electronic devices that can control other devices and systems. They are used in a wide range of applications, including consumer electronics, automotive systems, and industrial control systems.
- When working with microcontrollers, it is important to follow safety guidelines to prevent damage to the microcontroller, other devices, or the user.
- Some safety guidelines to follow when working with microcontrollers include:
  - Always read the datasheet and user manual for the microcontroller and any other devices being used.
  - Use proper tools and equipment, such as a multimeter, oscilloscope, and power supply.
  - Be aware of the voltage and current ratings of the microcontroller and other devices being used.
  - Use proper grounding and electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection.
  - Be cautious when working with high voltages or currents.
  - Follow proper procedures for handling, storing, and disposing of electronic components.
- By following these safety guidelines, users can ensure that they are working with microcontrollers in a safe and responsible manner.
