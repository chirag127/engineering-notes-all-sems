### Desktop

- A desktop is a personal computer designed for regular use at a single location on or near a desk or table due to its size and power requirements.
- The most common configuration has a case that houses the power supply, motherboard, disk storage, and peripheral interfaces, with a display screen, keyboard, and mouse for input.
- Desktops have an advantage over laptops in that the spare parts and extensions tend to be standardized, resulting in lower prices and greater availability.
- Desktops can be more easily upgraded and have more options for upgrades, making them more versatile than laptops.
- Desktops are also generally more reliable than laptops due to their stationary nature and the fact that they are less likely to be dropped or damaged.
- Desktops are commonly used in businesses, schools, and homes for a variety of tasks, including word processing, web browsing, gaming, and media playback.
