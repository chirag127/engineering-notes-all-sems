### DAC
A Digital-to-Analog Converter (DAC) is a device that converts digital signals into analog signals. It is used in many applications, including audio and video systems, instrumentation, and control systems. Here are some key points to remember about DACs:

1. DACs are used to convert digital signals into analog signals.
2. The resolution of a DAC is determined by the number of bits it uses to represent the analog signal.
3. The accuracy of a DAC is determined by how well it can reproduce the input digital signal.
4. There are several types of DACs, including binary-weighted, R-2R ladder, and sigma-delta DACs.
5. DACs can be used in many applications, including audio and video systems, instrumentation, and control systems.
