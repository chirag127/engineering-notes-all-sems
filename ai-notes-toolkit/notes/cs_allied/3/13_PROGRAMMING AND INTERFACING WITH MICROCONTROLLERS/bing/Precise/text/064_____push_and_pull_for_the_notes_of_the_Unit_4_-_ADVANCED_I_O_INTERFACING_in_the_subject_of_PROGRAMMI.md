### Push and Pull for the notes of the Unit 4 - ADVANCED I/O INTERFACING in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

- Push and pull are two different methods of driving an output pin of a microcontroller.
- In push mode, the microcontroller actively drives the output pin to a high or low voltage level.
- In pull mode, the microcontroller uses a resistor to weakly pull the output pin to a high or low voltage level.
- The choice between push and pull mode depends on the requirements of the circuit connected to the output pin.
- Push mode is typically used when the circuit requires a strong drive current, such as when driving an LED or a relay.
- Pull mode is typically used when the circuit requires a weak drive current, such as when interfacing with a high-impedance input.
- Some microcontrollers have configurable output pins that can be set to either push or pull mode.
- It is important to carefully consider the requirements of the circuit when choosing between push and pull mode to ensure proper operation of the circuit.
