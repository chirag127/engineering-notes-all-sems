### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

#### Basic Electronics

1. Electronics is the branch of science and technology that deals with the study of flow and control of electrons and the use of electronic devices.
2. The basic components of electronics include resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, and transistors.
3. A resistor is a passive two-terminal electrical component that implements electrical resistance as a circuit element.
4. A capacitor is a passive two-terminal electrical component that stores electrical energy in an electric field.
5. An inductor is a passive two-terminal electrical component that stores energy in a magnetic field when electric current flows through it.
6. A diode is a two-terminal electronic component that conducts current primarily in one direction.
7. A transistor is a semiconductor device used to amplify or switch electronic signals and electrical power.
8. These basic components can be combined to form more complex electronic circuits and systems.
9. Understanding the properties and behavior of these basic components is essential for the study of electronics and the design of electronic systems.
