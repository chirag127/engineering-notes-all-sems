### Open Frameworks

OpenFrameworks is an open-source C++ toolkit designed to assist the creative process by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation. It is commonly used for developing applications that involve real-time video, audio, graphics, and interaction.

Here are some key points to note about OpenFrameworks:

1. OpenFrameworks is designed to work as a general-purpose glue, and wraps together several commonly used libraries under a tidy interface.
2. It can be used for prototyping and developing applications across multiple platforms, including Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android.
3. OpenFrameworks is designed to be a starting point for creative coding, and provides a solid foundation for building complex interactive applications.
4. It is built around the idea of making it easy to work with common media types, such as images, sound, and video, and provides a simple API for working with these media types.
5. OpenFrameworks is community-driven, and has a large and active community of developers and artists who contribute to the project and provide support to other users.
