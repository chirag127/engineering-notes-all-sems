### Safety

1. When working with microcontrollers, it is important to take proper safety precautions to avoid injury or damage to equipment.
2. Always wear protective gear such as safety glasses and gloves when handling microcontrollers and other electronic components.
3. Be aware of the electrical hazards associated with working with microcontrollers and take steps to minimize the risk of electric shock.
4. Follow the manufacturer's instructions and guidelines when using microcontrollers and other electronic components.
5. Use caution when handling microcontrollers and other electronic components to avoid damaging them.
6. Keep your work area clean and organized to minimize the risk of accidents.
7. Dispose of electronic waste properly to avoid environmental harm.
8. Be aware of the potential for fire and take steps to minimize the risk, such as keeping flammable materials away from heat sources.
9. Always use the proper tools and equipment when working with microcontrollers to avoid injury or damage to the equipment.
10. Keep up to date with the latest safety guidelines and best practices to ensure that you are working safely with microcontrollers.
