# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### Mobile phones and similar devices

- Mobile phones, also known as cell phones or cellular phones, are handheld devices that allow users to make and receive phone calls and send and receive text messages.
- Mobile phones use cellular network technology to connect to a network of base stations, which are connected to the public switched telephone network (PSTN).
- In addition to basic communication functions, modern mobile phones also have a wide range of additional features, such as internet access, cameras, music players, and GPS navigation.
- Similar devices to mobile phones include smartphones, tablets, and personal digital assistants (PDAs).
- Smartphones are mobile phones that have advanced computing capabilities and connectivity options, allowing users to run a wide range of applications and access the internet.
- Tablets are similar to smartphones, but have larger screens and are primarily used for consuming media and browsing the internet.
- PDAs, or personal digital assistants, are handheld devices that were popular in the late 1990s and early 2000s. They were primarily used for organizing and managing personal information, such as contacts, calendars, and notes.
- Mobile phones and similar devices have revolutionized the way we communicate and access information, and have become an integral part of our daily lives.
