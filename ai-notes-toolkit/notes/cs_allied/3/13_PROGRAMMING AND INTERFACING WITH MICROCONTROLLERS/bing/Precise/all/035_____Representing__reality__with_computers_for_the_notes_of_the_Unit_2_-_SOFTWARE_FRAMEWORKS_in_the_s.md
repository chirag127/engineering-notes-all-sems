# Representing “reality” with computers

- Computers use a variety of methods to represent and simulate reality.
- One common method is through the use of **graphics**. Computers can generate and display images and animations that mimic real-world objects and scenes.
- Another method is through the use of **data structures**. Computers can store and manipulate data in ways that represent real-world information and relationships.
- **Simulation** is another way that computers can represent reality. By creating models of real-world systems and processes, computers can simulate their behavior and predict their outcomes.
- **Virtual reality** and **augmented reality** are technologies that allow users to interact with computer-generated simulations of reality in immersive and interactive ways.
- These methods and technologies are constantly evolving, allowing computers to represent reality in increasingly accurate and sophisticated ways.
