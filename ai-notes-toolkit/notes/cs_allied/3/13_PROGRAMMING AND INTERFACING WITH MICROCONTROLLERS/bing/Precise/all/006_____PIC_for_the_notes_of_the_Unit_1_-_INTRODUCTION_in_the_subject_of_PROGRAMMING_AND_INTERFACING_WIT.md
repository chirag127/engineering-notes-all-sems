# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

### PIC

- PIC stands for Peripheral Interface Controller.
- It is a family of microcontrollers developed by Microchip Technology.
- PIC microcontrollers are popular due to their ease of use, wide availability, and low cost.
- They are commonly used in embedded systems and for interfacing with various peripherals.
- PIC microcontrollers are available in a range of sizes and capabilities, from 8-bit to 32-bit, with varying amounts of memory and peripheral options.
- They are programmed using a variety of languages, including assembly, C, and BASIC.
- PIC microcontrollers are known for their low power consumption and robustness, making them suitable for use in a wide range of applications.
- Some common applications of PIC microcontrollers include industrial control systems, automotive systems, and consumer electronics.
