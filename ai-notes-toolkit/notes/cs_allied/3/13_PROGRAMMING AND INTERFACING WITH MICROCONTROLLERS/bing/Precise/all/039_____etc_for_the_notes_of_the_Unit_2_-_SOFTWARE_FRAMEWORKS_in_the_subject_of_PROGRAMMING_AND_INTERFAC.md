### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE FRAMEWORKS

1. A software framework is a reusable environment that provides a structure for developing software applications.
2. Frameworks are designed to facilitate the development process by providing pre-built components and libraries that can be used to build applications.
3. Frameworks can help to reduce the amount of time and effort required to develop an application by providing a set of tools and conventions that developers can follow.
4. Some common examples of software frameworks include Angular, React, and Vue for front-end web development, and Spring, Django, and Ruby on Rails for back-end development.
5. Frameworks can also provide a level of abstraction, allowing developers to focus on the specific functionality of their application rather than the underlying details of the technology stack.
6. When choosing a framework, it is important to consider factors such as the programming language, the level of abstraction, the community and support, and the ease of use and learning curve.
7. Frameworks can also have an impact on the performance, scalability, and maintainability of an application, so these factors should also be considered when making a decision.
