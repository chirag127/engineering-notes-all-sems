### DAC

DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is an electronic device that converts digital signals into analog signals. In the context of Unit 2 - SOFTWARE FRAMEWORKS in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS, a DAC is used to convert digital signals from a microcontroller into analog signals that can be used to control external devices.

Some key points to remember about DACs are:

1. DACs are used to convert digital signals into analog signals.
2. They are commonly used in microcontroller systems to control external devices.
3. The accuracy of a DAC is determined by its resolution, which is the number of bits used to represent the analog signal.
4. There are several types of DACs, including binary-weighted, R-2R ladder, and sigma-delta DACs.
5. The choice of DAC depends on the application and the desired accuracy and performance.
