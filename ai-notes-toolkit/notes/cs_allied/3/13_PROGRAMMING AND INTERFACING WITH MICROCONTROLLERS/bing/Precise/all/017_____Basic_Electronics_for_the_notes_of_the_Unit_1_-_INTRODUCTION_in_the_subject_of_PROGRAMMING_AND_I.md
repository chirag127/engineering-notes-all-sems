### Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

#### Basic Electronics

1. Electronics is the branch of science and technology that deals with the study of flow and control of electrons and the use of electronic devices.
2. The basic components of electronics include resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, and transistors.
3. A resistor is a passive two-terminal electrical component that implements electrical resistance as a circuit element. It is used to reduce current flow, adjust signal levels, and divide voltages.
4. A capacitor is a passive two-terminal electrical component that stores electrical energy in an electric field. It is used to block direct current while allowing alternating current to pass, to filter out interference, and to store energy.
5. An inductor is a passive two-terminal electrical component that stores energy in a magnetic field. It is used to oppose changes in current, to filter out high-frequency signals, and to store energy.
6. A diode is a two-terminal electronic component that conducts current primarily in one direction. It is used to convert alternating current to direct current, to protect circuits from voltage spikes, and to regulate voltage.
7. A transistor is a three-terminal electronic component that can amplify or switch electronic signals. It is used to amplify signals, to switch signals on and off, and to regulate voltage.
