# Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION: Parts Identification

1. **Microcontroller**: A microcontroller is a small computer on a single integrated circuit that is designed to control a specific operation in an embedded system.
2. **CPU**: The Central Processing Unit (CPU) is the brain of the microcontroller that executes instructions and performs calculations.
3. **Memory**: The memory of a microcontroller is used to store data and program instructions. It is divided into two types: program memory and data memory.
4. **Input/Output Ports**: Input/Output (I/O) ports are used to connect the microcontroller to external devices and sensors. They can be configured as inputs to read data or as outputs to control devices.
5. **Timers and Counters**: Timers and counters are used to measure time intervals and count events. They can be used for tasks such as generating precise time delays or measuring the frequency of a signal.
6. **Analog to Digital Converter**: An Analog to Digital Converter (ADC) is used to convert an analog signal into a digital value that can be read by the microcontroller.
7. **Serial Communication Interface**: A serial communication interface is used to transmit and receive data between the microcontroller and other devices using a serial protocol such as UART, SPI, or I2C.
8. **Interrupts**: Interrupts are used to temporarily halt the normal execution of the microcontroller and perform a specific task in response to an external or internal event.
