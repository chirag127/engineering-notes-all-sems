### DAC

DAC stands for Digital-to-Analog Converter. It is a system that converts a digital signal into an analog signal. A DAC is used in many applications such as music players, mobile phones, and televisions.

Here are some key points to remember about DACs:

1. A DAC converts a digital signal into an analog signal by using a reference voltage and a digital code.
2. The resolution of a DAC is determined by the number of bits in the digital code.
3. The accuracy of a DAC is determined by the reference voltage and the linearity of the conversion.
4. There are several types of DACs, including binary-weighted, R-2R ladder, and sigma-delta.
5. DACs can be used in many applications, including audio and video playback, signal generation, and control systems.
