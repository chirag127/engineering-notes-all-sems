## Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION

1. Introduction is the first chapter of any subject or topic.
2. It provides an overview of the subject or topic being studied.
3. It sets the stage for the rest of the material by providing background information and context.
4. It is important to read and understand the introduction before proceeding to the rest of the material.
5. The introduction may include definitions of key terms, a brief history of the subject, and the scope and objectives of the material.
6. It is important to pay attention to the introduction as it lays the foundation for the rest of the material.
