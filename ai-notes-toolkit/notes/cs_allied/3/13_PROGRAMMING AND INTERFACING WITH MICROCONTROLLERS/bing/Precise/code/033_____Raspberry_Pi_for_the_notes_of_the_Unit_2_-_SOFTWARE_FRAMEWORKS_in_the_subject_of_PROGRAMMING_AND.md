### Unit 2 - SOFTWARE FRAMEWORKS
#### Raspberry Pi

- Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed in the United Kingdom by the Raspberry Pi Foundation.
- The original model became popular for its low cost and small size, making it suitable for use in education, robotics, and home automation projects.
- The Raspberry Pi runs a variety of operating systems, including Raspbian, a Debian-based Linux distribution, and other Linux distributions such as Ubuntu and Arch Linux.
- The Raspberry Pi also supports other operating systems such as Windows 10 IoT Core, a version of Windows 10 designed for use on Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
- The Raspberry Pi has a large and active community of developers and users, who contribute to the development of software and hardware projects for the platform.
- The Raspberry Pi Foundation provides extensive documentation and tutorials for using the Raspberry Pi, making it accessible to users of all skill levels.
- The Raspberry Pi can be used with a variety of programming languages, including Python, C, C++, Java, and Scratch.
- The Raspberry Pi has a number of expansion boards, called HATs (Hardware Attached on Top), which can be used to add additional functionality to the device, such as sensors, displays, and motor controllers.
- The Raspberry Pi is a versatile platform that can be used for a wide range of projects, from simple educational tools to complex robotics and automation systems.
