### openFrameworks

- openFrameworks is an **open source C++ toolkit** for creative coding  .
- It is designed to **assist the creative process** by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation.
- It is a **cross-platform toolkit** that supports as many development environments and operating systems as possible, such as Windows, Linux, macOS, iOS, Android, Raspberry Pi, etc .
- It **wraps together several commonly used libraries**, including OpenGL, GLEW, GLUT, libtess2 and cairo for graphics, rtAudio, PortAudio or FMOD for audio input and output, FreeType for fonts, FreeImage for image saving and loading, Quicktime or GStreamer for video playback and grabber, Poco for a variety of utilities, OpenCV for computer vision, Assimp for 3D model loading, and Boost for some specific algorithms.
- It also provides a **set of core functionalities** that can be used to create a variety of applications, such as 2D and 3D graphics, sound, video, computer vision, networking, events, math, etc.
- It is a **community-driven project** that welcomes contributions from anyone who wants to improve, document, test or write examples for the toolkit.
- It is **licensed under the MIT License**, which means it is free to use for any purpose, including commercial projects, and can be modified and redistributed as long as the original license and authors are credited.
