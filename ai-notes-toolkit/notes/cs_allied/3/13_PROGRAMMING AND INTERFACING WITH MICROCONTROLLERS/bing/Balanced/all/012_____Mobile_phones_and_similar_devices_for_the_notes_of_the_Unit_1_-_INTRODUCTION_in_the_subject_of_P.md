# Mobile phones and similar devices

- Mobile phones are handheld devices that allow users to make and receive calls, send and receive text messages, access the internet, take photos, play music, use GPS and other applications.
- Smartphones are a type of mobile phone that have more advanced features and capabilities, such as touchscreens, high-resolution cameras, web browsers, email clients, social media apps, video calling, gaming, etc.
- PDAs (Personal Digital Assistants) are handheld devices that have similar functions to smartphones, but are mainly used for personal information management, such as calendars, contacts, notes, etc. Some PDAs can also make and receive calls, access the internet and run other applications.
- Tablets are larger than mobile phones and PDAs, but smaller than laptops. They have touchscreens and can run various applications, such as web browsing, email, gaming, e-books, etc. Some tablets can also make and receive calls, use cellular data and have cameras.
- Smartwatches are wearable devices that can connect to mobile phones or the internet and display notifications, messages, calls, fitness data, etc. Some smartwatches can also have standalone functions, such as GPS, music, voice control, etc.
- Some examples of mobile phones and similar devices are:

  - Apple iPhone: a popular smartphone that runs on iOS operating system and has a range of features, such as Face ID, Siri, Apple Pay, etc. 
  - Samsung Galaxy S22: a powerful Android flagship phone that has a high-end camera, fast processor, long battery life, etc. 
  - HP iPAQ Mobile Messenger: a PDA that has a QWERTY keyboard, a touch screen, a camera, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, etc. 
  - LG Prada: a mobile phone that has a touch screen, a camera, a music player, etc. but is not a smartphone. 
  - iPad: a tablet that runs on iOS operating system and has a large display, a camera, a microphone, a speaker, etc. 
  - Samsung Galaxy Tab: a tablet that runs on Android operating system and has a SIM card slot, a camera, a microphone, a speaker, etc. 
  - Apple Watch: a smartwatch that can pair with an iPhone and has a heart rate sensor, a gyroscope, an accelerometer, etc. 
  - Samsung Galaxy Watch: a smartwatch that can pair with an Android phone and has a blood pressure monitor, an ECG sensor, a GPS, etc.
