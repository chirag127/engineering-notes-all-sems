### Baby steps to Internet of Things

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies to connect and exchange data with other devices and systems over the internet  .
- IoT devices can range from ordinary household objects to sophisticated industrial tools, and can collect and share data about their usage and environment  .
- IoT enables various applications and benefits for different domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart manufacturing, and smart energy .
- The four pillars of IoT are data, device, analytics, and connectivity:
  - Data is the fuel of IoT and is collected by the devices from the physical world.
  - Device is the physical component or thing that collects the data and communicates with other devices or systems.
  - Analytics is the process of transforming the data into insights and actions that can improve the performance or efficiency of the devices or systems.
  - Connectivity is the means of transferring the data between the devices or systems, using various technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular, or satellite.
