### PIC

- PIC stands for **Peripheral Interface Controller**  , a type of microcontroller that was developed by **General Instruments** in 1985 and later by **Microchip Technologies** in 1993 .
- PIC microcontrollers are designed to enable simple programming and interfacing in embedded system design. They are widely used in various electronic devices such as smartphones, medical instruments, and acoustic devices.
- PIC microcontrollers have a **Harvard architecture**, which means they have separate buses for data and instruction memory. This allows faster and more efficient execution of instructions.
- PIC microcontrollers have different families, such as PIC10, PIC12, PIC16, PIC18, PIC24, PIC32, etc. Each family has different features, such as memory size, number of pins, instruction set, peripherals, etc. One of the most popular PIC microcontrollers is the **PIC16F84** .
- PIC microcontrollers can be programmed using different software tools, such as MPLAB IDE, PIC C Compiler, PIC Basic, etc. They can also be interfaced with various hardware devices, such as LCD, keypad, sensors, motors, etc. using different protocols, such as SPI, I2C, UART, etc.
