### Measurements for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

- Measurements are the process of obtaining the magnitude of a quantity relative to an agreed standard.
- Measurements are essential for designing, testing and debugging microcontroller-based systems.
- Measurements can be classified into two types: analog and digital.
- Analog measurements deal with continuous signals that vary in amplitude and frequency, such as voltage, current, resistance, temperature, etc.
- Digital measurements deal with discrete signals that have only two states: high or low, such as logic levels, binary numbers, pulses, etc.
- Measurements can be performed using various instruments, such as multimeters, oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, etc.
- Measurements can be interfaced with microcontrollers using various methods, such as analog-to-digital converters (ADCs), digital-to-analog converters (DACs), serial communication protocols, etc.
- Measurements can be displayed or stored using various devices, such as LCDs, LEDs, memory chips, etc.
- Measurements can be analyzed and processed using various techniques, such as arithmetic operations, logical operations, bitwise operations, etc.
