### openFrameworks

- openFrameworks is an open source C++ toolkit for creative coding  .
- It is designed to assist the creative process by providing a simple and intuitive framework for experimentation.
- It is a cross-platform toolkit that supports as many development environments and operating systems as possible, such as Windows, Linux, macOS, iOS, Android, Raspberry Pi, etc .
- It wraps together several commonly used libraries, such as OpenGL, GLEW, GLUT, libtess2 and cairo for graphics, rtAudio, PortAudio or FMOD for audio input and output, FreeType for fonts, FreeImage for image saving and loading, Quicktime or GStreamer for video playback and grabbing, and Poco for a variety of utilities.
- It also has a large collection of addons that extend its functionality, such as ofxOpenCV for computer vision, ofxKinect for interfacing with Kinect sensors, ofxOsc for sending and receiving OSC messages, and many more.
- It follows a set of coding conventions and style guidelines that make the code consistent and easy to read.
- It has a comprehensive documentation that includes reference for openFrameworks classes, functions and addons, as well as tutorials, examples, videos and books.
- It has a vibrant and supportive community that contributes to the development, maintenance and improvement of the toolkit, as well as sharing projects, ideas and feedback through the forum, GitHub, social media and events .
