### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - INTRODUCTION in the subject of PROGRAMMING AND INTERFACING WITH MICROCONTROLLERS

- A microcontroller is a small computer on a single integrated circuit that can be programmed to perform various tasks.
- A microcontroller consists of a central processing unit (CPU), memory, input/output (I/O) ports, timers, serial communication interfaces, analog-to-digital converters, and other peripherals.
- A microcontroller can be used to control devices such as sensors, motors, displays, keyboards, etc. by interfacing with them through the I/O ports.
- Programming a microcontroller involves writing instructions in a specific language (such as assembly or C) that tell the microcontroller what to do and how to interact with the external devices.
- Interfacing a microcontroller with external devices requires understanding the electrical characteristics, protocols, and standards of the devices and the microcontroller.
- Some examples of microcontroller applications are embedded systems, robotics, Internet of Things, smart home, wearable devices, etc.
