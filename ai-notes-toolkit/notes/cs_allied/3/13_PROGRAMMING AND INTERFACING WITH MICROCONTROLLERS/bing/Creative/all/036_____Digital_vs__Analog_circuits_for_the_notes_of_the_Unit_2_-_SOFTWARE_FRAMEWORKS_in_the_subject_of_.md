# Digital vs. Analog circuits

- Digital circuits operate on signals that have only two discrete values, usually called 0 and 1. Analog circuits operate on signals that vary continuously in amplitude and frequency.
- Digital circuits are easier to design and implement than analog circuits, as they use simple logic gates and binary arithmetic. Analog circuits require more precision and expertise in component selection and placement, as they are sensitive to noise and interference.
- Digital circuits can process and store large amounts of data, and can perform complex operations using algorithms and software. Analog circuits are more suitable for applications that require high speed, low power, and natural representation of signals, such as audio and video processing.
