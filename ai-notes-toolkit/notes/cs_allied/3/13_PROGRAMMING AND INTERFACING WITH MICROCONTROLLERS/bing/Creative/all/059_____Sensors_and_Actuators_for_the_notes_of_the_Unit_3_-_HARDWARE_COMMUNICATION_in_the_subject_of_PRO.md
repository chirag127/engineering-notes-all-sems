# Sensors and Actuators

## Definition

- A **sensor** is a device that produces an output signal for the purpose of sensing a physical phenomenon. Sensors identify events or changes in the environment and transmit this information to other devices. Sensors are placed at the input port of a system. Sensors measure both continuous process and discrete variables.
- An **actuator** is a component of a machine that is responsible for moving and controlling a mechanism or system. Actuators receive signals from control modules to perform physical actions. Actuators are fixed at the output port of a system.

## Use-Cases

- Sensors and actuators are a vital part of the next phase of industry. They often work together to automate and streamline industrial processes, such as facility management and manufacturing. They can also help prevent equipment failures and extend the life of assets.
- Some examples of sensors and actuators are:
  - Temperature sensors and heaters
  - Light sensors and LEDs
  - Pressure sensors and valves
  - Accelerometers and motors
  - Microphones and speakers
  - Cameras and displays
  - Touch sensors and haptic feedback devices
  - GPS and navigation systems
  - Biometric sensors and security systems
  - Gas sensors and alarms
