## Unit 4 - ADVANCED I/O INTERFACING

- This unit covers the concepts and techniques of interfacing input/output devices with microprocessors and microcontrollers.
- The main topics are:
  - Serial communication protocols, such as UART, SPI, I2C, and CAN.
  - Parallel communication protocols, such as ISA, PCI, and PCI Express.
  - Interrupts and interrupt handling, such as polling, vectored, and prioritized interrupts.
  - Direct memory access (DMA) and DMA controllers, such as single, block, and demand transfer modes.
  - Timers and counters, such as programmable interval timers, watchdog timers, and frequency measurement.
  - Analog-to-digital converters (ADCs) and digital-to-analog converters (DACs), such as successive approximation, dual slope, and sigma-delta converters.
  - Sensors and actuators, such as temperature, pressure, light, and sound sensors, and stepper motors, relays, and solenoids.
- The learning objectives are:
  - To understand the principles and applications of serial and parallel communication protocols.
  - To design and implement interrupt-driven and DMA-based I/O systems.
  - To use timers and counters for timing and frequency control.
  - To interface analog and digital signals with microprocessors and microcontrollers.
  - To select and use appropriate sensors and actuators for various applications.
