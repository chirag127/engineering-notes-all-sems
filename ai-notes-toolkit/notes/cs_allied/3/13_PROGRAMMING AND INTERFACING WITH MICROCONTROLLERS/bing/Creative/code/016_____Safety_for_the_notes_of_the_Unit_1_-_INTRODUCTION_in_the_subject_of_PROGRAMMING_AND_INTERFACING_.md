### Safety
- Safety is the state of being protected from harm or other non-desirable outcomes.
- Safety can also refer to the control of recognized hazards in order to achieve an acceptable level of risk.
- Safety is important for microcontroller programming and interfacing because it involves working with electrical circuits, devices, and signals that can cause damage, injury, or fire if not handled properly.
- Some of the safety precautions that should be followed when working with microcontrollers are:

  - Use a regulated power supply that matches the voltage and current requirements of the microcontroller and the circuit.
  - Avoid touching the circuit or the microcontroller pins when the power is on, as it can cause electric shock or damage the components.
  - Use proper tools and equipment, such as a multimeter, an oscilloscope, a soldering iron, and a breadboard, and follow the manufacturer's instructions for their use and maintenance.
  - Wear protective gear, such as gloves, goggles, and a mask, when soldering or cutting wires, as it can produce fumes, sparks, or sharp edges that can harm the eyes, skin, or lungs.
  - Keep the work area clean and organized, and dispose of any waste materials properly.
  - Follow the safety rules and regulations of the laboratory, classroom, or workplace where the microcontroller programming and interfacing is done.
