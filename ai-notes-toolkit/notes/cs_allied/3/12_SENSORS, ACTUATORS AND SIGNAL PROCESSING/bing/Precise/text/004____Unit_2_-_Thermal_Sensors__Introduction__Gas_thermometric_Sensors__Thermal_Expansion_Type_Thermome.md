## Unit 2 - Thermal Sensors

Thermal sensors are devices that measure temperature or temperature gradients. There are many types of thermal sensors, each with its own unique characteristics and applications.

1. **Introduction:** Thermal sensors are used in a wide range of applications, including industrial processes, environmental monitoring, and medical diagnostics.

2. **Gas thermometric Sensors:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the pressure or volume of a gas. They are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

3. **Thermal Expansion Type Thermometric Sensors:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the physical dimensions of a material due to thermal expansion. They are commonly used in thermostats and temperature gauges.

4. **Acoustic Temperature Sensor:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the speed of sound in a medium. They are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

5. **Dielectric Constant and Refractive Index Thermo-sensors:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the dielectric constant or refractive index of a material. They are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

6. **Helium Low Temperature Thermometer:** This type of thermometer uses the properties of helium to measure very low temperatures. It is commonly used in cryogenic applications.

7. **Nuclear Thermometer:** This type of thermometer uses the properties of atomic nuclei to measure temperature. It is commonly used in high-temperature applications.

8. **Magnetic Thermometer:** This type of thermometer uses the properties of magnetism to measure temperature. It is commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

9. **Resistance Change Type Thermometric Sensors:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the electrical resistance of a material. They are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

10. **Thermo-EMF Sensors:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the electromotive force (EMF) generated by a thermocouple. They are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

11. **Junction Semiconductor Types:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the voltage across a semiconductor junction. They are commonly used in electronic devices.

12. **Thermal Radiation Sensors:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting the thermal radiation emitted by an object. They are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.

13. **Quartz Crystal Thermo-electric Sensors:** These sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the resonant frequency of a quartz crystal. They are commonly used in precision temperature measurement applications.

14. **NQR Thermometry:** This type of thermometry uses the properties of nuclear quadrupole resonance (NQR) to measure temperature. It is commonly used in high-precision temperature measurement applications.

15. **Spectroscopic Thermometry:** This type of thermometry uses the properties of spectroscopy to measure temperature. It is commonly used in high-precision temperature measurement applications.

16. **Noise Thermometry:** This type of thermometry uses the properties of noise to measure temperature. It is commonly used in high-precision temperature measurement applications.

17. **Heat Flux Sensors:** These sensors measure the rate of heat transfer across a surface. They are commonly used in industrial and laboratory settings.
