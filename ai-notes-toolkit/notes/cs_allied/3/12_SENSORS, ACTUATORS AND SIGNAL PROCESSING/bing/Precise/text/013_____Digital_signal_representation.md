### Digital Signal Representation

Digital signals are a representation of a physical quantity in a discrete form. They are used to represent information in digital systems such as computers and communication systems. Here are some key points to understand about digital signal representation:

1. Digital signals are represented using a finite number of discrete values, typically binary digits (bits) of 0 and 1.
2. The process of converting an analog signal into a digital signal is called analog-to-digital conversion (ADC).
3. The resolution of a digital signal is determined by the number of bits used to represent each sample of the signal.
4. The sampling rate, or the number of samples taken per second, is an important factor in determining the quality of the digital representation of an analog signal.
5. Digital signals can be processed and manipulated using digital signal processing techniques, which can include filtering, compression, and error correction.
6. Digital signals can be transmitted over communication channels using various modulation techniques, which encode the digital information onto a carrier signal for transmission.
