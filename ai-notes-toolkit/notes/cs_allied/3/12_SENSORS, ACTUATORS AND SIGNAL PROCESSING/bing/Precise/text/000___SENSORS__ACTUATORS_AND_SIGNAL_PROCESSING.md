# SENSORS, ACTUATORS AND SIGNAL PROCESSING

Sensors, actuators, and signal processing are three key components of any control system. Here is an overview of each component:

1. **Sensors** are devices that detect and measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, and light. They convert these quantities into electrical signals that can be processed by a computer or control system.

2. **Actuators** are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions. For example, a motor is an actuator that converts electrical energy into mechanical motion.

3. **Signal processing** involves manipulating the electrical signals generated by sensors to extract useful information. This can include filtering out noise, amplifying weak signals, and converting analog signals into digital signals.

Together, these three components allow a control system to monitor its environment, make decisions based on that information, and take action to achieve a desired outcome.
