## Unit 2 - Thermal Sensors

### Introduction
Thermal sensors have been intensively developed and used in a wide range of applications from monitoring of industrial processes to environmental control. Thermal sensors are capable of measuring various thermal signals such as temperature, flow, acceleration, and angular velocity.

### Gas Thermometric Sensors
Gas thermometric sensors are a type of thermal sensor that measures temperature by detecting changes in the pressure or volume of a gas.

### Thermal Expansion Type Thermometric Sensors
Thermal expansion type thermometric sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the physical dimensions of a material due to thermal expansion.

### Acoustic Temperature Sensor
Acoustic temperature sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the speed of sound in a medium.

### Dielectric Constant and Refractive Index Thermo-sensors
Dielectric constant and refractive index thermo-sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the dielectric constant or refractive index of a material.

### Helium Low Temperature Thermometer
Helium low temperature thermometers measure temperature by detecting changes in the thermal properties of helium gas.

### Nuclear Thermometer
Nuclear thermometers measure temperature by detecting changes in the nuclear properties of a material.

### Magnetic Thermometer
Magnetic thermometers measure temperature by detecting changes in the magnetic properties of a material.

### Resistance Change Type Thermometric Sensors
Resistance change type thermometric sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the electrical resistance of a material. An example of this type of sensor is the thermistor, which is a temperature-sensing device whose resistance is a function of its temperature. Thermistors are available in two types: PTC (positive temperature coefficient) and NTC (negative temperature coefficient). The resistance of a PTC thermistor increases as the temperature increases.

### Thermo-EMF Sensors
Thermo-EMF sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the electromotive force (EMF) generated by a thermocouple.

### Junction Semiconductor Types
Junction semiconductor types measure temperature by detecting changes in the electrical properties of a semiconductor junction.

### Thermal Radiation Sensors
Thermal radiation sensors measure temperature by detecting the thermal radiation emitted by an object.

### Quartz Crystal Thermo-electric Sensors
Quartz crystal thermo-electric sensors measure temperature by detecting changes in the electrical properties of a quartz crystal.

### NQR Thermometry
NQR thermometry measures temperature by detecting changes in the nuclear quadrupole resonance (NQR) of a material.

### Spectroscopic Thermometry
Spectroscopic thermometry measures temperature by detecting changes in the spectroscopic properties of a material.

### Noise Thermometry
Noise thermometry measures temperature by detecting changes in the thermal noise generated by a material.

### Heat Flux Sensors
Heat flux sensors measure temperature by detecting the heat flux (rate of heat transfer) between two points.
