## Unit 4 - Actuators: Pneumatic and Hydraulic Actuation Systems

### Actuation systems
- Actuation systems are used to convert energy into mechanical motion.
- There are several types of actuation systems, including pneumatic, hydraulic, and electrical.

### Pneumatic and hydraulic systems
- Pneumatic systems use compressed air to generate mechanical motion.
- Hydraulic systems use pressurized fluid to generate mechanical motion.
- Both systems have similar components, including pumps, valves, and actuators.

### Directional Control valves
- Directional control valves are used to control the flow of fluid in a hydraulic or pneumatic system.
- They can be used to start, stop, or change the direction of fluid flow.

### Pressure control valves
- Pressure control valves are used to regulate the pressure of the fluid in a hydraulic or pneumatic system.
- They can be used to maintain a constant pressure, or to limit the maximum pressure in the system.

### Cylinders
- Cylinders are used to convert fluid pressure into linear mechanical motion.
- They consist of a piston and a cylinder, and can be single-acting or double-acting.

### Servo and proportional control valves
- Servo and proportional control valves are used to precisely control the flow of fluid in a hydraulic or pneumatic system.
- They can provide more accurate control than traditional directional control valves.

### Process control valves
- Process control valves are used to control the flow of fluid in industrial processes.
- They can be used to regulate the flow of fluid, or to control the pressure or temperature of the fluid.

### Rotary actuators
- Rotary actuators are used to convert fluid pressure into rotary mechanical motion.
- They can be used to rotate a shaft or other mechanical component.
