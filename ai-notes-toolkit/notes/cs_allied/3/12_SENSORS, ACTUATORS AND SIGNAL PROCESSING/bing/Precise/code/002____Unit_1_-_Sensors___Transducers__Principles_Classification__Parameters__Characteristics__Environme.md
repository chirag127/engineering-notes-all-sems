## Unit 1 - Sensors / Transducers: Principles Classification, Parameters, Characteristics, Environmental Parameters (EP), Characterization

1. **Sensors/Transducers** are devices that convert one form of energy into another. They are used to detect and measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, and light.
2. **Principles Classification** refers to the categorization of sensors/transducers based on their operating principles. Some common principles include resistive, capacitive, inductive, and optical.
3. **Parameters** are the measurable characteristics of a sensor/transducer. These include sensitivity, accuracy, resolution, and range.
4. **Characteristics** refer to the inherent properties of a sensor/transducer. These include linearity, hysteresis, and repeatability.
5. **Environmental Parameters (EP)** are external factors that can affect the performance of a sensor/transducer. These include temperature, humidity, and electromagnetic interference.
6. **Characterization** is the process of determining the performance of a sensor/transducer under various conditions. This involves measuring its parameters and characteristics under different environmental conditions.
