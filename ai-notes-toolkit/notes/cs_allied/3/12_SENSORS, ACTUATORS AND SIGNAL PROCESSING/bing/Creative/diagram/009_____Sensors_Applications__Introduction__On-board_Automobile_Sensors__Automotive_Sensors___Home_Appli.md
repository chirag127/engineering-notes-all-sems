### Sensors Applications: Introduction, On-board Automobile Sensors (Automotive Sensors), Home Appliance Sensors, Aerospace Sensors, Sensors for Manufacturing, Sensors for environmental Monitoring

#### Introduction
- Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, light, sound, motion, etc. and convert them into electrical signals.
- Sensors are widely used in various fields and industries for different purposes such as monitoring, control, safety, diagnostics, etc.
- Sensors can be classified into different types based on their principle of operation, output signal, measurement range, accuracy, etc.
- Some of the common types of sensors are thermocouples, thermistors, photodiodes, phototransistors, piezoelectric sensors, strain gauges, accelerometers, gyroscopes, pressure sensors, flow sensors, level sensors, humidity sensors, gas sensors, etc.

#### On-board Automobile Sensors (Automotive Sensors)
- Automotive sensors are sensors that are used in vehicles for various functions such as engine management, braking, traction, airbags, navigation, parking, etc.
- Some of the automotive applications of sensors are:

  - Braking and Traction control: Antilock Braking System (ABS) Sensors connected to the wheel, measures the speed of the wheel and sends the signal to the Electronic Control Unit (ECU) which adjusts the brake pressure to prevent skidding and improve stability. Traction Control System (TCS) Sensors also measure the wheel speed and detect any loss of traction and reduce the engine power or apply the brakes to prevent slipping.
  - Air Bags – Anti Cushion Restraint System (ACRS): Crush sensors and accelerometers placed in the vehicle detect the impact and the severity of the collision and trigger the airbags to inflate and protect the occupants from injury.
  - Engine Management: Various sensors such as oxygen sensors, mass air flow sensors, knock sensors, throttle position sensors, etc. monitor the engine performance and emissions and provide feedback to the ECU which adjusts the fuel injection, ignition timing, etc. to optimize the engine efficiency and reduce the pollution.
  - Navigation and Parking: Global Positioning System (GPS) Sensors receive signals from the satellites and determine the location, speed, and direction of the vehicle. Parking Sensors use ultrasonic or radar waves to detect the distance and the presence of obstacles around the vehicle and alert the driver or assist in parking.

#### Home Appliance Sensors
- Home appliance sensors are sensors that are used in household devices such as refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, etc. for various functions such as temperature control, water level detection, energy saving, etc.
- Some of the home appliance applications of sensors are:

  - Refrigerators: Temperature sensors measure the temperature inside the refrigerator and the freezer and send the signal to the thermostat which controls the compressor and the fan to maintain the desired temperature. Door sensors detect the opening and closing of the door and switch on or off the light and the fan accordingly. Humidity sensors measure the moisture level inside the refrigerator and adjust the defrost cycle to prevent frost formation.
  - Washing Machines: Water level sensors measure the amount of water in the tub and send the signal to the controller which controls the water inlet and the drain valves. Load sensors measure the weight of the clothes and adjust the water level and the washing time accordingly. Temperature sensors measure the temperature of the water and the clothes and control the heating element and the spin speed. Vibration sensors detect the imbalance of the load and correct it or stop the machine to prevent damage.
  - Air Conditioners: Temperature sensors measure the temperature of the room and the evaporator and send the signal to the thermostat which controls the compressor and the fan to maintain the desired temperature. Humidity sensors measure the humidity of the room and the evaporator and control the dehumidifier and the fan to maintain the desired humidity. Motion sensors detect the presence and the movement of the people in the room and adjust the temperature and the fan speed accordingly.

#### Aerospace Sensors
- Aerospace sensors are sensors that are used in aircraft, spacecraft, satellites, rockets, etc. for various functions such as navigation, control, communication, safety, etc.
- Some of the aerospace applications of sensors are:

  - Navigation: Inertial Navigation System (INS) Sensors such as accelerometers and gyroscopes measure the acceleration and the angular velocity of the aircraft or the spacecraft and calculate the position, velocity, and orientation. Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) Sensors such as GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, etc. receive signals from the satellites and determine the location, speed, and direction of the aircraft or the spacecraft
