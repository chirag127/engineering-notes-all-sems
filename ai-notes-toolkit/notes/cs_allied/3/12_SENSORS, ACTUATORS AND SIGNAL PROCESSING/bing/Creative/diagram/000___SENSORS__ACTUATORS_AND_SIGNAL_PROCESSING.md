# SENSORS, ACTUATORS AND SIGNAL PROCESSING

## Sensors
- A sensor is a device that converts a physical event into an electrical signal.
- Sensors are used to monitor physical activity and alert the control center to changes in asset performance or its environment.
- Sensors can be classified into different types based on the physical quantity they measure, such as temperature, pressure, light, sound, etc.
- Sensors can also be classified based on the principle of operation, such as resistive, capacitive, inductive, piezoelectric, etc.
- Sensors can have different characteristics, such as sensitivity, accuracy, resolution, range, linearity, etc.

## Actuators
- An actuator is a device that converts an electrical signal into a physical event.
- Actuators are used to perform physical actions, such as manipulating a robotic arm, opening a valve, or moving a motor.
- Actuators can be classified into different types based on the physical output they produce, such as linear, rotary, hydraulic, pneumatic, etc.
- Actuators can also be classified based on the principle of operation, such as electromagnetic, electrostatic, piezoelectric, etc.
- Actuators can have different characteristics, such as force, torque, speed, displacement, etc.

## Signal Processing
- Signal processing is the analysis, manipulation, and transformation of signals, such as sensor data, audio, images, etc.
- Signal processing can be performed in different domains, such as time, frequency, space, etc.
- Signal processing can have different objectives, such as filtering, compression, enhancement, detection, classification, etc.
- Signal processing can use different techniques, such as Fourier transform, wavelet transform, convolution, correlation, etc.
- Signal processing can be implemented in different platforms, such as hardware, software, or embedded systems.
