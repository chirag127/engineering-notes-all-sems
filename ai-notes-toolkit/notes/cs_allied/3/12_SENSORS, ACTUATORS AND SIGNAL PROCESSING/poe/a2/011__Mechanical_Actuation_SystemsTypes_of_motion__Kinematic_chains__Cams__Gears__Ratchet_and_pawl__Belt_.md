### Mechanical Actuation Systems

Types of motion:
- Linear motion
- Rotational motion
- Reciprocating motion

Kinematic chains:
- Open kinematic chain - End effector can move in work space
- Closed kinematic chain - End effector constrained to motion in a fixed pattern

Cams:
- Follower moves according to shape of cam
- Used for intermittent/reciprocating motion and cyclical operations

Gears:
- Used to transmit rotary motion and change rotation speed/torque
- Gear ratios - Inversely proportional to size of gears
- Gear trains - Arrangements of multiple gears for desired output speed/torque

Ratchet and pawl:
- Allows motion in one direction but prevents motion in opposite direction
- Used in mechanical clocks/wrenches

Belt and chain drives:
- Used to transmit rotary motion over a distance
- V-belts, synchronous belts, roller chains

Bearings:
- Reduce friction between rotating components and support radial/axial loads
- Ball bearings, roller bearings, sleeve bearings, journal bearings

Mechanical aspects of motor selection:
- Output torque requirements
- Speed requirements
- Load inertia
- Duty cycle
- Environment
- Cost/size constraints
