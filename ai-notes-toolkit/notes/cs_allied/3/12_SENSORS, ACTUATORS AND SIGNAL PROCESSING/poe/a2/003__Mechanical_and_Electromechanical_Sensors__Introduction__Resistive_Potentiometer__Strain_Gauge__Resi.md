### Mechanical and Electromechanical Sensors: Introduction
- Sensors convert a physical quantity into a signal which can be measured or recorded.
- Mechanical sensors contain moving parts and they produce an output signal in the form of displacement, velocity or acceleration.
- Electromechanical sensors contain transducers that convert the mechanical signal into an electrical signal.

### Resistive Potentiometer
- It contains a resistive strip and a sliding contact.
- The sliding contact moves over the resistive strip in response to the physical quantity to be measured.
- The resistance between the sliding contact and the ends of the resistive strip changes linearly with displacement.
- Output is in the form of change in potential which can be measured.
