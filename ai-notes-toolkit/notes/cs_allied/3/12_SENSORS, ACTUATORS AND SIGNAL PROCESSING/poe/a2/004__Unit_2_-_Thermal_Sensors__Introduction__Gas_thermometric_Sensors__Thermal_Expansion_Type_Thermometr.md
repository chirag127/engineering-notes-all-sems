## Unit 2 - Thermal Sensors: Introduction
- Thermal sensors are used to measure temperature and heat flow.
- They work on the principle that the physical properties of materials change with temperature.
- Different types of thermal sensors are gas thermometric sensors, thermal expansion sensors, resistance change sensors, thermoelectric sensors, etc.

## Gas Thermometric Sensors
- They contain gases like hydrogen, helium, nitrogen, etc. in a confined space.
- With increase in temperature, the gas expands and the corresponding pressure or volume change is measured to determine the temperature.
- They have high accuracy but are costly and complex.

## Thermal Expansion Sensors
- They contain two materials with different thermal expansion coefficients bonded together.
- With temperature change, the difference in expansion creates stress which is measured to determine temperature.
- Examples are bimetallic strip, bourdon tube, etc.
- They are inexpensive but have low accuracy.
