## Unit 2 - Thermal Sensors

Thermal sensors are devices that detect and measure temperature changes in a given environment. They are used in a variety of fields, including industrial, medical, and scientific research. In this unit, we will discuss various types of thermal sensors and their applications.

### Gas thermometric Sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the pressure, volume, or density of a gas to determine temperature.
- They are commonly used in industrial settings to measure the temperature of gases in pipelines or furnaces.

### Thermal Expansion Type Thermometric Sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the expansion or contraction of a material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in thermostats and other temperature control devices.

### Acoustic Temperature Sensor

- These sensors work by measuring the speed of sound in a given medium to determine temperature.
- They are commonly used in industrial and scientific applications where conventional temperature sensors are not suitable.

### Dielectric Constant and Refractive Index Thermo-sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the change in the dielectric constant or refractive index of a material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in materials research and other scientific applications.

### Helium Low Temperature Thermometer

- These sensors work by measuring the resistance of helium at very low temperatures.
- They are commonly used in cryogenics and low temperature research.

### Nuclear Thermometer

- These sensors work by measuring the energy emitted by radioactive materials to determine temperature.
- They are commonly used in nuclear power plants and other industrial applications.

### Magnetic Thermometer

- These sensors work by measuring the change in magnetic properties of a material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in materials research and other scientific applications.

### Resistance Change Type Thermometric Sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the change in resistance of a material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in electronic devices and temperature control systems.

### Thermo-EMF Sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the voltage generated by the thermoelectric effect in a material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in thermocouples and other temperature measurement devices.

### Junction Semiconductor Types

- These sensors work by measuring the change in electrical properties of a semiconductor material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in electronic devices and temperature control systems.

### Thermal Radiation Sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the amount of radiation emitted by a material to determine temperature.
- They are commonly used in industrial and scientific applications.

### Quartz Crystal Thermo-electric Sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the change in frequency of a quartz crystal as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in temperature measurement devices for high-precision applications.

### NQR Thermometry

- These sensors work by measuring the nuclear quadrupole resonance of a material to determine temperature.
- They are commonly used in materials research and other scientific applications.

### Spectroscopic Thermometry

- These sensors work by measuring the change in the spectrum of light emitted by a material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in materials research and other scientific applications.

### Noise Thermometry

- These sensors work by measuring the noise generated by a material as it is heated or cooled.
- They are commonly used in cryogenics and low temperature research.

### Heat Flux Sensors

- These sensors work by measuring the amount of heat transferred through a material to determine temperature.
- They are commonly used in industrial and scientific applications where accurate temperature measurement is important.
