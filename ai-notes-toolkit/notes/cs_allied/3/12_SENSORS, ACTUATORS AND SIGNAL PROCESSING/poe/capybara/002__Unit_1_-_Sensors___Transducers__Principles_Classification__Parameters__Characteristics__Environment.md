## Unit 1 - Sensors / Transducers: Principles Classification, Parameters, Characteristics, Environmental Parameters (EP), Characterization

In this unit, we will be discussing the following topics:

1. Principles of sensors and transducers:
    - Definition and basic concepts
    - Types of sensors and transducers
    - Operating principles of sensors and transducers

2. Classification of sensors and transducers:
    - Based on the type of input
    - Based on the type of output
    - Based on the application

3. Parameters of sensors and transducers:
    - Sensitivity
    - Resolution
    - Accuracy
    - Precision
    - Linearity

4. Characteristics of sensors and transducers:
    - Static characteristics
    - Dynamic characteristics
    - Hysteresis
    - Repeatability
    - Drift

5. Environmental Parameters (EP):
    - Temperature
    - Pressure
    - Humidity
    - Light
    - Noise

6. Characterization of sensors and transducers:
    - Calibration
    - Error analysis
    - Testing and validation

By the end of this unit, you will have a thorough understanding of the principles, classification, parameters, characteristics, environmental parameters, and characterization of sensors and transducers. This knowledge will be essential for understanding and designing sensor-based systems in various applications.
