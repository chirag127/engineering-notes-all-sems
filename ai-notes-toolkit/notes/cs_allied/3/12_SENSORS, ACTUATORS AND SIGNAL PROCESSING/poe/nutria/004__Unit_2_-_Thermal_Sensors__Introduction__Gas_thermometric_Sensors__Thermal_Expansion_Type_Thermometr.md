
## Unit 2 - Thermal Sensors: Introduction

Thermal sensors are devices used to measure temperature. They are used in a variety of applications such as medical, industrial, and aerospace. Thermal sensors can be divided into two main categories: gas thermometric sensors and thermal expansion type thermometric sensors. 

### Gas Thermometric Sensors

Gas thermometric sensors measure temperature by measuring the pressure of a gas. The most common type of gas thermometric sensor is the thermocouple. A thermocouple is a device composed of two dissimilar metals that are connected together. When the temperature of the two metals is different, a voltage is generated. 

### Thermal Expansion Type Thermometric Sensors

Thermal expansion type thermometric sensors measure temperature by measuring the expansion of a material due to a change in temperature. The most common type of thermal expansion type thermometric sensor is the bimetallic strip. This device consists of two strips of different metals that are connected together. When the temperature changes, the two strips expand or contract at different rates, causing the strip to bend. This bending can be used to measure temperature. 

### Acoustic Temperature Sensor

An acoustic temperature sensor is a device that measures temperature by measuring the speed of sound in a material. This device works by sending a sound wave through the material and measuring the time it takes for the wave to travel a known distance. The speed of sound is dependent on the temperature of the material, so the time it takes for the wave to travel can be used to calculate the temperature. 

### Dielectric Constant and Refractive Index Thermo-sensors

Dielectric constant and refractive index thermo-sensors measure temperature by measuring the change in the dielectric constant or refractive index of a material due to a change in temperature. These sensors work by passing an electric current through the material and measuring the change in the current. 

### Helium Low Temperature Thermometer

A helium low temperature thermometer is a device that measures temperature by measuring the pressure of helium gas. This device works by passing a current through a tube of helium gas and measuring the pressure of the gas. The pressure of the gas is dependent on the temperature, so the pressure can be used to calculate the temperature. 

### Nuclear Thermometer

A nuclear thermometer is a device that measures temperature by measuring the decay rate of a radioactive material. This device works by passing a current through a radioactive material and measuring the rate at which the material decays. The decay rate of the material is dependent on the temperature, so the decay rate can be used to calculate the temperature. 

### Magnetic Thermometer

A magnetic thermometer is a device that measures temperature by measuring the change in the magnetic properties of a material due to a change in temperature. This device works by passing a current through a material and measuring the change in the current. The current is dependent on the temperature, so the current can be used to calculate the temperature. 

### Resistance Change Type Thermometric Sensors

Resistance change type thermometric sensors measure temperature by measuring the change in the resistance of a material due to a change in temperature. These sensors work by passing a current through a material and measuring the change in the current. The current is dependent on the temperature, so the current can be used to calculate the temperature. 

### Thermo-EMF Sensors

Thermo-EMF sensors measure temperature by measuring the change in the electromotive force (EMF) of a material due to a change in temperature. These sensors work by passing a current through a material and measuring the change in the current. The current is dependent on the temperature, so the current can be used to calculate the temperature. 

### Junction Semiconductor Types

Junction semiconductor types measure temperature by measuring the change in the voltage of a semiconductor material due to a change in temperature. These sensors work by passing a current through a semiconductor material and measuring the change in the current. The current is dependent on the temperature, so the current can be used to calculate the temperature.

### Thermal Radiation Sensors

Thermal radiation sensors measure temperature by measuring the amount of thermal radiation emitted from a material. This device works by passing a current through a material and measuring the amount of thermal radiation emitted from the material. The amount of thermal radiation is dependent on the temperature, so the amount of thermal radiation can be used to calculate the temperature. 

### Quartz Crystal Thermo-electric Sensors

Quartz crystal thermo-electric sensors measure temperature by measuring the change in the electrical properties of a quartz crystal due to a change in temperature. These sensors work by passing a current through a quartz crystal and measuring the change in the current. The current is dependent on the temperature, so the current can be used to calculate the temperature.
