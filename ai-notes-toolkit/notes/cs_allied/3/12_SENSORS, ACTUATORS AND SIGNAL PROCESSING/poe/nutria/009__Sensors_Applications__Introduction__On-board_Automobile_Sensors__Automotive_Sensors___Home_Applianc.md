
### Sensors Applications: Introduction

* Sensors are devices used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, acceleration, and force.
* They are used in a variety of applications including automotive, aerospace, manufacturing, and environmental monitoring.

On-board Automobile Sensors (Automotive Sensors)

* Automotive sensors are used to monitor the performance of a vehicle's engine, transmission, and other systems. 
* They are used to detect changes in engine speed, temperature, and other parameters.
* Common automotive sensors include oxygen sensors, air-fuel ratio sensors, knock sensors, and exhaust gas recirculation sensors.

Home Appliance Sensors

* Home appliance sensors are used to monitor and control the operation of home appliances such as refrigerators, dishwashers, and washing machines. 
* They are used to detect temperature, humidity, water levels, and other parameters.
* Common home appliance sensors include temperature sensors, humidity sensors, pressure sensors, and water level sensors.

Aerospace Sensors

* Aerospace sensors are used to monitor the performance of aircraft and spacecraft. 
* They are used to detect changes in altitude, speed, acceleration, and other parameters. 
* Common aerospace sensors include airspeed sensors, altitude sensors, attitude sensors, and GPS receivers.

Sensors for Manufacturing

* Manufacturing sensors are used to monitor and control the operation of industrial machinery and processes. 
* They are used to detect changes in temperature, pressure, vibration, and other parameters. 
* Common manufacturing sensors include temperature sensors, pressure sensors, vibration sensors, and flow sensors.

Sensors for Environmental Monitoring

* Environmental sensors are used to monitor the environment for changes in air quality, water quality, and other parameters. 
* They are used to detect changes in temperature, humidity, air quality, and other parameters. 
* Common environmental sensors include temperature sensors, humidity sensors, air quality sensors, and water quality sensors.
