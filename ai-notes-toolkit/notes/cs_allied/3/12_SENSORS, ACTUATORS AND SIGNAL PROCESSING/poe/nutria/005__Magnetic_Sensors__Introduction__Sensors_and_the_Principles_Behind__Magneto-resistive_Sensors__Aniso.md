
### Magnetic Sensors: Introduction

Magnetic sensors are devices capable of detecting and measuring the magnitude and direction of a magnetic field. They are used in a wide variety of applications, including navigation systems, medical imaging, robotics, and industrial automation.

The most common types of magnetic sensors are magneto-resistive, anisotropic magneto-resistive, Hall effect, inductance, eddy current, angular/rotary movement transducers, synchronous resolvers, electromagnetic flow meters, switching magnetic sensors, and SQUID sensors.

### Sensors and the Principles Behind

Magnetic sensors use a variety of principles to detect and measure magnetic fields. The most common types of sensors are magneto-resistive, anisotropic magneto-resistive, Hall effect, inductance, eddy current, angular/rotary movement transducers, synchronous resolvers, electromagnetic flow meters, switching magnetic sensors, and SQUID sensors.

Magneto-resistive sensors measure the resistance of a material when exposed to a magnetic field. Anisotropic magneto-resistive sensors measure the resistance of a material when exposed to an anisotropic magnetic field. Hall effect sensors measure the voltage generated when a material is exposed to a magnetic field. Inductance sensors measure the current generated when a material is exposed to a magnetic field. Eddy current sensors measure the current generated when a material is exposed to an alternating magnetic field. Angular/rotary movement transducers measure the angular motion of a material when exposed to a magnetic field. Synchronous resolvers measure the position of a material when exposed to a magnetic field. Electromagnetic flow meters measure the flow of a material when exposed to a magnetic field. Switching magnetic sensors measure the presence or absence of a magnetic field. SQUID sensors measure the magnetic flux of a material when exposed to a magnetic field.

### Magneto-resistive Sensors

Magneto-resistive sensors measure the resistance of a material when exposed to a magnetic field. These sensors are commonly used in navigation systems, medical imaging, robotics, and industrial automation. They are typically composed of a thin film of material, such as cobalt, nickel, or iron, which is placed between two electrodes. When a magnetic field is applied, the resistance of the material changes, which can be measured.

### Anisotropic Magneto-resistive Sensing

Anisotropic magneto-resistive sensing is a type of magneto-resistive sensing that uses an anisotropic material, such as cobalt-iron, nickel-iron, or iron-manganese, to measure the resistance of a material when exposed to an anisotropic magnetic field. These sensors are commonly used in navigation systems, medical imaging, robotics, and industrial automation.
