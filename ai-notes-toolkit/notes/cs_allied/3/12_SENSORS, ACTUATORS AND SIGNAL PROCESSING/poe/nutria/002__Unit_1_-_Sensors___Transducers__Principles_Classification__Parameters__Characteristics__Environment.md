
## Unit 1 - Sensors / Transducers: Principles Classification, Parameters, Characteristics, Environmental Parameters (EP), Characterization

* Sensors are devices that measure a physical quantity and convert it into a signal suitable for processing, recording or controlling.
* Transducers are devices that convert energy from one form to another, such as electrical to mechanical energy or vice versa.
* The principles of sensors and transducers include the conversion of physical quantities into electrical signals, the use of transducers to convert electrical signals into mechanical motion, and the use of transducers to convert mechanical motion into electrical signals.
* Parameters are characteristics of a sensor or transducer that describe its performance. Examples of parameters include sensitivity, accuracy, resolution, linearity, and frequency response.
* Characteristics are properties of a sensor or transducer that describe its behavior. Examples of characteristics include temperature coefficient, hysteresis, and drift.
* Environmental Parameters (EP) are parameters that describe the environment in which a sensor or transducer is used. Examples of EP include temperature, humidity, pressure, and vibration.
* Characterization is the process of determining the performance of a sensor or transducer under specific conditions. This includes measuring parameters such as sensitivity, accuracy, resolution, linearity, and frequency response.
