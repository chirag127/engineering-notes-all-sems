
### Digital Signal Representation

1. Digital signals are representations of physical signals that have been converted into a binary code, which is a sequence of zeros and ones.
2. Digital signals are used in many applications, including data transmission, digital audio, digital video, and digital control systems.
3. Digital signals can be represented in a variety of ways, such as binary, decimal, hexadecimal, and ASCII.
4. Digital signals can be manipulated using a variety of techniques, such as filtering, modulation, and compression.
5. Digital signal processing (DSP) is the process of manipulating digital signals to achieve a desired result, such as enhancing the quality of a signal or extracting useful information from it.
6. Digital signal representation is used to represent physical signals in a digital form, which can then be manipulated and analyzed.
7. The most common methods of digital signal representation are Pulse Code Modulation (PCM), Delta-Sigma Modulation (DSM), and Pulse Width Modulation (PWM).
8. Digital signal representation can also be used to represent analog signals, such as sound waves and images.
9. Digital signal representation is used in many applications, such as telecommunications, audio and video processing, and computer networks.
