## Unit 2 - Thermal Sensors

Thermal sensors are devices used to measure temperature by detecting changes in various physical properties of materials. In this unit, we will cover various types of thermal sensors and their applications.

### Introduction

Thermal sensors are used in a wide range of applications, including industrial, medical, and scientific fields. They are used to measure temperature in various environments and conditions, from high-temperature furnaces to cryogenic chambers.

### Gas Thermometric Sensors

Gas thermometric sensors are based on the principle of measuring the pressure of a gas at a constant volume. As the temperature of the gas changes, the pressure also changes, allowing for accurate temperature measurements. These sensors are commonly used in industrial applications where high temperatures are present.

### Thermal Expansion Type Thermometric Sensors

Thermal expansion type thermometric sensors work by detecting changes in the length or volume of a material due to temperature changes. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific applications.

### Acoustic Temperature Sensor

Acoustic temperature sensors use sound waves to measure temperature. They work by measuring the speed of sound in a gas or liquid, which changes with temperature. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific fields.

### Dielectric Constant and Refractive Index Thermo-sensors

Dielectric constant and refractive index thermosensors work by measuring changes in the dielectric constant or refractive index of a material due to temperature changes. These sensors are commonly used in scientific and medical fields.

### Helium Low Temperature Thermometer

Helium low temperature thermometers use the properties of helium gas to measure very low temperatures. These sensors are commonly used in scientific and medical fields.

### Nuclear Thermometer

Nuclear thermometers use the properties of radioactive materials to measure temperature. These sensors are commonly used in scientific and medical fields.

### Magnetic Thermometer

Magnetic thermometers use the magnetic properties of materials to measure temperature. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific fields.

### Resistance Change Type Thermometric Sensors

Resistance change type thermometric sensors work by measuring changes in the electrical resistance of a material due to temperature changes. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific fields.

### Thermo-EMF Sensors

Thermo-EMF sensors work by measuring the electromotive force (EMF) generated by a temperature difference between two materials. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific fields.

### Junction Semiconductor Types

Junction semiconductor types are based on the principle of measuring the voltage across a junction between two different types of semiconductors. As temperature changes, the voltage also changes, allowing for accurate temperature measurements. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific fields.

### Thermal Radiation Sensors

Thermal radiation sensors work by measuring the amount of radiation emitted by a material due to its temperature. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific fields.

### Quartz Crystal Thermo-electric Sensors

Quartz crystal thermo-electric sensors work by measuring changes in the resonant frequency of a quartz crystal due to temperature changes. These sensors are commonly used in scientific and medical fields.

### NQR Thermometry

NQR thermometry uses the principles of nuclear quadrupole resonance (NQR) to measure temperature. These sensors are commonly used in scientific and medical fields.

### Spectroscopic Thermometry

Spectroscopic thermometry uses the principles of spectroscopy to measure temperature. These sensors are commonly used in scientific and medical fields.

### Noise Thermometry

Noise thermometry measures the noise level of a material due to its temperature. These sensors are commonly used in scientific and medical fields.

### Heat Flux Sensors

Heat flux sensors measure the flow of heat through a material. These sensors are commonly used in industrial and scientific fields.

In conclusion, thermal sensors are essential tools used for measuring temperature in various environments and conditions. Understanding the different types of thermal sensors and their applications is critical for their effective use in the industry and scientific fields.
