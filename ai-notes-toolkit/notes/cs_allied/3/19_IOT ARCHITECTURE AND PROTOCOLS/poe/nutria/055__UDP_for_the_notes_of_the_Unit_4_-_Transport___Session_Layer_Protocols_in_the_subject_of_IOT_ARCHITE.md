
### UDP for the notes of the Unit 4 - Transport & Session Layer Protocols in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

* UDP is a connectionless protocol in the transport layer of the Internet protocol suite. It is used to send short messages called datagrams from one host to another.
* UDP is faster than TCP as it does not require an acknowledgement of the delivery of the data.
* UDP is used in applications that require low latency and low overhead, such as streaming audio and video, gaming, and Voice over IP (VoIP).
* UDP is also used in Domain Name System (DNS) queries, network time protocol (NTP) synchronization, and Simple Network Management Protocol (SNMP) monitoring.
* UDP does not guarantee reliable delivery of data and is not suitable for applications that require reliable delivery.
* UDP is not a secure protocol and is vulnerable to spoofing and other attacks.
