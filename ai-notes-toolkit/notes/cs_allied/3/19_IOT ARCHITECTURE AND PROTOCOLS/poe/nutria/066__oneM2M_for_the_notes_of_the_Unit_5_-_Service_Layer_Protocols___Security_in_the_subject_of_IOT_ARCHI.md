
### oneM2M

oneM2M is a global standards initiative that enables the creation of Internet of Things (IoT) applications and services. It provides a common service layer and security framework for IoT applications and services, enabling interoperability and secure communication between different IoT devices and services.

oneM2M is used in a variety of different applications, including smart home automation, connected vehicles, healthcare, and industrial automation. It provides a robust, secure, and reliable communication infrastructure that is compatible with a variety of different protocols and technologies.

oneM2M is an open standards initiative that is managed by the oneM2M Partnership Project Office (PPO). The PPO is comprised of several organizations, including the European Telecommunications Standards Institute (ETSI), the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), and the Alliance for Internet of Things Innovation (AIOTI).

**Key Features of oneM2M:**

- Provides a common service layer and security framework for IoT applications and services.
- Enables interoperability and secure communication between different IoT devices and services.
- Supports a variety of different protocols and technologies.
- Managed by the oneM2M Partnership Project Office (PPO).
- Used in a variety of different applications, including smart home automation, connected vehicles, healthcare, and industrial automation.
