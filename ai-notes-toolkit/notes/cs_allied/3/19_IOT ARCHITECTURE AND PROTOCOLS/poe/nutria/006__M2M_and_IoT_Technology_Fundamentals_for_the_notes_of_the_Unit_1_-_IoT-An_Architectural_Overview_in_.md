

### M2M and IoT Technology Fundamentals

1. Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication is an automated communication between two or more machines, allowing them to exchange data and interact with each other without any human-to-human or human-to-machine interaction.

2. The Internet of Things (IoT) is an interconnected network of physical devices, such as sensors, actuators, and other electronic devices, which are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other. 

3. IoT applications are used to collect and analyse data, and to control and automate processes.

4. IoT architectures typically include components such as devices, gateways, cloud services, and applications.

5. IoT devices are the physical components of an IoT system, such as sensors, actuators, and other electronic components. They are connected to the internet and can communicate with each other.

6. Gateways are devices that connect IoT devices to the internet, enabling them to communicate with each other.

7. Cloud services are used to store, process, and analyse the data collected by IoT devices.

8. Applications are used to control and automate processes, and to analyse and visualise the data collected by IoT devices.

9. IoT protocols are used to communicate between IoT devices and gateways. Examples of IoT protocols include MQTT, CoAP, and Zigbee.

10. Security is an important aspect of IoT systems, and measures such as encryption and authentication must be implemented to ensure the security of the system.
