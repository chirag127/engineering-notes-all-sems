
### 6LoWPAN

6LoWPAN (IPv6 over Low-power Wireless Personal Area Networks) is a protocol designed to enable low-power, low-cost wireless networks to be used in Internet of Things (IoT) applications. It provides a way for small, low-power devices to communicate with each other over short distances and to connect to the internet.

The 6LoWPAN protocol is based on the IEEE 802.15.4 standard and is designed to work with small, low-power devices such as sensors, actuators, and smart meters. It is also designed to be energy efficient, allowing devices to run on batteries for long periods of time.
