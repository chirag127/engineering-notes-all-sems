
### Standards Considerations for the Notes of the Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview in the Subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

* It is important to understand the various standards and protocols that are used in the Internet of Things (IoT). 
* IoT devices must be able to communicate with each other and with external systems, so there must be standards that define how these devices communicate. 
* The most commonly used standards and protocols for IoT include: 
    * Bluetooth: Bluetooth is a wireless technology used for short-range communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
* Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi is a wireless technology used for long-range communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
* Zigbee: Zigbee is a wireless protocol used for short-range communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
* 6LoWPAN: 6LoWPAN is a wireless protocol used for low-power communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
* MQTT: MQTT is a messaging protocol used for communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
* CoAP: CoAP is a communication protocol used for communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
* AllJoyn: AllJoyn is an open source framework used for communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
* Z-Wave: Z-Wave is a wireless protocol used for communication between devices. It is used for a variety of applications, including home automation and healthcare.
