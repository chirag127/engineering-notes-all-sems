- RPL is a mesh routing protocol that is based on the Destination Oriented Directed Acyclic Graph (DODAG) topology.

- RPL is designed to be a self-organizing and self-configuring protocol, and it can be used with a variety of different network types, including wireless sensor networks, mesh networks, and ad-hoc networks.
- RPL is designed to be a low-power protocol, and it can be used to conserve energy by using techniques such as route pruning, route compression, and hop-by-hop flow control.
