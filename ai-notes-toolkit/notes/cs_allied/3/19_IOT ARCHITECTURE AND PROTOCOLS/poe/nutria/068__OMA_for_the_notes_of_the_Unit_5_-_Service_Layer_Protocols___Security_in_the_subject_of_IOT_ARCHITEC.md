
### OMA 

OMA (Open Mobile Alliance) is a standards organization that develops specifications for mobile networks. It was established in 2002 and is composed of over 200 members from the mobile industry. OMA is responsible for developing protocols and standards for mobile networks such as GSM, 3G, and LTE.

OMA has developed several protocols and standards for service layer security and protocols. These include:

* OMA-DM (Device Management): This protocol is used to manage and monitor mobile devices over the air. It allows for remote management of devices, including software updates, configuration, and provisioning.

* OMA-CP (Connection Protocol): This protocol is used to establish and manage connections between mobile devices and other devices over the air.

* OMA-LWM2M (Lightweight Machine-to-Machine): This protocol is used for managing and monitoring low-power devices over the air. It is designed for low-power devices such as sensors and actuators.

* OMA-DMS (Device Management Services): This protocol is used for managing and monitoring mobile devices over the air. It allows for remote management of devices, including software updates, configuration, and provisioning.

* OMA-Secure User Plane (SUPL): This protocol is used for secure communication between mobile devices and other devices. It provides secure communication over the air, including encryption and authentication.
