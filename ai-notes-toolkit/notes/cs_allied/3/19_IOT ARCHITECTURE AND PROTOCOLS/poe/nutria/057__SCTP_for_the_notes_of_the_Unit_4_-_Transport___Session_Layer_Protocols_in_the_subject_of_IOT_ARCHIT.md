
### SCTP for the notes of the Unit 4 - Transport & Session Layer Protocols in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

- Stream Control Transmission Protocol (SCTP) is a transport layer protocol that provides reliable, connection-oriented data delivery services.
- SCTP is a message-oriented protocol, which means that it sends data in the form of messages, rather than as a stream of bytes.
- SCTP supports multi-homing, which means that a single endpoint can be associated with multiple IP addresses. This provides redundancy and improved availability.
- SCTP also provides a number of features to improve reliability, including checksums, sequence numbers, and acknowledgments.
- SCTP is used in a variety of applications, including streaming media, telephony, and Internet of Things (IoT) applications.
