
### XMPP for the notes of the Unit 4 - Transport & Session Layer Protocols in the subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

- XMPP (Extensible Messaging and Presence Protocol) is an open-standard communication protocol for real-time messaging, presence, and collaboration.
- XMPP is used to build applications that enable real-time communication between two or more users over the Internet.
- XMPP enables users to exchange messages, files, audio, and video in real-time. 
- XMPP is a client-server protocol, which means that the client (e.g. a mobile device) and the server (e.g. an XMPP server) communicate with each other by exchanging messages.
- XMPP is an open, extensible protocol that is designed to be highly flexible and interoperable.
- XMPP is used in a variety of applications such as instant messaging, voice and video chat, file transfer, and presence services.
- XMPP is also used in Internet of Things (IoT) applications, including smart home automation, remote monitoring, and control.
- XMPP is designed to be secure, reliable, and scalable, and is used by many large organizations, including Google, Facebook, and Microsoft.
