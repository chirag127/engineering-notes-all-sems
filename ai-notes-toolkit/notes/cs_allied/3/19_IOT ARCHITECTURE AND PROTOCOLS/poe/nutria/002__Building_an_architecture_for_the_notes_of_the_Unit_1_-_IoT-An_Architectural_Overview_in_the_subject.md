
### Building an Architecture for the Notes of Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview in the Subject of IOT ARCHITECTURE AND PROTOCOLS

1. IoT architecture consists of the following components:
    * Sensors: These are the devices that capture data from the environment and send it to the IoT platform
    * Network: This is the communication medium that connects the sensors and the IoT platform
    * Platform: This is the layer that processes the data and stores it for further analysis
    * Applications: These are the applications that use the data to provide valuable insights
2. IoT protocols are the rules and standards that govern the communication between the devices and the platform. The most commonly used protocols are MQTT, CoAP, and XMPP.
3. Security is a major concern in IoT. It is important to ensure that the data is secure and the devices are not vulnerable to malicious attacks. The most commonly used security protocols are TLS, SSH, and IPSec.
4. Edge computing is used to process data at the edge of the network, instead of sending it to the cloud. This reduces latency and improves the performance of the system.
5. Data storage is an important part of the IoT architecture. It is important to ensure that the data is stored securely and is easily accessible. The most commonly used data storage solutions are databases, cloud storage, and distributed storage.
