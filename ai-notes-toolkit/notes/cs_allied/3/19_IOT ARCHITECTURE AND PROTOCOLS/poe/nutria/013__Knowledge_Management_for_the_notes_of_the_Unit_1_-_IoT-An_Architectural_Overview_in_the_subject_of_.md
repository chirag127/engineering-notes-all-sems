
### Knowledge Management for the Notes of Unit 1 - IoT-An Architectural Overview

1. Knowledge Management is a process of collecting, storing, organizing, and analyzing data to create useful information.
2. In the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), knowledge management refers to the process of gathering and using data from connected devices to make decisions and take actions.
3. Effective knowledge management can help organizations make better decisions and improve the efficiency of their operations.
4. IoT architecture is the foundation for the development of IoT systems. It includes the hardware, software, and communication protocols used to enable the communication and data exchange between devices.
5. IoT architectures can be divided into three main components: the physical layer, the network layer, and the application layer.
6. The physical layer includes the sensors, actuators, and other physical components used to collect data from the environment.
7. The network layer is responsible for connecting the physical devices and providing communication between them. This layer includes the communication protocols used to transfer data between the devices.
8. The application layer is responsible for the processing of the data and the execution of tasks. This layer includes the software and algorithms used to analyze the data and make decisions.
9. In order to ensure the security and reliability of the IoT systems, it is important to use secure communication protocols, such as TLS and IPSec, and to use secure authentication methods, such as digital certificates.
10. It is also important to use secure data storage solutions, such as encryption, to protect the data from unauthorized access.
