### Business processes in IoT

- A business process is a collection of related events, activities and decisions that involve a number of factors and resources, which collectively lead to an outcome that is of value for the organisation and the customer.
- Business processes can be improved with the Internet of Things (IoT), which is the network of physical objects embedded with sensors, software and connectivity that enable data exchange and interaction.
- IoT can enable business process automation, optimization, innovation and transformation by providing real-time data, insights, feedback and control over the physical and digital aspects of the business .
- Some examples of business processes that can benefit from IoT are:
  - Manufacturing: IoT can monitor and control the production process, optimize the supply chain, reduce waste and defects, and improve quality and efficiency.
  - Logistics: IoT can track and trace the location, condition and status of goods and vehicles, optimize the routing and delivery, and enhance the customer experience.
  - Healthcare: IoT can monitor and manage the health and wellness of patients and staff, improve the diagnosis and treatment, and enable remote care and telemedicine.
  - Retail: IoT can personalize the shopping experience, provide recommendations and offers, enhance the inventory and security management, and enable smart payments and loyalty programs.
- Some recommendations on implementing IoT business processes are:
  - To define the business process to improve and identify the problem to solve.
  - To use an end-to-end approach that covers the entire value chain of the process.
  - To make agile design and start with proof of concept prototyping.
  - To get on board the right people, with the best knowledge and skills.
  - To be persistent but acknowledgeable to failure and learn from it.
  - To be aware of the potential disruption and innovation that IoT can bring.
