## Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

1. Piezoelectric Materials
- Piezoelectric materials produce charge when strained
- Common piezoelectric materials: Quartz, Tourmaline, Ceramics (PZT, PMN-PT)
- Piezoelectric tensor relates stress and strain to electric field and electric displacement
- Poling process aligns electric dipoles to produce piezoelectric effect

2. Energy Harvesting Model
- Vibration energy harvesting uses piezoelectric material on a beam/plate
- Output power depends on frequency and amplitude of vibration
- Resonance can be used to increase strain and power output
- Full electromechanical model required to understand behavior and optimize harvester design

3. Linear Piezoelectric Theory
- Constitute equations relate stress and strain to electric field and electric displacement
- Mechanical boundary conditions: Force and moment balances
- Electrical boundary conditions: Charge conservation and applied voltage
- Compatibility conditions: Strain-displacement and electric field-potential relationships

4. Nonlinear Piezoelectric Theory
- Large deflections/strains can produce nonlinear effects not captured in linear theory
- Higher order terms in equations are required to account for nonlinear effects
- Nonlinearities include:
-- Geometric (large deflection)
-- Material (strain/field dependent properties)
-- Coupling (electromechanical interactions)
- Nonlinear models important for accurately predicting high amplitude/high power harvester response
