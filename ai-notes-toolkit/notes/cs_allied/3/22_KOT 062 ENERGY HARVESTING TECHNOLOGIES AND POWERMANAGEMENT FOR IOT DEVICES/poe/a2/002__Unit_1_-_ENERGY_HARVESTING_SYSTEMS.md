## Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

1. Introduction
- Energy harvesting is the process by which energy is extracted from external sources and converted into electric energy.
- The energy harvesters convert different forms of energy such as solar, thermal, kinetic, radio frequency, etc. into electricity.
- The key components of an energy harvesting system are transducers and power conditioning circuits.

2. Solar Energy Harvesting
- The solar energy can be harvested using photovoltaic effect which generates voltage when the photons of light hit the junction of two materials.
- The solar cells made of crystalline silicon or thin-film materials can be used to convert solar energy into electricity.
- The output power depends on the intensity of sunlight and size of the solar panel.
- Solar energy harvesting is suitable for outdoor applications and in places where sunlight is abundant.

3. Thermal Energy Harvesting
- The temperature gradient between two objects can be used to generate electricity using thermoelectric effect.
- The thermoelectric generators contain materials that produce voltage when there is a temperature difference across them.
- The output power depends on the temperature difference and the properties of the thermoelectric materials used.
- Thermal energy harvesting is suitable for waste heat recovery applications.
