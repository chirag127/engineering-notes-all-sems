### Bio-MEMS based applications

- Bio-MEMS refers to the application of Microelectromechanical Systems (MEMS) technology to miniaturize devices for biological and biomedical applications.
- Some of the Bio-MEMS based applications are:
    - Biosensors: Biosensors contain biological sensing elements integrated with transducers to detect analytes of biological importance like glucose, proteins, DNA, etc. The biological elements provide high specificity and the MEMS components provide label-free, real-time and high throughput detection.
    - Lab-on-a-chip: Miniaturized devices that integrate multiple laboratory functions on a single chip to manipulate and analyze biological components. They provide faster analysis using smaller samples and reagents. Examples include DNA analysis, cell sorting, etc.
    - Medical implants: MEMS technology is used to fabricate miniaturized implants like pressure sensors in the eyes for glaucoma patients or cochlear implants for hearing aids. They provide continuous monitoring or stimulation with minimal invasiveness.
    - Drug delivery: MEMS can be used to fabricate microneedles for transdermal drug delivery and miniature pumps for controlled drug release. They provide minimally invasive drug delivery with precise control over dosage and rate of delivery.
