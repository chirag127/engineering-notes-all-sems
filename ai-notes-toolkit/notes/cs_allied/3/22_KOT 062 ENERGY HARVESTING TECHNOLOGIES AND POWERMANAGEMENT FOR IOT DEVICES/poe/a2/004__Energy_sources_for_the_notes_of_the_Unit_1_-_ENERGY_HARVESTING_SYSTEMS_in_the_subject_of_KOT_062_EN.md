### Energy sources for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Solar Energy:
- Energy from the sun can be harvested using photovoltaic panels.
- The solar energy is converted into electrical energy which can be used to power devices.
- It is an abundant and renewable source of energy.

2. Mechanical Energy:
- Mechanical energy can be harvested from movements, vibrations, etc. using piezoelectric, electrostatic or electromagnetic transducers.
- The harvested mechanical energy is converted into electrical energy.
- Sources include human movements, machine vibrations, wind energy, etc.

3. Thermal Energy:
- The difference in temperature can be converted into electrical energy using thermoelectric generators.
- The Seebeck effect is utilized for thermal energy harvesting.
- Sources include waste heat from machines, solar thermal energy, etc.

4. Radio Frequency Energy:
- The radio frequency energy in the surroundings can be harvested using antenna and rectifier circuits.
- The RF energy is first received by the antenna and then converted into electrical energy.
- Sources include RF signals from cellular towers, Wi-Fi routers, etc.

The content summarizes some of the major sources of energy that can be harvested to generate electricity for powering IoT devices and other low power electronics. The harvested energy can be used to either directly power the devices or recharge batteries to power the devices.
