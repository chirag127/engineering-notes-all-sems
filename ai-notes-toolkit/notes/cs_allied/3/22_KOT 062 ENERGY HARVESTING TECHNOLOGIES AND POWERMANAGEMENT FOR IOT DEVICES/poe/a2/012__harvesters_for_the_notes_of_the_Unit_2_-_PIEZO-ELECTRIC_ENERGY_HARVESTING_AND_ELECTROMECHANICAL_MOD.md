### Harvesters for the notes of Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

1. Piezoelectric Harvesters
- Piezoelectric materials produce charge when stressed mechanically
- Common piezoelectric materials: Lead Zirconate Titanate (PZT), Polyvinylidene Fluoride (PVDF)
- Piezoelectric harvesters use vibration/strain to generate charge
- Advantages: high power density, simple fabrication
- Disadvantages: narrow frequency range, delicate

2. Electromagnetic Harvesters
- Electromagnetic induction used to generate current from motion
- Coil moves through magnetic field or magnetic field moves relative to coil
- Advantages: wide frequency range, well-understood technology
- Disadvantages: larger size, lower power density

3. Other Types of Harvesters
- Electrostatic - vary capacitor plates to generate charge
- Thermoelectric - generate voltage from temperature gradient
- Photovoltaic - generate voltage from light

4. Harvester Modeling
- Electromechanical modeling used to analyze and optimize harvester performance
- Important factors: natural frequency, damping, transducer efficiency, power management
- Modeling allows choice of harvester type and design for target application
