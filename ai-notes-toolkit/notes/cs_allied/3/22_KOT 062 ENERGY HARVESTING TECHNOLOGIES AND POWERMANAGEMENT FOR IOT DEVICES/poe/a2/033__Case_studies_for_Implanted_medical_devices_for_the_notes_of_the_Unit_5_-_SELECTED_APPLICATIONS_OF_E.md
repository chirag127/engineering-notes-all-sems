### Case studies for Implanted medical devices

- Pacemakers: Pacemakers are implantable medical devices used to treat abnormal heart rhythms. They use batteries that need replacement every 5-15 years. Researchers are working on piezoelectric and thermoelectric energy harvesters to power pacemakers and reduce the need for battery replacement surgeries.
- Neurostimulators: Neurostimulators are implantable devices used to treat conditions like Parkinson's disease and epilepsy. They deliver electric pulses to the brain/nervous system to block abnormal nerve signals. Recharging the batteries or replacing them requires additional surgeries. Energy harvesters using body heat and motion can eliminate the need for replacement surgeries.
- Glucose monitors: Implantable glucose monitors can help diabetics track blood sugar levels continuously. However, the electrodes and electronics require batteries which degrade over time. Researchers are developing glucose monitors powered by piezoelectric and thermoelectric energy harvesters to eliminate the need for battery replacement surgeries.
