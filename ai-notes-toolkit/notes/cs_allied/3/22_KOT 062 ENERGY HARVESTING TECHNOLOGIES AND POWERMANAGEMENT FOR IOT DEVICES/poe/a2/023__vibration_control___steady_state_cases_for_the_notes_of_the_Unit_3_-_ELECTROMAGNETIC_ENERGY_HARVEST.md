### Vibration control & steady state cases

- Vibration control is important to stabilize the energy harvesting system and maximize power output. Some ways to control vibrations:
- Using dampers: Mechanical dampers like shock absorbers reduce amplitude of vibrations. Electronic dampers use Harvesting circuit itself to dampen vibrations by adjusting load.
- Tuning natural frequency: Natural frequency of harvesting system can be tuned to match frequency of ambient vibrations for maximum output. This is done by adjusting stiffness/mass of system.
- Rectification: Harvested power is AC which is converted to DC using rectifiers. This affects oscillation stabilizing as well as power output. Proper rectification is important for steady state optimal performance.
- Various steady state conditions:
-- Harmonic excitation: If excitation is harmonic, system reaches steady state with constant amplitude and frequency. Easy to analyze but not always practical.
-- Periodic excitation: Excitation may be periodic but not harmonic. System reaches periodic steady state with periodic amplitude and frequency. More complex to analyze.
-- Aperiodic/random excitation: Excitation is random. System reaches statistical steady state with random, time-varying amplitude and frequency. Most difficult to analyze.
