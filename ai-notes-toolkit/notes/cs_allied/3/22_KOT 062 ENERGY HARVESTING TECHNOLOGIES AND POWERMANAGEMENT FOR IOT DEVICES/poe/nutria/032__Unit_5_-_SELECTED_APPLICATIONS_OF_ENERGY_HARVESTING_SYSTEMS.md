
## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

1. Energy harvesting systems are devices that capture energy from the environment and convert it into electrical energy. 
2. These systems can be used to power various applications, ranging from powering small electronic devices to powering entire buildings. 
3. Energy harvesting systems are becoming increasingly popular due to their ability to reduce energy costs and their environmental benefits. 
4. Common energy harvesting systems include solar, wind, geothermal, and wave energy systems. 
5. Solar energy harvesting systems use photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight into electricity. 
6. Wind energy harvesting systems use turbines to convert wind into electricity. 
7. Geothermal energy harvesting systems use heat from the Earth’s core to generate electricity. 
8. Wave energy harvesting systems use wave power to generate electricity. 
9. Energy harvesting systems can be used to power a variety of applications, such as lighting, heating, cooling, and communication systems. 
10. Energy harvesting systems can also be used to power medical devices, such as pacemakers and insulin pumps. 
11. Additionally, energy harvesting systems can be used to power sensors, such as motion sensors, temperature sensors, and humidity sensors. 
12. Energy harvesting systems can also be used to power smart homes, providing energy for appliances, lighting, and security systems.
