

### Piezoelectric Materials for Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

* Piezoelectric materials are materials that can convert mechanical energy into electrical energy and vice versa. This makes them ideal for energy harvesting applications, such as powering IoT devices.
* Piezoelectric materials are usually made of a crystalline material, such as quartz, and contain a large number of dipoles that are aligned in a specific direction. When a mechanical stress is applied to the material, the dipoles move, which creates an electric field.
* The amount of energy that can be harvested from a piezoelectric material is determined by its electromechanical properties, such as its piezoelectric coefficient and its dielectric constant.
* Piezoelectric materials can be used in a variety of applications, including sensors, actuators, and transducers. They can also be used for energy harvesting in IoT devices, as they can convert mechanical energy into electrical energy.
* Piezoelectric materials are relatively inexpensive and easy to manufacture, making them an attractive option for energy harvesting applications.
