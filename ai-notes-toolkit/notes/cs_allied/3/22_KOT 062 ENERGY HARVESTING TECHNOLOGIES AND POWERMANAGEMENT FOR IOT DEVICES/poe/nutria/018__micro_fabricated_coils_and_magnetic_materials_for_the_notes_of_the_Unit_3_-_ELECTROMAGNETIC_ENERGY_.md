
### Micro Fabricated Coils and Magnetic Materials

* Micro fabricated coils are coils that are designed and constructed on a microscopic level. They are typically used in applications such as magnetic energy harvesting, non-linear techniques, and other energy-related applications. 
* Micro fabricated coils are usually made from a combination of various metals and/or magnetic materials. The materials used depend on the specific application and the desired performance characteristics. 
* Magnetic materials are materials that are capable of producing a magnetic field. Examples of magnetic materials include ferrite, neodymium, and samarium-cobalt. 
* Non-linear techniques are techniques that involve the use of non-linear components such as transistors or diodes. These components are used to control the flow of electric current in a circuit. Non-linear techniques are often used to increase the efficiency of energy harvesting systems. 
* The use of micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials can help to increase the efficiency of energy harvesting systems and reduce the size of the overall system. This can result in a more compact and efficient energy harvesting system.
