
### Photovoltaic Cell Technologies for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Energy Harvesting Systems in the Subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Powermanagement for IoT Devices KCS

1. Photovoltaic (PV) cells, also known as solar cells, are devices that convert light energy into electrical energy.

2. PV cells are composed of semiconductor materials, such as silicon, that absorb light energy and produce an electrical current.

3. PV cells can be connected in series and/or parallel to increase the amount of power that can be generated.

4. PV cells are used in a variety of applications, including powering satellites, powering remote devices, and providing electricity in remote areas.

5. PV cells are becoming increasingly popular in the residential and commercial sector, as they provide an efficient and cost-effective way to generate electricity.

6. In order to maximize the efficiency of PV cells, they must be installed in an area with direct sunlight and protected from extreme temperatures.

7. PV cells require regular maintenance and cleaning to ensure optimal performance.

8. PV cells are a renewable energy source and can help reduce the reliance on fossil fuels.
