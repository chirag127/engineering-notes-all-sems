

### Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

1. Energy harvesting wireless sensors are devices used to collect and transmit data from a remote location without relying on a power source.

2. These sensors are typically powered by solar, thermal, or kinetic energy, and are able to store the energy they collect in order to operate.

3. They can be used in a variety of applications, such as environmental monitoring, security, and industrial automation.

4. The technology behind energy harvesting wireless sensors is based on the principles of radio frequency (RF) communication and the use of microcontrollers.

5. The microcontroller is responsible for collecting and processing the data from the sensor and sending it to a receiver.

6. The receiver then processes the data and sends it to a computer or other device for further analysis.

7. Energy harvesting wireless sensors are becoming increasingly popular due to their ability to reduce costs associated with traditional power sources.
