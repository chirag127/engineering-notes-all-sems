
# KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES

* Energy harvesting technologies are methods of capturing energy from the environment and converting it into a usable form, such as electricity. 
* Examples of energy harvesting technologies include solar, wind, thermal, kinetic, and radio frequency (RF) energy harvesting. 
* Power management for IoT devices refers to the design and implementation of systems that can effectively manage the power consumption of these devices. 
* This includes the selection of appropriate components, the development of efficient power management algorithms, and the integration of power management systems into the overall design of the device. 
* In order to maximize the efficiency of power management for IoT devices, it is important to consider the characteristics of the energy harvesting technology being used. 
* For example, solar energy harvesting technologies require the use of specialized components and algorithms to manage the power output of the device. 
* In addition, the design of the power management system must take into account the power requirements of the device, including the power requirements of the sensors and actuators. 
* Finally, the design of the power management system must also consider the power requirements of the communication system, as well as the power requirements of the data processing system.
