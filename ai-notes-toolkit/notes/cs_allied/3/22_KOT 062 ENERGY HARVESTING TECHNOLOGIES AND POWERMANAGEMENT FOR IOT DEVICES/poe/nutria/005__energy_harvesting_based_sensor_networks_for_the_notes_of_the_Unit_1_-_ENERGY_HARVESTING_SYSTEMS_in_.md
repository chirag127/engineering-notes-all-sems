
### Energy Harvesting Based Sensor Networks

1. Energy harvesting is the process of capturing energy from the environment, such as solar, wind, thermal, kinetic, and acoustic energy, and converting it into electrical energy for use in electronic devices.

2. Sensor networks are networks of interconnected sensors that collect data from the environment and transmit it to a central location. Sensor networks can be used in a variety of applications, including environmental monitoring, industrial process control, and medical monitoring.

3. Energy harvesting based sensor networks combine the two technologies to create a system that can collect and transmit data without the need for an external power source.

4. These networks are typically composed of energy harvesting modules, sensors, and communication devices. The energy harvesting module captures energy from the environment and converts it into electrical energy. The sensors collect data from the environment and transmit it to the communication device, which can then transmit the data to a central location.

5. Energy harvesting based sensor networks can be used in a variety of applications, including environmental monitoring, industrial process control, and medical monitoring. They provide a cost-effective and reliable way to collect and transmit data without the need for an external power source.
