
### Harvesting Microelectronic Circuits for the Notes of Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

1. Energy harvesting wireless sensors are a type of device that use energy from external sources, such as sunlight, vibrations, or radio waves, to power their operations. 
2. Microelectronic circuits are used in these sensors to capture, convert, and store energy from the external sources.
3. The most common type of energy harvesting circuit is a rectifier, which converts AC power to DC power. 
4. Other components of the energy harvesting circuit include capacitors, inductors, and transistors. 
5. The circuit must also be designed to optimize the efficiency of the energy harvesting process. 
6. The circuit must also be designed to protect the device from electrical surges. 
7. In addition, the circuit must be designed to provide the necessary power to the device. 
8. The circuit must also be designed to minimize power consumption. 
9. Finally, the circuit must be designed to be easily integrated into the device.
