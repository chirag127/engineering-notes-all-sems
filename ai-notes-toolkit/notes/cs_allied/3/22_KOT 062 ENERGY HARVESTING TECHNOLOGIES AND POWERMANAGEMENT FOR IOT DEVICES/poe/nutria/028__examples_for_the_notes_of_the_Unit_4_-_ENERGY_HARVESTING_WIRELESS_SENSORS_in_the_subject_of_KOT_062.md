
### Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS 

* Energy harvesting is a technology used to capture and store energy from sources such as solar, wind, and thermal. 
* Wireless sensors are devices that use radio frequency (RF) technology to send and receive data. 
* They are used in a variety of applications, such as environmental monitoring, medical monitoring, and industrial automation. 
* Wireless sensors are powered by energy harvesting, as they are often located in remote locations where access to a power source is not available. 
* Energy harvesting wireless sensors are used for a variety of purposes, including monitoring temperature, humidity, pressure, and other environmental conditions. 
* They can also be used to detect movement and measure acceleration, vibration, and other physical properties. 
* Energy harvesting wireless sensors are also used in industrial automation and robotics applications, such as controlling motors and other machinery. 
* They are also used in medical applications, such as monitoring vital signs or providing drug delivery. 
* Wireless sensors can be used in conjunction with other technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and Zigbee, to provide a more comprehensive data collection and analysis system.
