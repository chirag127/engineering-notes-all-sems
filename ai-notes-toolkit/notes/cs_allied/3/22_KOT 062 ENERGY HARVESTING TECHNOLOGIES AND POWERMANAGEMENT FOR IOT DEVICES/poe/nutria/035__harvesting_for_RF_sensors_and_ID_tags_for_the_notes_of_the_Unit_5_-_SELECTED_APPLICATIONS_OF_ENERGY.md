
### Harvesting for RF Sensors and ID Tags

- Radio Frequency (RF) sensors and ID tags are devices that harvest energy from radio waves and use it to power small electronic devices. 
- RF sensors and ID tags can be used to detect the presence of objects in a given area, as well as to track and identify objects. 
- RF sensors and ID tags can be used for a variety of applications, including security, surveillance, access control, and asset tracking. 
- RF sensors and ID tags are often used in Internet of Things (IoT) devices, such as smart home appliances, to enable remote monitoring and control. 
- RF sensors and ID tags can be powered by a variety of energy sources, including solar, wind, and thermal energy. 
- RF sensors and ID tags are typically powered by a small battery, and can be used to store energy for later use. 
- The energy harvested from RF sensors and ID tags can be used to power a variety of electronic devices, including sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers. 
- The use of RF sensors and ID tags can reduce the need for wires and cabling, and can help to reduce the cost and complexity of powering electronic devices.
