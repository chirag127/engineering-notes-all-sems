1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting (EMEH) is a technology that enables the conversion of energy from electromagnetic fields into electrical energy.

2. The most common form of EMEH is magnetoelectric energy harvesting, which involves the use of a magnet and an electric coil to generate an electric current.

3. Non-linear techniques are also used in EMEH, such as rectification and frequency conversion.

4. Micro-scale implementations of EMEH are typically used for low-power applications, such as powering small sensors or wireless communication devices.

5. Macro-scale implementations of EMEH are used for large-scale applications, such as powering electric vehicles or large-scale renewable energy systems.

6. In order to maximize the efficiency of EMEH systems, it is important to consider the type of electromagnetic field being harvested, the frequency of the field, and the size and shape of the harvesting device.
