### Case Studies for the Notes of the Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS in the Subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

In this unit, we will be discussing various case studies related to energy harvesting wireless sensors. These case studies will help us understand the practical applications of energy harvesting wireless sensors and how they can be used in various industries.

Here are some of the case studies that we will be discussing in this unit:

1. **Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors in Smart Buildings**

In this case study, we will be discussing how energy harvesting wireless sensors are used in smart buildings to monitor the energy consumption of various devices. The sensors are placed on various devices like HVAC systems, lighting systems, etc., and they collect data on their energy consumption. This data is then used to optimize the energy consumption of these devices, which leads to significant energy savings.

2. **Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors in Agriculture**

In this case study, we will be discussing how energy harvesting wireless sensors are used in agriculture to monitor various parameters like soil moisture, temperature, and humidity. The sensors are placed in the soil and they collect data on these parameters. This data is then used to optimize the irrigation and fertilization of crops, which leads to significant improvements in crop yield and quality.

3. **Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors in Industrial Automation**

In this case study, we will be discussing how energy harvesting wireless sensors are used in industrial automation to monitor various parameters like temperature, pressure, and vibration. The sensors are placed on various machines and they collect data on these parameters. This data is then used to optimize the performance of these machines, which leads to significant improvements in productivity and efficiency.

4. **Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors in Healthcare**

In this case study, we will be discussing how energy harvesting wireless sensors are used in healthcare to monitor various parameters like heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen levels. The sensors are placed on the patient's body and they collect data on these parameters. This data is then used to diagnose and treat various medical conditions, which leads to significant improvements in patient outcomes.

These case studies demonstrate the practical applications of energy harvesting wireless sensors in various industries. By understanding these applications, we can better appreciate the potential of this technology and how it can be used to improve our lives.
