### Introduction

In this unit, we will learn about Energy Harvesting Systems, which are useful for powering IoT devices. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

- Energy Harvesting Systems are designed to convert ambient energy from the environment into electrical energy that can be used to power IoT devices.

- There are various types of ambient energy sources that can be harvested, such as solar, thermal, mechanical, and electromagnetic energy.

- The choice of energy source depends on the application and the environment in which the IoT device will be used.

- Energy harvesting devices typically consist of a transducer, which converts the ambient energy into electrical energy, and a power management circuit, which stores and regulates the energy.

- The power management circuit is important for ensuring that the energy is stored efficiently and used optimally.

- Energy Harvesting Systems have many advantages over traditional battery-powered systems, such as increased lifespan, reduced maintenance, and improved sustainability.

- However, there are also some challenges associated with energy harvesting, such as the variability and unpredictability of ambient energy sources, and the need for efficient energy storage and management.

- In this unit, we will explore these challenges in more detail, and learn about the various techniques and technologies that are used to overcome them.

- By the end of this unit, you will have a comprehensive understanding of Energy Harvesting Systems, and be able to design and implement these systems for IoT applications.
