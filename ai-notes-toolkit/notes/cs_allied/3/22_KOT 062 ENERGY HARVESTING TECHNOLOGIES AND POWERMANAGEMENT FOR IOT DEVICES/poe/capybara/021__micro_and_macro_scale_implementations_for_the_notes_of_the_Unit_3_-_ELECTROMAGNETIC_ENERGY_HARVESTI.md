In this unit, we will discuss the implementation of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques on both micro and macro scales. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

#### Micro Scale Implementations

1. Micro scale implementations of electromagnetic energy harvesting involve the use of small devices that can convert ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. These devices typically use a combination of antennas and rectifiers to capture and convert the energy.
3. Examples of micro scale electromagnetic energy harvesting devices include RFID tags, wireless sensors, and energy harvesting nodes.
4. Non-linear techniques are often used in micro scale implementations to improve the efficiency of the energy conversion process.
5. Non-linear techniques include techniques such as voltage multiplication and resonant frequency tuning.

#### Macro Scale Implementations

1. Macro scale implementations of electromagnetic energy harvesting involve the use of larger devices that can capture and convert energy from larger sources, such as power lines or broadcast towers.
2. These devices typically use larger antennas and rectifiers than micro scale devices.
3. Macro scale electromagnetic energy harvesting devices are often used to power larger electronic devices, such as smart homes or electric vehicles.
4. Non-linear techniques are also used in macro scale implementations to improve efficiency.
5. One example of a non-linear technique used in macro scale electromagnetic energy harvesting is impedance matching.

In conclusion, micro and macro scale implementations of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques play an important role in the development of energy harvesting technologies for IoT devices. By understanding the differences between these scales and the techniques used in each, we can develop more efficient and effective energy harvesting solutions for a wide range of applications.
