## Unit 2 - PIEZO-ELECTRIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND ELECTROMECHANICAL MODELING

In this unit, we will be discussing Piezo-electric energy harvesting and electromechanical modeling. Here are some key points to keep in mind while studying this topic:

- Piezo-electric materials are those materials that produce an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress.
- Piezo-electric energy harvesting is the process of converting mechanical energy to electrical energy using piezo-electric materials.
- The piezo-electric effect was first discovered by Pierre and Jacques Curie in 1880.
- Piezo-electric materials are widely used in various applications, including sensors, actuators, and energy harvesting devices.
- The most commonly used piezo-electric materials are quartz, lead zirconate titanate (PZT), and polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF).
- The efficiency of piezo-electric energy harvesting depends on various factors, including the material properties, the geometry of the device, and the frequency of the mechanical vibrations.
- Electromechanical modeling is the process of mathematically describing the behavior of the piezo-electric devices under different conditions.
- The modeling techniques include finite element analysis (FEA), analytical modeling, and lumped parameter modeling.
- FEA is a numerical technique that uses a mesh of small elements to simulate the behavior of the piezo-electric device.
- Analytical modeling is a mathematical approach that uses equations to predict the behavior of the device.
- Lumped parameter modeling is a simplified approach that represents the device as a collection of discrete elements.
- The modeling techniques are used to optimize the design of the piezo-electric devices for specific applications.
- The optimization involves selecting the appropriate material, geometry, and operating conditions to maximize the efficiency and output power of the device.
- The piezo-electric energy harvesting devices have potential applications in various fields, including aerospace, automotive, and medical devices.

Keep these points in mind while studying Piezo-electric energy harvesting and electromechanical modeling. Understanding these concepts will help you to grasp the subject better and perform well on exams.
