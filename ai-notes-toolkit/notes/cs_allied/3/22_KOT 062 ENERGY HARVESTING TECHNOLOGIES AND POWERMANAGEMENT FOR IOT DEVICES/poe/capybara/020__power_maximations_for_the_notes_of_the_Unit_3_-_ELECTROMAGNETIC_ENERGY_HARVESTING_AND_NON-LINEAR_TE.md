Here are some key points to keep in mind when it comes to power maximization in the context of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques:

- The efficiency of an energy harvesting system can be improved by maximizing the power transfer between the energy source and the load. This involves optimizing the impedance matching between the two components.
- Non-linear techniques can be used to improve power transfer efficiency by manipulating the impedance of the energy harvesting circuit. This can be done using components such as diodes or transistors.
- In order to maximize power output, it is important to carefully select the type and configuration of the energy harvesting device. Factors such as the frequency range of the energy source and the load resistance can have a significant impact on the system's performance.
- Energy storage devices such as capacitors or batteries can be used to smooth out fluctuations in harvested power and provide a more stable source of energy for the load.
- In some cases, it may be beneficial to incorporate multiple energy harvesting devices or sources in order to increase overall power output and improve system reliability.
- It is important to carefully consider the trade-offs between power output, system complexity, and cost when designing an energy harvesting system. A balance must be struck between these factors in order to create a system that is both effective and practical.
