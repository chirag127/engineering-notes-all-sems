## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

In this unit, we will explore some of the selected applications of energy harvesting systems. Here are some points to keep in mind:

- Energy harvesting systems are being used in a wide range of applications to power various devices and systems without the need for external power sources.
- One of the most common applications of energy harvesting systems is in wireless sensor networks. These networks are used in a variety of industries, including agriculture, industrial automation, and environmental monitoring.
- Energy harvesting systems are also increasingly being used in wearable devices. These devices can harvest energy from the movement of the wearer, allowing them to operate without the need for batteries.
- Another important application of energy harvesting systems is in building automation. These systems can be used to power sensors and other devices that monitor and control various aspects of building operation, such as lighting, heating, and cooling.
- Energy harvesting systems are also being used in transportation applications, such as in the monitoring and control of traffic flow and in the powering of small sensors and devices on vehicles.
- Industrial applications of energy harvesting systems include powering wireless sensors and controls in manufacturing and process control systems.
- Finally, energy harvesting systems are increasingly being used in consumer electronics, such as in the powering of low-power devices like remote controls and smart home devices.

In conclusion, energy harvesting systems are being applied in a wide range of industries and applications, providing a sustainable and efficient means of powering devices and systems. Understanding these applications is essential for anyone interested in the field of energy harvesting systems.
