### Vibration Control & Steady State Cases

In the field of electromagnetic energy harvesting, vibration control and steady state cases play a crucial role in improving the performance of energy harvesting devices. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

- Vibration control techniques can help to reduce the impact of external vibrations on energy harvesting devices. This can be achieved through the use of materials with high damping properties or through the use of vibration isolators.
- Steady state cases refer to the situation where the input vibration frequency and amplitude are constant over time. In this case, energy harvesting devices can be designed to operate at their resonant frequency, which results in maximum power output.
- However, in practice, input vibrations are often non-stationary, meaning that the frequency and amplitude change over time. In such cases, it is important to use non-linear techniques to optimize power output.
- Non-linear techniques can be used to design energy harvesting devices that can adapt to changes in input vibration frequency and amplitude. This can be achieved through the use of adaptive control algorithms or through the use of non-linear energy harvesters.
- In addition to vibration control and steady state cases, it is also important to consider the impact of temperature on energy harvesting devices. Temperature can affect the performance of energy harvesters by changing their resonant frequency or by reducing the output voltage.
- To overcome the impact of temperature, it is important to design energy harvesting devices that are temperature-compensated. This can be achieved through the use of materials with temperature-dependent properties or through the use of temperature sensors and compensating circuits.

By considering these important points, it is possible to design energy harvesting devices that can operate effectively in a wide range of operating conditions, including those that are subject to external vibrations and temperature variations.
