## Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

Energy harvesting systems are devices that are used to extract energy from the environment and convert it into usable electrical energy. Energy harvesting systems have been gaining popularity in recent years due to their ability to provide a sustainable source of energy for a variety of applications. Here are some important points to note about energy harvesting systems:

1. Energy harvesting systems can be used to power a variety of devices, including sensors, wireless communication systems, and low power electronic devices.

2. The most commonly used energy sources for energy harvesting systems are solar, thermal, and vibration energy. These sources are abundant in the environment and can be easily harnessed for energy.

3. The efficiency of energy harvesting systems depends on the type of energy source used and the design of the system. It is important to optimize the design of the system to ensure maximum energy extraction.

4. Energy harvesting systems can be designed to operate in a variety of environments, including indoor and outdoor environments. The design of the system will depend on the specific application and environment in which it will be used.

5. The use of energy harvesting systems can help to reduce the reliance on batteries and other non-sustainable sources of energy. This can result in significant cost savings and reduce the environmental impact of energy production.

6. Energy harvesting systems are being used in a variety of applications, including building automation, industrial monitoring, and transportation. As the technology continues to advance, it is expected that energy harvesting systems will become even more widely used in the future.

Overall, energy harvesting systems offer an exciting opportunity to harness the power of the environment and provide a sustainable source of energy for a variety of applications. The design and optimization of these systems will continue to be an important area of research and development in the coming years.
