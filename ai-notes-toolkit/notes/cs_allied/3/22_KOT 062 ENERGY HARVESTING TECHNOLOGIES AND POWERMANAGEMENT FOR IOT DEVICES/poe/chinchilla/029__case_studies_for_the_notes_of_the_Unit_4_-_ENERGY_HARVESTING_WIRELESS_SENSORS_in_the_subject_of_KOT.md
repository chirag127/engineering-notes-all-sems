### Case Studies for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors

In this unit, we will be discussing various case studies related to energy harvesting wireless sensors. These case studies will help us understand the practical applications of energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices.

1. **Wireless Sensor Networks for Real-Time Energy Monitoring**

In this case study, wireless sensor networks were used to monitor energy consumption in real-time. The sensors were powered by energy harvesting technologies such as solar panels and thermoelectric generators. The data collected by the sensors was used to optimize energy consumption and reduce waste.

2. **Energy Harvesting for Wearable Devices**

In this case study, energy harvesting technologies were used to power wearable devices. The devices were designed to harvest energy from the motion of the user's body or from the surrounding environment. This eliminated the need for batteries and made the devices more environmentally friendly.

3. **Energy Harvesting for Structural Health Monitoring**

In this case study, energy harvesting technologies were used to power wireless sensors that monitored the health of structures such as bridges and buildings. The sensors were powered by piezoelectric materials that converted mechanical energy into electrical energy. This allowed for continuous monitoring without the need for battery replacements.

4. **Energy Harvesting for Smart Agriculture**

In this case study, energy harvesting technologies were used to power sensors that monitored soil moisture, temperature, and other environmental factors in agriculture. The sensors were powered by solar panels and were designed to be low-power and long-lasting. This allowed for more efficient use of resources and improved crop yields.

5. **Energy Harvesting for Smart Homes**

In this case study, energy harvesting technologies were used to power sensors that monitored temperature, humidity, and other environmental factors in homes. The sensors were powered by thermoelectric generators and were designed to be low-power and long-lasting. This allowed for more efficient use of energy and reduced energy costs.

These case studies demonstrate the practical applications of energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices. By harnessing energy from the environment, we can create more efficient and sustainable systems that benefit both the environment and society.
