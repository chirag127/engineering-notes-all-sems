## Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

In this unit, you will learn about the important concepts related to electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques. The following points will be covered:

1. Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting:
   - What is electromagnetic energy harvesting?
   - Why is it important?
   - Applications of electromagnetic energy harvesting.
   - How does electromagnetic energy harvesting work?
   - Advantages and disadvantages of electromagnetic energy harvesting.
   
2. Non-linear Techniques:
   - What are non-linear techniques?
   - Why are they important?
   - Applications of non-linear techniques.
   - How do non-linear techniques work?
   - Advantages and disadvantages of non-linear techniques.
   
3. Comparison between Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-linear Techniques:
   - Differences between electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques.
   - Advantages and disadvantages of each technique.
   - Which technique is better for specific applications?
   
4. Recent Developments in Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-linear Techniques:
   - New research and developments in electromagnetic energy harvesting.
   - New research and developments in non-linear techniques.
   - Future prospects for both techniques.
   
5. Design Considerations:
   - Factors to consider when designing electromagnetic energy harvesting systems.
   - Factors to consider when designing non-linear systems.
   - How to optimize the design of these systems.
   
6. Case Studies:
   - Examples of real-world applications of electromagnetic energy harvesting.
   - Examples of real-world applications of non-linear techniques.
   - Analysis of the effectiveness of these techniques in these applications.
   
By the end of this unit, you will have a thorough understanding of the principles, applications, advantages, and disadvantages of both electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques. You will also be able to evaluate and design these systems for specific applications.
