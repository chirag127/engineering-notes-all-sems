# Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

## Conversion

1. Conversion is the process of transforming one form of energy into another.
2. In the context of energy harvesting wireless sensors, conversion refers to the transformation of ambient energy sources into electrical energy that can be used to power the sensors.
3. Common ambient energy sources that can be harvested include solar, thermal, mechanical, and electromagnetic energy.
4. The conversion process typically involves the use of transducers, which are devices that convert one form of energy into another.
5. For example, a photovoltaic cell is a transducer that converts solar energy into electrical energy.
6. The efficiency of the conversion process is an important factor in the design of energy harvesting wireless sensors, as it determines how much of the harvested energy can be used to power the sensors.
7. Different conversion techniques and technologies are used depending on the type of ambient energy being harvested and the requirements of the wireless sensor system.
