# Vibration Control & Steady State Cases

Vibration control is a technique used to reduce or eliminate unwanted vibrations in a system. This is important in many applications, including energy harvesting, where vibrations can reduce the efficiency of the system or cause damage to the components.

In the context of electromagnetic energy harvesting, vibration control can be achieved through the use of various techniques, including:

1. Tuning the natural frequency of the system to match the frequency of the vibrations.
2. Using damping materials to absorb the vibrations.
3. Implementing active control systems to counteract the vibrations.

Steady state cases refer to situations where the system has reached a stable operating condition and is no longer changing. In the context of energy harvesting, this can refer to the point at which the energy harvester is producing a consistent amount of power.

In the study of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques, it is important to understand both vibration control and steady state cases in order to optimize the performance of the system.
