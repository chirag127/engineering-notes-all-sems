# Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

## Photovoltaic Cell Technologies

- Photovoltaic (PV) technologies, more commonly known as solar panels, generate power using devices that absorb energy from sunlight and convert it into electrical energy through semiconducting materials.
- These devices, known as solar cells, are then connected to form larger power-generating units known as modules or panels.
- PV gets its name from the process of converting light (photons) to electricity (voltage), which is called the photovoltaic effect.
- A single PV device is known as a cell, and these cells are connected together in chains to form larger units known as modules or panels.
- An individual PV cell is usually small, typically producing about 1 or 2 watts of power.
- These cells are made of different semiconductor materials.
