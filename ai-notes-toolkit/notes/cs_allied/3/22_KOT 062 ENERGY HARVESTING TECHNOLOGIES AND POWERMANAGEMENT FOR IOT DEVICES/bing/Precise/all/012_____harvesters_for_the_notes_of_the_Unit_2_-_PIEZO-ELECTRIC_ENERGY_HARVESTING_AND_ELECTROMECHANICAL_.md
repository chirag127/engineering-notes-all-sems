# Harvesters

Harvesters are devices that convert ambient energy sources into usable electrical energy. In the context of Unit 2 - Piezo-Electric Energy Harvesting and Electromechanical Modeling, of the subject KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, harvesters refer to piezoelectric energy harvesters.

Piezoelectric energy harvesters convert mechanical strain into electrical energy through the piezoelectric effect. This effect occurs in certain materials, such as quartz, that generate an electric charge in response to applied mechanical stress.

Some key points to consider when studying piezoelectric energy harvesters include:

1. The piezoelectric effect is reversible, meaning that an applied electric field can also generate mechanical strain in the material.
2. The amount of energy that can be harvested depends on the properties of the piezoelectric material, the magnitude of the mechanical strain, and the frequency of the strain.
3. Piezoelectric energy harvesters can be used to power small electronic devices, such as sensors and wireless transmitters, by converting ambient vibrations into electrical energy.
4. Electromechanical modeling is used to design and optimize piezoelectric energy harvesters for specific applications.
