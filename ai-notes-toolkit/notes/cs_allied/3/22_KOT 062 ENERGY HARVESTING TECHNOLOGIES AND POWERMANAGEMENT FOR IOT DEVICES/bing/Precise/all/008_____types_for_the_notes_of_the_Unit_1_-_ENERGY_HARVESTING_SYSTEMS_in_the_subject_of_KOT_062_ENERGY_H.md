# Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

Energy harvesting, also known as power harvesting or energy scavenging, is the process of capturing and storing small amounts of energy from external sources such as solar, thermal, wind, and kinetic energy. This energy can then be used to power electronic devices, particularly those used in the Internet of Things (IoT).

There are several types of energy harvesting systems, including:

1. **Solar Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from the sun using photovoltaic cells. The captured energy is then stored in a battery or capacitor for later use.

2. **Thermal Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from temperature differences, such as the difference between the temperature of a device and its surroundings. The captured energy is then converted into electricity using thermoelectric generators.

3. **Wind Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from the movement of air using small wind turbines. The captured energy is then stored in a battery or capacitor for later use.

4. **Kinetic Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from the movement of an object, such as a person walking or a vehicle moving. The captured energy is then stored in a battery or capacitor for later use.

These are just a few examples of the types of energy harvesting systems that can be used to power IoT devices. Each type of system has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of system will depend on the specific application and environment in which it will be used.
