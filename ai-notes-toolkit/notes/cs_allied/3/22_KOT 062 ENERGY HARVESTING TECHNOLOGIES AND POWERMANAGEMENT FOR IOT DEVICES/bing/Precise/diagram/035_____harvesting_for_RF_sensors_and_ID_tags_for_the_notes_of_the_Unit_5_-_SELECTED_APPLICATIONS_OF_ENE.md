### Harvesting for RF Sensors and ID Tags

Radio frequency (RF) sensors and identification (ID) tags are widely used in various applications, such as inventory management, asset tracking, and supply chain management. These devices typically require a power source to operate, and energy harvesting can provide a sustainable and maintenance-free solution.

1. RF energy harvesting involves capturing the energy from ambient RF signals, such as those from radio and television broadcasts, cell phone transmissions, and Wi-Fi signals.
2. This energy can be converted into electrical energy and used to power RF sensors and ID tags.
3. RF energy harvesting can eliminate the need for batteries, reducing maintenance costs and environmental impact.
4. RF energy harvesting can also enable the deployment of sensors and ID tags in remote or hard-to-reach locations, where replacing batteries would be difficult or impractical.
5. RF energy harvesting can be integrated into the design of RF sensors and ID tags, making them self-sustaining and maintenance-free.
