### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Energy harvesting is the process of capturing and storing small amounts of energy from external sources such as solar, thermal, wind, and kinetic energy.
2. This energy can be used to power small electronic devices, such as sensors, microcontrollers, and wireless communication devices.
3. Energy harvesting systems are becoming increasingly important for the Internet of Things (IoT) devices, as they can provide a sustainable power source for these devices, reducing the need for battery replacement or recharging.
4. Energy harvesting technologies include photovoltaic, thermoelectric, piezoelectric, and electromagnetic, among others.
5. Power management is a crucial aspect of energy harvesting systems, as it involves regulating and controlling the flow of energy from the source to the load.
6. Efficient power management can help to maximize the amount of energy harvested and prolong the lifetime of the energy storage device.
7. In this unit, we will explore the various energy harvesting technologies and power management techniques used in IoT devices.
