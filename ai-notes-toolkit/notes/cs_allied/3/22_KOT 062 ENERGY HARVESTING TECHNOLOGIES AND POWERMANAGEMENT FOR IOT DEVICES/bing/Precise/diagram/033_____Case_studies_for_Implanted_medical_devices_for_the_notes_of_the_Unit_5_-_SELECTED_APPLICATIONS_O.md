### Case studies for Implanted medical devices

Implanted medical devices are devices that are placed inside the body to monitor, diagnose, or treat medical conditions. These devices can be powered by energy harvesting systems, which convert energy from the environment into electrical energy to power the device. Here are some case studies of implanted medical devices that use energy harvesting systems:

1. **Pacemakers:** Pacemakers are devices that are implanted in the chest to regulate the heartbeat. Some pacemakers use energy harvesting systems that convert the motion of the chest during breathing into electrical energy to power the device.

2. **Implantable glucose monitors:** Implantable glucose monitors are devices that are placed under the skin to continuously monitor blood sugar levels. Some implantable glucose monitors use energy harvesting systems that convert the heat from the body into electrical energy to power the device.

3. **Cochlear implants:** Cochlear implants are devices that are implanted in the ear to restore hearing. Some cochlear implants use energy harvesting systems that convert the motion of the head into electrical energy to power the device.

These are just a few examples of implanted medical devices that use energy harvesting systems. As technology advances, it is likely that more and more implanted medical devices will use energy harvesting systems to power their operations. This can help to reduce the need for battery replacements and improve the longevity of the devices.
