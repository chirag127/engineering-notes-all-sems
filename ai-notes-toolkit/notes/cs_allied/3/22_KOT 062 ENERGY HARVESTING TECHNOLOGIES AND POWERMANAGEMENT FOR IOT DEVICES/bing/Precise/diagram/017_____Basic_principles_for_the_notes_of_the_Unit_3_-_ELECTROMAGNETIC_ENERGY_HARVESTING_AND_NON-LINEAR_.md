### Basic principles for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This energy can be harvested from sources such as radio waves, solar radiation, and electromagnetic fields.
3. Non-linear techniques are used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting by optimizing the conversion of electromagnetic energy into electrical energy.
4. These techniques include the use of non-linear materials, non-linear circuits, and non-linear control algorithms.
5. Electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques are important for the development of self-powered IoT devices, as they allow these devices to operate without the need for external power sources.
6. This can improve the reliability and longevity of IoT devices, as well as reduce their environmental impact.
7. Further study of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques is necessary to continue to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of these technologies.
