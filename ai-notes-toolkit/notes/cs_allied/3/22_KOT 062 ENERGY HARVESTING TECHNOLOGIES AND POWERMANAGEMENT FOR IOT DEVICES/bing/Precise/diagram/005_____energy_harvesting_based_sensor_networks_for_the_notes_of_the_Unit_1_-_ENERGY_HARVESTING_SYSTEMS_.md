# Energy Harvesting Based Sensor Networks

Energy harvesting based sensor networks are a type of wireless sensor network that utilizes energy harvesting technologies to power the sensors. This allows the sensors to operate without the need for an external power source, such as a battery. Here are some key points to consider when studying energy harvesting based sensor networks for Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS:

1. Energy harvesting technologies can include solar, thermal, vibration, and radio frequency (RF) energy harvesting.
2. The energy harvested is stored in a capacitor or rechargeable battery and used to power the sensor.
3. Energy harvesting based sensor networks can be used in remote or hard-to-reach locations where it is difficult to replace batteries.
4. The use of energy harvesting technologies can increase the lifespan of the sensor network and reduce maintenance costs.
5. Energy management is an important consideration in energy harvesting based sensor networks to ensure that the harvested energy is used efficiently.
6. Energy harvesting based sensor networks can be used in a variety of applications, including environmental monitoring, structural health monitoring, and smart agriculture.
