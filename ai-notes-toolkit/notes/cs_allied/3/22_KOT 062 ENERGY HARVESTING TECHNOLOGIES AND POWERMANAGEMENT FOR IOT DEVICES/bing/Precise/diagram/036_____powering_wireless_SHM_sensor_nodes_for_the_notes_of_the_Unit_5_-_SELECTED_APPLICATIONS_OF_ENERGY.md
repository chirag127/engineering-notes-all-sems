### Powering Wireless SHM Sensor Nodes

Wireless sensor nodes are used in Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) to monitor the condition of structures such as bridges, buildings, and pipelines. These sensor nodes require a power source to operate. In the context of Unit 5 - Selected Applications of Energy Harvesting Systems, in the subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, the following points are relevant:

1. Energy harvesting technologies can be used to power wireless SHM sensor nodes. This can eliminate the need for battery replacement, reducing maintenance costs and increasing the lifespan of the sensor nodes.
2. Common energy harvesting technologies used to power wireless SHM sensor nodes include solar, vibration, and thermal energy harvesting.
3. Solar energy harvesting uses photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight into electricity. This can be a suitable power source for SHM sensor nodes in outdoor environments with sufficient sunlight.
4. Vibration energy harvesting converts mechanical vibrations into electricity. This can be a suitable power source for SHM sensor nodes in environments with regular vibrations, such as on bridges or machinery.
5. Thermal energy harvesting converts temperature differences into electricity. This can be a suitable power source for SHM sensor nodes in environments with regular temperature changes, such as near HVAC systems or in industrial processes.
6. The choice of energy harvesting technology for powering wireless SHM sensor nodes depends on the specific environment and application. Factors to consider include the availability of the energy source, the power requirements of the sensor nodes, and the cost and complexity of the energy harvesting system.
7. Power management techniques can be used to optimize the performance of the energy harvesting system and the wireless SHM sensor nodes. This can include techniques such as duty cycling, power-aware routing, and adaptive sampling.
8. Energy storage devices such as batteries or supercapacitors can be used to store the harvested energy and provide a stable power source for the wireless SHM sensor nodes.
