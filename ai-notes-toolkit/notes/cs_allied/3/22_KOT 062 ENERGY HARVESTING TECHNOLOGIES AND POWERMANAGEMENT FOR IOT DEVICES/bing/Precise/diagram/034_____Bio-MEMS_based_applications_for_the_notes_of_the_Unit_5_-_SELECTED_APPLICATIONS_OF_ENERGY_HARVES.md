### Bio-MEMS based applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

Bio-MEMS (Biological Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems) are microsystems that integrate biological components with microelectronics and microfabrication technologies. These systems have a wide range of applications in the field of energy harvesting, particularly in the development of self-powered devices for the Internet of Things (IoT).

Some of the Bio-MEMS based applications for energy harvesting include:

1. **Implantable medical devices:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop implantable medical devices that harvest energy from the body to power themselves. For example, a pacemaker that uses the motion of the heart to generate electricity.

2. **Wearable devices:** Bio-MEMS can be integrated into wearable devices to harvest energy from the motion of the body. For example, a smartwatch that generates electricity from the movement of the wrist.

3. **Environmental monitoring:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop self-powered sensors for environmental monitoring. For example, a sensor that harvests energy from the wind or the sun to power itself.

4. **Wireless sensor networks:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop self-powered nodes for wireless sensor networks. These nodes can harvest energy from their environment to power themselves, reducing the need for batteries and maintenance.

Overall, Bio-MEMS based applications have the potential to significantly improve the efficiency and sustainability of energy harvesting systems for IoT devices. These systems can provide a reliable and long-lasting source of power, reducing the need for batteries and other external power sources.
