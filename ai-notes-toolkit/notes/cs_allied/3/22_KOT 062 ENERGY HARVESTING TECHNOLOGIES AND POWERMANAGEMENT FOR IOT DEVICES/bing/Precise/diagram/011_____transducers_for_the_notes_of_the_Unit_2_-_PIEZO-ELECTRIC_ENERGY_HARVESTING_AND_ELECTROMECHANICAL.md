### Transducers

Transducers are devices that convert one form of energy into another. In the context of Unit 2 - Piezo-Electric Energy Harvesting and Electromechanical Modeling in the subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, transducers are used to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy.

Some key points to note about transducers in this context are:

1. Piezoelectric transducers are a type of transducer that converts mechanical energy into electrical energy using the piezoelectric effect.
2. The piezoelectric effect is the ability of certain materials to generate an electric charge in response to applied mechanical stress.
3. Piezoelectric transducers are commonly used in energy harvesting applications to convert ambient mechanical vibrations into usable electrical energy.
4. Electromechanical modeling is used to design and optimize piezoelectric transducers for energy harvesting applications.
5. The performance of a piezoelectric transducer depends on factors such as the material properties, geometry, and operating conditions.
