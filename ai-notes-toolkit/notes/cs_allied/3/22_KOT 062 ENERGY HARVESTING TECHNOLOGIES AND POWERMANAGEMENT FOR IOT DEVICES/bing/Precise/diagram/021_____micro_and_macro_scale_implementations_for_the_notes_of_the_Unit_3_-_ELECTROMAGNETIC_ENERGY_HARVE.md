### Micro and Macro Scale Implementations

Unit 3 - Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-Linear Techniques

Subject: KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This energy can be harvested from sources such as radio waves, solar radiation, and even the Earth's magnetic field.
3. On a micro scale, electromagnetic energy harvesting can be used to power small devices such as sensors, wearables, and other IoT devices.
4. On a macro scale, electromagnetic energy harvesting can be used to generate electricity on a larger scale, such as through the use of solar panels or wind turbines.
5. Non-linear techniques, such as rectennas and frequency up-conversion, can be used to improve the efficiency of electromagnetic energy harvesting.
6. These techniques can help to capture and convert a wider range of frequencies and increase the amount of usable energy that can be harvested.
7. Overall, the use of electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques can provide a sustainable and renewable source of energy for both micro and macro scale implementations.
