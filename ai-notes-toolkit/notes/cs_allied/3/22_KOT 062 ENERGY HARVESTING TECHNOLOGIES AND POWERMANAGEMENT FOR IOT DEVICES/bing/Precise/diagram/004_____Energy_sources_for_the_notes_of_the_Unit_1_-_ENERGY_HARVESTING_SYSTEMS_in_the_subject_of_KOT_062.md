### Energy sources for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. **Solar energy:** Solar energy is the most abundant and widely used renewable energy source. It is harnessed through the use of photovoltaic cells, which convert sunlight into electricity.

2. **Wind energy:** Wind energy is harnessed through the use of wind turbines, which convert the kinetic energy of the wind into mechanical energy, which is then converted into electricity.

3. **Hydro energy:** Hydro energy is harnessed through the use of hydroelectric dams, which convert the potential energy of water into mechanical energy, which is then converted into electricity.

4. **Geothermal energy:** Geothermal energy is harnessed through the use of geothermal power plants, which use the heat from the Earth's core to generate steam, which is then used to generate electricity.

5. **Biomass energy:** Biomass energy is harnessed through the use of biomass power plants, which convert organic matter into energy through the process of combustion.

6. **Tidal energy:** Tidal energy is harnessed through the use of tidal power plants, which convert the kinetic energy of the tides into mechanical energy, which is then converted into electricity.

7. **Nuclear energy:** Nuclear energy is harnessed through the use of nuclear power plants, which use nuclear reactions to generate heat, which is then used to generate steam, which is then used to generate electricity.
