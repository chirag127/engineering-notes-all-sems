### Power sources for WSN

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSN) are composed of small, low-power devices that are used to monitor and collect data from their environment. These devices are typically powered by batteries, but there are other power sources that can be used to extend the lifetime of the network. Here are some of the power sources that can be used for WSN:

1. **Batteries:** Batteries are the most common power source for WSN. They are easy to install and can provide a reliable power source for the devices. However, batteries have a limited lifetime and need to be replaced periodically.

2. **Solar power:** Solar power can be used to power WSN devices. Solar panels can be installed on the devices to collect energy from the sun and convert it into electricity. This can provide a long-term power source for the devices, but it is dependent on the availability of sunlight.

3. **Wind power:** Wind power can also be used to power WSN devices. Small wind turbines can be installed on the devices to generate electricity from the wind. This can provide a long-term power source for the devices, but it is dependent on the availability of wind.

4. **Thermal energy:** Thermal energy can be harvested from the environment to power WSN devices. This can be done by using thermoelectric generators that convert temperature differences into electricity. This can provide a long-term power source for the devices, but it is dependent on the availability of temperature differences.

5. **Vibration energy:** Vibration energy can be harvested from the environment to power WSN devices. This can be done by using piezoelectric generators that convert mechanical vibrations into electricity. This can provide a long-term power source for the devices, but it is dependent on the availability of vibrations.

These are some of the power sources that can be used for WSN. The choice of power source will depend on the specific requirements of the network and the availability of the different power sources in the environment.
