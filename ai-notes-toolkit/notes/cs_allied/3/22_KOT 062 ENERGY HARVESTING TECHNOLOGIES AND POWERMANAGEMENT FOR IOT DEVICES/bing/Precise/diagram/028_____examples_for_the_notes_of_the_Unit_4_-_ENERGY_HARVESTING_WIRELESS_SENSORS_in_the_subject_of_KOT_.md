### Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

#### Introduction
Energy harvesting wireless sensors are devices that can convert ambient energy sources such as light, heat, and motion into electrical energy. This energy can then be used to power wireless sensors, eliminating the need for batteries or wired power sources.

#### Examples of Energy Harvesting Wireless Sensors
1. **Solar-powered sensors:** These sensors use photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight into electricity. They are commonly used in outdoor applications such as weather monitoring and agriculture.
2. **Thermoelectric sensors:** These sensors use the Seebeck effect to generate electricity from temperature differences. They can be used in applications such as industrial process monitoring and building automation.
3. **Piezoelectric sensors:** These sensors use the piezoelectric effect to generate electricity from mechanical stress or strain. They can be used in applications such as structural health monitoring and human motion tracking.
4. **Electromagnetic sensors:** These sensors use electromagnetic induction to generate electricity from motion. They can be used in applications such as vehicle monitoring and energy harvesting from machinery.

#### Conclusion
Energy harvesting wireless sensors provide a sustainable and maintenance-free solution for powering wireless sensors. They can be used in a wide range of applications and can help reduce the environmental impact of battery-powered sensors.
