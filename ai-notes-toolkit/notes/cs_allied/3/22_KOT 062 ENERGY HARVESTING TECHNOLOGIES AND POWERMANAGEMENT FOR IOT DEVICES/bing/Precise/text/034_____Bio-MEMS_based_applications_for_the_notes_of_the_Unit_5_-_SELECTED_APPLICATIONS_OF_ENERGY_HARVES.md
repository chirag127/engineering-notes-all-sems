### Bio-MEMS based applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

Bio-MEMS (Biological Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems) are microsystems that integrate biological components with microelectronics and microfabrication technologies. These systems have a wide range of applications in the field of energy harvesting, particularly in the development of self-powered devices for the Internet of Things (IoT).

Some of the Bio-MEMS based applications for energy harvesting include:

1. **Implantable medical devices:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop self-powered implantable medical devices that harvest energy from the body's natural movements or temperature differences. This can eliminate the need for battery replacement surgeries and improve the reliability and lifespan of the devices.

2. **Wearable devices:** Bio-MEMS can also be used to develop self-powered wearable devices that harvest energy from the user's movements or body heat. This can extend the battery life of the devices and reduce the need for frequent charging.

3. **Environmental monitoring:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop self-powered sensors for environmental monitoring that harvest energy from sources such as solar, wind, or temperature differences. This can enable the deployment of large-scale sensor networks for long-term monitoring without the need for battery replacement.

4. **Wireless sensor networks:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop self-powered wireless sensors that harvest energy from ambient sources such as light, heat, or vibrations. This can enable the deployment of large-scale wireless sensor networks for applications such as structural health monitoring, smart homes, and industrial automation.

Overall, Bio-MEMS based energy harvesting systems have the potential to significantly improve the efficiency, reliability, and sustainability of IoT devices and applications.
