### Electromechanical modeling of Lumped parameter model and coupled distributed parameter models and closed-form solutions

1. **Lumped parameter model:** A lumped parameter model is a simplified representation of a physical system that assumes that the system's behavior can be accurately modeled using a finite number of discrete elements or parameters. These elements are typically masses, springs, and dampers, and they are connected in such a way as to represent the system's dynamic behavior.

2. **Coupled distributed parameter models:** Coupled distributed parameter models are used to represent systems that cannot be accurately modeled using lumped parameter models. These models take into account the spatial distribution of the system's properties, such as mass, stiffness, and damping, and they use partial differential equations to describe the system's behavior.

3. **Closed-form solutions:** Closed-form solutions are analytical solutions to mathematical problems that can be expressed in a finite number of standard mathematical operations. These solutions are often preferred over numerical solutions because they provide exact answers and can be more easily interpreted.
