### Case Studies for the Notes of the Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS in the Subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. **Electric Field Energy Harvesting Powered Wireless Sensors for Smart Grid**: This case study discusses the use of electric field energy harvesting to power sensor nodes in wireless ubiquitous sensor networks and smart grid systems. The harvested average power is about 47 µW in this case.

2. **Energy Harvesting in Wireless Sensor Networks**: This book is organized into four major parts. The first part introduces essential fundamentals and methods, while the second part focuses on vibration converters and hybridization. The third part is dedicated to wireless energy transfer, including both RF and inductive energy transfer.

3. **Solar Energy Harvesting System for Wireless Sensors**: This case study discusses the use of solar energy harvesting to power wireless sensors. The general block diagram of the solar energy harvesting system for wireless sensors is shown in the study. The energy storage device is used to store generated energy as well as buffering the power required by sensor nodes.
