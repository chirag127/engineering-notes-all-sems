### Microgenerators

- Microgenerators are devices that generate electricity continuously when subjected to varying mechanical strain, such as ambient vibrations.
- Piezoelectric energy microgenerators are a type of microgenerator that utilizes the piezoelectric effect of different materials to generate electrical energy.
- The performance of piezoelectric energy microgenerators can be analyzed and modeled mathematically.
- Microelectromechanical system (MEMS)-based piezoelectric energy harvesting (PEH) devices can transform mechanical vibrations, such as those from domestic washing machines, into electrical energy.
- PEH devices can supply electrical energy to low-power IoT sensors that are exposed to mechanical vibrations in their surrounding environment.
- Electromechanical modeling and simulation approaches can be used to analyze the performance of piezoelectric energy harvesters and energy extraction networks.
