### Powering Wireless SHM Sensor Nodes

Wireless sensor nodes are used in Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) to monitor the condition of structures such as bridges, buildings, and pipelines. These sensor nodes require a power source to operate. In the context of Unit 5 - Selected Applications of Energy Harvesting Systems, in the subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, the following points can be considered:

1. Energy harvesting technologies can be used to power wireless SHM sensor nodes. These technologies convert ambient energy sources such as light, vibration, and temperature differences into electrical energy.
2. Solar energy is a common source of energy for powering wireless SHM sensor nodes. Photovoltaic cells can be used to convert sunlight into electrical energy.
3. Vibration energy harvesting can also be used to power wireless SHM sensor nodes. This involves converting the mechanical energy from vibrations into electrical energy using piezoelectric or electromagnetic transducers.
4. Thermal energy harvesting can be used to power wireless SHM sensor nodes in environments where there are temperature differences. This involves converting the thermal energy from temperature gradients into electrical energy using thermoelectric generators.
5. Energy storage devices such as batteries and supercapacitors can be used to store the harvested energy for later use by the wireless SHM sensor nodes.
6. Power management techniques can be used to optimize the use of the harvested energy by the wireless SHM sensor nodes. This can involve adjusting the duty cycle of the sensor nodes, using low power modes, and implementing energy-efficient communication protocols.
