### Energy Harvesting Based Sensor Networks

1. Energy harvesting technologies remove the hurdles associated with battery-backed sensor nodes .
2. Each sensor node on the wireless network has an energy-harvesting unit, energy storage unit, and sensors .
3. The energy-harvesting systems also store the energy which is generated, and this can be later be used when the energy source is passive .
4. Energy harvesting wireless sensor network (EH-WSN) is considered to be one of the key enabling technologies for the internet of things (IoT) construction .
5. Researchers are coming up with innovative ways to get energy from alternative sources—even human sweat .
6. Wireless sensor networks need energy harvesting to replace battery power .
7. It's clear that wireless sensor networks and mobile sensor systems are proliferating everywhere .
