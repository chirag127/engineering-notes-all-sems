### Energy sources for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. **Solar energy:** Solar energy is the most abundant and widely used renewable energy source. It is harnessed through the use of photovoltaic cells, which convert sunlight into electricity.

2. **Wind energy:** Wind energy is harnessed through the use of wind turbines, which convert the kinetic energy of the wind into mechanical energy, which is then converted into electricity.

3. **Hydro energy:** Hydro energy is harnessed through the use of water turbines, which convert the kinetic energy of flowing water into mechanical energy, which is then converted into electricity.

4. **Geothermal energy:** Geothermal energy is harnessed through the use of geothermal heat pumps, which use the constant temperature of the earth to heat and cool buildings.

5. **Biomass energy:** Biomass energy is harnessed through the use of biomass boilers, which burn organic matter such as wood, crops, or waste to produce heat and electricity.

6. **Nuclear energy:** Nuclear energy is harnessed through the use of nuclear reactors, which use the heat generated by nuclear fission to produce steam, which is then used to generate electricity.

7. **Fossil fuels:** Fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and natural gas are non-renewable energy sources that are used to generate electricity through the burning of the fuel.

These are some of the energy sources that can be used in energy harvesting systems. Each source has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of energy source will depend on factors such as availability, cost, and environmental impact. It is important to consider these factors when designing an energy harvesting system.
