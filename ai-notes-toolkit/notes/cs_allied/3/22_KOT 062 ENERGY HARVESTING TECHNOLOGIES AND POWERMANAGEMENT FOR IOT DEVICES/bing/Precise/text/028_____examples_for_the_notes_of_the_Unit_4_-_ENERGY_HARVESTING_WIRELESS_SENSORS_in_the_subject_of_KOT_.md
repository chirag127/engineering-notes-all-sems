### Examples for the notes of the Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Totalizers used by oil and gas field and flow meters used by water industry are examples of energy harvesting in wireless sensor network.
2. Watches that are powered by the kinetic energy of a person’s movement is an example of energy harvesting system for IoT sensors.
3. Piezoelectric powered damage tracking nodes for helicopters as well as solar powered strain and seismic sensor networks for bridges are examples of energy harvesting wireless sensors.
