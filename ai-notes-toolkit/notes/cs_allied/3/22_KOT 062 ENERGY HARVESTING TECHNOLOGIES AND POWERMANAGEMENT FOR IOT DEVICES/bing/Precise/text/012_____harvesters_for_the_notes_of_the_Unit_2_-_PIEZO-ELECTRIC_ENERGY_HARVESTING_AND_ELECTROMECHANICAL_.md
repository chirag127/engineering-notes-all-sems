### Harvesters

Harvesters are devices that convert ambient energy into electrical energy. In the context of Unit 2 - Piezo-Electric Energy Harvesting and Electromechanical Modeling of the subject KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS, harvesters refer to piezoelectric energy harvesters.

Piezoelectric energy harvesters convert mechanical strain into electrical energy through the piezoelectric effect. This effect occurs in certain materials, such as quartz, that generate an electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress.

Some key points to note about piezoelectric energy harvesters are:
- They can be used to harvest energy from sources such as vibrations, impacts, and acoustic noise.
- They are typically small in size and can be integrated into various devices and systems.
- They can be used to power low-power electronic devices, such as sensors and wireless communication devices.
- The amount of energy that can be harvested depends on factors such as the material properties, the frequency and amplitude of the mechanical strain, and the electrical load.

In summary, piezoelectric energy harvesters are devices that convert mechanical strain into electrical energy through the piezoelectric effect. They can be used to harvest energy from various sources and can be integrated into various devices and systems. The amount of energy that can be harvested depends on various factors.
