## Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

- Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of collecting small amounts of ambient energy to power wireless devices.
- Three types of models of electromagnetic energy harvesting systems have been developed:
    1. A linear electromagnetic energy harvester is connected to a linear interface circuit, which is a pure resistive electrical load.
    2. A nonlinear electromagnetic energy harvester is connected to a linear circuit.
    3. A nonlinear electromagnetic energy harvester is connected to a nonlinear circuit.
- A nonlinear electromagnetic energy harvester (EMEH) with high output voltage can be connected to a nonlinear interface circuit.
- A power management circuit (PMC) can be designed to match the load resistance of the nonlinear interface circuit using a buck-boost direct current-direct current (DC-DC) converter.
- Electromagnetic transducers can be used for the harvesting of kinetic (vibration) energy.
- The voltage generated by an electromagnetic transducer is linked to the product of the flux linkage gradient and the velocity.
- The performance and potential of energy-harvesting devices depend strongly on the performance and properties of the materials and techniques used.
