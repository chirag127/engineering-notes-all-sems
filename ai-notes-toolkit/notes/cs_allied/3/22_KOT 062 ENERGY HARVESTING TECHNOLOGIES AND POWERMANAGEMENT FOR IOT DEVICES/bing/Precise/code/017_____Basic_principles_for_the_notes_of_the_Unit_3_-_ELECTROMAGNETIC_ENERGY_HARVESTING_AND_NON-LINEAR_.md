### Basic principles for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This energy can be harvested from sources such as radio frequency (RF) signals, ambient light, and vibrations.
3. Non-linear techniques are used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting by optimizing the conversion of ambient energy into electrical energy.
4. These techniques include the use of non-linear materials, resonant circuits, and frequency up-conversion.
5. Electromagnetic energy harvesting can be used to power low-power devices such as sensors and wireless communication devices.
6. This technology has applications in the Internet of Things (IoT) where it can be used to power devices in remote or hard-to-reach locations.
7. Power management is an important aspect of energy harvesting systems, as it is necessary to efficiently store and use the harvested energy.
8. This can be achieved through the use of energy storage devices such as batteries and capacitors, as well as power management circuits that regulate the flow of energy.
