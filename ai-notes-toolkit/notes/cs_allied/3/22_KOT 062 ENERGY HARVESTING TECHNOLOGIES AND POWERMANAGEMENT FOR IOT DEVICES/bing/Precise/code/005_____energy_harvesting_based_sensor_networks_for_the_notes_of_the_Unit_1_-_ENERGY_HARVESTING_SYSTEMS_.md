### Energy Harvesting Based Sensor Networks

Energy harvesting based sensor networks are a type of wireless sensor network that uses energy harvesting techniques to power the sensors. This allows the sensors to operate without the need for batteries or external power sources, making them more sustainable and cost-effective.

Some key points to consider when studying energy harvesting based sensor networks for Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS are:

1. Energy harvesting techniques can include solar, thermal, vibration, and radio frequency energy.
2. The harvested energy is stored in a capacitor or rechargeable battery and used to power the sensor.
3. Energy harvesting based sensor networks can be used in a variety of applications, including environmental monitoring, structural health monitoring, and smart buildings.
4. The use of energy harvesting techniques can reduce the maintenance and replacement costs associated with traditional battery-powered sensors.
5. Energy management is a crucial aspect of energy harvesting based sensor networks, as the harvested energy must be efficiently used to ensure the longevity of the network.
