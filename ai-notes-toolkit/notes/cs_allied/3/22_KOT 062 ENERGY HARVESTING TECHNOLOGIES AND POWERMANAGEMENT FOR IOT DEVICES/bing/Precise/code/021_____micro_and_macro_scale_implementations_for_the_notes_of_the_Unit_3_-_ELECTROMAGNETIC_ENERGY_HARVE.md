### Micro and Macro Scale Implementations for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-Linear Techniques in the Subject of KOT 062 Energy Harvesting Technologies and Power Management for IoT Devices KCS

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This technology can be implemented on both micro and macro scales.
3. On a micro scale, electromagnetic energy harvesting can be used to power small electronic devices such as sensors, wearables, and IoT devices.
4. On a macro scale, electromagnetic energy harvesting can be used to generate electricity on a larger scale, such as in power plants or for powering large machinery.
5. Non-linear techniques can be used to improve the efficiency of electromagnetic energy harvesting by optimizing the conversion of electromagnetic energy into electrical energy.
6. These techniques can be applied to both micro and macro scale implementations of electromagnetic energy harvesting.
7. Examples of non-linear techniques include the use of non-linear resonant circuits and the use of non-linear materials in the design of electromagnetic energy harvesters.
8. The use of these techniques can result in improved energy conversion efficiency and increased power output from electromagnetic energy harvesting systems.
