### Types of Energy Harvesting Systems

Energy harvesting, also known as power harvesting or energy scavenging, is the process of capturing and storing small amounts of energy from external sources such as solar, thermal, wind, and kinetic energy. This energy can then be used to power electronic devices such as sensors, wireless communication devices, and wearable technology.

There are several types of energy harvesting systems, including:

1. **Solar Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from the sun using photovoltaic cells. The captured energy is then converted into electrical energy and stored in a battery or capacitor for later use.

2. **Thermal Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from temperature differences between two materials. The captured energy is then converted into electrical energy and stored in a battery or capacitor for later use.

3. **Wind Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from the movement of air using a small wind turbine. The captured energy is then converted into electrical energy and stored in a battery or capacitor for later use.

4. **Kinetic Energy Harvesting:** This type of energy harvesting system captures energy from the movement of an object, such as a person walking or a vehicle moving. The captured energy is then converted into electrical energy and stored in a battery or capacitor for later use.

These are some of the common types of energy harvesting systems used in the field of IoT devices. Each type of system has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of system depends on the specific application and environment in which it will be used.
