### Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

1. Energy harvesting is the process of capturing and storing energy from external sources such as light, heat, and motion. This energy can then be used to power wireless sensors and other devices.
2. Examples of energy harvesting in wireless sensors include totalizers used by the oil and gas field and flow meters used by the water industry .
3. Vibration energy can also be harvested when electrical motors interact with the process, leading to energy harvesting .
4. The power consumption of many devices used as IoT sensors is between 0.1 μW and 1 W, making it achievable with energy harvesting technologies .
5. Energy harvesting is already being used in devices such as watches that are powered by the kinetic energy of a person’s movement .
6. Energy harvesting can be achieved through various methods such as vibration, hybrid vibration energy converters, electromagnetic transducers, piezoelectric transducers, magneto-electric transducers, and non-linear broadband converters .
7. Energy harvesting can be used to power wireless sensors for various applications such as damage tracking nodes for helicopters and solar-powered strain and seismic sensor networks for bridges .
