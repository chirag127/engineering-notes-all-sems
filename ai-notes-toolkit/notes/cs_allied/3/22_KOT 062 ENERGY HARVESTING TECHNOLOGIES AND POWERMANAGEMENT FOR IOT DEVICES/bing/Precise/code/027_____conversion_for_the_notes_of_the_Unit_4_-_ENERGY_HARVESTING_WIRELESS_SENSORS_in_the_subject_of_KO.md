### Unit 4 - ENERGY HARVESTING WIRELESS SENSORS

1. Energy harvesting wireless sensors are devices that can convert ambient energy into electrical energy to power themselves.
2. These sensors can harvest energy from various sources such as light, heat, and motion.
3. The use of energy harvesting wireless sensors can reduce the need for battery replacement and maintenance.
4. These sensors can be used in various applications such as environmental monitoring, structural health monitoring, and wearable devices.
5. Energy harvesting technologies used in wireless sensors include photovoltaic, thermoelectric, piezoelectric, and electromagnetic.
6. Power management is an important aspect of energy harvesting wireless sensors to ensure efficient use of the harvested energy.
7. Various techniques such as maximum power point tracking and energy buffering can be used to improve the power management of these sensors.
8. The use of energy harvesting wireless sensors can contribute to the development of sustainable and self-powered IoT devices.
