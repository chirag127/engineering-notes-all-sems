## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

Energy harvesting systems are devices that convert ambient energy into usable electrical energy. These systems have a wide range of applications, including:

1. **Wireless Sensor Networks:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power wireless sensor networks, which are used for monitoring and data collection in various industries, such as agriculture, healthcare, and environmental monitoring.

2. **Consumer Electronics:** Energy harvesting systems can be used to power consumer electronics, such as mobile phones, laptops, and wearable devices. This can reduce the need for batteries and charging, making these devices more convenient to use.

3. **Transportation:** Energy harvesting systems can be used in transportation, such as in vehicles and airplanes, to power sensors and other electronic systems. This can improve the efficiency and safety of these modes of transportation.

4. **Building Automation:** Energy harvesting systems can be used in building automation, such as in lighting, heating, and cooling systems, to reduce energy consumption and improve the efficiency of these systems.

5. **Industrial Automation:** Energy harvesting systems can be used in industrial automation, such as in manufacturing and production processes, to power sensors and other electronic systems. This can improve the efficiency and safety of these processes.

These are just a few examples of the many applications of energy harvesting systems. These systems have the potential to revolutionize the way we use and consume energy, making our world more sustainable and efficient.
