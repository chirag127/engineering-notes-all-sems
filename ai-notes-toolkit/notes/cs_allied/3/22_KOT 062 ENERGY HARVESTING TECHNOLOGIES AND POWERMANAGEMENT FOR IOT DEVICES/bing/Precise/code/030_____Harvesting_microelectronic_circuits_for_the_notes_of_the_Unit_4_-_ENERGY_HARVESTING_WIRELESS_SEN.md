### Harvesting Microelectronic Circuits

- Harvesting microelectronic circuits are used to transfer and condition harvested energy and power with emphasis on the imposed requirements of microscale dimensions to maximize operational life by reducing all relevant power losses.
- The design of microelectronic circuits for energy harvesting involves broadband energy conversion, new methods, and technologies for energy conversion.
- Power management circuits and the implementation of voltage regulators are also important aspects of the design of microelectronic circuits for energy harvesting.
- A book titled "Microelectronic Circuit Design for Energy Harvesting Systems" provides a single-source reference to energy harvesting and its applications, serving as a practical guide to microelectronics design for energy harvesting, with application to mobile power supplies.
