### Bio-MEMS based applications for the notes of the Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

Bio-MEMS (Biological Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems) are microsystems that integrate biological components with microelectronics and microfabrication technologies. These systems have a wide range of applications in the field of energy harvesting, particularly in the development of self-powered devices for the Internet of Things (IoT).

Some of the Bio-MEMS based applications for energy harvesting include:

1. **Implantable medical devices:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop implantable medical devices that harvest energy from the body, such as from body heat or movement, to power themselves. This eliminates the need for batteries, reducing the size and weight of the device and increasing its lifespan.

2. **Environmental monitoring:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop self-powered sensors for environmental monitoring. These sensors can harvest energy from sources such as solar, wind, or vibrations to power themselves, allowing them to be deployed in remote or hard-to-reach locations.

3. **Wearable devices:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop wearable devices that harvest energy from the user's body movements or body heat to power themselves. This can include devices such as fitness trackers, smartwatches, or health monitors.

4. **Smart buildings:** Bio-MEMS can be used to develop self-powered sensors and control systems for smart buildings. These systems can harvest energy from sources such as solar, wind, or vibrations to power themselves, allowing for the development of more energy-efficient buildings.

Overall, Bio-MEMS based applications for energy harvesting have the potential to greatly improve the efficiency and sustainability of IoT devices, making them an important area of research and development.
