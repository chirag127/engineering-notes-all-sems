### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

1. Energy harvesting, also known as power harvesting or energy scavenging, is the process of capturing and storing small amounts of energy from external sources such as solar, thermal, wind, and kinetic energy.
2. This energy is then used to power small electronic devices such as sensors, microcontrollers, and wireless communication devices.
3. Energy harvesting systems are commonly used in remote or inaccessible locations where it is difficult or impractical to replace batteries or run power cables.
4. These systems are also used in applications where long-term, maintenance-free operation is required, such as in wireless sensor networks, wearable devices, and Internet of Things (IoT) devices.
5. Energy harvesting technologies include photovoltaic, thermoelectric, piezoelectric, and electromagnetic, among others.
6. Power management is an important aspect of energy harvesting systems, as it is necessary to efficiently store and regulate the harvested energy to ensure reliable operation of the device.
7. In this unit, we will explore the various energy harvesting technologies and power management techniques used in IoT devices.
