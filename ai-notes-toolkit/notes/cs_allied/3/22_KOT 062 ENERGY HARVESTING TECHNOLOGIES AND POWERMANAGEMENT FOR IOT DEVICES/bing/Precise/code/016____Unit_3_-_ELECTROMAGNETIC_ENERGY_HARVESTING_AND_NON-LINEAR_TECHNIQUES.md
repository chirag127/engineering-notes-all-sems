## Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

1. Electromagnetic energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient electromagnetic energy into usable electrical energy.
2. This energy can come from sources such as radio waves, solar radiation, and even the Earth's magnetic field.
3. Non-linear techniques are used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting systems.
4. These techniques can include the use of non-linear materials, non-linear circuits, and non-linear control algorithms.
5. Non-linear materials can exhibit properties such as hysteresis, which can be used to improve the efficiency of energy harvesting systems.
6. Non-linear circuits can include diodes, which can be used to rectify AC signals into DC signals, improving the efficiency of energy harvesting systems.
7. Non-linear control algorithms can be used to optimize the performance of energy harvesting systems, by adjusting parameters such as the load resistance and the duty cycle of the energy harvesting circuit.
8. Electromagnetic energy harvesting and non-linear techniques have many applications, including powering wireless sensors, charging batteries, and providing power to remote locations.
