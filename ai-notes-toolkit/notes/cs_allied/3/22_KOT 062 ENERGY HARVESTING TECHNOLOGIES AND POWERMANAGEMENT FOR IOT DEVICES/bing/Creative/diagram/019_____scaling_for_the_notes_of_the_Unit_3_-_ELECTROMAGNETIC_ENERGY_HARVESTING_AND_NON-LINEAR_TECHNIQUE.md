### Scaling in Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting

- Scaling is the study of how the performance of an energy harvesting device changes with its size, frequency, and input acceleration .
- Scaling is important for designing and optimizing energy harvesters for different applications and environments.
- Electromagnetic energy harvesting (EMEH) is the conversion of mechanical vibrations into electrical energy using electromagnetic induction.
- EMEH devices typically consist of a spring-mass system with a coil and a magnet, or a cantilever beam with a coil and a magnet.
- The output power of an EMEH device depends on the electromagnetic damping, which is proportional to the square of the dimension of the device.
- The output power also depends on the resonant frequency and the input acceleration of the device.
- The power density of an EMEH device is a metric that measures the output power per unit volume or mass of the device.
- The power density can be used to compare the performance of different EMEH devices and to evaluate the trade-offs between size, frequency, and acceleration.
- The power density of an EMEH device decreases with increasing size, decreases with decreasing frequency, and increases with increasing acceleration.
- The power density of an EMEH device can be improved by optimizing the design parameters, such as the coil resistance, the coil area, the magnet strength, the spring stiffness, and the mass ratio.
- The power density of an EMEH device can also be improved by using nonlinear techniques, such as bistability, frequency tuning, and synchronization.
