### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

- Energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient energy sources, such as solar, wind, thermal, kinetic, or electromagnetic, into electrical energy that can be used to power devices or systems.
- Energy harvesting systems consist of three main components: an energy harvester, a power management unit, and a load.
- An energy harvester is a device that converts ambient energy into electrical energy. Examples of energy harvesters are photovoltaic cells, piezoelectric generators, thermoelectric generators, electromagnetic generators, etc.
- A power management unit is a circuit that regulates, stores, and distributes the electrical energy generated by the energy harvester. It may include components such as voltage converters, batteries, capacitors, switches, etc.
- A load is a device or system that consumes the electrical energy provided by the power management unit. Examples of loads are sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, wireless transceivers, etc.
- Energy harvesting systems are useful for powering low-power devices or systems that operate in remote, inaccessible, or harsh environments, where replacing or recharging batteries is impractical or impossible.
- Energy harvesting systems are also beneficial for reducing the environmental impact and cost of batteries, as well as enhancing the reliability and lifetime of devices or systems.
- Energy harvesting systems are especially relevant for the Internet of Things (IoT) devices, which are networked devices that collect and exchange data with other devices or systems. IoT devices often have limited power sources and require frequent communication, which makes them ideal candidates for energy harvesting.
- Energy harvesting technologies and power management for IoT devices are the main topics of this subject, which aims to provide the students with the knowledge and skills to design, analyze, and implement energy harvesting systems for various IoT applications.
