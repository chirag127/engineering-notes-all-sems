### Strategies for enhancing the performance of energy harvesters

- Energy harvesters are devices that can convert ambient energy sources, such as vibration, wind, light, heat, etc., into electrical energy for powering low-power electronic devices, such as sensors, wireless communication modules, etc.
- Energy harvesting is a promising technology for the Internet of Things (IoT) devices, as it can provide a sustainable and maintenance-free power supply, reducing the dependence on batteries and wires.
- Piezoelectric energy harvesting (PEH) is one of the most widely studied techniques for harvesting vibration energy, as piezoelectric materials can generate electric charges when subjected to mechanical stress or strain.
- However, PEH has some limitations, such as low power output, narrow frequency bandwidth, and sensitivity to the resonant frequency of the ambient vibrations, which are often random and broadband.
- Therefore, various strategies have been proposed to enhance the performance of PEH, such as:

  - Adopting new materials with higher piezoelectric coefficients, such as lead-free ceramics, polymers, nanocomposites, etc. 
  - Optimizing the geometry and configuration of the harvesting devices, such as cantilevers, beams, plates, etc., to increase the strain and stress distribution. 
  - Introducing nonlinearities, such as bi-stability, snap-through, magnetic force, etc., to induce large-amplitude oscillations and widen the frequency bandwidth.  
  - Applying power-amplifying strategies, such as elastic foundations, synchronized switching, impedance matching, etc., to maximize the generated power and improve the efficiency. 
  - Hybridizing PEH with other energy harvesting techniques, such as electromagnetic, electrostatic, triboelectric, etc., to harvest multiple energy sources and enhance the output performance.  
  - Exploiting asymmetric potentials, such as Duffing, Leng, etc., to induce chaotic motions and increase the sensitivity to ambient vibrations. 

- These strategies can help to improve the performance of PEH and make it more suitable for various applications in the IoT devices.
