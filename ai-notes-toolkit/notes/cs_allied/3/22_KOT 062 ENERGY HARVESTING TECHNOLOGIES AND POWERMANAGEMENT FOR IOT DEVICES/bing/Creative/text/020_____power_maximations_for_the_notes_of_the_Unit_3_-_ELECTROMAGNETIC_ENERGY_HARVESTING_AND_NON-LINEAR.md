### Power Maximizations for the Notes of the Unit 3 - Electromagnetic Energy Harvesting and Non-Linear Techniques

- Electromagnetic energy harvesting (EMEH) is a technique that converts ambient mechanical vibrations into electrical energy using electromagnetic induction .
- EMEH devices typically consist of a coil and a magnet, which can be arranged in different configurations depending on the design and application .
- EMEH devices can be classified into linear and nonlinear systems, depending on the behavior of the magnet-coil relative motion and the output voltage.
- Linear EMEH devices have a constant output voltage and a narrow bandwidth of operation, which limits their performance and adaptability to varying environmental conditions.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices have a variable output voltage and a wider bandwidth of operation, which enhances their performance and adaptability to varying environmental conditions.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices can be further classified into different types, such as bistable, monostable, Duffing, magnetic levitation, etc., depending on the nonlinear mechanism involved  .
- Nonlinear EMEH devices require appropriate interface circuits and power management circuits to optimize the power transfer and storage.
- Power maximization techniques for nonlinear EMEH devices include impedance matching, frequency tuning, voltage rectification, DC-DC conversion, etc .
- Power maximization techniques for nonlinear EMEH devices aim to achieve high power density, high efficiency, and wide bandwidth of operation.
