### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

- Energy harvesting is the process of capturing and converting ambient energy sources, such as solar, wind, thermal, kinetic, or electromagnetic, into electrical energy that can be used to power devices or systems.
- Energy harvesting systems consist of three main components: an energy harvester, a power management circuit, and a storage element or a load.
- Energy harvesting systems can provide a sustainable and autonomous power supply for various applications, such as wireless sensor networks, wearable devices, biomedical implants, and internet of things (IoT) devices.
- Energy harvesting systems can offer several advantages over conventional power sources, such as batteries, such as:
  - Reducing the maintenance cost and environmental impact of replacing batteries.
  - Enabling the deployment of devices in remote, inaccessible, or hazardous locations.
  - Enhancing the reliability and lifetime of devices.
  - Improving the performance and functionality of devices by enabling new features or modes of operation.
- Energy harvesting systems also face several challenges and limitations, such as:
  - The variability and unpredictability of ambient energy sources.
  - The low power density and efficiency of energy harvesters.
  - The trade-off between the size, weight, and cost of energy harvesting systems and their output power and energy.
  - The compatibility and integration of energy harvesting systems with different devices and systems.
  - The design and optimization of energy harvesting systems for specific applications and environments.
