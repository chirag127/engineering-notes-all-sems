### Micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials

- Micro fabricated coils are miniature coils that are made using micro fabrication techniques such as lithography, electroplating, etching, etc. 
- Micro fabricated coils can be used for various applications such as magnetooptical data storage, electromagnetic energy harvesting, magnetic neurostimulation, etc.  
- Micro fabricated coils can be classified into two types: planar and three-dimensional.
- Planar micro coils have a flat structure with a transparent center that allows a laser beam to pass through. They consist of upper and lower magnetic films, a metal wire coil layer in the middle, and an insulating layer to isolate the magnetic core material and the wire.
- Three-dimensional micro coils have a cylindrical or helical structure that can generate a stronger magnetic field than planar micro coils. They can be fabricated by winding a metal wire around a cylindrical or helical mold and then removing the mold.
- The performance of micro fabricated coils depends on several factors such as the coil geometry, the coil resistance, the magnetic material, the excitation frequency, the excitation amplitude, the coil spacing, the coil thickness, etc.  
- The advantages of micro fabricated coils over conventional wound coils are: higher integration, higher precision, higher reliability, lower cost, and lower power consumption. 
- The challenges of micro fabricated coils are: the scaling of electromagnetic energy harvesters, the design limitations imposed by micro fabrication processes, the low coil resistance for high power efficiency, the low leak current from the probe into the experimental set-up, and the adaptive MEMS process technology.
