### Harvesters

- Harvesters are devices that convert ambient energy sources, such as mechanical vibrations, into electrical energy.
- Piezoelectric harvesters are a type of harvester that use piezoelectric materials, which generate electric charge when subjected to mechanical stress or strain.
- Piezoelectric harvesters can be used to power low-power devices, such as wireless sensors, microelectromechanical systems (MEMS), or implantable medical devices, by scavenging energy from their environment.
- Piezoelectric harvesters can have various forms and configurations, such as cantilevers, beams, plates, shells, or membranes, depending on the application and the excitation source.

### Electromechanical Modeling

- Electromechanical modeling is the process of developing mathematical models that describe the coupled mechanical and electrical behavior of piezoelectric harvesters.
- Electromechanical modeling can help to understand, analyze, design, and optimize piezoelectric harvesters for different applications and operating conditions.
- Electromechanical modeling can be performed at different levels of complexity and accuracy, such as lumped-parameter models, distributed-parameter models, finite element models, or analytical models.
- Electromechanical modeling can also account for various forms of excitation, such as base excitation, fluid flow, acoustic waves, or moving loads, and various types of piezoelectric materials, such as ceramics, polymers, or composites.
