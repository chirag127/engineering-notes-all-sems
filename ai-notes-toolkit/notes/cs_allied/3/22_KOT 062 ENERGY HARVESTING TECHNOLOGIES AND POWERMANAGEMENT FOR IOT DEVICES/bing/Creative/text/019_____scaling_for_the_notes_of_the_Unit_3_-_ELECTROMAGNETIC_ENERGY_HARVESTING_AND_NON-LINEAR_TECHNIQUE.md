### Scaling for the notes of the Unit 3 - ELECTROMAGNETIC ENERGY HARVESTING AND NON-LINEAR TECHNIQUES

- Electromagnetic energy harvesting (EMEH) is a technique to convert ambient mechanical vibrations into electrical energy using electromagnetic induction.
- EMEH devices typically consist of a coil and a magnet, which can be arranged in different configurations depending on the design and application.
- EMEH devices can be classified into linear and nonlinear types, depending on the behavior of the magnet-coil system under external excitation.
- Linear EMEH devices have a constant magnetic flux linkage between the coil and the magnet, and their output voltage is proportional to the velocity of the magnet.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices have a variable magnetic flux linkage between the coil and the magnet, and their output voltage depends on the displacement, velocity and acceleration of the magnet.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices can achieve higher output power and wider bandwidth than linear EMEH devices, but they also require more complex power management circuits and control strategies.
- Some examples of nonlinear EMEH devices are:
  - Magnetic levitation-based EMEH devices, which use magnetic repulsion or attraction to suspend the magnet above or below the coil, creating a nonlinear restoring force .
  - Bistable EMEH devices, which use two fixed magnets at the ends of the tube to create a potential well for the moving magnet, resulting in snap-through behavior .
  - Nonlinear interface circuit EMEH devices, which use a nonlinear electrical component, such as a diode, to modulate the current flow in the coil, creating a nonlinear electromechanical coupling.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices can be modeled using different approaches, such as analytical, numerical, experimental or hybrid methods, depending on the complexity and accuracy required.
- Nonlinear EMEH devices can be used for self-powered wireless sensor nodes, wearable electronics, biomedical implants, structural health monitoring, and other applications that require low-power and long-lasting energy sources.
