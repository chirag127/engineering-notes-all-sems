## Unit 5 - SELECTED APPLICATIONS OF ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS

- Energy harvesting systems are devices that collect ambient energy from the environment and convert it into electrical energy for powering wireless devices, sensors, and autonomous systems.
- Energy harvesting systems can extend the battery life, reduce the maintenance cost, and improve the reliability of remote and inaccessible systems that rely on batteries or wires for power supply.
- Energy harvesting systems can find applications in various sectors such as industrial, consumer electronics, building and home automation, wireless sensor networks, security systems, and others.
- Some of the selected applications of energy harvesting systems are:

  - **Industrial applications**: Energy harvesting systems can be used for remote monitoring, predictive maintenance, and energy-efficient solutions in industrial settings. For example, energy harvesting systems can power wireless sensors that measure temperature, pressure, vibration, humidity, and other parameters in industrial machines and equipment. These sensors can transmit the data to a central server for analysis and optimization of the industrial processes. Energy harvesting systems can also power actuators that control valves, switches, and other devices in industrial systems. Energy harvesting systems can reduce the need for battery replacement and wiring in harsh and hazardous industrial environments.
  - **Consumer electronics applications**: Energy harvesting systems can be used for powering wearable devices, smart watches, fitness trackers, and other low-power consumer electronics. For example, energy harvesting systems can harvest kinetic energy from the user's motion, thermal energy from the user's body heat, or solar energy from the ambient light to power these devices. Energy harvesting systems can enhance the user experience, convenience, and functionality of these devices by eliminating the need for frequent charging or battery replacement.
  - **Building and home automation applications**: Energy harvesting systems can be used for powering smart devices, sensors, and actuators that enable building and home automation. For example, energy harvesting systems can power wireless switches, dimmers, thermostats, smoke detectors, and other devices that control lighting, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, security, and entertainment systems in buildings and homes. Energy harvesting systems can improve the energy efficiency, comfort, safety, and security of buildings and homes by enabling wireless and battery-free operation of these devices.
  - **Wireless sensor network applications**: Energy harvesting systems can be used for powering wireless sensor nodes that form wireless sensor networks for various purposes. For example, energy harvesting systems can power wireless sensor nodes that monitor environmental parameters, such as temperature, humidity, air quality, soil moisture, and rainfall, for applications such as agriculture, forestry, wildlife, and weather forecasting. Energy harvesting systems can also power wireless sensor nodes that monitor structural parameters, such as strain, stress, displacement, and vibration, for applications such as civil engineering, transportation, and infrastructure. Energy harvesting systems can enable long-term, autonomous, and self-sustaining operation of wireless sensor networks by providing continuous and reliable power supply to the sensor nodes.
  - **Security system applications**: Energy harvesting systems can be used for powering security devices, such as cameras, alarms, locks, and sensors, that protect buildings, homes, and other assets from unauthorized access, theft, vandalism, and fire. For example, energy harvesting systems can power wireless cameras that capture and transmit images or videos of intruders or suspicious activities to a central server or a mobile device. Energy harvesting systems can also power wireless alarms that sound or send alerts when a security breach is detected. Energy harvesting systems can enhance the security and reliability of these devices by eliminating the need for wires or batteries that can be cut or drained by intruders.
