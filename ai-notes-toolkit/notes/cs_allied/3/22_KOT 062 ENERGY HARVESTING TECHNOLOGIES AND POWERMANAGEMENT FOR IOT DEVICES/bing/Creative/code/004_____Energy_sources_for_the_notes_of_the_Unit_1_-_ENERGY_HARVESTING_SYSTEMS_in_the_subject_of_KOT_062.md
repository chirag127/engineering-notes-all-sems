### Energy sources for the notes of the Unit 1 - ENERGY HARVESTING SYSTEMS in the subject of KOT 062 ENERGY HARVESTING TECHNOLOGIES AND POWERMANAGEMENT FOR IOT DEVICES KCS

- Energy harvesting is the process by which energy is derived from external sources (e.g., solar power, thermal energy, wind energy, salinity gradients, and kinetic energy, also known as ambient energy), and then stored for use by small, wireless autonomous devices, like sensors, microcontrollers, and actuators.
- Energy sources for energy harvesting include:
  - **Motion/mechanical energy** — Motion or mechanical energy is the energy associated with the movement or deformation of objects. It can be harvested from sources such as human or animal motion, vibrations, fluid flow, or piezoelectric materials. Motion or mechanical energy can be converted into electrical energy using methods such as electromagnetic induction, electrostatic induction, or piezoelectric effect .
  - **Light energy** — Light energy is the energy carried by electromagnetic radiation, such as visible light, infrared, or ultraviolet. Light energy can be harvested from sources such as natural or artificial light. Light energy can be converted into electrical energy using photovoltaic (PV) cells, which are made of materials that convert light into an electrical current .
  - **Thermal energy** — Thermal energy is the energy associated with the temperature difference or heat flow between two objects or environments. Thermal energy can be harvested from sources such as waste heat, body heat, or environmental temperature variations. Thermal energy can be converted into electrical energy using methods such as thermoelectric effect, thermionic emission, or pyroelectric effect .
  - **RF energy** — RF energy is the energy carried by radio frequency (RF) waves, such as microwaves, radio, or cellular signals. RF energy can be harvested from sources such as wireless communication networks, broadcast stations, or RFID tags. RF energy can be converted into electrical energy using RF energy harvesters, which are devices that transform RF energy into electrical energy .
  - **Chemical energy** — Chemical energy is the energy stored in the bonds of molecules, such as fuels, batteries, or biological cells. Chemical energy can be harvested from sources such as glucose, ethanol, or hydrogen. Chemical energy can be converted into electrical energy using methods such as fuel cells, biofuel cells, or enzymatic fuel cells .
