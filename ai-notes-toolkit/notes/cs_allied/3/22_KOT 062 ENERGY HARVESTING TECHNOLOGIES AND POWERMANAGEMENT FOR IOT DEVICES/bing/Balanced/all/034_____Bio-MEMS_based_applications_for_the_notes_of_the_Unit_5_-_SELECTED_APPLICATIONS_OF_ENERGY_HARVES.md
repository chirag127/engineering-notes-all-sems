# Bio-MEMS based applications

- Bio-MEMS stands for biomedical micro-electro-mechanical systems, which are devices that integrate biological, chemical, or physical components with microfabrication technologies.
- Bio-MEMS may or may not include any electrical or mechanical functions.
- Bio-MEMS have various applications in clinical medicine and environmental, biological, and chemical analysis.
- Some of the main application areas of Bio-MEMS are  :
  - Biomedical transducers: These are devices that convert one form of energy into another, such as pressure, temperature, or chemical signals into electrical signals. Examples are biosensors, microelectrodes, and microarrays.
  - Microfluidics: These are devices that manipulate small volumes of fluids, such as blood, urine, or saliva, using channels, valves, pumps, mixers, and separators. Examples are lab-on-a-chip, micro-total analysis systems, and organ-on-a-chip.
  - Medical implants: These are devices that are inserted into the body to monitor, stimulate, or replace the function of an organ or tissue. Examples are cochlear implants, pacemakers, and drug delivery systems.
  - Microsurgical tools: These are devices that enable minimally invasive surgery, such as cutting, suturing, or cauterizing tissues. Examples are microscalpels, microforceps, and microcatheters.
  - Tissue engineering: These are devices that support the growth and differentiation of cells and tissues, such as scaffolds, bioreactors, and microenvironments. Examples are artificial skin, bone, and cartilage.
