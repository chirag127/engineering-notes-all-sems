# Micro fabricated coils and magnetic materials

- Micro fabricated coils are miniature coils that are fabricated using microelectromechanical systems (MEMS) technology. They can be used for various applications such as magnetooptical data storage, electromagnetic energy harvesting, magnetic neurostimulation, etc.   
- Micro fabricated coils can be classified into two types: planar and three-dimensional. Planar coils are fabricated on a single substrate and have a flat shape. Three-dimensional coils are fabricated by stacking multiple layers of substrates and have a cylindrical or toroidal shape. 
- The performance of micro fabricated coils depends on several factors such as the number of turns, the coil geometry, the wire width and thickness, the coil spacing, the magnetic core material, the insulation layer, etc. 
- The advantages of micro fabricated coils over conventional wire-wound coils are: higher integration density, lower parasitic capacitance and inductance, better control of coil parameters, lower cost and mass production.  
- The challenges of micro fabricated coils are: lower power efficiency, higher fabrication complexity, lower magnetic field strength, higher heat dissipation, and design limitations due to scaling effects.   
- Magnetic materials are materials that can be magnetized by an external magnetic field. They can be classified into three types: ferromagnetic, paramagnetic, and diamagnetic. Ferromagnetic materials have a permanent magnetization and can enhance the magnetic field of a coil. Paramagnetic materials have a temporary magnetization and can align with the magnetic field of a coil. Diamagnetic materials have a negative magnetization and can oppose the magnetic field of a coil. 
- The properties of magnetic materials are characterized by several parameters such as the permeability, the coercivity, the remanence, the saturation magnetization, the Curie temperature, etc. 
- The choice of magnetic materials for micro fabricated coils depends on the application and the design requirements. Some of the common magnetic materials used for micro fabricated coils are: iron, nickel, cobalt, permalloy, ferrite, neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB), etc.
