### Metadata for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Metadata is data that describes other data. It provides information about the structure, content, quality, and context of the data.
- Metadata can be classified into different types, such as:
  - **Structural metadata:** It describes how the data is organized, such as the schema, format, or model of the data.
  - **Descriptive metadata:** It describes what the data is about, such as the title, author, subject, or keywords of the data.
  - **Administrative metadata:** It describes how the data is managed, such as the creation date, owner, access rights, or provenance of the data.
  - **Technical metadata:** It describes how the data is stored, processed, or accessed, such as the file type, size, encoding, or location of the data.
  - **Business metadata:** It describes how the data is used, such as the business rules, definitions, or metrics of the data.
- Metadata is important for the implementation of BI because it can help to:
  - **Integrate data:** Metadata can enable the mapping, transformation, and reconciliation of data from different sources, systems, or formats.
  - **Analyze data:** Metadata can enable the understanding, interpretation, and validation of data, as well as the identification of patterns, trends, or anomalies in the data.
  - **Share data:** Metadata can enable the discovery, retrieval, and exchange of data, as well as the communication of the meaning, value, and quality of the data.
  - **Govern data:** Metadata can enable the documentation, standardization, and compliance of data, as well as the monitoring, auditing, and security of the data.
