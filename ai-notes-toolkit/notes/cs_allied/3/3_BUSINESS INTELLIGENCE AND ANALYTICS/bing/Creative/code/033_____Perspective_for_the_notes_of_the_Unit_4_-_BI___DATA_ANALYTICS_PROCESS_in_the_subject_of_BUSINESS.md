### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Perspective in data analytics refers to the process of analyzing data from various viewpoints or perspectives to gain deeper insights and make informed decisions.
- There are different types of perspectives in data analytics, such as descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive .
- Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using historical data. It answers the question of "what".
- Diagnostic analytics explains why something has happened in the past using statistical techniques. It answers the question of "why".
- Predictive analytics forecasts what is likely to happen in the future using machine learning and data mining. It answers the question of "what if".
- Prescriptive analytics recommends what actions should be taken in the future to optimize outcomes using optimization and simulation. It answers the question of "how".
- The data analytics process consists of the following steps :
  - Define the business problem and the objectives of the analysis.
  - Collect and prepare the relevant data from various sources.
  - Explore and analyze the data using appropriate methods and tools.
  - Interpret and communicate the results and insights to stakeholders.
  - Implement and monitor the actions and outcomes based on the analysis.
- The data analytics process is iterative and dynamic, meaning that it can be repeated and refined as new data and feedback are available.
- The data analytics process requires collaboration and alignment among different roles and functions, such as business analysts, data scientists, data engineers, data stewards, and decision makers.
