### Difference between OLAP and OLTP

- OLAP stands for Online Analytical Processing, while OLTP stands for Online Transaction Processing.
- OLAP is optimized for conducting complex data analysis for smarter decision-making, while OLTP is ideal for making simple updates, insertions and deletions in databases.
- OLAP has a multi-dimensional schema, so it can support complex queries of multiple data facts and dimensions, while OLTP has a relational schema, so it can support simple queries of single data entities and attributes.
- OLAP processes data more slowly as compared to OLTP, as the queries often involve large numbers of records and calculations, while OLTP processes data more quickly, as the queries often involve small numbers of records and transactions.
- OLAP is used for data warehousing, business intelligence, and data mining, while OLTP is used for operational databases, e-commerce, and banking.
- OLAP is read-intensive, meaning it mostly retrieves data from the database, while OLTP is write-intensive, meaning it mostly modifies data in the database.
- OLAP is periodic, meaning it updates data at regular intervals, while OLTP is real-time, meaning it updates data immediately.
