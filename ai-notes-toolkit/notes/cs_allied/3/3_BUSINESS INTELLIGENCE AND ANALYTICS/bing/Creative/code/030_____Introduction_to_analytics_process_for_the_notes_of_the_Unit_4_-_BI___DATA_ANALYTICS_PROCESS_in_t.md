### Introduction to analytics process

- Analytics is the process of discovering, interpreting, and communicating significant patterns in data .
- Analytics helps us see insights and meaningful data that we might not otherwise detect.
- Analytics can be applied to different domains, such as website analytics, business analytics, social media analytics, etc.
- Analytics can be classified into four types: descriptive, predictive, prescriptive, and diagnostic.
  - Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using data visualization, dashboards, reports, etc.
  - Predictive analytics forecasts what might happen in the future using statistical models, machine learning, data mining, etc.
  - Prescriptive analytics suggests what actions should be taken to optimize a desired outcome using optimization, simulation, decision analysis, etc.
  - Diagnostic analytics explains why something happened in the past using root cause analysis, anomaly detection, drill-down, etc.
- Analytics process involves the following steps :
  - Define the problem or objective: Identify the business question or goal that needs to be addressed by analytics.
  - Collect and prepare the data: Gather the relevant data from various sources and clean, transform, and integrate it for analysis.
  - Analyze the data: Apply the appropriate analytical techniques and tools to explore, model, and interpret the data.
  - Communicate and act on the results: Present the findings and recommendations to the stakeholders and implement the actions to achieve the desired outcome.
