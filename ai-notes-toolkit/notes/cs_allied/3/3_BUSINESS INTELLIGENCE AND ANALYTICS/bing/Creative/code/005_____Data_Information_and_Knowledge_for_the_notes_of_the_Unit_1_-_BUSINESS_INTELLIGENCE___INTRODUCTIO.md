### Data Information and Knowledge

- Data is a collection of raw facts, such as numbers, words, images, sounds, or videos, that can be processed by a computer  .
- Information is data that has been processed, organized, structured, or presented in a meaningful way to provide context and meaning  .
- Knowledge is information that has been applied, analyzed, synthesized, or evaluated to produce insights, understanding, or wisdom  .
- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into information, and information into knowledge, to support data-driven decision making in organizations  .
- BI combines various tools and techniques, such as data analytics, data mining, data visualization, data tools and infrastructure, and best practices, to help organizations gain a comprehensive view of their data and use it to generate solutions and insights .
- BI can help organizations improve their performance, efficiency, productivity, profitability, customer satisfaction, competitive advantage, and strategic planning .
- BI can also help organizations identify and solve problems, discover and forecast trends, optimize and automate processes, and innovate and create new products or services .
