### Effective and Timely Decisions

- The main purpose of business intelligence systems is to provide knowledge workers with tools and methodologies that allow them to make effective and timely decisions  .
- Effective decisions are those that are based on reliable and relevant information and knowledge, that are aligned with the organizational goals and strategies, and that can be implemented and evaluated  .
- Timely decisions are those that are made within the appropriate time frame, considering the urgency and complexity of the situation, the availability and quality of the data, and the potential consequences of the action  .
- Effective and timely decisions can improve the performance, efficiency, and competitiveness of an organization, as well as the satisfaction and engagement of the stakeholders  .
- Business intelligence systems can support effective and timely decisions by:
  - Collecting and integrating data from various sources, both internal and external, and transforming them into meaningful and consistent information  .
  - Applying analytical techniques and methods, such as data mining, data visualization, and machine learning, to discover patterns, trends, and insights from the data  .
  - Presenting and communicating the results of the analysis in a clear and interactive way, using dashboards, reports, and charts, that can be customized and shared  .
  - Enabling collaboration and feedback among the decision makers and the data analysts, as well as the other stakeholders, to ensure the validity and usefulness of the information and the alignment of the decisions with the organizational objectives  .
