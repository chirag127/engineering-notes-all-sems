### Real Time Business Intelligent System

- A real time business intelligent system is a system that delivers business intelligence or information about business operations as they occur .
- Business intelligence is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data to support decision making and improve business performance.
- A real time business intelligent system aims to provide near to zero latency and access to information whenever it is required .
- A real time business intelligent system can use different architectures and techniques to enable real time data analysis, such as:
  - Event-based: This approach uses event driven systems that may use complex event processing, event stream processing, and mashup techniques to analyze events without storing them in a database.
  - Data warehouse: This approach uses a data warehouse that is refreshed frequently or continuously to store and query data.
  - Server-less technology: This approach uses cloud-based services and platforms that can scale and process data on demand without requiring servers.
- A real time business intelligent system can provide various benefits for organizations, such as:
  - Faster and more accurate decision making: By accessing the most relevant and up-to-date data, organizations can make better informed and timely decisions that can improve their performance and competitiveness.
  - Improved customer satisfaction: By responding to customer needs and preferences in real time, organizations can enhance their customer service and loyalty.
  - Increased operational efficiency: By monitoring and optimizing business processes and resources in real time, organizations can reduce costs and waste and increase productivity and quality.
  - Enhanced innovation and agility: By discovering new insights and opportunities from real time data, organizations can adapt and innovate faster and more effectively.
