### Effective and Timely Decisions

- Effective decisions are those that achieve the desired outcomes and goals of an organization or a person.
- Timely decisions are those that are made and implemented within an appropriate time frame, considering the urgency and complexity of the situation.
- Effective and timely decisions are important for business intelligence and analytics, as they enable the organization to:
  - Respond quickly and appropriately to changing market conditions, customer needs, and competitive threats.
  - Optimize the use of resources, such as data, information, knowledge, and human capital.
  - Enhance the performance, efficiency, and quality of business processes and operations.
  - Innovate and create new products, services, and business models.
  - Achieve strategic alignment and competitive advantage.
- To make effective and timely decisions, business intelligence and analytics require:
  - A clear definition of the decision problem and the objectives to be achieved.
  - A systematic and structured approach to collect, analyze, and interpret relevant data and information.
  - A critical and creative thinking process to generate, evaluate, and select the best alternative solutions.
  - A collaborative and communicative environment to involve and inform the relevant stakeholders and decision makers.
  - A feedback and learning mechanism to monitor and measure the outcomes and impacts of the decisions, and to improve the decision making process over time.
