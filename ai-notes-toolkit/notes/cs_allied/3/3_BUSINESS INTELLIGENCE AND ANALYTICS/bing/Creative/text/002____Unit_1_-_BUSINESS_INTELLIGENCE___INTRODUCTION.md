## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and transforming data into meaningful information that can help businesses make better decisions.
- BI can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Reporting: presenting data in a structured and easy-to-understand format, such as tables, charts, dashboards, etc.
  - Analytics: applying statistical and mathematical techniques to data to discover patterns, trends, correlations, outliers, etc.
  - Data mining: extracting hidden and useful knowledge from large and complex data sets, such as associations, classifications, clusters, etc.
  - Data warehousing: integrating data from multiple sources and storing them in a centralized and consistent manner, such as relational databases, multidimensional cubes, etc.
  - Data visualization: using graphical and interactive tools to display data in a visually appealing and intuitive way, such as maps, graphs, charts, etc.
- BI can provide various benefits to businesses, such as:
  - Improving performance: by measuring and monitoring key performance indicators (KPIs), such as sales, revenue, profit, customer satisfaction, etc.
  - Enhancing decision making: by providing timely, accurate, and relevant information to support strategic, tactical, and operational decisions.
  - Increasing competitiveness: by identifying opportunities, threats, strengths, and weaknesses in the market and the industry.
  - Reducing costs: by optimizing processes, resources, and operations, and eliminating waste and inefficiency.
  - Increasing innovation: by discovering new insights, ideas, and solutions from data.
