### Real Time Business Intelligent System

- A real time business intelligent system is a system that delivers business intelligence or information about business operations as they occur .
- Business intelligence is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data to support decision making and improve business performance.
- A real time business intelligent system aims to provide near to zero latency and access to information whenever it is required .
- A real time business intelligent system can use different architectures and techniques to enable real time data processing and analysis, such as:
  - Event-based: This approach uses event driven systems that can analyze data streams without storing them in a database. It can use complex event processing or event stream processing techniques to detect patterns, trends, and anomalies in real time data.
  - Data warehouse: This approach uses a data warehouse that is refreshed frequently or continuously to store and query data. It can use data integration, data quality, and data mining techniques to transform and analyze data.
  - Server-less: This approach uses cloud-based services that can scale and perform data processing and analysis on demand. It can use data lake, data pipeline, and data visualization techniques to store and present data.
- A real time business intelligent system can provide various benefits to organizations, such as:
  - Faster and better decision making: By providing timely and relevant information, a real time business intelligent system can help decision makers to respond to changing situations and opportunities quickly and effectively.
  - Improved customer satisfaction: By providing personalized and customized services, a real time business intelligent system can help to enhance customer loyalty and retention.
  - Increased operational efficiency: By optimizing business processes and resources, a real time business intelligent system can help to reduce costs and increase productivity.
  - Competitive advantage: By gaining insights and intelligence from real time data, a real time business intelligent system can help to create new products, services, and strategies that can differentiate an organization from its competitors.
