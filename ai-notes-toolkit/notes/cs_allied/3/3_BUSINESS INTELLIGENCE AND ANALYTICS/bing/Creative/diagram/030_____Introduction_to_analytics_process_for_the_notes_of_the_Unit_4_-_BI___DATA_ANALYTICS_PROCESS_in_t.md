### Introduction to analytics process

- Analytics is the process of discovering, interpreting, and communicating significant patterns in data .
- Analytics helps us see insights and meaningful data that we might not otherwise detect.
- Analytics can be applied to different domains, such as website analytics, business analytics, or social media analytics.
- Analytics can be classified into four types: descriptive, predictive, prescriptive, and diagnostic.
  - Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using data visualization and reporting tools.
  - Predictive analytics forecasts what might happen in the future using statistical and machine learning techniques.
  - Prescriptive analytics suggests what actions should be taken to optimize a desired outcome using optimization and simulation models.
  - Diagnostic analytics explains why something happened in the past using root cause analysis and anomaly detection methods.
- Analytics process consists of several steps, such as defining the problem, collecting and preparing the data, analyzing the data, interpreting and communicating the results, and implementing the solutions .
  - Defining the problem involves identifying the business objectives, the key questions, and the metrics to measure the performance.
  - Collecting and preparing the data involves acquiring the relevant data from various sources, cleaning and transforming the data, and ensuring the data quality and integrity.
  - Analyzing the data involves applying the appropriate analytical techniques, such as descriptive, predictive, prescriptive, or diagnostic, to the data to generate insights and answers.
  - Interpreting and communicating the results involves presenting the findings and recommendations in a clear and concise manner, using data visualization and storytelling tools, and engaging the stakeholders and decision-makers.
  - Implementing the solutions involves taking actions based on the results, monitoring and evaluating the outcomes, and refining and improving the process.
