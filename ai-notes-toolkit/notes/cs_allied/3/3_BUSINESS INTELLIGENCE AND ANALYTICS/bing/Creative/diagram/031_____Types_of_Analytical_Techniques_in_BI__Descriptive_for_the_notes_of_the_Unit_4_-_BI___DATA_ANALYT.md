### Types of Analytical Techniques in BI –Descriptive

- Descriptive analytics is the process of summarizing and visualizing historical data to provide insights into what has happened in the past.
- Descriptive analytics is the most basic and common type of analytics, and it is often used as a starting point for more advanced types of analytics, such as predictive and prescriptive analytics.
- Descriptive analytics can help answer questions such as: How many customers visited our website last month? What are the most popular products or services we offer? How satisfied are our customers with our service?
- The historical data collection for descriptive analytics is done using two main techniques – data aggregation and data mining.
  - Data aggregation is the process of collecting and organizing data into manageable data sets, such as tables, charts, or graphs.
  - Data mining is the process of analyzing data using various tools and methods, such as summary statistics, pattern tracking, or clustering, to discover hidden patterns, trends, or relationships in the data.
- Some examples of descriptive analytics are:
  - Traffic and engagement reports: These reports show the number of visitors, views, clicks, likes, shares, comments, or other metrics that indicate how users interact with a website, social media platform, or online content.
  - Sales and revenue reports: These reports show the amount of sales, revenue, profit, or other financial indicators that a business generates over a period of time, such as a month, quarter, or year.
  - Customer satisfaction surveys: These surveys measure the level of satisfaction, loyalty, or feedback that customers have with a product, service, or brand, using methods such as ratings, reviews, or net promoter score (NPS).
  - Inventory and supply chain reports: These reports show the amount of inventory, stock, or raw materials that a business has on hand, as well as the flow of goods and services from suppliers to customers, using methods such as barcodes, RFID tags, or GPS tracking.
