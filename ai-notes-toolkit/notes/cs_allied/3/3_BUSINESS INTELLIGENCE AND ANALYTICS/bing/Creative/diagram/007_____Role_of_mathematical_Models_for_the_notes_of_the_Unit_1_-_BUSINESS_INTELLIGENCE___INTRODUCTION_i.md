### Role of mathematical models in business intelligence

- A mathematical model is an abstract representation of a real-world system or problem that uses mathematical language and symbols to describe its behavior and outcomes .
- A business intelligence system provides decision makers with information and knowledge extracted from data, through the application of mathematical models and algorithms .
- The role of mathematical models in business intelligence is to:
  - Simplify and clarify complex situations and phenomena by focusing on the essential aspects and variables .
  - Analyze and understand the relationships and dependencies among the variables and parameters of the system or problem .
  - Predict and optimize the outcomes and performance of the system or problem under different scenarios and conditions  .
  - Support and justify the decision making process by providing quantitative and logical evidence and insights  .
- Some examples of mathematical models used in business intelligence are:
  - Statistical models: These models use descriptive and inferential statistics to summarize, visualize, and test data, and to estimate parameters and relationships .
  - Optimization models: These models use mathematical programming techniques to find the best or optimal solution among a set of feasible alternatives, subject to some constraints and objectives  .
  - Machine learning models: These models use artificial intelligence and data mining methods to learn from data and to make predictions, classifications, or recommendations  .
- The development of a mathematical model involves the following steps :
  - Define the problem and the objectives of the model.
  - Identify the relevant variables and parameters of the system or problem.
  - Formulate the mathematical equations or rules that describe the relationships and dependencies among the variables and parameters.
  - Validate and verify the model by comparing its results with empirical data or known solutions.
  - Analyze and interpret the model results and their implications for the decision making process.
  - Refine and improve the model by incorporating feedback and new information.
