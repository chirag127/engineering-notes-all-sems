### Effective and Timely Decisions

- Effective decisions are those that achieve the desired outcomes and goals of an organization or a person.
- Timely decisions are those that are made and implemented within the appropriate time frame, considering the urgency and importance of the situation.
- Effective and timely decisions are essential for the success and survival of any business in a competitive and dynamic environment.
- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data and information to support effective and timely decisions.
- BI can help decision makers to:
  - Understand the current state and performance of the business and its environment
  - Identify trends, patterns, opportunities, and threats
  - Evaluate alternative courses of action and their potential consequences
  - Monitor and measure the results and impacts of the decisions
  - Learn from the feedback and improve the decision making process
- BI can be applied to various levels and types of decisions, such as:
  - Strategic decisions: long-term, high-level, and complex decisions that affect the overall direction and vision of the business
  - Tactical decisions: medium-term, mid-level, and moderately complex decisions that affect the allocation and utilization of resources and capabilities
  - Operational decisions: short-term, low-level, and routine decisions that affect the execution and coordination of tasks and activities
  - Structured decisions: decisions that follow predefined rules and procedures and can be automated or standardized
  - Unstructured decisions: decisions that require human judgment and creativity and cannot be easily automated or standardized
- BI can be supported by various tools and technologies, such as:
  - Data sources: databases, data warehouses, data marts, data lakes, etc. that store and provide access to the raw data
  - Data integration: processes and techniques that combine and transform data from multiple sources into a consistent and unified format
  - Data quality: processes and techniques that ensure the accuracy, completeness, validity, and reliability of the data
  - Data analysis: processes and techniques that apply statistical, mathematical, and computational methods to discover and extract insights from the data
  - Data visualization: processes and techniques that present the data and analysis results in graphical and interactive forms, such as charts, graphs, dashboards, etc.
  - Data mining: processes and techniques that apply advanced algorithms and models to identify hidden patterns, relationships, and associations in the data
  - Machine learning: processes and techniques that apply artificial intelligence and self-learning algorithms and models to analyze and predict the data
  - Business analytics: processes and techniques that apply data analysis, data mining, and machine learning to solve specific business problems and generate actionable recommendations
  - Business performance management: processes and techniques that apply business analytics, data visualization, and feedback mechanisms to monitor and measure the performance of the business and its decisions
  - Business intelligence systems: systems and applications that integrate and coordinate the various tools and technologies of BI to provide a comprehensive and user-friendly platform for decision support
