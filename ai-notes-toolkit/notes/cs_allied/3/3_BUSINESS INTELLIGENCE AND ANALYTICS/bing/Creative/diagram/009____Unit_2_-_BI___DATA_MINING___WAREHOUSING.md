## Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING

- **Business Intelligence (BI)** is the process of transforming data into insights that help businesses make better decisions and improve their performance .
- **Data Warehousing (DW)** is the process of collecting, integrating, and storing data from multiple sources in a centralized, consistent, and accessible data store .
- **Data Mining (DM)** is the process of discovering patterns, trends, and relationships in large and complex data sets using statistical, mathematical, and machine learning techniques .

- BI, DW, and DM are interrelated and complementary concepts that support data analysis, data visualization, data discovery, and data-driven decision making .
- BI, DW, and DM have various applications and benefits for different domains and industries, such as finance, marketing, healthcare, education, retail, manufacturing, etc.
- BI, DW, and DM require various tools, technologies, and methodologies to implement and manage, such as data integration, data quality, data modeling, data security, data governance, etc.
- BI, DW, and DM also face various challenges and limitations, such as data complexity, data volume, data variety, data veracity, data privacy, data ethics, etc.
