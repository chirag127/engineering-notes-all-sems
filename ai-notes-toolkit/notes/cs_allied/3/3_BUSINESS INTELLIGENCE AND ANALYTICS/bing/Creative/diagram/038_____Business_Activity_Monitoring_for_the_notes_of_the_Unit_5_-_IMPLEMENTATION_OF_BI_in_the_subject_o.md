### Business Activity Monitoring (BAM)

- Business Activity Monitoring (BAM) is the process of monitoring business activities through business process management software .
- BAM provides real-time information about the status and results of various operations, processes, and transactions .
- BAM is used to improve the speed and effectiveness of business operations by keeping track of what is happening and making issues visible quickly.
- BAM also enables an enterprise to make better informed business decisions, quickly address problem areas, and re-position organizations to take full advantage of emerging opportunities.
- BAM is a way to provide a simplified business-focused view of what might be a complex underlying system or group of system interactions that execute to fulfill the business transaction.
- BAM solutions are used by managers and executives to monitor the performance of key business processes and activities, such as sales, customer service, order fulfillment, inventory management, etc.
- BAM solutions typically consist of the following components:
  - Data sources: These are the systems or applications that generate the data related to the business activities, such as ERP, CRM, SCM, etc.
  - Data collection: This is the process of extracting, transforming, and loading the data from the data sources to a central repository or a message queue.
  - Data analysis: This is the process of applying business rules, logic, and algorithms to the data to derive meaningful insights and metrics.
  - Data presentation: This is the process of displaying the data analysis results in a user-friendly and interactive manner, such as dashboards, reports, alerts, etc.
  - Data action: This is the process of triggering actions or workflows based on the data analysis results, such as sending notifications, escalating issues, initiating corrective measures, etc.
