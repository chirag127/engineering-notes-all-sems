# Business Process Management

- Business process management (BPM) is a discipline that uses various methods to discover, model, analyze, measure, improve and optimize business processes  .
- A business process is a set of activities that coordinate the behavior of people, systems, information and things to produce business outcomes in support of a business strategy.
- BPM aims to align business processes with the enterprise goals, improve efficiency and effectiveness, reduce costs and risks, and enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty .
- BPM involves the following steps :
  - **Discovery**: Identify and document the existing business processes and their performance metrics.
  - **Modeling**: Create graphical representations of the business processes and their variations, using standard notations such as Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN).
  - **Analysis**: Examine the business processes and their models to identify problems, bottlenecks, gaps, opportunities and areas for improvement.
  - **Measurement**: Monitor and evaluate the performance of the business processes using key performance indicators (KPIs) and benchmarks.
  - **Improvement**: Implement changes to the business processes based on the analysis and measurement results, using methods such as Lean, Six Sigma, Kaizen, etc.
  - **Optimization**: Continuously review and refine the business processes to ensure they meet the changing business needs and goals, using techniques such as simulation, testing, feedback, etc.
  - **Automation**: Use software tools and technologies to execute, control and coordinate the business processes, reducing human intervention and errors, and increasing speed and quality.
- BPM can be applied to any type of business process, such as operational, managerial, supporting, core, etc., and across any industry or domain, such as manufacturing, banking, healthcare, education, etc.
- BPM can benefit from the use of business intelligence (BI) and analytics, which can provide data-driven insights and recommendations for improving and optimizing the business processes .
- BPM can also enable the implementation of BI and analytics, by providing the data sources, workflows, rules and standards for the BI and analytics systems .
