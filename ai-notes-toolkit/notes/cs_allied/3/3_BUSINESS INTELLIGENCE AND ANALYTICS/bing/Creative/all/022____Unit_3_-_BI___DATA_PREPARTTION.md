## Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION

- Data preparation is the process of transforming raw data into a form that is suitable for analysis, reporting, and visualization.
- Data preparation involves various steps, such as data collection, data cleaning, data integration, data transformation, data enrichment, and data quality assessment.
- Data preparation is an essential and often time-consuming task in business intelligence (BI), as it affects the accuracy, reliability, and usability of the data and the insights derived from it.
- Data preparation can be performed using various tools and techniques, such as spreadsheets, databases, scripting languages, data integration tools, data quality tools, and data visualization tools.
- Data preparation can be done manually or automatically, depending on the complexity, volume, and variety of the data sources and the desired output format and quality.
- Data preparation can be done in batch or in real-time, depending on the frequency, latency, and freshness of the data and the analytical requirements.
- Data preparation can be done by different roles, such as data engineers, data analysts, data scientists, and business users, depending on the level of technical expertise, domain knowledge, and business needs.
