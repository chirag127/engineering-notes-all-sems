# Real Time Business Intelligent System

- Real Time Business Intelligent System (RTBI) is a concept that describes the process of delivering business intelligence (BI) or information about business operations as they occur .
- BI is the process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data to support decision making and improve business performance.
- RTBI aims to provide near to zero latency and access to information whenever it is required .
- RTBI can help organizations to monitor and respond to changing business conditions, identify opportunities and threats, and optimize business processes and performance.
- RTBI can be implemented using different architectures, such as:
  - Event-based: RTBI systems are event driven, and may use Complex Event Processing, Event Stream Processing and Mashup techniques to enable events to be analyzed without being first transformed and stored in a database.
  - Data warehouse: RTBI systems increase the refresh cycle of an existing data warehouse, and use techniques such as change data capture, data federation, and data virtualization to provide real-time access to data.
  - Server-less: RTBI systems use cloud-based services and platforms, such as Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud Platform, and Microsoft Azure, to provide scalable, elastic, and cost-effective data processing and analytics.
