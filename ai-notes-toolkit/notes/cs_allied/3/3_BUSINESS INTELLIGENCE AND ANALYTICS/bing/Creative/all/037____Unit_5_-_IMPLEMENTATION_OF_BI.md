## Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI

Business intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into insights that help businesses make better decisions, improve performance, and gain a competitive edge. BI involves collecting, integrating, analyzing, and presenting data from various sources, such as internal databases, external systems, web services, social media, etc. BI also includes tools and techniques for data visualization, reporting, dashboarding, data mining, predictive analytics, and more.

To implement BI successfully in an organization, the following steps are recommended  :

- Create a BI strategy that aligns with the business goals and objectives, identifies the key stakeholders and their needs, defines the scope and priorities of the BI project, and establishes the metrics and KPIs to measure the outcomes and benefits of BI.
- Appoint a BI team that consists of skilled and experienced professionals, such as data analysts, data engineers, data scientists, BI developers, BI managers, etc. The BI team can be in-house, outsourced, or a combination of both, depending on the budget, resources, and expertise available.
- Find the best BI software and tools that suit the organization's needs, requirements, and preferences. The BI software and tools should be user-friendly, scalable, secure, flexible, and compatible with the existing data sources and systems. The BI software and tools should also support the desired BI functionalities, such as data integration, data quality, data analysis, data visualization, etc.
- Choose the data storage, environment, and platform that will host the BI solution. The data storage can be on-premise, cloud-based, or hybrid, depending on the data volume, velocity, variety, and veracity. The environment can be development, testing, or production, depending on the stage of the BI project. The platform can be web-based, mobile-based, or desktop-based, depending on the user accessibility and convenience.
- Finetune the data preparation process that involves extracting, transforming, and loading (ETL) data from various sources into a centralized data warehouse or data lake. The data preparation process should ensure the data quality, accuracy, completeness, consistency, and timeliness. The data preparation process should also follow the data governance and security policies and standards of the organization.
- Consider more advanced BI solutions that can enhance the data analysis and decision-making capabilities of the organization, such as data mining, predictive analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, etc. These solutions can help discover hidden patterns, trends, and insights from the data, as well as provide recommendations, forecasts, and scenarios based on the data.
- Deploy and test the BI solution that meets the user expectations and requirements, as well as the business goals and objectives. The BI solution should be validated and verified for its functionality, performance, usability, reliability, and security. The BI solution should also be documented and maintained for its future updates and enhancements.
- Train and educate the staff and users on how to use the BI solution effectively and efficiently. The training and education should cover the basic and advanced features and functions of the BI solution, as well as the best practices and tips for data analysis and decision-making. The training and education should also encourage the feedback and suggestions from the staff and users on how to improve the BI solution.
- Monitor and evaluate the BI solution regularly and continuously for its impact and value. The monitoring and evaluation should use the metrics and KPIs defined in the BI strategy, as well as the feedback and suggestions from the staff and users. The monitoring and evaluation should also identify the strengths and weaknesses of the BI solution, as well as the opportunities and threats for its improvement and innovation.
- Review and revise the BI strategy periodically and dynamically based on the changing business needs and environment. The BI strategy should be flexible and adaptable to the new data sources, systems, technologies, and trends. The BI strategy should also be aligned and integrated with the overall business strategy and vision of the organization.
