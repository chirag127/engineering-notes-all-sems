## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and transforming data into meaningful information that can help businesses make better decisions and improve their performance.
- BI can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Reporting: presenting data in a structured and easy-to-understand format, such as tables, charts, dashboards, etc.
  - Analysis: exploring and examining data to discover patterns, trends, correlations, outliers, etc.
  - Data mining: applying statistical and machine learning techniques to extract hidden knowledge and insights from large and complex data sets.
  - Data warehousing: storing and organizing data from multiple sources in a centralized and consistent manner, enabling efficient and reliable data access and analysis.
  - Data quality: ensuring that the data is accurate, complete, consistent, and relevant for the intended use cases.
  - Data governance: establishing policies, standards, and procedures for managing and securing data throughout its lifecycle, ensuring compliance with regulations and best practices.
- BI can provide various benefits to businesses, such as:
  - Enhancing decision making: providing timely, accurate, and relevant information to support strategic, tactical, and operational decisions.
  - Improving performance: measuring and monitoring key performance indicators (KPIs) and identifying opportunities for improvement and optimization.
  - Increasing customer satisfaction: understanding customer needs, preferences, and behavior, and providing personalized and tailored products and services.
  - Gaining competitive advantage: discovering new markets, customers, products, and services, and developing innovative and differentiated strategies.
  - Reducing costs and risks: optimizing business processes, resources, and assets, and mitigating potential threats and challenges.
