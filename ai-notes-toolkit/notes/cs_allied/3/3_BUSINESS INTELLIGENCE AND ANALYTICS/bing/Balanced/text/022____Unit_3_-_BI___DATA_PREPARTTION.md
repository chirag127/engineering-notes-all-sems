## Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION

- Data preparation is the process of transforming raw data into a format that is suitable for analysis and visualization.
- Data preparation involves various steps, such as data collection, data cleaning, data integration, data transformation, data reduction, and data quality assessment.
- Data preparation is an essential and often time-consuming task in business intelligence (BI), as it affects the accuracy, reliability, and efficiency of the subsequent analysis and visualization.
- Data preparation can be performed using various tools and techniques, such as spreadsheets, databases, scripting languages, data integration tools, data quality tools, and data visualization tools.
- Data preparation can be done manually or automatically, depending on the complexity, volume, and variety of the data sources and the desired output format.
- Data preparation can be challenging due to the following issues:
  - Data quality: The data may contain errors, inconsistencies, missing values, outliers, duplicates, or irrelevant information that need to be detected and corrected.
  - Data integration: The data may come from different sources, formats, structures, or schemas that need to be combined and aligned.
  - Data transformation: The data may need to be converted, normalized, aggregated, filtered, sorted, or enriched to meet the requirements of the analysis and visualization.
  - Data reduction: The data may be too large or complex to be processed or visualized effectively, and may need to be summarized, sampled, or compressed to reduce its size or dimensionality.
  - Data quality assessment: The data may need to be evaluated and validated to ensure its completeness, correctness, consistency, and relevance for the analysis and visualization.
