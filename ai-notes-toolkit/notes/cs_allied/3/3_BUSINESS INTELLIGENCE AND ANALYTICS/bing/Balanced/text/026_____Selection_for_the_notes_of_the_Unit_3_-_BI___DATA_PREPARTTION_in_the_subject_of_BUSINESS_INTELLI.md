### Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Data preparation is the process of gathering, combining, structuring and organizing data so it can be used in business intelligence (BI), analytics and data visualization applications.
- Data preparation involves the following steps :
  - Data preprocessing: identifying and selecting the relevant data sources, such as databases, files, web services, etc.
  - Data profiling: analyzing the data to understand its structure, quality, and metadata, such as data types, formats, values, etc.
  - Data cleansing: correcting or removing errors, inconsistencies, duplicates, outliers, and missing values in the data.
  - Data validation: ensuring the data meets the business rules and requirements, such as data types, formats, values, etc.
  - Data transformation: converting, aggregating, filtering, sorting, joining, and splitting the data to create the desired output format and structure.
- Data preparation is considered the most difficult, expensive, and time-consuming task in a BI project, estimated to take 60%-80% of the time and cost.
- Data preparation tools are software applications that help users perform data preparation tasks more efficiently and effectively, by providing features such as data discovery, data quality, data integration, data enrichment, and data governance .
- Data preparation tools can be classified into two types:
  - Self-service data preparation tools: these tools are designed for business users and analysts who want to prepare their own data without relying on IT or data experts. They offer a user-friendly interface, drag-and-drop functionality, and automation capabilities. Examples of self-service data preparation tools are Microsoft Power BI, Tableau Prep, and Alteryx.
  - Augmented data preparation tools: these tools are powered by artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) techniques that can automate and enhance the data preparation process, by providing features such as data profiling, data cleansing, data transformation, and data lineage. Examples of augmented data preparation tools are Microsoft Power BI dataflows, IBM Watson Studio, and Trifacta.
- Data preparation is a crucial step in any BI project, as it ensures the data is accurate, consistent, and comprehensive, and ready for analysis and visualization. Data preparation tools can help users save time and resources, and improve the quality and reliability of their data.
