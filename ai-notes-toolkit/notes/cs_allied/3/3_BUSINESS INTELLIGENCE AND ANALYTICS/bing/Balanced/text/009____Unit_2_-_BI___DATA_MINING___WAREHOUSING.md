## Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING

- Data mining is the process of discovering patterns, trends, and insights from large and complex data sets using various techniques and algorithms.
- Data warehousing is the process of designing, building, and maintaining a centralized repository of integrated, consistent, and reliable data from multiple sources for business intelligence purposes.
- The main objectives of data mining and warehousing are to support decision making, enhance business performance, and gain competitive advantage.
- The main components of a data warehouse are:
  - Data sources: The original data sources that provide the raw data for the data warehouse, such as operational databases, external data, or legacy systems.
  - Data integration: The process of extracting, transforming, and loading (ETL) the data from the data sources into the data warehouse, ensuring data quality, consistency, and integrity.
  - Data storage: The physical and logical structure of the data warehouse, such as the data model, schema, partitioning, indexing, and compression techniques.
  - Data access: The tools and applications that enable users to query, analyze, and visualize the data in the data warehouse, such as online analytical processing (OLAP), data mining, reporting, and dashboards.
- The main benefits of data mining and warehousing are:
  - Data mining and warehousing can provide valuable insights into customer behavior, preferences, and needs, enabling businesses to improve customer satisfaction, retention, and loyalty.
  - Data mining and warehousing can help identify new opportunities, trends, and patterns in the market, enabling businesses to innovate, diversify, and expand their products and services.
  - Data mining and warehousing can help optimize business processes, operations, and resources, enabling businesses to reduce costs, increase efficiency, and enhance quality.
  - Data mining and warehousing can help monitor and evaluate business performance, outcomes, and impacts, enabling businesses to measure, benchmark, and improve their results and goals.
  - Data mining and warehousing can help support strategic decision making, planning, and forecasting, enabling businesses to align their actions with their vision, mission, and objectives.
