## Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI

- Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into insights that can help businesses make better decisions.
- BI implementation involves the following steps:
  - Define the business goals and objectives that BI will support.
  - Assess the current state of data sources, quality, and availability.
  - Design the data architecture and the BI solution that will meet the business needs.
  - Develop and test the data integration, data warehouse, and BI applications.
  - Deploy and maintain the BI solution and ensure its alignment with the business goals and objectives.
  - Evaluate the BI solution's performance and impact on the business outcomes and user satisfaction.
- BI implementation requires the collaboration of various stakeholders, such as business users, IT staff, data analysts, and BI vendors.
- BI implementation also faces various challenges, such as data quality issues, data security and privacy concerns, user adoption and training, and change management.
