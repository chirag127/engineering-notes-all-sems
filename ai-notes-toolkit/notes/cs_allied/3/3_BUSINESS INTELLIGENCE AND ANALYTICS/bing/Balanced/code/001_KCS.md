# KCS

KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service, a methodology that aims to improve service delivery and knowledge management in service organizations. Some of the main points about KCS are:

- KCS is based on the principle that knowledge is a by-product of solving problems and that the knowledge base should evolve with the demand and use of the knowledge.
- KCS involves creating, capturing, structuring, reusing, and improving knowledge as part of the service process, rather than as a separate activity.
- KCS empowers service agents to own and update the knowledge base, using a collaborative and peer-reviewed approach, rather than relying on a centralized team of experts or editors.
- KCS leverages the collective experience and expertise of the service organization, as well as the feedback and insights from the customers, to create relevant, accurate, and timely knowledge.
- KCS benefits both the service organization and the customers, by reducing costs, increasing efficiency, improving quality, enhancing satisfaction, and enabling innovation.
