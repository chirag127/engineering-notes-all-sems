# BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS

- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and transforming data into meaningful insights that can help businesses make better decisions and improve their performance.
- Business analytics (BA) is the application of statistical, mathematical, and computational techniques to data to discover patterns, trends, and relationships that can support decision making and problem solving.
- BI and BA are closely related, but they have some differences in scope, purpose, and methods.
- BI focuses on descriptive analytics, which answers the question of what happened or what is happening in the business. BI tools include dashboards, reports, scorecards, and data visualization that can help managers monitor and understand the current state of the business.
- BA focuses on predictive and prescriptive analytics, which answer the questions of what will happen or what should happen in the business. BA tools include data mining, machine learning, optimization, and simulation that can help managers forecast and optimize future outcomes and actions.
- BI and BA can be used together to provide a comprehensive view of the business and support data-driven decision making. BI can provide the context and background for BA, while BA can provide the guidance and recommendations for BI.
