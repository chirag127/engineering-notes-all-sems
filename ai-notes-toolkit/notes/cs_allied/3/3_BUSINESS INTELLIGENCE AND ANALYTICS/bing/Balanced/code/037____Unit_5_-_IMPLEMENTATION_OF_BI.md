## Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI

Business intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into insights that help businesses make better decisions, improve performance, and gain a competitive edge. BI implementation is the process of designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining a BI system in an organization. A BI system consists of various components, such as data sources, data warehouses, data integration tools, data analysis tools, data visualization tools, and data governance policies.

The following are some of the steps involved in BI implementation:

- Create a BI strategy: This involves defining the vision, goals, objectives, scope, and benefits of BI for the organization. It also involves identifying the key stakeholders, users, and sponsors of the BI project, and aligning their expectations and requirements. A BI strategy should also include a roadmap, a budget, and a timeline for the BI project.
- Set the key performance indicators (KPIs): These are the metrics that measure the progress and success of the BI project. KPIs should be aligned with the business goals and objectives, and should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. KPIs should also be communicated and monitored regularly throughout the BI project.
- Appoint stakeholders and educate the staff: This involves assigning roles and responsibilities to the different stakeholders involved in the BI project, such as the project manager, the business analyst, the data engineer, the data analyst, the data scientist, the data steward, and the end-user. It also involves providing training and education to the staff on the benefits, features, and usage of the BI system.
- Build a strong BI team or outsource: This involves deciding whether to build the BI system in-house or outsource it to a third-party vendor. Building the BI system in-house requires having the necessary skills, resources, and infrastructure to design, develop, test, and deploy the BI system. Outsourcing the BI system requires finding a reliable, experienced, and cost-effective vendor who can deliver the BI system according to the specifications and expectations of the organization.
- Find the best software for your needs: This involves selecting the appropriate software tools and platforms for the BI system, based on the data sources, data formats, data volumes, data quality, data analysis, data visualization, and data security needs of the organization. Some of the popular software tools and platforms for BI include Tableau, Power BI, Qlik, SAP, Oracle, IBM, and Microsoft.
- Choose your data storage, environment, and platform: This involves deciding where and how to store, process, and access the data for the BI system. Data storage options include on-premise, cloud, or hybrid solutions, depending on the cost, scalability, availability, and performance of the data. Data environment options include batch, streaming, or real-time processing, depending on the timeliness, complexity, and frequency of the data. Data platform options include relational, non-relational, or multi-model databases, depending on the structure, variety, and veracity of the data.
- Finetune your data preparation process: This involves cleaning, transforming, integrating, and enriching the data for the BI system. Data preparation is a crucial step in BI implementation, as it ensures the accuracy, completeness, consistency, and validity of the data. Data preparation tools and techniques include data profiling, data quality, data cleansing, data transformation, data integration, data enrichment, and data cataloging.
- Consider more advanced solutions: This involves exploring the possibilities of using more advanced technologies and methods for the BI system, such as artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), natural language processing (NLP), predictive analytics, prescriptive analytics, and cognitive analytics. These solutions can enhance the BI system by providing deeper insights, faster responses, and smarter recommendations.
- Deploy and test the BI system: This involves deploying the BI system to the production environment, and testing its functionality, performance, usability, and reliability. Testing the BI system involves verifying the data quality, data security, data analysis, data visualization, and data governance aspects of the BI system. Testing the BI system also involves collecting feedback and suggestions from the end-users and stakeholders, and making the necessary adjustments and improvements to the BI system.
- Maintain and update the BI system: This involves monitoring, evaluating, and improving the BI system on a regular basis, to ensure its relevance, effectiveness, and efficiency. Maintaining and updating the BI system involves checking the data quality, data security, data analysis, data visualization, and data governance aspects of the BI system, and resolving any issues or errors that may arise. Maintaining and updating the BI system also involves adding new features, functionalities, and capabilities
