## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

- Business intelligence (BI) is the process of transforming data into insights that can help businesses make better decisions.
- BI involves collecting, integrating, analyzing, and presenting data from various sources, such as internal databases, external websites, social media, sensors, etc.
- BI can be used for various purposes, such as reporting, dashboards, data visualization, data mining, predictive analytics, prescriptive analytics, etc.
- BI can provide benefits to businesses, such as improved performance, increased efficiency, reduced costs, enhanced customer satisfaction, competitive advantage, etc.
- BI can also pose challenges to businesses, such as data quality, data security, data privacy, data governance, ethical issues, etc.
- BI requires a combination of technical skills, such as data warehousing, data modeling, data integration, data analysis, data visualization, etc., and business skills, such as domain knowledge, communication, collaboration, problem-solving, etc.
- BI is a dynamic and evolving field that is influenced by various factors, such as technological advancements, business trends, customer expectations, regulatory requirements, etc.
