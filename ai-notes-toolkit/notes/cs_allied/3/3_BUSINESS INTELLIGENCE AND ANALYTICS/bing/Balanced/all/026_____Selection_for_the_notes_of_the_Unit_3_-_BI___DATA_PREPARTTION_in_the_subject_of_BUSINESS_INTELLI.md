# Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Data preparation is the process of gathering, combining, structuring and organizing data so it can be used in business intelligence (BI), analytics and data visualization applications.
- Data preparation involves various steps such as data discovery, data profiling, data cleansing, data transformation, data integration, data enrichment and data validation .
- Data preparation is an iterative and agile process that requires collaboration between data producers, data consumers and data analysts.
- Data preparation can be done using various tools such as Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, Alteryx, Trifacta, Talend and others .
- Data preparation can help improve the quality, accuracy and completeness of data sets, as well as reduce the time and effort required for data analysis and reporting .
- Data preparation can also enable self-service BI and analytics, where business users can access, explore and manipulate data without relying on IT or data experts .
- Data preparation can also support advanced BI and analytics scenarios, such as data science, machine learning and artificial intelligence, where data needs to be prepared in specific formats and structures .
- Data preparation can also facilitate data governance and compliance, where data sources, transformations, lineage and quality can be tracked and monitored .
- Data preparation can also enhance data security and privacy, where data can be masked, encrypted, anonymized or pseudonymized before being shared or published .
- Data preparation can also enable data democratization and literacy, where data can be made accessible, understandable and actionable for a wider range of users and stakeholders .
