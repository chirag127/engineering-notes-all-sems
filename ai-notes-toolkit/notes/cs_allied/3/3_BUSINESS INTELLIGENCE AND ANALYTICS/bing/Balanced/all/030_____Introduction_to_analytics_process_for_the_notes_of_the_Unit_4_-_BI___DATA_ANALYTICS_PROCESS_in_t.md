# Introduction to analytics process

- Analytics is the process of discovering, interpreting, and communicating significant patterns in data.
- Analytics helps us see insights and meaningful data that we might not otherwise detect.
- Analytics can be applied to various domains, such as website analytics, business analytics, social media analytics, etc.
- Analytics can be classified into four types: descriptive, predictive, prescriptive, and diagnostic.
- Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past using data visualization and reporting tools.
- Predictive analytics forecasts what is likely to happen in the future using statistical and machine learning techniques.
- Prescriptive analytics suggests what actions should be taken to optimize a desired outcome using optimization and simulation models.
- Diagnostic analytics explains why something happened in the past using root cause analysis and data mining.
- Analytics process involves several steps, such as defining the problem, collecting and preparing the data, analyzing the data, interpreting and communicating the results, and implementing the solutions .
- Analytics process requires collaboration among different stakeholders, such as business users, data analysts, data scientists, data engineers, etc.
- Analytics process can help improve business performance, customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and decision making.
