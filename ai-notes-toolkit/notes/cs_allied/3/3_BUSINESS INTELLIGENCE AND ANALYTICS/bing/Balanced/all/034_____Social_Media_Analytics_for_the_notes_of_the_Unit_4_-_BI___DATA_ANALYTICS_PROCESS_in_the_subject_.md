# Social Media Analytics

- Social media analytics is the process of gathering and analyzing data from social networks such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter   .
- Social media analytics is broader than metrics such as likes, follows, retweets, previews, clicks, and impressions gathered from individual channels. It also differs from reporting offered by services that support marketing campaigns such as LinkedIn or Google Analytics.
- Social media analytics is commonly used by marketers to track online conversations about products and companies . It can also help businesses to spot trends in consumer behavior that are relevant to their industry and can influence the success of their strategies.
- Social media analytics can support business decisions and measure the performance of actions based on those decisions through social media. For example, social media analytics can help businesses to:
  - Identify and target their potential customers and influencers
  - Monitor and improve their brand reputation and customer satisfaction
  - Optimize their content and campaigns for different platforms and audiences
  - Analyze the impact and return on investment of their social media activities
  - Benchmark their performance against their competitors and industry standards
- Social media analytics can be performed using various tools and techniques, such as:
  - Social media platforms' native analytics tools, such as Facebook Insights, Twitter Analytics, or Instagram Insights
  - Third-party social media analytics tools, such as Hootsuite, Sprout Social, or Buffer
  - Data mining and machine learning methods, such as sentiment analysis, topic modeling, or network analysis
  - Data visualization and reporting tools, such as Tableau, Power BI, or Google Data Studio
