# Behavioral BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

- Behavioral analytics is the process of collecting and analyzing data from actions performed by users of a digital product, such as an app or website.
- Behavioral analytics can help businesses understand how users interact with their products, what they want, and how to improve their experiences and satisfaction.
- The behavioral BI – data analytics process consists of the following steps:

  1. Define your business and analytics goals. What are the key performance indicators (KPIs) that you want to measure and optimize?
  2. Map out the critical paths that align with your goals. What are the user journeys or flows that lead to the desired outcomes?
  3. Organize your event taxonomy. What are the actions or events that users perform on your product that you want to track and analyze?
  4. Understand how users are being identified. How do you distinguish between different users and sessions on your product?
  5. Decide if you need cross-platform behavior analytics. Do you want to track and analyze user behavior across multiple devices or platforms, such as web, mobile, or desktop?
  6. Establish the ‘Minimum Viable Instrumentation’. What are the minimum events and properties that you need to track to answer your analytics questions?
  7. Track your events. How do you implement the tracking code or SDK on your product to capture the events and properties?
  8. Set user properties and event properties. What are the attributes or characteristics of users and events that you want to use for segmentation and analysis?
  9. Analyze your data. What are the tools or methods that you use to query, visualize, and interpret your behavioral data?
  10. Take action based on your insights. How do you use your behavioral data to make decisions and changes to your product or strategy?

- Behavioral analytics can help businesses gain insights into user behavior, preferences, needs, and feedback, and use them to improve their products, services, and customer loyalty .
