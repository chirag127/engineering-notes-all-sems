### Selection
Selection is the process of choosing the relevant data for a specific analysis or modeling task. Selection can be done at different levels, such as:

- Data source selection: Choosing the appropriate data sources that contain the information needed for the analysis or modeling. Data sources can be operational systems, data warehouses, data lakes, or other external sources.
- Data subset selection: Choosing a subset of data from a larger dataset that is representative, balanced, and manageable. Data subset selection can be done by sampling, filtering, or partitioning the data.
- Feature selection: Choosing a subset of features or variables from a dataset that are relevant, informative, and non-redundant for the analysis or modeling. Feature selection can be done by using statistical tests, correlation analysis, or machine learning methods.

Selection can have several benefits for data preparation, such as:

- Reducing the complexity and dimensionality of the data
- Improving the quality and accuracy of the analysis or modeling
- Saving time and resources for data processing and computation
- Enhancing the interpretability and explainability of the results

Selection can also have some challenges, such as:

- Finding the optimal balance between the amount and quality of the data
- Dealing with missing, noisy, or inconsistent data
- Evaluating the impact of selection on the analysis or modeling performance
- Avoiding bias or overfitting in the selection process

Selection can be done manually or automatically, depending on the knowledge of the data and the analysis or modeling objectives. Manual selection requires a deep understanding of the data and the chosen methods, while automatic selection requires testing and comparing different data transforms and sequences of transforms. Selection can also be integrated with other data preparation steps, such as cleaning, integration, transformation, and reduction.

Some questions to ask when doing selection are:

- What is the goal and scope of the analysis or modeling task?
- What are the available data sources and how reliable and relevant are they?
- What are the characteristics and quality of the data, such as size, format, structure, type, distribution, etc.?
- What are the features or variables of interest and how are they related to the target or outcome variable?
- What are the criteria and methods for selecting the data, such as sampling techniques, statistical tests, machine learning algorithms, etc.?
