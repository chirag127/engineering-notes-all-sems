## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

- Business intelligence (BI) is a term that covers a variety of methods and technologies for collecting, storing, analyzing, and presenting data that supports business decision making .
- BI can help businesses gain insights from their data, such as customer behavior, market trends, operational efficiency, and competitive advantage  .
- BI can also enable businesses to automate and optimize their processes, improve their performance, and achieve their strategic goals  .
- BI can be divided into two main categories: descriptive analytics and predictive analytics .
  - Descriptive analytics refers to the analysis of past and present data to understand what has happened and why .
  - Predictive analytics refers to the use of statistical models and machine learning to forecast future outcomes and trends based on historical and current data .
- BI can be delivered through various tools and platforms, such as reports, dashboards, charts, graphs, data warehouses, data lakes, and cloud services  .
- BI can be used by different types of users, such as business analysts, managers, executives, and end users, depending on their needs and skills  .
- BI can benefit from the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP), which can enhance the data analysis and presentation capabilities of BI tools.
- BI is a dynamic and evolving field that requires constant adaptation and innovation to keep up with the changing business needs and technologies.
