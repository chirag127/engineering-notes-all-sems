### ETL Process – Role of ETL for BI

- ETL stands for **Extract, Transform, Load**, which is a data pipeline used to collect data from various sources, manipulate them according to business rules, and load them into a data warehouse.
- ETL is the backbone of **Business Intelligence (BI)**, which is the process of analyzing data to gain insights and support decision making.
- The role of ETL for BI is to provide the following benefits:
  - **Data quality and consistency**: ETL ensures that the data is accurate, complete, and standardized across different sources, which improves the reliability and validity of the analysis.
  - **Data integration and accessibility**: ETL consolidates data from disparate and heterogeneous sources, such as databases, files, web services, etc., and makes them available in a centralized and structured format, which simplifies the data retrieval and exploration.
  - **Data transformation and enrichment**: ETL applies various transformations and calculations to the data, such as filtering, sorting, aggregating, joining, splitting, etc., to create new attributes, metrics, and dimensions, which enhance the data value and meaning.
  - **Data security and compliance**: ETL ensures that the data is protected from unauthorized access and modification, and that it adheres to the relevant regulations and policies, such as data privacy, data retention, data governance, etc., which reduces the risk and liability of the data usage.
  - **Data discovery and visualization**: ETL enables the data to be analyzed and presented in various forms, such as reports, dashboards, charts, graphs, etc., using BI tools, such as Power BI, Tableau, Qlik, etc., which facilitate the data understanding and communication.
