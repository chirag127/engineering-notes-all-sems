### Social Media Analytics

- Social media analytics is the process of gathering and analyzing data from social networks such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or Twitter   .
- Social media analytics can support business decisions and measure the performance of actions based on those decisions through social media.
- Social media analytics is broader than metrics such as likes, follows, retweets, previews, clicks, and impressions gathered from individual channels. It also differs from reporting offered by services that support marketing campaigns such as LinkedIn or Google Analytics.
- Social media analytics can benefit businesses by enabling marketers to spot trends in consumer behavior that are relevant to a business's industry and can influence the success of its products and services.
- Social media analytics can also help businesses to monitor their online reputation, identify influencers and advocates, understand customer sentiment and feedback, optimize content and campaigns, and measure the return on investment (ROI) of their social media efforts.
- Social media analytics can be performed using various tools and techniques, such as social media platforms' native analytics, third-party tools and software, data mining, text analysis, sentiment analysis, network analysis, and visualization.
- Social media analytics can be divided into four stages: data collection, data processing, data analysis, and data presentation.
  - Data collection involves gathering data from various social media sources, such as posts, comments, reviews, ratings, shares, mentions, hashtags, etc.
  - Data processing involves cleaning, filtering, transforming, and integrating the collected data into a suitable format for analysis.
  - Data analysis involves applying statistical and analytical methods to the processed data to extract insights and patterns.
  - Data presentation involves communicating the results of the analysis using reports, dashboards, charts, graphs, etc.
