### Complex Event Processing

Complex Event Processing (CEP) is the use of technology to predict high-level events likely to result from specific sets of low-level factors. The goal of CEP is to identify meaningful events (such as opportunities or threats) in real-time situations and respond to them as quickly as possible .

- CEP combines data from multiple sources to infer events or patterns that suggest more complicated circumstances.
- CEP matches continuously incoming events against a pattern and provides insight into what is happening.
- CEP allows personnel to take effective actions in response to specific opportunities or threats.
- CEP is an organizational tool that helps to aggregate a lot of different information and that identifies and analyzes cause-and-effect relationships among events in real time.

CEP is similar to event stream processing, which is a technology for aggregating, processing, and analyzing massive streams of data in order to gain real-time insights from events as they occur.
