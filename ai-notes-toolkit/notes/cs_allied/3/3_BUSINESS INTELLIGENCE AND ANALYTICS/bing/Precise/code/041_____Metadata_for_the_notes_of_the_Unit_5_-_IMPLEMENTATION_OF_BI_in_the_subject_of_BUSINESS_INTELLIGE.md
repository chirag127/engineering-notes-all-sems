### Metadata for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is a technology-driven process that helps users draw conclusions from data analysis. It includes data analytics and business analytics as parts of the whole process.
2. Implementing BI offers a myriad of benefits, including data clarity, increased efficiency, better customer experience, and improved employee satisfaction.
3. Data governance is an essential component of BI and analytics. It involves principles such as data integrity, data standardization and metadata, standardized change management, and audit capabilities.
4. BI relies heavily on the input and technologies used to transform unstructured or semi-structured data. These technologies can also be used for data mining and as front-end tools to work with big data.
5. The BI maturity model shows the level of evolution of BI in a company on a five-level scale, ranging from disorganized to optimized. The lowest level of the maturity model has data scattered across the organization and no data governance.
