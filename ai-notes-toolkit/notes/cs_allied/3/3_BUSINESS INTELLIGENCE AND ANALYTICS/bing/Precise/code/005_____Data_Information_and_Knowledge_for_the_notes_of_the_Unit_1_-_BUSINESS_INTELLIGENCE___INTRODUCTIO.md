### Data, Information, and Knowledge

Data, information, and knowledge are three key concepts in the field of business intelligence and analytics. These concepts are closely related, but they are not the same.

- **Data** refers to raw, unprocessed facts and figures. Data can be collected from various sources, such as sensors, surveys, and transaction records. Data by itself is not very useful, as it is often unorganized and difficult to interpret.

- **Information** is data that has been processed, organized, and structured in a way that makes it meaningful and useful. Information is created by analyzing and interpreting data, and it can be used to make decisions and solve problems.

- **Knowledge** is the understanding and insight gained from the analysis of information. Knowledge is the highest level of understanding, and it is used to make predictions, develop strategies, and drive innovation.

In the context of business intelligence and analytics, data is collected and transformed into information through various analytical processes. This information is then used to generate knowledge, which can be used to make informed decisions and drive business success.
