### Real Time Business Intelligent System

Real-time business intelligence (BI) is the process of delivering information and analysis in real-time or near real-time. This allows businesses to make decisions and take actions based on the most up-to-date information. Real-time BI can be achieved through a variety of methods, including:

1. **Data Warehousing:** A data warehouse is a large, centralized repository of data that is used to support business intelligence and decision-making activities. Data warehouses are designed to support the efficient querying and analysis of large volumes of data.

2. **Data Integration:** Data integration involves combining data from multiple sources into a single, unified view. This is often achieved through the use of Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) tools, which extract data from source systems, transform it into a common format, and load it into a data warehouse or other target system.

3. **Data Visualization:** Data visualization is the process of representing data in a visual format, such as a chart or graph. This makes it easier for users to understand and analyze the data.

4. **Dashboards and Reports:** Dashboards and reports are used to present data and analysis in a format that is easy to understand and act upon. Dashboards typically provide a high-level view of key performance indicators (KPIs), while reports provide more detailed information.

Real-time BI can provide many benefits to businesses, including:

- Improved decision-making: By providing up-to-date information, real-time BI allows businesses to make more informed decisions.

- Increased agility: Real-time BI allows businesses to respond more quickly to changing conditions, such as shifts in market demand or supply chain disruptions.

- Enhanced customer service: By providing real-time information about customer interactions and behavior, real-time BI can help businesses improve their customer service.

- Increased efficiency: Real-time BI can help businesses identify and address operational inefficiencies, leading to cost savings and improved performance.

Overall, real-time BI is an important tool for businesses looking to stay competitive in today's fast-paced business environment. By providing timely and accurate information, real-time BI can help businesses make better decisions, respond more quickly to changing conditions, and improve their overall performance.
