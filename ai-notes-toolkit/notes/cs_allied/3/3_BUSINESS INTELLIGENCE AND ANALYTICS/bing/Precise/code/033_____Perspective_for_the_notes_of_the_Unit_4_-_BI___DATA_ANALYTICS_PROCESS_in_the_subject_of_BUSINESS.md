### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. **Data Analytics Process**: The data analytics process is a series of steps that involves collecting, processing, and analyzing data to extract insights and knowledge from it.
2. **Data Collection**: The first step in the data analytics process is data collection. This involves gathering data from various sources such as databases, spreadsheets, and external data sources.
3. **Data Processing**: Once the data is collected, it needs to be processed to prepare it for analysis. This involves cleaning, transforming, and organizing the data.
4. **Data Analysis**: The next step is data analysis, where the processed data is analyzed using various techniques such as statistical analysis, data mining, and machine learning to extract insights and knowledge from it.
5. **Data Visualization**: Data visualization is the process of representing data in a visual format such as charts, graphs, and maps to make it easier to understand and interpret.
6. **Data Interpretation**: The final step in the data analytics process is data interpretation, where the insights and knowledge extracted from the data are interpreted and used to make informed decisions.
