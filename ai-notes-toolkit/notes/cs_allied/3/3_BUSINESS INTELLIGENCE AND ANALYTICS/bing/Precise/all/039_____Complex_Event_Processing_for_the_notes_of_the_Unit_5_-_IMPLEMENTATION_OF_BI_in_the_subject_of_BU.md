# Complex Event Processing

Complex Event Processing (CEP) is a method of tracking and analyzing streams of information about things that happen (events) and deriving a conclusion from them. It is used in the field of Business Intelligence and Analytics, specifically in the implementation of BI in Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI.

Some key points to note about CEP are:

1. CEP is used to identify meaningful events and respond to them as quickly as possible.
2. It can handle large volumes of data in real-time.
3. CEP can be used to detect patterns and relationships between events.
4. It can be used to predict future events based on past data.
5. CEP is used in various industries such as finance, healthcare, and transportation.

In summary, Complex Event Processing is a powerful tool for analyzing and responding to real-time data in the field of Business Intelligence and Analytics. It is an important topic to study for the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS.
