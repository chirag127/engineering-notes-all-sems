# Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION

### Selection

Selection is the process of choosing a subset of data from a larger dataset based on certain criteria. In the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics, selection is an important step in data preparation as it allows analysts to focus on the most relevant data for their analysis.

Some key points to consider when performing selection in data preparation for Business Intelligence and Analytics include:

1. Identifying the relevant data: Before performing selection, it is important to identify which data is relevant for the analysis. This can be done by understanding the business problem and the data available.

2. Defining selection criteria: Once the relevant data has been identified, selection criteria can be defined. These criteria can be based on various factors such as data values, data types, or data relationships.

3. Applying selection criteria: After defining the selection criteria, it can be applied to the larger dataset to extract the relevant subset of data.

4. Verifying the selected data: After performing selection, it is important to verify that the selected data is accurate and complete. This can be done by checking the data against the selection criteria and ensuring that all relevant data has been included.

Selection is a crucial step in data preparation for Business Intelligence and Analytics as it allows analysts to focus on the most relevant data for their analysis. By carefully identifying the relevant data, defining selection criteria, applying the criteria, and verifying the selected data, analysts can ensure that their analysis is based on accurate and complete data.
