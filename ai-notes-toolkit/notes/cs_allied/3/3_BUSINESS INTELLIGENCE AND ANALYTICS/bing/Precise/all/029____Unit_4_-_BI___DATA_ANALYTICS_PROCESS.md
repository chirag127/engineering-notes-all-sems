## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

Business Intelligence (BI) is a process that involves the collection, integration, analysis, and presentation of business information. The purpose of BI is to support better business decision making. Data analytics is a key component of BI, and it involves the use of statistical and mathematical methods to analyze data and extract insights.

The data analytics process typically involves the following steps:

1. **Data Collection:** This involves gathering data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, and external data sources.

2. **Data Integration:** This involves combining data from different sources into a single, unified view.

3. **Data Cleaning:** This involves removing any errors or inconsistencies in the data.

4. **Data Analysis:** This involves using statistical and mathematical methods to analyze the data and extract insights.

5. **Data Visualization:** This involves presenting the results of the analysis in a visual format, such as charts and graphs.

6. **Data Interpretation:** This involves interpreting the results of the analysis and drawing conclusions.

7. **Decision Making:** This involves using the insights gained from the analysis to make informed business decisions.

The data analytics process is an iterative one, and it is important to continually refine and improve the process to ensure that the insights gained are accurate and relevant. It is also important to ensure that the data used in the analysis is of high quality, as the accuracy of the results depends on the quality of the data.
