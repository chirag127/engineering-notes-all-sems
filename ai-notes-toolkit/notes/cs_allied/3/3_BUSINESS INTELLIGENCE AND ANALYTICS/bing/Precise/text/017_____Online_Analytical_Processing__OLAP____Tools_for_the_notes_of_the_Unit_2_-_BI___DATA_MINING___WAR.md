### Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) – Tools for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) is a category of software tools that allows analysts, managers, and executives to gain insight into data through fast, consistent, interactive access to a wide variety of possible views of information that has been transformed from raw data to reflect the real dimensionality of the enterprise as understood by the user.
- OLAP tools enable users to analyze data from multiple dimensions, providing a multidimensional view of data.
- OLAP tools are used for data mining and data warehousing, which are essential components of business intelligence and analytics.
- OLAP tools provide a user-friendly interface for interacting with data, allowing users to easily create reports, charts, and graphs.
- Some popular OLAP tools include Microsoft SQL Server Analysis Services, Oracle OLAP, IBM Cognos TM1, and SAP BusinessObjects.
- OLAP tools can be used for a variety of business intelligence and analytics applications, including sales analysis, financial reporting, budgeting and forecasting, and market research.
- OLAP tools can help businesses make better decisions by providing them with the ability to analyze data in a more meaningful way.
- OLAP tools can be used to analyze data from a variety of sources, including relational databases, spreadsheets, and flat files.
- OLAP tools can be used to create complex calculations and aggregations, allowing users to analyze data at a deeper level.
- OLAP tools can be used to create custom reports and dashboards, providing users with a personalized view of their data.
