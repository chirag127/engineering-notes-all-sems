### ETL Process – Role of ETL for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. ETL stands for Extract, Transform, and Load. It is a process used to integrate data from multiple sources and prepare it for analysis.
2. The first step in the ETL process is data extraction. This involves retrieving data from various sources such as databases, files, and applications.
3. The second step is data transformation. This involves cleaning, mapping, and converting the data into a format that can be loaded into a data warehouse.
4. The final step is data loading. This involves loading the transformed data into a data warehouse or other target system.
5. The ETL process plays a crucial role in data warehousing and business intelligence. It enables organizations to integrate data from disparate sources and create a single, unified view of their data.
6. This unified view of data is essential for effective data analysis and decision-making. It allows organizations to gain insights into their business operations and make data-driven decisions.
7. ETL tools are used to automate the ETL process. These tools can help to reduce the time and effort required to integrate data from multiple sources.
8. In summary, the ETL process is a key component of data warehousing and business intelligence. It enables organizations to integrate data from multiple sources and create a single, unified view of their data for analysis and decision-making.
