### Business Process Management

Business Process Management (BPM) is a discipline that involves the identification, design, documentation, execution, monitoring, and control of business processes to achieve organizational goals. BPM is an essential component of Business Intelligence and Analytics (BI&A) and is covered in Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI of the subject.

Some key points to note about BPM are:

1. BPM involves the use of various methods, techniques, and tools to analyze, model, improve, and optimize business processes.
2. BPM aims to increase efficiency, reduce costs, and improve customer satisfaction by streamlining business processes.
3. BPM is an ongoing process that involves continuous monitoring and improvement of business processes.
4. BPM can be applied to any type of business process, including operational, management, and support processes.
5. BPM often involves the use of software tools to automate and manage business processes.

In summary, BPM is a crucial aspect of BI&A that helps organizations to achieve their goals by improving the efficiency and effectiveness of their business processes. It is an ongoing process that requires continuous monitoring and improvement to ensure that business processes are optimized to meet the changing needs of the organization.
