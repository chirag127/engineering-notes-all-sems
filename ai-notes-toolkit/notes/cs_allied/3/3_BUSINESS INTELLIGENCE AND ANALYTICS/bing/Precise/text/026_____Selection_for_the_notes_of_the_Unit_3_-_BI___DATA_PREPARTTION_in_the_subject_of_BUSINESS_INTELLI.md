### Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data preparation is a critical step in the business intelligence and analytics process.
2. It involves cleaning, transforming, and organizing data to make it suitable for analysis.
3. Common data preparation tasks include removing duplicate records, dealing with missing values, and converting data types.
4. Data preparation can be time-consuming and requires a good understanding of the data and the analysis goals.
5. Proper data preparation can greatly improve the accuracy and reliability of the analysis results.
6. There are many tools and techniques available for data preparation, including data profiling, data cleansing, and data transformation.
7. It is important to document the data preparation process to ensure transparency and reproducibility.
