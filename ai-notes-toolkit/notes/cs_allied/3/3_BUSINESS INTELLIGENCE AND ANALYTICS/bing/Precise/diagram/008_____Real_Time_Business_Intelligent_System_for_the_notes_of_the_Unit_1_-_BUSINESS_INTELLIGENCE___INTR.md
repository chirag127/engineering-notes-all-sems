### Real Time Business Intelligent System

Real-time business intelligence (BI) is the process of delivering information and analysis in real-time, or near real-time, to support faster and more informed decision making. It involves the use of technologies and processes to access, analyze, and deliver data in a timely manner, allowing businesses to react quickly to changing conditions and take advantage of new opportunities.

Some key points to consider when discussing real-time BI include:

1. Real-time BI can help businesses make faster and more informed decisions by providing timely access to data and analysis.
2. It involves the use of technologies and processes to access, analyze, and deliver data in a timely manner.
3. Real-time BI can help businesses react quickly to changing conditions and take advantage of new opportunities.
4. It can be particularly useful in industries where conditions change rapidly, such as finance, manufacturing, and retail.
5. Real-time BI can be challenging to implement, as it requires the integration of multiple systems and the ability to process large amounts of data quickly.

Overall, real-time BI can provide significant benefits to businesses by enabling faster and more informed decision making. However, it requires careful planning and implementation to ensure that the necessary systems and processes are in place to support real-time data access and analysis.
