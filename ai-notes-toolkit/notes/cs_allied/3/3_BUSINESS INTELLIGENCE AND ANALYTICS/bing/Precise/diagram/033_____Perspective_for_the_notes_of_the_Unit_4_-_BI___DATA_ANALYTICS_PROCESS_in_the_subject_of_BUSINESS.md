### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. **Perspective** refers to the way in which data is viewed and interpreted.
2. It is important to consider the perspective of the data analyst, as well as the perspective of the intended audience, when analyzing and presenting data.
3. Different perspectives can lead to different interpretations of the same data.
4. It is important to be aware of potential biases and to strive for objectivity when analyzing data.
5. Considering multiple perspectives can lead to a more comprehensive understanding of the data and can help to identify potential issues or areas for further investigation.
6. In the context of business intelligence and analytics, perspective is an important factor in the data analytics process, as it can influence the way in which data is collected, analyzed, and presented.
7. It is important to consider the perspective of all stakeholders, including business leaders, data analysts, and end-users, when making decisions based on data analysis.
8. Effective communication and collaboration between stakeholders can help to ensure that all perspectives are considered and that the data analysis process is transparent and effective.
