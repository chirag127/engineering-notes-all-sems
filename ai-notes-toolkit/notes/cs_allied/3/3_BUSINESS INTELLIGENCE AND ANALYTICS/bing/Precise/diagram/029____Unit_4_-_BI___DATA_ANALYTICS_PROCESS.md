## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

Business Intelligence (BI) is a process that involves the collection, integration, analysis, and presentation of business information. The purpose of BI is to support better decision-making in an organization. Data analytics is a key component of BI, and it involves the use of statistical and quantitative techniques to derive insights from data.

The data analytics process typically involves the following steps:

1. **Data Collection**: This involves gathering data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, and external data sources.

2. **Data Integration**: This involves combining data from different sources into a single, consistent data set.

3. **Data Cleaning**: This involves removing any errors or inconsistencies in the data.

4. **Data Analysis**: This involves using statistical and quantitative techniques to analyze the data and derive insights.

5. **Data Visualization**: This involves presenting the results of the analysis in a visual format, such as charts or graphs.

6. **Decision Making**: This involves using the insights derived from the data analysis to make informed decisions.

The data analytics process is an iterative one, and it may involve going back and forth between the different steps to refine the analysis and improve the results. It is important to have a clear understanding of the business problem being addressed and the data available to ensure that the analysis is relevant and accurate.
