### Types of Analytical Techniques in BI – Descriptive

Descriptive analytics is a type of analytical technique used in Business Intelligence (BI) to summarize and describe historical data. It is the most basic form of data analysis and is often the first step in the data analytics process. Descriptive analytics is used to answer questions such as "What happened?" and "How often did it happen?".

Some common techniques used in descriptive analytics include:

1. **Data Aggregation:** This technique involves combining data from multiple sources to create a summary view of the data. This can be useful for identifying trends and patterns in the data.

2. **Data Mining:** This technique involves using algorithms to identify patterns and relationships in large datasets. Data mining can be used to identify correlations, associations, and clusters in the data.

3. **Data Visualization:** This technique involves using graphical representations of data to help users understand and interpret the data. Common data visualization techniques include charts, graphs, and maps.

4. **Statistical Analysis:** This technique involves using statistical methods to analyze and summarize data. Common statistical methods used in descriptive analytics include measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode) and measures of dispersion (range, variance, standard deviation).

Descriptive analytics is an important part of the BI data analytics process as it provides a foundation for more advanced analytical techniques such as predictive and prescriptive analytics. It is commonly used in a variety of business applications, including sales analysis, customer analysis, and financial analysis.
