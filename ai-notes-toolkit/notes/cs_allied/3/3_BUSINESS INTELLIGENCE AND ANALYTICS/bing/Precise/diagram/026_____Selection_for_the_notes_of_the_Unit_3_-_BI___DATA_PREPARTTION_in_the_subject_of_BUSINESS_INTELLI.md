### Selection for the notes of the Unit 3 - BI – DATA PREPARTTION in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data preparation is the process of cleaning, transforming, and organizing raw data into a format suitable for analysis.
2. Data preparation is a crucial step in the business intelligence and analytics process, as the quality of the data used for analysis can significantly impact the accuracy and reliability of the results.
3. Common data preparation tasks include data cleaning, data transformation, data integration, and data reduction.
4. Data cleaning involves identifying and correcting errors and inconsistencies in the data, such as missing or incorrect values.
5. Data transformation involves converting the data into a format suitable for analysis, such as normalizing or scaling the data.
6. Data integration involves combining data from multiple sources to create a unified view of the data.
7. Data reduction involves reducing the size of the data set by removing irrelevant or redundant data.
8. Effective data preparation can improve the efficiency and accuracy of the analysis, leading to better decision-making and improved business outcomes.
