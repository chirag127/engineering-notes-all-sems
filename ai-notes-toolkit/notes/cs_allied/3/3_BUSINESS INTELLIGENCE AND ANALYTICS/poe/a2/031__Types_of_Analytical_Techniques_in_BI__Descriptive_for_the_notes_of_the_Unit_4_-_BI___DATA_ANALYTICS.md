### Types of Analytical Techniques in BI –Descriptive

1. Descriptive Analytics: Descriptive analytics summarizes and presents data to derive insights and trends. It answers the question "What has happened?" by mining historical data and presenting key metrics, trends and patterns. Examples include data visualization, data aggregation, and reporting.
2. Diagnostic Analytics: Diagnostic analytics investigates the reasons behind the trends and patterns in the data. It answers the question "Why did it happen?" by analyzing relationships and causal factors in historical data. Examples include drill-down, data mining and correlations.
3. Predictive Analytics: Predictive analytics uses machine learning and statistical techniques to analyze current and historical data and make predictions. It answers the question "What could happen?" by modeling likely outcomes and forecasting future trends. Examples include regression, decision trees, and neural networks.
4. Prescriptive Analytics: Prescriptive analytics recommends one or more courses of action based on predictive analytics. It answers the question "What should we do?" by evaluating potential actions and their outcomes. Examples include optimization, recommendation systems, and decision support systems.
