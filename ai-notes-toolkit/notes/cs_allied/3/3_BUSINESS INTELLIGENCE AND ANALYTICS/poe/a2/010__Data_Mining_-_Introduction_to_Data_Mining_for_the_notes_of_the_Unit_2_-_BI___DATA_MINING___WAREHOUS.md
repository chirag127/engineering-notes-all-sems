### Data Mining - Introduction to Data Mining for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data Mining: Data mining is the process of extracting patterns from large data sets to discover hidden patterns and unknown correlations, finding relationships and insights into the data.
2. Why Data Mining?: The amount of data in the world is increasing exponentially. Data mining helps analyze this data and extract useful information from it. This enables organizations to make better business decisions and understand their customers better.
3. Data Mining Process: The process of data mining involves the following steps:
 - Gathering data: Collecting data from various sources.
 - Data preparation: Cleaning and formatting the data.
 - Data selection: Selecting the relevant data for the analysis.
 - Data transformation: Applying various transformations to the data to bring out useful patterns.
 - Data mining: Applying data mining algorithms and techniques to the prepared data.
 - Pattern evaluation: Evaluating the patterns and relationships found to identify the useful ones.
 - Knowledge presentation: Presenting the findings in an understandable way.
4. Data Mining Techniques: Some of the popular data mining techniques are:
 - Classification: To predict category or class of data. Examples: Spam detection, sentiment analysis.
 - Regression: To analyze relationship between variables and predict continuous values. Examples: Stock price prediction, sales prediction.
 - Clustering: To group similar data points into clusters. Examples: Customer segmentation, market segmentation.
 - Association rule learning: To find relationships between variables in large data sets. Examples: Market basket analysis, recommendations.
 - Anomaly detection: To identify anomalies and unusual patterns in the data. Examples: Fraud detection, fault detection.
