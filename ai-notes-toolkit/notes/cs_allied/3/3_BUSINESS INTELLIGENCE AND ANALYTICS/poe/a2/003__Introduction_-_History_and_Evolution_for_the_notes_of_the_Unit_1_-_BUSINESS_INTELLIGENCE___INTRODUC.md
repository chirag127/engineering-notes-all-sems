### Introduction - History and Evolution

1. Business Intelligence (BI) refers to skills, processes, technologies, applications and practices used to support business decision making.
2. The concept of BI has evolved over the years:
- initially, it was just reporting and analysis of business data using spreadsheets and charts.
- then data warehousing was introduced to consolidate and organize data from multiple sources.
- in 1990s, multidimensional databases and data mining techniques were adopted to enable advanced analytics.
- in 21st century, self-service BI tools were introduced along with Enterprise Data Warehouses to enable data democratization.
- currently, BI incorporates advanced analytics, data mining and AI/ML techniques to enable data-driven businesses.
3. Key milestones in evolution of BI:
- Spreadsheets (1970s): enabled basic data analysis and reporting
- Data Warehouses (late 1980s): consolidated data from multiple systems
- OLAP Cubes (mid 1990s): enabled multidimensional data analysis
- Data Mining (late 1990s): enabled machine learning based pattern discovery
- Self-Service BI (early 2000s): enabled data access and visualization for business users
- Advanced Analytics and AI/ML (current): enabling predictive and prescriptive analytics
