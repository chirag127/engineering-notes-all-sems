# KCS

Knowledge-Centered Service (KCS) is an approach to providing technical support which emphasizes:

1. Capturing and organizing knowledge - Technicians are required to document solutions to problems they solve, which are then reviewed and organized into a knowledge base.
2. Reusing knowledge - Before solving a new problem, technicians are required to search the knowledge base for relevant solutions or partial solutions.
3. Continuous improvement - The knowledge base is continually expanded and improved as new solutions are added and as technicians provide feedback on the usefulness of existing articles.

Benefits of KCS include:

- Faster resolution of problems through reuse of existing solutions.
- Higher quality solutions through review and collaborative improvement of knowledge base content.
- Reduced dependence on any one individual, since knowledge is collectively owned by the group.
- Ability to support growing numbers of problems and products as the knowledge base expands.

The success of a KCS deployment depends on:

- Strong management support and endorsement of knowledge-centered practices.
- Incentives and rewards for contributing to and reusing the knowledge base.
- Training on documentation and knowledge management for technical staff.
- Easy-to-use knowledge base authoring and searching tools.
- Tracking knowledge base usage and impact to continuously improve processes.

In summary, KCS is an approach focused on capturing and reusing technical knowledge to provide faster, higher quality support. When successfully implemented, KCS can improve support efficiency and scalability while reducing dependence on individual experts.
