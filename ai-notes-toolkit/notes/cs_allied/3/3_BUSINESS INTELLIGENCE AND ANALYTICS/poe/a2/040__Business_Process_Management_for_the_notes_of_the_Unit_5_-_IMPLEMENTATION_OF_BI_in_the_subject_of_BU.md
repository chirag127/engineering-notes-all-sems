### Business Process Management for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

- Business Process Management (BPM) is a disciplined approach to identify, design, execute, document, monitor, control, and measure both automated and non-automated business processes to achieve consistent outcomes and meet strategic goals.
- The key goals of BPM are to reduce costs, increase efficiency, reduce errors, and provide transparency.
- The major steps involved in BPM are:
-- Process Discovery: Identifying the processes and mapping them.
-- Process Modeling: Documenting the processes in a standard notation.
-- Process Analysis: Analyzing the processes for problems and inefficiencies.
-- Process Redesign: Redesigning the processes to remove inefficiencies.
-- Process Automation: Automating the processes using technology.
-- Process Monitoring: Continuously monitoring and optimizing the processes.
- BPM enables integration of people, processes, applications, and data to provide a holistic view and better decision making in an organization. It helps in digitally transforming businesses to achieve operational excellence.
