## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

1. Problem Definition - Identifying the business problem and framing it into an analytics problem.
- Understanding the business objectives and requirements.
- Determining the key metrics and key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure the solution.
- Specifying the success criteria for the solution.

2. Data Collection - Gathering relevant data from different sources required to solve the problem.
- Identifying the key data sources - internal and external.
- Extracting, combining and cleaning the data.
- Verifying and validating the data.

3. Data Analysis - Applying analytical techniques on the collected data to gain insights and solve the problem.
- Using descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analytics.
- Applying statistical analysis, data mining and machine learning methods.
- Visualizing the data and results using charts, plots etc. to discover patterns and insights.

4. Solution Implementation and Monitoring - Implementing the solution and monitoring its performance.
- Integrating the analytical models and insights into business processes.
- Monitoring the key metrics and KPIs and re-tuning the solution as required.

The above points cover the key steps in the data analytics process to solve a business problem using data. The process is iterative and helps in continuous improvement of the solution.
