### Iris Datasets for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. The Iris dataset contains 3 species of Iris flowers (Setosa, Versicolor, and Virginica) and measurements of petal and sepal sizes.
2. It is a multivariate dataset with 4 features: Sepal Length, Sepal Width, Petal Length and Petal Width.
3. The dataset contains 50 samples from each of the 3 species.
4. The dataset is used for testing and training classification models to predict the species of a new flower sample based on the measurements.
5. Some key purposes of the Iris dataset:
    - It is easy to understand and visualize.
    - It contains balanced classes (same number of samples in each class).
    - It contains numerical and categorical features.
    - It is good for testing simple classification algorithms.
6. The Iris dataset can be loaded and explored in Python using Pandas and Scikit-Learn libraries. Classification algorithms like Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, etc. can be trained and evaluated on this dataset.
