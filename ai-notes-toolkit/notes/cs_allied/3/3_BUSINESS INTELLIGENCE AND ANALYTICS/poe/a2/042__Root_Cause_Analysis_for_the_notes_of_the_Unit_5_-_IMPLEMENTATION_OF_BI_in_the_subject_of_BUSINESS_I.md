### Root Cause Analysis for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Identify the problem: The first step is to identify the problem accurately. Some questions to ask are:
- What is the problem?
- When and how did it occur?
- Who is impacted?
- What is the financial impact?

2. Gather data: Collect all relevant data regarding the problem. This could be logs, reports, interviews, etc. The more data gathered the better as it leads to an accurate analysis.

3. Analyze the data: Study the data in depth to find patterns and insights. Look for direct and indirect causes. Connect the dots to get a complete picture. Tools like 5 Whys, Fishbone diagram, histograms, etc. can be used.

4. Identify root causes: The key reasons that resulted in the problem are the root causes. These are fundamental underlying issues that need to be addressed to resolve the problem permanently.

5. Recommend solutions: Come up with solutions to address the root causes and resolve the primary problem. Evaluate pros and cons of each solution and determine the best approach to implement.

6. Implement and monitor: Implement the selected solutions and monitor their effectiveness. It may be required to refine or replace solutions if they are not working. Continue monitoring to ensure the problem does not recur.

The key steps in root cause analysis are identifying the problem, gathering data, analyzing the data, identifying root causes, recommending solutions and implementing solutions. Thorough analysis leads to identification of actual root causes and implementing the most apt solutions to resolve problems permanently.
