### Data Mart for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data Mart: A data mart is a smaller and more focused subset of a data warehouse. It is department-specific and contains data related to a specific business line or team. Data marts are typically designed for a single functional area and contain time-variant data.
2. Benefits of Data Marts:
- Focused on specific department: Data marts contain data specifically relevant to a department or business unit. This makes it easier to access required data.
- Quicker to implement: Data marts are smaller in scope than data warehouses, so they can be designed and implemented more quickly.
- Scalable: Additional data marts can be added as needed to serve other departments or business units.
3. Challenges with Data Marts:
- Redundancy of data: If multiple data marts are used, the same data may be replicated in multiple data marts which can lead to higher storage and management costs.
- Limited visibility: Since data marts only contain a subset of enterprise data, they do not provide a broad, enterprise-wide view of data. Some important relationships and insights may be missed.
- Inconsistency: If data marts are not properly integrated, they can contain inconsistent or contradictory information which can lead to erroneous analysis and decisions.
