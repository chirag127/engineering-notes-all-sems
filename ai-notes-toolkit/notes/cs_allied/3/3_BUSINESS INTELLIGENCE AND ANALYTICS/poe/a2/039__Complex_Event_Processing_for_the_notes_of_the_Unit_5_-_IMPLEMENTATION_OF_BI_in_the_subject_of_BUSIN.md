### Complex Event Processing

- Complex Event Processing (CEP) is a method of processing and analyzing streaming data in real-time.
- It detects complex patterns in the streaming data and uses the detected patterns to determine events and take actions.
- CEP uses continuous queries to detect relationships and patterns in the streaming data and identify significant events.
- The key capabilities of CEP are:
-- Event Correlation: Analyzing and correlating multiple events and streams to infer complex events or patterns.
-- Pattern Matching: Detecting specific patterns occurring in the events and data streams.
-- Sequencing: Detecting sequences and trends in the events.
-- Causality: Identifying cause and effect relationships between events.
-- Timing: Identifying timing relationships between events such as delays, durations, and frequency of events.
- The major applications of CEP are:
-- Fraud Detection: Identifying fraud patterns in banking transactions or credit card usage.
-- Algorithmic Trading: Detecting trading opportunities and patterns in the financial markets.
-- Network Monitoring: Detecting network anomalies, attacks, and performance issues.
-- Business Activity Monitoring: Monitoring business processes and detecting complex business events and situations.
-- IoT: Processing and analyzing streams of data from sensors and devices in the Internet of Things.
