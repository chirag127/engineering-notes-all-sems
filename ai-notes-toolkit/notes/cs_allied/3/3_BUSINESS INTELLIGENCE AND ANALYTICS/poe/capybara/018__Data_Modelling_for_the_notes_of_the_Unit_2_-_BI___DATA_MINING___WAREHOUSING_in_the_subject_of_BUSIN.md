### Data Modelling for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

Data modelling is a crucial step in building a well-designed data warehouse. Here are some key points to consider when creating data models:

- **Understand the business requirements:** Before creating a data model, it is important to understand the business requirements. This includes identifying the types of data that will be collected and stored in the data warehouse, as well as the business rules that will govern how the data is used.

- **Choose a data modelling technique:** There are several data modelling techniques available, including entity-relationship diagrams, dimensional modelling, and data flow diagrams. Choose the technique that is best suited to the needs of the business and the data being collected.

- **Identify entities and attributes:** Identify the key entities and attributes that will be included in the data model. Entities are the objects or concepts that will be represented in the data model, while attributes are the characteristics of those entities.

- **Create relationships:** Once the entities and attributes have been identified, create relationships between them. Relationships define how the entities are related to each other and how they interact.

- **Normalize the data:** Normalization is the process of organizing the data in a way that minimizes redundancy and ensures data consistency. This is typically done by breaking down large tables into smaller, more manageable tables.

- **Test the data model:** Test the data model to ensure that it meets the business requirements and is capable of handling the expected data volumes. This includes testing the model for scalability, performance, and data integrity.

By following these key points, you can create a well-designed data model that supports the needs of your business and enables effective data mining and analysis.
