## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

The following points outline the BI – Data Analytics Process:

- **Data Collection:** This is the first step in the BI - Data Analytics Process, where data is collected from various sources such as databases, spreadsheets, and cloud applications. The data collected should be relevant and accurate for successful analysis.

- **Data Analysis and Preparation:** In this step, the collected data is transformed into a usable format for analysis. This includes cleaning, transforming, and structuring the data. It is important to ensure that the data is free of errors and duplicates.

- **Data Modeling:** This is where data is organized into a model that can be used for analysis. The model should be designed to meet the needs of the business and to support the goals of the analysis.

- **Data Visualization:** This step involves creating visual representations of the data to help identify patterns, trends, and insights. This is important for communicating the results of the analysis to stakeholders.

- **Data Interpretation:** In this final step, the insights gained from the analysis are interpreted to help make informed decisions. This includes identifying trends, patterns, and relationships in the data, and using this information to make predictions or inform business strategy.

Overall, the BI - Data Analytics Process is a crucial tool for businesses to gain insights and make informed decisions. By collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data, businesses can identify trends, patterns, and opportunities that can help them to stay ahead in today's competitive market.
