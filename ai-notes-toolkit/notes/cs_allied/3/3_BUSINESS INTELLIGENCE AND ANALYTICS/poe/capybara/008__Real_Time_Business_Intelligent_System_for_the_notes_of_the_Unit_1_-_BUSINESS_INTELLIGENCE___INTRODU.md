### Real Time Business Intelligent System

Business Intelligence (BI) is a technology-driven process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting business information to support decision-making processes. In this unit, we will be discussing the introduction of Business Intelligence, and in particular, the Real Time Business Intelligent System.

The Real Time Business Intelligent System is a system that enables organizations to make informed decisions in real-time. It is a process of collecting, analyzing, and presenting data in real-time to support decision-making processes. The system consists of various components, such as data warehousing, data mining, and data visualization, which work together to provide real-time insights into business operations.

Below are some key points to understand about the Real Time Business Intelligent System:

- The system collects data from various sources, such as transactional databases, social media, and other external sources, in real-time.
- The data is processed and analyzed using various algorithms and techniques to identify patterns and trends.
- The system provides real-time insights into business operations, allowing organizations to make informed decisions quickly.
- Real-time insights enable organizations to respond to changing market conditions and customer needs promptly.
- The system provides various data visualization tools, such as charts, graphs, and dashboards, to represent data in a user-friendly format.
- The system requires a robust infrastructure to support real-time data processing and analysis, such as high-speed networks, high-performance storage, and powerful servers.
- The Real Time Business Intelligent System can be customized to meet the specific needs of an organization, such as forecasting, inventory management, and customer analysis.

In conclusion, the Real Time Business Intelligent System is a game-changer for organizations that require real-time insights into their business operations. It provides a competitive advantage by enabling organizations to make informed decisions quickly and respond to changing market conditions promptly. The system requires a robust infrastructure and can be customized to meet the specific needs of an organization.
