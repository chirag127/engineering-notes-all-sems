
### Data Reduction – Sampling

Data reduction is a process of reducing the size of a dataset by removing redundant or irrelevant information. This process is used to reduce the complexity of a dataset and make it easier to analyze. Sampling is a type of data reduction technique that involves taking a subset of data from a larger dataset. It is used to reduce the size of the dataset while still preserving the characteristics of the original data.

There are two main types of sampling:

- Random sampling: This is a method of selecting a sample from a population based on chance. It is a commonly used method because it ensures that the sample is representative of the population.

- Stratified sampling: This is a method of selecting a sample from a population by grouping the population into different strata (or groups). It is used when it is important to ensure that the sample is representative of the population.

Data reduction and sampling are important techniques for Business Intelligence and Analytics. They can be used to reduce the complexity of a dataset and make it easier to analyze. They can also be used to ensure that the sample used for analysis is representative of the population.
