
### Iris Datasets for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. The Iris Dataset is a popular dataset used in machine learning and data analytics. It contains 150 observations of iris flowers from three different species.

2. The dataset includes four features for each observation: sepal length, sepal width, petal length and petal width.

3. The data can be used to train a model to classify the species of iris flower based on the four features.

4. The dataset is commonly used to demonstrate basic machine learning techniques, such as linear and logistic regression, decision trees, and support vector machines.

5. The dataset is also useful for exploring data analytics techniques, such as clustering and dimensionality reduction.

6. The Iris Dataset is a useful tool for understanding the fundamentals of machine learning and data analytics.
