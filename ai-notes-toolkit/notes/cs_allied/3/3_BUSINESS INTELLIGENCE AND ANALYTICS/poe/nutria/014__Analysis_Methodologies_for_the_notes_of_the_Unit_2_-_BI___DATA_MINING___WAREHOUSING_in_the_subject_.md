
### Analysis Methodologies for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Data Exploration: This is the process of understanding the data by looking at its characteristics such as min, max, mean, variance, correlation, etc. This helps to identify any outliers or patterns in the data.

2. Data Pre-processing: This is the process of cleaning and transforming the data to make it more suitable for analysis. This includes tasks such as missing value imputation, normalization, outlier removal, etc.

3. Data Mining: This is the process of extracting useful information from the data. This includes techniques such as classification, clustering, association rule mining, etc.

4. Data Warehousing: This is the process of storing and organizing the data in a way that it can be used for analysis. This includes techniques such as dimensional modeling, star schemas, etc.

5. Decision Support Systems: This is the process of using the data to make decisions. This includes techniques such as data visualization, predictive analytics, etc.
