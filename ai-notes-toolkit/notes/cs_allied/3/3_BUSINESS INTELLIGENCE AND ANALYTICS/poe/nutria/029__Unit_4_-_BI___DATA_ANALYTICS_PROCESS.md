
## Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is a process of gathering, analyzing, and interpreting data to make better decisions.
2. Data Analytics is the process of analyzing data to identify patterns and trends in order to make informed decisions.
3. BI and Data Analytics can be used to gain insights into customer behavior, market trends, and internal operations.
4. The data analytics process typically involves the following steps:
    - Data Collection: Collecting the data from various sources.
    - Data Preparation: Cleaning, transforming, and organizing the data.
    - Data Analysis: Analyzing the data to identify patterns and trends.
    - Data Visualization: Presenting the data in a meaningful way.
5. BI and Data Analytics can be used to improve customer service, enhance marketing campaigns, and optimize internal operations.
