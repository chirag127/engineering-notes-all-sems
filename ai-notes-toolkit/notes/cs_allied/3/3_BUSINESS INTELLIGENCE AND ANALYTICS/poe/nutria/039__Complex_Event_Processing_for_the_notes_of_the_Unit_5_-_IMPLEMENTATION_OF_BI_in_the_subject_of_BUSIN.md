
### Complex Event Processing

Complex Event Processing (CEP) is a technology used to detect patterns in streams of data and generate meaningful insights. CEP is used to detect events that occur over time, such as trends and correlations. It is used to analyze data in real time and to detect patterns that may otherwise go unnoticed.

CEP is used in Business Intelligence (BI) and Analytics to detect changes in data that may indicate a need for action. For example, CEP can be used to detect changes in customer behavior, such as an increased usage of a particular product or service, or to detect changes in market trends.

CEP can be used to detect patterns in data that may indicate a need for action, such as a change in customer behavior or market trends. By analyzing data in real time, CEP can provide insights that can be used to make informed decisions.

CEP can be used to detect correlations between different data sets, such as customer behavior and market trends. By correlating data sets, CEP can provide insights that can be used to make decisions.

CEP can also be used to detect anomalies in data, such as fraudulent transactions or suspicious patterns of behavior. By detecting anomalies in data, CEP can provide insights that can be used to identify potential risks and take appropriate action.
