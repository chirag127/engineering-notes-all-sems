
### Predictive for the Notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the Subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) and Data Analytics (DA) are two related, yet distinct, processes. BI is the process of collecting and analyzing data to gain insights into the performance of an organization. DA is the process of using those insights to make predictions about the future.

2. The BI process begins with data collection. Data can come from internal sources such as customer databases, financial records, and operational systems, or from external sources such as market research and surveys.

3. Once data is collected, it is analyzed to identify patterns and trends. This analysis can be done manually or through the use of software tools.

4. The insights gained from the analysis are then used to make predictions about the future. This can be done through the use of predictive analytics tools such as machine learning algorithms.

5. Finally, the predictions are used to inform business decisions. This can involve optimizing existing processes, developing new products, or expanding into new markets.
