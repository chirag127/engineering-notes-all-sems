
### Representation of Input Data

* Input data can be represented in various ways, depending on the type of data and the intended purpose.
* Structured data is data that can be represented in a structured form, such as a database table or a spreadsheet. Structured data is often used for analysis and decision making.
* Unstructured data is data that cannot be represented in a structured form, such as text, images, audio, and video. Unstructured data is often used for discovery and exploration.
* Semi-structured data is data that can be partially structured, such as XML or JSON. Semi-structured data is often used for integration and linking of data.
* Data mining is the process of extracting useful information from large datasets. It involves the use of algorithms and techniques to identify patterns and relationships in data.
* Data warehousing is the process of storing large amounts of data in a central repository. Data warehouses can be used for analysis, reporting, and decision making.
