
### Real Time Business Intelligent System 

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is a set of technologies, processes, and applications that are used to collect, analyze, and present data in order to make better decisions. 
2. BI systems are designed to help organizations gain insights from their data, enabling them to make better decisions and optimize their operations. 
3. BI systems can be used to analyze data from a variety of sources, including structured and unstructured data, and to create models that can be used to predict future outcomes.

7. RTBI systems are designed to process data quickly, so that decisions can be made quickly and accurately. 
8. RTBI systems can be used in a variety of applications, including customer relationship management, supply chain management, and financial analysis.
