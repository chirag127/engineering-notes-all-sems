
## Unit 1 - BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE – INTRODUCTION

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is a set of tools, technologies, and processes that help organizations to collect, store, access, analyze, and present data in order to make better decisions.

2. BI can be used to identify trends, discover new insights, and improve operational efficiency. It can also be used to optimize customer service, increase sales, and identify new opportunities.

3. BI is based on the idea that data should be collected, stored, and analyzed in order to gain insights into the performance of the organization.

4. BI tools are used to collect, store, and analyze data from multiple sources, including databases, spreadsheets, and applications.

5. BI solutions can be used to create dashboards and reports that provide information about the performance of the organization.

6. BI can also be used to create predictive models that can be used to anticipate future trends and events.

7. BI is not a one-time solution; it is an ongoing process that requires careful planning and monitoring.
