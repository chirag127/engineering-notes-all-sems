
# Knowledge Centered Support (KCS)

KCS is a methodology for creating, managing and using knowledge in customer service and support. It is based on the idea that the most effective and efficient way to solve customer problems is to use knowledge that already exists in the organization. KCS is an approach to knowledge management that emphasizes the use of knowledge to improve customer service and support.

KCS involves a set of practices and processes that enable organizations to capture, organize, and use knowledge to solve customer problems. It is based on the idea that knowledge is the most effective and efficient way to solve customer problems. 

The goal of KCS is to create an environment in which customer service and support teams can easily access and use knowledge to solve customer problems. KCS also seeks to create an environment in which knowledge is constantly updated and improved.

KCS is based on the following core principles:

* Knowledge is the most effective and efficient way to solve customer problems
* Knowledge should be organized in a way that makes it easy to find and use
* Knowledge should be constantly updated and improved
* Knowledge should be shared across the organization
* Knowledge should be used to improve customer service and support processes
* Knowledge should be used to create a culture of learning and continuous improvement
