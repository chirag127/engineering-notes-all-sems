

### ETL Process – Role of ETL for the notes of the Unit 2 - BI – DATA MINING & WAREHOUSING in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. ETL stands for Extract, Transform, and Load. It is a process used to move data from one system to another.

2. ETL is used to collect data from multiple sources and then transform it into a format that is suitable for analysis and reporting.

3. ETL is used to store data in a data warehouse or other data repository. This allows users to access and analyze data from multiple sources in one place.

4. ETL is also used to clean and standardize data, as well as to detect and remove errors and inconsistencies.

5. ETL is essential for business intelligence and analytics, as it allows users to access and analyze data from multiple sources in one place. This allows them to make informed decisions based on accurate data.
