
### Metadata for the notes of the Unit 5 - IMPLEMENTATION OF BI in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is a set of techniques and tools that organizations use to collect, store, analyze and present data for business decision-making. 
2. BI technologies provide historical, current and predictive views of business operations, enabling organizations to make more informed decisions.
3. BI solutions can help organizations to identify trends, spot opportunities and solve problems.
4. Common BI tools include data warehouses, data visualization, dashboards, reporting and analytics software.
5. The implementation of BI requires the integration of data sources, the development of data models, and the deployment of BI applications.
6. The success of a BI implementation depends on the quality of data, the selection of the right tools, and the ability to use the data to make informed decisions.
