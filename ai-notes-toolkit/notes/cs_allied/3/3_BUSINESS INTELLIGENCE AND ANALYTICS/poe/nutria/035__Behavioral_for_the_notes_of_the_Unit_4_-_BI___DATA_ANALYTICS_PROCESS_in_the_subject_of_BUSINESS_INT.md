
### Behavioral for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

1. Business Intelligence (BI) is the process of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data to make informed decisions.
2. Data Analytics is the process of extracting insights from data, such as trends, correlations, and patterns.
3. The BI and Data Analytics process is a cycle of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting data to inform decisions.
4. Data is collected from internal and external sources, such as customer surveys, market research, and sales data.
5. Data is analyzed to identify patterns, trends, and correlations.
6. Data is interpreted to gain insights and inform decisions.
7. The BI and Data Analytics process is iterative, with data being collected, analyzed, and interpreted multiple times to gain deeper insights.
8. BI and Data Analytics can be used to gain insights into customer behavior, identify opportunities for growth, and improve decision-making.
