### Perspective for the notes of the Unit 4 - BI – DATA ANALYTICS PROCESS in the subject of BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND ANALYTICS KCS

In this unit, we will be discussing the data analytics process in the context of Business Intelligence and Analytics. It is important to understand the different stages of the process and how they contribute to the overall goal of extracting insights from data. Here are some key perspectives to keep in mind while studying this unit:

1. The data analytics process is iterative: It is important to remember that the data analytics process is not a linear one, but rather an iterative one. You will need to go back and forth between the different stages until you arrive at a satisfactory result.

2. The importance of data preparation: One of the most important stages in the data analytics process is data preparation. This involves cleaning, transforming and structuring the data in a way that makes it suitable for analysis. It is important to pay close attention to this stage as it can have a significant impact on the quality of the insights that are generated.

3. The role of visualization: Visualization is a key component of the data analytics process. It helps to communicate the insights that are generated in a way that is easy to understand and interpret. It is important to choose the right type of visualization for the data being analyzed and to ensure that it is clear and concise.

4. The importance of domain knowledge: While data analytics tools and techniques are important, it is equally important to have a good understanding of the domain in which you are working. This will help you to ask the right questions, identify the relevant data sources and interpret the results in a meaningful way.

5. The need for collaboration: Data analytics is a team sport. It is important to collaborate with others in order to get the best results. This includes working with domain experts, data engineers, and other data analysts. By working together, you can ensure that you are generating insights that are both accurate and actionable.

In summary, the data analytics process is an iterative one that requires careful attention to each stage. Data preparation, visualization, domain knowledge, and collaboration are all important factors to keep in mind when working with data. By understanding these perspectives, you will be better equipped to generate insights that can drive business decisions.
