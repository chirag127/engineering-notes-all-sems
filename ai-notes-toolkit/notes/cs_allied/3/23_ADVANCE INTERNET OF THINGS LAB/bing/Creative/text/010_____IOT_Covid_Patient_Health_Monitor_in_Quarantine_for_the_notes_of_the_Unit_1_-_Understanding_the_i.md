### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine is a system that uses internet-connected devices and sensors to measure and transmit the vital signs of covid-19 patients who are isolated at home or in a facility.
- The system aims to reduce the risk of infection for health workers and other patients, as well as to provide real-time data and alerts for timely intervention and treatment.
- The system consists of the following components:
  - A wearable device that measures the body temperature, pulse rate, and oxygen saturation of the patient using a temperature sensor, a heartbeat sensor, and a pulse oximeter sensor respectively. The device can also have a GPS module to track the location of the patient and a panic button to send an emergency signal.
  - A gateway device that collects the data from the wearable device and sends it to a cloud server using a wireless communication protocol such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular network.
  - A cloud server that stores and processes the data using machine learning algorithms and provides a web or mobile interface for the health workers and the patient to access and visualize the data. The server can also send notifications and alerts to the health workers and the patient based on predefined thresholds and rules.
  - A web or mobile application that allows the health workers and the patient to monitor the health status, view the historical data, and communicate with each other. The application can also provide feedback and guidance to the patient based on the data and the medical advice.
- The system can provide the following benefits:
  - It can reduce the burden on the health infrastructure and the need for frequent visits to the hospital or clinic.
  - It can improve the quality of care and the patient satisfaction by providing continuous and personalized monitoring and feedback.
  - It can enhance the safety and the well-being of the health workers and the patient by minimizing the exposure and the transmission of the virus.
  - It can support the decision making and the intervention of the health workers by providing accurate and timely data and alerts.
  - It can facilitate the research and the analysis of the covid-19 pandemic by providing large and diverse data sets.
