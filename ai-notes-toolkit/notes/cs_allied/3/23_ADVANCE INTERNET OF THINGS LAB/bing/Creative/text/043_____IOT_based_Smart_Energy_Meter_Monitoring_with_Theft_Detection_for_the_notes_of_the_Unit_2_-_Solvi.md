### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that aims to reduce the energy crisis and power theft by properly monitoring the energy consumption and avoiding energy wastage.
- The system uses smart energy meters that are connected to the Internet of Things (IoT) platform and can send and receive data from a central server or a mobile application .
- The system can detect and alert the power theft by using statistical regression methods or power line communication (PLC) techniques that compare the data from the consumer and distribution end smart meters  .
- The system can also provide real-time smart meter monitoring, billing, load management, and energy saving tips to the consumers and the utility providers   .
- The system can improve the efficiency, reliability, and security of the power distribution network and reduce the losses and costs due to power theft   .
