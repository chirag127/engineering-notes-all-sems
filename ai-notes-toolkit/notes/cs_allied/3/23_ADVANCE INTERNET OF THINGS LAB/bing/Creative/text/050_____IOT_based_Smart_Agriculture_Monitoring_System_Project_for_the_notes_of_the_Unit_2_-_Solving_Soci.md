### IOT based Smart Agriculture Monitoring System Project

- The project aims to use Internet of Things (IoT) and automation to monitor and control various environmental factors that affect the growth and yield of crops.
- The project consists of a network of sensors that collect data from the agricultural field and send it to a microcontroller or a cloud platform using wireless communication protocols.
- The sensors used in the project include temperature sensor, humidity sensor, soil moisture sensor, water level sensor, light sensor, and camera.
- The microcontroller or the cloud platform processes the data and sends commands to actuators such as water pump, LED strip, or MMS service to control the irrigation, lighting, or alerting system of the field.
- The project can help farmers to improve the efficiency and productivity of their crops, reduce water wastage, and prevent diseases and pests.
