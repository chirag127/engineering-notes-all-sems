### IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino

- Internet of Things (IoT) is the networking of physical things that can communicate with the help of sensors, electronics, software, and connectivity.
- Arduino is an open-source platform that consists of a microcontroller board and a software development environment that can be used to program and control the board.
- LPG (Liquified Petroleum Gas) is a common fuel used in households, industries and other places, but it can also pose a fire and explosion hazard if it leaks and accumulates in the air.
- An IOT based intelligent gas leakage detector using Arduino is a device that can sense the presence of LPG gas in the air using an MQ5 gas sensor, and send the data to an IOT module, such as ESP8266, that can transmit the information to a remote server or a mobile application  .
- The device can also trigger an alarm or a buzzer to alert the users of the gas leakage, and activate a relay to switch off the gas valve or other appliances to prevent further damage  .
- The device can be powered by a battery or a USB cable, and can be installed in homes, hotels, LPG gas storage areas, or any other places where gas leakage is a potential risk  .
- The device can be configured to set the minimum and maximum parameters for the gas level, and can also display the gas level on an LCD screen or an LED indicator .
- The device can be connected to the internet using WIFI or GSM, and can use protocols such as MQTT, HTTP, or TCP/IP to communicate with the server or the application .
- The device can also use cloud services such as ThingSpeak, Firebase, or Blynk to store and visualize the data, and send notifications or alerts to the users via email, SMS, or push messages .
- The device can be programmed using Arduino IDE, and can use libraries such as ESP8266WiFi, PubSubClient, LiquidCrystal, etc. to interface with the hardware and the software components .
