### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue is a system that aims to prevent the spread of COVID-19 by measuring the distance between individuals in queues and alerting them if they violate the social distancing norms  .
- The system consists of a four-wheel drive robot that follows a line on the ground to move along with the queue. The robot is equipped with an ultrasonic sensor, a buzzer, an LED, and a camera .
- The ultrasonic sensor is used to measure the distance between the robot and the person in front of it. If the distance is less than the predefined threshold (for example, 2 meters), the buzzer will sound and the LED will blink to warn the person to maintain a safe distance .
- The camera is used to capture the images of the queue and send them to a cloud server via Wi-Fi. The cloud server uses image processing and machine learning techniques to count the number of people in the queue, estimate their distances, and detect any violations of social distancing rules   .
- The cloud server also provides a web interface for the authorities to monitor the queue status, view the images, and generate reports and statistics   .
- The system is very essential for banks, government offices, shopping malls, schools, and theatres, among others, where long queues can last for hours every day .
- The system is based on the Internet of Things (IoT) concept, which is the interconnection of physical devices, sensors, and actuators with the internet and cloud services   .
- The system is an example of how IoT can be used to solve societal problems with the help of technology, data, and intelligence   .
