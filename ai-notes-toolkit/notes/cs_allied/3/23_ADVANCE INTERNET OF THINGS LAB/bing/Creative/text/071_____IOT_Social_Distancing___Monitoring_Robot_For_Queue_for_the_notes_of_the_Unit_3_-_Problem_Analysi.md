### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The aim of this project is to design and implement a robot that can monitor and enforce social distancing in queues, such as in banks, malls, schools, etc.   
- The robot is based on the Internet of Things (IoT) technology, which enables it to communicate with a cloud server and a mobile app for data storage and visualization.   
- The robot consists of the following components:
  - A four-wheel drive system with a line follower module to move along the queue and detect the line markings on the floor.  
  - An ultrasonic sensor to measure the distance between the robot and the person in front of it.  
  - A buzzer and an LED to alert the person if they are too close to the robot or the person behind them.  
  - A camera to capture the images of the queue and the faces of the people.  
  - A Raspberry Pi to process the sensor data and the images, and to send them to the cloud server via Wi-Fi.   
  - A cloud server to store and analyze the data, and to generate reports and statistics on the social distancing compliance.  
  - A mobile app to display the data and the reports, and to allow the user to control the robot remotely.  
- The robot works as follows:
  - The robot is placed at the end of the queue and follows the line markings on the floor.  
  - The robot measures the distance between itself and the person in front of it using the ultrasonic sensor.  
  - If the distance is less than the predefined threshold (e.g. 2 meters), the robot beeps the buzzer and flashes the LED to warn the person to maintain the social distance.  
  - The robot also measures the distance between the person in front of it and the person behind it using the camera and a face detection algorithm.  
  - If the distance is less than the threshold, the robot sends a message to the mobile app to notify the user.  
  - The robot captures the images of the queue and the faces of the people and sends them to the cloud server along with the sensor data.  
  - The cloud server stores and analyzes the data, and generates reports and statistics on the social distancing compliance, such as the number of violations, the average distance, the peak hours, etc.  
  - The mobile app displays the data and the reports, and allows the user to control the robot remotely, such as changing the threshold, turning on/off the buzzer or the LED, etc.  
- The benefits of this project are:
  - It can help prevent the spread of Covid-19 by ensuring social distancing in queues, which are common places where people gather and interact.  
  - It can reduce the workload and the risk of infection for the staff who are responsible for monitoring and enforcing social distancing in queues.  
  - It can provide useful data and insights on the social distancing behavior and compliance of the people, which can help improve the public health policies and strategies.  
  - It can enhance the awareness and the responsibility of the people to follow the social distancing guidelines and to protect themselves and others from Covid-19.
