### IOT Prison Break Monitoring & Alerting System

- The system is designed to track the location and activities of the inmates in a prison using radio frequency (RF) technology and Internet of Things (IoT).
- The system consists of a microcontroller-based circuit, RF trackers, RF receivers, and an online alerting portal.
- Each inmate is equipped with a RF tracker that transmits a unique code wirelessly to the RF receivers installed in the prison premises.
- The microcontroller scans through all the RF codes and compares them with the data stored in its memory.
- If any RF code is missing or out of range, it means that the inmate has escaped or attempted to escape from the prison.
- The microcontroller then sends the details of the escaped inmate to the online alerting portal using IoT.
- The online alerting portal displays the alert message and sounds an alarm to notify the authorities and the prison staff.
- The system aims to prevent prison breaks and enhance the security and safety of the prison.
