### IOT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance

- Floods are natural disasters that can cause severe damage to property and lives. They can also disrupt the normal functioning of society and economy.
- Early detection and avoidance of floods can help reduce the impact of floods and save lives and resources.
- IOT (Internet of Things) is a technology that enables the interconnection of devices and sensors over the internet, allowing data collection, analysis and communication.
- IOT can be used for early flood detection and avoidance by deploying sensor nodes at specific flood-prone locations, such as rivers, dams, bridges, etc.
- The sensor nodes can measure various parameters, such as water level, rainfall, soil moisture, temperature, etc., and send the data to a central server or cloud platform via wireless communication.
- The server or cloud platform can process the data using algorithms and models, and generate alerts and warnings based on predefined thresholds and criteria.
- The alerts and warnings can be sent to the authorities, stakeholders and the public via various channels, such as SMS, email, social media, etc.
- The alerts and warnings can help the authorities to take preventive and mitigative measures, such as evacuating people, closing roads, diverting water, etc.
- The alerts and warnings can also help the public to avoid risky areas, prepare for emergencies, and seek help if needed.
- IOT-based early flood detection and avoidance can improve the efficiency and accuracy of flood management, and enhance the resilience and safety of the society.
