### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

- An IoT temperature and mask scan entry system is a device that uses sensors and cameras to detect the body temperature and mask status of a person who wants to enter a building or a restricted area.
- The system is designed to prevent the spread of COVID-19 and other infectious diseases by allowing only healthy and masked people to enter.
- The system consists of the following components:
  - A contactless temperature scanner that uses infrared or thermal sensors to measure the forehead temperature of a person. The scanner has a high accuracy and can display the temperature on a screen or a speaker.
  - A mask detector that uses a camera and an image processing algorithm to recognize if a person is wearing a mask or not. The detector can also alert the person to wear a mask if they are not wearing one.
  - A gate or a barrier that is connected to the scanner and the detector. The gate or the barrier can open or close depending on the temperature and mask status of the person. The gate or the barrier can also be integrated with a biometric or a RFID system for authentication and access control.
  - A Raspberry Pi or a similar microcontroller that acts as the brain of the system. The Raspberry Pi can communicate with the scanner, the detector, and the gate or the barrier using GPIO pins or wireless protocols. The Raspberry Pi can also store the data and send it to a cloud server or a dashboard for monitoring and analysis.
- The system works as follows:
  - A person approaches the system and stands in front of the scanner and the detector.
  - The scanner measures the person's temperature and displays it on the screen or the speaker. The detector captures the person's image and checks if they are wearing a mask or not.
  - If the person's temperature is within the normal range and they are wearing a mask, the gate or the barrier opens and allows the person to enter. The Raspberry Pi records the person's details and sends them to the cloud server or the dashboard.
  - If the person's temperature is above the normal range or they are not wearing a mask, the gate or the barrier remains closed and prevents the person from entering. The Raspberry Pi alerts the person to check their temperature or wear a mask and sends an alarm to the cloud server or the dashboard.
- The system has the following advantages:
  - It is a low-cost and easy-to-install solution that can be used in various settings such as schools, offices, hospitals, malls, airports, etc.
  - It is a contactless and automated solution that reduces the risk of cross-contamination and human error.
  - It is a smart and connected solution that can provide real-time data and insights on the health and safety of the people and the environment.
