### IOT Garbage Monitoring Using Raspberry Pi

- IOT Garbage Monitoring Using Raspberry Pi is a project that aims to solve the problem of waste management and disposal in an efficient and smart way.
- The project uses ultrasonic sensors, Raspberry Pi, LCD display, buzzer, and IoT service to monitor the garbage level of the bins and alert the user when the bins are full or need to be emptied.
- The project works as follows:
  - The ultrasonic sensors are fixed over the bins and measure the distance between the sensor and the waste. The sensors work on the principle of Doppler's effect, which states that the frequency of a sound wave changes as the source or the observer moves.
  - The Raspberry Pi is a digital controller that receives the data from the sensors and processes it to calculate the garbage level of the bins. The Raspberry Pi also connects to the IoT service via Wi-Fi or Ethernet and sends the data to the remote server or cloud.
  - The LCD display shows the garbage level of the bins in percentage and the status of the IoT connection. The buzzer produces a sound when the bins are full or need to be emptied.
  - The IoT service allows the user to access the data from anywhere and anytime using a web browser or a mobile app. The user can also set the threshold level for the bins and receive notifications or alerts when the bins reach that level.
- The project has the following advantages:
  - It reduces the human effort and cost involved in waste management and disposal.
  - It prevents the overflow of garbage and the spread of diseases and pollution.
  - It optimizes the garbage collection and transportation process by avoiding unnecessary trips and routes.
  - It improves the cleanliness and hygiene of the environment and the society.
- The project can be used for the monitoring and management of garbage in various settings, such as:
  - Big organizations and industries where dozens of bins are present.
  - Homes for the purpose of automatic garbage disposal reminders.
  - Smart cities and communities for the purpose of efficient and sustainable waste management.
