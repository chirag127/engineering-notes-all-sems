### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a project that aims to measure and transmit water quality data using an RC boat equipped with sensors and an IOT module  .
- The project is relevant for solving societal problems with the help of IOT, as it can help to monitor and prevent water pollution, which is a major environmental and health issue  .
- The main components of the project are:
  - An RC boat with a motorized propeller system, a battery, and a waterproof casing    .
  - A water quality sensor module that can measure parameters such as pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, temperature, etc    .
  - An IOT module that can transmit the sensor data to an online server using Wi-Fi or GSM network    .
  - An RC remote that can control the movement of the boat and receive the sensor data on a display or a smartphone app    .
- The main steps of the project are:
  - Assembling the RC boat with the sensor and IOT modules, and testing the functionality and waterproofing    .
  - Developing the online server and the smartphone app to store and visualize the sensor data    .
  - Deploying the RC boat in a water body and controlling it with the RC remote    .
  - Collecting and analyzing the sensor data to assess the water quality and identify the sources and causes of pollution    .
- The main benefits of the project are:
  - It can provide real-time and accurate data on water quality, which can help to take preventive and corrective actions    .
  - It can cover a large area and access hard-to-reach locations, which can improve the efficiency and effectiveness of water monitoring    .
  - It can reduce the cost and risk of manual sampling and testing, which can enhance the safety and convenience of water monitoring    .
  - It can raise awareness and educate the public about the importance and challenges of water quality and pollution    .
