# IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

- An IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is a device that uses a contactless temperature scanner and a camera to capture images of people who want to enter a building or a facility.
- The device is connected to a gate-like structure that prevents entry if a high temperature or the absence of a mask is detected.
- The device is also connected to a cloud server that stores and analyzes the data collected by the device, such as the number of people scanned, the temperature readings, the mask status, and the entry status.
- The device can be controlled and monitored remotely through a web or mobile application that displays the data and allows the user to change the settings and parameters of the device, such as the temperature threshold, the mask detection algorithm, and the gate operation mode.
- The device is powered by a battery or a solar panel that can be recharged or replaced as needed.
- The device is designed to be portable and flexible, so that it can be placed in or in front of any building or facility that requires temperature and mask screening for COVID-19 prevention.
- The device is based on a Raspberry Pi system that uses a thermal and video camera, a temperature sensor, a servo motor, and a touch screen to perform the functions of the device.
- The device uses machine learning and computer vision techniques to detect the face and the mask of the person, and to measure the temperature accurately and reliably.
- The device can also alert the user or the authorities if a person with a high temperature or without a mask tries to enter the building or facility, or if the device malfunctions or runs out of power.
- The device is an affordable and effective solution that aims to increase COVID-19 indoor safety and reduce the risk of transmission and infection.
