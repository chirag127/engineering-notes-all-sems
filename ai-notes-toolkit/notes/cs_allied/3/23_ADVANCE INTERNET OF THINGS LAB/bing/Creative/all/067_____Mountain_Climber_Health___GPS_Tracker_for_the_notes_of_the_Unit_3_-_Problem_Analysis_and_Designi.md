# Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

## Problem Analysis

- Mountain climbing is a challenging and adventurous sport that requires physical fitness, mental toughness, and proper equipment.
- Mountain climbers face various risks and hazards such as altitude sickness, hypothermia, frostbite, avalanches, rock falls, and getting lost or stranded.
- Mountain climbers need to monitor their health and location constantly and communicate with their team members and base camp for safety and coordination.
- Traditional methods of health and location tracking such as pulse oximeters, compasses, maps, and radios are not sufficient, reliable, or convenient for mountain climbers.
- There is a need for a smart and integrated solution that can track the vital signs and GPS location of mountain climbers in real time and alert them or their team in case of any emergency or deviation.

## Designing a Solution

- A possible solution is to design a wearable device that can measure the heart rate, blood oxygen level, and body temperature of the mountain climber and transmit the data to a cloud server via a wireless network.
- The device can also receive the GPS coordinates from a satellite and send them to the cloud server along with the health data.
- The cloud server can store, process, and display the health and location data of the mountain climber on a web or mobile application that can be accessed by the team members and the base camp.
- The cloud server can also analyze the data and send alerts or notifications to the mountain climber or the team in case of any abnormality or danger such as low oxygen level, high heart rate, low body temperature, or deviation from the planned route.
- The device can also have a panic button that the mountain climber can press in case of any emergency to send a distress signal to the team and the base camp.
