# IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue is a project that aims to prevent the spread of COVID-19 by enforcing social distancing rules in public places where people form queues, such as banks, malls, schools, theatres, etc.  
- The robot consists of a four-wheel drive system, a line follower sensor, an ultrasonic sensor, a buzzer, an LCD display, and a Wi-Fi module. 
- The robot follows a line drawn on the ground along the queue and measures the distance between the people using the ultrasonic sensor. 
- If the distance is less than the recommended value (e.g., 6 feet), the robot alerts the people by sounding the buzzer and displaying a warning message on the LCD. 
- The robot also sends the data to a cloud server using the Wi-Fi module, where it can be monitored and analyzed by the authorities.  
- The robot can help reduce the risk of infection and transmission of the virus by encouraging people to maintain a safe distance from each other. 
- The robot can also provide useful statistics and insights on the compliance and effectiveness of the social distancing measures.
