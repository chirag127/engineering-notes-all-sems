# IOT based Smart Agriculture Monitoring System Project

## Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- The IOT based Smart Agriculture Monitoring System Project is a project that aims to use the Internet of Things (IOT) technology to monitor and control various parameters of agriculture fields, such as temperature, humidity, soil moisture, water level, and light intensity  .
- The project uses different sensors to collect data from the agriculture fields and sends it to a microcontroller, such as Arduino or NodeMCU, which processes the data and communicates with a cloud server or a mobile device using wireless protocols, such as Wi-Fi or GSM  .
- The project also uses actuators, such as water pumps or LED strips, to control the irrigation or lighting of the crops based on the sensor data and the user commands .
- The project can help the farmers to improve the yield and quality of the crops, reduce the water and energy consumption, and prevent the crop diseases and pests by providing real-time information and remote control of the agriculture fields   .
- The project is an example of smart farming, which is an emerging concept that uses IOT sensors and devices to provide information and automation for agriculture.
