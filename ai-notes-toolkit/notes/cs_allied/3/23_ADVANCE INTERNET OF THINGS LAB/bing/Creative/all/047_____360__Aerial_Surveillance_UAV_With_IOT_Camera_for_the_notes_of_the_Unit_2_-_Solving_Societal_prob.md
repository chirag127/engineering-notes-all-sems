# 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

- Aerial surveillance is the key to security and military based operations. It provides real time information on enemy movements which plays a key role in precision strikes  .
- Aerial surveillance can also be used for crowd monitoring, disaster management, wildlife conservation, and other applications that require a wide-angle view of the ground.
- A 360° aerial surveillance UAV with IOT camera is a system that combines a drone, a camera, and a wireless network to capture and transmit high-resolution images and videos from all directions  .
- The system consists of the following components  :
  - A drone or UAV (unmanned aerial vehicle) that can fly autonomously or remotely controlled by a user. The drone should be able to carry the camera and the battery, and have a stable flight performance.
  - A 360° camera that can capture images and videos from all angles. The camera should be rugged, waterproof, and have image stabilization features. An example of such a camera is the Garmin VIRB 360.
  - A wireless network that can transmit the data from the camera to a cloud server or a local device. The network should be reliable, secure, and have a high bandwidth. The network can use Wi-Fi, cellular, or satellite technologies.
- The system works as follows  :
  - The drone is launched from a base station or a mobile platform, and flies over the area of interest.
  - The camera captures images and videos from all directions, and stores them in a memory card or a cloud storage.
  - The wireless network transmits the data from the camera to a cloud server or a local device, where it can be processed, analyzed, and displayed.
  - The user can access the data from a web browser or a mobile app, and view the images and videos in a 360° format. The user can also control the drone and the camera remotely, and adjust the settings and parameters.
- The system has the following advantages  :
  - It provides a comprehensive and immersive view of the ground, which can help in identifying and tracking targets, detecting anomalies, and assessing situations.
  - It reduces the risk of missing important details or blind spots, which can affect the accuracy and effectiveness of the surveillance.
  - It enhances the safety and efficiency of the drone and the camera, as they do not need to rotate or tilt to capture different angles.
  - It enables the user to interact with the data in a more intuitive and engaging way, and to explore the scene from different perspectives.
