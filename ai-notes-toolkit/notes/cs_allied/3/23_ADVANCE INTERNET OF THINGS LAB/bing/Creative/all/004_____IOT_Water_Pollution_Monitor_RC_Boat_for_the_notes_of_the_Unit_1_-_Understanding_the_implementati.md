# IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a project that aims to measure and transmit water quality data using an RC boat equipped with sensors and an IOT module  .
- The project is remote-operated and controlled by an RC remote using which it can be maneuvered accordingly, a motorized propeller system to provide the forward and backward motion, and a rudder system to provide the left and right motion.
- The boat carries relevant sensors to measure water quality parameters such as pH, turbidity, temperature, dissolved oxygen, etc. and transmit them to the remote control terminal wirelessly.
- The remote control terminal displays the water quality data on an LCD screen and also sends them to an IOT server online using a Wi-Fi module .
- The IOT server stores and analyzes the water quality data and provides a web interface for users to access and visualize the data from anywhere .
- The project can help to monitor and maintain the water quality of lakes, rivers, ponds, etc. and alert the authorities in case of any pollution or contamination  .
