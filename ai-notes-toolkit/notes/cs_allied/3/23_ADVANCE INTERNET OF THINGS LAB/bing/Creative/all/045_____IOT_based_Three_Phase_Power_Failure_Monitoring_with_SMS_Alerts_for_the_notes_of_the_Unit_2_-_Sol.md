# IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

- This is a system that monitors the power supply of a three-phase system and alerts the authorized person through SMS when there is a failure in any one of the phases   .
- A three-phase system is a common method of alternating current electric power generation, transmission, and distribution. It consists of three conductors, each carrying a current that is offset by one-third of the cycle from the others.
- A phase loss, also known as a single phasing, occurs when one of the three phases gets lost due to a blown fuse, thermal overload, broken wire, worn contact, or mechanical failure  .
- A phase loss can cause serious damage to the equipment and appliances connected to the three-phase system, such as motors, pumps, compressors, etc. It can also result in reduced efficiency, increased power consumption, overheating, and fire hazards.
- The system uses an IOT device, such as an Arduino or a Raspberry Pi, to measure the voltage of each phase using potential transformers and display the values on an LCD screen .
- The system also uses a GSM module, such as a SIM900 or a SIM800, to send SMS alerts to the predefined mobile number when there is a phase loss .
- The system can be configured by sending commands to the GSM module through a call or an SMS. The commands can include setting the mobile number, the threshold voltage, the delay time, etc.
- The system can be useful for solving societal problems such as preventing power wastage, reducing maintenance costs, enhancing safety, and improving reliability of the power supply.
