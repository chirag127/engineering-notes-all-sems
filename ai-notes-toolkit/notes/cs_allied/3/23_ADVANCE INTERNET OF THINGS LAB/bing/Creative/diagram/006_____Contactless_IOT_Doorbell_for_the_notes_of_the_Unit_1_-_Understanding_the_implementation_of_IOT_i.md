### Contactless IOT Doorbell

- A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to ring a bell without touching it, using a proximity sensor or a camera to detect their presence.
- The device can also send notifications to the homeowner's smartphone or smart speaker, and allow them to see and talk to the visitor remotely, using a microphone and a speaker.
- The device can also integrate with other smart home devices, such as smart locks, lights, or cameras, to provide more security and convenience.
- The device can be powered by batteries, solar panels, or a wired connection, depending on the design and the location of the installation.
- The device can use various communication protocols, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, or cellular, to connect to the internet and the cloud, where the data and the commands are processed and stored.
- The device can use various cloud platforms, such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure, to host the backend services, such as authentication, database, storage, or analytics.
- The device can use various software frameworks, such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, or Node-RED, to program the logic and the functionality of the device, such as sensing, actuating, or communicating.
- The device can use various hardware components, such as ESP32, PIR sensor, camera, buzzer, LED, or LCD, to build the physical structure and the interface of the device, depending on the features and the design of the device.
