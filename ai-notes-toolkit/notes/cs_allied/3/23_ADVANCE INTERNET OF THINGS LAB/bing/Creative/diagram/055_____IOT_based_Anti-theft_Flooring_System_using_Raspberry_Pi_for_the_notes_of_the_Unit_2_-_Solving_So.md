### IOT based Anti-theft Flooring System using Raspberry Pi

- This system is designed to secure and guard the house in the absence of the owner by monitoring the entire floor for movement  .
- The system consists of secure flooring tiles connected with IOT, piezo sensors, a camera, a wifi modem and a Raspberry Pi controller .
- The system is activated when the owner leaves the house and turns it on through a mobile app.
- When the system detects any step on the floor, it sends a signal to the Raspberry Pi controller, which then moves the camera to the area where the movement was detected and captures an image .
- The image is then transmitted over the internet to the owner's email or mobile app, along with an alert message   .
- The owner can then check the image and take appropriate action, such as calling the police or a neighbor.
- The system can also be integrated with other security devices, such as alarms, sirens, locks, etc.
- The system is based on IOT, which enables remote monitoring and control, data analysis and cloud storage.
- The system is cost-effective, easy to install and maintain, and scalable to cover large areas .
- The system can help prevent theft, burglary, trespassing and other crimes, and provide peace of mind to the owner .
