### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- An IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a remote-controlled device that can measure and transmit water quality parameters such as pH, temperature, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, etc. to an online server using wireless communication  .
- The device consists of the following components:
  - A boat chassis with a motorized propeller system and a battery pack.
  - A microcontroller unit (such as Arduino) that controls the boat movement and the sensor readings.
  - A set of water quality sensors (such as pH sensor, temperature sensor, turbidity sensor, dissolved oxygen sensor, etc.) that are attached to the boat and submerged in the water.
  - A wireless communication module (such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GSM, etc.) that sends the sensor data to an online server or a remote terminal.
  - A remote control unit (such as an RC transmitter or a smartphone app) that allows the user to maneuver the boat and view the sensor data in real-time.
- The device can be used for the following purposes:
  - To monitor the water pollution level in various water bodies such as rivers, lakes, ponds, etc. and alert the authorities or the public about the water quality status.
  - To collect water samples from different locations and depths for further analysis and research.
  - To educate the students and the public about the importance of water conservation and environmental protection.
  - To demonstrate the applications and benefits of IOT technology in solving societal problems.
- The device can be improved by adding more features such as:
  - A GPS module that can track the location and movement of the boat and display it on a map.
  - A camera module that can capture images and videos of the water surface and the underwater environment.
  - A solar panel that can charge the battery and extend the operating time of the boat.
  - A water pump that can spray water to clean the sensors and prevent fouling.
  - A data logger that can store the sensor data locally for offline analysis and backup.
  - A display module that can show the sensor data and the boat status on the boat itself.
