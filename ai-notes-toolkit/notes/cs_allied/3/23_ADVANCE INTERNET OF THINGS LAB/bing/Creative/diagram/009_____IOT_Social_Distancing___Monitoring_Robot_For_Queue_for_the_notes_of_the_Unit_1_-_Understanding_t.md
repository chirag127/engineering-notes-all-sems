### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- This is a project that aims to prevent the spread of COVID-19 by enforcing social distancing rules in queues, such as in banks, malls, schools, etc.
- The project uses a four-wheel robot that follows a line along the queue and measures the distance between individuals using an ultrasonic sensor.
- The robot also has a camera and a speaker to capture the images of the queue and alert the violators of social distancing norms.
- The robot is connected to the internet using a Wi-Fi module and sends the data to a cloud platform, such as ThingSpeak, where it can be monitored and analyzed.
- The project also uses a mobile app to control the robot and display the live video feed and the distance readings.
- The project can be implemented using Arduino, ESP8266, HC-SR04, OV7670, and other components.
- The project can help reduce the risk of COVID-19 transmission and raise awareness about the importance of social distancing.
