### Contactless IOT Doorbell

- A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that uses internet of things technology to perform various functions such as ringing the doorbell, scanning the temperature, recognizing the face, and alerting the home owner of the visitors without any physical contact.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can be useful for preventing the spread of infectious diseases such as Covid-19, enhancing the security and convenience of the home, and providing a voice user interface for the visitors and the home owner.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can be designed using various components such as a microcontroller, a camera, a speaker, a temperature sensor, a buzzer, a button, and a display. The microcontroller can be a NodeMCU, a Raspberry Pi, or an Arduino board that can connect to the internet and communicate with other devices and platforms.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can use various platforms and services such as Blynk, Firebase, Twilio, or Telegram to store, process, and display the data from the sensors and the camera, and to send notifications and alerts to the home owner's smartphone or desktop.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can use various algorithms and libraries such as OpenCV, TensorFlow, or Face Recognition to perform face detection and recognition, and to compare the faces with a predefined database of authorized or unauthorized persons.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can use various methods and protocols such as MQTT, HTTP, or WebSockets to transfer the data between the devices and the platforms, and to enable real-time communication and streaming of the video and audio.
- A contactless IOT doorbell can have various features and functionalities such as:

  - Ringing the doorbell when a visitor presses the button or approaches the door.
  - Scanning the temperature of the visitor using a non-contact infrared sensor and displaying it on the screen.
  - Recognizing the face of the visitor using the camera and the face recognition algorithm and displaying the name or the status of the visitor on the screen.
  - Alerting the home owner of the arrival of the visitor using the speaker, the buzzer, or the notification service, and providing the option to accept or reject the visitor.
  - Sending the image, the temperature, and the name or the status of the visitor to the home owner's smartphone or desktop using the internet platform or service.
  - Allowing the home owner to communicate with the visitor using the voice user interface or the chat service, and to control the door lock or the alarm remotely.
  - Logging the data of the visitors and their activities on the online database or the cloud service, and providing the option to view, analyze, or delete the data.
