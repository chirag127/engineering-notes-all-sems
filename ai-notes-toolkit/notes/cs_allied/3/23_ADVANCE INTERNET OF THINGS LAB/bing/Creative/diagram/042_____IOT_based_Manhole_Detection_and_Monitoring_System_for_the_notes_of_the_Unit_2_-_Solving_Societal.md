### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- A drainage monitoring system plays a significant role in keeping towns and cities healthy and clean  .
- Most of the manholes are open without any observation that cause accidents . In India, many cities adopted emptying underground system because it is vital.
- Contamination of fresh water due to problem in sewage drainage system is also a concern. Poor management and monitoring of the manholes can also cause urban floods which are most common in crowded cities.
- IOT based manhole detection and monitoring system is a solution that uses sensors to detect and send alerts to authorities via GSM and GPS module when any manhole crosses its threshold values    .
- This system reduces the death risk of manual scavengers who clean the underground drainage and also benefits the public. It also helps in preventing the overflow of sewage water and maintaining the hygiene of the environment.
- The system consists of the following components  :
  - Water level sensor: To measure the water level in the manhole and detect any blockage or overflow.
  - Gas sensor: To measure the concentration of harmful gases such as methane, carbon monoxide, hydrogen sulfide, etc. in the manhole and alert the workers about the safety conditions.
  - Temperature sensor: To measure the temperature in the manhole and detect any fire or overheating.
  - Vibration sensor: To detect any movement or displacement of the manhole cover and prevent theft or vandalism.
  - Arduino: To process the sensor data and send it to the cloud server via GSM module.
  - GSM module: To communicate with the cloud server and the authorities via SMS or call.
  - GPS module: To locate the position of the manhole and send it to the authorities along with the sensor data.
  - Cloud server: To store and analyze the sensor data and generate reports and alerts for the authorities.
  - Web application: To display the sensor data and the status of the manholes on a map and provide a user interface for the authorities to monitor and manage the system.
