# IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine is a system that uses Internet of Things (IoT) devices and sensors to measure and monitor the vital signs of Covid-19 patients who are isolated at home or in a quarantine facility     .
- The system consists of the following components:
  - A wearable device that is attached to the patient's body and collects data on body temperature, pulse rate, and oxygen saturation  .
  - A gateway device that receives the data from the wearable device via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi and sends it to a cloud server via the internet     .
  - A cloud server that stores and processes the data and provides a web or mobile interface for the medical staff and the patient to access and visualize the data     .
  - A web or mobile application that displays the real-time and historical data of the patient's vital signs and alerts the medical staff and the patient if any abnormality is detected     .
- The system aims to achieve the following objectives:
  - To reduce the risk of infection and exposure for the medical staff and the patient by minimizing the physical contact and visits     .
  - To provide timely and accurate information on the patient's health condition and enable remote diagnosis and intervention by the medical staff     .
  - To improve the patient's compliance and awareness of their health status and encourage self-care and prevention measures     .
  - To relieve the pressure and burden on the health care system and resources by reducing the hospitalization and admission of mild and moderate cases     .
