### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

- The system consists of a smart dustbin that can automatically segregate the waste into different categories, such as metal, plastic, paper, and organic, using sensors and actuators  .
- The system also monitors the level of garbage in the dustbin using an ultrasonic sensor and sends the data to an IOT platform, such as IOT gecko  , over the internet using a Wi-Fi module.
- The IOT platform displays the bin level data and alerts the authorities when the garbage bins need to be emptied, thus reducing the manual intervention and improving the efficiency of waste management  .
- The system can help to solve the societal problems of increasing waste generation, environmental pollution, health hazards, and inefficient waste collection and disposal .
- The system can also promote the recycling of waste materials and reduce the landfill space and cost .
