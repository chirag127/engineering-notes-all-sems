### Wearable Computer With Temperature Distance Sensors

- A wearable computer is a device that can be worn on the body and provides computing and communication capabilities.
- A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors is a type of wearable computer that can measure the temperature and distance of objects or people in the environment using sensors.
- The sensors used in this device are lidar sensor and temperature sensor.
- Lidar sensor is a device that uses laser pulses to measure the distance and shape of objects by measuring the time of flight and reflection of the light.
- Temperature sensor is a device that measures the temperature of an object or the ambient air by converting the thermal energy into electrical signals.
- The wearable computer with temperature distance sensors is developed using a Raspberry Pi controller with a battery, touch screen display, lidar sensor, temperature sensor all together mounted in a compact way over wrist strap .
- The Raspberry Pi controller is a small, low-cost, single-board computer that can run various operating systems and applications.
- The battery provides the power supply for the device and can be recharged using a USB cable.
- The touch screen display is used to interact with the device and show the sensor data and other information.
- The device can communicate with other devices or networks using Bluetooth or Wi-Fi connectivity.
- The device can be used for various applications, such as:
  - Contactless temperature sensing anywhere
  - Contactless and accurate distance measurement using lidar
  - Health monitoring and wearable diagnostics
  - Navigation and obstacle detection
  - Education and entertainment
