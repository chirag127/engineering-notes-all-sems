# 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

## Introduction

- Aerial surveillance is the key to security and military based operations. It provides real time information on enemy movements which plays a key role in precision strikes  .
- Large drones are very large in size which can be easily detected by radar and shot down. Also, they are very expensive and require a lot of maintenance.
- Small drones are more suitable for covert operations as they are less visible and more agile. However, they have limited battery life and range, and often lack advanced features such as 360-degree camera and IOT connectivity.
- 360-degree camera enables a drone to capture a complete view of its surroundings, which can be useful for mapping, reconnaissance, and situational awareness.
- IOT connectivity allows a drone to communicate with other devices and systems over the internet, which can enable remote control, data transmission, and cloud-based analytics.

## Objective

- The objective of this project is to design and implement a 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV Drone With IOT Camera, which can overcome the limitations of existing drones and provide enhanced capabilities for aerial surveillance applications.
- The specific goals of this project are:

  - To design a small and lightweight drone that can fly for a long time and cover a large area.
  - To equip the drone with a 360-degree camera that can capture high-quality images and videos from all angles.
  - To enable the drone to connect to the internet and transmit the captured data to a cloud server or a mobile device.
  - To develop a user interface that can display the 360-degree view of the drone and allow the user to control the drone remotely.
  - To test the performance and functionality of the drone in different scenarios and environments.

## Components

- The main components of the 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV Drone With IOT Camera are  :

  - Drone frame: A lightweight and durable frame that can support the weight and balance of the drone and its components.
  - Drone motors: Four brushless DC motors that can provide the thrust and speed for the drone to fly and maneuver.
  - Drone propellers: Four propellers that can rotate at different speeds and directions to control the drone's movement and stability.
  - Drone battery: A rechargeable lithium-polymer battery that can power the drone and its components for a long time.
  - Drone controller: A microcontroller that can process the signals from the sensors and the user input and control the motors and the camera.
  - Drone sensors: A gyroscope, an accelerometer, and a barometer that can measure the orientation, acceleration, and altitude of the drone and provide feedback to the controller.
  - Drone camera: A 360-degree camera that can capture images and videos from all directions and store them in a memory card or transmit them to the internet.
  - Drone IOT module: A Wi-Fi or cellular module that can connect the drone to the internet and enable data transmission and remote control.
  - Drone antenna: An antenna that can enhance the signal strength and range of the IOT module.
  - User device: A smartphone, tablet, or laptop that can display the 360-degree view of the drone and allow the user to control the drone remotely.

## Implementation

- The implementation of the 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV Drone With IOT Camera involves the following steps  :

  - Assembling the drone frame and attaching the motors, propellers, battery, controller, sensors, camera, IOT module, and antenna.
  - Programming the controller to receive the signals from the sensors and the user device and control the motors and the camera accordingly.
  - Configuring the IOT module to connect to the internet and transmit the data from the camera to the cloud server or the user device.
  - Developing the user interface to display the 360-degree view of the drone and allow the user to control the drone remotely.
  - Testing the drone in different scenarios and environments and evaluating its performance and functionality.
