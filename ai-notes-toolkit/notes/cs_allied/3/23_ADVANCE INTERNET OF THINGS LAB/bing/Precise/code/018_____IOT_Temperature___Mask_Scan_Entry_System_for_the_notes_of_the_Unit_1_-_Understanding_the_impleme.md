### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is an implementation of the Internet of Things (IOT) technology that allows for the monitoring and control of entry into a building or facility based on temperature and mask-wearing.

1. The system uses sensors to detect the temperature of an individual and determine if they are wearing a mask.
2. If the individual's temperature is within a normal range and they are wearing a mask, the system will allow entry.
3. If the individual's temperature is above a certain threshold or they are not wearing a mask, the system will deny entry and may trigger an alarm or notification.
4. The system can be integrated with other IOT devices and systems, such as access control systems, to provide a comprehensive solution for managing entry into a building or facility.
5. The system can also collect and store data on temperature and mask-wearing, allowing for analysis and reporting on compliance with health and safety regulations.

This system is an example of how IOT technology can be used to improve health and safety in public spaces and buildings. It is a practical application of the concepts and principles covered in Unit 1 of the ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB course, which focuses on understanding the implementation of IOT.
