### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

The IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to solve societal problems related to waste management. This system is designed to segregate garbage and indicate the level of the bin. It is a part of Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.

The main features of the IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator are:

1. **Garbage Segregation:** The system is capable of segregating garbage into different categories such as organic, recyclable, and non-recyclable. This helps in the efficient management of waste and reduces the burden on landfills.

2. **Bin Level Indicator:** The system is equipped with sensors that can detect the level of garbage in the bin. When the bin is full, the system sends a notification to the concerned authorities for timely disposal of the waste.

3. **Real-time Monitoring:** The system can be monitored in real-time using a web or mobile application. This helps in the efficient management of waste and ensures timely disposal of garbage.

4. **Data Analytics:** The system collects data on the amount and type of waste generated. This data can be analyzed to identify trends and patterns in waste generation. This information can be used to develop better waste management strategies.

Overall, the IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is an innovative solution that uses IoT technology to solve societal problems related to waste management. It is an effective tool for managing waste in a sustainable and efficient manner.
