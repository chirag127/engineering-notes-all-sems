### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

#### Introduction
- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and exchange data.
- One of the applications of IoT is in waste management, where it can be used to improve the efficiency of garbage segregation and bin level monitoring.
- An IoT garbage segregator and bin level indicator is a system that uses sensors and other technologies to automatically sort waste into different categories and monitor the fill level of garbage bins.

#### Implementation
- The system consists of a garbage bin equipped with sensors and a microcontroller.
- The sensors can detect the type of waste being disposed of, such as metal, plastic, paper, or organic waste.
- The microcontroller processes the data from the sensors and activates the appropriate mechanism to sort the waste into the correct bin.
- The system also includes a bin level sensor that can detect the fill level of the bin and send an alert when it is full.
- The data collected by the system can be transmitted to a central server for analysis and monitoring.

#### Benefits
- The system can improve the efficiency of waste management by automating the segregation process and reducing the need for manual sorting.
- It can also help to reduce the environmental impact of waste by ensuring that recyclable materials are properly sorted and disposed of.
- The bin level monitoring feature can help to prevent overfilling of bins and reduce the risk of littering and pollution.
- The data collected by the system can be used to optimize waste collection routes and schedules, reducing costs and improving service.

#### Conclusion
- The IoT garbage segregator and bin level indicator is an example of how IoT technology can be applied to improve waste management and reduce the environmental impact of waste.
- By automating the segregation process and monitoring bin levels, the system can improve efficiency, reduce costs, and help to protect the environment.
