### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- An IOT based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system is a system that is designed to implement security and detection of hazards inside a coal mine.
- The system is comprised of sensors, which communicate over a low power communication protocol such as LoRa and send data to a central IoT gateway, where collected data is analyzed .
- The transmitter module has temperature, smoke, and methane sensors installed in it. The transmitter module also carries an LCD and an RF transmitter .
- This data can be seen locally on the LCD screen connected with the IoT gateway or can be further sent to the cloud through the internet .
- The system can monitor and control various parameters in the coal mines such as leakage of gas, earthquake, water level, and fire ignition detection in the coal mine .
- The key abstract is to use IoT to incorporate a coal mine safety monitoring system .
