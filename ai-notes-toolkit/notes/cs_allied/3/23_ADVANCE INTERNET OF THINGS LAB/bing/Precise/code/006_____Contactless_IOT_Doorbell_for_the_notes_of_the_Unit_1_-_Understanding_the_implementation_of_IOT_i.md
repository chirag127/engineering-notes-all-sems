### Contactless IOT Doorbell

A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to alert the residents of a home or building that they are at the door without physically touching the doorbell. This can be achieved through various methods such as motion detection, proximity sensors, or voice activation.

Some key points to consider when implementing a contactless IOT doorbell are:

1. **Sensor selection**: Choosing the right sensor for detecting the presence of a visitor is crucial. Motion sensors, proximity sensors, and voice activation are some common options.
2. **Connectivity**: The doorbell needs to be connected to the internet to send notifications to the residents. This can be achieved through Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular connectivity.
3. **Power source**: The doorbell needs a power source to function. This can be achieved through batteries or a wired connection to the building's power supply.
4. **Notification method**: The doorbell needs to notify the residents when a visitor is detected. This can be achieved through a mobile app, text message, or phone call.
5. **Security**: The doorbell should have security measures in place to prevent unauthorized access or tampering.
