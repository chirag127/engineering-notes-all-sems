### IOT Circuit Breaker Project

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. The IOT Circuit Breaker Project is an example of the implementation of the Internet of Things (IOT) in the field of electrical engineering.
2. The project involves the use of IOT technology to remotely monitor and control the operation of a circuit breaker.
3. A circuit breaker is an electrical switch designed to protect an electrical circuit from damage caused by excess current from an overload or short circuit.
4. The IOT Circuit Breaker Project allows for real-time monitoring of the circuit breaker's status and the ability to remotely control its operation.
5. This can be achieved through the use of sensors and communication technologies, such as Wi-Fi or cellular networks, to transmit data to a remote server or control center.
6. The data collected can be used to analyze the performance of the circuit breaker and make informed decisions about its operation.
7. The use of IOT technology in this project can improve the safety and reliability of the electrical system, as well as provide cost savings through remote monitoring and control.
8. This project is an example of how IOT technology can be applied to improve the operation and maintenance of electrical systems.
