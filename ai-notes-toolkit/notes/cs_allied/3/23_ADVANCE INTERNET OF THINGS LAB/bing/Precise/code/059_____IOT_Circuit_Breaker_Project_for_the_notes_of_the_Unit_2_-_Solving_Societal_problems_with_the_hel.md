### IOT Circuit Breaker Project

The IOT Circuit Breaker Project is a part of Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB. Here are some key points to note:

1. The IOT Circuit Breaker Project aims to solve the problem of electrical safety in households and industries.
2. The project involves the use of IOT technology to monitor and control the flow of electricity in a circuit.
3. The circuit breaker is designed to automatically disconnect the flow of electricity in the event of an overload or short circuit.
4. This helps to prevent electrical fires and other hazards that can result from electrical malfunctions.
5. The IOT technology used in the project allows for remote monitoring and control of the circuit breaker, providing an additional layer of safety and convenience.
6. The project has the potential to greatly improve electrical safety in households and industries, reducing the risk of electrical accidents and saving lives.
