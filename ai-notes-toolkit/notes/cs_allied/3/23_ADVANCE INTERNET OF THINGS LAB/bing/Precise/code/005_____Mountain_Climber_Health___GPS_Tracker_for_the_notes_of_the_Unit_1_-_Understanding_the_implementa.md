### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Mountain climbers face many challenges while climbing, including harsh weather conditions, altitude sickness, and physical exhaustion.
- To ensure their safety, it is important to monitor their health and location in real-time.
- A Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker is an IoT device that can help achieve this.
- The device can track the climber's location using GPS and monitor their vital signs, such as heart rate, oxygen saturation, and body temperature.
- The data collected by the device can be transmitted in real-time to a base station or a monitoring team, allowing for timely intervention in case of an emergency.
- The implementation of IoT in such a device can greatly enhance the safety of mountain climbers and provide peace of mind to their loved ones.
