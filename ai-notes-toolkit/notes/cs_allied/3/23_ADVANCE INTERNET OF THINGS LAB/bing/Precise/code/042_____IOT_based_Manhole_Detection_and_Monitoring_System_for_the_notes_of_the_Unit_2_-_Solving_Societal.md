### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

An IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to detect and monitor manholes in a city or town. This system can help to solve societal problems by improving the safety and sanitation of the city or town.

1. **Detection:** The system uses sensors to detect the presence of manholes and their location. These sensors can be placed on the ground or on vehicles that travel through the city or town.

2. **Monitoring:** The system can monitor the condition of the manholes, such as their depth, the level of water or sewage, and the presence of any blockages or damage. This information can be transmitted to a central control center in real-time.

3. **Alerts:** The system can send alerts to the relevant authorities if a manhole is detected to be in a dangerous condition, such as if it is overflowing or if its cover is missing. This can help to prevent accidents and improve public safety.

4. **Maintenance:** The system can also help to improve the maintenance of the manholes by providing information on their condition and usage. This can help the authorities to prioritize their maintenance efforts and to plan for future upgrades or repairs.

Overall, an IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System can help to improve the safety and sanitation of a city or town by providing real-time information on the condition of its manholes. This can help to prevent accidents, improve public health, and reduce the costs of maintenance and repairs.
