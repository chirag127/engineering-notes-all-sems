### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite for space research that is made up of multiples of 10×10×10 cm cubic units.
- CubeSats are commonly used for weather imaging and telemetry transmission.
- Weather imaging CubeSats are equipped with cameras and sensors to capture images and data of weather patterns and phenomena.
- The data collected by the CubeSat is transmitted back to Earth via telemetry transmission.
- Telemetry transmission is the wireless transmission of data from remote sources to a receiving station for monitoring and analysis.
- The use of CubeSats for weather imaging and telemetry transmission allows for more frequent and detailed observations of weather patterns, which can improve weather forecasting and our understanding of climate change.
- This technology is an example of the implementation of the Internet of Things (IoT) in space research, as the CubeSat is able to collect and transmit data to be analyzed and used by researchers on Earth.
