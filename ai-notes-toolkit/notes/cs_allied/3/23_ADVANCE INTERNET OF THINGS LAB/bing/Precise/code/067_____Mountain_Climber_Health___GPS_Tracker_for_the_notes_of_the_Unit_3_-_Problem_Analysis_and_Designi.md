### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. Mountain climbers face various health risks while climbing, such as altitude sickness, hypothermia, and dehydration.
2. A health and GPS tracker can help monitor the climber's vital signs and location in real-time.
3. The device can alert the climber or a remote monitoring team of any potential health issues or if the climber has strayed off course.
4. The device can also provide valuable data for research and analysis to improve safety measures for future climbs.
5. The design of the device should be compact, durable, and easy to use, with a long battery life and reliable connectivity.
6. The device should also be able to integrate with other climbing equipment and gear.
7. The data collected by the device can be analyzed to identify patterns and trends, and to develop predictive models for improved safety and performance.
8. The device can also provide valuable information for search and rescue operations in case of an emergency.
