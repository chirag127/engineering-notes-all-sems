### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- An IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a device that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor water pollution levels in bodies of water such as lakes, rivers, and oceans.
- The device is mounted on a remote-controlled (RC) boat, which allows it to navigate the water and collect data from various locations.
- The data collected by the device includes measurements of various water quality parameters such as pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, and turbidity.
- The data is transmitted in real-time to a cloud-based server, where it can be accessed and analyzed by users.
- The use of IoT technology allows for real-time monitoring of water pollution levels, which can help in the early detection and prevention of water pollution.
- The device can be used by government agencies, environmental organizations, and researchers to monitor water quality and track changes over time.
- The implementation of IoT in water pollution monitoring can help in the development of more effective and efficient strategies for water pollution control and prevention.
