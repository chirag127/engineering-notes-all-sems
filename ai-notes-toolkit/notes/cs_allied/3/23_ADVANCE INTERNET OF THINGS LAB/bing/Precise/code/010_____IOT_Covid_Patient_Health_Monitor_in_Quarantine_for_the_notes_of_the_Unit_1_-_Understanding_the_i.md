### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Internet of Things (IoT) technology can be used to monitor the health of COVID-19 patients in quarantine.
- IoT devices such as wearables and sensors can collect data on the patient's vital signs, including temperature, heart rate, and oxygen saturation.
- This data can be transmitted in real-time to healthcare professionals, allowing them to remotely monitor the patient's condition and respond quickly if necessary.
- IoT technology can also be used to monitor the patient's environment, such as air quality and humidity, to ensure optimal conditions for recovery.
- The use of IoT technology in this context can help to reduce the burden on healthcare systems and improve patient outcomes.
- It is important to ensure the security and privacy of the patient's data when implementing IoT solutions for health monitoring.
