## Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

1. **Problem Analysis**: The first step in designing a solution is to analyze the problem and understand its requirements. This involves identifying the inputs, outputs, and processes involved in the problem.

2. **Defining the Problem**: Once the problem has been analyzed, it is important to define it in a clear and concise manner. This helps to ensure that all stakeholders have a common understanding of the problem and its requirements.

3. **Identifying Constraints**: It is important to identify any constraints that may impact the design of the solution. These could include technical, financial, or time constraints.

4. **Designing the Solution**: Once the problem has been defined and the constraints have been identified, the next step is to design a solution that meets the requirements of the problem. This involves selecting an appropriate algorithm or data structure, and designing the user interface and user experience.

5. **Evaluating the Solution**: After the solution has been designed, it is important to evaluate it to ensure that it meets the requirements of the problem. This can be done through testing and user feedback.

6. **Iterating and Refining**: Based on the results of the evaluation, the solution may need to be iterated and refined to better meet the requirements of the problem. This process continues until a satisfactory solution is achieved.
