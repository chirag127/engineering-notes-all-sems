### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. Mountain climbers face many challenges while climbing, including harsh weather conditions, altitude sickness, and physical exhaustion.
2. A Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker can help climbers monitor their health and location in real-time.
3. The device can track the climber's heart rate, oxygen levels, and body temperature, alerting them to any potential health issues.
4. The GPS tracker can help climbers navigate their route and provide their location to rescue teams in case of an emergency.
5. The device can be connected to the internet, allowing climbers to share their location and health data with their support team in real-time.
6. This technology can help improve the safety of mountain climbers and provide valuable data for research on the effects of high altitude on the human body.
