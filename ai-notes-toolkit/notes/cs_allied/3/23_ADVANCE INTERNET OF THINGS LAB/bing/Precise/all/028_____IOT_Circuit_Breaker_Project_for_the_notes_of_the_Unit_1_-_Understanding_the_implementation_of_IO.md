# IOT Circuit Breaker Project

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- An IOT circuit breaker is a device that can be controlled remotely using the internet.
- It is used to switch on or off the flow of electricity in a circuit.
- The circuit breaker can be controlled using a smartphone or a computer.
- The device can be programmed to switch off the electricity supply in case of an overload or a short circuit.
- The circuit breaker can also be used to monitor the electricity consumption of a particular circuit.
- The device can be integrated with other IOT devices to create a smart home or a smart building.
- The circuit breaker can be used to reduce energy consumption and save on electricity bills.
- The device can be used to improve the safety of electrical systems.
