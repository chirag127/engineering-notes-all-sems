# IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

## Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT

### Introduction
- An IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to detect and monitor manholes in a city or town.
- The system is designed to prevent accidents and injuries caused by open manholes, which can be a major problem in many urban areas.

### How it works
- The system uses sensors placed in manholes to detect if the manhole cover is in place or not.
- If the sensor detects that the manhole cover is missing, it sends an alert to a central monitoring system.
- The central monitoring system can then notify the relevant authorities, such as the city's maintenance department, to take action and replace the missing manhole cover.

### Benefits
- The system can help prevent accidents and injuries caused by open manholes.
- It can also help the city's maintenance department to quickly identify and fix missing manhole covers, improving the safety and efficiency of the city's infrastructure.

### Conclusion
- An IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is an effective solution to the problem of open manholes in urban areas.
- By using IoT technology, the system can quickly detect and alert the relevant authorities to missing manhole covers, helping to prevent accidents and injuries and improve the safety of the city's infrastructure.
