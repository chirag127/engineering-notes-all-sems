# IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue is designed to limit the spread of Covid by measuring the distance between individuals in queues .
- This system is essential for places where long queues can last for hours every day, such as banks, government offices, shopping malls, schools, and theatres .
- The robot consists of a 4 wheel design system used to drive the robotic vehicle .
- It makes use of a line following principle to constantly move along with the queue and monitor for social distancing violations .
- Social distancing is of key importance during the current pandemic as it helps limit the spread of covid by observing distance between disease spreading individuals .
