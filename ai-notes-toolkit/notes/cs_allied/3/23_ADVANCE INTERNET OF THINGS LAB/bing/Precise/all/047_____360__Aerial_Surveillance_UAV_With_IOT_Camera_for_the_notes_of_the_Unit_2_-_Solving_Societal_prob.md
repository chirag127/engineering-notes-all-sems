# 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- A 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV with IOT Camera is a type of Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) that is equipped with an Internet of Things (IoT) camera.
- This type of UAV is capable of providing 360-degree aerial surveillance, allowing for a comprehensive view of the area being monitored.
- The IoT camera enables the UAV to transmit real-time data and images to a remote location, allowing for real-time monitoring and analysis.
- This technology can be used to solve societal problems by providing enhanced surveillance capabilities in areas such as border security, disaster management, and wildlife conservation.
- The use of IoT technology in UAVs allows for the integration of various sensors and devices, enabling the UAV to collect and transmit a wide range of data.
- This data can be used to improve decision-making and response times in emergency situations, ultimately helping to save lives and protect property.
- The use of 360° Aerial Surveillance UAVs with IOT Cameras is an example of how the Internet of Things can be used to solve societal problems and improve the quality of life for individuals and communities.
