# IOT Contactless Covid Testing Booth Automation

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. **Introduction:** The COVID-19 pandemic has brought about the need for innovative solutions to reduce the spread of the virus. One such solution is the use of IOT contactless COVID testing booths.
2. **Problem Analysis:** The main problem that needs to be addressed is the risk of transmission of the virus during the testing process. Traditional testing methods require close contact between the tester and the person being tested, increasing the risk of transmission.
3. **Designing a Solution:** An IOT contactless COVID testing booth can be designed to minimize the risk of transmission. The booth can be equipped with sensors and automated systems to collect samples without the need for close contact between the tester and the person being tested.
4. **Implementation:** The booth can be equipped with a temperature sensor to check for fever, a camera to capture an image of the person's face for identification, and a swab collection mechanism to collect a sample from the person's nose or throat.
5. **Data Collection and Analysis:** The data collected by the booth can be transmitted to a central database for analysis. The data can be used to track the spread of the virus and to identify potential hotspots.
6. **Conclusion:** The use of IOT contactless COVID testing booths can help reduce the risk of transmission of the virus during the testing process. The data collected by the booths can also be used to track the spread of the virus and to identify potential hotspots. This technology has the potential to play a crucial role in the fight against the COVID-19 pandemic.
