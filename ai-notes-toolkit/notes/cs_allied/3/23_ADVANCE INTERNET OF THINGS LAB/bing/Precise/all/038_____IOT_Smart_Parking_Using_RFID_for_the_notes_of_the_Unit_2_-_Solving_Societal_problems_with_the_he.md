# IOT Smart Parking Using RFID

## Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- **Introduction:** IOT Smart Parking using RFID is a system that uses RFID technology to manage and monitor parking spaces in real-time. This system can help solve societal problems such as traffic congestion, air pollution, and inefficient use of parking spaces.

- **RFID Technology:** RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) is a wireless technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. In the context of smart parking, RFID tags are attached to vehicles and RFID readers are installed at the entrance and exit of parking lots. When a vehicle enters or exits the parking lot, the RFID reader reads the tag and sends the information to the central system.

- **Real-time Monitoring:** The central system receives information from the RFID readers and updates the availability of parking spaces in real-time. This information can be accessed by drivers through a mobile app or a website, allowing them to find available parking spaces quickly and easily.

- **Benefits:** IOT Smart Parking using RFID can help reduce traffic congestion and air pollution by reducing the time drivers spend searching for parking spaces. It can also improve the efficiency of parking lots by ensuring that parking spaces are used effectively.

- **Conclusion:** IOT Smart Parking using RFID is an innovative solution that can help solve societal problems related to parking. By using RFID technology and real-time monitoring, this system can improve the efficiency of parking lots and reduce traffic congestion and air pollution.
