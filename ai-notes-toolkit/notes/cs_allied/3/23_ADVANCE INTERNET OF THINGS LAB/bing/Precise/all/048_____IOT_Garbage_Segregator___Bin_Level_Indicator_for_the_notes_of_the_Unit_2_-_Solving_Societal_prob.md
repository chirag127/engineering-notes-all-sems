# IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

## Introduction
- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share data.
- One application of IoT is in waste management, where it can be used to develop smart garbage segregators and bin level indicators.
- These devices can help solve societal problems by improving the efficiency and effectiveness of waste management.

## IOT Garbage Segregator
- An IoT garbage segregator is a device that can automatically sort waste into different categories, such as recyclable, organic, and non-recyclable.
- This can be achieved using sensors and machine learning algorithms to identify the type of waste and direct it to the appropriate bin.
- By segregating waste at the source, the amount of waste that goes to landfills can be reduced, and the recycling process can be made more efficient.

## Bin Level Indicator
- A bin level indicator is a device that can measure the fill level of a garbage bin and send this information to a central system.
- This can be achieved using sensors, such as ultrasonic or infrared sensors, to detect the distance between the top of the bin and the garbage inside.
- By monitoring the fill level of garbage bins, waste management companies can optimize their collection routes and schedules, reducing the number of trips and saving time and fuel.

## Conclusion
- The use of IoT in waste management can help solve societal problems by improving the efficiency and effectiveness of waste management.
- Smart garbage segregators and bin level indicators are two examples of how IoT can be applied in this field.
- By using these devices, we can reduce the amount of waste that goes to landfills, improve the recycling process, and optimize waste collection.
