### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. Mountain climbers face several health risks while climbing, including altitude sickness, hypothermia, and dehydration.
2. A health and GPS tracker can help monitor the climber's vital signs and location in real-time.
3. The device can alert the climber or a remote monitoring team if any health issues are detected.
4. The GPS tracker can also help locate the climber in case of an emergency.
5. The device can be designed to be compact and lightweight, making it easy to carry while climbing.
6. The device can be powered by a rechargeable battery and can be charged using solar power.
7. The device can be connected to a mobile app, allowing the climber to view their health data and location in real-time.
8. The device can also store data for later analysis, helping the climber to track their progress and improve their performance.
9. The device can be designed to be rugged and durable, able to withstand the harsh conditions of mountain climbing.
10. The device can be an essential tool for mountain climbers, helping to ensure their safety and well-being while climbing.
