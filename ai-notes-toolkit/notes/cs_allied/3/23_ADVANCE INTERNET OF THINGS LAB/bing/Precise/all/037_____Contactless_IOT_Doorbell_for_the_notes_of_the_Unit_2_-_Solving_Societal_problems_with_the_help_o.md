# Contactless IOT Doorbell

A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to alert the occupants of a house or building that they are at the door without physically touching the doorbell. This can be achieved through the use of sensors, such as infrared or motion sensors, that detect the presence of a visitor and trigger the doorbell to ring.

The use of a contactless IOT doorbell can help to solve societal problems by reducing the spread of germs and viruses. By eliminating the need for physical contact with the doorbell, the risk of transmitting diseases through touch is reduced.

Some key features of a contactless IOT doorbell may include:
- Infrared or motion sensors to detect the presence of a visitor
- Integration with a smartphone app to allow remote monitoring and control
- The ability to send notifications to the occupants when a visitor is detected
- Customizable settings, such as the volume and tone of the doorbell ring
- The ability to record and store footage of visitors for added security

In summary, a contactless IOT doorbell is a useful tool for reducing the spread of germs and viruses, while also providing added convenience and security for the occupants of a house or building. It is an example of how the Internet of Things can be used to solve societal problems.
