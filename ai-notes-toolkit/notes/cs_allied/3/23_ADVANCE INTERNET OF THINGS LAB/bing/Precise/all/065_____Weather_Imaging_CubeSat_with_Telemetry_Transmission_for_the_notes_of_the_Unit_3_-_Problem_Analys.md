# Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- CubeSats are small satellites that are used for space research and exploration.
- They are typically 10x10x10 cm in size and have a mass of no more than 1.33 kg.
- Weather Imaging CubeSats are designed to capture images of weather patterns and transmit them back to Earth for analysis.
- Telemetry transmission refers to the process of transmitting data from the CubeSat back to Earth.
- This data can include information about the CubeSat's position, orientation, and health, as well as the weather data it has collected.
- The use of CubeSats for weather imaging can provide valuable information for weather forecasting and climate research.
- The small size and low cost of CubeSats make them an attractive option for weather imaging, as multiple CubeSats can be launched to provide more comprehensive coverage.
- The design of a Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission involves several steps, including defining the mission objectives, selecting the appropriate sensors and instruments, and designing the communication system for telemetry transmission.
