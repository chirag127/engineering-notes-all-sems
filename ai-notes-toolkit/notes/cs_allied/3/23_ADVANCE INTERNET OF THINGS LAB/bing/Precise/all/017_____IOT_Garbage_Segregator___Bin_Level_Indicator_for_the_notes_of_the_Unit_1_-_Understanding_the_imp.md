# IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- An IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is a device that uses the Internet of Things (IOT) technology to segregate garbage and indicate the level of garbage in a bin.
- The device can be used to segregate garbage into different categories such as biodegradable, non-biodegradable, and recyclable.
- The bin level indicator can be used to monitor the level of garbage in the bin and send notifications when the bin is full.
- The device can be connected to the internet and can be monitored and controlled remotely.
- The implementation of IOT in this device can help in efficient garbage management and can contribute to a cleaner and healthier environment.
- The device can be used in households, offices, and public places to manage garbage effectively.
- The device can be integrated with other IOT devices and systems to create a smart garbage management system.
