# IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

## Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can collect and exchange data.
- IoT can be used to monitor the health of Covid patients in quarantine.
- This can be done by using sensors and wearable devices to collect data on the patient's vital signs, such as heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation.
- The data collected can be transmitted to a central system for monitoring and analysis.
- This can help healthcare professionals to remotely monitor the patient's condition and provide timely interventions if necessary.
- The use of IoT in this context can improve the quality of care for Covid patients in quarantine and reduce the burden on healthcare systems.
