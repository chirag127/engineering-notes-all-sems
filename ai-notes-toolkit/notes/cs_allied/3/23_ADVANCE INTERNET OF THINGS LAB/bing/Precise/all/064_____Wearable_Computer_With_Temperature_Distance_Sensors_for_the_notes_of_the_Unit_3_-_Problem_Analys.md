# Wearable Computer With Temperature Distance Sensors

A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors is a device that combines the power of a personal computer with the convenience of a wearable device. It is developed using a Raspberry Pi controller with a battery, touch screen display, lidar sensor, temperature sensor all together mounted in a compact way over a wrist strap. This makes a smart, easy to carry wearable computer .

Some of the features of this wearable computer include:
- The ability to get the power of a personal computer right on your hand.
- A touch display for easy interaction.
- Internet connectivity on a Smartwatch.
- The ability to google search from anywhere.
- Contactless temperature sensing anywhere .
- Contactless and accurate distance measurement using Lidar .

This device provides a convenient and portable way to access the power of a personal computer, while also providing useful features such as contactless temperature sensing and distance measurement. It is a useful tool for a variety of applications, including monitoring and controlling of furnaces, oil level sensors, intake air temperature sensors, and automotive sensors in industries.
