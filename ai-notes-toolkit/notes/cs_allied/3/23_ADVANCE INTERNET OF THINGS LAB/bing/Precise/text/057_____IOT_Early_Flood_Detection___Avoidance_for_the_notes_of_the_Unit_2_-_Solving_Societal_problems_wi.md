### IOT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance

- “IoT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance System” is an intelligent system that keeps close watch over various natural factors to predict a flood, so we can embrace ourselves for caution, to minimize the damage caused by the flood   .
- Natural disasters like a flood can be devastating leading to property damage and loss of lives   .
- This system focuses on providing early warnings to areas likely to be ravaged by flood events using Wireless Sensor Network (WSN).
- The system involves the deployment of sensor nodes at specific flood vulnerable locations for real-time flood monitoring and detection.
