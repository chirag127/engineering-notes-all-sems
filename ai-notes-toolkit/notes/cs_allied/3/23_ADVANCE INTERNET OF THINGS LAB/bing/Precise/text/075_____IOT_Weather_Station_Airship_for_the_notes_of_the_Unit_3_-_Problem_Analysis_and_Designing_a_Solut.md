### IOT Weather Station Airship

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- An IOT weather station airship is a device that can be used to collect and transmit weather data from remote locations.
- The airship is equipped with sensors that can measure temperature, humidity, wind speed, and other weather-related data.
- The data collected by the airship is transmitted to a central server using wireless communication technologies such as Wi-Fi or cellular networks.
- The data can then be analyzed and used to make weather predictions or to monitor weather conditions in real-time.
- The airship is powered by solar panels or batteries and can be remotely controlled to move to different locations.
- The use of an airship allows for the collection of weather data from locations that are difficult to access, such as mountainous regions or remote islands.
- The design of the airship and its sensors must take into account the harsh weather conditions that it may encounter, such as high winds or extreme temperatures.
- The airship must also be able to operate autonomously for extended periods of time without requiring maintenance or human intervention.
- The data collected by the airship can be used by meteorologists, researchers, and other stakeholders to improve weather forecasting and to better understand weather patterns and climate change.
