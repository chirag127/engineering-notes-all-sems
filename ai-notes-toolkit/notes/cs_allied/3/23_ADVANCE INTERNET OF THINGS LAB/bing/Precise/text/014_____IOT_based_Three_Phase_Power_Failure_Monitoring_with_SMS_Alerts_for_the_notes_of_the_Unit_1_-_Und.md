### IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts

- IOT based Three Phase Power Failure Monitoring with SMS Alerts is a system that monitors the power supply of three-phase systems and sends SMS alerts in case of a power failure.
- The system uses an IOT module to connect to the internet and send SMS alerts to the user's mobile phone.
- The system can be used in industries, factories, and other places where three-phase power supply is used.
- The system can help prevent damage to equipment and machinery due to power failure.
- The system can also help in reducing downtime and increasing productivity.
- The system can be easily installed and configured.
- The system can be accessed and monitored remotely using a web interface.
- The system can also be integrated with other IOT devices for enhanced functionality.
