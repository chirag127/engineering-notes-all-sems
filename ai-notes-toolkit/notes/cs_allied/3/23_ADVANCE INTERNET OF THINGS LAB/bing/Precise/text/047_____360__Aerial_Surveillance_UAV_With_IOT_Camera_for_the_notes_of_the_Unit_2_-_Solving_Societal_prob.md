### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

- Aerial surveillance is the key to security and military-based operations. It provides real-time information on enemy movements which plays a key role in precision strikes .
- 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV Drone With IOT Camera is a hardware project that is designed to provide real-time information on enemy movements.
- Large drones are very large in size which can be easily detected by the enemy. However, the 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV Drone With IOT Camera is compact and rugged, making it suitable for applications such as unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) that require image stabilization.
- The 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV Drone With IOT Camera is a fully autonomous drone-in-a-box solution that can be operated completely remotely.
- The UAV-based IoT platform can be used for crowd surveillance based on face recognition.
