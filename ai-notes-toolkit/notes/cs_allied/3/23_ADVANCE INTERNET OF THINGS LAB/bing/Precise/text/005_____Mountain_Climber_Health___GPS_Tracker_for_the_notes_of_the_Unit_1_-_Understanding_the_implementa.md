### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Mountain climbers face many challenges while climbing, including harsh weather conditions, altitude sickness, and physical exhaustion.
- To ensure their safety, it is important for climbers to monitor their health and location at all times.
- A Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker is a device that can help climbers achieve this.
- This device uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to collect and transmit data about the climber's health and location in real-time.
- The device can monitor the climber's heart rate, oxygen levels, and body temperature, and send alerts if any of these readings fall outside of a safe range.
- The GPS tracker can also provide the climber's exact location, which can be useful in case of an emergency.
- By using a Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker, climbers can ensure that they are taking the necessary precautions to stay safe while climbing.
