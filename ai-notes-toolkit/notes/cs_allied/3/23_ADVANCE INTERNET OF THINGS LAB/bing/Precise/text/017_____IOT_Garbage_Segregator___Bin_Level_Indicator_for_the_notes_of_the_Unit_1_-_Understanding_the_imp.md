### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

- The IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to segregate garbage and monitor the level of garbage in bins.
- The system consists of sensors that detect the type of garbage and segregate it accordingly. For example, the system can separate organic waste from inorganic waste.
- The system also includes sensors that monitor the level of garbage in the bin. When the bin is full, the system sends a notification to the garbage collection authorities to empty the bin.
- The implementation of this system can help in efficient garbage management and reduce the negative impact of garbage on the environment.
- This system is an example of how IoT technology can be used to solve real-world problems and improve our daily lives.
