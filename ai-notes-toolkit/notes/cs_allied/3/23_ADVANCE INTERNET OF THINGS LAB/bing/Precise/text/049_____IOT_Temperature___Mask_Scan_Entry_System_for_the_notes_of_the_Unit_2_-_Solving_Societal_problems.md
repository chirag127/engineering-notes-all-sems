### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is a solution that can help solve societal problems with the help of IOT. This system is part of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.

1. The system uses IOT technology to scan the temperature and mask-wearing status of individuals entering a building or facility.
2. The system can be set up at the entrance of a building or facility to ensure that individuals entering the building are wearing masks and do not have a fever.
3. The system can be connected to a database to store the temperature and mask-wearing status of individuals entering the building.
4. The system can be set up to deny entry to individuals who are not wearing masks or have a fever.
5. The system can help prevent the spread of infectious diseases by ensuring that individuals entering a building are wearing masks and do not have a fever.
6. The system can be integrated with other IOT devices to provide a comprehensive solution for preventing the spread of infectious diseases.

This system is an example of how IOT technology can be used to solve societal problems and improve the health and safety of individuals. It is an important topic to study and understand for students of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB.
