### Contactless IOT Doorbell

A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to alert the residents of a house or building that they are at the door without physically touching the doorbell. This can be achieved through the use of sensors, such as infrared or motion sensors, that detect the presence of a visitor and trigger the doorbell.

Some benefits of using a contactless IOT doorbell include:
1. Increased hygiene: By eliminating the need for physical contact with the doorbell, the spread of germs and viruses can be reduced.
2. Convenience: Visitors do not need to physically touch the doorbell, making it easier for those carrying items or with mobility issues.
3. Integration with smart home systems: The doorbell can be connected to other smart home devices, allowing for remote monitoring and control.

In the context of solving societal problems with the help of IOT, a contactless IOT doorbell can contribute to reducing the spread of infectious diseases and improving accessibility for those with mobility issues. It is an example of how technology can be used to address real-world problems and improve the quality of life for individuals and communities.
