### IOT Based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System

- An IOT based automatic vehicle accident detection and rescue system is a technology that uses sensors and communication modules to detect accidents and alert emergency services in real-time.
- The system is designed to solve the societal problem of delayed response to vehicle accidents, which can result in loss of life and property.
- The system uses sensors such as accelerometers, gyroscopes, and GPS modules to detect sudden changes in the vehicle's movement, which may indicate an accident.
- When an accident is detected, the system automatically sends an alert to emergency services, along with the location of the accident, using communication modules such as GSM or Wi-Fi.
- The system can also be integrated with other technologies such as smart traffic management systems to provide real-time traffic updates and reroute emergency vehicles to the accident site.
- The use of IOT technology in accident detection and rescue systems can significantly improve response times and save lives.
- This technology is an example of how IOT can be used to solve societal problems and improve the quality of life.
