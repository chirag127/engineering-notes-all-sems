### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor energy consumption and detect theft.
- The system consists of a smart energy meter that is connected to the internet and can send data to a central server for monitoring and analysis.
- The smart energy meter is capable of measuring the energy consumption of a household or building and sending the data to the central server in real-time.
- The central server can analyze the data and detect any unusual patterns that may indicate energy theft.
- The system can also send alerts to the user or the energy provider in case of any detected theft.
- This system can help to reduce energy theft and improve the efficiency of energy consumption.
- The system can be implemented in households, buildings, and industries to monitor energy consumption and detect theft.
- The use of IoT technology in this system allows for real-time monitoring and analysis of energy consumption data, making it easier to detect and prevent energy theft.
