### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite for space research that is made up of multiples of 10×10×10 cm cubic units.
- CubeSats are commonly used for weather imaging and telemetry transmission.
- Weather imaging CubeSats are equipped with cameras and sensors that capture images and data of weather patterns and phenomena.
- The data collected by the CubeSat is transmitted back to Earth via telemetry transmission.
- This data can be used to improve weather forecasting and monitoring, and to help solve societal problems related to weather and climate.
- The use of CubeSats for weather imaging and telemetry transmission is an example of how the Internet of Things (IoT) can be used to solve societal problems.
- IoT refers to the interconnected network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and connectivity that enables these objects to connect and exchange data.
- By leveraging the capabilities of IoT, CubeSats can provide valuable data and insights to help address societal challenges related to weather and climate.
