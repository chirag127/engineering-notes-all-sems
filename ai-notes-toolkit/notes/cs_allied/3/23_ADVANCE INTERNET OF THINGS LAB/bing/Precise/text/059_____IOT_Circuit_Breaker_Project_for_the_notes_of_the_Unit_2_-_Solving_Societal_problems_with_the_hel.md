### IOT Circuit Breaker Project

The IOT Circuit Breaker Project is a part of Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB. The project aims to use the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to improve the safety and reliability of electrical systems.

1. The IOT Circuit Breaker is a device that can be remotely controlled and monitored using IoT technology.
2. The device can be used to monitor the electrical system and detect any faults or overloads.
3. In the event of a fault or overload, the IOT Circuit Breaker can be remotely triggered to disconnect the affected circuit, preventing damage to the electrical system and reducing the risk of fire.
4. The IOT Circuit Breaker can also be used to remotely control the power supply to individual circuits, allowing for greater control and flexibility in managing the electrical system.
5. The use of IoT technology in the IOT Circuit Breaker Project can improve the safety and reliability of electrical systems, helping to solve societal problems related to electrical safety.
