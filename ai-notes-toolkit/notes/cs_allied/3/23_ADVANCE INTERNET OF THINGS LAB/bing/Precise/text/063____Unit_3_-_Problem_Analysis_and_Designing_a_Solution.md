## Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

1. **Problem Analysis**: The first step in designing a solution is to analyze the problem and understand its requirements. This involves identifying the problem, defining its scope, and gathering information about it.

2. **Requirement Specification**: Once the problem has been analyzed, the next step is to specify the requirements for the solution. This involves defining the objectives of the solution, the constraints that must be met, and the criteria for evaluating its success.

3. **Design**: After the requirements have been specified, the next step is to design a solution that meets those requirements. This involves developing a plan for how the solution will be implemented, including the algorithms, data structures, and interfaces that will be used.

4. **Implementation**: Once the design has been completed, the next step is to implement the solution. This involves writing code, testing it, and debugging it to ensure that it meets the requirements and works as intended.

5. **Evaluation**: After the solution has been implemented, it must be evaluated to determine whether it meets the requirements and solves the problem. This involves testing the solution, analyzing its performance, and making any necessary changes to improve it.

6. **Maintenance**: Once the solution has been evaluated and any necessary changes have been made, it must be maintained to ensure that it continues to work as intended. This involves fixing any bugs that are discovered, updating the solution to keep it current, and making any necessary changes to improve its performance.

In summary, the process of problem analysis and designing a solution involves analyzing the problem, specifying the requirements, designing a solution, implementing it, evaluating it, and maintaining it. Each of these steps is critical to the success of the solution and must be carefully considered and executed.
