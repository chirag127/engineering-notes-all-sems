### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

#### Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. An IOT based Smart Energy Meter is a device that measures the consumption of electrical energy and communicates the information to the electricity supplier for monitoring and billing purposes.
2. The device can be connected to the internet and can send real-time data to the supplier, allowing for more accurate billing and monitoring of energy usage.
3. The device can also detect any abnormal usage patterns, such as sudden spikes in energy consumption, which may indicate energy theft.
4. By implementing an IOT based Smart Energy Meter, energy suppliers can more effectively monitor and manage energy usage, leading to cost savings for both the supplier and the consumer.
5. The device can also provide the consumer with real-time information about their energy usage, allowing them to make more informed decisions about their energy consumption and potentially reduce their energy costs.
6. The implementation of IOT in the energy sector has the potential to revolutionize the way energy is monitored and managed, leading to increased efficiency and cost savings for all parties involved.
