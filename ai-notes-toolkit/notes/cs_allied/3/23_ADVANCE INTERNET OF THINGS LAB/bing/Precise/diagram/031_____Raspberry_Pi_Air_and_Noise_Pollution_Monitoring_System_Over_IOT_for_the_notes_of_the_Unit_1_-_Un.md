### Raspberry Pi Air and Noise Pollution Monitoring System Over IOT

The Raspberry Pi Air and Noise Pollution Monitoring System Over IOT is a system that uses sensors, a controller, an output device, and a Wi-Fi communication system to monitor air and sound pollution levels. The system contains three different sensors, which collect data that is continuously fed to a controller, in this case, a Raspberry Pi board.

The IOT-based method using Raspberry Pi is used to monitor and check live the Air Quality Index and the sound pollution of a region. The recommended technology comprises of two modules namely, the Air Quality Index Monitoring Module, the Sound Intensity Detection Module.

To measure the air and noise pollutants levels, the monitoring device employs a CO2 sensor, a methane sensor, and a microphone.

This system provides a convenient and efficient way to monitor air and sound pollution levels in real-time, allowing for timely action to be taken to address any issues that may arise.
