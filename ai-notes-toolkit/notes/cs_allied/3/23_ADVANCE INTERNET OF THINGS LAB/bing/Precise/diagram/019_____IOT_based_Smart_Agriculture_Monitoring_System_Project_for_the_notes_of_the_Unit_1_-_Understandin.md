### IOT based Smart Agriculture Monitoring System Project

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and share data.
2. IoT technology can be used in agriculture to create a smart agriculture monitoring system.
3. This system can use sensors to collect data on various environmental factors such as temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and light intensity.
4. The data collected by the sensors can be transmitted to a central server or cloud platform for analysis and processing.
5. Based on the data, the system can make decisions and take actions to optimize crop growth and yield.
6. For example, the system can automatically adjust irrigation, fertilization, and pest control based on the data collected by the sensors.
7. This can help farmers to increase their crop yield and reduce their costs by using resources more efficiently.
8. The implementation of IoT in agriculture can also help to improve food safety and traceability by providing real-time data on the growth and health of crops.
9. Overall, the use of IoT technology in agriculture can help to create a more sustainable and efficient food production system.
