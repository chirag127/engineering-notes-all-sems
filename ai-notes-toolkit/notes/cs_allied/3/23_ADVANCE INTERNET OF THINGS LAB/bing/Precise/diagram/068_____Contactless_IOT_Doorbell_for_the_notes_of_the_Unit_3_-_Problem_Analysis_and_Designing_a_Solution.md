### Contactless IOT Doorbell

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to alert the occupants of a building without physically touching the doorbell.
- This can be achieved through the use of sensors, such as infrared or proximity sensors, that detect the presence of a visitor and trigger the doorbell.
- The doorbell can be connected to the internet, allowing for remote monitoring and control.
- This can be useful in situations where the occupants are not physically present in the building, or for individuals with mobility or health issues that make it difficult to answer the door.
- The design of a contactless IOT doorbell involves analyzing the problem and identifying the requirements, such as the type of sensor to be used and the desired features of the doorbell.
- The solution can then be designed, taking into account factors such as the physical layout of the building and the desired user experience.
- The doorbell can be integrated with other IOT devices, such as smart locks and security cameras, to provide a comprehensive solution for building access and security.
