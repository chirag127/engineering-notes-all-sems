### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. The IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) technology to segregate garbage and monitor the level of garbage in bins.
2. The system uses sensors to detect the type of garbage and segregate it accordingly. For example, it can separate organic waste from inorganic waste.
3. The system also uses sensors to monitor the level of garbage in the bin. When the bin is full, the system sends a notification to the concerned authorities for garbage collection.
4. This system helps in efficient garbage management and reduces the burden on manual labor.
5. It also helps in reducing the negative impact of garbage on the environment by promoting proper segregation and disposal of waste.
6. The use of IoT technology in garbage management is a step towards solving societal problems and creating a cleaner and healthier environment.
