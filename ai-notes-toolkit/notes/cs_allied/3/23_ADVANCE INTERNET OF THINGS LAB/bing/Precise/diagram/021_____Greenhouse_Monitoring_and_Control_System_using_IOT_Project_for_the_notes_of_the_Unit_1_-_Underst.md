### Greenhouse Monitoring and Control System using IOT Project

- Greenhouse Monitoring and Control System using IOT Project is a system that uses the Internet of Things (IoT) to monitor and control the environmental conditions in a greenhouse     .
- The system uses various sensors to detect the temperature, light, humidity, and soil moisture in the greenhouse.
- A temperature sensor is used to detect the temperature inside the greenhouse. The readings from the sensor are sent to the microcontroller.
- The system can also monitor other environmental conditions such as the presence of fire.
- The goal of the system is to optimize the conditions for plant growth by continuously monitoring and controlling the environmental parameters .
- The system can automate the controlling of weather factors to enhance the plant growing process.
