### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

#### Introduction
- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can communicate with each other and exchange data.
- One of the applications of IoT is in waste management, where it can be used to develop smart garbage segregators and bin level indicators.
- These devices can help to improve the efficiency of waste collection and disposal, and reduce the environmental impact of waste.

#### Garbage Segregator
- An IoT garbage segregator is a device that can automatically sort waste into different categories, such as organic, recyclable, and non-recyclable.
- The device uses sensors and machine learning algorithms to identify the type of waste and sort it accordingly.
- This can help to reduce the amount of waste that goes to landfills and increase the amount of waste that is recycled.

#### Bin Level Indicator
- An IoT bin level indicator is a device that can monitor the fill level of a garbage bin and send notifications when the bin is full.
- The device uses sensors to detect the fill level and can communicate with a central server to send notifications to waste collection authorities.
- This can help to improve the efficiency of waste collection and reduce the amount of waste that overflows from bins.

#### Conclusion
- The implementation of IoT in waste management can help to improve the efficiency of waste collection and disposal, and reduce the environmental impact of waste.
- IoT garbage segregators and bin level indicators are two examples of how IoT can be used in waste management.
- These devices can help to reduce the amount of waste that goes to landfills, increase the amount of waste that is recycled, and improve the efficiency of waste collection.
