### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- An IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a remote-controlled boat that is used to monitor water pollution levels.
- The boat is equipped with sensors that record water quality data and transmit it to an IOT server online .
- This data can be used to help maintain clean water .
- The boat is remote-operated and can be maneuvered using an RC remote .
- A motorized propeller system provides forward motion .
- This technology can be used to solve societal problems by providing real-time information on water pollution levels, allowing for more effective management of water resources.
