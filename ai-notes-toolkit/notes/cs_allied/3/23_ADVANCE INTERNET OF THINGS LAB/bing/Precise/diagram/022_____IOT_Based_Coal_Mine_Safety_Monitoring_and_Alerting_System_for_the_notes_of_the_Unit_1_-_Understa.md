### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices that can collect and exchange data.
- In the context of coal mining, IoT can be used to improve safety by monitoring various parameters such as air quality, temperature, and humidity.
- Sensors can be placed throughout the mine to collect data on these parameters in real-time.
- The data collected by the sensors can be transmitted to a central monitoring system, where it can be analyzed to detect any potential safety hazards.
- If a hazard is detected, the system can alert the mine workers and management, allowing them to take appropriate action to prevent accidents.
- The use of IoT in coal mine safety monitoring can help to reduce the risk of accidents and improve overall safety in the mine.
- This system can also be used to monitor the health of the mine workers, by tracking their vital signs and alerting management if any abnormalities are detected.
- The implementation of an IoT-based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system can help to improve the safety and well-being of mine workers, while also increasing the efficiency and productivity of the mine.
