### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

Unit 1 - Understanding the implementation of IOT

1. A 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV with IOT Camera is a type of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) that is equipped with an IOT camera capable of capturing 360° images and videos.
2. The UAV can be remotely controlled and can fly over a specific area to capture real-time images and videos.
3. The IOT camera is connected to the internet, allowing the captured data to be transmitted and stored in the cloud for further analysis.
4. This technology can be used for various applications such as surveillance, monitoring, and inspection.
5. The implementation of IOT in this technology allows for real-time data transmission and analysis, making it a powerful tool for various industries.
6. The use of UAVs for aerial surveillance has several advantages, including the ability to cover large areas quickly and efficiently, and the ability to capture high-resolution images and videos.
7. The integration of IOT technology in UAVs is a growing trend, and it is expected to have a significant impact on various industries in the future.
