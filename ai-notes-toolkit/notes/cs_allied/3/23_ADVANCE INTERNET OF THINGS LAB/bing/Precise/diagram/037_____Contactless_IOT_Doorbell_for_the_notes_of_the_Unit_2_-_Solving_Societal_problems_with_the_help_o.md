### Contactless IOT Doorbell

A contactless IOT doorbell is a device that allows visitors to alert the occupants of a building or home that they are at the door without physically touching the doorbell. This can be achieved through various means, such as motion detection or voice activation.

1. **Reduced physical contact:** A contactless IOT doorbell reduces the need for physical contact, which can help prevent the spread of germs and viruses.
2. **Convenience:** A contactless IOT doorbell can be activated through various means, such as motion detection or voice activation, making it more convenient for visitors to use.
3. **Integration with other smart devices:** A contactless IOT doorbell can be integrated with other smart devices, such as a smart lock or a security camera, to provide additional functionality and security.
4. **Remote access:** A contactless IOT doorbell can be accessed remotely, allowing the occupants of the building or home to see and communicate with visitors even when they are not physically present.

In summary, a contactless IOT doorbell is a useful device that can help solve societal problems by reducing physical contact and increasing convenience and security. It is an example of how the Internet of Things can be used to improve our daily lives.
