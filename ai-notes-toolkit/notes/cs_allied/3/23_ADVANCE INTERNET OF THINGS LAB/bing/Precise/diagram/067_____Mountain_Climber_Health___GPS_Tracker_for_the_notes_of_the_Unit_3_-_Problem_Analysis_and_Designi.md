### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution

ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. Mountain climbers face several health risks while climbing, including altitude sickness, hypothermia, and dehydration.
2. A health and GPS tracker can help monitor the climber's vital signs and location in real-time.
3. The device can alert the climber or a remote monitoring team if any health issues are detected.
4. The GPS tracker can also help locate the climber in case of an emergency or if they get lost.
5. The device can be designed to be compact, lightweight, and durable to withstand the harsh conditions of mountain climbing.
6. The data collected by the device can be transmitted wirelessly to a remote monitoring team or a smartphone app.
7. The device can also provide useful information such as weather updates, route planning, and emergency contact information.
8. The design of the device should take into account the user's needs, such as ease of use, battery life, and reliability.
9. The device can be powered by a rechargeable battery or solar power.
10. The device can also incorporate other features such as a heart rate monitor, oxygen saturation monitor, and a pedometer.
