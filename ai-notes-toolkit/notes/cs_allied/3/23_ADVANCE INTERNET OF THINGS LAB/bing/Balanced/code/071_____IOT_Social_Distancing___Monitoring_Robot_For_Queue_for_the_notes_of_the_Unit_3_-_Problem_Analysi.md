### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

- The aim of this project is to design a robot that can monitor and enforce social distancing in queues, such as in banks, malls, schools, etc.   
- The robot uses a 4-wheel drive system and a line-following principle to move along with the queue and detect any violations of social distancing norms.  
- The robot is equipped with an ultrasonic sensor to measure the distance between individuals in the queue and a buzzer to alert them if they are too close.  
- The robot also has a camera and a Wi-Fi module to stream the video of the queue to a cloud server, where an image processing algorithm can count the number of people and calculate the average distance between them.   
- The robot can display the real-time statistics of the queue on an LCD screen, such as the number of people, the average distance, the waiting time, etc.   
- The robot can also communicate with other robots in the same area to coordinate the queue management and avoid congestion.  
- The robot can be controlled remotely through a web or mobile application, where the user can monitor the queue status, adjust the parameters, and send commands to the robot.   
- The robot can be powered by a rechargeable battery or a solar panel, depending on the availability of sunlight.  
- The robot can be customized to suit different environments and scenarios, such as indoor or outdoor, day or night, rainy or sunny, etc.    
- The robot can help prevent the spread of Covid-19 and other infectious diseases by ensuring social distancing in queues, which can reduce the risk of transmission and exposure.
