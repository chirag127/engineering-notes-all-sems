## Unit 2 - Solving Societal Problems with the help of IOT

- IOT stands for Internet of Things, which refers to the network of physical devices, sensors, actuators, and software that can collect, process, and exchange data over the internet.
- IOT can be used to solve various societal problems, such as environmental issues, health care, education, agriculture, transportation, and security.
- Some examples of how IOT can solve societal problems are:

  - Environmental issues: IOT can help monitor and reduce air pollution, water pollution, waste management, and energy consumption by using smart sensors, cloud computing, and data analytics. For instance, smart bins can detect the level of waste and send alerts to the waste collectors, smart meters can measure and optimize energy usage, and smart air quality sensors can provide real-time feedback and alerts to the users and authorities.
  - Health care: IOT can help improve the quality and accessibility of health care by using wearable devices, remote monitoring, telemedicine, and artificial intelligence. For instance, wearable devices can track the vital signs and activities of the patients and send alerts to the doctors, remote monitoring can enable the doctors to diagnose and treat the patients from a distance, telemedicine can provide online consultations and prescriptions, and artificial intelligence can assist the doctors in decision making and diagnosis.
  - Education: IOT can help enhance the learning experience and outcomes by using smart classrooms, interactive boards, virtual reality, and gamification. For instance, smart classrooms can adjust the lighting, temperature, and sound according to the preferences and needs of the students and teachers, interactive boards can enable the students and teachers to share and collaborate on the content, virtual reality can provide immersive and realistic simulations of the topics, and gamification can make the learning process more fun and engaging.
  - Agriculture: IOT can help improve the productivity and sustainability of agriculture by using smart irrigation, precision farming, and drones. For instance, smart irrigation can monitor and control the water level and quality of the soil and crops, precision farming can optimize the use of fertilizers, pesticides, and seeds according to the soil and crop conditions, and drones can assist in crop monitoring, spraying, and harvesting.
  - Transportation: IOT can help improve the efficiency and safety of transportation by using smart vehicles, smart traffic management, and smart parking. For instance, smart vehicles can communicate with each other and the infrastructure to avoid collisions, optimize routes, and reduce emissions, smart traffic management can monitor and control the traffic flow and signals to reduce congestion and accidents, and smart parking can guide the drivers to the available parking spaces and automate the payment process.
  - Security: IOT can help enhance the security and privacy of the users and the data by using biometric authentication, encryption, and blockchain. For instance, biometric authentication can verify the identity of the users by using their fingerprints, face, or voice, encryption can protect the data from unauthorized access and modification, and blockchain can provide a decentralized and transparent ledger of the data transactions and ownership.
