### IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG

- IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG is a system that uses electrocardiogram (ECG) sensors and internet of things (IOT) devices to monitor the heart condition of a patient remotely and in real-time.
- ECG is a technique that measures the electrical activity of the heart and displays it as a waveform or graph. ECG can help diagnose various heart problems such as arrhythmias, heart attacks, and heart failure.
- IOT is a network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, actuators, and communication modules that can interact with each other and with other devices or services over the internet.
- IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG consists of three main components: ECG sensor, IOT device, and cloud platform.
  - ECG sensor is a device that attaches to the chest or limbs of the patient and captures the ECG signals. The sensor can be a single-lead or multi-lead device that measures the voltage difference between two or more electrodes. The sensor can also measure other parameters such as heart rate and respiration rate.
  - IOT device is a device that connects to the ECG sensor and transmits the ECG data to the cloud platform. The device can be a microcontroller, such as Arduino, or a microcomputer, such as Raspberry Pi, that has a wireless module, such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. The device can also process, analyze, and display the ECG data locally or on a smartphone app.
  - Cloud platform is a service that receives, stores, and processes the ECG data from the IOT device. The platform can also provide visualization, notification, and diagnosis features for the ECG data. The platform can be a public cloud, such as AWS or Ubidots, or a private cloud, such as a hospital server.
- IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG has several advantages, such as:
  - It can provide continuous and real-time monitoring of the heart condition of the patient, which can help detect and prevent potential cardiac events.
  - It can reduce the cost and inconvenience of visiting a hospital or clinic for ECG tests, which can improve the accessibility and quality of health care.
  - It can enable remote consultation and collaboration among medical professionals, patients, and caregivers, which can enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of diagnosis and treatment.
  - It can generate large and diverse data sets of ECG signals, which can facilitate research and innovation in cardiac health and IOT applications.
