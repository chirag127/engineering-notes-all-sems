### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- IOT based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system is a project that aims to improve the safety and security of coal miners and detect the hazards inside a coal mine .
- The system consists of sensors, an IoT gateway, an LCD screen, an RF transmitter, and a cloud platform .
- The sensors are installed in the transmitter module, which is carried by the coal miners or placed in strategic locations in the coal mine.
- The sensors can measure the temperature, smoke, methane, water level, and earthquake in the coal mine  .
- The sensor data is transmitted to the IoT gateway using a low power communication protocol such as LoRa or Zigbee.
- The IoT gateway analyzes the sensor data and displays it on the LCD screen. It also sends the data to the cloud platform through the internet .
- The cloud platform can store, process, and visualize the sensor data, and provide alerts and notifications to the authorities and the coal miners in case of any emergency or abnormal situation .
- The RF transmitter can also send signals to the receiver module, which is connected to a buzzer and a LED, to alert the coal miners about the hazards.
- The system can help to prevent accidents, save lives, and reduce the environmental impact of coal mining .
