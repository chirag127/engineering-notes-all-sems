# IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor the energy consumption and detect the power theft in real time.
- The system consists of smart energy meters installed at the consumer end and the distribution end, which communicate with each other and a central server through wireless or wired network.
- The smart energy meters measure the voltage, current, power, energy and other parameters of the electricity supply and send the data to the server periodically or on demand.
- The server analyzes the data and compares it with the predefined threshold values or the historical data to detect any abnormality or discrepancy in the energy consumption or supply.
- The server also generates reports and alerts for the utility company and the consumers about the energy usage, billing, power quality and power theft.
- The system can also use statistical regression methods, machine learning algorithms or artificial neural networks to identify the patterns and trends of the power theft and improve the accuracy and efficiency of the detection.
- The system can also use smart contracts or blockchain technology to ensure the security and transparency of the data and transactions.
- The system can also use android applications or web interfaces to provide the user-friendly and interactive visualization and control of the smart energy meters and the server.
- The system can reduce the energy wastage, improve the power quality, increase the revenue for the utility company, and enhance the customer satisfaction and awareness.
