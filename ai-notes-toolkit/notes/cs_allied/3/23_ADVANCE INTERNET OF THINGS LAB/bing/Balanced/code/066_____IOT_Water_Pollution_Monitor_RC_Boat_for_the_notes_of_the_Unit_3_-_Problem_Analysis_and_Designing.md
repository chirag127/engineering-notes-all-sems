### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is a project that aims to measure and transmit water quality data using an RC boat equipped with sensors and an IOT module  .
- The project can be divided into four main components: the RC boat, the sensors, the IOT module, and the online server.
- The RC boat is a remote-controlled vehicle that can be maneuvered on the water surface using a motorized propeller system and a steering mechanism. The boat also has a battery, a microcontroller, and a wireless transmitter to communicate with the IOT module  .
- The sensors are attached to the RC boat and measure various parameters of water quality, such as pH, turbidity, temperature, dissolved oxygen, etc. The sensors send the data to the microcontroller, which processes and encodes it for transmission  .
- The IOT module is a device that receives the data from the RC boat via wireless communication and uploads it to an online server using a Wi-Fi or cellular network. The IOT module can be a smartphone, a laptop, or a dedicated device .
- The online server is a platform that stores and displays the water quality data received from the IOT module. The server can also provide analysis, visualization, and alert functions for the data. The server can be accessed by authorized users through a web browser or an app .

- The project can be useful for monitoring and maintaining the water quality of various water bodies, such as lakes, rivers, ponds, etc. The project can also help in detecting and preventing water pollution and its effects on the environment and human health  .
