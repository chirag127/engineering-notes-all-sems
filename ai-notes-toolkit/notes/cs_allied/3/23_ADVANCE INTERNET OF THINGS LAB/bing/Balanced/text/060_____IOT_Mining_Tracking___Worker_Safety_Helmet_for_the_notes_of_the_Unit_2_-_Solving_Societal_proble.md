### IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet

- IOT Mining Tracking & Worker Safety Helmet is a system that aims to improve the safety and efficiency of workers in the mining industry by using Internet of Things (IoT) technology .
- The system consists of two main components: a smart helmet and a tracker circuit .
- The smart helmet is worn by the workers and is equipped with sensors that can detect hazardous events such as high temperature, low humidity, gas leakage, fire, etc .
- The smart helmet also has a radio frequency (RF) module that can transmit the sensor data and the worker's identity and location to the tracker circuit .
- The tracker circuit is installed at the mining site and is connected to the Internet. It can receive the data from the smart helmets and send it to a cloud server or a web application for monitoring and analysis .
- The system can provide real-time information about the workers' health, location, and environment, and alert the authorities in case of any emergency  .
- The system can also help optimize the mining operations by reducing the risk of accidents, improving the productivity, and saving the resources  .
- The system is cost-effective, practical, eco-friendly, and reliable, and can be applied to various types of mining fields  .
