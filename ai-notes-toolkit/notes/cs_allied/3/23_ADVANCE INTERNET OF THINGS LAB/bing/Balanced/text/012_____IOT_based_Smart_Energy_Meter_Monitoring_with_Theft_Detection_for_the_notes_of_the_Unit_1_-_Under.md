### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor the energy consumption and detect the power theft in real-time.
- The system consists of smart energy meters installed at the consumer end and the distribution end, which communicate with each other and a central server through wireless or wired network.
- The smart energy meters measure the voltage, current, power, and energy parameters and send them to the server periodically or on demand.
- The server analyzes the data and compares the consumer end and distribution end readings to detect any discrepancy or abnormality that indicates power theft.
- The server also displays the energy consumption and billing information on a web or mobile application for the consumers and the utility company.
- The system can alert the utility company or the authorities in case of power theft detection and also disconnect the power supply remotely if needed.
- The system can also provide feedback to the consumers on their energy usage and suggest ways to reduce it and save money.
- The system can improve the efficiency and reliability of the power distribution network and reduce the losses due to power theft and wastage.
