### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

- A drainage monitoring system plays a significant role in keeping towns and cities healthy and clean .
- Most of the manholes are open without any observation that cause accidents . In India, many cities adopted emptying underground system because it is vital.
- Manual scavengers who clean the underground drainage face death risk and health hazards. Also, contamination of fresh water due to problem in sewage drainage system is of concern.
- IOT based manhole detection and monitoring system is a solution that uses sensors to detect and send alerts to authorities via GSM and GPS module when any manhole crosses its threshold values    .
- The system can monitor the water level, gas level, temperature, and pressure inside the manhole and send real-time data to a cloud server .
- The system can also detect the presence of manhole cover and alert if it is broken or missing.
- The system can reduce the death risk of manual scavengers, prevent urban floods, and improve the sanitation and safety of the city  .
