### Wearable Computer With Temperature Distance Sensors

- A wearable computer is a device that can be worn on the body and can perform computing tasks such as processing, storing, and displaying information.
- A wearable computer with temperature distance sensors is a type of wearable computer that can measure the temperature and distance of objects or people using contactless sensors such as lidar and infrared.
- Some of the applications of wearable computer with temperature distance sensors are:
  - Health monitoring: The device can monitor the body temperature of the wearer or other people and alert them of any abnormality or fever. It can also measure the distance between the wearer and other people and help them maintain social distancing during pandemics or crowded situations.
  - Navigation: The device can help the wearer navigate in unfamiliar or dark environments by detecting obstacles and providing feedback on the distance and direction. It can also provide maps and directions on the display or through voice commands.
  - Education: The device can enhance the learning experience of the wearer by providing interactive and immersive content on the display or through audio. It can also measure the temperature and distance of various objects or phenomena and help the wearer learn about science, math, or geography.
- Some of the components of wearable computer with temperature distance sensors are:
  - Raspberry Pi: This is a small and low-cost computer that can run various operating systems and programs. It can act as the main controller and processor of the device and communicate with other components through GPIO pins or wireless modules.
  - Battery: This is a rechargeable power source that can provide electricity to the device and its components. It can be attached to the wrist strap or the back of the device and connected to the Raspberry Pi through wires or a power bank.
  - Touch screen display: This is a small and flexible screen that can display graphics, text, or video on the device. It can also act as an input device that can detect the touch or gesture of the wearer and respond accordingly. It can be mounted on the front of the device and connected to the Raspberry Pi through HDMI or SPI.
  - Lidar sensor: This is a sensor that can measure the distance and speed of objects or people by emitting laser pulses and detecting the reflected signals. It can provide accurate and high-resolution data on the distance and direction of the target. It can be mounted on the side or the top of the device and connected to the Raspberry Pi through I2C or UART.
  - Temperature sensor: This is a sensor that can measure the temperature of objects or people by detecting the infrared radiation emitted by them. It can provide fast and contactless data on the temperature and alert the wearer of any abnormality or fever. It can be mounted on the side or the bottom of the device and connected to the Raspberry Pi through I2C or SPI.
