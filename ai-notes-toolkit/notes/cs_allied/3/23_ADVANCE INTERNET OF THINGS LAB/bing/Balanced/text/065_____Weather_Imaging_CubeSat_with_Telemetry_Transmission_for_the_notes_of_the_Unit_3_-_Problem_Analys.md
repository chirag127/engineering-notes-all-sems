### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite that has a standard size of 10x10x10 cm and a mass of up to 1.33 kg. CubeSats can be deployed in low Earth orbit for various applications, such as communication, GPS, remote sensing, and scientific research .
- A weather imaging CubeSat is a CubeSat that is equipped with a camera or a radiometer to capture images or measurements of the Earth's atmosphere, clouds, and precipitation. These data can be used for weather prediction and forecasting systems  .
- A telemetry transmission CubeSat is a CubeSat that is capable of transmitting data back to the Earth using a radio or a laser communication system. The data can be received by ground stations or other satellites and processed for analysis .
- A weather imaging CubeSat with telemetry transmission is a CubeSat that combines both functions of weather imaging and data transmission. It can provide real-time or near-real-time information about the weather conditions and phenomena in different regions of the Earth  .
- A weather imaging CubeSat with telemetry transmission can be designed and developed using the following steps:
  - Define the problem and the objectives of the CubeSat mission, such as the target area, the resolution, the frequency, and the duration of the observations.
  - Identify the requirements and constraints of the CubeSat system, such as the size, mass, power, orbit, communication, and data rate.
  - Select the appropriate components and subsystems for the CubeSat, such as the structure, the payload, the attitude control, the power supply, the communication, and the onboard computer.
  - Design and test the CubeSat hardware and software, such as the circuit boards, the sensors, the antennas, the algorithms, and the protocols.
  - Integrate and verify the CubeSat functionality and performance, such as the assembly, the wiring, the calibration, the environmental testing, and the launch readiness.
  - Deploy and operate the CubeSat in orbit, such as the launch, the separation, the activation, the commissioning, the data acquisition, and the data transmission.
  - Analyze and disseminate the CubeSat data, such as the processing, the visualization, the validation, the interpretation, and the publication.
