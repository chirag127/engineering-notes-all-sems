### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection

- IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection is a system that uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to monitor the energy consumption and detect the power theft in real-time.
- The system consists of smart energy meters installed at the consumer end and the distribution end, which communicate with each other and a central server through wireless or wired networks.
- The smart energy meters measure the voltage, current, power, and energy parameters and send them to the server periodically or on demand.
- The server analyzes the data and compares the consumer end and distribution end readings to detect any discrepancy or abnormality that indicates power theft.
- The server also displays the data on a web or mobile application for the authorized users to monitor the energy consumption and billing information.
- The system can alert the users and the authorities in case of power theft detection and provide the location and details of the theft.
- The system can also control the smart energy meters remotely and disconnect the power supply in case of non-payment or unauthorized usage.
- The system can reduce the energy losses, improve the efficiency, and increase the revenue for the power utility companies.
- The system can also help the consumers to save energy, reduce the bills, and avoid the penalties for power theft.
