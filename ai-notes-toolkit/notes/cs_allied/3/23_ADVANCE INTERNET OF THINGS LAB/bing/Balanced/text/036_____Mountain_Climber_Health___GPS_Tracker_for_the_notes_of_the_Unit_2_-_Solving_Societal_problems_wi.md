### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

- This is a project that aims to solve the societal problem of mountain climbers getting lost, injured, or suffering from health issues during their expeditions.
- The project uses Internet of Things (IoT) devices and sensors to monitor the climber's location, vital signs, and environmental conditions in real time.
- The project also uses a cloud-based platform to store, process, and visualize the data collected from the IoT devices and sensors, and to provide alerts and notifications to the climber and the rescue team in case of emergencies.
- The main components of the project are:

  - A wearable device that the climber wears on their wrist, which contains a GPS module, a heart rate sensor, a temperature sensor, a humidity sensor, a pressure sensor, and a battery.
  - A mobile application that the climber uses to communicate with the wearable device via Bluetooth, and to access the cloud platform via Wi-Fi or cellular network.
  - A cloud platform that the climber and the rescue team use to view the climber's location, vital signs, and environmental conditions on a map, and to receive alerts and notifications in case of anomalies or emergencies.
  - A web dashboard that the rescue team uses to monitor the climber's status and to coordinate the rescue operations if needed.

- The main benefits of the project are:

  - It enhances the safety and security of the climber by providing real-time information and alerts about their location, vital signs, and environmental conditions.
  - It reduces the risk of getting lost, injured, or suffering from health issues by detecting and notifying the climber and the rescue team of any potential hazards or emergencies.
  - It facilitates the rescue operations by providing accurate and timely information about the climber's status and location, and by enabling the rescue team to communicate with the climber via the mobile application.
  - It improves the climber's experience and performance by providing feedback and suggestions based on the data collected from the IoT devices and sensors.
