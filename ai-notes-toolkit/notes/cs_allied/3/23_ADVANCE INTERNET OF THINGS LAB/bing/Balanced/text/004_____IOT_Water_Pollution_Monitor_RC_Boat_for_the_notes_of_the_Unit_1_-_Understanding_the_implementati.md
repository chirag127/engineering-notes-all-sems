### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- An IOT water pollution monitor RC boat is a remote-controlled device that can measure and transmit water quality parameters to an online server using internet of things (IOT) technology  .
- The main components of an IOT water pollution monitor RC boat are:
  - A boat chassis with a motorized propeller system and a battery pack.
  - A microcontroller unit (MCU) that controls the boat movement and the sensor data acquisition.
  - A wireless module that connects the MCU to the internet via Wi-Fi or cellular network.
  - A set of sensors that measure water quality parameters such as pH, temperature, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, etc.
  - A display unit that shows the sensor readings and the boat status.
  - An RC remote that allows the user to steer the boat and switch between different modes of operation.
- The main advantages of an IOT water pollution monitor RC boat are:
  - It can access hard-to-reach areas of water bodies and collect real-time data.
  - It can reduce the human effort and risk involved in manual water sampling and testing.
  - It can provide a visual feedback of the water condition and alert the user of any abnormality.
  - It can store and upload the data to a cloud server for further analysis and visualization.
  - It can help in monitoring and maintaining the water quality and preventing water pollution.
