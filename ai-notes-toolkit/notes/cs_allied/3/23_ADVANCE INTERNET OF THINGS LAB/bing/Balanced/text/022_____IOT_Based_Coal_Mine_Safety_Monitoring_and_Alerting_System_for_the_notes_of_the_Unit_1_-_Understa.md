### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- IOT based coal mine safety monitoring and alerting system is a project that aims to improve the safety and security of coal miners and detect the hazards inside a coal mine  .
- The system consists of sensors, such as temperature, smoke, methane, and earthquake sensors, that are installed in the transmitter module inside the coal mine  .
- The transmitter module communicates with a central IoT gateway using a low power communication protocol, such as LoRa, and sends the data to the gateway for analysis  .
- The IoT gateway can display the data locally on an LCD screen or send it to the cloud through the internet for further processing and visualization  .
- The system can also alert the miners and the authorities in case of any abnormal conditions, such as high temperature, smoke, methane, or earthquake, using an RF receiver, a buzzer, and an LED  .
- The system can help to prevent accidents, save lives, and reduce losses in the coal mining industry   .
