### IOT Garbage Segregator & Bin Level Indicator

- Garbage segregation is the process of sorting waste into categories for recycling and disposal. IOT can help in automating this process using sensors and actuators.
- A garbage bin can be equipped with multiple sensors to detect the type of waste being dumped into it like metal, plastic, glass, paper, organic waste, etc. Based on the sensor outputs, a microcontroller can trigger actuators to route the waste into segregated bins for each category.
- The filling level of the garbage bins can also be monitored using fill level sensors. These sensor data can be transmitted over the internet to generate alerts for the garbage collection teams when the bins are full and need to be emptied.
- Such an IOT based smart garbage segregator and bin level indicator system helps in efficient waste management by automating the segregation process and tracking the bin fill levels. It makes the solid waste management system more effective, productive and optimized.
