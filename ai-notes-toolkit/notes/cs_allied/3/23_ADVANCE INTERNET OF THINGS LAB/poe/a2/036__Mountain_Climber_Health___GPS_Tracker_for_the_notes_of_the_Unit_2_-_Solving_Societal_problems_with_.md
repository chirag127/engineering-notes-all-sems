### Mountain Climber Health & GPS Tracker

- An IoT device can be developed to track the health parameters and location of mountain climbers.
- Sensors can be used to track heart rate, oxygen level, body temperature, etc. and send the data to a base station.
- A GPS module can track the location of the climbers and send location coordinates to the base station.
- The base station can monitor the health parameters and location of all climbers and alert in case of any emergency or abnormal readings.
- This can help in ensuring the safety of mountain climbers and enabling timely medical help in case of any health issues.
- The data can also be used to analyze the effects of high altitude on human body and take necessary precautions.
- The components required would be sensors, microcontroller, communication modules, GPS module, and power supply.
- The data can be sent to the base station via wireless communication technologies like Wi-Fi, GSM, etc. depending on the terrain and range.
