### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

1. Patients in home quarantine due to Covid-19 need to be monitored continuously for their health parameters like body temperature, blood oxygen levels, heart rate, etc. to identify health deterioration early.
2. An IOT based health monitoring system can be designed for covid patients in home quarantine. It will have medical sensors to capture the health parameters and a gateway device to transmit the data to a monitoring system.
3. The medical sensors can include temperature sensor, pulse oximeter sensor to measure blood oxygen levels and heart rate, ECG sensors to monitor heart activity, etc. These sensors can be wireless and worn by the patient.
4. The gateway device can collect the data from the sensors and transmit it to a monitoring system over the internet. It can have cellular or wifi connectivity to transmit the data.
5. The monitoring system can have an app or dashboard to display the health status and parameters of the patients. It can set thresholds for each parameter and trigger alerts if any abnormality is detected to help early intervention.
6. The system can help reduce the load on healthcare systems by enabling remote monitoring of quarantined covid patients and identifying health deterioration early to provide necessary medical assistance. It improves the safety of healthcare workers by reducing their exposure to infected patients.

The above points cover the key aspects of designing an IOT based health monitoring system for covid patients in home quarantine. The sensors, gateway device and monitoring system work together to continuously track the health parameters and notify if any medical attention is required.
