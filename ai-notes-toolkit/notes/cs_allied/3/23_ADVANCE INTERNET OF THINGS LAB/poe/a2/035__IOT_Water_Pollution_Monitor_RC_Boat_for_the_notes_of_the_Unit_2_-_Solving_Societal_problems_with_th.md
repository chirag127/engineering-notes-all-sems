### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- An IOT based RC boat is designed to monitor water pollution parameters like pH, temperature, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, etc. and send the data to a central server.
- The RC boat has sensors to detect the water pollution parameters which are connected to a microcontroller.
- The microcontroller collects the data from the sensors and sends it to a WiFi module.
- The WiFi module is connected to the internet and sends the data to a central server.
- The central server stores and analyzes the data and generates alerts if the pollution levels cross threshold limits.
- The alerts can be sent to relevant government authorities to take necessary actions.
- The RC boat can be remotely controlled to move to different locations of a water body and take measurements at multiple points leading to comprehensive water quality monitoring.
- IOT enables remote and real-time monitoring of water pollution which can help in better management and conservation of water resources.
