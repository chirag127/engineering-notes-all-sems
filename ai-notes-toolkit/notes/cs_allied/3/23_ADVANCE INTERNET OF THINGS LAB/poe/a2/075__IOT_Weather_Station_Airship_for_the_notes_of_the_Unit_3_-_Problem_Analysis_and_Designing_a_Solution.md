### IOT Weather Station Airship for the notes of the Unit 3 - Problem Analysis and Designing a Solution in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

1. Problem statement: To design an IOT based weather station that can be placed at higher altitudes using an airship for continuous weather monitoring.
2. Requirements:
    1. Sensors: Temperature, Humidity, Pressure, Wind speed, Wind direction, Altitude
    2. Microcontroller: To control the sensors and transmit the data
    3. Communication: Wireless communication module to transmit the sensor data
    4. Power supply: Battery to power the sensors, microcontroller and communication module
    5. Airship: To place the weather station at higher altitudes
3. Design:
    1. The sensors will be interfaced with the microcontroller which will be programmed to continuously sense the data and transmit it wirelessly.
    2. The microcontroller, communication module and power supply will be placed inside a protective enclosure.
    3. The protective enclosure will be attached to the airship. The airship will be filled with helium to generate required lift for reaching higher altitudes.
    4. The wireless data can be received at the ground station and monitored.
4. Working: The airship will fly to the required altitude. The sensors will continuously sense the weather parameters and transmit the data wirelessly. The data can be monitored at the ground station to get real-time weather updates from higher altitudes.
