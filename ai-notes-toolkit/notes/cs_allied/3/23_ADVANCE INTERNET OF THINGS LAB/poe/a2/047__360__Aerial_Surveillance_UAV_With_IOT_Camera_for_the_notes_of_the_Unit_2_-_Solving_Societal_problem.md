### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

- Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) or drones equipped with Internet of Things (IOT) cameras can be used for aerial surveillance of large areas.
- The IOT camera can provide a 360° view of the surroundings and stream real-time high resolution video to the base station.
- The wide coverage and high detail of the video can be useful for surveillance of borders, public gatherings, disaster management, wildlife monitoring, etc.
- The UAV can fly autonomously or be controlled remotely based on the application. Autonomous flight mode can be coordinated with other drones to provide uninterrupted surveillance.
- The video can be monitored live by security personnel or analyzed using computer vision and deep learning for automatic detection and tracking of objects, people, vehicles, etc.
- The capabilities of IOT and drones can be combined to enable smart surveillance systems for assisting security forces and enabling quick responses in case of unauthorized activities or dangerous situations.
