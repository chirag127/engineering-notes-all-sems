### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

- Patients in quarantine can be monitored remotely using IOT devices and sensors.
- Body temperature, heart rate, blood pressure and oxygen saturation level can be continuously monitored using wireless sensors.
- The sensor data can be collected by a central IOT gateway which can alert the medical staff in case of abnormalities.
- Patients can be provided with an emergency alert button to alert the staff in case of emergencies like difficulty in breathing.
- The location of the patient can also be tracked using beacons or GPS to ensure the patient stays in quarantine.
- The patient health data can be used to identify deteriorating health conditions early and provide timely medical assistance.
- IOT can enable remote monitoring of large numbers of quarantined patients by medical staff and reduce burden on hospitals.
- Concerns need to be addressed around data security, privacy and accuracy of sensors. Proper protocols need to be in place to handle health alerts and emergencies.
