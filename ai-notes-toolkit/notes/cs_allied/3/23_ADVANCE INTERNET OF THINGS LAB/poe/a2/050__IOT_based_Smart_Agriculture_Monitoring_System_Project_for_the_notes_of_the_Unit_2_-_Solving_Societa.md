### IOT based Smart Agriculture Monitoring System Project

- The project aims to monitor the agricultural fields using IOT sensors and devices.
- The sensors collect the data related to moisture, temperature, pH, soil nutrient content, etc. of the field.
- The collected data is sent to the microcontroller which processes the data and sends it to the cloud for storage and analysis.
- The data is analyzed to get insights into the health of crops and the required actions like irrigation, fertilization, etc.
- The analyzed data and actions are sent to the farmer through a mobile app or web interface for better decision making and optimization of agricultural produce.
- The system allows monitoring the field conditions from anywhere and automating the tasks, saving time and increasing yield.
- It demonstrates the use of IOT in solving societal problems like improving agriculture and food supply.
