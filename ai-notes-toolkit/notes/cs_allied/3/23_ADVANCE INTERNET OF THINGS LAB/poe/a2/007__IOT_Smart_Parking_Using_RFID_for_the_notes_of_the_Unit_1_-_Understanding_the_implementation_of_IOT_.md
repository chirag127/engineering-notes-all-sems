### IOT Smart Parking Using RFID

- RFID tags are placed on each parking slot.
- RFID reader is placed at the entry and exit of the parking.
- When a vehicle enters, the RFID tag on the slot is read by the reader and the slot number is allocated to the vehicle number in the database.
- When the vehicle exits, the slot number is marked vacant in the database.
- The availability of slots is updated in the database and displayed on a website or mobile app.
- Vacant slots are allocated to the incoming vehicles on first-come, first-served basis.
- This system removes manual tracking of occupied and vacant slots.
- It makes the parking system efficient, smart, and accurate.
- Hardware requirements: RFID tags, RFID readers, database, processor, and networking components.
