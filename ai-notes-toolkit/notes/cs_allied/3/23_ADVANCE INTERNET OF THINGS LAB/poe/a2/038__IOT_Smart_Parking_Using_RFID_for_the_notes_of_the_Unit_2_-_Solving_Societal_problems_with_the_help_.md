### IOT Smart Parking Using RFID

- RFID tags are placed on each parking slot.
- Each vehicle is equipped with an RFID tag or card.
- RFID reader is placed at the entry and exit of the parking lot.
- When a vehicle enters, the RFID tag is read and the slot number is allocated. The entry time and slot number are recorded.
- When the vehicle exits, the slot number and exit time are recorded.
- Based on this, the parking fees are calculated.
- The availability of slots is displayed on an LCD for guidance.
- This system helps reduce clutter and traffic in finding a parking slot.
- It leads to efficient utilization of available slots and generates accurate billing.
- The data can be used for statistical analysis of parking patterns.
