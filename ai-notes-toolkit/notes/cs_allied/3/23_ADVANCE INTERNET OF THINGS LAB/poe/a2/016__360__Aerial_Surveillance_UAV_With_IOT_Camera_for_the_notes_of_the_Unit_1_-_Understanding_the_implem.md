### 360° Aerial Surveillance UAV With IOT Camera

- A aerial surveillance drone with an Internet of Things (IOT) camera is used to capture 360° view of a specific area.
- The drone is fitted with a wide angle IOT camera which continuously captures and streams the aerial view footage to a central server.
- The streamed footage is stored in the central server and can be accessed simultaneously by multiple users to monitor the area.
- The drone can be controlled to change direction and height to get different views of the area under surveillance.
- The captured footage can be used to identify and track unauthorized activity, monitor high-security areas and provide an aerial view during emergency rescue operations.
- The drone streams high-definition footage to the server via Wi-Fi or cellular connectivity for accessing the footage from anywhere with an Internet connection.
- The implementation of IOT camera with drone makes the aerial surveillance autonomous, continuous, integrated and accessible from anywhere anytime.
