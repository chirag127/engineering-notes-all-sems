### IOT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance

1. Sensors: Place water level sensors at various locations near rivers or low lying areas to detect increase in water level. These sensors can detect even a slight increase in water level and alert the system.
2. Microcontrollers: The sensors are connected to microcontrollers which will monitor the sensor data continuously. As soon as the water level increases beyond a threshold, the microcontroller will trigger an alert.
3. Alert system: The alert system can include flashing lights, sirens, SMS or email alerts to the concerned authorities to take action. This alert will be sent out as soon as the microcontroller detects a dangerous increase in water level.
4. Remedial actions: Once the alert is received, the authorities can take remedial actions like informing the local residents to evacuate or taking steps to control the water level by releasing water from dams or other water bodies. These steps can help in reducing the damage due to floods.
5. Additional data collection: Other data like rainfall, humidity, etc. can also be collected to get a more accurate prediction of floods. This additional data along with the water level can help in generating a robust flood detection and prediction system.

The above points cover the key steps involved in implementing an IOT based early flood detection and avoidance system. A combination of sensors, microcontrollers, alert systems and remedial actions can help in developing an effective flood detection mechanism to reduce loss of lives and property.
