### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

- An IOT based system can be developed to scan the temperature and mask of a person entering a premises.
- A temperature sensor can be used to measure the body temperature of a person. If the temperature is above a certain threshold, it indicates fever and the person can be denied entry.
- A mask detection system using computer vision can check if the person is wearing a mask. If no mask is detected, the person can be denied entry.
- The data from the sensors can be sent to a microcontroller which can take a decision on allowing or denying entry based on temperature and mask detection.
- The microcontroller can be interfaced with a buzzer or LED which can indicate allowed or denied entry.
- The system can maintain logs of people entering and their body temperature and mask status which can be useful for contact tracing if any person is detected with COVID-19 symptoms.
- Such an IOT based system can be deployed at offices, hospitals, schools, etc. to regulate entry and maintain safety during the pandemic.
