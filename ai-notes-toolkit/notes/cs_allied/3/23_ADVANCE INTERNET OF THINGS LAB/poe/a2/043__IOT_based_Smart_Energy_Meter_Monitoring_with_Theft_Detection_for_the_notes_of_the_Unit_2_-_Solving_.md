### IOT based Smart Energy Meter Monitoring with Theft Detection for the notes of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB

- Smart Energy Meters monitor the power consumption in real time and alerts the consumer for any unusual activity or peak usage. This helps the consumer to optimize the power usage and save energy.
- Theft of electricity is a major problem faced by power distribution companies. By using smart energy meters which sends the readings to the server, any tampering of the meter or power theft can be detected.
- The smart energy meter will have sensors to detect the voltage, current and power factor. It will have a SIM card to send the readings to the server.
- The readings from the smart energy meters are sent to a central server which analyzes the data to detect any theft or unusual activity. The power usage pattern is studied and any significant deviation from the normal pattern is flagged for inspection.
- The utility company can remotely disconnect the power supply in case of any theft detected. This will reduce the revenue loss for the power distribution companies.
- The consumers can access the power consumption data and analyze their usage pattern. This enables them to optimize the power usage and reduce their electricity bills.
- The smart energy meters when implemented at a large scale can help the utility companies in better power management and planning as the power consumption pattern will be available as data.
