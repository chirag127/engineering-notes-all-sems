### Contactless IOT Doorbell

* A contactless IoT doorbell is a smart doorbell that eliminates the need to physically push a button to ring the bell. It uses sensors to detect a person's presence near the door and rings the bell automatically.
* It contains sensors like proximity sensors, motion sensors or pressure sensors to detect a person's presence near the door. Once detected, it sends a signal to the bell to ring.
* The IoT doorbell is connected to the home Wi-Fi network. It can send notifications to the homeowner's smartphone on detecting a person at the door. The homeowner can view who is at the door via a camera and communicate using a two-way audio system.
* Benefits:
    * Contactless and hygienic. Eliminates touching surfaces in current times.
    * Convenient for people with limited mobility or disabilities. They don't have to physically push the bell.
    * Can send alerts and enable communication even when the homeowner is not at home.
* Components required:
    * Sensors to detect presence (proximity sensor, motion sensor, etc.)
    * IoT enabled doorbell with bell, camera, speaker and microphone
    * Connectivity to home Wi-Fi network
* Working:
    1. The sensors continuously monitor the area near the door for presence detection
    2. On detecting a person, the sensors send a signal to the IoT doorbell to ring the bell
    3. The IoT doorbell sends an alert notification to the homeowner's smartphone regarding the detection
    4. The homeowner can view and communicate via the doorbell's camera and audio system
