### IOT Based Coal Mine Safety Monitoring and Alerting System

- IOT sensors are placed at various locations inside the coal mine to sense parameters like temperature, methane gas, carbon monoxide levels, presence of fire, smoke, seismic activities, etc.
- The sensed data is collected and sent to a central server which analyses the data to detect any hazardous conditions or emergencies inside the coal mine.
- In case of detection of abnormal levels of temperature, toxic gases or fire, the server triggers an alert and warning system to alert the miners to evacuate the mine. It can also trigger an automated fire suppression system to extinguish any fires.
- The seismic sensors can detect any tremors or instability in the mine which can alert the miners of a potential mine collapse allowing them to evacuate to safety.
- The IOT based system continuously monitors the coal mine 24/7 and sends out alerts to ensure the safety of the miners even in unpredictable and dangerous situations. It can help avoid loss of lives in case of disasters or mishaps in the coal mine.
- The data from the IOT sensors can also be used to get insights into the working and safety conditions of the coal mine to enhance productivity and profitability. The historical data can be analysed to identify patterns and take preventive measures.
