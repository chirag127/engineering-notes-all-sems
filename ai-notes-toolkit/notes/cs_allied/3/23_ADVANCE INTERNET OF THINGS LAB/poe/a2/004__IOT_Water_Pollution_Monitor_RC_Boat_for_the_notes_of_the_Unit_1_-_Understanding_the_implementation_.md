### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

1. An IOT based water pollution monitoring system is designed using a remotely controlled boat.
2. The boat is equipped with sensors to detect various water pollution parameters like pH, temperature, dissolved oxygen, turbidity, etc.
3. The sensor data is collected and uploaded to a cloud server using a microcontroller and wireless communication modules like WiFi or GPRS.
4. The cloud server stores and visualizes the data which can be accessed by authorities to monitor water pollution levels and take necessary actions.
5. The RC boat is controlled over the internet or using a mobile app which enables remote monitoring of pollution in water bodies.
6. Necessary actions like water filtering or increasing dissolved oxygen levels can be taken by operating pump motors or other actuators on the RC boat.
7. IOT technologies enable remote and continuous monitoring of water pollution which is more efficient than manual methods of occasional testing of water samples.
8. This system can be scaled and deployed in various water bodies to enable comprehensive monitoring and control of water pollution.
