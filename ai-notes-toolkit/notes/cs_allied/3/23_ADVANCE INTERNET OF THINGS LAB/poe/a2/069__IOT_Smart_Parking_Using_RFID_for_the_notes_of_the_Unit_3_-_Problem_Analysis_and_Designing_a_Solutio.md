### IOT Smart Parking Using RFID

- RFID tags are attached to each parking slot.
- RFID reader is placed at the entry and exit of the parking lot.
- When a car enters the parking lot, the RFID tag of the available parking slot is read by the RFID reader and the slot is allocated to the car. The slot number is sent to the database and displayed to the user.
- When the car exits the parking lot, the RFID reader reads the tag and updates the database marking the slot as available.
- The parking charges are calculated based on the time difference between entry and exit and deducted automatically.
- The status of available and occupied slots is displayed to the user through a web application or a mobile application for easy navigation to the nearest available slot.
- Additional features like calculating total revenue, updating parking rates etc can also be included.
- This system eliminates the need for manual allocation of slots and calculating charges thereby reducing human effort and errors. It also provides information about availability of slots to the users leading to efficient utilization of available space.
