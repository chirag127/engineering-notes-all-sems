### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite for space research that is made up of multiples of 10 cm × 10 cm × 10 cm cubic units.
- CubeSats are deployed from a launch vehicle to perform various missions like Earth observation, technology demonstration, etc.
- A Weather Imaging CubeSat is a special type of CubeSat that is equipped with imaging payloads to capture weather data and transmit it to the ground station.
- The imaging payload consists of cameras to capture images of cloud cover, weather systems, etc.
- The data captured by the imaging payload is transmitted to the ground station using radio frequency transmission also known as telemetry transmission.
- The ground station receives and processes the data sent by the CubeSat to derive useful weather information and insights.
- By deploying a constellation of such Weather Imaging CubeSats, near-real-time global weather monitoring can be achieved at a lower cost.
- This enables weather forecasting and weather-related applications that can help solve societal problems.
- Thus, a Weather Imaging CubeSat with telemetry transmission demonstrates the use of IoT in space to solve societal problems.
