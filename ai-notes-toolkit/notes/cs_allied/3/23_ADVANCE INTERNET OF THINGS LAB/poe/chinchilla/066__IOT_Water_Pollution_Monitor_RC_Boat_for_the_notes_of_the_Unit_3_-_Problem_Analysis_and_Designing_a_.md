### IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat

- Alerts and Notifications: The boat is equipped with an alert system that can notify authorities if the level of water pollution in the area exceeds a certain threshold. This feature is important for preventing environmental disasters and protecting aquatic life.

- Easy to Use: The IOT Water Pollution Monitor RC Boat is easy to use and can be operated by anyone with basic RC boat experience. The boat is lightweight and easy to transport, making it ideal for use in remote areas.
