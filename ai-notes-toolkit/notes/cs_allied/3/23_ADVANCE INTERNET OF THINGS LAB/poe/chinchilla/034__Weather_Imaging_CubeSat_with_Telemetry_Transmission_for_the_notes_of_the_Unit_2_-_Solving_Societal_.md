### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

The following points describe the Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission for the notes of the Unit 2 - Solving Societal problems with the help of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB:

- A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite that is used for space research and exploration.
- Weather Imaging CubeSats are designed and developed to capture and transmit weather-related information from space.
- These CubeSats are equipped with various sensors and imaging devices to capture weather-related data such as temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed, and direction.
- The captured data is then processed and transmitted back to Earth in the form of telemetry signals.
- Telemetry is the process of transmitting data from a remote location to a receiving station through wireless communication.
- The weather-related data captured by these CubeSats is used to develop weather models and forecasts, which can be used to predict natural disasters such as hurricanes, tornadoes, and floods.
- The use of CubeSats for weather imaging and telemetry transmission can help in solving societal problems related to natural disasters and weather-related events.
- CubeSats are cost-effective and can be launched in large numbers, providing greater coverage and accuracy of weather-related data.
- The use of IOT technology in CubeSats can enhance their capabilities and provide real-time monitoring and analysis of weather-related data.
- Weather Imaging CubeSats with Telemetry Transmission can also be used for commercial purposes such as agriculture, transportation, and energy sectors, where weather-related data plays a crucial role in decision-making.
- The development and deployment of CubeSats require expertise in various fields such as aerospace engineering, electronics, and computer science, providing opportunities for research and innovation in these fields.
