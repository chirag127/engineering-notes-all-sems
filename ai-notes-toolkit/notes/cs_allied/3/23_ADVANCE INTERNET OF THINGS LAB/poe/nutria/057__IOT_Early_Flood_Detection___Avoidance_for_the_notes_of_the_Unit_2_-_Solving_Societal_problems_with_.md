
### IOT Early Flood Detection & Avoidance

1. Floods can cause a lot of damage to people, property, and the environment.
2. IOT can be used to detect floods early and take preventive measures.
3. Sensors can be used to measure the water level and detect changes in water flow.
4. IOT can be used to monitor the weather, track rain, and provide early warnings.
5. IOT can also be used to control the flow of water in rivers and dams, preventing floods from happening.
6. IOT can be used to control water pumps, ensuring that water is diverted away from areas that are prone to flooding.
7. IOT can be used to provide real-time information about floods, allowing people to take preventive measures and evacuate if necessary.
8. IOT can also be used to monitor the quality of water after a flood, ensuring that it is safe to drink.
9. IOT can be used to automate the process of providing relief to those affected by floods. 
10. IOT can also be used to track the progress of flood recovery efforts.
