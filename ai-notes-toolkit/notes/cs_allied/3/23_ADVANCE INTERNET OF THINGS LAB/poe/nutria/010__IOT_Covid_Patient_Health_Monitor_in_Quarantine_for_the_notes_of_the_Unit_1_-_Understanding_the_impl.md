
### IOT Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data.

2. The application of IoT in healthcare has enabled the development of various technologies for monitoring and tracking patient health data. One such technology is the Covid Patient Health Monitor in Quarantine (CPHMQ), a system that monitors the health of Covid-19 patients who are in quarantine.

3. CPHMQ uses sensors and actuators to detect vital signs such as temperature, heart rate, respiration rate, oxygen saturation, and blood pressure. It also uses other sensors to detect the presence of any infectious diseases.

4. The data collected by the system is then transmitted to a remote server where it is analyzed and stored. The system also uses artificial intelligence algorithms to detect any abnormalities in the patient’s health.

5. The system also provides real-time alerts to medical personnel if any abnormalities are detected. This helps in providing timely medical attention to the patient.

6. The system also provides a dashboard that provides an overview of all the patients in quarantine and their health status. This helps medical personnel to monitor the health of all the patients in quarantine.

7. CPHMQ is an important tool for ensuring the safety and health of Covid-19 patients in quarantine. It helps in providing timely medical attention to the patients and also helps in monitoring their health status.
