
### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

Manhole detection and monitoring systems are an important part of solving societal problems with the help of the Internet of Things (IoT). This system can be used to detect and monitor the presence of manholes in urban areas.

1. **Benefits**: Manhole detection and monitoring systems provide a number of benefits, including improved safety and security, reduced costs, and improved efficiency.

2. **Technology**: Manhole detection and monitoring systems use various technologies, such as sensors, cameras, and wireless communication, to detect and monitor the presence of manholes.

3. **Applications**: Manhole detection and monitoring systems can be used in a variety of applications, such as public safety, water management, and traffic management.

4. **Impact**: Manhole detection and monitoring systems can have a significant impact on the environment and society, as they can help reduce the number of accidents caused by manholes, improve water management, and reduce traffic congestion.
