
### IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino 

This section covers the implementation of IOT in the subject of ADVANCE INTERNET OF THINGS LAB. 

1. An IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino can be used to detect gas leakage in residential and commercial buildings. 
2. It consists of an Arduino microcontroller, a gas sensor, and a relay module. 
3. The Arduino microcontroller is used to control the gas sensor and the relay module. 
4. The gas sensor detects the presence of gas in the air and sends a signal to the Arduino microcontroller when the concentration of gas exceeds a certain threshold. 
5. The Arduino microcontroller then triggers the relay module to switch on an alarm or to send a signal to a remote server. 
6. The remote server can then be used to alert the occupants of the building or to take appropriate action. 
7. The Arduino microcontroller can also be used to monitor the concentration of gas in the air and to generate reports. 
8. The IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino is an effective way to detect gas leakage in residential and commercial buildings.
