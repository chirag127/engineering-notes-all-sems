
### Raspberry Pi Air and Noise Pollution Monitoring System Over IOT

1. Air and noise pollution are major environmental issues that can have a significant impact on human health. 
2. The Internet of Things (IoT) can be used to monitor air and noise pollution in real-time, allowing for more accurate and timely responses to environmental issues.
3. The Raspberry Pi is a low-cost, single-board computer that can be used to build an air and noise pollution monitoring system.
4. The Raspberry Pi can be connected to an array of environmental sensors to measure air and noise pollution levels.
5. The data collected by the Raspberry Pi can be used to create visualizations and alert systems to inform users of potential health risks.
6. The Raspberry Pi can also be connected to the Internet to allow for remote monitoring and data sharing.
7. The Raspberry Pi air and noise pollution monitoring system can be used to help solve societal problems, such as air and noise pollution, by providing real-time data to inform decision-making.
