
### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items that are embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to collect and exchange data.

2. IoT can be used to develop a Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot for Queue, which can be used to monitor and maintain social distancing in public places such as supermarkets, banks, and airports.

3. The robot is equipped with sensors and cameras to detect people entering a queue and measure the distance between them.

4. The robot can also be programmed to alert people if they cross the pre-defined social distancing threshold.

5. The robot can be connected to a central system and can provide real-time data on the number of people in the queue and the distance between them.

6. This data can be used to monitor the effectiveness of social distancing measures and to make adjustments accordingly.
