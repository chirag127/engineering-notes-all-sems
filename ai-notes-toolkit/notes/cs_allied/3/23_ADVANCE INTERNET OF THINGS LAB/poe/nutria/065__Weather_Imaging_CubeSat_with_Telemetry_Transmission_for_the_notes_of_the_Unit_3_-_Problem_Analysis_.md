
### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

1. A CubeSat is a type of miniaturized satellite used for research and exploration purposes. It is typically a 10 cm cube-shaped structure made up of multiple units, each unit being 10 cm x 10 cm x 11.35 cm.

2. CubeSats are typically launched into space as piggyback payloads on larger satellites, or as part of a larger mission.

3. CubeSats are used to take images of Earth's surface, monitor weather, and transmit telemetry data back to Earth.

4. CubeSats typically use a combination of solar panels and batteries to generate power, and radio transmitters to send data back to Earth.

5. CubeSats can be designed with different types of sensors and communication systems, depending on the mission requirements.

6. CubeSats have become increasingly popular in recent years due to their low cost and relative ease of use. They are now being used for a variety of applications, including Earth observation, scientific research, and space exploration.
