
### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

1. IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry Systems are a type of technology used to identify individuals entering a public space.

2. The system works by scanning an individual's temperature and face mask to determine if they are healthy enough to enter the space.

3. This technology is useful for helping to protect the public from the spread of infectious diseases such as COVID-19.

4. IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry Systems are becoming increasingly popular in places such as airports, hospitals, and schools.

5. These systems use a combination of facial recognition technology and infrared sensors to accurately identify individuals and their temperature.

6. The data collected by the system can then be used to alert authorities of any potential health risks.

7. IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry Systems are an important part of the effort to reduce the spread of infectious diseases and protect the public.
