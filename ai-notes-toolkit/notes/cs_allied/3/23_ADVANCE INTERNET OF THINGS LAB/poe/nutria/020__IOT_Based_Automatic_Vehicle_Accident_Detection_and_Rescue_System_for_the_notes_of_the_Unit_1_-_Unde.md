
### IOT Based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data. 

2. The aim of an IOT based Automatic Vehicle Accident Detection and Rescue System is to detect and respond to accidents in real-time. The system uses various sensors and IoT enabled devices such as GPS, cameras, and accelerometers to detect and respond to accidents.

3. The system can be used to alert emergency services and provide assistance to victims. It also helps in preventing unnecessary traffic jams due to accidents.

4. The system can be used to monitor the health of drivers and passengers. It can detect signs of fatigue or illness and alert emergency services if necessary.

5. The system can be used to detect and respond to dangerous driving conditions such as icy roads, fog, and heavy rain. It can alert drivers to take caution or slow down. 

6. The system can also be used to detect and report traffic violations. It can be used to monitor speed limits, lane departure, and other traffic violations.

7. The system can be used to detect and respond to road rage incidents. It can alert law enforcement and help to prevent further escalation.

8. The system can be used to collect data on traffic patterns and road conditions. This data can be used to improve traffic management and road safety.
