
### Weather Imaging CubeSat with Telemetry Transmission

1. CubeSats are small satellites capable of performing a variety of tasks, such as Earth observation, climate monitoring, and communication.
2. They are typically equipped with sensors and cameras that can be used to monitor weather conditions and take pictures of Earth’s surface.
3. CubeSats can also be used to transmit telemetry data, which is information about the satellite’s position, speed, and other parameters.
4. CubeSats can be used to solve societal problems such as monitoring climate change, natural disasters, and air pollution.
5. CubeSats can also be used in Internet of Things (IoT) applications, such as tracking vehicles or monitoring agricultural fields.
6. CubeSats are usually powered by solar panels and have a short lifespan, usually lasting only a few months.
7. They are relatively inexpensive to build and launch, making them an attractive option for those looking to explore space on a budget.
