
### IOT based Intelligent Gas Leakage Detector Using Arduino

1. A gas leakage detector is a device used to detect the presence of gas in an area. 
2. It is usually used to detect the presence of combustible, toxic or explosive gases. 
3. The device is typically composed of a sensor, a detection circuit and an alarm. 
4. The sensor used in a gas leakage detector is typically a semiconductor sensor, which is sensitive to certain gases. 
5. The detection circuit is used to detect the presence of the gas and trigger the alarm. 
6. An Arduino microcontroller can be used to control the gas leakage detector. 
7. It can be used to control the sensitivity of the sensor, the duration of the alarm, and the type of alarm. 
8. The Arduino can also be used to collect data from the sensor and send it to a remote monitoring system. 
9. This system can be used to monitor the gas level in an area and alert the user if the gas level is too high. 
10. The Arduino can also be used to connect the gas leakage detector to the internet, allowing it to be monitored remotely.
