
### IOT Based Heart Monitoring System Using ECG

1. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects, such as sensors, that are connected to the internet and are able to exchange data with other connected devices. 
2. IoT-based heart monitoring systems use sensors to measure and monitor a patient's heart rate and rhythm. These systems are used in hospitals and other medical settings to diagnose and treat heart conditions.
3. The most common type of sensor used in these systems is the electrocardiogram (ECG). An ECG measures electrical activity in the heart and can detect abnormalities in the heart's rhythm.
4. IoT-based heart monitoring systems can provide real-time data on a patient's heart rate and rhythm, allowing for more accurate diagnosis and treatment of heart conditions.
5. In addition, these systems can be used to monitor a patient's heart rate and rhythm over a long period of time, providing valuable insights into a patient's health.
6. IoT-based heart monitoring systems can help to reduce the cost of healthcare by providing more accurate diagnosis and treatment of heart conditions.
7. IoT-based heart monitoring systems can also help to improve the quality of care for patients by providing real-time data on their heart rate and rhythm. 
8. Finally, these systems can help to reduce the risk of medical errors by providing more accurate and timely data on a patient's heart rate and rhythm.
