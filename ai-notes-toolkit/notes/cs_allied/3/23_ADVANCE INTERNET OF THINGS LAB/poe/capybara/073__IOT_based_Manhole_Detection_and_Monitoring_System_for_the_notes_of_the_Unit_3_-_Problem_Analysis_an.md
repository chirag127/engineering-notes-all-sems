### IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System

In the modern world, technology has made it possible to create a safer environment for people. One such technology is the IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System. This system is designed to detect and monitor manholes in a city to ensure that they are safe for people to use.

Here are some key features of the IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System:

- The system uses sensors to detect the presence of a manhole cover.
- The sensors are connected to a central server that monitors the status of all the manholes in the city.
- If a manhole cover is missing or damaged, an alert is sent to the central server.
- The central server then sends a notification to the concerned authorities to take action.
- The system also includes a monitoring dashboard that allows the authorities to track the status of all the manholes in the city.

Benefits of the IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System:

- The system ensures the safety of pedestrians and drivers by detecting and alerting the authorities about any missing or damaged manhole covers.
- The system helps to reduce the risk of accidents caused by open manholes.
- The system is cost-effective and requires minimal maintenance.
- The system helps to improve the overall infrastructure of the city and makes it a safer place for everyone.

In conclusion, the IOT based Manhole Detection and Monitoring System is a great example of how technology can be used to make our cities safer. With its advanced features and benefits, it is a must-have system for any modern city.
