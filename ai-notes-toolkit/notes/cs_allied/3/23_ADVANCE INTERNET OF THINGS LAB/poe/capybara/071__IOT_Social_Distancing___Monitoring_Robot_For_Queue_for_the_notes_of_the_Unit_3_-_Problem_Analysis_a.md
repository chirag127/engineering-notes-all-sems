### IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue

In today's world, social distancing has become an essential part of our daily routine due to the spread of COVID-19. The IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue is designed to ensure social distancing in queues and provide a safe environment for people.

Here are some of the key features of the IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue:

- The robot is equipped with sensors that can detect the distance between people in a queue. If the distance between two people is less than the recommended social distancing limit, the robot will alert them with a warning sound.

- The robot has a camera that can detect the number of people in a queue. If the queue exceeds a predefined limit, the robot will inform the authorities to take necessary actions.

- The robot is connected to the internet, which means it can send real-time data to a cloud server. This data can be used to monitor the number of people in a queue and the distance between them.

- The robot is designed to move autonomously, which means it can navigate through the queue without any human intervention.

- The robot has a display screen that shows the number of people in a queue and the recommended social distancing limit.

- The robot is equipped with a speaker system that can inform people about the importance of social distancing and the recommended distance between people.

- The robot is designed to be compact and lightweight, which means it can be easily deployed in any public place.

Overall, the IOT Social Distancing & Monitoring Robot For Queue is an innovative solution that can help to ensure social distancing in queues and provide a safe environment for people. With its advanced features and autonomous design, the robot can help to reduce the spread of COVID-19 and create a healthier and safer world for everyone.
