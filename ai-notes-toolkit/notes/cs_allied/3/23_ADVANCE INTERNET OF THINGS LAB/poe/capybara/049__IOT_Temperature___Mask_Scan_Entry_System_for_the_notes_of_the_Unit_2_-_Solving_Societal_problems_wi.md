### IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System

In today's world, where the COVID-19 pandemic has affected the entire globe, it is important to take precautions to prevent the spread of the virus. One solution to this problem is the use of an IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System. Here are some key points about this system:

- The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is a smart device that can be installed at the entry points of buildings, offices, malls, and other public places.
- The system is equipped with thermal sensors that can measure the temperature of individuals entering the premises.
- The system also has a camera that can detect whether the individual is wearing a mask or not.
- The data collected by the system is sent to a cloud server for analysis and storage.
- The system generates an alert if an individual's temperature is above the normal range or if they are not wearing a mask.
- The alert can be sent to the security personnel, building management, or the concerned authority.
- The IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System can help to prevent the spread of COVID-19 and other infectious diseases by detecting potential carriers of the virus.
- The system can also help to maintain social distancing norms by limiting the number of people entering the premises at one time.
- The system is easy to install, use, and maintain, making it a cost-effective solution for public safety.

In conclusion, the IOT Temperature & Mask Scan Entry System is a useful tool in the fight against COVID-19 and other infectious diseases. It is a simple yet effective way to ensure public safety and prevent the spread of the virus.
