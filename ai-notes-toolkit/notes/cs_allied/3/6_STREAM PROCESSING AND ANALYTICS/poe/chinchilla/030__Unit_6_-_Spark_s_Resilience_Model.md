## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

Spark’s Resilience Model is a framework that provides a roadmap for individuals to cope with adversity and recover from it. It is based on the principles of positive psychology and emphasizes the importance of building personal resilience to overcome challenges.

The following are the key components of Spark’s Resilience Model:

1. Self-awareness: This involves understanding oneself, including one's strengths, weaknesses, values, and beliefs. By being aware of oneself, individuals can make more informed decisions and take action that aligns with their goals and values.

2. Self-regulation: This involves managing one's emotions and behavior in response to situations. It involves being able to control one's impulses, adapt to change, and maintain a positive outlook even in difficult times.

3. Optimism: This involves having a positive outlook and focusing on the potential for growth and positive change. It involves seeing setbacks as temporary and believing that one can overcome challenges.

4. Connection: This involves building and maintaining positive relationships with others. It involves seeking social support and being part of a community that can provide emotional and practical help.

5. Purpose: This involves having a sense of meaning and direction in life. It involves setting goals and working towards them, as well as contributing to something larger than oneself.

By developing these five components, individuals can build their resilience and cope with adversity more effectively. The Spark’s Resilience Model is a useful tool for individuals who want to enhance their ability to bounce back from setbacks and thrive in the face of challenges.
