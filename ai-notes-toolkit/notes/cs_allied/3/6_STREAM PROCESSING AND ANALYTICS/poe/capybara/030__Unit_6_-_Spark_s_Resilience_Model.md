## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

The Spark’s Resilience Model is a framework designed to help individuals and organizations build resilience and overcome adversity. Here are some key points to understand about the model: 

- The Spark’s Resilience Model is based on the belief that resilience is a skill that can be learned and developed over time.

- The model is made up of five components: Strengths, Purpose, Adaptability, Resourcefulness, and Knowledge.

- Strengths refer to the unique talents and attributes that individuals and organizations possess. By identifying and leveraging these strengths, individuals and organizations can build confidence and resilience.

- Purpose is the driving force behind an individual or organization’s actions. Having a clear sense of purpose can help individuals and organizations stay focused and motivated, even in the face of adversity.

- Adaptability refers to the ability to adjust and respond to changing circumstances. Individuals and organizations that are adaptable are better able to navigate challenges and setbacks.

- Resourcefulness refers to the ability to identify and utilize resources to overcome obstacles. This includes both internal resources (such as skills and knowledge) and external resources (such as social support and technology).

- Knowledge refers to the understanding of oneself and the world around them. By cultivating knowledge and learning from past experiences, individuals and organizations can build resilience and better prepare for future challenges.

Overall, the Spark’s Resilience Model provides a comprehensive framework for building resilience and overcoming adversity. By focusing on strengths, purpose, adaptability, resourcefulness, and knowledge, individuals and organizations can develop the skills and mindset needed to thrive in the face of challenges.
