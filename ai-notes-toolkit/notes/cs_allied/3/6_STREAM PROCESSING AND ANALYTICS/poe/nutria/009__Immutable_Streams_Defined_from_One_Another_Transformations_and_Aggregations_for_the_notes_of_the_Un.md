
### Immutable Streams Defined from One AnotherTransformations and Aggregations

1. Immutable streams are streams that cannot be changed or modified. They are defined from one another based on their properties, such as the data type of the elements, the order of the elements, and the rate at which the elements are produced. 
2. Transformations are operations that modify elements in a stream. These operations can include filtering, mapping, and reducing.
3. Aggregations are operations that combine elements in a stream. These operations can include summing, averaging, and counting. 
4. Stream-processing models are used to process and analyze data in real-time. This model is useful for applications such as financial analytics, fraud detection, and recommendation systems.
