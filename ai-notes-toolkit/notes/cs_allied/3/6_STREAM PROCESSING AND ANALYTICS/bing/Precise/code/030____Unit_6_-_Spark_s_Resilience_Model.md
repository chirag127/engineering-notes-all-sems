## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

Spark’s Resilience Model is a framework for understanding and building resilience in individuals. The model is based on the idea that resilience is not a fixed trait, but rather a dynamic process that can be developed and strengthened over time.

The model consists of several key components, including:

1. **Self-awareness**: The ability to recognize and understand one’s own emotions, thoughts, and behaviors, and how they impact oneself and others.

2. **Self-regulation**: The ability to manage one’s emotions, thoughts, and behaviors in a healthy and productive manner.

3. **Mental agility**: The ability to think flexibly and adaptively, and to approach challenges and setbacks with a growth mindset.

4. **Strengths of character**: The identification and cultivation of personal strengths and values, and the use of these strengths to overcome adversity and achieve personal goals.

5. **Connection**: The development and maintenance of strong, supportive relationships with others, and the ability to seek and provide support when needed.

By focusing on these key components, individuals can develop greater resilience and the ability to bounce back from adversity. The model provides a framework for understanding and building resilience, and can be used by individuals, organizations, and communities to promote greater resilience and well-being.
