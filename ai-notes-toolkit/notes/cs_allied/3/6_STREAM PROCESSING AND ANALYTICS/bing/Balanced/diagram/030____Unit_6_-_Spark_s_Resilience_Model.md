## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

- Resilience is the ability to cope with stress and adversity, and to bounce back from challenges and difficulties.
- Spark’s resilience model is a framework that helps individuals and organizations to assess and improve their resilience levels.
- The model consists of four domains: **Self**, **Situation**, **Support**, and **Strategies**.
- Each domain has four subdomains that represent different aspects of resilience.
- The model can be visualized as a four-by-four grid, as shown below:

| Self | Situation | Support | Strategies |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-awareness | Situation awareness | Social support | Problem-solving |
| Self-regulation | Situation management | Professional support | Coping skills |
| Self-efficacy | Situation adaptation | Organizational support | Learning and growth |
| Self-care | Situation improvement | Community support | Meaning and purpose |

- The model can be used to measure one's resilience level by rating each subdomain on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
- The model can also be used to identify one's strengths and weaknesses, and to develop action plans to enhance one's resilience in each domain and subdomain.
- The model is based on the premise that resilience is not a fixed trait, but a dynamic and learnable skill that can be improved with practice and feedback.
