## Unit 6 - Spark’s Resilience Model

- Resilience is the ability to cope with stress and adversity, and to bounce back from challenges and difficulties.
- Spark’s resilience model is a framework that helps individuals and organizations to assess and improve their resilience levels.
- The model consists of four dimensions: **Self**, **Situation**, **Support**, and **Strategies**.
- Each dimension has three sub-dimensions that describe the key aspects of resilience in that area.
- The model can be used to identify the strengths and weaknesses of one’s resilience, and to develop action plans to enhance it.

### Self
- The self dimension refers to the personal characteristics and attributes that influence one’s resilience.
- The sub-dimensions are:
  - **Self-awareness**: The ability to recognize and understand one’s emotions, thoughts, values, strengths, and limitations.
  - **Self-regulation**: The ability to manage and control one’s emotions, impulses, and behaviors, and to adapt to changing situations and demands.
  - **Self-efficacy**: The belief in one’s capabilities and competence to achieve one’s goals and overcome challenges.

### Situation
- The situation dimension refers to the external factors and circumstances that affect one’s resilience.
- The sub-dimensions are:
  - **Situation awareness**: The ability to perceive and comprehend the relevant information and cues in the environment, and to anticipate the potential outcomes and consequences.
  - **Situation appraisal**: The ability to evaluate and interpret the situation in a realistic and positive way, and to identify the opportunities and resources available.
  - **Situation response**: The ability to choose and implement the appropriate actions and solutions to deal with the situation, and to monitor and adjust them as needed.

### Support
- The support dimension refers to the social and relational aspects that influence one’s resilience.
- The sub-dimensions are:
  - **Support awareness**: The ability to recognize and acknowledge the need for and availability of support from others, and to seek and accept it when necessary.
  - **Support provision**: The ability to provide and offer support to others who are facing challenges or difficulties, and to show empathy and compassion.
  - **Support network**: The quality and quantity of the relationships and connections that one has with others, such as family, friends, colleagues, and mentors.

### Strategies
- The strategies dimension refers to the specific techniques and methods that one uses to enhance one’s resilience.
- The sub-dimensions are:
  - **Cognitive strategies**: The strategies that involve the use of mental processes, such as reframing, positive thinking, problem-solving, and goal-setting, to cope with stress and challenges.
  - **Behavioral strategies**: The strategies that involve the use of physical actions, such as relaxation, exercise, sleep, and nutrition, to cope with stress and challenges.
  - **Emotional strategies**: The strategies that involve the use of emotional expressions, such as humor, gratitude, optimism, and spirituality, to cope with stress and challenges.
