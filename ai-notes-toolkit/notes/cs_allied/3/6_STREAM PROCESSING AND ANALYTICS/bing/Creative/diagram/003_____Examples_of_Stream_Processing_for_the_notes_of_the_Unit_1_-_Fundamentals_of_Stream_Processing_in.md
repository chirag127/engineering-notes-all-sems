### Examples of Stream Processing

- **Fraud and anomaly detection**: Stream processing can be used to monitor data streams for suspicious or abnormal patterns and events, such as fraudulent transactions, network intrusions, or sensor malfunctions. For example, one of the world’s largest credit card providers has been able to reduce their fraud write-downs by $800 million per year by using stream processing to detect and prevent fraud in real-time .
- **Internet of Things (IoT) edge analytics**: Stream processing can be used to analyze data from IoT devices, such as sensors, cameras, or wearables, at the edge of the network, where the data is generated. This can reduce latency, bandwidth, and storage costs, as well as enable faster and more accurate decision making. For example, stream processing can be used to monitor the health and performance of industrial equipment, vehicles, or smart home appliances, and trigger alerts or actions based on predefined rules or thresholds .
- **Personalized customer experience**: Stream processing can be used to deliver customized and relevant content, offers, or recommendations to customers based on their real-time behavior, preferences, or context. For example, stream processing can be used to provide personalized ads, product suggestions, or news feeds to online users, or to optimize the layout and design of websites or apps based on user feedback or engagement .
- **Stock market trading**: Stream processing can be used to process and analyze large volumes of financial data, such as stock prices, market trends, or news events, and execute trades based on predefined strategies or algorithms. For example, stream processing can be used to implement high-frequency trading, arbitrage, or market making, which require low latency and high throughput .
- **Digital experience monitoring**: Stream processing can be used to collect and analyze data from various sources, such as web servers, applications, or user devices, and measure the quality and performance of digital services or products. For example, stream processing can be used to monitor the availability, reliability, or responsiveness of websites or apps, and identify and resolve issues or bottlenecks that affect user satisfaction or retention .
