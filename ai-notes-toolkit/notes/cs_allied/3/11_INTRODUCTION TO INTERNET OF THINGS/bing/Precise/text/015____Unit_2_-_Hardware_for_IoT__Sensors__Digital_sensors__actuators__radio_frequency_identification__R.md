## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

1. **Sensors**: Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They convert physical parameters such as temperature, humidity, light, sound, and pressure into electrical signals that can be processed by a computer.

2. **Digital sensors**: Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal. They are commonly used in IoT applications because they can be easily interfaced with microcontrollers and other digital systems.

3. **Actuators**: Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions. They are used in IoT applications to control physical systems such as motors, valves, and lights.

4. **Radio frequency identification (RFID) technology**: RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is commonly used in IoT applications for asset tracking and inventory management.

5. **Wireless sensor networks**: Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly. They are commonly used in IoT applications for environmental monitoring and control.

6. **Participatory sensing technology**: Participatory sensing technology involves the use of mobile devices and social media to collect and share data about the environment. It is commonly used in IoT applications for citizen science and community engagement.

7. **Embedded Platforms for IoT**: Embedded platforms are computer systems that are designed to be integrated into other devices. They are commonly used in IoT applications to provide computing power and connectivity.

8. **Embedded computing basics**: Embedded computing involves the use of microcontrollers and other small computer systems to control and monitor physical systems.

9. **Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms**: There are several hardware platforms that are commonly used in IoT applications, including Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo, and ARM Cortex boards.

10. **Arduino**: Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is commonly used in IoT applications for prototyping and education.

11. **Netduino**: Netduino is an open-source electronics platform based on the .NET Micro Framework. It is commonly used in IoT applications for prototyping and education.

12. **Raspberry Pi**: Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation. It is commonly used in IoT applications for prototyping and education.

13. **BeagleBone**: BeagleBone is a series of open-source hardware single-board computers developed by BeagleBoard.org. It is commonly used in IoT applications for prototyping and education.

14. **Intel Galileo**: Intel Galileo is a series of development boards based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000. It is commonly used in IoT applications for prototyping and education.

15. **ARM Cortex**: ARM Cortex is a series of microcontroller cores developed by ARM Holdings. It is commonly used in IoT applications for embedded systems development.
