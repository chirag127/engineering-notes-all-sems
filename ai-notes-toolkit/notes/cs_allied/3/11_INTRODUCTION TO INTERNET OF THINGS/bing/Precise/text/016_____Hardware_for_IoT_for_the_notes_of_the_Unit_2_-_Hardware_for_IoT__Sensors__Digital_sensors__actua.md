### Hardware for IoT

Unit 2 of the subject "Introduction to Internet of Things" covers the hardware used in IoT systems. The following topics are included in this unit:

1. **Sensors**: Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They are used to collect data from the physical world and convert it into a digital format that can be processed by a computer.

2. **Digital sensors**: Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal. They are commonly used in IoT systems because they can be easily interfaced with microcontrollers and other digital devices.

3. **Actuators**: Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into a physical action. They are used to control physical systems, such as motors, valves, and switches.

4. **Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology**: RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is commonly used in inventory management, supply chain management, and asset tracking.

5. **Wireless sensor networks**: Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly. They are used to collect data from the environment and transmit it to a central location for processing.

6. **Participatory sensing technology**: Participatory sensing technology involves the use of mobile devices, such as smartphones, to collect data from the environment. This data is then shared with others to create a collective understanding of the environment.

7. **Embedded Platforms for IoT**: Embedded platforms are computer systems that are designed to be integrated into other devices. They are commonly used in IoT systems because they provide the computing power needed to process sensor data and control actuators.

8. **Embedded computing basics**: Embedded computing involves the use of microcontrollers and other small computing devices to control physical systems. It is a fundamental concept in the design of IoT systems.

9. **Overview of IoT supported Hardware platforms**: There are many hardware platforms that are commonly used in IoT systems. These include Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex.
