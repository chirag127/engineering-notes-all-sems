### Design Principles for Connected Devices

Design principles for connected devices in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT) and Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication are essential for creating effective and efficient systems. These principles include:

1. **IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization:** It is important to have a standardized approach to the design of IoT/M2M systems, including the various layers involved in the system. This can help to ensure interoperability and ease of integration between different devices and systems.

2. **Communication technologies:** The choice of communication technology is crucial in the design of connected devices. Factors such as range, bandwidth, power consumption, and security should be considered when selecting a communication technology.

3. **Data enrichment and consolidation:** Connected devices generate large amounts of data, and it is important to have mechanisms in place for enriching and consolidating this data. This can help to improve the accuracy and usefulness of the data, and can also help to reduce the amount of data that needs to be transmitted and stored.

4. **Ease of designing and affordability:** The design of connected devices should be as simple and straightforward as possible, in order to make it easy for developers to create new devices and systems. Additionally, the cost of designing and manufacturing connected devices should be kept as low as possible, in order to make the technology accessible to a wide range of users.

These design principles can help to guide the development of connected devices and systems, and can help to ensure that the resulting systems are effective, efficient, and user-friendly.
