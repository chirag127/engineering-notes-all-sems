### Sensors

Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They are used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, light, sound, pressure, and motion. Sensors are an essential component of IoT systems as they provide the data that is used to monitor and control the environment.

#### Digital Sensors

Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal. They are commonly used in IoT systems because they can be easily interfaced with microcontrollers and other digital systems. Examples of digital sensors include temperature sensors, light sensors, and motion sensors.

#### Actuators

Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into mechanical motion. They are used in IoT systems to control physical processes such as opening and closing valves, turning on and off lights, and adjusting the temperature. Examples of actuators include motors, solenoids, and relays.

#### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology is a wireless communication technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. RFID tags are attached to objects and contain a unique identifier that can be read by an RFID reader. RFID technology is commonly used in IoT systems for inventory management, asset tracking, and supply chain management.

#### Wireless Sensor Networks

Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly. They are used in IoT systems to collect data from the environment and transmit it to a central location for processing. Wireless sensor networks are commonly used in applications such as environmental monitoring, smart agriculture, and smart cities.

#### Participatory Sensing Technology

Participatory sensing technology is a type of sensing technology that involves the participation of individuals in the collection of data. It is commonly used in IoT systems to collect data from the environment using smartphones and other mobile devices. Participatory sensing technology is used in applications such as air quality monitoring, traffic monitoring, and urban planning.

#### Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms are computer systems that are designed to be integrated into other devices. They are commonly used in IoT systems to provide the computing power needed to process sensor data and control actuators. Examples of embedded platforms for IoT include Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo, and ARM Cortex.

#### Embedded Computing Basics

Embedded computing refers to the use of computer systems in devices that are not traditional computers. Embedded systems are designed to perform specific tasks and are often used in IoT systems to provide the computing power needed to process sensor data and control actuators.

#### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms

There are many hardware platforms that are supported by IoT systems. These platforms provide the computing power, connectivity, and other features needed to build IoT systems. Some of the most popular IoT supported hardware platforms include Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo, and ARM Cortex. These platforms vary in terms of their capabilities, cost, and ease of use, and the choice of platform will depend on the specific requirements of the IoT system being developed.
