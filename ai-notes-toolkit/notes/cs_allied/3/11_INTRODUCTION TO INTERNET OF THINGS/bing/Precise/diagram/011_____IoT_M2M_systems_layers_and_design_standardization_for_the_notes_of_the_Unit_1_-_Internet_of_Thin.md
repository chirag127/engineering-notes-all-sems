### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

IoT/M2M systems consist of several layers, each with its own set of design standards. These layers include:

1. **Device Layer:** This layer consists of the physical devices and sensors that collect data. Design standards for this layer focus on the physical characteristics of the devices, such as size, power consumption, and durability.

2. **Communication Layer:** This layer is responsible for transmitting data from the devices to the cloud or other systems. Design standards for this layer focus on communication protocols, data transmission rates, and security.

3. **Data Enrichment and Consolidation Layer:** This layer processes the data collected by the devices, enriching it with additional information and consolidating it for analysis. Design standards for this layer focus on data processing techniques, data storage, and data analysis.

4. **Application Layer:** This layer provides the interface between the IoT/M2M system and the user. Design standards for this layer focus on user experience, ease of use, and accessibility.

Design standardization is important for IoT/M2M systems to ensure interoperability, ease of designing, and affordability. By adhering to common design standards, developers can create systems that work seamlessly together, reducing the time and cost of development. Additionally, standardization can help to improve the security and reliability of IoT/M2M systems.
