### Sensor Deployment & Node Discovery

Sensor deployment and node discovery are important aspects of the network and communication aspects in IoT. These processes involve the placement of sensors and the identification of nodes within a network.

1. **Sensor Deployment:** The deployment of sensors involves the strategic placement of sensors within an environment to collect data. This can be done in a variety of ways, including random deployment, grid-based deployment, and cluster-based deployment. The method of deployment chosen will depend on the specific needs of the network and the environment in which the sensors are being placed.

2. **Node Discovery:** Once the sensors have been deployed, the next step is to discover the nodes within the network. This involves identifying the sensors and other devices that are connected to the network. This can be done through a variety of methods, including active and passive discovery. Active discovery involves sending out a signal to identify nodes, while passive discovery involves listening for signals from nodes.

Overall, sensor deployment and node discovery are crucial for the effective functioning of an IoT network. These processes ensure that data is collected and transmitted efficiently, allowing for the successful implementation of IoT applications.
