### Survey Routing Protocols

1. Routing protocols are essential for the seamless connectivity of things in IoT .
2. Various features, advantages, and challenges of these protocols are discussed in the literature .
3. Some protocols are based on the principles of swarm intelligence .
4. Security and trust management are important considerations in the design of IoT routing protocols .
5. Specialized protocols have been designed for routing among sensors and are part of the network layer .
6. The session layer protocols enable messaging among various elements of the IoT communication subsystem .
7. Several security and management protocols have also been developed for IoT .
