## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

### Sensors
- Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment.
- They convert physical parameters such as temperature, humidity, light, sound, pressure, etc. into electrical signals that can be processed by a computer.

### Digital Sensors
- Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal.
- They are typically more accurate and reliable than analog sensors.

### Actuators
- Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions.
- They are used to control the movement of mechanical systems.

### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology
- RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects.
- It is commonly used for inventory management and supply chain management.

### Wireless Sensor Networks
- Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly.
- They are used for monitoring and control applications in various fields such as agriculture, healthcare, and environmental monitoring.

### Participatory Sensing Technology
- Participatory sensing technology involves the collection of data by individuals using their personal mobile devices.
- It is used for applications such as traffic monitoring and air quality monitoring.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT
- Embedded computing basics: Embedded systems are computer systems that are integrated into other devices or products.
- Overview of IoT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex: These are popular hardware platforms used for building IoT devices. They offer a range of processing power, memory, and connectivity options to suit different IoT applications.
