### Design Principles for Connected Devices

Design principles for connected devices in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT) and Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication are essential for creating effective and efficient systems. These principles include:

1. **IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization**: It is important to have a standardized approach to the design of IoT/M2M systems, with well-defined layers and interfaces between them. This allows for interoperability between different devices and systems, and makes it easier to develop and deploy new applications.

2. **Communication technologies**: The choice of communication technology is crucial for the performance and reliability of IoT/M2M systems. Different technologies have different strengths and weaknesses, and the choice will depend on factors such as the amount of data to be transmitted, the distance between devices, and the need for real-time communication.

3. **Data enrichment and consolidation**: IoT/M2M systems generate large amounts of data, which must be processed and analyzed to extract useful information. Data enrichment and consolidation techniques can be used to combine data from multiple sources, and to extract meaningful insights.

4. **Ease of designing and affordability**: The design of IoT/M2M systems should be as simple and straightforward as possible, to make it easy for developers to create new applications. At the same time, the cost of the devices and systems must be kept low, to make them accessible to a wide range of users.

These principles provide a framework for the design of effective and efficient IoT/M2M systems, and can help to ensure that connected devices are able to deliver the full range of benefits that they promise.
