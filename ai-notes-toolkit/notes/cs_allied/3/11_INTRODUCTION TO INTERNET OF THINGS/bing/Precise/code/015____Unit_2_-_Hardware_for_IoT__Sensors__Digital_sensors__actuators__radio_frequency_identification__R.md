## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

### Sensors
Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in their environment. They are used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, light, and motion. Sensors can be analog or digital.

### Digital Sensors
Digital sensors convert the physical quantity they are measuring into a digital signal that can be read by a computer or microcontroller. Examples of digital sensors include temperature sensors, light sensors, and accelerometers.

### Actuators
Actuators are devices that convert an electrical signal into physical motion. They are used to control the movement of mechanical systems. Examples of actuators include motors, solenoids, and relays.

### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology
RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects. RFID tags are attached to objects and contain a unique identifier that can be read by an RFID reader.

### Wireless Sensor Networks
Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate with each other wirelessly. They are used to collect data from the environment and transmit it to a central location for processing.

### Participatory Sensing Technology
Participatory sensing technology involves individuals using their personal devices, such as smartphones, to collect and share data about their environment.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT
Embedded platforms are computer systems that are designed to perform specific tasks. They are used in IoT devices to collect, process, and transmit data.

#### Embedded Computing Basics
Embedded computing involves the use of microcontrollers and microprocessors to perform specific tasks. These devices are designed to be small, low-power, and inexpensive.

#### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms
There are several hardware platforms that are commonly used in IoT devices. These include:

- **Arduino**: An open-source platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software.
- **Netduino**: A platform similar to Arduino, but based on the .NET Micro Framework.
- **Raspberry Pi**: A small, low-cost computer that can run a full operating system.
- **BeagleBone**: A platform similar to Raspberry Pi, but with more powerful hardware.
- **Intel Galileo**: A platform based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000, designed for IoT applications.
- **ARM Cortex**: A family of microcontroller cores designed for use in embedded systems.
