## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

### Sensors
Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They convert physical parameters such as temperature, humidity, light, and sound into electrical signals that can be processed by a computer.

### Digital Sensors
Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal. They are commonly used in IoT applications because they can be easily interfaced with microcontrollers and other digital devices.

### Actuators
Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical actions. They are used in IoT applications to control physical systems such as motors, lights, and valves.

### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology
RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is commonly used in IoT applications for inventory management, asset tracking, and supply chain management.

### Wireless Sensor Networks
Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly. They are commonly used in IoT applications to monitor environmental conditions, track the movement of objects, and detect events.

### Participatory Sensing Technology
Participatory sensing technology involves the use of mobile devices to collect and share data. It is commonly used in IoT applications to gather data from a large number of users.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT
Embedded platforms are computer systems that are designed to be integrated into other devices. They are commonly used in IoT applications to provide computing power and connectivity.

#### Embedded Computing Basics
Embedded computing involves the use of microcontrollers and microprocessors to perform specific tasks. These devices are designed to be small, low-power, and inexpensive.

#### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms
There are several hardware platforms that are commonly used in IoT applications. These include Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex.

##### Arduino
Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is commonly used in IoT applications because of its simplicity and flexibility.

##### Netduino
Netduino is an open-source electronics platform based on the .NET Micro Framework. It is similar to Arduino but offers additional features such as support for networking and web services.

##### Raspberry Pi
Raspberry Pi is a series of small single-board computers. It is commonly used in IoT applications because of its low cost and support for a wide range of programming languages and operating systems.

##### BeagleBone
BeagleBone is a series of open-source single-board computers. It is commonly used in IoT applications because of its support for real-time processing and its ability to interface with a wide range of sensors and actuators.

##### Intel Galileo Boards
Intel Galileo boards are a series of development boards based on the Intel Quark SoC. They are commonly used in IoT applications because of their support for a wide range of operating systems and programming languages.

##### ARM Cortex
ARM Cortex is a family of microcontroller cores designed by ARM Holdings. They are commonly used in IoT applications because of their low power consumption and support for a wide range of peripherals.
