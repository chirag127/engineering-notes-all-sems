### Hardware for IoT

Unit 2 of the subject "Introduction to Internet of Things" covers the following topics:

1. **Sensors**: Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They are used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, light, sound, and pressure.

2. **Digital sensors**: Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal. They are used in IoT applications to measure and transmit data.

3. **Actuators**: Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical movement. They are used in IoT applications to control physical systems.

4. **Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology**: RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is used in IoT applications to track the location and movement of objects.

5. **Wireless sensor networks**: Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly. They are used in IoT applications to collect and transmit data.

6. **Participatory sensing technology**: Participatory sensing technology involves the use of mobile devices to collect and share data. It is used in IoT applications to gather data from a large number of users.

7. **Embedded Platforms for IoT**: Embedded platforms are computer systems that are designed to perform specific tasks. They are used in IoT applications to control and process data.

8. **Embedded computing basics**: Embedded computing involves the use of computer systems in devices that are not traditional computers. It is used in IoT applications to provide computing power to devices.

9. **Overview of IoT supported Hardware platforms**: There are several hardware platforms that support IoT applications. These include Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex.

10. **Arduino**: Arduino is an open-source electronics platform that is based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is used in IoT applications to control and process data.

11. **Netduino**: Netduino is an open-source electronics platform that is based on the .NET Micro Framework. It is used in IoT applications to control and process data.

12. **Raspberry Pi**: Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer that is designed to promote the teaching of basic computer science. It is used in IoT applications to control and process data.

13. **BeagleBone**: BeagleBone is a low-cost, community-supported development platform that is based on the ARM Cortex-A8 processor. It is used in IoT applications to control and process data.

14. **Intel Galileo boards**: Intel Galileo boards are development boards that are based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000 processor. They are used in IoT applications to control and process data.

15. **ARM Cortex**: ARM Cortex is a family of microcontroller cores that are designed by ARM Holdings. They are used in IoT applications to control and process data.
