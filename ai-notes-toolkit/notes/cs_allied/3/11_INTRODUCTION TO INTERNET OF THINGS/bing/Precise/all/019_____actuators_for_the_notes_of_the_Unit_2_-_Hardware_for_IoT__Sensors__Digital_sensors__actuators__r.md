### Actuators

Actuators are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy. They are used to control or move mechanical systems. In the context of IoT, actuators are used to control or manipulate the physical environment.

There are several types of actuators that are commonly used in IoT systems:

1. **Electric motors:** These are used to convert electrical energy into rotational motion. They are commonly used in applications such as robotics, automation, and transportation.

2. **Solenoids:** These are electromechanical devices that convert electrical energy into linear motion. They are commonly used in applications such as locking mechanisms, valves, and switches.

3. **Pneumatic actuators:** These use compressed air to generate linear or rotational motion. They are commonly used in applications such as industrial automation and robotics.

4. **Hydraulic actuators:** These use pressurized fluid to generate linear or rotational motion. They are commonly used in applications such as heavy machinery and transportation.

Actuators are an essential component of IoT systems as they allow the system to interact with and manipulate the physical environment. They are used in conjunction with sensors to create closed-loop control systems, where the sensor data is used to control the actuator and manipulate the environment in a desired manner.
