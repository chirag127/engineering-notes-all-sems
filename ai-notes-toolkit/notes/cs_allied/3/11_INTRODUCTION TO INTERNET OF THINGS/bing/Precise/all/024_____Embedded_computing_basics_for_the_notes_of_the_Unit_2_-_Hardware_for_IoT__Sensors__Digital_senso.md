# Embedded Computing Basics

Embedded computing refers to the use of computer systems and software to perform dedicated functions within larger systems. These systems are often integrated into other devices or products and are designed to perform specific tasks. Embedded computing systems are typically small, low-power, and have limited processing capabilities.

## Hardware for IoT

### Sensors

Sensors are devices that detect and respond to changes in the environment. They are used to measure physical quantities such as temperature, humidity, light, sound, and motion. Sensors can be analog or digital, and can be used to collect data for IoT applications.

### Digital Sensors

Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal. They are typically more accurate and reliable than analog sensors, and are easier to interface with other digital systems.

### Actuators

Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical motion. They are used to control the movement of mechanical systems, and can be used in IoT applications to control physical systems.

### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

RFID technology uses radio waves to identify and track objects. RFID tags are small, low-power devices that can be attached to objects and used to track their location and movement.

### Wireless Sensor Networks

Wireless sensor networks are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly. They are used to collect and transmit data from multiple sensors, and can be used in IoT applications to collect data from a large area.

### Participatory Sensing Technology

Participatory sensing technology refers to the use of mobile devices and sensors to collect data from individuals. This data can be used to monitor and track environmental conditions, and can be used in IoT applications to collect data from a large population.

## Embedded Platforms for IoT

### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms

There are several hardware platforms that are commonly used for IoT applications. These include Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo, and ARM Cortex boards.

#### Arduino

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is commonly used for IoT projects due to its simplicity and flexibility.

#### Netduino

Netduino is a similar platform to Arduino, but is based on the .NET Micro Framework. It is commonly used for IoT projects that require more advanced programming capabilities.

#### Raspberry Pi

Raspberry Pi is a small, low-cost computer that can be used for IoT projects. It is capable of running a full operating system and can be used for more advanced IoT applications.

#### BeagleBone

BeagleBone is a small, low-cost computer that is similar to the Raspberry Pi. It is commonly used for IoT projects that require more processing power and flexibility.

#### Intel Galileo

Intel Galileo is a microcontroller board based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000. It is commonly used for IoT projects that require more advanced processing capabilities.

#### ARM Cortex

ARM Cortex boards are microcontroller boards based on the ARM Cortex-M series of processors. They are commonly used for IoT projects that require low-power, high-performance processing capabilities.
