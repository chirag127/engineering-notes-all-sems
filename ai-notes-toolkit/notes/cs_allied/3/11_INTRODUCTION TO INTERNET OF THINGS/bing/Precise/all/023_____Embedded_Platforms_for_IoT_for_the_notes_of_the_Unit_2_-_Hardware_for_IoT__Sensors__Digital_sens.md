# Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms are an essential part of the Internet of Things (IoT) as they provide the computing power and connectivity required for IoT devices to function. These platforms are designed to be small, low-power, and capable of running specialized software to collect, process, and transmit data from sensors and other sources.

Some of the most popular embedded platforms for IoT include:

1. **Arduino**: An open-source platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is widely used for building digital devices and interactive objects that can sense and control physical devices.

2. **Netduino**: A platform similar to Arduino, but based on the .NET Micro Framework. It is designed to make it easy to develop IoT applications using C# and other .NET languages.

3. **Raspberry Pi**: A small, low-cost computer that can run a full version of Linux and other operating systems. It is widely used for building IoT devices and applications.

4. **BeagleBone**: A platform similar to Raspberry Pi, but with more powerful hardware and a focus on industrial applications.

5. **Intel Galileo**: A platform based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000, designed for building IoT devices and applications.

6. **ARM Cortex**: A family of microcontrollers designed for low-power, high-performance applications, including IoT devices.

These platforms provide a range of options for developers to build IoT devices and applications, with varying levels of complexity, power, and cost. Choosing the right platform depends on the specific requirements of the project, including the type of sensors and actuators being used, the amount of data being processed, and the connectivity options available.
