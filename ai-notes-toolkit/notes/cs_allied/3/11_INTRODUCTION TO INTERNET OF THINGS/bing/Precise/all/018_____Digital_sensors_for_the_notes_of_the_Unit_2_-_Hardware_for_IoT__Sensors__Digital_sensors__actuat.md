# Digital Sensors

Digital sensors are electronic devices that convert physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, or light intensity into digital signals that can be processed by a computer. These sensors are commonly used in IoT (Internet of Things) applications to collect data from the physical environment.

Some common types of digital sensors used in IoT include:
- Temperature sensors: These sensors measure the temperature of the environment and convert it into a digital signal.
- Pressure sensors: These sensors measure the pressure of a fluid or gas and convert it into a digital signal.
- Light sensors: These sensors measure the intensity of light and convert it into a digital signal.
- Humidity sensors: These sensors measure the humidity of the environment and convert it into a digital signal.

Digital sensors are an essential component of IoT systems as they provide the data that is used to monitor and control the physical environment. They are typically small, low-power devices that can be easily integrated into IoT systems.

In summary, digital sensors are electronic devices that convert physical quantities into digital signals. They are commonly used in IoT applications to collect data from the physical environment.
