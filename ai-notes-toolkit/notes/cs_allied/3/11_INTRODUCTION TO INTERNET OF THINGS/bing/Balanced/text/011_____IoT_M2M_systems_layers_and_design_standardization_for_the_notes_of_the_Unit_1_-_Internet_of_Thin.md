### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

- IoT/M2M systems are composed of devices, applications and networks that communicate and exchange data with each other.
- To enable interoperability and scalability of IoT/M2M systems, standardization is needed at different layers of the system architecture.
- One of the leading initiatives for IoT/M2M standardization is oneM2M, which was launched in 2012 by ETSI and 13 other founding members.
- The oneM2M architecture divides IoT functions into three major domains: the application layer, the service layer and the network layer  .
- The application layer is responsible for providing the functionality and user interface of the IoT/M2M system. It includes the devices, sensors, actuators and applications that interact with the system.
- The service layer is responsible for providing common services and functions that are shared by different applications and devices. It includes the data management, security, discovery, registration, subscription and notification services  .
- The network layer is responsible for providing the connectivity and transport of data between the devices and the service layer. It includes the access networks, gateways, routers and protocols that enable communication  .
- The oneM2M architecture promotes interoperability through IT-friendly APIs and supports a wide range of IoT technologies, such as cellular, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, LoRa, etc.
- The oneM2M architecture also defines a common data model and a resource structure that enable data enrichment and consolidation across different applications and devices.
- The oneM2M architecture aims to ease the designing and affordability of IoT/M2M systems by providing a standardized and modular framework that can be reused and adapted for different use cases and scenarios.
- Some examples of IoT/M2M systems that can benefit from the oneM2M architecture are smart cities, smart homes, smart grids, smart health, smart agriculture, etc .
