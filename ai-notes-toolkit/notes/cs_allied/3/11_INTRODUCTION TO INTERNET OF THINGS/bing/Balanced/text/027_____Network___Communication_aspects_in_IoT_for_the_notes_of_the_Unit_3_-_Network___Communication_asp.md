### Network & Communication aspects in IoT

- Network and communication aspects in IoT refer to the methods and protocols that enable IoT devices to communicate with each other, with applications, and with the cloud over the internet .
- IoT devices can use various local communication technologies, such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, Z-Wave, LoRa, etc., depending on their requirements and capabilities .
- IoT devices also need to use application protocols, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, etc., to define how the information content is transported and formatted .
- IoT devices may also need to use gateways, which are intermediate devices that translate and re-transmit information, typically linking local device networks to the internet .
- Some of the network and communication challenges and issues in IoT are:
  - Wireless Medium access issues: How to coordinate the access of multiple IoT devices to the shared wireless medium, such as radio frequency, without causing interference, congestion, or collision.
  - MAC protocol survey: How to design and evaluate different medium access control (MAC) protocols for IoT devices, such as TDMA, CSMA, ALOHA, etc., considering their performance, scalability, reliability, and energy efficiency.
  - Survey routing protocols: How to design and evaluate different routing protocols for IoT devices, such as AODV, DSR, OLSR, RPL, etc., considering their suitability, overhead, latency, and adaptability for different IoT scenarios.
  - Sensor deployment & Node discovery: How to deploy and organize IoT devices in different environments, such as indoor, outdoor, urban, rural, etc., and how to discover and identify the devices and their capabilities and locations.
  - Data aggregation & dissemination: How to collect and process the data generated by IoT devices, and how to disseminate the data to the relevant applications and services, considering the data quality, security, privacy, and efficiency.
