- Internet of Things (IoT) is the interconnection of computing devices embedded in everyday objects, enabling them to send and receive data over the internet or other communication networks .
- IoT has a vision of creating a smart and connected world, where physical objects can interact with each other and with humans, and provide services and solutions to various domains and applications.
- IoT has a conceptual framework that consists of four main components: things, networks, platforms and applications.
  - Things are the physical objects that have sensors, actuators, processors and communication modules, and can collect, process and transmit data.
  - Networks are the communication infrastructures that connect the things to the platforms and applications, and can use different technologies and protocols, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, cellular, etc.
  - Platforms are the software systems that provide the functionalities and services for the IoT, such as data storage, processing, analysis, visualization, security, etc.
  - Applications are the end-user scenarios and use cases that utilize the IoT, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
- IoT has an architectural view that can be divided into three layers: perception, network and application.
  - Perception layer is the lowest layer that consists of the things and their sensors and actuators, and is responsible for collecting and controlling the data from the physical world.
  - Network layer is the middle layer that consists of the networks and their communication modules, and is responsible for transmitting and routing the data from the perception layer to the application layer, and vice versa.
  - Application layer is the highest layer that consists of the platforms and their software modules, and is responsible for processing, analyzing and presenting the data from the network layer, and providing the services and solutions for the applications.
- IoT has various technologies behind it, such as sensor technology, wireless communication technology, cloud computing technology, big data technology, artificial intelligence technology, etc.
  - Sensor technology is the technology that enables the things to sense and measure the physical phenomena, such as temperature, humidity, pressure, motion, etc.
  - Wireless communication technology is the technology that enables the things to communicate with each other and with the platforms and applications, using different standards and protocols, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, ZigBee, cellular, etc.
  - Cloud computing technology is the technology that enables the platforms to store, process and analyze the large amount of data generated by the IoT, using distributed and scalable computing resources, such as servers, databases, etc.
  - Big data technology is the technology that enables the platforms to handle the variety, velocity and volume of the data generated by the IoT, using advanced techniques and tools, such as Hadoop, Spark, etc.
  - Artificial intelligence technology is the technology that enables the platforms and applications to extract meaningful insights and knowledge from the data generated by the IoT, using machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
- IoT has various sources that can generate the data, such as smart devices, smart sensors, smart meters, smart cameras, smart wearables, smart vehicles, smart appliances, etc.
- IoT has a type of communication called machine-to-machine (M2M) communication, which is the direct exchange of data between the things without human intervention, and can enable automation, optimization and coordination of the IoT.
- IoT has various examples that demonstrate its potential and benefits, such as smart home, smart city, smart health, smart agriculture, etc.
