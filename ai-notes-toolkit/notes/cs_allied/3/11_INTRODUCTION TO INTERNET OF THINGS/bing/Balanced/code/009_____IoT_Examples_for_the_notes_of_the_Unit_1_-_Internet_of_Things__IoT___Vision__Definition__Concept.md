# IoT Examples

The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that can collect, process, exchange and utilize data interrelatedly, via the internet or other communications networks. IoT has many different applications in various domains, such as transportation, home automation, healthcare, agriculture, retail, manufacturing and smart cities. Here are some examples of IoT devices and systems:

- **Connected cars**: These are vehicles that can communicate with other vehicles, infrastructure, cloud services and drivers through sensors, cameras, GPS, onboard computers and wireless technologies. Connected cars can provide features such as navigation, entertainment, safety, diagnostics, maintenance and remote control .
- **Smart appliances**: These are household devices that can be controlled and monitored remotely via smartphones, tablets or voice assistants. Smart appliances can provide features such as energy efficiency, convenience, security and automation . Examples of smart appliances include refrigerators, ovens, washing machines, air conditioners, lights and speakers.
- **Connected security systems**: These are systems that can protect homes and businesses from intruders, fire, flood and other hazards through sensors, cameras, alarms, locks and notifications. Connected security systems can provide features such as real-time monitoring, remote access, automation and emergency response .
- **Smart agriculture equipment**: These are devices that can help farmers optimize crop production, irrigation, pest control and animal welfare through sensors, drones, robots, satellites and cloud services. Smart agriculture equipment can provide features such as precision farming, data analytics, automation and sustainability .
- **Connected retail**: This is the use of IoT devices and systems to enhance the shopping experience, customer loyalty, inventory management and supply chain efficiency in the retail sector. Connected retail can provide features such as personalized recommendations, smart shelves, digital signage, mobile payments and smart logistics  .
- **Connected healthcare monitors**: These are devices that can track and transmit vital signs, health conditions, medication adherence and physical activity of patients and users through sensors, wearables, implants and cloud services. Connected healthcare monitors can provide features such as remote diagnosis, telemedicine, preventive care and wellness  .
- **Connected manufacturing equipment**: These are machines and tools that can improve the quality, efficiency, safety and flexibility of industrial processes through sensors, actuators, robots, RFID tags and cloud services. Connected manufacturing equipment can provide features such as predictive maintenance, process optimization, quality control and smart logistics  .
- **Connected cities**: These are urban areas that can enhance the livability, sustainability, mobility and governance of their citizens and visitors through sensors, cameras, smart meters, smart grids, smart lighting, smart parking and cloud services. Connected cities can provide features such as traffic management, waste management, air quality monitoring, public safety and citizen engagement  .
