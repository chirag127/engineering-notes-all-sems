## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

### Sensors

- Sensors are devices that provide a usable output in response to a specified measurement.
- Sensors can be classified into two types: analog and digital.
- Analog sensors produce a continuous output signal that varies with the physical quantity being measured, such as temperature, light, or sound.
- Digital sensors produce a discrete output signal that is either on or off, such as a switch, a button, or a motion detector.
- Sensors can be used for various applications in IoT, such as monitoring, control, automation, security, and health.

### Digital sensors

- Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output signal that is either on or off.
- Digital sensors can be further classified into two types: active and passive.
- Active digital sensors require an external power source to operate, such as a battery or a solar panel.
- Passive digital sensors do not require an external power source, but instead generate their own power from the physical quantity being measured, such as RFID tags.
- Digital sensors can be used for various applications in IoT, such as identification, tracking, authentication, and data transmission.

### Actuators

- Actuators are devices that convert an input signal into a physical action, such as movement, sound, or light.
- Actuators can be classified into two types: electric and non-electric.
- Electric actuators use electricity to perform the action, such as motors, speakers, or LEDs.
- Non-electric actuators use other forms of energy, such as pneumatic, hydraulic, or thermal, to perform the action, such as valves, pumps, or fans.
- Actuators can be used for various applications in IoT, such as feedback, control, automation, and interaction.

### Radio frequency identification (RFID) technology

- RFID technology is a wireless communication technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects.
- RFID technology consists of two main components: RFID tags and RFID readers.
- RFID tags are small devices that store a unique identifier and can be attached to objects.
- RFID readers are devices that emit radio waves and can read the identifier from the tags.
- RFID technology can be used for various applications in IoT, such as inventory management, asset tracking, access control, and payment systems.

### Wireless sensor networks

- Wireless sensor networks are networks of interconnected sensors that communicate wirelessly and cooperate to gather and provide information from the environment.
- Wireless sensor networks consist of three main components: sensor nodes, sink nodes, and base stations.
- Sensor nodes are small devices that have sensing, processing, and communication capabilities.
- Sink nodes are devices that collect data from the sensor nodes and forward it to the base station.
- Base stations are devices that receive data from the sink nodes and provide access to the network.
- Wireless sensor networks can be used for various applications in IoT, such as environmental monitoring, smart agriculture, disaster management, and health care.

### Participatory sensing technology

- Participatory sensing technology is a type of sensing technology that involves human participation and collaboration in the data collection and analysis process.
- Participatory sensing technology leverages the capabilities of mobile devices, such as smartphones, tablets, or wearables, that have various sensors, such as cameras, microphones, GPS, or accelerometers.
- Participatory sensing technology can be used for various applications in IoT, such as social networking, urban planning, traffic management, and citizen science.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT

#### Embedded computing basics

- Embedded computing is a type of computing that involves designing and implementing systems that have a dedicated function within a larger system.
- Embedded computing systems are typically composed of hardware and software components that work together to perform the function.
- Embedded computing systems are usually constrained by factors such as size, power, cost, and performance.
- Embedded computing systems can be used for various applications in IoT, such as smart devices, wearable devices, smart home, and smart city.
