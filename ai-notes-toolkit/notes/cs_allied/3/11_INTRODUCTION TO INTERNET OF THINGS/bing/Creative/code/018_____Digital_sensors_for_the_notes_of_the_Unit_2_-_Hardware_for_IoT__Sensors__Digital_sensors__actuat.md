### Digital sensors for IoT

- Digital sensors are pieces of hardware that detect changes in an environment and collect data in a digital format .
- Digital sensors can interact directly with an IoT microcontroller, without the need for an analog-to-digital converter.
- Digital sensors can be classified based on the physical quantity they measure, such as temperature, pressure, humidity, light, motion, sound, etc.
- Digital sensors can also be classified based on the communication protocol they use, such as I2C, SPI, UART, etc.
- Digital sensors are essential for bridging the digital world to the physical world in IoT applications, such as consumer devices, industry 4.0, and medical applications .
- Digital sensors can be integrated with other components, such as actuators, radio frequency identification (RFID) technology, wireless sensor networks, and participatory sensing technology, to create smart and connected IoT systems.
- Digital sensors can be used with various IoT supported hardware platforms, such as Arduino, Netduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex .
- Digital sensors can enable rapid IoT prototyping and testing, as well as data collection and analysis for smarter decisions.
