## Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

### Sensors, Digital sensors, actuators

- Sensors are devices that provide a usable output in response to a specified measurement, such as temperature, pressure, motion, etc. 
- Digital sensors are sensors that produce a digital output, such as binary or pulse signals, that can be easily processed by computers or microcontrollers. 
- Actuators are devices that perform an action in response to a signal, such as opening a valve, turning on a light, or moving a robot arm. 
- Sensors and actuators are essential components of IoT systems, as they enable the interaction between the physical and digital worlds. 

### Radio frequency identification (RFID) technology, wireless sensor networks, participatory sensing technology

- RFID is a technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects, such as tags, cards, or chips, that are attached to or embedded in the objects. 
- Wireless sensor networks (WSNs) are networks of interconnected sensors that cooperate to gather and provide information from the environment, such as temperature, humidity, sound, etc. 
- Participatory sensing technology is a technology that uses mobile devices, such as smartphones or tablets, to collect and share data from the users or their surroundings, such as location, activity, or air quality. 
- RFID, WSNs, and participatory sensing are key technologies that enable the IoT, as they provide wireless sensing and communication capabilities for various applications, such as smart cities, health care, agriculture, etc.  

### Embedded Platforms for IoT: Embedded computing basics, Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards and ARM cortex

- Embedded computing is a type of computing that involves designing and implementing software and hardware systems that are dedicated to a specific function or application, such as controlling a device, processing a signal, or communicating data. 
- Embedded platforms are hardware devices that support embedded computing, such as microcontrollers, microprocessors, or system-on-chips, that have memory, input/output, and communication interfaces. 
- IoT supported hardware platforms are embedded platforms that are designed or adapted to support IoT applications, such as sensing, actuating, or connecting to the internet. 
- Some examples of IoT supported hardware platforms are:

  - Arduino: a microcontroller-based platform that can be programmed using a simple and user-friendly language and can interface with various sensors and actuators. 
  - Netduino: a microcontroller-based platform that is compatible with Arduino and can be programmed using .NET languages, such as C# or Visual Basic. 
  - Raspberry Pi: a microprocessor-based platform that can run a full operating system, such as Linux or Windows, and can perform complex tasks, such as image processing or machine learning. 
  - BeagleBone: a microprocessor-based platform that can run a full operating system, such as Linux or Android, and can interface with various peripherals, such as cameras or displays. 
  - Intel Galileo: a microprocessor-based platform that is compatible with Arduino and can run a full operating system, such as Linux or Windows, and can connect to the internet using Ethernet or Wi-Fi. 
  - ARM Cortex: a family of microprocessor-based platforms that can run various operating systems, such as Linux, Android, or Windows, and can support various applications, such as gaming, multimedia, or networking.
