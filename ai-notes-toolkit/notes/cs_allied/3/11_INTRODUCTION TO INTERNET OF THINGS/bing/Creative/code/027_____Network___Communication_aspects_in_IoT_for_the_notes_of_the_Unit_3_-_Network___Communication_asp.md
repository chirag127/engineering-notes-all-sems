### Network & Communication aspects in IoT

- Network and communication aspects in IoT refer to the methods and protocols that enable IoT devices to communicate with each other, with applications, and with the cloud over the internet .
- IoT devices can use different types of communication, such as device-to-device, device-to-cloud, device-to-gateway, and device-to-application.
- IoT devices can also use different types of local communication, such as wired or wireless, short-range or long-range, and low-power or high-power.
- Some of the common wireless communication technologies for IoT devices are Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, Z-Wave, LoRa, and cellular .
- IoT devices need to use application protocols that define how information content is transported and formatted, such as MQTT, CoAP, HTTP, and AMQP .
- IoT devices may also need to use gateways that translate and re-transmit information, typically linking local device networks to the internet or the cloud.
- Some of the network and communication challenges and issues in IoT are:
  - Wireless medium access issues: how to coordinate the access of multiple IoT devices to the shared wireless medium, such as using contention-based or reservation-based schemes.
  - MAC protocol survey: how to design and evaluate different MAC protocols for IoT devices, such as considering energy efficiency, scalability, reliability, and latency.
  - Survey routing protocols: how to route information packets from IoT devices to their destinations, such as using hierarchical, geographic, or data-centric approaches.
  - Sensor deployment and node discovery: how to deploy and configure IoT devices in different environments, such as using random, deterministic, or adaptive methods.
  - Data aggregation and dissemination: how to collect and distribute data from and to IoT devices, such as using in-network processing, compression, or encryption techniques.
