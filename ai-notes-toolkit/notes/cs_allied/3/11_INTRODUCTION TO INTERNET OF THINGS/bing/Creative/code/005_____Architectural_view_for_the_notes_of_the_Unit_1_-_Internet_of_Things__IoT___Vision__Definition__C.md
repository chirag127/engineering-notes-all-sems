## Internet of Things (IoT): Vision, Definition, Conceptual Framework

- The **Internet of Things (IoT)** is the **networking capability** that allows **information** to be **sent** to and **received** from **objects and devices** (such as fixtures and kitchen appliances) using the **Internet** or other **communications networks** .
- The **vision** of IoT is to **enable** the **interconnection** and **integration** of the **physical world** and the **cyber space**. It aims to **create** a **smart environment** where **everything** can be **measured**, **monitored**, **controlled** and **optimized**.
- The **conceptual framework** of IoT consists of four **layers**: the **device layer**, the **network layer**, the **service layer** and the **application layer**.
  - The **device layer** includes the **physical objects** and **devices** that are **embedded** with **sensors**, **actuators**, **processors** and **communication modules** to **collect** and **transmit** data.
  - The **network layer** provides the **connectivity** and **transportation** of data among the **devices** and the **service layer** using various **wired** and **wireless** technologies, such as **Wi-Fi**, **Bluetooth**, **ZigBee**, **cellular**, **satellite**, etc.
  - The **service layer** offers the **data processing**, **storage**, **management** and **analysis** functions to **support** the **application layer**. It also provides the **security**, **privacy**, **trust** and **quality of service** mechanisms to **ensure** the **reliability** and **safety** of the IoT system.
  - The **application layer** delivers the **value-added services** and **solutions** to the **end users** and **stakeholders** based on the **data** and **information** obtained from the **service layer**. It covers various **domains** and **scenarios**, such as **smart home**, **smart city**, **smart health**, **smart agriculture**, etc.

## Architectural view, technology behind IoT, Sources of the IoT, M2M Communication, IoT Examples

- The **architectural view** of IoT depicts the **structure**, **components**, **relationships** and **interactions** of the IoT system from different **perspectives** and **levels of abstraction**. It can be **described** using various **models** and **standards**, such as the **three-tier model**, the **five-layer model**, the **seven-layer model**, the **ISO/IEC 30141 reference architecture**, the **ITU-T Y.2060 reference model**, etc.
- The **technology behind IoT** refers to the **enabling technologies** and **tools** that **facilitate** the **implementation** and **operation** of the IoT system. It includes the **hardware**, **software**, **protocols**, **platforms**, **frameworks**, **algorithms**, **methods**, etc. that are **involved** in the **device layer**, the **network layer**, the **service layer** and the **application layer**.
- The **sources of the IoT** are the **origins** and **providers** of the **data** and **information** that are **used** and **consumed** by the IoT system. They can be **classified** into three **categories**: the **physical sources**, the **virtual sources** and the **social sources**.
