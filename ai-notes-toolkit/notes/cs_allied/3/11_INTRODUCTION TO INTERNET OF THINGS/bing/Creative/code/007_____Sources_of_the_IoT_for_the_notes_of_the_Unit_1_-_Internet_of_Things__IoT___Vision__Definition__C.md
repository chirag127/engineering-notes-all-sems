### Sources of the IoT

- The Internet of Things (IoT) is the network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet.
- The sources of the IoT are the devices, sensors, and systems that generate, collect, and transmit data over the internet. Some examples of IoT sources are:
  - Temperature, flow, pressure, and humidity sensors that monitor environmental conditions and industrial processes.
  - Cameras, microphones, and motion detectors that capture images, sounds, and movements for security and surveillance purposes.
  - Smartphones, wearables, and smart home devices that track user activities, preferences, and health metrics.
  - RFID tags, GPS trackers, and barcodes that identify and locate objects and people.
  - Smart meters, thermostats, and lighting systems that optimize energy consumption and management.
- The sources of the IoT can be classified into three categories based on their application domains:
  - Consumer IoT: The devices and systems used by individuals and households for personal and entertainment purposes, such as smart TVs, speakers, and appliances.
  - Industrial IoT: The devices and systems used by enterprises and organizations for operational and business purposes, such as manufacturing, logistics, and healthcare.
  - Commercial IoT: The devices and systems used by public and private sectors for social and environmental purposes, such as smart cities, agriculture, and education.
