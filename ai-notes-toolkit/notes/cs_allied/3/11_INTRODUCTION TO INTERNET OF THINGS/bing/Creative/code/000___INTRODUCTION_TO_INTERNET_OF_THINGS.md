# Introduction to Internet of Things

- The Internet of Things (IoT) describes the network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet.
- IoT devices can range from ordinary household objects to sophisticated industrial tools, and can transfer data to one another without human intervention.
- IoT devices are assigned a unique identifier (UID) that enables them to communicate with each other and the cloud.
- IoT applications can be found in various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, smart manufacturing, smart energy, smart transportation, and more .
- IoT systems consist of functional blocks, such as devices, communications, services, management, security, and application.
- IoT devices are the sensors and actuators that collect and control data from the physical world.
- IoT communications are the protocols and technologies that enable data transmission and networking among IoT devices and the cloud.
- IoT services are the software components that provide data processing, storage, analytics, and visualization for IoT applications.
- IoT management is the process of configuring, monitoring, and maintaining IoT devices and systems.
- IoT security is the set of measures that protect IoT devices and data from unauthorized access, manipulation, or damage.
- IoT application is the end-user interface that provides functionality and value for the IoT system.
- IoT architecture is the design methodology that defines the structure, components, and interactions of an IoT system.
