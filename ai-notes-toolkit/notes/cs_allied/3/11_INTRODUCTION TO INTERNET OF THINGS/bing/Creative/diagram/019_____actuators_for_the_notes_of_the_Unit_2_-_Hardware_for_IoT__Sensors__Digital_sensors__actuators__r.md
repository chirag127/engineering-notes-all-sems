### Actuators for IoT

- An actuator is a device that converts energy into motion. It does this by taking an electrical signal and combining it with an energy source.
- In an IoT system, the actuator can act on data collected by sensors to create an outcome as determined by the chosen settings of the user.
- For example, a smart thermostat can use a temperature sensor to measure the room temperature and send a signal to an actuator that controls the heating or cooling system.
- There are different types of actuators for IoT, such as :
  - Servo Motors: A servomotor is a rotary actuator or linear actuator that allows for precise control of angular or linear position, velocity and acceleration. It can be used for robotics, automation, or precision control applications.
  - Stepper Motors: A stepper motor is a DC motor that moves in discrete steps. It can be used for positioning, speed control, or synchronization applications.
  - DC Motors: A DC motor is a continuous rotation motor that can be used for driving wheels, fans, pumps, or other devices that require constant speed or torque.
  - Linear Actuators: A linear actuator is a device that converts rotary motion into linear motion. It can be used for lifting, pushing, pulling, or adjusting objects or mechanisms.
  - Thermal/Magnetic Actuators: These are actuated by thermal or magnetic energy. Shape Memory Alloys (SMAs) or Magnetic Shape Memory Alloys (MSMAs) are examples of materials that can change their shape or size when heated or magnetized. They can be used for micro-actuation, biomedical, or aerospace applications.
  - Mechanical Actuators: A mechanical actuator executes movement by converting rotary motion into linear motion. It can be composed of gears, screws, levers, springs, or other mechanical components. It can be used for simple or complex mechanical tasks.
  - Soft Actuators: These are made of soft or flexible materials that can deform or bend when stimulated by electricity, light, heat, or chemicals. They can be used for soft robotics, wearable devices, or artificial muscles.
  - Light Activated Actuators: These are activated by light or laser beams. They can be made of photovoltaic cells, photochromic materials, or photomechanical materials. They can be used for optical switching, solar tracking, or optical communication.

- Actuators are essential components of IoT systems, as they enable physical actions based on sensor data and user commands. They can be used for various applications, such as smart home, smart city, smart industry, smart agriculture, smart healthcare, or smart transportation.
