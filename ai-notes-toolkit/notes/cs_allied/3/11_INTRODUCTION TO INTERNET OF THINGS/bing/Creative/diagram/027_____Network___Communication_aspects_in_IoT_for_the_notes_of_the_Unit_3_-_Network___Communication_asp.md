### Network & Communication aspects in IoT

- Network and communication aspects in IoT refer to the methods and protocols that enable IoT devices to exchange data and commands with other devices, applications, and services over the internet or local networks.
- IoT devices can communicate using different types of communication, such as device-to-device, device-to-cloud, device-to-gateway, and back-end data-sharing.
- IoT devices can use different types of communication protocols, such as wired or wireless, standard or proprietary, low-power or high-performance, depending on their requirements and capabilities.
- Some of the common communication protocols in IoT are:

  - Wireless Medium Access Control (MAC) protocols: These protocols define how IoT devices share the wireless medium and avoid collisions and interference. Some examples are IEEE 802.11 (Wi-Fi), IEEE 802.15.4 (Zigbee, Z-Wave, 6LoWPAN), IEEE 802.15.1 (Bluetooth), and LoRaWAN.
  - Routing protocols: These protocols define how IoT devices forward packets to their destinations, either directly or through intermediate nodes. Some examples are Ad hoc On-Demand Distance Vector (AODV), Dynamic Source Routing (DSR), Optimized Link State Routing (OLSR), and Routing Protocol for Low-Power and Lossy Networks (RPL).
  - Application protocols: These protocols define how IoT devices exchange information content and commands with other devices, applications, and services. Some examples are Constrained Application Protocol (CoAP), Message Queuing Telemetry Transport (MQTT), Hypertext Transfer Protocol (HTTP), and WebSocket.

- Some of the challenges and issues in network and communication aspects in IoT are:

  - Sensor deployment and node discovery: These refer to the processes of deploying IoT devices in the field and discovering their presence, location, and capabilities. Some of the factors that affect these processes are the number, type, and mobility of IoT devices, the network topology and connectivity, and the security and privacy requirements.
  - Data aggregation and dissemination: These refer to the processes of collecting, processing, and distributing data from IoT devices to other devices, applications, and services. Some of the factors that affect these processes are the data volume, velocity, and variety, the network bandwidth and latency, and the data quality and integrity.
