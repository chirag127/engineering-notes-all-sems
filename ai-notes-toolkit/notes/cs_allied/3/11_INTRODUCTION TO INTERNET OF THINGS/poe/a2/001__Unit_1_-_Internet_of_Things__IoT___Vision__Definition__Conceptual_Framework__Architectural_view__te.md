## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT):

- Vision: Connecting various devices over the internet and enabling them to send and receive data.
- Definition: Interconnection of everyday physical objects via the internet which allows them to send and receive data.
- Conceptual Framework: Network of physical objects, vehicles, buildings and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors and connectivity which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.
- Architectural view: Consists of sensors, connectivity, cloud and applications. Sensors collect data and send it to the cloud via connectivity which can be accessed by applications.
- Technology behind IoT: Sensors, microcontrollers, connectivity, cloud.
- Sources of the IoT: RFID, sensors, smartphones, etc.
- M2M Communication: Communication between machines or devices without human intervention. For example, autonomous vehicles communicating with traffic systems.
- IoT Examples: Smart home devices, smart cities, industrial automation, wearable devices, etc.

Design Principles for Connected Devices:

- IoT/M2M systems layers: Devices/sensors, connectivity/protocol, cloud/platform, application.
- Design standardization: Common standards for interoperability, communication protocols, security, etc.
- Communication technologies: WiFi, Bluetooth, cellular, RFID, NFC, etc.
- Data enrichment and consolidation: Aggregation, analysis and processing of data from various sources.
- Ease of designing and affordability: Components and technologies should be easy to incorporate and cost-effective.
