### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

- RFID is a wireless technology that uses radio waves to identify objects.
- RFID system consists of RFID tags and RFID readers.
- RFID tags are microchips that store data and are attached to objects. They contain antennas to enable them to receive and respond to radio-frequency queries from an RFID reader.
- RFID readers are devices that emit radio waves and receive responses from RFID tags. They convert the radio waves received from the tags into digital data that can be passed on to computers and networks.
- The RFID tags can be passive, semi-passive or active.
- Passive tags do not have an internal power source and get activated when they are in the electromagnetic field of the reader. Semi-passive and active tags have internal power sources. Active tags have longer range but are more expensive.
- RFID technology enables contactless identification of objects and can be used for applications such as access control, payment, supply chain management, etc.
- The advantages of RFID are that it does not require line of sight and can scan multiple tags simultaneously. However, RFID raises privacy concerns as tags can be read without the knowledge of the individual. Proper security measures need to be in place to address these concerns.
