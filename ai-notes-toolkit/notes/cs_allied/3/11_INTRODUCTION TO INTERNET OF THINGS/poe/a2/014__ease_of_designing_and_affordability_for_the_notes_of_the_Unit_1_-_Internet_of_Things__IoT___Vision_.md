 Here are the notes on ease of designing and affordability for the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT):

### Ease of Designing
- IoT devices and systems should be easy to design and develop to enable rapid innovation and deployment.
- Using open standards and protocols for connectivity, data formats, APIs, etc. enables interoperability and eases designing of IoT solutions.
- Modular design approaches allowing for reusability of components reduce time-to-market and costs.
- Cloud platforms provide ready-to-use services to easily connect devices and build applications.
- Low cost and low power components and development boards are enabling more developers and companies to build IoT solutions.

### Affordability
- Affordability of devices and overall IoT solutions is key for mass adoption and scaling the IoT.
- Cost of components like sensors, actuators, computing and communication modules is decreasing rapidly making devices more affordable.
- Cloud services and platforms provide cost-effective ways to process and store data and host applications.
- Open source software and hardware are reducing licensing and development costs.
- Mass production and increasing demand are driving costs down further following the traditional technology adoption lifecycle.

Overall, continued innovation and standardization in the areas of components, connectivity, cloud services, and development approaches are making it more feasible to design and deploy affordable IoT solutions that can scale to the vision of a fully connected world.
