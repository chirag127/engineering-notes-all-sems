### Internet of Things (IoT): Vision

- IoT refers to the interconnection of everyday physical objects and appliances via the internet.
- The vision of IoT is to enable everyday physical objects to be connected and exchange data.
- IoT aims to transform the way we live and work by creating smart spaces and smart environments.

### Definition

- IoT can be defined as a dynamic global network infrastructure with self-configuring capabilities based on standard and interoperable communication protocols where physical and virtual 'things' have identities, physical attributes, and virtual personalities and use intelligent interfaces, and are seamlessly integrated into the information network.

### Conceptual Framework

- The key components of an IoT system are:
- Sensors and actuators: To sense and collect data from the physical world and perform actions.
- Connectivity: To provide network connectivity between the components.
- Data processing: To analyze and process the data collected.
- Applications: To offer interfaces and interactions with the users.
