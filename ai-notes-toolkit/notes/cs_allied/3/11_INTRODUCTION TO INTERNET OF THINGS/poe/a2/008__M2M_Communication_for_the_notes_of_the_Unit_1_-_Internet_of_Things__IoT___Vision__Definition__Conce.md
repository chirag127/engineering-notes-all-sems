### M2M Communication

- M2M refers to Machine-to-Machine communication which is the automated communication between devices without human intervention.
- M2M communication is the foundation of IoT as it allows connected devices to exchange data and communicate with each other.
- The key components of M2M communication are:
-- Connected devices: These are the machines or devices that can send and receive data.
-- Connectivity: This refers to the network that provides connectivity between the devices. It can be cellular, Wi-Fi, etc.
-- Platform: The platform allows the connected devices to communicate, store, analyze and manage the data. It acts as an intermediary between the devices and the applications.
-- Applications: The applications make use of the data collected from the connected devices to provide various services to the users.

- The key benefits of M2M communication are:
-- Automation: It enables automation of processes and tasks without human intervention.
-- Remote monitoring: It allows remote monitoring of devices, systems, and environments.
-- Faster response: It enables faster response to events or situations by triggering actions automatically.
-- Cost efficiency: It leads to cost savings through automation and remote management.
-- Increased throughput: It increases the throughput and efficiency through coordinated communication between devices.

- The applications of M2M communication include smart homes, smart cities, industrial automation, telemedicine, smart grids, and more. M2M communication is a key enabling technology for the Internet of Things.
