### Survey Routing Protocols

Routing is an essential aspect of communication in IoT devices. It refers to the process of determining the path that data takes between nodes in a network. Routing protocols play a significant role in addressing this process.

Here are some of the survey routing protocols used in IoT:

1. RPL (Routing Protocol for Low-Power and Lossy Networks): It is an IPv6-based protocol that is designed for low-power and lossy networks (LLNs). It is used for routing packets between IoT devices that have limited power, memory, and processing capabilities.

2. AODV (Ad hoc On-demand Distance Vector): It is a reactive routing protocol that is used in mobile ad hoc networks (MANETs). It is used to discover routes between nodes only when they need to communicate.

3. DSR (Dynamic Source Routing): It is another reactive protocol that is used in MANETs. It allows nodes to dynamically discover routes between themselves.

4. OLSR (Optimized Link State Routing): It is a proactive routing protocol that is used in mobile ad hoc networks. It is used to maintain a constant communication between nodes by periodically broadcasting information about the network.

5. BATMAN (Better Approach To Mobile Adhoc Networking): It is a proactive protocol that is used to route packets in wireless mesh networks. It is designed to provide high throughput and low latency.

6. ZigBee Routing Protocol: It is a mesh networking protocol that is designed to provide low-latency and reliable communication between devices. It is used in low-power wireless networks.

7. BLE Mesh Routing Protocol: It is a mesh networking protocol that is designed for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices. It is used to provide reliable and scalable communication in IoT devices.

In conclusion, routing protocols play a crucial role in IoT communication. They enable devices to communicate with each other efficiently and effectively. The choice of a routing protocol depends on the specific requirements of the IoT system.
