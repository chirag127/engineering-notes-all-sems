### Embedded Platforms for IoT

In the world of Internet of Things (IoT), embedded platforms play a crucial role in enabling the connection and communication between devices. Embedded platforms are small, low-power devices that are integrated into larger systems to provide specific functionalities. These platforms are designed to be highly efficient, reliable, and scalable, making them ideal for IoT applications.

#### Embedded Computing Basics

Embedded computing refers to the use of computer technology in non-computer devices such as sensors, appliances, and vehicles. Embedded computing systems are typically designed to perform specific tasks and operate in real-time environments with limited resources.

The key components of an embedded computing system include:

- Microprocessor or microcontroller
- Memory
- Input/output interfaces
- Power supply
- Peripheral devices

#### Overview of IoT Supported Hardware Platforms

There are several hardware platforms that are commonly used in IoT applications. These platforms provide a range of features and capabilities that make them suitable for different types of IoT devices.

Some of the popular IoT supported hardware platforms include:

- Arduino: An open-source electronics platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software.
- Netduino: An open-source electronics platform based on the .NET Micro Framework.
- Raspberry Pi: A low-cost, credit-card-sized computer that can be used for a variety of purposes, including IoT applications.
- BeagleBone: A low-cost, high-performance platform for embedded applications.
- Intel Galileo: A microcontroller board based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000.
- ARM Cortex: A family of microcontroller and microprocessor cores that are widely used in embedded systems.

Each of these platforms has its own strengths and weaknesses, and the choice of platform depends on the specific requirements of the IoT application.

In summary, embedded platforms play a critical role in the development of IoT applications. They provide the necessary computing power, memory, and input/output interfaces to enable devices to communicate with each other and with the cloud. By selecting the right hardware platform, IoT developers can create robust, scalable, and efficient systems that can meet the demands of a wide range of applications.
