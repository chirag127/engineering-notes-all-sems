### Wireless Sensor Networks

- A Wireless Sensor Network (WSN) is a network of interconnected sensors that communicate with each other wirelessly.
- The sensors in a WSN are equipped with transceivers, processors, and energy sources.
- The transceivers allow the sensors to communicate with each other, while the processors enable them to process the data they collect.
- The energy sources can be batteries or energy harvesting devices that convert ambient energy sources such as light, heat, or motion into electrical energy to power the sensors.
- WSNs are used in various applications such as environmental monitoring, healthcare, agriculture, and home automation.
- One of the main advantages of WSNs is their ability to operate in harsh and remote environments, where traditional wired networks are either not feasible or too expensive to deploy.
- WSNs can also be easily deployed and reconfigured, making them ideal for dynamic and ad-hoc networking scenarios.
- However, WSNs also face several challenges such as limited battery life, low data rates, and security concerns.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT

- An embedded platform is a computer system that is designed to perform a specific function and is integrated into a larger system.
- Embedded platforms are widely used in IoT applications, where they serve as the interface between the physical world and the digital world.
- The most commonly used embedded platforms for IoT include Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry Pi, BeagleBone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex.
- Arduino is an open-source electronics platform based on a simple microcontroller board, which can be programmed using a simplified programming language.
- NetArduino is a variant of Arduino that supports Internet connectivity through Ethernet or Wi-Fi.
- Raspberry Pi is a credit-card-sized computer that runs Linux and can be used for various IoT applications such as media center, home automation, and robotics.
- BeagleBone is a low-cost, high-performance single-board computer that runs Linux and is designed for embedded applications.
- Intel Galileo boards are based on Intel architecture and support various operating systems such as Linux and Windows.
- ARM Cortex is a family of microcontroller and microprocessor cores that are widely used in embedded systems and IoT applications.
- Embedded platforms for IoT provide various features such as low-power consumption, high processing power, and connectivity options that make them suitable for various IoT applications.
