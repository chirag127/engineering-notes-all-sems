### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) is a wireless technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It allows for the automatic identification and tracking of items, making it a valuable technology for the Internet of Things (IoT). Here are some key points about RFID technology:

- RFID technology uses tags or labels that are attached to objects. These tags contain an antenna and a microchip that stores information about the object.
- RFID readers send out radio waves that activate the tags and read the information stored in them.
- RFID technology can be used for a variety of applications, including inventory management, supply chain management, and asset tracking.
- RFID technology is divided into two categories: passive and active. Passive RFID tags do not have a power source and rely on the energy from the reader to power them. Active RFID tags have a power source and can transmit information over longer distances.
- RFID technology has several benefits, including improved efficiency, accuracy, and visibility. It can help reduce costs and improve productivity in various industries.

Overall, RFID technology is an important component of the hardware for IoT. It allows for the automatic identification and tracking of objects, making it a valuable tool for businesses and organizations.
