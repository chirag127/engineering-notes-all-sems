### Participatory Sensing Technology

Participatory sensing technology is a type of technology that involves individuals in the process of collecting and sharing data using their personal devices. It allows for the collection of large amounts of data from multiple sources, which can then be used for various purposes such as scientific research, environmental monitoring, and urban planning.

Here are some key points to keep in mind about participatory sensing technology:

- It involves the use of personal devices such as smartphones, tablets, and wearables to collect and share data.
- The data collected can be used for various purposes such as environmental monitoring, traffic management, and healthcare.
- Participatory sensing technology can be used to collect a wide range of data including location, sound, and air quality.
- It relies on the participation of individuals who are willing to share their data.
- Privacy and security are important considerations when it comes to participatory sensing technology.
- Data collected through participatory sensing technology can be used to inform decision-making and policy development.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms are an essential component of the Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem. They provide the foundation for the development of IoT applications and enable the connectivity of devices and sensors. Here are some key points to keep in mind about embedded platforms for IoT:

- Embedded computing basics refer to the use of computer systems that are integrated into other devices such as appliances, vehicles, and medical equipment.
- IoT supported hardware platforms such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM cortex are commonly used for IoT applications.
- These platforms provide a range of features including connectivity, processing power, and storage capacity.
- They are designed to be low-cost and energy-efficient, making them suitable for use in a wide range of applications.
- Embedded platforms are essential for the development of IoT applications, as they enable devices and sensors to communicate with each other and with the cloud.

In conclusion, participatory sensing technology and embedded platforms are key components of the IoT ecosystem. They enable the collection and sharing of data, as well as the development of IoT applications. It is important to understand the capabilities and limitations of these technologies in order to make informed decisions about their use.
