## Unit 3 - Network & Communication aspects in IoT: 

In this unit, we will explore various aspects of network and communication in the Internet of Things (IoT) domain. The following topics will be covered:

### Wireless Medium access issues
- The wireless medium in IoT is a shared resource, and multiple devices may compete for access to it.
- Due to the limited bandwidth and interference from other devices, accessing the medium becomes a challenging task.
- Various techniques, such as frequency hopping and spread spectrum, can be used to improve medium access.

### MAC protocol survey
- The Medium Access Control (MAC) protocol plays a crucial role in IoT networks.
- It governs the access to the wireless medium and ensures that devices can communicate without interfering with each other.
- Various MAC protocols, such as Carrier Sense Multiple Access (CSMA) and Time Division Multiple Access (TDMA), can be used in IoT networks.
- Each protocol has its advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of protocol depends on the application and network requirements.

### Survey routing protocols
- Routing is a critical aspect of IoT networks, as devices may need to communicate with each other over multiple hops.
- Various routing protocols, such as Ad-hoc On-demand Distance Vector (AODV) and Dynamic Source Routing (DSR), can be used in IoT networks.
- Each protocol has its advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of protocol depends on the application and network requirements.

### Sensor deployment & Node discovery
- Sensors are an integral part of IoT networks, and their deployment plays a crucial role in the network performance.
- Various factors, such as sensor placement, coverage, and power consumption, should be considered while deploying sensors.
- Node discovery is the process of identifying new devices in the network, and it is essential for the network's scalability.

### Data aggregation & dissemination
- In IoT networks, multiple sensors may generate data that needs to be aggregated and processed.
- Various techniques, such as data fusion and event detection, can be used to process the data.
- Disseminating the processed data to the appropriate devices is crucial for the network's performance and application requirements.

In conclusion, this unit covers various aspects of network and communication in IoT networks, such as medium access, MAC protocols, routing protocols, sensor deployment, and data aggregation. Understanding these topics is essential for designing efficient and scalable IoT networks.
