### Design Principles for Connected Devices

As the Internet of Things (IoT) continues to evolve, it is important to consider the design principles for connected devices. These principles are critical in ensuring that connected devices are effective, efficient, and safe to use. Here are some key design principles for connected devices:

1. IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization: The IoT/M2M systems consist of various layers that work together to provide a seamless user experience. It is important to standardize the design of these layers to ensure that devices can communicate effectively with each other.

2. Communication technologies: Connected devices need to be able to communicate with each other and with other devices on the network. It is important to choose the right communication technologies that are reliable and secure.

3. Data enrichment and consolidation: Connected devices generate a vast amount of data. It is important to enrich and consolidate this data to gain insights and make informed decisions.

4. Ease of designing: Designing connected devices should be easy and straightforward. This will encourage more developers to create new devices and expand the IoT ecosystem.

5. Affordability: Connected devices should be affordable for everyone. This will ensure that more people can benefit from the IoT and connected devices.

By following these design principles, connected devices can become more efficient, effective, and safe to use.
