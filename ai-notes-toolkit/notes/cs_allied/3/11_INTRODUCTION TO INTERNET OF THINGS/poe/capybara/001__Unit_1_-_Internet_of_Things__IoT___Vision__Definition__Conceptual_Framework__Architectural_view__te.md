## Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

### Vision
- IoT is a network of physical objects that can communicate and interact with each other through the Internet.
- The vision of IoT is to create a connected world where devices can work together seamlessly to improve efficiency and enhance our lives.

### Definition
- The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to the interconnectivity of devices, sensors, and objects that can collect and exchange data over the internet.
- The IoT allows for the automation of tasks and the creation of new applications that can improve our daily lives.

### Conceptual Framework
- The IoT is based on the idea of connecting physical objects to the internet in order to collect and share data.
- This framework allows for the creation of smart devices that can be controlled remotely and can interact with other devices.

### Architectural View
- The architecture of the IoT is based on a network of devices that can communicate with each other and with the cloud.
- This architecture includes sensors, actuators, and gateways that connect devices to the internet.

### Technology Behind IoT
- The technology behind IoT includes wireless communication technologies such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular networks.
- Other technologies include cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and big data analytics.

### Sources of the IoT
- The IoT is made up of a variety of devices, including smartphones, wearables, smart home devices, and industrial sensors.
- These devices collect and share data in order to improve efficiency and enhance our lives.

### M2M Communication
- Machine-to-Machine (M2M) communication is a key component of the IoT.
- M2M communication allows devices to communicate with each other without human intervention, enabling automation and increased efficiency.

### IoT Examples
- Examples of IoT devices include smart thermostats, home security systems, and fitness trackers.
- Industrial examples include sensors that monitor manufacturing processes and supply chain management systems.

### Design Principles for Connected Devices
- Design principles for connected devices include IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing, and affordability.
- These principles ensure that IoT devices are reliable, secure, and easy to use.
