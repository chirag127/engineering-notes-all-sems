### Architectural View for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT)

#### Vision, Definition, and Conceptual Framework
- IoT refers to the interconnected network of physical devices that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies that enable them to collect and exchange data.
- The vision of IoT is to create a world where devices can communicate with each other seamlessly and allow for more efficient and effective communication and decision making.
- The conceptual framework of IoT involves the collection, processing, and analysis of data from various sources to enable intelligent decision making.

#### Architectural View
- The architectural view of IoT involves the different layers of the IoT system, including the device layer, connectivity layer, middleware layer, application layer, and business layer.
- The device layer consists of the physical devices that are embedded with sensors and other technologies that enable them to collect and transmit data.
- The connectivity layer involves the various communication technologies that enable the devices to transmit data to other devices or to the cloud.
- The middleware layer provides the necessary software and services to enable the devices to communicate with each other and with other systems.
- The application layer involves the various applications and services that are built on top of the middleware layer to enable intelligent decision making.
- The business layer involves the various business processes and systems that enable organizations to leverage the data collected by the IoT system to improve their operations and decision making.

#### Technology behind IoT
- The technology behind IoT involves various hardware and software components, including sensors, actuators, microcontrollers, communication modules, and cloud-based platforms.
- Sensors are used to collect data from various sources, such as temperature, humidity, and light, while actuators are used to control the physical environment.
- Microcontrollers provide the necessary computing power to process the data and make decisions based on the data.
- Communication modules enable the devices to transmit data to other devices or to the cloud.
- Cloud-based platforms provide the necessary infrastructure and services to enable the devices to communicate with each other and with other systems.

#### Sources of the IoT
- The sources of the IoT include various domains, such as industrial IoT, consumer IoT, and healthcare IoT.
- Industrial IoT involves the application of IoT technologies in industrial settings to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
- Consumer IoT involves the application of IoT technologies in consumer products, such as smart home devices and wearables.
- Healthcare IoT involves the application of IoT technologies in healthcare settings to improve patient outcomes and reduce costs.

#### M2M Communication
- M2M communication involves the direct communication between machines or devices without human intervention.
- M2M communication enables the devices to exchange data and make decisions based on the data, without the need for human input.

#### IoT Examples
- Examples of IoT applications include smart homes, smart cities, smart grids, and industrial automation.
- Smart homes involve the use of IoT technologies to automate various aspects of the home, such as lighting, heating, and security.
- Smart cities involve the use of IoT technologies to improve the efficiency and sustainability of urban environments.
- Smart grids involve the use of IoT technologies to optimize the distribution and consumption of energy.
- Industrial automation involves the use of IoT technologies to improve the efficiency and safety of industrial processes.

#### Design Principles for Connected Devices
- The design principles for connected devices involve the various layers of the IoT/M2M system, including design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing, and affordability.
- Design standardization involves the use of common design principles and standards to enable interoperability between devices and systems.
- Communication technologies involve the various communication protocols and standards that enable the devices to communicate with each other and with other systems.
- Data enrichment and consolidation involve the processing and analysis of data to enable intelligent decision making.
- Ease of designing involves the use of user-friendly design tools and platforms to enable the development of IoT applications.
- Affordability involves the use of cost-effective components and technologies to enable the widespread adoption of IoT/M2M systems.
