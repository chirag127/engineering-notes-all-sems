### IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization

In the world of Internet of Things (IoT), there are several layers and standards that need to be followed in order to ensure seamless communication and integration between connected devices. Here are some important points to keep in mind regarding IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization:

- There are typically four layers in an IoT system: the perception layer, the network layer, the data processing layer, and the application layer.
- The perception layer consists of sensors and other devices that gather data from the physical world.
- The network layer is responsible for transmitting data between devices and handling issues such as security and reliability.
- The data processing layer is where data is analyzed and processed, often using artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms.
- The application layer is where users interact with the system and make use of the insights and data gathered by the IoT devices.
- Standardization is important in IoT design in order to ensure that devices from different manufacturers can communicate with each other seamlessly.
- Communication technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks are often used in IoT systems.
- Data enrichment and consolidation is also important in IoT design, as it allows for more accurate and useful analysis of the data gathered by the devices.
- Ease of designing and affordability are important considerations in IoT design, as they can help make IoT technology more accessible to a wider range of users and industries.

By following these design principles and standards, IoT systems can be created that are reliable, secure, and effective in gathering and analyzing data from the physical world.
