
### Actuators for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

* Actuators are devices that can cause physical movement or changes in the environment in response to a signal or command.
* Digital sensors are devices that can detect or measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, light, and sound.
* Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology is used to identify and track objects using radio waves.
* Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) are networks of sensors that communicate wirelessly with each other and with other devices.
* Participatory Sensing Technology allows users to collect, share, and use data from their environment.

### Embedded Platforms for IoT

* Embedded computing basics includes understanding of embedded systems and their components.
* Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards and ARM cortex.
* Arduino is an open source microcontroller platform that is used to build interactive electronic projects.
* NetArduino is a .NET-based microcontroller platform that enables users to build Internet-connected projects.
* Raspberry Pi is a single-board computer used for a variety of applications including programming, robotics, and home automation.
* Beagle Bone is a low cost, open source single-board computer based on the ARM Cortex-A8 processor.
* Intel Galileo boards are based on the Intel Quark SoC X1000 processor and are used to develop interactive projects.
* ARM Cortex is a family of 32-bit and 64-bit microprocessors designed by ARM Holdings.
