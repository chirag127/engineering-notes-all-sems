
### Architectural view for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): 

* Vision: A vision of the Internet of Things is a world where physical objects are seamlessly integrated into the information network, and where the physical objects can become active participants in business processes.

* Definition: The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects embedded with electronics, software, sensors, and network connectivity, which enables these objects to collect and exchange data.

* Conceptual Framework: The conceptual framework for the Internet of Things includes four layers: sensing, communication, processing, and application.

* Architectural view: The architectural view of the Internet of Things is based on a distributed architecture, where the objects are connected to the Internet and can communicate with each other, as well as with other entities.

* Technology behind IoT: The technology behind the Internet of Things is based on a variety of technologies, including RFID, sensors, wireless networks, cloud computing, and Big Data.

* Sources of the IoT: The sources of the Internet of Things include the Internet, the physical world, and the user.

* M2M Communication: Machine-to-machine (M2M) communication is the communication between two machines, where the machines can exchange data and act on the data received.

* IoT Examples: Examples of the Internet of Things include connected cars, connected homes, smart cities, and connected health.

* Design Principles for Connected Devices: Design principles for connected devices include IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability.
