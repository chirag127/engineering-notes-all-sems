
### Digital Sensors for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

* Sensors: Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical and environmental conditions such as temperature, pressure, humidity, motion, light, sound, and other phenomena. They convert these measurements into electrical signals that can be read and interpreted by computers.

* Digital Sensors: Digital sensors are sensors that measure and convert physical properties into digital data. They are typically used to measure temperature, pressure, humidity, motion, light, sound, and other phenomena.

* Actuators: Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into mechanical motion. They are used to control the motion of robots, motors, valves, and other mechanical devices.

* Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology: RFID technology is a wireless communication system that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. It is commonly used for inventory control, access control, and asset tracking.

* Wireless Sensor Networks: Wireless sensor networks are networks of small, battery-powered sensors that are connected wirelessly and communicate with each other. They are used for monitoring and controlling physical processes.

* Participatory Sensing Technology: Participatory sensing technology is a type of sensing technology that relies on humans to collect and share data. It is used for monitoring and controlling physical processes.

* Embedded Computing Basics: Embedded computing is the use of computers and microprocessors in devices and machines to perform specific tasks. It is used to control and monitor physical processes.

* Overview of IOT Supported Hardware Platforms: There are several hardware platforms that support IoT applications, such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry Pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM cortex. Each platform has its own unique features and capabilities.
