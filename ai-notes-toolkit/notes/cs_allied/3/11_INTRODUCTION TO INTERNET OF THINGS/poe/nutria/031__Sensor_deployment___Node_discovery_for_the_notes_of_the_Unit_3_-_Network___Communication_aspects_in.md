
### Sensor Deployment & Node Discovery

Sensor deployment and node discovery are two important aspects of network and communication in the Internet of Things (IoT).

* **Sensor Deployment**: Sensor deployment is the process of placing sensors in an environment in order to collect data. It involves selecting the right type of sensor and positioning it in the right place in order to collect the most accurate data.

* **Node Discovery**: Node discovery is the process of discovering nodes in a network. It includes finding out what type of nodes are in the network, their location, and their capabilities. This information is used to create an efficient and secure network.

* **Wireless Medium Access Issues**: Wireless medium access issues refer to the problems that arise when multiple devices attempt to access the same wireless medium at the same time. These issues include interference, contention, and collisions.

* **MAC Protocol Survey**: MAC (Media Access Control) protocols are used to control access to a shared medium in a network. A MAC protocol survey involves examining different MAC protocols and their advantages and disadvantages.

* **Survey Routing Protocols**: Routing protocols are used to determine the best route for data to travel through a network. A survey routing protocol survey involves examining different routing protocols and their advantages and disadvantages.

* **Data Aggregation & Dissemination**: Data aggregation and dissemination are two important aspects of data management in the Internet of Things. Data aggregation involves collecting and analyzing data from multiple sources in order to gain insights. Data dissemination involves sharing the collected data with other devices and systems.
