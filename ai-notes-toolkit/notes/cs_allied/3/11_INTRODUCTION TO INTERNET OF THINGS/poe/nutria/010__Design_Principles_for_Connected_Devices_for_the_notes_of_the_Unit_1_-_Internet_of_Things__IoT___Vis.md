
### Design Principles for Connected Devices 

1. **Internet of Things (IoT):** Vision, Definition, Conceptual Framework, Architectural view, technology behind IoT, Sources of the IoT, M2M Communication, IoT Examples. 
2. **Design Principles for Connected Devices:** IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability. 
3. **Introduction to Internet of Things:** Understanding the fundamentals of IoT and its applications. 
4. **Design Standardization:** Ensuring that connected devices are compatible and can communicate with each other. 
5. **Communication Technologies:** Utilizing various communication protocols and technologies for data transmission and reception. 
6. **Data Enrichment and Consolidation:** Collecting and integrating data from multiple sources to create a comprehensive view. 
7. **Ease of Designing:** Designing connected devices that are simple to use and set up. 
8. **Affordability:** Making sure that connected devices are cost-effective and accessible to all.
