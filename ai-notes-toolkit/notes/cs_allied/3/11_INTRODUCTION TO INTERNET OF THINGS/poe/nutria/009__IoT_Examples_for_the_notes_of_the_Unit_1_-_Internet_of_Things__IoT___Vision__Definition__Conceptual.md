
### IoT Examples for the notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): 

1. Vision: A vision of the Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects that are connected to the internet and can exchange data with each other.

2. Definition: The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical objects (devices, vehicles, buildings, etc.) that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet.

3. Conceptual Framework: The Internet of Things (IoT) is a concept that describes the connection of physical objects to the internet, allowing them to send and receive data. The objects are connected using sensors, software, and other technologies.

4. Architectural view: The Internet of Things (IoT) architecture consists of three main components: sensing, communication, and computing. Sensors are used to collect data from the physical environment, communication protocols are used to transmit the data, and computing devices are used to process the data.

5. Technology behind IoT: The technology behind IoT includes a wide range of technologies such as sensors, wireless communication protocols, cloud computing, big data, and artificial intelligence.

6. Sources of the IoT: The sources of the IoT include a wide range of devices such as smartphones, wearables, medical devices, industrial equipment, and connected vehicles.

7. M2M Communication: Machine-to-machine (M2M) communication is a type of communication that occurs between two or more machines without the need for human intervention.

8. IoT Examples: Examples of IoT applications include connected cars, smart homes, connected healthcare, and industrial automation.

9. Design Principles for Connected Devices: Design principles for connected devices include standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing, and affordability.
