
### Participatory Sensing Technology

Participatory sensing technology is a type of technology used in Internet of Things (IoT) applications. It is used to collect data from various sources such as sensors, digital sensors, actuators, radio frequency identification (RFID) technology, and wireless sensor networks. 

#### Hardware for IoT

The hardware used in IoT applications includes sensors, digital sensors, actuators, RFID technology, and wireless sensor networks. Sensors are used to detect changes in the environment, such as temperature, pressure, and motion. Digital sensors are used to measure digital signals, such as voltage, current, and frequency. Actuators are used to control physical elements, such as motors, valves, and pumps. RFID technology is used to identify and track objects. Wireless sensor networks are used to collect data from multiple sources and transmit it to a central computer.

#### Embedded Platforms for IoT

Embedded platforms for IoT include embedded computing basics, overviews of IoT supported hardware platforms such as Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry Pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM cortex. These platforms are used to create custom applications and control systems. They provide a range of features such as low power consumption, high performance, and scalability.
