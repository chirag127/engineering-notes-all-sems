

### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms 

The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of physical devices, vehicles, home appliances, and other items embedded with electronics, software, sensors, actuators, and network connectivity which enable these objects to connect and exchange data. This technology enables the collection and exchange of data from a variety of devices, allowing for the automation of various tasks.

In this unit, we will be discussing the various hardware platforms that are used to support IoT. These include Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards and ARM cortex. 

#### Sensors

Sensors are devices that detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, or light. They are used to detect changes in the environment or in a system, and can be used to trigger an action or response. Sensors can be used to detect changes in temperature, pressure, humidity, motion, sound, and more.

#### Digital Sensors

Digital sensors are sensors that output a digital signal. These sensors are used to detect and measure physical phenomena, such as temperature, pressure, or light. Digital sensors are usually connected to a microcontroller or other computing device, and the data is processed and stored for further analysis.

#### Actuators

Actuators are devices that convert energy into motion. They are used to move or control mechanical systems, such as motors, valves, and pumps. Actuators can be used to control the speed, direction, and position of a system.

#### Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) Technology

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) is a technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects. RFID tags are small, inexpensive tags that can be attached to objects, such as products or packages. The tags are read by an RFID reader, which can be used to track the objects in a system.

#### Wireless Sensor Networks

Wireless Sensor Networks (WSNs) are networks of wireless sensors that are used to collect and transmit data. WSNs are used to monitor physical or environmental conditions, such as temperature, pressure, or humidity.

#### Participatory Sensing Technology

Participatory Sensing Technology (PST) is a technology that allows users to collect data from their environment using their mobile devices. PST enables users to collect data about their environment, such as air quality, noise levels, and traffic conditions.

#### Embedded Computing Basics

Embedded computing is the use of computer systems to control physical devices. Embedded computing systems are used to control a variety of devices, such as robots, medical equipment, and consumer electronics.

#### Overview of IOT supported Hardware platforms 

The various hardware platforms that are used to support IoT include Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards and ARM cortex. 

- Arduino is an open-source hardware platform based on easy-to-use hardware and software. It is used to create interactive electronic projects.

- NetArduino is an open-source hardware platform based on the Arduino platform. It is used to create network-connected projects.

- Raspberry pi is a low-cost, single-board computer developed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation. It is used to create projects that require more computing power than the Arduino platform can provide.

- Beagle Bone is a low-cost, single-board computer developed by the BeagleBoard.org Foundation. It is used to create projects that require more computing power than the Raspberry Pi platform can provide.

- Intel Galileo boards are single-board computers developed by Intel. They are used to create projects that require more computing power than the Beagle Bone platform can provide.

- ARM Cortex is a family of low-power, 32-bit microcontrollers developed by ARM Holdings. They are used to create projects that require more computing power than the Intel Galileo boards can provide.
