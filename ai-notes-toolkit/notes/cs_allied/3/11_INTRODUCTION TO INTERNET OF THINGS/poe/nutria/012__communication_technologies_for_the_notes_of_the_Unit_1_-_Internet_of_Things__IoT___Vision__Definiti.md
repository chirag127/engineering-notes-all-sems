
### Communication Technologies for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Internet of Things (IoT): 

* Vision: The vision of IoT is to create a network of physical objects that can communicate with each other, share data, and be controlled remotely.
* Definition: IoT is a network of physical objects, such as sensors, actuators, and other devices, connected to the internet, allowing them to send and receive data.
* Conceptual Framework: IoT is based on the concept of connecting physical objects to the internet so that they can be monitored and controlled remotely.
* Architectural View: IoT can be divided into three main components: sensors, actuators, and communication infrastructure.
* Technology Behind IoT: IoT is powered by a variety of technologies, including wireless communication, cloud computing, and machine learning.
* Sources of the IoT: IoT data can be collected from a variety of sources, such as mobile phones, wearables, and connected devices.
* M2M Communication: M2M communication is a type of communication between machines, such as sensors and actuators, that enables them to exchange data.
* IoT Examples: Examples of IoT applications include smart homes, connected cars, and wearable devices.
* Design Principles for Connected Devices: Design principles for connected devices include IoT/M2M systems layers and design standardization, communication technologies, data enrichment and consolidation, ease of designing and affordability.
