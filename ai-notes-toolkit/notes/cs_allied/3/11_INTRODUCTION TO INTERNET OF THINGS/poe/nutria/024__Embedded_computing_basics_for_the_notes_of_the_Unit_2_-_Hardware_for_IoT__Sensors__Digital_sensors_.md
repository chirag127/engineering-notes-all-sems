
### Embedded Computing Basics for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hardware for IoT

* Embedded computing is the use of computers and computer hardware components to create systems that can perform specific tasks.
* Sensors are devices that measure physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, humidity, or acceleration and convert them into electrical signals. Digital sensors are sensors that measure digital signals.
* Actuators are devices that convert electrical signals into physical motion or mechanical work.
* Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) technology is a wireless communication technology that uses radio waves to identify and track objects.
* Wireless sensor networks are networks of wireless sensors that can be used to monitor and control physical processes.
* Participatory sensing technology is a type of sensing technology that uses crowdsourcing to collect data from users.
* Embedded platforms for IoT are platforms that provide the necessary hardware and software for creating and deploying IoT applications. Examples of these platforms include Arduino, NetArduino, Raspberry Pi, Beagle Bone, Intel Galileo boards, and ARM Cortex.
