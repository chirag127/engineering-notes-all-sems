### Statistical Moments for the notes of the Unit 4 - Feature Extraction in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

Statistical Moments are crucial features in Image Analytics. They are numerical values that provide information about the distribution of pixel values in an image. Here are some key points to help you understand statistical moments better:

1. **Mean**: The mean is the average of all pixel values in an image. It provides information about the overall brightness of the image. A higher mean value indicates a brighter image, while a lower mean value indicates a darker image.

2. **Variance**: The variance measures the spread of pixel values around the mean. It provides information about the contrast of the image. A higher variance indicates a greater contrast, while a lower variance indicates a lower contrast.

3. **Skewness**: Skewness measures the asymmetry of the distribution of pixel values. It provides information about the shape of the image. A positive skewness value indicates a distribution that is skewed to the right, while a negative skewness value indicates a distribution that is skewed to the left.

4. **Kurtosis**: Kurtosis measures the peakedness of the distribution of pixel values. It provides information about the sharpness of the image. A higher kurtosis value indicates a sharper image, while a lower kurtosis value indicates a smoother image.

5. **Central Moments**: Central moments measure the distribution of pixel values around the central point. They are used to calculate other statistical moments such as skewness and kurtosis.

6. **Normalized Moments**: Normalized moments are a scaled version of central moments. They are used to make statistical moments invariant to changes in scale, rotation, and translation.

In conclusion, statistical moments are fundamental features in Image Analytics. They provide valuable information about the distribution of pixel values in an image, which can be used for various image processing tasks such as segmentation, object recognition, and image enhancement. Understanding the different statistical moments and their properties is crucial for effective feature extraction and analysis in Image Analytics.
