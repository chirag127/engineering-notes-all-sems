# IMAGE ANALYTICS

Image analytics is the process of analyzing images to extract meaningful insights and information. It is a technique that uses various algorithms and tools to extract, analyze, and interpret data from images. Here are some key points to understand about image analytics:

- Image analytics is used in various fields, such as healthcare, retail, and manufacturing, to analyze and interpret images related to their respective fields.
- It is used to extract information from images, such as facial recognition, object detection, and image segmentation.
- Image analytics is used in the healthcare industry to analyze medical images, such as X-rays and CT scans, to diagnose diseases and injuries.
- It is used in the retail industry to analyze customer behavior by analyzing their shopping patterns and preferences through CCTV cameras and other imaging devices.
- Image analytics is used in the manufacturing industry to detect defects in products by analyzing images of the products during the manufacturing process.
- It is also used in the security industry to detect suspicious behavior through CCTV cameras and other imaging devices.
- Image analytics uses various algorithms, such as deep learning and computer vision, to analyze and interpret images.
- It is a rapidly growing field due to the increasing use of imaging devices and the need for automated analysis and interpretation of images.
- The accuracy of image analytics depends on the quality of the images being analyzed, the algorithms used, and the expertise of the analyst.

In conclusion, image analytics is a powerful technique that is used in various fields to extract meaningful insights and information from images. It is a rapidly growing field that is expected to have a significant impact on various industries in the coming years.
