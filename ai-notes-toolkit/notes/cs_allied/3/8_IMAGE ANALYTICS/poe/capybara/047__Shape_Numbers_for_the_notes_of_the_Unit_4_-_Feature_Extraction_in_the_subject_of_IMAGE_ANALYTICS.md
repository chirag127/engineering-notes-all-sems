### Shape Numbers

In image analytics, shape numbers are used to describe the shape of an object in an image. These numbers are calculated based on the object's boundary and are used as features in various image recognition and classification tasks. Here are some important shape numbers to know:

1. **Area:** The area of an object is the number of pixels it covers in an image. It is a simple and commonly used shape number.

2. **Perimeter:** The perimeter of an object is the length of its boundary. It is calculated by adding the lengths of all the edges that make up the boundary.

3. **Compactness:** Compactness is a measure of how compact an object's shape is. It is calculated as the ratio of the object's area to the square of its perimeter. Objects with a high compactness value have a more regular shape.

4. **Circularity:** Circularity is a measure of how close an object's shape is to a perfect circle. It is calculated as the ratio of the object's perimeter to the perimeter of a circle with the same area. Objects with a circularity value close to 1 are more circular in shape.

5. **Elongation:** Elongation is a measure of how elongated an object's shape is. It is calculated as the ratio of the maximum distance between two points on the object's boundary to the minimum distance between two points on the boundary. Objects with a high elongation value are more elongated in shape.

6. **Orientation:** Orientation is a measure of the angle at which an object is oriented with respect to a reference axis. It is usually calculated as the angle between the major axis of the object and the x-axis.

7. **Convexity:** Convexity is a measure of how convex an object's shape is. It is calculated by dividing the area of the object by the area of its convex hull. Objects with a high convexity value have a more convex shape.

Knowing these shape numbers is important for feature extraction in image analytics. They can be used as features in various machine learning algorithms for image recognition and classification tasks.
