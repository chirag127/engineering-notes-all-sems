One of the key operations in Morphological Image Processing is Opening and Closing. ### Opening and Closing

1. Opening is a Morphological operation that is used to remove small objects from an image while preserving the larger objects. It is achieved by erosion followed by dilation.

2. Closing is a Morphological operation that is used to fill small holes and gaps in an image while preserving the larger objects. It is achieved by dilation followed by erosion.

3. Opening and Closing are dual operations, which means that Closing an image is equivalent to Opening the complement of the image and vice versa.

4. Opening and Closing operations are useful in various applications of Image Analytics such as noise reduction, object detection, and segmentation.

5. Opening can be used to remove small noise or unwanted features from an image, making it easier to detect and segment the larger objects.

6. Closing can be used to fill small gaps or holes in an image, making it easier to detect and segment the larger objects.

7. Opening and Closing can be combined to create more complex operations such as Top Hat and Bottom Hat transformations, which are used for image enhancement and feature extraction.
