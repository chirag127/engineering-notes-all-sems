### Background for the notes of the Unit 5 - Image Pattern Classification in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

In this unit, we will be discussing image pattern classification, which is an important aspect of image analytics. Before we dive into the details of image pattern classification, let us first understand some background concepts.

1. **Image Analytics:** Image analytics is the process of analyzing and interpreting images to derive useful insights. It involves the use of various techniques such as image processing, computer vision, and machine learning to extract information from images.

2. **Image Processing:** Image processing is the method of enhancing the quality of an image or extracting useful information from it. It involves techniques such as image filtering, segmentation, and feature extraction.

3. **Computer Vision:** Computer vision is a field of study that focuses on enabling computers to interpret and understand the visual world. It involves various techniques such as object recognition, image segmentation, and motion analysis.

4. **Machine Learning:** Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that involves the use of algorithms to learn from data and make predictions or decisions. It involves various techniques such as supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning.

5. **Image Pattern Classification:** Image pattern classification is the process of categorizing images into different classes based on their features. It involves the use of machine learning algorithms such as support vector machines, decision trees, and neural networks.

By understanding these background concepts, we can better understand the process of image pattern classification and its application in various industries such as healthcare, automotive, and security.
