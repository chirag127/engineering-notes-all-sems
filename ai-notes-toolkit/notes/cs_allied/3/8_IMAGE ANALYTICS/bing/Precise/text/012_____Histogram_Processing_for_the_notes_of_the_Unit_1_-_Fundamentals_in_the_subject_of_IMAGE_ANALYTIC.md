### Histogram Processing

Histogram processing is a technique used in image processing to enhance the contrast of an image. It involves the manipulation of the image's histogram, which is a graphical representation of the distribution of pixel intensities in the image.

1. **Histogram Equalization**: This technique involves redistributing the pixel intensities in the image so that they are spread out more evenly. This can enhance the contrast of the image and make it easier to see details that were previously hidden.

2. **Histogram Stretching**: This technique involves stretching the range of pixel intensities in the image. This can be useful when the image has a narrow range of pixel intensities, as it can help to bring out details in the image.

3. **Histogram Matching**: This technique involves matching the histogram of one image to the histogram of another image. This can be useful when trying to compare two images or when trying to make one image look like another.

These are some of the basic techniques used in histogram processing. They can be used to enhance the contrast of an image and make it easier to see details that were previously hidden. These techniques are commonly used in image analytics and can be useful when studying for exams on the subject.
