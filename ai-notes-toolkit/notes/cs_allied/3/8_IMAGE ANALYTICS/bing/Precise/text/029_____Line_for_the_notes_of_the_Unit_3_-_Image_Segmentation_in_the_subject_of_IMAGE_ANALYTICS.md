### Unit 3 - Image Segmentation in Image Analytics

Image segmentation is the process of dividing an image into multiple segments or regions, each of which corresponds to a different object or part of the image. The goal of image segmentation is to simplify and/or change the representation of an image into something that is more meaningful and easier to analyze.

Some key points to remember about image segmentation are:

1. Image segmentation is used to locate objects and boundaries (lines, curves, etc.) in images.
2. It is typically used to identify objects and their boundaries within an image.
3. There are several different techniques that can be used for image segmentation, including thresholding, clustering, and edge detection.
4. The choice of segmentation technique will depend on the specific requirements of the application.
5. Image segmentation is an important step in many image analysis and computer vision tasks, such as object recognition and tracking.
