### Some Basic Descriptors for the notes of the Unit 4 - Feature Extraction in the subject of IMAGE ANALYTICS

1. **Feature extraction** is the process of extracting relevant and informative features from an image to represent its visual content.
2. **Descriptors** are algorithms that compute a representation of an image or a region of an image, based on certain characteristics or features.
3. Some common image descriptors include:
    - **Color histograms**: represent the distribution of colors in an image.
    - **Texture descriptors**: capture the texture or patterns present in an image.
    - **Shape descriptors**: represent the shape of objects in an image.
    - **Local feature descriptors**: describe local regions of an image, such as corners or edges.
4. Descriptors are used in various image analysis tasks, such as image classification, object recognition, and image retrieval.
5. The choice of descriptor depends on the specific task and the characteristics of the images being analyzed. For example, color histograms may be more useful for classifying images based on their overall color, while local feature descriptors may be more useful for recognizing specific objects within an image.
