## Unit 1 - Fundamentals

This unit covers the basic concepts and principles of computer science, such as:

- What is a computer and how does it work?
- What are the main components of a computer system and what are their functions?
- What are the different types of software and how are they developed?
- What are the basic concepts of programming and how can they be applied to solve problems?
- What are the common data structures and algorithms and how can they be used to store and manipulate information?
- What are the ethical, social and legal issues related to computing and how can they be addressed?

The main objectives of this unit are to:

- Understand the structure and operation of a computer system
- Identify and describe the different types of software and their development processes
- Explain and apply the basic concepts of programming, such as variables, data types, operators, expressions, statements, control structures, functions and parameters
- Use appropriate data structures and algorithms to store and manipulate information, such as arrays, lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, sorting and searching
- Analyze and evaluate the ethical, social and legal implications of computing and its applications

The main topics of this unit are:

- Computer systems: hardware, software, input, output, processing, storage, communication, networks, operating systems, user interfaces, system software, application software, software development life cycle, software engineering, testing, debugging, documentation
- Programming: syntax, semantics, logic, pseudocode, flowcharts, programming languages, compilers, interpreters, IDEs, variables, data types, operators, expressions, statements, assignment, arithmetic, relational, logical, bitwise, conditional, loops, functions, parameters, arguments, return values, scope, recursion, modules, libraries, APIs
- Data structures and algorithms: abstract data types, arrays, lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, hashing, sorting, searching, complexity, efficiency, performance, trade-offs, Big O notation
- Ethics, social and legal issues: privacy, security, cybercrime, hacking, encryption, digital citizenship, intellectual property, plagiarism, copyright, patents, licenses, open source, free software, social media, online communication, digital divide, accessibility, inclusivity, diversity
