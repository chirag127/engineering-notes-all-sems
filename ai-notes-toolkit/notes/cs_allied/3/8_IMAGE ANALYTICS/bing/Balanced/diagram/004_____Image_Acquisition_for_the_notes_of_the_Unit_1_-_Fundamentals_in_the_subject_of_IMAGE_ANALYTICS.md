### Image Acquisition

- Image acquisition is the first step in image processing. It involves retrieving the image from a source, usually a hardware-based source.
- Image acquisition can be done by various devices, such as cameras, scanners, microscopes, sensors, etc. The devices convert the optical signals into electrical signals, which can be stored and processed by a computer.
- Image acquisition can be affected by various factors, such as illumination, noise, resolution, distortion, etc. These factors can degrade the quality of the image and affect the subsequent processing steps.
- Image acquisition can be done in different modes, such as analog or digital, grayscale or color, still or video, etc. The mode of image acquisition depends on the application and the requirements of the image processing task.
- Image acquisition can be done for different purposes, such as analysis, enhancement, compression, recognition, segmentation, etc. The purpose of image acquisition determines the choice of the device, the mode, and the parameters of the image acquisition process.
