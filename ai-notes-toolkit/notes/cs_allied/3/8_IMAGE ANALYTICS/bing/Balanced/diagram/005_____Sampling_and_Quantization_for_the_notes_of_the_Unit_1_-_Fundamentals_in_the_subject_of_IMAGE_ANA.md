### Sampling and Quantization

- Sampling and quantization are two basic procedures for processing digital images that convert continuous voltage signals obtained from sensors into digital images.
- Sampling is the process of digitizing the coordinate values (x and y) of the image, while quantization is the process of digitizing the amplitude values (f) of the image .
- Sampling determines the spatial resolution of the digitized image, which is the number of pixels per unit area. Quantization determines the number of grey levels or colors in the digitized image, which is the number of bits per pixel .
- The sampling rate and the quantization level affect the quality and the size of the digital image. A higher sampling rate and a higher quantization level result in a more accurate representation of the original image, but also require more storage space and processing time .
