### Introduction

- Image analytics is the process of applying computer vision techniques and machine learning algorithms to extract information and insights from digital images.
- Image analytics can be used for various applications, such as face recognition, object detection, medical imaging, security, entertainment, etc.
- Image analytics involves the following steps:
  - Image acquisition: capturing or obtaining images from various sources, such as cameras, scanners, satellites, etc.
  - Image preprocessing: enhancing or modifying images to improve their quality, reduce noise, correct distortions, etc.
  - Image segmentation: dividing images into meaningful regions or objects based on some criteria, such as color, texture, shape, etc.
  - Image feature extraction: extracting relevant characteristics or attributes from images, such as edges, corners, keypoints, descriptors, etc.
  - Image classification: assigning labels or categories to images or regions based on some criteria, such as supervised or unsupervised learning, etc.
  - Image analysis: performing various tasks or operations on images, such as recognition, detection, tracking, matching, retrieval, etc.
- Image analytics requires the knowledge of various concepts and techniques from mathematics, statistics, computer science, and engineering, such as linear algebra, calculus, probability, optimization, data structures, algorithms, etc.
- Image analytics also requires the knowledge of various tools and frameworks, such as Python, OpenCV, TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.
