Triple DES (3DES) is a symmetric-key block cipher that uses three rounds of the Data Encryption Standard (DES) algorithm. It encrypts data in 64-bit blocks using a key size of 168 or 112 bits. 3DES provides a higher level of security than DES by applying the DES algorithm three times to each data block, using different keys for each round. This makes it more resistant to brute-force attacks and other cryptanalysis techniques. 3DES is widely used in financial and government applications due to its security and efficiency. It operates in several modes of operation, including ECB, CBC, and CFB, to provide confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of data. Despite its popularity, 3DES has been criticized for its slow encryption and decryption times and its limited key size compared to modern block ciphers.
