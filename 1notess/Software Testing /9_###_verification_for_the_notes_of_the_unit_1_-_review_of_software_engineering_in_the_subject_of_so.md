### Verification for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Verification is the process of evaluating a system or its component(s) with the intent to determine whether the developed product satisfies the specified requirements. In the context of Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing, verification is used to ensure that the software under test meets the requirements specified by the customer or end-user. This process is performed through a combination of techniques such as inspections, walkthroughs, and peer reviews. The goal of verification is to catch defects as early as possible in the software development process, which helps to reduce the cost of fixing defects later in the process. Additionally, verification helps to improve the quality of the software by catching defects before they become more complex and harder to fix.
