### Risk Analysis for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing
Risk Analysis in Regression Testing:
1. Identify potential risks: Determine areas of the software that may be affected by changes and prioritize them based on the likelihood and impact of the risks.
2. Evaluate test coverage: Ensure that adequate test coverage exists for the areas identified as high-risk.
3. Prioritize tests: Prioritize regression tests based on the risk level of the areas being tested.
4. Re-run tests: Re-run tests that were previously run to ensure that changes have not introduced new defects.
5. Monitor results: Monitor the results of regression tests and identify any new defects that may have been introduced.

Note: This process should be repeated regularly to ensure that the software remains stable and free of defects.
