### Priority category for the notes of the Unit 4 - Regression Testing in the subject of Software Testing
Priority categories for regression testing:

1. High priority: Tests cases that cover critical functionality and have a high impact on the system if they fail.

2. Medium priority: Tests cases that cover important functionality, but have a lower impact on the system if they fail.

3. Low priority: Tests cases that cover less important functionality and have a minimal impact on the system if they fail.

It's important to prioritize regression tests to ensure that the most critical functionality is tested first and resources are used effectively.
