### Difference Between Verification and Validation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Review of Software Engineering in the subject of Software Testing
Verification and validation are two key concepts in software engineering, related to the quality assurance of software products.

Verification: refers to the process of evaluating software system or component during or at the end of the development process to determine whether it satisfies specified requirements. This includes activities like reviewing, walkthrough, inspection, and testing.

Validation: refers to the process of evaluating a software system or component during or at the end of the development process to determine whether it satisfies specified requirements and is fit for its intended use. This includes activities like testing, simulation, and demonstration.

In summary, verification is focused on ensuring that the software product is built right, while validation is focused on ensuring that the software product is right for its intended use.
