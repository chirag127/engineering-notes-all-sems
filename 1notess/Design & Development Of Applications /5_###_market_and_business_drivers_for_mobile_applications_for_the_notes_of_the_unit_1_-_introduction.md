### Market and business drivers for mobile applications for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075) in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
Market drivers for mobile applications:
1. Increasing smartphone usage
2. Growing demand for on-the-go access to information and services
3. Advancements in mobile technology
4. Growing number of mobile internet users
5. Increase in mobile commerce and payments

Business drivers for mobile applications:
1. Improved customer engagement and experience
2. Increased brand visibility and accessibility
3. Better data collection and analysis 
4. New revenue streams through app purchases and in-app advertising
5. Improved operational efficiency and productivity.
