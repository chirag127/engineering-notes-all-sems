### Packaging and deployment for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technology I – ANDROID of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075) in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
Packaging and deployment for the notes of Unit 4 - Technology I - ANDROID in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications (KCS075) involves the following steps:
1. Building the Android application using Android Studio or other tools.
2. Compiling the source code into an APK (Android Package) file.
3. Signing the APK with a digital signature to verify the identity of the author.
4. Distributing the APK through the Google Play Store, third-party app stores, or directly to users.
5. Installing the APK on an Android device.
6. Running the application on the device.
7. Updating the application when necessary by releasing a new version of the APK.
