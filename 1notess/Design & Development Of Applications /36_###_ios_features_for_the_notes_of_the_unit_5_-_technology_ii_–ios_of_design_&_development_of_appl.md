### iOS features for the notes of the Unit 5 - Technology II –iOS of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075) in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
1. User interface: Clean and intuitive interface with a focus on simplicity and ease of use.
2. Notes creation: Ability to create notes with text, images, and checklists.
3. Formatting options: Ability to format text with bold, italic, underline, and bullet points.
4. Notes organization: Ability to categorize notes into folders and tag them for easy searching.
5. Notes syncing: Ability to sync notes across all Apple devices through iCloud.
6. Reminders: Ability to set reminders for individual notes.
7. Voice notes: Ability to record voice notes and transcribe them into text.
8. Handwriting recognition: Ability to write notes by hand and have them recognized as text.
9. Sharing options: Ability to share notes with others through email, message, or AirDrop.
10. Password protection: Option to password-protect sensitive notes.
