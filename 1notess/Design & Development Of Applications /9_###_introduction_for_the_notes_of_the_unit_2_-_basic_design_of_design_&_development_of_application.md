### Introduction for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Design of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075) in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
The Unit 2 of the subject Design & Development of Applications (KCS075) focuses on the Basic Design of Design & Development of Applications. This unit provides an overview of the design process and the various steps involved in creating an effective design for software applications. The topics covered in this unit include design principles, user-centered design, prototyping, and design documentation. Understanding these concepts is crucial for students who wish to develop effective software applications that meet the needs of users.
