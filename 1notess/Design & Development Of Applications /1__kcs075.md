KCS075 is a model of a stepper motor manufactured by the company "Kysan Electronics". Stepper motors are a type of electric motor that move in precise steps, rather than continuously like a regular DC motor. The KCS075 model is a hybrid stepper motor, which means it uses both permanent magnets and electromagnetic coils to produce motion. It has a step angle of 1.8 degrees and can be driven with either bipolar or unipolar current. It has a holding torque of 0.75 Nm and a maximum current rating of 2.5 A per phase. The motor is commonly used in applications requiring precise control of position and speed, such as in CNC machines, robotics, and printing equipment.
