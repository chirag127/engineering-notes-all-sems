### Introduction to Objective C for the notes of the Unit 5 - Technology II –iOS of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075) in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
Objective-C is a high-level programming language used for developing applications for Apple's iOS and macOS operating systems. It is an object-oriented language that adds Smalltalk-style messaging to the C programming language. It is a dynamic language that supports dynamic binding, which allows for late binding of objects. Objective-C is also known for its use of dynamic dispatch, which enables the language to support late binding. Objective-C is widely used in the development of iOS and macOS applications, and it is a key component of the Cocoa and Cocoa Touch frameworks.
