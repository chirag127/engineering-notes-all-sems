### Basics of embedded systems design for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Design of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075) in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
Embedded systems design:

1. Definition: A combination of hardware and software integrated into a single system to perform a specific task.

2. Characteristics: Real-time, low power consumption, small form factor, cost-effectiveness.

3. Hardware components: Microcontroller, memory, input/output devices, power supply.

4. Software components: Operating system, device drivers, application software.

5. Design process: Requirements gathering, architecture design, hardware design, software design, testing and debugging.

6. Development tools: Compilers, debuggers, emulators, integrated development environments (IDE).

7. Key challenges: Performance optimization, power consumption, reliability, security.
