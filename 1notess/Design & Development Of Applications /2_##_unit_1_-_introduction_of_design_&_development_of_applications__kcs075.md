## Unit 1 - Introduction of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
Unit 1 of KCS075 covers the basics of design and development of applications. It introduces key concepts such as software development life cycle, software design patterns, and user-centered design. Students learn how to apply these concepts to real-world projects and develop a solid understanding of the software development process. Topics covered include requirements gathering, prototyping, testing, and deployment. Additionally, students will learn about various software development tools and methodologies, such as Agile and Waterfall, and how to choose the right approach for a given project. Overall, Unit 1 provides a strong foundation for students who are interested in pursuing a career in software development.
