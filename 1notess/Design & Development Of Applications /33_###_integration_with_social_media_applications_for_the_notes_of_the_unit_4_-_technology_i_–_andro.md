### Integration with social media applications for the notes of the Unit 4 - Technology I – ANDROID of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075) in the subject of Design & Development Of Applications ( KCS075)
Integration with social media applications for the notes of Unit 4 - Technology I - ANDROID of Design & Development Of Applications (KCS075) involves connecting the notes app to popular social media platforms such as Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram. This integration allows users to share their notes directly from the app to their social media profiles. It also enables users to view their friends' notes and comments on them. Additionally, users can also receive notifications for updates and new notes from their friends. This integration helps in increasing the visibility and reach of the notes app and makes it easier for users to share their notes with a wider audience.
