## Unit 4 - Beowulf Cluster
The Beowulf cluster is a type of computer cluster designed to perform high-performance computing. It is named after the epic poem Beowulf and typically consists of a group of inexpensive computers connected together with fast interconnects to form a single high-performance system. Beowulf clusters are often used for scientific computing, simulations, and data analysis. They are also used in industry for engineering simulations, financial modeling, and other demanding applications. The key feature of a Beowulf cluster is its ability to distribute processing tasks across multiple nodes, allowing for parallel processing and increased performance.
