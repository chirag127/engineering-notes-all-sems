### Storage for the notes of the Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing in the subject
There are several options for storing notes for Unit 5 - Overview of Cloud Computing, including:

1. Physical notebook: Write down notes by hand in a physical notebook, which can be easily accessed and reviewed.

2. Digital Notebook: Use a digital notebook app, such as Evernote or OneNote, to store notes electronically. These apps allow for easy organization, search and sharing.

3. Cloud Storage: Store notes in the cloud using a cloud storage service, such as Google Drive or Dropbox, for easy access from any device and secure backup.

4. Online Document: Use an online document creation tool, such as Google Docs or Microsoft Office Online, to create and store notes in the cloud.

5. Mind Mapping Tool: Use a mind mapping tool, such as XMind or MindNode, to visually organize and store notes in a way that is easy to understand and review.

Regardless of the method chosen, it's important to regularly review and update notes to ensure they remain relevant and accurate.
