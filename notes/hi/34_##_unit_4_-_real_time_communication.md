## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication
Unit 4 - Real Time Communication is a technology that allows for the immediate exchange of information between devices, allowing for real-time communication. This can include video conferencing, instant messaging, and voice calls. It is made possible through the use of the internet, which enables the rapid transfer of data between devices in real-time. Some popular examples of real-time communication technologies include Skype, Zoom, and WhatsApp. The use of real-time communication has revolutionized the way people communicate, making it possible to have face-to-face conversations with people from anywhere in the world, at any time.
