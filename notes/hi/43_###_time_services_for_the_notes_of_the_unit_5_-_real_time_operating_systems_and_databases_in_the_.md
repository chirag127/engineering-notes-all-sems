### Time Services for the notes of the Unit 5 - Real Time Operating Systems and Databases in the subject of Real Time System
Time Services:
- Provide time-related functions for real-time systems, such as timekeeping and synchronization.
- Typically implemented as part of the operating system or as a separate library.
- Offer functions such as getting the current time, setting a timer, and waiting for a timer to expire.
- Used by applications to perform time-sensitive tasks, such as scheduling, time-stamping, and time-based events.
- Can use hardware clocks or software algorithms to maintain time accuracy.
- Can be affected by external factors such as power loss, network disruptions, and clock drift.
- Often have to be designed for real-time constraints, such as low latency and high accuracy.
