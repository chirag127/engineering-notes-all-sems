### Dynamic Versus Static Systems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System
Dynamic Systems: 
- Allow the scheduling of tasks to change during runtime
- Responsive to changing conditions
- Can handle unpredictable workloads

Static Systems: 
- Scheduling is fixed at compile time 
- Predictable behavior 
- Efficient, but inflexible 

In real-time systems, dynamic scheduling is often preferred as it provides greater adaptability to changing conditions, while static scheduling is preferred for systems with predictable workloads where efficiency is crucial. Both approaches have their benefits and drawbacks, and the choice between them depends on the specific requirements of the system being developed.
