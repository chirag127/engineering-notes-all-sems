### Soft and Hard RT Communication systems for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System
Soft Real-Time Systems:
- Meet deadlines most of the time, but occasional missed deadlines are acceptable
- Used in multimedia, gaming, and some control systems
- Tolerance for delay variations

Hard Real-Time Systems:
- Deadlines must be met strictly and consistently
- Used in safety-critical systems, such as aerospace and medical equipment
- No tolerance for delay variations

Both types of systems prioritize timely processing of tasks, but hard real-time systems have a higher level of urgency and reliability requirements.
