### Clock Driven Approach for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System
The Clock Driven Approach is a scheduling algorithm used in real-time systems. It assigns a fixed time slice, called a quantum, to each task in the system. Each task is executed for its assigned quantum, and if it is not completed, it is pre-empted and moved to the end of the queue. This approach ensures that all tasks receive a fair share of the CPU time, and that the system is predictable, as each task knows exactly when it will be executed. The Clock Driven Approach is simple to implement, but can lead to poor performance if the quanta are too large, as tasks may not be able to complete within their assigned time slice.
