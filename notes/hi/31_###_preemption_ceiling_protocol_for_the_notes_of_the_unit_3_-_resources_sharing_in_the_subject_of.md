### Preemption Ceiling Protocol for the notes of the Unit 3 - Resources Sharing in the subject of Real Time System
Preemption Ceiling Protocol is a priority-based resource sharing protocol in real-time systems. It assigns priority levels to tasks in the system and assigns a ceiling priority level to each resource. When a task requests a resource, it must have a priority level equal to or higher than the ceiling priority level of the resource. If a higher priority task requests the same resource, the lower priority task will be preempted and its execution will be suspended. This ensures that high priority tasks receive the resources they need in a timely manner. The protocol helps to ensure predictable and deterministic behavior in real-time systems.
