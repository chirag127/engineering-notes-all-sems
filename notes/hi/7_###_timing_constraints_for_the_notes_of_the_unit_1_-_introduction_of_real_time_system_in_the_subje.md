### Timing Constraints for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System
Timing constraints in Real Time Systems refer to the requirements for the timing aspects of system behavior. These constraints include deadlines, response times, and jitter. Deadlines refer to the maximum amount of time a task can take to complete. Response times refer to the amount of time it takes for a system to respond to a request. Jitter refers to the variability in response time. Timing constraints are important in real-time systems because they determine the system's ability to meet its objectives and provide predictable and reliable behavior.

ChatGPT: Hard real-time systems have strict timing constraints and missed deadlines can result in catastrophic consequences. Soft real-time systems have less stringent timing constraints and missed deadlines may result in degraded performance but do not cause catastrophic consequences.
