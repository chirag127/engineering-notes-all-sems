### Priority-Based Service and Weighted Round-Robin Service Disciplines for Switched Networks for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System
Priority-Based Service:
- A prioritization scheme for scheduling network traffic in a switched network
- Traffic is assigned a priority level and is transmitted in order of priority level
- High priority traffic is transmitted before low priority traffic
- Avoids delay-sensitive traffic such as voice or video being impacted by bulk data traffic

Weighted Round-Robin Service:
- A scheduling scheme for network traffic in a switched network
- Traffic is divided into multiple queues, each with a different priority level
- Traffic is transmitted in a cyclic order, with each queue being serviced for a specified amount of time
- The amount of time each queue is serviced can be weighted to reflect its priority level
- Avoids low priority traffic monopolizing the network and impacting high priority traffic.
