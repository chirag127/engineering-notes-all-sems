### Temporal Parameters of Real Time Workload for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System
Temporal parameters of real-time workload refer to the timing constraints and deadlines of tasks in real-time systems. 
1. Period: The time interval between two consecutive instances of a task. 
2. Deadline: The time by which a task must be completed. 
3. Critical Time: The maximum time a task can take to complete without violating its deadline. 
4. Response Time: The time taken by a system to respond to a request. 
5. Utilization: The percentage of time a task uses the system's resources. 
6. Latency: The time delay between the occurrence of an event and the system's response to that event.
