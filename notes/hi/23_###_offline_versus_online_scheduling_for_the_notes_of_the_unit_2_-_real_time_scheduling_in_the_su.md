### Offline Versus Online Scheduling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System
Offline Scheduling:
- Determines task assignments before system execution
- Can use complex algorithms to optimize task assignments
- Does not account for changes in system conditions during execution

Online Scheduling:
- Determines task assignments during system execution
- Must make decisions based on current system conditions
- Can respond to changes in system conditions, but may not be able to optimize task assignments as well as offline scheduling

Both methods have trade-offs and the choice between them depends on the requirements of the real-time system.
