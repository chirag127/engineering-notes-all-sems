## Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System
Real-time systems are computer systems that respond to external events within a specified time frame. Unit 1 covers the introduction to real-time systems, including:

1. Definition and characteristics of real-time systems
2. Types of real-time systems (hard, firm, and soft)
3. Importance and applications of real-time systems
4. Challenges and constraints in real-time systems design and implementation

This unit provides a comprehensive overview of real-time systems and lays the foundation for further study in the field.
