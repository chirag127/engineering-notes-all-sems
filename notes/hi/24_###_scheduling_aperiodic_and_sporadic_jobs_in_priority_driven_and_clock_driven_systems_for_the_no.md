### Scheduling Aperiodic and Sporadic jobs in Priority Driven and Clock Driven Systems for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System
Scheduling Aperiodic and Sporadic Jobs:
- Aperiodic jobs are tasks with unpredictable arrival times and no fixed execution time.
- Sporadic jobs are a type of aperiodic task with minimum inter-arrival time constraints.

Priority Driven Systems:
- In priority driven systems, tasks are assigned priorities and are executed in order of their priority.
- Aperiodic jobs are assigned a priority dynamically when they arrive.
- Sporadic jobs are assigned a priority based on their minimum inter-arrival time constraints.

Clock Driven Systems:
- In clock driven systems, tasks are executed at fixed intervals determined by the clock.
- Aperiodic jobs are executed when the clock ticks, and their execution time is determined by their priority.
- Sporadic jobs are executed at their minimum inter-arrival time constraints, and their execution time is determined by their priority.
