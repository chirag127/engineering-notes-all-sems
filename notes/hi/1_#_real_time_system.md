# Real Time System
Real-time systems are computer systems that are designed to respond to inputs within a specified time frame, typically in the order of milliseconds or microseconds. These systems are used in a wide range of applications, including control systems, multimedia systems, and communication systems. Real-time systems are characterized by their ability to process data in real-time, which means that they must be able to respond to inputs within a specified time frame. To achieve this, real-time systems must have predictable and deterministic behavior, meaning that they must be able to perform their tasks in a consistent and reliable manner, even under heavy loads. Additionally, real-time systems must be able to handle multiple tasks simultaneously, and must be able to prioritize tasks based on their importance and urgency.
