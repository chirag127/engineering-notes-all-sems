### Basic Concepts in Real time Communication for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System
-Real-time communication: refers to communication that occurs in real-time, meaning that there is no delay between the sender and the receiver.
-Protocols: set of rules that govern real-time communication, such as TCP/IP, UDP, and RTP.
-Latency: the time it takes for a message to travel from the sender to the receiver.
-Jitter: the variation in latency that can occur in real-time communication.
-Packet loss: the number of data packets that are lost during transmission.
-Synchronization: ensuring that events occur at the same time across different systems.
-Quality of Service (QoS): measures the quality of real-time communication, including factors such as latency, jitter, and packet loss.
-Multicast: a type of real-time communication where a single sender sends data to multiple receivers.
