

### Activities in SPM

* **Planning:** This is the process of setting goals and objectives and then developing a plan to achieve them. It involves analyzing the current situation, identifying potential risks and opportunities, and then creating a strategy to achieve the desired outcome.

* **Organizing:** This is the process of putting together resources, people, and plans to achieve the desired goals. It includes assigning tasks and responsibilities, allocating resources, and establishing reporting structures.

* **Leading:** This is the process of motivating, inspiring, and guiding people to achieve the goals. It involves setting expectations, providing feedback, and creating an environment of trust and collaboration.

* **Controlling:** This is the process of monitoring and measuring progress towards the goals. It involves setting up systems to track progress and identify potential issues, and then taking corrective action when needed.
