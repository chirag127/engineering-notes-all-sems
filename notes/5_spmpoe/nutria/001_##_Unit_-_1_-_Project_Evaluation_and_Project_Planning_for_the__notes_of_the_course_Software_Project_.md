
## Unit - 1 - Project Evaluation and Project Planning for the  notes of the course Software Project Management

Project Evaluation and Planning is an important part of software project management. It helps to ensure that the project is successful by assessing its feasibility, cost, and risk. It also involves developing a plan for how the project will be implemented, including the resources and personnel needed.

### Feasibility

The first step in project evaluation and planning is to assess the feasibility of the project. This involves considering the technical, economic, and organizational aspects of the project to determine if it can be completed within the available resources and budget.

### Cost

The second step is to estimate the cost of the project. This includes the cost of materials, labor, and other resources needed to complete the project. It also includes any costs associated with training personnel or acquiring new equipment.

### Risk

The third step is to assess the risks associated with the project. This involves identifying any potential risks that could affect the project’s success, such as changes in technology or the availability of resources. It also involves developing strategies to mitigate these risks.

### Plan

The fourth step is to develop a plan for how the project will be implemented. This includes deciding on the timeline, tasks, and resources needed to complete the project. It also includes setting milestones and deadlines for each task.

### Mnemonics

Mnemonics can be useful for remembering key concepts in project evaluation and planning. For example, the acronym “FEAR” can help to remember the four steps of project evaluation and planning: Feasibility, Cost, Risk, and Plan.
