

### Agile Methods in Software Project Management

- Agile methods are iterative and incremental approaches to software development that focus on delivering working software quickly and responding to changing customer requirements. 
- Agile methods emphasize face-to-face communication, collaboration between self-organizing, cross-functional teams, and customer feedback. 
- The most popular agile methods are Scrum, Kanban, and Extreme Programming (XP).
- Agile methods are based on the principles outlined in the Agile Manifesto, which emphasize individuals and interactions over processes and tools, working software over comprehensive documentation, customer collaboration over contract negotiation, and responding to change over following a plan.
- Agile methods are designed to be adaptive and flexible, allowing teams to respond quickly to changes in customer requirements or technology.
- Agile teams use a variety of tools and techniques to manage their work, such as user stories, sprints, retrospectives, and stand-ups.
- Agile teams also use a variety of metrics to measure their progress, such as velocity, burn-up charts, and cumulative flow diagrams.
- Agile methods are widely used in software development, but can also be applied to other types of projects, such as product development and service delivery.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks: 
- Agile Manifesto: PICR (People, Interactions, Collaboration, Responding)
- Scrum: RICE (Release, Iteration, Capacity, Estimation)
- Kanban: WIP (Work In Progress)
- XP: TDD (Test-Driven Development)
