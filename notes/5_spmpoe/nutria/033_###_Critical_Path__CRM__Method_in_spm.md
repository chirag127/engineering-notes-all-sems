
### Critical Path (CRM) Method in spm

The Critical Path Method (CPM) is a project management technique used to plan, schedule, and control the activities of a project. It is used to identify the most important tasks in a project and the order in which they should be completed.

The CPM is a tool used to identify the tasks that need to be completed in order to complete the project on time. It is used to determine the sequence of tasks, the duration of each task, and the resources needed for each task. It also helps to identify any potential risks that may affect the project’s timeline.

The CPM is based on the concept of a “critical path”, which is the sequence of tasks that must be completed in order to complete the project. The critical path is determined by identifying the tasks that have the longest duration and the most dependencies.
