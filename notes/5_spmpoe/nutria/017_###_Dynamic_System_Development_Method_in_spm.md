
### Dynamic System Development Method in spm

Dynamic System Development Method (DSDM) is a framework for software development that was developed in the 1990s and is still in use today. It is an agile methodology that focuses on delivering the highest quality product within a short period of time.

DSDM is based on the following principles:

- Focus on the business need: The primary focus of the project should be on delivering the highest quality product in the shortest amount of time.

- Deliver on time: The project should be completed within the specified time frame.

- Collaborate: All stakeholders should work together to ensure that the project is successful.

- Never compromise quality: Quality should never be compromised in order to meet the timeline.

- Build incrementally from firm foundations: The project should be broken down into small, manageable pieces and built up incrementally.

- Develop iteratively: The project should be developed in iterations, with each iteration building on the previous one.

- Communicate continuously and clearly: Communication between all stakeholders should be clear and consistent.

- Demonstrate control: The project should be managed and monitored closely to ensure that it is on track.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks for DSDM:

- FCD: Focus on the business need, Deliver on time, and never Compromise on quality.

- CIRCLE: Collaborate, Iterate, Communicate, Leverage, and Execute.

- DRY: Don't Repeat Yourself.

- KISS: Keep It Simple, Stupid.
