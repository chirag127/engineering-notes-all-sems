

### Formulating Network Model in spm

* Network models are mathematical models used to study the behavior of a system, such as a computer network, over time. 
* Network models are used to simulate the behavior of a system under different conditions and to analyze the effects of changes in the system's structure.
* Network models are based on the principles of graph theory and can be represented as a graph, with nodes representing the components of a system and edges representing the connections between the components.
* The most common type of network model is the directed graph, where the edges are directed from one node to another.
* Network models can be used to study the flow of information in a system, the reliability of a system, the performance of a system, and the scalability of a system.
* Network models can also be used to study the behavior of a system under different conditions, such as congestion, or to analyze the effects of changes in the system's structure, such as the addition of new nodes or edges.
* Network models can be used to find optimal paths through a network, to identify bottlenecks in the system, and to identify potential areas of improvement.
* Network models can also be used to predict the behavior of a system under different conditions, such as when the system is under attack.
* Network models can be used to analyze the security of a system and to identify potential vulnerabilities.
* Network models can also be used to study the performance of a system under different conditions, such as when the system is overloaded.
* Mnemonics and learning tricks can be used to help remember the different components of a network model and the relationships between them. For example, the acronym "NODES" can be used to remember the components of a network model: Nodes, Edges, Direction, and Structure.
