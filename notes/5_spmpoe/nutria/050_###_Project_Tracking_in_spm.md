
### Project Tracking in SPM

Project Tracking in SPM is a process of monitoring the progress of a project and ensuring that it is completed on time and within budget. It requires the use of various tools, such as Gantt Charts, to track the progress of the project and identify any potential problems.

Mnemonics:

* **P**lan - Create a plan for the project and set realistic deadlines.
* **R**esources - Allocate the necessary resources to complete the project.
* **O**bjectives - Establish the objectives of the project and ensure that they are met.
* **J**ournal - Keep a journal of the progress of the project and any changes made.
* **E**valuate - Evaluate the progress of the project and identify any areas for improvement.
* **C**ontrol - Monitor the progress of the project to ensure that it is on track.
* **T**roubleshoot - Identify and address any potential problems that arise.

Advantages:

* Allows the project to be monitored and controlled in real-time.
* Ensures that the project is completed on time and within budget.
* Identifies potential problems before they become major issues.
* Improves communication between team members.

Disadvantages:

* Can be time consuming and tedious.
* Can be costly to implement.
* Can be difficult to maintain.
