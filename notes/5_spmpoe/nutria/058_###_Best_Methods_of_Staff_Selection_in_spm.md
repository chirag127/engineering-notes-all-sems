
### Best Methods of Staff Selection in SPM

1. **Resume Screening:** This is an initial step in the recruitment process, in which resumes are screened to identify potential candidates. This step helps to reduce the number of applicants to be considered for further steps in the recruitment process.

2. **Job Interviews:** This is an important step in the recruitment process, in which the potential candidates are interviewed to assess their qualifications, skills and aptitude for the job.

3. **Reference Checks:** This is a step in the recruitment process, in which the potential candidates are checked for their references from previous employers. This helps to assess the candidate's work experience and performance.

4. **Psychometric Tests:** This is a step in the recruitment process, in which the potential candidates are tested for their aptitude and personality. This helps to identify the candidate's suitability for the job.

5. **Skills Tests:** This is a step in the recruitment process, in which the potential candidates are tested for their technical and non-technical skills. This helps to identify the candidate's level of proficiency in the required skills.

6. **Background Checks:** This is a step in the recruitment process, in which the potential candidates are checked for their past criminal records and financial histories. This helps to assess the candidate's eligibility for the job.

7. **Medical Tests:** This is a step in the recruitment process, in which the potential candidates are checked for any medical conditions. This helps to assess the candidate's fitness for the job.
