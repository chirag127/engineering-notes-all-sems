
### Dispersed and Virtual Teams in SPM

* Dispersed and virtual teams are teams of people who work together to achieve a common goal, but who are geographically distributed and do not physically meet. 
* In software project management (SPM), dispersed and virtual teams are becoming increasingly common due to the rise of remote working and the global nature of many projects. 
* When managing a dispersed and virtual team, it is important to understand the different challenges that come with this type of team structure. 
* Communication is a key challenge for dispersed and virtual teams, as it is difficult to ensure that everyone is on the same page and that all team members feel included. 
* In order to effectively manage a dispersed and virtual team, it is important to have clear communication protocols and to ensure that everyone is aware of the team's goals and objectives. 
* It is also important to ensure that all team members have access to the same resources, such as project management software and collaboration tools, so that everyone is able to work together effectively. 
* Additionally, it is important to ensure that team members are able to connect with each other in meaningful ways, such as through video conferencing and virtual team building activities.
