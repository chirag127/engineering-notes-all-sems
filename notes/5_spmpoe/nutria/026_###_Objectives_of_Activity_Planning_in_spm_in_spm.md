
### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

* To define the project objectives and scope
* To identify the activities necessary for achieving the project objectives
* To assign responsibility for completing the activities
* To identify the resources needed for each activity
* To estimate the duration of each activity
* To develop a network of activities
* To identify the critical activities
* To develop a project schedule
* To identify the risks associated with each activity
* To develop a plan for monitoring and controlling the project progress
* To develop a plan for managing changes to the project
