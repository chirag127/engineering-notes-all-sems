
### Framework for Management and Control in SPM

* **Planning:** Planning is the process of setting objectives and determining the best strategies to achieve those objectives. It involves analyzing the current situation, identifying potential problems, and developing solutions. It also includes setting goals, objectives, and action plans.

* **Organizing:** Organizing is the process of arranging resources to achieve the objectives set in the planning process. It involves assigning tasks, delegating authority, and allocating resources. It also includes establishing structures, policies, and procedures.

* **Leading:** Leading is the process of motivating and directing people to achieve the objectives set in the planning process. It involves setting expectations, providing direction, and inspiring people to take action. It also includes providing feedback, coaching, and guidance.

* **Controlling:** Controlling is the process of monitoring progress and taking corrective action when necessary. It involves measuring performance, comparing it to objectives, and making adjustments when needed. It also includes setting standards, monitoring results, and taking corrective action.

* **Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:** Mnemonics and learning tricks are useful tools for memorizing and understanding concepts. For example, the acronym "P-O-L-C" can be used to remember the four components of the framework for management and control in SPM: Planning, Organizing, Leading, and Controlling.
