
### Organizational Structures in spm

Organizational Structures in spm are a set of rules and regulations that determine the way in which organizations are managed and the roles and responsibilities of individuals within the organization. It defines the relationships between people and departments, the chain of command, and the division of labor.

Organizational Structures in spm can be divided into two main categories:

1. Hierarchical Structures: These are structures that are based on a hierarchy of authority and responsibility. The most common hierarchical structures are:
    - Functional Structure: This structure is based on division of labor, with each employee assigned to a specific task or area of expertise.
    - Divisional Structure: This structure is based on division of tasks among different divisions or departments.
    - Matrix Structure: This structure is based on the combination of both functional and divisional structures.
2. Network Structures: These are structures that are based on collaboration and cooperation between different departments or organizations. The most common network structures are:
    - Project-Based Structures: This structure is based on the organization of teams to work on specific projects.
    - Virtual Structures: This structure is based on the use of technology to facilitate communication and collaboration between different departments or organizations.

Mnemonics and Learning Tricks:

Organizational Structures in spm can be difficult to remember, but there are some mnemonics and learning tricks that can help:

- F-D-M: This is a mnemonic for the three main types of organizational structures – Functional, Divisional, and Matrix.
- P-V-T: This is a mnemonic for the two main types of network structures – Project-Based and Virtual.
- L-A-R: This is a mnemonic for the three main components of an organizational structure – Leadership, Authority, and Responsibility.
