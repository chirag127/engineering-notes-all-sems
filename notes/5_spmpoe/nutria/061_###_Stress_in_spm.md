

### Stress in SPM

* Stress in SPM is a phenomenon that occurs when an individual experiences a feeling of pressure, tension, or strain due to a particular situation or event.
* Stress can be caused by both positive and negative events. Positive events can include getting a promotion, getting married, or having a baby. Negative events can include the death of a loved one, losing a job, or facing financial difficulties.
* Stress can have both physical and psychological effects, such as headaches, fatigue, difficulty concentrating, and irritability.
* It is important to manage stress in order to maintain physical and mental health. Some strategies for managing stress include:
    * Taking time to relax and unwind
    * Practicing mindfulness and meditation
    * Engaging in physical activity
    * Talking to a friend or family member
    * Eating a balanced diet
    * Getting plenty of sleep
    * Seeking professional help if needed
* Mnemonics and learning tricks can be helpful for remembering information related to stress in SPM. For example, the acronym "STOP" can be used to remember the following:
    * S - Slow down and take a break
    * T - Talk to someone
    * O - Observe your thoughts and feelings
    * P - Put things into perspective
