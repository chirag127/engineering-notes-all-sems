
### Stepwise Project Planning in spm

* Stepwise project planning in SPM involves breaking down the project into smaller parts and then completing each part one by one. 
* It is a systematic approach to project management and helps in reducing the risks associated with the project.
* The first step in the process is to identify the project objectives. This involves understanding the goals and objectives of the project and ensuring they are achievable.
* The second step is to create a project plan. This involves listing out the tasks that need to be completed and the order in which they should be completed.
* The third step is to assign resources and roles. This involves assigning people to specific tasks and ensuring they have the necessary skills and resources to complete them.
* The fourth step is to monitor and control the project. This involves tracking progress and making adjustments as needed.
* The fifth step is to review the project. This involves assessing the project's performance and making changes as needed.
* Finally, the sixth step is to close the project. This involves making sure all the tasks have been completed and the project has been successfully completed.

Mnemonics:
* SPPM: Stepwise Project Planning and Management.
