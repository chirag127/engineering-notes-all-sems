
### Extreme Programming in spm

* Extreme Programming (XP) is a software development methodology that focuses on customer satisfaction and rapid delivery of software products. It is an agile methodology that emphasizes communication, simplicity, feedback, and courage.
* XP is based on four core values: communication, simplicity, feedback, and courage. 
* XP also emphasizes continuous integration, automated testing, refactoring, and pair programming. 
* XP encourages teams to work together to create a product that meets customer requirements. 
* XP emphasizes the importance of customer feedback, which can be used to adjust the product to meet the customer's needs. 
* XP also encourages teams to use simple design techniques to create a product that can be easily maintained and changed.
* XP encourages teams to use automated testing to ensure the quality of the product. 
* XP also encourages teams to use refactoring to improve the design of the product. 
* XP encourages teams to use pair programming to ensure that the code is of high quality. 
* XP encourages teams to use continuous integration to ensure that the product is always up-to-date. 
* XP also encourages teams to use continuous delivery to ensure that the product is always available to the customer. 
* XP emphasizes the importance of communication between team members, which can help to ensure that the product is of high quality. 
* XP encourages teams to use the "test-driven development" approach to ensure that the product meets customer requirements. 
* XP also encourages teams to use the "agile development" approach to ensure that the product is delivered quickly and efficiently.
