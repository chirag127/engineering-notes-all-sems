
### Setting Objectives in SPM

* Objectives are the goals that one sets to achieve a certain outcome. In SPM (Strategic Performance Management), objectives are the targets that an organization sets for itself to measure performance.
* Objectives should be SMART:
  * Specific: Objectives should be clear, concise and well-defined.
  * Measurable: Objectives should be quantifiable, so that progress can be tracked and measured.
  * Achievable: Objectives should be realistic and achievable within a given timeframe.
  * Relevant: Objectives should be relevant to the organization’s overall goals and strategies.
  * Time-bound: Objectives should have a timeline for completion.
* Objectives are usually set at the organizational, departmental, and individual levels.
* Objectives should be aligned with the organization’s overall strategies and goals.
* Objectives should be communicated to all stakeholders to ensure they are aware of them and can work towards achieving them.
* Objectives should be reviewed regularly to ensure that they are still relevant and achievable.
* Objectives should be monitored and evaluated to ensure that they are being met and that the desired outcomes are being achieved.
