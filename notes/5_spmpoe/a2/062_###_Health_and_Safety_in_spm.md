### Health and Safety in spm

#### Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Always wear appropriate PPE like safety goggles, gloves, dust mask, ear plugs, safety shoes, hard hat, safety harness, etc. depending on the task.
- PPE protects from injuries, dust, noise, falling objects, etc.
- Ensure PPE fits properly and is appropriate for the task. Ill-fitting PPE can be more dangerous.

#### Safe Equipment Use
- Inspect equipment before use and ensure all guards are in place.
- Never disable safety mechanisms or interlocks.
- Always follow operating procedures and safety guidelines for equipment.
- Ensure proper training is provided before operating equipment.
- Report any damage or issues with equipment to the appropriate person.

#### Hazardous Substances
- Properly label and store all chemicals and substances. Keep in a ventilated area away from high temperatures.
- Always use hazardous substances in a fume hood or with adequate ventilation.
- Carefully follow material safety data sheets (MSDS) and use appropriate PPE when handling hazardous substances.
- Safely dispose of hazardous waste and chemicals as per regulations. Never pour down the drain.

#### Fire Safety
- Keep fire exits and escape routes clear of obstacles.
- Know the location of fire alarms, extinguishers, hoses, and escapes in the workplace.
- Regularly test and maintain fire alarms and extinguishers.
- Keep flammable liquids, gases, and materials safely stored and away from ignition sources.
- Be familiar with emergency procedures and regularly practice fire drills.
