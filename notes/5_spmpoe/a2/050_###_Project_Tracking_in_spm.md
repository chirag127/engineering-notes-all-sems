### Project Tracking in spm

Project tracking is an important aspect of project management. It involves monitoring the progress of a project and identifying any deviations from the plan. Some key points about project tracking in spm are:

1. Define metrics to track: Clearly define metrics and KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) to track the progress of the project. These could include budget, schedule, quality, etc. Tracking the right metrics is critical to effective project tracking.

2. Capture actuals: Regularly capture the actual metrics and progress data. This could be done through status reports, inspections, reviews, etc. The actual data must be accurate and timely to make sure any issues are identified early.

3. Compare actuals vs plan: Compare the actual metrics and progress data with the original plan. Analyze the differences and identify any deviations or gaps. Early identification of issues is important to take corrective actions.

4. Take corrective actions: For any deviations or gaps identified, take necessary corrective actions to bring the project back on track. This could include replanning parts of the project, redirecting resources, etc. Corrective actions must be taken promptly before the issues become too severe.

5. Report and monitor: Continuously report on the project tracking metrics and status. Closely monitor the progress and KPIs, and make sure the corrective actions are effective. Regular reporting and monitoring helps maintain tight control over the project.

Some mnemonics for remembering the key points:

- DCTAC: Define metrics, Capture actuals, Compare, Take action, Report and monitor

- KPITRC: KPIs, Progress data, Issues, Take action, Report, Correct

The project tracking process is iterative and is performed throughout the project lifecycle to keep the project on track towards a successful completion. Effective project tracking, aided by the right tools and systems, is crucial to project management.
