## Unit - 2 - Project Life Cycle and Effort Estimation

The project life cycle typically consists of the following phases:

1. Requirements Analysis: The customer's requirements are gathered and analyzed. The requirements are prioritized and specifications are created.
2. Design: The software design is developed based on the requirements specifications. The design may include interface design, architecture design, and database design.
3. Implementation: The software is developed as per the design specifications. Programming and software testing are done in this phase.
4. Testing: System testing, acceptance testing, and other forms of testing are conducted to identify and fix any defects or bugs.
5. Deployment: The final software is deployed and made available to the customer.
6. Maintenance: Necessary changes are made to the software to fix defects, improve performance, or adapt to environment changes.

To estimate the effort required for a software project, the following factors are considered:

- Size of the software to be developed - Estimated using LOC (Lines of Code) or Function Points
- Complexity of the software - Dependencies between modules, novelty of functions, etc. More complex software requires more effort.
- Experience of the team - Effort estimates are lower for teams with more experience in similar projects.
- Tools and techniques used - Use of automated tools and efficient SDLC methodologies reduces effort.
- Requirements volatility - Chances of changing requirements increases effort as rework may be required.

Project Estimation Techniques:

- Expert Judgment: Relying on expert opinion to provide estimates. Not very accurate but quick.
- Analogous Estimation: Basing estimates on similar previous projects. Also not very accurate.
- Bottom-up Estimation: Breaking down the project into smaller components and estimating the effort for individual components. The total effort is the sum of the components. More accurate but time-consuming.
- Three-point Estimation: Giving optimistic, pessimistic, and most likely estimates for a component. The total is calculated using the Beta distribution to provide a probabilistic estimate.
- Simulation and Modeling: Building a mathematical model of the project and simulating it to provide estimates. Very accurate but complex and time-consuming to build the proper model.
