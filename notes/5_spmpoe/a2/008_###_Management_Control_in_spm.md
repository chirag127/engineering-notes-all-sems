### Management Control in spm

- Management control is the process by which managers assure that resources are obtained and used effectively and efficiently in the accomplishment of the organization's objectives.
- The key elements of management control are:

1. Setting standards: Standards are the criteria or benchmarks against which actual performance is measured. Standards can be in the form of goals, objectives, quotas, budgets, etc.
2. Measuring performance: The measurement of actual performance and then comparing it with the pre-determined standards is critical to management control. Performance can be measured using a variety of qualitative and quantitative techniques.
3. Evaluating deviations: The deviations between actual and standard performance are identified and evaluated. Positive and negative deviations need to be investigated to determine their causes.
4. Taking corrective actions: Appropriate actions are taken to correct the deviations and ensure that future performance meets the standards. This may involve changing strategies, reallocating resources, providing training, modifying processes, etc.

- Mnemonics to remember key elements:
SMEDC - Setting standards, Measuring performance, Evaluating deviations, Taking corrective actions

- Management control facilitates planning and coordination, motivates employees, helps in performance evaluation, and leads to achieving organizational goals efficiently and effectively.
- Disadvantages: May discourage innovation, Inflexible, Time-consuming and costly process, Standards may become outdated.
- Examples: Budgetary control, Quality control, Sales target control, etc.
