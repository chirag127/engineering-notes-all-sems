### Managing People in spm

- Recruitment and Selection: Hiring the right candidates for the right job. Conducting interviews, evaluations and making the final selection.
- Training and Development: Providing necessary training to improve skills and overall development of employees. This could be on-the-job or formal training.
- Performance Management: Monitoring performance, providing feedback and taking corrective actions. This includes setting clear expectations, reviewing goals, and appraisals.
- Compensation and Benefits: Determining appropriate compensation and benefits packages to retain talent. This includes salaries, bonuses, healthcare, leave, etc.
- Labor Relations: Maintaining positive relationships with labor unions and addressing grievances. This includes negotiating contracts and resolving disputes.
- Safety and Health: Ensuring a safe and healthy work environment. This includes following regulations and providing necessary facilities and equipments.
- Work-Life Balance: Implementing policies and steps to avoid work-life conflicts and improve work-life balance. This could include flexi-timings, paid time-offs, etc.

Mnemonics:
- Right People - Recruitment
- Train People - Development
- Check Performance - Appraisals
- C(ompensation) and B(enefits)
- Labor unions - Relations
- Safe - Health
- Life - Work balance

Advantages: Increased productivity, reduced turnover, legal compliance, increased job satisfaction and loyalty, cost savings from training and recruitments.
Disadvantages: Time and cost intensive, challenges in implementation, conflicts with labor unions, challenges in policy enforcement.
Examples: Google provides many perks and benefits. Many companies provide workplace wellness programs.
Applications: Essential for organizational success and growth. Applicable to all companies with employees.
