### Dynamic System Development Method in spm

The Dynamic Systems Development Method (DSDM) is an agile project delivery framework, initially used for software development and IT projects, but can be applied to any project. DSDM consists of eight principles and a set of practical techniques to apply those principles. The principles are:

1. Focus on the Business Need
2. Deliver on Time
3. Collaborate
4. Never Compromise Quality
5. Build Incrementally from Firm Foundations
6. Develop Iteratively
7. Communicate Continuously and Clearly
8. Demonstrate Control

Some key techniques used in DSDM are:

- Timeboxing: Fixing timescales and delivering to them
- MoSCoW prioritization: Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have
- Prototyping: Creating prototypes to explore requirements and options
- Workshop sessions: Bringing stakeholders together to make progress
- Modeling: Using models to explore requirements and solutions
- Step-wise refinement: Breaking down deliverables into manageable chunks
- Daily stand-up meetings: Short regular meetings to track progress and resolve issues

Advantages of DSDM:

- Focuses on business needs and delivering value
- Incorporates testing and quality assurance throughout the lifecycle
- Embraces change and flexible to changing requirements
- Increases visibility and control through regular meetings and reviews

Disadvantages of DSDM:

- Can be administratively heavy due to extra processes and controls
- Relies on skilled and committed teams to be effective
- Not suited to smaller, simpler projects due to overhead
- Success depends on whole-team buy-in to principles and practices

Some mnemonics to remember DSDM:

- 'Time, Quality, Business' - Focus of DSDM
- 'Must, Should, Could, Won't' - MoSCoW prioritization
- 'Workshops, Models, Prototypes' - Key techniques

DSDM has applications in projects requiring speed and flexibility, projects with emerging requirements, and projects using agile methodologies. Its iterative and incremental approach helps address risks and uncertainties efficiently in such projects.
