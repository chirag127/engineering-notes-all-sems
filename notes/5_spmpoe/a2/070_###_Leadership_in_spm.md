### Leadership in spm

Leadership refers to the ability of an individual to influence, motivate, and enable others to contribute toward the effectiveness and success of the organizations of which they are members.

Key points about leadership in spm:

1. Leaders have a clear vision and purpose: Leaders have a clear idea of what they want to achieve and communicate it to others. They envision the future and create an ideal and unique image of where the group is headed.

2. Leaders are good communicators: They communicate the vision and goals to others clearly and concisely. They are also good listeners and encourage feedback and input from followers.

3. Leaders show confidence: They gain confidence and trust of followers through their actions and decisions. They make decisions and accept consequences. They also acknowledge their own weaknesses and limitations.

4. Leaders lead by example: They practice what they preach and serve as a role model for others. They demonstrate consistency between words and actions. This builds followers' respect and trust.

5. Leaders empower others: They delegate responsibilities and authority. They encourage and motivate followers to contribute and develop their full potential. They give followers freedom and support to work independently and make decisions.

6. Leaders continuously improve: They engage in continuous self-improvement through learning and reflecting on their experiences. They also provide feedback and opportunities for growth and development of their followers.

Mnemonics:

- Vivid vision
- Communicate clearly
- Confidence counts
- Lead by example
- Empower others
- Continuous improvement
