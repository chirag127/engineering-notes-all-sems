### Objectives of Activity Planning in spm in spm

The key objectives of activity planning in software project management (spm) are:

1. Identify and sequence project activities - The first step is to identify all the major activities required to complete the project. Then these activities are sequenced in the correct order based on their dependencies. This creates the project schedule.
2. Estimate activity durations - The duration or effort required for each activity is estimated. This is done with the help of historical data and expertise. The estimates are used to create the project schedule and budget.
3. Assign resources to activities - The required resources such as manpower, infrastructure, equipment, etc. are allocated to the various project activities as per the estimates and schedule.
4. Create the project schedule - A project schedule is created by sequencing the activities and allocating resources to them. It depicts the start and end dates of each activity and the critical path to complete the project.
5. Monitor and control - The project schedule is monitored and corrective actions are taken if required. This helps to meet project timelines and goals. Unforeseen issues are resolved through replanning as needed.

**Mnemonics:** SPAMS - Sequence, Prepare estimates, Assign resources, Make schedule, Supervise

**Learning tricks:**
- Sequence - Arrange activities step-by-step. Start with final deliverable and work backwards.
- Prepare estimates - Get estimates from experience or break down work and estimate smaller parts. Add buffers for unknowns.
- Assign resources - Allocate resources to activities based on requirements and availability. Manage resource conflicts.
- Make schedule - Create schedule by sequencing activities and assigning resources. Use diagrams or project management software.
- Supervise - Regularly monitor progress and take corrective actions. Update schedule and resources as needed.
