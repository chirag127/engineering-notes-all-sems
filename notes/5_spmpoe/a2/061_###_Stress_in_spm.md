### Stress in spm

The following points discuss stress in spm:

1. Stress is the physical and psychological tension or strain that an individual experiences due to external pressure or demands. In spm, the stress can be due to:
- Heavy curriculum and vast syllabus to cover in limited time.
- Pressure to score high grades and meet expectations.
- Competition with peers and fear of failure.
- Financial constraints to pay fees or buy study materials.
- Personal issues like family problems, relationships, health issues, etc.

2. Effects of stress:
- Mental: Anxiety, worry, depression, sleeplessness, lack of concentration.
- Physical: Headaches, nausea, fatigue, rapid heart rate, high blood pressure.
- Behavioral: Irritability, aggression, aloofness, changes in appetite or habits.

3. Coping strategies:
- Prioritize tasks and prepare timetable for organized study.
- Take breaks in between to relax the mind and body. Practice deep breathing.
- Eat healthy and exercise regularly to release pent up stress and tension.
- Discuss difficulties with parents, teachers or counselors and seek help.
- Make friends and participate in recreational activities to divert mind from stressors.
- Stay positive and practice mindfulness to reduce negative thoughts and worry.

4. If stress is persistent, seek professional help from psychologist or psychiatrist. Stress management techniques and medications can help cope with severe and prolonged stress.
