### Organizational Structures in spm

The following are the major organizational structures in spm:

1. Functional Structure:
- Groups employees by specialized function (eg. marketing, production, finance, etc.)
- Employees report to department heads who report to the CEO
- Efficient for routine tasks but can hinder cross-functional work
- Mnemonic: Functions together

2. Divisional Structure:
- Groups employees by product line, geographic region, or customer type
- Division heads report to the CEO
- Responsive to differing external factors but can lead to duplication of resources
- Mnemonic: Divided we stand

3. Matrix Structure:
- Combines functional and divisional structures
- Employees report to both a functional head and a product/project manager
- Facilitates knowledge sharing but can lead to conflict over employee responsibilities
- Mnemonic: Double the lines of reporting
