### Cost Monitoring in spm

- Cost monitoring is the process of tracking and controlling the costs incurred in a project. It is an important task in project management to ensure the project is completed within the approved budget.
- Some key steps in cost monitoring:
- Collect actual cost incurred vs planned cost - Get the actual costs incurred for each activity and resource and compare it with the planned budget to identify any variances.
- Analyze variances - Study the reasons for variances, whether due to inefficiencies or changes in scope. Take corrective actions if required to control costs.
- Forecast final cost - Based on the actual performance and variances, forecast the final cost of the project and check if it is within the approved budget. If not, propose mitigation plans.
- Report status - Regularly report cost status to stakeholders and management. This helps identify and resolve any cost issues early.

Mnemonics:
- CAPCC - Collect, Analyze, Forecast, Corrective action, Communicate
- ACVAR - Actual, Planned, Variance, Analysis, Resolution

Advantages:
- Helps complete projects within budget
- Identifies inefficient areas and takes timely actions
- Informs stakeholders and management about project cost health

Disadvantages:
- Involves effort and cost to monitor and track actual costs
- Accuracy of forecasts depends on the amount and quality of data collected

Examples:
- Creating cost reports, cost variance reports, earned value reports, etc. to track actual costs vs planned budget.
- Regular meetings to analyze variances and take corrective actions.
- Updating the overall project cost estimate based on current performance.

Applications:
- All projects where managing costs is important
- Large and complex projects where intensive cost monitoring is necessary
- Projects with tight budgets or cost constraints
