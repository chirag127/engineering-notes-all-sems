## Unit - 1 - Project Evaluation and Project Planning

### Project Evaluation
- Evaluate existing systems and software to determine feasibility of new projects
- Determine if a project is worth the investment of time and money
- Consider technical, economic and operational factors
- Examples: Cost-benefit analysis, return on investment

**Mnemonic**: CAN I afford a new project? (Consider technical, economic and operational factors)

### Project Planning
- Develop a systematic approach to project development
- Examples: Gantt charts, critical path method, project evaluation and review technique (PERT)
- Define project scope, cost, schedule and resource requirements
- Manage risk and quality
- Project planning involves progressive elaboration

**Trick**: Remember the project management triangle: Scope, Cost, Time. Trade-off one factor for another.

**Advantages**: Increased probability of success, meets stakeholder expectations, handles limited resources
**Disadvantages**: Time-consuming, plan can become outdated, lack of flexibility

**Applications**: Software/website development, construction, product launches, etc.
