### Dispersed and Virtual Teams in spm

A dispersed team is one where team members are geographically separated and have to collaborate using communication technologies.
A virtual team is one in which members rarely or never meet face to face, communicating and collaborating instead through technology.

Advantages:
- Access to wider range of skills and expertise. team members can be recruited from a wider area.
- often lower costs (reduced office space, less travel).
- Increased productivity due to 24/7 work cycle (time differences allow work to progress around the clock).

Disadvantages:
- Additional challenges in communication and coordination. Need to make extra effort to communicate frequently and clearly.
- Isolation and lack of social interaction can reduce team cohesion and satisfaction.
- Technical issues can disrupt team working and tasks. Reliance on technology brings risks of systems failures.

Mnemonics:
- VIRTUAL - Very Isolated, Really Technical, Ultra Long-distance
- DISPERSED - Distance Induces Stress, Poor Communication Exacerbates Relationship Strain, Every System Failure Risks work Effort and Schedule Delays

Tips for success:
- Invest in communications infrastructure and technology training.
- Promote frequent interaction through video conferencing, shared workspaces, instant messaging, etc.
- Clearly define goals, schedules, responsibilities and measures of success.
- Build team cohesion through team-building activities (even if virtual).
- Have a strong leadership presence to hold the team together.

Examples:
- Global software development teams
- International research collaborations
- Customer service teams with global coverage
- Globally distributed workforce of a multinational corporation
