### Working in Teams in spm

Working in teams is an important skill that is evaluated in spm examinations. Here are some key points to keep in mind while working in teams:

1. Divide work evenly: The work should be divided evenly among team members based on their strengths and interests. This ensures equal workload and utilization of diverse skills.

2. Communicate effectively: Effective communication is vital for teamwork. Teams should communicate clearly, frequently and provide constructive feedback to achieve the common goal.

3. Resolve conflicts: Conflicts are inevitable in teams. Teams should handle conflicts in a professional manner through discussions and compromise rather than avoiding them.

4. Share responsibilities: Teams should not depend on a single member to complete the work. Responsibilities should be shared to enable learning, growth and healthy dependence on each other.

5. Meet deadlines: Teams should set interim deadlines and milestones to complete the work on time while maintaining good quality. Late submission severely impacts performance.

6. Review and revise: Teams should set time aside to review the work done and make required revisions or improvements to produce the best possible output.

7. Value inputs: Teams should value inputs and efforts put in by all members. Appropriate credit should be given to individuals while submitting group work.

Some mnemonics to remember tips for teamwork:

- DIVIDE: Divide work evenly
- COMMUNICATE: Communicate effectively
- RESOLVE: Resolve conflicts
- SHARE: Share responsibilities
- MEET: Meet deadlines
- REVIEW: Review and revise
- VALUE: Value inputs
