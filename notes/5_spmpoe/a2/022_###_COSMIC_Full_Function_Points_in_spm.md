### COSMIC Full Function Points in spm

COSMIC stands for Communication, Organization, Sequencing, Monitoring, Internal processing, and Control, which are the 6 full function points in Sprague-Marquardt Model of Student Cognitive Processing. These full function points help in understanding how students perceive, interpret, store, retrieve and use information and direct thinking to achieve their goals.

Communication: This deals with how students encode and decode information. Mnemonics: C for Comm, think of communication as sending and receiving coded information or messages.

Organization: This deals with how students arrange and structure information. Mnemonics: O for Org, think of organization as ordering information in a logical sequence.

Sequencing: This deals with how students order information and integrate past and present information. Mnemonics: S for Seq, think of sequencing as following a step-by-step logical order.

Monitoring: This deals with how students oversee, regulate and assess their own cognitive processing. Mnemonics: M for Mon, think of monitoring as managing your thinking to evaluate how you are learning or performing a task.

Internal processing: This deals with how students mentally manipulate and transform information. Mnemonics: I for Int, think of internal processing as internal manipulation of information through imagining, summarizing, comparing, etc.

Control: This deals with how students direct their attention and cognitive resources. Mnemonics: C for Con, think of control as consciously controlling your thinking by focusing attention, allocating mental effort, etc.

To learn and understand the 6 full function points in depth, it is helpful to understand the definitions, examples and applications of each point. Detailed diagrams or codes are not required to learn them but can be included if helpful. The mnemonics can make them easy to remember. Understanding how these function points work together in the learning process can help in studying strategies and techniques. The full function point model provides a framework to understand and improve students' cognitive processing.
