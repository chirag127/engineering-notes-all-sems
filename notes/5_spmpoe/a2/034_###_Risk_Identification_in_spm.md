### Risk Identification in spm

Risk identification is the first step in the risk management process. It involves identifying the potential risks that could affect the project objectives. Some key points to keep in mind for risk identification in spm are:

1. Brainstorming: Conduct brainstorming sessions with the project team and stakeholders to identify as many risks as possible. This helps bring in different perspectives and experiences to identify a comprehensive list of risks.
2. Documentation review: Review project documents like the business case, requirements, design, etc. to identify risks related to assumptions, constraints, technical challenges, etc.
3. Expert judgment: Consult experts in the project domain or those who have experience with similar projects. Their experience and knowledge can help uncover risks that may not be apparent to the core project team.
4. Diagramming techniques: Use diagramming techniques like flowcharts, process maps, etc. to visually identify potential risks in the processes and interactions. This may reveal risks that can be missed in a text-based analysis.
5. Risk categories: Identify risks under major categories like technical, cost, schedule, quality, resources, etc. This can help ensure that no category of risks is missed out during risk identification.

**Mnemonics:**

- **OPPORTUNITY:** A mnemonic to cover key risk categories - Organizational, Project, Process, Objective, Resources, Technology, User, Interface, Timelines, and External events.
- **SWOT:** Analyzing strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats can uncover risks related to internal and external factors.

**Tips:**

- Be specific while identifying risks. Vague terms like 'delay' or 'issue' are not useful. Clearly define the potential event and impact.
- Focus on risks, not issues. Issues are problems that have already occurred whereas risks are future uncertainties that may or may not occur.
- Risk identification is an ongoing process. New risks may emerge or the likelihood/impact of identified risks may change as the project progresses. Continue risk monitoring and identification throughout the project lifecycle.
