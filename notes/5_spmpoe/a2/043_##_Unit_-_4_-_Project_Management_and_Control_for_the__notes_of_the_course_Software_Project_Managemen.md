## Unit - 4 - Project Management and Control

- Project monitoring and control involves tracking the project's progress, monitoring the budget and schedule, managing changes, and taking corrective action as needed.
- Some key control techniques are:
-- Status review meetings: Regular meetings to review the project status, identify any issues, and take corrective actions.
-- Milestone tracking: Tracking key milestones and deliverables to monitor progress. Missing milestones indicate potential issues that need to be addressed.
-- Earned value management (EVM): A method to measure project performance by comparing the amount of work planned to be done versus what was actually done to estimate costs and timelines.
-- Variance analysis: Analyzing differences between planned and actual results to identify issues early and take corrective actions.
-- Change management: Having a formal process to manage changes to the project scope, budget, or schedule. Each change is evaluated for impact and approved before implementation.

Some tips for effective project control:

- Define clear metrics and thresholds to measure progress and trigger corrective actions.
- Track major milestones and deliverables, not every small task.
- Automate status reporting and data collection as much as possible.
- Evaluate variances early and often. Don't wait until the end of the project to identify issues.
- Communicate status regularly and frequently with stakeholders.
- Take corrective actions promptly to get the project back on track.
