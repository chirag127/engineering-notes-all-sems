### Stepwise Project Planning in spm

The stepwise project planning in spm involves the following steps:

1. Understanding the project objective: The first and foremost step is to understand the exact objective and requirements of the project. All the stakeholders must be clear about the final outcome expected from the project.

2. Feasibility study: After understanding the project objective, a feasibility study is conducted to check whether the project is practically viable and can be accomplished with the available resources and constraints. The feasibility study analyzes the operational, technical and economic feasibility of the project.

3. Planning the project: If the feasibility study gives a green signal, then the project planning is done. The major tasks involved in project planning are:

- Breaking down the major project objective into smaller, manageable sub-tasks.
- Estimating the time and cost required to complete each sub-task.
- Preparing the project schedule by properly sequencing the sub-tasks.
- Assigning responsibilities to team members.
- Planning for resources and budget.

4. Execution and monitoring: The project plan is then put into action and the project work starts. The project progress is closely monitored and controlled. Regular reviews are done and corrective actions are taken whenever required.

5. Project closure: Once all the project work is completed as per the plan, the final step is to close the project. A final review or audit is done and the end results are evaluated. The team is dispersed and the completed project is handed over to the client.

Some learning tricks to remember the stepwise project planning process:

- UNDERSTAND - FEASIBILITY - PLAN - EXECUTE - CLOSE
- Uncover the need, Check feasibility, Make the plan, Get it done, Complete and close

The stepwise planning gives a systematic approach to project management and increases the chances of project success. It helps to complete the project within the constraints of time, cost and resources.
