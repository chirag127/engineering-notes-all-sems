### Sequencing and Scheduling in spm

Sequencing and scheduling are important aspects of project management. Some key points about sequencing and scheduling in spm are:

1. Sequencing - Determining the order of the project activities. The sequence of activities is crucial as the completion of one activity may be dependent on the completion of another activity. There are dependencies between activities which must be considered while sequencing the activities. Some common types of dependencies are:

- Finish-to-start (FS): Subsequent activity can start only after the completion of the previous activity.
- Start-to-start (SS): Subsequent activity can start only after the start of the previous activity.
- Finish-to-finish (FF): Subsequent activity can finish only after the completion of the previous activity.

2. Scheduling - Assigning start and finish dates to the project activities. The schedule is created based on the sequence of activities and the estimates of the resources and time required for each activity. Some key steps in scheduling are:

- Estimating the duration and resource requirements of activities.
- Determining the schedule constraints such as deadlines.
- Allocating resources to the activities.
- Calculating the start and finish dates of the activities based on dependencies and resource availability.
- Monitoring the schedule and making adjustments as required.

Some advantages of creating a proper schedule are:

- It gives a structured plan of the project with deadlines for each activity.
- It helps in estimating the time and cost required to complete the project.
- It aids in monitoring the progress and adjusting the schedule as required.
- It ensures that the desired outcomes are delivered on time while utilizing the resources efficiently.

Some disadvantages of a rigid schedule are:

- It may be unrealistic if not prepared properly based on accurate estimates and assumptions.
- It leaves little room for adjustments which may be required due to uncertainties.
- It may lead to rushed work and low quality output in order to meet tight deadlines.

 Mnemonics and learning tricks:
 - For sequencing: "Finish first, then Start" (FF-SS)
 - For scheduling: "Estimate, allocate, calculate, monitor"
