### Communications Genres in spm

The following are the major communication genres tested in SPM:

1. Speech
- Prepared speech: Students are given a topic and asked to prepare and deliver a speech within a time limit.
- Impromptu speech: Students are given a broad topic or issue and asked to deliver a speech without preparation.

Learning tips:
- Memorize a structure for speeches (e.g. Introduction, Body, Conclusion)
- Practise delivering speeches in front of a mirror or to friends and family
- Prepare for common speech topics (e.g. environment, school life, technology)
- For impromptu speeches, have a bank of examples and stories ready that can be adapted to different topics

2. Report
- Formal report: Students are given data or findings and asked to write a structured report.
- Magazine/newspaper report: Students are given a scenario and asked to write a less formal report in the style of a magazine or newspaper article.

Learning tips:
- Understand the different sections of a formal report (e.g. Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion)
- Practise writing reports based on graphs, data, findings from experiments, etc.
- For less formal reports, be concise and weed out unnecessary details. Write in an engaging style with an active voice.

3. Essay
- Students are given an essay topic and asked to present a logical argument or discussion.

Learning tips:
- Have a bank of examples and evidence ready to support common essay topics
- Structure your essay with an intro paragraph, body paragraphs that flow together, and conclusion paragraph
- Present a balanced argument or discussion, addressing alternatives and counter-arguments to your position
- Use transition words to connect paragraphs and make the essay coherent
