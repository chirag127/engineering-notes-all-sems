### Objectives of Activity Planning in SPM

Activity planning is an essential process in project management as it helps to ensure that all the activities required to complete a project are identified, organized, and scheduled appropriately. The objectives of activity planning in SPM include:

1. To identify all the activities required to complete the project: The first objective of activity planning is to identify all the activities required to complete the project. This involves breaking down the project into smaller, manageable tasks that can be easily accomplished.

2. To establish the sequence of activities: Once all the activities required to complete the project have been identified, the next objective is to establish the sequence in which these activities should be carried out. This involves determining the dependencies between activities and arranging them in a logical order.

3. To estimate the duration of activities: Another objective of activity planning is to estimate the duration of each activity. This involves determining the amount of time required to complete each task, taking into account factors such as resource availability, skill level, and complexity.

4. To assign resources to activities: After the duration of each activity has been estimated, the next objective is to assign the necessary resources to each activity. This involves determining the number of staff, equipment, and materials required for each task.

5. To develop a schedule for the project: The final objective of activity planning is to develop a schedule for the project. This involves organizing the activities in a timeline, taking into account any constraints such as deadlines or resource availability. The schedule should be realistic and achievable, and should be communicated to all project stakeholders.

Mnemonics and learning tricks:

One useful mnemonic for remembering the objectives of activity planning in SPM is "I Eat Apples And Drink Smoothies." Each letter of the mnemonic corresponds to one of the objectives: Identify activities, Establish sequence, Estimate duration, Assign resources, Develop schedule. This can be a helpful way to remember the key objectives of activity planning and ensure that all necessary steps are taken to effectively manage a project.
