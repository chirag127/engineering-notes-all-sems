### Communications Genres in SPM

In SPM, communications genres refer to various types of written and spoken texts that are used to convey information, ideas, opinions, and emotions to specific audiences. These genres are important for students to understand as they form the basis of effective communication and are commonly tested in exams. Here are the main communications genres in SPM:

1. Narrative - This genre tells a story and is often used in literature. It has a beginning, middle, and end, and may have a moral or lesson. Mnemonic: "Narrative is like a novel with a story."

2. Descriptive - This genre describes a person, place, or thing in detail. It uses sensory details to create a vivid image in the reader's mind. Mnemonic: "Descriptive is like describing a picture."

3. Expository - This genre explains or informs the reader about a topic. It presents facts, statistics, and examples to support the writer's point of view. Mnemonic: "Expository is like an essay with information."

4. Argumentative - This genre presents an argument and attempts to persuade the reader to agree with the writer's point of view. It uses evidence, logic, and emotional appeals to support the argument. Mnemonic: "Argumentative is like a debate with points."

5. Reflective - This genre is a personal response to an event, experience, or idea. It uses the writer's own thoughts and feelings to explore the topic. Mnemonic: "Reflective is like reflecting on your own thoughts."

6. Speech - This genre is spoken rather than written and is used to address an audience. It may be persuasive, informative, or entertaining. Mnemonic: "Speech is like speaking to a crowd."

7. Report - This genre presents information about a topic in a factual and organized manner. It may include tables, graphs, and diagrams to illustrate the data. Mnemonic: "Report is like presenting information in a report card."

Overall, understanding these communications genres is essential for effective communication and success in exams. By remembering the mnemonics and characteristics of each genre, students can easily identify and analyze them in various texts.
