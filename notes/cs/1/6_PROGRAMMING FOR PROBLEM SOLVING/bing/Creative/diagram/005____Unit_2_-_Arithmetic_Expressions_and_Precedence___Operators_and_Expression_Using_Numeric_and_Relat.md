## Unit 2 - Arithmetic Expressions and Precedence

### Operators and Expression Using Numeric and Relational Operators

- An operator is a symbol that performs a specific operation on one or more operands.
- An operand is a value or a variable that is involved in an operation.
- An expression is a combination of operators and operands that evaluates to a single value.
- Numeric operators are used to perform arithmetic operations on numeric operands, such as addition (+), subtraction (-), multiplication (*), division (/), modulus (%), exponentiation (**), etc.
- Relational operators are used to compare two operands and return a boolean value (True or False), such as equal to (==), not equal to (!=), greater than (>), less than (<), greater than or equal to (>=), less than or equal to (<=), etc.

### Mixed Operands, Type Conversion, Logical Operators, Bit Operations

- Mixed operands are operands of different data types, such as integers, floats, strings, etc.
- Type conversion is the process of changing the data type of an operand to match the other operand or the expected result, such as int(), float(), str(), etc.
- Logical operators are used to combine two or more boolean values or expressions and return a boolean value, such as and, or, not, etc.
- Bit operations are used to manipulate the individual bits of an operand, such as bitwise and (&), bitwise or (|), bitwise xor (^), bitwise not (~), bitwise left shift (<<), bitwise right shift (>>), etc.

### Assignment Operator, Operator Precedence and Associativity

- Assignment operator (=) is used to assign a value to a variable, such as x = 10, y = x + 5, etc.
- Operator precedence is the order in which operators are evaluated in an expression, such as parentheses (), exponentiation (**), multiplication (*), division (/), modulus (%), addition (+), subtraction (-), etc.
- Associativity is the order in which operators of the same precedence are evaluated in an expression, such as left to right or right to left, such as 2 ** 3 ** 2 = 2 ** (3 ** 2) (right to left), 10 - 5 - 2 = (10 - 5) - 2 (left to right), etc.
