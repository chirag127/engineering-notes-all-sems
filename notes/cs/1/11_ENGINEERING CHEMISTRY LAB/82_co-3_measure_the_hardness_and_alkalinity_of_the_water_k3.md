To measure the hardness and alkalinity of water, the following steps can be taken:
1. Obtain a sample of the water to be tested
2. Prepare a hardness test kit (e.g. EDTA method) and an alkalinity test kit (e.g. titrimetric method)
3. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for performing the hardness test
4. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for performing the alkalinity test
5. Record the results of the tests
6. Compare the results to the permissible levels of hardness and alkalinity in water, as determined by regulatory agencies.

Hardness in water is caused by the presence of dissolved minerals, such as calcium and magnesium ions, while alkalinity is a measure of the water's ability to neutralize acids. Both hardness and alkalinity can affect the quality of the water and its suitability for various uses. The results of the tests can be used to assess the safety of the water for drinking and other purposes.
