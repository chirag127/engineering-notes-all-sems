14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution.

Determining the cell conductance of a solution is a method used to measure the ability of a solution to conduct electric current. This measurement is important for understanding the behavior of ions in solution and for determining the concentration of ions in a sample. Cell conductance is typically measured using an instrument called a conductivity meter, which applies an electric current to the solution and measures the resulting electrical conductance. The conductance is then used to calculate the concentration of ions in the solution. The accuracy of the determination of cell conductance depends on several factors, including the quality of the instrument and the temperature and pH of the solution. The results of cell conductance measurements are often used in a variety of applications, including water treatment, industrial processes, and environmental monitoring. Overall, the determination of cell conductance is an important analytical method that provides valuable information about the chemical composition of solutions.
