Completion of a course should result in students acquiring a set of skills, knowledge, and competencies that are relevant to the course content. The objectives of a course should be clearly defined, and the assessment methods used should align with these objectives. Upon completion of a course, students should be able to demonstrate their mastery of the material through various forms of assessment, such as exams, projects, and assignments. The specific skills and competencies that students should be able to demonstrate upon completion of a course will vary depending on the course content and the learning outcomes defined by the instructor. However, some common skills and competencies that students should be able to demonstrate upon completion of a course include critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration. The ultimate goal of a course is to prepare students for future academic or professional pursuits, and the assessment methods used should reflect this goal.
