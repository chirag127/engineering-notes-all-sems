Course Objectives:

Objectives refer to specific, measurable, and time-bound goals that an individual, organization, or project aims to achieve. Objectives serve as a roadmap to guide decision-making and prioritize actions. They are used to align resources and efforts towards a common purpose and to hold individuals and organizations accountable for their results. Objectives are often developed as part of a larger strategy or plan and should be aligned with the overall mission and vision of the organization. Effective objectives are SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound. This helps to ensure that objectives are clearly defined and realistic, and that progress towards achieving them can be tracked and evaluated. Objectives play a critical role in driving success, and their development and implementation is a key aspect of effective planning and management.
