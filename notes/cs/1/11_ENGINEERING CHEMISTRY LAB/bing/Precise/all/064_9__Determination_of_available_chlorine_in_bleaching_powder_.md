# Determination of Available Chlorine in Bleaching Powder

1. **Aim**: To determine the available chlorine in the given sample of bleaching powder by the iodometric method.
2. **Principle**: Bleaching powder is commonly used as a disinfectant. The chlorine present in the bleaching powder gets reduced with time.
3. **Method**: The available chlorine present in the bleaching powder sample is determined iodometrically by treating its solution with an excess of potassium iodide solution in the acidic medium. The liberated iodine (I2) is treated with sodium thiosulphate (Na2S2O3) solution using freshly prepared starch solution as an indicator to be added near the endpoint.
4. **Calculation**: The chlorine liberated is called available chlorine, and the calculation of available chlorine is called iodometry. Take a W (gm) sample of bleaching powder; and when bleaching powder is treated with dilute acid or water, it liberates chlorine gas.
