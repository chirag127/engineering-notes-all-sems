# K3

K3 can refer to several things, including a synthetic form of vitamin K and a tax form. Here is some information on both:

1. **Vitamin K3**: Vitamin K3, also known as menadione, is a synthetic or artificially produced form of vitamin K. This is unlike the other two forms of vitamin K — vitamin K1, known as phylloquinone, and vitamin K2, called menaquinone, which occur naturally. Vitamin K3 may be converted into K2 in your liver. Vitamin K is important for blood clotting and bone health.

2. **Schedule K-3 (Form 1065)**: Schedule K-3 (Form 1065) is a tax form used by the International Department of the Treasury Internal Revenue Service to report a partner’s share of income, deductions, credits, etc.
