# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS LAB KCS

Engineering Graphics is a subject that helps students in many ways for making images, describing the shape, size, finish, color, and construction of any object . It is the language of engineers and is meant for communicating their ideas, thoughts, and designs to others .

Some of the topics that are covered in an Engineering Graphics Lab course include:
- Engineering drawing as a means of communication 
- Principles of engineering graphics 
- Freehand sketching 
- Introduction to AutoCAD and AutoCAD commands 
- Engineering drawing with AutoCAD 
- Orthographic projection 
- Lines and dimensioning 
- Reading blueprints 
- Normal, inclined, and cylindrical surfaces 
- Sectional views
