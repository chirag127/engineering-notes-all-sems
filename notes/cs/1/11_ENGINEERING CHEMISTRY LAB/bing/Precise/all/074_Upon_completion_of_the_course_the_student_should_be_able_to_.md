# Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Demonstrate an understanding of the fundamental concepts and principles of the subject matter.
- Apply the knowledge and skills acquired in the course to solve problems and make informed decisions.
- Communicate effectively using the language and terminology of the subject matter.
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
- Demonstrate the ability to think critically and creatively.
- Show an awareness of the ethical, social, and environmental implications of the subject matter.
- Engage in self-directed learning and take responsibility for their own learning.
- Demonstrate the ability to use technology effectively in the learning process.
