## Course Objectives:

1. To provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. To develop critical thinking and analytical skills.
3. To enhance the ability to communicate effectively, both orally and in writing.
4. To foster an appreciation for the subject and its relevance to the real world.
5. To prepare students for further study or professional development in the field.
6. To encourage independent learning and self-motivation.
7. To promote collaboration and teamwork.
8. To provide opportunities for practical application of knowledge and skills.
9. To instill ethical and professional values.
10. To assess student learning and provide feedback for improvement.
