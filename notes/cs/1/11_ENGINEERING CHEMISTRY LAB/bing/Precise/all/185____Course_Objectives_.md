## Course Objectives:

1. To provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. To equip students with the necessary skills and knowledge to apply the concepts learned in real-life situations.
3. To encourage critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
4. To foster a deep appreciation for the subject and its relevance in the world.
5. To prepare students for further study or career opportunities in the field.
6. To promote active engagement and participation in the learning process.
7. To facilitate the development of effective communication and collaboration skills.
8. To provide a supportive and inclusive learning environment that caters to diverse learning needs.
9. To assess student progress and provide timely feedback to support their learning journey.
10. To encourage lifelong learning and continuous professional development.
