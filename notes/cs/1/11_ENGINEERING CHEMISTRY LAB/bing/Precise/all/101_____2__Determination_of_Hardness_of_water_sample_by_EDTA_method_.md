### 2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method

- Hardness of water is a measure of the concentration of calcium and magnesium ions present in the water.
- The EDTA (Ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid) method is a common method used to determine the hardness of water.
- In this method, a solution of EDTA is used as a titrant to react with the calcium and magnesium ions present in the water sample.
- The end point of the titration is determined using an indicator such as Eriochrome Black T, which changes color when all the calcium and magnesium ions have reacted with the EDTA.
- The volume of EDTA solution used in the titration is then used to calculate the hardness of the water sample.
- This method is simple, accurate, and widely used in the analysis of water samples.
