# K3

- K3 is a term that can refer to several different things, depending on the context in which it is used.
- One common use of the term K3 is to refer to Vitamin K3, also known as Menadione. It is a synthetic, artificially produced form of vitamin K that doesn’t occur naturally.
- Unlike the other two forms of vitamin K — vitamin K1, known as phylloquinone, and vitamin K2, called menaquinone, Vitamin K3 may be converted into K2 in your liver.
- Vitamin K is important for blood clotting and bone health.
- Another use of the term K3 is to refer to Schedule K-3 (Form 1065) of the IRS tax forms. It is used to report a partner’s share of income, deductions, credits, etc. for international tax purposes.
