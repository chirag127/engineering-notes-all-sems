# Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator

1. Ferrous ammonium sulfate, also known as Mohr's salt, is a double salt of iron sulfate and ammonium sulfate.
2. Its chemical formula is (NH4)2Fe(SO4)2.6H2O.
3. The strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate can be determined using an external indicator.
4. An external indicator is a substance that changes color when the reaction is complete.
5. One common external indicator used in this experiment is potassium permanganate (KMnO4).
6. Potassium permanganate is a strong oxidizing agent that reacts with ferrous ions to produce ferric ions and manganese dioxide.
7. The reaction is complete when the pink color of the potassium permanganate solution disappears.
8. The strength of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution can be calculated by measuring the volume of potassium permanganate solution required to complete the reaction.
9. This method is known as a titration.
10. The strength of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution is expressed in terms of its molarity, which is the number of moles of solute per liter of solution.
