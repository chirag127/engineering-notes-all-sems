## Course Outcomes:
- Define and explain the key concepts and principles of the course.
- Demonstrate an understanding of the course material through application and analysis.
- Communicate effectively in written and oral forms.
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
- Develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
- Apply ethical principles and values to decision-making.
- Demonstrate an awareness of diverse perspectives and the ability to engage in respectful dialogue.
- Use technology effectively to access, organize, and present information.
- Demonstrate a commitment to lifelong learning and personal and professional development.
