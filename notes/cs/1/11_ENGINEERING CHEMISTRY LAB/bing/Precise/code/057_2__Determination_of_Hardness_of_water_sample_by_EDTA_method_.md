# 2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method

- Hardness of water is a measure of the concentration of calcium and magnesium ions in water.
- The EDTA (ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid) method is a common method used to determine the hardness of water.
- In this method, a solution of EDTA is used to titrate a water sample.
- The EDTA binds with the calcium and magnesium ions present in the water, forming a complex.
- An indicator such as Eriochrome Black T is used to signal the end point of the titration.
- The amount of EDTA used in the titration is proportional to the concentration of calcium and magnesium ions in the water sample.
- The hardness of the water sample can be calculated using the volume of EDTA solution used in the titration.
- This method is simple, accurate, and widely used in the determination of water hardness.
