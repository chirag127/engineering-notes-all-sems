# Preparation of Different Resins

Resins can be derived from various sources such as polymers, monomers, oils, fatty acids, and natural sources . They have a wide range of applications including paints and coatings, personal care and home care, pharmaceuticals, rubber, plastics and their coatings, electrical and jewelry articles, decorative items, and wood coatings .

There are two categories of resinous products: natural resins and prepared resins . So far, no general method has been suggested or proposed for the preparation of resins .

Different types of resins include phenolic resins, alkyd resins, epoxy resins, UV resins, polyester resins, and polyurethane resins  . The majority of resins are made up of two elements: the base resin and a catalyst (hardener). Combining the two elements results in a chemical reaction that allows the resin to set .

For example, by manipulating the structure of the linker, resins ranging from extremely acid labile to base labile can be prepared. Using linkers additionally allows the preparation of resins with special applications such as DHP resin utilized as a solid-phase support for alcohols or Weinreb resin utilized for preparing aldehydes and ketones .
