# 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder

The available chlorine present in a bleaching powder sample can be determined iodometrically by treating its solution with an excess of potassium iodide solution in an acidic medium. The liberated iodine (I2) is then treated with sodium thiosulphate (Na2S2O3) solution using freshly prepared starch solution as an indicator to be added near the endpoint.

This process is known as iodometry, and the calculation of available chlorine is called iodometric calculation. A sample of bleaching powder is taken, and when treated with dilute acid or water, it liberates chlorine gas.

The available chlorine in the given sample of bleaching powder can be determined by the iodometric method. Bleaching powder is commonly used as a disinfectant, and the chlorine present in the bleaching powder gets reduced with time.
