### 12. Preparation of Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin

Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin is a type of thermosetting polymer that is commonly used in the production of adhesives, finishes, and molded objects. Here are the steps involved in the preparation of UF resin:

1. **Reacting urea and formaldehyde:** The first step in the preparation of UF resin is the reaction of urea and formaldehyde in an aqueous solution. The molar ratio of urea to formaldehyde is typically between 1:1.5 and 1:2.5. The reaction is carried out at a pH of 7-8 and a temperature of 85-95°C.

2. **Condensation:** The next step is the condensation of the reaction mixture. This is done by heating the mixture to 100-105°C and maintaining this temperature for 1-3 hours. During this time, the water content of the mixture is reduced, and the viscosity of the mixture increases.

3. **Cooling and neutralization:** After the condensation step, the mixture is cooled to room temperature. The pH of the mixture is then adjusted to 7-8 by adding an acid or a base.

4. **Filtration:** The mixture is then filtered to remove any impurities or unreacted materials.

5. **Storage:** The resulting UF resin is stored in a cool and dry place until it is ready for use.

These are the basic steps involved in the preparation of UF resin. The exact conditions and parameters may vary depending on the specific application and desired properties of the final product.
