## Course Objectives:

1. To provide a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. To develop critical thinking and analytical skills.
3. To enhance the ability to apply theoretical concepts to practical situations.
4. To encourage independent learning and research.
5. To prepare students for further academic or professional pursuits in the field.
6. To foster an appreciation for the subject and its relevance to various aspects of life.
7. To promote effective communication and collaboration skills.
8. To instill ethical and responsible behavior in the pursuit of knowledge.
