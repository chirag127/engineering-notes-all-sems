### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

Adipic acid and paracetamol are two different compounds with different chemical structures and properties. Here is a brief overview of their preparation methods:

#### Adipic Acid
Adipic acid is an organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is a white crystalline powder that is mainly used in the production of nylon. Adipic acid can be prepared by several methods, including:

1. Oxidation of cyclohexanol or cyclohexanone with nitric acid. This is the most common industrial method for the production of adipic acid.
2. Oxidation of cyclohexene with hydrogen peroxide in the presence of a tungsten catalyst.
3. Hydrogenation of phenol to cyclohexanone, followed by oxidation with nitric acid.

#### Paracetamol
Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is an analgesic and antipyretic drug that is widely used to relieve pain and reduce fever. It is prepared by the following method:

1. Nitration of phenol to produce para-nitrophenol.
2. Reduction of para-nitrophenol to para-aminophenol.
3. Acetylation of para-aminophenol with acetic anhydride to produce paracetamol.
