Upon completion of the course, the student should be able to:

1. Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the course material.
2. Apply the knowledge and skills learned in the course to real-world situations.
3. Analyze and evaluate information related to the course material.
4. Communicate effectively, both orally and in writing, about the course material.
5. Work collaboratively with others to complete course-related tasks.
6. Demonstrate critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
7. Use technology effectively to enhance learning and complete course-related tasks.
8. Demonstrate ethical behavior and professionalism in all course-related activities.
9. Engage in continuous learning and professional development related to the course material.
