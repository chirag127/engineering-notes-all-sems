3. The rate law for the hydrolysis of esters can be written as: rate = k[ester][water], where k is the rate constant, [ester] is the concentration of the ester, and [water] is the concentration of water.
4. By measuring the rate of reaction at different concentrations of the ester and water, the rate constant k can be determined using the method of initial rates.
