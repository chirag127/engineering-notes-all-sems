# Preparation of Adipic Acid

- Adipic acid is an organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is a white crystalline powder that is used as a precursor for the production of nylon, polyurethanes, and other products of commercial interest.
- Adipic acid is produced from a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol called KA oil, the abbreviation of ketone-alcohol oil. The KA oil is oxidized with nitric acid to give adipic acid, via a multistep pathway .
- The oxidation of KA oil involves the following steps :
  - Formation of nitrosonium ion (NO+) by the reaction of nitric acid and sulfuric acid.
  - Nitrosation of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol to form nitrosocyclohexane and nitrosocyclohexanol, respectively.
  - Dehydration of nitrosocyclohexanol to form cyclohexanone oxime.
  - Rearrangement of cyclohexanone oxime to form 6-nitrosohexanone.
  - Oxidation of 6-nitrosohexanone to form 6-nitrohexanoic acid.
  - Hydrolysis of 6-nitrohexanoic acid to form adipic acid and nitrous acid.
- The overall reaction can be written as :

  ![C6H10O + 2HNO3 -> C6H10O4 + 2NO + 2H2O](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?C_6H_{10}O%20&plus;%202HNO_3%20%5Crightarrow%20C_6H_{10}O_4%20&plus;%202NO%20&plus;%202H_2O)

- The reaction is exothermic and requires careful control of temperature and pressure to avoid runaway reactions and explosions .
- The adipic acid is purified by crystallization from water and separated from the by-products such as nitrous oxide, nitric oxide, and water .
