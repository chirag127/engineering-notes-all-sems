# Course Objectives:

- To introduce the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence (AI) and its applications.
- To familiarize the students with the methods and techniques of problem solving, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, and learning in AI.
- To provide the students with the skills and tools to design and implement AI systems using various programming languages and frameworks.
- To expose the students to the current trends and challenges in AI research and development.
- To encourage the students to explore the ethical, social, and legal implications of AI in various domains and contexts.
