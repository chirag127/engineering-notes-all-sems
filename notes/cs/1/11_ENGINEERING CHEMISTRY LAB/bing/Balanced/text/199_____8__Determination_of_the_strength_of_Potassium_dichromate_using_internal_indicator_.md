### 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

- Potassium dichromate is a primary standard substance that can be used to determine the strength of a solution of an oxidizing or reducing agent.
- The internal indicator is a substance that changes color when the equivalence point is reached in a titration. It is added to the solution being titrated, not to the titrant.
- An example of an internal indicator is diphenylamine, which is used to titrate potassium dichromate against ferrous ammonium sulphate (FAS) solution.
- The procedure for the determination of the strength of potassium dichromate using internal indicator is as follows:

  - Weigh accurately about 0.2 g of potassium dichromate and dissolve it in 100 mL of distilled water in a volumetric flask. This is the standard solution of potassium dichromate.
  - Pipette out 10 mL of FAS solution into a conical flask and add 10 mL of dilute sulphuric acid and a few drops of diphenylamine indicator.
  - Fill a burette with the standard solution of potassium dichromate and note the initial reading.
  - Titrate the FAS solution with the potassium dichromate solution until a faint blue color appears in the solution. This indicates the end point of the titration. Note the final reading of the burette.
  - Repeat the titration for two more times and take the average of the readings.
  - Calculate the strength of potassium dichromate using the formula:

    - Strength of potassium dichromate = (Volume of potassium dichromate x Normality of potassium dichromate x Equivalent weight of potassium dichromate) / (Volume of FAS x Equivalent weight of FAS)
    - Normality of potassium dichromate = (Weight of potassium dichromate x 1000) / (Molecular weight of potassium dichromate x Volume of potassium dichromate)
    - Equivalent weight of potassium dichromate = Molecular weight of potassium dichromate / 6
    - Equivalent weight of FAS = Molecular weight of FAS / 1
    - Molecular weight of potassium dichromate = 294.2 g/mol
    - Molecular weight of FAS = 392.2 g/mol
