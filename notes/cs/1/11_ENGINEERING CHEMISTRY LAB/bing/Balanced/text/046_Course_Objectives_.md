# Course Objectives:

- To provide an overview of the main concepts and methods of artificial intelligence (AI).
- To introduce the basic techniques of problem solving, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, and learning in AI.
- To illustrate the applications and limitations of AI in various domains such as games, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and ethics.
- To develop the skills of designing, implementing, testing, and evaluating AI systems using Python and relevant libraries and frameworks.
- To foster the critical thinking and creativity of the students in solving real-world problems using AI.
