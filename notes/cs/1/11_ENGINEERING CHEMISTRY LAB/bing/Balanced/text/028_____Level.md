### Level

- A level is a tool or device that is used to measure or indicate whether a surface is horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb).
- A level can also refer to the position or height of something relative to a reference point or standard, such as sea level, ground level, or eye level.
- A level can also mean a degree of intensity, quality, or achievement, such as a high level of skill, a low level of difficulty, or a level of satisfaction.
- A level can also be a stage or phase in a process, system, or hierarchy, such as a level of education, a level of security, or a level of management.
- A level can also be a unit of measurement in some fields, such as sound level, blood sugar level, or energy level.
