### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Identify and explain the main concepts, methods, and applications of artificial intelligence.
- Apply basic techniques of search, knowledge representation, reasoning, planning, and machine learning to solve simple AI problems.
- Implement and evaluate AI algorithms using appropriate programming tools and frameworks.
- Analyze and compare the strengths and limitations of different AI approaches and paradigms.
- Recognize and discuss the ethical, social, and legal implications of AI systems and their impact on human society.
