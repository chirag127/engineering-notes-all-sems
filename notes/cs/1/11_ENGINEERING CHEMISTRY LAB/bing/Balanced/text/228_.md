# The topic is

- A topic is a subject or a theme that is discussed or written about.
- A topic can be general or specific, depending on the purpose and scope of the discussion or the writing.
- A topic can be chosen by the speaker, the writer, the audience, or the assignment.
- A topic can be expressed as a word, a phrase, a question, or a statement.
- A topic can be related to other topics by using categories, subtopics, examples, or comparisons.
- A topic can be developed by using facts, opinions, arguments, evidence, or examples.
- A topic can be organized by using an outline, a mind map, a graphic organizer, or a thesis statement.
- A topic can be evaluated by using criteria, feedback, revision, or editing.
