# Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

- Adipic acid is an organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2. It is the most important dicarboxylic acid, mainly used as a precursor for the production of nylon.
- Paracetamol is an organic compound with the formula C8H9NO2. It is a widely used antipyretic and analgesic drug.

## Preparation of Adipic acid

- Adipic acid is produced from a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol called KA oil, the abbreviation of ketone-alcohol oil.
- The KA oil is oxidized with nitric acid to give adipic acid, via a multistep pathway .
- The overall reaction is:

![adipic acid synthesis](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/4/4c/Adipic_acid_synthesis.png/800px-Adipic_acid_synthesis.png)

- The main steps are:

  - Nitration of KA oil to form a mixture of nitro compounds.
  - Oxidation of the nitro compounds to form a mixture of carboxylic acids.
  - Hydrolysis of the carboxylic acids to form adipic acid and other by-products.

## Preparation of Paracetamol

- Paracetamol is prepared from p-aminophenol by acetylating it with acetic anhydride in the presence of 3-4 drops of concentrated sulphuric acid as catalyst .
- The reaction is:

![paracetamol synthesis](https://labmonk.com/sites/default/files/inline-images/paracetamol%20synthesis.png)

- The mechanism is:

  - Protonation of the oxygen atom of acetic anhydride by sulphuric acid.
  - Nucleophilic attack of the nitrogen atom of p-aminophenol on the carbonyl carbon of acetic anhydride.
  - Elimination of acetic acid and formation of paracetamol.

- Paracetamol can also be prepared from phenol by a three-step synthesis involving nitration, reduction and acetylation.
