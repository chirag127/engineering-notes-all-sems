# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS LAB KCS

- Engineering graphics is the discipline of creating and interpreting graphical representations of engineering objects, such as mechanical parts, structures, circuits, etc.
- Engineering graphics lab is a practical course that teaches students how to use various tools and techniques to create and analyze engineering drawings, such as sketching, drafting, dimensioning, projection, sectioning, etc.
- Engineering graphics lab also helps students to develop spatial visualization skills, which are essential for understanding and designing three-dimensional objects and systems.
- Engineering graphics lab covers the following topics:

  - Introduction to engineering graphics and drawing instruments
  - Classical geometry and construction of curves
  - Orthographic projection and multi-view drawing
  - Isometric projection and pictorial drawing
  - Dimensioning and tolerancing
  - Sectional views and cutting planes
  - Auxiliary views and development of surfaces
  - Intersection of solids and solids of revolution
  - Computer-aided design (CAD) and drafting software
  - Engineering graphics standards and codes

- Engineering graphics lab requires students to perform various exercises and assignments using drawing sheets, drawing boards, instruments, and software. Students are expected to follow the instructions and guidelines given by the instructor and the lab manual.
- Engineering graphics lab is a core course for engineering students of various branches, such as civil, mechanical, electrical, computer science, etc. It provides the foundation for further courses in engineering design, analysis, and simulation.
