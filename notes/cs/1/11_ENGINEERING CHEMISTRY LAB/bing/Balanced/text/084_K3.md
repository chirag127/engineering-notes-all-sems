# K3

- K3 is a type of visa that allows a foreign spouse of a U.S. citizen to enter and reside in the United States while waiting for the approval of their immigrant visa petition.
- K3 visa holders are eligible to apply for a work permit and a social security number in the U.S.
- K3 visa holders can also travel outside the U.S. and re-enter with a valid visa.
- K3 visa holders must file Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status, to obtain a green card and become a permanent resident of the U.S.
- K3 visa holders can bring their children under the age of 21 to the U.S. as K4 visa holders, as long as they are unmarried and listed on the petition.
- K3 visa holders must meet the following requirements:
  - They must be legally married to a U.S. citizen.
  - They must have a pending Form I-130, Petition for Alien Relative, filed by their U.S. citizen spouse.
  - They must have a valid passport and a medical examination.
  - They must have a police clearance certificate and proof of their relationship with their U.S. citizen spouse.
  - They must not be inadmissible to the U.S. for any reason, such as criminal history, health issues, or immigration violations.
- K3 visa processing time varies depending on the country of origin, the U.S. consulate, and the availability of documents. It can take from a few months to over a year to obtain a K3 visa.
