# Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

## Adipic acid

- Adipic acid is the organic compound with the formula (CH2)4(COOH)2.
- It is the most important dicarboxylic acid, mainly used as a precursor for the production of nylon.
- Adipic acid is produced from a mixture of cyclohexanone and cyclohexanol called KA oil, the abbreviation of ketone-alcohol oil.
- The KA oil is oxidized with nitric acid to give adipic acid, via a multistep pathway.

The steps are:

1. Oxidation of KA oil to a mixture of cyclohexanone oxime and cyclohexanol nitrate.
2. Rearrangement of cyclohexanone oxime to caprolactam, which is hydrolyzed to 6-aminohexanoic acid.
3. Oxidation of cyclohexanol nitrate to adipic acid and nitrous oxide.

## Paracetamol

- Paracetamol is an antipyretic and analgesic drug with the formula C8H9NO2.
- It is derived from p-aminophenol by acetylation with acetic anhydride in the presence of concentrated sulfuric acid as a catalyst.
- The reaction is:

![paracetamol synthesis](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Paracetamol_synthesis.svg/800px-Paracetamol_synthesis.svg.png)

- Paracetamol is available as tablets, capsules, syrups, suspensions, and injections.
- It is used to treat mild to moderate pain and fever.
