# K3 Surface

A K3 surface is a type of algebraic surface that has some special properties. Here are some points to know about K3 surfaces:

- A K3 surface is a smooth proper geometrically connected algebraic surface that has trivial canonical bundle and no non-zero global holomorphic 2-forms .
- A K3 surface is a minimal surface of Kodaira dimension zero, meaning that it has no curves of negative self-intersection and no non-trivial holomorphic vector fields .
- A K3 surface is simply connected, meaning that it has no non-trivial loops that can be shrunk to a point .
- A K3 surface has 24 singular points, called the nodes, which are isolated ordinary double points .
- A K3 surface has a rich geometry and topology, and it plays an important role in the study of smooth 4-manifolds, mirror symmetry, string theory, and arithmetic geometry  .
- A K3 surface can be constructed in various ways, such as by taking a smooth quartic surface in projective 3-space, or by taking a Kummer surface associated to an abelian surface  .
