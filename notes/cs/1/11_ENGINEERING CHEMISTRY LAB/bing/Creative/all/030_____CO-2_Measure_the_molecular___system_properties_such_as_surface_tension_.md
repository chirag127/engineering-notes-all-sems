# CO2: Molecular and System Properties

## Molecular Properties

- CO2 is a linear molecule with a bond angle of 180 degrees and a bond length of 116.3 pm.
- CO2 has a molecular weight of 44.0095 g/mol and a molar volume of 22.26 L/mol at STP.
- CO2 is a non-polar molecule with a dipole moment of zero and a quadrupole moment of 4.67 x 10^-40 C m^2.
- CO2 has a vibrational spectrum with three normal modes: symmetric stretching, bending, and asymmetric stretching.
- CO2 has a rotational spectrum with two allowed transitions: J -> J+1 and J -> J-1, where J is the rotational quantum number.

## System Properties

- CO2 is a gas at standard temperature and pressure, but it can be liquefied or solidified under certain conditions of temperature and pressure.
- CO2 has a critical point at 304.25 K and 73.8 bar, and a triple point at 216.58 K and 5.185 bar.
- CO2 has a density of 1.977 kg/m^3 at STP, and it varies with temperature and pressure according to the equation of state.
- CO2 has a surface tension of 0.0126 N/m at its boiling point of 194.7 K and 1 atm, and it decreases with increasing temperature and pressure .
- CO2 has a viscosity of 0.0148 mPa s at STP, and it increases with increasing pressure and decreases with increasing temperature.
- CO2 has a specific heat capacity of 0.844 kJ/kg K at STP, and it varies with temperature and pressure according to the equation of state.
- CO2 has a thermal conductivity of 0.0166 W/m K at STP, and it increases with increasing temperature and pressure.
