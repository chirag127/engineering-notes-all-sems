# CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

- Analytical instruments are devices that are used to measure, analyze, or identify the chemical, physical, or biological properties of a sample or a substance.
- Analytical instruments have various applications in different fields, such as pharmaceutical, food-processing, chemical, clinical, environmental, forensic, and life science research.
- Analytical instruments can be classified into different types based on the principle or technique they use, such as spectroscopy, mass spectrometry, electrochemical analysis, thermal analysis, separation analysis, microscopy, and hybrid techniques.
- Some examples of analytical instruments are:

  - Refractometer: measures the refractive index of a liquid or a solid sample, which is related to its concentration, purity, or composition.
  - Spectrophotometer: measures the amount of light absorbed or transmitted by a sample at different wavelengths, which can be used to determine its concentration, structure, or identity.
  - Electrochemical instrument: measures the electrical properties of a sample, such as its potential, current, resistance, or conductivity, which can be used to determine its redox state, pH, or concentration of ions or molecules.
  - Calorimeter: measures the amount of heat released or absorbed by a sample during a chemical or physical process, which can be used to determine its enthalpy, entropy, or specific heat capacity.
  - Automatic density meter: measures the density or specific gravity of a liquid or a solid sample, which can be used to determine its purity, composition, or quality.
  - Conductivity meter: measures the ability of a sample to conduct electricity, which can be used to determine its concentration, salinity, or quality.
  - Colony counter: counts the number of microbial colonies on a culture plate, which can be used to determine the microbial load, growth rate, or viability of a sample.
  - Automatic titrator: performs a titration automatically by adding a titrant to a sample and measuring the change in a parameter, such as pH, color, or potential, which can be used to determine the concentration, acidity, or alkalinity of a sample.
  - Fiberscope: uses a flexible optical fiber to transmit light and images from a remote location, which can be used to inspect, diagnose, or treat inaccessible areas or organs.
  - Demagnetizer: removes the magnetic field from a sample or a device, which can be used to prevent interference, damage, or data loss.
  - Gas chromatography-mass spectrometry (GC-MS): combines gas chromatography and mass spectrometry to separate and identify the components of a complex mixture based on their volatility and mass-to-charge ratio.
  - High-performance liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry (HPLC-MS): combines high-performance liquid chromatography and mass spectrometry to separate and identify the components of a complex mixture based on their polarity and mass-to-charge ratio.
