# 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route.

- Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is used as a raw material for the production of nylon-6,6, a synthetic polymer widely used in various applications.
- Paracetamol is an analgesic and antipyretic drug that is commonly used to relieve pain and fever.
- Conventional and green routes are two different approaches for the synthesis of organic compounds, based on the environmental impact, energy consumption, and waste generation of the processes.
- Conventional routes are the traditional methods that use fossil fuels, harsh chemicals, high temperature and pressure, and generate toxic by-products and emissions.
- Green routes are the alternative methods that use renewable resources, benign reagents, mild conditions, and minimize waste and pollution.

## Synthesis of adipic acid by conventional and green route

- The conventional route for the synthesis of adipic acid is the nitric acid oxidation of cyclohexane, cyclohexanol, or cyclohexanone, which are derived from benzene, a fossil fuel. This process requires high temperature and pressure, and produces large amounts of nitrous oxide (N2O), a greenhouse gas, as a by-product.
- The green route for the synthesis of adipic acid is the direct oxidation of cyclohexene, which can be obtained from glucose, a renewable resource. This process uses hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) as an oxidant, and sodium tungstate (Na2WO4) as a catalyst, in a microemulsion system. This process operates at low temperature and pressure, and produces pure adipic acid in high yield, without any harmful by-products. The catalyst and the surfactant used for the microemulsion can be recycled and reused.

## Synthesis of paracetamol by conventional and green route

- The conventional route for the synthesis of paracetamol is the acetylation of 4-aminophenol, which is derived from phenol, a fossil fuel. This process uses acetic anhydride (CH3CO)2O as an acetylating agent, and produces acetic acid (CH3COOH) as a by-product. This process requires high temperature and pressure, and generates waste and pollution.
- The green route for the synthesis of paracetamol is the acetylation of 4-aminophenol, which can be obtained from glucose, a renewable resource. This process uses acetic acid (CH3COOH) as an acetylating agent, and sodium acetate (CH3COONa) as a catalyst, in a microwave-assisted reaction. This process operates at low temperature and pressure, and produces paracetamol in high yield, without any harmful by-products. The catalyst can be recovered and reused.
