# Level

- A level is a tool or device used to measure or indicate whether a surface is horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb).
- A level consists of a horizontal tube or vial filled with a liquid (usually water, alcohol, or oil) and an air bubble that moves when the level is tilted.
- A level is used to align or adjust objects such as shelves, picture frames, furniture, or construction materials.
- A level can also be a digital or electronic device that uses sensors or lasers to measure the angle or inclination of a surface.
- A level can also be a unit of measurement or a standard of comparison, such as a skill level, a difficulty level, or a water level.
