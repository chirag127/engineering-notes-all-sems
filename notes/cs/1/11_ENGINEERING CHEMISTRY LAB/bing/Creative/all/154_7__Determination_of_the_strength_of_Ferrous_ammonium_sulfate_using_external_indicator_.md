# Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator

- Ferrous ammonium sulfate, also known as Mohr's salt, is a light green crystalline salt with the formula (NH4)2Fe(SO4)2.6H2O. It is used as a reducing agent in redox titrations.
- Potassium dichromate, K2Cr2O7, is a strong oxidizing agent that can oxidize ferrous ions to ferric ions in acidic medium. It has a bright orange color and forms a green chromium(III) salt after the reaction.
- Potassium ferricyanide, K3[Fe(CN)6], is a red-brown compound that can form a blue precipitate of Prussian blue with ferric ions. It is used as an external indicator in the titration of ferrous ammonium sulfate and potassium dichromate.
- The principle of the titration is based on the following redox reaction:

  6Fe2+ + Cr2O7^2- + 14H+ -> 6Fe3+ + 2Cr3+ + 7H2O

- The end point of the titration is detected by adding a drop of potassium ferricyanide solution on a white tile after each addition of potassium dichromate. When the blue color of Prussian blue appears, the titration is complete.
- The procedure of the titration is as follows:

  - Prepare a standard solution of potassium dichromate by dissolving a known mass of the salt in distilled water and making up the volume in a volumetric flask.
  - Pipette out a known volume of ferrous ammonium sulfate solution into a conical flask and add some dilute sulfuric acid to acidify the solution.
  - Fill a burette with the standard potassium dichromate solution and note the initial reading.
  - Titrate the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution with the potassium dichromate solution, adding a drop of potassium ferricyanide solution on a white tile after each addition of potassium dichromate.
  - Stop the titration when the blue color of Prussian blue appears on the white tile and note the final burette reading.
  - Repeat the titration for at least two more times and take the average of the concordant readings.
  - Calculate the strength of the ferrous ammonium sulfate solution using the formula:

    N1V1 = N2V2

    where N1 and N2 are the normalities of the ferrous ammonium sulfate and potassium dichromate solutions, and V1 and V2 are the volumes of the ferrous ammonium sulfate and potassium dichromate solutions used in the titration.
