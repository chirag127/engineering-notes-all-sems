# Level

- A level is a tool or device used to measure or indicate whether a surface is horizontal (level) or vertical (plumb).
- A level consists of a horizontal tube or vial filled with a liquid (usually water or alcohol) and an air bubble that moves when the level is tilted.
- A level is used to align or adjust objects such as shelves, picture frames, tables, walls, floors, etc. to ensure that they are parallel or perpendicular to the ground or another reference plane.
- A level can also be used to measure angles or slopes by reading the position of the bubble relative to the markings on the tube or vial.
- There are different types of levels, such as spirit levels, bubble levels, laser levels, digital levels, etc. that vary in size, shape, accuracy, and functionality.
