# Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Define the basic concepts and terminology of artificial intelligence, such as agents, environments, rationality, search, knowledge representation, inference, planning, learning, natural language processing, computer vision, etc.
- Explain the advantages and limitations of different types of artificial intelligence techniques, such as heuristic search, adversarial search, constraint satisfaction, logic, probabilistic reasoning, neural networks, genetic algorithms, etc.
- Apply appropriate artificial intelligence techniques to solve various problems, such as game playing, scheduling, optimization, classification, regression, clustering, etc.
- Implement and evaluate artificial intelligence algorithms using programming languages and tools, such as Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.
- Analyze and compare the performance and complexity of different artificial intelligence algorithms, such as time, space, accuracy, scalability, robustness, etc.
- Critically assess the ethical and social implications of artificial intelligence, such as fairness, accountability, transparency, privacy, safety, etc.
