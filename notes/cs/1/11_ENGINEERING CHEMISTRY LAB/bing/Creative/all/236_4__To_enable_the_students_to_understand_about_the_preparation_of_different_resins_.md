# Preparation of Different Resins

Resins are organic substances that are solid or semi-solid at room temperature and can be dissolved in organic solvents. Resins have various applications in industries such as paints, coatings, adhesives, plastics, rubber, electrical, jewelry, etc. Resins can be classified into two categories: natural resins and synthetic resins.

## Natural Resins

Natural resins are obtained from plants or animals. They are usually mixtures of terpenes, phenols, acids, and other compounds. Some examples of natural resins are:

- Rosin: a brittle resin obtained from pine trees. It is used as a flux in soldering, a varnish ingredient, a violin bow lubricant, etc.
- Shellac: a resin secreted by the lac insect. It is used as a wood finish, a food glaze, a nail polish, etc.
- Amber: a fossilized resin from ancient coniferous trees. It is used as a gemstone, a jewelry material, a source of ambergris, etc.
- Copal: a resin from various tropical trees. It is used as a varnish, a perfume ingredient, an incense, etc.
- Dragon's blood: a red resin from various plants. It is used as a dye, a medicine, a ritual substance, etc.

## Synthetic Resins

Synthetic resins are artificially produced by chemical reactions. They are usually polymers or oligomers of monomers, oils, fatty acids, etc. Some examples of synthetic resins are:

- Epoxy resin: a thermosetting resin formed by the reaction of epoxides and amines. It is used as an adhesive, a coating, a casting material, a composite matrix, etc.
- UV resin: a resin that cures under ultraviolet light. It is used as a jewelry material, a craft material, a dental material, etc.
- Polyester resin: a thermosetting resin formed by the reaction of polyols and acids. It is used as a fiber, a fabric, a plastic, a boat material, etc.
- Polyurethane resin: a thermosetting resin formed by the reaction of polyols and isocyanates. It is used as a foam, a rubber, a coating, a sealant, etc.
- Phenolic resin: a thermosetting resin formed by the reaction of phenols and aldehydes. It is used as a resin impregnation, a brake lining, an electrical component, a laminate, etc.
- Alkyd resin: a thermoplastic resin formed by the reaction of polyols and acids. It is used as a paint, a varnish, a coating, a resin modifier, etc.
