# Acid and Paracetamol by Conventional and Green Route

- Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a common painkiller and antipyretic drug that is derived from phenol.
- There are different methods to synthesize paracetamol from phenol, which can be classified as conventional and green routes.
- Conventional routes involve multiple steps, use of hazardous reagents and solvents, and generate by-products and waste.
- Green routes aim to reduce the environmental impact of the synthesis by using fewer steps, safer reagents and solvents, and minimizing by-products and waste.

## Conventional Route

- One of the conventional routes to synthesize paracetamol from phenol is as follows :

1. Nitration of phenol with nitric acid to form p-nitrophenol.
2. Reduction of p-nitrophenol with tin and hydrochloric acid to form p-aminophenol.
3. Acetylation of p-aminophenol with acetic anhydride to form paracetamol.

![Conventional route](https://i.imgur.com/8JZg7aG.png)

- The drawbacks of this route are:

  - It involves three steps, each requiring heating, stirring, and purification.
  - It uses nitric acid, which is corrosive and toxic, and generates nitrous oxide, which is a greenhouse gas.
  - It uses tin, which is expensive and scarce, and generates tin chloride, which is a hazardous waste.
  - It uses acetic anhydride, which is flammable and irritant, and generates acetic acid, which is a waste.

## Green Route

- One of the green routes to synthesize paracetamol from phenol is as follows :

1. Direct amidation of phenol with acetic acid and ammonium acetate in the presence of a zeolite catalyst under microwave irradiation to form paracetamol.

![Green route](https://i.imgur.com/0Q0aZ8x.png)

- The advantages of this route are:

  - It involves only one step, which is fast and efficient under microwave irradiation.
  - It uses acetic acid and ammonium acetate, which are cheap and biodegradable, and do not generate any waste.
  - It uses a zeolite catalyst, which is reusable and environmentally friendly, and does not leach any metal ions.
  - It does not use any organic solvent, which reduces the risk of fire and pollution.
