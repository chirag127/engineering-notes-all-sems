### 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution

- Cell conductance is the measure of the ability of a solution to conduct electric current through it.
- Cell conductance depends on the concentration, type and mobility of the ions present in the solution, as well as the temperature and geometry of the cell.
- Cell conductance is measured by applying an alternating current (AC) of known frequency and amplitude to the cell and measuring the resulting voltage drop across the cell.
- Cell conductance is related to the cell resistance by the equation: `G = 1/R`, where `G` is the cell conductance, `R` is the cell resistance, and `1` is the unit of conductance (siemens or S).
- Cell conductance is also related to the specific conductance or conductivity of the solution by the equation: `G = k x A / l`, where `k` is the specific conductance, `A` is the cross-sectional area of the cell, and `l` is the length of the cell.
- To determine the cell conductance of a solution, the following steps are followed:

  - Prepare a standard solution of known specific conductance, such as potassium chloride (KCl).
  - Rinse the cell with distilled water and then with the standard solution.
  - Fill the cell with the standard solution and connect it to the conductance meter.
  - Adjust the frequency and amplitude of the AC source to obtain a stable reading on the meter.
  - Record the cell resistance and calculate the cell constant using the equation: `C = k x R`, where `C` is the cell constant and `k` is the specific conductance of the standard solution.
  - Rinse the cell with distilled water and then with the unknown solution.
  - Fill the cell with the unknown solution and connect it to the conductance meter.
  - Adjust the frequency and amplitude of the AC source to obtain a stable reading on the meter.
  - Record the cell resistance and calculate the cell conductance using the equation: `G = 1/R`.
  - Repeat the procedure for different concentrations of the unknown solution and plot a graph of cell conductance versus concentration.
  - Analyze the graph to determine the relationship between cell conductance and concentration for the unknown solution.
