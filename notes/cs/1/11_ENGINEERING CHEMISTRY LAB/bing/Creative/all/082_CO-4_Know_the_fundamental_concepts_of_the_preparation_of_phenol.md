# CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol

Phenol is an aromatic compound with a hydroxyl group (-OH) attached to a benzene ring. It is also known as carbolic acid or phenic acid. Phenol has antiseptic and disinfectant properties and is used in the manufacture of plastics, dyes, explosives, and drugs.

There are several methods of preparation of phenol, which can be classified into two categories: laboratory methods and industrial methods.

## Laboratory methods of preparation of phenol

- **From haloarenes:** Haloarenes are aromatic compounds with a halogen atom (-X) attached to a benzene ring. Examples of haloarenes are chlorobenzene, bromobenzene, and iodobenzene. When a haloarene is fused with sodium hydroxide at high temperature and pressure, sodium phenoxide is produced. Sodium phenoxide is then acidified with dilute acid or carbon dioxide to give phenol. This reaction is known as the Dow process or the Fries process  .

  - Example: Chlorobenzene + Sodium hydroxide → Sodium phenoxide + Sodium chloride
  - Sodium phenoxide + Dilute acid → Phenol + Water

- **From benzene sulphonic acid:** Benzene sulphonic acid is an aromatic compound with a sulphonic acid group (-SO3H) attached to a benzene ring. It is formed by the sulphonation of benzene with concentrated sulphuric acid. When the sodium salt of benzene sulphonic acid is fused with sodium hydroxide at high temperature, sodium phenoxide is produced. Sodium phenoxide is then acidified with dilute acid or carbon dioxide to give phenol  .

  - Example: Benzene + Concentrated sulphuric acid → Benzene sulphonic acid + Water
  - Sodium benzenesulphonate + Sodium hydroxide → Sodium phenoxide + Sodium sulphate
  - Sodium phenoxide + Dilute acid → Phenol + Water

- **From diazonium salts:** Diazonium salts are organic compounds with a diazonium group (-N2+) attached to an aromatic ring. They are formed by the reaction of an aromatic amine with nitrous acid. When a diazonium salt is treated with water, it undergoes hydrolysis to give phenol and nitrogen gas  .

  - Example: Aniline + Nitrous acid → Benzene diazonium chloride + Water
  - Benzene diazonium chloride + Water → Phenol + Nitrogen gas + Hydrochloric acid

## Industrial methods of preparation of phenol

- **From coal tar:** Coal tar is a dark brown liquid obtained as a by-product of the destructive distillation of coal. It contains various aromatic compounds, including phenol. Phenol is extracted from the middle oil fraction of coal tar by fractional distillation and purification .

- **From cumene:** Cumene is an organic compound with an isopropyl group (-CH(CH3)2) attached to a benzene ring. It is formed by the alkylation of benzene with propene. When cumene is oxidized by air, it forms cumene hydroperoxide, which is an unstable compound. Cumene hydroperoxide is then decomposed by heating or by acid catalysis to give phenol and acetone. This reaction is known as the cumene process or the Hock process  .

  - Example: Benzene + Propene → Cumene
  - Cumene + Air → Cumene hydroperoxide
  - Cumene hydroperoxide → Phenol + Acetone
