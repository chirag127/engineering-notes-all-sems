### 7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

Ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) is a compound commonly used as an iron source in various applications, including food fortification and agriculture. To determine the strength of FAS, you can use an external indicator method, which involves adding a known amount of FAS to a solution containing an indicator and then measuring the change in color to determine the concentration of iron ions.

The following steps can be followed to determine the strength of FAS using an external indicator:

1. Prepare a solution of FAS by dissolving a known mass of the compound in a known volume of water.
2. Prepare a solution of the external indicator, such as 1,10-phenanthroline, by dissolving a known mass of the indicator in a known volume of water.
3. Add a known volume of the FAS solution to a known volume of the indicator solution.
4. Observe the color change and measure the absorbance of the solution using a spectrophotometer.
5. Use the absorbance data to calculate the concentration of iron ions in the solution and the strength of the FAS.
6. Repeat the experiment several times to obtain an average value for the strength of the FAS.
7. Compare the average value obtained with the known value for the strength of FAS to determine the accuracy of the experiment.

This method allows for the determination of the strength of FAS based on the change in color of the indicator solution, which is proportional to the concentration of iron ions in the solution.
