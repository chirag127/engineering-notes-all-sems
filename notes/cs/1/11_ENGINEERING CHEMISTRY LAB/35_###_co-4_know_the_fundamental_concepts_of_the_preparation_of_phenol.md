### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol

Phenol is a commonly used organic chemical that is widely used in the production of a variety of products, including pharmaceuticals, plastics, and resins. The preparation of phenol involves the conversion of benzene into phenol through a chemical reaction known as the cumene process. This process involves the reaction of benzene with propylene to form cumene, which is then oxidized to form phenol and acetone. The reaction conditions, including temperature, pressure, and catalyst, are carefully controlled to ensure the efficient and safe production of phenol. The purity of the phenol produced is also important, and various purification methods, such as distillation, are used to remove impurities. Overall, the preparation of phenol is a complex process that requires careful control of reaction conditions and purification methods to ensure the production of high-quality phenol.
