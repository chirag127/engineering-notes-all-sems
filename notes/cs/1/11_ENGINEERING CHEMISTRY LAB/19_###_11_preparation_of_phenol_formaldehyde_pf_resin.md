### 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin.

The preparation of phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin involves the following steps:

1. Mixing phenol and formaldehyde in a reactor.
2. Adjusting the pH to around 8-9 with an alkaline catalyst.
3. Heating the mixture to around 80-90°C for 2-3 hours.
4. Adding a hardener, such as paraformaldehyde, to the mixture.
5. Continuing to heat the mixture for another 1-2 hours.
6. Cooling the mixture to room temperature.
7. Filtering the mixture to remove impurities.
8. Adding water and other ingredients, such as fillers, to adjust the viscosity and properties of the resin.
9. Mixing the solution until homogeneous.
10. Pouring the solution into molds or applying it to surfaces as needed.
11. Allowing the resin to cure, typically for 24-48 hours, before using it.
