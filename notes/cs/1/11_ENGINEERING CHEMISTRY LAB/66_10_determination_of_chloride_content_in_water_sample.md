10. Determination of chloride content in water sample.

To determine the chloride content in a water sample, the following steps can be taken: 
1. Obtain a sample of water to be tested
2. Prepare a standard solution of known chloride concentration
3. Add a chloride-specific reagent to the water sample (e.g. Mohr's salt)
4. Compare the color of the water sample to the standard solution to determine the chloride concentration
5. Repeat the test several times to obtain an average chloride concentration
6. Calculate the chloride content of the water sample based on the average concentration
7. Compare the results to the permissible levels of chloride in water, as determined by regulatory agencies.

This method is widely used in water quality testing and is a quick and simple way to determine the chloride content of a water sample. The results can be used to assess the safety of the water for drinking and other purposes.
