### viscosity, conductance of solution, chloride and iron content in the water.

Viscosity: It is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. The higher the viscosity, the thicker the fluid.

Conductance of Solution: It is a measure of a solution's ability to conduct electricity. The higher the conductance, the better the solution conducts electricity.

Chloride Content: Chloride is an ion found in water and is commonly used as a measure of water quality. High chloride levels can indicate pollution or contamination.

Iron Content: Iron is a metal that can be found in water. High iron levels can cause discoloration, staining, and a metallic taste in water. Excessive iron levels can also indicate contamination.
