LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

Experiments are systematic procedures designed to test a hypothesis or explore a phenomenon. They are used in a wide range of fields, including science, engineering, medicine, and social sciences. The goal of an experiment is to gather empirical data and use it to draw conclusions about the relationship between variables. Experiments are characterized by their controlled conditions, manipulation of variables, and measurement of outcomes. There are several types of experiments, including laboratory experiments, field experiments, and natural experiments. The design of an experiment is crucial to its success, as it must ensure that the results are valid and reliable. Experiments can also be used to test theories, validate models, and generate new knowledge. The results of experiments are often used to make decisions, guide policy, and inform future research. Overall, experiments play a crucial role in advancing our understanding of the world and solving practical problems.
