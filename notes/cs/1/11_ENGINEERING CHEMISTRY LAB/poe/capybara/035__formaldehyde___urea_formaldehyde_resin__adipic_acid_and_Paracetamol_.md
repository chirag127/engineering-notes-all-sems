### Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

Formaldehyde:
- A colorless gas with a pungent odor.
- Used in the production of resins, plastics, and textiles.
- Also used as a disinfectant and preservative.
- Can be harmful if inhaled in high concentrations.

Urea Formaldehyde Resin:
- A type of thermosetting plastic.
- Made by combining urea and formaldehyde.
- Used in the production of adhesives, laminates, and particleboard.
- Can emit formaldehyde gas, which can be harmful if inhaled in high concentrations.

Adipic Acid:
- A white crystalline solid.
- Used in the production of nylon and other synthetic fibers.
- Also used as a food additive and in the production of plastics and polyurethane foams.
- Considered safe for use in food and consumer products.

Paracetamol:
- A common pain reliever and fever reducer.
- Also known as acetaminophen.
- Works by blocking the production of certain chemicals in the body that cause pain and fever.
- Can be found in many over-the-counter medications and is generally considered safe when used as directed.

It is important to handle these chemicals with care and follow proper safety protocols to avoid any potential harm or hazards. It is also important to properly dispose of any waste or unused chemicals to prevent environmental contamination.
