# Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

Formaldehyde and Urea Formaldehyde Resin:
- Formaldehyde is a colorless gas with a pungent odor that is used in the manufacturing of many household and industrial products.
- Urea Formaldehyde Resin is a synthetic resin that is made by combining formaldehyde and urea.
- It is commonly used in the production of particle board, plywood, and other wood products.
- Urea Formaldehyde Resin is also used in the manufacturing of electrical components, textiles, and laminates.
- It has the advantage of being water-resistant and durable.

Adipic Acid:
- Adipic Acid is a white crystalline powder that is used in the production of nylon.
- It is also used in the manufacturing of plasticizers, lubricants, and other industrial products.
- Adipic Acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is produced from a mixture of cyclohexanol and cyclohexanone.
- It is also used in the food industry as a flavoring agent and as a pH regulator in some beverages.

Paracetamol:
- Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a common pain reliever and fever reducer.
- It is used to treat mild to moderate pain, headaches, and fever.
- Paracetamol is available in tablets, capsules, and liquid form.
- It is one of the most widely used over-the-counter medications in the world.
- Paracetamol is considered safe and effective when used as directed, but can cause serious liver damage if taken in large doses.
