### CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

In this section, you will learn about the different analytical instruments used in various fields of science and technology. Understanding the use of these instruments is crucial for conducting accurate and reliable experiments and analyses. The following are the different analytical instruments and their uses:

1. Spectrophotometer: A spectrophotometer is used to measure the amount of light absorbed or transmitted by a sample. It is commonly used in chemistry, biochemistry, and microbiology for quantitative analysis of compounds.

2. Chromatography: Chromatography is a technique used for separating and identifying components in a mixture. There are various types of chromatography, such as gas chromatography, liquid chromatography, and thin-layer chromatography.

3. Mass spectrometer: A mass spectrometer is used for analyzing the chemical composition of a sample. It is commonly used in analytical chemistry, biochemistry, and environmental science.

4. X-ray diffraction: X-ray diffraction is used to determine the crystal structure of a compound. It is commonly used in material science, chemistry, and physics.

5. Microscope: A microscope is used to magnify and visualize small objects or structures. There are various types of microscopes, such as light microscopes, electron microscopes, and scanning probe microscopes.

6. Nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy: NMR spectroscopy is used for analyzing the chemical structure of a compound. It is commonly used in organic chemistry, biochemistry, and medicinal chemistry.

7. Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR): FTIR is used for analyzing the chemical composition of a sample. It is commonly used in chemistry, biochemistry, and materials science.

8. Atomic force microscopy (AFM): AFM is used to visualize and measure the surface properties of a sample. It is commonly used in material science, biology, and physics.

By understanding the use of these analytical instruments, you will be able to choose the appropriate instrument for your experiments and analyses, and interpret the results accurately. It is essential to have a good understanding of the principles and applications of these instruments, as they are widely used in various fields of science and technology.
