### 7. Determination of the Strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate Using External Indicator

Ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) is a commonly used chemical in various industries, such as the pharmaceutical and agriculture industries. Its strength, or the amount of iron in the compound, is an important parameter that needs to be determined accurately.

The strength of FAS can be determined using an external indicator, such as potassium permanganate. The following steps can be followed to determine the strength of FAS:

1. Preparation of Standard Solution: Prepare a standard solution of potassium permanganate by dissolving a known amount of potassium permanganate in distilled water to make a 0.1N solution.

2. Titration of FAS: Weigh a known amount of FAS and dissolve it in distilled water in a conical flask. Add a few drops of dilute sulfuric acid to the solution to make it acidic. Add a few drops of the external indicator, potassium permanganate, to the solution.

3. Titration of the Standard Solution: Titrate the FAS solution with the standard solution of potassium permanganate until the color of the solution changes from pink to colorless. This indicates the end point of the titration.

4. Calculation of Strength: Calculate the strength of FAS using the following formula:

Strength of FAS = (Volume of Standard Solution × Normality of Standard Solution × Molecular Weight of Iron)/(Weight of FAS Taken × 1000)

Where,
Volume of Standard Solution = Volume of Standard Solution used in titration
Normality of Standard Solution = Normality of Standard Solution used in titration
Molecular Weight of Iron = 56
Weight of FAS Taken = Weight of FAS taken for titration

5. Repeat the Experiment: Repeat the above steps at least three times to obtain an average value for the strength of FAS.

6. Report the Results: Report the strength of FAS as the average of the three titrations, along with the standard deviation.

By following the above procedure, the strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate can be determined accurately using an external indicator. It is important to note that proper safety precautions should be taken while handling the chemicals, and the experiment should be performed in a well-ventilated area.
