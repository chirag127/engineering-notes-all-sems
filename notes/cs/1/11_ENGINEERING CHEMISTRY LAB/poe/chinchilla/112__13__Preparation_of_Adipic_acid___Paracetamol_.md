### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

Adipic acid and Paracetamol are important organic compounds used in various industries. Here are the methods for their preparation:

#### Preparation of Adipic acid:

1. Oxidation of Cyclohexene: Cyclohexene is oxidized with nitric acid to form adipic acid.

2. Oxidation of Cyclohexanone: Cyclohexanone is oxidized with nitric acid to form adipic acid.

3. Hydrolysis of Cyclohexanone Oxime: Cyclohexanone oxime is hydrolyzed with sulfuric acid to form adipic acid.

4. Hydrolysis of Caprolactam: Caprolactam is hydrolyzed with sulfuric acid to form adipic acid.

#### Preparation of Paracetamol:

1. Nitration of Phenol: Phenol is nitrated with a mixture of nitric acid and sulfuric acid to form 4-nitrophenol.

2. Reduction of 4-nitrophenol: 4-nitrophenol is reduced with sodium borohydride to form 4-aminophenol.

3. Acetylation of 4-aminophenol: 4-aminophenol is acetylated with acetic anhydride to form Paracetamol.

In conclusion, the above-listed methods are the commonly used methods for the preparation of Adipic acid and Paracetamol. It is important to note that the process involves the use of potentially hazardous chemicals and should only be performed by trained professionals in a laboratory setting.
