## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

As a student of science, it is important to have a strong foundation in experimental techniques. The following is a list of experiments that are commonly performed in science laboratories. These experiments cover a variety of scientific disciplines and are essential for understanding key concepts in science.

1. The Pendulum Experiment: This experiment involves measuring the time taken for a pendulum to complete one oscillation. This experiment is used to study the properties of harmonic motion.

2. The Boyle's Law Experiment: This experiment involves measuring the pressure and volume of a gas at constant temperature. This experiment is used to study the relationship between pressure and volume in a gas.

3. The Young's Double Slit Experiment: This experiment involves shining a light through two narrow slits and observing the interference pattern formed on a screen. This experiment is used to study the wave nature of light.

4. The Millikan Oil Drop Experiment: This experiment involves measuring the charge on an electron by observing the motion of oil droplets in an electric field. This experiment is used to study the fundamental properties of matter.

5. The Michelson-Morley Experiment: This experiment involves measuring the speed of light in different directions to detect the presence of the aether, a hypothetical medium that was once thought to exist. This experiment is used to study the properties of light and the nature of space and time.

6. The Galvanometer Experiment: This experiment involves measuring the current flowing through a wire using a galvanometer. This experiment is used to study the properties of electrical circuits.

7. The Magnetic Field Mapping Experiment: This experiment involves mapping the magnetic field around a magnet using a compass. This experiment is used to study the properties of magnetic fields.

8. The Acid-Base Titration Experiment: This experiment involves using a titration to determine the concentration of an acid or a base. This experiment is used to study the properties of acids and bases.

9. The Spectroscopy Experiment: This experiment involves observing the spectrum of light emitted or absorbed by a substance. This experiment is used to study the properties of atoms and molecules.

10. The Enzyme Kinetics Experiment: This experiment involves measuring the rate of an enzyme-catalyzed reaction. This experiment is used to study the properties of enzymes and the kinetics of chemical reactions.

These experiments form the basis for many scientific investigations and are essential for developing a strong understanding of science. By performing these experiments and analyzing the results, students can gain valuable insights into the properties of the natural world.
