## Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

Potassium dichromate is a chemical compound commonly used in analytical chemistry as an oxidizing agent. Its strength can be determined using an internal indicator. Here are the steps to determine the strength of potassium dichromate using an internal indicator:
