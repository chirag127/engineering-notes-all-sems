### 9. Determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder.

Bleaching powder is a white or pale yellow powder that is commonly used as a bleaching agent, disinfectant, and as a source of chlorine in various chemical processes. The available chlorine in bleaching powder is the amount of chlorine that can be released from the powder when it is dissolved in water. The determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder is an important analytical test that is used to ensure the quality of the product.

The following are the steps involved in the determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder:

1. Preparation of the sample: Weigh accurately about 2 grams of bleaching powder sample and dissolve it in 100 ml of distilled water. Mix the solution thoroughly and filter it through a filter paper to remove any insoluble impurities.

2. Standardization of the sodium thiosulfate solution: Prepare a standard solution of sodium thiosulfate by dissolving 25 g of pure sodium thiosulfate in 1 liter of distilled water. Add a few drops of a starch solution to the standard solution. Titrate the standard solution against 0.1 N iodine solution until a blue color appears. Note down the volume of iodine solution used for the titration.

3. Titration: Add 10 ml of 10% potassium iodide solution to the filtered bleaching powder solution. Then add 10 ml of 1 N sulfuric acid to the solution. Titrate the solution against the standard sodium thiosulfate solution until the yellow color disappears. Add a few drops of starch solution to the solution and continue the titration until the blue color disappears. Note down the volume of sodium thiosulfate solution used for the titration.

4. Calculation: The available chlorine in the bleaching powder sample can be calculated using the following formula:

Available chlorine (%) = (volume of sodium thiosulfate solution used × normality of sodium thiosulfate solution × 0.0035) / weight of the sample (in grams)

Where 0.0035 is the equivalent weight of available chlorine.

In conclusion, the determination of available chlorine in bleaching powder is a simple and important analytical test that is used to ensure the quality of the product. By following the above steps, the available chlorine content in the bleaching powder sample can be accurately determined.
