### Formaldehyde

- Chemical formula: CH2O
- Formaldehyde is a colorless, strong-smelling gas that is commonly used in the production of many household and industrial products.
- It is used in the production of resins, plastics, textiles, and paper products.
- It is also used in some medical procedures as a disinfectant and preservative.
- Formaldehyde is known to be a carcinogen, meaning it can cause cancer in humans.
- Exposure to formaldehyde can cause respiratory problems, skin irritation, and other health issues.
- To reduce exposure to formaldehyde, it is important to use products that have low levels of formaldehyde or are formaldehyde-free.

### Urea Formaldehyde Resin

- Chemical formula: (NH2)2CO + CH2O
- Urea formaldehyde resin is a type of thermosetting resin that is commonly used in the production of wood-based products such as plywood and particleboard.
- It is also used in the production of electrical equipment, automotive parts, and textiles.
- Urea formaldehyde resin is known for its high strength and durability.
- However, it is also known to emit formaldehyde gas, which can be harmful to human health.
- To reduce exposure to formaldehyde from urea formaldehyde resin, it is important to use products that have low levels of formaldehyde or are formaldehyde-free.

### Adipic Acid

- Chemical formula: C6H10O4
- Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is commonly used in the production of nylon, polyurethane, and other synthetic fibers and resins.
- It is also used in the production of food additives, such as flavorings and gelling agents.
- Adipic acid is known for its low toxicity and is considered safe for human consumption.
- However, excessive exposure to adipic acid can cause skin irritation, eye irritation, and respiratory problems.

### Paracetamol

- Chemical formula: C8H9NO2
- Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a common pain reliever and fever reducer.
- It is one of the most widely used drugs in the world and is available over the counter in many countries.
- Paracetamol works by blocking the production of certain chemicals in the body that cause pain and inflammation.
- It is generally considered safe for human consumption when taken in recommended doses.
- However, excessive use of paracetamol can cause liver damage and other health problems.
