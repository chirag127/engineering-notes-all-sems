# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS LAB KCS

Engineering Graphics is a fundamental subject for engineering students. It provides a platform to understand the basics of engineering drawing and its applications. The Engineering Graphics Lab is an essential component of this subject, which offers practical exposure to the students. This lab is designed to help students develop their technical and practical skills in Engineering Graphics. The following points will help you understand the objectives, equipment, and procedures of the Engineering Graphics Lab at KCS.

## Objectives of Engineering Graphics Lab

The Engineering Graphics Lab at KCS has the following objectives:

- To provide hands-on experience in drafting, modeling, and visualization techniques used in engineering.
- To enhance the students' technical and practical skills in Engineering Graphics.
- To apply the principles of engineering drawing in various fields of engineering.
- To develop a professional attitude towards engineering drawing and related activities.

## Equipment Required in Engineering Graphics Lab

The following equipment is required in the Engineering Graphics Lab at KCS:

- Drawing board, T-square, and set square for drafting.
- Compass, divider, and protractor for accurate measurements.
- Drafting instruments such as pencils, erasers, and scales.
- Computer systems with drafting software such as AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and CATIA.
- Printer and scanner for printing and scanning drawings.
- Projectors and screens for displaying drawings and presentations.

## Procedures in Engineering Graphics Lab

The Engineering Graphics Lab at KCS follows the following procedures:

- Students are provided with a set of instructions and guidelines for each experiment or project.
- Students are required to follow the instructions carefully and complete the task within the given time frame.
- The lab instructor supervises the students' work and provides assistance when required.
- The completed drawings or models are checked for accuracy, neatness, and completeness.
- The students are required to submit a report on each experiment or project, which includes the objectives, methodology, results, and conclusions.

In conclusion, the Engineering Graphics Lab at KCS is an essential component of the Engineering Graphics subject, which provides practical exposure to the students. The lab is equipped with modern tools and equipment necessary for drafting, modeling, and visualization techniques used in engineering. The lab follows a set of procedures to ensure the students' technical and practical skills are enhanced. By following the instructions and guidelines, students can develop a professional attitude towards engineering drawing and related activities.
