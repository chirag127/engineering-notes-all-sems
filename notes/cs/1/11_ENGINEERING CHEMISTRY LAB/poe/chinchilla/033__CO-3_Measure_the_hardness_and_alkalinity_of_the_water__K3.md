### CO-3 Measure the Hardness and Alkalinity of the Water. K3

Water is an essential element for human life, and it is essential to ensure that the water we consume is safe for drinking. The hardness and alkalinity of water are two important factors that determine its quality. In this topic, we will learn how to measure the hardness and alkalinity of water.

Here are the steps to measure the hardness and alkalinity of water:

1. Hardness of water: Hardness is the measure of the amount of dissolved calcium and magnesium ions in the water. The hardness of water can be measured using a titration method.

   - Take a water sample of about 100 ml and add a few drops of indicator solution.
   - Titrate the water sample with a standard ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid (EDTA) solution until the color of the water sample changes from red to blue.
   - Note down the volume of EDTA solution used for titration.
   - Calculate the hardness of water using the formula: Hardness (in mg/L) = Volume of EDTA solution used x Normality of EDTA solution x 1000 / Volume of water sample.

2. Alkalinity of water: Alkalinity is the measure of the amount of bicarbonate, carbonate, and hydroxide ions in the water. The alkalinity of water can be measured using the titration method.

   - Take a water sample of about 100 ml and add a few drops of indicator solution.
   - Titrate the water sample with a standard sulfuric acid (H2SO4) solution until the color of the water sample changes from blue to yellow.
   - Note down the volume of H2SO4 solution used for titration.
   - Calculate the alkalinity of water using the formula: Alkalinity (in mg/L) = Volume of H2SO4 solution used x Normality of H2SO4 solution x 50000 / Volume of water sample.

It is important to note that the hardness and alkalinity of water can have an impact on the taste, appearance, and suitability of the water for certain uses. By measuring the hardness and alkalinity of water, we can ensure the quality of water and take necessary steps to make it suitable for consumption.

In conclusion, measuring the hardness and alkalinity of water is a crucial step in ensuring the quality of water. By following the above steps, we can accurately measure the hardness and alkalinity of water and take necessary actions to make it safe for consumption.
