### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route.

Organic compounds are compounds that contain carbon atoms. Synthesis of organic compounds involves the creation of new molecules by combining simpler molecules through chemical reactions. Adipic acid and paracetamol are two commonly synthesized organic compounds that have various applications. In this section, we will discuss the conventional and green route synthesis of adipic acid and paracetamol.

#### Synthesis of Adipic Acid

Adipic acid is a dicarboxylic acid that is primarily used in the production of nylon. It can be synthesized through the following routes:

1. Conventional Route: In the conventional route, adipic acid is synthesized through the oxidation of cyclohexene with nitric acid. The reaction proceeds in two stages, with the formation of cyclohexene oxide as an intermediate.

2. Green Route: The green route involves the use of bio-based feedstocks such as glucose or lignocellulosic biomass. The feedstock is converted into muconic acid through fermentation and then oxidized to adipic acid.

#### Synthesis of Paracetamol

Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a commonly used analgesic and antipyretic medication. It can be synthesized through the following routes:

1. Conventional Route: In the conventional route, paracetamol is synthesized through the nitration of phenol with nitric acid, followed by reduction of the nitro group to an amino group and acetylation.

2. Green Route: The green route involves the use of renewable feedstocks such as lignin or cellulose. The feedstock is converted into p-hydroxybenzoic acid through depolymerization and then acetylated to form paracetamol.

In conclusion, the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol can be achieved through both conventional and green routes. The choice of the route depends on various factors such as availability of feedstock, environmental impact, and economic feasibility. Understanding the synthesis of these compounds is essential for students studying organic chemistry and related fields.
