## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

Here is a list of experiments that students may encounter in their science classes:

1. **Chemistry:** 
- Determination of melting point of a solid substance. 
- Preparation of soap using vegetable oil. 
- Study of the reaction between iron and sulfur.
- Determination of the concentration of an unknown acid using a standard base.

2. **Physics:**
- Measurement of the acceleration due to gravity using a simple pendulum. 
- Determination of the coefficient of friction between two surfaces. 
- Study of the behavior of a spring-mass system. 
- Verification of Ohm's Law using a circuit.

3. **Biology:**
- Study of plant pigments through paper chromatography. 
- Observation of mitosis in onion root tips. 
- Determination of the effect of pH on enzyme activity. 
- Study of the growth of yeast in different sugar solutions.

4. **Geology:**
- Study of the properties of minerals using various physical and chemical tests. 
- Determination of the density of a rock sample. 
- Study of the erosion and deposition of sediment by water. 
- Identification of fossils and their ages through stratigraphy.

5. **Environmental Science:**
- Investigation of the effect of different pollutants on the germination of seeds. 
- Study of the properties of soil and its effects on plant growth. 
- Analysis of the quality of water from different sources. 
- Study of the greenhouse effect and the impact of global warming on the environment.

These experiments are designed to provide students with hands-on experiences that reinforce the concepts taught in class. They also help students develop important skills such as observation, data collection, and analysis. It is important to follow proper safety procedures when conducting experiments and to always ask for guidance from a teacher or lab supervisor.
