### 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

Potassium dichromate is a widely used oxidizing agent in chemical analysis. Its strength can be determined using an internal indicator such as ferrous ammonium sulfate. The procedure for determining the strength of Potassium dichromate using an internal indicator is as follows:

1. Preparation of the solution of Potassium dichromate: Dissolve a weighed quantity of Potassium dichromate in distilled water to obtain a standard solution of known concentration.

2. Preparation of the solution of Ferrous ammonium sulfate: Dissolve a weighed quantity of Ferrous ammonium sulfate in distilled water to obtain a standard solution of known concentration.

3. Titration: Pipette a known volume of Potassium dichromate solution into a conical flask. Add a few drops of dilute sulfuric acid and heat the solution to boiling. Titrate the hot solution with Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution until the green color of Potassium dichromate changes to a brownish-red color. This indicates the end point of the titration.

4. Calculation: Calculate the strength of Potassium dichromate using the following formula:

Strength of Potassium dichromate = (Volume of Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution used x Normality of Ferrous ammonium sulfate solution x Molecular weight of Potassium dichromate) / Volume of Potassium dichromate solution taken.

5. Precautions: The following precautions should be taken during the determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator:

- All glassware should be clean and dry.
- The solutions should be standardized before use.
- The titration should be carried out at a constant temperature.
- The burette should be properly calibrated.
- The end point of the titration should be carefully observed.

In conclusion, the determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator is a simple and reliable method for analyzing the concentration of Potassium dichromate. With careful attention to the procedure and precautions, accurate results can be obtained.
