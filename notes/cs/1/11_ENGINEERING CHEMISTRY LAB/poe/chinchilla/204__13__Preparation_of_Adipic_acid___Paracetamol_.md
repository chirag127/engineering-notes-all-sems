### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

Adipic acid and Paracetamol are important chemicals that are widely used in various industries. Here are the preparation methods for both:

#### Preparation of Adipic Acid

1. The most common method of preparing Adipic acid is the oxidation of cyclohexene using potassium permanganate or nitric acid as an oxidizing agent.

2. Cyclohexene is first converted into cyclohexene oxide using an acid catalyst such as sulfuric acid.

3. The cyclohexene oxide is then oxidized using potassium permanganate or nitric acid to produce Adipic acid.

4. The Adipic acid is separated from the reaction mixture by precipitation, filtration, and crystallization.

5. The yield of Adipic acid is typically around 50-60% by weight.

#### Preparation of Paracetamol

1. Paracetamol is synthesized through the reaction of p-aminophenol and acetic anhydride.

2. The reaction is catalyzed by a strong acid catalyst such as sulfuric acid or hydrochloric acid.

3. The p-aminophenol is first dissolved in a solvent such as water or ethanol.

4. Acetic anhydride is then added slowly to the reaction mixture while stirring.

5. The mixture is heated under reflux for several hours to complete the reaction.

6. The Paracetamol is separated from the reaction mixture by filtration and crystallization.

7. The yield of Paracetamol is typically around 90-95% by weight.

In conclusion, the preparation of Adipic acid and Paracetamol involves different chemical reactions and procedures. Understanding these methods is crucial for those working in the chemical industry or studying chemistry.
