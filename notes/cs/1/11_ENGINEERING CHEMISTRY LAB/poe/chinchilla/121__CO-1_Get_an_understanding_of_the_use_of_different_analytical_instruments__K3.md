### CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3.

In this section, we will discuss the use of different analytical instruments that are commonly used in various research fields. By the end of this section, you will have a comprehensive understanding of the following analytical instruments:

1. Spectrophotometer:

   - A spectrophotometer is an instrument used to measure the absorbance or transmittance of light by a sample. It is commonly used in chemical analysis, biochemistry, and molecular biology.

2. Chromatography:

   - Chromatography is a technique used to separate and identify the components of a mixture. There are various types of chromatography, such as gas chromatography and liquid chromatography, each with its own set of applications.

3. Mass spectrometry:

   - Mass spectrometry is an analytical technique used to determine the mass of particles in a sample. It is commonly used in chemistry, biochemistry, and forensic science.

4. Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy:

   - Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy is a technique used to study the magnetic properties of atomic nuclei. It is commonly used in chemistry, biochemistry, and physics.

5. X-ray diffraction:

   - X-ray diffraction is a technique used to determine the structure of crystals. It is commonly used in materials science and chemistry.

6. Atomic force microscopy:

   - Atomic force microscopy is a technique used to study the surface of a sample at the atomic level. It is commonly used in materials science, biology, and physics.

7. Electron microscopy:

   - Electron microscopy is a technique used to study the structure of a sample at the nanoscale level. It is commonly used in materials science, biology, and physics.

In conclusion, the above-mentioned analytical instruments are widely used in various research fields. Each instrument has its own set of applications and advantages, and understanding how to use them can greatly enhance the quality of research.
