### 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a Solution

Cell conductance is the measure of the ability of a solution to conduct electricity. It is an important parameter in electrochemistry and is used to determine the concentration of ions in a solution. The following points outline the methods and procedures for determining the cell conductance of a solution:

1. Select a suitable cell: A conductivity cell is used to measure the cell conductance of a solution. The cell consists of two electrodes, a reference electrode, and a measuring electrode. A glass conductivity cell is commonly used for this purpose.

2. Calibration of the cell: Before measuring the conductance of the solution, the cell must be calibrated using a standard solution of known conductivity. This is done to ensure accurate measurements.

3. Preparation of the sample solution: The sample solution must be prepared by dissolving the required amount of solute in a solvent. The solution must be stirred well to ensure uniformity.

4. Temperature control: The temperature of the solution must be maintained at a constant value throughout the measurement process. This is important because the conductance of a solution is dependent on temperature.

5. Measurement of cell conductance: The conductivity cell is immersed in the solution, and the conductance of the solution is measured. The conductance is measured in siemens (S) or mhos (℧).

6. Calculation of cell conductance: The cell conductance is calculated using the formula:

    Cell conductance = (measured conductance × cell constant) / cell length

    where cell constant is the factor that relates the conductance of the solution to the conductance of the cell, and cell length is the length of the cell.

7. Determination of concentration: The cell conductance is used to determine the concentration of ions in the solution. The concentration can be calculated using standard tables or graphs.

In conclusion, the determination of cell conductance is an important procedure in electrochemistry. The process involves the selection of a suitable cell, calibration of the cell, preparation of the sample solution, temperature control, measurement of cell conductance, calculation of cell conductance, and determination of concentration. Accurate measurements of cell conductance can be used to determine the concentration of ions in a solution, which is important in many scientific and industrial applications.
