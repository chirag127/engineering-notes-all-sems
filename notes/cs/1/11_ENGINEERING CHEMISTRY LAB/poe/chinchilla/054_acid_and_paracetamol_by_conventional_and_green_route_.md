# Acid and Paracetamol by Conventional and Green Route

Acid and paracetamol are two commonly used compounds in the pharmaceutical industry. They can be synthesized using both conventional and green routes. In this article, we will discuss the synthesis of acid and paracetamol using both routes.

## Conventional Route

The conventional route for the synthesis of acid and paracetamol involves the following steps:

### Synthesis of Acid

1. The starting material for the synthesis of acid is benzene.
2. Benzene is first nitrated with a mixture of nitric and sulfuric acid to give nitrobenzene.
3. Nitrobenzene is then reduced with iron and hydrochloric acid to give aniline.
4. Aniline is then diazotized with nitrous acid to give diazonium salt.
5. The diazonium salt is then coupled with salicylic acid to give acetylsalicylic acid, commonly known as aspirin.

### Synthesis of Paracetamol

1. The starting material for the synthesis of paracetamol is phenol.
2. Phenol is first nitrated with a mixture of nitric and sulfuric acid to give picric acid.
3. Picric acid is then reduced with iron and hydrochloric acid to give 4-aminophenol.
4. 4-aminophenol is then acetylated with acetic anhydride to give paracetamol.

## Green Route

The green route for the synthesis of acid and paracetamol involves the use of eco-friendly and sustainable methods. The steps involved in the green synthesis of acid and paracetamol are:

### Synthesis of Acid

1. The starting material for the synthesis of acid is salicylic acid.
2. Salicylic acid is reacted with acetic anhydride and a small amount of sulfuric acid to give acetylsalicylic acid, commonly known as aspirin.

### Synthesis of Paracetamol

1. The starting material for the synthesis of paracetamol is phenol.
2. Phenol is reacted with nitrous acid in the presence of hydrochloric acid to give 4-nitrophenol.
3. 4-nitrophenol is then reduced with sodium borohydride to give 4-aminophenol.
4. 4-aminophenol is then acetylated with acetic anhydride to give paracetamol.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, acid and paracetamol can be synthesized using both conventional and green routes. The conventional route involves multiple steps and the use of harsh chemicals, whereas the green route involves eco-friendly and sustainable methods. It is important to consider the environmental impact of the synthesis of compounds and strive towards the use of green chemistry principles in the pharmaceutical industry.
