# 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution

The determination of cell conductance of a solution is an important technique used in analytical chemistry to measure the electrical conductivity of a solution. This is a crucial parameter for the characterization of a solution as it is related to the concentration of ions present in the solution. Here are the steps involved in the determination of cell conductance of a solution:

1. Preparation of the solution: Prepare the solution whose cell conductance is to be determined. The solution should be prepared accurately to the desired concentration.

2. Set up the cell: Set up a conductance cell by using two electrodes, one made of platinum and the other of silver. These electrodes should be immersed in the solution whose conductance is to be measured.

3. Calibration of the cell: Calibrate the cell by using a standard solution of known conductivity. This will help in the determination of the cell constant, which is a crucial parameter for the calculation of cell conductance.

4. Measurement of the conductance: Once the cell is calibrated, measure the conductance of the solution using a conductance meter. The conductance meter measures the resistance of the solution and converts it into conductance.

5. Calculation of the cell conductance: Once the conductance is measured, calculate the cell conductance using the formula:

   Conductance of solution = Conductance of cell x Cell constant

   The cell constant is determined during the calibration of the cell and is expressed in units of cm^-1.

6. Interpretation of results: Finally, interpret the results obtained. The conductance of the solution is directly proportional to the concentration of ions present in the solution. Therefore, the higher the conductance, the higher the concentration of ions present in the solution.

In conclusion, the determination of cell conductance of a solution is a crucial technique used in analytical chemistry to measure the electrical conductivity of a solution. By following the above steps, accurate measurements can be obtained, which can be used for the characterization of the solution.
