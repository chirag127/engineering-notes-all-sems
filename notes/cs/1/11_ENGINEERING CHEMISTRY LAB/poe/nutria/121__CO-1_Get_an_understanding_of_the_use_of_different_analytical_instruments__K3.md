
### CO-1 Get an Understanding of the Use of Different Analytical Instruments. K3

1. Analytical instruments are used to measure and analyze physical and chemical properties of different materials.

2. Examples of analytical instruments include spectrometers, chromatographs, and mass spectrometers.

3. Spectrometers measure the intensity of light emitted from a sample and can be used to identify elements and compounds in a sample.

4. Chromatographs separate and analyze the components of a sample, such as gas or liquid.

5. Mass spectrometers measure the mass of a sample and can be used to identify elements and compounds in a sample.

6. Analytical instruments are used in a variety of fields, including chemistry, biology, and medicine.
