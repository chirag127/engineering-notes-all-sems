

#### 4. Determination of pH by titrimetric method

* Titrimetric methods are used to determine the pH of a solution.
* The method involves titrating a solution with a known concentration of acid or base until the endpoint is reached.
* The endpoint is determined by a color change or by using a pH indicator.
* The amount of acid or base used to reach the endpoint is then used to calculate the pH of the solution.
* Titrimetric methods are accurate and can be used in a variety of situations, including the determination of the pH of a buffer solution.
