
### 1. Calibration of Analytical Equipment and Apparatus

1. Calibration is the process of comparing the performance of a device to a known standard and adjusting it accordingly.
2. It is important to ensure that the analytical equipment and apparatus used in laboratories are properly calibrated to achieve accurate results.
3. The calibration process includes setting up the equipment, verifying its performance, and adjusting the equipment to meet a predetermined standard.
4. The calibration of analytical equipment and apparatus can be done manually or with the help of automated systems.
5. It is important to ensure that the calibration process is done regularly and accurately to ensure the accuracy of the results.
6. The calibration process should be documented and the results should be compared to the expected results.
7. The calibration process should be done in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and the applicable standards.
8. The calibration process should be documented and the results should be reviewed regularly.
9. The calibration process should be done in a controlled environment to ensure the accuracy of the results.
10. The calibration process should be done in accordance with the applicable safety regulations.
