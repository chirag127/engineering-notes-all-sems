

### CO-2 Measure the Molecular/System Properties Such as Surface Tension
1. Carbon dioxide (CO2) is a compound composed of two oxygen atoms covalently bonded to a single carbon atom.
2. The physical properties of CO2 are determined by its molecular structure.
3. At room temperature, CO2 is a colorless, odorless gas.
4. The boiling point of CO2 is -78.5°C (-109.3°F) and the melting point is -57.8°C (-72.8°F).
5. The density of CO2 is 1.98 kg/m3 at 25°C (77°F).
6. The surface tension of CO2 is 0.072 N/m.
7. The viscosity of CO2 is 0.0018 Pa·s.
8. The refractive index of CO2 is 1.00045.
9. The solubility of CO2 in water is 0.8 g/L at 25°C (77°F).
