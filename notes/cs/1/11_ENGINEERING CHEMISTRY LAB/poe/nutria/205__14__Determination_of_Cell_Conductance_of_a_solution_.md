
### 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a Solution

1. Cell conductance is a measure of the ability of a solution to conduct an electrical current. 
2. It is defined as the ratio of the current to the voltage applied across the solution. 
3. Cell conductance can be measured by measuring the current and voltage across a solution. 
4. The cell conductance of a solution is affected by the type of ions present in the solution, their concentration, and the temperature of the solution. 
5. The cell conductance of a solution can be determined using a conductance meter. 
6. The conductance meter measures the current and voltage across the solution and calculates the cell conductance. 
7. The cell conductance of a solution can also be determined by measuring the resistance of the solution. 
8. The resistance of a solution can be measured using a Wheatstone bridge circuit. 
9. The cell conductance of a solution can also be determined by measuring the capacitance of the solution. 
10. The capacitance of a solution can be measured using an impedance analyzer.
