
### Course Outcomes Bloom's

1. Demonstrate an understanding of the fundamental concepts, theories, principles, and processes of a given subject or field.
2. Develop an understanding of the connections between the fundamental concepts, theories, principles, and processes of a given subject or field.
3. Demonstrate an ability to apply the fundamental concepts, theories, principles, and processes of a given subject or field.
4. Demonstrate an ability to analyze and evaluate the fundamental concepts, theories, principles, and processes of a given subject or field.
5. Demonstrate an ability to synthesize the fundamental concepts, theories, principles, and processes of a given subject or field.
6. Demonstrate an ability to create new knowledge based on the fundamental concepts, theories, principles, and processes of a given subject or field.
