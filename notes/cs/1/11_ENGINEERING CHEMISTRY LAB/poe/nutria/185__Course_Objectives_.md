
## Course Objectives:
1. Understand the fundamentals of computer science and programming.
2. Develop problem-solving skills and apply them to computer science and programming.
3. Develop an understanding of the underlying principles of computer hardware and software.
4. Develop an understanding of the principles of computer networks, security, and data storage.
5. Develop an understanding of the principles of software engineering and software development.
6. Develop an understanding of the principles of database design and management.
7. Develop an understanding of the principles of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and robotics.
8. Develop an understanding of the principles of user interface design and user experience.
