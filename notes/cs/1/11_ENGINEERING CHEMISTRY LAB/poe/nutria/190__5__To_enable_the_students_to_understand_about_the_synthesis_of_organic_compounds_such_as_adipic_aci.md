
### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route

1. Synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol is a process of combining two or more components to create a new compound. 
2. Conventional synthesis of organic compounds involves the use of chemical reagents and catalysts to produce the desired product. 
3. Green synthesis is a more sustainable approach to synthesis, which involves the use of renewable resources, natural catalysts, and mild reaction conditions. 
4. Adipic acid is a white crystalline solid that is used as a precursor in the manufacture of nylon and other polymers. It is produced by the oxidation of cyclohexanol with nitric acid. 
5. Paracetamol is a commonly used analgesic and antipyretic drug. It is synthesized through the conversion of p-aminophenol to acetaminophen. 
6. The synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol can be achieved by both conventional and green routes. The choice of route depends on the desired product and the available resources.
