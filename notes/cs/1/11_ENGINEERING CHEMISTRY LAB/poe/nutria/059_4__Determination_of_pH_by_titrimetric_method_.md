
#### 4. Determination of pH by titrimetric method

1. Titrimetric methods are used to determine the pH of a solution. 
2. This method involves titrating a solution of known concentration with a standard solution of known concentration until the endpoint of the reaction is reached. 
3. The pH of the solution is then determined by the volume of the standard solution used in the titration. 
4. The endpoint of the titration is determined by using an indicator, which changes color when the reaction is complete. 
5. The endpoint can also be determined by using a pH meter, which measures the pH of the solution during the titration. 
6. This method is accurate and reliable and can be used to determine the pH of a variety of solutions.
