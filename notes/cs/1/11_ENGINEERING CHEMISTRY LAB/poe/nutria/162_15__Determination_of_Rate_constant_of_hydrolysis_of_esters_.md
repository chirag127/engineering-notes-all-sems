
# 15. Determination of Rate Constant of Hydrolysis of Esters

1. Esters are organic compounds which are formed by the reaction between an acid and an alcohol.
2. The hydrolysis of esters is a reaction in which esters are broken down by reacting with water.
3. The rate of hydrolysis of esters can be determined by measuring the amount of hydrolysis product formed in a given period of time.
4. The rate of hydrolysis is usually expressed in terms of the rate constant, which is the rate of formation of the hydrolysis product per unit time.
5. The rate constant can be determined experimentally by measuring the amount of hydrolysis product formed in a given period of time.
6. The rate constant can also be determined using the Arrhenius equation, which relates the rate constant to the temperature and the activation energy of the reaction.
7. The rate constant can be used to predict the rate of hydrolysis of esters at different temperatures.
