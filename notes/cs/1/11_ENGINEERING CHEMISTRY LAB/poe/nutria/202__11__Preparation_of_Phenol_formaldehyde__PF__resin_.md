
### 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) Resin

1. Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin is a thermosetting polymer that is produced by the reaction of phenol and formaldehyde in the presence of an acid catalyst.
2. The reaction is typically carried out at a temperature of 100-130°C and a pressure of 0.5-2.5 MPa.
3. The reaction is exothermic and the heat of reaction is usually sufficient to maintain the reaction temperature.
4. The reaction is usually complete within 2-3 hours, but the reaction time can be extended to 8-10 hours for higher molecular weight resins.
5. The viscosity of the resin increases with increasing molecular weight, and the molecular weight can be controlled by adding an inhibitor such as paraformaldehyde or an amine.
6. The resin can be further modified by adding other monomers such as urea or melamine to increase the water resistance or flame retardancy of the resin.
7. The resin can also be modified by adding fillers such as silica or alumina to reduce the cost and improve the physical properties of the resin.
8. The resin can be used to produce a wide range of products such as laminates, adhesives, coatings, and mouldings.
