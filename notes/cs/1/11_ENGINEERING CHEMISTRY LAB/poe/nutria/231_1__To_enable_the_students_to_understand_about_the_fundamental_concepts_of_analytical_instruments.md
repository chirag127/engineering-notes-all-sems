
# 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

1. Analytical instruments are tools used to measure physical, chemical, and biological properties.
2. These instruments are used to measure the concentration of a substance, the size and shape of particles, the pressure and temperature of a system, and the composition of a material.
3. They are used in a variety of fields, including medicine, engineering, and environmental science.
4. Common types of analytical instruments include chromatographs, spectrometers, microscopes, and mass spectrometers.
5. Each instrument has its own unique set of features and capabilities, and it is important to understand the basics of each instrument before using it.
6. The principles of operation of analytical instruments involve the use of physical, chemical, and biological principles.
7. The use of these principles helps to determine the concentration of a substance, the size and shape of particles, the pressure and temperature of a system, and the composition of a material.
8. The accuracy and precision of the measurements obtained with analytical instruments depend on the quality of the instrument, the calibration of the instrument, and the user's understanding of the instrument.
