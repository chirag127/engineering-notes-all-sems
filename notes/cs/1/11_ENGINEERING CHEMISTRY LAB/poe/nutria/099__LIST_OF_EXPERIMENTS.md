
## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

1. The Stanford Prison Experiment: This experiment was conducted in 1971 by psychologist Philip Zimbardo at Stanford University. It was designed to measure the psychological effects of becoming a prisoner or a guard.

2. The Milgram Experiment: This experiment was conducted in 1961 by psychologist Stanley Milgram at Yale University. It was designed to measure the willingness of participants to obey orders even when they conflict with their personal conscience.

3. The Asch Conformity Experiments: This experiment was conducted in 1951 by psychologist Solomon Asch at Swarthmore College. It was designed to measure the degree to which social pressure from a majority group could affect a person's opinion.

4. The Little Albert Experiment: This experiment was conducted in 1920 by psychologist John Watson at Johns Hopkins University. It was designed to measure the effects of classical conditioning on a child.

5. The Bystander Effect: This experiment was conducted in 1968 by psychologist John Darley and Bibb Latane at New York University. It was designed to measure the influence of the presence of others on a person's willingness to help in an emergency situation.
