

### 5. Determination of Surface Tension of a Given Liquid

1. Surface tension is a property of a liquid surface that allows it to resist an external force. 
2. Surface tension is caused by the cohesive forces between molecules of the same liquid. 
3. Surface tension can be measured by the force needed to break the surface of a liquid. 
4. A common method of measuring surface tension is the Du Noüy ring method, which uses a ring suspended from a wire to measure the force needed to break the surface of a liquid. 
5. The surface tension of a liquid can also be determined by measuring the force needed to move a needle through the surface of the liquid. 
6. The surface tension of a given liquid can be used to calculate the surface energy of the liquid, which is a measure of the energy required to break the surface of the liquid.
