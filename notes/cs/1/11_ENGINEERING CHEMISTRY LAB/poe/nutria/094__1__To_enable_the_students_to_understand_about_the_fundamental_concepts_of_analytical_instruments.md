
### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

1. Analytical instruments are tools used to measure physical properties of a sample, such as its composition, structure, and other characteristics.
2. These instruments are used in a wide range of fields, such as chemistry, biology, physics, and engineering.
3. Analytical instruments can be used to measure a variety of properties, including chemical composition, physical properties, and electrical properties.
4. Different analytical instruments are used to measure different properties. For example, chromatography is used to measure the chemical composition of a sample, while spectroscopy is used to measure the physical and electrical properties of a sample.
5. Analytical instruments are typically used in laboratories, where they are operated by trained professionals.
6. The accuracy and precision of an analytical instrument depend on the quality of the instrument and the skill of the operator.
7. It is important to understand the limitations of an analytical instrument and the conditions under which it should be used.
