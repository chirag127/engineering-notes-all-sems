
### 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity of water

* Chloride content in water can be determined by titration with silver nitrate.
* Hardness of water is typically determined by titration with EDTA, and is usually expressed in terms of calcium carbonate equivalents.
* Alkalinity of water can be determined by titration with a strong acid such as sulfuric acid, and is usually expressed in terms of calcium carbonate equivalents.
