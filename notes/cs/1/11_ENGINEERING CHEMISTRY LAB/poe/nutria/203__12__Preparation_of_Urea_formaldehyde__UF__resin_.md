
### 12. Preparation of Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin

1. Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin is a thermosetting resin that is produced when urea and formaldehyde are reacted together.
2. UF resin is typically used in the production of adhesives, coatings, and moldings.
3. The reaction between urea and formaldehyde is catalyzed by an acid or a base, such as sulfuric acid or sodium hydroxide.
4. The reaction is exothermic, meaning that it produces heat as it progresses.
5. The reaction is typically carried out in the presence of a solvent, such as water or alcohol.
6. The reaction is typically conducted at temperatures between 60-90°C.
7. The reaction typically takes several hours to complete, depending on the desired degree of polymerization.
8. The degree of polymerization can be adjusted by varying the concentration of urea, formaldehyde, and catalyst.
9. The end product of the reaction is a white, semi-solid material that is insoluble in water.
10. UF resins are typically used in the production of adhesives, coatings, and moldings due to their excellent strength, hardness, and durability.
