
# 12. Preparation of Urea Formaldehyde (UF) Resin

1. Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin is a thermosetting polymer produced by the condensation reaction of urea and formaldehyde.
2. UF resin is synthesized by the reaction of urea and formaldehyde in the presence of an acid catalyst at a temperature of 80-90°C.
3. The reaction proceeds in two stages, with the formaldehyde first reacting with the urea to form methylolureas, which then condense to form the final polymer.
4. The degree of polymerization of the UF resin depends on the ratio of urea to formaldehyde used in the reaction, with higher ratios producing higher molecular weight polymers.
5. The UF resin is usually supplied as a powder or granules which can be dissolved in water to form a solution.
6. The UF resin solution is then used as an adhesive or in the manufacture of moulded products such as laminates and particle boards.
