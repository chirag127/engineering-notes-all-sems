
# K3

1. K3 stands for Knowledge, Skills, and Attitudes. It is a way of understanding and assessing the knowledge, skills, and attitudes that people need to be successful in their roles. 
2. Knowledge is the information that people need to understand the context in which they are working and to make decisions. 
3. Skills are the abilities that people need to be able to do their work. These skills can include technical skills, interpersonal skills, problem-solving skills, and communication skills. 
4. Attitudes are the beliefs and values that people have about their work and the environment in which they are working. Attitudes can affect how people approach their work and how they interact with others. 
5. K3 is an important concept for understanding and assessing the knowledge, skills, and attitudes that people need to be successful in their roles. It can be used to identify gaps in knowledge, skills, and attitudes and to develop strategies for addressing those gaps.
