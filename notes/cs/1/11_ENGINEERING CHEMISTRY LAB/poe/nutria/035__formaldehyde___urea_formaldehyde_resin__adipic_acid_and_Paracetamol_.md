
### Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

* Formaldehyde is a colorless, flammable gas with a strong odor. It is used in a variety of industrial and consumer products, including adhesives, paints, and plastics. Urea formaldehyde resin is a thermosetting plastic made from a reaction between formaldehyde and urea. It is used in many products, such as furniture, flooring, and cabinetry.
* Adipic acid is a white, odorless, crystalline solid. It is used in the manufacture of nylon, polyurethanes, and other synthetic fibers.
* Paracetamol is an analgesic drug used to treat pain and fever. It is available in tablet and liquid form.
