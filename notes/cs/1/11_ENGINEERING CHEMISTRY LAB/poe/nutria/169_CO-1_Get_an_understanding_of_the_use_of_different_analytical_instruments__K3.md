
# CO-1 Get an Understanding of the Use of Different Analytical Instruments. K3

1. Analytical instruments are tools used to measure, analyze, and interpret physical and chemical properties of substances. 
2. Common analytical instruments include spectroscopy, chromatography, mass spectrometry, and microscopy.
3. Spectroscopy is a technique used to measure the intensity of light at various wavelengths. It is used to identify the composition of a substance.
4. Chromatography is a technique used to separate mixtures of substances. It is used to identify and quantify components in a mixture.
5. Mass spectrometry is a technique used to measure the mass-to-charge ratio of ions. It is used to identify and quantify molecules.
6. Microscopy is a technique used to observe small objects. It is used to identify and characterize structures and particles.
