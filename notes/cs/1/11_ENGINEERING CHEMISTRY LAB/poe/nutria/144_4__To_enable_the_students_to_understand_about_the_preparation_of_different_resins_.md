

#### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins

* Resins are produced from natural sources, such as tree sap, or synthetically, such as epoxy. 
* The production of resins involves the extraction of the raw material and the conversion of it into a usable form. 
* A variety of processes are used to produce different resins, such as polymerization, condensation, and cross-linking. 
* The properties of a resin depend on the type of raw material used, the type of process used to produce it, and the additives used to modify the properties of the resin. 
* Resins can be used in a wide range of applications, such as adhesives, coatings, and composites.
