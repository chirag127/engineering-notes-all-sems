
## Course Outcomes:
- Develop an understanding of the fundamentals of computer science and its applications in the world today. 
- Gain an understanding of the principles of programming and software development. 
- Learn to apply problem-solving techniques to design and implement software solutions. 
- Develop the ability to work collaboratively on software projects. 
- Develop the ability to analyze and evaluate the effectiveness of software solutions. 
- Understand the impact of computing on individuals, organizations, and society.
