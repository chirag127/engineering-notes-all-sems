
# Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

* Formaldehyde is an organic compound composed of a single carbon atom surrounded by two hydrogen atoms. It is a colorless, flammable gas with a pungent odor. It is used in the production of plastics, resins, and other materials.
* Urea formaldehyde resin is a thermoset plastic made from urea, formaldehyde, and other chemicals. It is used in a wide variety of applications including furniture, insulation, and adhesives.
* Adipic acid is a white, crystalline organic acid with a sour taste. It is used in the production of nylon, polyurethanes, and other polymers.
* Paracetamol is an analgesic drug used to treat pain and fever. It works by blocking the production of certain chemicals in the body that cause inflammation and pain.
