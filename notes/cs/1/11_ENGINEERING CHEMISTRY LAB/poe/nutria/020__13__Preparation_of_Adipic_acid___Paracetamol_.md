
### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

* Adipic acid is a white, crystalline solid that is soluble in water. It is used in the production of nylon and is also used as a food additive.
* Paracetamol is a white, crystalline solid that is also soluble in water. It is used as an analgesic and antipyretic and is also used as a food additive.
* Adipic acid can be prepared by the oxidation of cyclohexanol, using nitric acid as the oxidizing agent.
* Paracetamol can be prepared by the reaction of p-aminophenol with acetic anhydride, followed by neutralization with sodium hydroxide.
