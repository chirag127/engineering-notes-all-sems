
### 2. Determination of Hardness of Water Sample by EDTA Method

1. Hardness of water can be determined by a titration method using EDTA (ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid).

2. EDTA is a chelating agent that binds to the metal ions in water, such as calcium and magnesium, and forms a complex.

3. The amount of EDTA required to bind all the metal ions in the sample is determined by titration.

4. The titration is performed by adding a known amount of EDTA to the sample and then titrating with a standard solution of a metal salt, such as calcium chloride.

5. The amount of EDTA required to bind all the metal ions in the sample is determined by the amount of metal salt required to titrate the EDTA.

6. The hardness of the water sample is then calculated by multiplying the amount of metal salt required to titrate the EDTA by the factor for the metal ion being tested.
