
## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS
1. Boyle's Law: This experiment was first performed by Robert Boyle in 1662. It states that the volume of a gas is inversely proportional to the pressure exerted on it.
2. Charles' Law: This experiment was first performed by Jacques Charles in 1787. It states that the volume of a gas is directly proportional to its temperature when pressure is held constant.
3. Avogadro's Law: This experiment was first performed by Amedeo Avogadro in 1811. It states that the volume of a gas is directly proportional to the number of moles of the gas present when pressure and temperature are held constant.
4. Gay-Lussac's Law: This experiment was first performed by Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac in 1809. It states that the pressure of a gas is directly proportional to the temperature when volume is held constant.
5. Graham's Law: This experiment was first performed by Thomas Graham in 1848. It states that the rate of effusion of a gas is inversely proportional to the square root of its molecular mass.
