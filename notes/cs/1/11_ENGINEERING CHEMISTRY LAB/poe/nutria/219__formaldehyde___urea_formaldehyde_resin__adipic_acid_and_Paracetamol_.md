
### Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

- Formaldehyde is a colorless, flammable, strong-smelling chemical used in building materials and many household products. It is used to make products such as plywood, particleboard, and other pressed-wood products; certain insulation materials; glues and adhesives; permanent-press fabrics; paper product coatings; and certain household cleaning products. 

- Urea formaldehyde resin is a synthetic resin made by combining urea and formaldehyde. It is used to make adhesives, binders, coatings, laminates, and molding compounds.

- Adipic acid is an organic compound used in the manufacture of nylon, plasticizers, and lubricants. It is also used as a food additive and in the production of some drugs.

- Paracetamol is a widely used analgesic and antipyretic drug. It is used to treat fever, pain, and other symptoms.
