
### To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of

* pH is a measure of acidity or alkalinity of a solution. It is measured on a scale from 0 to 14, with 0 being the most acidic, 7 being neutral and 14 being the most alkaline. 
* Surface tension is the ability of a liquid to resist an external force. It is measured in dynes/cm. 
* Viscosity is the measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is measured in poise or centipoise.
