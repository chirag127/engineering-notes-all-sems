
# 8. Determination of the Strength of Potassium Dichromate Using Internal Indicator

1. Potassium dichromate is a strong oxidizing agent and is commonly used in the titration of a reducing agent.

2. The strength of potassium dichromate can be determined by titrating it with a standard solution of a reducing agent, such as sodium thiosulfate.

3. The endpoint of the titration is usually determined using an external indicator, such as phenolphthalein.

4. An internal indicator can also be used to determine the endpoint of the titration.

5. The internal indicator is usually a solution of potassium iodide, which is added to the titration mixture at the start of the titration.

6. When the titration is complete, the solution will be orange in color, indicating that all of the potassium dichromate has been consumed.
