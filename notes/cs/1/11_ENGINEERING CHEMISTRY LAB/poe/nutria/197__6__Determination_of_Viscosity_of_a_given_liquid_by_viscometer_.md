
### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer

1. Viscosity is a measure of a liquid's resistance to flow and is an important physical property of a liquid.
2. Viscosity can be measured using a viscometer, which is a device that measures the resistance of a fluid to flow.
3. A viscometer consists of two concentric cylinders, one inside the other, with a small gap between them.
4. The inner cylinder is filled with the liquid to be tested and the outer cylinder is filled with a liquid of known viscosity.
5. The viscometer measures the time taken for the inner cylinder to rotate a certain number of revolutions when both cylinders are rotated.
6. The viscosity of the test liquid is then determined from the time taken for the inner cylinder to rotate the specified number of revolutions.
7. The viscosity of the liquid can be calculated by dividing the time taken by the number of revolutions.
8. The viscosity of the liquid can also be determined by measuring the force required to move the inner cylinder through the liquid.
