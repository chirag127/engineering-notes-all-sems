
# Acid and Paracetamol by Conventional and Green Route

* Acids are substances that release hydrogen ions when dissolved in water. They are commonly used in many industries, such as food and beverage, pharmaceutical, and chemical.
* Paracetamol is a synthetic compound used to treat fever and pain. It is commonly found in over-the-counter medications and is available in both tablet and liquid forms.
* Conventional routes of synthesis involve the use of hazardous chemicals and can be expensive.
* Green routes of synthesis are more environmentally friendly and cost-effective. They involve the use of natural products such as plant extracts and enzymes.
* Synthesis of acid and paracetamol using green routes typically involves the use of biocatalysts, such as enzymes and microorganisms. These biocatalysts can be used to catalyze the reaction and reduce the amount of energy and hazardous chemicals required.
* The use of green routes for the synthesis of acid and paracetamol can lead to improved product quality, reduced environmental impact, and cost savings.
