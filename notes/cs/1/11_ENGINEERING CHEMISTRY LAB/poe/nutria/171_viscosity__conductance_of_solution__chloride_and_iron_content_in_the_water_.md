

# Viscosity, Conductance of Solution, Chloride and Iron Content in Water

1. Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is affected by temperature, pressure, and the concentration of dissolved solids. In water, viscosity is affected by the concentration of dissolved salts, such as chloride, and the presence of suspended particles, such as iron.

2. Conductance is a measure of how easily electricity flows through a solution. It is affected by the concentration of dissolved ions, such as chloride and iron. The higher the concentration of dissolved ions, the higher the conductivity.

3. Chloride is an anion (negatively charged ion) that is found in many natural waters. It is an essential nutrient for plants and animals, but can be toxic in high concentrations.

4. Iron is a micronutrient found in many natural waters. It is essential for healthy plant growth, but can be toxic in high concentrations. Iron can also cause discoloration and staining of surfaces and fabrics.
