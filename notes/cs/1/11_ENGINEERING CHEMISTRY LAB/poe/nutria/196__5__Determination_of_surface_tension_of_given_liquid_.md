
### 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid

- Surface tension is a property of a liquid that causes it to resist an external force, resulting in a surface that acts like an elastic film. 
- Surface tension is caused by the cohesive forces between molecules of the same substance that are in contact with each other. 
- The surface tension of a liquid can be determined by measuring the force required to break a curved surface of the liquid. This is usually done using a device known as a tensiometer.
- The surface tension of a given liquid can also be determined using a device known as a capillary tube. This device consists of a tube that is partially filled with the liquid to be tested. The force of the surface tension of the liquid causes the liquid to rise up the tube. The height to which the liquid rises is a measure of the surface tension.
- Other methods of determining the surface tension of a liquid involve measuring the pressure required to break a bubble of the liquid or measuring the force required to draw a line on the surface of the liquid.
