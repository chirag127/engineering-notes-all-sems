
### 5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid

1. Surface tension is the property of a liquid that causes it to resist an external force.

2. Surface tension is a result of the cohesive forces between the molecules of the liquid.

3. Surface tension can be measured using a variety of methods, such as the Wilhelmy plate method or the capillary rise method.

4. In the Wilhelmy plate method, a plate is placed on the surface of the liquid and the force required to pull it up is measured.

5. In the capillary rise method, the liquid is drawn up a capillary tube and the height to which it rises is measured.

6. The surface tension of a liquid can be used to calculate its surface energy.
