
### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins

- Resins are materials derived from plants, animals, or synthetic sources.
- Resins are generally composed of complex mixtures of organic compounds, such as terpenes, and can be used for a variety of purposes.
- Resins can be used as adhesives, coatings, and sealants.
- Natural resins can be produced by plants and animals, while synthetic resins are produced from petroleum-based chemicals.
- Resins can be prepared by a variety of methods, including distillation, fractional distillation, extraction, and polymerization.
- Distillation is the process of heating a liquid to produce vapors that can be condensed back into liquid form.
- Fractional distillation is a process of separating components of a mixture based on their boiling points.
- Extraction is the process of separating a solid from a liquid by dissolving it in a solvent.
- Polymerization is the process of combining two or more molecules to form larger molecules.
