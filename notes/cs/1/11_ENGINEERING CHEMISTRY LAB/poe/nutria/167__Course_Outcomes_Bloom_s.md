
# Course Outcomes Bloom's

1. Understand and explain Bloom's Taxonomy and its application to course outcomes.
2. Analyze course outcomes and determine their level of complexity according to Bloom's Taxonomy.
3. Design course outcomes that align with Bloom's Taxonomy.
4. Create course outcomes that are measurable and assessable according to Bloom's Taxonomy.
5. Develop course outcomes that are applicable to the subject matter and relevant to the course objectives.
6. Evaluate course outcomes and their effectiveness in achieving the course objectives.
7. Synthesize course outcomes to ensure they are comprehensive and meaningful.
