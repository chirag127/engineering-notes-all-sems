
### 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity of water:

1. Chloride content can be determined by titration with silver nitrate, using a spectrophotometer to measure the intensity of the silver chloride color.
2. Hardness of water can be determined by EDTA titration, using a colorimeter to measure the intensity of the EDTA-metal ion complex.
3. Alkalinity of water can be measured by titration with hydrochloric acid, using a pH meter to measure the pH of the solution.
