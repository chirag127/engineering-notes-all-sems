
### 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a Solution

1. Cell conductance is a measure of the ability of a solution to conduct electricity. 
2. It is expressed as the ratio of the electrical current passing through the solution to the potential difference across it. 
3. Cell conductance is affected by the concentration of ions in a solution, as well as the temperature. 
4. The cell conductance of a solution can be determined using a conductivity meter. 
5. This instrument is used to measure the electrical resistance of a solution by passing a known current through it. 
6. The cell conductance is then calculated from the measured resistance and the applied voltage. 
7. The cell conductance of a solution can also be determined by measuring the current and voltage across a known resistance in the solution. 
8. The cell conductance is then calculated from the measured current and voltage.
