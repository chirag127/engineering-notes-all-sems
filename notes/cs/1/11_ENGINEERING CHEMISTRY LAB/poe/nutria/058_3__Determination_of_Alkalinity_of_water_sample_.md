

### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of Water Sample

* Alkalinity is the measure of the capacity of water to resist changes in pH that would make the water more acidic. 
* Alkalinity is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) of calcium carbonate (CaCO3). 
* Alkalinity is an important parameter for water quality and can be used to assess the water's ability to resist changes in pH.
* Alkalinity can be determined by titration with a strong acid, such as hydrochloric acid (HCl).
* The titration is performed by adding small amounts of acid to a sample of water until the pH of the water drops below a certain point. 
* The amount of acid added is proportional to the alkalinity of the water sample.
* The alkalinity of the sample can be determined by measuring the amount of acid added and then calculating the alkalinity from the results.
