
### 8. Determination of the Strength of Potassium Dichromate Using Internal Indicator

1. Potassium dichromate is a strong oxidizing agent, and its strength can be determined by titration with a standard solution of a reducing agent, such as sodium thiosulfate.
2. The strength of potassium dichromate can also be determined using an internal indicator, such as methyl orange.
3. The internal indicator is added to the potassium dichromate solution, and the solution is titrated with the standard solution of sodium thiosulfate.
4. The end point of the titration is indicated by a change in the colour of the solution.
5. The strength of the potassium dichromate is calculated by using the formula: Strength = (Volume of sodium thiosulfate) x (Molarity of sodium thiosulfate) / (Volume of potassium dichromate).
