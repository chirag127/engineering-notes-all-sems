
### Viscosity, Conductance of Solution, Chloride and Iron Content in the Water

* Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is determined by measuring the amount of force required to move a given amount of fluid through a given distance.
* Conductance of solution is a measure of the ability of an aqueous solution to conduct electricity. It is determined by measuring the amount of electrical current that is conducted by the solution.
* Chloride is an anion found in many aqueous solutions. It is a major component of seawater and is also found in many industrial and municipal wastewater effluents.
* Iron is an essential nutrient for many organisms. It can be found in many natural waters, but its concentration can vary greatly depending on the source. Iron can also be released into the environment from industrial and agricultural activities.
