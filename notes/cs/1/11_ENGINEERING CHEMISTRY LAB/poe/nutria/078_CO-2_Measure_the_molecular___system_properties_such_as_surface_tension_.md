

# CO₂: Measuring Molecular and System Properties such as Surface Tension

1. CO₂ is a molecular compound composed of two oxygen atoms and one carbon atom.
2. The molecular weight of CO₂ is 44.01 g/mol.
3. The boiling point of CO₂ is -78.5 °C.
4. The surface tension of CO₂ can be measured using a tensiometer.
5. The surface tension of CO₂ is approximately 24.2 mN/m at room temperature.
6. The surface tension of CO₂ is affected by temperature and pressure, so these variables must be taken into account when measuring surface tension.
7. The surface tension of CO₂ can also be measured using a Wilhelmy plate method or a pendant drop method.
8. The surface tension of CO₂ can also be measured using a bubble pressure method.
9. The surface tension of CO₂ can be used to calculate the surface area of a liquid.
