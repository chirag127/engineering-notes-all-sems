
# Level
1. A level is a type of measurement used to determine the height or depth of an object, person, or other entity.
2. Levels can be used to measure the vertical distance between two points, or to measure the angle of a slope.
3. Levels are commonly used in construction, engineering, surveying, and other fields.
4. Levels typically consist of an elongated body with a bubble vial attached to it.
5. The bubble vial is used to measure the angle of the level relative to a flat surface.
6. Levels are usually calibrated to indicate a level surface when the bubble is centered in the vial. 
7. Levels can also be used to measure the relative height of objects with respect to one another.
