
## CO-3 Measure the hardness and alkalinity of the water. K3

1. Hardness of water is the measure of the concentration of calcium and magnesium ions in the water. 
2. Alkalinity of water is the measure of the concentration of bicarbonate, carbonate, hydroxide and other alkaline substances in the water.
3. Hardness and alkalinity of water can be measured with a titration test using a titration kit.
4. The titration process involves adding drops of a reagent to the water sample until a color change is observed.
5. The number of drops of reagent added to the water sample is then used to calculate the hardness and alkalinity of the water.
6. Hardness and alkalinity of water can also be measured with a water hardness test strip.
7. The test strip will change color when dipped into the water sample, and the color change can be compared to a chart to determine the hardness and alkalinity of the water.
