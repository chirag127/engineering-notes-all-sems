
### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:
- Understand the fundamentals of computer science
- Identify and describe the various components of computer systems
- Explain the principles of algorithmic problem-solving
- Construct and analyze algorithms
- Implement algorithms in a programming language
- Understand the principles of data structures
- Design and implement data structures
- Understand the principles of computer networks
- Understand the principles of operating systems
- Design and implement distributed systems
- Understand the principles of database systems
- Design and implement database systems
- Understand the principles of artificial intelligence
- Design and implement intelligent systems
