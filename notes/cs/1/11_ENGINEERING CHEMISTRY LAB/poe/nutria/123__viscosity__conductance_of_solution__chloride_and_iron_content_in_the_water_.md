
### Viscosity, Conductance of Solution, Chloride and Iron Content in the Water

* Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. It is determined by the forces between molecules and is affected by temperature, pressure and the type of fluid.
* Conductance of a solution is a measure of how easily electricity flows through it. It is determined by the concentration of ions in the solution, as well as the type of ions present.
* Chloride is an anion found in many aqueous solutions. It is a major component of seawater and is commonly found in natural waters.
* Iron is an essential nutrient for many organisms and is found in many natural waters. It can be present in a variety of forms, including soluble and insoluble forms.
