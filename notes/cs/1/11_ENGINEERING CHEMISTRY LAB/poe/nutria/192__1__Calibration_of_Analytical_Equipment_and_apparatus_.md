
### 1. Calibration of Analytical Equipment and Apparatus

1. Calibration is the process of verifying the accuracy of the measurements taken by a particular instrument or device.
2. The process involves comparing the instrument’s readings to a known standard and adjusting the instrument’s settings to ensure accuracy.
3. Calibration is important to ensure that measurements taken are accurate and reliable.
4. Analytical equipment and apparatus are used in laboratories and other settings to measure physical and chemical properties of substances.
5. This type of equipment may include pH meters, spectrophotometers, chromatographs, thermometers, and balances.
6. In order to ensure the accuracy of the measurements taken by the equipment, calibration is necessary.
7. The process of calibrating analytical equipment and apparatus involves comparing the instrument’s readings to a known standard, and adjusting the instrument’s settings to ensure accuracy.
8. Calibration should be done regularly to ensure accuracy and reliability of measurements taken.
9. Proper calibration can also help to reduce errors and improve the accuracy of the results.
