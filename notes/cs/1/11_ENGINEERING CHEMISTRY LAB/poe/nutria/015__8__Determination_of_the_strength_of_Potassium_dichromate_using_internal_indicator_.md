
### 8. Determination of the Strength of Potassium Dichromate Using Internal Indicator

1. Potassium dichromate is an inorganic compound, with the chemical formula K2Cr2O7. It is a strong oxidizing agent and is used in a variety of industrial and laboratory applications.

2. An internal indicator can be used to determine the strength of a potassium dichromate solution. This indicator is a compound which changes colour at a certain pH.

3. To determine the strength of a potassium dichromate solution, a sample of the solution is taken and a few drops of the internal indicator are added. The colour change of the indicator will indicate the strength of the solution.

4. The strength of a potassium dichromate solution can also be determined by titration. In this method, a known volume of the potassium dichromate solution is titrated with a standard solution of a base. The volume of the base required to neutralize the acid is an indication of the strength of the potassium dichromate solution.

5. The strength of a potassium dichromate solution can also be determined by spectrophotometry. In this method, the absorbance of the solution is measured at a certain wavelength. The absorbance of the solution is an indication of the strength of the solution.
