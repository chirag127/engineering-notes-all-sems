
### 3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of a liquid.

- **pH** is a measure of the acidity or basicity of a liquid. It is measured on a scale from 0 to 14, with 7 being neutral. A pH below 7 is acidic, and a pH above 7 is basic.

- **Surface tension** is a measure of the cohesion of the molecules of a liquid. It is measured in units of force per unit length, such as dynes/cm.

- **Viscosity** is a measure of the resistance of a liquid to flow. It is measured in units of force times distance, such as poise or centipoise.
