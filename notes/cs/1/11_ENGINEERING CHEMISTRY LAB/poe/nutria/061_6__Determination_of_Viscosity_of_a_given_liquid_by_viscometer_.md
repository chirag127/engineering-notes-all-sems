

### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer

1. Viscometers are instruments used to measure the viscosity of a liquid.
2. The viscosity of a liquid is a measure of its resistance to flow.
3. The viscosity of a liquid can be determined by measuring the time it takes for a given volume of liquid to flow through a capillary tube.
4. The viscosity of a given liquid can also be determined by using a viscometer.
5. A viscometer consists of a cup, a spindle and an adjustable weight.
6. The cup is filled with the liquid to be tested and the spindle is placed in the cup.
7. The adjustable weight is then placed on the spindle, and the time it takes for the spindle to reach a certain depth is measured.
8. The viscosity of the liquid is then calculated based on the time it took for the spindle to reach the desired depth.
