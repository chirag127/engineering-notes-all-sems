
### 7. Determination of the Strength of Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate Using External Indicator

1. Ferrous ammonium sulfate is an inorganic compound that is composed of iron, ammonium, and sulfate ions.
2. This compound is commonly used in analytical chemistry to determine the strength of a solution, and is often used in titration experiments.
3. An external indicator is a chemical compound that can be used to determine the end point of a titration.
4. The most commonly used external indicator for ferrous ammonium sulfate is thymol blue.
5. Thymol blue changes color when exposed to ferrous ammonium sulfate, and this color change indicates the end point of the titration.
6. The strength of ferrous ammonium sulfate can be determined by adding a known amount of thymol blue to the solution and observing the color change.
7. Once the color change has been observed, the strength of the ferrous ammonium sulfate can be determined by comparing the color change to a standard chart.
