
#### 4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins

1. Resins are a type of natural or synthetic polymer made up of many repeating units linked together.
2. Natural resins are usually produced by plants, while synthetic resins are produced in a laboratory.
3. Resins can be used for a variety of applications, including coatings, adhesives, paints, and plastics.
4. Preparation of different resins involves the selection of the appropriate monomers, polymerization of the monomers, and post-polymerization treatments.
5. The selection of the monomers is based on the desired properties of the final product.
6. The polymerization process may involve either a chemical or physical reaction, depending on the type of monomers used.
7. Post-polymerization treatments may include curing, cross-linking, and other treatments to improve the properties of the resin.
