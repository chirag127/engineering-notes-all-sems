
### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

1. Analytical instruments are used to measure physical and chemical properties in order to gain insight into the composition and characteristics of a sample.
2. These instruments can be used in a variety of fields, such as food and beverage testing, environmental monitoring, pharmaceutical analysis, and more.
3. Common analytical instruments include spectroscopy, chromatography, and mass spectrometry.
4. Spectroscopy is a technique that uses light or other forms of electromagnetic radiation to measure the composition of a sample.
5. Chromatography is a separation technique that uses a stationary phase and a mobile phase to separate components of a sample.
6. Mass spectrometry is a technique used to identify and quantify the chemical components of a sample.
7. Analytical instruments can be used to measure physical properties such as pH, viscosity, and refractive index, as well as chemical properties such as concentration, purity, and stability.
8. These instruments can also be used to analyze complex mixtures and detect trace amounts of substances.
9. The accuracy and precision of the measurements taken with analytical instruments depend on the quality of the instrument and the skill of the operator.
