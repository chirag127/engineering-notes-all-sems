
# 9. Determination of Available Chlorine in Bleaching Powder

1. Bleaching powder is a white, odorless powder that contains calcium hypochlorite and is used as a disinfectant and bleaching agent.
2. Available chlorine is the amount of chlorine in the bleaching powder that is available for disinfecting or bleaching.
3. The available chlorine can be measured by titration with sodium thiosulfate.
4. To determine the available chlorine, a sample of the bleaching powder is dissolved in water and then titrated with a standardized sodium thiosulfate solution.
5. The amount of sodium thiosulfate used in the titration is proportional to the amount of available chlorine present in the sample.
6. The end point of the titration is determined by the addition of a few drops of starch solution, which causes the solution to turn blue.
7. The amount of available chlorine in the sample is calculated based on the amount of sodium thiosulfate used in the titration.
