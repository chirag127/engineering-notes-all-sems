
### CO-1 Get an Understanding of the Use of Different Analytical Instruments. K3

1. Analytical instruments are tools used to measure, analyze, and interpret physical properties of substances.
2. Common analytical instruments include spectrophotometers, chromatographs, mass spectrometers, microscopes, and refractometers.
3. Spectrophotometers measure the amount of light absorbed by a substance, which can be used to determine the concentration of a solution.
4. Chromatographs are used to separate and analyze mixtures of substances.
5. Mass spectrometers measure the mass-to-charge ratio of ions in a sample, which can be used to identify the components of a mixture.
6. Microscopes are used to observe and analyze small particles and structures.
7. Refractometers measure the refractive index of a substance, which can be used to determine the concentration of a solution.
