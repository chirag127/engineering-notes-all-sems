
# Experiments 
1.  Conduct an experiment to measure the speed of sound in air. 
2.  Measure the acceleration due to gravity experimentally. 
3.  Investigate the relationship between current and potential difference in a resistor. 
4.  Determine the resistance of a given wire by plotting a graph of potential difference versus current. 
5.  Determine the wavelength of light emitted by a sodium lamp. 
6.  Investigate the relationship between the length of a wire and its resistance. 
7.  Determine the frequency of an AC supply. 
8.  Investigate the effect of temperature on the resistance of a given material. 
9.  Measure the capacitance of a capacitor. 
10. Investigate the effect of frequency on the power factor of an AC circuit.
