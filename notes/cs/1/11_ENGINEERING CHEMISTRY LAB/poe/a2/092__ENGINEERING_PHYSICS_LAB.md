# ENGINEERING PHYSICS LAB

1. Introduction to Lab Equipment
- Vernier caliper- used to measure internal and external dimensions of an object with high precision.
- Screw gauge- used to measure the thickness of thin sheets and diameter of wires.
- Spherometer- used to measure curvature or the shape of spherical surfaces.
- Stopwatch- used to measure time intervals with high precision.
- Simple pendulum- used to study periodic motion and verify gravitational acceleration.

2. Measurements and Errors
- Types of errors: systematic error, random error, gross error.
- Absolute and relative errors.
- Significant figures and rounding off numbers.
- Rules for calculation to get correct answers with proper precision.

3. Experiments to Study Units and Dimensions
- To verify the laws of conservation of mass and energy.
- To determine the density of solid and liquid.
- To determine volume, density and linear expansion coefficient of a material.

4. Experiments Based on Mechanics
- To determine the acceleration due to gravity using simple pendulum.
- To find the moment of inertia of a flywheel.
- To find the velocity and acceleration of an object using ticker tape timer.
