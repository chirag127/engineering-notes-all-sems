# 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution

1. Take two platinum electrodes and immerse them in the given solution. Connect the electrodes to a cell containing a known resistance R.
2. Note the resistance reading R' of the cell.
3. Calculate the cell conductance G using the formula:
G = 1/R'

4. The cell conductance gives a measure of the ability of the solution to conduct electric current. It depends on the nature, number and mobility of the ions present in the solution.
5. A higher value of conductance indicates a larger number of free ions and greater ionic mobility leading to easier conduction of electric current. A lower value of conductance indicates fewer free ions or lower ionic mobility leading to difficulty in conduction of electric current.
