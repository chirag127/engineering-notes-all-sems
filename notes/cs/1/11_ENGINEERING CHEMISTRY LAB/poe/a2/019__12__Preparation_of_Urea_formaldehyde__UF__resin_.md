### 12. Preparation of Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin.

1. Urea and formaldehyde are taken in 1:2 molar ratio in a reactor.
2. The mixture is heated to 90-95°C with continuous stirring.
3. As the temperature increases, the mixture becomes viscous. The temperature is maintained for 1-2 hours.
4. An acid catalyst such as hydrochloric acid or sulphuric acid is added to start the polymerization reaction. The temperature is raised to 105-110°C and maintained for few hours.
5. When the resin attains desired viscosity, the heat is removed and the resin is cooled.
6. The resin is neutralized using a base to stop the reaction.
7. The resin is diluted for storage and transport.

The key points to note are:
1. Precise molar ratio of urea and formaldehyde is important to control the molecular weight and properties of resin.
2. Heating and maintaining the temperature is critical for the reaction to proceed and to achieve desired viscosity.
3. The type and amount of acid catalyst added controls the rate of reaction and final properties of resin.
4. The extent of reaction is monitored by measuring viscosity. When desired viscosity is reached, the reaction is stopped.
5. The resin is neutralized and diluted for storage and further use.
