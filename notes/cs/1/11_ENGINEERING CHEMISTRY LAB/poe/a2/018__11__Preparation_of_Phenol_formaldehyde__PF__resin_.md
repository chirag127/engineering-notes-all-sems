### 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin.

1. React phenol and formaldehyde in the presence of an alkaline catalyst such as sodium hydroxide.
2. The reaction is exothermic and proceeds rapidly at first. As the molecular weight of the resin increases, the rate of reaction decreases.
3. The properties of the resin depend upon the molar ratio of phenol to formaldehyde and the degree of polymerization.
4. Higher molar ratios of formaldehyde to phenol and higher degrees of polymerization lead to more cross-linking and higher softening points and better chemical resistance. However, processability is reduced.
5. The alkaline catalyst is neutralized and the resin is cooled and filtered.
6. The resin can be supplied as a powder or aqueous solution of various solids contents.
7. Fillers, plasticizers, and other additives can be incorporated depending on the end use.
8. Curing of the resin involves additional polymerization and cross-linking as a result of the application of heat and pressure or by means of a catalyst. This leads to a three-dimensional network polymer with good mechanical and chemical properties.
