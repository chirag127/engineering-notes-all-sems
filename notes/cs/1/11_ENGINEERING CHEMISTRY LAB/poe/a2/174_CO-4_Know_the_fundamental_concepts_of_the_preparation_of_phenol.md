# CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol

1. Cumene process: Propylene is reacted with benzene in the presence of phosphoric acid catalyst to produce cumene. Cumene is then oxidized to produce cumene hydroperoxide, which upon acidification produces phenol and acetone. This is the major industrial process for the production of phenol.

2. Dow process: Benzene is reacted with propylene in the presence of aluminium chloride catalyst to produce chlorobenzene. Chlorobenzene is then hydrolyzed with calcium hydroxide to produce phenol.

3. Raschig process: Benzene is nitrated to produce nitrobenzene, which upon reduction with iron and hydrochloric acid produces aniline. Aniline is then reacted with nitrous acid to produce phenol. This process is obsolete now.

4. From diazonium salt: The diazonium salt of aniline is thermally decomposed to produce phenol. This process again is obsolete.
