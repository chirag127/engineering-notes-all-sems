### 16. Element detection and identification of functional groups in organic compounds.

1. Elemental analysis: To determine the percentage composition of elements (C, H, N, O, halogens etc.) in an organic compound. Methods used are:
- Combustion method: Substance is burned in oxygen and products are analyzed volumetrically or gravimetrically.
- Chromatographic methods.

2. Identification of functional groups:
- Test for unsaturation: Bromine water test, Baeyer's test, Sudan III test etc.
- Test for alkynes: Acidified KMnO4, Ammoniacal AgNO3 etc.
- Test for aldehydes and ketones: Fehling's solution, Tollens' reagent, 2,4-DNP test etc.
- Test for carboxylic acids: Litmus test, NaHCO3 test etc.
- Test for amines: Ninhydrin test, picric acid test etc.
- Test for halogens: Beilstein test, silver nitrate test etc.

The tests are based on characteristic chemical reactions of the functional groups. Procedures and inferences of the tests must be clearly understood to identify the functional groups in organic compounds.
