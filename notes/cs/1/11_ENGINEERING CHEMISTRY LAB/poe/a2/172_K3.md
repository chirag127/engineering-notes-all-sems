# K3

1. K3 is the *third* tier in the Indian education system. It refers to classes XI and XII.
2. In K3, students choose their specific streams - Science, Commerce or Humanities. The stream chosen leads to different career paths.
3. In the Science stream, students study subjects like Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology. This paves the way for careers in engineering and medicine.
4. The Commerce stream comprises subjects like Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics and Mathematics. This stream prepares students for careers in management, banking, accounting, etc.
5. The Humanities or Arts stream includes subjects like History, Geography, Political Science, Sociology, Psychology, Economics, etc. This stream leads to careers in social sciences, law, designing, teaching, etc.
6. At the end of K3, students appear for board exams which are critical for getting admissions to undergraduate courses. Performance in these exams is extremely important.
7. K3 is a critical juncture where students decide the direction of their future careers and lives. They must choose the stream wisely based on their interests, strengths and career aspirations.
