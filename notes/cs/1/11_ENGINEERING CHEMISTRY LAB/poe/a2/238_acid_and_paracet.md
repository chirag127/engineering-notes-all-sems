# Acid and Paracetamol

<header>
Acids and paracetamol are common compounds with distinct properties and uses. This study material summarizes key facts about acids and paracetamol.
</header>

## Acids

- Acids taste sour and have a pH less than 7
- Acids turn blue litmus paper red
- Acids neutralize bases to form salts
- Common acids include hydrochloric acid, sulfuric acid, and acetic acid
- Acids have hydrogen ions that can donate to other molecules

## Paracetamol

- Paracetamol is an analgesic (pain reliever) and antipyretic (fever reducer)
- Paracetamol's chemical name is N-acetyl-p-aminophenol
- Paracetamol is acetaminophen, which is the active ingredient in Tylenol
- Paracetamol works by inhibiting cyclooxygenase enzymes to reduce prostaglandin production
- The maximum daily dose of paracetamol is 4,000 mg to avoid liver damage
