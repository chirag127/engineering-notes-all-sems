### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route.

1. Adipic acid:
- Synthesis by oxidation of cyclohexanol or cyclohexanone (conventional route)
- Synthesis by bio-based routes using microorganisms (green route)
2. Paracetamol:
- Synthesis from p-aminophenol using peroxyacids (conventional route)
- Synthesis from p-aminophenol using hydrogen peroxide and formic acid (green route)
3. Comparison of conventional and green routes:
- Conventional routes use toxic chemicals and generate waste
- Green routes are sustainable and eco-friendly using bio-based feedstocks and enzymes
- However, green routes may have lower yields and higher costs
4. Industrial significance of synthesizing organic compounds via green routes
- Reduces pollution and dependence on petrochemicals
- Aligns with principles of green chemistry and sustainable development
