### 12. Preparation of Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin.

1. Urea and formaldehyde are the two main ingredients used to prepare UF resin.
2. Urea and formaldehyde are mixed in 1:2 molar ratio and the mixture is heated to 60-90°C with constant stirring.
3. An acid catalyst such as hydrochloric acid or sulphuric acid is added to initiate the polymerization reaction. The addition of acid decreases the activation energy of the reaction and increases the rate of reaction.
4. As the reaction proceeds, the viscosity of the mixture increases significantly. The reaction is stopped at the desired viscosity by neutralizing the acid and cooling the mixture.
5. The resulting resin is diluted with water to the desired concentration and pH is adjusted to 7-8 for safe handling and storage.
6. The properties of the final resin depend on the molar ratio of urea to formaldehyde and degree of polymerization which in turn depends on the reaction time and temperature.
