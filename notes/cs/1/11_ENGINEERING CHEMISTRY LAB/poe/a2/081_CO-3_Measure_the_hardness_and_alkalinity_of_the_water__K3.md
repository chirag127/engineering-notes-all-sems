# CO-3 Measure the hardness and alkalinity of the water. K3

1. Hardness of water:
- The hardness of water is measured in terms of calcium and magnesium salts present in it.
- These salts are commonly known as calcium and magnesium bicarbonates and chlorides.
- Hard water contains dissolved polyvalent metallic ions, usually calcium and magnesium.
- The hardness of water can be determined using EDTA titration method.
- The endpoints in EDTA titration are determined using indicators like Eriochrome Black T or Patton-Reeder indicator.

2. Alkalinity of water:
- Alkalinity refers to the ability of water to neutralize acids.
- It is caused due to the presence of carbonate and bicarbonate salts such as sodium carbonate, sodium bicarbonate, calcium carbonate, etc.
- The alkalinity of water can be determined by acid-base titration using 0.1 N HCl and phenolphthalein indicator.
- The alkalinity is expressed in terms of equivalent quantities of calcium carbonate.
