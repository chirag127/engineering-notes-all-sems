# Analytical Instruments - Fundamental Concepts

1. Spectroscopy
- Study of interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation.
- Types: UV-Visible, Infrared, NMR, Mass spectrometry, etc.
- Used to determine qualitative and quantitative information about the analyte.

2. Chromatography
- Physical method of separation in which the components to be separated are distributed between two phases, one stationary and one moving.
- Types: Thin layer chromatography, column chromatography, gas chromatography, high performance liquid chromatography, etc.
- Used to separate and analyze complex mixtures.

3. Electrophoresis
- Movement of charged particles under an electric field.
- Used to separate charged molecules based on size and charge.
- Types: Gel electrophoresis, Capillary electrophoresis, etc.

4. Thermal analysis
- Study of changes in physical properties of a material as a function of temperature.
- Instrument: Differential scanning calorimeter.
- Used to analyze thermal transitions, decomposition, glass transitions, cure reactions, etc. of a sample.
