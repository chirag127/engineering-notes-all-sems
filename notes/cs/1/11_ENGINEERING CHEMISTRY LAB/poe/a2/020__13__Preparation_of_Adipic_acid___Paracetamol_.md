### 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol.

**Adipic acid**:

1. Oxidation of cyclohexanol or cyclohexanone with nitric acid.
2. Hydration of butadiene followed by oxidation.

**Properties**: White crystalline solid; M.P. 152°C; solubility in water (0.34%); KMnO4 oxidizes it to oxalic acid.

**Uses**: Manufacture of nylon and other polyamides.

**Paracetamol**:

1. Acetylation of p-aminophenol with acetic anhydride.

**Properties**: Colourless crystalline solid; M.P. 169°C; soluble in hot water and polar organic solvents.

**Uses**: As an analgesic and antipyretic.
