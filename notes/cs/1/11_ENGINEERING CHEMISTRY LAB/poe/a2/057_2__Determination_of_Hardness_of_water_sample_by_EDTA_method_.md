## 2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method.

- EDTA stands for Ethylene Diamine Tetra Acetic Acid. It is a hexadentate ligand which can bind to metal ions forming stable complexes.
- The hardness of water is determined by the amount of calcium and magnesium salts present in it. EDTA method determines the total hardness of water by titrating it against a standard EDTA solution.
- Procedure:
-- Take 100ml of the given water sample in a conical flask
-- Add 3-4 drops of eriochrome black T indicator to it. The solution will appear wine red in color.
-- Titrate the solution with 0.01M EDTA solution from a burette till the color of solution changes to blue.
-- Note down the volume of EDTA solution consumed.
-- Perform a blank titration with distilled water and note the volume of EDTA consumed.
-- Subtract the volume of EDTA in blank from the volume consumed by the sample.
-- 1ml of 0.01M EDTA is equivalent to 1mg of CaCO3. Calculate the hardness from this.
- The hardness is expressed as mg/L or ppm of CaCO3. This method can be used to determine temporary as well as permanent hardness of water. It is a very accurate method for hardness determination.
