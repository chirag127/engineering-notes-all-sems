### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments.

1. Spectroscopy: Study of interaction of electromagnetic radiation with matter. Includes techniques such as Atomic Absorption Spectroscopy, Atomic Emission Spectroscopy, Infrared Spectroscopy, UV-Visible Spectroscopy, NMR Spectroscopy, Mass Spectroscopy, etc. Used to identify and quantify analytes.

2. Chromatography: Separation technique based on differential partitioning between mobile and stationary phases. Includes techniques such as Thin Layer Chromatography, Column Chromatography, Gas Chromatography, High Performance Liquid Chromatography, etc. Used to separate and purify analytes.

3. Electrophoresis: Separation technique based on differential migration of charged analytes in an electric field. Includes techniques such as Gel Electrophoresis, Capillary Electrophoresis, Isoelectric Focusing, etc. Used to separate analytes based on size and charge.

4. Thermal Analysis: Based on measurement of physical and chemical changes of a material as a function of increasing temperature. Includes techniques such as Thermogravimetry, Differential Thermal Analysis, Differential Scanning Calorimetry, etc. Used to study thermal properties of analytes.

5. Electrochemistry: Based on electric potential difference to drive a redox reaction. Includes techniques such as Cyclic Voltammetry, Potentiometry, Coulometry, etc. Used to study and apply redox reactions.
