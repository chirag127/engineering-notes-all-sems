### 10. Determination of chloride content in water sample.

1. Take 100 mL of given water sample in a conical flask.
2. Add 2-3 drops of potassium chromate indicator solution to it. The solution turns yellow.
3. Titrate the solution with silver nitrate solution (0.1 N) from a burette.
4. Shake the conical flask after each addition of silver nitrate solution to ensure proper mixing.
5. Continue titration till the yellow colour of potassium chromate just disappears.
6. Note the burette reading at this point. It gives the volume of silver nitrate solution used.
7. Repeat the titration twice or thrice and take the average of burette readings.
8. Calculate the chloride content in given water sample using the formula,

Cl− (in mg/L) = ( burette reading × N × 35.45 ) / sample volume

9. Where, N is the molarity of silver nitrate and 35.45 is the molecular weight of chloride.
