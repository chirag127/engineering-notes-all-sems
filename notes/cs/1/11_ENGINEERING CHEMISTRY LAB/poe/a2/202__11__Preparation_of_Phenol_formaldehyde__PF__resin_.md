### 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin.

1. Phenol and formaldehyde are the two main reactants used in the preparation of phenol formaldehyde resin.
2. The molar ratio of phenol to formaldehyde can vary depending upon the type of resin required. Generally, it is kept between 1:1 to 1:2.
3. An excess of formaldehyde is used to produce highly cross-linked, moisture-resistant resin.
4. A catalyst such as sodium hydroxide is used to promote the reaction.
5. The reaction is carried out in batch reactors at temperature ranging from 70-100°C.
6. As the reaction proceeds, the viscosity of the reaction mixture increases due to the formation of high molecular weight poly-mers.
7. Once the desired properties are achieved, the reaction is stopped by neutralizing the catalyst and cooling the reaction mixture.
8. The resin is then filtered and stabilized by adding inhibitors to prevent gelation.

The resin so produced has a three-dimensional network structure and contains methylol groups. It can undergo condensation with the release of water to form a thermosetting plastic. It has good combination of properties such as hardness, strength, moisture resistance, dimensional stability, etc. and finds many applications in wood adhesives and laminates.
