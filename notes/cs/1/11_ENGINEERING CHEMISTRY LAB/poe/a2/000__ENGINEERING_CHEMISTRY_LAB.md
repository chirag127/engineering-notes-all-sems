# ENGINEERING CHEMISTRY LAB

1. Titration
- Titration is a method used to determine the concentration of an unknown solution.
- It involves gradual addition of a standard solution to the unknown solution until the reaction is complete.
- The end point of the titration is detected using indicators.
- The concentration of the unknown solution is calculated using the volume of standard solution added.

2. pH Measurement
- pH of a solution is measured using a pH meter or pH paper.
- pH meter consists of a probe with electrodes to measure the H+ ion concentration.
- pH paper contains dyes that change color with pH. The pH is determined by comparing the color with a standard scale.
- pH has values from 0 to 14 with 0 being very acidic, 7 being neutral and 14 being very basic.

3. Qualitative Analysis of Cations
- Cations in a given salt can be identified using certain chemical tests.
- Flame test is used to identify metal ions that emit characteristic color in the flame.
- Precipitation reactions are used to separate cation groups and identify individual cations.
- Cations are identified by observing properties of precipitates like color, solubility, etc. and comparing with known reactions of cations.
