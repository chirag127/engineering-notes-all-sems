# 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

- Take about 0.5g of ferrous ammonium sulphate and dissolve it in 100ml of water. 
- Add 2-3 drops of concentrated sulphuric acid and 10ml of phosphoric acid solution. 
- Dilute 10ml of given potassium dichromate solution to 100ml with water. 
- Take 20ml of ferrous ammonium sulphate solution in a conical flask and add 2ml of potassium dichromate solution. 
- Add 3-4 drops of fresh potassium ferricyanide solution as an external indicator. 
- Titrate the solution with dichromate solution till the blue colour just disappears. 
- Note down the volume of dichromate solution used. 
- Repeat the experiment thrice and take the mean of the three readings. 
- Calculate the molarity and strength of potassium dichromate solution.
