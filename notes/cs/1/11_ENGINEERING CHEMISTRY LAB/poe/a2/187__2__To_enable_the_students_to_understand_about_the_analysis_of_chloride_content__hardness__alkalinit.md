### 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity of water.

1. Chloride content:
- Water is treated with silver nitrate solution.
- White precipitate of silver chloride is formed in the presence of chloride.
- The precipitate is filtered, dried and weighed.
- The weight of silver chloride precipitate is determined to measure chloride content.

2. Hardness of water:
- Temporary hardness is determined by titrating water with standard EDTA solution.
- Magnesium and calcium ions form complex with EDTA which is determined by indicator color change.
- Permanent hardness is determined by titrating water with standard sodium carbonate solution.
- Carbonate ions react with calcium and magnesium ions in the water which is determined by indicator color change.

3. Alkalinity of water:
- Water is titrated with standard hydrochloric acid or sulphuric acid solution.
- Carbonate and bicarbonate alkalinity react with the acid which is determined by indicator color change.
- The volume of acid reacted corresponds to alkalinity content which can be calculated.
