# Acid and Paracetamol by Conventional and Green Route

1. Conventional Route
- Acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin) is reacted with acetic anhydride which forms aspirin acetate
- Aspirin acetate is hydrolyzed to form salicylic acid
- Salicylic acid is reacted with phosphorus trichloride and phosphoryl chloride to form salicyloyl chloride
- Salicyloyl chloride is reacted with 4-aminophenol to form paracetamol

2. Green Route
- Eugenol from clove oil is oxidized to form vanillin
- Vanillin is reacted with hydrocyanic acid to form 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde cyanohydrin
- 4-Hydroxybenzaldehyde cyanohydrin is hydrolyzed to form 4-hydroxybenzaldehyde
- 4-Hydroxybenzaldehyde is reduced to 4-aminophenol
- 4-Aminophenol is reacted with salicyloyl chloride to form paracetamol

The conventional route uses toxic chemicals and produces harmful wastes while the green route uses eugenol from natural resources resulting in an eco-friendly process. Both the routes produce paracetamol which is an extensively used analgesic and antipyretic drug.
