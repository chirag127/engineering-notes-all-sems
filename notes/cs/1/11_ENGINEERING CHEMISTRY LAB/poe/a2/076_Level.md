# Level

## Point 1
- Level refers to the height or depth of something in relation to a reference point.
- The level of a quantity refers to its relative value or magnitude.
- In measurement, level is a device which allows heights or depths to be measured relative to a point of reference.

## Point 2
- Leveling is the procedure of measuring heights of physical points, and using these to derive the height of other points relative to some vertical datum.
- Differences in height between points are called gradients. Spirit level tools allow leveling to determine if a surface is horizontal or matches a specified gradient.
- Leveling measurements are converted into relative heights using a vertical datum.

## Point 3
- Leveling procedures are widely used in surveying and construction, where measurements refer to established vertical references or benchmarks to monitor the flatness or grade elevation of a construction project.
- Laser level tools project horizontal planes to aid leveling procedures and offer speed and accuracy compared to traditional spirit levels.
