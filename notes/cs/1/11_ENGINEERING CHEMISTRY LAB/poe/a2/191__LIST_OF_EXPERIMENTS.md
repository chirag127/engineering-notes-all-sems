## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

1. Experimental Determination of Young's Modulus
- Measure the length of a metal rod as a function of the applied force. Plot stress vs strain and determine the slope which gives Young's modulus.

2. Experimental Determination of the Acceleration Due to Gravity
- Use simple pendulum and measure the time period for small angles of oscillations. Use the relation T = 2π√(l/g) to calculate the value of acceleration due to gravity.

3. Experimental Determination of Specific Heat Capacity
- Supply a known amount of heat to a solid sphere and measure the resulting temperature change. Use the relation Q = mcΔT to calculate the specific heat capacity.

4. Experimental Determination of the Focal Length of a Convex Lens
- Place the lens between a source and a screen. Measure the distance between the lens and screen for focused images of distant and near objects. Use the lens formula 1/f = 1/u + 1/v to calculate the focal length.
