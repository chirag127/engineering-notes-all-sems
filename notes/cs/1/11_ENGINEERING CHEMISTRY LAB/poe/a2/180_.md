# Topic: History of Computers

1. Mechanical computers
- The first mechanical computers were designed in the 17th century to aid in astronomical calculations.
- They used gears and levers to perform calculations automatically.
- English mathematician Charles Babbage designed the Difference Engine and Analytical Engine in the 1830s which are often considered the first automatic, general-purpose computing engines.

2. Vacuum tubes and transistors
- In the 20th century, the first electronic computers were created.
- They used vacuum tubes for circuitry and were enormous, room-sized machines.
- The transistor was invented in 1947 and enabled smaller and more powerful computers.
- The first personal computers were introduced in the 1970s, popularized in the 1980s with Apple II and IBM PC.

3. The microprocessor and personal computing
- The microprocessor was introduced in 1971, combining the functions of a computer's central processing unit on a single integrated circuit.
- This led to the first microcomputers and the golden age of personal computing with systems running CP/M and later DOS operating systems.
- 16-bit processors and Microsoft Windows led to wider adoption of personal computers for business and home use.
