# ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS LAB

1. Introduction
- Engineering Mathematics Laboratory aims to strengthen the concepts taught in Engineering Mathematics theory classes.
- It provides hands-on experience in using mathematical techniques and tools to model and solve engineering problems.
- Experiments are designed to cover topics from various branches of Engineering Mathematics like Calculus, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations, Numerical Methods, and Statistics.

2. Experiments
- Curve Fitting and Interpolation: To fit curves to given data points and estimate values using interpolation.
- Numerical Solution of Equations: To implement and analyze numerical techniques to solve equations.
- Numerical Integration and Differentiation: To compute integrals and derivatives numerically and analyze errors.
- Solution of Differential Equations: To solve differential equations numerically using Euler's and Runge-Kutta methods and study stability and accuracy.
- Regression and Correlation: To find correlation between variables and fit regression lines.
- Index Properties of Matrices: To verify properties of determinants and matrices like symmetry, skew-symmetry, etc.
- Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors: To calculate eigenvalues and eigenvectors of matrices and study their properties.
