### 5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic acid and paracetamol by conventional and green route.

1. Adipic acid
- Synthesis by oxidation of cyclohexanol and cyclohexanone (conventional route)
- Synthesis by bio-based routes using glucose from biomass as starting material (green route)

2. Paracetamol
- Synthesis by nitration of phenol followed by reduction (conventional route)
- Synthesis by oxidation of p-cresol using hydrogen peroxide (green route)

The conventional routes involve use of harsh chemicals and generate waste. The green routes are more sustainable and eco-friendly as they utilize biomass feedstock and use greener oxidants/reductants. Students will understand the merits of green chemistry approach in synthesizing industrially important compounds.
