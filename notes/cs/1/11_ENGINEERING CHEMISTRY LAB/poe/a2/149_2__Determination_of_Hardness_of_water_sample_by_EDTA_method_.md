## 2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method.

- Take 250 mL conical flask and add 100 mL of water sample to it.
- Add 2-3 drops of Eriochrome Black T indicator to it. The color of solution will change to wine red.
- Fill the burette with standard EDTA solution.
- Titrate the water sample with EDTA solution till the color of solution changes from wine red to blue.
- Note down the burette reading. This gives the total hardness of water sample.
- 1 mL of 0.01M EDTA solution corresponds to 1mg of CaCO3 hardness.
- Calculate the total hardness in mg/L.
