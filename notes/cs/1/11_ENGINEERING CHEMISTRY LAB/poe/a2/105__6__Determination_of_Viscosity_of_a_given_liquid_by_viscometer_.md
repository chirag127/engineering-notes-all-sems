### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer.

1. Take the viscometer and clean it properly. Fill it with the given liquid up to the mark.
2. Note the time taken by the liquid to flow from upper mark to lower mark. This is called efflux time (t).
3. Calculate the viscosity (η) using the formula:
η = K × t
Where, K is the viscometer constant.
4. The SI unit of viscosity is Pascal second (Pa s).
5. Higher the viscosity, higher is the efflux time and vice versa.
6. The viscosity of a liquid depends upon intermolecular forces between the molecules of the liquid. Stronger the intermolecular forces, higher is the viscosity.
