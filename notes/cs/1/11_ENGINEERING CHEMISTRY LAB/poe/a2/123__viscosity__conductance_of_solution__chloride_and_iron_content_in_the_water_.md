### Viscosity, Conductance of Solution, Chloride and Iron Content in Water

**Viscosity:**

- Viscosity is the resistance offered by a fluid to the flow of other fluids through it.
- It depends on the molecular interaction between the layers of the fluid.
- Higher the viscosity, higher is the resistance and lower is the flow.
- Water has low viscosity as the molecular interactions are weak due to its polar nature.

**Conductance of Solution:**

- Conductance is the ability of a solution to conduct electric current.
- It depends on the presence of free ions and their mobility in the solution.
- Solutions of strong electrolytes have high conductance as they have high amount of free ions.
- Pure water has very low conductance but addition of electrolytes increases its conductance.

**Chloride and Iron Content in Water:**

- Chloride and iron are commonly found in water bodies as dissolved salts and their content can be determined by titration methods.
- High chloride content makes the water non-potable as it gives a salty taste and may have adverse health effects in excess.
- Excess of iron gives a reddish colour to water and stains clothes or utensils washed in such water. It can be removed using certain treatments.
- The permissible limits of chloride and iron in drinking water are 200 mg/L and 0.3 mg/L respectively as per WHO standards.
