### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol.

1. From cumene (isopropylbenzene): This industrial method involves the air oxidation of cumene to produce cumene hydroperoxide, which is then cleaved in presence of acid to produce phenol and acetone.
2. From benzene via benzene sulfonic acid: Benzene is first sulfonated using concentrated sulfuric acid to produce benzene sulfonic acid. The sulfonic acid group is then replaced by hydroxyl group on heating with alkali.
3. From chlorobenzene: Chlorobenzene can be converted to phenol by heating with aqueous sodium hydroxide. The chlorine atom is replaced by the hydroxyl group in this reaction.
4. From aniline: Aniline can be oxidized to produce nitrobenzene, which is then reduced to produce phenol. But this method is not used industrially due to low yield of phenol.
