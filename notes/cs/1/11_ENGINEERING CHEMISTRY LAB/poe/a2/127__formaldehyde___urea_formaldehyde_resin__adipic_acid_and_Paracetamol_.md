### Formaldehyde & Urea Formaldehyde Resin, Adipic Acid and Paracetamol

1. Formaldehyde (HCHO) is an organic compound with the formula CH2O. It is a colorless gas with a characteristic pungent odor. It is naturally produced in small amounts in the body and is used industrially to produce building materials and household products. Urea formaldehyde resin is a synthetic resin produced by reacting urea and formaldehyde. It is used as an adhesive in the manufacturing of particleboard and medium-density fiberboard.

2. Adipic acid is a white crystalline solid organic compound. It is used industrially to produce nylon and polyurethanes. Nylon-6,6 is produced by reacting adipic acid with hexamethylenediamine. Adipic acid is one of the most commercially significant carboxylic acids produced worldwide. It is prepared on an industrial scale by oxidation of a mixture of cyclohexanol and cyclohexanone with nitric acid.

3. Paracetamol, also known as acetaminophen, is a medication used to treat pain and fever. It is typically used for mild to moderate pain. Evidence for its use to relieve fever in children is mixed. It is often sold over the counter and usually comes in the form of tablets, capsules, or liquids. Paracetamol is believed to work by acting on prostaglandin production in the brain and body. It is generally safe and effective when used at recommended doses.
