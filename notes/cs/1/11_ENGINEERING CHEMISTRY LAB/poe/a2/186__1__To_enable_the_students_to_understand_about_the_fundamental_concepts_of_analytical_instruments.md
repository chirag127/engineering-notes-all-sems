### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments.

1. Spectroscopy
- Electromagnetic radiation and its interaction with matter
- Emission, absorption and scattering of radiation
- Types of spectroscopy: UV-visible, fluorescence, IR, NMR, MS, etc.
- Principles and applications of different spectroscopic techniques

2. Chromatography
- Principles of chromatography: partition, adsorption, ion-exchange, size-exclusion, etc.
- Stationary and mobile phases
- Applications of different chromatographic techniques: TLC, column, HPLC, GC, etc.
- Sample preparation for chromatography

3. Electrochemistry
- Conduction of electricity, electrolytes and non-electrolytes
- Faraday's laws of electrolysis
- Electrode potentials, Nernst equation and its applications
- Potentiometry, voltammetry, amperometry and their applications

4. Thermal analysis
- Principles of thermogravimetry, differential thermal analysis and differential scanning calorimetry
- Applications of thermal analysis techniques to study thermal decomposition, melting, glass transition, curing, oxidation, etc.
