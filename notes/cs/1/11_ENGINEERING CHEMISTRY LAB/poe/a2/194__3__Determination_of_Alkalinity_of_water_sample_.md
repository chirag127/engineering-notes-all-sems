### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample.

1. Take 100ml of the given water sample in a conical flask.
2. Add 2-3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to it.
3. Titrate the solution with 0.1N HCl from a burette until the pink colour disappears.
4. Note down the initial and final readings on the burette.
5. Calculate the alkalinity in terms of mg/L of CaCO3 using the following formula:

Alkalinity (mg/L) = [(Initial reading - Final reading) x Concentration of HCl x Equivalent weight of CaCO3] / Volume of water sample

6. Repeat the experiment two more times and take the average of the three values as the alkalinity of the given water sample in terms of mg/L of CaCO3.

The alkalinity determination gives an overall measure of the acid-neutralizing capacity of water and is reported as CaCO3 to have a uniform scale of expression. It indicates the amount of strong acid required to neutralize the carbonate, bicarbonate and hydroxide alkalinity in the water sample.
