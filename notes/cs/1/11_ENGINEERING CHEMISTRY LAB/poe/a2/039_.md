# Topic: Photosynthesis

## Process of Photosynthesis
1. Plants take in carbon dioxide from the air through their leaves
2. Plants absorb water from the soil through their roots
3. Sunlight energy is captured by chlorophyll in the plant leaves
4. Carbon dioxide and water combine to form glucose (food) and oxygen in the chloroplasts of leaves
5. Glucose is used by plants as an energy source to grow and thrive
6. Oxygen is released as a byproduct of photosynthesis

## Requirements for Photosynthesis
1. Carbon dioxide: absorbed from the air through leaves
2. Water: absorbed from soil through roots
3. Sunlight: captured by chlorophyll in leaves
4. Chlorophyll: pigment required to capture sunlight energy
5. Minerals: absorbed from soil through roots as nutrients for plant growth

## Rate of Photosynthesis
1. Increases with increasing carbon dioxide levels
2. Increases with increasing sunlight levels
3. Decreases with increasing temperature beyond an optimal level
4. Requires replenishment of water in leaves to continue at a steady pace
