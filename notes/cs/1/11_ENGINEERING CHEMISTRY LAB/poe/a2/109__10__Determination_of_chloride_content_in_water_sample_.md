### 10. Determination of chloride content in water sample.

1. Take a known volume of the water sample in a conical flask.
2. Add a few drops of potassium chromate indicator to the sample. Potassium chromate indicator is yellow in colour.
3. Titrate the sample against silver nitrate solution taken in a burette.
4. Silver nitrate reacts with chloride ions to form white silver chloride precipitate.
5. As the titration proceeds, at the equivalence point, a permanent yellow precipitate of silver chromate just appears.
6. Note down the titre value and calculate the chloride content using the formula:
Chloride content (mg/L) = (Titre value x molarity of AgNO3 x 35.5) / Volume of water sample (in litres)

7. The end point can also be detected conductometrically. At the equivalence point, there will be a sharp fall in conductivity.
8. The method is based on argentometric titration and is very accurate for determination of chloride content.
