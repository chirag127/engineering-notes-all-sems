# How to Succeed in Competitive Exams

1. Prepare well: Competitive exams require extensive preparation and practice. Prepare a study schedule and stick to it rigorously. Cover the entire syllabus and all topics thoroughly. Solve ample practice problems and previous years’ question papers to get familiar with the exam pattern and types of questions.

2. Focus on fundamentals: Rather than mugging up solutions or formulae, focus on understanding concepts and fundamentals. A strong foundation in fundamentals will help you apply knowledge to new types of questions. Learn techniques to solve problems rather than just memorizing steps.

3. Time management: Time management is critical in competitive exams which have negative marking. Attempt questions you are confident about first, then go on to uncertain ones. Make educated guesses for questions you are unlikely to solve. Do not get stuck on any one question and waste too much time. Move on if a question seems very difficult.

4. Stay healthy: Your mental and physical health are equally important. Get adequate sleep, eat healthy and exercise regularly. This will keep you energized, improve concentration and develop a positive attitude. Staying healthy will help you handle the stress and pressure of competitive exams well.

5. Revision: Keep revising notes and concepts till the exam date. This will help strengthen your memory of topics and keep concepts fresh in your mind. Solve some practice problems every day in the final few weeks leading up to the exam for active preparation.
