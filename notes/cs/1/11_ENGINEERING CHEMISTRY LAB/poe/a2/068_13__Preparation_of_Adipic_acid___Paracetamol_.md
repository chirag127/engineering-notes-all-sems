# 13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol

**Adipic acid**

1. Cyclohexanol is oxidized to cyclohexanone using chromic acid.
2. Cyclohexanone is oxidized to cyclohexanone monoacetaldehyde using nitric acid.
3. Cyclohexanone monoacetaldehyde is oxidized to adipic acid using nitric acid.
4. The adipic acid is purified by recrystallization.

**Paracetamol**

1. P-aminophenol is acetylated using acetic anhydride to produce paracetamol.
2. The product is cooled and filtered to obtain paracetamol.
3. The paracetamol is purified by recrystallization.
