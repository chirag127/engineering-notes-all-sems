### 7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

1. Take about 0.5 g of Ferrous ammonium sulfate in a conical flask.
2. Add 50 ml of distilled water and heat to dissolve it.
3. Add few drops of ammonium thiocyanate solution as an external indicator.
4. Titrate the solution with standard potassium dichromate solution till the color changes from green to dark red.
5. Note the volume of potassium dichromate solution used.
6. Repeat the titration with a fresh solution of Ferrous ammonium sulfate. Take the average of the two readings.
7. Calculate the percentage of FeSO4.7H2O in the given sample using the formula:
% of FeSO4.7H2O = (Volume of K2Cr2O7 x Molarity of K2Cr2O7 x Equivalent weight of FeSO4.7H2O) / (Weight of the sample x 1000)
