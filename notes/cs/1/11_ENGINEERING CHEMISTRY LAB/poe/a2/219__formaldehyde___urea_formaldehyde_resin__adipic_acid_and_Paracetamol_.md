2. Adipic acid is a white crystalline solid organic compound. It is used industrially to produce nylon. Adipic acid reacts with hexamethylene diamine to form nylon 66, a widely used synthetic polymer. Nylon 66 is strong, rigid, heat-resistant, and durable. It is used to produce fibers, plastics, and films in various applications.

3. Paracetamol is an analgesic and antipyretic drug. It is used to reduce fever and provide relief from minor aches and pains. It is a widely used over-the-counter pain reliever and fever reducer.
