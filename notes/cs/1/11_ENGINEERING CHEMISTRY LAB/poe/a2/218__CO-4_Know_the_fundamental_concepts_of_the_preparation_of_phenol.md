### CO-4 Know the fundamental concepts of the preparation of phenol.

1. From benzene: Benzene is oxidized to phenol using oxygen in the presence of catalyst. However, this method is not used industrially due to high cost and handling difficulties of oxygen and benzene.
2. From cumene: This is the industrial method for preparation of phenol. Cumene (isopropylbenzene) is oxidized to produce acetone and phenol. This method is known as Hock process. The proportion of phenol and acetone can be adjusted by controlling the extent of oxidation of cumene.
3. From diazonium salts: When diazonium salts such as benzenediazonium chloride are heated, they decompose to produce phenol along with nitrogen gas. However, this method has limitations due to the difficulty in preparing diazonium salts and their tendency to explode.
4. From chlorobenzene: Chlorobenzene can be converted to phenol by heating with aqueous solution of sodium hydroxide. This method was used earlier but due to the high cost of chlorobenzene, it is not used nowadays.
