# 14. Determination of Cell Conductance of a solution.

1. Take a U-tube containing the given solution. Connect the ends of the U-tube to a source of constant current and a sensitive galvanometer to measure potential difference across the ends of the U-tube.
2. Note the potential difference V for the given current I. Calculate conductance of the solution using the relation:
Conductance = 1/Resistance = Current/Potential difference
3. Repeat the experiment with different concentrations of the solution and plot a graph between Conductance and Concentration.
4. The conductance increases with increase in concentration as there are more ions to carry current. The nature of the plot is a straight line passing through the origin.
5. The molar conductance can be calculated using the relation:
Molar conductance (Lambda) = Conductance/concentration in molarity.
