# ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS LAB KCS

**1. Linear Programming Problem**

- Linear programming problem is a mathematical optimization technique to achieve the best outcome in a mathematical model whose requirements are represented by linear relationships.
- It involves maximizing or minimizing a linear objective function subject to linear constraints in the form of equalities and inequalities.
- The canonical form of a linear programming problem consists of an objective function and a set of constraints.
- The two main approaches to solve a linear programming problem are graphical method and simplex method.

**2. Numerical Methods**

- Numerical methods are algorithms that use numerical approximation to obtain solutions to various computational problems.
- They are used to solve mathematical problems that cannot be solved analytically.
- Some common numerical methods are Newton's method, bisection method, false position method, and iterative methods.
- These methods are often needed to solve nonlinear equations and systems of equations. They involve successive approximations to find the root of a function or solution to a system.
- The accuracy of the solution depends on choosing an appropriate tolerance and the speed of convergence depends on the method used.
