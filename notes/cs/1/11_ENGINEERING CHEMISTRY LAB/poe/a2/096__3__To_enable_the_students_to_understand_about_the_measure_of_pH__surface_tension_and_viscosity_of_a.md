### 3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of a liquid.

- pH: The pH of a liquid is measured using a pH meter or pH paper. A low pH indicates an acidic solution while a high pH indicates a basic solution. The pH scale ranges from 0 to 14 with 7 being neutral.
- Surface tension: Surface tension is measured using the Du Nouy ring method or the capillary rise method. It is the tendency of liquid molecules to stick together and resist external forces. Liquids with high surface tension have a strong molecular attraction.
- Viscosity: Viscosity is measured using instruments called viscometers. It is the resistance of a liquid to flow and pour. Liquids with high viscosity are thick and flow slowly while liquids with low viscosity are thin and flow rapidly. The viscosity depends on the molecular interaction between the liquid molecules.
