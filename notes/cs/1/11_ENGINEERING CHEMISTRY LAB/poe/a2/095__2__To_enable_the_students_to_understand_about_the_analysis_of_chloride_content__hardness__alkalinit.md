### 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity of water.

1.  Chloride content: Chloride content is analyzed by titrating the water sample with standard silver nitrate solution using potassium chromate as indicator. The end point is determined by the appearance of red color. The chloride content is calculated by using the formula,
    Chloride content (mg/L) = (Titre value x Molarity of silver nitrate x Equivalent weight of chloride) / Volume of water sample (in L)
    Where, Equivalent weight of chloride is 35.5
2.  Hardness: Hardness is analyzed by titrating the water sample with standard EDTA solution using Eriochrome black T indicator. The end point is determined by the color change of indicator from wine red to blue. The hardness is calculated using the formula,
    Hardness (mg/L) = (Titre value x Molarity of EDTA) / Volume of water sample (in L)
3.  Alkalinity: Alkalinity is analyzed by titrating the water sample with standard hydrochloric acid using phenolphthalein indicator. The end point is determined by the color change of indicator from pink to colorless. The alkalinity is calculated using the formula,
    Alkalinity (mg/L) = (Titre value x Molarity of HCl x Equivalent weight of CaCO3) / Volume of water sample (in L)
    Where, Equivalent weight of CaCO3 is 50

The above points cover the basic analysis procedure and calculations for finding chloride content, hardness and alkalinity of water. This will enable the students to understand the water quality parameters in a formal way without any external links or emojis.
