## 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity

1. Chloride content
- Chlorides are salts of hydrochloric acid. Common chlorides include sodium chloride and potassium chloride.
- Excess chloride can indicate seawater intrusion or the use of salt to de-ice roads.
- The chloride concentration is measured using a chloride-selective electrode or ion chromatography.

2. Hardness
- Hardness is a measure of divalent metal ions such as calcium and magnesium. These ions interfere with the effectiveness of soaps and can form scale in boilers or pipes.
- Hardness is measured using titration with an EDTA solution or an ion-selective electrode.
- The total concentration of divalent metal ions is reported in mg/L as calcium carbonate.

3. Alkalinity
- Alkalinity is a measure of the acid-neutralizing capacity of water and is primarily due to carbonate, bicarbonate, and hydroxide ions.
- Alkalinity has effects on pH and can influence corrosion and scaling.
- Alkalinity is measured using acid titration or calculated from pH and dissolved carbon dioxide measurements.
- Alkalinity is reported in mg/L as calcium carbonate.
