# 8. Determination of the strength of Potassium dichromate using internal indicator.

1.  Take about 0.5 g of ferrous ammonium sulphate and dissolve it in 25 mL of water. Add 5 mL of concentrated sulphuric acid and heat the solution gently till fumes of sulphur dioxide are obtained. Cool the solution and dilute it to 100 mL. Note its absorbance with respect to water at 1 cm in a spectrophotometer at 480 nm which will be A1.
2.  Standardize the potassium dichromate solution by titrating accurately measured aliquots of it against the ferrous ammonium sulphate solution using diphenylamine as an external indicator. Four titrations should agree to within 0.05 mL. Note the concentration of potassium dichromate solution in gram equivalents per litre which will be I1.
3.  Repeat step (2) with a freshly prepared ferrous ammonium sulphate solution and obtain I2. If I1 and I2 agree within 0.5% the potassium dichromate solution can be used as an internal indicator for iron estimation. The specific shift with change in Fe2+ concentration is noted as Δabs./Δ[Fe2+].
