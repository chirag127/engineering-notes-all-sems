### 10. Determination of chloride content in water sample.

1. Take 100 mL of the water sample in a conical flask.
2. Add 3-4 drops of potassium chromate indicator solution to it. The solution will remain yellow in color.
3. Titrate the solution with 0.1 M silver nitrate solution from a burette till the yellow color changes to reddish-brown.
4. Note the burette reading. It gives the volume of 0.1 M silver nitrate solution used.
5. Calculate the chloride content in mg/L using the formula:
    Chloride content (mg/L) = (Volume of AgNO3 used × Concentration of AgNO3 × 35.45) / 100
