# Water

1. Water (H2O) is a liquid substance that covers about 70% of the Earth's surface. It is vital for life on Earth.
2. Water molecules are made up of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom (H2O). The hydrogen atoms attach to one side of the oxygen atom and bond at a 104.5° angle. This unique structure gives water some exceptional properties.
3. One of water's exceptional properties is its high heat capacity. It takes a long time to heat up and a long time to cool down. This property makes water an excellent temperature stabilizer on Earth. It helps regulate the climate and prevents drastic fluctuations in temperature.
4. Another remarkable property is water's high surface tension. The water molecules on the surface stick together tightly, which allows some insects to walk on water and allows water to have a convex meniscus. The high surface tension is also essential for the process of osmosis in plants.
5. Water also has a high latent heat of vaporization. It takes a long time to convert between liquid and gas states. This property allows water to store a large amount of energy as it changes state, which makes it an efficient temperature regulator. The oceans and water bodies on Earth take a long time to heat up and cool down due to this property.
6. Water is also an excellent solvent due to its polarity. It can dissolve more substances than any other liquid. This ability makes water a superb transport medium in plants and animals. Many biological processes occur in aqueous solutions.

The above points summarize some of the key properties of water and how they are essential for life on Earth. Water's unique molecular structure gives it exceptional properties that make it invaluable for sustaining all forms of life.
