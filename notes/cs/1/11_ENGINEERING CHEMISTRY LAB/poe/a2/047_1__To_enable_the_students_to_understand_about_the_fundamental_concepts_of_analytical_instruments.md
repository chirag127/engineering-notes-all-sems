# 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

- Analytical instruments are used to analyze the composition of a sample. They rely on various physical and chemical principles to detect and quantify substances.
- Key components of analytical instruments are:
-- Sample introduction system: To introduce the sample to the instrument. E.g. auto-sampler.
-- Detector: To detect the analyte of interest. E.g. Photodetector, Electrochemical detector.
-- Separator: To separate the analytes. E.g. Columns, Electrodes.
-- Processor: To process the signals from detectors and display the results. E.g. Integrator, Computer.
- Key principles used are:
-- Spectroscopy: Based on interaction of electromagnetic radiation with matter. E.g. Atomic absorption spectrometry, Infrared spectroscopy.
-- Chromatography: Based on separation of analytes over a stationary phase. E.g. Gas chromatography, High performance liquid chromatography.
-- Electrochemistry: Based on electrical properties of analytes. E.g. Amperometry, Potentiometry.
- Selectivity, sensitivity, accuracy, precision, robustness, and ease of use are key performance metrics for analytical instruments.
