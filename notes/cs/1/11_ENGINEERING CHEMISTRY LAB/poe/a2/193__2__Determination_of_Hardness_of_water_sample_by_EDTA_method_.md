### 2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method.

1. Take 250 ml of the given water sample in a conical flask.

2. Add 3-4 drops of Eriochrome Black T indicator solution to it. The solution will appear dark pink.

3. Prepare standard EDTA solution of 0.01M concentration.

4. Take 10 ml of EDTA solution in a burette and add it slowly to the conical flask containing water sample while stirring constantly.

5. As EDTA is added, the color of the solution will change from dark pink to purple and finally to blue. Note down the burette reading when the color changes to purple. This indicates the end point.

6. Repeat the titration with two more samples and note down the readings. Average out the three readings to get the final end point.

7. Calculate the total alkalinity and hardness of water using the standard procedure.

The EDTA method determines the total hardness of water which includes both calcium and magnesium hardness. The method is based on the complexation of calcium and magnesium ions by EDTA followed by detection of endpoint using Eriochrome Black T indicator. The total hardness is directly proportional to the volume of EDTA solution consumed.
