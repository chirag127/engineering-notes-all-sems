### 11. Preparation of Phenol formaldehyde (PF) resin.

1. Phenol and formaldehyde are the two monomers used to prepare phenol formaldehyde resin.
2. The molar ratio of phenol to formaldehyde can vary from 1:1 to 1:2 depending upon the type of resin required.
3. An acid catalyst such as hydrochloric acid or oxalic acid is used to carry out the polymerization reaction.
4. The reaction is carried out in three stages:

a. Initially, phenol and formaldehyde react to form hydroxymethyl phenol in the presence of acid catalyst.
b. Then, the hydroxymethyl phenol reacts with more phenol to form dihydroxydiphenyl methane.
c. Finally, the dihydroxydiphenyl methane reacts with more formaldehyde and gets cross-linked to form 3D network polymer.

5. The extent of polymerization and cross-linking decides the properties of the resin.
6. The reaction is carried out at a temperature of 60-100°C.
7. Once the desired viscosity is reached, the reaction is stopped by neutralizing the acid catalyst. The resin is then cooled and stored.
