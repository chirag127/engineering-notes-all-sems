### 2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method.

1.  Take 100 mL of water sample in a conical flask.
2.  Add 3-4 drops of Eriochrome Black T indicator solution to it. The solution will appear bluish-black in color.
3.  Prepare EDTA solution of known concentration, say 0.01 M.
4.  Using a burette, add EDTA solution to the water sample slowly while stirring continuously.
5.  As EDTA binds to the metal ions present in the water sample, the indicator color will change from bluish-black to red.
6.  Note down the EDTA volume added at the color change point. This gives the volume of EDTA required to complex the metal ions present in the water sample.
7.  From this, calculate the water hardness in terms of mg of CaCO3 per liter.

The EDTA method is based on the complexation of metal ions (especially Ca2+ and Mg2+) present in water by EDTA. The endpoint is detected using an indicator which changes color with the complexation reaction. By titrating the water sample with EDTA, the hardness can be quantified in terms of equivalents of CaCO3.
