# Topic: History of Computers

I. Mechanical and Electrical Computers (1642-1940)
- Pascaline (1642): First mechanical calculator invented by Blaise Pascal
- Analytical Engine (1837): Proposed by Charles Babbage with separate storage and processing
- Tabulating machines (1880s-1940s): Herman Hollerith's punch card based machines for census data processing

II. Vacuum Tubes and First Generation Computers (1940-1956)
-ENIAC (1946): Electronic Numerical Integrator And Computer - first general purpose electronic computer
-UNIVAC 1101 (1951): First commercial computer for business and government applications

III. Transistors and Second Generation Computers (1956-1963)
-Transistors replaced vacuum tubes making computers smaller, faster, more energy efficient and reliable
-IBM 7090 (1959): Transistor based scientific computer

IV. Integrated Circuits and Third Generation Computers (1964-1971)
-Integrated circuits (ICs) or microchips incorporated thousands of transistors onto a single silicon chip
-IBM System/360 (1964): Family of compatible third generation computers using microchips

V. Microprocessors and Fourth Generation Computers (1971-Present)
-Microprocessors (1971): Single chip CPUs leading to first personal computers in 1970s
-Growth of personal and home computers, laptops, tablets and smartphones
