## 10. Determination of chloride content in water sample.

1. Take a clean conical flask and add 100 ml of the given water sample to it.
2. Add 2-3 drops of potassium chromate indicator to the water sample. Potassium chromate indicator is yellow in colour.
3. Titrate the water sample with silver nitrate solution (0.1 M) until the yellow colour of potassium chromate just disappears.
4. Note the volume of silver nitrate solution consumed.
5. Calculate the chloride content in mg/l using the formula -
Chloride content (in mg/l) = (Volume of AgNO3 in ml x Concentration of AgNO3 in M) x 35.45
