# LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

1. Experiment to show the presence of oxygen in air
- Take a burning candle and place it in a covered beaker. The candle extinguishes after some time due to consumption of oxygen. This shows that oxygen is present in air.

2. Experiment to show carbon dioxide is produced during respiration
- Take two test tubes - one filled with limewater and the other with soda lime. Breathe into each test tube through a straw. The limewater turns milky which indicates carbon dioxide is produced during respiration. The soda lime does not change colour.

3. Experiment to show formation of a precipitate
- Take solutions of barium chloride and sodium sulphate in two test tubes. Mix the two solutions. A white precipitate of barium sulphate is formed which settles down at the bottom. This shows that insoluble substances are formed when certain salts are mixed.

4. Experiment to show the presence of water in the soil
- Take a lump of soil and heat it in a china dish. The water present in the soil evaporates forming water vapours that condense on the sides of the dish. This shows that water is present in the soil.
