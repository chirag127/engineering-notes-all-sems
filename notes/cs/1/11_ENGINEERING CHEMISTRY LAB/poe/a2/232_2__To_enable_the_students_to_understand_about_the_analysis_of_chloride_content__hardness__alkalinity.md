## 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity

* Chloride content: The chloride content in water can be determined by titrating it against silver nitrate solution using potassium chromate as indicator. The titrated volume of silver nitrate solution is proportional to the chloride content in the given water sample. Higher chloride content indicates water pollution.
* Hardness: The hardness of water can be determined by titrating it against standard EDTA solution using eriochrome black T as indicator. The titrated volume of EDTA solution is inversely proportional to the hardness of the water sample. Higher hardness is mainly due to the presence of calcium and magnesium salts.
* Alkalinity: The alkalinity of water can be determined by titrating it against standard HCl solution using methyl orange as indicator. The titrated volume of HCl solution is proportional to the alkalinity of the given water sample. Higher alkalinity affects the pH of water and can lead to incrustation problems.

The above methods are to be understood and performed under the guidance of the teacher to analyze the given water samples for chloride content, hardness and alkalinity. Regular practice will make the students adept at handling such determinations with accuracy.
