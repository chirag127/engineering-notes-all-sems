5. To enable the students to understand about the synthesis of organic compounds such as adipic

The synthesis of organic compounds like adipic acid involves a series of chemical reactions that convert starting materials into the desired product. The goal is to understand the underlying principles and mechanisms of these reactions, as well as the factors that influence their outcome. This includes knowledge of reactants, solvents, catalysts, and conditions such as temperature and pressure. The study of organic synthesis also includes the development of new and efficient methods for synthesizing organic compounds, as well as the optimization of existing methods. Understanding the synthesis of organic compounds like adipic acid is important for a variety of applications, including the production of industrial chemicals, pharmaceuticals, and consumer products.
