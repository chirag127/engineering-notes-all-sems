### Level

A "level" is a term used to describe a specific stage, degree, or standard of achievement, skill, or competence in a particular area of activity. A level can also refer to a floor or surface that is horizontal or flat. In games, levels refer to the series of challenges or tasks that the player must complete to progress through the game. Additionally, "level" can also refer to the amount or measurement of something, such as a liquid or sound.
