### 3. Determination of Alkalinity of water sample.

The determination of alkalinity in water samples is a common analytical method used to measure the capacity of water to neutralize acids. Alkalinity is an important water quality parameter that provides information about the presence of various chemical species in water, including bicarbonates, carbonates, and hydroxides. The determination of alkalinity can be performed using a variety of methods, including titration, spectrophotometry, and colorimetry. The most common method is titration, in which a known volume of water sample is titrated with a standardized acid solution until the pH of the sample reaches a predetermined endpoint. The amount of acid required to reach the endpoint is then used to calculate the alkalinity of the sample. The accuracy of the determination of alkalinity depends on several factors, including the quality of the reagents used, the accuracy of the titration equipment, and the method used to calculate the results. Overall, the determination of alkalinity is an important analytical method that provides valuable information about the chemical composition of water samples.
