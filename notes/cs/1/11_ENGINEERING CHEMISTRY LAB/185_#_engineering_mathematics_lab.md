# ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS LAB

Engineering Mathematics Lab refers to a laboratory or course designed to teach the mathematical concepts and techniques that are fundamental to the study and practice of engineering. This lab typically covers topics such as:

1. Linear Algebra: including systems of linear equations, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

2. Calculus: including differentiation, integration, optimization, and applications to engineering problems.

3. Differential Equations: including first-order and higher-order linear and nonlinear differential equations, and their applications to engineering problems.

4. Numerical Methods: including numerical solutions of differential equations, optimization, and regression analysis.

5. Probability and Statistics: including probability distributions, hypothesis testing, regression analysis, and design of experiments.

The Engineering Mathematics Lab provides hands-on experience with mathematical software and tools, and emphasizes the use of mathematical models and methods to solve real-world engineering problems.
