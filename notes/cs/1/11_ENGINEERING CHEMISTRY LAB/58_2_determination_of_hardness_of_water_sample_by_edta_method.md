2. Determination of Hardness of water sample by EDTA method.

The EDTA method is used to determine the hardness of water samples. Hardness in water is caused by the presence of dissolved minerals, such as calcium and magnesium ions. The EDTA method measures the total hardness of water by complexing with the metal ions and determining the amount of EDTA required to completely complex with the metal ions. The following are the steps to determine the hardness of water by EDTA method:
1. Prepare a sample of the water to be tested.
2. Add a known volume of the water sample to a flask.
3. Add a known volume of EDTA solution to the flask.
4. Add a pH indicator, such as Eriochrome Black T, to the flask.
5. Titrate the solution with a standard calcium or magnesium solution until the color change of the indicator indicates that all of the metal ions have been complexed with the EDTA.
6. Calculate the hardness of the water sample based on the volume of the standard solution used in the titration.
7. Repeat the procedure multiple times to obtain an average hardness value.

The EDTA method is a widely used and reliable method for determining the hardness of water samples.
