### 1. Calibration of Analytical Equipment and apparatus.

Calibration of Analytical Equipment and Apparatus:

Calibration is the process of verifying the accuracy of an analytical instrument or apparatus. It is an important step in ensuring that the results obtained from an analytical instrument are accurate and reliable.

1. Purpose: The purpose of calibrating analytical equipment and apparatus is to verify that they are operating within their specified limits and to ensure that the results obtained from the instrument are accurate and reliable.

2. Steps: The steps involved in calibrating analytical equipment and apparatus are as follows:

- Selection of a suitable reference standard: A reference standard is a known and well-characterized substance or material that is used to verify the accuracy of an analytical instrument. The reference standard should be selected based on the type of analysis being performed and the range of the analytical instrument.

- Preparation of the reference standard: The reference standard should be prepared according to the manufacturer's instructions or a standard procedure.

- Calibration of the instrument: The instrument should be calibrated using the prepared reference standard. This involves adjusting the instrument so that it gives the correct readings for the reference standard.

- Verification of the calibration: The calibration should be verified by repeating the measurement of the reference standard and comparing the results to the expected values.

3. Frequency: The frequency of calibration depends on the type of analytical instrument and the type of analysis being performed. Some instruments may need to be calibrated daily, while others may only need to be calibrated once a year.

In conclusion, the calibration of analytical equipment and apparatus is an important step in ensuring that the results obtained from an analytical instrument are accurate and reliable. The process involves selecting a suitable reference standard, preparing the reference standard, calibrating the instrument, and verifying the calibration. The frequency of calibration depends on the type of analytical instrument and the type of analysis being performed.
