### 1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

Objective 1: To Enable the Students to Understand the Fundamental Concepts of Analytical Instruments:

This objective aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments, including their design, operation, and applications. The objective is to equip students with the knowledge and skills needed to effectively use analytical instruments in a variety of settings, including research, industry, and academia.

The course content will cover topics such as the principles of analytical instrumentation, including spectroscopy, chromatography, and electrochemistry. Students will learn about the different types of analytical instruments, including spectrometers, chromatographs, and potentiostats, and how they are used to measure different parameters, such as chemical composition, concentration, and electrochemical potential.

In addition, students will learn about the different applications of analytical instruments, including environmental monitoring, food analysis, and pharmaceutical analysis. They will also learn about the limitations and challenges associated with different analytical techniques and how to overcome these limitations.

Overall, the objective is to provide students with a solid foundation in the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments and to prepare them for careers in a variety of fields where analytical instruments are used.
