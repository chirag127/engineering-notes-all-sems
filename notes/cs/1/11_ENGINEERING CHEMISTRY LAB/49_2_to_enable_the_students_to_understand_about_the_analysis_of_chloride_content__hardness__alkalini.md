2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity

Analysis of chloride content, hardness, and alkalinity is an important aspect of water quality analysis. These parameters are used to determine the suitability of water for various purposes, such as drinking, irrigation, and industrial use. The chloride content of water is an indicator of the presence of salts, which can affect the taste and odor of water. Hardness is a measure of the amount of calcium and magnesium ions in water, which can cause scaling and other problems in plumbing systems. Alkalinity is a measure of the ability of water to neutralize acids, which is important for maintaining a stable pH level in water. Understanding these parameters is important for students as it helps them understand the basic principles of water quality analysis and the factors that affect water quality.
