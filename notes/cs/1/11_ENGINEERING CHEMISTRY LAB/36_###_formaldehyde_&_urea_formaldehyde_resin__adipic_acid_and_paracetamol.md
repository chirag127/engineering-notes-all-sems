### formaldehyde & urea formaldehyde resin, adipic acid and Paracetamol.

Formaldehyde: Formaldehyde is a colorless gas with a strong, pungent odor. It is used in the production of resins, textiles, and adhesives. It is also used as a disinfectant, preservative, and embalming fluid.

Urea Formaldehyde Resin: Urea formaldehyde resin is a thermosetting plastic made from urea and formaldehyde. It is used in the production of laminates, adhesives, and molded products.

Adipic Acid: Adipic acid is a white crystalline solid that is used in the production of nylon. It is also used as a food and pharmaceutical additive.

Paracetamol: Paracetamol is a pain reliever and fever reducer. It is used to treat headaches, muscle aches, toothaches, and other types of pain. It is also used to reduce fever.
