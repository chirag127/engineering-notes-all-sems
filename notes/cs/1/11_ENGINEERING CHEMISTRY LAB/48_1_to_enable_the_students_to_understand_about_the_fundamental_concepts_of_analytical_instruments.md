1. To enable the students to understand about the fundamental concepts of analytical instruments

Analytical instruments are tools used to measure physical and chemical properties of substances. They play a crucial role in various fields including medicine, biology, chemistry, and physics. The fundamental concepts of analytical instruments include:

1. Measurement principles: Understanding how the instrument measures the property of interest.

2. Sensitivity: The ability of the instrument to detect small changes in the property being measured.

3. Precision: The ability of the instrument to consistently produce the same results.

4. Accuracy: The degree to which the instrument's measurement matches the actual value of the property being measured.

5. Calibration: The process of adjusting the instrument to ensure accurate measurements.

6. Data processing: The methods used to analyze and interpret the data obtained from the instrument.

7. Interferences: Factors that can affect the accuracy of the measurement, such as temperature, humidity, and other environmental factors.

By understanding these concepts, students can better appreciate the capabilities and limitations of analytical instruments and how to use them effectively.
