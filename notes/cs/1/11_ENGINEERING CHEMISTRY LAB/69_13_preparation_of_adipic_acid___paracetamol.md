13. Preparation of Adipic acid / Paracetamol.

Preparation of Adipic acid and Paracetamol are two different processes. 

Preparation of Adipic acid: 
1. Obtain the raw materials, including cyclohexene and cyclohexanol
2. React the raw materials in the presence of a catalyst to produce cyclohexanone
3. Oxidize the cyclohexanone to produce adipic acid
4. Purify the adipic acid through crystallization and filtration
5. Dry the crystals to obtain the final product. 

Preparation of Paracetamol: 
1. Obtain the raw materials, including phenol and acetic anhydride
2. React the raw materials in the presence of a catalyst to produce acetaminophen
3. Reduce the acetaminophen to produce paracetamol
4. Purify the paracetamol through crystallization and filtration
5. Dry the crystals to obtain the final product. 

Both processes require careful control of reaction conditions, including temperature, pressure, and catalyst concentration, to obtain high yields of the desired product. It is also important to follow proper safety procedures to protect workers and the environment.
