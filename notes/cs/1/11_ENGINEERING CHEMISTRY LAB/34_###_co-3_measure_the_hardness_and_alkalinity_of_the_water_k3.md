### CO-3 Measure the hardness and alkalinity of the water. K3

CO-3 is a measure of water hardness and alkalinity. Hardness is a measure of the amount of calcium and magnesium ions in the water. Alkalinity is a measure of the water's ability to neutralize acids. Both hardness and alkalinity can be measured using a test kit or a water testing laboratory. The results of the test will indicate the levels of hardness and alkalinity in the water, which can be used to determine the best course of action for treating the water.
