5. Determination of surface tension of given liquid.

To determine the surface tension of a liquid, you can perform a number of experiments, including:

1. Drop weight method: In this method, a weight is attached to a wire and lowered into the liquid. The surface tension of the liquid is calculated from the maximum height to which the weight can be raised before breaking the surface.

2. Capillary rise method: In this method, the liquid is placed in a capillary tube and the height to which it rises is measured. The surface tension of the liquid is calculated from the height and the diameter of the tube.

3. Pendant drop method: In this method, a drop of liquid is suspended from a needle and the shape of the drop is observed. The surface tension of the liquid is calculated from the shape of the drop and the dimensions of the needle.

4. Wilhelmy plate method: In this method, a flat plate is immersed in the liquid and the force required to pull the plate out of the liquid is measured. The surface tension of the liquid is calculated from the force and the dimensions of the plate.

These experiments can be used to determine the surface tension of a liquid at different temperatures and under different conditions, providing valuable information about the properties of the liquid and its suitability for various applications.
