### CO-1 Get an understanding of the use of different analytical instruments. K3

Analytical instruments are used to measure physical and chemical properties of substances. Some common types include:
- Spectrophotometers: measure the amount of light absorbed or transmitted by a sample
- Chromatographs: separate and analyze components of a mixture
- Mass spectrometers: determine the mass and composition of molecules
- X-ray diffractometers: analyze crystal structures
- Nuclear magnetic resonance spectrometers: study the magnetic properties of molecules.
It is important to understand the use and limitations of each instrument to accurately interpret results.
