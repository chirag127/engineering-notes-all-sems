### 4. Determination of pH by titrimetric method.

The titrimetric method is used to determine the pH of a solution by measuring the volume of a titrant (a solution of known concentration) required to neutralize the solution of unknown pH. The following steps can be taken to determine the pH by titrimetric method:
1. Prepare a solution of unknown pH
2. Add a pH indicator to the solution (e.g. phenolphthalein)
3. Slowly add a titrant (e.g. sodium hydroxide) to the solution while stirring
4. Observe the color change of the indicator to determine the endpoint of the reaction
5. Repeat the titration several times to obtain an average volume of the titrant required to neutralize the solution
6. Calculate the pH using the volume of the titrant and the concentration of the titrant
7. Compare the results to a reference value to validate the accuracy of the measurement.

The titrimetric method is a precise and reliable method for determining the pH of a solution, and it is widely used in various applications, including industrial processes, environmental monitoring, and laboratory research.
