4. To enable the students to understand about the preparation of different resins.

To enable students to understand the preparation of different resins, they can follow these steps:
1. Choose a resin type to prepare (e.g. epoxy, polyester, etc.)
2. Gather the necessary raw materials and equipment (e.g. monomers, initiators, mixing equipment)
3. Follow the manufacturer's instructions for mixing the resin components
4. Pour the mixture into a mold or container
5. Allow the resin to cure, following the manufacturer's instructions for curing time and temperature
6. Remove the cured resin from the mold or container
7. Evaluate the physical and chemical properties of the resin (e.g. hardness, tensile strength, etc.)
8. Compare the properties of the resin to those of other resins to determine its suitability for a specific application.

This process can be repeated for different types of resins to compare their properties and determine the best resin for a given application. It is important to follow the manufacturer's instructions carefully to ensure the proper preparation of the resin.
