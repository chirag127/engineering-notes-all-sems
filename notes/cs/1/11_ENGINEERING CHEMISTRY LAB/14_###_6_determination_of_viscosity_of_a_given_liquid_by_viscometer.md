### 6. Determination of Viscosity of a given liquid by viscometer.

A viscometer is an instrument used to measure the viscosity of a liquid. The viscosity of a liquid is a measure of its resistance to flow. To determine the viscosity of a liquid using a viscometer, the following steps are followed:

1. Fill the viscometer with the liquid to be tested.
2. Immerse the viscometer in a constant temperature bath.
3. Apply a rotating force to the liquid using the viscometer.
4. Measure the torque required to maintain a constant rotating speed.
5. Calculate the viscosity from the measured torque and rotating speed.
6. Repeat the measurement several times to obtain an average viscosity.

The viscosity of a liquid can be expressed in units of poise or centipoise. The viscosity of a liquid is an important property that affects its flow behavior and can impact various industrial processes.
