### 12. Preparation of Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin.

Urea formaldehyde (UF) resin is an adhesive that is commonly used in the manufacturing of wood products. The preparation process involves the reaction of urea and formaldehyde in the presence of an acid catalyst.

1. Urea and formaldehyde are mixed in a reactor in the desired proportions.
2. An acid catalyst, such as sulfuric acid, is added to the mixture.
3. The reactor is heated to a temperature of around 80-90°C, and the reaction is allowed to proceed for several hours.
4. The reaction mixture is cooled, and any solid particles are filtered out.
5. The resulting UF resin is a clear, colorless liquid that is used as an adhesive for wood products.
