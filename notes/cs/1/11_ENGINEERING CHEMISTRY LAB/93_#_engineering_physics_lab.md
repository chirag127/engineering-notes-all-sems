# ENGINEERING PHYSICS LAB

Engineering Physics Lab is a laboratory course that provides hands-on experience for engineering students in the application of the principles of physics. The course typically covers topics such as mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, and optics. The course is designed to complement the theoretical concepts covered in Engineering Physics courses and to provide students with practical experience in performing experiments and analyzing data. The course typically involves a series of experiments that are designed to demonstrate specific physical concepts and to give students the opportunity to apply the concepts they have learned. The experiments are performed in a laboratory setting, and students are expected to use proper laboratory techniques and to follow safety procedures. The course is an important part of the engineering curriculum, as it provides students with a deeper understanding of the principles of physics and prepares them for careers in engineering and technology.
