### 2. To enable the students to understand about the analysis of chloride content, hardness, alkalinity of water.

Objective 2: To Enable the Students to Understand the Analysis of Chloride Content, Hardness, and Alkalinity of Water:

This objective aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the analysis of chloride content, hardness, and alkalinity of water. The objective is to equip students with the knowledge and skills needed to effectively analyze water samples for these parameters and to interpret the results.

The course content will cover topics such as the principles of water analysis, including sample preparation, sample analysis, and data interpretation. Students will learn about the different methods used to analyze water samples for chloride content, hardness, and alkalinity, such as titration, ion chromatography, and spectrophotometry.

In addition, students will learn about the importance of water analysis and the role of water quality in various applications, such as drinking water, industrial water, and environmental monitoring. They will also learn about the limitations and challenges associated with different water analysis techniques and how to overcome these limitations.

Overall, the objective is to provide students with a solid foundation in the analysis of chloride content, hardness, and alkalinity of water and to prepare them for careers in fields where water analysis is important, such as environmental science, water resources management, and public health.
