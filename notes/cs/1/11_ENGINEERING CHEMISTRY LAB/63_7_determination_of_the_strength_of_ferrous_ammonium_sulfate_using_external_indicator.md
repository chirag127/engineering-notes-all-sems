7. Determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate using external indicator.

The determination of the strength of Ferrous ammonium sulfate (FAS) using an external indicator is a commonly used analytical method in chemistry. FAS is a complex iron salt that is used as a reducing agent in various chemical reactions. The strength of FAS is determined by measuring the amount of iron ions in solution, which can be done using an external indicator. The most common external indicator used for this purpose is potassium ferrocyanide. The FAS solution is titrated with a known volume of potassium ferrocyanide solution until a color change occurs, indicating that all the iron ions have reacted with the indicator. The amount of potassium ferrocyanide required to reach the endpoint is then used to calculate the strength of the FAS solution. The accuracy of the determination of FAS strength depends on several factors, including the quality of the reagents used, the accuracy of the titration equipment, and the method used to calculate the results. Overall, the determination of FAS strength using an external indicator is a simple and effective method for measuring the concentration of iron ions in solution.
