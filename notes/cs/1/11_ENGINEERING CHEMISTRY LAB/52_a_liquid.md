A liquid is a state of matter that has a definite volume but takes the shape of its container. It is intermediate between a solid and a gas and has a higher density than a gas but a lower viscosity. Liquids have a continuous molecular arrangement and are able to flow, making them useful for many industrial and household applications. Some common examples of liquids include water, oil, and alcohol. The properties of a liquid are determined by its intermolecular forces, temperature, and pressure.
