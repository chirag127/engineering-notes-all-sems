### CO-5 Estimate the rate constant of reaction. K3

The rate constant of a reaction, denoted by K, is a measure of the speed at which a reaction occurs. It is defined as the change in concentration of a reactant or product over time, and is usually expressed in units of mol/L/s. To estimate the rate constant of a reaction, one can perform experiments to measure the change in concentration of a reactant or product over time, and then use mathematical models to fit the data and determine the value of K. This can be done by plotting the data and using regression analysis to determine the slope of the line, which is directly proportional to the rate constant. Alternatively, one can use a method such as the integrated rate law, which involves integrating the rate equation over time to determine the value of K.
