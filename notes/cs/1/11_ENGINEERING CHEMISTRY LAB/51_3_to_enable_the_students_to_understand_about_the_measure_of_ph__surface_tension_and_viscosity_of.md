3. To enable the students to understand about the measure of pH, surface tension and viscosity of

The objective is to educate students about the measurement of pH, surface tension and viscosity. pH is a measure of the acidity or basicity of a solution and is expressed on a scale from 0 to 14. Surface tension is the measure of the cohesive forces between the molecules of a liquid, which cause it to behave as if it were a stretched elastic membrane. Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow and is related to its internal friction. Understanding these properties is important in fields such as chemistry, biology, and engineering, as they are relevant to a variety of real-world applications and processes. By measuring these properties, students can gain a deeper understanding of the fundamental principles that govern the behavior of fluids.
