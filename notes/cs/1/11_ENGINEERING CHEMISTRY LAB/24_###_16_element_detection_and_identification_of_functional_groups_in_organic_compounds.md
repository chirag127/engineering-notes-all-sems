### 16. Element detection and identification of functional groups in organic compounds.

Element detection in organic compounds is done using techniques such as mass spectrometry, infrared spectroscopy, and nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy. These techniques allow the determination of the molecular formula and the elemental composition of the compound.

Functional groups in organic compounds are specific arrangements of atoms that give the molecule its characteristic properties and reactivity. Common functional groups include alcohols, amines, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, and esters. Identification of functional groups can be done through various methods including infrared spectroscopy, proton NMR spectroscopy, and chemical reactions.
