Paracetamol is a commonly used pain reliever and fever reducer. It works by blocking the production of prostaglandins, which are chemicals that cause pain and inflammation. Paracetamol is available in many forms, including tablets, capsules, and syrups. It is typically taken orally and is well tolerated, with few side effects. However, it is important to follow the recommended dosage and to avoid taking more than the recommended amount, as this can lead to liver damage. Paracetamol is widely used and is considered to be a safe and effective pain reliever for most people.
