Viral multiplication is the process by which viruses replicate and spread within a host organism. This process involves several steps, including attachment, penetration, replication, and release.

Attachment: The virus attaches to specific receptors on the surface of the host cell.

Penetration: The virus enters the host cell and releases its genetic material into the cytoplasm.

Replication: The viral genetic material is replicated within the host cell, producing new viral particles.

Release: The new viral particles are released from the host cell and spread to other cells, leading to the spread of the virus.

Viral multiplication is a complex process that is dependent on the specific type of virus and the host organism. Understanding the process of viral multiplication is important for understanding the spread of viral diseases and for developing treatments and vaccines.
