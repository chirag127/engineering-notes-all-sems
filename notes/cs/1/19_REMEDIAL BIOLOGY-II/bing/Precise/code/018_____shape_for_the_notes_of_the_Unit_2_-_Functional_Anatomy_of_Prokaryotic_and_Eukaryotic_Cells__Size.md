### Shape of Bacterial Cells

- Bacterial cells come in a variety of shapes, including spheres (cocci), rods (bacilli), and spirals (spirilla).
- The shape of a bacterial cell is determined by its cell wall, which is a rigid structure that surrounds the cell membrane and provides support and protection.
- The shape of a bacterial cell can also be influenced by environmental factors, such as temperature and nutrient availability.
- Some bacteria can change their shape in response to changes in their environment, a process known as pleomorphism.
- The arrangement of bacterial cells can also vary, with some bacteria forming clusters, chains, or pairs.
- The shape and arrangement of bacterial cells can be used to help identify and classify different species of bacteria.
