### Shape of Bacterial Cells

- Bacterial cells come in a variety of shapes, including spherical (coccus), rod-shaped (bacillus), and spiral (spirillum, spirochete, or vibrio).
- The shape of a bacterial cell is determined by its cell wall and cytoskeleton.
- The arrangement of bacterial cells is also influenced by their shape. For example, cocci can be found in pairs (diplococci), chains (streptococci), or clusters (staphylococci).
- The shape and arrangement of bacterial cells can be used to help identify and classify different species of bacteria.
- Some bacteria are capable of changing their shape, a process known as pleomorphism. This can occur in response to environmental conditions or as part of the bacterial life cycle.
- The shape of a bacterial cell can also influence its ability to move, adhere to surfaces, and evade the immune system.
