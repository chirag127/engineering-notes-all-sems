### Relationship of Carbohydrate Metabolism to Other Compounds

- The breakdown and synthesis of carbohydrates, proteins, and lipids connect with the pathways of glucose catabolism .
- Simple sugars such as glucose, fructose, and galactose are catabolized during glycolysis .
- Fatty acids from fats connect with glucose catabolism through acetyl CoA .
- Amino acids from proteins connect with glucose catabolism through pyruvate, acetyl CoA, and components of the citric acid cycle .
- Metabolic pathways should be thought of as porous, meaning substances enter from other pathways, and intermediates leave for other pathways .
