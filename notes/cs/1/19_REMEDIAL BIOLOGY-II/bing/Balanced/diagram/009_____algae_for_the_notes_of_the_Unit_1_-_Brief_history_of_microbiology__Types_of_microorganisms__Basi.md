### Algae
- Algae are a large and diverse group of photosynthetic eukaryotic organisms that include seaweeds and many single-celled forms .
- Algae are not plants, but they share some characteristics with them, such as having chlorophyll and cell walls .
- Algae have many types of life cycles, and they range in size from microscopic to giant kelps that reach 60 metres in length.
- Algae can be classified into different groups based on their pigments, cell structure, and evolutionary history. Some of the major groups are:
  - Green algae: They have chlorophyll a and b, and store starch in their chloroplasts. They are the closest relatives of plants. Some examples are Spirogyra, Volvox, and Ulva .
  - Brown algae: They have chlorophyll a and c, and a brown pigment called fucoxanthin. They store carbohydrates as laminarin and mannitol. They are mostly marine and include kelps, rockweeds, and sargassum .
  - Red algae: They have chlorophyll a and d, and a red pigment called phycoerythrin. They store carbohydrates as floridean starch. They are mostly marine and include coralline algae, nori, and Irish moss .
  - Diatoms: They have chlorophyll a and c, and a yellow-brown pigment called diatoxanthin. They store carbohydrates as chrysolaminarin and oil. They have silica cell walls that form intricate patterns. They are mostly marine and freshwater, and are important producers of oxygen and organic matter .
  - Dinoflagellates: They have chlorophyll a and c, and various other pigments. They store carbohydrates as starch and oil. They have two flagella that make them spin. They are mostly marine and freshwater, and some are bioluminescent or cause red tides .
  - Euglenoids: They have chlorophyll a and b, and a red eyespot that detects light. They store carbohydrates as paramylon. They have one or two flagella and a flexible pellicle. They are mostly freshwater, and some are heterotrophic or mixotrophic .
- Algae play an important role in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals, such as agar, carrageenan, alginates, iodine, biofuels, antioxidants, and vitamins .
