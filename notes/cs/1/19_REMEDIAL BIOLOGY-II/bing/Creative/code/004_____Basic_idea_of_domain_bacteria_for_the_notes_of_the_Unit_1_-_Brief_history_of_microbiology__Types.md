### Basic idea of domain bacteria

- Domain bacteria is one of the three domains of life, along with domain archaea and domain eukarya.
- Domain bacteria includes the prokaryotes people encounter on an everyday basis. Prokaryotes are organisms that lack a membrane-bound nucleus and other organelles.
- Most bacterial species are heterotrophic; that is, they acquire their food from organic matter. The largest number of bacteria are saprobic, meaning that they feed on dead or decaying organic matter. A few bacterial species are parasitic; these bacteria live within host organisms and cause disease.
- Bacteria can also be classified by their shape, cell wall structure, metabolic pathways, and response to oxygen.
- The domain bacteria is divided into several phyla, such as proteobacteria, cyanobacteria, firmicutes, actinobacteria, and spirochaetes. Each phylum has its own characteristics and diversity of species.
- Bacteria play important roles in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals, such as antibiotics, enzymes, vitamins, hormones, and biofuels . Bacteria also participate in various biogeochemical cycles, such as nitrogen, carbon, and sulfur cycles .
- Bacteria can be studied using various methods, such as microscopy, staining, culturing, molecular biology, and bioinformatics . Bacteria can also be genetically modified for various purposes, such as bioremediation, biotechnology, and medicine .
