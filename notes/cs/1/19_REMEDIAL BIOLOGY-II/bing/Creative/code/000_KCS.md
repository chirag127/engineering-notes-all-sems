# KCS

- KCS stands for Knowledge-Centered Service or Knowledge-Centered Support .
- It is a best practice methodology that provides a detailed description of how service organizations can work more effectively with knowledge.
- The main goal of KCS is to improve service delivery, become more productive, decrease costs, and increase service levels to customers.
- KCS is based on four basic principles :
  - Create content as a by-product of solving problems.
  - Evolve content based on demand and usage.
  - Develop a knowledge base of collective experience.
  - Reward learning, collaboration, sharing, and improvement.
- KCS has two main processes: the Solve Loop and the Evolve Loop .
  - The Solve Loop is the process of capturing, structuring, and reusing knowledge during the problem-solving process.
  - The Evolve Loop is the process of analyzing, improving, and governing the knowledge base and the KCS practices.
- KCS has several benefits for service organizations, such as :
  - Increased customer satisfaction and loyalty.
  - Reduced time to resolution and repeat incidents.
  - Improved operational efficiency and scalability.
  - Enhanced employee engagement and retention.
  - Increased innovation and value creation.
