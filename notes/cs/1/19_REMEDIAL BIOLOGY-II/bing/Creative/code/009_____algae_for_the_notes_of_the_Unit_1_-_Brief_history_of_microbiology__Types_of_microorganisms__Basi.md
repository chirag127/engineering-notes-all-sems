# Algae

- Algae are a large and diverse group of photosynthetic eukaryotic organisms that include seaweeds and many single-celled forms .
- Algae are not plants, but they share some characteristics with them, such as having chlorophyll, cell walls, and the ability to produce oxygen .
- Algae belong to the kingdom Protista, which is a polyphyletic group of organisms that do not fit into any other kingdom .
- Algae can be classified into different groups based on their pigments, cell structure, storage products, and life cycles .
- Some of the major groups of algae are:
  - Green algae (Chlorophyta): They have chlorophyll a and b, store starch, and have cellulose in their cell walls. They are the most closely related to plants. Some examples are Spirogyra, Volvox, and Ulva .
  - Brown algae (Phaeophyta): They have chlorophyll a and c, and a brown pigment called fucoxanthin. They store laminarin and mannitol, and have alginic acid in their cell walls. They are mostly marine and include the largest algae, such as kelps .
  - Red algae (Rhodophyta): They have chlorophyll a and d, and a red pigment called phycoerythrin. They store floridean starch, and have agar and carrageenan in their cell walls. They are mostly marine and can live in deep waters .
  - Diatoms (Bacillariophyta): They have chlorophyll a and c, and a yellow-brown pigment called diatoxanthin. They store oil or chrysolaminarin, and have silica in their cell walls. They are mostly aquatic and have a variety of shapes and patterns .
  - Dinoflagellates (Dinophyta): They have chlorophyll a and c, and a red or brown pigment called peridinin. They store starch or oil, and have cellulose plates in their cell walls. They are mostly aquatic and have two flagella. Some of them can produce bioluminescence or toxins .
  - Euglenoids (Euglenophyta): They have chlorophyll a and b, and a yellow-green pigment called xanthophyll. They store paramylon, and have no cell wall but a proteinaceous pellicle. They are mostly freshwater and have one or two flagella. Some of them can lose their chloroplasts and become heterotrophic .
- Algae have many types of life cycles, ranging from simple asexual reproduction to complex alternation of generations .
- Algae play an important role in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals, such as agar, alginates, carrageenans, iodine, biofuels, antioxidants, and anti-inflammatory agents .
