### Algae
- Algae are a large and diverse group of photosynthetic eukaryotic organisms that are typically aquatic and include seaweeds and many single-celled forms  .
- Algae are not plants, but they share some characteristics with them, such as having chlorophyll, cell walls, and the ability to produce oxygen .
- Algae belong to the kingdom Protista, which is a polyphyletic group of organisms that do not fit into any other kingdom.
- Algae have many types of life cycles, ranging from simple asexual reproduction to complex alternation of generations.
- Algae vary in size from microscopic to macroscopic, and they can be found in almost every habitat on Earth, from freshwater to marine, from soil to snow, from hot springs to polar regions.
- Algae are important for the biosphere, as they produce a large amount of oxygen, serve as food and habitat for many aquatic animals, and are sources of biofuels, bioplastics, pharmaceuticals, and other useful products .
