# Enzymes

- Enzymes are **biological molecules** (typically proteins) that significantly speed up the rate of virtually all of the chemical reactions that take place within cells  .
- Enzymes are **vital for life** and serve a wide range of important functions in the body, such as aiding in digestion and metabolism .
- Enzymes act as **catalysts**, which means they lower the activation energy needed for a reaction to occur, and they increase the rate of the reaction .
- Enzymes are **specific**, which means they only catalyze one type of reaction or substrate .
- Enzymes have an **active site**, which is the region where the substrate binds to the enzyme and the reaction takes place .
- Enzymes can be **regulated** by various factors, such as temperature, pH, inhibitors, activators, and feedback mechanisms .
- Enzymes are **classified** into six major groups based on the type of reaction they catalyze: oxidoreductases, transferases, hydrolases, lyases, isomerases, and ligases.
