### ATP for the notes of the Unit 4 - Energy Utilization

- Structure of mitochondria: Double membrane organelle, inner membrane folded into cristae to increase surface area for ATP synthesis.
- Cellular respiration: Process of oxidizing nutrients to produce ATP. Takes place in mitochondria.
- Relationship of carbohydrate metabolism to other compounds: Carbohydrates broken down to pyruvate which enters Krebs cycle to produce NADH and FADH2. These carry electrons to ETC to produce ATP.
- Glycolysis: Breakdown of glucose into 2 pyruvate molecules, produces 2 ATP and 2 NADH. Takes place in cytoplasm.
- Formation of acetyl co-A: Pyruvate converted to acetyl co-A which enters Krebs cycle. Takes place in mitochondria matrix.
- Krebs cycle: Acetyl co-A oxidized to carbon dioxide, produces 3 NADH, 1 FADH2 and 1 ATP. Takes place in mitochondria matrix.
- Electron Transport System and Oxidative Phosphorylation: NADH and FADH2 transfer electrons to establish proton gradient used to synthesize maximum ATP. Takes place in mitochondria inner membrane.
- ATP: Adenosine Triphosphate, main energy currency of cells. Made through substrate level and oxidative phosphorylation.
- Factors affecting respiration: Available oxygen, nutrients, pH, temperature, hormones, allosteric regulators.
