### Role of microorganisms in the production of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals

- Microorganisms like bacteria, fungi, algae are used for large scale production of various industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals.
- This is known as industrial microbiology or biotechnology.
- Some of the important products are:
    - Alcohol: Ethanol produced by yeast is used as biofuel and in production of alcoholic beverages.
    - Organic acids: Citric acid produced by Aspergillus niger is used as acidulant in food industry. Lactic acid produced by Lactobacillus is used in wine and cheese industry.
    - Enzymes: Various enzymes like amylase, protease, lipase produced by bacteria and fungi are used in detergent industry.
    - Antibiotics: Penicillin produced by Penicillium notatum and cephalosporins produced by Cephalosporium acremonium are important antibiotics.
    - Vaccines: Bacterial and viral vaccines are produced using microorganisms.
    - Biopolymers: Polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB) produced by Ralstonia eutropha and Alginate produced by seaweeds are biodegradable polymers and have wide applications.
- Microorganisms are preferred for industrial production due to fast growth, easy to culture, genetic manipulations possible and economical production in large quantities.
- With advancements in biotechnology, genetically modified microorganisms are used to produce human proteins, antibodies and various other pharmaceuticals.
