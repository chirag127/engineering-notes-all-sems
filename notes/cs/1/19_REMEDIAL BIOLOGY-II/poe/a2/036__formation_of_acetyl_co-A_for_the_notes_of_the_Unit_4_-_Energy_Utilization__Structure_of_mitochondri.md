### Formation of Acetyl Co-A

- Pyruvate produced from glycolysis enters the mitochondrial matrix.
- Pyruvate is oxidized to acetyl CoA in two steps:
- First, pyruvate is decarboxylated by pyruvate dehydrogenase complex to form acetaldehyde with release of CO2. NAD+ is reduced to NADH.
- Second, acetaldehyde is oxidized to acetyl CoA by acetaldehyde dehydrogenase enzyme with the reduction of NAD+ to NADH.
- The acetyl CoA formed enters the Krebs cycle for complete oxidation to CO2 and H2O with the release of energy.
- The reduction of NAD+ to NADH in both the steps transfers the energy from pyruvate to NADH which carries the high energy electrons to electron transport system.
- The formation of acetyl CoA links glycolysis and Krebs cycle and facilitates the transfer of energy from carbohydrates to other cellular processes.
