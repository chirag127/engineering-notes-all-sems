### Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells

Prokaryotic cells:
- Bacteria are prokaryotic cells.
- Lack membrane-bound organelles like nucleus, mitochondria, etc.
- Genetic material is a single circular chromosome located in the cytoplasm.
- Ribosomes are smaller in size (70S).
- Reproduce asexually by binary fission.

Eukaryotic cells:
- Found in all multicellular organisms.
- Contain membrane-bound organelles like nucleus, mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum, etc.
- Larger in size than prokaryotic cells.
- Genetic material is located inside the nucleus in the form of linear chromosomes.
- Ribosomes are larger in size (80S).
- Undergo both sexual and asexual reproduction.

Size, shape, and arrangement of bacterial cells:
- Bacteria exhibit various shapes like cocci (spherical), bacilli (rod-shaped), spiral, etc.
- Arrangement can be singles (cocci), pairs (diplococci), chains (streptobacilli), clusters (staphylococci), etc.
- Size ranges from 0.5 to 5 ??m.

Structure and function of cells:
- Cell membrane: Acts as a barrier and facilitates transport of materials.
- Cytoplasm: Site for various metabolic activities. Contains enzymes and ribosomes.
- Ribosomes: Sites for protein synthesis.
- Nucleus (in eukaryotes): Contains DNA and is responsible for storing and transmitting genetic information.
- Other organelles like mitochondria, chloroplasts, ER, Golgi apparatus, etc. have specific functions related to energy, synthesis of macromolecules, transport, etc.
