### Gram +ve bacteria

- Gram +ve bacteria retain the crystal violet stain during Gram staining procedure due to the presence of thick peptidoglycan layer.
- Examples: Bacillus, Staphylococcus, Streptococcus, Clostridium, Corynebacterium, Listeria, etc.
- Characteristics:
    - Thick peptidoglycan layer
    - Teichoic acids present in the cell wall
    - Mostly spherical/cocci or rod shaped
    - Mostly non-spore formers except Bacillus and Clostridium which forms spores.
    - Mostly non-motile except few bacilli which are flagellated.
- Habitat: soil, water, plants, animals including humans.
- Role: decomposition of organic matter, production of dairy products, causing food spoilage, pathogens causing diseases, production of antibiotics.
