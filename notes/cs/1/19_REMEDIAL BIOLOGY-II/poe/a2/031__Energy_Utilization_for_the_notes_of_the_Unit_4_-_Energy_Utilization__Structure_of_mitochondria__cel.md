### Energy Utilization

**Structure of Mitochondria:**

- Double membrane organelle
- Inner membrane forms cristae which houses enzymes for Krebs cycle and ETC
- Matrix contains enzymes for beta-oxidation and Krebs cycle

**Cellular Respiration:**

- Process of breakdown of glucose to produce energy (ATP)
- Takes place in mitochondria
- 3 stages: Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, Electron Transport System

**Relationship of carbohydrate metabolism to other compounds:**

- Pyruvate from glycolysis enters Krebs cycle
- Acetyl CoA from beta-oxidation of fats enters Krebs cycle
- NADH and FADH2 from Krebs cycle feed electrons to ETC
- Amino acids from proteins degraded to TCA cycle intermediates to produce energy

**Glycolysis:**

- Occurs in cytoplasm
- Glucose converted to 2 pyruvate molecules
- Yields 2 ATP, 2 NADH
- Does not require oxygen (anaerobic)

**Formation of Acetyl CoA:**

- Pyruvate from glycolysis oxidized to acetyl CoA
- Requires oxygen (aerobic)
- Produces NADH and CO2

**Factors affecting respiration:**

- Oxygen availability: Anaerobic in absence of oxygen
- Substrate availability: Respiration rate depends on availability of glucose and oxygen
- Temperature: Increases with increase in temperature (Q10 = 2-3)
- pH: Optimum pH is 7, acidic/alkaline pH decreases rate
- Inhibitors: Cyanide inhibits cytochrome oxidase, affects ETS; Antimycin inhibits electron flow between complexes
