### Relationship of Carbohydrate Metabolism to Other Compounds

- Carbohydrate metabolism is linked to fat metabolism through acetyl CoA. Acetyl CoA produced from pyruvate in glycolysis can enter the Krebs cycle to produce energy or can be used to synthesize fatty acids.
- Amino acid metabolism is also linked to carbohydrate metabolism. Many amino acids can be converted to intermediates of the glycolytic pathway or the Krebs cycle and thus provide energy or gluconeogenic precursors.
- The pentose phosphate pathway, an alternative route for glucose-6-phosphate metabolism, produces NADPH which is important for biosynthesis of fatty acids and steroids.
- The conversion of pyruvate to lactate by lactate dehydrogenase in anaerobic conditions regenerates NAD+ which is necessary to continue glycolysis. This links carbohydrate and energy metabolism.
