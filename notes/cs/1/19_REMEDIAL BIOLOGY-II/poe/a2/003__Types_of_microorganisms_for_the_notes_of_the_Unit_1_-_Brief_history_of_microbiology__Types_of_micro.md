### Types of microorganisms

1. Bacteria
- Proteobacteria: E. coli, Salmonella, Pseudomonas
- Non-proteobacteria: Cyanobacteria, Spirochaetes, Actinobacteria (Streptomyces)
- Gram positive: Bacillus, Staphylococcus, Streptococcus
- Gram negative: E. coli, Pseudomonas, Salmonella

2. Fungi
- Yeast: Candida, Saccharomyces
- Molds: Aspergillus, Penicillium, Mucor

3. Algae
- Chlorophyceae (green algae): Chlamydomonas, Ulothrix, volvox
- Phaeophyceae (brown algae): Laminaria, Fucus
- Rhodophyceae (red algae): Polysiphonia, Gracilaria

4. Protozoa
- Amoeba, Paramecium, Plasmodium, Entamoeba, Toxoplasma

5. Helminths
- Platyhelminthes: Tapeworm, Fluke
- Nemathelminthes: Ascaris, Wuchereria

6. Viruses
- Structure: Capsid (protein coat) and nucleic acid (DNA/RNA)
- Multiplication: Using host cell machinery

7. Lichens
- Formed from symbiotic association of fungi and algae/cyanobacteria

8. Role of microorganisms in industrial and pharmaceutical production
- Enzymes: Amylase, cellulase, protease, lipase, lactase, etc.
- Antibiotics: Penicillin, streptomycin, cephalosporins, etc.
- Other drugs: Citric acid, statins, etc.
- Food and beverage production: Yogurt, cheese, kimchi, sauerkraut, vinegar, etc.
