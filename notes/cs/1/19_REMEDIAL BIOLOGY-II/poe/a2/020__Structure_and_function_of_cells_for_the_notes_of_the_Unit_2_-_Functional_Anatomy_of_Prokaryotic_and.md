### Structure and function of cells for the notes of the Unit 2 - Functional Anatomy of Prokaryotic and Eukaryotic Cells:

1. Size, shape, and arrangement of bacterial cells:
- Bacteria are prokaryotic cells that come in different shapes - spherical (cocci), rod-shaped (bacilli), spiral (spirochetes), comma-shaped (vibrios).
- The shape is determined by the cell wall and provides structural strength and resistance to osmotic pressure changes.
- Bacteria can exist as single cells or arrange in characteristic patterns - pairs (diplococci), chains (streptobacilli), clusters (staphylococci), etc.

2. Structure and function of cells:
- Both prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells have plasma membranes, cytoplasm, and ribosomes.
- Only eukaryotic cells have membrane-bound organelles like nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, ER, Golgi apparatus, etc.
- The nucleus controls all cell activities and contains DNA. Mitochondria generate ATP via cellular respiration. Chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis in plant cells.
- The ER and Golgi apparatus are involved in protein synthesis and modification.
- The structure of each organelle is suited to its function to facilitate efficient cellular processes.
