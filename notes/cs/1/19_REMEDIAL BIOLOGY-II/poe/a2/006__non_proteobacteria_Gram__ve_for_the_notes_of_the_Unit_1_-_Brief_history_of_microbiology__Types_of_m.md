### Non-proteobacteria Gram-negative bacteria

- **Pseudomonads**: Rod-shaped, aerobic, common in soil and water, opportunistic pathogens. Ex: Pseudomonas aeruginosa causes infections in immunocompromised patients.
- **Bacteroides**: Anaerobic, rod-shaped, found in animal intestines, some species opportunistic pathogens.
- **Fusobacteria**: Anaerobic, tapering rods, found in mouth and gut, some species opportunistic pathogens.
- **Spirochaetes**: Spiral-shaped, motile, some species opportunistic pathogens. Ex: Treponema pallidum causes syphilis.
