
### Viral Multiplication 

Viral multiplication is the process by which viruses reproduce themselves. Viruses are microscopic organisms that are not considered living, but are able to replicate themselves and cause disease. Viral multiplication involves the following steps:

1. Attachment: Viruses attach to a host cell and inject their genetic material into it. 
2. Penetration: The viral genetic material enters the host cell, where it takes over the host cell's functions and begins to replicate itself. 
3. Synthesis: The viral genetic material is used to create new viral particles, which are released from the host cell. 
4. Release: The new viral particles are released from the host cell and can spread to other cells, continuing the cycle of viral multiplication. 

Viral multiplication is an important part of the life cycle of viruses. It is also an important factor in the spread of viral diseases. A better understanding of viral multiplication can help in the development of treatments and vaccines for viral diseases.
