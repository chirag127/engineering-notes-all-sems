
### ATP for the notes of the Unit 4 - Energy Utilization

* Structure of mitochondria: Mitochondria are organelles found in eukaryotic cells. They are the main sites of energy production in the cell, and are responsible for the conversion of energy from food into a form that is usable by the cell. Mitochondria are composed of two membranes: an outer membrane and an inner membrane. The inner membrane is folded into cristae that increase the surface area available for energy production.

* Cellular respiration: Cellular respiration is the process by which cells convert energy from food into a form that is usable by the cell. It involves the breakdown of glucose molecules into smaller molecules, and the release of energy in the form of ATP. Cellular respiration occurs in three stages: glycolysis, the Krebs cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation.

* Relationship of carbohydrate metabolism to other compounds: Carbohydrate metabolism is the process by which carbohydrates are broken down into smaller molecules and used as a source of energy. This process is related to other metabolic processes such as protein and fat metabolism, as all of these processes involve the breakdown of molecules and the release of energy.

* Glycolysis: Glycolysis is the first step in cellular respiration. It involves the breakdown of glucose molecules into two molecules of pyruvate, with the release of energy in the form of ATP.

* Formation of acetyl co-A: Acetyl co-A is a molecule that is formed from the breakdown of pyruvate molecules in the Krebs cycle. It is used as a source of energy in the cell, and can also be used to produce other molecules such as fatty acids.

* Kreb cycle: The Krebs cycle is the second stage of cellular respiration. It involves the breakdown of acetyl co-A molecules into smaller molecules, with the release of energy in the form of ATP.

* Electron Transport System and Oxidative Phosphorylation: The Electron Transport System and Oxidative Phosphorylation are the third stage of cellular respiration. In this stage, electrons are transported along the electron transport chain, with the release of energy in the form of ATP.

* ATP: ATP (adenosine triphosphate) is a molecule that is used as a source of energy in the cell. It is produced during cellular respiration, and is used to power many of the cell's activities.

* Factors affecting respiration: There are several factors that can affect the rate of respiration in cells. These include temperature, pH, oxygen availability, and the availability of nutrients.
