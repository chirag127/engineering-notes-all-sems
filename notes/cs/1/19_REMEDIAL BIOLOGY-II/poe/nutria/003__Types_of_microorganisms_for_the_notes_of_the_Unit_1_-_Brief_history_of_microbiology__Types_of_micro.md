
### Types of Microorganisms

1. Bacteria: Bacteria are single-celled microorganisms that belong to the Domain Bacteria. They can be further divided into two groups, Proteobacteria and non-Proteobacteria. Proteobacteria are Gram-negative and non-Proteobacteria are Gram-positive.

2. Lichens: Lichens are composite organisms that are formed when a fungus and an algae or cyanobacterium live together in a symbiotic relationship.

3. Algae: Algae are a diverse group of aquatic organisms that range from simple, single-celled organisms to complex multicellular forms.

4. Protozoa: Protozoa are single-celled organisms that can move independently and feed on organic matter.

5. Helminthes: Helminthes are parasitic worms that can cause infections in humans and other animals.

6. Viruses: Viruses are small infectious agents that cannot replicate on their own. They require a host cell to replicate and cause infection.

7. Role of Microorganisms in Industrial Chemicals and Pharmaceuticals: Microorganisms are used in the production of a variety of industrial chemicals and pharmaceuticals, such as antibiotics, enzymes, vitamins, and other compounds.
