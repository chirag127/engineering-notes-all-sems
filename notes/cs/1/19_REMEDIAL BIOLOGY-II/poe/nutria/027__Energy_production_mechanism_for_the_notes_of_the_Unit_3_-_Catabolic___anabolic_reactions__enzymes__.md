
### Energy production mechanism

1. Catabolic and anabolic reactions are metabolic processes that involve breaking down and building up of molecules, respectively. 
2. Enzymes are proteins that act as catalysts in metabolic reactions, speeding up the rate of reaction. 
3. Carbohydrate metabolism involves the breakdown of sugars and starches into energy molecules. 
4. Lipid catabolism is the breakdown of fat molecules into energy-rich molecules. 
5. Protein catabolism is the breakdown of proteins into energy-rich molecules. 
6. The energy production mechanism involves the conversion of energy-rich molecules into ATP (adenosine triphosphate), which is the primary energy source for cells. 
7. Metabolic diversity refers to the variety of metabolic pathways used by different organisms to obtain energy. 
8. Pathways of energy use refer to the pathways taken by energy molecules in order to be used by cells. 
9. Integration of metabolism in the subject of Remedial Biology-II KCS refers to the application of metabolic principles to the study of biological systems.
