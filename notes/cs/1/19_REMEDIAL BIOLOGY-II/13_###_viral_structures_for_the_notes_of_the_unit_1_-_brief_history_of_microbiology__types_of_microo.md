Viral Structures:
- Viruses are tiny infectious agents that cannot replicate on their own and need a host cell to reproduce.
- The structure of a virus consists of genetic material (DNA or RNA) surrounded by a protein coat called a capsid.
- Some viruses also have an outer envelope composed of lipids.
- The capsid protects the viral genetic material and helps the virus infect host cells.
- The shape of the capsid can vary, with some viruses having a spherical shape and others having a more complex shape.
- The size of viruses also varies, with some being only a few nanometers in diameter, while others are much larger.
- Understanding the structure of viruses is important for understanding how they infect host cells and cause disease.
