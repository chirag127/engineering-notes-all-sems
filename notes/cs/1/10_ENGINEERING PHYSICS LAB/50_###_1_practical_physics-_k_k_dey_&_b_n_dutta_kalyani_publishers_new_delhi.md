### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

"Practical Physics" by K. K. Dey and B. N. Dutta is a textbook that provides a comprehensive introduction to the principles and applications of physics. The book is published by Kalyani Publishers in New Delhi and is designed for students of physics at the undergraduate level.

The book covers a wide range of topics in physics, including mechanics, thermodynamics, waves and optics, electromagnetism, and modern physics. Each chapter provides a clear and concise explanation of the fundamental concepts and principles, along with numerous examples and illustrations to help students understand the material. The book also includes a variety of practical exercises and experiments, allowing students to apply their knowledge and develop their experimental skills.

In addition to the comprehensive coverage of physics, the book also includes a number of pedagogical features to support student learning. These include review questions, key terms, and summaries at the end of each chapter, as well as numerous diagrams and illustrations to help clarify complex concepts.

Overall, "Practical Physics" by K. K. Dey and B. N. Dutta is a valuable resource for students of physics, providing a comprehensive introduction to the subject and a wealth of practical exercises and experiments to help students develop their knowledge and skills.
