At the end of a course, students will typically be able to demonstrate a range of knowledge, skills, and competencies that they have acquired during the course. The specific outcomes that students will be able to achieve will depend on the content and focus of the course.

2. Skills: Students will have developed a range of practical skills that are relevant to the course content. For example, they may have learned how to use software or tools, conduct research, or analyze data.

3. Competencies: Students will have developed a range of personal competencies that are relevant to the course content. For example, they may have improved their critical thinking, problem-solving, or communication skills.

In conclusion, at the end of a course, students will typically be able to demonstrate a range of knowledge, skills, and competencies that they have acquired during the course.
