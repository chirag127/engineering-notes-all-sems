4. Determining the Focal Length of the Combination of Two Lenses Separated by a Distance:

The focal length of a lens is a measure of its ability to focus light. In optics, the focal length of a lens is defined as the distance between the lens and the point where the light rays coming from infinity converge to a single point.

When two lenses are separated by a distance, the effective focal length of the combination can be determined using the lens formula. The lens formula states that the product of the distance between the lenses and the sum of their reciprocal focal lengths is equal to 1.

To determine the focal length of the combination, the following steps should be taken:

1. Measure the distance between the two lenses.

2. Measure the focal lengths of each lens.

3. Use the lens formula to calculate the effective focal length of the combination.

The lens formula can be used to determine the effective focal length of any combination of lenses, regardless of the number of lenses or their separation distance. This information can be used to design optical systems, such as cameras and telescopes, and to understand the behavior of light as it passes through lenses.
