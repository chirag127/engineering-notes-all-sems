#### Group B

Group B was a category of rally cars that was active from 1982-1986. It was defined by the FIA (Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile) as "vehicles built to a new, more stringent set of regulations that allowed greater freedom in design and engineering." The result was a new breed of rally car that was faster, more powerful, and more technologically advanced than their predecessors. Some of the most famous Group B rally cars include the Audi Quattro, Peugeot 205 T16, Lancia Delta S4, and Ford RS200. However, the category was eventually discontinued due to safety concerns after several fatal accidents involving both drivers and spectators. Despite this, Group B rally cars have remained popular among fans and collectors, and their legacy continues to influence the sport of rally racing to this day.
