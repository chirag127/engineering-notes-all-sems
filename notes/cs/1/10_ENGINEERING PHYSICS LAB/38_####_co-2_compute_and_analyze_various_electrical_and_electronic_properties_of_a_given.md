5. Determine the optical properties of CO2, such as its refractive index and absorption spectra, to understand its interaction with light. 
6. Study the mechanical properties of CO2, such as its compressibility and tensile strength, to understand its behavior under stress. 
7. Analyze the chemical properties of CO2, such as its reactivity and stability, to understand its behavior in different environments.
