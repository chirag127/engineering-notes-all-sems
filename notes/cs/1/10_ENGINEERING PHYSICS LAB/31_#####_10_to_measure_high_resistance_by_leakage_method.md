##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

Measurement of high resistance by leakage method:

The leakage method is a technique used to measure high resistance values in electrical circuits. It is based on the principle of measuring the leakage current that flows through a high resistance when it is connected in series with a low resistance in a complete circuit.

1. Equipment: To measure high resistance by the leakage method, you will need a digital multimeter (DMM), a low resistance, and a high resistance. The low resistance is used to create a complete circuit, and the DMM is used to measure the voltage across the high resistance and the leakage current.

2. Circuit setup: The high resistance is connected in series with the low resistance in a complete circuit. The DMM is connected in parallel with the high resistance to measure the voltage across it.

3. Measurement: The voltage across the high resistance is measured using the DMM. The leakage current is calculated by dividing the voltage by the resistance value.

4. Accuracy: The accuracy of the measurement depends on the accuracy of the DMM and the stability of the circuit. It is important to ensure that the circuit is stable and that the DMM is calibrated correctly.

In conclusion, the leakage method is a technique used to measure high resistance values in electrical circuits. It involves connecting the high resistance in series with a low resistance in a complete circuit and measuring the voltage across the high resistance and the leakage current. The accuracy of the measurement depends on the accuracy of the DMM and the stability of the circuit.
