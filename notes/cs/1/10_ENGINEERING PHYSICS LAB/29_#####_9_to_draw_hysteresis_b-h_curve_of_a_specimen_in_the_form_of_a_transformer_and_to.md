##### 9. To draw hysteresis (B-H curve) of a specimen in the form of a transformer and to

To draw a B-H curve (hysteresis loop) of a transformer specimen, you need to perform the following steps:
1. Prepare the specimen: This involves cutting a piece of the transformer core and winding it with a coil.
2. Set up the equipment: You need a B-H loop tracer, a power supply, and an oscilloscope. 
3. Apply a varying magnetic field (H): The power supply is used to apply a magnetic field to the specimen. 
4. Measure the magnetic flux density (B): The B-H loop tracer measures the magnetic flux density (B) in response to the applied magnetic field (H).
5. Plot the data: Plot the data on a graph, with magnetic field (H) on the x-axis and magnetic flux density (B) on the y-axis. 
6. Repeat steps 3-5 for multiple magnetic fields: Repeat the process for multiple magnetic fields to get a complete hysteresis loop.
7. Analyze the data: The shape of the hysteresis loop provides information about the magnetic properties of the specimen.

Note: The steps may vary slightly depending on the equipment and method used.
