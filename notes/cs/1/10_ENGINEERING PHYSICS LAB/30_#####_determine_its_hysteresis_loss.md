Hysteresis loss is a type of energy loss that occurs in magnetic materials when they are subjected to a changing magnetic field. It is related to the lag in the magnetic polarization of the material as the magnetic field changes direction.

To determine the hysteresis loss in a magnetic material, there are several methods that can be used, including the B-H loop and the Jiles-Atherton model.

1. B-H loop: The B-H loop is a graphical representation of the relationship between magnetic field strength (B) and magnetic flux density (H) in a magnetic material. The hysteresis loop is obtained by measuring the magnetic flux density as the magnetic field is increased and decreased. The area enclosed by the loop represents the hysteresis loss for a complete cycle of magnetic field change.

2. Jiles-Atherton model: The Jiles-Atherton model is a mathematical model that describes the behavior of magnetic materials under a changing magnetic field. The model takes into account the magnetic properties of the material, such as magnetic susceptibility and magnetic permeability, and predicts the hysteresis loss as a function of magnetic field strength and frequency.

In conclusion, to determine the hysteresis loss in a magnetic material, methods such as the B-H loop and the Jiles-Atherton model can be used. These methods provide a graphical or mathematical representation of the relationship between magnetic field strength and magnetic flux density, and allow for the calculation of the hysteresis loss for a complete cycle of magnetic field change.
