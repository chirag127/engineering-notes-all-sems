##### 1. To determine the energy band gap of a given semiconductor material.

To determine the energy band gap of a semiconductor material, you can use a number of experimental techniques, including:
1. Photoluminescence spectroscopy
2. Optical absorption spectroscopy
3. Electron paramagnetic resonance spectroscopy
4. Thermally stimulated current spectroscopy
5. Electron beam induced current spectroscopy

The energy band gap is an important parameter in determining the electronic properties of a material and its suitability for various applications, such as solar cells, LEDs, and electronic devices. By measuring the absorption or emission of light, or the response of the material to an applied electric field, you can determine the energy band gap and other important parameters such as the density of states and the mobility of electrons and holes.
