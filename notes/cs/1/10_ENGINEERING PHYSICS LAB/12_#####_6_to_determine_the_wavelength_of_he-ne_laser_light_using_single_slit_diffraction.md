##### 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

"To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction" is an experiment that aims to measure the wavelength of light emitted by a He-Ne laser using the principle of diffraction.

1. Diffraction: Diffraction is the bending of light as it passes through a narrow opening, such as a single slit. The amount of diffraction depends on the size of the opening and the wavelength of the light.

2. Single Slit Diffraction: In single slit diffraction, light passes through a single, narrow slit and spreads out on the other side, forming a diffraction pattern. The diffraction pattern is characterized by bright and dark fringes that occur at specific angles.

3. Experiment: To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light, a single slit is placed in front of the laser and the diffraction pattern is observed on a screen placed behind the slit. The distance between the slit and the screen, as well as the width of the slit, are known. By measuring the angles of the bright fringes, the wavelength of the laser light can be calculated using the formula for single slit diffraction.

4. Precautions: To ensure accurate results, it is important to maintain a constant distance between the slit and the screen, and to use a clean and well-aligned laser. The width of the slit should also be carefully controlled to minimize errors.

In conclusion, "To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction" is an experiment that uses the principle of diffraction to measure the wavelength of light emitted by a He-Ne laser. The experiment involves observing the diffraction pattern produced by a single slit, and calculating the wavelength of the laser light based on the angles of the bright fringes. To ensure accurate results, it is important to maintain a constant distance between the slit and the screen, and to use a clean and well-aligned laser.
