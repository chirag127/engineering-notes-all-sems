#### 4. To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance

When two lenses are placed in contact, the combination is called a lens system. The focal length of the system can be calculated using the following formula:

1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/(f1*f2)

Where f1 and f2 are the focal lengths of the individual lenses, d is the distance between the lenses, and f is the focal length of the lens system.

To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance, follow these steps:

1. Measure the focal length of each lens using a lensometer or by using the lens formula: 1/f = (n-1)(1/R1 - 1/R2), where n is the refractive index of the lens, R1 is the radius of curvature of one side of the lens, and R2 is the radius of curvature of the other side of the lens.

3. Substitute the values of f1, f2, and d into the formula:

1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/(f1*f2)

4. Solve for f to obtain the focal length of the lens system.
