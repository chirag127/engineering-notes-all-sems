##### 2. To study Hall effect and determine Hall coefficient, carrier density and mobility of a semiconductor

1. The Hall effect is used to determine the carrier type (electrons or holes), concentration and mobility in a semiconductor.
2. Connect the semiconductor sample, contact pads and power supply as shown in the circuit diagram.
3. Pass current through the sample and measure the Hall voltage (VH) across the two sides of the sample.
4. Calculate the Hall coefficient (RH) using the relation: RH = VH/IB
5. Calculate the carrier density (n) and mobility (μ) using the relations:
n = 1/RH.q
μ = 1/ne.RH
6. The sign of the Hall coefficient indicates whether the charge carriers are electrons (RH is negative) or holes (RH is positive). The magnitude of RH gives the carrier concentration and mobility.
7. Tabulate the results for different values of current through the sample. Plot a graph of Hall voltage Vs current to check linearity which indicates Ohmic contacts.
