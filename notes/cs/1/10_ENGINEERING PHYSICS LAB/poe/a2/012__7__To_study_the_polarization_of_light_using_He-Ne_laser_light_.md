##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

1.  Set up the He-Ne laser and ensure it is operational. The laser light emitted will be unpolarized.
2.  Place a polarizing sheet (linear polarizer) in the path of the laser beam. Slowly rotate the polarizer to observe the intensity of transmitted light. It will go through a maximum and minimum as the polarizer is rotated. This shows that the light is polarized in a particular plane by the polarizer.
3.  Replace the linear polarizer with a circular polarizer and repeat step 2. In this case, the intensity of transmitted light remains unchanged as the polarizer is rotated. This shows that the circularly polarized light does not have a fixed plane of polarization.
4.  Use a quarter wave plate after the linear polarizer and observe the variations in intensity as the wave plate is rotated. This demonstrates the conversion between linear and circular polarization.

The above steps demonstrate the polarization of light from an unpolarized He-Ne laser source using linear polarizer, circular polarizer and quarter wave plate. The observations show that the polarization state of light can be linear, circular or elliptical depending on the type of polarizer used.
