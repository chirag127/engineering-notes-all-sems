##### 8. To calibrate the given ammeter and voltmeter by potentiometer.

1. Connect the ammeter and voltmeter in series with a variable resistance (potentiometer).
2. Note down the initial readings of ammeter and voltmeter.
3. Adjust the potentiometer and change the current through the ammeter.
4. Note down the corresponding voltmeter readings for different ammeter readings.
5. Plot a graph between voltmeter and ammeter readings.
6. The graph should be a straight line passing through the origin. If not, adjust the zero adjustment screws of ammeter and voltmeter to get the straight line graph passing through origin.
7. Once calibrated, disconnect the instruments and use.

The calibration ensures that the instruments show accurate readings. A potentiometer is used to vary the current and obtain different readings on instruments to plot the graph and perform calibration.
