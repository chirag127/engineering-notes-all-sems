##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

- Connect the unknown resistance (Rx) across a variable voltage source.
- Connect a milli-ammeter in series with the resistance to measure the current flowing through it.
- Initially apply a small potential difference across the resistance and adjust it until a suitable current starts flowing through the circuit. Note down this voltage reading (V1).
- Now slowly increase the potential difference and note down the voltage (V2) when the current is doubled.
- Using the values of V1, V2 and the known current, calculate the unknown resistance (Rx) using the voltage divider rule.
- This method is useful for measuring resistance values greater than 1MΩ since the small leakage currents are measured. The high resistance to be measured is connected in series with a voltage source and ammeter. The voltage across the resistance is slowly increased till the current is measurable. The voltage difference for a known increase in current is used to calculate the resistance.
