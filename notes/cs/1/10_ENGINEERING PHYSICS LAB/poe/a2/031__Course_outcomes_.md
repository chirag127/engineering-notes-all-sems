## Course outcomes:

1. Students will understand the fundamental concepts and principles of software engineering.
- They will understand the software development lifecycle and process models.
- They will learn various techniques and tools used in requirement analysis, design, implementation, testing, and maintenance of software.

2. Students will learn to apply software engineering concepts and techniques to design and develop software solutions.
- They will work on a variety of individual and group programming projects and case studies to develop software solutions for some real-world problems.
- They will learn to use appropriate tools and languages for different software solutions.

3. Students will develop soft skills such as communication skills, teamwork skills, and time management skills that are required for software engineering professionals.
- They will work collaboratively in teams to analyze problems, design solutions, and implement software.
- They will learn to document their work and communicate with others effectively.

4. Students will understand the social, ethical, and professional responsibilities of software engineers.
- They will understand the impact of software solutions on individuals, organizations, and society.
- They will develop an awareness of the moral and ethical issues surrounding the use of software and its applications.
