#### CO-5 Study and estimate the performance and parameter of given equipment by using:

1. Specifications - Refer to the equipment specifications to understand its capabilities and limitations. Check parameters like speed, capacity, power consumption, noise levels, etc. and compare with requirements.
2. Trend analysis - Analyse trends from historical data to estimate future performance. For example, check failure rates and wavelengths to predict Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF) and Mean Time To Repair (MTTR).
3. Calculations - Use standard formulas and calculations to estimate metrics. For example, calculate speed from spindle RPM, calculate access time from seek time and rotational latency, etc.
4.Graphs and charts - Plot graphs and charts to visualise performance and trends. For example, plot throughput vs load charts or CPU usage vs time charts.
5.Comparisons - Compare the equipment under evaluation with similar equipment that is already in use. Note down pros and cons of each and how the new equipment would complement or replace existing ones.
