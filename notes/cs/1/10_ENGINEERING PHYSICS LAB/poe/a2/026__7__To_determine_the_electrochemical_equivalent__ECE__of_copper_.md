##### 7. To determine the electrochemical equivalent (ECE) of copper:

1. Take a clean copper electrode and weigh it accurately. Record the mass (m1) in grams.
2. Immerse the electrode in a solution of copper(II) sulfate and pass a known current (I) through it for time (t) in seconds.
3. Remove the electrode, rinse it with distilled water and weigh it again. Record the mass (m2).
4. The mass increase (m2 - m1) is the mass of copper deposited. Calculate the electrochemical equivalent of copper (ECE) using:

ECE = (m2 - m1) / It

The unit for ECE is grams/coulomb.

This experiment can be repeated with different currents and time intervals to get consistent values of ECE. The electrochemical equivalent is a constant for a given electrode reaction at a given temperature.
