##### 5. To determine resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance.

1.  Take the given resistor and measure its length `l` and cross-sectional area `A`. Note down the values.
2.  Measure the resistance `R` of the resistor using a multimeter. Note down the value.
3.  Calculate the resistance per unit length (ρ) using the formula:
    > ρ = R/l
    Where,
    ρ = Resistance per unit length (Ω/m)
    R = Resistance of resistor (Ω)
    l = Length of resistor (m)
4.  Calculate the specific resistance (ρs) using the formula:
    > ρs = ρ x A
    Where,
    ρs = Specific resistance (Ω-m)
    ρ = Resistance per unit length (Ω/m)
    A = Cross-sectional area of resistor (m2)
5.  Tabulate the calculated values of ρ and ρs.
