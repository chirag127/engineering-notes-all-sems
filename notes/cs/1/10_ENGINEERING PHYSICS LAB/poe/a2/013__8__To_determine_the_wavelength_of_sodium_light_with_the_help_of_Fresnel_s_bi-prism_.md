#### 8. To determine the wavelength of sodium light with the help of Fresnel’s bi-prism.

- Take a Fresnel’s bi-prism and sodium light source.
- Adjust the sodium light source such that the light falls normally on one face of the bi-prism.
- Two beams will emerge making an angle with each other. Measure this angle with the help of a graduated circle.
- Let the angle be ‘2α’. Note down the value of ‘α’.
- The wavelength of sodium light (λ) can be calculated using the relation:

λ = 2d sinα

Where,
d = thickness of the bi-prism
α = angle of emergence

- The wavelength of sodium light thus calculated comes out to be approximately 5890 Å.
