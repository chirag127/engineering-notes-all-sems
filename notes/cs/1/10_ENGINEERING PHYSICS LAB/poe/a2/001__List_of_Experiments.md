## List of Experiments

1. Cavendish's experiment: This experiment was conducted by Henry Cavendish in 1798 to measure the universal gravitational constant (G) and density of Earth. He used a torsion balance with two lead spheres suspended from opposite arms to measure the gravitational attraction between them and large lead spheres.

2. Michelson-Morley experiment: This experiment was conducted by Albert Michelson and Edward Morley in 1887 to measure the ether drift and check the validity of ether theory. They used an interferometer to measure the speed of light in perpendicular directions and found no difference, disproving the ether theory. This led to the development of special theory of relativity.

3. Gold leaf electroscope: This is an instrument used to detect and measure electric charges. It consists of two thin gold leaves suspended from a metal wire. When an electric charge is brought near the gold leaves, they diverge due to repulsion between like charges. The extent of divergence can be used to compare the magnitude of charges. It is used to demonstrate electrostatic induction and charging by conduction and friction.

4. Millikan's oil drop experiment: This experiment was conducted by Robert Millikan in 1909 to measure the elementary electric charge. He sprayed oil droplets in an electric field and measured their fall velocity and equilibrium position to calculate the charge on the droplets. He showed that charges on droplets were integer multiples of a fundamental charge, confirming the quantization of electric charge.
