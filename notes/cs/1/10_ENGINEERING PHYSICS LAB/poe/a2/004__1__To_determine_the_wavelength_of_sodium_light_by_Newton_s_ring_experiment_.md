##### 1. To determine the wavelength of sodium light by Newton’s ring experiment.

1. Take a plano-convex lens and place it over a plane glass plate with the convex surface facing downwards.
2. Draw a circular ring of sodium light on the top surface of the lens by placing a sodium flame near its edge.
3. Measure the diameter of the ring and the radius of curvature of the lens.
4. Calculate the wavelength of sodium light using the formula:
Wavelength = 2 x (radius of curvature) x (order of ring) / diameter of ring

5. The wavelength of sodium light comes out to be ~5893 Å.

The experiment demonstrates the phenomenon of interference of light and is used to determine the wavelength of monochromatic light sources like sodium flame. The results can be used to calculate the refractive index of the material of the lens using the relation:
Refractive index = (radius of curvature) / (diameter of ring x wavelength)
