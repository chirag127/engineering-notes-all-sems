#### Various Experiments

1. Scientific Method
- Make an observation or identify a problem or question to be answered.
- Formulate a hypothesis or a potential explanation.
- Design and perform an experiment to test the hypothesis.
- Analyze the data and draw a conclusion.
- Communicate the results.

2. Controlled Experiments
- Hold all variables constant except the one being tested.
- Use a control group and experimental group.
- The control group establishes a baseline for comparison.
- Repeatable experiments are most valid.

3. Observations and Measurements
- Qualitative observations describe properties or events.
- Quantitative measurements use numbers to measure properties.
- Measurements must be precise, accurate, and repeatable.
- Choose appropriate tools for measurements.
