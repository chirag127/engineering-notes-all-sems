##### 5. To measure attenuation in an optical fiber:

1. Connect the optical fiber to a light source (LED or laser) at one end. Connect the other end of the optical fiber to an optical power meter.
2. Switch on the light source and note down the initial optical power reading on the power meter (P<sub>i</sub>).
3. Increase the length of the optical fiber and note down the optical power reading (P<sub>f</sub>).
4. Calculate the attenuation (α) in dB/km using the formula:

α = (10log<sub>10</sub>(P<sub>i</sub>/P<sub>f</sub>)) × (1/l)

Where l is the length of the optical fiber in km.
5. Repeat step #3 and #4 for different wavelengths to get the attenuation spectrum and check for spikes.
6. The attenuation coefficient depends on the materials and quality of the optical fiber. Lower attenuation leads to less signal loss over longer transmission distances.
