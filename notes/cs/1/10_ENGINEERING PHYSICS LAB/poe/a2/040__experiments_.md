#### Experiments

1. Scientific Method: The scientific method is the fundamental process by which scientific knowledge is acquired and scientific discoveries are made. It involves the following steps:
- Making an observation about the world.
- Formulating a hypothesis that may explain the observation.
- Designing an experiment to test the hypothesis.
- Collecting and analyzing the data from the experiment.
- Drawing a conclusion and evaluating whether it supports or refutes the hypothesis.
- Communicating the results.

2. Controlled Experiments: A controlled experiment is an experiment that controls all factors except one and measures the effect of the one variable factor on the outcome. This is done to ensure that the experiment is fair and accurate and the results can be clearly attributed to the variable being tested. Controlled experiments are widely used in scientific research to test hypotheses and theories.

3. Variables: The factors that can change or be changed in an experiment are known as variables. There are three types of variables:
- Independent variable: The variable that is intentionally changed or manipulated by the researcher to test its effect on the dependent variable.
- Dependent variable: The variable that is observed or measured as a possible effect of the independent variable.
- Controlled variable: Variables that are kept constant to avoid confounding the effects of the independent variable.
