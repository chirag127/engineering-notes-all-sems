##### 4. To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance:

1. Let the power of the lenses be P1 and P2 and their focal lengths be f1 and f2 respectively.
2. The power of the combination is P = P1 + P2
3. The focal length of the combination is f = 1/P
4. Therefore, the focal length of the combination of two lenses is given by:

f = 1/(1/f1 + 1/f2)

The formula can be derived from the lens maker's formula and applying the thin lens approximation. This method can be extended to find the focal length of a combination of multiple lenses separated by distances. The final result only depends on the individual powers or focal lengths of the constituent lenses.
