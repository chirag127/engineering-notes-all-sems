#### CO-3 Verify different established laws with the help of optical and electrical

1. Ohm's law: Ohm's law states that the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the voltage applied across it, as long as the temperature and other physical conditions remain constant. It can be verified by using a resistor, a voltmeter, and an ammeter. Connect the resistor to a voltage source and measure the current and voltage to check if they are directly proportional.

2. Kirchhoff's laws: Kirchhoff's laws state that (1) the sum of currents entering a node is zero, and (2) the sum of voltage drops around a closed loop is zero. They can be verified using a circuit with multiple resistors, voltage sources, and current sources. Measure the currents and voltages to check if Kirchhoff's laws hold true for the circuit.

3. Faraday's law: Faraday's law states that the induced electromotive force in a closed circuit is equal to the negative of the rate of change of the magnetic flux through the circuit. It can be verified by passing a changing magnetic field through a coil and measuring the induced EMF. The EMF and rate of change of magnetic flux will be found to have an inverse relationship, verifying Faraday's law.
