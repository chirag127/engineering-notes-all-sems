
##### Given Semiconductor Material using Hall Effect Setup

1. The Hall effect is a physical phenomenon in which a voltage difference is generated across a sample when an electric current is passed through it in the presence of an external magnetic field. 
2. The Hall effect setup consists of a sample of semiconductor material, a source of electric current, and a magnetic field. 
3. When an electric current is passed through the sample, the magnetic field causes a voltage difference to be generated across the sample. 
4. This voltage difference is known as the Hall voltage, and it can be measured using a voltmeter. 
5. The Hall effect can be used to measure the electrical conductivity of a sample, as well as its carrier density and mobility. 
6. It can also be used to determine the type of semiconductor material, as different materials have different Hall effect characteristics. 
7. The Hall effect is an important tool for characterizing the electrical properties of semiconductor materials.
