
#### CO-4 Determine and calculate various physical properties of a given material by using

* Physical properties of a material refer to characteristics that can be observed or measured without changing the composition of the material.
* Examples of physical properties include: density, boiling point, melting point, electrical conductivity, thermal conductivity, and refractive index.
* Density is a measure of the mass of a material per unit volume. It can be calculated by dividing the mass by the volume. 
* Boiling point is the temperature at which a liquid boils and changes to a gas.
* Melting point is the temperature at which a solid melts and changes to a liquid.
* Electrical conductivity is a measure of how well a material conducts electricity. It is usually measured in units of siemens per meter (S/m).
* Thermal conductivity is a measure of how well a material conducts heat. It is usually measured in units of watts per meter per Kelvin (W/mK).
* Refractive index is a measure of how much a material bends light. It is usually measured in units of reciprocal meters (1/m).
