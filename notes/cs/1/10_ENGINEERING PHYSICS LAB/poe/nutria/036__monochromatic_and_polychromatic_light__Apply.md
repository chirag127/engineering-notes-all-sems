
#### Monochromatic and Polychromatic Light

* Monochromatic light is light that consists of a single wavelength, or color. It is typically produced by a laser or a light-emitting diode (LED).
* Polychromatic light is light that consists of multiple wavelengths, or colors. It is typically produced by a light bulb or a natural light source such as the sun.
* Monochromatic light is used in applications such as spectroscopy, medical imaging, and laser surgery.
* Polychromatic light is used in applications such as photography, video production, and lighting for stage productions.
* Monochromatic light can be manipulated to produce a range of colors by passing it through a diffraction grating or prism.
* Polychromatic light can be manipulated to produce a range of colors by passing it through a filter or a color wheel.
