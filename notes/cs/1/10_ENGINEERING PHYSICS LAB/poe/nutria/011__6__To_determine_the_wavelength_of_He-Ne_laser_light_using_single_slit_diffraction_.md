
##### 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction

* The He-Ne laser light is a type of monochromatic light, meaning it has a single wavelength.
* To determine the wavelength of the He-Ne laser light, a single slit diffraction experiment can be performed.
* In this experiment, a single slit is placed in front of the laser light source and a screen is placed at a distance from the slit.
* When the laser light passes through the single slit, it diffracts and forms a pattern on the screen which is known as the diffraction pattern.
* The wavelength of the laser light can then be determined by measuring the distance between the two consecutive bright spots in the diffraction pattern.
