
## List of Experiments 

1. The Boyle-Mariotte Law: This law states that the pressure of a given mass of gas is inversely proportional to its volume at a constant temperature. This law can be tested by measuring the pressure of a gas at different volumes.

2. Charles' Law: This law states that the volume of a given mass of gas is directly proportional to its temperature at a constant pressure. This law can be tested by measuring the volume of a gas at different temperatures.

3. Avogadro's Law: This law states that the volume of a gas is directly proportional to the number of moles of gas present at a constant temperature and pressure. This law can be tested by measuring the volume of a gas at different molar amounts.

4. Graham's Law of Diffusion: This law states that the rate of diffusion of a gas is inversely proportional to the square root of its molecular mass. This law can be tested by measuring the rate of diffusion of different gases.

5. Dalton's Law of Partial Pressures: This law states that the total pressure of a mixture of gases is equal to the sum of the partial pressures of each individual gas. This law can be tested by measuring the pressure of a gas mixture at different temperatures.
