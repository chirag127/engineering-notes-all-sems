
##### 5. To determine resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance

1. Resistance per unit length (ρ) is defined as the resistance of a conductor of unit length. It is measured in ohms per meter (Ω/m).
2. Specific Resistance (ρ) is the resistance of a unit cube of the material at a specified temperature. It is measured in ohm-meters (Ω-m).
3. The resistance of a given conductor can be calculated using the formula: R = ρL, where R is the resistance of the conductor, ρ is the resistance per unit length, and L is the length of the conductor.
4. The specific resistance of a given conductor can be calculated using the formula: ρ = R/L, where R is the resistance of the conductor, ρ is the specific resistance, and L is the length of the conductor.
5. The resistance of a conductor can also be determined by measuring its resistance at a given temperature using an ohmmeter.
