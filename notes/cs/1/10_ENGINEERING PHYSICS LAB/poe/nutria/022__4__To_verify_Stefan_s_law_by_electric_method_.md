

##### 4. To verify Stefan’s law by electric method

1. Stefan's law states that the total energy radiated per unit surface area of a black body in thermal equilibrium is directly proportional to the fourth power of its absolute temperature.
1. Electric method is one of the methods used to verify Stefan's law.
1. In this method, a black body is placed in a cavity and its temperature is varied by an electric heater.
1. The radiation emitted by the black body is measured by a bolometer.
1. The bolometer measures the temperature of the black body by measuring the intensity of the radiation emitted.
1. The temperature of the black body is then plotted against the intensity of the radiation emitted and a graph is obtained.
1. The graph obtained should be a straight line, which shows that the intensity of the radiation is directly proportional to the fourth power of the absolute temperature of the black body.
1. Thus, Stefan's law is verified by the electric method.
