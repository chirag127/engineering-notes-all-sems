
##### 8. To calibrate the given ammeter and voltmeter by potentiometer

1. Connect the given ammeter and voltmeter in series with the potentiometer.
2. Adjust the resistance of the potentiometer to get the desired readings in the ammeter and voltmeter.
3. The resistance of the potentiometer must be adjusted to get the readings of the ammeter and voltmeter as close as possible to the known values.
4. Record the readings of the ammeter and voltmeter for each position of the potentiometer.
5. Repeat the above steps for different known values of the ammeter and voltmeter.
6. Plot the graph of the readings of the ammeter and voltmeter against the known values.
7. If the graph is linear and passes through the origin, then the ammeter and voltmeter are calibrated.
