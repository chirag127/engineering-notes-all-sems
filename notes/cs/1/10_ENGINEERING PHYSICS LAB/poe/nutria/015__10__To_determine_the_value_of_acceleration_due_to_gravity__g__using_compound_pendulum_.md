
##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum

1. A compound pendulum is a physical pendulum consisting of a rigid body suspended by a single string or rod.
2. The acceleration due to gravity (g) can be determined by measuring the time period of the compound pendulum.
3. The time period of a compound pendulum is given by the equation: 
T = 2π√(I/mgL)
where T is the time period, I is the moment of inertia, m is the mass of the body, g is the acceleration due to gravity and L is the length of the string.
4. To determine the value of g, the moment of inertia, mass and length of the string have to be measured.
5. The moment of inertia of the body can be determined by the following equation:
I = mr²
where r is the radius of the body.
6. The mass of the body can be determined by using a weighing machine.
7. The length of the string can be determined by using a ruler.
8. Once all the measurements have been taken, the value of g can be calculated using the equation:
g = 4π²L/T²I
9. Finally, the value of g can be compared with the accepted value of 9.8 m/s².
