

#### Apply

1. To apply means to make use of something, to put something into practice, or to make a formal request. 
2. In the context of an application, it can refer to the process of submitting a form or document to an organization, such as an application for a job or a loan. 
3. It can also refer to the act of using a tool or method to complete a task, such as applying a mathematical equation to solve a problem. 
4. Applying can also refer to the act of adhering to a set of rules or regulations, such as applying the law or following a code of conduct. 
5. In the context of education, applying is often used to refer to the process of submitting an application to a college or university. 
6. In the context of technology, applying can refer to the process of installing a software program or updating a system.
