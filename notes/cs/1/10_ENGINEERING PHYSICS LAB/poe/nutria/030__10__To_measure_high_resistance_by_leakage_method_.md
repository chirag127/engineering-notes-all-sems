
##### 10. To Measure High Resistance by Leakage Method
1. Leakage method is used to measure the resistance of a circuit or component that has a high resistance value.
2. The circuit is connected to an ohmmeter and a current source.
3. The current source is connected to the circuit, and the ohmmeter is connected in parallel with the circuit.
4. The current source supplies a constant current to the circuit, and the ohmmeter measures the voltage drop across the circuit.
5. The resistance of the circuit is calculated by dividing the voltage drop by the current supplied by the current source.
6. The resistance value obtained using the leakage method is usually accurate to within 1%.
7. This method is suitable for measuring resistances of greater than 1 MΩ.
