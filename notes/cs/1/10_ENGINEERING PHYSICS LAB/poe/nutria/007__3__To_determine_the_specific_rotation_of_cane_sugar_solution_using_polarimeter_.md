
##### 3. To determine the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using polarimeter

1. A polarimeter is an instrument used to measure the amount of rotation caused by a sample solution when polarized light is passed through it.
2. The specific rotation of a solution is the amount of rotation of polarized light caused by a sample solution when a certain concentration of the solution is placed in the polarimeter.
3. To measure the specific rotation of cane sugar solution, a sample of the solution is placed in the polarimeter and the angle of rotation is recorded.
4. The specific rotation of the solution is then calculated using the equation:
    
    α = [α]/c * l

where α is the angle of rotation, [α] is the specific rotation, c is the concentration of the solution and l is the length of the polarimeter tube.
5. The specific rotation of the sample solution can then be used to determine the optical activity of the sample.
