

#### Apply

1. To apply something means to make use of it. 
2. To apply a rule or law means to put it into practice. 
3. To apply a solution means to use it to solve a problem. 
4. To apply for a job means to submit a request for employment. 
5. To apply for a loan or grant means to submit a request for money. 
6. To apply a technique means to use it to achieve a desired result. 
7. To apply a process means to use it to produce a desired outcome. 
8. To apply a method means to use it to achieve a specific goal.
