
## Course Outcomes:
1. Develop an understanding of the fundamentals of computer science, including algorithms, data structures, and programming languages.
2. Develop the ability to design and implement software applications.
3. Develop the ability to analyze and evaluate software systems.
4. Develop the ability to communicate effectively in both written and oral forms.
5. Develop the ability to work collaboratively in teams.
6. Develop an understanding of professional, ethical, legal, security, and social issues related to computer science.
