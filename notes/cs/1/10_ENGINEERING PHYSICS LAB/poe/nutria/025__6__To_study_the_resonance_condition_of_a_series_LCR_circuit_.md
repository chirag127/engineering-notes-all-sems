

##### 6. To Study the Resonance Condition of a Series LCR Circuit

1. A series LCR circuit is a combination of a resistor, an inductor, and a capacitor connected in a series.

2. The resonance condition of a series LCR circuit occurs when the capacitive reactance and the inductive reactance are equal.

3. At resonance, the total impedance of the circuit is equal to the resistance of the resistor.

4. The resonance frequency of a series LCR circuit is calculated by the formula: $$f_r = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}$$, where L is the inductance, and C is the capacitance.

5. The resonance frequency of a series LCR circuit can be adjusted by changing either the inductance or the capacitance.
