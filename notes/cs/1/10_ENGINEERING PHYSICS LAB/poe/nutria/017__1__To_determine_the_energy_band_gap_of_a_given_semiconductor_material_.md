

##### 1. To determine the energy band gap of a given semiconductor material

1. The energy band gap of a semiconductor material is the energy difference between the top of the valence band and the bottom of the conduction band. 
2. The energy band gap can be determined experimentally by measuring the absorption of light of different energy levels. 
3. The absorption of light of different energies can be measured by using photoluminescence spectroscopy, optical absorption spectroscopy, or electrical conductivity measurements. 
4. The energy band gap of a semiconductor material can also be determined theoretically by using band structure calculations. 
5. Band structure calculations are based on the quantum mechanical model of a solid and can predict the energy band gap of a semiconductor material.
