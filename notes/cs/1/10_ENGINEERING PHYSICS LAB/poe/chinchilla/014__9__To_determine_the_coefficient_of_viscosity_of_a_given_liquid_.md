##### 9. To determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid.

Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. The coefficient of viscosity is a parameter that indicates the magnitude of this resistance. The coefficient of viscosity is important in fields such as fluid mechanics and chemical engineering. Here are the steps to determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid:

1. Set up the experimental apparatus: The apparatus consists of a glass capillary tube, a stand, a stopwatch, and the liquid sample whose viscosity is to be measured.

2. Measure the dimensions of the capillary tube: The inside diameter and the length of the capillary tube are measured using a Vernier caliper.

3. Fill the capillary tube with the liquid sample: The capillary tube is completely filled with the liquid sample.

4. Mount the capillary tube vertically on the stand: The tube is mounted in such a way that it is vertical and the liquid sample is not in contact with the walls of the tube.

5. Measure the time taken by the liquid sample to flow through the capillary tube: The stopwatch is started as soon as the liquid sample starts flowing through the capillary tube. The time taken for the liquid sample to flow from the top to the bottom of the tube is measured.

6. Repeat the experiment: The experiment is repeated several times to get an average value of the time taken by the liquid sample to flow through the capillary tube.
