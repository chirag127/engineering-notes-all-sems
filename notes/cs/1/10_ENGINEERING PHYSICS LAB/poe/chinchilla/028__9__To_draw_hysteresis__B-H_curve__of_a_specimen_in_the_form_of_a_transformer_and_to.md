##### 9. To draw hysteresis (B-H curve) of a specimen in the form of a transformer and to.

In order to understand the magnetic properties of a material, it is important to analyze the hysteresis (B-H) curve. This can be done by performing a simple experiment with a specimen in the form of a transformer. The following points can guide you through the process of drawing the hysteresis curve:

1. Start by setting up the transformer experiment. The transformer consists of a primary coil and a secondary coil wound around a magnetic core. The primary coil is connected to a power source, while the secondary coil is connected to a voltmeter.

2. Next, apply an alternating current to the primary coil. This will cause the magnetic field in the core to alternate in direction.

3. As the magnetic field in the core changes, it will induce a voltage in the secondary coil. Record the voltage readings for different values of the applied current.

4. From the recorded voltage readings, calculate the magnetic field strength (H) in the core. This can be done using the formula H = N*I/L, where N is the number of turns in the coil, I is the current flowing through the coil, and L is the length of the core.

5. Plot the magnetic field strength (H) on the x-axis and the magnetic flux density (B) on the y-axis. The result will be a hysteresis (B-H) curve that shows the magnetic properties of the specimen.

6. Analyze the hysteresis curve to understand the magnetic properties of the specimen. The curve will show the relationship between the magnetic field strength and the magnetic flux density, as well as the hysteresis loop that represents the energy loss in the core.

7. Use the hysteresis curve to determine the magnetic properties of the specimen, such as its coercive force, magnetic saturation, and remanence.

8. Repeat the experiment with different specimens to compare their hysteresis curves and determine their magnetic properties.

9. Finally, make sure to properly label the axes and include a legend to clearly indicate the data points on the hysteresis curve.

By following these steps, you can successfully draw the hysteresis (B-H) curve of a specimen in the form of a transformer and analyze its magnetic properties. This information can be useful in a variety of fields, such as electrical engineering, materials science, and physics.
