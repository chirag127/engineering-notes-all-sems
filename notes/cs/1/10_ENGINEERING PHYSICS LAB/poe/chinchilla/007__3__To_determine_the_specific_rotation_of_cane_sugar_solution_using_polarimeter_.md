### To Determine the Specific Rotation of Cane Sugar Solution Using Polarimeter

In analytical chemistry, it is essential to determine the concentration and purity of a substance accurately. One of the methods to determine the purity of a substance is by measuring the specific rotation of a solution using a polarimeter. The specific rotation is the angle of rotation of the plane of polarized light caused by a given substance at a specific concentration and temperature. Here are the steps to determine the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using a polarimeter:

1. Calibration of the Polarimeter: 
   - Start by calibrating the polarimeter with a blank solution, which is optically inactive, such as distilled water or ethanol.
   - Place a sample tube filled with the blank solution in the polarimeter, and adjust the polarimeter to read zero degrees of rotation.
   - Repeat the process two more times to ensure the accuracy of the calibration.

2. Preparation of Cane Sugar Solution:
   - Prepare a 10% w/v solution of cane sugar by dissolving 10 g of cane sugar in 100 mL of distilled water.
   - Stir the solution until the sugar is completely dissolved.
   - Filter the solution to remove any impurities.

3. Measurement of Specific Rotation:
   - Fill a sample tube with the prepared cane sugar solution up to the marked line.
   - Place the filled sample tube in the polarimeter and read the angle of rotation.
   - Repeat the measurement two more times and take the average of the readings.
   - Calculate the specific rotation using the formula: 
     **α = α_observed / (l x c)**
     where α is the specific rotation, α_observed is the observed angle of rotation, l is the length of the sample tube in decimeters, and c is the concentration of the solution in g/mL.

4. Interpretation of Results:
   - Compare the obtained specific rotation value with the literature value of cane sugar.
   - If the observed value is within the acceptable range of the literature value, then the cane sugar solution is considered pure.
   - If the observed value deviates significantly from the literature value, then further purification or analysis of the solution is required.

In conclusion, the specific rotation of a solution is a crucial parameter that helps to determine the purity and concentration of a substance accurately. By following the above steps, one can determine the specific rotation of a cane sugar solution using a polarimeter.
