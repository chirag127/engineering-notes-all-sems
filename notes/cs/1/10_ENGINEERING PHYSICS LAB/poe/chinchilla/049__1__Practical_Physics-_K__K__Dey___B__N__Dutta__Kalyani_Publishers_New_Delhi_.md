### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

Practical Physics is a crucial aspect of physics education, as it provides students with hands-on experience of the concepts they learn in theory. K.K. Dey and B.N. Dutta's book "Practical Physics" is an excellent resource for students who want to learn about experimental physics. Here are some important points from the book:

1. The book covers a wide range of experiments in various branches of physics, such as mechanics, optics, electricity and magnetism, and modern physics.

2. It provides detailed instructions on how to set up and perform each experiment, as well as how to analyze and interpret the results.

3. The book also includes a chapter on error analysis, which is essential for understanding the limitations of experimental measurements.

4. The authors have included a large number of diagrams and illustrations to help students visualize the experiments and understand the underlying principles.

5. Practical Physics also includes a section on laboratory safety, which is crucial for students to follow to avoid accidents and injuries.

6. The book provides a list of common laboratory equipment and their uses, which is helpful for students who are new to experimental physics.

7. Each chapter includes a set of review questions and problems, which test the students' understanding of the concepts and help them prepare for exams.

8. The book also includes a section on data analysis and presentation, which is useful for students who want to learn how to present their experimental results in a clear and concise manner.

In conclusion, Practical Physics by K.K. Dey and B.N. Dutta is an excellent resource for students who want to learn about experimental physics. The book provides detailed instructions on how to perform various experiments, as well as how to analyze and interpret the results. With its numerous diagrams, illustrations, and review questions, the book is an ideal companion for students who want to excel in their physics experiments and exams.
