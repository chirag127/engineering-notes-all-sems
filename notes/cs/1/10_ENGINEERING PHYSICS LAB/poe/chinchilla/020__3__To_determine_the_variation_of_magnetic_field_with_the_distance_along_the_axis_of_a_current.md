### 3. To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current

Magnetic fields are an important aspect of electromagnetism and are essential in many aspects of modern technology. Understanding how the magnetic field varies with distance along the axis of a current is important in the study of electromagnetism. In this topic, we will discuss the methods used to determine the variation of magnetic field with distance along the axis of a current.

#### Introduction

Before we begin, let us first understand what we mean by the magnetic field. The magnetic field is a vector quantity that describes the magnetic influence on a charged particle. It is represented by the symbol B and is measured in units of tesla (T).

#### Magnetic field due to a current-carrying wire

A current-carrying wire produces a magnetic field around it. The direction of the magnetic field can be determined using the right-hand rule. The strength of the magnetic field depends on the current flowing through the wire and the distance from the wire.

#### Calculation of magnetic field along the axis of a current-carrying wire

To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current, we can use the Biot-Savart law. The Biot-Savart law is a mathematical expression that relates the magnetic field at a point to the current flowing through a wire and the distance from the wire.

The Biot-Savart law can be expressed as:

$$ \mathbf{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \int_C \frac{I\, d\boldsymbol{\ell} \times \mathbf{r}}{r^3} $$

where B is the magnetic field at a point, I is the current flowing through a wire, dℓ is a small element of the wire, r is the distance from the wire to the point, and μ0 is the permeability of free space.

Using this equation, we can calculate the magnetic field at any point along the axis of a current-carrying wire.

#### Experimental setup

To determine the variation of magnetic field with distance along the axis of a current, we can set up an experimental apparatus as follows:

- A wire carrying a current is placed on a stand.
- A magnetic field sensor is placed at various distances along the axis of the wire.
- The magnetic field sensor is connected to a data logger to record the magnetic field readings.
- The current flowing through the wire is varied, and the magnetic field readings are recorded at each distance.

#### Data analysis

After the experiment is conducted, the data collected can be plotted as a graph of magnetic field strength against distance along the axis of the wire. The graph will show how the magnetic field varies with distance along the axis of the wire.

#### Conclusion

In conclusion, the variation of magnetic field with distance along the axis of a current can be determined using the Biot-Savart law. By setting up an experimental apparatus and recording the magnetic field readings at various distances along the axis of a current-carrying wire, we can determine how the magnetic field varies with distance. Understanding this variation is important in the study of electromagnetism and has many practical applications.
