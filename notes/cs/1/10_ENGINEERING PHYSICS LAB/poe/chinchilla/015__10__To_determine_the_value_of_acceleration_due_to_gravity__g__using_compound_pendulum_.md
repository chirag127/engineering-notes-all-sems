##### Introduction

A compound pendulum is a physical system that consists of a rigid body suspended from a pivot point, which is not located at the center of mass of the body. The motion of the compound pendulum is governed by the force of gravity acting on the body, and the value of the acceleration due to gravity (g) can be determined experimentally using this system. In this guide, we will discuss the steps involved in determining the value of acceleration due to gravity using a compound pendulum.

##### Materials Required

- Compound pendulum apparatus
- Stop watch or timer
- Measuring tape or ruler
- Weights or masses
- Clamp stand
- Spirit level

##### Procedure

1. Set up the compound pendulum apparatus by suspending the rigid body from the pivot point using a thread or wire.
2. Ensure that the pendulum is suspended vertically and the pivot point is at the center of the clamp stand.
3. Use a spirit level to ensure that the pendulum is hanging vertically.
4. Measure the length of the pendulum from the pivot point to the center of mass of the body using a measuring tape or ruler.
5. Record the length of the pendulum as L.
6. Attach a small weight or mass to the bottom of the pendulum.
7. Displace the pendulum through a small angle (less than 10 degrees) and release it.
8. Use a stop watch or timer to measure the time period of the pendulum's oscillation.
9. Repeat the above steps for different masses attached to the pendulum.
10. Record the time period T and the corresponding mass m for each trial.
11. Calculate the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using the following formula:

   `g = 4π^2L/T^2`

- Tabulate the values of time period T and mass m for each trial.
- Calculate the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) for each trial using the above formula.
- Compute the mean value of g from the individual values obtained in each trial.

##### Discussion

- The value of acceleration due to gravity (g) obtained through this experiment should be close to the standard value of 9.81 m/s^2.
- The accuracy of the experiment can be improved by repeating the trials multiple times and taking the average value of g.
- The measured value of g may be affected by environmental factors such as air resistance and temperature, so it is important to conduct the experiment in a controlled environment.

##### Conclusion

The value of acceleration due to gravity (g) can be determined experimentally using a compound pendulum.
