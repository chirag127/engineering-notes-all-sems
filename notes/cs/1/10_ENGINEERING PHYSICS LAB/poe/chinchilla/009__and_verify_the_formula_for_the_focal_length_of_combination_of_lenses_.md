##### Formula for the Focal Length of Combination of Lenses

When two or more lenses are combined, they form what is known as a lens system. In such a system, light passes through each lens and is refracted, resulting in an overall image formation. To determine the focal length of a lens system, we use the following formula:

1/f = (1/f_1) + (1/f_2) + ... + (1/f_n)

Where:
- f is the focal length of the lens system
- f_1, f_2, ..., f_n are the focal lengths of the individual lenses in the system

It is important to note that this formula only applies to thin lenses, which are lenses that are much thinner than their focal length. Additionally, the formula assumes that the lenses are positioned close to each other, so that the distance between them is negligible.

To use the above formula, follow these steps:

1. Determine the focal length of each individual lens in the system.
2. Substitute the values of f_1, f_2, ..., f_n into the formula.
3. Solve for f, which will give you the focal length of the lens system.

1/s + 1/s' = 1/f

Where:
- s is the distance between the object and the lens system
- s' is the distance between the image and the lens system

By rearranging this formula, you can solve for s' or s, depending on which value you want to find.
