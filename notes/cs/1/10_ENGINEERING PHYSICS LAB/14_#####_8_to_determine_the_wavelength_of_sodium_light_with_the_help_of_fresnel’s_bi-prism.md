##### 8. To determine the wavelength of sodium light with the help of Fresnel’s bi-prism.

Fresnel's bi-prism is a device used to determine the wavelength of light by observing the interference pattern created by the light passing through the prism. The principle behind this experiment is that light of different wavelengths will produce different interference patterns. To determine the wavelength of sodium light, the following steps can be followed:

1. Obtain a Fresnel bi-prism and a light source that emits sodium light.

2. Place the bi-prism in front of the light source and adjust the position of the light source so that the light passes through the bi-prism.

3. Observe the interference pattern created by the light passing through the bi-prism.

4. Measure the distance between the bright fringes and the dark fringes in the interference pattern.

5. Use the formula λ = d/n, where λ is the wavelength of light, d is the distance between the bright fringes, and n is the number of fringes, to calculate the wavelength of the sodium light.

6. Repeat the experiment several times to obtain an average value for the wavelength.

7. Compare the average value obtained with the known value for the wavelength of sodium light to determine the accuracy of the experiment.

8. If necessary, make adjustments to the experiment and repeat the steps to obtain a more accurate result.
