##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum.

The compound pendulum is a type of pendulum that consists of a rigid body suspended from a pivot point. The value of acceleration due to gravity (g) can be determined using a compound pendulum by measuring the period of oscillation of the pendulum and using the formula for the period of a compound pendulum.

1. Experiment setup: The experiment involves suspending a rigid body from a pivot point and measuring the time it takes for the pendulum to complete one full oscillation. The length of the pendulum and the mass of the rigid body can also be measured.

2. Calculation of period: The period of the pendulum can be calculated using the formula T = 2π √(l/g), where T is the period, l is the length of the pendulum, and g is the acceleration due to gravity. By measuring the period of the pendulum and the length of the pendulum, the value of g can be determined.

Factors affecting accuracy: The accuracy of the measurement of g using a compound pendulum can be affected by several factors, including the length of the pendulum, the mass of the rigid body, and the temperature of the environment. It is important to control these factors as much as possible to ensure accurate results.

4. Limitations: The method of determining g using a compound pendulum is limited by the accuracy of the equipment used and the precision of the measurements. It is also limited by the assumption that the pendulum is a simple pendulum, which may not always be the case.

In conclusion, the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) can be determined using a compound pendulum by measuring the period of oscillation of the pendulum and using the formula for the period of a compound pendulum.
