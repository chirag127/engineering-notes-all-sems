##### 4. To verify Stefan’s law by electric method.

To verify Stefan's law using the electric method, you need to measure the temperature of a black body radiator and the amount of electrical power it emits. Stefan's law states that the total power emitted by a black body radiator is proportional to the fourth power of its temperature.

To perform the experiment, you would need the following equipment:
- Black body radiator
- Power meter
- Thermometer
- Electrical heating element
- Insulating material

Steps:
1. Place the black body radiator inside an insulated enclosure to prevent heat loss.
2. Heat the black body radiator using the electrical heating element.
3. Measure the temperature of the black body radiator using the thermometer.
4. Measure the electrical power emitted by the black body radiator using the power meter.
5. Repeat steps 2 to 4 for different temperatures of the black body radiator.
6. Plot the electrical power emitted versus the fourth power of the temperature.
7. Compare the plot with the theoretical curve predicted by Stefan's law.

If the plot agrees with the theoretical curve, then Stefan's law is verified.
