### 2. Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical by Katiyar & Pandey is a textbook that covers the fundamental principles of physics and their application in engineering. It provides a comprehensive overview of the subjects, including mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, and optics. The book is designed for students pursuing engineering degrees, and it covers both the theoretical and practical aspects of the subjects. The authors use clear explanations, examples, and illustrations to help students understand complex concepts. The book also includes a large number of problems and solutions to help students practice and reinforce their understanding of the material. It is published by Wiley India and is widely used as a reference and study guide by engineering students.
