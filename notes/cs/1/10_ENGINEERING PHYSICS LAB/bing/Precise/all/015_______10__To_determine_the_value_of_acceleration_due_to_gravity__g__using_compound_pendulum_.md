##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum.

The compound pendulum is a rigid body swinging in a vertical plane about a horizontal axis passing through the body. The time period of a compound pendulum is given by the formula T = 2π√(I/mgd), where I is the moment of inertia of the pendulum about the axis of rotation, m is the mass of the pendulum, g is the acceleration due to gravity, and d is the distance between the center of mass of the pendulum and the axis of rotation.

To determine the value of g using a compound pendulum, the following steps can be followed:

1. Suspend the compound pendulum from a rigid support and set it into oscillation.
2. Measure the time period of the pendulum for a given number of oscillations using a stopwatch.
3. Calculate the average time period of the pendulum.
4. Measure the mass of the pendulum using a balance.
5. Determine the distance between the center of mass of the pendulum and the axis of rotation.
6. Calculate the moment of inertia of the pendulum about the axis of rotation using the parallel axis theorem.
7. Substitute the values of the time period, mass, distance, and moment of inertia in the formula T = 2π√(I/mgd) and solve for g.

This experiment can be repeated with different pendulums to obtain more accurate results. The value of g obtained from this experiment can be compared with the standard value of g to check the accuracy of the experiment.
