##### 8. To calibrate the given ammeter and voltmeter by potentiometer.

Calibration is the process of comparing the readings of an instrument with a known standard to determine the accuracy of the instrument. In this experiment, we will calibrate an ammeter and a voltmeter using a potentiometer.

The following steps can be followed to calibrate the given ammeter and voltmeter by potentiometer:

1. Set up the circuit as shown in the diagram, with the ammeter and voltmeter connected in series with the potentiometer and a battery.
2. Adjust the potentiometer to obtain a known current through the circuit.
3. Record the reading of the ammeter and compare it with the known current.
4. If the ammeter reading is not equal to the known current, adjust the zero error of the ammeter accordingly.
5. Repeat the above steps for different values of current to obtain a calibration curve for the ammeter.
6. Similarly, adjust the potentiometer to obtain a known voltage across the voltmeter.
7. Record the reading of the voltmeter and compare it with the known voltage.
8. If the voltmeter reading is not equal to the known voltage, adjust the zero error of the voltmeter accordingly.
9. Repeat the above steps for different values of voltage to obtain a calibration curve for the voltmeter.

By following the above steps, the given ammeter and voltmeter can be calibrated using a potentiometer. It is important to calibrate these instruments to ensure accurate measurements in experiments.
