##### 4. To verify Stefan’s law by electric method

Stefan's Law states that the total energy radiated per unit surface area of a black body across all wavelengths per unit time is directly proportional to the fourth power of the black body's thermodynamic temperature.

To verify Stefan's Law by electric method, the following steps can be followed:

1. Set up the apparatus, which includes a Leslie cube, a thermopile, a galvanometer, and a low voltage power supply.
2. Fill the Leslie cube with boiling water and place it on a heatproof mat.
3. Connect the thermopile to the galvanometer and the low voltage power supply.
4. Place the thermopile close to one of the faces of the Leslie cube.
5. Record the deflection of the galvanometer.
6. Repeat the experiment with the thermopile at different distances from the Leslie cube.
7. Plot a graph of the galvanometer deflection against the inverse square of the distance between the thermopile and the Leslie cube.
8. The graph should be a straight line, verifying Stefan's Law.
