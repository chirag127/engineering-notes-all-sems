##### 3. To determine the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using polarimeter.

A polarimeter is an instrument used to measure the angle of rotation of plane-polarized light as it passes through an optically active substance. The specific rotation of a substance is defined as the angle of rotation of plane-polarized light per unit path length and per unit concentration of the substance.

To determine the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using a polarimeter, the following steps can be followed:

1. Prepare a solution of known concentration of cane sugar by dissolving a known mass of cane sugar in a known volume of water.
2. Fill a polarimeter tube with the cane sugar solution and place it in the polarimeter.
3. Adjust the analyzer of the polarimeter until the field of view is dark.
4. Record the angle of rotation shown on the polarimeter scale.
5. Repeat the above steps for different concentrations of cane sugar solution.
6. Calculate the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using the formula: specific rotation = angle of rotation / (path length x concentration).

By following these steps, the specific rotation of cane sugar solution can be determined using a polarimeter. This value can be used to determine the concentration of cane sugar in unknown solutions by measuring their angle of rotation and using the specific rotation to calculate their concentration.
