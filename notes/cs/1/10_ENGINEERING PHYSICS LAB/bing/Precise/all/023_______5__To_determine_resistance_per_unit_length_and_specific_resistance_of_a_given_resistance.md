##### 5. To determine resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance

1. Resistance per unit length is the resistance of a conductor divided by its length. It is usually measured in ohms per meter (Ω/m).
2. Specific resistance, also known as resistivity, is a measure of the intrinsic resistance of a material. It is usually measured in ohm-meters (Ω·m).
3. To determine the resistance per unit length of a given resistance, the resistance of the conductor is measured using an ohmmeter and then divided by its length.
4. To determine the specific resistance of a given resistance, the resistance per unit length is multiplied by the cross-sectional area of the conductor.
5. The specific resistance of a material is dependent on its temperature, so it is important to take this into account when making measurements.
6. The specific resistance of a material can also be determined using the formula ρ = RA/l, where ρ is the specific resistance, R is the resistance of the conductor, A is the cross-sectional area of the conductor, and l is the length of the conductor.
7. It is important to use accurate and precise measuring instruments when determining the resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance to ensure accurate results.
