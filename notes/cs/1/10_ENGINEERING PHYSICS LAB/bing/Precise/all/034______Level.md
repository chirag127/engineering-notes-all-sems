#### Level

- A level is a tool used to determine if a surface is horizontal or vertical.
- It consists of a small glass tube filled with liquid, with an air bubble inside.
- When the level is placed on a surface, the bubble will move to the highest point of the tube.
- If the bubble is centered between two marks on the tube, the surface is level.
- Levels come in various sizes and shapes, including spirit levels, laser levels, and digital levels.
- They are commonly used in construction, carpentry, and surveying.
- Levels can also be used to hang pictures or shelves, or to level appliances such as washing machines or refrigerators.
- It is important to use a level when installing floors, cabinets, or countertops to ensure that they are even and stable.
- A level can also be used to check the slope of a surface, such as a driveway or a patio, to ensure proper drainage.
- Using a level can help to improve the accuracy and quality of a project.
