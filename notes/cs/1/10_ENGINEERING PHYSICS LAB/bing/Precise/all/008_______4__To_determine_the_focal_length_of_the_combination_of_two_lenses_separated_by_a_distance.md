##### 4. To determine the focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance

The focal length of the combination of two lenses separated by a distance can be determined using the formula:

1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/f1f2

Where:
- f is the focal length of the combination of the two lenses
- f1 is the focal length of the first lens
- f2 is the focal length of the second lens
- d is the distance between the two lenses

To determine the focal length of the combination of the two lenses, the following steps can be followed:
1. Measure the focal length of the first lens (f1) using a suitable method.
2. Measure the focal length of the second lens (f2) using a suitable method.
3. Measure the distance between the two lenses (d).
4. Substitute the values of f1, f2, and d into the formula above to calculate the focal length of the combination of the two lenses (f).

It is important to note that the formula above is only valid when the two lenses are thin and the distance between them is small compared to their focal lengths. If the lenses are thick or the distance between them is large, more complex calculations may be required to determine the focal length of the combination of the two lenses.
