### 2. Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

- "Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical" is a book written by Katiyar & Pandey and published by Wiley India.
- The book covers the fundamental concepts of physics and their applications in engineering.
- It is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the subject for students pursuing engineering courses.
- The book is divided into two parts: theory and practical.
- The theory part covers topics such as mechanics, waves and oscillations, optics, electromagnetism, and modern physics.
- The practical part includes experiments and activities that help students understand the concepts discussed in the theory part.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for students to understand the subject.
- It includes numerous examples, illustrations, and exercises to help students learn and practice the concepts.
- Overall, "Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical" by Katiyar & Pandey is a valuable resource for students studying engineering physics.
