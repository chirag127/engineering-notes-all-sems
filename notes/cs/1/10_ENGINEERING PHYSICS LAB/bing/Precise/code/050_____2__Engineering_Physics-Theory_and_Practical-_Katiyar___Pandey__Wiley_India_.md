### 2. Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

- Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical is a book written by Katiyar & Pandey and published by Wiley India.
- The book covers the fundamental concepts of physics and their applications in engineering.
- It is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the subject to engineering students.
- The book is divided into two parts: Theory and Practical.
- The Theory part covers topics such as mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, optics, and modern physics.
- The Practical part includes experiments and practical applications of the concepts covered in the Theory part.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for students to understand and learn from.
- It is a valuable resource for engineering students preparing for exams or seeking to deepen their understanding of physics and its applications in engineering.
