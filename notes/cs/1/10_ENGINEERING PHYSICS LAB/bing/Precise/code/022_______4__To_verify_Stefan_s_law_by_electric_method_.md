##### 4. To verify Stefan’s law by electric method.

Stefan's law states that the total energy radiated per unit surface area of a black body across all wavelengths per unit time is directly proportional to the fourth power of the black body's thermodynamic temperature.

To verify Stefan's law by electric method, the following steps can be followed:

1. Set up the apparatus, which includes a Leslie's cube, a thermopile, a galvanometer, and a low voltage power supply.
2. Fill the Leslie's cube with boiling water and place it in front of the thermopile.
3. Connect the thermopile to the galvanometer and the low voltage power supply.
4. Observe the deflection of the galvanometer needle and record the readings.
5. Repeat the experiment with different temperatures of water in the Leslie's cube.
6. Plot a graph of the galvanometer deflection against the temperature of the water in the Leslie's cube.
7. The graph should be a straight line, indicating that the total energy radiated is directly proportional to the fourth power of the temperature, thus verifying Stefan's law.
