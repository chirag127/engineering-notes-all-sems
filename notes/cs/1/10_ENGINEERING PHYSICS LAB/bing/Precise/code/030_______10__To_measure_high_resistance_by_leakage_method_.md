##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

The leakage method is used to measure high resistance values. This method is based on the principle of measuring the leakage current flowing through the insulation resistance. The following are the steps involved in measuring high resistance by the leakage method:

1. The first step is to connect the high resistance to be measured in series with a low resistance of known value.
2. A voltage source is then applied across the series combination of the high and low resistances.
3. The voltage drop across the low resistance is measured using a voltmeter.
4. The leakage current flowing through the high resistance can be calculated using Ohm's law, by dividing the voltage drop across the low resistance by its resistance value.
5. The value of the high resistance can then be calculated by dividing the applied voltage by the calculated leakage current.

This method is commonly used to measure the insulation resistance of electrical equipment and cables. It is important to note that the accuracy of the measurement depends on the accuracy of the low resistance and the voltmeter used in the measurement. It is also important to ensure that the applied voltage is constant during the measurement.
