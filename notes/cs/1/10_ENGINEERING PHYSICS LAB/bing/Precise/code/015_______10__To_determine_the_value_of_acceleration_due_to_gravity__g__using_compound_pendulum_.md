##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum.

1. The compound pendulum is a rigid body swinging in a vertical plane about a horizontal axis passing through the body.
2. The time period of a compound pendulum is given by the formula T = 2π √(I/mgd), where I is the moment of inertia of the pendulum about the axis of rotation, m is the mass of the pendulum, g is the acceleration due to gravity, and d is the distance between the center of mass of the pendulum and the axis of rotation.
3. To determine the value of g using a compound pendulum, the time period of the pendulum is measured for different values of d.
4. A graph is plotted between T^2 and d, and the slope of the graph is used to calculate the value of g.
5. The value of g can be calculated using the formula g = 4π^2I/(m*slope).
6. The experiment should be repeated for different values of d to obtain an accurate value of g.
7. The value of g obtained from the experiment can be compared with the standard value of g to check the accuracy of the experiment.
8. Precautions should be taken while performing the experiment, such as ensuring that the pendulum swings in a vertical plane and that the amplitude of oscillation is small.
9. The experiment should be performed in a controlled environment to minimize the effect of external factors such as air resistance.
10. The compound pendulum method is a simple and accurate method to determine the value of g. It is widely used in laboratories and educational institutions.
