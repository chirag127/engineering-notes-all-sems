##### 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

1. The experiment is performed using a He-Ne laser, a single slit, and a screen.
2. The He-Ne laser emits a beam of light that passes through the single slit.
3. The light diffracts as it passes through the slit and forms an interference pattern on the screen.
4. The distance between the central maximum and the first minimum of the interference pattern is measured.
5. The wavelength of the laser light can be calculated using the formula: λ = ax / L, where λ is the wavelength, a is the width of the slit, x is the distance between the central maximum and the first minimum, and L is the distance between the slit and the screen.
6. The experiment can be repeated with different slit widths to obtain more accurate results.
7. The calculated wavelength can be compared to the known wavelength of He-Ne laser light to verify the accuracy of the experiment.
