### 3. Engineering Physics Practical- S K Gupta ( Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- Engineering Physics Practical is a book written by S K Gupta and published by Krishna Prakashan Meerut.
- The book is intended for students studying engineering and covers practical aspects of physics.
- It includes experiments and practical applications of physics concepts relevant to engineering.
- The book is designed to help students develop practical skills and gain hands-on experience in applying physics to engineering problems.
- It is a valuable resource for students preparing for exams and for those who want to deepen their understanding of physics in an engineering context.
