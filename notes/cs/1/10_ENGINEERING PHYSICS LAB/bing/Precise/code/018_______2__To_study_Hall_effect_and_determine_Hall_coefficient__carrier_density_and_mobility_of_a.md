##### 2. To study Hall effect and determine Hall coefficient, carrier density and mobility of a given semiconductor material

The Hall effect is the production of a voltage difference across an electrical conductor, transverse to an electric current in the conductor and to an applied magnetic field perpendicular to the current. It was discovered by Edwin Hall in 1879.

The Hall effect can be used to determine the Hall coefficient, carrier density, and mobility of a given semiconductor material. These properties are important for understanding the electrical behavior of the material.

To determine these properties, the following steps are typically followed:

1. A sample of the semiconductor material is prepared in the form of a thin rectangular plate.
2. The sample is placed in a magnetic field perpendicular to the plate.
3. A current is passed through the sample in a direction perpendicular to both the magnetic field and the plate.
4. The voltage difference between the two sides of the plate is measured.
5. The Hall coefficient, carrier density, and mobility can be calculated from the measured voltage difference, current, magnetic field strength, and sample dimensions.

The Hall coefficient is a measure of the strength of the Hall effect in a material. It is defined as the ratio of the induced electric field to the product of the current density and the magnetic field strength.

The carrier density is the number of charge carriers (electrons or holes) per unit volume in the material. It can be calculated from the Hall coefficient and the elementary charge.

The mobility is a measure of how easily the charge carriers can move through the material. It can be calculated from the Hall coefficient, the carrier density, and the conductivity of the material.

In summary, the Hall effect can be used to determine important electrical properties of semiconductor materials, including the Hall coefficient, carrier density, and mobility. These properties are important for understanding the behavior of the material in electronic devices.
