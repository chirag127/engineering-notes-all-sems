##### Hysteresis Loss

Hysteresis loss is the energy loss that occurs in a magnetic material due to the reversal of magnetization. It is a type of energy loss that occurs in ferromagnetic materials when they are subjected to a changing magnetic field. The energy loss is due to the movement of the magnetic domains within the material.

To determine the hysteresis loss, the following steps can be followed:

1. Measure the area of the hysteresis loop: The hysteresis loop is a graph that shows the relationship between the magnetic field strength and the magnetization of the material. The area of the hysteresis loop represents the energy loss per cycle.

2. Calculate the frequency of the magnetic field: The frequency of the magnetic field is the number of cycles per second. It can be calculated by measuring the time it takes for one complete cycle of the magnetic field.

3. Multiply the area of the hysteresis loop by the frequency of the magnetic field: The product of the area of the hysteresis loop and the frequency of the magnetic field gives the hysteresis loss per second.

4. Multiply the hysteresis loss per second by the time: To determine the total hysteresis loss, the hysteresis loss per second is multiplied by the time over which the magnetic field is applied.

In summary, to determine the hysteresis loss, the area of the hysteresis loop is measured, the frequency of the magnetic field is calculated, and the hysteresis loss per second is determined by multiplying the area of the hysteresis loop by the frequency of the magnetic field. The total hysteresis loss is then calculated by multiplying the hysteresis loss per second by the time over which the magnetic field is applied.
