##### 3. To determine the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using polarimeter.

A polarimeter is an instrument used to measure the angle of rotation of plane-polarized light as it passes through a sample of a substance. The specific rotation of a substance is defined as the observed angle of rotation, α, divided by the path length, l, in decimeters, and the concentration, c, in grams per milliliter.

The specific rotation of a substance can be calculated using the formula: 

`[α]λT = α / (l * c)`

Where:
- `[α]λT` is the specific rotation of the substance at a given wavelength, λ, and temperature, T.
- `α` is the observed angle of rotation.
- `l` is the path length in decimeters.
- `c` is the concentration in grams per milliliter.

To determine the specific rotation of a cane sugar solution using a polarimeter, the following steps can be followed:

1. Prepare a solution of cane sugar with a known concentration.
2. Fill a polarimeter tube with the cane sugar solution and place it in the polarimeter.
3. Adjust the polarimeter to the desired wavelength and temperature.
4. Measure the angle of rotation of the plane-polarized light as it passes through the cane sugar solution.
5. Use the formula above to calculate the specific rotation of the cane sugar solution.

It is important to note that the specific rotation of a substance can vary with changes in wavelength and temperature. Therefore, it is important to specify the wavelength and temperature at which the specific rotation was determined.
