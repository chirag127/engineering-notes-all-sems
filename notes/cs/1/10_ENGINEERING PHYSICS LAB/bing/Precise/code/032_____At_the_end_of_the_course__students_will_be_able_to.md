### At the end of the course, students will be able to:
- Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the subject matter.
- Apply the concepts and theories learned in the course to real-world situations.
- Analyze and evaluate information critically and effectively.
- Communicate their ideas and arguments clearly and effectively.
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
- Demonstrate ethical and professional behavior.
- Develop skills for lifelong learning and personal growth.
