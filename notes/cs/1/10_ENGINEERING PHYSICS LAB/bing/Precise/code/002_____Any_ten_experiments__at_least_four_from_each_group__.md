### Any ten experiments (at least four from each group)

Here are ten experiments, with at least four from each group:

#### Group 1: Physics
1. **The Double-Slit Experiment**: This experiment demonstrates the wave-particle duality of light and matter.
2. **The Michelson-Morley Experiment**: This experiment was designed to detect the presence of the hypothetical luminiferous aether, a medium thought to be necessary for the propagation of light.
3. **The Millikan Oil Drop Experiment**: This experiment measured the charge of the electron.
4. **The Rutherford Gold Foil Experiment**: This experiment demonstrated the existence of the atomic nucleus.

#### Group 2: Chemistry
1. **The Iodine Clock Reaction**: This experiment demonstrates the concepts of reaction rates and chemical kinetics.
2. **The Briggs-Rauscher Oscillating Reaction**: This experiment demonstrates the concepts of oscillating reactions and chemical kinetics.
3. **The Belousov-Zhabotinsky Reaction**: This experiment demonstrates the concepts of oscillating reactions and chemical kinetics.
4. **The Thermite Reaction**: This experiment demonstrates the concepts of exothermic reactions and redox reactions.

#### Group 3: Biology
1. **The Hershey-Chase Experiment**: This experiment demonstrated that DNA, not protein, is the genetic material of life.
2. **The Meselson-Stahl Experiment**: This experiment demonstrated that DNA replication is semi-conservative.
