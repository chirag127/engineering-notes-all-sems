#### Apply

1. The term "apply" can refer to the act of putting something into operation or use, such as applying a rule or principle.
2. It can also refer to the act of making a formal request or submission, such as applying for a job or a loan.
3. In the context of mathematics or science, "apply" can refer to the use of a formula, theorem, or principle to solve a problem or explain a phenomenon.
4. In general, to apply something means to put it into practical use or to make use of it in a specific situation.
