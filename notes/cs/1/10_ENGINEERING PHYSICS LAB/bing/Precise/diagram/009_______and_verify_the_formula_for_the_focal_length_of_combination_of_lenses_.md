##### Verification of the formula for the focal length of combination of lenses

The formula for the focal length of a combination of lenses is given by:

1/f = 1/f1 + 1/f2 - d/f1f2

where f is the focal length of the combination of lenses, f1 and f2 are the focal lengths of the individual lenses, and d is the distance between the two lenses.

To verify this formula, we can perform an experiment using a convex lens and a concave lens. The steps for the experiment are as follows:

1. Place a convex lens of known focal length f1 on an optical bench.
2. Place a concave lens of known focal length f2 at a distance d from the convex lens.
3. Place an object at a distance greater than f1 from the convex lens.
4. Adjust the position of the screen until a sharp image of the object is formed on it.
5. Measure the distance between the object and the screen. This distance is the image distance v.
6. Measure the distance between the object and the convex lens. This distance is the object distance u.
7. Using the lens formula 1/f = 1/u + 1/v, calculate the focal length f of the combination of lenses.
8. Compare the calculated value of f with the value obtained using the formula for the focal length of a combination of lenses.

The experimentally obtained value of f should be in close agreement with the value obtained using the formula for the focal length of a combination of lenses, thus verifying the formula.
