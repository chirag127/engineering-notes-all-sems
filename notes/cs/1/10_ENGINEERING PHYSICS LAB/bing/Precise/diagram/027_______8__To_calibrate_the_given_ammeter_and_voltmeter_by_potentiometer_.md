##### 8. To calibrate the given ammeter and voltmeter by potentiometer.

Calibration is the process of comparing the readings of an instrument with a known standard to determine the accuracy of the instrument. In this experiment, we will calibrate an ammeter and a voltmeter using a potentiometer.

The following steps can be followed to calibrate the given ammeter and voltmeter by potentiometer:

1. Connect the ammeter in series with a battery and a resistor of known resistance.
2. Measure the current flowing through the circuit using the ammeter.
3. Connect the potentiometer in parallel with the ammeter and adjust the potentiometer until the potential difference across the ammeter is zero.
4. Measure the current flowing through the circuit using the potentiometer.
5. Compare the readings of the ammeter and the potentiometer to determine the accuracy of the ammeter.
6. Repeat the above steps for the voltmeter by connecting it in parallel with the battery and measuring the potential difference across the battery.
7. Adjust the potentiometer until the potential difference across the voltmeter is zero and measure the potential difference using the potentiometer.
8. Compare the readings of the voltmeter and the potentiometer to determine the accuracy of the voltmeter.

By following these steps, the given ammeter and voltmeter can be calibrated using a potentiometer. This will help to ensure that the readings of the ammeter and voltmeter are accurate and reliable.
