##### Carrying Coil and Estimate the Radius of the Coil

A carrying coil is a coil of wire that carries an electric current. The magnetic field produced by the current in the coil can be used to estimate the radius of the coil. Here are the steps to estimate the radius of a carrying coil:

1. Measure the magnetic field strength at the center of the coil using a Gaussmeter.
2. Determine the number of turns in the coil.
3. Use the formula B = (μ₀ * I * N) / (2 * R) to calculate the radius of the coil, where B is the magnetic field strength, μ₀ is the permeability of free space, I is the current in the coil, N is the number of turns in the coil, and R is the radius of the coil.
4. Solve the equation for R to find the estimated radius of the coil.
