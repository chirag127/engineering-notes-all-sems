##### 3. To determine the specific rotation of cane sugar solution using polarimeter.

A polarimeter is an instrument used to measure the angle of rotation of plane-polarized light as it passes through an optically active substance. The specific rotation of a substance is the angle of rotation of plane-polarized light per unit path length and per unit concentration of the substance.

To determine the specific rotation of a cane sugar solution using a polarimeter, the following steps can be followed:

1. Prepare a solution of cane sugar by dissolving a known mass of cane sugar in a known volume of water.
2. Fill a polarimeter tube with the cane sugar solution and place it in the polarimeter.
3. Adjust the polarimeter to obtain a reading of the angle of rotation of the plane-polarized light as it passes through the cane sugar solution.
4. Calculate the specific rotation of the cane sugar solution using the formula: specific rotation = (observed rotation) / (concentration x path length).

The specific rotation of the cane sugar solution can be used to determine the concentration of the solution, or to identify the presence of other optically active substances in the solution. It is important to note that the specific rotation of a substance is dependent on the wavelength of the light used, the temperature of the solution, and the solvent used to prepare the solution. These factors should be controlled and reported when determining the specific rotation of a substance.
