#### Monochromatic and Polychromatic Light

- **Monochromatic light** refers to light that consists of a single wavelength or color. This type of light is produced by lasers, LEDs, and certain types of lamps.
- **Polychromatic light**, on the other hand, consists of multiple wavelengths or colors. This type of light is produced by most light sources, including the sun, incandescent bulbs, and fluorescent lamps.
- Monochromatic light is often used in scientific and medical applications, such as spectroscopy and microscopy, where precise control of the wavelength of light is required.
- Polychromatic light is more commonly used in everyday applications, such as lighting and photography, where a broad spectrum of colors is desired.
- The human eye is more sensitive to polychromatic light, as it contains multiple wavelengths that stimulate the different types of photoreceptor cells in the retina.
- Monochromatic light can appear less bright than polychromatic light of the same intensity, as it only stimulates one type of photoreceptor cell.
