#### Apply

- The term "apply" can have several meanings depending on the context in which it is used.
- In general, to apply means to make a formal request or to put something into operation or use.
- For example, one can apply for a job, a loan, or a permit by submitting an application or request to the appropriate authority.
- In the context of mathematics or science, to apply can mean to use a rule, principle, or formula to solve a problem or to understand a situation.
- In the context of cosmetics or medicine, to apply can mean to put a substance onto a surface, such as applying makeup to the face or applying ointment to a wound.
- In the context of law, to apply can mean to make a legal claim or to request a court to make a ruling or decision.
