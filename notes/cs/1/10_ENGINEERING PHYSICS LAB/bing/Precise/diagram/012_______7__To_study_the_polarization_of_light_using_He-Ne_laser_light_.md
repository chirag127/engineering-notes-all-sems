##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

Polarization is a property of light that describes the direction of its electric field. Light can be polarized in several ways, including linear, circular, and elliptical polarization.

A He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as the lasing medium. The light emitted by a He-Ne laser is typically linearly polarized.

To study the polarization of light using a He-Ne laser, the following steps can be followed:

1. Set up the He-Ne laser and turn it on.
2. Place a polarizer in the path of the laser beam.
3. Rotate the polarizer and observe the changes in the intensity of the laser light.
4. Record the intensity of the laser light as a function of the angle of the polarizer.
5. Analyze the data to determine the polarization state of the laser light.

By following these steps, one can study the polarization of light using a He-Ne laser. This can provide valuable insights into the properties of light and its interaction with matter.
