#### CO CO Statement Bloom’s

CO CO Statement Bloom’s is a topic that refers to the use of Bloom's Taxonomy in the creation of Course Outcome (CO) statements. Bloom's Taxonomy is a framework for categorizing educational goals and objectives into different levels of complexity and specificity. It is often used by educators to design assessments, curriculum, and instructional methods.

Here are some key points to consider when using Bloom's Taxonomy to create CO statements:

1. Bloom's Taxonomy consists of six levels: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. Each level represents a different type of cognitive process, with Remembering being the most basic and Creating being the most complex.
2. When creating CO statements, it is important to consider the level of Bloom's Taxonomy that is most appropriate for the course and its objectives. For example, a course that focuses on basic knowledge and comprehension may have CO statements that align with the Remembering and Understanding levels of Bloom's Taxonomy.
3. CO statements should be specific, measurable, and achievable. They should clearly state what the student is expected to know or be able to do by the end of the course.
4. It is important to align the CO statements with the course content, instructional methods, and assessments. This ensures that the course is designed to effectively support student learning and achievement of the stated outcomes.

In summary, CO CO Statement Bloom’s refers to the use of Bloom's Taxonomy in the creation of Course Outcome statements. This framework can be a useful tool for educators in designing effective courses that support student learning and achievement.
