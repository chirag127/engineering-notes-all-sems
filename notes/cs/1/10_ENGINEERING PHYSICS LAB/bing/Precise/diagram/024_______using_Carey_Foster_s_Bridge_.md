##### Using Carey Foster's Bridge

Carey Foster's Bridge is an electrical circuit used to measure medium resistances or small differences between two large resistances. It is a variant of the Wheatstone bridge, invented by Carey Foster .

- **Construction**: The construction of Carey Foster's bridge circuit is similar to the Wheatstone bridge, which consists of four arms in which four resistances are connected .
- **Working**: Carey Foster bridge measures fundamentally the difference between the resistance of two elements .
- **Applications**: Carey Foster bridge can calculate medium resistances or can compare and measure the two large/equal resistance values with small variations .
