### Any ten experiments (at least four from each group)

#### Group 1: Physics
1. **The Double-Slit Experiment**: This experiment demonstrates the wave-particle duality of light, where light behaves as both a wave and a particle.
2. **The Michelson-Morley Experiment**: This experiment was designed to detect the presence of the hypothetical luminiferous aether, a medium through which light was thought to propagate.
3. **The Millikan Oil Drop Experiment**: This experiment measured the charge of an electron by balancing the gravitational and electrical forces on tiny charged droplets of oil suspended between two metal electrodes.
4. **The Rutherford Gold Foil Experiment**: This experiment demonstrated the existence of a small, dense, positively charged nucleus at the center of an atom.

#### Group 2: Chemistry
1. **The Iodine Clock Reaction**: This experiment demonstrates the concept of reaction rates and how they can be affected by factors such as temperature and concentration.
2. **The Briggs-Rauscher Oscillating Reaction**: This experiment demonstrates the concept of chemical oscillations, where the concentrations of reactants and products oscillate over time.
3. **The Belousov-Zhabotinsky Reaction**: This experiment demonstrates the concept of chemical oscillations and the emergence of complex patterns in a chemical system.
4. **The Elephant Toothpaste Experiment**: This experiment demonstrates the decomposition of hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen gas, catalyzed by potassium iodide.

#### Group 3: Biology
1. **The Miller-Urey Experiment**: This experiment simulated the conditions thought to be present on the early Earth and tested the hypothesis that the building blocks of life could have formed spontaneously under these conditions.
2. **The Hershey-Chase Experiment**: This experiment demonstrated that DNA, not protein, is the genetic material of life.
