##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum.

The compound pendulum is an experimental setup used to determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g). The setup consists of a rigid body suspended from a fixed horizontal axis, which is allowed to oscillate freely under the influence of gravity. 1. The compound pendulum is set up and allowed to oscillate freely.
2. The time period of oscillation is measured using a stopwatch.
3. The mass of the body and the distance between the center of mass and the axis of suspension are measured.
4. The moment of inertia of the body about the axis of suspension is calculated using the parallel axis theorem.
5. The value of g is calculated using the formula g = 4π²(I/mdT²).
