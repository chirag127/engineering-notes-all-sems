##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

1. **Introduction:** Polarization is a property of light that describes the direction of its electric field. Light can be polarized in different ways, such as linear, circular, or elliptical polarization.

2. **He-Ne laser light:** A He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as the lasing medium. The light emitted by a He-Ne laser is typically linearly polarized.

3. **Polarization of light:** When light passes through a polarizing material, only the component of the electric field that is parallel to the polarization axis of the material is transmitted. The other component is absorbed or reflected.

4. **Experiment:** To study the polarization of light using a He-Ne laser, one can set up an experiment with a polarizer and an analyzer. The polarizer is placed in the path of the laser light to polarize it, and the analyzer is used to measure the intensity of the transmitted light.

5. **Observations:** By rotating the analyzer and measuring the intensity of the transmitted light, one can observe how the intensity changes with the angle between the polarization axis of the polarizer and the analyzer. This can provide information about the polarization state of the laser light.

6. **Conclusion:** The study of the polarization of light using a He-Ne laser can provide valuable insights into the properties of light and its interaction with matter. It is an important topic in the field of optics and photonics.
