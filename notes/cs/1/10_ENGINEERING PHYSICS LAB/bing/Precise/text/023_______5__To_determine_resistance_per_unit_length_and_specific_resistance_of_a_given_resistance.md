##### 5. To determine resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance

- Resistance is a measure of the opposition to the flow of electric current through a conductor.
- The resistance of a conductor depends on its length, cross-sectional area, and the material it is made of.
- The resistance per unit length is the resistance of a conductor divided by its length. It is usually measured in ohms per meter (Ω/m).
- The specific resistance or resistivity of a material is a measure of the material's inherent resistance to the flow of electric current. It is usually measured in ohm-meters (Ωm).
- To determine the resistance per unit length and specific resistance of a given resistance, the following steps can be followed:
    1. Measure the length of the conductor using a ruler or measuring tape.
    2. Measure the resistance of the conductor using an ohmmeter.
    3. Divide the resistance by the length of the conductor to obtain the resistance per unit length.
    4. To determine the specific resistance, the cross-sectional area of the conductor must also be measured. This can be done by measuring the diameter of the conductor and using the formula for the area of a circle (πr^2, where r is the radius of the conductor).
    5. The specific resistance can then be calculated by multiplying the resistance per unit length by the cross-sectional area of the conductor.
