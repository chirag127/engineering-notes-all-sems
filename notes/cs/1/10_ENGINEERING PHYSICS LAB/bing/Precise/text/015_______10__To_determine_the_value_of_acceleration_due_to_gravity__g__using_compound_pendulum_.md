##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum.

1. The compound pendulum is a rigid body swinging in a vertical plane about a horizontal axis passing through the body.
2. The time period of a compound pendulum is given by the formula T = 2π √(I/mgd), where I is the moment of inertia of the pendulum about the axis of rotation, m is the mass of the pendulum, g is the acceleration due to gravity, and d is the distance between the center of mass of the pendulum and the axis of rotation.
3. To determine the value of g using a compound pendulum, the time period of the pendulum is measured for different values of d.
4. A graph is plotted between T^2 and d, and the slope of the graph is used to calculate the value of g.
5. The slope of the graph is equal to 4π^2(I/mg), so g can be calculated as g = 4π^2(I/m*slope).
6. The value of g obtained using this method is an average value for the location where the experiment is performed.
7. This method of determining the value of g is useful because it can be performed using simple equipment and does not require advanced technology.
8. The accuracy of the result depends on the accuracy of the measurements of the time period, the mass of the pendulum, and the distance d.
9. The experiment can be repeated multiple times to obtain an average value for g and to reduce the uncertainty in the result.
10. The value of g determined using this method can be compared with the standard value of g to check the accuracy of the result.
