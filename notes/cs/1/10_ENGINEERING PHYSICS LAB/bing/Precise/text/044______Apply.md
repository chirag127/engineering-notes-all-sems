#### Apply

- The word "apply" can have several meanings depending on the context in which it is used.
- It can mean to make a formal request, usually in writing, for something such as a job, a loan, or admission to a school or college.
- It can also mean to put or spread something such as paint, cream, or glue onto a surface.
- Another meaning of "apply" is to use something such as a rule, system, or skill in a particular situation or for a particular purpose.
- In mathematics, "apply" can mean to use a mathematical operation or formula to a set of numbers or other mathematical objects.
- In general, "apply" means to use something in a practical or relevant way.
