#### Monochromatic and Polychromatic Light

- Monochromatic light is light that has only one wavelength or frequency. It is pure and uniform in color. Examples of monochromatic light sources are lasers, LEDs, and sodium lamps  .
- Polychromatic light is light that has more than one wavelength or frequency. It is composed of a mixture of colors. Examples of polychromatic light sources are white light, sunlight, and incandescent lamps  .
- Monochromatic and polychromatic light have different applications and properties. Some of them are:

  - Monochromatic light is useful for spectroscopy, interferometry, holography, and optical communication, as it can provide high resolution, coherence, and stability .
  - Polychromatic light is useful for photography, colorimetry, and illumination, as it can provide a natural and realistic representation of objects and scenes .
  - Monochromatic light can be easily separated and filtered using prisms, gratings, or filters, as it has a single angle of refraction or diffraction .
  - Polychromatic light can be dispersed or diffracted into its constituent colors using prisms, gratings, or monochromators, as it has a range of angles of refraction or diffraction .
  - Monochromatic light can produce interference and diffraction patterns, as it has a constant phase difference between its waves .
  - Polychromatic light can produce interference and diffraction patterns, but they are less visible and more complex, as it has a varying phase difference between its waves .
