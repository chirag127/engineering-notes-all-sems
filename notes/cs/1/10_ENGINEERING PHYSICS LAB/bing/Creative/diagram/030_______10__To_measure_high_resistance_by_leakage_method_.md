##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

- High resistance is the resistance of a circuit or a device that is much higher than the normal or expected value, usually in the order of megaohms or gigaohms.
- High resistance can be measured by various methods, such as megger, Wheatstone bridge, direct deflection method, etc. One of the methods is the leakage method, which is based on the principle of charging and discharging a capacitor through the resistance to be measured.
- The leakage method consists of the following steps:

  - A capacitor of known capacitance C is charged by a battery of voltage V through a resistance R, which is the resistance to be measured. The charging current is measured by an ammeter A.
  - The battery is disconnected and the capacitor is allowed to discharge through the resistance R. The discharging current is measured by another ammeter A'.
  - The time taken for the discharging current to fall from a certain value I to I/2.718 is noted by a stopwatch. This time is called the time constant of the circuit, denoted by τ.
  - The resistance R is calculated by the formula: R = τ/C
