#### Experiments

- An experiment is a scientific procedure that tests a hypothesis or a prediction.
- An experiment usually involves manipulating one or more variables and measuring their effects on another variable, called the dependent variable.
- The variable that is manipulated is called the independent variable, and the variable that is held constant is called the control variable.
- An experiment should have a clear aim, a detailed method, a fair and reliable design, and a valid and accurate analysis of the results.
- An experiment should also follow ethical principles and minimize any harm or risk to the participants, animals, or the environment.
- Some examples of experiments are:

  - Testing the effect of different fertilizers on plant growth.
  - Testing the effect of different drugs on blood pressure.
  - Testing the effect of different colors on mood.
  - Testing the effect of different temperatures on enzyme activity.
