Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India) is a book written by A.K. Katiyar and C.K. Pandey for engineering students of Uttar Pradesh Technical University, Lucknow . It covers the revised syllabus of Engineering Physics-I (NAS-101), Engineering Physics-II (NAS-201) and Practical (NAS-151/251) of the university.

The book consists of 12 chapters and 2 appendices. The chapters are:

1. Interference of Light
2. Diffraction of Light
3. Polarization of Light
4. Laser and Fiber Optics
5. Quantum Mechanics
6. Crystal Structure
7. X-Rays and Bragg's Law
8. Dielectric and Magnetic Properties of Materials
9. Superconductivity
10. Nanotechnology
11. Nuclear Physics
12. Semiconductor Physics

The appendices are:

A. Units and Dimensions
B. Physical Constants and Conversion Factors

The book also contains numerous solved and unsolved problems, multiple choice questions, review questions and practical exercises for each chapter. The book aims to provide a clear and concise understanding of the fundamental concepts and applications of engineering physics .

Some of the main features of the book are:

- It follows the latest syllabus and examination pattern of UPTU.
- It provides a balanced coverage of both theory and practical aspects of engineering physics.
- It explains the concepts with the help of diagrams, graphs, tables and examples.
- It includes a large number of numerical problems and exercises for practice and self-assessment.
- It incorporates the recent developments and trends in the field of engineering physics, such as nanotechnology, superconductivity, laser and fiber optics, etc.
- It is written in a simple and lucid language, suitable for undergraduate students of engineering .
