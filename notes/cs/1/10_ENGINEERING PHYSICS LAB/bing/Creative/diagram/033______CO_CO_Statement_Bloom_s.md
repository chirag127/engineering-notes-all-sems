#### CO CO Statement Bloom’s

- CO stands for Course Outcomes, which are the specific and measurable learning objectives that students should achieve by the end of a course.
- CO statement is a concise and clear description of what students will be able to do after completing a course, using action verbs that reflect the level of cognitive skills required.
- Bloom's taxonomy is a framework for classifying the levels of cognitive skills, from the lowest (remembering) to the highest (creating).
- Bloom's taxonomy can help teachers write effective CO statements by providing a list of verbs that correspond to each level of cognitive skills .
- Bloom's taxonomy can also help teachers design appropriate assessment methods and activities that align with the CO statements and measure the students' learning outcomes .
- The revised Bloom's taxonomy consists of six levels of cognitive skills, each with a set of verbs and examples of CO statements:

  - Remembering: The ability to recall or recognize facts, terms, concepts, or procedures. Verbs: define, list, name, identify, label, etc. Example: Define the key terms and concepts of computer science.
  - Understanding: The ability to explain, interpret, summarize, or paraphrase information. Verbs: describe, explain, illustrate, compare, classify, etc. Example: Explain the basic principles of programming languages.
  - Applying: The ability to use knowledge or skills in new or familiar situations. Verbs: apply, solve, use, demonstrate, calculate, etc. Example: Apply the appropriate algorithm to solve a given problem.
  - Analyzing: The ability to break down information into parts and examine their relationships or functions. Verbs: analyze, compare, contrast, differentiate, evaluate, etc. Example: Analyze the strengths and weaknesses of different data structures.
  - Evaluating: The ability to judge the quality, value, or effectiveness of something based on criteria or standards. Verbs: evaluate, critique, justify, argue, recommend, etc. Example: Evaluate the usability and functionality of a software application.
  - Creating: The ability to generate, produce, or synthesize new or original ideas, products, or solutions. Verbs: create, design, develop, construct, invent, etc. Example: Create a mobile app that meets the user's needs and specifications.
