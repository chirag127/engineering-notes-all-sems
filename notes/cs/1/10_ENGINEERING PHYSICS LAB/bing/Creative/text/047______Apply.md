#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to put something into action or use, or to request something formally, such as a job, admission, or loan.
- Apply can also mean to cover or spread something over a surface, or to make something adhere or stick to something else.
- Apply has several synonyms, such as employ, implement, utilize, administer, enforce, execute, operate, practice, or exert.
- Apply has several antonyms, such as ignore, neglect, disregard, reject, refuse, or withdraw.
- Apply can be used in different contexts, such as:

  - To apply a rule or principle: This means to follow or use the rule or principle in a specific situation or case. For example, "We need to apply the law equally to everyone."
  - To apply oneself: This means to work hard or diligently at something, or to devote oneself to something. For example, "She applied herself to her studies and graduated with honors."
  - To apply for something: This means to submit an application or request for something, such as a job, a grant, a visa, or a scholarship. For example, "He applied for a loan to start his own business."
  - To apply something to something: This means to put or spread something over something else, or to make something stick or adhere to something else. For example, "She applied sunscreen to her skin before going outside."
  - To apply to something or someone: This means to be relevant or suitable to something or someone, or to affect or involve something or someone. For example, "These rules apply to all members of the club."
