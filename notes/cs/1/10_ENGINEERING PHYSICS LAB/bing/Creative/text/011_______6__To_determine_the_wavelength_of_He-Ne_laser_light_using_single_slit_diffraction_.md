##### 6. To determine the wavelength of He-Ne laser light using single slit diffraction.

- Single slit diffraction is the phenomenon of spreading out of light waves past an opening that is comparable to the wavelength of the light.
- When a He-Ne laser (λ = 633 nm) shines through a single slit of width D, it produces a diffraction pattern on a screen that is placed at a distance L from the slit .
- The diffraction pattern consists of a bright central maximum and a series of alternating bright and dark fringes on either side of it.
- The angular position of the mth dark fringe (minimum) is given by the formula: sin θ_m = m λ / D, where m is an integer.
- The linear position of the mth dark fringe on the screen is given by the formula: y_m = L tan θ_m ≈ L sin θ_m, where L >> D.
- To determine the wavelength of the He-Ne laser light, we can measure the distance between two consecutive dark fringes on the screen, such as the m = 1 and m = 2 minima, and use the formula: λ = 2 D y / L, where y is the distance between the minima.
- For example, if the slit width is D = 0.1 mm, the screen distance is L = 1.5 m, and the distance between the m = 1 and m = 2 minima is y = 2.3 cm, then the wavelength of the He-Ne laser light is λ = 2 × 0.1 × 10^-3 × 2.3 × 10^-2 / 1.5 ≈ 613 nm.
- This value is close to the actual wavelength of the He-Ne laser light, which is 633 nm. The difference may be due to experimental errors or approximations.
