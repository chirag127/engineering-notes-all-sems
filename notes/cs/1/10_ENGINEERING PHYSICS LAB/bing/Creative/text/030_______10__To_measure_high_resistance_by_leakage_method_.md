##### 10. To measure high resistance by leakage method.

- High resistance by leakage method is a technique for measuring very high value of resistance (generally above 20M Ω) that cannot be measured accurately by digital multimeters or LCR meters  .
- The principle of this method is to apply a known voltage source to the resistance under test and measure the leakage current through it by a sensitive voltmeter.
- The leakage current is proportional to the applied voltage and inversely proportional to the resistance, according to Ohm's law.
- The resistance can be calculated by dividing the applied voltage by the leakage current.

![Circuit diagram for high resistance by leakage method](https://electricalacademia.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Resistance-Measurement-Methods-4.png)

- The steps for performing this experiment are as follows:
  - Connect the circuit as shown in the diagram, with the resistance R to be measured between the terminals A and B.
  - Adjust the voltage source V to the desired value, such as the normal operating voltage of the resistance.
  - Measure the voltage across the voltmeter Vm, which is equal to the leakage current through the resistance R.
  - Calculate the resistance R by dividing the voltage V by the voltage Vm.
  - Repeat the experiment for different values of V and Vm and record the results in a table.
  - Plot a graph of V versus Vm and verify that it is a straight line passing through the origin, indicating a linear relationship between the applied voltage and the leakage current.
  - Calculate the slope of the graph, which is equal to the resistance R. Compare the value of R obtained from the graph with the value obtained from the calculation.
