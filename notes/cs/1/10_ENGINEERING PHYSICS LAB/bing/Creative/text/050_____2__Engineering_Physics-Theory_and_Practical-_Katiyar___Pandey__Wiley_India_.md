### 2. Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

- This book is a textbook for engineering students of U. P. Technical University, Lucknow, as per the revised syllabus of Engineering Physics-I (NAS-101), Engineering Physics-II (NAS-201) and Practical (NAS-151/251)   .
- The book covers topics such as crystallography, quantum mechanics, solid state physics, nuclear physics, lasers, superconductivity, nanotechnology, fiber optics, holography, and acoustics  .
- The book also includes experiments related to the theory topics, such as determination of lattice constants, verification of de Broglie's equation, measurement of Planck's constant, verification of Stefan's law, study of photoelectric effect, determination of band gap, study of Hall effect, measurement of magnetic susceptibility, study of nuclear decay, study of laser characteristics, study of fiber optics, and recording and reconstruction of holograms  .
- The book aims to provide a clear and concise explanation of the concepts and principles of engineering physics, with an emphasis on applications and problem-solving  .
- The book also contains numerous solved examples, illustrations, diagrams, tables, and graphs to aid the understanding of the topics  .
- The book also provides review questions, multiple choice questions, and numerical problems at the end of each chapter for practice and revision  .
- The book is suitable for undergraduate engineering students of all branches, as well as for diploma and polytechnic students  .
