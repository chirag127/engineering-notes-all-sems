### Any ten experiments (at least four from each group)

Here are some examples of experiments that you can do at home or in school, using simple materials and equipment. They are divided into two groups: physical science and life science.

#### Physical science experiments

- **Dancing popcorn**: In this experiment, you will observe how popcorn kernels can dance in a jar of water and vinegar, due to the bubbles of carbon dioxide gas produced by the reaction of baking soda and vinegar.
- **Lemon volcano**: In this experiment, you will create a mini volcano using a lemon, baking soda, and food coloring. You will see how the citric acid in the lemon juice reacts with the baking soda to produce a fizzy eruption of foam.
- **Turning pennies green**: In this experiment, you will learn how to make pennies look old and green, by exposing them to vinegar and salt. You will see how the copper in the pennies reacts with the acetic acid in the vinegar and the chloride ions in the salt to form a green layer of copper carbonate.
- **Exploding baggies**: In this experiment, you will create a chemical reaction inside a zip-top bag, using vinegar and baking soda. You will see how the bag inflates and eventually pops, due to the pressure of the carbon dioxide gas produced by the reaction.
- **Hot ice**: In this experiment, you will make a supercooled solution of sodium acetate, using vinegar and baking soda. You will see how the solution can crystallize instantly when you touch it with a seed crystal, releasing heat in the process.

#### Life science experiments

- **Calm in the storm**: In this experiment, you will build a tornado tube to simulate a tornado in a bottle. You will see how the water swirls and forms a vortex when you flip the bottle. You will also learn how this experiment can remind you to rely on God in any situation.
- **Glitter germs**: In this experiment, you will use glitter and water to demonstrate how germs can spread from one person to another. You will see how the glitter sticks to your hands and transfers to other objects or people. You will also learn the importance of washing your hands properly to prevent the spread of germs.
- **Walking water**: In this experiment, you will use water, food coloring, and paper towels to create a rainbow of colors. You will see how the water travels from one jar to another through the paper towels, due to capillary action. You will also learn how this experiment can show you the beauty of God's creation.
- **Snow fluff**: In this experiment, you will make fake snow using shaving cream and cornstarch. You will see how the mixture feels soft and fluffy, like real snow. You will also learn how this experiment can help you appreciate the wonders of winter.
- **Squishy turkeys**: In this experiment, you will make squishy turkeys using balloons, flour, and googly eyes. You will see how the balloons can be shaped and decorated to look like turkeys. You will also learn how this experiment can help you celebrate Thanksgiving and be thankful for God's blessings.
