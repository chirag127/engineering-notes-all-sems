### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- This is a textbook of practical physics for undergraduate students of science and engineering.
- It covers the experiments and measurements of various physical phenomena and properties, such as mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, magnetism, and modern physics.
- It provides detailed descriptions of the experimental setups, procedures, observations, calculations, and results, along with relevant diagrams, graphs, and tables.
- It also includes theoretical background, error analysis, and numerical problems for each experiment.
- It follows the syllabus and guidelines of various universities and boards of India.
- It aims to develop the skills and concepts of experimental physics among the students and to prepare them for further studies and research.
