# 9. To determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid.

## Aim
To determine the coefficient of viscosity of a given liquid by measuring the terminal velocity of a spherical body falling through the liquid.

## Theory
The coefficient of viscosity of a liquid is a measure of its resistance to flow. It is defined as the ratio of the shear stress to the rate of shear strain in the liquid.

When a spherical body of radius r and density ρ falls through a liquid of density σ and viscosity η, it experiences three forces:

- The weight of the body, W = mg, acting downwards, where m is the mass of the body and g is the acceleration due to gravity.
- The upthrust or buoyant force, U = Vσg, acting upwards, where V is the volume of the body.
- The viscous drag force, F = 6πηrv, acting upwards, where v is the velocity of the body relative to the liquid.

At terminal velocity, the net force on the body is zero, i.e., W - U - F = 0. Therefore,

mg - Vσg - 6πηrv = 0

or

v = (2/9)(r^2)g(ρ - σ)/η

The coefficient of viscosity of the liquid can be calculated by measuring the terminal velocity, the radius and the density of the body, and the density of the liquid.

## Apparatus
- A glass tube of uniform cross-section and about 1 m long, filled with the liquid whose viscosity is to be determined.
- A spherical body of known radius and density, such as a steel ball or a glass bead.
- A stopwatch to measure the time of fall of the body.
- A meter scale to measure the length of the tube.
- A balance to measure the mass of the body.
- A hydrometer to measure the density of the liquid.

## Procedure
- Fill the glass tube with the liquid and clamp it vertically on a stand.
- Measure the length of the tube, L, with the meter scale.
- Measure the mass of the spherical body, m, with the balance and calculate its radius, r, and density, ρ, using the formula V = (4/3)πr^3 and ρ = m/V.
- Measure the density of the liquid, σ, with the hydrometer.
- Drop the spherical body gently into the tube from the top and start the stopwatch as soon as it enters the liquid.
- Stop the stopwatch when the body reaches the bottom of the tube and note the time of fall, t.
- Repeat the experiment for at least five times and take the average time of fall, t̅.
- Calculate the terminal velocity of the body, v, using the formula v = L/t̅.
- Calculate the coefficient of viscosity of the liquid, η, using the formula η = (2/9)(r^2)g(ρ - σ)/v.
