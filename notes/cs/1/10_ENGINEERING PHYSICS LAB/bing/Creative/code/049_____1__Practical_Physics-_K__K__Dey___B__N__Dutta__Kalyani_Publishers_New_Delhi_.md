### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

This book is a comprehensive guide for undergraduate students of physics who want to perform experiments in various fields of physics. The book covers the following topics:

- **Measurement and Error Analysis**: This chapter introduces the basic concepts of measurement, units, dimensions, errors, and propagation of errors. It also explains how to use different types of measuring instruments, such as vernier calipers, screw gauge, spherometer, and micrometer.
- **Mechanics**: This chapter deals with the experiments related to the laws of motion, friction, moment of inertia, simple pendulum, compound pendulum, torsion pendulum, and physical pendulum. It also explains how to use different types of apparatus, such as Atwood's machine, Maxwell's wheel, flywheel, and Kater's pendulum.
- **Properties of Matter**: This chapter covers the experiments related to the elasticity, surface tension, viscosity, and thermal expansion of solids, liquids, and gases. It also explains how to use different types of apparatus, such as Young's modulus apparatus, Searle's apparatus, Lee's disc, Jolly's bulb, and Boyle's law apparatus.
- **Heat**: This chapter deals with the experiments related to the specific heat, latent heat, thermal conductivity, and radiation of solids, liquids, and gases. It also explains how to use different types of apparatus, such as calorimeter, Joule's calorimeter, Searle's calorimeter, Lees and Charlton's apparatus, and Stefan's law apparatus.
- **Sound**: This chapter covers the experiments related to the frequency, wavelength, velocity, and resonance of sound waves. It also explains how to use different types of apparatus, such as tuning fork, sonometer, resonance tube, Kundt's tube, and Melde's apparatus.
- **Light**: This chapter deals with the experiments related to the reflection, refraction, interference, diffraction, polarization, and dispersion of light waves. It also explains how to use different types of apparatus, such as plane mirror, convex lens, concave lens, prism, spectrometer, Newton's rings apparatus, Fresnel's biprism, Young's double slit, diffraction grating, polarimeter, and spectroscope.
- **Electricity and Magnetism**: This chapter covers the experiments related to the electric potential, electric current, electric resistance, electromotive force, capacitance, inductance, and magnetic field of electric and magnetic circuits. It also explains how to use different types of apparatus, such as voltmeter, ammeter, galvanometer, potentiometer, Wheatstone's bridge, meter bridge, post office box, ballistic galvanometer, capacitor, inductor, solenoid, and magnetometer.
- **Modern Physics**: This chapter deals with the experiments related to the photoelectric effect, Compton effect, X-rays, atomic spectra, nuclear physics, and radioactivity. It also explains how to use different types of apparatus, such as photocell, phototube, Wilson cloud chamber, Geiger-Muller counter, scintillation counter, X-ray tube, spectrometer, and spectroscope.
