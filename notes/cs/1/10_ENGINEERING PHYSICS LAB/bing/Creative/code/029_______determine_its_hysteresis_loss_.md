- Hysteresis loss is the energy dissipated as heat in a magnetic material when it is subjected to a changing magnetic field.
- Hysteresis loss occurs because the magnetic domains in the material do not align and reverse instantly with the applied field, but lag behind it, creating a loop in the magnetization curve, called the hysteresis loop.
