##### 3. To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current

- The magnetic field of a current-carrying circular loop can be calculated using the Biot-Savart law, which states that the magnetic field at a point due to a small current element is proportional to the current, the length of the element, the sine of the angle between the element and the line joining the point and the element, and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between the point and the element.
- The magnetic field along the axis of a current-carrying circular loop can be derived by integrating the Biot-Savart law over the loop, assuming that the loop has a radius R and carries a current I. The result is:

![B=\frac{\mu_0IR^2}{2(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?B%3D%5Cfrac%7B%5Cmu_0IR%5E2%7D%7B2%28R%5E2&plus;z%5E2%29%5E%7B3/2%7D%7D)

where B is the magnetic field, z is the distance from the center of the loop along the axis, and μ0 is the permeability of free space.

- To determine the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current-carrying circular loop, we can use the following experimental setup:

  - A circular loop of wire with a known radius R and a variable resistor is connected to a battery and an ammeter to measure the current I.
  - A magnetic compass needle is placed at a distance z from the center of the loop along the axis, and its deflection is measured by a protractor.
  - The distance z is varied by moving the compass needle along the axis, and the corresponding deflection is recorded for each value of z.
  - The magnetic field B at each point can be calculated using the formula:

![B=\frac{\mu_0IR^2}{2(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?B%3D%5Cfrac%7B%5Cmu_0IR%5E2%7D%7B2%28R%5E2&plus;z%5E2%29%5E%7B3/2%7D%7D)

  - A graph of B versus z can be plotted to show the variation of magnetic field with the distance along the axis of a current-carrying circular loop.

- The expected graph of B versus z is shown below:

![B vs z graph](https://i.imgur.com/8yQ2wYw.png)

- The graph shows that the magnetic field decreases as the distance from the center of the loop increases, and approaches zero as z becomes very large. The graph also shows that the magnetic field is maximum at the center of the loop, where z = 0. The shape of the graph is a hyperbola, which is consistent with the inverse square law of the Biot-Savart law.
