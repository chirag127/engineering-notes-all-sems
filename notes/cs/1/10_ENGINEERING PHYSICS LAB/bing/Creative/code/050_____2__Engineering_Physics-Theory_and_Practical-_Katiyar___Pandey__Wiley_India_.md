# Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

- This book is as per the revised syllabus of Engineering Physics-I (NAS-101), Engineering Physics-II (NAS-201) and Practical (NAS-151/251) of Uttar Pradesh Technical University (UPTU), Lucknow  .
- It covers the topics of mechanics, optics, waves and oscillations, quantum mechanics, solid state physics, semiconductor devices, nanotechnology, lasers, fibre optics and holography .
- It also includes experiments based on these topics, along with the theory, procedure, observations, calculations and results  .
- It aims to provide a clear and concise explanation of the physical concepts and principles, and to develop the analytical and problem-solving skills of the students .
- It contains numerous solved examples, illustrations, diagrams, tables, graphs and exercises  .
- It also provides review questions, multiple choice questions, numerical problems and previous year's question papers at the end of each chapter  .
- It is suitable for the students of engineering and technology of various branches and disciplines  .
