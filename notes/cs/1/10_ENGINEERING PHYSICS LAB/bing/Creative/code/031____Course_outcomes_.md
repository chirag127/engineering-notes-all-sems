## Course outcomes:

- A course outcome is a statement of what a student should be able to do or demonstrate by the end of a course.
- Course outcomes are derived from the course objectives, which are the broad goals of the course.
- Course outcomes are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
- Course outcomes should align with the program outcomes, which are the expected skills and competencies of the graduates of a program.
- Course outcomes should be communicated to the students at the beginning of the course and assessed throughout the course using various methods and tools.
- Course outcomes should be reviewed and revised periodically to ensure their relevance and quality.
