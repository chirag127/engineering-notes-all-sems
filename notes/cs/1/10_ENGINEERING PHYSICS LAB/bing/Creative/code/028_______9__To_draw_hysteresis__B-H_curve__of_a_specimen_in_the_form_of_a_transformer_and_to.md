##### 9. To draw hysteresis (B-H curve) of a specimen in the form of a transformer and to

- A hysteresis (B-H curve) is a graph that shows the relationship between the magnetic flux density (B) and the magnetic field intensity (H) of a magnetic material.
- A transformer is a device that transfers electrical energy from one circuit to another by electromagnetic induction. It consists of two or more coils of wire wrapped around a common core, which can be made of iron or other ferromagnetic materials.
- The core of a transformer is subjected to alternating magnetic fields as the input current changes. This causes the magnetic domains in the core to align and reverse repeatedly, resulting in energy loss due to hysteresis.
- The area of the B-H curve represents the energy loss per cycle due to hysteresis. The shape of the B-H curve depends on the type and quality of the core material. Soft magnetic materials have a narrow B-H curve, while hard magnetic materials have a wide B-H curve.
- To draw the B-H curve of a specimen in the form of a transformer, the following steps can be followed:
  - Connect the primary coil of the transformer to an AC voltage source and the secondary coil to a load resistor and an oscilloscope.
  - Measure the input current and the output voltage of the transformer using a current probe and a voltage probe, respectively.
  - Plot the input current versus the output voltage on the oscilloscope screen. This will give a loop-shaped curve, which is the B-H curve of the core material.
  - Adjust the input voltage and the load resistor to vary the size and shape of the B-H curve. Observe how the area of the curve changes with different parameters.
