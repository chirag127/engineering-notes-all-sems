##### 9. To draw hysteresis (B-H curve) of a specimen in the form of a transformer and to

- A hysteresis (B-H) curve is a graph that shows how the magnetic flux density (B) of a material changes with the applied magnetic field intensity (H).
- A transformer is a device that transfers electrical energy from one circuit to another by using electromagnetic induction. It consists of two or more coils of wire wrapped around a magnetic core.
- The magnetic core of a transformer is subjected to alternating magnetic fields as the input and output voltages change. This causes the core to undergo a cycle of magnetization and demagnetization, which results in energy loss due to hysteresis.
- The area of the B-H curve represents the energy loss per cycle due to hysteresis. The shape of the B-H curve depends on the type and quality of the core material. Soft magnetic materials have a narrow B-H curve, which means they have low hysteresis loss and high permeability. Hard magnetic materials have a wide B-H curve, which means they have high hysteresis loss and low permeability.
- To draw the B-H curve of a specimen in the form of a transformer, the following steps can be followed:

  - Connect the primary coil of the transformer to an AC voltage source and the secondary coil to a load resistor and an oscilloscope.
  - Measure the input voltage (V1) and current (I1) of the primary coil and the output voltage (V2) and current (I2) of the secondary coil using a multimeter or a current probe.
  - Calculate the magnetic field intensity (H) of the core using the formula H = N1I1/l, where N1 is the number of turns of the primary coil and l is the length of the core.
  - Calculate the magnetic flux density (B) of the core using the formula B = N2V2/Af, where N2 is the number of turns of the secondary coil, A is the cross-sectional area of the core, and f is the frequency of the AC source.
  - Plot the values of B and H on a graph paper or a spreadsheet software. The resulting curve is the B-H curve of the specimen.
  - Repeat the above steps for different values of the input voltage and observe how the B-H curve changes.
