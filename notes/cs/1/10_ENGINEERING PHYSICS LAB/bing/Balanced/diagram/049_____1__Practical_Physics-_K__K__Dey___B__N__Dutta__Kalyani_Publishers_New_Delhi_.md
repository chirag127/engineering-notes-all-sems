### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- This is a book on practical physics for undergraduate students of science and engineering.
- It covers various experiments and topics related to mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics and modern physics.
- It provides detailed descriptions of the experimental procedures, apparatus, observations, calculations and results.
- It also includes diagrams, graphs, tables, formulae and sample questions for each experiment.
- It aims to help students develop the skills of observation, measurement, analysis and interpretation of physical phenomena.
- It is based on the syllabus of various Indian universities and follows the guidelines of the University Grants Commission (UGC).
- It is written by K. K. Dey and B. N. Dutta, who are professors of physics at the University of Calcutta and Jadavpur University respectively.
- It is published by Kalyani Publishers, New Delhi, in 2008. It has 434 pages and an ISBN of 978-9327269376.
