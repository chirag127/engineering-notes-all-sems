##### 2. To determine the wavelength of different spectral lines of mercury light using plane

- Mercury light is a type of gas discharge lamp that emits light when an electric current passes through a vapor of mercury.
- Mercury light has a characteristic spectrum of discrete wavelengths, or spectral lines, that correspond to the energy transitions of the electrons in the mercury atoms.
- A plane diffraction grating is a device that splits a beam of light into its component wavelengths by using a series of parallel slits that act as sources of interference.
- The diffraction grating equation relates the wavelength of the light, the angle of diffraction, the order of the spectrum, and the grating spacing as follows:

`lambda = d sin theta / n`

where `lambda` is the wavelength, `d` is the grating spacing, `theta` is the angle of diffraction, and `n` is the order of the spectrum.

  - Rotate the telescope to observe the first-order spectrum, which is the diffracted light that forms an angle with the zeroth-order spectrum.
