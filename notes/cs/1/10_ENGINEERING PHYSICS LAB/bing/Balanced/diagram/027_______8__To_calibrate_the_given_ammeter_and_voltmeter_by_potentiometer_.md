##### 8. To calibrate the given ammeter and voltmeter by potentiometer.

- The aim of this experiment is to compare the readings of a given ammeter and voltmeter with the true values of current and voltage obtained by a potentiometer, and to find the correction factors for the instruments.
- The principle of this experiment is based on the fact that a potentiometer can measure the potential difference between two points in a circuit without drawing any current from it, and hence can give the true value of the voltage.
- The apparatus required for this experiment are: a potentiometer, a battery, a rheostat, a key, a galvanometer, a jockey, a standard cell, a given ammeter, a given voltmeter, a resistance box, and connecting wires.
