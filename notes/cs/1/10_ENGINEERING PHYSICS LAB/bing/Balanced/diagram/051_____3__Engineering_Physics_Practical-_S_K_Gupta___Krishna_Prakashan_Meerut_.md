### 3. Engineering Physics Practical- S K Gupta ( Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- This is a book that contains practical experiments and activities related to engineering physics.
- The book is written by S K Gupta and Sankalp Gupta, and published by Krishna Prakashan Media in 2014.
- The book covers topics such as optics, interference, diffraction, polarization, laser, fibre optics, holography, relativity, mechanics, and electronics.
- The book has 280 pages and is divided into 12 chapters, each with a set of objectives, theory, procedure, observations, calculations, results, and questions.
- The book also has appendices that provide useful information such as physical constants, conversion factors, error analysis, and graphs.
- The book is intended for undergraduate students of engineering and technology, and aims to develop their skills and understanding of physics through practical work.
- The book follows the syllabus and guidelines of the Uttar Pradesh Technical University (UPTU).
