### Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

- This is a book written by A.K. Katiyar and C.K. Pandey, published by Wiley India in 2015    .
- It is intended for engineering students of U. P. Technical University, Lucknow, and covers the revised syllabus of Engineering Physics-I (NAS-101), Engineering Physics-II (NAS-201) and Practical (NAS-151/251).
- The book consists of 14 chapters and 10 experiments, covering topics such as interference, diffraction, polarization, lasers, fiber optics, holography, crystal structure, X-rays, superconductivity, nanotechnology, quantum mechanics, nuclear physics, and special theory of relativity   .
- The book aims to provide a clear and concise presentation of the concepts and principles of engineering physics, with an emphasis on practical applications and problem-solving skills  .
- The book features numerous solved examples, illustrations, diagrams, tables, and summary points to enhance the understanding of the subject matter  .
- The book also includes multiple choice questions, review questions, and numerical problems at the end of each chapter, as well as a model question paper and a glossary of important terms  .
- The book is available in both print and digital formats   .
