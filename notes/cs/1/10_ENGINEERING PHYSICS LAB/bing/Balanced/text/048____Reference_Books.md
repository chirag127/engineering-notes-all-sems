## Reference Books

- Reference books are books that contain information on a specific topic or field of study, such as dictionaries, encyclopedias, atlases, handbooks, manuals, etc.
- Reference books are usually not meant to be read cover to cover, but rather to be consulted for specific facts, definitions, explanations, or instructions.
- Reference books are often organized alphabetically, chronologically, topically, or thematically, depending on the nature and purpose of the book.
- Reference books are usually updated periodically to reflect the latest developments and research in the subject area.
- Reference books are useful for finding background information, verifying facts, learning new terms, or getting an overview of a topic.
- Reference books are usually available in libraries, bookstores, or online databases. Some examples of online reference databases are Britannica, Oxford English Dictionary, WorldCat, etc.
