### 1. Practical Physics- K. K. Dey & B. N. Dutta (Kalyani Publishers New Delhi)

- This is a book on practical physics for undergraduate students of science and engineering.
- It covers various experiments and topics related to mechanics, heat, optics, electricity, magnetism, electronics and modern physics.
- It provides detailed descriptions of the experimental methods, apparatus, observations, calculations and results.
- It also includes relevant theory, diagrams, graphs, tables and numerical examples to illustrate the concepts and principles involved.
- It aims to develop the skills and techniques of performing experiments, analyzing data, drawing conclusions and reporting results.
- It follows the syllabus and guidelines of various universities and examination boards in India.
