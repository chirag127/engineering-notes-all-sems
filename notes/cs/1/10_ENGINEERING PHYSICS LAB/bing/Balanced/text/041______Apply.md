#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to use something for a particular purpose, to put something into operation, or to request something formally.
- Apply can also mean to be relevant or valid in a given situation, or to have an effect or influence on something or someone.
- Some common synonyms of apply are employ, use, utilize, and exploit.
- Some common phrases that use apply are:

  - apply oneself: to work hard and diligently at something
  - apply for: to submit an application or request for something, such as a job, a loan, or a scholarship
  - apply to: to be relevant or appropriate to something or someone, or to contact someone for a specific purpose
  - apply pressure: to exert force or influence on something or someone, often to achieve a desired outcome

- Some examples of apply in sentences are:

  - She applied the cream to her rash and hoped it would heal soon.
  - He applied the brakes when he saw the red light.
  - She applied for a visa to travel to Canada.
  - The same rules apply to everyone, regardless of their status.
  - He applied to Harvard University, but he was rejected.
  - She applied pressure on the wound to stop the bleeding.
  - He applied himself to his studies and graduated with honors.
