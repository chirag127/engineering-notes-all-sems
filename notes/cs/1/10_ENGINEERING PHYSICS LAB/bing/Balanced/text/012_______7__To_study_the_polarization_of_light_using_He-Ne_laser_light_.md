##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

- Polarization of light is the phenomenon of restricting the vibrations of the electric field vector of light in a single plane.
- He-Ne laser light is a type of coherent light source that emits light of a single wavelength (632.8 nm) and a single polarization state.
- To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light, we need the following apparatus: a He-Ne laser, a polarizer, an analyzer, a photodetector, and a digital multimeter.
- The experimental setup is as follows:

```
    He-Ne laser
        |
        |
        V
    Polarizer
        |
        |
        V
    Analyzer
        |
        |
        V
    Photodetector
        |
        |
        V
    Digital multimeter
```

- The polarizer is a device that allows only the light with a certain polarization direction to pass through, while blocking the light with other polarization directions. The analyzer is another polarizer that can be rotated to change its polarization direction.
- The photodetector is a device that converts the intensity of light into an electric current, which can be measured by the digital multimeter.
- The procedure is as follows:
  - Turn on the He-Ne laser and adjust its position so that the laser beam is incident on the polarizer.
  - Place the analyzer after the polarizer and align its polarization direction with that of the polarizer. This is called the parallel position.
  - Place the photodetector after the analyzer and connect it to the digital multimeter. Record the reading of the current on the multimeter, which corresponds to the intensity of the transmitted light.
  - Rotate the analyzer by a small angle (say 10 degrees) and record the new reading of the current on the multimeter. Repeat this step until the analyzer is rotated by 180 degrees. This is called the crossed position.
  - Plot a graph of the current (I) versus the angle of rotation of the analyzer (theta).
  - The graph should show a sinusoidal variation of the current with the angle, according to the Malus' law: I = I0 cos^2(theta), where I0 is the maximum current when the analyzer is in the parallel position.
  - The polarization of light can be verified by observing that the current is zero when the analyzer is in the crossed position, meaning that no light is transmitted through the analyzer. This shows that the He-Ne laser light has a single polarization state and that the polarizer and the analyzer can filter out the light with different polarization directions.
