##### 10. To determine the value of acceleration due to gravity (g) using compound pendulum.

- A compound pendulum is a rigid body that can oscillate about a horizontal axis passing through it.
- The time period of a compound pendulum depends on its length, mass, and distance of the center of mass from the axis of oscillation.
- The value of acceleration due to gravity (g) can be determined by measuring the time period of a compound pendulum for different values of the distance of the center of mass from the axis of oscillation.
- The formula for the time period of a compound pendulum is:

  T = 2π √(I/mgd)

  where T is the time period, I is the moment of inertia of the pendulum about the axis of oscillation, m is the mass of the pendulum, g is the acceleration due to gravity, and d is the distance of the center of mass from the axis of oscillation.

- The formula for the moment of inertia of a compound pendulum about the axis of oscillation is:

  I = Icm + md^2

  where Icm is the moment of inertia of the pendulum about its center of mass.

- To determine the value of g using a compound pendulum, the following steps are followed:

  - A compound pendulum of known mass and length is suspended from a rigid support such that it can oscillate freely about a horizontal axis passing through it.
  - The distance of the center of mass of the pendulum from the axis of oscillation is measured and recorded as d.
  - The time taken for 20 oscillations of the pendulum is measured using a stopwatch and recorded as t.
  - The time period of the pendulum is calculated by dividing t by 20 and recorded as T.
  - The moment of inertia of the pendulum about the axis of oscillation is calculated using the formula I = Icm + md^2 and recorded as I.
  - The value of g is calculated using the formula g = 4π^2I/mTd^2 and recorded as g.
  - The experiment is repeated for different values of d and the corresponding values of T, I, and g are recorded in a table.
  - A graph of T^2 versus d is plotted and the slope of the graph is calculated.
  - The value of g is also calculated using the formula g = 4π^2/slope and compared with the values obtained from the table.
  - The sources of error and the precautions taken to minimize them are discussed.
