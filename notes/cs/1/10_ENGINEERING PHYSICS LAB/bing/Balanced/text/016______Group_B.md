#### Group B

- Group B is one of the four groups of the periodic table, consisting of elements with four valence electrons.
- The elements in group B are carbon (C), silicon (Si), germanium (Ge), tin (Sn), lead (Pb), and flerovium (Fl).
- The elements in group B have diverse physical and chemical properties, ranging from nonmetals (C) to metalloids (Si, Ge) to metals (Sn, Pb, Fl).
- The elements in group B are important for various applications, such as organic chemistry (C), semiconductors (Si, Ge), alloys (Sn, Pb), and nuclear physics (Fl).
- The elements in group B show a gradual decrease in electronegativity, ionization energy, and melting point from top to bottom, and an increase in atomic radius, metallic character, and reactivity with acids.
