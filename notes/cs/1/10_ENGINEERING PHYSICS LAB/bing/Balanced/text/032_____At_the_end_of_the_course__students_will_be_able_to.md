### At the end of the course, students will be able to

- Identify and explain the main concepts and principles of the subject matter.
- Apply the acquired knowledge and skills to solve problems and perform tasks related to the course objectives.
- Analyze and evaluate information from various sources and perspectives using critical thinking and reasoning skills.
- Communicate effectively and professionally using appropriate language and formats.
- Demonstrate ethical awareness and responsibility in academic and professional contexts.
- Collaborate and cooperate with others in diverse and intercultural settings.
- Reflect on their own learning process and outcomes and identify areas for improvement.
