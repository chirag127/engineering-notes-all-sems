#### Apply

- Apply is a verb that means to put something into action or use, or to request something formally, such as a job, admission, or loan.
- Apply can also mean to spread or attach something onto another surface, such as applying makeup, glue, or paint.
- Apply has several synonyms, such as employ, implement, utilize, administer, enforce, execute, operate, practice, and submit.
- Apply can be used in different contexts and with different prepositions, such as apply for, apply to, apply oneself, and apply something to something.
- Some examples of sentences using apply are:

  - She applied for a scholarship to study abroad.
  - He applied the brakes when he saw the red light.
  - You need to apply yourself more if you want to succeed.
  - How does this rule apply to your situation?
  - She applied a thin layer of moisturizer to her face.
