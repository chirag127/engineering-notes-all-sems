### Engineering Physics Practical- S K Gupta ( Krishna Prakashan Meerut)

- Engineering Physics Practical is a book written by S K Gupta and published by Krishna Prakashan Meerut in 2014.
- The book contains 280 pages and covers various topics related to the practical aspects of engineering physics, such as optics, electronics, mechanics, and thermodynamics.
- The book is designed for undergraduate students of engineering and provides them with the necessary theoretical background and experimental techniques to perform the experiments in the laboratory.
- The book is divided into 12 chapters, each containing a brief introduction, objectives, theory, experimental setup, procedure, observations, calculations, results, and discussion.
- The book also includes several diagrams, graphs, tables, and formulas to illustrate the concepts and procedures.
- The book follows the syllabus of various universities and technical boards of India and is suitable for the students of B.Tech, B.E, B.Sc, and diploma courses.
- The book is available in both print and digital formats and can be purchased online or from the publisher's website .
- Some of the topics covered in the book are:

  - Measurement of wavelength of monochromatic light by Newton's rings method.
  - Determination of refractive index of a prism by spectrometer.
  - Study of polarization of light by Malus' law and Brewster's law.
  - Verification of Stefan's law of radiation.
  - Measurement of specific heat of a solid by method of mixtures.
  - Study of LCR circuit and resonance phenomenon.
  - Measurement of e/m of an electron by Thomson's method.
  - Study of Hall effect and determination of Hall coefficient.
  - Study of photoelectric effect and determination of Planck's constant.
  - Study of transistor characteristics and applications.
  - Study of logic gates and Boolean algebra.
  - Study of operational amplifier and its applications.

- The book is a useful resource for the students who want to learn the practical aspects of engineering physics and enhance their skills and knowledge in the field.
