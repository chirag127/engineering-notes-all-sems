# Engineering Physics-Theory and Practical- Katiyar & Pandey (Wiley India)

- This book is a textbook for engineering students of U. P. Technical University, Lucknow, as per the revised syllabus of Engineering Physics-I (NAS-101), Engineering Physics-II (NAS-201) and Practical (NAS-151/251)   .
- The book covers the topics of mechanics, optics, thermodynamics, quantum mechanics, solid state physics, nuclear physics, and materials science   .
- The book also includes experiments based on the theory topics, with detailed diagrams, procedures, observations, calculations, and results   .
- The book aims to provide a clear and concise explanation of the concepts, with examples, illustrations, and numerical problems   .
- The book also contains objective questions, review questions, and exercise problems at the end of each chapter, to test the understanding of the students   .
- The book is written by A.K. Katiyar and C.K. Pandey, who are professors of physics at Harcourt Butler Technological Institute, Kanpur  .
- The book is published by Wiley India, and has a second edition released in 2017  .
