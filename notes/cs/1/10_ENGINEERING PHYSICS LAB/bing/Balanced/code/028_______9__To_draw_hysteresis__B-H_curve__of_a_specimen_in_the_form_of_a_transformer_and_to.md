##### 9. To draw hysteresis (B-H curve) of a specimen in the form of a transformer and to

- A hysteresis (B-H) curve is a graph that shows how the magnetic flux density (B) of a material changes with the applied magnetic field intensity (H).
- A transformer is a device that transfers electrical energy from one circuit to another by electromagnetic induction. It consists of two or more coils of wire wrapped around a magnetic core.
- The magnetic core of a transformer is subjected to alternating magnetic fields as the input voltage and current change. This causes the core to undergo a cycle of magnetization and demagnetization, which is represented by a hysteresis loop on the B-H curve.
- The shape and size of the hysteresis loop depend on the properties of the core material, such as its permeability, coercivity, and saturation. Different materials have different B-H curves, which affect the performance and efficiency of the transformer.
- To draw the hysteresis (B-H) curve of a specimen in the form of a transformer, the following steps are required:

  - Connect the primary coil of the transformer to an AC voltage source and the secondary coil to a load resistor and an oscilloscope.
  - Measure the input voltage and current of the primary coil and the output voltage and current of the secondary coil using the oscilloscope.
  - Calculate the magnetic field intensity (H) of the core using the formula H = N * I / l, where N is the number of turns of the primary coil, I is the input current, and l is the length of the core.
  - Calculate the magnetic flux density (B) of the core using the formula B = V * N / A * f, where V is the output voltage, N is the number of turns of the secondary coil, A is the cross-sectional area of the core, and f is the frequency of the AC source.
  - Plot the values of B and H on a graph paper or a computer software, and draw a smooth curve that passes through the points. This is the hysteresis (B-H) curve of the specimen.
  - Observe the shape and size of the hysteresis loop and compare it with the B-H curves of different materials. The area of the loop represents the energy loss due to hysteresis in the core, which reduces the efficiency of the transformer.
