##### 6. To study the resonance condition of a series LCR circuit.

- A series LCR circuit consists of an inductor (L), a capacitor (C) and a resistor (R) connected in series with an alternating voltage source (V).
- The current (I) in the circuit is the same for all the components, but the voltages across them are different and depend on their reactances (X_L and X_C) and resistance (R).
- The reactance of an inductor is given by X_L = 2πfL, where f is the frequency of the source and L is the inductance of the coil.
- The reactance of a capacitor is given by X_C = 1/(2πfC), where C is the capacitance of the capacitor.
- The total impedance (Z) of the circuit is given by Z = R + j(X_L - X_C), where j is the imaginary unit.
- The impedance is a complex quantity that has a magnitude and a phase angle. The magnitude of the impedance is given by |Z| = √(R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2) and the phase angle is given by tan(φ) = (X_L - X_C)/R, where φ is the angle between the current and the voltage.
- The resonance condition of a series LCR circuit is when the reactance of the inductor is equal to the reactance of the capacitor, i.e., X_L = X_C. This implies that the impedance is purely resistive and has a minimum value of R. The phase angle is zero and the current is in phase with the voltage.
- The resonance frequency (f_0) of the circuit is given by f_0 = 1/(2π√(LC)), where L and C are the inductance and capacitance of the circuit respectively.
- At resonance, the power dissipated in the circuit is maximum and equal to P = I^2R, where I is the current and R is the resistance.
- The quality factor (Q) of the circuit is a measure of how sharp the resonance is. It is given by Q = X_L/R = X_C/R = 1/(2πf_0RC), where X_L, X_C, R and f_0 are the reactance of the inductor, the reactance of the capacitor, the resistance and the resonance frequency of the circuit respectively.
- The quality factor is inversely proportional to the bandwidth (Δf) of the circuit, which is the range of frequencies for which the power dissipation is at least half of the maximum value. The bandwidth is given by Δf = f_0/Q, where f_0 and Q are the resonance frequency and the quality factor of the circuit respectively.
