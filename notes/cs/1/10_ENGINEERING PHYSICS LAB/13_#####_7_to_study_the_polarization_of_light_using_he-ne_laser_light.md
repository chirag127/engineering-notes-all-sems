##### 7. To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light.

To study the polarization of light using He-Ne laser light, you need the following steps:
1. Obtain a He-Ne laser light source
2. Place a polarizer in front of the laser light source
3. Adjust the angle of the polarizer to change the polarization of the light
4. Observe the effect of the polarizer on the light intensity
5. Repeat step 3 and 4 for different angles of the polarizer
6. Plot the relationship between the angle of the polarizer and the light intensity
7. Analyze the results to determine the polarization of the light.
