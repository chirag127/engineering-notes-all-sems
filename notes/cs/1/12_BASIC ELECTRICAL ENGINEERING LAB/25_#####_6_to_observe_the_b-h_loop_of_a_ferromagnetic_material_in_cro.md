##### 6. To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in CRO.

1. Obtain a ferromagnetic material sample and connect it to the CRO.
2. Apply a DC current to the sample to magnetize it.
3. Plot the magnetic field (B) vs. current (I) to obtain the B-H loop.
4. Increase the current to the sample to reach saturation magnetization.
5. Decrease the current to observe the hysteresis loop.
6. Observe and measure the loop shape, including the remanence, coercive field, and saturation magnetization.
