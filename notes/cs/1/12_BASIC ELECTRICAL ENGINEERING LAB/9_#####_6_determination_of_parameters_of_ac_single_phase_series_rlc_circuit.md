##### 6. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit

To determine the parameters of an AC single phase series RLC circuit, the following steps are taken:
1. Obtain the circuit diagram and determine the values of R, L, C.
2. Measure the supply voltage and frequency.
3. Measure the current and voltage across each component.
4. Use Ohm's law and Kirchhoff's laws to find the impedance of each component.
5. Calculate the total impedance of the circuit.
6. Determine the phase angle between the supply voltage and the current.
7. Use the impedance and phase angle to find the magnitude of the impedance.
8. Finally, use the magnitude and phase angle to find the values of the resistance, inductive reactance, and capacitive reactance.
