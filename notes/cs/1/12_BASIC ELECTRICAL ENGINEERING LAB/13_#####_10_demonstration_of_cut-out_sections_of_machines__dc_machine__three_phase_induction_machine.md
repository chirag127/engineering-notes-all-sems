##### 10. Demonstration of cut-out sections of machines: dc machine, three phase induction machine, single-phase induction machine and synchronous machine.

1. DC Machine: A cut-out section of a DC machine will show the stator and rotor components, including the field winding and armature winding, as well as the commutator and brushes.

2. Three-Phase Induction Machine: A cut-out section of a three-phase induction machine will show the stator and rotor components, including the stator winding and rotor winding, as well as the bearing system.

3. Single-Phase Induction Machine: A cut-out section of a single-phase induction machine will show the stator and rotor components, including the stator winding, rotor winding, and centrifugal switch.

4. Synchronous Machine: A cut-out section of a synchronous machine will show the stator and rotor components, including the stator winding and rotor winding, as well as the bearing system and the excitation system.
