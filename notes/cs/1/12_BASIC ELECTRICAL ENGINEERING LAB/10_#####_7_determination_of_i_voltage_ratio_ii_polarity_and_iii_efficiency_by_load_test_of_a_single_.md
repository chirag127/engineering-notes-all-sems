##### 7. Determination of (i) Voltage ratio (ii) polarity and (iii) efficiency by load test of a single phase Transformer

Determining the voltage ratio, polarity, and efficiency of a single-phase transformer can be done by performing a load test. The load test involves applying a known load to the transformer and measuring the voltage and current inputs and outputs. The following steps can be followed to determine the voltage ratio, polarity, and efficiency of a single-phase transformer:

1. Connect the primary and secondary windings of the transformer to a power source and a load, respectively.

2. Measure the voltage and current inputs to the primary winding and the voltage and current outputs from the secondary winding.

3. Calculate the voltage ratio by dividing the secondary voltage by the primary voltage.

4. Determine the polarity of the secondary winding by observing the direction of the current flow in the secondary winding relative to the primary winding.

5. Calculate the efficiency of the transformer by dividing the output power by the input power and multiplying by 100%.

6. Repeat the test several times to obtain an average value for the voltage ratio, polarity, and efficiency.

7. Compare the average values obtained with the known values for the transformer to determine the accuracy of the test.

The load test is a simple and effective way to determine the voltage ratio, polarity, and efficiency of a single-phase transformer, and provides valuable information about the performance and suitability of the transformer for various applications.
