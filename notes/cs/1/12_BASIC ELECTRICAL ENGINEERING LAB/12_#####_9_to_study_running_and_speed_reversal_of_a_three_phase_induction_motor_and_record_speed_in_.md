##### 9. To study running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions.

Study of Running and Speed Reversal of a Three-Phase Induction Motor:

A three-phase induction motor is a type of electric motor that operates on the principle of electromagnetic induction. The motor can be made to run in either direction by reversing the direction of the current in the stator windings.

1. Equipment setup: A three-phase induction motor, a power supply, and a speed measurement device (e.g. tachometer) are required to perform this experiment. The motor should be connected to the power supply, and the speed measurement device should be connected to the motor shaft.

2. Running the motor in one direction: The motor should be started in one direction by applying power to the stator windings. The speed of the motor should be measured and recorded.

3. Reversing the direction of the current: The direction of the current in the stator windings should be reversed to change the direction of rotation of the motor. The speed of the motor should be measured and recorded in the new direction.

4. Comparison of speed in both directions: The speeds of the motor in both directions should be compared and the results should be recorded. The speed of the motor in one direction may be different from the speed in the other direction due to differences in the load on the motor.

5. Analysis: The results of the experiment should be analyzed to determine the effect of reversing the direction of the current on the speed of the motor. The results should be compared to the theoretical values to determine the accuracy of the experiment.

In conclusion, the study of running and speed reversal of a three-phase induction motor involves starting the motor in one direction, reversing the direction of the current in the stator windings, and measuring the speed of the motor in both directions. The results of the experiment can be used to determine the effect of reversing the direction of the current on the speed of the motor, and to compare the results to the theoretical values.
