
#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab

1. Virtual labs can provide users with a wide range of experiments and activities, such as: 
    * Physics: simulations of physical phenomena, such as light refraction, electrical circuits and the Doppler effect. 
    * Chemistry: simulations of chemical reactions, such as the combustion of hydrocarbons and the reaction of acids and bases. 
    * Biology: simulations of biological processes, such as cell division and photosynthesis.
2. Virtual labs can also provide users with access to laboratory equipment, such as microscopes and spectrometers, which can be used to conduct experiments. 
3. Virtual labs can provide users with access to data analysis tools, such as graphing calculators and statistical software. 
4. Virtual labs can also provide users with access to simulations of real-world experiments, such as the famous Millikan Oil Drop experiment. 
5. By using virtual labs, users can gain experience with laboratory techniques, data analysis and scientific inquiry without the need for expensive equipment or hazardous materials.
