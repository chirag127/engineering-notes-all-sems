

##### 5. Measurement of power in 3-phase circuit by two-wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load

1. In a 3-phase system, the power can be measured by two-wattmeter method.
2. This method is based on the measurement of power in each phase of the system.
3. The wattmeter used in the two-wattmeter method is connected in the star or delta connection of the load.
4. The wattmeter measures the power in each phase and the total power of the system is calculated by adding the power in each phase.
5. The power factor of the system can also be determined using the two-wattmeter method.
6. The power factor of the system is calculated by dividing the total power in the system by the total apparent power in the system.
7. The power factor of the system can be calculated for both star and delta connected loads.
