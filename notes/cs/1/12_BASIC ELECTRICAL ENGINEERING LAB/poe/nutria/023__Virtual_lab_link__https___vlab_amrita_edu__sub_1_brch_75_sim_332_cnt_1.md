

##### Introduction to Virtual Lab

* Virtual Lab is an online platform that provides access to simulations and experiments in various fields of science and engineering.
* It is developed by Amrita University and allows students to perform experiments and simulations in a virtual environment.
* The platform is designed to be interactive and user-friendly, making it easy for students to understand the concepts and principles behind the experiments.
* The Virtual Lab platform provides access to a wide range of simulations and experiments in fields such as electronics, electrical engineering, computer science, physics, chemistry, and biology.
* The simulations and experiments can be accessed through the Virtual Lab link provided above.
* The experiments and simulations are designed to help students understand the concepts in an interactive and engaging manner.
* The platform also provides access to tutorials and other resources to help students understand the experiments better.
* The Virtual Lab platform is free to use and can be accessed from any device with an internet connection.
