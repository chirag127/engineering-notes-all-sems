

## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

1. Boyle's Law: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the pressure and volume of a gas. It was first proposed by Robert Boyle in 1662.

2. Charles's Law: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the volume and temperature of a gas. It was first proposed by Jacques Charles in 1787.

3. Avogadro's Law: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the volume and number of moles of a gas. It was first proposed by Amedeo Avogadro in 1811.

4. Ideal Gas Law: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the pressure, volume, temperature, and number of moles of a gas. It was first proposed by Emile Clapeyron in 1834.

5. Faraday's Law of Electrolysis: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the quantity of a substance deposited at an electrode and the amount of electric current passed through an electrolyte. It was first proposed by Michael Faraday in 1833.

6. Ohm's Law: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the voltage and current of an electrical circuit. It was first proposed by Georg Ohm in 1827.

7. Bernoulli's Principle: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the pressure and velocity of a fluid. It was first proposed by Daniel Bernoulli in 1738.

8. Pascal's Law: This is an experiment to determine the relationship between the pressure and height of a fluid. It was first proposed by Blaise Pascal in 1647.
