
##### 5. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit

1. The parameters of a single-phase series RLC circuit can be determined by applying the following equations:
    - Inductive Reactance: $$X_L = 2\pi fL$$
    - Capacitive Reactance: $$X_C = \frac{1}{2\pi fC}$$
    - Total Reactance: $$X_{T} = X_L + X_C$$
    - Impedance: $$Z = \sqrt{R^2 + X_{T}^2}$$
    - Phase Angle: $$\theta = \tan^{-1}\frac{X_T}{R}$$
    - Apparent Power: $$S = V \times I$$
    - Active Power: $$P = V \times I \times \cos \theta$$
    - Reactive Power: $$Q = V \times I \times \sin \theta$$
2. The parameters of a series RLC circuit can also be determined by using the phasor diagram. The phasor diagram is a graphical representation of the circuit parameters where the magnitude and phase angle of each component are represented by vectors. The magnitude of the vectors is determined by the equations mentioned above and the phase angle is determined by the relative position of the components in the circuit.
3. The parameters of a series RLC circuit can also be determined by using the mesh analysis method. In this method, the mesh currents are determined by applying Kirchhoff's current law and then the circuit parameters can be determined by applying the equations mentioned above.
