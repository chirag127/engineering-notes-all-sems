
##### 4. Connection and Measurement of Power Consumption of a Fluorescent Lamp (Tube Light)

1. A fluorescent lamp (tube light) is an electric light that uses electricity to excite mercury vapor in a tube, which produces ultraviolet light. 
2. This ultraviolet light then causes a phosphor to fluoresce, producing visible light.
3. Fluorescent lamps are more efficient than incandescent lamps, and can last up to 10 times longer.
4. To connect a fluorescent lamp, the lamp is inserted into the holder and the holder is connected to the power supply.
5. The power consumption of a fluorescent lamp can be measured using a wattmeter.
6. The wattmeter is connected in series with the lamp, and the power consumption of the lamp can be read from the wattmeter.
