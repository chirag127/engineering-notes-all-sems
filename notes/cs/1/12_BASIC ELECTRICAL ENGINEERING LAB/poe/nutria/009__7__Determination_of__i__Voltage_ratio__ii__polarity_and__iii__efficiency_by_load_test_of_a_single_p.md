
##### 7. Determination of (i) Voltage ratio (ii) polarity and (iii) efficiency by load test of a single phase Transformer

1. Voltage ratio: The voltage ratio of a single phase transformer can be determined by connecting it to a variable voltage supply and measuring the voltage at the primary and secondary windings. 

2. Polarity: The polarity of the transformer can be determined by connecting it to a variable voltage supply and measuring the voltage across the primary and secondary windings. The polarity of the transformer can also be determined by connecting it to an ammeter and observing the direction of the current flow.

3. Efficiency: The efficiency of a single phase transformer can be determined by connecting it to a variable voltage supply and measuring the input and output power. The efficiency is then calculated as the output power divided by the input power.
