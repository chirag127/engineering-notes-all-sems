
##### 6. To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in CRO

1. A ferromagnetic material is a material that is strongly affected by a magnetic field.
2. The B-H loop is a graph that represents the relationship between the magnetizing field (H) and the magnetic flux density (B) of a ferromagnetic material.
3. The B-H loop is typically constructed by applying a range of increasing and decreasing magnetic fields to a ferromagnetic material and recording the corresponding magnetic flux density.
4. The B-H loop can be observed using a Cathode Ray Oscilloscope (CRO).
5. To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material using a CRO, the ferromagnetic material is placed in the magnetic field of the CRO.
6. The CRO is then used to measure the magnetic field and the corresponding magnetic flux density.
7. The resulting data is plotted on a graph to form the B-H loop.
8. The shape of the B-H loop can be used to determine the magnetic properties of the ferromagnetic material.
