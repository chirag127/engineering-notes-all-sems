

##### CO 4 Calculate efficiency of a single phase transformer and DC machine. K4

1. Efficiency of a single phase transformer is the ratio of output power to input power, expressed as a percentage.

2. The efficiency of a DC machine can be calculated by dividing the output power by the input power and then multiplying the result by 100.

3. The single phase transformer efficiency is affected by the load, the voltage, the frequency, the temperature, and the age of the transformer.

4. The DC machine efficiency is affected by the load, the speed, the temperature, and the age of the machine.

5. In order to maximize the efficiency of a single phase transformer, it is important to ensure that the load is balanced, the voltage is correct, the frequency is correct, the temperature is correct, and the transformer is not too old.

6. In order to maximize the efficiency of a DC machine, it is important to ensure that the load is balanced, the speed is correct, the temperature is correct, and the machine is not too old.
