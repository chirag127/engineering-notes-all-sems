

##### 6. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit

1. The ac single phase series RLC circuit consists of a resistor (R), an inductor (L) and a capacitor (C) connected in series.
2. The circuit can be used to measure the parameters of an ac signal, such as the frequency, the peak voltage, the peak current and the phase angle between the voltage and the current.
3. The frequency of the ac signal is determined by the ratio of the inductive reactance (XL) to the capacitive reactance (XC).
4. The peak voltage is determined by the total impedance of the circuit (ZT).
5. The peak current is determined by the total impedance of the circuit and the peak voltage.
6. The phase angle between the voltage and the current is determined by the difference between the inductive reactance and the capacitive reactance.
