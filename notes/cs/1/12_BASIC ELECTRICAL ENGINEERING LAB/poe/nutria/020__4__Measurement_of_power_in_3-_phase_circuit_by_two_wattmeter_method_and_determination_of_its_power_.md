
##### 4. Measurement of power in 3-phase circuit by two wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.
1. Power in three-phase circuit can be measured using two wattmeter method.
2. For star connected load, the two wattmeters measure the power in two of the three phases.
3. For delta connected load, the two wattmeters measure the power in two of the three lines.
4. The power factor of the circuit is determined by the ratio of the power measured by the two wattmeters.
5. The power factor is the ratio of the active power to the apparent power.
6. The active power is the power measured by the wattmeters, while the apparent power is the product of the voltage and current.
