

##### CO 2 Demonstrate the behavior of AC circuits connected to single phase AC supply and measure power in single phase as well as three phase electrical circuits. K4

1. AC circuits are electrical circuits that use alternating current. This type of current flows in one direction, then reverses and flows in the opposite direction.

2. AC circuits are typically connected to a single-phase AC supply, which is a type of alternating current power source. This power source is often used in residential and commercial applications.

3. In order to measure the power of an AC circuit, it is necessary to measure both the voltage and the current. This can be done using a multimeter.

4. Power in single-phase AC circuits can be calculated using the formula P = VI, where P is the power, V is the voltage, and I is the current.

5. Three-phase AC circuits are more complex than single-phase AC circuits. In order to measure the power of a three-phase AC circuit, it is necessary to measure the voltage and current in each of the three phases.

6. Power in three-phase AC circuits can be calculated using the formula P = 3VIcosΦ, where P is the power, V is the voltage, I is the current, and Φ is the phase angle.
