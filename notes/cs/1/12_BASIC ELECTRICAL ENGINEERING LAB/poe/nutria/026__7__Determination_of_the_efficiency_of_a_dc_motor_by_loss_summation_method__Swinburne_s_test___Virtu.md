

##### 7. Determination of the Efficiency of a DC Motor by Loss Summation Method (Swinburne's Test)

1. The Swinburne's Test is a method of determining the efficiency of a DC motor by summing up the losses.
2. The efficiency of the DC motor is calculated by measuring the power input to the motor and the power output from the motor.
3. The losses in the motor can be divided into two categories: losses due to friction and losses due to electrical resistance.
4. The losses due to friction are calculated by measuring the torque and speed of the motor.
5. The losses due to electrical resistance are calculated by measuring the current and voltage of the motor.
6. The total losses are calculated by summing up the losses due to friction and the losses due to electrical resistance.
7. The efficiency of the motor is calculated by subtracting the total losses from the power input to the motor.
