
##### 9. To study running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor and record speed in both directions

1. A three phase induction motor is an AC motor in which the stator windings are connected to a three phase AC power supply and the rotor is given a rotating magnetic field to produce torque.

2. The speed of the motor is determined by the frequency of the AC power supply, and the direction of rotation is determined by the phase sequence of the supply.

3. To study the running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor, the following steps should be followed:

a. Connect the stator windings of the motor to a three phase AC power supply.

b. Change the phase sequence of the AC power supply to reverse the direction of rotation.

c. Measure the speed of the motor in both directions and record the results.

d. Calculate the efficiency of the motor in both directions.

4. By studying the running and speed reversal of a three phase induction motor, it is possible to gain insight into the performance of the motor and to identify any problems or areas for improvement.
