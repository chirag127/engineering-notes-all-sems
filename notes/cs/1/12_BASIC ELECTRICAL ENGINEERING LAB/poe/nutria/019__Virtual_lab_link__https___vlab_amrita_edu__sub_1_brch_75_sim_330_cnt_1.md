
##### Virtual Lab

1. Virtual Lab is an online platform that provides access to a range of interactive experiments and simulations in various disciplines.
2. It enables students to gain hands-on experience in a safe and secure virtual environment.
3. The Virtual Lab platform is developed by Amrita University, India, and provides access to experiments and simulations in various disciplines such as Electrical and Electronics, Computer Science, Physics, and Chemistry.
4. It is a great resource for students to gain a better understanding of the concepts and principles they are learning in their respective courses.
5. The platform is easy to use and provides a user-friendly interface.
6. It also provides a variety of tools and resources to help students learn more effectively.
7. The Virtual Lab platform is free to use and provides access to a wide range of experiments and simulations.
