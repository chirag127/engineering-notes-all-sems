
# Virtual Lab

Virtual labs are a great way to experiment with different technologies and gain hands-on experience in a safe and controlled environment. The following link provides access to a virtual lab:

* **URL:** https://vlab.amrita.edu/?sub=1&brch=75&sim=313&cnt=1

This virtual lab provides access to a range of simulations and experiments related to electrical engineering. It is an excellent resource for students to gain a better understanding of the principles and applications of electrical engineering.

The virtual lab includes the following features:

* **Simulation:** A range of simulations related to electrical engineering topics.
* **Experiments:** Experiments that can be conducted in a safe and controlled environment.
* **Data Analysis:** Analyze the data collected from the experiments.
* **Quizzes:** Assess your understanding of the topics with quizzes.
* **Tutorials:** Step-by-step tutorials to help you understand the concepts.
* **Assignments:** Assignments to help you practice and apply the concepts.
