
##### CO 3 Perform experiment illustrating BH curve of magnetic materials. K3

1. BH curves are graphical representations of the relationship between magnetic flux density (B) and magnetic field strength (H).
2. BH curves can be used to measure the magnetic properties of materials, such as their coercivity, remanence, and saturation magnetization.
3. The experiment involves creating a BH curve by plotting the magnetic flux density (B) against the magnetic field strength (H).
4. The experiment requires a source of magnetic field, such as an electromagnet or a permanent magnet, and a device to measure the magnetic flux density, such as a Hall effect sensor.
5. The experiment is conducted by increasing the magnetic field strength (H) from 0 to its maximum value, while measuring the corresponding magnetic flux density (B) at each step.
6. The resulting BH curve can be used to measure the coercivity, remanence, and saturation magnetization of the material being tested.
