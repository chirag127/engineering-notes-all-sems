

##### CO 1 Conduct experiments illustrating the application of KVL/KCL and network theorems to DC electrical circuits. K3

1. Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) states that the sum of the voltages around a closed loop in a circuit is equal to zero.
2. Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL) states that the sum of the currents entering and exiting a node in a circuit is equal to zero.
3. Network Theorems provide a set of equations that allow us to calculate the voltage, current, and power of a circuit.
4. Ohm's Law states that the voltage across a resistor is proportional to the current flowing through it.
5. Thevenin's Theorem states that any linear circuit can be replaced by an equivalent circuit consisting of a voltage source in series with a resistor.
6. Norton's Theorem states that any linear circuit can be replaced by an equivalent circuit consisting of a current source in parallel with a resistor.
7. Superposition Theorem states that the response of a linear circuit to multiple sources can be determined by considering each source separately and then adding the responses together.
8. Maximum Power Transfer Theorem states that the maximum power is transferred from a source to a load when the load resistance is equal to the source resistance.
