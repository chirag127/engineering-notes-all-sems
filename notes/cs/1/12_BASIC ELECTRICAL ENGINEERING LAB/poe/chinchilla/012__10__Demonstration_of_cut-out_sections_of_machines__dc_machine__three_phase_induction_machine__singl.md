##### Introduction 

Cut-out sections of machines are a useful tool for learning about how machines work. By removing parts of the machine, it is possible to see the internal workings and understand how they operate. In this section, we will look at the cut-out sections of various machines, including DC machines, three-phase induction machines, single-phase induction machines, and synchronous machines.

##### DC Machine 

A DC machine is a device that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. It is commonly used in electric vehicles and industrial applications. The cut-out section of a DC machine provides an excellent way to understand the internal workings of the device. Here are the parts you can expect to see in a cut-out section of a DC machine:

- Armature
- Commutator
- Brushes
- Field windings
- Stator

##### Three-Phase Induction Machine 

A three-phase induction machine is an AC electric motor that derives its power from three-phase electrical power. It is commonly used in industrial applications and is known for its high efficiency and reliability. Here are the parts you can expect to see in a cut-out section of a three-phase induction machine:

- Stator
- Rotor
- Windings
- Core

##### Single-Phase Induction Machine 

A single-phase induction machine is an AC electric motor that derives its power from a single-phase electrical power. It is commonly used in household appliances, such as fans, washing machines, and refrigerators. Here are the parts you can expect to see in a cut-out section of a single-phase induction machine:

- Stator
- Rotor
- Windings
- Core

##### Synchronous Machine 

A synchronous machine is a type of AC electric motor that operates at a constant speed. It is commonly used in power plants and large industrial applications. Here are the parts you can expect to see in a cut-out section of a synchronous machine:

- Stator
- Rotor
- Windings
- Core
