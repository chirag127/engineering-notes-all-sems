##### CO 2: Demonstrating the Behavior of AC Circuits Connected to Single Phase AC Supply and Measuring Power in Single Phase as well as Three Phase Electrical Circuits. K4

In this topic, we will learn about the behavior of AC circuits connected to a single-phase AC supply and how to measure power in single-phase as well as three-phase electrical circuits. The following points will be covered in this topic:

1. AC Circuits Connected to Single-Phase AC Supply: 

   - Alternating Current (AC) circuits are those circuits in which the current and voltage change their direction periodically. 
   
   - A single-phase AC supply is a type of AC supply that provides a sinusoidal voltage waveform which changes its polarity after every half cycle. 
   
   - In a single-phase AC circuit, the current and voltage are in phase, i.e., they reach their maximum and minimum values at the same time. 
   
   - The behavior of AC circuits connected to a single-phase AC supply can be studied using various parameters such as voltage, current, power, power factor, etc.

2. Measuring Power in Single-Phase Electrical Circuits:

   - Power in a single-phase electrical circuit can be measured using different methods such as the wattmeter method, ammeter-voltmeter method, etc. 
   
   - The wattmeter method is the most accurate method for measuring power in a single-phase electrical circuit. It consists of two coils, one current coil, and one voltage coil, which are connected in series and parallel with the load, respectively. 
   
   - The ammeter-voltmeter method is a less accurate method for measuring power in a single-phase electrical circuit. In this method, an ammeter is connected in series with the load, and a voltmeter is connected across the load. The power is calculated using the formula P=VIcos(θ), where θ is the phase angle between the voltage and current.

3. Measuring Power in Three-Phase Electrical Circuits:

   - Three-phase electrical circuits are those circuits in which three sinusoidal voltages are generated, with a phase difference of 120 degrees between them. 
   
   - Power in a three-phase electrical circuit can be measured using different methods such as the two-wattmeter method, three-wattmeter method, etc. 
   
   - The two-wattmeter method is the most commonly used method for measuring power in a three-phase electrical circuit. It consists of two wattmeters, which are connected in two of the three phases, and the load is connected to the remaining phase. 
   
   - The three-wattmeter method is a more accurate method for measuring power in a three-phase electrical circuit. In this method, three wattmeters are connected in each of the three phases of the circuit.

In conclusion, this topic deals with the behavior of AC circuits connected to a single-phase AC supply and how to measure power in single-phase as well as three-phase electrical circuits. The understanding of these concepts is essential for anyone working in the field of electrical engineering.
