#### K1 – Remember

- Remembering is the first step in the learning process.
- It involves recalling previously learned information from memory.
- This can be done through repetition and practice.
- Techniques such as mnemonics and visual aids can also be used to aid memory retention.
- It is important to remember key concepts and facts in order to build a solid foundation for further learning.

#### K2 – Understand

- Understanding involves comprehending the meaning of information.
- This can be achieved through reading and analyzing information in context.
- It is important to understand the relationships between different pieces of information.
- Concept mapping and summarizing can be useful techniques for achieving understanding.
- Understanding enables learners to apply and analyze information effectively.

#### K3 – Apply

- Applying involves using knowledge and skills in a new or unfamiliar context.
- This can be achieved through problem-solving, experimentation, and practical application.
- Application enables learners to transfer knowledge and skills to real-world scenarios.
- It is important to practice and develop skills through application.

#### K4 – Analyze

- Analyzing involves breaking down information into its constituent parts and examining their relationships.
- This can be achieved through critical thinking and questioning.
- Analyzing enables learners to identify patterns and connections between different pieces of information.
- It is important to analyze information in order to draw conclusions and make informed decisions.

#### K5 – Evaluate

- Evaluating involves making judgments about the quality or value of information or ideas.
- This can be achieved through assessing evidence and arguments.
- Evaluating enables learners to make informed decisions and form opinions.
- It is important to evaluate information in order to make sound judgments and avoid biases.

#### K6 – Create

- Creating involves using knowledge and skills to generate new ideas, products, or solutions.
- This can be achieved through creativity, innovation, and problem-solving.
- Creating enables learners to apply knowledge and skills in new and meaningful ways.
- It is important to create in order to develop a deeper understanding and appreciation of concepts and ideas.
