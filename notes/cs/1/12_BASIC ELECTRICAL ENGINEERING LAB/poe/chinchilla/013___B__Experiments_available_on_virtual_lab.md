#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab

In virtual labs, you can conduct a variety of experiments without the need for physical equipment. Here are some of the experiments available on virtual labs:

- **Chemistry experiments:** You can simulate chemical reactions, titrations, and calorimetry experiments using virtual labs. These experiments allow you to explore different chemical reactions and their properties.

- **Physics experiments:** Virtual labs also offer physics experiments such as projectile motion, optics, and electricity and magnetism. These experiments allow you to explore different concepts and theories in physics.

- **Biology experiments:** In virtual labs, you can conduct experiments on cells, DNA, and genetics. These experiments allow you to explore the functioning of cells and the principles of genetics.

- **Environmental science experiments:** Virtual labs also offer experiments in environmental science such as air pollution, water pollution, and climate change. These experiments allow you to explore the impact of human activities on the environment.

- **Engineering experiments:** Virtual labs offer engineering experiments such as circuit design, material testing, and mechanical system design. These experiments allow you to explore different engineering concepts and their applications.

- **Computer science experiments:** In virtual labs, you can conduct experiments on programming languages, algorithms, and data structures. These experiments allow you to explore different computer science concepts and their applications.

In conclusion, virtual labs offer a wide range of experiments across various disciplines. These experiments allow you to explore different concepts and theories without the need for physical equipment.
