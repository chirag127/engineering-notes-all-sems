##### 1. Verification of Kirchhoff’s laws.

1. Kirchhoff’s current law: Sum of currents entering a node is zero.
- Take a circuit with multiple branches meeting at a node. Measure currents in each branch entering the node using ammeters. Sum of measured currents will be zero, verifying the law.

2. Kirchhoff’s voltage law: Sum of voltage rises and drops in a closed loop is zero.
- Select a closed loop in the circuit. Measure voltage between two points in each component of the loop using voltmeters. Sum of measured voltage rises and drops will be zero, verifying the law.
