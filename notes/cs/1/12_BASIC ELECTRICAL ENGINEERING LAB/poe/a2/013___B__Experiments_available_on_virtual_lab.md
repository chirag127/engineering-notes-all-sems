#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab:

1. Ohm's law: Measure the voltage across a resistor for different values of current and verify Ohm's law.
2. Resistors in series and parallel: Connect resistors in series and parallel and measure the equivalent resistance.Verify the relationships: 1/R_equivalent = 1/R1 + 1/R2 and 1/R_equivalent = sum of (1/R_i).
3. RC circuits: Charge and discharge a capacitor through a resistor and observe the exponential voltage decay. Measure the time constant and verify that it equals RC.
4. Magnetic field of a current carrying coil: Measure the magnetic field inside a current carrying coil and verify that it depends on the current and number of turns.
5. Electromagnetic induction: Move a magnet through a coil and measure the induced emf. Verify that it depends on the rate of change of magnetic flux and polarity.
6. Half-wave, full-wave rectification: Connect a step-down transformer, diode and load resistor to get DC output. Observe the output waveform for half-wave and full-wave rectification and measure the DC voltage.

The experiments cover fundamental concepts in basic electricity and magnetism. The measurements can be taken directly with sensors and the results can be analyzed to verify theoretical relationships and laws. The virtual lab provides an interactive platform to perform experiments and gain hands-on learning experience.
