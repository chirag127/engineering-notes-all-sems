- The tachometer method uses a device called a tachometer to measure the speed of the motor. It works by measuring the number of revolutions per minute (RPM) of the motor shaft. 
- The slip method is used to calculate the speed of the motor by measuring the difference between the synchronous speed of the motor and its actual speed.

- Reversing the direction of a three-phase induction motor can be achieved by interchanging any two of the three supply lines.

- DC generators convert mechanical energy into electrical energy, while DC motors convert electrical energy into mechanical energy. 
- AC machines are classified into two types: AC generators and AC motors. 
- AC generators convert mechanical energy into electrical energy, while AC motors convert electrical energy into mechanical energy.
