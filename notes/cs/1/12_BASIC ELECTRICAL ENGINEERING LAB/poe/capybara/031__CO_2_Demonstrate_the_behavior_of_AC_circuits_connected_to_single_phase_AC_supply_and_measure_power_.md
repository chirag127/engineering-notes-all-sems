##### AC Circuits Connected to Single Phase AC Supply

AC circuits connected to a single phase AC supply exhibit certain behaviors that can be measured and analyzed. Here are some points to keep in mind:

- AC circuits consist of components such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors that affect the behavior of the circuit.
- The voltage and current in AC circuits are constantly changing and can be represented by sinusoidal waveforms.
- The frequency of the AC supply determines the rate at which the voltage and current oscillate.
- The phase angle between the voltage and current waveforms can affect the power factor of the circuit.
- In single phase AC circuits, the voltage and current waveforms have a phase difference of 0 or 180 degrees.
- Power in single phase AC circuits can be measured using techniques such as the wattmeter method or the two wattmeter method.
- The power factor of a single phase AC circuit can be improved by adding capacitors or inductors to the circuit.

##### Three Phase Electrical Circuits

In three phase electrical circuits, three AC voltage sources are used instead of one. Here are some points to keep in mind:

- Three phase electrical circuits are more efficient than single phase circuits as they require less conductor material and can deliver more power.
- The voltage waveforms in a three phase system are 120 degrees out of phase with each other.
- The current waveforms in a three phase system are also 120 degrees out of phase with each other.
- The power in a three phase system can be measured using the three wattmeter method or the two wattmeter method.
- The power factor of a three phase system can be improved by adding capacitors or inductors to the circuit.
- Three phase electrical circuits are commonly used in industrial and commercial applications.
