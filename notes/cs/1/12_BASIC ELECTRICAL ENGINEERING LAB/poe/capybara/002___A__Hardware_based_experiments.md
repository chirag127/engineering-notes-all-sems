#### (A) Hardware based experiments
- Hardware experiments involve the use of physical components to test and verify the functionality of a system or device.
- These experiments can range from simple circuit testing to complex system integration and testing.
- Hardware experiments are often used in the development of new products or to troubleshoot existing systems.
- One common type of hardware experiment is circuit testing, which involves the use of a circuit board to test the functionality of individual components and their interactions with each other.
- Another type of hardware experiment is system integration testing, which involves the use of multiple hardware components to test the functionality of a larger system.
- Hardware experiments can also be used to test the durability and reliability of a system, such as through stress testing or environmental testing.
- It is important to carefully plan and execute hardware experiments, as they can be expensive and time-consuming.
- Proper safety precautions should always be taken when conducting hardware experiments, such as using protective equipment and following established protocols.
