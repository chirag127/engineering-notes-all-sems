## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

1. To determine the melting point of a substance using a melting point apparatus.
2. To determine the boiling point of a liquid using a boiling point apparatus.
3. To prepare a standard solution of a given solute and determine its concentration using titration.
4. To perform a simple distillation of a mixture of two miscible liquids and determine their relative amounts.
5. To perform a fractional distillation of a mixture of two miscible liquids and determine their relative amounts.
6. To determine the solubility of a solute in a solvent at different temperatures and plot a solubility curve.
7. To prepare a colloidal solution of a given solute and study its properties.
8. To study the effect of concentration on the rate of reaction between sodium thiosulphate and hydrochloric acid.
9. To study the effect of temperature on the rate of reaction between potassium permanganate and oxalic acid.
10. To study the effect of catalyst on the rate of reaction between hydrogen peroxide and potassium iodide.
11. To determine the empirical formula of a compound by the method of combustion.
12. To determine the molecular weight of a volatile liquid by Victor Meyer's method.
13. To determine the surface tension of a liquid by the capillary rise method.
14. To determine the refractive index of a glass prism using a spectrometer.
15. To determine the focal length of a convex lens using a lens formula. 

These experiments are essential for gaining practical knowledge in the field of chemistry. They help in understanding the concepts of chemistry in a better way and provide hands-on experience in performing various laboratory techniques. It is important to perform these experiments under the guidance of a trained professional in a well-equipped laboratory to ensure safety and accuracy of the results.
