##### Study of phenomenon of resonance in RLC series circuit and obtain resonant frequency.

In this topic, we will be studying the phenomenon of resonance in an RLC series circuit and how to obtain the resonant frequency. The RLC series circuit consists of a resistor (R), an inductor (L), and a capacitor (C) connected in series.

The following points will help you understand the phenomenon of resonance in an RLC series circuit:

- Resonance is a phenomenon that occurs when an alternating current (AC) is passed through an RLC series circuit and the circuit reaches a state where the voltage across the capacitor and the current flowing through the circuit are in phase.
- At resonance, the impedance of the circuit is minimum and the current flowing through the circuit is maximum.
- The resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit can be calculated using the formula: 

  `f = 1 / (2π√(LC))`

  where f is the resonant frequency in Hertz (Hz), L is the inductance in Henrys (H), and C is the capacitance in Farads (F).

- The resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit can also be obtained experimentally by varying the frequency of the AC source and measuring the current flowing through the circuit using an ammeter.
- The resonant frequency can be identified by the frequency at which the current flowing through the circuit is maximum.
- The quality factor (Q) of an RLC series circuit is a measure of the sharpness of resonance and is defined as the ratio of the energy stored in the circuit to the energy dissipated in the circuit. 

  `Q = 1 / R √(L / C)`

  where R is the resistance in Ohms (Ω), L is the inductance in Henrys (H), and C is the capacitance in Farads (F).

- The quality factor of an RLC series circuit can be measured experimentally by measuring the bandwidth of the circuit at half-power points.

In conclusion, the study of the phenomenon of resonance in an RLC series circuit and obtaining the resonant frequency is important in understanding the behavior of electrical circuits. The resonant frequency can be calculated using a formula or obtained experimentally, and the quality factor of the circuit can be measured to determine the sharpness of resonance.
