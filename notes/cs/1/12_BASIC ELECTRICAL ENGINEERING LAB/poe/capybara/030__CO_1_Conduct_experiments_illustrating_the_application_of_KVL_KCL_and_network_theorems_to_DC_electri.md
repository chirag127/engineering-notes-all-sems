##### CO 1 Conduct experiments illustrating the application of KVL/KCL and network theorems to DC electrical circuits. K3

In order to understand the application of KVL/KCL and network theorems to DC electrical circuits, it is important to conduct experiments. Here are some experiments that can be conducted to illustrate the application of these concepts:

1. Experiment to illustrate KVL: 
   - Connect a battery, resistor, and voltmeter in series.
   - Measure the voltage across the resistor using the voltmeter.
   - Change the value of the resistor and measure the voltage again.
   - Repeat the experiment for different resistor values.
   - Plot the voltage values against the resistor values.
   - The resulting graph should show a linear relationship, which is in accordance with KVL.

2. Experiment to illustrate KCL:
   - Connect a battery, two resistors, and an ammeter in parallel.
   - Measure the current flowing through each resistor using the ammeter.
   - Change the value of one resistor and measure the current again.
   - Repeat the experiment for different resistor values.
   - The sum of the currents flowing through each resistor should be equal to the total current flowing through the circuit, which is in accordance with KCL.

3. Experiment to illustrate network theorems:
   - Use a circuit with multiple resistors in series and/or parallel.
   - Measure the voltage and current for each resistor.
   - Use Kirchhoff's laws and Ohm's law to calculate the voltage and current for each resistor.
   - Use the superposition theorem to calculate the total voltage and current for the circuit.
   - Use the Thevenin's theorem to calculate the equivalent resistance and voltage of the circuit.
   - Use the Norton's theorem to calculate the equivalent current and resistance of the circuit.
   
By conducting these experiments, students can gain a better understanding of KVL, KCL, and network theorems and how they are applied to DC electrical circuits.
