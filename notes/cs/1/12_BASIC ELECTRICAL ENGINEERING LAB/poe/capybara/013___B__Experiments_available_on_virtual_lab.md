#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab

Virtual labs offer a wide range of experiments that can be conducted online. These experiments are designed to simulate real-life experiments, allowing students to gain practical knowledge and experience without the need for a physical laboratory. Some of the experiments available on virtual labs include:

- Biology experiments: Virtual labs offer a range of biology experiments, including the study of cells, tissues, and organs. These experiments allow students to understand the principles of cell biology, genetics, and physiology.

- Chemistry experiments: Virtual labs offer a range of chemistry experiments, including the study of chemical reactions, the properties of elements, and the behavior of gases. These experiments allow students to understand the principles of chemistry and apply them to real-world situations.

- Physics experiments: Virtual labs offer a range of physics experiments, including the study of mechanics, optics, and electromagnetism. These experiments allow students to understand the principles of physics and apply them to real-world situations.

- Engineering experiments: Virtual labs offer a range of engineering experiments, including the study of circuits, robotics, and materials science. These experiments allow students to understand the principles of engineering and apply them to real-world situations.

- Environmental science experiments: Virtual labs offer a range of environmental science experiments, including the study of ecosystems, climate change, and pollution. These experiments allow students to understand the principles of environmental science and apply them to real-world situations.

In conclusion, virtual labs offer a wide range of experiments that can be conducted online without the need for a physical laboratory. These experiments provide students with practical knowledge and experience, allowing them to understand the principles of science and engineering and apply them to real-world situations.
