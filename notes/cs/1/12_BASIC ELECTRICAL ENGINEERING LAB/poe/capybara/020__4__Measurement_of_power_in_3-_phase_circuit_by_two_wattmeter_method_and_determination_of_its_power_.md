#### 4. Measurement of power in 3-phase circuit by two wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

In a three-phase circuit, power can be measured by using two wattmeters. This method is called the two wattmeter method. In this method, two wattmeters are connected in the circuit, and the readings of these wattmeters are used to calculate the power.

The power factor of the load can also be determined using the two wattmeter method. The power factor is an important parameter that describes the efficiency of the circuit.

The following are the steps for measuring power in a three-phase circuit using the two wattmeter method:

1. Connect the two wattmeters in the circuit. One wattmeter is connected between the first phase and the second phase, and the other wattmeter is connected between the second phase and the third phase.

2. Note down the readings of the two wattmeters.

3. Calculate the total power by adding the readings of the two wattmeters. The formula for calculating the total power is P = W1 + W2.

4. Calculate the power factor of the circuit using the formula PF = P/S, where P is the total power, and S is the apparent power.

5. The above steps can be repeated for both star and delta connected loads.

In conclusion, the two wattmeter method is an effective way to measure power in a three-phase circuit. It is also useful for determining the power factor of the load.
