##### Determination of Parameters of AC Single Phase Series RLC Circuit:

In an AC single-phase series circuit, the circuit components include a resistor, an inductor, and a capacitor connected in series. The circuit's parameters, such as impedance, phase angle, and power factor, can be determined using the following methods:

1. Impedance: The impedance of the circuit can be determined by measuring the voltage across the circuit and the current flowing through it using an oscilloscope. The impedance is given by the following formula:

   Z = V / I

2. Phase Angle: The phase angle between the voltage and current can be determined by measuring the time difference between the voltage and current waveforms on the oscilloscope. The phase angle is given by the following formula:

   Φ = cos⁻¹ (P.F)

3. Power Factor: The power factor of the circuit can be determined by measuring the active power (P) and the apparent power (S) using a wattmeter. The power factor is given by the following formula:

   P.F = P / S
