##### Determination of the Efficiency of a DC Motor by Loss Summation Method (Swinburne's Test)

In this experiment, we will learn about the loss summation method, which is also known as Swinburne's test. By using Swinburne's test, we can determine the efficiency of a DC motor. Here are the steps to perform this experiment:

1. Connect the DC motor to be tested with a generator.

2. Now, connect the generator to the supply mains and switch it on.

3. Measure the voltage and current of the generator and record it.

4. Connect a voltmeter across the armature of the DC motor and measure the voltage.

5. Connect an ammeter in series with the DC motor and measure the current.

6. Record the readings of the voltmeter and ammeter.

7. Repeat the above steps for different loads on the motor.

8. Calculate the input power to the motor by multiplying the measured voltage and current.

9. Calculate the output power of the motor by multiplying the measured voltage and current of the armature.

10. Calculate the power loss due to armature resistance by multiplying the square of the armature current with its resistance.

11. Calculate the stray power loss by subtracting the input power and output power from the total power.

12. Calculate the mechanical power output of the motor by subtracting the stray power loss from the output power.

13. Finally, calculate the efficiency of the motor by dividing the mechanical power output by the input power.

By performing this experiment, we can determine the efficiency of a DC motor using the loss summation method or Swinburne's test. This method is widely used in industries to determine the efficiency of DC motors.
