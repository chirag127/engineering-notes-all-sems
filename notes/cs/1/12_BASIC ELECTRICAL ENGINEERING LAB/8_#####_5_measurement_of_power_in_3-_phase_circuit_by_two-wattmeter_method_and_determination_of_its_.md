##### 5. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two-wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

Measurement of Power in a 3-Phase Circuit by Two-Wattmeter Method:

The measurement of power in a 3-phase circuit is an important task in electrical engineering. The two-wattmeter method is a common method used to measure the power in a 3-phase circuit. This method involves using two wattmeters to measure the power in each of the three phases of the circuit.

1. Circuit setup: A 3-phase circuit should be set up with a load connected to the circuit. The load can be either star-connected or delta-connected.

2. Wattmeter connection: The two wattmeters should be connected in the circuit, with one wattmeter connected in each of the two lines of the circuit. The wattmeters should be connected such that the current flowing through each wattmeter is in the same direction.

3. Power measurement: The power in each of the three phases of the circuit can be determined by reading the readings from the two wattmeters. The power in each phase can be calculated as the product of the voltage and current in that phase.

Determination of Power Factor for Star and Delta Connected Loads:

The power factor of a 3-phase circuit is a measure of the efficiency of the circuit in converting electrical energy into useful work. The power factor can be determined for both star-connected and delta-connected loads.

1. Star-connected load: For a star-connected load, the power factor can be determined by dividing the real power (measured in watts) by the apparent power (measured in volt-amperes).

2. Delta-connected load: For a delta-connected load, the power factor can be determined by dividing the sum of the real powers in each of the three phases by the sum of the apparent powers in each of the three phases.

In conclusion, the measurement of power in a 3-phase circuit by the two-wattmeter method is a common method used to determine the power in each of the three phases of the circuit.
