##### 5. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit.

1. Measurement of resistance (R): Use a multimeter to measure resistance of each component in the circuit.
2. Measurement of inductance (L): Use an LCR meter to measure inductance of the inductor.
3. Measurement of capacitance (C): Use a capacitance meter to measure capacitance of the capacitor.
4. Determination of supply voltage (V): Measure the voltage across the circuit using a multimeter.
5. Calculation of impedance (Z): Use Ohm's law and impedance formula to calculate impedance of the circuit.
