##### 3. Study of phenomenon of resonance in RLC series circuit and obtain resonant frequency.

The phenomenon of resonance in an RLC series circuit refers to the condition where the circuit exhibits maximum energy transfer between the electrical and magnetic components of the circuit. This occurs when the frequency of the applied voltage is equal to the resonant frequency of the circuit.

To obtain the resonant frequency of an RLC series circuit, the following steps can be followed:

1. Construct the circuit using a resistor (R), an inductor (L), and a capacitor (C) connected in series.

2. Apply an AC voltage source to the circuit and measure the current passing through the circuit as a function of frequency.

3. Plot the magnitude of the current as a function of frequency and observe the resonant peak in the plot.

4. The frequency at which the resonant peak occurs is the resonant frequency of the circuit.

5. The resonant frequency can also be calculated using the formula: f_res = 1 / (2 * pi * sqrt(L * C)), where f_res is the resonant frequency, L is the inductance, and C is the capacitance.

6. Compare the calculated resonant frequency with the measured resonant frequency to verify the accuracy of the calculation.

The study of the phenomenon of resonance in an RLC series circuit provides important insights into the behavior of electrical circuits and the interplay between electrical and magnetic components. By understanding the resonant frequency of a circuit, engineers and technicians can design circuits that are optimized for specific applications and requirements.
