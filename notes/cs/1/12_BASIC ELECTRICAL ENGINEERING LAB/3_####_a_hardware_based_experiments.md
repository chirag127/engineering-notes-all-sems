#### (A) Hardware based experiments

(A) Hardware-Based Experiments:

Hardware-based experiments involve the use of physical equipment and instruments to perform experiments and gather data. These experiments are designed to demonstrate the principles of a particular field of study and to provide hands-on experience with the equipment and techniques used in that field.

Some examples of hardware-based experiments include:

1. Measuring the resistance of a wire using an ohmmeter
2. Determining the capacitance of a capacitor using an oscilloscope
3. Measuring the inductance of a coil using an LCR meter
4. Characterizing a diode using a multimeter
5. Measuring the frequency response of a filter using an oscilloscope
6. Characterizing the gain and bandwidth of an amplifier using an oscilloscope
7. Measuring the transfer function of a system using a frequency response analyzer
8. Characterizing the noise figure of a amplifier using a spectrum analyzer
9. Measuring the power output of a radio transmitter using a power meter
10. Characterizing the performance of a motor using a tachometer and oscilloscope.

These experiments provide students with a practical understanding of the principles of electronics and electrical engineering, as well as experience with the equipment and techniques used in the field.
