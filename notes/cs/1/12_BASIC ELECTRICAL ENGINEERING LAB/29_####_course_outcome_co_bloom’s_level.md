#### Course Outcome (CO) Bloom’s Level

Course Outcomes (COs) are statements that describe what a student should know or be able to do by the end of a course. Bloom's Taxonomy is a framework for organizing learning objectives into six levels of increasing complexity: Remembering, Understanding, Applying, Analyzing, Evaluating, and Creating. COs can be aligned with Bloom's levels to specify the level of cognitive complexity required to meet the outcome. For example, a CO at the "Remembering" level might ask students to recall facts, while a CO at the "Creating" level might ask students to design a new product or solution. This alignment helps instructors design assessments and activities that match the desired level of learning, and helps students understand the level of mastery expected of them.
