##### 8. Determination of efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test

- A dc shunt motor is a type of dc motor that has a shunt field winding connected in parallel with the armature winding. The shunt field winding provides a constant magnetic field for the armature to rotate.
- The efficiency of a dc shunt motor is the ratio of the output power to the input power. The output power is the mechanical power delivered by the motor to the load, and the input power is the electrical power supplied to the motor from the source.
- A load test is a method of determining the efficiency of a dc shunt motor by varying the load on the motor and measuring the input and output parameters. The load test can be performed by using a brake drum or a dynamometer to apply a mechanical load on the motor shaft.
- The procedure of the load test is as follows:

  - Connect the dc shunt motor to the dc source and the load device. Also connect the ammeter, voltmeter, wattmeter, and tachometer to measure the input current, input voltage, input power, and speed of the motor respectively.
  - Start the motor and adjust the field rheostat to obtain the rated voltage across the armature terminals.
  - Vary the load on the motor by adjusting the brake drum or the dynamometer and record the readings of the ammeter, voltmeter, wattmeter, and tachometer for different values of load.
  - Calculate the output power of the motor for each load by using the formula: P_out = T * ω, where T is the torque and ω is the angular speed of the motor shaft.
  - Calculate the efficiency of the motor for each load by using the formula: η = P_out / P_in, where P_in is the input power of the motor.
  - Plot the graphs of input power, output power, and efficiency versus load and observe the variation of these parameters with the load.

- The advantages of the load test are:

  - It is a simple and direct method of determining the efficiency of the motor.
  - It gives the actual performance of the motor under different load conditions.
  - It does not require any additional equipment or instruments other than the load device and the measuring devices.

- The disadvantages of the load test are:

  - It consumes a lot of power and causes heating of the motor and the load device.
  - It may not be possible to apply the full load on the motor due to the limitations of the load device or the source.
  - It may not be accurate due to the errors in the measurement of the input and output parameters.
