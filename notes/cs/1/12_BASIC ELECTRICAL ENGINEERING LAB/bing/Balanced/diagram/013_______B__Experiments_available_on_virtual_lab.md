#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab

- Virtual lab is a platform that allows users to perform experiments online without the need for physical equipment or facilities.
- Virtual lab provides a variety of experiments in different domains such as physics, chemistry, biology, engineering, mathematics, etc.
- Some examples of experiments available on virtual lab are:

  - **Physics**: Pendulum, Projectile Motion, Simple Harmonic Motion, Newton's Laws of Motion, etc.
  - **Chemistry**: Acid-Base Titration, Le Chatelier's Principle, Electrochemistry, Chemical Equilibrium, etc.
  - **Biology**: Cell Structure and Function, DNA Extraction, Enzyme Kinetics, Photosynthesis, etc.
  - **Engineering**: Circuit Simulation, Logic Gates, Signal Processing, Control Systems, etc.
  - **Mathematics**: Graph Plotting, Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability, etc.

- Virtual lab experiments are designed to simulate the real-world scenarios and provide interactive feedback and guidance to the users.
- Virtual lab experiments can help users to learn the concepts, principles, and applications of various subjects in a fun and engaging way.
- Virtual lab experiments can also help users to develop their skills in scientific inquiry, problem-solving, and critical thinking.
