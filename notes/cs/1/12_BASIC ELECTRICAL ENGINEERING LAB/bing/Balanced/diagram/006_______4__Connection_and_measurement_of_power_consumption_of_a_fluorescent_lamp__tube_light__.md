- A fluorescent lamp is a type of electric light that uses a gas-filled tube coated with phosphor to produce visible light. The gas inside the tube is ionized by an electric current, which causes the phosphor to emit light.
- A fluorescent lamp requires a ballast to regulate the current and voltage in the circuit. The ballast can be either magnetic or electronic, depending on the type and design of the lamp. The ballast also provides the necessary high voltage to start the lamp.
- The power consumption of a fluorescent lamp depends on the wattage rating of the lamp and the ballast. The wattage rating of the lamp is the amount of power it consumes when operating at its rated voltage and current. The wattage rating of the ballast is the amount of power it dissipates as heat and losses in the circuit.
- The power consumption of a fluorescent lamp can be measured using a wattmeter, which is a device that measures the product of voltage and current in an AC circuit. The wattmeter has two coils, one connected in series with the load and the other connected across the supply voltage. The wattmeter shows the true power in watts, which is the average of the instantaneous power over a cycle.
- To connect and measure the power consumption of a fluorescent lamp, the following steps can be followed:

  - Connect the supply voltage to the input terminals of the ballast. The supply voltage should match the rated voltage of the ballast and the lamp.
  - Connect the output terminals of the ballast to the pins of the lamp. The polarity of the connections should be correct, as indicated by the markings on the ballast and the lamp.
  - Connect the wattmeter in series with the supply voltage and the ballast. The wattmeter should have a suitable range and accuracy for the expected power consumption of the lamp and the ballast.
  - Switch on the supply voltage and observe the reading on the wattmeter. The reading should be close to the sum of the wattage ratings of the lamp and the ballast. If the reading is too high or too low, check the connections and the ratings of the components.
  - Switch off the supply voltage and disconnect the wattmeter and the lamp from the circuit.
