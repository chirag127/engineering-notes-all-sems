##### CO 3 Perform experiment illustrating BH curve of magnetic materials. K3

- The BH curve of a magnetic material is a plot of the magnetic flux density B versus the magnetic field intensity H.
- The BH curve shows the relationship between the magnetization of the material and the applied magnetic field, as well as the hysteresis loop that occurs when the material is magnetized and demagnetized.
- The BH curve can be used to measure the magnetic properties of the material, such as the coercivity, the remanence, the saturation magnetization, and the permeability.
- To perform the experiment, the following steps are required:
  - Prepare a solenoid coil with a known number of turns and a variable current source.
  - Insert a magnetic material sample inside the coil and connect a flux meter to measure the magnetic flux through the sample.
  - Vary the current in the coil and record the corresponding values of the magnetic flux and the magnetic field intensity.
  - Plot the BH curve using the recorded data and identify the hysteresis loop and its parameters.
  - Repeat the experiment with different magnetic material samples and compare their BH curves and magnetic properties.
