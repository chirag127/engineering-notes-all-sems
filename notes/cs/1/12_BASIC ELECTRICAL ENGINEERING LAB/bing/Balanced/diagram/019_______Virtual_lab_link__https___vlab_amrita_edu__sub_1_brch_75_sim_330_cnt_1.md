##### Virtual lab

A virtual lab is a simulation-based laboratory that allows students to perform experiments and learn concepts online, without the need for physical equipment or facilities. Virtual labs can provide remote access, interactive feedback, and personalized learning to students from various disciplines of science and engineering.

Some of the benefits of virtual labs are:

- They can reduce the cost, time, and risk involved in conducting real experiments.
- They can enhance the understanding and retention of concepts by providing visual and auditory cues, animations, and interactive elements.
- They can offer flexibility and convenience to students and teachers, who can access the labs anytime and anywhere with an internet connection.
- They can supplement and complement the existing physical labs, by providing additional experiments, scenarios, and variations that may not be possible or feasible in real labs.
- They can promote inquiry-based learning, by allowing students to explore, manipulate, and test various parameters and variables in the experiments.

One of the examples of virtual labs is the Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham Virtual Lab, which is a collaborative project between Amrita University, Dayalbagh University, NIT Karnataka, and COE Pune. The project aims to provide virtual labs in various domains, such as physics, chemistry, biology, biotechnology, computer science, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, and civil engineering. The project has developed over 220 simulation-based experiments, which are available on the website https://vlab.amrita.edu/.
