# Determination of efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test

- A dc shunt motor is a type of dc motor where the field winding is connected in parallel with the armature winding. This means that the field current is independent of the armature current and the speed is relatively constant.
- The efficiency of a dc shunt motor is the ratio of the output power to the input power. It can be expressed as:

$$\eta = \frac{P_{out}}{P_{in}} = \frac{T \omega}{V I}$$

where $\eta$ is the efficiency, $P_{out}$ is the output power, $P_{in}$ is the input power, $T$ is the torque, $\omega$ is the angular speed, $V$ is the terminal voltage, and $I$ is the total current.
- To determine the efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test, the following steps are followed:

  - Connect the dc shunt motor to a suitable load, such as a brake drum or a dynamometer, and measure the torque and speed of the motor.
  - Measure the terminal voltage and the total current of the motor using a voltmeter and an ammeter.
  - Calculate the output power of the motor using the formula: $P_{out} = T \omega$
  - Calculate the input power of the motor using the formula: $P_{in} = V I$
  - Calculate the efficiency of the motor using the formula: $\eta = \frac{P_{out}}{P_{in}}$
  - Repeat the above steps for different values of load and plot a graph of efficiency versus output power. The maximum efficiency occurs at the point where the slope of the curve is zero.
