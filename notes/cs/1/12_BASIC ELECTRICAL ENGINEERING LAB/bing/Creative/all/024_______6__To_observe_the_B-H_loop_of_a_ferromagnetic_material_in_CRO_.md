##### 6. To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in CRO.

- A B-H loop is a graphical representation of the relationship between the magnetic flux density (B) and the magnetic field intensity (H) of a ferromagnetic material.
- A ferromagnetic material is one that can be magnetized by an external magnetic field and retain some magnetization after the field is removed.
- A CRO (cathode ray oscilloscope) is an instrument that can display the variation of an electrical signal on a screen.
- To observe the B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in CRO, the following steps are followed:

  1. A ferromagnetic material, such as an iron ring, is wound with two coils: a primary coil and a secondary coil. The primary coil is connected to an AC source, which provides the varying magnetic field H. The secondary coil is connected to a galvanometer, which measures the induced EMF due to the changing magnetic flux B.
  2. The CRO is set up with the X-axis input connected to the AC source and the Y-axis input connected to the galvanometer. The CRO is adjusted to obtain a stable and clear display on the screen.
  3. As the AC source is varied, the magnetic field H changes in magnitude and direction. This causes the magnetic flux B to change accordingly, inducing an EMF in the secondary coil. The EMF is proportional to the rate of change of B, and its polarity depends on the direction of B.
  4. The CRO displays the variation of B (Y-axis) with respect to H (X-axis) as a loop-shaped curve on the screen. This is the B-H loop of the ferromagnetic material.
  5. The shape and size of the B-H loop depend on the properties of the ferromagnetic material, such as its coercivity, remanence, saturation, and hysteresis. These parameters can be determined from the B-H loop as follows:

    - Coercivity: The value of H required to reduce B to zero. It is a measure of the resistance of the material to demagnetization.
    - Remanence: The value of B remaining when H is reduced to zero. It is a measure of the residual magnetization of the material.
    - Saturation: The maximum value of B that the material can attain for a given H. It is a measure of the magnetic capacity of the material.
    - Hysteresis: The area enclosed by the B-H loop. It is a measure of the energy loss due to the reversal of magnetization of the material.

  6. The B-H loop of a ferromagnetic material in CRO can be used to study the magnetic behavior and characteristics of the material, and to compare different materials for their suitability in various applications.
