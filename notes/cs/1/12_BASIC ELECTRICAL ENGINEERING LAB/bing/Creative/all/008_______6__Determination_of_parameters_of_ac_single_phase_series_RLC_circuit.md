# 6. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit

- A series RLC circuit is a circuit that consists of a resistor (R), an inductor (L), and a capacitor (C) connected in series to an alternating current (ac) source.
- The parameters of a series RLC circuit are the resistance (R), the inductance (L), the capacitance (C), the voltage (V), the current (I), the frequency (f), the impedance (Z), the phase angle (φ), the power factor (pf), and the power (P).
- The voltage (V) is the peak value of the sinusoidal voltage source. The current (I) is the peak value of the sinusoidal current that flows through the circuit. The frequency (f) is the number of cycles of the ac source per second, measured in hertz (Hz).
- The impedance (Z) is the total opposition to the current flow in the circuit, measured in ohms (Ω). It is given by the formula:

  Z = √(R² + (XL - XC)²)

  where XL is the inductive reactance and XC is the capacitive reactance. XL and XC are given by the formulas:

  XL = 2πfL

  XC = 1 / (2πfC)

- The phase angle (φ) is the angle between the voltage and the current in the circuit, measured in degrees or radians. It indicates the phase difference between the voltage and the current. It is given by the formula:

  φ = tan⁻¹((XL - XC) / R)

- The power factor (pf) is the ratio of the real power to the apparent power in the circuit. It indicates how efficiently the circuit uses the power supplied by the source. It is given by the formula:

  pf = cos(φ)

- The power (P) is the rate of energy transfer in the circuit, measured in watts (W). It is given by the formula:

  P = VIpf

- To determine the parameters of a series RLC circuit, one can use the following steps:

  1. Measure or obtain the values of R, L, C, V, and f.
  2. Calculate XL and XC using the formulas above.
  3. Calculate Z using the formula above.
  4. Calculate I using the formula I = V / Z.
  5. Calculate φ using the formula above.
  6. Calculate pf using the formula above.
  7. Calculate P using the formula above.

- Alternatively, one can use an instrument called an impedance bridge or a vector network analyzer to measure the impedance and the phase angle of the circuit directly, and then calculate the other parameters using the formulas above.
