# K1 – Remember, K2 – Understand, K3 – Apply, K4 – Analyze, K5 – Evaluate, K6 – Create

- These are the six levels of cognitive learning according to Bloom's taxonomy, a framework for classifying educational objectives and outcomes.
- The levels are arranged in a hierarchy, from lower-order to higher-order thinking skills, and represent the degree of complexity and depth of learning.
- The levels are:

  - **K1 – Remember**: This level involves recalling facts, terms, definitions, concepts, or procedures from memory. Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: list, name, define, identify, label, recognize, recall, etc.
  - **K2 – Understand**: This level involves explaining the meaning, interpretation, or summary of information, ideas, or concepts. Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: describe, explain, paraphrase, summarize, illustrate, classify, compare, etc.
  - **K3 – Apply**: This level involves using knowledge, rules, methods, or principles to solve problems or perform tasks in new or familiar situations. Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: apply, use, demonstrate, solve, calculate, implement, etc.
  - **K4 – Analyze**: This level involves breaking down information, ideas, or concepts into parts or components, and examining the relationships, patterns, or connections among them. Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: analyze, compare, contrast, differentiate, distinguish, examine, etc.
  - **K5 – Evaluate**: This level involves making judgments, assessments, or critiques based on criteria, standards, or evidence. Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: evaluate, judge, critique, appraise, argue, justify, etc.
  - **K6 – Create**: This level involves generating, producing, or synthesizing new or original ideas, products, or solutions from existing or given information, ideas, or concepts. Examples of verbs that indicate this level are: create, design, develop, construct, compose, invent, etc.

- The levels can be used to design learning objectives, activities, and assessments that align with the intended cognitive outcomes of instruction.
