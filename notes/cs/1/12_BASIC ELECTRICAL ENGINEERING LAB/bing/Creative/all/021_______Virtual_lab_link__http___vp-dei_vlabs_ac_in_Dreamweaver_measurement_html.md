# Virtual Lab: Dreamweaver Measurement

- A virtual lab is a simulated environment that allows students to perform experiments or activities online without the need for physical equipment or facilities .
- Dreamweaver is a web development software that enables users to create, edit, and manage websites and web applications.
- Measurement is the process of determining the size, quantity, or degree of something using a standard unit or system.

## Objectives

- To learn about the different types of measuring devices used in electrical engineering, such as ammeter, voltmeter, wattmeter, and power factor meter .
- To understand the principles and techniques of measuring electrical power, voltage, current, and power factor in various circuits .
- To practice using Dreamweaver to create and modify web pages that display the results of the virtual lab experiments.

## Procedure

- Access the virtual lab link: http://vp-dei.vlabs.ac.in/Dreamweaver/measurement.html
- Read the instructions and theory sections to familiarize yourself with the concepts and terms related to the virtual lab .
- Choose one of the four experiments from the menu: power measurement, voltage measurement, current measurement, or power factor measurement .
- Follow the steps given in the experiment page to set up the circuit, connect the measuring devices, and observe the readings .
- Record the data and calculations in a table or a spreadsheet .
- Use Dreamweaver to create a web page that displays the data and calculations, as well as a summary and conclusion of the experiment.
- Save and publish the web page using Dreamweaver's built-in features.
- Review and evaluate your web page for accuracy, clarity, and design.
