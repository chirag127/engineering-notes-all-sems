The topic you have chosen is **Virtual Lab**. Here is some information about it:

- Virtual Lab is a project by **Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham** and other partner institutions to provide remote-access to labs in various disciplines of science and engineering .
- Virtual Lab uses state-of-the-art computer simulation technology to create real world environments and problem handling capabilities.
- Virtual Lab consists of simulations, animations, graphics and videos that help students retain the real feel of a laboratory, while conducting the experiment from an internet-enabled computer terminal .
- Virtual Lab covers various domains such as physics, chemistry, biology, biotechnology, computer science, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, etc  .
- Virtual Lab aims to universalize education, enhance the quality of learning, and bridge the gap between institutions that have the physical laboratory and those that do not .
