- Some common types of machines that have cut-out sections are dc machine, three phase induction machine, single-phase induction machine and synchronous machine .
- A dc machine is a machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa using direct current. It has two main parts: the stator and the rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the field windings and the brushes. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the armature windings and the commutator .
- A three phase induction machine is a machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy using alternating current. It has two main parts: the stator and the rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the three phase windings and produces a rotating magnetic field. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the conductors and induces an electromotive force due to the magnetic field .
- A single-phase induction machine is a machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy using single-phase alternating current. It has two main parts: the stator and the rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the single-phase windings and produces a pulsating magnetic field. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the conductors and induces an electromotive force due to the magnetic field .
- A synchronous machine is a machine that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa using alternating current. It has two main parts: the stator and the rotor. The stator is the stationary part that contains the three phase windings and produces a rotating magnetic field. The rotor is the rotating part that contains the permanent magnets or the field windings and rotates at the same speed as the magnetic field .
