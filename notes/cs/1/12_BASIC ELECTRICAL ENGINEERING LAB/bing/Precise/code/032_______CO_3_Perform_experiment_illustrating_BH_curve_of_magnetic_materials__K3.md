##### CO 3 Perform experiment illustrating BH curve of magnetic materials. K3

The BH curve, also known as the hysteresis loop, is a graphical representation of the relationship between the magnetic field strength (H) and the magnetic flux density (B) of a magnetic material. The curve is used to characterize the magnetic properties of a material and can be used to predict its behavior in the presence of an external magnetic field.

To perform an experiment illustrating the BH curve of a magnetic material, the following steps can be followed:

1. Prepare a sample of the magnetic material to be tested. This can be in the form of a ring or a toroid.
2. Set up the test equipment, which typically includes a magnetizing coil, a flux meter, and a power supply.
3. Place the sample inside the magnetizing coil and connect the flux meter to measure the magnetic flux density (B) of the sample.
4. Gradually increase the current in the magnetizing coil to increase the magnetic field strength (H) applied to the sample.
5. Record the values of B and H as the current is increased.
6. Gradually decrease the current in the magnetizing coil to reduce the magnetic field strength (H) applied to the sample.
7. Record the values of B and H as the current is decreased.
8. Plot the recorded values of B and H on a graph to obtain the BH curve of the magnetic material.

The shape of the BH curve can provide valuable information about the magnetic properties of the material, such as its coercivity, remanence, and saturation magnetization. These properties can be used to predict the behavior of the material in the presence of an external magnetic field and can be useful in the design of magnetic devices and systems.
