##### 7. Determination of the efficiency of a dc motor by loss summation method (Swinburne's test)

Swinburne's test is a method used to determine the efficiency of a DC motor. This test is performed by measuring the losses in the motor and then calculating the efficiency using the loss summation method. The following are the steps involved in performing Swinburne's test:

1. The first step is to measure the no-load losses of the motor. This is done by running the motor at its rated speed without any load connected to it. The power input to the motor is measured using a wattmeter.

2. The next step is to measure the copper losses in the armature and field windings. This is done by measuring the current flowing through the windings and the resistance of the windings.

3. Once the no-load losses and copper losses have been measured, the efficiency of the motor can be calculated using the loss summation method. This method involves adding up all the losses in the motor and then subtracting them from the input power to determine the output power.

4. The efficiency of the motor can then be calculated by dividing the output power by the input power and multiplying by 100 to express the result as a percentage.
