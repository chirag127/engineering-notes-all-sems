#### (A) Hardware based experiments

Hardware-based experiments are experiments that involve the use of physical equipment or devices to conduct research. These experiments can be used to test the performance, reliability, and functionality of hardware components, systems, and devices.

Some examples of hardware-based experiments include:

1. Testing the performance of computer processors, memory, and storage devices.
2. Evaluating the reliability and durability of electronic components and systems.
3. Measuring the accuracy and precision of sensors and measurement devices.
4. Assessing the functionality and usability of consumer electronics and appliances.

Hardware-based experiments can provide valuable insights into the design and development of new technologies, as well as help to improve the performance and reliability of existing systems. These experiments can be conducted in a controlled laboratory environment, or in real-world settings to gather data and information under realistic conditions.
