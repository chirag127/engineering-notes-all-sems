##### Virtual Lab

Virtual Lab is an online platform that provides students with the opportunity to conduct experiments and learn about various scientific concepts in a virtual environment. The link provided leads to a virtual lab on the topic of measurement, hosted by the Virtual Labs project at the Dayalbagh Educational Institute.

In this virtual lab, students can learn about the following topics:

1. The importance of measurement in scientific experiments.
2. The different units of measurement and their conversions.
3. The use of various measuring instruments such as rulers, vernier calipers, and micrometers.
4. The concept of precision and accuracy in measurement.
5. The proper techniques for taking measurements and recording data.

This virtual lab provides an interactive and engaging learning experience for students, allowing them to gain a deeper understanding of the topic of measurement. It is a valuable resource for students preparing for exams or for anyone interested in learning more about this fundamental scientific concept.
