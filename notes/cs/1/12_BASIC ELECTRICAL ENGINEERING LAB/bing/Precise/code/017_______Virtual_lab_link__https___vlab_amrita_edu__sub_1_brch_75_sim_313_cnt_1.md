##### Virtual Lab

Virtual labs are online tools that offer a space for students to engage with their subject matter interactively. They can be used for pre-lab preparation, online lab courses, post-lab review, make-up labs, or used with a textbook to bridge the gap between lab and lecture courses. Virtual labs are available in various disciplines of Science and Engineering, including Anatomy & Physiology, Biology (Majors and Non-majors), Botany, Environmental Science, Genetics, Human Biology, Medical Assisting, Microbiology, and Nutrition.

Virtual labs are designed to enthuse students to conduct experiments by arousing their curiosity. This helps them in learning basic and advanced concepts through remote experimentation. Some virtual labs are even available for free and include built-in quizzes, notebooks, and references.

Virtual labs are a great way to provide remote-access to simulation-based Labs and improve the quality of care and education programs . They are a valuable resource for faculty and students in the remote learning context.
