##### 5. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit

An AC single-phase series RLC circuit consists of a resistor (R), an inductor (L), and a capacitor (C) connected in series with an AC voltage source. The parameters of this circuit can be determined using the following steps:

1. **Impedance**: The total impedance (Z) of the circuit can be calculated using the formula Z = R + j(XL - XC), where XL is the inductive reactance and XC is the capacitive reactance. XL can be calculated as XL = 2πfL, where f is the frequency of the AC source and L is the inductance. XC can be calculated as XC = 1/(2πfC), where C is the capacitance.

2. **Current**: The current (I) flowing through the circuit can be calculated using Ohm's law, I = V/Z, where V is the voltage of the AC source.

3. **Voltage**: The voltage across each component can be calculated using Ohm's law. The voltage across the resistor (VR) is VR = IR, the voltage across the inductor (VL) is VL = IXL, and the voltage across the capacitor (VC) is VC = IXC.

4. **Power**: The average power (P) consumed by the circuit can be calculated using the formula P = I^2R.

5. **Phase angle**: The phase angle (φ) between the voltage and the current can be calculated using the formula tan(φ) = (XL - XC)/R.

These are the basic steps to determine the parameters of an AC single-phase series RLC circuit. It is important to note that the values of the parameters may vary depending on the frequency of the AC source.
