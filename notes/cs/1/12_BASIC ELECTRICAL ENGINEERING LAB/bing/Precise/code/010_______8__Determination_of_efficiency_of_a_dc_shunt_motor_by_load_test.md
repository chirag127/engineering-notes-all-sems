##### 8. Determination of efficiency of a dc shunt motor by load test

The efficiency of a DC shunt motor can be determined by conducting a load test. The load test involves applying a load to the motor and measuring its performance under the load. The following steps can be followed to determine the efficiency of a DC shunt motor by load test:

1. Connect the DC shunt motor to a suitable load, such as a brake or a dynamometer.
2. Measure the input power to the motor by measuring the voltage and current supplied to the motor.
3. Measure the output power of the motor by measuring the torque and speed of the motor.
4. Calculate the efficiency of the motor using the formula: Efficiency = (Output Power/Input Power) * 100%
5. Repeat the above steps for different loads and plot the efficiency vs. load curve.

The efficiency of the motor can be determined from the efficiency vs. load curve. The maximum efficiency of the motor occurs at the point where the efficiency vs. load curve is maximum. The efficiency of the motor decreases as the load on the motor increases.
