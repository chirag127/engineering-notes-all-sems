#### At the end of this course, the students should be able to:
- Understand the fundamental concepts and principles of the subject.
- Apply the knowledge and skills acquired in the course to solve problems and make informed decisions.
- Communicate effectively using the language and terminology of the subject.
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals.
- Demonstrate critical thinking and analytical skills.
- Conduct research and present findings in a clear and concise manner.
- Demonstrate ethical and responsible behavior in the application of the subject.
- Continue to learn and adapt to new developments in the field.
