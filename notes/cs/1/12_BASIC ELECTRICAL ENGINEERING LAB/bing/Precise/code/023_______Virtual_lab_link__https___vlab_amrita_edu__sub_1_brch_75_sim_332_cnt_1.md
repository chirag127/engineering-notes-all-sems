##### Virtual Lab

Virtual labs are online tools that offer a space for students to engage with their subject matter interactively. They can be used for pre-lab preparation, online lab courses, post-lab review, make-up labs, or used with a textbook to bridge the gap between lab and lecture courses . Virtual labs are available in various disciplines of Science and Engineering, including Anatomy & Physiology, Biology (Majors and Non-majors), Botany, Environmental Science, Genetics, Human Biology, Medical Assisting, Microbiology, and Nutrition . They are designed to enthuse students to conduct experiments by arousing their curiosity and help them in learning basic and advanced concepts through remote experimentation .

Some benefits of virtual labs include:
- They provide remote-access to simulation-based Labs.
- They can be used in the remote learning context.
- They offer a robust online professional development system.
- They include built-in quizzes, notebooks, and references.
