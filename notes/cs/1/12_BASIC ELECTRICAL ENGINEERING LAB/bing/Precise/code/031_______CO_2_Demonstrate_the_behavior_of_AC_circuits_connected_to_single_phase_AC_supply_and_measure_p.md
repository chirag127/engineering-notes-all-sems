##### CO 2 Demonstrate the behavior of AC circuits connected to single phase AC supply and measure power in single phase as well as three phase electrical circuits. K4

- An AC circuit is an electrical circuit that is powered by an alternating current (AC) source.
- A single-phase AC supply is an electric power supply that uses a single sinusoidal voltage waveform.
- In a single-phase AC circuit, the current and voltage vary sinusoidally with time.
- The behavior of an AC circuit connected to a single-phase AC supply can be analyzed using circuit analysis techniques such as Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Laws, and Thevenin's Theorem.
- Power in a single-phase AC circuit can be measured using a wattmeter, which measures the product of the voltage and current.
- A three-phase electrical circuit is a type of polyphase system that uses three conductors to carry three alternating currents of the same frequency but with a phase difference of 120 degrees.
- Power in a three-phase electrical circuit can be measured using a three-phase wattmeter, which measures the sum of the power in each of the three phases.
- The behavior of AC circuits and the measurement of power in single-phase and three-phase electrical circuits are important concepts in electrical engineering and are commonly studied in courses on circuit analysis and power systems.
