##### 5. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two-wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

- The two-wattmeter method is used to measure the power in a three-phase circuit.
- This method involves the use of two wattmeters, which are connected to measure the power in two of the three phases.
- The power in the third phase is calculated by subtracting the sum of the readings of the two wattmeters from the total power.
- The power factor of the circuit can be determined by calculating the ratio of the total power to the product of the line voltage, line current, and the square root of three.
- This method can be used for both star and delta connected loads.
- The power factor is an important parameter in the analysis of three-phase circuits, as it indicates the efficiency of the circuit in converting electrical energy into useful work.
- A high power factor indicates that the circuit is operating efficiently, while a low power factor indicates that there is a significant amount of reactive power in the circuit, which reduces its efficiency.
