#### (B) Experiments available on virtual lab
There are a variety of virtual lab experiments available online for students to explore and learn from. Some of the virtual lab experiments available are:

1. **Virtual Chemistry Experiments** - A collection of interactive web-based chemistry tutorials .
2. **Mixed Reception** - Solve a mysterious death by gathering clues, interviewing suspects, and analyzing evidence .
3. **The Virtual Lab** - An online simulation of a chemistry lab designed to help students link chemical computations with authentic laboratory chemistry .
4. **PraxiLabs** - Offers virtual experiments that adapt to any learning style, with progress tracking, walkthrough videos, lab manuals, enrichment documents, immediate feedback, MCQ quizzes, and much more .
5. **VRLab Academy** - Offers more than 200 science experiments available on VR and PC, with virtual reality technology to increase the ability to learn through virtual memory .
6. **American Chemical Society** - Offers virtual chemistry and simulations, including the chemistry of aspirin, with four levels of experiments, including synthesis, thin layer chromatography, and reaction conditions .
7. **Mystery Science** - Offers a wealth of virtual science labs, activities, and experiments for students in elementary school, covering topics from weather to astronomy, humans to animals .
8. **Molecular Workbench** - Offers hundreds of virtual science labs for physics, biology, chemistry, and more .
9. **National Science Digital Library** - Offers ChemCollective Virtual Labs, a deep dive into chemistry with virtual labs .
10. **PBS Nova Labs** - A platform for teens and older learners to explore and examine cutting-edge issues .

These are just a few examples of the many virtual lab experiments available online for students to explore and learn from.
