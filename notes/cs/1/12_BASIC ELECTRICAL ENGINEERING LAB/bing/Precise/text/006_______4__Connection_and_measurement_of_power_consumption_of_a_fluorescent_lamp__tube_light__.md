##### 4. Connection and measurement of power consumption of a fluorescent lamp (tube light)

A fluorescent lamp, also known as a tube light, is a low-pressure mercury-vapor gas-discharge lamp that uses fluorescence to produce visible light. To measure the power consumption of a fluorescent lamp, the following steps can be taken:

1. **Connect the lamp to a power source:** The fluorescent lamp should be connected to a power source using the appropriate wiring and connections. Ensure that the connections are secure and that the lamp is properly installed.

2. **Measure the current:** Use a clamp meter to measure the current flowing through the circuit. Place the clamp around one of the wires connected to the lamp and take the reading.

3. **Measure the voltage:** Use a multimeter to measure the voltage across the lamp. Place the probes of the multimeter on the appropriate points in the circuit and take the reading.

4. **Calculate the power consumption:** The power consumption of the lamp can be calculated by multiplying the current and voltage readings. The result will be the power consumption in watts.

It is important to note that the power consumption of a fluorescent lamp may vary depending on factors such as the age of the lamp, the temperature of the environment, and the quality of the power supply. Regularly measuring the power consumption of a fluorescent lamp can help to identify any changes in its performance and efficiency.
