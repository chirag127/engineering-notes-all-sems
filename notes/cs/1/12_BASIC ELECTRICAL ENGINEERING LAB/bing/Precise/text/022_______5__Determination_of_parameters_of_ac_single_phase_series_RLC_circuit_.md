##### 5. Determination of parameters of ac single phase series RLC circuit

An AC single-phase series RLC circuit consists of a resistor (R), an inductor (L), and a capacitor (C) connected in series with an AC voltage source. The parameters of this circuit can be determined using the following steps:

1. **Determine the impedance (Z)**: The impedance of the circuit is the total opposition to the flow of current. It is calculated using the formula Z = √(R^2 + (XL - XC)^2), where XL is the inductive reactance and XC is the capacitive reactance.

2. **Determine the current (I)**: The current flowing through the circuit can be calculated using Ohm's law, I = V/Z, where V is the applied voltage.

3. **Determine the voltage across each component**: The voltage across each component can be calculated using Ohm's law, VR = IR, VL = IL, and VC = IC, where VR, VL, and VC are the voltages across the resistor, inductor, and capacitor, respectively.

4. **Determine the power factor**: The power factor is the ratio of the real power to the apparent power. It can be calculated using the formula PF = cos(Φ), where Φ is the phase angle between the voltage and current.

5. **Determine the resonance frequency**: The resonance frequency is the frequency at which the inductive reactance and capacitive reactance are equal. It can be calculated using the formula f = 1/(2π√(LC)).

These are the basic steps to determine the parameters of an AC single-phase series RLC circuit. It is important to note that the values of the parameters may vary depending on the frequency of the applied voltage.
