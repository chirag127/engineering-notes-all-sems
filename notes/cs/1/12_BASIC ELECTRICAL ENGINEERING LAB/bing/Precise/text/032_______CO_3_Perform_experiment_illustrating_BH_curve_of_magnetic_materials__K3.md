##### CO 3 Perform experiment illustrating BH curve of magnetic materials. K3

The BH curve, also known as the hysteresis loop, is a graphical representation of the relationship between the magnetic field strength (H) and the magnetic flux density (B) of a magnetic material. The curve is used to illustrate the magnetic properties of a material, including its magnetization, saturation, and coercivity.

To perform an experiment illustrating the BH curve of magnetic materials, the following steps can be followed:

1. Prepare a sample of the magnetic material to be tested. This can be in the form of a ring or a toroid.
2. Set up the test equipment, including a magnetometer, a power supply, and an oscilloscope.
3. Apply an alternating current to the sample to create a magnetic field.
4. Measure the magnetic flux density (B) of the sample using the magnetometer.
5. Plot the magnetic field strength (H) against the magnetic flux density (B) to create the BH curve.
6. Observe the shape of the curve and analyze the magnetic properties of the material, including its magnetization, saturation, and coercivity.

This experiment can be used to illustrate the magnetic properties of different materials and to compare their behavior under different conditions. It is an important tool in the study of magnetism and magnetic materials.
