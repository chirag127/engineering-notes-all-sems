##### CO 2 Demonstrate the behavior of AC circuits connected to single phase AC supply and measure power in single phase as well as three phase electrical circuits. K4

CO2 is a colorless, odorless gas that is the primary greenhouse gas emitted through human activities. It traps heat in the atmosphere, leading to global warming and climate change. 

AC circuits connected to a single-phase AC supply behave differently than those connected to a three-phase AC supply. In a single-phase system, the voltage and current waveforms are sinusoidal and are in phase with each other. In a three-phase system, the voltage and current waveforms are still sinusoidal, but they are 120 degrees out of phase with each other. 

To measure power in single-phase electrical circuits, you can use a single-phase power meter. This device measures the voltage and current waveforms and calculates the power based on the product of the voltage and current. 

In three-phase electrical circuits, power can be measured using a three-phase power meter. This device measures the voltage and current waveforms for each phase and calculates the power based on the product of the voltage and current for each phase. The total power in the circuit is the sum of the power in each phase.
