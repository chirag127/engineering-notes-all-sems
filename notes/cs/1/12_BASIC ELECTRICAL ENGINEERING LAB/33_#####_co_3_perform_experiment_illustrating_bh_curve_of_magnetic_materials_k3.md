##### CO 3 Perform experiment illustrating BH curve of magnetic materials. K3

To illustrate the B-H curve of magnetic materials, you can perform the following experiment:
1. Set up a circuit with a coil of wire and a solenoid.
2. Apply a direct current (DC) to the coil to create a magnetic field in the solenoid.
3. Measure the magnetic field strength (B) using a Gaussmeter.
4. Record the magnetic field strength (B) and the current (I) flowing through the coil.
5. Increase the current and repeat steps 3 and 4.
6. Plot the data to create a B-H curve.
7. Observe the shape of the curve and the relationship between magnetic field strength (B) and current (I).
