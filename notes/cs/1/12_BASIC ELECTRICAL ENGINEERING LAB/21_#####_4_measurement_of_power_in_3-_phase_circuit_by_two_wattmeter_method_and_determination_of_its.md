##### 4. Measurement of power in 3- phase circuit by two wattmeter method and determination of its power factor for star as well as delta connected load.

The two wattmeter method is used to measure the power in a 3-phase circuit. The power in a 3-phase system can be calculated using two wattmeters. The wattmeters measure the power in each of the three phases and the power factor can be determined from the readings.

For a star connected load, the power factor is calculated using the formula:
P.F = (W1 - W2) / (W1 + W2)
Where W1 and W2 are the readings from the two wattmeters.

For a delta connected load, the power factor is calculated using the formula:
P.F = (3W1 - W2) / (3W1 + W2)
Where W1 and W2 are the readings from the two wattmeters.
