#### K1 – Remember, K2 – Understand, K3 – Apply, K4 – Analyze, K5 – Evaluate, K6 – Create

K1 to K6 are levels of learning objectives in Bloom's Taxonomy, a framework for classifying educational goals and objectives. 

K1 – Remember: The learner can recall or remember previously learned information. 
K2 – Understand: The learner can comprehend the meaning of the information. 
K3 – Apply: The learner can use the information in a new situation. 
K4 – Analyze: The learner can break down the information into its component parts and understand how they relate to each other. 
K5 – Evaluate: The learner can make judgments about the value of the information. 
K6 – Create: The learner can use the information to create something new. 

Bloom's Taxonomy provides a useful framework for organizing learning objectives and for designing educational activities and assessments. By using the taxonomy, educators can ensure that their teaching and assessment activities are aligned with the desired learning outcomes.
