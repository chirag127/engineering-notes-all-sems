# Analytic functions

- A complex function f(z) is **analytic** if it has a complex derivative f'(z) at every point in its domain .
- A complex function f(z) is **holomorphic** if it is complex differentiable at every point in an open subset of the complex plane.
- A complex function f(z) is **entire** if it is analytic in the whole complex plane.
- A complex function f(z) is **meromorphic** if it is analytic in the complex plane except for a set of isolated singularities.
- A complex function f(z) is **harmonic** if it satisfies the Laplace equation in two dimensions.
- A complex function f(z) is **conformal** if it preserves angles and orientation locally.

## Properties of analytic functions

- Analytic functions are **infinitely differentiable**, meaning that they have derivatives of all orders.
- Analytic functions are **equal to their Taylor series** in a neighborhood of any point in their domain, meaning that they can be approximated by polynomials.
- Analytic functions satisfy the **Cauchy-Riemann equations**, which relate the partial derivatives of the real and imaginary parts of the function .
- Analytic functions satisfy the **Cauchy integral formula**, which gives the value of the function at any point in terms of a contour integral around that point.
- Analytic functions satisfy the **maximum modulus principle**, which states that the modulus of an analytic function cannot have a local maximum in the interior of its domain.
- Analytic functions satisfy the **identity theorem**, which states that if two analytic functions agree on a set that has a limit point in their domain, then they agree everywhere in their domain.
