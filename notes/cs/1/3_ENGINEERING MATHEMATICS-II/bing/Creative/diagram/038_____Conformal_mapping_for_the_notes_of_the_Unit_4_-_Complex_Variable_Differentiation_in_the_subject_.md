### Conformal mapping

- A conformal mapping is a function that preserves local angles and orientation between curves in the complex plane .
- A conformal mapping is also called a conformal map, conformal transformation, angle-preserving transformation, or biholomorphic map .
- A complex function is conformal at any point where it is analytic and has a nonzero derivative .
- A conformal mapping is one-to-one and holomorphic on an open set in the plane, and its inverse function is also holomorphic.
- Conformal mappings are useful for transforming simple harmonic solutions into those applicable to more complicated shapes.
- Some examples of conformal mappings are the identity function, the exponential function, the logarithmic function, the Möbius transformation, and the Joukowsky transformation.
