Power Generation Industry: 
- Involves the production of electricity to meet the energy demands of society. 
- Main sources of power generation include fossil fuels (coal, natural gas, oil), nuclear, hydro, wind and solar.
- Key players in the industry include utility companies, independent power producers and renewable energy developers.
- Industry is regulated by government agencies, with policies aimed at promoting sustainability and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

Chemical Industry:
- Involves the production of chemicals and chemical-based products for various end-uses.
- Major segments include petrochemicals, specialty chemicals, and pharmaceuticals.
- Key players include large chemical companies, independent manufacturers, and suppliers.
- Industry is subject to regulations for health and safety, environmental protection, and fair trade practices.
