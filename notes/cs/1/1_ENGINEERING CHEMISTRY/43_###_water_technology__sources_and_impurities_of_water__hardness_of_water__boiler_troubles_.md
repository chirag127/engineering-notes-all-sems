### Water Technology: Sources and impurities of water, Hardness of water, Boiler troubles,

Water technology refers to the study and application of techniques and technologies used to treat, purify, and distribute water. 

Sources of water include surface water (rivers, lakes, and streams), groundwater (wells), and municipal water supplies. Impurities in water can include physical, chemical, and biological contaminants. 

Water hardness is a measure of the amount of minerals, specifically calcium and magnesium, in water. High levels of hardness can cause issues in household appliances and industrial processes. 

Boiler troubles refer to problems that occur in boilers used for heating or generating steam. These can include issues with water quality, such as scaling and corrosion, as well as mechanical problems with the boiler itself. Proper water treatment and maintenance can help prevent these issues.
