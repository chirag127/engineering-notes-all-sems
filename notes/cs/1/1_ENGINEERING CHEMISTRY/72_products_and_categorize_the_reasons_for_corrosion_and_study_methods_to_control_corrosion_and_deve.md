Reasons for corrosion:
1. Chemical reactions: corrosion occurs due to chemical reactions between metal and environment.
2. Electrochemical reactions: corrosion occurs due to the flow of electrons between metal and environment.
3. Microbial activity: corrosion occurs due to the action of microorganisms on metal surfaces.

Methods to control corrosion:
1. Protective coatings: applying a protective coating to metal surfaces to prevent corrosion.
2. Cathodic protection: controlling corrosion by applying a direct current to metal surfaces.
3. Materials selection: selecting materials that are resistant to corrosion.
4. Environmental control: controlling the environment to reduce the risk of corrosion.

Study methods to control corrosion:
1. Laboratory testing: testing materials and coatings in a controlled laboratory environment.
2. Field testing: testing materials and coatings in real-world environments.
3. Computer simulations: using computer models to predict corrosion behavior.
4. Monitoring: monitoring metal surfaces for signs of corrosion and taking appropriate action.
