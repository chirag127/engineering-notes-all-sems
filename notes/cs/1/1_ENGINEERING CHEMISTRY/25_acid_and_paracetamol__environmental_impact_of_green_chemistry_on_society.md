Paracetamol is a widely used pain reliever and fever reducer. It works by blocking the production of prostaglandins, which are substances in the body that cause pain and inflammation.

Green chemistry is a branch of chemistry that focuses on the development of chemical processes and products that reduce or eliminate the use and generation of hazardous substances. This approach has a positive impact on the environment, as it reduces waste and pollution, conserves energy and resources, and reduces the risk of chemical exposure to human health and the environment.

The use of green chemistry principles in the production of paracetamol results in safer and more sustainable processes, reducing the release of harmful chemicals into the environment and reducing waste. This has a positive impact on local communities and the planet as a whole, promoting sustainable development and a healthier environment for future generations.
