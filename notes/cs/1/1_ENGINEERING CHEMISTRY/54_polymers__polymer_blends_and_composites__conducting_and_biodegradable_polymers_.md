Polymers, Polymer Blends and Composites, Conducting and Biodegradable polymers,

Polymer blends and composites are materials made by combining two or more polymers together to create a new material with improved properties. 

Conducting polymers are a class of polymers that have the ability to conduct electricity, making them useful in a variety of applications, such as batteries, sensors, and electronic devices.

Biodegradable polymers are polymers that can be broken down by natural processes into non-toxic substances, making them a more environmentally friendly alternative to traditional petroleum-based plastics. They are used in applications such as packaging, agriculture, and medical devices.
