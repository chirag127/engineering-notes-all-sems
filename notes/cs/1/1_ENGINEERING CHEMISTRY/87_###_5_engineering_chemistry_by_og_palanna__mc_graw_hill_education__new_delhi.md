### 5. Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna, Mc Graw Hill Education, New Delhi

Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna is a comprehensive textbook for engineering students, published by McGraw Hill Education, New Delhi. The book covers various topics in chemical engineering, including chemical kinetics, thermodynamics, electrochemistry, corrosion, water treatment, and environmental chemistry. It provides a clear and concise explanation of the fundamental concepts and principles of engineering chemistry, making it an essential resource for students pursuing engineering degrees. The book includes numerous examples, illustrations, and exercises to help students understand and apply the concepts they learn. Additionally, the book is written in a user-friendly style, making it accessible to students of all levels. The book is widely used in engineering colleges and universities across India and is considered a valuable resource for students preparing for engineering exams.
