Chemistry of Advanced Materials:

Liquid Crystals: Liquid crystals are materials that have properties of both liquids and solids. They have a defined molecular orientation, but are able to flow like a liquid. Liquid crystals are used in various applications including displays for televisions and computers.

Nanomaterials: Nanomaterials are materials with structures on the nanoscale, typically less than 100 nanometers in size. They have unique properties due to their small size, including increased surface area and improved optical, electrical, and thermal properties. Nanomaterials are used in a variety of applications, including electronics, energy storage, and biomedical devices.

Graphite & Fullerene: Graphite is a form of carbon that is commonly used as a lubricant and in batteries. Fullerene is a type of carbon molecule that is made up of 60 or more carbon atoms arranged in a spherical shape. Fullerenes have unique properties and are used in various applications, including as catalysts and in the production of nanomaterials.
