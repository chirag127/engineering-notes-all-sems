### 7. University Chemistry by BH Mahan

7th Edition of "University Chemistry" by B.H. Mahan is a comprehensive textbook for undergraduate students studying chemistry. It covers the fundamental principles of chemistry, including the structure of atoms and molecules, chemical reactions, thermodynamics, and kinetics. The book includes numerous worked examples, problems, and illustrations to help students understand the concepts. The text is written in a clear and concise manner, making it accessible to students with varying levels of prior knowledge in chemistry. Additionally, the book includes a strong emphasis on real-world applications of chemistry, helping students to see the relevance of the subject to their future careers. Overall, "University Chemistry" by B.H. Mahan is a valuable resource for students seeking a thorough understanding of the subject.
