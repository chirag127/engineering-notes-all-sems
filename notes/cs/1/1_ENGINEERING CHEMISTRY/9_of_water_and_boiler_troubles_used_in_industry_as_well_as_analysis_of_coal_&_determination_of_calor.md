Water and Boiler Troubles in Industry:

Water and boiler troubles are common issues in industrial boilers, which are used to produce steam for power generation and other industrial processes. Some of the most common water and boiler troubles include:

1. Scale formation: Scale formation occurs when minerals in the water precipitate and form a hard layer on the inside of the boiler, reducing heat transfer efficiency and causing corrosion.

2. Corrosion: Corrosion occurs when the metal components of the boiler are exposed to water and air, causing them to deteriorate over time.

3. Foaming: Foaming occurs when air bubbles form in the water, reducing heat transfer efficiency and causing steam production to become unstable.

4. Priming: Priming occurs when water is carried over with the steam, causing damage to the turbine and reducing the efficiency of the power generation process.

Advantages of addressing water and boiler troubles include:

1. Improved efficiency: Addressing water and boiler troubles can improve the efficiency of the power generation process and reduce the cost of energy production.

2. Increased lifespan: Addressing water and boiler troubles can increase the lifespan of the boiler, reducing the need for frequent replacements and maintenance.

Disadvantages of not addressing water and boiler troubles include:

1. Reduced efficiency: If water and boiler troubles are not addressed, the efficiency of the power generation process will be reduced, leading to higher energy costs.

2. Increased maintenance costs: If water and boiler troubles are not addressed, the need for frequent maintenance and replacements will increase, leading to higher maintenance costs.

Analysis of Coal & Determination of Calorific Values:

Coal analysis is the process of determining the composition and properties of coal, including its calorific value. The calorific value of coal is the amount of energy that can be obtained from the combustion of a given amount of coal.

Advantages of coal analysis and determination of calorific values include:

1. Improved efficiency: Knowing the calorific value of coal can improve the efficiency of the power generation process and reduce the cost of energy production.

2. Better understanding: Coal analysis and determination of calorific values provide a better understanding of the properties of coal and how it can be used in power generation.

Disadvantages of not performing coal analysis and determination of calorific values include:

1. Reduced efficiency: If the calorific value of coal is not known, the efficiency of the power generation process will be reduced, leading to higher energy costs.

2. Inadequate utilization: If the calorific value of coal is not known, it may not be utilized to its full potential, leading to waste and reduced energy production.

In conclusion, water and boiler troubles are common issues in industrial boilers, and addressing these troubles can improve efficiency and increase the lifespan of the boiler. Coal analysis and determination of calorific values are important for improving the efficiency of the power generation process and reducing energy costs. Not performing coal analysis and determination of calorific values can lead to reduced efficiency and inadequate utilization of coal.
