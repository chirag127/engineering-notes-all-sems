### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

Upon completion of a course, students should have a clear understanding of what they have learned and what they can expect to achieve. The specific objectives of a course will vary depending on the subject matter and the intended audience, but some common objectives include:

1. Knowledge acquisition: Students should have a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter, including key concepts, theories, and principles.

2. Skill development: Students should have developed specific skills, such as problem-solving, critical thinking, and communication skills.

3. Application of knowledge: Students should have the opportunity to apply what they have learned to real-world situations and problems.

4. Preparation for future studies or careers: Students should be prepared for future studies or careers in a particular field.

5. Personal growth and development: Students should have grown and developed as individuals, through self-reflection and personal growth.

It is important to clearly state the course objectives in order to provide students with a clear understanding of what they will learn and what they can expect to gain from the course. This helps to ensure that students are motivated and engaged, and that they are able to achieve their goals.
