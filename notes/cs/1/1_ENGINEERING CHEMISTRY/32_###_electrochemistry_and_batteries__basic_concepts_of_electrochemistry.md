### Electrochemistry and Batteries: Basic concepts of electrochemistry.

Electrochemistry is the study of the relationship between electrical energy and chemical reactions. Basic concepts include:

1. Redox reactions: chemical reactions where electrons are transferred from one species to another.

2. Electrode potential: the potential difference between a metal and its ions in solution.

3. Galvanic cells: devices that convert chemical energy into electrical energy through redox reactions.

4. Electrolytic cells: devices that convert electrical energy into chemical energy through redox reactions.

5. Batteries: devices that store and provide electrical energy through redox reactions.

6. Faraday's law of electrolysis: the amount of substance produced during electrolysis is proportional to the charge passed through the solution.

7. Nernst equation: predicts the electrode potential of a half-cell in an electrochemical cell under non-standard conditions.

These concepts are important in the design and use of batteries, which are widely used in many applications, including portable electronics, electric vehicles, and renewable energy storage.
