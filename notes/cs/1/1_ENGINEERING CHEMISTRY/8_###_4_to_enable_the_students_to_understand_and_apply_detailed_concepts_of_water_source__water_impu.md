### 4. To enable the students to understand and apply detailed concepts of water source, water impurities, hardness

Water Source:

Water source refers to the origin of water, which can be either surface water or groundwater.

1. Surface water: Surface water is water that is found on the surface of the Earth, such as in rivers, lakes, and oceans.

2. Groundwater: Groundwater is water that is found underground, in spaces between rocks and gravels, or in aquifers.

Water Impurities:

Water impurities refer to any substances that are present in water that are not naturally occurring and can affect the quality of the water.

1. Physical impurities: Physical impurities include substances such as dirt, sand, and other solid particles that are present in water.

2. Chemical impurities: Chemical impurities include substances such as pollutants, salts, and minerals that are present in water.

3. Biological impurities: Biological impurities include microorganisms such as bacteria, viruses, and parasites that are present in water.

Hardness:

Hardness is a measure of the amount of dissolved minerals, such as calcium and magnesium, present in water. Hard water can cause problems such as clogging of pipes, reduced soap efficacy, and scaling on appliances.

1. Temporary hardness: Temporary hardness is caused by the presence of dissolved bicarbonates in water, and can be removed by boiling the water.

2. Permanent hardness: Permanent hardness is caused by the presence of dissolved salts, such as calcium and magnesium sulfates, and cannot be removed by boiling the water.

Advantages of understanding water source, water impurities, and hardness:

1. Improved water quality: Understanding the sources and impurities of water can help to improve the quality of the water and ensure that it is safe for consumption and use.

2. Better water management: Understanding the sources and impurities of water can help to better manage the water resources and ensure that they are used efficiently and sustainably.

3. Improved health: Understanding the sources and impurities of water can help to improve public health, as contaminated water can cause illnesses and diseases.

Disadvantages of not understanding water source, water impurities, and hardness:

1. Contaminated water: Not understanding the sources and impurities of water can lead to contaminated water, which can cause illnesses and diseases.

2. Inefficient water management: Not understanding the sources and impurities of water can lead to inefficient water management, which can result in the depletion of water resources.

3. Poor water quality: Not understanding the sources and impurities of water can result in poor water quality, which can affect the taste, appearance, and safety of the water.

In conclusion, water source refers to the origin of water, which can be either surface water or groundwater. Water impurities refer to any substances that are present in water that are not naturally occurring and can affect the quality of the water. Hardness is a measure of the amount of dissolved minerals, such as calcium and magnesium, present in water. Understanding the sources and impurities of water, as well as the hardness of water, can help to improve the quality of the water, ensure that it is safe for consumption and use, and better manage the water resources.
