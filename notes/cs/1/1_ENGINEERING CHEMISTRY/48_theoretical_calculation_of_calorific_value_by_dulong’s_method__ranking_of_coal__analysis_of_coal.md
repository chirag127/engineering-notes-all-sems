Theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong’s method, Ranking of Coal, Analysis of coal

Calorific Value by Dulong's Method: Dulong's formula is used to calculate the calorific value of coal. The formula is given by: Calorific value = (3.7 x Carbon %) + (0.8 x Hydrogen %) + (0.3 x Nitrogen %) + (0.6 x Sulfur %) + (0.2 x Ash %)

Ranking of Coal: Coal is ranked based on its carbon content, energy content, and sulfur content. The most commonly used ranking system is the ASTM system, which ranks coal from highest to lowest as follows: Anthracite, Bituminous, Sub-Bituminous, and Lignite.

Analysis of Coal: Analysis of coal involves determining its composition and properties, such as calorific value, sulfur content, ash content, volatile matter content, fixed carbon content, and moisture content. These properties are used to determine the quality and suitability of coal for various applications.
