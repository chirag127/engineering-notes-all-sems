
# Polymers

Polymers are large molecules composed of repeating structural units. These molecules are characterized by their high molecular mass, which can range from a few hundred to several million. Polymers can be found in a variety of products, including plastics, rubber, adhesives, fabrics, and even pharmaceuticals.

1. Types of Polymers
   - Natural Polymers: Natural polymers are those that are found in nature, such as proteins, wood, and silk. 
   - Synthetic Polymers: Synthetic polymers are those that are created by humans, such as plastics and synthetic rubber.
2. Structure of Polymers
   - Monomers: Monomers are the small molecules that are linked together to form polymers. 
   - Polymerization: Polymerization is the process in which monomers are linked together to form polymers.
3. Properties of Polymers
   - Flexibility: Polymers are flexible due to their long chain structure, which allows them to bend and stretch.
   - Strength: Polymers can be strong, depending on the type of monomer used and the way the polymer is formed.
   - Durability: Polymers are generally durable and can withstand high temperatures, which makes them ideal for a variety of applications.
