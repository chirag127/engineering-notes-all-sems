
## Hours
- Hours are a unit of time that is used to measure the duration of an event or activity.
- The hour is the base unit of time in the International System of Units (SI).
- The hour is typically divided into 60 minutes, each of which is divided into 60 seconds.
- The symbol for hour is "h" and it is abbreviated as hr.
- A day is equal to 24 hours.
- A week is equal to 168 hours.
- A month is equal to 730 hours.
- A year is equal to 8760 hours.
