
# Process), Determination of Hardness and Alkalinity, Numerical Problems

1. Hardness: Hardness is a measure of the amount of calcium and magnesium ions present in water. It is expressed in ppm (parts per million) or mg/L (milligrams per liter).

2. Alkalinity: Alkalinity is a measure of the buffering capacity of water. It is expressed in ppm (parts per million) or mg/L (milligrams per liter).

3. Numerical Problems: Numerical problems involve solving equations or using numerical methods to solve problems. These problems can be solved using calculators or computers. Examples of numerical problems include finding the roots of a polynomial equation, solving a system of linear equations, and finding the minimum or maximum of a function.
