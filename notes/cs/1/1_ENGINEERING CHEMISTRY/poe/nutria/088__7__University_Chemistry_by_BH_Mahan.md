
### 7. University Chemistry by BH Mahan

1. Chemistry is the scientific study of matter and its properties, structure, composition, and behavior.
2. Chemical reactions involve the rearrangement of atoms to form new substances.
3. The laws of thermodynamics govern the energetics of chemical reactions.
4. Chemical bonds are the forces that hold atoms together in molecules and crystals.
5. Chemical equilibrium is the state in which there is no net change in the concentrations of reactants and products.
6. Kinetics is the study of the rates of chemical reactions and the factors that affect them.
7. Organic chemistry is the study of compounds that contain carbon and hydrogen.
8. Analytical chemistry is the study of the composition of substances.
9. Physical chemistry is the study of the physical properties of matter and the interactions between matter and energy.
10. Inorganic chemistry is the study of the properties and reactions of inorganic compounds.
