
### Organometallic Compounds: General methods of Preparation and Applications

* Organometallic compounds are molecules that contain at least one metal-carbon bond.
* They are generally prepared by reacting a metal with an organic compound.
* Common methods of preparation include metathesis reactions, alkyl-metal exchange, and metal-halogen exchange.
* Common applications of organometallic compounds include catalysts for organic synthesis, reagents for organic transformations, and ligands for coordination complexes.
* Organometallic compounds are also used in the pharmaceutical and petrochemical industries.
