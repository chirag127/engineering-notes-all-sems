
# Magnetic characters and numerical problems

1. Magnetic characters are symbols that represent the direction and magnitude of a magnetic field. They are typically written as arrows, with the length of the arrow representing the magnitude of the field and the direction of the arrow representing the direction of the field.

2. Numerical problems involving magnetic fields can be solved using the right-hand rule. This rule states that if the right thumb is pointed in the direction of the magnetic field, the fingers will curl in the direction of the force that the field exerts on a moving charge.

3. The force exerted by a magnetic field on a moving charge can be calculated using the equation F = qv × B, where q is the charge of the particle, v is the velocity of the particle, and B is the magnetic field.

4. The Lorentz force law states that the force exerted by a magnetic field on a charged particle is equal to the product of the particle's charge and the cross product of its velocity and the magnetic field. This law can be used to calculate the force exerted by a magnetic field on a charged particle.
