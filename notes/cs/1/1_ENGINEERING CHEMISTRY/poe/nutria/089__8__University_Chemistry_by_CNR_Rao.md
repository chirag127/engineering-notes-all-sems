
### 8. University Chemistry by CNR Rao

* Chemistry is the study of matter, its properties, and how it interacts with other matter and energy.
* CNR Rao is an Indian chemist and a recipient of the Bharat Ratna, the highest civilian award in India.
* University Chemistry is a branch of chemistry that focuses on topics related to the study of matter, its properties, and its reactions with other substances.
* This branch of chemistry includes topics such as atomic and molecular structure, chemical bonding, thermodynamics, kinetics, electrochemistry, and spectroscopy.
* It also covers topics such as organic chemistry, inorganic chemistry, biochemistry, and physical chemistry.
* The study of university chemistry is essential for students who wish to pursue a career in the chemical sciences.
