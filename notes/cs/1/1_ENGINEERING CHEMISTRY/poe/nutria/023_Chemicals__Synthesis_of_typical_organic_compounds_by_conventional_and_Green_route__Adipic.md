
# Chemicals, Synthesis of typical organic compounds by conventional and Green route (Adipic)

* Organic compounds can be synthesized by conventional methods, which typically involve the use of toxic reagents and solvents, and often produce large amounts of waste.
* Green synthesis is an alternative method of synthesizing organic compounds that uses fewer hazardous reagents and solvents, and produces less waste. 
* Adipic acid is an example of an organic compound that can be synthesized by both conventional and green methods.
* Conventional synthesis of adipic acid involves the oxidation of cyclohexanol with nitric acid, followed by hydrolysis of the resulting cyclohexanone oxime.
* Green synthesis of adipic acid involves the oxidation of cyclohexanol with hydrogen peroxide, followed by hydrolysis of the resulting cyclohexanone oxime.
* Both conventional and green syntheses of adipic acid produce the same product, but the green route is more eco-friendly and produces less waste.
