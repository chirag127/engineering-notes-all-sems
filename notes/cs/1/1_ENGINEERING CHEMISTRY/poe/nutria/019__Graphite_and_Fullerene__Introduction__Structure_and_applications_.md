
### Graphite and Fullerene; Introduction, Structure and Applications

* Graphite is a naturally occurring form of crystalline carbon. It is composed of layers of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice, which gives it its unique properties. Graphite is a very soft material and is often used in pencils, lubricants and as a conductor in electrical components.

* Fullerene is a form of carbon consisting of spherical molecules made up of 60 or more carbon atoms. It has many unique properties, such as being the strongest material known and having the highest thermal and electrical conductivity of any known material. Fullerenes have been used in a variety of applications, including solar cells, medical treatments and lubricants.

* The structure of graphite and fullerene are very different. Graphite consists of layers of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice, while fullerene consists of spherical molecules made up of 60 or more carbon atoms.

* Graphite and fullerene have many applications. Graphite is used in pencils, lubricants and as a conductor in electrical components, while fullerenes have been used in solar cells, medical treatments and lubricants.
