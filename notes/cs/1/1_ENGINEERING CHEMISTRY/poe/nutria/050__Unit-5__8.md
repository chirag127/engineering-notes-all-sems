
## Unit-5: 8

1. Unit-5: 8 is a unit of study focusing on the principles of information security.
2. It covers topics such as risk management, access control, authentication, encryption, and data integrity.
3. Risk management is the process of assessing the potential risks associated with an information system and taking steps to reduce those risks.
4. Access control is the process of restricting access to information systems and data, ensuring that only authorized users can access them.
5. Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user or system before granting access to a system or data.
6. Encryption is the process of transforming data into a form that cannot be read by unauthorized users, ensuring data confidentiality.
7. Data integrity is the process of ensuring that data has not been tampered with or corrupted during transmission or storage.
8. Unit-5: 8 is an important unit of study for anyone interested in information security.
