
# Isomerism, Chiral Drugs

1. Isomerism is a form of chemical structure in which two or more compounds have the same molecular formula but different structural formulas. 
2. Chiral drugs are molecules that have the same molecular formula but different spatial arrangements, resulting in two forms that are mirror images of each other.
3. These molecules are known as enantiomers, and they can have different effects on the body depending on which form is ingested.
4. Chiral drugs are often used in medicine to treat a variety of conditions, as they can be more effective than non-chiral drugs.
5. Enantiomers can be separated using a variety of methods, including chromatography and crystallization.
6. It is important to note that chiral drugs can have different effects on the body depending on which enantiomer is ingested, so it is important to ensure that the correct form is administered.
