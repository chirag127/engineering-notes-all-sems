
### Atomic and Molecular Structure: Molecular orbital’s of diatomic molecules, Bond Order

1. Molecular orbitals are mathematical functions that describe the properties of electrons in a molecule. 
2. Molecular orbitals are derived from the combination of atomic orbitals from each atom in the molecule.
3. Molecular orbitals are classified as either bonding, antibonding, or nonbonding orbitals.
4. Bond order is a measure of the strength of a chemical bond between two atoms. 
5. Bond order is determined by the number of electrons in a bonding molecular orbital minus the number of electrons in an antibonding molecular orbital. 
6. The bond order of a diatomic molecule can range from 0 to 4, with 0 indicating that the molecule is not bound and 4 indicating a very strong bond.
