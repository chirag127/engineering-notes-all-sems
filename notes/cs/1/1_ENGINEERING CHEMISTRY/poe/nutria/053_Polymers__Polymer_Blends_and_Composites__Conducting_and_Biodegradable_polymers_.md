
# Polymers

Polymers are large molecules made up of repeating structural units connected by covalent chemical bonds. They can be either synthetic or naturally occurring and are characterized by their ability to be molded into various shapes and sizes. Synthetic polymers are used in a wide variety of applications, including adhesives, plastics, coatings, fibers, and elastomers.

# Polymer Blends and Composites

Polymer blends and composites are materials composed of two or more polymers, or a polymer and a non-polymeric material. These materials are used in a variety of applications, including automotive and aerospace components, medical devices, and consumer products. The properties of the material can be tailored to specific needs by varying the composition of the blend or composite.

# Conducting Polymers

Conducting polymers are polymers that can conduct electricity. These materials are used in a variety of applications, including organic electronics, sensors, and energy storage devices. They are typically made from conjugated polymers chains, which contain alternating single and double bonds along the backbone of the polymer.

# Biodegradable Polymers

Biodegradable polymers are polymers that can be broken down by microorganisms into simpler molecules. These materials are used in a variety of applications, including packaging and medical devices. They are typically made from polymers that contain hydrolyzable linkages, which can be broken down into smaller molecules by the action of water.
