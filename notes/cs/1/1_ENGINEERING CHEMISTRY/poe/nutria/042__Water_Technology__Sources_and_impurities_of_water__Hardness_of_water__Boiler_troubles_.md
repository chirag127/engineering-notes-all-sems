
### Water Technology: Sources and impurities of water, Hardness of water, Boiler troubles

- Sources of water: Water can be obtained from a variety of sources, including rivers, lakes, groundwater, and aquifers. The quality of water from each source varies and can be affected by environmental factors, such as pollution and natural events.

- Impurities of water: Water can contain a variety of impurities, including minerals, bacteria, and chemicals. These impurities can affect the taste, odor, and color of the water, as well as its suitability for use in various applications.

- Hardness of water: Hardness of water is a measure of the amount of calcium and magnesium present in the water. Hard water can cause scaling in pipes and fixtures, and can affect the effectiveness of soaps and detergents.

- Boiler troubles: Boilers are used to heat water for a variety of applications. If the water is too hard, it can cause scaling and other problems in the boiler, which can lead to reduced efficiency and increased maintenance costs.
