
### Fuels and Combustion: Definition, Classification, Characteristics of a good fuel, Calorific

* **Definition:** A fuel is any material that can be used to produce energy, usually in the form of heat. Combustion is the process of burning a fuel in the presence of oxygen to produce energy.

* **Classification:** Fuels are generally classified according to their composition (e.g. solid, liquid, or gaseous) and their source (e.g. fossil fuels or renewable sources).

* **Characteristics of a good fuel:** A good fuel should be easy to ignite, produce a large amount of energy, and have a low environmental impact.

* **Calorific:** The calorific value of a fuel is a measure of the amount of energy it can produce when burned.
