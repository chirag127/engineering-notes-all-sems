
### Electrochemistry and Batteries: Basic Concepts of Electrochemistry

1. Electrochemistry is the branch of chemistry that studies the relationship between electrical energy and chemical reactions.
2. A battery is a device that stores energy in the form of chemical energy and releases it as electrical energy.
3. A galvanic cell is the basic unit of a battery. It consists of two half-cells, each containing an electrode and an electrolyte.
4. The anode is the electrode where oxidation occurs and electrons are released. The cathode is the electrode where reduction occurs and electrons are absorbed.
5. The electrolyte is a solution that contains ions and allows them to move between the electrodes.
6. The electric current is produced by the movement of ions from the anode to the cathode.
7. The voltage of a battery is determined by the difference in the standard reduction potentials of the two half-cells.
8. The capacity of a battery is determined by the amount of energy it can store and release.
9. Rechargeable batteries can be recharged by applying an external voltage.
10. Batteries are used in a wide variety of applications, such as powering electronic devices and electric vehicles.
