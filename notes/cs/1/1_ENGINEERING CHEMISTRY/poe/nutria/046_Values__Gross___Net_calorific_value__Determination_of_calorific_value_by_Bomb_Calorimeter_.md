
# Values, Gross & Net Calorific Value, Determination of Calorific Value by Bomb Calorimeter

* Calorific value is the amount of energy released when a material is burned.
* The gross calorific value is the total energy released when a material is burned in oxygen, while the net calorific value is the energy released when the material is burned in air.
* The determination of calorific value is done using a bomb calorimeter. This device has a pressure-tight container, called a bomb, which is filled with oxygen.
* The material to be tested is placed in the bomb and then ignited. The heat released by the burning material is absorbed by a known mass of water in the calorimeter, and the change in temperature is measured.
* The calorific value of the material is then calculated using the equation:

Calorific Value = (Mass of Water x Specific Heat Capacity x Change in Temperature) / Mass of Material

* Calorific values are used to compare the energy content of different materials and to determine the efficiency of combustion processes.
