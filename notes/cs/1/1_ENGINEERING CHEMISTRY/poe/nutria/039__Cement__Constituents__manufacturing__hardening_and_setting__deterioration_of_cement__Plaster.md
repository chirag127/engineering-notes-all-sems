
### Cement; Constituents, Manufacturing, Hardening and Setting, Deterioration of Cement, Plaster

* Cement is a construction material made of a mixture of materials such as limestone, clay, iron oxide, and silica, which are heated and ground together. 
* Cement is used to bind other materials together and is the main ingredient of concrete.
* The main constituents of cement are calcium oxide, silicon oxide, aluminum oxide, and iron oxide. 
* Cement is manufactured by heating the raw materials to high temperatures in a kiln and then grinding them into a fine powder. 
* Cement hardens when water is added to it and sets when it has hardened. 
* Deterioration of cement can be caused by the presence of sulfates and chlorides in the environment, as well as by the presence of water. 
* Plaster is a material made of a mixture of cement, sand, and water, which is used to coat walls and ceilings.
