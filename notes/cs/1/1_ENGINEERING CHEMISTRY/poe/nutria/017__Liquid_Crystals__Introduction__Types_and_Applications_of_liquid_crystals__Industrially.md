
### Liquid Crystals; Introduction, Types and Applications of liquid crystals, Industrially

* Liquid crystals are a type of matter that have properties of both liquids and solids. They are composed of molecules that are arranged in an ordered pattern, but are able to move around freely like a liquid.

* There are three main types of liquid crystals: nematic, smectic, and cholesteric. Nematic liquid crystals are the most common and are used in LCD displays. Smectic liquid crystals are used in ferroelectric LCDs, while cholesteric liquid crystals are used in reflective LCDs.

* Liquid crystals have many industrial applications. They are used in LCD displays for computers, televisions, and other electronic devices. They are also used in optical components, such as lenses and polarizers, and in thermometers, thermochromic ink, and other temperature-sensitive materials.

* Liquid crystals can also be used to create smart materials that can react to their environment. For example, they can be used to create fabrics that can change color in response to heat, or materials that can increase their stiffness when exposed to a certain amount of force.
