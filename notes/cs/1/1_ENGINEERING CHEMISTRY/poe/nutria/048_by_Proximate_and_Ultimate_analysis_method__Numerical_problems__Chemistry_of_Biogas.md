
# Proximate and Ultimate Analysis Method
Proximate analysis is a method of determining the approximate percentage of the main components of a substance, such as ash, moisture, volatile matter, and fixed carbon. Ultimate analysis is a method of determining the exact percentage of the main components of a substance, such as carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, and sulfur.

## Chemistry of Biogas
Biogas is a type of renewable energy produced from the decomposition of organic matter. The main components of biogas are methane (CH4) and carbon dioxide (CO2). The chemistry of biogas involves the reaction of these components with other elements in the environment, such as oxygen, to produce energy. The reaction of methane and oxygen is known as the methanogenesis reaction, which is the main source of energy produced by biogas.
