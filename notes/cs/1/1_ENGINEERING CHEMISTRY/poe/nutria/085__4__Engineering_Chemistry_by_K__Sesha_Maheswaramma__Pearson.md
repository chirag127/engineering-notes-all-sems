
### 4. Engineering Chemistry by K. Sesha Maheswaramma, Pearson

1. Engineering chemistry is a branch of chemistry that applies the principles of chemistry to the design, development, and production of engineering materials. 
2. The field is concerned with the study of the structure, properties, and reactivity of materials, and the development of methods to manufacture and use them. 
3. Engineering chemistry involves the study of the chemical processes involved in the production, processing, and use of materials. 
4. It includes the study of the properties of materials, such as their strength, hardness, electrical and thermal conductivity, and optical properties. 
5. It also includes the study of the chemical processes used in the production and processing of materials, such as oxidation, corrosion, and electrochemical processes. 
6. Engineering chemistry also involves the development of new materials, such as polymers, composites, and nanomaterials, and the study of their properties and applications.
