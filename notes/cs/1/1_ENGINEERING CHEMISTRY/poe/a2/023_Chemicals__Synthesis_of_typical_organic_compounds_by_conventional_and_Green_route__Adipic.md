# Chemicals, Synthesis of typical organic compounds by conventional and Green route (Adipic)

## Conventional route

- Adipic acid is produced industrially by the nitric acid oxidation of a cyclohexane feedstock. 
- The synthesis consists of two steps:
- Step 1: Cyclohexane is oxidized to a mixture of adipic acid and cyclohexanone using nitric acid as the oxidant. This is an exothermic reaction that produces nitrous oxide as a byproduct.
- Step 2: The resulting mixture of adipic acid and cyclohexanone is subsequently hydrogenated to convert the cyclohexanone to additional adipic acid.
- The yield of adipic acid from cyclohexane using this two-step process is around 85%.

## Green route

- New "green" routes to adipic acid that avoid the use of nitric acid have been developed due to environmental concerns and cost.
- One such route involves the catalytic oxidation of cyclohexene or cyclohexanol. The catalytic oxidation can be achieved using oxygen and acetic acid at 150-200 °C in the presence of a cobalt or manganese catalyst. This is a more direct route than the nitric acid process and produces fewer byproducts.
- Another potential green route is bio-based and utilizes sugar from corn or sugar beets. Certain microorganisms can convert the sugar to glucaric acid, which can then be converted to adipic acid through hydrogenation. However, this route is not yet economical due to low yields and high costs.
