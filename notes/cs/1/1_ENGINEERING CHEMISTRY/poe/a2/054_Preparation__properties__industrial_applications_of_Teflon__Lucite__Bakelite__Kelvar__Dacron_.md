Preparation, properties, industrial applications of Teflon, Lucite, Bakelite, Kelvar, Dacron,

Preparation:

- Teflon: By polymerization of tetrafluoroethylene.
- Lucite: By polymerization of methyl methacrylate.
- Bakelite: By condensation of phenol and formaldehyde.
- Kelvar: By polycondensation of p-phenylene terephthalamide.
- Dacron: By polycondensation of ethylene glycol and terephthalic acid.

Properties:

- Teflon: Chemically inert, high melting point, low friction.
- Lucite: Transparent, weather resistant, high impact strength.
- Bakelite: High electrical insulation, heat resistant, high mechanical strength.
- Kelvar: High tensile strength, fatigue resistant, chemically resistant.
- Dacron: High tensile strength, durable, resistant to stretching and shrinking.

Industrial applications:

- Teflon: Non-stick cookware, wiring insulation, bearings.
- Lucite: Aircraft windows, signs, medical applications.
- Bakelite: Electrical switches, billiard balls, kitchenware.
- Kelvar: Ropes, tires, conveyor belts, protective armour.
- Dacron: Fabrics, sailcloth, tire cords, filter cloth.
