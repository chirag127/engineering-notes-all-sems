# Paris (Population)

1. Paris is the capital and most populous city of France with over 2.2 million people living in the dense central city and almost 12 million people living in the whole metropolitan area.

2. The Paris Region is one of the most populated areas in Europe with 12.1 million people at the 2016 census. It has numerous satellite cities like Versailles, Saint-Denis, Argenteuil or Evry with populations ranging from 100,000 to 380,000 inhabitants.

3. The population of Paris peaked in the late 19th century and halved during the two World Wars. After the 1940s, Paris experienced massive migrational flows and its population became very diverse ethnically, culturally and religiously.

4. Most residents in the center of Paris consist of professionals, managers, civil servants and their families. Paris is a very young and educated city, with nearly 40% of its population aged between 20 and 39 years and 66% of residents having an education after the baccalaureate.

5. Historically, Paris was a city of immigrants from all around France but also from abroad, especially from former French colonies in North Africa and Southeast Asia. Today, more than 20% of Parisians do not have French citizenship and a large number are foreigners from Asian and African countries.
