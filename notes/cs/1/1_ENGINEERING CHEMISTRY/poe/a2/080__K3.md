### K3

1. K3 refers to Knowledge, Skills and Attitudes - three key areas of learning that are fundamental to the development of an individual.

2. Knowledge refers to information that is acquired through study or experience. It is about being aware of facts, truths, or information.

3. Skills refer to the ability to do something well, especially as a result of training or practice. They are practical abilities that are developed through practice.

4. Attitudes refer to a settled way of thinking or feeling about something. They are psychological tendencies that are expressed through evaluating people or things with some degree of favor or disfavor.

5. These three aspects of learning are interconnected. What we know influences what we do and feel, and vice versa. Hence, for holistic development, all three - knowledge, skills, and attitudes - must be addressed.

6. The K3 framework is useful to design learning experiences and evaluate learner outcomes. It helps to take a comprehensive approach that develops individuals cognitively, psychomotorily, and affectively.
