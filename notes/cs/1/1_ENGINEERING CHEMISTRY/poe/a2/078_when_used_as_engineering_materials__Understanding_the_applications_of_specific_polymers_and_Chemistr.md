# When used as engineering materials: Understanding the applications of specific polymers and Chemistry

1.  Polymers are long chain molecules that have unique properties suitable for various engineering applications.
2.  Thermoplastics:
    -   Polyethylene (PE): Used in packaging, plastic bags, containers, plastic furniture. It is chemically resistant and has good insulation properties.
    -   Polypropylene (PP): Used in packaging, bottles, pipes, automobile parts. It has good chemical resistance and durability.
    -   Polyvinyl chloride (PVC): Used in pipes, windows, flooring, roofing sheets. It is stiff, strong, and chemical resistant but hazardous to produce and dispose of.
3.  Thermosets:
    -   Epoxy resins: Used as adhesives, paints, electronic components. They have high strength and chemical resistance.
    -   Polyester and vinyl ester: Used in marine and automotive components. They have good mechanical properties and are resistant to moisture and chemicals.
4.  The properties of polymers can be tuned by copolymerization, blending, and the addition of reinforcing agents like carbon fiber to create composites for specific applications.
5.  The large scale production and disposal of polymers have environmental impacts which can be addressed by recycling, using bio-based and biodegradable polymers, and material conservation.
