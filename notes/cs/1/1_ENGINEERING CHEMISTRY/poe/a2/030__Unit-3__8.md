## Unit-3: 8

8. Classification of Elements and Periodicity in Properties

- Elements can be classified into metals, non-metals and metalloids based on their properties.
- Metals are lustrous, malleable, ductile and are good conductors of heat and electricity. They form positive ions by losing electrons. Examples: Na, K, Ca, Al, Cu, Ag, Au, etc.
- Non-metals are generally dull, brittle and poor conductors of heat and electricity. They form negative ions by gaining electrons. Examples: H, O, N, Cl, P, S, etc.
- Metalloids are elements that have properties intermediate between metals and non-metals, e.g. B, Si, Ge, As, Sb, Te, Po.
- The periodic table classifies elements according to their periodic properties in terms of their atomic number, electron configurations and chemical properties. Elements are arranged in increasing atomic number in the periodic table.
- Elements show a periodic repetition of properties along a period and down a group in the periodic table. This periodicity occurs due to the similarity in the outer electron configurations of elements within a period or a group.
