# Polymer Composites

## What are Polymer Composites?

- Polymer composites are materials that consist of a polymer matrix and reinforcement materials such as fibers, particles or flakes.
- The polymer matrix acts as a host material that transfers load to the reinforcement material.
- The reinforcement materials absorb stresses and improve the mechanical properties such as strength, stiffness, impact resistance, wear resistance, etc. of the polymer.

## Types of Polymer Composites

- Fiber-reinforced polymer composites: Fibers such as glass, carbon or aramid are embedded in a polymer matrix. The fibers take up stresses and enhance strength and stiffness.
- Particle-filled polymer composites: Reinforcing particles such as talc, mica or glass microspheres are dispersed in a polymer matrix. The particles enhance properties such as dimensional stability and wear resistance.
- Flake-filled polymer composites: Reinforcing flakes such as glass or mica are oriented in a polymer matrix. The flakes increase properties such as impact resistance and tensile strength in a particular direction.

## Applications of Polymer Composites

- Automotive components such as drive shafts, leaf springs, bumpers, etc.
- Aircraft components such as radomes, fairings, tail sections, etc.
- Boat and ship components such as hulls, masts, etc.
- Sports goods such as golf clubs, bike frames, etc.
- Pipes, tanks and structural components due to high strength-to-weight ratio.
- Biomedical implants due to biocompatibility and adjustable mechanical properties.
