### 1. To enable the students to understand about the Chemistry of Atomic and Molecular structure, Chemistry of

1. Atoms:
- Made up of protons, neutrons and electrons
- Protons and neutrons in the nucleus, electrons revolve around the nucleus
- Valency: Number of electrons in the outermost shell
- Elements have characteristic properties determined by atom structure

2. Periodic Table:
- Arranged according to proton number
- Properties repeat periodically
- Metals, non-metals and metalloids
- Groups have similar properties

3. Bonding:
- Ionic: Transfer of electrons between metals and non-metals
- Covalent: Sharing of electrons between non-metals
- Metallic: Sea of delocalized electrons between metal atoms
- Determine properties of compounds

4. Intermolecular Forces:
- Temporary attractive forces between molecules
- Affect melting and boiling points
- Types: Dipole-dipole, hydrogen bonds, van der Waals forces
