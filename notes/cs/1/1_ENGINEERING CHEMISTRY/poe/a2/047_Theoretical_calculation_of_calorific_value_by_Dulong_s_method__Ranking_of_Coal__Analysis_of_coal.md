# Theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong’s method, Ranking of Coal, Analysis of coal

**Theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong’s method**

1. Dulong’s formula - Calorific value (in kcal/kg) = 807C + 3470(H − O/8)
2. Where C, H and O are the percentages of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen respectively in the coal
3. The formula assumes that all the carbon is burnt to CO2 and all the hydrogen to H2O
4. It gives the theoretical calorific value. The actual calorific value will be slightly less due to incomplete combustion and other losses

**Ranking of Coal**

1. Coals are ranked from the highest to lowest carbon content as anthracite, bituminous, sub-bituminous and lignite
2. Anthracite has the highest carbon (above 87%) and calorific value. It is hard and has a metallic lustre
3. Bituminous coal has carbon 70-87%. It is medium hard and often has bands of bright and dull material
4. Sub-bituminous coal has carbon 35-70%. It is dull, earthy and soft
5. Lignite has carbon 25-35%. It is brownish, soft and more like wood in texture and composition

**Analysis of coal**

1. Proximate analysis - Moisture, volatile matter, fixed carbon and ash are determined
2. Ultimate analysis - Carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, sulphur and oxygen are determined by chemical analyses
3. Heating value determination - Calorific value is determined using a bomb calorimeter which measures the heat liberated by complete combustion of a coal sample
