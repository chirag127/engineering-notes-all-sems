# Products and Corrosion

Corrosion is the gradual destruction of materials by chemical reaction with their environment. It is a major problem that affects a wide range of products and structures. There are several reasons why corrosion occurs and various methods to control or prevent corrosion.

## Reasons for Corrosion

1. Presence of moisture: Corrosion requires the presence of water or moisture to act as an electrolyte for the chemical reaction to occur. Dry environments do not support corrosion.
2. Presence of oxygen: The presence of oxygen is necessary for oxidation reactions that lead to corrosion of metals. Corrosion will not occur in environments devoid of oxygen like in vacuum or inert gas environments.
3. Presence of ions: The presence of ions like chloride, nitrate, carbonate, etc. can influence the corrosion process by affecting the pH level or conducting electricity.
4. Environmental pollutants: Environmental pollutants like acid rain, industrial fumes, etc. can react with materials and accelerate the corrosion process.
5. Microbial action: Micro-organisms like bacteria can act on materials and form corrosion. This type of corrosion is known as microbiologically influenced corrosion (MIC).

## Methods to Control Corrosion

1. Material selection: Choosing a material that is corrosion resistant in the specific environment can prevent corrosion. Stainless steel, plastics, etc. have better corrosion resistance than carbon steel.
2. Surface treatments: Applying protective coatings like paints, lacquers or metallic coatings can create a barrier between the material and surrounding environment to prevent corrosion. Anodization and plating are effective surface treatment methods.
3. Design changes: The design can be modified to avoid stagnation of water or provide channels for water to drain to prevent corrosion. Indoor storage of products protects from environmental exposure.
4. Cathodic protection: Cathodic protection is a technique where a sacrificial anode is connected to the structure to be protected. The anode gets corroded instead of the structure, thereby protecting it.
5. Chemical inhibitors: Chemicals like chromates can be added to environments to inhibit the corrosion reaction by affecting the corrosion mechanism. However, many inhibitors are toxic and their use is getting restricted.
