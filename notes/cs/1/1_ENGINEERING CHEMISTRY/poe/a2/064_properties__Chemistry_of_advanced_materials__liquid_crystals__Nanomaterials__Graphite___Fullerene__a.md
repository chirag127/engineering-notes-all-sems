# Properties, Chemistry of advanced materials (liquid crystals, Nanomaterials, Graphite & Fullerene)

## Liquid Crystals

- Liquid crystals are organic materials that exhibit properties intermediate between liquids and solids.
- They have molecular order and structure but flow like liquids.
- Examples: 4-Cyano-4'-pentylbiphenyl (5CB), 4-Cyano-4''-octylbiphenyl (8CB)
- Properties: Viscosity, birefringence, dielectric anisotropy, elasticity
- Applications: LCDs, thermometers, etc.

## Nanomaterials

- Nanomaterials have at least one dimension in the nanoscale (1-100 nm).
- They have unique properties arising from their small size such as large surface area to volume ratio.
- Examples: Carbon nanotubes, fullerenes, quantum dots, metal nanoparticles, etc.
- Properties: Mechanical, electrical, optical, magnetic, chemical, etc.
- Applications: Electronics, medicine, energy, environment, etc.

## Graphite and Fullerenes

- Graphite: Made of layers of hexagonal arrays of carbon atoms. Properties: High electrical and thermal conductivity. Applications: Battery electrodes, lubricants, etc.
- Fullerenes: Closed-cage carbon molecules. Example: C60 buckminsterfullerene. Properties: High chemical stability. Applications: Electronics, medicine, superconductors, etc.
