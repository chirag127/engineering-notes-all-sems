### 2. To enable the students to understand and apply the detailed concepts of spectroscopic techniques and

1. Spectroscopy: Study of interaction of electromagnetic radiation with matter. It involves absorption, emission, scattering of radiation.
2. Types: Based on type of radiation- UV-Vis spec, IR spec, NMR spec, Mass spec. Based on process- absorption spec, emission spec, scattering spec.
3. UV-Vis spectroscopy: Electromagnetic radiation in UV (200-400nm) and visible (400-800nm) range. Molecules absorb specific wavelengths. Measure absorbance at specific wavelengths to study structure of molecules.
4. Infrared spectroscopy (IR): Radiation in IR range (800-1600nm). Molecules vibrate and rotate, absorb IR at specific frequencies. Study vibrational and rotational energy levels to know molecular structure and bonds.
5. Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy (NMR): Nuclei like 1H and 13C absorb energy from applied magnetic field at specific resonance frequencies. Study resonance peaks to know structure of organic molecules.
