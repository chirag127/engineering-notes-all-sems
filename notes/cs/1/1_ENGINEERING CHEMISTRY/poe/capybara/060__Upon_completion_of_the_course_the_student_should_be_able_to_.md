### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Understand the basic concepts and principles of the subject matter covered in the course.
- Analyze and interpret data related to the subject matter.
- Apply theoretical knowledge to real-world scenarios.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of different strategies and techniques used in the subject matter.
- Develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills related to the subject matter.
- Communicate effectively using appropriate terminology and concepts related to the subject matter.
- Demonstrate ethical and professional behavior in the use and application of subject matter knowledge.
- Use technology and other resources effectively to enhance learning and problem-solving related to the subject matter.
- Identify and evaluate emerging trends and developments related to the subject matter.
- Collaborate effectively with others to achieve common goals related to the subject matter. 

Upon completion of this course, students will have a solid foundation in the subject matter and will be able to apply their knowledge to various real-life scenarios. They will also have developed critical thinking and problem-solving skills, which are essential in a professional setting. Moreover, they will have learned how to communicate effectively and professionally using appropriate terminology and concepts related to the subject matter. The use of technology and other resources will also have been emphasized to enhance learning and problem-solving related to the subject matter. Students will be equipped with the necessary knowledge and skills to keep up-to-date with emerging trends and developments related to the subject matter. Finally, students will have practiced effective collaboration with others, which will prepare them for future teamwork opportunities.
