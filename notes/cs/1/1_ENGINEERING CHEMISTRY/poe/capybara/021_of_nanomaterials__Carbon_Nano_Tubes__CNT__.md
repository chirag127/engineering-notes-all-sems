# Nanomaterials: Carbon Nano Tubes (CNT)

Carbon Nano Tubes (CNTs) are cylindrical carbon molecules that have a diameter of a few nanometers to several micrometers. They are a type of nanomaterial that exhibits unique properties, making them attractive for a wide range of applications.

Here are some key points about Carbon Nano Tubes:

- CNTs are made up of rolled-up sheets of graphene, a single layer of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice.
- They can be classified into two types: Single-Walled Carbon Nano Tubes (SWCNTs) and Multi-Walled Carbon Nano Tubes (MWCNTs), depending on the number of graphene layers they contain.
- CNTs have exceptional mechanical, thermal, and electrical properties due to their unique structure and high surface area-to-volume ratio.
- They have extremely high tensile strength, making them stronger than steel, yet much lighter.
- CNTs have excellent thermal conductivity, which makes them ideal for heat dissipation applications in electronics and other industries.
- They also exhibit high electrical conductivity, making them suitable for use in sensors, transistors, and other electronic devices.
- CNTs are being explored for use in a wide range of applications, including electronics, energy storage, drug delivery, and water purification, among others.

In conclusion, Carbon Nano Tubes are a fascinating class of nanomaterials with unique properties that make them attractive for a wide range of applications. As research into their properties and potential applications continues, we can expect to see CNTs being used in an ever-increasing number of industries and technologies in the coming years.
