### 4. Engineering Chemistry by K. Sesha Maheswaramma, Pearson

Engineering Chemistry is a vital subject for all engineering students, as it provides the foundation for understanding the chemical principles and their applications in various engineering fields. K. Sesha Maheswaramma's Engineering Chemistry by Pearson is an excellent book for learning the fundamentals of engineering chemistry. Here are some essential topics covered in the book:

- Introduction to Engineering Chemistry: This chapter covers the basic concepts of chemistry and their applications in engineering. It includes topics such as atomic structure, chemical bonding, and chemical reactions.

- Water Chemistry: This chapter discusses the physical and chemical properties of water and its importance in various engineering applications. It includes topics such as the structure of water, water hardness, and treatment of water.

- Corrosion and Its Control: This chapter covers the various types of corrosion and their mechanism. It includes topics such as electrochemical corrosion, stress corrosion, and corrosion control methods.

- Polymers: This chapter discusses the chemical and physical properties of polymers and their applications in various engineering fields. It includes topics such as polymerization, types of polymers, and polymer processing.

- Fuels and Combustion: This chapter covers the various types of fuels, their properties, and combustion reactions. It includes topics such as fossil fuels, alternative fuels, and combustion efficiency.

- Environmental Chemistry: This chapter discusses the impact of chemical pollutants on the environment and their control measures. It includes topics such as air pollution, water pollution, and soil pollution.

- Nanochemistry: This chapter covers the basics of nanotechnology and their applications in various engineering fields. It includes topics such as synthesis of nanoparticles, characterization methods, and nanomaterials.

Engineering Chemistry by K. Sesha Maheswaramma is a comprehensive book that covers all the essential topics of engineering chemistry. It is an excellent resource for students who want to build a strong foundation in chemical principles and their applications in engineering fields.
