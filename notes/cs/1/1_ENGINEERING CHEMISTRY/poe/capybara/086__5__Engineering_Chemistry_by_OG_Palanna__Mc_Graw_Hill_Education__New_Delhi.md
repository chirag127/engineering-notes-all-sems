### 5. Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna, Mc Graw Hill Education, New Delhi

Engineering Chemistry is a branch of chemistry that deals with the application of chemical principles in various engineering fields. The book "Engineering Chemistry" by OG Palanna, published by Mc Graw Hill Education in New Delhi, is a comprehensive guide for engineering students to understand the fundamental concepts and applications of chemistry in engineering.

The book covers the following topics:

1. Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding:

- The fundamental principles of atomic structure, electronic configuration, and chemical bonding are explained in detail.
- The concepts of ionic, covalent, and metallic bonding are discussed with relevant examples.
- The structure and properties of molecules are also covered.

2. States of Matter:

- The three states of matter, i.e., solid, liquid, and gas, are explained with their characteristics and properties.
- The behavior of gases is discussed in terms of the gas laws and the kinetic theory of gases.
- The concept of solutions and their properties, such as concentration, colligative properties, and osmosis, are also covered.

3. Chemical Thermodynamics:

- The laws of thermodynamics and the concept of entropy are explained with their applications in engineering.
- The thermochemical reactions and their calculations are also discussed.

4. Electrochemistry:

- The principles of electrochemistry and its applications in engineering, such as electroplating, corrosion, and batteries, are explained in detail.
- The concept of electrolysis and Faraday's laws are also covered.

5. Chemical Kinetics:

- The principles of chemical kinetics and the factors affecting the rate of a chemical reaction are discussed.
- The concept of catalysis and its applications in engineering are also covered.

6. Surface Chemistry:

- The concept of surface chemistry and the properties of surfaces, such as adsorption, catalysis, and colloids, are explained in detail.
- The applications of surface chemistry in engineering, such as in the production of catalysts and surfactants, are also discussed.

7. Environmental Chemistry:

- The principles of environmental chemistry and its applications in engineering, such as in the treatment of water and air pollution, are explained in detail.
- The concept of green chemistry and its importance in sustainable development are also covered.

Overall, "Engineering Chemistry" by OG Palanna is a comprehensive guide for engineering students to understand the fundamental concepts and applications of chemistry in various engineering fields. The book is written in a simple language and is easy to understand, making it an excellent resource for exam preparation.
