# Paris (POP)

Paris (POP) is the primary airport serving Paris, the capital city of France. Here are some key things to know about Paris (POP):

- **Location:** Paris (POP) is located 23 km (14 miles) northeast of Paris city center.
- **Terminals:** The airport has two terminals: Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. Terminal 2 is further divided into seven sub-terminals (2A to 2G).
- **Airlines:** Paris (POP) is a hub for Air France and a focus city for easyJet. It also serves as a hub for other airlines such as Delta Air Lines and KLM.
- **Destinations:** Paris (POP) serves a wide range of destinations around the world, including many cities in Europe, Asia, Africa, North America, and South America.
- **Passenger traffic:** In 2019, Paris (POP) handled over 76 million passengers, making it the 10th busiest airport in the world and the second busiest airport in Europe (after London Heathrow).
- **Facilities:** Paris (POP) offers a range of facilities for passengers, including shops, restaurants, lounges, and a variety of services such as currency exchange, luggage storage, and car rental.
- **Transportation:** There are several options for getting to and from Paris (POP), including taxis, buses, trains, and rental cars. The airport is also connected to Paris city center by the RER B train line.
- **Expansion plans:** Paris (POP) is currently undergoing a major expansion project called "Paris-CDG Express" which will add a new train service between the airport and Paris city center. The airport is also planning to expand Terminal 2 and build a new Terminal 4 in the future.

Overall, Paris (POP) is a significant airport in Europe and an important gateway to Paris and France. Its range of destinations, facilities, and transportation options make it a popular choice for travelers from around the world.
