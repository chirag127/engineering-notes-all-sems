### 6. Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawala, Dhanpat Rai Publishing Comp, New Delhi

- Engineering chemistry is a branch of engineering that deals with the application of chemistry principles in engineering practices.
- The book "Engineering Chemistry" by Shashi Chawala, published by Dhanpat Rai Publishing Company in New Delhi, is a comprehensive guide for studying engineering chemistry.
- The book covers various topics such as electrochemistry, fuels and combustion, lubricants, polymers, surface chemistry, and environmental chemistry.
- The book provides a detailed explanation of the fundamental concepts of engineering chemistry, making it an excellent resource for students pursuing engineering courses.
- The book also includes numerous examples, illustrations, and solved problems to help students understand the concepts better.
- The chapter on electrochemistry covers topics such as types of electrodes, electrochemical cells, and corrosion. It also includes a discussion on batteries, fuel cells, and electroplating.
- The chapter on fuels and combustion covers topics such as types of fuels, combustion processes, and different types of combustion engines. It also includes a discussion on alternative fuels.
- The chapter on lubricants covers topics such as types of lubricants, their properties, and applications. It also includes a discussion on additives and lubricating systems.
- The chapter on polymers covers topics such as types of polymers, their properties, and applications. It also includes a discussion on polymerization reactions and polymer processing.
- The chapter on surface chemistry covers topics such as adsorption, catalysis, and surfactants. It also includes a discussion on colloids and emulsions.
- The chapter on environmental chemistry covers topics such as water and air pollution, their sources, and control measures. It also includes a discussion on green chemistry and sustainable development.
- In conclusion, "Engineering Chemistry" by Shashi Chawala is an essential resource for students studying engineering chemistry. Its comprehensive coverage of the subject and numerous examples and solved problems make it an excellent study material for exams.
