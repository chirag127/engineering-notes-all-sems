### 2. Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd

Engineering Chemistry is an important subject for students pursuing engineering courses. The subject covers a wide range of topics that are essential for understanding the physical and chemical properties of materials used in engineering applications. Here are some key points to help you understand the book "Engineering Chemistry" by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd:

- The book covers a wide range of topics related to chemical reactions, chemical equilibrium, acids and bases, electrochemistry, corrosion, fuels, lubricants, and polymers.
- The book is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles of chemistry that are relevant to engineering applications.
- The book also includes a detailed discussion on the properties and applications of various engineering materials such as ceramics, glasses, composites, and semiconductors.
- The book is divided into several chapters, each of which covers a specific topic related to engineering chemistry.
- The book includes numerous examples, illustrations, and diagrams to help students understand the concepts discussed in each chapter.
- The book also includes a large number of practice problems and exercises to help students test their understanding of the subject.
- The book is written in a clear and concise language, making it easy for students to understand and follow the concepts discussed in each chapter.
- The book is an excellent resource for students pursuing engineering courses, as well as for professionals working in the field of engineering who want to deepen their understanding of the fundamental principles of chemistry.

In conclusion, "Engineering Chemistry" by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd is an essential resource for students pursuing engineering courses. The book covers a wide range of topics related to chemistry and engineering, providing a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles of chemistry that are essential for understanding the properties and applications of materials used in engineering applications. The book is well-organized, easy to understand, and includes numerous examples and practice problems to help students test their understanding of the subject.
