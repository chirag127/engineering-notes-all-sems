# Theoretical Calculation of Calorific Value by Dulong's Method, Ranking of Coal, Analysis of Coal

Coal is an important fossil fuel used for various industrial and commercial purposes. The calorific value of coal is an important parameter that determines its quality and value. In this article, we will discuss the theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong's method, ranking of coal, and analysis of coal.

## Theoretical Calculation of Calorific Value by Dulong's Method
The calorific value of coal is defined as the amount of heat energy released when a unit mass of coal is burned completely. Dulong's method is a theoretical method used to calculate the calorific value of coal. According to this method, the calorific value of coal can be calculated using the following formula:

Calorific Value = (83.14 x C% + 34.18 x (H2 - O2/8) + 93.05 x S%) / 100

Where C%, H2, O2, and S% are the percentages of carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, and sulfur present in the coal, respectively.

## Ranking of Coal
Coal is ranked based on its carbon content, moisture content, and calorific value. The ranking of coal is as follows:

1. Anthracite: This is the highest rank of coal and has a high carbon content, low moisture content, and high calorific value.
2. Bituminous: This is the most common type of coal and has a medium carbon content, medium moisture content, and medium calorific value.
3. Lignite: This is a low-rank coal and has a low carbon content, high moisture content, and low calorific value.
4. Sub-bituminous: This is a lower-middle rank coal and has a lower carbon content, higher moisture content, and lower calorific value.

## Analysis of Coal
Coal analysis is the process of determining the composition and properties of coal. The analysis of coal includes the following parameters:

1. Proximate Analysis: This analysis determines the moisture content, volatile matter, fixed carbon, and ash content of coal.
2. Ultimate Analysis: This analysis determines the carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, sulfur, and oxygen content of coal.
3. Gross Calorific Value: This analysis determines the amount of heat energy released when a unit mass of coal is burned completely.

In conclusion, the theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong's method, ranking of coal, and analysis of coal are important aspects that determine the quality and value of coal. These parameters are used in various industries and commercial sectors for the efficient use of coal as a fuel.
