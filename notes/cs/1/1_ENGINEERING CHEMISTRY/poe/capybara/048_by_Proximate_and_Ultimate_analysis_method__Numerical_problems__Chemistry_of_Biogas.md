# By Proximate and Ultimate Analysis Method

- Proximate analysis is the measurement of the moisture, volatile matter, ash and fixed carbon in a sample of fuel.
- Ultimate analysis is the determination of the chemical composition of a sample, which typically includes the percentage of carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, sulfur, and oxygen.
- Proximate analysis is used to determine the quality and performance of fuel, while ultimate analysis is used to determine the fuel's chemical composition.

# Numerical problems

- A biogas sample is found to have the following composition by volume: 50% methane, 20% carbon dioxide, 20% nitrogen, 5% oxygen, and 5% hydrogen. Calculate the heating value of this biogas sample.

- Solution: 
The heating value of methane is 55.5 MJ/kg. We can calculate the heating value of the biogas sample by first calculating the heating value of each component and then taking a weighted average. 

- Heating value of methane: 50% x 55.5 MJ/kg = 27.75 MJ/kg
- Heating value of carbon dioxide: 20% x 0 MJ/kg = 0 MJ/kg
- Heating value of nitrogen: 20% x 0 MJ/kg = 0 MJ/kg
- Heating value of oxygen: 5% x 0 MJ/kg = 0 MJ/kg
- Heating value of hydrogen: 5% x 141.8 MJ/kg = 7.09 MJ/kg

- Weighted average: (27.75 + 0 + 0 + 0 + 7.09) / 100 = 0.35 MJ/m3

# Chemistry of Biogas

- Biogas is a gas mixture produced by the anaerobic digestion or fermentation of organic matter, such as agricultural waste, food waste, or sewage.
- Biogas typically contains 50-70% methane, 20-40% carbon dioxide, and small amounts of other gases, such as nitrogen and hydrogen.
- The chemical reaction that produces biogas is called anaerobic digestion. In this process, microorganisms break down organic matter in the absence of oxygen, producing biogas as a byproduct.
- The main chemical components of biogas are methane (CH4) and carbon dioxide (CO2). Methane is the primary component of biogas and is responsible for its combustible properties. Carbon dioxide is a non-combustible gas that is typically removed from biogas before it is used as a fuel.
- Biogas can be used as a renewable energy source for heating, cooking, and electricity generation. It is a sustainable alternative to fossil fuels, as it is produced from organic waste that would otherwise be discarded.
