Proximate analysis is a method used to determine the amounts of macronutrients (carbohydrates, proteins, and fats) and micronutrients (vitamins and minerals) in a sample of food or feed. This analysis provides information about the energy content and nutrient composition of the sample.

Ultimate analysis is a method used to determine the elemental composition of a sample, including the amounts of carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, sulfur, and other elements. This analysis provides information about the chemical structure of the sample and can be used to identify the type of material and its potential uses.

Numerical problems are mathematical problems in which numerical values are used to find solutions. These problems can be solved using a variety of mathematical techniques, such as algebra, calculus, and numerical methods.

Chemistry of biogas refers to the chemical processes involved in the production of biogas, which is a mixture of methane and carbon dioxide produced by the anaerobic digestion of organic matter. This includes the conversion of organic matter into simple sugars, the production of acetic acid and other organic acids, and the final production of methane by methanogenic bacteria. Understanding the chemistry of biogas is important for optimizing the production of biogas and for understanding the environmental impact of biogas production.
