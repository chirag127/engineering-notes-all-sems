stereochemistry.

Stereochemistry is the study of the spatial arrangement of atoms in molecules and their effect on the properties of the molecule. It deals with the three-dimensional arrangement of atoms in a molecule and how this arrangement affects the molecule's chemical and physical properties. Stereochemistry is important in areas such as drug design, as the spatial arrangement of atoms in a molecule can affect how the molecule interacts with other molecules, including enzymes and receptors. Stereoisomers, which are molecules with the same chemical formula but different arrangements of atoms, can have different properties and biological activities.
