### 2. Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd

Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara is a textbook for engineering students, published by S Chand & Co Ltd. It covers fundamental concepts in chemistry and their applications in various branches of engineering. The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for students to understand and retain the information. It includes topics such as chemical thermodynamics, chemical kinetics, electrochemistry, corrosion, water treatment, and environmental chemistry. The book also includes numerous examples and practice problems to help reinforce the concepts covered. Additionally, it includes a comprehensive glossary of terms and a list of references for further reading. Overall, Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara is a comprehensive resource for engineering students looking to gain a strong foundation in the principles of chemistry.
