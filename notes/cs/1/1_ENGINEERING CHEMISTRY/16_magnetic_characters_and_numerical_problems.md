Characters:

Characters are the people, animals, or other entities that appear in a story, play, or other form of narrative. They are the central figures in a narrative and drive the plot forward.

1. Protagonist: The protagonist is the main character in a story, and is often the hero or heroine of the tale.

2. Antagonist: The antagonist is the character who opposes the protagonist and creates conflict in the story.

3. Supporting characters: Supporting characters are characters who play a secondary role in the story and support the protagonist or antagonist.

Advantages of characters in a narrative include:

1. Engagement: Characters help to engage the reader or viewer by providing a connection to the story and making it more relatable.

2. Plot development: Characters drive the plot forward and help to develop the story.

Disadvantages of characters in a narrative include:

1. Stereotyping: Characters can be stereotypical and perpetuate negative or harmful stereotypes.

2. Flat characters: Characters can be flat and one-dimensional, lacking depth and complexity.

Numerical problems are mathematical problems that require numerical solutions, such as finding the value of an unknown variable. Numerical problems can be found in a variety of fields, including engineering, physics, and finance.

1. Real-world applications: Numerical problems are often based on real-world scenarios and can be used to solve real-world problems.

2. Problem-solving skills: Solving numerical problems helps to develop problem-solving skills and critical thinking.

Disadvantages of numerical problems include:

1. Complexity: Numerical problems can be complex and difficult to solve, requiring advanced mathematical skills.

2. Limited solutions: Numerical problems often have limited solutions, and finding the correct solution can be challenging.
