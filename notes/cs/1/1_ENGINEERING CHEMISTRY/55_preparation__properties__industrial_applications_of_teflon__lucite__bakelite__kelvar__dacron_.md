Preparation, properties, industrial applications of Teflon, Lucite, Bakelite, Kelvar, Dacron,

1. Teflon: Teflon is a synthetic fluoropolymer with a low coefficient of friction and high temperature resistance. It has non-stick properties and is used in cookware, as a coating for electrical wire and cable, and in industrial applications where chemical resistance is required.

2. Lucite: Lucite is a type of acrylic plastic with high clarity and transparency. It is used in a variety of applications, including windows, skylights, and signs, as well as in the production of aircraft canopies and lenses for lights and cameras.

3. Bakelite: Bakelite is a type of phenolic plastic known for its electrical insulation properties, heat resistance, and durability. It was widely used in the early 20th century for electrical components, telephones, and radios, and is now mainly used in the production of electrical insulators and circuit breaker parts.

4. Kelvar: Kelvar is a type of aramid fiber with high strength and heat resistance. It is used in a variety of applications, including body armor, ropes, and composites for aircraft and automobiles.

5. Dacron: Dacron is a type of polyester fiber with high strength, elasticity, and resistance to moisture and UV light. It is used in a variety of applications, including clothing, bedding, and upholstery, as well as in the production of ropes and sailcloth.
