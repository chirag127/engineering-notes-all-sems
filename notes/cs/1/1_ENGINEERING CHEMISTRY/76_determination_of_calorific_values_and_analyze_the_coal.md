Calorific Value:
- Calorific value is the amount of energy released when a substance is burned.
- It is used to measure the energy content of fuels such as coal, natural gas, and biomass.
- Calorific value is expressed in units of energy per unit of mass (e.g. joules per kilogram).

Analysis of Coal:
- Coal is a fossil fuel that is widely used as a source of energy for power generation.
- The quality of coal is determined by its calorific value, ash content, sulfur content, and moisture content.
- Higher calorific value coal is more energy-dense and produces more energy when burned.
- Low-quality coal with high ash and sulfur content is less desirable as it produces more pollutants and greenhouse gases when burned.
- The analysis of coal helps to determine its suitability for use in power generation and other applications.
