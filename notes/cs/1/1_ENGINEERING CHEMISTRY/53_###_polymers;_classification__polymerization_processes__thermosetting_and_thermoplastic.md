### Polymers; Classification, Polymerization processes, Thermosetting and Thermoplastic

Polymers are large molecules made up of repeating units, called monomers. They have a wide range of applications, including in plastics, rubber, and textiles. 

Classification of polymers includes thermoplastics, elastomers, and thermosets. 

Polymerization is the process of chemically bonding monomers to form a polymer. There are several methods of polymerization, including addition polymerization and condensation polymerization. 

Thermosetting polymers are those that, once set, cannot be remelted. They are often used in applications where high heat resistance is needed, such as in electrical components. 

Thermoplastic polymers, on the other hand, can be melted and reshaped multiple times. They are commonly used in applications such as packaging and consumer goods.
