### Fuels and Combustion: Definition, Classification, Characteristics of a good fuel, Calorific

Fuels are substances that release energy when burned. Combustion is the process of burning fuel, which involves a chemical reaction between the fuel and an oxidizing agent (usually air) that releases heat and produces gases.

Fuel classification:
- Solid fuels: coal, wood, peat, etc.
- Liquid fuels: gasoline, diesel, kerosene, etc.
- Gaseous fuels: natural gas, propane, etc.

Characteristics of a good fuel:
- High calorific value: amount of heat released per unit of fuel.
- Availability: easily obtainable and stored.
- Ease of transportation: easy to transport from one place to another.
- Ease of handling: easy to handle and store.
- Low cost: affordable for widespread use.
- Clean burning: produces low emissions of pollutants.

Calorific value: the amount of heat released when a fuel is burned, usually measured in units of energy per unit of fuel (e.g. joules per kilogram or BTU per pound).
