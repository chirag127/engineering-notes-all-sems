Values, Gross & Net calorific value, Determination of calorific value by Bomb Calorimeter,

Gross Calorific Value (GCV) is the total amount of heat released upon complete combustion of a substance, including the heat released from the reaction of the substance with the oxygen in the air. Net Calorific Value (NCV) is the amount of heat released upon complete combustion of a substance, excluding the heat released from the reaction of the substance with the oxygen in the air.

Determination of calorific value by Bomb Calorimeter: A bomb calorimeter is used to measure the heat of combustion of a substance. The substance is placed in a bomb with oxygen, and the bomb is then ignited. The heat released from the reaction is transferred to water surrounding the bomb, causing the temperature of the water to rise. The change in temperature of the water is used to calculate the heat of combustion of the substance.
