### 1. To enable the students to understand about the Chemistry of Atomic and Molecular structure, Chemistry of

- Atomic and molecular structure refers to the arrangement and interactions of atoms and molecules in matter.
- Atomic structure deals with the subatomic particles that make up atoms, such as protons, neutrons, and electrons, and how they are distributed in the nucleus and the electron cloud.
- Molecular structure deals with the formation and properties of molecules, which are groups of atoms held together by chemical bonds, such as covalent, ionic, or metallic bonds.
- Chemistry of atomic and molecular structure involves studying the following concepts and topics:

  - Atomic models and theories, such as the Bohr model, the quantum mechanical model, and the orbital hybridization theory, that explain the behavior and characteristics of atoms and their electrons.
  - Periodic table and periodic trends, such as atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity, and electron affinity, that show the patterns and variations of atomic properties across different elements and groups.
  - Chemical bonding and molecular geometry, such as the Lewis structure, the VSEPR theory, the valence bond theory, and the molecular orbital theory, that describe the formation, shape, and polarity of molecules and their bonds.
  - Intermolecular forces and states of matter, such as the London dispersion forces, the dipole-dipole forces, the hydrogen bonding, and the van der Waals forces, that explain the attractions and repulsions between molecules and their effects on the physical properties and phase transitions of matter.
