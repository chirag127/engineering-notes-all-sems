### CO-1 Get an understanding of the theoretical principles of chemistry of molecular structure, bonding and

- Molecular structure refers to the arrangement of atoms in a molecule and the chemical bonds that hold them together.
- Bonding is the process of forming stable associations between atoms by sharing or transferring electrons.
- Theoretical principles of chemistry of molecular structure and bonding include:

  - The concept of atomic orbitals and molecular orbitals, which describe the regions of space where electrons are most likely to be found around atoms and molecules, respectively.
  - The concept of hybridization, which explains how atomic orbitals can combine to form new hybrid orbitals with different shapes and orientations, suitable for forming bonds with other atoms.
  - The concept of valence bond theory, which describes how covalent bonds are formed by the overlap of hybrid orbitals or atomic orbitals from different atoms, resulting in the formation of sigma and pi bonds.
  - The concept of molecular orbital theory, which describes how covalent bonds are formed by the combination of atomic orbitals from different atoms to form molecular orbitals, which can be bonding or antibonding, depending on their energy and symmetry.
  - The concept of Lewis structures, which represent the arrangement of valence electrons in a molecule using dots and lines, indicating the number and type of bonds and the formal charges of atoms.
  - The concept of resonance, which explains how some molecules can have more than one valid Lewis structure, and how the actual structure is a hybrid of these resonance structures, with delocalized electrons and partial bonds.
  - The concept of VSEPR theory, which predicts the three-dimensional shape of a molecule based on the repulsion between electron pairs around a central atom.
  - The concept of polarity, which describes how some molecules have uneven distribution of charge, resulting in a dipole moment, which affects the intermolecular forces and physical properties of the molecule.
  - The concept of electronegativity, which measures the tendency of an atom to attract electrons in a bond, and how it affects the bond polarity and the polarity of the molecule.
  - The concept of bond order, bond length and bond energy, which describe the strength and stability of a bond, and how they are related to the number and type of bonds and the molecular orbital configuration.
