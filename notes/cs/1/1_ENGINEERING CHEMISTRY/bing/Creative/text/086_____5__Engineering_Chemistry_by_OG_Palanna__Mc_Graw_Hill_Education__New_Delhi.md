### 5. Engineering Chemistry by OG Palanna, Mc Graw Hill Education, New Delhi

- This is a textbook that covers the fundamental concepts and applications of engineering chemistry for undergraduate engineering students.
- The book is divided into 16 chapters, each focusing on a specific topic such as water treatment, corrosion, polymers, fuels, electrochemistry, nanotechnology, etc.
- The book provides a balanced coverage of both theoretical and practical aspects of engineering chemistry, with numerous examples, problems, and case studies.
- The book also includes appendices, glossary, and index for easy reference and revision.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, with diagrams, tables, and figures to illustrate the concepts and data.
- The book is suitable for students of various branches of engineering, such as civil, mechanical, electrical, electronics, chemical, etc.
