### K3

- K3 is a type of synthetic cannabinoid that mimics the effects of THC, the main psychoactive component of cannabis.
- K3 is also known as Spice, K2, or synthetic marijuana, and is sold as a herbal incense or potpourri that can be smoked or vaporized.
- K3 is not detected by standard drug tests and is often marketed as a legal alternative to cannabis, but it is illegal in many countries and states due to its health risks and potential for abuse.
- K3 can cause unpredictable and adverse effects such as anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, psychosis, seizures, vomiting, chest pain, and even death.
- K3 can also lead to dependence and withdrawal symptoms such as insomnia, irritability, depression, and cravings.
- K3 is not safe to use and can have serious consequences for physical and mental health.
