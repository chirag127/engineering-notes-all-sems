### Units Course Outcomes Bloom’s

- A unit course outcome is a statement that describes what a student should be able to do or demonstrate after completing a unit of study.
- A unit course outcome should be aligned with the course learning outcomes, which are the overall goals of the course.
- A unit course outcome should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).
- A unit course outcome should also indicate the level of cognitive skill or ability that the student is expected to demonstrate, according to Bloom's taxonomy of learning domains.
- Bloom's taxonomy is a framework that classifies learning objectives into six levels of complexity and specificity: knowledge, comprehension, application, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation.
- Knowledge is the lowest level of cognitive skill and involves recalling or recognizing facts, terms, concepts, or principles.
- Comprehension is the second level of cognitive skill and involves understanding the meaning, interpretation, or explanation of facts, terms, concepts, or principles.
- Application is the third level of cognitive skill and involves using or applying facts, terms, concepts, or principles to new or different situations or problems.
- Analysis is the fourth level of cognitive skill and involves breaking down or examining facts, terms, concepts, or principles into their component parts or relationships.
- Synthesis is the fifth level of cognitive skill and involves combining or integrating facts, terms, concepts, or principles to form new or original ideas, products, or solutions.
- Evaluation is the highest level of cognitive skill and involves judging or assessing the value, quality, relevance, or effectiveness of facts, terms, concepts, or principles based on criteria or standards.
- An example of a unit course outcome that uses Bloom's taxonomy is: "By the end of this unit, students will be able to evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of different leadership styles and apply them to various organizational contexts." This outcome indicates that the student is expected to demonstrate the highest level of cognitive skill (evaluation) and also use or apply the knowledge and comprehension of leadership styles to new or different situations or problems (application).
