# Water and Boiler Troubles Used in Industry

Water is a vital medium for many industrial and commercial processes, such as boilers, cooling towers, cooling systems and other closed circuit systems. However, water can also cause various problems in these systems, such as corrosion, scaling, fouling, and microbiological growth. These problems can affect the efficiency, safety, and reliability of the equipment, and may require costly repairs or replacements. Therefore, water treatment and quality control are essential for the proper operation and maintenance of water-based systems.

Some of the common water and boiler troubles used in industry are:

- **Priming**: This is the phenomenon of water droplets being carried away with the steam from the boiler, resulting in wet and low-quality steam. Priming can be caused by high water levels, sudden changes in steam demand, improper boiler design, or contaminated feed water. Priming can lead to water hammer, erosion, and damage to the steam system .
- **Foaming**: This is the formation of a layer of froth or stable foam on the surface of the water in the boiler, which reduces the steam space and increases the water level. Foaming can be caused by high concentrations of dissolved solids, organic matter, or oil in the feed water, or by low boiler pressure or temperature. Foaming can result in carryover, corrosion, and reduced efficiency .
- **Scaling**: This is the deposition of hard and insoluble minerals, such as calcium carbonate, on the internal surfaces of the boiler and the heat transfer equipment. Scaling can be caused by high hardness, alkalinity, or temperature of the feed water, or by inadequate water treatment or blowdown. Scaling can reduce the heat transfer efficiency, increase the fuel consumption, and cause overheating and failure of the metal parts  .
- **Corrosion**: This is the deterioration of the metal surfaces of the boiler and the water system due to chemical or electrochemical reactions with the water or the dissolved gases, such as oxygen and carbon dioxide. Corrosion can be caused by low pH, high dissolved oxygen, high chloride, or low alkalinity of the feed water, or by improper water treatment or material selection. Corrosion can lead to leaks, cracks, pitting, and reduced strength and life span of the equipment   .
- **Fouling**: This is the accumulation of soft and sticky substances, such as organic matter, oil, grease, or microbiological growth, on the internal surfaces of the boiler and the water system. Fouling can be caused by contamination of the feed water, inadequate water treatment, or low water velocity. Fouling can reduce the heat transfer efficiency, increase the pressure drop, and promote corrosion and scaling  .
- **Microbiological growth**: This is the proliferation of microorganisms, such as bacteria, algae, fungi, or protozoa, in the water system, especially in the cooling towers and the condensate return lines. Microbiological growth can be caused by the presence of nutrients, such as organic matter, phosphorus, or nitrogen, in the water, or by the favorable temperature and pH conditions. Microbiological growth can cause fouling, corrosion, biofilms, and health hazards  .

To prevent or minimize these water and boiler troubles, various water treatment methods and quality control measures are used, such as:

- **Softening**: This is the removal of hardness-causing minerals, such as calcium and magnesium, from the feed water, by using ion exchange resins, lime-soda process, or zeolite process. Softening can prevent or reduce scaling and corrosion .
- **Deaeration**: This is the removal of dissolved gases, such as oxygen and carbon dioxide, from the feed water, by using mechanical devices, such as vacuum pumps, steam injectors, or spray trays, or by using chemical agents, such as sodium sulfite or hydrazine. Deaeration can prevent or reduce corrosion and pitting .
- **pH adjustment**: This is the control of the acidity or alkalinity of the feed water, by using chemical agents, such as sodium hydroxide, sodium carbonate, or phosphates. pH adjustment can prevent or reduce corrosion and scaling .
