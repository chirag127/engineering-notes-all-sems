### CO-5 Develop the understanding of Chemical structure of polymers and its effect on their various properties

- Polymers are very long carbon-based molecules that have a wide range of beneficial structural (and sometimes thermal) properties.
- The chemical structure of polymers determines how they behave and interact with other molecules and substances.
- Some of the factors that affect the properties of polymers are:
  - Chain length: The longer the polymer chain, the higher the molecular weight, the stronger the intermolecular forces, and the higher the melting point, tensile strength, and viscosity .
  - Branching: The more branched the polymer chain, the lower the degree of crystallinity, the lower the density, the lower the melting point, and the higher the flexibility .
  - Side groups: The presence of different functional groups or atoms attached to the polymer chain can affect the polarity, solubility, reactivity, and compatibility of the polymer with other substances .
  - Cross-linking: The formation of covalent bonds between different polymer chains can increase the rigidity, hardness, thermal stability, and resistance to solvents and deformation of the polymer .
  - Crystallinity: The degree of order and alignment of the polymer chains in the solid state can affect the transparency, stiffness, elasticity, and thermal conductivity of the polymer .
  - Hydrogen bonding: The formation of intermolecular forces between the hydrogen atoms and electronegative atoms (such as oxygen, nitrogen, or fluorine) in the polymer chain can increase the strength, toughness, and melting point of the polymer.
