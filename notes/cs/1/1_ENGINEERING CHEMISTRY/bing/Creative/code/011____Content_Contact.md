## Content Contact

- Content contact is a term used in media studies to describe the degree of exposure or engagement that a person has with a specific type of media content.
- Content contact can be measured by various indicators, such as the frequency, duration, intensity, diversity, and selectivity of media use.
- Content contact can also be influenced by various factors, such as the availability, accessibility, affordability, attractiveness, and relevance of media content.
- Content contact can have various effects on the person, such as cognitive, affective, behavioral, and social outcomes.
- Content contact can vary across different media platforms, genres, formats, and topics. For example, a person may have high content contact with news, but low content contact with fiction.
- Content contact can also vary across different contexts, such as personal, interpersonal, organizational, and societal. For example, a person may have different content contact with media at home, at work, or in public spaces.
