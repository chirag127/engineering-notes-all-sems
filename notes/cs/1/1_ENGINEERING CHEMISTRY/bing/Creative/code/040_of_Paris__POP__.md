# Paris

Paris is the capital and most populous city of France, with an estimated population of 2,165,423 residents in 2019 in an area of more than 105 km² (41 sq mi), making it the fourth-most populated city in the European Union as well as the 30th most densely populated city in the world in 2022. Paris is a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture, and is often regarded as the most romantic city in the world.

## History

Paris was founded in the 3rd century BCE by a Celtic people called the Parisii, who gave the city its name. The city was conquered by the Romans in the 1st century BCE, and became one of the most important cities of the Roman Empire. Paris was the capital of the Frankish kingdom in the 6th century, and later the capital of France under the Capetian, Valois, and Bourbon dynasties. Paris was the center of the French Revolution in the 18th century, and the site of many political and social upheavals in the 19th and 20th centuries, such as the Paris Commune, the World Wars, and the May 1968 protests. Paris is also known for its cultural and artistic movements, such as the Gothic, Renaissance, Baroque, Rococo, Neoclassical, Romantic, Realist, Impressionist, Modernist, and Postmodernist styles .

## Geography

Paris is located in the north-central part of France, along the Seine River, which divides the city into two parts: the Right Bank (Rive Droite) and the Left Bank (Rive Gauche). The city is surrounded by a ring road called the Boulevard Périphérique, and is divided into 20 administrative districts called arrondissements, which are numbered from 1 to 20 in a clockwise spiral from the center. The city covers an area of 105.4 km² (40.7 sq mi), and has an average altitude of 35 m (115 ft) above sea level. The highest point in the city is the hill of Montmartre, which reaches 130 m (427 ft) at its summit .

## Climate

Paris has a temperate oceanic climate, with mild winters and warm summers. The average annual temperature is 12.3 °C (54.1 °F), and the average annual precipitation is 641 mm (25.2 in). The city is influenced by the Atlantic Ocean, which moderates the temperature and brings rainfall throughout the year. The coldest month is January, with an average temperature of 5.0 °C (41.0 °F), and the warmest month is July, with an average temperature of 20.6 °C (69.1 °F). The wettest month is May, with an average precipitation of 65 mm (2.6 in), and the driest month is August, with an average precipitation of 43 mm (1.7 in). The city can experience heat waves, cold snaps, thunderstorms, fog, and snow, but these are usually rare or short-lived .

## Culture

Paris is a world-renowned center of culture, with a rich and diverse heritage of art, architecture, literature, music, cinema, fashion, and cuisine. The city is home to many famous landmarks, such as the Eiffel Tower, the Arc de Triomphe, the Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Louvre Museum, the Musée d'Orsay, the Sacré-Cœur Basilica, and the Palace of Versailles. The city also has many museums, galleries, theaters, opera houses, concert halls, libraries, parks, gardens, and monuments that showcase its artistic and historical legacy. Paris is also known for its vibrant and cosmopolitan nightlife, with many bars, cafes, restaurants, clubs, and cabarets that cater to different tastes and preferences. The city hosts many cultural events and festivals throughout the year, such as the Paris Fashion Week, the Paris Book Fair, the Paris Jazz Festival, the Bastille Day, and the Nuit Blanche  .
