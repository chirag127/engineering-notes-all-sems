# K3

- K3 is a synthetic, artificially produced form of vitamin K that doesn’t occur naturally .
- K3 is also known as menadione .
- K3 may be converted into K2 in your liver.
- K3 is important for blood clotting and bone health.
- K3 has demonstrated anticancer and antibacterial properties in test-tube studies.
- K3 is not approved for human use and may cause serious side effects such as liver damage, anemia, and oxidative stress .
- K3 is sometimes used as a supplement for animals, especially poultry and fish, but it may also pose health risks for them .
- K3 is not the same as Schedule K-3 (Form 1065), which is a tax form for reporting international transactions of partnerships.
