# Applicable in industrial process

- Industrial processes are the activities that transform raw materials into finished products or intermediate goods.
- Industrial processes can be classified into four main categories: chemical, physical, biological and mechanical.
- Chemical processes involve changing the chemical composition or structure of the materials, such as combustion, oxidation, reduction, polymerization, etc.
- Physical processes involve changing the physical properties or state of the materials, such as melting, freezing, evaporation, condensation, etc.
- Biological processes involve using living organisms or enzymes to modify the materials, such as fermentation, biodegradation, biosynthesis, etc.
- Mechanical processes involve applying force or motion to the materials, such as cutting, grinding, drilling, molding, etc.
- Some examples of industrial processes are:

  - Oil refining: a chemical process that separates crude oil into different fractions based on their boiling points and uses various catalysts and reactors to convert them into useful products, such as gasoline, diesel, kerosene, etc.
  - Paper making: a physical and mechanical process that converts wood pulp into paper sheets by removing water and impurities, and then coating, drying and cutting them into desired sizes and shapes.
  - Brewing: a biological and chemical process that converts malted barley, water, hops and yeast into beer by mashing, boiling, fermenting and bottling them.
  - Steel making: a chemical and physical process that converts iron ore, coke and limestone into steel by smelting, refining and casting them.
