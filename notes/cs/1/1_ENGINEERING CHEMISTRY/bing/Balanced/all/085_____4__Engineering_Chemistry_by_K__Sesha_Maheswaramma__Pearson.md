# Engineering Chemistry by K. Sesha Maheswaramma, Pearson

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook that covers the fundamental concepts and applications of chemistry in the field of engineering.
- The book is divided into four units: Water Technology, Fuels and Combustion, Polymers and Composites, and Corrosion and Surface Engineering.
- The book aims to provide a comprehensive and systematic treatment of the topics, with an emphasis on problem-solving and numerical methods.
- The book also includes numerous examples, exercises, and case studies to illustrate the relevance and practical aspects of chemistry in engineering.
- The book is suitable for undergraduate students of engineering, as well as for professionals and researchers who want to refresh their knowledge of chemistry and its applications.
