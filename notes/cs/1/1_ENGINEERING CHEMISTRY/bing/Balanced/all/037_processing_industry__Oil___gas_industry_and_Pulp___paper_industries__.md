# Processing Industry, Oil & Gas Industry and Pulp & Paper Industry

- Processing industry is a broad term that covers various sectors that transform raw materials into finished products, such as food, beverages, textiles, chemicals, metals, plastics, etc.
- Oil & gas industry is a sector that explores, extracts, refines, transports, and markets petroleum and natural gas products, such as gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, lubricants, asphalt, etc.
- Pulp & paper industry is a sector that produces pulp from wood or other fibrous materials, and manufactures paper and paperboard products, such as newsprint, printing paper, packaging paper, tissue paper, etc.

## Processing Industry

- The processing industry is a major contributor to the global economy, as it adds value to the raw materials and creates a variety of products for different markets and consumers.
- The processing industry is also a significant user of energy, water, and other resources, and generates various environmental impacts, such as greenhouse gas emissions, waste, and pollution.
- The processing industry faces many challenges and opportunities, such as increasing demand, changing consumer preferences, technological innovation, digital transformation, sustainability, and competitiveness.

## Oil & Gas Industry

- The oil & gas industry is a vital source of energy for the world, as it supplies about 55% of the global primary energy demand, according to the International Energy Agency (IEA).
- The oil & gas industry is also a major employer, investor, and taxpayer, as it supports millions of jobs, contributes to public revenues, and funds infrastructure and social development projects.
- The oil & gas industry faces many challenges and opportunities, such as declining reserves, volatile prices, geopolitical risks, environmental regulations, energy transition, and innovation.

## Pulp & Paper Industry

- The pulp & paper industry is a key provider of materials for communication, education, hygiene, and packaging, as it produces about 420 million tonnes of paper and paperboard annually, according to the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).
- The pulp & paper industry is also a major user of wood, water, and energy, and generates various environmental impacts, such as deforestation, greenhouse gas emissions, waste, and pollution.
- The pulp & paper industry faces many challenges and opportunities, such as declining demand, digitalization, recycling, innovation, and sustainability.
