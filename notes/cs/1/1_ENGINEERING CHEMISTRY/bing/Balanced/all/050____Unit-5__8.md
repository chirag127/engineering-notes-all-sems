## Unit-5: 8

- This unit covers the topic of **8-bit microprocessors**, which are electronic devices that can perform arithmetic, logic, and control operations on 8-bit data.
- The unit introduces the basic concepts of microprocessor architecture, instruction set, addressing modes, and interfacing techniques.
- The unit also discusses the features and applications of the **Intel 8085 microprocessor**, which is one of the most popular 8-bit microprocessors in the history of computing.
- The unit consists of the following subtopics:

  - 8.1 Introduction to Microprocessors
    - Definition and classification of microprocessors
    - Evolution and generations of microprocessors
    - Advantages and disadvantages of microprocessors
    - Applications of microprocessors
  - 8.2 Architecture of 8085 Microprocessor
    - Block diagram and pin diagram of 8085 microprocessor
    - Registers and flags of 8085 microprocessor
    - Bus system and bus signals of 8085 microprocessor
    - Clock and reset circuits of 8085 microprocessor
  - 8.3 Instruction Set of 8085 Microprocessor
    - Types and formats of instructions
    - Opcode and operand of instructions
    - Classification and examples of instructions
    - Instruction cycle and timing diagram of instructions
  - 8.4 Addressing Modes of 8085 Microprocessor
    - Definition and types of addressing modes
    - Advantages and disadvantages of addressing modes
    - Examples and calculations of addressing modes
  - 8.5 Interfacing of 8085 Microprocessor
    - Definition and need of interfacing
    - Types and examples of interfacing devices
    - Memory and I/O interfacing techniques
    - Interrupts and DMA in 8085 microprocessor
