# Engineering Chemistry by Rath & Singh, 2nd Edition, Cengage Learning India Pvt Ltd Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook that covers the fundamental concepts and applications of chemistry in the field of engineering.
- The book is divided into 12 chapters, each focusing on a specific topic related to engineering chemistry, such as water, fuels, corrosion, polymers, nanomaterials, etc.
- The book aims to provide a comprehensive and updated knowledge of the subject, as well as to develop the analytical and problem-solving skills of the students.
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with numerous examples, diagrams, tables, and figures to illustrate the concepts and principles.
- The book also includes multiple-choice questions, review questions, numerical problems, and case studies at the end of each chapter to test the understanding and application of the students.
- The book is suitable for the first-year undergraduate students of engineering and technology, as well as for the students preparing for various competitive examinations.
