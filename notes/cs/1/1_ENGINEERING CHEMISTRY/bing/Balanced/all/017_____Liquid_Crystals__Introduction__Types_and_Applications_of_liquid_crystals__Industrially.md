# Liquid Crystals; Introduction, Types and Applications of liquid crystals, Industrially

- Liquid crystals are substances that exhibit a phase of matter that is between a solid and a liquid, with some degree of molecular order and mobility  .
- Liquid crystals can be classified into two main categories: **thermotropic** and **lyotropic** .
  - Thermotropic liquid crystals are formed by heating a crystalline solid and lose their order when heated above a certain temperature .
  - Lyotropic liquid crystals are formed by dissolving certain molecules in a solvent and depend on the concentration and temperature of the solution .
- Liquid crystals can also be classified by the type of molecular order they exhibit, such as **nematic**, **smectic**, **cholesteric**, **discotic**, and **columnar** .
  - Nematic liquid crystals have molecules that are aligned along a common direction, called the director, but have no positional order .
  - Smectic liquid crystals have molecules that are arranged in layers, with each layer having a different director .
  - Cholesteric liquid crystals are a type of nematic liquid crystals that have a helical twist of the director along the axis of the layers .
  - Discotic liquid crystals have molecules that are shaped like discs and are arranged in columns .
  - Columnar liquid crystals have molecules that are shaped like rods and are arranged in columns .
- Liquid crystals have many applications in various fields, such as display devices, sensors, lasers, medical diagnostics, and pharmaceuticals  .
  - Display devices use liquid crystals to modulate the light passing through them, creating images on screens .
  - Sensors use liquid crystals to detect changes in temperature, pressure, electric field, magnetic field, or chemical environment, by measuring the changes in their optical properties .
  - Lasers use liquid crystals to control the polarization, wavelength, and intensity of the laser beam, by applying electric or magnetic fields .
  - Medical diagnostics use liquid crystals to monitor the health status of patients, such as blood glucose level, cholesterol level, or infection, by using liquid crystal-based biosensors .
  - Pharmaceuticals use liquid crystals to deliver drugs to specific targets in the body, such as tumors, by using liquid crystal-based carriers or vesicles .
