### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

- Identify and explain the main concepts, theories and methods of sociology.
- Apply sociological perspectives and analytical tools to various social phenomena and issues.
- Compare and contrast different sociological approaches and paradigms.
- Evaluate the strengths and limitations of different sources of sociological data and evidence.
- Conduct basic sociological research using appropriate methods and ethical principles.
- Communicate sociological arguments and findings effectively in written and oral forms.
- Demonstrate critical thinking and reflexivity in relation to their own and others' social positions and experiences.
