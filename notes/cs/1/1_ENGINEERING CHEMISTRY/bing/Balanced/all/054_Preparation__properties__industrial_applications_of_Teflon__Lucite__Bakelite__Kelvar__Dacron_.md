# Preparation, properties, industrial applications of Teflon, Lucite, Bakelite, Kelvar, Dacron

## Teflon

- Teflon is a synthetic polymer of tetrafluoroethylene (C2F4) with the formula (C2F4)n.
- It is prepared by free radical polymerization of tetrafluoroethylene in a pressurized vessel at high temperature with a catalyst such as peroxide or persulfate.
- It has the following properties:
  - It is a white solid compound at room temperature with a density of about 2.2 g/cm3.
  - It has a high melting point of about 600 K and a low glass transition temperature of about 130 K.
  - It is chemically inert and resistant to most acids, bases, solvents, and oxidizing agents. The only chemicals that can affect it are alkali metals and some fluorinating agents.
  - It has a low coefficient of friction and a high dielectric strength, making it suitable for non-stick coatings and electrical insulation.
  - It has a low water absorption capacity and a high thermal stability, allowing it to withstand extreme temperatures and humidity.
- It has the following industrial applications:
  - It is widely used as a non-stick coating for cookware, bakeware, and other utensils.
  - It is also used as a lubricant for bearings, gears, valves, and other mechanical parts.
  - It is employed as a protective coating for wires, cables, connectors, and printed circuit boards in the electrical and electronics industry.
  - It is also used as a fabric protector for clothing, carpets, upholstery, and other textiles, as it repels water, oil, and stains.
  - It is also used as a component of some medical devices, such as catheters, sutures, implants, and artificial joints, as it is biocompatible and resistant to infection.

## Lucite

- Lucite is a trade name for polymethyl methacrylate (PMMA), a transparent thermoplastic polymer of methyl methacrylate (MMA) with the formula (C5O2H8)n.
- It is prepared by free radical polymerization of methyl methacrylate in a bulk or suspension process with a catalyst such as benzoyl peroxide or azobisisobutyronitrile.
- It has the following properties:
  - It is a clear, colorless, and lightweight material with a density of about 1.2 g/cm3.
  - It has a high refractive index of about 1.49 and a low dispersion of about 0.009, making it suitable for optical applications.
  - It has a good resistance to ultraviolet light, weathering, and aging, but it is prone to scratching and cracking.
  - It has a moderate mechanical strength and rigidity, but it is brittle and has a low impact resistance.
  - It has a low thermal conductivity and a high thermal expansion coefficient, making it susceptible to thermal stress and deformation.
- It has the following industrial applications:
  - It is widely used as a substitute for glass in windows, skylights, lenses, mirrors, and other transparent objects.
  - It is also used as a material for signs, displays, sculptures, jewelry, and other decorative items.
  - It is also used as a component of some medical devices, such as dentures, artificial eyes, and intraocular lenses, as it is biocompatible and easy to mold.
  - It is also used as a matrix for some composite materials, such as carbon fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) and glass fiber reinforced plastic (GFRP), as it provides transparency and strength.

## Bakelite

- Bakelite is a trade name for phenol-formaldehyde resin, a thermosetting polymer of phenol and formaldehyde with the formula (C6H6O-CH2O)n.
- It is prepared by condensation polymerization of phenol and formaldehyde in an acidic or alkaline medium with a catalyst such as zinc chloride, hydrochloric acid, or ammonia .
- It has the following properties :
  - It is a dark brown or black solid material with a density of about 1.4 g/cm3.
  - It has a high melting point of about 800 K and a high decomposition temperature of about 1000 K, making it suitable for high-temperature applications.
