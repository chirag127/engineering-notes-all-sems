### Units Course Outcomes Bloom's

- Units course outcomes are statements that describe what students are expected to know and be able to do by the end of a unit of study.
- Bloom's taxonomy is a framework that classifies learning objectives into six levels of cognitive complexity: remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate, and create.
- Units course outcomes should be aligned with Bloom's taxonomy to ensure that students achieve different levels of learning and demonstrate higher-order thinking skills.
- Some examples of units course outcomes and their corresponding Bloom's levels are:

  - By the end of this unit, students will be able to **remember** the definitions and formulas of basic concepts in physics, such as force, mass, acceleration, energy, and momentum. (Bloom's level: remember)
  - By the end of this unit, students will be able to **understand** the principles and applications of Newton's laws of motion and conservation of energy and momentum. (Bloom's level: understand)
  - By the end of this unit, students will be able to **apply** the concepts and formulas of physics to solve problems involving motion, forces, energy, and collisions. (Bloom's level: apply)
  - By the end of this unit, students will be able to **analyze** the relationships and differences between various types of motion, such as linear, circular, and projectile motion. (Bloom's level: analyze)
  - By the end of this unit, students will be able to **evaluate** the validity and accuracy of different methods and assumptions used in solving physics problems. (Bloom's level: evaluate)
  - By the end of this unit, students will be able to **create** their own experiments and models to demonstrate and explain the phenomena of physics. (Bloom's level: create)

- Units course outcomes should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) to ensure that they are clear, realistic, and assessable.
- Units course outcomes should be communicated to students at the beginning of the unit and reviewed throughout the unit to monitor their progress and provide feedback.
