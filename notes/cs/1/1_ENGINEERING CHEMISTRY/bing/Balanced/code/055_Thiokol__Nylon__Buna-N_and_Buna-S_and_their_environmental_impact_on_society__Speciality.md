# Thiokol, Nylon, Buna-N and Buna-S and their environmental impact on society, Speciality

- Thiokol is a synthetic rubber made from the co-polymerization of ethylene dichloride and sodium polysulfide. It is used as a sealant, adhesive, and binder for solid rocket propellants. 
- Nylon is a synthetic polymer made from the condensation of diamines and dicarboxylic acids. It is used as a fiber for clothing, carpets, ropes, and parachutes. It is also used as a plastic for gears, bearings, and other mechanical parts. 
- Buna-N is a synthetic rubber made from the co-polymerization of acrylonitrile and butadiene. It is also known as nitrile rubber or NBR. It is used as a seal, gasket, hose, and glove material for oil, fuel, and chemical resistance. 
- Buna-S is a synthetic rubber made from the co-polymerization of styrene and butadiene. It is also known as styrene butadiene rubber or SBR. It is used as a tire, conveyor belt, and shoe sole material for abrasion and wear resistance. 

## Environmental impact

- All of these synthetic polymers are derived from petroleum or natural gas, which are non-renewable and contribute to greenhouse gas emissions and climate change. 
- All of these synthetic polymers are non-biodegradable and persist in the environment for long periods of time. They can accumulate in landfills, oceans, and soil, posing threats to wildlife and human health. 
- All of these synthetic polymers can release toxic chemicals during their production, use, and disposal. These chemicals can contaminate air, water, and soil, and cause various health problems such as cancer, respiratory diseases, and hormonal disorders. 

## Speciality

- Thiokol is a special synthetic rubber because it has excellent low-temperature flexibility and resistance to oxidation and ozone. It can withstand extreme temperatures and pressures in rocket engines. 
- Nylon is a special synthetic polymer because it has high strength, toughness, and elasticity. It can be spun into fine filaments or molded into various shapes. It can also be blended with other fibers or plastics to enhance its properties. 
- Buna-N is a special synthetic rubber because it has high resistance to oil, fuel, and other chemicals. It can also withstand high temperatures and abrasion. It can be modified with additives or co-polymers to improve its performance. 
- Buna-S is a special synthetic rubber because it has high abrasion and wear resistance. It can also be vulcanized with sulfur to increase its strength and durability. It can be mixed with other rubbers or fillers to reduce its cost and improve its properties.
