### K3

- K3 is a type of surface mount technology (SMT) component package that is commonly used for integrated circuits (ICs).
- K3 stands for **Kilopak 3**, which refers to the size and shape of the package. K3 packages have a rectangular body with 32 pins on each of the four sides, for a total of 128 pins. The pins are spaced 0.8 mm apart and the body dimensions are 14 mm by 14 mm.
- K3 packages are designed to be soldered directly onto printed circuit boards (PCBs) using a reflow soldering process. This reduces the need for through-hole drilling and wiring, and allows for higher density and smaller size of PCBs.
- K3 packages are suitable for high-performance ICs that require a large number of connections, such as microprocessors, memory chips, and digital signal processors. Some examples of ICs that use K3 packages are the Intel 80386, the Motorola 68030, and the Texas Instruments TMS320C30.
