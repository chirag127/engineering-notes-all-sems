### Organometallic Compounds: General methods of preparation and applications of

- Organometallic compounds are compounds that contain at least one metal-carbon bond, where the carbon is part of an organic group.
- Organometallic compounds are classified according to the type of metal-carbon bond, such as ionic, covalent, or metalloidal.
- Organometallic compounds have diverse physical and chemical properties, depending on the nature of the metal, the organic group, and the ligands attached to the metal.
- Organometallic compounds are widely used in various fields, such as catalysis, medicine, materials, and energy  .

#### General methods of preparation of organometallic compounds

- One common method of preparing organometallic compounds is by the reaction of a metal with an organic halide, such as R-X, where R is an alkyl or aryl group and X is a halogen. For example, the reaction of magnesium with bromobenzene produces phenylmagnesium bromide, an organomagnesium compound:

  Mg + C6H5Br -> C6H5MgBr

- Another common method of preparing organometallic compounds is by the reaction of a metal with an acidic hydrocarbon, such as an alkyne or a cyclopentadiene. For example, the reaction of sodium with 1,3-cyclopentadiene produces cyclopentadienyl sodium, an organosodium compound:

  2 Na + C5H6 -> 2 C5H5Na + H2

- A third common method of preparing organometallic compounds is by the reaction of a metal with a carbonyl compound, such as an aldehyde or a ketone. For example, the reaction of lithium with acetone produces propyl lithium, an organolithium compound:

  2 Li + (CH3)2CO -> 2 CH3CH2Li + CO

#### General applications of organometallic compounds

- Organometallic compounds are widely used as catalysts for various organic reactions, such as polymerization, hydrogenation, oxidation, and carbon-carbon bond formation . For example, Ziegler-Natta catalysts, which are based on organotitanium compounds, are used for the polymerization of olefins:

  n CH2=CH2 + TiCl4 + Al(C2H5)3 -> (CH2-CH2)n + TiCl3 + AlCl3 + C2H6

- Organometallic compounds are also used in the field of medicine, as they have potential applications in drug delivery, diagnosis, and therapy . For example, cisplatin, which is an organoplatinum compound, is used as an anticancer drug:

  cis-[Pt(NH3)2Cl2] + DNA -> cis-[Pt(NH3)2(DNA)]Cl2

- Organometallic compounds are also used in the field of materials, as they have potential applications in gas adsorption and purification, nanotechnology, and optoelectronics . For example, metal-organic frameworks (MOFs), which are based on organometallic linkers, are used for the adsorption and separation of gases, such as CO2, CH4, and H2:

  M + R-COOH -> M-R-COO + H+

- Organometallic compounds are also used in the field of energy, as they have potential applications in carbon dioxide fixation, reduction, and utilization, as well as in renewable energy sources, such as solar cells and fuel cells . For example, organometallic complexes, such as Ru(bpy)3Cl2, are used as photosensitizers for the reduction of CO2 to CO and H2:

  Ru(bpy)3Cl2 + hν -> Ru(bpy)3Cl2*

  Ru(bpy)3Cl2* + CO2 + 2 H+ -> Ru(bpy)3Cl2 + CO + H2O
