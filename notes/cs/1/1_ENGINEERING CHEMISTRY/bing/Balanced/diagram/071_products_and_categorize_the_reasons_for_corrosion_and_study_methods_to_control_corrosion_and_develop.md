# Products and Corrosion

## What is corrosion?

- Corrosion is the deterioration of a material, usually a metal, due to a chemical or electrochemical reaction with its environment.
- Corrosion can cause loss of material, degradation of mechanical properties, aesthetic damage, and environmental pollution.
- Corrosion can affect various products, such as pipelines, bridges, vehicles, buildings, appliances, electronics, and medical devices.

## What are the reasons for corrosion?

- Corrosion can be caused by various factors, such as:

  - The nature of the metal and its surface condition
  - The nature of the corrosive environment, such as temperature, humidity, acidity, salinity, and oxygen concentration
  - The presence of other metals or substances that can accelerate or inhibit corrosion, such as galvanic couples, catalysts, or inhibitors
  - The mechanical stresses and strains on the metal, such as abrasion, fatigue, or creep
  - The design and fabrication of the product, such as shape, size, joints, welds, and coatings

## What are the methods to control corrosion?

- Corrosion can be controlled by various methods, such as:

  - Material selection: choosing a metal that is resistant to corrosion in a given environment, or using non-metallic materials, such as plastics, ceramics, or composites
  - Surface treatment: applying a protective layer on the metal surface, such as paint, plating, anodizing, or conversion coating, to isolate it from the corrosive environment
  - Cathodic protection: applying an external electric current or a sacrificial anode to the metal, to make it the cathode of an electrochemical cell, and prevent it from corroding
  - Corrosion inhibitors: adding a substance to the corrosive environment, such as a salt, an acid, or an organic compound, to reduce the corrosion rate of the metal
  - Environmental modification: changing the conditions of the corrosive environment, such as reducing the temperature, humidity, acidity, salinity, or oxygen concentration, to make it less aggressive
  - Design and fabrication: designing and fabricating the product to minimize the exposure of the metal to the corrosive environment, such as avoiding sharp corners, crevices, or dissimilar metals, and ensuring proper drainage, ventilation, and maintenance

## How to develop products that are resistant to corrosion?

- To develop products that are resistant to corrosion, one should:

  - Understand the mechanisms and factors of corrosion, and how they affect different metals and environments
  - Perform corrosion testing and analysis, using various methods and standards, to evaluate the corrosion performance and durability of the product
  - Apply the appropriate corrosion control methods, based on the material selection, surface treatment, cathodic protection, corrosion inhibitors, and environmental modification
  - Follow the best practices and guidelines for the design and fabrication of the product, to avoid corrosion problems and failures
  - Monitor and inspect the product regularly, to detect and prevent corrosion damage and defects
