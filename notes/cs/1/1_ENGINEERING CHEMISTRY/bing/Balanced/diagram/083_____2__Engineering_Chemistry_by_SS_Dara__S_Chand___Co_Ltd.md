### 2. Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd

This is a textbook for engineering students that covers the basic concepts and applications of chemistry in various fields of engineering. The book is divided into 16 chapters, each with a summary, review questions, and numerical problems. The chapters are:

- Chapter 1: Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding
- Chapter 2: Periodic Properties and Hydrogen
- Chapter 3: s-Block Elements
- Chapter 4: p-Block Elements
- Chapter 5: d- and f-Block Elements
- Chapter 6: Coordination Compounds
- Chapter 7: Chemical Thermodynamics
- Chapter 8: Chemical Kinetics
- Chapter 9: Electrochemistry
- Chapter 10: Solutions and Colligative Properties
- Chapter 11: Ionic Equilibria
- Chapter 12: Phase Rule and Alloys
- Chapter 13: Water and Its Treatment
- Chapter 14: Fuels and Combustion
- Chapter 15: Polymers and Composites
- Chapter 16: Corrosion and Its Prevention

The book aims to provide a comprehensive and systematic treatment of the fundamental principles and applications of chemistry in engineering. It also covers the latest developments and trends in the field, such as nanotechnology, green chemistry, and biotechnology. The book is suitable for undergraduate students of all branches of engineering, as well as for those preparing for competitive examinations.
