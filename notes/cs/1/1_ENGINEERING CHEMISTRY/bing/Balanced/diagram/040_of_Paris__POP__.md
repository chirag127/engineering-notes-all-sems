# Paris

Paris is the capital and most populous city of France, with an estimated population of 2,165,423 residents in 2019 in an area of more than 105 km² (41 sq mi), making it the fourth-most populated city in the European Union as well as the 30th most densely populated city in the world in 2022. Paris is a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture, and is often regarded as the most romantic city in the world.

## History

Paris was founded in the 3rd century BCE by a Celtic people called the Parisii, who gave the city its name. The city was conquered by the Romans in the 1st century BCE, and became one of the most important cities of the Roman Empire. Paris was the capital of the Frankish kingdom in the 6th century, and later the capital of France under the Capetian, Valois, and Bourbon dynasties. Paris was the center of the French Revolution in the 18th century, and the site of many political and social upheavals in the 19th and 20th centuries, such as the Paris Commune, the World Wars, and the May 1968 protests. Paris is also known for its cultural and artistic movements, such as the Gothic, Renaissance, Baroque, Rococo, Neoclassical, Romantic, Realist, Impressionist, Modernist, and Postmodernist styles .

## Geography

Paris is located in the north-central part of France, along the Seine River, which divides the city into two parts: the Right Bank (Rive Droite) and the Left Bank (Rive Gauche). The city is surrounded by a ring road called the Boulevard Périphérique, and is divided into 20 administrative districts called arrondissements, which are numbered from 1 to 20 in a clockwise spiral from the center of the city. The city has a temperate oceanic climate, with mild winters and warm summers, and an average annual precipitation of 641 mm (25.2 in) .

## Culture

Paris is a world-renowned destination for culture, with more than 1,800 monuments, 173 museums, 145 theaters, and 380 cinemas. Some of the most famous landmarks in Paris include the Eiffel Tower, the Arc de Triomphe, the Notre-Dame Cathedral, the Louvre Museum, the Musée d'Orsay, and the Sacré-Cœur Basilica. Paris is also home to many festivals, events, and celebrations, such as the Paris Fashion Week, the Paris Book Fair, the Bastille Day, and the Paris Marathon. Paris is also known for its cuisine, which is influenced by various regional and international traditions, and features dishes such as croissants, macarons, frog legs, steak and frites, eclairs, and hot chocolate. Some of the typical souvenirs to buy in Paris include cheese, dried sausage, wine, berets, and chocolates  .

## Travel

Paris is one of the most visited cities in the world, with more than 38 million tourists in 2019. Paris is well-connected by air, rail, road, and water, and has two international airports: Charles de Gaulle and Orly. Paris also has six major train stations, and a public transportation system that includes the metro, the RER, the bus, the tram, and the bike. Paris offers a variety of accommodation options, from luxury hotels to budget hostels, and a range of activities and attractions, from historical monuments to modern art galleries, from scenic parks to lively nightlife.
