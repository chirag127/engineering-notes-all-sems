### 1. To enable the students to understand about the Chemistry of Atomic and Molecular structure, Chemistry of

- Atomic and molecular structure refers to the arrangement of atoms and molecules in matter, and how this affects the physical and chemical properties of substances.
- Atomic structure is determined by the number and distribution of protons, neutrons, and electrons in an atom. Protons and neutrons form the nucleus, while electrons orbit around it in shells or orbitals. The number of protons defines the atomic number and the identity of the element, while the number of neutrons defines the atomic mass and the isotope of the element. The number of electrons determines the charge and the chemical reactivity of the atom.
- Molecular structure is determined by the type and number of atoms that are bonded together, and the shape and orientation of these bonds. Atoms can form different types of bonds, such as ionic, covalent, metallic, or hydrogen bonds, depending on their electronegativity and valence electrons. The shape and orientation of the bonds are influenced by the repulsion and attraction between the electron pairs and the nuclei, and can be predicted by the valence shell electron pair repulsion (VSEPR) theory or the molecular orbital (MO) theory. The molecular structure affects the polarity, intermolecular forces, and physical and chemical properties of the substance.
