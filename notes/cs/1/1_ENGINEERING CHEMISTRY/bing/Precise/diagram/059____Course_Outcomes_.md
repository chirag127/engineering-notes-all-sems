## Course Outcomes:

1. Understanding of the subject matter and its relevance to the field of study.
2. Development of critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
3. Ability to apply theoretical concepts to practical situations.
4. Improvement in communication and presentation skills.
5. Enhancement of teamwork and collaboration abilities.
6. Acquisition of research and analytical skills.
7. Familiarity with current trends and developments in the field.
8. Preparation for further academic or professional pursuits.
