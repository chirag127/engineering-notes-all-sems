# Polymers, Polymer Blends and Composites, Conducting and Biodegradable polymers

## Polymers
- Polymers are large molecules composed of repeating structural units connected by covalent chemical bonds.
- The process of converting monomers into polymers is called polymerization.
- Polymers can be classified into two types: addition polymers and condensation polymers.
- Addition polymers are formed by the addition of monomers with unsaturated bonds, while condensation polymers are formed by the elimination of a small molecule, such as water, during polymerization.

## Polymer Blends and Composites
- Polymer blends are mixtures of two or more polymers that are physically mixed together.
- Polymer composites are materials made by combining two or more different types of materials to create a new material with improved properties.
- The most common type of polymer composite is a fiber-reinforced composite, where fibers of one material are embedded in a matrix of another material.

## Conducting Polymers
- Conducting polymers are a class of polymers that have the ability to conduct electricity.
- They are typically conjugated polymers, meaning they have alternating single and double bonds along the polymer chain.
- Conducting polymers can be used in a variety of applications, including batteries, sensors, and displays.

## Biodegradable Polymers
- Biodegradable polymers are polymers that can be broken down by microorganisms into natural substances such as water, carbon dioxide, and biomass.
- They are typically made from renewable resources, such as starch, cellulose, and lactic acid.
- Biodegradable polymers are used in a variety of applications, including packaging, agriculture, and medicine.
