### Upon completion of the course the student should be able to:

1. Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. Apply the knowledge and skills acquired in the course to solve problems and make informed decisions.
3. Communicate effectively using the language and terminology of the subject.
4. Work effectively both independently and as part of a team.
5. Demonstrate critical thinking and analytical skills.
6. Use appropriate technology and tools to enhance learning and problem-solving.
7. Demonstrate ethical and professional behavior.
8. Engage in lifelong learning and continuous professional development.
