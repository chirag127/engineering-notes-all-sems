### University Chemistry by CNR Rao
- University Chemistry is a book written by CNR Rao.
- CNR Rao is an Indian chemist who has worked mainly in solid-state and structural chemistry.
- The book is intended for undergraduate students studying chemistry.
- It covers a wide range of topics in chemistry, including atomic structure, chemical bonding, and chemical reactions.
- The book is known for its clear explanations and well-organized content.
- It is a popular choice for students preparing for exams in chemistry.
- The book is available in both print and electronic formats.
- It is widely used as a textbook in universities and colleges.
