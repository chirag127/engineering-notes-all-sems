### 7. University Chemistry by BH Mahan

- University Chemistry is a textbook written by BH Mahan.
- It is a comprehensive guide to the subject of chemistry at the university level.
- The book covers a wide range of topics, including atomic structure, chemical bonding, thermodynamics, and kinetics.
- It is written in a clear and concise manner, making it accessible to students of all levels.
- The book includes numerous examples and practice problems to help students understand and apply the concepts presented.
- It is a valuable resource for students studying chemistry at the university level, as well as for those preparing for exams in the subject.
