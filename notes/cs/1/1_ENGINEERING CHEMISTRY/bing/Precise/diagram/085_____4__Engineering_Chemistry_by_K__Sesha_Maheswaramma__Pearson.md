### 4. Engineering Chemistry by K. Sesha Maheswaramma, Pearson

Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by K. Sesha Maheswaramma and published by Pearson. The book covers the fundamental concepts of chemistry and their applications in engineering. Some of the key topics covered in the book include:

1. Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding
2. States of Matter
3. Chemical Thermodynamics
4. Electrochemistry
5. Chemical Kinetics
6. Surface Chemistry
7. Coordination Compounds
8. Organic Chemistry
9. Polymers
10. Environmental Chemistry

This book is designed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the subject and is suitable for students pursuing engineering courses. It is written in a clear and concise manner, with numerous examples and illustrations to aid in understanding the concepts. The book also includes practice problems and exercises to help students test their knowledge and prepare for exams.
