# Water and Boiler Troubles Used in Industry

Water is a primary medium used in many commercial, manufacturing, and industrial processes. These include the use of boilers and steam raising plant, cooling towers, cooling systems, and other closed circuit systems . However, there are several common problems that can occur when using water in industrial boilers. Some of these problems include:

1. **Missing Insulation**: If the insulation on a boiler is missing, it will have reduced efficiency as the insulation works to contain heat in the system .
2. **Irregular or Infrequent Maintenance**: Irregular maintenance is usually at the heart of every boiler problem .
3. **Water Leaks**: Water leaks can cause damage to the unit .
4. **Pressure-Related Issues**: Pressure-related issues can cause damage to the unit .
5. **Scale Buildup & Blockages**: Scale buildup and blockages can cause damage to the unit .

# Analysis of Coal & Determination of Calorific Values

Calorific value is a measure of the amount of energy produced from a unit weight of coal when it is combusted in oxygen. A measured sample of coal is completely combusted in a bomb calorimeter, which is a device for measuring heat . The calorific value mainly comes from the combustion of organic components (C, H, N). In addition, moisture and ash in coal will absorb part of the heat during combustion .

There are several methods for determining the calorific value of coal, including the use of complex thermal analysis techniques such as TG/DTG and DTA . These methods can be used to determine the calorific values of coals of different origin and the results can be compared with those obtained using an adiabatic bomb calorimeter by the standard ASTM method .
