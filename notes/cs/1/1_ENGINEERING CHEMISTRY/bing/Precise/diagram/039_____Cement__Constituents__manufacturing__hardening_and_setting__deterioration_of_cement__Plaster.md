### Cement

Cement is a binding material that sets, hardens, and adheres to other materials to bind them together. It is manufactured through a closely controlled chemical combination of calcium, silicon, aluminum, iron, and other ingredients .

#### Manufacturing

Cement is manufactured by grinding the raw materials, which include limestone, clay, and shale, to a fine powder. This powder is then heated in a kiln to around 1450°C, which causes the materials to fuse together to form clinker. The clinker is then ground to a fine powder and mixed with gypsum to produce cement .

#### Hardening and Setting

Cement hardens and sets as a result of hydration, which is a chemical combination of the cement compounds with water. This process yields submicroscopic crystals or a gel-like material with a high surface area. The hydration, setting, and hardening of Portland cement are constrained by C3S and C3A, and any admixtures that affect the hydration of these compounds can change the performance of the hydration, setting, and hardening of Portland cement  .

#### Deterioration of Cement

Set cement and concrete can suffer deterioration from attack by some natural or artificial chemical agents. The alumina compound is the most vulnerable to chemical attack in soils containing sulfate salts or in seawater, while the iron compound and the two calcium silicates are more resistant. Concrete can also deteriorate for a variety of other reasons, including corrosion of embedded metals, freeze-thaw cycles, and alkali-aggregate reactions  .

#### Plaster

Plaster is a building material used for coating walls and ceilings. It is made by mixing cement, sand, and water, and can be applied in a single or multiple coats. Plaster provides a smooth, durable surface that can be painted or wallpapered.
