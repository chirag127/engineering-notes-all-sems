### CO-5 Develop the understanding of Chemical structure of polymers and its effect on their various properties

1. Polymers are large molecules made up of repeating units called monomers.
2. The chemical structure of a polymer refers to the arrangement of its monomers and the bonds between them.
3. The chemical structure of a polymer can affect its properties such as strength, elasticity, and melting point.
4. The arrangement of monomers in a polymer can be linear, branched, or cross-linked.
5. Linear polymers have a simple, straight chain structure and are usually strong and rigid.
6. Branched polymers have side chains attached to the main chain, which can make them more flexible and less dense.
7. Cross-linked polymers have covalent bonds between the chains, which can make them very strong and resistant to heat and chemicals.
8. The type of monomers used and the way they are bonded together can also affect the properties of the polymer.
9. For example, polymers made from monomers with polar groups can have stronger intermolecular forces and higher melting points.
10. The degree of polymerization, or the number of monomers in a polymer chain, can also affect its properties.
11. Longer chains can result in stronger, more rigid polymers, while shorter chains can result in more flexible, less dense polymers.
