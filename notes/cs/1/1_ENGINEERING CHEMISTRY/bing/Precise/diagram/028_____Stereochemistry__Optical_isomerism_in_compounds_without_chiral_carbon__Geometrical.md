### Stereochemistry: Optical isomerism in compounds without chiral carbon, Geometrical

Stereochemistry is the study of the spatial arrangement of atoms in molecules and the effect of this arrangement on the chemical and physical properties of the molecules. Optical isomerism and geometrical isomerism are two types of stereoisomerism.

1. **Optical Isomerism**: Optical isomerism occurs when a compound has similar bonds but a different spatial arrangement of atoms, resulting in non-superimposable mirror images, known as enantiomers. Enantiomers differ from one another in their optical activity. Optical isomerism occurs in chiral molecules in solution, which appear to be mirror images of one another.

2. **Geometrical Isomerism**: Geometrical isomerism occurs due to the different geometric configurations of the atoms in a molecule. Geometrical stereoisomers are a common occurrence in heteroleptic compounds.

It is important to note that optical isomerism is not limited to molecules with a single chiral center. The chirality of molecules can result from the presence of chiral centers, as well as from the presence of chiral axes or planes.
