### 4. To enable the students to understand and apply detailed concepts of water source, water impurities, hardness

Water is an essential resource for life and is found in various sources such as rivers, lakes, groundwater, and oceans. It is important to understand the different sources of water and their characteristics to ensure that the water we use is safe and suitable for consumption and other purposes.

Water impurities refer to substances or particles that are present in water and can affect its quality. These impurities can be classified into two categories: dissolved and suspended. Dissolved impurities are substances that are dissolved in water, such as salts and minerals. Suspended impurities are particles that are not dissolved in water, such as sand and silt.

Hardness is a measure of the amount of dissolved calcium and magnesium in water. Water with high levels of these minerals is considered hard, while water with low levels is considered soft. Hard water can cause problems such as scaling in pipes and appliances, and can also affect the taste and appearance of water.

It is important for students to understand these concepts in order to make informed decisions about water use and treatment. By understanding the sources of water, the types of impurities present, and the hardness of water, students can apply this knowledge to ensure that the water they use is safe and suitable for their needs.
