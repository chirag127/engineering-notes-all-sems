### 2. Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd

Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by SS Dara and published by S Chand & Co Ltd. It is a comprehensive guide for students studying engineering chemistry and covers a wide range of topics including:

1. Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding
2. States of Matter
3. Chemical Thermodynamics
4. Electrochemistry
5. Chemical Kinetics
6. Surface Chemistry
7. Coordination Compounds
8. Nuclear Chemistry
9. Organic Chemistry
10. Polymers

This book is widely used by students and is known for its clear explanations and well-organized content. It is a valuable resource for anyone studying engineering chemistry.
