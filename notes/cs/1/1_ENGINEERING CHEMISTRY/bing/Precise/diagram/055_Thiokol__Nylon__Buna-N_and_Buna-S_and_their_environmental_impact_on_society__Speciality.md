# Thiokol, Nylon, Buna-N and Buna-S and their environmental impact on society, Speciality

## Thiokol
- Thiokol is a synthetic rubber that is used in the production of solid rocket propellants and sealants.
- There is no information available on the environmental impact of Thiokol.

## Nylon
- Nylon is the world's first entirely synthetic polymer fiber, introduced by the DuPont company in 1938.
- Known for its strength, durability, and flexibility.
- There is no information available on the environmental impact of Nylon.

## Buna-N
- Buna-N, also known as nitrile butadiene rubber, NBR, and acrylonitrile butadiene rubber, is a synthetic rubber derived from acrylonitrile (ACN) and butadiene.
- Trade names include Perbunan, Nipol, Krynac and Europrene.
- This rubber is unusual in being resistant to oil, fuel, and other chemicals.
- There is no information available on the environmental impact of Buna-N.

## Buna-S
- Buna-S, now known as styrene butadiene rubber (SBR), was being produced in large quantities in Germany by 1935.
- IG Farben scientists also developed nitrile rubber Buna-N in 1931, now known as NBR, and began mass production in 1935.
- There is no information available on the environmental impact of Buna-S.
