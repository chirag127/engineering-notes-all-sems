# Theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong’s method, Ranking of Coal, Analysis of coal

## Theoretical calculation of calorific value by Dulong’s method
Dulong's formula is used to calculate the calorific value of a fuel. The formula is as follows:

Gross Calorific Value = 33800C + 144200(H - O/8) + 9400S

Where:
- C is the percentage of carbon
- H is the percentage of hydrogen
- O is the percentage of oxygen
- S is the percentage of sulfur

This formula assumes that all the carbon, hydrogen, and sulfur in the fuel are completely burned and that all the heat generated is recovered.

## Ranking of Coal
Coal is ranked based on its carbon content, heating value, and sulfur content. The ranks of coal, from lowest to highest, are:
1. Lignite
2. Sub-bituminous
3. Bituminous
4. Anthracite

Lignite has the lowest carbon content and heating value, while anthracite has the highest. The sulfur content of coal also affects its rank, with lower sulfur content being more desirable.

## Analysis of coal
Coal analysis is the process of determining the composition and properties of coal. This can include measurements of the following:
- Moisture content
- Ash content
- Volatile matter
- Fixed carbon
- Sulfur content
- Heating value
- Carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, and oxygen content

These measurements can be used to determine the rank of the coal, its suitability for a particular use, and its environmental impact when burned. Coal analysis is typically performed using standardized laboratory methods.
