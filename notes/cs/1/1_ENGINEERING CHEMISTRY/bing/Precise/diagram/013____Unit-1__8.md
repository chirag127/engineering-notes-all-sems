## Unit-1: 8

1. The number 8 is an even integer.
2. It is the cube of 2.
3. It is the only Fibonacci number, aside from 1, that is a perfect cube.
4. In the base 10 numeral system, 8 is the highest digit.
5. The number 8 is considered lucky in Chinese culture.
6. In music, an octave is the interval between one musical pitch and another with half or double its frequency.
7. In computer science, a byte is a unit of data that is eight bits long.
8. In chess, the board is an 8x8 grid.
