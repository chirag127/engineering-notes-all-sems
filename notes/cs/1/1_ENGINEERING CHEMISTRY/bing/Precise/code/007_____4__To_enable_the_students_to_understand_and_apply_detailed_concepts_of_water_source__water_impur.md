### 4. To enable the students to understand and apply detailed concepts of water source, water impurities, hardness

Water is an essential resource for life and is used for various purposes such as drinking, agriculture, and industrial processes. It is important to understand the sources of water, the impurities present in it, and its hardness to ensure its safe and efficient use.

1. **Water sources:** Water can be obtained from various sources such as surface water (rivers, lakes, and reservoirs), groundwater (wells and springs), and rainwater. The choice of water source depends on factors such as availability, quality, and cost.

2. **Water impurities:** Water from natural sources is not pure and contains various impurities such as dissolved minerals, organic matter, and microorganisms. These impurities can affect the taste, odor, and color of water and can also have health implications.

3. **Hardness:** Hardness is a measure of the concentration of calcium and magnesium ions in water. Hard water can cause scaling in pipes and appliances and can also interfere with the action of soap and detergents. Water hardness can be reduced by methods such as ion exchange and reverse osmosis.

In conclusion, understanding the concepts of water source, water impurities, and hardness is important for the safe and efficient use of water. Students should be able to apply these concepts to make informed decisions about water use and treatment.
