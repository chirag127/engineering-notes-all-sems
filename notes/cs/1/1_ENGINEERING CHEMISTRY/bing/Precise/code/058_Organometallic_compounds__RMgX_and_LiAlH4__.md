# Organometallic Compounds (RMgX and LiAlH4)

Organometallic compounds are compounds that contain a metal-carbon bond. Two important types of organometallic compounds are RMgX and LiAlH4.

## RMgX

RMgX, where X is a halide, is known as a Grignard reagent. Grignard reagents are named after Victor Grignard. They are usually written as R-Mg-X, but in fact, the magnesium(II) center is tetrahedral when dissolved in Lewis basic solvents.

Grignard reagents are important due to their use in organic synthesis and as synthons for a range of organometallic compounds. They react with organic carbonyls (aldehydes, ketones, and esters) to yield the alcohol on hydrolysis. This synthetic route is useful for the formation of primary, secondary, and terminal alcohols.

## LiAlH4

LiAlH4 is an organometallic compound that is commonly used as a reducing agent in organic synthesis. It is a powerful reducing agent that can reduce a wide range of functional groups, including aldehydes, ketones, esters, and carboxylic acids.

LiAlH4 is a tetrahydridoaluminate complex that is formed by the reaction of lithium hydride with aluminum chloride. It is a white solid that is soluble in ether and tetrahydrofuran (THF) but reacts violently with water, releasing hydrogen gas.

In summary, organometallic compounds RMgX and LiAlH4 are important reagents in organic synthesis due to their ability to react with a wide range of functional groups to form new compounds.
