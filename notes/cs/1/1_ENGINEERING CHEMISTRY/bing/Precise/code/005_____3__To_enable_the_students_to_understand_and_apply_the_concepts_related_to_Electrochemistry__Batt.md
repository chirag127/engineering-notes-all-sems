### 3. To enable the students to understand and apply the concepts related to Electrochemistry, Batteries, Corrosion

Electrochemistry is the branch of chemistry that deals with the relationship between electricity and chemical reactions. It involves the study of the movement of electrons in chemical reactions and the resulting changes in matter.

Some key concepts in electrochemistry include:

- **Redox reactions:** These are reactions in which electrons are transferred from one species to another. The species that loses electrons is oxidized, while the species that gains electrons is reduced.

- **Electrochemical cells:** These are devices that convert chemical energy into electrical energy or vice versa. There are two types of electrochemical cells: galvanic cells and electrolytic cells.

- **Galvanic cells:** These are electrochemical cells in which a spontaneous redox reaction generates an electric current. A common example is a battery.

- **Electrolytic cells:** These are electrochemical cells in which an electric current drives a non-spontaneous redox reaction. A common example is the electrolysis of water to produce hydrogen and oxygen gas.

Batteries are devices that store chemical energy and convert it into electrical energy. There are many different types of batteries, but they all consist of one or more electrochemical cells. Some common types of batteries include:

- **Primary batteries:** These are batteries that cannot be recharged. Once the chemical reactions inside the battery are complete, the battery is dead and must be disposed of.

- **Secondary batteries:** These are batteries that can be recharged. The chemical reactions inside the battery can be reversed by applying an external electric current.

Corrosion is the degradation of a material due to chemical reactions with its environment. It is a common problem in many industries, as it can cause damage to infrastructure and equipment. Some common causes of corrosion include:

- **Oxidation:** This is the most common form of corrosion. It occurs when a metal reacts with oxygen to form an oxide.

- **Electrochemical corrosion:** This occurs when two different metals are in contact with each other in the presence of an electrolyte. The more reactive metal will corrode, while the less reactive metal will be protected.

- **Galvanic corrosion:** This is a type of electrochemical corrosion that occurs when two different metals are in contact with each other in the presence of an electrolyte. The more reactive metal will corrode, while the less reactive metal will be protected.

In summary, electrochemistry, batteries, and corrosion are important concepts in chemistry that have many practical applications. Understanding these concepts can help students to better understand the world around them and to make informed decisions about the use of technology.
