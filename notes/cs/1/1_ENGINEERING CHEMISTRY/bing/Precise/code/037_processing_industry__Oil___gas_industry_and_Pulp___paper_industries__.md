# Processing Industry, Oil & Gas Industry and Pulp & Paper Industries

## Processing Industry
- The processing industry involves the transformation of raw materials into finished products.
- This industry includes a wide range of sectors such as food processing, textile processing, and chemical processing.
- The processing industry plays a crucial role in the economy by adding value to raw materials and creating employment opportunities.

## Oil & Gas Industry
- The oil and gas industry is involved in the exploration, extraction, refining, and distribution of oil and gas.
- This industry is critical to the global economy as it provides the primary source of energy for transportation, heating, and electricity generation.
- The oil and gas industry is divided into three main sectors: upstream, midstream, and downstream.

## Pulp & Paper Industries
- The pulp and paper industry is involved in the production of paper and paper products from wood and other raw materials.
- This industry plays a vital role in the global economy by providing paper products for communication, packaging, and hygiene.
- The pulp and paper industry is divided into two main sectors: pulp production and paper production.
