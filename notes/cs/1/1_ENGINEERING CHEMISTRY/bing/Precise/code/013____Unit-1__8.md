## Unit-1: 8

1. The number 8 is an even integer.
2. It is the cube of 2.
3. It is a composite number, with its divisors being 1, 2, 4, and 8.
4. In the decimal number system, 8 is the highest digit.
5. The number 8 is considered lucky in some cultures, such as Chinese culture.
6. In music, an octave is the interval between one musical pitch and another with double its frequency.
7. In computer science, a byte is typically composed of 8 bits.
8. In sports, the number 8 is often associated with the center position in various team sports.
