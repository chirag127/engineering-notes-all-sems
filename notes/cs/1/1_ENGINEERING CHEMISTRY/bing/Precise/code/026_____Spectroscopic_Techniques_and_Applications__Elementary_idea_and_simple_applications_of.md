### Spectroscopic Techniques and Applications: Elementary idea and simple applications of

Spectroscopy is the study of the interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation. It is a powerful tool for the analysis of the structure and composition of materials. Some of the elementary ideas and simple applications of spectroscopy are:

1. **Identification of elements and compounds**: Spectroscopy can be used to identify the presence of specific elements or compounds in a sample by analyzing the characteristic absorption or emission spectra.

2. **Quantitative analysis**: Spectroscopy can be used to determine the concentration of a particular substance in a sample by measuring the intensity of its characteristic spectral lines.

3. **Molecular structure determination**: Spectroscopy can provide information about the structure of molecules, including bond lengths, bond angles, and the presence of functional groups.

4. **Remote sensing**: Spectroscopy can be used to remotely sense the composition of materials, such as the atmosphere of a planet or the surface of a distant object.

These are just a few examples of the many applications of spectroscopy. Spectroscopy is a versatile and powerful tool that is widely used in many fields, including chemistry, physics, biology, and environmental science.
