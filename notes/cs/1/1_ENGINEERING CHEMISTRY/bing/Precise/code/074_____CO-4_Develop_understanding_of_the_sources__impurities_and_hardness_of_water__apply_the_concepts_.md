### CO-4 Develop understanding of the sources, impurities and hardness of water, apply the concepts of

1. **Sources of water**: Water can be obtained from various sources such as rivers, lakes, oceans, groundwater, and rainwater.
2. **Impurities in water**: Water from natural sources may contain impurities such as dissolved minerals, organic matter, and microorganisms. These impurities can affect the taste, odor, and appearance of water, as well as its suitability for certain uses.
3. **Hardness of water**: Hardness refers to the presence of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, in water. Hard water can cause problems such as scaling in pipes and reduced effectiveness of soaps and detergents.
4. **Concepts of water treatment**: Water treatment involves the removal of impurities from water to make it suitable for its intended use. This can be achieved through various methods such as filtration, sedimentation, and disinfection.
