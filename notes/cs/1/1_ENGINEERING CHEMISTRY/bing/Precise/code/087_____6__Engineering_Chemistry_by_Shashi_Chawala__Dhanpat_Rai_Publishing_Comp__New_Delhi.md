### 6. Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawala, Dhanpat Rai Publishing Comp, New Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by Shashi Chawla and published by Dhanpat Rai Publishing Comp, New Delhi .
- The book covers the syllabus for the Engineering Chemistry course offered to first-year B.E/B.Tech students of various Indian universities .
- The book includes topics such as electrochemistry, environmental chemistry, organic reaction mechanism, etc .
- The book has received positive reviews and ratings, with an average rating of 4.18 on Goodreads .
