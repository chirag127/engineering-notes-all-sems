- **Nylon**: Nylon is a synthetic fiber derived from petrochemicals, which are non-biodegradable, resulting in environmental degradation. The production of nylon is associated with the same negative environmental impacts as fossil fuels, including exacerbating the climate crisis with greenhouse gas emissions. Nylon clothing also contributes to microfiber pollution   .

- **Buna-N**: Buna-N, also known as NBR rubber, is the seal industry’s most widely used and economical elastomer. It displays excellent resistance to petroleum-based oils, fuels, water, alcohols, silicone greases, and hydraulic fluids . However, one of the major constraints to market growth in Europe and North America is environmental regulation and concern for the environmental impact associated with these products and their manufacture. For example, butadiene has been listed as a toxic substance under the Canadian Environmental Protection Act .

- **Buna-S**: Buna-S is similar to GR-S, a technology that proved crucial to solving the rubber crisis facing the United States during WWII . Buna® brand provides the automotive sector with a multilateral range of modern butadiene rubber products playing key roles in improving grip, resilience, and lowering rolling resistance. All these improvements go a long way toward helping to reduce CO2 emissions and positively impacting the planet .
