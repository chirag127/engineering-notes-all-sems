### 2. Engineering Chemistry by SS Dara, S Chand & Co Ltd

- "Engineering Chemistry" is a textbook written by S. S. Dara and published by S. Chand & Co Ltd. 
- The book is written exclusively for students of all branches of engineering, keeping in view their professional requirements. 
- It is a comprehensive book, divided into 26 chapters. 
- The latest edition of the book is available for purchase.
