## Unit-1: 8

1. The number 8 is an even integer that comes after 7 and before 9.
2. In the base 10 number system, 8 is the cube of 2.
3. The number 8 is considered lucky in some cultures, such as Chinese culture, where it is associated with wealth and prosperity.
4. In music, an octave is the interval between one musical pitch and another with half or double its frequency, and is named after the Latin word for "eight".
5. In computer science, a byte is typically made up of 8 bits.
6. In the RGB color model, each color channel is often represented by 8 bits, allowing for 256 different shades of each primary color.
7. In sports, the number 8 is often associated with the position of the center in basketball and the scrum-half in rugby.
8. In the periodic table, the element with the atomic number 8 is oxygen.
