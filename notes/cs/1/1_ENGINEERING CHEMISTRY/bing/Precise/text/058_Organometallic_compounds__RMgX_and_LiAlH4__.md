# Organometallic Compounds (RMgX and LiAlH4)

Organometallic compounds are compounds that contain a metal-carbon bond. RMgX, where X is a halide, is known as a Grignard reagent. Grignard reagents are named after Victor Grignard. They are usually written as R-Mg-X, but in fact, the magnesium(II) center is tetrahedral when dissolved in Lewis basic solvents.

Grignard reagents are important due to their use in organic synthesis and as synthons for a range of organometallic compounds. They react with organic carbonyls (aldehydes, ketones, and esters) to yield the alcohol on hydrolysis. This synthetic route is useful for the formation of primary, secondary, and terminal alcohols.

LiAlH4 is another important organometallic compound. It is a reducing agent commonly used in organic synthesis. It can reduce aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, and esters to their corresponding alcohols.
