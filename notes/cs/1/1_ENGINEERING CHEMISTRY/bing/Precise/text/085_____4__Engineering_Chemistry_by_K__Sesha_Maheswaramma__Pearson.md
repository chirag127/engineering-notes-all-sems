### 4. Engineering Chemistry by K. Sesha Maheswaramma, Pearson

- "Engineering Chemistry" is a textbook written by K. Sesha Maheswaramma and published by Pearson.
- The book covers fundamental concepts in chemistry and their applications in engineering.
- Topics covered in the book include atomic structure, chemical bonding, phase rule, solutions, electrochemistry, corrosion, and water technology.
- The book is designed for undergraduate students in engineering and provides a comprehensive introduction to the subject.
- It includes numerous examples, illustrations, and exercises to aid in understanding and application of the concepts.
- The book is well-organized and written in a clear and concise manner, making it an excellent resource for students and professionals alike.
