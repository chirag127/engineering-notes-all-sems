### 7. University Chemistry by BH Mahan

- University Chemistry by BH Mahan is a widely used textbook for undergraduate chemistry courses.
- The book covers a broad range of topics in general chemistry, including atomic structure, chemical bonding, thermodynamics, and kinetics.
- It is known for its clear explanations and well-organized presentation of material.
- The book includes numerous examples and practice problems to help students understand and apply the concepts.
- It is a valuable resource for students studying chemistry at the university level.
