1. **Molecular structure** refers to the three-dimensional arrangement of atoms within a molecule. It is determined by the type of atoms, the number of atoms, and the type of chemical bonds between the atoms.
2. **Chemical bonding** is the process by which atoms combine to form molecules. There are several types of chemical bonds, including covalent bonds, ionic bonds, and metallic bonds.
3. **Covalent bonds** are formed when atoms share electrons. This type of bond is common in organic molecules, such as those found in living organisms.
4. **Ionic bonds** are formed when one atom transfers an electron to another atom. This type of bond is common in salts, such as sodium chloride (NaCl).
5. **Metallic bonds** are formed when electrons are shared among many atoms. This type of bond is common in metals, such as copper and iron.
6. The **theoretical principles** of chemistry help us understand how and why atoms combine to form molecules, and how the properties of molecules are determined by their molecular structure and bonding.
