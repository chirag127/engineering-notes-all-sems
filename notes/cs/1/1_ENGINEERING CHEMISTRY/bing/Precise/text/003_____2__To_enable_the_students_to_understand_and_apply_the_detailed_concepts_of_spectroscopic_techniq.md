### 2. To enable the students to understand and apply the detailed concepts of spectroscopic techniques:

- Spectroscopy is the study of the interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation.
- Spectroscopic techniques are used to analyze the chemical composition and structure of materials.
- There are several types of spectroscopy, including infrared, ultraviolet-visible, and nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy.
- Infrared spectroscopy measures the absorption of infrared radiation by molecules, which can provide information about the functional groups present in a molecule.
- Ultraviolet-visible spectroscopy measures the absorption of ultraviolet and visible light by molecules, which can provide information about the electronic structure of a molecule.
- Nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy measures the absorption of radiofrequency radiation by atomic nuclei in a magnetic field, which can provide detailed information about the structure and dynamics of molecules.
- Spectroscopic techniques can be applied in a wide range of fields, including chemistry, biology, physics, and materials science.
- To effectively use spectroscopic techniques, students must have a strong understanding of the underlying principles and be able to interpret the data obtained from these techniques.
