### 6. Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawala, Dhanpat Rai Publishing Comp, New Delhi

- Engineering Chemistry is a textbook written by Shashi Chawala and published by Dhanpat Rai Publishing Company, New Delhi.
- The book is intended for undergraduate students in engineering and covers the fundamental concepts of chemistry as applied to engineering.
- The book is divided into several chapters, each covering a specific topic in the field of engineering chemistry.
- Some of the topics covered in the book include atomic structure, chemical bonding, states of matter, chemical thermodynamics, electrochemistry, and corrosion.
- The book also includes numerous examples, illustrations, and exercises to help students understand the concepts presented.
- Engineering Chemistry by Shashi Chawala is a widely used textbook in engineering programs and is considered a valuable resource for students studying engineering chemistry.
