### CO-2 Apply the fundamental concepts of determination of structure with various spectral techniques

1. Spectroscopy is the study of the interaction between matter and electromagnetic radiation.
2. Spectral techniques are used to determine the structure of molecules by analyzing the interaction of electromagnetic radiation with the molecule.
3. Some common spectral techniques include infrared (IR) spectroscopy, nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy, and mass spectrometry (MS).
4. In IR spectroscopy, the molecule absorbs infrared radiation, causing vibrations in the chemical bonds. The resulting spectrum can be used to identify functional groups and determine the structure of the molecule.
5. In NMR spectroscopy, the molecule is placed in a magnetic field and exposed to radiofrequency radiation. The resulting spectrum can be used to determine the chemical environment of the nuclei in the molecule and provide information about the structure of the molecule.
6. In MS, the molecule is ionized and the resulting fragments are separated based on their mass-to-charge ratio. The resulting spectrum can be used to determine the molecular weight and structure of the molecule.
7. These techniques can be used in combination to provide a more complete picture of the structure of the molecule. For example, IR and NMR spectroscopy can be used to identify functional groups and determine the chemical environment of the nuclei, while MS can provide information about the molecular weight and fragmentation pattern of the molecule.
