### Liquid Crystals

Liquid crystals are a state of matter that has properties between those of conventional liquids and those of solid crystals. They are called liquid crystals because they exhibit the mechanical properties of a liquid, but have the optical properties of a single crystal. 

#### Types of Liquid Crystals

Liquid crystals can be classified into two types: thermotropic and lyotropic. Thermotropic liquid crystals are obtained by melting a crystalline solid, while lyotropic liquid crystals are found in certain colloidal solutions, such as aqueous solutions of tobacco mosaic virus and certain polymers.

#### Applications of Liquid Crystals

Liquid crystals have a wide range of commercial applications. They are used in liquid crystal displays (LCDs) in digital watches, calculators, and computer and video displays. Apart from display-related applications, liquid crystals are also attractive for several kinds of sensing applications. Some other applications would be in the areas of lasers and medical diagnostics.

#### Industry

The flat panel display is the most recognized device where liquid crystals are widely used. The use of liquid crystals in display technology stems from the nature of chirality and the high electro-optic coefficient of these mediums, thereby making them significantly advantageous. There are also ongoing developments in the industry towards liquid crystal-based smart fabrics.
