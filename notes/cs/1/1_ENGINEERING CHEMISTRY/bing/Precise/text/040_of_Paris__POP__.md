# Paris Population

- Paris is the capital and most populous city of France.
- As of January 1, 2019, the population of Paris was 2,165,423 residents within its administrative city limits .
- Paris is the fourth-most populated city in the European Union as well as the 30th most densely populated city in the world in 2022 .
- The city is surrounded by the Paris unité urbaine, or urban area, the most populous urban area in the European Union .
- The suburban area of Paris is home to 11 million people, while the city proper has a population of 2.1 million .
