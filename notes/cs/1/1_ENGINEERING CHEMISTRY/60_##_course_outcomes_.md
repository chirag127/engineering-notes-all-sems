## Course Outcomes:

Course Outcomes (COs) are statements that describe what students are expected to know and be able to do by the end of a course. They are specific, measurable, and aligned with the overall goals and objectives of the program. 

The development of COs is an important step in the course design process, as they provide a clear understanding of the learning goals and expectations for students. COs also serve as a basis for evaluating student learning and the effectiveness of the course.

COs are usually expressed in terms of knowledge, skills, attitudes, or values that the students should acquire or demonstrate by the end of the course. For example, a CO for a chemistry course might be "Students will be able to apply chemical concepts and principles to solve real-world problems."

In addition to serving as a basis for assessment, COs also provide guidance for course content and instructional strategies. They help instructors focus on what is important and prioritize the most essential topics and skills.

Overall, the development and use of COs contribute to the quality and effectiveness of the course and the student learning experience.
