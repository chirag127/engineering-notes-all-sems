### Fibre Optics: Principle and construction of optical fiber for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Fiber optics refers to the technology of transmitting data, voice, and video signals over long distances using light signals in optical fibers. The principle of fiber optics is based on total internal reflection, where light is reflected back into the core of the fiber and is prevented from leaking out.

Construction: Optical fibers consist of a core made of glass or plastic, surrounded by a cladding layer with a lower refractive index. The core and cladding are covered by a protective coating. Light is launched into the fiber core using a light source and travels through the fiber due to total internal reflection.

Advantages: High bandwidth, immunity to electromagnetic interference, and the ability to transmit signals over long distances without degradation.

Applications: Fiber optics is widely used in telecommunications, medicine, and military applications.
