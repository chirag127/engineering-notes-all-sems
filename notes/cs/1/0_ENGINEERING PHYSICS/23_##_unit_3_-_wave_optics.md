## Unit 3 - Wave Optics

Wave Optics is a branch of optics that deals with the behavior of light waves. It describes how light waves propagate, interfere, and diffract. Unit 3 covers the following topics:

1. Wave Nature of Light
2. Huygens' Principle
3. Interference of Light Waves
4. Young's Double-Slit Experiment
5. Diffraction of Light Waves
6. Resolving Power of Optical Instruments

Wave optics is important for understanding the behavior of light in optical systems such as lenses, mirrors, and gratings. The study of wave optics provides a deeper understanding of the fundamental principles of light and its interactions with matter.
