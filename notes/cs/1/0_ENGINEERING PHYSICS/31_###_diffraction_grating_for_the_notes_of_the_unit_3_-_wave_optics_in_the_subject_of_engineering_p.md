### Diffraction grating for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

A diffraction grating is an optical component with a surface ruled with fine parallel lines. It diffracts light into its component wavelengths, producing a spectrum. The number of lines per unit length and the spacing between them determine the diffraction efficiency. Diffraction gratings are used in spectroscopy, laser systems, and other applications requiring precise control of light.
