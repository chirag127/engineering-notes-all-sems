### Displacement current for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Displacement current is a term used in electromagnetism to describe a current that arises in a dielectric material due to changes in electric flux. It is a result of the polarization of the dielectric material, which causes the movement of charged particles within the material. The displacement current is proportional to the rate of change of the electric flux, and it is an important factor in the calculation of electromagnetic fields. The displacement current is often used to explain the behavior of capacitors, which store electrical energy in the form of an electric field. The displacement current also plays a role in the generation of electromagnetic waves, such as radio waves and light.
