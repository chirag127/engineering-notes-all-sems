### Phase velocity and group velocity for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Phase velocity: It is the velocity of a wavefront through a medium. It is defined as the ratio of the wavelength to the period of the wave.

Group velocity: It is the velocity of the energy or information carried by a wave. It is defined as the rate of change of the wavevector with frequency.

In general, phase velocity and group velocity are not equal. However, for long-wavelength, low-frequency waves in a homogeneous medium, they are approximately equal. In general, phase velocity is greater than or equal to the group velocity.

Note: The concepts of phase velocity and group velocity are important in understanding the behavior of waves in various media.
