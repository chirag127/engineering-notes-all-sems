### Newton’s Rings and its applications for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Newton's Rings are an optical phenomenon that occur when light is reflected between two surfaces, one of which is curved. The rings are formed due to the interference of the light waves and are seen as concentric circles centered on the point of contact between the two surfaces. Applications of Newton's Rings include:

-Measuring surface roughness and curvature 
-Determining the refractive index of a material 
-Calibrating lenses and optical systems 
-Studying the optical properties of thin films.
