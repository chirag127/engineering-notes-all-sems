## Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser

Fiber optics is the technology of transmitting information as light signals through optical fibers. It is used in many applications, including telecommunications, medical equipment, and industrial automation. The core of the fiber is made of glass or plastic and is surrounded by a cladding material that reflects light back into the core. This allows the light to travel long distances without significant loss of signal strength. 

Lasers are devices that produce intense, focused beams of light. They have many applications, including cutting and welding, medical treatments, and scientific research. Lasers can also be used in fiber optics to transmit information over long distances. The light emitted by a laser is monochromatic, meaning it has a single color and wavelength, and is highly collimated, meaning the light is tightly focused into a narrow beam.
