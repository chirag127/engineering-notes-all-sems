### Meissner effect for the notes of the Unit 5 - Superconductors and Nano-Materials: in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Meissner effect is a phenomenon in superconductors where a magnetic field is expelled from the interior of the superconductor. This effect is due to the presence of superconducting electrons, which have zero electrical resistance and are able to move in a coordinated manner, creating a current that opposes the magnetic field. The Meissner effect is a key feature of superconductivity and is used to distinguish superconductors from normal conductors. It is also important for understanding the behavior of superconductors in magnetic fields and for designing superconducting devices such as magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) machines and particle accelerators.
