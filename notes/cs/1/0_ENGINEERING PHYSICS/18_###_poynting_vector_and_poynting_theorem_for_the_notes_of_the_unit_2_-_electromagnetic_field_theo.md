### Poynting vector and Poynting theorem for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Poynting vector represents the flow of energy in an electromagnetic field. It is defined as the cross product of electric field (E) and magnetic field (B) vectors, mathematically represented as S = E x B. The direction of Poynting vector gives the direction of energy flow and its magnitude gives the rate of energy flow per unit area.

Poynting theorem states that the rate of energy flow into a closed surface is equal to the rate of change of energy inside the surface. Mathematically, it is represented as ∇.S = -dU/dt, where S is the Poynting vector, U is the energy density and t is time. This theorem provides a way to calculate the energy flow in an electromagnetic field and is useful in various applications such as power transmission and energy storage.
