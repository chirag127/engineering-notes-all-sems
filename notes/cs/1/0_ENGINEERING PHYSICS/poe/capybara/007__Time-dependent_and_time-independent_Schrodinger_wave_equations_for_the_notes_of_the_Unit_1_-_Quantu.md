### Time-dependent and time-independent Schrodinger wave equations for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS.

In the field of quantum mechanics, the Schrodinger equation plays a vital role in understanding the behavior of particles in a quantum system. The Schrodinger equation can be expressed in two forms: time-dependent and time-independent. Here we will discuss both forms of the Schrodinger wave equation.

#### Time-independent Schrodinger wave equation

The time-independent Schrodinger equation describes the stationary states of a quantum system, which do not change with time. It is given by:

Here, E is the energy of the system, and ψ is the wave function. This equation is used to calculate the allowed energy levels of a particle in a potential well.

#### Differences between time-dependent and time-independent Schrodinger wave equations

- The time-dependent Schrodinger equation describes the evolution of a system over time, while the time-independent Schrodinger equation describes the stationary states of a system.
- The time-dependent Schrodinger equation involves both time and position variables, while the time-independent Schrodinger equation only involves position variables.
- The time-dependent Schrodinger equation is used to calculate the probability density of finding a particle at a particular position and time, while the time-independent Schrodinger equation is used to calculate the allowed energy levels of a particle in a potential well.

In conclusion, the Schrodinger wave equation is a crucial tool in quantum mechanics that helps us understand the behavior of particles in a quantum system. The time-dependent and time-independent forms of the equation provide different insights into the properties of the system, and both equations are essential for solving various problems in quantum mechanics.
