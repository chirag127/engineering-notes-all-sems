### Poynting vector and Poynting theorem for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

In the study of electromagnetic fields, the concept of Poynting vector and Poynting theorem plays a crucial role. Here are some key points to help you understand these concepts:

- The Poynting vector is a mathematical representation of the flow of energy in an electromagnetic field. It is denoted by the symbol S and is given by the cross product of the electric field vector and the magnetic field vector.
- The Poynting vector gives the direction and magnitude of the energy per unit area per unit time that is flowing through a given point in a given direction. Its units are watts per square meter.
- The Poynting vector can be used to calculate the total radiated power of an antenna and is also used to calculate the power density of an electromagnetic wave in a given medium.
- The Poynting theorem is a statement of the conservation of energy in an electromagnetic field. It states that the rate of change of energy stored in a given volume of space is equal to the negative of the net outward flux of the Poynting vector through the surface enclosing the volume.
- The Poynting theorem can be used to calculate the power absorbed by a material or the power radiated by an antenna. It is also used to calculate the power density of an electromagnetic wave in a given medium.

In summary, the Poynting vector and Poynting theorem are important concepts in the study of electromagnetic fields. Understanding these concepts is crucial for engineers working in the field of electromagnetics, as they are used to design antennas, calculate power densities, and analyze electromagnetic waves.
