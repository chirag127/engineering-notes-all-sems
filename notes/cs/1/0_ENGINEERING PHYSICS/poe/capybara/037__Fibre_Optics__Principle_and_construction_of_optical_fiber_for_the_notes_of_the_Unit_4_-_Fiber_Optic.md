### Fibre Optics: Principle and construction of optical fiber

Fibre Optics is a technology that uses light to transmit data through optical fibers. Optical fibers are thin, flexible, and transparent glass or plastic fibers that are used to transmit light signals over long distances. In this section, we will discuss the principle and construction of optical fibers.

#### Principle of Optical Fiber

The principle of optical fiber is based on the phenomenon of total internal reflection (TIR). When light travels through a medium, it bends or refracts. However, when the angle of incidence is greater than the critical angle, the light is reflected back into the same medium. This phenomenon is called total internal reflection.

In an optical fiber, the core is made of a material that has a higher refractive index than the cladding. When light enters the core, it is reflected off the boundary between the core and cladding, and continues to travel down the fiber through a series of reflections.

#### Construction of Optical Fiber

An optical fiber consists of three main parts: the core, the cladding, and the coating.

- Core: The core is the central part of the fiber through which light travels. It is made of a material that has a higher refractive index than the cladding. The core is typically made of glass or plastic.

- Cladding: The cladding is a layer of material that surrounds the core. It is made of a material that has a lower refractive index than the core. The cladding helps to keep the light in the core by reflecting it back into the core when it tries to escape.

- Coating: The coating is a layer of protective material that surrounds the cladding. It is usually made of plastic and helps to protect the fiber from damage.

The optical fiber is usually made by heating and pulling a glass or plastic preform, which is a rod-shaped piece of glass or plastic. The preform is heated until it becomes soft and then it is pulled to create the fiber. The diameter of the fiber is determined by the size of the preform.

In conclusion, optical fibers are an important technology that is used for long-distance communication. The principle of optical fiber is based on the phenomenon of total internal reflection, and the construction of the fiber consists of three main parts: the core, the cladding, and the coating.
