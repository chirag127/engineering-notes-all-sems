### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

Solid-state and gas lasers are two types of lasers used in various applications. Here are some points to understand the difference between Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser):

#### Solid-state Laser (Ruby Laser)

- A solid-state laser is a type of laser that uses a solid-state gain medium, such as a crystal or glass, to produce the laser beam.
- The Ruby laser is one of the most common types of solid-state lasers, which uses a synthetic ruby crystal as the gain medium.
- The Ruby laser produces short pulses of light with a wavelength of 694.3 nm.
- These lasers are commonly used in scientific research, medical applications, and military applications.
- The Ruby laser is known for its high power output and its ability to produce extremely short pulses of light.

#### Gas Laser (He-Ne Laser)

- A gas laser uses a gas as the active medium to produce the laser beam.
- The He-Ne laser is one of the most common types of gas lasers, which uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as the active medium.
- The He-Ne laser produces a continuous wave of light with a wavelength of 632.8 nm.
- These lasers are commonly used in scientific research, educational demonstrations, and in barcode scanners.
- Gas lasers are known for their high beam quality, which makes them useful in applications that require precision.

In conclusion, both solid-state and gas lasers have their own unique properties that make them useful in various applications. The Ruby laser is known for its high power output and short pulses, while the He-Ne laser is known for its continuous wave and high beam quality.
