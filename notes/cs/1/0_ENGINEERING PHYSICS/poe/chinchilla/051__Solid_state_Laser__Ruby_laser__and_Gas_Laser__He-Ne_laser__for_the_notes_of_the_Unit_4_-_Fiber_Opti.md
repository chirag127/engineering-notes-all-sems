### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

In this section, we will discuss two types of lasers - Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser). Let's dive into the details of each type:

#### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser)

1. Ruby laser is a type of solid-state laser that uses a synthetic ruby crystal as a lasing medium.

2. The ruby crystal is excited by a high-energy flashlamp to produce a population inversion of energy levels, which leads to the stimulated emission of photons.

3. The emitted photons are then amplified by the ruby crystal, which acts as a resonator, and finally, a coherent beam of laser light is produced.

4. The wavelength of the ruby laser is in the red region of the electromagnetic spectrum, typically around 694 nanometers.

5. Ruby lasers have a high output power and can produce pulses with durations as short as a few nanoseconds.

6. Ruby lasers are used in various applications, such as in laser rangefinders, holography, and tattoo removal.

#### Gas Laser (He-Ne laser)

1. He-Ne laser is a type of gas laser that uses a mixture of helium and neon gases as a lasing medium.

2. The gas mixture is excited by a high voltage electrical discharge, which leads to a population inversion of energy levels and the stimulated emission of photons.

3. The emitted photons are then amplified by the gas mixture, which acts as a resonator, and finally, a coherent beam of laser light is produced.

4. The wavelength of the He-Ne laser is typically in the red or green region of the electromagnetic spectrum, around 632.8 nanometers or 543.5 nanometers, respectively.

5. He-Ne lasers have a low output power compared to other types of lasers, but they have a very high coherence and low divergence of the laser beam.

6. He-Ne lasers are used in various applications, such as in barcode scanners, interferometry, and alignment of optical components.

In conclusion, Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser) are two types of lasers that operate on different principles and have different applications.
