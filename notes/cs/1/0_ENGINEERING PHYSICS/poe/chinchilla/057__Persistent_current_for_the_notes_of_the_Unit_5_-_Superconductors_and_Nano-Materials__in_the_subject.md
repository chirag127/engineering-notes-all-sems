### Persistent Current for the notes of the Unit 5 - Superconductors and Nano-Materials: in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Persistent current is a fascinating phenomenon that occurs in superconductors, and it is essential to understand its properties and behavior. In this unit, we will discuss the persistent current and its significance in superconductors and nano-materials. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- A persistent current is a current that flows indefinitely in a superconductor loop with zero resistance.
- The persistent current occurs due to the formation of a closed loop of superconducting material, which is cooled below its critical temperature.
- The persistent current is also known as the persistent current state or the persistent current mode.
- In superconducting loops, the persistent current is proportional to the cross-sectional area of the loop and the critical current density of the superconductor.
- The persistent current is a crucial property of superconductors and has several practical applications, such as in the development of high-speed digital circuits, sensors, and energy storage devices.
- The persistent current can be experimentally observed by measuring the magnetic field of the superconducting loop, which shows a constant value even after the external magnetic field is removed.
- The persistent current is affected by various factors such as the temperature, magnetic field, and the size of the superconducting loop.
- The persistent current is also observed in other nano-materials such as carbon nanotubes and graphene, which are relatively new materials with exciting properties.
- The persistent current in nano-materials can be controlled by manipulating the geometry and electronic properties of the nanostructure, which opens up new avenues for research and development.

In conclusion, understanding the persistent current is crucial for the design and development of superconducting and nano-material-based devices. It is a fascinating phenomenon that has practical applications in various fields, including electronics, energy, and sensing. Therefore, it is essential to study the properties and behavior of the persistent current in detail to unlock the full potential of superconductors and nano-materials.
