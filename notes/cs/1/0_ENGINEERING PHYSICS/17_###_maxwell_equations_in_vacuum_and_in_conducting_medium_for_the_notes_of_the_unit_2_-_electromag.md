### Maxwell equations in vacuum and in conducting medium for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Maxwell's Equations describe the behavior of electric and magnetic fields in both vacuum and conducting media.

In vacuum:
1. Gauss's Law: Electric flux through any closed surface is proportional to the charge enclosed within the surface.
2. Faraday's Law: A changing magnetic field induces an electric field.
3. Ampere's Law with Maxwell's correction: The line integral of magnetic field around a closed loop is proportional to the current passing through the loop.
4. Gauss's Law for Magnetism: There are no magnetic monopoles.

In conducting medium:
1. Gauss's Law: Electric flux through any closed surface is proportional to the charge enclosed within the surface.
2. Faraday's Law: A changing magnetic field induces an electric field.
3. Ohm's Law: The electric field within a conductor is proportional to the current density.
4. Ampere's Law with Maxwell's correction: The line integral of magnetic field around a closed loop is proportional to the current passing through the loop.
