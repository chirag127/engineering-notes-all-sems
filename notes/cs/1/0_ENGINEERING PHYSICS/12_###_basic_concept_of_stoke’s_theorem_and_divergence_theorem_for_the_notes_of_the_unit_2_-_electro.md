### Basic concept of Stoke’s theorem and Divergence theorem for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Stoke's Theorem: 
- states that the line integral of a vector field around a closed curve is equal to the surface integral of the curl of the vector field over a surface bounded by the curve.
- relates the circulation of a vector field around a closed curve to the curl of the vector field over a surface bounded by the curve.

Divergence Theorem:
- states that the surface integral of the normal component of a vector field over a closed surface is equal to the volume integral of the divergence of the vector field over the volume enclosed by the surface.
- relates the amount of a vector field flowing out of a closed surface to the amount of the divergence of the vector field within the volume enclosed by the surface.
