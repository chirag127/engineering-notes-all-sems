### Einstein’s Coefficients for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Einstein's coefficients describe the probabilities for the absorption and emission of photons in quantum mechanics. There are two coefficients, A and B, which describe the rate of absorption and emission of photons, respectively. These coefficients are important in the field of fiber optics and laser technology. In fiber optics, they are used to calculate the loss of light in optical fibers, and in laser technology, they are used to determine the gain of a laser. The coefficients are dependent on the energy levels of the material and the frequency of the light. Understanding Einstein's coefficients is crucial for designing and optimizing optical systems in engineering physics.
