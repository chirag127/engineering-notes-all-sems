### Time-dependent and time-independent Schrodinger wave equations for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Time-dependent Schrodinger wave equation: 
  Describes how the wave function of a particle changes with time. It is given by:
  iℏ ∂ψ/∂t = Hψ
  where i is the imaginary unit, ℏ is the reduced Planck constant, ψ is the wave function, t is time and H is the Hamiltonian operator representing the total energy of the system.

- Time-independent Schrodinger wave equation:
  Describes the wave function of a particle in a given potential, without considering the time-dependence. It is given by:
  Hψ = Eψ
  where E is the energy eigenvalue of the system and H and ψ are as defined above.

Note: The solutions of these equations give the probabilities of finding a particle at a given location in space and time.
