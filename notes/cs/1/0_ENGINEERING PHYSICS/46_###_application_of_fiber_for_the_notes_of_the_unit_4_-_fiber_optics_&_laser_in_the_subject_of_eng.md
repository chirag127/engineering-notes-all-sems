### Application of fiber for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Fiber optics is the study of transmitting information using light through optical fibers. It has numerous applications in various fields including: 
1. Telecommunications: fiber optic cables are used for high-speed data transmission in telecommunications networks 
2. Medical: fiber optic devices are used for medical imaging and endoscopy 
3. Lighting: fiber optic lighting is used in various applications such as automotive lighting and stage lighting 
4. Sensing: fiber optic sensors are used for temperature, pressure, and strain sensing 
5. Military: fiber optic technology is used in military communication systems for secure and fast transmission of data 
6. Industrial: fiber optic technology is used in industrial automation and control systems for sensing and data transmission. 

Laser technology has a wide range of applications including: 
1. Material processing: laser cutting, welding, and marking 
2. Medical: laser therapy and surgery 
3. Military: laser range finding and target designation 
4. Entertainment: laser displays and light shows 
5. Scientific research: spectroscopy and interferometry.
