### Particle in a one-Dimensional box for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

A particle in a one-dimensional box is a model system used in quantum mechanics to study the behavior of particles confined to a limited space. The particle is confined to a region between two fixed walls, and its energy is quantized, meaning it can only have certain values. The quantization of energy arises from the wave-particle duality of quantum mechanics, which states that particles can also exhibit wave-like behavior. The allowed energy levels and wave functions of the particle in the box can be found using the Schrödinger equation. The wave functions are standing waves, meaning they do not change with time, and they form a complete basis for the description of the particle's state. This model is used to understand the basic principles of quantum mechanics and to study the behavior of particles in confined spaces.
