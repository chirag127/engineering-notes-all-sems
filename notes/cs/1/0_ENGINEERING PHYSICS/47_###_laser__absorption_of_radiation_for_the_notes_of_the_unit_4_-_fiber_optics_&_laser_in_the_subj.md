### Laser: Absorption of radiation for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Laser: Absorption of radiation refers to the process of a material absorbing energy from a laser beam. This can occur due to the interaction between the laser beam and the material's electrons, leading to the absorption of photons and a corresponding increase in the energy of the electrons. Absorption can result in a reduction of the intensity of the laser beam, leading to a decrease in the amount of light transmitted through the material. Absorption is a critical factor in the design and performance of fiber optic communication systems and laser devices.
