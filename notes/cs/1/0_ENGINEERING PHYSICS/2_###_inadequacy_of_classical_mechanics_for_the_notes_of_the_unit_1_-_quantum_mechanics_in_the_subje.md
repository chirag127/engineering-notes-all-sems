### Inadequacy of classical mechanics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Inadequacy of classical mechanics:
1. At the atomic and subatomic level, classical mechanics fails to accurately predict behavior.
2. Cannot explain phenomena such as wave-particle duality and quantization of energy.
3. Does not account for the probabilistic nature of quantum systems.
4. Cannot describe the behavior of entangled particles.
5. Fails to explain the stability of atoms and molecules.
