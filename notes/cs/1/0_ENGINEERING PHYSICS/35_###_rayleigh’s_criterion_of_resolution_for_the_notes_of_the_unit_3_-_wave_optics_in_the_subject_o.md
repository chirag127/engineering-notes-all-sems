### Rayleigh’s criterion of resolution for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Rayleigh's criterion of resolution is a method to determine the minimum separation between two point sources of light that can be distinguished as separate sources by a telescope or an optical instrument. It states that the minimum separation between two point sources is equal to 1.22 times the wavelength of light divided by the diameter of the objective lens. The criterion is based on the fact that the diffraction pattern of two point sources will overlap and become indistinguishable when their separation is less than the Rayleigh criterion. In wave optics, Rayleigh's criterion is an important tool for evaluating the resolving power of optical instruments.
