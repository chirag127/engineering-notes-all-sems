### Plane electromagnetic waves in conducting medium for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Plane electromagnetic waves in conducting medium:
- Electromagnetic waves can travel through conductive materials like metals.
- In a conducting medium, the wave experiences skin depth effect, where the amplitude of the wave decreases exponentially with depth.
- The wave velocity in a conducting medium is less than in vacuum.
- The wave impedance in a conducting medium is different from that in vacuum.
- The wave polarization in a conducting medium is also affected.
