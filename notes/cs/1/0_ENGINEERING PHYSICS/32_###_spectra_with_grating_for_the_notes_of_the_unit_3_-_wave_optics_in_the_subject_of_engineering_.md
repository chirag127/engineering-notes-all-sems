### Spectra with grating for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Spectra with Grating:
1. Dispersion: Grating separates light into its component wavelengths, producing a spectrum.
2. Resolution: Grating can have high resolution, allowing for precise measurement of wavelengths.
3. Efficiency: Gratings can be highly efficient, transmitting a large portion of incident light.
4. Multiple orders: Grating can produce multiple orders of spectra, providing more information.
5. Wavelength range: Gratings can be designed for specific wavelength ranges, from UV to IR.
