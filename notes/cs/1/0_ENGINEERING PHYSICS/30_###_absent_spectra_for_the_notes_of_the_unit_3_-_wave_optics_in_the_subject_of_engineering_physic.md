### Absent spectra for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Absorption Spectra:
1. Absorption: Light energy is absorbed by a material and its intensity decreases.
2. Wavelength-dependent: Absorption varies based on the wavelength of light.
3. Characteristic: Each material has a unique absorption spectrum.
4. Analytical tool: Absorption spectra can be used to identify and quantify the presence of materials.
5. Continuous or line: Absorption can be either continuous or occur at specific wavelengths (line spectra).
