### Step index and graded index fibers for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Step index fiber:
- A type of optical fiber where the core has a uniform refractive index.
- Light travels in a straight path through the core due to the uniform refractive index.
- Light is reflected back into the core at the core-cladding interface.
- Used in short-distance communication systems.

Graded index fiber:
- A type of optical fiber where the refractive index decreases gradually from the core to the cladding.
- Light travels in a curved path through the core due to the gradient of the refractive index.
- Minimizes the amount of light reflected back into the core, reducing signal attenuation.
- Used in long-distance communication systems.
