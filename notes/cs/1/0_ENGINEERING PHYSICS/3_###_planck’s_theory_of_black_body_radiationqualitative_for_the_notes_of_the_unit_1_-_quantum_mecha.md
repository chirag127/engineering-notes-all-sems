### Planck’s theory of black body radiation(qualitative) for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Planck's theory of black body radiation states that a black body (an object that absorbs all wavelengths of light) emits energy at a specific frequency that depends only on its temperature. He proposed that energy is emitted and absorbed in discrete packets called quanta, rather than continuously. This led to the development of quantum mechanics.

A gray body, on the other hand, absorbs and emits some wavelengths of light, but not all. The frequency at which it emits energy depends on both its temperature and its emissivity (the fraction of energy it emits compared to a black body).
