### Laser applications for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

1. Material Processing: Lasers are used for cutting, welding, drilling, and marking of various materials.

2. Medical Applications: Lasers are used for surgical procedures, cancer treatment, and dental procedures.

3. Communication: Fiber optic communication systems use lasers for transmitting information over long distances.

4. Metrology: Lasers are used for measuring distances, angles, and time with high accuracy.

5. Spectroscopy: Lasers are used for studying the interaction of light with matter and for analyzing the composition of materials.

6. Scientific Research: Lasers are used in various scientific experiments, such as studying the properties of materials, measuring the speed of light, and studying the behavior of atoms and molecules.

7. Military & Defense: Lasers are used for target designation, rangefinding, and as weapons.

8. Industrial Processing: Lasers are used for quality control, material analysis, and surface treatment in various industries.
