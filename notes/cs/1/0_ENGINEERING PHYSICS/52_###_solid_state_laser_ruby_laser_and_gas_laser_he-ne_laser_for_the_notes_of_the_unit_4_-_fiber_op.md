### Solid state Laser (Ruby laser) and Gas Laser (He-Ne laser) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Solid State Laser (Ruby laser):
- Ruby lasers produce laser light from a solid crystal of ruby (Al2O3:Cr3+)
- They emit red light at 694.3 nm and have a high energy output.
- They work by optically pumping the ruby crystal with a flash lamp, causing it to emit laser light.
- The laser light is then collimated and focused through a lens.

Gas Laser (He-Ne laser):
- He-Ne lasers produce laser light from a mixture of helium and neon gases.
- They emit light at 632.8 nm and are widely used in industrial, scientific, and medical applications.
- They work by creating an electric discharge in the gas mixture, which excites the atoms and causes them to emit laser light.
- The laser light is collimated and focused through a lens.
