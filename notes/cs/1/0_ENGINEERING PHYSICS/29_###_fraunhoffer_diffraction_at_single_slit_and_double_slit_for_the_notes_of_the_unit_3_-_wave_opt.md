### Fraunhoffer diffraction at single slit and double slit for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Fraunhofer Diffraction:

Single Slit:
- Occurs when light passes through a narrow opening, causing the light waves to spread out
- Result is a diffraction pattern of bright and dark fringes on a screen
- The width of the fringes is proportional to the wavelength of the light
- The position of the fringes is determined by the width of the slit and the distance from the slit to the screen

Double Slit:
- Occurs when light passes through two parallel slits, causing the light waves to interfere with each other
- Result is an interference pattern of bright and dark fringes on a screen
- The position of the fringes is determined by the distance between the slits and the distance from the slits to the screen
- The number of fringes is proportional to the wavelength of the light and the distance between the slits
