## Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics

- Quantum mechanics is a branch of physics that deals with the behavior of matter and light on the atomic and subatomic scale .
- Quantum mechanics is based on the idea that physical quantities, such as energy, momentum, angular momentum, electric charge, etc., can only take discrete values, called quanta .
- Quantum mechanics also introduces the concept of wave-particle duality, which states that all particles have both wave and particle properties, and vice versa .
- Quantum mechanics is governed by a set of mathematical rules and equations, such as the Schrödinger equation, the Heisenberg uncertainty principle, the Pauli exclusion principle, etc., that describe the probability of finding a particle in a given state or location  .
- Quantum mechanics has many applications and implications in various fields of science and technology, such as quantum chemistry, quantum field theory, quantum information, quantum cryptography, quantum computing, etc .
