### Quantum wires and Quantum well

- Quantum wires and quantum wells are examples of **nanostructures** that exhibit **quantum confinement** effects due to their reduced dimensions.
- Quantum confinement occurs when the size of a material is comparable to or smaller than the **de Broglie wavelength** of the charge carriers, such as electrons and holes.
- Quantum confinement leads to the formation of **discrete energy levels** and changes in the electronic, optical and transport properties of the material.

#### Quantum wells

- A quantum well is a **two-dimensional** (2D) structure or layer of a **lower bandgap** semiconductor material embedded in a **higher bandgap** matrix material.
- A quantum well can be formed by techniques such as **molecular beam epitaxy** or **chemical vapor deposition**.
- A quantum well confines the charge carriers in the direction perpendicular to the layer, while allowing them to move freely in the plane of the layer.
- A quantum well has a **parabolic** potential well in the direction of confinement and a **constant** potential in the other directions.
- A quantum well has a **discrete** set of energy levels for the confined direction, called **subbands**, and a **continuous** spectrum for the free directions, called **bands**.
- A quantum well can be used to make devices such as **quantum well lasers**, **quantum well infrared photodetectors**, **quantum cascade lasers**, and **quantum well solar cells**.

#### Quantum wires

- A quantum wire is a **one-dimensional** (1D) structure or nanowire of a lower bandgap semiconductor material embedded in a higher bandgap matrix material.
- A quantum wire can be formed by techniques such as **nanolithography**, **self-assembly**, or **template-based synthesis**.
- A quantum wire confines the charge carriers in two directions perpendicular to the nanowire axis, while allowing them to move freely along the nanowire axis.
- A quantum wire has a **parabolic** potential well in the directions of confinement and a **constant** potential in the direction of freedom.
- A quantum wire has a **discrete** set of energy levels for the confined directions, called **subbands**, and a **continuous** spectrum for the free direction, called **band**.
- A quantum wire can be used to make devices such as **quantum wire transistors**, **quantum wire field-effect transistors**, **quantum wire sensors**, and **quantum wire interconnects**.
