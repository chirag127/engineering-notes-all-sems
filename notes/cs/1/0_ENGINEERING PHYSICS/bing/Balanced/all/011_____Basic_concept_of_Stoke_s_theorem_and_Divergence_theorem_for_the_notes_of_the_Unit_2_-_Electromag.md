# Basic concept of Stoke’s theorem and Divergence theorem

- Stoke's theorem and Divergence theorem are two important theorems in vector calculus that relate the integrals of vector fields over different dimensions.
- Stoke's theorem (also known as Kelvin-Stoke's theorem) states that the line integral of a vector field around a closed curve is equal to the surface integral of the curl of the vector field over any surface bounded by the curve .
- Divergence theorem (also known as Gauss's theorem) states that the surface integral of a vector field over a closed surface is equal to the volume integral of the divergence of the vector field over any volume enclosed by the surface .
- Both theorems are generalizations of the fundamental theorem of calculus, which states that the integral of a function over an interval is equal to the difference of the function values at the endpoints of the interval .
- Both theorems can be used to simplify the calculation of integrals of vector fields over complicated domains by reducing the dimension of the integration .
- Both theorems have applications in physics, such as electromagnetism, fluid dynamics, and heat transfer .
