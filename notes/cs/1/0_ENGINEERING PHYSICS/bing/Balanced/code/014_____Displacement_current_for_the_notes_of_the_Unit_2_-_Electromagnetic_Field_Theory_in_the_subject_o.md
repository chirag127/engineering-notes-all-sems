### Displacement current

- Displacement current is a phenomenon in electromagnetism that is analogous to an ordinary electric current, but occurs when the electric field changes with time .
- Displacement current density is the quantity ∂D/∂t, where D is the electric displacement field, and it has the same units as electric current density (A/m^2^) .

- Displacement current is necessary to explain the magnetic fields produced by changing electric fields, such as in a capacitor with a time-varying voltage .
- Displacement current also plays a central role in the propagation of electromagnetic waves, such as light and radio waves, through empty space or vacuum . A traveling, varying magnetic field is everywhere associated with a periodically changing electric field that can be conceived in terms of a displacement current.
