### Dispersive Power

- Dispersive power is a measure of how much a material can separate different wavelengths of light by refraction.
- Dispersive power of a prism is defined as the ratio of angular dispersion to mean deviation produced by the prism  .
- Angular dispersion is the difference between the angles of deviation of two extreme wavelengths of light (usually violet and red) when they pass through the prism.
- Mean deviation is the angle of deviation of the mean ray (the ray having the mean wavelength) when it passes symmetrically through the prism.
- The formula for dispersive power is given as:

$$\omega = \frac{\theta}{\delta}$$

where $\omega$ is the dispersive power, $\theta$ is the angular dispersion, and $\delta$ is the mean deviation.

- Dispersive power depends on the material and shape of the prism, as well as the wavelengths of light used.
- Dispersive power is inversely proportional to the refractive index of the material, and directly proportional to the angle of the prism.
- Dispersive power also depends on the difference between the refractive indices of the two extreme wavelengths of light. The greater the difference, the higher the dispersive power.
- The formula for dispersive power in terms of refractive indices is given as :

$$\omega = \frac{\mu - 1}{\mu_v - \mu_r}$$

where $\mu$ is the refractive index of the mean ray, $\mu_v$ is the refractive index of the violet ray, and $\mu_r$ is the refractive index of the red ray.

- Dispersive power can be used to compare the ability of different materials or prisms to disperse light into its spectrum.
- Dispersive power can also be used to calculate the angular dispersion or the mean deviation of a prism, if the other quantities are known.

- Example 1: Calculating Dispersive Power from Angles of Deviation

  White light dispersed by a prism has wavelengths ranging from 400 nm to 700 nm. The angle of deviation of the 400 nm wavelength is 23.5°, and the angle of deviation of the 700 nm wavelength is 21.5°. The angle of deviation of the mean ray is 22.5°. What is the dispersive power of the prism?

  Solution:

  The angular dispersion of the prism is the difference between the angles of deviation of the violet and red rays:

  $$\theta = 23.5° - 21.5° = 2°$$

  The mean deviation of the prism is the angle of deviation of the mean ray:

  $$\delta = 22.5°$$

  The dispersive power of the prism is the ratio of angular dispersion to mean deviation:

  $$\omega = \frac{\theta}{\delta} = \frac{2°}{22.5°} = 0.089$$

  The dispersive power of the prism is 0.089.
