### Population inversion

- Population inversion is a state of a system, for example a laser gain medium, where a higher-lying energy level is more strongly populated than a lower-lying level.
- Population inversion is an important concept for lasers optics, as it is essential for light amplification by stimulated emission.
- Population inversion cannot occur under conditions of thermal equilibrium, where the level populations are described by a Boltzmann distribution.
- Population inversion can be achieved by various methods, such as optical pumping, electrical pumping, chemical pumping, or collisional pumping.
- Population inversion can be achieved in different types of lasers, such as gas lasers, solid-state lasers, fiber lasers, and semiconductor lasers   .
- Population inversion can be characterized by the inversion factor, which is the ratio of the population densities of the upper and lower levels involved in the laser transition.
