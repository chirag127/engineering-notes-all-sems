### Fibre Optics: Principle and construction of optical fiber

- Fibre optics is the science of transmitting data, voice, and images by the passage of light through thin, transparent fibers.
- Optical fibers are flexible, transparent fibers made by drawing glass or plastic to a diameter slightly thicker than that of a human hair.
- Optical fibers work on the principle of total internal reflection of light, which means that no part of the light signal gets refracted or transmitted to the second medium, but instead, all of the signal is retained inside the fiber .
- The condition for total internal reflection is that the light must travel from an optically denser medium (such as glass) to an optically rarer medium (such as air) and the angle of incidence must be greater than the critical angle for the boundary.
- The construction of an optical fiber consists of three main parts: the core, the cladding, and the protective layer .
  - The core is the innermost part of the fiber, where the light is transmitted. It is made of glass or plastic with a high refractive index (n1).
  - The cladding is the outer layer of the fiber, which surrounds the core. It is made of glass or plastic with a lower refractive index (n2) than the core. The cladding acts as a mirror that reflects the light back into the core, preventing it from escaping.
  - The protective layer is the outermost layer of the fiber, which covers the cladding. It is made of plastic or rubber and provides mechanical strength and protection from moisture, dust, and abrasion.
- The diameter of the core and the cladding vary depending on the type and application of the optical fiber. The typical values are 8 μm and 125 μm respectively for a single-mode fiber, and 50 μm and 125 μm respectively for a multi-mode fiber.
- A single-mode fiber allows only one mode of light to propagate through the core, which means that the light travels in a straight line with minimal dispersion and attenuation. A single-mode fiber is used for long-distance and high-bandwidth communication.
- A multi-mode fiber allows multiple modes of light to propagate through the core, which means that the light travels in different paths with different speeds and angles. A multi-mode fiber is used for short-distance and low-bandwidth communication.
