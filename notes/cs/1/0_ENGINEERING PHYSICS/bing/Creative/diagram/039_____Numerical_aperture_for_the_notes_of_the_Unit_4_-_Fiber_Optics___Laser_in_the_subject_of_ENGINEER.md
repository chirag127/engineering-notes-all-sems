### Numerical aperture

- Numerical aperture (NA) is the measure of the ability of an optical fiber to collect or confine the incident light ray inside it .
- It is among the most basic property of optical fiber and shows the efficiency with which light is collected inside the fiber in order to get propagated.
- Numerical aperture is defined as the sine of half of the angle of the fiber's light acceptance cone , i.e.,

$$
NA = \sin \theta_a
$$

where $\theta_a$ is called the acceptance angle.

- The acceptance angle is the maximum angle at which the light can enter the fiber and still be guided along the core .
- The numerical aperture depends on the refractive indices of the core and the cladding of the fiber, as well as the wavelength of the light  .
- The numerical aperture can be calculated by using the following formula  :

$$
NA = \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2}
$$

where $n_1$ is the refractive index of the core and $n_2$ is the refractive index of the cladding.

- The numerical aperture can also be expressed in terms of the critical angle $\theta_c$, which is the angle of incidence at which the light is totally internally reflected at the core-cladding interface  , i.e.,

$$
NA = n_0 \sin \theta_c
$$

where $n_0$ is the refractive index of the medium outside the fiber (usually air).

- The numerical aperture determines the bandwidth, the coupling efficiency, and the modal dispersion of the fiber  .
- A higher numerical aperture means a larger acceptance angle, which allows more light to enter the fiber, but also increases the modal dispersion and reduces the bandwidth  .
- A lower numerical aperture means a smaller acceptance angle, which reduces the amount of light that can enter the fiber, but also decreases the modal dispersion and increases the bandwidth  .
- Numerical aperture is commonly used in microscopy to describe the acceptance cone of an objective (and hence its light-gathering ability and resolution), and in fiber optics, in which it describes the range of angles within which light that is incident on the fiber will be transmitted along it.
