### Relation between electric and magnetic fields of an electromagnetic wave

- An electromagnetic wave is a disturbance that propagates through space and time, carrying both electric and magnetic fields.
- The electric and magnetic fields of an electromagnetic wave are perpendicular to each other and to the direction of propagation .
- The electric and magnetic fields of an electromagnetic wave have the same magnitude and frequency, and they oscillate in phase.
- The electric and magnetic fields of an electromagnetic wave are related by Maxwell's equations, which describe how a changing electric field generates a magnetic field, and vice versa.
- The relationship between electric and magnetic fields is what allows the formation of electromagnetic waves, including light and heat, and is fundamental to the working of the universe in its present form.
- The relationship between electric and magnetic fields can be demonstrated by various experiments, such as passing a steady current through a straight conductor, which produces a magnetic field around it, or moving a magnet near a coil of wire, which induces an electric current in it .
