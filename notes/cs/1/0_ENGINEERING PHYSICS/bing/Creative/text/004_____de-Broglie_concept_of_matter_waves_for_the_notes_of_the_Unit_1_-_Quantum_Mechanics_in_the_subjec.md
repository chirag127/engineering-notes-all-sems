### de-Broglie concept of matter waves

- The de-Broglie concept of matter waves is based on the idea that **all matter exhibits wave-like properties** when it is in motion .
- This concept was proposed by Louis de Broglie in 1924 as a **generalization of the photon theory of light**, which states that light can behave as both a wave and a particle .
- The de-Broglie concept of matter waves implies that **matter has a dual nature**, similar to light. When matter is at rest, it shows particle properties, such as mass and momentum. When matter is in motion, it shows wave properties, such as wavelength and frequency .
- The de-Broglie concept of matter waves can be expressed by the following equation  :

    \[ \lambda = \frac{h}{p} \]

    where $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the matter wave, $h$ is the Planck's constant, and $p$ is the momentum of the matter particle.
- The de-Broglie concept of matter waves can explain some of the **quantum phenomena** observed in the microscopic world, such as the **quantization of the electron's angular momentum** in the hydrogen atom, the **diffraction and interference of electrons** by crystals, and the **uncertainty principle** of Heisenberg  .
