# Acceptance cone

- The acceptance cone is the cone-shaped region of space around the optical fiber that defines the range of angles within which light can enter the fiber and be propagated through it .
- The acceptance cone is derived by rotating the acceptance angle, which is the maximum angle with the axis of the optical fiber at which light can enter the fiber, around the fiber axis .
- The acceptance angle depends on the refractive indices of the core, the cladding, and the surrounding medium of the optical fiber .
- The acceptance cone can also be characterized by the numerical aperture (NA), which is a measure of the light-gathering ability and resolution of the optical fiber .
- The numerical aperture is defined as the sine of the acceptance angle multiplied by the refractive index of the surrounding medium .
- The larger the acceptance cone, the larger the numerical aperture, and the more light can be coupled into the bound modes of the optical fiber .
