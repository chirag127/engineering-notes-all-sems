## Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser

Fiber optics and lasers are two important technologies that have revolutionized the field of telecommunications and medicine. Here are some key points to know about these technologies:

1. **Fiber Optics** is a technology that uses thin, flexible fibers of glass or plastic to transmit light signals over long distances. The light signals carry information, such as voice, video, and data.

2. **Lasers** are devices that produce intense beams of light. They work by stimulating the emission of light from atoms or molecules. Lasers have many applications, including telecommunications, medicine, and manufacturing.

3. In telecommunications, fiber optics and lasers are used together to transmit information over long distances. Lasers generate the light signals, which are then sent through the fiber optic cables.

4. Fiber optic cables have several advantages over traditional copper cables. They can transmit information over longer distances, have higher bandwidth, and are immune to electromagnetic interference.

5. In medicine, lasers are used for a variety of procedures, including surgery, vision correction, and cancer treatment. They offer precision and control, minimizing damage to surrounding tissue.

6. Fiber optics and lasers have also found applications in other fields, such as manufacturing, where they are used for cutting, welding, and marking materials.

7. The development of fiber optics and lasers has led to many advancements and has greatly impacted our daily lives. They continue to be an active area of research and development, with new applications being discovered regularly.
