# Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser: Application of Fiber

Fiber optics is a technology that uses glass or plastic fibers to transmit data. It has many applications in various fields, including:

1. **Telecommunications**: Fiber optic cables are used to transmit telephone, internet, and cable television signals over long distances. They offer higher bandwidth and faster data transmission rates than traditional copper cables.

2. **Medical**: Fiber optics is used in medical equipment such as endoscopes, which allow doctors to see inside the body without performing surgery. They are also used in surgical instruments, such as lasers, to perform minimally invasive procedures.

3. **Sensors**: Fiber optic sensors are used to measure temperature, pressure, and other physical properties. They are used in various industries, including oil and gas, aerospace, and civil engineering.

4. **Lighting**: Fiber optic lighting is used in decorative and architectural applications, such as in museums, theaters, and swimming pools. It is also used in automotive and aircraft lighting.

5. **Military**: Fiber optics is used in military communications and surveillance systems. It offers secure and reliable data transmission, even in harsh environments.

These are just a few examples of the many applications of fiber optics. The technology continues to evolve, and new applications are being developed all the time.
