### Coherent sources for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Coherent sources are sources of light that emit waves of the same frequency and constant phase difference.
- The waves from coherent sources interfere with each other to produce a stable interference pattern.
- Coherent sources can be obtained by dividing the light from a single source into two or more parts, using devices such as beam splitters or mirrors.
- Lasers are an example of a source of coherent light.
- Coherence is important in applications such as holography, interferometry, and optical communication.
