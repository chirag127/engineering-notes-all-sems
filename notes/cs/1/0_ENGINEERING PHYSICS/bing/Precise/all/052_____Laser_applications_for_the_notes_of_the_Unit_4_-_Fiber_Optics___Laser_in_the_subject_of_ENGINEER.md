# Laser Applications

Lasers have a wide range of applications in various fields. Here are some of the most common applications of lasers in the field of engineering physics:

1. **Communication:** Lasers are used in fiber optic communication systems to transmit data over long distances. The laser light is modulated to carry the information and is sent through an optical fiber.

2. **Medicine:** Lasers are used in various medical procedures such as laser eye surgery, laser hair removal, and laser tattoo removal. They are also used in medical imaging and diagnostics.

3. **Industry:** Lasers are used in various industrial processes such as cutting, welding, and drilling. They are also used for marking and engraving.

4. **Measurement:** Lasers are used for precise measurements in various fields such as surveying, construction, and manufacturing. They are also used in scientific research for measuring distances, velocities, and other physical quantities.

5. **Entertainment:** Lasers are used in various forms of entertainment such as laser light shows, laser tag, and laser projection.

These are just a few of the many applications of lasers in the field of engineering physics. Lasers have revolutionized many fields and continue to be an important tool in research and industry.
