### Dispersion for the notes of the Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

1. Dispersion is the phenomenon of splitting of white light into its constituent colors.
2. It occurs due to the dependence of the refractive index of a medium on the wavelength of light.
3. In fiber optics, dispersion can cause a broadening of light pulses as they travel along the fiber, leading to a reduction in the data transmission rate.
4. There are two main types of dispersion in optical fibers: chromatic dispersion and modal dispersion.
5. Chromatic dispersion occurs due to the variation of the refractive index of the core material with wavelength.
6. Modal dispersion occurs due to the different propagation times of the various modes of light in a multimode fiber.
7. Dispersion can be minimized by using single-mode fibers, dispersion-shifted fibers, or dispersion compensating fibers.
8. Dispersion compensation techniques can also be employed to mitigate the effects of dispersion in long-haul optical communication systems.
