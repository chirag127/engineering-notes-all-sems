### Coherent sources for the notes of the Unit 3 - Wave Optics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- Coherent sources are sources of light that emit waves of the same frequency and constant phase difference.
- The waves produced by coherent sources interfere with each other, producing an interference pattern.
- Coherent sources can be created by dividing a single light source into two or more beams using a beam splitter or by using lasers.
- Lasers produce coherent light by stimulating the emission of photons from atoms or molecules in a process called stimulated emission.
- Coherent sources are important in many applications, including holography, interferometry, and optical communications.
- Incoherent sources, on the other hand, emit light with a range of frequencies and phases, and the waves do not interfere with each other in a predictable manner.
- Incoherent sources include incandescent light bulbs, the sun, and most other natural light sources.
- Coherence is a measure of the correlation between the phases of two waves, and it is an important property of light in many optical systems.
