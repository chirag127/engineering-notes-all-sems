### Skin Depth

Skin depth is an important concept in Electromagnetic Field Theory. It is defined as the distance over which the magnitude of the electric or magnetic field is reduced by a factor of 1/e (about 37%) of the value at the surface. This distance is referred to as the skin depth of the conductor.

- Skin depth is represented by the symbol δ.
- It depends on the frequency of the current and the electrical and magnetic properties of the conductor.
- An electromagnetic wave entering a conducting surface is damped and reduces in amplitude by a factor of 1/e in a distance δ.
- Theoretically, for eddy current, skin depth is given by the formula: δ ≈ 1/√πfμσ, where f is the coil frequency, μ is the magnetic permeability (in H/mm) of the material, and σ is the electrical conductivity (in %IACS) of the material.
