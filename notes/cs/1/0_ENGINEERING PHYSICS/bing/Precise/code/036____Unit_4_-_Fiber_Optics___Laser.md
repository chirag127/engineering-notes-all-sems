## Unit 4 - Fiber Optics & Laser

Fiber optics and lasers are two important technologies that have revolutionized the field of telecommunications and medicine. Here are some key points to know about these technologies:

1. **Fiber Optics** is a technology that uses thin, flexible fibers of glass or plastic to transmit data as light signals over long distances. These fibers are bundled together to form a cable, which can carry a large amount of data at high speeds.

2. **Lasers** are devices that produce intense beams of light. They work by stimulating the emission of light from atoms or molecules, resulting in a highly focused and coherent beam of light.

3. Fiber optic cables are used in telecommunications to transmit data over long distances. They have several advantages over traditional copper cables, including higher bandwidth, longer transmission distances, and immunity to electromagnetic interference.

4. Lasers have many applications in medicine, including surgery, diagnostics, and therapy. They can be used to cut, vaporize, or coagulate tissue, and are often used in procedures such as LASIK eye surgery and cancer treatment.

5. Both fiber optics and lasers rely on the properties of light and its interaction with matter. Understanding the principles of optics and the behavior of light is essential to understanding these technologies.

6. Fiber optics and lasers have revolutionized many fields, and their applications continue to expand as new technologies and techniques are developed. They are essential tools in modern telecommunications, medicine, and many other fields.
