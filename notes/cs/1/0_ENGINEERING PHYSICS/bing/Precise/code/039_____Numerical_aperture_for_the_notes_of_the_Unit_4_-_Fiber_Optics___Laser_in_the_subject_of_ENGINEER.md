### Numerical Aperture

Numerical aperture (NA) is a measure of the angular acceptance of an optical system, such as an imaging system or a fiber optic. It is defined based on geometrical considerations and is a theoretical parameter calculated from the optical design .

In fiber optics, the numerical aperture describes the range of angles within which light that is incident on the fiber will be transmitted along it . It is considered as the light-gathering capacity of an optical fiber. The numerical aperture is defined as the sine of half of the angle of the fiber's light acceptance cone, i.e., NA = sin(θa), where θa is called the acceptance cone angle .

In laser physics, the numerical aperture is defined slightly differently. Laser beams spread out as they propagate, but slowly. Far away from the narrowest part of the beam, the spread is roughly linear with distance, forming a cone of light in the "far field" .
