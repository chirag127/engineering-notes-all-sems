### Continuity equation for current density for the notes of the Unit 2 - Electromagnetic Field Theory in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

- The continuity equation for current density is a mathematical expression that describes the conservation of electric charge.
- It states that the rate of change of electric charge in a volume is equal to the net current flowing into the volume.
- Mathematically, the continuity equation is expressed as: ∇ • J + ∂ρ/∂t = 0, where J is the current density, ρ is the charge density, and t is time.
- The equation can be derived from the principle of conservation of charge, which states that the total electric charge in an isolated system is conserved.
- The continuity equation is an important concept in electromagnetism and is used to analyze the behavior of electric currents in various situations.
- It is also used in the study of electromagnetic waves and their interaction with matter.
