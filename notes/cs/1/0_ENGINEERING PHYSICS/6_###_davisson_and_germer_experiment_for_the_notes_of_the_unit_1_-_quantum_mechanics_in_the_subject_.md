### Davisson and Germer Experiment for the notes of the Unit 1 - Quantum Mechanics in the subject of ENGINEERING PHYSICS

Davisson-Germer Experiment:
1. Discovery of electron diffraction: Proved electrons have wave-like properties.
2. Evidence of wave-particle duality: Experiment showed electrons behaving as both particles and waves.
3. Confirmation of de Broglie hypothesis: Experiment supported de Broglie's idea that all matter has wave-like properties.
4. Importance in development of quantum mechanics: Experiment helped establish the foundations of quantum mechanics.
5. Conducted with a beam of electrons: Electrons were directed towards a nickel crystal, producing diffraction pattern.
