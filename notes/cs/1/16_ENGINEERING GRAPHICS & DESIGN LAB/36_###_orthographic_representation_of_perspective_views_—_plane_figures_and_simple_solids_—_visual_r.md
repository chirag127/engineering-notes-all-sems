### Orthographic representation of perspective views — Plane figures and simple solids — Visual Ray Method for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Orthographic representation of perspective views is a method of representing 3D objects on a 2D plane. In this method, multiple views of an object are drawn from different angles to give a complete representation of the object.

Plane figures are 2D shapes like circles, rectangles, and triangles. Simple solids are 3D objects like cubes, cylinders, and spheres.

The Visual Ray Method is a method of creating an isometric projection of an object. In this method, rays are drawn from the vertices of the object to the isometric plane. The intersection of these rays with the plane is the isometric projection of the object.
