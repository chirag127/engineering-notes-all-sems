### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Pyramid:
- 3D geometric shape with a polygon base and triangular sides that meet at a common point (apex)
- Regular pyramids have congruent triangular sides and a regular polygon base
- Volume formula: (1/3)Bh where B is the area of the base and h is the height from the base to the apex
- Right pyramid has its apex directly above the center of the base
- Oblique pyramid has its apex not directly above the center of the base.

Development of Surfaces:
- The process of creating a 2D representation of a 3D object by projecting its outlines onto a plane.
- Used in engineering graphics to represent 3D objects for manufacturing and construction purposes.
- Orthographic projection is a type of development of surfaces where multiple views of an object are created from different angles to represent its full form.
