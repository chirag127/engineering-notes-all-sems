### Projection of Lines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Projection of Lines:

- A projection is a representation of an object on a flat surface.
- A line projection is the representation of a line in space on a drawing plane.
- There are two types of line projections: true length and dimension lines.
- True length projections show the actual length of the line in space.
- Dimension lines are used to indicate the size or distance between lines on a drawing.
- The length of dimension lines is not necessarily the same as the true length of the line in space.
- The location of the line on the drawing plane is determined by the angle of the line in space and the position of the observer.

Orthographic Projections:

- Orthographic projection is a method of representing a 3D object on a 2D surface.
- In orthographic projection, the object is viewed from multiple directions, and each view is drawn on a separate plane.
- The six standard views in orthographic projection are front, top, right, bottom, left, and back.
- Orthographic projections are used in engineering and technical drawings to accurately represent the size, shape, and location of objects in space.
- Orthographic projections are also used to create animations, simulations, and virtual reality environments.
