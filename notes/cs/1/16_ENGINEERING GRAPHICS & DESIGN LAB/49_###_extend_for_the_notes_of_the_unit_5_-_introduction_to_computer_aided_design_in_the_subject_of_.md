### Extend for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Extend:
- A CAD command used to extend an object to meet another object or a specified boundary.
- Used to create a continuous line or surface between two separate objects.
- Can extend lines, arcs, and curves to meet at a common endpoint.
- Helps in creating accurate and precise drawings, by extending objects to meet specific requirements.
- Used in combination with other CAD commands, such as trim and fillet, to create complex shapes and designs.
