### Cylinder for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

A cylinder is a three-dimensional geometric shape with two parallel bases that are congruent circles. It has a curved lateral surface and a height perpendicular to the bases. It is one of the five regular solids and has applications in engineering and design, such as in the design of engines, storage containers, and pressure vessels. The volume of a cylinder can be calculated using the formula V = πr^2h, where r is the radius of the base and h is the height. The lateral surface area can be calculated using the formula A = 2πrh, and the total surface area can be calculated using the formula A = 2πr^2 + 2πrh. Cylinders can be developed into a flat pattern using the method of direct development or by unfolding the lateral surface along a generatrix.
