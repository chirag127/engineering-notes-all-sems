### Principles of Engineering Graphics and their significance

- Engineering graphics is the graphical representation of engineering concepts, designs, and specifications using standard symbols, conventions, and rules.
- Engineering graphics is essential for communicating engineering ideas and information effectively and accurately among engineers, technicians, manufacturers, and customers.
- Engineering graphics can be classified into different types according to the purpose, field, and method of drawing, such as mechanical, electrical, civil, architectural, and computer-aided design (CAD) drawings.
- Engineering graphics follows certain principles and standards to ensure clarity, consistency, and correctness of the drawings, such as orthographic projection, dimensioning, tolerancing, scaling, and annotation.
- Engineering graphics is a legal instrument that defines the rights and obligations of the parties involved in the design, fabrication, and use of the engineering product or system.
- Engineering graphics is also a tool for analysis, synthesis, and optimization of engineering problems, such as descriptive geometry, surface development, and perspective drawing.
- Engineering graphics is evolving with the advancement of technology, such as model-based definition (MBD), which is a digital representation of the product that integrates geometry, annotations, and metadata.
