### Development of surfaces for various regular solids such as prism

- The development of surface of an object means the unrolling and unfolding of all surfaces of the object on a plane  .
- If the surface of a solid is laid out on a plain surface, the shape thus obtained is called the development of that solid  .
- In other words, the development of a solid is the shape of a plain sheet that by proper folding could be converted into the shape of the concerned solid  .
- The development of surfaces is useful for designing and making patterns for sheet metal work, paper craft, cardboard models, etc .
- A regular solid is a solid whose faces are congruent regular polygons and whose polyhedral angles are congruent.
- A prism is a regular solid whose bases are congruent polygons and whose lateral faces are parallelograms.
- To develop the surface of a prism, we need to know the shape and dimensions of the base and the height of the prism .
- The development of the surface of a prism consists of the following steps :
  - Draw the base of the prism on a plain sheet.
  - Draw the lateral edges of the prism perpendicular to the base and equal to the height of the prism.
  - Draw the lateral faces of the prism as parallelograms with the lateral edges as the sides and the base as the base.
  - Cut out the shape along the boundary of the development.
  - Fold the lateral faces along the lateral edges and join them to form the prism.

- The development of the surface of other regular solids such as cylinder, cone, pyramid, etc. follows similar steps, but with different shapes of the base and the lateral faces .
- The development of the surface of a solid can be verified by folding the plain sheet along the edges and checking if it matches the shape of the solid .
