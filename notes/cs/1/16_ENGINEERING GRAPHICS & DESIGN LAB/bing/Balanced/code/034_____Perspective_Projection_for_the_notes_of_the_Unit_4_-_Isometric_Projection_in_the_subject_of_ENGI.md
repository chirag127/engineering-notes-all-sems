### Perspective Projection

- Perspective projection is a type of projection that shows a three-dimensional object as it appears to the human eye from a certain point of view .
- Perspective projection is used by artists and designers to create realistic and aesthetic drawings of scenes and objects .
- Perspective projection is based on the principle that the lines of projection from the object to the center of projection (or the station point) are not parallel, but converge at a single point.
- Perspective projection has the advantage of showing the depth and relative size of the object, but the disadvantage of distorting the actual dimensions and angles of the object .
- Perspective projection is very rarely used in engineering drawings, because it is difficult and time-consuming to construct and measure accurately .
- Perspective projection can be classified into three types, depending on the position of the center of projection relative to the object: one-point, two-point, and three-point perspective.
- One-point perspective is when the center of projection is on the same plane as the object, and only one principal axis of the object is perpendicular to the plane of projection.
- Two-point perspective is when the center of projection is on the same plane as the object, and two principal axes of the object are oblique to the plane of projection.
- Three-point perspective is when the center of projection is not on the same plane as the object, and all three principal axes of the object are oblique to the plane of projection.
