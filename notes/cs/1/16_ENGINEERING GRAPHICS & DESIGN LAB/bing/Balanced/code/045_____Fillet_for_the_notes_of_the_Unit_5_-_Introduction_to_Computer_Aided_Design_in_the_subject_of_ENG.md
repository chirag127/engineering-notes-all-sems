# Fillet

- A fillet is a curved surface that connects two adjacent faces or edges of a solid object, such as a mechanical part or a 3D model.
- A fillet has a radius value that determines the size and shape of the curve.
- Fillets are used to smooth sharp corners, reduce stress concentrations, improve appearance, and facilitate manufacturing processes such as casting, molding, and machining.
- Fillets are commonly used geometrical features in technical drawings. In most of the technical drawings created, fillets are generally of the same size.
- To dimension a fillet, an arrow is drawn from the dimension line to the center of the arc of the fillet, and the radius value is written with the letter R, such as R10 .
- If there are multiple fillets of the same size on a drawing, they can be dimensioned with a single note, such as 4XR10, which means four fillets with a radius of 10 units.
- Fillets can be quickly designed onto parts using 3D solid modeling engineering CAD software by invoking the function and picking edges of interest.
- Fillets can also be created by sketching an arc and using the trim or extend commands to remove or add material.
