### Polyline

- A polyline is a continuous line that is composed of one or more connected straight line segments, which, together, make up a shape.
- A polyline is a single object that can be edited as a whole or by its individual segments.
- Polylines are used in geographic information systems (GIS) and computer-aided design (CAD) software to represent boundaries, roads, rivers, and other lines on maps.
- Polylines can be open, closed, or a combination of both. A closed polyline forms a polygon.
- Polylines can have different widths, colors, linetypes, and elevations for each segment.
- Polylines can also have arc segments, which are curved lines that are tangent to the adjacent segments.
- Polylines can be created by specifying the endpoints of each segment, or by using existing objects such as lines, arcs, circles, or ellipses.
- Polylines can be modified by using commands such as PEDIT, TRIM, EXTEND, OFFSET, FILLET, CHAMFER, SPLINE, and JOIN.
- Polylines can be converted to other objects such as regions, splines, or 3D polylines.
