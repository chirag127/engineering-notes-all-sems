### Cylinder
- A cylinder is a solid that has two circular bases of equal size and shape, and a curved surface that connects them.
- The axis of a cylinder is the line segment that joins the centers of the two bases.
- The height of a cylinder is the distance between the two bases.
- The radius of a cylinder is the distance from the axis to the edge of the base.
- The lateral surface of a cylinder is the curved surface that connects the two bases.
- The total surface area of a cylinder is the sum of the areas of the two bases and the lateral surface.
- The volume of a cylinder is the product of the area of the base and the height.

### Sections of Cylinder
- A section of a cylinder is the intersection of the cylinder with a plane.
- The shape of the section depends on the position and orientation of the plane.
- If the plane is parallel to the axis of the cylinder, the section is a circle.
- If the plane is perpendicular to the axis of the cylinder, the section is a rectangle.
- If the plane is inclined to the axis of the cylinder, the section is an ellipse.
- If the plane is oblique to the axis of the cylinder, the section is a parabola or a hyperbola.
