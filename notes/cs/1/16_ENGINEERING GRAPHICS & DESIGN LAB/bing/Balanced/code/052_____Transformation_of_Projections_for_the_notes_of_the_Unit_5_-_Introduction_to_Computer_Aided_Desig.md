### Transformation of Projections

- A projection is a method of representing a three-dimensional (3D) object on a two-dimensional (2D) plane, such as a sheet of paper or a computer screen.
- A projection is produced by connecting the points where the lines of sight from an observer to the object pierce the projection plane.
- As a result, the 3D object is transformed into a 2D view that shows some of its features, such as shape, size, and orientation.
- There are different types of projections, depending on the relative position and orientation of the observer, the object, and the projection plane.
- Some of the common types of projections are:
  - Parallel projection: A projection where the lines of sight are assumed to be parallel, and the projection plane is perpendicular to a principal axis of the object.
