### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex.
- The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, such as a square, a triangle, a hexagon, etc.
- The height of a pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the vertex to the base plane.
- The slant height of a pyramid is the length of the altitude of any triangular face.
- The lateral surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the areas of all the triangular faces, excluding the base.
- The total surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the lateral surface area and the base area.

- Sections of regular solids are the plane figures obtained by cutting the solids by a plane, called the section plane or the cutting plane.
- The true shape of the section is the shape of the plane figure that is obtained when the section plane is viewed perpendicularly.
- The true shape of the section can be determined by projecting the points of intersection of the section plane and the edges of the solid on a plane parallel to the section plane.
- Sections of regular solids are useful for understanding the internal structure, dimensions, and volume of the solids.

- Development of surfaces of solids is the process of unfolding or unrolling the surfaces of the solids on a plane, called the development plane or the reference plane.
- The development of surfaces of solids is useful for making patterns or templates for cutting and bending materials, such as sheet metal, paper, cardboard, etc.
- The development of surfaces of solids can be done by various methods, such as parallel line method, radial line method, triangulation method, etc.
- The development of surfaces of solids should be done without any distortion or overlapping of the surfaces, and should show the true dimensions and shapes of the surfaces.
