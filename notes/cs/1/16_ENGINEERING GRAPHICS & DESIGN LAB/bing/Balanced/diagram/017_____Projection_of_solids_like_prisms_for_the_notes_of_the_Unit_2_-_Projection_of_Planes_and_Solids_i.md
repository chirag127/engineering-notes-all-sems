### Projection of solids like prisms

- A solid is a three-dimensional object that has length, breadth and height. A prism is a solid that has two congruent and parallel faces called bases and the other faces are parallelograms called lateral faces.
- A projection of a solid is a two-dimensional representation of the solid on a plane, such as a horizontal plane (HP) or a vertical plane (VP). The projection shows the shape and size of the solid as seen from a particular direction.
- There are two types of projection of solids: orthographic projection and isometric projection. Orthographic projection shows the true shape and size of the solid on the plane of projection, but not the depth. Isometric projection shows the three-dimensional appearance of the solid, but not the true shape and size.
