### Offset for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- An offset is the number of address locations added to a base address in order to get to a specific absolute address.
- An offset can also refer to a parallel curve or surface that is a constant distance away from another curve or surface   .
- Offset curves and surfaces are useful in computer aided design and machining applications, such as NC machining, pattern design, contouring, etc  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can be constructed by various methods, such as using normal vectors, cubic splines, rational B-splines, etc  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can have different properties and complexities depending on the shape and degree of the original curve or surface  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can be modified by changing the layer, distance, direction, or source of the offset.
