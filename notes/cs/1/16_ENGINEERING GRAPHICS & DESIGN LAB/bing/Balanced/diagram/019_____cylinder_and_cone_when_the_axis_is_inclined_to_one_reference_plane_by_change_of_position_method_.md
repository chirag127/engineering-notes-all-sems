- A cylinder and a cone are solids of revolution, which are generated by rotating a plane surface about one of its sides.
- The axis of a cylinder or a cone is the line about which the plane surface is rotated.
- The projection of a cylinder or a cone is the view of the solid as seen from a particular direction.
- When the axis of a cylinder or a cone is inclined to one reference plane (either HP or VP) and parallel to the other, the projection of the solid will show the true shape of the base and the top of the solid .
- The change of position method is a technique to draw the projection of a cylinder or a cone when the axis is inclined to one reference plane by rotating the solid about an axis perpendicular to the reference plane until the axis becomes parallel to the reference plane .
- The steps involved in the change of position method are :
  - Draw the projection of the solid when the axis is parallel to the reference plane (either HP or VP) and perpendicular to the other.
  - Mark the center of the base and the top of the solid and join them to get the axis.
  - Draw a circle with the center as the center of the base and the radius as the length of the axis. This is the auxiliary circle.
  - Mark the angle of inclination of the axis with the reference plane on the auxiliary circle and draw a line from the center to the point of intersection. This is the inclined axis.
  - Draw the projection of the base and the top of the solid by projecting the points on the original projection to the inclined axis.
  - Erase the original projection and the auxiliary circle.
