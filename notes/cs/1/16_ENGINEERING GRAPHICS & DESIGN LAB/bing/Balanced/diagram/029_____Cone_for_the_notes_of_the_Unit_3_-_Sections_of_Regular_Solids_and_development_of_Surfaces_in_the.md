### Cone

A cone is a solid generated by revolving a right-angled triangle about one of its sides, which is called the axis of the cone. The base of the cone is a circle whose center lies on the axis of the cone. The vertex of the cone is the point where the axis intersects the surface of the cone.

#### Sections of a cone

When a cone is cut by a plane, the intersection of the plane and the surface of the cone is called a conic section. There are four types of conic sections: circle, ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola. The type of conic section depends on the angle between the plane and the axis of the cone.

- If the plane is perpendicular to the axis of the cone, the conic section is a circle.
- If the plane is inclined to the axis of the cone, but not parallel to any generator of the cone, the conic section is an ellipse.
- If the plane is parallel to a generator of the cone, the conic section is a parabola.
- If the plane is inclined to the axis of the cone, and intersects both the upper and lower nappes of the cone, the conic section is a hyperbola.

#### Development of a cone

The development of a cone is the process of unfolding the surface of the cone onto a plane. The development of a cone is a sector of a circle whose radius is equal to the slant height of the cone, and whose angle is equal to the angle subtended by the base of the cone at the vertex.

To draw the development of a cone, follow these steps:

- Draw the front view and the top view of the cone, showing the axis, the base, and the slant height.
- Draw a horizontal line and mark a point O on it. This will be the center of the development circle.
- From O, draw a line perpendicular to the horizontal line and mark a point A on it. This will be the vertex of the development sector.
- From A, draw an arc with radius equal to the slant height of the cone, and mark a point B on it. This will be the end point of the development sector.
- From B, draw a line parallel to the horizontal line and mark a point C on it. This will be the other end point of the development sector.
- Measure the angle subtended by the base of the cone at the vertex in the top view, and mark the same angle at O in the development circle. This will be the angle of the development sector.
- Join A, B, and C to form the development sector. This is the development of the cone.
