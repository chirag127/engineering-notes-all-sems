### Principles of Engineering Graphics and their significance

- Engineering graphics is the graphical representation of engineering concepts, designs, and specifications using standardized symbols, conventions, and rules.
- Engineering graphics is essential for communicating engineering ideas and information effectively and accurately among engineers, technicians, manufacturers, and customers.
- Engineering graphics can be classified into different types according to the purpose, field, and method of drawing, such as mechanical, electrical, civil, architectural, and computer-aided design (CAD) drawings.
- Engineering graphics follows certain principles and standards to ensure clarity, consistency, and correctness of the drawings, such as orthographic projection, dimensioning, tolerancing, scaling, and annotation.
- Engineering graphics can be created and modified using various media and tools, such as paper, pencil, ruler, compass, drafting board, computer, software, printer, and plotter.
- Engineering graphics can be integrated with model-based definition (MBD) or digital product definition (DPD), which is the practice of using 3D models with embedded data to define and document engineering products.
- Engineering graphics can help engineers to visualize, analyze, design, test, and optimize engineering problems and solutions, as well as to communicate and collaborate with other stakeholders.
