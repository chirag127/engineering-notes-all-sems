### Polyline

- A polyline is a continuous line that is composed of one or more connected straight line segments, which, together, make up a shape .
- A polyline can be open, closed, or a combination of both .
- A polyline can have straight line segments, arc segments, or a combination of the two.
- A polyline is created as a single object, which means it can be edited, moved, copied, or deleted as a whole.
- Some reasons to use polylines include:
  - To create complex shapes that are easier to modify and manipulate than individual line segments.
  - To apply properties such as width, color, linetype, or elevation to the entire polyline.
  - To create boundaries for hatching, regions, or extrusions.
  - To represent geographic features such as roads, rivers, or contours on maps.
- Some examples of polylines are:
  - A rectangle, which is a closed polyline with four straight line segments.
  - A circle, which is a closed polyline with one arc segment.
  - A hexagon, which is a closed polyline with six straight line segments.
  - A spiral, which is an open polyline with multiple arc segments.
