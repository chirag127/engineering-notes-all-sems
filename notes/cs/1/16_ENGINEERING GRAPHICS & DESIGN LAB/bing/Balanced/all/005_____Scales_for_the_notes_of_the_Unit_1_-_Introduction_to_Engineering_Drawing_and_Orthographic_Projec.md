# Scales for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

- Scales are used to measure and represent the actual dimensions of an object or a part of it on a drawing.
- Scales can be classified into three types: plain scales, diagonal scales, and vernier scales.
- Plain scales are used to measure and show two units of length (one main unit and one sub-unit) on a drawing. For example, a plain scale can show meters and centimeters, or feet and inches.
- Diagonal scales are used to measure and show three units of length (one main unit and two sub-units) on a drawing. For example, a diagonal scale can show meters, decimeters, and centimeters, or feet, inches, and fractions of an inch.
- Vernier scales are used to measure and show very small sub-divisions of a unit on a drawing. For example, a vernier scale can show millimeters, or fractions of a millimeter, on a drawing.
- Scales can also be classified into two categories: engineering scales and architectural scales.
- Engineering scales are used to represent objects in their true size or in a fixed ratio of reduction or enlargement. For example, a 1:1 scale means that one unit on the drawing is equal to one unit in reality, and a 1:2 scale means that one unit on the drawing is equal to two units in reality.
- Architectural scales are used to represent the ratio of a drawing to the actual size of the object. For example, a 1/4" = 1' scale means that one quarter of an inch on the drawing is equal to one foot in reality, and a 1/8" = 1' scale means that one eighth of an inch on the drawing is equal to one foot in reality.
- To convert between different scales, you can use the following formula:

  - New scale / Old scale = New measurement / Old measurement

  - For example, to convert from a 1/4" = 1' scale to a 1/8" = 1' scale, you can use the formula:

    - 1/8" / 1/4" = New measurement / Old measurement

    - Simplifying the fractions, you get:

      - 1/2 = New measurement / Old measurement

    - Cross-multiplying, you get:

      - New measurement = Old measurement / 2

    - This means that you have to divide the old measurement by 2 to get the new measurement on the 1/8" = 1' scale.
