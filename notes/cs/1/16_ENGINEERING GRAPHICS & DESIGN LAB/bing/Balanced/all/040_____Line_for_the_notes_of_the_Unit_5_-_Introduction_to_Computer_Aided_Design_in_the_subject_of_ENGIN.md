# Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design

- Computer Aided Design (CAD) is the use of computer software to design and document a product's design process.
- CAD software can create, modify, analyze, and optimize a design using graphical symbols such as points, lines, curves, planes, and shapes.
- CAD software can also generate 2D or 3D models, drawings, and animations of the design.
- CAD software can increase the productivity, quality, and communication of the designer, and create a database for manufacturing .
- CAD software can be used by engineers, architects, and construction managers for various applications such as mechanical, electrical, civil, architectural, and industrial design .
- Some of the best CAD software available are AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, Creo, Inventor, SketchUp, and Revit.
