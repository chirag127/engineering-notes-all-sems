# Polyline

- A polyline is a continuous line that is composed of one or more connected straight line segments, which, together, make up a shape.
- A polyline is a single object that can be edited as a whole or by its individual segments.
- A polyline can have different properties, such as width, color, linetype, and elevation.
- A polyline can be open, closed, or a combination of both. A closed polyline forms a polygon.
- A polyline can also have arc segments, which are curved lines that connect two points. Arc segments can have different properties, such as radius, angle, direction, and bulge.
- Polylines are used in geographic information systems (GIS) and computer-aided design (CAD) software to represent boundaries, roads, rivers, and other lines on maps.
- Polylines are useful for applying non-continuous linetypes across vertices, creating complex shapes, and reducing the number of objects in a drawing.
