# Geometry and topology of engineered components

- Geometry is the branch of mathematics that studies the properties, measurements, and relationships of points, lines, angles, surfaces, and solids in space.
- Topology is the branch of mathematics that studies the properties of shapes that are preserved under continuous deformations, such as stretching, twisting, bending, and shrinking, but not tearing or gluing.
- Geometry and topology are closely related, as many geometric concepts can be generalized and abstracted to topological ones, and many topological problems can be approached using geometric methods and tools.
- In engineering, geometry and topology are important for designing, modeling, analyzing, and optimizing the shape, structure, and function of components, systems, and materials.
- Some of the applications of geometry and topology in engineering are:

  - Computer-aided design (CAD): CAD is the use of computer software to create, modify, analyze, and optimize the design of engineering products and processes. CAD software often uses geometric and topological representations of the objects, such as curves, surfaces, solids, meshes, and parametric models, to manipulate and visualize them in various ways.
  - Finite element analysis (FEA): FEA is a numerical method for solving problems involving the behavior of physical systems under external forces, such as stress, strain, heat, fluid flow, and electromagnetism. FEA software often uses geometric and topological discretizations of the domain, such as elements, nodes, edges, and faces, to approximate the governing equations and boundary conditions.
  - Computational geometry: Computational geometry is the study of algorithms and data structures for solving geometric problems, such as intersection, convex hull, Voronoi diagram, Delaunay triangulation, mesh generation, and geometric optimization. Computational geometry is useful for many engineering applications, such as computer graphics, computer vision, robotics, geographic information systems, and computer-aided manufacturing.
  - Computational topology: Computational topology is the study of algorithms and data structures for solving topological problems, such as homology, cohomology, persistent homology, Euler characteristic, Betti numbers, and topological data analysis. Computational topology is useful for many engineering applications, such as shape analysis, feature extraction, pattern recognition, data visualization, and machine learning.
  - Topological optimization: Topological optimization is a method for finding the optimal shape and topology of a structure or a material that satisfies certain performance criteria, such as stiffness, strength, weight, or functionality. Topological optimization often uses geometric and topological variables, such as density, level set, phase field, or homogenization, to represent and modify the design domain.
