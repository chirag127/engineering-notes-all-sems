# Offset for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Offset is a term used in computer engineering and low-level programming to denote the number of address locations added to a base address in order to get to a specific absolute address.
- Offset is also a term used in computer aided design (CAD) and machining (CAM) to denote the construction of parallel curves and surfaces that are at a constant distance from a given curve or surface .
- Offset curves and surfaces are useful for various applications, such as NC machining, pattern design, collision detection, and geometric modeling  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can be generated by different methods, such as approximation, interpolation, subdivision, and implicitization  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can be represented by different forms, such as polynomials, rational functions, splines, and NURBS  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can have different properties, such as continuity, smoothness, self-intersection, and curvature  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can be manipulated by different operations, such as trimming, blending, filleting, and Boolean operations  .
- Offset curves and surfaces can be displayed by different techniques, such as wireframe, shaded, and contour plots  .
