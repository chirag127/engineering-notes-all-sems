# Sections of Solids

- A section of a solid is the shape obtained by cutting the solid with a plane, called the section plane or cutting plane, and removing the part of the solid on one side of the plane.
- Sections of solids are used to show the hidden or internal features of an object in technical drawings.
- Sections of solids can be classified into two types: full section and half section.
- A full section is obtained when the section plane passes through the entire object and divides it into two equal parts.
- A half section is obtained when the section plane passes through half of the object and removes one quarter of the object.
- The shape of the section depends on the orientation of the section plane and the type of the solid.
- Some common types of solids are prisms, pyramids, cylinders and cones.
- Prisms and cylinders have parallel bases and their sections are parallel to the bases.
- Pyramids and cones have a common vertex and their sections are similar to the bases.
- The true shape of the section is the shape of the section as seen perpendicular to the section plane.
- The true shape of the section can be obtained by projecting the section on a plane parallel to the section plane.
- The true shape of the section can be used to locate the position of the section plane if the section plane is unknown.
