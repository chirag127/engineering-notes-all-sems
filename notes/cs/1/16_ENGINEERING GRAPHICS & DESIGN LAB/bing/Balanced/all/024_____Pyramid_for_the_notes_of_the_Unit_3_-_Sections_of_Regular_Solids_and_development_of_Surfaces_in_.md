# Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex.
- The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, and the number of faces is equal to the number of sides of the base.
- The axis of a pyramid is the perpendicular line joining the vertex and the center of the base.
- The slant height of a pyramid is the distance from the vertex to the midpoint of any edge of the base.
- The height of a pyramid is the distance from the vertex to the base plane.
- The lateral surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the areas of the triangular faces, and the total surface area is the sum of the lateral surface area and the base area.
- The volume of a pyramid is one-third of the product of the base area and the height.

## Sections of Pyramids

- A section of a pyramid is the intersection of the pyramid with a cutting plane.
- The cutting plane can be parallel, perpendicular, or inclined to the base or the axis of the pyramid.
- The shape of the section depends on the position and orientation of the cutting plane.
- The true shape of the section is the shape obtained when the cutting plane is viewed perpendicularly.
- To draw the true shape of the section, the following steps are followed:

  - Draw the front and top views of the pyramid in the first angle projection, showing the cutting plane in both views.
  - Mark the points of intersection of the cutting plane and the edges of the pyramid in both views, and label them with letters.
  - Draw a line parallel to the cutting plane and project the points of intersection from the front view to the line.
  - Draw perpendiculars from the projected points to the cutting plane and measure the distances of the points from the base plane in the top view.
  - Transfer the distances to the perpendiculars and mark the points of intersection with the cutting plane.
  - Join the points of intersection in order to obtain the true shape of the section.

## Development of Pyramids

- The development of a pyramid is the unfolding of the lateral surface of the pyramid on a plane.
- The development of a pyramid shows the true shapes and sizes of the triangular faces of the pyramid.
- To draw the development of a pyramid, the following steps are followed:

  - Draw the front and top views of the pyramid in the first angle projection, showing the base and the axis of the pyramid in both views.
  - Mark the vertices of the base and the vertex of the pyramid in both views, and label them with letters.
  - Draw a horizontal line and project the vertices of the base from the front view to the line.
  - Draw arcs with the vertex of the pyramid as the center and the slant heights of the pyramid as the radii, cutting the horizontal line at the projected points.
  - Mark the points of intersection of the arcs and the horizontal line, and label them with the same letters as the vertices of the base.
  - Join the points of intersection with straight lines to form the development of the lateral surface of the pyramid.
  - Draw the development of the base inside the development of the lateral surface, using the same dimensions as the base in the front view.
