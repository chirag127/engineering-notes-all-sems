# Engineering Graphics and Design Lab

Engineering graphics and design lab is a course that introduces the students to the principles and techniques of engineering drawing, sketching, and computer-aided design (CAD). The course aims to develop the students' graphic skills for communication of concepts, ideas, and design of engineering products. The course also exposes the students to the existing national and international standards related to technical drawings.

The course consists of lectures and laboratory sessions, where the students learn and practice the following topics:

- Engineering design process and problem-solving methods
- Spatial visualization and orthographic projection
- Pictorial sketching and freehand drawing
- Dimensioning and tolerancing
- Geometric construction and descriptive geometry
- Sectional views and auxiliary views
- Multiview drawings and working drawings
- Introduction to CAD and solid modeling
- Assembly modeling and exploded views
- Surface modeling and rendering
- Engineering analysis and simulation
- Engineering graphics applications in various disciplines

The course requires the students to use a 3D solid modeling software package, such as SolidWorks, AutoCAD, or Inventor, to create and modify engineering models and drawings. The students are also expected to use a 2D drafting software, such as DraftSight, to create and edit 2D drawings. The students are evaluated based on their performance in quizzes, assignments, projects, and exams.

The course outcomes are:

- The students will be able to apply the engineering design process and problem-solving methods to engineering graphics and design problems.
- The students will be able to communicate engineering concepts, ideas, and design using appropriate graphical techniques and standards.
- The students will be able to create and interpret engineering drawings and sketches using orthographic projection, pictorial sketching, and freehand drawing.
- The students will be able to apply dimensioning and tolerancing principles and practices to engineering drawings and models.
- The students will be able to use geometric construction and descriptive geometry methods to solve engineering graphics and design problems.
- The students will be able to create and interpret sectional views, auxiliary views, multiview drawings, and working drawings of engineering objects and systems.
- The students will be able to use CAD and solid modeling software to create and modify engineering models and drawings.
- The students will be able to create and interpret assembly models and exploded views of engineering systems and components.
- The students will be able to use surface modeling and rendering techniques to create and present realistic and aesthetic engineering models and drawings.
- The students will be able to use engineering analysis and simulation tools to verify and optimize engineering models and designs.
- The students will be able to demonstrate the applications of engineering graphics and design in various engineering disciplines and fields.
