### Cylinder

- A cylinder is a three-dimensional solid that has two circular bases and a curved surface that connects them.
- The axis of a cylinder is the line segment that joins the centers of the bases and is perpendicular to them.
- The height of a cylinder is the distance between the bases along the axis.
- The radius of a cylinder is the distance from the axis to the edge of the base.
- A cylinder can be classified as a right cylinder or an oblique cylinder depending on the angle between the axis and the base.
- A right cylinder has its axis perpendicular to the base, while an oblique cylinder has its axis inclined to the base.
- A cylinder can also be classified as a circular cylinder or an elliptical cylinder depending on the shape of the base.
- A circular cylinder has a circular base, while an elliptical cylinder has an elliptical base.
- A special case of a circular cylinder is a cone, which has one of the bases reduced to a point.
- A cone can be a right cone or an oblique cone depending on the angle between the axis and the base.
- A special case of a cone is a sphere, which has both bases reduced to a point and the height equal to the diameter.
