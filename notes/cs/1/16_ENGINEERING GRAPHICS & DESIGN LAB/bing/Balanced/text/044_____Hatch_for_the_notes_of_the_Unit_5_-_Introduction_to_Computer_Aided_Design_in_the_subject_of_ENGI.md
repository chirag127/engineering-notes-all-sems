### Hatch for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Hatch is a command in AutoCAD that is used to fill an area of selected objects with a hatch pattern or fill.
- Hatch patterns or fills represent materials to be used and incorporated within a design, such as concrete, brick, wood, etc.
- Hatch can also be used to designate areas or room types, such as office, kitchen, bathroom, etc.
- Hatch command can be accessed from the Draw menu of the Home tab in the ribbon.
- To use the hatch command, follow these steps:
  - Select the Hatch command from the Draw menu or type HATCH in the command line and press Enter.
  - Choose a hatch pattern or fill from the Pattern panel or the Hatch Creation tab. You can also create your own custom hatch patterns or fills.
  - Specify the hatch boundary by selecting objects or picking points. You can also use the Select option to select existing objects as hatch boundaries.
  - Adjust the hatch properties, such as angle, scale, origin, transparency, etc, from the Properties panel or the Hatch Creation tab.
  - Press Enter to apply the hatch or fill to the selected area.
- You can modify the hatch or fill by using the Hatch Edit command or by double-clicking on the hatch object.
- You can also associate the hatch or fill to the boundary objects, so that the hatch or fill updates automatically when the boundary objects change.
- To associate the hatch or fill to the boundary objects, follow these steps:
  - Select the hatch or fill object and right-click to open the shortcut menu.
  - Choose Hatch Edit from the menu or type HATCHEDIT in the command line and press Enter.
  - In the Hatch Edit dialog box, check the box for Associative and click OK.
  - The hatch or fill will now be linked to the boundary objects and will update accordingly when the boundary objects are modified.
- Hatch command is a useful tool for creating and editing hatch patterns or fills in computer aided design. It helps to convey the material and spatial information of a design in a clear and consistent way. Hatch command also follows the computer aided design standards set by the GSA, which require the use of the United States National CAD Standard® (NCS) for hatch patterns or fills.
