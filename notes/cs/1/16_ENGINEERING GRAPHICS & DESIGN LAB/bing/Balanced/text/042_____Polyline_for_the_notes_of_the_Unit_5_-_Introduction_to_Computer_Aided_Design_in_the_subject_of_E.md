### Polyline

- A polyline is a continuous line that is composed of one or more connected straight line segments, which, together, make up a shape.
- A polyline is a single object that can be edited as a whole or by its individual segments.
- Polylines are used in geographic information systems (GIS) and computer-aided design (CAD) software to represent boundaries, roads, rivers, and other lines on maps.
- Polylines can be open, closed, or a combination of both. A closed polyline forms a polygon, such as a rectangle or a circle.
- Polylines can have different properties, such as width, color, layer, linetype, and elevation.
- Polylines can also have curved segments, such as arcs, splines, or elliptical arcs.
- Polylines can be created, modified, and deleted using various commands and tools in CAD software, such as AutoCAD.
- Polylines are useful for creating complex shapes, applying non-continuous linetypes, and applying hatches and fills.
