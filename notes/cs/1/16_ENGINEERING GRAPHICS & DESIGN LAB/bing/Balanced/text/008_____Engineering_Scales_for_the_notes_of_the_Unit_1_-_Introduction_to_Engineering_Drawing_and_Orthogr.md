### Engineering Scales

- Engineering scales are tools that are used to measure and represent the dimensions of objects in engineering drawings.
- Engineering scales can be used to create drawings that are larger or smaller than the actual objects, depending on the purpose and accuracy of the drawing.
- Engineering scales can also be used to measure distances and angles on existing drawings or plans.
- There are different types of engineering scales, each with its own advantages and disadvantages. Some of the common types are:

  - Plain scale: A plain scale is a line that is divided into equal parts, and the first part is further subdivided into smaller parts. A plain scale can be used to represent two units, such as meters and centimeters, or a unit and a fraction, such as inches and eighths of an inch .
  - Diagonal scale: A diagonal scale is a line that is divided into equal parts, and each part is further divided into smaller parts by diagonal lines. A diagonal scale can be used to represent three units, such as meters, decimeters and centimeters, or a unit and two fractions, such as inches, tenths of an inch and hundredths of an inch .
  - Comparative scale: A comparative scale is a line that is divided into two or more segments, each representing a different unit or ratio. A comparative scale can be used to compare the sizes of different objects or to convert between different units or scales .
  - Vernier scale: A vernier scale is a line that is divided into equal parts, and a movable scale that is slightly shorter than the fixed scale is placed over it. A vernier scale can be used to measure fractions of a part more accurately than a plain scale, by aligning the marks on the two scales .
  - Scale of chords: A scale of chords is a line that is divided into equal parts, and each part is marked with the length of the chord of a circle with a given radius. A scale of chords can be used to measure angles on a drawing, by placing the scale over the vertex of the angle and aligning the chord with the sides of the angle .

- To use an engineering scale, one must follow these steps:

  - Select the appropriate type and size of the scale for the drawing or measurement.
  - Align the zero mark of the scale with the starting point of the dimension or distance to be measured or drawn.
  - Read or mark the end point of the dimension or distance on the scale, using the subdivisions and units as needed.
  - Repeat the process for other dimensions or distances as required.
