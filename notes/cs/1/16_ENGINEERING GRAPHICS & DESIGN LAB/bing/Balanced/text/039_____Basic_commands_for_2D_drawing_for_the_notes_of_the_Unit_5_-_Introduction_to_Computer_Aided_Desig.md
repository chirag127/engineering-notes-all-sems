### Basic commands for 2D drawing

- To create a 2D drawing, you need to use a software application that supports 2D drawing, such as AutoCAD, SketchUp, or Inkscape.
- A 2D drawing consists of geometric shapes, such as lines, circles, arcs, polygons, and curves, that are defined by their coordinates, dimensions, and properties.
- To draw a shape, you need to specify its starting point, end point, radius, angle, or other parameters, depending on the type of shape.
- Some basic commands for 2D drawing are:

  - **LINE**: Draws a straight line segment between two points. You can specify the points by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates.
  - **CIRCLE**: Draws a circle with a given center point and radius. You can specify the center point by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates, and the radius by dragging the cursor or entering a value.
  - **ARC**: Draws an arc of a circle with a given center point, radius, and angle. You can specify the center point by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates, the radius by dragging the cursor or entering a value, and the angle by specifying the start and end points of the arc or entering the values.
  - **POLYGON**: Draws a closed polygon with a given number of sides and a specified center point. You can specify the number of sides by entering a value, and the center point by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates. The polygon is drawn with one side horizontal and the other sides equally spaced around the center point.
  - **RECTANGLE**: Draws a rectangle with two opposite corners specified. You can specify the corners by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates.
  - **ELLIPSE**: Draws an ellipse with a given center point, major axis, and minor axis. You can specify the center point by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates, the major axis by dragging the cursor or entering a value, and the minor axis by specifying the ratio of the minor axis to the major axis or entering a value.
  - **SPLINE**: Draws a smooth curve that passes through a series of points. You can specify the points by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates. The curve is interpolated between the points using a mathematical function.

- To modify a shape, you can use commands such as:

  - **MOVE**: Moves a shape from one location to another. You can select the shape by clicking on it or using a selection window, and specify the new location by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates.
  - **ROTATE**: Rotates a shape around a given point by a specified angle. You can select the shape by clicking on it or using a selection window, specify the point by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates, and specify the angle by dragging the cursor or entering a value.
  - **SCALE**: Changes the size of a shape by a given factor. You can select the shape by clicking on it or using a selection window, specify the factor by dragging the cursor or entering a value, and specify the base point by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates.
  - **TRIM**: Cuts off a part of a shape that extends beyond another shape. You can select the shape to be trimmed by clicking on it or using a selection window, and select the cutting shape by clicking on it or using a selection window.
  - **EXTEND**: Extends a shape to meet another shape. You can select the shape to be extended by clicking on it or using a selection window, and select the boundary shape by clicking on it or using a selection window.
  - **FILLET**: Rounds off the corner of two intersecting lines or curves by a given radius. You can select the lines or curves by clicking on them or using a selection window, and specify the radius by dragging the cursor or entering a value.
