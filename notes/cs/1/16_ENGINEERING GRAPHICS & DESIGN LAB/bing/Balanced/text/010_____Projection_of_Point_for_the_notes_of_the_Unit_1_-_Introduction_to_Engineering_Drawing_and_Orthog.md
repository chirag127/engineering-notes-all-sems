### Projection of Point

- Projection in engineering drawing is a technique for representing a three-dimensional object on a two-dimensional drawing.
- Projection involves projecting the object onto a plane and drawing a two-dimensional representation of it.
- A geometrically represented image (visual image or figure) of an object obtained on a surface or plane is referred to as a projection in an engineering drawing.
- A point, line, plane, solid, machine component, or building may be the object.
- Projections in technical drawings are created based on the observer or reader of the technical drawing.
- If the distance from the observer to the object is infinite, then the projection lines are assumed to be parallel, and the projection is called a parallel projection.
- Parallel projections can be classified into orthographic projections and oblique projections.
- Orthographic projections are parallel projections where the projection plane is perpendicular to the projection lines.
- Oblique projections are parallel projections where the projection plane is not perpendicular to the projection lines.
- Orthographic projections are commonly used in engineering drawings because they show the true shape and size of the object.
- Oblique projections are used to show the three-dimensional appearance of the object, but they distort the shape and size of the object.
- Orthographic projections can be further divided into first-angle projection and third-angle projection, depending on the relative position of the object and the projection planes.
- First-angle projection is used in Europe and Asia, where the object is placed in the first quadrant and the projection planes are unfolded to the right and below the object.
- Third-angle projection is used in North America and Australia, where the object is placed in the third quadrant and the projection planes are unfolded to the left and above the object.
- The projection of a point is the point where the projection line from the point meets the projection plane.
- The projection of a point can be located by using the coordinates of the point and the projection plane.
- The projection of a point can be shown in different views, such as front view, top view, and side view, depending on the orientation of the projection plane.
- The projection of a point can be used to find the distance of the point from the projection plane, the angle of the point with the projection plane, and the true position of the point in space.
