### Offset for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Offset is a technique of creating a parallel or concentric copy of an object or a curve in a computer-aided design (CAD) software.
- Offset can be used for various purposes, such as creating gaps, clearances, borders, outlines, patterns, etc.
- Offset can be applied to different types of objects, such as lines, circles, arcs, splines, polylines, etc.
- Offset can be performed by different methods, such as specifying a distance, selecting a point, or using a reference object.
- Offset can be modified by changing the direction, distance, or layer of the offset object.
- Offset can be controlled by various settings, such as offset gap type, offset join type, offset current layer, etc.
