### Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Computer Aided Design (CAD) is the use of computer software to create, modify, analyze, and optimize a design. CAD software can help engineers, architects, and other professionals to produce high-quality drawings, models, and simulations of their projects. 
- CAD software can also provide information on the materials, dimensions, tolerances, and manufacturing processes of a design, as well as perform various calculations and tests to ensure its functionality and feasibility. 
- CAD software can be classified into two types: 2D and 3D. 2D CAD software allows the user to create and edit drawings using lines, curves, shapes, and text. 3D CAD software allows the user to create and manipulate solid models that can be viewed from different angles and perspectives. 
- CAD software can be used for various applications in different fields of engineering, such as civil, mechanical, electrical, and aerospace engineering. Some examples of CAD software are AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, and SketchUp.  
- Engineering Graphics and Design Lab is a course that introduces the students to the fundamentals of engineering graphics and CAD using a 3D solid modeling software package. The course covers topics such as geometric construction, sketching, orthographic projection, isometric, sectional and detailed views, geometric dimensioning and tolerancing, engineering drawings and assemblies. 
- The course aims to develop the students' skills in visualizing, communicating, and documenting their design ideas using the language of engineering graphics. The course also exposes the students to the design process, ideation, and problem-solving techniques using CAD software.  
- The course requires the students to complete various assignments, quizzes, and projects using the CAD software. The students are expected to learn the basic functions and features of the CAD software, as well as follow the standards and conventions of engineering graphics. The students are also expected to apply their creativity and critical thinking skills to design and model simple engineering components and systems.
