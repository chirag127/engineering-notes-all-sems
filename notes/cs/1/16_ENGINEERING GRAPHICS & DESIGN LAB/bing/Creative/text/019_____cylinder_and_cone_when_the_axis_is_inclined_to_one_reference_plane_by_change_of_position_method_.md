### Cylinder and cone when the axis is inclined to one reference plane by change of position method

- A cylinder or a cone is said to be inclined to one reference plane when its axis is inclined to that plane and parallel to the other plane.
- To draw the projections of an inclined cylinder or cone, the change of position method can be used. This method involves the following steps:
  - Draw the projections of the cylinder or cone in its simple position, i.e., when its axis is perpendicular to one reference plane and parallel to the other plane.
  - Mark the points on the base circle or ellipse of the cylinder or cone in the simple position.
  - Rotate the cylinder or cone about its axis until it is inclined to the reference plane by the given angle. This will change the positions of the points on the base circle or ellipse.
  - Draw the new positions of the points on the base circle or ellipse by projecting them from the simple position.
  - Join the new positions of the points to form the base circle or ellipse of the cylinder or cone in the inclined position.
  - Draw the projections of the cylinder or cone in the inclined position by projecting the base circle or ellipse and the axis from the simple position.
  - Erase the projections of the cylinder or cone in the simple position.
