### Scales

- A scale is a ratio that compares the dimensions of a drawing to the actual dimensions of the object.
- Scales are used to reduce or enlarge the size of an object while maintaining its proportions and accuracy.
- Scales are expressed as fractions or ratios, such as 1:10, 1/10, or 10%.
- Scales can be classified into three types: plain scales, diagonal scales, and vernier scales.

#### Plain Scales

- A plain scale is a scale that shows the measurements of one unit or dimension, such as length, width, or height.
- A plain scale is drawn as a straight line divided into equal parts, each representing a certain value of the unit.
- A plain scale can be used to measure or convert the dimensions of an object in the same unit or in different units.
- For example, a plain scale of 1:10 can be used to measure or convert the dimensions of an object in centimeters or millimeters.

#### Diagonal Scales

- A diagonal scale is a scale that shows the measurements of two units or dimensions, such as length and breadth, or area.
- A diagonal scale is drawn as a rectangle divided into a number of equal parts, each representing a certain value of the first unit. Then, diagonal lines are drawn from the corners of the rectangle to the opposite sides, dividing each part into sub-parts, each representing a certain value of the second unit.
- A diagonal scale can be used to measure or convert the dimensions or area of an object in the same units or in different units.
- For example, a diagonal scale of 1:10 can be used to measure or convert the dimensions or area of an object in square centimeters or square millimeters.

#### Vernier Scales

- A vernier scale is a scale that shows the measurements of a unit or dimension with a high degree of accuracy and precision.
- A vernier scale is drawn as a combination of two scales: a main scale and a vernier scale. The main scale is a plain scale that shows the measurements of the unit in large divisions. The vernier scale is a smaller scale that slides along the main scale and shows the measurements of the unit in smaller divisions.
- A vernier scale can be used to measure or convert the dimensions of an object with a fractional or decimal value.
- For example, a vernier scale of 1:10 can be used to measure or convert the dimensions of an object in centimeters or millimeters with an accuracy of 0.1 mm or 0.01 cm.
