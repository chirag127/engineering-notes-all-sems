### Cone

- A cone is a three-dimensional solid that has a circular base and a curved surface that tapers to a point called the vertex or apex.
- The height of a cone is the perpendicular distance from the base to the vertex.
- The slant height of a cone is the length of the sloping edge from the base to the vertex.
- The radius of a cone is the radius of the circular base.
- The base angle of a cone is the angle between the slant height and the height.

![Cone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9b/Cone_3d.png/240px-Cone_3d.png)

- A cone can be classified into two types: right cone and oblique cone.
- A right cone is a cone whose vertex is directly above the center of the base.
- An oblique cone is a cone whose vertex is not directly above the center of the base.

![Right cone and oblique cone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9f/Right_and_oblique_cones.png/240px-Right_and_oblique_cones.png)

- A cone can also be classified into two types based on the shape of the base: circular cone and elliptical cone.
- A circular cone is a cone whose base is a circle.
- An elliptical cone is a cone whose base is an ellipse.

![Circular cone and elliptical cone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8c/Circular_and_elliptical_cones.png/240px-Circular_and_elliptical_cones.png)

- A section of a cone is the intersection of a plane and the cone.
- A section of a cone can be classified into four types: circle, ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola.
- A circle is the section of a cone when the plane is perpendicular to the axis of the cone.
- An ellipse is the section of a cone when the plane is oblique to the axis of the cone and cuts both nappes of the cone.
- A parabola is the section of a cone when the plane is parallel to a generator of the cone and cuts one nappe of the cone.
- A hyperbola is the section of a cone when the plane is parallel to the axis of the cone and cuts both nappes of the cone.

![Sections of a cone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6a/Conic_sections.svg/240px-Conic_sections.svg.png)

- The development of a surface of a solid is the unfolding of the surface on a plane.
- The development of a surface of a cone is a sector of a circle whose radius is the slant height of the cone and whose angle is equal to the base angle of the cone multiplied by the number of sides of the base polygon.
- The development of a surface of a cone can be obtained by cutting the cone along a generator and flattening the surface on a plane.
