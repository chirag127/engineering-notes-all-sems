# Engineering Graphics and Design Lab

Engineering graphics and design lab is a course that introduces the students to the principles and techniques of engineering drawing, computer-aided design (CAD), and engineering design. The course covers the following topics:

- Engineering design process and problem-solving methods.
- Descriptive geometry and projection theory, including orthographic, isometric, and oblique projections.
- Pictorial sketching and freehand drawing of engineering objects and systems.
- Dimensioning and tolerancing of engineering drawings according to national and international standards.
- Computer-aided design (CAD) using a 3D solid modeling software package, such as SolidWorks, AutoCAD, or Inventor.
- Application of engineering graphics and design in the solution of engineering problems, such as mechanisms, structures, and devices.

The course consists of lectures and laboratory sessions, where the students practice their graphic skills and apply them to design projects. The course also requires the students to submit assignments, quizzes, and a final project report. The course objectives are:

- To develop the students' graphic skills for communication of concepts, ideas, and design of engineering products.
- To expose the students to the existing national and international standards related to technical drawings.
- To enhance the students' spatial visualization and creativity abilities.
- To familiarize the students with the CAD software and its applications in engineering design.
- To provide the students with hands-on experience in engineering design and problem-solving.
