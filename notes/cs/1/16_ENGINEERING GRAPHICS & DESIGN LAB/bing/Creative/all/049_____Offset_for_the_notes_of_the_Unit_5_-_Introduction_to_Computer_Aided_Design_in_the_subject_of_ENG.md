- Computer Aided Design (CAD) is the use of computer software to create, modify, analyze, and optimize the design of a product or a system.
- CAD software can perform various tasks such as drafting, modeling, rendering, simulation, analysis, and optimization of a design.
- CAD software can be classified into two categories: 2D CAD and 3D CAD.
- 2D CAD software is used to create and edit drawings of objects in two dimensions, such as plans, elevations, sections, and details.
- 3D CAD software is used to create and edit models of objects in three dimensions, such as solids, surfaces, and meshes.
- 3D CAD software can also generate 2D drawings from 3D models, and vice versa.
- CAD software can be integrated with other software such as Computer Aided Manufacturing (CAM), Computer Aided Engineering (CAE), and Building Information Modeling (BIM) to facilitate the design and production process.
- CAD software can offer various benefits such as improved accuracy, efficiency, productivity, quality, and creativity of the design.
- CAD software can also pose some challenges such as high cost, complexity, compatibility, security, and ethical issues.
- Some examples of popular CAD software are AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, SketchUp, and Revit.
