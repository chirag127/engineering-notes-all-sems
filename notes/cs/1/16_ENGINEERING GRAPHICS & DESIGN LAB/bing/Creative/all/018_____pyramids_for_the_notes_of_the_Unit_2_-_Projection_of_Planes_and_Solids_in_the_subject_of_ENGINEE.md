# Pyramids

A pyramid is a solid figure with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex. The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, and the number of faces is equal to the number of sides of the base. The height of a pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the vertex to the base.

## Projection of Pyramids

Projection of pyramids is the process of drawing the orthographic views of a pyramid on a plane. The orthographic views are the front view, the top view and the side view. The front view shows the shape and size of the base and the height of the pyramid. The top view shows the shape and orientation of the base and the location of the vertex. The side view shows the slant height and the inclination of the faces.

The projection of pyramids can be done in different positions, depending on how the pyramid is placed on the horizontal plane (HP) and the vertical plane (VP). The position of the pyramid can be described by the following terms:

- Axis: The line joining the vertex and the center of the base.
- Base edge: The edge of the base that is parallel to the VP or the HP.
- Base angle: The angle between the axis and the base edge.
- Inclination: The angle between the axis and the HP or the VP.

The projection of pyramids can be classified into the following types:

- Pyramids with the axis perpendicular to the HP and parallel to the VP. In this case, the front view is a rectangle and the top view is a polygon. The base angle is 90 degrees and the inclination is zero.
- Pyramids with the axis parallel to the HP and inclined to the VP. In this case, the front view is a trapezoid and the top view is a polygon. The base angle is equal to the inclination and the inclination is less than 90 degrees.
- Pyramids with the axis inclined to the HP and parallel to the VP. In this case, the front view is a polygon and the top view is a trapezoid. The base angle is 90 degrees and the inclination is less than 90 degrees.
- Pyramids with the axis inclined to both the HP and the VP. In this case, the front view and the top view are both polygons. The base angle and the inclination are both less than 90 degrees.

The steps to draw the projection of pyramids are as follows:

- Draw the base of the pyramid on the HP or the VP, depending on the position of the pyramid.
- Draw the axis of the pyramid and mark the vertex.
- Draw the projectors from the vertex and the base vertices to the other plane.
- Draw the faces of the pyramid by joining the projectors.
- Label the views and the dimensions.
