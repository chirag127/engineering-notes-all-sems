# Trim for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Computer Aided Design (CAD) is the use of software to create, modify, analyze, or optimize a design.
- CAD software can help draft documentation, visualize 2D or 3D design concepts, simulate real-world functionality, and perform various calculations.
- Trim is a common operation in CAD software that allows the user to cut or remove unwanted parts of a geometry using another geometry, a plane, or a sketch as a cutting tool .
- Trim can be used to create complex shapes, adjust the size or shape of a geometry, or simplify a design by removing unnecessary details .
- To use the trim command in AutoCAD, one of the most popular CAD software, the user can follow these steps:
  - Click on the TRIM icon or simply write TRIM and hit ENTER
  - Select all objects in the drawing window
  - Right click once on the mouse
  - Select the segment to trim
  - Hit ENTER
- Trim can also be used to explore the design space of deep neural network (DNN) accelerators for both inference and training.
- TRIM is an infrastructure model that helps hardware architects evaluate different hardware choices to develop efficient DNN architecture design.
- TRIM considers both inter-layer and intra-layer activities of a DNN and evaluates the performance, power, and area of different hardware configurations.
