# Cylinder

A cylinder is a three-dimensional solid that has two circular bases and a curved surface connecting them. The bases are parallel and congruent, and the axis of the cylinder is the line segment joining the centers of the bases. The height of the cylinder is the distance between the bases.

Some points to remember about cylinders are:

- The top and bottom views of a cylinder are circles.
- The front and side views of a cylinder are rectangles.
- The isometric view of a cylinder is an ellipse with two vertical lines at the ends.
- The development of a cylinder is a rectangle with the same height as the cylinder and the same length as the circumference of the base.
- The true shape of the base of a cylinder is a circle, but it appears as an ellipse in oblique or isometric projection.
