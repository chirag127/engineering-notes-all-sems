# Fillet

- A fillet is a rounding of an interior or exterior corner of a part design.
- Fillets are used for decorative purposes, stress relief, ease of manufacturing, and smooth transitions.
- Fillets are also called rounds, especially when applied to exterior corners.
- Fillets are the softening geometric entities on concave edges.
- Fillets are represented by circular arcs in technical drawings.
- Fillets are specified by their radius, which is half of the diameter of the circular arc.
- Fillets can be tangent or non-tangent to the adjacent surfaces.
- Fillets can be constant or variable in radius along the edge.
- Fillets can be applied to 2D or 3D shapes, such as lines, curves, polygons, or solids.
- Fillets can be created using various tools in computer aided design (CAD) software, such as sketch, extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, or fillet commands.
