# Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex. The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, and the number of faces is equal to the number of sides of the base. The height of the pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the vertex to the base plane.
- A section of a pyramid is the intersection of the pyramid with a cutting plane. The cutting plane can be parallel, perpendicular, or inclined to the base or the axis of the pyramid. The shape of the section depends on the position and orientation of the cutting plane. The true shape of the section is the shape obtained when the section plane is viewed perpendicularly.
- A development of a pyramid is the unfolding of the lateral surface of the pyramid into a flat plane. The development shows the true shape and size of each face of the pyramid. The development can be obtained by cutting the pyramid along some edges and spreading it out on a plane. The development can be used to make a paper model of the pyramid by folding and gluing the edges together.
- The steps to draw the section and development of a pyramid are:

  - Draw the front view and the top view of the pyramid in the first quadrant, using the given dimensions and orientations. Mark the base, the axis, and the vertex of the pyramid. Label the corners of the base as A, B, C, D in the clockwise direction.
  - Draw the cutting plane in the front view and the top view, using the given angle and position. Mark the points of intersection of the cutting plane and the edges of the pyramid as P, Q, R, S in the clockwise direction. Join these points to form the section line. Shade the portion of the pyramid above the cutting plane.
  - To draw the true shape of the section, draw a line XY parallel to the cutting plane and at a convenient distance below the front view. Project the points P, Q, R, S from the front view to the line XY. Mark them as P', Q', R', S'. Join these points to form the true shape of the section. Label it as PQRS.
  - To draw the development of the pyramid, draw a line MN parallel to one of the edges of the base and at a convenient distance to the right of the top view. Project the points A, B, C, D from the top view to the line MN. Mark them as A', B', C', D'. Join these points to form the base of the development. Label it as ABCD.
  - From the point A', draw a line perpendicular to MN and equal to the slant height of the pyramid. Mark the point as V'. This is the vertex of the development. Join V' to B', C', D' to form the triangular faces of the development. Label them as AB'V', BC'V', CD'V', DA'V'.
  - From the point P', draw a line parallel to MN and intersecting the line V'B' at P''. Similarly, draw lines from Q', R', S' parallel to MN and intersecting the lines V'C', V'D', V'A' at Q'', R'', S'' respectively. These are the points of intersection of the cutting plane and the development. Join these points to form the section line on the development. Label it as PQRS. Shade the portion of the development above the section line.
