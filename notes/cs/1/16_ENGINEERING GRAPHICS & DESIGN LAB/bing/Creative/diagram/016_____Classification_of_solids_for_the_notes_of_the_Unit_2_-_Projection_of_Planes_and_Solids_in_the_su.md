### Classification of solids

- A solid is an object that has three dimensions, i.e., length, breadth and height.
- In engineering graphics, solids are classified into two main types: **prisms** and **pyramids**.
- A prism is a solid whose bases are congruent polygons and whose lateral faces are parallelograms.
- A pyramid is a solid whose base is a polygon and whose lateral faces are triangles that meet at a common vertex.
- Prisms and pyramids can be further classified according to the shape of their bases and the orientation of their axes .
- The following table summarizes the classification of solids:

| Type of solid | Shape of base | Orientation of axis | Example |
|---------------|---------------|---------------------|---------|
| Prism         | Triangle      | Parallel to VP or HP | Triangular prism |
| Prism         | Square        | Parallel to VP or HP | Square prism |
| Prism         | Pentagon      | Parallel to VP or HP | Pentagonal prism |
| Prism         | Hexagon       | Parallel to VP or HP | Hexagonal prism |
| Prism         | Circle        | Parallel to VP or HP | Cylinder |
| Pyramid       | Triangle      | Perpendicular to VP or HP | Triangular pyramid |
| Pyramid       | Square        | Perpendicular to VP or HP | Square pyramid |
| Pyramid       | Pentagon      | Perpendicular to VP or HP | Pentagonal pyramid |
| Pyramid       | Hexagon       | Perpendicular to VP or HP | Hexagonal pyramid |
| Pyramid       | Circle        | Perpendicular to VP or HP | Cone |

- Another way to classify solids is based on how they are generated by revolving a plane figure about a fixed axis .
- These solids are called **solids of revolution** and include cylinders, cones, spheres, torus, etc .
