### Cone

A cone is a three-dimensional solid that has a circular base and a curved surface that tapers to a vertex. The vertex is the point where the axis of the cone intersects the curved surface. The axis of the cone is the line that passes through the center of the base and the vertex. The height of the cone is the distance from the base to the vertex. The slant height of the cone is the distance from the vertex to any point on the edge of the base. The base radius of the cone is the radius of the circular base.

![Cone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/8e/Cone_%28geometry%29.svg/1200px-Cone_%28geometry%29.svg.png)

A cone can be cut by a plane in different ways to produce different conic sections. A conic section is a curve that is formed by the intersection of a plane and a cone. The four types of conic sections are:

- Circle: A circle is formed when the plane is perpendicular to the axis of the cone and cuts the cone symmetrically.
- Ellipse: An ellipse is formed when the plane is tilted to the axis of the cone and cuts the cone symmetrically.
- Parabola: A parabola is formed when the plane is parallel to one of the generators of the cone. A generator is a line that passes through the vertex and a point on the base of the cone.
- Hyperbola: A hyperbola is formed when the plane is tilted to the axis of the cone and cuts both the upper and lower parts of the cone.

![Conic sections](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3a/Conic_Sections.svg/1200px-Conic_Sections.svg.png)

The development of a cone is the process of unfolding the curved surface of the cone into a flat plane. The development of a cone is a sector of a circle whose radius is equal to the slant height of the cone and whose angle is equal to the base circumference of the cone divided by the slant height of the cone. The development of a cone can be used to find the surface area of the cone or to make a paper model of the cone.

![Development of a cone](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/0a/Development_of_a_cone.svg/1200px-Development_of_a_cone.svg.png)

The development of a sectioned cone is the process of unfolding the part of the curved surface of the cone that is cut by a plane into a flat plane. The development of a sectioned cone is a segment of a sector of a circle whose radius is equal to the slant height of the cone and whose angle is equal to the arc length of the sectioned base divided by the slant height of the cone. The development of a sectioned cone can be used to find the area of the sectioned surface or to make a paper model of the sectioned cone.
