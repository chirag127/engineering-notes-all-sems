### Projection of solids like prisms

- A solid is a three-dimensional object that has length, breadth and height. A prism is a solid that has two congruent and parallel faces called bases and the other faces are parallelograms called lateral faces.
- The projection of a solid is the representation of its shape and size on a plane surface, such as a sheet of paper. There are two types of projection: orthographic and isometric.
- Orthographic projection is a method of drawing a solid in which the views are taken perpendicular to the principal planes of projection, namely the horizontal plane (HP) and the vertical plane (VP). The views are called the top view, the front view and the side view.
- Isometric projection is a method of drawing a solid in which the views are taken along the isometric axes, which are three mutually perpendicular lines that make equal angles of 120 degrees with each other. The views are called the isometric views.
- To draw the projection of a prism, we need to know the following information:
  - The shape and size of the base of the prism.
  - The length of the prism or the height of the lateral faces.
  - The position and orientation of the prism with respect to the planes of projection.
  - The type of projection required: orthographic or isometric.
- Depending on the position and orientation of the prism, there are different methods of drawing its projection, such as the change of position method, the auxiliary plane method, the rotation method, etc.
- The general steps for drawing the projection of a prism are as follows:
  - Draw the base of the prism on the plane of projection that is parallel to it. This will be the true shape and size of the base.
  - Draw the projectors from the vertices of the base perpendicular to the plane of projection that is perpendicular to it. This will give the length of the prism or the height of the lateral faces.
  - Draw the base of the prism on the other plane of projection by joining the ends of the projectors. This will be the foreshortened shape and size of the base.
  - Draw the lateral faces of the prism by joining the corresponding vertices of the two bases. This will complete the projection of the prism.
  - Label the views and dimensions as per the convention.
