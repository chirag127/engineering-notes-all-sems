### Orthographic representation of perspective views

- Orthographic projection is a form of parallel projection in which all the projection lines are orthogonal to the projection plane.
- Orthographic projection is also a perspective projection, in which the point of perspective is at infinite distance .
- Orthographic projection can represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions by using several two-dimensional views of the object.
- The most common orthographic views are the front, top and side views, which show the object from different directions.
- Orthographic views can also show hidden features, dimensions, and symbols to convey additional information about the object.
- Orthographic views are useful for engineering and technical drawings, as they provide accurate and unambiguous information about the shape and size of the object.
