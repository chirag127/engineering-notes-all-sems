### Cone

A cone is a solid that has a circular base and a single vertex. It can be generated by rotating a right-angled triangle about one of its sides.

The following are some important terms related to a cone:

- Axis: The straight line joining the vertex and the center of the base.
- Slant height: The distance from the vertex to any point on the base edge.
- Base radius: The radius of the circular base.
- Apex angle: The angle between the slant height and the axis.
- Frustum: The portion of a cone that is cut off by a plane parallel to the base.

#### Sections of a cone

A section of a cone is the curve obtained by cutting the cone by a plane. Depending on the position and orientation of the cutting plane, the section can be a circle, an ellipse, a parabola, or a hyperbola.

The following are some rules to determine the type of section:

- If the cutting plane is perpendicular to the axis, the section is a circle.
- If the cutting plane is inclined to the axis and cuts all the generators, the section is an ellipse.
- If the cutting plane is parallel to a generator and cuts the remaining generators, the section is a parabola.
- If the cutting plane is parallel to the axis and cuts the two nappes of the cone, the section is a hyperbola.

#### Development of a cone

The development of a cone is the process of unfolding the cone surface into a plane. The development of a cone is a sector of a circle with radius equal to the slant height and central angle equal to the apex angle.

The following are the steps to construct the development of a cone:

- Draw the front view and the top view of the cone.
- Draw a horizontal line and mark a point O on it.
- With O as center and slant height as radius, draw an arc.
- With the same radius, mark points A and B on the arc such that the angle AOB is equal to the apex angle.
- Join OA and OB to form the sector OAB, which is the development of the cone.
- Cut out the sector and fold it along OA and OB to form the cone.

#### Development of a sectioned cone

The development of a sectioned cone is the process of unfolding the cone surface that is cut by a plane into a plane. The development of a sectioned cone is a sector of a circle with radius equal to the slant height and central angle equal to the apex angle, with a portion of the sector removed according to the cutting plane.

The following are the steps to construct the development of a sectioned cone:

- Draw the front view and the top view of the cone and the cutting plane.
- Draw the true shape of the section by projecting the points of intersection of the cutting plane and the cone on a plane parallel to the cutting plane.
- Draw a horizontal line and mark a point O on it.
- With O as center and slant height as radius, draw an arc.
- With the same radius, mark points A and B on the arc such that the angle AOB is equal to the apex angle.
- Join OA and OB to form the sector OAB, which is the development of the cone.
- Mark the points of intersection of the cutting plane and the cone on the sector OAB by measuring the distances along the generators from the vertex.
- Join the points of intersection to form the outline of the section on the sector OAB.
- Cut out the sector and the section and fold them along OA and OB to form the sectioned cone.
