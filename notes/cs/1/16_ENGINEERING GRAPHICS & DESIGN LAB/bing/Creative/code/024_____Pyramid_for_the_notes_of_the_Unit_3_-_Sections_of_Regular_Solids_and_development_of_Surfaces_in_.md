### Pyramid

- A pyramid is a polyhedron that has a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex called the apex.
- The base can be any regular or irregular polygon, such as a square, a triangle, a hexagon, etc.
- The height of a pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the apex to the base plane.
- The slant height of a pyramid is the distance from the apex to the midpoint of an edge of the base.
- The lateral surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the areas of the triangular faces, excluding the base.
- The total surface area of a pyramid is the sum of the lateral surface area and the base area.
