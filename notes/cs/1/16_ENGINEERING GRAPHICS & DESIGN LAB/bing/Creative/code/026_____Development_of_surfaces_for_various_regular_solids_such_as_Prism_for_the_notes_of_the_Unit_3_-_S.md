### Development of surfaces for various regular solids such as Prism

- Development of surfaces is a technique of drawing the shapes of solids as they would appear when the surfaces of solids are laid out on a plane surface .
- Development of surfaces is useful for designing and manufacturing objects such as sheet metal parts, paper boxes, pipes, etc .
- There are different methods of development of surfaces, such as parallel-line method, radial-line method, triangulation method, and approximate method .
- Parallel-line method is used for developing prisms and single curved surfaces like cylinders, in which all the edges or generators of lateral surfaces are parallel to each other .
- The steps for developing the surface of a prism by parallel-line method are :
  - Draw the front view and the top view of the prism in the first quadrant.
  - Divide the base of the prism into equal parts and number them.
  - Draw a horizontal line equal to the perimeter of the base and divide it into the same number of equal parts as the base. This line is called the stretch-out line.
  - Draw perpendiculars from the points on the stretch-out line and mark them with the corresponding numbers of the base.
  - Project the heights of the lateral faces from the front view to the development and draw horizontal lines to complete the development.
  - Add dimensions and labels to the development.
