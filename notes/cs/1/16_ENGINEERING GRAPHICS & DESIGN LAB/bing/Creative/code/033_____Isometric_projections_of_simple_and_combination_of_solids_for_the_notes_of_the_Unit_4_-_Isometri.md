### Isometric projections of simple and combination of solids

- Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions in technical and engineering drawings.
- It is an axonometric projection in which the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angle between any two of them is 120 degrees .
- Isometric projection preserves the relative proportions of the object's dimensions along the axes, but not the angles between them.
- Isometric projection is useful for showing the general shape and arrangement of the object, but not the exact measurements or details.
- To draw an isometric projection of a simple solid, such as a cube, a cylinder, or a cone, the following steps are followed:
  - Choose a suitable scale and orientation for the object.
  - Draw three mutually perpendicular lines, called isometric axes, representing the length, width, and height of the object. These lines should be equally inclined to the drawing surface and equal distances along them should be drawn equal.
  - Project the vertices of the object along the isometric axes and join them to form the outline of the object.
  - Shade or hatch the faces of the object to indicate the depth and direction of the light source.
- To draw an isometric projection of a combination of solids, such as a cylinder on a cube, a cone on a cylinder, or a sphere on a cone, the following steps are followed:
  - Draw the isometric projection of the base solid, such as the cube, the cylinder, or the cone, following the steps above.
  - Locate the center and the radius of the top face of the base solid and draw a circle using an isometric ellipse template or a compass.
  - Draw the isometric projection of the second solid, such as the cylinder, the cone, or the sphere, on top of the base solid, following the steps above.
  - Erase the hidden lines and edges of the base solid that are covered by the second solid.
  - Shade or hatch the faces of the solids to indicate the depth and direction of the light source.
