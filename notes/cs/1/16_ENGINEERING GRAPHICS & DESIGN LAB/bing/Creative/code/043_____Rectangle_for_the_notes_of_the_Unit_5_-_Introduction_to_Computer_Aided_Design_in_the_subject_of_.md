### Rectangle

- A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles and opposite sides equal in length.
- A rectangle can be defined by two parameters: its length and its width.
- A rectangle can also be defined by the coordinates of its four vertices or by the coordinates of one vertex and the length and width.
- A rectangle can be drawn in a computer aided design (CAD) software by using the following steps:
  - Select the Rectangle tool from the Draw menu or the toolbar.
  - Specify the first corner point of the rectangle by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates.
  - Specify the opposite corner point of the rectangle by clicking on the drawing area or entering the coordinates or the length and width.
  - Press Enter or Esc to complete the rectangle.
- A rectangle can be modified in a CAD software by using the following tools:
  - Move: to change the position of the rectangle by specifying a base point and a displacement vector.
  - Rotate: to change the orientation of the rectangle by specifying a center point and an angle of rotation.
  - Scale: to change the size of the rectangle by specifying a base point and a scale factor.
  - Mirror: to create a mirror image of the rectangle by specifying a mirror line.
  - Trim: to cut off the parts of the rectangle that extend beyond a cutting edge.
  - Extend: to extend the parts of the rectangle that are shorter than a boundary edge.
  - Fillet: to create a rounded corner at the intersection of two edges of the rectangle by specifying a radius.
  - Chamfer: to create a beveled corner at the intersection of two edges of the rectangle by specifying a distance or an angle.
