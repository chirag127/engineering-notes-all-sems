### Isometric Projection

Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions in technical and engineering drawings. It is an axonometric projection in which the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angle between any two of them is 120 degrees.

Here are some key points to remember about isometric projection:

1. Isometric projection is a form of axonometric projection, which is a type of parallel projection.
2. In an isometric projection, the three axes of the object are equally foreshortened and the angles between them are all 120 degrees.
3. Isometric projection is commonly used in technical and engineering drawings to represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
4. Isometric projection can be used to create an isometric view of an object, which is a 2D representation of the object that appears to be 3D.
5. Isometric projection is useful for showing the overall shape of an object, but it can be difficult to accurately represent the object's proportions and details.
