### Scales for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. **Introduction to Engineering Drawing:** Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel associated with the engineering profession. It is used to convey ideas and information about the design and construction of objects.

2. **Orthographic Projections:** Orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is a form of parallel projection, where the view direction is orthogonal to the projection plane.

3. **Scales:** In engineering drawing, scales are used to represent the size of an object in a drawing. There are two types of scales: full scale and reduced scale.

    - **Full Scale:** Full scale means that the size of the object in the drawing is the same as the size of the object in reality. This is also known as 1:1 scale.

    - **Reduced Scale:** Reduced scale means that the size of the object in the drawing is smaller than the size of the object in reality. This is also known as a scale factor. For example, a scale factor of 1:2 means that the object in the drawing is half the size of the object in reality.

4. **Types of Scales:** There are several types of scales used in engineering drawing, including:

    - **Plain Scale:** A plain scale is a straight line divided into equal parts, with each part representing a unit of measurement.

    - **Diagonal Scale:** A diagonal scale is used to measure fractions of a unit. It is constructed by drawing a diagonal line from the zero point of the scale to the point representing the largest fraction to be measured.

    - **Vernier Scale:** A vernier scale is used to measure very small fractions of a unit. It consists of two scales, one fixed and one movable, with the movable scale having a slightly smaller unit of measurement than the fixed scale.

    - **Comparative Scale:** A comparative scale is used to compare the sizes of two or more objects. It consists of two or more scales, each representing a different unit of measurement.
