### Classification of Solids

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, the classification of solids is an important topic in Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids. Solids have been classified into two types: crystalline solids and amorphous solids.

- **Crystalline Solids**: The solids having sharp edges and well-defined planes are called crystals or crystalline solids. Crystalline solids are also called true solids.

- **Amorphous Solids**: Amorphous solids are solids that do not have a well-defined shape or form. They do not have a regular arrangement of particles and do not exhibit a sharp melting point.

In a solid substance, the constituent particles are held together by strong forces of attraction, and because of this, the particles of solids are packed in proper order or symmetry. Therefore, a solid has a definite shape, a distinct boundary, a fixed volume, and negligible compressibility.
