### Sections of Solids

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 3 covers the topic of Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces. Here are some key points to remember about Sections of Solids:

1. A section of a solid is the shape obtained when a solid is cut by a plane.
2. The plane that cuts the solid is called the cutting plane or the section plane.
3. The shape of the section depends on the orientation of the cutting plane with respect to the solid.
4. The section can be represented graphically by drawing the intersection of the cutting plane with the solid.
5. Sections of solids are used to reveal the internal structure of the solid and to provide a better understanding of its geometry.
6. Common types of sections include full sections, half sections, and offset sections.
7. Full sections are obtained when the cutting plane passes completely through the solid.
8. Half sections are obtained when the cutting plane passes halfway through the solid.
9. Offset sections are obtained when the cutting plane is offset from the center of the solid.
