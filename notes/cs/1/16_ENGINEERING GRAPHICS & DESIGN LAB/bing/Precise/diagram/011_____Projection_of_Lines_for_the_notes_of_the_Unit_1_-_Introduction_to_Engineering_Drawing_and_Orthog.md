### Projection of Lines

Projection of lines is a fundamental concept in engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It is the first topic in Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Projection of lines refers to the representation of a 3D line on a 2D plane.
2. The line is projected onto the plane using parallel projection or perspective projection.
3. In parallel projection, the projectors are parallel to each other and perpendicular to the plane of projection.
4. In perspective projection, the projectors converge at a single point called the center of projection or the vanishing point.
5. The type of projection used depends on the purpose of the drawing and the level of detail required.
6. Orthographic projection is a type of parallel projection commonly used in engineering drawing.
7. In orthographic projection, the object is viewed from multiple directions and the views are arranged in a standard layout.
8. The most common layout is the third-angle projection, where the top, front, and right-side views are arranged in a specific manner.
9. The first-angle projection is another layout used in some countries.
10. The choice of layout depends on the standards and conventions followed in the region.
