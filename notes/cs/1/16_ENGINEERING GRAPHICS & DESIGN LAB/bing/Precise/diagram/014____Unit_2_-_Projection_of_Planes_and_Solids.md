## Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids

1. **Introduction:** In this unit, we will study the projection of planes and solids. Projection is the representation of a three-dimensional object on a two-dimensional plane, such as a piece of paper or a computer screen.

2. **Projection of Planes:** A plane is a flat, two-dimensional surface. When projecting a plane onto another plane, the resulting figure is called a projection. The projection of a plane can be found by drawing lines from the points on the plane to the plane of projection, perpendicular to the plane of projection.

3. **Projection of Solids:** A solid is a three-dimensional object. When projecting a solid onto a plane, the resulting figure is called a projection. The projection of a solid can be found by drawing lines from the points on the solid to the plane of projection, perpendicular to the plane of projection.

4. **Types of Projections:** There are two main types of projections: parallel and perspective. In a parallel projection, the lines of projection are parallel to each other. In a perspective projection, the lines of projection converge at a single point, called the vanishing point.

5. **Orthographic Projection:** Orthographic projection is a type of parallel projection. In orthographic projection, the lines of projection are perpendicular to the plane of projection. Orthographic projection is commonly used in technical drawing and engineering.

6. **Isometric Projection:** Isometric projection is a type of parallel projection. In isometric projection, the lines of projection are at an angle to the plane of projection. Isometric projection is commonly used in technical drawing and engineering to represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.

7. **Conclusion:** In this unit, we have learned about the projection of planes and solids. We have discussed the different types of projections and their uses in technical drawing and engineering. Understanding projection is important for representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
