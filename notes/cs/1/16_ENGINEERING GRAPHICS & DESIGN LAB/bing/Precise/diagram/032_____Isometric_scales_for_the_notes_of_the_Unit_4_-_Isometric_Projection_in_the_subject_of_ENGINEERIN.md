### Isometric scales for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
- Isometric scales are used to measure the dimensions of an object in isometric projection.
- In isometric projection, the three axes of the object are equally inclined to the plane of projection, and the angles between them are 120 degrees.
- To construct an isometric scale, the true lengths of the object along the three axes are divided by the same scale factor.
- The scale factor is determined by the angle of the isometric projection, which is typically 30 degrees.
- The isometric scale is then used to measure the dimensions of the object in the isometric projection.
- Isometric scales are useful for creating accurate and proportional representations of three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
