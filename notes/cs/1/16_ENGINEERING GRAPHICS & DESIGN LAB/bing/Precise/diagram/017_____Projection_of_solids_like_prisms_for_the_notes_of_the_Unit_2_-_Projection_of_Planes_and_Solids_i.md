### Projection of Solids like Prisms

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, the projection of solids like prisms is an important topic in Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids. Here are some key points to remember:

1. A prism is a polyhedron with two parallel and congruent bases and rectangular lateral faces.
2. The projection of a prism is the representation of the prism on a plane.
3. The projection of a prism can be obtained by projecting its vertices and edges onto the plane of projection.
4. The type of projection used (orthographic or isometric) will affect the appearance of the projected prism.
5. In orthographic projection, the prism is projected onto a plane perpendicular to one of its principal axes, resulting in a true size and shape view of one of its faces.
6. In isometric projection, the prism is projected onto a plane at an angle, resulting in a distorted but more visually descriptive view of the prism.
7. The choice of projection method will depend on the purpose of the drawing and the information that needs to be conveyed.
