### Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- Computer Aided Design (CAD) is the use of computer software to design and document a product's design process.
- CAD software is used to create 2D and 3D models of products, as well as to generate technical drawings and other design documentation.
- CAD is used in many industries, including engineering, architecture, and product design.
- CAD software can improve the design process by allowing designers to visualize and test their designs before they are built.
- Some common CAD software programs include AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and CATIA.
- CAD can be used in conjunction with other computer-aided technologies, such as Computer Aided Manufacturing (CAM) and Computer Aided Engineering (CAE).
- CAD can also be used to create virtual prototypes, which can be used to test and refine designs before physical prototypes are built.
- The use of CAD can improve the efficiency and accuracy of the design process, as well as reduce the time and cost associated with product development.
