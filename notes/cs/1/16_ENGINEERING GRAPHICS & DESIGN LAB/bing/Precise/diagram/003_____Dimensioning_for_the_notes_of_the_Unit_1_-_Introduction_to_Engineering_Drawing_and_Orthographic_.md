### Dimensioning

Dimensioning is the process of adding measurements to a drawing. It is an essential part of creating an engineering drawing, as it provides the necessary information for the manufacture and assembly of the object being designed. Here are some key points to remember when dimensioning:

1. **Clarity:** Dimensions should be placed in a clear and concise manner, avoiding any ambiguity or confusion.
2. **Completeness:** All necessary dimensions should be included in the drawing, leaving no room for assumptions or guesswork.
3. **Accuracy:** Dimensions should be accurate and precise, reflecting the intended size and shape of the object.
4. **Units:** The units of measurement should be clearly stated and consistent throughout the drawing.
5. **Placement:** Dimensions should be placed in a logical and organized manner, avoiding clutter and overlapping.
6. **Readability:** The text and numbers used for dimensioning should be legible and easy to read.

These are some of the key points to keep in mind when dimensioning an engineering drawing. Proper dimensioning is crucial for the successful manufacture and assembly of the object being designed.
