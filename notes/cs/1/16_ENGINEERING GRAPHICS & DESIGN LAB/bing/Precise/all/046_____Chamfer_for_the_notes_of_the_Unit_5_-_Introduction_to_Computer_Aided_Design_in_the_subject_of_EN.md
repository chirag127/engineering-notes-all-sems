# Chamfer

Chamfer is a technique used in computer-aided design (CAD) to create a beveled edge or corner. It is commonly used in engineering graphics and design to improve the appearance of an object, to remove sharp edges, or to facilitate assembly.

Here are some key points to remember about chamfering in the context of CAD:

1. Chamfering can be applied to both 2D and 3D objects.
2. In 2D, chamfering is the process of creating a beveled edge by cutting away material at a 45-degree angle.
3. In 3D, chamfering can be applied to edges, corners, and faces of an object.
4. Chamfering can be achieved using various tools and techniques, such as using a chamfer tool or by manually drawing the chamfered edge.
5. The size and angle of the chamfer can be adjusted to achieve the desired result.
6. Chamfering can improve the appearance of an object, remove sharp edges, and facilitate assembly.
