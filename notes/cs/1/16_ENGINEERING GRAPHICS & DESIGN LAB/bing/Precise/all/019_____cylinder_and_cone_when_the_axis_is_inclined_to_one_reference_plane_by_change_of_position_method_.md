# Cylinder and Cone when the Axis is Inclined to One Reference Plane by Change of Position Method

## Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. The change of position method is used to draw the projections of a cylinder or cone when its axis is inclined to one of the reference planes.
2. This method involves changing the position of the cylinder or cone so that its axis is parallel to one of the reference planes.
3. The projections of the cylinder or cone are then drawn in this new position.
4. The true shape and size of the base of the cylinder or cone can be obtained in this position.
5. The projections are then rotated back to their original position to obtain the final projections of the cylinder or cone with its axis inclined to one of the reference planes.
6. This method is useful when the axis of the cylinder or cone is inclined to one of the reference planes and parallel to the other.
7. It is also useful when the axis of the cylinder or cone is inclined to both reference planes.
