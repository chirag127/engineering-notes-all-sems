### Rectangle

1. A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles.
2. Opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel and equal in length.
3. The diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length and bisect each other.
4. The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width.
5. In a coordinate plane, a rectangle can be defined by the coordinates of its four vertices.
6. In computer-aided design, rectangles are commonly used as basic shapes for creating more complex designs.
7. Rectangles can be created using various tools in CAD software, such as the rectangle tool or by specifying the coordinates of its vertices.
8. Rectangles can be modified by changing their dimensions, rotating them, or applying various transformations.
9. Rectangles can also be used as a reference for creating other shapes, such as circles or polygons, by using their edges or diagonals as guides.
10. In engineering graphics and design, rectangles are often used to represent objects with rectangular shapes, such as boxes or building structures.
