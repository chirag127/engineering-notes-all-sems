### Circle

1. A circle is a geometric shape that consists of all points in a plane that are equidistant from a given point, called the center.
2. The distance between any point on the circle and the center is called the radius.
3. A line segment connecting two points on the circle and passing through the center is called a diameter. The diameter is twice the length of the radius.
4. The circumference of a circle is the distance around the circle, and is given by the formula C = 2πr, where r is the radius.
5. The area of a circle is given by the formula A = πr^2, where r is the radius.
6. In computer-aided design (CAD), circles can be created using various tools and commands, such as the Circle or Arc command.
7. Circles can be used in CAD to create various shapes and designs, such as wheels, gears, and round objects.
8. In CAD, circles can be modified and manipulated using various tools and commands, such as the Trim, Extend, or Offset command.
9. Circles can also be used in conjunction with other geometric shapes, such as lines and arcs, to create more complex designs.
10. Understanding the properties and uses of circles is important in the field of engineering graphics and design.
