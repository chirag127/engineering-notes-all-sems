### Fillet

- A fillet is a curved surface that connects two surfaces that meet at a sharp angle.
- Fillets are used to smooth out sharp edges and corners in a design.
- Fillets can improve the strength and durability of a part by reducing stress concentrations.
- Fillets can also improve the appearance of a part by making it look more aesthetically pleasing.
- In computer-aided design (CAD) software, fillets can be added to a design using the fillet command.
- The fillet command allows the user to specify the radius of the fillet and the surfaces to which it should be applied.
- Fillets can be added to both 2D and 3D designs.
- Fillets can also be used to blend surfaces together in a smooth and continuous manner.
- Fillets are an important tool in the design process and can help to improve the functionality and appearance of a part.
