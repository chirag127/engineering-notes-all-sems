# Transformation of Projections

Transformation of projections is a topic in Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB. It involves the conversion of one type of projection to another. Some key points to remember about transformation of projections are:

1. Transformation of projections is used to represent 3D objects in 2D.
2. It involves changing the orientation of the object with respect to the plane of projection.
3. The most common types of projections used in engineering graphics are orthographic and isometric projections.
4. Orthographic projections represent the object as it appears from the front, top, and side views.
5. Isometric projections represent the object as it appears from a corner view, with all three dimensions visible.
6. To transform an orthographic projection into an isometric projection, the object must be rotated along one or more of its axes.
7. Computer-aided design (CAD) software can be used to perform transformation of projections quickly and accurately.
