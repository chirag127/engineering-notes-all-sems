# Classification of Solids

Solids can be classified into two main categories: Crystalline and Amorphous.

1. **Crystalline Solids**: These are solids that have a regular and repeating pattern of atoms, ions, or molecules. They have a definite shape and a sharp melting point. Examples of crystalline solids include metals, salts, and minerals.

2. **Amorphous Solids**: These are solids that do not have a regular and repeating pattern of atoms, ions, or molecules. They have an irregular shape and do not have a sharp melting point. Examples of amorphous solids include glass, rubber, and plastics.
