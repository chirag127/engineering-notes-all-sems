# Isometric Projections of Simple and Combination of Solids

Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is commonly used in engineering, architecture, and related fields. In isometric projection, the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angles between them are 120 degrees.

## Simple Solids

Simple solids are basic three-dimensional shapes such as cubes, spheres, cylinders, and cones. In isometric projection, these shapes can be represented using their isometric views. For example, a cube can be represented by drawing its edges as parallel lines and its faces as parallelograms.

## Combination of Solids

Combination of solids refers to the representation of objects that are made up of multiple simple solids. In isometric projection, these objects can be represented by combining the isometric views of the individual solids. For example, a cylinder on top of a cube can be represented by drawing the isometric view of the cube and then adding the isometric view of the cylinder on top of it.

## Key Points

- Isometric projection is a method for visually representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
- In isometric projection, the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angles between them are 120 degrees.
- Simple solids such as cubes, spheres, cylinders, and cones can be represented using their isometric views.
- Combination of solids can be represented by combining the isometric views of the individual solids.
