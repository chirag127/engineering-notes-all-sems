# Projection of Straight Lines

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, the topic of projection of straight lines is covered in Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections.

1. A straight line can be defined as the shortest distance between two points.
2. In engineering drawing, the projection of a straight line refers to the representation of the line on a plane.
3. The projection of a straight line can be obtained by drawing perpendiculars from the endpoints of the line to the plane of projection.
4. The points where the perpendiculars meet the plane of projection are called the projectors.
5. The line joining the projectors is the projection of the straight line on the plane.
6. The length of the projection of a straight line on a plane is equal to the length of the line multiplied by the cosine of the angle between the line and the plane.
7. The angle between the line and the plane is called the angle of inclination.
8. The projection of a straight line can be used to determine the true length of the line and its angle of inclination with the plane of projection.
