# Projection of Lines

Projection of lines is a fundamental concept in engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It is a part of Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

Here are some key points to remember about the projection of lines:

1. Projection of lines refers to the representation of a 3D line on a 2D plane.
2. The projection of a line can be obtained by drawing perpendiculars from the endpoints of the line to the plane of projection.
3. The length of the projected line may be different from the actual length of the line.
4. The angle between the projected line and the plane of projection is called the angle of inclination.
5. The true length of a line can be obtained by projecting the line onto a plane perpendicular to the line.
6. The projection of a line can be used to determine the true shape of an object when viewed from a particular direction.

These are some of the basic concepts related to the projection of lines. It is important to have a good understanding of these concepts in order to effectively use engineering drawing and orthographic projections.
