# Right Regular Solids and Auxiliary Views for the True Shape of the Sections such as Prism

## Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces in the Subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab

1. A right regular solid is a three-dimensional geometric figure with a polygonal base, straight sides, and a height perpendicular to the base.
2. The true shape of a section is the shape that is revealed when a solid is cut by a plane.
3. An auxiliary view is a view that is used to show the true shape of a feature that is not parallel to any of the principal planes of projection.
4. A prism is a type of right regular solid that has two parallel and congruent polygonal bases and rectangular sides.
5. To find the true shape of a section of a prism, an auxiliary view can be used.
6. The auxiliary view is created by projecting the section onto a plane that is perpendicular to the cutting plane.
7. This will reveal the true shape of the section, which can then be used to create a development of the surface of the prism.
