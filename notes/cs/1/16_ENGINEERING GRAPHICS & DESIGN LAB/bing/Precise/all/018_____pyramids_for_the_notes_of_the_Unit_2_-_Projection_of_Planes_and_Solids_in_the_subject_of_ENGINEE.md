# Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids

## Pyramids

- A pyramid is a polyhedron formed by connecting a polygonal base and a point, called the apex, by triangular faces.
- In engineering graphics, the projection of pyramids is studied.
- The projection of a pyramid can be done when the axis is inclined to one plane by the change of position method.
- An example of a projection of a pyramid is a square pyramid of base 30mm and height 60mm that rests with one of its base edges on the HP. The axis of the pyramid makes an angle of 45° with the HP.
- The section of a pyramid can also be studied in engineering graphics. The section of a pyramid in a simple vertical position (axis perpendicular to HP alone) can be done by planes either inclined to HP or VP alone to find the true shape of the section.
