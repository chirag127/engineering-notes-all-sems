# Isometric Projection

Isometric projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is a type of axonometric projection, which is a category of orthographic projections. In isometric projection, the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angles between them are 120 degrees.

Here are some key points to remember about isometric projection:

1. Isometric projection is used to create a pictorial view of an object, where the three axes are equally foreshortened.
2. The angles between the axes are 120 degrees.
3. Isometric projection is a type of axonometric projection, which is a category of orthographic projections.
4. In isometric projection, the object is rotated along one or more of its axes relative to the projection plane.
5. Isometric projection is commonly used in technical drawings, video games, and architectural illustrations.
