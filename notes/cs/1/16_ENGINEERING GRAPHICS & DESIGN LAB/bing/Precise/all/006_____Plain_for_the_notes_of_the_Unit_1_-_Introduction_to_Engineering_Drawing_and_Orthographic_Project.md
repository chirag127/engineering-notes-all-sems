# Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

## Introduction to Engineering Drawing
- Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel to convey technical information.
- It is used to communicate ideas, design intent, and information about the shape, size, and features of an object.
- Engineering drawings are created using standard conventions and symbols to ensure that they are understood by all who use them.

## Orthographic Projections
- Orthographic projection is a method of representing a three-dimensional object in two dimensions.
- It is a type of parallel projection where the projection lines are perpendicular to the projection plane.
- Orthographic projections are commonly used in engineering drawings to show different views of an object.
- The three most common views used in orthographic projections are the front view, top view, and right side view.
- These views are arranged in a specific way, known as the "third-angle projection" method, to provide a complete representation of the object.
