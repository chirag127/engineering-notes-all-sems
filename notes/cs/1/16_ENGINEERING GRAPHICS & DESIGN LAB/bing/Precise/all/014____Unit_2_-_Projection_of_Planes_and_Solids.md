## Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids

1. **Projection of Planes**: Projection of planes refers to the representation of a plane on a two-dimensional surface, such as a piece of paper or a computer screen. This is achieved by drawing the plane in such a way that it appears to be viewed from a particular angle or perspective.

2. **Types of Projections**: There are two main types of projections used to represent planes: parallel projection and perspective projection. In parallel projection, the lines of projection are parallel to each other, while in perspective projection, the lines of projection converge at a single point.

3. **Projection of Solids**: Projection of solids refers to the representation of a three-dimensional object on a two-dimensional surface. This is achieved by drawing the object in such a way that it appears to be viewed from a particular angle or perspective.

4. **Orthographic Projection**: Orthographic projection is a type of parallel projection used to represent solids. In this type of projection, the object is viewed from one of its principal planes, and the lines of projection are perpendicular to the plane of projection.

5. **Isometric Projection**: Isometric projection is a type of parallel projection used to represent solids. In this type of projection, the object is viewed from a corner, and the lines of projection are at an angle of 120 degrees to each other.

6. **Oblique Projection**: Oblique projection is a type of parallel projection used to represent solids. In this type of projection, the object is viewed from an angle, and the lines of projection are not perpendicular to the plane of projection.

7. **Perspective Projection**: Perspective projection is a type of projection used to represent solids. In this type of projection, the object is viewed from a point, and the lines of projection converge at that point.

8. **Applications**: Projection of planes and solids is used in various fields, such as engineering, architecture, and art, to represent objects in a two-dimensional format. It is also used in computer graphics to create realistic images of three-dimensional objects.
