### Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

1. Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel to convey technical information.
2. It is a precise and standardized method of representing objects and their relationships in two or three dimensions.
3. Orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
4. It is a type of parallel projection where the projection lines are orthogonal to the projection plane.
5. Orthographic projections are commonly used in engineering and technical drawings to show multiple views of an object.
6. The three most common views used in orthographic projections are the top, front, and right side views.
7. These views are typically arranged in a standard layout known as the "third-angle projection" in the United States and the "first-angle projection" in Europe and Asia.
8. Orthographic projections are useful for showing the shape and size of an object, as well as its relationship to other objects in an assembly.
9. They are also used to create detailed working drawings for manufacturing and construction.
