### Cylinder

A cylinder is a three-dimensional geometric shape that has two parallel and congruent circular bases connected by a curved surface. It is a type of prism, where the cross-section along the axis remains the same. The axis of the cylinder is the line joining the centers of the two bases.

Some key points to remember about cylinders are:
- The volume of a cylinder is calculated by multiplying the area of the base by the height of the cylinder.
- The lateral surface area of a cylinder is calculated by multiplying the circumference of the base by the height of the cylinder.
- The total surface area of a cylinder is calculated by adding the lateral surface area and the areas of the two bases.
- A cylinder can be classified as a right cylinder or an oblique cylinder. In a right cylinder, the axis is perpendicular to the bases, while in an oblique cylinder, the axis is not perpendicular to the bases.
- The development of the lateral surface of a right cylinder is a rectangle, while the development of the lateral surface of an oblique cylinder is a parallelogram.
