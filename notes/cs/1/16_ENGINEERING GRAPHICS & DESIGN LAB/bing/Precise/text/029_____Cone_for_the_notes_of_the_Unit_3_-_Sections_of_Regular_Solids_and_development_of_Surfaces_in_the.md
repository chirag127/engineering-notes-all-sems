### Cone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. A cone is a three-dimensional geometric shape that tapers smoothly from a flat base to a point called the apex or vertex.
2. A cone is formed by a set of line segments, half-lines, or lines connecting a common point, the apex, to all of the points on a base that is in a plane that does not contain the apex.
3. The base of a cone can be any shape, but it is often a circle or an ellipse.
4. The axis of a cone is the straight line passing through the apex and the center of the base.
5. The lateral surface of a cone is the curved surface that connects the base to the apex.
6. The slant height of a cone is the distance from any point on the circle of the base to the apex along the lateral surface.
7. The volume of a cone is one third the product of the area of the base and the height of the cone.
8. The surface area of a cone is the sum of the area of the base and the lateral surface area.
9. The development of the surface of a cone is a sector of a circle with radius equal to the slant height of the cone.
10. The angle of the sector is equal to the circumference of the base of the cone divided by the slant height of the cone.
