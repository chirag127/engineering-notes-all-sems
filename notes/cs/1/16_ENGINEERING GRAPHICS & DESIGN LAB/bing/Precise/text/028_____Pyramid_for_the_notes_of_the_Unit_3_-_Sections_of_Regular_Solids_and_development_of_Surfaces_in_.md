### Pyramid for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

- A pyramid is a polyhedron with a polygonal base and triangular faces that meet at a common point called the apex.
- The base of a pyramid can be any polygon, including a square, rectangle, triangle, or hexagon.
- The triangular faces of a pyramid are called lateral faces.
- The height of a pyramid is the perpendicular distance from the base to the apex.
- The slant height of a pyramid is the distance from the center of the base to the midpoint of a lateral edge.
- The volume of a pyramid is calculated as one-third the product of the area of the base and the height of the pyramid.
- The surface area of a pyramid is calculated as the sum of the areas of the base and the lateral faces.
- In engineering graphics and design, the development of the surface of a pyramid involves unfolding the pyramid into a flat pattern that can be cut and folded to form the 3D shape.
- The development of the surface of a pyramid is useful in manufacturing and construction, where it is necessary to create a flat pattern that can be cut and folded to form the desired 3D shape.
