### Projection of Solids like Prisms

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, the projection of solids like prisms is an important topic in Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids. Here are some key points to remember:

1. A prism is a polyhedron with two parallel and congruent bases and rectangular or parallelogram faces connecting the bases.
2. The projection of a prism is the representation of the prism on a plane.
3. The projection of a prism can be obtained by projecting its vertices onto the plane and connecting the projected points.
4. The type of projection used (orthographic, isometric, etc.) will affect the appearance of the projected prism.
5. The orientation of the prism relative to the plane of projection will also affect the appearance of the projected prism.
6. It is important to carefully choose the projection method and orientation to accurately represent the prism in the desired view.

These are some of the key points to remember when studying the projection of solids like prisms in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab. It is important to practice drawing and visualizing the projections to fully understand the concepts.
