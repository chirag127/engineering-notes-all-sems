### Dimensioning

Dimensioning is the process of adding measurements to a drawing. It is an essential part of technical drawing and is used to convey the size and proportions of an object. Here are some key points to remember when dimensioning:

1. Dimensions should be placed in a clear and unambiguous manner, avoiding any confusion.
2. The dimension lines should be spaced uniformly and should not cross each other.
3. The dimension lines should be broken for the insertion of the dimension value.
4. The units of measurement should be clearly stated, either in the title block or next to the dimension value.
5. The dimension value should be placed above the dimension line and should be centered between the extension lines.
6. The extension lines should be drawn perpendicular to the dimension line and should extend slightly beyond the dimension line.
7. The arrowheads should be of uniform size and style and should touch the extension lines.
8. The dimensioning should be done in such a way that the drawing can be understood without the need for additional explanations.

These are some of the basic rules for dimensioning in technical drawing. It is important to follow these rules to ensure that the drawing is clear and easy to understand.
