### Projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 1 covers the topic of Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections. One of the important concepts in this unit is the projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes.

1. A line is said to be inclined to a plane when it is not perpendicular to the plane.
2. When a line is inclined to one plane, its projections on that plane will appear as a line inclined to the reference line.
3. When a line is inclined to both planes, its projections on both planes will appear as lines inclined to the reference lines.
4. The true length of the line can be determined by drawing an auxiliary view perpendicular to the line.
5. The angle of inclination of the line to the plane can also be determined by drawing an auxiliary view perpendicular to the line.

These concepts are important to understand when creating orthographic projections in engineering drawing. It is important to practice drawing these projections to gain proficiency in this skill.
