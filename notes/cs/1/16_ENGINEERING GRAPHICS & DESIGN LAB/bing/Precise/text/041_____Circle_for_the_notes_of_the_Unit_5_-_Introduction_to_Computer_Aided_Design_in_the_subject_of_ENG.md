### Circle

A circle is a geometric shape that consists of all points in a plane that are equidistant from a given point, called the center. The distance between any point on the circle and the center is called the radius. Here are some important properties of circles:

1. The circumference of a circle is the distance around the circle, and is given by the formula C = 2πr, where r is the radius of the circle.
2. The area of a circle is the amount of space inside the circle, and is given by the formula A = πr^2, where r is the radius of the circle.
3. A chord of a circle is a line segment that connects two points on the circle. The longest chord of a circle is its diameter, which passes through the center of the circle and is twice the length of the radius.
4. A tangent to a circle is a line that touches the circle at exactly one point. The tangent is perpendicular to the radius drawn to the point of contact.
5. An arc of a circle is a portion of the circumference of the circle. The length of an arc is given by the formula L = rθ, where r is the radius of the circle and θ is the measure of the central angle in radians.
6. A sector of a circle is a region bounded by two radii and an arc. The area of a sector is given by the formula A = 1/2 r^2θ, where r is the radius of the circle and θ is the measure of the central angle in radians.
