### Rectangle
- A rectangle is a quadrilateral with four right angles.
- Opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel and equal in length.
- The diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length.
- The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its width.
- The perimeter of a rectangle is calculated by adding the lengths of all four sides.
- In computer-aided design, rectangles can be created using the rectangle tool or by specifying the coordinates of two opposite corners.
- Rectangles are commonly used in engineering graphics and design to represent objects with flat, rectangular surfaces.
