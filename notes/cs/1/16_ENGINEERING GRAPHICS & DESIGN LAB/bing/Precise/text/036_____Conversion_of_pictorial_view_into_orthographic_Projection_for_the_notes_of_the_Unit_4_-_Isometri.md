### Conversion of pictorial view into orthographic Projection for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. **Orthographic projection** is a technique used to represent a three-dimensional object in two dimensions.
2. It is commonly used in engineering and technical drawings to convey the shape and size of an object in a clear and concise manner.
3. To convert a pictorial view into an orthographic projection, the object is first imagined to be enclosed within a glass box.
4. The object is then projected onto the sides of the box, resulting in a series of two-dimensional views.
5. Each view represents the object as seen from a different direction, typically the front, top, and side views.
6. These views are then arranged in a standard layout, with the front view at the bottom, the top view above it, and the side view to the right.
7. The resulting orthographic projection provides a complete and accurate representation of the object, allowing it to be easily understood and manufactured.
