### Transformation of Projections

Transformation of projections is a technique used in engineering graphics and design to represent a 3D object in 2D. This is achieved by projecting the object onto a 2D plane, such as a piece of paper or a computer screen. The transformation of projections is an important concept in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, particularly in the unit on Introduction to Computer Aided Design.

Some key points to remember about transformation of projections are:

1. The transformation of projections is used to represent 3D objects in 2D.
2. This is achieved by projecting the object onto a 2D plane.
3. The technique is important in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab.
4. It is particularly relevant in the unit on Introduction to Computer Aided Design.
