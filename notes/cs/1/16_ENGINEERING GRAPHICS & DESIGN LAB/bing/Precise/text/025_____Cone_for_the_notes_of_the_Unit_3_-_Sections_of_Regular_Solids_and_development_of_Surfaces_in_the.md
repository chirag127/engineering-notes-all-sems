### Cone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. A cone is a three-dimensional geometric shape that tapers smoothly from a flat base to a point called the apex or vertex.
2. The lateral surface of a cone is the curved surface that connects the base to the apex.
3. The axis of a cone is the straight line passing through the apex and the center of the base.
4. The height of a cone is the distance from the apex to the base along the axis.
5. The slant height of a cone is the distance from the apex to the edge of the base along the lateral surface.
6. The volume of a cone is calculated as one third of the product of the base area and the height.
7. The lateral surface area of a cone is calculated as the product of the base circumference and the slant height divided by two.
8. The total surface area of a cone is the sum of the base area and the lateral surface area.
9. A cone can be developed by cutting a sector from a circle and joining the two straight edges together.
10. The radius of the circle used to develop a cone is equal to the slant height of the cone.
11. The angle of the sector used to develop a cone is equal to the ratio of the base circumference to the slant height multiplied by 360 degrees.
