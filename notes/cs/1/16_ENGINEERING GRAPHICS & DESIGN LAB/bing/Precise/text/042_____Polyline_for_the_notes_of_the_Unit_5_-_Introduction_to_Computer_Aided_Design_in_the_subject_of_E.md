### Polyline
- A polyline is a continuous line composed of one or more line segments.
- It can be used to represent a variety of shapes, such as polygons, open or closed curves, and even 3D objects.
- In computer-aided design (CAD) software, polylines are often used to create complex shapes by joining multiple line segments together.
- Polylines can be edited by moving, adding, or deleting the vertices that define the line segments.
- They can also be manipulated using various CAD tools, such as offset, trim, and extend.
- Polylines are an essential tool in engineering graphics and design, allowing for the creation of precise and complex shapes.
