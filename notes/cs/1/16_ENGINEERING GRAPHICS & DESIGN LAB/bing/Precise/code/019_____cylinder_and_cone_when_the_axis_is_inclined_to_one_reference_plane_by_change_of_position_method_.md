### Cylinder and Cone when the Axis is Inclined to One Reference Plane by Change of Position Method

#### Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the Subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab

1. The change of position method is used to draw the projections of a cylinder or cone when its axis is inclined to one of the reference planes.
2. The first step is to draw the projections of the cylinder or cone when its axis is parallel to one of the reference planes.
3. The next step is to rotate the cylinder or cone about one of its ends until its axis is inclined to the reference plane at the desired angle.
4. The projections of the cylinder or cone are then redrawn to show the new position.
5. This method can be used to draw the projections of a cylinder or cone when its axis is inclined to the horizontal plane (HP) or the vertical plane (VP).
6. The change of position method is a useful technique for drawing the projections of objects with curved surfaces, such as cylinders and cones.
