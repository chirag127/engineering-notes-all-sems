### Scales for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. **Introduction to Engineering Drawing**: Engineering drawing is a graphical language used by engineers and other technical personnel to convey technical information.
2. **Orthographic Projections**: Orthographic projection is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
3. **Types of Scales**: There are several types of scales used in engineering drawing, including full scale, reduced scale, and enlarged scale.
4. **Full Scale**: Full scale drawings show the object at its actual size.
5. **Reduced Scale**: Reduced scale drawings show the object smaller than its actual size.
6. **Enlarged Scale**: Enlarged scale drawings show the object larger than its actual size.
7. **Choosing the Appropriate Scale**: The scale chosen for a drawing should be based on the size of the object being drawn and the level of detail required.
8. **Representing Scale on a Drawing**: The scale of a drawing is typically represented as a ratio, such as 1:1 for full scale or 1:2 for half scale.
9. **Using Scales in Orthographic Projections**: When creating orthographic projections, it is important to use the same scale for all views of the object to ensure accuracy and consistency.
