### Polyline
- A polyline is a continuous line composed of one or more line segments.
- It can be used to represent a variety of shapes, such as polygons, open or closed curves, and even 3D objects.
- In computer-aided design (CAD) software, a polyline is typically created by specifying the coordinates of its vertices.
- The line segments between the vertices can be straight or curved, depending on the software and the options selected by the user.
- Polylines can be edited by moving, adding, or deleting vertices, or by changing the properties of the line segments between them.
- They are commonly used in technical drawings, such as architectural plans, engineering schematics, and maps.
- In the context of the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, polylines can be used to create precise and accurate drawings for various design projects.
