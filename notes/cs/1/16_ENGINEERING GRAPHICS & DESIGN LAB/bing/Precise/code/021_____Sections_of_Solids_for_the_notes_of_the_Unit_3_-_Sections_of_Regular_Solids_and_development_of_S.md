### Sections of Solids

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 3 covers the topic of Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces. Here are some key points to remember about Sections of Solids:

1. A section of a solid is the shape obtained when a solid is cut by a plane.
2. The plane that cuts the solid is called the cutting plane or the section plane.
3. The shape of the section depends on the orientation of the cutting plane with respect to the solid.
4. The section can be represented graphically by drawing the intersection of the cutting plane with the solid.
5. The section can also be represented by hatching or shading the area of the section.
6. Different types of sections can be obtained by varying the orientation of the cutting plane.
7. Common types of sections include longitudinal, transverse, and oblique sections.
8. The development of a surface is the process of unfolding a 3D shape into a 2D shape.
9. The development of a surface is used to create patterns for making objects from sheet materials.
10. The development of a surface can be done by drawing the edges of the 3D shape on a 2D plane and connecting them in the correct order.
