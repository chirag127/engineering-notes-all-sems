### Projection of polygonal surface and circular lamina located in first quadrant inclined to one or both reference planes

1. Projection of plane surfaces like rectangle, square, pentagon, hexagon, circle- surfaces inclined to one reference plane .
2. Inclination of its surface with one of the reference planes will be given .
3. Inclination of one of its edges with other reference plane will be given (Hence this will be a case of an object inclined to both reference Planes) .
4. To draw their projections means F.V, T.V. & S.V .
5. Projection of plane circle, circular lamina inclined to both the planes can be drawn using change of position method .
