### Right Regular Solids and Auxiliary Views for the True Shape of the Sections such as Prism

Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab

1. A right regular solid is a polyhedron with all its faces being congruent regular polygons and all its solid angles being congruent.
2. The most common right regular solids are the Platonic solids, which are the tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron.
3. An auxiliary view is an orthographic view that is projected onto a plane that is not parallel to any of the principal planes of projection.
4. Auxiliary views are used to show the true shape and size of an inclined or oblique surface.
5. In the case of a prism, an auxiliary view can be used to show the true shape of a section of the prism.
6. To obtain the true shape of a section of a prism, an auxiliary plane is passed through the section and perpendicular to the cutting plane.
7. The auxiliary view is then projected onto this auxiliary plane to show the true shape of the section.
