### Projection of Straight Lines

In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 1 covers the topic of Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections. One of the important concepts in this unit is the projection of straight lines.

1. A straight line can be defined as the shortest distance between two points.
2. In engineering drawing, the projection of a straight line refers to the representation of the line on a plane.
3. The projection of a straight line can be obtained by joining the projections of its end points on the plane.
4. The projection of a straight line can be either parallel or perpendicular to the plane of projection.
5. If the line is parallel to the plane of projection, its projection will be a straight line of the same length as the original line.
6. If the line is perpendicular to the plane of projection, its projection will be a point.
7. If the line is inclined to the plane of projection, its projection will be a straight line shorter than the original line.
8. The angle between the line and its projection is called the angle of inclination.
9. The true length of a line can be determined by projecting it onto a plane perpendicular to the line.
