# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Engineering Graphics and Design Lab is a course that teaches students the fundamentals of technical drawing and design. The course covers topics such as:

1. **Orthographic Projection:** This is a method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is commonly used in engineering and architecture to create detailed drawings of objects.

2. **Isometric Projection:** This is another method of representing three-dimensional objects in two dimensions. It is commonly used in technical illustrations and video game design.

3. **Dimensioning and Tolerancing:** This involves specifying the size and geometric characteristics of an object. It is an important part of the design process, as it ensures that parts fit together correctly and function as intended.

4. **Computer-Aided Design (CAD):** This is the use of computer software to create, modify, analyze, and optimize a design. CAD is widely used in engineering, architecture, and product design.

5. **Engineering Design Process:** This is a systematic approach to solving engineering problems. It involves identifying the problem, researching and generating ideas, selecting the best solution, and testing and refining the design.

The Engineering Graphics and Design Lab provides students with hands-on experience in using these techniques and tools to create technical drawings and designs. It is an essential course for students pursuing a career in engineering or design.
