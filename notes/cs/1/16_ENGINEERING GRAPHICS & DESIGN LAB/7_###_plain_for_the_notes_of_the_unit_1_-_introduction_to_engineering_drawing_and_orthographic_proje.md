### Plain for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Plain:
- A flat surface with no curves or undulations.
- Used as a reference in engineering drawing to define the orientation of features.
- Essential for creating orthographic projections, which are 2D representations of 3D objects.
- Orthographic projections show multiple views of an object, such as top, front, and side views.
- Used in engineering and design to communicate ideas and plans clearly and accurately.
- Helps in the visualization and analysis of objects before they are manufactured.
