### Projection of Point for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Projection of Point is a fundamental concept in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab and is covered in the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections. The purpose of this unit is to introduce students to the basic principles of engineering drawing and to provide them with a solid foundation in the use of orthographic projections.

Orthographic projections are a type of two-dimensional representation of three-dimensional objects. They are used to create technical drawings that accurately represent the shape and size of an object, and they are an essential tool for engineers and designers.

In this unit, students will learn how to create orthographic projections of points, lines, and simple objects. They will also learn how to interpret these projections and use them to create accurate technical drawings.

The notes for this unit will cover the following topics:

1. Introduction to Engineering Drawing: Overview of the importance of engineering drawing and its role in the design and manufacturing process.

2. Orthographic Projections: Definition and explanation of orthographic projections, including first-angle and third-angle projections.

3. Projection of Points: Techniques for creating orthographic projections of points, including the use of the principle of projections and the projection of points onto a reference plane.

4. Projection of Lines: Techniques for creating orthographic projections of lines, including the use of the principle of projections and the projection of lines onto a reference plane.

5. Simple Object Projections: Techniques for creating orthographic projections of simple objects, including the use of the principle of projections and the projection of objects onto a reference plane.

By the end of this unit, students will have a solid understanding of the principles of engineering drawing and orthographic projections, and they will be able to create accurate technical drawings using these tools and techniques.
