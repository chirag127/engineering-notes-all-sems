### Projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Projection of lines inclined to one plane:

- Lines inclined to one plane are projected onto the plane of projection by extending them until they intersect the plane of projection.

- The resulting line is called a true length or full-size line.

- A true length line represents the actual length of the object line as it would appear if viewed from the front or top.

Projection of lines inclined to both planes:

- Lines inclined to both planes are projected onto the plane of projection by extending them until they intersect the plane of projection.

- The resulting line is called a true length or full-size line.

- A true length line represents the actual length of the object line as it would appear if viewed from the front or top.

- To obtain a full view of the object, multiple projections are taken from different angles and combined to form a complete image of the object.

- This is called multi-view projection and is commonly used in engineering drawings and blueprints.
