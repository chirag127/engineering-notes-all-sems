### Conversion of Isometric Views to Orthographic Views and Vice-Versa in AutoCAD for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

In AutoCAD, conversion of isometric views to orthographic views and vice-versa is a useful tool for creating and modifying 3D objects. Isometric views show a 3D representation of an object, while orthographic views show multiple 2D views from different angles. By converting between these views, the user can easily switch between a 3D representation and a set of 2D views, making it easier to understand and modify the object. In AutoCAD, this conversion can be done using the "Isometric Snap" or "3D Orbit" command. The notes for Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab should cover the steps to convert between isometric and orthographic views in AutoCAD, the benefits of doing so, and the importance of accurate representation in 3D design.
