Projection of Solids:

1. Definition: Projection of solids refers to the representation of a 3D object on a 2D plane.

2. Types: There are two types of projections for solids, namely, orthographic and isometric projections.

3. Orthographic Projection: It involves projecting the solid from three different directions, namely, top, front and side views, to create a complete 2D representation.

4. Isometric Projection: It involves creating a 3D representation of a solid through a 30-degree rotation along each axis.

5. Steps for Projection of Solids:
1. Identify the front view, top view, and side view of the solid.
2. Project the views onto the corresponding plane.
3. Use auxiliary lines and hidden lines to complete the representation.

6. Applications: Projection of solids is used in engineering graphics, product design, and technical drawings.

7. Importance: Projection of solids helps in visualizing and communicating complex 3D objects in a 2D format, making it easier to understand and interpret.
