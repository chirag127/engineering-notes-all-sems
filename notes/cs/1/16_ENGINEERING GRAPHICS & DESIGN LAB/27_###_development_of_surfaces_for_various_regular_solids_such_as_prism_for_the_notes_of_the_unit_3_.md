Development of Surfaces for Regular Solids:

In Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces, the development of surfaces for various regular solids such as prisms is an important topic. A regular solid is a three-dimensional object that has the same shape and size on all sides.

1. Prism: A prism is a regular solid that has two parallel and congruent bases that are connected by rectangular faces. The development of a surface for a prism involves creating a two-dimensional representation of the three-dimensional object. This can be done by unrolling the surface of the prism and laying it flat.

```
Steps for developing the surface of a prism:

1. Draw the top and bottom bases of the prism.
2. Draw the lateral faces of the prism by connecting the corresponding points on the top and bottom bases.
3. Unroll the surface of the prism and lay it flat.

```
