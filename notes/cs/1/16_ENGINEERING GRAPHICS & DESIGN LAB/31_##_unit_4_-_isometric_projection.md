## Unit 4 - Isometric Projection

Isometric Projection is a type of pictorial drawing that represents three-dimensional objects in two dimensions while maintaining the relative proportions of the object's height, width, and depth. It is an axonometric projection where the angle between the projection lines and the plane of projection is 120 degrees. This creates a unique visual style that is useful for technical illustrations, architectural drawings, and video game design. The main advantage of isometric projection is that it provides a clear and accurate representation of the object's shape and size, making it easier to understand the object's form and function. To create an isometric drawing, the object is rotated along one axis, typically the vertical axis, and then viewed from a fixed angle. The resulting image is a 2D representation of the 3D object that is easy to read and interpret.
