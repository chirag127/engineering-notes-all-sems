### Rectangle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Rectangle:
- A shape with four sides and four right angles.
- Used in computer-aided design (CAD) to create objects with straight lines and uniform dimensions.
- Often used as a basic building block for more complex shapes and designs.
- Can be defined by specifying its length, width, and position in 2D space.
- Used in engineering and design to create objects such as boxes, frames, and templates.
- Can be easily manipulated and modified in CAD software, making it a versatile and useful shape.
