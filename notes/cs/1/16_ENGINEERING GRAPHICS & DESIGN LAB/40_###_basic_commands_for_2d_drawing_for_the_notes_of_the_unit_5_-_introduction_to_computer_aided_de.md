### Basic commands for 2D drawing for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

The Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab typically covers the basics of 2D drawing using computer-aided design (CAD) software. The following are some of the basic commands that students may learn in this unit:

1. Line: This command allows students to draw straight lines of various lengths and angles.

2. Circle: This command allows students to draw circles of various sizes and center points.

3. Arc: This command allows students to draw arcs of various sizes, center points, and angles.

4. Rectangle: This command allows students to draw rectangles of various sizes and orientations.

5. Polygon: This command allows students to draw polyggon shapes of various sides and angles.

6. Text: This command allows students to add text to their drawings, including annotations, labels, and dimensions.

7. Zoom: This command allows students to zoom in and out of their drawings to view details more closely or to see the overall picture.

8. Pan: This command allows students to move the view of their drawings in any direction, allowing for easy navigation and editing.

These basic commands form the foundation for more advanced CAD techniques, such as 3D modeling, rendering, and animation. By learning these basic commands, students will be able to create simple 2D drawings and start to develop the skills and knowledge they need to create more complex designs.
