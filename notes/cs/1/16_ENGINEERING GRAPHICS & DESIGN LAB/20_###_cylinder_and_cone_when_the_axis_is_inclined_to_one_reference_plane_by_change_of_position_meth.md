### cylinder and cone when the axis is inclined to one reference plane by change of position method for the notes of the Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Cylinder:
- Cylinder is a solid with two parallel and congruent circular bases and a lateral surface that connects the two bases.
- When the axis of a cylinder is inclined to one reference plane, the change of position method is used to project it onto the reference plane. 
- Firstly, the cylinder is rotated about one of its bases until its axis becomes perpendicular to the reference plane. 
- Secondly, the cylinder is then projected onto the reference plane as a right circular cylinder. 

Cone:
- Cone is a solid with a circular base and a single vertex that is not on the base.
- When the axis of a cone is inclined to one reference plane, the change of position method is used to project it onto the reference plane. 
- Firstly, the cone is rotated about its vertex until its axis becomes perpendicular to the reference plane. 
- Secondly, the cone is then projected onto the reference plane as a right circular cone.
