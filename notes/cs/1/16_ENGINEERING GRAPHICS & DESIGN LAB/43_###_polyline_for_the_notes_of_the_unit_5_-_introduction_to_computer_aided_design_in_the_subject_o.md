### Polyline for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Polyline is a feature in the field of Computer Aided Design (CAD) that allows a user to create a series of connected line segments in a single continuous line. It is used in engineering graphics and design to create precise drawings and illustrations.

In the context of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Polyline is an important tool for students to learn as it allows them to create complex shapes and figures with ease and accuracy. The Polyline feature can be used to create a variety of shapes, including straight lines, arcs, and curves, and can be manipulated to create complex figures with multiple segments.

When using Polyline, students will learn how to use the various tools and commands available in CAD software to create and manipulate lines, including the ability to add, delete, and modify individual segments. They will also learn how to use the Polyline feature to create precise drawings and illustrations, including the ability to snap to grid lines and other reference points, and to control the thickness and color of lines.

In conclusion, Polyline is an essential tool for students learning about Computer Aided Design in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab. It provides a powerful and flexible way to create complex shapes and figures, and is an important tool for students to master as they learn to use CAD software to create precise and accurate drawings and illustrations.
