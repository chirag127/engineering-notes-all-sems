### Isometric projections of simple and combination of solids for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

"Isometric Projections" is a topic covered in Unit 4 of the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab. It focuses on the creation of isometric drawings of simple and complex objects, or "solids". 

An isometric projection is a type of 3D representation in which the object's height, width, and depth are shown in a single drawing, giving a more complete and accurate representation of the object.

The topics covered in this unit may include:

1. Simple Solids: The creation of isometric projections of simple objects, such as cubes, prisms, and cylinders.

2. Combination of Solids: The creation of isometric projections of complex objects, made up of multiple simple solids combined together.

3. Isometric Scales: The use of isometric scales to accurately measure and draw the dimensions of objects in an isometric projection.

4. Isometric Axes: The understanding of the isometric axes and how they are used to create accurate isometric projections.

5. Isometric Views: The creation of multiple isometric views of an object, showing different aspects and details of the object.

The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of isometric projections and the skills to create accurate and detailed isometric drawings of simple and complex objects. This knowledge is important for students pursuing careers in engineering and design, as isometric projections are commonly used in these fields to represent objects in a clear and accurate manner.
