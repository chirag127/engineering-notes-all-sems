### Cone for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Cone:
- A cone is a 3D geometric shape with a circular base and a single vertex.
- The height of a cone is the perpendicular distance from the vertex to the base.
- The slant height of a cone is the distance from the vertex to the circumference of the base.
- The lateral surface area of a cone is equal to the product of π and the slant height and the radius of the base.
- The volume of a cone is equal to one-third the product of π and the radius of the base and the height.
- The development of a cone is the process of flattening its lateral surface onto a plane, creating a 2D representation.
