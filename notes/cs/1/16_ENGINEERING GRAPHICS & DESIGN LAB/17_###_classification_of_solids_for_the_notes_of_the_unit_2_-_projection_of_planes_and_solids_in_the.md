Classification of Solids in Engineering Graphics & Design Lab:

Solids are three-dimensional objects that have length, width, and height. In the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, solids are classified based on their shape and number of faces. The following are the main classifications of solids:

1. Regular Solids: Regular solids are solids that have the same shape, size, and orientation of their faces. Examples of regular solids include cubes, pyramids, and spheres.

2. Irregular Solids: Irregular solids are solids that do not have the same shape, size, and orientation of their faces. Examples of irregular solids include cones, cylinders, and prisms.

3. Polyhedra: Polyhedra are solids that have flat faces and straight edges. Examples of polyhedra include cubes, pyramids, and prisms.

4. Non-Polyhedra: Non-polyhedra are solids that do not have flat faces and straight edges. Examples of non-polyhedra include spheres, cylinders, and cones.

5. Convex Solids: Convex solids are solids that have all of their interior angles less than 180 degrees. Examples of convex solids include cubes, spheres, and pyramids.

6. Concave Solids: Concave solids are solids that have at least one interior angle greater than 180 degrees. Examples of concave solids include cones and cylinders.

In conclusion, solids in Engineering Graphics & Design Lab are classified based on their shape and number of faces. Understanding the classification of solids is important for working with solids in this subject, as it helps to understand the different types of solids and their properties.
