
### Isometric Projection of Simple and Combination of Solids

2. Isometric projection of combination of solids is the representation of a combination of two or more solid objects in a three-dimensional space. It is an axonometric projection in which the three coordinate axes appear equally foreshortened and the angles between any two of them are 120 degrees. 

3. In isometric projection, the lines of projection are perpendicular to the plane of projection. The plane of projection is parallel to the isometric axes.

5. The isometric projection of simple solids can be represented by using cubes, cylinders, pyramids, and other regular solids.
