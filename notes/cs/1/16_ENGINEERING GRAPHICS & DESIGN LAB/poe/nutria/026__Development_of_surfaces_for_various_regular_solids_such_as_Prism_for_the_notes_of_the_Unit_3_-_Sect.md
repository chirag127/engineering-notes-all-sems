
### Development of Surfaces for Various Regular Solids

1. Prisms are three-dimensional shapes with two identical, parallel bases connected by flat faces. They are classified according to the number of faces they have.
    * Triangular prisms have three rectangular faces and two triangular faces.
    * Square prisms have four rectangular faces.
    * Pentagonal prisms have five rectangular faces and two pentagonal faces.
    * Hexagonal prisms have six rectangular faces and two hexagonal faces.
2. Pyramids are three-dimensional shapes with one base and triangular faces that meet at a point. They are classified according to the number of faces they have.
    * Triangular pyramids have four triangular faces.
    * Square pyramids have five triangular faces.
    * Pentagonal pyramids have six triangular faces.
    * Hexagonal pyramids have seven triangular faces.
3. Cylinders are three-dimensional shapes with two circular bases connected by a curved surface.
4. Cones are three-dimensional shapes with one circular base and one curved surface that meets at a point.
5. Spheres are three-dimensional shapes with a curved surface that is the same distance from a central point in all directions.

These shapes can be developed into two-dimensional surfaces by drawing the outlines of the faces. This process is known as "development of surfaces". It is used to create two-dimensional plans and elevations of three-dimensional objects. It is an important skill used in engineering graphics and design.
