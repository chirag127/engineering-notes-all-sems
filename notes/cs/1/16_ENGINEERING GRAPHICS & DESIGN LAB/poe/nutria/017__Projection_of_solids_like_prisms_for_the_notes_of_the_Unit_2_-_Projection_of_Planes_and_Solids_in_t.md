
### Projection of Solids like Prisms
1. Prisms are 3-dimensional objects that have two parallel and identical faces called bases, and the other faces are rectangles.
2. The bases can be either regular or irregular polygons.
3. The other faces of the prism are called lateral faces and they are all parallelograms.
4. The lateral faces of a prism can be either equal or unequal.
5. The height of the prism is the perpendicular distance between the bases.
6. The lateral edges of a prism are equal in length.
7. The lateral edges of a prism are equal in length and they form equal angles with the base.
8. The angle between the lateral faces of a prism is the same as the angle between the bases.
9. Prisms are classified according to the shape of their bases.
10. The most common types of prisms are triangular prisms, rectangular prisms, and pentagonal prisms.
