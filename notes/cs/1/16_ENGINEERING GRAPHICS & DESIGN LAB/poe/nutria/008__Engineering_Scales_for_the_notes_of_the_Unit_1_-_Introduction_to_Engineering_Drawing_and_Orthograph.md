
### Engineering Scales

1. An engineering scale is a tool used to accurately measure distances on a drawing.
2. There are a variety of engineering scales available, including English scales, metric scales, and combination scales.
3. The most common engineering scales are the English scales, which are divided into two categories: the inch scale and the foot scale.
4. The inch scale is divided into tenths, hundredths, and thousandths.
5. The foot scale is divided into halves, quarters, eighths, and sixteenths.
6. The metric scale is divided into millimeters, centimeters, and meters.
7. Combination scales are a combination of metric and English scales, and are often used in engineering drawing.
8. When using an engineering scale, it is important to remember to read the scale from the left to the right.
9. The length of the line on the drawing is measured by counting the number of divisions on the scale and then multiplying it by the scale value.
10. For example, if a line is measured on a 1/4 inch scale, and the line has four divisions, then the length of the line is one inch.
