
### Orthographic Projection

1. Orthographic projection is a type of engineering drawing that uses a two-dimensional representation of three-dimensional objects.

2. In orthographic projection, the object is projected onto a plane, and the resulting drawing is composed of two or more views of the object.

3. These views are typically the front, top, and side view of the object.

4. Orthographic projection is used to create engineering drawings that are used in the design and manufacture of products.

5. The views created in orthographic projection are used to accurately represent the size and shape of the object, and to provide measurements that can be used to create the product.

6. Orthographic projection is also used in the creation of 3D models, as it allows the modeler to accurately represent the object in a three-dimensional space.
