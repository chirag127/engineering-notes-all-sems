
### Pyramids

Pyramids are three-dimensional geometric shapes with four triangular faces. They are characterized by their apex, or the point on the top, and the base, which is the bottom surface. Pyramids are commonly used in engineering graphics and design for projection of planes and solids.

* Pyramids are made up of four triangular faces, each of which is connected to the apex at the top.
* The base of a pyramid is the bottom surface and can be of any shape.
* Pyramids are used in engineering graphics and design for projection of planes and solids.
* Pyramids can be classified as regular or irregular, depending on the shape and size of the faces.
* The faces of a regular pyramid are all the same size and shape.
* The faces of an irregular pyramid are of different sizes and shapes.
* Pyramids can be used to represent objects in three dimensions.
* Pyramids can be used to represent different views of an object, such as the top, front, and side views.
* Pyramids can be used to create 3D models of objects.
