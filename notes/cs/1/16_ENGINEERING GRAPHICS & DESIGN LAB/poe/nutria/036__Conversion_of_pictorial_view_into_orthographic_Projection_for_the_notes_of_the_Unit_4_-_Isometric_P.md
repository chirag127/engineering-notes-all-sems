
### Conversion of Pictorial View into Orthographic Projection

1. Orthographic projection is a type of projection used in engineering and technical drawing to represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
2. It is a form of parallel projection, in which all the projection lines are orthogonal to the projection plane, resulting in every plane of the scene appearing in affine transformation on the viewing surface.
3. In orthographic projection, there are three planes of projection: the top, front and side planes.
4. The top plane is known as the plan view, the front plane is known as the elevation view and the side plane is known as the profile view.
5. The plan view is an image of the object as seen from the top, the elevation view is an image of the object as seen from the front, and the profile view is an image of the object as seen from the side.
6. To convert a pictorial view into an orthographic projection, the object must be rotated so that the desired view is perpendicular to the view plane.
7. The object can then be projected onto the view plane using parallel projection lines.
8. The resulting orthographic projection is a two-dimensional representation of the object.
