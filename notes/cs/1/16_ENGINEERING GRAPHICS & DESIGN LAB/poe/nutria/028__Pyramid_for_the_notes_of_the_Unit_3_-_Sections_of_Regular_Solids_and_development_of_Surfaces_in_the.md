
### Pyramid 

* A pyramid is a three-dimensional object that has a square base and four triangular sides that meet at a single point at the top. 
* Pyramids can be regular or irregular in shape. Regular pyramids have all sides and angles of equal measure, while irregular pyramids have sides and angles of different measure. 
* Pyramids can be used to represent a variety of objects, such as buildings, mountains, and other structures. 
* Pyramids can also be used to represent sections of regular solids, such as cubes, octahedrons, and dodecahedrons. 
* Pyramids can also be used to develop surfaces, such as cones, cylinders, and spheres. 
* Pyramids can be constructed from a variety of materials including paper, wood, clay, and plastic. 
* Pyramids can be used in a variety of engineering and design applications, such as architectural models, furniture design, and product design.
