
### Classification of Solids

1. **Regular Solids**: These are solids in which all faces are regular polygons and all angles are equal. Examples of regular solids include cubes, tetrahedrons, octahedrons, and dodecahedrons.

2. **Irregular Solids**: These are solids in which the faces are not regular polygons and the angles are not equal. Examples of irregular solids include prisms, pyramids, cylinders, and cones.

3. **Combined Solids**: These are solids that are composed of two or more regular or irregular solids. Examples of combined solids include rectangular solids, hexagonal solids, and star-shaped solids.

4. **Plane Figures**: These are figures that lie in a single plane. Examples of plane figures include triangles, squares, and circles.
