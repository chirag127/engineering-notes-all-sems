
### Pyramid

1. Pyramids are three-dimensional objects with a polygon base and triangular faces that meet at a common vertex.
2. Regular pyramids are those with a regular polygon as the base.
3. The number of faces of a regular pyramid is equal to the number of sides on the base.
4. The lateral edges of a regular pyramid are all equal in length.
5. The slant height of a regular pyramid is the distance between the center of the base and the vertex.
6. The sum of the angles at the vertex of a regular pyramid is equal to two right angles.
7. The total surface area of a regular pyramid is the sum of the area of the base and the area of each of its faces.
8. The volume of a regular pyramid is one-third of the product of the area of the base and the height of the pyramid.
