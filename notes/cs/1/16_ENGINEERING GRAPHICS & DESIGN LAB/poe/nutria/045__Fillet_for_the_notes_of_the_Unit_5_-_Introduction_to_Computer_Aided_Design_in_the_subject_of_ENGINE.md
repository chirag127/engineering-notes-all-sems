
### Fillet 

* Fillet is a type of rounded corner used in engineering and architectural design. 
* It is used to create smooth transitions between two surfaces, or to add a decorative touch to a design.
* Fillets can be created manually by hand or with a CAD program. 
* In CAD, fillets are created by specifying a radius and two points on the edges of the surfaces that are to be joined. 
* The CAD program then creates a smooth transition between the two surfaces at the specified radius. 
* Fillets can also be created with a variety of other tools, such as chamfer bits or router bits. 
* Fillets are often used in mechanical engineering designs to reduce stress concentrations, as well as in architectural designs to create a more aesthetically pleasing look.
