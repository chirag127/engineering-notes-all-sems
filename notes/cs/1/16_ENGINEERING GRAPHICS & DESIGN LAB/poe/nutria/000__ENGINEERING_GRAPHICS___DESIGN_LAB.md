
# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Engineering Graphics & Design Lab is a course that focuses on the application of engineering graphics and design principles in the development of engineering products.

2. The course includes topics such as engineering drawing conventions, dimensioning, tolerancing, materials selection and manufacturing processes.

3. Students will learn to create and interpret engineering drawings, use CAD software to create 2D and 3D models, and use design principles to solve engineering problems.

4. The course also covers engineering design processes, such as brainstorming, prototyping, and testing.

5. The course will also cover topics such as product design and development, industrial design, and engineering economics.

6. The course will also provide students with the opportunity to develop their own engineering projects.
