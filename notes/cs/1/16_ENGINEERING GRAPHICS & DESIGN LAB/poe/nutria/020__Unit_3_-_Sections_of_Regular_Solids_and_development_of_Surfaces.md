
## Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and Development of Surfaces

* Regular solids are three-dimensional figures that have all of their faces the same size and shape. Examples of regular solids include cubes, cuboids, pyramids, prisms, and cylinders.
* Development of surfaces is the process of unfolding a three-dimensional shape into two dimensions. This is done by cutting and flattening the shape, then tracing it onto paper.
* Sections of regular solids are the two-dimensional shapes that are created when a regular solid is cut. This can be done in various ways, such as slicing it in half, slicing it along its edges, or slicing it in a particular plane.
* The cross-sections of a regular solid can be used to identify the shape. For example, a cube will have six square cross-sections, while a pyramid will have four triangular cross-sections.
* The development of surfaces can also be used to identify the shape of a regular solid. For example, a cube will have six square surfaces when unfolded, while a pyramid will have four triangular surfaces when unfolded.
