
### Extend for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Computer Aided Design (CAD) is a computer-based tool used to create, modify, analyze, and optimize designs. It is used in a variety of industries, including engineering, architecture, and product design.

2. CAD software can be used to create 3D models of objects, as well as 2D drawings of parts and assemblies. It can also be used for analysis, such as stress and fatigue analysis, and for optimization, such as weight reduction and cost optimization.

3. CAD software is used to create designs, but it can also be used to simulate and analyze the performance of the design. This includes simulating the motion of parts, analyzing the strength and durability of the design, and optimizing the design for cost and weight.

4. CAD software is used to create drawings for manufacturing, such as CNC machining, 3D printing, and injection molding. It is also used to create drawings for fabrication, such as welding, bending, and cutting.

5. CAD software is used to create drawings for assembly, such as assembly drawings, exploded views, and bill of materials. It is also used to create drawings for inspection, such as inspection plans and checklists.

6. CAD software is used to create drawings for documentation, such as product manuals and user guides. It is also used to create drawings for marketing, such as product renderings and animations.
