
### Dimensioning for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the Subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Dimensioning is the process of providing numerical values to the components of a drawing.
2. Dimension lines are used to indicate the size of a feature or part. They are usually drawn as a thin line with arrows at each end.
3. The arrows indicate the direction in which the dimension is to be measured.
4. The dimension value is usually written in the middle of the dimension line.
5. Dimension lines should be drawn parallel to the viewing plane.
6. Dimension lines should not cross each other.
7. The most common type of dimension is the linear dimension. This is used to indicate the length of a line or the distance between two points.
8. Angular dimensions are used to indicate the angle between two lines or planes.
9. Radial dimensions are used to indicate the radius of a circle or arc.
10. Orthographic projections are a type of drawing that uses two-dimensional views to represent three-dimensional objects.
11. Orthographic projections are used to create technical drawings and engineering drawings.
12. Orthographic projections are also used to create illustrations and diagrams.
