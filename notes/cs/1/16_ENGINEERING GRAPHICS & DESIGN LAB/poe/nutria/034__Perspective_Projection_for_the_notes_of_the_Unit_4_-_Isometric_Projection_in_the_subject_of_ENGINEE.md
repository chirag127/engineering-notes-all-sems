
### Perspective Projection

* Perspective projection is a type of projection used to represent three-dimensional objects in two-dimensional drawings. 
* It is based on the principle that objects appear smaller as their distance from the observer increases. 
* The technique is used to create the illusion of depth in a two-dimensional drawing. 
* In perspective projection, the lines of projection are parallel and the vanishing point is located at an infinite distance. 
* The most common type of perspective projection is the one-point perspective, in which all lines of projection converge at one point. 
* In two-point perspective, the lines of projection converge at two points, while in three-point perspective, the lines of projection converge at three points. 
* Perspective projection is used in engineering graphics and design to represent three-dimensional objects in two-dimensional drawings. 
* It is also used in photography and cinematography to create the illusion of depth.
