
### Trim for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

* Trim is a feature in Computer Aided Design (CAD) software that allows the user to remove unnecessary parts of a design.
* Trim can be used to remove edges, faces, or entire components from a design.
* Trim can also be used to adjust the size of a design by removing unwanted parts.
* Trim is a fast and efficient way to adjust the shape of a design without having to redraw it.
* Trim can also be used to join two separate parts of a design together.
* Trim can be used to create complex shapes and curves with precision.
* Trim can be used to make precise measurements and adjustments to a design.
