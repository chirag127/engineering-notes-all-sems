
## Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids

1. Projection of a point on a plane: A point can be projected on a plane by drawing a line perpendicular to the plane from the point. The point of intersection of the line and the plane is the projection of the point on the plane.

2. Projection of a straight line on a plane: A straight line can be projected on a plane by drawing a line perpendicular to the plane from any point on the line. The point of intersection of the line and the plane is the projection of the line on the plane.

3. Projection of a plane on another plane: A plane can be projected on another plane by drawing a line perpendicular to the plane from any point on the plane. The point of intersection of the line and the other plane is the projection of the plane on the other plane.

4. Projection of a solid on a plane: A solid can be projected on a plane by drawing a line perpendicular to the plane from any point on the solid. The point of intersection of the line and the plane is the projection of the solid on the plane.
