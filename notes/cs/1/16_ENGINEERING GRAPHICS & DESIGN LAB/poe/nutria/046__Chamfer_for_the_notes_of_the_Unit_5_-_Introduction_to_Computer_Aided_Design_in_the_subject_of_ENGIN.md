
### Chamfer

Chamfer is a technique used in Computer Aided Design (CAD) to create a beveled edge on a 3D object. It is commonly used to create a smooth transition between two surfaces or to create a decorative edge.

The chamfer tool is available in most CAD software packages and is used to create a beveled edge by adding a sloped surface between two edges. The chamfer tool can be used to create a variety of shapes, such as a V-shaped notch or a rounded corner.

The chamfer tool can also be used to create a decorative edge on an object. This can be used to give an object a more finished look or to add a unique design element.

Chamfers can be used in a variety of applications, from industrial designs to architectural designs. They are also used in the production of furniture and other items.
