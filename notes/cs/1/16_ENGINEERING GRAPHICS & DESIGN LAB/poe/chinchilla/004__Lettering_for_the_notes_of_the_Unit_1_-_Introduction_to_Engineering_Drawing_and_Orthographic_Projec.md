### Lettering for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

In the world of engineering drawing, lettering plays an essential role in conveying information, making it a crucial skill to master. Here are some key points to keep in mind when it comes to lettering:

1. Legibility is key. The purpose of lettering is to communicate information effectively, so it's crucial that your lettering is clear and easy to read. Make sure your letters are well-formed and consistent in size and style.

2. Use the proper tools. To achieve legible lettering, you'll need the right tools, including a good-quality pen or pencil with a fine point, and a ruler or straight edge to help keep your lines straight.

3. Choose the right style. There are several styles of lettering commonly used in engineering drawing, including Gothic, Roman, and Script. Each style has its own unique characteristics, so choose the one that best suits your needs.

4. Use guidelines. To keep your lettering consistent and uniform, it's a good idea to use guidelines to help keep your letters straight and aligned. You can use a ruler or straight edge to draw these guidelines lightly on your paper before you start lettering.

5. Practice, practice, practice. Like any skill, lettering takes practice to master. Set aside time to practice your lettering regularly, and don't be discouraged if it takes time to get the hang of it.

Remember, good lettering is an essential component of effective engineering drawing, so take the time to develop this skill and make sure your lettering is clear, legible, and consistent.
