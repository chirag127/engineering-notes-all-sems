# ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Engineering Graphics & Design Lab is an essential part of the engineering curriculum. The lab provides students with a practical understanding of the concepts learned in the theoretical classes. In this lab, students learn how to create, design, and analyze engineering drawings and models using various software tools. The following are some of the key points that students must keep in mind while studying for their Engineering Graphics & Design Lab exams:

## Understanding the basics

- The lab is focused on providing students with practical exposure to engineering graphics and design principles.
- Students must have a good understanding of engineering drawing standards, projections, and dimensioning to perform well in the lab.
- They must also be familiar with the use of various drawing instruments, such as T-squares, triangles, compasses, and protractors.

## CAD software

- Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software is extensively used in the lab for creating and analyzing engineering models and drawings.
- Students must be proficient in using CAD software such as AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and CATIA to create 3D models and 2D drawings.
- They must also be able to use the software tools for dimensioning, scaling, and creating cross-sections of the models.

## Design principles

- The lab also covers various design principles such as form, function, aesthetics, and ergonomics.
- Students must understand the importance of these principles in engineering design and how they impact the final product.
- They must be able to apply these principles while designing products and creating engineering models.

## Analysis and testing

- The lab also covers the analysis and testing of engineering models.
- Students must be able to analyze the models for strength, durability, and performance using tools such as Finite Element Analysis (FEA).
- They must also be able to perform various tests such as stress testing, fatigue testing, and vibration testing on the models.

## Project work

- The lab also includes project work where students are required to design and create a model or drawing based on a given problem statement.
- Students must be able to apply the concepts and principles learned in the lab to solve the problem and create a functional and aesthetically pleasing product.
- The project work is evaluated based on various parameters such as design, functionality, aesthetics, and innovation.

In conclusion, Engineering Graphics & Design Lab is a crucial part of the engineering curriculum, and students must take it seriously to gain practical exposure to engineering graphics and design principles. By understanding the basics, mastering CAD software, applying design principles, and performing analysis and testing, students can excel in the lab and become successful engineers.
