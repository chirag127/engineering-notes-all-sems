### Rectangle

In Computer Aided Design (CAD), a rectangle is a closed shape with four straight sides, where the opposite sides are parallel and equal in length. Rectangles are commonly used in engineering drawings and designs for various purposes.

Below are some important points to note about rectangles in CAD:

- To draw a rectangle in CAD, select the rectangle tool from the toolbar or type the command RECTANGLE in the command line.
- The rectangle tool requires two inputs: the first input is the starting point of the rectangle, and the second input is the opposite corner of the rectangle. Alternatively, you can specify the length and width of the rectangle.
- The rectangle tool can also be used to draw squares, which are rectangles with equal length and width. To draw a square, specify the length and width as equal values.
- Rectangles can be modified in CAD by changing their dimensions, position, or orientation. You can use the modify tools such as stretch, move, rotate, and scale to make these changes.
- In CAD, rectangles can be used to represent various objects such as doors, windows, tables, and frames. They are also used in the construction of geometric shapes such as polygons and circles.
- To add text or dimensions to a rectangle, use the text or dimension tools in CAD.
- In CAD, rectangles can be filled with colors or patterns to represent different materials or textures.

In conclusion, rectangles are an important shape in CAD and are used extensively in engineering drawings and designs. Understanding how to draw and modify rectangles is essential for creating accurate and detailed designs.
