### Projection of Point

In Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections, the projection of a point is a fundamental concept that is used extensively in creating technical drawings. A point can be projected onto a plane or a surface, and the resulting projection can be used to create a two-dimensional representation of a three-dimensional object.

Here are some key points to understand the concept of Projection of Point:

- A point is a location in space that has no size or dimension.
- In Engineering Drawing, a point is represented by a dot.
- A projection is a representation of an object in two dimensions.
- A projection of a point is the image of that point on a plane or surface.
- There are two types of projections: orthogonal projection and perspective projection.
- Orthogonal projection is used in Engineering Drawing and involves projecting the object onto a series of planes that are perpendicular to each other.
- Perspective projection is used in art and involves projecting the object onto a plane at an angle to create a sense of depth and distance.
- The projection of a point can be determined by drawing a line from the point to the plane or surface onto which it is being projected.
- The point at which this line intersects the plane or surface is the projection of the point.
- The projection of a point is always perpendicular to the plane or surface onto which it is being projected.
- The projection of a point can be used to determine the position of the point in three-dimensional space.
- In Orthographic Projections, the projection of a point can be used to create a series of views that represent the object from different angles.

In conclusion, the projection of a point is a crucial concept in Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections. It is essential to understand the principles of projection in order to accurately represent three-dimensional objects in two dimensions.
