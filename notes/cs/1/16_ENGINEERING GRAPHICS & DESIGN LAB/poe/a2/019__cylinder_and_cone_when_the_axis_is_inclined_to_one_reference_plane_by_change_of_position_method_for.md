### Cylinder and cone when the axis is inclined to one reference plane by change of position method

- Take the cylinder/cone in the first view.
- Imagine the cylinder/cone to be inclined to the VP by rotating it about the horizontal/vertical trace.
- Draw the traces of the inclined plane.
- Project the cylinder/cone onto the inclined plane.
- Obtain the true shape of the base by drawing the ellipse by revolving the circle about the axis of inclination.
- Project the cylinder/cone onto the horizontal/VP.
- Label all the parts.

This method is used when the axis of the cylinder/cone is parallel either to HP or VP. By imagining the rotation of the cylinder/cone, the projections can be obtained easily. This method can also be used to draw the projections of cylinder/cone when the axis is inclined to both the planes.
