### Conversion of pictorial view into orthographic Projection

1. To convert an isometric view into an orthographic projection, first identify the isometric axes i.e. X', Y' and Z' axes.
2. Then project the isometric view onto the corresponding orthographic planes i.e. X-Y plane, Y-Z plane and Z-X plane.
3. Now label the points on the isometric view with the corresponding point labels in the orthographic view.
4. Then drop perpendiculars from the labeled points in the isometric view onto the respective orthographic planes.
5. Mark the intersection of the perpendiculars on the respective orthographic planes to get the orthographic projection view.
6. Connect the points in the orthographic projection to get the required view.
7. Similarly, the other two orthographic views can be obtained by projecting the isometric view onto the other two orthographic planes.

The steps are explained with illustrative examples for better understanding. The isometric view and the corresponding orthographic projection views are marked and properly labeled for clarity.
