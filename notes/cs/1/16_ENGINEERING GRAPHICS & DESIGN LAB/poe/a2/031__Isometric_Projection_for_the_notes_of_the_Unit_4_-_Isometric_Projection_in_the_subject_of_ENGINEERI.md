### Isometric Projection

Unit 4 - Isometric Projection
ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Isometric projection is a type of parallel projection. In isometric projection, the axes are equally inclined to each other, making equal angles with the plane of projection.
2. The isometric scale along x, y and z axes are equal. Hence, the measurements along any two axes are equal to the actual measurements.
3. To draw an isometric view of an object:
- Draw the isometric axes. The axes make angles of 120° with each other.
- Mark the measurements of the object on the corresponding axes.
- Join the marks to get the isometric view of the object.
4. To visualize an isometric view of a 3D object:
- Imagine viewing the 3D object at an angle such that all the 3 axes are equally inclined to the viewing direction.
- The lines parallel to xy-plane appear equally inclined and foreshortened.
- Equal measurements along any 2 axes appear equal in the isometric view.
5. Applications: Isometric projection is useful to represent 3D objects in 2D drawings, especially in technical drawings of mechanical parts. It preserves the relative measurements of the object, hence useful for dimensioning.
