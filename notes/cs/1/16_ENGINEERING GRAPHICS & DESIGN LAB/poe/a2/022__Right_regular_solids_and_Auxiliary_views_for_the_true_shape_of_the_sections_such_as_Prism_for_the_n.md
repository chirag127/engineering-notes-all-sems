### Right regular solids and Auxiliary views for the true shape of the sections such as Prism for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB:

1. Right Regular Solids:
- A solid bounded by regular polygon faces equally spaced around a point is called a regular solid.
- There are five right regular solids:
    - Tetrahedron: 4 triangular faces
    - Cube: 6 square faces
    - Octahedron: 8 triangular faces
    - Dodecahedron: 12 pentagonal faces
    - Icosahedron: 20 triangular faces

2. Auxiliary Views:
- To visualize the true shape of a section of a 3D object, auxiliary views are used.
- The section plane is aligned with one of the main planes of projection and the true shape is projected on the corresponding auxiliary plane.
- For example, to get the true shape of a section on the XY plane, the section is projected on the vertical auxiliary plane.

3. Development of Surfaces:
- The development of a surface is the 2D pattern that can be folded or rolled into the 3D shape of the surface.
- The development of the curved surface of prisms and pyramids can be found and used for construction and fabrication purposes.
- The development is obtained by imagining that the surface is made of flexible sheet and then flattening it out. Lines of curvature are marked on the development to show how to fold it back into 3D shape.
