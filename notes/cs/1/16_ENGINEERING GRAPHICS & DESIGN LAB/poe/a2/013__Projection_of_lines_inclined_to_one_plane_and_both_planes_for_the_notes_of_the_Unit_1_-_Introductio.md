### Projection of lines inclined to one plane and both planes

1. Lines inclined to one plane:
- When a line is inclined to only one plane, it will intersect that plane in a point and make an acute angle with that plane.
- The line will be projected as a shorter line and its true length will not be shown in the view.
- The ray of projection will pass through the end point of the line which is nearer to the plane.

2. Lines inclined to both planes:
- When a line is inclined to both the planes, it will intersect both the planes in two points and will make acute angles with both the planes.
- In this case, both the projections of the line will be foreshortened. The ray of projection will pass through the end point which is nearer to the corresponding plane.
- The true length of the line will not be shown in either of the views.
