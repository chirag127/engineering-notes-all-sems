### Polyline

- A polyline is a series of line segments created as a single object.
- To draw a polyline, specify multiple points in the drawing area.
- A polyline does not have a defined shape and can have any number of sides. It can be open or closed.
- To close a polyline and form a polygon, specify the first point again as the last point.
- The properties of a polyline such as color, layer, and line type can be changed as a single unit after it has been drawn.
- Polylines are useful for creating outlines of objects or for divining a shape into segments in CAD software.
- Polylines can be smoothed, trimmed, offset, or breakpoint-edited like lines and arcs.
