### Sections of Solids

1. Longitudinal section: A section parallel to the axis of the solid. It shows the internal details of the object.
2. Transverse section: A section perpendicular to the axis of the solid. It shows the cross-sectional view and internal details of the object.
3. Oblique section: A slanted section that is at an angle to the axis of the solid. It shows the slanted cut surface and internal details of the object.

The sections are used to show the internal details and components of a 3D solid that would otherwise not be visible from the exterior. They are useful for parts Assembly and manufacturing.

The development of surfaces involves unrolling a 3D surface into a 2D plane. It is useful to find the flat pattern of surfaces to cut the material. The developed surface equals the area of the 3D surface. The development of certain surfaces like cubes, prisms, pyramids, cylinders, and cones can be achieved using geometrical methods.

The sections and development of surfaces are key topics under 3D visualization and spatial sense. They are essential skills for engineering graphics and design.
