### Classification of solids for the notes of the Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Simple solids:
- Cube: 6 equal square faces, 12 equal edges, 8 vertices
- Cuboid: 6 rectangular faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices
- Prism: 2 equal and parallel faces called bases, lateral faces are parallelograms
- Cylinder: Circular base and curved lateral surface
- Pyramid: Polygonal base and triangular lateral faces meeting at a point called apex
- Cone: Circular base and curved lateral surface tapering to a point called apex
- Sphere: Curved surface consisting of points equidistant from a fixed point called centre

2. Composite solids: Formed by combining two or more simple solids
- Hollow solids: Formed by combining two simple solids, leaving space in between
3. Irregular solids: Solids not defined by any standard formula and do not have any symmetry
