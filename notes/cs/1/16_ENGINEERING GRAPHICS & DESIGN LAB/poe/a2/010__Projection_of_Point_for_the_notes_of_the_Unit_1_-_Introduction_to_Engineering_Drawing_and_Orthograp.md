### Projection of Point for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. A point has no dimensions. It merely indicates a location in space.
2. To show the precise location of a point, it is projected onto 2 or more planes of projection to get its projections.
3. The projections of a point on two perpendicular planes are obtained by drawing lines from the given point perpendicular to the planes of projection. The feet of these perpendiculars are the required projections.
4. The First Angle Projection and Third Angle Projection are the two methods employed. In First Angle Projection, the projecting lines are drawn from the point to the planes of projections. In Third Angle Projection, projectors are drawn from the planes of projection to the point.
5. In multi-view drawings, more than two projections may be shown to indicate the exact spatial location of the point. The number of views will depend on the complexity of the object.
