### Projection of polygonal surface and circular lamina located in first quadrant inclined to one or both reference planes

1. Polygonal surface:
- Surface bounded by straight lines
- Lines forming sides of polygon
- Polygon taken in first quadrant and inclined to HP or VP or both
- Projection: Tracing edges of polygon on corresponding planes
- True shape: Naming polygon in its original position and projecting to get true shape

2. Circular lamina:
- Surface bounded by curved line (circle)
- Circle taken in first quadrant and inclined to HP or VP or both
- Projection: Tracing circumference of circle on corresponding planes
- True shape: Naming circle in its original position and projecting to get true shape
