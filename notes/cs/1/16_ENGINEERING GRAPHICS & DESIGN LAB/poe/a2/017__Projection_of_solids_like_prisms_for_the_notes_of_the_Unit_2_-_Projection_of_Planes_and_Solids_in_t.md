### Projection of solids like prisms for the notes of the Unit 2 - Projection of Planes and Solids in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Prisms: A prism is a polyhedron with two parallel, congruent faces (bases) and other faces (lateral faces) that are parallelograms.
2. Types of prisms:
    1. Right prism: A prism with lateral edges perpendicular to the bases.
    2. Oblique prism: A prism with lateral edges non-perpendicular to the bases.
3. Projection of prisms:
    1. Project the perimeter of one base.
        1. Project the center point of the base.
        2. Project other vertices of the base and join to get the projected shape of the base.
    2. Project the lateral edges.
        1. Project the end points of the edges and join to get the projected lateral edges.
    3. Project the other base and join with the projected lateral edges to get the projection of the entire prism.
4. True shape and projection of right and oblique prisms can be studied similarly by projecting the edges and vertices.
5. Revolution of prisms about an axis produces prismoids which can also be studied similarly.
