### Basic commands for 2D drawing for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Draw line - To draw a straight line between two points.
2. Draw polyline - To draw a series of connected straight lines.
3. Draw arc - To draw a portion of a circle.
4. Draw circle - To draw a complete circle.
5. Draw ellipse - To draw an oval shape.
6. Draw spline - To draw a smooth curve that passes through a set of points.
7. Draw revision cloud - To draw a polyline that encloses an irregularly shaped area.
8. Draw rectangle - To draw a four-sided shape with opposite sides equal and angles 90 degrees.
9. Draw polygon - To draw a multi-sided shape.
10. Draw hatch - To fill an enclosed area with a pattern.

The commands can be used with specific measurements and angles to draw the required 2D shape. The properties of the drawn objects like color, thickness, etc. can be modified as required. These basic drawing commands are essential to create technical drawings of parts, assemblies and machines.
