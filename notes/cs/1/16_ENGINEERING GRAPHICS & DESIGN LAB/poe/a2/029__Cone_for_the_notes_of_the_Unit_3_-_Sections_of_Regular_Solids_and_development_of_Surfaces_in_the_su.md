### Cone

- A cone is a 3D solid shape with a circular base and a pointed top.
- The pointed top is called the apex or vertex. The circular base is called the base circle.
- The line joining the apex and the center of the base circle is called the axis of the cone.
- The slant height of a cone is the distance between the apex and the edge of the base circle.
- The height of a cone is the length of a line drawn from the apex perpendicular to the base circle.
- To draw the development of a cone:
- Draw a sector of the base circle
- Draw radial lines from the center of the base circle to the edge of the sector
- Extend the radial lines upward and join their extended portions to form the slant height of the cone
- The development will be in the shape of a sector of a circle
- The developed view can be rolled back to form the actual 3D shape of the cone.
