### Orthographic representation of perspective views — Plane figures and simple solids — Visual Ray Method for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Orthographic Projection: It is a method of representing three dimensional objects in two dimensions. It consists of projecting an object onto a plane surface by means of projecting lines at right angles to the plane surface. This results in accurate size and shape descriptions but lacks pictorial appearance.

2. Isometric Projection: It is a type of axonometric projection where three sides of an object are shown equally foreshortened. The isometric axes are 120° apart. This results in a sharp and clear three dimensional effect with measurable drawings.

3. Visual Ray Method: This method involves visualizing rays of light from the observers eye to the object. The rays are then projected onto the drawing sheet. This results in a perspective view which gives a three dimensional effect along with visual depth. However, the drawings are not measurable.

The notes cover the following in detail:

1. Concept of orthographic projection and its types - multiview drawings and principal views.
2. Isometric scale and isometric projections of points and lines.
3. Isometric projections of planes - triangles, circles and combination of planes.
4. Isometric projections of simple solids - cubes, prisms, pyramids, cylinders and spheres.
5. Visual ray method for perspective projections of plane figures and solids.
