### Chamfer for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Chamfer is a transitional edge between two surfaces. It is made by cutting off the sharp edge or corner of a shape to make a beveled edge.
2. Chamfer helps to avoid stress concentration and makes the design aesthetically appealing.
3. In CAD, chamfer can be applied on straight edges, rounded edges or curved edges.
4. The size and shape of the chamfer can be defined by the user. It can be symmetrical or asymmetrical.
5. Chamfer helps in designing mechanical components as it increases the strength and durability and provides a finished look.
6. In CAD software, the chamfer tool can be used to create chamfers of different sizes by specifying the chamfer distance and angle. The chamfer can be applied on multiple edges simultaneously using the chamfer tool.
