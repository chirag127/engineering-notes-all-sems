### Fillet for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Fillet is a feature in CAD software that is used to round the edges of 2 intersecting lines or surfaces. It gives a curved corner instead of a sharp corner.
2. To apply fillet:
- Select the Fillet tool from the toolbar or menu.
- Select the 2 lines or surfaces you want to fillet.
- Enter the fillet radius value. The radius depends on the sharpness you want for the corner. Larger radius gives a wider curve.
- Click on the corner you want to fillet.
- The fillet will be applied on the selected corner.
3. Benefits of using fillet:
- It strengthens the design by removing sharp corners which can lead to stress concentrations.
- It gives a smooth finish and aesthetic appeal to the design.
- It can be used to modify an existing sharp corner to a round corner as per the requirements.

4. Notes:
- Fillet can be applied to intersecting lines, arcs or splines.
- The radius value should be within the limits allowed by the intersecting elements otherwise the fillet cannot be created.
- Multiple fillets of different radii can be applied in a single design based on the corner requirements.
