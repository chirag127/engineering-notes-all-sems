### Engineering Scales

- Engineering scales are instruments used for measuring distances and transferring measurements from one drawing to another.
- The most common types of engineering scales are:
- Plain scale: Has graduations of equal spacing to measure distances.
- Vernier scale: Has a sliding scale that allows measurement of fractional parts of graduations on the main scale. More precise than plain scale.
- Triangular scale: Has multiple scales in different ratios on a triangular strip. Used for measuring and plotting various scales.
- Architect scale: Has scales in ratios of 1:100, 1:50, 1:25, etc. Used by architects.
- Metric scale: Has graduations in millimeters and centimeters.
