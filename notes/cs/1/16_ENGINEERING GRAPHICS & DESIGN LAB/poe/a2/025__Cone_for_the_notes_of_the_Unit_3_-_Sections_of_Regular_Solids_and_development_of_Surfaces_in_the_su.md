### Cone

- A cone is a 3D shape with a circular base and a curved side that narrows towards a point called the apex or vertex.
- The height of a cone is the distance from the base to the apex.
- The slant height of a cone is the distance from the edge of the base to the apex.
- To construct a cone, draw a circle and mark a point above the circle for the apex. Connect the apex to the edge of the circle.
- The development of a cone is a sector of a circle. To obtain the development, unfold the cone along a slant height and flatten it out. The arc of the sector represents the slant height of the cone.
- Cone surfaces can be divided into frustums to calculate their surface areas and volumes. A frustum is the portion of a cone between two parallel planes.
- Formulas:
 - Surface area of cone = πrl + πr^2
 - Volume of cone = (1/3)πr^2h
 - Surface area of frustum = π(R^2 + r^2 + Rr)
 - Volume of frustum = (1/3)πh(R^2 + Rr + r^2)
