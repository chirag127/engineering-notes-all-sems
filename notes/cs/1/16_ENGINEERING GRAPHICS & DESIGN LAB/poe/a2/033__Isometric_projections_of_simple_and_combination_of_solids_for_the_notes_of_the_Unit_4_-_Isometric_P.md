### Isometric projections of simple and combination of solids for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Draw the isometric view of a cube.
- Draw one face of the cube.
- Draw the adjacent face at an angle of 120 degree.
- Complete the cube by drawing the remaining faces.
- Label the axes as X, Y and Z.

2. Draw the isometric view of a cylinder.
- Draw a circle which represents the base of the cylinder.
- Draw two parallel lines on either side of the circle to represent the curved surface of the cylinder.
- Label the axes as X, Y and Z.

3. Draw the isometric view of a combination of solids like:
- A cube resting on a cylinder.
- Two cubes joined together.
- A cylinder resting on a cube.

Follow the steps:
1. Draw the isometric view of one solid.
2. Without changing the direction of axes, draw the isometric view of the other solid.
3. Ensure there is no gap in between the solids.
4. Label the axes as X, Y and Z.
