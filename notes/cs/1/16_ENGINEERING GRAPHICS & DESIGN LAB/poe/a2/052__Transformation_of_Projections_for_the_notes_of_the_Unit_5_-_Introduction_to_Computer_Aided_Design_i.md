### Transformation of Projections for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Translation: It is the movement of an object from one position to another in a straight path. The new position of the object can be specified with respect to the old position by giving the magnitude and direction of translation.
2. Rotation: It is the movement of an object about an axis resulting in a change in orientation. The angle of rotation and the axis of rotation must be specified to define the rotation of an object.
3. Scaling: It is the expansion or contraction of an object with respect to one or more of its dimensions. The scale factor determines the magnitude of scaling.
4. Reflection: It is the flipping of an object about a plane. The orientation of the plane of reflection must be specified to define the reflection of an object.

The transformation of projections can be achieved by applying the transformation on the projections of the 3D objects. The transformed projections will represent the appearance of the 3D objects after transformation. This is useful in performing various design and analysis tasks on 3D objects using their 2D representations.
