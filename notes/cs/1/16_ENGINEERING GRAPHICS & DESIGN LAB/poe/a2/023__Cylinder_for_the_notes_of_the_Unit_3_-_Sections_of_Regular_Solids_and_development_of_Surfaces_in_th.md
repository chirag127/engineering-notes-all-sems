### Cylinder for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. Cylinder: A cylinder is a three-dimensional solid shape with a circular base and straight sides. The diameter and height are the important dimensions of a cylinder.
2. Types of cylinders:
 - Right circular cylinder: Has a circular base and sides perpendicular to the base.
 - Oblique cylinder: Has a circular base but sides are inclined to the base.
3. Properties of right circular cylinder:
 - Area of circular base = πr2
 - Lateral area = 2πrh
 - Total surface area = 2πr(r + h)
 - Volume = πr2h
4. Development of surfaces: The development of a surface is the unrolling or flattening of the surface into a plane without distorting its size or shape. The development of a right circular cylinder will be a rectangle. The width of the rectangle will be the circumference of the base and the length will be the height of the cylinder.
