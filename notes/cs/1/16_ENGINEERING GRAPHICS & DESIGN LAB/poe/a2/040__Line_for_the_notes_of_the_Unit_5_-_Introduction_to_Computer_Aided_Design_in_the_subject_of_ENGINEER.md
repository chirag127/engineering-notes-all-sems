### Line for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

1. CAD stands for Computer Aided Design. It refers to software tools that are used to design and draft digital prototypes of physical products.
2. CAD software allows engineers and architects to create technical drawings of products and structures. It replaces manual drafting techniques and allows much more efficiency and precision.
3. Common features of CAD software include:
- Drawing tools to create and edit digital designs.
- Object snapping tools to enable precise drawing.
- Layering system to organize drawings.
- Template files and symbol libraries.
- 3D modeling capabilities.
- Rendering tools to create realistic representations.
4. The benefits of CAD include:
- Increased productivity and efficiency.
- Higher precision and accuracy.
- Easier modification and revision of designs.
- Creation of modular designs that can be reused.
- Data can be shared digitally across teams.
- Physical prototypes can be 3D printed for evaluation.
5. Popular CAD software packages include AutoCAD, Solidworks, Fusion 360, and Blender. The software used will depend on the specific needs and budget of a company or team.
