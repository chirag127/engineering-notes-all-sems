### Projection of straight lines for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB.

1. Projection of a line:
- A line in space can be projected on to any plane of projection as a line.
- The line joining the projectors of the end points of the line in space will be its projection.
- The projection of a line may be:
-- Parallel to the line in space
-- Non-parallel (inclined at some angle)
-- Coincident with the line in space (when line is parallel to the plane of projection)

2. Types of projection of a line:
- True length projection: When the line in space is perpendicular to the plane of projection.
- Foreshortened projection: When the line in space is inclined to the plane of projection. The length of projection will be less than its true length.
- Enlarged projection: When the line in space is inclined to the plane of projection. The length of projection will be more than its true length.

3. Determination of true length of a line:
- The true length of a line can be determined if its plan and elevation are given.
- Square law: 'The true length of a line is equal to the square root of the sum of the squares of its plan and elevation'
