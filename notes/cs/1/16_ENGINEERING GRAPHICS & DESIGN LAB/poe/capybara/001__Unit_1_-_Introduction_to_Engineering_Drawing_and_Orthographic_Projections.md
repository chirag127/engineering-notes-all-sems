## Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections

Engineering drawing is a graphical representation of an object or a structure that is used to communicate information about the design and construction of the object to others. Orthographic projection is a technique used to represent a three-dimensional object in two dimensions.

### What is Engineering Drawing?

Engineering drawing is a type of technical drawing used to define and describe products in a way that is clear and complete. It is a graphical representation of an object or a structure that is used to communicate information about the design and construction of the object to others.

### Types of Engineering Drawings

There are several types of engineering drawings, including:

- Assembly Drawings
- Detail Drawings
- Installation Drawings
- Piping and Instrumentation Drawings
- Structural Drawings

### Orthographic Projection

Orthographic projection is a technique used to represent a three-dimensional object in two dimensions. It is a method of projection that is used to create two-dimensional images of a three-dimensional object by projecting lines perpendicular to the plane of projection.

### Types of Orthographic Projections

There are several types of orthographic projections, including:

- First Angle Projection
- Third Angle Projection

### Orthographic Projection Views

Orthographic projection views include:

- Front View
- Top View
- Right Side View
- Left Side View
- Bottom View
- Rear View

### Advantages of Orthographic Projection

Some of the advantages of orthographic projection include:

- Easy to read and interpret
- Shows all sides of an object
- Shows dimensions and measurements clearly
- Can be used for manufacturing and construction purposes

### Conclusion

Engineering drawing and orthographic projection are important tools used in the design and construction of objects and structures. They are essential for communicating technical information to others and ensuring that products are built to the correct specifications. By understanding the principles of engineering drawing and orthographic projection, engineers and designers can create accurate and effective designs that meet the needs of their clients and customers.
