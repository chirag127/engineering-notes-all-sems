### Projection of Point

Projection of point is the process of drawing the image of a point on a plane or a surface. It is an important concept in engineering drawing and orthographic projections.

Here are some key points to understand about projection of point:

- A point is a location in space that has no size or shape. It is represented by a dot in engineering drawing.
- A projection line is a straight line that connects the point to the plane or surface onto which the point is projected.
- The projection of a point onto a plane is the intersection of the projection line with the plane. This intersection point is the image of the point on the plane.
- The projection of a point onto a surface is the intersection of the projection line with the surface. This intersection point is the image of the point on the surface.
- The projection line can be drawn at any angle to the plane or surface, but it is usually drawn perpendicular to the plane or surface in engineering drawing.
- The projection of a point can be used to locate the point on a drawing or to create a perspective view of an object.

To summarize, projection of point is an essential concept in engineering drawing and orthographic projections. It involves drawing the image of a point on a plane or a surface using a projection line. Understanding projection of point is crucial for creating accurate and detailed engineering drawings.
