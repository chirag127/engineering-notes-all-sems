## Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design

Unit 5 covers the basics of computer-aided design (CAD), including:
1. Definition and history of CAD
2. Types of CAD software and their uses
3. 2D and 3D modeling techniques
4. Importance of CAD in various industries
5. Advantages and disadvantages of CAD

CAD is a technology that uses computers to assist in the creation, modification, and optimization of designs. The use of CAD has revolutionized the way products are designed and manufactured, leading to increased efficiency and accuracy.

CAD software is used in a variety of industries, including architecture, engineering, product design, and manufacturing. There are two main types of CAD software: 2D and 3D. 2D CAD software is used for creating flat, two-dimensional designs, while 3D CAD software is used for creating more complex, three-dimensional designs.

In Unit 5, students will learn about the different types of CAD software and their uses, as well as the importance of CAD in various industries. They will also learn about the advantages and disadvantages of using CAD, including increased accuracy and efficiency, but also the potential for decreased creativity.

Overall, Unit 5 provides a comprehensive introduction to the world of computer-aided design, providing students with a solid foundation for further study and exploration in this field.
