### Isometric scales for the notes of the Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Isometric scales are used in engineering graphics and design to create isometric projections of 3D objects. They are designed to show the true lengths of lines in a 3D object while maintaining the correct angles between them. This allows the user to create an accurate representation of the object in 2D. The isometric scales typically used in engineering graphics are 30°, 45°, and 60°. These angles are chosen because they allow for the creation of isometric projections with the correct proportions and angles. The notes for Unit 4 - Isometric Projection in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab should cover the use of these scales, how to use them to create isometric projections, and the importance of accuracy in isometric drawings.
