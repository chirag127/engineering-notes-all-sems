### Scales for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Scales in Engineering Graphics & Design Lab refer to the ratio of the size of the object being drawn to its actual size. Scales are used to represent large objects in a smaller size on a drawing. There are two types of scales used in engineering graphics: 

1. Engineering Scales: Used to measure linear distances on a drawing. Examples include 1:1, 1:2, 1:10, 1:20, etc.

2. Graphic Scales: Used to measure areas and volumes on a drawing. Examples include 1:50, 1:100, 1:200, etc.

In Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections, the concept of scales is introduced to help students understand the importance of accurate representation of objects in drawings. The use of proper scales helps to maintain the proportions and dimensions of objects, ensuring that the end product is a true representation of the original object.
