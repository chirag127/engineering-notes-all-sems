### Hatch for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Hatch:
- A pattern of lines used to fill an area in an engineering drawing.
- Indicates material, surface finish or other properties of the object.
- Can be created in CAD software with various styles, spacing, and angle options.
- Helps to distinguish different parts or materials in a drawing.
- Can be used in combination with other annotations such as dimensions and notes to provide complete information.
