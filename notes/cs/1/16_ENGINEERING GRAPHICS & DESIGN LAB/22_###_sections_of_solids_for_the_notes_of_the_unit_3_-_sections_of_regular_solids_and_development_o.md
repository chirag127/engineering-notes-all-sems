### Sections of Solids for the notes of the Unit 3 - Sections of Regular Solids and development of Surfaces in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Sections of Solids:
1. Introduction to sections of solids
2. Types of sections (Full, Half, Quarter, etc.)
3. Development of Surfaces
4. Types of Development of Surfaces (Rectangular, Triangular, Trapezoidal, etc.)
5. Steps for developing a surface
6. Applications of sections of solids in engineering graphics

It is important to note that sections of solids are used to represent a three-dimensional object on a two-dimensional plane. The various types of sections are used to represent different parts of the solid object. The development of surfaces is used to create a flat representation of a curved surface. The steps for developing a surface involve creating a cross-section of the object and then using that cross-section to create a flat representation of the surface. These concepts are important in engineering graphics as they help engineers to visualize and understand the objects they are designing.
