### Orthographic Projection for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Orthographic Projection is a method of representing 3D objects in 2D drawings. It involves projecting views of an object from different angles onto a single plane. There are three common orthographic projections: top, front, and side views. These views are used to create a complete representation of an object. Orthographic projections are widely used in engineering and technical drawings to accurately represent objects in a 2D format. The use of orthographic projections helps to simplify complex 3D objects and makes it easier to understand the object’s design and features.

In Engineering Graphics & Design Lab, Unit 1 - Introduction to Engineering Drawing and Orthographic Projections, students will learn about the basics of engineering drawing and how to create orthographic projections. They will learn about the different types of views used in orthographic projections, including top, front, and side views. They will also learn about the principles of projection and how to create an accurate representation of an object in 2D. Additionally, students will learn about the use of orthographic projections in engineering and technical drawings and how they are used to communicate design ideas and specifications.
