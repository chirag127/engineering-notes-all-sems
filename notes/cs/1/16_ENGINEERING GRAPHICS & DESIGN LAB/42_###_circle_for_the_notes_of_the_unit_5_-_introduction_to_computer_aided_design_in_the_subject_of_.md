### Circle for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

Circle:
- Geometric shape defined by all points equidistant from a central point.
- Used in engineering drawings for circular shapes such as gears, holes, etc.
- Can be defined by center point and radius or by 3 points on the circumference.
- CAD software can easily create and manipulate circles, applying dimensional constraints and modifications.
- Can be used in combination with lines and arcs to create complex shapes.
