### Trim for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design in the subject of ENGINEERING GRAPHICS & DESIGN LAB

"Unit 5 - Introduction to Computer Aided Design" in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab focuses on the use of computer software to create and edit technical drawings and designs. This unit covers the following topics:

1. Overview of CAD: An introduction to the basics of computer-aided design, including the history and evolution of CAD software, and the advantages and disadvantages of using CAD in engineering and design.

2. CAD Software: An overview of the different types of CAD software available, including 2D and 3D CAD programs, and the features and capabilities of each.

3. CAD Workflow: A discussion of the typical workflow for creating and editing technical drawings and designs using CAD software, including the steps involved in creating and editing geometry, adding annotations and dimensions, and exporting the final design.

4. CAD Applications: An overview of the various applications of CAD in engineering and design, including product design, architectural design, and mechanical engineering.

5. CAD Techniques: An introduction to the various techniques and tools used in CAD, such as sketching, modeling, and rendering, and how they are applied in the design process.

The goal of this unit is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of computer-aided design and its applications in engineering and design, and to develop their skills in using CAD software to create and edit technical drawings and designs.
