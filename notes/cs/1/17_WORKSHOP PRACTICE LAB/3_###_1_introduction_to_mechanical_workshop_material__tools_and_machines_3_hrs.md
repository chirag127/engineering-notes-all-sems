### 1 Introduction to Mechanical workshop material, tools and machines 3 Hrs

The 1 Introduction to Mechanical Workshop is a course that covers the basics of mechanical workshop materials, tools, and machines. 1. Types of Mechanical Workshop Materials: Overview of the different types of materials used in mechanical workshops, including metals, plastics, and composites.

2. Mechanical Workshop Tools: Overview of the different types of tools used in mechanical workshops, including hand tools, power tools, and measuring tools.

3. Mechanical Workshop Machines: Overview of the different types of machines used in mechanical workshops, including lathes, mills, and drill presses.
