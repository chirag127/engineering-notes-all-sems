### 3 Fitting shop 3 Hrs

"3 Fitting shop" refers to a workshop in the field of engineering or manufacturing where components are fitted together to form a complete product. The workshop typically lasts for 3 hours.

In the context of a 3 Fitting shop, students will learn about the various techniques and tools used to fit components together, including the use of hand and power tools, as well as the use of jigs, fixtures, and other specialized tools. They will also learn about the various types of joints used in engineering and manufacturing, including butt joints, lap joints, and T-joints.

- Overview of the fitting process

- Techniques and tools used in fitting components together
- Safety considerations when working in a fitting shop
