#### To study layout, safety measures and different engineering materials (mild steel, medium carbon steel, high carbon steel, high speed steel and cast iron etc) used in workshop.

The study of layout, safety measures, and different engineering materials used in a workshop is an important aspect of the field of engineering. This study is designed to help students understand the importance of proper workshop layout, and the various safety measures that must be taken to ensure the safety of workers and equipment.

In terms of workshop layout, students should learn about the different types of work areas, such as machine shops, welding areas, and storage areas, and how to arrange these areas in an efficient and safe manner. They should also learn about the importance of proper ventilation, lighting, and access to emergency exits.

When it comes to safety measures, students should learn about the various hazards that are present in a workshop, such as fire hazards, electrical hazards, and hazards related to the use of machinery. They should also learn about the importance of proper personal protective equipment, such as safety glasses and gloves, and the importance of following proper procedures when using machinery and equipment.

Finally, students should learn about the different engineering materials used in a workshop, including mild steel, medium carbon steel, high carbon steel, high speed steel, and cast iron. They should learn about the properties and characteristics of each material, and how to use these materials in a safe and effective manner.

Overall, the study of layout, safety measures, and different engineering materials used in a workshop is an important aspect of the field of engineering, as it provides students with the knowledge and skills they need to work safely and effectively in a workshop environment.
