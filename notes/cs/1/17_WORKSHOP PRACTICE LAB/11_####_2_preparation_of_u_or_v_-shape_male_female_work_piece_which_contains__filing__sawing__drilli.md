#### 2. Preparation of U or V -Shape Male Female Work piece which contains: Filing, Sawing, Drilling, Grinding.

The preparation of U or V-shaped male and female workpieces involves several steps, including filing, sawing, drilling, and grinding. These steps are used to create precise and accurate shapes that fit together perfectly.

1. Filing: Filing is the process of removing material from the workpiece using a file, a tool with a rough surface that is used to shape and smooth metal. Filing is used to create the basic shape of the U or V-shaped workpiece and to remove any rough edges or burrs.

2. Sawing: Sawing is the process of cutting the workpiece using a saw, a tool with a sharp blade that is used to make precise cuts. Sawing is used to create the final shape of the U or V-shaped workpiece, to cut the workpiece to the correct length, and to create any necessary notches or slots.

3. Drilling: Drilling is the process of making holes in the workpiece using a drill, a tool with a rotating cutting edge that is used to make precise holes. Drilling is used to create any necessary holes in the workpiece, such as for screws or bolts.

4. Grinding: Grinding is the process of removing material from the workpiece using a grinding wheel, a tool with a rotating abrasive surface that is used to shape and smooth metal. Grinding is used to refine the shape of the U or V-shaped workpiece, to remove any remaining rough edges or burrs, and to improve the surface finish.

The goal of these steps is to create a U or V-shaped workpiece that fits together precisely and accurately, and that is ready for use in a final assembly. By following these steps, students will learn the skills and techniques needed to create precise and accurate metal workpieces.
