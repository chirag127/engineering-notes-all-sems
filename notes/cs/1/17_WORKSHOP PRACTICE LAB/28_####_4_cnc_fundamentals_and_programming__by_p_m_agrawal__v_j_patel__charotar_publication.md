#### 4. CNC Fundamentals and Programming, By P. M. Agrawal, V. J. Patel, Charotar Publication.

CNC Fundamentals and Programming is a book written by P. M. Agrawal and V. J. Patel and published by Charotar Publication. The book provides a comprehensive overview of the fundamentals of CNC (Computer Numerical Control) technology and programming.

The book covers key concepts such as CNC machine components, CNC control systems, and CNC programming languages. It also includes detailed explanations of CNC programming codes and techniques, including G-code and M-code programming.

In addition to the technical content, the book also includes practical examples and exercises to help readers apply the concepts they have learned. The book is designed for students, engineers, and technicians who are interested in learning about CNC technology and programming.

Overall, CNC Fundamentals and Programming is a comprehensive and well-written guide to the fundamentals of CNC technology and programming. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced professional, this book provides a solid foundation for understanding and working with CNC systems.
