
### 6 Moulding and Casting Shop 6 Hrs

1. Moulding and casting is a process used to create objects from a variety of materials. It involves pouring molten material into a mould and allowing it to cool and harden.

2. The mould is usually made of metal or plastic and is designed to create a particular shape. It is important to use the right mould for the job, as the shape of the finished product will be determined by the shape of the mould.

3. The casting process involves heating the material to a high temperature before it is poured into the mould. This is usually done in a furnace or kiln.

4. After the material has been heated, it is poured into the mould and allowed to cool and harden. Depending on the type of material, this process can take anywhere from a few minutes to several hours.

5. Once the material has cooled and hardened, it is removed from the mould and is ready to be used. It is important to use the right tools and techniques when removing the material from the mould, as this can affect the quality of the finished product.

6. Moulding and casting is used in a variety of industries, including automotive, aerospace, and medical. It is also used to create sculptures, jewellery, and other decorative items.
