

#### To determine the least count of Vernier calliper, vernier height gauge, micrometer (Screw gauge) and take different reading over given metallic pieces using these instruments

* Vernier Caliper: It is a measuring instrument which is used to measure the internal and external dimensions of an object. It consists of two scales, the main scale and the vernier scale. The main scale is graduated in centimeters (or millimeters) and the vernier scale is graduated in tenths of a millimeter or hundredths of a centimeter. The least count of vernier caliper is 0.1 mm or 0.01 cm.

* Vernier Height Gauge: It is a measuring instrument used to measure the height of an object. It consists of a main scale and a vernier scale. The main scale is graduated in centimeters and the vernier scale is graduated in tenths of a millimeter or hundredths of a centimeter. The least count of vernier height gauge is 0.1 mm or 0.01 cm.

* Micrometer (Screw Gauge): It is a measuring instrument which is used to measure the diameter of an object. It consists of a main scale and a vernier scale. The main scale is graduated in millimeters and the vernier scale is graduated in thousandths of a millimeter. The least count of micrometer (screw gauge) is 0.001 mm.

To take different readings over given metallic pieces using these instruments, the object should be placed in between the jaws of the instrument and the reading should be taken by noting the main scale and the vernier scale. The reading should be noted in the form of the main scale reading plus the vernier scale reading. For example, if the main scale reading is 5 cm and the vernier scale reading is 0.4 cm, then the reading should be noted as 5.4 cm.
