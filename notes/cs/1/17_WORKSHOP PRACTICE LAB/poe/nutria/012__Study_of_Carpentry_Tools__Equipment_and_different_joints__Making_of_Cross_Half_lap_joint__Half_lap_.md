

#### Study of Carpentry Tools, Equipment and different joints. Making of Cross Half lap joint, Half lap Dovetail joint and Mortise Tenon Joint

- Carpentry is a craft that involves the use of tools to construct items such as furniture, cabinets, and other objects. 
- To complete carpentry projects, a carpenter must have the right tools, equipment, and knowledge of different joints. 
- Cross half lap joints are used to join two pieces of wood together. To make this joint, the carpenter cuts a half-lap on each piece of wood and then fits them together. 
- Half lap dovetail joints are used to join two pieces of wood together at a right angle. To make this joint, the carpenter cuts a half-lap on the end of each piece of wood and then fits them together. 
- Mortise and tenon joints are used to join two pieces of wood together. To make this joint, the carpenter cuts a mortise into one piece of wood and a tenon into the other. The tenon is then inserted into the mortise and secured with glue.
