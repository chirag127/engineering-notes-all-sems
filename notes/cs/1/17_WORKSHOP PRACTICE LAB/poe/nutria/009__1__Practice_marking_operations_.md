
#### 1. Practice marking operations
* Marking operations are the actions taken by a business to ensure that the quality of their products or services meets the standards set by the company.
* Quality assurance is an important part of any business, and it is important to ensure that all marking operations are done correctly.
* Common marking operations include inspection, testing, and sorting.
* Inspection involves looking at the product or service to ensure that it meets the required standards.
* Testing involves using a variety of methods to ensure that the product or service meets the required standards.
* Sorting involves separating the products or services into different categories based on quality.
* Quality assurance is a process, and it is important to regularly review the marking operations to ensure that they are effective.
