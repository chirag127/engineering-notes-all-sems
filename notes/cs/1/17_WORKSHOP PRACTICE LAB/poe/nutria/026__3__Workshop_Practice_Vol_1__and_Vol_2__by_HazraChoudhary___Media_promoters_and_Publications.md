
#### 3. Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2, by HazraChoudhary, Media Promoters and Publications
1. Workshop Practice Vol 1 provides an in-depth look into the process of setting up and running a successful workshop. It covers topics such as:
    - Identifying and researching potential workshop topics
    - Creating an effective workshop agenda
    - Planning and preparing for the workshop
    - Running the workshop
    - Evaluating the workshop
2. Workshop Practice Vol 2 builds on the knowledge from Vol 1 and dives deeper into the topics of:
    - Developing a workshop team
    - Creating an effective workshop environment
    - Managing the workshop
    - Developing a post-workshop strategy
3. Both volumes of Workshop Practice provide valuable insights into the process of running a successful workshop and provide practical tips and techniques to help ensure the success of any workshop.
