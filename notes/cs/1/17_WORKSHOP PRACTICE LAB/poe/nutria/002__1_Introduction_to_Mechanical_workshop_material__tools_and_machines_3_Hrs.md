
### 1 Introduction to Mechanical Workshop Material, Tools and Machines 3 Hrs

* Mechanical workshops are areas where mechanical engineering is practiced.
* They contain a variety of tools and machines used to create and modify mechanical parts, components, and assemblies.
* Common tools and machines found in a mechanical workshop include lathes, milling machines, grinders, drill presses, and saws.
* Lathes are used to shape and cut metal components.
* Milling machines are used to shape and cut metal components.
* Grinders are used to sharpen and refine metal components.
* Drill presses are used to drill holes in metal components.
* Saws are used to cut metal components.
* Other tools used in a mechanical workshop include calipers, micrometers, and gauges.
* Calipers are used to measure the size of components.
* Micrometers are used to measure the thickness of components.
* Gauges are used to measure the pressure of components.
