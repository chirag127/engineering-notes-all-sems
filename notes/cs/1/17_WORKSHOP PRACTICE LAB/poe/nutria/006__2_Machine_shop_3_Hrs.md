
### 2 Machine Shop 3 Hrs

1. A machine shop is a facility that houses a variety of machines and tools used for metalworking and other related activities.
2. The machines found in a machine shop typically include lathes, milling machines, drill presses, and grinders.
3. A machine shop can also contain other equipment such as welding machines, saws, and sanders.
4. In order to maintain the machines and tools in a machine shop, it is important to keep them clean and lubricated.
5. The machines should be inspected regularly to ensure that they are functioning properly.
6. It is also important to use the correct safety equipment when operating the machines in a machine shop.
7. The three main types of machine shops are manual, CNC, and robotic.
8. Manual machine shops are the most basic type of machine shop and are used for simple tasks such as drilling and tapping.
9. CNC machine shops are more advanced and can be used for more complex tasks such as milling and turning.
10. Robotic machine shops are the most advanced type of machine shop and are used for tasks such as welding and assembly.
11. In order to become proficient in operating the machines in a machine shop, it is important to have a good understanding of the different types of machines and their functions.
12. It is also important to have a good understanding of the safety protocols and procedures that are necessary when operating the machines.
13. With proper training and experience, a person can become an expert in operating the machines in a machine shop.
