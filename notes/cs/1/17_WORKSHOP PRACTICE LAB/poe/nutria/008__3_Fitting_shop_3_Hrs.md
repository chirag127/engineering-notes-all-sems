
### 3 Fitting Shops in 3 Hours
1. **The Tailor's Workshop**: Located in the heart of the city, The Tailor's Workshop offers custom-made clothing and tailoring services. They specialize in suits, blazers, and other formal attire. They also provide alterations and repairs. 
2. **The Seamstress**: The Seamstress is a small boutique offering a wide selection of ready-to-wear clothing, as well as custom-made clothing. They specialize in bridal wear and evening gowns. 
3. **The Fabric Store**: The Fabric Store is a one-stop shop for all your fabric needs. They offer a wide selection of fabrics, patterns, and notions, as well as custom-made clothing and alterations. They also offer classes on sewing and pattern-making.
