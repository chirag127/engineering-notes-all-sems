

#### Introduction to Patterns, pattern allowances, ingredients of moulding sand and melting furnaces. Foundry tools and their purposes Demo of mould preparation and Aluminum casting Practice – Study and Preparation of mould for Plastic

- Patterns are the physical representation of the object that needs to be cast. Pattern allowances are the extra material added to the pattern to account for shrinkage of the metal during the cooling process.
- The ingredients of moulding sand are silica, clay, and water. The sand is mixed and blended to create a homogeneous mixture. The sand is then packed into the mould to form a cavity.
- Melting furnaces are used to melt the metal and pour it into the mould cavity. Foundry tools are used to shape the molten metal and to remove the casting from the mould.
- The demo of mould preparation involves the use of a pattern, core boxes, flask, and moulding sand. The pattern is placed into the core box and the sand is packed around it. The flask is then placed over the core box and the sand is packed into the flask.
- Aluminum casting is the process of pouring molten aluminum into a mould and allowing it to cool and solidify. The practice of pouring molten aluminum requires the use of safety equipment and proper technique.
- The study and preparation of mould for plastic involves the use of a pattern, core box, flask, and plastic resin. The pattern is placed into the core box and the plastic resin is poured into the flask. The flask is then placed over the core box and the plastic resin is poured into the mould. The mould is then allowed to cool and the plastic part is removed.
