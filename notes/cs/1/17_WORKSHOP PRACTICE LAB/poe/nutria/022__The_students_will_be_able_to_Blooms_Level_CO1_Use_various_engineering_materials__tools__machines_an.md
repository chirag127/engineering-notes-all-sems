
### The Students Will Be Able to:
- Use various engineering materials, tools, machines and measuring equipments (Blooms Level CO1)
- Perform machine operations in lathe and CNC machine (K3 CO2)
- Perform manufacturing operations on components in fitting and carpentry shop (K3 CO3)
- Perform operations in welding, moulding, casting and gas cutting (K3 CO4)
- Fabricate a job by 3D printing manufacturing technique K3 (K3 CO5)
