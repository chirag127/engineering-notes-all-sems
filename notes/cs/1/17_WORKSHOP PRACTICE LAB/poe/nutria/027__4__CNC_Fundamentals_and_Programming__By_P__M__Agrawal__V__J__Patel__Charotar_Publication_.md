

#### 4. CNC Fundamentals and Programming

1. CNC stands for Computer Numerical Control. It is a technology that uses computer-controlled machines to perform operations such as cutting, drilling, and milling.
2. CNC programming involves writing instructions for a CNC machine to execute. The instructions are written in a programming language such as G-code, which is a standard language for CNC machines.
3. CNC machines can be used for a variety of operations, including drilling, milling, turning, and grinding.
4. CNC machines are used in a wide range of industries, from automotive to aerospace.
5. CNC programming involves writing instructions for a CNC machine to execute. The instructions are written in a programming language such as G-code, which is a standard language for CNC machines.
6. CNC machines can be programmed to produce parts with high accuracy and repeatability.
7. CNC machines require regular maintenance to ensure they are running optimally.
8. Safety is an important consideration when working with CNC machines. Proper safety procedures must be followed to ensure the safety of the operator and other personnel.
