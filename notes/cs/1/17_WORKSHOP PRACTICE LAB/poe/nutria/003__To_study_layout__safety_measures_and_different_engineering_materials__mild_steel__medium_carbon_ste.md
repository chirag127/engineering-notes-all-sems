

#### To study layout, safety measures and different engineering materials used in workshop

1. Layout: 
    - A workshop layout is an important part of any workshop, as it helps ensure that the space is used efficiently and that safety is maintained.
    - The layout should take into account the size and shape of the workshop, the type of work to be done, and any equipment that will be used.
    - The layout should also be designed to allow for easy access to tools and materials.

2. Safety Measures:
    - All workshops should have appropriate safety measures in place, including appropriate lighting, ventilation, and fire safety equipment.
    - Protective clothing and equipment, such as safety glasses, gloves, and protective footwear, should also be worn.
    - A first aid kit and other emergency equipment should also be available.

3. Engineering Materials:
    - Mild Steel: Mild steel is a low-carbon steel and is one of the most common materials used in workshops. It is easy to work with and is relatively inexpensive.
    - Medium Carbon Steel: Medium carbon steel is a higher-carbon steel and is stronger than mild steel. It is more difficult to work with and is more expensive than mild steel.
    - High Carbon Steel: High carbon steel is the strongest of the three types of steel and is the most difficult to work with. It is also the most expensive.
    - High Speed Steel: High speed steel is a type of steel that is designed to be used at high speeds. It is more expensive than other types of steel.
    - Cast Iron: Cast iron is a type of iron that is melted and poured into a mold to form a shape. It is strong and durable, but is difficult to work with.
