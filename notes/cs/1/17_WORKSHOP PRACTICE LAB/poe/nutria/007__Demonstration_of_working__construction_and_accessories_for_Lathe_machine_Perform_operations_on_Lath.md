

#### Demonstration of Working, Construction and Accessories for Lathe Machine

A lathe machine is a machine tool used for shaping and machining metal, wood, and other materials. It is commonly used for facing, plane turning, step turning, taper turning, threading, knurling, and parting.

* Construction of Lathe Machine: The main components of a lathe machine are the bed, headstock, tailstock, carriage, and tool post. The bed is the foundation of the machine and supports all other components. The headstock contains the spindle, which rotates the workpiece. The tailstock provides support to the workpiece and can be adjusted to different positions. The carriage holds the cutting tool and is moved along the bed to complete the machining operation. The tool post holds the cutting tools.

* Working of Lathe Machine: The workpiece is placed between the headstock and tailstock and is secured in place. The cutting tool is mounted on the tool post and is moved along the bed by the carriage. The spindle is rotated at a certain speed and the cutting tool is fed into the workpiece to shape it.

* Accessories for Lathe Machine: A number of accessories are available for lathe machines, such as live centers, faceplates, steady rests, and follow rests. Live centers are used to support the workpiece in the headstock and tailstock. Faceplates are used to hold the workpiece in place. Steady rests and follow rests are used to support the workpiece during machining.
