
#### 2. Mechanical Workshop Practice, K C John, PHI

1. Introduction to Mechanical Workshop Practice:
   - Workshop practice is an important part of engineering and technology education. 
   - It is the practice of applying the principles of engineering, science and mathematics in the design and manufacture of machines and tools. 
   - Workshop practice helps students to develop their skills in the design, construction, maintenance and operation of machines and tools.

2. Types of Workshop Practice:
   - Machine shop practice: This involves the use of power tools and machine tools to fabricate and assemble components.
   - Foundry practice: This involves the use of furnaces and casting techniques to produce castings from metals and alloys.
   - Sheet metal practice: This involves the use of tools and techniques to form and shape sheet metal components.
   - Welding practice: This involves the use of welding techniques to join metals and other materials.

3. Safety in the Workshop:
   - Safety is of paramount importance in the workshop. 
   - All safety regulations and guidelines must be followed at all times.
   - The use of protective clothing, such as safety glasses, gloves, and aprons, is essential. 
   - All tools and machines must be used in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions.

4. Workshop Tools and Equipment:
   - The workshop is equipped with a variety of tools and machines, such as lathes, milling machines, drill presses, grinders, and saws. 
   - Hand tools, such as chisels, hammers, files, and wrenches, are also used. 
   - The selection of tools and machines depends on the type of work to be done.
