#### Blooms Level CO1: Use various engineering materials, tools, machines, and measuring equipment
To become a successful engineer, it is important to have a good understanding of the different engineering materials, tools, machines, and measuring equipment. Here are some key points to help you understand these concepts:

* Engineering materials: Engineering materials include metals, polymers, ceramics, and composites. They are used to create structures, machines, and other engineering products. It is essential to understand the properties and characteristics of these materials to select the appropriate material for a specific application.

* Tools: Various tools are used in the engineering field, such as hammers, screwdrivers, pliers, wrenches, and many others. These tools are used to assemble and disassemble components, and it is essential to know how to use them correctly.

* Machines: Machines are used to transform the engineering materials into useful products. The lathe and CNC machines are used to perform operations like drilling, turning, and milling.

* Measuring equipment: Measuring equipment is used to measure the dimensions of engineering components. Common measuring equipment includes Vernier calipers, micrometers, and height gauges.

#### K3 CO2: Perform machine operations in lathe and CNC machine
The lathe and CNC machines are essential tools in the engineering field. Here are some key points to help you understand these concepts:

* Lathe machine: A lathe machine is used to perform operations like drilling, turning, and milling. It is essential to understand the different parts of the lathe machine and how to operate it safely.

* CNC machine: A CNC machine is used to perform complex operations like cutting, drilling, and shaping. It is essential to know how to program the CNC machine and operate it safely.

#### K3 CO3: Perform manufacturing operations on components in fitting and carpentry shop
Fitting and carpentry shops are essential parts of the engineering field. Here are some key points to help you understand these concepts:

* Fitting shop: A fitting shop is used to manufacture and assemble components using various tools like hand tools, power tools, and welding equipment. It is essential to know how to use these tools safely and correctly.

* Carpentry shop: A carpentry shop is used to manufacture wooden components using various tools like saws, chisels, and planes. It is essential to know how to use these tools safely and correctly.

#### K3 CO4: Perform operations in welding, molding, casting, and gas cutting
Welding, molding, casting, and gas cutting are essential operations in the engineering field. Here are some key points to help you understand these concepts:

* Welding: Welding is used to join two or more metal components. It is essential to know how to perform welding safely and correctly.

* Molding: Molding is used to manufacture components using molds. It is essential to know how to prepare the mold and the molding material correctly.

* Casting: Casting is used to manufacture components using a mold and molten metal. It is essential to know how to prepare the mold and perform casting safely and correctly.

* Gas cutting: Gas cutting is used to cut metal components using a gas flame. It is essential to know how to use the gas cutting equipment safely and correctly.

#### K3 CO5: Fabricate a job by 3D printing manufacturing technique
3D printing is a relatively new technology in the engineering field. Here are some key points to help you understand this concept:

* 3D printing: 3D printing is a manufacturing technique that uses a computer to create a three-dimensional object from a digital model. It is essential to know how to prepare the digital model and operate the 3D printer safely and correctly.
