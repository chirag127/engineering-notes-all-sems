#### 3. Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2, by HazraChoudhary , Media promoters and Publications

Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2 are two books written by HazraChoudhary and published by Media promoters and Publications. These books are helpful for students who want to learn about workshop practices and techniques. Below are some key points about these books:

#### Workshop Practice Vol 1

1. This book introduces the reader to the basic concepts of workshop practices and provides a comprehensive overview of various tools and equipment used in a workshop.
2. It covers topics such as marking out, drilling, tapping, reaming, and grinding, among others.
3. The book also includes detailed information on the use of hand tools, power tools, and measuring instruments.
4. It provides step-by-step instructions and illustrations for various workshop operations, making it easy for students to understand and learn.
5. The book also includes a section on safety precautions to be taken while working in a workshop, which is important for students to understand before starting any practical work.

#### Workshop Practice Vol 2

1. This book focuses on advanced workshop practices and techniques and is a continuation of Workshop Practice Vol 1.
2. It covers topics such as lathe operations, milling operations, shaping operations, and grinding operations.
3. The book also includes a section on welding and soldering techniques, which are important for students who want to pursue a career in manufacturing or engineering.
4. It provides detailed information on the use of different types of machines and tools used in advanced workshop practices.
5. The book also includes a section on quality control and inspection, which is important for students who want to ensure that their work meets industry standards.

In conclusion, Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2, by HazraChoudhary, are two important books for students who want to learn about workshop practices and techniques. These books provide comprehensive coverage of various tools and equipment used in a workshop, step-by-step instructions and illustrations for various workshop operations, and safety precautions to be taken while working in a workshop. Students who read these books will gain a better understanding of workshop practices and will be better prepared for hands-on practical work.
