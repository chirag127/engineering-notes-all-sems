#### 2. Mechanical Workshop Practice, K C John, PHI

Mechanical Workshop Practice is an important subject in the field of mechanical engineering. It is a practical course that teaches students about the various tools, machines, and processes used in mechanical workshops. This subject is essential for students who want to work in manufacturing industries or pursue a career in mechanical engineering.

The following are the key topics covered in Mechanical Workshop Practice:

1. Introduction to Workshop Practice
- Definition of Workshop Practice
- Types of workshops
- Safety precautions in the workshop
- Basic hand tools used in the workshop

2. Workshop Processes
- Marking and layout tools
- Cutting tools
- Grinding and finishing tools
- Measuring tools
- Welding and brazing
- Casting and forging
- Sheet metal work

3. Machine Tools
- Types of machine tools
- Lathe machine
- Drilling machine
- Shaping machine
- Milling machine
- Grinding machine

4. Mechanical Measurements
- Use of Vernier caliper, micrometer, and other measuring instruments
- Measurement of length, diameter, and thickness
- Measurement of angles and surface finish

5. Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM)
- Introduction to CAM
- Types of CAM systems
- Advantages and disadvantages of CAM
- Applications of CAM in manufacturing

6. Quality Control
- Definition of quality control
- Types of quality control
- Statistical quality control
- Quality control tools and techniques

In conclusion, Mechanical Workshop Practice is a crucial subject for mechanical engineering students. It provides hands-on training in the use of tools, machines, and processes used in mechanical workshops. By studying this subject, students can gain practical skills and knowledge that can help them excel in the field of mechanical engineering.
