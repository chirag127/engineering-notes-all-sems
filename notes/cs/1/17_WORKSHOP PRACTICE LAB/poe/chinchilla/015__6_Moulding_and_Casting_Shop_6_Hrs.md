### 6 Moulding and Casting Shop 6 Hrs

Moulding and casting are essential techniques in manufacturing, and mastering them can provide an edge in the industry. The following are fundamental concepts and techniques for moulding and casting.

1. Types of Moulds
- Two-part moulds
- Waste-moulds
- Shell moulds

2. Materials for Moulding
- Plaster of Paris
- Silicone rubber
- Polyurethane rubber
- Latex rubber

3. Techniques for Moulding
- Pouring the material into the mould
- Brushing or spraying the material onto the surface
- Trowelling or spreading the material onto the surface

4. Materials for Casting
- Resin
- Metal alloys
- Plaster of Paris
- Concrete

5. Techniques for Casting
- Gravity casting
- Pressure casting
- Vacuum casting

6. Post-Casting Techniques
- Trimming and finishing the casting
- Polishing the casting
- Painting or coating the casting

In a six-hour moulding and casting workshop, participants will be able to learn and practice these techniques, as well as create their own moulds and castings. The workshop will include demonstrations, hands-on experience, and guidance from experienced instructors.

By the end of the workshop, participants will have gained a basic understanding of moulding and casting techniques, as well as the ability to create their own moulds and castings. These skills can be applied in various manufacturing industries, such as automotive, aerospace, and consumer goods.
