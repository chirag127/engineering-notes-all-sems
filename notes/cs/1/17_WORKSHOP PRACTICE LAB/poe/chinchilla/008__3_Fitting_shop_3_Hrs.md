### 3 Fitting Shop 3 Hrs

A fitting shop is an essential part of any manufacturing unit where various components are fabricated and assembled to make the final product. In this section, we will discuss the various aspects of a fitting shop and the operations that are carried out there.

#### 1. Tools and Equipment
- A fitting shop is equipped with a range of tools and equipment that are required for various operations such as drilling, tapping, reaming, shaping, and cutting.
- Some of the common tools found in a fitting shop include hammers, screwdrivers, wrenches, pliers, chisels, saws, and files.
- The equipment found in a fitting shop includes drilling machines, lathes, milling machines, shaping machines, and grinding machines.

#### 2. Operations
- The fitting shop is responsible for various operations such as marking, cutting, drilling, tapping, reaming, and shaping of various components.
- The marking operation involves marking the components at the required locations to indicate the position where the drilling, cutting, or shaping operations need to be carried out.
- The cutting operation involves cutting the components to the required size and shape using tools such as saws, shears, and cutters.
- The drilling, tapping, and reaming operations involve creating holes in the components using drilling machines and other tools.
- The shaping operation involves shaping the components to the required shape and size using shaping machines and other tools.

#### 3. Safety Measures
- Safety is a critical aspect of any fitting shop, and various safety measures need to be implemented to ensure the safety of the workers.
- The workers in the fitting shop need to wear appropriate personal protective equipment such as safety goggles, gloves, and aprons to protect themselves from the hazards.
- The machines in the fitting shop need to be regularly maintained and inspected to ensure they are in good working condition.
- The workers need to be trained on the proper use of tools and equipment and the safety measures that need to be taken while working in the fitting shop.

In conclusion, a fitting shop is a crucial part of any manufacturing unit where various components are fabricated and assembled to make the final product. The tools and equipment, operations, and safety measures discussed in this section are essential for the smooth functioning of a fitting shop.
