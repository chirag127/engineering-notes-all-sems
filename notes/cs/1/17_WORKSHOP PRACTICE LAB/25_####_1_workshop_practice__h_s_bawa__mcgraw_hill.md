#### 1. Workshop Practice, H S Bawa, McGraw Hill

"Workshop Practice" by H S Bawa is a book that provides information and guidance on the various skills and techniques used in workshop practice, including metalworking and woodworking. The book is published by McGraw Hill.

The book covers a wide range of topics, including:

1. Basic Workshop Tools: An overview of the various tools used in workshop practice, including hand tools, power tools, and measuring instruments.

2. Metalworking Techniques: Detailed instructions and illustrations on metalworking techniques, including cutting, drilling, tapping, and welding.

3. Woodworking Techniques: Detailed instructions and illustrations on woodworking techniques, including sawing, planing, sanding, and finishing.

4. Project Planning and Design: Guidance on project planning and design, including selecting materials, creating drawings, and estimating costs.

5. Safety in the Workshop: Information on safety in the workshop, including the use of personal protective equipment, handling hazardous materials, and following safe work practices.

The goal of the book is to provide readers with a comprehensive understanding of workshop practice, and to develop their skills and knowledge in metalworking and woodworking. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced workshop practitioner, "Workshop Practice" by H S Bawa is an excellent resource to have on hand.
