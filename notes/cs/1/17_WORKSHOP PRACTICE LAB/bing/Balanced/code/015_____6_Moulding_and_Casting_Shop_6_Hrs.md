### 6 Moulding and Casting Shop 6 Hrs

Moulding and casting are two processes that are often used in the manufacturing of products. Both processes involve creating a negative space in order to create a positive product. However, there are some key differences between moulding and casting that you should be aware of.

- Moulding is the process of shaping a material by using a rigid frame or model called a mould. The mould can be made of various materials, such as metal, wood, plastic, or clay. The material to be moulded is usually a liquid or a pliable solid that can fill the mould cavity. Examples of moulding materials are molten metal, clay, resin, wax, or rubber. Moulding can produce complex shapes and details, but it requires a separate mould for each product. Moulding can be classified into different types, such as sand moulding, die casting, injection moulding, blow moulding, etc.

- Casting is the process of pouring a liquid material into a mould and letting it solidify. The mould can be made of various materials, such as metal, plaster, sand, or wax. The material to be casted is usually a metal, a plastic, or a ceramic that can melt and flow into the mould cavity. Examples of casting materials are iron, steel, aluminum, bronze, brass, copper, zinc, lead, tin, pewter, etc. Casting can produce simple or complex shapes, but it does not require a separate mould for each product. Casting can be classified into different types, such as sand casting, investment casting, die casting, centrifugal casting, etc.

A moulding and casting shop is a place where moulding and casting processes are performed. A moulding and casting shop may have different equipment and tools for different types of moulding and casting, such as furnaces, crucibles, ladles, mould boxes, moulding machines, casting machines, etc. A moulding and casting shop may also have different materials and supplies for different types of moulding and casting, such as moulding sand, binders, cores, patterns, metal alloys, fluxes, degassers, etc. A moulding and casting shop may produce various products, such as sculpture, jewelry, tools, parts, etc.
