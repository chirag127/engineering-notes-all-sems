#### 2. Mechanical Workshop Practice, K C John, PHI

- This is a book written by K C John and published by PHI Learning in 2010 .
- The book covers the basic concepts and skills of various workshop processes and techniques used in mechanical engineering  .
- The book is divided into 12 chapters, each focusing on a different workshop process or technique, such as:
  - Chapter 1: Introduction to Workshop Practice
  - Chapter 2: Measuring Instruments and Gauges
  - Chapter 3: Fitting
  - Chapter 4: Sheet Metal Work
  - Chapter 5: Carpentry
  - Chapter 6: Pattern Making
  - Chapter 7: Foundry
  - Chapter 8: Welding
  - Chapter 9: Smithy and Forging
  - Chapter 10: Machining
  - Chapter 11: Surface Finishing
  - Chapter 12: Heat Treatment
- The book describes the general principles of each workshop process, such as primary and secondary shaping processes, metal joining methods, surface finishing and heat treatment  .
- The book also explains the tools, equipment, materials, methods, safety precautions and applications of each workshop process, with diagrams, tables, examples and exercises  .
- The book is intended for undergraduate students of mechanical engineering and related disciplines, as well as for professionals and hobbyists who want to learn the basics of workshop practice  .
