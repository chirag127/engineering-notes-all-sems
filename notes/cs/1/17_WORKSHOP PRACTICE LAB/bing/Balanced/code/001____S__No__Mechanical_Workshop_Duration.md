## S. No. Mechanical Workshop Duration

- S. No. stands for serial number, which is a way of numbering items in a list or a table.
- Mechanical workshop is a practical course that teaches students the basic skills and knowledge of working with different types of materials, tools and machines in a mechanical engineering context.
- Duration is the amount of time that something lasts or takes to complete.
- The table below shows an example of how to use S. No., mechanical workshop and duration to organize a list of workshop activities.

| S. No. | Mechanical Workshop | Duration |
| ------ | ------------------- | -------- |
| 1      | Carpentry           | 2 hours  |
| 2      | Fitting             | 1.5 hours|
| 3      | Welding             | 2 hours  |
| 4      | Machining           | 3 hours  |
| 5      | Sheet metal work    | 2 hours  |

- The table can be used to plan, schedule and track the progress of the workshop activities. It can also help to evaluate the learning outcomes and the performance of the students.
