#### 2. Preparation of U or V -Shape Male Female Work piece which contains: Filing, Sawing, Drilling, Grinding.

- A U or V shape male female work piece is a type of metal bending that involves creating a U or V shaped groove on one piece of metal and a matching protrusion on another piece of metal, so that they can fit together snugly.
- The process of preparing a U or V shape male female work piece consists of four main steps: filing, sawing, drilling, and grinding.
- Filing is the process of smoothing and shaping the metal surfaces using a metal file. Filing helps to remove any burrs, rough edges, or irregularities that may interfere with the bending or fitting of the metal pieces.
- Sawing is the process of cutting the metal pieces to the desired length and width using a metal saw. Sawing helps to create the basic outline of the U or V shape on the metal pieces.
- Drilling is the process of making holes on the metal pieces using a metal drill. Drilling helps to create the pivot points for the bending of the metal pieces, as well as the attachment points for the male and female parts.
- Grinding is the process of finishing and polishing the metal surfaces using a metal grinder. Grinding helps to remove any excess material, sharp edges, or imperfections that may affect the quality or appearance of the U or V shape male female work piece.
