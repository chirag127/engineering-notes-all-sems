# Introduction to Mechanical workshop material, tools and machines

## Objectives

- To understand the basic concepts of mechanical workshop
- To identify the common materials, tools and machines used in mechanical workshop
- To learn the safety precautions and rules for working in mechanical workshop

## Mechanical workshop

- A mechanical workshop is a place where various mechanical operations are performed on different materials, such as metal, wood, plastic, etc.
- A mechanical workshop may have different sections, such as fitting, carpentry, welding, machining, sheet metal, etc., depending on the type of work and products.
- A mechanical workshop requires various tools and machines to perform different tasks, such as cutting, drilling, shaping, joining, finishing, etc.
- A mechanical workshop also requires proper safety measures and equipment to prevent accidents and injuries.

## Materials

- The materials used in mechanical workshop are mainly classified into two types: metals and non-metals.
- Metals are materials that have high strength, ductility, conductivity, and resistance to corrosion. They are further classified into ferrous and non-ferrous metals, depending on the presence of iron in them.
- Ferrous metals are metals that contain iron, such as steel, cast iron, wrought iron, etc. They are magnetic, heavy, and prone to rusting.
- Non-ferrous metals are metals that do not contain iron, such as aluminum, copper, brass, bronze, etc. They are non-magnetic, light, and resistant to corrosion.
- Non-metals are materials that have low strength, brittleness, insulation, and combustibility. They are further classified into organic and inorganic non-metals, depending on the presence of carbon in them.
- Organic non-metals are materials that contain carbon, such as wood, rubber, plastic, etc. They are biodegradable, flexible, and flammable.
- Inorganic non-metals are materials that do not contain carbon, such as glass, ceramic, stone, etc. They are non-biodegradable, rigid, and non-flammable.

## Tools

- The tools used in mechanical workshop are mainly classified into two types: hand tools and power tools.
- Hand tools are tools that are operated by human force, such as hammer, screwdriver, wrench, file, etc. They are simple, cheap, and portable.
- Power tools are tools that are operated by electric, pneumatic, or hydraulic power, such as drill, grinder, saw, lathe, etc. They are complex, expensive, and stationary.
- The tools used in mechanical workshop are also classified according to their functions, such as measuring tools, marking tools, cutting tools, holding tools, joining tools, finishing tools, etc.

## Machines

- The machines used in mechanical workshop are devices that use power to perform mechanical work, such as shaping, machining, forming, etc.
- The machines used in mechanical workshop are mainly classified into two types: conventional machines and CNC machines.
- Conventional machines are machines that are manually controlled by the operator, such as lathe, milling, drilling, etc. They are simple, versatile, and flexible.
- CNC machines are machines that are automatically controlled by a computer program, such as CNC lathe, CNC milling, CNC drilling, etc. They are complex, precise, and efficient.

## Safety precautions and rules

- The safety precautions and rules for working in mechanical workshop are guidelines that help to prevent accidents and injuries, and ensure a safe and healthy work environment.
- Some of the common safety precautions and rules for working in mechanical workshop are:

  - Wear appropriate personal protective equipment, such as gloves, goggles, apron, shoes, etc.
  - Follow the instructions and warnings of the tools and machines, and use them correctly and carefully.
  - Keep the work area clean and organized, and remove any hazards or obstacles.
  - Do not touch or operate any tools or machines that you are not familiar with or authorized to use.
  - Report any defects, damages, or malfunctions of the tools or machines to the supervisor or technician.
  - Do not work under the influence of alcohol, drugs, or fatigue.
  - Do not play, joke, or distract others while working in the workshop.
  - In case of any emergency, stop the work, switch off the power, and seek help or medical attention.
