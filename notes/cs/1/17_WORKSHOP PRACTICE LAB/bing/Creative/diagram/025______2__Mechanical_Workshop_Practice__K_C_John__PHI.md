#### 2. Mechanical Workshop Practice, K C John, PHI

- This is a book written by K C John and published by PHI Learning in 2010.
- The book describes the general principles of different workshop processes such as primary and secondary shaping processes, metal joining methods, surface finishing and heat treatment.
- The book also covers the basic tools and equipment used in various workshops, such as measuring instruments, hand tools, machine tools, welding equipment, and casting equipment.
- The book is intended for the students of mechanical engineering and related disciplines who need to learn the practical aspects of workshop technology.
- The book is divided into 12 chapters, each covering a specific topic related to workshop practice. The chapters are:

  - Chapter 1: Introduction to Workshop Practice
  - Chapter 2: Measuring Instruments
  - Chapter 3: Hand Tools
  - Chapter 4: Machine Tools
  - Chapter 5: Drilling and Reaming
  - Chapter 6: Turning and Milling
  - Chapter 7: Shaping and Planing
  - Chapter 8: Grinding and Finishing
  - Chapter 9: Welding and Cutting
  - Chapter 10: Soldering and Brazing
  - Chapter 11: Casting and Moulding
  - Chapter 12: Heat Treatment and Testing

- The book is written in a simple and concise manner, with numerous diagrams, tables, and examples to illustrate the concepts and procedures. The book also includes review questions and exercises at the end of each chapter to test the understanding of the readers.
