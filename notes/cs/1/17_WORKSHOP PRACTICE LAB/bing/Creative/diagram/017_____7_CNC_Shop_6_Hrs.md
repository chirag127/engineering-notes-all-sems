### 7 CNC Shop 6 Hrs

- CNC stands for Computer Numerical Control, which is a process of using computers to control machine tools such as lathes, mills, routers, and grinders.
- CNC machines can perform various operations such as drilling, cutting, shaping, and engraving on different materials such as metal, wood, plastic, and composite.
- CNC machines are programmed using a code called G-code, which specifies the coordinates, speed, feed, and direction of the tool movement.
- CNC machines have many advantages over conventional machines, such as higher accuracy, precision, efficiency, flexibility, and productivity.
- CNC machines also have some disadvantages, such as higher initial cost, maintenance, and training requirements, as well as safety and environmental issues.
- CNC machines are widely used in various industries, such as aerospace, automotive, medical, military, and manufacturing.
- CNC machines are classified into different types based on the number of axes, the configuration, and the function of the machine. Some common types are:

  - 2-axis CNC machines: These machines have two linear axes, usually X and Y, and can perform simple operations such as turning and milling.
  - 3-axis CNC machines: These machines have three linear axes, usually X, Y, and Z, and can perform more complex operations such as drilling and contouring.
  - 4-axis CNC machines: These machines have four axes, usually X, Y, Z, and A, where A is a rotary axis around one of the linear axes. These machines can perform operations such as indexing and tapping.
  - 5-axis CNC machines: These machines have five axes, usually X, Y, Z, A, and B, where A and B are rotary axes around two of the linear axes. These machines can perform operations such as sculpting and trimming.
  - CNC lathes: These machines have a spindle that holds and rotates the workpiece, and a tool turret that moves along the spindle axis and can hold multiple tools. These machines can perform operations such as turning, facing, threading, and boring.
  - CNC mills: These machines have a spindle that holds and rotates the tool, and a table that holds and moves the workpiece along three or more axes. These machines can perform operations such as milling, drilling, and tapping.
  - CNC routers: These machines have a spindle that holds and rotates the tool, and a table that holds and moves the workpiece along two or more axes. These machines can perform operations such as cutting, carving, and engraving on wood, plastic, and composite materials.
  - CNC grinders: These machines have a grinding wheel that rotates and contacts the workpiece, and a table that holds and moves the workpiece along one or more axes. These machines can perform operations such as grinding, sharpening, and polishing on metal and ceramic materials.
