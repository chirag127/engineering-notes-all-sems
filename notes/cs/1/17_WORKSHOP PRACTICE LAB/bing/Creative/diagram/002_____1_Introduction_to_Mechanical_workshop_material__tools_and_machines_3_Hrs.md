### 1 Introduction to Mechanical workshop material, tools and machines 3 Hrs

- Mechanical workshop is a place where various types of materials, tools and machines are used to perform different operations such as cutting, shaping, drilling, joining, etc.
- The main objectives of mechanical workshop are to:
  - Provide practical exposure to the students about the use and application of different materials, tools and machines.
  - Develop the skills and abilities of the students to perform various operations with accuracy and precision.
  - Enhance the creativity and problem-solving abilities of the students by giving them various tasks and projects.
- The main components of mechanical workshop are:
  - Materials: These are the raw materials or workpieces that are used to make various products or parts. Examples of materials are metals, alloys, plastics, wood, etc.
  - Tools: These are the devices or instruments that are used to perform various operations on the materials. Examples of tools are hammers, chisels, files, hacksaws, wrenches, screwdrivers, etc.
  - Machines: These are the machines or equipment that are used to perform various operations on the materials with the help of tools. Examples of machines are lathes, milling machines, drilling machines, welding machines, etc.
- The main types of mechanical workshop are:
  - Fitting shop: This is the workshop where various operations such as filing, sawing, chipping, tapping, etc. are performed on the materials to fit them together or to make them into desired shapes and sizes.
  - Carpentry shop: This is the workshop where various operations such as cutting, planning, joining, etc. are performed on the wood or wooden materials to make various products such as furniture, doors, windows, etc.
  - Sheet metal shop: This is the workshop where various operations such as cutting, bending, forming, riveting, etc. are performed on the sheet metal or thin metal materials to make various products such as ducts, containers, cabinets, etc.
  - Welding shop: This is the workshop where various operations such as arc welding, gas welding, spot welding, etc. are performed on the metal or metallic materials to join them together or to make them into desired shapes and sizes.
  - Machine shop: This is the workshop where various operations such as turning, milling, drilling, grinding, etc. are performed on the metal or metallic materials to make various products such as shafts, gears, pulleys, etc.
  - Foundry shop: This is the workshop where various operations such as melting, molding, casting, etc. are performed on the metal or metallic materials to make various products such as engine blocks, pistons, valves, etc.
