### 4 Carpentry Shop 3 Hrs

- Carpentry is the craft of cutting, shaping, and joining wood to make structures, furniture, and other items.
- A carpentry shop is a place where carpenters work with various tools and materials to create wood products.
- Some of the common tools used in a carpentry shop are:
  - Saws: used to cut wood into different shapes and sizes. There are different types of saws, such as hand saws, circular saws, jigsaws, and table saws.
  - Hammers: used to drive nails, screws, and other fasteners into wood. There are different types of hammers, such as claw hammers, ball-peen hammers, and mallets.
  - Chisels: used to carve, shape, and smooth wood. There are different types of chisels, such as bevel-edge chisels, mortise chisels, and gouges.
  - Planes: used to shave, level, and smooth wood surfaces. There are different types of planes, such as block planes, jack planes, and smoothing planes.
  - Squares: used to measure and mark angles and alignments on wood. There are different types of squares, such as try squares, combination squares, and framing squares.
  - Levels: used to check the horizontal and vertical alignment of wood. There are different types of levels, such as spirit levels, bubble levels, and laser levels.
  - Clamps: used to hold wood pieces together while working on them. There are different types of clamps, such as C-clamps, bar clamps, and pipe clamps.
  - Drills: used to make holes in wood. There are different types of drills, such as hand drills, electric drills, and cordless drills.
  - Sanders: used to smooth and polish wood surfaces. There are different types of sanders, such as belt sanders, orbital sanders, and disc sanders.
  - Routers: used to create grooves, edges, and shapes on wood. There are different types of routers, such as fixed-base routers, plunge routers, and trim routers.
- Some of the common materials used in a carpentry shop are:
  - Wood: the main material used in carpentry. There are different types of wood, such as hardwood, softwood, plywood, and particleboard.
  - Nails: used to join wood pieces together. There are different types of nails, such as common nails, finishing nails, and brad nails.
  - Screws: used to join wood pieces together. There are different types of screws, such as wood screws, drywall screws, and deck screws.
  - Glue: used to bond wood pieces together. There are different types of glue, such as wood glue, epoxy glue, and polyurethane glue.
  - Varnish: used to protect and enhance the appearance of wood. There are different types of varnish, such as oil-based varnish, water-based varnish, and shellac varnish.
  - Paint: used to color and decorate wood. There are different types of paint, such as latex paint, oil paint, and acrylic paint.
  - Stain: used to change the color and grain of wood. There are different types of stain, such as oil-based stain, water-based stain, and gel stain.
- Some of the common products made in a carpentry shop are:
  - Furniture: such as tables, chairs, cabinets, shelves, and beds.
  - Doors: such as panel doors, flush doors, and sliding doors.
  - Windows: such as casement windows, sash windows, and bay windows.
  - Frames: such as picture frames, mirror frames, and door frames.
  - Toys: such as wooden blocks, puzzles, and cars.
  - Crafts: such as boxes, trays, and coasters.
