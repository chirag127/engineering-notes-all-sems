#### Total 33 Hrs

- Total 33 Hrs is a time management technique that aims to help students achieve their academic goals in a balanced and efficient way.
- The technique is based on the idea that students need to spend 33 hours per week on their studies, divided into three categories: 11 hours of lectures, 11 hours of self-study, and 11 hours of revision and practice.
- The benefits of using Total 33 Hrs are:
  - It helps students plan their weekly schedule and prioritize their tasks.
  - It ensures that students cover all the aspects of learning: input, output, and feedback.
  - It reduces stress and procrastination by breaking down the workload into manageable chunks.
  - It improves students' retention and understanding of the material by reinforcing it through different modes of learning.
  - It boosts students' confidence and motivation by showing them their progress and achievements.
- The steps of implementing Total 33 Hrs are:
  - Identify your weekly goals and deadlines for each subject or course.
  - Allocate 11 hours of lectures per week, either in person or online, and attend them regularly and actively.
  - Allocate 11 hours of self-study per week, and use them to review the lecture notes, read the textbooks, do the assignments, and research the topics.
  - Allocate 11 hours of revision and practice per week, and use them to test yourself, solve problems, do past papers, and get feedback from peers or teachers.
  - Adjust your schedule and hours as needed, depending on your workload, difficulty, and preferences.
  - Track your time and tasks using a planner, a calendar, or an app, and review your performance and results at the end of each week.
