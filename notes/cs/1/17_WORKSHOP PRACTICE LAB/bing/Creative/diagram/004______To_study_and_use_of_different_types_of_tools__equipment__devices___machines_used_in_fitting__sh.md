#### To study and use of different types of tools, equipment, devices & machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section.

- Fitting is the process of assembling, adjusting, and repairing parts or components to make them fit together properly and function well.
- Sheet metal is the metal that has been formed into thin and flat pieces, which can be cut, bent, and shaped into various products or structures.
- Welding is the process of joining two or more pieces of metal by applying heat, pressure, or both, to create a strong and permanent bond.

Some of the common tools, equipment, devices, and machines used in these processes are:

- **Planishing hammer**: A planishing hammer is used as a sheet metal tool to flatten, finely shape, straighten, and smooth sheet surfaces. It consists of a metal head with a flat or slightly curved face and a wooden or metal handle. The hammer is struck against a metal stake or anvil to shape the sheet metal.
- **Angle grinder**: An angle grinder is a power tool that can be used for cutting, grinding, polishing, and sanding metal. It has a rotating abrasive disc that can be attached with different types of blades or attachments depending on the material and the task. Angle grinders are versatile and can be used for fitting, sheet metal, and welding applications.
- **Cleco fastener**: A Cleco fastener is a temporary fastening device that is used to hold two or more pieces of sheet metal together during drilling, riveting, or welding. It consists of a cylindrical body with a spring-loaded plunger and a pair of jaws that can grip the sheet metal. Cleco fasteners are inserted into pre-drilled holes and can be easily removed after the permanent fastening is done.
- **Wire cutter**: A wire cutter is a hand tool that is used to cut wires or thin metal rods. It has a pair of sharp blades that can shear through the wire or rod with a squeezing action. Wire cutters are useful for cutting excess metal or trimming wires during fitting, sheet metal, and welding processes.
- **Punch flange**: A punch flange is a sheet metal tool that is used to create a flange or a raised edge around a hole in the sheet metal. It consists of a metal punch and a die that can be operated by a lever or a hydraulic press. The punch flange can create a uniform and smooth flange that can be used for joining or reinforcing the sheet metal.
- **Hydraulic press brake**: A hydraulic press brake is a machine that is used to bend sheet metal into various shapes and angles. It consists of a fixed lower bed and a movable upper beam that can apply a high amount of force to the sheet metal. The sheet metal is placed between a punch and a die that have the desired shape and angle. The hydraulic press brake can bend the sheet metal with precision and accuracy.
- **Swing-beam shear**: A swing-beam shear is a machine that is used to cut sheet metal into smaller pieces or strips. It consists of a fixed lower blade and a movable upper blade that can swing down to cut the sheet metal. The sheet metal is fed into the machine and clamped between the blades. The swing-beam shear can cut the sheet metal with speed and efficiency.
- **Ironworker**: An ironworker is a machine that is used to perform various tasks on metal, such as punching, shearing, notching, bending, and forming. It consists of a hydraulic or mechanical system that can operate different types of tools or attachments. The metal is placed into the machine and the desired tool or attachment is activated to perform the task. Ironworkers are versatile and can be used for fitting, sheet metal, and welding applications.
- **Plate roll**: A plate roll is a machine that is used to roll sheet metal into cylindrical or conical shapes. It consists of three or four rollers that can rotate and bend the sheet metal. The sheet metal is fed into the machine and the rollers are adjusted to create the desired shape. Plate rolls can create smooth and uniform curves on the sheet metal.
- **Profile roll**: A profile roll is a machine that is used to roll metal bars, rods, tubes, or profiles into circular or spiral shapes. It consists of three or four rollers that can rotate and bend the metal. The metal is fed into the machine and the rollers are adjusted to create the desired shape. Profile rolls can create complex and intricate shapes on the metal.
