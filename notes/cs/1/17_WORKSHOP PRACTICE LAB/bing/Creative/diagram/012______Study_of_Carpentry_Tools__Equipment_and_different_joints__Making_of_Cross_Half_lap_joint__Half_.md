#### Study of Carpentry Tools, Equipment and different joints. Making of Cross Half lap joint, Half lap Dovetail joint and Mortise Tenon Joint

Carpentry is the skill of making and repairing wooden objects and structures. Carpentry tools and equipment are the instruments and devices that carpenters use to perform various tasks such as measuring, cutting, joining, shaping, and finishing wood. Different types of carpentry tools and equipment have different functions and purposes.

Some of the common carpentry tools and equipment are:

- **Hammer**: A tool with a metal head and a wooden or plastic handle, used to drive nails, pins, or other fasteners into wood or other materials. There are different types of hammers, such as framing, trim, roofing, claw, ball-peen, and sledge hammers, depending on the size, shape, and weight of the head and the intended use.
- **Saw**: A tool with a blade that has teeth along one edge, used to cut wood or other materials by moving the blade back and forth. There are different types of saws, such as hand saws, circular saws, jigsaws, band saws, table saws, and miter saws, depending on the shape, size, and power of the blade and the intended use.
- **Pliers**: A tool with two metal jaws that can be opened and closed by a handle, used to grip, bend, cut, or twist wires, nails, or other materials. There are different types of pliers, such as needle-nose, slip-joint, locking, diagonal, and wire-stripping pliers, depending on the shape and size of the jaws and the intended use.
- **Chisel**: A tool with a metal blade that has a sharp edge at one end and a wooden or plastic handle at the other, used to cut, carve, or shape wood or other materials by striking the blade with a hammer or a mallet. There are different types of chisels, such as flat, gouge, mortise, and dovetail chisels, depending on the shape and size of the blade and the intended use.
- **Drill**: A tool with a rotating bit that has a sharp point or edge at one end, used to make holes in wood or other materials by pushing the bit into the material. There are different types of drills, such as hand drills, electric drills, cordless drills, and drill presses, depending on the power source, size, and speed of the bit and the intended use.
- **Screwdriver**: A tool with a metal shaft that has a flat or cross-shaped tip at one end and a wooden or plastic handle at the other, used to turn screws or other fasteners with matching slots or heads. There are different types of screwdrivers, such as flat-head, Phillips, Torx, and hex screwdrivers, depending on the shape and size of the tip and the intended use.

Some of the common carpentry joints are:

- **Cross half lap joint**: A joint where two pieces of wood are cut halfway through their thickness and overlapped at right angles, forming a cross shape. The joint is secured by glue, nails, or screws. This joint is used to make frames, ladders, or grids.
- **Half lap dovetail joint**: A joint where two pieces of wood are cut halfway through their thickness and overlapped at an angle, forming a dovetail shape. The joint is secured by glue, nails, or screws. This joint is used to make corners, boxes, or drawers.
- **Mortise and tenon joint**: A joint where one piece of wood has a hole (mortise) cut into it and another piece of wood has a projection (tenon) that fits into the hole. The joint is secured by glue, wedges, or pins. This joint is used to make frames, doors, or furniture.
