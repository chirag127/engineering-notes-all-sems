# Engineering Materials and Manufacturing Processes

## CO1: Use various engineering materials, tools, machines and measuring equipments.

- Engineering materials are the substances that are used to create or manufacture products, structures, machines, and systems. They can be classified into four main categories: metals, ceramics, polymers, and composites.
- Engineering tools are the devices or instruments that are used to perform various tasks or operations in engineering, such as cutting, drilling, shaping, measuring, etc. Some examples of engineering tools are hammers, saws, wrenches, screwdrivers, calipers, micrometers, etc.
- Engineering machines are the machines that are used to produce or modify engineering materials, products, or components. They can be classified into two main types: conventional machines and computer numerical control (CNC) machines. Conventional machines are operated manually or mechanically, while CNC machines are controlled by a computer program. Some examples of engineering machines are lathes, milling machines, drilling machines, grinding machines, etc.
- Engineering measuring equipments are the devices or instruments that are used to measure or test various physical properties or dimensions of engineering materials, products, or components. They can be classified into two main types: direct measuring equipments and indirect measuring equipments. Direct measuring equipments are those that give the measurement directly, such as rulers, scales, vernier calipers, micrometers, etc. Indirect measuring equipments are those that give the measurement indirectly, such as gauges, indicators, sensors, etc.

## CO2: Perform machine operations in lathe and CNC machine.

- A lathe is a machine that rotates a workpiece about an axis of rotation and performs various operations on it, such as turning, facing, knurling, threading, etc. The main parts of a lathe are the headstock, tailstock, carriage, bed, and spindle. The headstock contains the motor and the spindle, which holds and rotates the workpiece. The tailstock supports the other end of the workpiece and can be moved along the bed. The carriage contains the tool post, which holds and moves the cutting tool along the workpiece. The bed is the base of the machine and supports the headstock, tailstock, and carriage.
- A CNC machine is a machine that is controlled by a computer program and can perform various operations on a workpiece, such as milling, drilling, tapping, etc. The main parts of a CNC machine are the controller, spindle, table, and tool changer. The controller contains the computer and the software that controls the machine. The spindle holds and rotates the cutting tool. The table holds and moves the workpiece along the spindle. The tool changer stores and changes the cutting tools automatically.
- Some of the machine operations that can be performed in a lathe and a CNC machine are:

  - Turning: It is the operation of removing excess material from the outer surface of a cylindrical workpiece by a single-point cutting tool.
  - Facing: It is the operation of removing excess material from the end surface of a cylindrical workpiece by a single-point cutting tool.
  - Knurling: It is the operation of producing a pattern of grooves or ridges on the outer surface of a cylindrical workpiece by a knurling tool.
  - Threading: It is the operation of producing a helical groove or thread on the outer or inner surface of a cylindrical workpiece by a single-point or multi-point cutting tool.
  - Milling: It is the operation of removing excess material from the surface of a workpiece by a multi-point cutting tool called a milling cutter.
  - Drilling: It is the operation of producing a hole in a workpiece by a multi-point cutting tool called a drill bit.
  - Tapping: It is the operation of producing a thread in a hole by a multi-point cutting tool called a tap.
