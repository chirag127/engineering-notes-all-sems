# 4 Carpentry Shop 3 Hrs

- Carpentry is the skill of making and repairing wooden objects and structures, such as furniture, doors, windows, stairs, etc.
- A carpentry shop is a place where carpenters work with various tools and materials to create and fix wooden items.
- A carpentry shop may have different sections or areas for different purposes, such as:
  - A cutting area, where wood is measured and cut to the desired size and shape using saws, chisels, planes, etc.
  - A joining area, where wood pieces are joined together using nails, screws, glue, dowels, etc.
  - A finishing area, where wood surfaces are smoothed, sanded, stained, painted, varnished, etc.
  - A storage area, where wood and other materials are kept and organized.
  - A display area, where finished products are showcased and sold to customers.
- A carpentry shop may also have some safety equipment and rules to prevent accidents and injuries, such as:
  - Wearing protective gear, such as gloves, goggles, masks, etc.
  - Using tools properly and carefully, and keeping them sharp and clean.
  - Keeping the work area tidy and free of clutter and debris.
  - Avoiding distractions and following instructions.
  - Reporting any hazards or problems to the supervisor or manager.
