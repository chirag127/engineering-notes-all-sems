# 7 CNC Shop 6 Hrs

- CNC stands for Computer Numerical Control, which is a process of using computers to control machine tools such as lathes, mills, routers, and grinders.
- CNC machines can perform various operations such as drilling, cutting, shaping, and engraving on different materials such as metal, wood, plastic, and composite.
- CNC machines are programmed using a code called G-code, which specifies the coordinates, speed, feed, and direction of the tool movement.
- CNC machines can also use a code called M-code, which controls the auxiliary functions of the machine such as spindle rotation, coolant flow, and tool change.
- CNC machines can produce high-quality and precise parts with less waste, less human error, and less labor cost than conventional machines.
- CNC machines can also be integrated with other technologies such as CAD (Computer-Aided Design), CAM (Computer-Aided Manufacturing), and CMM (Coordinate Measuring Machine) to create a complete manufacturing system.
- CNC machines require regular maintenance, calibration, and inspection to ensure their optimal performance and safety. Some common problems that can affect CNC machines are tool wear, backlash, thermal expansion, and vibration.
