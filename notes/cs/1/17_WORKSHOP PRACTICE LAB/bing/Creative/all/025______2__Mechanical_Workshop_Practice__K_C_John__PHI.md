# Mechanical Workshop Practice, K C John, PHI

Mechanical Workshop Practice is a book written by K C John and published by PHI Learning in 2010. It is intended for students of mechanical engineering and related disciplines who need to learn the basic skills and techniques of workshop practice. The book covers the following topics:

- Introduction to workshop practice, safety, tools and equipment, measurement and layout, and engineering drawing.
- Primary shaping processes, such as casting, forging, rolling, extrusion, drawing, and sheet metal forming.
- Secondary shaping processes, such as machining, turning, milling, drilling, shaping, planing, slotting, and grinding.
- Metal joining methods, such as welding, brazing, soldering, riveting, and fastening.
- Surface finishing and heat treatment, such as filing, scraping, lapping, honing, polishing, electroplating, anodizing, hardening, tempering, annealing, and case hardening.

The book provides theoretical explanations, practical examples, illustrations, and exercises to help the students understand and apply the concepts and skills of workshop practice. The book also includes a glossary of technical terms and a list of references for further reading.
