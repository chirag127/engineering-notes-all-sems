#### 1. Workshop Practice, H S Bawa, McGraw Hill

- This is a book that covers the basic concepts and skills of workshop technology for engineering students.
- The book is divided into 16 chapters, each focusing on a different aspect of workshop practice, such as safety, tools, materials, machining, welding, fitting, carpentry, sheet metal work, plumbing, electrical wiring, etc.
- The book provides theoretical explanations, practical examples, diagrams, illustrations, exercises, and review questions for each chapter.
- The book aims to help students develop the necessary knowledge and skills for working in various types of workshops, such as mechanical, electrical, civil, and electronics.
- The book also covers the latest trends and developments in workshop technology, such as CNC machines, CAD/CAM, 3D printing, robotics, etc.
