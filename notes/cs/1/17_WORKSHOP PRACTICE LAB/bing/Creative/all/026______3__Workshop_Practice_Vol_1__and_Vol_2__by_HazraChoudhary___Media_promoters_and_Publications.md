#### 3. Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2, by HazraChoudhary , Media promoters and Publications

- Workshop Practice is a course that introduces the students to the basic concepts and skills of various workshop processes, such as fitting, sheet metal, soldering, welding, carpentry, etc.
- The course aims to develop the students' practical knowledge and abilities to perform different types of workshop operations and to use various tools and machines safely and efficiently.
- The course also helps the students to understand the applications and limitations of different workshop processes and materials in engineering and manufacturing.
- The course is divided into two volumes: Volume 1 and Volume 2, written by S.K. Hajra Choudhury and published by Media Promoters and Publications.
- Volume 1 covers the topics of manufacturing processes, such as casting, forging, metal cutting, machining, joining, powder metallurgy, etc. It also includes the theory and practice of metrology, inspection and quality control.
- Volume 2 covers the topics of machine tools, such as lathe, drilling, milling, shaping, planing, slotting, grinding, etc. It also includes the theory and practice of computer numerical control (CNC), automation and robotics.
- The books are written in a simple and lucid language, with numerous diagrams, tables, charts and examples. The books also contain exercises, review questions and objective type questions at the end of each chapter.
- The books are suitable for undergraduate students of mechanical engineering and related disciplines, as well as for diploma and vocational courses. The books are also useful for practicing engineers and technicians who want to update their knowledge and skills in workshop practice.
