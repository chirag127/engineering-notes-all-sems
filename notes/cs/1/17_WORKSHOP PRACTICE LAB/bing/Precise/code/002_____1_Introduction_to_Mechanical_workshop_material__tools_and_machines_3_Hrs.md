### 1 Introduction to Mechanical workshop material, tools and machines 3 Hrs

A mechanical workshop is a place where various materials, tools, and machines are used to manufacture, repair, or maintain mechanical systems. In this section, we will introduce some common materials, tools, and machines found in a mechanical workshop.

#### Materials
- **Metals**: Metals such as steel, aluminum, and copper are commonly used in mechanical workshops due to their strength, durability, and conductivity.
- **Plastics**: Plastics are also commonly used in mechanical workshops due to their versatility and low cost. Some common types of plastics used in mechanical workshops include polyethylene, polypropylene, and PVC.
- **Wood**: Wood is often used in mechanical workshops for its strength, durability, and aesthetic appeal.

#### Tools
- **Hand tools**: Hand tools such as hammers, screwdrivers, and pliers are commonly used in mechanical workshops for tasks such as fastening, cutting, and shaping.
- **Power tools**: Power tools such as drills, saws, and grinders are also commonly used in mechanical workshops for tasks that require more power and speed.
- **Measuring tools**: Measuring tools such as rulers, calipers, and micrometers are used in mechanical workshops to ensure accuracy and precision in the manufacturing process.

#### Machines
- **Lathes**: Lathes are machines used in mechanical workshops to shape materials such as metal, wood, or plastic by rotating them against a cutting tool.
- **Milling machines**: Milling machines are used in mechanical workshops to cut and shape materials using a rotating cutting tool.
- **Drilling machines**: Drilling machines are used in mechanical workshops to create holes in materials using a rotating drill bit.

In summary, a mechanical workshop is a place where various materials, tools, and machines are used to manufacture, repair, or maintain mechanical systems. Understanding the common materials, tools, and machines found in a mechanical workshop is essential for anyone working in or studying the field of mechanics.
