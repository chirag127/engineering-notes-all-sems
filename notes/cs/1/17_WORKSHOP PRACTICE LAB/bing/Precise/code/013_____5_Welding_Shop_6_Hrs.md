### 5 Welding Shop 6 Hrs

Welding is a fabrication process that joins two or more parts by heating the surfaces to their melting points and applying pressure to fuse them together. Welding is used in a variety of industries, including construction, manufacturing, and repair.

A welding shop is a facility where welding operations are performed. A typical welding shop may include the following equipment:

1. Welding machines: These are used to generate the heat and electrical current required for welding.
2. Welding torches: These are used to direct the heat and electrical current to the area being welded.
3. Welding electrodes: These are used to conduct the electrical current to the area being welded.
4. Protective equipment: This includes items such as welding helmets, gloves, and aprons to protect the welder from heat, sparks, and ultraviolet radiation.
5. Hand tools: These include items such as hammers, pliers, and wire brushes for preparing and finishing the weld.

A 6-hour welding shop session may involve a variety of activities, including:

1. Preparing the materials to be welded: This may involve cutting, shaping, and cleaning the materials.
2. Setting up the welding equipment: This may involve selecting the appropriate welding machine, torch, and electrode for the job.
3. Welding the materials: This involves using the welding equipment to join the materials together.
4. Finishing the weld: This may involve grinding, sanding, or polishing the weld to improve its appearance and strength.
5. Cleaning up: This involves disposing of any waste materials and returning the equipment to its proper storage location.

Welding is a skilled trade that requires training and practice to master. A 6-hour welding shop session can provide valuable hands-on experience for those learning the trade.
