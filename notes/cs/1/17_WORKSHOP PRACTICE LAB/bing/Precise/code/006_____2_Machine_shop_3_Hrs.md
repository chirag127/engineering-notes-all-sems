### Machine Shop (3 hours)

A machine shop is a facility where machining is performed. Machining is the process of removing material from a workpiece to create a desired shape or surface. Here are some key points to know about machine shops:

1. **Machining equipment**: Machine shops typically contain a variety of equipment used for cutting, shaping, drilling, and finishing metal and other materials. Some common types of equipment found in machine shops include lathes, milling machines, drill presses, and grinders.

2. **Skilled workers**: Machine shops employ skilled workers, such as machinists and tool and die makers, who are trained to operate the equipment and produce precision parts.

3. **Types of work**: Machine shops can produce a wide range of parts, from small components used in electronics to large, complex parts used in aerospace and other industries. They may also perform repair and maintenance work on machinery and equipment.

4. **Safety**: Safety is a top priority in machine shops, as the equipment can be dangerous if not used properly. Workers must follow strict safety protocols and wear appropriate personal protective equipment.

5. **Quality control**: Machine shops must adhere to strict quality control standards to ensure that the parts they produce meet the required specifications. This may involve using precision measuring instruments to verify the dimensions and tolerances of the parts.

6. **Computer-aided manufacturing**: Many machine shops now use computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) software to control the equipment and automate the production process. This can increase efficiency and accuracy, and reduce the need for manual labor.
