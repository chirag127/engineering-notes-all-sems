### Fitting Shop (3 Hours)

1. A fitting shop is a workshop where the process of fitting, assembling, and finishing of machine parts takes place.
2. The fitting shop is equipped with various hand tools, power tools, and measuring instruments to carry out the fitting process.
3. The fitting process involves shaping, cutting, drilling, filing, and grinding of metal parts to achieve the desired shape and size.
4. The fitting shop is an essential part of any manufacturing industry, as it is responsible for the final assembly and finishing of machine parts.
5. The fitting shop requires skilled workers who are trained in the use of various tools and instruments to carry out the fitting process accurately and efficiently.
6. The duration of the fitting process can vary depending on the complexity of the machine parts being assembled and the level of precision required.
7. In this context, 3 hours may be allocated for the fitting process in the fitting shop. This time may be used for the fitting, assembling, and finishing of machine parts.
