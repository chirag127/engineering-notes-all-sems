# Workshop Practice Lab:

Workshop Practice Lab is a practical course that introduces students to the basic techniques and tools used in various engineering workshops. The course is designed to provide hands-on experience in the use of workshop tools and equipment, and to develop skills in the fabrication of simple engineering components. Some of the key topics covered in the Workshop Practice Lab include:

1. Safety: Students learn about the importance of safety in the workshop, including the use of personal protective equipment and safe working practices.

2. Hand Tools: Students are introduced to the use of common hand tools such as hammers, screwdrivers, pliers, and wrenches.

3. Measuring Instruments: Students learn how to use measuring instruments such as vernier calipers, micrometers, and dial gauges to take accurate measurements.

4. Machine Tools: Students gain experience in the use of machine tools such as lathes, milling machines, and drilling machines.

5. Welding: Students are introduced to the basics of welding, including the use of welding equipment and the different types of welding processes.

6. Sheet Metal Work: Students learn how to cut, bend, and shape sheet metal to create simple components.

7. Fitting: Students learn how to fit and assemble mechanical components using techniques such as filing, sawing, and drilling.

The Workshop Practice Lab provides a solid foundation for students who wish to pursue further studies or careers in engineering and related fields. It is an essential course for anyone who wants to gain practical skills and experience in the use of workshop tools and equipment.
