#### Study of Carpentry Tools, Equipment and different joints. Making of Cross Half lap joint, Half lap Dovetail joint and Mortise Tenon Joint

Carpentry is a skilled trade that involves the cutting, shaping, and installation of building materials during the construction of buildings, ships, timber bridges, concrete formwork, etc. Carpenters use various tools and equipment to carry out their work. Some of the common tools and equipment used in carpentry include:

1. **Saws**: Saws are used to cut wood to the desired size and shape. There are different types of saws, including hand saws, circular saws, and jigsaws.

2. **Hammers**: Hammers are used to drive nails into wood. There are different types of hammers, including claw hammers and framing hammers.

3. **Chisels**: Chisels are used to shape wood by removing small pieces of material. There are different types of chisels, including bevel-edge chisels and mortise chisels.

4. **Planes**: Planes are used to smooth and shape wood. There are different types of planes, including block planes and smoothing planes.

5. **Squares**: Squares are used to ensure that angles are accurate. There are different types of squares, including combination squares and framing squares.

6. **Measuring tools**: Measuring tools are used to ensure that cuts are accurate. There are different types of measuring tools, including tape measures and rulers.

Carpenters use different joints to join pieces of wood together. Some of the common joints used in carpentry include:

1. **Cross Half Lap Joint**: A cross half lap joint is made by removing half of the thickness of two pieces of wood and fitting them together. This joint is strong and is often used in framing.

2. **Half Lap Dovetail Joint**: A half lap dovetail joint is similar to a cross half lap joint, but the ends of the wood are cut in a dovetail shape. This joint is strong and is often used in furniture making.

3. **Mortise and Tenon Joint**: A mortise and tenon joint is made by cutting a mortise (hole) in one piece of wood and a tenon (tongue) on the other piece of wood. The tenon is then inserted into the mortise and the joint is secured with glue or a peg. This joint is strong and is often used in furniture making.

In conclusion, carpentry involves the use of various tools and equipment to cut, shape, and install building materials. Carpenters use different joints to join pieces of wood together, including cross half lap joints, half lap dovetail joints, and mortise and tenon joints. These joints are strong and are used in various applications, including framing and furniture making.
