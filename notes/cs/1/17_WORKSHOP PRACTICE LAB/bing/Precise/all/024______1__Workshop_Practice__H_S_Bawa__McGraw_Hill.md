#### 1. Workshop Practice, H S Bawa, McGraw Hill

Workshop Practice is a book written by H S Bawa and published by McGraw Hill. It is a comprehensive guide to the various aspects of workshop practice, including:

- Introduction to workshop practice
- Safety in the workshop
- Hand tools and their uses
- Measuring instruments and their uses
- Marking and layout tools
- Cutting tools and their uses
- Machine tools and their operations
- Joining processes
- Finishing processes
- Maintenance of tools and equipment

This book is a valuable resource for students and professionals alike, providing detailed information and practical guidance on the various aspects of workshop practice. It is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow. The book is well-organized and includes numerous illustrations and diagrams to aid in understanding the concepts presented.
