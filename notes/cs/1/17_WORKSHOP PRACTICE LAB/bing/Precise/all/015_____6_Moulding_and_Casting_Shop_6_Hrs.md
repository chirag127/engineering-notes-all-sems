# 6 Moulding and Casting Shop 6 Hrs

Moulding and casting are manufacturing processes used to produce complex shapes. These processes involve pouring molten metal into a mould, which contains a hollow cavity of the desired shape, and then allowing the metal to cool and solidify. The solidified part is then removed from the mould to complete the process.

1. **Moulding** is the process of manufacturing by shaping liquid or pliable raw material using a rigid frame called a mould or matrix.
2. **Casting** is a manufacturing process in which a liquid material is usually poured into a mould, which contains a hollow cavity of the desired shape, and then allowed to solidify.
3. The most common materials used in casting are metals, but other materials such as plastics, glass, and concrete can also be cast.
4. The moulding and casting process can be used to produce a wide variety of products, including engine blocks, pipes, and decorative items.
5. There are several different types of moulding and casting processes, including sand casting, investment casting, and die casting.
6. Each process has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of process will depend on factors such as the desired shape, size, and material of the final product.
