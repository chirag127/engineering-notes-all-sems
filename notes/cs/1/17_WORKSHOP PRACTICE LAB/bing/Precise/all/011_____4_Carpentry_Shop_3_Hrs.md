# 4 Carpentry Shop 3 Hrs

Carpentry is a skilled trade and a craft in which the primary work performed is the cutting, shaping, and installation of building materials during the construction of buildings, ships, timber bridges, concrete formwork, etc. Carpenters traditionally worked with natural wood and did the rougher work such as framing, but today many other materials are also used and sometimes the finer trades of cabinetmaking and furniture building are considered carpentry.

A carpentry shop is a workshop where carpenters work. It is typically equipped with tools and machinery for cutting, shaping, and joining wood. Some common tools and machinery found in a carpentry shop include:

1. Saws: Used for cutting wood to size. Some common types of saws found in a carpentry shop include hand saws, circular saws, and jigsaws.
2. Planes: Used for smoothing and shaping wood. Some common types of planes found in a carpentry shop include hand planes, block planes, and jointer planes.
3. Chisels: Used for cutting and shaping wood. Some common types of chisels found in a carpentry shop include bevel-edge chisels, mortise chisels, and paring chisels.
4. Hammers: Used for driving nails and other fasteners. Some common types of hammers found in a carpentry shop include claw hammers, framing hammers, and ball-peen hammers.
5. Drills: Used for making holes in wood. Some common types of drills found in a carpentry shop include hand drills, power drills, and drill presses.

A carpentry shop may also have specialized machinery such as a table saw, band saw, or lathe for more advanced woodworking tasks.

In a 3-hour session at a carpentry shop, one could expect to learn and practice basic carpentry skills such as measuring, cutting, and joining wood. One could also expect to learn about the different types of wood and their properties, as well as how to select the right wood for a particular project. Additionally, one could expect to learn about the safe and proper use of tools and machinery in the carpentry shop.
