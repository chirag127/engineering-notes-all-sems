#### 3. Workshop Practice Vol 1, and Vol 2, by HazraChoudhary , Media promoters and Publications

- Workshop Practice is a two-volume book set written by HazraChoudhary and published by Media Promoters and Publications.
- The books provide a comprehensive guide to workshop practices and techniques.
- Volume 1 covers topics such as hand tools, measuring instruments, marking and layout, fitting, sheet metal work, and carpentry.
- Volume 2 covers topics such as welding, smithy and forging, foundry, turning, and machine tools.
- The books are written in a clear and concise manner, making them suitable for students and professionals alike.
- The books include numerous diagrams and illustrations to aid in understanding the concepts presented.
- Overall, Workshop Practice Vol 1 and Vol 2 by HazraChoudhary provide a valuable resource for anyone interested in workshop practices and techniques.
