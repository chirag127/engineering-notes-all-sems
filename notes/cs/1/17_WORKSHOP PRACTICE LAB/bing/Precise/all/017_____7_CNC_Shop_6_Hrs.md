### 7 CNC Shop 6 Hrs

1. CNC stands for Computer Numerical Control.
2. A CNC shop is a workshop where CNC machines are used to manufacture parts and components.
3. CNC machines are controlled by computer programs, which allow for precise and accurate cutting, shaping, and drilling of materials.
4. A 6-hour shift in a CNC shop would involve operating and monitoring the CNC machines, loading and unloading materials, and performing quality checks on finished products.
5. CNC shops can produce a wide range of parts and components, from small, intricate pieces to large, complex structures.
6. CNC technology has revolutionized the manufacturing industry, allowing for faster production times and higher levels of precision and accuracy.
7. Working in a CNC shop requires technical knowledge and skills, as well as the ability to read and interpret technical drawings and specifications.
