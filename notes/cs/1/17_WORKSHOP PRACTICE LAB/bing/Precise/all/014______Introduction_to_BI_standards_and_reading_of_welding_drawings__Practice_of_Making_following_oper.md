#### Introduction to BI standards and reading of welding drawings. Practice of Making following operations Butt Joint Lap Joint TIG Welding MIG Welding

BI standards, or Business Intelligence standards, are a set of guidelines and best practices for the design, development, and implementation of BI systems. These standards help ensure that BI systems are effective, efficient, and provide accurate and reliable information to support business decision-making.

Reading welding drawings is an essential skill for anyone involved in the welding industry. Welding drawings provide detailed information about the design and specifications of a welded joint, including the type of joint, the materials to be used, and the welding process to be employed.

Butt Joint: A butt joint is a type of joint where two pieces of material are joined end to end. This type of joint is commonly used in welding to join two pieces of metal together.

Lap Joint: A lap joint is a type of joint where two pieces of material are overlapped and joined together. This type of joint is commonly used in welding to join two pieces of metal together.

TIG Welding: TIG welding, or Tungsten Inert Gas welding, is a type of welding process that uses a non-consumable tungsten electrode to produce the weld. This type of welding is commonly used for welding thin materials and for welding in tight spaces.

MIG Welding: MIG welding, or Metal Inert Gas welding, is a type of welding process that uses a consumable wire electrode to produce the weld. This type of welding is commonly used for welding thicker materials and for welding in open spaces.
