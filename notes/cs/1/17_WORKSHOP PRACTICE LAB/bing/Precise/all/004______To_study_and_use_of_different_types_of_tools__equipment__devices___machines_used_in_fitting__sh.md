#### To study and use of different types of tools, equipment, devices & machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section.

Fitting, sheet metal, and welding are essential processes in the manufacturing industry. These processes require the use of various tools, equipment, devices, and machines to achieve the desired results. Here is a list of some of the most commonly used tools, equipment, devices, and machines in fitting, sheet metal, and welding sections:

1. **Fitting tools:** These include hand tools such as hammers, chisels, files, hacksaws, and wrenches. These tools are used for cutting, shaping, and fastening metal parts.
2. **Sheet metal tools:** These include tools such as shears, punches, and bending brakes. These tools are used for cutting, shaping, and bending sheet metal.
3. **Welding equipment:** This includes equipment such as welding machines, welding torches, and protective gear. Welding equipment is used to join metal parts together using heat and pressure.
4. **Measuring and marking tools:** These include tools such as rulers, calipers, and scribers. These tools are used to measure and mark metal parts for cutting and shaping.
5. **Machines:** These include machines such as lathes, milling machines, and drill presses. These machines are used for cutting, shaping, and drilling metal parts.

It is important to use the right tools, equipment, devices, and machines for each process to achieve the desired results. Proper training and safety precautions should also be followed when using these tools and equipment.
