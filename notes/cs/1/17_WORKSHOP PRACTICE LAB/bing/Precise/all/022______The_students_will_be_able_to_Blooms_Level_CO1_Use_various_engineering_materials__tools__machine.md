#### The students will be able to:

CO1. **Use various engineering materials, tools, machines and measuring equipments.** (Blooms Level: K3)
- Students will learn about the properties and applications of different engineering materials.
- They will be trained to use various tools, machines and measuring equipments in an engineering setting.

CO2. **Perform machine operations in lathe and CNC machine.** (Blooms Level: K3)
- Students will learn to operate lathe and CNC machines.
- They will be able to perform various machining operations such as turning, facing, drilling, and threading.

CO3. **Perform manufacturing operations on components in fitting and carpentry shop.** (Blooms Level: K3)
- Students will learn to perform various manufacturing operations in fitting and carpentry shops.
- They will be able to produce components using techniques such as sawing, chiseling, and planing.

CO4. **Perform operations in welding, moulding, casting and gas cutting.** (Blooms Level: K3)
- Students will learn to perform various operations in welding, moulding, casting and gas cutting.
- They will be able to produce components using techniques such as arc welding, sand casting, and oxy-fuel cutting.

CO5. **Fabricate a job by 3D printing manufacturing technique.** (Blooms Level: K3)
- Students will learn to use 3D printing technology to fabricate components.
- They will be able to produce components using techniques such as fused deposition modeling and stereolithography.
