# Introduction to Mechanical Workshop Material, Tools and Machines

Mechanical workshop is a place where various materials, tools and machines are used to manufacture, repair or maintain mechanical systems. In this section, we will discuss the following topics:

1. **Materials**: Different types of materials used in mechanical workshop such as metals, plastics, composites, etc.
2. **Tools**: Various hand tools and power tools used in mechanical workshop such as hammers, screwdrivers, drills, etc.
3. **Machines**: Different types of machines used in mechanical workshop such as lathes, milling machines, drilling machines, etc.

## Materials

In a mechanical workshop, various materials are used to manufacture, repair or maintain mechanical systems. Some of the commonly used materials are:

- **Metals**: Metals such as steel, aluminum, copper, etc. are widely used in mechanical workshop due to their strength, durability and other properties.
- **Plastics**: Plastics are also used in mechanical workshop due to their lightweight, corrosion resistance and other properties.
- **Composites**: Composites are materials made by combining two or more different materials to achieve desired properties. They are widely used in mechanical workshop due to their high strength-to-weight ratio, corrosion resistance, etc.

## Tools

Various hand tools and power tools are used in mechanical workshop to carry out different tasks. Some of the commonly used tools are:

- **Hammers**: Hammers are used to drive nails, fit parts, forge metal, etc.
- **Screwdrivers**: Screwdrivers are used to turn screws with slotted heads.
- **Drills**: Drills are used to make holes in materials.

## Machines

Different types of machines are used in mechanical workshop to carry out various tasks. Some of the commonly used machines are:

- **Lathes**: Lathes are used to shape materials by rotating them against a cutting tool.
- **Milling machines**: Milling machines are used to shape materials by removing material using a rotating cutting tool.
- **Drilling machines**: Drilling machines are used to make holes in materials using a rotating cutting tool.
