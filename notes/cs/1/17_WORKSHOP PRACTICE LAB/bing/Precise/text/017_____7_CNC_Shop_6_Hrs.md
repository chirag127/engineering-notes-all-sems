### 7 CNC Shop 6 Hrs

1. CNC stands for Computer Numerical Control.
2. A CNC shop is a workshop where CNC machines are used to manufacture parts and products.
3. CNC machines are controlled by computer programs, which allow for precise and accurate cutting, drilling, and shaping of materials.
4. A 6-hour shift in a CNC shop would involve operating and monitoring the CNC machines, loading and unloading materials, and performing quality checks on finished products.
5. CNC shops can produce a wide variety of products, from small, intricate parts to large, complex structures.
6. CNC technology has revolutionized the manufacturing industry, allowing for faster production times and increased efficiency.
7. Working in a CNC shop requires technical knowledge and skills, as well as attention to detail and the ability to follow instructions and procedures.
