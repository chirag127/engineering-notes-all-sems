#### Demonstration of working, construction and accessories for Lathe machine Perform operations on Lathe - Facing, Plane Turning , step turning, taper turning, threading, knurling and parting.

A lathe is a machine tool that rotates a workpiece about an axis of rotation to perform various operations such as cutting, sanding, knurling, drilling, deformation, facing, and turning. The lathe is one of the oldest and most important machine tools.

The main parts of a lathe machine are:
- Headstock: It is the main part of the lathe machine that holds and rotates the workpiece.
- Tailstock: It is used to support the other end of the workpiece.
- Bed: It is the base of the lathe machine that supports all the other parts.
- Carriage: It is used to move the cutting tool along the workpiece.
- Cross slide: It is used to move the cutting tool perpendicular to the workpiece.
- Compound rest: It is used to set the cutting tool at an angle to the workpiece.
- Tool post: It is used to hold the cutting tool.

The main operations that can be performed on a lathe machine are:
- Facing: It is the operation of machining the ends of a workpiece to produce a flat surface.
- Plane Turning: It is the operation of machining a cylindrical surface on a workpiece.
- Step Turning: It is the operation of machining different diameters on a workpiece.
- Taper Turning: It is the operation of machining a conical surface on a workpiece.
- Threading: It is the operation of cutting threads on a workpiece.
- Knurling: It is the operation of producing a rough surface on a workpiece.
- Parting: It is the operation of cutting a workpiece into two or more pieces.

These are the basic operations that can be performed on a lathe machine. There are many other operations and accessories that can be used to perform more complex tasks. It is important to understand the construction and working of a lathe machine to be able to use it effectively.
