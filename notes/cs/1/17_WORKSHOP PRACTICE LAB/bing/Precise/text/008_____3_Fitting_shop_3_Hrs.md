### Fitting Shop (3 hours)

1. A fitting shop is a workshop where the process of fitting takes place.
2. Fitting is the process of assembling, shaping, and finishing parts to make them fit together accurately and precisely.
3. Fitting is an important step in the manufacturing process, as it ensures that the final product is of high quality and functions as intended.
4. In a fitting shop, workers use various tools and machines to shape and finish parts, such as lathes, milling machines, and grinders.
5. Fitting can involve processes such as cutting, drilling, tapping, reaming, and grinding.
6. Fitting is a skilled trade, and workers in a fitting shop must have a good understanding of materials, tools, and processes to produce high-quality work.
7. A fitting shop may be found in various industries, including manufacturing, construction, and engineering.
8. In a 3-hour session in a fitting shop, one could expect to learn about the tools and processes used in fitting, as well as gain hands-on experience in shaping and finishing parts.
