### Course Outcome:

- A course outcome is a statement that describes the knowledge, skills, and abilities that students are expected to have acquired by the end of a course.
- Course outcomes are used to assess the effectiveness of a course in achieving its intended goals.
- Course outcomes are typically written in measurable terms, using action verbs that describe what students should be able to do by the end of the course.
- Course outcomes should be aligned with the overall goals and objectives of the program or curriculum in which the course is offered.
- Course outcomes can be used to guide the development of course content, assessments, and teaching strategies.
- Course outcomes can also be used to communicate expectations to students and to provide a basis for evaluating student learning.
- Course outcomes should be reviewed and revised periodically to ensure that they remain relevant and up-to-date.
