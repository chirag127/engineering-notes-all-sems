#### 2. Mechanical Workshop Practice, K C John, PHI
- Mechanical Workshop Practice is a book written by K.C. John and published by PHI Learning in 2010 .
- The book has an ISBN 10 of 812034166X and an ISBN 13 of 9788120341661 .
- The book describes the general principles of different workshop processes such as primary and secondary shaping processes, metal joining methods, surface finishing, and heat treatment .
- The workshop processes are explained with the help of diagrams and photographs .
- The book is intended for undergraduate students of mechanical engineering and related disciplines .
