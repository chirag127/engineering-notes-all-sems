#### To study and use of different types of tools, equipment, devices & machines used in fitting, sheet metal and welding section.

To study and use different types of tools, equipment, devices, and machines in the fitting, sheet metal, and welding section, one should:

1. Learn about the different types of tools and equipment used in each section, including hand tools, power tools, and specialized equipment.
2. Familiarize oneself with the proper use, maintenance, and storage of each tool and equipment.
3. Study the safety guidelines and procedures for using each tool and equipment, including the proper use of personal protective equipment.
4. Practice using each tool and equipment under the supervision of a skilled instructor or mentor.
5. Continuously improve skills and knowledge through hands-on practice and ongoing training.

Examples of tools and equipment used in the fitting, sheet metal, and welding sections include:
- Hand tools such as pliers, wrenches, and hammers
- Power tools such as drill presses, band saws, and grinders
- Welding equipment such as MIG welders, TIG welders, and plasma cutters
- Sheet metal forming equipment such as brake presses and roll formers
- Measuring and layout tools such as micrometers, calipers, and rulers

By studying and using these tools, equipment, devices, and machines, individuals can improve their skills and efficiency in the fitting, sheet metal, and welding sections, leading to better quality work and increased productivity.
