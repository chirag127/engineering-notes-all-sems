#### Study of main features and working parts of CNC machine and accessories that can be used. Perform different operations on metal components using any CNC machines

"Study of main features and working parts of CNC machine and accessories that can be used" refers to the process of learning about the components and capabilities of a computer numerical control (CNC) machine and the accessories that can be used with it. CNC machines are automated machine tools that use computer control to perform a variety of operations on metal components.

The main features and working parts of a CNC machine typically include:

1. Control Unit: The control unit is the computer component of the CNC machine, which contains the software and hardware used to control the machine.

2. Axis Mechanisms: The axis mechanisms are the mechanical components of the CNC machine that control the movement of the cutting tool along the X, Y, and Z axes.

3. Cutting Tool: The cutting tool is the component of the CNC machine that performs the cutting or shaping operations on the metal components.

4. Workholding Devices: The workholding devices are the components that hold the metal components in place during the cutting or shaping operations.

5. Coolant Systems: The coolant systems are used to cool the cutting tool and metal components during the cutting or shaping operations.

The accessories that can be used with a CNC machine include:

1. Cutting Tools: Different types of cutting tools, such as end mills, drills, and reamers, can be used to perform different operations on metal components.

2. Workholding Devices: Different types of workholding devices, such as vises, clamps, and fixtures, can be used to hold metal components in place during the cutting or shaping operations.

3. Tool Holders: Tool holders are used to hold the cutting tools in place during the cutting or shaping operations.

4. Coolant Systems: Different types of coolant systems, such as mist coolant systems and flood coolant systems, can be used to cool the cutting tool and metal components during the cutting or shaping operations.

The goal of studying the main features and working parts of a CNC machine and its accessories is to develop an understanding of how the machine works and what it is capable of, and to gain the skills and knowledge needed to perform different operations on metal components using a CNC machine.
