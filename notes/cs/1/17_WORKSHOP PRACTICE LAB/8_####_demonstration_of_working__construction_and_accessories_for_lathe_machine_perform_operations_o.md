#### Demonstration of working, construction and accessories for Lathe machine Perform operations on Lathe - Facing, Plane Turning , step turning, taper turning, threading, knurling and parting.

Lathe machine is a versatile machine tool that is used for a wide range of metal-cutting and shaping operations. In the demonstration of working, construction, and accessories of Lathe machine, students will learn about the various parts of a Lathe machine, including the bed, headstock, tailstock, and carriage.

Additionally, students will learn about the various accessories that can be used with a Lathe machine, such as cutting tools, tool holders, and workholding devices. These accessories are essential for performing various operations on the Lathe machine, including facing, plane turning, step turning, taper turning, threading, knurling, and parting.

In the demonstration of working and construction of Lathe machine, students will learn about the various components of the machine and how they work together to perform metal-cutting operations. They will also learn about the different types of Lathe machines, including engine Lathes, turret Lathes, and CNC Lathes.

In the demonstration of Lathe machine operations, students will learn how to perform various metal-cutting operations using the Lathe machine. This may include facing, which involves cutting a flat surface on a workpiece, plane turning, which involves cutting a cylindrical surface, step turning, which involves cutting a stepped surface, taper turning, which involves cutting a tapered surface, threading, which involves cutting a threaded surface, knurling, which involves creating a pattern on the surface of a workpiece, and parting, which involves cutting a workpiece into two separate pieces.

Overall, the demonstration of working, construction, and accessories for Lathe machine and the performance of operations on Lathe machine is an important part of the learning process in the subject of Engineering Graphics & Design Lab KCS. By learning these skills, students will be equipped with the knowledge and skills they need to effectively use Lathe machines in their future careers.
