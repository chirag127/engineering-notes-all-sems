### 4 Carpentry Shop 3 Hrs

Carpentry Shop is a hands-on course that teaches the basic skills and techniques of carpentry. The course typically lasts 3 hours and covers the following topics:

1. Tools and Equipment: Introduction to the various tools used in carpentry, including hand tools, power tools, and measuring tools.
2. Wood Selection: Understanding the different types of wood and how to select the right wood for a particular project.
3. Measuring and Marking: Learning how to measure and mark accurately to ensure proper cuts and fittings.
4. Cutting Techniques: Introduction to different cutting techniques, including sawing, chiseling, and sanding.
5. Joinery: Understanding the different types of joints and how to create them, including butt joints, miter joints, and dovetail joints.
6. Finishing Techniques: Techniques for finishing a project, including sanding, staining, and varnishing.

During the course, students will have the opportunity to practice their skills and complete a small carpentry project under the guidance of an experienced instructor. The course is designed to provide students with the foundational knowledge and skills required to pursue further carpentry training or to tackle small carpentry projects on their own.
