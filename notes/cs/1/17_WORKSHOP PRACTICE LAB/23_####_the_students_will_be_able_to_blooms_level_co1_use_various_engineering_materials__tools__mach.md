The objectives of the engineering course are to equip students with the knowledge and skills to use various engineering materials, tools, machines and measuring equipment, perform machine operations in lathe and CNC machine, perform manufacturing operations on components in fitting and carpentry shop, perform operations in welding, molding, casting and gas cutting, and fabricate a job using 3D printing manufacturing technique.

1. Engineering Materials: Students will learn about different types of engineering materials, including metals, plastics, composites, and ceramics. They will learn how to select the appropriate material for a given application and how to work with each material effectively.

2. Tools and Machines: Students will learn about the various tools and machines used in engineering, including lathes, CNC machines, and measuring equipment. They will learn how to use these tools and machines safely and effectively, and how to perform basic maintenance and troubleshooting.

3. Machine Operations: Students will learn how to perform machine operations, including turning, drilling, and milling, on lathes and CNC machines. They will also learn about the different types of cutting tools and how to select the appropriate tool for a given operation.

4. Manufacturing Operations: Students will learn how to perform manufacturing operations on components in fitting and carpentry shops. This will include operations such as drilling, tapping, and reaming, as well as basic joinery techniques.

5. Welding, Molding, Casting, and Gas Cutting: Students will learn about the different welding processes, including arc welding, gas welding, and TIG welding. They will also learn about molding and casting techniques, and how to perform gas cutting operations.

6. 3D Printing: Students will learn about 3D printing technology and how to fabricate a job using this technique. This will include designing a component using 3D modeling software and operating a 3D printer to produce the component.

By the end of the course, students will have a comprehensive understanding of the various materials, tools, machines, and manufacturing techniques used in engineering, and will be able to apply this knowledge in a practical setting.
