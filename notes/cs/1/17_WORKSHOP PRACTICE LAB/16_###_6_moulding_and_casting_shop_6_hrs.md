### 6 Moulding and Casting Shop 6 Hrs

Moulding and casting is a process used in manufacturing to create a duplicate of an object using a mold and a liquid material that hardens into a solid form. In a moulding and casting shop, individuals will learn how to create molds, prepare casting materials, and cast objects.

During a 6-hour moulding and casting shop, individuals can expect to cover the following topics:

1. Types of molds: Participants will learn about the different types of molds, including silicone, latex, and plaster molds, and their uses.

2. Mold-making techniques: Participants will learn how to create molds using various techniques, including the use of a mold box, mold release agents, and molding materials.

3. Casting materials: Participants will learn about the different types of casting materials, including resins, waxes, and metals, and their properties.

4. Preparing casting materials: Participants will learn how to prepare casting materials, including mixing and heating the materials to the correct temperature.

5. Pouring the mold: Participants will learn how to pour the casting material into the mold and ensure it is properly filled.

6. Demolding: Participants will learn how to remove the cast object from the mold, including the use of tools and techniques to safely demold the object.

By the end of the 6-hour moulding and casting shop, participants will have gained hands-on experience in creating molds and casting objects, and will have a better understanding of the moulding and casting process.
