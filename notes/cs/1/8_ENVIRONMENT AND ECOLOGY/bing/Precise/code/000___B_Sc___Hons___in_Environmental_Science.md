# B.Sc. (Hons.) in Environmental Science

- B.Sc. (Hons.) in Environmental Science is an undergraduate program that focuses on the study of the natural environment and the impact of human activities on it.
- In this course, students learn to investigate the interaction of biological and physical systems in the environment.
- Some of the major topics students may study in this course are animal studies, climate, energy, water, soil, dynamics of the plants, local plants, animal populations and their communities.
- B.Sc. (Hons.) Environmental Science brings together knowledge from a variety of fields to give students a deep understanding of the environmental challenges facing the world today.
- Through a practical, hands-on approach that runs through all the modules, students gain exposure to the skills and technology employed at the forefront of environmental science.
- This degree explores the multiple disciplines needed to understand, manage and protect our planet. Students assess environmental problems, propose solutions, and gain a comprehensive understanding of the natural environment.
- The program covers a wide range of topics, including ecology, geology, hydrology, atmospheric science, conservation biology, and environmental management.
