
# B.Sc. (Hons.) in Environmental Science

* Environmental Science is a multidisciplinary field that combines natural sciences, social sciences, and other disciplines to study the environment and the way humans interact with it.
* It focuses on the understanding of processes and systems that operate in the natural environment, as well as the impact of human activities on the environment and its resources.
* Students learn to evaluate the impacts of human activities and develop solutions to address environmental issues.
* Courses cover topics such as ecology, geology, hydrology, atmospheric science, oceanography, biology, chemistry, and physics.
* Students also learn about environmental law, policy, economics, and management, as well as the principles of sustainability.
* Graduates of this program are prepared to work in a variety of fields, including environmental consulting, natural resource management, and environmental education.
