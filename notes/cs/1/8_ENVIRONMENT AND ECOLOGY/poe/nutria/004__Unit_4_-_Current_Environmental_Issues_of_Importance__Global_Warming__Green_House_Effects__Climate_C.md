
## Unit 4 - Current Environmental Issues of Importance

* Global Warming: Global warming is the long-term rise in the average temperature of the Earth's climate system. It is a major aspect of climate change, and has been demonstrated by direct temperature measurements and by measurements of various effects of the warming.
* Green House Effects: Greenhouse effect is the process by which radiation from a planet's atmosphere warms the planet's surface to a temperature above what it would be without its atmosphere.
* Climate Change: Climate change is a long-term shift in the average weather patterns that characterize a particular region. It is caused by changes in the atmosphere, the land surface, and the ocean, and is linked to both natural and human activities.
* Acid Rain: Acid rain is rain that has been made acidic by certain pollutants in the air. It can have harmful effects on plants, aquatic animals, and infrastructure.
* Ozone Layer Formation and Depletion: The ozone layer is a region of the Earth's atmosphere that absorbs most of the Sun's ultraviolet radiation. It is being depleted by certain pollutants, leading to increased levels of ultraviolet radiation reaching the Earth's surface.
* Population Growth and Automobile Pollution: Population growth and automobile pollution are two of the major causes of air pollution. Automobile pollution is caused by the burning of fossil fuels, and population growth can lead to increased emissions of pollutants.
* Burning of Paddy Straw: Burning of paddy straw is a major source of air pollution in many parts of the world. Paddy straw burning releases carbon dioxide, nitrogen oxides, and other pollutants into the air, which can have a detrimental effect on human health and the environment.
