### ideal and practical transformer for the notes of the Unit 3 - Transformers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Ideal Transformer:
1. Infinite turns ratio
2. Zero resistance in all windings
3. Zero leakage inductance
4. Zero core losses
5. Infinite power handling capacity

Practical Transformer:
1. Finite turns ratio
2. Non-zero resistance in windings
3. Non-zero leakage inductance
4. Non-zero core losses
5. Limited power handling capacity

The practical transformer deviates from the ideal transformer due to the presence of winding resistance, leakage inductance, and core losses. These limitations affect the performance of the transformer, such as efficiency, regulation, and temperature rise. Nevertheless, practical transformers are designed to minimize these limitations and provide efficient and reliable power conversion.
