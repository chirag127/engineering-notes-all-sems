### applications of DC motors (simple numerical problems) for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

DC Motors have numerous applications in different fields such as:
1. Automobiles - power windows, windshield wipers, power steering, etc.
2. Robotics - actuators for movement control.
3. Industrial machinery - conveyor belts, cranes, hoists, etc.
4. Aerospace - control surfaces, actuators for landing gear, etc.
5. Household appliances - vacuum cleaners, washing machines, etc.

Simple Numerical Problems:
1. A DC motor has an armature resistance of 0.2 ohm and an inductance of 0.1 H. Calculate the time constant.
Answer: Time constant = L/R = 0.1/0.2 = 0.5 s

2. A DC motor has a rated armature current of 10 A and a back emf of 8 V. Calculate the armature voltage.
Answer: Armature voltage = Back emf + I * R = 8 + 10 * 0.2 = 12 V

3. A DC motor has a speed of 1000 RPM and a torque of 5 Nm. Calculate the output power.
Answer: Output power = 2 * pi * N * T / 60 = 2 * pi * 1000 * 5 / 60 = 167.07 W
