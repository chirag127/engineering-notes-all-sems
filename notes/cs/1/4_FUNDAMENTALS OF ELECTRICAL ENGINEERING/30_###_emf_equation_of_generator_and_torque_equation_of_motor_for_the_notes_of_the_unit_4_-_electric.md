### EMF equation of generator and torque equation of motor for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

EMF Equation of Generator:
EMF (electromotive force) is the voltage produced by a generator. The equation for EMF is given by:
EMF = dΦ/dt + IaRa
where dΦ/dt is the rate of change of magnetic flux, Ia is the armature current, and Ra is the armature resistance.

Torque Equation of Motor:
Torque is the twisting force produced by the motor. The equation for torque is given by:
T = Kt * Ia
where T is the torque, Kt is the torque constant, and Ia is the armature current.
