### DC machines for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

DC Machines:
1. DC machines are electrical machines that convert DC electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa.
2. Types: DC Generators (convert mechanical energy to DC electrical energy) and DC Motors (convert DC electrical energy to mechanical energy).
3. Working principle: DC generators use Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction, where a conductor moving in a magnetic field induces a voltage in the conductor. DC motors use the interaction between the magnetic field and the current in the armature to generate torque.
4. Components: Armature, field winding, commutator, brushes.
5. Applications: DC machines are widely used in industrial drives, electric vehicles, and portable power tools.
6. Advantages: Simple construction, robust, reliable, and efficient.
7. Limitations: Limited speed range, high maintenance, and sensitivity to load changes.
