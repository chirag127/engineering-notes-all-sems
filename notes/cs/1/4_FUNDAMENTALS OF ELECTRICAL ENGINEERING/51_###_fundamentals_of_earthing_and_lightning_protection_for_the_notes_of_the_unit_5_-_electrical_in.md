### Fundamentals of earthing and lightning protection for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering deals with the study of the design and installation of electrical systems. One important aspect of electrical installations is earthing and lightning protection.

Earthing refers to the process of connecting electrical equipment and systems to the ground to ensure safety and to provide a reference point for electrical measurements. The earthing system provides a low-resistance path for fault currents, which helps to minimize the risk of electrical shock and fire.

Lightning protection is the process of protecting electrical equipment and systems from the damaging effects of lightning strikes. This is achieved by installing lightning rods and other protective devices that divert the lightning energy away from the electrical equipment and into the ground.

In this unit, students learn about the basic principles of earthing and lightning protection, including the design and installation of earthing systems, lightning protection systems, and lightning arresters. The study of earthing and lightning protection is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in electrical engineering, as it provides a foundation for understanding the safety and protection requirements of electrical systems.
