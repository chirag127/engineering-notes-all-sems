### Principle of operation and introduction to methods of starting for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

The principle of operation of electrical machines refers to the way in which they convert electrical energy into mechanical energy and vice versa. This is typically achieved through the interaction of magnetic fields with conductors to generate a force that drives the machine.

Introduction to methods of starting:
1. Direct On Line (DOL)
2. Star-Delta
3. Auto Transformer
4. Soft Starter
5. Variable Frequency Drive (VFD)
