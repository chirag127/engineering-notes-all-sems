### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

In the study of steady state analysis of single phase AC circuits, it is important to understand the relationship between the voltage and current in both star and delta connections. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

#### Star Connection
- In a star connection, the three phase windings are connected to a common point, or neutral point.
- The voltage across each phase winding is equal to the phase voltage, which is also known as the line-to-neutral voltage.
- The line voltage, which is the voltage between any two phase windings, is equal to the phase voltage multiplied by the square root of three.
- The current in each phase winding is equal to the line current divided by the square root of three.
- The total power in a star connection is equal to the sum of the power in each phase winding.

#### Delta Connection
- In a delta connection, the three phase windings are connected in a closed loop.
- The voltage across each phase winding is equal to the line voltage, which is also known as the phase-to-phase voltage.
- The line current, which is the current in each phase winding, is equal to the phase current multiplied by the square root of three.
- The total power in a delta connection is equal to three times the power in each phase winding.

It is important to note that the choice between star and delta connections depends on the specific application and the desired voltage and current levels. By understanding the voltage and current relations in both types of connections, one can make an informed decision when designing or analyzing a single phase AC circuit.
