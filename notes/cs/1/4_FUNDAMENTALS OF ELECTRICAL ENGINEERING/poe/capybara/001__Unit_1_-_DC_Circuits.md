## Unit 1 - DC Circuits

DC (Direct Current) circuits are a fundamental part of electrical engineering. This unit covers the following topics:

### 1.1 Introduction to DC circuits
- Definition of DC circuits
- Basic elements of a DC circuit
- Kirchhoff's laws
- DC circuit analysis techniques

### 1.2 Resistors
- Definition of a resistor
- Ohm's law
- Resistor color code
- Series and parallel resistor circuits

### 1.3 Capacitors
- Definition of a capacitor
- Capacitor types
- Capacitance and charge
- Series and parallel capacitor circuits

### 1.4 Inductors
- Definition of an inductor
- Inductance and magnetic flux
- Series and parallel inductor circuits
- Inductive reactance

### 1.5 DC Power
- Power in DC circuits
- Energy and power in resistors
- Energy and power in capacitors
- Energy and power in inductors

### 1.6 Circuit Analysis Techniques
- Nodal analysis
- Mesh analysis
- Superposition
- Thevenin's theorem
- Norton's theorem

By mastering the concepts and techniques covered in this unit, you will be able to analyze and design basic DC circuits.
