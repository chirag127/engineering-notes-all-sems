## Unit 3 - Transformers

Transformers are devices that transfer electrical energy from one circuit to another. They can either increase or decrease the voltage of an alternating current (AC) power supply. Here are some important points to understand about transformers:

- Transformers operate on AC power only. They cannot be used with direct current (DC) power.
- There are two types of transformers: step-up transformers and step-down transformers. Step-up transformers increase the voltage of an AC power supply, while step-down transformers decrease the voltage.
- Transformers are made up of two coils of wire wrapped around a common iron core. The coil that receives the input voltage is called the primary coil, while the coil that delivers the output voltage is called the secondary coil.
- The amount of voltage change that a transformer can provide is determined by the ratio of the number of turns in the primary coil to the number of turns in the secondary coil. This is known as the turns ratio.
- Transformers are highly efficient devices, with efficiencies of up to 99%. However, some energy is lost as heat due to resistance in the wire and the core of the transformer.
- Transformers are used in a variety of applications, such as power distribution, electronic equipment, and lighting systems.
- Transformers must be designed with the correct voltage rating and power rating for the intended application. Using a transformer with the wrong specifications can lead to damage or failure of the device.
- Transformers require proper maintenance, such as regular inspection and cleaning, to ensure they function correctly and safely.
- Transformers can be dangerous if not handled properly. They can produce high voltages and currents that can cause electric shock or fire. Anyone working with transformers should receive proper training and follow safety guidelines.

In conclusion, transformers are important devices for transferring electrical energy between circuits. Understanding their basic principles and applications is essential for anyone working with electrical systems.
