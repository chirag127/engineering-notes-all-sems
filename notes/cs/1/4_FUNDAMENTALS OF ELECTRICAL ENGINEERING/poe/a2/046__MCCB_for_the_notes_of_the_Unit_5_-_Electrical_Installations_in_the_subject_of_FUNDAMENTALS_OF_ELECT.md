### MCCB

- MCCB stands for Molded Case Circuit Breaker.
- It is a type of overload protection device used for low voltage electrical circuits.
- It consists of a thermostat that trips the circuit in case of overcurrent or short circuit.
- The tripping characteristics of MCCB can be adjusted using an adjustable thermal overload release.
- MCCB is cheap, compact and maintenance free but has slow tripping speed.
- It is used for protection of residential and industrial circuits, motors, heaters, etc.
- The current ratings of MCCB range from 15A to 100A.
- The trip unit of MCCB consists of a bimetallic strip and a solenoid coil mechanism. The bimetallic strip deflects and releases the latch mechanism to trip the breaker in case of overload. The solenoid coil creates a magnetic field to release the latch faster in case of short circuit.
- MCCB has good voltage withstanding capability and is not affected by high inrush currents. However, it may fail to provide effective protection against some types of short circuits.
