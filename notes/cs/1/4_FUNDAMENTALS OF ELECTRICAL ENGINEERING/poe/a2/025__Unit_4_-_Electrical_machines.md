## Unit 4 - Electrical machines

1. DC machines
- DC generator: Converts mechanical energy into DC electrical energy. Parts: Stator, Rotor, Commutator, Brushes. Working principle: Rotation of rotor induces current in stator coils, commutator reverses current direction, resulting in DC.
- DC motor: Converts DC electrical energy into mechanical energy. Working principle: Current flows through rotor coils and interacts with magnetic field to produce rotation. Torque is proportional to current.

2. AC machines
- AC generator (alternator): Converts mechanical energy into AC electrical energy. Parts: Stator, Rotor. Working principle: Rotation of rotor induces AC current in stator coils. Frequency of AC depends on rotor speed.
- AC motor: Converts AC electrical energy into mechanical energy. Types: Induction motor, Synchronous motor. Working principle: Interaction of magnetic fields of stator and rotor coils produces rotation.

3. Transformers
- Step-up transformer: Increases voltage, decreases current. Primary coil has fewer turns than secondary coil.
- Step-down transformer: Decreases voltage, increases current. Primary coil has more turns than secondary coil.
- Working principle: Mutual induction between two coils transfers energy via alternating magnetic fields. No direct connection between coils. Ratios of turns determine voltage transformation ratio.
