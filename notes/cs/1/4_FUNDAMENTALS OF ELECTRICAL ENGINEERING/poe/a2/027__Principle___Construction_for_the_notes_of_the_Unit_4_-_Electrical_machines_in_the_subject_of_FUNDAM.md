### Principle & Construction for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. DC Machine
- Components: Stator ( field system), Rotor (armature), Commutator
- Working: Conversion of electrical energy into mechanical energy and vice-versa based on the interaction between magnetic fields of stator and rotor.
- Types: Series, Shunt, Compound

2. Transformer
- Components: Primary winding, Secondary winding, Core
- Working: Transfer of electrical energy between two circuits through electromagnetic induction. Conversion of voltage and current levels as per the turns ratio.
- Types: Step-up, Step-down, Distribution, Power

3. Induction Machine
- Components: Stator, Rotor, Shaft
- Working: Rotor field is produced due to induction from rotating magnetic field of stator. Electrical energy converted into mechanical energy and vice-versa.
- Types: Three phase induction motor, single phase induction motor

4. Synchronous Machine
- Components: Stator, Rotor, Exciter
- Working: Rotor field is produced by DC excitation and rotates at constant speed in synchronization with rotating magnetic field of stator.
- Types: Alternator, Motor
