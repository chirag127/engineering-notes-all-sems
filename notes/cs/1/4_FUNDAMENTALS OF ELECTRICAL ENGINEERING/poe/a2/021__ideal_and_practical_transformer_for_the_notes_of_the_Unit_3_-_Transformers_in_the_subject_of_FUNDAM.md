### Ideal and Practical Transformer

1. Ideal Transformer:
- It has 100% efficiency.
- Windings have zero resistance.
- Core has zero reluctance.
- There are no energy losses.
- Voltage transformation ratio is equal to turn's ratio.

2. Practical Transformer:
- Has some amount of energy losses like copper loss, iron loss, etc. which reduces efficiency.
- Windings have some resistance which causes copper loss.
- Core has some reluctance which causes iron loss.
- Voltage transformation ratio is slightly different from turn's ratio due to losses.
- It requires cooling arrangements as losses are dissipated as heat.
