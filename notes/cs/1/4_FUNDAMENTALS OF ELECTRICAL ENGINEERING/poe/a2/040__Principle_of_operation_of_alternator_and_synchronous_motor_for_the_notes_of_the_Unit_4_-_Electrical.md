### Principle of operation of alternator and synchronous motor

1. Alternator
- Alternator generates alternating current (AC) electricity.
- It consists of a rotating magnetic field called rotor and a stationary coil of wire called stator.
- As the rotor rotates, it induces an alternating voltage in the stator coils.
- The frequency of the induced voltage depends on the speed of rotation of the rotor.
- By varying the speed, the frequency of the output voltage can be controlled.

2. Synchronous motor
- A synchronous motor is an AC motor whose rotation is synchronized with the frequency of the supply current.
- It has a rotating magnetic field produced by the interaction of the stator AC current and rotor direct current.
- The rotor poles lock into synchronization with the rotating stator magnetic field.
- The rotation speed depends on and is constant at the supply frequency. It cannot vary its speed based on load.
- It requires an additional mechanism to start the motor due to the synchronization requirement.
