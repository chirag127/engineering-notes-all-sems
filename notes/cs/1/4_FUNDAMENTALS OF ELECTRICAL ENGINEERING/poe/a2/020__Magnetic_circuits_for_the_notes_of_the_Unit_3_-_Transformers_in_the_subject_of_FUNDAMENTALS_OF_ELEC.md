### Magnetic circuits for the notes of the Unit 3 - Transformers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. Magnetic circuit: The path followed by magnetic flux is known as magnetic circuit. It is analogous to electric circuit.
2. Different elements of magnetic circuit: The magnetic circuit consists of
    - Magnetic flux source (Current carrying conductor)
    - Air gap
    - Reluctance ( opposition offered by the material to the flow of magnetic flux)
    - Permeance (Ease with which the material allows the flow of magnetic flux)
3. Flux flow comparison with electric current:
    - MMF or magnetomotive force (Analogous to voltage)
    - Reluctance (Analogous to resistance)
    - Permeance (Analogous to conductance)
    - Flux or magnetic flux (Analogous to electric current)
4. Flux flow equation: Magnetic flux (φ) flowing through a magnetic circuit is given by,
φ = MMF / Reluctance

5. Important terms and relations:
    - Reluctance (R) = L/Aμ
    - Permeance (P) = Aμ/L
    - Flux density (B) = φ/Area
    - Field intensity (H) = MMF/Length
