### Applications of Electrical Machines (Unit 4)

1. Motors
- AC motors: Used in industrial drives, home appliances, electric vehicles, etc.
- DC motors: Used in robotic actuators, disk drives, toys, etc.

2. Transformers
- Step-up transformers: Used in power transmission to increase voltage
- Step-down transformers: Used in power distribution to decrease voltage to safe levels
- Isolation transformers: Used to isolate circuits and for safety

3. Generators
- AC generators: Used in power plants to generate electricity
- DC generators: Used in applications requiring direct current like batteries

The applications of the various electrical machines covered in Unit 4 allow for the generation, transformation and utilization of electric power in many critical applications.
