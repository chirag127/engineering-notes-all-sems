### Equivalent Circuit for Transformers

- A transformer can be represented by an equivalent circuit consisting of an ideal transformer and certain resistances.
- The ideal transformer has primary turns Np and secondary turns Ns. It shows no power loss and has perfect coupling between windings.
- The magnetizing branch represents the core loss and consists of a resistance Rm and inductance Lm in series. The resistance Rm accounts for the power loss in the core.
- The leakage inductance of the windings is represented by inductances Lp and Ls in the primary and secondary circuits respectively. They are shown in series with the ideal transformer.
- The winding resistances are represented by resistances Rp and Rs in the primary and secondary circuits respectively. They account for the copper loss or I2R loss in the windings.
- The equivalent circuit is used to analyze the performance of transformers under different operating conditions and load variations. It can be used to determine regulation, efficiency, and other parameters.
