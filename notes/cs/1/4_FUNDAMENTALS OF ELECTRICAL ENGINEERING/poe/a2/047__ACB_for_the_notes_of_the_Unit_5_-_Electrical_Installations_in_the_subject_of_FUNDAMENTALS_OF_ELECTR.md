### ACB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. ACB or Air Circuit Breaker is a switch used for protecting electrical circuits from overload, short circuits and overload protection. It is fast-acting switch and is used where small overload or short circuits are likely to occur frequently.

2. The main components of ACB are:
- Fixed and moving contacts
- Operating mechanism
- Trip device

3. Working:
- Under normal conditions, the contacts are closed allowing current to flow.
- In case of overload or short circuit, the trip device senses the fault and sends a signal to the operating mechanism.
- The operating mechanism then rapidly separates the contacts, breaking the circuit and stopping the flow of current.
- Once the fault is cleared, the operating mechanism can be reset manually to close the contacts again.

4. Advantages:
- Fast operation
- No maintenance
- Reliable
- Repeatable operation

5. Applications:
- Low voltage circuits
- Small motors
- Control circuits
- Capacitor banks
