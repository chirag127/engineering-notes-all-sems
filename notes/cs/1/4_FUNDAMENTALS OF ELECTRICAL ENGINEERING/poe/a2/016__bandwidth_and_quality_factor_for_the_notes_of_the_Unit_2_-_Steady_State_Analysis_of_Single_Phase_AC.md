### Bandwidth and Quality Factor

- Bandwidth is defined as the range of frequencies over which the circuit/system produces the output within a specified range of variation (usually 3 dB variation). It is measured in Hz or rad/sec.
- Quality factor (Q-factor) is defined as the ratio of resonant/center frequency to the bandwidth. A higher Q-factor implies a lower bandwidth and vice-versa. It indicates the sharpness of the resonance and is dimensionless.

- Bandwidth decreases and Q-factor increases with increase in L and C. Increase in R increases bandwidth and decreases Q-factor.
- A high Q-factor is desired for selective circuits while a low Q-factor (high bandwidth) is desired for non-selective circuits.
