### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

- Star or Y connection: In star connection, the terminals of three resistors or impedances are connected to a common junction point. The voltage across each resistor is equal to the line voltage V multiplied by square root 3 divided by 2 (V√3/2). The current through each resistor is equal to the line current I.
- Delta or ∆ connection: In delta connection, the terminals of three resistors or impedances are connected in series forming a closed loop. The voltage across each resistor is equal to the line voltage V. The current through each resistor is equal to the line current I divided by square root 3 (I/√3).
- Relations between line quantities: For both star and delta connections, the following relations hold true:
Vline = Vphase√3 (for star)
Vline = Vphase (for delta)
Iline = Iphase (for star)
Iline = Iphase√3 (for delta)

Where Vline and Iline are line quantities and Vphase and Iphase are phase quantities. The above relations can be used to convert between line and phase quantities for star-delta transformations.
