### Types for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. DC Machines
- DC Generator: Converts mechanical energy into DC electrical energy.
- DC Motor: Converts DC electrical energy into mechanical energy.

2. Synchronous Machines
- Alternator: Generates alternating current (AC) electric power from rotational mechanical energy.
- Synchronous Motor: Runs at a synchronous speed proportional to the frequency of the AC power supply.

3. Induction Machines
- Induction Generator: Converts mechanical energy into electric power using electromagnetic induction.
- Induction Motor: Converts electric power into mechanical energy using electromagnetic induction.
