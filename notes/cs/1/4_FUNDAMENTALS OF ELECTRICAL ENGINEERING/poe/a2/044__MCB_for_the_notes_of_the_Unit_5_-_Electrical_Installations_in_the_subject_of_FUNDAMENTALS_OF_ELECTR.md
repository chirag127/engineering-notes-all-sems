### MCB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations

- MCB stands for Miniature Circuit Breaker.
- It is a type of low voltage circuit breaker that is used for protection against overload and short circuit in electrical installations.
- MCBs are faster and more sensitive than fuses.
- They can be manually reset after the fault is cleared unlike fuses which have to be replaced.
- MCBs are available in various rated capacities like 5A, 10A, 15A, 20A, etc. The rating should be selected based on the load connected.
- The operating characteristics of an MCB are specified by the trip current (Im) and the magnetic release (Ir).
- The trip current is the value of current at which the MCB will trip and disconnect the circuit. It is always higher than the operating current but lower than the short circuit current.
- The magnetic release current is the minimum value of current at which the MCB will remain closed without tripping. This prevents nuisance tripping at normal operating conditions.
- MCBs provide both overload protection and short circuit protection. The build-in thermal and magnetic mechanisms operate to trip the MCB in case of a fault.
- MCBs are compact, cheaper and safer than other protection devices which makes them suitable for residential and commercial electrical installations.
