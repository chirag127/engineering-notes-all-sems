### Kirchhoff's laws for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- Kirchhoff's current law: The sum of currents entering a node equals the sum of currents leaving the node.
- Kirchhoff's voltage law: The sum of voltage rises and drops around any closed loop must be zero.
- These laws are useful in analyzing complex circuits by breaking them into simpler loops and nodes.
- They provide a systematic means of performing circuit analysis to determine currents, voltages and power.
- Kirchhoff's laws are applicable to both AC and DC circuits. They are fundamental laws governing the flow of charge and energy in electric circuits.
