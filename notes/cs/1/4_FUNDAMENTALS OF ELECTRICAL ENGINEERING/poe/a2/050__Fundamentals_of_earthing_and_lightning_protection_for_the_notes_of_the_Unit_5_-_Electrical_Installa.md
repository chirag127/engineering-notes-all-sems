### Fundamentals of earthing and lightning protection

1. Earthing
- Earthing is connecting an electric circuit or device to the ground to pass electric current safely to the earth.
- It ensures safety of persons and equipment from electric shocks.
- The earth has vast area to dissipate the electric current and has very low resistance, so it is used as a return path.
- The earthing electrode is buried in the moist earth to provide good contact.
- The earthing resistance should be low and constant for efficient earthing.

2. Lightning protection
- Lightning protection system provides a path to the ground for the lightning strike current to dissipate without damaging the structure and equipment.
- It consists of lightning rods or air terminals to attract the lightning strike, down conductors to provide path to the ground, and earth electrodes buried in the soil.
- The materials used should have high electric conductivity and mechanical strength.
- The earth resistance of lightning protection system should be very low to efficiently conduct the high surge current to the ground.

The above points cover the fundamentals of earthing and lightning protection which are essential concepts in electrical installations to ensure safety.
