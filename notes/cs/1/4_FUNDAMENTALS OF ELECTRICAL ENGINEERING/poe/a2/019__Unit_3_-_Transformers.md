## Unit 3 - Transformers

1. Transformers are a type of neural network architecture for machine learning that is gaining popularity.
2. They were introduced in 2017 and are based on the Transformer model which is capable of processing input data of variable length while retaining contextual information.
3. The key advantage of Transformers is that they do not require fixed-sized input and output, unlike RNNs and LSTMs which make them more suitable for natural language processing tasks.
4. Transformers contain an encoder-decoder structure where the encoder encodes the input sequence into a continuous vector representation and the decoder generates the output sequence from the continuous vector representation.
5. The transformer architecture is made up of stacks of self-attention layers instead of recurrent layers which enable parallel processing of inputs leading to faster training and inference.
6. Transformers have achieved state-of-the-art results in machine translation, text summarization, question answering and other NLP tasks and are being adopted by big tech companies in their AI systems.
