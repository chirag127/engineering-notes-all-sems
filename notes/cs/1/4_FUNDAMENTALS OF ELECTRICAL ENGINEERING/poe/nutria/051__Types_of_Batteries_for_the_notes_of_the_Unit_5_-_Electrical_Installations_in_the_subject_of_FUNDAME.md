
### Types of Batteries 

1. Lead-Acid Batteries: Lead-acid batteries are the most common type of battery used in vehicles. They are composed of lead plates submerged in a sulfuric acid solution. Lead-acid batteries are rechargeable and can provide a large amount of power when needed. 

2. Lithium-Ion Batteries: Lithium-ion batteries are a newer type of battery technology that is quickly becoming popular in consumer electronics. They are composed of a lithium-ion compound and are rechargeable. They are lightweight and can provide a large amount of power in a small package. 

3. Nickel-Cadmium Batteries: Nickel-cadmium batteries are composed of nickel and cadmium and are rechargeable. They are typically used in small applications such as remote controls and toys. 

4. Alkaline Batteries: Alkaline batteries are composed of zinc and manganese and are not rechargeable. They are commonly used in household items such as flashlights and remote controls. 

5. Silver Oxide Batteries: Silver oxide batteries are composed of silver and oxygen and are rechargeable. They are typically used in small applications such as watches and calculators.
