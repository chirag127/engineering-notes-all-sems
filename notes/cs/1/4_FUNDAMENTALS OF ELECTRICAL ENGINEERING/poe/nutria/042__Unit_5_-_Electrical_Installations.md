
## Unit 5 - Electrical Installations

1. Electrical installations are systems of electrical components used to supply, distribute, and use electrical power.
2. Electrical installations must comply with the applicable standards and regulations set by the local authority.
3. Electrical installations can be classified into two main types: fixed installations and portable installations.
4. Fixed installations are those that are permanently fixed in place, such as wiring in a building or an industrial plant.
5. Portable installations are those that are designed to be moved from one place to another, such as portable tools or appliances.
6. Electrical installations must be designed and installed in accordance with the applicable safety standards and regulations.
7. Electrical installations must be tested and inspected to ensure that they are safe to use.
8. Electrical installations must be maintained regularly to ensure that they remain in good condition and in compliance with the applicable safety standards and regulations.
