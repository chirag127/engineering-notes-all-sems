
### Three phase balanced circuits

1. Three-phase circuits are circuits that involve three alternating current sources connected to three loads. 
2. The three sources can be connected in either a star or delta configuration. 
3. In a star configuration, the three sources are connected to a common point, or neutral, and the loads are connected to the three sources. 
4. In a delta configuration, the three sources are connected in a triangular pattern and the loads are connected to the three sources.
5. In a balanced three-phase circuit, the voltage and current at each phase are equal. 
6. This means that the total power delivered to the circuit is equal to the sum of the power delivered to each phase. 
7. The power factor of a balanced three-phase circuit is unity, meaning that all the power is used to do useful work.
8. The advantage of a balanced three-phase circuit is that it is more efficient than an unbalanced circuit, as all the power is used for useful work. 
9. The disadvantage of a balanced three-phase circuit is that it is more complex to analyze than an unbalanced circuit. 
10. In a balanced three-phase circuit, the voltage and current at each phase are equal, and the phase angle between them is zero.
