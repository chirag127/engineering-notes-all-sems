
### MCCB

MCCB stands for Molded Case Circuit Breaker. It is an electrical device used to protect circuits from overcurrent, short circuits and other electrical faults. It is commonly used in residential and commercial buildings to protect the wiring and electrical appliances from damage.

- MCCB is a type of circuit breaker that is used to protect circuits from overcurrent and short circuits.
- It is designed to trip quickly when the current exceeds the rated current, thus preventing damage to wiring and electrical appliances.
- MCCBs are available in various sizes and ratings, depending on the application.
- The MCCB must be selected according to the size and type of the circuit it is protecting.
- The installation of an MCCB must be done in accordance with the local electrical codes and regulations.
- MCCBs must be tested regularly to ensure that they are functioning properly and are able to protect the circuit from overcurrent and short circuits.
