
### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections 

1. In a star connection, the voltage between any two of the three terminals is equal to the phase voltage of the system. 
2. The sum of the phase voltages is equal to the line voltage. 
3. The line current is equal to the phase current. 
4. The phase current is equal to the line current divided by the square root of 3. 
5. The power delivered to the load is equal to the product of the phase voltage and the phase current. 
6. The power delivered to the load is equal to three times the product of the line voltage and the line current. 
7. In a delta connection, the line voltage is equal to the phase voltage. 
8. The line current is equal to the phase current multiplied by the square root of 3. 
9. The power delivered to the load is equal to the product of the line voltage and the line current. 
10. The power delivered to the load is equal to three times the product of the phase voltage and the phase current.
