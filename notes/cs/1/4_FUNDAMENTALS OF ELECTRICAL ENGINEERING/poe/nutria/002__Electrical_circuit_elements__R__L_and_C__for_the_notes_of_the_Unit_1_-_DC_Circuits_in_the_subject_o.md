
### Electrical circuit elements (R, L and C)

1. Resistance (R): Resistance is a measure of the opposition to current flow in an electrical circuit. It is measured in ohms and represented by the symbol Ω.

2. Inductance (L): Inductance is a measure of the opposition of an electrical circuit to changes in current. It is measured in henrys and represented by the symbol H.

3. Capacitance (C): Capacitance is a measure of the ability of an electrical circuit to store energy in the form of an electric field. It is measured in farads and represented by the symbol F.

4. Unit 1 - DC Circuits: DC circuits are electrical circuits that use direct current (DC) instead of alternating current (AC). DC circuits are used in a variety of applications, including battery-powered devices, automotive systems, and many consumer electronics.
