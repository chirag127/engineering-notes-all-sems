
### Unilateral and Bilateral Elements for the Notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

1. Unilateral elements are those which allow current to flow in one direction only, such as a diode.
2. Bilateral elements are those which allow current to flow in both directions, such as a transistor.
3. Unilateral elements are used to control the flow of current in a circuit, while bilateral elements are used to amplify or switch signals.
4. The resistance of a unilateral element is constant and independent of the voltage applied to it, while the resistance of a bilateral element varies with the voltage applied to it.
5. Unilateral elements are used in rectification circuits and to protect circuits from overvoltage or overcurrent conditions, while bilateral elements are used in amplification and switching circuits.
6. Unilateral elements are usually made of semiconductor materials, while bilateral elements are usually made of metal-oxide-semiconductor materials.
7. Unilateral elements can be used to convert AC to DC, while bilateral elements can be used to convert DC to AC.
