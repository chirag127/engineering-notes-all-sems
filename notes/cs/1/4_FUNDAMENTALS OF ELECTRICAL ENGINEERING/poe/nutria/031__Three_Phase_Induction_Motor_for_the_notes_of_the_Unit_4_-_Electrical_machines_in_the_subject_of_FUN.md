
### Three Phase Induction Motor

* A three-phase induction motor is an electrical machine which converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. 
* It consists of a stator and a rotor. The stator consists of three windings, which are connected in a three-phase configuration, and the rotor is an electromagnet. 
* The stator windings are supplied with three-phase AC current which produces a rotating magnetic field. 
* This rotating magnetic field induces a voltage in the rotor, which in turn produces a current in the rotor windings. 
* The interaction between the rotating magnetic field of the stator and the current in the rotor produces a torque which causes the rotor to rotate. 
* The speed of the rotor is always lower than the speed of the rotating magnetic field, and the difference in speed is called the slip. 
* The speed of the rotor can be controlled by varying the frequency of the supply voltage or by using a variable frequency drive. 
* The efficiency of the motor depends on the design of the motor, the load on the motor, and the supply voltage.
