
### Kirchhoff’s Laws for the Notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

1. **Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL):** This law states that the algebraic sum of the currents entering and leaving any node of a circuit is equal to zero.

2. **Kirchhoff’s Voltage Law (KVL):** This law states that the algebraic sum of the voltages around any closed loop in a circuit is equal to zero.

3. **Loop Rule:** This rule states that the algebraic sum of the voltage drops and rises around any closed loop in a circuit is equal to zero.

4. **Node Rule:** This rule states that the algebraic sum of the currents entering and leaving any node in a circuit is equal to zero.
