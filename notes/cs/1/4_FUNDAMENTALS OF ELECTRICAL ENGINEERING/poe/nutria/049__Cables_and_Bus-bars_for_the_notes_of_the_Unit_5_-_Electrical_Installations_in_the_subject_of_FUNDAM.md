
### Cables and Bus-bars for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- Cables are electrical conductors used for the transmission of energy from one point to another. They are usually made of copper or aluminum and are insulated with a variety of materials.

- Bus-bars are electrical conductors used to provide a common connection point between multiple circuits. They are usually made of copper or aluminum and are insulated with a variety of materials.

- The size of the cable and bus-bar used depends on the amount of current that needs to be transmitted. The larger the size, the more current can be transmitted.

- The type of insulation used for cables and bus-bars is important. It needs to be able to withstand the temperature and voltage of the circuit in order to ensure safety.

- Cables and bus-bars need to be connected securely to ensure that the current is transmitted properly and safely. This is done using terminals, connectors, and lugs.

- In order to maintain safety, cables and bus-bars need to be inspected regularly for signs of wear and tear. Any damaged components should be replaced immediately.
