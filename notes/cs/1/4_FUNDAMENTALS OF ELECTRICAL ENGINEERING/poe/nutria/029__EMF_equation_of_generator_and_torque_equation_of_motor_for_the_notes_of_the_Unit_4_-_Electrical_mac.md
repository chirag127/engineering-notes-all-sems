
### EMF Equation of Generator

1. The EMF equation of a generator is derived from Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction.
2. According to Faraday's law, the EMF induced in a conductor is proportional to the rate of change of magnetic flux linked with the conductor.
3. The EMF equation of a generator can be expressed as follows:

\begin{equation}
E = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}
\end{equation}

Where,

\begin{itemize}
\item E = EMF induced in the conductor (in volts)
\item $\Phi_B$ = Magnetic flux linked with the conductor (in Webers)
\item t = Time (in seconds)
\end{itemize}

### Torque Equation of Motor

1. The torque equation of a motor is derived from the principle of conservation of energy.
2. According to the principle of conservation of energy, the power developed by the motor is equal to the power input to the motor.
3. The torque equation of a motor can be expressed as follows:

\begin{equation}
T = \frac{P}{\omega}
\end{equation}

Where,

\begin{itemize}
\item T = Torque developed by the motor (in N-m)
\item P = Power input to the motor (in Watts)
\item $\omega$ = Angular speed of the motor (in radians/second)
\end{itemize}
