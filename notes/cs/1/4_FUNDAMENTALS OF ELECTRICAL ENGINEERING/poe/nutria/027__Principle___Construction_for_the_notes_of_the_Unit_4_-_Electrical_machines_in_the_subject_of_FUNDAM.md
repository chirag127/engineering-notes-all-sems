
### Principle & Construction of Electrical Machines

* Electrical machines are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa. 
* The two main types of electrical machines are motors and generators. 
* Motors convert electrical energy into mechanical energy, while generators convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. 
* Motors and generators are typically constructed from a number of different components, including an armature, stator, rotor, and field windings. 
* The armature is the part of the machine that rotates, and is typically composed of a large number of individual coils of wire. 
* The stator is the stationary part of the machine and is typically composed of a number of individual magnets. 
* The rotor is the rotating part of the machine and is typically composed of a number of individual magnets. 
* The field windings are the windings that are used to create the magnetic field that interacts with the armature and stator to create the desired motion. 
* Motors and generators can be classified based on the type of construction, such as permanent magnet, wound field, or brushless. 
* Motors and generators can also be classified based on the type of power they use, such as AC or DC.
