
## Unit 3 - Transformers

1. A transformer is an electrical device used to transfer energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic induction. 
2. Transformers come in a variety of sizes and shapes, and are used in many different applications. 
3. Transformers are classified based on the type of core used, the number of windings, and the type of winding. 
4. The primary winding is the winding that receives electrical energy from a power source, and the secondary winding is the winding that delivers electrical energy to the load. 
5. Transformers are designed to convert voltage, current and frequency from one level to another. 
6. Transformers can also be used to isolate circuits from one another, to protect against electrical shock, and to provide impedance matching between circuits. 
7. Transformers can be used in a variety of applications, including power transmission, power distribution, and power conversion. 
8. Transformers are used in a variety of industries, including the automotive, aerospace, medical, and telecommunications industries.
