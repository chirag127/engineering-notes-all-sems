
## Unit 4 - Electrical machines

1. Electrical machines are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy, or vice versa. 
2. The most common electrical machines are transformers, motors, and generators. 
3. Transformers are used to convert electrical energy from one voltage level to another. 
4. Motors convert electrical energy into mechanical energy, while generators convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. 
5. Motors and generators are typically classified by their type of construction, such as squirrel-cage, wound-rotor, or synchronous. 
6. Motors and generators can also be classified by their power rating, such as low, medium, or high voltage. 
7. Motors and generators can also be classified by their speed, such as single-speed, two-speed, or variable-speed. 
8. Motors and generators also have different types of control systems, such as open-loop or closed-loop. 
9. Motors and generators can also be classified by their efficiency, such as high-efficiency or low-efficiency. 
10. Motors and generators can also be classified by their power factor, such as high-power factor or low-power factor.
