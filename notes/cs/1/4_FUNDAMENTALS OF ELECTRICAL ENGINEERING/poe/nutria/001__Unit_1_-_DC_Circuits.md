
## Unit 1 - DC Circuits

* DC circuits are electric circuits that use direct current (DC) to power electrical components.
* DC circuits can be divided into two categories: series and parallel. 
* In a series circuit, the current follows one path and all components are connected in a single loop. 
* In a parallel circuit, the current splits into multiple paths and components are connected in multiple loops. 
* Ohm's Law is used to calculate the voltage, current, and resistance in a DC circuit.
* Kirchhoff's Laws are used to analyze the flow of current in a DC circuit.
* The voltage divider and current divider rules can be used to calculate the voltage and current in a DC circuit. 
* Thevenin's and Norton's Theorems can be used to simplify complex DC circuits. 
* Superposition Theorem can be used to analyze DC circuits with multiple sources. 
* Maximum power transfer theorem can be used to determine the optimum load resistance in a DC circuit.
