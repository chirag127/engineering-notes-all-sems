
### Applications for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical Machines in the Subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering

1. Electric motors are widely used in industrial applications such as pumps, fans, and conveyors.
2. Electric generators are used to generate electricity from sources such as wind, water, and nuclear power.
3. Electric transformers are used to change the voltage of electricity from one level to another.
4. Electric relays are used to control the flow of electricity in circuits.
5. Electric switches are used to turn electrical devices on and off.
6. Electric capacitors are used to store electrical energy.
7. Electric resistors are used to control the flow of electricity in circuits.
8. Electric inductors are used to create magnetic fields.
9. Electric transducers are used to convert electrical signals into other forms of energy.
10. Electric actuators are used to move objects in response to electrical signals.
