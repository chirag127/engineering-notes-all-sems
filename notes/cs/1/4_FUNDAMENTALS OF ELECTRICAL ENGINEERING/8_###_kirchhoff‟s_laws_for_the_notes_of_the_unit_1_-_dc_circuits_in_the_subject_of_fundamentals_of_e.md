### Kirchhoff‟s laws for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Kirchhoff's laws are fundamental principles used in electrical engineering to analyze circuits. 

1. Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL): The sum of all currents entering a node (junction) in a circuit must equal the sum of all currents leaving that node. 

2. Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL): The sum of all voltage drops and rises around a closed loop in a circuit must equal zero. 

These laws are used to determine the current and voltage in a circuit and can be applied to both direct current (DC) and alternating current (AC) circuits. They are essential for understanding the behavior of complex electrical circuits and are widely used in circuit analysis and design.
