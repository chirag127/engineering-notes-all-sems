### MCB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

MCB stands for Miniature Circuit Breaker, it is a type of electrical protection device that is used to prevent overloading and short-circuits in electrical systems. It operates by automatically disconnecting the circuit when a fault current is detected. MCBs are typically used in residential and small commercial electrical installations. 

Features: 
1. Detects overloading and short-circuits 
2. Automatically disconnects the circuit 
3. Resettable 
4. Small and compact 
5. Easy to install 

Advantages: 
1. Protects electrical system and equipment 
2. Prevents electrical fires 
3. Prevents damage to electrical devices 
4. Increases safety 
5. Cost-effective 

Disadvantages: 
1. Limited protection 
2. Not suitable for high current applications 
3. Can be tripped by high inrush current 
4. Maintenance required 

Applications: 
1. Residential electrical installations 
2. Small commercial electrical installations 
3. Office buildings 
4. Hospitals 
5. Schools
