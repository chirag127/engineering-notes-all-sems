### MCCB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

MCCB (Molded Case Circuit Breaker) is a type of electrical protection device used in electrical installations to prevent overloading and short-circuits. It consists of a molded case that contains the electrical components and provides insulation and protection. MCCBs are designed to detect overloading and short-circuits and disconnect the electrical circuit when these conditions occur. Unit 5 of Electrical Installations in the subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering covers the use of MCCBs in electrical installations, including the selection and sizing of MCCBs, the installation and testing of MCCBs, and the maintenance and repair of MCCBs. Understanding the use of MCCBs is important for students studying Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the protection of electrical systems and the safe operation of electrical installations.
