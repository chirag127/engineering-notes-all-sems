### Single Phase Induction motor for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Single phase induction motor is a type of AC motor that operates on single phase power supply. It consists of a stator, rotor, and a capacitor. The stator contains a single phase winding that produces a rotating magnetic field, while the rotor contains a short-circuited winding. The capacitor is used to provide the necessary phase shift to start the motor. The motor operates on the principle of induction, where the rotating magnetic field induces a current in the rotor, producing a torque that drives the motor. The speed of the motor is determined by the frequency of the power supply and the number of poles in the stator winding. Single phase induction motors are commonly used in small appliances and tools, such as fans, pumps, and power tools. They are simple, reliable, and inexpensive.
