### Three phase balanced circuits for the notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Three phase balanced circuits are AC electrical systems that consist of three voltage sources that are evenly spaced in time and have the same magnitude. These circuits are used in many industrial and commercial applications, as they provide a more efficient and reliable source of power compared to single-phase systems. Unit 2 of Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits covers the analysis of three phase balanced circuits, including the calculation of voltages, currents, and power in these systems. The unit also covers the analysis of three-phase transformers, which are used to step up or step down the voltage in these circuits. Understanding the analysis of three-phase balanced circuits is important for students studying Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering, as it provides a foundation for more advanced topics in power systems and electrical machines.
