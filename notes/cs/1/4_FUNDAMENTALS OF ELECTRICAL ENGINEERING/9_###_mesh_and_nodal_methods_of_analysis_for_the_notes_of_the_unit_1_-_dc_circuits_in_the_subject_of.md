### Mesh and nodal methods of analysis for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Mesh and nodal methods are two commonly used techniques for analyzing electrical circuits. In Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering, Unit 1 - DC Circuits, these methods are used to find the unknown voltages and currents in a circuit. Mesh analysis is a method that uses Kirchhoff's laws to form a system of equations that can be solved for the unknown voltages in a circuit. The method involves selecting a set of closed loops in the circuit and writing an equation for each loop in terms of the voltages around the loop. Nodal analysis is a method that uses Kirchhoff's current law to form a system of equations that can be solved for the unknown voltages in a circuit. The method involves selecting a set of nodes in the circuit and writing an equation for each node in terms of the voltages at the node and the currents flowing into and out of the node. Both mesh and nodal methods are widely used in electrical engineering and are essential for students pursuing engineering degrees. Understanding these methods is crucial for analyzing and designing electrical circuits.
