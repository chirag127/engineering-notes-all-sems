### ELCB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

ELCB (Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker) is a safety device used in electrical installations to protect against electrical shock. It is a type of circuit breaker that detects and interrupts the flow of current in a circuit if it exceeds a certain value, indicating a potential electrical shock hazard. In the subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering, Unit 5 - Electrical Installations, ELCBs are studied as a critical component of electrical safety systems. They work by measuring the difference between the current flowing in the live conductors and the current flowing in the earth conductor. If the difference exceeds a certain value, the ELCB will trip and interrupt the flow of current, preventing the possibility of electrical shock. ELCBs are commonly used in residential, commercial, and industrial electrical installations, and are required by electrical safety codes and standards. Understanding the operation, selection, and application of ELCBs is essential for students pursuing electrical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in electrical safety and electrical installations.
