### equivalent circuit for the notes of the Unit 3 - Transformers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

The equivalent circuit for transformers in Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering Unit 3 can be represented by an ideal transformer model consisting of:
-Primary winding resistance (R1) and inductance (L1) in series
-Secondary winding resistance (R2) and inductance (L2) in series
-Leakage inductance (M) in series with the above two windings
-Mutual inductance (k) connecting the two windings
This model represents the basic behavior of a transformer under steady-state conditions. The model helps in analyzing the voltage regulation, efficiency, and load characteristics of the transformer.
