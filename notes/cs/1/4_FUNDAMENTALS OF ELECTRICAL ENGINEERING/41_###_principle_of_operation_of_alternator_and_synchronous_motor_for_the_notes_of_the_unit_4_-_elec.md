### Principle of operation of alternator and synchronous motor for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Principle of Operation of Alternator:
- Alternator is an AC generator that converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
- It works on the principle of electromagnetic induction, where a rotating magnetic field induces an AC voltage in the stator winding.
- The magnetic field is produced by the rotor, which is rotated by an external prime mover (e.g. steam turbine, diesel engine).
- The stator winding is connected to an external load, which receives the AC voltage and current generated by the alternator.

Principle of Operation of Synchronous Motor:
- Synchronous motor is an AC motor that runs at a constant speed, synchronized with the frequency of the AC supply.
- It works on the principle of electromagnetic interaction between the stator and rotor magnetic fields.
- The stator winding is excited by an AC voltage, which creates a rotating magnetic field.
- The rotor has a permanent magnet or wound field winding, which aligns itself with the stator magnetic field.
- The interaction between the stator and rotor magnetic fields produces a torque that rotates the rotor at a constant speed, synchronized with the frequency of the AC supply.
