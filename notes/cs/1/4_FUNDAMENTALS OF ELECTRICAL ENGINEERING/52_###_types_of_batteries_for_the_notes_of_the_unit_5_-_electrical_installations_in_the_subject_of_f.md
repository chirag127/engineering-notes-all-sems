### Types of Batteries for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING KCS

Types of Batteries:
1. Lead-Acid Batteries: used in vehicles and backup power systems.
2. Lithium-Ion Batteries: used in portable devices and electric vehicles.
3. Nickel-Cadmium Batteries: used in portable devices and backup power systems.
4. Alkaline Batteries: used in household devices and flashlights.
5. Zinc-Carbon Batteries: used in low-drain devices like remote controls.
6. Lithium Polymer Batteries: used in portable devices and electric vehicles.
7. Nickel-Metal Hydride Batteries: used in portable devices and hybrid vehicles.
