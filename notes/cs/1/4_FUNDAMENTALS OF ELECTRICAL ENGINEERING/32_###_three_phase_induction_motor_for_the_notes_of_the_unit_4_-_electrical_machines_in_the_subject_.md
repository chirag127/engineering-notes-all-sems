### Three Phase Induction Motor for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

A Three Phase Induction Motor is a type of electric motor that operates on the principle of electromagnetic induction. It is widely used in various industrial and commercial applications due to its reliability, efficiency, and low cost. In the subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering, Unit 4 - Electrical Machines, Three Phase Induction Motors are studied in detail. The motor consists of a rotor, which rotates inside a stator that contains three phase windings. The interaction between the magnetic fields produced by the stator and rotor causes the rotor to rotate, providing mechanical power. The speed and torque of the motor can be controlled by varying the frequency and magnitude of the supply voltage. Three Phase Induction Motors are also classified into different types based on their construction, including squirrel cage and wound rotor motors. Understanding the principles and operation of Three Phase Induction Motors is essential for students pursuing electrical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in electrical machines and power electronics.
