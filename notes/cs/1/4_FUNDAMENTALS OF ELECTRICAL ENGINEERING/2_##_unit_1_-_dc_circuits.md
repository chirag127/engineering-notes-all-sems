## Unit 1 - DC Circuits

Unit 1 - DC Circuits is a fundamental course in electrical engineering that covers the principles of direct current (DC) electrical circuits. This unit covers topics such as Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, equivalent circuits, circuit analysis techniques, and network theorems. Students learn how to analyze and solve DC circuits, calculate voltages, currents, and resistances, and understand the behavior of different types of electrical components such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors. The unit also covers the use of circuit analysis tools, such as nodal analysis and mesh analysis, to simplify the analysis of complex circuits. Understanding DC circuits is essential for students pursuing electrical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in electrical engineering, including AC circuits, digital circuits, and power systems.
