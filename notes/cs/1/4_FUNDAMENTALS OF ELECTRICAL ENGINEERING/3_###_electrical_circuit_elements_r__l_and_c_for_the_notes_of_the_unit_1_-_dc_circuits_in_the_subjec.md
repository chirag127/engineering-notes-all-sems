### Electrical circuit elements (R, L and C) for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Electrical Circuit Elements:
- Resistor (R): An electrical component that resists the flow of electric current and converts electrical energy into heat.
- Inductor (L): An electrical component that stores energy in a magnetic field when electric current flows through it.
- Capacitor (C): An electrical component that stores energy in an electric field when a voltage difference exists across its plates.

Applications:
- Resistor: Used to limit current, divide voltage, provide biasing, and generate heat.
- Inductor: Used in filters, transformers, and electromagnetic devices.
- Capacitor: Used in filters, timing circuits, and energy storage.
