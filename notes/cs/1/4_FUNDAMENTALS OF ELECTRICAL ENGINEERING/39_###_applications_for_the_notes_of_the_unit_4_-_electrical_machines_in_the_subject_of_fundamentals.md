### applications for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. Motors: Understanding the principles of operation and control of AC and DC motors, including speed control, torque control, and power factor improvement.

2. Generators: Understanding the principles of operation and control of AC and DC generators, including voltage regulation, excitation control, and parallel operation.

3. Transformers: Understanding the principles of operation and control of transformers, including voltage regulation, efficiency, and load sharing.

4. Power Electronics: Understanding the principles of power electronics, including power conversion, rectifiers, inverters, and power factor correction.

5. Drives: Understanding the principles of drives, including AC drives, DC drives, and servo drives.

6. Power Systems: Understanding the principles of power systems, including power transmission, distribution, and protection.

7. Renewable Energy: Understanding the principles of renewable energy, including wind energy, solar energy, and hydro energy.

8. Energy Efficiency: Understanding the principles of energy efficiency, including energy audits, energy management, and energy conservation.
