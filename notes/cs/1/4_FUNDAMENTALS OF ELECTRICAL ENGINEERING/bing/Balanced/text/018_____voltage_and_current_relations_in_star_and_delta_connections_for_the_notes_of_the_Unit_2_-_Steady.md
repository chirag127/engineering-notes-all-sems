### Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

- A three-phase system can be connected in two different styles: star or delta .
- In a star connection, the three phase windings are connected in such a way that one end of each winding is joined together to form a common neutral point, and the other end is connected to the line terminals .
- In a delta connection, the three phase windings are connected in a closed loop, such that each winding is in series with the other two, and the line terminals are connected at the junctions of the windings .
- The voltage and current relations in star and delta connections are as follows  :

  - In a star connection, the line voltage (the voltage between any two line terminals) is equal to the square root of three times the phase voltage (the voltage across any phase winding). Mathematically, V_L = √3 V_P.
  - In a star connection, the line current (the current in any line) is equal to the phase current (the current in the corresponding phase winding). Mathematically, I_L = I_P.
  - In a delta connection, the line voltage is equal to the phase voltage. Mathematically, V_L = V_P.
  - In a delta connection, the line current is equal to the square root of three times the phase current. Mathematically, I_L = √3 I_P.
- The power transmitted by both the connections is the same, as long as the phase voltage and phase current are the same in both cases. Mathematically, P = 3 V_P I_P cos θ, where θ is the power factor angle.
