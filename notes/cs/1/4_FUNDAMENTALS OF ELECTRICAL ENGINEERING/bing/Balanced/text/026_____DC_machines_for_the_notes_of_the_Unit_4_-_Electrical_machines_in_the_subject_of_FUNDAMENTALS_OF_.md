### DC Machines

- A DC machine is an electromechanical device that is used to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa.
- The working principle of a DC machine is based on an effect when a current carrying conductor is placed in a magnetic field, and then the magnetic force generates a torque that rotates the DC machine.
- The main components of a DC machine are:
  - Yoke: The outer frame of the machine that provides mechanical support and magnetic flux path.
  - Poles and pole shoes: The projections on the inner side of the yoke that carry field windings and create magnetic poles.
  - Armature core: The cylindrical rotor that has slots for armature windings and commutator segments.
  - Armature winding: The winding that carries the armature current and interacts with the magnetic field.
  - Commutator: The device that connects the armature winding to the external circuit and reverses the direction of current periodically.
  - Brushes: The conductors that slide on the commutator and transfer the current between the armature and the external circuit.
- The types of DC machines are classified based on the connection of field winding to the armature winding:
  - Separately excited DC machine: The field winding is supplied by a separate DC source.
  - Shunt-wound DC machine: The field winding is connected in parallel with the armature winding.
  - Series-wound DC machine: The field winding is connected in series with the armature winding.
  - Compound-wound DC machine: The field winding is composed of two parts, one in parallel and one in series with the armature winding.
- The applications of DC machines are:
  - DC generators: They are used to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy. They are mainly used for battery charging, electroplating, arc welding, lighting, etc.
  - DC motors: They are used to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy. They are mainly used for traction, cranes, hoists, elevators, electric vehicles, etc.
