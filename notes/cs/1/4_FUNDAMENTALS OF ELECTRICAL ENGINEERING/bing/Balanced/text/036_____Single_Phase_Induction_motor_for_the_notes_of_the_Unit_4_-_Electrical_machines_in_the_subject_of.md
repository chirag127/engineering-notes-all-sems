### Single Phase Induction Motor

- A single phase induction motor is a type of AC motor that operates on single phase power supply .
- It has a stator with distributed single phase winding and a rotor with a cage winding .
- The stator and the rotor have a uniform air gap, but no electrical connection.
- The stator produces a pulsating magnetic field, rather than a rotating one as in a three phase motor .
- This means that the single phase induction motor has no starting torque and cannot start by itself.
- To make the motor self-starting, various methods are used to create a rotating magnetic field at least at the start, such as:
  - Split-phase method: The stator has two windings, one main and one auxiliary, with different resistances and inductances. The auxiliary winding is connected to a centrifugal switch or a relay that disconnects it when the motor reaches a certain speed. The phase difference between the two windings creates a rotating field.
  - Capacitor-start method: The stator has two windings, one main and one auxiliary, with a capacitor connected in series with the auxiliary winding. The capacitor creates a phase shift between the two windings, resulting in a rotating field. The auxiliary winding is disconnected by a switch or a relay when the motor reaches a certain speed.
  - Permanent-split capacitor method: The stator has two windings, one main and one auxiliary, with a capacitor connected in series with the auxiliary winding. The capacitor remains in the circuit permanently, creating a phase shift between the two windings and a rotating field. This method has a higher efficiency and a lower noise than the capacitor-start method .
  - Shaded-pole method: The stator has a single winding with a part of each pole shaded by a copper ring or a short-circuited coil. The shaded part of the pole creates a phase shift and a rotating field. This method is simple and cheap, but has a low power factor and a low efficiency.
- Once the motor is started, the induced currents in the rotor create a magnetic field that interacts with the stator field and produces a torque, and thus rotation.
- The speed of the single phase induction motor depends on the frequency of the supply voltage and the number of poles in the stator. The slip, which is the difference between the synchronous speed and the actual speed, is usually small.
- The single phase induction motor is widely used in domestic and industrial applications, such as fans, blowers, pumps, compressors, refrigerators, washing machines, etc.
