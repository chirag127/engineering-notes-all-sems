# Electrical circuit elements (R, L and C)

- Electrical circuit elements are the basic components that make up an electric circuit, such as resistors, inductors and capacitors.
- These elements have different properties and behaviors that affect the current, voltage and power in a circuit.
- The following are some definitions and formulas related to these elements.

## Resistors (R)

- A resistor is a device that opposes the flow of electric current in a circuit and converts electrical energy into heat.
- The resistance (R) of a resistor is the ratio of the voltage (V) across it to the current (I) through it, measured in ohms (Ω).
- Ohm's law states that V = IR for a resistor.
- The power (P) dissipated by a resistor is the product of the voltage and the current, measured in watts (W).
- P = VI = I^2 R = V^2 / R for a resistor.

## Inductors (L)

- An inductor is a device that stores energy in a magnetic field when electric current flows through it and opposes any change in the current.
- The inductance (L) of an inductor is the ratio of the magnetic flux (Φ) linked with it to the current (I) through it, measured in henrys (H).
- Faraday's law states that the voltage (V) across an inductor is proportional to the rate of change of the current, measured in volts (V).
- V = L dI/dt for an inductor.
- The energy (E) stored in an inductor is the product of the inductance and the square of the current, measured in joules (J).
- E = 1/2 LI^2 for an inductor.

## Capacitors (C)

- A capacitor is a device that stores energy in an electric field when electric charge accumulates on its plates and opposes any change in the voltage.
- The capacitance (C) of a capacitor is the ratio of the charge (Q) on its plates to the voltage (V) across it, measured in farads (F).
- Q = CV for a capacitor.
- The voltage (V) across a capacitor is proportional to the rate of change of the charge, measured in volts (V).
- V = 1/C ∫ Q dt for a capacitor.
- The energy (E) stored in a capacitor is the product of the capacitance and the square of the voltage, measured in joules (J).
- E = 1/2 CV^2 for a capacitor.
