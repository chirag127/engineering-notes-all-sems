# Applications of DC motors (simple numerical problems)

DC motors are electric motors that are powered by direct current (DC), such as from a battery or DC power supply. They have different types, features and applications depending on their design and operation. Here are some of the main applications of DC motors:

- **Computer equipment**: DC motors are used for CPU cooling fans and the drive motors for HDDs and CD-ROM drives. They provide low noise, high speed and precise control. 
- **Audio and video equipment**: DC motors are also used in audio CD, DVD, and Blu-ray players. They offer smooth rotation, low vibration and accurate positioning. 
- **Home appliances**: DC motors are used in various home appliances, such as vacuum cleaners, sewing machines, electric fans, hair dryers, etc. They provide high torque, variable speed and low power consumption.  
- **Automobiles**: DC motors are used in many parts of automobiles, such as wiper motors, power seat motors, power window motors, etc. They offer high reliability, durability and efficiency. 
- **Industrial machinery and medical equipment**: DC motors are used as servo motors in industrial robots, CNC machines, etc. They provide high precision, fast response and wide range of speed. DC motors are also used in fan motors in respirators and oxygen concentrators. They offer low noise, long life and stable operation.  

There are four main types of DC motors: permanent magnet, series, shunt and compound. Each type has its own advantages and disadvantages, and is suitable for different applications. Here are some examples of simple numerical problems related to DC motors:

- **Problem 1**: A permanent magnet DC motor has a rated voltage of 12 V and a rated current of 2 A. The motor has a resistance of 2 ohms and an armature constant of 0.1 Nm/A. What is the rated torque and speed of the motor?
- **Solution 1**: The rated torque of the motor is given by:

  `T = kI`

  where `k` is the armature constant and `I` is the current. Substituting the values, we get:

  `T = 0.1 x 2`

  `T = 0.2 Nm`

  The rated speed of the motor is given by:

  `N = (V - IR) / k`

  where `V` is the voltage and `R` is the resistance. Substituting the values, we get:

  `N = (12 - 2 x 2) / 0.1`

  `N = 40 rad/s`

- **Problem 2**: A series DC motor has a rated voltage of 120 V and a rated power of 1.5 kW. The motor has a resistance of 0.5 ohms and an armature constant of 0.05 Nm/A. What is the rated current and speed of the motor?
- **Solution 2**: The rated current of the motor is given by:

  `I = P / V`

  where `P` is the power and `V` is the voltage. Substituting the values, we get:

  `I = 1500 / 120`

  `I = 12.5 A`

  The rated speed of the motor is given by:

  `N = V / (kI + R)`

  where `k` is the armature constant and `R` is the resistance. Substituting the values, we get:

  `N = 120 / (0.05 x 12.5 + 0.5)`

  `N = 153.85 rad/s`
