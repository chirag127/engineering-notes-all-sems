# Regulation and Efficiency of Transformers

- Regulation of a transformer is the measure of how well the output voltage of the transformer remains constant under varying load conditions. It is defined as the percentage change in the secondary voltage from no-load to full-load at a given power factor and frequency.
- Regulation of a transformer can be calculated by the formula:

  - % Regulation = [(V<sub>nl</sub> - V<sub>fl</sub>) / V<sub>fl</sub>] x 100

  - where V<sub>nl</sub> is the no-load secondary voltage and V<sub>fl</sub> is the full-load secondary voltage.

- A transformer with low regulation has a more constant output voltage and is more desirable for most applications. The factors that affect the regulation of a transformer are the resistance and reactance of the primary and secondary windings, the leakage flux, and the power factor of the load.
- Efficiency of a transformer is the ratio of the output power to the input power. It is expressed as a percentage and indicates how well the transformer converts the electrical energy from the primary to the secondary. It is given by the formula:

  - % Efficiency = [(P<sub>out</sub>) / (P<sub>in</sub>)] x 100

  - where P<sub>out</sub> is the output power and P<sub>in</sub> is the input power.

- The efficiency of a transformer depends on the losses that occur in the transformer. The main sources of losses are the copper losses (due to the resistance of the windings) and the iron losses (due to the hysteresis and eddy currents in the core). The efficiency of a transformer can be improved by reducing these losses.
- The Department of Energy (DOE) has established minimum efficiency standards for distribution transformers sold in the United States. These standards aim to reduce the energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions of the transformers and to save money for the consumers and utilities  .
- The DOE 2016 efficiency standards are the most recent and stringent ones, and they apply to liquid-immersed, medium-voltage dry-type, and low-voltage dry-type distribution transformers. The standards specify the minimum efficiency levels for different types and sizes of transformers, based on their kVA ratings and voltage classes.
