### Principle of operation and introduction to methods of starting for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. Electrical machines are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa.
2. The principle of operation of electrical machines is based on the interaction between magnetic fields and electric currents.
3. The two main types of electrical machines are motors and generators.
4. Motors convert electrical energy into mechanical energy, while generators convert mechanical energy into electrical energy.
5. There are several methods of starting electrical machines, including direct-on-line starting, star-delta starting, and soft starting.
6. Direct-on-line starting involves applying the full voltage to the motor windings, which can result in high starting currents and mechanical stress on the machine.
7. Star-delta starting reduces the starting current by connecting the motor windings in a star configuration during starting, and then switching to a delta configuration for normal operation.
8. Soft starting gradually increases the voltage applied to the motor windings, reducing the starting current and mechanical stress on the machine.
9. The choice of starting method depends on factors such as the size and type of the machine, the load characteristics, and the power supply conditions.
