### Types of Electrical Machines

Electrical machines can be broadly classified into two types:

1. **Static Machines**: These refer to stationary machines such as transformers, which may be single or three-phase transformers .

2. **Dynamic Machines**: These refer to both DC and AC machines. DC machines include both DC motors and generators, while AC machines include synchronous and asynchronous machines .

Some of the main types of electrical machines are:

- **Transformer**: In a transformer, both the input and output are electrical power .

- **Generator**: An electric generator is a device that converts mechanical energy to electrical energy .

- **Motor**: An electric motor converts electricity to mechanical power .
