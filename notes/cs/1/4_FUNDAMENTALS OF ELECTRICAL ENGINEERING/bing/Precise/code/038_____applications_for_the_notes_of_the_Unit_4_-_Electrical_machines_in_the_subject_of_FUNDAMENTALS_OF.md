### Applications for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. Electrical machines are used in a wide range of applications, including power generation, transmission, and distribution.
2. They are used in various industries, such as manufacturing, mining, and transportation, to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa.
3. Electrical machines are also used in household appliances, such as washing machines, refrigerators, and air conditioners, to perform various tasks.
4. In the field of transportation, electrical machines are used in electric vehicles, trains, and aircraft to provide propulsion.
5. Electrical machines are also used in renewable energy systems, such as wind turbines and solar panels, to generate electricity.
6. In the field of robotics and automation, electrical machines are used to provide motion and control to various systems.
7. Electrical machines are also used in medical equipment, such as MRI machines and ventilators, to provide essential functions.
