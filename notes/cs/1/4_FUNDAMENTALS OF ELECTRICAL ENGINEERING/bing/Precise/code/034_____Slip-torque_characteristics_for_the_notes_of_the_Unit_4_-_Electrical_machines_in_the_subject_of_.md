### Slip-torque characteristics for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- The slip-torque characteristic of an induction motor describes the relationship between the slip of the motor and the torque produced by the motor.
- Slip is defined as the difference between the synchronous speed of the rotor and the actual speed of the rotor, expressed as a percentage of the synchronous speed.
- The torque produced by an induction motor is directly proportional to the slip of the motor, up to a certain point.
- As the slip increases, the torque produced by the motor also increases, until it reaches a maximum value known as the breakdown torque.
- Beyond the breakdown torque, the torque produced by the motor decreases as the slip increases.
- The slip-torque characteristic of an induction motor is an important factor in determining the performance of the motor in various applications.
- By understanding the slip-torque characteristic of an induction motor, it is possible to select the appropriate motor for a given application and to optimize its performance.
