### Analysis of single phase AC Circuits consisting R-L-C combination (Series and Parallel)

#### Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits

#### FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

1. **Introduction:** In single phase AC circuits, the analysis of circuits consisting of resistors (R), inductors (L), and capacitors (C) in series or parallel combinations is important to understand the behavior of the circuit.

2. **Series R-L-C Circuit:** In a series R-L-C circuit, the current is the same through all the components. The voltage across each component is different and is determined by the impedance of the component. The total impedance of the circuit is the sum of the individual impedances.

3. **Parallel R-L-C Circuit:** In a parallel R-L-C circuit, the voltage is the same across all the components. The current through each component is different and is determined by the admittance of the component. The total admittance of the circuit is the sum of the individual admittances.

4. **Impedance and Admittance:** Impedance is the opposition to the flow of current in an AC circuit. It is a complex quantity and is represented as Z = R + jX, where R is the resistance and X is the reactance. Admittance is the reciprocal of impedance and is represented as Y = 1/Z.

5. **Phasor Diagrams:** Phasor diagrams are used to represent the magnitude and phase of the voltage and current in an AC circuit. They are useful in visualizing the relationships between the different components in the circuit.

6. **Power Factor:** Power factor is the ratio of the real power to the apparent power in an AC circuit. It is an important parameter in the analysis of AC circuits as it affects the efficiency of the circuit.

7. **Resonance:** Resonance occurs in an R-L-C circuit when the inductive reactance and the capacitive reactance are equal in magnitude. At resonance, the circuit behaves as a purely resistive circuit and the power factor is unity.

8. **Conclusion:** The analysis of single phase AC circuits consisting of R-L-C combinations in series or parallel is important in understanding the behavior of the circuit. The use of phasor diagrams, impedance and admittance calculations, and power factor analysis can provide valuable insights into the operation of the circuit. Resonance is a special condition that can occur in R-L-C circuits and has important implications for the circuit's behavior.
