### Concept of active and passive elements for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- **Active elements** are components that are capable of generating or amplifying electrical energy. They are capable of controlling the flow of electrons and can provide gain or amplification to a circuit. Examples of active elements include transistors, operational amplifiers, and voltage-controlled oscillators.

- **Passive elements**, on the other hand, are components that do not generate or amplify electrical energy. They are incapable of controlling the flow of electrons and do not provide gain or amplification to a circuit. Examples of passive elements include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

- In a DC circuit, active elements are typically used to provide power to the circuit, while passive elements are used to control the flow of current and voltage within the circuit.

- Active and passive elements play important roles in the design and operation of DC circuits. Understanding the differences between these two types of elements is essential for anyone studying or working with electrical engineering.
