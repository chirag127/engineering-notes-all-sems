### Unit 4 - Electrical Machines: Applications of DC Motors (Simple Numerical Problems)

DC motors are widely used in various applications due to their versatility and efficiency. Some of the common applications of DC motors include:

1. **Electric vehicles:** DC motors are used in electric vehicles such as electric cars, bikes, and scooters. They provide high starting torque and smooth acceleration, making them ideal for these applications.

2. **Industrial machinery:** DC motors are used in various industrial machinery such as lathes, drills, and milling machines. They provide precise speed control and high torque, making them suitable for these applications.

3. **Home appliances:** DC motors are used in home appliances such as vacuum cleaners, washing machines, and fans. They provide high efficiency and low noise operation, making them ideal for these applications.

4. **Robotics:** DC motors are used in robotics for precise motion control. They provide high torque and precise speed control, making them suitable for these applications.

Simple numerical problems involving DC motors can be solved using the basic principles of DC motor operation. These problems may involve calculating the torque, speed, or power of a DC motor given certain parameters.

For example, consider a DC motor with an armature resistance of 0.5 ohms and a field resistance of 100 ohms. The motor is connected to a 240V DC supply. The armature current is 10A. What is the back EMF of the motor?

Solution:
The back EMF of the motor can be calculated using the formula: Eb = V - IaRa
Where,
Eb = back EMF
V = supply voltage
Ia = armature current
Ra = armature resistance

Substituting the given values, we get:
Eb = 240 - 10 * 0.5
Eb = 235V

Hence, the back EMF of the motor is 235V.

This is just one example of a simple numerical problem involving DC motors. There are many other problems that can be solved using the basic principles of DC motor operation. It is important to have a good understanding of these principles in order to solve such problems effectively.
