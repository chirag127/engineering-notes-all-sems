### Three Phase Synchronous Machines

Three Phase Synchronous Machines are a type of electrical machine that can operate as either a motor or a generator. These machines are commonly used in power generation, industrial drives, and other applications where precise speed control is required.

1. **Three-Phase Synchronous Generator or Alternator**: A synchronous machine that converts mechanical energy into 3-phase electrical energy through the process of electromagnetic induction is known as a 3-phase synchronous generator or alternator.

2. **Three-Phase Synchronous Motor**: A synchronous machine that converts three-phase electricity into mechanical energy is known as a three-phase synchronous motor. Like any other electric motor, a synchronous motor also consists of two major parts namely stator and rotor.

3. **Advantages of Three-Phase Machines**: Large AC machines are three-phase type synchronous machines because of the following reasons. For the same size of the frame, three-phase machines have nearly 1.5 times the output than that of the single-phase machine. Three-phase power is transmitted and distributed more economical than single-phase power.
