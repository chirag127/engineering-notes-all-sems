### ACB for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

- ACB stands for Air Circuit Breaker.
- ACB is a type of circuit breaker that uses air as the medium of arc extinction.
- ACB is used to protect electrical circuits from damage caused by overcurrent, short circuit, or ground fault.
- ACB is commonly used in low voltage applications, typically below 1000V.
- ACB can be classified into two types: plain air circuit breaker and air blast circuit breaker.
- Plain air circuit breaker uses the arc chute principle to extinguish the arc, while air blast circuit breaker uses a blast of compressed air to extinguish the arc.
- ACB is typically used in industrial and commercial applications, such as power distribution systems, motor control centers, and switchgear.
- ACB is preferred over other types of circuit breakers due to its high breaking capacity, fast operation, and low maintenance requirements.
- ACB can be manually or automatically operated, and can be equipped with various accessories, such as shunt trip, under-voltage release, and auxiliary contacts.
- ACB should be regularly maintained to ensure proper operation and to extend its service life.
