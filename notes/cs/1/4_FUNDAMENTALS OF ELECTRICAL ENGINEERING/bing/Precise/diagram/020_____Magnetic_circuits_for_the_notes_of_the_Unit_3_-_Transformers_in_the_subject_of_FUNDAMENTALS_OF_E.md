### Magnetic Circuits

Magnetic circuits are an essential component of transformers, which are electrical devices consisting of two or more coils of wire used to transfer electrical energy by means of a changing magnetic field . Transformers operate on the principles of electromagnetic induction, specifically mutual induction .

A transformer is an electromagnetic device that transfers electric energy from one circuit to another magnetically . It is usually employed to transfer energy between circuits of different voltages . A transformer's magnetic core contains two or more windings .

Magnetic circuits have several basic quantities, including magnetic flux, flux density, and magnetizing force . A B-H curve can be used to analyze basic magnetic circuits, and it includes aspects such as hysteresis, coercivity, and retentivity .

Magnetic circuits have various applications, including transformers and relays . A simple magnetic circuit consists of a magnetic core, which may be comprised of a single material such as sheet steel, or multiple sections and air gaps .

This chapter contains basic concepts that can be used for all rotating machines and for power transformers. It presents a more complete analysis and more detailed circuit models of real-life transformers .
