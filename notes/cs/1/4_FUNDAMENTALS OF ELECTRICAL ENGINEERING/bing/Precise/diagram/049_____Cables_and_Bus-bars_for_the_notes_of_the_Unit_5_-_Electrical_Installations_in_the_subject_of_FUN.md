### Cables and Bus-bars

Cables and bus-bars are used in electrical installations to distribute electrical power. They are essential components of any electrical system and are used to connect various electrical devices and equipment.

1. **Cables**: Cables are insulated conductors used to transmit electrical power. They are available in various sizes and types, depending on the voltage level and current carrying capacity required. Cables can be classified into different types based on their insulation, such as PVC, XLPE, and EPR.

2. **Bus-bars**: Bus-bars are thick strips of copper or aluminum used to distribute electrical power. They are typically used in switchgear and distribution boards to connect various electrical devices. Bus-bars are available in various sizes and shapes, depending on the current carrying capacity required.

In summary, cables and bus-bars are essential components of electrical installations, used to distribute electrical power to various devices and equipment. They are available in various sizes and types, depending on the requirements of the electrical system.
