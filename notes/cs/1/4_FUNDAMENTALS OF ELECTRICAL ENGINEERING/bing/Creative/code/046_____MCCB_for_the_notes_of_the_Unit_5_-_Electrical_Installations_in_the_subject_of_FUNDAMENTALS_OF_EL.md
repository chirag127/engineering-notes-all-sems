### MCCB

MCCB stands for Moulded Case Circuit Breaker. It is a type of circuit breaker that protects the electrical circuit from overloading, short circuit, instantaneous over current and earth fault. It is an advanced version of MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker)  .

Some of the features of MCCB are:

- It has a current rating of up to 2500 A  .
- It can be used for a wide range of voltages and frequencies with adjustable trip settings  .
- It has thermal or thermal-magnetic operation, which means it can sense both the temperature and the magnetic field of the current  .
- It can be manually or electrically operated, which means it can be switched on or off by hand or by a remote control  .
- It can be integrated with other accessories, such as shunt trip, under voltage release, auxiliary contacts, etc.  .

Some of the advantages of MCCB are:

- It provides reliable and accurate protection for the electrical circuit  .
- It can be easily installed and maintained, as it does not require any oil or gas  .
- It can be customized according to the specific needs of the application  .
- It has a long service life and high breaking capacity  .

Some of the disadvantages of MCCB are:

- It is more expensive than MCB  .
- It is larger and heavier than MCB  .
- It may not be suitable for very low current applications  .

Some of the applications of MCCB are:

- It can be used for industrial, commercial and residential purposes  .
- It can be used for power distribution, motor control, lighting, heating, air conditioning, etc.  .
- It can be used for backup protection, selective protection, coordination protection, etc.  .
