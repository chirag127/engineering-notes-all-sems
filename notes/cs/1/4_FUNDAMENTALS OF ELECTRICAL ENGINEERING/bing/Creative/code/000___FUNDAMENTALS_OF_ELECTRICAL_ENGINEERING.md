# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Electrical engineering is the branch of engineering that deals with the study and application of electricity, electronics, and electromagnetism. Electrical engineers design, develop, test, and supervise the operation of electrical and electronic devices, systems, and networks.

Some of the topics that are covered in the fundamentals of electrical engineering are:

- **Basic concepts and quantities**: such as voltage, current, power, energy, resistance, capacitance, inductance, Kirchhoff's laws, Ohm's law, and Thevenin's and Norton's theorems.
- **Circuit analysis**: such as mesh and nodal analysis, superposition, source transformation, and maximum power transfer.
- **AC circuits**: such as sinusoidal sources, phasors, impedance, admittance, complex power, power factor, resonance, and frequency response.
- **Three-phase circuits**: such as balanced and unbalanced systems, star and delta connections, power measurement, and power factor correction.
- **Transformers**: such as construction, operation, equivalent circuit, efficiency, regulation, and auto-transformers.
- **Electromagnetic induction**: such as Faraday's law, Lenz's law, self and mutual inductance, and energy stored in magnetic fields.
- **Motors and generators**: such as DC machines, induction motors, synchronous machines, and power electronic converters.
- **Diodes and transistors**: such as PN junction, forward and reverse bias, rectifiers, filters, amplifiers, and switches.
- **Digital logic**: such as binary numbers, logic gates, combinational and sequential circuits, and microprocessors.
- **Signals and systems**: such as analog and digital signals, Fourier series and transform, Laplace transform, and Z-transform.

These topics provide the foundation for further studies in electrical engineering and related fields, such as electronics, communication, control, power, and computer engineering.
