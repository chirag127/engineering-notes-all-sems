### Concept of active and passive elements

- Active and passive elements are the two main types of electronic circuit elements.
- An active element is a circuit component that produces electrical energy or provides amplification . It can control the direction and magnitude of current or voltage in a circuit. It has the function of gain for electronic circuits.
- A passive element is a circuit component that consumes or stores electrical energy . It cannot supply energy to another circuit or control the current or voltage on its own. It does not have any amplification function.
- Examples of active elements are batteries, generators, transistors, operational amplifiers, etc  .
- Examples of passive elements are resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, etc  .
- Active elements are used for current control and voltage control in electronic circuits. They can also perform logic operations, signal processing, and communication functions.
- Passive elements are used for energy storage, discharge, oscillation, filtering, and signal coupling in electronic circuits. They can also provide impedance matching, damping, and protection functions.
