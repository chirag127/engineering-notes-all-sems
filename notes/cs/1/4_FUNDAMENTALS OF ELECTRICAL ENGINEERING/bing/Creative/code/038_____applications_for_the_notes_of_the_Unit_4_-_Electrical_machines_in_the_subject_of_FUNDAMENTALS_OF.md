# Applications of Electrical Machines

Electrical machines are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy or vice versa. They are classified into two main categories: rotating and linear. Rotating electrical machines are further divided into DC machines, AC machines, and universal machines. Linear electrical machines are less common and are used for linear motion applications.

Some of the applications of electrical machines are:

- **DC machines**: DC machines are used for supplying excitation systems of alternators, in electrolytic processes, welding processes, and variable speed motor drives. DC machines can also be used as generators in renewable energy sources such as wind turbines and solar panels .
- **AC machines**: AC machines are used for generating and transmitting electrical power, as well as for various industrial, domestic, and commercial applications. AC machines include synchronous machines and induction machines. Synchronous machines are used as alternators in power plants, as synchronous motors in constant speed drives, and as synchronous condensers for power factor correction. Induction machines are used as induction motors in variable speed drives, fans, pumps, compressors, conveyors, and other loads that require torque and speed control .
- **Universal machines**: Universal machines are motors that can operate on both AC and DC supply. They are used in applications that require high starting torque and speed regulation, such as power tools, household appliances, and electric vehicles.
