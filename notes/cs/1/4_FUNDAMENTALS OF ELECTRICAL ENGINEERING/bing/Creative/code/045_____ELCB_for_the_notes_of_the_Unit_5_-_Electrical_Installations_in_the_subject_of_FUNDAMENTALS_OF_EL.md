# ELCB

- ELCB stands for Earth Leakage Circuit Breaker   .
- It is a safety device used in electrical installations with high Earth impedance to prevent shock  .
- It detects small stray voltages on the metal enclosures of electrical equipment, and interrupts the circuit if a dangerous voltage is detected  .
- It is a specialised type of latching relay that has a building's incoming mains power connected through its switching contacts so that the ELCB disconnects the power when earth leakage is detected .
- It detects fault currents from live to the Earth (ground) wire within the installation it protects .

## Types of ELCB

- There are two types of ELCB: voltage-operated and current-operated  .
- Voltage-operated ELCB: It measures the voltage difference between the metal body of the equipment and the Earth wire. It trips when the voltage exceeds a preset value  .
- Current-operated ELCB: It measures the current difference between the live and neutral wires. It trips when the current difference exceeds a preset value  .
- Current-operated ELCB is more sensitive and reliable than voltage-operated ELCB  .

## Advantages of ELCB

- ELCB provides protection against electrical shock and fire hazards caused by earth leakage  .
- ELCB can detect very low levels of leakage current that may not be detected by other devices  .
- ELCB can isolate the faulty circuit quickly and prevent further damage  .
- ELCB can be easily reset after tripping without replacing any components  .

## Disadvantages of ELCB

- ELCB may not work properly if the Earth wire is broken or disconnected  .
- ELCB may cause nuisance tripping due to external factors such as lightning, static electricity, or capacitive coupling  .
- ELCB may not detect leakage currents that flow through other paths such as water pipes, metal structures, or human bodies  .
- ELCB may not be compatible with some types of equipment that generate harmonics or have high inrush currents  .

## Applications of ELCB

- ELCB is widely used in residential, commercial, and industrial electrical installations to provide safety against earth leakage  .
- ELCB is especially suitable for applications where the equipment is exposed to moisture, dust, or corrosion, such as bathrooms, kitchens, garages, workshops, etc  .
- ELCB is also recommended for applications where the equipment has a high risk of insulation failure, such as electric heaters, motors, transformers, etc  .
