### Bandwidth and Quality Factor

- Bandwidth of a resonant circuit is the range of frequencies over which the current or voltage amplitude is equal to or greater than 70.7% of its maximum value at the resonant frequency .
- Quality factor of a resonant circuit is a dimensionless parameter that measures how well the circuit can store and transfer energy at the resonant frequency .
- The quality factor is related to the bandwidth as follows: Q = f_r / BW, where f_r is the resonant frequency and BW is the bandwidth .
- A high quality factor implies a narrow bandwidth, which means the circuit is more selective and has less energy loss. A low quality factor implies a wide bandwidth, which means the circuit is less selective and has more energy loss.
- The quality factor and the bandwidth can be calculated from the circuit parameters, such as resistance, inductance, and capacitance, depending on whether the circuit is series or parallel .
