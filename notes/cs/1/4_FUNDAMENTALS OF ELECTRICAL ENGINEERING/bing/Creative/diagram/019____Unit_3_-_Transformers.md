## Unit 3 - Transformers

Transformers are electrical devices that transfer energy between two or more circuits through electromagnetic induction. Transformers can change the voltage, current, or impedance of an alternating current (AC) circuit.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this unit, you should be able to:

- Explain the principle of electromagnetic induction and how it is used in transformers.
- Identify the main components of a transformer and their functions.
- Distinguish between step-up and step-down transformers and their applications.
- Calculate the voltage, current, and power in the primary and secondary circuits of a transformer using the transformer equations.
- Describe the types of losses and inefficiencies in transformers and how to minimize them.
- Compare the advantages and disadvantages of different types of transformers, such as iron-core, air-core, auto, and isolation transformers.
