### MCCB

- MCCB stands for **Moulded Case Circuit Breaker** .
- It is an **automatic electrical device** that protects the electrical circuit from **overload, short circuit, instantaneous over current and earth fault**   .
- It is an **advanced version** of MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker) since it operates like one but has **higher current ratings** and **adjustable trip settings**  .
- It can be used for a **wide range of voltages and frequencies** with different types of trip units .
- It consists of a **moulded case** that encloses the contacts, arc chutes, trip unit and operating mechanism .
- It can be **manually or electrically operated** by a toggle or a motor .
- It can be **reset** after a fault clearance without replacing any component .

### Types of MCCB

- MCCBs can be classified into three types based on their **interrupting capacity** :

  - **Standard interrupting capacity**: These MCCBs can interrupt currents up to 10 kA and are suitable for residential and commercial applications.
  - **High interrupting capacity**: These MCCBs can interrupt currents up to 65 kA and are suitable for industrial applications.
  - **Extra high interrupting capacity**: These MCCBs can interrupt currents up to 200 kA and are suitable for special applications.

- MCCBs can also be classified into three types based on their **trip units** :

  - **Thermal trip unit**: This trip unit uses a bimetallic strip that bends due to the heat generated by the current and releases a latch that opens the contacts. It provides **overload protection** with a **time delay**.
  - **Magnetic trip unit**: This trip unit uses an electromagnet that attracts a plunger that releases a latch that opens the contacts. It provides **short circuit protection** with **instantaneous tripping**.
  - **Thermal-magnetic trip unit**: This trip unit combines both thermal and magnetic elements and provides both **overload and short circuit protection** with **adjustable time delay and trip current**.
