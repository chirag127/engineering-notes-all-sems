## Unit 5 - Electrical Installations

- Electrical installations are the construction or installation of electrical wiring and the permanent attachment or installation of electrical products in or on any structure that is not itself an electrical product.
- Electrical installations also include the installation, repair, alteration and maintenance of electrical conductors, fittings, devices and fixtures for heating, lighting or power purposes, regardless of the voltage.
- Electrical installations must comply with the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) standards, which are the global standards for electrical engineering.
- Electrical installations require professional and licensed installers who are insured and background checked, to ensure safety and quality of work.
- Electrical installations can be classified into different types, such as domestic, commercial, industrial, or special, depending on the purpose, location, and complexity of the installation.
- Electrical installations involve various components, such as cables, switches, sockets, circuit breakers, fuses, transformers, generators, motors, lighting fixtures, appliances, and more.
- Electrical installations must follow the principles of electrical engineering, such as Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, power factor, voltage drop, earthing, protection, and more.
- Electrical installations must be tested and inspected regularly, to ensure they are functioning properly and safely, and to identify any faults or defects that need to be corrected.
