### Form and peak factors

- Form factor is a parameter used in describing AC waveforms and is given by the ratio between the RMS value and the average value of the alternating quantity.
- Peak factor is also called as crest factor and is defined as the ratio of the peak or maximum value to the RMS value of the alternating quantity.
- The form factor and peak factor are useful in determining the shape and quality of the AC waveform.
- The form factor and peak factor values for different types of AC waveforms are given below :

| Waveform | Form factor | Peak factor |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Sinusoidal | 1.11 | 1.414 |
| Half-wave rectified | 1.57 | 2 |
| Full-wave rectified | 1.11 | 1.414 |
| Triangular | 1.15 | 1.732 |
| Square | 1 | 1 |

- For a square waveform, the form factor and peak factor are the same and equal to one.
- For a sinusoidal waveform, the form factor and peak factor are constant and independent of the amplitude and frequency.
- For other waveforms, the form factor and peak factor may vary depending on the shape and symmetry of the waveform.
