## Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits

- In this unit, we will learn how to analyze single phase AC circuits using phasors, impedance, admittance, and power factor.
- We will also learn how to apply Kirchhoff's laws, mesh analysis, nodal analysis, superposition, Thevenin's theorem, and Norton's theorem to AC circuits.
- We will study the behavior of resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers in AC circuits, and how to calculate their equivalent series and parallel impedances.
- We will also learn how to measure and calculate the active power, reactive power, apparent power, and power factor in AC circuits, and how to improve the power factor using capacitors.
- We will also learn how to analyze balanced and unbalanced three-phase AC circuits using star and delta connections, and how to calculate the line and phase voltages and currents.

### Learning Outcomes

- By the end of this unit, you should be able to:
  - Define and use phasors, impedance, admittance, and power factor to represent AC quantities.
  - Apply Kirchhoff's laws, mesh analysis, nodal analysis, superposition, Thevenin's theorem, and Norton's theorem to AC circuits.
  - Analyze the behavior of resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers in AC circuits, and calculate their equivalent series and parallel impedances.
  - Measure and calculate the active power, reactive power, apparent power, and power factor in AC circuits, and improve the power factor using capacitors.
  - Analyze balanced and unbalanced three-phase AC circuits using star and delta connections, and calculate the line and phase voltages and currents.
