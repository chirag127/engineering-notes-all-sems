# Voltage and Current Relations in Star and Delta Connections

- A three-phase system can be connected in two different configurations: star (or Y) and delta (or Δ).
- In a star connection, one end of each phase winding is connected to a common point called the neutral point, and the other end is connected to the line terminal. The line voltage is the voltage between any two line terminals, and the phase voltage is the voltage between any line terminal and the neutral point.
- In a delta connection, each phase winding is connected between two line terminals, forming a closed loop. The line voltage is the same as the phase voltage, and the line current is the current flowing in any line.
- The voltage and current relations in star and delta connections are given by the following formulas :

  - Star connection:

    - Line voltage = √3 × Phase voltage
    - Line current = Phase current

  - Delta connection:

    - Line voltage = Phase voltage
    - Line current = √3 × Phase current

- The power transmitted by both the connections is the same, given by the formula:

  - Power = √3 × Line voltage × Line current × Power factor

- The advantages and disadvantages of star and delta connections depend on the application and the load characteristics. Some of the factors to consider are:

  - Star connection provides a neutral point, which can be used for grounding or connecting single-phase loads. Delta connection does not have a neutral point, and can only supply three-phase loads.
  - Star connection has lower line currents and higher line voltages than delta connection for the same power and phase voltage. This reduces the conductor size and the voltage drop in the transmission lines. Delta connection has higher line currents and lower line voltages than star connection for the same power and phase voltage. This increases the conductor size and the voltage drop in the transmission lines.
  - Star connection has higher insulation requirements and lower fault currents than delta connection for the same phase voltage. This improves the safety and reliability of the system. Delta connection has lower insulation requirements and higher fault currents than star connection for the same phase voltage. This reduces the safety and reliability of the system.
