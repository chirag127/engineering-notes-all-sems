# MCCB

MCCB stands for Moulded Case Circuit Breaker. It is a type of electrical protection device that is used to protect the electrical circuit from overload, short circuit, instantaneous over current and earth fault . It is an advanced version of MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker), which can handle higher currents and have adjustable trip settings .

Some of the features of MCCB are:

- It has a current rating of up to 2500 A .
- It can be used for a wide range of voltages and frequencies .
- It has thermal or thermal-magnetic operation, which means it can sense both the temperature and the magnetic field of the current .
- It has a moulded case, which is made of plastic or metal, that encloses the contacts, arc extinguishing mechanism and trip unit .
- It can be manually or electrically operated, and can be reset after a fault .
- It can have different types of trip units, such as fixed, interchangeable or electronic .
- It can have different types of accessories, such as shunt trip, under voltage release, auxiliary contacts, alarm contacts, etc .

Some of the advantages of MCCB are:

- It provides reliable and accurate protection for the circuit .
- It can be customized according to the application and load requirements .
- It can be easily installed and maintained .
- It can withstand high short circuit currents and mechanical stresses .
- It can reduce the arc energy and duration by using various arc extinguishing techniques .

Some of the disadvantages of MCCB are:

- It is more expensive than MCB .
- It is larger and heavier than MCB .
- It may require external devices for remote control and monitoring .
- It may have a lower breaking capacity than air circuit breakers.
