# Applications of DC motors (simple numerical problems)

DC motors are electric motors that are powered by direct current (DC), such as from a battery or DC power supply. They have various types and applications, depending on their design and characteristics. Here are some of the common applications of DC motors, along with some simple numerical problems to illustrate their working principles.

## Series DC motors

Series DC motors are motors where the armature and field windings are connected in series. They have high starting torque and can vary their speed with the load. They are suitable for applications where high torque and speed variations are required, such as traction systems, cranes, air compressors, vacuum cleaners, sewing machines, etc.

### Example problem

A series DC motor has an armature resistance of 0.5 ohms and a field resistance of 0.2 ohms. It is connected to a 230 V DC supply and draws a current of 20 A. Calculate the following:

- The back emf of the motor
- The power input and output of the motor
- The efficiency of the motor

### Solution

- The back emf of the motor is given by:

  E = V - I(R_a + R_f)

  where E is the back emf, V is the supply voltage, I is the current, R_a is the armature resistance, and R_f is the field resistance.

  Substituting the given values, we get:

  E = 230 - 20(0.5 + 0.2)

  E = 186 V

- The power input of the motor is given by:

  P_in = VI

  where P_in is the power input, V is the supply voltage, and I is the current.

  Substituting the given values, we get:

  P_in = 230 x 20

  P_in = 4600 W

- The power output of the motor is given by:

  P_out = EI

  where P_out is the power output, E is the back emf, and I is the current.

  Substituting the given values, we get:

  P_out = 186 x 20

  P_out = 3720 W

- The efficiency of the motor is given by:

  η = P_out / P_in

  where η is the efficiency, P_out is the power output, and P_in is the power input.

  Substituting the given values, we get:

  η = 3720 / 4600

  η = 0.8087

  η = 80.87%

## Shunt DC motors

Shunt DC motors are motors where the armature and field windings are connected in parallel. They have low starting torque and can maintain a constant speed with the load. They are suitable for applications where constant speed and low torque are required, such as fans, blowers, pumps, lathes, drills, etc.
