### Concept of active and passive elements for the notes of the Unit 1 - DC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Active and Passive Elements:
- Active Elements: Electrical components that can generate or supply electrical energy to a circuit. Examples: battery, generator, and transistor.
- Passive Elements: Electrical components that do not generate electrical energy but only consume it. Examples: resistor, capacitor, and inductor.

Applications:
- Active Elements: Used in power supplies, amplifiers, and switching circuits.
- Passive Elements: Used in filters, timing circuits, energy storage, and current limiting.
