### Types of Wires for the notes of the Unit 5 - Electrical Installations in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Types of Wires:

1. Copper Wires: Most commonly used, good conductor, durable and flexible.

2. Aluminum Wires: Cheaper than copper, less conductive, prone to corrosion.

3. Steel Wires: Strong and durable, used for overhead transmission.

4. Brass Wires: Good conductor, resistant to corrosion, used in electrical fittings.

5. Nickel Wires: Resistant to corrosion and heat, used in high-temperature applications.

6. Tungsten Wires: High melting point, used in high-temperature applications.

7. Silver Wires: Best conductor, expensive, used in high-end electrical applications.

8. Insulated Wires: Wrapped in insulating material, used in electrical circuits to prevent electrical shock.
