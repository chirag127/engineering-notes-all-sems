### Form and peak factors for the notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Form and peak factors are important concepts in the steady-state analysis of single-phase AC circuits. The form factor is a measure of the average value of a waveform relative to its peak value. The peak factor is the ratio of the peak value of a waveform to its average value. These concepts are used to determine the RMS (root mean square) value of a waveform, which is a measure of its effective value and is used in the calculation of power in AC circuits. Understanding form and peak factors is crucial for students studying Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering, as they provide a basis for understanding the behavior of AC circuits and the calculation of power in these circuits.
