### their applications for the notes of the Unit 4 - Electrical machines in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Applications of Electrical Machines Unit 4:
1. Motors: used in various industries for driving machinery, pumps, fans, etc.
2. Generators: used in power plants to generate electricity.
3. Transformers: used to change AC voltage levels in power transmission and distribution.
4. Electric vehicles: use motors for propulsion.
5. Elevators: use motors for lift operation.
6. Wind turbines: use generators to convert wind energy into electricity.
7. Electric pumps: used in various industrial and household applications.
8. Electric fans: used for ventilation and cooling in various applications.
9. Electric tools: used in various industries for drilling, grinding, cutting, etc.
10. Electric heaters: used for heating in various applications.
