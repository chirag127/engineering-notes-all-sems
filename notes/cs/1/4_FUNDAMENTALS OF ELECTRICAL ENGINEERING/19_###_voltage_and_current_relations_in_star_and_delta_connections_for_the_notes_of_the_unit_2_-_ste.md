### voltage and current relations in star and delta connections for the notes of the Unit 2 - Steady State Analysis of Single Phase AC Circuits in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Voltage and Current Relations in Star (Y) Connection:
- In a star connection, the voltage across each branch is equal to the phase voltage (Vp) and the current flowing through each branch is equal to 1/√3 times the phase current (Ip).
- The phase voltage (Vp) is equal to the line voltage (Vl) divided by √3.
- The phase current (Ip) is equal to the line current (Il).

Voltage and Current Relations in Delta (Δ) Connection:
- In a delta connection, the line voltage (Vl) is equal to the phase voltage (Vp).
- The line current (Il) is equal to √3 times the phase current (Ip).
- The voltage across each branch is equal to √3 times the phase voltage (Vp) and the current flowing through each branch is equal to the phase current (Ip).
