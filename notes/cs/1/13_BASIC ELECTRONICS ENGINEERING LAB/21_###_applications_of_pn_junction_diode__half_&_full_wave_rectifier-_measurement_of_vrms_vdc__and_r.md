PN Junction diodes are widely used in various applications such as rectifiers, voltage regulators, clippers, and clampers. 

Half-wave rectifiers: They are used to convert AC voltage to DC voltage by allowing only half of the input waveform to pass through the diode. 

Full-wave rectifiers: They are used to convert AC voltage to DC voltage by allowing both positive and negative half cycles of the input waveform to pass through the diodes. 

Measurement of Vrms, Vdc, and Ripple Factor:

Vrms: It is the root mean square voltage of the AC waveform. It is used to calculate the average power of an AC waveform.

Vdc: It is the average voltage of the DC waveform obtained after rectification.

Ripple Factor: It is the measure of the amount of AC component present in the rectified DC waveform. It is defined as the ratio of the root mean square value of the AC component to the average value of the DC component.
