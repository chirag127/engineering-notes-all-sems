### 3. PCB Lab: a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB. b. Etching & drilling of PCB

a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB:
1. Create the circuit design using software like Eagle, KiCAD, etc.
2. Export the design as a black and white image.
3. Print the image onto a transparent film using a laser printer.
4. Align the film onto a copper clad board.
5. Expose the board to UV light to transfer the circuit onto the board.
6. Develop the board using a developer solution.
7. Rinse the board with water to remove the developer solution.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:
1. Immerse the board in an etching solution to remove the unneeded copper.
2. Rinse the board with water.
3. Drill holes for components using a CNC machine or hand drill.
