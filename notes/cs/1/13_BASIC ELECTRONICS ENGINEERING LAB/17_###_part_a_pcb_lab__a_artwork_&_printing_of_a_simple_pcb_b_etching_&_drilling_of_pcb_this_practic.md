### Part A PCB Lab: a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB. b. Etching & drilling of PCB This practical is not possible by virtual lab. It will be conducted only in physical mode

Part A of the PCB Lab covers the artwork and printing of a simple PCB, as well as the etching and drilling of the PCB. The artwork involves creating a design for the PCB using specialized software. This design includes the placement of components, trace routing, and other details. The design is then printed onto the PCB material using a specialized printer. 

Etching involves using a chemical solution to remove the unneeded copper from the PCB, leaving only the desired trace patterns. Drilling involves using a specialized drill to make holes in the PCB where components will be inserted. 

This practical cannot be conducted in a virtual lab, as it requires hands-on experience with the equipment and materials used in PCB fabrication.
