4. Characteristics of Zener Diode:

A Zener diode is a type of diode that is designed to operate in the reverse breakdown region and maintain a constant voltage across the diode. The following are the key characteristics of a Zener diode:

1. V-I Characteristics: The V-I (Voltage-Current) characteristics of a Zener diode show how the voltage across the diode changes with the current flowing through it. In the forward-biased region, the Zener diode behaves like a conventional diode, and in the reverse-biased region, the Zener diode operates in the breakdown region and maintains a constant voltage across the diode.

2. Graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance: The forward and reverse resistance of a Zener diode can be measured graphically by plotting the voltage across the diode against the current flowing through it. In the forward-biased region, the Zener diode has a low forward resistance, and in the reverse-biased region, the Zener diode has a high reverse resistance.

3. Reverse breakdown voltage: The reverse breakdown voltage is the voltage at which the Zener diode starts to operate in the breakdown region and maintain a constant voltage across the diode. The reverse breakdown voltage is a key parameter in the design of Zener diode circuits and should be carefully selected to meet the requirements of the circuit.

4. Zener impedance: The Zener impedance is the impedance of the Zener diode in the reverse-biased region and is a measure of the resistance to the flow of current through the diode. The Zener impedance is typically low, which allows the Zener diode to maintain a constant voltage across the diode.

In conclusion, the Zener diode is a special type of diode that is designed to operate in the reverse breakdown region and maintain a constant voltage across the diode. The V-I characteristics of a Zener diode, the graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance, the reverse breakdown voltage, and the Zener impedance are important parameters in the design of Zener diode circuits. Understanding the characteristics of Zener diodes is essential for designing efficient and reliable electrical circuits.
