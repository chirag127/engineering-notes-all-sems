### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on the ir ratings.

Objective 1: Study of Various Types of Active & Passive Components Based on their Ratings:

This objective aims to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the different types of active and passive components used in electronics and electrical engineering, as well as their ratings. The objective is to equip students with the knowledge and skills needed to select and use these components in their projects and designs.

The course content will cover topics such as the different types of active and passive components, including resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, transistors, and integrated circuits. Students will learn about the different ratings of these components, including their resistance, capacitance, inductance, voltage, and current ratings.

In addition, students will learn about the different applications of active and passive components, including power management, signal processing, and communication. They will also learn about the limitations and challenges associated with different components and how to overcome these limitations.

Overall, the objective is to provide students with a solid foundation in the study of various types of active and passive components based on their ratings and to prepare them for careers in fields where electronics and electrical engineering are important, such as telecommunications, power engineering, and consumer electronics.
