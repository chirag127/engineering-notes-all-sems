### 4. Winding shop: Step down transformer winding of less than 5VA.

Winding shop is a place where electrical transformers are wound, i.e. the wire is wound around the core. A step down transformer winding is used to reduce the voltage of an electrical signal. A transformer of less than 5VA is a small-sized transformer, typically used in low power applications. The winding process involves winding the wire around the core in a specific pattern to create the desired number of turns. The number of turns determines the voltage ratio of the transformer. The winding must be done with care to ensure that the wire is evenly spaced and not damaged. The winding process is a critical step in the manufacture of transformers and must be done with precision to ensure that the transformer performs as expected.
