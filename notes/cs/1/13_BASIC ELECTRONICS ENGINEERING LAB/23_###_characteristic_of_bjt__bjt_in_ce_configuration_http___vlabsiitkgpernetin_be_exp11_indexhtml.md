### Characteristic of BJT: BJT in CE configuration. http://vlabs.iitkgp.ernet.in/be/exp11/index.html

BJT (Bipolar Junction Transistor) CE (Common Emitter) configuration has the following characteristics:
1. Input impedance is high
2. Output impedance is low
3. Voltage gain is high
4. Current gain is moderate
5. Power gain is moderate
6. Operating point is stable
7. Input is applied to the base, output is taken from the collector.
8. The emitter is common to both input and output.
9. It is commonly used for amplification purposes.
