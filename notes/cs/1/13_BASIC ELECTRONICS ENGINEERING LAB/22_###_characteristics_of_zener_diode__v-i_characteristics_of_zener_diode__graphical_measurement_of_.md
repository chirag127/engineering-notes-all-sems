### Characteristics of Zener diode: V-I characteristics of Zener diode, Graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance. http://vlabs.iitkgp.ernet.in/be/exp10/index.html

Zener diode is a specialized type of diode that operates in reverse bias mode. Its main characteristic is the Zener breakdown voltage. When the reverse voltage across the diode exceeds this voltage, the diode begins to conduct in the reverse direction.

V-I Characteristics: 
- In reverse bias, the Zener diode has a nearly constant voltage across it, called the Zener voltage, for a wide range of current. 
- In forward bias, it behaves like a normal diode, with a forward voltage drop of around 0.7V.

Graphical measurement:
- The forward resistance of a Zener diode can be measured using a forward bias voltage, and the resulting current can be measured. 
- The reverse resistance can be measured using a reverse bias voltage, and the resulting current can be measured.
