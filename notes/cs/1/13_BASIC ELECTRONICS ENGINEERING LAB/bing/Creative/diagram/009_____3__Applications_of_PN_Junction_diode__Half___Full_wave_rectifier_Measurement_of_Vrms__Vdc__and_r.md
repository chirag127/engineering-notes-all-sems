### 3. Applications of PN Junction diode: Half & Full wave rectifierMeasurement of Vrms, Vdc, and ripple factor.

- A PN junction diode is a device that allows current to flow in one direction only. It has two terminals: anode and cathode. The anode is connected to the p-type semiconductor and the cathode is connected to the n-type semiconductor. When the anode is positive with respect to the cathode, the diode is forward biased and conducts current. When the anode is negative with respect to the cathode, the diode is reverse biased and blocks current .
- A rectifier is a circuit that converts alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC). Rectifiers use diodes to allow only the positive or negative half of the AC waveform to pass through, while blocking the other half. Rectifiers are used in power supplies, detectors, modulators, and other applications that require DC voltage or current .
- A half-wave rectifier is a rectifier that uses only one diode to convert AC to DC. It allows only one half of the AC cycle to pass through, while blocking the other half. The output of a half-wave rectifier is a pulsating DC voltage that has the same frequency as the input AC voltage .
- A full-wave rectifier is a rectifier that uses two or four diodes to convert AC to DC. It allows both halves of the AC cycle to pass through, but in opposite directions. The output of a full-wave rectifier is a smoother DC voltage that has twice the frequency as the input AC voltage .
- Vrms is the root mean square value of the AC voltage. It is the effective value of the AC voltage that produces the same heating effect as a DC voltage of the same magnitude. Vrms can be calculated by taking the square root of the average of the squares of the instantaneous voltages over one cycle .
- Vdc is the average or mean value of the DC voltage. It is the value of the DC voltage that is equivalent to the area under the curve of the rectified output voltage over one cycle. Vdc can be calculated by taking the average of the instantaneous voltages over one cycle .
- Ripple factor is a measure of the variation or fluctuation of the DC output voltage of a rectifier. It is the ratio of the root mean square value of the AC component to the average value of the DC component of the output voltage. Ripple factor can be calculated by taking the square root of the ratio of the mean square value of the AC component to the square of the average value of the DC component of the output voltage .
