## Part (C):

- Part (C) is the third and final part of the header diagram.
- It contains the information about the data payload, such as the length, type, and content of the data.
- The data payload is the actual information that is being transmitted or received by the network device.
- The length of the data payload is usually expressed in bytes or bits, and it indicates how much data is contained in the payload.
- The type of the data payload is usually expressed in a code or a name, and it indicates what kind of data is contained in the payload, such as text, image, audio, video, etc.
- The content of the data payload is the actual data itself, such as the text message, the image file, the audio clip, the video stream, etc.
- The data payload can be encrypted, compressed, or encoded to enhance the security, efficiency, or compatibility of the data transmission.
- The data payload can also be divided into smaller segments or packets to facilitate the data transmission over the network.
