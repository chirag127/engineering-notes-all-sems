## Part (C):

- Part (C) is the third and final part of the assignment.
- In this part, you will apply the concepts and techniques learned in the course to analyze a real-world dataset of your choice.
- You will need to perform the following tasks:
  - Choose a dataset that is relevant to your interests or domain of study. The dataset should be publicly available and contain at least 1000 observations and 10 variables. You can use any source of data, such as Kaggle, UCI Machine Learning Repository, Google Dataset Search, etc.
  - Write a brief introduction about the dataset, its source, and its main features. Explain why you chose this dataset and what questions you want to answer with it.
  - Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) on the dataset using appropriate tools and techniques. You should include descriptive statistics, data visualization, data cleaning, data transformation, and feature engineering. You should also identify and handle any missing values, outliers, or anomalies in the data.
  - Apply one or more supervised or unsupervised machine learning methods to the dataset to answer your research questions. You should justify your choice of methods and evaluate their performance using appropriate metrics and validation techniques. You should also interpret the results and discuss their implications and limitations.
  - Write a conclusion that summarizes your findings and provides recommendations or suggestions for further research or improvement.
  - Submit your code, report, and presentation slides as separate files. Your code should be well-commented and reproducible. Your report should be written in a clear and concise manner, following the structure of introduction, EDA, methods, results, discussion, and conclusion. Your presentation slides should highlight the main points and insights from your analysis, using effective visual aids and storytelling techniques.
