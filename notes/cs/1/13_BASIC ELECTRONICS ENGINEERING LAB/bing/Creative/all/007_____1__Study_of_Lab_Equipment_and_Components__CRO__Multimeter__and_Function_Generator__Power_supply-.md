# 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- The objective of this topic is to study various electronic components and design of various electronic circuits like power supply, audio and power amplifiers.
- The following are some of the lab equipment and components that are commonly used in electronics experiments:

  - **CRO**: A cathode ray oscilloscope (CRO) is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure the voltage, frequency, phase, and shape of the signal. It consists of a cathode ray tube (CRT), a vertical and a horizontal amplifier, a time base generator, and a trigger circuit .
  - **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a device that can measure various electrical quantities such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, and continuity. It can be either analog or digital. It consists of a selector switch, a display, and probes .
  - **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that can produce different types of periodic signals such as sine, square, triangle, and sawtooth waves. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and duty cycle of the signal. It consists of a signal generator, a frequency control, an amplitude control, and an output terminal .
  - **Power Supply**: A power supply is a device that converts the alternating current (AC) from the mains to a direct current (DC) of a desired voltage and current. It can be either fixed or variable. It consists of a transformer, a rectifier, a filter, a regulator, and an output terminal .
  - **Active Components**: Active components are those that can amplify or control the flow of electric current. They require an external source of power to operate. Some examples are transistors, diodes, integrated circuits, and optoelectronic devices .
  - **Passive Components**: Passive components are those that can store or dissipate electric energy. They do not require an external source of power to operate. Some examples are resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers .
  - **Bread Board**: A bread board is a device that allows the temporary connection of electronic components without soldering. It consists of a plastic board with rows of holes and metal strips underneath. The holes are connected by the metal strips in a specific pattern. It is used for prototyping and testing electronic circuits .

- The students can study the characteristics of electronic components and measuring instruments by using the lab equipment and components. They can also design and test various electronic circuits by using the bread board and the power supply.
