### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- The objective of this topic is to study various electronic components and design of various electronic circuits like power supply, audio and power amplifiers.
- The main lab equipment and components are:
  - CRO: Cathode Ray Oscilloscope, a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure voltage, frequency, phase, and other parameters of a signal .
  - Multimeter: A device that can measure multiple electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, and continuity. It can be analog or digital .
  - Function Generator: A device that can generate different types of periodic signals, such as sine, square, triangle, and pulse waves. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and shape of the output signal .
  - Power Supply: A device that provides a constant and regulated voltage and current to a circuit. It can be AC or DC, and can have single or multiple outputs .
  - Active Components: Electronic components that can amplify or control the flow of electric current, such as transistors, diodes, LEDs, and integrated circuits .
  - Passive Components: Electronic components that do not amplify or control the current, but only store or dissipate energy, such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers .
  - Bread Board: A board with holes and metal strips that can be used to connect electronic components without soldering. It allows easy prototyping and testing of circuits .
- The students can study the characteristics of electronic components and measuring instruments by using them in various experiments and observing their behavior and output .
- The students can also learn how to use the software that integrates measurement and analysis across the lab equipment, such as Keysight Smart Bench Essentials. This software can help the students to visualize, capture, and share data from multiple instruments on a single screen, and to perform advanced analysis and simulation of circuits.
