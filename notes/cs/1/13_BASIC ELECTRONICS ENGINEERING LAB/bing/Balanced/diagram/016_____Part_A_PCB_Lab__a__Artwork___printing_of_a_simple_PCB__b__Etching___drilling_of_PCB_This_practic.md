### Part A PCB Lab: a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB. b. Etching & drilling of PCB

- PCB stands for printed circuit board, which is a thin board made of fiberglass, composite epoxy, or other laminate material.
- PCBs are used to connect electronic components, such as resistors, capacitors, transistors, and integrated circuits, using conductive tracks, pads, and vias.
- The process of making a PCB involves several steps, such as designing, artwork, printing, etching, drilling, and soldering.

#### Artwork & printing of a simple PCB

- Artwork is the graphical representation of the circuit layout, which shows the placement and routing of the components and the tracks on the PCB.
- Artwork can be created using software tools, such as Eagle, KiCad, or Altium, or by hand-drawing on a transparent sheet or paper.
- Printing is the process of transferring the artwork onto a copper-clad board, which is the base material for the PCB.
- Printing can be done using various methods, such as photoresist, toner transfer, or direct inkjet.
- Photoresist method involves coating the copper board with a light-sensitive material, exposing it to ultraviolet light through the artwork, and developing it with a chemical solution to remove the unwanted copper.
- Toner transfer method involves printing the artwork on a glossy paper using a laser printer, ironing it onto the copper board, and peeling off the paper to leave the toner on the board.
- Direct inkjet method involves printing the artwork directly on the copper board using a special inkjet printer that can print conductive ink.

#### Etching & drilling of PCB

- Etching is the process of removing the unwanted copper from the board, leaving only the tracks and pads that form the circuit.
- Etching can be done using various chemicals, such as ferric chloride, ammonium persulfate, or sodium hydroxide, that dissolve the copper.
- Etching can be done by immersing the board in the etchant solution, spraying the etchant on the board, or using a sponge or brush to apply the etchant on the board.
- Drilling is the process of making holes on the board for mounting the components and connecting the vias.
- Drilling can be done using a drill press, a hand drill, or a CNC machine, depending on the accuracy and complexity of the board.
- Drilling can be done before or after etching, depending on the method of printing and the type of components.
