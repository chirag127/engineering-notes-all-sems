### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- The objective of this topic is to study various electronic components and design of various electronic circuits like power supply, audio and power amplifiers.
- The students can study the characteristics of electronic components and measuring instruments.
- The lab equipment and components are:

  - **CRO**: Cathode Ray Oscilloscope. It is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal. It can measure voltage, frequency, phase, and other parameters of a signal.
  - **Multimeter**: It is a device that can measure multiple electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, etc. It can be analog or digital.
  - **Function Generator**: It is a device that can generate different types of electrical signals, such as sine, square, triangular, etc. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and shape of the signals.
  - **Power supply**: It is a device that provides a constant and regulated voltage or current to a circuit. It can be AC or DC, fixed or variable.
  - **Active components**: They are the components that can control the flow of electric current in a circuit. They require an external power source to operate. Examples are transistors, diodes, LEDs, etc.
  - **Passive components**: They are the components that do not control the current flow in a circuit. They do not require an external power source to operate. Examples are resistors, capacitors, inductors, etc.
  - **Breadboard**: It is a board that allows the user to create temporary circuits by inserting wires and components into holes. It is useful for testing and prototyping circuits.

- The following are some of the applications of the lab equipment and components:

  - CRO: It can be used to analyze the behavior and performance of a circuit or a signal. It can also be used to troubleshoot and debug circuits.
  - Multimeter: It can be used to measure the electrical parameters of a circuit or a component. It can also be used to test the continuity and polarity of a circuit.
  - Function Generator: It can be used to provide input signals to a circuit or a device. It can also be used to simulate different scenarios and conditions for a circuit.
  - Power supply: It can be used to power a circuit or a device. It can also be used to vary the voltage or current of a circuit.
  - Active components: They can be used to amplify, switch, rectify, or modulate signals in a circuit. They can also be used to create logic gates and circuits.
  - Passive components: They can be used to limit, filter, store, or transfer energy in a circuit. They can also be used to create passive filters and oscillators.
  - Breadboard: It can be used to create and test different circuits without soldering or permanent connections. It can also be used to modify and experiment with circuits.
