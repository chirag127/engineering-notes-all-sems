### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- CRO stands for Cathode Ray Oscilloscope. It is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure the voltage, frequency, phase, and other characteristics of the signal. It consists of a cathode ray tube, a vertical and a horizontal amplifier, a time base generator, and a trigger circuit .
- Multimeter is a device that can measure multiple electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, and continuity. It can be either analog or digital. It consists of a display, a selector switch, and probes .
- Function Generator is a device that can generate different types of electrical signals, such as sine, square, triangular, and pulse waves. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and shape of the signal. It consists of a signal generator, a frequency control, an amplitude control, and an output terminal .
- Power Supply is a device that can provide a constant and regulated voltage and current to a circuit. It can be either AC or DC. It consists of a transformer, a rectifier, a filter, and a regulator .
- Active Components are the components that can amplify or control the flow of electrical signals, such as transistors, diodes, LEDs, and integrated circuits. They require an external power source to operate .
- Passive Components are the components that can store or dissipate electrical energy, such as resistors, capacitors, inductors, and switches. They do not require an external power source to operate .
- Bread Board is a device that can be used to create temporary circuits without soldering. It consists of a plastic board with holes and metal strips that connect the components. It can be used to test and prototype circuits .
