# Part B Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, Function Generator, Power supply, Active, Passive Components and Bread Board

- The objective of this course is to study various electronic components and design of various electronic circuits like power supply, audio and power amplifiers .
- The students can study the characteristics of electronic components and measuring instruments .
- The lab equipment and components are:

  - CRO: A cathode ray oscilloscope (CRO) is a device that displays the waveform of an electrical signal on a screen. It can measure voltage, frequency, phase, and other parameters of a signal .
  - Multimeter: A multimeter is a device that can measure multiple electrical quantities, such as voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, and continuity. It can be analog or digital .
  - Function Generator: A function generator is a device that can produce various types of periodic signals, such as sine, square, triangle, and sawtooth waves. It can vary the frequency, amplitude, and shape of the output signal .
  - Power supply: A power supply is a device that converts AC voltage from the mains to DC voltage for the electronic circuits. It can provide fixed or variable output voltage and current .
  - Active components: Active components are electronic devices that can amplify, oscillate, or switch electrical signals. They require an external power source to operate. Examples are transistors, diodes, LEDs, and integrated circuits .
  - Passive components: Passive components are electronic devices that can store, filter, or dissipate electrical energy. They do not require an external power source to operate. Examples are resistors, capacitors, inductors, and transformers .
  - Breadboard: A breadboard is a device that allows the user to create temporary electronic circuits without soldering. It has a grid of holes that are connected by metal strips underneath. The user can insert wires and components into the holes to form connections .

- These test equipment and components can be demonstrated online from any lab of ECE department or physical mode is only option depending on the availability and accessibility of the resources. Online mode may require simulation software or remote access to the lab devices. Physical mode may require proper safety and precaution measures.
