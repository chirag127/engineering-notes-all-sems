### 4. Characteristics of Zener diode: V-I characteristics of zener diode, Graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance.

- The forward characteristic of the volt-ampere characteristic curve of the Zener diode is similar to that of an ordinary diode.
- The reverse characteristic is that when the reverse voltage is lower than the reverse breakdown voltage, the reverse resistance is very large and the reverse leakage current is extremely small.
- The V-I characteristic of Zener diode indicates that characteristic of Zener diode in forward bias is same as PN junction diode.
- In reverse bias, a negligible constant current flow through the zener diode but the current becomes abruptly large at certain voltage. This voltage is called as zener voltage.
- This sudden and sharp increase in zener current is called as zener breakdown.
- A Zener Diode is constructed for operation in the reverse breakdown region. The relation between I-V is almost linear in this case Vz = Vz0 + Izrz, where rz is the dynamic resistance of the zener at the operating point. Vz0 is the voltage at which the straight-line approximation of the I-V characteristic intersects the horizontal axis.
