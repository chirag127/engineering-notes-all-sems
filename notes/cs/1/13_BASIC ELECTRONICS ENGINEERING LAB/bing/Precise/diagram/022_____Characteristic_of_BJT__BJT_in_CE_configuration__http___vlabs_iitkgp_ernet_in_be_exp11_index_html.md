### Characteristic of BJT: BJT in CE configuration

- The Common Emitter (CE) Configuration is the most commonly used circuit for transistor-based amplifiers .
- In the Common Emitter or grounded emitter configuration, the input signal is applied between the base and the emitter, while the output is taken from between the collector and the emitter .
- The qualitative characteristic curves of a BJT indicate the four regions of operation: the saturation, the cutoff, the active, and the breakdown .
- Each family of curves is drawn for a different base current .
