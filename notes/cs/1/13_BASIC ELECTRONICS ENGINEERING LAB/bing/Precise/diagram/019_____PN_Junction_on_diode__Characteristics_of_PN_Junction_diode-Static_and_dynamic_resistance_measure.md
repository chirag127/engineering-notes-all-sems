### PN Junction on diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode-Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph.

A PN junction diode is a two-terminal electronic device that allows the flow of current in only one direction. It is formed by joining a p-type semiconductor material with an n-type semiconductor material.

#### Static Resistance:

Static resistance is the resistance offered by the diode to the flow of current when it is in its forward-biased condition. It is calculated by dividing the DC voltage applied across the diode by the DC current flowing through it.

#### Dynamic Resistance:

Dynamic resistance is the resistance offered by the diode to the flow of current when it is in its forward-biased condition and the applied voltage is changing. It is calculated by dividing the change in voltage across the diode by the change in current flowing through it.

#### Measurement from Graph:

The static and dynamic resistance of a PN junction diode can be measured from its forward-bias characteristic curve. The static resistance is calculated by taking the ratio of the voltage and current at a particular point on the curve. The dynamic resistance is calculated by taking the slope of the tangent drawn to the curve at that point.
