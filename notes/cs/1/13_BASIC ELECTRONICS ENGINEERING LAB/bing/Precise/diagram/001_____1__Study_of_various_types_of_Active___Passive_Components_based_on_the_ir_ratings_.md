### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings

Active and passive components are the two main types of electronic components. They are distinguished by their ability to amplify signals or control the flow of current.

- **Active Components:** These components rely on a source of energy and can control the flow of current. They are capable of amplifying signals and are commonly used in electronic circuits. Examples of active components include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

- **Passive Components:** These components do not require a source of energy to operate and cannot amplify signals. They are used to store, filter, or regulate the flow of current in a circuit. Examples of passive components include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

The ratings of these components are important to consider when designing and building electronic circuits. Ratings such as voltage, current, and power can affect the performance and safety of a circuit. It is important to choose components with appropriate ratings for the intended application.

- **Voltage Rating:** This rating specifies the maximum voltage that a component can safely withstand. Exceeding this rating can cause damage to the component and potentially the entire circuit.

- **Current Rating:** This rating specifies the maximum current that a component can safely carry. Exceeding this rating can cause the component to overheat and potentially fail.

- **Power Rating:** This rating specifies the maximum power that a component can safely dissipate. Exceeding this rating can cause the component to overheat and potentially fail.

In summary, active and passive components are distinguished by their ability to amplify signals or control the flow of current. The ratings of these components are important to consider when designing and building electronic circuits. Choosing components with appropriate ratings for the intended application can help ensure the performance and safety of a circuit.
