## (D) Experiments available on virtual lab

Virtual labs provide a platform for students to perform experiments and simulations online. There are a variety of virtual labs available that offer a wide range of experiments for students to perform. Some of the virtual labs and the experiments they offer are:

1. **Virtual Chemistry Experiments Collection** - A collection of interactive web-based chemistry tutorials .
2. **Mixed Reception** - Solve a mysterious death by gathering clues, interviewing suspects, and analyzing evidence .
3. **The Virtual Lab** - An online simulation of a chemistry lab designed to help students link chemical computations with authentic laboratory chemistry .
4. **PraxiLabs** - Offers virtual experiments that are adaptive to any learning style. It includes progress tracking, walkthrough videos, lab manuals, enrichment documents, immediate feedback, MCQ quizzes, and much more .
5. **VRLab Academy** - Offers more than 200 science experiments available on VR and PC .
6. **CK–12 Chemistry Simulations** - Nearly two dozen simulations cover topics like average atomic mass, solubility with rock candy, and freezing point depression with road salt .
7. **Mystery Science** - Offers a wealth of virtual science labs, activities, and experiments for students in elementary school. From weather to astronomy, humans to animals, they’ve got you covered .
8. **Molecular Workbench** - Offers hundreds of virtual science labs for physics, biology, chemistry, and more .
9. **National Science Digital Library: ChemCollective Virtual Labs** - Take a deep dive into chemistry with these virtual labs .
10. **PBS: Nova Labs** - A terrific platform for teens and older learners to explore and examine cutting-edge issues .

These are just a few examples of the many virtual labs available that offer a wide range of experiments for students to perform. Virtual labs provide a safe and convenient way for students to perform experiments and learn about science. They are a valuable resource for students and educators alike.
