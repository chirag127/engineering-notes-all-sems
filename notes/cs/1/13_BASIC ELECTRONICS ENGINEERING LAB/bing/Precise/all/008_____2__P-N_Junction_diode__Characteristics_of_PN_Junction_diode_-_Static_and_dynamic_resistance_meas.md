### 2. P-N Junction diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode - Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph.

A P-N junction diode is a semiconductor device that is formed by the junction of a p-type semiconductor and an n-type semiconductor. The characteristics of a P-N junction diode can be studied by measuring its static and dynamic resistance from its current-voltage (I-V) graph.

- **Static Resistance:** The static resistance of a P-N junction diode is the ratio of the DC voltage applied across the diode to the DC current flowing through it. It can be calculated by taking the ratio of the voltage and current at a particular point on the I-V graph of the diode.

- **Dynamic Resistance:** The dynamic resistance of a P-N junction diode is the ratio of the small change in voltage to the corresponding small change in current at a particular operating point on the I-V graph of the diode. It can be calculated by taking the slope of the tangent to the I-V curve at that operating point.

The static and dynamic resistance measurements of a P-N junction diode provide valuable information about its operating characteristics and can be used to design and analyze electronic circuits that use diodes. It is important to note that the static and dynamic resistance values of a diode are not constant and vary with the operating conditions of the diode.
