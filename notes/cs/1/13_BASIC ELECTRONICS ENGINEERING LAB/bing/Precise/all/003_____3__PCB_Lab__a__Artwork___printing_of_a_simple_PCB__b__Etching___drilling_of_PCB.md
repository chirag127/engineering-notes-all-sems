### 3. PCB Lab: 
a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB: 
- The first step in creating a PCB is to design the artwork. This can be done using a software program such as Eagle or Altium Designer.
- The artwork represents the layout of the copper traces and other components on the PCB.
- Once the artwork is complete, it is printed onto a transparent film using a laser printer.
- This film is then used to create a photomask, which is used in the next step of the process.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:
- The photomask is placed over a copper-clad board, and the board is exposed to UV light.
- The areas of the board that are exposed to the light will harden, while the areas that are covered by the photomask will remain soft.
- The board is then developed, which removes the soft areas of the board, leaving only the hardened copper traces.
- The board is then etched, which removes the remaining copper, leaving only the traces that were hardened by the UV light.
- Finally, the board is drilled to create holes for the components to be mounted.
