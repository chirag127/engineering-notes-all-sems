### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

- **CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope)**: A CRO is an electronic device used to display and analyze electrical signals. It consists of a cathode ray tube (CRT) that produces an electron beam, which is deflected by electric fields to produce a visible trace on the screen.

- **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a device used to measure electrical quantities such as voltage, current, and resistance. It can be either analog or digital, and can have a variety of features and functions.

- **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that produces a variety of electrical waveforms, such as sine, square, and triangle waves. It is used to test and calibrate electronic equipment.

- **Power Supply**: A power supply is a device that provides electrical power to other devices. It can be either AC or DC, and can have a variety of features and functions.

- **Active Components**: Active components are electronic components that require an external power source to operate. Examples include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

- **Passive Components**: Passive components are electronic components that do not require an external power source to operate. Examples include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

- **Bread Board**: A breadboard is a device used to prototype electronic circuits. It consists of a plastic board with a grid of holes, into which components can be inserted and connected using jumper wires.
