### 4. Characteristics of Zener diode: V-I characteristics of zener diode, Graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance.

A Zener diode is a type of diode that is designed to operate in the reverse breakdown region. The V-I characteristics of a Zener diode can be divided into two regions: the forward region and the reverse region.

1. **Forward Region:** In the forward region, the Zener diode behaves like a normal diode. When a forward voltage is applied, the diode starts conducting and the current increases rapidly. The forward resistance of the diode is very low.

2. **Reverse Region:** In the reverse region, the Zener diode behaves differently. When a reverse voltage is applied, the diode does not conduct until the voltage reaches the Zener voltage. At this point, the diode starts conducting and the current increases rapidly. The reverse resistance of the diode is very high.

The graphical measurement of the forward and reverse resistance of a Zener diode can be done by plotting the V-I characteristics of the diode. The slope of the curve in the forward region gives the forward resistance, while the slope of the curve in the reverse region gives the reverse resistance.
