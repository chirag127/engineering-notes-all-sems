### Part B Study of Lab Equipment’s and Components: CRO, Multi meter, Function Generator, Power supplyActive, Passive Components and Bread Board

1. **CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope)**: A CRO is an electronic test instrument that displays electrical signals graphically, usually as a voltage waveform. It is used to observe the change of an electrical signal over time, such as voltage or current.

2. **Multimeter**: A multimeter is a handheld device that combines several measurement functions in one unit. It is used to measure voltage, current, and resistance.

3. **Function Generator**: A function generator is a device that produces electrical waveforms. These waveforms can be either repetitive or single-shot, and can be of various shapes, such as sine, square, triangle, and sawtooth.

4. **Power Supply**: A power supply is an electronic device that supplies electric power to an electrical load. It converts one form of electrical energy to another, such as AC to DC.

5. **Active Components**: Active components are electronic components that require a source of energy to operate. Examples include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits.

6. **Passive Components**: Passive components are electronic components that do not require a source of energy to operate. Examples include resistors, capacitors, and inductors.

7. **Breadboard**: A breadboard is a construction base for prototyping of electronics. It allows the user to create temporary circuits without the need for soldering.

These test equipment can be demonstrated online from any lab of the ECE department or physical mode is the only option.
