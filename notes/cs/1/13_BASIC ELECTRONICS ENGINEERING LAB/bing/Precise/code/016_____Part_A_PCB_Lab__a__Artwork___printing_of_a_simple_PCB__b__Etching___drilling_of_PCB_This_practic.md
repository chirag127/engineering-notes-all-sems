### Part A PCB Lab: 

a. Artwork & printing of a simple PCB: 
- The first step in creating a PCB is to design the artwork. This involves creating a schematic diagram of the circuit and then laying out the components on a virtual board.
- Once the design is complete, it is printed onto a special type of paper called transfer paper. This paper is then used to transfer the design onto a copper-clad board.
- The transfer paper is placed onto the copper-clad board and heat is applied to transfer the design onto the board. This process is called printing.

b. Etching & drilling of PCB:
- After the design has been transferred onto the copper-clad board, the next step is to etch away the unwanted copper to reveal the circuit pattern.
- This is done by using a chemical etchant that dissolves the copper. The board is then rinsed and dried.
- Once the etching process is complete, the board is drilled to create holes for the components to be inserted. The holes are drilled using a special drill bit designed for PCBs.
