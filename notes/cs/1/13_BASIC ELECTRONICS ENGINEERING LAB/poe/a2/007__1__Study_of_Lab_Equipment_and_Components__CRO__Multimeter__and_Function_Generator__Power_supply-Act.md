### 1. Study of Lab Equipment and Components: CRO, Multimeter, and Function Generator, Power supply-Active, Passive Components and Bread Board.

1. CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope):
- Used to analyze electrical signals graphically as a 2D plot of one or more signal variables (voltage, current, frequency) versus time.
- Has a cathode ray tube with an electron gun that shoots a beam of electrons onto a phosphorescent screen. The electron beam is deflected in both X and Y directions by a pair of deflection coils which are driven by voltages generated from the input signal. This results in the display of a waveform that can be analyzed.
- Key parts: Cathode ray tube, deflection plates, time base, input terminals.

2. Multimeter:
- Used to measure electrical quantities like voltage, current, resistance, capacitance, frequency, continuity, etc.
- Can be analog or digital and contains a variety of scales and ranges to measure different quantities.
- Key parts: Display, selector switch, input jacks.

3. Function Generator:
- Produces electrical waveforms of different shapes (sine, square, triangle, sawtooth, etc.) at a specific frequency and amplitude.
- Can control the frequency and amplitude of output and is used to provide test signals to electronic circuits.
- Key parts: Oscillator circuit, waveform shaping circuit, frequency and amplitude controls.
