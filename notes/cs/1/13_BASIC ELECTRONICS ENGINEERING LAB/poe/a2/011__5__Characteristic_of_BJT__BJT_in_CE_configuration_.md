### 5. Characteristic of BJT: BJT in CE configuration.

1. In CE configuration, the base terminal of BJT is connected to the ground and the emitter terminal is connected to the supply voltage.
2. The input voltage is applied between the base and emitter terminals. The output is taken across the collector and emitter terminals.
3. When there is no input voltage, the BJT is in cutoff region. The output voltage is zero.
4. As the input voltage increases, the BJT enters the active region and the output voltage starts increasing. The BJT behaves as a voltage controlled current source in this region.
5. In saturation region, the output voltage remains almost constant even for large increase in input voltage. The BJT behaves as a switch in this region.
6. The above characteristics can be plotted on output characteristics and transfer characteristics curve to analyze the BJT in CE configuration.
