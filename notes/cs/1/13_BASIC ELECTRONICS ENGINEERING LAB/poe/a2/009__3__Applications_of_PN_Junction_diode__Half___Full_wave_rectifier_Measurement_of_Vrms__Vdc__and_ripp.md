### 3. Applications of PN Junction diode: Half & Full wave rectifierMeasurement of Vrms, Vdc, and ripple factor

- Half wave rectifier: It converts AC to DC using one half cycle of the input AC signal. During the positive half cycle, the diode is forward biased and conducts current, while in the negative half cycle, the diode is reverse biased and blocks the current. The output DC voltage has some ripples.
- Full wave rectifier: It converts the complete AC signal into DC signal using both half cycles of the input. This can be achieved using a bridge rectifier configuration which provides a more smooth output DC voltage with lesser ripples compared to half wave rectifier.
- The RMS, average and ripple factor measurements can be done across the output of the rectifiers to analyze their performance. The RMS voltage gives the equivalent DC voltage that would produce the same heating effect as the AC voltage. The average voltage is the mean of the rectified voltage over one complete cycle. The ripple factor is the ratio of the RMS value of the ripple voltage to the DC voltage.
