### Characteristic of BJT: BJT in CE configuration

1. In CE configuration, the base terminal of BJT is forward biased by connecting it to the positive terminal of the supply voltage. The emitter-base junction is forward biased and the collector-base junction is reverse biased.
2. The input characteristic shows that as the base voltage (VBE) increases, the base current (IB) increases exponentially. This indicates that BJT behaves like a diode at the base-emitter junction.
3. The output characteristic shows that the collector current (IC) increases with the increase in the collector-emitter voltage (VCE). For a given VCE, IC increases with the increase in the base current (IB). This shows the current control property of BJT where a small change in IB results in a large change in the IC.
4. There exist a saturation region and an ohmic region in the output characteristic based on the behavior of IC with VCE. In the saturation region, IC becomes independent of VCE whereas in the ohmic region IC increases with VCE.
5. The transistors can be used as an amplifier, oscillator, and switch based on the biasing and the region of operation in the output characteristic.
