### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings.

1. Resistors:
- Fixed value resistors: Have fixed resistance value. Eg: Carbon composition, Wire wound, Metal film etc.
- Variable resistors: Can vary resistance. Eg: Potentiometer, Rheostat, Pressure sensors etc.
- Based on temperature coefficient: Positive, Negative, Zero.

2. Capacitors:
- Polarized: Electrolytic, Tantalum.
- Non-polarized: Ceramic, Paper, Mica, Plastic film.
- Based on dielectrics used and application.
- Ratings: Voltage, Capacitance, Temperature, Frequency.

3. Inductors:
- Air core: Low inductance, low cost.
- Ferromagnetic core: High inductance, frequency dependent.
- Based on core material and application.
- Ratings: Inductance, Current carrying capacity, Temperature.

4. Diodes:
- PN junction diodes: General purpose, Switching.
- Special purpose diodes: Zener, Varactor, Tunnel, LED, Laser etc.
- Ratings: Peak inverse voltage, Average forward current, Power dissipation.
