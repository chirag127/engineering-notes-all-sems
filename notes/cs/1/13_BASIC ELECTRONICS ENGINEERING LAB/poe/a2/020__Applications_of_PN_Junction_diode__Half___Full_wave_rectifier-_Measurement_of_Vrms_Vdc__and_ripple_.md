### Applications of PN Junction diode: Half & Full wave rectifier- Measurement of Vrms,Vdc, and ripple factor.

1. Half wave rectifier:
- PN junction diode is connected in series with a load resistance across an AC supply.
- During positive half cycle, diode is forward biased and allows current to flow through it. The output voltage across load resistance is equal to the applied voltage.
- During negative half cycle, diode is reverse biased and does not conduct. The output voltage is zero.
- Output is a pulsed DC voltage containing AC components.

2. Full wave rectifier:
- Uses a bridge rectifier circuit which consists of 4 diodes connected in a bridge configuration.
- During both half cycles, the diodes are forward biased in pairs and allow current to flow in the same direction through the load resistance.
- Output is a constant magnitude DC voltage with minimum ripple.

3. Measurement of Vrms, Vdc and ripple factor:
- Vrms can be measured using a multimeter
- Vdc can be measured using a multimeter
- Ripple factor = (Vrms/Vdc) - 1
