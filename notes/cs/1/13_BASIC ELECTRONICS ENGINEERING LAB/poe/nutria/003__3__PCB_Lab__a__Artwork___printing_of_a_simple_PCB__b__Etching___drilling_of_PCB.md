
### 3. PCB Lab: 
a. Artwork & Printing of a Simple PCB
- Artwork of a printed circuit board (PCB) is the process of creating an electronic design on a computer and transferring it to a board. 
- This involves creating a schematic diagram, laying out the components on a board, and then sending the design to a PCB fabrication house. 
- The fabrication house will then print the design onto the board and send it back to the user. 

b. Etching & Drilling of PCB
- Etching is the process of removing unwanted copper from the PCB to create the desired circuit pattern. 
- This is done using a chemical solution, such as ferric chloride, which will corrode the copper and leave the desired pattern. 
- Drilling is then used to create the holes for the components to be inserted into the board. This is done by using a drill press and a drill bit to create the holes in the board.
