
### Characteristics of Zener Diode

* Zener diodes are a type of semiconductor device that can be used to regulate voltage.
* The V-I characteristics of a Zener diode are non-linear and can be approximated by a straight line for voltages below the Zener voltage.
* Above the Zener voltage, the diode exhibits a negative resistance characteristic.
* Graphical measurement of the forward and reverse resistance of a Zener diode can be done using an ohmmeter.
* The Zener voltage can be determined by measuring the voltage at which the current through the diode increases exponentially.
* Zener diodes can also be used to provide a reference voltage for a circuit.
