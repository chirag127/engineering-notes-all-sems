
## Part (C):

1. Formality is a way of speaking or writing that shows respect and politeness.
2. Formal language is used in professional and academic settings, and is characterized by the use of precise vocabulary and correct grammar.
3. In contrast, informal language is more casual and relaxed, and is often used in everyday conversations.
4. When writing formally, it is important to avoid using slang, colloquialisms, and idioms.
5. Additionally, it is important to avoid using contractions, and to use complete sentences that are properly punctuated.
6. Formal language should also be respectful and inclusive, avoiding any language that could be seen as offensive or discriminatory.
