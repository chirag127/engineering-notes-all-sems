
### 4. Characteristics of Zener Diode

* Zener diode is a special type of diode that is designed to conduct current in the reverse direction when the voltage across it reaches a certain value, known as the breakdown voltage or Zener voltage.
* The V-I characteristics of a Zener diode are different from that of a normal diode. When the voltage across the diode is less than the breakdown voltage, the current through the diode is almost zero. When the voltage across the diode reaches the breakdown voltage, the current through the diode increases rapidly.
* The graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance of a Zener diode is the same as that of a normal diode. The forward resistance is measured by applying a positive voltage to the diode and measuring the current through it. The reverse resistance is measured by applying a negative voltage to the diode and measuring the current through it.
