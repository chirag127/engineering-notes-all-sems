
## Part A:

1. Formality is a way of speaking or writing that is considered appropriate for a particular situation. It is often used to show respect and politeness. 
2. Formal language is often characterized by the use of specific vocabulary and expressions, as well as a more formal tone. It is often used in professional or academic settings. 
3. In contrast, informal language is more relaxed and conversational. It is often used in casual conversations or when speaking with friends and family. 
4. Formality should be adjusted to the context and the people involved. For example, speaking formally with a professor is usually more appropriate than speaking informally with them. 
5. It is important to be aware of the level of formality that is expected in a given situation, as using the wrong level of formality can lead to misunderstandings or even offense.
