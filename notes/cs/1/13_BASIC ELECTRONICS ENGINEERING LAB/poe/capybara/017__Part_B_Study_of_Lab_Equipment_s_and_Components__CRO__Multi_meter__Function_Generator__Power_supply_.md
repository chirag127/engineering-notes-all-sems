### Part B Study of Lab Equipment’s and Components

In this section, we will cover the study of various lab equipment and components that are commonly used in electronics and communication engineering. These include:

- CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope): A CRO is an electronic device that is used to display and analyze the waveform of electrical signals. It is an essential tool for analyzing and troubleshooting electronic circuits.

- Multi meter: A multi meter is a device that is used to measure various electrical quantities such as voltage, current, and resistance. It is an essential tool for testing and troubleshooting electronic circuits.

- Function Generator: A function generator is an electronic device that is used to generate various types of electrical signals such as sine waves, square waves, and sawtooth waves. It is an essential tool for testing and troubleshooting electronic circuits.

- Power supply - Active: An active power supply is a device that is used to provide a constant and stable output voltage or current to electronic circuits. It is an essential tool for testing and troubleshooting electronic circuits.

- Passive Components: Passive components are electronic components that do not require a power source to operate. These include resistors, capacitors, and inductors. They are essential components in electronic circuits.

- Bread Board: A breadboard is a device that is used to prototype electronic circuits. It allows electronic components to be easily connected and disconnected without the need for soldering.

These test equipment and components can be demonstrated online from any lab of the ECE department. However, physical demonstration is also an option. It is important to have a good understanding of these equipment and components in order to be successful in the field of electronics and communication engineering.
