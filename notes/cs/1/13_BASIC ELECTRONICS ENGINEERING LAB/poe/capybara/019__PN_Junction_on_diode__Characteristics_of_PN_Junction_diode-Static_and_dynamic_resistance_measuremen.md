### PN Junction on Diode: Characteristics of PN Junction Diode-Static and Dynamic Resistance Measurement from Graph

In electronics, a PN junction diode is one of the fundamental building blocks. It is a type of semiconductor device that is used for various electronic applications. The following are some key points on the characteristics of PN junction diodes and static and dynamic resistance measurement from graphs:

#### Characteristics of PN Junction Diode

- A PN junction diode is formed by placing a p-type semiconductor material in contact with an n-type semiconductor material. This forms a PN junction.

- The PN junction diode has two terminals: an anode and a cathode.

- The PN junction diode allows current to flow from the anode to the cathode when the diode is forward-biased, but not when it is reverse-biased.

- The forward voltage drop across the diode is typically around 0.7 volts.

- The reverse breakdown voltage is the maximum reverse voltage that the diode can withstand without breaking down.

- The reverse saturation current is the current that flows when the diode is reverse-biased and has reached its reverse breakdown voltage.

#### Static Resistance Measurement from Graph

- Static resistance is the resistance of a diode that is not changing with time.

- The static resistance of a diode can be determined from its current-voltage (I-V) characteristic graph.

- To measure the static resistance of a diode, we plot the I-V curve of the diode on a graph.

- The slope of the I-V curve at a particular point gives the static resistance of the diode at that point.

#### Dynamic Resistance Measurement from Graph

- Dynamic resistance is the resistance of a diode that is changing with time.

- The dynamic resistance of a diode can also be determined from its I-V characteristic graph.

- To measure the dynamic resistance of a diode, we apply a small AC voltage to the diode and measure the current through the diode.

- The dynamic resistance of the diode is the ratio of the change in voltage to the change in current.

In conclusion, the PN junction diode is a fundamental component in electronics. Understanding its characteristics and how to measure its static and dynamic resistance is crucial to many electronic applications.
