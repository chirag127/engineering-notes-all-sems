### 1. Study of various types of Active & Passive Components based on their ratings.

In order to understand the functioning of electronic circuits, it is important to have a thorough knowledge of the different types of components that are used in them. Components can be broadly classified into two categories - active and passive components. 

Active components are those that require an external source of energy to function. They are capable of amplifying or controlling the flow of electrical signals. Examples of active components include transistors, diodes, and integrated circuits. 

Passive components, on the other hand, do not require an external source of energy to function. They are used to store, filter, or regulate electrical signals. Examples of passive components include resistors, capacitors, and inductors. 

Both active and passive components are rated based on their specifications. These ratings are important as they determine the maximum amount of electrical energy that can be safely handled by the component. 

Some of the key ratings for active components include:

- Voltage rating: This is the maximum voltage that the component can withstand without breaking down.

- Current rating: This is the maximum current that the component can handle without getting damaged.

- Power rating: This is the maximum amount of power that the component can dissipate without getting damaged.

- Frequency rating: This is the maximum frequency at which the component can operate.

Passive components are also rated based on their specifications. Some of the key ratings for passive components include:

- Resistance rating: This is the amount of resistance that the component offers to the flow of electrical current.

- Capacitance rating: This is the amount of electrical charge that the component can store.

- Inductance rating: This is the ability of the component to store energy in a magnetic field.

- Voltage rating: This is the maximum voltage that the component can withstand without breaking down.

By studying the ratings of different types of active and passive components, one can gain a better understanding of their capabilities and limitations. This knowledge is essential for designing and troubleshooting electronic circuits.
