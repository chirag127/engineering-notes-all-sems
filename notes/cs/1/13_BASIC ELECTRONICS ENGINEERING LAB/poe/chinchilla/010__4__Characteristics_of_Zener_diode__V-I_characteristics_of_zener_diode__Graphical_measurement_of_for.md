### 4. Characteristics of Zener Diode:

A Zener diode is a specialized type of diode designed to operate in reverse breakdown region. It is used as a voltage regulator and is capable of maintaining a constant voltage across its terminals despite fluctuations in the input voltage. The following are the characteristics of a Zener diode.

1. V-I Characteristics of Zener Diode:
The V-I characteristics of a Zener diode are non-linear and are characterized by two regions: the forward region and the reverse region. In the forward region, the Zener diode behaves like a regular diode and conducts current only when the voltage applied across it is greater than the threshold voltage. In the reverse region, the Zener diode operates in the breakdown region and conducts current in the reverse direction. The voltage at which the Zener diode enters the breakdown region is called the Zener voltage, and it is a key parameter for selecting a Zener diode.

2. Graphical Measurement of Forward and Reverse Resistance:
The forward resistance of a Zener diode can be measured by applying a small forward bias voltage and measuring the current flowing through the diode. The reverse resistance can be measured by applying a small reverse bias voltage and measuring the current flowing through the diode. The graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance is done by plotting the current versus voltage characteristics of the diode on a graph. The slope of the curve in the forward region gives the forward resistance, while the slope of the curve in the reverse region gives the reverse resistance.

In conclusion, the V-I characteristics of a Zener diode are non-linear, and it operates in the reverse breakdown region. The Zener voltage is a key parameter for selecting a Zener diode, and the graphical measurement of forward and reverse resistance is done by plotting the current versus voltage characteristics of the diode on a graph.
