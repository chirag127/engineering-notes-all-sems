### 2. P-N Junction diode: Characteristics of PN Junction diode - Static and dynamic resistance measurement from graph.

A P-N junction diode is a two-terminal electronic device made from a P-type semiconductor material and an N-type semiconductor material that are joined together. The characteristics of a P-N junction diode can be studied by measuring its static and dynamic resistance.

Static resistance refers to the resistance of the diode when it is not conducting current, and dynamic resistance refers to the resistance of the diode when it is conducting current.

To measure the static and dynamic resistance of a P-N junction diode, the following steps can be followed:

1. Connect the diode to a power source and a load.

2. Apply a varying voltage to the diode and measure the corresponding current.

3. Plot the voltage-current (V-I) characteristic of the diode, which shows the relationship between the voltage and the current.

4. Measure the static resistance by calculating the slope of the V-I characteristic in the region where the diode is not conducting current.

5. Measure the dynamic resistance by calculating the slope of the V-I characteristic in the region where the diode is conducting current.

6. Repeat the experiment several times to obtain an average value for the static and dynamic resistance.

7. Compare the average values obtained with the known values for the diode to determine the accuracy of the measurements.

The static and dynamic resistance of a P-N junction diode can provide important information about the performance and behavior of the diode, and can be used to optimize its performance for specific applications. By understanding the characteristics of a P-N junction diode, engineers and technicians can design and implement effective and reliable electronic circuits.
