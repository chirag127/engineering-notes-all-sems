Part B Study of Lab Equipment’s and Components:
- CRO (Cathode Ray Oscilloscope): a type of electronic test equipment used to display and analyze signals.
- Multimeter: a device used to measure electrical parameters such as voltage, current, and resistance.
- Function Generator: a device used to generate electrical signals such as sine, square, and triangle waves.
- Power Supply: a device that provides electrical power to other equipment.
- Active Components: electronic components that require a power source to operate, such as transistors and diodes.
- Passive Components: electronic components that do not require a power source to operate, such as resistors and capacitors.
- Breadboard: a device used to prototype and test circuits.

Demonstration of these equipment can be done either online or in a physical lab.
