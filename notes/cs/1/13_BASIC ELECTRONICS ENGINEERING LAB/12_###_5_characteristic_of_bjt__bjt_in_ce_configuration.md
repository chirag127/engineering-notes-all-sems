### 5. Characteristic of BJT: BJT in CE configuration.

1. Input Impedance: High input impedance due to the presence of the base-emitter junction.
2. Current Gain: The current gain is moderate, typically between 50 and 200.
3. Output Impedance: Low output impedance, making it suitable for driving low-impedance loads.
4. Voltage Gain: The voltage gain is low, typically less than unity.
5. Common Emitter Configuration: The common emitter configuration is the most widely used configuration for amplifiers.
