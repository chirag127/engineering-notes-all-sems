Evidences for Biological Evolution:
- Biological evolution refers to the process by which species change over time through the accumulation of genetic variations.
- There is overwhelming evidence for biological evolution, including:
  - Fossil record: the fossil record provides evidence of the existence of extinct species and the gradual change of species over time.
  - Comparative anatomy: the similarities and differences in the anatomy of different species provide evidence of their evolutionary relationships.
  - Comparative biochemistry: the similarities and differences in the biochemistry of different species provide evidence of their evolutionary relationships.
  - Biogeography: the distribution of species across the world provides evidence of their evolutionary history and how they have adapted to different environments.
  - Embryology: the similarities in the development of embryos of different species provides evidence of their evolutionary relationships.
- These and other lines of evidence provide strong support for the theory of biological evolution and the idea that species have changed over time through the accumulation of genetic variations.
