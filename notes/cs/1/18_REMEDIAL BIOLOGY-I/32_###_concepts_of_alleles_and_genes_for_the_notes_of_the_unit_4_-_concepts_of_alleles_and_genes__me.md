Concepts of Alleles and Genes:
- Alleles and genes are fundamental concepts in genetics, the study of heredity and variation in organisms.
- A gene is a segment of DNA that contains the instructions for making a specific protein or RNA molecule.
- An allele is a variant form of a gene that can arise due to mutations.
- For example, a gene for eye color may have two alleles, one for brown eyes and one for blue eyes.
- An individual has two copies of each gene, one from each parent, and these copies can be the same or different alleles.
- The combination of alleles that an individual inherits determines its traits, such as eye color, hair color, and height.
- The expression of a trait is determined by the dominant allele, which masks the effect of the recessive allele.
- Alleles and genes play a crucial role in the variation and evolution of populations, as changes in the DNA sequence can lead to new traits and adaptations.
