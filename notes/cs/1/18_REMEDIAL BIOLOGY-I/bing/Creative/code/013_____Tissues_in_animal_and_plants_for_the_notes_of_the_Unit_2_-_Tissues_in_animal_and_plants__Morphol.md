### Tissues in animal and plants

- Tissues are groups of similar cells that work together to perform a specific function.
- There are four main types of animal tissues: epithelial, connective, muscle, and nervous.
- There are three main types of plant tissues: epidermis, ground tissue, and vascular tissue.
- Some examples of animal and plant tissues are:

| Tissue | Present in animal or plant | Function |
|--------|---------------------------|----------|
| Blood (connective tissue) | Animal | Transports oxygen, dissolved food, carbon dioxide, and waste products |
| Cartilage (connective tissue) | Animal | Acts as a cushioning material between bones and joints |
| Muscle | Animal | Can contract to allow movement of body parts |
| Nerve | Animal | Allows rapid communication between different parts of the body |
| Epithelium | Animal | Covers the surface of the body and lines the internal organs |
| Bone (connective tissue) | Animal | Provides support and protection for the body and stores minerals |
| Skin (epithelial tissue) | Animal | Protects the body from infection, dehydration, and injury |
| Epidermis | Plant | Forms the outer layer of the plant and protects it from water loss and pathogens |
| Ground tissue | Plant | Fills the space between the epidermis and the vascular tissue and performs various functions such as photosynthesis, storage, and support |
| Vascular tissue | Plant | Transports water and minerals from the roots to the leaves and sugars from the leaves to the rest of the plant |
| Xylem (vascular tissue) | Plant | Conducts water and minerals upward from the roots |
| Phloem (vascular tissue) | Plant | Conducts sugars and other organic substances downward from the leaves |
| Parenchyma (ground tissue) | Plant | Performs photosynthesis, storage, and secretion |
| Collenchyma (ground tissue) | Plant | Provides flexible support for the growing parts of the plant |
| Sclerenchyma (ground tissue) | Plant | Provides rigid support and strength for the mature parts of the plant |
| Fruit (ground tissue) | Plant | Develops from the ovary of a flower and contains seeds |
