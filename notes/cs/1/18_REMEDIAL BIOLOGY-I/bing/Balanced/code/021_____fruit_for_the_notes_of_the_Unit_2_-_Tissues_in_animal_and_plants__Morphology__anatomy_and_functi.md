### Fruit

- A fruit is the **soft, pulpy part of a flowering plant** that contains seeds .
- It is formed from the **ovaries of angiosperms** (plants that produce flowers and seeds enclosed in a carpel) and is exclusive only to this group of plants .
- Fruits are a characteristic of flowering plants. As soon as **pollen and fertilization** occur, the ovary of a plant becomes a fruit and the ovules become a seed.
- Fruits are classified into four types namely: **simple, aggregate, multiple, and accessory**.
  - Simple fruits are derived from a single ovary of a single flower, such as apples, cherries, and tomatoes.
  - Aggregate fruits are formed from several ovaries of a single flower, such as raspberries, blackberries, and strawberries.
  - Multiple fruits are developed from the fusion of several flowers and their ovaries, such as pineapples, figs, and mulberries.
  - Accessory fruits are fruits that include other floral parts besides the ovary, such as pears, peaches, and oranges.
- Fruits have various **functions** in the life cycle of plants, such as:
  - Protecting the seeds from desiccation, predation, and infection.
  - Dispersing the seeds to new locations by attracting animals, wind, or water.
  - Providing food and nutrients for humans and other animals .
  - Contributing to the reproduction and evolution of plants by facilitating cross-pollination and genetic variation.
