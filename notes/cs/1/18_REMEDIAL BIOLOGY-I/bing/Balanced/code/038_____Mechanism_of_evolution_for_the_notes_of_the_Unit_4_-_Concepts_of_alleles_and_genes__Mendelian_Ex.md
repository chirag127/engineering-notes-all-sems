### Mechanism of evolution

- Evolution is the process by which modern organisms have descended from ancient ancestors.
- Evolution is responsible for both the remarkable similarities and the amazing diversity of life.
- Evolution occurs when the frequencies of traits (and the genes that control them) in a population change over generations.
- There are five main mechanisms of evolution: natural selection, sexual selection, artificial selection, genetic drift, and gene flow.
- Natural selection is the process by which individuals with certain traits have higher survival and reproduction than others in a given environment .
- Sexual selection is the process by which individuals with certain traits have higher mating success than others in a given population.
- Artificial selection is the process by which humans intentionally breed individuals with certain traits for a desired outcome.
- Genetic drift is the process by which random events cause changes in the frequencies of traits (and the genes that control them) in a population, especially in small populations .
- Gene flow is the process by which individuals (and their genes) move between populations, increasing genetic variation and reducing genetic differences .
- These mechanisms of evolution can act together or separately to produce evolutionary change.
