### Basic chemical constituents of living body

- Living beings are composed of various chemical elements and compounds that are essential for their structure, function, and regulation.
- The most abundant elements in living beings are oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, and nitrogen, which together make up about 96% of the human body mass .
- Oxygen is the most abundant element in humans and animals, and it is involved in many biochemical reactions, such as cellular respiration, oxidation, and hydrolysis .
- Carbon is the basis of all organic molecules, such as carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, and nucleic acids, which are the building blocks of life .
- Hydrogen is present in every biomolecule, and it forms bonds with other atoms, such as oxygen and nitrogen, to create water, amino acids, and nucleotides .
- Nitrogen is a component of amino acids, nucleotides, and many other biomolecules, and it is essential for the synthesis of proteins and nucleic acids .
- Other elements that are important for living beings are calcium, phosphorus, potassium, sulfur, sodium, chlorine, and magnesium, which together make up about 3.85% of the human body mass .
- Calcium and phosphorus are the main constituents of bones and teeth, and they also play roles in muscle contraction, nerve transmission, and blood clotting.
- Potassium, sodium, and chlorine are involved in maintaining the fluid balance and the electrical potential of cells, and they also participate in nerve impulses and muscle contractions.
- Sulfur is a component of some amino acids, such as cysteine and methionine, and it is involved in the formation of disulfide bonds that stabilize the structure of proteins .
- Magnesium is a cofactor for many enzymes, and it is also important for the synthesis of nucleic acids and the function of chlorophyll in plants.
- In addition to these elements, there are also trace elements, such as iron, copper, zinc, iodine, selenium, and manganese, that are present in very small amounts in living beings, but are essential for the function of some enzymes, hormones, and other biomolecules .
- The chemical composition of living beings can vary depending on the species, the environment, the diet, and the physiological state, but the basic elements and compounds are common to all forms of life.
