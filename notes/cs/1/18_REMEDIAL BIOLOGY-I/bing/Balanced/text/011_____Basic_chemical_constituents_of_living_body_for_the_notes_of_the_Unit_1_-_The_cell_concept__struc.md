### Basic chemical constituents of living body

- The living body is composed of various chemical elements and compounds that are essential for its structure and function.
- The most abundant elements in the living body are oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, calcium, and phosphorus, which make up about 99% of the body mass .
- These elements are found in different types of organic molecules, such as carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, and nucleic acids, which are the building blocks of life .
- Carbohydrates are the main source of energy for the living body and are composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen atoms. They include simple sugars, such as glucose and fructose, and complex polysaccharides, such as starch and cellulose.
- Lipids are hydrophobic molecules that store energy and form the cell membranes. They are composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen atoms, and sometimes phosphorus and nitrogen atoms. They include fats, oils, waxes, steroids, and phospholipids.
- Proteins are the most diverse and functional molecules in the living body. They are composed of carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, and sulfur atoms, and sometimes other elements, such as iron and zinc. They are made of amino acids, which are linked by peptide bonds. They perform various roles, such as catalyzing reactions, transporting substances, providing structure, and regulating processes.
- Nucleic acids are the genetic material of the living body. They are composed of carbon, hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, and phosphorus atoms. They are made of nucleotides, which are linked by phosphodiester bonds. They store and transmit information, such as DNA and RNA.
- Water is the most abundant compound in the living body. It makes up about 60% of the body mass and is found in both the intracellular and extracellular fluids. It is composed of hydrogen and oxygen atoms, and has unique properties, such as high heat capacity, high surface tension, and polarity. It is essential for maintaining homeostasis, dissolving substances, facilitating reactions, and regulating temperature.
- Other elements and compounds that are found in trace amounts in the living body are called micronutrients. They include potassium, sodium, chlorine, magnesium, iron, iodine, copper, zinc, selenium, and fluorine. They are involved in various functions, such as maintaining fluid balance, transmitting nerve impulses, carrying oxygen, forming enzymes, and preventing diseases  .
