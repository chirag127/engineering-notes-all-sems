### The cell concept

- The cell concept is the idea that **the cell is the fundamental unit of life**  and that all living things are composed of one or more cells.
- The cell concept also implies that **cells arise from pre-existing cells** by a process of cell division, and that **cells carry genetic information** that is passed on to their offspring.
- The cell concept is one of the foundations of modern biology, and it helps us understand the structure, function, diversity and evolution of living organisms .

### Structure of prokaryotic, eukaryotic, plant and animal cells

- Prokaryotic cells are cells that **lack a nucleus** and other membrane-bound organelles, such as bacteria and archaea .
- Prokaryotic cells have a **simple structure** that consists of a **cell membrane**, a **cell wall**, a **cytoplasm**, and **genetic material** (DNA or RNA) that is often circular and located in a region called the **nucleoid** .
- Prokaryotic cells may also have **external structures**, such as **flagella**, **pili**, or **capsules**, that help them move, attach, or protect themselves .
- Eukaryotic cells are cells that **have a nucleus** and other membrane-bound organelles, such as protists, fungi, plants and animals .
- Eukaryotic cells have a **complex structure** that consists of a **cell membrane**, a **cytoplasm**, and a **nucleus** that contains the **genetic material** (DNA) in the form of linear chromosomes .
- Eukaryotic cells also have various **organelles**, such as **mitochondria**, **endoplasmic reticulum**, **Golgi apparatus**, **lysosomes**, **peroxisomes**, **ribosomes**, **centrioles**, and **cytoskeleton**, that perform different functions, such as energy production, protein synthesis, transport, digestion, detoxification, and movement .
- Plant cells are a type of eukaryotic cells that **have some additional features** that distinguish them from animal cells .
- Plant cells have a **cell wall** that provides rigidity and support, a **large central vacuole** that stores water and other substances, and **plastids**, such as **chloroplasts**, that are involved in photosynthesis and other metabolic processes .
- Animal cells are a type of eukaryotic cells that **lack some features** that plant cells have, such as a cell wall, a central vacuole, and plastids .
- Animal cells have **smaller vacuoles** that are used for storage and transport, and **centrioles** that are involved in cell division and organization of the cytoskeleton .

### Structure and function of cell membrane

- The cell membrane is a **thin, flexible, and selectively permeable** layer that surrounds the cytoplasm of a cell and separates it from the external environment .
- The cell membrane is composed of a **phospholipid bilayer** with embedded **proteins**, **carbohydrates**, and **cholesterol** molecules .
- The phospholipid bilayer consists of two layers of phospholipids, which are molecules that have a **hydrophilic** (water-loving) head and a **hydrophobic** (water-fearing) tail .
- The hydrophilic heads face the aqueous environments inside and outside the cell, while the hydrophobic tails face each other in the interior of the membrane .
- The proteins in the cell membrane can be **integral** or **peripheral** .
- Integral proteins span the entire membrane and are involved in **transport**, **signaling**, or **attachment** of the cell .
- Peripheral proteins are attached to the surface of the membrane
