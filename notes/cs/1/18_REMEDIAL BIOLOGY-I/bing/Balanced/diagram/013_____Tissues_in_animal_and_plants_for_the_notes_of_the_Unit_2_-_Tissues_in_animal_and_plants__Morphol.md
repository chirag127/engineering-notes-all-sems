### Tissues in animal and plants

- Tissues are groups of similar cells that work together to perform a specific function.
- There are four main types of animal tissues: epithelial, connective, muscle, and nervous.
- There are three main types of plant tissues: epidermis, ground tissue, and vascular tissue.

#### Epithelial tissue

- Covers the body surface and lines the body cavities, organs, and glands.
- Provides protection, secretion, absorption, and sensation.
- Examples: skin, lining of mouth, stomach, and intestines.

#### Connective tissue

- Supports, binds, and protects other tissues and organs.
- Consists of cells, fibers, and ground substance (matrix).
- Examples: blood, bone, cartilage, adipose (fat), and tendons.

#### Muscle tissue

- Can contract to allow movement of body parts or substances.
- Consists of cells with contractile proteins (actin and myosin).
- Examples: skeletal muscle, cardiac muscle, and smooth muscle.

#### Nervous tissue

- Allows rapid communication between different parts of the body.
- Consists of neurons (nerve cells) and glial cells (support cells).
- Examples: brain, spinal cord, and nerves.

#### Epidermis

- The outermost layer of plant tissue that covers and protects the plant body.
- Consists of a single layer of cells, often with a waxy cuticle to prevent water loss.
- Examples: leaf epidermis, root epidermis, stem epidermis.

#### Ground tissue

- The bulk of the plant tissue that fills the space between the epidermis and the vascular tissue.
- Performs various functions such as photosynthesis, storage, and support.
- Examples: parenchyma, collenchyma, and sclerenchyma.

#### Vascular tissue

- The plant tissue that transports water and minerals from the roots to the leaves, and organic substances from the leaves to the rest of the plant.
- Consists of two types of conducting cells: xylem and phloem.
- Examples: xylem vessels, phloem tubes, and vascular bundles.
