### Fruit

- A fruit is a mature ovary of a flowering plant, usually containing seeds.
- Fruits are the means by which flowering plants disseminate their seeds.
- Fruits can be classified into three types based on their development: simple, aggregate, and multiple.
- Simple fruits develop from a single ovary of a single flower.
- Aggregate fruits develop from multiple ovaries of a single flower.
- Multiple fruits develop from the ovaries of multiple flowers.
- Fruits can also be classified based on their texture: fleshy or dry.
- Fleshy fruits have a soft, juicy pericarp at maturity, while dry fruits have a hard or papery pericarp.
- Some common examples of fleshy fruits include berries, drupes, and pomes.
- Some common examples of dry fruits include nuts, legumes, and capsules.
- The formation of fruit is triggered by the fertilization of the ovules within the ovary.
- After fertilization, the ovary begins to grow and develop into a fruit, while the other parts of the flower wither and fall off.
- The seeds within the fruit are dispersed by various means, including wind, water, and animals.
- Fruits play an important role in the reproduction and dispersal of flowering plants.
- Fruits also provide a source of food for animals, including humans, and play an important role in many ecosystems.
