### Classification of living organisms

Classification of living organisms is the scientific process of arranging living organisms into different groups and subgroups based on their similarities and differences. This process is also known as Biological classification.

- The system of classification was developed by Carl Linnaeus in the eighteenth century.
- Organisms are commonly named by the binomial system of genus and species.
- The classification of species allows the subdivision of living organisms into smaller and more specialized groups.
- The five kingdoms of living organisms are: animals, plants, fungi, protists, and prokaryotes.
