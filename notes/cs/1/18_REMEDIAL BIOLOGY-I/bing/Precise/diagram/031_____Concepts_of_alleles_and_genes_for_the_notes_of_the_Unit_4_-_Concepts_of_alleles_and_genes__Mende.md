### Concepts of Alleles and Genes

1. **Alleles** are different versions of the same gene that arise through mutation. They are responsible for the variation in traits among individuals of a species.
2. A **gene** is a unit of heredity that is transferred from a parent to offspring and is held to determine some characteristic of the offspring.
3. Genes are located on chromosomes and are composed of DNA.
4. Each individual has two alleles for a given gene, one inherited from each parent.
5. The combination of alleles determines the expression of the trait.
6. Alleles can be dominant or recessive. A dominant allele will always be expressed, while a recessive allele will only be expressed if the individual has two copies of the recessive allele.
7. The expression of a trait can also be influenced by the environment and interactions between multiple genes.
