## Unit 2 - Tissues in animal and plants, Morphology, anatomy and functions of different parts of plants: Root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed.

### Tissues in Animals and Plants
- Tissues are groups of cells that work together to perform a specific function.
- In animals, there are four main types of tissues: epithelial, connective, muscle, and nervous.
- In plants, there are three main types of tissues: dermal, ground, and vascular.

### Morphology, Anatomy and Functions of Different Parts of Plants
#### Root
- The root anchors the plant in the ground and absorbs water and nutrients from the soil.
- The root system can be divided into two main types: taproot and fibrous root.
- The taproot is a single, large root that grows deep into the soil, while fibrous roots are a network of smaller roots that spread out near the surface of the soil.

#### Stem
- The stem supports the plant and transports water, nutrients, and sugars between the roots and the leaves.
- The stem can be divided into two main types: herbaceous and woody.
- Herbaceous stems are soft and green, while woody stems are hard and brown.

#### Leaf
- The leaf is the main organ of photosynthesis, where the plant converts sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen.
- Leaves can have different shapes, sizes, and arrangements on the stem.
- The main parts of a leaf are the blade, petiole, and stipules.

#### Inflorescence
- The inflorescence is the arrangement of flowers on a stem.
- There are many different types of inflorescences, including spikes, racemes, panicles, and umbels.
- The type of inflorescence can affect the way the flowers are pollinated and how the seeds are dispersed.

#### Flower
- The flower is the reproductive organ of the plant.
- Flowers can have male and/or female reproductive organs.
- The main parts of a flower are the sepals, petals, stamens, and carpels.

#### Fruit
- The fruit is the mature ovary of a flower.
- Fruits can be fleshy or dry, and can have different shapes, sizes, and colors.
- The main function of the fruit is to protect and disperse the seeds.

#### Seed
- The seed is the mature ovule of a flower.
- Seeds can have different shapes, sizes, and colors.
- The main function of the seed is to grow into a new plant.
