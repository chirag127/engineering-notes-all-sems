Structure of Prokaryotic Cells:
- Prokaryotic cells are the simplest type of cells and are found in single-celled organisms such as bacteria.
- Prokaryotic cells are smaller and simpler in structure compared to eukaryotic cells.
- They lack a nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles.
- The genetic material in prokaryotic cells is a single circular chromosome located in the cytoplasm.
- Prokaryotic cells have a cell wall that provides structure and protection.
- They also have a plasma membrane, which regulates the exchange of materials between the cell and its environment.
- Some prokaryotic cells have a capsule, a slime layer, or pili, which help with adhesion and protection.
- Prokaryotic cells also have ribosomes, which are involved in protein synthesis.
- They may also have flagella, which are used for motility.
- Prokaryotic cells are adapted to a wide range of environments and play important roles in many ecosystems.
