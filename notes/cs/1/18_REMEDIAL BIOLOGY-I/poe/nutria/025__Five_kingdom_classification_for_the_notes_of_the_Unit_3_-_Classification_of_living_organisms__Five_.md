
### Five Kingdom Classification

* Classification of living organisms is based on five kingdom classification. 
* The five kingdoms are: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia. 
* Each kingdom is divided into several phyla, classes, orders, families, genera and species. 
* The major groups of organisms in each kingdom are as follows: 
  * Monera: Bacteria and Cyanobacteria 
  * Protista: Algae, Protozoa and Slime Moulds 
  * Fungi: Mushrooms, Moulds, Yeasts and Lichens 
  * Plantae: Mosses, Ferns, Conifers and Flowering Plants 
  * Animalia: Sponges, Coelenterata, Flatworms, Roundworms, Segmented Worms, Arthropods, Molluscs, Echinoderms and Chordates 
* The principles of classification in each kingdom are based on the characteristics of the organisms. 
* Systematic and binomial system of nomenclature is used to classify organisms. 
* Systematic nomenclature is based on the taxonomic hierarchy of organisms. 
* Binomial nomenclature is the scientific naming system used to identify and name organisms. 
* The two parts of a scientific name are the genus and species.
