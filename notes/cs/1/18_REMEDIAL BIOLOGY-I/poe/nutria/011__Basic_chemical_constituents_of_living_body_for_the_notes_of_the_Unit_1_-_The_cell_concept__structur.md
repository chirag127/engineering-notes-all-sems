
### Basic Chemical Constituents of Living Body

* Water: Water is the most abundant molecule in living organisms, making up to 70-95% of the total mass of cells. It serves as a medium for biochemical reactions and helps to regulate the temperature of the body.
* Carbohydrates: Carbohydrates are the main source of energy for living organisms. They are composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen atoms and are classified as monosaccharides, disaccharides, and polysaccharides.
* Lipids: Lipids are a group of molecules that include fats, oils, waxes, and steroids. They are composed of carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen atoms and are important for energy storage, insulation, and cell membrane structure.
* Proteins: Proteins are large molecules composed of amino acids. They are important for the structure and function of cells and tissues, and they are involved in many metabolic processes.
* Nucleic Acids: Nucleic acids are molecules that contain genetic information. They are composed of nucleotides, which are made up of nitrogenous bases, sugars, and phosphate groups.
* Vitamins and Minerals: Vitamins and minerals are essential for the proper functioning of the body. They are required for the synthesis of enzymes, hormones, and other molecules, and they are important for growth and development.
