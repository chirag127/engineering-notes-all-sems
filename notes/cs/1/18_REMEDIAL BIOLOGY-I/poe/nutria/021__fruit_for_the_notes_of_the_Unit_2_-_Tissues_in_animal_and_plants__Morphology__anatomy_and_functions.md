
### Fruit

Fruit is the mature ovary of a flowering plant, containing the seeds of the plant. It is often edible, sweet and juicy, and is the part of the plant that is most often consumed. 

Fruits are formed from the ovary of the flower, and can be either simple (a single ovary) or compound (multiple ovaries). The seeds of the fruit are often contained within the fruit, and can be dispersed by animals, wind, or water. 

Fruits are an important part of the diet of many animals, including humans, and the nutrients contained in fruits are essential for health. Fruits are also used in many culinary dishes, and can be used to make jams, jellies, and other foods. 

Fruits are also important for the propagation of plants, as the seeds contained within them can be spread to new locations, allowing plants to colonize new areas.
