
### Concepts of Alleles and Genes

* Alleles are alternative forms of a gene, located at the same locus on homologous chromosomes. 
* Genes are sections of DNA that code for specific traits. 
* Mendelian experiments have been used to study the inheritance of traits.

### Cell Cycle (Elementary Idea)

* The cell cycle is the process by which a cell grows and divides. 
* It consists of two distinct phases: interphase and mitosis. 
* During interphase, the cell grows and prepares for division. 
* During mitosis, the cell divides into two daughter cells.

### Mitosis and Meiosis

* Mitosis is the process by which a cell divides into two daughter cells that are genetically identical to the parent cell. 
* Meiosis is a type of cell division that produces four daughter cells with half the number of chromosomes as the parent cell.

### Techniques to Study Mitosis and Meiosis

* Microscopy is the most common technique used to study mitosis and meiosis. 
* Other techniques include flow cytometry, fluorescent in situ hybridization (FISH), and immunofluorescence.

### Origin of Life

* The origin of life is a mystery that has puzzled scientists for centuries. 
* It is widely accepted that life originated in the oceans, but the exact mechanism remains unknown. 
* Several theories have been proposed, including the primordial soup theory and the RNA world hypothesis.

### Evidences for Biological Evolution

* Biological evolution is the process by which populations of organisms change over time. 
* Evidence for biological evolution includes the fossil record, DNA analysis, and the observation of natural selection.

### Mechanism of Evolution − Variation (Mutation and Recombination)

* Variation is the basis for evolution. 
* Variation can be caused by mutations and recombination. 
* Mutations are changes in the DNA sequence of an organism. 
* Recombination is the exchange of genetic material between two chromosomes.

### Natural Selection with Examples

* Natural selection is the process by which certain traits become more or less common in a population over time. 
* Examples of natural selection include the development of antibiotic resistance in bacteria and the development of camouflage in animals.

### Types of Natural Selection

* There are four main types of natural selection: directional, stabilizing, disruptive, and sexual selection. 
* Directional selection occurs when a trait is favored over all other traits. 
* Stabilizing selection occurs when a trait is favored over extreme traits. 
* Disruptive selection occurs when extreme traits are favored over intermediate traits. 
* Sexual selection occurs when traits are favored by members of the opposite sex.
