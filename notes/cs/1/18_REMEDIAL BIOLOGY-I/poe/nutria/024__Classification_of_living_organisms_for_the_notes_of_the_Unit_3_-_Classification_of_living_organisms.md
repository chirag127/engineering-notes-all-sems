
### Classification of Living Organisms

* Five kingdom classification is a system of classifying living organisms into five distinct kingdoms: Animalia, Plantae, Fungi, Protista, and Monera. 
* Major groups within each kingdom are determined by shared characteristics. 
* Principles of classification in each kingdom are based on similarities and differences in the characteristics of organisms. 
* Systematic and binomial system of nomenclature is used to classify and name organisms. 
* The binomial system assigns a two-part scientific name to each organism, consisting of the genus and species.
* The concept of animal and plant classification is based on the characteristics of organisms and their evolutionary relationships. 
* In the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS, the classification of living organisms is studied in detail.
