
### Animal Cells 

* Animal cells are eukaryotic cells, which means they contain membrane-bound organelles and a nucleus. 
* They are typically larger than prokaryotic cells and have a more complex structure. 
* The cell membrane of an animal cell is a selectively permeable membrane that regulates the movement of substances into and out of the cell. 
* The cell organelles of an animal cell include the nucleus, mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi apparatus, lysosomes, and peroxisomes. 
* Each of these organelles has a specific role in the cell, such as the nucleus which stores genetic material, the mitochondria which produces energy, and the endoplasmic reticulum which synthesizes proteins. 
* Cell signaling is the process by which cells communicate with each other. This communication is essential for the normal functioning of the body. 
* A compound microscope is a scientific instrument used to magnify small objects. It is composed of two lenses, an objective lens and an eyepiece. 
* The basic chemical constituents of living bodies include carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, nucleic acids, and water. These molecules are essential for the functioning of all living organisms.
