
### Tissues in Animal and Plants

* Animals and plants are composed of tissues, which are groups of cells that work together to perform a specific function. 
* Animal tissues can be classified into four main types: epithelial, connective, muscular, and nervous. 
* Plant tissues can be classified into three main types: dermal, vascular, and ground. 

#### Morphology, Anatomy and Functions of Different Parts of Plants

* Root: The root is the part of the plant that is responsible for anchoring the plant in the soil and absorbing water and minerals. 
* Stem: The stem is the part of the plant responsible for providing support and transporting materials between the leaves and the roots. 
* Leaf: The leaf is the part of the plant responsible for photosynthesis, the process of converting light energy into chemical energy. 
* Inflorescence: An inflorescence is a group of flowers that are arranged on a stem. 
* Flower: A flower is the reproductive organ of the plant, and is composed of four main parts: sepals, petals, stamens, and pistils. 
* Fruit: The fruit is the part of the plant that contains the seeds, and is responsible for the dispersal of the plant's seeds. 
* Seed: The seed is the part of the plant that contains the embryo and is responsible for the reproduction of the plant.
