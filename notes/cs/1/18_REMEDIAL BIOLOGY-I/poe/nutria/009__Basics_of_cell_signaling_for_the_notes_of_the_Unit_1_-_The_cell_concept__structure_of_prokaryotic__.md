

### Basics of Cell Signaling

1. Cell signaling is a process by which cells communicate with each other by exchanging chemical and physical signals.

2. The signals are usually proteins called ligands that bind to receptors on the cell surface.

3. The binding of the ligand to the receptor triggers a series of biochemical reactions inside the cell, which can result in changes in the cell’s activity.

4. Cell signaling is essential for the development and maintenance of multicellular organisms.

5. Cell signaling is also important for the functioning of the immune system, as it allows the body to respond to pathogens and other foreign substances.

6. Cell signaling can also be used to control the growth and development of cells, as well as to regulate the metabolism of the cell.
