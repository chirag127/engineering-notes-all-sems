
### Mechanism of Evolution 

* Alleles and genes are the building blocks of evolution. Alleles are variations of genes that can be passed down from one generation to the next. 
* Mendelian experiments are experiments that study the inheritance of traits from one generation to the next. 
* Cell cycle is the process by which cells divide and replicate. Mitosis is the process of cell division, while meiosis is the process of creating gametes, or reproductive cells. 
* Techniques to study mitosis and meiosis include microscopy, cell staining, and genetic analysis. 
* Origin of life is the study of the emergence of life from non-living matter. Evidence for biological evolution includes fossil records, genetic analysis, and comparative anatomy. 
* Mechanism of evolution is the process by which species evolve over time. Variation is the process of change in an organism over time, and can be caused by mutation or recombination. 
* Natural selection is a process of evolution in which certain traits become more common in a population over time because they give the organism a survival advantage. Examples of natural selection include changes in beak size in birds, fur color in rabbits, and camouflage in insects. 
* Types of natural selection include directional selection, stabilizing selection, and disruptive selection.
