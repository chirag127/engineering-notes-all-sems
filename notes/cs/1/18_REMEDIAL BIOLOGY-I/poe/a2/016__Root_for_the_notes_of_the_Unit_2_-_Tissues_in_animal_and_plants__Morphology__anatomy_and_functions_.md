### Root for the notes of the Unit 2 - Tissues in animal and plants, Morphology, anatomy and functions of different parts of plants: Root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed. in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS.

1. Root:
- Anchor plant to the ground and absorb water and minerals
- Primary root: Taproot system in dicots, adventitious in monocots
- Secondary roots: Branching of primary root, increases absorption surface area
- Root hairs: Extension of epidermal cells, increases surface area for absorption
- Root cap: Protects root tip, senses soil conditions

2. Stem:
- Transport of water, minerals and nutrients
- Support for leaves, flowers and fruits
- Herbaceous: Soft, green and thick e.g. annuals
- Woody: Hard and thick e.g. trees and shrubs
- Modified: Tendrils, thorns, etc.

3. Leaf:
- Photosynthesis
- Transport of water and nutrients
- Stores food
- Blade: Lamina, flattened part; Petiole: Stalk
- Venation: Reticulate in dicots, parallel in monocots
- Modification: Spines, tendrils, storage, etc.
