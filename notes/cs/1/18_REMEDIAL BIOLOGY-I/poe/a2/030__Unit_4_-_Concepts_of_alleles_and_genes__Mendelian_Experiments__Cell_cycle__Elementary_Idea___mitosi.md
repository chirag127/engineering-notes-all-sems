## Unit 4 - Concepts of alleles and genes, Mendelian Experiments, Cell cycle (Elementary Idea), mitosis and meiosis, techniques to study mitosis and meiosis. Origin of life, evidences for biological evolution, Mechanism of evolution −Variation (Mutation and Recombination), Natural Selection with examples, Types of natural selection.

1. Alleles and Genes
- Alleles are alternative forms of a gene occupying the same locus on a chromosome.
- Genes are the functional unit of heredity which control inheritance of traits. They are made up of DNA and are located on chromosomes.

2. Mendelian Experiments
- Gregor Mendel conducted experiments on pea plants and proposed laws of inheritance including Law of Dominance, Law of Segregation and Law of Independent Assortment.
- Monohybrid and Dihybrid crosses to study single and multiple traits inheritance.

3. Cell Cycle and Cell Division
- Cell cycle has four phases: G1, S, G2 and M. In M phase, mitosis occurs which is divided into prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase.
- Mitosis results in two identical daughter cells. Meiosis results in four cells with half the genetic material.
- Techniques to study mitosis and meiosis: Microscopy, staining.

4. Origin of Life and Biological Evolution
- Origin of first life on Earth, abiogenesis theories.
- Evidences for biological evolution: Comparative anatomy, molecular biology, biogeography, fossils, homologous and analogous structures.
- Mechanism of evolution: Mutation, recombination, natural selection, gene flow, genetic drift. Types of natural selection: Directional, stabilizing, disruptive.
