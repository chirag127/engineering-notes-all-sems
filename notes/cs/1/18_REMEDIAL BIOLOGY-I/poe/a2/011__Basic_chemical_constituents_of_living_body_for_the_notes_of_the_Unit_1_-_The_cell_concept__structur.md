### Basic chemical constituents of living body

- Carbon: Present in the form of carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, and nucleic acids. It forms the basic structural framework of all biomolecules.
- Hydrogen: It is essential for the formation of water and also present in carbohydrates, proteins, and lipids. It helps in maintaining the pH of the body.
- Oxygen: It is essential for respiration. It is present in water and all biomolecules. It helps in the release of energy from food.
- Nitrogen: It is present in proteins and nucleic acids. It is essential for growth and reproduction.
- Phosphorus: It is present in nucleic acids and cell membranes. It is important for energy transfer and storage.
- Sulfur: It is present in proteins. It is important for protein structure and function.
- Other elements: Sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium, iron, etc. are required in small quantities for various metabolic activities.

The elements combine to form the basic building blocks or monomers of biomolecules like amino acids, sugars, fatty acids, and nucleotides. These monomers combine to form polymers like proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, and nucleic acids which are essential for the structure, function, and organization of the cell.
