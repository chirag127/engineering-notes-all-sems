### Cell cycle

- Cell cycle is the series of events that take place in a cell leading to its division and replication.
- It involves interphase and mitosis(M phase)
- Interphase has 3 phases: G1 phase(cell growth), S phase(DNA replication) and G2 phase(cell growth)
- Mitosis has 4 phases: Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase and Telophase.
- In mitosis, chromosomes are separated into 2 daughter cells, ensuring equal distribution of genetic material.
- Meiosis is cell division which produces gametes(eggs and sperm) with half the chromosomes as body cells. It has 2 cycles - Meiosis I and Meiosis II.
- Mitosis study techniques: prepared slides of onion root tip, staining techniques(Giemsa), time lapse photography.
- Meiosis study techniques: acetocarmine squash technique, Feulgen reaction.

Origin of life and evolution:
- Origin of first living cell from inorganic matter. Miller-Urey experiment gives evidence.
- Evidence for evolution: Homologous structures, Molecular similarities, Fossil records, Comparative embryology, Biogeography.
- Mechanism of evolution: Variation(Mutation and Recombination), Natural selection(Directional, Stabilizing, Disruptive), Speciation.
