## Unit 2 - Tissues in animal and plants, Morphology, anatomy and functions of different parts of plants: Root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed.

- Plant tissues: Meristematic tissue (responsible for growth), Permanent tissue (responsible for structure and function)
- Animal tissues: Epithelial tissue (lining), Connective tissue (support), Muscle tissue (movement), Nervous tissue (coordination)
- Root: Anchor plant, absorb water and minerals, store food
- Stem: Transport of water and minerals, provide support, produce new leaves/flowers
- Leaf: Photosynthesis, transpiration, gaseous exchange
- Inflorescence: Arrangement of flowers on the floral axis
- Flower: Reproduction, attract pollinators
- Fruit: Contains seeds, dispersal of seeds, protection of seeds
- Seed: Embryo (new plant) surrounded by food storage tissue, dispersal and survival of species
