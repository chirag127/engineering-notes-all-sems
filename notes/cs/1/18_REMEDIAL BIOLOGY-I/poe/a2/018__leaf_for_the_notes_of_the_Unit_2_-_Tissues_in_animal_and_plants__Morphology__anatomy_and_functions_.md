### Leaf

- Leaf is a flattened, lateral outgrowth of the stem in plants that is typically green and photosynthetic.
- Types: Simple leaf, Compound leaf
- Parts: Blade, Petiole, Stipules
- Blade: Lamina, Venation (Reticulate, Parallel)
- Functions: Photosynthesis, Transpiration, Gaseous exchange
- Structure: Epidermis, Mesophyll, Veins
- Modifications: Tendrils, Spines, Scales, Pitchers
