### Major groups for the notes of Unit 3 - Classification of living organisms

1. Five Kingdom Classification
- Monera: Prokaryotic cells (bacteria)
- Protista: Eukaryotic cells (protozoa, algae)
- Fungi: Eukaryotic cells (mushrooms, molds)
- Plantae: Eukaryotic cells (plants)
- Animalia: Eukaryotic cells (animals)

2. Principles of classification
- Cell structure: Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic
- Mode of nutrition: Autotrophic vs Heterotrophic
- Complexity of organization: Unicellular vs Multicellular
- Presence/absence of cell wall
- Presence/absence of chlorophyll

3. Systematic and Binomial system of nomenclature
- Genus and species names in Latin
- Genus starts with capital letter, species in lowercase
- Italicized or underlined when handwritten
- Provides universal naming system for organisms

4. Concept of animal and plant classification
- Based on shared characteristics and evolutionary relationships
- Traditionally focused on morphology but now includes genetics and molecular data
- Classifications change as new data/relationships are discovered
- Aims to reflect natural relationships and classify unknown organisms
