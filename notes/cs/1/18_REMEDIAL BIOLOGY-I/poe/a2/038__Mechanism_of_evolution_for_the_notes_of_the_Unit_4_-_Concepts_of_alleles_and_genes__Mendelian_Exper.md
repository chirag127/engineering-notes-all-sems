**Concepts of alleles and genes:**
- Alleles are alternative forms of a gene that govern the inheritance of a particular trait.
- A gene is a unit of inheritance that is transferred from parents to offspring and determines a particular trait.

**Mendelian Experiments:**
- Gregor Mendel conducted experiments on garden pea plants and concluded the laws of inheritance including the law of segregation and independent assortment.
- Law of Segregation: Pairs of alleles segregate during gamete formation.
- Law of Independent Assortment: Alleles of different genes assort independently during gamete formation.

**Cell cycle, mitosis and meiosis:**
- Cell cycle is the series of events that takes place in a cell leading to its division and duplication. It consists of interphase, mitosis and cytokinesis.
- Mitosis results in the production of two identical daughter cells from a single parent cell. It consists of prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase.
- Meiosis results in the production of four haploid cells from a diploid cell. It consists of meiosis I and meiosis II which further consist of prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase.
- Techniques to study mitosis and meiosis: Microscopy, labeling and staining techniques.

**Origin of life and evidences for biological evolution:**
- Origin of first life on Earth, chemical evolution and primordial soup theory.
- Evidences for biological evolution: Comparative anatomy, comparative embryology, biogeography, palaeontology, molecular biology.

**Mechanism of evolution - Variation and Natural Selection:**
- Variation: Mutation and recombination lead to variations in organisms.
- Natural Selection: The process by which organisms with favourable variations survive and reproduce more than those with unfavourable variations. It results in the adaptation of populations to the environment.
- Types of Natural Selection: Directional, stabilizing and disruptive selection.
