### The cell concept for the notes of the Unit 1 - The cell concept, structure of prokaryotic, eukaryotic cells, plant cells and animal cells

- Cell is the structural and functional unit of life.
- Discovery of cell by Robert Hooke in 1665.
- Cell theory by Schleiden and Schwann in 1838-39.
- All living organisms are made up of cells.
- Cells arise from pre-existing cells.
- Cell is made up of components that work together as a system.

**Prokaryotic cell**
- Bacteria are prokaryotic cells.
- Lack membrane bound organelles like mitochondria, chloroplast, nucleus.
- DNA is organized as a single circular chromosome in the cytoplasm.
- Examples: E. coli, Bacteria, Archaea.

**Eukaryotic cell**
- Plant and animal cells are eukaryotic cells.
- Presence of membrane bound organelles like nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplast, etc.
- DNA is organized into linear chromosomes in the nucleus.
- Examples: Amoeba, Yeast, Plant and animal cells.

**Structure and function of plant cell and animal cell**
- Common components: Nucleus, Mitochondria, Endoplasmic reticulum, Ribosomes, etc.
- Unique components of plant cell: Cell wall, Chloroplast, Vacuole.
- Unique components of animal cell: Centrioles, Lysosomes.
- Functions of cell components: Structural support (cell wall), Energy production (mitochondria), Protein synthesis (ribosomes), waste removal (lysosomes), Storage (vacuole), etc.
