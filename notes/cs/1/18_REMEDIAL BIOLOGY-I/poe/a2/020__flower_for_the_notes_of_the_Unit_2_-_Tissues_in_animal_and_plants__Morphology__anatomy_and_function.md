### Flower

- Flower is the reproductive part of a plant. It contains reproductive organs surrounded by petals and sepals.
- Parts of a flower:
    - Petals: Colorful leaves that attract pollinators like insects or birds.
    - Sepals: Green leaves that enclose a flower when it's in bud form.
    - Reproductive organs: Stamens ( male) and pistil (female).
- Functions:
    - Attract pollinators through scent and color to facilitate reproduction.
    - Protect the reproductive organs.
- Types of flowers:
    - Complete flower: Has all 4 parts - petals, sepals, pistil and stamens. Example: Hibiscus.
    - Incomplete flower: Lacks one or more parts. Example: Corn lacks petals.
    - Bisexual flower: Has both male and female reproductive parts.
    - Unisexual flower: Has either male or female parts. Example: Papaya and cucumber.
