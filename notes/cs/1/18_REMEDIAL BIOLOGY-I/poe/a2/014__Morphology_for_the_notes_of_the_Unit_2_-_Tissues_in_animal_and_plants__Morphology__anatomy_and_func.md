### Morphology for the notes of the Unit 2 - Tissues in animal and plants, Morphology, anatomy and functions of different parts of plants: Root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit and seed. in the subject of REMEDIAL BIOLOGY-I KCS.

- Tissues: Groups of cells that work together to perform a specialized function. Animals have 4 basic tissues - epithelial, connective, muscle and nervous tissues. Plants have meristematic and permanent tissues.
- Root: Anchor plant and absorb water and minerals. Has root cap, root hairs and tap root system/fibrous root system.
- Stem: Transport water and minerals. Has nodes and internodes. 2 types - herbaceous and woody.
- Leaf: Carry out photosynthesis. Has petiole, lamina, veins and stomata. 2 types - simple and compound.
- Inflorescence: Arrangement of flowers on the stem. Types - spike, raceme, cyme, umbel, panicle, etc.
- Flower: Reproductive part of plant. Has sepals, petals, stamens and pistil (carpels).
- Fruit: Mature ovary containing seeds. Can be fleshy/dry, indehiscent/dehiscent.
- Seed: Contains embryo and stored food. Has seed coat, endosperm and embryo.
