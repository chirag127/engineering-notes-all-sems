# KCS

1. Knowledge: The information and skills acquired through experience or education. The theoretical or practical understanding of a subject.

2. Classification: The process of organizing information, data, or items into categories for easier search, retrieval, and use. Classification systems are designed to bring order and consistency to collections of information.

3. Structure: The arrangement of and relations between the parts of something. Clear structure makes information easy to follow and understand. Structuring information involves organizing it in a logical and coherent manner.

The key points to remember about KCS are:

1. Knowledge refers to information and skills gained through learning or experience.
2. Classification is a system used to organize information or items into categories.
3. Structure involves arranging information in a logical and coherent manner to make it easy to understand.

Following a consistent KCS approach ensures information is organized and presented in an accessible way for users. Using structured knowledge and classification systems leads to effective information management and retrieval.
