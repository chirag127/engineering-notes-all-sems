### Animal Cells

- Animal cells are eukaryotic cells surrounded by a plasma membrane.
- The shape of animal cells is irregular and it varies based on the function of the cell.
- The major organelles present in animal cells are:
    - Nucleus: Contains DNA, regulates cell activities
    - Mitochondria: Produces energy for cell activities in the form of ATP
    - Endoplasmic reticulum: Transport of materials within the cell
    - Golgi apparatus: Packaging and transport of proteins
    - Lysosomes: Digestion of materials within the cell
    - Centrosomes: Helps in cell division
    - Cytoskeleton: Provides shape and structure to the cell and helps in movement
- The plasma membrane controls the movement of materials in and out of the cell and is made up of a lipid bilayer with embedded proteins.
- Cell signaling involves the transmission of molecular signals between cells to regulate cell activities. Signals are received and transmitted via proteins in the plasma membrane.
- A compound microscope is used to observe the cellular structure and organelles. It magnifies the image of the specimen using glass lenses.
- The major chemical constituents essential for the body functions are carbohydrates, proteins, lipids, vitamins, minerals, fiber, and water.
