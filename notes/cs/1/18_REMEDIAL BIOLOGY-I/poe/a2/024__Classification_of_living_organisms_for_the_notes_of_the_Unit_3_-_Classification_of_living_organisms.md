### Classification of living organisms

- Five kingdom classification: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, Animalia
- Major groups and principles of classification in each kingdom:
- Monera: Prokaryotic unicellular organisms, classified based on cell structure, nutrition mode, etc. Example: Bacteria
- Protista: Eukaryotic unicellular organisms, classified based on mode of locomotion, nutrition, etc. Example: Amoeba, Plasmodium
- Fungi: Eukaryotic heterotrophic organisms, classified based on mode of nutrition, reproduction, etc. Example: Mushrooms, Molds
- Plantae: Eukaryotic autotrophic organisms, classified based on presence of chloroplast, cell wall components, etc. Example: Algae, Bryophytes, Pteridophytes, Gymnosperms, Angiosperms
- Animalia: Eukaryotic heterotrophic organisms, classified based on presence of organs, body symmetry, coelom, segmentation, etc. Example: Porifera, Cnidaria, Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, Echinodermata, Chordata
- Binomial system of nomenclature: Genus followed by species name in Latin
- Concept of animal and plant classification: Based on shared characteristics and evolutionary relationships
