Seed:
- A seed is a reproductive structure produced by flowering plants (angiosperms) that contains a embryo plant and stored food.
- The seed provides the necessary resources for the embryo to germinate and grow into a new plant.
- The outer layer of the seed is called the seed coat and protects the embryo and stored food.
- The embryo is composed of a shoot and root and will develop into a new plant when the seed germinates.
- The stored food in the seed provides energy for the growing embryo until it can produce its own food through photosynthesis.
- Seeds can be dispersed by wind, water, animals, or other means to help ensure that the new plant has a suitable location to grow.
- Some seeds require specific conditions, such as light or temperature, to germinate and grow.
- Seeds are an important part of the life cycle of flowering plants and play a crucial role in the survival and reproduction of the species.
