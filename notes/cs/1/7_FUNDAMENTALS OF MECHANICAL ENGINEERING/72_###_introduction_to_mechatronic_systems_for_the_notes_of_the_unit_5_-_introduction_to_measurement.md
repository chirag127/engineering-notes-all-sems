### Introduction to Mechatronic Systems for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Mechatronic systems are complex systems that integrate mechanical, electrical, and computer systems for the design and control of devices and machines. Mechatronic systems are used in a wide range of applications, including manufacturing, transportation, and consumer products. Unit 5 of Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the introduction to mechatronic systems, including the components of mechatronic systems, the design and control of mechatronic systems, and the application of mechatronics in real-world systems. Understanding the introduction to mechatronic systems is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a foundation for understanding the principles of mechatronics and the design and control of complex systems.
