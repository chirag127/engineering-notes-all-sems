### transmission and power devices for the notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles:

In Unit 2 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, you will learn about the transmission and power devices used in internal combustion (IC) engines and electric vehicles.

1. Transmission Devices: The transmission of a vehicle is responsible for transmitting power from the engine to the wheels. The most common transmission devices used in vehicles are manual transmissions and automatic transmissions.

2. Manual Transmissions: Manual transmissions use a clutch to engage and disengage the engine from the transmission. The driver must manually shift gears to change the speed and torque of the vehicle.

3. Automatic Transmissions: Automatic transmissions use hydraulic and electronic controls to automatically shift gears based on the speed and load of the vehicle.

4. Power Devices in IC Engines: The power devices in an internal combustion engine convert the energy stored in fuel into mechanical energy that can be used to power the vehicle. The most common power devices in IC engines are spark-ignition (SI) engines and compression-ignition (CI) engines.

5. Spark-Ignition (SI) Engines: SI engines use a spark plug to ignite the fuel-air mixture in the engine. They are commonly used in passenger cars, light trucks, and motorcycles.

6. Compression-Ignition (CI) Engines: CI engines use high compression to ignite the fuel-air mixture in the engine. They are commonly used in heavy-duty vehicles, such as trucks and buses.

7. Power Devices in Electric Vehicles: The power devices in an electric vehicle convert electrical energy stored in batteries into mechanical energy that can be used to power the vehicle. The most common power devices in electric vehicles are electric motors.

8. Electric Motors: Electric motors are the main power devices in electric vehicles. They use magnetic fields and electrical currents to generate rotational motion that can be used to power the vehicle.

In conclusion, in Unit 2 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, you will learn about the transmission and power devices used in internal combustion (IC) engines and electric vehicles. The most common transmission devices used in vehicles are manual transmissions and automatic transmissions. The power devices in IC engines are spark-ignition (SI) engines and compression-ignition (CI) engines. The power devices in electric vehicles are electric motors.
