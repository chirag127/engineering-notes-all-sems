## Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles is a topic in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering that deals with the study of internal combustion engines and electric vehicles.

Internal combustion engines (IC engines) are heat engines that convert the energy stored in fuel into mechanical energy. They are widely used in vehicles, power plants, and other mechanical systems.

Electric vehicles (EVs) are vehicles that are powered by electricity from batteries or other energy storage devices. They are becoming increasingly popular as an alternative to traditional gasoline-powered vehicles due to their lower emissions and improved fuel efficiency.

In this unit, students learn about the basic principles of IC engines and EVs, including their design, operation, and performance. They also learn about the different types of IC engines and EVs, including spark-ignition engines, diesel engines, and hybrid electric vehicles, and their applications in various industries.

The study of IC engines and EVs is an important part of mechanical engineering, as these technologies play a crucial role in the design and operation of vehicles and mechanical systems. Understanding the principles and applications of IC engines and EVs is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in mechanical engineering, as they will be involved in the design, development, and implementation of these technologies.
