### Normal and shear Stress for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Unit 1 - Normal and Shear Stress:

Mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the study of forces and their effect on matter. In Unit 1 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, it is important to understand the concept of normal and shear stress.

1. Normal Stress: Normal stress is a type of stress that acts perpendicular to a surface and is caused by a force that is applied perpendicular to the surface. Normal stress is also known as axial stress.

2. Shear Stress: Shear stress is a type of stress that acts parallel to a surface and is caused by a force that is applied parallel to the surface. Shear stress is also known as tangential stress.

3. Calculation of Normal Stress: Normal stress can be calculated using the formula:
   normal stress = force/area

4. Calculation of Shear Stress: Shear stress can be calculated using the formula:
   shear stress = force/area

5. Applications of Normal and Shear Stress: Normal and shear stress play a crucial role in the design and analysis of mechanical components and structures. Normal stress is used to determine the strength of materials under compression, while shear stress is used to determine the strength of materials under shear.
