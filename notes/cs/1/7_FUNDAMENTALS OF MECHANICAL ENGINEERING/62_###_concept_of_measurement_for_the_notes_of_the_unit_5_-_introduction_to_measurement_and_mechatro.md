### Concept of Measurement for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

The concept of measurement is the process of determining the size, amount, or degree of a physical quantity. In the context of mechanical engineering, measurement is used to determine the physical characteristics of a system or to monitor its performance. Unit 5 of Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the concept of measurement, including the types of measurements, the accuracy and precision of measurements, and the use of different types of measuring instruments. Understanding the concept of measurement is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the design and operation of mechanical systems and the principles of mechatronics.
