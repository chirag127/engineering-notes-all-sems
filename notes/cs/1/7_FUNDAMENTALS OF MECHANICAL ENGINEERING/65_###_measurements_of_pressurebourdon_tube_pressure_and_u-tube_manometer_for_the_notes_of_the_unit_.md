### measurements of pressure(Bourdon Tube Pressure and U-Tube Manometer) for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Pressure measurement is an important aspect of mechanical engineering and is used in a wide range of applications, including fluid systems, thermodynamics, and mechatronics. In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics, two common methods of pressure measurement are studied in detail: the Bourdon Tube Pressure Gauge and the U-Tube Manometer.

1. Bourdon Tube Pressure Gauge: This is a type of mechanical pressure gauge that uses a curved metal tube to measure pressure. The tube is connected to the system being measured, and changes in pressure cause the tube to change shape. This change in shape is used to indicate the pressure on a dial.

2. U-Tube Manometer: This is a type of fluid pressure gauge that uses a U-shaped tube filled with a liquid, such as water or oil, to measure pressure. The pressure being measured pushes against the liquid in the tube, causing the liquid to rise or fall. The change in height of the liquid is used to indicate the pressure on a scale.

Understanding these methods of pressure measurement is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as they form the basis for many advanced topics in fluid mechanics and mechatronics. These methods are used in a wide range of applications, including automotive engineering, aerospace engineering, and energy systems.
