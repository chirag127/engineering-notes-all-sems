### pressure for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Pressure is a scalar quantity that describes the force per unit area exerted by a fluid. In fluid mechanics, pressure is a fundamental concept that is used to describe the behavior of fluids and to calculate the forces and energy in fluid systems. Unit 4 of Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the concept of pressure, including the definition and calculation of pressure, the behavior of fluids under pressure, and the use of pressure in the analysis of fluid systems. Understanding pressure is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the behavior of fluids and the principles of thermodynamics.
