### construction and working of window air conditioner for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

A window air conditioner is a type of air conditioning unit that is installed in a window or through a wall. It consists of a refrigeration system, a fan, and a set of controls. The refrigeration system cools the air by removing heat from the air and transferring it to the outside of the building. The fan circulates the cooled air throughout the room. The controls allow the user to adjust the temperature and fan speed. Unit 3 of Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the construction and working of window air conditioners, including the components of the refrigeration system, the operation of the fan, and the use of controls. Understanding the construction and working of window air conditioners is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the principles of refrigeration and air conditioning and the design of these systems.
