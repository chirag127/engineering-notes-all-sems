### elastic constants and their relationship for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Elastic Constants:

Unit 1 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers Introduction to Mechanics, including the concept of elastic constants. Elastic constants are numerical values that describe the relationship between stress and strain in a material.

1. Types of Elastic Constants: There are several different types of elastic constants, including Young's modulus, shear modulus, and bulk modulus.

2. Young's Modulus: Young's modulus is a measure of the stiffness of a material. It is defined as the ratio of longitudinal stress to longitudinal strain.

3. Shear Modulus: The shear modulus is a measure of the resistance of a material to shear stress. It is defined as the ratio of shear stress to shear strain.

4. Bulk Modulus: The bulk modulus is a measure of the resistance of a material to volumetric compression. It is defined as the ratio of volumetric stress to volumetric strain.

Relationship between Elastic Constants:

1. Hooke's Law: Hooke's law states that the stress applied to a material is proportional to the strain produced, within the elastic limit of the material. This relationship is described by the elastic constants.

2. Modulus of Elasticity: The modulus of elasticity is a measure of the stiffness of a material. It is equal to the ratio of stress to strain and is equal to the Young's modulus.

3. Poisson's Ratio: Poisson's ratio is the ratio of transverse strain to longitudinal strain in a material. It is a measure of the relationship between the lateral and longitudinal strains in a material.
