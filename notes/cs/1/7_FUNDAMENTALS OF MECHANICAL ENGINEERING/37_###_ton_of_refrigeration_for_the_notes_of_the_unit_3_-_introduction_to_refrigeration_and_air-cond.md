Ton of Refrigeration:

The ton of refrigeration is a unit of measurement used in refrigeration and air-conditioning to describe the amount of heat that must be removed from a space in order to maintain a desired temperature.

1. Definition: A ton of refrigeration is defined as the amount of heat that must be removed from a space in order to freeze one ton (2000 pounds) of water at 32°F in 24 hours.

2. Calculation: The ton of refrigeration can be calculated by multiplying the amount of heat removed by the specific heat of water and dividing by the latent heat of fusion of water.

3. Applications: The ton of refrigeration is used in the design and operation of refrigeration and air-conditioning systems to determine the size and capacity of the system required to maintain a desired temperature.
