### Refrigeration for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a low-temperature substance and rejecting it to a high-temperature substance, with the aim of cooling the low-temperature substance. Refrigeration is used in many applications, including air-conditioning, food preservation, and industrial processes.

In Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, students learn about the basic principles of refrigeration, including the laws of thermodynamics, refrigeration cycles, and refrigerants. They also learn about the different types of refrigeration systems, including vapor-compression refrigeration systems, absorption refrigeration systems, and thermoelectric refrigeration systems.

The study of refrigeration is important for students who plan to pursue careers in mechanical engineering, as refrigeration is widely used in many industries, including HVAC, food processing, and transportation. Understanding the principles and applications of refrigeration is essential for engineers to design and implement efficient and reliable refrigeration systems.
