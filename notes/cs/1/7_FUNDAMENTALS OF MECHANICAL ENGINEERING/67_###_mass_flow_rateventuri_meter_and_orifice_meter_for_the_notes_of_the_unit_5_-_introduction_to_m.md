### mass flow rate(Venturi Meter and Orifice Meter) for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Mass flow rate is the rate at which mass flows through a system. In fluid mechanics, it is an important parameter used to describe the flow of fluids and to calculate the amount of fluid flowing through a system. Venturi meter and orifice meter are two types of flow meters used to measure mass flow rate. A Venturi meter uses the principle of Bernoulli's equation to measure the flow rate by constricting the flow of fluid and measuring the pressure drop across the constriction. An orifice meter uses an orifice plate to constrict the flow of fluid and measure the pressure drop across the orifice. Unit 5 of Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the concepts of mass flow rate, Venturi meter, and orifice meter, including the principles of operation, the calculation of mass flow rate, and the application of these flow meters in fluid systems. Understanding mass flow rate, Venturi meter, and orifice meter is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the measurement of fluid flow and the application of mechatronics in fluid systems.
