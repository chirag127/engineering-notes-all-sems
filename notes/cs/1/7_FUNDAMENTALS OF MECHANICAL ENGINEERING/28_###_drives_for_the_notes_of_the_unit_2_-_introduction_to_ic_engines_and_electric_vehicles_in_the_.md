Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles:

Unit 2 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the introduction to internal combustion (IC) engines and electric vehicles. It is important to understand the basic concepts of these technologies to appreciate their applications in modern vehicles.

1. Internal Combustion Engines (IC Engines): IC engines are engines that burn fuel and air inside a combustion chamber to produce power. The power is then transferred to the wheels of a vehicle through a transmission system.

2. Types of IC Engines: There are two main types of IC engines: spark-ignition (SI) engines and compression-ignition (CI) engines. SI engines are typically used in cars and light-duty vehicles, while CI engines are typically used in heavy-duty vehicles and diesel-powered vehicles.

3. Working of IC Engines: The working of an IC engine involves four main processes: intake, compression, combustion, and exhaust. During the intake process, fuel and air are drawn into the engine. During the compression process, the fuel and air are compressed within the engine. During the combustion process, the fuel and air ignite and burn, producing power. During the exhaust process, the burned gases are expelled from the engine.

4. Electric Vehicles (EVs): Electric vehicles are vehicles that are powered by an electric motor and a battery. EVs do not have an internal combustion engine and produce no emissions.

5. Types of Electric Vehicles: There are two main types of electric vehicles: pure electric vehicles (PEVs) and hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs). PEVs are powered solely by an electric motor and battery, while HEVs have both an internal combustion engine and an electric motor.

6. Working of Electric Vehicles: The working of an electric vehicle involves the conversion of electrical energy stored in the battery into mechanical energy by the electric motor. The mechanical energy is then used to power the wheels of the vehicle.

In conclusion, Unit 2 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the introduction to internal combustion (IC) engines and electric vehicles. IC engines are engines that burn fuel and air inside a combustion chamber to produce power. There are two main types of IC engines: spark-ignition (SI) engines and compression-ignition (CI) engines. Electric vehicles are vehicles that are powered by an electric motor and a battery and produce no emissions. There are two main types of electric vehicles: pure electric vehicles (PEVs) and hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs).
