### Refrigerating effect for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning:

Unit 3 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning. Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a substance or space to lower its temperature. Air-conditioning is the process of controlling the temperature, humidity, and air quality of a space.

1. Refrigerating Effect: The refrigerating effect is the amount of heat removed from a substance or space during the refrigeration process. The refrigerating effect is expressed in units of energy, such as joules or BTUs.

2. Refrigerants: Refrigerants are substances that are used to transfer heat from one place to another during the refrigeration process. Common refrigerants include chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs), and hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs).

3. Refrigeration Cycles: Refrigeration cycles are the series of processes that refrigerants undergo as they transfer heat from one place to another. The most common refrigeration cycles are the vapor-compression cycle and the absorption cycle.

4. Air-Conditioning: Air-conditioning is the process of controlling the temperature, humidity, and air quality of a space. Air-conditioning systems use refrigeration cycles to remove heat from the air in a space and cool it.

5. Types of Air-Conditioning Systems: There are several types of air-conditioning systems, including central air-conditioning systems, window air-conditioners, and portable air-conditioners.
