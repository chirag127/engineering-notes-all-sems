## Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics:
Mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the study of motion, forces, and energy. It is concerned with the behavior of objects under the influence of forces and the relationships between forces, motion, and energy. Mechanics is divided into two main branches: classical mechanics and quantum mechanics.

Classical Mechanics:
Classical mechanics is the study of motion and forces on macroscopic objects, such as planets, stars, and everyday objects. It is based on the laws of motion formulated by Sir Isaac Newton and deals with the behavior of objects under the influence of forces such as gravity, friction, and tension.

Quantum Mechanics:
Quantum mechanics is the study of motion and forces on a microscopic scale, such as atoms, molecules, and subatomic particles. It is based on the principles of quantum theory and deals with the behavior of objects that are subject to the laws of quantum mechanics, such as the Heisenberg uncertainty principle and the Schrödinger equation.

Applications:
Mechanics is used in a wide range of fields, including engineering, physics, astronomy, and biology. It is used to understand the behavior of objects under the influence of forces, to design and analyze mechanical systems, and to study the motion and behavior of celestial bodies.
