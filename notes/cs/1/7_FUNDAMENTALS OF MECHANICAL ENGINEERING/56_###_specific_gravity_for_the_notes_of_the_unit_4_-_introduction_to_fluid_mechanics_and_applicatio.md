### specific gravity for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Specific gravity is a dimensionless quantity that is used to compare the density of a substance to the density of water. It is defined as the ratio of the density of the substance to the density of water at a specified temperature. In fluid mechanics, specific gravity is used to determine the relative density of fluids and is an important factor in the design and operation of fluid systems. Unit 4 of Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the concept of specific gravity, including the calculation of specific gravity, the use of specific gravity in fluid mechanics, and the application of specific gravity in the design and operation of fluid systems. Understanding specific gravity is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the behavior of fluids and the principles of fluid mechanics.
