### Scope for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEER

The scope of Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering is to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the principles and techniques of measurement and instrumentation in mechanical engineering. The unit covers the fundamentals of measurement accuracy and precision, signal processing, data acquisition, and control systems. It also provides an introduction to the field of mechatronics, which is the integration of mechanical, electrical, and computer systems for the design and control of complex systems. The scope of this unit is to equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary to understand and apply these concepts in the design and control of mechanical systems.
