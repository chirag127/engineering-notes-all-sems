### Error in measurements for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Error in Measurements:
1. Systematic Error: consistently biased results in the same direction, caused by faulty equipment or incorrect calibration.
2. Random Error: unpredictable and inconsistent results, caused by fluctuations in the environment or measurement process.
3. Gross Error: large, easily noticeable errors, caused by human error or equipment malfunction.
4. Precision Error: repeatability of measurements, determined by the number of significant figures or decimal places.
5. Accuracy Error: closeness of measurements to the true value, determined by systematic and random errors.
6. Bias Error: systematic error due to personal bias or preconceived notions.
7. Resolution Error: smallest distinguishable change in a measurement, determined by the equipment's capability.
