### advantages of HV for the notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering deals with the study of internal combustion engines and electric vehicles. High voltage (HV) systems are an important aspect of electric vehicles.

The advantages of HV systems in electric vehicles include:

1. Increased Energy Efficiency: HV systems allow for more efficient use of energy, as more power can be transmitted with less energy loss.

2. Improved Performance: HV systems provide improved performance in electric vehicles, as they allow for higher power outputs and increased torque.

3. Reduced Weight: HV systems are lighter than low voltage systems, reducing the overall weight of electric vehicles and improving their efficiency.

4. Enhanced Safety: HV systems are designed with safety in mind, with multiple layers of protection to prevent electrical shock and other hazards.

In this unit, students learn about the basic principles of IC engines and electric vehicles, including the components, operation, and performance of these systems. The study of HV systems in electric vehicles is an important part of this unit, as it provides a foundation for understanding the design and operation of these systems.
