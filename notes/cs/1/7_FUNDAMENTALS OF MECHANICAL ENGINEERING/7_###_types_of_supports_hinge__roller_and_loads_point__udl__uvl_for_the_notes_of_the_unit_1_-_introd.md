### Types of supports (Hinge, Roller) and loads (Point, UDL, UVL) for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics:

Mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the study of motion, forces, and their effects. In Unit 1 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, it is important to understand the basics of the different types of supports and loads.

1. Types of Supports: Supports are the components that hold a structure in place and prevent it from collapsing. There are two main types of supports: hinge supports and roller supports.

2. Hinge Supports: Hinge supports are supports that allow rotational motion but prevent translational motion. Hinge supports are used in structures such as doors, gates, and bridges.

3. Roller Supports: Roller supports are supports that allow translational motion but prevent rotational motion. Roller supports are used in structures such as conveyor belts and roller coasters.

4. Types of Loads: Loads are the forces that act on a structure and cause it to deform. There are three main types of loads: point loads, uniform distributed loads, and uniform variable loads.

5. Point Loads: Point loads are loads that act at a single point on a structure. Point loads are used to model loads such as a single weight or a concentrated force.

6. Uniform Distributed Loads (UDL): Uniform distributed loads are loads that are evenly distributed over a length of a structure. UDLs are used to model loads such as snow, wind, and water.

7. Uniform Variable Loads (UVL): Uniform variable loads are loads that vary in magnitude over a length of a structure. UVLs are used to model loads such as traffic on a bridge or the weight of a cantilever beam.

In conclusion, mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the study of motion, forces, and their effects.
