### Force moment and couple for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Force, Moment, and Couple:
- Force: A push or pull on an object that results in a change in its velocity or motion. It is a vector quantity and is measured in newtons (N).
- Moment: A measure of the tendency of a force to rotate an object about a fixed point. It is also known as torque and is measured in newton-meters (Nm).
- Couple: A pair of equal and opposite forces that act on an object at different points. A couple creates a net moment, but does not result in a net force.

Applications:
- Forces are used to describe the behavior of objects under the influence of external forces, such as gravity, friction, and tension.
- Moments are used to describe the rotational behavior of objects, such as the torque produced by a rotating shaft or the moment of inertia of a body.
- Couples are used to describe the behavior of objects that are subject to rotational forces, such as the forces acting on a lever or a beam.
