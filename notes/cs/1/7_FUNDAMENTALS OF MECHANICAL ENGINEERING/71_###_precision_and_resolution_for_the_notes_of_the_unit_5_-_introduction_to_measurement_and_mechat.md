### precision and resolution for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Precision and resolution are important concepts in measurement. In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics, precision and resolution are studied in detail. 

Precision refers to the repeatability of a measurement, or how close a series of measurements are to each other. High precision means that the measurements are very close to each other, while low precision means that the measurements are widely scattered.

Resolution refers to the smallest change in a physical quantity that can be detected by a measuring instrument. High resolution means that the instrument can detect small changes in the physical quantity, while low resolution means that the instrument is not sensitive to small changes.

Understanding precision and resolution is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in engineering, including control systems, instrumentation, and mechatronics. Precision and resolution are important factors to consider when selecting measuring instruments and designing measurement systems.
