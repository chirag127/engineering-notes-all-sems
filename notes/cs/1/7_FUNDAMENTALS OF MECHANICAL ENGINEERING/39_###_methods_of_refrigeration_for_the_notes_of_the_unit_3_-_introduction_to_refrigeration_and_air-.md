Methods of Refrigeration:

Unit 3 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning. Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a substance or space to lower its temperature. There are several methods of refrigeration, including:

1. Mechanical Compression Refrigeration: This method uses a refrigerant that is compressed and expanded to remove heat from a space. A compressor is used to compress the refrigerant, which is then expanded to remove heat from the space.

2. Absorption Refrigeration: This method uses an absorbent and a refrigerant to remove heat from a space. The absorbent absorbs the refrigerant, which is then evaporated to remove heat from the space.

3. Evaporative Refrigeration: This method uses the evaporation of a refrigerant to remove heat from a space. The refrigerant is evaporated in a heat exchanger, which removes heat from the space.

4. Thermoelectric Refrigeration: This method uses the Peltier effect to remove heat from a space. The Peltier effect is the cooling that occurs when a current is passed through a junction between two different conductors.

5. Magnetic Refrigeration: This method uses magnetic materials to remove heat from a space. The magnetic material is subjected to a magnetic field, which causes it to change temperature and remove heat from the space.

In conclusion, there are several methods of refrigeration, including mechanical compression refrigeration, absorption refrigeration, evaporative refrigeration, thermoelectric refrigeration, and magnetic refrigeration. Understanding the different methods of refrigeration is important for understanding the basics of refrigeration and air-conditioning.
