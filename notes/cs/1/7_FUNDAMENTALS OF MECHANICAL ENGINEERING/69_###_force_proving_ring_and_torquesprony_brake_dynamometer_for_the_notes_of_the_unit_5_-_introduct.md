### force (Proving Ring) and torques(Prony Brake Dynamometer) for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics, students learn about the measurement of forces and torques.

Force is a physical quantity that describes the interaction between two objects. One common method of measuring force is the proving ring, which is a type of load cell that measures force by deforming under load.

Torque is a measure of the twisting force exerted by an object. One common method of measuring torque is the Prony brake dynamometer, which uses a brake to apply a torque to a rotating shaft and measure the resulting force.

Understanding the measurement of forces and torques is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in engineering, including control systems, instrumentation, and mechatronics. The measurement of forces and torques is used in a wide range of applications, including automotive engineering, aerospace engineering, and energy systems.
