### dynamic and kinematic viscosity for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Dynamic viscosity and kinematic viscosity are two important concepts in fluid mechanics that describe the resistance of a fluid to flow.

Dynamic viscosity is a measure of a fluid's internal resistance to flow and is defined as the ratio of shear stress to shear rate. It is a measure of the fluid's internal friction and is expressed in units of Pa·s (Pascal-seconds).

Kinematic viscosity is the ratio of dynamic viscosity to density and is expressed in units of m²/s (square meters per second). It is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow and is independent of the fluid's density.
