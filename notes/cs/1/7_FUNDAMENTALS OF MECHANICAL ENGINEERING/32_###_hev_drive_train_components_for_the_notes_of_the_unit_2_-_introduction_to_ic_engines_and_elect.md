### HEV drive train components for the notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

HEV Drive Train Components:

In Unit 2 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, the Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles is covered. Within this unit, the components of a Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV) drive train are also covered.

1. Internal Combustion Engine (ICE): The ICE is the traditional engine found in most vehicles. It runs on gasoline or diesel fuel and provides the power to drive the wheels of the vehicle.

2. Electric Motor: The electric motor is the main component of the HEV drive train. It provides power to the wheels of the vehicle and acts as a generator to recharge the battery.

3. Battery: The battery stores energy that can be used by the electric motor to power the vehicle. The battery is recharged by the ICE or by regenerative braking.

4. Power Electronics: The power electronics control the flow of energy between the battery, electric motor, and ICE. It includes components such as the inverter, DC/DC converter, and motor controller.

5. Transmission: The transmission is responsible for transferring power from the engine and electric motor to the wheels of the vehicle. In an HEV, the transmission is designed to work with both the ICE and electric motor.

6. Drive Shaft: The drive shaft is a rotating shaft that transfers power from the transmission to the wheels of the vehicle.

7. Differential: The differential is a component that distributes power to the wheels of the vehicle. It allows the wheels to rotate at different speeds, which is necessary for cornering.

In conclusion, the components of a Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV) drive train include the Internal Combustion Engine (ICE), electric motor, battery, power electronics, transmission, drive shaft, and differential. These components work together to provide power to the wheels of the vehicle and to control the flow of energy.
