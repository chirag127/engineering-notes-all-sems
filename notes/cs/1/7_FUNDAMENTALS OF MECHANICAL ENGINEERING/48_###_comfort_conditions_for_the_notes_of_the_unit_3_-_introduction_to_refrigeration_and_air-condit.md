### comfort conditions for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Comfort conditions refer to the physical and psychological factors that determine the level of comfort experienced by a person in a particular environment. In the context of refrigeration and air conditioning, comfort conditions typically include temperature, humidity, air movement, and air quality. Unit 3 of Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the factors that influence comfort conditions and how these factors are controlled and maintained in indoor environments. Understanding comfort conditions is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a foundation for understanding the design and operation of refrigeration and air conditioning systems.
