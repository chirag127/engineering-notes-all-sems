### humidity for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Humidity is the amount of water vapor in the air. It is an important parameter in the study of refrigeration and air-conditioning, as it affects the comfort and well-being of people in indoor environments.

In Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, students learn about the basic principles of humidity, including how it is measured, how it affects the air-conditioning process, and how it can be controlled.

Humidity is typically measured in terms of relative humidity, which is the ratio of the amount of water vapor in the air to the maximum amount of water vapor that can be present at a given temperature. High relative humidity can cause discomfort and health problems, while low relative humidity can cause dry skin and respiratory problems.

In air-conditioning systems, humidity is controlled by removing or adding water vapor to the air. This is typically achieved using a dehumidifier or a humidifier, respectively.

The study of humidity is important for students who plan to pursue careers in mechanical engineering, as it provides a foundation for understanding the principles and applications of refrigeration and air-conditioning systems. Understanding the basic principles of humidity is essential for the design and operation of comfortable and healthy indoor environments.
