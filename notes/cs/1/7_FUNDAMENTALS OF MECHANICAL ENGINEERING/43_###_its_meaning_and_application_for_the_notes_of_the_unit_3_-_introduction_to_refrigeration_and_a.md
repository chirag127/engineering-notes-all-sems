### Its meaning and application for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning (RAC) are important fields of mechanical engineering that deal with the design, operation, and maintenance of systems that control temperature and humidity in buildings and vehicles. In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning, students learn about the basic principles and applications of RAC systems.

Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a low-temperature medium and rejecting it to a high-temperature medium. This is achieved by using refrigerants, which are fluids that change state as they absorb and release heat. Refrigeration is used in a variety of applications, including food preservation, air-conditioning, and industrial processes.

Air-conditioning is the process of controlling the temperature, humidity, and air quality in a building or vehicle. This is achieved by using refrigeration and ventilation systems to remove heat and moisture from the air. Air-conditioning is used in a variety of applications, including residential and commercial buildings, vehicles, and aircraft.

Understanding the principles and applications of refrigeration and air-conditioning is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in thermal engineering, energy systems, and building services engineering.
