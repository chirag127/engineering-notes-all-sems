### dew point temperatures for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Dew point temperature is the temperature at which the water vapor in air condenses into liquid form. In refrigeration and air conditioning, dew point temperature is used to determine the moisture content of air, and is a critical factor in the design and operation of refrigeration and air conditioning systems. Unit 3 of Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the concept of dew point temperature, including the calculation of dew point temperature, the effect of dew point temperature on refrigeration and air conditioning systems, and the use of dew point temperature in the design and operation of these systems. Understanding dew point temperature is important for students studying Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the behavior of refrigeration and air conditioning systems and the principles of thermodynamics.
