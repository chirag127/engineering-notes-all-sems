### Air-Conditioning for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Air-conditioning is a process of controlling the temperature, humidity, and air quality of indoor air to provide a comfortable and healthy environment. In Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, students learn about the basic principles of air-conditioning, including the components, operation, and maintenance of air-conditioning systems.

The components of an air-conditioning system include a refrigerant, a compressor, a condenser, an evaporator, and an expansion valve. The refrigerant is a substance that is used to transfer heat from one place to another. The compressor is used to compress the refrigerant and pump it through the system. The condenser is used to reject heat to the outside air, while the evaporator is used to absorb heat from the indoor air. The expansion valve is used to regulate the flow of refrigerant into the evaporator.

In this unit, students learn about the different types of air-conditioning systems, including window air-conditioners, split air-conditioners, central air-conditioners, and refrigeration systems. They also learn about the different refrigerants used in air-conditioning systems and their impact on the environment.

The study of air-conditioning is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in mechanical engineering, as air-conditioning systems are widely used in residential, commercial, and industrial applications. Understanding the principles and applications of air-conditioning is important for engineers to design and implement systems that are efficient, reliable, and environmentally friendly.
