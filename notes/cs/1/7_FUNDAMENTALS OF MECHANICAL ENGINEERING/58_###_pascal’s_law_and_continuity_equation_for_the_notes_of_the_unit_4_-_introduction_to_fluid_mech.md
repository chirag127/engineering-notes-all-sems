Pascal's Law and Continuity Equation:

Unit 4 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications. In this unit, it is important to understand the basics of fluid mechanics, including Pascal's law and the continuity equation.

1. Pascal's Law: Pascal's law states that the pressure applied to a confined fluid is transmitted undiminished to every point of the fluid and to the walls of the container. This law is the basis for the operation of hydraulic systems.

2. Continuity Equation: The continuity equation states that the rate of flow of a fluid through a pipe is equal to the product of the cross-sectional area of the pipe and the velocity of the fluid.

3. Applications: Pascal's law and the continuity equation are used in the design and analysis of hydraulic systems, including hydraulic pumps, valves, and actuators. They are also used in the design of fluid pipelines, including water and gas pipelines.
