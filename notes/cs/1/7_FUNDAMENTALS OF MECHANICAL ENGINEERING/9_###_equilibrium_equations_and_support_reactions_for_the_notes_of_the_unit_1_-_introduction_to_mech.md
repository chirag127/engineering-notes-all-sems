Equilibrium Equations and Support Reactions:

Unit 1 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the introduction to Mechanics, including the concepts of equilibrium equations and support reactions.

1. Equilibrium Equations: Equilibrium equations are mathematical statements that describe the balance of forces acting on a body. There are three equilibrium equations: the sum of forces in the x-direction, the sum of forces in the y-direction, and the sum of moments.

2. Support Reactions: Support reactions are the forces and moments that are exerted by a support on a body. They are essential in determining the forces and moments acting on a body and are used to solve for the unknown forces and moments in a system.

3. Free Body Diagrams: Free body diagrams are drawings that show the forces and moments acting on a body. They are used to simplify the analysis of a system and to determine the unknown forces and moments.

4. Determining Support Reactions: To determine the support reactions, the free body diagram of a body must be created. The unknown forces and moments in the system can then be solved for using the equilibrium equations.
