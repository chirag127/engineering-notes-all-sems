Wet Bulb:

The wet bulb temperature is a measure of the lowest temperature that can be achieved by evaporative cooling. It is an important concept in the field of refrigeration and air-conditioning and is covered in Unit 3 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering.

1. Definition: The wet bulb temperature is the temperature that is read from a thermometer covered in a wet wick and exposed to air flow. The wet bulb temperature represents the lowest temperature that can be achieved by evaporative cooling.

2. Calculation: The wet bulb temperature can be calculated by exposing a thermometer covered in a wet wick to air flow and measuring the temperature.

3. Applications: The wet bulb temperature is used in the field of refrigeration and air-conditioning to determine the relative humidity of air and to calculate the dew point temperature.

Importance: The wet bulb temperature is an important concept in refrigeration and air-conditioning because it provides a measure of the lowest temperature that can be achieved by evaporative cooling.
