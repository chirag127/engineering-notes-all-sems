### strain(Bonded and Unbonded Strain Gauge) for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Strain is a measure of the deformation of a material caused by an applied force. In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics, students learn about the measurement of strain using strain gauges. There are two types of strain gauges: bonded and unbonded.

1. Bonded Strain Gauges: Bonded strain gauges are attached directly to the surface of the material being tested and are used to measure the strain in the material. The gauge consists of a thin metal foil that is bonded to the surface of the material and connected to a measuring instrument.

2. Unbonded Strain Gauges: Unbonded strain gauges are not attached to the surface of the material being tested and are used to measure the strain in the material by measuring the change in resistance of the gauge. The gauge consists of a thin metal wire that is wrapped around the material and connected to a measuring instrument.

Understanding the principles and applications of strain gauges is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in engineering, including control systems, instrumentation, and mechatronics.
