## Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

1. An **Internal Combustion Engine (IC Engine)** is a type of heat engine where the combustion of fuel occurs within the engine, producing mechanical work.
2. The most common types of IC engines are **reciprocating engines**, where the combustion of fuel occurs within a cylinder and the resulting pressure drives a piston.
3. IC engines can be powered by a variety of fuels, including gasoline, diesel, and natural gas.
4. **Electric Vehicles (EVs)**, on the other hand, are powered by electricity stored in a battery.
5. EVs produce zero tailpipe emissions and can be charged from an external power source or through regenerative braking.
6. The use of EVs can reduce dependence on fossil fuels and reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
7. Both IC engines and EVs have their own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice between the two depends on factors such as cost, range, and environmental impact.
