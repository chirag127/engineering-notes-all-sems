### Ton of Refrigeration

- A ton of refrigeration (TR) is a unit of power used to describe the heat-extraction capacity of refrigeration and air conditioning equipment.
- It is defined as the amount of heat required to melt one ton (2000 pounds) of ice in a 24-hour period.
- One ton of refrigeration is equal to 12,000 British Thermal Units (BTUs) per hour or 3.516 kilowatts (kW).
- The term originated during the early days of refrigeration when ice was the primary means of cooling. The cooling capacity of an ice-making plant was measured by the amount of ice it could produce in a day.
- Today, the term is still used to describe the cooling capacity of air conditioning and refrigeration systems, although the actual cooling capacity may not be directly related to the production of ice.
- In the context of refrigeration and air conditioning, the term "ton" does not refer to the weight of the equipment, but rather to its cooling capacity.
- The cooling capacity of a refrigeration or air conditioning system is often expressed in tons of refrigeration, with larger systems having a higher tonnage.
- When selecting a refrigeration or air conditioning system, it is important to consider the tonnage to ensure that the system has sufficient cooling capacity for the intended application.
