### Scope for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Introduction to Measurement: Definition, significance, and types of measurement.
2. Measurement Systems: Classification, characteristics, and selection criteria.
3. Sensors and Transducers: Definition, classification, and selection criteria.
4. Signal Conditioning: Definition, types, and applications.
5. Data Acquisition and Processing: Definition, types, and applications.
6. Introduction to Mechatronics: Definition, significance, and applications.
7. Mechatronic Systems: Components, design, and applications.
8. Case Studies: Real-world examples of mechatronic systems in various industries.
