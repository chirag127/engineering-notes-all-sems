### Temperature (Thermocouple and Optical Pyrometer)

Temperature is a measure of the average kinetic energy of the particles in a system. It is an important physical quantity that is used in many fields, including engineering, physics, and chemistry.

#### Thermocouple

A thermocouple is a type of temperature sensor that consists of two dissimilar metal wires joined at one end. When the junction of the two metals is heated or cooled, a voltage is produced that can be correlated to temperature. Thermocouples are widely used in science and industry because they are inexpensive, rugged, and can measure a wide range of temperatures.

#### Optical Pyrometer

An optical pyrometer is a non-contact temperature measurement device that measures the thermal radiation emitted by an object. It works by comparing the brightness of the object to the brightness of a calibrated light source, and using this comparison to determine the temperature of the object. Optical pyrometers are useful for measuring the temperature of objects that are difficult to access or that are moving, such as molten metal or the surface of a furnace.
