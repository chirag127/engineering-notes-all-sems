### Resultant of Force System- Concurrent and Non-Concurrent Coplanar Forces

#### Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

##### Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering

1. A force system is a collection of forces acting on a body or a group of bodies.
2. The resultant of a force system is a single force that has the same effect on the body as the original force system.
3. Concurrent forces are forces whose lines of action intersect at a common point.
4. Non-concurrent forces are forces whose lines of action do not intersect at a common point.
5. Coplanar forces are forces that lie in the same plane.
6. The resultant of a concurrent coplanar force system can be found using the parallelogram law or the triangle law of vector addition.
7. The resultant of a non-concurrent coplanar force system can be found using the method of moments or the method of resolution.
