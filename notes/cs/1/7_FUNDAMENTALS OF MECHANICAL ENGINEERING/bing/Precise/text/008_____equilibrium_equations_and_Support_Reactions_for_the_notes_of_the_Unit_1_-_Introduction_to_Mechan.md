### Equilibrium Equations and Support Reactions

In the study of mechanics, equilibrium refers to a state where the net force and net moment acting on a body are zero. This means that the body is either at rest or moving with a constant velocity. To determine the forces acting on a body in equilibrium, we can use the following equations:

1. **Sum of forces in the x-direction:** ΣFx = 0
2. **Sum of forces in the y-direction:** ΣFy = 0
3. **Sum of moments about any point:** ΣM = 0

These equations are known as the **equilibrium equations**. They can be used to solve for unknown forces and moments acting on a body in equilibrium.

Support reactions are the forces and moments that a support exerts on a body to keep it in equilibrium. Common types of supports include fixed supports, pin supports, and roller supports. Each type of support can exert different types of support reactions. For example, a fixed support can exert forces in the x and y directions, as well as a moment, while a pin support can only exert forces in the x and y directions.

To determine the support reactions, we can use the equilibrium equations and the known forces and moments acting on the body. By solving the equations, we can find the unknown support reactions.
