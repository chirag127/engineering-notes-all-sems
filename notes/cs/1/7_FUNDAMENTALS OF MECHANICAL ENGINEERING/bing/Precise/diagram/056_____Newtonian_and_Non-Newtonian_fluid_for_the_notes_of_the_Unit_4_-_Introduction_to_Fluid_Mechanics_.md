### Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications
#### Newtonian and Non-Newtonian fluid

1. **Newtonian fluid** is a fluid that has a constant viscosity and flows consistently under applied stress, regardless of the rate of shear. The viscosity of a Newtonian fluid is only affected by changes in temperature or pressure. Examples of Newtonian fluids include water, air, and honey.

2. **Non-Newtonian fluid** is a fluid that does not have a constant viscosity and its flow behavior changes under applied stress. The viscosity of a Non-Newtonian fluid is dependent on the rate of shear, and can increase or decrease with changes in the rate of shear. Examples of Non-Newtonian fluids include ketchup, blood, and quicksand.

3. The difference between Newtonian and Non-Newtonian fluids is important in the study of fluid mechanics and its applications, as it affects the behavior of fluids under different conditions and in different systems.

4. In the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING, understanding the properties and behavior of Newtonian and Non-Newtonian fluids is essential for the design and analysis of fluid systems and their components.
