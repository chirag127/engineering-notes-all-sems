### HEV Drive Train Components

A hybrid electric vehicle (HEV) combines a conventional internal combustion engine (ICE) propulsion system with an electric propulsion system. The presence of the electric powertrain is intended to achieve either better fuel economy than a conventional vehicle or better performance. The main components of the HEV drive train are:

1. **Internal Combustion Engine (ICE):** The ICE in an HEV is typically smaller and more efficient than in a conventional vehicle. It is used to provide propulsion power and to charge the battery.

2. **Electric Motor:** The electric motor in an HEV is used to provide additional propulsion power and to recover energy during braking.

3. **Battery:** The battery in an HEV is used to store electrical energy that is generated by the ICE and recovered during braking. It is also used to provide power to the electric motor.

4. **Power Electronics:** The power electronics in an HEV are used to control the flow of electrical energy between the battery, electric motor, and ICE.

5. **Transmission:** The transmission in an HEV is used to transfer power from the ICE and electric motor to the wheels. It may be a conventional transmission or a continuously variable transmission (CVT).

6. **Regenerative Braking System:** The regenerative braking system in an HEV is used to recover energy during braking and store it in the battery.

These are the main components of the HEV drive train. Each component plays a crucial role in the overall operation of the vehicle. It is important to have a good understanding of these components in order to fully understand the operation of an HEV.
