## Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

### IC Engines

1. An internal combustion engine (IC engine) is a type of heat engine where the combustion of fuel occurs within the engine, producing mechanical work.
2. The most common types of IC engines are the reciprocating engine and the rotary engine.
3. In a reciprocating engine, the combustion of fuel takes place in a cylinder, and the expanding gases push a piston, which in turn rotates a crankshaft to produce mechanical work.
4. In a rotary engine, the combustion of fuel takes place in a chamber, and the expanding gases push against a rotor, which rotates to produce mechanical work.
5. IC engines can be powered by a variety of fuels, including gasoline, diesel, and natural gas.

### Electric Vehicles

1. An electric vehicle (EV) is a vehicle that is powered by an electric motor, rather than an internal combustion engine.
2. EVs can be powered by a variety of sources, including batteries, fuel cells, and solar panels.
3. The most common type of EV is the battery electric vehicle (BEV), which is powered by a rechargeable battery.
4. EVs produce zero tailpipe emissions, making them a more environmentally friendly option than vehicles powered by internal combustion engines.
5. The range of an EV depends on the capacity of its battery, and can vary widely between different models.
