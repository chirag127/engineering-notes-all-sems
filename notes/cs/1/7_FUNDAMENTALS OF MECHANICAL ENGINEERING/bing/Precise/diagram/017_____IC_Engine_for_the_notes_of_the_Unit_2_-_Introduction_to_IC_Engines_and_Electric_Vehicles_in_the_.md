### IC Engine

An internal combustion engine (IC engine) is a type of heat engine where the combustion of fuel occurs within the engine, producing mechanical work. This mechanical work is used to power vehicles, machines, and other devices. The most common types of IC engines are the spark ignition (SI) engine, which is used in gasoline-powered vehicles, and the compression ignition (CI) engine, which is used in diesel-powered vehicles.

Some key points to remember about IC engines are:

1. The combustion of fuel occurs within the engine, producing mechanical work.
2. The most common types of IC engines are the spark ignition (SI) engine and the compression ignition (CI) engine.
3. SI engines are used in gasoline-powered vehicles, while CI engines are used in diesel-powered vehicles.
4. IC engines are used to power vehicles, machines, and other devices.
