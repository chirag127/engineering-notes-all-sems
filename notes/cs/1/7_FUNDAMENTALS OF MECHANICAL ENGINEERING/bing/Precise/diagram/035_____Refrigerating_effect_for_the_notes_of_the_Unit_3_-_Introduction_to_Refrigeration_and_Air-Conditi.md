### Refrigerating Effect

- Refrigerating effect is the quantity of heat absorbed from a refrigerated space .
- It is the measure of the cooling effect produced by the refrigerant in the evaporator .
- The required refrigerating effect is derived from the equation: Qe = mm × Cp,m × ΔT, where Qe is the evaporator load, Cp,m is the specific heat of the substance being cooled, and ΔT is the difference between the initial and final temperature of the substance in the evaporator .
- The refrigeration duty, or refrigeration capacity, refers to the total amount of heat absorbed in the chiller by the process, generally expressed in kW .
