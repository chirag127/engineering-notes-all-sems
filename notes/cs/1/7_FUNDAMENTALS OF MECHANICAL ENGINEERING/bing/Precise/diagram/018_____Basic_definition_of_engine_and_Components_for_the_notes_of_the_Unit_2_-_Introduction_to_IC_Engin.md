### Basic definition of engine and Components for the notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

An engine is a machine designed to convert one form of energy into mechanical energy. Internal combustion engines (IC engines) are a type of engine that burn fuel within the engine itself to produce mechanical energy. Electric vehicles, on the other hand, use electric motors to convert electrical energy into mechanical energy.

The main components of an IC engine include:
- Cylinder: The cylinder is the main body of the engine where the fuel is burned and the piston moves up and down.
- Piston: The piston is a cylindrical component that moves up and down within the cylinder, compressing the fuel-air mixture and transmitting the force of the explosion to the crankshaft.
- Crankshaft: The crankshaft converts the linear motion of the piston into rotational motion, which is used to power the wheels of the vehicle.
- Fuel system: The fuel system delivers fuel to the engine, where it is mixed with air and burned to produce energy.
- Cooling system: The cooling system helps to regulate the temperature of the engine, preventing it from overheating.
- Exhaust system: The exhaust system removes the waste gases produced by the combustion process from the engine.
