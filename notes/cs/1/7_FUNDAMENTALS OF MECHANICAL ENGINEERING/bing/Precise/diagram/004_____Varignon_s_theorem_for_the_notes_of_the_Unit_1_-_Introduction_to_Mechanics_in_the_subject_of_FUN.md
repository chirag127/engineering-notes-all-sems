### Varignon's Theorem

Varignon's Theorem is a statement in mechanics that deals with the moment of a force. It states that the moment of a force about a point is equal to the algebraic sum of the moments of the components of the force about the same point.

In other words, if a force is resolved into two or more components, the moment of the force about a point is equal to the sum of the moments of the components about the same point.

This theorem is useful in solving problems involving the moment of a force, as it allows us to break down a complex force into simpler components and calculate their moments separately.
