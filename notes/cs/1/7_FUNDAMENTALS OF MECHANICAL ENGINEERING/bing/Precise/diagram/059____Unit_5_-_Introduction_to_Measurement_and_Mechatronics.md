## Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics

1. **Measurement** is the process of determining the value of a physical quantity by comparing it with a known standard.
2. **Mechatronics** is a multidisciplinary field that combines mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering to design and develop advanced systems and products.
3. Mechatronics systems often involve the use of sensors and actuators to measure and control physical quantities.
4. Common measurement techniques include the use of scales, rulers, and gauges to measure length, mass, and pressure, respectively.
5. In mechatronics, measurements are often taken using electronic sensors, which convert physical quantities into electrical signals that can be processed by a computer.
6. Examples of mechatronic systems include robots, automated manufacturing systems, and smart home appliances.
7. The design of mechatronic systems involves the integration of mechanical, electrical, and computer components to achieve a desired functionality.
8. Mechatronics is a rapidly growing field with applications in a wide range of industries, including automotive, aerospace, and consumer electronics.
