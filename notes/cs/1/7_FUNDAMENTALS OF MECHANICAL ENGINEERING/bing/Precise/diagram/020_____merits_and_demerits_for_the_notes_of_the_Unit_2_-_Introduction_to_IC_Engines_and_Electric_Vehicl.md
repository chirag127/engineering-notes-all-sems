### Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

#### Merits of IC Engines:
1. IC engines have a high power-to-weight ratio, making them suitable for use in vehicles.
2. They are highly reliable and have a long lifespan.
3. IC engines can be easily refueled, making them convenient for long-distance travel.
4. They are widely available and have a well-established infrastructure for fueling and maintenance.

#### Demerits of IC Engines:
1. IC engines produce harmful emissions, contributing to air pollution and climate change.
2. They are less efficient than electric vehicles, resulting in higher fuel costs.
3. IC engines require regular maintenance, which can be costly.
4. They are noisy and can contribute to noise pollution.

#### Merits of Electric Vehicles:
1. Electric vehicles produce zero tailpipe emissions, making them environmentally friendly.
2. They are highly efficient, resulting in lower operating costs.
3. Electric vehicles require less maintenance than IC engines, reducing long-term costs.
4. They are quiet and smooth to drive.

#### Demerits of Electric Vehicles:
1. Electric vehicles have a limited range and require regular charging, which can be inconvenient.
2. The charging infrastructure is not as well-established as the fueling infrastructure for IC engines.
3. Electric vehicles have a higher upfront cost than IC engines.
4. The production of batteries for electric vehicles has environmental impacts.
