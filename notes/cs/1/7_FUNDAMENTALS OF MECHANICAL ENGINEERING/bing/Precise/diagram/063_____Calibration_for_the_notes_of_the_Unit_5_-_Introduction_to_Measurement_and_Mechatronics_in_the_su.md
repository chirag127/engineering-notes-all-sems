### Calibration

Calibration is the process of comparing the values obtained by a measuring instrument with the corresponding values of a standard. It is an essential step in ensuring the accuracy and reliability of measurements.

Here are some key points to remember about calibration:

1. Calibration is necessary for all measuring instruments, including those used in mechatronics systems.
2. Calibration should be performed regularly to ensure that the instrument is providing accurate readings.
3. The frequency of calibration depends on factors such as the type of instrument, its usage, and the environment in which it is used.
4. Calibration can be performed in-house or by an external calibration service.
5. A calibration certificate is issued after the calibration process, which provides information about the instrument's accuracy and any adjustments made during the calibration process.

Calibration is an important part of the measurement process and is essential for ensuring the accuracy and reliability of measurements in mechatronics systems. It is important to follow the recommended calibration procedures and schedule to ensure that the instrument is providing accurate readings.
