#### Measurements of Pressure

There are several methods to measure pressure, two of which are the Bourdon Tube Pressure and the U-Tube Manometer.

##### Bourdon Tube Pressure
- The Bourdon Tube Pressure gauge is a mechanical device used to measure the pressure of liquids or gases.
- It consists of a curved tube, which is flattened and bent into a C-shape or a helix.
- When the pressure inside the tube increases, the tube tends to straighten, causing the movement of a pointer attached to the tube.
- The movement of the pointer is proportional to the change in pressure.

##### U-Tube Manometer
- The U-Tube Manometer is a simple device used to measure pressure.
- It consists of a U-shaped tube filled with a liquid, usually mercury or water.
- The pressure to be measured is applied to one end of the tube, causing the liquid to rise on that side and fall on the other side.
- The difference in height between the two sides of the tube is proportional to the pressure being measured.
