### Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications

Unit 4 of the subject Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the topic of Fluid Mechanics and its Applications. Fluid mechanics is the branch of physics that studies the mechanics of fluids (liquids, gases, and plasmas) and the forces on them. It has applications in a wide range of disciplines, including mechanical, civil, chemical, and biomedical engineering, geophysics, oceanography, meteorology, astrophysics, and biology.

Some of the key concepts covered in this unit include:
- Properties of fluids: density, viscosity, surface tension, and compressibility
- Fluid statics: pressure, buoyancy, and stability of floating bodies
- Fluid dynamics: continuity equation, Bernoulli's equation, and flow measurement
- Applications of fluid mechanics: hydraulic machines, fluid power systems, and aerodynamics

This unit provides a fundamental understanding of the behavior of fluids and their interactions with solid bodies. It also introduces students to the practical applications of fluid mechanics in engineering and other fields. By the end of this unit, students should be able to analyze and solve problems involving fluid mechanics and apply their knowledge to real-world situations.
