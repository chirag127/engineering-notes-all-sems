### Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications
#### Working principles of hydraulic turbines (Pelton Wheel and Francis) & pumps (Centrifugal and Reciprocating) and their classifications and hydraulic lift

- Hydraulic turbines are machines that convert the energy of flowing water into mechanical energy.
- There are two main types of hydraulic turbines: impulse turbines and reaction turbines.
- The Pelton Wheel is an example of an impulse turbine. It operates by converting the kinetic energy of the water into mechanical energy through the use of a series of buckets mounted on the perimeter of a wheel.
- The Francis turbine is a type of reaction turbine. It operates by converting the pressure energy of the water into mechanical energy through the use of a series of fixed and moving blades.
- Hydraulic pumps are machines that convert mechanical energy into hydraulic energy.
- There are two main types of hydraulic pumps: positive displacement pumps and non-positive displacement pumps.
- Centrifugal pumps are a type of non-positive displacement pump. They operate by using a rotating impeller to create a flow of fluid.
- Reciprocating pumps are a type of positive displacement pump. They operate by using a piston or plunger to create a flow of fluid.
- Hydraulic lifts are machines that use hydraulic power to lift heavy loads.
- They operate by using a hydraulic cylinder to apply force to a load, causing it to rise.
