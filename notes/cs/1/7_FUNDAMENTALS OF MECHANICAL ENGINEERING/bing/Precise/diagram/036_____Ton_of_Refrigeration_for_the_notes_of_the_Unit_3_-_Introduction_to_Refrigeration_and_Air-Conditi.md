### Ton of Refrigeration

- A ton of refrigeration (TR) is a unit of power used to describe the heat-extraction capacity of refrigeration and air conditioning equipment.
- It is defined as the amount of heat required to melt one ton (2000 pounds) of ice in a 24-hour period.
- One ton of refrigeration is equal to 12,000 British Thermal Units (BTUs) per hour or 3.516 kilowatts (kW).
- The term originated during the early days of refrigeration when ice was the primary means of cooling. The capacity of a refrigeration system was measured by how many tons of ice it could produce in a day.
- Today, the term is still used to describe the cooling capacity of air conditioning and refrigeration systems, although the actual cooling process no longer involves the production of ice.
- In the context of refrigeration and air conditioning, the term "ton" does not refer to the weight of the equipment, but rather to its cooling capacity.
