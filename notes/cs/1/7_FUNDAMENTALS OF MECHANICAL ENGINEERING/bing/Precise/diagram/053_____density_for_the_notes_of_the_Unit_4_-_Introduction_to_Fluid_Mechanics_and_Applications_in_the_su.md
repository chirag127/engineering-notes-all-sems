### Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications
#### Density
- Density is a measure of mass per unit volume.
- The formula for density is `density = mass/volume`.
- The SI unit for density is kilograms per cubic meter (kg/m³).
- Density is an important property of fluids, as it affects the behavior of fluids in motion.
- The density of a fluid can change with temperature and pressure.
- An object will float in a fluid if its density is less than the density of the fluid, and will sink if its density is greater than the density of the fluid.
- The density of a fluid can be measured using a hydrometer or by calculating the mass and volume of a sample of the fluid.
- The density of a fluid is used in many calculations in fluid mechanics, including the calculation of buoyancy force and fluid flow rate.
