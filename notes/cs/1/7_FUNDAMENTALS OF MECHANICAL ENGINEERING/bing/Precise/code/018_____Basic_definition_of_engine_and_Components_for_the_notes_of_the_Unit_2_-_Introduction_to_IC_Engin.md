### Basic definition of engine and Components for the notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

An engine is a mechanical device that converts energy into motion. The energy can come from various sources such as fuel, heat, or electricity. The motion produced by an engine is used to power machines, vehicles, and other devices.

The main components of an internal combustion engine (IC engine) are:
1. Cylinder: The cylinder is the main body of the engine where the fuel is burned and the power is produced.
2. Piston: The piston moves up and down inside the cylinder, compressing the fuel-air mixture and transmitting the power produced by the combustion to the crankshaft.
3. Crankshaft: The crankshaft converts the linear motion of the piston into rotational motion, which is used to power the wheels of a vehicle or other machinery.
4. Connecting rod: The connecting rod connects the piston to the crankshaft and transmits the power produced by the combustion to the crankshaft.
5. Valves: The valves control the flow of air and fuel into the cylinder and the flow of exhaust gases out of the cylinder.
6. Spark plug: The spark plug ignites the fuel-air mixture in the cylinder, causing the combustion that produces power.
7. Fuel injector: The fuel injector sprays fuel into the cylinder in a precise and controlled manner, ensuring efficient combustion.

An electric vehicle (EV) is a vehicle that is powered by an electric motor instead of an internal combustion engine. The electric motor is powered by a battery, which can be recharged using an external power source. The main components of an electric vehicle are:
1. Electric motor: The electric motor converts electrical energy into motion, which is used to power the wheels of the vehicle.
2. Battery: The battery stores electrical energy, which is used to power the electric motor.
3. Controller: The controller regulates the flow of electrical energy from the battery to the electric motor, controlling the speed and torque of the motor.
4. Charger: The charger is used to recharge the battery using an external power source.
5. Regenerative braking system: The regenerative braking system captures the energy lost during braking and uses it to recharge the battery, increasing the efficiency of the vehicle.
