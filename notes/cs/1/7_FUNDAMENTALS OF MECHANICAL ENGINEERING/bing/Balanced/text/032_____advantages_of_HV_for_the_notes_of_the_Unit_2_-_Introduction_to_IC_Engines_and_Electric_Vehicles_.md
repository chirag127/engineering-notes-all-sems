### Advantages of HV

HV stands for high voltage, which is a term used to describe electrical systems that operate at voltages above 1000 V for alternating current (AC) or 1500 V for direct current (DC). HV systems are used for power transmission and distribution, as well as for some industrial and marine applications. HV systems have some advantages over low voltage (LV) systems, such as:

- Reduced voltage drop: Voltage drop is the decrease in voltage along a transmission line due to the resistance of the conductor and the impedance of the load. HV systems have lower current for the same power, which reduces the voltage drop and improves the voltage regulation .
- Reduced transmission wire cost: HV systems require thinner conductors for the same power, which reduces the cost of building and maintaining the transmission line. HV systems also allow for longer distances between towers or poles, which reduces the number of structures needed  .
- Reduced power loss: HV systems have lower current for the same power, which reduces the power loss due to the Joule effect (heating of the conductor). HV systems also have lower reactive power loss due to the capacitance and inductance of the transmission line. HV systems can achieve power loss of only about 3% for every 1000 km, depending on the system construction and voltage level.
- Increased power transfer capacity: HV systems can transfer more power over a given transmission line than LV systems, which increases the efficiency and reliability of the power grid. HV systems can also interconnect different power grids and enable the exchange of power between regions.
- Reduced fossil fuel dependency: HV systems can be used to integrate renewable energy sources, such as wind and solar, into the power grid. HV systems can also be used to power hybrid vehicles, which combine an internal combustion engine (ICE) and an electric motor to reduce fuel consumption and emissions. Hybrid vehicles can utilize the high voltage battery pack and electric motor for propulsion, which reduces the reliance on fossil fuels.
