### Concept of Measurement

- Measurement is the process of assigning numbers or symbols to physical quantities or attributes of objects or phenomena, according to certain rules or standards.
- Measurement is essential for engineering design, analysis, testing, quality control, and maintenance of mechanical systems and components.
- The science of measurement is known as metrology, which includes the theory, methods, and instruments of measurement .
- Measurement can be classified into different types, such as direct and indirect, absolute and relative, static and dynamic, and analog and digital .
- Measurement can involve various physical quantities or attributes, such as length, mass, time, angle, temperature, force, pressure, velocity, acceleration, torque, power, energy, etc .
- Measurement can also involve geometrical features or quality characteristics of objects, such as squareness, roundness, roughness, parallelism, flatness, straightness, etc .
- Measurement requires the use of appropriate instruments or devices, which can be classified into different categories, such as contact and non-contact, mechanical and electrical, optical and acoustic, etc .
- Measurement involves the comparison of the quantity or attribute to be measured with a known standard or reference, which can be fixed or variable, primary or secondary, national or international, etc .
- Measurement is subject to various sources of error or uncertainty, which can be classified into different types, such as systematic and random, gross and human, instrumental and environmental, etc .
- Measurement can be expressed in different units or systems, such as SI (International System of Units), CGS (Centimeter-Gram-Second), FPS (Foot-Pound-Second), etc .
- Measurement can be analyzed and evaluated using different methods or techniques, such as statistical analysis, error propagation, uncertainty analysis, calibration, etc .
