### HEV drive train components

- A hybrid electric vehicle (HEV) is a type of hybrid vehicle that combines a conventional internal combustion engine (ICE) system with an electric propulsion system (hybrid vehicle drivetrain) .
- The main components of an HEV drivetrain are  :
  - Electric traction motors/controllers: These are devices that convert electrical energy into mechanical energy to drive the wheels of the vehicle. They also control the speed, torque and direction of the motor. Some HEVs use one motor, while others use two or more motors for different modes of operation.
  - Electric energy storage systems: These are devices that store electrical energy for later use. They can be batteries, ultracapacitors, flywheels or fuel cells. They provide power to the electric motor when needed, and also receive power from the motor or the ICE during regenerative braking or charging.
  - Internal combustion engine (ICE): This is a conventional engine that burns fuel to produce mechanical power. It can be gasoline, diesel, ethanol, natural gas or hydrogen. It can either directly drive the wheels, or act as a generator to charge the electric energy storage system or power the electric motor.
  - Fuel tank: This is a container that holds the fuel for the ICE. It can vary in size and shape depending on the type and amount of fuel used.
  - Control board: This is a device that manages the operation of the HEV drivetrain. It monitors the state of charge, temperature, voltage and current of the electric energy storage system, the speed, torque and power of the electric motor, the fuel level, temperature and emissions of the ICE, and the speed, acceleration and braking of the vehicle. It also determines the optimal mode of operation for the HEV drivetrain, such as series, parallel or power-split, and switches between them as needed.
- The HEV drivetrain can be classified into different types based on the configuration and connection of the components, such as :
  - Series hybrid: In this type, the ICE is only connected to a generator, which charges the electric energy storage system or powers the electric motor. The electric motor is the only device that drives the wheels. This type has the advantage of high efficiency and low emissions, but the disadvantage of high cost and weight.
  - Parallel hybrid: In this type, the ICE and the electric motor are both connected to the transmission, which drives the wheels. The electric motor can assist the ICE during acceleration or take over during low-speed or stop-start driving. The ICE can also charge the electric energy storage system or power the electric motor during cruising or deceleration. This type has the advantage of low cost and weight, but the disadvantage of lower efficiency and higher emissions.
  - Power-split hybrid: In this type, the ICE and the electric motor are both connected to a power-split device, such as a planetary gear set, which splits the power between the transmission and the generator. The transmission drives the wheels, while the generator charges the electric energy storage system or powers the electric motor. The electric motor can assist the ICE during acceleration or take over during low-speed or stop-start driving. The ICE can also charge the electric energy storage system or power the electric motor during cruising or deceleration. This type has the advantage of high efficiency and flexibility, but the disadvantage of high complexity and cost.
