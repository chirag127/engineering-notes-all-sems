### Factor of Safety

- Factor of safety (FoS) is a ratio of the maximum strength or load that a system can withstand to the intended design load or stress that the system is expected to carry.
- FoS expresses how much stronger a system is than it needs to be for an intended load. It is a measure of the reliability and safety of a system.
- FoS can be calculated using the formula:

    FoS = Maximum Strength / Design Load

- For a system to be considered safe, FoS needs to be greater than 1. A higher FoS means a higher margin of safety and a lower risk of failure.
- FoS depends on various factors, such as the type of material, the loading conditions, the environmental factors, the accuracy of calculations, the quality of manufacturing, the level of inspection, and the consequences of failure.
- FoS is also known as safety factor (SF) and is used interchangeably in engineering design.
