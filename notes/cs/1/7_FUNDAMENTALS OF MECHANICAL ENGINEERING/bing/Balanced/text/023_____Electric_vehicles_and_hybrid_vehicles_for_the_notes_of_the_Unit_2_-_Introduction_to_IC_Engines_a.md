### Electric vehicles and hybrid vehicles

- Electric vehicles (EVs) are vehicles that use an electric motor and a battery pack as the main source of propulsion. EVs do not have an internal combustion engine (ICE) or a fuel tank, and they do not emit tailpipe emissions. EVs can be recharged by plugging into an external power source, such as a wall outlet or a charging station .
- Hybrid vehicles are vehicles that use both an ICE and an electric motor as sources of propulsion. Hybrid vehicles have a battery pack that can store electricity generated by the ICE or by regenerative braking. Hybrid vehicles can switch between the ICE and the electric motor depending on the driving conditions and the battery level .
- Plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs) are a type of hybrid vehicle that can be plugged into an external power source to recharge the battery pack. PHEVs have a larger battery capacity and a longer electric range than conventional hybrid vehicles. PHEVs can operate as EVs when the battery is sufficiently charged, or as hybrid vehicles when the battery is depleted .
- The main advantages of EVs and PHEVs are that they can reduce greenhouse gas emissions, improve fuel economy, and lower operating costs compared to conventional vehicles. EVs and PHEVs can also benefit from incentives and subsidies from governments and utilities to promote their adoption .
- The main challenges of EVs and PHEVs are that they require a reliable and accessible charging infrastructure, have a higher upfront cost than conventional vehicles, and have a limited driving range and battery life compared to conventional vehicles. EVs and PHEVs also depend on the electricity grid and the sources of electricity generation, which may not be clean or renewable .
- Hybrid vehicles are a compromise between EVs and conventional vehicles, as they can offer some of the benefits of EVs, such as lower emissions and better fuel economy, while avoiding some of the drawbacks, such as limited range and high cost. However, hybrid vehicles still rely on fossil fuels and emit tailpipe emissions, and they have a more complex and heavier drivetrain than EVs .
