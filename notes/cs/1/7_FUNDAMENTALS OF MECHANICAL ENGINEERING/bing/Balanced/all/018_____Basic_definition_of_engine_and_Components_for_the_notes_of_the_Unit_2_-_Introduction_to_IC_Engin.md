# Basic definition of engine and components

- An engine is a machine that converts one or more forms of energy into mechanical energy that turns the road wheels.
- The most common type of engine in automobiles is the internal combustion engine, which burns fuel and air inside a cylinder to produce power.
- The essential parts of an internal combustion engine include the block, cylinder head, valves, pistons, and piston rings .
- The block is the main structure of the engine that contains the cylinders and the crankshaft.
- The cylinder head is the part that covers the top of the cylinders and houses the valves that let air and fuel mixture into the engine and exhaust gases out of the engine .
- The valves are controlled by a camshaft that opens and closes them at the right time for optimal power production .
- The pistons are cylindrical parts that move up and down inside the cylinders and compress the air and fuel mixture before ignition .
- The piston rings are metal rings that seal the gap between the pistons and the cylinder walls and prevent leakage of gas and oil .
- Other important parts of an internal combustion engine include the crankshaft, connecting rods, flywheel, spark plugs, fuel injectors, oil pump, water pump, and timing belt or chain  .
- The crankshaft is the part that converts the linear motion of the pistons into rotational motion that drives the wheels .
- The connecting rods are rods that connect the pistons to the crankshaft and transmit the force of the pistons to the crankshaft .
- The flywheel is a heavy metal disc that is attached to the crankshaft and helps to smooth out the fluctuations in the engine speed .
- The spark plugs are devices that create a spark to ignite the air and fuel mixture in the cylinders .
- The fuel injectors are devices that spray fuel into the cylinders in a precise amount and timing .
- The oil pump is a device that circulates oil throughout the engine to lubricate and cool the moving parts .
- The water pump is a device that circulates coolant throughout the engine to prevent overheating .
- The timing belt or chain is a device that synchronizes the movement of the camshaft and the crankshaft and ensures that the valves open and close at the correct time .
