# Electric vehicles and hybrid vehicles

- Electric vehicles (EVs) are vehicles that use an electric motor and a battery pack as the main source of propulsion. EVs do not have an internal combustion engine (ICE) or a fuel tank, and they do not emit tailpipe emissions. EVs can be recharged by plugging into an external power source, such as a wall outlet or a charging station .
- Hybrid vehicles are vehicles that use a combination of an ICE and an electric motor to provide propulsion. Hybrid vehicles have both a fuel tank and a battery pack, and they can switch between using gasoline and electricity depending on the driving conditions. Hybrid vehicles can reduce fuel consumption and emissions compared to conventional vehicles, but they still rely on fossil fuels .
- Plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs) are a type of hybrid vehicle that can be plugged into an external power source to recharge their battery pack. PHEVs have a larger battery capacity and a longer electric range than conventional hybrid vehicles, and they can operate as pure EVs when the battery is fully charged. PHEVs can offer the benefits of both EVs and hybrid vehicles, but they are more expensive and complex to maintain .
- Some of the advantages of EVs and hybrid vehicles are:
  - They can reduce greenhouse gas emissions and air pollution by using less or no gasoline .
  - They can lower fuel costs and increase energy security by using electricity from various sources, such as renewable energy .
  - They can improve performance and efficiency by using regenerative braking, which recovers kinetic energy and stores it in the battery .
  - They can offer a smoother and quieter ride than conventional vehicles, as they have fewer moving parts and less engine noise .
- Some of the challenges and limitations of EVs and hybrid vehicles are:
  - They have a higher upfront cost than conventional vehicles, as they require more advanced technology and materials .
  - They have a limited driving range and availability of charging infrastructure, especially for EVs, which can cause range anxiety and inconvenience for drivers .
  - They have a lower resale value and a shorter battery life than conventional vehicles, as they are subject to degradation and obsolescence over time .
  - They have a higher environmental impact in terms of battery production and disposal, as they use rare and toxic materials that can cause pollution and resource depletion .
