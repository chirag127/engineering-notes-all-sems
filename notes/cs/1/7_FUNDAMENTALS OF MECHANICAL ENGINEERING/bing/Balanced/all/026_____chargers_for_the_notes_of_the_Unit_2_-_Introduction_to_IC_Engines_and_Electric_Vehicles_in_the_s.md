# Chargers for Electric Vehicles

- Electric vehicles (EVs) need chargers to replenish their batteries and extend their driving range.
- There are different types and levels of chargers for EVs, depending on the power source, the charging speed, and the connector type.
- The main types of chargers for EVs are:

  - Level 1 chargers: These are the most common and basic chargers that can plug into a standard 120-volt (120V) AC wall outlet. They usually come with the vehicle at purchase and deliver about 1.4 kilowatts (kW) of charge, providing 4 miles of driving range per hour of charging. They are suitable for overnight or long-term charging at home or work, but they are not very fast or efficient.  
  - Level 2 chargers: These are faster and more efficient chargers that can plug into a 240-volt (240V) AC outlet or a dedicated charging station. They deliver about 3.3 to 19.2 kW of charge, providing 10 to 60 miles of driving range per hour of charging. They are suitable for daily or short-term charging at home, work, or public places. They require a special installation and may cost more than Level 1 chargers.  
  - Level 3 chargers: These are the fastest and most powerful chargers that can use a 480-volt (480V) DC current or a high-voltage AC current. They deliver about 50 to 350 kW of charge, providing 180 to 1000 miles of driving range per hour of charging. They are suitable for rapid or emergency charging on highways or long trips. They are also known as DC fast chargers or superchargers. They are not widely available and may be expensive to use.  

- The main types of connectors for EV chargers are:

  - SAE J1772: This is the standard connector for Level 1 and Level 2 chargers in the US. It is compatible with most EVs, except Tesla. It has five pins and a circular shape.  
  - Tesla: This is the proprietary connector for Tesla vehicles and chargers. It is compatible with Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 chargers, but only with Tesla vehicles. It has four pins and a flat shape.  
  - CHAdeMO: This is the standard connector for Level 3 chargers in Japan and some other countries. It is compatible with some EVs, such as Nissan Leaf and Mitsubishi Outlander. It has 10 pins and a D-shape.  
  - CCS: This is the standard connector for Level 3 chargers in Europe and some other countries. It is compatible with some EVs, such as BMW i3 and Volkswagen e-Golf. It has seven pins and a combined shape of SAE J1772 and DC pins.
