### Construction and Working of Domestic Refrigerator

A domestic refrigerator is a device that uses the vapor compression cycle to maintain a low temperature inside a closed cabinet. It is used to store and preserve food items, beverages, and other perishable goods.

The main components of a domestic refrigerator are:

- **Compressor**: It is a hermetically sealed unit that compresses the refrigerant gas and increases its pressure and temperature. It is located at the lower part of the refrigerator.
- **Condenser**: It is a fin and tube type heat exchanger that cools down the hot refrigerant gas and condenses it into a liquid. It is located at the back of the refrigerator and dissipates heat to the surrounding air.
- **Expansion valve**: It is a capillary tube that reduces the pressure and temperature of the liquid refrigerant and allows it to enter the evaporator. It is located between the condenser and the evaporator.
- **Evaporator**: It is a coil of copper tubing that absorbs heat from the inside of the refrigerator and evaporates the liquid refrigerant into a gas. It is located in the freezer box and maintains a temperature of about -25°C.
- **Thermostat**: It is a device that controls the temperature inside the refrigerator by switching the compressor on and off according to the set point. It is located inside the refrigerator and senses the temperature of the air.
- **Accumulator**: It is a vessel that collects any liquid refrigerant that may have escaped from the evaporator and prevents it from entering the compressor. It is located near the compressor and protects it from damage.
- **Drier and strainer**: They are devices that remove any moisture and impurities from the refrigerant and prevent them from clogging the capillary tube. They are located before the expansion valve and after the condenser.

The working principle of a domestic refrigerator is as follows:

- The compressor draws the low-pressure refrigerant gas from the evaporator and compresses it to a high pressure and temperature.
- The hot refrigerant gas flows through the condenser and releases its heat to the ambient air. The refrigerant gas condenses into a liquid and flows through the drier and strainer.
- The liquid refrigerant passes through the expansion valve and undergoes a sudden drop in pressure and temperature. The refrigerant becomes a cold liquid-vapor mixture and enters the evaporator.
- The cold refrigerant flows through the evaporator coils and absorbs heat from the air and the items inside the refrigerator. The refrigerant evaporates into a gas and lowers the temperature of the refrigerator.
- The refrigerant gas returns to the compressor and the cycle repeats.
