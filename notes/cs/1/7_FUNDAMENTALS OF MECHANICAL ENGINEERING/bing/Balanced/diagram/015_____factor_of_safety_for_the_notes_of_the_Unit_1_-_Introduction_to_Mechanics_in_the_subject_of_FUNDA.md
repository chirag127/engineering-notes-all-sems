### Factor of Safety

- Factor of safety (FoS) is a ratio of the maximum strength or load that a system can withstand to the intended design load or stress that the system is expected to carry .
- FoS indicates how much stronger a system is than it needs to be for a given load.
- FoS can be calculated using the formula:

    FoS = Maximum Strength / Design Load

- For a system to be considered safe, FoS should be greater than 1 .
- FoS is used to account for uncertainties in design, material properties, manufacturing, testing, and loading conditions.
- FoS can vary depending on the type of material, system, and application.
- FoS can also be expressed as a percentage of the design load or stress that the system can tolerate before failure.
- FoS can be increased by using stronger or more durable materials, increasing the cross-sectional area or thickness of the system, reducing the design load or stress, or adding redundancy or backup systems.
