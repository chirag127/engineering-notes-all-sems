### Refrigeration

- Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a substance or a space and transferring it to a higher-temperature reservoir  .
- Refrigeration is an artificial or human-made cooling method that is used for various purposes such as preserving food, cooling beverages, making ice, air conditioning, industrial processes, etc.
- Refrigeration can be achieved by different methods, but the most common ones are based on the vapor compression cycle or the vapor absorption cycle .
- In the vapor compression cycle, a refrigerant (a substance that can change its phase from liquid to vapor and vice versa) is compressed, condensed, expanded, and evaporated in a closed loop, absorbing heat from the low-temperature space and rejecting it to the high-temperature space .
- In the vapor absorption cycle, a refrigerant and an absorbent (a substance that can dissolve the refrigerant) are used in a similar loop, but the compression of the refrigerant is replaced by the absorption and desorption of the refrigerant by the absorbent, which requires a heat source .
- Refrigerants are classified into different groups based on their chemical composition, such as ammonia, hydrocarbons, halocarbons, etc .
- Refrigerants should have certain properties, such as low boiling point, high latent heat, low toxicity, low flammability, high stability, low environmental impact, etc .
- Refrigeration has many advantages, such as extending the shelf life of food, improving the comfort and health of people, enhancing the efficiency and quality of industrial processes, etc .
- Refrigeration also has some disadvantages, such as high energy consumption, greenhouse gas emissions, ozone depletion, noise pollution, etc .
- Refrigeration is a vital and growing field of engineering and science that has many applications and challenges in the modern world   .
