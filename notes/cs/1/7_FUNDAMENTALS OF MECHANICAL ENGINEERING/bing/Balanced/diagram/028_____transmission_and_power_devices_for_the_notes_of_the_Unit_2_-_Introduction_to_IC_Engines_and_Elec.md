### Transmission and Power Devices for IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

- Transmission is a system that transmits the power from the engine or motor to the wheels of a vehicle. It consists of various components such as clutch, gearbox, driveshaft, differential, and axle.
- Power devices are the components that convert, regulate, or control the electrical energy in a vehicle. They include alternator, battery, motor, inverter, and power control unit.
- IC engines and electric vehicles have different transmission and power devices, depending on their energy source and architecture.

#### Transmission and Power Devices for IC Engines

- IC engines use fuel (such as gasoline or diesel) to generate mechanical power through combustion. The power is then transmitted to the wheels through a clutch, a gearbox, and a differential.
- Clutch is a device that connects or disconnects the engine and the gearbox. It allows the driver to change gears smoothly and to start or stop the vehicle.
- Gearbox is a device that changes the speed and torque of the engine output. It has different gear ratios that can be selected by the driver or automatically. It helps the engine to operate efficiently and to match the vehicle speed and load.
- Driveshaft is a shaft that transfers the power from the gearbox to the differential. It can be a single piece or a two-piece shaft with a universal joint or a constant velocity joint.
- Differential is a device that splits the power from the driveshaft to the two wheels of the same axle. It allows the wheels to rotate at different speeds when the vehicle turns. It also provides a final gear reduction to increase the torque at the wheels.
- Axle is a shaft that connects the differential and the wheel. It can be a solid axle or an independent axle, depending on the suspension system of the vehicle.
- Alternator is a device that converts the mechanical power from the engine to electrical power. It charges the battery and supplies power to the electrical devices of the vehicle.
- Battery is a device that stores electrical energy and provides power to the starter motor, the ignition system, and the electrical devices of the vehicle when the engine is off or the alternator is not sufficient.
- Starter motor is a device that uses electrical power from the battery to crank the engine and start the combustion process.
- Ignition system is a system that generates a spark to ignite the fuel-air mixture in the engine cylinders. It consists of components such as spark plugs, ignition coil, distributor, and ignition switch.

#### Transmission and Power Devices for Electric Vehicles

- Electric vehicles use electricity to generate mechanical power through an electric motor. The power is then transmitted to the wheels through a single-speed gearbox and a differential.
- Electric motor is a device that converts electrical energy to mechanical energy. It can be a direct current (DC) motor or an alternating current (AC) motor, depending on the type of power supply and the controller.
- Single-speed gearbox is a device that provides a fixed gear ratio between the motor and the differential. It reduces the speed and increases the torque of the motor output. It eliminates the need for a clutch and a multi-speed gearbox, as the motor can operate at a wide range of speeds and torques.
- Driveshaft, differential, and axle are similar to those of IC engines, except that they may have different specifications and configurations to suit the electric vehicle performance and design.
- Battery is a device that stores electrical energy and provides power to the motor and the electrical devices of the vehicle. It can be composed of different types of cells, such as lead-acid, nickel-metal hydride, lithium-ion, or lithium-polymer.
- On-board charger is a device that converts the alternating current (AC) from the external power source to direct current (DC) to charge the battery. It can have different power levels and charging modes, such as slow, fast, or rapid charging.
- Electric power control unit (EPCU) is a device that controls the power flow between the battery, the motor, and the on-board charger. It consists of components such as an inverter, a converter, and a controller.
- Inverter is a device that converts the DC power from the battery to AC power for the motor. It also controls the frequency and amplitude of the AC power to vary the speed and torque of the motor.
- Converter is a device that converts the AC power from the on-board charger to DC power for the battery. It also regulates the voltage and current of the DC power to optimize the charging process and protect the battery.
