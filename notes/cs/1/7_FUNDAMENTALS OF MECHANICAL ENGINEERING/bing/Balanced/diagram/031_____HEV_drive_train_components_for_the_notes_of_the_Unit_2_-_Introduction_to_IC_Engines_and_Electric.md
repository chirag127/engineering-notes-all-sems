### HEV drive train components

A hybrid electric vehicle (HEV) is a type of hybrid vehicle that combines a conventional internal combustion engine (ICE) system with an electric propulsion system (hybrid vehicle drivetrain). The presence of the electric powertrain is intended to achieve either better fuel economy than a conventional vehicle or better performance.

The main components of an HEV drivetrain are :

- **Electric traction motors/controllers**: These are the devices that convert electrical energy from the battery or other sources into mechanical energy to drive the wheels. They also control the speed and torque of the motor according to the driver's demand and the vehicle's operating conditions. The motors can be either AC or DC, and the controllers can be either voltage-source or current-source inverters.
- **Electric energy storage systems**: These are the devices that store electrical energy for later use by the electric motor or other loads. They can be either batteries or ultracapacitors, or a combination of both. Batteries have high energy density but low power density, while ultracapacitors have high power density but low energy density. The choice of the storage system depends on the design and performance requirements of the HEV.
- **Internal combustion engine (ICE)**: This is the conventional power source that burns fuel (such as gasoline or diesel) to produce mechanical energy. The ICE can be either spark-ignition or compression-ignition, and can have different configurations (such as inline, V, or W). The ICE can also be equipped with various technologies to improve its efficiency and reduce its emissions, such as variable valve timing, turbocharging, or direct injection.
- **Fuel tank**: This is the device that stores the fuel for the ICE. The fuel tank can be either metal or plastic, and can have different shapes and sizes depending on the vehicle's design and space constraints. The fuel tank can also have various features to prevent fuel leakage, evaporation, or contamination, such as seals, vents, or filters.
- **Control board**: This is the device that coordinates the operation of the ICE and the electric motor, and manages the energy flow between the storage system and the loads. The control board can be either a microcontroller or a microprocessor, and can have different algorithms and strategies to optimize the performance and efficiency of the HEV. The control board can also communicate with other systems and sensors in the vehicle, such as the brake, throttle, or transmission.
