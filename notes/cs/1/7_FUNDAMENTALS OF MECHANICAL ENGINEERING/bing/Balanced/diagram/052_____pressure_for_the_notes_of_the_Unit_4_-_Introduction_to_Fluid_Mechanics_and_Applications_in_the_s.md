### Pressure

- Pressure is the physical force exerted on an object per unit area    .
- Pressure is denoted by the capital letter P or the lowercase letter p .
- The basic formula for pressure is P = F / A, where F is the force and A is the area    .
- The SI unit of pressure is the Pascal (Pa), where 1 Pa = 1 N/m^2^, i.e. one Newton per square meter .
- Pressure can be increased by increasing the force or decreasing the area, and vice versa .
- Pressure can be measured using various instruments, such as barometers, manometers, gauges, etc.
- Pressure can be classified into different types, such as atmospheric pressure, gauge pressure, absolute pressure, hydrostatic pressure, etc.
- Pressure is an important concept in fluid mechanics, as it affects the flow and behavior of fluids .
