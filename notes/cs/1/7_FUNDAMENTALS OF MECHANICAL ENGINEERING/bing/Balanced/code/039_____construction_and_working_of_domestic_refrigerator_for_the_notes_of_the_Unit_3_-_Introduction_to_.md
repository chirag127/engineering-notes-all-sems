### Construction and working of domestic refrigerator

A domestic refrigerator is a device that uses the vapor compression cycle to maintain a low temperature inside a closed cabinet and preserve food items. The main components of a domestic refrigerator are:

- A hermetically sealed compressor that compresses the refrigerant gas and increases its pressure and temperature .
- A fin and tube type evaporator that absorbs heat from the air inside the cabinet and evaporates the liquid refrigerant into gas .
- An accumulator that collects any liquid refrigerant that may have escaped from the evaporator and prevents it from entering the compressor.
- A thermostat that controls the temperature inside the cabinet by switching the compressor on and off as needed.
- An air-cooled condenser that releases the heat from the hot refrigerant gas to the surrounding air and condenses it into liquid .
- A capillary tube that acts as an expansion device and reduces the pressure and temperature of the liquid refrigerant before it enters the evaporator .
- A drier and strainer that removes any moisture and impurities from the refrigerant and protects the system from corrosion and clogging.

The working principle of a domestic refrigerator is as follows :

- The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas (usually R-134a or R-600a) and sends it to the condenser.
- The condenser coils on the back of the refrigerator let the hot refrigerant gas dissipate its heat to the ambient air and change into liquid.
- The liquid refrigerant flows through the capillary tube and experiences a sudden drop in pressure and temperature.
- The cold liquid refrigerant enters the evaporator coils inside the freezer box and absorbs heat from the contents of the refrigerator, making them cold. The refrigerant also changes into gas as it evaporates.
- The gas refrigerant returns to the compressor through the accumulator and the cycle repeats.

The thermostat monitors the temperature inside the refrigerator and switches the compressor on and off to maintain the desired level of cooling. The refrigerator also has a defrost system that periodically melts the ice that forms on the evaporator coils and drains it away.
