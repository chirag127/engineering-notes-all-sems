### Refrigeration

Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a substance, space or system and transferring it to another place, where it is rejected or used for some purpose. Refrigeration is used to lower and/or maintain the temperature of the substance, space or system below the ambient temperature. Refrigeration is an artificial or human-made cooling method.

Some of the uses of refrigeration are:

- To preserve food and beverages by slowing down the growth of microorganisms and chemical reactions.
- To provide thermal comfort for humans and animals by cooling the air in buildings, vehicles and other spaces.
- To enable industrial processes that require low temperatures, such as liquefaction of gases, cryogenic applications, ice making, etc.
- To improve the performance and efficiency of some devices, such as electronic components, power plants, etc.

There are two main types of refrigeration systems:

- Vapor compression refrigeration system: This system uses a refrigerant, which is a fluid that changes its phase from liquid to vapor and back, to absorb and release heat. The refrigerant is compressed, condensed, expanded and evaporated in a closed cycle, while exchanging heat with the surroundings. This system is the most common and widely used type of refrigeration system .
- Vapor absorption refrigeration system: This system also uses a refrigerant, but instead of compressing it mechanically, it uses a secondary fluid, called an absorbent, to absorb and release the refrigerant. The refrigerant and the absorbent form a solution, which is separated by heat and pressure. This system is less efficient and more complex than the vapor compression system, but it can use low-grade heat sources, such as solar energy, waste heat, etc.

Refrigerants are the fluids that are used in refrigeration systems to carry heat from one place to another. Refrigerants have different properties, such as boiling point, pressure, density, specific heat, etc., that affect their performance and suitability for different applications. Refrigerants are classified into different groups, based on their chemical composition, such as:

- Natural refrigerants: These are substances that occur naturally in the environment, such as water, air, ammonia, carbon dioxide, etc. They have low or zero global warming potential (GWP) and ozone depletion potential (ODP), but they may have some drawbacks, such as toxicity, flammability, low efficiency, etc.
- Synthetic refrigerants: These are substances that are artificially produced by chemical reactions, such as chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs), hydrochlorofluorocarbons (HCFCs), hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), etc. They have high efficiency and compatibility with various materials, but they also have high GWP and ODP, which contribute to the greenhouse effect and the depletion of the ozone layer.
- Blended refrigerants: These are mixtures of two or more refrigerants, which are designed to have desirable properties, such as low GWP and ODP, high efficiency, etc. They may have different compositions, such as azeotropic, zeotropic or near-azeotropic, depending on their behavior during phase change.

Some of the advantages of refrigeration systems are:

- They can provide cooling at any desired temperature and for any duration, unlike natural cooling methods, such as evaporation, radiation, etc.
- They can improve the quality and safety of food and beverages, by preventing spoilage and contamination.
- They can enhance the comfort and health of humans and animals, by reducing heat stress and humidity.
- They can enable new technologies and innovations, such as superconductivity, cryosurgery, etc.

Some of the disadvantages of refrigeration systems are:

- They consume a lot of energy and resources, which may have negative impacts on the environment and the economy.
- They may cause noise and vibration, which may affect the surroundings and the users.
- They may leak or malfunction, which may result in loss of cooling, damage to the equipment and the environment, and safety hazards.
