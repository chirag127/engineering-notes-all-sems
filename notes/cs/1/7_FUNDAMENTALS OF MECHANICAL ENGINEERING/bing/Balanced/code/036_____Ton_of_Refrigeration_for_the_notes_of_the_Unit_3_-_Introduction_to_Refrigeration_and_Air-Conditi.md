### Ton of Refrigeration

- A ton of refrigeration (TR or TOR), also called a refrigeration ton (RT), is a unit of power used in some countries (especially in North America) to describe the heat-extraction capacity of refrigeration and air conditioning equipment .
- It is defined as the rate of heat transfer that results in the freezing or melting of 1 short ton (2,000 lb; 907 kg) of pure ice at 0 °C (32 °F) in 24 hours .
- A ton of refrigeration is equivalent to 211 kJ/min or 200 Btu/min .
- A refrigeration system with a cooling capacity of 1 TR can remove 211 kJ of heat from a substance every minute, or 12,660 kJ every hour, or 303,840 kJ every day.
- The cooling load of a refrigeration system is usually expressed in tons of refrigeration, or simply tons. For example, a building that requires 300,000 Btu/h of cooling has a cooling load of 300,000 / 12,000 = 25 tons.
