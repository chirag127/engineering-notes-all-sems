- HV stands for high voltage, which is a type of electrical power transmission system that uses higher voltages than the standard low voltage systems.
- HV systems have several advantages over low voltage systems, such as  :
  - Reduced size and weight of generators, motors, cables, transformers and other electrical components, which saves space, material and cost.
  - Lower current for the same power output, which reduces the power loss, voltage drop and heating effect in the transmission line, and improves the voltage regulation and power efficiency.
  - Higher capacity to transfer large amounts of power over long distances, which enables interconnection of grids and integration of renewable energy sources.
  - Lower capital and maintenance costs, as HV systems require fewer substations, towers and conductors than low voltage systems.
- HV systems are also used in hybrid vehicles, which are vehicles that combine an internal combustion engine (ICE) and an electric motor powered by a high voltage battery pack.
- Hybrid vehicles have several advantages over conventional ICE vehicles, such as:
  - Better fuel efficiency and lower carbon emissions, as hybrid vehicles can switch between the ICE and the electric motor depending on the driving conditions and conserve more energy.
  - Less dependency on fossil fuels, as hybrid vehicles can use the electric motor for propulsion and reduce the fuel consumption and emissions of the ICE.
  - Higher performance and reliability, as hybrid vehicles can use the electric motor to assist the ICE during acceleration and braking, and reduce the wear and tear of the ICE.
  - Potential savings and incentives, as hybrid vehicles can reduce the fuel and maintenance costs, and benefit from tax credits and rebates in some countries.
