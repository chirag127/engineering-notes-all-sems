### Force (Proving Ring)

- Force is the reaction between two bodies or components, which can be either tensile (pull) or compressive (push) .
- A proving ring is a device that measures force by the deformation of a calibrated elastic ring .
- A proving ring consists of a steel ring with a circular cross-section, two knife edges attached to the ring at opposite ends, and a dial gauge or a load cell to measure the displacement or the load at the center of the ring .
- When a force is applied to the knife edges, the ring deforms elastically and the displacement or the load at the center is proportional to the applied force .
- A proving ring can be used to calibrate other force-measuring devices, such as platform balances or hydraulic jacks .
- A proving ring has advantages such as high accuracy, simplicity, and durability, but also disadvantages such as sensitivity to temperature, alignment, and friction .

### Torque (Prony Brake Dynamometer)

- Torque is the turning force applied to a rotating shaft, which is the product of the force and the distance from the axis of rotation  .
- A dynamometer is a device that measures the torque or the power transmitted by a rotating shaft .
- A Prony brake is a type of dynamometer that uses friction to measure the torque produced by an engine or a motor    .
- A Prony brake consists of a wooden or metal block that clamps around the shaft, a lever arm attached to the block, and a spring balance or a weight at the end of the lever arm   .
- When the shaft rotates, the block exerts a frictional force on the shaft, which is balanced by the force measured by the spring balance or the weight at the end of the lever arm   .
- The torque is calculated by multiplying the force measured by the lever arm length   .
- The power is calculated by multiplying the torque by the angular speed of the shaft  .
- A Prony brake has advantages such as low cost, simplicity, and versatility, but also disadvantages such as low efficiency, high wear, and limited range   .
