### Fluids properties for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Fluids are substances that flow and take the shape of their container. In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications, the properties of fluids are studied in detail. Some important properties of fluids include:

1. Density: The mass per unit volume of a fluid.

2. Pressure: The force per unit area exerted by a fluid.

3. Viscosity: The resistance of a fluid to flow.

4. Surface Tension: The property of a fluid that causes it to act as a thin, elastic sheet.

5. Compressibility: The ability of a fluid to change its volume under pressure.

6. Vapor Pressure: The pressure exerted by a fluid in its vapor state.

Understanding these properties is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as they form the basis for many advanced topics in fluid mechanics and thermodynamics. These properties are used to analyze and design fluid systems, including pumps, turbines, and heat exchangers, and are used in a wide range of applications, including automotive engineering, aerospace engineering, and energy systems.
