## Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications

Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications is a topic in the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering that deals with the study of the behavior of fluids at rest and in motion. Fluid mechanics is a branch of physics that deals with the study of fluids and their properties, including density, viscosity, and pressure.

In this unit, students learn about the basic principles of fluid mechanics, including fluid statics, fluid dynamics, and fluid kinematics. They also learn about the different types of fluid flow, including laminar flow and turbulent flow, and the equations that describe fluid flow, such as the Navier-Stokes equations.

In addition to the study of fluid mechanics, students also learn about the applications of fluid mechanics in various fields, including aerodynamics, hydrodynamics, and biomedical engineering.

The study of fluid mechanics is important for students who plan to pursue careers in mechanical engineering, as fluid mechanics is widely used in many industries, including aerospace, energy, and transportation. Understanding the principles and applications of fluid mechanics is essential for engineers to design and implement efficient and reliable fluid systems.
