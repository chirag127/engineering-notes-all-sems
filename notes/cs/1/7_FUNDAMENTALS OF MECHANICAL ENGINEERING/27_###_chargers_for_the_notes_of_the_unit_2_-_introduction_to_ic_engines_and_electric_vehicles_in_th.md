Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles:

Unit 2 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the introduction to Internal Combustion (IC) Engines and Electric Vehicles. It is important to understand the concepts of IC engines and electric vehicles in order to understand the principles of mechanical engineering.

1. Internal Combustion Engines: An internal combustion engine is a type of engine that uses fuel and air to produce energy. The fuel is burned inside the engine, converting the chemical energy into mechanical energy.

2. Types of IC Engines: There are two main types of internal combustion engines: reciprocating engines and rotary engines. Reciprocating engines use a piston to convert the energy into mechanical energy, while rotary engines use a rotor to convert the energy into mechanical energy.

3. Electric Vehicles: An electric vehicle is a type of vehicle that uses an electric motor to produce energy. Electric vehicles are powered by batteries and do not use fuel.

4. Types of Electric Vehicles: There are two main types of electric vehicles: pure electric vehicles and hybrid electric vehicles. Pure electric vehicles are powered solely by batteries, while hybrid electric vehicles use both an electric motor and an internal combustion engine.

5. Advantages and Disadvantages: Both internal combustion engines and electric vehicles have advantages and disadvantages. Internal combustion engines are more widely available and have a longer range, but they produce emissions and require fuel. Electric vehicles produce no emissions and are more environmentally friendly, but they have a shorter range and are more expensive.

In conclusion, Unit 2 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering covers the introduction to internal combustion engines and electric vehicles. It is important to understand the concepts of internal combustion engines, including the types of engines and their advantages and disadvantages, as well as the types of electric vehicles and their advantages and disadvantages.
