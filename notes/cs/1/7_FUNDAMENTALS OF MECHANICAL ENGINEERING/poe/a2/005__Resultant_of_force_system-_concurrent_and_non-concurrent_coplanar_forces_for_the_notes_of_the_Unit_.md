### Resultant of force system- concurrent and non-concurrent coplanar forces

- Concurrent coplanar forces: When multiple forces acting on a body intersect at a common point, they are called concurrent coplanar forces. The resultant of such a system can be determined by taking the vector sum of individual forces. As the forces act through a common point, the magnitude and direction of the resultant force can be determined without considering the moment of forces about any point.
- Non-concurrent coplanar forces: When forces acting on a body lie in a same plane but their lines of action do not meet at a common point, they are called non-concurrent coplanar forces. The resultant force of such a system can be determined by:

1. Resolving each force into components along two perpendicular axes lying in the plane
2. Taking the algebraic sum of the resolved components of forces along each axis to determine the magnitude and direction of the resultant force
3. Constructing the resultant force using the determined magnitude and direction

Additionally, the point of application of the resultant force can be determined by considering the moment balance of forces about any point.

The above points describe the procedure to determine the resultant of force system consisting of concurrent and non-concurrent coplanar forces. The concepts are important to understand the dynamics of particles and rigid bodies.
