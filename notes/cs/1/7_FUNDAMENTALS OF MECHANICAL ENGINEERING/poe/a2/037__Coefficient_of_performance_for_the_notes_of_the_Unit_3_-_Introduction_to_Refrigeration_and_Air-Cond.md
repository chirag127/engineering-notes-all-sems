### Coefficient of performance for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Coefficient of performance (COP) is the ratio of useful heat removal to the work required for a refrigeration cycle.
2. A higher COP indicates a more efficient system since less work input is required to achieve the same amount of cooling.
3. The COP depends on the properties of the refrigerant and the thermodynamic process in the refrigeration cycle.
4. For an ideal cycle, the maximum COP is determined by the temperature levels of the cycle (the lower the temperature difference, the higher the COP).
5. In actual systems, the COP is lower than the ideal due to irreversibilities such as pressure drops, heat transfer across a finite temperature difference, electrical motor inefficiencies, etc.
6. An air-conditioning system typically has a lower COP than a refrigeration system because it has to remove latent heat in addition to sensible heat, and it operates over a smaller temperature range.
