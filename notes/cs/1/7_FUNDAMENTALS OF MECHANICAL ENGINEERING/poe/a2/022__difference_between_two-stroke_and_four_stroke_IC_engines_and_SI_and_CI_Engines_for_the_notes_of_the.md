### Difference between two-stroke and four stroke IC engines and SI and CI Engines

Four stroke IC engine:

- Four strokes namely intake, compression, power and exhaust strokes complete in two revolutions of crankshaft.
- Intake and exhaust valves are present.
- More efficient and produces less emissions.
- Examples: Petrol and diesel engines.

Two stroke IC engine:

- Two strokes namely power and exhaust strokes complete in one revolution of crankshaft.
- No intake and exhaust valves. Ports are used.
- Less efficient and produces more emissions.
- Examples: Petrol engines in scooters and motorcycles.

SI engine:

- Uses spark ignition where air-fuel mixture is ignited by spark plug.
- Used in petrol engines.
- Higher compression ratio.

CI engine:

- Uses compression ignition where air-fuel mixture is ignited due to high temperature and pressure.
- Used in diesel engines.
- Higher compression ratio.
