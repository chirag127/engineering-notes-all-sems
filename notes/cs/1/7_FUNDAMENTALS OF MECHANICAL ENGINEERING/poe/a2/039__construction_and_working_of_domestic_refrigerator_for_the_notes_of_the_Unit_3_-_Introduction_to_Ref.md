### Construction and working of domestic refrigerator

1. Cabinet: The cabinet is the outer body of the refrigerator. It is made up of sheet metal with insulating material like polyurethane foam in between the double walls. The cabinet has a door to access the inside and shelves to store food items.
2. Cooling unit: The cooling unit consists of the compressor, condenser coil, expansion valve and evaporator coil.
3. Compressor: The compressor is a pump which compresses the refrigerant vapour and circulates it through the cooling system. It is located at the bottom of the refrigerator and makes a humming sound.
4. Condenser coil: The condenser coil is a long coiled tube located at the back of the refrigerator. The hot compressed refrigerant vapour from the compressor enters the condenser coil. Here, due to the cooling fan, the vapour loses heat and converts into liquid.
5. Expansion valve: The expansion valve is a throttling device located between the condenser coil and evaporator coil. The liquid refrigerant from the condenser coil enters the expansion valve. Here, its pressure drops suddenly, resulting in flashing of some liquid into vapour. This mixture enters the evaporator coil.
6. Evaporator coil: The evaporator coil is a coiled tube located inside the refrigerator. The mixture of liquid and vapour refrigerant from the expansion valve enters the evaporator coil. Here, due to the low pressure, the liquid boils and absorbs heat from the surroundings to get converted into vapour. This cools the refrigerator and maintains a low temperature.
7. Thermoelectric cooling: Some refrigerators use thermoelectric cooling instead of vapor-compression refrigeration. It uses electricity to produce cooling through thermoelectric effect. Such refrigerators are more environment-friendly but have higher power consumption and less efficiency.
