### Transmission and Power Devices

- Clutches: Used to connect and disconnect two rotating shafts and transmit torque. Types: Friction clutch, Centrifugal clutch, Hydraulic clutch, Electromagnetic clutch.
- Gear boxes: Used to change the speed and torque of a rotating shaft. Types: Manual gearbox, Automatic gearbox, Continuously variable transmission (CVT).
- Shafts: Used to transmit power between two components. Types: Solid shafts, Hollow shafts, Universal joints, Gear shafts.
- Belts and pulleys: Used to transmit power between two parallel shafts. Types: Flat belt drive, V-belt drive.
- Chains and sprockets: Used to transmit power between two parallel shafts. Types: Roller chain drive.
