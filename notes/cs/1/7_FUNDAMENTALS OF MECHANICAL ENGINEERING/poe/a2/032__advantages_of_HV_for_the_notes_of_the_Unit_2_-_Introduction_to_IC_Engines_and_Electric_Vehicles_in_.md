### Advantages of Hybrid Vehicles (HV)

1. Higher Fuel Efficiency: HVs have both IC engine and electric motor which can power the vehicle independently or together depending on the driving conditions. This results in higher fuel efficiency and lower emissions.
2. Reduced Emissions: HVs produce lower emissions as the electric motor is used under low load conditions which produces zero direct emissions. The IC engine is used only when required resulting in overall lower emissions.
3. Quick Acceleration: The electric motor provides high torque at low RPM resulting in quick acceleration. This provides good drivability and performance.
4. Regenerative Braking: The electric motor acts as a generator during braking and recovers some of the kinetic energy as electric energy which is stored in batteries. This increases the efficiency and range of HVs.
5. Flexibility: HVs provide the flexibility of both gasoline and electric vehicles. The vehicle can be operated in different modes depending on the driving conditions and requirements.
