### Principle of Transmissibility

- The internal forces between two particles of a body are equal action-reaction force pairs and their line of action passes through the particles.
- If the line of action of an internal force remains unchanged, the force is said to be transmitted across the body. This is known as the principle of transmissibility of force.
- The internal forces are transmitted from particle to particle within the body along their lines of action. The transmitted forces across any section of the body must be in equilibrium.
- The principle of transmissibility holds good only if the shape and size of the body do not change. If the shape or size of the body changes due to applied loads, the lines of action of internal forces also change. In such situations, the principle of transmissibility does not hold good.
