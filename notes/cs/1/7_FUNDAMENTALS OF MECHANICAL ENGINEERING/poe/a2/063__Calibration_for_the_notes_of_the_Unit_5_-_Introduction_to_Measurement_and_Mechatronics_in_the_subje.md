### Calibration for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Calibration is the process of comparing the reading of an instrument with the standard to detect and adjust any discrepancy.
2. The standard used can be a certified instrument, physical constant or certified reference material.
3. Calibration is done to ensure the accuracy and precision of the instrument.
4. Calibration may be done for a single point or for a range of values (multi-point calibration).
5. The calibration is valid only for a specified range of use and needs to be re-done if there are changes in the environmental conditions.
6. It is necessary to calibrate instruments at regular intervals or when there are deviations in observations.
7. The calibration records should be properly maintained.
8. The calibration status of the instrument should be considered while taking measurements and interpreting the results.
