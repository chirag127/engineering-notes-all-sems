### Working principles of hydraulic turbines (Pelton Wheel and Francis)& pumps (Centrifugal and Reciprocating) and their classifications and hydraulic lift for the notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Hydraulic Turbines
- Pelton Wheel: Uses impulse action. Jets of water strike the blades of the turbine. Suitable for high heads and low flow rates.
- Francis Turbine: Uses pressure and kinetic energy of water. Suitable for medium heads and medium to high flow rates.
- Classification: According to type of energy conversion (impulse and reaction) and direction of flow (tangential and radial)

2. Hydraulic Pumps
- Centrifugal Pump: Uses centrifugal action to pump water. Suitable for low to medium heads and high flow rates.
- Reciprocating Pump: Uses pistons to pump water. Suitable for high heads and low flow rates.
- Classification: According to mechanism of pumping (centrifugal and reciprocating)

3. Hydraulic Lift
- Used to lift water from lower elevations to higher elevations.
- Uses Pascal's law which states that pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is transmitted equally in all directions.
- Uses one of the above types of pumps to achieve the required lift.
