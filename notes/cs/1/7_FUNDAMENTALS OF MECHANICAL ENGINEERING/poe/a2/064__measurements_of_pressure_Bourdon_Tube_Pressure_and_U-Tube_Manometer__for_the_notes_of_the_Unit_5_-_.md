### Measurements of Pressure (Bourdon Tube Pressure and U-Tube Manometer)

1. Bourdon Tube Pressure Gauge
- A coiled hollow tube with an oval cross-section is used.
- One end of the tube is connected to the pressure source.
- As pressure increases, the tube tends to straighten out.
- This motion is magnified and displayed on a calibrated scale.
- It is used for measuring fluid pressures above atmospheric pressure.

2. U-Tube Manometer
- It consists of a U-shaped glass tube filled with a liquid.
- The two arms of the tube are connected to the two pressure sources.
- The difference in liquid levels gives the pressure difference between the two sources.
- The liquid used is generally mercury or coloured water.
- It is suitable for measuring pressure differences or vacuum.
