### Comfort Conditions for Notes of Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning

1. Thermal Comfort: The condition of mind that expresses satisfaction with the thermal environment. It is the state of mind that causes a person to neither feel too hot nor too cold.
2. Factors Affecting Thermal Comfort:
- Temperature: The surrounding temperature affects comfort. Optimum temperature range for comfort is 68-72°F or 20-22°C with 50-60% relative humidity.
- Humidity: Both high and low humidity levels affect comfort. ideal humidity levels are in the range of 40-60% RH.
- Air Velocity: High air velocities can lead to wind chill and decreased comfort. Ideal values are below 80 fpm or 0.4 m/s.
- Radiation: Radiant heat gains or losses can affect comfort due to surrounding surfaces and their temperatures.
- Metabolic Rate: Activity level affects heat generated by the body which impacts comfort.
3. Comfort Charts: Charts showing combinations of temperature and humidity which produce comfort for a given activity level. An example is the ASHRAE comfort chart.
4. Comfort Zones: Areas on a psychrometric chart indicating combinations of temperature and humidity which produce thermal comfort. Position of comfort zone depends on activity level.
