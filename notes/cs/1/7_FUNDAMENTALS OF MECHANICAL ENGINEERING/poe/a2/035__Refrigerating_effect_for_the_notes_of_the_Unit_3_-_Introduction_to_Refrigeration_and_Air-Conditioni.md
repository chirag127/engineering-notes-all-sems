### Refrigerating effect for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. The refrigerating effect is the amount of heat removed from the refrigerated space or substance. It is measured in joules (J) or kilojoules (kJ) or British thermal units (Btu).
2. The coefficient of performance (COP) of a refrigeration system is the ratio of the refrigerating effect to the work required to produce it. A higher COP indicates a more efficient system.
3. The capacity of a refrigeration system is the maximum amount of refrigerating effect that it can produce. It is usually measured in ton of refrigeration (TR) where 1 TR = 3.52 kW or 12,000 Btu/h.
4. The evaporator is the part of the refrigeration system where the refrigerant evaporates by absorbing heat from the refrigerated space. The refrigerated space is in thermal contact with the evaporator.
5. The condenser is where the refrigerant vapor releases heat and condenses into liquid. The heat released is dissipated into the surrounding medium with the help of a fan.
