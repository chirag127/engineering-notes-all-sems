### Introduction

1. Fluid Mechanics is the study of forces that act on objects immersed in or moving through fluids and the effects of those forces.
2. Fluids are substances that deform continuously under the action of shearing forces. This property is known as fluidity.
3. Fluids can be classified into liquids and gases. Liquids have definite volumes but take the shape of their container. Gases have no definite volume or shape.
4. The three states of matter are solid, liquid and gas. Matter can change its state depending on temperature and pressure. For example, water changes from solid to liquid at 0°C and from liquid to gas at 100°C.
5. Fluid mechanics has numerous applications in engineering such as design of pipes, pumps, turbines, bearings, gears, etc. It is also used to study flow of blood in the circulatory system and air flow over airplanes.
6. The study of fluids at rest is known as hydrostatics and fluids in motion is known as fluid dynamics. Fluid dynamics incorporates concepts such as continuity, momentum, energy, vorticity, Bernoulli's equation, etc.
7. The properties of fluids and the forces acting on them can be understood and analyzed with the help of laws and principles. Some important laws and principles in fluid mechanics are Archimedes' principle, Pascal's law, Bernoulli's equation, Reynolds number, etc.
