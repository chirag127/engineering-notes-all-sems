### Pressure

- Pressure is defined as force per unit area applied in a direction perpendicular to the surface.
- Pressure is a scalar quantity and has units of N/m2 or Pa.
- Pressure acts equally in all directions.
- The pressure at a point has the same magnitude in all directions.
- Pressure is transmitted undiminished in all directions in a static fluid.
- The pressure in a fluid at rest is the same at all points at the same depth.
- The pressure in a fluid depends on depth/density of the fluid and acceleration due to gravity.
- The pressure increases linearly with depth in a fluid at rest. Pressure increases by density x acceleration due to gravity x depth.
- Pressure measurement: Manometers, pressure gauges, pressure transducers.
