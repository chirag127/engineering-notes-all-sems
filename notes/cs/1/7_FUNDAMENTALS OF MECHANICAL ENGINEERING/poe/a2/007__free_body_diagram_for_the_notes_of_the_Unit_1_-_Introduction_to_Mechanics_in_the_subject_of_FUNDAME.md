### Free Body Diagram

- A free body diagram is a diagram that depicts the forces acting on a body in isolation from its surroundings.
- It is a vector diagram that shows all the forces acting on an object, the magnitude of the forces, and the direction of the forces.
- To draw a free body diagram:

1. Identify the object and draw a simple representation of it.
2. Identify all the forces acting on the object and determine the direction of each force.
3. Represent each force with an arrow. The length of the arrow shaft indicates the magnitude of the force.
4. Label each force arrow with a description of the force.
5. Draw the forces to scale if quantitative information is given about the forces.

- Free body diagrams are useful to analyze forces and accelerations, determine equilibrium, and solve force problems.
- They help break down a complex problem into simpler parts and are a key tool for solving mechanics problems.
