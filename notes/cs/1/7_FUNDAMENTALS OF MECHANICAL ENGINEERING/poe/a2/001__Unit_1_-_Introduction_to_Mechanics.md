## Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

1. Mechanics: Mechanics is the study of forces and their effects on the motion of objects. It is one of the fundamental branches of physics.
2. Newton's Laws of Motion: Issac Newton proposed three laws that describe how forces affect the motion of objects:

- An object at rest stays at rest and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by an unbalanced force.
- The acceleration a of a body as produced by a net force F is directly proportional to the magnitude of the net force, in the same direction as the net force, and inversely proportional to the mass m of the body, i.e., F = ma.
- For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

3. Inertia: Inertia is the resistance of an object to a change in its state of motion. An object will keep doing what it's doing unless a force acts on it. More massive objects have greater inertia.
