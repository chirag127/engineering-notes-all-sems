### Drives for Internal Combustion Engines and Electric Vehicles

1. Internal Combustion Engines
- Piston-Crank mechanism: Converts reciprocating motion of pistons into rotary motion of crankshaft.
- Valve operation: Intake and exhaust valves operated by camshaft allow air-fuel mixture intake and exhaust gas outflow.
- Fuel injection: Fuel is injected into engine cylinders for efficient combustion.
- Ignition: Spark from spark plug initiates combustion of air-fuel mixture.

2. Electric Vehicles
- Electric motor: Converts electrical energy into mechanical rotary motion.
- Battery: Stores electrical energy and provides power to the motor.
- Power electronics: Controls the electric power and regulates the interaction between battery and motor.
- Inverter: Converts DC power from battery to AC for driving the motor.
