### Electric vehicles and hybrid vehicles

- Electric vehicles (EVs) use electric motors instead of an internal combustion engine (ICE) to propel the vehicle. They run on rechargeable lithium-ion batteries that supply electricity to the motor.
- Hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs) have both an ICE and an electric motor. The vehicle automatically switches between the two power sources based on driving conditions to maximize efficiency. The electric motor is powered by rechargeable batteries.
- Advantages of EVs and HEVs:
    - Environmentally friendly - they produce zero direct emissions.
    - Lower fuel costs since they are powered by electricity.
    - Quieter ride due to the absence of an ICE.
- Disadvantages of EVs and HEVs:
    - Higher upfront costs to purchase the vehicles and batteries.
    - Limited range on a single charge compared to gas-powered cars.
    - Long recharging times required, which can be inconvenient.
    - Reliance on an electrical grid for fuel.
- The future of electric vehicles is promising with improving technology and more models becoming available. They play an important role in reducing emissions and oil dependence. However, challenges around range, cost, and infrastructure still remain to be addressed for wider adoption.
