## Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning

Refrigeration and air conditioning systems are used to control the temperature and humidity of indoor environments. It is important to understand the basic principles of refrigeration and air conditioning to design, install, and maintain these systems.

### Refrigeration

Refrigeration is the process of removing heat from a space or substance and transferring it to another location. This is achieved through the use of a refrigerant, which absorbs heat as it evaporates and releases heat as it condenses.

#### Components of a Refrigeration System

- Compressor: compresses the refrigerant gas, increasing its temperature and pressure
- Condenser: removes heat from the refrigerant gas, causing it to condense into a liquid
- Expansion valve: reduces the pressure of the liquid refrigerant, causing it to evaporate and absorb heat
- Evaporator: absorbs heat from the space or substance being cooled, causing the refrigerant to evaporate

#### Types of Refrigeration Systems

- Vapor-compression refrigeration: the most common type of refrigeration system, used in household and commercial refrigerators and air conditioners
- Absorption refrigeration: uses a heat source to drive the refrigeration cycle, often used in industrial applications

### Air Conditioning

Air conditioning is the process of controlling the temperature, humidity, and air quality of indoor environments. It is achieved through the use of a refrigeration cycle, with additional components for air circulation and filtration.

#### Components of an Air Conditioning System

- Compressor: same as in a refrigeration system
- Condenser: same as in a refrigeration system
- Expansion valve: same as in a refrigeration system
- Evaporator: same as in a refrigeration system
- Air handler: moves air throughout the system and contains the evaporator and filter
- Filter: removes particles from the air before it enters the system

#### Types of Air Conditioning Systems

- Central air conditioning: a whole-house system that uses ductwork to distribute cooled air throughout a building
- Window air conditioning: a self-contained unit that fits in a window and cools a single room
- Split-system air conditioning: a system that uses both an indoor and outdoor unit to cool a single room or small area
