## Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles

In this unit, we will be discussing the basics of IC Engines and Electric Vehicles. The following points will be covered:

### IC Engines

- IC Engines, also known as Internal Combustion Engines, are heat engines that convert the chemical energy of fuel into mechanical energy.
- There are two types of IC Engines - Spark Ignition Engines and Compression Ignition Engines.
- Spark Ignition Engines use spark plugs to ignite the fuel-air mixture, while Compression Ignition Engines use compression to ignite the fuel-air mixture.
- IC Engines are classified based on the number of strokes they require to complete one cycle. The most common types of IC Engines are Two-Stroke Engines and Four-Stroke Engines.
- Two-Stroke Engines complete one cycle in two strokes, while Four-Stroke Engines complete one cycle in four strokes.
- IC Engines are widely used in automobiles, motorcycles, airplanes, and boats.

### Electric Vehicles

- Electric Vehicles, also known as EVs, are vehicles that use one or more electric motors for propulsion.
- EVs use electricity stored in batteries, fuel cells, or capacitors to power the electric motors.
- There are three types of EVs - Battery Electric Vehicles, Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles, and Hybrid Electric Vehicles.
- Battery Electric Vehicles use only batteries to power the electric motors and have no internal combustion engine.
- Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles use both batteries and an internal combustion engine to power the electric motors.
- Hybrid Electric Vehicles use both batteries and an internal combustion engine to power the electric motors, but the internal combustion engine is used only to recharge the batteries and is not connected to the drive wheels.
- EVs have several advantages over conventional vehicles, including lower operating costs, lower emissions, and quieter operation.

In conclusion, IC Engines and Electric Vehicles are two different types of propulsion systems used in vehicles. While IC Engines are widely used, EVs are becoming increasingly popular due to their many advantages. Understanding the basics of both systems is important for anyone interested in the automotive industry.
