### Fluids Properties

Fluid mechanics deals with the study of fluids and their properties. These properties include:

- Density: It refers to the mass per unit volume of a fluid. It is denoted by the symbol ρ (rho) and is measured in kg/m³.

- Viscosity: It is the measure of a fluid's resistance to flow. The higher the viscosity, the more resistant the fluid is to flow. The symbol used to denote viscosity is μ (mu) and is measured in Pa.s.

- Surface tension: It is the attractive force exerted by the molecules of a fluid on each other at the surface of the fluid. It is denoted by the symbol σ (sigma) and is measured in N/m.

- Compressibility: It is the measure of how much a fluid's volume changes when subjected to pressure. It is denoted by the symbol β (beta) and is measured in 1/Pa.

- Thermal conductivity: It is the measure of how well a fluid conducts heat. It is denoted by the symbol k and is measured in W/m.K.

- Specific heat: It is the measure of the amount of heat required to raise the temperature of a fluid by a certain amount. It is denoted by the symbol C and is measured in J/kg.K.

- Bulk modulus: It is the measure of a fluid's resistance to compression. It is denoted by the symbol K and is measured in Pa.

- Velocity: It is the measure of the speed and direction of a fluid's flow. It is denoted by the symbol v and is measured in m/s.

- Pressure: It is the measure of the force exerted by a fluid per unit area. It is denoted by the symbol p and is measured in Pa.

These properties play a crucial role in understanding fluid mechanics and its applications in various fields of engineering. Understanding these properties can help in predicting the behavior of fluids under different conditions and designing systems that work efficiently with fluids.
