## Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications

Fluid mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the study of fluids in motion. It is an important subject that is widely used in various fields such as engineering, physics, chemistry, and biology. In this unit, we will cover the basics of fluid mechanics and its applications.

### Properties of Fluids

- Fluids are substances that can flow and take the shape of their container.
- They have no definite shape and can be compressed.
- Fluids have two types of properties: physical properties and mechanical properties.
- Physical properties of fluids include density, viscosity, surface tension, and compressibility.
- Mechanical properties of fluids include pressure, velocity, and acceleration.

### Fluid Statics

- Fluid statics is the study of fluids at rest.
- The pressure at any point in a fluid is the same in all directions.
- The pressure at the bottom of a fluid is higher than at the top.
- The pressure in a fluid depends on the depth and the density of the fluid.

### Fluid Dynamics

- Fluid dynamics is the study of fluids in motion.
- The continuity equation states that the mass flow rate of a fluid is constant.
- The Bernoulli's equation relates the pressure, velocity, and height of a fluid.
- The Reynolds number is used to determine whether the flow of a fluid is laminar or turbulent.

### Applications of Fluid Mechanics

- Fluid mechanics is used in the design of aircraft, ships, and submarines.
- It is used in the design of pipelines and pumps for the transportation of fluids.
- It is used in the design of turbines and engines for the generation of power.
- It is used in the study of blood flow in the human body and the design of medical devices.

In conclusion, fluid mechanics is an important subject that has numerous applications in various fields. Understanding the basics of fluid mechanics is essential for anyone interested in pursuing a career in engineering, physics, or any related field.
