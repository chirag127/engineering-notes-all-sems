### Calibration for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Calibration is the process of comparing a measuring device or instrument with a known standard to determine its accuracy. Calibration is an essential step in any measurement process as it ensures that the instrument is providing accurate and reliable measurements.

Here are some important points to keep in mind when it comes to calibration:

- Calibration should be performed regularly, especially for instruments that are used frequently or in critical applications.
- Calibration should be performed by a qualified technician or calibration laboratory that has the necessary equipment and expertise.
- The calibration process involves comparing the instrument's readings with known standards and making adjustments as necessary to ensure accuracy.
- Calibration certificates should be provided by the calibration laboratory to document the results of the calibration and the traceability of the standards used.
- Instruments that have been dropped or otherwise damaged should be calibrated before being used again to ensure that they are still accurate.
- Calibration records should be maintained to document the calibration history of each instrument and to schedule future calibrations.

In addition to calibration, it is also important to properly maintain and handle measuring instruments to ensure their accuracy and reliability. Here are some tips for instrument maintenance:

- Store instruments in a dry, clean, and secure location when not in use.
- Clean instruments regularly using appropriate cleaning agents and methods.
- Handle instruments carefully to avoid damage or contamination.
- Replace worn or damaged parts as necessary to maintain accuracy.
- Follow the manufacturer's recommendations for maintenance and calibration schedules.

In summary, calibration is a critical step in ensuring the accuracy and reliability of measuring instruments. Regular calibration by qualified technicians or calibration laboratories, along with proper maintenance and handling, can help ensure that instruments provide accurate and reliable measurements over time.
