### Ton of Refrigeration

- Ton of Refrigeration (TR) is a unit of refrigeration capacity.
- It is defined as the amount of heat that must be removed from one short ton of water at 0°C to freeze it to ice at the same temperature in 24 hours.
- The TR is equal to 12,000 British Thermal Units (BTUs) per hour or 3.517 kilowatts (kW) of cooling power.
- The TR is commonly used in the refrigeration and air-conditioning industry to measure the cooling capacity of refrigeration systems.
- For example, a refrigeration system with a cooling capacity of 5 TR can remove 60,000 BTUs of heat per hour or 17.585 kW of cooling power.
- The TR is also used to rate the performance of air-conditioning systems in terms of their cooling capacity.
- The ton of refrigeration is an important concept in the design and operation of refrigeration and air-conditioning systems.
- It is important to properly size the refrigeration system to ensure that it has the necessary cooling capacity to meet the requirements of the application.
- Oversizing or undersizing the refrigeration system can lead to inefficiencies and increased operating costs.
- The TR is just one of many factors that need to be considered when designing and operating refrigeration and air-conditioning systems.
