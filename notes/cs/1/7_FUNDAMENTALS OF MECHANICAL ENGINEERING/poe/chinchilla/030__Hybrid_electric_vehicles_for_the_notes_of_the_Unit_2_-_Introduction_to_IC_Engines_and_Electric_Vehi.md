### Hybrid Electric Vehicles

Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEVs) are a type of vehicle that combines an internal combustion engine (ICE) with an electric motor and battery. This combination allows the vehicle to use less fuel and emit less pollution than traditional gas-powered vehicles.

Here are some key points to understand about HEVs:

- HEVs are powered by both an ICE and an electric motor. The ICE provides power to the wheels through the transmission, while the electric motor assists the ICE and can also provide power to the wheels.
- The battery in an HEV is charged through regenerative braking, which captures energy that is normally lost during braking and uses it to charge the battery.
- HEVs can operate in a variety of modes, including electric-only, ICE-only, or a combination of the two. The vehicle's onboard computer determines the best mode for the driving conditions to maximize efficiency.
- HEVs can achieve higher fuel efficiency than traditional gas-powered vehicles, since the electric motor can assist the ICE during acceleration and provide power during low-speed driving.
- HEVs can also reduce emissions of greenhouse gases and pollutants, since the electric motor can help reduce the workload on the ICE and allow it to operate more efficiently.

There are several types of HEVs, including:

- Series hybrid: In this type of HEV, the ICE is used solely to generate electricity to power the electric motor. The ICE does not provide power directly to the wheels.
- Parallel hybrid: In this type of HEV, both the ICE and electric motor can provide power to the wheels simultaneously.
- Series-parallel hybrid: This type of HEV combines aspects of both the series and parallel hybrid designs, allowing for greater flexibility in the vehicle's operation.

HEVs are becoming increasingly popular as consumers seek more fuel-efficient and environmentally-friendly vehicles. Understanding the key components and operation of HEVs is important for those working in the field of mechanical engineering, as the development and optimization of these vehicles is an important area of research and innovation.
