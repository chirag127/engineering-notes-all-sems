### Scope for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEER

In this unit, you will learn about the basics of measurement and mechatronics, which are essential fields in mechanical engineering. The following are the scope of the notes for this unit:

1. Introduction to measurement:
   - Definition of measurement and its importance in engineering.
   - Different types of measurement systems, such as absolute and comparative measurement systems.
   - Units of measurement, such as SI units and English units.
   - Errors in measurement and their classification, such as systematic and random errors.

2. Measuring instruments:
   - Classification of measuring instruments, such as mechanical, electrical, and optical measuring instruments.
   - Principles of operation of measuring instruments, such as strain gauges, thermocouples, and LVDTs.
   - Calibration of measuring instruments and its importance.

3. Mechatronics:
   - Definition of mechatronics and its applications in mechanical engineering.
   - Elements of mechatronics, such as sensors, actuators, and microcontrollers.
   - Design and analysis of mechatronic systems.

4. Control systems:
   - Definition of control systems and their importance in mechatronics.
   - Types of control systems, such as open-loop and closed-loop control systems.
   - Design and analysis of control systems using transfer function and block diagrams.

5. Applications of measurement and mechatronics in mechanical engineering:
   - Examples of applications of measurement and mechatronics in mechanical engineering, such as robotics, automation, and machining.
   - Importance of measurement and mechatronics in product design and development.

In conclusion, this unit will provide you with the basic knowledge and understanding of measurement and mechatronics, which are crucial fields in mechanical engineering. The scope of the notes covers the fundamental concepts, principles, and applications of measurement and mechatronics, which will help you in your future career in mechanical engineering.
