## Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

Mechanics is the branch of physics that deals with the study of motion, forces, and energy. It is an essential subject for understanding the physical world around us. In this unit, you will learn the fundamentals of mechanics, including:

- **Kinematics**: This is the study of motion without considering the forces that cause it. You will learn about displacement, velocity, acceleration, and other concepts related to motion.

- **Dynamics**: This is the study of the forces that cause motion. You will learn about Newton's laws of motion, force, momentum, and energy.

- **Statics**: This is the study of objects at rest. You will learn about equilibrium, torque, and the center of gravity.

- **Work and Energy**: This is the study of the relationship between work and energy. You will learn about potential and kinetic energy, conservation of energy, and power.

- **Circular Motion**: This is the study of motion in a circular path. You will learn about centripetal force, angular velocity, and acceleration.

- **Fluid Mechanics**: This is the study of fluids in motion. You will learn about pressure, buoyancy, and Bernoulli's principle.

In addition to these topics, you will also learn about vectors, coordinate systems, and the importance of units in physics.

It is important to understand the fundamental concepts of mechanics as they form the basis of many other areas of physics, such as thermodynamics, electromagnetism, and quantum mechanics. By the end of this unit, you should have a solid understanding of the principles of mechanics and be able to apply them to solve problems.
