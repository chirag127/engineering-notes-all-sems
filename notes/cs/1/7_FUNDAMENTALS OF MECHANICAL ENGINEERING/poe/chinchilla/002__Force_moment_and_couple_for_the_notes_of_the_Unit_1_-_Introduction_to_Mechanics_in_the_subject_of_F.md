### Force Moment and Couple

Force, moment, and couple are some of the fundamental concepts in mechanics. Understanding these concepts is crucial for the analysis and design of mechanical systems. In this section, we will discuss force, moment, and couple in detail.

#### Force

Force is a vector quantity that is used to describe the interaction between two bodies. It is defined as the product of mass and acceleration and is denoted by the symbol F. The SI unit of force is Newton (N).

#### Moment

Moment is a vector quantity that is used to describe the rotational effect of a force. It is defined as the product of force and the perpendicular distance between the point of application of the force and the pivot point. Moment is denoted by the symbol M, and its SI unit is Newton-meter (Nm).

#### Couple

A couple is a system of two equal and opposite forces that act on a body but do not have a net force on the body. It produces a moment that tends to rotate the body. The distance between the two forces is called the arm of the couple, and its magnitude is the product of one of the forces and the arm length. The SI unit of couple is Newton-meter (Nm).

In summary, force, moment, and couple are fundamental concepts in mechanics that are used to describe the interaction between two bodies and the rotational effect of a force. Understanding these concepts is crucial for the analysis and design of mechanical systems.
