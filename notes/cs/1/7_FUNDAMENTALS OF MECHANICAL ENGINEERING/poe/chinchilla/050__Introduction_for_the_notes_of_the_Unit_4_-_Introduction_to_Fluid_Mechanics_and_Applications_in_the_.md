### Introduction

In Unit 4 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, we will dive into the world of Fluid Mechanics and Applications. This unit will introduce you to the fundamental concepts of fluid mechanics and their applications in various industries. Here are some key points that you should keep in mind while studying this unit:

- Fluid mechanics is the study of fluids and their behavior under various conditions like pressure, temperature, and velocity.
- Fluids can be classified into two categories: liquids and gases. Both of these behave differently under different conditions, and this is what makes fluid mechanics a fascinating subject.
- The study of fluid mechanics is important because it has applications in several industries like aerospace, automotive, civil, and chemical engineering. Understanding the behavior of fluids is crucial in designing various systems and structures.
- The unit will cover topics like fluid properties, fluid statics, fluid dynamics, Bernoulli's equation, and applications of fluid mechanics in various industries.
- You will learn about the basic properties of fluids like density, viscosity, and surface tension. These properties are essential to understand the behavior of fluids under different conditions.
- Fluid statics deals with fluids at rest, while fluid dynamics deals with fluids in motion. You will learn about the laws governing the behavior of fluids in both these conditions.
- Bernoulli's equation is a fundamental equation in fluid mechanics. It describes the relationship between the pressure, velocity, and elevation of a fluid in a system.
- You will also learn about the applications of fluid mechanics in various industries like aerospace, automotive, and civil engineering. Understanding the behavior of fluids is crucial in designing aircraft, cars, and buildings.
- Finally, you will learn how to apply the principles of fluid mechanics to solve practical problems. You will be introduced to various tools and techniques used in fluid mechanics to analyze and design systems.

In conclusion, Unit 4 of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering is an exciting unit that will introduce you to the world of fluid mechanics and its applications. By the end of this unit, you will have a solid understanding of the fundamental concepts of fluid mechanics and their practical applications. Good luck with your studies!
