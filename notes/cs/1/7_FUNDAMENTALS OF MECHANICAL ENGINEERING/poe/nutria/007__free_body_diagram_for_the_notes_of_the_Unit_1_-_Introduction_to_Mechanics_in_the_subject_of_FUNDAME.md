
### Free Body Diagram for Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics in Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering

* A free body diagram (FBD) is a graphical representation of the forces and moments that act on a body or system.
* It is used to represent the forces and moments acting on a body or system in order to analyze the motion of the body or system.
* An FBD should include all forces and moments that act on the body or system, including gravity, friction, air resistance, etc.
* The FBD should also include the reactions from the support surfaces, such as a wall or a floor.
* A free body diagram should be drawn in such a way that all forces and moments can be clearly seen, and that the direction of each force or moment can be determined.
* The FBD should be labeled with the type of force or moment (e.g. gravity, friction, etc.) and the magnitude of the force or moment (if known).
* When drawing a free body diagram, it is important to ensure that all forces and moments are drawn to scale. This will help to accurately analyze the motion of the body or system.
