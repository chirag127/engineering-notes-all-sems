
### Air-Conditioning for the notes of the Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

- Air-conditioning is a process of controlling the temperature, humidity, and purity of the air in an enclosed space. 
- It is achieved by using a refrigeration cycle and a heating cycle, both of which are controlled by a thermostat. 
- The main components of an air-conditioning system are the compressor, condenser, evaporator, and expansion valve.
- The compressor compresses the refrigerant, which increases its temperature and pressure. 
- The condenser then cools the refrigerant, which decreases its temperature and pressure. 
- The cooled refrigerant is then passed through the evaporator, which absorbs the heat from the air in the enclosed space, thus cooling it. 
- The expansion valve then reduces the pressure of the refrigerant, allowing it to evaporate and absorb more heat from the air. 
- The cooled air is then circulated through the space by a fan. 
- The thermostat is used to regulate the temperature of the space, by controlling the compressor and the heater.
