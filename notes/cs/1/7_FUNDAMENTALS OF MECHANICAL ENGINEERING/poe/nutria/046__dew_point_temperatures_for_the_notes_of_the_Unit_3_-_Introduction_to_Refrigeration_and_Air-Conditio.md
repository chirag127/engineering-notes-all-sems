
### Dew Point Temperatures for Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning

* Dew point temperature is the temperature at which air becomes saturated with water vapor.
* When air is cooled to its dew point temperature, the air can no longer hold all of the water vapor and some of the water vapor condenses into liquid droplets.
* The dew point temperature is an important factor in air conditioning and refrigeration systems as it determines the amount of moisture in the air.
* The dew point temperature is also used to calculate the relative humidity of the air.
* The dew point temperature is affected by the temperature and pressure of the air, as well as the amount of water vapor present in the air.
