
### Components of an EV 
1. Battery - The battery is the main source of power for an Electric Vehicle. It usually consists of multiple cells connected in series and parallel. The battery provides the necessary power to the motor to move the vehicle.
2. Motor - The motor is the main component of an Electric Vehicle. It is responsible for converting electrical energy from the battery into mechanical energy to move the vehicle.
3. Controller - The controller is responsible for controlling the flow of electricity from the battery to the motor. It is responsible for regulating the speed and power of the motor.
4. Charger - The charger is responsible for charging the battery of the Electric Vehicle. It converts the AC power from the wall outlet into DC power which is then used to charge the battery.
5. Inverter - The inverter is responsible for converting the DC power from the battery into AC power which is then used to power the motor.
6. Transmission - The transmission is responsible for transferring the mechanical power from the motor to the wheels of the vehicle. It is usually composed of a series of gears which are used to adjust the speed of the vehicle.
