
## Unit 1 - Introduction to Mechanics

1. Mechanics is the branch of physical science that studies the motion of objects and the forces that cause them to move. 
2. It is a fundamental part of physics and is closely related to other physical sciences such as chemistry, astronomy, and geology.
3. Mechanics is divided into two main branches: classical mechanics and quantum mechanics. Classical mechanics deals with the motion of macroscopic objects while quantum mechanics deals with the motion of subatomic particles.
4. The fundamental principles of mechanics are Newton's laws of motion and the law of conservation of energy. 
5. Newton's laws of motion describe how objects move and interact with each other, while the law of conservation of energy states that energy can neither be created nor destroyed. 
6. These laws are used to describe and predict the motion of objects in a variety of situations, from the motion of planets to the motion of a car. 
7. Mechanics is used in many fields, including engineering, aerospace, robotics, and medicine.
