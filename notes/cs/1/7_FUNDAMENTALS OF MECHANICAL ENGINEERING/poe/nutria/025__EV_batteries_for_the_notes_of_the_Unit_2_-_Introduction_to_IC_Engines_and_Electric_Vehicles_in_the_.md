
### EV Batteries

* Electric vehicles (EVs) are powered by batteries that store electrical energy.
* Different types of batteries are used in EVs, including lead-acid, nickel-metal hydride, and lithium-ion.
* Lead-acid batteries are the oldest type of EV battery, but are heavy, bulky, and have a short range.
* Nickel-metal hydride batteries are lighter and more efficient than lead-acid batteries, but still have a short range.
* Lithium-ion batteries are the most popular type of EV battery, as they are light, efficient, and have a long range.
* EV batteries are typically charged by plugging them into an external power source, but some vehicles are equipped with regenerative braking systems that can recharge the battery while driving.
