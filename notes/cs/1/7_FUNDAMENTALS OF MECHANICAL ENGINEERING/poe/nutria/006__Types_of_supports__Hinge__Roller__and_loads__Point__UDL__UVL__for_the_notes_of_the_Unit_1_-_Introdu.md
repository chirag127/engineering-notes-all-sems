
### Types of Supports (Hinge, Roller) and Loads (Point, UDL, UVL)

* Supports are the elements of a structure which are used to keep it in place and transfer the load to the ground. They can be classified into two types:
  * Hinge: A hinge support allows one degree of freedom, i.e. rotation.
  * Roller: A roller support allows two degrees of freedom, i.e. rotation and translation.

* Loads are the forces which act on a structure and cause it to deform. They can be classified into three types:
  * Point Load: A point load is a concentrated force which acts on a single point on the structure.
  * UDL (Uniformly Distributed Load): A UDL is a force which is distributed over a certain length of the structure.
  * UVL (Uniformly Varying Load): A UVL is a force which varies in magnitude over a certain length of the structure.
