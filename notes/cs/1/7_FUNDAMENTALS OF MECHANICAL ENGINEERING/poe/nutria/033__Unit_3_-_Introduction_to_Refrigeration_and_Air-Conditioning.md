
## Unit 3 - Introduction to Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning

1. Refrigeration is the process of cooling a space, substance, or system to lower and maintain its temperature below the ambient one.

2. Air-conditioning is a type of refrigeration technology that uses mechanical, electrical, and chemical processes to cool air or remove moisture from it.

3. Refrigeration and air-conditioning systems are used to control temperature and humidity levels in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings.

4. Refrigerants are the fluids used in refrigeration and air-conditioning systems to absorb and release heat.

5. Refrigeration and air-conditioning systems can be divided into two categories: vapor-compression and absorption systems.

6. Vapor-compression systems are the most common type of refrigeration and air-conditioning systems and use a compressor to increase the pressure of the refrigerant.

7. Absorption systems use a chemical process to absorb heat from the air or space being cooled.

8. Refrigeration and air-conditioning systems are used in a variety of applications, including food storage and preservation, medical care, and industrial processes.
