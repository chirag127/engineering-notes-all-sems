
### Hybrid Electric Vehicles 

Hybrid electric vehicles (HEVs) are a type of vehicle that combines the traditional internal combustion engine (ICE) with an electric motor. They are designed to reduce fuel consumption and emissions while providing a more efficient driving experience. 

HEVs are typically powered by a combination of gasoline and electricity, but may also include diesel, natural gas, and other fuels. The electric motor is usually powered by a rechargeable battery, which can be recharged from a wall outlet or from regenerative braking. When the battery is depleted, the ICE will kick in to provide power. 

HEVs offer several advantages over traditional ICE vehicles. They are more efficient, produce fewer emissions, and are generally quieter. Additionally, they can provide a smoother and more comfortable ride. 

HEVs are becoming increasingly popular due to their efficiency and environmental benefits. They are also becoming more affordable, making them a viable option for many drivers. 

HEVs are an important part of the future of transportation, and their popularity is only likely to increase in the coming years.
