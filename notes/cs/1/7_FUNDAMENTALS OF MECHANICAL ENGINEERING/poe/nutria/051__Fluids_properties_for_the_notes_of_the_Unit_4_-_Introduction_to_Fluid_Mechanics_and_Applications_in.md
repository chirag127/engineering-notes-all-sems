
### Fluids Properties for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications in the Subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

* Fluids are substances that are capable of flowing and are not rigid. Examples of fluids include liquids and gases.
* Fluids can be characterized by their density, viscosity, and surface tension.
* Density is the mass per unit volume of a substance and is usually expressed in kg/m3.
* Viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to flow and is usually expressed in Pa-s.
* Surface tension is a measure of the cohesive forces between molecules of a fluid and is usually expressed in N/m.
* Fluids can also be classified as either Newtonian or non-Newtonian, depending on their behavior under stress. Newtonian fluids have a constant viscosity, while non-Newtonian fluids have a variable viscosity.
* The study of fluid mechanics deals with the behavior of fluids, including the forces acting on them, and the resulting motion of the fluids.
* Applications of fluid mechanics include aerodynamics, hydrodynamics, and thermodynamics.
