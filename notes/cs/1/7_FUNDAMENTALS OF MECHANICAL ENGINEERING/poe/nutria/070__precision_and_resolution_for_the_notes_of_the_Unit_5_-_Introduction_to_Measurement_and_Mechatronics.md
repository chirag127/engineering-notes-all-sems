
### Precision and Resolution

* Precision and resolution are two important concepts in measurement and mechatronics.
* Precision refers to the degree of accuracy of a measurement. It is the degree to which repeated measurements of the same quantity will give the same result.
* Resolution is the smallest change in a quantity that can be detected by a measuring device. It is the smallest difference between two values that can be distinguished by a measuring device.
* Precision and resolution are closely related. Higher precision implies higher resolution.
* Precision and resolution are important factors to consider when selecting a measuring device.
