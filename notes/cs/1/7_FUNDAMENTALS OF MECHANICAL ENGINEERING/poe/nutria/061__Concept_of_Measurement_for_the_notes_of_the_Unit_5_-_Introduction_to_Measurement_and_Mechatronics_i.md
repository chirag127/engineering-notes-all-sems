
### Concept of Measurement 

1. Measurement is the process of determining the size, amount, or degree of something. It is used to compare an unknown quantity to a known quantity. 
2. Measurement systems are used to measure physical quantities such as length, area, volume, weight, temperature, pressure, and force. 
3. Measurement systems can also be used to measure intangible quantities such as time, velocity, acceleration, power, and energy. 
4. Measurement systems are used in a variety of fields, including engineering, science, and industry. 
5. Mechatronics is a field of engineering that combines mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering to create systems that are capable of sensing, controlling, and communicating with the environment. 
6. Mechatronic systems are used in a variety of applications, such as robotics, automation, and control systems. 
7. Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics is a course that provides an overview of the fundamentals of measurement systems and mechatronic systems. 
8. The course covers topics such as sensors, transducers, signal conditioning, data acquisition, and control systems.
