
### Chargers for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the Subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Internal combustion (IC) engines are used in vehicles to convert chemical energy into mechanical energy.
2. An electric vehicle (EV) is powered by one or more electric motors, which use electricity stored in batteries or fuel cells to power the vehicle.
3. Chargers are devices used to recharge the batteries in electric vehicles.
4. Chargers can be either AC or DC. AC chargers use alternating current to charge the battery, while DC chargers use direct current.
5. Chargers can be either Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3. Level 1 chargers use 120 volts, Level 2 chargers use 240 volts, and Level 3 chargers use 480 volts.
6. Chargers come in a variety of shapes and sizes, and can be installed either in the vehicle or in the home.
7. Charging times vary depending on the type of charger and the size of the battery.
8. Charging an EV can be more expensive than fueling an IC engine vehicle, but it is more efficient and produces fewer emissions.
