
### Ton of Refrigeration

1. The ton of refrigeration (TR) is a unit of measurement used to measure cooling capacity. It is the amount of cooling a system can provide in an hour.
1. One ton of refrigeration is equal to 12,000 BTU/hr (British Thermal Units per hour).
1. Refrigeration systems are used to cool air or other substances, such as food.
1. Refrigeration systems are used in air conditioning, food processing, and other applications.
1. Refrigeration systems use a refrigerant to absorb heat from the air or substance being cooled and transfer it to the outside air.
1. Refrigeration systems use compressors, condensers, evaporators, and expansion valves to transfer heat.
1. The efficiency of a refrigeration system is measured by its coefficient of performance (COP).
1. The COP is the ratio of the cooling capacity of the system to the energy input.
1. The higher the COP, the more efficient the system is.
