
### Electric Vehicles and Hybrid Vehicles

* Electric vehicles (EVs) are powered by electric motors and batteries, while hybrid vehicles (HVs) use a combination of electric motors and internal combustion engines (ICEs).
* EVs are typically more energy efficient than HVs, as they have lower energy losses due to friction, air drag, and other factors.
* EVs have fewer moving parts than HVs, which reduces maintenance costs and increases reliability.
* EVs can be charged from an external power source, while HVs must be refueled with gasoline or diesel fuel.
* EVs can be powered by renewable energy sources, such as solar or wind power, while HVs are limited to non-renewable sources, such as petroleum-based fuels.
* EVs are typically quieter than HVs, as they have no engine noise.
* EVs have lower emissions than HVs, as they produce no direct emissions of pollutants.
* EVs can be more expensive than HVs, due to the cost of the electric motor and battery.
