2. Calibration is essential for ensuring the accuracy of measurements and is an important part of quality control.
3. It is important to understand the basic principles of measurement and mechatronics in order to properly calibrate a system.
4. Calibration involves setting the input and output values of a system to known or accepted standards.
5. It is important to use the correct calibration equipment and procedures in order to ensure accuracy.
6. The accuracy of a calibration can be affected by environmental factors such as temperature, humidity, and vibration.
7. Calibration should be performed regularly to ensure the accuracy of the system.
