
### Concepts of Accuracy for the Notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the Subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

1. Accuracy is the degree of conformity of a measured value to a standard or known value.
2. Accuracy is usually expressed as a percentage of error, which is the difference between the measured value and the standard value.
3. Resolution is the smallest change in the measured value that can be detected by the measuring system.
4. Repeatability is the ability of a measuring system to reproduce the same result when the same measurement is repeated under the same conditions.
5. Precision is the degree of reproducibility of a measuring system, which is determined by the number of significant figures in a measured value.
6. Linearity is the ability of a measuring system to produce a linear response over its entire range of measurement.
7. Hysteresis is the difference in the measured value when the same measurement is made in two different directions.
8. Drift is the gradual change in the measured value over time due to changes in the environment or the measuring system.
9. Calibration is the process of adjusting a measuring system to a known standard.
