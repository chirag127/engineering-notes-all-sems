
## Unit 4 - Introduction to Fluid Mechanics and Applications

1. Fluid Mechanics is the study of the behavior of liquids and gases when subjected to forces and changes in temperature and pressure.
2. Fluid dynamics is the branch of fluid mechanics that deals with the motion of fluids.
3. Fluid statics is the branch of fluid mechanics that deals with the behavior of fluids at rest.
4. Bernoulli’s equation is an important equation in fluid mechanics that relates pressure, velocity, and elevation of a fluid.
5. The Navier-Stokes equations are a set of equations used to describe the motion of a fluid in a given region.
6. Applications of fluid mechanics include aerodynamics, hydrodynamics, and fluid power.
7. Aerodynamics is the study of the motion of air and other gases.
8. Hydrodynamics is the study of the motion of liquids.
9. Fluid power is the use of fluids to generate mechanical energy, such as in hydraulic systems.
