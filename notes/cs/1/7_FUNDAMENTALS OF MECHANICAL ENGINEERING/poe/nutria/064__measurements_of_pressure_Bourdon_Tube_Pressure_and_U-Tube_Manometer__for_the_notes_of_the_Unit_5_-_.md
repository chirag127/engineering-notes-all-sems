
### Measurements of Pressure (Bourdon Tube Pressure and U-Tube Manometer)

* Bourdon Tube Pressure: This type of pressure measurement is based on the principle of elasticity. It is a curved tube filled with a fluid, usually oil or water. When pressure is applied to the tube, it straightens and causes the pointer to move on a scale.

* U-Tube Manometer: This type of pressure measurement is based on the principle of hydrostatic pressure. It consists of two vertical tubes connected at the bottom, with one side being open to the atmosphere. When pressure is applied to the open side, the fluid in the tubes rises and falls, and the difference in height can be measured by the pointer on the scale.
