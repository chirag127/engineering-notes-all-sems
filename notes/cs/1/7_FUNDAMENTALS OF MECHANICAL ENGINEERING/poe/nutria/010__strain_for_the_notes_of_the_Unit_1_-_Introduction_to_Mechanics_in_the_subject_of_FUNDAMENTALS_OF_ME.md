
### Strain

* Strain is a measure of deformation that results from applied forces. It is defined as the relative change in length of a material when subjected to an applied force.
* Strain can be described as either tensile or compressive. Tensile strain is a stretching of the material, while compressive strain is a squeezing of the material.
* Strain can also be described as either elastic or plastic. Elastic strain is a reversible deformation, while plastic strain is an irreversible deformation.
* Strain can be measured using various techniques, such as strain gauges and extensometers.
* Strain can be used to calculate the stress in a material, as well as the modulus of elasticity.
