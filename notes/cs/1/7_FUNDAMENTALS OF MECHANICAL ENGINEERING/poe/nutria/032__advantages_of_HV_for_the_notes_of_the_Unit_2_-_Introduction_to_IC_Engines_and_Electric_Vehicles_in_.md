
### Advantages of Hybrid Vehicles (HV)

1. Hybrid vehicles use both an internal combustion engine (ICE) and an electric motor. This combination of both technologies allows the vehicle to be more efficient and reduce emissions.

2. Hybrid vehicles can switch between the ICE and the electric motor depending on the driving conditions. This allows for better fuel economy and improved performance.

3. Hybrid vehicles are more reliable than traditional ICE vehicles due to their ability to switch between power sources. This reduces the risk of breakdowns and increases the lifespan of the vehicle.

4. Hybrid vehicles are more environmentally friendly than traditional ICE vehicles due to their lower emissions. This improves air quality and helps reduce the effects of global warming.

5. Hybrid vehicles are becoming increasingly popular due to their efficiency and environmental benefits. This allows for more choice when selecting a new vehicle.
