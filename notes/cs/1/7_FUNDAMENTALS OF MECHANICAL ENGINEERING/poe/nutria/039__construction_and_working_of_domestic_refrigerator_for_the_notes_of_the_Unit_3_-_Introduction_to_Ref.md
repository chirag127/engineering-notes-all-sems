
### Construction and Working of Domestic Refrigerator
1. Refrigerators are cooling appliances that use a refrigeration cycle to cool the air inside the refrigerator. 
2. The main components of a refrigerator are the compressor, condenser, evaporator, and expansion valve. 
3. The compressor compresses the refrigerant gas, which causes it to become hot and high pressure. 
4. The hot and high-pressure gas then enters the condenser, where it is cooled and turned into a liquid. 
5. The liquid then passes through the expansion valve, where it is turned into a low-pressure and low-temperature gas. 
6. The gas then enters the evaporator, where it absorbs heat from the air inside the refrigerator and turns back into a liquid. 
7. The liquid then passes back through the compressor, completing the cycle and cooling the air inside the refrigerator.
