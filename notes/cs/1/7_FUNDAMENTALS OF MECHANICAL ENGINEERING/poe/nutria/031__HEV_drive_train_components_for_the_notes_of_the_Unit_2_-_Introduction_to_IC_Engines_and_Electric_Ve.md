
### HEV Drive Train Components

1. Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV): An HEV is a vehicle that combines an internal combustion engine (ICE) with an electric motor. The electric motor is powered by a battery, which is recharged by the ICE.

2. Battery: The battery stores energy from the ICE and supplies power to the electric motor.

3. Electric Motor: The electric motor is used to propel the vehicle and is powered by the battery.

4. Internal Combustion Engine (ICE): The ICE is used to generate power for the electric motor and to recharge the battery.

5. Regulator/Inverter: The regulator/inverter is used to control the flow of electricity between the battery and the electric motor.

6. Transmission: The transmission is used to transfer power from the ICE to the wheels.
