
### Normal and Shear Stress

* Normal stress is a type of stress that is applied perpendicular to the surface of an object. It is caused by forces that are applied in the same direction.
* Shear stress is a type of stress that is applied parallel to the surface of an object. It is caused by forces that are applied in opposite directions.
* Both normal and shear stress can cause an object to deform.
* The magnitude of normal and shear stress is determined by the magnitude of the applied force and the area over which the force is applied.
* The stress experienced by an object is also affected by its material properties, such as its elasticity, strength, and ductility.
* The relationship between normal and shear stress is known as the Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion. This criterion states that the shear stress must exceed a certain threshold before the material fails.
