
### Pascal’s Law and Continuity Equation

Pascal’s Law states that when a pressure is applied to a confined fluid, the pressure is transmitted undiminished in all directions. This law is used to explain the behavior of liquids when confined in a container.

The Continuity Equation states that the rate of flow of a fluid through a given cross-sectional area is equal to the product of the area and the average velocity of the fluid. This equation is used to describe the behavior of fluids in various applications, such as fluid flow through pipes or channels.

These two laws are important for understanding the behavior of fluids and are essential for engineers in the field of mechanical engineering. By understanding these laws, engineers can better design systems and components that involve the use of fluids.
