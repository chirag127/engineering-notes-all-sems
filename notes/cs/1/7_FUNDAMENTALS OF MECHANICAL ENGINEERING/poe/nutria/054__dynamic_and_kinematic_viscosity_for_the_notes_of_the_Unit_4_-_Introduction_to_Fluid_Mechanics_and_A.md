
### Dynamic and Kinematic Viscosity

* Dynamic viscosity is a measure of a fluid's resistance to shear or flow. It is also known as the absolute viscosity and is measured in units of Pascal-seconds (Pa-s).
* Kinematic viscosity is the ratio of the dynamic viscosity to the density of a fluid. It is measured in units of square meters per second (m^2/s).
* The viscosity of a fluid is a measure of its resistance to flow. Viscosity is an important property of fluids because it affects the behavior of the fluid in a variety of ways.
* Viscosity affects the drag force acting on a body moving through a fluid, the rate of heat transfer, and the rate of mass transfer.
* Viscosity also affects the rate of diffusion of a substance in a fluid, and the rate of mixing of two fluids.
* Viscosity is an important factor in determining the flow of fluids in pipes and other channels, and the flow of fluids over surfaces.
* The viscosity of a fluid is determined by the temperature, pressure, and composition of the fluid. The viscosity of a fluid increases with increasing temperature, and decreases with increasing pressure.
