
### Equilibrium Equations and Support Reactions

1. The **equilibrium equations** are a set of equations used to describe the forces and moments that act on a body in equilibrium. These equations are based on Newton’s laws of motion and are used to determine the unknown forces and moments that act on a body.

2. A **support reaction** is the force or moment that a support exerts on a body in order to keep it in equilibrium. The support reaction can be determined by applying the equilibrium equations to the body.

3. The **free body diagram** is a graphical representation of the forces and moments that act on a body. It is used to help visualize the forces and moments that act on a body and to determine the support reactions.

4. The **equilibrium equations** can be written in vector form as:

- $\sum F_x=0$
- $\sum F_y=0$
- $\sum M_z=0$

Where $F_x$, $F_y$, and $M_z$ are the x-component, y-component, and z-component of the force and moment vectors respectively.

5. The **support reactions** can be determined by solving the equilibrium equations for the unknown forces and moments. This can be done by using the methods of substitution or elimination.
