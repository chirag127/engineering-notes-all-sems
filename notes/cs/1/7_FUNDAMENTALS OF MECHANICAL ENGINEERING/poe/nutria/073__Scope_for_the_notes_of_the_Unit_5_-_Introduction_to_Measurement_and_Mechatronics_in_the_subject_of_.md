
### Scope for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEER

1. Introduction to Mechatronics: An overview of the principles, components, and applications of mechatronics, with a focus on the integration of mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering.

2. Mechatronics System Design: A look at the design of mechatronic systems, including the selection of components and integration of systems.

3. Measurement and Control: An introduction to the principles and techniques used in the measurement and control of mechatronic systems.

4. Sensors and Actuators: A look at the types of sensors and actuators used in mechatronic systems, as well as the principles and techniques of their operation.

5. Digital Control Systems: An overview of the principles and techniques of digital control systems, and how they are used in mechatronic systems.

6. Robotics: An introduction to the principles and techniques used in robotic systems, with a focus on their use in mechatronic systems.

7. Automation: An overview of the principles and techniques used in automated systems, and how they are used in mechatronic systems.
