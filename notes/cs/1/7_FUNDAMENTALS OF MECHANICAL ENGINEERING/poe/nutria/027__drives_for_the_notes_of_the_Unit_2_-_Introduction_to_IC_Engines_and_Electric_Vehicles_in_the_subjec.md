
### Drives for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Introduction to IC Engines and Electric Vehicles in the Subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering

1. Internal Combustion (IC) Engines: IC engines are engines that convert chemical energy into mechanical energy. They are typically powered by gasoline, diesel, or other combustible fuels. IC engines are used in cars, trucks, boats, and other vehicles.

2. Electric Vehicles: Electric vehicles are powered by electric motors and powered by electricity from batteries, fuel cells, or other energy sources. They are typically more efficient than IC engines and have lower emissions.

3. Hybrid Vehicles: Hybrid vehicles are powered by both IC engines and electric motors. They are typically more efficient than either IC engines or electric vehicles alone.

4. Fuel Cells: Fuel cells are electrochemical devices that convert chemical energy into electrical energy. They are typically used in electric vehicles and are more efficient than IC engines or electric motors.

5. Thermodynamics: Thermodynamics is the study of energy and its conversion from one form to another. It is important to understand thermodynamics when studying IC engines and electric vehicles.
