
### Temperature (Thermocouple and Optical Pyrometer)

1. A thermocouple is a device that measures temperature by using two different metals joined together. The junction of the two metals produces a voltage that is proportional to the temperature. 
2. An optical pyrometer is a device that measures temperature by using the thermal radiation emitted by an object. The intensity of the radiation is proportional to the temperature of the object. 
3. Thermocouples are often used in industrial applications because they are relatively inexpensive and easy to use. They can measure temperatures up to 2000°C.
4. Optical pyrometers are often used in high-temperature applications because they are more accurate than thermocouples. They can measure temperatures up to 3500°C.
5. Both thermocouples and optical pyrometers are used in the field of mechatronics to measure temperature in a variety of applications.
