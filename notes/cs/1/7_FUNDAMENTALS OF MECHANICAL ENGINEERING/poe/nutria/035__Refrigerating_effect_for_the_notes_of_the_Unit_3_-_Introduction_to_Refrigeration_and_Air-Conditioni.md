
### Refrigerating Effect

* Refrigeration is the process of cooling a space, substance, or system by removing heat from it. This is usually done by transferring the heat to a colder environment. 
* Refrigeration is used in many applications, including food storage, air conditioning, and industrial cooling.
* Air-conditioning is a form of refrigeration that uses a compressor, evaporator, and condenser to cool air.
* The refrigerating effect is the amount of heat that must be removed from a system in order to achieve a desired temperature.
* This effect is measured in British Thermal Units (BTUs) and is typically expressed in terms of the amount of heat removed per hour.
* The refrigerating effect is affected by the type of refrigerant used, the size of the system, and the temperature of the environment.
