### Introduction to Measurement for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Measurement is the process of determining the size, amount, or degree of a physical quantity. In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics, students learn about the basics of measurement and its applications in engineering. The unit covers topics such as units of measurement, measurement accuracy and precision, and measurement errors. Students also learn about different types of measuring instruments, including mechanical, electrical, and electronic instruments. Understanding the basics of measurement is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in engineering, including control systems, instrumentation, and mechatronics.
