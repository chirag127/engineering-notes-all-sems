### Concepts of accuracy for the notes of the Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Accuracy is a measure of how close a measured value is to the true value of a physical quantity. In the subject of Fundamentals of Mechanical Engineering, Unit 5 - Introduction to Measurement and Mechatronics, students learn about the concepts of accuracy in measurement. The unit covers topics such as systematic and random errors, and how they affect the accuracy of a measurement. Students also learn about the difference between accuracy and precision, and how to improve the accuracy of a measurement through the use of calibration and compensation techniques. Understanding the concepts of accuracy is essential for students pursuing mechanical engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in engineering, including control systems, instrumentation, and mechatronics.
