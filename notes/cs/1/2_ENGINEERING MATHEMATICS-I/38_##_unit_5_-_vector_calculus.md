## Unit 5 - Vector Calculus

Vector Calculus is a branch of mathematics that deals with the study of vectors, their derivatives and integrals. It is a fundamental tool for solving problems in physics, engineering, and mathematics. 

In Unit 5, topics covered include:
- Vector functions and space curves
- Partial derivatives
- Gradient vector, tangent plane, and normal line
- Directional derivatives and the gradient theorem
- Extrema of functions of several variables
- Lagrange multipliers
- Multiple integrals
- Line integrals
- Green's theorem, Stokes' theorem, and the divergence theorem.

By the end of Unit 5, students should be able to use vector calculus to analyze and solve problems in a variety of fields.
