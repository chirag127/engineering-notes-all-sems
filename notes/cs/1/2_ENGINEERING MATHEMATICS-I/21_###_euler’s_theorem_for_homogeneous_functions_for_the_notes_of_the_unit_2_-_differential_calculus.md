### Euler’s Theorem for homogeneous functions for the notes of the Unit 2 - Differential Calculus- I in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Euler's Theorem states that if a function is homogeneous of degree n, then its partial derivatives satisfy the following relationship:

f(tx,ty)=t^nf(x,y) where n is an integer, t is a scalar and (x,y) are variables.

This means that a homogeneous function can be scaled by a factor of t, and its output will also be scaled by t^n.

Applications of Euler's Theorem include finding the maximum and minimum values of homogeneous functions, and solving optimization problems involving homogeneous functions.

In engineering mathematics, Euler's Theorem is used in various fields such as mechanics, thermodynamics, and economics.
