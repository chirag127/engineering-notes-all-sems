### Vector differentiation: Gradient for the notes of the Unit 5 - Vector Calculus in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Vector differentiation refers to the process of finding the rate of change of a vector-valued function with respect to time or any other variable. Gradient is a vector operator that gives the rate of change of a scalar function in the direction of the steepest ascent. It is defined as the derivative of a scalar function with respect to its input vector. In vector calculus, gradient is used to find the maximum or minimum values of a scalar function and to find the direction of maximum increase or decrease of a scalar function. The gradient is a vector of partial derivatives and is denoted by ∇f(x). It is used in various engineering applications, such as fluid dynamics and optimization.
