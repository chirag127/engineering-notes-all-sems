### Cayley-Hamilton Theorem and its application for the notes of the Unit 1 - Matrices in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Cayley-Hamilton Theorem states that a square matrix satisfies its own characteristic equation. It states that if A is a square matrix of size n, then there exists a polynomial p(x) of degree at most n such that p(A) = 0. This theorem is useful in finding the inverse of a matrix, as well as in the study of linear transformations.

Applications in Engineering Mathematics-I:
1. Finding inverse of a matrix
2. Characterizing similarity between matrices
3. Studying linear transformations
4. Solving linear systems of equations

Note: The theorem is named after Arthur Cayley and William Rowan Hamilton.
