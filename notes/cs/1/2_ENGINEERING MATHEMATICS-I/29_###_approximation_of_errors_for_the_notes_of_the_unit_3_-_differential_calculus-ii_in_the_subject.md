### Approximation of errors for the notes of the Unit 3 - Differential Calculus-II in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

The approximation of errors in the unit 3 of Differential Calculus-II in Engineering Mathematics-I can be defined as the difference between the true value of a quantity and its estimated value. This can be calculated using various methods such as Taylor's theorem, Maxima and Minima, and numerical methods like Euler's method and Runge-Kutta method. The purpose of approximation of errors is to determine the accuracy of the calculated results and to improve the solution if necessary. It is an important concept in engineering mathematics and is widely used in various engineering fields such as electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, and civil engineering.
