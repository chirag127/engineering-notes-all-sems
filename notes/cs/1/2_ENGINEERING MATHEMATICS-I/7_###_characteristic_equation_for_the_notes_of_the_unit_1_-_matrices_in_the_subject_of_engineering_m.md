### Characteristic equation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Matrices in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Characteristic equation is a mathematical equation used to find the eigenvalues of a matrix in linear algebra. In the subject of Engineering Mathematics-I, Unit 1 - Matrices, the characteristic equation is often used to determine the stability of a system represented by a matrix. The eigenvalues of a matrix are scalar values that, when multiplied by a corresponding eigenvector, result in a scalar multiple of the eigenvector. The eigenvalues of a matrix can be used to determine the behavior of the system it represents, such as stability or instability. To find the eigenvalues of a matrix, one must first write the characteristic equation, which is obtained by subtracting each possible eigenvalue from the main diagonal of the matrix and then finding the determinant of the resulting matrix. Once the characteristic equation is found, the eigenvalues can be found by solving the equation. In the context of Engineering Mathematics-I, Unit 1 - Matrices, understanding the characteristic equation and its use in finding eigenvalues is crucial for students pursuing engineering degrees.
