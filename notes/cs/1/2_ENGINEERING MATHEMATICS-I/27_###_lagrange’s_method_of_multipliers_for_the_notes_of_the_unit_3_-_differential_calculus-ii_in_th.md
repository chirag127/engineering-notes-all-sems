### Lagrange’s method of multipliers for the notes of the Unit 3 - Differential Calculus-II in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Lagrange's Method of Multipliers is a mathematical technique used to find the maximum or minimum of a function subject to constraints. In Differential Calculus-II, this method is used to solve optimization problems, where the objective is to find the maximum or minimum of a function subject to one or more constraints.

In Lagrange's method, the objective function and the constraint equations are combined into a single function called the Lagrangian. The maximum or minimum of the Lagrangian is found by taking the partial derivatives of the Lagrangian with respect to the variables and setting them equal to zero.

Lagrange's method is useful in engineering mathematics because it can be applied to a wide range of optimization problems, including problems involving multiple variables and constraints. It is also relatively simple to implement, making it a useful tool for engineers to solve real-world problems.
