### Total derivative for the notes of the Unit 2 - Differential Calculus- I in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Total derivative is a concept in differential calculus that relates the change in a function to the change in its inputs. It is used to find the rate of change of a dependent variable with respect to an independent variable. The total derivative is represented by the symbol d/dx. 

In Engineering Mathematics, the total derivative is used to find the maximum or minimum values of a function and to find the critical points of a function. It is also used to find the tangent line to a curve and to find the equation of a curve.

The total derivative can be found using the limit definition of the derivative or using partial derivatives. The limit definition of the derivative states that the derivative of a function at a point is equal to the limit of the ratio of the change in the function to the change in the independent variable as the change in the independent variable approaches zero.

The partial derivative of a function is the derivative of the function with respect to one variable, holding all other variables constant. The total derivative can be found by taking the partial derivative of the function with respect to each variable and summing them up.

In conclusion, the total derivative is a useful tool in Engineering Mathematics for finding the rate of change of a dependent variable with respect to an independent variable and for finding the maximum or minimum values of a function.
