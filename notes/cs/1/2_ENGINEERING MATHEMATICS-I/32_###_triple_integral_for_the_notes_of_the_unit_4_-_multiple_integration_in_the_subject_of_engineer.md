### Triple integral for the notes of the Unit 4 - Multiple integration in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Triple Integral:
- A triple integral is an extension of double integral to integrate a function over a three-dimensional region in space. 
- It is used to calculate the volume of a solid region in 3D space. 
- The triple integral is written as ∫∫∫f(x,y,z)dV, where f(x,y,z) is the integrand and dV is the infinitesimal volume element. 
- The limits of integration are defined by the region of integration, which is usually a solid with boundaries defined by equations. 
- Triple integrals can be evaluated using cylindrical or spherical coordinates, depending on the geometry of the region of integration. 
- It is a powerful tool in fields such as physics, engineering, and economics to model real-world systems and solve problems involving volumes and masses.
