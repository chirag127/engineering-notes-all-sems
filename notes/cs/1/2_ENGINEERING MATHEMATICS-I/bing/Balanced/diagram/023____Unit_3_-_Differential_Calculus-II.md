## Unit 3 - Differential Calculus-II

- This unit covers the following topics:

  - Applications of derivatives: optimization problems, related rates, curve sketching, linear approximation and differentials, mean value theorem, L'Hospital's rule, Newton's method.
  - Indeterminate forms and improper integrals: types of indeterminate forms, evaluation of limits using L'Hospital's rule, definition and properties of improper integrals, comparison test, convergence and divergence of improper integrals.
  - Infinite series: definition and examples of sequences and series, convergence and divergence tests, absolute and conditional convergence, power series, radius and interval of convergence, Taylor and Maclaurin series, applications of power series.

- The main objectives of this unit are:

  - To learn how to use derivatives to solve various problems involving optimization, rates of change, graphing, approximation, and numerical methods.
  - To understand the concept of indeterminate forms and how to apply L'Hospital's rule to evaluate limits of such forms.
  - To learn the concept of improper integrals and how to determine their convergence or divergence using comparison test and other methods.
  - To learn the concept of infinite series and how to test their convergence or divergence using various criteria.
  - To learn how to represent functions as power series and how to manipulate and differentiate power series.
  - To learn how to find the Taylor and Maclaurin series of a function and how to use them to approximate functions and evaluate limits.

- The main outcomes of this unit are:

  - The student will be able to use derivatives to solve optimization problems, related rates problems, curve sketching problems, linear approximation and differentials problems, and apply the mean value theorem and L'Hospital's rule to various situations.
  - The student will be able to identify and evaluate indeterminate forms and improper integrals using appropriate methods and techniques.
  - The student will be able to determine the convergence or divergence of infinite series using various tests and criteria, and distinguish between absolute and conditional convergence.
  - The student will be able to find the power series representation of a function, determine its radius and interval of convergence, and perform operations and differentiation on power series.
  - The student will be able to find the Taylor and Maclaurin series of a function, and use them to approximate functions and evaluate limits.
