### Double integral

- A double integral is a way to integrate over a two-dimensional area. It helps you to find the volume under a surface or the area of a curved region .
- The double integral of a function of two variables, say f(x,y), over a rectangular region R is denoted by:

![\Large \iint_R f(x,y) \,dA = \iint_R f(x,y) \,dx \,dy](https://latex.codecogs.com/png.latex?\Large&space;\iint_R&space;f(x,y)&space;\,dA&space;=&space;\iint_R&space;f(x,y)&space;\,dx&space;\,dy)

- The double integral can be computed by first integrating with respect to one variable, say x, and then integrating with respect to the other variable, say y. This is called the **iterated integral** .
- The order of integration can be changed if the region R is simple enough. The limits of integration must be adjusted accordingly .
- The double integral can be extended to functions of more than two variables, such as triple integrals or multiple integrals .
