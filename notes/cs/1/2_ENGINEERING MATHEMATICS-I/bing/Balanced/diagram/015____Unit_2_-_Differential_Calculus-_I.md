## Unit 2 - Differential Calculus- I

- Differential calculus is the branch of mathematics that studies the rates of change of functions and their properties.
- The main concept of differential calculus is the derivative, which measures the instantaneous rate of change of a function at a point.
- The derivative of a function f(x) is denoted by f'(x) or dy/dx, where y = f(x).
- The derivative of a function can be interpreted geometrically as the slope of the tangent line to the graph of the function at a point, or physically as the velocity of a moving object whose position is given by the function.
- The derivative of a function can be calculated using various rules and formulas, such as the power rule, the product rule, the quotient rule, the chain rule, and the implicit differentiation.
- The derivative of a function can be used to find the critical points, where the function has a local maximum or minimum, or a horizontal tangent line.
- The derivative of a function can also be used to find the intervals of increase and decrease, where the function is rising or falling, and the concavity, where the function is curving upwards or downwards.
- The derivative of a function can also be used to find the inflection points, where the function changes its concavity, or the slope of the tangent line changes its sign.
- The derivative of a function can also be used to find the asymptotes, where the function approaches infinity or a finite value as the input variable approaches a certain value or infinity.
- The derivative of a function can also be used to find the relative and absolute extrema, where the function attains its highest or lowest value in a given interval or in its entire domain.
- The derivative of a function can also be used to find the rate of change of a quantity with respect to another quantity, such as the marginal cost, the marginal revenue, the marginal profit, etc.
- The derivative of a function can also be used to find the optimization problems, where the function represents the objective function that needs to be maximized or minimized subject to some constraints.
