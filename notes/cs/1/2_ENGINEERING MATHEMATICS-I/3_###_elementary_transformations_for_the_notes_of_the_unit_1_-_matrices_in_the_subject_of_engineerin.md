### Elementary transformations for the notes of the Unit 1 - Matrices in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Elementary Transformations for Matrices:

In the subject of Engineering Mathematics-I, unit 1 on matrices, elementary transformations refer to the basic operations that can be performed on matrices to simplify a given matrix or to solve a system of linear equations.

1. Interchange of Rows: Interchange of rows involves swapping two rows in a matrix, which does not change the solution of the system of linear equations.

2. Interchange of Columns: Interchange of columns involves swapping two columns in a matrix, which does not change the solution of the system of linear equations.

3. Multiplication of a Row by a Scalar: Multiplication of a row by a scalar involves multiplying all elements of a row by a non-zero scalar, which does not change the solution of the system of linear equations.

4. Addition of a Multiple of One Row to Another: Addition of a multiple of one row to another involves adding a scalar multiple of one row to another row, which does not change the solution of the system of linear equations.

These elementary transformations are used to simplify a matrix and to solve a system of linear equations by transforming the matrix into a row echelon form or a reduced row echelon form, which makes the solution of the system of linear equations easier to find.

In conclusion, elementary transformations for matrices in the subject of Engineering Mathematics-I are basic operations that can be performed on matrices to simplify a given matrix or to solve a system of linear equations. These transformations include the interchange of rows and columns, multiplication of a row by a scalar, and addition of a multiple of one row to another.
