### Liouville’s extensions of Dirichlet’s integral for the notes of the Unit 4 - Multiple integration in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Liouville's extension of Dirichlet's integral is a mathematical concept that relates to multiple integrals. Dirichlet's integral is a type of definite integral that involves a function of two variables. Liouville's extension extends the concept of Dirichlet's integral to functions of more than two variables, making it possible to integrate functions of three or more variables. This concept is important in engineering mathematics, as it provides a way to calculate the volume or other properties of objects in three-dimensional space. Understanding Liouville's extension of Dirichlet's integral is crucial for students studying Engineering Mathematics-I, as it provides a foundation for more advanced topics in multivariable calculus and differential equations.
