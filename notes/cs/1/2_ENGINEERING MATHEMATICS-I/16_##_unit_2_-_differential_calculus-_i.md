## Unit 2 - Differential Calculus- I

Differential calculus is a branch of mathematics that deals with the study of rates of change and slopes of curves. It is a fundamental tool in science and engineering, and is used to model a wide range of physical, biological, and economic systems. The central idea of differential calculus is the concept of a derivative, which gives the rate of change of a function at a particular point. The derivative is calculated using limit and differentiation rules. The study of differential calculus includes techniques for finding local maxima and minima, optimization, and curve sketching. The first unit of differential calculus focuses on the basics of derivatives and their applications, including the power rule, product rule, quotient rule, chain rule, and implicit differentiation.
