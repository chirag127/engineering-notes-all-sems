### Double integral for the notes of the Unit 4 - Multiple integration in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

The double integral is a fundamental concept in multiple integration, a branch of mathematics that deals with the integration of functions of more than one variable. In engineering mathematics, the double integral is used to calculate the area or volume of a region in two-dimensional or three-dimensional space, respectively. The double integral is defined as the limit of a sum of small rectangles or parallelograms, each with an area equal to the product of two functions. The functions represent the length and width of the rectangle or parallelogram, and the sum represents the total area of the region. The double integral can be evaluated using various techniques, including polar coordinates, cylindrical coordinates, and spherical coordinates. It is a powerful tool for solving real-world problems in engineering and physics, such as calculating the volume of a solid body or determining the distribution of a physical quantity over a region.
