### Partial derivatives for the notes of the Unit 2 - Differential Calculus- I in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Partial derivatives are derivatives of a function of multiple variables with respect to one of the variables, holding the others constant. They represent the rate of change of a function in a particular direction. To find partial derivatives, partial differentiation is applied to the function. The result is a new function that describes the rate of change of the original function in a specific direction. The partial derivative of a function f(x,y) with respect to x is denoted as ∂f/∂x or simply df/dx. The partial derivative of a function f(x,y) with respect to y is denoted as ∂f/∂y or df/dy. Partial derivatives can be used to find local extrema, saddle points, and to study the behavior of the function in different directions.
