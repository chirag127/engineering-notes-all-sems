### Skew-Hermitian for the notes of the Unit 1 - Matrices in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Skew-Hermitian matrices are matrices in linear algebra that have complex entries and satisfy the property that their transpose is equal to their negative. They are important in the study of linear transformations and eigenvalue problems, and have applications in quantum mechanics, signal processing, and control theory. In Engineering Mathematics-I, the study of Skew-Hermitian matrices is typically covered in Unit 1 - Matrices. Topics covered may include the definition of Skew-Hermitian matrices, their properties, and their use in solving eigenvalue problems. The study of Skew-Hermitian matrices is an important part of the foundation of Engineering Mathematics, and students are typically expected to have a strong understanding of the concepts covered in this unit.
