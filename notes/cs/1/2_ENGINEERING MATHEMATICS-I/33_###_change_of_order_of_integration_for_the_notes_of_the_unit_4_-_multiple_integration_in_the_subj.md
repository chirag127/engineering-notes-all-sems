### Change of order of integration for the notes of the Unit 4 - Multiple integration in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Change of order of integration refers to the process of switching the order of integration in a multiple integral. This is useful when the limits of integration are easier to express in a different order. The formula for change of order of integration is given by:

∬R f(x,y) dxdy = ∬R f(y,x) dydx, where R is the region of integration.

It is important to note that the change of order of integration only works for regions of integration that are symmetrical with respect to the line y=x.

In Engineering Mathematics-I, this concept is used to solve problems involving multiple integrals, especially when the limits of integration are difficult to evaluate in one order. It is also used to find the volume of objects with more complex shapes.
