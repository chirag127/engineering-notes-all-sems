### Jacobians for the notes of the Unit 3 - Differential Calculus-II in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Jacobians are used in multivariable calculus to find the derivative of a vector-valued function. Given a function mapping R^n to R^m, the Jacobian is the m x n matrix of its partial derivatives. It represents the rate of change of the output with respect to changes in the input. The determinant of the Jacobian is used to find the volume change under the mapping, and is important in physics and engineering applications.
