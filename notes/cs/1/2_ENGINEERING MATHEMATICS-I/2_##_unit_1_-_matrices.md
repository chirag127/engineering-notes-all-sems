## Unit 1 - Matrices

Matrices are a fundamental mathematical tool used in various branches of mathematics, including linear algebra, calculus, and engineering. A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers, symbols, or expressions, arranged in rows and columns. The size of a matrix is defined by its dimensions, which are the number of rows and columns. Matrices can be used to represent linear transformations, systems of linear equations, and data sets. In linear algebra, matrices are used to study the properties of linear transformations and to solve systems of linear equations. In engineering, matrices are used to model and solve problems in fields such as control systems, electrical engineering, and computer graphics. The basic operations of matrices include addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication, matrix multiplication, and transposition. The study of matrices is central to the study of linear algebra and is a crucial component of many engineering and mathematical disciplines.
