### Beta and Gama function and their properties for the notes of the Unit 4 - Multiple integration in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Beta Function: 
- Defined as B(x,y) = ∫0 to 1 t^(x-1) * (1-t)^(y-1) dt 
- Properties: 
  1. B(x,y) = B(y,x) 
  2. B(x,y) = Γ(x) * Γ(y) / Γ(x+y) 
  3. B(x,y) converges if x,y > 0 

Gamma Function: 
- Defined as Γ(x) = ∫0 to ∞ t^(x-1) * e^(-t) dt 
- Properties: 
  1. Γ(x+1) = x * Γ(x) 
  2. Γ(n) = (n-1)! for n ∈ N 
  3. Γ(x) converges if x > 0
