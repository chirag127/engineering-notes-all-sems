### Change of variables for the notes of the Unit 2 - Differential Calculus- I in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Change of variables is a technique used to simplify integrals by transforming the integration variable. The basic idea is to find a new variable that makes the integrand easier to integrate. The change of variables formula states that if a function f(x) is transformed to g(u) by the substitution x=h(u), then the definite integral of f(x) from a to b is equal to the definite integral of g(u) from h^-1(a) to h^-1(b) multiplied by the absolute value of the derivative of h at each point. This technique is useful for solving definite integrals that cannot be solved using elementary functions. The key is to choose an appropriate substitution that simplifies the integrand.
