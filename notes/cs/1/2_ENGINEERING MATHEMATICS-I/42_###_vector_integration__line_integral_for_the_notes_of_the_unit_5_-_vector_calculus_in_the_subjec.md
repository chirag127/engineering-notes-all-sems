### Vector Integration: Line integral for the notes of the Unit 5 - Vector Calculus in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Vector integration, also known as line integral, is a fundamental concept in the subject of Engineering Mathematics-I, specifically in the Unit 5 - Vector Calculus. It involves integrating a vector field along a specified curve. Line integrals are used to calculate the work done by a force along a path, the circulation of a vector field, and the potential of a vector field. The line integral is calculated by taking the dot product of the vector field and the differential of the curve. The line integral can be calculated using various techniques, including Green's Theorem, Stokes' Theorem, and the Fundamental Theorem of Line Integrals. These theorems provide a way to calculate line integrals in terms of simpler integrals, making them easier to solve. Understanding line integrals is essential for students studying engineering mathematics and engineering physics, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in these fields.
