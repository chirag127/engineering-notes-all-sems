### Eigen values and Eigen vectors for the notes of the Unit 1 - Matrices in the subject of ENGINEERING MATHEMATICS-I

Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors are important concepts in the field of Engineering Mathematics, particularly in the study of matrices.

Eigenvalue: An eigenvalue is a scalar value that is associated with a linear transformation, such as a matrix. It represents the amount by which a vector is stretched or shrunk by the transformation.

Eigenvector: An eigenvector is a non-zero vector that is associated with an eigenvalue. It represents the direction in which a vector is stretched or shrunk by the transformation.

The relationship between eigenvalues and eigenvectors is described by the eigenvalue equation: Av = λv, where A is a matrix, v is an eigenvector, and λ is an eigenvalue. The equation states that when a matrix A is multiplied by an eigenvector v, the result is a scalar multiple of v, where the scalar is the eigenvalue λ.

Applications of eigenvalues and eigenvectors in Engineering Mathematics include:

1. Determining the stability of systems: Eigenvalues can be used to determine the stability of systems, such as dynamic systems, by analyzing the growth or decay of the eigenvectors.

2. Solving differential equations: Eigenvalues and eigenvectors can be used to solve differential equations, such as the eigenvalue problem, by transforming the equation into a simpler form.

3. Image processing: Eigenvalues and eigenvectors can be used in image processing, such as image compression, by transforming the image into a new coordinate system defined by the eigenvectors.

In conclusion, eigenvalues and eigenvectors are important concepts in the field of Engineering Mathematics, particularly in the study of matrices. They describe the amount and direction of stretching or shrinking of vectors by a linear transformation, and have applications in determining the stability of systems, solving differential equations, and image processing.
