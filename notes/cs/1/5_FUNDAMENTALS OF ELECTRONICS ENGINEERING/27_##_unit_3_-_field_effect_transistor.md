## Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor

Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor (FET) is a topic in the subject of Fundamentals of Electrical Engineering that deals with the study of a type of transistor used in electronic circuits. FETs are three-terminal devices that control the flow of electrical current by modulating the conductivity of a channel of semiconductor material.

FETs are classified into two main types: junction field effect transistors (JFETs) and metal-oxide-semiconductor field effect transistors (MOSFETs). JFETs are bipolar devices that control the flow of current by modulating the voltage across the channel, while MOSFETs are unipolar devices that control the flow of current by modulating the gate-source voltage.

In this unit, students learn about the basic principles of operation, construction, and applications of FETs. They also learn about the various types of FETs, including JFETs and MOSFETs, and their different characteristics, such as input impedance, voltage gain, and frequency response.

The study of FETs is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in electrical engineering, as FETs are widely used in many electronic circuits, including amplifiers, switches, and power electronics applications. Understanding the principles and applications of FETs is important for engineers to design and implement electronic circuits that are reliable and efficient.
