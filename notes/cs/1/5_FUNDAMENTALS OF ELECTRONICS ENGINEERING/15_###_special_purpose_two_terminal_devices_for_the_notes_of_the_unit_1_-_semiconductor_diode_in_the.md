### Special Purpose two terminal Devices for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Special Purpose two terminal Devices:
1. Zener Diodes: used for voltage regulation and voltage reference
2. Schottky Diodes: used for fast switching and low voltage drop
3. Tunnel Diodes: used for high-frequency amplification and switching applications
4. Light Emitting Diodes (LEDs): used for lighting and indicator applications
5. Photodiodes: used for optical detection and signal conversion
6. PIN Diodes: used for RF and microwave applications.
7. Varactor Diodes: used for tuning circuits in radio and television applications.
