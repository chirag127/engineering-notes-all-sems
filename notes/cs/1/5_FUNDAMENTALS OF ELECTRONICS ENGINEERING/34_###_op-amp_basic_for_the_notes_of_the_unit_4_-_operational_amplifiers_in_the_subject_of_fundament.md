### Op-Amp basic for the notes of the Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

An operational amplifier (Op-Amp) is a type of integrated circuit that is widely used in electronic circuits. It is a high-gain direct-coupled amplifier that can be used to amplify signals in a wide range of applications, including signal processing, control systems, and communication systems.

The basic components of an Op-Amp include the input stage, the differential amplifier stage, and the output stage. The input stage consists of two input terminals, one for the positive input and one for the negative input. The differential amplifier stage amplifies the difference between the two input signals, and the output stage provides the amplified output signal.

In Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, students learn about the basic principles of Op-Amps, including their circuit design, applications, and limitations. The study of Op-Amps is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in electronics engineering, as they are widely used in many electronic circuits and systems. Understanding the basic principles of Op-Amps is essential for the design and analysis of electronic circuits and systems.
