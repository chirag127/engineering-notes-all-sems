### Introduction of Basic and Universal Gates for the notes of the Unit 5 - Digital Electronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Basic and Universal Gates are fundamental building blocks of digital electronics. Basic gates are the simplest type of digital logic gates and include AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, and XOR gates. Universal gates are more complex gates that can be used to implement any Boolean function, and include NAND and NOR gates. Unit 5 of Digital Electronics in the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering covers the introduction of basic and universal gates, including the operation and implementation of these gates, and their use in digital circuits. Understanding basic and universal gates is important for students studying Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, as they provide a foundation for understanding digital circuits and digital systems.
