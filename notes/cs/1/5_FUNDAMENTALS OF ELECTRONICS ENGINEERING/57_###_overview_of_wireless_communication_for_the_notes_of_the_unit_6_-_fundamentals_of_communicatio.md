### Overview of wireless communication for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Wireless communication is a method of transmitting information from one point to another without the use of physical wires. In the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering, students learn about the basics of wireless communication. The unit covers topics such as the electromagnetic spectrum, modulation techniques, wireless channel models, and wireless communication systems. Wireless communication is widely used in modern communication systems, including cellular networks, Wi-Fi, and satellite communication. The unit also covers the different types of wireless communication systems, including point-to-point, point-to-multipoint, and broadcast systems. Understanding the basics of wireless communication is essential for students pursuing electronics engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in communications engineering, including wireless networking and mobile communication.
