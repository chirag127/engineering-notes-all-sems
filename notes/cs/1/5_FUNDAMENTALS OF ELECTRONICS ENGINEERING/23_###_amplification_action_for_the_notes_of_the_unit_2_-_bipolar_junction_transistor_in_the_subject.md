### Amplification action for the notes of the Unit 2 - Bipolar Junction Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Amplification in BJT refers to the process of increasing the strength of a weak signal by using the transistor as an active component. The basic idea behind amplification is to use the transistor to control the flow of current in a circuit, thus increasing the voltage or current of the input signal. The BJT has three regions: Emitter, Base, and Collector. The input signal is applied to the base, which controls the flow of current between the emitter and collector. The output signal is taken from the collector. The amount of amplification is determined by the transistor's current gain, represented by the symbol hFE. To summarize, the BJT is used for amplification by controlling the flow of current in a circuit, resulting in an increased output signal.
