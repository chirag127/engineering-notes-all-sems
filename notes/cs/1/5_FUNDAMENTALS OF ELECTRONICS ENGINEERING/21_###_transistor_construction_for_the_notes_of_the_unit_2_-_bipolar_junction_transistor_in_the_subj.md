### Transistor Construction for the notes of the Unit 2 - Bipolar Junction Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

The Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT) is a three-layer semiconductor device consisting of two pn junctions. The three layers are the emitter, base, and collector. The emitter-base junction is forward-biased, while the base-collector junction is reverse-biased. The emitter layer is heavily doped to provide a large number of free electrons, while the base layer is lightly doped to provide a thin layer for control. The collector layer is moderately doped to provide a low resistance path for the collected charges. The BJT operates as a current-controlled current source, where the base current controls the collector current, allowing for amplification of a small input signal.
