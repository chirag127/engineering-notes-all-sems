## Unit 5 - Digital Electronics

Unit 5 - Digital Electronics:
- Study of digital signals, digital circuits, and digital systems
- Digital signals are represented by binary values (0 or 1)
- Digital circuits are built using logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, etc.)
- Digital systems can be used for data processing, control systems, and communication systems
- Topics include binary arithmetic, Boolean algebra, logic gates, flip-flops, counters, and digital-to-analog conversion.
