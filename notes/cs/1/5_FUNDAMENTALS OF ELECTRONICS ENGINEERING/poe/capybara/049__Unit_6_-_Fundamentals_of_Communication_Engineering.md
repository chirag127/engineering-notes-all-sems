## Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering

In this unit, we will cover the following topics related to communication engineering:

### 1. Communication Systems

- Definition of Communication Systems
- Basic Elements of Communication Systems
- Types of Communication Systems
- Analog and Digital Communication Systems

### 2. Signals and Spectra

- Definition of Signals
- Types of Signals
- Time and Frequency Domain Representations of Signals
- Fourier Transform and Spectral Analysis

### 3. Modulation Techniques

- Definition of Modulation
- Amplitude Modulation (AM)
- Frequency Modulation (FM)
- Phase Modulation (PM)
- Pulse Modulation Techniques

### 4. Demodulation Techniques

- Definition of Demodulation
- Envelope Detection
- Synchronous Detection
- Frequency Discrimination
- Phase Discrimination

### 5. Noise and Interference

- Definition of Noise and Interference
- Types of Noise and Interference
- Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)
- Noise Figure
- Interference Rejection Ratio (IRR)

### 6. Channel Coding and Error Control

- Definition of Channel Coding
- Types of Channel Coding
- Error Detection and Correction Techniques
- Block and Convolutional Codes
- Interleaving and Concatenation

By studying these topics, you will gain a fundamental understanding of communication engineering and its various components. This knowledge will be useful in designing and analyzing communication systems in various fields such as telecommunications, radio broadcasting, and satellite communication.
