### Special Purpose two terminal Devices

In the field of electronics engineering, there are several special purpose two terminal devices that are widely used. These devices have unique characteristics and are designed for specific applications. In this section, we will discuss some of the most important special purpose two terminal devices.

1. **Zener Diode**: A zener diode is a type of diode that is designed to operate in the reverse breakdown region. It is commonly used as a voltage regulator in electronic circuits. When a zener diode is connected in reverse bias, it allows current to flow in the reverse direction when a certain voltage is reached. This voltage is called the zener voltage and it remains constant over a wide range of current. 

2. **Varactor Diode**: A varactor diode, also known as a variable capacitance diode, is a type of diode that has a variable capacitance. It is used in tuning circuits and frequency modulators. When a voltage is applied to the varactor diode, its capacitance changes and this change can be used to tune a resonant circuit.

3. **Schottky Diode**: A Schottky diode is a type of diode that has a low forward voltage drop and fast switching speed. It is commonly used as a rectifier in power supplies and in high frequency applications. Schottky diodes are also used in mixers and detectors.

4. **Light Emitting Diode (LED)**: An LED is a type of diode that emits light when forward biased. It is commonly used as an indicator in electronic circuits and in lighting applications. LEDs are available in different colors and are energy-efficient.

5. **Photodiode**: A photodiode is a type of diode that converts light into current. It is commonly used in light sensors and optical communication systems. When light is incident on a photodiode, it generates a current that is proportional to the intensity of the light.

These special purpose two terminal devices have unique characteristics that make them suitable for specific applications. They are widely used in various electronic circuits and systems. It is important for electronics engineers to have a good understanding of these devices and their applications.
