### Number system & representation

In the world of digital electronics, numbers play a crucial role in the representation of information. Understanding the basics of number systems and their representation is essential for any student of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering. In this unit, we will cover the following topics:

1. Decimal Number System:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to binary, octal, and hexadecimal number systems
2. Binary Number System:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to decimal, octal, and hexadecimal number systems
3. Octal Number System:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to decimal, binary, and hexadecimal number systems
4. Hexadecimal Number System:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to decimal, binary, and octal number systems
5. Signed Magnitude Representation:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to binary
6. 1's Complement Representation:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to binary
7. 2's Complement Representation:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to binary
8. Floating Point Representation:
   - Definition and representation
   - Conversion to decimal and binary

By understanding the concepts of number systems and their representation, you will be able to solve problems related to digital electronics with ease. Practice and application of these concepts will help you gain confidence in the subject and achieve success in your exams.
