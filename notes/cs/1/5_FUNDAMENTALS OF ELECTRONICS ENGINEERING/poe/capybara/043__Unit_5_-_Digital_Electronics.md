## Unit 5 - Digital Electronics

Digital electronics is a branch of electronics that deals with the study of digital circuits and systems. In this unit, you will learn about the basics of digital electronics, which includes:

- **Number Systems:** The binary number system is the foundation of digital electronics. You will learn about the different number systems used in digital electronics, including binary, octal, and hexadecimal.

- **Logic Gates:** Logic gates are the fundamental building blocks of digital circuits. You will learn about the different types of logic gates, such as AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, and XOR gates.

- **Boolean Algebra:** Boolean algebra is a mathematical system used to analyze and design digital circuits. You will learn about the different laws and theorems of Boolean algebra, including De Morgan's theorem, distributive law, and associative law.

- **Combinational Logic Circuits:** Combinational logic circuits are those that produce an output based on the input values at that instant. You will learn about the different types of combinational logic circuits, including adders, subtractors, multiplexers, and demultiplexers.

- **Sequential Logic Circuits:** Sequential logic circuits are those that produce an output based on the input values and the previous state of the circuit. You will learn about the different types of sequential logic circuits, including flip-flops, shift registers, and counters.

- **Memory Devices:** Memory devices are used to store digital information. You will learn about the different types of memory devices, including RAM, ROM, and EEPROM.

- **Digital Integrated Circuits:** Digital integrated circuits are the building blocks of digital electronics systems. You will learn about the different types of digital integrated circuits, including TTL, CMOS, and ECL.

In conclusion, this unit covers the basics of digital electronics, including number systems, logic gates, Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential logic circuits, memory devices, and digital integrated circuits. By understanding these concepts, you will be able to design and analyze digital circuits and systems.
