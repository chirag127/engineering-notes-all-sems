
### Electromagnetic spectrum

* Electromagnetic spectrum is the range of all frequencies of electromagnetic radiation. 
* It includes radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, x-rays, and gamma rays. 
* Electromagnetic radiation is produced when an electric charge accelerates or oscillates. 
* The spectrum is divided into different frequency bands, which are named according to their frequency. 
* Radio waves have the longest wavelength, while gamma rays have the shortest wavelength. 
* Frequency and wavelength are inversely proportional, meaning that higher frequency waves have shorter wavelengths and vice versa. 
* Electromagnetic radiation can be used for communication, navigation, and imaging. 
* It is also used in medical treatments such as radiation therapy.
