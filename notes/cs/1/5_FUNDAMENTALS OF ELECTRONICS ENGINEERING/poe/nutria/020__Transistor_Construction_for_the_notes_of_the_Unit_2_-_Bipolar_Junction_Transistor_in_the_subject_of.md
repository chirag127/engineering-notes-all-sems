
### Transistor Construction for the notes of the Unit 2 - Bipolar Junction Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. A transistor is a semiconductor device that consists of three terminals connected to a semiconductor material.
2. The three terminals are the base, collector, and emitter. 
3. The base terminal is used to control the current flow between the collector and emitter.
4. The collector and emitter terminals are used to connect the device to an external circuit. 
5. A bipolar junction transistor (BJT) is a type of transistor that uses two types of semiconductor materials, an n-type and a p-type. 
6. The n-type material is used for the emitter and the p-type material is used for the base and collector. 
7. When a voltage is applied to the base terminal, electrons flow from the emitter to the collector, allowing current to flow between the collector and emitter. 
8. The amount of current that flows between the collector and emitter is controlled by the voltage applied to the base terminal.
