
## Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode

1. A semiconductor diode is a two-terminal electronic component that primarily consists of a semiconductor material.

2. It has two regions: the anode and the cathode. The anode is the positive terminal and the cathode is the negative terminal.

3. When a voltage is applied across the diode, current will flow from the anode to the cathode, but not the other way around. This is known as the diode's forward bias.

4. When the voltage is reversed, the diode will not allow current to flow and this is known as the diode's reverse bias.

5. The diode is a unidirectional device, meaning it allows current to flow in only one direction.

6. Diodes are commonly used in rectifier circuits, which convert alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC).

7. They are also used as voltage regulators, voltage limiters, and switches.
