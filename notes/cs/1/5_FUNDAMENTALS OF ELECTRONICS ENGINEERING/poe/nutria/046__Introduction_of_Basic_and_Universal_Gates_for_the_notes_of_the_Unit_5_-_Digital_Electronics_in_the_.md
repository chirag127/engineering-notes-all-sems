
### Introduction of Basic and Universal Gates

1. A **gate** is an electronic device that performs logical operations on one or more signals.
2. Gates are the basic building blocks of digital electronics.
3. Gates can be classified into two types: **basic** and **universal**. 
4. Basic gates are the simplest type of gate, and perform a single logical operation. Examples of basic gates include AND, OR, and NOT.
5. Universal gates are more complex, and can perform multiple logical operations. Examples of universal gates include NAND, NOR, and XOR. 
6. By combining basic and universal gates, more complex logic functions can be created.
7. Digital logic gates are used in a variety of applications, including computers, calculators, and other electronic devices.
