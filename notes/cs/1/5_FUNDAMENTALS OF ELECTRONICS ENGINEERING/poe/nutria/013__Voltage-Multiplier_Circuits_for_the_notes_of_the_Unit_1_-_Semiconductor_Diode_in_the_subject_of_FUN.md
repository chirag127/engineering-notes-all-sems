
### Voltage-Multiplier Circuits

* Voltage-multiplier circuits are electronic circuits used for converting a low-voltage AC input into a higher-voltage DC output.
* Voltage-multiplier circuits are commonly used in power supplies, battery chargers, and other applications where a high-voltage DC output is needed.
* The most common type of voltage-multiplier circuit is the diode-capacitor voltage-multiplier, also known as a "charge-pump" circuit.
* The diode-capacitor voltage-multiplier consists of a series of diodes and capacitors connected in a "ladder" configuration.
* Each diode and capacitor in the ladder is connected in such a way that the voltage across each diode and capacitor is doubled with each step.
* This allows the circuit to convert a low-voltage AC input into a higher-voltage DC output.
* Voltage-multiplier circuits can also be used to convert a low-voltage AC input into a higher-voltage AC output, by adding an inductor to the circuit.
* Voltage-multiplier circuits can be used for a variety of applications, including powering high-voltage devices, charging batteries, and providing power for other electronic circuits.
