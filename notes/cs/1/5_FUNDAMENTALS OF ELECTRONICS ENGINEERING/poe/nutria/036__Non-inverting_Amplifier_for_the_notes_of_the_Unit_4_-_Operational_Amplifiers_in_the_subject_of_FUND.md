
### Non-inverting Amplifier

* A non-inverting amplifier is an electronic circuit that amplifies an input signal without changing its phase.
* It is composed of an operational amplifier and two resistors. 
* The input signal is applied to the non-inverting terminal of the operational amplifier and the output signal is taken from the output terminal. 
* The gain of the non-inverting amplifier is determined by the ratio of the two resistors. 
* The gain of the non-inverting amplifier is always greater than one and can be adjusted by changing the values of the two resistors. 
* The non-inverting amplifier is used for applications such as signal conditioning, signal amplification, and voltage buffering.
