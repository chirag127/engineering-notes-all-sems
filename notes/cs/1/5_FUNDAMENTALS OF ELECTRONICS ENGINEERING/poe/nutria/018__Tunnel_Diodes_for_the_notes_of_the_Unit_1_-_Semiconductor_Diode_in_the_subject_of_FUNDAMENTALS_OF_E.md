
### Tunnel Diodes 

* Tunnel diodes are a type of semiconductor diode that exhibits negative resistance, allowing them to be used in high-frequency oscillators and amplifiers. 
* They are also used in switching circuits and in digital logic circuits.
* Tunnel diodes are made from a heavily doped semiconductor material, typically gallium arsenide, and have a very thin depletion region. 
* When a voltage is applied across the diode, electrons can tunnel through the thin depletion region and create a current. 
* The current-voltage (I-V) characteristic of a tunnel diode is non-linear, with a negative resistance region at low voltages. 
* This negative resistance region is useful for generating high-frequency oscillations and amplifying signals. 
* Tunnel diodes are also used in switching circuits, where they can switch between two different states quickly. 
* They are also used in digital logic circuits, where they can be used as logic gates.
