
### Special Purpose two terminal Devices for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. A semiconductor diode is a two-terminal electronic device that acts as a switch and rectifier in electrical circuits.
2. It is made from a semiconductor material such as silicon, germanium, or gallium arsenide and has two terminals, an anode and a cathode.
3. The diode is forward-biased when the positive voltage is applied to the anode and the negative voltage to the cathode.
4. When forward-biased, the diode allows current to flow in the forward direction.
5. When reverse-biased, the diode does not allow current to flow in the reverse direction.
6. The diode can also be used as a rectifier, which converts alternating current (AC) to direct current (DC).
7. Special purpose two terminal devices such as Zener diodes, light-emitting diodes (LEDs), and photodiodes are also available.
8. Zener diodes are used for voltage regulation and protection in electronic circuits.
9. LEDs are used for illumination and indicator lights.
10. Photodiodes are used for light detection and sensing in optical systems.
