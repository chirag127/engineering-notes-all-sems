
### Zener Diode as Shunt Regulator

* A Zener diode is a type of semiconductor diode that is designed to operate in the breakdown region.
* It is used as a shunt regulator to regulate the voltage across a circuit.
* The Zener diode has a low forward voltage drop and a high reverse breakdown voltage.
* The Zener diode is connected in reverse bias and operates in the breakdown region.
* The Zener diode is used to maintain a constant voltage across a load.
* The Zener diode is used as a voltage reference in many applications.
* The Zener diode can be used as a voltage regulator in a linear power supply.
* The Zener diode can be used in circuit protection to limit the voltage across a circuit.
* The Zener diode can be used in voltage clamping circuits to limit the voltage across a circuit.
