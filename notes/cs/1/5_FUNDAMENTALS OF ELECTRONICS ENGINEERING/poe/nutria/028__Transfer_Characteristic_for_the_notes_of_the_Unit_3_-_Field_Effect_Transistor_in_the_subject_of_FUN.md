
### Transfer Characteristic for the notes of the Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. A field-effect transistor (FET) is a type of transistor that uses an electric field to control the flow of current.

2. FETs are three-terminal devices, with a source, a drain, and a gate. The gate is used to control the flow of current between the source and the drain.

3. The transfer characteristic of an FET describes the relationship between the drain current and the gate-source voltage.

4. The transfer characteristic of an FET is usually represented as a graph, with the drain current plotted on the y-axis and the gate-source voltage plotted on the x-axis.

5. The transfer characteristic of an FET is typically a curve, with the drain current increasing as the gate-source voltage increases.

6. The transfer characteristic of an FET is affected by several factors, including the type of FET (e.g. n-channel or p-channel), the gate-source voltage, the drain-source voltage, and the temperature.
