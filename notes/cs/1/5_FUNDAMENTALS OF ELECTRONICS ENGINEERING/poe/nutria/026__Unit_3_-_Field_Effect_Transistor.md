
## Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor

1. A field-effect transistor (FET) is a type of transistor that uses an electric field to control the flow of current. 
2. FETs are three-terminal devices, consisting of a source, a drain, and a gate. 
3. The gate terminal is insulated from the other two terminals and acts as a control element. 
4. When a voltage is applied to the gate, it modulates the conductivity of the channel between the source and the drain, allowing current to flow between them. 
5. FETs can be used as amplifiers, switches, or voltage-controlled resistors. 
6. FETs are used in a wide variety of applications, including radio frequency (RF) electronics, analog signal processing, and digital logic circuits.
