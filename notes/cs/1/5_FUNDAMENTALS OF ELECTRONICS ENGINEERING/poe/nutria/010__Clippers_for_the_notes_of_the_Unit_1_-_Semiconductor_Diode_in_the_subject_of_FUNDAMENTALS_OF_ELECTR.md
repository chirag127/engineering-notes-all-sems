
### Clippers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. Clippers are electronic circuits that are used to limit the maximum amplitude of a signal. 
2. They are widely used in electronics engineering to prevent the signal from exceeding a certain level. 
3. The most common type of clipper is the diode clipper, which uses a diode to limit the peak voltage of the signal. 
4. The diode clipper can be designed to produce either a positive or a negative clipping effect. 
5. In a positive clipping circuit, the diode is connected in series with the input signal and the output is taken across the diode. 
6. When the input voltage exceeds the diode’s forward voltage drop, the diode conducts, allowing the input signal to pass through to the output. 
7. In a negative clipping circuit, the diode is connected in parallel with the input signal and the output is taken across the diode. 
8. When the input voltage exceeds the diode’s reverse voltage drop, the diode conducts, allowing the input signal to pass through to the output. 
9. Clippers are used in many applications, such as audio amplifiers and radio transmitters.
