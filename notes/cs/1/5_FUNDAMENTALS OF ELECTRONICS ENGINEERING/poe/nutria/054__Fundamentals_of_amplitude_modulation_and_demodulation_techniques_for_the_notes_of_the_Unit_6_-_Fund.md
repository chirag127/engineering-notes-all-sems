

### Fundamentals of Amplitude Modulation and Demodulation Techniques 

1. Amplitude Modulation (AM) is a type of modulation used in radio communication systems, wherein the amplitude of the carrier wave is varied in accordance with the amplitude of the message signal.

2. Demodulation is the process of recovering the original message signal from the modulated carrier wave.

3. The most common type of demodulation used in communication systems is the envelope detector or the synchronous detector.

4. AM signals can be demodulated using either linear or non-linear techniques.

5. Linear techniques such as the synchronous detector or the envelope detector are used for demodulating AM signals.

6. Non-linear techniques such as the Foster-Seeley discriminator and the ratio detector are used for demodulating AM signals.

7. AM signals can also be demodulated using digital techniques such as the Goertzel algorithm.

8. The amplitude of the demodulated signal is proportional to the amplitude of the modulated signal.
