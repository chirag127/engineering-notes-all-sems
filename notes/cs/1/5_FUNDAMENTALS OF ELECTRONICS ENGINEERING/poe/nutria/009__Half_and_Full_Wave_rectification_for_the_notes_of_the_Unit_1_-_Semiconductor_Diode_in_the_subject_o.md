
### Half and Full Wave Rectification

* Half Wave Rectification: Half wave rectification is a process of converting AC (Alternating Current) to DC (Direct Current) by allowing only one-half cycle of the AC waveform to pass through the diode. The diode acts as a switch, allowing only the positive half of the AC waveform to pass through to the output.
* Full Wave Rectification: Full wave rectification is a process of converting AC (Alternating Current) to DC (Direct Current) by allowing both half cycles of the AC waveform to pass through the diode. The two diodes act as switches, allowing both the positive and negative half of the AC waveform to pass through to the output.
