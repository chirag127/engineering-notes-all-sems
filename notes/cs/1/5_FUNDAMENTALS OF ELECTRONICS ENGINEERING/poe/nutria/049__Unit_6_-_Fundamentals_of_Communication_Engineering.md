
## Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering

1. Communication engineering is the study of how information is transmitted and received over communication networks.
2. It involves the design and implementation of communication systems, such as radio, television, satellite, and cellular networks.
3. Communication engineering also involves the analysis of communication signals, such as speech, audio, and video signals.
4. Communication engineering also involves the study of coding and modulation techniques for efficient transmission of information.
5. Communication engineering also involves the study of wireless communication systems, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks.
6. Communication engineering also involves the study of antenna design and propagation of electromagnetic waves.
7. Communication engineering also involves the study of network protocols, such as TCP/IP, and network security.
8. Communication engineering also involves the study of network topologies and routing algorithms.
9. Communication engineering also involves the study of digital signal processing techniques, such as compression, filtering, and modulation.
10. Communication engineering also involves the study of communication networks, such as the internet, and the design of communication systems.
