### Varactor Diodes

- Varactor diodes are semiconductor diodes that are specifically designed to take advantage of the change in capacitance with voltage.
- The junction capacitance of a normal diode varies with the applied voltage - it decreases with increasing reverse voltage. This property can be exploited in varactor diodes to produce a voltage-controlled capacitance.
- Varactor diodes are typically manufactured from silicon or gallium arsenide and have a very low breakdown voltage. This enables the capacitance to vary over a relatively wide range.
- The capacitance of a varactor diode can be varied by a factor of up to 10 or even more. However it is non-linear and this needs to be taken into account in any application.
- Varactor diodes are used as capacitors in a number of tuning applications where a change in frequency is required including in voltage controlled oscillators, radio frequency tuners, and phase locked loops.
- By changing the reverse bias on the diode, it is possible to change the capacitance and hence tune to different frequencies. This makes varactor diodes very useful in many radio frequency tuning applications.
