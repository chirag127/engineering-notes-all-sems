### Transfer Characteristic for the notes of the Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. The transfer characteristic of a FET can be obtained by plotting the drain current (ID) versus the gate-source voltage (VGS) for different values of drain-source voltage (VDS). 
2. The shape of the transfer characteristic is basically similar to that of a vacuum tube with following regions:
- Cut-off region: When VGS < VP, the FET is in cut-off and acts as an open switch. ID = 0 in this region.
- Ohmic region or Linear region: When VGS lies between VP and VPP, the FET operates in its ohmic region and ID increases linearly with VGS.
- Saturation region: When VGS > VPP, the FET operates in its saturation region and ID becomes independent of VGS and is determined by VDS.
3. The drain characteristics can be plotted between ID and VDS for different values of VGS. The drain characteristics are either rectangular hyperbolas or consist of linear and saturation regions.
4. The operation of a FET can be explained with the help of potential diagrams which show variation of potential with distance.
