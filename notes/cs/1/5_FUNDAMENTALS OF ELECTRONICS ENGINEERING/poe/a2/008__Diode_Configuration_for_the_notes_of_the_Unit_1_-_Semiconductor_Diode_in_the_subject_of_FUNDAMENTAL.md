### Diode Configuration

1. Forward Bias Configuration: When the P-type material is connected to the positive terminal and N-type material is connected to the negative terminal of a battery, it is called forward bias configuration. In this configuration, the diode allows maximum current to flow through it.
2. Reverse Bias Configuration: When the P-type material is connected to the negative terminal and N-type material is connected to the positive terminal of a battery, it is called reverse bias configuration. In this configuration, the diode does not allow any significant current to flow through it. Only a small amount of saturation current flows through the diode.
3. Typical I-V characteristics: The current versus voltage (I-V) characteristics of a diode shows the relation between the current flowing through the diode and the voltage applied across the diode. For forward bias, the current increases exponentially with increase in voltage whereas for reverse bias, the current remains almost constant and does not increase significantly with increase in voltage.
