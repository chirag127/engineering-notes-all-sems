### MOSFET (MOS) (Depletion and Enhancement) Type for the notes of the Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- MOSFET stands for Metal Oxide Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor. It is a voltage controlled device.
- It has 4 terminals - Source, Drain, Gate and Substrate.
- The gate is insulated from the channel by a thin layer of oxide. Hence it is called as Metal Oxide Semiconductor (MOS).
- The conductivity of the channel between source and drain is controlled by the gate voltage.
- There are 2 types of MOSFET - Depletion MOSFET and Enhancement MOSFET.
- In Depletion MOSFET, the channel is depleted when no gate voltage is applied. It requires -ve gate voltage to create a channel and allow current to flow.
- In Enhancement MOSFET, no channel is formed when no gate voltage is applied. It requires +ve gate voltage to create a channel and allow current to flow.
- MOSFET has very high input impedance, no transconductance and operates in high frequency range. It can be easily interfaced with digital circuits.
- MOSFET has various configurations such as common-source, common-gate and common-drain. Common-source is the most preferred configuration.
