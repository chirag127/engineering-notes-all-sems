### Basics of signal representation and analysis for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. Representation of signals
- Time domain representation: Signal represented as a function of time. Example: Sine and cosine waves.
- Frequency domain representation: Signal represented as a function of frequency. Example: Spectrum of a signal. Fourier transform is used to convert between time and frequency domain representations.

2. Types of signals
- Periodic signals: Signals that repeat their values at regular intervals. Example: Sine and cosine waves.
- Aperiodic signals: Signals that do not repeat their values at regular intervals. Example: Exponential signal.

3. Analysis of signals
- Amplitude analysis: Determining amplitude variations with respect to time. Used to analyze the strength or magnitude of a signal.
- Frequency analysis: Determining frequency components and their strengths. Used for spectrum analysis and filtering applications. Fourier transform is used for frequency analysis.
- Phase analysis: Determining phase differences and shifts. Used to analyze delay between two signals.
