### Clippers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- Clippers are circuits that are used to remove a portion of the applied waveform to get the required output waveform.
- They are also called clampers as they clamp the output waveform to a particular voltage level.
- The diode is the main component used in clipper circuits to perform the clipping action. The diode conducts during the positive or negative portion of the input cycle, thereby clipping the input waveform.
- Based on the portion of the waveform clipped, clippers are classified into:
- Positive clipper - clips the positive half cycles of the input waveform
- Negative clipper - clips the negative half cycles of the input waveform
- Positive and negative clipper - clips both positive and negative half cycles of the input waveform
- Applications - signal limiting, noise removal, waveform shaping.
