### Diode Equivalent Circuits

- A diode can be represented by an ideal equivalent circuit for analysis purposes.
- The ideal diode circuit consists of a voltage-controlled switch that closes when the voltage across the diode is positive (forward-biased) and opens when the voltage across the diode is negative (reverse-biased).
- The switch closure in the ideal diode circuit represents the low resistance of the diode during forward conduction. The switch opening represents the high resistance of the diode during reverse bias.
- The ideal diode equivalent circuit is useful for analyzing the steady-state behavior of diode circuits. However, it does not model the capacitance of a real diode, which becomes important when analyzing transient circuit behavior.
- A more complete diode model is the diode capacitance model, which incorporates the junction capacitance of a real diode. The diode capacitance model provides a more accurate representation of a real diode at high frequencies.
