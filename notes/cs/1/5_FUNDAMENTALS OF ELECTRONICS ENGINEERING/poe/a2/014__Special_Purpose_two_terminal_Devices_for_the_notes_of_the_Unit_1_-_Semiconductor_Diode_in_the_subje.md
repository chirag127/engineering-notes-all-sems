### Special Purpose two terminal Devices for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. Diode:
- A two terminal semiconductor device that allows current to flow only in one direction.
- When forward biased, it allows current and when reverse biased, it blocks current.
- Types: PN junction diode, Schottky diode, Zener diode, Light emitting diode, Photodiode, Varactor diode.

2. Zener Diode:
- A heavily doped PN junction diode that is designed to operate in the reverse breakdown region.
- Used for voltage regulation and reference as it maintains a constant voltage across its terminals after reaching the breakdown voltage.
- Breakdown voltage can be varied based on doping level.

3. Light Emitting Diode (LED):
- A PN junction diode that emits light when forward biased.
- When electrons cross the junction, they release energy in the form of light. Color of light depends on the material used.
- Used in optical fiber communication, LED displays, etc.
