### Integrator for the notes of the Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

1. An integrator is an op-amp circuit which performs integration of the input signal.
2. It converts the input voltage signal into an output current signal.
3. The output voltage increases indefinitely as the input is applied for longer duration.
4. The integrator circuit uses a capacitor in the feedback path.
5. The capacitor gets charged with respect to the applied input voltage which results in the change in output voltage.
6. The output voltage is the integral of the input voltage.
7. The integrator circuit has a constant gain of -1/sC where s is the Laplace variable and C is the value of the capacitor.
8. The integrator circuit can be used to generate ramp signals and also for analog computation applications.
9. The frequency response of an integrator shows that the gain increases with decreasing frequency.
10. The integrator circuit has a high-pass filter characteristic.
