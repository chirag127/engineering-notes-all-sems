### Diode Application for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- Diodes are used as rectifiers in power supply circuits to convert AC to DC. This is the most common application of diodes.
- Diodes are used as clampers to shift the dc voltage level and ac signal to a desired level.
- Diodes are used as voltage multipliers to obtain a high dc voltage from ac signal.
- Diodes are used as detectors in radio receivers to demodulate the AM signals.
- Diodes are used as limiters to limit the amplitude of ac signals.
- Diodes are used as oscillators to generate electronic oscillations.
- Light emitting diodes (LEDs) are used as indicators in various electronic circuits and devices.
- Photo diodes are used as light sensors in various applications like photo resistors, etc.
- Diodes are used in logic gates (like OR gate and AND gate) and also in various other digital circuits.
- Diodes are used in various regulated power supply circuits.
