### ideal and practical Diodes for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering deals with the study of diodes, which are two-terminal electronic devices that allow current to flow in only one direction.

Ideal diodes are theoretical models that have zero resistance in the forward direction and infinite resistance in the reverse direction. Practical diodes, on the other hand, have a small but finite resistance in the forward direction and a larger but finite resistance in the reverse direction.

In this unit, students learn about the basic properties of ideal and practical diodes, including their I-V characteristics and their applications in electronics circuits. They also learn about the different types of diodes, including pn-junction diodes, Zener diodes, and Schottky diodes, and their applications in rectification, voltage regulation, and other electronic circuits.

The study of diodes is an important part of electronics engineering, as diodes are widely used in many electronic devices and systems, including power supplies, electronic circuits, and communication systems. Understanding the properties and applications of diodes is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in electronics engineering, as they will be involved in the design and development of electronic devices and systems.
