### Common Collector Configuration for the notes of the Unit 2 - Bipolar Junction Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Common Collector Configuration: 
- Also known as Emitter Follower
- Transistor is connected such that the emitter is common to both input and output
- Input is applied to the base and output is taken from the collector
- Acts as voltage amplifier with gain less than 1
- Impedance is high at input and low at output
- Used as buffer stage, voltage stabilizer, and current amplifier.
