### Electromagnetic spectrum for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Electromagnetic Spectrum:
- Range of all frequencies of electromagnetic radiation
- Includes radio waves, microwaves, infrared, visible light, ultraviolet, X-rays, and gamma rays
- Each frequency range has unique properties and uses in communication engineering
- Radio waves have long wavelengths and low frequencies, used for long-distance communication
- Microwaves have shorter wavelengths and higher frequencies, used for satellite communication
- Infrared has even shorter wavelengths, used for remote control and thermal imaging
- Visible light has even shorter wavelengths, used for optical communication
- Ultraviolet, X-rays, and gamma rays have even shorter wavelengths and higher frequencies, used in medical and scientific applications.
