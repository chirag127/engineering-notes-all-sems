### K Map Minimization upto 6 Variables for the notes of the Unit 5 - Digital Electronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Karnaugh Map (K-Map) Minimization:
- Method to simplify Boolean expressions
- Graphical representation of truth table
- Used to minimize the number of gates in digital circuits
- For 6 variables, K-Map is divided into 64 cells
- Adjacent cells with same value are grouped to form a "group"
- Largest possible group is selected and its corresponding minterm is used
- Process is repeated until all minterms are used in the simplified expression.
