## Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering

Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering:

- Introduces the fundamental concepts of communication systems
- Covers communication channels, modulation, and demodulation techniques
- Study of analog and digital communication systems
- Discusses the basics of data transmission, error detection and correction
- Study of wireless communication systems and their applications
- Learn about the principles of communication networks and their protocols
- Discusses the basics of satellite and optical communication systems

The goal of this unit is to understand the principles and applications of communication engineering and how they are used in the design and implementation of communication systems.
