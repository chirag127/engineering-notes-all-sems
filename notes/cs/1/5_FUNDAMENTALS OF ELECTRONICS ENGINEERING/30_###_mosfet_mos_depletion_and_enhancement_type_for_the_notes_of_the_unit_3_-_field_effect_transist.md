### MOSFET (MOS) (Depletion and Enhancement) Type for the notes of the Unit 3 - Field Effect Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

MOSFET (Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Field-Effect Transistor) is a type of Field Effect Transistor (FET) that uses a metal gate, oxide insulator, and a semiconductor channel. There are two types of MOSFETs: Depletion-Type and Enhancement-Type.

Depletion-Type MOSFET: In this type, the channel is normally on and the gate-source voltage (VGS) is used to turn off the channel by depleting the charge carriers in the channel. This type is used for load switching applications.

Enhancement-Type MOSFET: In this type, the channel is normally off and the gate-source voltage (VGS) is used to turn on the channel by enhancing the charge carriers in the channel. This type is used for amplification and switching applications.

Both types of MOSFETs have a high input impedance, fast switching speed, and low power consumption, making them widely used in digital and analog circuits.
