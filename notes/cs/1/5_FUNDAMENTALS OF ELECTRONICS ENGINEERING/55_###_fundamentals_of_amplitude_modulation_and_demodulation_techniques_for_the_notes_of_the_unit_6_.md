### Fundamentals of amplitude modulation and demodulation techniques for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Amplitude Modulation (AM) and demodulation are fundamental concepts in communication engineering and are covered in Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering. AM is a technique used to transmit information by modulating the amplitude of a carrier signal. The carrier signal is a sine wave with a constant frequency and amplitude, and the information is added to the carrier signal by varying its amplitude in accordance with the information signal. The resulting modulated signal is then transmitted over a communication channel to the receiver, where the demodulation process is used to recover the original information signal. The demodulation process involves detecting the amplitude of the modulated signal and converting it back into the original information signal. Understanding the fundamentals of AM and demodulation is essential for students pursuing electronics engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in communication engineering and electronics.
