### Varactor Diodes

- A varactor diode is a type of diode that behaves as a variable capacitor. 
- Its capacitance at the p-n junction changes with the change in reverse voltage applied across its terminals. 
- It is mainly used to replace mechanical variable capacitors in applications such as voltage-controlled oscillators, parametric amplifiers, and frequency multipliers.  
- It always operates in reverse bias condition, where the diode does not conduct current but allows the electric field to vary across the depletion region. 
- The capacitance of a varactor diode is given by the formula: 

$$C = C_0 \sqrt{\frac{V_0}{V_0 + V_r}}$$

where $C$ is the capacitance, $C_0$ is the zero-bias capacitance, $V_0$ is the built-in potential, and $V_r$ is the reverse voltage.
