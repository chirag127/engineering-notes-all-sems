# Overview of wireless communication

- Wireless communication is the transmission of information over a distance without the help of wires, cables or any other forms of electrical conductors .
- Wireless communication uses electromagnetic signals, such as radio waves, microwaves, infrared, optical, and satellite signals, to broadcast data from sending facilities to intermediate and end-user devices .
- Wireless communication can be used for various applications, such as cellular telephony, wireless access to the internet, wireless home networking, wireless sensor networks, wireless local area networks, wireless personal area networks, wireless metropolitan area networks, wireless wide area networks, and wireless ad hoc networks .
- Wireless communication has several advantages over wired communication, such as mobility, flexibility, scalability, cost-effectiveness, and reliability .
- Wireless communication also has some challenges and limitations, such as interference, noise, security, power consumption, bandwidth, and range .
