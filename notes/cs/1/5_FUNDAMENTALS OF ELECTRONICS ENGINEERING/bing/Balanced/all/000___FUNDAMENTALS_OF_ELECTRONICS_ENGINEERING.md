# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Electronics engineering is a branch of engineering that deals with the design, development and testing of electronic devices and systems. Electronics engineers work with various components and circuits that manipulate electric signals, such as resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, microcontrollers, sensors, etc.

Some of the topics that are covered in the fundamentals of electronics engineering are:

- Electricity: The flow of electric charge through a conductor. There are two types of electric signals: alternating current (AC) and direct current (DC). AC is the type of current that changes direction periodically, such as the power supply from the wall socket. DC is the type of current that flows in one direction only, such as the power supply from a battery.
- Circuits: A complete and closed path through which electric current can flow. A circuit consists of various elements, such as power sources, loads, switches, wires, etc. A circuit can be analyzed using various laws and methods, such as Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, Thevenin's theorem, etc.
- Resistance: The opposition to the flow of electric current in a conductor. Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω) and depends on factors such as the material, length, cross-sectional area and temperature of the conductor. Resistance can be used to control the amount of current and voltage in a circuit.
- Series and parallel circuits: The arrangement of circuit elements in a circuit. In a series circuit, the elements are connected one after another, such that the same current flows through all of them. In a parallel circuit, the elements are connected across each other, such that the same voltage is applied across all of them. The total resistance, current and voltage in a series or parallel circuit can be calculated using formulas and rules.
- Basic components: The common elements that are used in electronic circuits, such as resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, etc. Each component has a specific function and symbol in a circuit diagram. For example, a resistor is used to limit the current, a capacitor is used to store electric charge, a diode is used to allow current to flow in one direction only, a transistor is used to amplify or switch electric signals, etc.
- Resistors: The most basic and widely used component in electronic circuits. A resistor is a device that has a fixed or variable resistance value. Resistors can be classified into different types, such as fixed resistors, variable resistors, potentiometers, thermistors, etc. Resistors can be connected in series or parallel to obtain the desired resistance value in a circuit.
- Capacitors: Another common and important component in electronic circuits. A capacitor is a device that can store electric charge and energy in an electric field. A capacitor consists of two conductive plates separated by an insulator, such as air, paper, ceramic, etc. Capacitors can be classified into different types, such as fixed capacitors, variable capacitors, electrolytic capacitors, etc. Capacitors can be connected in series or parallel to obtain the desired capacitance value in a circuit.
- Diodes: A component that allows current to flow in one direction only. A diode is a device that has two terminals, called anode and cathode. A diode is made of a semiconductor material, such as silicon, germanium, etc. A diode can be classified into different types, such as rectifier diodes, zener diodes, light-emitting diodes (LEDs), etc. Diodes can be used for various purposes, such as rectification, voltage regulation, signal modulation, etc.
