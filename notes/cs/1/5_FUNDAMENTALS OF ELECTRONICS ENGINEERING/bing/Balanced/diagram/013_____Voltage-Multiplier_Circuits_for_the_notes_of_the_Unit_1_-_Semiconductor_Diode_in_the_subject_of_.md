### Voltage-Multiplier Circuits

- A voltage multiplier is an electronic circuit consisting of capacitors and diodes and is used to multiply or rise the voltage level of an AC signal.
- The voltage multiplier receives an AC voltage of lower value, converts it into a DC voltage and increases its voltage level.
- Voltage multiplier circuits are classified as voltage doubler’s, tripler’s, or quadrupler’s, etc, depending on the ratio of the output voltage to the input voltage.
- In theory any desired amount of voltage multiplication can be obtained and a cascade of “N” doublers, would produce an output voltage of 2N.Vp volts, where Vp is the peak input voltage.
- Voltage multipliers can be used to generate a few volts for electronic appliances, to millions of volts for purposes such as high-energy physics experiments and lightning safety testing.

#### Voltage Doubler Circuit

- A voltage doubler is a voltage multiplier circuit that produces an output voltage that is twice the peak input voltage.
- A voltage doubler consists of two diodes and two capacitors connected in a specific configuration.
- There are two types of voltage doubler circuits: half-wave and full-wave.
- A half-wave voltage doubler consists of a capacitor connected in parallel with the AC input and a diode connected in series with the output.
- A full-wave voltage doubler consists of two half-wave voltage doublers connected in series, with a common ground.
- The operation of a voltage doubler circuit can be explained by the charging and discharging of the capacitors during the positive and negative half cycles of the AC input.
- The output voltage of a voltage doubler circuit is given by Vout = 2Vp - 2Vd, where Vp is the peak input voltage and Vd is the diode voltage drop.
- The output voltage of a voltage doubler circuit is not exactly twice the input voltage, due to the voltage drop across the diodes and the capacitor leakage.
- The output voltage of a voltage doubler circuit can be increased by adding more stages of half-wave voltage doublers in series.

#### Voltage Tripler Circuit

- A voltage tripler is a voltage multiplier circuit that produces an output voltage that is three times the peak input voltage.
- A voltage tripler consists of three diodes and three capacitors connected in a specific configuration.
- A voltage tripler circuit can be derived from a full-wave voltage doubler circuit by adding another stage of half-wave voltage doubler in series.
- The operation of a voltage tripler circuit can be explained by the charging and discharging of the capacitors during the positive and negative half cycles of the AC input.
- The output voltage of a voltage tripler circuit is given by Vout = 3Vp - 3Vd, where Vp is the peak input voltage and Vd is the diode voltage drop.
- The output voltage of a voltage tripler circuit is not exactly three times the input voltage, due to the voltage drop across the diodes and the capacitor leakage.
- The output voltage of a voltage tripler circuit can be increased by adding more stages of half-wave voltage doublers in series.

#### Voltage Quadrupler Circuit
