# FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Electronics engineering is a branch of engineering that deals with the design, development and testing of electronic devices and systems. Electronics engineers work with various components and technologies such as resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, integrated circuits, microcontrollers, sensors, wireless communication, digital signal processing, and more.

Some of the topics that are covered in the fundamentals of electronics engineering are:

- Electricity: The study of electric charge, current, voltage, power, energy, and circuits. Electricity is the basis of all electronic devices and systems.
- Resistance: The measure of how much a material or a component opposes the flow of electric current. Resistance is an important factor in determining the behavior and performance of electronic circuits.
- Series and parallel circuits: The two basic ways of connecting components in a circuit. Series circuits have only one path for current to flow, while parallel circuits have multiple paths. The total resistance, voltage, and current in a series or parallel circuit can be calculated using simple formulas.
- Basic components: The common elements that are used to build electronic circuits and systems. Some of the basic components are resistors, capacitors, diodes, transistors, LEDs, switches, and potentiometers. Each component has a specific function and symbol in a circuit diagram.
- Resistors: Components that limit the flow of current in a circuit. Resistors are used to control the voltage and current levels, to divide or distribute voltage, to protect other components, and to create different types of circuits such as voltage dividers, current sources, and filters.
- Capacitors: Components that store electric charge and energy. Capacitors are used to smooth out voltage fluctuations, to block direct current and pass alternating current, to store and release energy, and to create different types of circuits such as timers, oscillators, and filters.
- Diodes: Components that allow current to flow in one direction only. Diodes are used to convert alternating current to direct current, to prevent reverse current flow, to regulate voltage, and to create different types of circuits such as rectifiers, clippers, clampers, and logic gates.
- Transistors: Components that can amplify or switch electric signals. Transistors are the building blocks of modern electronics and can be used to create different types of circuits such as amplifiers, oscillators, switches, and logic gates.
