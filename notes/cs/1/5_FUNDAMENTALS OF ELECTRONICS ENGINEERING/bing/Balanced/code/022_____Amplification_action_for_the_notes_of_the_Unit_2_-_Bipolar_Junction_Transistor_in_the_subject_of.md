### Amplification action for the notes of the Unit 2 - Bipolar Junction Transistor in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

- A bipolar junction transistor (BJT) is a three-terminal device that can amplify the flow of current or voltage depending on the configuration .
- A BJT consists of two p-n junctions, one between the base and the emitter, and the other between the base and the collector .
- The base is the control terminal, the emitter is the input terminal, and the collector is the output terminal .
- A BJT can operate in three regions: cut-off, active, and saturation  .
- In the cut-off region, the base-emitter junction and the base-collector junction are both reverse biased, and no current flows through the transistor  .
- In the active region, the base-emitter junction is forward biased and the base-collector junction is reverse biased, and the transistor acts as an amplifier  .
- In the saturation region, the base-emitter junction and the base-collector junction are both forward biased, and the transistor acts as a switch  .
- The amplification action of a BJT is based on the fact that a small change in the base current can cause a large change in the collector current, as the collector current is proportional to the base current by a factor called the current gain   .
- The current gain of a BJT depends on the type and configuration of the transistor. There are two types of BJTs: npn and pnp, which differ in the polarity of the terminals and the direction of the current flow .
- There are three basic configurations of BJTs: common emitter, common base, and common collector, which differ in the input and output terminals and the voltage and current gains  .
- A common emitter configuration has the emitter as the common terminal, the base as the input terminal, and the collector as the output terminal. It has a high voltage gain and a high current gain  .
- A common base configuration has the base as the common terminal, the emitter as the input terminal, and the collector as the output terminal. It has a low voltage gain and a high current gain  .
- A common collector configuration has the collector as the common terminal, the base as the input terminal, and the emitter as the output terminal. It has a low voltage gain and a low current gain  .
- A BJT amplifier can be designed by choosing the appropriate type, configuration, and biasing of the transistor, and by using external components such as resistors and capacitors to control the input and output impedances, the frequency response, and the feedback of the circuit .
- A BJT amplifier can be used for various applications, such as audio amplification, signal processing, radio transmission, power conversion, and switching .
