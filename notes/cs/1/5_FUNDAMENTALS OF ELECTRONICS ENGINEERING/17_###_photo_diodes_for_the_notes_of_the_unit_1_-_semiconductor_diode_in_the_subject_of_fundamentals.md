### Photo Diodes for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Photo diodes are a type of semiconductor diode that convert light into electrical current. They work by absorbing photons and generating electron-hole pairs, which then generate a current. Photo diodes have a high sensitivity to light and a fast response time, making them useful in a variety of applications such as optical communication, light detection, and image sensors. They can be operated in either photoconductive or photovoltaic mode, depending on the application. Photo diodes can also be used in conjunction with amplifiers to detect low-level light signals. Photo diodes have a reverse-biased p-n junction and are usually made of silicon or other semiconductors.
