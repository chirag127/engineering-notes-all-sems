### Comparators for the notes of the Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

A comparator is an electronic circuit that compares two input voltages and outputs a digital signal indicating which input is larger. Comparators are used in many applications, including analog-to-digital converters, zero crossing detectors, and flip-flops. Unit 4 of Operational Amplifiers in the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering covers the use of comparators in electronic circuits, including the design and analysis of comparator circuits, the use of comparators in feedback circuits, and the use of comparators in applications such as analog-to-digital conversion. Understanding the use of comparators is important for students studying Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, as it provides a basis for understanding the behavior of electronic circuits and the design of digital systems.
