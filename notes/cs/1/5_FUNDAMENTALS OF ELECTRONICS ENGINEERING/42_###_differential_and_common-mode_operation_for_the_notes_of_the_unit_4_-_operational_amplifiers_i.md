### Differential and Common-Mode Operation for the notes of the Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Differential Operation: In differential operation, the input signals are applied to both the positive and negative input terminals of the operational amplifier. The difference between the two inputs is amplified and outputted. This mode of operation is used to reject common-mode signals, which are present in both inputs.

Common-Mode Operation: In common-mode operation, the input signals are applied to both the positive and negative input terminals of the operational amplifier, but the same signal is applied to both inputs. The operational amplifier amplifies the common-mode signal and outputs it. This mode of operation is used to amplify signals that are present in both inputs.
