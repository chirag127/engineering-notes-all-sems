### Introduction to Wireless Communication for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Wireless communication refers to the transfer of information over a distance without the use of physical connections, such as wires or cables. In the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering, students learn about the basics of wireless communication systems. This includes the different types of wireless communication systems, such as cellular networks, Wi-Fi networks, and satellite communication systems. Students also learn about the different technologies used in wireless communication, such as radio waves, infrared, and microwave technology. The unit covers the principles of modulation and demodulation, which are used to encode and decode information for transmission over a wireless channel. Additionally, students learn about the challenges of wireless communication, such as fading, interference, and limited bandwidth, and how these challenges are addressed through techniques such as error correction and multiplexing. Understanding the fundamentals of wireless communication is essential for students pursuing electronics engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in communication engineering and wireless networks.
