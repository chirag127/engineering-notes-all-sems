### Elements of a Communication System for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Elements of a Communication System:
1. Message source: originates the information to be transmitted
2. Transmitter: converts the message into a signal suitable for transmission
3. Channel: medium through which the signal is transmitted
4. Receiver: converts the received signal back into a usable form
5. Destination: receives the message in its original form
6. Noise: any unwanted signal that interferes with the transmission of the message
7. Feedback: information about the performance of the system that is used to improve it.
