### Fundamentals of Satellite & Radar Communication for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING KCS

Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering deals with the study of the principles and techniques used in communication systems. One important aspect of communication engineering is satellite and radar communication.

Satellite communication is the use of artificial satellites to provide communication services, such as voice, data, and video transmission. Satellite communication systems use a network of satellites in orbit around the Earth to relay signals from one point on the Earth to another.

Radar communication is the use of radio waves to detect and track objects, such as aircraft, ships, and vehicles. Radar communication systems use a combination of transmitters, receivers, and antennas to send and receive signals, and to determine the distance, speed, and direction of objects.

In this unit, students learn about the basic principles of satellite and radar communication, including the design and operation of satellite and radar communication systems, and the techniques used to analyze and optimize these systems. The study of satellite and radar communication is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in electronics engineering, as these technologies play a crucial role in many communication and navigation systems.
