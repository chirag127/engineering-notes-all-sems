### using Boolean algebra simplification of Boolean function for the notes of the Unit 5 - Digital Electronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Boolean Algebra Simplification of Boolean Function:
- Method for reducing a Boolean expression to its simplest form
- Uses rules of Boolean algebra such as AND, OR, NOT, De Morgan's theorem, etc.
- Involves combining like terms, removing redundant terms, and using identities to simplify the expression
- Resulting expression represents the same logic function as the original but with fewer terms
- Used to minimize the number of gates required to implement a digital circuit.
