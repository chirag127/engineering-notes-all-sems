### Number system & representation for the notes of the Unit 5 - Digital Electronics in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Unit 5 - Digital Electronics in the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering deals with the study of digital circuits and systems. One important aspect of digital electronics is the number system and representation.

A number system is a system of representing numbers using symbols or digits. The most commonly used number systems in digital electronics are the binary, octal, and hexadecimal number systems.

In binary number system, numbers are represented using only two symbols, 0 and 1. The binary system is used in digital electronics because it is easy to implement using electronic components such as transistors and diodes.

Octal and hexadecimal number systems are used to simplify the representation of binary numbers. In octal number system, numbers are represented using eight symbols, 0 to 7. In hexadecimal number system, numbers are represented using sixteen symbols, 0 to 9 and A to F.

In this unit, students learn about the basic principles of number systems and representation in digital electronics, including the conversion between different number systems and the representation of numbers using binary, octal, and hexadecimal systems. The study of number systems and representation is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in electronics engineering, as it provides a foundation for understanding the representation of data in digital circuits and systems.
