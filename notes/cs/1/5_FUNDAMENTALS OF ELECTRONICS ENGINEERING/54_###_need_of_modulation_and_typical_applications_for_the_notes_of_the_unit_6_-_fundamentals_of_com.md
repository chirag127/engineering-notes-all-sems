### Need of modulation and typical applications for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Need of Modulation:
- Modulation is the process of encoding information onto a carrier signal
- This allows the information to be transmitted over long distances
- Modulation is necessary because the information-carrying signal is often too weak to be transmitted over long distances without degradation

Typical Applications:
- Radio and television broadcasting
- Wireless communication systems (e.g. cell phones, Wi-Fi)
- Satellite communication systems
- Digital audio and video broadcasting
- Control systems (e.g. industrial control, remote control)
- Navigation systems (e.g. GPS)
