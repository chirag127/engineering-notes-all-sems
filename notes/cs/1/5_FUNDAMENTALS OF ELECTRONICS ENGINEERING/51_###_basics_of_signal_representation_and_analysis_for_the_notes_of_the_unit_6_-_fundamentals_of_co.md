### Basics of signal representation and analysis for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Basics of signal representation and analysis:
- Signals are representations of physical quantities such as voltage, current, and power
- Signals can be analyzed using time and frequency domain methods
- In time domain, signals are represented as a function of time, and analyzed using techniques such as time-domain analysis and waveform analysis
- In frequency domain, signals are represented as a function of frequency, and analyzed using techniques such as Fourier analysis, spectral analysis, and frequency response analysis
- Understanding signal representation and analysis is crucial for communication systems design and analysis.
