### Clampers for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Clampers are electronic circuits that are used to modify the DC level of a signal. They are commonly used in electronics applications to shift the DC level of a signal to a desired value. There are two main types of clampers: positive clampers and negative clampers.

Positive clampers shift the DC level of a signal to a higher value, while negative clampers shift the DC level of a signal to a lower value. Clampers are implemented using a variety of components, including diodes, capacitors, and resistors.

In the context of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, the study of clampers is a fundamental part of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode. Clampers are an important component in many electronic circuits, including power supplies, signal processing circuits, and communication systems. Understanding the principles and applications of clampers is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in electronics engineering, as they will be involved in the design, implementation, and maintenance of electronic systems.
