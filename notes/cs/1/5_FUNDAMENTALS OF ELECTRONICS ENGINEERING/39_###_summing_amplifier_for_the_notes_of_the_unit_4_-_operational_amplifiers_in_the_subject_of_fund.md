### Summing Amplifier for the notes of the Unit 4 - Operational Amplifiers in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Summing Amplifier:
- Circuit that adds multiple input signals into a single output signal
- Uses an operational amplifier (Op-Amp)
- Input signals are connected to the Op-Amp's inverting input terminal
- Each input signal is weighted by a resistor
- Output is proportional to the sum of the weighted input signals
- Can be used for signal mixing, signal addition, and error correction.
