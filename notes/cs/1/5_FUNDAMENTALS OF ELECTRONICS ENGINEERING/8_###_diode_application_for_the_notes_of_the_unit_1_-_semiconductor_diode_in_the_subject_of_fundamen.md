### Diode Application for the notes of the Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

A diode is a type of semiconductor device that allows current to flow in only one direction. In the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, Unit 1 - Semiconductor Diode, the applications of diodes are studied in detail. Some common applications of diodes include rectification, voltage regulation, signal clipping and limiting, and protection against over-voltage and reverse-polarity conditions. Rectifiers are used to convert AC power to DC power, and are used in power supplies and battery chargers. Voltage regulation diodes are used to regulate the voltage in a circuit. Signal clipping and limiting diodes are used to clip or limit the amplitude of a signal, and are used in audio and video signal processing. Protection diodes are used to protect electronic circuits and devices against over-voltage and reverse-polarity conditions. Understanding the applications of diodes is essential for students pursuing electronics engineering degrees, as it forms the basis for many advanced topics in electronics and electrical engineering.
