### cellular communication for the notes of the Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of FUNDAMENTALS OF ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

Cellular communication is a type of wireless communication that uses a network of cell sites to provide communication services to mobile devices. It is a key component of modern communication systems, providing voice and data services to users on the move.

In Unit 6 - Fundamentals of Communication Engineering in the subject of Fundamentals of Electronics Engineering, students learn about the basic principles of cellular communication, including the structure and operation of cellular networks, the use of multiple access techniques, and the use of modulation and coding to transmit data over the air interface.

Cellular communication is an important topic in electronics engineering, as it forms the basis for the design and operation of modern communication systems. Understanding the principles of cellular communication is essential for students who plan to pursue careers in the field of electronics engineering, as they will be involved in the design, development, and implementation of communication systems.
