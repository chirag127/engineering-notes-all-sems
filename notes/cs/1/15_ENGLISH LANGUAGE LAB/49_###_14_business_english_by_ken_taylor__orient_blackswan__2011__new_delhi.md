### 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

"Business English" by Ken Taylor, published by Orient Blackswan in 2011 in New Delhi, is a book that focuses on developing English language skills for business purposes. The book covers a range of topics related to business communication, including letter writing, report writing, negotiating, and giving presentations.

The book provides practical advice and strategies for improving business English skills, including tips on how to write clear and concise emails, how to prepare for a business meeting, and how to use appropriate language and tone in different business contexts. It also includes exercises and activities to help readers practice and reinforce the skills they have learned.

The author uses real-life business scenarios and examples to help readers understand the principles of effective business communication and apply them in real-life situations. The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it accessible to readers with varying levels of knowledge and experience in business English.

In conclusion, "Business English" by Ken Taylor is a comprehensive guide to developing English language skills for business purposes. Whether you are a student, a professional, or simply someone looking to improve your business English skills, this book provides practical advice and strategies for achieving your goals.
