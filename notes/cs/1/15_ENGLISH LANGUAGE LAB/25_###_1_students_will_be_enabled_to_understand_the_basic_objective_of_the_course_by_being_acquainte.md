### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

The course on communication skills aims to equip students with the ability to effectively communicate in various situations. To achieve this objective, the course focuses on five key dimensions of communication: Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking, and Speaking.

1. Reading: Students will learn the importance of being able to comprehend written text and how to effectively read and analyze written materials. This includes understanding the main ideas and details, as well as recognizing patterns and making connections between different texts.

2. Writing: Students will learn how to effectively communicate their ideas and thoughts through writing. This includes learning how to organize their thoughts, structure their writing, and use appropriate language and tone.

3. Listening: Students will learn the importance of active listening and how to effectively listen to and understand others. This includes learning how to pay attention, ask questions, and provide feedback.

4. Thinking: Students will learn how to think critically and creatively, and how to apply these skills to solve problems and make decisions. This includes learning how to analyze information, evaluate arguments, and generate new ideas.

5. Speaking: Students will learn how to effectively communicate their ideas and thoughts through speaking. This includes learning how to organize their thoughts, structure their speech, and use appropriate language and tone.

By the end of the course, students will have a comprehensive understanding of the five key dimensions of communication and will be able to apply these skills in a variety of real-world situations.
