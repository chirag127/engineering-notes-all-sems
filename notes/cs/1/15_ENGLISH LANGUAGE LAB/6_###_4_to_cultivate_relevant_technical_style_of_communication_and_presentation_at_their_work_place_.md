### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

To cultivate a relevant technical style of communication and presentation, it is important to follow the following steps:

1. Know your audience: Before presenting or communicating technical information, it is important to know the background and level of understanding of your audience. This will help you to tailor your communication to their needs and ensure they can understand the information being presented.

2. Use clear and concise language: Technical information can be complex, so it is important to use clear and concise language. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that your audience may not understand.

3. Use visual aids: Visual aids such as diagrams, graphs, and images can help to simplify complex information and make it easier for your audience to understand.

4. Practice: Rehearse your presentation or communication before delivering it. This will help you to identify areas that need improvement and ensure you are comfortable with the material.

5. Be organized: Present your information in an organized and structured manner. This will help your audience to follow the information and understand the key points.

6. Be confident: Confidence is key when delivering technical information. If you are confident in your knowledge and understanding of the material, this will help to engage your audience and make them more receptive to the information being presented.

7. Seek feedback: After delivering your presentation or communication, seek feedback from your audience. This will help you to identify areas for improvement and ensure you are providing the most effective technical communication possible.
