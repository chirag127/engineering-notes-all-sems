#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills. Interactive and Communicative Practical with emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication, based on International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.)

To improve students' employability skills, it is important for them to practice the language through a variety of activities and exercises. These activities should focus on interactive and communicative practicals with an emphasis on oral presentation and spoken communication.

One approach is to use the International Phonetic Alphabets (IPA) as a basis for these activities. The IPA is a standardized system for transcribing the sounds of speech, and it can be used to help students improve their pronunciation and speaking skills.

Examples of activities that can be used to practice these skills include:
- Giving oral presentations on a topic of their choice
- Participating in group discussions and debates
- Role-playing exercises in which students take on different characters and interact with each other
- Improvisational activities that encourage students to think on their feet and respond to unexpected situations
- Pronunciation exercises that focus on specific sounds or words

In addition to these activities, it is also important to provide students with feedback on their performance. This feedback should be constructive and focused on areas for improvement, as well as areas where the student is excelling.

By incorporating these types of activities and exercises into their language learning, students will have the opportunity to practice and improve their employability skills in a fun and engaging way.
