### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

A Theme Presentation or Keynote Presentation based on the correct methodologies of argumentation is a presentation that is designed to effectively communicate an idea or argument to an audience. To achieve this, the presenter must utilize a variety of argumentation techniques that are based on sound reasoning and evidence.

The first step in creating a Theme Presentation or Keynote Presentation is to clearly define the argument that is being made. This involves identifying the main points that will be discussed and the evidence that will be used to support those points. The presenter must also consider the audience that will be receiving the presentation and tailor the argument to their needs and interests.

Once the argument has been defined, the presenter must then choose the appropriate methodologies of argumentation to use. This may include using logic, evidence, and examples to support the argument, as well as using persuasive language to engage the audience and build their interest in the topic.

One of the key components of a Theme Presentation or Keynote Presentation is the use of visual aids, such as slides or diagrams, to help illustrate the argument and make it more accessible to the audience. The presenter must carefully consider the design and content of these visual aids, ensuring that they are clear, concise, and visually appealing.

In addition to visual aids, the presenter must also consider the use of other multimedia elements, such as videos or audio recordings, to help illustrate the argument and engage the audience. The presenter must also be prepared to address any objections or counterarguments that may arise during the presentation, and must be able to effectively respond to these objections in a way that reinforces the argument being made.

Finally, the presenter must be prepared to deliver the presentation in a clear, confident, and engaging manner. This may involve practicing the presentation in advance, and rehearsing the delivery of the argument and visual aids.

In conclusion, a Theme Presentation or Keynote Presentation based on the correct methodologies of argumentation is a powerful tool for effectively communicating an idea or argument to an audience. By utilizing sound reasoning, evidence, and persuasive language, and by incorporating visual aids and multimedia elements, the presenter can create a presentation that is both engaging and convincing.
