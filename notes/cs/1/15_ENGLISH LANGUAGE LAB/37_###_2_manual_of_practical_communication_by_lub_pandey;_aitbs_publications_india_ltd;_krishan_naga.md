### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

"Manual of Practical Communication" by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in 2013 is a comprehensive guide to effective communication skills. The manual provides students with practical tips and strategies for improving their communication skills in a variety of settings, including the workplace, academic settings, and personal relationships.

The manual covers a wide range of topics, including verbal and nonverbal communication, active listening, effective speaking, writing skills, and conflict resolution. The author provides clear and concise explanations of each topic, along with practical exercises and examples to help students apply the concepts they have learned.

One of the strengths of the manual is its focus on practical skills. The author provides students with a range of practical tools and techniques that they can use to improve their communication skills, such as active listening exercises, effective speaking strategies, and writing tips. These tools and techniques are designed to help students develop their communication skills in a hands-on, interactive way, so that they can see the results of their efforts in real-time.

In addition to its focus on practical skills, the manual also provides students with a comprehensive overview of the key theories and concepts that underlie effective communication. This includes an overview of the different types of communication, the role of nonverbal cues, and the importance of context in communication.

Overall, "Manual of Practical Communication" by L.U.B. Pandey is an excellent resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills. Whether you are a student, a professional, or simply someone who wants to be more effective in your personal and professional relationships, this manual provides you with the tools and strategies you need to succeed.
