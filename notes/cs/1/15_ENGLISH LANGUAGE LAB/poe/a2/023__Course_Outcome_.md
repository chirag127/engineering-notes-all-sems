1. Learn the fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence like machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing etc.
2. Understand the application of AI in various domains such as healthcare, transportation, education, finance, agriculture etc.
3. Gain hands-on experience in solving real-world problems using AI techniques.
4. Learn to implement AI algorithms and models using Python and its libraries like NumPy, SciPy, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras etc.
5. Understand the ethical and social implications of AI and its impact on society.
6. Develop skills to work on projects, papers and participate in hackathons/competitions related to AI.
