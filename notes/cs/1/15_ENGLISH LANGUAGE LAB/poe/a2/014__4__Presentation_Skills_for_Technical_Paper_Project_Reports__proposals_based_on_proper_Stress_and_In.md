### 4. Presentation Skills for Technical Paper/Project Reports/ proposals based on proper Stress and Intonation Mechanics

1. Prepare:
- Understand the topic/subject thoroughly. Have a clear understanding of the key points to be conveyed.
- Structure the content logically with an introduction, body and conclusion. Arrange the points in a systematic flow.
- Practice the presentation multiple times in front of a mirror or to friends/family to get comfortable with the material and timing.

2. Posture and Body Language:
- Stand up straight with shoulders back and head held high.
- Make eye contact with the audience. Engage with them through hand gestures to emphasize important points.
- Smile and relax your body to appear confident and composed.

3. Voice and Speech:
- Speak clearly by pronouncing words correctly. Vary your tone and pitch to keep the audience engaged.
- Place emphasis on key terms and phrases through variations in speech - pause, speed, volume, pitch, tone. This helps convey the importance and meaning effectively.
- The rate of speech should not be too fast or too slow. A steady, confident pace is ideal for comprehension.

4. Slides and Visuals:
- Use slides/presentation visuals to enhance the talk, not read off them.
- Minimal text, visuals and graphs are more impactful. They accentuate the speech and make the presentation more interesting and memorable.
- Ensure the font size is large enough to be read from the back of the room. The theme and colors are professional and minimalist.

The above points should help in delivering an effective presentation with the right stress and intonation mechanics. Practice and passion for the topic are key to a successful presentation.
