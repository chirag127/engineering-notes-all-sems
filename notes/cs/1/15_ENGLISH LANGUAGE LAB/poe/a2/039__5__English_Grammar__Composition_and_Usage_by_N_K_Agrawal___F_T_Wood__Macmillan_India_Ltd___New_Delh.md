### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

1. This book covers all the aspects of English grammar in a systematic and comprehensive manner. It explains the basic concepts of grammar starting from parts of speech to complex sentence structures.

2. The book provides ample examples and exercises to help students understand the concepts and apply the learning. It discusses the rules of correct word order, suitable tense forms and other aspects of grammar to enable students to use English accurately and appropriately.

3. The book also covers essential guidance on effective writing and composition skills. It explains how to organize thoughts, how to develop paragraphs and essays, how to use appropriate vocabulary and present ideas coherently. This helps students to communicate their ideas clearly and compellingly in both speech and writing.

4. This is a comprehensive resource for students preparing for exams such as CAT, MAT, Bank PO, SSC CGLE, etc. and for those studying English grammar, composition and usage in general. The systematic and structured layout of topics makes it easy to understand and follow.

5. The key features of this book are: comprehensive coverage of grammar topics, clear explanations of concepts, sufficient examples and exercises, guidance on effective writing, and question-answer sections for self-evaluation. It is a handy reference guide for learning and practising English.
