### 3. Students will apply it at their work place for writing purposes such as Presentation/official drafting/administrative communication and use it for document/project/report/research paper writing.

- Students will learn formal writing skills that can be applied at workplace for various official purposes:
- Presentations: Students will learn to write in a formal tone with structured content and logical flow of ideas which can be used to create persuasive and impactful presentations at work.
- Official drafting: The skills of formal writing will enable students to draft emails, memos, letters, reports etc. in a professional manner with proper formatting and language for administrative communication at workplace.
- Document writing: Students will be able to apply formal writing techniques to create reports, documents, research papers etc. with clarity and conciseness which is essential for professional document writing.
- Project reporting: The skills will make students adept at writing project updates and final reports with standard structure, objective language, and professional flair.
- Research paper writing: Students will learn to logically build arguments or analysis and write in a formal manner with consistent flow which can be employed to author research papers or assignments at workplace.

Overall, the lessons in formal writing will equip students with the skills required to apply in various writing tasks at their workplace with professional competence. The blend of structure, language, and style will enable students to make a positive impact through their writing.
