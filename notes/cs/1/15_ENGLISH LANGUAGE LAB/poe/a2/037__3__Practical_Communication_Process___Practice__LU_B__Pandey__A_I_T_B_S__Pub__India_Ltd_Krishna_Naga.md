### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

1. Communication is a two-way process: It involves both information transmission as well as receipt. For communication to be effective, both the sender and the receiver should participate actively.

2. Clear understanding of the message: For effective communication, the sender must present the message clearly and the receiver must understand it in the same sense and spirit. This requires a common frame of reference between the sender and the receiver regarding words, ideas and thought process.

3. Choice of appropriate medium: The choice of the medium of communication depends upon the nature of the message, urgency, distance and cost considerations. Face to face communication is the richest medium but oral and written forms are also used depending upon the situation.

4. Feedback: Feedback is an important component of the communication process. It helps the sender know whether the receiver has understood the message properly or not. The sender may then take corrective action if required. Proper feedback leads to a continuous loop of communication.

5. Barriers to effective communication: There are several barriers to effective communication such as linguistic barriers, perceptual barriers, emotional barriers, physical barriers, semantic barriers, cultural barriers, gender barriers, etc. These barriers should be minimized for improving communication effectiveness.

6. Improving communication effectiveness: Some tips to improve communication effectiveness are:

- Use simple and clear language
- Listen actively and encourage two-way communication
- Provide proper feedback
- Be empathetic and respectful towards the receiver
- Choose appropriate communication medium and channel
- Remove perceptual and cultural differences
- observing non-verbal cues and body language
