### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

1. Clearly define the theme or topic of presentation. For example, "Impact of Technology on Education".
2. Do thorough research on the theme from various sources. Gather relevant facts, statistics, examples, quotes from experts, etc. to support your key points.
3. Structure the presentation logically with an introduction, body, and conclusion.
4. In the introduction, present the main theme and give an overview of key points to be discussed. This outlines the scope and direction of the presentation for the audience.
5. In the body, discuss 3-4 key points in detail with evidence and examples. Use a persuasive approach with a balanced analysis of pros and cons. Sequence the key points in a logical flow.
6. In the conclusion, summarize the main points and overall theme. End with a strong summary statement or call-to-action if appropriate.
7. Use simple and clear language. Varied sentence structure and active voice make the presentation more engaging.
8. Include visuals like images, charts, graphs, videos, etc. to enhance the presentation and cater to visual learners in the audience.
9. Practice the presentation several times to boost confidence and speak clearly, confidently, and with the right pauses and emphasis.
10. Leave time for questions and answers. This is a great way to further engage the audience and clarify any points.

The above points should help in creating a compelling Theme Presentation using the correct methodologies of argumentation and logical flow of key ideas with supportive evidence. A formal tone, structured content, and impactful visuals/delivery can make the presentation effective.
