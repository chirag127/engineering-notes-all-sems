### • Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press.

1. Cambridge English Empower is an English language learning course developed by Cambridge University Press.
2. It focuses on developing practical language skills that are critical for success in life, study and work.
3. The course teaches language that is relevant to learners' lives and interests and provides opportunities to apply new language through authentic tasks and projects.
4. It promotes learner autonomy and confidence through step-by-step learning, constant feedback and a supportive learning environment.
5. The course is aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and supports learners at B1 and B2 levels.
6. It includes interactive components, videos and digital resources to engage students and facilitate effective learning.
7. Cambridge English Empower prepares learners with the practical language skills and confidence to progress towards Cambridge English: First (FCE), Cambridge English: Advanced (CAE) and Cambridge English: Proficiency (CPE).
