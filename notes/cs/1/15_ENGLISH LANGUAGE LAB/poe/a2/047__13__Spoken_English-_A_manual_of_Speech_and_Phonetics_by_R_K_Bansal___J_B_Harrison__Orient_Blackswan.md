### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- The book provides a comprehensive study of the phonetic system of English. It explains the correct pronunciation of consonant and vowel sounds.
- It covers topics like the organs of speech, classification of vowels and consonants, syllable formation, accent, intonation, etc.
- The articulation and pronunciation of individual sounds are explained with the help of diagrams of vocal organs.
- Transcription exercises are given to enable the learner to apply the principles of English phonetics in practice.
- It contains passages in phonetic transcription to enable the learner to acquire skill in the practical application of the principles of English pronunciation.
- The book would be useful for learners of English as well as teachers, speech therapists and actors.
- It would develop phonetic awareness and skill required to speak English with clarity and confidence.
