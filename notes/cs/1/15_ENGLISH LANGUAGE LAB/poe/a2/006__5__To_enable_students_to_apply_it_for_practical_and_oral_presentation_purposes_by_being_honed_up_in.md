### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

1. Presentation skills: Students should be trained in effective presentation skills such as:
- Making eye contact with the audience
- Using hand gestures appropriately to emphasize important points
- Speaking clearly and with confidence
- Using slides effectively with minimal text and relevant graphics to enhance the talk
- Telling a story or narrative to engage the audience
- Preparing and practicing the presentation multiple times before the actual talk

2. Voice dynamics: Students should be trained to modulate their voice effectively while presenting or speaking including:
- Varying the pitch and tone of their voice to avoid a monotone
- Using pauses for effect to give the audience time to absorb key points
- Speaking with energy and enthusiasm to keep the audience engaged
- Projecting their voice and speaking at an appropriate pace and volume
- Using techniques like stress and rhythm to emphasize critical parts of the talk

The purpose of honing presentation skills and voice dynamics is to enable students to give impactful and engaging presentations and talks. With deliberate practice of the skills mentioned above, students can become accomplished public speakers.
