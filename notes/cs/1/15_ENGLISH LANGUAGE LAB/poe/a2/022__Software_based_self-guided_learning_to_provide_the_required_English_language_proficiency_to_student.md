#### Software based self-guided learning to provide the required English language proficiency to students from an employability and career readiness standpoint. The software should align to Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and deliver a CEFR level – B2 upon completion.

- The software will contain learning modules focused on development of reading, writing, listening and speaking skills required for employability and career readiness.
- The reading modules will focus on vocabulary development and comprehension of informational and functional texts. The students will be assessed on their ability to scan, locate and retrieve specific information from a text.
- The writing modules will focus on writing for specific purposes like descriptions, discussions, resume and cover letter writing. The students will be assessed on their ability to coherently structure information and arguments and effectively convey them to the target audience.
- The listening modules will focus on understanding a range of accents and informal speech. The students will be assessed on their ability to follow extended speech and lectures, understand the main ideas and specific details, and infer the relationship between speakers.
- The speaking modules will focus on conducting phone calls, leaving messages, participating in meetings and interviews. The students will be assessed on their pronunciation, fluency, grammatical accuracy, vocabulary, and ability to express and exchange opinions and negotiate.
- The software will adapt to individual learner needs by providing customized feedback and focused practice. Learners can work through modules at their own pace and the software will track learner progress to ensure completion of all CEFR level – B2 requirements.
