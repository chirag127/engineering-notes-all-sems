### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

1. Startup presentations: Students present their business ideas or startups in a limited time. They pitch to investors or judges to get funding or win prizes. It helps in developing skills like communicating ideas, marketing strategies, handling Q&A etc.

2. Video portfolio: Students create video demonstrations of their skills and talents like coding projects, artworks, designs, photography, videography etc. and present them. It shows their creative skills and helps in creating a digital presence.

3. Extempore: Students are given a topic and they have to speak on it for 1-2 minutes without any preparation. It helps in improving spontaneity, presence of mind, logical thinking, and communication skills.

4. Role play: Students are given roles to play in a particular situation and they have to act it out. It helps in understanding different perspectives, dealing with conflict, and making practical decisions.

5. Just a Minute (JAM): Students speak on a given topic for one minute without repetition, hesitation or deviation. It boosts confidence, improves vocabulary and fluency, and teaches discipline in speech.

These activities help students learn skills that are essential for their professional development. They gain experience and exposure, build confidence, and have fun while learning.
