### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

- Communication is a two-way process involving a sender and a receiver. Effective communication requires clarity, conciseness and confidence in communicating thoughts and ideas.
- The key steps to develop communication skills are:
- Listening: Pay attention to the speaker, understand their perspective and avoid interrupting them. Ask questions to clarify doubts.
- Reading: Read extensively to expand vocabulary and grasp concepts. Read actively by summarizing and analyzing content.
- Writing: Write clearly and concisely while structuring content logically. Use simple and declarative language. Proofread to correct errors.
- Speaking: Prepare before speaking. Speak clearly, vary tone and pace. Share key messages and include anecdotes/examples to engage the audience.
- Observation: Observe how others communicate effectively. Analyze what works well and what does not. Incorporate useful techniques and styles into your own communication.
- Practice: Regularly practice your communication skills in both formal and informal settings. Feedback from others can help identify areas for improvement.
- Communication technologies: Choose appropriate communication channels based on audience and purpose. Effectively use various technologies to connect.
