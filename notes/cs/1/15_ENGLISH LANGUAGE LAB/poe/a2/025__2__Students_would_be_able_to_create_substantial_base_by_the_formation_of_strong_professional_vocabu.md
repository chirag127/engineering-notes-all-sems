### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

1. Students would be able to comprehend and grasp the meaning of new and unfamiliar words through determining the meaning from context, analyzing word formation and utilizing the knowledge of roots, prefixes and suffixes.
2. Students would be able to expand their vocabulary by discovering the connotative power of new words through exposure to resourceful reading materials, engaging in productive discussions and delivering balanced presentations.
3. Students would be able to apply the newly-gained vocabulary in varied contexts through practicing reading and listening with greater understanding, writing coherently and fluently as well as participating in conversations distinctly and confidently.
4. Students would be able to strengthen their vocabulary in particular academic disciplines or subjects by concentrating on subject-specific terms, phrases and concepts to communicate their thoughts penetratingly.

The content aims to highlight how students can build a strong professional vocabulary through various methods and apply the vocabulary in multiple ways across platforms to excel in comprehension, reading, writing and speaking. The points lay emphasis on determining meaning of new words, expanding vocabulary, applying vocabulary in varied contexts and strengthening subject-specific vocabulary.
