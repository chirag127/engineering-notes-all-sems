### 5. Students will apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics. They will apply techniques for developing interpersonal communication skills and positive attitude leading to their professional competence.

The ability to present oneself effectively is a crucial skill in today's professional world. It is not just about delivering a message; it is also about how the message is presented. Students need to hone their presentation skills, voice-dynamics, and interpersonal communication skills to become competent professionals. Here are some techniques that students can apply to improve their presentation skills and develop a positive attitude.

1. Develop a clear and concise message: Before starting any presentation, it is essential to have a clear message in mind. Students need to identify the primary objective of the presentation and develop a concise message that can be easily understood by the audience. The message should be structured logically, and the supporting points should be relevant to the topic.

2. Practice, practice, practice: The more students practice their presentation, the more confident they will become. They should practice in front of a mirror or a friend and try to get feedback on their presentation skills. They should also record themselves and watch the recording to identify areas for improvement.

3. Use body language effectively: Non-verbal communication can have a significant impact on how the message is received. Students should use body language effectively to convey confidence, enthusiasm, and authority. They should maintain eye contact, use gestures to emphasize key points, and stand up straight to project confidence.

4. Use voice-dynamics to create impact: Voice-dynamics is an essential aspect of effective communication. Students should vary their tone, pitch, and volume to create impact and emphasize key points. They should also use pauses effectively to give the audience time to reflect on the message.

5. Develop interpersonal communication skills: Effective communication is not just about delivering a message; it is also about how the message is received. Students should develop interpersonal communication skills to build rapport with their audience. They should listen actively, ask questions, and be responsive to feedback.

6. Develop a positive attitude: Attitude is everything when it comes to effective communication. Students should approach their presentations with a positive attitude and believe in their ability to deliver the message effectively. They should be confident, enthusiastic, and engaging to capture the audience's attention and create a lasting impression.

In conclusion, developing presentation skills, voice-dynamics, and interpersonal communication skills are essential for students to become competent professionals. By applying the techniques mentioned above, students can improve their presentation skills, develop a positive attitude, and become effective communicators.
