## Course Outcome:

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand the fundamental concepts and principles of the subject matter
- Analyze and evaluate complex problems related to the course material
- Apply critical thinking and problem-solving skills to develop effective solutions
- Communicate their ideas and findings clearly and effectively in both written and oral formats
- Demonstrate a mastery of relevant technological tools and software applications
- Work collaboratively with others to achieve common goals
- Develop a strong work ethic and a commitment to lifelong learning
- Evaluate and apply ethical principles in the context of the subject matter
- Recognize the cultural and societal implications of the subject matter
- Engage in independent research and inquiry to further their understanding of the subject matter.
