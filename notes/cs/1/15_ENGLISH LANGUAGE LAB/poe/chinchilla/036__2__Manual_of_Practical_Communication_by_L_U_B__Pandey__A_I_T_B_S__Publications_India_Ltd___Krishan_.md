### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

The Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey is a comprehensive guide for individuals looking to improve their communication skills. This guide covers various aspects of communication, from verbal and nonverbal communication to effective listening and public speaking. Here are some key points from the manual:

1. Verbal Communication: 
- This refers to the use of words to convey a message.
- Effective verbal communication involves clear and concise language, proper tone, and appropriate use of language.
- It is important to consider the audience and tailor the language accordingly.
- Avoid using slang, jargon, or offensive language.

2. Nonverbal Communication: 
- This refers to the use of body language, facial expressions, and gestures to convey a message.
- Nonverbal communication can sometimes speak louder than words.
- It is important to be aware of your own nonverbal cues and to interpret those of others correctly.

3. Listening Skills: 
- Effective communication involves not just speaking but also listening.
- Active listening involves paying attention to the speaker, asking questions, and providing feedback.
- It is important to avoid distractions and show genuine interest in what the speaker is saying.

4. Public Speaking: 
- Public speaking involves delivering a message to a large audience.
- It is important to prepare well, organize the message, and use appropriate language.
- Effective public speaking involves engaging the audience, using appropriate tone and body language, and maintaining eye contact.

5. Written Communication: 
- Written communication involves conveying a message through written words.
- It is important to use clear and concise language, organize the message, and proofread the document for errors.
- Consider the audience and tailor the language accordingly.

6. Interpersonal Communication: 
- Interpersonal communication involves communicating with others on a one-on-one basis.
- It is important to use appropriate language, be an active listener, and show empathy.
- Interpersonal communication skills are important in building relationships and resolving conflicts.

The Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey is a valuable resource for individuals looking to improve their communication skills. By following the guidelines in this manual, individuals can become effective communicators in various settings, including personal, professional, and academic contexts.
