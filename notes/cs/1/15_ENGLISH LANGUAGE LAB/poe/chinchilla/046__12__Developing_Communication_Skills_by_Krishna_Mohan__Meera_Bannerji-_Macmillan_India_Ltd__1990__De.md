### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

Developing effective communication skills is essential in both personal and professional settings. Here are some key points from the book "Developing Communication Skills" by Krishna Mohan and Meera Bannerji that will help you improve your communication skills:

1. Understanding the importance of communication: Communication is the foundation of all human interactions. It is essential for building relationships, expressing thoughts and ideas, and conveying information.

2. Developing good listening skills: Listening is a crucial aspect of communication. To be an effective communicator, you must be able to listen actively and attentively. This involves paying attention to the speaker, asking questions, and clarifying information.

3. Improving your speaking skills: Speaking clearly and effectively is important for conveying your ideas and thoughts. To improve your speaking skills, practice speaking in front of a mirror, record yourself speaking and listen back, and seek feedback from others.

4. Cultivating empathy: Empathy is the ability to understand and share the feelings of others. It is a key component of effective communication, as it helps to build trust and rapport.

5. Using appropriate body language: Nonverbal cues such as facial expressions, gestures, and posture can convey a lot of information. It is important to be aware of your body language when communicating and to use it effectively to reinforce your message.

6. Developing writing skills: Writing is an important aspect of communication in both personal and professional settings. To improve your writing skills, practice writing regularly, seek feedback from others, and study the writing styles of others.

7. Understanding cultural differences: Cultural differences can impact communication in significant ways. It is important to be aware of these differences and to adapt your communication style accordingly.

By following these key points, you can develop effective communication skills that will help you in all areas of your life.
