### 4. Students will be made to evaluate the correct and error-free writing by being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivate relevant technical style of communication & presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

In order to excel in the workplace and academic settings, it is crucial to possess strong writing skills. The ability to communicate effectively through written communication can greatly impact one's success in various fields. Therefore, students will be made to evaluate the correct and error-free writing by being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivate relevant technical style of communication & presentation at their work place and also for academic uses. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

1. English Grammar: 
   - Students should have a strong foundation in English grammar, including proper use of nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, and conjunctions.
   - It is important to understand the correct use of tenses, subject-verb agreement, and sentence structure.
   - Students should also be familiar with common grammar errors and how to avoid them.

2. Technical Style of Communication: 
   - Depending on the field of study or work, students should be able to communicate using technical vocabulary and jargon.
   - This requires an understanding of the specific terminology used in the field, as well as the ability to convey complex ideas in a clear and concise manner.
   - Students should also be able to use appropriate formatting, such as headings, bullet points, and tables, to effectively present information.

3. Presentation Skills:
   - In addition to strong writing skills, students should also cultivate relevant technical style of communication & presentation skills.
   - This includes the ability to create effective presentations using visual aids, such as PowerPoint, and to deliver presentations confidently and professionally.
   - Students should also be able to adapt their presentation style to different audiences and situations.

4. Error-free Writing: 
   - Students should take the time to proofread and edit their written work to ensure that it is error-free.
   - This includes checking for grammar and spelling errors, as well as ensuring that the formatting is consistent and appropriate.
   - It is also important to ensure that the content is clear, concise, and well-organized.

5. Practice:
   - The best way to improve writing and presentation skills is through practice.
   - Students should take advantage of opportunities to write and present in various settings, such as class assignments, group projects, and internships.
   - Seeking feedback from peers, professors, and mentors can also help identify areas for improvement and provide valuable insights.

In conclusion, strong writing and presentation skills are essential for success in both academic and professional settings. By being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivating relevant technical style of communication & presentation skills, students can effectively communicate their ideas and achieve their goals.
