### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

English Grammar:

1. Parts of speech: Nouns, Pronouns, Adjectives, Verbs, Adverbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions and Interjections.
2. Tenses: Present, Past and Future tenses; Simple, Continuous, Perfect and Perfect Continuous tenses.
3. Active and Passive voice: Formation of passive voice, conversion of active voice into passive voice and vice versa.
4. Direct and Indirect speech: Reporting verbs, changes in tense, pronouns and adverbs in indirect speech.
5. Articles: Definite and indefinite articles.
6. Degrees of comparison: Positive, Comparative and Superlative degrees of adjectives and adverbs.
7. Sentence structure: Subject, Predicate, Object, Complement, Phrase and Clause.
8. Conditional sentences: Types of conditional sentences and their usage.
9. Question tags: Formation and usage of question tags.
10. Gerunds: Formation and usage of gerunds.

Composition:

1. Paragraph writing: Introduction, Body and Conclusion; Unity, Coherence and Emphasis.
2. Letter writing: Types of letters, parts of a letter, format and style.
3. Precis writing: Writing a precis, summarizing a passage.
4. Comprehension: Reading comprehension, answering questions based on a passage.

Usage:

1. Idioms and Phrases: Commonly used idioms and phrases and their meanings.
2. Synonyms and Antonyms: Words with similar and opposite meanings.
3. Homonyms and Homophones: Words with the same spelling or pronunciation but different meanings.
4. Spelling: Commonly misspelled words and their correct spellings.
5. Punctuations: Proper usage of punctuations like full stop, comma, semi-colon, colon, apostrophe, etc.
6. Vocabulary: Building vocabulary through reading, learning new words and their meanings.

It is essential to have a good grasp of English grammar, composition and usage to communicate effectively in English. Studying the book "English Grammar, Composition and Usage" by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi can help students improve their language skills and prepare for exams.
