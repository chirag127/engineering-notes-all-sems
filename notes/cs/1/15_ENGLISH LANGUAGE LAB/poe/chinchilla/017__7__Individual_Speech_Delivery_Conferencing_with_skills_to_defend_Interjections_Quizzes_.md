### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

In individual speech delivery and conferencing, it is important to have the skills to defend interjections and quizzes. The following points provide guidance on how to do so:

1. Be prepared: Before delivering a speech or participating in a conference, make sure you have thoroughly prepared your material. This includes researching and organizing your topic, as well as anticipating potential interjections or quiz questions.

2. Stay focused: During your speech or conference, stay focused on your topic and maintain your composure. This will help you to defend against interjections and quizzes that may be thrown your way.

3. Listen carefully: When someone interjects or asks a quiz question, listen carefully to what they are saying. Try to understand their perspective and respond thoughtfully.

4. Stay calm: If you are faced with a challenging interjection or quiz question, stay calm and composed. Take a moment to gather your thoughts and respond in a clear and concise manner.

5. Use evidence: When defending against interjections or quiz questions, use evidence to support your argument. This will help to strengthen your position and convince others of your point of view.

6. Be respectful: When responding to interjections or quiz questions, be respectful and courteous. Avoid becoming defensive or confrontational, as this will only escalate the situation.

7. Practice: The more you practice individual speech delivery and conferencing, the better you will become at defending against interjections and quizzes. Take advantage of opportunities to practice and refine your skills.

By following these guidelines, you can develop the skills necessary to defend against interjections and quizzes during individual speech delivery and conferencing. With practice and preparation, you can become a confident and effective communicator.
