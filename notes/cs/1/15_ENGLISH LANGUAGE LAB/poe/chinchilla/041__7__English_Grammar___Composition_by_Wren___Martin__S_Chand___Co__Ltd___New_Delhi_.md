### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi is a comprehensive guide to learning the English language. It is widely used by students, teachers, and professionals to improve their English language skills. Here are some key points to keep in mind about this book:

- The book is divided into two parts: grammar and composition. The grammar section covers all aspects of English grammar, including parts of speech, tenses, syntax, and punctuation. The composition section covers writing skills, including essay writing, letter writing, and precis writing.

- The book is suitable for all levels of learners, from beginners to advanced learners. The explanations are clear and easy to understand, and the exercises are designed to reinforce the concepts learned.

- The book includes a large number of exercises and examples to help learners practice and master the concepts. The exercises are graded in difficulty, so learners can start with the easier ones and progress to the more challenging ones.

- The book also includes a section on vocabulary, which is essential for developing strong language skills. The vocabulary section includes lists of common words, synonyms, antonyms, and idioms.

- The book has been updated regularly to reflect changes in the English language. The latest edition includes new examples, exercises, and explanations to keep up with the evolving language.

- The book is widely recognized as one of the best resources for learning English grammar and composition. It is used by schools, colleges, and universities around the world.

In conclusion, English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi is an excellent resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills. It is comprehensive, easy to understand, and packed with exercises and examples to help learners practice and master the concepts. Whether you are a beginner or an advanced learner, this book is a must-have for anyone serious about improving their English language skills.
