### 3. Students will apply it at their work place for writing purposes such as Presentation/official drafting/administrative communication and use it for document/project/report/research paper writing.

Here are some key points for students to keep in mind when applying writing skills in the workplace:

- Always begin with a clear understanding of the purpose and audience of the writing task. This will help you tailor your writing style and tone to the specific situation and ensure that your message is effectively communicated.
- Use formal language and appropriate business terminology, but avoid overly technical or jargon-heavy language that may be difficult for others to understand.
- Follow the standard conventions for formatting and structuring different types of documents, such as presentations, reports, and research papers. This will help ensure that your work looks professional and is easy to read and navigate.
- Pay close attention to grammar, punctuation, and spelling to ensure that your writing is clear, concise, and error-free. This will help you avoid misunderstandings and demonstrate your attention to detail and professionalism.
- Use visual aids and other supporting materials, such as graphs, charts, and tables, to help convey complex information and data in an easy-to-understand format.
- When communicating with colleagues or superiors, be sure to keep your tone respectful and professional, even in difficult or contentious situations. This will help you establish strong working relationships and maintain a positive workplace culture.
