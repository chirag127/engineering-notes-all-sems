### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

Here are some key points to keep in mind when preparing for and delivering startup presentations, video portfolios, extempore speeches, role play scenarios, and Just a Minute (JAM) sessions:

1. Research: Before preparing for any of these activities, it is important to research the topic thoroughly. This includes understanding the target audience, the purpose of the presentation or speech, and the key talking points that need to be covered.

2. Structure: Once you have a clear understanding of the topic, it is important to structure your presentation, speech, or role play scenario in a logical and organized manner. This includes creating an introduction, body, and conclusion, and ensuring that each section flows smoothly into the next.

3. Practice: Practice is key to delivering a successful presentation or speech. This includes rehearsing your content, timing yourself, and practicing your delivery. It is also important to practice answering potential questions that may arise during your presentation or speech.

4. Visual aids: Visual aids, such as PowerPoint slides or video clips, can be a powerful tool to enhance your presentation or speech. However, it is important to use them sparingly and ensure that they are relevant to the topic and not distracting from your message.

5. Body language: Your body language can also play a key role in the success of your presentation, speech, or role play scenario. This includes maintaining eye contact, using appropriate gestures, and projecting confidence and enthusiasm.

6. Feedback: Soliciting feedback from peers, mentors, or coaches can be a valuable tool to improve your presentation or speech. This includes asking for constructive criticism, identifying areas for improvement, and incorporating this feedback into your next practice session.

By keeping these key points in mind, you can deliver successful startup presentations, video portfolios, extempore speeches, role play scenarios, and Just a Minute (JAM) sessions. Good luck!
