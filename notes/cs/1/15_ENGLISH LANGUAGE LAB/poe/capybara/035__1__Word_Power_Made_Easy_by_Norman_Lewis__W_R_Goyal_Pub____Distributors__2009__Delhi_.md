### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

Word Power Made Easy is an essential book for anyone looking to improve their vocabulary. This book is written by Norman Lewis and published by W.R. Goyal Pub. & Distributors in 2009. Here are some key points to keep in mind while studying this book:

- The book is divided into three sections: Building Your Vocabulary, Testing Your Vocabulary, and Using Your Vocabulary.
- The first section, Building Your Vocabulary, is further divided into chapters that cover various topics such as Synonyms, Antonyms, Analogies, and Pronunciation.
- The second section, Testing Your Vocabulary, includes exercises to help you test your knowledge and understanding of the words you have learned in the first section.
- The third section, Using Your Vocabulary, provides examples of how to use the words you have learned in your everyday life.
- The book is structured in a way that makes it easy to follow and learn from.
- The author, Norman Lewis, uses a simple and straightforward writing style that is easy to understand.
- The book is filled with practical tips and techniques that can help you improve your vocabulary quickly and effectively.
- The exercises in the book are designed to be challenging but not overwhelming, so you can progress at your own pace.
- The book includes a comprehensive list of words and their meanings, as well as synonyms and antonyms, making it a valuable reference tool.
- The book is suitable for anyone looking to improve their vocabulary, regardless of their age or educational background.
- Overall, Word Power Made Easy is an excellent resource for anyone looking to expand their vocabulary and improve their communication skills.
