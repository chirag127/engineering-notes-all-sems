### 5. Students will apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics. They will apply techniques for developing interpersonal communication skills and positive attitude leading to their professional competence.

- Practical and oral presentations are essential skills for students to develop as they prepare to enter the workforce. Through honing their presentation skills and voice dynamics, students will be better equipped to effectively communicate their ideas and persuade others in both personal and professional settings.

- To develop these skills, students should practice delivering presentations in front of peers and receiving feedback on their delivery style. This will help them identify areas for improvement and refine their approach to public speaking.

- In addition to practicing their delivery, students should also focus on developing their interpersonal communication skills. This includes techniques such as active listening, effective questioning, and nonverbal communication. By mastering these skills, students will be better able to connect with others and build strong relationships in both their personal and professional lives.

- A positive attitude is also essential for professional competence. Students should strive to maintain a positive mindset, even in the face of challenges or setbacks. This will help them stay motivated and focused on achieving their goals.

- Finally, students should focus on developing their voice dynamics. This includes techniques such as varying their pitch and tone, emphasizing key points, and using appropriate pauses. By mastering these techniques, students can make their presentations more engaging and impactful.

- Overall, by applying these techniques and developing their presentation skills, interpersonal communication skills, and positive attitude, students will be better equipped to succeed in their future careers and personal lives.
