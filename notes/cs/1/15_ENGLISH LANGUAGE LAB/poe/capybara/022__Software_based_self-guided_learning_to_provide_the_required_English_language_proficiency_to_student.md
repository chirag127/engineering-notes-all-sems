#### Software based self-guided learning to provide the required English language proficiency to students from an employability and career readiness standpoint. The software should align to Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and deliver a CEFR level – B2 upon completion.

The following points will guide you through the software-based self-guided learning to provide the required English language proficiency to students from an employability and career readiness standpoint:

- The software should have a user-friendly interface that allows students to easily navigate through the program.
- The program should include various modules that cover all aspects of the English language, including grammar, vocabulary, writing, reading, and listening.
- Each module should be designed to align with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and deliver a CEFR level – B2 upon completion.
- The program should include interactive exercises and quizzes that allow students to test their knowledge and receive immediate feedback.
- The software should use artificial intelligence (AI) to adapt to the individual learning needs of each student and provide personalized feedback and recommendations.
- The software should be available on multiple devices, including desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones, to allow for flexible learning.
- The program should include a progress tracker that allows students to monitor their performance and track their progress over time.
- The software should provide students with the opportunity to practice their English language skills through real-world scenarios and case studies that align with employability and career readiness.
- The program should offer additional resources, such as English language podcasts, articles, and videos, to supplement the learning experience.
- The software should have a support team available to assist students with any technical issues or questions they may have throughout their learning journey.
