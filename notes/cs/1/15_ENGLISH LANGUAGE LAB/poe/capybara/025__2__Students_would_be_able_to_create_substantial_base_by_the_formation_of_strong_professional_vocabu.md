### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

To be able to have a strong professional vocabulary is important in various fields. Here are some points that will help students create a substantial base for their vocabulary:

- Reading: One of the best ways to improve vocabulary is by reading. Students should read a variety of materials such as books, articles, and newspapers to increase their exposure to different words and their usage in different contexts. 
- Word Lists: Creating and studying word lists is another way to improve vocabulary. Students can focus on words that are specific to their field of study or career goals. They can also learn words that are commonly used in professional settings.
- Contextual Learning: Learning words in context is essential for understanding their meaning and usage. Students can learn new words by reading them in sentences or paragraphs and understanding how they are used in a particular context.
- Practice: Practice is important for retaining new vocabulary. Students can practice by using new words in their writing and speaking. They can also try to use synonyms and antonyms to expand their vocabulary.
- Use of Technology: There are many online tools and apps available to help students improve their vocabulary. These tools offer various activities and games that can make learning new words fun and engaging.

By following these points, students can create a strong professional vocabulary that they can use in various platforms and modes such as comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking. Having a strong vocabulary will not only help them in their academic and professional pursuits but also in their personal lives.
