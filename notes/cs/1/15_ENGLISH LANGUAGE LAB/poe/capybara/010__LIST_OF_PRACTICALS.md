1. Determination of the boiling point of a liquid using a thermometer and a boiling tube.

8. Identification of different functional groups in organic compounds using chemical tests.
9. Separation of the components of a mixture using different techniques like filtration, evaporation, and distillation.
10. Determination of the percentage composition of a mixture using gravimetric analysis.
