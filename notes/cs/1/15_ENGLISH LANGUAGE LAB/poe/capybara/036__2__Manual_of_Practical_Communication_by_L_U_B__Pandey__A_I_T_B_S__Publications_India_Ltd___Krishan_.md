### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

The Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey is an essential resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills. The book covers a wide range of topics related to communication, including verbal and nonverbal communication, effective listening, and interpersonal communication.

Here are some key takeaways from the Manual of Practical Communication:

- Verbal communication: This refers to the use of words to convey a message. To be an effective communicator, it is important to choose your words carefully and speak clearly. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that your audience may not understand. Also, pay attention to your tone of voice, as this can greatly impact how your message is received.

- Nonverbal communication: This includes body language, facial expressions, and gestures. Nonverbal cues can convey a lot of information, so it's important to be aware of your body language when communicating. For example, maintaining eye contact and smiling can help establish rapport with your audience.

- Effective listening: Good communication is a two-way street, and listening is just as important as speaking. When listening, be fully present and give the speaker your undivided attention. Avoid interrupting or jumping to conclusions, and ask clarifying questions to ensure you understand the speaker's message.

- Interpersonal communication: This refers to communication between two or more people. Building strong relationships is key to effective interpersonal communication. This involves being empathetic, showing respect, and being open to feedback.

Overall, the Manual of Practical Communication is a valuable resource for anyone looking to sharpen their communication skills. By following the tips outlined in the book, you can become a more effective communicator and build stronger relationships with those around you.
