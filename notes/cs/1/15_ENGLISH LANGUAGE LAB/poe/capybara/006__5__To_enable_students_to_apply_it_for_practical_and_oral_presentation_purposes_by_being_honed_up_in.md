### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- Presentation skills refer to the ability to effectively communicate information to an audience. 
- A good presentation involves a clear and concise message, organized and engaging content, and effective delivery. 
- Effective delivery includes factors such as body language, tone of voice, and pacing. 
- Practicing presentation skills can help students in a variety of settings, including academic presentations, job interviews, and public speaking engagements. 

- Voice-dynamics refers to the way in which a person uses their voice to convey a message. 
- This includes factors such as volume, tone, pitch, and inflection. 
- Having strong voice-dynamics can help a presenter to capture and maintain the attention of their audience. 
- Practicing voice-dynamics can also help to reduce anxiety and increase confidence in public speaking situations. 

- To apply these skills in a practical setting, students should consider participating in presentation or public speaking opportunities, such as academic presentations, debate teams, or public speaking clubs. 
- Practicing in these settings can help students to refine their skills and gain experience. 
- Students can also seek out feedback from peers or instructors to identify areas for improvement. 

- In addition to practical experience, students can also hone their skills by studying presentations or speeches by skilled communicators. 
- Analyzing the techniques used by successful speakers can help students to identify effective strategies and incorporate them into their own presentations. 
- Resources such as TED Talks, academic lectures, and political speeches can provide valuable examples to study. 

- Overall, developing strong presentation skills and voice-dynamics can be a valuable asset in both academic and professional settings. 
- By practicing these skills and seeking out opportunities for improvement, students can become more confident and effective communicators.
