
### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi

1. Spoken English is a manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, published by Orient Blackswan in 2013 in New Delhi.
2. The manual provides an introduction to the science of phonetics, focusing on its application in the analysis and description of spoken English.
3. It covers topics such as the production of speech sounds, the structure of syllables, accent, intonation, and the use of non-standard varieties of English.
4. The manual also includes exercises and activities to help readers develop their skills in spoken English.
5. The manual is suitable for both beginners and advanced learners of English, as well as for teachers and researchers in the field of phonetics.
