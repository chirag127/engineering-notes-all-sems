
### 4. English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi

1. Grammar is the study of the structure and form of a language.
2. It includes the study of the rules of syntax, morphology, and phonology.
3. Syntax is the study of the order and arrangement of words in sentences.
4. Morphology is the study of the forms of words, such as inflections and derivations.
5. Phonology is the study of the sound system of a language.
6. English grammar is the set of rules and conventions that govern the use of the English language.
7. It covers topics such as spelling, punctuation, sentence structure, verb tenses, and parts of speech.
8. English usage is the way in which the English language is used in everyday speech and writing.
9. It includes the conventions of spoken English, such as pronunciation and intonation, as well as the conventions of written English, such as spelling and punctuation.
