
### 4. Presentation Skills for Technical Paper/Project Reports/ Proposals Based on Proper Stress and Intonation Mechanics

* Presentation skills are essential for effective communication of technical information.
* Proper stress and intonation are key elements of effective presentations.
* Stress refers to the emphasis given to certain syllables in a word or phrase.
* Intonation is the rise and fall of the voice when speaking.
* To ensure that your presentation is clear and understandable, it is important to use the correct stress and intonation.
* To practice your presentation skills, record yourself speaking and listen back to identify areas that need improvement.
* Use pauses and gestures to emphasize key points and draw attention to important information.
* Speak slowly and clearly, and practice using a variety of tones and inflections.
* Practice presenting in front of a mirror or with a friend to help build confidence.
