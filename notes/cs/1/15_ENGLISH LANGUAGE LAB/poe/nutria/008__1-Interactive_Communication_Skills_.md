
### 1-Interactive Communication Skills:

1. Active Listening: Active listening is the practice of carefully listening to another person's words and attempting to understand their meaning. It involves both verbal and non-verbal cues, such as maintaining eye contact, nodding, and providing verbal affirmations.

2. Empathy: Empathy is the ability to recognize and share the feelings of another person. It is important to be able to put yourself in someone else's shoes in order to truly understand them and their situation.

3. Clarity: Clarity is the practice of expressing yourself clearly and concisely. This involves using language that is easily understood and avoiding jargon or slang.

4. Open-Mindedness: Open-mindedness is the practice of being open to new ideas and perspectives. It is important to be able to consider different points of view and be willing to compromise in order to reach a mutually beneficial outcome.

5. Respect: Respect is the practice of treating others with kindness and consideration. This involves being mindful of another person's feelings and beliefs, even if they differ from your own.
