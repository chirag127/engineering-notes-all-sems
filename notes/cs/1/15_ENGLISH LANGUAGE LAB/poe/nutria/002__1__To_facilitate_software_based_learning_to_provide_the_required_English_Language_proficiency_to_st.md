
### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students

1. English language proficiency can be improved through software-based learning. 
2. Software-based learning allows students to practice and improve their language skills at their own pace and in a comfortable environment.
3. Software-based learning can be used to teach a variety of English language skills, including grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, reading, writing, and listening.
4. Software-based learning can provide students with immediate feedback on their progress and help them identify areas for improvement.
5. Software-based learning can also be used to supplement traditional classroom instruction, allowing students to practice and reinforce the concepts they have learned in the classroom.
6. Software-based learning can be tailored to the individual student's needs and interests, making it a more engaging and effective learning experience.
