
### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses

1. When communicating with colleagues, customers, and other stakeholders, use a professional and formal tone.
2. Avoid using slang, jargon, and overly technical language when speaking with those who may not be familiar with the subject matter.
3. When presenting information, use visuals such as graphs and charts to illustrate your points.
4. When writing documents, use proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
5. Make sure to proofread documents before sending them out.
6. When giving a presentation, practice beforehand and use appropriate body language.
7. Use multimedia when possible to make presentations more engaging.
8. Be mindful of cultural differences when communicating with people from different backgrounds.
