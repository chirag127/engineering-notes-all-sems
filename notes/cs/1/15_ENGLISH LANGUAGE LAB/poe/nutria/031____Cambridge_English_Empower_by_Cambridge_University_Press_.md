
### • Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press

* Cambridge English Empower is an advanced level course for adult and young adult learners of English, published by Cambridge University Press.
* The course focuses on developing all four language skills: reading, writing, speaking and listening.
* It includes a range of different activities to help learners develop their language skills, such as reading and listening comprehension tasks, grammar and vocabulary exercises, and speaking and writing tasks.
* It also includes a range of digital resources to help learners practice their language skills, such as interactive activities, audio recordings and videos.
* Cambridge English Empower also includes a range of exam preparation materials, such as practice tests and exam tips.
