
### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- Developing presentation skills requires practice and honing. 
- Voice dynamics should be developed to ensure a clear and effective delivery of the presentation.
- Body language should be taken into account when delivering a presentation, as it is an important factor in conveying the message.
- Preparation is key to delivering a successful presentation. Research the topic, structure the presentation, rehearse and practice.
- Visual aids can be used to help illustrate the points being made and make the presentation more engaging.
- Ensure that the presentation is concise and to the point. Avoid rambling on or going off topic.
- It is important to be aware of the audience and tailor the presentation accordingly.
- Have a clear conclusion and be prepared to answer any questions.
