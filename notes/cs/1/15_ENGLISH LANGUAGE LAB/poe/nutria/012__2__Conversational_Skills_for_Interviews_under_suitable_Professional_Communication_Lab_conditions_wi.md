
### 2. Conversational Skills for Interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics

* Body language: During an interview, it is important to maintain good body language. This includes maintaining eye contact, sitting up straight, and having an open posture.
* Tone: It is important to maintain a professional tone during an interview. This includes speaking in a clear and confident voice, and avoiding slang and colloquialisms.
* Listening: An important part of any conversation is listening. It is important to listen to the interviewer to ensure that you are answering the questions correctly and in a timely manner.
* Asking questions: It is important to ask questions during an interview. This shows the interviewer that you are engaged and interested in the position.
* Follow up: After the interview, it is important to follow up with the interviewer to thank them for their time and to reiterate your interest in the position.
