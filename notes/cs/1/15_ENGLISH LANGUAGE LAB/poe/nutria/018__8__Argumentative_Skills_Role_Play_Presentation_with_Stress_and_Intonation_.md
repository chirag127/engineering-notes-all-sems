
### 8. Argumentative Skills/Role Play Presentation with Stress and Intonation

* Argumentative skills involve the ability to make a point effectively and persuasively.
* Stress and intonation are important elements of argumentation, as they help to emphasize key points and create a sense of urgency.
* In order to make an effective argument, one must be aware of the needs and objectives of their audience, and tailor their argument accordingly.
* It is also important to be aware of the tone and language used in order to ensure that the point is made in the most effective way possible.
* Role play can be a useful tool for practicing argumentative skills, as it allows for the development of effective techniques in a safe environment.
* When role playing, it is important to pay attention to the intonation and stress used when making a point.
* This can be done by emphasizing certain words or phrases, or by speaking in a louder or softer tone.
* It is also important to be aware of body language, as this can be used to reinforce the point being made.
* Practicing argumentative skills in a role play setting can help to develop effective techniques which can be used in real-life situations.
