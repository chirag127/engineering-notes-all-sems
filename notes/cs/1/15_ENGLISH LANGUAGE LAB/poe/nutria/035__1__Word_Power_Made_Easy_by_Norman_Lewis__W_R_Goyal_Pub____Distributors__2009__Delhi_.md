
### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi

Word Power Made Easy is a comprehensive book written by Norman Lewis, published by W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors in 2009. It is designed to help readers build a strong vocabulary, improve their communication skills, and become more confident in their use of English. The book contains a variety of exercises, including word games, crosswords, and antonym and synonym tests. It also provides tips and strategies for improving one's writing and speaking skills. The book is divided into 12 chapters, each focusing on a different aspect of English language usage. The topics covered include:

* Grammar and Usage
* Parts of Speech
* Word Building
* Word Roots
* Word Families
* Idioms and Phrases
* Spelling
* Punctuation
* Vocabulary Building
* Reading Comprehension
* Writing Skills
* Test Taking Strategies

Word Power Made Easy is an invaluable resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills. It is a comprehensive guide that provides readers with the tools they need to become more confident and proficient in their use of the language.
