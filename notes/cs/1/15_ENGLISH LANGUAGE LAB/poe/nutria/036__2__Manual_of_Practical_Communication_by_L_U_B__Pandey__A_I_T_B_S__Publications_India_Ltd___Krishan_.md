
### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

1. The purpose of the manual is to provide a comprehensive guide to practical communication.
2. It covers the fundamentals of communication, such as verbal and non-verbal communication, active listening, and the use of body language.
3. It also covers the use of communication in various contexts, such as in the workplace, in relationships, and in public speaking.
4. The manual provides tips and strategies for effective communication, such as using appropriate language, being aware of cultural differences, and managing conflict.
5. It also includes case studies and exercises to help readers practice and improve their communication skills.
