
### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking

1. Reading: Reading is the ability to comprehend written words and sentences. It involves the ability to decode words, understand their meaning, and interpret the text.

2. Writing: Writing is the ability to communicate thoughts and ideas in a written form. It involves the ability to organize thoughts, use proper grammar and spelling, and create cohesive sentences and paragraphs.

3. Listening: Listening is the ability to comprehend spoken words and sentences. It involves the ability to process spoken words, understand their meaning, and interpret the speaker’s intent.

4. Thinking: Thinking is the ability to analyze and synthesize information. It involves the ability to reason logically, draw conclusions, and make decisions.

5. Speaking: Speaking is the ability to communicate thoughts and ideas in a spoken form. It involves the ability to organize thoughts, use proper grammar and pronunciation, and create cohesive sentences and conversations.
