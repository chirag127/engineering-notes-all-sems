### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

To hone up presentation skills and voice-dynamics:

1. Start by understanding your audience and what they expect from your presentation.
2. Plan and organize your presentation content in a clear and concise manner.
3. Practice your presentation several times to become comfortable with the material.
4. Pay attention to your body language, gestures, and facial expressions to make a positive impact.
5. Use visual aids, such as slides, charts, or images, to help illustrate your points.
6. Vary your tone, pace, and volume to keep the audience engaged.
7. Use storytelling techniques to make your presentation more interesting.
8. Encourage audience interaction through questions, discussions, or activities.
9. Be prepared to handle questions or objections that may arise during the presentation.
10. Evaluate your performance after each presentation and make improvements where necessary.
11. Seek feedback from others to identify areas for improvement.
12. Consider taking courses or workshops to further develop your presentation skills.
