### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

English Grammar & Composition is a comprehensive guide to the rules of English grammar and composition, written by Wren and Martin and published by S. Chand & Co. Ltd. in New Delhi. The book covers a wide range of topics, including parts of speech, sentence structure, punctuation, and writing skills.

The book is organized in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for students to understand and apply the rules of English grammar. The book includes numerous examples and exercises, allowing students to practice what they have learned and develop their writing skills.

In addition to its comprehensive coverage of English grammar, the book also includes a section on composition, providing students with guidance on how to write clear and effective essays, letters, and other types of written communication.

The book is widely used as a textbook in schools and colleges, and is considered a classic in the field of English language and composition. It is also a useful resource for anyone looking to improve their English grammar and writing skills.

Overall, English Grammar & Composition by Wren and Martin is a comprehensive and well-organized guide to the rules of English grammar and composition, providing students and others with the knowledge and skills they need to write clear and effective English.
