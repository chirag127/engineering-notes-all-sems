### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, published by Macmillan India Ltd. in 1990, is a book that focuses on developing effective communication skills for individuals. The book covers various aspects of communication, including verbal and nonverbal communication, listening skills, public speaking, and written communication.

The book provides practical advice and strategies for improving communication skills, including tips on how to prepare for a speech, how to overcome nervousness, and how to structure a persuasive argument. It also includes exercises and activities to help readers practice and reinforce the skills they have learned.

The author uses a combination of theory and practical examples to help readers understand the principles of effective communication and apply them in real-life situations. The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it accessible to readers with varying levels of knowledge and experience in communication.

In conclusion, Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan is a comprehensive guide to developing effective communication skills. Whether you are a student, a professional, or simply someone looking to improve your communication skills, this book provides practical advice and strategies for achieving your goals.
