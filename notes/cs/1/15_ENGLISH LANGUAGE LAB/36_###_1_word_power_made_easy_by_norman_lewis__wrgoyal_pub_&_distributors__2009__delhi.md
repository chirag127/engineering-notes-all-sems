### 1. Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

"Word Power Made Easy" is a book written by Norman Lewis and published by W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors in Delhi in 2009. The book is designed to help readers improve their vocabulary and increase their ability to use words effectively in writing and speaking.

Some of the key features of the book include:

1. Explanations of word meanings and origins: The book provides clear and concise definitions of words, as well as information about their etymology and usage.

2. Practice exercises: The book includes a variety of exercises and activities designed to help readers apply their new vocabulary knowledge and improve their word power.

3. Word lists: The book includes extensive lists of words, organized by theme and level of difficulty, to help readers build their vocabulary.

4. Tips and strategies: The book provides tips and strategies for improving vocabulary, such as using word roots and prefixes, and learning words in context.

The author, Norman Lewis, was a renowned vocabulary expert and language teacher, and his book is widely regarded as one of the best resources for improving vocabulary and word power. Whether you are a student, professional, or anyone else who wants to improve their ability to use words effectively, "Word Power Made Easy" is an excellent resource to have on hand.
