### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

"English Grammar, Composition and Usage" by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood is a textbook that provides comprehensive coverage of the grammar, composition, and usage of the English language. This textbook is published by Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi, and is designed to help students improve their writing and communication skills.

The textbook covers a wide range of topics, including:

1. Grammar: The textbook provides a comprehensive overview of the grammar of the English language, including parts of speech, sentence structure, and verb tenses.

2. Composition: The textbook provides guidance on writing effective sentences, paragraphs, and essays, and covers topics such as organization, coherence, and style.

3. Usage: The textbook provides guidance on the correct usage of common words and expressions in the English language, and covers topics such as word choice, punctuation, and sentence structure.

4. Practice exercises: The textbook includes a range of practice exercises to help students reinforce their understanding of the concepts covered, and to build their writing and communication skills.

Overall, "English Grammar, Composition and Usage" by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood is a comprehensive and effective resource for students who want to improve their writing and communication skills in the English language.
