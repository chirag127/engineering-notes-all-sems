### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

The book "Practical Communication Process & Practice" by L.U.B. Pandey is a comprehensive guide to communication processes and practices. It was published by A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd in 2013 and is headquartered in Krishna Nagar, Delhi.

The book covers various aspects of communication, including its definition, types, and elements. It also covers the various communication channels, such as verbal, nonverbal, and written communication. Additionally, the book covers various types of communication, such as formal, informal, and interpersonal communication.

The book also covers various communication skills, such as listening, speaking, reading, and writing. It provides practical tips and exercises to improve these skills and become a more effective communicator.

Overall, "Practical Communication Process & Practice" is a useful resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills and understand the communication process.
