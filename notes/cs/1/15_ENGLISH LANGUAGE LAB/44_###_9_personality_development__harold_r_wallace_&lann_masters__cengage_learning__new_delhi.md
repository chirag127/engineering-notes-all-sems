### 9. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace &L.Ann Masters, Cengage Learning, New Delhi

"Personality Development" is a book written by Harold R. Wallace and L.Ann Masters and published by Cengage Learning in New Delhi. The book provides an in-depth examination of the concept of personality development and offers practical guidance on how individuals can develop their personal and professional skills.

The book covers a wide range of topics related to personality development, including emotional intelligence, communication skills, leadership, and time management. It provides a comprehensive overview of the theories and models of personality development and offers practical exercises and activities to help individuals develop their own personality.

One of the key strengths of the book is its focus on practical application. The authors provide a variety of exercises, activities, and case studies that help individuals apply the concepts they have learned to their own lives. This makes the book an excellent resource for anyone looking to develop their personality and to improve their personal and professional skills.

In addition to its practical focus, the book also provides a comprehensive overview of the latest research and theories in the field of personality development. This makes it an excellent resource for students and professionals who are looking to deepen their understanding of this important topic.

Overall, "Personality Development" by Harold R. Wallace and L.Ann Masters is an excellent resource for anyone looking to develop their personality and to improve their personal and professional skills. Whether you are a student, a professional, or simply someone looking to grow and develop, this book provides the insights and guidance you need to achieve your goals.
