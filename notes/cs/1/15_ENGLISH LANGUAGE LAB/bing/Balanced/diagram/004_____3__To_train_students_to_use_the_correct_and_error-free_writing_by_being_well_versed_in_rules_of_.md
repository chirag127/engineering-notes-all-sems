### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

- Writing is a skill that requires practice, feedback, and revision. It also requires a good knowledge of the rules of English grammar, which govern how words and sentences are formed and used.
- Grammar is the system of rules that defines the structure and meaning of language. It includes the rules of syntax (how words are arranged into phrases and sentences), morphology (how words are formed and inflected), and semantics (how words and sentences convey meaning).
- Learning grammar helps students to use the correct and error-free writing, which is essential for effective communication, academic success, and professional development. It also helps students to avoid common errors, such as spelling mistakes, punctuation errors, subject-verb agreement errors, and sentence fragments.
- To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar, teachers can use various strategies, such as:
  - Teaching grammar explicitly and systematically, using clear definitions, examples, and exercises.
  - Integrating grammar instruction with writing instruction, using authentic texts and tasks that require students to apply the grammar rules they have learned.
  - Providing feedback and correction on students' writing, using error codes, comments, and suggestions for improvement.
  - Encouraging students to self-edit and peer-edit their writing, using checklists, rubrics, and tools such as grammar checkers and dictionaries.
  - Exposing students to a variety of texts and genres, using reading and listening activities that highlight the grammar features and conventions of different types of writing.
  - Motivating students to learn grammar, using games, quizzes, and activities that make grammar fun and relevant.
