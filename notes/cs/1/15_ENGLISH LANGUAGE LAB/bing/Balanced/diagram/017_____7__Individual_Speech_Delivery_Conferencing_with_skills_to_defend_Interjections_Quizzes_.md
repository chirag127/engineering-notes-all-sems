### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes.

- Individual speech delivery is the process of presenting a speech to an audience, either in person or online, using verbal and nonverbal communication skills.
- Conferencing is the process of engaging in a dialogue with the audience, either during or after the speech, to clarify, elaborate, or respond to questions or comments.
- Skills to defend interjections/quizzes are the abilities to handle interruptions, challenges, or tests from the audience or the evaluator, using logic, evidence, and rhetoric.

Some points to consider for individual speech delivery/conferencing with skills to defend interjections/quizzes are:

- Prepare and practice your speech beforehand, considering the purpose, audience, context, and format of your presentation.
- Use appropriate language, tone, and style for your speech, avoiding jargon, slang, or offensive words.
- Use vocal variety, eye contact, gestures, facial expressions, and posture to enhance your speech and convey your message effectively.
- Use visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, or images, to support your speech and make it more engaging and memorable.
- Adapt your speech to the feedback and reactions of your audience, such as their interest, attention, questions, or objections.
- Be confident, courteous, and respectful when delivering your speech and conferencing with your audience, avoiding defensive or aggressive behavior.
- Anticipate and prepare for possible interjections/quizzes from your audience or the evaluator, such as clarifications, objections, opinions, or factual questions.
- Acknowledge and address the interjections/quizzes in a polite and professional manner, using evidence, logic, and rhetoric to support your claims and refute the counterclaims.
- Summarize and conclude your speech and conferencing with a clear and memorable message, highlighting the main points and implications of your presentation.
