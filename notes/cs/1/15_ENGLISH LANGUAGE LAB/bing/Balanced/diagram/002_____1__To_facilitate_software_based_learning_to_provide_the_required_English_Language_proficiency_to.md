### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

- Software based learning is a form of education that uses computer programs, applications, or web platforms to deliver instruction, assessment, feedback, and interaction to students.
- Software based learning can provide the required English Language proficiency to students by:
  - Offering personalized and adaptive learning paths that cater to the individual needs, levels, and goals of each student.
  - Providing a variety of multimedia resources, such as audio, video, text, images, and animations, that can enhance the comprehension and retention of linguistic concepts and skills.
  - Enabling interactive and collaborative learning activities, such as games, quizzes, simulations, discussions, and projects, that can motivate and engage students in authentic and meaningful contexts.
  - Giving immediate and constructive feedback and guidance to students, as well as tracking and reporting their progress and performance, that can help them monitor and improve their learning outcomes.
  - Allowing flexible and convenient access to learning materials and support, anytime and anywhere, that can suit the preferences and schedules of different students.
