### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- This is a book that covers various aspects of communication skills, such as listening, speaking, reading, writing, and non-verbal communication.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I deals with the basics of communication, such as the process, types, barriers, and principles of effective communication. Part II focuses on the oral communication skills, such as pronunciation, voice modulation, conversation, presentation, and group discussion. Part III covers the written communication skills, such as grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, spelling, and various forms of writing, such as letters, memos, reports, and resumes. Part IV discusses the non-verbal communication skills, such as body language, gestures, facial expressions, and eye contact.
- The book is designed for students and professionals who want to improve their communication skills in various contexts and situations. The book provides practical examples, exercises, and tips to help the readers apply the concepts and techniques of communication.
- The book is written by L.U.B. Pandey, a professor of English and communication skills at the Institute of Engineering and Technology, Lucknow, India. The book is published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd., a leading publisher of academic and professional books in India. The book was first published in 2007 and has been revised and updated in 2013.
