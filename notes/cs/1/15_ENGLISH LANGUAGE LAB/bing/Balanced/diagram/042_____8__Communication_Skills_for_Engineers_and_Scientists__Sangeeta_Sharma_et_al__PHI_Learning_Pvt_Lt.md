### 8. Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists, Sangeeta Sharma et.al. PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd, 2011, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide for undergraduate students of engineering and science to develop their communication skills in various contexts and situations.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part A covers the principles and practices of technical communication, and Part B covers the applications and genres of technical communication.
- Part A consists of 11 chapters that deal with topics such as technical report writing, communication breakdown, listening, business letters, advertising, communicating without words, and group discussions.
- Part B consists of 9 chapters that deal with topics such as resumes, job applications, interviews, presentations, proposals, feasibility reports, progress reports, and research papers.
- The book aims to help students to communicate effectively in their academic and professional careers, as well as to develop their critical thinking and analytical skills.
- The book provides numerous examples, exercises, case studies, and activities to illustrate and reinforce the concepts and skills of technical communication.
- The book also includes a glossary of technical terms, a bibliography of useful references, and an index for easy access to the topics.
