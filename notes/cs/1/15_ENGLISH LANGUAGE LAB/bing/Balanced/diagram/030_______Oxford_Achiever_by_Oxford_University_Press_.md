### Oxford Achiever by Oxford University Press

- Oxford Achiever is a web-based learning and assessment system that supports the development of 21st century skills in students.
- It covers English and Science subjects for grades 1 to 10, aligned with the curricula of various regions and countries.
- It provides interactive and engaging learning resources, such as animations, videos, games, and simulations, that cater to different learning styles and abilities.
- It also offers adaptive and formative assessments, such as quizzes, tests, and tasks, that measure students' progress and performance in various skills and competencies.
- It enables teachers to monitor and track students' learning outcomes, identify their strengths and weaknesses, and provide feedback and guidance.
- It helps students to develop self-directed learning skills, such as goal-setting, planning, reflection, and evaluation, through the use of e-portfolios and learning journals.
- It fosters a collaborative and communicative learning environment, where students can interact with their peers and teachers through online forums and chats.
- It supports the integration of technology in education, by providing access to a variety of digital tools and resources, such as dictionaries, calculators, and translators.
