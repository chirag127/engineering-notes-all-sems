# 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

- This book is primarily designed as a text for advanced level courses in communication skills.
- It covers various aspects of communication, such as verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, group communication, presentation skills, and writing skills.
- It also provides practical exercises and activities to help learners develop and improve their communication abilities.
- Some of the topics covered in the book are:

  - The nature and process of communication
  - Barriers to effective communication
  - Communication styles and strategies
  - Non-verbal communication and body language
  - Listening and feedback skills
  - Interpersonal communication and conflict resolution
  - Group communication and team building
  - Oral presentation skills and public speaking
  - Business communication and report writing
  - Technical communication and documentation
  - Communication in the digital age and social media

- The book is written in a simple and clear language, with examples and illustrations from various contexts and disciplines.
- It also includes a glossary of key terms and concepts, and a bibliography of relevant sources.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, and professionals who want to enhance their communication skills and competence.
