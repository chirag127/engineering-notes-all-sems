### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

- Professional vocabulary refers to the words and phrases that are specific to a certain field or domain, such as medicine, law, engineering, business, etc.
- Professional vocabulary is important for students who want to pursue a career in a particular field, as it helps them communicate effectively and accurately with other professionals, clients, customers, etc.
- Professional vocabulary also helps students demonstrate their knowledge and skills in various situations, such as exams, interviews, presentations, reports, etc.
- To create a substantial base of professional vocabulary, students need to:
  - Identify the sources of professional vocabulary, such as textbooks, journals, websites, podcasts, etc. that are relevant to their field of interest or study.
  - Read, listen, and watch these sources regularly and actively, paying attention to the context, meaning, and usage of the words and phrases.
  - Record and review the new words and phrases they encounter, using dictionaries, glossaries, or online tools to check their definitions, pronunciations, synonyms, antonyms, etc.
  - Practice using the words and phrases in different modes, such as comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking, by doing exercises, quizzes, tests, etc. that require them to apply their professional vocabulary.
  - Seek feedback and guidance from teachers, mentors, peers, or experts in their field, who can help them correct their mistakes, clarify their doubts, and expand their vocabulary.
- By following these steps, students would be able to create a substantial base of professional vocabulary that they can apply at different platforms and through numerous modes, such as comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking, etc. This would enhance their academic performance, career prospects, and professional development.
