### 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a self-study book for learners of English who want to improve their communication skills in a business context.
- The book contains 50 units, each focusing on a specific aspect of business English, such as meetings, presentations, negotiations, emails, reports, etc.
- Each unit consists of a brief introduction, a main text, a vocabulary section, a grammar section, a practice section, and a summary.
- The book also includes a glossary, a key to the exercises, and an audio CD with listening material.
- The book is suitable for intermediate to advanced learners of English who want to enhance their business English skills in a practical and effective way.
- The book is written by Ken Taylor, a British author and trainer who has extensive experience in teaching business English in various countries and contexts. He is also the co-author of The Vital Link, a web-based self-learning material for international business communication.
