### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- This topic is about how students can use their knowledge and skills in practical and oral presentation situations, such as giving speeches, reports, demonstrations, or interviews.
- Presentation skills are the abilities to plan, prepare, deliver, and evaluate effective and engaging presentations that suit the purpose, audience, and context.
- Voice-dynamics are the aspects of vocal expression that convey meaning, emotion, and attitude, such as volume, pitch, tone, pace, and pauses.
- To be honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics, students need to:

  - Understand the purpose and objectives of their presentation, and tailor their content and delivery accordingly.
  - Analyze the audience and their expectations, and adapt their language, style, and tone to suit them.
  - Organize their content in a clear, logical, and coherent structure, with an introduction, body, and conclusion.
  - Use appropriate and effective visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, images, or videos, to support their message and enhance their presentation.
  - Practice their presentation beforehand, and rehearse their voice-dynamics, such as speaking loudly, clearly, confidently, and expressively, using variations in volume, pitch, tone, pace, and pauses to emphasize key points, create interest, and engage the audience.
  - Deliver their presentation with confidence, enthusiasm, and professionalism, maintaining eye contact, body language, and gestures that convey their message and connect with the audience.
  - Evaluate their presentation afterwards, and seek feedback from others, to identify their strengths and areas for improvement, and to reflect on their learning and performance.
