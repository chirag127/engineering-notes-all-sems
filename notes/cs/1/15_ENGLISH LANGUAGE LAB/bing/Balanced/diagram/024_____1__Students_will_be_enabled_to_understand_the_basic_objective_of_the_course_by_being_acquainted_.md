### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- Communication skills are essential for academic and professional success, as well as personal and social well-being.
- Communication skills involve the ability to use language effectively and appropriately in various contexts and situations, such as formal and informal, oral and written, verbal and non-verbal, etc.
- Communication skills can be divided into five main dimensions: reading, writing, listening, thinking and speaking. Each dimension has its own purpose, process and outcome, and requires different strategies and techniques to master.
- Reading is the process of decoding and comprehending written texts, such as books, articles, reports, etc. Reading skills include the ability to identify the main idea, the supporting details, the tone and the purpose of the text, as well as to infer, analyze, evaluate and synthesize information from different sources.
- Writing is the process of encoding and expressing ideas, thoughts, opinions, arguments, etc. in written form, such as essays, letters, emails, etc. Writing skills include the ability to organize, structure, develop and revise the content, as well as to use appropriate language, grammar, vocabulary, style and format for the intended audience and purpose.
- Listening is the process of receiving and interpreting oral messages, such as lectures, speeches, conversations, etc. Listening skills include the ability to pay attention, to follow the speaker's logic and intention, to identify the main points and the supporting details, to recognize the tone and the mood of the speaker, as well as to respond, question and provide feedback appropriately.
- Thinking is the process of using cognitive skills, such as reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, critical thinking, creative thinking, etc. to process and generate information, ideas, solutions, etc. Thinking skills include the ability to define, analyze, compare, contrast, evaluate, synthesize and apply information, as well as to use logic, evidence, criteria and perspectives to support and challenge arguments and opinions.
- Speaking is the process of delivering oral messages, such as presentations, discussions, debates, etc. Speaking skills include the ability to plan, prepare and practice the content, as well as to use appropriate language, pronunciation, intonation, stress, gesture and eye contact to convey the message clearly, confidently and persuasively to the audience.

- The basic objective of the course is to help students develop and improve their communication skills in all five dimensions, by providing them with theoretical knowledge, practical exercises and feedback. By the end of the course, students should be able to communicate effectively and appropriately in various academic and professional contexts and situations.
