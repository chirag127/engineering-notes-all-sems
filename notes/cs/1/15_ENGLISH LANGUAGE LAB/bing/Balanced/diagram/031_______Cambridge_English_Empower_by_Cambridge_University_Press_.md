### Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press

- Cambridge English Empower is a general adult course that combines course content from Cambridge University Press with validated assessment from the experts at Cambridge English Language Assessment .
- It is more than just a course book - it's a complete solution for effective learning and teaching .
- It covers six levels from A1 to C1, each with a Student's Book, a Workbook, a Teacher's Book, an Online Assessment and Practice package, and a Classroom Presentation Tool .
- It aims to help learners develop the skills and confidence they need to communicate effectively in English in a variety of situations .
- It provides learners with regular and meaningful feedback on their progress, based on the Cambridge English Corpus and the English Vocabulary Profile .
- It also offers teachers a rich and flexible set of resources to support their teaching, including a comprehensive Teacher's Book, a DVD with authentic video clips, and an online teacher training course .
- It is suitable for adult and young adult learners who want to improve their general English proficiency and prepare for international exams such as Cambridge English Qualifications or IELTS  .
