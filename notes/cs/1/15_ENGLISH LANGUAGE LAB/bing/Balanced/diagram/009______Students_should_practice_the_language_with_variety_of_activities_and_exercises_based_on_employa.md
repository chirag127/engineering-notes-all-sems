#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills. Interactive and Communicative Practical with emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication, based on International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.)

- Language learning is a skill that can enhance one's employability and career prospects in the globalized world. Therefore, students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises that can help them develop the competencies required for the workplace.
- Some of the employability skills that can be improved by language practice are: communication, teamwork, problem-solving, creativity, critical thinking, intercultural awareness, digital literacy, and self-management.
- Interactive and communicative practicals are effective ways of practicing the language in realistic and meaningful contexts. They can involve tasks such as role-plays, simulations, debates, discussions, presentations, interviews, and negotiations. These activities can help students to use the language for various purposes and audiences, as well as to develop their fluency, accuracy, and confidence.
- Oral presentation or spoken communication is a key skill that can showcase one's knowledge, ideas, and personality. It can also demonstrate one's ability to organize, structure, and deliver information in a clear and engaging way. Therefore, students should practice their oral presentation skills with feedback and guidance from their peers and teachers.
- International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.) are a system of symbols that represent the sounds of any language. They can help students to improve their pronunciation, intonation, and stress patterns, as well as to understand the differences and similarities between languages. Students should learn the I.P.A. symbols and use them to transcribe and read words and sentences in the target language. They can also use online tools and resources to practice their listening and speaking skills with the I.P.A.
