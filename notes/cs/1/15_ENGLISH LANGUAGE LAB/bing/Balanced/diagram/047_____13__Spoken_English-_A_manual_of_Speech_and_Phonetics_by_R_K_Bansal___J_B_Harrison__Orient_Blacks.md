### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- This is a book for students who wish to improve their pronunciation of English and acquire the correct patterns of stress, rhythm and intonation  .
- The book consists of 20 chapters that cover various aspects of speech and phonetics, such as the organs of speech, the sounds of English, the phonetic transcription, the syllable and word stress, the intonation, the weak forms and contractions, the connected speech, the regional and social variations, and the common errors in pronunciation .
- The book also includes a CD that contains recorded material for practice and self-evaluation   .
- The book is suitable for students of various levels, from beginners to advanced, and can be used for self-study or in the classroom  .
- The book is written by R.K.Bansal and J.B.Harrison, who are both experienced teachers and researchers in the field of English language and linguistics .
- The book is published by Orient Blackswan, a leading publisher of academic and general books in India .
