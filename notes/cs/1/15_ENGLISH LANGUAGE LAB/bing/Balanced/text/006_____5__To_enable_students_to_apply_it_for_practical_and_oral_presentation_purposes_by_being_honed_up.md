### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- This is a learning objective that aims to help students use their knowledge and skills in real-life situations, such as giving presentations, speeches, or reports.
- Presentation skills are the abilities to plan, prepare, deliver, and evaluate effective oral presentations using appropriate language, structure, and visual aids.
- Voice-dynamics are the aspects of vocal expression that convey meaning, emotion, and attitude, such as volume, pitch, tone, speed, and pauses.
- To achieve this objective, students need to:

  - Understand the purpose, audience, and context of their presentations, and tailor their content and style accordingly.
  - Organize their ideas in a clear, logical, and coherent way, using transitions, signposts, and summaries to guide the listeners.
  - Use appropriate language, grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation to communicate their message effectively and accurately.
  - Select and use suitable visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, or images, to support their points and engage the audience.
  - Practice their delivery, using voice-dynamics to enhance their expression, confidence, and credibility.
  - Solicit and respond to feedback, questions, and comments from the audience, and reflect on their strengths and areas for improvement.
