### 9. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace &L.Ann Masters, Cengage Learning, New Delhi

- This is a book that covers various topics related to personal development for life and work, such as self-awareness, soft skills, critical thinking skills, communication skills, and professional workplace attitudes and skills  .
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I: Understanding Yourself, Part II: Understanding Human Relations, Part III: Improving Human Relations, and Part IV: Putting It All Together  .
- Some of the main objectives of the book are to help students:
  - Assess their personal strengths and weaknesses and set realistic goals for improvement.
  - Develop effective interpersonal skills and work well with others in diverse situations.
  - Apply critical thinking and problem-solving skills to various personal and professional challenges.
  - Communicate clearly and confidently in oral and written forms.
  - Demonstrate a positive work ethic and a high level of professionalism in the workplace.
- The book also includes various features to enhance learning, such as self-assessment exercises, case studies, role plays, group activities, and online resources   .
