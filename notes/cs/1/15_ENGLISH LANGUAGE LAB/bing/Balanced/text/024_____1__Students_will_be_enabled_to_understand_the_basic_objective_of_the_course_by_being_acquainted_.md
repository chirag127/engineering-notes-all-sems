### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- Communication skills are essential for academic and professional success, as well as for personal and social development.
- Communication skills involve the ability to use language effectively and appropriately in various contexts and situations, both verbally and non-verbally.
- Communication skills can be classified into five main dimensions: reading, writing, listening, thinking and speaking. Each dimension has its own purpose, process and challenges.
- Reading is the process of decoding and comprehending written texts, such as books, articles, reports, etc. Reading skills include the ability to identify the main idea, supporting details, tone, purpose and audience of a text, as well as to infer, analyze, evaluate and synthesize information from different sources.
- Writing is the process of encoding and expressing ideas, thoughts, opinions, arguments, etc. in written form, such as essays, letters, emails, etc. Writing skills include the ability to organize, structure, draft, revise, edit and proofread a text, as well as to use appropriate language, style, format and conventions for different purposes and audiences.
- Listening is the process of receiving, attending, understanding and responding to spoken messages, such as lectures, presentations, conversations, etc. Listening skills include the ability to recognize the main points, supporting details, tone, purpose and audience of a message, as well as to infer, analyze, evaluate and synthesize information from different sources.
- Thinking is the process of generating, exploring, evaluating and applying ideas, thoughts, opinions, arguments, etc. in various situations and contexts. Thinking skills include the ability to use critical, creative, logical and reflective thinking, as well as to use problem-solving, decision-making and metacognitive strategies.
- Speaking is the process of producing and delivering spoken messages, such as speeches, discussions, debates, etc. Speaking skills include the ability to plan, prepare, rehearse, deliver and evaluate a speech, as well as to use appropriate language, voice, body language and visual aids for different purposes and audiences.
- The basic objective of the course is to help students develop and improve their communication skills in all five dimensions, by providing them with theoretical knowledge, practical exercises, feedback and guidance. By the end of the course, students should be able to communicate effectively and confidently in various academic and professional settings, as well as in their personal and social lives.
