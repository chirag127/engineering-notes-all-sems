### MePro by Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd.

- MePro is a digital English language learning platform that aims to transform English language learning for students and professionals.
- MePro uses artificial intelligence and adaptive learning to provide personalized feedback and guidance to learners based on their strengths and weaknesses.
- MePro covers four key skills of English language: listening, speaking, reading and writing, and also helps learners improve their grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation.
- MePro offers various courses and modules for different levels and purposes, such as general English, business English, academic English, test preparation, etc.
- MePro also provides learners with a globally recognized certificate of proficiency based on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) upon completion of the course.
- MePro is a product of Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd., which is a subsidiary of Pearson, the world's leading learning company .
- Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd. is registered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, and offers a range of educational products and services, such as textbooks, digital content, assessments, teacher training, etc .
- Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd. has received several awards and recognitions for its excellence and innovation in the education sector, such as the Bright Spot Award, the EdTech Review Award, the India Education Awards, etc .
