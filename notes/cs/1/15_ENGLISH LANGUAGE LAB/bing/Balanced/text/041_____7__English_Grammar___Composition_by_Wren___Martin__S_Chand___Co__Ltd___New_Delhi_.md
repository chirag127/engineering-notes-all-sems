### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

- This is a comprehensive book on English grammar and composition for students of different levels, from primary to advanced.
- The book covers various topics such as parts of speech, sentence structure, clauses, phrases, punctuation, spelling, vocabulary, idioms, letter writing, essay writing, precis writing, and comprehension.
- The book follows a clear and logical progression, with each chapter building on the previous one. The book also provides ample exercises and examples for practice and revision.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part I deals with grammar and Part II deals with composition. Part I has 69 chapters and Part II has 11 chapters.
- The book is based on the revised edition of High School English Grammar and Composition by the same authors, which was first published in 1935 and has been widely used by students and teachers of English.
- The book is suitable for both self-study and classroom use. It can also be used as a reference book for learners of English as a second or foreign language.
