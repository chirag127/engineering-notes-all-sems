### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- Communication skills are essential for academic and professional success, as well as for personal and social development.
- Communication skills can be classified into five main dimensions: reading, writing, listening, thinking and speaking.
- Reading is the process of decoding and comprehending written texts. Reading skills include the ability to identify the main idea, summarize, infer, analyze, evaluate and synthesize information from various sources.
- Writing is the process of encoding and expressing ideas in written form. Writing skills include the ability to organize, structure, draft, revise, edit and proofread texts for different purposes, audiences and genres.
- Listening is the process of receiving and interpreting oral messages. Listening skills include the ability to pay attention, follow instructions, ask questions, clarify, paraphrase, summarize and respond to verbal communication.
- Thinking is the process of generating and applying ideas, knowledge and reasoning to solve problems and make decisions. Thinking skills include the ability to identify, define, analyze, evaluate, synthesize and apply information from various sources and perspectives.
- Speaking is the process of conveying and exchanging ideas orally. Speaking skills include the ability to articulate, pronounce, modulate, adapt, persuade and interact with others in various contexts and situations.
