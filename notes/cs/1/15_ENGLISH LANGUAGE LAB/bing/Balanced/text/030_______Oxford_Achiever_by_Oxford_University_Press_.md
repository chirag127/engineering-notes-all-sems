### Oxford Achiever by Oxford University Press

- Oxford Achiever is a web-based learning and assessment system that supports students' self-directed learning and helps them develop 21st century skills.
- Oxford Achiever covers English and Mathematics for primary and secondary levels, and Science for secondary level.
- Oxford Achiever provides interactive and engaging learning resources that are aligned with the curriculum and learning objectives of each level and subject.
- Oxford Achiever also offers adaptive and diagnostic assessments that measure students' progress and identify their strengths and weaknesses.
- Oxford Achiever enables teachers to monitor students' performance and provide feedback and guidance through a user-friendly dashboard and reporting system.
- Oxford Achiever empowers students to take charge of their own learning and achieve their academic goals.
