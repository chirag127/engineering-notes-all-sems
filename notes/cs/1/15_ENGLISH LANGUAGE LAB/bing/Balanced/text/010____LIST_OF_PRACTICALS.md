## LIST OF PRACTICALS

- A practical is a hands-on activity that involves applying theoretical knowledge to a specific problem or task.
- Practicals can be conducted in various fields of study, such as science, engineering, arts, or humanities.
- Practicals can help students to develop skills, such as observation, experimentation, analysis, creativity, or communication.
- Practicals can also help students to understand the relevance and applicability of their subject matter to real-world situations.
- Some examples of practicals are:

  - Performing a chemical reaction and measuring its rate and products.
  - Designing and testing a circuit or a device using electronic components.
  - Creating and presenting a portfolio of artworks or a performance piece.
  - Conducting a survey or an interview and analyzing the data.
  - Writing a program or a script using a programming language or a software tool.
