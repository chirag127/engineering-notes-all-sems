### Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press

- Cambridge English Empower is a general adult course that combines course content from Cambridge University Press with validated assessment from the experts at Cambridge English Language Assessment  .
- It is a complete solution for effective learning and teaching of English for adult and young adult learners  .
- It consists of six levels, from A1 (Starter) to C1 (Advanced), each with a student's book, a workbook, a teacher's book, an online assessment package, and a classroom presentation tool  .
- It follows a learner-centered approach that focuses on developing the four skills (reading, writing, listening, and speaking), as well as grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation  .
- It provides learners with regular and meaningful feedback on their progress and performance, using the Cambridge English Corpus and the Cambridge Learner Corpus  .
- It helps learners prepare for the Cambridge English Qualifications, such as the Preliminary, First, and Advanced exams  .
- It offers a rich and varied learning experience that engages and motivates learners to achieve their goals  .
