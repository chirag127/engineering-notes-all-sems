### 3. Students will apply it at their work place for writing purposes such as Presentation/official drafting/administrative communication and use it for document/project/report/research paper writing.

- This topic is about the practical applications of the skills and knowledge acquired from the course.
- Students will learn how to use the appropriate language, tone, format, and style for different types of writing purposes in their work place.
- Students will also learn how to organize, structure, and present their ideas clearly and coherently in various forms of written communication.
- Some examples of writing purposes that students will encounter in their work place are:

  - Presentation: A presentation is a form of oral communication that involves delivering information, arguments, or proposals to an audience. A presentation usually has a specific goal, such as persuading, informing, or instructing. A presentation also requires a written component, such as a script, slides, handouts, or notes, that supports the speaker and helps the audience follow the main points. Students will learn how to write effective presentations that are engaging, concise, and relevant to the audience and the context.
  - Official drafting: Official drafting is a form of written communication that involves creating formal documents, such as letters, memos, reports, proposals, or contracts, that are used for official or legal purposes. Official drafting requires a high level of accuracy, clarity, and professionalism, as well as adherence to specific conventions and standards. Students will learn how to write official drafts that are precise, coherent, and courteous, and that meet the expectations and requirements of the recipients and the situation.
  - Administrative communication: Administrative communication is a form of written communication that involves managing and coordinating various tasks, activities, or processes within an organization or a department. Administrative communication includes writing emails, notices, agendas, minutes, forms, or instructions, that are used for internal or external communication. Administrative communication requires a high level of efficiency, organization, and politeness, as well as awareness of the tone and the purpose of the message. Students will learn how to write administrative communication that are clear, concise, and respectful, and that achieve the intended outcomes and responses.
  - Document/project/report/research paper writing: Document/project/report/research paper writing is a form of written communication that involves producing comprehensive and detailed documents that present the results, findings, or recommendations of a specific study, investigation, or project. Document/project/report/research paper writing includes writing abstracts, introductions, literature reviews, methods, results, discussions, conclusions, or references, that are used for academic or professional purposes. Document/project/report/research paper writing requires a high level of critical thinking, analysis, synthesis, and evaluation, as well as adherence to specific guidelines and formats. Students will learn how to write document/project/report/research paper writing that are logical, coherent, and credible, and that demonstrate their understanding and contribution to the field or the topic.
