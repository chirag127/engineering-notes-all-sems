### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

- Software based learning is a form of computer-assisted language learning (CALL) that uses software applications or programs to deliver interactive and adaptive instruction in English language skills such as reading, writing, listening, speaking, grammar, and vocabulary.
- Software based learning can provide the following benefits to students who want to improve their English language proficiency:
  - It can offer personalized and differentiated learning paths based on the students' needs, interests, goals, and preferences.
  - It can provide immediate and constructive feedback, error correction, and reinforcement to the students' responses and performance.
  - It can simulate authentic and meaningful contexts and situations for language use and communication.
  - It can enhance the students' motivation, engagement, and autonomy in learning.
  - It can supplement and complement the traditional classroom instruction and provide additional practice and exposure to the target language.
- Some examples of software based learning tools for English language learning are:
  - Duolingo: a gamified and adaptive platform that teaches English and other languages through short lessons, quizzes, and stories.
  - Rosetta Stone: a comprehensive and immersive program that uses images, sounds, and speech recognition to teach English and other languages.
  - Memrise: a spaced repetition and memory-based system that helps learners memorize words, phrases, and grammar rules in English and other languages.
  - Quizlet: a flashcard and study app that allows learners to create, share, and access sets of terms and definitions in English and other languages.
  - LingQ: a content-based and community-driven platform that helps learners acquire vocabulary and improve comprehension by reading and listening to texts in English and other languages.
