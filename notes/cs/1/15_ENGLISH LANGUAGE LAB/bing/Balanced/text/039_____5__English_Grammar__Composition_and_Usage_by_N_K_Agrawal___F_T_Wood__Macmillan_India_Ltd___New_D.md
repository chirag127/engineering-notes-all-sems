### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

This is a comprehensive textbook for students of English language and literature. It covers the following topics:

- The parts of speech and their functions in sentences
- The structure and types of sentences, clauses and phrases
- The rules and conventions of punctuation and capitalization
- The principles and methods of paragraph and essay writing
- The common errors and pitfalls in grammar and usage
- The vocabulary and idioms of standard and formal English
- The stylistic devices and figures of speech in literature
- The techniques of summarizing, paraphrasing and quoting
- The formats and guidelines for writing letters, reports, proposals and other forms of communication

The book is divided into four sections: Grammar, Composition, Usage and Literature. Each section consists of several chapters with clear explanations, examples, exercises and tests. The book also provides appendices on irregular verbs, spelling rules, prefixes and suffixes, and common abbreviations. The book is suitable for students of various levels and backgrounds, as well as for teachers and learners of English as a second or foreign language.
