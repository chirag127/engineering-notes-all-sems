### 4. English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi.

This book is a comprehensive guide to the rules and principles of English grammar and usage. It covers the following topics:

- The parts of speech and their functions in sentences
- The types and structures of sentences and clauses
- The formation and use of verb tenses and aspects
- The rules and exceptions of subject-verb agreement and pronoun-antecedent agreement
- The formation and use of nouns, pronouns, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections
- The rules and conventions of punctuation, capitalization, spelling, and hyphenation
- The common errors and pitfalls in grammar and usage and how to avoid them
- The stylistic and rhetorical aspects of grammar and usage, such as clarity, coherence, conciseness, variety, and tone
- The differences and similarities between British and American English
- The usage of idioms, phrasal verbs, and collocations

The book is divided into 20 chapters, each with a summary, exercises, and answers. It also includes appendices on irregular verbs, verb patterns, and word formation. It is suitable for students, teachers, and professionals who want to improve their knowledge and skills in English grammar and usage.
