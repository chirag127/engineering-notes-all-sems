### New Interactions by McGraw-Hill India

- New Interactions is a series of textbooks for English language learners from beginner to advanced levels.
- The series aims to develop the four skills of listening, speaking, reading and writing, as well as grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation.
- The series is based on the communicative approach, which encourages learners to interact with authentic texts and tasks in meaningful contexts.
- The series consists of six levels: New Interactions Access, New Interactions 1, New Interactions 2, New Interactions 3, New Interactions 4 and New Interactions 5.
- Each level has a student book, a workbook, a teacher's edition, an audio CD and a companion website.
- The student book contains 12 units, each with a theme, a goal and a project. The units are divided into four sections: listening and speaking, reading and writing, grammar and vocabulary, and pronunciation and spelling.
- The workbook provides additional practice and reinforcement of the language skills and concepts covered in the student book.
- The teacher's edition provides detailed lesson plans, teaching tips, answer keys, tests and supplementary materials.
- The audio CD contains the recordings of the listening texts and activities in the student book and the workbook.
- The companion website offers interactive exercises, games, videos, podcasts and other resources to enhance the learning experience.
