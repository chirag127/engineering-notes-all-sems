### 4. Students will be made to evaluate the correct and error-free writing by being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivate relevant technical style of communication &presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- This topic covers the importance of writing skills for students in various contexts, such as academic, professional, and personal.
- Writing skills involve the ability to produce clear, coherent, and accurate texts that convey the intended message and purpose to the audience.
- Writing skills also require the knowledge and application of the rules of English grammar, such as syntax, punctuation, spelling, and vocabulary.
- Writing skills can be improved by practicing different types of writing, such as essays, reports, letters, emails, summaries, etc., and by receiving feedback from peers, teachers, or experts.
- Writing skills can help students to achieve better grades, to communicate effectively with others, to express their opinions and ideas, and to enhance their critical thinking and creativity.
- Writing skills can also prepare students for the workplace, where they may need to write technical documents, proposals, memos, presentations, etc., that follow specific formats, styles, and standards.
- Writing skills can also benefit students in their personal lives, where they may need to write for social media, blogs, journals, etc., that reflect their personality, interests, and values.
