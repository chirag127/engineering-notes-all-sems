### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation is a type of speech that introduces, develops, and summarizes a central idea or message for an audience.
- The purpose of a theme presentation or a keynote presentation is to inform, persuade, inspire, or motivate the audience to take action, change their perspective, or adopt a new behavior.
- The correct methodologies of argumentation are the principles and techniques of reasoning and evidence that support the validity and soundness of the speaker's claims and arguments.
- Some of the correct methodologies of argumentation are:

  - Establishing a clear and specific thesis statement that expresses the main claim or proposition of the speech.
  - Providing sufficient and relevant supporting points that explain, illustrate, or justify the thesis statement.
  - Organizing the supporting points in a logical and coherent manner that follows a clear pattern of arrangement, such as chronological, spatial, topical, problem-solution, cause-effect, etc.
  - Using credible and reliable sources of information and data to back up the supporting points and cite them properly according to the citation style required by the occasion or the audience.
  - Acknowledging and refuting the possible counterarguments or objections that the audience may have against the thesis statement or the supporting points.
  - Using appropriate language, tone, and style that suit the purpose, audience, and occasion of the speech.
  - Delivering the speech with confidence, clarity, and enthusiasm, using effective verbal and nonverbal communication skills, such as eye contact, gestures, voice modulation, etc.
  - Concluding the speech with a summary of the main points, a restatement of the thesis statement, and a call to action or a memorable statement that leaves a lasting impression on the audience.
