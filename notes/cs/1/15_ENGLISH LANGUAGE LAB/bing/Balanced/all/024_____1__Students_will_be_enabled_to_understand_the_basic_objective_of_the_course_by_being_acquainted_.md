# 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- Communication skills are essential for academic and professional success, as well as for personal and social development.
- Communication skills involve the ability to use language effectively and appropriately in various contexts and situations, such as formal and informal, oral and written, verbal and non-verbal, etc.
- Communication skills can be classified into five main dimensions: reading, writing, listening, thinking and speaking. Each dimension has its own purpose, process and outcome, and requires different strategies and techniques to master.
- Reading is the process of extracting meaning from written texts, such as books, articles, reports, etc. Reading skills include the ability to comprehend, analyze, evaluate and synthesize information from various sources and genres, as well as to apply critical thinking and creativity to the texts.
- Writing is the process of producing written texts, such as essays, letters, emails, etc. Writing skills include the ability to organize, express, support and revise ideas in a clear, coherent and accurate manner, as well as to adapt to different purposes, audiences and formats.
- Listening is the process of receiving and interpreting oral messages, such as lectures, presentations, conversations, etc. Listening skills include the ability to pay attention, understand, remember and respond to the messages, as well as to infer and evaluate the speaker's intentions, attitudes and emotions.
- Thinking is the process of generating, exploring and refining ideas, opinions and arguments, using logic, reasoning and evidence. Thinking skills include the ability to identify, classify, compare and contrast, cause and effect, problem-solve and decision-make, as well as to apply creativity and innovation to the ideas.
- Speaking is the process of delivering oral messages, such as speeches, discussions, debates, etc. Speaking skills include the ability to plan, prepare, practice and perform the messages, as well as to use appropriate language, voice, body language and visual aids to communicate effectively and confidently.

- The basic objective of the course is to enable students to develop and improve their communication skills in all five dimensions, by providing them with theoretical knowledge, practical exercises and feedback. By the end of the course, students will be able to:

  - Read various types of texts with comprehension, analysis, evaluation and synthesis.
  - Write various types of texts with organization, expression, support and revision.
  - Listen to various types of messages with attention, understanding, memory and response.
  - Think of various types of ideas with logic, reasoning, evidence and creativity.
  - Speak on various types of topics with planning, preparation, practice and performance.

- The course will also help students to:

  - Enhance their vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation and fluency in the language.
  - Develop their intercultural awareness and sensitivity in communication.
  - Foster their critical thinking and problem-solving skills in communication.
  - Increase their confidence and motivation in communication.
