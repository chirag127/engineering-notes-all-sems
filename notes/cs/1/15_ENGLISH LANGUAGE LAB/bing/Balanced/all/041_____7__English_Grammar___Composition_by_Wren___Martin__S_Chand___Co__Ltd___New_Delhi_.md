# 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

- This is a comprehensive book on English grammar and composition for students of different levels, from primary to advanced.
- The book covers various aspects of grammar, such as parts of speech, sentence structure, phrases and clauses, punctuation, spelling, vocabulary, and usage.
- The book also provides ample practice exercises, examples, and illustrations to help students master the rules and principles of grammar and composition.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part I deals with grammar and Part II deals with composition.
- Part I consists of 69 chapters, each focusing on a specific topic of grammar, such as nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, interjections, articles, tenses, voice, mood, narration, transformation, analysis, synthesis, and parsing.
- Part II consists of 22 chapters, each focusing on a specific type of composition, such as essay writing, letter writing, precis writing, paragraph writing, expansion of passages, comprehension, note making, summary writing, report writing, and email writing.
- The book also contains appendices on common errors, idioms and phrases, synonyms and antonyms, and word power.
- The book is suitable for self-study as well as classroom teaching. It is widely used by students preparing for various competitive examinations, such as SSC, UPSC, Bank PO, and others.
- The book is written in a clear, simple, and concise manner, with easy-to-follow explanations and examples. It is one of the most popular and authoritative books on English grammar and composition in India.
