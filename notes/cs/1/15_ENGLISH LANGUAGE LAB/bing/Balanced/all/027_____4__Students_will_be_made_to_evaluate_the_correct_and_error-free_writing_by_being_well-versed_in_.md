# 4. Students will be made to evaluate the correct and error-free writing by being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivate relevant technical style of communication &presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- This topic covers the importance of writing skills for students who want to succeed in their academic and professional careers.
- Writing skills involve the ability to produce clear, coherent, and error-free texts that follow the rules of English grammar and the conventions of different genres and formats.
- Writing skills also include the ability to adapt one's style and tone to the purpose, audience, and context of the communication or presentation.
- To evaluate the correct and error-free writing, students need to be well-versed in the rules of English grammar, such as syntax, morphology, punctuation, spelling, and vocabulary.
- Students also need to be aware of the common errors and pitfalls that can affect the quality and clarity of their writing, such as ambiguity, redundancy, inconsistency, plagiarism, and logical fallacies.
- To cultivate the relevant technical style of communication and presentation, students need to learn how to use appropriate language, terminology, and formats for different types of texts, such as reports, proposals, essays, articles, emails, and slides.
- Students also need to learn how to organize their ideas, support their arguments, cite their sources, and edit and proofread their drafts.
- By developing their writing skills, students can enhance their academic performance, improve their professional prospects, and communicate effectively and confidently in various situations and settings.
