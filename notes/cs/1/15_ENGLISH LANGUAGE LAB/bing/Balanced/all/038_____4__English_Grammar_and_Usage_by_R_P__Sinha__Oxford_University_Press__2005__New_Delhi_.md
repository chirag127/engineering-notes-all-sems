# 4. English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi.

This book is a comprehensive guide to the rules and principles of English grammar and usage. It covers the following topics:

- Parts of speech: nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
- Sentence structure: subjects, predicates, objects, complements, modifiers, clauses, and phrases.
- Tenses: present, past, future, and their forms and functions.
- Voice: active and passive voice, and their transformations.
- Mood: indicative, imperative, subjunctive, and conditional mood, and their expressions.
- Modals: can, could, may, might, will, would, shall, should, must, and their meanings and uses.
- Conditionals: types of conditional sentences, and their formation and usage.
- Reported speech: direct and indirect speech, and their rules and changes.
- Questions: types of questions, and their formation and punctuation.
- Negatives: types of negatives, and their formation and placement.
- Articles: definite and indefinite articles, and their rules and exceptions.
- Determiners: types of determiners, and their functions and positions.
- Pronouns: types of pronouns, and their cases, numbers, genders, and agreements.
- Adjectives: types of adjectives, and their degrees, comparisons, and order.
- Verbs: types of verbs, and their forms, aspects, moods, and voices.
- Adverbs: types of adverbs, and their degrees, comparisons, and positions.
- Prepositions: types of prepositions, and their meanings and uses.
- Conjunctions: types of conjunctions, and their functions and positions.
- Interjections: types of interjections, and their meanings and punctuations.
- Punctuation: types of punctuation marks, and their rules and uses.
- Spelling: common spelling rules and exceptions, and tips for improving spelling.
- Vocabulary: common words and phrases, and their meanings and usage.
- Idioms: common idioms and expressions, and their meanings and usage.
- Figures of speech: types of figures of speech, and their definitions and examples.
- Writing skills: types of writing, and their features and formats.
- Reading skills: types of reading, and their strategies and techniques.
- Listening skills: types of listening, and their skills and tips.
- Speaking skills: types of speaking, and their skills and tips.
