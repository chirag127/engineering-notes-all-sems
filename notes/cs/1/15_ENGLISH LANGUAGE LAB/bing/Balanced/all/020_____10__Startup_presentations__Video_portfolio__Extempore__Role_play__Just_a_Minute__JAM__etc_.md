# 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

These are some of the common types of activities that are used to assess the communication skills, creativity, and personality of candidates in various fields and domains. Here is a brief overview of each activity and some tips on how to prepare and perform well in them.

## Startup presentations

- A startup presentation is a short and concise pitch that showcases the idea, vision, and value proposition of a new venture or project to potential investors, customers, or partners.
- The purpose of a startup presentation is to capture the attention and interest of the audience, demonstrate the problem and solution, highlight the market opportunity and competitive advantage, and persuade them to take action or provide feedback.
- A startup presentation usually follows a standard structure, such as:

  - Introduction: Introduce yourself, your team, and your company name and logo.
  - Problem: Explain the pain point or need that your target customers are facing and why it is important to solve it.
  - Solution: Describe how your product or service solves the problem and what benefits it offers to the customers.
  - Market: Define the size and characteristics of your target market and how you plan to reach and acquire customers.
  - Competition: Identify your main competitors and how you differentiate yourself from them in terms of features, benefits, pricing, etc.
  - Business model: Explain how you generate revenue and what are your costs and profit margins.
  - Traction: Showcase any evidence of customer validation, user feedback, sales, partnerships, or awards that you have achieved so far.
  - Ask: Specify what you are looking for from the audience, such as funding, feedback, referrals, or collaboration, and how they can contact you.

- A startup presentation should be clear, concise, and compelling, using simple and engaging language, visuals, and storytelling techniques. It should also be tailored to the specific audience and context, and address any potential questions or objections that they might have.
- A startup presentation should be rehearsed and practiced multiple times, preferably with feedback from peers, mentors, or experts, and refined based on the results. It should also be delivered with confidence, enthusiasm, and passion, while maintaining eye contact, body language, and voice modulation.

## Video portfolio

- A video portfolio is a collection of short videos that showcase the skills, talents, and achievements of a person or a team in a specific field or domain.
- The purpose of a video portfolio is to demonstrate the quality and diversity of the work, highlight the strengths and personality of the creator, and impress the viewers with the creativity and professionalism of the presentation.
- A video portfolio usually consists of several clips, each focusing on a different aspect or project of the work, such as:

  - Introduction: Introduce yourself, your background, and your goals or objectives.
  - Samples: Show examples of your best or most relevant work, explaining the context, process, and outcome of each project.
  - Testimonials: Include feedback or endorsements from your clients, customers, or collaborators, highlighting the value and impact of your work.
  - Conclusion: Summarize your main points, express your gratitude, and provide your contact information.

- A video portfolio should be visually appealing, informative, and persuasive, using high-quality images, sound, and editing, as well as clear and concise narration, captions, and transitions. It should also be consistent, coherent, and customized, reflecting the style, tone, and purpose of the work and the audience.
- A video portfolio should be planned and prepared carefully, selecting the best and most relevant samples, arranging them in a logical and engaging order, and scripting and rehearsing the narration. It should also be reviewed and revised multiple times, ensuring that it meets the technical and content requirements and expectations of the viewers.

## Extempore

- An extempore is a spontaneous and impromptu speech or presentation on a given topic or theme, without any prior preparation or notes.
- The purpose of an extempore is to test the ability to think on one's feet, organize and express one's thoughts clearly and coherently, and communicate effectively and confidently in front of an audience.
- An extempore usually follows a basic structure, such as:

  - Introduction: Introduce the topic and state the main idea or thesis of the speech.
  - Body: Develop the main idea with supporting points, examples, facts, or arguments, using a logical and coherent sequence and transition words.
  - Conclusion: Restate the main idea and summarize the main points, ending with a memorable or impactful statement or call to action.
