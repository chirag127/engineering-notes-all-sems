# Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press

- Cambridge English Empower is a general adult course that combines course content from Cambridge University Press with validated assessment from the experts at Cambridge English Language Assessment .
- It is a complete solution for effective learning and teaching of English for adult and young adult learners .
- It consists of six levels: Starter, Elementary, Pre-intermediate, Intermediate, Upper Intermediate, and Advanced .
- Each level has a Student's Book, a Workbook, a Teacher's Book, a Class Audio CD, a Class DVD, and an Online Assessment and Practice platform .
- The Student's Book gives learners an immediate sense of purpose and clear learning objectives for each unit  .
- The Workbook provides further practice of the language and skills covered in the Student's Book .
- The Teacher's Book includes detailed teaching notes, answer keys, extra activities, photocopiable resources, and progress tests .
- The Class Audio CD contains all the listening material for the Student's Book .
- The Class DVD contains video clips that illustrate the topics and language of the Student's Book .
- The Online Assessment and Practice platform provides learners with access to interactive activities, videos, tests, and feedback on their progress .
- The course aims to help learners develop their confidence, fluency, and accuracy in English, as well as prepare them for internationally recognized exams such as Cambridge English Qualifications .
- The course is based on the latest research on language learning and teaching, and uses authentic texts and videos from a variety of sources .
- The course also incorporates the Cambridge Learner Corpus, a database of real learner errors, to help learners avoid common mistakes and improve their accuracy .
- The course is suitable for learners of different ages, backgrounds, and interests, and can be adapted to different teaching contexts and styles .
