# Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, W.R.Goyal Pub. & Distributors, 2009, Delhi.

- Word Power Made Easy is a book by Norman Lewis, an English language expert and lexicographer.
- The book aims to help readers improve their vocabulary and master the correct usage of words.
- The book is divided into three main sections: Part One: Getting Off to a Good Start, Part Two: Gaining Increased Momentum, and Part Three: Finishing with a Feeling of Complete Success.
- Each section consists of several chapters that cover different aspects of word building, such as roots, prefixes, suffixes, synonyms, antonyms, word analysis, word formation, etymology, and pronunciation.
- The book also provides exercises, quizzes, tests, and reviews to help readers practice and reinforce their learning.
- The book is written in a simple, clear, and engaging style, with examples, anecdotes, and humor to make the learning process enjoyable and effective.
- The book is suitable for students, professionals, and anyone who wants to improve their vocabulary and communication skills.
