## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

- Part A: This part consists of oral communication skills, which include listening, speaking, and presentation skills. The objectives of this part are to:
  - Enhance the students' ability to comprehend and respond to various types of oral communication.
  - Develop the students' confidence and competence in speaking effectively in different situations and contexts.
  - Train the students to plan, prepare, and deliver effective oral presentations using appropriate visual aids and technology.
- Part B: This part consists of written communication skills, which include reading, writing, and documentation skills. The objectives of this part are to:
  - Improve the students' ability to read and comprehend various types of written texts.
  - Develop the students' proficiency and accuracy in writing different types of documents and reports.
  - Familiarize the students with the conventions and formats of professional communication and documentation.
