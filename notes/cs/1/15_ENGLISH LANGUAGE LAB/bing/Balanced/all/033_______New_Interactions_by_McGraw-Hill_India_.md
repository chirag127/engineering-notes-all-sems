# New Interactions by McGraw-Hill India

- New Interactions is a series of textbooks for English language learners from McGraw-Hill India.
- The series consists of six levels, from beginner to advanced, and covers the four skills of listening, speaking, reading, and writing.
- The series aims to develop communicative competence, critical thinking, and cultural awareness among learners.
- The series follows a learner-centered approach, with activities that encourage interaction, collaboration, and reflection.
- The series also integrates digital resources, such as audio, video, and online practice, to enhance learning outcomes and motivation.
- The series is aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR) and the Cambridge English Qualifications.
