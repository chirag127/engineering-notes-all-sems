# 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

This book is a comprehensive guide to the rules and principles of English grammar, composition and usage. It covers the following topics:

- Parts of speech: nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions and interjections.
- Sentence structure: subject, predicate, object, complement, modifier, clause and phrase.
- Types of sentences: simple, compound, complex and compound-complex sentences.
- Tenses: present, past and future tenses, and their forms and uses.
- Voice: active and passive voice, and their rules and transformations.
- Mood: indicative, imperative, subjunctive and conditional moods, and their forms and functions.
- Modals: modal verbs, their meanings and uses, and their rules and exceptions.
- Non-finites: infinitives, gerunds and participles, and their forms and functions.
- Direct and indirect speech: rules and methods of changing direct speech into indirect speech, and vice versa.
- Punctuation: rules and uses of various punctuation marks, such as comma, semicolon, colon, apostrophe, quotation marks, etc.
- Spelling: rules and tips for correct spelling of common and difficult words, and spelling variations in British and American English.
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, homonyms, homophones, homographs, prefixes, suffixes, root words, etc.
- Idioms and phrases: meanings and uses of common idioms and phrases in English.
- Composition: types and formats of various forms of writing, such as essay, letter, report, summary, précis, etc.
- Usage: common errors and mistakes in English grammar, composition and usage, and how to avoid and correct them.
