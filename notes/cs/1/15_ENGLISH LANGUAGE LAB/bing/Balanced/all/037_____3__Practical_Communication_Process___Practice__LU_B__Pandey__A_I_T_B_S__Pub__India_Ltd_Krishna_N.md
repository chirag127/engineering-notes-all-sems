# 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

- This is a book that covers the basics of communication skills and techniques for technical and professional purposes.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I - Communication Process and Principles, Part II - Communication Skills and Strategies, Part III - Communication in Specific Contexts, and Part IV - Communication for Career Development.
- Some of the topics covered in the book are:

  - The nature, functions, and types of communication
  - The barriers and facilitators of effective communication
  - The verbal and non-verbal aspects of communication
  - The listening and feedback skills
  - The interpersonal and group communication skills
  - The written and oral communication skills
  - The presentation and public speaking skills
  - The communication for meetings, interviews, and negotiations
  - The communication for reports, proposals, and technical papers
  - The communication for resumes, cover letters, and job applications

- The book is intended for students and professionals who want to improve their communication skills and enhance their career prospects.
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with examples, exercises, and case studies to illustrate the concepts and techniques.
- The book is based on the syllabus of the technical communication course prescribed by the A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) .
