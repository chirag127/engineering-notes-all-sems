### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

- The objective of this topic is to help students improve their writing skills by learning and applying the rules of English grammar.
- Writing is a form of communication that requires clarity, accuracy, and coherence. Writing errors can affect the meaning, credibility, and effectiveness of the message.
- English grammar is the system of rules that governs how words and sentences are structured, used, and punctuated in the language. Grammar rules cover various aspects of writing, such as word order, verb tense, agreement, voice, mood, modifiers, pronouns, articles, prepositions, conjunctions, and punctuation.
- To use the correct and error-free writing, students need to be well versed in the rules of English grammar. This means that they need to know the definitions, functions, and examples of the grammatical elements, as well as how to identify and correct common grammatical errors.
- Some of the benefits of being well versed in the rules of English grammar are:

  - It enhances the clarity and precision of the writing, making it easier for the reader to understand the message and the purpose of the writer.
  - It increases the confidence and credibility of the writer, as it shows that they have a good command of the language and respect the conventions of writing.
  - It allows the writer to express their ideas and opinions more effectively and persuasively, as it helps them to organize their thoughts, use appropriate tone and style, and avoid ambiguity and confusion.
  - It fosters the creativity and originality of the writer, as it enables them to use a variety of words, sentences, and structures to convey their meaning and engage the reader's interest.

- Some of the strategies to train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in the rules of English grammar are:

  - Provide clear and concise explanations and examples of the grammar rules, using relevant and authentic texts and contexts.
  - Encourage students to practice and apply the grammar rules in their own writing, using different genres, purposes, and audiences.
  - Give constructive and specific feedback on the students' writing, pointing out the strengths and weaknesses, and suggesting ways to improve and revise their work.
  - Use a variety of activities and exercises to reinforce and review the grammar rules, such as quizzes, games, worksheets, editing, and peer review.
  - Motivate and challenge students to learn and use the grammar rules, by setting realistic and achievable goals, praising their efforts and progress, and providing them with opportunities to showcase their work.
