### 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a book by Ken Taylor that aims to help learners improve their communication skills in various business contexts.
- The book is divided into 50 units, each focusing on a specific topic or skill, such as meetings, negotiations, presentations, emails, reports, etc.
- Each unit consists of four sections: introduction, language focus, practice, and review.
- The introduction provides the context and objectives of the unit, as well as some key vocabulary and expressions.
- The language focus explains the grammar, pronunciation, and style points relevant to the unit, with examples and exercises.
- The practice section offers a variety of activities to help learners apply the language and skills they have learned, such as role plays, simulations, dialogues, etc.
- The review section summarizes the main points of the unit and provides further practice and feedback.
- The book also includes a glossary, an answer key, and a CD-ROM with audio recordings of the dialogues and exercises.
- The book is suitable for intermediate to advanced learners of English who want to improve their business communication skills in a global context.
- The book is published by Orient Blackswan, a leading publisher of academic and educational books in India.
