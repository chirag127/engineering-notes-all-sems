### 1. Group Discussion: Practical based on Accurate and Current Grammatical Patterns.

- A group discussion is a type of oral communication activity where a small number of participants exchange their views on a given topic or issue.
- The purpose of a group discussion is to enhance the participants' critical thinking, listening, speaking, and interpersonal skills, as well as to explore different perspectives and opinions on the topic or issue.
- A group discussion can be formal or informal, depending on the context and the objective of the activity. A formal group discussion is usually moderated by a leader or a facilitator, who introduces the topic, sets the rules and the time limit, and guides the discussion. An informal group discussion is more spontaneous and flexible, where the participants can freely initiate and change the topic, and there is no fixed role for the leader or the facilitator.
- A group discussion can be evaluated based on various criteria, such as the relevance, clarity, coherence, and logic of the arguments, the use of evidence and examples, the respect for other participants' views, the contribution to the discussion, and the ability to reach a consensus or a conclusion.
- A practical based on accurate and current grammatical patterns is a type of group discussion where the participants are expected to use correct and appropriate grammar in their speech, according to the standard rules and conventions of the language. The participants should also be aware of the recent changes and developments in the grammatical patterns of the language, and use them accordingly in their speech.
- A practical based on accurate and current grammatical patterns can help the participants to improve their grammatical competence, accuracy, and fluency in the language, as well as to demonstrate their knowledge and understanding of the language system. It can also help them to avoid common grammatical errors and mistakes, and to communicate more effectively and confidently in the language.
- Some examples of topics for a practical based on accurate and current grammatical patterns are:

  - The use of singular they as a gender-neutral pronoun in English.
  - The difference between the subjunctive and the indicative mood in Spanish.
  - The rules and exceptions for the formation of the plural nouns in German.
  - The influence of social media and texting on the grammar of French.
  - The comparison of the passive voice and the active voice in Chinese.
