# Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists, Sangeeta Sharma et.al. PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd, 2011, New Delhi.

- This is a book that covers various aspects of technical communication that students of engineering and science should be familiar with.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part A deals with the planning, designing and drafting of documents for different situations and applications, such as technical reports, business letters, advertisements, resumes, etc. Part B focuses on the oral communication skills, such as listening, speaking, presenting, interviewing, negotiating, etc.
- The book aims to help students develop their communication skills in both written and spoken modes, as well as their interpersonal and intercultural skills, which are essential for professional success.
- The book also provides examples, exercises, case studies, and self-assessment tests to enhance the learning outcomes of the students.
- Some of the topics covered in the book are:

  - Technical report writing: This chapter explains the purpose, types, structure, and style of technical reports, and provides guidelines for writing effective reports.
  - Communication breakdown: This chapter discusses the causes, effects, and remedies of communication breakdown, and how to avoid or overcome communication barriers.
  - Listening: This chapter explores the importance, types, and stages of listening, and offers tips for improving listening skills.
  - Business letters: This chapter describes the format, layout, and language of business letters, and illustrates how to write different kinds of letters, such as inquiry, order, complaint, adjustment, etc.
  - Advertising: This chapter analyzes the features, functions, and techniques of advertising, and shows how to design and evaluate advertisements.
  - Communicating without words: This chapter examines the role and impact of non-verbal communication, such as body language, facial expressions, gestures, eye contact, etc.
  - Group discussions: This chapter explains the purpose, process, and skills of group discussions, and provides tips for participating and leading group discussions effectively.

- The book is suitable for undergraduate students of engineering and science, as well as for professionals who want to improve their communication skills.
