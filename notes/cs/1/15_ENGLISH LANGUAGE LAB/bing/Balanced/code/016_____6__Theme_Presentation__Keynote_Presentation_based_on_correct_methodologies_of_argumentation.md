### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation is a type of speech that aims to introduce, explain, or persuade the audience on a specific topic or theme.
- A theme presentation or a keynote presentation should be based on correct methodologies of argumentation, which are the principles and techniques of reasoning and presenting evidence to support a claim or a position.
- Some of the correct methodologies of argumentation are:

  - **Identify the purpose and the audience** of the presentation. The purpose can be informative, persuasive, or motivational. The audience can be general, specific, or mixed. The purpose and the audience will determine the tone, style, and content of the presentation.
  - **Choose a clear and relevant topic** that matches the purpose and the audience. The topic should be interesting, significant, and manageable. It should also be narrowed down to a specific aspect or angle that can be covered in the allotted time.
  - **Conduct thorough and credible research** on the topic. The research should include both primary and secondary sources, such as interviews, surveys, experiments, books, articles, reports, etc. The research should provide sufficient and reliable evidence to support the main points and arguments of the presentation.
  - **Organize the presentation** into a clear and logical structure. The presentation should have an introduction, a body, and a conclusion. The introduction should capture the attention of the audience, establish the credibility of the speaker, and state the thesis or the main idea of the presentation. The body should develop the main points and arguments of the presentation, using evidence, examples, statistics, facts, etc. The conclusion should summarize the main points and arguments, restate the thesis, and provide a call to action or a recommendation for the audience.
  - **Use appropriate language and style** for the presentation. The language and style should be clear, concise, and coherent. The language and style should also be respectful, polite, and professional. The language and style should avoid jargon, slang, bias, fallacies, and errors.
  - **Use effective delivery skills** for the presentation. The delivery skills include verbal and non-verbal communication, such as voice, eye contact, gestures, posture, facial expressions, etc. The delivery skills should enhance the message and the impression of the presentation. The delivery skills should also adapt to the feedback and the reactions of the audience.
