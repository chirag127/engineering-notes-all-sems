### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

- This book is designed to help anyone who wants to improve their skills in communication, both oral and written.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, such as:
  - The nature and process of communication
  - The barriers and aids to communication
  - The types and modes of communication
  - The principles and strategies of effective communication
  - The skills and techniques of listening, speaking, reading, and writing
  - The communication in different contexts and situations, such as academic, professional, social, and intercultural
- The book provides examples, exercises, and activities to help the reader practice and apply the concepts and skills learned.
- The book also includes a glossary of key terms and a bibliography of useful references.
