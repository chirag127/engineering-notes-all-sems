### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

This is a comprehensive book on English grammar, composition and usage for students of different levels. It covers the following topics:

- Parts of speech: nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions and interjections.
- Sentence structure: subject, predicate, object, complement, modifier, clause and phrase.
- Types of sentences: simple, compound, complex and compound-complex sentences.
- Tenses: present, past and future tenses, and their forms and uses.
- Voice: active and passive voice, and their rules and transformations.
- Mood: indicative, imperative, subjunctive and conditional moods, and their forms and functions.
- Modals: modal auxiliaries, their meanings and uses.
- Non-finite verbs: infinitives, gerunds and participles, and their forms and functions.
- Concord: subject-verb agreement, and their rules and exceptions.
- Direct and indirect speech: rules and methods of changing direct speech into indirect speech, and vice versa.
- Punctuation: comma, semicolon, colon, full stop, question mark, exclamation mark, apostrophe, quotation marks, hyphen, dash and brackets, and their uses and rules.
- Spelling: common spelling rules and patterns, and their exceptions.
- Vocabulary: synonyms, antonyms, homonyms, homophones, prefixes, suffixes, root words, word formation, idioms and phrases.
- Composition: paragraph writing, essay writing, letter writing, report writing, précis writing, dialogue writing and story writing.
- Usage: common errors in English, and their correction and prevention.
