### Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press

- Cambridge English Empower is a general adult course that combines course content from Cambridge University Press with validated assessment from the experts at Cambridge English Language Assessment .
- It is a complete solution for effective learning and teaching of English for adult and young adult learners .
- It consists of six levels: Starter, Elementary, Pre-intermediate, Intermediate, Upper Intermediate, and Advanced .
- Each level has a Student's Book, a Workbook, a Teacher's Book, a Presentation Plus software, and an Online Assessment and Practice platform .
- The Student's Book provides learners with an immediate sense of purpose and clear learning objectives, as well as engaging topics and tasks, and regular opportunities for self-assessment and reflection  .
- The Workbook offers additional practice for the language and skills covered in the Student's Book, as well as extra reading, writing, and listening activities .
- The Teacher's Book includes detailed teaching notes, answer keys, extra activities, photocopiable resources, and assessment options .
- The Presentation Plus software allows teachers to present and interact with the Student's Book, Workbook, and Class Audio, as well as access the Teacher's Book and Assessment package .
- The Online Assessment and Practice platform provides learners with access to interactive grammar and vocabulary activities, video and audio resources, and progress tests, as well as teachers with access to placement tests, unit tests, and end-of-level tests .
