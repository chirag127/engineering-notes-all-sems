## What is professional vocabulary?

- Professional vocabulary is the set of words and phrases that are used in specific fields or domains of work, such as medicine, law, engineering, business, etc.
- Professional vocabulary is also known as technical vocabulary, domain-specific vocabulary, or jargon.
- Professional vocabulary helps to communicate precisely and efficiently with other experts or professionals in the same field or domain.
- Professional vocabulary also helps to demonstrate one's knowledge, credibility, and competence in a given field or domain.

## Why is professional vocabulary important for students?

- Students who want to pursue a career in a specific field or domain need to learn and master the professional vocabulary of that field or domain.
- Students who learn and master the professional vocabulary of their chosen field or domain can create a substantial base for their future success and growth.

  - Enhance their comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking skills in their field or domain.
  - Improve their academic performance and grades in their courses and exams.

  - Expand their network and opportunities with other professionals and experts in their field or domain.
