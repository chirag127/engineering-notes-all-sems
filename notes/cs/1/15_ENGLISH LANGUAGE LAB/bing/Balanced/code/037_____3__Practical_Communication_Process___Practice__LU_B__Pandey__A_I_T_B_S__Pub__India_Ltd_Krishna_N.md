### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013.

This is a book that covers various aspects of technical communication, such as:

- The nature, process, and principles of communication.
- The types, modes, and barriers of communication.
- The skills and strategies of effective communication, such as listening, speaking, reading, writing, and presenting.
- The formats and styles of various technical documents, such as letters, reports, proposals, manuals, and resumes.
- The use of language, grammar, punctuation, and vocabulary in technical communication.
- The role of communication in professional and ethical contexts, such as teamwork, intercultural communication, and conflict resolution.

Some of the main features of this book are:

- It provides clear and concise explanations of the concepts and techniques of technical communication.
- It includes numerous examples, illustrations, exercises, and activities to enhance the learning and practice of communication skills.
- It incorporates the latest trends and developments in the field of technical communication, such as digital media, social media, and online communication.
- It is suitable for students and professionals of various disciplines, such as engineering, science, management, and humanities.
