#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills. Interactive and Communicative Practical with emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication, based on International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.)

- To improve language skills, students should engage in a variety of activities and exercises that focus on employability skills.
- Interactive and communicative practicals can help students develop their oral presentation and spoken communication skills.
- Using the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) can help students improve their pronunciation and understanding of the sounds of the language.
- By practicing the language in a variety of contexts, students can develop their language skills in a more well-rounded manner.
- Employability skills, such as teamwork, problem-solving, and communication, can be developed through language practice.
- Oral presentation and spoken communication are important skills for many jobs, and practicing these skills can help students prepare for the workforce.
