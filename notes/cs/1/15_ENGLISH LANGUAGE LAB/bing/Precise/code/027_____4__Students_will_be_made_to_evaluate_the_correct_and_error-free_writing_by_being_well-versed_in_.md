### 4. Students will be made to evaluate the correct and error-free writing by being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivate relevant technical style of communication &presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- To write correctly and without errors, students must have a strong understanding of the rules of English grammar.
- This includes knowledge of sentence structure, punctuation, and spelling.
- Students must also be able to apply this knowledge in a technical style of communication, which is appropriate for the workplace and academic settings.
- This involves using clear and concise language, avoiding jargon, and presenting information in a logical and organized manner.
- By mastering these skills, students will be able to effectively communicate their ideas and present their work in a professional and polished manner.
