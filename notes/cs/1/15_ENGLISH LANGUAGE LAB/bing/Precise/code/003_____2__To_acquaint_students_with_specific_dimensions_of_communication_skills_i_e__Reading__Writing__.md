### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- **Reading:** Reading is the process of interpreting written language and understanding its meaning. It is an essential skill for acquiring knowledge and information. To improve reading skills, students can practice reading texts of varying difficulty, engage in reading comprehension exercises, and expand their vocabulary.

- **Writing:** Writing is the process of expressing thoughts, ideas, and information through written language. It is an important skill for effective communication. To improve writing skills, students can practice writing different types of texts, focus on grammar and spelling, and learn to organize their thoughts and ideas effectively.

- **Listening:** Listening is the process of receiving, interpreting, and understanding spoken language. It is a crucial skill for effective communication and building relationships. To improve listening skills, students can practice active listening, pay attention to nonverbal cues, and engage in exercises that improve concentration and focus.

- **Thinking:** Thinking is the process of considering, analyzing, and evaluating information to form opinions, make decisions, and solve problems. It is an essential skill for critical thinking and problem-solving. To improve thinking skills, students can engage in exercises that challenge their reasoning and logic, practice problem-solving, and learn to think critically and creatively.

- **Speaking:** Speaking is the process of expressing thoughts, ideas, and information through spoken language. It is an important skill for effective communication and building relationships. To improve speaking skills, students can practice speaking in front of others, focus on pronunciation and intonation, and learn to organize their thoughts and ideas effectively.
