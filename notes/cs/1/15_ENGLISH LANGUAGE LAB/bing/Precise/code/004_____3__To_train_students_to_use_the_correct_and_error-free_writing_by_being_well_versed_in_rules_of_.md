### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

- English grammar is a set of rules that govern the structure of sentences, phrases, and words in the English language.
- To write correctly and without errors, students must have a good understanding of these rules.
- Grammar rules include the proper use of punctuation, capitalization, verb tense, subject-verb agreement, and sentence structure.
- Students can improve their grammar skills by studying grammar rules, practicing writing, and receiving feedback from teachers or peers.
- There are many resources available to help students improve their grammar, including textbooks, online resources, and grammar software.
- By being well-versed in the rules of English grammar, students can improve their writing skills and communicate more effectively.
