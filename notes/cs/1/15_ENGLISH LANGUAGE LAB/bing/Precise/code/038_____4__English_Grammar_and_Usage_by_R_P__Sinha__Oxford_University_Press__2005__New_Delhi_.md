### 4. English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi.

- "English Grammar and Usage" is a book written by R.P. Sinha and published by Oxford University Press in 2005.
- The book is intended to help readers improve their understanding of the English language and its grammar.
- It covers a wide range of topics, including parts of speech, sentence structure, punctuation, and common usage errors.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills, whether for academic, professional, or personal purposes.
- The book is widely available and can be purchased from major booksellers.
