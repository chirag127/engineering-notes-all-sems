### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

A theme presentation or keynote presentation is a type of speech that is delivered to set the underlying tone and summarize the core message of an event or conference. The presentation is usually delivered by a subject matter expert or a renowned speaker. The success of a theme presentation or keynote presentation depends on the speaker's ability to engage the audience and convey the message effectively. One of the ways to achieve this is by using correct methodologies of argumentation. Here are some points to consider while preparing and delivering a theme presentation or keynote presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation:

1. **Understand the audience**: It is important to understand the audience's background, interests, and expectations to tailor the presentation accordingly. This will help in making the presentation more engaging and relevant to the audience.

2. **Define the purpose**: Clearly define the purpose of the presentation and the message that needs to be conveyed. This will help in keeping the presentation focused and on track.

3. **Use evidence to support arguments**: Use relevant and credible evidence to support the arguments made in the presentation. This will help in making the presentation more convincing and persuasive.

4. **Use logical reasoning**: Use logical reasoning to present the arguments in a structured and coherent manner. This will help in making the presentation more understandable and easy to follow.

5. **Address counterarguments**: Address potential counterarguments and provide evidence to refute them. This will help in making the presentation more robust and comprehensive.

6. **Use rhetorical devices**: Use rhetorical devices such as repetition, analogy, and storytelling to make the presentation more engaging and memorable.

7. **Practice delivery**: Practice the delivery of the presentation to improve the pacing, tone, and body language. This will help in making the presentation more effective and impactful.

In summary, a theme presentation or keynote presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation involves understanding the audience, defining the purpose, using evidence to support arguments, using logical reasoning, addressing counterarguments, using rhetorical devices, and practicing delivery. By following these points, a speaker can deliver a successful theme presentation or keynote presentation that engages the audience and effectively conveys the message.
