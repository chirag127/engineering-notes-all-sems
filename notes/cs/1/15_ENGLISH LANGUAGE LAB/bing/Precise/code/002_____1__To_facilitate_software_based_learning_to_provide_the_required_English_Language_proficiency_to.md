### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

- Software-based learning can provide a flexible and interactive learning experience for students to improve their English language proficiency.
- With the use of technology, students can access a wide range of multimedia resources, such as videos, audio recordings, and interactive exercises, to enhance their language skills.
- Software-based learning can also provide personalized learning experiences, allowing students to learn at their own pace and focus on areas where they need improvement.
- The use of software can also facilitate communication and collaboration among students, providing opportunities for peer learning and feedback.
- In addition, software-based learning can provide teachers with tools to monitor student progress and provide targeted feedback and support.
- Overall, the use of software can provide a comprehensive and engaging learning experience for students to improve their English language proficiency.
