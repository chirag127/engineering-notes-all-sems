## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

1. **Part A: Language Lab**
    - This part focuses on improving the language skills of the students.
    - It includes exercises and activities to enhance grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation.
    - Students will practice speaking, listening, reading, and writing in the target language.

2. **Part B: Soft Skills Lab**
    - This part focuses on developing the soft skills of the students.
    - It includes training in areas such as interpersonal communication, teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving.
    - Students will participate in group discussions, role-plays, and presentations to improve their soft skills.

Both parts of the Professional Communication Lab are essential for students to develop effective communication skills in their personal and professional lives.
