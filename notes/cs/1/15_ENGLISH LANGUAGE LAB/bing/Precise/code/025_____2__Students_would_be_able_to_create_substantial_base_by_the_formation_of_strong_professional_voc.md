### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

- The development of a strong professional vocabulary is essential for students to succeed in their academic and professional careers.
- By building a substantial base of vocabulary, students can improve their comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking skills.
- This can be achieved through various modes such as reading, writing, and speaking exercises, as well as through the use of vocabulary-building tools and resources.
- The application of a strong professional vocabulary can be beneficial across different platforms, including academic, professional, and social settings.
- By mastering a strong professional vocabulary, students can effectively communicate their ideas and thoughts, and can also better understand the ideas and thoughts of others.
- This can lead to improved academic performance, as well as increased opportunities for professional advancement and success.
