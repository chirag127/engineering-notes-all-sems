#### Software-based self-guided learning for English language proficiency

- Software-based self-guided learning can provide students with the required English language proficiency for employability and career readiness.
- The software should align with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) and deliver a CEFR level of B2 upon completion.
- CEFR is a widely recognized standard for describing language proficiency and is used by employers and educational institutions to assess the language skills of individuals.
- A CEFR level of B2 indicates that the individual can understand the main ideas of complex text on both concrete and abstract topics, including technical discussions in their field of specialization.
- The individual can interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers quite possible without strain for either party.
- The individual can produce clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects and explain a viewpoint on a topical issue giving the advantages and disadvantages of various options.
- Software-based self-guided learning allows students to learn at their own pace and on their own schedule, making it a flexible and convenient option for improving their English language proficiency.
- The software can provide interactive lessons, exercises, and assessments to help students improve their reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills.
- The software can also provide feedback and track progress, allowing students to see their improvement over time and identify areas where they need to focus their efforts.
- By providing a structured and comprehensive program aligned with the CEFR, software-based self-guided learning can effectively prepare students for success in their careers and in the global marketplace.
