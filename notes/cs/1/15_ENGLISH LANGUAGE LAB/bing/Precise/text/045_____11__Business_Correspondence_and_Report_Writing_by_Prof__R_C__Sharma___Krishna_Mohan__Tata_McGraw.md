### 11. Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan, Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd., 2001, New Delhi.

- Business correspondence refers to the exchange of information in a written format for the process of business activities.
- Business correspondence can take place between organizations, within organizations or between the customers and the organization.
- The purpose of business correspondence is to convey information, persuade the reader to take a particular action, or to establish or maintain a relationship.
- Report writing is the process of researching, organizing, and presenting information in a written format.
- Reports are used to convey information, analyze data, and make recommendations.
- Effective report writing requires careful planning, research, analysis, and clear and concise writing.
- The book "Business Correspondence and Report Writing" by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan provides a comprehensive guide to effective business communication and report writing.
- The book covers topics such as the principles of effective business correspondence, types of business letters, and the process of report writing.
- The book is published by Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd. and was released in 2001 in New Delhi.
