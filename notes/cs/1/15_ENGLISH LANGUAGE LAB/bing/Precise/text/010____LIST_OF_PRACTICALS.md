## LIST OF PRACTICALS

1. Identification of different types of plant and animal tissues.
2. Determination of the melting point of an organic compound.
3. Determination of the boiling point of an organic compound.
4. Determination of the refractive index of a liquid using a refractometer.
5. Preparation of a standard solution of sodium carbonate.
6. Determination of the strength of a given solution of hydrochloric acid by titrating it against the standard solution of sodium carbonate.
7. Study of the characteristics of Spirogyra, Agaricus, Moss, Fern, Pinus and an Angiospermic plant.
8. Study of the external features of the root, stem, leaf and flower of monocotyledonous and dicotyledonous plants.
9. Study of the osmosis by potato osmometer.
10. Study of plasmolysis in epidermal peels of Rhoeo leaves.
