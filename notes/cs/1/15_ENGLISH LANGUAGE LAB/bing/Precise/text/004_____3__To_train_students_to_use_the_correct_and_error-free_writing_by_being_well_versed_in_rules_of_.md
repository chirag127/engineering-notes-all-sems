### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

- English grammar is a set of rules that govern the structure of sentences and the use of words in the English language.
- To write correctly and without errors, it is important to have a good understanding of these rules.
- Students can improve their writing skills by studying the rules of English grammar and practicing their application in writing.
- Some common areas of English grammar that students may need to focus on include verb tense, subject-verb agreement, pronoun usage, and punctuation.
- There are many resources available to help students learn and practice English grammar, including textbooks, online tutorials, and grammar exercises.
- By being well-versed in the rules of English grammar, students can improve the clarity and effectiveness of their writing, and avoid common errors that can detract from the quality of their work.
