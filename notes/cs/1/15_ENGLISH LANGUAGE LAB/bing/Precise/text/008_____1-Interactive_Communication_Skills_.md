### 1-Interactive Communication Skills:

Interactive communication skills refer to the ability to effectively communicate and interact with others. These skills are essential for building and maintaining relationships, both personal and professional. Some key components of interactive communication skills include:

1. **Active Listening**: This involves paying attention to what the other person is saying, and showing that you are listening by providing feedback, such as nodding or asking questions.

2. **Verbal Communication**: This includes the ability to clearly and effectively convey information and ideas through spoken words.

3. **Non-Verbal Communication**: This includes the use of body language, facial expressions, and gestures to convey information and emotions.

4. **Empathy**: This involves understanding and sharing the feelings of others, and being able to respond in a supportive and appropriate manner.

5. **Conflict Resolution**: This involves the ability to effectively resolve conflicts and disagreements in a calm and respectful manner.

6. **Assertiveness**: This involves the ability to confidently and respectfully express your thoughts, feelings, and needs, while also being able to listen to and consider the thoughts, feelings, and needs of others.

7. **Negotiation**: This involves the ability to reach mutually beneficial agreements through discussion and compromise.

Improving your interactive communication skills can help you build stronger relationships, work more effectively with others, and achieve greater success in your personal and professional life.
