### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

1. **Preparation**: Prepare your speech or presentation thoroughly. Know your material inside and out, and anticipate potential questions or interjections from your audience.
2. **Confidence**: Speak with confidence and conviction. This will help you to maintain control of the situation and to respond effectively to interjections or quizzes.
3. **Active Listening**: Listen carefully to the interjection or quiz, and try to understand the underlying concern or question. This will help you to respond in a way that addresses the issue effectively.
4. **Stay Calm**: Remain calm and composed, even if the interjection or quiz is challenging or confrontational. Take a deep breath, and respond in a measured and respectful manner.
5. **Acknowledge and Address**: Acknowledge the interjection or quiz, and address it directly. If you don't know the answer, be honest and say so. If you need more time to respond, ask for it.
6. **Stay on Topic**: Keep your response focused and on-topic. Avoid going off on tangents or getting sidetracked by unrelated issues.
7. **Be Respectful**: Always be respectful and professional in your response, even if the interjection or quiz is confrontational or challenging. Remember that you are representing yourself and your organization, and your response should reflect that.

These are some key points to keep in mind when delivering a speech or presentation and defending against interjections or quizzes. By following these guidelines, you can effectively manage these situations and maintain control of the conversation.
