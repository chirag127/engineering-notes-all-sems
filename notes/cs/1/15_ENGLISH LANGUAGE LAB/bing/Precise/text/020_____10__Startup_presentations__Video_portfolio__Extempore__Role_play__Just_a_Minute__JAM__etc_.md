### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

- **Startup presentations** are presentations given by entrepreneurs to pitch their business ideas to potential investors, customers, or partners. These presentations typically include information about the business concept, target market, financial projections, and marketing strategies.

- A **video portfolio** is a collection of videos that showcase an individual's skills, talents, and accomplishments. Video portfolios are often used by artists, performers, and other creative professionals to demonstrate their abilities to potential clients or employers.

- **Extempore** is a type of public speaking where the speaker is given little or no time to prepare their speech. Extempore speeches are often used to test a speaker's ability to think on their feet and communicate effectively under pressure.

- **Role play** is a type of simulation where participants take on the roles of different characters and act out scenarios. Role play is often used in training and education to help individuals develop skills such as communication, problem-solving, and empathy.

- **Just a Minute (JAM)** is a game where participants are given a topic and must speak about it for one minute without hesitation, repetition, or deviation. JAM is often used as a fun way to improve public speaking skills and quick thinking.

These are just a few examples of the many different types of activities that can be used to develop and showcase communication and presentation skills. Each activity has its own unique benefits and can be tailored to meet the needs of different individuals and situations.
