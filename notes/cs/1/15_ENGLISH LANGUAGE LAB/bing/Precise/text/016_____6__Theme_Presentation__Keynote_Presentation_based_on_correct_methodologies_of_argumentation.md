### 6. Theme Presentation/ Keynote Presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation

A theme presentation or keynote presentation is a type of speech that is delivered to set the underlying tone and summarize the core message of an event or conference. The presentation is usually delivered by a subject matter expert or a well-known figure in the field. The success of a theme presentation or keynote presentation depends on the speaker's ability to engage the audience and convey the message effectively.

Here are some key points to consider when preparing and delivering a theme presentation or keynote presentation based on correct methodologies of argumentation:

1. **Understand the audience:** Before preparing the presentation, it is important to understand the audience and their expectations. This will help you tailor the content and delivery style to meet their needs.

2. **Define the purpose:** Clearly define the purpose of the presentation and the key message that you want to convey. This will help you stay focused and ensure that the presentation is coherent and well-structured.

3. **Use evidence to support your arguments:** Use facts, statistics, and other forms of evidence to support your arguments. This will make your presentation more convincing and help the audience understand the reasoning behind your arguments.

4. **Use clear and concise language:** Use clear and concise language to convey your message. Avoid using jargon or technical terms that the audience may not understand.

5. **Engage the audience:** Use techniques such as storytelling, humor, and visual aids to engage the audience and keep them interested in the presentation.

6. **Practice:** Practice delivering the presentation to improve your delivery and build confidence. This will also help you identify any areas that need improvement.

By following these key points, you can prepare and deliver a theme presentation or keynote presentation that is engaging, informative, and based on correct methodologies of argumentation.
