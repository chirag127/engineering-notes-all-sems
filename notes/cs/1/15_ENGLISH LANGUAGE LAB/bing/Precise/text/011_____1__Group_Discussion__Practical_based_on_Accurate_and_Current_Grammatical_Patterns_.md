### 1. Group Discussion: Practical based on Accurate and Current Grammatical Patterns.

Group discussion is a practical activity that involves a group of people discussing a topic or issue. It is an effective way to improve communication skills, critical thinking, and teamwork. In order to have a successful group discussion, it is important to use accurate and current grammatical patterns. Here are some points to consider:

- **Use proper grammar**: It is important to use proper grammar during a group discussion to effectively communicate your ideas and thoughts. This includes using correct verb tenses, subject-verb agreement, and sentence structure.

- **Stay current with language trends**: Language is constantly evolving, and it is important to stay up-to-date with current language trends and usage. This includes being aware of new words and phrases, as well as changes in the way language is used.

- **Practice active listening**: Active listening is an important skill in group discussions. It involves paying attention to what others are saying, asking questions, and providing feedback. This helps to ensure that everyone's ideas are heard and considered.

- **Be respectful**: It is important to be respectful during a group discussion. This includes avoiding interrupting others, using polite language, and being open to different opinions and ideas.

- **Stay on topic**: It is important to stay on topic during a group discussion. This helps to keep the discussion focused and productive. If the discussion starts to stray from the topic, it is important to gently steer it back on track.

Overall, using accurate and current grammatical patterns is an important part of having a successful group discussion. By following these points, you can improve your communication skills and have more productive discussions.
