### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- **Reading**: Students will learn to read effectively and efficiently, with the goal of improving comprehension and retention of information.
- **Writing**: Students will learn to write clearly and concisely, with the goal of effectively communicating their ideas and thoughts.
- **Listening**: Students will learn to listen actively and attentively, with the goal of understanding and retaining information.
- **Thinking**: Students will learn to think critically and creatively, with the goal of analyzing information and generating new ideas.
- **Speaking**: Students will learn to speak confidently and persuasively, with the goal of effectively communicating their ideas and thoughts.

By mastering these five dimensions of communication skills, students will be better equipped to succeed in their academic and professional pursuits.
