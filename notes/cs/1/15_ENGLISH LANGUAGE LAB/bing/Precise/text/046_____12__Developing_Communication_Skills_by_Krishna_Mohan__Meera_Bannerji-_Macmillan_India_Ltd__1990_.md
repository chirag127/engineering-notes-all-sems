### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi

- "Developing Communication Skills" is a book written by Krishna Mohan and Meera Banerjee, published by Macmillan India Ltd. in 1990, Delhi.
- The book is designed for anyone who wants to improve their communication skills.
- It guides the reader in their choice of words, body language, and confidence.
- The book aims to improve a person's oral as well as written communication.
- It is primarily designed as a text for advanced level courses in communication skills.
- However, it would prove equally useful as an effective aid to professionals in performing their day-to-day communicative tasks.
- The book has received positive reviews and ratings, with an average rating of 3.67 on Goodreads.
