## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

1. **Part 1: Theory** - This part of the lab will focus on the theoretical aspects of professional communication. It will cover topics such as effective communication, verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, and presentation skills.

2. **Part 2: Practical** - This part of the lab will focus on the practical application of the concepts learned in the theory part. Students will participate in activities such as role-playing, group discussions, and presentations to practice and improve their professional communication skills.

Both parts of the lab are essential for developing effective professional communication skills. The theory part provides the foundation and understanding of the concepts, while the practical part allows for the application and practice of these concepts in real-life scenarios.
