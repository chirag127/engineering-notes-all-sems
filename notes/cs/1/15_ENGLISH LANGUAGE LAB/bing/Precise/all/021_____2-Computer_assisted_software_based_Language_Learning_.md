### 2-Computer assisted software based Language Learning:

1. Computer-assisted language learning (CALL) refers to the use of computer software to support language learning.
2. CALL software can provide interactive and multimedia content to enhance the language learning experience.
3. CALL software can provide immediate feedback to learners, allowing them to identify and correct their mistakes.
4. CALL software can be used to supplement traditional classroom instruction or as a standalone tool for self-study.
5. CALL software can provide learners with access to authentic language materials, such as news articles, videos, and podcasts.
6. CALL software can be used to facilitate communication and collaboration among learners, allowing them to practice their language skills with others.
7. CALL software can be customized to meet the needs and learning styles of individual learners.
8. CALL software can provide learners with opportunities to practice their language skills in a safe and controlled environment.
9. CALL software can provide learners with access to a wide range of language learning resources, including grammar and vocabulary exercises, reading and listening comprehension activities, and writing and speaking practice.
10. CALL software can provide learners with a flexible and convenient way to learn a language, allowing them to study at their own pace and on their own schedule.
