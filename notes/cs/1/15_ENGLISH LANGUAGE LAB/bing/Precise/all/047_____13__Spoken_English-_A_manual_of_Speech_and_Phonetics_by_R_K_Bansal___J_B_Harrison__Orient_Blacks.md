### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- Spoken English: A Manual of Speech and Phonetics is a book for students who wish to improve their pronunciation of English and acquire the correct patterns of stress, rhythm and intonation.
- The book is published by Orient BlackSwan and was released in 2013.
- The authors of the book are R.K.Bansal and J.B.Harrison.
- The book is available in paperback format and comes with a CD.
