### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

- The development of a strong professional vocabulary is essential for students to effectively communicate in various professional settings.
- By building a substantial base of professional vocabulary, students can improve their comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking skills.
- This can be achieved through various modes of learning, such as reading professional literature, practicing writing and speaking, and engaging in discussions with peers and mentors.
- The application of professional vocabulary can take place on different platforms, such as in academic papers, presentations, and professional correspondence.
- By mastering professional vocabulary, students can enhance their ability to convey their ideas and thoughts in a clear and concise manner, which can greatly benefit their academic and professional pursuits.
