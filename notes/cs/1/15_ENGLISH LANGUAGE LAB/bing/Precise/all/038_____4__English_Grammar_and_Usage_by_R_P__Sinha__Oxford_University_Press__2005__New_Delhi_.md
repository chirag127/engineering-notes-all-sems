# English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi

- "English Grammar and Usage" is a book written by R.P. Sinha and published by Oxford University Press in 2005 in New Delhi.
- The book covers various aspects of English grammar and usage, providing readers with a comprehensive understanding of the subject.
- The book is divided into chapters, each focusing on a specific topic related to English grammar and usage.
- The chapters are well-organized and the content is presented in a clear and concise manner, making it easy for readers to understand and follow.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, and anyone interested in improving their knowledge of English grammar and usage.
- Overall, "English Grammar and Usage" by R.P. Sinha is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their understanding of the English language.
