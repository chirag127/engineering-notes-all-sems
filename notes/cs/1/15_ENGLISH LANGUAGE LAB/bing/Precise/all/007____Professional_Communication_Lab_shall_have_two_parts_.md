## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

1. **Part A: Language Lab**
    - This part focuses on improving the language skills of the students.
    - It includes exercises and activities to enhance reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills.
    - The aim is to help students communicate effectively in professional settings.

2. **Part B: Soft Skills Lab**
    - This part focuses on developing the soft skills of the students.
    - It includes training in areas such as interpersonal communication, teamwork, leadership, and problem-solving.
    - The aim is to prepare students for the professional world by equipping them with the necessary soft skills.

Both parts of the Professional Communication Lab are essential for students to develop effective communication skills and succeed in their professional lives.
