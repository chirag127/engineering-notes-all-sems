### 3. Practical Communication Process & Practice, LU.B. Pandey: A.I.T.B.S. Pub. India Ltd Krishna Nagar, Delhi, 2013

Practical Communication Process & Practice is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in Krishna Nagar, Delhi in 2013 . The book is a guide to effective communication and covers various aspects of the communication process. It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills.

Some key points from the book include:
- Communication is a practice that can be improved with effort and practice.
- Effective communication involves understanding the theory-practice relationship.
- The communication process involves evaluating the response of the listener and collaborating to agree on a plan.
- Empathy is an important aspect of effective communication and involves identifying and responding to the emotions of the listener.
