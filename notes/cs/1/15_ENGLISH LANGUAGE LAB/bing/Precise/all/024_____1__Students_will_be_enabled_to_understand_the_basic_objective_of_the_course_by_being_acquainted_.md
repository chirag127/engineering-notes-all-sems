# 1. Understanding the Basic Objective of the Course

The course aims to acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills, including:

- **Reading:** Students will learn how to effectively read and comprehend written material, including books, articles, and other texts.

- **Writing:** Students will learn how to effectively express their thoughts and ideas in writing, including essays, reports, and other written assignments.

- **Listening:** Students will learn how to actively listen and comprehend spoken language, including lectures, presentations, and conversations.

- **Thinking:** Students will learn how to critically analyze and evaluate information, including arguments, evidence, and opinions.

- **Speaking:** Students will learn how to effectively communicate their thoughts and ideas through spoken language, including presentations, discussions, and debates.

By mastering these dimensions of communication skills, students will be better equipped to succeed in their academic and professional pursuits.
