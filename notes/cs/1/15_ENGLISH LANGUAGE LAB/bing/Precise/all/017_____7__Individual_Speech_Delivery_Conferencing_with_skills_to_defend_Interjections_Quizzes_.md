# 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

1. **Preparation**: Prepare your speech or presentation thoroughly. Know your material inside and out, and anticipate potential questions or interjections from your audience.
2. **Confidence**: Speak with confidence and conviction. This will help you to maintain control of the conversation and to effectively defend against interjections or quizzes.
3. **Active Listening**: Listen actively to the interjections or quizzes from your audience. This will help you to understand their concerns and to address them effectively.
4. **Stay Calm**: Stay calm and composed, even if the interjections or quizzes are challenging or confrontational. This will help you to maintain control of the situation and to respond effectively.
5. **Acknowledge and Address**: Acknowledge the interjection or quiz, and address it directly. This will demonstrate that you are listening to your audience and that you are willing to engage with their concerns.
6. **Stay on Topic**: Stay focused on the topic at hand, and avoid getting sidetracked by unrelated issues. This will help you to maintain control of the conversation and to effectively address the interjections or quizzes.
7. **Provide Evidence**: Provide evidence to support your arguments or points. This will help you to effectively defend against interjections or quizzes and to persuade your audience.

These are some of the key skills that can help you to effectively deliver a speech or presentation and to defend against interjections or quizzes from your audience. By preparing thoroughly, speaking with confidence, listening actively, staying calm, acknowledging and addressing concerns, staying on topic, and providing evidence to support your arguments, you can effectively engage with your audience and deliver a successful speech or presentation.
