### 8. Argumentative Skills/Role Play Presentation with Stress and Intonation

1. **Argumentative skills** refer to the ability to present and defend a point of view using logical reasoning and evidence.
2. **Role play presentation** is a technique used to practice argumentative skills in a simulated situation.
3. **Stress and intonation** are important aspects of spoken language that can convey meaning and emotion.
4. In an argumentative role play presentation, the use of stress and intonation can help to emphasize important points and convey conviction.
5. To improve argumentative skills, it is important to practice constructing logical arguments, using evidence to support your points, and presenting your arguments in a clear and convincing manner.
6. Role play presentations can provide a safe and controlled environment to practice these skills.
7. Paying attention to stress and intonation can help to improve the effectiveness of your arguments and make your presentation more engaging.
8. Practice and feedback from others can help to improve your argumentative skills and your ability to use stress and intonation effectively in a role play presentation.
