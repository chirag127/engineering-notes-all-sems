# 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a book written by Ken Taylor and published by Orient Blackswan in 2011 in New Delhi.
- The book focuses on teaching English language skills for use in a business context.
- It covers topics such as business writing, presentations, meetings, negotiations, and socializing in a business setting.
- The book is designed for learners of English as a second language who are looking to improve their communication skills in a professional setting.
- It includes practical exercises and activities to help learners develop their language skills and apply them in real-life situations.
- The book is suitable for both self-study and classroom use, and is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their business English skills.
