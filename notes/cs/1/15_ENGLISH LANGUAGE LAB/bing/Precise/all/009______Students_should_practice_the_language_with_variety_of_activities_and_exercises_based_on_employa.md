#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills. Interactive and Communicative Practical with emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication, based on International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.)

1. **Variety of activities and exercises:** Students should engage in a range of activities and exercises that focus on different aspects of language learning, such as grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and fluency. This will help them develop a well-rounded set of skills that are essential for effective communication in the workplace.

2. **Employability skills:** Language learning should be geared towards developing employability skills, such as teamwork, problem-solving, and critical thinking. These skills are highly valued by employers and can help students succeed in their future careers.

3. **Interactive and Communicative Practical:** Language learning should be interactive and practical, with a focus on real-life communication. Students should have ample opportunities to practice speaking and listening in a variety of contexts, such as role-plays, debates, and presentations.

4. **Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication:** Oral presentation and spoken communication skills are essential for success in the workplace. Students should be encouraged to develop their public speaking skills and to practice delivering presentations in a clear, confident, and engaging manner.

5. **International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.):** The International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) is a system for transcribing the sounds of speech. Students should be introduced to the IPA and encouraged to use it as a tool for improving their pronunciation and understanding of the sounds of the language they are learning.

In summary, language learning should be practical, interactive, and focused on developing employability skills. Students should engage in a variety of activities and exercises, with an emphasis on oral presentation and spoken communication, and should be introduced to the International Phonetic Alphabet as a tool for improving their pronunciation.
