### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

- English Grammar & Composition is a book written by Wren & Martin and published by S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.
- The book is widely used as a reference for English grammar and composition.
- It covers various topics related to English grammar, including parts of speech, tenses, active and passive voice, direct and indirect speech, and more.
- The book also includes exercises and examples to help readers practice and improve their grammar skills.
- It is a popular choice for students preparing for exams, as well as for anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
- The book is available in various formats, including paperback and e-book.
- It is recommended for anyone looking to improve their English grammar and composition skills.
