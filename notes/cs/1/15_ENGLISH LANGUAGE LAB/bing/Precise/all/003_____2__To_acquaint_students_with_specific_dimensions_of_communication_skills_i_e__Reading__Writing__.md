# 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- **Reading:** Reading is the process of decoding written language and comprehending its meaning. It is an essential skill for acquiring knowledge and information. Students should be encouraged to read a variety of texts, including books, articles, and online sources, to improve their reading skills and expand their knowledge.

- **Writing:** Writing is the process of encoding thoughts and ideas into written language. It is an important skill for expressing oneself and communicating with others. Students should be taught the basics of grammar, spelling, and punctuation, as well as how to organize their thoughts and ideas into coherent and well-structured written pieces.

- **Listening:** Listening is the process of receiving and interpreting auditory information. It is an essential skill for effective communication and understanding others. Students should be taught to listen actively and attentively, and to ask questions and provide feedback to improve their understanding.

- **Thinking:** Thinking is the process of using one's cognitive abilities to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information. It is an important skill for problem-solving and decision-making. Students should be encouraged to think critically and creatively, and to apply their knowledge and skills to new situations.

- **Speaking:** Speaking is the process of using spoken language to communicate with others. It is an essential skill for expressing oneself and interacting with others. Students should be taught to speak clearly and confidently, and to use appropriate body language and tone of voice to convey their message effectively.

These five dimensions of communication skills are interrelated and essential for effective communication and success in personal, academic, and professional life. Students should be encouraged to develop and improve their skills in all of these areas.
