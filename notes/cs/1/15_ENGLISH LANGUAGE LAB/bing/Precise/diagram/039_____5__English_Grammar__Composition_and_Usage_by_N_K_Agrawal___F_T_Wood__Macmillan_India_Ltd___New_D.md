### 5. English Grammar, Composition and Usage by N.K.Agrawal & F.T.Wood, Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.

- "English Grammar, Composition and Usage" is a book written by N.K.Agrawal and F.T.Wood.
- The book is published by Macmillan India Ltd., New Delhi.
- It covers various topics related to English grammar, composition, and usage.
- The book is designed to help students improve their English language skills.
- It provides clear explanations of grammatical concepts and rules, along with examples and exercises to reinforce understanding.
- The book also covers topics such as sentence structure, punctuation, and vocabulary development.
- It is a valuable resource for students preparing for exams or for anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
