### 2. Conversational Skills for Interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics

Conversational skills are essential for successful interviews. Here are some tips for improving your conversational skills for interviews:

1. **Prepare**: Research the company and the position you are applying for. This will help you to answer questions confidently and ask relevant questions.

2. **Listen actively**: Pay attention to what the interviewer is saying and respond thoughtfully. This shows that you are interested and engaged in the conversation.

3. **Speak clearly**: Speak in a clear and concise manner. Avoid using filler words such as "um" or "like".

4. **Use appropriate body language**: Body language, or kinesics, is an important aspect of communication. Make eye contact, sit up straight, and use gestures to emphasize your points.

5. **Be confident**: Confidence is key in an interview. Believe in yourself and your abilities.

6. **Be polite**: Remember to say "please" and "thank you" and to address the interviewer by their title and last name.

7. **Be yourself**: Don't try to be someone you're not. Be genuine and authentic in your responses.

By following these tips, you can improve your conversational skills for interviews and increase your chances of success. Remember, practice makes perfect, so don't be afraid to practice your skills with friends or family before the interview. Good luck!🍀
