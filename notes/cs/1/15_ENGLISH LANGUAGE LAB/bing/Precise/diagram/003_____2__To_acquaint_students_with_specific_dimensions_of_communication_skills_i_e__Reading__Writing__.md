### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

Communication skills are essential for personal and professional success. They enable individuals to effectively convey their thoughts, ideas, and feelings to others. There are five specific dimensions of communication skills that students should be acquainted with:

1. **Reading:** Reading is the ability to understand and interpret written information. It involves recognizing words, understanding their meaning, and comprehending the ideas presented in a text.

2. **Writing:** Writing is the ability to express thoughts, ideas, and feelings in written form. It involves organizing information, using appropriate grammar and vocabulary, and effectively conveying a message to the reader.

3. **Listening:** Listening is the ability to accurately receive and interpret messages in the communication process. It involves paying attention to what is being said, understanding the speaker's perspective, and providing feedback.

4. **Thinking:** Thinking is the ability to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information. It involves using logic and reasoning to form opinions, make decisions, and solve problems.

5. **Speaking:** Speaking is the ability to effectively convey thoughts, ideas, and feelings through spoken words. It involves using appropriate tone, volume, and body language to engage the listener and convey a message.

By developing these five dimensions of communication skills, students can improve their ability to effectively communicate with others in personal and professional settings.
