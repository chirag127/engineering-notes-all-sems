### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes

1. **Preparation**: Prepare your speech or presentation thoroughly. Know your material inside and out, and anticipate potential questions or interjections.
2. **Confidence**: Speak with confidence and conviction. This will help you to maintain control of the conversation and to defend against interjections.
3. **Active Listening**: Listen actively to the interjections or questions. This will help you to understand the concerns or objections of the audience and to respond effectively.
4. **Stay Calm**: Stay calm and composed, even if the interjection or question is confrontational or challenging. This will help you to maintain control of the situation and to respond effectively.
5. **Address the Concern**: Address the concern or objection directly and respectfully. This will help to defuse the situation and to maintain a positive and productive dialogue.
6. **Stay on Topic**: Stay focused on the topic at hand, and avoid getting sidetracked by unrelated issues or concerns.
7. **Provide Evidence**: Provide evidence or examples to support your arguments or points. This will help to strengthen your position and to defend against interjections or objections.
