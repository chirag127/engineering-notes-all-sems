### 11. Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan, Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd., 2001, New Delhi.

- Business correspondence refers to the exchange of information in a written format for the process of business activities.
- Report writing is the process of researching, organizing, and presenting information in a structured format.
- Business correspondence and report writing are essential skills for effective communication in the business world.
- The book by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan provides a comprehensive guide to the principles and practices of business correspondence and report writing.
- It covers topics such as the structure and format of business letters, memos, and reports, as well as the use of appropriate language and tone.
- The book also includes practical examples and exercises to help readers develop their skills in business correspondence and report writing.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills in the business world.
