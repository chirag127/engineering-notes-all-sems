### 1. Group Discussion: Practical based on Accurate and Current Grammatical Patterns

Group discussion is a practical activity that involves a group of people discussing a topic or issue. It is an effective way to improve communication skills, critical thinking, and teamwork. In a group discussion, it is important to use accurate and current grammatical patterns to convey ideas clearly and effectively. Here are some points to consider when participating in a group discussion:

- Use proper sentence structure and word order to convey your ideas clearly.
- Use appropriate verb tenses to indicate the time frame of your ideas.
- Use correct subject-verb agreement to ensure that your sentences are grammatically correct.
- Use appropriate pronouns to refer to people or things in the discussion.
- Use correct punctuation to clarify the meaning of your sentences.
- Use appropriate vocabulary to convey your ideas effectively.
- Avoid using slang or informal language in a formal group discussion.
- Pay attention to the tone of your voice and body language to convey your ideas effectively.
- Listen actively to the ideas of others and respond respectfully.
- Stay on topic and avoid going off on tangents.

By following these guidelines, you can participate effectively in a group discussion and improve your communication skills. It is important to practice using accurate and current grammatical patterns to convey your ideas clearly and effectively.
