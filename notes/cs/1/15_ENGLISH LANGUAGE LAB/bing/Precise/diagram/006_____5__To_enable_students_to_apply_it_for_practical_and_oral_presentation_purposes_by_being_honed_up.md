### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

- Presentation skills and voice-dynamics are essential for students to effectively communicate their ideas and knowledge in practical and oral presentations.
- By being honed up in these skills, students can deliver their presentations with confidence, clarity, and impact.
- Presentation skills include the ability to organize and structure the content, use visual aids effectively, engage the audience, and handle questions and feedback.
- Voice-dynamics refers to the use of vocal variety, such as changes in pitch, volume, and pace, to convey emotions and emphasis.
- Students can improve their presentation skills and voice-dynamics through practice, feedback, and training.
- By mastering these skills, students can apply them in various settings, such as academic presentations, job interviews, and public speaking events.
