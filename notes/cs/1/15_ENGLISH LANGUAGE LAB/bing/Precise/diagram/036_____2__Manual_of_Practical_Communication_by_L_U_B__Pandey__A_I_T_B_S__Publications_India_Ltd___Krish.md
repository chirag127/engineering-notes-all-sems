### 2. Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- The "Manual of Practical Communication" is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey and published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in 2013.
- The book is a comprehensive guide to effective communication, covering various aspects of verbal and non-verbal communication.
- It provides practical tips and techniques for improving communication skills, including listening, speaking, writing, and presenting.
- The book is intended for students, professionals, and anyone who wants to improve their communication skills.
- It is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- The book is available for purchase from A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd. in Krishan Nagar, Delhi.
