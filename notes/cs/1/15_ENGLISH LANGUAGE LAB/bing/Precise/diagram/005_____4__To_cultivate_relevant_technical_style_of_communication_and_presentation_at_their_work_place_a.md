### 4. To cultivate relevant technical style of communication and presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

- Technical communication is a type of communication that conveys technical information to a specific audience.
- Technical communication can take many forms, including written, visual, and oral.
- Technical communication is important in the workplace and in academic settings because it allows individuals to effectively convey complex information to others.
- To cultivate a relevant technical style of communication, individuals should focus on clarity, concision, and accuracy.
- In the workplace, technical communication can be used to convey information about products, services, or processes to colleagues, customers, or other stakeholders.
- In academic settings, technical communication can be used to present research findings, explain complex concepts, or provide instructions for assignments or projects.
- To improve their technical communication skills, individuals can practice writing and presenting technical information, seek feedback from others, and study best practices in the field.
- Effective technical communication can help individuals succeed in their careers and in their academic pursuits. It is an important skill to develop and refine.
