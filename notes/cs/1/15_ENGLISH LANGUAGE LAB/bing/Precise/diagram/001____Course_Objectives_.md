## Course Objectives:

1. To provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the subject matter.
2. To develop critical thinking and analytical skills.
3. To enhance the ability to apply theoretical concepts to real-world situations.
4. To foster an appreciation for the subject and its relevance to various fields.
5. To prepare students for further study or professional development in the subject area.
6. To encourage active participation and engagement in the learning process.
7. To promote the development of effective communication skills.
8. To facilitate the acquisition of knowledge and skills necessary for success in the subject.
