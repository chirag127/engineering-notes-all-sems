### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to the pronunciation and intonation of spoken English for learners and teachers.
- The book covers the following topics:
  - The organs of speech and their functions
  - The phonetic symbols and transcription of English sounds
  - The classification and description of English vowels and consonants
  - The features of stress, rhythm, and intonation in English speech
  - The common errors and difficulties in the pronunciation of English by speakers of different languages
  - The drills and exercises for improving the accuracy and fluency of spoken English
- The book also provides audio recordings of the examples and exercises on a CD-ROM, as well as a glossary of phonetic terms and a list of references.
- The book is suitable for students of English language and literature, linguistics, communication, and education, as well as for teachers and trainers of spoken English.
