### 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

- This is a book that aims to help anyone who wants to improve their skills in communication, both oral and written.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, such as:
  - The nature and process of communication
  - The barriers and aids to communication
  - The types and modes of communication
  - The skills of listening, speaking, reading and writing
  - The principles and techniques of effective communication
  - The applications of communication skills in different contexts and situations
- The book is divided into three parts:
  - Part I: Communication: An Overview
  - Part II: Communication Skills
  - Part III: Communication in Practice
- The book is designed as a text for advanced level courses in communication skills, but it can also be used by professionals and general readers who want to enhance their communicative competence.
- The book is based on the conventions of communication in English followed in India, and it provides examples, exercises and activities to help the readers practice and improve their communication skills.
