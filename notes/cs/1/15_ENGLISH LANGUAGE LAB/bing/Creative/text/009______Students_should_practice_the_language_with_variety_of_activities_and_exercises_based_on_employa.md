#### Students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises based on employability skills. Interactive and Communicative Practical with emphasis on Oral Presentation/Spoken Communication, based on International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.)

- Language learning is a skill that can enhance one's employability and career prospects in a globalized world. Therefore, students should practice the language with variety of activities and exercises that can help them develop their linguistic competence and confidence.
- Employability skills are the abilities and attributes that employers look for in potential employees, such as communication, teamwork, problem-solving, creativity, and initiative. Students should practice the language with activities and exercises that can help them demonstrate and improve these skills in different contexts and situations.
- Interactive and communicative practical are the methods of language teaching and learning that involve the active participation and interaction of the students and the teacher, as well as the use of authentic and meaningful materials and tasks. These methods can help the students to use the language for real purposes and to develop their fluency and accuracy.
- Oral presentation and spoken communication are the skills of delivering information and expressing ideas clearly and effectively in spoken form, using appropriate language, tone, and body language. These skills are essential for academic and professional success, as well as for social and personal development. Students should practice the language with activities and exercises that can help them to prepare and deliver oral presentations and to engage in spoken communication with different audiences and purposes.
- International Phonetic Alphabets (I.P.A.) are the symbols that represent the sounds of the language, regardless of the spelling or the writing system. They can help the students to learn and pronounce the words correctly and to understand the differences and similarities between the sounds of their native language and the target language. Students should practice the language with activities and exercises that can help them to learn and use the I.P.A. symbols and to improve their pronunciation and listening skills.
