### 14. Business English by Ken Taylor, Orient Blackswan, 2011, New Delhi

- Business English is a form of English that is used in professional contexts, such as meetings, presentations, negotiations, emails, reports, etc.
- Business English can vary depending on the industry, culture, and purpose of communication, but it generally follows some common principles, such as clarity, accuracy, politeness, and formality.
- Business English by Ken Taylor is a book that aims to help learners of English as a second language improve their business communication skills in 50 practical ways.
- The book covers topics such as grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, writing, speaking, listening, and reading, and provides tips, examples, exercises, and self-tests for each area.
- The book is suitable for intermediate to advanced learners of English who want to enhance their confidence and competence in business situations.
- The book is published by Orient Blackswan, a leading academic publisher in India, and was released in 2011.
