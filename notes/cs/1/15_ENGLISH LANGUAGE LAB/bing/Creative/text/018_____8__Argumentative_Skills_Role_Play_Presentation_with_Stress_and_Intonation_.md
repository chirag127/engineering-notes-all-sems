### 8. Argumentative Skills/Role Play Presentation with Stress and Intonation.

- Argumentative skills are the ability to present and defend a position or opinion using logical and persuasive arguments.
- Role play presentation is a type of oral communication where the speaker assumes a specific role or character and interacts with other speakers or an audience.
- Stress and intonation are the features of speech that convey emphasis, emotion, attitude, and meaning through variations in loudness, pitch, and duration.
- To improve your argumentative skills and role play presentation with stress and intonation, you should:

  - Identify the purpose and audience of your argument or role play. What are you trying to achieve and who are you trying to convince or entertain?
  - Research and organize your information and evidence. Use credible sources, facts, statistics, examples, and quotations to support your claims and counterarguments.
  - Structure your argument or role play logically and clearly. Use an introduction, body, and conclusion to present your main points and address the opposing views.
  - Use appropriate language and tone for your argument or role play. Avoid informal, rude, or offensive words and expressions. Use polite, respectful, and assertive language to express your opinions and feelings.
  - Use stress and intonation to enhance your argument or role play. Stress the important words and phrases to show emphasis and contrast. Use rising and falling intonation to show questions, statements, agreement, disagreement, surprise, etc.
  - Practice your argument or role play before presenting it. Record yourself and listen to your voice. Check if your stress and intonation are clear and effective. Ask for feedback from others and improve your performance.
