### 1-Interactive Communication Skills:

- Interactive communication skills are the abilities to communicate effectively and appropriately with others in various situations, such as conversations, interviews, presentations, negotiations, etc.
- Interactive communication skills involve both verbal and non-verbal elements, such as language, tone, voice, body language, facial expressions, gestures, eye contact, etc.
- Interactive communication skills also require listening and feedback skills, such as active listening, paraphrasing, summarizing, questioning, clarifying, confirming, etc.
- Interactive communication skills can be improved by practicing, observing, reflecting, and receiving feedback from others.
- Interactive communication skills are important for personal, professional, and social development, as they help to build rapport, trust, understanding, cooperation, and influence with others.
