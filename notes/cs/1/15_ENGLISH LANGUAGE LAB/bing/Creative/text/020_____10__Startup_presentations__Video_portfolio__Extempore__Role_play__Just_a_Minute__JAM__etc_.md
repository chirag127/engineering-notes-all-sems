### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

- These are some of the common types of activities that are used to assess the communication skills, creativity, and personality of candidates in various fields and domains.
- Startup presentations are short and concise pitches that showcase the value proposition, market opportunity, and competitive advantage of a new business idea or venture.
- Video portfolio is a collection of clips that demonstrate the skills, achievements, and interests of a candidate in a visual and engaging way.
- Extempore is a spontaneous speech that is given on a random topic without any prior preparation or notes.
- Role play is a simulation of a real-life scenario where the candidates act out the roles of different characters and interact with each other according to the given situation and objectives.
- Just a Minute (JAM) is a game where the candidates have to speak on a given topic for one minute without hesitation, repetition, or deviation.
- Some of the benefits of these activities are:
  - They help to showcase the candidates' oral communication, presentation, and persuasion skills.
  - They help to evaluate the candidates' creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities.
  - They help to reveal the candidates' personality, attitude, and motivation.
  - They help to enhance the candidates' confidence, self-awareness, and adaptability.
- Some of the challenges of these activities are:
  - They require the candidates to think on their feet and cope with the pressure and uncertainty of the situation.
  - They require the candidates to balance the content, structure, and delivery of their speech or performance.
  - They require the candidates to be aware of the audience, purpose, and context of the activity.
  - They require the candidates to be prepared for the feedback, questions, and comments from the evaluators or peers.
