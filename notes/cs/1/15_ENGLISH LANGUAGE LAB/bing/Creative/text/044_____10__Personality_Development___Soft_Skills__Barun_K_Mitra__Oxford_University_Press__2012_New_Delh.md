### 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi.

- This book is a textbook that addresses the requirements of students of any discipline who are on the threshold of starting their careers. The book would also prove beneficial to young managers who have already had a taste of corporate life .
- The book aims to provide crucial insights into various facets of developing one's personality, as well as to improve written, verbal, and non-verbal communication skills.
- The book covers topics such as self-awareness, self-motivation, emotional intelligence, time management, stress management, interpersonal skills, group dynamics, leadership skills, etiquette and manners, presentation skills, and writing skills  .
- The book adopts a learner-friendly approach and uses various pedagogical tools such as learning objectives, case studies, activities, exercises, role plays, self-assessment tests, and summary points to enhance the learning outcomes .
- The book also provides online resources for instructors and students, such as PowerPoint slides, instructor's manual, and additional reading material.
