### MePro by Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd.

- MePro is a digital English language learning platform developed by Pearson, the world's learning company.
- MePro aims to transform English language learning for students and professionals by providing personalized and adaptive learning paths, real-time feedback, and gamified features.
- MePro covers four key skills of English language: listening, speaking, reading, and writing, as well as grammar and vocabulary.
- MePro uses artificial intelligence and natural language processing to assess the learners' proficiency level, identify their strengths and weaknesses, and recommend suitable learning activities and resources.
- MePro also offers live online classes with certified trainers, interactive videos, podcasts, articles, and quizzes to enhance the learners' engagement and motivation.
- MePro is aligned with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), an international standard for describing language ability.
- MePro is suitable for learners of different age groups, backgrounds, and goals, such as school and college students, working professionals, job seekers, and test takers.
- MePro is available as a web-based platform and a mobile app, and can be accessed anytime, anywhere, and on any device.
- MePro is one of the digital products offered by Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd., a subsidiary of Pearson plc, that provides learning solutions and services to the Indian market .
- MePro has received positive feedback and recognition from various stakeholders, such as educators, learners, and employers, for its effectiveness, innovation, and user-friendliness.
