# 1. Students will be enabled to understand the basic objective of the course by being acquainted with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

- Communication skills are essential for academic and professional success, as well as for personal and social development.
- The course aims to help students develop and improve their communication skills in various contexts and situations, such as academic writing, presentations, group discussions, interviews, debates, etc.
- The course covers five specific dimensions of communication skills: reading, writing, listening, thinking and speaking. Each dimension has its own objectives, strategies and techniques that students will learn and practice throughout the course.
- Reading: Students will learn how to read effectively and efficiently, how to comprehend and analyze different types of texts, how to identify the main ideas and supporting details, how to infer and evaluate the author's purpose, tone, perspective and argument, how to use reading strategies such as skimming, scanning, summarizing, paraphrasing, etc.
- Writing: Students will learn how to write clearly and coherently, how to organize and structure their ideas, how to use appropriate language, style, tone and format, how to avoid grammatical and spelling errors, how to use different types of writing such as descriptive, narrative, expository, persuasive, etc.
- Listening: Students will learn how to listen actively and attentively, how to understand and interpret verbal and non-verbal cues, how to respond and give feedback, how to take notes and summarize, how to overcome barriers and distractions, how to use different types of listening such as informative, critical, empathic, etc.
- Thinking: Students will learn how to think critically and creatively, how to analyze and synthesize information, how to solve problems and make decisions, how to use logic and reasoning, how to apply different types of thinking such as deductive, inductive, lateral, etc.
- Speaking: Students will learn how to speak confidently and fluently, how to use appropriate pronunciation, intonation, stress and rhythm, how to use effective body language and eye contact, how to adapt to the audience and the purpose, how to use different types of speaking such as informative, persuasive, impromptu, etc.
