## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

- Part A: This part consists of oral communication skills and focuses on the following aspects:
  - Phonetics: The study of speech sounds, their production, transcription and classification.
  - Pronunciation: The correct and standard way of speaking a language, with attention to stress, intonation and rhythm.
  - Fluency: The ability to speak smoothly and confidently, without hesitation, repetition or pauses.
  - Vocabulary: The range and variety of words and expressions used in speaking a language, with attention to collocations, idioms and synonyms.
  - Grammar: The rules and structures of a language that govern how words and sentences are formed and used.
  - Presentation skills: The techniques and strategies of planning, preparing and delivering an effective oral presentation, with attention to audience, purpose, content, structure, language, voice, body language and visual aids.
  - Group discussion: The skills and strategies of participating in a formal or informal discussion on a given topic, with attention to listening, speaking, turn-taking, agreeing, disagreeing, questioning, clarifying, summarizing and concluding.
  - Interview skills: The skills and strategies of preparing for and facing a job interview, with attention to resume, cover letter, dress code, etiquette, body language, questions and answers.

- Part B: This part consists of written communication skills and focuses on the following aspects:
  - Reading comprehension: The ability to understand and interpret a given text, with attention to main idea, supporting details, inference, tone, purpose and vocabulary.
  - Writing skills: The ability to produce clear, coherent and accurate texts, with attention to format, style, tone, purpose, audience, content, organization, language, grammar, spelling and punctuation.
  - Technical writing: The type of writing that deals with specialized topics, such as science, engineering, technology, business, etc., with attention to clarity, conciseness, accuracy, objectivity, consistency and usability.
  - Report writing: The type of writing that presents and analyzes information, data, findings, recommendations, etc., with attention to format, structure, language, style, graphics and references.
  - Proposal writing: The type of writing that seeks to persuade or convince the reader to accept or approve a plan, project, idea, etc., with attention to format, structure, language, style, graphics and references.
  - Email writing: The type of writing that uses electronic mail as a medium of communication, with attention to format, subject line, salutation, body, closing, signature and attachments.
