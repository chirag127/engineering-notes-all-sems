## LIST OF PRACTICALS

- A practical is a hands-on activity that allows students to apply and demonstrate their theoretical knowledge and skills in a specific subject or field.
- Practicals can take various forms, such as experiments, simulations, fieldwork, projects, case studies, demonstrations, etc.
- Practicals can have different purposes, such as developing practical skills, enhancing conceptual understanding, testing hypotheses, solving problems, collecting data, analyzing results, etc.
- Practicals can be conducted individually or in groups, depending on the nature and objectives of the activity.
- Practicals can be assessed in different ways, such as by observation, report, presentation, portfolio, etc.
- Practicals can have different levels of difficulty, complexity, and duration, depending on the learning outcomes and expectations of the course or program.
- Practicals can be integrated with other learning activities, such as lectures, tutorials, seminars, etc., to create a balanced and coherent curriculum.
- Practicals can be designed and implemented using various methods, tools, and resources, such as laboratory equipment, software, online platforms, etc.
- Practicals can be influenced by various factors, such as safety, ethics, availability, feasibility, etc., that need to be considered and addressed in the planning and execution stages.
