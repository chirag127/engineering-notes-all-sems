# 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

These are some of the common types of activities that are used to assess the communication skills, creativity, and personality of candidates in various fields and domains. Here is a brief overview of each activity and some tips on how to prepare and perform well in them.

## Startup presentations

- A startup presentation is a short and concise pitch that showcases the idea, vision, and value proposition of a new venture or project.
- The purpose of a startup presentation is to attract the attention and interest of potential investors, customers, partners, or other stakeholders.
- A startup presentation typically consists of 10 to 15 slides that cover the following topics: problem, solution, market, competition, business model, traction, team, and ask.
- A startup presentation should be clear, compelling, and credible, using data, facts, and stories to support the claims and assumptions.
- A startup presentation should also be tailored to the specific audience and context, highlighting the benefits and opportunities for them.
- Some tips for creating and delivering a startup presentation are:
  - Start with a hook that captures the attention and curiosity of the audience.
  - Use simple and concise language that avoids jargon and technical terms.
  - Use visuals, charts, graphs, and images that illustrate the key points and data.
  - Practice and rehearse the presentation multiple times, preferably with feedback from others.
  - Anticipate and prepare for possible questions and objections from the audience.

## Video portfolio

- A video portfolio is a collection of short videos that showcase the skills, achievements, and personality of a candidate or a professional.
- The purpose of a video portfolio is to demonstrate the competence, creativity, and communication style of the candidate or the professional in a dynamic and engaging way.
- A video portfolio typically consists of 3 to 5 videos that cover the following topics: introduction, work samples, testimonials, and closing.
- A video portfolio should be relevant, authentic, and professional, using high-quality audio and video equipment and editing software.
- A video portfolio should also be customized to the specific field and domain, highlighting the relevant skills and experiences for the desired position or role.
- Some tips for creating and delivering a video portfolio are:
  - Plan and script the content and structure of each video, using a clear and logical flow.
  - Choose a suitable location and background that is quiet, well-lit, and appropriate for the topic and tone of the video.
  - Dress and act professionally and confidently, using a friendly and enthusiastic voice and body language.
  - Keep the videos short and concise, ideally between 2 to 5 minutes each.
  - Review and edit the videos, removing any errors, distractions, or unnecessary parts.

## Extempore

- An extempore is a spontaneous and impromptu speech that is given on a given topic or theme without any prior preparation or notes.
- The purpose of an extempore is to test the ability, knowledge, and opinion of the speaker on a current or relevant issue or subject.
- An extempore typically lasts for 2 to 3 minutes and covers the following aspects: introduction, main points, examples, and conclusion.
- An extempore should be coherent, logical, and persuasive, using facts, statistics, and anecdotes to support the arguments and views.
- An extempore should also be fluent, clear, and confident, using appropriate vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation.
- Some tips for preparing and delivering an extempore are:
  - Think and brainstorm quickly and critically about the topic, identifying the key points and examples.
  - Organize and structure the speech, using a simple and easy-to-follow format.
  - Speak and express clearly and confidently, using a moderate and consistent pace and tone.
  - Avoid and overcome any hesitation, repetition, or deviation, using filler words, pauses, or transitions.
  - Conclude and summarize the speech, restating the main points and views.
