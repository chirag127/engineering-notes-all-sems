# Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press

- Cambridge English Empower is a **general adult course** that combines course content from Cambridge University Press with validated assessment from the experts at Cambridge English Language Assessment .
- It is more than just a course book - it's a **complete solution** for effective learning and teaching .
- It covers **six levels** from A1 to C1, each with a Student's Book, a Workbook, a Teacher's Book, an Online Assessment and Practice package, and a Classroom Presentation Tool .
- It provides **personalized learning paths** for students based on their initial and ongoing test results, and offers **continuous feedback** on their progress .
- It follows the **Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR)** and prepares students for the Cambridge English Qualifications and other international exams .
- It uses **authentic video** from sources like the BBC and TED Talks to engage students and expose them to real-world language .
- It develops **all four skills** (reading, writing, listening, and speaking) as well as grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation .
- It fosters **learner autonomy** and **critical thinking** skills through activities that encourage students to reflect on their own learning and use language creatively .
- It supports **teachers** with a range of resources, including lesson plans, worksheets, tests, tips, and videos .
