# Manual of Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar,2013, Delhi

- This is a book that covers various aspects of communication skills, such as listening, speaking, reading, writing, and non-verbal communication.
- The book aims to help students and professionals improve their communication skills in different contexts and situations, such as academic, professional, social, and personal.
- The book is divided into 12 chapters, each focusing on a specific topic or skill related to communication. The chapters are:

  - Chapter 1: Introduction to Communication
  - Chapter 2: Listening Skills
  - Chapter 3: Speaking Skills
  - Chapter 4: Reading Skills
  - Chapter 5: Writing Skills
  - Chapter 6: Non-verbal Communication
  - Chapter 7: Interpersonal Communication
  - Chapter 8: Group Communication
  - Chapter 9: Public Speaking
  - Chapter 10: Business Communication
  - Chapter 11: Technical Communication
  - Chapter 12: Communication and Culture

- The book provides theoretical concepts, practical examples, exercises, and activities to help the readers understand and practice the communication skills.
- The book also includes a glossary of key terms, a bibliography of references, and an index for easy navigation.
- The book is written in a simple and clear language, with a user-friendly layout and design.
- The book is suitable for students and professionals of various disciplines and fields, such as engineering, management, science, arts, and humanities.
