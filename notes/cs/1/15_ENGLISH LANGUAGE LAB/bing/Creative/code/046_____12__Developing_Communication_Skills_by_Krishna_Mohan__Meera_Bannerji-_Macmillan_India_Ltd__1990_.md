# 12. Developing Communication Skills by Krishna Mohan, Meera Bannerji- Macmillan India Ltd. 1990, Delhi.

- This book is designed to help anyone who wants to improve their skills in communication, both oral and written.
- The book covers various aspects of communication, such as:
  - The nature and process of communication
  - The barriers and aids to communication
  - The types and modes of communication
  - The skills and strategies of communication
  - The conventions and styles of communication
  - The applications and contexts of communication
- The book provides theoretical insights, practical examples, exercises, and feedback to help the reader develop their communication skills  .
- The book is divided into three parts:
  - Part I: Communication: An Overview
  - Part II: Communication Skills
  - Part III: Communication in Practice
- The book is suitable for advanced level courses in communication skills, as well as for professionals who want to enhance their communicative performance .
