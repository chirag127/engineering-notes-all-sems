- **Predicting**: While reading or listening to a text or audio, make predictions about what will happen next, what the main idea is, or what the author's or speaker's intention is. This can help engage with the text or audio, monitor comprehension, and check for accuracy.
- **Questioning**: After reading or listening to a text or audio, ask questions to clarify, summarize, evaluate, or extend the text or audio. This can help deepen understanding, identify gaps, and connect the text or audio to other texts, audios, or personal experiences.

- **Evaluating**: After reading or listening to a text or audio, form an opinion or judgment about the text or audio based on evidence, criteria, or standards. This can help analyze the quality, credibility, relevance, or usefulness of the text or audio, and develop critical thinking skills.
