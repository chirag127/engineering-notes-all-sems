### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to the pronunciation and speech patterns of English, especially for Indian learners.
- The book covers the following topics:
  - The nature and function of speech sounds, including vowels, consonants, diphthongs, and stress patterns.
  - The phonetic transcription of English words, using the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA).
  - The differences between British and American English, as well as regional and social variations of English.
  - The common errors and difficulties faced by Indian learners of English, such as interference from the mother tongue, spelling pronunciation, and word stress.
  - The techniques and exercises for improving the clarity, fluency, and accuracy of spoken English, such as minimal pairs, tongue twisters, drills, and dialogues.
  - The aspects of connected speech, such as intonation, rhythm, and linking.
  - The features of spoken discourse, such as turn-taking, coherence, and politeness.
- The book is divided into four parts:
  - Part I: Introduction to Speech and Phonetics, which provides the basic concepts and terminology of speech and phonetics.
  - Part II: The Sounds of English, which describes the inventory and classification of English speech sounds, and their phonetic symbols.
  - Part III: The Pronunciation of English, which explains the rules and patterns of English pronunciation, and the common errors and difficulties of Indian learners.
  - Part IV: The Practice of Spoken English, which offers practical exercises and activities for improving the spoken English skills of the learners.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, and professionals who want to improve their spoken English proficiency and confidence.
- The book is accompanied by a CD-ROM, which contains audio recordings of the examples, exercises, and dialogues in the book, as well as additional material and resources.
