### Cambridge English Empower by Cambridge University Press

- Cambridge English Empower is a general adult course that combines course content from Cambridge University Press with validated assessment from the experts at Cambridge English Language Assessment .
- It is more than just a course book - it's a complete solution for effective learning and teaching .
- It covers six levels from A1 to C1, each corresponding to the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) levels .
- It provides learners with an immediate sense of purpose and clear learning objectives, based on the Cambridge English Corpus and the English Vocabulary Profile  .
- It offers a unique mix of engaging classroom materials, validated and reliable assessment, and personalised online practice .
- It helps learners develop the skills they need to communicate confidently and effectively in English, both in and out of the classroom .
- It empowers teachers with a range of flexible teaching tools and resources, including a comprehensive teacher's book, presentation software, online teacher training and support, and a learning management system .
- It is published by Cambridge University Press, one of the world's leading publishers of English language teaching materials, with over 400 years of experience and expertise  .
