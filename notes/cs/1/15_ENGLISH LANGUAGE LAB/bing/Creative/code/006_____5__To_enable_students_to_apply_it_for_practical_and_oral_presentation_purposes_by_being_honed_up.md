### 5. To enable students to apply it for practical and oral presentation purposes by being honed up in presentation skills and voice-dynamics.

This is a possible objective of a course or a module that aims to teach students how to communicate effectively in oral and written forms. Some of the subtopics that could be covered under this objective are:

- The importance of presentation skills and voice-dynamics for academic and professional success.
- The principles and techniques of designing and delivering effective presentations, such as choosing a topic, defining a purpose, analyzing the audience, organizing the content, using visual aids, and handling questions.
- The elements and strategies of voice-dynamics, such as volume, pitch, tone, pace, pause, emphasis, and modulation.
- The common challenges and pitfalls of oral presentations, such as nervousness, monotony, distraction, and technical issues, and how to overcome them.
- The methods and criteria of evaluating and improving one's own and others' presentations, such as feedback, self-reflection, peer review, and rubrics.

Some of the activities and assessments that could be used to achieve this objective are:

- Practicing and performing oral presentations on various topics and contexts, such as informative, persuasive, demonstrative, and impromptu speeches.
- Recording and analyzing one's own and others' presentations, using audio and video tools, and identifying the strengths and areas for improvement.
- Participating in group discussions and debates, and applying the skills of listening, questioning, arguing, and collaborating.
- Completing written assignments and quizzes that test the knowledge and application of presentation skills and voice-dynamics.
- Creating and presenting a portfolio that showcases one's progress and achievements in oral and written communication.
