### 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi.

- This book is a textbook that addresses the requirements of students of any discipline who are on the threshold of starting their careers. The book would also prove beneficial to young managers who have already had a taste of corporate life .
- The book aims to provide crucial insights into various facets of developing one's personality, as well as to improve written, verbal, and non-verbal communication skills.
- The book covers topics such as self-awareness, motivation, attitude, goal setting, emotional intelligence, leadership, group dynamics, etiquette, presentation skills, resume writing, interview skills, and stress management  .
- The book adopts a learner-centric approach and provides ample examples, case studies, activities, and self-assessment tools to help the readers apply the concepts to real-life situations .
- The book also includes a section on soft skills for the differently abled, which is a unique feature that sensitizes the readers to the challenges faced by people with disabilities and how they can overcome them with confidence and grace.
- The second edition of the book has been revised and updated to incorporate the latest trends and practices in the field of personality development and soft skills.
