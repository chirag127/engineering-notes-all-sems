## LIST OF PRACTICALS

- A practical is a hands-on activity that allows students to apply and demonstrate their theoretical knowledge and skills in a specific subject or field.
- Practicals can take various forms, such as experiments, simulations, fieldwork, projects, case studies, demonstrations, etc.
- Practicals can have different purposes, such as developing practical skills, testing hypotheses, collecting data, solving problems, creating products, etc.
- Practicals can have different outcomes, such as reports, presentations, portfolios, models, prototypes, etc.
- Practicals can have different assessment methods, such as observation, feedback, rubrics, checklists, peer review, etc.
- Practicals can have different benefits, such as enhancing learning, motivation, engagement, creativity, collaboration, etc.
- Practicals can have different challenges, such as safety, ethics, resources, time, guidance, etc.
