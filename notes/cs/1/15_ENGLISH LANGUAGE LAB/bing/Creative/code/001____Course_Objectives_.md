## Course Objectives:

- To introduce the basic concepts and principles of artificial intelligence (AI) and its applications.
- To develop the skills and techniques for designing, implementing, and evaluating AI systems and agents.
- To explore the ethical, social, and legal implications of AI and its impact on human society.
- To provide a foundation for further study and research in AI and related fields.

Some of the topics covered in this course are:

- Search methods and algorithms for problem-solving and optimization.
- Knowledge representation and reasoning using logic, rules, and ontologies.
- Planning and scheduling techniques for goal-directed behavior and action execution.
- Machine learning methods and tools for data analysis and model building.
- Natural language processing and understanding for human-computer interaction and communication.
- Computer vision and image processing for scene analysis and recognition.
- Robotics and embodied agents for perception, navigation, and manipulation.
- Artificial neural networks and deep learning for complex and high-dimensional data.
- Expert systems and decision support systems for domain-specific tasks and applications.
- Multi-agent systems and distributed AI for coordination and cooperation.
