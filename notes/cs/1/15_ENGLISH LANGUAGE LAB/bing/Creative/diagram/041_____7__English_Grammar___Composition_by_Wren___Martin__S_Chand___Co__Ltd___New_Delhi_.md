### 7. English Grammar & Composition by Wren & Martin, S.Chand & Co. Ltd., New Delhi.

- This is a comprehensive book on English grammar and composition that covers the basics of grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, and writing skills.
- The book is divided into two parts: Part I deals with the essentials of grammar, such as parts of speech, sentence structure, phrases and clauses, agreement, tense, voice, mood, and narration. Part II deals with the practical aspects of composition, such as paragraph writing, essay writing, letter writing, precis writing, comprehension, and expansion.
- The book is suitable for students of various levels, from high school to college, as well as for competitive exams, such as SSC, UPSC, and Bank PO.
- The book is written in a clear and simple language, with plenty of examples, exercises, and tests to reinforce the concepts and practice the skills.
- The book also provides useful tips and guidelines on common errors, idioms and phrases, synonyms and antonyms, and word power.
- The book is one of the most popular and widely used books on English grammar and composition in India, and has been revised and updated several times to keep pace with the changing trends and needs of the learners.
