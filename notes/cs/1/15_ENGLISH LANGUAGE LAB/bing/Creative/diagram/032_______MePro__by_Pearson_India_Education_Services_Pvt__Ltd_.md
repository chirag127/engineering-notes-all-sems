### MePro by Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd.

- MePro is an online English language learning platform that aims to transform English language learning for students and professionals.
- MePro uses artificial intelligence and adaptive learning to provide personalized feedback and guidance to learners based on their proficiency level, learning style, and goals.
- MePro offers a comprehensive curriculum that covers all four skills of listening, speaking, reading, and writing, as well as grammar, vocabulary, and pronunciation.
- MePro also provides learners with access to a variety of authentic content from Pearson and other sources, such as videos, podcasts, articles, and books, to enhance their exposure and engagement with the language.
- MePro helps learners to track their progress and performance through regular assessments, reports, and certificates, and also offers them opportunities to interact with other learners and experts through live sessions and forums.
- MePro is suitable for learners of different age groups, backgrounds, and domains, such as school and college students, working professionals, and test takers.
- MePro is a product of Pearson India Education Services Pvt. Ltd., which is a subsidiary of Pearson, the world's leading learning company that provides educational content, assessment, and digital services to over 100 million learners around the world .
