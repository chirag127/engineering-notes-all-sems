### 2. Students would be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as Comprehension, reading, writing and speaking etc.

Building a strong professional vocabulary is an important aspect of language learning. This involves acquiring a wide range of words and expressions that can be used in a variety of contexts and across different modes of communication, such as comprehension, reading, writing, and speaking.

To achieve this, students can start by reading widely in their field of study or profession, paying attention to new words and expressions and adding them to their vocabulary. They can also use online dictionaries and thesauruses to find definitions and synonyms for new words.

In addition, students can participate in language learning activities that focus on vocabulary building, such as word games, crossword puzzles, and vocabulary quizzes. They can also practice using new words in writing assignments, oral presentations, and conversation with native speakers.

It is also important for students to actively seek out opportunities to use their professional vocabulary in real-life situations, such as in the workplace, in professional associations, or in academic settings. This will help them to build their confidence and fluency in using the words and expressions they have learned.

Overall, building a strong professional vocabulary requires a combination of reading, learning activities, practice, and real-life use. By focusing on acquiring a wide range of words and expressions that can be used across different modes of communication, students can create a substantial base for their professional language development.
