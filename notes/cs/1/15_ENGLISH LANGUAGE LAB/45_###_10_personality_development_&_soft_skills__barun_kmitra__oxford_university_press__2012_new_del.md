### 10. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, 2012 New Delhi.

Personality Development & Soft Skills is a book written by Barun K. Mitra and published by Oxford University Press in 2012 in New Delhi. The book focuses on helping individuals develop their personality and soft skills, which are essential for success in both personal and professional life.

The book covers a range of topics related to personality development and soft skills, including self-awareness, communication skills, time management, leadership, and teamwork. Through a combination of practical advice, real-life examples, and exercises, the author provides readers with the tools and strategies they need to develop their personality and soft skills.

In addition to its focus on personality development and soft skills, the book also provides readers with an understanding of the underlying theories and concepts that drive these areas of development. This includes an examination of the key personality traits and soft skills that are most important for success, as well as an exploration of the various theories and models that have been developed to help individuals understand and develop these traits and skills.

Overall, Personality Development & Soft Skills is an excellent resource for anyone looking to develop their personality and soft skills. Whether you are a student, a professional, or simply someone looking to improve your life, this book provides you with the knowledge and tools you need to succeed.
