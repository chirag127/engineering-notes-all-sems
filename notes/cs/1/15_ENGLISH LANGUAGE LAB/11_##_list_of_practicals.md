## LIST OF PRACTICALS

A list of practicals refers to a collection of hands-on activities or experiments that are performed in a laboratory or workshop setting. The purpose of practicals is to provide students with hands-on experience and to reinforce their understanding of theoretical concepts. Practicals can be found in a wide range of disciplines, including science, engineering, and technology. Some common examples of practicals include:

1. Microscopy: observing and studying the structure and behavior of microorganisms and cells.

2. Circuit Design: building and testing electrical circuits to understand the principles of electrical engineering.

3. Chemical Reactions: performing chemical reactions to study the properties and behavior of different substances.

4. Programming: writing and testing computer programs to understand the principles of computer science and software engineering.

5. Robotics: building and programming robots to understand the principles of robotics and automation.

6. Mechanics: performing experiments to study the principles of mechanics, such as force, motion, and energy.

7. Biology: performing experiments to study the structure and function of living organisms.

The list of practicals will vary depending on the discipline and course of study, but the overall goal is to provide students with hands-on experience and to reinforce their understanding of theoretical concepts.
