### 13. Spoken English- A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K.Bansal & J.B.Harrison, Orient Blackswan, 2013, New Delhi.

Spoken English: A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K. Bansal and J.B. Harrison, published by Orient Blackswan in 2013, is a comprehensive guide to the study of speech and phonetics. The book is designed for students, teachers, and anyone interested in improving their spoken English skills.

The book covers various aspects of speech and phonetics, including the anatomy and physiology of speech, the production of speech sounds, and the study of intonation and stress. It also includes a comprehensive study of English phonetics, including the sounds of English, and the rules for combining sounds to form words and sentences.

The authors use clear and concise explanations, supported by illustrations and examples, to help readers understand the principles of speech and phonetics. The book also includes exercises and activities to help readers practice and reinforce the skills they have learned.

In addition, the book provides practical advice and strategies for improving spoken English skills, including tips on how to improve pronunciation, how to reduce accent, and how to speak with confidence and clarity.

Overall, Spoken English: A manual of Speech and Phonetics by R.K. Bansal and J.B. Harrison is a comprehensive and accessible guide to the study of speech and phonetics. Whether you are a student, a teacher, or simply someone interested in improving your spoken English skills, this book provides the knowledge and skills you need to achieve your goals.
