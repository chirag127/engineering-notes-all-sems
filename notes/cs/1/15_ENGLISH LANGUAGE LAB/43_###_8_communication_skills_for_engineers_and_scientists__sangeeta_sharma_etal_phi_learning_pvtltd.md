### 8. Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists, Sangeeta Sharma et.al. PHI Learning Pvt.Ltd, 2011, New Delhi.

"Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists" by Sangeeta Sharma et.al. is a book that focuses on developing effective communication skills for engineers and scientists. The book was published by PHI Learning Pvt. Ltd. in 2011 and is based in New Delhi.

The book covers various aspects of communication, including writing, speaking, and presentation skills, and provides practical guidance and tips for improving communication skills in the context of engineering and science.

The book is designed for engineers and scientists who need to communicate technical information and ideas to a variety of audiences, including colleagues, clients, and the general public. It covers topics such as writing reports, giving presentations, and communicating technical information in a clear and concise manner.

The book also includes practical exercises and case studies to help readers apply the concepts and techniques covered in the book to real-world situations. Additionally, the book provides tips and strategies for overcoming common communication challenges, such as dealing with difficult questions and handling objections.

Overall, "Communication Skills for Engineers and Scientists" is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills in the context of engineering and science. The practical guidance and tips provided in the book can help readers to effectively communicate technical information and ideas and to build their confidence and credibility as communicators.
