### 10. Startup presentations, Video portfolio, Extempore, Role play, Just a Minute (JAM) etc.

1. Startup presentations: A startup presentation is a way for entrepreneurs to pitch their business idea to potential investors, partners, or customers. The goal is to explain the problem the startup is solving, the solution they are offering, and the potential market.

2. Video portfolio: A video portfolio is a collection of videos that showcase a person's skills, achievements, and experiences. It is often used by creatives, such as filmmakers, graphic designers, and animators, to demonstrate their work to potential clients or employers.

3. Extempore: Extempore is a public speaking exercise where participants are given a topic and have to speak on it without any prior preparation. The goal is to improve spontaneous speaking skills and quick thinking.

4. Role play: Role play is an acting exercise where participants take on different characters and act out scenarios in a simulated environment. The goal is to improve communication and interpersonal skills.

5. Just a Minute (JAM): JAM is a speaking exercise where participants have to speak for one minute on a given topic without hesitation, repetition, or deviation. The goal is to improve fluency and confidence in public speaking.

6. Group discussion: Group discussion is an interactive exercise where participants engage in a conversation on a given topic. The goal is to improve communication, teamwork, and leadership skills.

7. Debates: Debates are structured arguments between two or more participants on a given topic. The goal is to improve critical thinking, public speaking, and persuasion skills.

8. Case studies: Case studies are real-life examples of a business or organizational problem. Participants analyze the case and come up with solutions. The goal is to improve problem-solving and decision-making skills.

9. Simulation games: Simulation games are interactive exercises that simulate real-life scenarios. Participants make decisions and see the consequences of their actions. The goal is to improve decision-making and strategic thinking skills.

10. Brainstorming sessions: Brainstorming sessions are group exercises where participants generate ideas and solutions to a given problem. The goal is to improve creativity and innovation skills.
