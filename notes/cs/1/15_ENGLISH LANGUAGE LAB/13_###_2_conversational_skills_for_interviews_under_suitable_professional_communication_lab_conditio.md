### 2. Conversational Skills for Interviews under suitable Professional Communication Lab conditions with emphasis on Kinesics.

Kinesics refers to the study of body language and non-verbal communication. Here are some key kinesic skills to keep in mind during a professional interview:

1. Eye Contact: Maintaining eye contact shows that you are engaged and interested in the conversation.

2. Posture: Good posture demonstrates confidence and professionalism. Stand or sit up straight and avoid slouching.

3. Gestures: Use gestures to emphasize your points, but avoid excessive or distracting movements.

4. Facial Expressions: Your facial expressions can convey your emotions and interest in the conversation. Smile, nod, and show genuine interest.

5. Voice: Speak clearly and at a moderate pace. Avoid speaking too quickly or too slowly.

In addition to kinesic skills, it's also important to pay attention to your overall communication style during an interview. Be polite, professional, and respectful. Listen actively and ask thoughtful questions. Show your enthusiasm and interest in the role and the company.
