### 11. Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma & Krishna Mohan, Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd., 2001, New Delhi.

Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma and Krishna Mohan, published by Tata McGraw Hill & Co. Ltd., is a book that focuses on the skills and techniques needed to write effective business correspondence and reports. The book covers a wide range of topics, including writing letters, memos, emails, and reports, and provides practical advice and strategies for improving writing skills.

The authors provide a clear and concise overview of the key elements of business correspondence and report writing, including structure, tone, and style. They also provide practical examples and exercises to help readers understand and apply the concepts covered in the book.

The book is written for individuals who want to improve their writing skills for business purposes, such as students, professionals, and entrepreneurs. It is particularly useful for those who need to write clear, concise, and effective business correspondence and reports that are appropriate for a variety of audiences and purposes.

In conclusion, Business Correspondence and Report Writing by Prof. R.C. Sharma and Krishna Mohan is a comprehensive guide to writing effective business correspondence and reports. Whether you are new to business writing or looking to improve your skills, this book provides practical advice and strategies for achieving your goals.
