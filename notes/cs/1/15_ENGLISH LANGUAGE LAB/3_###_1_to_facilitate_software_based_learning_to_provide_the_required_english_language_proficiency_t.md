### 1. To facilitate software based learning to provide the required English Language proficiency to students.

Software-based learning is an innovative approach to language learning that utilizes technology to provide students with the tools and resources they need to develop their English language proficiency. The goal of software-based learning is to make language learning more accessible, engaging, and effective for students of all levels.

One of the key benefits of software-based learning is that it provides students with a flexible and convenient way to study. With software-based learning, students can access their language course materials from anywhere, at any time, and at their own pace. This makes it possible for students to fit language learning into their busy schedules, and to progress at a pace that is comfortable for them.

Another benefit of software-based learning is that it provides students with a highly interactive and engaging learning experience. Through interactive exercises, games, and simulations, students are able to practice their language skills in a fun and engaging way. This helps to keep students motivated and engaged, and to reinforce the language concepts they are learning.

Software-based learning also provides students with real-time feedback on their language performance. This feedback is essential for helping students to identify areas where they need to improve, and to track their progress over time. With software-based learning, students can see how their language skills are developing, and can adjust their study strategies accordingly.

Finally, software-based learning provides students with access to a wealth of language resources, including audio and video materials, online dictionaries and grammar guides, and more. This makes it possible for students to study English in a comprehensive and well-rounded way, and to build their language skills in a variety of different contexts.

Overall, software-based learning is an excellent tool for facilitating English language proficiency in students. Whether you are a beginner or an advanced learner, software-based learning provides you with the resources and support you need to develop your language skills and achieve your goals.
