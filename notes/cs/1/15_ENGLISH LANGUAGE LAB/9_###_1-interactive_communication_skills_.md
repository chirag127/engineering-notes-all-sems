### 1-Interactive Communication Skills:

Interactive Communication Skills refer to the ability to effectively communicate and engage with others in a two-way exchange. This includes active listening, expressing oneself clearly, and adapting communication style to suit the audience and context. Key elements of Interactive Communication Skills include:

1. Active Listening: Paying attention to what the other person is saying and demonstrating that you understand their message.
2. Clarity: Expressing oneself in a clear and concise manner, avoiding ambiguity and misunderstandings.
3. Empathy: Understanding the perspective and feelings of others, and responding in a way that shows you care.
4. Adaptability: Adjusting your communication style to suit the context, audience, and purpose of the interaction.
5. Confidence: Speaking up and expressing your thoughts and opinions in a clear and assertive manner.
6. Non-verbal Communication: Using body language and other non-verbal cues to reinforce or complement verbal communication.
7. Questioning: Asking questions to clarify understanding, seek information, or engage in dialogue.

Developing strong Interactive Communication Skills requires practice and self-awareness, as well as a willingness to learn and grow. By continuously improving these skills, individuals can build stronger relationships, resolve conflicts more effectively, and achieve their goals more efficiently.
