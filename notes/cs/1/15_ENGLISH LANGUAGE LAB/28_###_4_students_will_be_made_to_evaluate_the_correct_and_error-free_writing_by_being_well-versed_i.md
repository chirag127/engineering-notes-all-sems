### 4. Students will be made to evaluate the correct and error-free writing by being well-versed in rules of English grammar and cultivate relevant technical style of communication &presentation at their work place and also for academic uses.

English grammar and technical writing are important skills for students to develop in order to effectively communicate and present information in both academic and professional settings. The goal of this aspect of education is to help students:

1. Evaluate written material for accuracy and correctness: Students will learn how to identify and correct common grammar errors, such as subject-verb agreement, verb tense, and punctuation.

2. Cultivate a relevant technical style of communication: Students will learn how to effectively communicate technical information in a clear and concise manner, using appropriate technical vocabulary and writing conventions.

3. Prepare for academic and professional uses: By developing their skills in English grammar and technical writing, students will be better equipped to succeed in academic and professional settings, where clear and effective communication is essential.

4. Improve writing skills and confidence: By learning and practicing the rules of English grammar and technical writing, students will become more confident and skilled writers, which will benefit them in all areas of their lives.

Overall, the goal of teaching English grammar and technical writing is to help students become effective and confident communicators, both in academic and professional settings.
