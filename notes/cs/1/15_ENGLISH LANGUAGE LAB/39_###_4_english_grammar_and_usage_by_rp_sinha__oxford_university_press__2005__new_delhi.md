### 4. English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha, Oxford University Press, 2005, New Delhi.

"English Grammar and Usage" by R.P. Sinha, published by Oxford University Press in 2005, is a comprehensive guide to the English language and its grammar. This book is designed for students and language learners of all levels, and provides a clear and concise overview of the various grammatical structures and usage patterns in English.

The book covers a wide range of topics, including sentence structure, verb tenses, nouns and pronouns, adjectives and adverbs, prepositions and conjunctions, and much more. Each topic is presented in a clear and easy-to-understand manner, with examples and exercises to help reinforce the concepts being presented.

In addition to its comprehensive coverage of English grammar, "English Grammar and Usage" also includes a wealth of tips and tricks for improving one's English writing and speaking skills. The book provides practical advice on how to avoid common grammatical errors, and how to use language more effectively in a variety of different contexts.

One of the key strengths of "English Grammar and Usage" is its focus on practical, real-world applications of English grammar. The book provides numerous examples of how the various grammatical structures and usage patterns are used in everyday writing and speaking, and helps students to see the relevance of the concepts they are learning.

Overall, "English Grammar and Usage" by R.P. Sinha is an excellent resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills. Whether you are a beginner or an advanced learner, this book provides a comprehensive and accessible guide to the English language and its grammar, and is an essential tool for anyone looking to improve their language proficiency.
