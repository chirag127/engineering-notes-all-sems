### 3. Students will apply it at their work place for writing purposes such as Presentation/official drafting/administrative communication and use it for document/project/report/research paper writing.

Writing is an important aspect of professional life, and there are various types of writing that students will encounter in their work places. Some common examples include presentations, official drafting, administrative communication, and document/project/report/research paper writing.

In order to effectively apply writing skills in a professional setting, students should focus on developing clear, concise, and well-structured writing that is easy to understand and meets the needs of their audience. This may involve researching the topic, outlining the main points, and using appropriate tone and language.

In addition, students should also be familiar with the conventions and standards of the specific type of writing they are engaged in, such as the use of proper citation styles for research papers, or the use of clear and concise language for presentations.

Finally, students should also be willing to revise and edit their writing, as this is an important part of the writing process and helps ensure that the final product is of high quality.
