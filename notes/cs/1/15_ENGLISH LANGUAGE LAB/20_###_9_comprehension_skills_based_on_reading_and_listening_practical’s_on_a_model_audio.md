### 9. Comprehension Skills based on Reading and Listening Practical’s on a model Audio

Comprehension skills based on reading and listening practicals on a model audio refers to the process of improving one's ability to understand and retain information from written or spoken material. This can be achieved through various activities, such as:

1. Reading comprehension: involves actively engaging with written text, such as a novel or article, to understand the main ideas, themes, and details.

2. Listening comprehension: involves actively listening to spoken material, such as a lecture or conversation, to understand the main ideas, themes, and details.

3. Model audio practicals: refers to exercises or activities that use audio material, such as a recorded lecture or conversation, to practice and improve comprehension skills.

These practicals may involve activities such as summarizing key points, answering questions based on the material, or identifying specific information in the audio. The goal of these practicals is to develop the ability to actively engage with and understand spoken or written material, and to retain that information for later use.
