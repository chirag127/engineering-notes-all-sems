### 3. To train students to use the correct and error-free writing by being well versed in rules of English grammar.

To train students to use correct and error-free writing, it is important to focus on teaching the rules of English grammar. This includes teaching the proper use of nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and other parts of speech, as well as the rules for sentence structure and punctuation.

One effective method for teaching grammar is to provide students with clear explanations of the rules, along with examples and exercises to practice what they have learned. This can include worksheets, writing assignments, and in-class activities that focus on specific grammar concepts.

Another important aspect of teaching grammar is to provide students with opportunities to receive feedback on their writing. This can include having students peer-review each other's work, or having teachers provide written comments and suggestions for improvement.

It is also important to encourage students to read widely and pay attention to the grammar and style of the writing they encounter. This can help them develop a better understanding of the rules of English grammar and improve their own writing skills.

In addition, it is helpful to provide students with resources such as grammar books, online resources, and writing guides that they can use to reinforce what they have learned and seek help when they need it.

Overall, to train students to use correct and error-free writing, it is essential to focus on teaching the rules of English grammar, providing opportunities for practice and feedback, encouraging reading and self-reflection, and providing access to resources and support.
