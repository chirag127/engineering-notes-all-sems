## Professional Communication Lab shall have two parts:

The Professional Communication Lab is a laboratory that provides students with the opportunity to develop their communication skills in a professional setting. The lab is divided into two parts, each with a distinct focus.

Part 1: Writing and Presentation Skills
In this part of the lab, students are provided with opportunities to develop their writing and presentation skills. This may include activities such as writing business letters, memos, and reports, as well as preparing and delivering presentations. Students are also provided with feedback on their writing and presentation skills, which helps them to identify areas for improvement and to develop their skills over time.

Part 2: Interpersonal Communication Skills
In this part of the lab, students are provided with opportunities to develop their interpersonal communication skills. This may include activities such as role-playing, mock interviews, and group discussions. These activities provide students with the opportunity to practice their communication skills in a variety of real-world scenarios, and to receive feedback on their performance.

Overall, the Professional Communication Lab provides a supportive and collaborative environment where students can develop their communication skills in a hands-on, interactive setting. Whether you are a student who is just starting to explore the world of professional communication, or a more advanced student who is looking to refine your skills, the Professional Communication Lab is an excellent place to start.
