### 7. Individual Speech Delivery/Conferencing with skills to defend Interjections/Quizzes.

Individual speech delivery and conferencing are important skills for effective communication. When delivering a speech, it is important to be confident, articulate, and engaging. This can be achieved through careful preparation, including researching the topic, practicing the speech, and being familiar with the audience.

In terms of conferencing, it is important to be able to effectively participate in group discussions, listen actively to others, and express your own ideas clearly and concisely. To defend against interjections or quizzes during a conference, it is important to be well-prepared and knowledgeable about the topic being discussed. This can be achieved by researching the topic thoroughly and anticipating potential challenges or questions that may arise during the conference.

In addition, it is important to have strong critical thinking skills and be able to respond to interjections or quizzes in a clear and concise manner. This can be achieved by anticipating potential challenges or questions, preparing responses in advance, and being confident and articulate when delivering your response.

Overall, effective individual speech delivery and conferencing require a combination of preparation, knowledge, and strong communication skills. By being well-prepared, confident, and articulate, you can effectively defend against interjections or quizzes and participate in group discussions in a meaningful and effective manner.
