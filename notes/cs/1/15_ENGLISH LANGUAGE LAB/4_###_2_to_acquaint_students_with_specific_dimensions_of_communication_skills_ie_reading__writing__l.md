### 2. To acquaint students with specific dimensions of communication skills i.e. Reading, Writing, Listening, Thinking and Speaking.

Communication skills are the foundation of effective and efficient human interaction. There are five key dimensions of communication skills:

1. Reading: The ability to comprehend written text and understand its meaning.
2. Writing: The ability to express thoughts and ideas through written words.
3. Listening: The ability to pay attention and understand spoken language.
4. Thinking: The ability to analyze information, solve problems, and make decisions.
5. Speaking: The ability to express thoughts and ideas through spoken language.

To develop these five dimensions of communication skills, one can:

1. Read widely and regularly to improve reading skills
2. Write frequently, in various forms such as journaling, essays, or creative writing, to improve writing skills.
3. Listen actively, by paying attention to the speaker, asking questions, and providing feedback, to improve listening skills.
4. Engage in critical thinking exercises, such as problem-solving and decision-making, to improve thinking skills.
5. Practice speaking in various settings, such as public speaking, group discussions, and presentations, to improve speaking skills.

By developing these five dimensions of communication skills, one can become a more effective and confident communicator.
