### Unit-4: Presentation and Interaction Skills 8

Unit 4 "Presentation and Interaction Skills" focuses on developing the ability to effectively communicate ideas and information through various modes of presentation. It covers the following topics:

1. Preparing and delivering presentations 
2. Using visual aids 
3. Engaging and managing the audience 
4. Handling questions and objections 
5. Interpersonal communication skills 
6. Effective listening and non-verbal communication 
7. Group dynamics and team communication 
8. Public speaking and managing stage fright. 

The objective of this unit is to equip individuals with the skills to present information in a clear, articulate and engaging manner, and to interact effectively with others in various situations.
