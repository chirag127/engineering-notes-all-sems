### Active Listening :Meaning and Art of Listening, Traits of a Good Listener,

Active Listening:

Active listening is a communication skill that involves paying close attention to the speaker, understanding their message, and responding in an appropriate manner.

1. Meaning and Art of Listening: Active listening is a deliberate and focused form of listening that requires the listener to be fully present and engaged in the conversation. The art of active listening involves paying attention to nonverbal cues, such as body language and tone of voice, as well as the words being spoken.

2. Traits of a Good Listener: Good listeners possess certain traits that help them to be effective in their listening. These include being empathetic, non-judgmental, open-minded, and patient. Good listeners also have the ability to ask clarifying questions and provide feedback to help ensure that they have accurately understood the speaker's message.

In conclusion, active listening is a critical communication skill that involves paying close attention to the speaker and understanding their message. It requires the listener to be fully present and engaged in the conversation and possess certain traits, such as empathy, non-judgmentalness, open-mindedness, and patience. Good listeners also have the ability to ask clarifying questions and provide feedback to help ensure that they have accurately understood the speaker's message.
