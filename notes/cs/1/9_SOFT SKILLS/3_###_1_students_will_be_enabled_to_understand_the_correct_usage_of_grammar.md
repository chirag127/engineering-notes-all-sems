### 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

1. Understanding Correct Grammar Usage:

In this objective, students will learn the importance of using correct grammar in written and spoken communication. They will be taught the rules and conventions of grammar, including syntax, punctuation, and vocabulary, and how to apply them in a variety of contexts.

The focus will be on building a strong foundation in grammar, with an emphasis on common errors and how to avoid them. Students will learn to recognize and correct grammatical mistakes in their own writing and speech, and to communicate effectively and confidently in a range of situations.

By the end of this objective, students will have a good understanding of the rules of grammar and be able to apply them correctly in their own writing and speech. This will help them to communicate effectively and confidently, and to express their ideas and opinions in a clear and concise manner.
