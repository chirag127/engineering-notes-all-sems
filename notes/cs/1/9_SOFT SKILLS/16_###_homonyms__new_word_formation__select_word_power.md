### Homonyms, New word Formation, Select word power

Homonyms, New Word Formation, and Select Word Power:

1. Homonyms: Homonyms are words that are spelled and pronounced the same, but have different meanings. For example, "bass" can refer to a type of fish or a low-frequency sound.

2. New Word Formation: New words can be formed through various processes, such as combining existing words, using prefixes and suffixes, and borrowing words from other languages.

3. Select Word Power: Select word power refers to the ability to choose the right word to express a specific idea or concept. This is an important skill for effective communication and writing. To develop select word power, it is recommended to expand your vocabulary through reading, writing, and speaking. Additionally, understanding the nuances of word meanings and usage can help you make more informed word choices.
