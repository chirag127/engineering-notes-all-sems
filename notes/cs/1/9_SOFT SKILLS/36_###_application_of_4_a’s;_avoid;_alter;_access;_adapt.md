### Application of 4 A’s; Avoid; Alter; Access; Adapt

The 4 A's (Avoid, Alter, Access, Adapt) is a framework used to manage wildlife-human conflicts.

1. Avoid: This involves reducing the likelihood of conflicts by avoiding or minimizing human activities in areas where wildlife is known to occur.

2. Alter: This involves modifying human activities to reduce the likelihood of conflicts. For example, changing the timing of activities, modifying the design of structures, or using wildlife-friendly materials.

3. Access: This involves controlling access to areas where wildlife is present. For example, fencing, gates, or signs can be used to restrict human access.

4. Adapt: This involves adapting to the presence of wildlife and learning to live with it. This can include education and outreach programs, developing coexistence plans, and creating financial incentives for responsible wildlife management.
