### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

Business communication is an essential skill for managers to effectively convey information and ideas within and outside the organization. The book, "Business Communication for Managers" by Payal Mehra, published by Pearson in Delhi in 2012, is a comprehensive guide for managers to develop effective communication skills. The book covers the following topics:

1. Introduction to Business Communication
- Definition and importance of business communication
- Communication process
- Types of communication
- Barriers to communication

2. Written Communication
- Principles of effective written communication
- Types of written communication
- Writing effective emails, memos, letters, and reports
- Business writing style and tone
- Proofreading and editing

3. Oral Communication
- Principles of effective oral communication
- Types of oral communication
- Verbal and nonverbal communication
- Public speaking and presentations
- Active listening

4. Interpersonal Communication
- Communication in groups and teams
- Conflict resolution
- Negotiation and persuasion
- Cultural and gender differences in communication

5. Technology and Communication
- Use of technology in communication
- Social media and networking
- Virtual teams and remote communication
- Ethics and privacy in communication

6. Business Communication Strategy
- Developing a communication plan
- Audience analysis
- Message development and delivery
- Feedback and evaluation

"Business Communication for Managers" is a valuable resource for managers to improve their communication skills and enhance their professional success. The book provides practical tips, real-world examples, and exercises to help managers communicate effectively in various business situations.
