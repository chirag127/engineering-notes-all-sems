### Vocabulary for Its Application at Different Platforms and Through Numerous Modes

As individuals and businesses continue to communicate digitally, it is essential to have a strong grasp of the vocabulary used in various platforms and modes. Here are some key terms to know:

#### Email Communication

- Subject Line: The brief summary of the email's content that appears in the recipient's inbox.
- CC (Carbon Copy): A feature that allows the sender to add additional recipients to the email without requiring their direct response.
- BCC (Blind Carbon Copy): A feature that allows the sender to add additional recipients without revealing their identity to other recipients.
- Attachment: A file or document that is attached to the email for the recipient to view or download.

#### Social Media Communication

- Hashtag: A word or phrase preceded by the # symbol used to categorize and search for content related to a specific topic.
- Trending: Refers to a topic or hashtag that has gained significant popularity on social media at a particular time.
- Algorithm: The set of rules used by social media platforms to determine what content to show in a user's feed.
- Engagement: The interaction a user has with a post, including likes, comments, and shares.

#### Video and Voice Communication

- Bandwidth: The amount of data that can be transmitted over a network within a specific time frame.
- Resolution: The clarity and sharpness of an image or video.
- Codec: The software used to compress and decompress digital media files for transmission and storage.
- Latency: The time delay between sending and receiving data in a communication system.

#### Instant Messaging

- Emoji: A small pictogram used to express emotions or ideas in digital communication.
- Emoticon: A combination of keyboard characters used to represent facial expressions or emotions in text-based communication.
- Read Receipt: A feature that indicates when a message has been read by the recipient.
- Group Chat: A feature that allows multiple users to communicate in a single chat thread.

By mastering these terms and understanding their application across different platforms and modes, individuals and businesses can communicate more effectively and efficiently in the digital age.
