### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for

In today's world, effective communication is an essential skill that is required in almost every aspect of life. Whether it's personal or professional, being able to communicate effectively can make a huge difference. In this module, you will learn the basics of communication skills and how to apply them in your daily life.

Here are the key points that you will cover in this module:

1. Understanding the importance of communication: Communication is the foundation of all human interactions. It is essential to convey your message clearly and effectively to others. You will learn why communication is important and how it can impact your life.

2. Developing effective listening skills: Listening is an important aspect of communication. You will learn how to listen actively and effectively, how to ask questions, and how to respond appropriately.

3. Improving your verbal communication skills: Verbal communication involves speaking clearly and effectively. You will learn how to use appropriate language, tone, and volume when speaking to others.

4. Enhancing your non-verbal communication skills: Non-verbal communication involves body language, facial expressions, and gestures. You will learn how to use non-verbal cues to communicate effectively.

5. Mastering written communication skills: Written communication is essential in today's digital age. You will learn how to write effective emails, memos, and reports.

6. Applying communication skills in different contexts: Communication skills are required in various situations, such as interviews, meetings, and presentations. You will learn how to apply your communication skills in different contexts.

By the end of this module, you will have a solid understanding of the basics of communication skills and how to apply them in your daily life. You will be able to communicate more effectively with others, which will benefit you both personally and professionally.
