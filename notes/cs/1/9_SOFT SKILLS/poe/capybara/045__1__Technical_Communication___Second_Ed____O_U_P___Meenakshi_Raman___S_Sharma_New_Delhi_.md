### 1. Technical Communication, (Second Ed.); O.U.P., Meenakshi Raman & S.Sharma New Delhi

1. Technical communication is a means of conveying technical information or instructions to an audience in a clear, concise, and effective manner.

2. The book "Technical Communication" by Meenakshi Raman and S. Sharma provides a comprehensive understanding of technical communication, its principles, and practices.

3. The book covers the following topics:

   * The basics of technical communication, including its purpose and audience analysis.
   
   * The different types of technical documents, such as reports, proposals, manuals, and instructions, and how to create them.
   
   * The importance of style and tone in technical communication, and how to achieve clarity and coherence in writing.
   
   * The use of visuals, such as graphs, charts, tables, and diagrams, to enhance communication and understanding.
   
   * The role of technology in technical communication, including the use of software tools and online resources.
   
   * The ethical issues in technical communication, such as accuracy, honesty, and confidentiality.
   
   * The process of revising and editing technical documents, and the importance of peer review and feedback.
   
4. The book is written in a clear and concise language, making it easy to understand for readers with different levels of technical knowledge.

5. The book also provides numerous examples and exercises, which help readers to apply the principles and techniques of technical communication in practice.

6. Overall, "Technical Communication" is an essential resource for anyone who wants to improve their technical communication skills and create effective technical documents.
