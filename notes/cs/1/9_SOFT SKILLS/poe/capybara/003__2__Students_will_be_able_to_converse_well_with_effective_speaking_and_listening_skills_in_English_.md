### 2. Students will be able to converse well with effective speaking and listening skills in English.

In order to converse well in English, students must develop effective speaking and listening skills. Here are some key points to keep in mind:

- Speaking clearly and confidently is crucial to effective communication. Students should practice speaking English aloud as much as possible, paying attention to their pronunciation and intonation.
- Listening actively is just as important as speaking clearly. Students should make a conscious effort to listen attentively to others when they speak, asking questions and clarifying when necessary.
- Good communication involves more than just words. Students should also focus on their body language, eye contact, and tone of voice when speaking and listening.
- Vocabulary and grammar are important, but they are not the only factors in effective communication. Students should also be aware of cultural differences in communication styles and adapt accordingly.
- Practice makes perfect. Students should take every opportunity to practice their speaking and listening skills, whether in class, with peers, or with native speakers of English. Feedback from others can also be helpful in identifying areas for improvement.
