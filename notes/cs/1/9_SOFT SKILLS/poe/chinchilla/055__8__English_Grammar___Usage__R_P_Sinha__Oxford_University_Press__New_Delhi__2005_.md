### 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

English Grammar and Usage is a comprehensive guide on the rules of English grammar and how to use them effectively. Written by R.P. Sinha and published by Oxford University Press, this book is a valuable resource for students, teachers, and anyone looking to improve their English language skills. Here are some key points to keep in mind when studying this book:

1. Parts of speech: English Grammar and Usage covers the eight parts of speech - nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections. It explains the functions of each part of speech and how to use them correctly in sentences.

2. Sentence structure: The book also covers the structure of sentences, including subject-verb agreement, sentence types (declarative, interrogative, imperative, and exclamatory), and sentence fragments and run-on sentences.

3. Punctuation: Proper punctuation is essential for clear and effective communication in written English. This book covers punctuation marks such as commas, periods, semicolons, colons, apostrophes, quotation marks, and hyphens, and how to use them correctly.

4. Common errors: English Grammar and Usage also addresses common errors in English, such as using the wrong verb tense, confusing homophones (words that sound the same but have different meanings), and using incorrect word order.

5. Idioms and phrasal verbs: Idioms and phrasal verbs are an important part of English language usage. This book provides explanations and examples of common idioms and phrasal verbs, as well as how to use them correctly in context.

6. Writing skills: The book also includes tips for improving writing skills, such as how to write clear and concise sentences, how to organize ideas in paragraphs, and how to avoid common writing mistakes.

7. Exercises and practice: To reinforce the concepts covered in the book, English Grammar and Usage includes numerous exercises and practice activities. These exercises cover a range of topics, from basic grammar rules to more advanced concepts.

8. Additional resources: Finally, the book includes a list of additional resources for further study, including dictionaries, thesauruses, and grammar reference books.

In conclusion, English Grammar and Usage by R.P. Sinha is an excellent resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills. By studying the concepts covered in this book and completing the exercises and practice activities, readers can gain a solid understanding of English grammar and usage and become more effective communicators in both written and spoken English.
