### Minutes of Meeting

Minutes of Meeting (MoM) is a written record of discussions, decisions, and actions taken during a meeting. It is an official document that serves as a reference for participants and non-participants of the meeting.

Here are some key points to keep in mind when preparing Minutes of Meeting:

1. Purpose: Clearly state the purpose of the meeting at the beginning of the MoM. This helps readers understand the context of the discussion and decisions made during the meeting.

2. Attendees: List the names of attendees and their roles in the meeting. This helps readers identify who was present and who was absent.

3. Agenda: Include a brief summary of the agenda items discussed during the meeting. This helps readers understand the topics covered during the meeting.

4. Discussion: Provide a summary of the discussion that took place during the meeting. This should include key points raised by participants, any arguments or debates that occurred, and any questions that were asked.

5. Decisions: Record the decisions that were made during the meeting. This should include the outcome of any votes taken, any resolutions that were passed, and any action items that were assigned.

6. Action Items: List the action items that were assigned during the meeting. This should include the person responsible for each action item and the deadline for completion. 

7. Next Steps: Provide a brief summary of the next steps that will be taken after the meeting. This helps readers understand what to expect in the future.

8. Review and Approval: Once the MoM is complete, it should be reviewed and approved by the participants of the meeting. This ensures that the MoM accurately reflects the discussion and decisions made during the meeting.

In conclusion, Minutes of Meeting are an important tool for keeping track of discussions, decisions, and actions taken during a meeting. By following the key points outlined above, you can ensure that your MoM is comprehensive, accurate, and effective.
