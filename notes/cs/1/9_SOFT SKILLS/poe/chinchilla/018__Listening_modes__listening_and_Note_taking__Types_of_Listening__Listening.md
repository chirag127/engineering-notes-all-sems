### Listening Modes:

1. Active Listening: This is a mode of listening where the listener is fully engaged in the conversation or presentation. The listener pays attention to the speaker, asks questions, and gives feedback. This mode of listening helps the listener to retain information and build relationships.

2. Passive Listening: In this mode of listening, the listener is not fully engaged in the conversation or presentation. The listener may be distracted or not paying attention. This mode of listening may result in the listener missing important information.

### Listening and Note Taking:

1. Listening and note-taking are important skills for students and professionals.

2. Effective note-taking involves identifying key points and organizing information in a logical manner.

3. The Cornell Method is a popular note-taking technique that involves dividing the page into three sections: notes, cues, and summary.

4. When listening, it is important to focus on the speaker's main points, take notes, and ask questions for clarification.

### Types of Listening:

1. Discriminative Listening: This type of listening involves distinguishing different sounds and identifying their meanings.

2. Comprehensive Listening: This type of listening involves understanding the meaning of the message being conveyed.

3. Critical Listening: This type of listening involves evaluating the message being conveyed and analyzing it for accuracy and validity.

4. Empathetic Listening: This type of listening involves understanding the speaker's feelings and emotions.

### Listening:

1. Listening is an important skill for effective communication.

2. It is important to be an active listener, paying attention to the speaker and providing feedback.

3. Effective listening involves asking questions, summarizing what has been said, and providing feedback.

4. Listening skills can be developed through practice and training.
