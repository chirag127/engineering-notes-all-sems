### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation;

Listening to TED Talks is an excellent way to improve your English language skills, especially when it comes to pronunciation. Here are some techniques you can use to make the most of your TED Talk audio listening with script reading:

1. Listen to the audio first: Before you start reading the script, listen to the audio of the TED Talk. Focus on the pronunciation and intonation of the speaker, and try to imitate it.

2. Read along with the script: Once you have listened to the audio, read along with the script. This will help you to improve your reading skills and comprehension.

3. Record yourself: After listening to the audio and reading along with the script, record yourself reading the script. This will help you to identify any areas where you need to improve your pronunciation.

4. Listen and repeat: Listen to a sentence or phrase in the TED Talk and repeat it several times until you feel comfortable with the pronunciation.

5. Break down difficult words: If you come across a difficult word in the script, break it down into syllables and practice pronouncing each syllable separately.

6. Practice stress and intonation: Pay attention to the stress and intonation of the speaker, and try to imitate it. This will help you to sound more natural when speaking English.

7. Use subtitles: If you are having difficulty following the TED Talk, turn on the subtitles. This will help you to improve your listening and reading skills.

8. Repeat the process: Repeat the process with different TED Talks to improve your pronunciation and listening skills.

Remember to be patient and persistent when practicing these techniques. Improving your pronunciation and listening skills takes time and practice. By using these techniques, you will be on your way to speaking English more fluently and confidently.
