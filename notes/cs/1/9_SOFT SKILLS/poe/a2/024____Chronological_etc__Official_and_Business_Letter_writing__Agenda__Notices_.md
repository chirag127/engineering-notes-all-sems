### Chronological Order

### Official and Business Letter Writing

1. Maintain a formal and professional tone.
2. Include a proper salutation (Dear Mr./Ms. Last Name).
3. State the purpose of the letter clearly in the first paragraph.
4. Use a straightforward and concise writing style.
5. Close with a polite and professional sign-off (Sincerely, Best regards).

### Agenda

1. List the topics or items to be discussed or acted upon.
2. Organize the topics in a logical order.
3. Provide enough detail for attendees to understand the purpose and expected outcomes.
4. Circulate the agenda in advance of a meeting.
5. Indicate the time allotted for each topic.

### Notices

1. Inform or alert readers about important information.
2. Written in a concise and straightforward style.
3. Include key details: who, what, when, where, why, how.
4. Distributed through appropriate channels to reach the intended audience.
5. May include an RSVP or call-to-action.
