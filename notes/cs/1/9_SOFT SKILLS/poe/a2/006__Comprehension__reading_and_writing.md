### Comprehension, reading and writing.

1. Reading comprehension:
- Read the given text thoroughly to understand the theme, main ideas, characters, plot, etc.
- Analyze and interpret the text to get the underlying meaning.
- Infer the implications and draw conclusions.
- Gauge the tone and mood of the author.

2. Speed reading:
- Minimize subvocalization (saying or hearing words in your head).
- Remove regressions (re-reading parts of the text).
- Increase peripheral vision and decrease fixation (avoid re-reading lines or words).
- Read in chunks (groups of words or lines) rather than individual words.

3. Note-taking:
- Capture key terms and main ideas.
- Use abbreviations and symbols.
- Organize notes in a logical flow or outline form.
- Review and rewrite notes within 24 hours to retain information.

4. Writing:
- Have a clear theme or thesis for your writing.
- Structure your content in a logical flow with an introduction, body, and conclusion.
- Use simple and clear language.
- Provide examples and evidence to support your key points.
- proofread thoroughly to check for any errors or typos.
