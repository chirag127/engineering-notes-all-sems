## Prescribed Books:

1. Physics - HC Verma
This book covers the complete Physics syllabus in a comprehensive yet easy to understand manner. It contains solved examples with detailed solutions to help students understand concepts better. The chapters contain a good number of unsolved questions for practice.

2. Chemistry - NCERT
NCERT Chemistry books are the most recommended books for studying Chemistry for board exams and entrance exams like JEE and NEET. The books are written in a simple language with proper explanations of concepts and examples. They cover the complete Chemistry syllabus.

3. Mathematics - RD Sharma
RD Sharma Class 11 and 12 books for Mathematics cover all topics as per the latest CBSE syllabus. The books contain solved examples with step-by-step solutions to help students understand concepts and formulas easily. A large number of practice questions are given at the end of each chapter.

4. Biology - NCERT
Like Chemistry, NCERT Biology books are the most recommended books for studying Biology. They cover the latest CBSE syllabus in a simple and easy to understand language with diagrams and examples. The books help build a strong foundation of biological concepts and theories for entrance and board exams.
