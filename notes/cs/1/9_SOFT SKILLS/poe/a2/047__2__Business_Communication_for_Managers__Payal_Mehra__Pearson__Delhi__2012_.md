### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

1. Effective communication is key to managerial effectiveness. Managers spend a large portion of their time communicating.
2. Barriers to effective communication must be overcome. Some key barriers are:
- Information overload: Too much information can lead to confusion and poor comprehension.
- Language: Use of complex terminology or jargon can create barriers. Communication should be in simple and clear language.
- Cultural differences: Cultural norms influence communication style and interpretation of messages. Managers must understand cultural differences and communicate in a style appropriate for the audience.
3. Effective communication involves both the transmission of information and the receiving and understanding of the message. Managers must tailor the message based on the audience and ensure there is proper feedback and understanding.
4. Different communication media have different strengths and weaknesses. Managers must choose the appropriate medium based on factors like urgency, importance, and audience. Oral communication is best for dialogue but written communication provides a permanent record.
5. Listening is an important part of communication. Managers must practice active listening to fully understand problems and the needs of team members. They should provide their full attention, avoid distractions, and use affirmations to confirm understanding.
