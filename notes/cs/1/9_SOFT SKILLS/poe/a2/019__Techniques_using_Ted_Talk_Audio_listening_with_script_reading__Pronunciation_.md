### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation;

1. Listen to a Ted Talk audio with script in hand:
- Listen to the audio once without the script to get the overall flow and content of the talk.
- Then listen again with the script in hand to connect the speech with the written text. This helps in learning pronunciation, pace, and emphasis.
- Try to imitate the speaker's style of presenting the content. Observe the pauses and tones to get the actual emotion and impact.

2. Listen and repeat challenging words or sentences:
- Note down words or sentences that are difficult to understand or pronounce.
- Listen to that part of the audio again and again and repeat after the speaker until you get it right.
- This technique is useful in learning the correct pronunciation and articulation of words and phrases.

3. Re-create the talk:
- After listening to the complete audio a couple of times, try to re-create the talk in your own words.
- Present it out loud in front of a mirror or to a friend.
- This helps in grasping the concept thoroughly and gaining confidence in speaking.

4. Watch the video of the talk:
- Watching the original video of the Ted talk adds another layer of understanding.
- You can see the body language, visuals, and presentation techniques used by the speaker.
- Comparatively analyze your re-creation of the talk with the original to further enhance your learning.
