### Active Listening :Meaning and Art of Listening, Traits of a Good Listener,

1. **Meaning**: Active listening is a communication technique where the listener fully concentrates on what the speaker is saying and understands their message. The listener maintains eye contact, does not interrupt the speaker, and listens to understand rather than just reply.

2. **Art of Listening**: The art of active listening involves:
- Maintaining eye contact: Looking at the speaker shows you are engaged and interested.
- Avoiding distractions: Minimize distractions like phones and focus on the speaker.
- Listening to understand: Listen to understand the speaker's perspective and message rather than just reply.
- Avoiding interruptions: Do not interrupt the speaker or finish their sentences. Allow them to speak freely and communicate their thoughts.
- Providing feedback: Nod or say "uh-huh" to show you are listening. You can also summarize what the speaker said to confirm your understanding.

3. **Traits of a Good Listener**: The traits of a good active listener are:
- Patience: Listen for as long as the speaker wants to talk without rushing them.
- Open-mindedness: Be receptive to the speaker's thoughts and opinions even if they differ from your own.
- Focus: Focus your attention on the speaker and avoid distractions.
- Respect: Respect the speaker's time to speak and communicate their message.
- Non-judgmental: Avoid criticizing or passing judgments on the speaker or their message.
- Responsiveness: Provide appropriate verbal and non-verbal feedback that you are listening.
