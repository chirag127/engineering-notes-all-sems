### Proxemics, Chronemics, and Presentation Strategies: planning, preparation,

1. Proxemics: The study of how people use physical space and distance in various settings. For presentations, be aware of intimate, personal, social, and public zones and respect the audience's comfort with distance. Plan your movement and use of stage space accordingly.

2. Chronemics: The study of how people perceive and structure time. For presentations, be punctual and mindful of audience members' time. Prepare with several options and paths through your material so that you can adapt to the time available.

3. Presentation strategies: Preparation is key to an effective presentation. Practice your delivery, know your material thoroughly, and plan the following:

- Opening: Start strong to engage the audience. Preview key points.
- Transition sentences: Provide signposts to keep the audience oriented in your material.
- Visual aids: Use slides, handouts, or other aids to enhance, not distract or duplicate, your verbal message. Practice with the technology ahead of time.
- Delivery: Speak clearly and with varied pace and tone to maintain interest. Show confidence through eye contact, stance, and voice. Keep within time limits.
- Closing: Summarize key points and end with a strong statement. Thank the audience for their attention.
