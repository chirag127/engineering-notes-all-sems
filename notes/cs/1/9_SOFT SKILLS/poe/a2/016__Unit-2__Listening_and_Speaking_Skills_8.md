### Unit-2: Listening and Speaking Skills 8

1. Listen actively: Pay attention to the speaker and the context. Make eye contact, don't interrupt, and listen to understand rather than just reply.
2. Take notes: Note down key points to remember information and to follow up with questions later. Focus on jotting down main ideas rather than trying to write word-for-word.
3. Ask questions: Ask questions to clarify information and show interest. Frame open-ended questions to encourage a conversation, not just a short reply.
4. Share information: Speak clearly and with confidence. Share the relevant information in an organized manner. Highlight key points and summarize when possible.
5. Discuss and collaborate: Share your perspective and acknowledge others' perspectives. Build on others' ideas and come up with solutions collectively in a respectful manner. Compromise when needed.

The points summarize key strategies to develop strong listening and speaking skills which are essential to effective communication. Actively listening, taking notes, asking questions, sharing information clearly, and collaborating with others are skills that can be practiced and improved consistently.
