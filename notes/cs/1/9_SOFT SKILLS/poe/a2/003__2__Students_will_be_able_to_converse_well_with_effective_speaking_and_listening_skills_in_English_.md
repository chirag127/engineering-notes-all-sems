### 2. Students will be able to converse well with effective speaking and listening skills in English.

- Speaking: Students will be able to:
-- Express opinions and ideas clearly and coherently
-- Share relevant personal experiences and anecdotes to convey the message effectively
-- Use appropriate body language and tone of voice for different types of conversations
-- Ask questions and engage in small talk to keep the conversation going

- Listening: Students will be able to:
-- Pay attention to the speaker and maintain eye contact
-- Avoid interrupting the speaker and allow them to finish
-- Provide appropriate verbal and non-verbal feedback like nodding or saying "uh-huh"
-- Summarize and reflect on what the speaker said to confirm understanding
-- Respond appropriately based on the context and tone of the conversation

Effective communication requires both speaking and listening skills to have a constructive dialogue. Students will practice both skills through various classroom activities to improve their ability to have fluent and meaningful conversations in English. Regular practice and feedback will enable students to apply these skills in real-world situations.
