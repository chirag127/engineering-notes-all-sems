### 3. Students will be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional skills:

1. Communication Skills: Strong verbal and written communication skills are essential for any professional. Students should work on their communication skills by participating in debates, discussions, and strengthening their vocabulary.

2. Problem-Solving Skills: Professionals are often faced with complex problems that need to be solved. Students can develop analytical and critical thinking skills through working on case studies, puzzles, and logic-based activities.

3. Teamwork Skills: Most professional work is done in teams. Students should participate in team projects and activities to learn how to collaborate, delegate, and work with others to achieve a common goal.

4. Technical Skills: Specific to each profession are the technical skills required. Students should keep themselves up-to-date with the latest tools and skills of their chosen professions to be industry-ready.

5. Domain Knowledge: A strong understanding of the subject matter or industry domain is key to being a professional. Students must build a solid foundation of knowledge in their area of study to apply in their future professions.
