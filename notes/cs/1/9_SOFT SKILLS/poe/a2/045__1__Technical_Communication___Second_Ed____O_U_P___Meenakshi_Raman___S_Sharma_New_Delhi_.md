### 1. Technical Communication, (Second Ed.); O.U.P., Meenakshi Raman & S.Sharma New Delhi,

1. Technical communication refers to a broad range of documents and processes that convey technical information.
2. The core elements of technical communication include:
- Instructions: Step-by-step explanations of how to do something.
- Proposals: Documents that persuade someone to take a particular course of action.
- Reports: Documents that present an analysis of data or observations to convey information.
3. Technical writing emphasizes clarity, concision, and logical organization. It is written in an impersonal tone and avoids emotive language.
4. Important characteristics of technical writing include:
- Accuracy: Information is factually correct and verifiable.
- Clarity: Ideas are expressed simply and unambiguously.
- Brevity: Only essential information is included, avoiding unnecessary words.
- Structure: Content follows a logical flow and format.
5. The technical writing process involves:
- Understanding the purpose and audience
- Collecting necessary information
- Organizing the information
- Writing a draft
- Revising for clarity and concision
- Editing
- Reviewing and testing (if applicable)
