## Content Contact

1. Content contact refers to the ways in which readers interact with and access content. This includes:
- Reading - Reading the content either online or in print format.
- Downloading - Downloading content such as PDFs, ebooks, slideshows, etc. for reading offline.
- Viewing - Viewing images, videos, infographics, slideshows, etc.
- Listening - Listening to audio content such as podcasts.

2. Types of content contact:
- Self-paced - Readers access and interact with content on their own schedule.
- Scheduled - Content is released on a set schedule, e.g. weekly podcast episodes or online courses with weekly modules.
- Synchronous - Readers access content simultaneously, e.g. live webinars or events.
- Asynchronous - Readers access content on-demand on their own timeline. Most content is asynchronous.

3. The ways in which readers can access content impacts the type of content to create and how to distribute it. Consider the formats and channels that best suit your content and audience. Maximizing content contact opportunities can increase readership and engagement.
