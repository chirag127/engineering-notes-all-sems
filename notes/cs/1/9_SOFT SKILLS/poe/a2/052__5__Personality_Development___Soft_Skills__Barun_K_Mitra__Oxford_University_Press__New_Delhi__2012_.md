### 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

- Present yourself with confidence: Stand tall, make eye contact, smile, greet others with enthusiasm. Develop a firm handshake.
- Develop communication skills: Listen actively, speak clearly and with confidence, be an engaged conversationalist.
- Build interpersonal skills: Be friendly and positive, develop your emotional intelligence, collaborate and work well with others, resolve conflicts effectively.
- Enhance your motivation and attitude: Set goals, reward yourself, develop positive beliefs and attitudes, maintain a positive body language and self-talk.
- Adaptability and managing change: Learn to respond to change and uncertainty, think of challenges as opportunities, update your skills and be open to new ideas.
- Continuous self-improvement: Develop a regular habit of reflection, seek feedback, pursue continuous learning. Keep a positive outlook and never stop trying.
