### Speaking style ; content and sequencing

1. Be concise and avoid unnecessary words or rambling
- Organize your thoughts before speaking
- Present key points first followed by explanations and examples

2. Use a clear and simple language
- Avoid complex words or jargon unless required for the audience and topic
- Present one idea at a time and logically sequence them
- Repeat key points for emphasis and to aid retention

3. Include relevant data, facts, statistics, quotes and examples
- Facts and data help to strengthen arguments and make presentations credible
- Apt quotes or examples enhance engagement and make the content relatable or interesting

4. Modulate your tone, pitch and pace for a compelling delivery
- Use pauses and vary pace for effect, to highlight key points or allow time for reflections
- Show enthusiasm or emotion if appropriate for the topic and audience
- Maintain eye contact and use hand gestures naturally to connect with the audience

5. Close with a key summary or call to action if required
- Summarize main points and key takeaways for the audience
- End with a concluding statement or call to action if appropriate to the topic and goals of the presentation
