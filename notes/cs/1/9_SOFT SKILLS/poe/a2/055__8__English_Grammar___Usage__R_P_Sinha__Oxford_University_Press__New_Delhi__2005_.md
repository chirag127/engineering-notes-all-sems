### 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

1. The book covers various aspects of English grammar in a systematic and structured manner. It starts with the basic concepts of grammar like parts of speech, phrases and clauses.
2. It then moves on to cover tense, aspect, modality, voice, mood and nominals. The book explains each grammar concept with examples and clear explanations.
3. The book also provides numerous exercises for practice to help the reader understand and apply the concepts.
4. The language used is simple and easy to understand. The book can be useful for learners preparing for competitive exams as well as students studying in schools and colleges.
5. Some key points covered in the book:
- Parts of speech: Nouns, pronouns, adjectives, verbs, adverbs, prepositions, conjunctions, interjections.
- Phrases and clauses: Noun phrases, verb phrases, adjective phrases, adverb phrases, subordinate clauses, relative clauses.
- Tenses: Present, past, future, present perfect, past perfect, future perfect.
- Aspects: Perfective, imperfective, continuous, habitual.
- Voice: Active voice, passive voice.
- Mood: Indicative, imperative, subjunctive.
