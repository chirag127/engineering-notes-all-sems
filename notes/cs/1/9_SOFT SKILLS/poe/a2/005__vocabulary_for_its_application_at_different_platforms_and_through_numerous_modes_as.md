### Vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as:

1. Reading comprehension: A wide range of vocabulary helps in understanding the written content thoroughly. It aids in grasping the meaning of sentences and paragraphs in a better way.

2. Communication: An enriched vocabulary supports in communicating thoughts and ideas effectively. It helps in articulating opinions and conveying messages aptly.

3. Personality development: The choice of words reflects one's personality. An impressive and impactful vocabulary enhances one's personality and creates a good impression.

4. Career growth: A strong vocabulary is essential for career development and success in professional life. It is necessary to achieve milestones and accomplish goals at work.

5. Scoring high: An extensive vocabulary is critical for scoring well in examinations, especially in verbal ability tests, essay writing, and interviews. It makes a significant impact on performance.

In summary, vocabulary is fundamental for progress and prosperity in all walks of life. Continuous learning and application of new words can boost vocabulary power. A repertoire of appropriate and articulate words is key to expressing oneself distinctively and making a difference.
