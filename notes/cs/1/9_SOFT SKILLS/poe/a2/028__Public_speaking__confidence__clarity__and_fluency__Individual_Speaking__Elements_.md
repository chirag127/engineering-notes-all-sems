### Public speaking: confidence, clarity, and fluency, Individual Speaking: Elements;

1. Confidence
- Believe in yourself and your message
- Prepare thoroughly
- Focus on your message, not your anxiety
- Visualize yourself succeeding

2. Clarity
- Have a clear theme or message
- Structure your talk logically
- Use simple language
- Provide examples and stories to illustrate your key points

3. Fluency
- Know your material inside and out
- Practice out loud, ideally in front of others
- Speak slowly and pause occasionally for effect
- Use vocal variety to keep audience engaged: change pace, pause, alter tone, etc.
