### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for:

1. Listening: Student will learn how to listen actively by maintaining eye contact, not interrupting the speaker and paraphrasing what the speaker said to confirm understanding.

2. Speaking: Student will learn how to speak clearly and confidently by organizing thoughts before speaking, using consistent volume and pace and using language appropriate for the audience and situation.

3. Writing: Student will learn how to write in a clear, concise and organized manner by structuring content logically, using simple and concise language and adjusting tone and style as per the audience and purpose.

4. Presenting: Student will learn how to engage audiences through effective presentations by using slides appropriately, speaking conversationally and confidently and addressing the key points that align with the presentation goals.

The skills of communication will help students in their professional and personal lives to connect, convey and collaborate effectively. With regular practice of the techniques learned, students can overcome communication challenges and become compelling communicators.
