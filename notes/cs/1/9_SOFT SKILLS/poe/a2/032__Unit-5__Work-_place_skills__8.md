### Unit-5: Work- place skills: 8

8. Communication skills:

- Avoid being too casual or informal. Maintain a professional demeanor.
- Listen actively and pay attention to body language and tone.
- Be concise and clear. Avoid unnecessary words and complex sentences.
- Share information proactively that impacts work or team members.
- Provide constructive feedback politely and respectfully.
- Adapt communication style based on audience and medium (email vs phone vs in-person).

9. Problem-solving skills:

- Identify the root cause of problems and not just symptoms. Ask probing questions.
- Brainstorm solutions and evaluate pros and cons of each.
- Do not get attached to solutions too quickly. Remain objective.
- Seek input from others to get different perspectives.
- Take a systematic approach to solving complex problems. Break down into smaller steps.
- Take action and follow through to resolution.

10. Critical thinking skills:

- Question assumptions and status quo. Do not accept things at face value.
- Analyze situations methodically, looking at facts and data.
- Identify logical flaws or biases in arguments or reasoning.
- Consider alternate possibilities and perspectives.
- Make balanced judgments based on a multidimensional evaluation of the situation.
- Draw insights and inferences that go beyond obvious or initial conclusions.
