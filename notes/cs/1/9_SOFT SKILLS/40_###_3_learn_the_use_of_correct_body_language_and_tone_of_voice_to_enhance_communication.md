### 3. Learn the use of correct body language and tone of voice to enhance communication.

Body Language and Tone of Voice in Communication:
1. Posture: upright, open stance conveys confidence, while slouching or crossing arms can indicate disinterest or defensiveness.
2. Eye Contact: maintaining eye contact shows interest and engagement, while avoiding eye contact can indicate dishonesty or discomfort.
3. Gestures: appropriate gestures can emphasize a point or show agreement, while inappropriate gestures can distract or undermine the message.
4. Facial Expressions: facial expressions can reflect emotions and help convey the message, while a neutral or blank expression can indicate disinterest or confusion.
5. Tone of Voice: tone of voice can convey emotions and emphasis, while monotone or inconsistent tone can bore or confuse the listener.
6. Volume: appropriate volume can make the message clear and audible, while too loud or too soft volume can be distracting or difficult to hear.
7. Pace: appropriate pace can keep the listener engaged, while too fast or too slow pace can bore or confuse the listener.
