### Responding; Mental health at work place: Managing Stress; Techniques:

Responding to mental health in the workplace involves creating a supportive environment for employees. Managing stress is a key aspect of this, as stress can significantly impact an individual's mental health. 

Techniques for managing stress at work include: 
1. Time management and prioritization 
2. Exercise and physical activity 
3. Mindfulness and relaxation 
4. Good sleep habits 
5. Healthy eating 
6. Seeking support from colleagues or a mental health professional 
7. Setting realistic goals and boundaries 
8. Taking time off when needed 
9. Engaging in enjoyable activities outside of work 
10. Seeking help from an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) if available.
