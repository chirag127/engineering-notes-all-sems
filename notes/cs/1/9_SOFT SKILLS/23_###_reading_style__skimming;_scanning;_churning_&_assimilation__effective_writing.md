### Reading style: Skimming; Scanning; Churning & Assimilation, Effective writing

Reading style:
1. Skimming: Quickly scanning text to get a general idea of content. 
2. Scanning: Reading text to find specific information. 
3. Churning: Active reading to understand and retain information. 
4. Assimilation: Integrating new information with existing knowledge. 

Effective writing:
1. Clear and concise language. 
2. Well-organized structure. 
3. Appropriate tone and style. 
4. Active voice. 
5. Evidence-based arguments.
