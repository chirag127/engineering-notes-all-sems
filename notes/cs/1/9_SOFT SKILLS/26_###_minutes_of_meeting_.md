### Minutes of meeting,

Minutes of meeting is a written document that records the proceedings of a meeting. It includes a summary of the discussions, decisions, and action items agreed upon during the meeting. The purpose of minutes of meeting is to provide a record of what was discussed and agreed upon during the meeting, and to ensure that everyone is on the same page.

1. Preparation: Minutes of meeting should be prepared as soon as possible after the meeting, while the details are still fresh in the minds of the participants. The person responsible for preparing the minutes should have a clear understanding of the meeting's objectives, agenda, and discussions.

2. Content: Minutes of meeting should include the following information:
- Date, time, and location of the meeting
- Names of attendees and absentees
- Agenda items and discussion points
- Decisions and action items agreed upon
- Any other significant information discussed during the meeting

3. Format: Minutes of meeting can be prepared in a variety of formats, including bullet points, paragraph form, or a table. The format should be clear, concise, and easy to read.

4. Approval: Minutes of meeting should be reviewed and approved by the participants at the next meeting. This ensures that the minutes accurately reflect the proceedings of the meeting and that everyone is in agreement with the decisions and action items agreed upon.

5. Distribution: Minutes of meeting should be distributed to all participants, as well as any other relevant stakeholders. This ensures that everyone has access to the information discussed during the meeting and can take action on the agreed-upon decisions and action items.

In conclusion, minutes of meeting is a written document that records the proceedings of a meeting. It is an important tool for ensuring that everyone is on the same page and that the decisions and action items agreed upon during the meeting are carried out.
