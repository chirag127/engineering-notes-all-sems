### 3. Students will be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional

networks through internships and co-op programs.

This objective focuses on helping students build a strong professional network. This can be achieved through participating in internships and co-op programs. These programs allow students to work with professionals in their field, gain hands-on experience, and build relationships with people in the industry. By doing so, students can create a substantial base of professional contacts that can be valuable in their future careers. This can lead to job opportunities, mentorship, and access to industry insights and information. Building a professional network is a critical aspect of career development and can help students achieve their long-term goals.
