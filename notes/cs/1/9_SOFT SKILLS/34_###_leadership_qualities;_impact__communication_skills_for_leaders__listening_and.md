### Leadership qualities; Impact, Communication skills for Leaders: Listening and

Leadership qualities:
1. Visionary: ability to see the big picture and chart a course for the future.
2. Adaptable: ability to change direction and approach when circumstances demand it.
3. Decisive: ability to make tough decisions quickly and confidently.
4. Empathetic: ability to understand and respond to the needs and emotions of others.
5. Ethical: ability to act with integrity and transparency.

Impact:
1. Inspiring: ability to motivate and engage others towards a common goal.
2. Transformative: ability to bring about positive change in people and organizations.
3. Innovative: ability to introduce new ideas and approaches to solve problems.

Communication skills:
1. Listening: ability to actively listen and understand others' perspectives.
2. Articulate: ability to clearly and effectively communicate ideas and messages.
3. Persuasive: ability to convince others to support a course of action.
4. Inclusive: ability to engage and involve diverse perspectives in decision making.
