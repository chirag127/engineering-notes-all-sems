### 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

5. Personality Development & Soft Skills by Barun K. Mitra:

The book "Personality Development & Soft Skills" by Barun K. Mitra is a comprehensive guide to improving one's personal and professional skills. The book was published by Oxford University Press in New Delhi in 2012.

The book covers a range of topics related to personality development and soft skills, including communication skills, time management, leadership, and teamwork. The author provides practical advice and exercises to help readers develop and refine these skills, and to become more confident and effective in both personal and professional settings.

In addition to the practical tips and exercises, the book also includes case studies and examples to illustrate the concepts and techniques discussed. This makes the book a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their personality and soft skills, whether for personal or professional development.

Overall, "Personality Development & Soft Skills" by Barun K. Mitra is a comprehensive guide to improving one's personal and professional skills. The book provides practical advice, exercises, and case studies to help readers develop and refine their skills, and to become more confident and effective in both personal and professional settings.
