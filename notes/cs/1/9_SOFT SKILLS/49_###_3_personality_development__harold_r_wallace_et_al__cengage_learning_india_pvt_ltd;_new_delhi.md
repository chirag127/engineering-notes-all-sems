### 3. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace et. al, Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd; New Delhi

"Personality Development" by Harold R. Wallace et. al is a book published by Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd in New Delhi. The book focuses on the study of personality and its development. It provides an overview of the theories and concepts related to personality and how they can be applied to improve personal growth and development. The book covers various aspects of personality, including traits, motivation, emotions, and social behavior. It provides practical tips and techniques for enhancing personal skills and abilities, such as communication, leadership, and problem-solving. The book is aimed at students, professionals, and anyone interested in improving their personality and overall well-being.
