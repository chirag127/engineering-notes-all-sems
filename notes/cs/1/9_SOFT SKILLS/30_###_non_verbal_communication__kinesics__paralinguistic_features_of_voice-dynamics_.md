### Non verbal Communication: Kinesics, Paralinguistic features of Voice-Dynamics,

Nonverbal Communication:
1. Kinesics: refers to body language and gestures, such as posture, facial expressions, and hand movements.
2. Paralinguistic features of Voice-Dynamics: refers to nonverbal cues in speech, such as tone, pitch, volume, and rhythm.
3. Eye contact: refers to the amount of eye contact made during communication, which can convey interest, confidence, or power.
4. Touch: refers to physical contact, such as a handshake or hug, which can convey affection, comfort, or aggression.
5. Proximity: refers to the physical distance between individuals during communication, which can convey intimacy, dominance, or discomfort.
6. Appearance: refers to clothing, grooming, and personal style, which can convey status, professionalism, or personality.
7. Time: refers to the use of time during communication, such as punctuality or duration of conversation, which can convey respect, interest, or impatience.
