### Listening modes, listening and Note taking, Types of Listening, Listening

Listening modes: 
1. Active listening: involves paying close attention and engaging with the speaker. 
2. Passive listening: involves passively hearing the speaker without much engagement. 
3. Selective listening: involves focusing on specific parts of the message. 

Listening and note-taking: 
Note-taking helps to improve listening by allowing the listener to focus on key information and retain it better. 

Types of Listening: 
1. Informational listening: involves listening to gather information. 
2. Critical listening: involves evaluating and analyzing the information being heard. 
3. Empathetic listening: involves understanding and relating to the speaker's feelings. 

Listening: 
Listening is the process of receiving and comprehending spoken or auditory information. It is an important communication skill that helps in building relationships, solving problems, and making decisions.
