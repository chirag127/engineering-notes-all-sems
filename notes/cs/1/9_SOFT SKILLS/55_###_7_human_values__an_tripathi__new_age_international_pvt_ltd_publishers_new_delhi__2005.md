### 7. Human Values, A.N. Tripathi, New Age International Pvt. Ltd. Publishers New Delhi, 2005.

Human Values: A.N. Tripathi's book "Human Values" (New Age International Pvt. Ltd. Publishers, New Delhi, 2005) is a comprehensive guide to understanding the values that guide human behavior. The book covers various aspects of human values, including their definition, classification, and origin. It also discusses the role of values in shaping individual and social behavior, and how they can be used to promote ethical and moral conduct. The author provides a detailed analysis of the different types of values, such as personal, social, cultural, and universal values, and how they influence human behavior. The book concludes with a discussion of the importance of values in shaping the future of society and the role of education in promoting human values. Overall, the book provides a comprehensive overview of the topic of human values and is a valuable resource for anyone interested in understanding the role of values in shaping human behavior.
