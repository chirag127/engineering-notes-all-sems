### 2. Demonstrate active listening with comprehension, and the ability to write clear and wellstructured emails and proposals.

Active Listening with Comprehension:
1. Pay attention to speaker: focus on the speaker's words, tone, and body language.
2. Avoid distractions: minimize distractions like phone or computer use.
3. Ask questions: clarify information and show interest in the speaker's message.
4. Paraphrase: restate the speaker's words to ensure understanding.
5. Provide feedback: nod, smile, or use other non-verbal cues to show engagement.

Clear and Well-Structured Emails and Proposals:
1. Define the purpose: clearly state the purpose of the email or proposal.
2. Use a professional tone: avoid slang, abbreviations, and emoticons.
3. Be concise: use short, simple sentences and bullet points.
4. Use a clear structure: use headings, subheadings, and bullet points to organize information.
5. Proofread: check for spelling and grammar errors before sending.
6. Personalize: address the recipient by name and tailor the content to their needs.
7. Provide clear instructions: specify actions required from the recipient.
8. Include relevant attachments: attach supporting documents or files.
