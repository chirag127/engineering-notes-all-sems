### Minutes of meeting

- Minutes of meeting are a written record of the main points and decisions that were discussed and agreed upon in a meeting.
- Minutes of meeting serve as a reference for the participants and other stakeholders who were not present in the meeting, as well as a reminder of the actions and responsibilities that were assigned.
- Minutes of meeting should include the following information:
  - The date, time, and location of the meeting
  - The names and roles of the attendees and the absentees
  - The agenda of the meeting and the objectives
  - The summary of the discussion and the outcomes for each agenda item
  - The action items and the deadlines for each task
  - The next steps and the date of the next meeting
- Minutes of meeting should be clear, concise, and accurate, and should avoid personal opinions, irrelevant details, and ambiguous language.
- Minutes of meeting should be prepared and circulated as soon as possible after the meeting, preferably within 24 hours, to ensure that the information is fresh and accurate.
- Minutes of meeting should be reviewed and approved by the chairperson or the facilitator of the meeting, and then distributed to all the participants and other relevant parties.
- Minutes of meeting should be stored and archived in a secure and accessible location, and should be referred to and updated in the subsequent meetings.
