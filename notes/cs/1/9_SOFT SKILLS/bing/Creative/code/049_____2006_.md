### 2006

2006 was a year that marked many significant events in the world, such as:

- **Politics and conflicts**: President George W. Bush signed the USA Patriot Act into law, extending the government's authority to combat terrorism. Democrats gained control of both houses of Congress during the midterm elections, ending 12 years of Republican dominance. Saddam Hussein, the former dictator of Iraq, was executed by hanging after being convicted of crimes against humanity . North Korea conducted its first nuclear test, defying international sanctions and warnings . The Israel-Lebanon War erupted, lasting 34 days and killing over 1,000 people . The United Nations Security Council imposed sanctions on Iran over its nuclear program .

- **Science and technology**: Pluto was demoted from a planet to a dwarf planet by the International Astronomical Union, reducing the number of planets in the solar system to eight . The New Horizons spacecraft was launched by NASA, beginning its journey to explore Pluto and the Kuiper Belt . The Human Genome Project announced the completion of the first draft of the chimpanzee genome, revealing that humans and chimps share 96% of their DNA. Twitter, the microblogging platform, was launched by Jack Dorsey, Biz Stone, and Evan Williams. Google acquired YouTube, the video-sharing website, for $1.65 billion.

- **Culture and entertainment**: The Da Vinci Code, the controversial novel by Dan Brown, was adapted into a blockbuster film starring Tom Hanks and Audrey Tautou. High School Musical, the musical comedy film, became a pop culture phenomenon, launching the careers of Zac Efron, Vanessa Hudgens, and others. Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man's Chest, the second installment of the swashbuckling franchise, became the highest-grossing film of the year, earning over $1 billion worldwide. The 78th Academy Awards honored the best films of 2005, with Crash winning the Best Picture award in a surprise upset over Brokeback Mountain. The 48th Grammy Awards celebrated the best music of 2005, with U2 winning five awards, including Album of the Year for How to Dismantle an Atomic Bomb.

- **Sports and games**: The 2006 Winter Olympics were held in Turin, Italy, with Germany topping the medal table with 29 medals, followed by the United States with 25 . The 2006 FIFA World Cup was hosted by Germany, with Italy defeating France in a penalty shootout to win their fourth title . The 2006 Commonwealth Games were held in Melbourne, Australia, with Australia dominating the event with 221 medals, followed by England with 110. The 2006 Asian Games were held in Doha, Qatar, with China leading the medal tally with 316 medals, followed by South Korea with 193. The 2006 World Series of Poker was won by Jamie Gold, who took home a record $12 million prize.

- **Disasters and tragedies**: A massive mudslide occurred in Southern Leyte, Philippines, killing an estimated 1,126 people . An earthquake struck Java, Indonesia, killing over 6,600 people and displacing 1.5 million others . A series of bombings in Mumbai, India, killed 209 people and injured over 700 . A plane crash in Ukraine killed 170 people, most of them Russian tourists . A fire at a nightclub in Bucharest, Romania, killed 64 people and injured 146 . Steve Irwin, the Australian wildlife expert and television personality, died after being pierced in the chest by a stingray barb while filming a documentary .
