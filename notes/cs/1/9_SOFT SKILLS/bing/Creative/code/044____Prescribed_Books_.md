## Prescribed Books:

- Prescribed books are the books that are recommended by the syllabus or the instructor for a particular course or subject.
- Prescribed books are usually selected based on their relevance, quality, and availability for the course content and objectives.
- Prescribed books may vary depending on the level, institution, and discipline of the course or subject.
- Prescribed books are intended to provide the students with the essential knowledge, skills, and perspectives for the course or subject.
- Prescribed books may also include supplementary materials, such as exercises, case studies, examples, and references, to enhance the learning experience.
- Prescribed books are expected to be read and studied by the students throughout the course or subject, and may be assessed by various methods, such as quizzes, assignments, exams, and projects.
- Prescribed books may have different editions, formats, and publishers, and students should check with their instructor or syllabus for the specific version required for the course or subject.
- Prescribed books may be available in different sources, such as libraries, bookstores, online platforms, or open access repositories, and students should obtain them as soon as possible to avoid delays or shortages.
- Prescribed books may be subject to change or update by the instructor or syllabus, and students should follow the latest announcements or notifications for any modifications or additions.
