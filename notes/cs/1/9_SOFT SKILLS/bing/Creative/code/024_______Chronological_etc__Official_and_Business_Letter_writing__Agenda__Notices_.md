#### Official and Business Letter Writing

- Official and business letter writing follows a standard format and style, which may vary depending on the purpose, context, and audience of the letter.
- Official and business letter writing typically consists of the following elements: sender's address, date, recipient's address, salutation, subject line, body, complimentary close, signature, and enclosures (if any).
- Official and business letter writing should be clear, concise, courteous, and professional, and should avoid using slang, jargon, abbreviations, or emoticons.

#### Agenda
- Agenda is a list of items or topics that are to be discussed or decided in a meeting or event.
- Agenda helps to organize and structure the meeting or event, and to inform the participants of the objectives, expectations, and responsibilities.
- Agenda usually includes the following elements: title, date, time, location, participants, chairperson, objectives, items or topics, time allocation, and any other relevant information.
- Agenda should be prepared and distributed in advance, and should be followed and updated during the meeting or event.

#### Notices
- Notices are brief announcements or information that are intended to inform or alert a specific audience or public.
- Notices can be used for various purposes, such as invitations, reminders, warnings, instructions, advertisements, etc.
- Notices usually include the following elements: heading, date, body, and signature or name of the issuer.
