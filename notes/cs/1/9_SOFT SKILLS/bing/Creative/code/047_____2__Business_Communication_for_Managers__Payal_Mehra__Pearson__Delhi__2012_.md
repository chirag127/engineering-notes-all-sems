### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

- This is a book written by **Payal Mehra**, an Associate Professor of Communication at the Indian Institute of Management Lucknow .
- The book is published by **Pearson Publishing** and was released in **February 2012**.
- The book is aimed at **MBA students** and covers all the major communication topics included in MBA syllabi across the country  .
- The book is divided into **four parts**: Part I: Fundamentals of Business Communication, Part II: Business Writing, Part III: Oral Communication, and Part IV: Communication in Special Situations .
- The book is **student-friendly**, **practical** and **example-driven**, and demonstrates methods of effective communication in various business scenarios  .
- The book also provides **self-assessment exercises**, **case studies**, **role plays**, and **communication tips** to help students improve their communication skills .
- The book has received positive reviews from readers and experts, and is considered a useful and comprehensive guide for business communication .
