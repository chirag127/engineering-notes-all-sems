# 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

- This book is a textbook that addresses the requirements of students of any discipline who are on the threshold of starting their careers. The book would also prove beneficial to young managers who have already had a taste of corporate life   .
- The book aims to provide crucial insights into various facets of developing one's personality, as well as to improve written, verbal, and non-verbal communication skills.
- The book covers topics such as personality development, soft skills, communication skills, resume writing, group discussion, interview skills, presentation skills, business etiquette, emotional intelligence, and time management.
- The book is divided into 16 chapters, each with learning objectives, key points, summary, review questions, and case studies.
- The book also provides online resources such as audio clips, video clips, and additional reading material for the students and instructors.
- The book is written in a simple and lucid style, with examples, anecdotes, and illustrations to make the concepts clear and interesting.
- The book is based on the author's extensive experience as a trainer, consultant, and academician in the field of personality development and soft skills.
