### Unit-4: Presentation and Interaction Skills

- Presentation and interaction skills are the abilities to communicate effectively and confidently with an audience, using appropriate verbal and non-verbal cues, visual aids, and technology.
- Presentation and interaction skills are important for various purposes, such as delivering information, persuading others, demonstrating knowledge, or showcasing a product or service.
- Presentation and interaction skills can be improved by following some general guidelines, such as:
  - Know your audience: analyze their needs, expectations, background, and interests, and tailor your presentation accordingly.
  - Know your purpose: define the main goal and message of your presentation, and structure it logically and coherently.
  - Know your content: research and prepare your material thoroughly, and use reliable and relevant sources of information.
  - Know your style: choose an appropriate tone, language, and delivery method for your presentation, and practice your speech and body language.
  - Know your tools: select and design effective visual aids, such as slides, charts, graphs, images, or videos, and use appropriate technology, such as a projector, microphone, or pointer.
- Presentation and interaction skills can be evaluated by using some criteria, such as:
  - Content: the accuracy, relevance, clarity, and completeness of the information presented.
  - Organization: the logical and coherent flow, transition, and conclusion of the presentation.
  - Delivery: the confidence, fluency, pronunciation, intonation, and eye contact of the speaker.
  - Visual aids: the quality, suitability, and visibility of the visual aids used.
  - Interaction: the engagement, responsiveness, and feedback of the speaker and the audience.
