### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for:

- Effective oral and written communication in academic and professional settings.
- Developing interpersonal and intercultural competence and awareness.
- Enhancing critical thinking and problem-solving skills through various modes of communication.
- Expressing ideas clearly, coherently and persuasively in different contexts and formats.
- Using appropriate language, tone, style and format for different audiences and purposes.
- Applying feedback and self-reflection to improve communication skills and performance.
- Demonstrating ethical and responsible communication practices and values.
