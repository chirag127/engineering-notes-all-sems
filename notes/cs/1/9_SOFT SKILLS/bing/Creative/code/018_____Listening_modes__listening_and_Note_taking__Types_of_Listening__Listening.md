# Listening modes, listening and note taking, types of listening, and listening

## Listening modes
- Listening modes are the different ways of processing and responding to what we hear.
- There are four main listening modes: **active**, **critical**, **empathic**, and **appreciative**.
- Active listening is when we pay close attention to the speaker, ask questions, paraphrase, and give feedback.
- Critical listening is when we evaluate the speaker's arguments, evidence, logic, and credibility.
- Empathic listening is when we try to understand the speaker's feelings, emotions, and perspective.
- Appreciative listening is when we enjoy the speaker's message, style, or delivery.

## Listening and note taking
- Listening and note taking are important skills for learning and retaining information from lectures, presentations, or podcasts.
- Some tips for effective listening and note taking are:
  - Prepare before the listening session by reviewing the topic, setting a goal, and eliminating distractions.
  - During the listening session, focus on the main ideas, key points, and examples. Use symbols, abbreviations, and outlines to organize your notes. Write down any questions or comments you have.
  - After the listening session, review your notes, fill in any gaps, and summarize the main points. Connect the information to what you already know or want to learn more about.

## Types of listening
- There are different types of listening depending on the purpose, context, and relationship of the communication.
- Some common types of listening are: **informative**, **persuasive**, **entertaining**, **relational**, and **therapeutic**.
- Informative listening is when we listen to learn or acquire new information or knowledge.
- Persuasive listening is when we listen to be convinced or influenced by the speaker's message or point of view.
- Entertaining listening is when we listen for pleasure, amusement, or enjoyment.
- Relational listening is when we listen to build or maintain a relationship with the speaker or other listeners.
- Therapeutic listening is when we listen to help or support the speaker with a problem or issue.

## Listening
- Listening is the process of receiving, attending, understanding, remembering, and responding to verbal and nonverbal messages.
- Listening is a complex and active skill that requires mental and physical effort, concentration, and motivation.
- Listening is different from hearing, which is the physiological process of perceiving sound waves.
- Listening is essential for effective communication, learning, and interpersonal relationships.
