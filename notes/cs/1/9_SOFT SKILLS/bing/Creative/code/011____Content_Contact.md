## Content Contact

- Content contact is a term used in communication studies to describe the degree of interaction and engagement between a media user and a media text.
- Content contact can be influenced by various factors, such as the user's motivation, attention, involvement, immersion, identification, parasocial interaction, presence, transportation, and flow.
- Content contact can have various effects on the user, such as cognitive, affective, behavioral, and physiological responses, as well as learning, persuasion, enjoyment, and satisfaction.
- Content contact can be measured by various methods, such as self-report scales, physiological indicators, behavioral observations, and content analysis.
- Content contact can vary across different media platforms, genres, formats, and contexts, such as television, film, video games, social media, news, entertainment, education, and health.
