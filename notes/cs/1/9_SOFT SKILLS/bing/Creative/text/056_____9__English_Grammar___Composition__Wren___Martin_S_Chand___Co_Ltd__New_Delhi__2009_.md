### 9. English Grammar & Composition, Wren & Martin S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi, 2009.

This is a book that covers the fundamentals of English grammar and composition. It is divided into two parts: Part I deals with the principles of grammar, and Part II deals with the art of writing. The book is intended for students, teachers, and anyone who wants to improve their English skills. Some of the topics covered in the book are:

- The sentence and its parts: This chapter explains the basic structure of a sentence, the types of sentences, the subject and predicate, the phrase and clause, and the kinds of clauses.
- The noun: This chapter defines the noun, the kinds of nouns, the gender, number, and case of nouns, and the use of nouns in sentences.
- The pronoun: This chapter defines the pronoun, the kinds of pronouns, the agreement of pronouns, the use of pronouns in sentences, and the common errors in the use of pronouns.
- The adjective: This chapter defines the adjective, the kinds of adjectives, the degrees of comparison, the use of adjectives in sentences, and the common errors in the use of adjectives.
- The verb: This chapter defines the verb, the kinds of verbs, the tense and mood of verbs, the voice and form of verbs, the agreement of verbs, the use of verbs in sentences, and the common errors in the use of verbs.
- The adverb: This chapter defines the adverb, the kinds of adverbs, the position and comparison of adverbs, the use of adverbs in sentences, and the common errors in the use of adverbs.
- The preposition: This chapter defines the preposition, the kinds of prepositions, the use of prepositions in sentences, and the common errors in the use of prepositions.
- The conjunction: This chapter defines the conjunction, the kinds of conjunctions, the use of conjunctions in sentences, and the common errors in the use of conjunctions.
- The interjection: This chapter defines the interjection, the kinds of interjections, and the use of interjections in sentences.
- The analysis of sentences: This chapter explains how to analyze sentences into their parts and clauses, and how to identify the subject, predicate, object, complement, and modifier in a sentence.
- The transformation of sentences: This chapter explains how to transform sentences from one form to another, such as from active to passive, from direct to indirect, from affirmative to negative, from simple to compound or complex, and vice versa.
- The synthesis of sentences: This chapter explains how to combine two or more sentences into one, such as by using coordination, subordination, apposition, or participles.
- The punctuation: This chapter explains the rules and principles of punctuation, and the use of various punctuation marks, such as the comma, the semicolon, the colon, the full stop, the question mark, the exclamation mark, the quotation marks, the apostrophe, the hyphen, the dash, the brackets, and the ellipsis.
- The composition: This chapter explains the process and techniques of writing, such as planning, outlining, drafting, revising, editing, and proofreading. It also provides guidance on how to write different types of compositions, such as paragraphs, essays, letters, applications, summaries, précis, reports, and stories. It also gives examples and exercises for practice.
