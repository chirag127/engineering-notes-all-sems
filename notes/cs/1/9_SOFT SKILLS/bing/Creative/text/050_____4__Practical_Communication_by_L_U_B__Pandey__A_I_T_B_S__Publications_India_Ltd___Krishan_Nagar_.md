### 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- This is a book that covers various aspects of communication skills, such as listening, speaking, reading, writing, and non-verbal communication.
- The book is divided into four units: Unit I: Communication Process and Practice, Unit II: Oral Communication, Unit III: Written Communication, and Unit IV: Non-Verbal Communication.
- The book aims to help students develop their communication competence and confidence in various situations, such as academic, professional, and social contexts.
- The book provides theoretical concepts, practical examples, exercises, and activities to enhance the learning outcomes of the students.
- The book also includes topics such as pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar, presentation skills, report writing, letter writing, resume writing, and group discussion.
- The book is based on the syllabus of the Technical Communication course offered by the Uttar Pradesh Technical University, Lucknow  .
- The book is written by L.U.B. Pandey, who is a professor of English and Communication Skills at the Institute of Engineering and Technology, Lucknow.
- The book is published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd., which is located at Krishan Nagar, Delhi  .
