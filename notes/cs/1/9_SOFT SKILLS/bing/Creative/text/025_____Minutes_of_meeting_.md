### Minutes of meeting

- Minutes of meeting are a written record of the main points and decisions that were discussed in a meeting.
- Minutes of meeting serve as a reference for the participants and other stakeholders who were not present in the meeting.
- Minutes of meeting should include the following information:

  - The date, time, and location of the meeting
  - The names and roles of the attendees and the absentees
  - The agenda and objectives of the meeting
  - The summary of the discussion and the action items for each agenda item
  - The decisions made and the votes taken, if any
  - The deadlines and responsibilities for the action items
  - The date and time of the next meeting, if applicable

- Minutes of meeting should be clear, concise, and accurate. They should avoid personal opinions, irrelevant details, and ambiguous language.
- Minutes of meeting should be prepared and distributed as soon as possible after the meeting, preferably within 24 hours.
- Minutes of meeting should be reviewed and approved by the chairperson or the facilitator of the meeting, and by the participants if necessary.
- Minutes of meeting should be stored and archived in a secure and accessible location for future reference.
