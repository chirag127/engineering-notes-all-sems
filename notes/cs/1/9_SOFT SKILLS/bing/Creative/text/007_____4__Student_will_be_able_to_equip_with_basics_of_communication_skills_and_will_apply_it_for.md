### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for:

- Effective communication in personal and professional settings.
- Communication skills are essential for building rapport, expressing ideas, listening actively, giving feedback, resolving conflicts, and persuading others.
- Some of the basic communication skills are:

  - Verbal communication: using clear and concise language, choosing appropriate words and tone, avoiding jargon and slang, and speaking with confidence and enthusiasm.
  - Non-verbal communication: using body language, eye contact, facial expressions, gestures, and posture to convey messages and emotions, and to show interest and respect.
  - Written communication: using correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and format, organizing information logically, using headings and bullet points, and proofreading and editing.
  - Visual communication: using charts, graphs, diagrams, images, and videos to support and illustrate verbal or written messages, and to make them more engaging and memorable.
  - Interpersonal communication: using empathy, active listening, questioning, paraphrasing, and summarizing to understand and respond to others, and to build trust and rapport.
  - Group communication: using teamwork, collaboration, leadership, and facilitation skills to work effectively with others, and to achieve common goals and objectives.
  - Cross-cultural communication: using awareness, sensitivity, and respect to communicate with people from different backgrounds, cultures, and perspectives, and to avoid misunderstandings and stereotypes.

- Communication skills can be applied for various purposes and contexts, such as:

  - Academic communication: using communication skills to participate in class discussions, presentations, projects, and assignments, and to communicate with teachers and peers.
  - Professional communication: using communication skills to communicate with colleagues, managers, clients, and customers, and to perform tasks and duties in the workplace.
  - Social communication: using communication skills to interact with friends, family, and strangers, and to engage in casual and formal conversations.
  - Media communication: using communication skills to access, analyze, and create media messages, such as news, advertisements, blogs, podcasts, and social media posts.
  - Public communication: using communication skills to speak in front of an audience, such as giving a speech, a lecture, or a demonstration, and to persuade, inform, or entertain them.
