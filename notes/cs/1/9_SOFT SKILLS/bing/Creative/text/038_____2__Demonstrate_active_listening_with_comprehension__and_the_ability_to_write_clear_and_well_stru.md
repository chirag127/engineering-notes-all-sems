Active listening is the process of paying attention to what someone is saying, and showing interest and understanding by using verbal and non-verbal cues. Comprehension is the ability to understand the meaning and intention of the speaker, and to respond appropriately. Active listening with comprehension is an important skill for effective communication, especially in professional settings.

Some of the benefits of active listening with comprehension are:

- It builds rapport and trust with the speaker.
- It shows respect and empathy for the speaker's feelings and opinions.
- It helps to avoid misunderstandings and conflicts.
- It enhances learning and problem-solving.
- It improves the quality of feedback and suggestions.

Some of the strategies for active listening with comprehension are:

- Maintain eye contact and a positive body language.
- Use open-ended questions and paraphrasing to clarify and confirm the speaker's message.
- Use affirming words and gestures to show interest and encouragement.
- Avoid interrupting, judging, or criticizing the speaker.
- Summarize the main points and ask for feedback at the end of the conversation.

Writing clear and well-structured emails and proposals is another essential skill for professional communication. Emails and proposals are formal documents that convey information, requests, or recommendations to a specific audience.
