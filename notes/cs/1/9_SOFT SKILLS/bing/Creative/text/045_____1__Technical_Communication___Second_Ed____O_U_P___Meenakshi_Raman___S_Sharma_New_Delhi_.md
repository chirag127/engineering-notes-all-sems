### 1. Technical Communication, (Second Ed.); O.U.P., Meenakshi Raman & S.Sharma New Delhi

- Technical Communication is a comprehensive textbook that covers the principles and practices of effective communication in engineering contexts.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I introduces the basics of communication, such as language skills, listening skills, and non-verbal communication. Part II deals with the types and formats of technical documents, such as letters, memos, reports, proposals, and manuals. Part III focuses on the oral communication skills, such as presentations, meetings, and interviews. Part IV discusses the ethical, legal, and intercultural aspects of technical communication.
- The book is written in a clear and concise style, with numerous examples, exercises, and case studies to illustrate the concepts and techniques. The book also provides guidelines for using various software tools, such as word processors, spreadsheets, graphics, and multimedia, for creating and enhancing technical documents and presentations.
- The book is intended for undergraduate students of engineering, as well as professionals and practitioners who need to communicate effectively in technical settings. The book is based on the syllabi of various universities and technical institutes in India and abroad.
- The book is authored by Meenakshi Raman and Sangeeta Sharma, who are experienced teachers and researchers in the field of technical communication. They have published several books and papers on the subject and have conducted workshops and training programs for students and professionals. They are currently associated with the Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS), Pilani, India.
