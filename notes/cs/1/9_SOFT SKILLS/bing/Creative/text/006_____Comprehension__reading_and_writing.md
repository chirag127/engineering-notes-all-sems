### Comprehension, reading and writing

- Comprehension is the ability to understand and interpret written or spoken texts.
- Reading is the process of decoding written symbols and extracting meaning from them.
- Writing is the process of encoding thoughts and ideas into written symbols and conveying meaning to others.
- Comprehension, reading and writing are interrelated skills that develop through exposure, practice and feedback.
- Comprehension, reading and writing are essential for communication, learning and personal development.

Some of the subskills involved in comprehension, reading and writing are:

- Phonological awareness: the ability to recognize and manipulate the sounds of spoken language.
- Phonics: the ability to match sounds to letters and blend them to form words.
- Vocabulary: the knowledge of words and their meanings.
- Fluency: the ability to read and write smoothly, accurately and with expression.
- Grammar: the knowledge of the rules and conventions of language.
- Text structure: the knowledge of how texts are organized and formatted for different purposes and audiences.
- Comprehension strategies: the techniques used to monitor and enhance understanding of texts, such as predicting, questioning, summarizing, inferring, etc.
- Writing process: the steps involved in planning, drafting, revising, editing and publishing a text.
- Writing traits: the qualities of effective writing, such as ideas, organization, voice, word choice, sentence fluency and conventions.
