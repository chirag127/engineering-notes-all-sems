### 5. Students will be able to build up personal traits that will make the transition from institution

- Personal traits are the characteristics, qualities, and habits that define a person's identity and behavior.
- Transition from institution refers to the process of moving from one educational or professional setting to another, such as from school to college, or from college to work.
- Building up personal traits that will make the transition from institution easier and smoother is an important goal for students, as it will help them adapt to new environments, cope with challenges, and achieve their potential.
- Some of the personal traits that students can develop to facilitate their transition from institution are:

  - **Self-awareness**: The ability to recognize one's own strengths, weaknesses, emotions, values, and goals, and to use this information to guide one's actions and decisions.
  - **Self-management**: The ability to regulate one's own emotions, thoughts, and behaviors, and to cope with stress, challenges, and setbacks in a positive and constructive way.
  - **Social awareness**: The ability to empathize with others, understand different perspectives and cultures, and respect diversity and inclusion.
  - **Social skills**: The ability to communicate effectively, collaborate with others, resolve conflicts, and build positive relationships.
  - **Growth mindset**: The belief that one's abilities and intelligence can be improved through effort, feedback, and learning, and that challenges and failures are opportunities for growth and development.
  - **Resilience**: The ability to bounce back from difficulties, overcome obstacles, and persevere in the face of adversity.
  - **Curiosity**: The desire to explore new ideas, learn new things, and seek new experiences.
  - **Creativity**: The ability to generate original and innovative solutions, express oneself in diverse ways, and think outside the box.
  - **Critical thinking**: The ability to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information, identify and solve problems, and make informed and logical judgments.
  - **Self-efficacy**: The belief in one's own capabilities and competence to achieve one's goals and overcome challenges.
