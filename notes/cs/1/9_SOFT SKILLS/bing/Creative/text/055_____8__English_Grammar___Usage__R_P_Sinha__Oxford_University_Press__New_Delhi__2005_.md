### 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

- This is a comprehensive and authoritative guide to English grammar and usage, written by a renowned linguist and teacher.
- The book covers all the major aspects of English grammar, such as word classes, phrases, clauses, sentences, punctuation, spelling, and pronunciation.
- The book also provides clear and practical advice on how to use English effectively in different contexts, such as academic writing, business communication, and everyday conversation.
- The book is organized into four parts: Part I introduces the basic concepts and terminology of grammar; Part II explains the structure and functions of words and phrases; Part III deals with the syntax and semantics of clauses and sentences; and Part IV covers the aspects of usage, such as style, register, tone, and politeness.
- The book is richly illustrated with examples, exercises, tables, and diagrams, and includes a glossary, an index, and a list of references.
- The book is suitable for learners and teachers of English at all levels, as well as for anyone who wants to improve their knowledge and skills in English grammar and usage.
