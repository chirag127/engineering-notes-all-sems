### Vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes

- Vocabulary is the set of words that a person knows or uses in a particular language or subject.
- Vocabulary can be learned and improved through various methods, such as reading, listening, speaking, writing, and using digital tools.
- Different platforms and modes can offer different advantages and challenges for vocabulary learning, such as:
  - Apps: Apps are software applications that can be installed on mobile devices or accessed online. Apps can provide interactive and engaging ways to learn vocabulary, such as games, quizzes, flashcards, word clouds, etc. Apps can also offer personalized and adaptive learning, feedback, and progress tracking. Some examples of vocabulary apps are Vocab1, Vocabulary Builder, Vocab Victor, etc.
  - Websites: Websites are online pages that can be accessed through a web browser. Websites can offer a variety of resources and activities to learn vocabulary, such as dictionaries, thesauruses, word lists, videos, podcasts, blogs, etc. Websites can also allow learners to interact with other users and share their vocabulary knowledge. Some examples of vocabulary websites are Vocabulary.com, Vocabulary.co.il, WordSift, etc.
  - Books: Books are printed or electronic texts that can be read for pleasure or information. Books can expose learners to a wide range of vocabulary in different genres, topics, and contexts. Books can also help learners to infer the meaning of unknown words from the context, and to use strategies such as glossaries, footnotes, and annotations. Some examples of vocabulary books are Word Power Made Easy by Norman Lewis, 1100 Words You Need to Know by Murray Bromberg and Melvin Gordon, etc.
  - Audio: Audio is sound that can be heard through speakers or headphones. Audio can include spoken words, music, sound effects, etc. Audio can help learners to improve their listening comprehension and pronunciation of vocabulary, as well as to learn new words from the context and tone. Audio can also stimulate learners' interest and imagination. Some examples of vocabulary audio are BBC Learning English, VOA Learning English, etc.
  - Video: Video is a series of moving images that can be seen on a screen. Video can include spoken words, subtitles, images, animations, etc. Video can help learners to learn vocabulary from the visual and auditory cues, as well as to see how words are used in real-life situations and conversations. Video can also make vocabulary learning more fun and memorable. Some examples of vocabulary video are TED-Ed, Learn English with TV Series, etc.
