### 9. English Grammar & Composition, Wren & Martin S.Chand & Co Ltd, New Delhi, 2009.

This is a book that covers the fundamentals of English grammar and composition. It is divided into two parts: Part I deals with the parts of speech, sentence structure, punctuation, and usage; Part II deals with the principles of composition, such as paragraph writing, essay writing, letter writing, and precis writing. Some of the main topics covered in this book are:

- Nouns: kinds, number, gender, case, and functions
- Pronouns: kinds, agreement, and reference
- Adjectives: kinds, comparison, and position
- Verbs: kinds, tense, voice, mood, and agreement
- Adverbs: kinds, comparison, and position
- Prepositions: kinds, use, and omission
- Conjunctions: kinds, use, and punctuation
- Interjections: kinds and use
- Articles: definite and indefinite
- The sentence: kinds, structure, analysis, and transformation
- The phrase: kinds and functions
- The clause: kinds and functions
- The paragraph: kinds, structure, unity, and coherence
- The essay: kinds, structure, style, and format
- The letter: kinds, structure, style, and format
- The precis: definition, rules, and steps

The book also contains exercises, examples, illustrations, and appendices for reference and practice. It is suitable for students, teachers, and learners of English as a second language. It is one of the most popular and widely used books on English grammar and composition.
