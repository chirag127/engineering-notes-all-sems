### 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- This is a book that covers the process and practice of communication skills in various contexts and situations.
- The book aims to help the readers develop their abilities to communicate effectively in oral and written forms, as well as to understand the barriers and challenges of communication.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I - Communication: An Overview, Part II - Oral Communication, Part III - Written Communication, and Part IV - Communication in Special Situations.
- Some of the topics covered in the book are:

  - The nature, scope, and functions of communication
  - The types, modes, and levels of communication
  - The principles, elements, and models of communication
  - The factors affecting communication and the ways to overcome them
  - The skills and strategies of listening, speaking, reading, and writing
  - The formats and styles of various forms of oral and written communication, such as letters, reports, proposals, memos, emails, etc.
  - The techniques and tools of presentation and public speaking
  - The etiquette and ethics of communication
  - The communication skills for group discussion, interview, meeting, negotiation, etc.
  - The communication skills for intercultural, interpersonal, and intrapersonal communication
  - The communication skills for crisis, conflict, and media communication

- The book is based on the latest syllabus and guidelines of the Uttar Pradesh Technical University (UPTU) and other technical universities in India  .
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with examples, illustrations, exercises, and summaries to enhance the learning outcomes.
- The book is suitable for undergraduate and postgraduate students of engineering, management, and other professional courses, as well as for anyone who wants to improve their communication skills.
