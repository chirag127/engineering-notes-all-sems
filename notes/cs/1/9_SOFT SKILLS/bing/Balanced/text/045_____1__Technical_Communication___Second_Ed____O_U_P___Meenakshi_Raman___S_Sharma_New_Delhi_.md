### Technical Communication, (Second Ed.); O.U.P., Meenakshi Raman & S.Sharma New Delhi

- Technical Communication is a comprehensive textbook that covers the principles and practices of effective communication in engineering contexts.
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I introduces the basics of communication, such as language skills, listening skills, and non-verbal communication. Part II deals with the forms and functions of written communication, such as letters, memos, reports, proposals, and emails. Part III focuses on the oral communication skills, such as presentations, meetings, interviews, and group discussions. Part IV explores the emerging trends and technologies in communication, such as online communication, social media, and professional ethics.
- The book is designed to meet the needs of undergraduate students of engineering and other technical disciplines. It provides numerous examples, exercises, case studies, and illustrations to enhance the understanding and application of communication skills. It also includes a glossary of key terms and a list of references for further reading.
- The book is written by Meenakshi Raman and Sangeeta Sharma, who are both experienced teachers and researchers in the field of communication. They have authored several other books and articles on communication and related topics. They are currently associated with the Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, India.
