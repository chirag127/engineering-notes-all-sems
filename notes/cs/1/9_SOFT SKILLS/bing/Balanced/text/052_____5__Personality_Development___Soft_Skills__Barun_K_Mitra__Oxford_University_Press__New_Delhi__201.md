### 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

- This is a textbook that aims to equip readers with the necessary skills required to be an effective communicator and a successful professional in the competitive world.
- The book covers various topics such as personality development, soft skills, communication skills, resume writing, group discussion, interview skills, presentation skills, business etiquette, and interpersonal skills.
- The book also provides practical tips, examples, exercises, self-assessment tests, and case studies to help readers apply the concepts and techniques in real-life situations.
- The book is written in a simple and lucid language, with a student-friendly approach and a contemporary outlook.
- The book is suitable for students of any discipline who are on the threshold of starting their careers, as well as young managers who have already had a taste of corporate life.
