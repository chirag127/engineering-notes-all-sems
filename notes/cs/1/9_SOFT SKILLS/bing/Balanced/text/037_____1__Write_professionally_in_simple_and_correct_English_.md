### 1. Write professionally in simple and correct English.

- Writing professionally means using clear, concise and respectful language that is appropriate for the intended audience and purpose.
- Writing in simple and correct English means avoiding unnecessary jargon, slang, abbreviations, errors and ambiguity that may confuse or offend the reader.
- Some tips for writing professionally in simple and correct English are:

  - Know your audience and tailor your tone, style and vocabulary accordingly.
  - Use active voice, short sentences and paragraphs, and simple words whenever possible.
  - Use proper grammar, spelling, punctuation and capitalization.
  - Use headings, bullet points, lists and tables to organize and present your information effectively.
  - Avoid repetition, redundancy, filler words and vague expressions.
  - Use transitions, connectors and signposts to guide the reader through your text and show the logical relationships between your ideas.
  - Use examples, evidence, facts and figures to support your claims and arguments.
  - Cite your sources and give credit where it is due.
  - Proofread and edit your text before sending or publishing it.
  - Use online tools and resources, such as dictionaries, thesauruses, grammar checkers and style guides, to help you improve your writing skills.
