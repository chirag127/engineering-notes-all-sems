### Unit-2: Listening and Speaking Skills

- Listening and speaking skills are essential for effective communication in any language.
- Listening is the process of receiving, understanding, and interpreting spoken messages.
- Speaking is the process of producing and delivering spoken messages.
- Listening and speaking skills are interrelated and interdependent. They influence and support each other in various ways.
- Listening and speaking skills can be improved by practicing various strategies and techniques, such as:

  - Setting a purpose and a goal for listening or speaking
  - Activating prior knowledge and making predictions
  - Paying attention to the speaker's tone, pitch, stress, and intonation
  - Using context clues and non-verbal cues to infer meaning
  - Asking and answering questions to clarify and check understanding
  - Summarizing and paraphrasing the main points and details
  - Giving and receiving feedback
  - Using appropriate vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation
  - Using discourse markers and cohesive devices to organize and connect ideas
  - Adapting the message to the audience, purpose, and situation
  - Using polite and respectful language and expressions
  - Being confident and assertive, but not aggressive or rude

- Listening and speaking skills can be applied to various contexts and purposes, such as:

  - Academic: listening to lectures, presentations, podcasts, etc.; speaking in class discussions, seminars, oral exams, etc.
  - Professional: listening to meetings, conferences, interviews, etc.; speaking in negotiations, presentations, reports, etc.
  - Social: listening to conversations, stories, jokes, etc.; speaking in casual chats, greetings, invitations, etc.
  - Personal: listening to music, movies, audiobooks, etc.; speaking in self-expression, reflection, narration, etc.
