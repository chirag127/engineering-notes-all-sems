### 2006

- 2006 was a common year that started on a Sunday in the Gregorian calendar.
- Some of the major events that happened in 2006 are:
  - The Winter Olympics were held in Turin, Italy, from February 10 to 26.
  - The International Astronomical Union redefined the term "planet", demoting Pluto to a dwarf planet on August 24.
  - North Korea conducted its first nuclear test on October 9, sparking international condemnation and sanctions.
  - Saddam Hussein, the former president of Iraq, was executed by hanging on December 30, after being convicted of crimes against humanity.
- Some of the notable people who died in 2006 are:
  - Steve Irwin, the Australian wildlife expert and television personality, who was killed by a stingray on September 4.
  - James Brown, the American singer and songwriter, who was known as the "Godfather of Soul", who died of pneumonia on December 25.
  - Gerald Ford, the 38th president of the United States, who died of natural causes on December 26.
- Some of the popular films that were released in 2006 are:
  - Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man's Chest, the second installment of the fantasy adventure franchise, starring Johnny Depp, Orlando Bloom, and Keira Knightley.
  - The Da Vinci Code, the mystery thriller based on the best-selling novel by Dan Brown, starring Tom Hanks, Audrey Tautou, and Ian McKellen.
  - The Departed, the crime drama directed by Martin Scorsese, starring Leonardo DiCaprio, Matt Damon, Jack Nicholson, and Mark Wahlberg.
- Some of the best-selling books that were published in 2006 are:
  - For One More Day, by Mitch Albom, a novel about a man who gets to spend one more day with his deceased mother.
  - The Road, by Cormac McCarthy, a post-apocalyptic novel about a father and son who travel across a devastated landscape.
  - The Kite Runner, by Khaled Hosseini, a novel about the friendship and betrayal of two boys in Afghanistan.
