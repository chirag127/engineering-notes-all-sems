### Leadership qualities; Impact, Communication skills for Leaders: Listening and

- Leadership is the ability to influence, motivate, and inspire others to achieve a common goal or vision.
- Leadership qualities are the characteristics, skills, and behaviors that make a leader effective and respected.
- Some of the most important leadership qualities are:

  - Impact: The ability to create positive change and make a difference in the lives of others, the organization, and the world.
  - Communication skills: The ability to express oneself clearly, confidently, and persuasively, both verbally and non-verbally, and to listen actively and empathetically to others.
  - Listening and: The ability to pay attention to what others are saying, understand their perspectives and emotions, and respond appropriately and constructively.
  - Vision: The ability to see the big picture, imagine the future, and communicate a compelling direction and purpose for others to follow.
  - Integrity: The ability to act in accordance with one's values, principles, and ethical standards, and to be honest, trustworthy, and accountable.
  - Courage: The ability to face challenges, risks, and uncertainties, and to act decisively and confidently in the face of opposition or criticism.
  - Creativity: The ability to generate new and innovative ideas, solutions, and approaches, and to foster a culture of learning and experimentation.
  - Collaboration: The ability to work effectively with others, leverage diversity, and build strong and positive relationships.
  - Self-awareness: The ability to recognize one's strengths, weaknesses, emotions, and biases, and to seek feedback and learn from experience.
  - Adaptability: The ability to adjust to changing situations, demands, and expectations, and to embrace change and uncertainty as opportunities for growth and improvement.
