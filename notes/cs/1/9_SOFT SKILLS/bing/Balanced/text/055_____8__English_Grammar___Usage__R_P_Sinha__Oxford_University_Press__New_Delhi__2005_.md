### 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

This is a book that covers the basic and advanced aspects of English grammar and usage. It is divided into 12 chapters, each focusing on a different topic. The book aims to help learners improve their accuracy and fluency in English. Some of the topics covered in the book are:

- Parts of speech and their functions
- Sentence structure and types
- Tenses and aspects
- Modals and auxiliaries
- Voice and mood
- Clauses and phrases
- Concord and agreement
- Punctuation and capitalization
- Word formation and vocabulary
- Idioms and phrasal verbs
- Common errors and pitfalls
- Style and register

The book also provides exercises and tests at the end of each chapter, along with answer keys and explanations. The book is suitable for students, teachers, and professionals who want to master the rules and conventions of English grammar and usage.
