### 10. Soft Skills for Everyone. Jeff Butterfield, Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd; New Delhi 2017.

- This book is a guide to developing and enhancing the soft skills that are essential for personal and professional success in today's world.
- Soft skills are the interpersonal, communication, problem-solving, and self-management abilities that enable people to work effectively and harmoniously with others, and to achieve their goals.
- The book covers 10 key soft skills that are relevant for everyone, regardless of their background, education, or career path. These are:
  - Self-awareness: The ability to recognize and understand one's own emotions, strengths, weaknesses, values, and motives, and how they affect one's behavior and relationships.
  - Self-management: The ability to regulate one's emotions, impulses, and actions, and to cope with stress, challenges, and change in a positive and productive way.
  - Communication: The ability to express oneself clearly, confidently, and respectfully in verbal, nonverbal, and written forms, and to listen actively and empathetically to others.
  - Teamwork: The ability to collaborate effectively and cooperatively with others, and to contribute to a shared vision, goal, or outcome.
  - Interpersonal skills: The ability to build and maintain positive and trusting relationships with others, and to handle conflicts and disagreements constructively and diplomatically.
  - Diversity awareness: The ability to appreciate and respect the differences and similarities among people, such as their cultures, beliefs, values, perspectives, and experiences, and to adapt one's behavior and communication accordingly.
  - Critical thinking: The ability to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information from various sources and perspectives, and to apply logic, creativity, and judgment to solve problems and make decisions.
  - Learning skills: The ability to acquire, retain, and apply new knowledge and skills, and to seek feedback and improvement opportunities continuously.
  - Professionalism: The ability to demonstrate a positive and ethical attitude, a high standard of performance, and a commitment to excellence in one's work and career.
  - Leadership: The ability to inspire, influence, and empower others to achieve a common goal, and to provide direction, guidance, and support as needed.
- The book provides practical tips, examples, exercises, and self-assessments to help readers develop and improve their soft skills, and to apply them in various personal and professional contexts.
- The book also offers advice on how to showcase one's soft skills to potential employers, and how to use them to advance one's career and achieve one's goals.
