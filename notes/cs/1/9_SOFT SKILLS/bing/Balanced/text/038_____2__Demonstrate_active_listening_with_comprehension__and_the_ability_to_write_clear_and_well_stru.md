### 2. Demonstrate active listening with comprehension, and the ability to write clear and well-structured emails and proposals.

- Active listening is the process of paying attention to what the speaker is saying, and showing interest and engagement through verbal and non-verbal cues.
- Comprehension is the ability to understand the meaning and intention of the speaker, and to respond appropriately and effectively.
- Writing clear and well-structured emails and proposals is the skill of communicating your ideas, requests, or suggestions in a concise, logical, and polite manner, using appropriate tone, format, and language.

Some points to demonstrate active listening with comprehension, and the ability to write clear and well-structured emails and proposals are:

- Before listening or writing, identify the purpose and goal of the communication, and the expectations and needs of the audience.
- During listening, use techniques such as paraphrasing, summarizing, asking open-ended questions, and giving feedback to show that you are following and understanding the speaker.
- After listening, confirm your understanding and agreement, or clarify any doubts or disagreements, and express your appreciation and gratitude for the speaker's time and input.
- Before writing, plan and organize your content, and decide on the main message, the supporting points, and the call to action.
- During writing, use clear and simple language, avoid jargon and slang, and use correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- After writing, proofread and edit your email or proposal, and check for clarity, coherence, and completeness.
- Use appropriate salutations, greetings, and closings, and maintain a respectful and professional tone throughout your email or proposal.
- Use a suitable format and layout for your email or proposal, and include relevant details such as subject line, date, recipient, sender, introduction, body, conclusion, and signature.
- Attach any necessary documents or files, and cite any sources or references that you use in your email or proposal.
