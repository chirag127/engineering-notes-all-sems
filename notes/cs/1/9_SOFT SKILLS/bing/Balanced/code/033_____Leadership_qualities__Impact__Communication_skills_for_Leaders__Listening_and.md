# Leadership qualities; Impact, Communication skills for Leaders: Listening

## Leadership qualities

- Leadership is the ability to influence, motivate, and inspire others to achieve a common goal or vision.
- Leadership qualities are the characteristics, skills, and behaviors that make a person an effective leader.
- Some of the most important leadership qualities are:

  - Vision: A leader has a clear and compelling vision of the future that aligns with the values and mission of the organization or group.
  - Integrity: A leader acts with honesty, ethics, and accountability, and builds trust and respect among followers and stakeholders.
  - Confidence: A leader has self-confidence and self-awareness, and expresses confidence in the abilities and potential of others.
  - Communication: A leader communicates effectively and persuasively, using verbal, non-verbal, and written skills, and listens actively and empathetically to others.
  - Creativity: A leader fosters a culture of innovation and creativity, and encourages and supports new ideas and solutions.
  - Adaptability: A leader is flexible and resilient, and adapts to changing situations and challenges, and learns from feedback and mistakes.
  - Collaboration: A leader builds and maintains positive and productive relationships with diverse individuals and groups, and promotes teamwork and cooperation.
  - Empowerment: A leader delegates authority and responsibility to others, and empowers them to make decisions and take actions that contribute to the goal or vision.
  - Motivation: A leader inspires and motivates others to perform at their best, and recognizes and rewards their achievements and contributions.

## Impact

- Impact is the measure of the positive or negative effects that a leader's actions and decisions have on the organization, group, or society.
- Impact can be assessed at different levels, such as individual, team, organizational, or societal, and using different criteria, such as performance, satisfaction, engagement, retention, loyalty, reputation, or social responsibility.
- Some of the ways that a leader can increase their impact are:

  - Aligning their vision and actions with the needs and expectations of their followers and stakeholders, and communicating them clearly and consistently.
  - Developing and implementing effective strategies and plans that achieve the desired outcomes and goals, and monitoring and evaluating their progress and results.
  - Leveraging their strengths and resources, and addressing their weaknesses and gaps, and seeking continuous improvement and development.
  - Seeking and providing constructive feedback, and learning from successes and failures, and applying the lessons learned to future situations and challenges.
  - Demonstrating ethical and responsible leadership, and acting as a role model and mentor for others, and contributing to the common good and social welfare.

## Communication skills for leaders: Listening

- Listening is one of the most essential and powerful communication skills for leaders, as it enables them to understand, connect, and influence others.
- Listening is the process of receiving, attending, interpreting, and responding to verbal and non-verbal messages from others.
- Listening skills for leaders include:

  - Active listening: Active listening is the skill of paying full attention to the speaker, and showing interest and engagement through verbal and non-verbal cues, such as eye contact, nodding, paraphrasing, and asking questions.
  - Empathetic listening: Empathetic listening is the skill of sensing and sharing the emotions and feelings of the speaker, and showing empathy and compassion through verbal and non-verbal cues, such as tone, expression, and validation.
  - Critical listening: Critical listening is the skill of analyzing and evaluating the content and logic of the speaker's message, and identifying the strengths, weaknesses, assumptions, biases, and implications of their arguments and claims.
  - Strategic listening: Strategic listening is the skill of identifying and focusing on the key information and messages that are relevant and important for the leader's goals and objectives, and using them to influence and persuade others.
