### 3. Personality Development, Harold R. Wallace et. al, Cengage Learning India Pvt. Ltd; New Delhi

- Personality Development is a book by Harold R. Wallace and L. Ann Masters that offers a systematic program for developing self-understanding, promoting personal growth, and preparing for successful employment  .
- The book is divided into four parts: Part I: Self-Understanding, Part II: Interpersonal Skills, Part III: Critical Thinking Skills, and Part IV: Professional Skills .
- Part I: Self-Understanding covers topics such as self-awareness, self-esteem, self-concept, values, goals, motivation, and learning styles .
- Part II: Interpersonal Skills covers topics such as communication, listening, nonverbal communication, feedback, conflict resolution, teamwork, and diversity .
- Part III: Critical Thinking Skills covers topics such as problem solving, decision making, creativity, and innovation .
- Part IV: Professional Skills covers topics such as work ethics, professionalism, career planning, job search, resume writing, interviewing, and workplace etiquette .
- The book is designed to help employees recognize the important role personality plays in the work environment and to develop the qualities and skills that today's employers demand  .
- The book is also available as an e-book and as a part of Cengage Learning's CourseMate, which provides interactive learning, study, and exam preparation tools that support the printed textbook .
