### Unit-3: Reading and Writing Skills: 8

- This unit focuses on developing the skills of reading and writing in academic contexts.
- Reading skills include:
  - Identifying the main idea and supporting details of a text.
  - Recognizing the structure and organization of a text.
  - Skimming and scanning for specific information.
  - Inferring the meaning of unfamiliar words and phrases from the context.
  - Evaluating the credibility and relevance of a source.
  - Summarizing and paraphrasing the main points of a text.
- Writing skills include:
  - Planning and outlining the content and structure of a writing task.
  - Writing clear and coherent sentences and paragraphs.
  - Using appropriate vocabulary, grammar, punctuation and spelling.
  - Applying the conventions of academic writing, such as citation and referencing.
  - Editing and revising the draft for clarity, accuracy and coherence.
  - Using feedback to improve the quality of the final product.
- The unit provides various types of texts and writing tasks for practice and assessment, such as essays, reports, reviews, summaries, etc.
