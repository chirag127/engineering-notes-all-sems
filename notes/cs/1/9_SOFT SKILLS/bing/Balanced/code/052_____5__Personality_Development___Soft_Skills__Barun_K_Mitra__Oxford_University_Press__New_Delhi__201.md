### 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

- This is a textbook that aims to equip readers with the necessary skills required to be an effective communicator and a successful professional in the competitive world.
- The book covers various topics such as personality development, soft skills, communication skills, resume writing, group discussion, interview skills, presentation skills, business etiquette, and corporate culture.
- The book also provides practical tips, examples, exercises, and self-assessment tests to help readers improve their skills and confidence.
- The book is divided into 15 chapters, each focusing on a specific aspect of personality development and soft skills. The chapters are as follows:

  - Chapter 1: Personality Development: A Must for Leadership and Career Growth
  - Chapter 2: Soft Skills: Demanded by Every Employer
  - Chapter 3: Communication Skills: Spoken English, Phonetics, Accent, Intonation
  - Chapter 4: Your Resume or Curriculum Vitae: The First Step Forward
  - Chapter 5: Group Discussion: A Test of Your Soft Skills
  - Chapter 6: Interview Skills: How to Face an Interview Board
  - Chapter 7: Presentation Skills: How to Make Effective Presentations
  - Chapter 8: Business Etiquette: How to Behave in a Professional Setting
  - Chapter 9: Corporate Culture: How to Adapt to the Organizational Environment
  - Chapter 10: Time Management: How to Manage Your Time Effectively
  - Chapter 11: Stress Management: How to Cope with Stress at Work
  - Chapter 12: Conflict Management: How to Resolve Conflicts Constructively
  - Chapter 13: Teamwork: How to Work in a Team
  - Chapter 14: Leadership: How to Lead and Motivate Others
  - Chapter 15: Ethics: How to Be Ethical and Responsible

- The book is written by Barun K. Mitra, who is a former professor of English and communication skills at IIT Kharagpur. He has over 40 years of experience in teaching, training, and consulting in the fields of communication and soft skills.
- The book is published by Oxford University Press, which is a leading academic publisher in India and the world. The book was first published in 2012 and has been revised and updated in 2016. The book is available in paperback and e-book formats.
