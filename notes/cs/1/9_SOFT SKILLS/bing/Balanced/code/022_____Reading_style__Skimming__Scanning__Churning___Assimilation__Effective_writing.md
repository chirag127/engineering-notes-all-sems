### Reading style: Skimming; Scanning; Churning & Assimilation, Effective writing

- Reading is a skill that involves extracting meaning from written texts.
- Different reading styles can be used for different purposes and contexts.
- Skimming is a reading style that involves quickly looking over a text to get the main idea or gist.
- Scanning is a reading style that involves searching for specific information or keywords in a text.
- Churning is a reading style that involves reading a text word by word, without understanding the meaning or context.
- Assimilation is a reading style that involves integrating the information from a text with one's prior knowledge and experience, and applying it to new situations.
- Effective writing is a skill that involves communicating one's ideas clearly, concisely, and coherently, using appropriate language, tone, and structure.
- Some tips for effective writing are:
  - Plan and organize your writing before you start.
  - Know your audience and purpose, and tailor your writing accordingly.
  - Use clear and simple language, and avoid jargon, slang, and ambiguity.
  - Use transitions and connectors to link your ideas and paragraphs.
  - Use varied sentence structures and vocabulary, and avoid repetition and redundancy.
  - Use correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation, and proofread your writing carefully.
  - Use feedback and revision to improve your writing.
