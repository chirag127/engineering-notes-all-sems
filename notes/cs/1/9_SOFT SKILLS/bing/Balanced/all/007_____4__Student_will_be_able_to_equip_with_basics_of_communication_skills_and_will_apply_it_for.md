### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for:

- Effective interpersonal communication in various contexts and situations, such as academic, professional, social and personal.
- Developing and delivering oral presentations with clarity, confidence and appropriate language, tone and body language.
- Writing clear, concise and coherent texts for different purposes and audiences, such as essays, reports, letters, emails and summaries.
- Reading and comprehending texts of various genres and levels of difficulty, such as articles, books, journals and manuals.
- Listening and responding to spoken texts of various types and sources, such as lectures, podcasts, interviews and conversations.
- Using appropriate strategies and tools for communication, such as brainstorming, outlining, drafting, revising, editing, proofreading, referencing and feedback.
- Recognizing and avoiding common errors and pitfalls in communication, such as grammatical, lexical, syntactical, semantic, pragmatic and stylistic errors.
- Demonstrating awareness and respect for the diversity and intercultural aspects of communication, such as norms, values, beliefs, customs and etiquette.
