# 2. Demonstrate active listening with comprehension, and the ability to write clear and well-structured emails and proposals.

- Active listening is the process of paying attention to what the speaker is saying, and showing interest and engagement through verbal and non-verbal cues.
- Comprehension is the ability to understand the meaning and intention of the speaker, and to ask relevant questions or provide feedback when needed.
- Writing clear and well-structured emails and proposals is the skill of communicating effectively and professionally in written form, using appropriate language, tone, format, and organization.

Some tips to demonstrate active listening with comprehension, and the ability to write clear and well-structured emails and proposals are:

- For active listening, use techniques such as paraphrasing, summarizing, reflecting, and empathizing to show that you are listening and understanding the speaker.
- For comprehension, ask open-ended questions, clarify doubts, confirm assumptions, and check for understanding to ensure that you and the speaker are on the same page.
- For writing emails and proposals, follow the basic structure of introduction, body, and conclusion, and use clear and concise language, proper grammar and punctuation, and polite and respectful tone.
- For writing emails, use a relevant subject line, address the recipient appropriately, state the purpose and main points of the email, and end with a call to action or a courteous sign-off.
- For writing proposals, use a catchy title, provide an executive summary, outline the problem and the solution, describe the benefits and the costs, and include a timeline and a conclusion.
