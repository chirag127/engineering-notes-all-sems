# 1. Students will be enabled to understand the correct usage of grammar.

Grammar is the set of rules and conventions that govern how words and sentences are structured and used in a language. Grammar helps to communicate clearly and effectively, and to avoid misunderstandings and errors. 

Some of the main aspects of grammar are:

- Parts of speech: These are the categories of words based on their function and meaning, such as nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, and interjections.
- Sentence structure: This is the way words are arranged and organized in a sentence, following the rules of syntax and word order. A sentence usually consists of a subject, a predicate, and a punctuation mark.
- Tenses: These are the forms of verbs that indicate the time and aspect of an action or state, such as present, past, future, simple, continuous, perfect, and perfect continuous.
- Voice: This is the way a verb expresses the relationship between the subject and the object of a sentence, such as active or passive.
- Mood: This is the way a verb expresses the attitude or mode of the speaker or writer, such as indicative, imperative, subjunctive, or conditional.
- Modality: This is the way a verb expresses the degree of certainty, possibility, necessity, or obligation of an action or state, such as modal verbs (can, could, may, might, must, shall, should, will, would) and modal adverbs (certainly, probably, possibly, etc.).
- Agreement: This is the way words match or correspond with each other in terms of number, person, gender, or case, such as subject-verb agreement and noun-pronoun agreement.
- Punctuation: These are the symbols and marks that separate and clarify the elements of a sentence, such as commas, periods, question marks, exclamation marks, quotation marks, apostrophes, colons, semicolons, hyphens, dashes, parentheses, brackets, and ellipses.

To understand the correct usage of grammar, students need to:

- Learn the definitions and examples of the different aspects of grammar.
- Practice identifying and applying the rules and conventions of grammar in various contexts and situations.
- Review and revise their own and others' written and spoken language for grammatical accuracy and clarity.
- Seek feedback and guidance from teachers, peers, and reliable sources on their grammatical performance and improvement.
