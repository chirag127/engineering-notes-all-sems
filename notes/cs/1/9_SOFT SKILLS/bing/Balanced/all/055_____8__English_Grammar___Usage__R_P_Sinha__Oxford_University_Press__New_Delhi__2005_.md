# 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

- This is a book that covers the basic and advanced aspects of English grammar and usage.
- The book is divided into 12 units, each focusing on a different topic such as parts of speech, tenses, clauses, voice, modals, etc.
- The book provides clear explanations, examples, exercises, and tests for each topic.
- The book also includes a glossary of grammatical terms, a list of common errors, and a key to the exercises and tests.
- The book is suitable for students, teachers, and learners of English as a second or foreign language.
- The book aims to help the readers improve their accuracy, fluency, and confidence in using English.
