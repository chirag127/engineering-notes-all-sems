# Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation

- Ted Talk is a platform that features speakers from various fields and backgrounds who share their ideas and insights on various topics.
- Ted Talk audio listening with script reading is a technique that can help learners improve their listening comprehension, vocabulary, and pronunciation skills.
- Pronunciation is the way a word or a language is spoken, or the manner in which someone utters a word.
- Some of the benefits of using Ted Talk audio listening with script reading and pronunciation are:

  - It exposes learners to authentic and diverse spoken English from different speakers and accents.
  - It helps learners to follow the main ideas and details of the speakers' messages and to check their understanding by reading the transcripts.
  - It enables learners to learn new words and expressions and to see how they are used in context.
  - It allows learners to practice their pronunciation by imitating the speakers' intonation, stress, and rhythm.
  - It enhances learners' confidence and motivation to communicate in English.

- Some of the steps to use Ted Talk audio listening with script reading and pronunciation are:

  - Choose a Ted Talk that interests you and that matches your level of English proficiency.
  - Listen to the audio without reading the transcript and try to understand the main points and the speaker's purpose.
  - Listen to the audio again and read the transcript at the same time. Pay attention to the words and phrases that you don't know or that are difficult to pronounce.
  - Look up the meanings and pronunciations of the unfamiliar words and phrases using a dictionary or an online tool.
  - Listen to the audio again and repeat after the speaker. Try to mimic the speaker's voice, tone, and speed. Focus on the sounds, syllables, and words that are stressed or emphasized.
  - Record yourself reading the transcript and compare your pronunciation with the speaker's. Identify your strengths and areas for improvement.
