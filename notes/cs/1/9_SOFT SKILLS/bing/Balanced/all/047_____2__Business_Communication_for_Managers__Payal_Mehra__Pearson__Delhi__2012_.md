# Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012

- This is a book written by Payal Mehra, an Associate Professor of Communication at the Indian Institute of Management Lucknow, a premier business school of India.
- The book was published by Pearson Publishing in February 2012 and is aimed at MBA students and managers who want to improve their communication skills in various business contexts.
- The book covers all the major communication topics included in MBA syllabi across the country, such as:
  - Theories and models of communication
  - Interpersonal communication and listening skills
  - Non-verbal communication and body language
  - Business writing and presentation skills
  - Business etiquette and cross-cultural communication
  - Communication in teams and groups
  - Communication in conflict and negotiation
  - Communication in leadership and motivation
  - Communication in crisis and change management
  - Communication audit and evaluation
- The book is student-friendly, practical and example-driven, with numerous case studies, exercises, activities, tips and illustrations from real-life business scenarios .
- The book also provides online resources for instructors and students, such as PowerPoint slides, test banks, instructor's manual and video clips .
- The book has received positive reviews from academics and practitioners, who have praised its comprehensive coverage, clarity, relevance and applicability of the concepts and skills .
- The book has a second edition, which was published in 2016, with updated content, new cases and examples, and more emphasis on digital communication and social media.
