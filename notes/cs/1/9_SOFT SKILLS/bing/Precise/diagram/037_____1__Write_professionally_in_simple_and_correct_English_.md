### 1. Write professionally in simple and correct English.

- Use proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- Avoid using slang, jargon, or colloquial language.
- Use a formal tone and avoid using contractions.
- Use active voice instead of passive voice.
- Use clear and concise language.
- Organize your writing into paragraphs with clear topic sentences.
- Use transitional words and phrases to connect ideas.
- Proofread your writing to ensure it is free of errors.
