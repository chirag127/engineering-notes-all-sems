### 2. Students will be able to converse well with effective speaking and listening skills in English.

Effective speaking and listening skills are essential for students to be able to communicate and converse well in English. Here are some points to consider:

1. **Practice speaking and listening regularly:** Regular practice can help students improve their speaking and listening skills. This can be done through activities such as conversation practice with a partner, listening to English podcasts or audiobooks, and participating in group discussions.

2. **Expand vocabulary:** A good vocabulary is essential for effective communication. Students can expand their vocabulary by reading, using flashcards, and learning new words and phrases.

3. **Pay attention to pronunciation and intonation:** Proper pronunciation and intonation can greatly improve the clarity and effectiveness of spoken communication. Students can practice their pronunciation and intonation by listening to native speakers and repeating after them.

4. **Use appropriate body language:** Body language can convey a lot of information and can greatly enhance communication. Students should pay attention to their body language and use appropriate gestures and facial expressions when speaking.

5. **Listen actively:** Active listening involves paying attention to the speaker, providing feedback, and asking questions to clarify understanding. Students should practice active listening to improve their comprehension and communication skills.

By following these points, students can improve their speaking and listening skills and be able to converse well in English.
