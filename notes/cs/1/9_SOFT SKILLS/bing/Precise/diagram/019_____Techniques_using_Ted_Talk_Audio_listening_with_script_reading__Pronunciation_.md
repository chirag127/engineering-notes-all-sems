### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation:

1. **Listen and read along**: One technique is to listen to the audio of a Ted Talk while reading along with the script. This can help improve comprehension and pronunciation by allowing the listener to see how the words are spelled and how they are pronounced in context.

2. **Pause and repeat**: Another technique is to pause the audio periodically and repeat what was just said. This can help improve pronunciation by allowing the listener to practice saying the words and phrases correctly.

3. **Imitate the speaker**: A third technique is to try to imitate the speaker's intonation, rhythm, and pronunciation. This can help improve pronunciation by allowing the listener to practice speaking in a more natural and fluent manner.

4. **Take notes**: Taking notes while listening to a Ted Talk can also be helpful. This can help improve comprehension by allowing the listener to organize the information and make connections between different ideas.

5. **Discuss with others**: Finally, discussing the Ted Talk with others can also be beneficial. This can help improve comprehension, pronunciation, and speaking skills by allowing the listener to practice expressing their thoughts and ideas in a conversational setting.
