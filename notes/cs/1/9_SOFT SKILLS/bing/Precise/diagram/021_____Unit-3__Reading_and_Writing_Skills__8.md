### Unit-3: Reading and Writing Skills: 8

1. **Reading Skills:** Reading is an essential skill for academic success. It involves the ability to understand and interpret written text, and to extract meaning from it.

2. **Types of Reading:** There are several types of reading, including skimming, scanning, intensive reading, and extensive reading. Each type of reading serves a different purpose and requires different skills.

3. **Skimming:** Skimming involves quickly looking over a text to get a general idea of its content. This is useful when you need to quickly determine if a text is relevant to your needs.

4. **Scanning:** Scanning involves quickly searching for specific information within a text. This is useful when you need to find a particular piece of information, such as a date or a name.

5. **Intensive Reading:** Intensive reading involves carefully reading a text in detail, with the goal of achieving a deep understanding of the content. This is useful when you need to study a text for academic purposes.

6. **Extensive Reading:** Extensive reading involves reading large amounts of text for pleasure or general knowledge. This type of reading can help improve your vocabulary and overall reading skills.

7. **Writing Skills:** Writing is another essential skill for academic success. It involves the ability to communicate your ideas and thoughts effectively through written text.

8. **Types of Writing:** There are several types of writing, including descriptive, narrative, expository, and persuasive writing. Each type of writing serves a different purpose and requires different skills.

9. **Descriptive Writing:** Descriptive writing involves using vivid language to describe a person, place, or thing. This type of writing is often used in creative writing, such as poetry or fiction.

10. **Narrative Writing:** Narrative writing involves telling a story. This type of writing is often used in creative writing, such as short stories or novels.

11. **Expository Writing:** Expository writing involves explaining or informing. This type of writing is often used in academic writing, such as essays or research papers.

12. **Persuasive Writing:** Persuasive writing involves convincing the reader of a particular point of view. This type of writing is often used in argumentative essays or opinion pieces.
