### Chronological etc, Official and Business Letter writing, Agenda, Notices

1. **Chronological:** Chronological refers to the arrangement of events in the order in which they occurred. This is a common format used in history writing, where events are presented in the order in which they happened.

2. **Official and Business Letter writing:** Official and business letters are formal letters used for communication in a professional setting. These letters follow a specific format and use formal language. They are used for various purposes such as making inquiries, placing orders, making complaints, and requesting information.

3. **Agenda:** An agenda is a list of items to be discussed or acted upon during a meeting. It is usually prepared in advance and distributed to the participants before the meeting. The agenda helps to keep the meeting focused and on track.

4. **Notices:** A notice is a written or printed announcement that conveys information to a specific audience. Notices are commonly used to inform people about events, changes, or other important information. They are usually brief and to the point, and are often posted in a public place or distributed to a specific group of people.
