### 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

- Personality Development & Soft Skills is a book written by Barun K. Mitra and published by Oxford University Press in New Delhi in 2012.
- The book focuses on the development of an individual's personality and the acquisition of soft skills.
- Soft skills refer to a set of interpersonal and communication skills that are essential for success in the workplace and in life.
- The book covers topics such as self-awareness, emotional intelligence, communication skills, time management, and leadership.
- It provides practical tips and exercises to help readers develop their personality and improve their soft skills.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- It is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their personal and professional development.
