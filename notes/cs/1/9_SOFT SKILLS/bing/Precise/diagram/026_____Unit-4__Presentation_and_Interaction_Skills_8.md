### Unit-4: Presentation and Interaction Skills

1. **Presentation Skills**: The ability to effectively communicate information and ideas to an audience through a structured and engaging presentation.
    - **Preparation**: Research and organize the content, create visual aids, and rehearse the presentation.
    - **Delivery**: Use clear and concise language, maintain eye contact, and use body language to engage the audience.
    - **Engagement**: Encourage audience participation and respond to questions and feedback.
2. **Interaction Skills**: The ability to effectively communicate and collaborate with others in a professional setting.
    - **Active Listening**: Pay attention to what others are saying, ask clarifying questions, and provide feedback.
    - **Effective Communication**: Use clear and concise language, be aware of nonverbal cues, and adapt communication style to the audience.
    - **Collaboration**: Work effectively with others to achieve a common goal, share ideas and resources, and resolve conflicts.

These skills are essential for success in the professional world and can be developed through practice and training.
