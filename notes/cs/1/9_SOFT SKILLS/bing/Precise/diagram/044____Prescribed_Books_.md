## Prescribed Books:

- Prescribed books are books that are recommended by an educational institution or a teacher for a particular course or subject.
- These books are chosen based on their relevance to the course material and their ability to provide comprehensive information on the subject.
- Prescribed books are often used as the primary source of information for students and are considered essential reading for the course.
- Students are expected to read and study the prescribed books in order to gain a thorough understanding of the subject matter.
- In some cases, prescribed books may be accompanied by additional reading materials such as articles, research papers, or other books that provide supplementary information.
- It is important for students to obtain the prescribed books for their course in order to ensure that they have access to the necessary information for their studies.
- Prescribed books can be purchased from bookstores or online retailers, or they may be available for loan from the library.
- It is the responsibility of the student to ensure that they have the necessary books for their course and to keep up with the required reading.
