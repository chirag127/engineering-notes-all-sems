### 2. Demonstrate active listening with comprehension, and the ability to write clear and well-structured emails and proposals.

- **Active listening** involves paying full attention to the speaker, avoiding distractions, and providing verbal and non-verbal feedback to show understanding and engagement.
- To demonstrate active listening, one can use techniques such as paraphrasing, summarizing, and asking clarifying questions.
- Comprehension is the ability to understand and interpret the information being conveyed by the speaker.
- Writing clear and well-structured emails and proposals involves organizing information in a logical and coherent manner, using appropriate language and tone, and following standard formatting conventions.
- Effective emails and proposals should have a clear purpose, be concise and to the point, and provide all necessary information to the recipient.
- It is important to proofread and edit emails and proposals before sending them to ensure that they are free of errors and convey the intended message effectively.
