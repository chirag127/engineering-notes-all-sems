### 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

- English Grammar & Usage is a book written by R.P.Sinha and published by Oxford University Press in New Delhi in 2005.
- The book covers various aspects of English grammar and usage.
- It is intended to help readers improve their understanding and use of the English language.
- The book is organized into chapters, each covering a specific topic related to English grammar and usage.
- Topics covered in the book include parts of speech, sentence structure, verb tenses, and punctuation.
- The book also includes exercises and examples to help readers practice and apply the concepts covered in the book.
- English Grammar & Usage is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
