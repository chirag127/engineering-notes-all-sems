### 4. Practical Communication by L.U.B. Pandey; A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.; Krishan Nagar

- Practical Communication is a book written by L.U.B. Pandey.
- It is published by A.I.T.B.S. Publications India Ltd.
- The publisher is located in Krishan Nagar.
- The book focuses on practical aspects of communication.
- It is a useful resource for those looking to improve their communication skills in a practical manner.
- The book covers various topics related to communication, including verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, and effective writing.
- It is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- The book is suitable for students, professionals, and anyone looking to improve their communication skills.
