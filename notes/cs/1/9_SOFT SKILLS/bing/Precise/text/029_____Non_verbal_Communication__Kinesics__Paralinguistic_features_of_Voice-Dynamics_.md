### Non verbal Communication: Kinesics, Paralinguistic features of Voice-Dynamics

Nonverbal communication refers to the ways in which we communicate without using words. It includes body language, facial expressions, gestures, and tone of voice. Nonverbal communication is an important part of how we convey meaning and emotions to others.

1. **Kinesics** refers to the study of body movements, including facial expressions, gestures, and posture. It is an important aspect of nonverbal communication, as our body language can convey a great deal of information about our emotions, attitudes, and intentions.

2. **Paralinguistic features of Voice-Dynamics** refer to the nonverbal elements of speech, such as tone, pitch, volume, and rate. These features can convey a great deal of information about the speaker's emotions and attitudes, and can also be used to emphasize or clarify the meaning of the words being spoken.

In summary, nonverbal communication is an important part of how we convey meaning and emotions to others. Kinesics and paralinguistic features of voice-dynamics are two important aspects of nonverbal communication that can provide valuable information about the speaker's emotions, attitudes, and intentions. It is important to be aware of these nonverbal cues when communicating with others, as they can greatly influence the way our messages are received and interpreted.
