### 2. Business Communication for Managers, Payal Mehra, Pearson, Delhi, 2012.

- Business Communication for Managers is a book written by Payal Mehra and published by Pearson in Delhi in 2012.
- The book focuses on the importance of effective communication in the business world, particularly for managers.
- It covers various aspects of business communication, including verbal and non-verbal communication, listening skills, and cross-cultural communication.
- The book also discusses the role of technology in business communication and provides practical tips for effective communication in various business scenarios.
- It is a valuable resource for managers and business professionals looking to improve their communication skills and build stronger relationships with colleagues, clients, and customers.
