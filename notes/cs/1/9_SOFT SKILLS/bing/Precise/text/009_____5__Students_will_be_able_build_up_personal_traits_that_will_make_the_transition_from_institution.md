### 5. Students will be able build up personal traits that will make the transition from institution

1. **Time management:** Students can develop the ability to prioritize tasks and manage their time effectively, which will help them balance their academic and personal responsibilities.
2. **Self-motivation:** Students can learn to motivate themselves to achieve their goals, which will help them stay focused and driven in their academic and professional pursuits.
3. **Adaptability:** Students can develop the ability to adapt to new situations and challenges, which will help them navigate the transition from an academic institution to the professional world.
4. **Communication skills:** Students can improve their communication skills, which will help them effectively convey their ideas and collaborate with others in a professional setting.
5. **Critical thinking:** Students can develop their critical thinking skills, which will help them analyze information, solve problems, and make informed decisions in their academic and professional lives.
