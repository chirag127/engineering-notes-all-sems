### Leadership Qualities: Impact, Communication Skills for Leaders: Listening

Leadership is the ability to guide, inspire, and influence others to achieve a common goal. Effective leaders possess certain qualities that enable them to make an impact and communicate effectively with their team. Some of these qualities include:

1. **Impact:** Leaders have the ability to make a positive impact on their team and organization. They lead by example and inspire others to achieve their goals.

2. **Communication Skills:** Effective communication is essential for leaders. They must be able to clearly articulate their vision and goals, and communicate effectively with their team.

3. **Listening:** Leaders must be good listeners. They must be able to actively listen to their team members, understand their concerns, and respond appropriately.

Effective leaders possess a combination of these qualities, which enables them to make an impact and communicate effectively with their team. By developing these qualities, leaders can inspire and motivate their team to achieve their goals.
