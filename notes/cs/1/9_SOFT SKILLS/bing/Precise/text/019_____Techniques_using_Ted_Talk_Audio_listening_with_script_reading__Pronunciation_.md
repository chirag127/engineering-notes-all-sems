### Techniques using Ted Talk Audio listening with script reading, Pronunciation:

1. **Listen to the audio while reading the script:** This can help improve your listening and reading skills simultaneously. By following along with the script, you can better understand the speaker's pronunciation, intonation, and emphasis.

2. **Pause and repeat difficult sections:** If you come across a section that is difficult to understand, pause the audio and repeat it several times until you can understand it clearly. This can help improve your listening comprehension.

3. **Practice pronunciation:** After listening to the audio, try to mimic the speaker's pronunciation, intonation, and emphasis. This can help improve your own pronunciation and speaking skills.

4. **Take notes:** While listening to the audio, take notes on the main ideas and important details. This can help improve your listening comprehension and retention of the information.

5. **Discuss the content with others:** After listening to the audio, discuss the content with others to further improve your understanding and retention of the information. This can also help improve your speaking skills.

6. **Use the audio as a model for your own presentations:** If you are preparing for a presentation, use the audio as a model for your own presentation. Pay attention to the speaker's pacing, intonation, and emphasis, and try to incorporate these elements into your own presentation. This can help improve your presentation skills.
