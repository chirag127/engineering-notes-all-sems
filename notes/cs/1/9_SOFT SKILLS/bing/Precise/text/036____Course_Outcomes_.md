## Course Outcomes:

1. Understanding of the subject matter: Students will gain a thorough understanding of the key concepts, theories, and principles of the course.

2. Development of critical thinking skills: Students will learn to analyze, evaluate, and synthesize information to make informed decisions and solve problems.

3. Improvement of communication skills: Students will learn to effectively communicate their ideas and arguments in both written and oral forms.

4. Enhancement of research skills: Students will learn to conduct research using a variety of sources and methods, and to critically evaluate the credibility and relevance of information.

5. Application of knowledge: Students will learn to apply their knowledge and skills to real-world situations and problems.

6. Development of lifelong learning skills: Students will learn to take responsibility for their own learning and to continue to develop their knowledge and skills beyond the course.

7. Collaboration and teamwork: Students will learn to work effectively with others to achieve common goals.

8. Ethical and social responsibility: Students will learn to consider the ethical and social implications of their actions and decisions.
