### Chronological etc, Official and Business Letter writing, Agenda, Notices

- **Chronological:** Chronological order refers to the arrangement of events, items or information in the order in which they occurred or should occur. This is commonly used in writing historical accounts, biographies, and narratives.

- **Official and Business Letter writing:** Official and business letters are formal letters used for communication in a professional setting. These letters follow a specific format and tone, and are used for various purposes such as making inquiries, placing orders, making complaints, or requesting information.

- **Agenda:** An agenda is a list of items to be discussed or acted upon during a meeting or event. It is typically prepared in advance and distributed to participants to help them prepare for the meeting.

- **Notices:** A notice is a written or printed announcement that provides information or instructions to a specific audience. Notices are commonly used to inform people about events, changes, or other important information. They can be posted in public places, sent by mail or email, or published in newspapers or other media.
