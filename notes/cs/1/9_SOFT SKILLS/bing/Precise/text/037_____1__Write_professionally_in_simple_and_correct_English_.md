### 1. Write professionally in simple and correct English.

- Use proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
- Avoid using slang, jargon, or colloquial language.
- Use a formal tone and avoid using contractions.
- Use active voice instead of passive voice.
- Be concise and avoid using unnecessary words or phrases.
- Use proper sentence structure and vary sentence length.
- Use transitional words and phrases to connect ideas and improve the flow of the text.
- Use appropriate vocabulary and avoid using overly complex words or phrases.
- Proofread your writing to ensure it is free of errors and easy to understand.
- Use formatting, such as headings, bullet points, and bold or italic text, to improve the readability of the text.
