### Unit-4: Presentation and Interaction Skills

1. **Presentation Skills:** The ability to effectively communicate information and ideas to an audience through a structured and engaging presentation.
2. **Preparation:** Research and organize the content of the presentation, create visual aids, and practice the delivery.
3. **Delivery:** Use clear and concise language, maintain eye contact, and use body language to engage the audience.
4. **Interaction Skills:** The ability to effectively communicate and collaborate with others in a group or team setting.
5. **Active Listening:** Pay attention to what others are saying, ask clarifying questions, and provide feedback.
6. **Effective Communication:** Use clear and concise language, be respectful, and use appropriate body language.
7. **Conflict Resolution:** Identify and address conflicts in a constructive manner, and work towards finding a mutually beneficial solution.
8. **Collaboration:** Work together towards a common goal, share ideas and resources, and support each other.

These skills are essential for effective communication and collaboration in both personal and professional settings. They can be developed and improved through practice and training.
