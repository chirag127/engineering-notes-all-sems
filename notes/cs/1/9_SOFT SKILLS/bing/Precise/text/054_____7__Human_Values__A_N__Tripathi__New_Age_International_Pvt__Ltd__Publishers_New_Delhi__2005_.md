### 7. Human Values, A.N. Tripathi, New Age International Pvt. Ltd. Publishers New Delhi, 2005

- The book "Human Values" is written by A.N. Tripathi and published by New Age International Pvt. Ltd. Publishers in New Delhi in 2005 .
- The book presents a holistic picture of the good life, which transcends the prevalent narrow understanding of what constitutes success, fulfillment, and happiness in life .
- It offers a fresh interpretation of human values as the collectivity of all those values which enrich the different dimensions of life- material, societal, psychological, aesthetic, ethical, and spiritual .
- A harmonious realization of all these values helps us grow as truly and fully human persons .
- The book synthesizes the insights of both the East and the West and presents an integrated view of the human person and the good life .
