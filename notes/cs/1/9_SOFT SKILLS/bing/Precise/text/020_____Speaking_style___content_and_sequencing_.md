### Speaking style; content and sequencing

1. Speaking style refers to the way a person communicates verbally, including their tone, pace, and use of language.
2. The content of a speech refers to the information or message being conveyed.
3. Sequencing refers to the order in which the content is presented.
4. Effective communication requires a balance between speaking style, content, and sequencing.
5. A speaker's style should be appropriate for the audience and the message being conveyed.
6. The content should be well-organized and presented in a logical sequence to help the audience understand and retain the information.
7. Using techniques such as repetition, summarizing, and signposting can help to clarify the structure and main points of the speech.
8. It is important to consider the audience's prior knowledge and interests when selecting and organizing the content.
9. The use of visual aids, such as slides or handouts, can help to reinforce the content and make it more engaging for the audience.
10. Practice and preparation are key to delivering a successful speech, with a clear and confident speaking style, well-organized content, and effective sequencing.
