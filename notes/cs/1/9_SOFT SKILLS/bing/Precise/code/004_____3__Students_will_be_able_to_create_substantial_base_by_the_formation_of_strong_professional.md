### 3. Students will be able to create substantial base by the formation of strong professional

- A strong professional base is essential for students to succeed in their careers.
- This can be achieved through various means such as internships, networking, and professional development courses.
- Internships provide students with hands-on experience in their field of study and help them apply their knowledge in a practical setting.
- Networking allows students to connect with professionals in their field and learn from their experiences.
- Professional development courses help students acquire new skills and knowledge that are relevant to their careers.
- By forming a strong professional base, students can increase their chances of finding employment and advancing in their careers.
