### 5. Personality Development & Soft Skills, Barun K.Mitra, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2012.

- Personality Development & Soft Skills is a book written by Barun K. Mitra and published by Oxford University Press in New Delhi in 2012.
- The book focuses on the development of an individual's personality and the acquisition of soft skills.
- Soft skills refer to a set of interpersonal and social skills that are essential for personal and professional success.
- The book provides practical tips and techniques for improving communication skills, building self-confidence, managing time effectively, and developing a positive attitude.
- The book also covers topics such as emotional intelligence, leadership, teamwork, and conflict resolution.
- The book is written in a clear and concise manner and is suitable for students, professionals, and anyone interested in personal development.
