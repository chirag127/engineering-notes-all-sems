## Prescribed Books:

Prescribed books are the books that are recommended by an educational institution or a board of education for a particular course or subject. These books are selected by experts in the field and are considered to be the most relevant and comprehensive sources of information for the course.

Some of the benefits of using prescribed books are:

1. They provide a structured and organized approach to learning the subject.
2. They are written by experts in the field and provide accurate and reliable information.
3. They are updated regularly to include the latest developments in the field.
4. They provide a common reference for students and teachers, making it easier to communicate and discuss the subject.

It is important to note that while prescribed books are an excellent resource for learning, they are not the only source of information. Students are encouraged to supplement their learning with additional reading and research. This can help to broaden their understanding of the subject and develop critical thinking skills.
