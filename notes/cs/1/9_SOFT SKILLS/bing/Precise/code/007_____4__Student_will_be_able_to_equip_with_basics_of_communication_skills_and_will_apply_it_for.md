### 4. Student will be able to equip with basics of communication skills and will apply it for:

1. **Effective communication:** Students will learn the basics of effective communication, including active listening, clear and concise speaking, and nonverbal communication.
2. **Interpersonal communication:** Students will learn how to communicate effectively in one-on-one and small group settings, including conflict resolution and negotiation skills.
3. **Public speaking:** Students will learn the basics of public speaking, including how to prepare and deliver a speech, and how to engage and connect with an audience.
4. **Written communication:** Students will learn the basics of written communication, including grammar, punctuation, and style, as well as how to write effectively for different audiences and purposes.
5. **Cross-cultural communication:** Students will learn the basics of cross-cultural communication, including how to communicate effectively with people from different cultural backgrounds, and how to navigate cultural differences.

By applying these skills, students will be able to communicate more effectively in personal, academic, and professional settings.
