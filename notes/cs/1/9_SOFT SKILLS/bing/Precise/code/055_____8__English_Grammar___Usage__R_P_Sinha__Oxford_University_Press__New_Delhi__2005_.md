### 8. English Grammar & Usage, R.P.Sinha, Oxford University Press, New Delhi, 2005.

- This book is a comprehensive guide to English grammar and usage.
- It is written by R.P.Sinha and published by Oxford University Press in New Delhi in 2005.
- The book covers all aspects of English grammar, including parts of speech, sentence structure, punctuation, and usage.
- It is written in a clear and concise manner, making it easy to understand and follow.
- The book is suitable for both native and non-native speakers of English, and is ideal for students, teachers, and anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
- It includes numerous examples and exercises to help readers practice and reinforce their understanding of the material.
- Overall, English Grammar & Usage by R.P.Sinha is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their English language skills.
