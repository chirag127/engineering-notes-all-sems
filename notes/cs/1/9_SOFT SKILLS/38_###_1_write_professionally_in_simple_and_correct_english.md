### 1. Write professionally in simple and correct English.

Writing Professionally:
1. Use clear, concise language: avoid complex words and sentences, communicate ideas effectively.
2. Use proper grammar and spelling: check for errors, maintain a professional tone.
3. Avoid slang, colloquialisms, and informal language: maintain a professional tone.
4. Use active voice: make writing more engaging and direct.
5. Avoid abbreviations and acronyms: unless widely accepted or defined earlier in the text.
6. Use appropriate tone: adapt to the audience and purpose of the writing.
7. Cite sources correctly: acknowledge others' work and avoid plagiarism.
8. Use proper formatting: organize information for easy reading, use headings and bullet points.
