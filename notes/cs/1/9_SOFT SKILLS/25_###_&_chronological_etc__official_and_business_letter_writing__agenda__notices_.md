Unit 2 - Official and Business Letter Writing, Agenda, Notices:

Unit 2 of Business Communication covers the various types of official and business letters, agendas, and notices.

1. Official and Business Letter Writing: Official and business letters are written for formal communication between organizations and individuals. These letters follow a specific format and include elements such as a header, date, address, salutation, body, closing, and signature.

2. Agenda: An agenda is a list of items to be discussed or acted upon at a meeting. It is used to keep the meeting organized and on track, and is typically distributed to attendees in advance of the meeting.

3. Notices: Notices are written to inform individuals or organizations about important events, changes, or decisions. They can be used for a variety of purposes, including announcing meetings, changes in policies, or the availability of new products or services.

In conclusion, Unit 2 of Business Communication covers the various types of official and business letters, agendas, and notices. Understanding the format and purpose of these types of communication is important for effective business communication.
