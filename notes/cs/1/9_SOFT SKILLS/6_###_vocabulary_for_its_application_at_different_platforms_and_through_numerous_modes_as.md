### vocabulary for its application at different platforms and through numerous modes as

Vocabulary refers to the set of words and phrases that a person knows and uses in communication. The application of vocabulary can vary across different platforms and modes of communication.

1. Platforms: Vocabulary can be applied in different platforms such as written communication (e.g. books, emails, reports), spoken communication (e.g. conversation, public speaking), and digital communication (e.g. social media, instant messaging).

2. Modes: Vocabulary can be applied through various modes of communication, including formal and informal. In formal communication, such as business writing or academic writing, a more advanced vocabulary is often used. In informal communication, such as casual conversation or text messaging, a more relaxed vocabulary is often used.

3. Purpose: The purpose of communication also affects the vocabulary used. For example, technical vocabulary is used in scientific writing, while legal vocabulary is used in legal documents.

4. Audience: The audience for the communication also affects the vocabulary used. For example, a vocabulary that is appropriate for a young audience may not be appropriate for an older audience.

In conclusion, vocabulary is a critical component of communication and its application can vary depending on the platform, mode, purpose, and audience of the communication. Understanding the appropriate vocabulary for different situations is an important skill for effective communication.
