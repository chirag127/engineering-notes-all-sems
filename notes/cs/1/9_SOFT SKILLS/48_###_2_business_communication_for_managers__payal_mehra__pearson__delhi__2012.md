2. Business Communication for Managers by Payal Mehra:

Business Communication for Managers is a book written by Payal Mehra and published by Pearson in Delhi in 2012. The book provides a comprehensive guide to effective business communication for managers, covering a range of topics including written and oral communication, interpersonal communication, and cross-cultural communication.

The book is designed to help managers develop the skills they need to communicate effectively in a business environment. It covers key concepts such as the importance of clear and concise language, the use of appropriate tone and style, and the importance of adapting communication to different audiences and contexts.

The book is organized into chapters that cover various aspects of business communication, including writing business letters and reports, giving presentations, and communicating in meetings. It also includes practical examples and exercises to help reinforce the concepts covered.

In conclusion, Business Communication for Managers by Payal Mehra is a comprehensive guide to effective business communication for managers. The book provides a range of practical tips and techniques to help managers develop their communication skills, and covers key concepts such as the importance of clear and concise language, the use of appropriate tone and style, and the importance of adapting communication to different audiences and contexts.
