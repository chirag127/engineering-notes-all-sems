Technical Communication:

Technical Communication is a book written by Meenakshi Raman and S. Sharma and published by Oxford University Press (OUP) in New Delhi. This book is the second edition and provides an in-depth understanding of technical communication, including its purpose, principles, and techniques.

1. Purpose: The purpose of technical communication is to convey technical information to a specific audience in a clear, concise, and effective manner. Technical communication is used in various fields, including engineering, science, and technology, to communicate complex ideas and information to audiences with varying levels of technical expertise.

2. Principles: The principles of technical communication include clarity, conciseness, accuracy, and audience-centeredness. These principles ensure that technical information is communicated effectively to the intended audience.

3. Techniques: The techniques used in technical communication include writing, visual aids, and oral presentations. These techniques are used to convey technical information in a clear and concise manner and to engage the audience.

In conclusion, Technical Communication is a comprehensive guide to technical communication, providing an overview of its purpose, principles, and techniques. This book is written by Meenakshi Raman and S. Sharma and published by Oxford University Press (OUP) in New Delhi and is an important resource for anyone interested in technical communication.
