### Delhi 2013.

Delhi, the capital of India, in 2013 was a city of over 25 million people. It was a year of significant political and social events, including the Delhi Assembly elections and the formation of a new government. The city also faced challenges such as air pollution, traffic congestion, and water scarcity. Despite these challenges, Delhi continued to grow as a hub for business, culture, and education. The city hosted several international events, including the World Culture Festival, which attracted over 3 million people. Overall, 2013 was an important year in the development of Delhi as a global city.
