### Unit-1 Applied Grammar and Usage: 8

Unit 1 - Applied Grammar and Usage:

Unit 1 of Applied Grammar and Usage covers the 8 parts of speech, which are the building blocks of the English language. These include:

1. Nouns: Nouns are words that refer to people, places, things, or ideas. Examples include "dog," "city," and "love."

2. Pronouns: Pronouns are words that take the place of nouns. Examples include "he," "she," "it," and "they."

3. Verbs: Verbs are words that describe actions or states of being. Examples include "run," "is," and "were."

4. Adjectives: Adjectives are words that describe nouns or pronouns. Examples include "red," "happy," and "big."

5. Adverbs: Adverbs are words that describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs. Examples include "quickly," "very," and "well."

6. Prepositions: Prepositions are words that show the relationship between a noun or pronoun and other words in a sentence. Examples include "in," "on," and "with."

7. Conjunctions: Conjunctions are words that connect words, phrases, or clauses. Examples include "and," "or," and "but."

8. Interjections: Interjections are words or phrases that express strong emotion. Examples include "oh," "wow," and "ouch."

In conclusion, Unit 1 of Applied Grammar and Usage covers the 8 parts of speech, which are the building blocks of the English language. Understanding the different parts of speech and how they are used in sentences is essential for effective communication in the English language.
