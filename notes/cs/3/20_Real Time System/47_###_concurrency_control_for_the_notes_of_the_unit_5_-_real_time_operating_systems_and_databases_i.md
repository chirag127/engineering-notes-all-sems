Unit 5 - Real Time Operating Systems and Databases in the subject of Real Time System will cover the concept of Concurrency Control, a critical aspect of real-time systems.

Concurrency Control refers to the techniques used to manage access to shared resources in a real-time system, such as databases, memory, and I/O devices. Concurrency Control is used to prevent conflicts between tasks and to ensure that real-time tasks have access to the resources they need to complete their tasks in a timely manner.
