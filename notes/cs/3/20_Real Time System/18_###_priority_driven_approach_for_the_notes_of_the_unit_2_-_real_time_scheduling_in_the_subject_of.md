### Priority Driven Approach for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System

Priority-driven approach is a scheduling method used in real-time systems to schedule tasks based on their priority. The following are some of the key concepts related to priority-driven approach:

1. Task Scheduling: Task scheduling is the process of determining the order in which tasks are executed in a real-time system, and priority-driven approach is one method of task scheduling.

2. Task Priorities: Task priorities are assigned to tasks in a real-time system, and are used to determine the order in which tasks are executed. Tasks with higher priorities are executed before tasks with lower priorities.

3. Preemptive Scheduling: Preemptive scheduling is a scheduling method that allows tasks with higher priorities to be executed before tasks with lower priorities, even if the tasks with lower priorities have already started executing. Preemptive scheduling is often used in real-time systems to ensure that tasks with higher priorities are executed in a timely manner.

4. Non-Preemptive Scheduling: Non-preemptive scheduling is a scheduling method that does not allow tasks with higher priorities to be executed before tasks with lower priorities, once the tasks with lower priorities have started executing. Non-preemptive scheduling is used in some real-time systems to ensure that tasks are executed in a predictable manner.

5. Priority Inversion: Priority inversion is a situation where a task with a higher priority is blocked from executing because a task with a lower priority is executing. Priority inversion can lead to unpredictable behavior in real-time systems, and can be addressed using various methods, such as priority inheritance, priority ceiling, and more.

6. Performance Evaluation: Performance evaluation is the process of measuring the effectiveness of a scheduling method, and can be performed using metrics such as response time, completion time, and more. Performance evaluation is important for comparing different scheduling methods and for selecting the best method for a given real-time system.

In this unit, you will learn about priority-driven approach, including task scheduling, task priorities, preemptive scheduling, non-preemptive scheduling, priority inversion, and performance evaluation.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of priority-driven approach, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of real-time scheduling.
