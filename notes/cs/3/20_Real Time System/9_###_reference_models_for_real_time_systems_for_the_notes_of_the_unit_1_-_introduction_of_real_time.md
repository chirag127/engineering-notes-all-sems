### Reference Models for Real Time Systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

The Reference Models for Real Time Systems section of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time Systems should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Reference Models (models used as a basis for designing, developing, and evaluating real-time systems)
2. Overview of Reference Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Reference Models (e.g. functional, architectural, operational, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Reference Models (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Functionality of Reference Models (e.g. what reference models are used for, what they provide)
6. Overview of Functionality of Reference Models (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Reference Model Characteristics (e.g. properties of reference models, what makes a good reference model)
8. Overview of Reference Model Characteristics (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Reference Model Selection (e.g. how to choose the right reference model for a particular real-time system)
10. Overview of Reference Model Selection (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Reference Models in Industry Applications (how reference models are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Reference Models for Real Time Systems are an important component of the subject of Real Time Systems. Understanding the principles and techniques of reference models helps students to develop the skills required to design, develop, and evaluate real-time systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
