### Soft and Hard RT Communication systems for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System

The Soft and Hard RT Communication Systems section of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time Systems should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Soft Real-Time Communication Systems (communication systems that provide a best-effort delivery of messages in real-time)
2. Overview of Soft Real-Time Communication Systems (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Definition of Hard Real-Time Communication Systems (communication systems that provide a guaranteed delivery of messages in real-time)
4. Overview of Hard Real-Time Communication Systems (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Characteristics (e.g. reliability, latency, jitter, etc.)
6. Overview of Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Characteristics (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Protocols (e.g. TCP/IP, UDP, etc.)
8. Overview of Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Protocols (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Performance (e.g. how well the systems perform in real-time communication)
10. Overview of Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Applications (e.g. control systems, multimedia systems, etc.)
12. Overview of Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System Applications (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication System in Industry Applications (how the systems are used in various industries)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Soft and Hard Real-Time Communication Systems are an important component of the subject of Real Time Systems, especially in the context of real-time communication. Understanding the principles and techniques of real-time communication systems helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective real-time communication solutions. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
