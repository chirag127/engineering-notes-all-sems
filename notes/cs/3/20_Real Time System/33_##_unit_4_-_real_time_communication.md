## Unit 4 - Real Time Communication

The Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time Systems should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Real Time Communication (communication in real-time systems that is time-sensitive)
2. Overview of Real Time Communication (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Types of Real Time Communication (e.g. synchronous, asynchronous, etc.)
4. Overview of Types of Real Time Communication (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Real Time Communication Protocols (e.g. protocols used in real-time communication systems)
6. Overview of Real Time Communication Protocols (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Real Time Communication in Distributed Systems (e.g. communication in real-time systems that are distributed across multiple nodes)
8. Overview of Real Time Communication in Distributed Systems (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Real Time Communication in Embedded Systems (e.g. communication in real-time systems that are embedded in devices)
10. Overview of Real Time Communication in Embedded Systems (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Real Time Communication in Industry Applications (how real-time communication is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Real Time Communication is an important component of the subject of Real Time Systems. Understanding the principles and techniques of real-time communication helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective real-time communication systems. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
