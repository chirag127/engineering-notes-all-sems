### Model of Real Time Communication for the notes of the Unit 4 - Real Time Communication in the subject of Real Time System

The model of real-time communication is a framework for understanding the communication requirements and constraints in real-time systems. The following are some of the key concepts related to the model of real-time communication:

1. Communication Requirements: Communication requirements in real-time systems include the timing constraints for transmitting and receiving data, the reliability of the communication channel, and the security of the communication channel.

2. Communication Constraints: Communication constraints in real-time systems include the bandwidth of the communication channel, the latency of the communication channel, and the availability of the communication channel.

3. Real-Time Protocols: Real-time protocols are communication protocols that are designed to meet the communication requirements and constraints of real-time systems. Real-time protocols include protocols for transmitting and receiving data, such as TCP/IP, and protocols for ensuring the reliability and security of the communication channel, such as SSL/TLS.

4. Network Topology: Network topology is the arrangement of the components of a communication network, including nodes, links, and routers. Network topology affects the performance and reliability of the communication network, and must be carefully designed to meet the communication requirements and constraints of real-time systems.

5. Quality of Service (QoS): Quality of Service (QoS) is a measure of the performance of a communication network, and includes metrics such as bandwidth, latency, and reliability. QoS is an important factor in the design of real-time communication systems, as it affects the ability of the system to meet the communication requirements and constraints of real-time systems.

6. Performance Evaluation: Performance evaluation is the process of measuring the effectiveness of a communication network, and can be performed using metrics such as QoS, utilization, and more. Performance evaluation is important for comparing different communication networks and for selecting the best network for a given real-time system.

In this unit, you will learn about the model of real-time communication, including communication requirements, communication constraints, real-time protocols, network topology, quality of service (QoS), and performance evaluation.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of real-time communication, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of real-time systems.
