### Model of Real Time Communication

- Real time communication is any online communication that happens in real time, with negligible latency and without intermediate storage  .
- Real time communication can be classified into two types: real time traffic and real time control.
- Real time traffic consists of streams of messages that are generated by their sources and delivered to their respective destinations on a continuous basis.
- Real time traffic includes periodic, aperiodic and sporadic messages, which have different characteristics and requirements.
- Real time control consists of commands and feedback signals that are exchanged between controllers and controlled devices in real time systems.
- Real time control requires high reliability, low latency and bounded jitter.
- In the model of real time communication, end users of the message application systems are sources and destinations residing in different hosts .
- The network interface of each host contains input queue and output queue, which are allocated to input and output buffers to store queuing information .
- The network consists of routers and links that forward messages from sources to destinations .
- The network can be modeled as a graph, where nodes represent routers and hosts, and edges represent links .
- The network can have different topologies, such as bus, ring, star, mesh, tree, etc .
- The network can have different protocols, such as Ethernet, Token Ring, ATM, etc .
- The network can have different performance metrics, such as throughput, delay and jitter .
- Throughput is the amount of data that can be transmitted over a link or a network in a given time .
- Delay is the time taken for a message to travel from source to destination .
- Jitter is the variation in delay for different messages in the same stream .
- The model of real time communication can be used to analyze and design real time systems that involve communication among different components .
- The model of real time communication can be used to evaluate the performance and quality of service of real time systems .
- The model of real time communication can be used to compare and select different communication technologies and protocols for real time systems .
