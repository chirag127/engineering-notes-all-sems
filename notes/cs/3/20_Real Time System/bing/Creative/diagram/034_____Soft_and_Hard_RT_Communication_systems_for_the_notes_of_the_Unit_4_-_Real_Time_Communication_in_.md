### Soft and Hard Real Time Communication Systems

- Real time communication systems are systems that exchange information between two or more entities with strict timing constraints.
- Real time communication systems can be classified into two types: hard real time and soft real time .
- The difference between hard and soft real time communication systems is the consequences of missing the deadlines .
- A deadline is the maximum time allowed for a task to complete its execution.
- In hard real time communication systems, missing a deadline can result in catastrophic failure or unacceptable loss . For example, a missile guidance system, a pacemaker, or an air traffic control system are hard real time communication systems .
- In soft real time communication systems, missing a deadline can result in degraded performance or reduced quality of service, but not in failure or loss . For example, a video conferencing system, a multimedia player, or a web server are soft real time communication systems .
- Hard real time communication systems are deterministic, meaning that they can guarantee the worst-case execution time of each task .
- Soft real time communication systems are probabilistic, meaning that they can only estimate the average or expected execution time of each task .
- Hard real time communication systems require strict scheduling algorithms and priority assignment to ensure that all deadlines are met .
- Soft real time communication systems can use more flexible scheduling algorithms and priority assignment to optimize the overall performance or quality of service .
- Hard real time communication systems are more difficult to design, implement, and verify than soft real time communication systems .
- Soft real time communication systems are more adaptable and scalable than hard real time communication systems .
