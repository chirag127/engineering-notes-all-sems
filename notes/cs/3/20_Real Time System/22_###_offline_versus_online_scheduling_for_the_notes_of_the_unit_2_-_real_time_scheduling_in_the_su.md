### Offline Versus Online Scheduling for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System

Offline and online scheduling are two approaches to scheduling tasks in real-time systems. The following are some of the key concepts related to offline and online scheduling:

1. Scheduling: Scheduling is the process of determining the order in which tasks are executed in a real-time system. Scheduling is an important aspect of real-time systems, as it can affect the performance, reliability, and safety of the system.

2. Offline Scheduling: Offline scheduling is a scheduling approach in which the scheduling of tasks is performed before the system is executed. In offline scheduling, the scheduling algorithm has complete information about the tasks, including their release times, deadlines, and processing requirements.

3. Online Scheduling: Online scheduling is a scheduling approach in which the scheduling of tasks is performed during the execution of the system. In online scheduling, the scheduling algorithm does not have complete information about the tasks, and must make decisions about the scheduling of tasks based on the information available at the time.

4. Advantages: Offline scheduling has the advantage of being able to perform the scheduling of tasks in a more controlled environment, with complete information about the tasks. Online scheduling has the advantage of being able to respond to changes in the system, such as the arrival of new tasks or changes in the processing requirements of existing tasks.

5. Disadvantages: Offline scheduling has the disadvantage of being inflexible, as it cannot respond to changes in the system. Online scheduling has the disadvantage of being more complex, as the scheduling algorithm must make decisions about the scheduling of tasks based on incomplete information.
