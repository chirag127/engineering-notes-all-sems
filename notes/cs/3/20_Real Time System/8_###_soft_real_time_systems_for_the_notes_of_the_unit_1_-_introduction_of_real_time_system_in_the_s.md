### Soft Real Time Systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System will cover the concept of Soft Real Time Systems, a type of real-time system.

Soft Real Time Systems are real-time systems that are not subject to strict timing constraints, but still require timely processing of data. They are used in applications where the consequences of missing a deadline are not catastrophic, but still require timely processing.
