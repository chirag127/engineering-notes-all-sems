### Definition for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

Real-time systems are computer systems that process and respond to external events within a fixed time frame. These systems are designed to operate in a time-critical environment and must meet strict timing constraints. In this unit, we will explore the fundamental concepts of real-time systems and their importance in various fields.

The following are the key points that will be covered in this unit:

1. Definition of Real-Time Systems: The definition and characteristics of real-time systems will be discussed, including the different types of real-time systems.

2. Timing Constraints: The importance of timing constraints in real-time systems and how they affect system design will be explained.

3. Real-Time Operating Systems: The role of real-time operating systems in real-time system design and their features will be discussed.

4. Real-Time Applications: The different types of real-time applications, including hard and soft real-time systems, will be explored.

5. Real-Time Scheduling: The various real-time scheduling algorithms and their advantages and disadvantages will be discussed.

6. Real-Time Communication: The communication protocols used in real-time systems and their importance will be explained.

7. Case Studies: Real-time systems in various fields, such as aerospace, automotive, and medical, will be presented as case studies to illustrate the importance of real-time systems in these industries.

In summary, this unit will provide a comprehensive introduction to real-time systems and their importance in various fields. The topics covered in this unit will provide a foundation for further study in the subject of real-time systems.
