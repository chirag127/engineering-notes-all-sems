
### Clock Driven Approach for the notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the subject of Real Time System

1. Clock driven scheduling is a method of scheduling tasks in a real-time system. 
2. This method is based on the idea that the system will execute tasks based on the clock time. 
3. The clock time is based on a predefined schedule of tasks that are to be executed at specified times. 
4. The tasks are usually stored in a queue and are executed in the order they are stored. 
5. This method is often used in embedded systems where the tasks must be completed at specific times. 
6. Clock driven scheduling is also used in operating systems where tasks are scheduled to be executed at specific times. 
7. This method is often used in real-time systems where tasks must be completed in a timely manner. 
8. The main advantage of this method is that it is easy to implement and it is possible to predict the execution time of tasks. 
9. The main disadvantage of this method is that it is not suitable for tasks that require dynamic scheduling. 
10. Clock driven scheduling is also not suitable for tasks that require a large amount of resources to be allocated.
