
### Internet and Resource Reservation Protocols

* Internet Protocol (IP) is a set of rules that govern the transmission of data over the internet. It is the primary protocol used for communication between computers on the internet and is responsible for routing data packets from one computer to another.

* Resource Reservation Protocol (RSVP) is a network-layer protocol used to reserve resources in a network. It is used to ensure that the quality of service (QoS) requirements of the applications are met. RSVP works by reserving resources ahead of time and then releasing them when they are no longer needed.

* The combination of IP and RSVP is used to provide end-to-end quality of service (QoS) for real-time applications such as voice and video. RSVP is used to reserve resources for the real-time application, while IP is used to route the data packets to their destination.
