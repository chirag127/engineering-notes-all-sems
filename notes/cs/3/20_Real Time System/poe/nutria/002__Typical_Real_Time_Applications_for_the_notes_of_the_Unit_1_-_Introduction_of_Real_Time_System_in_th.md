
### Typical Real Time Applications
1. Automotive: Cruise control systems, engine control systems, and anti-lock braking systems are all examples of real-time applications in the automotive industry.
2. Aerospace: Aircraft navigation systems and flight control systems are examples of real-time applications in the aerospace industry.
3. Robotics: Robotics applications often require real-time control and coordination of multiple components.
4. Industrial Automation: Automated production lines and process control systems are examples of real-time applications in industrial automation.
5. Networking: Network routers and switches are examples of real-time applications in networking.
6. Medical: Medical imaging and patient monitoring systems are examples of real-time applications in the medical field.
7. Military: Missile guidance systems and radar systems are examples of real-time applications in the military.
