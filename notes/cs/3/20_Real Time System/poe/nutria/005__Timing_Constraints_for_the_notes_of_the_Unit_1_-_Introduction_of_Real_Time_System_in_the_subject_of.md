
### Timing Constraints for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System in the subject of Real Time System

1. Real-time systems are systems that must respond to external events within a certain time frame.
2. Timing constraints are used to ensure that the system meets its deadlines and that the system behavior is predictable.
3. The timing constraints of a real-time system depend on the type of task that the system is required to perform.
4. Hard real-time systems have timing constraints that must be met for the system to operate correctly.
5. Soft real-time systems have timing constraints that must be met for the system to operate optimally.
6. Timing constraints are usually expressed in terms of deadlines, response times, and throughput.
7. The scheduling algorithm used by the system must be able to meet the timing constraints of the system.
8. Timing constraints can be used to limit the amount of resources that a system can use.
9. Timing constraints can also be used to ensure that the system does not exceed its capacity.
