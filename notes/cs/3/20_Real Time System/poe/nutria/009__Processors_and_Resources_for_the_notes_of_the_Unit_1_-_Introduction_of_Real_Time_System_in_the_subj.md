
### Processors and Resources for the Notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction of Real Time System

1. Processors:
    * Real-time processors are designed to process data as quickly as possible and meet the deadlines of applications. 
    * The most common real-time processors are central processing units (CPUs) and digital signal processors (DSPs). 
    * CPUs are used for general purpose computing and are capable of multitasking, while DSPs are designed to perform specific, repetitive tasks quickly and efficiently. 
    * Other types of processors used in real-time systems include microcontrollers, application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), and field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs). 

2. Resources: 
    * Resources are the components of a system that are used to perform tasks. 
    * In real-time systems, resources include memory, storage, and input/output (I/O) devices. 
    * Memory is used to store data and instructions, while storage is used to store large amounts of data for long-term use. 
    * I/O devices are used to send and receive data from external systems. 
    * Other resources used in real-time systems include networks, sensors, and actuators.
