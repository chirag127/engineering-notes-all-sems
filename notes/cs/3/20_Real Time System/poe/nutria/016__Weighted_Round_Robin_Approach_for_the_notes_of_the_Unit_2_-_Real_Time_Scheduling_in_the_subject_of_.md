
### Weighted Round Robin Approach for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Real Time Scheduling in the Subject of Real Time System

1. Weighted Round Robin (WRR) is a scheduling algorithm used in real-time systems to ensure that each process is allocated a certain amount of CPU time.
2. WRR is based on the Round Robin scheduling algorithm, but it adds the concept of weights to it.
3. Each process is assigned a weight, which is used to determine the amount of CPU time it will receive.
4. The higher the weight, the more CPU time the process will receive.
5. WRR is used in situations where processes have different priorities or levels of importance.
6. WRR ensures that processes with higher weights receive more CPU time than processes with lower weights.
7. WRR is a preemptive scheduling algorithm, meaning that it can be interrupted by processes with higher weights.
8. WRR is a good choice for real-time systems because it ensures that important processes receive the CPU time they need.
