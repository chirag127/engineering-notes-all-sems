
### Soft Real Time Systems 

* Soft real-time systems are computer systems that guarantee that certain tasks will be completed within a certain amount of time. 
* These systems are often used in fields such as robotics, aerospace, and medical imaging. 
* Soft real-time systems are characterized by their ability to meet deadlines, but with some flexibility. 
* The deadlines for these systems are not absolute, and the system can tolerate some delay in completing tasks without compromising the overall performance of the system. 
* Soft real-time systems are typically used when a system needs to respond to external events in a timely manner, but there is some flexibility in the timing of the response. 
* Examples of soft real-time systems include computer games, video streaming, and interactive simulations.
