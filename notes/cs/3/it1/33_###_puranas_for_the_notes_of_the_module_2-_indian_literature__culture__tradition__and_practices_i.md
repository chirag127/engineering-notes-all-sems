### Puranas for the notes of the Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

The Puranas are a genre of Hindu sacred texts that were written in Sanskrit between 400 BCE and 1500 CE. They are a collection of myths, legends, and religious teachings that are meant to explain the history of the universe, the creation of the world, and the genealogy of the gods and heroes.

The word “Purana” means “ancient” or “old” in Sanskrit, and the Puranas are considered to be a secondary source of Hindu religious knowledge, after the Vedas. There are 18 major Puranas and 18 minor Puranas, and they are divided into two categories: Sattvic Puranas and Rajasic Puranas. The Sattvic Puranas are considered to be more spiritual and moral in nature, while the Rajasic Puranas are considered to be more worldly and focused on worldly power.

The Puranas are an important source of Hindu mythology and religious teachings, and they contain stories about the creation of the world, the adventures of the gods and heroes, and the genealogy of the gods and kings. They also contain teachings on dharma, or moral and ethical conduct, as well as descriptions of the afterlife and the cycle of reincarnation.

In addition to religious and mythological content, the Puranas also contain information on Hindu cosmology, astronomy, geography, and genealogy. They provide a rich source of information on Hindu culture and tradition, and they continue to be an important part of Hindu religious life and practice.
