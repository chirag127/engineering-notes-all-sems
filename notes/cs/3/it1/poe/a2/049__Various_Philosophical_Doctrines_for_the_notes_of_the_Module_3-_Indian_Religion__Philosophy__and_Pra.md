### Various Philosophical Doctrines

- Samkhya: Dualistic philosophy. Proposes two eternal realities - Prakriti (matter) and Purusha (consciousness). Aim is to attain liberation of Purusha from Prakriti.
- Yoga: Focuses on meditation and physical postures to attain liberation. Follows Samkhya metaphysics but believes Ishvara (God) is the supreme purusha who directs passive Prakriti.
- Nyaya: Focuses on epistemology and logic. Proposes four sources of valid knowledge - perception, inference, comparison, and testimony. Aims to remove ignorance and attain liberation.
- Vaisheshika: Proposes atomistic pluralism. Says universe is composed of eternal atoms and Categories (substance, quality, action, universals, and particulars). Liberation results from correct knowledge of Categories.
- Mimamsa: Focuses on interpretations of Vedas. Believes in performance of Vedic sacrifices and karma as means to liberation. Later emphasizes jnana (knowledge) as path to liberation.
- Vedanta: Major proponent of non-dualism (Advaita). Proposes Brahman as the eternal, infinite, conscious reality. Aims to attain liberation through removing ignorance of one's true identity as Brahman.
