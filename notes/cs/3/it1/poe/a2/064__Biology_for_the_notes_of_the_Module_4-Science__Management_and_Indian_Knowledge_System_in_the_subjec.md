### Biology for the notes of the Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. The study of life and living organisms is known as Biology. It includes structure, function, growth, evolution, distribution and taxonomy of all living things.
2. The basic unit of life is a cell. The cell is composed of various molecules like proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, nucleic acids, etc. These molecules are essential for the structure, organization, functioning, and reproduction of cells.
3. The cell is the structural and functional unit of life. It has a definite shape and size surrounded by a membrane. The nucleus is the control center of the cell that contains DNA. Other cell organelles carry out specific functions to sustain the life of the cell.
4. The diversity of living organisms is a result of evolution. The theory of evolution states that all species of organisms arise and develop through natural selection acting on small inherited variations that occur by mutation and recombination of genes. Evolution leads to the emergence of new species and the extinction of others.
5. The living world is classified into five kingdoms: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, and Animalia. The species under each kingdom share a set of common characteristics. The modern taxonomic classification is based on domains, followed by kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus, and species.
