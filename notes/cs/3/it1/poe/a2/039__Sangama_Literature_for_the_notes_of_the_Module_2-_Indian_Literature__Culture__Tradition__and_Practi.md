### Sangama Literature

- Sangama literature refers to a body of poetic literature composed in Tamil during the Sangam period from around 300 BCE to 300 CE.
- The Sangam literature collection includes 2,381 poems composed by 473 poets, including both men and women, on a wide range of topics from love and sexual union to war, death, and renunciation.
- The poems were composed in the conventions of Tamil literature, including metrics based on sound and syntax. The literature provides a vivid social and cultural commentary on ancient Tamil life and thought.
- The poems are divided into eight anthologies based on the theme of the poems: Erotic (akam) and Heroic (puram). The akam poems deal with personal or subjective topics like love and emotion. The puram poems address more public topics like heroism and warfare.
- Sangam literature is highly regarded as an important element of Classical Tamil civilization and has significantly influenced later Tamil literary traditions. The poems provide an important source of information about the ancient Tamil society, culture, and traditions.
