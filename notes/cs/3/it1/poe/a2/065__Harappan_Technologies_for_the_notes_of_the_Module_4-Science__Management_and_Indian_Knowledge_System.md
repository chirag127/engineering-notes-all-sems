### Harappan Technologies

- The Harappans were skilled craftsmen and engineers.
- They made pottery on a wheel, bricks of a uniform size, and intricate stone and metal sculptures.
- They had cloth-making industries and made cotton garments.
- They used a decimal system of weights based on the binary division.
- Their cities had an efficient system of sanitation and water management using underground drains and manholes.
- They had agriculture tools like ploughs made of wood and copper.
- They traded with West Asia and imported precious stones, seashells and finished artefacts.
- They had the knowledge of proto-dentistry as indicated by drilled molar crowns found in excavations.
- Their seals and tablets had an undeciphered script indicating existence of a writing system.
- They made jewellery of gold, silver, ivory, steatite, shell, faience and glazed faience beads.
- Their cities displayed an efficient town planning with a grid pattern of streets.
- Their remains indicate the existence of metallurgy furnaces and production of bronze objects and tools.
- They made terracotta carts and toys which provide glimpses into their culture and daily life.
