### The Seven Limbs of the State

The seven limbs (saptanga) of the state in the Indian tradition are:

1. The king - The head of the state who wields supreme authority.
2. Ministers - They aid and advise the king in governance.
3. Territory - The land over which the king exerts sovereignty.
4. Fortified city - The capital city with a fort that serves as the center of administration.
5. Treasury - The storehouse of wealth and resources of the state.
6. Army - The military force that defends the state and enforces its writ.
7. Allies - Other rulers and entities that are in alliance or treaty with the state.

These seven limbs are indispensable for the functioning, security and prosperity of the state. The presence and proper functioning of all the seven limbs is necessary for a strong and stable state. The relative importance of the limbs has been differently emphasized by various thinkers. But there is general agreement on these seven constituents of the state.
