### The Mahabharata

- One of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India, the other being the Ramayana.
- Composed by Vyasa, it contains over 100,000 shlokas (couplets) and is longer than Homer's Iliad and Odyssey combined.
- The story revolves around the conflict between two groups of cousins, the Kauravas (Duryodhana and his 99 brothers) and Pandavas (Yudhishthir, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakul and Sahadev) which finally lead to Kurukshetra War.
- Major characters include Lord Krishna, who plays the role of a mentor and charioteer to the Pandava prince Arjuna.
- It includes the famous Bhagavad Gita, a scripture that contains spiritual guidance and teachings.
- Portrays a variety of complex relationships between different characters in human conditions and has multiple dimensions - a complex mix of interwoven plots, mythology, philosophy and theology.
- Considered as one of the greatest works in world literature that provides deep insights into ancient Indian culture, ethics, government, duties and purpose of life.
