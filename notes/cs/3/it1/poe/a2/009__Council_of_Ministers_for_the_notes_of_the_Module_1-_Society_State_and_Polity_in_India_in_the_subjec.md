### Council of Ministers

- The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.
- The Prime Minister is the head of the council of ministers. He allocates and distributes portfolios to the ministers.
- The Council of Ministers aids and advises the President in the exercise of his functions.
- The Ministers in charge of departments are individually responsible to the Parliament for the administration of their respective departments.
- The Council of Ministers stands collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha. If there is a policy failure or lapse on the part of the government, the Council of Ministers has to resign.
- The Council of Ministers gets its legitimacy from the fact that it has the confidence of the Lok Sabha. In case it loses the confidence of the Lok Sabha, it must resign.
- The size of the Council of Ministers depends on the discretion of the Prime Minister. However, it should not exceed 15% of the total strength of the Lok Sabha.
