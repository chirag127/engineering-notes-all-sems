### Kautilya’s Arthashastra for the notes of the Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Kautilya’s Arthashastra is an ancient Indian treatise on statecraft, economic policy and military strategy authored by Chanakya, who was also known as Kautilya.
- It was written around 300 BCE and is considered as one of the earliest texts in political science and economics.
- The text consists of 15 books which cover various topics such as the role of the king, military strategy, civil service regulations, etc.
- Some of the key points from Arthashastra are:
-- The king's duty is to ensure the happiness and welfare of his subjects.
-- The king should be adept in duties of both peace and war.
-- Taxes should not be too high and should be levied judiciously.
-- The state should ensure irrigation, mining and trade for economic stability.
-- Maintenance of law and order and protection from external aggression is essential.
-- Espionage and covert operations for the security of the state are encouraged.
-- Regulations for salaries, promotions, punishments, etc. for government officials.
- Kautilya’s Arthashastra provides extraordinary insight into the functioning of the Mauryan administration under Chandragupta Maurya. It gives a holistic view of the ancient Indian polity and society. It continues to be an influential treatise on politics, economics and governance in the modern world.
