### The Vedas for the notes of the Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY.

- The Vedas are the oldest scriptures of Hinduism and are considered to be revealed scriptures. They are comprised of four collections - Rigveda, Yajurveda, Samaveda and Atharvaveda.
- Rigveda is the earliest composition and includes hymns recited by sages in praise of various deities. The hymns mainly focus on Agni (fire) and Indra (king of gods).
- Yajurveda contains the mantras used in rituals and sacrifices. The mantras are in both prose and verse forms.
- Samaveda includes melodies or tunes to be chanted during sacrificial rituals. The verses are mostly from Rigveda.
- Atharvaveda includes magic spells and charms for prevalent diseases and calamities. The hymns are used in auspicious occasions as well as to ward off evil.
- The Vedas are considered Shruti as the primordial sages intuitively perceived cosmic truths. They are written in Vedic Sanskrit and contain philosophy, prayers, rituals and much more. The Vedas form the foundation of Hinduism and have impacted Indian traditions and practices significantly.
