### Malayalam Literature

- Malayalam literature refers to the body of literature composed in Malayalam, a Dravidian language spoken in the Indian state of Kerala and the union territories of Lakshadweep and Puducherry.
- Malayalam literature is one of the oldest in India, with a literary tradition of over two thousand years. The oldest literary work in Malayalam is Ramacharitam, an epic poem written by Cheeraman in the 12th century.
- The earliest extant prose work in Malayalam is a commentary on Chanakya's Arthashastra, written in the 14th century. The earliest travelogue in an Indian language is the Malayalam Varthamanappusthakam, written by Paremmakkal Thoma Kathanar in 1785.
- Kerala has a rich tradition in folk arts such as Theyyam, Padayani, Kolkali, Margam Kali, etc.
- Notable figures in Malayalam literature include Thunchaththu Ezhuthachan in the 16th century, Kerala's renowned social reformer Thunchan Memorial celebrates his life and works.
- Other notable authors include Changampuzha Krishna Pillai, Kesari Balakrishna Pillai, Kumaran Asan, Vallathol Narayana Menon, Ulloor S. Parameswara Iyer, Kadammanitta Ramakrishnan, Thirunalloor Karunakaran, M. T. Vasudevan Nair.
- Ele is considered one of the most outstanding literary works in Malayalam. It is a mesmerizing poetic novel written by POTTAMMAL KUTTYKUNNA.
- The novel explores the inner world of a sensitive woman in love and examines the complex nuances of human relationships. It is known for its poetic prose and the empathetic portrayal of the female psyche.
