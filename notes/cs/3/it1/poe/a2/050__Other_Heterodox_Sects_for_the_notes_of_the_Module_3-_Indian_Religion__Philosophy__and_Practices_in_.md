### Other Heterodox Sects

- Charvakas: Materialists who rejected the Vedas and focussed on direct perception. They believed that nothing exists except matter and there is no afterlife. The goal is happiness in this life through the pursuit of kama (pleasure).
- Ajivikas: Fatalists who believed that everything is predetermined and human effort is futile. They followed strict asceticism and austerity. The goal was to gain merit to ensure a better rebirth.
- Jainism: Founded by Mahavira, Jainism believes in strict non-violence or ahimsa and non-attachment to spiritual progress. They follow ascetic practices and believe in the liberation of the soul from rebirth through self-effort. There are two sects: Digambaras and Svetambaras.
- Buddhism: Founded by Gautama Buddha, Buddhism believes in the Middle Way to reach Nirvana and escape rebirth. The Four Noble Truths and Eightfold Path are core beliefs. There are two main branches: Theravada and Mahayana which include Zen and Tibetan Buddhism.
