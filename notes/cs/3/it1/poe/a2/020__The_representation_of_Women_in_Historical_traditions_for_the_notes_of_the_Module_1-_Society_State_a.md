### The representation of Women in Historical traditions

- In ancient Indian traditions, women were accorded a high status in society. They were considered as equal partners in life with men and were respected for their wisdom and leadership qualities.
- Epics like Ramayana and Mahabharata highlight the strong characters of women like Sita, Draupadi who questioned unjust actions and societal norms. They made their own choices regarding marriage and were equal participants in royal gatherings and battles.
- However, the later Dharmashastras rigidly defined gender roles and placed women as subordinate to men. They were expected to be devoted to their fathers, husbands and sons and have limited freedom. Their sexuality and reproductive powers were placed under tight control.
- Devi worship which worships the female divine represented the power of women. However, this shifted to viewing women as temptresses who could mislead men and hence needed to be controlled.
- The position of women has seen many transitions through history. While women in ancient times and epics were portrayed as strong figures, the Dharmashastras relegated their status. The colonial period initiated social reform movements to regain women their rights as equal citizens which continue till today. The changing status of women can be seen as a reflection of the values of different time periods in Indian history.
