### Kingship for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- King was considered as a representative of divine on earth. He was expected to maintain cosmic order and Dharma.
- Kingship was hereditary and patriarchal. Eldest son of the previous king was the heir.
- Coronation ritual (Rajsuya Yajna) conferred divine sanctity and legitimacy on the king.
- King's main duty was to protect his subjects and uphold Dharma. He had to be just and righteous.
- King was the pivot of the administration and dispensed justice. He was assisted by council of ministers and priests.
- King collected taxes and tribute which was vital for royal treasury and to fund wars and public works.
- Concept of divine right of kings declined after the rise of republics and democracies. Modern states are based on constitution and popular sovereignty.
