# INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Indian society is considered as a tradition-bound society. Traditional customs and ways of life still influence the Indian society to a great extent.
- Family and kinship are an important aspect of Indian culture. Indians value strong family and family bonds are emphasized. Respect for elders and relatives is also an important cultural aspect.
- The caste system and religion play an important role in Indian society. Traditional occupations and beliefs are determined by one's caste and Indians traditionally marry within their own caste.
- Festivals are an important part of Indian culture and many festivals are celebrated throughout the year based on the Indian calendar. Some popular festivals are Diwali, Holi, Onam, etc. These festivals are marked by rituals, traditional foods, customs, etc.
- Traditional Indian clothing varies based on region, religion, and community. Saree is a traditional female garment worn in many parts of India. Dhoti and Kurta are traditional male clothing.
- Indian cuisine is diverse, reflecting the country's diverse regions and influences. Spices and herbs are an important part of Indian cooking. Common foods include flatbread like Roti or Naan, rice, lentils, vegetables, and curries.
- Traditional Indian art forms include folk dances like Bhangra and Ghoomar, classical dances like Bharatanatyam and Kathakali, music like Hindustani and Carnatic, theatre forms like Kathakali, etc.
- Traditional Indian values include respect, non-violence, generosity, forgiveness, honesty, etc. as emphasized by teachings in ancient Indian scriptures. However, the society is becoming more progressive and western values are increasingly influencing the younger generation.
