### Medicine in India

- Ancient Indian medical system Ayurveda focused on preventive and holistic medicine. It emphasised on maintaining health rather than curing diseases.
- Ayurveda classified diseases into metabolic, physiological, psychological domains. Treatment involved internal medicines, external applications, surgical methods, rejuvenation therapies.
- Sushruta Samhita and Charaka Samhita are two foundational Ayurvedic texts which describe medicinal plants, methods of diagnosis and treatment of diseases.
- Unani system originated in Greece and developed in Islamic world. It was introduced in India by Muslims. It is based on the teachings of Greek physician Hippocrates and Roman physician Galen.
- Unani medicines are based on natural herbs and minerals. It focuses on removing the imbalance of humours (blood, phlegm, yellow bile, black bile) in the body to restore health.
- Siddha system is practiced mainly in Tamil Nadu. It is strongly influenced by the traditional knowledge of Tamil people. Siddha medicines are prepared using herbs, minerals and metals.
- Traditional Indian medicine systems have received global attention due to their natural therapies and holistic approach. The National Medicinal Plants Board was set up in India to coordinate research and development of medicinal plants.
