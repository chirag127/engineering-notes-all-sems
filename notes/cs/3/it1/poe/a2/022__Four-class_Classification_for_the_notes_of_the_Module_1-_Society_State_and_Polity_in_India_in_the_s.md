### Four-class Classification for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Brahmanas: They were at the top of the social hierarchy. They were priests and teachers. They performed religious rituals and ceremonies.

2. Kshatriyas: They were the warriors and rulers. They were responsible for maintaining law and order.

3. Vaishyas: They were traders and businessmen. They were involved in commercial activities and production of goods.

4. Shudras: They were laborers and servants. They served the other three classes and were not allowed to study the Vedas.

The classification was hierarchical and the Brahmanas enjoyed the highest status while the Shudras were at the bottom of the social order. The classification was based on the notion of purity of birth and the kind of occupation. The classification reflected a stratified society with limited social mobility.
