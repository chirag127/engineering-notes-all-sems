### Northern Indian Languages & Literature

- Sanskrit: Rig Veda, Upanishads, Brahmanas, Aranyakas, Ramayana, Mahabharata
- Hindi: dharmic epics and Prakrit love poetry translated, Bhakti poetry (Surdas, Tulsidas, Mirabai), modern literature (Premchand)
- Punjabi: Gurmukhi-script, Sikh religious texts (Guru Granth Sahib), folk stories and epic poetry (Heer Ranjha)
- Kashmiri: influenced by Sanskrit and Persian, Lal Ded's mystic verses, modern poets (Agha Shahid Ali)
- Sindhi: influenced by Sanskrit, Arabic-Persian,oral tradition ballads (Shah Jo Choondro), poetry of Shah Abdul Latif
- Rajasthani: influenced by Sanskrit and folk forms, heroic ballads of Pabuji and Devnarayan,Bhakti poets (Mirabai), modern authors (Kailash Kabir 'Maloji')
- Gujarati: Jain and Vaishnava devotional literature, poetry of Narsinh Mehta and Akho, modern authors (Rajendra Shah, Manilal Dwivedi)
