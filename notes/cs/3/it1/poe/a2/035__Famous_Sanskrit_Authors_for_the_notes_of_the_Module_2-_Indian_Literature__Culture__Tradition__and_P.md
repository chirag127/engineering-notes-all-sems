### Famous Sanskrit Authors

1. Kalidasa: Kalidasa was one of the greatest Sanskrit poets and dramatists. His prominent works include Abhijnanashakuntala, Kumarsambhava, Meghaduta, and Raghuvamsha. Abhijnanashakuntala is considered as one of the best Sanskrit dramas.

2. Bhasa: Bhasa was an ancient Sanskrit dramatist and poet. His prominent works include Swapnavasavadatta, Pancharatra, and Pratijna Yaugandharayana. He is considered as one of the most distinguished playwrights in Sanskrit literature.

3. Bhartrihari: Bhartrihari was a renowned Sanskrit poet. He is particularly known for his three collections of verses called Shatakatrayam which includes the famous works of Niti Shatakam, Shringara Shatakam, and Vairagya Shatakam. His verses highlighted virtues like wisdom, love and renunciation.

4. Kalidasa: Kalidasa was an eminent Sanskrit poet and dramatist. His prominent works include Raghuvamsha, Kumarasambhava, Meghaduta, and Abhijnanashakuntala which is considered as one of the best Sanskrit dramas. His works focused on themes like romance, nature, and nobility.

5. Panini: Panini was an ancient Indian grammarian. He is known for his Sanskrit grammar treatise called Ashtadhyayi which presented a comprehensive and systematic grammar of Sanskrit. Panini's Ashtadhyayi has been influential in the linguistic analysis of Sanskrit.
