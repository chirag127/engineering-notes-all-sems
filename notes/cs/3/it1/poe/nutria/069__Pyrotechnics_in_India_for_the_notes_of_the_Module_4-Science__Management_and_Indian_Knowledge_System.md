
### Pyrotechnics in India

Pyrotechnics is a form of entertainment that has been popular in India for centuries. It involves the use of fireworks, sparklers, and other forms of explosives to create a show of light, sound, and color.

* Pyrotechnics have been used in India since ancient times. Fireworks were used in festivals and celebrations to bring joy and excitement.

* In modern times, pyrotechnics have become a popular form of entertainment. They are often used in large-scale events such as weddings, religious festivals, and concerts.

* Pyrotechnics are regulated by the Indian government. All pyrotechnic displays must be approved by the local authorities and conducted in accordance with safety regulations.

* Pyrotechnics are often used in India to celebrate special occasions. Diwali, the Festival of Lights, is one of the most popular festivals in India and is celebrated with fireworks and sparklers.

* Pyrotechnics can be dangerous if not used properly. It is important to follow safety instructions when setting off fireworks or sparklers.
