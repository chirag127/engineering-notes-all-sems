
## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

1. India is a federal republic, with a parliamentary system of government. The President of India is the head of state, while the Prime Minister of India is the head of government. 
2. The Indian Constitution is the supreme law of the land, and is the longest written constitution of any country in the world. It provides for a secular democratic republic with a parliamentary system of government. 
3. India is a multi-party system, with numerous political parties at the national, state and local levels. The major political parties in India are the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), the Indian National Congress (INC), the Communist Party of India (CPI) and the Communist Party of India (Marxist) (CPM). 
4. India has a federal system of government, with the central government having authority over the entire country, and the state governments having authority over their respective states. 
5. The Indian Parliament is the supreme legislative body of the country. It consists of two houses, the Rajya Sabha (Upper House) and the Lok Sabha (Lower House). 
6. The judiciary in India is divided into the Supreme Court, High Courts, and District Courts. The Supreme Court is the highest court in the country, with the power to interpret the Constitution. 
7. India is a secular nation, with no state religion. The Indian Constitution guarantees freedom of religion to all citizens, and prohibits discrimination on the basis of religion. 
8. India is a multi-lingual and multi-ethnic society, with more than 22 officially recognized languages. Hindi is the official language of the union government, while English is the official language of government business. 
9. India is a diverse and multicultural nation, with a rich cultural heritage. The country is home to numerous religions, ethnicities, and languages, and has a long history of cultural exchange and interaction. 
10. India is a rapidly developing nation, with a large and growing economy. It is one of the fastest-growing economies in the world, and has the world's third-largest economy in terms of purchasing power parity.
