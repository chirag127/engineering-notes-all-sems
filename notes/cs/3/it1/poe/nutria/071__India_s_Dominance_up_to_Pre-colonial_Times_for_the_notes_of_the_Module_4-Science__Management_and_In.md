
### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times

* India was a major contributor to the world’s cultural, scientific, and technological development for centuries before the arrival of the British.
* Ancient India was a major center of learning and knowledge, and was home to some of the world’s earliest universities.
* Indian scholars made important contributions to mathematics, astronomy, medicine, and other fields of science.
* Indian mathematicians developed a number of important algorithms, including the use of zero and decimal notation.
* Indian astronomers developed sophisticated observational instruments and made important discoveries about the solar system.
* Indian doctors made significant advances in medical science and developed a variety of treatments for various diseases.
* Indian merchants and traders developed an extensive network of trade routes throughout the region, connecting India with other parts of the world.
* Indian culture and religion also had a major influence on the development of other cultures and religions, including Buddhism, Hinduism, and Jainism.
