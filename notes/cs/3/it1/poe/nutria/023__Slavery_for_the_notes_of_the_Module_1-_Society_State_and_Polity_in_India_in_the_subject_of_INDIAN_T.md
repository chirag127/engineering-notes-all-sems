
### Slavery in India

Slavery has been a part of Indian society for thousands of years. It is believed to have first appeared in the Indian subcontinent around the 6th century BCE. Throughout the centuries, slavery has been used as a way of providing labor, as well as a form of punishment.

* Slavery in India was mainly done through the caste system, where people were born into a certain caste and could not change it. This system limited the mobility of individuals and ensured that those born into lower castes were kept in servitude.
* Slavery was also used as a way of punishment for criminals or those who had committed a crime. This was often done through debt bondage, where criminals would be forced to work off their debts.
* In some cases, slavery was used to provide labor for large-scale projects such as the construction of forts and palaces.
* Slavery was also used to provide labor for the agricultural sector, where slaves were used to plough fields and tend to crops.
* Slavery was abolished in India in 1843, but it still exists in some parts of the country in the form of bonded labor. This is often done through debt bondage, where people are forced to work off their debts and are unable to leave the situation.
