
### Agriculture in India

* Agriculture is an important sector of the Indian economy, contributing around 15-18% of the Gross Domestic Product (GDP). 
* India has a long history of agriculture, with evidence of it dating back to the Indus Valley Civilization. 
* India is the world’s second-largest producer of food grains and the third-largest producer of rice and wheat. 
* Indian agriculture is largely dependent on the monsoon rains, with about 70% of the agricultural land being rainfed. 
* India is the largest producer of milk, pulses, jute, and tea, and the second-largest producer of fruits and vegetables. 
* The government of India has implemented various schemes and policies to boost agricultural productivity and ensure food security. 
* These include the National Food Security Act, the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana, and the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi.
