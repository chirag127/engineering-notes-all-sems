
### Medicine in India

* Indian medical traditions have a long history, with references to medical practices in ancient Hindu and Buddhist texts.
* Ayurveda is one of the oldest medical systems in the world, and is still widely practiced in India. It is based on the belief that health and wellness depend on a balance between the body, mind, and spirit.
* Unani medicine, a system of traditional medicine developed in the medieval Islamic world, is also practiced in India.
* Modern medicine is increasingly popular in India, and many hospitals and medical centers offer the latest treatments and technologies.
* India is home to a number of traditional healing practices, such as yoga, naturopathy, and homeopathy. These practices are widely used in India to treat a variety of conditions.
