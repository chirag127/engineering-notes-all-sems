
### Understanding Gender as a Social Category
1. Gender is a social category that is used to classify people based on their sex, gender identity, and gender expression.
2. Gender is a complex concept that is shaped by culture, history, and social norms.
3. Gender roles and expectations vary across societies and can change over time.
4. Gender is often used to distinguish between masculine and feminine characteristics, behaviors, and roles.
5. Gender identity is an individual's internal understanding of their own gender, which may or may not correspond to their assigned sex at birth.
6. Gender expression is the external manifestation of one's gender identity, which may include dress, hairstyle, body language, and other forms of expression.
7. Gender can be a source of social inequality, as individuals may face discrimination or be marginalized based on their gender identity or expression.
8. In India, gender roles are often seen as traditional and are deeply rooted in cultural norms.
9. In recent years, there has been an increased focus on gender equality and the empowerment of women in India.
