

### Challenges faced by Women

1. Social Inequality: Women in India have traditionally been at a disadvantage compared to men in terms of access to education, health care, and other basic services. This inequality has been further exacerbated by the patriarchal structure of Indian society.

2. Gender-Based Violence: Women in India are often subjected to physical, emotional, and sexual violence. This violence is often perpetrated by family members, and is often a result of patriarchal attitudes.

3. Lack of Economic Empowerment: Women in India are often denied access to economic opportunities and are thus unable to participate in the formal economy. This lack of economic empowerment has resulted in a lack of financial security for many women.

4. Political Marginalization: Women in India are often excluded from the political process, and are thus unable to influence policy decisions that affect their lives.

5. Inadequate Representation in the Media: Women in India are often portrayed in the media in a negative light, and are rarely given a platform to express their views or opinions. This lack of representation has resulted in a lack of visibility for women in Indian society.
