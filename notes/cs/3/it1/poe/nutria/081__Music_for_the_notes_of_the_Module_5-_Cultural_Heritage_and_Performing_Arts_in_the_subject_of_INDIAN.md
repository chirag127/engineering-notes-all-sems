

### Music for the Notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the Subject of Indian Traditions, Cultural and Society

1. Indian music is an ancient art form that has been passed down through many generations. It is characterized by its use of intricate rhythms and melodies, as well as its improvisational nature.

2. Indian music is traditionally divided into two categories: Hindustani and Carnatic. Hindustani music is found in the northern parts of India, while Carnatic music is found in the south.

3. Indian music has been influenced by various cultures throughout its history, such as Persian and Islamic cultures, as well as the more recent influence of British colonialism.

4. Indian music is often performed in ensembles, with instruments such as the sitar, tabla, and harmonium.

5. Indian music is also closely associated with dance, as the two are often performed together.

6. Indian music is closely connected to Hinduism, as many of its songs and melodies are based on Hindu mythology and beliefs.

7. Indian music is also closely connected to the spiritual practice of yoga, as many of its songs and melodies are used to help practitioners focus on their practice.

8. Indian music has been popularized in the West through the works of artists such as Ravi Shankar, Zakir Hussain, and A.R. Rahman.
