
### Harappan Technologies

The Harappan civilization was one of the most advanced civilizations of its time. It was located in the Indus Valley and is believed to have flourished from 3300 BCE to 1300 BCE. The Harappans were known for their advanced engineering and technological achievements.

* Harappan people developed a system of urban planning that included brick-built houses, granaries, and public baths.
* They developed an efficient drainage system to keep their cities clean.
* Harappans developed a sophisticated system of weights and measures, which was used to measure length, capacity, and weight.
* Harappans were skilled in the use of bronze and copper. They made tools and weapons out of these metals.
* Harappans had a sophisticated trade network, which included trading with Mesopotamia and other civilizations in the region.
* Harappans also had a highly developed system of writing, which is still being studied by scholars.
* They were also skilled in the use of pottery and developed a number of different types of pottery.
* The Harappans were also skilled in the use of irrigation and developed a number of different types of irrigation systems.
* They were also skilled in the use of mathematics and developed a number of different types of mathematical calculations.
