
### Sculptures for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Indian sculpture is one of the oldest and most important forms of art in the country's history.
- It dates back to the Indus Valley Civilization, where small terracotta figures of the human form were found.
- During the ancient and medieval periods, stone sculptures were carved in temples and palaces. These were often depictions of Hindu gods and goddesses, as well as images of kings and queens.
- In the modern era, Indian sculptors have experimented with new materials, such as metal and wood.
- Many contemporary Indian sculptors are inspired by traditional Indian art, but also draw on international trends.
- Sculptures of Indian gods and goddesses are often found in public spaces, such as parks and gardens. They are also found in temples, homes, and museums.
- Indian sculpture is an important part of the country's cultural heritage, and is often used to represent its rich history and vibrant culture.
