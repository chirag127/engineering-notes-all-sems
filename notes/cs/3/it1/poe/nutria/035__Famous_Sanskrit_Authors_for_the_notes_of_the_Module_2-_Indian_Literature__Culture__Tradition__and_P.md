
### Famous Sanskrit Authors

1. Valmiki: Valmiki is considered to be the author of the first Sanskrit epic, the Ramayana. He is believed to have lived in the 8th century BCE.

2. Vyasa: Vyasa is the author of the Mahabharata, the great Hindu epic. He is credited with composing the Bhagavad Gita and is believed to have lived in the 3rd century BCE.

3. Kalidasa: Kalidasa is one of the most renowned Sanskrit poets and dramatists. He is credited with writing the plays Shakuntala and Abhijnana Shakuntalam, as well as the epic poem Raghuvamsha. He is believed to have lived in the 4th or 5th century CE.

4. Bhavabhuti: Bhavabhuti was an 8th century Sanskrit playwright and poet. He is credited with writing the plays Malati-Madhava, Uttararamacharita, and Mahaviracharita.

5. Bana: Bana was a 7th century Sanskrit poet and author of the epic Harshacharita. He is known for his use of vivid imagery and complex metaphors.
