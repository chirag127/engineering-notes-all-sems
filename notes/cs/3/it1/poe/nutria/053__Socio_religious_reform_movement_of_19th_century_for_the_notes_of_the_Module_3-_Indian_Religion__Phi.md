
### Socio Religious Reform Movement of 19th Century

* The 19th century saw a number of socio-religious reform movements in India, which aimed to reform and modernize traditional religious beliefs and practices. 
* The Brahmo Samaj, founded in 1828 by Raja Ram Mohan Roy, was one of the most influential of these movements. It sought to reform Hinduism by rejecting idol worship and the caste system, and by emphasizing the importance of monotheism and the authority of the Vedas. 
* The Prarthana Samaj, founded in 1867 by Mahadev Govind Ranade, was another important reform movement. It sought to promote social reforms such as the education of women and the abolition of child marriage.
* The Arya Samaj, founded in 1875 by Swami Dayanand Saraswati, was a Hindu reform movement which sought to revive Vedic traditions and promote monotheism. It also emphasized the importance of social reforms such as the abolition of the caste system and the promotion of education.
* The Theosophical Society, founded in 1875 by Madame Blavatsky and Colonel Olcott, was an influential movement which sought to promote spiritualism and the study of Eastern religions. 
* The Ramakrishna Mission, founded in 1897 by Swami Vivekananda, was a Hindu reform movement which sought to promote tolerance and the unity of all religions. It also emphasized the importance of social service and education.
