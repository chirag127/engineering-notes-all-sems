
### Telugu Literature

Telugu literature is an ancient form of literature, with records of writing dating back to the 11th century. Telugu literature is largely written in the form of poetry, including epics, lyric poetry, and drama. It has a rich tradition of folk literature, with a wide variety of genres, including songs, stories, and proverbs.

Telugu literature has been heavily influenced by Sanskrit literature, and is often seen as a part of the larger Sanskritic tradition. Telugu literature is also heavily influenced by the Bhakti movement, which was a spiritual movement that focused on devotion to God.

The most famous works of Telugu literature include the Mahabharata, Ramayana, and Bhagavata Purana. Other notable works include the Ranganatha Ramayanam, the Manu Charitra, and the Srimad Bhagavatam.

Telugu literature has had a major influence on South Indian culture, and is seen as an important part of the region's cultural heritage. It has been an important source of inspiration for writers, poets, and artists from all over India.
