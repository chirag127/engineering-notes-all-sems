

### Evolution of Script and Languages in India

1. India has a long history of written languages, with some of the earliest evidence of writing found in the Indus Valley civilization.

2. The earliest known script used in the Indian subcontinent is the Brahmi script, which was used for writing Sanskrit, Prakrit, and other languages in the region.

3. The Devanagari script is the most widely used script in India today, and is used for writing Hindi, Marathi, Nepali, and other languages.

4. Other scripts used in India today include the Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, and Bengali scripts.

5. India also has a variety of dialects, with each region having its own distinct language.

6. The Sanskrit language is the oldest known language in India, and is still used as a liturgical language in Hinduism.

7. English is also widely spoken in India, and is used as the official language of communication in many government and business settings.
