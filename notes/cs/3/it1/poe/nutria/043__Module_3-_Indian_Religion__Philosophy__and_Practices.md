

## Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices

1. Hinduism is the major religion in India, and is based on the teachings of the Vedas, Upanishads, and other religious texts. It is polytheistic, and emphasizes the importance of dharma (righteousness) and karma (actions).

2. Buddhism is another major religion in India, and is based on the teachings of the Buddha. It is non-theistic, and emphasizes the importance of the Four Noble Truths, the Eightfold Path, and the practice of meditation.

3. Jainism is an ancient Indian religion that is based on the teachings of the Jain saints. It is non-theistic and emphasizes the importance of non-violence and the practice of ahimsa (non-harm).

4. Sikhism is an Indian religion that is based on the teachings of the Guru Granth Sahib. It is monotheistic and emphasizes the importance of service to God and the practice of seva (selfless service).

5. Indian philosophy is a rich tradition that includes the Samkhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Mimamsa, and Vedanta schools. It emphasizes the importance of understanding the nature of reality and the relationship between the individual and the divine.

6. Indian practices include yoga, meditation, and puja (ritual worship). These practices are used to cultivate physical, mental, and spiritual wellbeing.
