
### Buddhist And Jain Literature in Pali,Prakrit And Sanskrit

Buddhist and Jain literature in Pali, Prakrit and Sanskrit has been a major source of knowledge and understanding of Indian culture, tradition, and practices. 

* Buddhist literature in Pali is the oldest and most important source of Buddhist teachings. It includes the three main collections of scriptures, known as the Tripitaka, which include the Sutta Pitaka, the Vinaya Pitaka and the Abhidhamma Pitaka. 

* Jain literature in Prakrit and Sanskrit is also an important source of knowledge and understanding of Indian culture, tradition, and practices. It includes the twelve Angas, the eleven Upangas, the ten Prakirnakas and the nine Niryuktis. 

* Sanskrit literature is the most important source of knowledge and understanding of Indian culture, tradition, and practices. It includes the Vedas, the Upanishads, the Puranas, the Mahabharata, the Ramayana and the Bhagavad Gita. 

These three sources of literature have been instrumental in preserving and passing down the knowledge and understanding of Indian culture, tradition, and practices. They have also shaped the way we think about and practice our religion.
