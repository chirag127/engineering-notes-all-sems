
### Council of Ministers for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. The Council of Ministers is the highest executive organ of the Government of India. It is responsible for the administration of the country and the implementation of the policies of the Government. 
2. The Council of Ministers is headed by the Prime Minister, who is appointed by the President of India. 
3. The Council of Ministers comprises of Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State and Deputy Ministers. 
4. The Cabinet Ministers are appointed by the President on the advice of the Prime Minister. 
5. The Ministers of State and Deputy Ministers are appointed by the President on the advice of the Prime Minister and the Cabinet Ministers. 
6. The Council of Ministers is responsible for the formulation and implementation of the policies of the Government of India. 
7. The Council of Ministers is responsible for the formulation and implementation of the laws of the Government of India. 
8. The Council of Ministers is responsible for the formulation and implementation of the financial policies of the Government of India. 
9. The Council of Ministers is responsible for the formulation and implementation of the foreign policies of the Government of India. 
10. The Council of Ministers is responsible for the formulation and implementation of the economic policies of the Government of India.
