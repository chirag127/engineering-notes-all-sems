
### Administration for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

* Administration in India is the responsibility of both the Central and State Governments.
* The Constitution of India divides the powers of the central and state governments into three lists - the Union List, the State List and the Concurrent List.
* In India, the Union Government is responsible for the defense of the country, foreign policy, currency and coinage, postal services, communication, banking and insurance.
* The State Governments are responsible for the administration of the state, public order, health, education, agriculture, irrigation, police, local government, industry, commerce, and taxation.
* The Concurrent List contains subjects of common concern to both the Union and State Governments, such as criminal law, civil procedure, economic and social planning, forests, trade unions, marriage and divorce, electricity, and population control.
* The Constitution of India also provides for the establishment of local governments, such as panchayats and municipalities, which are responsible for the local administration of the villages and towns.
