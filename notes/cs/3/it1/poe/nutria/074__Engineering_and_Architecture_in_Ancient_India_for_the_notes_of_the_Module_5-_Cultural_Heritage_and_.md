
### Engineering and Architecture in Ancient India

1. Ancient India had a rich history of engineering and architecture, with many of the most iconic monuments in India being the result of their innovation and skill. 
2. The Indus Valley Civilization is credited with the development of the first planned cities in the world, with evidence of sophisticated sewage systems and drainage systems.
3. The Mauryan Empire is noted for their use of arches and columns in their architecture, which was seen in the famous monuments of the period such as the Ashoka Pillar.
4. The Gupta period saw the development of the first Hindu temples in India, which were built using a variety of materials and architectural styles.
5. The Chola dynasty is credited with some of the most iconic monuments in India, such as the Brihadeeswarar Temple in Thanjavur and the Shore Temple in Mahabalipuram.
6. The Mughal period saw the development of some of the most iconic monuments in India, such as the Taj Mahal, the Red Fort, and the Humayun's Tomb.
7. The British period saw the development of many iconic buildings in India, such as the Victoria Memorial in Kolkata, the Rashtrapati Bhavan in New Delhi, and the Gateway of India in Mumbai.
8. Modern India has seen the development of many iconic buildings, such as the Lotus Temple in New Delhi, the Bandra-Worli Sea Link in Mumbai, and the Hyderabad Metro.
