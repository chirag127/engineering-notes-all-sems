
### Pre-Vedic and Vedic Religion

* Pre-Vedic religion is believed to have existed in India before the Vedic period and is associated with the Indus Valley civilization. It is believed to have been a polytheistic religion, with its own gods and goddesses, rituals and beliefs.

* Vedic religion is the religion of the Vedic period, which lasted from around 1500 BCE to 500 BCE. It was a polytheistic religion and was centered around the Vedic texts, which are the oldest scriptures of Hinduism. It also had its own gods and goddesses, rituals, and beliefs.

* The Vedic period saw the emergence of the caste system, which divided the society into four main castes: the Brahmins (priests), the Kshatriyas (warriors), the Vaishyas (merchants), and the Shudras (labourers).

* Vedic religion also saw the emergence of the Upanishads, which are philosophical texts that form the basis of Hinduism. These texts focus on the concept of Brahman, which is the ultimate reality underlying the universe.

* The Vedic period also saw the emergence of the Bhagavad Gita, which is a central text of Hinduism. It is a dialogue between the god Krishna and the warrior Arjuna and is considered to be a spiritual guide for Hindus.

* The Vedic period also saw the emergence of the Vedanta, which is a philosophical system that is based on the Upanishads. It is focused on the concept of Brahman and emphasizes the importance of understanding the true nature of reality.
