
### Trade in Ancient India

* Trade in ancient India was mainly facilitated through barter system and the use of coins. 
* Indian merchants traded in goods like spices, textiles, and jewelry with foreign countries like Rome, Egypt, and China.
* The most important trading centers in ancient India were located in the cities of Varanasi, Pataliputra and Taxila.
* The Silk Road was a major trade route that connected India to the Chinese empire and other parts of Asia.
* Indian merchants also traded with the Arab traders who brought goods from the Middle East.
* Indian merchants also traded extensively with the Roman Empire and exported goods such as spices, indigo, and textiles.
* The traders in ancient India also used ships and boats to transport goods across the country and to other countries.
* The trade in ancient India was regulated by the government and the guilds.
* The guilds were associations of traders that regulated the quality of goods and services.
* The guilds also regulated the prices and wages of the traders.
* The Indian traders also used the local currency, called the punch-marked coins, for trading activities.
