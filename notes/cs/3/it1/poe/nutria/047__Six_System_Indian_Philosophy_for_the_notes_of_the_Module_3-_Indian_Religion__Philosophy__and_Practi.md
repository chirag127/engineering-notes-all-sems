
### Six System Indian Philosophy

1. **Samkhya**: This school of thought is one of the oldest in India and is based on the dualism of purusha (spirit) and prakriti (matter). It focuses on the analysis of the mind and the body and the interplay between them.

2. **Yoga**: This school of thought is based on the teachings of Patanjali and is concerned with the practice of meditation and physical postures to achieve union with the divine.

3. **Nyaya**: This school of thought is based on the teachings of Gautama and focuses on the logical analysis of reality. It is concerned with the nature of knowledge and the means of acquiring it.

4. **Vaisheshika**: This school of thought is based on the teachings of Kanada and is concerned with the analysis of reality in terms of atoms and elementary substances.

5. **Purva Mimamsa**: This school of thought is based on the teachings of Jaimini and is concerned with the interpretation of the Vedas. It is concerned with the ritualistic aspects of Vedic religion.

6. **Vedanta**: This school of thought is based on the teachings of Badarayana and is concerned with the interpretation of the Upanishads. It is concerned with the ultimate spiritual truth of the universe.
