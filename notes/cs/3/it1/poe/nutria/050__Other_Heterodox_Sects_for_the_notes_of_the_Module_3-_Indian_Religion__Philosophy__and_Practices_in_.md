
### Other Heterodox Sects 

1. Jainism: A religion that originated in India around the 6th century BCE, Jainism emphasizes the spiritual independence of the individual and the necessity of non-violence and non-attachment to material possessions. 

2. Buddhism: A religion based on the teachings of Siddhartha Gautama, Buddhism is a non-theistic religion that emphasizes the practice of meditation and the cultivation of wisdom and compassion. 

3. Sikhism: Founded in the 15th century by Guru Nanak, Sikhism is a monotheistic religion that emphasizes the importance of living an ethical life and service to others. 

4. Vaishnavism: A branch of Hinduism, Vaishnavism is centered around the worship of Vishnu and his various incarnations.

5. Shaktism: A branch of Hinduism, Shaktism is centered around the worship of the female divine, Shakti. 

6. Tantrism: A set of spiritual practices that originated in India, Tantrism is focused on the attainment of spiritual liberation through the use of ritual and meditation.
