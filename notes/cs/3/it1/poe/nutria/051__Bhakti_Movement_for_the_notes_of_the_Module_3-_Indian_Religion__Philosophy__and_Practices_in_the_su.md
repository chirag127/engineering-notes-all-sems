
### Bhakti Movement 

* The Bhakti Movement is a religious movement that originated in India during the medieval period and spread throughout the Indian subcontinent. 
* The movement was based on the devotional worship of a personal God and was popularized by various saints and preachers, such as Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, Kabir, Nanak and others. 
* The Bhakti Movement had a profound impact on the religious, social and cultural life of India, and had a major influence on the development of modern Hinduism.
* The Bhakti Movement encouraged people to practice personal devotion to God and to express their love and devotion in song and poetry. 
* The movement also rejected the caste system and the traditional Hindu ritual practices, instead emphasizing the importance of personal spiritual growth and inner transformation. 
* The Bhakti Movement was also a reaction against the authoritarianism of the Brahmin priests, and was an attempt to make religion more accessible to the common people. 
* The Bhakti Movement had a major influence on the development of Indian music, literature, and art. 
* The movement also encouraged the spread of Hinduism to other parts of the world.
