
### Martial Arts Traditions

Martial arts are an ancient tradition in India, with some forms of the practice dating back thousands of years. The martial arts of India have been used for self-defense, physical fitness, and spiritual development.

- Kalaripayattu is an ancient martial art form from Kerala that is believed to be the oldest surviving form of martial arts in the world. It is known for its use of weapons, including swords, spears and sticks.
- Gatka is a martial art form from Punjab that originated in the 16th century. It is known for its use of swords and shields, and is believed to have been used by the Sikh warriors.
- Malla-yuddha is an ancient wrestling style from India that dates back to the 4th century BC. It is known for its use of grappling, throws, and joint locks.
- Varma Kalai is an ancient martial art form from Tamil Nadu that is believed to have been developed by the Siddha masters. It is known for its use of pressure point strikes and is believed to be an effective form of self-defense.
- Silambam is a martial art form from Tamil Nadu that is believed to have been developed by the Siddha masters. It is known for its use of sticks and is believed to be an effective form of self-defense.
