

### Āshrama or the Stages of Life

The Hindu tradition identifies four stages (āshrama) of life that an individual must pass through in order to achieve spiritual fulfillment. These stages are: 

1. Brahmacharya: The student stage, in which one learns the Vedas, practices self-discipline, and develops the knowledge and skills necessary for life.

2. Grihastha: The householder stage, in which one is responsible for providing for and supporting a family.

3. Vanaprastha: The forest-dweller stage, in which one retires from family life and devotes oneself to spiritual pursuits.

4. Sannyasa: The renunciation stage, in which one withdraws from all worldly concerns and dedicates oneself entirely to spiritual pursuits.

The āshrama system is based on the idea that life is a journey of spiritual growth, and each stage of life should be devoted to cultivating spiritual knowledge and wisdom.
