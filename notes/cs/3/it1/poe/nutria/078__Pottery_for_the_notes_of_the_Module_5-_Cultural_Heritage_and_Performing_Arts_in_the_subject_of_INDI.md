
### Pottery for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

* Pottery is an ancient art form in India, with some of the earliest examples dating back to the Neolithic period.
* Pottery is used in many different aspects of Indian culture, from religious ceremonies to everyday life.
* Pottery is often decorated with intricate designs, symbols and motifs that have spiritual and cultural significance.
* Different regions of India have their own unique pottery styles, such as the terracotta pottery of Gujarat and the black pottery of Assam.
* Pottery is still a popular art form in India, with many artisans continuing to create traditional and modern pieces.
* Pottery is also used as a form of cultural expression, with some pieces telling stories and conveying messages.
