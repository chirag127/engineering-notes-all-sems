### Puranas

Puranas are ancient Hindu texts that provide insights into Indian culture, tradition, and practices. They contain stories about deities, kings, and sages, and offer guidance on religious rituals, moral values, and social norms. Here are some key points to remember about Puranas:

- There are 18 major Puranas and 18 minor Puranas, making a total of 36 Puranas.
- The major Puranas are divided into three categories based on the predominant deity: Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva.
- Each Purana is composed of several sections, including creation stories, genealogies of gods and sages, descriptions of religious rituals, and accounts of historical events.
- Puranas are believed to have been composed between 400 CE to 1500 CE, although some sections may be much older.
- They were originally transmitted orally and later written down in Sanskrit.
- Puranas played a significant role in the spread and preservation of Hinduism, as they were accessible to people of all castes and provided a common cultural and religious framework.
- The Puranas also contain valuable information about Indian society, including its social hierarchy, gender roles, and economic practices.
- Puranic stories and characters are often depicted in Indian art and literature, including classical dances, paintings, and folk traditions.
- Despite their importance, Puranas are not considered authoritative texts in Hinduism and are subject to interpretation and adaptation.
- The study of Puranas is a valuable tool for understanding the diversity and complexity of Indian culture, religion, and society.
