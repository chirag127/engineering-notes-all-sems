### Shankaracharya

Shankaracharya, also known as Adi Shankaracharya, was a great Indian philosopher and theologian who lived in the 8th century CE. He is known for his profound contributions to the development of Hindu philosophy, particularly Advaita Vedanta.

Here are some key points to know about Shankaracharya:

- Shankaracharya was born in Kalady, Kerala, in South India. He lived from 788-820 CE.
- According to legend, Shankaracharya was a child prodigy who mastered the Vedas and other scriptures at a young age.
- Shankaracharya is credited with reviving the philosophy of Advaita Vedanta, which emphasizes the oneness of the individual soul (Atman) with the ultimate reality (Brahman).
- Shankaracharya wrote many commentaries on the ancient Hindu scriptures, including the Upanishads, the Bhagavad Gita, and the Brahma Sutras.
- Shankaracharya also established four major monasteries (mathas) in different parts of India, which continue to be important centers of learning and spiritual practice today.
- Shankaracharya's teachings had a profound impact on Indian philosophy and religion, and his legacy continues to be felt in modern times.
