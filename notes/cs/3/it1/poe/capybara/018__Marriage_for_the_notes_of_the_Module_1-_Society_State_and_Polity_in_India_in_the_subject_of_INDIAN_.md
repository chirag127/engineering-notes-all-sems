### Marriage

Marriage is an important aspect of Indian society and culture. It is a sacred union between two individuals and their families. Here are some key points about marriage in India:

- In India, arranged marriages are still prevalent. Parents or family members usually take the lead in finding a suitable partner for their children. However, love marriages are becoming increasingly common, especially in urban areas.

- Marriage is seen as a lifelong commitment and divorce is still considered taboo in many parts of the country.

- The wedding ceremony is an elaborate affair and can last for several days. It involves various rituals and customs that vary depending on the region and religion of the couple.

- Dowry, although illegal, is still prevalent in some parts of the country. It is a practice where the bride's family gives gifts or money to the groom's family as a condition of the marriage.

- The institution of marriage is closely linked to the caste system in India. Inter-caste marriages are still considered controversial in many parts of the country.

- Same-sex marriages are not legal in India, although there is a growing movement for their recognition and acceptance.

- The Indian government has introduced various laws to protect women's rights in marriage, such as the Dowry Prohibition Act and the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act.

Overall, marriage in India is a complex and multifaceted institution that reflects the country's diverse cultural and social fabric.
