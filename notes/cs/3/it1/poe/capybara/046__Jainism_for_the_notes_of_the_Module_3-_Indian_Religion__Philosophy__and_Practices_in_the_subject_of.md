### Jainism

Jainism is a religion that originated in ancient India. Here are some key points to remember:

- Jainism is based on the teachings of Lord Mahavira, who lived in the 6th century BCE.
- Jains believe in the concept of ahimsa, or non-violence, and follow strict vegetarian diets.
- The religion is divided into two major sects: Digambara and Svetambara.
- Jainism places great emphasis on the pursuit of knowledge and self-improvement through meditation and self-discipline.
- Jains practice asceticism and renunciation of material possessions.
- Jainism teaches that the universe is eternal and that there are an infinite number of souls that can be reincarnated.
- Jains believe in the concept of karma, which holds that all actions have consequences that affect the soul in future lives.
- Jainism has had a significant impact on Indian culture and society, particularly in the areas of art, literature, and philosophy.
- Today, there are an estimated 4.2 million Jains worldwide, with the majority living in India.
