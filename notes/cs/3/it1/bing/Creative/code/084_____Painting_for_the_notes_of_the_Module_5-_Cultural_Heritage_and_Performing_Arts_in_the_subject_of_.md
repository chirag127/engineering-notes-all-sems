### Painting

Painting is one of the oldest and most diverse forms of artistic expression in India. It reflects the rich cultural heritage and diversity of the country, as well as the influences of various historical periods, regions, religions, and dynasties.

Some of the main features of Indian painting are:

- It can be broadly classified as murals, miniatures, and paintings on cloth .
- Murals are large works executed on the walls of solid structures, such as temples, caves, palaces, and forts. They depict religious, mythological, historical, or secular themes. Some of the famous examples of murals are the Ajanta Caves, the Kailashnath Temple, the Brihadeshwara Temple, and the Hampi monuments.
- Miniatures are small-scale paintings done on perishable materials such as paper, palm leaf, wood, ivory, or cloth. They are usually made for books, albums, or court records. They illustrate literary, religious, or historical texts, or portray scenes from court life, nature, or daily life. Some of the prominent styles of miniatures are the Pala, Mughal, Rajput, Deccan, and Pahari schools.
- Paintings on cloth are also known as patachitra, which means picture on cloth. They are usually made of cotton or silk, and painted with natural or synthetic colors. They depict religious stories, folk tales, or local customs. They are often used as scrolls for storytelling, or as banners for festivals or processions. Some of the well-known forms of paintings on cloth are the Madhubani, Warli, Kalamkari, Pichhwai, and Thangka paintings.
- Indian painting has a very long tradition and history, dating back to the prehistoric times. The earliest examples of Indian painting are the rock paintings of places like Bhimbetka, which show scenes of hunting, dancing, and animal life. Over the centuries, Indian painting has evolved and diversified, influenced by various factors such as religion, politics, trade, and foreign contact .
- Indian painting is characterized by its use of vibrant colors, intricate patterns, elaborate symbolism, and stylized representation of figures and landscapes. It also shows a remarkable diversity of styles, techniques, and themes, reflecting the regional and cultural variations of the country. Indian painting is not only a form of aesthetic expression, but also a medium of communication, education, and devotion .
