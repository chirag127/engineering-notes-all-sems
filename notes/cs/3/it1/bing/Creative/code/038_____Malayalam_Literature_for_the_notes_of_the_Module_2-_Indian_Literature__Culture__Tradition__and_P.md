### Malayalam Literature

- Malayalam is a South-Dravidian language spoken in the Indian state of Kerala and some parts of Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Lakshadweep.
- Malayalam literature comprises the literary texts written in Malayalam, which reflect the culture, tradition and history of Kerala and its people.
- The earliest extant literary work in Malayalam is **Ramacharitam** (late 12th or early 13th century), an epic poem based on the Sanskrit Ramayana, composed by Cheeraman in the Kolezhuthu script.
- The next phase of Malayalam literature is marked by the emergence of **Manipravalam** (ruby coral), a style of writing that blended Malayalam and Sanskrit words and grammar. This style was mainly used for composing erotic poetry and courtly romances, such as **Unniyachi Charitam**, **Unnichiruthevi Charitam** and **Kannassa Ramayanam** .
- The most prominent poet of the Manipravalam period was **Thunchaththu Ezhuthachan** (16th century), who is considered the father of modern Malayalam literature. He translated the Sanskrit epics Ramayana and Mahabharata into Malayalam using the **Kilippattu** (parrot song) style, which employed simple and elegant Malayalam verses. He also composed **Adhyatma Ramayanam**, **Harinama Kirtanam**, **Devimahatmyam** and other devotional works .
- The 17th and 18th centuries saw the development of **Pattu** (song) literature, which was influenced by Tamil and folk traditions. The Pattu poets used the **Vanchippattu** (boat song) and **Oppana** (bridal song) forms to narrate stories from history, mythology and folklore. Some of the notable Pattu works are **Krishnagatha** by Cherusseri Namboothiri, **Ramakathapattu** by Kadungathur Narayana Pisharoti, **Thacholi Othenan** by Kunchan Nambiar and **Kannaki Charitam** by Kadalur Subrahmanya Iyer .
- The first travelogue in any Indian language is the Malayalam **Varthamanappusthakam** (1785), written by Paremmakkal Thoma Kathanar, a Syrian Christian priest. It describes his journey to Rome and other places in Europe and Asia.
- The 19th century witnessed the emergence of modern prose and poetry in Malayalam, influenced by Western education, journalism and social reform movements. The first Malayalam novel is **Indulekha** (1889) by O. Chandu Menon, a realistic portrayal of the Nair society. The first Malayalam short story is **Vasanavikriti** (1891) by C.V. Raman Pillai, a satirical critique of the feudal system. The first Malayalam drama is **Pattabakki** (1891) by K.C. Kesava Pillai, a historical play based on the life of Velu Thampi Dalawa .
- The 20th century saw the flourishing of Malayalam literature in various genres and styles, reflecting the social, political and cultural changes in Kerala. Some of the prominent Malayalam writers of this period are Kumaran Asan, Vallathol Narayana Menon, Ulloor S. Parameswara Iyer, G. Sankara Kurup, Changampuzha Krishna Pillai, Thakazhi Sivasankara Pillai, Vaikom Muhammad Basheer, M.T. Vasudevan Nair, O.V. Vijayan, Kamala Surayya, M. Mukundan, O.N.V. Kurup, Akkitham Achuthan Namboothiri, M. Leelavathy, K. Sachidanandan, Sugathakumari, N.S. Madhavan, Anand, K.R. Meera, Benyamin, K. Satchidanandan and many others .
