### Current developments in arts and culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries and many smaller ones, and a variety of folk traditions, performing arts, rites and rituals, paintings and writings.
- India has also been actively working towards creating a unique cultural identity for its cities, especially the tier 1 and tier 2 cities, by promoting art and culture initiatives, festivals, museums, heritage walks, and public art projects.
- India has also been exploring new trends in contemporary art, such as abstract art, digital art, installation art, performance art, and street art, that reflect the social, political, and environmental issues of the country and the world.
- India has also been participating in the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, which aims to foster lifelong learning and sustainable development through culture and education. Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have joined the network recently .
