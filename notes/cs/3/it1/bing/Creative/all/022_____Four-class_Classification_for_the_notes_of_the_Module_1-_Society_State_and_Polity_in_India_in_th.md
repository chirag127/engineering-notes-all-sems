## Social Structure

- Social structure refers to the patterns of social relationships and interactions among individuals and groups in a society.
- It includes aspects such as caste, class, gender, religion, ethnicity, language, region, etc.
- Social structure influences the distribution of power, resources, opportunities and constraints in a society.
- It also shapes the identities, values, norms and beliefs of people.
- Some of the key features of Indian social structure are:

  - Diversity: India is a diverse society with multiple social groups based on various criteria such as caste, class, gender, religion, ethnicity, language, region, etc. These groups have different histories, cultures, lifestyles and aspirations.
  - Hierarchy: India has a hierarchical social structure with unequal relations of dominance and subordination among social groups. Caste is one of the most prominent bases of hierarchy in India, which divides people into four main categories: Brahmins (priests and scholars), Kshatriyas (warriors and rulers), Vaishyas (merchants and traders) and Shudras (servants and labourers). There is also a fifth category of Dalits (untouchables) who are considered outside the caste system and face discrimination and oppression.
  - Patriarchy: India has a patriarchal social structure with male dominance and female subordination in various spheres of life. Gender is a social construct that assigns different roles, rights and responsibilities to men and women. Women face multiple forms of discrimination and violence such as dowry, female foeticide, domestic abuse, rape, etc.
  - Pluralism: India is a plural society with multiple religions and faiths coexisting and interacting with each other. Religion is a system of beliefs and practices that gives meaning and purpose to human life. India has six major religions: Hinduism, Islam, Christianity, Sikhism, Buddhism and Jainism. There are also various sects, cults and movements within and across these religions.
  - Regionalism: India is a regional society with different regions having distinct geographical, historical, cultural and linguistic features. Region is a territorial unit that defines the identity and interests of people living in it. India has 28 states and 8 union territories, each with its own administrative, political and economic system. There are also various regional movements and conflicts in India, such as separatism, autonomy, federalism, etc.
