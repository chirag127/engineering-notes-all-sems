# Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of art, music, dance, theatre, literature, architecture, and festivals.
- India is also home to 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and many smaller ones, and a vibrant contemporary art scene.
- Some of the current developments in arts and culture in India are:

  - Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, which aims to promote lifelong learning and social inclusion through culture and education .
  - India has nominated the Hoysala Temples of Karnataka, which are examples of the unique Hoysala style of architecture and sculpture, for inscription in the World Heritage List.
  - India has got back a 10th-century sandstone sculpture of a goat-headed Yogini, which was stolen from a temple in Uttar Pradesh and smuggled to France, after a legal battle of seven years.
  - The National Museum of Customs and GST in New Delhi has launched a digital exhibition titled 'Dharohar', which showcases the history and evolution of customs and taxation in India from ancient times to the present day.
  - The Archaeological Survey of India has made new discoveries at Rakhigarhi, a Harappan site in Haryana, which include a burial site, a copper smelter, and a seal with an animal motif.
  - The Ministry of Culture has launched a project called 'Devayatanam', which aims to document and disseminate the rich diversity of Indian temple architecture and art across the country.
  - The Republic Day Parade 2022, which was held on 26th January, showcased the cultural diversity and unity of India through various tableaux, cultural performances, and folk dances.
  - The contemporary Indian art scene has witnessed new trends and innovations in painting, sculpture, installation, photography, and digital media, influenced by global and local socio-political and cultural issues.
  - The Indian film industry, especially Bollywood and regional cinema, has produced some critically acclaimed and commercially successful movies that reflect the changing aspirations, challenges, and realities of the Indian society.
