# Pyrotechnics in India

Pyrotechnics is the science and art of creating and using fireworks and other explosive devices for entertainment, military, or industrial purposes. Pyrotechnics has a long and rich history in India, dating back to ancient times. Here are some points to note about pyrotechnics in India:

- According to some sources, fireworks were invented in India by the alchemists of the Gupta dynasty (320-550 CE) who experimented with various chemical mixtures and gunpowder. They used fireworks for religious ceremonies, festivals, and warfare. 
- Fireworks and pyrotechnic shows existed as a form of royal entertainment in many medieval Indian kingdoms during festivals, events and special occasions like weddings. Manufacturing formulas for fireworks describing pyrotechnic mixtures are found within Kautukachintamani, a Sanskrit volume by Gajapati Prataparudradeva (1497-1539), a reputed king of the Suryavamsa dynasty of Orissa. 
- One of the earliest notes of pyrotechnical shows in India is made by Abdur Razzaq, the ambassador of the Timurid Sultan Shahrukh to the court of the Vijayanagar king Devaraya II in 1443. He described a grand display of fireworks at the king's palace, which lasted for several hours and amazed the spectators. 
- Pyrotechnics also played a role in the maritime history of India, as they were used for signaling, rescue, and defense purposes by the seafarers. Vessel owners and manufacturers in India have always worked to create a secure environment for seafarers at all times, making provision of dated pyrotechnics a top priority for safeguarding the lives of seafarers in times of emergencies at sea. 
- In modern times, pyrotechnics has become a popular and integral part of the Indian culture and celebrations, especially during Diwali, the festival of lights. Fireworks are also used for various public and private events, such as weddings, birthdays, inaugurations, concerts, sports, and political rallies. India has a large and thriving fireworks industry, which employs thousands of people and produces a variety of products, such as sparklers, rockets, fountains, wheels, shells, and crackers. 
- India also has some of the leading pyrotechnic companies and professionals in the world, who have showcased their skills and creativity in national and international events. For example, Amir Morani Fireworks (AMFW), is the pioneer display fireworks company in India with over decades of experience in fireworks displays and special effects. AMFW has had the honor to serve its clients with displays, ranging from small-scale indoor concerts to grand-scale remotely synchronized aerial displays. 
- Pyrotechnics in India is not only a source of entertainment and joy, but also a reflection of the country's rich and diverse heritage, culture, and spirit. Pyrotechnics in India is a manifestation of the Indian philosophy of "Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam", which means "the world is one family".
