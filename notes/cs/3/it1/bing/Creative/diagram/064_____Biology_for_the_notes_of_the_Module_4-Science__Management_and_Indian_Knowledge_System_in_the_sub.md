### Biology
- Biology is the study of life and living organisms, including their structure, function, evolution, and diversity.
- India has a rich and diverse biological heritage, with many contributions to the fields of botany, zoology, ecology, medicine, and biotechnology.
- Some of the notable achievements of Indian biology are:

  - The discovery of the Indus Valley Civilization (IVC), which had a sophisticated knowledge of urban planning, sanitation, agriculture, animal husbandry, and medicine .
  - The development of Ayurveda, the ancient system of holistic medicine that is based on the principles of balance, harmony, and natural healing .
  - The classification of plants and animals by various ancient scholars, such as Charaka, Sushruta, Varahamihira, and Kautilya .
  - The invention of the concept of zero and the decimal system by the mathematician-astronomer Aryabhata, which facilitated the calculation of large numbers and fractions in biology .
  - The discovery of the circulation of blood by the physician Sushruta, who also performed the first plastic surgery and cataract surgery .
  - The observation of the life cycle of plants and animals by the naturalist and philosopher Kanada, who also proposed the atomic theory of matter .
  - The exploration of the diversity of flora and fauna by the Mughal emperor Babur, who also introduced many exotic plants and fruits to India .
  - The documentation of the medicinal properties of plants and animals by the physician and botanist Ibn Battuta, who also traveled extensively across India and other countries .
  - The establishment of the Asiatic Society of Bengal by the British scholar Sir William Jones, who also translated many Sanskrit texts on biology and other subjects .
  - The discovery of the genetic role of chromosomes by the cytologist and Nobel laureate Sir C.V. Raman, who also explained the scattering of light by molecules .
  - The discovery of the structure of DNA by the biophysicist and Nobel laureate Sir J.C. Bose, who also invented the radio and the crescograph .
  - The development of the green revolution by the agronomist and Nobel laureate Dr. M.S. Swaminathan, who also improved the yield and quality of crops .
  - The development of the biotechnology industry by the entrepreneur and philanthropist Dr. Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, who also founded Biocon, India's largest biopharmaceutical company .
