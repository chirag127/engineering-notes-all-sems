### Theatre

- Theatre is one of the most ancient forms of art and expression in India, dating back to the Vedic period (1500-500 BCE) .
- Theatre in India began as a narrative form, in which recitation, singing and dancing were integral elements . It also incorporated various aspects of literature, music, sculpture, painting and architecture .
- Theatre in India was influenced by various religious and cultural traditions, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Islam and colonialism . It also reflected the diversity of languages, regions, castes and communities in India .
- Theatre in India can be broadly classified into two categories: classical and folk . Classical theatre is based on the ancient treatises of dramaturgy, such as the Natyashastra by Bharata Muni, which lays down the principles and rules of theatre . Folk theatre is more spontaneous, flexible and diverse, drawing from local myths, legends, rituals and customs .
- Some of the major forms of classical theatre in India are Sanskrit drama, Kutiyattam, Koodiyattam, Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Yakshagana, Bhavai, Nautanki and Tamasha  . Some of the major forms of folk theatre in India are Bhand Pather, Swang, Rasleela, Ramleela, Jatra, Chhau, Bhaona, Therukoothu and Naga Mandala  .
- Theatre in India underwent a transformation in the colonial and post-colonial periods, with the introduction of Western influences, modern themes, social issues and new techniques . Some of the prominent playwrights and theatre personalities of this period are Rabindranath Tagore, Mohan Rakesh, Vijay Tendulkar, Girish Karnad, Badal Sircar, Habib Tanvir, Utpal Dutt, Satyadev Dubey, Ebrahim Alkazi, Prithviraj Kapoor and Shyam Benegal  .
- Theatre in India today is a vibrant and diverse field, with many genres, languages, styles and forms . It also faces many challenges, such as lack of funding, infrastructure, audience and recognition . Theatre in India also participates in various festivals, both national and international, to showcase its rich and varied heritage . Some of the major theatre festivals in India are Prithvi Theatre Festival, Bharat Rang Mahotsav, Jairangam, Nandikar's National Theatre Festival and META .
