## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

This module aims to provide an overview of the main features and characteristics of the society, state and polity in India. It will cover the following topics:

- The concept and definition of society, state and polity, and their interrelationship.
- The historical evolution of the Indian society, state and polity, from ancient to modern times, and the major factors and forces that shaped them.
- The diversity and complexity of the Indian society, state and polity, in terms of culture, religion, language, caste, class, gender, ethnicity, region, etc.
- The challenges and opportunities faced by the Indian society, state and polity, in the context of globalization, democratization, development, social movements, identity politics, etc.
- The current trends and issues in the Indian society, state and polity, and their implications for the future.

Some of the learning objectives of this module are:

- To understand the basic concepts and terms related to society, state and polity, and their application to the Indian context.
- To gain a historical perspective on the formation and transformation of the Indian society, state and polity, and the role of various actors and agencies in the process.
- To appreciate the diversity and complexity of the Indian society, state and polity, and the sources of unity and conflict among them.
- To analyze the challenges and opportunities faced by the Indian society, state and polity, and the responses and strategies adopted by them.
- To develop a critical and comparative outlook on the Indian society, state and polity, and their prospects and problems.
