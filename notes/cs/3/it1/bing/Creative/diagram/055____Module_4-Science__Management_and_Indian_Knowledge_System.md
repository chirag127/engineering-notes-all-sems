## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

- This module aims to introduce the concept and scope of Indian Knowledge System (IKS) and its relevance for contemporary science and management.
- IKS is a term that refers to the diverse and rich intellectual traditions of India that have evolved over millennia in various domains of knowledge, such as mathematics, astronomy, medicine, linguistics, logic, ethics, aesthetics, etc.
- IKS is not a monolithic or static system, but a dynamic and pluralistic one that reflects the diversity and complexity of Indian culture and society.
- IKS is based on certain foundational principles, such as the unity of existence, the interdependence of all phenomena, the cyclical nature of time, the harmony of nature and human, the quest for liberation, etc.
- IKS has contributed significantly to the development of science and technology, both in ancient and modern times, by providing original insights, methods, concepts, and applications.
- IKS has also influenced the management practices and theories in India and abroad, by offering holistic and humanistic approaches, ethical values, and spiritual dimensions.
- IKS is not only a source of historical pride, but also a potential resource for addressing the contemporary challenges and opportunities in science and management, such as sustainability, innovation, diversity, ethics, etc.
- IKS can be studied and applied in an interdisciplinary and integrative manner, by drawing upon the insights and methods from various disciplines, such as philosophy, history, sociology, psychology, etc.
- IKS can also be learned and appreciated through various modes of expression, such as texts, oral traditions, arts, crafts, rituals, etc.
- IKS can be a part of mainstream education, by offering elective credit courses, research projects, workshops, seminars, etc. to the students and faculty of technical institutes.
- IKS can also be a part of lifelong learning, by engaging with the various stakeholders and communities that are involved in the preservation, dissemination, and application of IKS  .
