### Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of art, music, dance, theatre, literature, architecture, and festivals.
- India has 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and many smaller ones, and a vibrant folk and classical tradition of performing arts.
- India has also been actively promoting its cultural identity and diversity through various initiatives and platforms, such as:
  - Joining the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, which aims to foster lifelong learning and social inclusion through culture and education. Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have joined this network recently .
  - Nominating the Hoysala Temples of Karnataka for inscription in the World Heritage List, which are examples of the unique Hoysala style of architecture and sculpture that flourished between the 11th and 14th centuries.
  - Getting back the Goat-Headed Yogini Statue, which was stolen from a temple in Bihar in 1991 and was recovered from a museum in Canada. The statue is a rare example of the Tantric cult of Yoginis, who were female deities associated with mystical powers and practices.
  - Organizing the Dharohar exhibition, which showcases the history and achievements of the National Museum of Customs and GST, which was established in 1976 and has a collection of over 10,000 artefacts related to customs, trade, and taxation.
  - Celebrating the Republic Day Parade, which is an annual event that showcases the cultural and military diversity and prowess of the country. The parade features various tableaux, cultural performances, and awards for the best participants.
  - Exploring new trends and innovations in contemporary Indian art, which reflects the social, political, and cultural changes and challenges of the modern era. Contemporary Indian artists use various mediums, styles, and themes to express their creativity and vision.
  - Preserving and promoting the ancient and medieval heritage of the country, such as the Harappan civilization, the temple architecture, the classical music and dance forms, the folk arts and crafts, and the regional languages and literatures  .
