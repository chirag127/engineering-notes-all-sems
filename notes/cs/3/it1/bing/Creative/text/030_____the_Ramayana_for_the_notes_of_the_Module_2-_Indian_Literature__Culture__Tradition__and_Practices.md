### The Ramayana

- The Ramayana is a Hindu epic about the life of the legendary Prince Rama of Kosala    .
- The Ramayana is composed of seven books, or kandas, that narrate the main events of Rama's life, from his birth to his death   .
- The Ramayana is attributed to the sage Valmiki, who is believed to have written it in Sanskrit between the 5th and 4th centuries BCE   .
- The Ramayana is one of the two major epics of India, along with the Mahabharata, and has influenced the literature, culture, art, and religion of India and other parts of Asia   .
- The Ramayana is also considered to be a sacred text by Hindus, who revere Rama as an incarnation of Vishnu, the supreme god of the Hindu trinity   .

#### Summary of the Ramayana

- The Ramayana begins with the introduction of King Dasaratha of Kosala, who is childless and wants a son to inherit his throne .
- He performs a sacrifice with the help of his mentor, and is blessed with four sons: Rama, Bharata, Lakshmana, and Shatrughna .
- Rama is the eldest and the most virtuous of the brothers, and is loved by all   .
- He marries Sita, the daughter of King Janaka of Mithila, who is also an incarnation of Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth and Vishnu's consort   .
- Rama's stepmother, Kaikeyi, is jealous of Rama's popularity and wants her son Bharata to be the king instead   .
- She uses a boon granted by Dasaratha to banish Rama to the forest for 14 years and make Bharata the heir   .
- Rama accepts his fate and leaves with Sita and Lakshmana, his loyal brother   .
- Bharata, however, is not happy with his mother's scheme and refuses to take the throne   .
- He goes to the forest to persuade Rama to return, but Rama insists on fulfilling his duty and honoring his father's word   .
- Bharata takes Rama's sandals and places them on the throne as a symbol of Rama's authority, and vows to rule as his regent until he comes back   .

- While in the forest, Rama encounters many sages, animals, and demons, and helps them in various ways   .
- He also meets Hanuman, the monkey king, who becomes his devoted follower and friend   .
- The main antagonist of the Ramayana is Ravana, the ten-headed king of Lanka, who is a powerful and evil demon   .
- He is obsessed with Sita and wants to possess her as his wife   .
