### Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of art, music, dance, literature, architecture, and festivals. India is also home to 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and many smaller ones as well .
- India has been actively promoting its art and culture both nationally and internationally, through various initiatives and platforms. Some of the recent developments in this field are:

  - Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, which aims to foster lifelong learning and sustainable development .
  - India has nominated the Hoysala Temples of Karnataka, which are examples of the unique Hoysala style of architecture, for inscription in the World Heritage List .
  - India has successfully retrieved a 10th-century Yogini statue, which was stolen from a temple in Bihar and smuggled to France, after a legal battle of 24 years .
  - India has launched the Dharohar project, which is a digital museum of the customs and GST department, showcasing the history and evolution of taxation and trade in India .
  - India has witnessed a surge in contemporary art, which explores new trends and themes in Indian paintings, such as social issues, urbanization, globalization, and identity .
  - India has celebrated its 72nd Republic Day with a grand parade, which showcased the cultural diversity and achievements of the country. The parade also featured a tableau of the Ram Temple in Ayodhya, which is under construction after a historic verdict by the Supreme Court .
  - India has hosted the Sydney WorldPride, which is Australia's flagship pride celebration that brings the LGBT community together. The event featured parties, exhibitions, workshops, dining experiences, and iconic destinations running inclusive programs .

- These developments reflect the dynamism and vibrancy of India's art and culture, which are constantly evolving and adapting to the changing times and contexts. They also demonstrate India's commitment to preserving and promoting its cultural heritage, as well as embracing diversity and inclusivity.
