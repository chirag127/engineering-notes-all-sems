## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

- Society is a group of people who share a common culture, language, religion, ethnicity, or history.
- State is a political entity that has a defined territory, a government, and sovereignty over its people and resources.
- Polity is a form or system of government or political organization.
- India is a diverse and complex society with multiple religions, languages, castes, regions, and ethnicities.
- India is also a democratic republic with a parliamentary system of government and a federal structure of states and union territories.
- India's Constitution, adopted in 1950, is the supreme law of the land and guarantees fundamental rights and freedoms to all citizens.
- India's polity is influenced by various factors such as colonial legacy, nationalist movement, social movements, identity politics, regional aspirations, and global forces.
- Some of the major issues and challenges facing India's society and polity are poverty, inequality, corruption, communalism, secularism, federalism, human rights, and development.

Some of the main topics covered in this module are:

- Historical evolution of Indian society and state from ancient to modern times.
- Constitutional framework and institutions of Indian polity such as the president, the prime minister, the parliament, the judiciary, the election commission, etc.
- Political parties and ideologies in India such as the Congress, the BJP, the Left, the regional parties, etc.
- Social movements and civil society in India such as the Dalit movement, the women's movement, the environmental movement, the human rights movement, etc.
- Identity politics and diversity in India such as caste, religion, language, ethnicity, gender, etc.
- Regionalism and federalism in India such as the formation and reorganization of states, the autonomy and special status of some regions, the center-state relations, etc.
- India's foreign policy and international relations such as the non-alignment, the Panchsheel, the South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC), the BRICS, the United Nations, etc.
- India's development and challenges such as the economic reforms, the social sector, the infrastructure, the environment, the governance, the security, etc.
