### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

India is a country of diverse and rich cultural heritage that has influenced many aspects of the world. Some of the major contributions of India to the world are:

- **Education**: India gave the world its first university, Takshashila University, which was established in the 6th century BCE and had more than 10,000 students from various countries . India also had other ancient centers of learning, such as Nalanda, Vikramashila, and Taxila, that attracted scholars from different regions of Asia.
- **Mathematics**: India gave the world the numeral zero, which is essential for arithmetic, algebra, calculus, and other branches of mathematics . India also developed the decimal system, the concept of infinity, and various mathematical concepts such as trigonometry, geometry, algebra, and calculus.
- **Games**: India gave the world the game of chess, which originated from an ancient Indian game called chaturanga . India also inspired the game of snakes and ladders, which was derived from an Indian game called mokshapat that taught moral lessons. India also invented the game of cards, which was based on the Indian concept of the four suits representing the four varnas or social classes.
- **Medicine**: India gave the world the cure for leprosy, which was discovered by an Indian physician named Sushruta in the 6th century BCE. India also developed the system of ayurveda, which is one of the oldest forms of holistic medicine that uses natural herbs, diet, and lifestyle to treat various diseases. India also contributed to the fields of surgery, dentistry, ophthalmology, and anatomy, among others.
- **Art and Literature**: India gave the world some of the finest examples of art and literature, such as the Vedas, the Upanishads, the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, the Bhagavad Gita, the Panchatantra, the Kamasutra, and the Arthashastra . India also produced some of the most beautiful and intricate sculptures, paintings, architecture, and music, such as the Ajanta and Ellora caves, the Khajuraho temples, the Taj Mahal, the Sanchi stupa, the Bharatanatyam dance, and the Carnatic and Hindustani music.
- **Religion and Philosophy**: India gave the world some of the major religions and philosophies, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism, that have millions of followers across the globe . India also developed some of the most profound and influential schools of thought, such as the Vedanta, the Yoga, the Nyaya, the Mimamsa, the Samkhya, and the Advaita, that explored the nature of reality, the self, and the supreme being.
- **Science and Technology**: India gave the world some of the most advanced and innovative inventions and discoveries, such as the iron pillar of Delhi, which is a metallurgical marvel that has not rusted for over 1600 years. India also invented the cotton gin, the spinning wheel, the ink, the paper, the shampoo, the button, the ruler, the umbrella, the diamond, the sugar, the steel, and the rocket, among others . India also made significant contributions to the fields of astronomy, astrology, chemistry, metallurgy, and navigation, among others.
