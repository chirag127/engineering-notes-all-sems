### Harappan Script

- Harappan script is the name given to the symbols used by the Indus Valley Civilization, which flourished in the northwest of India and Pakistan from about 2500 to 1900 BCE.
- Harappan script is also known as Indus script, as it was discovered during the excavation of Harappan city.
- Harappan script consists of about 400 signs, which are mostly pictographic or ideographic, representing objects, animals, plants, actions, or concepts .
- Harappan script is written from right to left, sometimes with a boustrophedon (alternating direction) pattern .
- Harappan script is found on various types of inscriptions, such as seals, pottery, copper tablets, tools, and bricks . Most of the inscriptions are very short, consisting of only a few signs.
- Harappan script has not been deciphered satisfactorily, as there are no bilingual inscriptions or known underlying language . Some scholars have proposed that Harappan script is related to Dravidian, Sumerian, or Brahmi languages, but these claims are not widely accepted .
- Harappan script is one of the oldest known scripts of India, and it reflects the culture and civilization of the Indus Valley people . It is a valuable source of information for understanding the history and heritage of India.
