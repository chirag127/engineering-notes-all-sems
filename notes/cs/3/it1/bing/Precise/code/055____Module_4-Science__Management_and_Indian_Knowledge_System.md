## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

1. Science and Management: Science and management are two distinct fields of study that have a significant impact on society. Science is the systematic study of the natural world, while management is the art of planning, organizing, leading, and controlling resources to achieve specific goals.

2. Indian Knowledge System: The Indian knowledge system is a rich and diverse tradition that encompasses a wide range of disciplines, including philosophy, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, and the arts. This system has made significant contributions to the development of science and technology, and continues to influence contemporary thought and practice.

3. Science and Indian Knowledge System: The Indian knowledge system has a long history of scientific inquiry and discovery. Ancient Indian scholars made significant contributions to fields such as mathematics, astronomy, and medicine, and their work continues to be studied and revered today.

4. Management and Indian Knowledge System: The Indian knowledge system also has a rich tradition of management principles and practices. Ancient Indian texts such as the Arthashastra and the Manusmriti contain detailed discussions of statecraft, economics, and governance, and provide valuable insights into the art of management.

5. Contemporary Relevance: The Indian knowledge system continues to be relevant in contemporary times, and its principles and practices are being applied in a wide range of fields, including science, management, and the arts. The study of this system can provide valuable insights and perspectives, and can help to promote a deeper understanding of the world and our place in it.
