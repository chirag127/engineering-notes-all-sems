### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times

India has a rich history of dominance in various fields up to pre-colonial times. Here are some key points to note:

1. India was a major player in the global economy, with its share of world GDP estimated to be as high as 25% in the early 17th century.
2. India was known for its advanced systems of agriculture, irrigation, and water management.
3. India was a leader in the production of textiles, with its cotton and silk fabrics being highly sought after in global markets.
4. India had a well-developed system of medicine, with the Ayurvedic system being widely practiced.
5. India was also known for its advanced systems of mathematics, astronomy, and metallurgy.
6. India had a rich tradition of literature, music, and the arts, with its epics, such as the Ramayana and the Mahabharata, being widely known and celebrated.
7. India was also home to several major religions, including Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, and Sikhism, which had a profound impact on the world.

These are just a few examples of India's dominance up to pre-colonial times. India's rich history and contributions to the world in various fields are a testament to its strength and resilience.
