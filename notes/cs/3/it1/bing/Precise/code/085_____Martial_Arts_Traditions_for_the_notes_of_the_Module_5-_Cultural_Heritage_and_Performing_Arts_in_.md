### Martial Arts Traditions

Martial arts are codified systems and traditions of combat practices, which are practised for a variety of reasons – self-defence, competition, physical health and fitness, entertainment, as well as mental, physical, and spiritual development. India has an ancient tradition in diverse martial arts.

- **Kalaripayattu**: Often known as the ‘Mother Of All Martial Arts’, this 3000-year old art form originating from Kerala, draws inspiration from the raw power, swift movements and sinuous strength of majestic animals – the lion, tiger, elephant, wild boar, snake and crocodile.

- **Khusti**: The Indian Wrestling is also a part of Indian Martial arts found throughout the India.

- **Thang-Ta**: The ancient Manipuri Martial Art, also known as Huyen Lallong, is Thang-Ta. Swords and spears are used in Manipuri Martial arts, a powerful yet gracefully complex technique.

- **Lathi**: Lathi is India’s oldest armed martial art. It is also regarded as one of the world’s earliest martial arts weapons.

These are just a few examples of the rich tradition of martial arts in India. There are many more forms and styles that have developed over the centuries. Each has its own unique history, techniques, and cultural significance.
