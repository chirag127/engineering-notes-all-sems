### Persian and Urdu

#### Persian
- Persian is an Iranian language belonging to the Indo-Iranian branch of the Indo-European family of languages.
- It is primarily spoken in Iran, Afghanistan, and Tajikistan, and some other regions which historically were Persianate societies.
- Persian has had a considerable influence on neighboring languages, particularly the Turkic languages in Central Asia, Caucasus, and Anatolia, as well as Urdu, Hindi, and other Indian languages.

#### Urdu
- Urdu is an Indo-Aryan language spoken mainly in Pakistan and India.
- It is the official language of Pakistan and is also widely spoken in the Indian states of Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Telangana, and the national capital, Delhi.
- Urdu is closely related to Hindi and the two languages share a common grammar and vocabulary, but differ in their writing systems and cultural associations.
- Urdu has been influenced by Persian and Arabic, and its vocabulary contains many words from these languages.
