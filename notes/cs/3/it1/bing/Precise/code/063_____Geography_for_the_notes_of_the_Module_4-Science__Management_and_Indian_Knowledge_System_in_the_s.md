### Geography for the notes of the Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. India is located in the northern hemisphere, in the southern part of Asia.
2. It is the seventh-largest country in the world by area and the second-most populous country.
3. India is surrounded by the Bay of Bengal in the east, the Arabian Sea in the west, and the Indian Ocean to the south.
4. The Himalayan mountain range in the north separates India from the rest of Asia.
5. India has a diverse climate, ranging from tropical in the south to temperate in the north.
6. The country is divided into 28 states and 8 union territories.
7. India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage, with many different languages, religions, and customs.
8. Agriculture is the primary occupation of the majority of the population, with the country being one of the largest producers of wheat, rice, and other crops.
9. India has a rapidly growing economy and is a member of the BRICS group of emerging economies.
10. The country has made significant progress in the fields of science and technology, with a strong focus on space exploration and renewable energy.
