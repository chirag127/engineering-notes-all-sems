### Āshrama or the Stages of Life

Āshrama is a term used in Hinduism to describe the four stages of life. These stages are:

1. **Brahmacharya** (Student): This stage is focused on education and learning. The individual is expected to remain celibate and focus on acquiring knowledge and developing character.

2. **Grihastha** (Householder): This stage is focused on building a family and fulfilling one's duties to society. The individual is expected to marry, raise children, and contribute to the community.

3. **Vanaprastha** (Hermit or Forest-dweller): This stage is focused on spiritual pursuits and withdrawal from worldly affairs. The individual is expected to gradually detach from material possessions and relationships and devote themselves to spiritual practices.

4. **Sannyasa** (Renunciate): This stage is focused on renunciation and the pursuit of spiritual liberation. The individual is expected to renounce all worldly attachments and live a life of simplicity and contemplation.

These stages are not strictly followed by all Hindus and may vary depending on individual circumstances and cultural practices. However, they provide a general framework for understanding the progression of life in Hindu society.
