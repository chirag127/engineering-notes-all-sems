## Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

1. Indian literature has a rich and diverse history, with works in many languages including Sanskrit, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, and others.
2. Indian culture is characterized by its diversity, with many different customs, traditions, and practices found throughout the country.
3. Indian traditions include a wide range of practices, such as the celebration of festivals, the practice of yoga and meditation, and the observance of religious rituals.
4. Indian practices include a variety of social customs, such as the joint family system, arranged marriages, and the caste system.
5. Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices are all deeply interconnected, with each influencing and shaping the others.
