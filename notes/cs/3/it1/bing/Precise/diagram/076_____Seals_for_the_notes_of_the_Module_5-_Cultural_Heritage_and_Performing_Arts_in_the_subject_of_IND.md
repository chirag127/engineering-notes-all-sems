### Seals for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Seals are an important part of Indian cultural heritage and performing arts.
2. Seals have been used in Indian society for thousands of years, providing food, garment, heat, and light for people living in challenging Northern climates.
3. Seal hunting is central to Indigenous culture and allows communities to share traditional customs, skills, and values from generation to generation.
4. Archeological evidence indicates that Indigenous People in Canada have been hunting seals for at least 4,000 years.
5. Seals have also been used in Indian art, such as sculptures, coins, and pottery .
6. Seals are part of the larger cultural heritage and performing arts of India, which includes dance, music, theatre, drama, painting, and martial arts traditions .
7. Indian traditions, culture, and society have a rich history of growth and development in the performing arts.
8. The performing arts of India have a well-placed significance in the civilization and tradition of Indian society.
