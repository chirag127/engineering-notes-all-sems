### Puppetry

Puppetry is one of the most ancient forms of entertainment in India that combines all the elements of performing arts as well as visual art such as painting, sculpture, music, dance, drama etc. 

- **Traditional Folk Media**: Puppetry is one of the traditional art forms which is used as an effective medium of entertainment and communication among the rural masses. 
- **Storytelling**: Indian Puppetry, popularly known as Kathputli, is an ancient performance that involves storytelling through playing the puppets. The puppeteers went from place to place to entertain the people by performing mythological stories, historical romances, and so on. 
- **Types of Puppets**: India is home to many contemporary initiatives that renovate puppetry while supporting the preservation of heritage styles. Living traditions include different types of puppets: string puppets, rod puppets, glove puppets, and shadow puppets. 
- **History**: Several literary sources report the existence of puppetry in ancient times. Puppetry is mentioned in the Mahabharata (dated from the 9th century BCE). 
- **Regional Variations**: Different regions in India have their own unique styles of puppetry. For example, the art of Santhal puppetry, the Chadar Badar, is an ancient art form practiced by the Santhals, one of the largest tribal communities of India mainly found in the states of West Bengal, Jharkhand, Bihar, and Odisha.
