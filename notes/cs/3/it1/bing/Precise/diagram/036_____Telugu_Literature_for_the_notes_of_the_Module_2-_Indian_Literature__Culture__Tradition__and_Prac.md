### Telugu Literature

Telugu literature is the body of works written in the Telugu language, one of the classical languages of India. It has a rich and diverse history, with influences from Sanskrit, Prakrit, and Tamil literature.

1. Early Telugu literature was mostly religious and philosophical in nature, with works such as the Mahabharata and Ramayana being translated into Telugu.
2. The 11th century saw the emergence of the Telugu poetic tradition, with Nannaya Bhattaraka being the first known Telugu poet.
3. The Vijayanagara Empire (14th-17th centuries) was a golden age for Telugu literature, with poets such as Srinatha, Potana, and Allasani Peddana producing works of great literary merit.
4. The 19th and 20th centuries saw the rise of social and political consciousness in Telugu literature, with writers such as Gurajada Apparao and Sri Sri addressing issues of social justice and inequality.
5. Modern Telugu literature continues to be vibrant and diverse, with writers exploring a wide range of themes and styles.
