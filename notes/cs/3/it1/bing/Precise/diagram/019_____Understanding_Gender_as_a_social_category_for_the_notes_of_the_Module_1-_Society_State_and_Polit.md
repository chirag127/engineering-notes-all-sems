### Understanding Gender as a social category for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Gender is a social construct that refers to the roles, behaviors, activities, and attributes that a given society considers appropriate for men and women.
- Gender is not the same as sex, which refers to the biological and physiological characteristics that define men and women.
- Gender roles and expectations vary across cultures and can change over time.
- Gender is often used to categorize individuals and can influence access to resources, opportunities, and power.
- Gender inequality refers to the unequal treatment or perceptions of individuals based on their gender and can manifest in various forms, including unequal pay, limited access to education and healthcare, and gender-based violence.
- Understanding gender as a social category is important for addressing issues of gender inequality and promoting gender equity.
