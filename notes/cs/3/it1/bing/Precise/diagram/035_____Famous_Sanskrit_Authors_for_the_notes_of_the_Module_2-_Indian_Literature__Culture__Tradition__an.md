### Famous Sanskrit Authors

Sanskrit is one of the oldest languages in the world and has a rich literary tradition. Here are some famous Sanskrit authors:

1. **Kalidasa**: Kalidasa is widely regarded as the greatest poet and dramatist in the Sanskrit language. His most famous works include the plays Abhijnanasakuntalam and Vikramorvasiyam, and the epic poems Raghuvamsa and Kumarasambhava.

2. **Valmiki**: Valmiki is the author of the epic poem Ramayana, which tells the story of Rama, an incarnation of the god Vishnu. Valmiki is also considered to be the first poet in Sanskrit literature.

3. **Vyasa**: Vyasa is the legendary author of the epic poem Mahabharata, which is one of the two major Sanskrit epics of ancient India. He is also credited with compiling the Vedas, the ancient Hindu scriptures.

4. **Bhasa**: Bhasa was a Sanskrit playwright who lived around the 2nd or 3rd century BC. He is known for his plays based on the Mahabharata and Ramayana, as well as for his original plays.

5. **Bhartrihari**: Bhartrihari was a poet and philosopher who lived around the 5th century AD. He is known for his poetry collections, which include the Sringara Sataka, the Niti Sataka, and the Vairagya Sataka.

These are just a few of the many famous Sanskrit authors who have contributed to the rich literary tradition of the language. Their works continue to be studied and appreciated today.
