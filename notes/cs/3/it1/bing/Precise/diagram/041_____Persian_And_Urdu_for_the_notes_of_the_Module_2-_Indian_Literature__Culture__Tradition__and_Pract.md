### Persian And Urdu

#### Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

#### Indian Traditions, Cultural and Society

- Persian and Urdu are two languages that have had a significant impact on Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices.
- Persian was the official language of the Mughal Empire, which ruled over large parts of India from the 16th to the 19th century.
- Urdu, on the other hand, developed as a language of the common people, with influences from Persian, Arabic, and local Indian languages.
- Both Persian and Urdu have a rich literary tradition, with famous poets and writers such as Mirza Ghalib, Amir Khusro, and Allama Iqbal.
- Persian and Urdu have also influenced Indian music, with many classical and semi-classical compositions being based on Persian and Urdu poetry.
- In terms of culture and tradition, Persian and Urdu have left their mark on Indian cuisine, clothing, and architecture.
- Many famous Indian dishes, such as biryani and kebabs, have their origins in Persian cuisine.
- Similarly, traditional Indian clothing such as the sherwani and the anarkali have been influenced by Persian fashion.
- In architecture, the Mughal style, which is characterized by the use of domes, arches, and intricate ornamentation, has its roots in Persian architecture.
- In conclusion, Persian and Urdu have had a profound impact on Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices, and their influence can still be seen and felt in modern-day India.
