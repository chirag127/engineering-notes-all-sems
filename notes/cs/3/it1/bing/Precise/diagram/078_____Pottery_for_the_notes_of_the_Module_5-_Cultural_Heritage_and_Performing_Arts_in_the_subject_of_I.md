### Pottery

Pottery is one of the most timeless living craft traditions of India. It has an ancient history and is one of the most tangible and iconic elements of Indian art. The real beginning of Indian pottery began with the Indus Valley Civilization and the art of shaping and baking clay articles as pottery, earthenware, and porcelain has continued through the ages .

- In India, being an agricultural country, pots for storage of water and grains were in demand.
- Pottery is one of those important mediums through which men have expressed their emotions.
- Pottery is practiced across the length and breadth of the country. A visit to the countryside and “potter’ villages” speaks volumes about the economic importance of this craft.
- Evidence of pottery has been found in the early settlements of Lahuradewa and later the Indus Valley Civilization.
- Today, it is a cultural art that is still practiced extensively in the Indian subcontinent.
- Pottery plays an important role in studying culture and reconstructing the past.
