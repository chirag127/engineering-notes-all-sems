### Chemistry in India

Chemistry has a long history in India, with evidence of chemical techniques dating back to the Indus Valley or Harappan civilization (3rd millennium BCE). In ancient texts, there is information available on metals, ores, their quarries, compounds, and alloys. There are also details of hundreds of devices used in chemical experiments in ‘Rasayan Shastra’ which we call ‘chemistry’. Ayurveda, which used a variety of minerals, also played an important role in the development of chemistry.

In modern times, the scope of chemistry as a career option is immense in India. Candidates can pursue degree, diploma, and certificate courses in chemistry at undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral levels. Some of the best global universities for chemistry in India include the Indian Institute of Science (IISC) in Bangalore and the Indian Institutes of Technology (IIT) in Bombay.

The Royal Society of Chemistry also has a presence in India, with events and exhibitions taking place in the country.
