### Astronomy in India

- Astronomy has a long history in the Indian subcontinent, stretching from pre-historic to modern times. Some of the earliest roots of Indian astronomy can be dated to the period of the Indus Valley civilization or earlier .
- Indian astronomy was influenced by Greek astronomy beginning in the 4th century BCE and through the early centuries of the Common Era, for example by the Yavanajataka and the Romaka Siddhanta, a Sanskrit translation of a Greek text disseminated from the 2nd century .
- Astronomy later developed as a discipline of Vedanga, or one of the "auxiliary disciplines" associated with the study of the Vedas .
- The Sūryasiddhānta, the most popular work on astronomy in North India, is attributed to divine authorship but seems to have been composed about AD 800. It adopts the midnight epoch and certain elements from the old Saurasiddhānta, but differs from it in other respects .
- Indian astronomy and astrology were closely related. The ancient Indians believed that the position of the planets determined our fortunes, particularly Saturn and Mars .
