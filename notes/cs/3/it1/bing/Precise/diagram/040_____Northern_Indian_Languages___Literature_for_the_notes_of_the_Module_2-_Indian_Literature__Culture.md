### Northern Indian Languages & Literature

#### Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

##### Indian Traditions, Cultural and Society

1. The development of North Indian languages can be traced back to the early medieval period, where the old Apabhramsha language had taken new forms or was in the process of evolving into other forms .
2. These languages were evolving at two levels: the spoken and the written language .
3. The modern languages of northern India emerged from these and other related languages .
4. The literature of these languages depended largely on the ancient Indian background, which includes two Sanskrit epic poems, the Mahabharata and Ramayana, as well as the Bhagavata-purana and the other Puranas .
5. North India was the birthplace of Kalidasa, who wrote classic Sanskrit plays like Mālavikāgnimitram, Abhijñānaśākuntalam and Vikramōrvaśīyam and poems like Raghuvaṃśa, Kumārasambhava, Ṛtusaṃhāra and Meghadūta in which the use of imagination and similes remains unequaled by any other literary work .
