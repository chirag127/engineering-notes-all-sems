### The Ramayana

The Ramayana is a Hindu epic about the life of the legendary Prince Rama of Kosala. It is one of the two great epic poems of India, the other being the Mahabharata. The Ramayana was composed in Sanskrit, probably not before 300 BCE, by the poet Valmiki and in its present form consists of some 24,000 couplets divided into seven books .

- The story follows the life of Prince Rama, who is exiled from his homeland with his wife Sita .
- While in exile, Rama battles the demon king Ravan, who abducts Sita .
- Rama defeats the demons and rescues Sita .
- They return from exile and Rama becomes king .

This epic poem is an important part of Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices. It tells the story of the virtuous, wise, and powerful prince Rama, who is able to accomplish what no other man has ever done: he lifts and strings the bow of Siva, and by so doing earns the right to marry the beautiful Sita .
