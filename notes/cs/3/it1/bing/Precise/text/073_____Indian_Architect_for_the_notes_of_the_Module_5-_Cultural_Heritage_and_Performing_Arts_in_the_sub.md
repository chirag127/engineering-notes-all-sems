### Indian Architect

Module 5 of the subject Indian Traditions, Culture, and Society focuses on Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts. This module covers various topics related to Indian architecture, engineering, and architecture in ancient India, sculptures, seals, coins, pottery, puppetry, dance, music, theatre, drama, painting, martial arts traditions, fairs and festivals, current developments in arts and culture, and India's cultural contribution to the world.

- The Harappan period is known for its expert town planning and engineering skills. Excavations at Harappa, Mohenjodaro, and several other sites of the Indus Valley Civilization revealed the existence of a very modern urban civilization.
- Indian architecture has a long and rich history, with influences from various regions, religions, and cultures. It has evolved over time to incorporate elements from different styles and periods.
- Indian architecture is known for its intricate carvings, detailed sculptures, and grand structures.
