### Persian And Urdu

#### Module 2- Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

1. Persian and Urdu are two languages that have had a significant impact on Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices.
2. Persian was the court language of the Mughal Empire, which ruled over large parts of India from the 16th to the 19th century. As a result, Persian had a strong influence on the development of Indian literature, particularly in the areas of poetry and prose.
3. Urdu, on the other hand, developed as a language of the common people, with roots in the local languages of northern India, as well as Persian and Arabic. It became a popular language for poetry and other forms of artistic expression, and has had a lasting impact on Indian culture and tradition.
4. Both Persian and Urdu have contributed to the rich tapestry of Indian literature, with many famous poets and writers working in these languages. Some notable examples include Mirza Ghalib, Amir Khusro, and Allama Iqbal.
5. Persian and Urdu have also influenced other aspects of Indian culture, such as music, art, and architecture. Many of the most famous monuments in India, such as the Taj Mahal, were built during the Mughal period and feature Persian and Urdu inscriptions.
6. In conclusion, Persian and Urdu have played an important role in shaping Indian literature, culture, tradition, and practices, and their influence can still be seen and felt today.
