### Marriage in the context of Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Marriage is a social institution that is recognized by the state and society.
- In India, marriage is considered a sacred bond between two individuals and their families.
- The institution of marriage has undergone significant changes in India over the years.
- Arranged marriages are still common in India, where families play a significant role in choosing a partner for their children.
- The legal age for marriage in India is 18 for women and 21 for men.
- The Indian constitution guarantees the right to marry a person of one's choice, irrespective of caste, religion, or gender.
- The Indian government has introduced several laws to protect the rights of married individuals, including laws against dowry, domestic violence, and child marriage.
- Marriage is not just a personal matter, but also has social, economic, and legal implications.
- The institution of marriage is constantly evolving, and its role and significance in Indian society continue to be debated and discussed.
