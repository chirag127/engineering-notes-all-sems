### Force Theory for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- The Force Theory of the origin of the state is one of the theories that explain the formation of the state in ancient India.
- According to this theory, the state was created by force, where one person or a group of people were able to establish control over a territory and its inhabitants through the use of force.
- This theory was not widely acclaimed in the ancient Indian polity, as the king was considered to be subordinate to the law, which was made by the society and not by him. The community as a whole was given greater importance than the king.
- The use of force has been a long-drawn history of warfare, bloodshed, and violence in the Indian subcontinent, where rulers have continued to exploit this 'legitimate use of force' in order to rule large parts.
- The Force Theory is one of the theories covered in the syllabus of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY, along with other theories such as Evolutionary Theory, Mystical Theory, and Contract Theory .
