### Famous Sanskrit Authors

1. **Valmiki**: He is the author of the epic Ramayana.
2. **Maharshi Krishndvaipayan Vedvyasa**: He is the author of the epic Mahabharata.
3. **Bharat Muni**: He is the author of Natyashastra.
4. **Bhasa**: He is the author of Svapnavasavdatta.
5. **Lagadha**: He wrote Vedanga Jyotisha, the earliest Indian text on astronomy.
6. **Charaka**: He is one of the most important authors in Ayurveda.
7. **Vishnu Sharma**: He is the author of Panchatantra.
8. **Nagasena**: He is an author from the 2nd century BC.
9. **Śhudraka**: He is a famous Sanskrit dramatist.
10. **Asvaghosa**: He is a famous Sanskrit dramatist.
11. **Kalidasa**: He is a famous Sanskrit dramatist.
12. **Satyavrat Shastri**: He became the first Sanskrit author to win the Jnanpith Award, India's highest literary award.
13. **Vidyadhar Shastri**: He wrote two epic poems (Mahakavya), seven shorter poems, three plays and three songs of praise (stavana kavya). He received the Vidyavachaspati award in 1962.
