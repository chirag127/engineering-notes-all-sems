### Music for the notes of the Module 5- Cultural Heritage and Performing Arts in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

1. Music is an integral part of Indian culture and has a rich history and tradition.
2. Indian music can be broadly classified into two categories: Hindustani music and Carnatic music.
3. Hindustani music is mainly found in the northern and central regions of India, while Carnatic music is mainly found in the southern regions.
4. Both Hindustani and Carnatic music have their own unique styles, instruments, and compositions.
5. Indian music has a strong spiritual and devotional aspect, with many compositions dedicated to Hindu gods and goddesses.
6. Indian music has also been influenced by various other cultures, including Persian, Central Asian, and European.
7. Indian music has a wide range of instruments, including string instruments, percussion instruments, and wind instruments.
8. Some of the most popular Indian instruments include the sitar, tabla, sarod, and harmonium.
9. Indian music has a rich tradition of vocal music, with various styles and genres, including classical, devotional, and folk.
10. Indian music has also played a significant role in Indian cinema, with many popular songs and compositions being featured in Bollywood movies.
