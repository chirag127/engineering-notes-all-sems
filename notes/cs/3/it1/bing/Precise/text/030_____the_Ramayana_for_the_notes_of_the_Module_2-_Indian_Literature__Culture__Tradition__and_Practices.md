### The Ramayana

The Ramayana is a Hindu epic about the life of the legendary Prince Rama of Kosala . It is one of the two great epic poems of India, the other being the Mahabharata . The Ramayana was composed in Sanskrit, probably not before 300 BCE, by the poet Valmiki and in its present form consists of some 24,000 couplets divided into seven books .

The story follows Rama and his wife Sita, who are exiled from Rama's homeland . While in exile, Rama battles the demon king Ravan, who abducts Sita . Rama defeats the demons and rescues Sita. They return from exile and Rama becomes king .

Rama is the virtuous, wise, and powerful prince of Ayohya . As a young man, he is able to accomplish what no other man has ever done: he lifts and strings the bow of Siva, and by so doing earns the right to marry the beautiful Sita .
