### Council of Ministers

The Council of Ministers is the principal executive organ of the Government of India. It exercises executive authority in India and functions as the senior decision-making body of the executive branch. The Council of Ministers is chaired by the Prime Minister and consists of the heads of each of the executive government ministries .

There are five categories of the Council of Ministers, in descending order of rank:
1. Prime Minister: Leader of the executive of the Government of India.
2. Deputy Prime Minister (if any): Presides as Prime Minister in his absence or as the senior-most Cabinet Minister.
3. Cabinet Ministers: Senior Ministers who hold important portfolios.
4. Ministers of State: Junior Ministers who assist Cabinet Ministers.
5. Union Minister of State (Independent Charge): Junior Ministers who hold independent charge of a ministry  .

The President of India appoints each of them .
