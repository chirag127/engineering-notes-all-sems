# Sangama Literature

Sangama Literature is a collection of classical Tamil literature that was produced during the Sangam period, which is estimated to have lasted from 300 BCE to 300 CE. This literature is considered to be one of the greatest works of Tamil literature and is known for its secular nature and its focus on human emotions and relationships.

Here are some key points about Sangama Literature:

1. Sangama Literature is divided into two main groups: the Eight Anthologies (Ettuthokai) and the Ten Idylls (Pattuppattu).
2. The Eight Anthologies consist of eight collections of poetry, each containing between 100 and 400 poems. These poems are primarily concerned with love and war.
3. The Ten Idylls are longer poems that deal with a variety of subjects, including ethics, politics, and history.
4. Sangama Literature also includes two long epic poems, the Silappatikaram and the Manimekalai, which are considered to be among the greatest works of Tamil literature.
5. The poems in Sangama Literature were composed by a large number of poets, many of whom are anonymous. Some of the most famous poets of the Sangam period include Kapilar, Paranar, and Avvaiyar.
6. Sangama Literature is known for its use of the Tamil language, which is considered to be one of the oldest and most sophisticated languages in the world.
7. The poems in Sangama Literature are characterized by their use of complex metaphors and their focus on the emotions and experiences of everyday people.
8. Sangama Literature has had a profound influence on the development of Tamil literature and culture, and its themes and ideas continue to be relevant today.
