### Kannada Literature

Kannada literature is the literature written in the Kannada language, one of the official languages of India and the native language of the state of Karnataka. Kannada literature has a rich history and tradition, dating back to the 9th century.

1. Early Kannada literature was mainly composed of poetry and was heavily influenced by Sanskrit literature.
2. The earliest known Kannada work is the Kavirajamarga, a treatise on poetics written by King Nripatunga Amoghavarsha I in the 9th century.
3. During the 12th century, the Vachana Sahitya movement emerged, which was characterized by the composition of devotional poetry in the Kannada language.
4. The most famous poet of this movement was Basavanna, whose poems, known as Vachanas, are still widely read and recited today.
5. In the 16th century, the Haridasa movement emerged, which was characterized by the composition of devotional songs in praise of the Hindu god Vishnu.
6. The most famous poet of this movement was Purandara Dasa, who is considered the father of Carnatic music.
7. Modern Kannada literature began in the 19th century and was heavily influenced by the Western literary tradition.
8. The most famous modern Kannada writer is Kuvempu, who was awarded the Jnanpith Award, India's highest literary honor, for his epic poem, Sri Ramayana Darshanam.
