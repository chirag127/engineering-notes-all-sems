# Persian and Urdu

## Module 2: Indian Literature, Culture, Tradition, and Practices

### Persian
- Persian is an Indo-Iranian language spoken primarily in Iran, Afghanistan, and Tajikistan.
- It has had a significant influence on the Indian subcontinent, particularly in the areas of art, architecture, and literature.
- Persian was the official language of the Mughal Empire in India and was widely used in the courts and administration.
- Many famous works of Indian literature were written in Persian, including the poetry of Amir Khusrau and the historical accounts of Abul Fazl.

### Urdu
- Urdu is an Indo-Aryan language spoken primarily in Pakistan and India.
- It developed as a lingua franca among the Muslim soldiers of the Mughal Empire and was heavily influenced by Persian and Arabic.
- Urdu is written in a modified form of the Persian alphabet and has a rich literary tradition, with famous poets such as Mirza Ghalib and Allama Iqbal.
- Urdu is one of the official languages of India and is widely spoken in the northern states, particularly in Uttar Pradesh and Delhi.
