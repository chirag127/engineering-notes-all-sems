### Current developments in Arts and Cultural

1. India has a significant cultural capital that includes 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and a diversely vibrant cultural heritage .
2. Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have recently joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities .
3. The first edition of Delhi Art Week will unfold in 4 ‘Art Zones’ at India Gate, Jawaharlal Stadium, Lotus Temple, and Qutub Minar. This celebration of art will see over 30 galleries, public and private museums participating .
4. India has one of the world’s largest collections of songs, music, dance, theatre, folk traditions, performing arts, rites and rituals, paintings, and writings that are known as the ‘Intangible Cultural Heritage’ (ICH) of humanity .
5. The Ministry of Culture, Government of India, provides financial assistance for cultural activities in performing arts, allied cultural activities, and Tagore Cultural Complexes. It also has a scheme of scholarship and fellowship for the promotion of art and culture .
