# Other Heterodox Sects

- Heterodox schools of Indian philosophy are those that do not accept the authority of the Vedas.
- These schools include Buddhism, Jainism, Ajivika, and Charvaka.
- Almost every school of Indian philosophy is associated with a religious sect.
- The majority of Asian Indians practice Hinduism, while other major religions include Sikhism, Buddhism, Jainism, Christianity, and Islam.
- The Ajivikas philosophy held that all things are preordained, and therefore religious or ethical practice has no effect on one's future.
- The Carvaka school is characterized as a materialistic and aesthetic school of thought.
