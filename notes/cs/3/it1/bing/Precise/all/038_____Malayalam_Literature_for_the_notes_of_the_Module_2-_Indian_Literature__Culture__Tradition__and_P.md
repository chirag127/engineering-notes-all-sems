### Malayalam Literature

Malayalam literature refers to the body of literature written in the Malayalam language, which is the official language of the Indian state of Kerala and the union territories of Lakshadweep and Puducherry.

1. Malayalam literature has a rich history dating back to the 12th century.
2. The earliest known Malayalam work is Ramacharitam, an epic poem written by Cheeraman in the 13th century.
3. The development of Malayalam literature can be divided into three periods: the ancient period, the medieval period, and the modern period.
4. The ancient period saw the development of Malayalam as a distinct language and the emergence of various literary forms such as poetry, drama, and prose.
5. The medieval period saw the growth of devotional literature, with the composition of hymns and songs in praise of Hindu gods and goddesses.
6. The modern period, which began in the late 19th century, saw the emergence of a new literary movement that sought to reflect the social and political changes taking place in Kerala.
7. Some of the notable Malayalam writers of the modern period include Kumaran Asan, Vallathol Narayana Menon, and Ulloor S. Parameswara Iyer.
8. Malayalam literature continues to thrive today, with a vibrant contemporary literary scene that includes poetry, fiction, drama, and non-fiction.
