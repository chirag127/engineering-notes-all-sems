# Agriculture in India

Agriculture is one of the most important sectors in India, as it is the primary source of livelihood for about 58% of India’s population . India has the world's largest cattle herd (buffaloes), largest area planted to wheat, rice, and cotton, and is the largest producer of milk, pulses, and spices in the world .

- India is the world's largest producer of many fresh fruits like banana, mango, guava, papaya, lemon and vegetables like chickpea, okra and milk, major spices like chili pepper, ginger, fibrous crops such as jute, staples such as millets and castor oilseed .

- The history of agriculture in India dates back to the Indus Valley civilization era, and even before that in some parts of southern India .

- Over 54% of the country's land is classified as arable and the agriculture industry comprises half of the labor market .

- Agriculture, with its allied sectors, is the largest source of livelihoods in India. 70 percent of its rural households still depend primarily on agriculture for their livelihood, with 82 percent of farmers being small and marginal .

- In 2017-18, total food grain production was estimated at 275 million tonnes (MT) .

- India's agriculture is feeling the effects of extreme weather events, with reports of temperatures reaching 54°C in some areas, endangering farming, biodiversity and life itself .

- The Agriculture Ministry’s budgetary allocation as a percentage of the total budget of the Centre has come down from 4.41% in 2020-21 to 2.57% in 2023-24 .
