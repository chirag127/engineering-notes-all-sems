# Writing Technology in India

### Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Technical writing in India is a lesser known profession which is fast gaining recognition. Majority of the technical writing job vacancies in India are with the software companies and web development companies.
- There are several top technical writing institutes in India, such as TechnoKraft, a leading training hub in Bangalore, which has trained thousands of students in technical writing, instructional design, and software training.
- Online platforms such as Coursera also offer technical writing courses in India, with programs provided by institutions such as the Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology.
- Digitalization and the widespread proliferation of Roman-alphabet keyboards in India have led to the use of ad hoc Romanizations for transcribing Indian languages on the internet.
- Technical writing courses are mostly offered as certificate or diploma courses and are mostly pursued by students of science background. The average fee to pursue a Technical writing course ranges from INR 5,000 to INR 40,000.
- India has a nostalgic passion for old typewriters, with institutes such as the Umapathi Typewriting Institute teaching typing in three languages – English, Hindi and the local language of Tamil.
