# Dance

Dance is an integral part of Indian culture and society. It has a rich history and tradition that can be traced back to the Harappan culture, where the discovery of the bronze statue of a dancing girl testifies to the fact that some women in Harappa performed dances.

- Indian classical dance has a tradition of thousands of years and is related to Hindu gods and goddesses that speak of their life stories, such as Shiva and Vishnu.
- According to Indian mythology, dance was created by the gods.
- Dancing is one of the most renowned Hindu arts because it integrates song, drama, shape, and line.
- Indian dance emphasizes gestures, body positions, and head movements.
- Bharat Natyam is a major genre of classical dance that originated inside the Hindu temples and is usually accompanied by Carnatic music.
- Bharat Natyam has always been a solo dance that expresses Hindu religious themes and spiritual ideas, particularly of Shaivism.
- India also has a rich tradition of folk and tribal dances, with each state having its own unique dance forms.
- Performing arts in India are ancient but are still in practice today and enjoy a status in Indian culture and showcase Indian tradition.
- Performing arts in India are not just about artistic expression, but also about the way Indians enjoy festivals, heritage, and ceremonies.
