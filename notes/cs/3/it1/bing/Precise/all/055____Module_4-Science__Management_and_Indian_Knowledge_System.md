## Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System

1. Science and Management are two distinct fields of study, but they are interrelated in many ways.
2. Science is the systematic study of the natural world, while Management is the art and science of organizing and directing human and material resources towards the achievement of specific goals.
3. Indian Knowledge System is a rich and diverse body of knowledge that has evolved over thousands of years and encompasses a wide range of disciplines, including science, mathematics, medicine, philosophy, and the arts.
4. The Indian Knowledge System has made significant contributions to the fields of science and management, and its principles and practices continue to be relevant and applicable in the modern world.
5. Some of the key principles of the Indian Knowledge System that are relevant to science and management include the importance of observation and experimentation, the value of holistic thinking, and the need for ethical and sustainable practices.
6. In conclusion, the study of Science, Management, and the Indian Knowledge System can provide valuable insights and tools for addressing the complex challenges facing the world today.
