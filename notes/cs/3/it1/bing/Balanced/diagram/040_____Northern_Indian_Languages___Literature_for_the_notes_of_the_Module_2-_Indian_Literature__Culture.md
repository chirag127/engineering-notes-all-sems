### Northern Indian Languages & Literature

- Northern India is a diverse region with many languages and dialects, some of which are mutually intelligible and some of which are not.
- The most widely spoken languages in Northern India are Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Kashmiri, and Nepali. These languages belong to the Indo-Aryan branch of the Indo-European language family, except for Nepali, which belongs to the Sino-Tibetan family.
- Hindi is the official language of India and the most widely spoken language in Northern India. It is written in the Devanagari script and has many regional varieties, such as Bhojpuri, Maithili, Awadhi, and Braj Bhasha. Hindi is influenced by Sanskrit, Persian, and Arabic, and has a rich literary tradition dating back to the medieval period.
- Urdu is the national language of Pakistan and one of the official languages of India. It is spoken mainly by Muslims in Northern India, especially in Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, and Jammu and Kashmir. It is written in the Perso-Arabic script and has a close affinity with Hindi, sharing much of its vocabulary and grammar. Urdu is influenced by Persian, Arabic, and Turkish, and has a distinguished literary tradition, especially in poetry, such as ghazals and nazms.
- Punjabi is the official language of Punjab, a state in Northern India, and the provincial language of Punjab, a province in Pakistan. It is spoken by Sikhs, Hindus, and Muslims in Northern India, and by Muslims and Christians in Pakistan. It is written in the Gurmukhi script in India and in the Shahmukhi script in Pakistan. Punjabi is influenced by Sanskrit, Persian, and Arabic, and has a vibrant literary tradition, especially in folk songs, ballads, and religious hymns.
- Kashmiri is the official language of Jammu and Kashmir, a union territory in Northern India. It is spoken by the majority of the population in the Kashmir Valley, and by some communities in other parts of the region. It is written in the Perso-Arabic script, but also has its own native script, called Sharda. Kashmiri is influenced by Sanskrit, Persian, and Tibetan, and has a rich literary tradition, especially in poetry, such as lalas and vakh.
- Nepali is the official language of Nepal and one of the official languages of India. It is spoken by the people of Nepal and by some communities in Northern India, especially in Sikkim, West Bengal, Uttarakhand, and Assam. It is written in the Devanagari script and has many regional dialects, such as Darjeeling, Limbu, and Rai. Nepali is influenced by Sanskrit, Tibetan, and Hindi, and has a diverse literary tradition, including epics, folk tales, and modern novels.
