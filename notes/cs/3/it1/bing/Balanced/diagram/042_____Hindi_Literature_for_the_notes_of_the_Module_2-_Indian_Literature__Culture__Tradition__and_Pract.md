- Hindi literature is the literature written in the various Hindi languages, which have writing systems. Hindi languages are derived from the Indo-Aryan branch of the Indo-European language family.
- Hindi literature can be divided into four main periods: Adikal (the Early Period), Bhaktikal (the Devotional Period), Ritikal (the Scholastic Period), and Adhunik (the Modern Period).
- The earliest forms of Hindi literature are the poetry of Apabhraṃśa, which are dialects of Prakrit, such as Awadhi, Magadhi, Ardhamagadhi, and Marwari. These poems date back to the 11th century and reflect the religious and cultural diversity of India .
- The Adikal period (from the 11th to the 14th century) is marked by the emergence of the heroic poetry, which celebrates the valor and heroism of the Rajput warriors. The most famous examples are the Prithviraj Raso by Chand Bardai and the Alha-Khand by Jagnayak.
- The Bhaktikal period (from the 14th to the 17th century) is characterized by the devotional poetry, which expresses the love and devotion of the poets to various deities, such as Vishnu, Shiva, Rama, Krishna, and the Goddess. The most prominent poets of this period are Kabir, Surdas, Tulsidas, Meera Bai, and Raskhan.
- The Ritikal period (from the 17th to the 19th century) is dominated by the scholastic poetry, which follows the rules and conventions of the classical Sanskrit poetics. The poets of this period focus on the themes of love, romance, and aesthetics. The most notable poets of this period are Bihari, Keshavdas, Matiram, and Ghananand.
- The Adhunik period (from the 19th century onwards) is influenced by the modern trends and movements of the world literature, such as realism, nationalism, social reform, and experimentation. The poets of this period explore the issues of identity, society, politics, and culture. The most influential poets of this period are Bharatendu Harishchandra, Maithili Sharan Gupt, Mahadevi Varma, Ramdhari Singh Dinkar, and Harivansh Rai Bachchan .
- Hindi literature is composed in three broad styles: Gadya (prose), Padya (poetry), and Campū (prosimetrum, a mixture of prose and poetry). Hindi prose includes genres such as novels, short stories, essays, biographies, and travelogues. Hindi poetry includes genres such as epics, lyrics, ballads, and ghazals .
- Hindi literature reflects the rich and diverse heritage of the Indian culture, tradition, and practices. It also contributes to the development and enrichment of the Hindi language and its various dialects .
