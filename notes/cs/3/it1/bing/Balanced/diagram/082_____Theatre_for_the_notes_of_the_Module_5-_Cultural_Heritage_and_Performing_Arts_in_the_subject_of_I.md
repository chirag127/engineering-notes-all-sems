### Theatre

- Theatre is one of the most ancient forms of art and expression in India, dating back to the Vedic period.
- Theatre in India began as a narrative form, in which recitation, singing and dancing were integral elements.
- Theatre in India encompasses various genres, such as classical, folk, modern, and contemporary, each with its own distinctive features and styles.
- Some of the major forms of classical theatre in India are Sanskrit drama, Kutiyattam, Kathakali, Koodiyattam, Yakshagana, and Bhavai .
- Some of the major forms of folk theatre in India are Nautanki, Tamasha, Jatra, Bhand Pather, Swang, and Rasleela .
- Some of the major forms of modern theatre in India are Parsi theatre, IPTA, Prithvi theatre, and experimental theatre .
- Some of the major festivals of theatre in India are Prithvi Theatre Festival, Bharat Rang Mahotsav, Jairangam, and Nandikar's National Theatre Festival.
- Theatre in India reflects the diversity, richness, and complexity of the Indian culture, society, and history .
