### Indian’s Cultural Contribution to the World

India is a country of diverse and rich cultural heritage, which has influenced many aspects of the world civilization. Some of the major contributions of India to the world are:

- India gave the world its first university – Takshashila University, which was established around 700 BCE and had more than 10,000 students from different countries studying various subjects .
- India gave the world the numeral, Zero, which is the basis of modern mathematics and science. The concept of zero was developed by the Indian mathematicians around the 5th century CE .
- The game of Chess originated in India, where it was called Chaturanga, meaning four divisions of the army. The game was later adopted by the Persians, Arabs, and Europeans .
- Indians were the first ones to use and invent buttons, which were made of seashells and used to fasten clothes. Buttons were used in India as early as 2000 BCE .
- Shampoo originated from India, where it was derived from the Sanskrit word champu, meaning to massage. The practice of washing hair with herbs and oils was common in India since ancient times .
- India gave the cure for Leprosy, which was a dreaded disease in the ancient world. The Indian physician Sushruta described the symptoms and treatment of leprosy in his medical treatise Sushruta Samhita, which was written around the 6th century BCE .
- Snakes and Ladders was inspired from an Indian game called Mokshapat, which was a moral lesson on karma and destiny. The game was later introduced to England by the British colonialists in the 19th century .
- India has a rich tradition of literature, music, dance, and theatre, which have influenced the world culture in various ways. Some of the examples are the epic poems Ramayana and Mahabharata, the classical music systems of Hindustani and Carnatic, the classical dance forms of Bharatanatyam and Kathak, and the theatrical forms of Sanskrit drama and folk theatre  .
- India has a diverse and vibrant religious and philosophical thought, which has shaped the world views of many people. Some of the major religions and philosophies that originated in India are Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Sikhism, Vedanta, Yoga, and Ayurveda  .
- India has a remarkable history of scientific and technological innovations, which have contributed to the advancement of human knowledge and welfare. Some of the examples are the decimal system, the concept of pi, the Pythagorean theorem, the heliocentric model of the solar system, the concept of gravity, the concept of atom, the metallurgy of iron and steel, the invention of cotton, the invention of paper, the invention of ink, the invention of sugar, the invention of chess, the invention of the ruler, the invention of the calendar, the invention of the zero, the invention of the number system, the invention of algebra, the invention of trigonometry, the invention of calculus, the invention of astronomy, the invention of astrology, the invention of medicine, the invention of surgery, the invention of anesthesia, the invention of plastic surgery, the invention of cataract surgery, the invention of vaccination, the invention of the spinning wheel, the invention of the printing press, the invention of the radio, the invention of the wireless communication, the invention of the rocket, the invention of the missile, the invention of the nuclear reactor, the invention of the computer, the invention of the internet, and the invention of the artificial intelligence  .
