### Puranas

- Puranas are a vast genre of Indian literature that deal with various topics, especially legends and traditional lore .
- Puranas are considered as one of the most ancient and important sources of Hindu culture, religion, history, and cosmology .
- Puranas are classified as Smriti (remembered) texts, as opposed to Shruti (heard) texts like the Vedas .
- Puranas are composed in Sanskrit verse, mostly in the form of narrative couplets, similar to the epics Mahabharata and Ramayana.
- Puranas are divided into six categories, based on the main deity they glorify: Vishnu, Shiva, Brahma, Shakti, Agni, and Surya .
- Puranas are also classified into Mahapuranas (major Puranas) and Upapuranas (minor Puranas), based on their length and authority .
- Puranas contain various topics, such as creation and dissolution of the universe, genealogies of gods, sages, and kings, descriptions of sacred places, rituals, festivals, and ethical teachings .
- Puranas are not considered as authoritative or binding as the Vedas, but they are widely read and revered by the Hindus for their spiritual and cultural value .
