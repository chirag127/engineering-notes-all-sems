# Understanding Gender as a Social Category

- Gender is a social category that refers to the social and cultural meanings, expectations, and practices associated with being male or female in a given society .
- Gender is not the same as sex, which is a biological classification based on body parts. Sex is assigned at birth, while gender is learned and performed through socialization .
- Gender is a social construct, which means that it is created and maintained by society through norms, values, beliefs, language, symbols, and institutions .
- Gender is also a social structure, which means that it shapes and organizes social relations, interactions, and inequalities between men and women and among different groups of people .
- Gender is a dynamic and fluid category, which means that it can change over time and across contexts, and that people can express and experience their gender in diverse and complex ways .
- Gender is influenced by and intersects with other social categories, such as class, race, ethnicity, religion, age, sexuality, and disability, creating multiple and varied identities and experiences of oppression and privilege .
- Gender is a key dimension of social analysis, which means that it helps us to understand how society works, how social problems are created and solved, and how social change can be achieved .
