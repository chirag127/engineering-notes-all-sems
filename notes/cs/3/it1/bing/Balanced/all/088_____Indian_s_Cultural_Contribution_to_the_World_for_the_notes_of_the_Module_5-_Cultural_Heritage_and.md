# Indian's Cultural Contribution to the World

India is a country of diverse and rich cultural heritage, with a history of more than 5000 years. India has influenced and contributed to the world in various fields, such as art, literature, philosophy, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, architecture, music, dance, and games. Some of the notable contributions of India to the world are:

- India gave the world its first university, Takshashila University, which was established around 700 BCE and had more than 10,000 students from different countries .
- India gave the world the numeral system, which is the basis of modern mathematics and science. The concept of zero, decimal system, and place value system were developed in India .
- India gave the world the game of chess, which originated from an ancient Indian game called chaturanga, meaning four divisions of the army .
- India gave the world the use and invention of buttons, which were first used in India as ornaments and seals around 2000 BCE .
- India gave the world the shampoo, which derived from the Sanskrit word champu, meaning to massage or knead. The practice of washing hair with herbs and oils was introduced by Indian traders to Europe in the 18th century .
- India gave the world the cure for leprosy, which was discovered by an Indian physician named Sushruta in the 6th century BCE. He also performed the first plastic surgery and cataract surgery in the world .
- India gave the world the game of snakes and ladders, which was inspired by an Indian game called mokshapat, meaning the ladder to salvation. The game was used to teach moral values and karma to children .
- India gave the world the art of yoga, which is a system of physical, mental, and spiritual practices that originated in India around 5000 years ago. Yoga is widely practiced around the world for its health and wellness benefits .
- India gave the world the philosophy of non-violence, which was advocated by Mahatma Gandhi, the leader of the Indian independence movement. His principles of ahimsa (non-violence) and satyagraha (truth force) inspired many global leaders and movements, such as Martin Luther King Jr., Nelson Mandela, and the civil rights movement .
- India gave the world the literature of epics, such as the Ramayana and the Mahabharata, which are among the longest and oldest poems in the world. These epics contain stories of heroism, morality, and spirituality that have influenced many cultures and religions .
- India gave the world the architecture of temples, monuments, and sculptures, such as the Taj Mahal, the Khajuraho temples, the Ajanta and Ellora caves, and the Konark Sun Temple. These structures showcase the artistic and engineering skills of Indian craftsmen and reflect the diversity of Indian culture and religion .
- India gave the world the music of ragas, which are melodic modes that form the basis of Indian classical music. Indian music has influenced many genres of music, such as jazz, rock, pop, and fusion .
- India gave the world the dance of bharatanatyam, which is one of the oldest and most refined forms of classical dance in the world. Bharatanatyam is a graceful and expressive dance that combines movement, gesture, and emotion to convey a story or a theme .
- India gave the world the concept of democracy, which was practiced in ancient India as early as the 6th century BCE. The republics of Vaishali, Magadha, and Kosala had elected assemblies and councils that governed their affairs .
- India gave the world the science of ayurveda, which is a system of holistic medicine that originated in India around 3000 years ago. Ayurveda uses natural herbs, diet, and lifestyle to balance the body, mind, and spirit .
