# Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of art, music, dance, theatre, literature, architecture, and festivals.
- India is also home to 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and many smaller ones, and a vibrant contemporary art scene.
- Some of the current developments in arts and culture in India are:

  - Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, which aims to promote lifelong learning and social inclusion through culture and education.
  - India has nominated the Hoysala Temples of Karnataka, which are examples of the unique Hoysala style of architecture and sculpture, for inscription in the World Heritage List.
  - India has got back a 10th-century sandstone sculpture of a goat-headed Yogini, which was stolen and smuggled out of the country, from the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York.
  - India has launched the Dharohar project, which is a digital museum of the customs and GST department, showcasing the history and evolution of taxation and trade in India.
  - India has made new discoveries at Rakhigarhi, a Harappan site in Haryana, which reveal the presence of a cemetery, a fortification wall, and a script-like signboard.
  - India has celebrated the Republic Day Parade 2022, which featured various cultural and artistic performances, such as the tableau of the Ministry of Culture depicting the 75th anniversary of India's independence, the folk dances of different states and union territories, and the martial arts of the armed forces.
  - India has witnessed the emergence of new trends in contemporary Indian paintings, such as abstract expressionism, minimalism, pop art, graffiti, digital art, and mixed media, which reflect the influences of globalization, urbanization, and social issues on the Indian artists.
  - India has hosted and participated in various international cultural events and festivals, such as the Sydney WorldPride, which is Australia's flagship pride celebration that brings the LGBT community together, the Jaipur Literature Festival, which is the world's largest free literary festival, and the Kochi-Muziris Biennale, which is India's largest contemporary art event .
