# Mathematics in India

Mathematics in India is the discipline of mathematics as it developed in the Indian subcontinent from ancient to modern times. Some of the main features and achievements of Indian mathematics are:

- The invention of the decimal place-value system, which is the basis of the modern decimal number system. The earliest evidence of this system is found in the Bakhshali manuscript, dating from the 3rd or 4th century CE .
- The discovery of zero as a number and a symbol, and its use in arithmetic operations. The concept of zero was first mentioned by the mathematician and astronomer Brahmagupta in his treatise Brahmasphutasiddhanta, written in 628 CE.
- The development of algebra, trigonometry, geometry, and calculus, and their applications to astronomy, astrology, and other sciences. Some of the notable mathematicians who contributed to these fields were Aryabhata, Bhaskara II, Varahamihira, Mahavira, and Madhava .
- The formulation of various mathematical theorems, formulas, and methods, such as the Pythagorean theorem, the quadratic formula, the sine and cosine rules, the solution of indeterminate equations, the approximation of pi, and the infinite series for trigonometric functions .
- The creation of various mathematical texts and treatises, such as the Sulbasutras, the Aryabhatiya, the Lilavati, the Siddhanta Shiromani, the Ganita Sara Sangraha, and the Yuktibhasa, which preserved and transmitted the mathematical knowledge of India to other cultures  .

Indian mathematics was influenced by and influenced the mathematics of other civilizations, such as Greece, China, Persia, and Arabia. It also had a lasting impact on the development of mathematics in Europe, especially during the Renaissance and the Enlightenment periods  .
