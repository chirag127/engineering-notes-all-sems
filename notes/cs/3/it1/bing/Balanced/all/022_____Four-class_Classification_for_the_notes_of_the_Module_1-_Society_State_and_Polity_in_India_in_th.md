## Society, state and polity: definitions and dimensions

- Society: a group of people who share a common culture, identity, norms and values and interact with each other on the basis of mutual recognition and cooperation.
- State: a political organization that has a defined territory, a permanent population, a government and the capacity to maintain law and order and enter into relations with other states.
- Polity: a system of governance that regulates the relations between the state and the society and determines the distribution of power, rights and responsibilities among different actors and institutions.
- The dimensions of society, state and polity include:

  - Economic: the production, distribution and consumption of goods and services and the allocation of resources and wealth among different groups and classes.
  - Political: the exercise of authority, influence and control over the affairs of the state and the society and the participation and representation of different groups and interests in decision-making processes.
  - Social: the patterns of interaction, communication and association among different groups and individuals and the formation and maintenance of social identities, roles and statuses.
  - Cultural: the expression and transmission of beliefs, values, norms, symbols and practices that shape the worldview, identity and behavior of different groups and individuals.

## The evolution of Indian society, state and polity: ancient, medieval and modern periods

- The ancient period (c. 1500 BCE - c. 600 CE): characterized by the emergence and development of the Vedic civilization, the Mauryan and Gupta empires, the rise of Buddhism and Jainism, the formation of regional kingdoms and the spread of Indian culture and trade across Asia.
- The medieval period (c. 600 CE - c. 1750 CE): characterized by the invasion and rule of various foreign dynasties, such as the Kushans, the Huns, the Arabs, the Turks, the Mongols and the Mughals, the emergence and growth of Hindu bhakti and Islamic Sufi movements, the consolidation and expansion of regional kingdoms and the establishment of the Delhi Sultanate and the Mughal Empire.
- The modern period (c. 1750 CE - present): characterized by the advent and impact of colonialism, nationalism, democracy, secularism, socialism and globalization, the rise and fall of the British Raj, the struggle and achievement of independence, the formation and development of the Indian Republic, the partition and integration of India, the challenges and achievements of nation-building, development and democracy.
