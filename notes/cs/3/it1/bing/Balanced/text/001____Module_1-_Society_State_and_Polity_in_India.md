## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

- Society is a group of people who share a common culture, language, religion, ethnicity, or history.
- State is a political entity that has a defined territory, a government, and sovereignty over its internal and external affairs.
- Polity is a form or system of government or political organization.
- India is a diverse and complex society with multiple religions, languages, castes, regions, and ethnic groups.
- India is also a democratic republic with a parliamentary system of government and a federal structure of power distribution.
- India's Constitution, adopted in 1950, is the supreme law of the land and guarantees fundamental rights and freedoms to all citizens.
- India's polity is influenced by various factors such as colonial legacy, nationalist movement, social movements, identity politics, regionalism, secularism, and globalization.
- Some of the major challenges faced by India's polity are poverty, inequality, corruption, communalism, terrorism, and environmental degradation.
- Some of the major achievements of India's polity are economic growth, social welfare, democratic participation, diversity management, and international relations.
