### The Vedas

- The Vedas are the oldest and most authoritative scriptures of Hinduism, the religion of India. They are composed in an ancient form of Sanskrit, the language of classical Indian civilization. The word Veda means "knowledge" or "vision" and refers to the divine wisdom revealed to the ancient sages or rishis .
- The Vedas consist of four collections of hymns, rituals, mantras, and philosophical treatises, each known as a Veda. They are: the Rigveda, the Yajurveda, the Samaveda, and the Atharvaveda . Each Veda has four sections: the Samhitas (the core texts), the Brahmanas (commentaries on the rituals), the Aranyakas (forest texts for ascetics), and the Upanishads (mystical and philosophical teachings) .
- The Vedas are considered to be of divine origin and eternal, as they are not attributed to any human author. They are believed to have been revealed to the rishis through a process of shruti (hearing) and smriti (remembering). The rishis then transmitted the Vedas orally to their disciples, who preserved them through memorization and recitation for generations  .
- The Vedas are the source of the dharma, the moral and religious law that governs the life of Hindus. They prescribe the duties, rites, and obligations of the four varnas (social classes) and the four ashramas (stages of life). They also contain the cosmogonic myths, the stories of gods and goddesses, the ethical principles, and the metaphysical doctrines of Hinduism  .
- The Vedas are also the foundation of the six schools of orthodox Hindu philosophy, which seek to interpret and systematize the teachings of the Vedas. They are: the Mimamsa (ritual exegesis), the Vedanta (end of the Vedas), the Nyaya (logic), the Vaisheshika (atomism), the Samkhya (enumeration), and the Yoga (discipline) .
- The Vedas are the most revered and influential texts in the history of Indian culture, literature, and spirituality. They have inspired countless poets, thinkers, saints, and reformers, and have shaped the identity and values of the Hindu civilization  .
