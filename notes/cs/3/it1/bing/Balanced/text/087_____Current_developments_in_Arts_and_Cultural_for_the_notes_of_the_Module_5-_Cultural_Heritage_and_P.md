### Current developments in Arts and Culture in India

- India has a rich and diverse cultural heritage that includes various forms of art, music, dance, theatre, literature, architecture, and festivals.
- India is also home to 37 world heritage sites, two thriving film industries, and many smaller ones, and a vibrant contemporary art scene.
- Some of the current developments in arts and culture in India are:

  - Three Indian cities, Thrissur and Nilambur in Kerala and Warangal in Telangana, have joined the UNESCO Global Network of Learning Cities, which aims to promote lifelong learning and social inclusion through culture and education.
  - India has nominated the Hoysala Temples of Karnataka, which are examples of the unique Hoysala style of architecture and sculpture, for inscription in the World Heritage List.
  - India has got back a 10th-century sandstone sculpture of a goat-headed Yogini, which was stolen and smuggled out of the country, from France. The Yogini is a rare iconographic representation of a female deity in Hinduism and Buddhism.
  - India has launched a new museum, Dharohar, which showcases the history and evolution of customs and GST in the country. The museum displays various artefacts, documents, uniforms, and stamps related to the taxation system in India.
  - India has witnessed a surge in contemporary art, which explores new trends, themes, mediums, and techniques in Indian paintings. Some of the prominent contemporary artists in India are Subodh Gupta, Bharti Kher, Jitish Kallat, Anish Kapoor, and Nalini Malani.
  - India has celebrated its 72nd Republic Day with a grand parade that showcased the cultural diversity and achievements of the country. The parade also featured various awards for the best tableaux, cultural performances, and marching contingents.
  - India has also been promoting its cultural heritage and performing arts through various initiatives, such as the Rashtriya Sanskriti Mahotsav, the Bharat Parv, the Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat, and the Incredible India campaigns. These initiatives aim to showcase the rich and varied cultural traditions of India and foster national integration and tourism.
