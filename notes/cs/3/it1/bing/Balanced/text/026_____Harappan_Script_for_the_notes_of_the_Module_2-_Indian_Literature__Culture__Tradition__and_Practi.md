### Harappan Script

- Harappan script is the name given to the symbols used by the Indus Valley Civilization, which flourished in the northwest of India and Pakistan from about 2500 to 1900 BCE.
- Harappan script is also known as Indus script, as it was discovered during the excavation of Harappan city.
- Harappan script consists of about 400 signs, which are mostly pictographic or ideographic, representing objects, animals, plants, or abstract concepts .
- Harappan script has not been deciphered yet, as there are no bilingual inscriptions or known underlying language to compare it with .
- Harappan script is found on various types of artifacts, such as seals, pottery, copper tablets, tools, and ivory .
- Harappan script is usually written from right to left, sometimes in a boustrophedon style (alternating direction of lines), and occasionally in a spiral or circular pattern .
- Harappan script is considered to be a logo-syllabic writing system, where some symbols express ideas or words while others represent sounds.
- Harappan script is the oldest known script of India and one of the earliest writing systems in the world .
- Harappan script is a source of mystery and fascination for scholars and enthusiasts, who have proposed various theories and interpretations of its meaning and origin.
