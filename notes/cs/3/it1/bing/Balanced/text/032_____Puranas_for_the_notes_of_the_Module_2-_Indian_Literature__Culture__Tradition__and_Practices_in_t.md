### Puranas

- Puranas are a vast genre of Indian literature that deal with various topics, especially legends and traditional lore .
- Puranas are considered as one of the most ancient sources of Hinduism and are regarded as the fifth Veda.
- Puranas are composed in Sanskrit verse and date from the 4th century BCE to about 1000 CE.
- Puranas are written in a narrative style, similar to the epics Mahabharata and Ramayana.
- Puranas are divided into six categories, based on the main deity they glorify: Vishnu, Shiva, Brahma, Shakti, Agni, and Surya.
- Puranas contain various stories, myths, legends, genealogies, cosmology, rituals, festivals, temples, geography, and history of India .
- Puranas are also known for their symbolic and allegorical interpretations of the events and characters they describe.
- Puranas are not considered as authoritative or infallible by the orthodox schools of Hinduism, but are revered as sacred and inspirational by the devotees .
- Puranas are a rich source of cultural and religious information for the study of Indian traditions, society, and history.
