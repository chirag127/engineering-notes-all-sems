### Dance

Dance is a form of art and expression that involves movement of the body, rhythm, and emotion. Dance is an important part of Indian culture and society, as it reflects the diversity, history, and values of the people. Some of the main points to note about dance in Indian culture are:

- Dance is one of the oldest forms of art and entertainment in India. It is believed that dance originated as a form of worship to the gods and goddesses, and later evolved into a medium of storytelling, communication, and celebration .
- Dance in India can be classified into two broad categories: classical and folk. Classical dance forms are based on ancient texts and traditions, and follow strict rules of technique, style, and expression. Folk dance forms are more regional and spontaneous, and reflect the customs, beliefs, and lifestyles of the people.
- Some of the most prominent classical dance forms in India are Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, and Odissi. Each of these dance forms has its own origin, history, and characteristics, and is performed with specific costumes, music, and gestures.
- Folk dance forms in India are more diverse and varied, and are often performed during festivals, weddings, harvests, and other occasions. Some of the popular folk dance forms in India are Bhangra, Garba, Dandiya, Ghoomar, Bihu, Chhau, and Lavani. These dance forms showcase the regional and cultural diversity of India.
- Dance in India is not only a form of art, but also a form of social and cultural expression. Dance reflects the values, emotions, and aspirations of the people, and also influences their identity, status, and relationships. Dance can also be a form of protest, resistance, and empowerment, especially for the marginalized and oppressed groups in society .
- Dance in India is a living and dynamic tradition that continues to evolve and adapt to the changing times and contexts. Dance in India is influenced by various factors, such as globalization, modernization, media, and politics, and also incorporates elements from other cultures and genres. Dance in India is also a source of inspiration and innovation for the contemporary and global dance scene.
