### Challenges faced by Women for the notes of the Module 1- Society State and Polity in India in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

- Women in India face various challenges that are rooted in socio-cultural, economic, and political factors. Some of the major challenges are:

  - Gender discrimination and inequality: Women are often denied equal opportunities and rights in education, employment, health care, and decision making. They face discrimination and violence at home, in the workplace, and in public spaces. They are also subjected to harmful practices such as child marriage, dowry, female foeticide, and domestic violence  .

  - Poverty and lack of resources: Women are more likely to be poor and have limited access to resources such as land, credit, and technology. They also bear a disproportionate burden of unpaid domestic and care work, which limits their time and mobility. They face challenges in accessing basic services such as water, sanitation, electricity, and transport   .

  - Health and reproductive issues: Women in India face various health and reproductive issues such as maternal mortality, malnutrition, anemia, HIV/AIDS, and sexually transmitted infections. They also face barriers in accessing quality health care and family planning services. They have low autonomy and agency over their own bodies and reproductive choices   .

  - Legal and political empowerment: Women in India have limited representation and participation in the legal and political spheres. They face challenges in accessing justice and legal aid, especially in cases of violence and harassment. They also face obstacles in exercising their voting rights and joining political parties and movements. They have low awareness and influence over policies and laws that affect their lives  .

- These challenges are interrelated and require a holistic and multi-sectoral approach to address them. Some of the possible solutions are:

  - Promoting gender equality and women's rights through education, awareness, and advocacy. This includes challenging gender stereotypes and norms, empowering women and girls with life skills and leadership, and engaging men and boys as allies and partners   .

  - Enhancing women's economic empowerment and livelihood opportunities through skill development, entrepreneurship, and access to finance and markets. This also includes ensuring decent work conditions, equal pay, and social protection for women workers   .

  - Improving women's health and well-being through preventive and curative health care, nutrition, and sanitation. This also includes ensuring reproductive rights and choices, and addressing gender-based violence and its consequences   .

  - Strengthening women's legal and political empowerment through legal literacy, access to justice, and participation in governance and policy making. This also includes increasing women's representation and voice in the judiciary, legislature, executive, and civil society  .
