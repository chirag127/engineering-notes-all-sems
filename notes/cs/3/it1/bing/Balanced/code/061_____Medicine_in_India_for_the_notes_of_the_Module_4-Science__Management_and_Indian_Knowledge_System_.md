### Medicine in India

- Medicine in India has a long and rich history, dating back to the ancient times. India has contributed to various fields of medicine, such as surgery, pharmacology, anatomy, physiology, and psychology.
- Medicine in India can be broadly classified into two categories: modern evidence-based medicine and traditional systems of medicine.
- Modern evidence-based medicine in India is based on the scientific principles and methods of diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases. The standard entry-to-practice degree in modern evidence-based medicine in India is the Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS).
- Traditional systems of medicine in India are based on the holistic and natural approaches to health and wellness. They include Ayurveda, Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy (collectively referred to as AYUSH). These systems have their own theories, principles, and practices of diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases .
- Ayurveda is one of the oldest and most widely practiced systems of medicine in India. It is based on the concept of balance among the five elements (earth, water, fire, air, and ether) and the three doshas (vata, pitta, and kapha) that govern the body, mind, and spirit. Ayurveda uses various methods, such as diet, herbs, massage, meditation, and yoga, to restore and maintain health.
- Yoga is a system of physical, mental, and spiritual practices that originated in India. It aims to achieve harmony and union between the individual and the universal consciousness. Yoga involves various postures, breathing exercises, meditation, and ethical principles to enhance physical, mental, and spiritual well-being.
- Naturopathy is a system of medicine that emphasizes the healing power of nature and the self-healing ability of the body. It uses natural methods, such as diet, exercise, hydrotherapy, massage, and counseling, to prevent and treat diseases.
- Unani is a system of medicine that originated in ancient Greece and was developed by Arab and Persian physicians. It is based on the concept of four humors (blood, phlegm, yellow bile, and black bile) and four temperaments (sanguine, phlegmatic, choleric, and melancholic) that determine the health and personality of a person. Unani uses various methods, such as diet, herbs, cupping, leeching, and surgery, to balance the humors and restore health.
- Siddha is a system of medicine that originated in ancient Tamil Nadu. It is based on the concept of 96 basic principles (tattvas) that constitute the human body and the universe. Siddha uses various methods, such as diet, herbs, metals, minerals, and alchemy, to purify and rejuvenate the body and soul.
- Homeopathy is a system of medicine that was founded by a German physician, Samuel Hahnemann, in the 18th century. It is based on the principle of "like cures like", which means that a substance that causes symptoms in a healthy person can cure those symptoms in a sick person. Homeopathy uses highly diluted substances, called remedies, to stimulate the body's own healing response.
