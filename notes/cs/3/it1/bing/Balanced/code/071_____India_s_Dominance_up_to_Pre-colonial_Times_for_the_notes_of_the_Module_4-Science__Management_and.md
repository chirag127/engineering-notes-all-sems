### India’s Dominance up to Pre-colonial Times

- India has a long and rich history that dates back to 2500 BC, when the Indus Valley civilization emerged as one of the earliest urban cultures in the world.
- India witnessed the rise and fall of many empires and dynasties that contributed to its cultural, religious, political, and economic diversity and development .
- Some of the notable empires and dynasties that ruled India before the colonial period include the Mauryan Empire (321-185 BC), the Gupta Empire (320-550 AD), the Chola Empire (9th-13th century AD), the Delhi Sultanate (1206-1526 AD), and the Mughal Empire (1526-1857 AD)  .
- India was a self-sufficient and flourishing economy before the advent of colonial rule, with a large share of the world trade and a high level of agricultural and industrial production.
- India was also known for its achievements in science, mathematics, astronomy, medicine, literature, art, architecture, and music, which influenced many other civilizations and cultures  .
- India had a rich and diverse social and religious fabric, with various faiths and sects coexisting and interacting, such as Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Islam, Sikhism, Christianity, and Zoroastrianism  .
- India had a complex and dynamic political system, with various forms of governance and administration, such as monarchy, republic, federation, and confederation, and a well-developed system of law and justice  .
- India had an extensive and integrated network of trade and communication, both within and outside the subcontinent, which facilitated the exchange of goods, ideas, and people  .
- India had a strong and resilient sense of identity and culture, which enabled it to withstand and adapt to various invasions, migrations, and influences from other regions and countries  .
