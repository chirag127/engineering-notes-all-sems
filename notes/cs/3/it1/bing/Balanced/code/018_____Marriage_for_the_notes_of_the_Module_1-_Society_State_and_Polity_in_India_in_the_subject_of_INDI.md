### Marriage

- Marriage is a social institution that regulates the sexual and reproductive behavior of individuals in a society.
- Marriage also establishes rights and obligations between the spouses and their kin, such as inheritance, property, residence, and child custody.
- Marriage can be classified into different types based on the criteria of number of partners, choice of partners, and rules of descent and alliance.
- Some of the common types of marriage are:

  - Monogamy: A marriage between one man and one woman.
  - Polygamy: A marriage involving more than one spouse at the same time.
    - Polygyny: A marriage in which a man has more than one wife.
    - Polyandry: A marriage in which a woman has more than one husband.
  - Endogamy: A marriage within a specific group, such as caste, clan, or tribe.
  - Exogamy: A marriage outside a specific group, such as caste, clan, or tribe.
  - Hypergamy: A marriage in which a person marries someone of a higher status or rank.
  - Hypogamy: A marriage in which a person marries someone of a lower status or rank.
  - Anuloma: A marriage in which a man of a higher caste marries a woman of a lower caste.
  - Pratiloma: A marriage in which a woman of a higher caste marries a man of a lower caste.
  - Levirate: A marriage in which a widow marries her deceased husband's brother.
  - Sororate: A marriage in which a widower marries his deceased wife's sister.
  - Cross-cousin marriage: A marriage between the children of siblings of the opposite sex, such as a man marrying his mother's brother's daughter.
  - Parallel-cousin marriage: A marriage between the children of siblings of the same sex, such as a man marrying his father's brother's daughter.
