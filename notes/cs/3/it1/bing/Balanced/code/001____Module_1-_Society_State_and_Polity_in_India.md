## Module 1- Society State and Polity in India

- This module aims to introduce the concepts of society, state and polity and their interrelations in the context of India.
- Society is a group of people who share a common culture, language, religion, ethnicity, history, values and norms of behavior.
- State is a political entity that has a defined territory, a government, a population and the capacity to enter into relations with other states.
- Polity is a form or system of government that regulates the political affairs of a state and its society.
- India is a diverse and complex society that has been shaped by various historical, cultural, social and political factors.
- India's state and polity have evolved through different phases of colonialism, nationalism, independence, democracy, federalism, secularism and globalization.
- India's state and polity face various challenges and opportunities in the 21st century, such as regionalism, communalism, casteism, corruption, development, human rights, environment, security and foreign policy.

Some of the topics covered in this module are:

- The concept and meaning of society, state and polity and their interrelations.
- The historical and cultural background of Indian society and its diversity and unity.
- The colonial and nationalist influences on the formation of Indian state and polity.
- The constitutional and institutional framework of Indian democracy and federalism.
- The role and impact of social movements, civil society, media and judiciary on Indian state and polity.
- The major issues and debates in Indian state and polity, such as secularism, citizenship, reservation, governance, development, human rights, environment, security and foreign policy.
