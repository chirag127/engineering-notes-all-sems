The Council of Ministers is a key institution in the government of India, responsible for the administration of the country and the implementation of government policies. The Council of Ministers is headed by the Prime Minister and is comprised of senior ministers who are responsible for specific portfolios, such as finance, defense, home affairs, and foreign affairs.

The Council of Ministers is responsible for advising the President of India on the administration of the country and for implementing the policies of the government. The Prime Minister is the head of the Council of Ministers and is responsible for leading the government and ensuring that its policies are implemented effectively.

The Council of Ministers is accountable to the Parliament of India, which consists of the Lok Sabha (the lower house) and the Rajya Sabha (the upper house). The Parliament has the power to hold the government accountable for its actions and to pass laws that govern the country.

In the Indian political system, the Council of Ministers plays a crucial role in the formulation and implementation of government policies. The ministers are responsible for overseeing the administration of their respective departments and for ensuring that government policies are implemented effectively. They also play a key role in representing the government in Parliament and in engaging with stakeholders on important policy issues.
