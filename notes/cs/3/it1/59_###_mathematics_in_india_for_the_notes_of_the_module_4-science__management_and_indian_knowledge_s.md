### Mathematics in India for the notes of the Module 4-Science, Management and Indian Knowledge System in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

Mathematics in India has a rich and ancient history, dating back to the Vedic period. During this time, Indian scholars made significant contributions to the field of mathematics, including the development of the decimal system and the invention of the zero.

One of the most notable figures in the history of mathematics in India was Aryabhata, who lived in the 5th century CE. He made important contributions to the fields of astronomy and mathematics, including the development of trigonometry and the calculation of pi to four decimal places.

Another important figure in the history of mathematics in India was Bhaskara, who lived in the 12th century CE. He made significant contributions to the fields of algebra and calculus, and is considered one of the greatest mathematicians of the medieval period.

In addition to these individual contributions, mathematics in India was also influenced by various religious and philosophical traditions, such as Buddhism and Hinduism. These traditions emphasized the importance of knowledge and learning, and encouraged the study of mathematics as a means of understanding the natural world.

Despite these early achievements, mathematics in India declined during the colonial period, as the British focused their efforts on promoting Western science and mathematics. However, in recent years, there has been a resurgence of interest in the history of mathematics in India, and efforts are being made to preserve and promote this rich cultural heritage.

In conclusion, mathematics in India has a rich and ancient history, with notable contributions from scholars such as Aryabhata and Bhaskara. The study of mathematics was influenced by religious and philosophical traditions, and continues to play an important role in Indian culture and society today.
