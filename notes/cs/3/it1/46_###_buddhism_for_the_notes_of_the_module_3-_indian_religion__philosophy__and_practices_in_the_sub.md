### Buddhism for the notes of the Module 3- Indian Religion, Philosophy, and Practices in the subject of INDIAN TRADITIONS, CULTURAL AND SOCIETY

Buddhism is a major world religion and philosophical system that originated in ancient India around the 5th century BCE. It was founded by the Buddha, also known as Siddhartha Gautama, who was born into a wealthy family in what is now modern-day Nepal.

The central teachings of Buddhism revolve around the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path. The Four Noble Truths state that life is characterized by suffering, that suffering arises from craving and attachment, that it is possible to end suffering, and that the path to the end of suffering is the Eightfold Path. The Eightfold Path consists of right understanding, right intention, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right mindfulness, and right concentration.

Buddhism is a non-theistic religion, meaning that it does not worship a deity or gods, but instead focuses on the individual's path to enlightenment. The ultimate goal of Buddhism is to achieve Nirvana, a state of everlasting peace and happiness, by following the Eightfold Path and breaking the cycle of rebirth.

Buddhism has spread throughout the world and has taken on different forms in different countries and cultures. There are two main branches of Buddhism: Theravada and Mahayana. Theravada, also known as the "Way of the Elders," is the more traditional and conservative form of Buddhism, and is practiced primarily in Southeast Asia. Mahayana, also known as the "Great Vehicle," is a more liberal form of Buddhism that is practiced primarily in East Asia, and places a greater emphasis on compassion and the bodhisattva ideal.

In conclusion, Buddhism is a major world religion and philosophical system that offers a path to enlightenment and the end of suffering. Its teachings and practices have had a profound influence on the cultures and societies of the countries in which it is practiced, and it continues to be a source of inspiration and guidance for millions of people around the world.
