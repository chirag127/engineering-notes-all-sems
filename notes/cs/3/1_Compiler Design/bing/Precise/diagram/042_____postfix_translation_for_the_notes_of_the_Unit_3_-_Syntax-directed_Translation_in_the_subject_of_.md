### Postfix Translation

Postfix translation is a method of syntax-directed translation that is used to convert an infix expression into a postfix expression. This is done as part of the process of translating a high-level language program into machine code. Here are some key points to remember about postfix translation:

1. Postfix translation is also known as reverse Polish notation.
2. In postfix notation, the operator is placed after its operands.
3. Postfix notation eliminates the need for parentheses to specify the order of operations.
4. Postfix translation can be performed using a stack data structure.
5. The postfix expression is evaluated from left to right, with operands being pushed onto the stack and operators being applied to the top two operands on the stack.
6. Postfix translation is used in the syntax-directed translation phase of the compilation process.
