### BNF notation for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Compiler in the subject of Compiler Design

Unit 1 of the Compiler Design course focuses on Introduction to Compiler, including the aspect of Backus-Naur Form (BNF) notation.

BNF notation is a formal method for describing the syntax of a programming language or other formal language. It is used to specify the rules for constructing valid statements or expressions in a language. BNF notation consists of a set of production rules, where each rule defines a nonterminal symbol and the sequence of symbols that can replace it.

In the context of the Compiler Design course, students in Unit 1 will learn about BNF notation as part of the discussion on Introduction to Compiler. This may include working with examples of BNF notation, analyzing the use of BNF notation in compiler design, and learning how to use BNF notation to specify the syntax of a programming language.
