## Unit 2 - Basic Parsing Techniques

Basic parsing techniques are methods for analyzing and breaking down a string of text into smaller, meaningful components. The goal is to extract information from the text and convert it into a structured format that is easier to use.

Some common techniques include:

1. Tokenization: Breaking down the text into individual words or symbols.

2. Part-of-speech tagging: Assigning a label to each word indicating its grammatical role.

3. Named entity recognition: Identifying specific entities such as people, organizations, and locations.

4. Dependency parsing: Analyzing the relationships between words in a sentence to determine their grammatical structure.

5. Constituency parsing: Breaking down a sentence into its constituent parts, such as noun phrases and verb phrases.

These techniques are used in a variety of applications, including natural language processing, information retrieval, and text classification. They are fundamental building blocks for more advanced techniques, such as sentiment analysis and machine translation.
