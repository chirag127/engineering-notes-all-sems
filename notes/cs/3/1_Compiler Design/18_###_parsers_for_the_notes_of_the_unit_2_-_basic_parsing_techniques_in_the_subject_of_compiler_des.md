### Parsers for the notes of the Unit 2 - Basic Parsing Techniques in the subject of Compiler Design

Unit 2 of the Compiler Design course focuses on Basic Parsing Techniques, including the aspect of Parsers.

A parser is a component of a compiler that is responsible for analyzing the structure of the program and constructing a parse tree. The parse tree represents the structure of the program and includes information about the relationships between the various tokens in the program.

Parsers use a variety of techniques to analyze the structure of the program, including top-down parsing, bottom-up parsing, and recursive descent parsing. Each of these techniques has its own advantages and disadvantages, and the choice of technique depends on the specific requirements of the compiler and the programming language being compiled.

In the context of the Compiler Design course, students in Unit 2 will learn about parsers as part of the discussion on Basic Parsing Techniques. This may include working with examples of parsers, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different parsing techniques, and learning how to design and implement parsers in a compiler.
