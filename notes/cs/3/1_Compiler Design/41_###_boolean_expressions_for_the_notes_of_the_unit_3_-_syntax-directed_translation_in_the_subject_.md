### Boolean expressions for the notes of the Unit 3 - Syntax-directed Translation in the subject of Compiler Design

Unit 3 of the Compiler Design course focuses on Syntax-directed Translation, including the aspect of Boolean expressions.

Boolean expressions are expressions that evaluate to either true or false, and are used in programming languages to make decisions and control the flow of a program. Boolean expressions are typically constructed using logical operators, such as AND, OR, NOT, and others.

In the context of the Compiler Design course, students in Unit 3 will learn about Boolean expressions as part of the discussion on Syntax-directed Translation. This may include working with examples of Boolean expressions, analyzing the syntax and semantics of Boolean expressions, and learning how to translate Boolean expressions into code.
