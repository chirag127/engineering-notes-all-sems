### Planning process for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Designing

Unit 1 of the Web Designing course focuses on Introduction, including the aspect of Planning process.

The planning process is a critical step in the web design process, as it involves defining the goals and objectives of the website, determining the target audience, and creating a roadmap for the design and development of the website.

The planning process typically includes the following steps:
1. Define the goals and objectives of the website, such as increasing sales, providing information, or building a community.
2. Determine the target audience, including their needs, interests, and behaviors.
3. Conduct a competitive analysis to understand the strengths and weaknesses of similar websites.
4. Define the content and functionality of the website, including the pages, features, and navigation.
5. Create a site map, which is a visual representation of the structure and organization of the website.
6. Develop a project timeline, which outlines the schedule for the design and development of the website.

In the context of the Web Designing course, students in Unit 1 will learn about the planning process as part of the discussion on Introduction. This may include working with examples of the planning process, analyzing the benefits and limitations of the planning process, and learning how to implement the planning process in a web design project.
