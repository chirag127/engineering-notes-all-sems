### Creating Emails in Cpanel for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. Log in to the Cpanel account of the web hosting.
2. Go to Email Accounts section.
3. Click on Add New Account button to create a new email account.
4. Enter the Email Address, Password, and Alias (optional) for the new email account.
5. Select the appropriate Email Plan and set the Quota limit if required.
6. Click Create Account button to create the new email account.
7. The new email account will be created and will be displayed in the list of email accounts.
8. Similarly, more email accounts can be added as per the requirement.
9. The email accounts can be managed or deleted from the list of accounts.
10. The email accounts can be accessed using any email client or webmail by using the email address and password.
