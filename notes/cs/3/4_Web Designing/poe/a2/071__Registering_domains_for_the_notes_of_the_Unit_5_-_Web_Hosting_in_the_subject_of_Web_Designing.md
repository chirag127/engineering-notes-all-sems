### Registering domains for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing.

1.  Choose a domain name: Select a unique domain name that is relevant to your website or business. It should be short, easy to remember and spell, and not already taken. You can check domain name availability on registrar websites or third-party tools.
2.  Choose a domain name registrar: You need to register your domain name through a domain name registrar. Compare registrars based on pricing, features, and customer support to choose one. Some popular registrars are GoDaddy, Namecheap, Bluehost, etc.
3.  Purchase your domain: Once you select a registrar, you can purchase your domain on their website. You will need to pay a small annual fee to register and renew the domain each year. The exact pricing will depend on the domain extension you choose (.com, .net, .org, etc.).
4.  Complete the registration: After purchasing, you will need to enter some information like your contact details and then accept the registrar's terms of service to complete the registration. This will make your domain officially yours for the registration period you choose.
5.  Set up DNS: For your domain to be accessible through the web, you need to set up DNS by creating DNS records that point your domain to your web hosting service's servers. The details for this will be provided by your web hosting service.
