### Basics of SEO for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. Choose relevant page titles and descriptions: Page titles and descriptions are the first things search engines see about your website. Choosing titles and descriptions that accurately reflect the content of your pages and include relevant keywords helps search engines understand what your pages are about.

2. Optimize page content: The actual content of your pages is very important for SEO. Make sure your pages' content delivers value to users and includes relevant keywords naturally woven into sentences and headings. Unique, well-written content gives search engines more value to offer users.

3. Build internal links: Linking to other useful content on your own website helps search engines understand the overall structure and organization of your content. This, in turn, helps them determine how to rank your pages in search results. Think about how users might navigate your content and include links to relevant related content.

4. Earn external links: Getting other websites to link to yours is like earning votes of confidence from other sites on the web. The more high-quality external websites that link to yours, the more authoritative search engines will view your content. Focus on creating content other websites will find valuable and want to reference or link to.

5. Ensure a fast website load time: Website speed is a ranking factor for search engines. Slow load times can hurt your SEO, so be sure to optimize images, limit server requests, and take other steps to speed up your site. A fast, responsive website leads to a better user experience, which search engines want to reward.

6. Optimize for mobile-friendliness: An increasing percentage of web traffic comes from mobile devices, so having a mobile-friendly website is crucial for SEO. Websites that are not mobile-optimized may be penalized in search rankings. Using a responsive design and having pages that display and function well on mobile are important.
