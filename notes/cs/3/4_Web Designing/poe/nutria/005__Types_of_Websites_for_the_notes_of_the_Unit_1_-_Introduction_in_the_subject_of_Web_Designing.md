
### Types of Websites

1. Static websites: Static websites are webpages that are written in HTML and CSS and do not change. They are typically used to display information such as contact information, product descriptions, and other static content.

2. Dynamic websites: Dynamic websites are webpages that are written in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and can change depending on user input. They are typically used for interactive applications such as e-commerce stores, online forums, and other interactive webpages.

3. Responsive websites: Responsive websites are webpages that are designed to adapt to different screen sizes and devices. They use a combination of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to ensure that the webpage looks good on all devices.

4. Single-page websites: Single-page websites are webpages that are designed to be viewed on a single page. They are typically used for portfolios, one-page applications, and other simple websites.
