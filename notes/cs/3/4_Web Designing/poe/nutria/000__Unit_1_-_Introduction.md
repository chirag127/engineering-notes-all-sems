
## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. Introduction is the first step towards understanding a concept. It provides the basic information that one needs to know before delving into the details. 
2. An introduction typically includes a brief explanation of the concept and its purpose, followed by a brief overview of the main points. 
3. It is important to keep the introduction concise and to the point, so that readers can quickly get the gist of the material. 
4. The introduction should also provide a brief outline of the topics that will be discussed in the following sections. 
5. Additionally, it should provide a clear indication of what readers can expect to gain from the material.
