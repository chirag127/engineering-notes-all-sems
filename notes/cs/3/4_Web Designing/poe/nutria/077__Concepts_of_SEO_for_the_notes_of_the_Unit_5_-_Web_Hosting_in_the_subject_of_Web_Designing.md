
### Concepts of SEO for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization, which is the practice of optimizing a website to increase its visibility on search engine results pages (SERPs).

2. SEO involves a variety of techniques, including keyword research, content optimization, link building, and more.

3. Keyword research is the process of finding and analyzing keywords related to a website's topic. This helps to identify the best keywords to target for SEO purposes.

4. Content optimization is the process of optimizing website content for search engines. This includes optimizing titles, meta descriptions, headings, and other elements of the page.

5. Link building is the process of acquiring external links from other websites. This helps to increase the website's authority and relevance in the eyes of search engines.

6. Technical SEO is the process of optimizing the technical aspects of a website, such as page speed, mobile-friendliness, and structured data.

7. Local SEO is the process of optimizing a website for local search results. This includes optimizing titles, meta descriptions, and other elements of the page.

8. Analyzing and understanding search engine algorithms is an important part of SEO. This helps to identify the best practices to use for increasing visibility in SERPs.
