
### Creating Emails in Cpanel for the Notes of Unit 5 - Web Hosting

1. Log in to the cPanel of your web hosting account.
2. Under the **Email** section, click on **Email Accounts**.
3. Enter the desired email address, password, and mailbox quota.
4. Click **Create Account** to create the email account.
5. To access the email account, click on **Check Email**.
6. To access webmail, click on **Webmail**.
7. To access the email settings, click on **Email Routing**.
8. To manage the email account, click on **Manage/Add/Remove Accounts**.
9. To access the email filters, click on **Email Filters**.
10. To access the autoresponders, click on **Autoresponders**.
