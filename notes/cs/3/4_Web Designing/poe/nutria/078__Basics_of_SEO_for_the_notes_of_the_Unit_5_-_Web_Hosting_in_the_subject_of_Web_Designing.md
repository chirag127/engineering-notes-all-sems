
### Basics of SEO for the Notes of Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the Subject of Web Designing

1. SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It is a method of optimizing web content to make it more visible and accessible to search engines.

2. The goal of SEO is to increase organic (non-paid) traffic to a website by improving its visibility in search engine results.

3. SEO involves optimizing content for keywords and phrases, as well as optimizing the structure of a website to make it easier for search engines to crawl and index its content.

4. SEO also involves creating a website that is user-friendly and engaging, as well as optimizing the website's performance and speed.

5. SEO can be divided into two main categories: on-page SEO and off-page SEO. On-page SEO involves optimizing the content and structure of a website, while off-page SEO involves optimizing the website's presence in search engine results.

6. SEO also involves link building, which involves building relationships with other websites and creating backlinks to a website. This helps to increase the website's visibility in search engine results. 

7. SEO also involves monitoring and analyzing the performance of a website in search engine results. This helps to identify areas for improvement and to ensure that the website is optimized for the most relevant keywords and phrases.
