### Creating Emails in Cpanel

In this section, we will cover the steps to create email accounts using cPanel. Follow these steps to create an email account:

1. Login to your cPanel account.

2. Click on the "Email Accounts" option under the "Email" section.

3. Enter the email address you want to create in the "Email" field.

4. Select the domain name from the drop-down menu.

5. Enter a strong password for your email account.

6. Set the mailbox quota (optional).

7. Click on the "Create Account" button to create your email account.

8. You can access your email account by clicking on the "Access Webmail" option next to your email account.

9. You can also configure your email account in an email client such as Microsoft Outlook or Mozilla Thunderbird by using the email settings provided in cPanel.

10. To delete an email account, click on the "Delete" button next to the email account you want to delete.

11. You can also manage your email accounts by using cPanel's email management features, including email filters, email forwarding, and autoresponders.

By following these simple steps, you can easily create and manage your email accounts using cPanel. It is important to keep your email accounts secure by using strong passwords and regularly changing them. Additionally, you should always keep your email client and antivirus software up-to-date to prevent any security breaches.
