### Creating Emails in Cpanel for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

Unit 5 of the Web Designing course focuses on the topic of Web Hosting, including the aspect of Creating Emails in cPanel.

cPanel is a popular web hosting control panel that provides a graphical user interface for managing various aspects of a web hosting account, including email accounts.

To create an email account in cPanel, you must first log in to your cPanel account. Once logged in, you can access the Email Accounts section, where you can create new email accounts, manage existing email accounts, and configure email settings.

To create a new email account, you will need to provide a username and password for the email account, and specify the email account's quota (the amount of storage space allocated to the email account). You can also configure advanced settings, such as email forwarding, autoresponders, and email filters.

Once you have created an email account in cPanel, you can access your email using a webmail client, such as Horde, SquirrelMail, or Roundcube, or you can configure an email client, such as Microsoft Outlook or Apple Mail, to access your email using the appropriate email settings.
