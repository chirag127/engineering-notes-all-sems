### Types of Websites for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Designing

Websites can be classified into several types based on their purpose and functionality. Some common types of websites include:

1. Personal websites: Personal websites are created by individuals to showcase their personal information, portfolio, or blog.

2. Business websites: Business websites are created by companies to promote their products or services, provide information about the company, and communicate with customers.

3. E-commerce websites: E-commerce websites are created to sell products or services online. They typically include a shopping cart, payment gateway, and order management system.

4. Informational websites: Informational websites are created to provide information on a particular subject or topic. They can be created by individuals, organizations, or government agencies.

5. News websites: News websites are created to provide up-to-date news and information on current events.

6. Social media websites: Social media websites are created to allow users to connect and share information with others. Examples include Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram.

7. Blog websites: Blog websites are created to allow individuals or organizations to share their thoughts and opinions on a particular subject or topic.

In this unit, you will learn about the different types of websites and their features, as well as the basics of web design, including web design principles, layout, and typography. You will also learn about the various tools and technologies used in web design, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of web design, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of web design.
