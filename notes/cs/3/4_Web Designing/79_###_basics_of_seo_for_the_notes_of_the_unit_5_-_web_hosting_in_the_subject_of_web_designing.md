### Basics of SEO for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

The Basics of SEO section of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of SEO (search engine optimization, the process of optimizing a website for search engines)
2. Overview of SEO (what it is, why it is important, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Key elements of SEO (keywords, meta tags, content, links, etc.)
4. Overview of keywords (the words and phrases used to describe the content of a website)
5. Overview of meta tags (HTML tags used to provide information about a website to search engines)
6. Overview of content (the text and media used to describe the content of a website)
7. Overview of links (the connections between pages on a website, and between websites)
8. On-page optimization (optimizing the content and structure of a website)
9. Off-page optimization (optimizing external factors, such as links, that impact the visibility of a website)
10. Local SEO (optimizing a website for local search results)
11. Technical SEO (optimizing the technical aspects of a website, such as site speed and mobile responsiveness)
12. SEO and user experience (balancing optimization and user experience)
13. Limitations of SEO (algorithm changes, competition, etc.)
14. Techniques for overcoming limitations (staying up-to-date with best practices, monitoring results, etc.)
15. Further reading and resources (books, tutorials, online resources)

Basics of SEO is an important component of the subject of Web Designing. Understanding the principles and techniques of SEO helps students to develop the skills required to make websites more visible and accessible to search engines. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
