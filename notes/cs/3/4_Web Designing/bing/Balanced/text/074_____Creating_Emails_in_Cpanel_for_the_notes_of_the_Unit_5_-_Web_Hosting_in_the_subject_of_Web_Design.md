### Creating Emails in Cpanel

- Cpanel is a web hosting control panel that allows users to manage their websites, domains, email accounts, databases, and other aspects of their web hosting account.
- One of the features of Cpanel is the ability to create and manage email accounts for your domain name, such as info@yourdomain.com or sales@yourdomain.com.
- To create an email account in Cpanel, you need to follow these steps    :

  1. Log in to your Cpanel account using your username and password.
  2. In the Email section, click on the Email Accounts icon or link.
  3. Click on the Create or + Create button on the right-hand side of the screen.
  4. Enter a new email address in the Username text box, such as info or sales. You can also select the domain name from the drop-down menu if you have more than one domain in your account.
  5. In the Password section, enter a secure password in the Password text box, or use the Generate button to create a random password. You can also see the password strength indicator to check how strong your password is.
  6. Optionally, you can set a storage quota for your email account, which limits the amount of disk space it can use. You can enter a specific value in megabytes, or select Unlimited to allow unlimited disk space.
  7. Click on the Create button to create your email account. You will see a confirmation message and a list of your email accounts.

- To access your email account, you can use the webmail interface provided by Cpanel, or use an email client such as Outlook, Thunderbird, or Gmail. You will need to configure your email client with the correct settings, such as your email address, password, incoming and outgoing server names, and port numbers. You can find these settings in the Email Accounts section of Cpanel, under the Connect Devices button. You can also use the Auto-Configure or Manual Settings options to download the configuration files or instructions for your email client.
