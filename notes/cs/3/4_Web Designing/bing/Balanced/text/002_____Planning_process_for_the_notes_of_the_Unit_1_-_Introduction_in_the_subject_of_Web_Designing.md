### Planning process for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Designing

- The planning process for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Designing involves the following steps:

  - Define the purpose and scope of the notes. What are the main objectives and learning outcomes of the unit? What are the key concepts and topics that the notes should cover? How much detail and depth is required for the notes?
  - Identify the target audience and their needs. Who are the intended readers of the notes? What are their prior knowledge and skills in web designing? What are their expectations and preferences for the notes? How will they use the notes for learning and revision?
  - Research and gather relevant information and resources. What are the best sources of information and examples for the unit? How can the notes be supported by evidence, facts, and data? What are the current trends and developments in web designing that the notes should reflect?
  - Organize and structure the information and resources. How can the notes be divided into logical sections and subsections? What are the main headings and subheadings for the notes? How can the notes be arranged in a clear and coherent sequence? How can the notes be formatted and styled for readability and accessibility?
