# Creating Emails in Cpanel

- Cpanel is a web hosting control panel that allows you to manage various aspects of your website, such as domains, files, databases, and email accounts.
- To create an email account in Cpanel, you need to have access to your Cpanel dashboard and a domain name that is hosted on your Cpanel account.
- The steps to create an email account in Cpanel are as follows:

  1. Log in to your Cpanel dashboard using your username and password.
  2. In the Email section, click on the Email Accounts icon or link.
  3. Click on the Create button on the right-hand side of the screen.
  4. Enter a new email address in the Username text box. For example, if your domain name is example.com, you can enter info@example.com as your email address.
  5. In the Password section, enter a secure password in the Password text box. You can also use the Generate button to create a random password. Make sure to note down or copy your password for future use.
  6. In the Storage Space section, you can specify how much disk space you want to allocate for your email account. You can either enter a value in megabytes or use the slider to adjust the space. You can also select Unlimited if you don't want to limit the storage space for your email account.
  7. Click on the Create button at the bottom of the page to create your email account.

- After creating your email account, you can access it using webmail or an email client, such as Outlook, Thunderbird, or Gmail. To access your email account using webmail, you can follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Cpanel dashboard using your username and password.
  2. In the Email section, click on the Email Accounts icon or link.
  3. Find the email account that you want to access and click on the Check Email button next to it.
  4. You will see a list of webmail applications that you can use to access your email account, such as Horde, Roundcube, or SquirrelMail. Choose the one that you prefer and click on the Open button.
  5. You will be redirected to the webmail interface, where you can read, compose, and manage your emails.

- To access your email account using an email client, you need to configure the email client with the correct settings for your email account. You can find the settings for your email account by following these steps:

  1. Log in to your Cpanel dashboard using your username and password.
  2. In the Email section, click on the Email Accounts icon or link.
  3. Find the email account that you want to access and click on the Connect Devices button next to it.
  4. You will see a list of options for connecting your email account to different devices and applications, such as Mail Client Automatic Configuration, Mail Client Manual Settings, or Mobile Devices. Choose the one that suits your needs and follow the instructions on the screen.
  5. You will need to enter the following information in your email client:

    - Your email address and password
    - Your incoming and outgoing mail server names
    - Your incoming and outgoing mail server ports
    - Your incoming and outgoing mail server security protocols
    - Your incoming and outgoing mail server authentication methods

  6. After entering the settings, you can test your email account by sending and receiving emails.
