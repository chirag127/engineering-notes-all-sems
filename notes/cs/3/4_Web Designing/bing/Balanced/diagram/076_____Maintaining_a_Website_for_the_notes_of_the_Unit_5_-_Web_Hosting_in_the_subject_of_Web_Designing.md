### Maintaining a Website

- Website maintenance is the practice of monitoring your website’s overall health and performance.
- Website maintenance includes tasks like ensuring all the links on your site are working, regularly updating content, and fixing any broken links.
- Website maintenance is important for several reasons, such as:
  - The majority of customers conduct online research before making a purchase, so your website needs to be up-to-date and functional.
  - Your website informs users’ first impressions, so you want to make sure it is attractive, user-friendly, and secure.
  - Your website helps grow your sales, so you want to optimize it for conversions, SEO, and speed.
  - Site visitors expect your website to be fast, reliable, and secure, so you want to avoid any downtime, errors, or breaches that may harm your reputation and trust.
- Website maintenance can be done on a regular basis, such as daily, weekly, monthly, or quarterly, depending on the size and complexity of your website.
- Website maintenance can be done by yourself, by hiring a professional, or by using a website maintenance service.
- Website maintenance involves the following steps:
  - Check for outdated plug-ins and themes and update them to the latest versions
  - Run security scans and install any patches or updates to prevent malware or hacking
  - Update your content and images to keep them fresh and relevant
  - Check for broken links and fix them or remove them
  - Fix any minor bugs or errors that may affect the functionality or appearance of your website
  - Monitor your performance and analytics and make any improvements or adjustments
  - Backup your website and database regularly and store them in a safe location
  - Test your website on different browsers and devices and ensure it is responsive and compatible
  - Review your SEO strategy and keywords and optimize your website for search engines
  - Check your domain name and hosting plan and renew them before they expire
  - Review your website goals and objectives and measure your progress and results
  - Solicit feedback from your users and customers and implement any changes or suggestions
