### Concepts of SEO for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

- SEO stands for **Search Engine Optimization**, which is the process of improving the **visibility** and **relevance** of a website or a web page in the **organic** (unpaid) results of a search engine, such as Google or Bing.
- SEO involves taking steps to **optimize** the content, structure, design, and technical aspects of a website or a web page, so that it can **match** the **intent** and **expectations** of the users who are searching for related keywords or topics.
- SEO is important because it can help a website or a web page **attract** more **quality** and **quantity** of **traffic**, which can lead to more **conversions**, **sales**, **leads**, or **engagement**.
- SEO is a **dynamic** and **ongoing** process that requires constant **monitoring**, **analysis**, and **adjustment** of the website or the web page, as well as the **research** and **understanding** of the **market**, **competition**, and **search engine algorithms**.
- SEO can be divided into three main categories: **on-page SEO**, **off-page SEO**, and **technical SEO**.
  - **On-page SEO** refers to the optimization of the **content** and **elements** that are visible and controllable on the website or the web page, such as the **title**, **headings**, **meta tags**, **URL**, **images**, **text**, **keywords**, **internal links**, etc.
  - **Off-page SEO** refers to the optimization of the **factors** and **activities** that are external and not directly controllable by the website or the web page owner, such as the **backlinks**, **social media**, **reviews**, **mentions**, **authority**, **reputation**, etc.
  - **Technical SEO** refers to the optimization of the **infrastructure** and **performance** of the website or the web page, such as the **speed**, **security**, **crawling**, **indexing**, **mobile-friendliness**, **structured data**, etc.
- SEO is influenced by various **concepts** and **principles** that are constantly evolving and changing, such as the following:
  - **Core Web Vitals**: A set of **metrics** that measure the **user experience** and **satisfaction** of a website or a web page, based on the **loading**, **interactivity**, and **visual stability** of the page.
  - **Mobile-First Indexing**: A **method** that prioritizes the **mobile version** of a website or a web page for **ranking** and **displaying** in the search engine results, as most of the users are using **mobile devices** to access the web.
  - **Machine Learning and Automation**: A **technology** that enables the search engines to **learn** from the **data** and **behavior** of the users and the websites, and to **automate** some of the **tasks** and **decisions** involved in the SEO process, such as the **keyword selection**, **content generation**, **ranking factors**, etc.
  - **E-A-T**: An **acronym** that stands for **Expertise**, **Authoritativeness**, and **Trustworthiness**, which are the **qualities** that the search engines evaluate and expect from the **content creators** and the **websites** that provide the information to the users.
  - **Knowledge Gap, Semantics, and Entities**: A **concept** that refers to the **difference** between the **information** that the users are looking for and the **information** that the websites are providing, and how the search engines use the **meaning**, **context**, and **relationships** of the **words**, **phrases**, and **entities** to **bridge** that gap and to **understand** the **intent** and the **relevance** of the queries and the results.
