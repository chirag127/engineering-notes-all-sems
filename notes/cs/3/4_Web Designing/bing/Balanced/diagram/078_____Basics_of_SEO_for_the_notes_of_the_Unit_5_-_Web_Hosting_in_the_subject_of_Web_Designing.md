# Basics of SEO

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization, which is the process of improving the ranking and visibility of a website in search engines. The higher or more frequently a website is displayed in a search engine list (like Google), the more visitors it is expected to receive.

SEO involves five main steps:

- **Keyword research.** Find what people search for and what keywords are relevant to your website's content and goals.
- **Content creation.** Craft content that matches the search intent and provides value to the visitors. Use keywords strategically and naturally in your content.
- **On-page SEO.** Make your content as clear and user-friendly as possible. Use meta tags, headings, images, links, and other elements to optimize your content for search engines and users.
- **Link building.** Build trust and authority from other websites by getting links from relevant and reputable sources. Links signal to search engines that your website is trustworthy and valuable.
- **Technical SEO.** Help search engines find, crawl, and index your content efficiently. Fix any errors or issues that might affect your website's performance, speed, security, and usability.

By following these steps, you can improve your website's SEO and attract more organic traffic from search engines. SEO is an ongoing process that requires constant monitoring, analysis, and improvement. SEO is also influenced by factors such as competition, algorithm changes, user behavior, and trends. Therefore, it is important to stay updated and adapt to the changing SEO landscape.
