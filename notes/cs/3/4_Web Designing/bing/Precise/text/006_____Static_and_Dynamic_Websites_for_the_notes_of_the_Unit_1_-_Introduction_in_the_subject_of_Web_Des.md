### Static and Dynamic Websites

#### Unit 1 - Introduction

- A **static website** is a website that consists of fixed content and displays the same information to every user. The content of a static website is stored in HTML files and is not generated dynamically.

- A **dynamic website**, on the other hand, is a website that generates content dynamically and can display different content to different users. The content of a dynamic website is generated by server-side scripts, such as PHP or ASP.NET, and is stored in a database.

- Static websites are simpler to create and maintain, as they do not require any server-side scripting or database management. They are suitable for small websites with a limited amount of content that does not change frequently.

- Dynamic websites, on the other hand, are more complex to create and maintain, as they require server-side scripting and database management. They are suitable for larger websites with a large amount of content that changes frequently, such as e-commerce websites or social networking websites.

- Static websites are faster to load, as the content is already stored in HTML files and does not need to be generated dynamically. Dynamic websites, on the other hand, may be slower to load, as the content needs to be generated dynamically by server-side scripts.

- Static websites are less flexible, as the content is fixed and cannot be changed dynamically. Dynamic websites, on the other hand, are more flexible, as the content can be changed dynamically based on user input or other factors.

- In summary, static websites are suitable for small, simple websites with a limited amount of content that does not change frequently, while dynamic websites are suitable for larger, more complex websites with a large amount of content that changes frequently. The choice between a static and a dynamic website depends on the specific needs and requirements of the website.
