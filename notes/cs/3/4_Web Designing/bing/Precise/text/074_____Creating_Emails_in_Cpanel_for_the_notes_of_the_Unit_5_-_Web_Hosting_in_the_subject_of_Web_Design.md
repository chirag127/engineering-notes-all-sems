### Creating Emails in Cpanel for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. Log in to your cPanel account.
2. Navigate to the Email section and click on the Email Accounts icon.
3. Click on the Create button to create a new email account.
4. Enter the desired email address and password for the new account.
5. Select the desired storage space for the new account.
6. Click on the Create button to create the new email account.
7. The new email account will now be listed in the Email Accounts section.

Note: The steps may vary slightly depending on the version of cPanel being used. It is important to consult the documentation provided by your web hosting provider for specific instructions.
