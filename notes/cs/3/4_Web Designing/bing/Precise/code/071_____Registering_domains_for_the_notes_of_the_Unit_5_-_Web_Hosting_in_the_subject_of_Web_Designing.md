### Registering domains for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. A domain name is the address of your website that people type in the browser URL bar to visit your website.
2. In simple terms, if your website was a house, then your domain name will be its address.
3. The process of registering a domain name is called Domain Name Registration.
4. To register a domain name, you need to visit a domain registrar. A domain registrar is a company that manages the reservation of domain names.
5. Some popular domain registrars are GoDaddy, Namecheap, and Google Domains.
6. When registering a domain name, you need to choose a domain name that is available and pay a registration fee.
7. The registration fee varies depending on the domain registrar and the domain extension you choose.
8. Once you have registered a domain name, it is yours to use for the period of time you have paid for.
9. You can renew your domain registration before it expires to keep using it.
10. It is important to choose a domain name that is easy to remember, easy to spell, and represents your brand or website.
