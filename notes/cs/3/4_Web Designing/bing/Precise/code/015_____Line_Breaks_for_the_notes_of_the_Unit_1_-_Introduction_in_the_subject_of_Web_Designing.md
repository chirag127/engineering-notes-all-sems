### Line Breaks

- In HTML, line breaks are used to start a new line of text.
- The `<br>` tag is used to insert a line break.
- The `<br>` tag is an empty tag, meaning it does not require a closing tag.
- The `<br>` tag should be used sparingly, as excessive use can lead to poorly formatted text.
- To create a paragraph break, use the `<p>` tag instead of multiple `<br>` tags.
- In CSS, the `white-space` property can be used to control how white space and line breaks are handled within an element.
- The `white-space` property can take several values, including `normal`, `nowrap`, `pre`, `pre-wrap`, and `pre-line`.
- The `normal` value collapses white space and breaks lines as necessary.
- The `nowrap` value collapses white space but prevents line breaks.
- The `pre` value preserves white space and only breaks lines at newline characters.
- The `pre-wrap` value preserves white space and breaks lines as necessary.
- The `pre-line` value collapses white space but breaks lines at newline characters or as necessary.
