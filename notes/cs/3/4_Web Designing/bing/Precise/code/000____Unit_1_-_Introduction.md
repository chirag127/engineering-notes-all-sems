## Unit 1 - Introduction

1. The introduction is the first part of any written work, whether it is an essay, a research paper, or a book.
2. The purpose of the introduction is to provide the reader with an overview of the topic and to present the main ideas or arguments that will be discussed in the work.
3. The introduction should be clear, concise, and engaging, and should provide the reader with a reason to continue reading.
4. A good introduction should include a thesis statement, which is a sentence that summarizes the main argument or point of the work.
5. The introduction may also include background information, definitions of key terms, and a brief overview of the structure of the work.
6. It is important to note that the introduction is not a summary of the entire work, but rather a preview of what is to come.
7. The length of the introduction will vary depending on the length and complexity of the work, but it should generally be no more than 10% of the total length of the work.
8. In conclusion, the introduction is a crucial part of any written work, as it sets the tone for the rest of the work and provides the reader with a roadmap of what to expect.
