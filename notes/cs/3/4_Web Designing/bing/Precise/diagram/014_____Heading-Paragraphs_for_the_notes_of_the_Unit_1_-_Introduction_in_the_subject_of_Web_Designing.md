### Unit 1 - Introduction to Web Designing

#### Heading-Paragraphs

1. A heading is a brief descriptive title that summarizes the content of the section that follows it.
2. Headings are typically larger and more prominent than the text that follows them.
3. Headings are used to organize content and make it easier for users to scan and find the information they are looking for.
4. Headings should be clear, concise, and descriptive.
5. Paragraphs are blocks of text that are separated from other blocks of text by a blank line or indentation.
6. Paragraphs are used to organize content into smaller, more manageable chunks.
7. Each paragraph should contain a single idea or topic.
8. Paragraphs should be well-structured and easy to read, with clear transitions between ideas.
9. The use of headings and paragraphs is essential in creating well-structured, easy-to-read web content.
