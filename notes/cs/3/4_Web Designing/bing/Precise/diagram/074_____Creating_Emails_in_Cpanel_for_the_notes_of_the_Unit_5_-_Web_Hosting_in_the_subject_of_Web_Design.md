### Creating Emails in Cpanel

Cpanel is a popular web hosting control panel that allows users to manage various aspects of their website, including creating and managing email accounts. Here are the steps to create an email account in Cpanel:

1. Log in to your Cpanel account.
2. Navigate to the "Email" section and click on the "Email Accounts" icon.
3. Click on the "Create" button to create a new email account.
4. Enter the desired email address and password for the new account.
5. Select the desired storage space for the new email account.
6. Click on the "Create" button to create the new email account.

Once the email account is created, you can access it using webmail or by configuring an email client to connect to the email server using the provided settings. You can also manage the email account, such as changing the password or deleting the account, from the "Email Accounts" section in Cpanel.
