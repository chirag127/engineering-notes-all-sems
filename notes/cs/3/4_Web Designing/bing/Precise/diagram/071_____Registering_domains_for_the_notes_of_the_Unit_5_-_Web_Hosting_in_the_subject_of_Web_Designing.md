### Registering domains for the notes of the Unit 5 - Web Hosting in the subject of Web Designing

1. A domain name is the address of your website that people type in the browser URL bar to visit your website.
2. In simple terms, if your website was a house, then your domain name will be its address.
3. The process of buying a domain name is called Domain Name Registration.
4. To register a domain name, you will need to visit a domain name registrar.
5. Domain name registrars are companies that manage the reservation of internet domain names.
6. You will need to provide some personal information and make a payment to complete the domain name registration process.
7. Once you have successfully registered your domain name, you will have the exclusive rights to use it for the duration of your registration period.
8. You can renew your domain name registration before it expires to continue using it.
9. It is important to choose a domain name that is easy to remember, easy to spell, and represents your brand or business.
10. There are many domain name extensions to choose from, such as .com, .net, .org, .biz, .info, and more. Choose the one that best fits your needs.
