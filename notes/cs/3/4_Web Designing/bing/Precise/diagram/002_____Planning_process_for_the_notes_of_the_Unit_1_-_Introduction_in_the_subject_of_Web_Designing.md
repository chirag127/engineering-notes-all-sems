### Planning process for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction in the subject of Web Designing

1. Define the scope and objectives of the notes.
2. Identify the key concepts and topics to be covered in the notes.
3. Research and gather information on the key concepts and topics.
4. Organize the information into a logical and coherent structure.
5. Write the notes in a clear and concise manner, using appropriate language and terminology.
6. Use diagrams, illustrations, and examples to aid understanding and retention of the material.
7. Review and revise the notes to ensure accuracy and completeness.
8. Format the notes for ease of use and readability.
