### Web Hosting Basics

- Web hosting is an online service that makes your website’s content accessible on the internet.
- When you purchase a web hosting plan, you are renting space on a physical server to store all the website’s files and data.
- Web hosts provide the hosting technology and resources required for the effective and secure operation of your website.
- Web hosting is essential for any website, as it allows you to share your site with everyone else.
- There are different types of web hosting services, such as shared hosting, VPS hosting, dedicated hosting, cloud hosting, etc.
- Each type of web hosting has its own advantages and disadvantages, depending on your website’s needs and budget.
- To host a website, you need to follow these four steps:
  - Decide on your website type (static or dynamic)
  - Research hosting providers and compare their features and prices
  - Select a hosting plan that suits your website’s requirements
  - Get a domain name for your site and connect it to your hosting account
- A domain name is a unique address that identifies your website on the internet, such as www.example.com.
- You can register a domain name from a domain registrar or get one for free from some hosting providers.
- A domain name should be easy to remember, relevant to your website’s topic, and not too long or complicated.
