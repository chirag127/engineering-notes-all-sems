### Domains and Hosting

- A **domain** is the address or the name of a website that visitors can use to access it on the internet. For example, `www.example.com` is a domain name.
- A **hosting** is the service or the space where the website files and data are stored on a web server. For example, `Hostinger` is a web hosting provider that offers different hosting plans for different types of websites.
- A domain and a hosting are two different components of a website, but they work together to make websites possible. A domain is like a street address that points to a specific location, while a hosting is like a house that contains the contents of the website.
- To have a functioning website, you need both a domain and a hosting. You can buy a domain and a hosting from the same provider or from different providers, but you need to connect them by changing the domain name servers (DNS) settings.
- A domain and a hosting are usually paid services that require annual or monthly fees. The cost of a domain and a hosting depends on various factors, such as the domain extension, the hosting plan, the features, the performance, the security, and the support.
- A domain and a hosting are essential for web designing, as they allow you to create, publish, and manage your website online. You can choose a domain and a hosting that suit your website needs, goals, and budget.
