## Unit 1 - Introduction

- This unit provides an overview of the course, its objectives, and its scope.
- The course aims to teach the basic concepts and techniques of artificial intelligence (AI), a branch of computer science that studies how to create machines and software that can perform tasks that require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, planning, and decision making.
- The course covers the following topics:

  - The history and evolution of AI, its applications and challenges, and its ethical and social implications.
  - The methods and tools for representing and manipulating knowledge, such as logic, search, and inference.
  - The principles and algorithms for learning from data and experience, such as supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning.
  - The approaches and techniques for solving complex and uncertain problems, such as planning, optimization, and game playing.
  - The models and architectures for designing and implementing intelligent agents, such as reactive, deliberative, and hybrid agents.

- The course also introduces some of the current research areas and trends in AI, such as natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and artificial neural networks.
- The course requires some background in mathematics, such as discrete mathematics, probability, and linear algebra, and some programming skills, such as Python or Java.
- The course consists of lectures, readings, assignments, quizzes, and a final project. The course is divided into 10 units, each with a specific topic and learning outcomes. The course follows the textbook "Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig, 4th edition, 2020.
