### Types of Websites

A website is a collection of web pages that are linked together and hosted on a server. A website can have a specific purpose, such as providing information, entertainment, education, or commerce. Websites can be accessed through a web browser or a mobile app.

There are many different types of websites, depending on their content, functionality, and design. Some of the most common types of websites are:

- **E-commerce website**: An e-commerce website allows you to sell products or services from your online store. You can accept payments, manage inventory, and track orders. Examples of e-commerce websites are Amazon, eBay, and Shopify.
- **Business website**: A business website showcases your company, its products, services, values, and mission. It can also include contact information, testimonials, and a blog. Examples of business websites are Apple, Microsoft, and Starbucks.
- **Portfolio website**: A portfolio website showcases your work, skills, and achievements. It can be used by artists, designers, photographers, writers, or anyone who wants to display their creative work. Examples of portfolio websites are Behance, Dribbble, and Medium.
- **Blogging website**: A blogging website allows you to share your ideas, opinions, stories, or knowledge with your audience. You can also interact with your readers through comments, social media, and email. Examples of blogging websites are WordPress, Blogger, and Tumblr.
- **Media or news website**: A media or news website provides information, entertainment, or education to the public. It can include articles, videos, podcasts, or live streams. Examples of media or news websites are CNN, BBC, and YouTube.
- **Educational website**: An educational website offers courses, tutorials, or resources for learning. It can also include quizzes, assignments, or certificates. Examples of educational websites are Khan Academy, Coursera, and Udemy.
- **Social media website**: A social media website allows you to connect with other people, share content, and communicate. It can also include features such as groups, pages, stories, or live videos. Examples of social media websites are Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram.
- **Forum website**: A forum website allows you to create and join discussions on various topics. You can also ask questions, give answers, or share opinions. Examples of forum websites are Reddit, Quora, and Stack Overflow.
- **Wiki website**: A wiki website allows you to create and edit content collaboratively. It can also include references, links, and history. Examples of wiki websites are Wikipedia, Wiktionary, and Wikibooks.
- **Personal website**: A personal website allows you to share your personal information, such as your name, biography, hobbies, or resume. It can also include a blog, a portfolio, or a contact form. Examples of personal websites are Tim Berners-Lee, Elon Musk, and Oprah Winfrey.
