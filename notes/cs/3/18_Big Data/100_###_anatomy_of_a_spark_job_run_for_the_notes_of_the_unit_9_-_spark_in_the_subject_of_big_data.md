### anatomy of a Spark job run for the notes of the Unit 9 - Spark in the subject of Big Data

The anatomy of a Spark job run refers to the various components and stages involved in executing a Spark job in a Spark cluster. The following are some of the key concepts related to the anatomy of a Spark job run:

1. Spark Job: A Spark job refers to a unit of work that is executed in a Spark cluster, and consists of one or more stages of processing.

2. Spark Application: A Spark application refers to a complete Spark job, including all stages of processing, data inputs and outputs, and any necessary configuration information.

3. Spark Context: The Spark context is the central component of a Spark application, and is responsible for managing the execution of Spark jobs in the cluster.

4. RDD (Resilient Distributed Dataset): RDDs are the fundamental data structure used in Spark, and represent a collection of data that is partitioned and distributed across the nodes in a Spark cluster.

5. Spark Transformation: Spark transformations are operations that are performed on RDDs to produce new RDDs, and include operations such as map, filter, and reduce.

6. Spark Action: Spark actions are operations that are performed on RDDs to produce a final result, and include operations such as count, collect, and save.

7. Spark Task: A Spark task refers to a unit of work that is executed on a single node in a Spark cluster, and is the smallest unit of parallel processing in Spark.

8. Spark Stage: A Spark stage refers to a set of tasks that are executed together, and is a logical unit of processing in Spark.

In this unit, you will learn about the anatomy of a Spark job run, including Spark job, Spark application, Spark context, RDD, Spark transformation, Spark action, Spark task, and Spark stage.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of Spark, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of big data processing with Spark.
