### capped collections for the notes of the Unit 8 - MongoDB in the subject of Big Data

Unit 8 - MongoDB in the subject of Big Data will cover the concept of capped collections, a special type of collections in MongoDB that have a fixed size and automatically overwrite the oldest documents when the collection reaches its maximum size.

Capped collections are a special type of collections in MongoDB that have a fixed size and automatically overwrite the oldest documents when the collection reaches its maximum size. This makes capped collections useful for storing data that is constantly changing, such as log data or real-time data, where the most recent data is more important than the older data.
