### YARN for the notes of the Unit 6 - Hadoop Eco System and YARN in the subject of Big Data

YARN (Yet Another Resource Negotiator) is a resource management system used by Hadoop to allocate resources and manage jobs in a Hadoop cluster. The following are some of the key concepts related to YARN:

1. Resource Management: YARN is responsible for managing the resources in a Hadoop cluster, including the allocation of CPU, memory, and network bandwidth to the various jobs and tasks running in the cluster.

2. Job Scheduling: YARN is responsible for scheduling jobs and tasks in a Hadoop cluster, and determines the order in which jobs and tasks are executed based on factors such as job priority, resource availability, and data locality.

3. Cluster Utilization: YARN is responsible for optimizing the utilization of resources in a Hadoop cluster, and ensures that resources are used efficiently and effectively to maximize the processing power of the cluster.

4. Scalability: YARN enables the scalability of a Hadoop cluster by allowing the addition of new nodes and resources, and by enabling the dynamic allocation of resources to meet the changing demands of the cluster.

5. Flexibility: YARN provides a flexible and modular architecture that allows for the integration of a variety of processing frameworks and tools, including MapReduce, Spark, and others.

6. Cluster Management: YARN provides a centralized management system for a Hadoop cluster, and enables the administration and management of the cluster, including the monitoring of cluster performance, the management of data storage, and the management of cluster security.

In this unit, you will learn about YARN, including resource management, job scheduling, cluster utilization, scalability, flexibility, and cluster management.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of YARN, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of big data processing and management.
