### functions and closures for the notes of the Unit 10 - SCALA in the subject of Big Data

The Functions and Closures section of the Unit 10 - SCALA in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Functions (a block of code that performs a specific task)
2. Overview of Functions (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Definition of Closures (a function that can reference variables outside its scope)
4. Overview of Closures (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Functions in Scala (e.g. how to define, call, and pass functions in Scala)
6. Overview of Functions in Scala (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Closures in Scala (e.g. how to define and use closures in Scala)
8. Overview of Closures in Scala (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Higher-Order Functions (e.g. functions that take other functions as arguments or return functions as results)
10. Overview of Higher-Order Functions (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Functions and Closures in industry applications (how functions and closures are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Functions and Closures are important components of the subject of Big Data, especially in the context of SCALA. Understanding the principles and techniques of functions and closures helps students to develop the skills required to write efficient and effective code in Scala. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
