### built-in control structures for the notes of the Unit 10 - SCALA in the subject of Big Data

The Built-in Control Structures section of the Unit 10 - SCALA in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Built-in Control Structures (control structures that are built into the Scala language)
2. Overview of Built-in Control Structures (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Conditional Statements (e.g. if, if-else, match)
4. Overview of Conditional Statements (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Loops (e.g. for, while)
6. Overview of Loops (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Exception Handling (e.g. try-catch)
8. Overview of Exception Handling (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Built-in Control Structures in Scala (e.g. how they are used in Scala)
10. Overview of Built-in Control Structures in Scala (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Built-in Control Structures in industry applications (how built-in control structures are used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Built-in Control Structures is an important component of the subject of Big Data, especially in the context of SCALA. Understanding the principles and techniques of built-in control structures in Scala helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective Scala programs. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
