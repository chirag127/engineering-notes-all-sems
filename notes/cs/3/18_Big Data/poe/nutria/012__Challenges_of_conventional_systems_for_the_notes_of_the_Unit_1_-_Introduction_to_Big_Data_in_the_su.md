
### Challenges of Conventional Systems for the Notes of Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the Subject of Big Data

1. Conventional systems are not able to handle large amounts of data efficiently, leading to slower processing times.
2. Conventional systems are not able to store data in a distributed fashion, meaning data can be easily corrupted or lost.
3. Conventional systems are not able to support real-time analytics, making it difficult to make decisions based on up-to-date data.
4. Conventional systems are not able to scale up or down easily, meaning resources must be allocated in advance.
5. Conventional systems are not able to handle unstructured data, making it difficult to extract insights from such data.
6. Conventional systems are not able to provide fault tolerance, meaning a single failure can cause the entire system to fail.
7. Conventional systems are not able to provide high availability, meaning downtime can have a significant impact on operations.
