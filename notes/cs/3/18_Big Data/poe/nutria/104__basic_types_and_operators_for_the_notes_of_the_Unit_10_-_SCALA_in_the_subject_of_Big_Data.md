
### Basic Types and Operators for Unit 10 - SCALA in the Subject of Big Data

* Scala is a statically-typed language, meaning that every variable and expression has a type that is known at compile time. 
* Scala has a rich type system, including primitive types such as `Int`, `Double`, `Boolean`, and `String`. 
* Scala also has collections types, such as `List`, `Map`, and `Set`. 
* Operators are special symbols that perform specific operations on one or more operands. 
* In Scala, operators are methods that are invoked using infix notation.
* Arithmetic operators such as `+`, `-`, `*`, and `/` are used to perform basic mathematical operations.
* Comparison operators such as `==`, `!=`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, and `>=` are used to compare two values. 
* Logical operators such as `&&` and `||` are used to combine boolean expressions. 
* Assignment operators such as `=`, `+=`, `-=`, `*=`, and `/=` are used to assign values to variables. 
* Bitwise operators such as `&`, `|`, `^`, `~`, `<<`, and `>>` are used to manipulate individual bits in a value.
