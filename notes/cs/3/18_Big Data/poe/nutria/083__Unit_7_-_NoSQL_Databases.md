
## Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases

NoSQL databases are non-relational databases that can provide advantages in scalability, performance, and flexibility when compared to traditional relational databases. 

1. NoSQL databases are schema-less, meaning they can store data in any structure, and can easily accommodate changes in the data structure.

2. NoSQL databases are distributed, meaning they can be spread across multiple servers, allowing for easy scalability and high availability.

3. NoSQL databases are often used for large-scale data processing and analytics, as they can store and process large volumes of data more quickly and efficiently than traditional relational databases.

4. NoSQL databases are often used for applications that require high throughput, such as real-time applications, as they can handle large amounts of data quickly and efficiently.

5. NoSQL databases are often used for applications that require data to be stored in different formats, such as JSON or XML, as they can easily accommodate different data formats.

6. NoSQL databases are often used for applications that require data to be stored in different locations, as they can easily replicate data across multiple servers.

7. NoSQL databases are often used for applications that require data to be accessed from multiple sources, as they can easily integrate with other applications and services.
