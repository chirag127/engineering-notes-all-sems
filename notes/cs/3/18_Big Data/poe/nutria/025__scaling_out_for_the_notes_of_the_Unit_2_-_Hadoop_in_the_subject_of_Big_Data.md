
### Scaling Out for the Notes of the Unit 2 - Hadoop in the Subject of Big Data

1. Scaling out is the process of increasing the capacity of a system by adding additional nodes.
2. In the context of big data, scaling out is the process of adding more nodes to a Hadoop cluster to increase its capacity and performance.
3. Scaling out enables the system to process more data at the same time, making it more efficient and cost-effective.
4. Scaling out also allows for more flexibility, as the system can be easily adjusted to meet changing needs.
5. When scaling out, it is important to consider the hardware and software requirements for each node.
6. Additionally, the nodes must be properly configured and connected in order to ensure that the system is running efficiently.
7. Finally, it is important to monitor the system performance to ensure that the system is running as expected.
