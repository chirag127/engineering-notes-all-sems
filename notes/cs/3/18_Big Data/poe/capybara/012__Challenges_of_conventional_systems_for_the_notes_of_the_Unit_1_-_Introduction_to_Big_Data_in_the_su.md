### Challenges of conventional systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data

Big Data is a vast field that deals with large amounts of unstructured data. With the vast amount of data, it is important to have systems that can handle and process this data efficiently. Conventional systems, however, have certain limitations when it comes to processing Big Data. Here are some of the challenges of conventional systems for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data:

1. **Limited processing power:** Conventional systems are not designed to handle large amounts of data. They have limited processing power and can take a lot of time to process data. This can be a big challenge when it comes to processing Big Data, which can take hours or even days to process.

2. **Limited storage capacity:** Conventional systems have limited storage capacity, which can be a big challenge when it comes to storing Big Data. Big Data requires vast amounts of storage space, and conventional systems may not be able to handle it.

3. **Limited scalability:** Conventional systems are not designed to scale up or down depending on the amount of data being processed. This can be a big challenge when it comes to processing Big Data, which can vary in size depending on the task being performed.

4. **Lack of real-time processing:** Conventional systems are not designed to perform real-time processing, which is a big challenge when it comes to processing Big Data. Real-time processing is important in Big Data because it allows for data to be processed and analyzed in real-time.

5. **Difficulty in handling unstructured data:** Conventional systems are designed to handle structured data, which is data that is organized in a specific format. Big Data, on the other hand, is often unstructured data, which can be a big challenge for conventional systems.

In conclusion, conventional systems have certain limitations when it comes to processing Big Data. These limitations can be a big challenge for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data. To overcome these challenges, new systems and technologies have been developed that are specifically designed to handle Big Data.
