### Fair and Capacity for the Notes of the Unit 6 - Hadoop Eco System and YARN in the Subject of Big Data

In this unit, we will be discussing the Hadoop Eco System and YARN. Here are some important points to keep in mind:

1. Hadoop Eco System is an open-source framework used for storing and processing large data sets. It consists of various tools and components such as HDFS, MapReduce, Hive, Pig, and HBase.

2. YARN (Yet Another Resource Negotiator) is a component of Hadoop Eco System responsible for resource management and job scheduling. It enables multiple data processing engines such as MapReduce, Spark, and Flink to run on the same cluster.

3. Fair scheduling is a technique used by YARN to allocate resources to different jobs based on their priority and resource requirements. It ensures that resources are allocated fairly among all the jobs running on the cluster.

4. Capacity scheduling is another technique used by YARN to allocate resources based on predefined quotas. It allows users to reserve a certain amount of resources for their jobs, ensuring that they have access to the required resources when needed.

5. YARN also provides a web-based interface called the YARN Resource Manager, which allows users to monitor and manage the jobs running on the cluster.

6. Hadoop Eco System and YARN are widely used in the Big Data industry for processing large data sets efficiently. Understanding these concepts is crucial for anyone working with Big Data.
