### 5 Vs of Big Data

**Volume:** Refers to the huge amount of data generated every day. The size of data is exponentially increasing day by day.

**Velocity:** Refers to the speed at which the data is generated and processed. The data generation speed is very high in today's world.

**Variety:** Refers to the different types and formats of data. The data today comes in all formats like structured, unstructured, text, audio, video, etc.

**Veracity:** Refers to the uncertainty or trustworthiness of the data. The data may contain noise, biases or abnormalities. The veracity of the data needs to be checked before analysis.

**Value:** Refers to the value that can be extracted from the data. The ultimate goal is to extract useful information and insights from the raw data that can provide value to the business.

The 5 Vs of Big Data help in understanding the properties of Big Data and the challenges involved in processing the data. Proper analysis of massive volumes of data with high velocity and varied formats can derive value for businesses.
