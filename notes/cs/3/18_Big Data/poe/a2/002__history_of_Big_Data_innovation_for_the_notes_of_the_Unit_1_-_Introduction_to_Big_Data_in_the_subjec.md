### History of Big Data Innovation

1. 1980s - The term "Big Data" was first used to describe datasets that were too large to handle using traditional databases and software.
2. 1990s - Companies like Walmart start to collect data from their supply chain, customers, etc. and use the data for data mining and gaining business insights.
3. 2000s - Advances in hardware, networks, and software enabled storage and analysis of larger datasets. Hadoop is introduced in 2008, enabling distributed processing of large data sets across clusters of computers.
4. 2010s - Continued exponential growth in data volumes, data sources, and data types and improvements in machine learning algorithms and cloud computing resources enable advanced analytics and insights. The Internet of Things and sensors generate large streams of data.
5. Today - "Big Data" refers to techniques and technologies that enable the management and analysis of these large and complex datasets to discover patterns, make predictions, and drive decisions. Machine learning and AI play a significant role in Big Data analysis and applications.
