### scaling out for the notes of the Unit 2 - Hadoop in the subject of Big Data

The Scaling Out section of the Unit 2 - Hadoop in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Scaling Out (the process of adding more resources to a system to increase its capacity and performance)
2. Overview of Scaling Out (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Scaling Out in Hadoop (e.g. how Hadoop can be scaled out to handle large amounts of data)
4. Overview of Scaling Out in Hadoop (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Scaling Out with Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) (e.g. how HDFS can be scaled out to handle large amounts of data)
6. Overview of Scaling Out with HDFS (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Scaling Out with Hadoop MapReduce (e.g. how MapReduce can be scaled out to handle large amounts of data)
8. Overview of Scaling Out with MapReduce (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Scaling Out and Performance (e.g. optimizing scaling out for performance)
10. Overview of Scaling Out and Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Scaling Out in industry applications (how scaling out is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Scaling Out is an important component of the subject of Big Data, especially in the context of Hadoop. Understanding the principles and techniques of scaling out helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement scalable big data solutions. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
