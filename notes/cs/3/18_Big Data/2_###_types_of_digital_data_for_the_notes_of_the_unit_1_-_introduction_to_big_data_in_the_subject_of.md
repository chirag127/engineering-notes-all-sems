### Types of digital data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data

Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data will cover the concept of Types of Digital Data, a classification of data based on its structure and format.

Digital data can be classified into several types, including structured data, semi-structured data, and unstructured data. Structured data is data that is organized into a well-defined format, such as a database or spreadsheet. Semi-structured data is data that has some structure, but is not as well-defined as structured data. Unstructured data is data that does not have a well-defined structure, such as text, audio, or video.

In the context of Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data, students will learn about the different types of digital data, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data. This may include working with examples of each type of data, analyzing the syntax and structure of each type of data, and learning how to use each type of data in a big data context.

In Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data, students will also learn about the benefits and limitations of each type of digital data, and will learn about alternative approaches to classifying digital data, such as using data models and data ontologies.
