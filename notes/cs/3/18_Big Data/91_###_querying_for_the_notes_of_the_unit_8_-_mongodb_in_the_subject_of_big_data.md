### querying for the notes of the Unit 8 - MongoDB in the subject of Big Data

The Querying section of the Unit 8 - MongoDB in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Querying (the process of retrieving data from a database)
2. Overview of Querying (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. MongoDB Query Language (e.g. the syntax and structure of MongoDB queries)
4. Overview of MongoDB Query Language (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Querying Documents (e.g. retrieving data from individual documents in a MongoDB collection)
6. Overview of Querying Documents (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Querying Collections (e.g. retrieving data from multiple documents in a MongoDB collection)
8. Overview of Querying Collections (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Querying with Projections (e.g. specifying the fields to include or exclude in query results)
10. Overview of Querying with Projections (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Querying with Conditions (e.g. filtering query results based on specific conditions)
12. Overview of Querying with Conditions (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Querying with Aggregation (e.g. grouping and summarizing data in query results)
14. Overview of Querying with Aggregation (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
15. Querying in industry applications (how querying is used in various industries)
16. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Querying is an important component of the subject of Big Data, especially in the context of MongoDB. Understanding the principles and techniques of querying helps students to develop the skills required to retrieve data from MongoDB databases. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
