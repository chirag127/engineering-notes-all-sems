### fair and capacity for the notes of the Unit 6 - Hadoop Eco System and YARN in the subject of Big Data

Fair and capacity scheduling are important concepts in the Hadoop ecosystem, particularly in the context of YARN (Yet Another Resource Negotiator), the resource management system used by Hadoop. The following are some of the key concepts related to fair and capacity scheduling in YARN:

1. Fair Scheduling: Fair scheduling is a scheduling algorithm used in YARN to allocate resources to jobs in a fair and equitable manner, taking into account the resource requirements of each job and the available resources in the cluster.

2. Capacity Scheduling: Capacity scheduling is a scheduling algorithm used in YARN to allocate resources to jobs based on the capacity of the cluster, ensuring that resources are used efficiently and effectively to support the processing of big data sets.

3. Resource Allocation: Resource allocation in YARN determines how resources, such as CPU, memory, and network bandwidth, are allocated to jobs in the cluster, and is critical to the performance and scalability of the Hadoop ecosystem.

4. Resource Utilization: Resource utilization in YARN refers to the use of resources in the cluster, and is critical to the performance and scalability of the Hadoop ecosystem, as it determines the processing power and capacity of the cluster.

5. Resource Management: Resource management in YARN involves the allocation, utilization, and management of resources in the cluster, and is critical to the performance and scalability of the Hadoop ecosystem.

6. Scheduler Plugins: Scheduler plugins are extensions to the YARN scheduler that allow for the customization of the scheduling algorithms used in YARN, and can be used to implement fair and capacity scheduling algorithms to meet the specific needs of a big data application.

In this unit, you will learn about fair and capacity scheduling in the Hadoop ecosystem, including fair scheduling, capacity scheduling, resource allocation, resource utilization, resource management, and scheduler plugins.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of fair and capacity scheduling in YARN, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of big data processing and management.
