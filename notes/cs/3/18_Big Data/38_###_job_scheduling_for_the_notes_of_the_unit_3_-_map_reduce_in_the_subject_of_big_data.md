### job scheduling for the notes of the Unit 3 - Map Reduce in the subject of Big Data

The Job Scheduling section of the Unit 3 - MapReduce in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Job Scheduling (the process of scheduling and executing tasks in a computing environment)
2. Overview of Job Scheduling (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Job Scheduling in MapReduce (e.g. scheduling and executing MapReduce tasks in a Hadoop cluster)
4. Overview of Job Scheduling in MapReduce (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Job Scheduling Algorithms (e.g. FIFO, Fair Scheduling, Capacity Scheduling, etc.)
6. Overview of Job Scheduling Algorithms (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Job Scheduling and Performance (e.g. optimizing job scheduling for performance)
8. Overview of Job Scheduling and Performance (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Job Scheduling in industry applications (how job scheduling is used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Job Scheduling is an important component of the subject of Big Data, especially in the context of MapReduce. Understanding the principles and techniques of job scheduling helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement efficient and effective big data solutions using MapReduce. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
