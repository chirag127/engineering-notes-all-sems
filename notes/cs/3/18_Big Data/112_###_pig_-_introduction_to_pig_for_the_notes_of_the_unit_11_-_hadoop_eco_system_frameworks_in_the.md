### Pig - Introduction to PIG for the notes of the Unit 11 - Hadoop Eco System Frameworks in the subject of Big Data

The Introduction to Pig section of the Unit 11 - Hadoop Eco System Frameworks in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Pig (a high-level platform for creating MapReduce programs used with Apache Hadoop)
2. Overview of Pig (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Pig Architecture (e.g. how Pig works and its components)
4. Overview of Pig Architecture (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Pig Latin (e.g. the scripting language used in Pig for creating MapReduce programs)
6. Overview of Pig Latin (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Pig Data Types (e.g. the different data types supported by Pig)
8. Overview of Pig Data Types (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Pig Operators (e.g. the operations used in Pig Latin for processing data)
10. Overview of Pig Operators (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. Pig UDFs (User-Defined Functions) (e.g. functions written in a programming language such as Java and used in Pig)
12. Overview of Pig UDFs (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
13. Pig in industry applications (how Pig is used in various industries)
14. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Pig is an important component of the subject of Big Data, especially in the context of Hadoop Eco System Frameworks. Understanding the principles and techniques of Pig helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective big data processing solutions using Pig. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
