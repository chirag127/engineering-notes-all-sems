## Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data

- Big data is a term that refers to the large, complex, and diverse datasets that are generated from various sources and applications, such as social media, e-commerce, sensors, and IoT devices.
- Big data has four main characteristics: volume, velocity, variety, and veracity. Volume refers to the amount of data, velocity refers to the speed of data generation and processing, variety refers to the types and formats of data, and veracity refers to the quality and reliability of data.
- Big data poses several challenges and opportunities for data management and analysis, such as storage, processing, integration, querying, visualization, and mining.
- Big data analytics is the process of applying advanced techniques and tools to extract useful insights and knowledge from big data, such as patterns, trends, correlations, and anomalies.
- Big data analytics can be classified into three types: descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive. Descriptive analytics summarizes what has happened in the past, predictive analytics forecasts what will happen in the future, and prescriptive analytics recommends what actions to take to optimize outcomes.
- Big data analytics can be applied to various domains and applications, such as business, health, education, security, and science, to improve decision making, enhance performance, and create value.
