### Intelligent Data Analysis for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data

- Intelligent Data Analysis (IDA) is an interdisciplinary study that is concerned with the extraction of useful knowledge from data, drawing techniques from a variety of fields, such as artificial intelligence, high-performance computing, pattern recognition, and statistics .
- IDA aims to discover hidden patterns, trends, associations, and anomalies in data, and to use them for decision making, prediction, classification, and explanation .
- IDA can be applied to different types of data, such as structured, unstructured, semi-structured, temporal, spatial, textual, multimedia, and streaming data .
- IDA can also handle different challenges of data, such as volume, velocity, variety, veracity, and value, which are collectively known as the 5Vs of big data .
- IDA generally consists of three stages: data preparation, data mining, and result validation and explanation .
  - Data preparation involves the processes of data collection, integration, cleaning, transformation, and reduction, to make the data suitable for analysis .
  - Data mining involves the application of various algorithms and techniques, such as clustering, classification, regression, association rule mining, anomaly detection, and text mining, to extract meaningful patterns and insights from the data .
  - Result validation and explanation involves the evaluation of the quality and usefulness of the discovered patterns and insights, and the presentation of them in an understandable and actionable way, using methods such as visualization, interpretation, and reporting .
- IDA can benefit from various artificial intelligence techniques, such as machine learning, neural networks, fuzzy logic, inductive logic programming, support vector machines, and kernel methods, to enhance the performance and accuracy of data analysis .
- IDA can also benefit from various data visualization techniques, such as charts, graphs, maps, dashboards, and interactive displays, to facilitate the exploration and communication of data and results .
- IDA is a rapidly evolving and expanding field, with many applications and opportunities in various domains, such as business, science, engineering, health, education, and social sciences .
