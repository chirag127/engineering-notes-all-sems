### Modern Data Analytic Tools

- Data analytic tools are software applications that enable users to collect, process, analyze, and visualize data from various sources.
- Modern data analytic tools are designed to handle large and complex datasets, often referred to as big data, and to provide insights and solutions for various business problems and opportunities.
- Some of the modern data analytic tools that are widely used in 2023 are:

  - **Python**: Python is a general-purpose programming language that supports multiple paradigms, such as object-oriented, functional, and procedural. Python has a rich set of libraries and frameworks for data analysis, such as NumPy, pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Keras. Python is also easy to learn, write, and read, making it a popular choice for data analysts and data scientists  .
  - **R**: R is a programming language and environment for statistical computing and graphics. R has a comprehensive collection of packages and functions for data manipulation, visualization, modeling, and machine learning, such as dplyr, ggplot2, tidyr, caret, and xgboost. R is also widely used for academic and research purposes, as well as for creating interactive dashboards and reports  .
  - **SAS**: SAS is a software suite for advanced analytics, business intelligence, data management, and predictive analytics. SAS offers a variety of tools and solutions for different domains and industries, such as SAS Enterprise Guide, SAS Enterprise Miner, SAS Visual Analytics, and SAS Viya. SAS is known for its reliability, scalability, and security, as well as its support and training services .
  - **Excel**: Excel is a spreadsheet application that is part of the Microsoft Office suite. Excel allows users to perform calculations, organize data, create charts and graphs, and apply formulas and functions. Excel is also integrated with other Microsoft tools, such as Power Query, Data Model, Power Pivot, and Power BI, which enable users to perform more advanced data analysis and visualization tasks .
  - **Power BI**: Power BI is a business analytics service that provides interactive data visualization and reporting capabilities. Power BI allows users to connect to various data sources, transform and model data, create dashboards and reports, and share and collaborate with others. Power BI also supports artificial intelligence and natural language processing features, such as Q&A and Quick Insights  .
  - **Tableau**: Tableau is a data visualization and analytics platform that helps users to explore and understand data. Tableau allows users to connect to various data sources, create and customize charts and graphs, apply filters and calculations, and tell stories with data. Tableau also offers a range of products and solutions, such as Tableau Desktop, Tableau Server, Tableau Online, and Tableau Prep  .
  - **Apache Spark**: Apache Spark is an open-source distributed computing framework that enables fast and scalable data processing and analysis. Spark supports multiple programming languages, such as Scala, Python, Java, and R, and multiple libraries and modules, such as Spark SQL, Spark Streaming, Spark MLlib, and Spark GraphX. Spark is also compatible with various data sources and platforms, such as Hadoop, Hive, Kafka, and AWS  .

- These are some of the modern data analytic tools that can help users to leverage the power and potential of big data in 2023. However, there are many other tools and technologies that are also available and evolving in the field of data and analytics. Therefore, users should always keep learning and updating their skills and knowledge to stay ahead of the curve.
