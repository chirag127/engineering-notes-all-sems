### Drivers for Big Data

Big data is the term used to describe the large and complex datasets that are generated from various sources and require advanced techniques and technologies to store, process, and analyze. Big data has become a valuable asset for many organizations and industries that want to gain insights, improve decision making, and create new products and services. However, big data also poses many challenges and opportunities that require understanding the drivers and motivations behind its adoption and use.

According to the Enterprise Big Data Framework , there are six main business drivers for big data:

- **The digitization of society**: The widespread use of digital devices, such as smartphones, tablets, laptops, and sensors, has increased the amount and variety of data that is generated and collected every day. This data includes text, images, videos, audio, geolocation, and biometric data, among others. The digitization of society also enables new forms of communication, collaboration, and interaction, such as social media, e-commerce, and online gaming, that generate more data and feedback.
- **The plummeting of technology costs**: The rapid advancement and innovation of technology has reduced the costs and increased the performance of hardware and software that are used to store, process, and analyze big data. For example, the cost of storage has decreased from $0.24 per gigabyte in 2005 to $0.02 per gigabyte in 2020. The cost of processing has also decreased with the emergence of cloud computing, which allows users to access and use computing resources on demand and pay only for what they use.
- **Connectivity through cloud computing**: Cloud computing is a model of delivering computing services over the internet, such as servers, storage, databases, networks, software, and analytics. Cloud computing enables users to access and use big data from anywhere and anytime, without having to invest in and maintain their own infrastructure. Cloud computing also provides scalability, elasticity, and reliability, which are essential for handling big data workloads and applications.
- **Increased knowledge about data science**: Data science is the interdisciplinary field that combines mathematics, statistics, computer science, and domain knowledge to extract meaningful insights from big data. Data science has become a popular and in-demand skill in the market, as more organizations and industries recognize the value and potential of big data. Data science also enables new methods and techniques for data collection, preparation, analysis, and visualization, such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and data mining.
- **Social media applications**: Social media is a platform that allows users to create, share, and consume content and information online, such as blogs, microblogs, social networks, forums, wikis, and podcasts. Social media generates a large amount of data that reflects the opinions, preferences, behaviors, and sentiments of users, as well as their interactions and relationships with others. Social media data can be used for various purposes, such as marketing, customer service, reputation management, and trend analysis.
- **The upcoming Internet-of-Things (IoT)**: IoT is a network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and connectivity that enable them to collect and exchange data with other devices and systems. IoT can be applied to various domains, such as smart homes, smart cities, smart health, smart agriculture, and smart manufacturing, that can benefit from the data generated by the IoT devices. IoT data can be used to monitor, control, and optimize the performance and efficiency of the physical objects and processes, as well as to create new services and business models.

These drivers for big data illustrate the importance and relevance of big data in the modern society and economy, as well as the challenges and opportunities that it presents. Understanding these drivers can help organizations and industries to adopt and use big data effectively and strategically, and to gain competitive advantage and value from it.
