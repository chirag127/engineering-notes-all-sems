# Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

- Big data is the term used to describe large and complex datasets that are difficult to process, store, analyze, and visualize using traditional tools and methods.
- Big data has many applications and benefits in various domains, such as business, science, health, education, and government.
- However, big data also poses many challenges and risks, especially in terms of security, compliance, auditing, and protection.
- Security refers to the measures and mechanisms that ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of big data and its related systems and processes.
- Compliance refers to the adherence to the laws, regulations, standards, and policies that govern the collection, processing, storage, and use of big data and its related systems and processes.
- Auditing refers to the process of examining and verifying the records, activities, and performance of big data and its related systems and processes, to ensure their accuracy, validity, and reliability.
- Protection refers to the safeguards and backups that prevent or minimize the loss, damage, or corruption of big data and its related systems and processes, due to natural or human-caused disasters or accidents.

Some of the features and best practices of security, compliance, auditing, and protection for big data are:

- Encrypting big data at rest and in transit, using strong and standard algorithms and keys, to prevent unauthorized access or modification.
- Implementing granular and role-based access control policies and mechanisms, to limit and monitor who can access or modify big data and its related systems and processes, and for what purposes.
- Applying data masking, anonymization, or pseudonymization techniques, to protect the privacy and identity of the individuals or entities whose data is collected or processed, especially for sensitive or personal data.
- Performing regular and comprehensive security audits and assessments, to identify and address any vulnerabilities, threats, or breaches in big data and its related systems and processes, and to ensure compliance with the applicable laws, regulations, standards, and policies.
- Storing audit information separately and securely, and enabling detailed and timely logging and reporting, to collect and analyze the information about the activities and performance of big data and its related systems and processes, and to provide evidence and accountability for any incidents or issues.
- Using big data analytics and machine learning techniques, to enhance the security, compliance, and auditing capabilities, by detecting and preventing any anomalies, frauds, or attacks, and by providing insights and recommendations for improvement.
- Creating and maintaining backups and replicas of big data and its related systems and processes, in different locations and formats, to ensure the availability and recoverability of big data in case of any failure, outage, or disaster.
