### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

- Security: The process of protecting big data from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. Security involves implementing various measures such as encryption, authentication, authorization, firewall, etc. to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of big data. Security also involves monitoring and detecting any potential threats or breaches and responding accordingly. Security is essential for ensuring the trustworthiness and reliability of big data and preventing any legal, ethical, or reputational risks.
- Compliance: The process of adhering to the rules, regulations, standards, or policies that govern the collection, processing, storage, or usage of big data. Compliance involves ensuring that big data is collected and used in a lawful, ethical, and transparent manner, respecting the rights and privacy of the data subjects and stakeholders. Compliance also involves reporting and documenting any incidents or issues related to big data and resolving them in a timely and appropriate manner. Compliance is essential for avoiding any penalties, fines, or lawsuits that may arise from violating any applicable laws or norms.
- Auditing: The process of verifying and validating the quality, accuracy, completeness, and consistency of big data. Auditing involves conducting regular checks and reviews of the big data sources, systems, processes, and outputs to ensure that they meet the expected standards and requirements. Auditing also involves identifying and correcting any errors, anomalies, or discrepancies that may affect the performance or outcomes of big data analytics. Auditing is essential for ensuring the credibility and usefulness of big data and enhancing the confidence and satisfaction of the data users and consumers.
- Protection: The process of preserving and safeguarding big data from any loss, damage, or corruption. Protection involves implementing various measures such as backup, recovery, replication, redundancy, etc. to ensure the availability and durability of big data. Protection also involves ensuring that big data is disposed of or archived in a secure and proper manner when it is no longer needed or relevant. Protection is essential for ensuring the continuity and sustainability of big data and preventing any negative impacts or consequences that may result from losing or compromising big data.
