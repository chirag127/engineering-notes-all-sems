### Drivers for Big Data

Big data is the term used to describe the large and complex datasets that are generated from various sources and require advanced techniques and technologies to store, process, and analyze. Big data has become a key asset for many organizations and industries that want to gain insights, improve decision making, and create value from their data. 

There are several factors that have contributed to the emergence and growth of big data in the recent years. These factors are also known as the drivers for big data, as they create the demand and the opportunity for big data analytics. Some of the main drivers for big data are:

- **The digitization of society**: As more and more aspects of human life and activity are captured and recorded by digital devices and platforms, the amount and variety of data that is generated increases exponentially. Examples of such data sources include online transactions, social media posts, web browsing, mobile applications, sensors, cameras, GPS, etc. According to some estimates, the global data volume is expected to reach 175 zettabytes by 2025, up from 33 zettabytes in 2018  .

- **The drop in technology costs**: The advances in hardware and software technologies have made it possible and affordable to store, process, and analyze big data. The cost of data storage has decreased significantly over the years, from $437,000 per gigabyte in 1980 to $0.02 per gigabyte in 2020 . The emergence of cloud computing has also enabled the scalability, elasticity, and accessibility of big data solutions, as well as reduced the upfront investment and maintenance costs. Moreover, the development of new algorithms and tools, such as machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data visualization, have enhanced the capabilities and efficiency of big data analytics.

- **Connectivity through cloud computing**: Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services, such as servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics, etc., over the internet. Cloud computing provides several benefits for big data, such as:

  - **Scalability**: Cloud computing allows big data applications to scale up or down according to the demand and the availability of resources, without affecting the performance or the quality of service.
  - **Elasticity**: Cloud computing enables big data applications to dynamically adjust the amount and the type of resources that are allocated, depending on the workload and the requirements.
  - **Accessibility**: Cloud computing allows big data applications to be accessed from anywhere and anytime, as long as there is an internet connection. This also facilitates the collaboration and the sharing of data and insights among different users and stakeholders.
  - **Cost-effectiveness**: Cloud computing reduces the capital and operational expenses of big data, as the users only pay for the resources that they use, and do not have to invest in purchasing and maintaining their own infrastructure and software  .

- **Increased knowledge about data science**: Data science is the interdisciplinary field that combines mathematics, statistics, computer science, and domain knowledge to extract meaningful insights from data. Data science has become a popular and valuable skill in the modern economy, as more and more organizations and industries recognize the potential and the benefits of big data analytics. The demand for data scientists and data analysts has increased significantly in the recent years, as well as the availability of educational and training programs, online courses, books, blogs, podcasts, etc., that aim to teach and promote data science .

- **Social media applications**: Social media platforms, such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, YouTube, etc., are among the major sources and consumers of big data. Social media generates a large amount of data every day, from the posts, comments, likes, shares, views, etc., of the users, as well as the metadata, such as the location, time, device, etc., of the activities. Social media also consumes a large amount of data, as it uses big data analytics to provide personalized and targeted content, recommendations, advertisements, etc., to the users, as well as to monitor and improve the user experience, engagement, and satisfaction. Social media also enables the creation and the dissemination of user-generated content, such as blogs, videos, podcasts, etc., that can provide valuable insights and opinions on various topics and issues  .
