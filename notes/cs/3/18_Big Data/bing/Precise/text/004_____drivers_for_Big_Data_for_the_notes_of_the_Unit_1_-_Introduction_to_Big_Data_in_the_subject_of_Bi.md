### Drivers for Big Data

Big Data has quickly risen to become one of the most desired topics in the industry. The main business drivers for such rising demand for Big Data Analytics are:

1. **The digitization of society** - With the increasing digitization of society, more and more data is being generated and collected, creating a need for Big Data Analytics to make sense of it all.
2. **The drop in technology costs** - The cost of technology has decreased significantly, making it more accessible for businesses to adopt Big Data Analytics.
3. **Connectivity through cloud computing** - Cloud computing has made it easier for businesses to connect and share data, further driving the need for Big Data Analytics.
4. **Increased knowledge about data science** - As knowledge about data science has increased, so has the demand for Big Data Analytics to make use of this knowledge.
5. **Social media applications** - The rise of social media has generated a vast amount of data, creating a need for Big Data Analytics to make sense of it all.
6. **The rise of Internet-of-Things (IoT)** - The rise of IoT has led to an increase in the amount of data being generated, further driving the need for Big Data Analytics.
