### Administering Hadoop

Administering Hadoop involves the implementation and ongoing administration of the Hadoop infrastructure. Some of the responsibilities of a Hadoop administrator include:

1. Maintenance of the file system .
2. Managing and monitoring Hadoop clustering, including the name node and data nodes .
3. Monitoring the Hadoop cluster over time .
4. Determining the size of the cluster based on the Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) .
5. Adding and removing new nodes and ensuring their availability .
6. Setting up new Hadoop users by working with delivery teams .
7. Performance tuning .
8. Managing and reviewing Hadoop log files .
9. Installing operating system and Hadoop update patches by collaborating with application teams .
10. Serving as the point of contact for vendor escalation .

Hadoop administration also includes HDFS administration and MapReduce administration. HDFS administration involves monitoring the HDFS file structure, location, and updated files . MapReduce administration involves monitoring nodal configurations, applications, and application status .

To get started with administering Hadoop, you can refer to the Hadoop documentation, which includes information on setting up a single-node or multi-node Hadoop installation . It is also valuable for an administrator to have a basic understanding of how the components of HDFS, such as the name node, secondary name node, and data nodes, organize their persistent data on disk .
