### Schedulers for the notes of the Unit 6 - Hadoop Eco System and YARN in the subject of Big Data

- **YARN** stands for **Yet Another Resource Negotiator** and is one of the core components of the Hadoop ecosystem.
- It was introduced in **Hadoop 2.0** to remove the bottleneck on Job Tracker which was present in Hadoop 1.0.
- YARN has **three built-in schedulers**: the **FIFO**, **Fair** and **Capacity Scheduler**.
- These schedulers provide users different methods to allocate resources of a Hadoop cluster to execute their MapReduce jobs.
- However, they do not guarantee that their jobs will be executed within a specific deadline.
- There are also other big data schedulers such as **Oozie** and **Airflow**.
