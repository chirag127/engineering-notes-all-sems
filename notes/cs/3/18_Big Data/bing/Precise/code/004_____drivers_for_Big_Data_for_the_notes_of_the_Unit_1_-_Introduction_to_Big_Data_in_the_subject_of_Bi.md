### Drivers for Big Data

Big Data has quickly risen to become one of the most desired topics in the industry. The main business drivers for such rising demand for Big Data Analytics are:

1. **The digitization of society** - As society becomes more digitized, the amount of data generated increases, driving the need for Big Data Analytics.
2. **The drop in technology costs** - The cost of technology has decreased, making it more accessible for businesses to adopt Big Data Analytics.
3. **Connectivity through cloud computing** - Cloud computing has made it easier for businesses to connect and share data, driving the need for Big Data Analytics.
4. **Increased knowledge about data science** - As knowledge about data science increases, businesses are better able to understand the value of Big Data Analytics.
5. **Social media applications** - The rise of social media has generated a large amount of data, driving the need for Big Data Analytics.
6. **The rise of Internet-of-Things (IoT)** - The rise of IoT has generated a large amount of data, driving the need for Big Data Analytics.

These drivers have led to the growth, use, and exploitation of Big Data. Big Data has become a core strategy for many successful companies such as Apple, Amazon, Facebook, and Netflix.
