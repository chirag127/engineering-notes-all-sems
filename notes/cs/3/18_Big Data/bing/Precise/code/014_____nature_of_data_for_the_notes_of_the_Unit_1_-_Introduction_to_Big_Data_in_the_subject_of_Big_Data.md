### Nature of Data

1. **Volume**: Big Data implies large volumes of data. It can range from terabytes to petabytes of data.
2. **Variety**: Big Data includes data from a variety of sources and in a variety of formats, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data.
3. **Velocity**: Big Data is generated at a high speed and must be processed and analyzed in real-time.
4. **Veracity**: Big Data can be uncertain and incomplete, and its quality and accuracy must be managed.
5. **Value**: Big Data has the potential to provide valuable insights and drive decision-making, but it must be analyzed and interpreted correctly to extract this value.
