# Administering Hadoop

Administering Hadoop involves managing and monitoring the Hadoop infrastructure, including the Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS) and the MapReduce framework. Some of the responsibilities of a Hadoop administrator include:

1. Maintenance of the file system .
2. Managing and monitoring Hadoop clusters, including the NameNode and DataNodes .
3. Monitoring the Hadoop cluster over time .
4. Determining the size of the cluster based on HDFS .
5. Adding and removing new nodes and ensuring their availability .
6. Setting up new Hadoop users by working with delivery teams .
7. Performance tuning .
8. Managing and reviewing Hadoop log files .
9. Installing operating system and Hadoop update patches by collaborating with application teams .
10. Serving as the point of contact for vendor escalation .

A Hadoop administrator should have a basic understanding of how the components of HDFS, including the NameNode, the Secondary NameNode, and the DataNodes, organize their persistent data on disk . This knowledge can help diagnose problems and identify issues. The Hadoop documentation provides information on how to set up a single-node or multi-node Hadoop installation .
