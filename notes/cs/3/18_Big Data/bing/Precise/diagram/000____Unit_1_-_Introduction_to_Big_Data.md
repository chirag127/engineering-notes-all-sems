## Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data

Big data refers to the large, diverse sets of information that grow at ever-increasing rates. It encompasses the volume of information, the velocity or speed at which it is created and collected, and the variety or scope of the data points being covered. Big data often comes from multiple sources and arrives in multiple formats.

1. **Volume**: The amount of data being generated and stored is growing at an exponential rate. This is due to the increasing number of devices and applications that are collecting and storing data.

2. **Velocity**: The speed at which data is being generated and processed is also increasing. This is due to the increasing number of real-time data sources and the need for real-time processing and analysis.

3. **Variety**: Data is being generated in many different formats, including structured data (such as numbers and dates), semi-structured data (such as XML and JSON), and unstructured data (such as text and images).

Big data can be used to improve decision-making, uncover hidden patterns, and provide insights that were previously unavailable. It is used in many industries, including healthcare, finance, and retail, to improve operations, reduce costs, and increase customer satisfaction.
