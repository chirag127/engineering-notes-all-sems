### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection

1. **Security:** Big data security refers to the measures, tools, and processes used to protect big data from attacks, theft, or other malicious activities. This includes data encryption, access controls, and network security measures.

2. **Compliance:** Compliance refers to the adherence to laws, regulations, and industry standards related to the collection, storage, and use of big data. This includes data privacy laws, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and industry-specific regulations, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

3. **Auditing:** Auditing refers to the process of reviewing and verifying the accuracy and completeness of data, as well as the processes and controls used to manage it. This includes data quality checks, data lineage tracking, and data access audits.

4. **Protection:** Protection refers to the measures taken to prevent data loss or corruption, such as data backup and disaster recovery planning. This also includes measures to ensure data integrity, such as data validation and error checking.

These features are essential for ensuring the safe and responsible use of big data, and for maintaining the trust of individuals and organizations whose data is being collected and analyzed.
