### Administering Hadoop

Administering Hadoop involves the implementation and ongoing administration of the Hadoop infrastructure. This includes tasks such as:

1. Maintenance of the file system .
2. Managing and monitoring Hadoop clustering, including the name node and data nodes .
3. Monitoring the Hadoop cluster over time .
4. Determining the size of the cluster based on HDFS .
5. Adding and removing new nodes and ensuring their availability .
6. Setting up new Hadoop users by working with delivery teams .
7. Performance tuning .
8. Managing and reviewing Hadoop log files .
9. Installing operating system and Hadoop update patches in collaboration with application teams .
10. Serving as the point of contact for vendor escalation .

Hadoop administration also includes the administration of HDFS and MapReduce. HDFS administration involves monitoring the HDFS file structure, location, and updated files . MapReduce administration involves monitoring nodal configurations, applications, and application status .

As an administrator, it is also important to have a basic understanding of how the components of HDFS, including the namenode, the secondary namenode, and the datanodes, organize their persistent data on disk. This knowledge can help diagnose problems or identify when something is not functioning correctly .
