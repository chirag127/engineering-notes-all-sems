### Challenges of Conventional Systems

1. **Scalability**: Conventional systems are not designed to handle the large volumes of data generated by modern applications. As data grows, it becomes increasingly difficult to scale these systems to meet the demands of processing and storage.

2. **Performance**: The performance of conventional systems can degrade as data volumes increase, making it difficult to process and analyze data in a timely manner.

3. **Cost**: The cost of scaling conventional systems to handle large volumes of data can be prohibitively expensive, requiring significant investments in hardware and infrastructure.

4. **Data Variety**: Conventional systems are often designed to handle structured data, but modern applications generate a wide variety of data types, including unstructured and semi-structured data. This can make it difficult to integrate and analyze data from multiple sources.

5. **Real-time Processing**: Many modern applications require real-time processing of data, but conventional systems are not designed for this type of workload. This can result in delays and reduced responsiveness.

6. **Data Management**: Managing large volumes of data can be challenging with conventional systems, requiring significant effort to ensure data quality, consistency, and availability.

These are some of the challenges that conventional systems face when dealing with big data. Big data technologies have been developed to address these challenges and provide more effective solutions for managing and analyzing large volumes of data.
