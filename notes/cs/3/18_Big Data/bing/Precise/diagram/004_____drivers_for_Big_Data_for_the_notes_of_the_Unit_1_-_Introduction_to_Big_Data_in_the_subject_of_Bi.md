### Drivers for Big Data

1. **Volume**: The amount of data being generated and stored is growing at an exponential rate. This is due to the increasing use of digital devices, social media, and the Internet of Things (IoT).

2. **Velocity**: The speed at which data is being generated and processed is also increasing. This is due to the growing use of real-time data processing and streaming technologies.

3. **Variety**: Data is being generated in a wide variety of formats, including structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data. This is due to the increasing use of different types of digital devices and applications.

4. **Veracity**: The accuracy and reliability of data is becoming increasingly important. This is due to the growing use of data-driven decision making and the need for high-quality data.

5. **Value**: The potential value that can be derived from data is also a key driver for Big Data. This is due to the growing use of advanced analytics and machine learning technologies to extract insights and knowledge from data.
