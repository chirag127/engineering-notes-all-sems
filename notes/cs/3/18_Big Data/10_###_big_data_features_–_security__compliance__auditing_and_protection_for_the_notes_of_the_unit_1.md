### Big Data features – security, compliance, auditing and protection for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Big Data in the subject of Big Data

Security, compliance, auditing, and protection are important features in big data systems, as they ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data. The following are some of the key concepts related to security, compliance, auditing, and protection in big data systems:

1. Data Encryption: Data encryption is the process of converting data into a coded format, to protect it from unauthorized access.

2. Access Control: Access control is the process of regulating who has access to data, and what actions they can perform on the data.

3. Compliance: Compliance refers to the process of ensuring that data is processed and stored in accordance with legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Auditing: Auditing is the process of monitoring and recording data access and processing, to ensure compliance with security and privacy policies.

5. Data Backup and Recovery: Data backup and recovery is the process of creating and maintaining copies of data, to ensure that data can be recovered in the event of a disaster or failure.

6. Data Masking: Data masking is the process of obscuring sensitive data, to protect it from unauthorized access.

In this unit, you will learn about security, compliance, auditing, and protection in big data systems, including data encryption, access control, compliance, auditing, data backup and recovery, and data masking.

This unit will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of security, compliance, auditing, and protection in big data systems, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of big data.
