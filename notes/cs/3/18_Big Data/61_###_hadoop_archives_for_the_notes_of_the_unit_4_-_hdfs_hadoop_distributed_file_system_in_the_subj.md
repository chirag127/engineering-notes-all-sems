### Hadoop archives for the notes of the Unit 4 - HDFS (Hadoop Distributed File System) in the subject of Big Data

The Hadoop Archives section of the Unit 4 - HDFS (Hadoop Distributed File System) in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of Hadoop Archives (a data compression and archiving format for HDFS)
2. Overview of Hadoop Archives (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. Creating Hadoop Archives (e.g. steps involved, best practices, etc.)
4. Overview of Creating Hadoop Archives (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. Accessing Hadoop Archives (e.g. how to access and extract data from Hadoop archives)
6. Overview of Accessing Hadoop Archives (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. Hadoop Archives and Data Management (e.g. how Hadoop archives can be used for data management)
8. Overview of Hadoop Archives and Data Management (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. Hadoop Archives in industry applications (how Hadoop archives are used in various industries)
10. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

Hadoop Archives is an important component of the subject of Big Data, especially in the context of HDFS. Understanding the principles and techniques of Hadoop Archives helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data management solutions using HDFS. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
