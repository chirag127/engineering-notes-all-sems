## Unit 8 - MongoDB

The Unit 8 - MongoDB in the subject of Big Data should cover the following topics:

1. Definition of MongoDB (a NoSQL database for storing and retrieving large amounts of data)
2. Overview of MongoDB (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
3. MongoDB Data Model (e.g. how data is stored and organized in MongoDB)
4. Overview of MongoDB Data Model (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
5. MongoDB Queries (e.g. how to retrieve data from MongoDB)
6. Overview of MongoDB Queries (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
7. MongoDB Indexes (e.g. how to optimize MongoDB queries using indexes)
8. Overview of MongoDB Indexes (what they are, when they are used, advantages and disadvantages)
9. MongoDB Aggregation (e.g. how to perform complex data analysis using MongoDB aggregation)
10. Overview of MongoDB Aggregation (what it is, when it is used, advantages and disadvantages)
11. MongoDB in industry applications (how MongoDB is used in various industries)
12. Further reading and resources (books, articles, online resources)

MongoDB is an important component of the subject of Big Data. Understanding the principles and techniques of MongoDB helps students to develop the skills required to design and implement effective data storage and retrieval solutions using MongoDB. The hands-on practice and further reading resources will help students to develop their skills and deepen their understanding of the material.
