## Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases

Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data will cover the concepts of NoSQL databases and their use in processing and storing large amounts of data.

NoSQL databases are a type of database that are designed to handle large amounts of data and are optimized for processing data in a distributed and scalable manner. Unlike traditional relational databases, NoSQL databases do not use a fixed schema and can handle unstructured and semi-structured data.

In the context of Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data, students will learn about the concepts of NoSQL databases, including the benefits and limitations of using NoSQL databases. This may include working with examples of NoSQL databases, analyzing the syntax and structure of NoSQL databases, and learning how to use NoSQL databases to process and store large amounts of data.

In Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data, students will also learn about the different types of NoSQL databases, including document-oriented databases, key-value stores, and columnar databases. They will learn about the use cases for each type of NoSQL database and how to choose the right type of NoSQL database for a given use case.

In Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data, students will also learn about the benefits and limitations of using NoSQL databases, and will learn about alternative approaches to processing and storing large amounts of data, such as using data warehousing and business intelligence tools or using Hadoop.

In summary, in Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data, students will learn about the concepts of NoSQL databases and their use in processing and storing large amounts of data. They will learn about the different types of NoSQL databases, the benefits and limitations of using NoSQL databases, and alternative approaches to processing and storing large amounts of data.
