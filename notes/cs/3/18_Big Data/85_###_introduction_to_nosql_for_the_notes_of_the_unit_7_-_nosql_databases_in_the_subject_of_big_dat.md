### Introduction to NoSQL for the notes of the Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data

Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data will cover the basics of NoSQL, the type of databases used for big data processing and analytics.

NoSQL databases are a type of databases that are designed to handle large amounts of unstructured or semi-structured data, and are used for big data processing and analytics. Unlike traditional relational databases, NoSQL databases do not use a fixed schema and can handle data that is not well-structured.

In the context of Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data, students will learn about NoSQL databases, including the benefits and limitations of using NoSQL databases for big data processing and analytics. This may include working with examples of NoSQL databases, analyzing the syntax and structure of NoSQL databases, and learning how to use NoSQL databases to store, process, and analyze large amounts of unstructured or semi-structured data.

In Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data, students will also learn about the different types of NoSQL databases, such as document databases, key-value databases, and graph databases, and will learn about the use cases and applications of NoSQL databases.

In summary, in Unit 7 - NoSQL Databases in the subject of Big Data, students will learn about NoSQL databases, the type of databases used for big data processing and analytics. They will learn about the benefits and limitations of using NoSQL databases for big data processing and analytics, and will learn about the different types of NoSQL databases and their use cases and applications.
