### Running MRv1 in YARN for the notes of the Unit 6 - Hadoop Eco System and YARN in the subject of Big Data

Running MRv1 (MapReduce version 1) in YARN (Yet Another Resource Negotiator) refers to the process of executing MapReduce jobs in a Hadoop cluster that uses YARN as its resource manager. The following are some of the key concepts related to running MRv1 in YARN:

1. MRv1: MRv1 is the first version of the MapReduce framework, and provides a basic framework for processing and analyzing large data sets in Hadoop.

2. YARN: YARN is the resource manager used in Hadoop, and is responsible for allocating resources and managing jobs in the cluster.

3. MRv1 Job Execution: MRv1 job execution in YARN involves the submission of a MapReduce job to YARN, which is then responsible for allocating resources and executing the job in the cluster.

4. MRv1 Job Scheduling: MRv1 job scheduling in YARN involves the allocation of resources to MRv1 jobs, and is performed by YARN based on the resource requirements of the jobs and the availability of resources in the cluster.

5. MRv1 Job Monitoring: MRv1 job monitoring in YARN involves the monitoring of MRv1 jobs as they run in the cluster, and is performed by YARN to ensure that jobs are executing correctly and efficiently.

6. MRv1 Job Management: MRv1 job management in YARN involves the management of MRv1 jobs, including the starting, stopping, and monitoring of jobs, and is performed by YARN to ensure that jobs are executed correctly and efficiently.
