### market based modelling for the notes of the Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in the subject of Data Analytics

Unit 5 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frequent Itemsets and Clustering, including the aspect of market based modeling.

Market based modeling is a technique used in data analytics to analyze market data, such as sales data, to understand consumer behavior and make predictions about future market trends. Market based modeling uses techniques such as association rule mining, clustering, and regression analysis to analyze market data and make predictions.

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 5 will learn about market based modeling as part of the discussion on Frequent Itemsets and Clustering. This may include working with examples of market data, analyzing the benefits and limitations of market based modeling, and learning how to implement market based modeling techniques to make predictions about future market trends.
