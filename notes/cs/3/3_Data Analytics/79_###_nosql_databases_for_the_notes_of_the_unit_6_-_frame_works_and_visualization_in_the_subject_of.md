### NoSQL Databases for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

Unit 6 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frameworks and Visualization, including the aspect of NoSQL databases.

NoSQL databases are a type of database that do not use the traditional relational database model. Instead, they use alternative data models, such as key-value, document, graph, and columnar, to store and manage data.

NoSQL databases are designed to handle large amounts of unstructured and semi-structured data, and are often used in big data and real-time data processing applications. They are also known for their scalability, high performance, and flexibility.

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 6 will learn about NoSQL databases as part of the discussion on Frameworks and Visualization. This may include working with examples of NoSQL databases, analyzing the benefits and limitations of NoSQL databases, and learning how to use NoSQL databases in a data analytics project.
