### systems and applications for the notes of the Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization in the subject of Data Analytics

Unit 6 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frameworks and Visualization, including the aspect of systems and applications.

Systems and applications refer to the software and hardware components that are used to implement data analytics solutions. This includes the data analytics software frameworks, such as Apache Spark and Hadoop, as well as the hardware infrastructure, such as servers, storage, and networks.

In the context of the Data Analytics course, students in Unit 6 will learn about systems and applications as part of the discussion on Frameworks and Visualization. This may include working with examples of data analytics systems and applications, analyzing the benefits and limitations of different systems and applications, and learning how to choose and implement the appropriate systems and applications for a specific data analytics project.

In summary, Unit 6 of the Data Analytics course focuses on Frameworks and Visualization, and includes the aspect of systems and applications. Systems and applications refer to the software and hardware components that are used to implement data analytics solutions, and students will learn about systems and applications through interactive activities and hands-on experiences, and will learn how to choose and implement the appropriate systems and applications for a specific data analytics project.
