### analysis vs reporting for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of Data Analytics

Data analytics is the process of examining, cleaning, transforming, and modeling data with the goal of discovering useful information, drawing conclusions, and supporting decision-making. The two main components of data analytics are analysis and reporting.

Analysis is the process of exploring and examining data to identify patterns, relationships, and insights. It involves a series of steps, including data preparation, data exploration, and data modeling. During the analysis phase, data analysts use various techniques, such as statistical analysis, data visualization, and machine learning, to uncover insights and draw conclusions from the data.

Reporting, on the other hand, is the process of presenting the results of the analysis in a clear and concise manner. This involves creating reports, dashboards, and visualizations that communicate the insights and conclusions drawn from the data. Reports should be designed in a way that is easy to understand and interpret, and they should be tailored to the specific audience they are intended for.

In this unit, you will learn about the basics of data analytics, including the different stages of the data analytics process, the techniques used in data analysis, and the principles of effective data reporting. You will also learn about the various tools and technologies used in data analytics, and you will explore the various concepts and techniques used in the field of data analytics. This will provide a foundation for understanding the principles and practices of data analytics, and for exploring the various concepts and techniques used in the field of data analytics.
