## Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

Unit 1 introduces the basics of data analytics, including:
1) Definition of data analytics and its applications in various industries.
2) Types of data such as structured, unstructured, and semi-structured.
3) Data collection methods, including sampling and survey design.
4) Data cleaning and preparation, including handling missing values and outliers.
5) Overview of data visualization and its importance in data analysis.
6) Introduction to statistical concepts like mean, median, mode, and standard deviation.
7) Overview of various data analysis techniques, including descriptive statistics, inferential statistics, and predictive modeling.

The goal of Unit 1 is to provide a foundational understanding of data analytics and its role in making informed decisions based on data.
