### applications of data analytics for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of Data Analytics

1. Business Intelligence: Analyzing data to gain insights into business performance and make informed decisions. 
2. Customer Analytics: Understanding customer behavior and preferences to improve marketing and sales efforts. 
3. Fraud Detection: Identifying suspicious activity in financial transactions to prevent financial losses. 
4. Predictive Maintenance: Predictive modeling to predict when equipment will fail, allowing for proactive maintenance. 
5. Supply Chain Optimization: Analyzing data to improve efficiency and reduce costs in the supply chain. 
6. Healthcare Analytics: Using data to improve patient outcomes and reduce costs in the healthcare industry. 
7. Sports Analytics: Analyzing data to gain insights into player performance and make strategic decisions in sports.
