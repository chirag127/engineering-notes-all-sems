### Sources and nature of data for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of Data Analytics

Sources of data:
1. Surveys and questionnaires
2. Transactions and records (e.g. sales, medical)
3. Social media and web data (e.g. comments, posts)
4. Sensors and IoT devices (e.g. GPS, wearable tech)
5. Government and publicly available datasets (e.g. census, weather)

Nature of data:
1. Structured: organized in a defined format (e.g. database tables)
2. Unstructured: loosely organized or unorganized (e.g. text, images)
3. Numerical: consists of numbers (e.g. sales figures)
4. Categorical: consists of categories or labels (e.g. gender, product type)
5. Continuous: can take on any value within a range (e.g. temperature)
6. Discrete: can only take on specific values (e.g. number of items sold)
