# Various Phases of Data Analytics Lifecycle

- Data analytics lifecycle is a series of steps that guide the process of transforming data into insights and actions for business or project goals  .
- The data analytics lifecycle is based on the CRISP-DM methodology, which is an open-standard analytics model developed by IBM.
- The data analytics lifecycle consists of six phases  :
  - **Business understanding**: This phase involves defining the problem or opportunity, identifying the objectives and requirements, and establishing the success criteria for the analytics project  .
  - **Data understanding**: This phase involves collecting, exploring, and describing the data sources, quality, and characteristics, as well as identifying any potential issues or gaps in the data  .
  - **Data preparation**: This phase involves cleaning, transforming, integrating, and formatting the data to make it ready for analysis, as well as creating any derived variables or features  .
  - **Exploratory analysis**: This phase involves applying various statistical and visualization techniques to explore the data, identify patterns, trends, outliers, and relationships, and generate hypotheses and insights  .
  - **Validation**: This phase involves testing and validating the hypotheses and insights generated in the previous phase, using appropriate methods such as hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, or machine learning models  .
  - **Communication**: This phase involves presenting and communicating the results, insights, and recommendations of the analysis to the stakeholders, using clear and effective formats such as reports, dashboards, or stories  .
- The data analytics lifecycle is not a linear or rigid process, but rather an iterative and flexible one, that can be adapted to different contexts, data types, and analytical goals  .
