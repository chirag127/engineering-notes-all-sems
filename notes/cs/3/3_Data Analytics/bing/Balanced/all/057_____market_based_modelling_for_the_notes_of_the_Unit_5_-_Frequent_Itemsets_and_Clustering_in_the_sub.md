# Market Based Modelling

- Market based modelling is a technique of data analytics that aims to understand the behavior and preferences of customers based on their transactions in a market.
- Market based modelling can help marketers to identify the most profitable products, segments, and channels, as well as to optimize pricing, promotion, and product assortment strategies.
- Market based modelling can also help marketers to discover cross-selling and up-selling opportunities, as well as to measure the impact of marketing campaigns and loyalty programs.
- Market based modelling relies on two main types of data: transaction data and customer data. Transaction data records the details of each purchase made by a customer, such as the product, quantity, price, date, and time. Customer data contains information about the characteristics and demographics of each customer, such as age, gender, income, and location.
- Market based modelling can use various analytical methods and techniques, such as association rules, market basket analysis, clustering, segmentation, and predictive modelling. These methods and techniques can help to uncover patterns and insights from the data, such as the frequency, recency, and monetary value of purchases, the affinity and complementarity of products, the similarity and dissimilarity of customers, and the likelihood and propensity of future purchases.
- Market based modelling can provide valuable inputs for decision making and strategy formulation in marketing. By using market based modelling, marketers can gain a deeper understanding of their customers and their needs, preferences, and behaviors, as well as of their competitors and their offerings. This can help to improve customer satisfaction, retention, and loyalty, as well as to increase market share, revenue, and profitability.
