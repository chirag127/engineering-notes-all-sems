# Model Planning

- Model planning is the third phase of the data analytics lifecycle, after data preparation and data exploration.
- The goal of model planning is to select and apply appropriate analytical techniques to the data, such as regression, classification, clustering, association rules, etc.
- Model planning involves the following steps:

  - Identify the business objective and the analytical problem: What is the question or decision that needs to be answered or made based on the data? What are the criteria for success and the constraints for the solution?
  - Select the variables and the target: What are the input variables (features) and the output variable (target) for the analytical technique? How are they measured and scaled? Are there any missing values or outliers?
  - Choose the analytical technique: What is the best technique to apply to the data, given the objective, the problem, the variables, and the target? What are the assumptions and limitations of the technique? How will the technique handle the data characteristics, such as size, dimensionality, distribution, etc.?
  - Prepare the data for modeling: How will the data be split into training, validation, and testing sets? How will the data be transformed, normalized, or standardized for the technique? How will the data be balanced or sampled for the technique?
