## Unit 6 - Frame Works and Visualization

- In this unit, you will learn about the concepts and tools of data visualization, which is the process of presenting data in graphical or pictorial forms to communicate insights and patterns.
- You will also learn about some popular frameworks for data visualization, such as matplotlib, seaborn, plotly, and bokeh, which are Python libraries that provide various functionalities and options for creating interactive and static plots.
- The objectives of this unit are:

  - To understand the principles and elements of data visualization, such as data types, variables, aesthetics, scales, and layers.
  - To explore the different types of plots and charts that can be used for data visualization, such as scatter plots, line plots, bar charts, histograms, box plots, pie charts, and heat maps.
  - To compare and contrast the advantages and disadvantages of different frameworks for data visualization, such as matplotlib, seaborn, plotly, and bokeh.
  - To apply the frameworks for data visualization to create and customize plots and charts for different datasets and scenarios.
  - To evaluate the effectiveness and quality of data visualization based on the criteria of clarity, accuracy, aesthetics, and ethics.
