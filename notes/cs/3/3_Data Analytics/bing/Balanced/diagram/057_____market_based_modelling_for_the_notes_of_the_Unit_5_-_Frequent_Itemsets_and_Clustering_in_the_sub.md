# Market Based Modelling

- Market based modelling is a technique of data analytics that aims to understand the behavior and preferences of customers based on their transactions in a market.
- Market based modelling can help marketers to identify the most profitable products, segments, and channels, as well as to design effective promotional strategies and cross-selling opportunities.
- Market based modelling can also help marketers to measure the impact of their marketing campaigns and optimize their marketing mix.
- Market based modelling relies on various types of data, such as transactional data, customer data, product data, and external data, to build descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive models.
- Market based modelling can use different methods and techniques, such as association rules, market basket analysis, customer lifetime value, segmentation, clustering, and recommendation systems.
- Market based modelling can provide valuable insights and actionable recommendations for marketers to improve their decision making and business performance.
