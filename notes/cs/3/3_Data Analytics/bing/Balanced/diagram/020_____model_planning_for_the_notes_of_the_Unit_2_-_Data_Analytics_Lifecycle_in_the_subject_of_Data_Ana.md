### Model Planning

- The second phase of the Data Analytics Lifecycle, after the discovery phase.
- The main goal is to determine the methods and techniques to use for data analysis, based on the business objectives and the available data.
- The key activities include:
  - Identifying the variables and the relationships among them that are relevant for the analysis.
  - Selecting the appropriate analytical techniques, such as regression, classification, clustering, etc., based on the type and nature of the data and the problem.
  - Developing a hypothesis or a set of hypotheses to test with the data.
  - Preparing a detailed plan for data collection, preparation, analysis, and validation.
- The key outputs include:
  - A data model that describes the structure, format, and quality of the data to be used for analysis.
  - A method selection matrix that compares and contrasts the advantages and disadvantages of different analytical techniques for the problem.
  - A hypothesis statement that defines the expected outcome or the relationship to be tested with the data.
  - A project plan that outlines the tasks, resources, timeline, and deliverables for the analysis.
