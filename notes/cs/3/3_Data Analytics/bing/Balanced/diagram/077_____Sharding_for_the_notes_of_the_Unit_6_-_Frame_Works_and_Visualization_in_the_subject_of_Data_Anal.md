### Sharding

- Sharding is a database partitioning technique that splits a large database into smaller, faster, and more manageable parts called shards  .
- Sharding is used by blockchain companies and other applications that need to scale up and process more transactions per second.
- Sharding can improve the performance, availability, and security of a database by distributing the data and the workload across multiple nodes or servers.
- Sharding can be done horizontally or vertically. Horizontal sharding divides the data by rows, while vertical sharding divides the data by columns.
- Sharding can also be done based on different criteria, such as key, range, hash, or list. For example, a database of customers can be sharded by their geographic location, their purchase history, or their customer ID.
- Sharding has some challenges and trade-offs, such as data consistency, complexity, and overhead. Sharding requires careful planning and design to ensure that the data is balanced, accessible, and secure across the shards.
