### Communicating Results

- Communicating results is the fifth phase of the data analytics lifecycle, which involves six phases: data discovery, data preparation, planning of data models, building of data models, communicating results, and operationalization .
- The purpose of this phase is to check the results of the data analysis project and determine whether it met the business objectives and expectations  .
- The results are scrutinized by the entire team along with its stakeholders to draw inferences on the key findings and summarize the entire work done .
- The main tasks in this phase are :
  - Validate the results and ensure they are accurate, reliable, and reproducible.
  - Interpret the results and explain their meaning and implications for the business problem or opportunity.
  - Visualize the results and present them in a clear, concise, and engaging way using charts, graphs, tables, dashboards, etc.
  - Communicate the results and recommendations to the relevant audience, such as managers, clients, or decision-makers, using oral, written, or online methods.
  - Solicit feedback and suggestions from the audience and address any questions or concerns they may have.
- The main challenges in this phase are :
  - Choosing the appropriate format and level of detail for the communication, depending on the audience and the purpose.
  - Balancing the technical and business aspects of the communication, and avoiding jargon, ambiguity, or bias.
  - Highlighting the main insights and actionable recommendations, and avoiding information overload or irrelevant details.
  - Ensuring the communication is ethical, honest, and respectful, and follows the data privacy and security policies.
- The main outcomes of this phase are :
  - A final report or presentation that summarizes the data analysis project, its results, and its recommendations.
  - A feedback loop that allows the team and the stakeholders to learn from the project and improve the data analytics process.
  - A decision or action plan that is based on the results and recommendations of the data analysis project.
