### Various phases of data analytics lifecycle

The data analytics lifecycle is a framework that guides the process of transforming data into insights that can be used to achieve business goals. It consists of six phases, based on the CRISP-DM methodology :

1. **Business understanding**: This phase involves defining the problem or opportunity, identifying the objectives and success criteria, and understanding the context and constraints of the project.
2. **Data understanding**: This phase involves collecting, exploring, and describing the data, as well as assessing its quality, relevance, and availability.
3. **Data preparation**: This phase involves cleaning, transforming, integrating, and formatting the data, as well as selecting the appropriate subsets and variables for analysis.
4. **Modeling**: This phase involves applying various analytical techniques and algorithms to the data, such as regression, classification, clustering, or association, to discover patterns, relationships, or insights.
5. **Evaluation**: This phase involves assessing the validity, reliability, and usefulness of the results, as well as comparing different models and approaches, and selecting the best one for the project.
6. **Deployment**: This phase involves communicating, presenting, and implementing the results, as well as monitoring, updating, and maintaining the models and solutions.
