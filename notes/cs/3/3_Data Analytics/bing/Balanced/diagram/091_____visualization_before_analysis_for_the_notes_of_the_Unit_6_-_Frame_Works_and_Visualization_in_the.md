### Visualization before analysis

- Visualization is the process of creating and displaying graphical representations of data or information.
- Visualization can help to explore, understand, communicate, and present data or information in a meaningful and effective way.
- Visualization can also support the analysis of data or information by revealing patterns, trends, outliers, relationships, distributions, and other insights that may not be easily detected by numerical or textual methods.
- Visualization before analysis is the practice of creating and examining visualizations of data or information before applying any analytical techniques or methods.
- Visualization before analysis can help to:

  - Gain an overview of the data or information and its characteristics, such as size, shape, type, quality, and variability.
  - Identify the main questions or objectives of the analysis and the relevant variables or attributes to focus on.
  - Choose the appropriate analytical techniques or methods that suit the data or information and the questions or objectives.
  - Detect and correct any errors, anomalies, or inconsistencies in the data or information that may affect the analysis.
  - Generate and test hypotheses or assumptions about the data or information and its underlying phenomena or processes.
  - Explore different perspectives, scenarios, or alternatives of the data or information and their implications or consequences.
  - Communicate and share the data or information and the analysis results with others in a clear and engaging way.
