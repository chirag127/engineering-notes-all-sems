### Model Planning

- Model planning is the third phase of the data analytics lifecycle, following data preparation and data exploration.
- The goal of model planning is to select and apply appropriate analytical techniques to the data, such as regression, classification, clustering, association rules, etc.
- The steps involved in model planning are:

  - Identify the business objectives and the expected outcomes of the analysis.
  - Define the evaluation metrics and criteria for model performance and validation.
  - Choose the suitable modeling techniques based on the data characteristics, the business problem, and the available resources.
  - Prepare the data for modeling, such as splitting the data into training, validation, and test sets, handling missing values and outliers, transforming and scaling the data, etc.
  - Build and train the models using the selected techniques and the prepared data.
  - Compare and evaluate the models using the predefined metrics and criteria, such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC curve, AUC, etc.
  - Select the best model or models that meet the business objectives and the expected outcomes.
