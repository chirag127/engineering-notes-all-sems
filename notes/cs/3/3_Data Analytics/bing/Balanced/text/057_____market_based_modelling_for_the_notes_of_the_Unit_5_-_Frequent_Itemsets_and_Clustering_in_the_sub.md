### Market Based Modelling

- Market based modelling is a technique of data analysis that aims to discover the patterns and preferences of customers based on their transactions or interactions with a business.
- Market based modelling can help marketers to understand the customer behavior, segment the customers, optimize the product assortment, design effective promotions, and recommend personalized products.
- Market based modelling can be performed using various methods, such as association rules, market basket analysis, sequential pattern mining, and collaborative filtering .
- Association rules are rules that express the relationship between items or attributes in a dataset, such as "if a customer buys bread, then they are likely to buy butter as well".
- Market basket analysis is a technique of finding the frequent itemsets or combinations of items that occur together in a dataset, such as "bread and butter", "milk and cereal", or "beer and diapers".
- Sequential pattern mining is a technique of finding the frequent sequences or ordered lists of items that occur in a dataset, such as "a customer who buys a laptop, then a mouse, then a keyboard" or "a customer who visits a website, then clicks on an ad, then buys a product".
- Collaborative filtering is a technique of predicting the preferences or ratings of a user based on the preferences or ratings of other similar users, such as "a customer who likes action movies is likely to like the movies that other action movie fans like" or "a customer who rates a product highly is likely to rate other similar products highly".
- Market based modelling can be applied to various domains, such as retail, e-commerce, entertainment, education, health care, and social media .
- Market based modelling can provide valuable insights and benefits for businesses, such as increasing customer satisfaction, loyalty, retention, and revenue, improving marketing efficiency and effectiveness, and discovering new business opportunities and challenges   .
