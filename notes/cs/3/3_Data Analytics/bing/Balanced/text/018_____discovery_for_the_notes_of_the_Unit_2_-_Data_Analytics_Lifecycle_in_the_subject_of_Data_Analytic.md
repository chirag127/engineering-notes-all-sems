### Discovery

Discovery is the first phase of the data analytics lifecycle, which defines the roadmap of how data is generated, collected, processed, used, and analyzed to achieve business goals. In this phase, the following tasks are performed:

- Understanding the business issue: This involves identifying the problem or opportunity that data analytics can address, defining the objectives and scope of the project, and establishing the success criteria and metrics.
- Understanding the data set: This involves collecting the relevant data sources, exploring the data quality and availability, and assessing the data suitability and feasibility for the project.
- Preparing the data: This involves cleaning, transforming, integrating, and formatting the data to make it ready for analysis.
- Exploratory analysis: This involves applying descriptive and inferential statistics, visualizing the data, and identifying patterns, trends, outliers, and relationships in the data.
- Validation: This involves verifying the assumptions, hypotheses, and results of the exploratory analysis, and ensuring the data and methods are reliable and accurate.

The discovery phase is crucial for setting the foundation and direction of the data analytics project, and for ensuring the data and methods are appropriate and valid for the business issue. The output of this phase is a clear understanding of the data and the problem, and a plan for the next phases of the data analytics lifecycle.
