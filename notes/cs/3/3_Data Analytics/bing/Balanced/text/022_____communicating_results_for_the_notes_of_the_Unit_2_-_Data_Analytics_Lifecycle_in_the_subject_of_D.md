### Communicating Results

- Communicating results is the final phase of the data analytics lifecycle, where the insights and recommendations derived from the analysis are presented to the stakeholders.
- The main objectives of communicating results are to:
  - Inform the stakeholders about the findings and implications of the analysis.
  - Persuade the stakeholders to take actions based on the analysis.
  - Educate the stakeholders about the data, methods, and assumptions used in the analysis.
- The main challenges of communicating results are to:
  - Tailor the message to the audience, considering their background, expectations, and needs.
  - Choose the appropriate format, medium, and level of detail for the presentation.
  - Use clear, concise, and compelling language and visuals to convey the key points and recommendations.
  - Address the potential questions, concerns, and objections from the stakeholders.
- The main steps of communicating results are to:
  - Plan the presentation, defining the purpose, scope, and audience of the communication.
  - Prepare the content, organizing the information, creating the visuals, and writing the script or notes.
  - Deliver the presentation, using effective verbal and non-verbal communication skills, and engaging the audience.
  - Follow up the presentation, soliciting feedback, answering questions, and providing additional information or support if needed.
