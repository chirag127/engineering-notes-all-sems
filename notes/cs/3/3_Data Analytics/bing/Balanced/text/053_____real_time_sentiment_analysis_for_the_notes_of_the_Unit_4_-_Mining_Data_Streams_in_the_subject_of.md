### Real Time Sentiment Analysis

- Real time sentiment analysis is a machine learning technique that automatically recognizes and extracts the sentiment in a text whenever it occurs .
- Sentiment is the subjective attitude or emotion expressed by a person or a group of people towards a topic, such as a brand, a product, a service, or an event.
- Sentiment analysis can be used to analyze live social and online feeds, such as comments, posts, reviews, tweets, etc., and derive insights from them  .
- Some of the applications of real time sentiment analysis are:
  - Customer service: to monitor customer feedback and satisfaction, and respond to issues and complaints in a timely manner.
  - Marketing: to measure brand reputation and awareness, and identify influencers and advocates.
  - Product development: to understand customer needs and preferences, and improve product features and quality.
  - Market research: to gauge public opinion and sentiment towards competitors, trends, and events.
- The process of real time sentiment analysis follows these four steps:
  - Breaking down the text into components: sentences, phrases, tokens, and parts of speech.
  - Identifying each phrase and component.
  - Assigning a sentiment score to each phrase with plus or minus points.
  - Combining scores for a final sentiment analysis.
- Some of the challenges of real time sentiment analysis are:
  - Handling noisy and unstructured data, such as slang, abbreviations, emojis, etc.
  - Dealing with sarcasm, irony, and humor, which can be difficult to detect and interpret.
  - Accounting for context, tone, and intent, which can vary depending on the source, the audience, and the situation.
  - Adapting to different languages, domains, and cultures, which can have different expressions and norms of sentiment.
