### Need of Data Analytics

- Data analytics is the science of analyzing raw data to make conclusions about that information.
- Data analytics can help a business optimize its performance, perform more efficiently, maximize profit, or make more strategically-guided decisions.
- Data analytics can also reveal trends and metrics that would otherwise be lost in the mass of information.
- Data analytics can be applied to any type of information, such as customer behavior, market trends, social media, health care, education, etc .
- Data analytics can use various techniques, such as descriptive analytics, diagnostic analytics, predictive analytics, and prescriptive analytics, to answer different types of questions.
- Data analytics requires skills in data collection, data transformation, data organization, data visualization, data interpretation, and data communication.
- Data analytics is a growing and in-demand field, with many career opportunities for data analysts, data scientists, data engineers, data architects, etc.
