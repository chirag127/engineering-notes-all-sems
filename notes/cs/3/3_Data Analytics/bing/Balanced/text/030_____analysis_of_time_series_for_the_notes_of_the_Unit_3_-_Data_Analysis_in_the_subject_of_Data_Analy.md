### Analysis of Time Series

- A time series is a series of data points that are ordered in time, usually at equally spaced intervals.
- Time series analysis is the process of analyzing a time series to extract meaningful statistics and characteristics of the data .
- Time series analysis can be used for various purposes, such as:
  - Describing the patterns and trends in the data, such as seasonality, cycles, or outliers.
  - Explaining the causes and effects of the data, such as identifying the factors that influence the data or the impact of the data on other variables.
  - Forecasting the future values of the data, such as predicting the sales, demand, or price of a product or service.
- Time series analysis can involve different types of models, such as:
  - Univariate models, which analyze a single variable over time, such as the monthly temperature or the daily stock price.
  - Multivariate models, which analyze multiple variables over time, such as the relationship between the GDP and the unemployment rate or the interaction between the supply and demand of a commodity.
  - Linear models, which assume a linear relationship between the variables, such as the simple regression or the autoregressive integrated moving average (ARIMA) models.
  - Nonlinear models, which allow for more complex and flexible relationships between the variables, such as the neural networks or the exponential smoothing models.
