### Characteristics of Data

Data is any form of information that has been gathered and organized in a meaningful format that can be processed further. Data can be used for various purposes, such as analysis, decision making, communication, etc. Data can be classified into different types, such as numerical, categorical, ordinal, etc. depending on the nature and level of measurement of the information.

Data quality is an important aspect of data that affects its usability and reliability. Data quality can be assessed by various characteristics, such as :

- **Accuracy**: The degree to which the data reflects the true or correct values of the phenomenon or entity that it represents. Accuracy can be affected by errors, biases, or inconsistencies in the data collection, processing, or storage methods.
- **Relevance**: The degree to which the data meets the needs and expectations of the user or the purpose for which it is intended. Relevance can be influenced by the timeliness, completeness, and appropriateness of the data.
- **Consistency**: The degree to which the data is compatible and coherent across different sources, systems, or contexts. Consistency can be ensured by following common standards, definitions, and formats for the data.
- **Reliability**: The degree to which the data can be trusted and verified as accurate and consistent. Reliability can be enhanced by using credible and authoritative sources, methods, and tools for the data.
- **Uniqueness**: The degree to which the data is free of duplicates or redundancies. Uniqueness can be achieved by using proper identifiers, keys, or indexes for the data.

Data can also be described by various statistical measures that summarize its distribution, central tendency, and variability :

- **Distribution**: The frequency or proportion of each value or category of the data. Distribution can be represented by graphs, tables, or charts that show the shape, spread, and outliers of the data.
- **Central tendency**: The average or typical value or category of the data. Central tendency can be measured by the mean, median, or mode of the data, depending on the type and distribution of the data.
- **Variability**: The degree to which the data values or categories differ from each other or from the central tendency. Variability can be quantified by the range, standard deviation, or variance of the data, depending on the type and distribution of the data.
