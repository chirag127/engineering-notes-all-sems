### Operationalization

- Operationalization is the process of deploying the analytics solution into the production environment and ensuring that it works as intended.
- Operationalization involves the following steps:
  - Testing the solution on a subset of the production data and validating the results.
  - Integrating the solution with the existing systems and processes, such as data sources, workflows, user interfaces, security, etc.
  - Monitoring the performance and accuracy of the solution and making adjustments as needed.
  - Documenting the solution and providing training and support to the end users and stakeholders.
- Operationalization requires collaboration and communication among the data analysts, the business users, the IT staff, and the management.
- Operationalization is an iterative and ongoing process that aims to deliver value and insights to the business.
