### Model Planning

- Model planning is the third phase of the data analytics lifecycle, following the data preparation phase.
- The goal of model planning is to determine the appropriate techniques and methods to analyze the data and address the business problem or opportunity.
- The steps involved in model planning are:

  - Selecting the variables and features that are relevant for the analysis and the target variable that represents the outcome of interest.
  - Choosing the modeling technique or algorithm that best suits the data and the problem, such as regression, classification, clustering, etc.
  - Evaluating the performance and accuracy of the model using various metrics and methods, such as confusion matrix, ROC curve, cross-validation, etc.
  - Comparing and selecting the best model among the alternatives, based on the evaluation results and the business objectives.
  - Documenting the model assumptions, limitations, and results for future reference and communication.
