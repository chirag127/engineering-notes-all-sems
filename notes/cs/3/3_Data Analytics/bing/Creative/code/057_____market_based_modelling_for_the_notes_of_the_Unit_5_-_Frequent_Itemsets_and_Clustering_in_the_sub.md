### Market Based Modelling

- Market based modelling is a technique of data analytics that aims to understand the behavior and preferences of customers based on their transactions in a market.
- Market based modelling can help marketers to identify the patterns of customer purchases, such as which items are frequently bought together, which items are substitutes or complements, and which items are associated with certain segments or demographics.
- Market based modelling can also help marketers to design effective marketing strategies, such as product bundling, cross-selling, up-selling, pricing, promotion, and recommendation.
- Market based modelling can be performed using various methods, such as association rules, market basket analysis, and clustering.

#### Association Rules

- Association rules are rules that describe the relationships between items in a transactional dataset, such as "if a customer buys item A, then they are likely to buy item B as well".
- Association rules can be measured by two metrics: support and confidence. Support is the proportion of transactions that contain both item A and item B, while confidence is the proportion of transactions that contain item A that also contain item B.
- Association rules can be generated using algorithms such as Apriori, Eclat, or FP-Growth, which iteratively find frequent itemsets and derive rules from them.
- Association rules can be used to discover the patterns of customer purchases, such as which items are frequently bought together, and to recommend products to customers based on their previous purchases.

#### Market Basket Analysis

- Market basket analysis is a technique of data analytics that analyzes the transactions of customers in a market to find the combinations of items that are frequently bought together.
- Market basket analysis can help marketers to understand the customer needs, preferences, and behavior, and to optimize the product assortment, placement, and promotion.
- Market basket analysis can be performed using association rules, which describe the relationships between items in a transactional dataset, or using other methods such as lift, leverage, or conviction, which measure the strength and significance of the associations.
- Market basket analysis can be used to design effective marketing strategies, such as product bundling, cross-selling, up-selling, pricing, and promotion, based on the customer purchase patterns.

#### Clustering

- Clustering is a technique of data analytics that groups the objects in a dataset into clusters based on their similarity or dissimilarity.
- Clustering can help marketers to segment the customers in a market based on their characteristics, behavior, or preferences, and to tailor the marketing strategies for each segment.
- Clustering can be performed using various methods, such as k-means, hierarchical, or density-based clustering, which differ in the way they define and find the clusters.
- Clustering can be used to discover the customer segments in a market, such as which customers are loyal, profitable, or responsive, and to target them with personalized products, offers, or messages.
