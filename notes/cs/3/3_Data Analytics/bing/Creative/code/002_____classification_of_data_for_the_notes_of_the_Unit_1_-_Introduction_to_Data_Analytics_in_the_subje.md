### Classification of Data

Classification of data is the process of organizing data by relevant categories so that it may be used and protected more efficiently. Data classification can be done according to various criteria, such as:

- **Data type**: Data can be classified based on the type of information they contain, such as numerical, categorical, textual, image, audio, video, etc. For example, numerical data can be further classified into discrete or continuous, ordinal or nominal, ratio or interval, etc.
- **Data source**: Data can be classified based on the origin or source of the data, such as primary or secondary, internal or external, experimental or observational, etc. For example, primary data are collected by the researcher for a specific purpose, while secondary data are obtained from existing sources, such as books, journals, websites, etc.
- **Data quality**: Data can be classified based on the quality or accuracy of the data, such as valid or invalid, complete or incomplete, consistent or inconsistent, reliable or unreliable, etc. For example, valid data are those that conform to the rules or standards of the data collection process, while invalid data are those that do not.
- **Data security**: Data can be classified based on the level of security or sensitivity of the data, such as public or private, internal or external, restricted or unrestricted, confidential or unclassified, etc. For example, public data are those that are freely accessible for anyone, while private data are those that are only accessible for authorized persons or entities.
- **Data characteristics**: Data can be classified based on the characteristics or attributes of the data, such as qualitative or quantitative, descriptive or inferential, static or dynamic, structured or unstructured, etc. For example, qualitative data are those that are based on some attributes or quality, such as gender, honesty, hair color, religion, etc., while quantitative data are those that are based on some quantity or measurement, such as height, weight, income, age, etc.

The objectives of data classification are:

- To make data easier to locate and retrieve
- To enhance data analysis and interpretation
- To facilitate data integration and sharing
- To improve data security and compliance
- To optimize data storage and management
