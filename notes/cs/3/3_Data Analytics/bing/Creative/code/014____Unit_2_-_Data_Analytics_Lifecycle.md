# Unit 2 - Data Analytics Lifecycle

The data analytics lifecycle is a framework for planning and executing data analysis projects. It consists of six phases:

- **Discovery**: This phase involves identifying the business problem, defining the objectives and scope of the analysis, and gathering the relevant data sources and stakeholders.
- **Data preparation**: This phase involves cleaning, transforming, and integrating the data into a suitable format for analysis. It also involves exploring the data to understand its characteristics, quality, and distribution.
- **Model planning**: This phase involves selecting the appropriate analytical techniques, tools, and algorithms to address the business problem. It also involves creating and validating hypotheses, and designing experiments or tests to evaluate the results.
- **Model building**: This phase involves applying the selected techniques, tools, and algorithms to the prepared data, and generating outputs such as models, scores, or insights. It also involves assessing the performance, accuracy, and validity of the outputs, and refining them as needed.
- **Communicating results**: This phase involves presenting and visualizing the outputs in a clear and concise manner, and explaining the findings, implications, and recommendations to the stakeholders. It also involves documenting the methodology, assumptions, and limitations of the analysis.
- **Operationalizing**: This phase involves deploying the outputs into the business environment, and monitoring and evaluating their impact and effectiveness. It also involves updating and maintaining the outputs as the data and business conditions change, and identifying new opportunities for analysis.
