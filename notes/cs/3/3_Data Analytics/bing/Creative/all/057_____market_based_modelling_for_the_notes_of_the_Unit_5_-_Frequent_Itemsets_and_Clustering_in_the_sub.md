# Market Based Modelling

- Market based modelling is a technique of data analytics that aims to understand the behavior and preferences of customers based on their transactions in a market.
- Market based modelling can help marketers to identify the patterns of customer purchases, such as which items are frequently bought together, which items are substitutes or complements, and which items are associated with certain segments or demographics.
- Market based modelling can also help marketers to design effective marketing strategies, such as product bundling, cross-selling, up-selling, pricing, promotion, and recommendation.
- Market based modelling can be performed using various methods, such as association rules, market basket analysis, and clustering.

## Association Rules

- Association rules are a type of market based modelling that discovers the relationships between items in a transactional database.
- Association rules are expressed in the form of X -> Y, where X and Y are sets of items, and the rule implies that if a customer buys X, they are likely to buy Y as well.
- Association rules are evaluated by two measures: support and confidence.
- Support is the proportion of transactions that contain both X and Y, and reflects the frequency of the rule.
- Confidence is the proportion of transactions that contain X that also contain Y, and reflects the strength of the rule.
- Association rules can be generated using algorithms such as Apriori, Eclat, and FP-Growth, which use different strategies to prune the search space and find the most relevant rules.

## Market Basket Analysis

- Market basket analysis is a type of market based modelling that analyzes the co-occurrence of items in a transactional database.
- Market basket analysis can help marketers to understand the customer preferences, needs, and behavior, and to identify the opportunities for cross-selling and up-selling.
- Market basket analysis can be performed using association rules, which can reveal the items that are frequently bought together, and the items that are conditionally dependent on each other.
- Market basket analysis can also be performed using other methods, such as lift, leverage, conviction, and correlation, which can measure the degree of association between items.

## Clustering

- Clustering is a type of market based modelling that groups the customers or items based on their similarity or dissimilarity.
- Clustering can help marketers to segment the market, target the customers, and personalize the offers.
- Clustering can be performed using various methods, such as k-means, hierarchical, density-based, and model-based clustering, which use different criteria and algorithms to form the clusters.
- Clustering can also be performed using different types of data, such as numerical, categorical, or mixed data, which require different measures of distance or similarity.
