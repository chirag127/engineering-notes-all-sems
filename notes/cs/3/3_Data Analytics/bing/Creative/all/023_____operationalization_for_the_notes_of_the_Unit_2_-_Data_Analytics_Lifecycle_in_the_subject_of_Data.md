# Operationalization

- Operationalization is the final phase of the data analytics lifecycle, where the results of the analysis are deployed and integrated into the business processes.
- Operationalization involves the following steps:
  - **Deployment**: This is the process of making the analytical model or solution available for use by the intended users or systems. Deployment can be done in various ways, such as embedding the model into an application, creating a dashboard or report, or publishing the model as a web service or API.
  - **Monitoring**: This is the process of tracking the performance and accuracy of the analytical model or solution over time, and identifying any issues or anomalies that may arise. Monitoring can be done using various metrics, such as accuracy, precision, recall, error rate, or business value.
  - **Maintenance**: This is the process of updating and improving the analytical model or solution based on the feedback and results from the monitoring process. Maintenance can involve retraining the model with new data, tuning the parameters, or modifying the features or algorithms.
  - **Feedback**: This is the process of collecting and analyzing the feedback from the users or stakeholders of the analytical model or solution, and incorporating their suggestions or requirements into the maintenance process. Feedback can be obtained through various channels, such as surveys, interviews, or online reviews.

- Operationalization is important for ensuring the success and sustainability of the data analytics project, as it ensures that the analytical model or solution is aligned with the business goals and objectives, and delivers the expected value and benefits.
