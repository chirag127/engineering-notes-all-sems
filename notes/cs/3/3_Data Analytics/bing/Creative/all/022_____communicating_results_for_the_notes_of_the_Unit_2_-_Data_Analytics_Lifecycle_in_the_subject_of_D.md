# Communicating Results

- Communicating results is the final phase of the data analytics lifecycle, where the insights and recommendations derived from the data analysis are presented to the stakeholders or clients.
- Communicating results effectively is crucial for ensuring that the data analysis project meets its objectives and delivers value to the organization or the problem domain.
- Communicating results involves the following steps:

  - **Identify the audience and their needs**: The data analyst should understand who are the intended recipients of the results, what are their expectations, preferences, and level of technical knowledge, and what are the key messages or actions that they need to take away from the results.
  - **Choose the appropriate format and medium**: The data analyst should select the best way to convey the results, such as reports, dashboards, slides, infographics, videos, etc., and the best channel to deliver them, such as email, web, mobile, etc. The format and medium should match the audience's needs, the type and complexity of the results, and the available resources and time.
  - **Design and create the deliverables**: The data analyst should use the principles of data visualization and storytelling to design and create the deliverables that showcase the results in a clear, concise, and compelling way. The deliverables should include the following elements:

    - A title and an introduction that provide the context and the purpose of the data analysis project
    - A summary and a conclusion that highlight the main findings and recommendations
    - A body that contains the data visualizations, tables, charts, graphs, etc., that support the findings and recommendations
    - A methodology and a data dictionary that explain the data sources, the data preparation, the data analysis techniques, and the data definitions used in the project
    - A references and an appendix that cite the sources of information and provide additional details or supplementary materials

  - **Review and refine the deliverables**: The data analyst should review and refine the deliverables to ensure that they are accurate, complete, consistent, and error-free. The data analyst should also solicit feedback from the stakeholders or clients and incorporate their suggestions or corrections to improve the quality and relevance of the deliverables.
  - **Present and distribute the deliverables**: The data analyst should present and distribute the deliverables to the stakeholders or clients in a timely and professional manner. The data analyst should also prepare for the presentation by rehearsing, anticipating questions, and having backup materials. The data analyst should also follow up with the stakeholders or clients to ensure that they have received and understood the deliverables, and to address any issues or concerns that they may have.
