# Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

### Applications of Data Analytics

Data analytics is used in various fields to help in decision making, budgeting, planning, etc. Some of the applications of data analytics include:

1. **Fraud Detection**: Carefully analyzing customer spending trends can reveal unusual patterns in their spending habits .
2. **Energy Management**: Power distributing companies can use data analytics to predict the amount of energy to be consumed around a specific time of the day .
3. **Digital Advertisement**: Targeted advertisement is a vital part of digital marketing .
4. **Security**: Predictive analysis has helped in dropping crime .
5. **Transportation**: Data analytics can be used to revolutionize transportation .
6. **Risk Detection**: Data analytics can be used to detect risks .

These are just a few examples of the many applications of data analytics. It is used in various industries to drive improved decisions, business processes, and outcomes .
