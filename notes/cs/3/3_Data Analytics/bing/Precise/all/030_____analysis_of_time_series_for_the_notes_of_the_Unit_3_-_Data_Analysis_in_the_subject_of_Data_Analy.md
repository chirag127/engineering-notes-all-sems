# Analysis of Time Series

Time series analysis is a statistical technique that deals with time series data, or trend analysis. Time series data means that data is in a series of particular time periods or intervals. The data is considered in three types: Time series data, cross-sectional data, and pooled data. Here, we will discuss the time series data.

## Unit 3 - Data Analysis

### Key Concepts

1. **Trend Analysis**: Trend analysis is the widespread practice of collecting information and attempting to spot a pattern, or trend, in the information.
2. **Seasonality**: Seasonality refers to periodic fluctuations. For example, electricity consumption is high during the day and low during the night.
3. **Cyclic Movements**: Cyclic movements are movements that are not fixed in terms of calendar time. For example, business cycles that repeat at irregular intervals from several years to more than a decade.
4. **Random or Irregular Movements**: Random or irregular movements are caused by unpredictable factors. These factors can be natural disasters, strikes, and so on.

### Methods of Time Series Analysis

1. **Graphical Method**: This method includes techniques like Line charts, Bar charts, Histograms, Scatter diagrams, etc.
2. **Semi-Average Method**: This method is used to estimate the trend of time series data. It involves dividing the time series into two or more parts and finding the average of each part.
3. **Moving Average Method**: This method is used to smooth out short-term fluctuations and highlight longer-term trends or cycles.
4. **Exponential Smoothing Method**: This method is a time series forecasting method for univariate data that can be used to forecast data points by using the weighted average of past observations.

### Applications of Time Series Analysis

1. **Economic Forecasting**: Time series analysis is used to forecast economic data like stock prices, sales, and employment.
2. **Sales Forecasting**: Time series analysis is used to forecast the sales of a product.
3. **Budgetary Analysis**: Time series analysis is used to forecast the budget of a company or a country.
4. **Process and Quality Control**: Time series analysis is used to forecast the quality of a product.
