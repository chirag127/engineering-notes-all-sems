# Estimating Moments for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the Subject of Data Analytics

1. Moments are statistical measures that describe the shape of a data distribution.
2. The first moment is the mean, which measures the central tendency of the data.
3. The second moment is the variance, which measures the spread of the data.
4. Higher-order moments, such as skewness (third moment) and kurtosis (fourth moment), measure the asymmetry and peakedness of the data distribution, respectively.
5. In the context of mining data streams, it is often necessary to estimate moments of the data distribution in an online fashion, as the data is continuously arriving.
6. One approach to estimating moments in data streams is the use of sketching algorithms, which provide approximate answers with provable guarantees on the accuracy of the estimates.
7. Another approach is the use of sampling techniques, where a representative sample of the data stream is maintained and used to estimate the moments of the entire data distribution.
8. Both sketching and sampling techniques can be used to estimate moments in a space-efficient manner, making them suitable for use in resource-constrained environments such as data stream processing systems.
