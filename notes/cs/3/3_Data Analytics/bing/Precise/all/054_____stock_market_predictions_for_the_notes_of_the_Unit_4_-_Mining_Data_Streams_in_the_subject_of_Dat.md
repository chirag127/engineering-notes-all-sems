# Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in Data Analytics: Stock Market Predictions

- Stock market data analysis needs the help of artificial intelligence and data mining techniques.
- The volatility of stock prices depends on gains or losses of certain companies.
- Many people consider stock market prediction as gambling.
- The main purpose of forecasting by data mining in the stock market is to discover knowledge that can assist decision-makers.
- It is important that companies use data mining with utmost care to improve their business by increasing revenue and reducing costs.
- Data analysis tools such as regression, support vector machine, decision tree and random forest can be used to extract knowledge.
- The predictive approach is a technique of data mining that forecasts predictions based on historical data or on aggregate indicators.
- Precisely predicting stocks is essential for investors to gain enormous profits.
- However, the volatility of the market makes this kind of prediction highly difficult.
- Data Mining and Machine Learning could be used to guide an investor’s decisions.
