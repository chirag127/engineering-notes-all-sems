### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

In the context of the Data Analytics Lifecycle, there are several key roles that are essential for the success of an analytic project. These roles include:

1. **Project Sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for providing the necessary resources and support for the project. They also help to define the project's goals and objectives and ensure that the project aligns with the organization's overall strategy.

2. **Project Manager**: The project manager is responsible for the day-to-day management of the project. They coordinate the project team, manage the project schedule, and ensure that the project is completed on time and within budget.

3. **Data Scientist**: The data scientist is responsible for analyzing the data and developing predictive models. They use their expertise in statistics, machine learning, and data mining to extract insights from the data and make data-driven decisions.

4. **Data Engineer**: The data engineer is responsible for designing, building, and maintaining the data infrastructure. They ensure that the data is properly collected, stored, and processed so that it can be easily accessed and analyzed by the data scientist.

5. **Business Analyst**: The business analyst is responsible for understanding the business context and translating the business requirements into technical requirements. They work closely with the data scientist to ensure that the data analysis and predictive models are aligned with the business needs.

6. **Subject Matter Expert**: The subject matter expert is an individual with deep knowledge and expertise in a specific domain. They provide valuable insights and guidance to the project team and help to ensure that the project is grounded in the reality of the business.

These roles are critical for the success of an analytic project and it is important to ensure that the right individuals are in place to fulfill these roles. Each role brings a unique set of skills and expertise to the project and they must work together effectively to achieve the project's goals and objectives.
