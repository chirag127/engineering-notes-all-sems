### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

#### Unstructured Data

- Unstructured data refers to data that does not have a predefined data model or is not organized in a pre-defined manner.
- Unstructured data can be in the form of text, images, videos, audio, and other multimedia content.
- Unstructured data is often generated by human activities, such as social media posts, emails, and documents.
- Unstructured data can be difficult to analyze due to its lack of structure and the need for specialized tools and techniques to extract meaningful information.
- Unstructured data can provide valuable insights and can be used in various applications, such as sentiment analysis, trend analysis, and customer behavior analysis.
- Techniques such as natural language processing, text mining, and image recognition can be used to analyze unstructured data.
- The growth of unstructured data has led to the development of big data technologies, such as Hadoop and NoSQL databases, to store and process large volumes of unstructured data.
