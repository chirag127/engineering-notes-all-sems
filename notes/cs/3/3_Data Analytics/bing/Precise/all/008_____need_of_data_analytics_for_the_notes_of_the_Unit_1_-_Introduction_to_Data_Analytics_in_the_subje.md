# Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

### Need of Data Analytics

1. **Data-driven decision making:** Data analytics enables organizations to make informed decisions based on data, rather than relying on intuition or assumptions.
2. **Identifying trends and patterns:** Data analytics can help organizations identify trends and patterns in their data, allowing them to make predictions and plan for the future.
3. **Improving operational efficiency:** By analyzing data, organizations can identify areas where they can improve their operations, leading to increased efficiency and cost savings.
4. **Enhancing customer experience:** Data analytics can be used to analyze customer data, allowing organizations to better understand their customers and provide a more personalized experience.
5. **Competitive advantage:** Organizations that effectively use data analytics can gain a competitive advantage over their rivals by making better decisions and responding more quickly to changes in the market.
