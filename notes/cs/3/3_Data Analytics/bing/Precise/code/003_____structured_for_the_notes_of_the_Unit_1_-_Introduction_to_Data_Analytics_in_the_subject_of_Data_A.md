### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

1. **Data Analytics** is the process of examining, cleaning, transforming, and modeling data to discover useful information, draw conclusions, and support decision-making.
2. It involves the use of **statistical and quantitative methods**, as well as **predictive modeling** and **machine learning** techniques, to analyze and interpret data.
3. Data Analytics is used in various industries, including **business, healthcare, sports, and government**, to make data-driven decisions.
4. The **data analysis process** typically involves several steps, including **data collection**, **data preprocessing**, **data exploration**, **data modeling**, and **data visualization**.
5. **Data collection** involves gathering data from various sources, such as databases, spreadsheets, and text files.
6. **Data preprocessing** involves cleaning and transforming the data to prepare it for analysis. This may include removing missing or incorrect data, converting data types, and normalizing data.
7. **Data exploration** involves examining the data to identify patterns and relationships. This may include calculating summary statistics, creating visualizations, and performing hypothesis tests.
8. **Data modeling** involves building predictive or explanatory models using machine learning or statistical techniques. This may include regression analysis, classification, and clustering.
9. **Data visualization** involves creating graphical representations of the data to communicate insights and findings. This may include bar charts, scatter plots, and heat maps.
10. Data Analytics requires a combination of **technical skills**, such as programming and statistics, and **domain knowledge** to effectively analyze and interpret data.
