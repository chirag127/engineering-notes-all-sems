### Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics is a branch of statistics that deals with the collection, analysis, interpretation, presentation, and organization of data. It is used to summarize and describe the main features of a dataset, either through numerical calculations or graphical representations.

Here are some key points to remember about descriptive statistics:

1. Descriptive statistics is used to summarize and describe data, not to make inferences or predictions about a population.
2. Common measures of central tendency include the mean, median, and mode.
3. Measures of variability or dispersion include the range, variance, and standard deviation.
4. Graphical representations of data, such as histograms, box plots, and scatter plots, can also be used to describe data.
5. Descriptive statistics can be used to identify patterns and trends in data, but it cannot be used to make conclusions about cause and effect relationships.

Descriptive statistics is an important tool in data analysis and is often used in conjunction with inferential statistics to draw conclusions about a population based on a sample of data.
