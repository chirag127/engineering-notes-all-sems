### Linear Systems Analysis & Nonlinear Dynamics

#### Linear Systems Analysis
- Linear dynamical systems can be solved exactly, in contrast to most nonlinear ones .
- Occasionally, a nonlinear system can be solved exactly by a change of variables to a linear system .
- The solutions of (almost) any nonlinear system can be well-approximated by an equivalent linear system near its fixed points .
- Linear algebra and linear dynamical systems have applications in circuits, signal processing, communications, and control systems .
- Least-squares approximations of over-determined equations and least-norm solutions of underdetermined equations are topics in linear systems analysis .

#### Nonlinear Dynamics
- Nonlinear dynamical systems describe the evolution of a state variable in time in the form of ordinary differential equations or as discrete mappings .
- Dynamical systems theory studies the solutions of such equations and mappings and their dependence on initial conditions and parameters .
- For nonlinear analysis, some components can yield, and additional inelastic properties are needed for these components .
- These properties are more complicated than the elastic properties .
- Linear stability analysis of continuous-time nonlinear systems involves finding an equilibrium point of the system, calculating the Jacobian matrix of the system at the equilibrium point, and calculating the eigenvalues of the Jacobian matrix .
- Experimental technique and data analysis have seen such dramatic progress that, by now, most fundamental properties of nonlinear dynamical systems have been observed in the laboratory .
- Great efforts are being made to exploit ideas from chaos theory wherever the data displays more structure than can be captured by traditional methods .
