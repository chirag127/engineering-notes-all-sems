### Sources and nature of data

Data is a collection of facts, figures, and statistics that can be processed to produce information. Data can be collected from various sources and can be of different types. Here are some of the sources and nature of data:

1. **Primary data:** Primary data is the data that is collected directly from the source. It is original and first-hand information. Some of the methods of collecting primary data are surveys, interviews, and observations.

2. **Secondary data:** Secondary data is the data that has already been collected and processed by someone else. It is second-hand information. Some of the sources of secondary data are books, journals, and the internet.

3. **Quantitative data:** Quantitative data is the data that can be measured and expressed in numbers. It is numerical in nature. Some examples of quantitative data are height, weight, and temperature.

4. **Qualitative data:** Qualitative data is the data that cannot be measured and expressed in numbers. It is descriptive in nature. Some examples of qualitative data are color, taste, and texture.

5. **Structured data:** Structured data is the data that is organized in a specific format or schema. It is easy to search, analyze, and process. Some examples of structured data are spreadsheets and databases.

6. **Unstructured data:** Unstructured data is the data that is not organized in a specific format or schema. It is difficult to search, analyze, and process. Some examples of unstructured data are emails, videos, and images.

These are some of the sources and nature of data that are important to understand in the subject of Data Analytics. Understanding the sources and nature of data can help in selecting the appropriate methods for collecting, processing, and analyzing data.
