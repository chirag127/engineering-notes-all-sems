### Market-Based Modelling

Market-based modelling is a technique used in data analytics to analyze customer behavior and preferences. It is commonly used in the field of market basket analysis, which involves the identification of frequent itemsets and the discovery of association rules between items.

Here are some key points to consider when studying market-based modelling:

1. Market-based modelling is used to analyze customer behavior and preferences by examining the items that customers frequently purchase together.

2. This technique is commonly used in market basket analysis, which involves the identification of frequent itemsets and the discovery of association rules between items.

3. Market-based modelling can help businesses to better understand their customers and to make more informed decisions about product placement, promotions, and pricing.

4. Frequent itemsets are groups of items that are commonly purchased together, and association rules are used to identify relationships between these items.

5. Clustering is another technique that can be used in conjunction with market-based modelling to group similar customers together based on their purchasing behavior.

6. Market-based modelling can be used to improve customer satisfaction and loyalty by providing more personalized and relevant recommendations to customers.

7. This technique can also be used to identify cross-selling and up-selling opportunities, which can help to increase sales and revenue.
