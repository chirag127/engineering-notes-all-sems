### Estimating Moments for the Notes of the Unit 4 - Mining Data Streams in the Subject of Data Analytics

1. Moments are statistical measures that describe the shape of a data distribution.
2. The first moment is the mean, which measures the central tendency of the data.
3. The second moment is the variance, which measures the spread of the data.
4. Higher-order moments, such as skewness (third moment) and kurtosis (fourth moment), measure the asymmetry and peakedness of the data distribution, respectively.
5. In the context of mining data streams, it is often necessary to estimate moments from a sample of the data, rather than calculating them exactly from the entire data set.
6. There are several techniques for estimating moments from data streams, including the Alon-Matias-Szegedy (AMS) algorithm, the Flajolet-Martin (FM) algorithm, and the Count-Min Sketch (CMS) algorithm.
7. These algorithms use probabilistic data structures and random sampling to provide approximate estimates of moments with high accuracy and low computational cost.
8. Estimating moments from data streams is useful for tasks such as anomaly detection, data summarization, and trend analysis.
