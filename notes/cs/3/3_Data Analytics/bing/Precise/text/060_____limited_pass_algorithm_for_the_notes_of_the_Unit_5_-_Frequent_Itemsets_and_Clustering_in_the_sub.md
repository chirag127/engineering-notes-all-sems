### Limited Pass Algorithm

The Limited Pass Algorithm is a method used in data analytics for finding frequent itemsets and clustering. It is part of the study material for Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in the subject of Data Analytics. Here are some key points to remember about the Limited Pass Algorithm:

1. The Limited Pass Algorithm is an efficient method for finding frequent itemsets in large datasets.
2. It works by making multiple passes over the data, each time counting the occurrences of itemsets and discarding those that do not meet a minimum support threshold.
3. The algorithm is called "limited" because it limits the number of passes over the data, making it more efficient than other methods that require multiple passes.
4. The Limited Pass Algorithm is commonly used in market basket analysis, where it can help identify patterns and associations between items purchased by customers.
5. It is also used in clustering, where it can help group similar items together based on their co-occurrence in the data.
