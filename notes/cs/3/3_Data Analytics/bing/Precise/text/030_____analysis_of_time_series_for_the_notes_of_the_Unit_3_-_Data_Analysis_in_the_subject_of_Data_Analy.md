### Analysis of Time Series

Time series analysis is a statistical technique that deals with time series data, or trend analysis. Time series data means that data is in a series of particular time periods or intervals. The data is considered in three types: Time series data, cross-sectional data, and pooled data. Here are some important points to note about time series analysis:

1. Time series analysis is used to understand the underlying forces leading to a particular trend in the data.
2. It is used to forecast future values based on previously observed values.
3. Time series analysis can be used to identify the nature of the phenomenon represented by the series of observations.
4. It can also be used to identify the underlying structure of the data.
5. Time series analysis can be used to detect any changes in the data that may have occurred over time.
6. It can also be used to identify any patterns or relationships that may exist between different time series data sets.
