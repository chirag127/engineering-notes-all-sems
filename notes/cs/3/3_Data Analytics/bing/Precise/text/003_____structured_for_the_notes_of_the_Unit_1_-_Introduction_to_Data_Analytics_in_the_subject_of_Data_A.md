### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

1. **Data Analytics** is the process of examining, cleaning, transforming, and modeling data to discover useful information, draw conclusions, and support decision-making.
2. It involves the use of **statistical and quantitative methods**, as well as **predictive modeling** and **machine learning** techniques, to analyze and interpret data.
3. Data Analytics can be applied to a wide range of fields, including **business, finance, healthcare, sports, and social sciences**.
4. The goal of Data Analytics is to **extract insights** from data and **turn them into actionable information** that can be used to improve decision-making and performance.
5. Some common techniques used in Data Analytics include **data mining, data visualization, and predictive modeling**.
6. Data Analytics is a **multidisciplinary field** that requires knowledge of **statistics, computer science, and domain expertise**.
7. The rise of **big data** and the increasing availability of **large datasets** have made Data Analytics an increasingly important tool for organizations of all sizes.
