### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics
#### Need of Data Analytics

1. **Data-driven decision making:** Data analytics enables organizations to make informed decisions based on data, rather than relying on intuition or assumptions.
2. **Identifying trends and patterns:** Data analytics can help organizations identify trends and patterns in their data, which can be used to improve business processes, customer experience, and overall performance.
3. **Predictive analytics:** Data analytics can be used to make predictions about future events or behaviors, allowing organizations to take proactive measures to mitigate risks or capitalize on opportunities.
4. **Cost savings and efficiency:** By analyzing data, organizations can identify areas where they can reduce costs and improve efficiency.
5. **Competitive advantage:** Organizations that effectively use data analytics can gain a competitive advantage over their rivals by making better decisions, improving their operations, and delivering better products and services to their customers.
