### Characteristics of Data

Data is a collection of facts, figures, and statistics that are collected, processed, and stored for analysis and reference. The following are some of the characteristics of data:

1. **Accuracy**: Data should be accurate and free from errors. It should represent the true value of the information it is intended to convey.

2. **Relevance**: Data should be relevant to the purpose for which it is being collected and used. It should be applicable to the problem or decision at hand.

3. **Timeliness**: Data should be up-to-date and available when it is needed. Outdated data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

4. **Completeness**: Data should be complete and include all the necessary information. Missing data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

5. **Consistency**: Data should be consistent and follow a standard format. Inconsistent data can lead to confusion and errors in analysis.

6. **Accessibility**: Data should be easily accessible to those who need it. It should be stored in a way that allows for easy retrieval and analysis.

7. **Reliability**: Data should be reliable and come from a trustworthy source. Unreliable data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

These are some of the key characteristics of data that are important for data analytics. Understanding these characteristics can help ensure that data is collected, processed, and analyzed in a way that leads to accurate and meaningful insights.
