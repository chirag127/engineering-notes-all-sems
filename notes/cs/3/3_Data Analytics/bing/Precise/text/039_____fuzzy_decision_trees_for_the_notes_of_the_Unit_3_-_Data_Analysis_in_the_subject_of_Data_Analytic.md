### Fuzzy Decision Trees

Fuzzy decision trees are a type of decision tree that is used for classification problems. They are more efficient for treating learning data of mixed type, including both numerical and categorical data. They are more robust in tolerating imprecise, conflicting, and missing information.

A fuzzy decision tree induction method is based on the reduction of classification ambiguity with fuzzy evidence. Fuzzy decision trees represent classification knowledge more naturally to the way of human thinking.

Fuzzy decision trees are built on the basis of pre-constructed decision trees using conventional methods with the aim of maximizing the classification accuracy. Subsequently, the instance whose belongingness is ambiguous is identified and determined as a non-commitment case.

In summary, fuzzy decision trees are a powerful tool for classification problems, especially when dealing with mixed data types and imprecise, conflicting, or missing information. They represent classification knowledge in a way that is more natural to human thinking and are more robust in tolerating imprecise, conflict, and missing information.
