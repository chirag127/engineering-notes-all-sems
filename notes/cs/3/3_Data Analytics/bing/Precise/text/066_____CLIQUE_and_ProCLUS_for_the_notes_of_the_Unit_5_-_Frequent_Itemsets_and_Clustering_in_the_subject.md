### CLIQUE and ProCLUS

- CLIQUE (Clustering In QUEst) is an algorithm for subspace clustering in high dimensional data.
- CLIQUE partitions the data space into non-overlapping rectangular units and identifies dense units.
- The dense units are then merged to form clusters.
- ProCLUS (PROjected CLUStering) is another algorithm for subspace clustering in high dimensional data.
- ProCLUS is based on the idea of projected clustering, where clusters exist in subspaces of the data space.
- ProCLUS first identifies a set of cluster representatives and then finds the relevant subspaces for each cluster.
- Both CLIQUE and ProCLUS are useful for finding clusters in high dimensional data, where clusters may exist in subspaces of the data space.
- These algorithms are part of the study of frequent itemsets and clustering in the subject of data analytics.
