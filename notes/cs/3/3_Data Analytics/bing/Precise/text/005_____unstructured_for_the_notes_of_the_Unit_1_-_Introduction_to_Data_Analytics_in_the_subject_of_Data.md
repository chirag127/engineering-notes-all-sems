### Unstructured Data

- Unstructured data refers to data that does not have a predefined data model or is not organized in a pre-defined manner.
- It is often text-heavy, but may contain data such as dates, numbers, and facts as well.
- Examples of unstructured data include emails, social media posts, videos, photos, audio files, and web pages.
- Unstructured data can be difficult to analyze and process due to its lack of structure.
- Techniques such as natural language processing, text analytics, and data mining can be used to extract valuable information from unstructured data.
- Unstructured data is becoming increasingly important in the field of data analytics as it can provide valuable insights that structured data may not be able to provide.
