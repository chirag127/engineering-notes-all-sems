### Unit 3 - Data Analysis: Analysis of Time Series

Time series analysis is a statistical technique that deals with time series data, or trend analysis. Time series data means that data is in a series of particular time periods or intervals. The data is considered in three types: Time series data, cross-sectional data, and pooled data. Here, we will discuss the time series data.

1. **Trend Analysis:** Trend analysis is the practice of collecting information and attempting to spot a pattern, or trend, in the information. In some fields of study, the term "trend analysis" has more formally defined meanings.

2. **Seasonal Analysis:** Seasonal analysis is the study of data that exhibits a seasonal pattern. Seasonal patterns are any predictable fluctuation or movement that recurs or repeats over a one-year period.

3. **Cyclical Analysis:** Cyclical analysis is the study of data that exhibits a cyclical pattern. A cycle is a sequence of data points that typically repeat every several years.

4. **Forecasting:** Forecasting is the process of making predictions of the future based on past and present data and most commonly by analysis of trends.

5. **Exponential Smoothing:** Exponential smoothing is a time series forecasting method for univariate data that can be used to forecast data points by using weighted averages of past observations.

6. **ARIMA:** ARIMA, short for 'AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average', is actually a class of models that 'explains' a given time series based on its own past values, that is, its own lags and the lagged forecast errors.

7. **Box-Jenkins Method:** The Box-Jenkins method is a forecasting technique that can capture complex patterns and relationships in time series data. It is based on the idea that a time series can be represented as a combination of a trend, seasonal, and random component.

8. **Decomposition:** Decomposition is the process of breaking down a time series into its component parts: trend, seasonal, and irregular. This can help to better understand the underlying patterns and relationships in the data.

These are some of the key concepts and techniques used in the analysis of time series data. Understanding and applying these techniques can help in making more accurate predictions and forecasts based on time series data.
