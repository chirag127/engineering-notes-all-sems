### Characteristics of Data

Data is a collection of facts, figures, and statistics that are collected, analyzed, and interpreted for decision-making. The following are some of the characteristics of data:

1. **Accuracy**: Data must be accurate and free from errors. Inaccurate data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

2. **Relevance**: Data must be relevant to the problem or decision at hand. Irrelevant data can lead to confusion and incorrect conclusions.

3. **Timeliness**: Data must be timely and up-to-date. Outdated data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

4. **Completeness**: Data must be complete and include all relevant information. Incomplete data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

5. **Consistency**: Data must be consistent and follow a standard format. Inconsistent data can lead to confusion and incorrect conclusions.

6. **Accessibility**: Data must be easily accessible to those who need it. Inaccessible data can lead to delays and incorrect decisions.

7. **Reliability**: Data must be reliable and come from a trustworthy source. Unreliable data can lead to incorrect conclusions and decisions.

These are some of the key characteristics of data that are important for effective data analysis and decision-making. It is important to ensure that the data used in analysis meets these criteria to ensure accurate and reliable results.
