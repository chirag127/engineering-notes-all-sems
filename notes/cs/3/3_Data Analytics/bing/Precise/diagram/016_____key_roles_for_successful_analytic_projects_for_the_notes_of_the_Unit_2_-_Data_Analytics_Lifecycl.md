### Key Roles for Successful Analytic Projects

In order to successfully execute an analytic project, several key roles must be fulfilled. These roles include:

1. **Project Sponsor**: The project sponsor is responsible for providing the necessary resources and support for the project. They also help to define the project's goals and objectives.

2. **Project Manager**: The project manager is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of the project. They ensure that the project stays on track and that all team members are working towards the project's goals.

3. **Data Scientist**: The data scientist is responsible for analyzing the data and developing models to help answer the project's questions. They use their expertise in statistics, machine learning, and data analysis to provide insights and recommendations.

4. **Data Engineer**: The data engineer is responsible for designing and building the data infrastructure. They ensure that the data is properly collected, stored, and processed so that it can be used by the data scientist.

5. **Business Analyst**: The business analyst is responsible for understanding the business context of the project. They work with the project sponsor and other stakeholders to ensure that the project's goals align with the organization's overall strategy.

6. **Subject Matter Expert**: The subject matter expert provides domain-specific knowledge and expertise. They help the team to understand the nuances of the data and the problem being addressed.

By having these key roles fulfilled, an analytic project is more likely to be successful and achieve its goals.
