### Analysis vs Reporting

#### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

- **Analysis** involves examining data and information in order to draw conclusions and insights. It is a process of discovering, interpreting, and communicating meaningful patterns in data.

- **Reporting**, on the other hand, is the process of organizing and presenting data and information in a structured format. It is used to communicate the results of data analysis to stakeholders.

- The main difference between analysis and reporting is that analysis is focused on understanding and interpreting data, while reporting is focused on presenting data in a clear and concise manner.

- Analysis is a critical component of data analytics, as it allows us to gain insights and make informed decisions based on the data we have collected.

- Reporting is also important, as it allows us to communicate our findings to others and helps to ensure that data is used effectively within an organization.

- In summary, analysis and reporting are two distinct but complementary processes in data analytics. Analysis helps us to understand and interpret data, while reporting helps us to communicate our findings to others. Both are essential for effective data-driven decision making.
