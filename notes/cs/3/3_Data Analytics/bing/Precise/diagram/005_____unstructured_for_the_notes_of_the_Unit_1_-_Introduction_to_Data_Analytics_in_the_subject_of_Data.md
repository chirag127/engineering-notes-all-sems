### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics

#### Unstructured Data

- Unstructured data refers to data that does not have a predefined data model or is not organized in a pre-defined manner.
- This type of data is often text-heavy, but may also contain other forms of data such as images, videos, and audio files.
- Examples of unstructured data include emails, social media posts, customer reviews, and call center transcripts.
- Unstructured data can be difficult to analyze due to its lack of structure, but it can provide valuable insights when properly processed and analyzed.
- Techniques such as natural language processing, text analytics, and sentiment analysis can be used to extract meaningful information from unstructured data.
- The growth of unstructured data has led to the development of big data technologies such as Hadoop and NoSQL databases to store and process this type of data.
