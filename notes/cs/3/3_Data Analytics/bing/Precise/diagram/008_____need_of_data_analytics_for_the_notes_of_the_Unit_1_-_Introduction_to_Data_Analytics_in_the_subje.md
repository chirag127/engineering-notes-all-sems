### Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics
#### Need of Data Analytics

1. **Data-driven decision making:** Data analytics enables organizations to make informed decisions based on data, rather than relying on intuition or assumptions.
2. **Identifying trends and patterns:** Data analytics can help organizations identify trends and patterns in their data, which can be used to improve business processes and gain a competitive advantage.
3. **Improving efficiency and productivity:** By analyzing data, organizations can identify areas where they can improve efficiency and productivity, leading to cost savings and increased profitability.
4. **Predictive analytics:** Data analytics can be used to make predictions about future events or behaviors, allowing organizations to take proactive measures to mitigate risks or capitalize on opportunities.
5. **Personalization:** Data analytics can be used to personalize products, services, and marketing efforts, leading to increased customer satisfaction and loyalty.
6. **Fraud detection:** Data analytics can be used to detect fraudulent activities, allowing organizations to take measures to prevent financial losses.
7. **Performance measurement:** Data analytics can be used to measure the performance of various aspects of an organization, allowing for continuous improvement and optimization.
