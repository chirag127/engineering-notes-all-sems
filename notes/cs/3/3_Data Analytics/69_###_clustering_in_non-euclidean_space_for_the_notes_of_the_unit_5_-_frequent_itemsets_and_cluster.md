### clustering in non-euclidean space for the notes of the Unit 5 - Frequent Itemsets and Clustering in the subject of Data Analytics

Clustering in Non-Euclidean Space:
- Clustering is the process of grouping similar objects together.
- In non-Euclidean spaces, traditional Euclidean distance measures may not be appropriate.
- Non-Euclidean distance measures, such as cosine similarity or Jaccard distance, can be used in these spaces.
- Clustering algorithms, such as k-means and hierarchical clustering, can be adapted to work in non-Euclidean spaces.
