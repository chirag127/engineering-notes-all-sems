### evolution of analytic scalability for the notes of the Unit 1 - Introduction to Data Analytics in the subject of Data Analytics

Evolution of analytic scalability refers to the growth and development of techniques, tools, and platforms for efficiently processing and analyzing large amounts of data. 

1. Early days: Simple statistical methods and spreadsheet software were used to analyze small datasets. 

2. Emergence of big data: With the growth of data, traditional methods became insufficient. Distributed computing and NoSQL databases emerged to handle large datasets.

3. Cloud computing: Cloud computing platforms provided scalable infrastructure for data storage and processing. 

4. Advancements in machine learning: Machine learning algorithms, such as deep learning, improved the ability to analyze complex data patterns. 

5. Integration of big data and AI: Big data and AI technologies were combined to provide more advanced and sophisticated analytics capabilities.

6. Advancements in data visualization: Data visualization tools and techniques improved the ability to communicate insights and patterns in data. 

7. The current state: Analytic scalability continues to evolve with advancements in technology and increasing demand for data-driven insights.
